## 耶和華懲罰耶路撒冷

<div className="bt">

</div>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="詩篇 74:11; 詩篇 75:10; 詩篇 75:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">祂發烈怒毀滅以色列全軍。</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">敵人襲來時，祂收回援手。</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">祂像烈焰一樣吞噬雅各。</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="耶利米哀歌 2:2; 耶利米書 30:14; 耶利米書 52:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">主像敵人一樣毀滅以色列，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">吞噬她的宮殿，夷平她的堡壘，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">使猶大人悲傷哀哭。</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="以西結書 24:21; 阿摩司書 2:5; 詩篇 74:3–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">主撇棄自己的祭壇，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">憎惡自己的聖所，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">把耶路撒冷的殿牆交給敵人。</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">他們像過節一樣，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">在耶和華的殿中喧嚷吵鬧。</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
