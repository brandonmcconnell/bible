<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="以賽亞書 51:7; 詩篇 94:15; 希伯來書 12:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">耶和華說：「追求公義、</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">尋求耶和華的人啊，聽我說。</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">你們要追想那磐石，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">你們是從那裡被鑿出來的；</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">你們要追想那巖穴，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">你們是從那裡被挖出來的。</span><br /></span>

<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
