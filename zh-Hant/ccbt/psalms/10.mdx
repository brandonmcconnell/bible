<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="詩篇 7:14; 詩篇 140:9; 詩篇 140:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">他們滿口咒詛、謊話和恐嚇之言，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">舌頭沾滿禍害和邪惡。</span><br /></span>
