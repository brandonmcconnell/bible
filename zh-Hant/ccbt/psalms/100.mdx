<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">感恩詩。</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="腓立比書 4:4; 詩篇 71:23; 詩篇 95:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">你們要歡然事奉耶和華，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">到祂面前來歡唱。</span><br /></span>
