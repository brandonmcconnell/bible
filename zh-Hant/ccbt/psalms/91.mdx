<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="詩篇 61:4; 詩篇 57:1; 詩篇 17:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">祂必用祂的羽毛遮蓋你，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">你必在祂翅膀下蒙庇護，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">祂的信實是你的盾牌和壁壘。</span><br /></span>
