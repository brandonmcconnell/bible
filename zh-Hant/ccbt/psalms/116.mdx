<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="詩篇 145:18–19; 詩篇 40:1; 詩篇 55:16–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">因為祂側耳聽我的祈求，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">我一生都要求告祂。</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="詩篇 18:4–6; 詩篇 38:6; 詩篇 32:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">死亡的繩索纏繞我，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">陰間的恐怖籠罩我，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">我陷入困苦和憂愁。</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="詩篇 19:7; 馬太福音 11:25; 詩篇 142:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">耶和華保護心地單純的人，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">危難之時祂拯救了我。</span><br /></span>
