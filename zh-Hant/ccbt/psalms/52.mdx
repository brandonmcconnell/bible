## 上帝的審判和慈愛

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">以東人多益去告訴掃羅：「大衛到了亞希米勒家。」那時大衛作了這首訓誨詩，交給樂長。</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="詩篇 103:17; 詩篇 94:4; 詩篇 107:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">勇士啊，你為何以作惡為榮？</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">上帝的慈愛永遠長存。</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="箴言 18:21; 詩篇 57:4; 耶利米書 9:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">你這詭詐的人啊，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">舌頭利如剃刀，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">盡是害人的奸計。</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="詩篇 120:3; 雅各書 3:6–9; 撒母耳記上 22:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">你有詭詐的舌頭，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">好說惡言惡語。</span><br /></span>
