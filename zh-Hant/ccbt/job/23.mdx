<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="約伯記 13:18; 但以理書 9:18–19; 出埃及記 32:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">我好在祂面前陳明案情，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">滔滔不絕地為自己申辯；</span><br /></span>
