## 智慧頌

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="約伯記 11:6; 約伯記 28:21–23; 約伯記 38:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">礦中的路徑，猛禽不知道，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">鷹眼也未曾見過，</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <span className="pl pl-1">猛獸未曾踏足，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">獅子未曾經過。</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="那鴻書 1:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">世人開鑿堅石，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">翻動山的根基，</span><br /></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="以賽亞書 13:12; 以西結書 28:13; 詩篇 45:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">俄斐的金子無法與她相比，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">紅瑪瑙和藍寶石不能與她媲美；</span><br /></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
