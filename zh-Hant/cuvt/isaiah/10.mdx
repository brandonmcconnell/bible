<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="耶利米書 51:20–24; 以賽亞書 14:25; 以賽亞書 30:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">亞述是我怒氣的棍，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">手中拿我惱恨的杖。</span><br /></span>
