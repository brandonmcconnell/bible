<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="以賽亞書 52:1; 啟示錄 14:10; 詩篇 60:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">耶路撒冷啊，興起！</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">興起！站起來！</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">你從耶和華手中喝了他忿怒之杯，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">喝了那使人東倒西歪的爵，以致喝盡。</span><br /></span>
