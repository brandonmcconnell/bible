<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="阿摩司書 2:1–3; 耶利米書 48:1–47; 以賽亞書 11:14"> 論摩押的默示：<br /><span className="pl pl-1">一夜之間，摩押的亞珥變為荒廢，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">歸於無有；</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">一夜之間，摩押的基珥變為荒廢，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">歸於無有。</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="耶利米書 48:1; 利未記 21:5; 以賽亞書 3:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">他們上巴益，又往底本，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">到高處去哭泣。</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">摩押人因尼波和米底巴哀號，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">各人頭上光禿，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">鬍鬚剃淨。</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="約拿書 3:6–8; 耶利米書 19:13; 申命記 22:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">他們在街市上都腰束麻布，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">在房頂上和寬闊處俱各哀號，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">眼淚汪汪。</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="耶利米書 48:34; 列王紀上 19:4; 約拿書 4:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">希實本和以利亞利悲哀的聲音達到雅雜，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">所以摩押帶兵器的高聲喊嚷，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">人心戰兢。</span><br /></span>
