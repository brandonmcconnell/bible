<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="箴言 19:11; 箴言 12:16; 彌迦書 7:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">愚妄人怒氣全發；</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">智慧人忍氣含怒。</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="箴言 20:8; 列王紀下 10:6–7; 詩篇 52:2–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">君王若聽謊言，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">他一切臣僕都是奸惡。</span><br /></span>
