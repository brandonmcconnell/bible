<div className="bt">

<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="羅馬書 3:18; 箴言 16:6; 詩篇 143:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">惡人的罪過在他心裏說：</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">我眼中不怕上帝！</span><br /></span>
