<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="那鸿书 2:10; 以赛亚书 2:19; 以赛亚书 42:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">看哪，耶和华使地空虚，变为荒凉；</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">又翻转大地，将居民分散。</span><br /></span>
