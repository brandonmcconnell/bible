## 蒙主赦宥乃为有福

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">大卫的训诲诗。</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="罗马书 4:6–8; 以赛亚书 44:22; 诗篇 85:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">得赦免其过、遮盖其罪的，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">这人是有福的！</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="约翰福音 1:47; 哥林多后书 5:19–21; 启示录 14:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">凡心里没有诡诈，耶和华不算为有罪的，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">这人是有福的！</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="箴言 28:13; 诗篇 38:8; 诗篇 38:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">我闭口不认罪的时候，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">因终日唉哼而骨头枯干。</span><br /></span>
