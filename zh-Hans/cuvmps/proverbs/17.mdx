<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="箴言 29:12; 箴言 12:19; 诗篇 50:16–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">愚顽人说美言本不相宜，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">何况君王说谎话呢？</span><br /></span>
