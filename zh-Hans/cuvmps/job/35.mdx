<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> 以利户又说：</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="箴言 13:20; 约伯记 34:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">我要回答你和在你这里的朋友。</span><br /></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="出埃及记 2:23; 约伯记 34:28; 出埃及记 3:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">“人因多受欺压就哀求，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">因受能者的辖制</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“辖制”原文作“膀臂”。">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">便求救，</span><br /></span>
