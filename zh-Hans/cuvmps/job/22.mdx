## 以利法历指约伯之罪

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> 提幔人以利法回答说：</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="约伯记 14:3; 诗篇 143:2; 启示录 3:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">岂是因你敬畏他，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">就责备你、审判你吗？</span><br /></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
