<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="以赛亚书 2:4; 约珥书 3:9–16; 马太福音 25:31–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">他必在多国的民中施行审判，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">为远方强盛的国断定是非。</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">他们要将刀打成犁头，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">把枪打成镰刀。</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">这国不举刀攻击那国，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">他们也不再学习战事。</span><br /></span>
