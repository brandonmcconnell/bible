## 惡人終難免禍

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="傳道書 7:9; 何西阿書 7:11; 創世記 30:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">憤怒害死愚妄人，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">嫉妒殺死痴迷人。</span><br /></span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="詩篇 50:15; 約伯記 22:21; 詩篇 37:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">「至於我，我必仰望神，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">把我的事情託付他。</span><br /></span>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
