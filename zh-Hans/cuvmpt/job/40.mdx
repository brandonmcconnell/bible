<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="約伯記 38:1; 約伯記 40:6"> 耶和華又對約伯說：</span>

<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="約伯記 38:3; 約伯記 42:4; 約伯記 23:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">「你要如勇士束腰，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">我問你，你可以指示我。</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="羅馬書 3:4; 以賽亞書 28:18; 約伯記 32:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">你豈可廢棄我所擬定的？</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">豈可定我有罪，好顯自己為義嗎？</span><br /></span>
