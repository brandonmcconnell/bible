## 勸民歌頌耶和華之慈愛救恩

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">一篇詩。</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="出埃及記 15:6; 以賽亞書 52:10; 詩篇 33:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">你們要向耶和華唱新歌，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">因為他行過奇妙的事，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">他的右手和聖臂施行救恩。</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="以賽亞書 52:10; 以賽亞書 62:2; 詩篇 22:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">耶和華發明了他的救恩，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">在列邦人眼前顯出公義。</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="詩篇 22:27; 使徒行傳 13:47; 申命記 4:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">記念他向以色列家所發的慈愛、所憑的信實，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">地的四極都看見我們神的救恩。</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
