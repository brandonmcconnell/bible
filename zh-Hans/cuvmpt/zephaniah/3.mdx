<div className="bp poetry">

## 勸俟以色列復興

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>

## 宜歌頌主恩

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="以賽亞書 12:6; 撒迦利亞書 2:10–11; 彌迦書 4:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">錫安的民哪，應當歌唱！</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">以色列啊，應當歡呼！</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">耶路撒冷的民哪，應當滿心歡喜快樂！</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="以賽亞書 51:22; 西番雅書 3:5; 以賽亞書 33:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">耶和華已經除去你的刑罰，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">趕出你的仇敵。</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">以色列的王耶和華在你中間，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">你必不再懼怕災禍。</span><br /></span>
