<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="箴言 20:19; 箴言 25:9; 利未记 19:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">嚼舌者泄露秘密，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">忠信者守口如瓶。</span></span>
