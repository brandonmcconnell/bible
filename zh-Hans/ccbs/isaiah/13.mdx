<div className="bp poetry">

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="约珥书 1:15; 以赛亚书 13:9; 阿摩司书 5:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">你们哀号吧！</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">因为耶和华的日子近了，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">全能者施行毁灭的时候到了。</span><br /></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
