<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="罗马书 11:8; 以赛亚书 44:18; 帖撒罗尼迦后书 2:9–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">因为耶和华把沉睡的灵倾倒在你们身上，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">祂封住你们的眼睛，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">盖住你们的头。</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">你们的眼睛就是先知，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">你们的头就是先见。</span></span>
