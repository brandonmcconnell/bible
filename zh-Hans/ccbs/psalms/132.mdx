<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">上圣殿朝圣之诗。</div>

<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="历代志下 6:41–42; 民数记 10:35–36; 诗篇 68:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">耶和华啊，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">求你起来和你大能的约柜一同进入安息之所。</span><br /></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
