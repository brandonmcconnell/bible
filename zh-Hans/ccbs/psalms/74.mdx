<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="民数记 20:11; 以赛亚书 48:21; 诗篇 105:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">你曾开辟泉源和溪流，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">也曾使滔滔河水枯干。</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="诗篇 136:7–9; 马太福音 5:45; 诗篇 104:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">白昼和黑夜都属于你，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">你设立了日月。</span><br /></span>
