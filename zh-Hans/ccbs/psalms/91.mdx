<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="诗篇 61:4; 诗篇 57:1; 诗篇 17:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">祂必用祂的羽毛遮盖你，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">你必在祂翅膀下蒙庇护，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">祂的信实是你的盾牌和壁垒。</span><br /></span>
