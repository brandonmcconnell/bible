<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">以斯拉人以探的训诲诗。</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="诗篇 101:1; 诗篇 89:8; 诗篇 119:90"> <span className="pl pl-1">我要永远称颂耶和华的慈爱！</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">我的口要传扬你的信实直到万代。</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="诗篇 36:5; 尼希米记 9:31; 诗篇 89:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">我要宣告：</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">你的慈爱存到永远，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">你的信实坚立在天。</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="诗篇 86:8; 诗篇 71:19; 诗篇 113:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">天上有谁能与耶和华相比，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">众天使中谁能像祂？</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="诗篇 87:4; 诗篇 68:30; 诗篇 144:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">你击碎海怪</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“海怪”希伯来文是“拉哈伯”。">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">，使其毙命，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">以大能的臂膀驱散仇敌。</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="诗篇 72:5; 诗篇 89:29; 以赛亚书 59:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">他的后裔必永无穷尽，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">他的王位必在我面前如日长存，</span><br /></span>
