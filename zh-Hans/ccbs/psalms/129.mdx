## 求上帝击退仇敌

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">上圣殿朝圣之诗。</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="诗篇 124:1; 耶利米书 2:2; 何西阿书 11:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">以色列要说：</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“我从小就深受仇敌的迫害，</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="诗篇 34:19; 马太福音 16:18; 哥林多后书 4:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">我从小就深受仇敌的迫害，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">但他们没能胜过我。</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="以赛亚书 51:23; 诗篇 141:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">他们如同农夫在我背上耕田，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">耕出长长的犁沟。”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="诗篇 119:137; 耶利米哀歌 3:22; 尼希米记 9:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">耶和华是公义的，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">祂砍断了恶人的绑索。</span><br /></span>
