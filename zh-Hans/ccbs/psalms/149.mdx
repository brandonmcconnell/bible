## 以色列要赞美上帝

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="诗篇 33:3; 启示录 5:9; 诗篇 148:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">你们要赞美耶和华！</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">要向耶和华唱新歌，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">在祂忠心子民的聚会中颂赞祂！</span><br /></span>
