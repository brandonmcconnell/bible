## 颂扬上帝的拯救

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">一首诗歌。</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="出埃及记 15:6; 以赛亚书 52:10; 诗篇 33:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">你们要向耶和华唱新歌，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">因为祂的作为奇妙，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">祂以右手和圣洁的臂膀施行拯救。</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="以赛亚书 52:10; 以赛亚书 62:2; 诗篇 22:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">耶和华显明了祂的拯救之恩，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">向列邦彰显了祂的公义。</span><br /></span>
