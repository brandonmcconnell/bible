<div className="hs">亚萨的训诲诗。</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="出埃及记 9:19; 出埃及记 9:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">又用冰雹毁灭他们的牛群，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">用闪电毁灭他们的牲畜。</span><br /></span>
