<div className="bt">

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="以赛亚书 56:7; 以赛亚书 28:16; 以弗所书 2:20–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">耶和华的城坐落在圣山上。</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="诗篇 132:13–14; 诗篇 78:67–69; 申命记 12:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">祂喜爱锡安的门，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">胜过雅各其他住处。</span><br /></span>
