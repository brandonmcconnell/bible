<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="以西结书 27:2; 以西结书 26:17; 列王纪下 24:6"> “你要为以色列的首领唱哀歌，</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="以西结书 22:25; 列王纪下 23:31–32; 历代志下 36:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">母狮养大一头幼狮，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">使它成为一头猛狮。</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">它学会了捕猎和吃人。</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="历代志下 36:5; 历代志下 36:9; 耶利米书 26:1–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">它出入狮群，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">长成一头猛狮，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">学会了捕食和吃人。</span><br /></span>
