<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="诗篇 9:15; 约伯记 22:10; 诗篇 35:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">他自陷网罗，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">步入圈套。</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="以赛亚书 8:14–15; 约伯记 1:15; 约伯记 1:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">套索缠住他的脚跟，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">网罗紧紧地罩住他。</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="撒母耳记上 2:36; 帖撒罗尼迦前书 5:3; 诗篇 34:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">他饿得气力衰竭，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">灾祸随时临到他。</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="何西阿书 9:16; 以赛亚书 5:24; 约伯记 15:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">他下面的根茎枯干，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">上面的枝子枯萎。</span><br /></span>
