<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="诗篇 144:5–15; 申命记 33:26; 撒母耳记下 22:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">他又使诸天下垂，亲自降临；</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">他的脚下是幽深的黑暗。</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="诗篇 104:3; 撒母耳记下 22:11–12; 诗篇 99:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">他乘驾基路伯飞翔，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">在风的翅膀上翱翔。</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="诗篇 97:2; 申命记 4:11; 诗篇 91:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">他以黑暗作他的藏身处，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">以众水的黑暗、天空的乌云作他四周的帷幕。</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="诗篇 104:7; 撒母耳记上 7:10; 诗篇 140:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">耶和华在天上打雷，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">至高者发出声音，降下冰雹和火炭；</span><br /></span>
