</div>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="诗篇 97:12; 启示录 4:8; 历代志上 16:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">耶和华的圣民哪，你们要歌颂他，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">称赞他可记念的圣名。</span><br /></span>
