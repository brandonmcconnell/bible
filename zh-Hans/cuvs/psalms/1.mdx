## 真福

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="诗篇 26:4–5; 箴言 4:14–15; 箴言 13:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">不从恶人的计谋，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">不站罪人的道路，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">不坐亵慢人的座位，</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="约书亚记 1:8; 诗篇 119:92; 诗篇 119:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">惟喜爱耶和华的律法，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">昼夜思想，</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">这人便为有福！</span><br /></span>
