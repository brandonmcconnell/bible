## Shepherd the Flock of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:3–4; Luke 24:48; Romans 8:17–18"> The elders who are among you I exhort, as a fellow elder and a witness of the sufferings of Christ, and also a partaker of the glory that shall be revealed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:7; 1 Timothy 3:8; Titus 3:1"> shepherd the flock of God among you, overseeing not by compulsion, but rather willingly, not in fondness for dishonest gain, but rather eagerly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:9; Philippians 3:17; Ezekiel 34:4"> nor as being masters over the flock under your care, but rather being examples to the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 1:12; 1 Corinthians 9:25; 1 John 3:2"> And when the Chief Shepherd appears, you will receive the unfading crown of glory.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Submit to God, Resist the Devil

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 4:6; Proverbs 3:34; Ephesians 5:21"> Likewise, younger people, subject yourselves to the elders. And all of you be subject to one another, and be clothed with humility, because<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“God resists the proud,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but gives grace to the humble.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:10; Luke 14:11; Proverbs 29:23"> Therefore humble yourselves under the mighty hand of God, that He may exalt you in due time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 55:22; Philippians 4:6; Psalms 37:5"> casting all your anxiety upon Him, because He cares about you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:11; James 4:7; Ephesians 4:27"> Be sober, be watchful; your adversary the devil walks about as a roaring lion, seeking someone he may devour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 4:7; Acts 14:22; Ephesians 6:11–13"> Resist him, being steadfast in your faith, knowing that the same sufferings are experienced by your brotherhood in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Peter 1:6–7"> But may the God of all grace, the One having called you into His eternal glory in Christ Jesus, after you have suffered a little while, may He Himself make you complete—He will establish, strengthen, and firmly ground you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 4:11; Romans 11:36; Revelation 1:6"> To Him be the glory and the power, forever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Greetings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:22; Acts 11:23; 1 Corinthians 15:1"> By Silvanus the faithful brother, as I consider him, I have written to you briefly, exhorting and testifying this to be the true grace of God in which you stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Acts 12:25; Psalms 87:4"> She who is in Babylon, elect together with you, greets you; and so does Mark my son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 16:16; Ephesians 6:23; Romans 8:1"> Greet one another with a kiss of love. Peace to you all who are in Christ Jesus. Amen.</span>
</div>
