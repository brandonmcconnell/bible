## Wives and Husbands

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:18; Ephesians 5:33; Ephesians 5:22–24"> Wives, likewise, subject yourselves to your own husbands, so that even if some are disobedient to the word, that by the conduct of their wives, without a word, they shall be gained for Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 1:15; Philippians 1:27"> when they observe your chaste conduct with fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9–10; Romans 12:2; Isaiah 3:18–24"> Of whom let it not be the outward adorning of braiding of hair, and wearing of gold, or of putting on of fine clothes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:12; 1 Samuel 16:7; Romans 2:29"> rather let it be the hidden person of the heart, with the incorruptible attitude of a gentle and quiet spirit, which is very precious before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Titus 2:3–4; 1 Timothy 5:5; Luke 2:37"> For thus formerly, the holy women who hoped on God also adorned themselves, subjecting themselves to their own husbands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:12; Romans 9:7–9; Isaiah 57:11"> as Sarah obeyed Abraham, calling him lord, of whom you became children, when you do good and are not afraid of any terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 3:19; Genesis 2:23–24; Matthew 19:3–9"> Husbands, likewise, live together according to knowledge as with a weaker vessel, with the wife, showing her honor as also a fellow heir of the grace of life, in order that your prayers may not be hindered.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Suffering for Righteousness' Sake

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 12:10; Ephesians 4:31–2; 1 Peter 1:22"> Finally, all of you be like-minded, sympathetic, loving the brothers, compassionate, friendly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; Romans 12:17; Romans 12:14"> not rendering evil for evil or reviling for reviling, but on the contrary blessing, knowing that you were called for this purpose, that you may inherit a blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 34:12–16; James 1:26; 1 Peter 2:1"> For<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He that desires to love life</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and see good days,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">let him stop his tongue from evil,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and his lips from speaking guile.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Psalms 34:14; Hebrews 12:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let him turn away from evil and do good;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">let him seek peace and pursue it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 34:15–16; John 9:31; Proverbs 15:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the eyes of the Lord are upon the righteous,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and His ears open to their prayer;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the face of the Lord is against those doing evil.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Romans 8:28; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> And who is he that shall injure you if you become imitators of that which is good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; 1 Peter 2:19–20; Matthew 5:10–12"> But even if you should suffer on account of righteousness, you are blessed. “But do not fear their threats, nor be troubled.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; 2 Timothy 2:25–26; Luke 21:14–15"> But sanctify the Lord God in your hearts, and always be prepared with a defense to everyone who asks you a reason for the hope that is in you, with meekness and fear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Hebrews 13:18; 1 Peter 2:15"> having a good conscience, so that in what they speak against you as evildoers, those reviling your good conduct in Christ may be put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 4:19; Matthew 26:39"> For it is better to suffer for doing good, if it is the will of God, than for doing evil.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Christ's Suffering and Ours

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Hebrews 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For Christ also suffered once to atone for sins, the just for the unjust, that He might bring you to God, being put to death in the flesh, but made alive by the Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 4:6; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 42:7"> by whom also He went and preached to the spirits in prison,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5; Genesis 6:3"> who formerly disobeyed, when the longsuffering of God was waiting in the days of Noah, when the ark was being prepared, in which a few, that is, eight souls, were saved through water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 16:16; Ezekiel 36:25–26; Acts 2:38"> which as an antitype, baptism now also saves us—(not the putting away of the filth of the flesh, but an appeal to God for a good conscience), through the resurrection of Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 10:12"> who having gone into heaven, is at the right hand of God, angels and authorities and powers being subjected to Him.</span>
</div>
</div>
