## Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 1:1; Acts 2:5–11; Hebrews 11:13"> Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ,<br />To the elect sojourners of the Dispersion of Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:13; Ephesians 1:4–5; Hebrews 12:24"> according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, in sanctification of the Spirit, for obedience and sprinkling of the blood of Jesus Christ: Grace to you and peace be multiplied.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Born Again to a Living Hope

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:4; Romans 15:13"> Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who according to His great mercy has begotten us again to a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Hebrews 9:15; 1 Peter 5:4"> into an inheritance incorruptible and undefiled and unfading, reserved in the heavens for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Ephesians 2:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:13–14"> who by the power of God are being guarded through faith for the salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 5:10; Romans 12:12"> In which you greatly rejoice, though for a little while now, if need be, you have been distressed by various trials,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 23:10; Isaiah 48:10; 1 Peter 4:12"> so that the genuineness of your faith, which is more precious than gold that perishes, though being tested with fire, may be found to praise, honor, and glory at the revelation of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 20:29; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Romans 15:13"> Whom not having seen, you love, in whom now not seeing, but believing, you rejoice with inexpressible joy and glorified,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 6:22; James 1:21; Hebrews 11:13"> obtaining for yourselves the outcome your faith—the salvation of your souls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:17; Luke 10:24; Luke 24:25–27"> Concerning this salvation the prophets have sought out and inquired carefully, having prophesied of the grace that would come to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 52:13–14; 2 Peter 1:21; Luke 24:25–27"> searching for who or what kind of time the Spirit of Christ in them was revealing when He testified beforehand the sufferings of Christ and the glories which were to follow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 15:26; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Ephesians 3:10"> To them it was revealed that, not to themselves, but to you they were ministering these things, which things were now announced to you by those who proclaimed the gospel to you by the Holy Spirit sent from heaven—things which angels desire to look into.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Called to be Holy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Luke 12:35; 1 Peter 4:7"> Therefore gird up the loins of your mind, stay sober, put your hope fully in the grace brought to you at the revelation of Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Titus 3:3–5; 1 Peter 4:2–3"> as obedient children, not fashioning yourselves after the former lusts, as you did in your ignorance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:48; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Hebrews 12:14"> but as He who called you is holy, you also be holy in all manner of life,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Leviticus 19:2; Leviticus 20:7"> because it is written, “Be holy, for I am holy.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:34–35; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Matthew 6:9"> And if you call on the Father, who without partiality judges according to each one's work, pass the time of your sojourning here in fear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:20; Titus 2:14; Psalms 49:7–8"> knowing that you were not redeemed with corruptible things, like silver or gold, from your futile way of life handed down from your forefathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 1:7; 1 Peter 2:22–24; John 1:29"> but with the precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb blameless and spotless,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:9–10; Hebrews 9:26"> having been foreknown indeed before the foundation of the world, He was made manifest in these last times for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Hebrews 2:9; John 12:44"> who through Him believe in God, who raised Him from the dead and gave Him glory, so that your faith and hope are in God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Enduring Word

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Hebrews 13:1; Romans 12:9–10"> Since you have purified your souls by obedience to the truth through the Spirit in sincere love for the brothers, love one another fervently from a pure heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; Hebrews 4:12; John 1:13"> having been born again, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, through the word of God which lives and abides forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; Psalms 103:15; James 4:14"> For<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“All flesh is as grass,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and all the glory of man is as the flower of the grass.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The grass withers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and its flower falls away,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Matthew 5:18; Psalms 119:89"> <span className="pl pl-1">but the word of the Lord endures forever.”</span><br />Now this is the word which was proclaimed to you.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
