## A Living Stone and a Holy People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ephesians 4:31; James 1:21; James 4:11"> Therefore having put away all malice, all guile, hypocrisy, envy, and all slanders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 5:12–13; Ephesians 4:15; 2 Peter 3:18"> as newborn babes, desire the genuine milk of the word, that by it you may grow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 34:8; Hebrews 6:5–6; Psalms 63:5"> if indeed you have tasted that the Lord is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Acts 4:11–12; 1 Peter 2:7"> Coming to Him as to a living stone, having been rejected indeed by men, but chosen by God and precious,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Peter 2:9; 1 Corinthians 3:9"> you also, as living stones, are being built into a spiritual house, a holy priesthood, to offer up spiritual sacrifices acceptable to God through Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Romans 9:32–33; 1 Peter 2:4"> For it is also contained in the Scripture,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, I place in Zion</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a chief cornerstone, chosen, precious,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he who believes on Him shall by no means be put to shame.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Mark 12:10–11; Acts 4:11–12"> Therefore, to you who believe, He is precious; but to those who are disobedient,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The stone which the builders rejected</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">has become the chief cornerstone,”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; 1 Corinthians 1:23; Romans 9:32–33"> <span className="pl pl-1">and “a stone of stumbling</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and a rock of offense.”</span><br />They stumble, being disobedient to the word, to which they also were appointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Deuteronomy 7:6; Revelation 1:6"> But you are a chosen race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for God's own possession, that you may proclaim the excellent virtues of Him who called you out of darkness into His marvelous light;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; Hebrews 4:16; Hosea 1:9–10"> who formerly were not a people but now are the people of God, who had not received mercy, but now have received mercy.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Living Before the World

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; Galatians 5:24; 1 Peter 4:2"> Beloved, I implore you as sojourners and pilgrims, to abstain from the lusts of the flesh which war against the soul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; Matthew 5:16; Titus 2:7–8"> having your manner of life noble among the Gentiles, so that, whenever they speak against you as evildoers, when they observe the good works, they may glorify God in the day of visitation.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Submission to Government

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 3:1; Romans 13:1–7; 1 Timothy 2:1–2"> Therefore subject yourselves to every human institution on account of the Lord: whether to the king as to one having authority,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 13:3–4"> or to governors, as to those being sent for the punishment of evildoers and for the praise of those doing good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17; Titus 2:8; Matthew 7:26"> For this is the will of God, that by doing good you may silence the ignorance of foolish men—</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Romans 6:22; Romans 6:18"> as free, yet not using freedom as a cover for wickedness, but as bondservants of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; Romans 12:10; Romans 13:7"> Honor all people. Love the brotherhood. Fear God. Honor the king.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Submission to Masters

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Titus 2:9–10; Ephesians 6:5–7; Colossians 3:22–25"> Servants, subject yourselves to your masters with all fear, not only to the good and gentle, but also to the crooked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:10–12; 1 Peter 3:14–17; 1 Peter 2:20"> For this is admirable, if because of conscience toward God someone endures pain, suffering unjustly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:19; 1 Peter 3:17; Matthew 5:10–12"> For what glory is it, if when you sin and are beaten, you endure? But if when you do good and suffer for it, you endure, this is admirable before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 16:24; John 13:15"> For to this you were called, because Christ also suffered for us, leaving behind an example for you, that you should follow in His footsteps,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 4:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Who committed no sin,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor was deceit found in His mouth”;</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 Peter 4:19; Hebrews 12:3"> who, being verbally abused, did not return verbal insults, when He suffered, He did not threaten, but committed Himself to Him who judges righteously;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–6; Matthew 8:17; Romans 6:11"> who Himself bore our sins in His body on the tree, in order that having died to sins, we might live unto righteousness—by whose stripes you were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6; Ezekiel 34:6; Luke 15:4–6"> For you were like sheep going astray, but you have turned back now to the Shepherd and Overseer of your souls.</span>
</div>
</div>
