## The Angel and the Little Scroll

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Matthew 17:2; Revelation 1:15–16"> And I saw a mighty angel coming down from heaven, having been clothed with a cloud, and a rainbow on his head, and his face was like the sun, and his feet like pillars of fire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:9–10; Revelation 10:5; Revelation 5:1–5"> and having in his hand a scroll having been opened. And he put his right foot on the sea, and his left foot on the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 31:4; Revelation 4:5; Joel 3:16"> and cried with a loud voice, as when a lion roars. And when he cried out, the seven thunders uttered their own voices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 12:4; Daniel 8:26; Daniel 12:9"> Now when the seven thunders spoke, I was about to write. But I heard a voice from heaven saying, “Seal up the things which the seven thunders have spoken, and do not write them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Deuteronomy 32:40; Revelation 16:17"> And the angel whom I saw standing on the sea and on the land raised his right hand to heaven</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 4:11; Revelation 4:9; Revelation 16:17"> and swore by Him who lives forever and ever, who created the heaven and the things in it, the earth and the things in it, and the sea and the things in it, that there should be no more delay;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 3:3–9; Romans 16:25; Luke 24:44–47"> but in the days of the voice of the seventh angel, whenever he is about to sound his trumpet, the mystery of God would be finished, as He declared to His servants the prophets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 10:2; Revelation 10:4–5; Isaiah 30:21"> Then the voice which I heard from heaven was speaking with me again and saying, “Go, take the little scroll which was open in the hand of the angel standing on the sea and on the land.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:1–3; Jeremiah 15:16; Ezekiel 2:8"> And I went to the angel, saying to him to give to me the little scroll. And he said to me, “Take it and eat it; and it shall make your stomach bitter, but it shall be as sweet as honey in your mouth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:3; Proverbs 16:24; Psalms 119:103"> Then I took the scroll out of the angel's hand and ate it, and it was as sweet as honey in my mouth. And when I ate it, my stomach was made bitter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:4; Jeremiah 1:9–10; Revelation 17:15"> And he said to me, “You must prophesy again over many peoples, nations, languages, and kings.”</span>
</div>
</div>
