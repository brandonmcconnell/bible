## The Lamb Takes the Scroll

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:9–10; Isaiah 29:11; Revelation 5:7"> And I saw in the right hand of Him that sat on the throne a scroll, having been written inside and outside, and having been sealed with seven seals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 29:11–12; Revelation 5:5; Revelation 10:1"> Then I saw a strong angel proclaiming with a loud voice, “Who is worthy to open the scroll and to break its seals?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 5:13; Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34"> And no one in heaven above or on the earth or under the earth was able to open the scroll, nor to look at it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 12:8–9; Revelation 4:1"> And I began to weep much, because no one was found worthy to open the scroll, nor to look at it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 11:1; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Revelation 22:16"> But one of the elders said to me, “Do not weep. Behold, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has overcome, to open the scroll and its seven seals.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 4:10; John 1:29; Revelation 5:12"> And I saw in the midst of the throne and of the four living beings, and in the midst of the elders, a Lamb standing as if slain, having seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven Spirits of God sent forth into all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 5:1; Revelation 4:2–3"> Then He came and took out of the right hand of Him that sat on the throne.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Worthy is the Lamb

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Revelation 8:3–4; Philippians 2:9–11"> And when He took the scroll, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell down before the Lamb, each having a harp, and golden bowls being filled with incense, which are the prayers of the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; 1 John 1:7; Matthew 26:28"> And they sang a new song, saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are worthy to take the scroll,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And to open its seals;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because You were slain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And You redeemed us to God by Your blood,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Out of every tribe and language and people and nation;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 1:6; Daniel 7:27; Revelation 20:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">And have made them kings and priests to our God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And they will reign on the earth.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 7:10; Hebrews 12:22; Revelation 4:4"> Then I looked, and I heard as it were the voice of many angels around the throne, and the living beings, and the elders. And the number of them was ten thousand times ten thousand, and a thousand thousands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Revelation 19:1; Revelation 4:11"> saying with a loud voice:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Worthy is the Lamb who was slain</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">To receive power and wealth and wisdom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And strength and honor and glory and blessing!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; 1 Chronicles 29:11; Psalms 148:2–13"> And every creature which is in heaven and on the earth and under the earth and on the sea, and all that are in them, I heard saying: “Blessing and honor and glory and power to Him that sits upon the throne, and to the Lamb, forever and ever. Amen!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 19:4; Revelation 4:9–11; Revelation 5:8"> And the four living beings said “Amen!” And the elders fell down and worshiped.</span>
