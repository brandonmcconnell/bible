## The Seven Angels with Seven Plagues

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 26:21; Revelation 15:6–7; Revelation 21:9"> Then I saw another sign in heaven, great and marvelous: seven angels having the seven last plagues, because in them the wrath of God is finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 4:6; Revelation 12:11; Revelation 19:1–7"> And I saw something like a glassy sea having been mixed with fire, and those who prevailed over the beast, and over his image, and over the number of his name, standing on the glassy sea, having the harps of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Exodus 15:1–18; Psalms 139:14"> And they sing the song of Moses, the servant of God, and the song of the Lamb, saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Great and marvelous are Your works,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Lord God Almighty!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Righteous and true are Your ways,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">O King of the nations!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Malachi 1:11; Jeremiah 10:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who shall not fear You, O Lord, and glorify Your name?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For You alone are holy.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For all nations shall come and worship before You,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because Your righteous judgments have been manifested.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Numbers 1:50; Matthew 27:51"> After these things I saw, and the temple of the tabernacle of the testimony in heaven was opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Revelation 15:1; Ezekiel 44:17–18"> And out of the temple came the seven angels having the seven plagues, who were dressed in pure bright linen, and having their chests girded with golden belts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 10:6; Revelation 5:8; Revelation 16:2–1"> Then one of the four living beings gave to the seven angels seven golden bowls full of the wrath of God who lives forever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:4; 1 Kings 8:10–11; Exodus 40:34–35"> And the temple was filled by the smoke from the glory of God and from His power, and no one was able to enter into the temple until the seven plagues of the seven angels were finished.</span>
</div>
