## The River of Life

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 7:17; John 4:14; Psalms 36:8–9"> And he showed me a pure river of water of life, bright as crystal, proceeding from the throne of God and of the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; 1 Peter 2:24; Revelation 2:7"> In the middle of its street, and on both sides of the river was the tree of life, producing twelve fruits, each yielding its fruit every month. And the leaves of the tree are for the healing of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Revelation 21:3–4; John 12:26"> And there shall be no more curse, but the throne of God and of the Lamb shall be in it, and His servants will minister to Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Revelation 14:1; Revelation 3:12"> And they shall see His face, and His name shall be on their foreheads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Daniel 7:18; Revelation 20:4"> And night shall not exist. And they shall need neither lamp nor light of the sun, because the Lord God shall illuminate them. And they shall reign forever and ever.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Time is Near

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:1; Revelation 21:5; Revelation 19:9"> Then he said to me, “These words are faithful and true.” And the Lord, the God of the spirits of the prophets sent His angel to show His servants the things which must come about soon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 1:3; Revelation 3:11; Revelation 22:20"> “Behold, I am coming quickly! Blessed is he that keeps the words of the prophecy of this book.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Revelation 1:1; Revelation 19:19"> And I, John, am the one hearing and seeing these things. And when I heard and when I saw, I fell down to worship before the feet of the angel showing to me these things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Colossians 2:18–19; Revelation 14:7"> Then he said to me, “See that you do not! For I am your fellow servant, and I am of your brothers the prophets, and of those who keep the words of this book. Worship God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 8:26; Revelation 1:3; 1 Peter 4:7"> And he said to me, “Do not seal the words of the prophecy of this book, for the time is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:10; Proverbs 4:18; Ezekiel 3:27"> He who acts unjustly, let him act unjustly still; and the filthy, let him be filthy still; and the righteous, let him be righteousness still; and the holy, let him be sanctified still.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Testifies to the Churches

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Isaiah 62:11; Isaiah 40:10"> “And behold, I am coming quickly, and My reward is with Me, to render to each one according to his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 21:6; Isaiah 44:6"> I am the Alpha and the Omega, the First and the Last, the Beginning and the End.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; 1 John 5:3; Revelation 22:7"> Blessed are those who do His commandments, so that their right shall be to the tree of life, and they may enter by the gates into the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; Galatians 5:19–21; Philippians 3:2"> But outside are dogs and drug users and sexually immoral and murderers and idolaters, and everyone who loves and practices a lie.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; 2 Peter 1:19; Isaiah 11:1"> “I, Jesus, have sent My angel to testify these things to you for the churches. I am the Root and the Stock of David, the Bright and Morning Star.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:14; Revelation 21:6; Isaiah 55:1–3"> And the Spirit and the bride say, “Come!” And let him who hears say, “Come!” And let him who thirsts come. And whosoever will, let him take the water of life freely.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Warning

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:2; Proverbs 30:6"> I testify to everyone hearing the words of the prophecy of this book: If anyone adds to them, may God add to him the plagues written in this book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:2; Luke 11:52; Revelation 22:2"> And if anyone takes away from the words of the book of this prophecy, may God take away his part from the tree of Life, and from the holy city, and from the things written in this book.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 22:7; Hebrews 9:28; Revelation 22:12"> He who testifies to these things says, “Yes, I am coming quickly.”<br />Amen. Yes, come, Lord Jesus!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:18; Romans 16:20; 2 Corinthians 13:14"> The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with all the saints. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
