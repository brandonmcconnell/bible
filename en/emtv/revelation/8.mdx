## The Seventh Seal and the Golden Censer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zechariah 2:13; Habakkuk 2:20; Revelation 6:1"> And when He opened the seventh seal, silence occurred in heaven for about half an hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 1:19; Revelation 11:15; Revelation 8:6–1"> And I saw the seven angels who stood before God, and seven trumpets were given to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 5:8; Revelation 8:4; Psalms 141:2"> Then another angel, having a golden censer, came and stood at the altar. And much incense was given to him, so that he could offer it with the prayers of all the saints upon the golden altar which is before the throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Luke 1:10; Revelation 8:3"> And the smoke of the incense went up with the prayers of the saints out of the hand of the angel before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 4:5; Revelation 11:19; Ezekiel 10:2–7"> And the angel took the censer, and filled it with fire from the altar, and he cast it to the earth. And there were thunders and voices and lightnings and an earthquake.</span>
</div>

## The Seven Trumpets

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 8:2"> So the seven angels who had the seven trumpets prepared themselves to sound the trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Revelation 9:4; Revelation 9:18"> And the first one sounded his trumpet, and there was hail and fire, having been mixed with blood, and they were thrown to the earth; and a third of the earth was burned up, and a third of the trees was burned up, and all green grass was burned up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:25; Exodus 7:17–21; Mark 11:23"> Then the second angel sounded his trumpet, and something like a great burning mountain was cast into the sea, and a third of the sea became blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 16:3; Revelation 8:7; Revelation 8:12"> And a third of the living creatures in the sea died, and a third of the ships were destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 16:4; Revelation 9:1; Isaiah 14:12"> Then the third angel sounded his trumpet, and a great star fell from heaven, burning like a torch, and it fell on a third of the rivers and on the fountains of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:15; Jeremiah 9:15; Deuteronomy 29:18"> And the name of the star is called Wormwood. And a third of the waters turned into wormwood, and many men died from the waters, because they were made bitter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Joel 2:31; Joel 2:10"> Then the fourth angel sounded his trumpet, and a third of the sun was struck, a third of the moon, and a third of the stars, so that a third of them was darkened. A third of the day did not shine, and likewise the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 9:12; Revelation 11:14; Revelation 14:6"> And I saw, and I heard an eagle flying through the midst of heaven, saying with a loud voice, “Woe, woe, woe for those who dwell on the earth, from the remaining blasts of the trumpet of the three angels who are about to sound!”</span>
