## The 144,000 of Israel Sealed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Daniel 7:2; Jeremiah 49:36"> And after this I saw four angels standing at the four corners of the earth, holding the four winds of the earth, so that a wind should not blow on the earth, nor on the sea, nor on any tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; 2 Corinthians 1:22; Revelation 7:3–8"> Then I saw another angel coming up from the east, having the seal of the living God. And he cried with a loud voice to the four angels to whom it had been granted to harm the earth and the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:4; Revelation 22:4; Revelation 9:4"> saying, “Do not harm the earth, nor the sea, nor the trees, until we have sealed the servants of our God on their foreheads.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 14:1; Revelation 14:3; Matthew 19:28"> And I heard the number of those having been sealed, one hundred and forty-four thousand having been sealed out of every tribe of the sons of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 1:2–4; Numbers 10:14–27; Numbers 13:4–16"> of the tribe of Judah twelve thousand having been sealed; of the tribe of Reuben twelve thousand; of the tribe of Gad twelve thousand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 2:36"> of the tribe of Asher twelve thousand; of the tribe of Naphtali twelve thousand; of the tribe of Manasseh twelve thousand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:23; Numbers 26:14; Numbers 25:14"> of the tribe of Simeon twelve thousand; of the tribe of Levi twelve thousand; of the tribe of Issachar twelve thousand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> of the tribe of Zebulun twelve thousand; of the tribe of Joseph twelve thousand; of the tribe of Benjamin twelve thousand, having been sealed.</span>
</div>

## A Great Multitude from Every Nation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; John 12:13; Psalms 117:1–2"> After these things I looked, and behold, a great multitude which no man could number, out of every nation, and from tribes and peoples and languages, standing before the throne and before the Lamb, having been clothed in long white robes, with palm branches in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 19:1; Psalms 3:8; Isaiah 45:21"> And they cried out with a loud voice, saying, “Salvation belongs to our God, to Him who sits on the throne, and to the Lamb!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 5:11–13; Revelation 19:4–6; Revelation 4:10"> And all the angels stood around the throne, and the elders and the four living beings, and they fell down on their faces before His throne and they worshiped God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 5:12–14; Jude 1:25; Psalms 100:4"> saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Amen! Blessing and glory and wisdom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Thanksgiving and honor and power and might,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Be to our God forever and ever. Amen.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Revelation 4:10; Revelation 5:5"> Then one of the elders answered, saying to me, “Who are these arrayed in long white robes, and where did they come from?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 1:7; Revelation 22:14; Hebrews 13:12"> And I said to him, “My lord, you know.” So he said to me, “These are those who come out of the Great Tribulation, and have washed their robes and they made them white in the blood of the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 21:3–4; Revelation 22:3; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> Therefore they are before the throne of God, and they serve Him day and night in His temple. And He that sits on the throne shall dwell among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:10; Psalms 121:6; Matthew 5:6"> They shall neither hunger anymore nor thirst anymore; nor shall the sun by any means strike them, nor any scorching heat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 7:37–38; Psalms 36:8–9; John 4:14"> because the Lamb who is in the midst of the throne shepherds them, and He leads them to the springs of the waters of life. And God will wipe away every tear from their eyes.”</span>
</div>
