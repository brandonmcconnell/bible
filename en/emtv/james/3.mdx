## The Untamable Tongue

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:47–48; 1 Timothy 1:7; 1 Peter 5:3"> My brothers, let not many become teachers, knowing that we shall receive greater judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:26; Proverbs 13:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> For we all stumble in many ways. If anyone does not stumble in word, he is a mature man, able also to bridle the whole body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 32:9; Psalms 39:1; James 1:26"> Consider that we put bits in horses' mouths that they may obey us, and we guide their whole body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 107:25–27; Matthew 8:24; Acts 27:14–38"> Consider also the ships: although they are so large and are driven by strong winds, they are guided by a very small rudder wherever the impulse of the pilot desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:21; Jeremiah 9:3–8; Proverbs 12:18"> Even so the tongue is a small member and it boasts great things.<br />See how great a forest is kindled by such a small fire!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 120:2–3; Proverbs 16:27; Mark 7:15"> And the tongue is a fire, the very world of iniquity. Thus the tongue is set among our members, as that which defiles the whole body, and sets on fire the course of nature; and it is set on fire by hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 5:4"> For every kind of both beasts and birds, of both reptiles and sea creatures, is tamed and has been tamed by mankind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalms 140:3; Psalms 55:21"> But no man can tame the tongue. It is an unrestrainable evil, full of deadly poison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Genesis 1:26–27; Psalms 62:4"> With it we bless our God and Father, and with it we curse men, who are made in the likeness of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:14; 1 Peter 3:9; 1 Corinthians 3:3"> Out of the same mouth come blessings and curses. My brothers, these things ought not to be so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The spring does not pour forth from the same opening both the sweet and the bitter water, does it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:43–44; Matthew 12:33; Matthew 7:16–20"> It is not possible, my brothers, for a fig tree to produce olives, or a vine to produce figs, is it? Thus no spring is able to produce both salt and sweet water.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Wisdom from Above

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; James 3:17; Matthew 5:5"> Is there anyone wise and understanding among you? Let him show by his good conduct that his works are in meekness of wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 3:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4; Philippians 2:3"> But if you have bitter jealousy and strife in your heart, do not boast and lie against the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> This wisdom is not that which comes down from above, but is earthly, sensual, demonic.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 3:14; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> For where jealousy and strife exist, there is turmoil and every evil work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 5:22–23; Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> But the wisdom from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, willing to yield, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality and without hypocrisy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Hosea 10:12; Philippians 1:11"> Now the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace by those making peace.</span>
</div>
