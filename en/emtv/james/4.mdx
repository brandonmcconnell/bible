## Warning Against Worldliness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Galatians 5:17; Romans 7:23"> From where come wars and disputes among you? Is it not from here, from your lusts, which war in your members?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 16:24; Matthew 7:7–8; James 1:5"> You lust and you do not have. You murder and are jealous and you cannot obtain. You fight and make war. You do not have because you do not ask.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 5:14; 1 John 3:22; James 1:6–7"> You ask and do not receive, because you ask with wrong motives, that you may spend it in gratifying your lusts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Romans 8:7; John 15:19"> Adulterers and adulteresses! Do you not know that friendship with the world is enmity with God? Whoever therefore wants to be a friend of the world is constituted an enemy of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 6:19; Numbers 11:29"> Or do you think that the Scripture says in vain, “The Spirit who dwells in us yearns jealously”?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 3:34; 1 Peter 5:5"> But He gives more grace. Therefore He says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“God resists the proud,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but gives grace to the humble.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Humility Cures Worldliness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–9; Ephesians 4:27; Ephesians 6:11–12"> Therefore submit to God. Resist the devil and he will flee from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; Hebrews 10:22; Malachi 3:7"> Draw near to God and He will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners; and purify your hearts, you double-minded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10–11; Matthew 5:4; Luke 6:25"> Lament and mourn and weep! Let your laughter be turned into mourning and your joy into dejection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 147:6; Matthew 23:12; 1 Peter 5:6"> Be humbled before the Lord, and He will exalt you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Do Not Judge a Brother

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 2:1; Romans 2:1; James 5:9"> Do not speak evil of one another, brothers. He who speaks evil against a brother and judges his brother speaks against the law and judges the law. And if you judge the law, you are not a doer of the law but a judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 14:4; Romans 2:1; Matthew 10:28"> There is one Lawgiver, who is able to save and to destroy. But who are you who judges the other?</span>
</div>
</div>

## Boasting About Tomorrow

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Luke 12:17–20; Genesis 11:3–4"> Come now, you who say, “Today and tomorrow let us travel to this city, and let us spend a year there, and let us trade and make a profit”—</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; Psalms 39:5; 1 John 2:17"> you who do not know what will happen tomorrow. For what is your life? For it will be a vapor that appears for a little time, but then also disappears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Hebrews 6:3; Lamentations 3:37"> Instead you ought to say, “If the Lord wills, we shall live and do this or that.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; 1 Corinthians 5:6; Psalms 52:7"> But now you boast in your arrogance. All such boasting is evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:41; John 13:17; John 15:22"> Therefore, to him who knows to do good and does not do it, to him it is sin.</span>
</div>
</div>
