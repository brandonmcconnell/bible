## Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 3:18; Romans 16:23; 1 Corinthians 1:14"> The Elder,<br />To the beloved Gaius, whom I love in truth:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:3–9; 2 Peter 3:18; Colossians 1:4–6"> Beloved, concerning all things I pray you to prosper and be in health, just as your soul prospers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 John 1:4; 2 John 1:2; Psalms 119:11"> For I rejoiced greatly when the brothers came and witnessed to the truth which is in you, just as you walk in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 4:19; 2 Timothy 1:2; Proverbs 23:24"> I have no greater joy than these things, that I hear that my children are walking in truth.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Support and Opposition

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; Luke 12:42; Luke 16:10–12"> Beloved, you do faithfully whatever you work for the brothers and for strangers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; Titus 3:13; Acts 15:3"> who gave testimony concerning your love before the church, with reference to whom you will do well if you send them forth in a manner worthy of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:5; Revelation 2:3; 2 Kings 5:15–16"> for on behalf of the Name they went out, taking nothing from the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–9; 1 Corinthians 16:10–11; 2 Corinthians 7:2–3"> We therefore ought to receive such, in order that we may become fellow workers with the truth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 1:7–16; Luke 22:24–27; Matthew 23:4–8"> I wrote to the church, but Diotrephes, who loves the preeminence over them, does not receive us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 66:5; 2 Corinthians 13:2; Luke 6:22"> On account of this, if I come, I will call to mind his works which he does, slandering us with evil words. And not being content with that, he does not receive the brothers, and hinders those who desire to, casting them out of the church.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–17; Psalms 37:27; Ephesians 5:1"> Beloved, do not imitate the evil, but the good. He that does good is of God; he that does evil has not seen God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; John 21:24; Acts 22:12"> Demetrius has a good testimony with everyone, and of the truth itself. And we also testify, and you know that our testimony is true.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Greetings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 John 1:12"> I had many things to write, but I do not wish to write to you with pen and ink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 43:23; Ephesians 6:23; Romans 16:1–16"> But I wish to see you shortly, and we shall speak face to face. Peace to you. Our friends greet you. Greet the friends by name.</span>
</div>
</div>
