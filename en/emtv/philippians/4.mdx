## Exhortation, Encouragement, and Prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; 1 Corinthians 1:10; Mark 9:50"> I implore Euodia and I implore Syntyche to be of the same mind in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:5; Revelation 13:8; Luke 10:20"> Yes, I ask you also, true companion, help these women who labored together with me in the gospel, and with Clement also, and the rest of my fellow workers, whose names are in the Book of Life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:16–18; Romans 12:12; James 1:2–4"> Rejoice in the Lord always. Again I will say, rejoice!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:8–9; Matthew 6:25; Titus 3:2"> Let your gentleness be known to all men. The Lord is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; 1 Peter 5:7; Matthew 6:25–33"> Be anxious for nothing, but in everything by prayer and petition, along with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 14:27; Isaiah 26:3; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> and the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and minds in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 3:17; Titus 2:7; Romans 12:9–21"> Finally, brothers, as many things as are true, as many as are honorable, as many as are just, as many as are pure, as many as are pleasing, as many as are lovely—if there is any virtue and if there is any praise—meditate on these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 1:22; Philippians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–8"> The things which you learned and received and heard and saw in me, practice these things; and the God of peace shall be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; Galatians 6:6; 2 Corinthians 7:6–7"> But I rejoiced in the Lord greatly that now once again you have revived your mindfulness on my behalf, inasmuch as indeed you were mindful, but you lacked opportunity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–9; Hebrews 13:5–6; Matthew 6:31–34"> Not that I am speaking with respect to need, for I have learned to be content in whatever state I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; 2 Corinthians 12:7–10; 2 Corinthians 11:27"> I know how to be brought low, and I know how to live in prosperity. In every place and in all circumstances I have learned both to be full and to be hungry, both to abound and to be in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Colossians 1:11"> I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Philippians 1:7; Hebrews 10:34"> Nevertheless you have done well that you shared in my affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 11:8–12; Acts 16:40–5"> Now you Philippians also know that in the beginning of the gospel, when I went out from Macedonia, no church shared with me in an accounting of giving and receiving except you only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; Acts 17:1; 1 Thessalonians 2:18"> For even in Thessalonica, both once and again you sent help for my needs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Titus 3:14; Hebrews 6:10; 2 Corinthians 9:9–13"> Not that I seek the gift, but I seek the fruit which is increasing to your account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Philippians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 9:12"> And I have all things and abound. I have been made full, having received from Epaphroditus the things sent from you, a fragrant aroma, an acceptable sacrifice, well pleasing to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8–11; Psalms 84:11; 1 Peter 5:10"> And my God will supply all your need according to His riches in glory in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Psalms 115:1; Revelation 14:7"> Now to our God and Father be glory forever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Greetings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 1:2; Philemon 1:23–24; Colossians 4:10–14"> Greet every saint in Christ Jesus. The brothers who are with me greet you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 1:13; 2 Corinthians 13:13; Romans 16:16"> All the saints greet you, but especially those of the household of Caesar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:14; Romans 16:20; Romans 16:23"> The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen.</span>
