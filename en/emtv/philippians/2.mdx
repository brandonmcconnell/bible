## Unity Through Humility

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:12; 1 John 4:12; 1 John 4:7–8"> Therefore if there is any consolation in Christ, if any comfort of love, if any fellowship of the Spirit, if any affection and acts of compassion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 12:16; 1 Peter 3:8–9; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> complete my joy by being of the same mind, having the same love, being united in spirit, intent on one purpose,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 12:10; Galatians 5:26; Ephesians 5:21"> doing nothing according to selfish ambition or conceit, but with humility, regarding one another as being better than yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Romans 15:1; James 2:8"> Let each of you look out not only for his own concerns, but also for the concerns of others.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Humbled and Exalted Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 11:29; 1 Peter 2:21"> Let this mind be in you which was also in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 5:18; 2 Corinthians 4:4; Hebrews 1:3"> who, existing in the form of God, thought it not robbery to be equal with God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:9; Matthew 20:28; John 1:14"> but made Himself of no reputation, taking the form of a bondservant, and coming to be in the likeness of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:18; Hebrews 12:2; Romans 5:19"> And being found in appearance as a man, He humbled Himself, becoming obedient unto death, even the death of the cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Matthew 28:18; 1 Peter 3:22"> Therefore God also has highly exalted Him, and has bestowed on Him the name which is above every name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 45:23–25; Matthew 28:18; Revelation 5:13–14"> that at the name of Jesus every knee will bow, of those in heaven, and of those on earth, and of those under the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Romans 14:11; John 13:13"> and that every tongue will confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Lights in the World

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–10; Luke 13:23–24; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> So then, my beloved, just as you have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:21; 2 Corinthians 3:5; 2 Timothy 1:9"> for it is God who is at work in you, both to will and to do for His good pleasure.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 4:9; 1 Corinthians 10:10; Philippians 2:3"> Do all things without grumbling and disputing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:14–16; 1 Peter 2:12; Matthew 5:45"> in order that you may become blameless and innocent, children of God above reproach in the midst of a crooked and perverted generation, among whom you shine as light bearers in the world,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:26; Galatians 2:2; 1 John 1:1"> holding fast the word of life, so that I may rejoice in the day of Christ, that I have not run in vain or labored in vain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6; Romans 15:16; Hebrews 13:15–16"> But even if I am being poured upon the sacrifice and service of your faith, I rejoice; and I rejoice with you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 1:2–4; Philippians 4:4; Philippians 3:1"> Likewise you also rejoice, and rejoice together with me.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul Commends Timothy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:17; 1 Thessalonians 3:6–8; 1 Thessalonians 3:2"> Now I hope in the Lord Jesus to send Timothy to you shortly, that I also may be encouraged when I learn of your circumstances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Philippians 2:2; 1 Corinthians 16:10; Philippians 2:22"> For I have no one like-minded, who will be genuinely concerned about your circumstances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Matthew 16:24; Philippians 2:4"> For all seek their own interests, not the things of Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Corinthians 2:9; 2 Timothy 1:2"> And you know his approved character, that as a child with his father he served with me as a slave for the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:3; Philippians 2:19"> Therefore I hope to send him at once, as soon as I may see how my circumstances go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="3 John 1:14; Philemon 1:22; Philippians 1:25–26"> But I trust in the Lord that I myself will also come shortly.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul Commends Epaphroditus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 4:18; Philemon 1:1–2; Philippians 4:3"> Yet I considered it necessary to send to you Epaphroditus, my brother, fellow worker, and fellow soldier, and your messenger and minister to my needs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Proverbs 12:25; Ephesians 3:13"> since he was longing for you all, and was distressed because you had heard that he was sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Philippians 2:30; Psalms 103:3–4"> For indeed he was sick, and near death; but God had mercy on him, and not only on him but on me also, lest I should have sorrow upon sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 16:22; 2 Timothy 1:4; Philippians 2:26–27"> Therefore I sent him more eagerly, in order that when you see him again you may have joy, and I may be less anxious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12; 1 Corinthians 16:18; 1 Timothy 5:17"> Receive him therefore in the Lord with the utmost joy, and honor such men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:17; Acts 20:24; Philippians 4:10"> because for the work of Christ he drew near unto death, being careless with his life, in order that he might fill up that which was lacking in your service to me.</span>
</div>
</div>
