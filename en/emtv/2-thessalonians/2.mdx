## The Great Falling Away and the Man of Sin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Mark 13:27; Matthew 24:31"> Now, brothers, concerning the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our gathering together to Him, we beseech you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:1–2; Mark 13:7; Matthew 24:4–6"> not to be quickly shaken from your mind, nor be disturbed, neither by spirit nor by word nor by letter, as if from us, as though the day of Christ has come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 24:4–6; 1 John 2:18; Ephesians 5:6"> Let no one deceive you by any means; for that day will not come unless the falling away comes first, and the man of sin is unveiled, the son of perdition,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Daniel 7:25; Revelation 13:6–7"> who opposes and exalts himself above all that is called God, or every object of worship, so that he sits as God in the temple of God, showing himself that he is God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Peter 1:15; Galatians 5:21; Matthew 16:9"> Do you not remember that while I was still with you, I was telling you these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:3; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> And now you know that which is restraining, that he may be revealed in his own time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 4:3; Revelation 17:7; 2 Timothy 2:17–18"> For the mystery of lawlessness is already at work; only He who now restrains will continue until one arises from out of the midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:3; Revelation 19:20–21; Isaiah 11:4"> And then the lawless one will be unveiled, whom the Lord will consume with the breath of His mouth, and will destroy by the brightness of His coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; Revelation 19:20; Ephesians 2:2"> The coming of the lawless one is according to the working of Satan, with all power, signs, and lying wonders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:18; Proverbs 1:7; 2 Corinthians 4:2–3"> and with all deceit of unrighteousness among those who perish, because they did not receive the love of the truth, that they might be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 1:28; John 12:39–43; 1 Timothy 4:1"> And because of this, God will send them a strong delusion, in order for them to believe the lie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Romans 8:7–8; Mark 16:16"> so that they all might be damned who did not believe the truth, but delighted in unrighteousness.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Stand Firm

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:4–5; 1 Thessalonians 1:4"> But we are obligated to give thanks to God always for you, brothers beloved by the Lord, because God chose you from the beginning for salvation, through sanctification of the Spirit and belief in the truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; 1 Peter 5:10; 1 Thessalonians 1:5"> to which He called you through our gospel, for the obtaining of the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:2; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 2 Thessalonians 2:2"> So then, brothers, stand fast and hold to the traditions which you were taught, whether by word or our letter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 5:2–5; John 3:16; 1 Peter 1:3–8"> Now may our Lord Jesus Christ Himself, and our God and Father, who loved us and gave us eternal comfort and a good hope by grace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:2"> comfort your hearts and establish you in every good word and work.</span>
</div>
</div>
