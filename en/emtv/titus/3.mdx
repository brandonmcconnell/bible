## Be Ready for Every Good Work

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 13:1–7; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> Remind them to be subject to rulers and authorities, to be obedient, to be ready for every good work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:31; 2 Timothy 2:24–25; Ephesians 4:2"> to slander no one, to be peaceable, gentle, demonstrating all meekness to all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–3; Romans 6:17; Colossians 1:21"> For we ourselves were also once foolish, disobedient, being deceived, being enslaved to various lusts and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hateful, and hating one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–10; Titus 2:10–11; 1 Timothy 4:10"> But when the kindness and the love of God our Savior toward man appeared,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8–9; 2 Timothy 1:9; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> not by works of righteousness which we have done, but according to His mercy He saved us, through the washing of regeneration and renewing of the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Romans 5:5; Acts 10:45"> whom He poured out on us richly through Jesus Christ our Savior,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Titus 2:11; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> that having been justified by His grace we might become heirs according to the hope of eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Titus 3:14; Titus 1:9; Psalms 16:2–3"> Faithful is this word, and concerning these things I want you to strongly affirm, so that those who have believed in God may take thought to maintain good works. These things are good and profitable to men.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Avoid Dissension

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:14; 2 Timothy 2:23; 1 Timothy 4:7"> But avoid foolish disputes and genealogies, and strifes and legal fights; for they are unprofitable and futile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 John 1:10; Matthew 18:15–17"> Reject a divisive man after the first and second admonition,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26; Luke 19:22; Matthew 25:26–28"> knowing that such a person has been perverted and is sinning, being self-condemned.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Instructions

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:9; 2 Timothy 4:12; 1 Corinthians 16:8–9"> Whenever I shall send Artemas to you, or Tychicus, hasten to come to me at Nicopolis, for I have decided to spend the winter there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 18:24; Matthew 22:35; 3 John 1:6–8"> Speedily send forward Zenas the lawyer and Apollos, in order that they may lack nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Titus 3:8; Matthew 7:19"> And let our people also learn to maintain good works, to meet urgent needs, lest they be unfruitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Ephesians 6:23–24; 2 John 1:1–2"> All who are with me greet you. Greet those who love us in the faith. Grace be with you all. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
