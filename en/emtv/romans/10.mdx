## Israel Needs the Gospel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:20–22; Romans 9:1–3; John 5:34"> Brothers, the good pleasure of my heart and my supplication to God on behalf of Israel, is for their salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 21:20; Romans 9:31–32; Proverbs 19:2"> For I testify concerning them, that they have a zeal for God, but not according to knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Luke 16:15; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For they, being ignorant of the righteousness of God, and seeking to establish their own righteousness, have not submitted themselves unto the righteousness of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 3:24; Romans 8:3–4; Matthew 5:17–18"> For Christ is the end of the law for righteousness to everyone that believes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Nehemiah 9:29"> For Moses writes about the righteousness which is of the law, “That the man who does those things shall live by them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:11–14; Proverbs 30:4; Ephesians 4:8–10"> But the righteousness of faith says, “Do not say in your heart, ‘Who shall ascend into heaven?’ ” (that is, to bring Christ down)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Revelation 1:18; Romans 4:25"> or, “ ‘Who shall descend into the abyss?’ ” (that is, to bring up Christ from the dead).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:14; Romans 1:16–17; 1 Timothy 4:6"> But what does it say? “The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart” (that is, the word of faith which we proclaim):</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 12:8; Acts 16:31; Philippians 2:11"> that if you confess with your mouth the Lord Jesus, and you believe in your heart that God has raised Him from the dead, you shall be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Romans 10:9; Galatians 2:16"> For with the heart one believes unto righteousness, and with the mouth confession is made unto salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Romans 9:33; 1 Peter 2:6"> For the Scripture says, “Whoever believes on Him shall not be put to shame.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; Revelation 17:14; Galatians 3:28"> For there is no distinction between Jew and Greek, for the same Lord of all is rich to all who call on Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:21; Joel 2:32"> For “whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Israel Rejects the Gospel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 26:17–18; Titus 1:3"> How then shall they call on Him in whom they have not believed? And how are they to believe in Him of whom they have not heard? And how shall they hear apart from a preacher?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Nahum 1:15; Ephesians 2:17"> And how shall they preach unless they are sent? Just as it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“How beautiful are the feet of those who preach the gospel of peace,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of those preaching the gospel of good things!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 3:3; John 12:38–40"> But they have not all obeyed the gospel. For Isaiah says, “Lord, who has believed our report?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 3:2; Galatians 3:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:13"> So then faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 19:4; Romans 1:8; Matthew 24:14"> But I say, have they not heard? Yes, indeed they have:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Their voice went out to all the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their words to the ends of the world.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Romans 11:11; Romans 11:14"> But I say, did Israel not know? First Moses says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will provoke you to jealousy by those who are not a nation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">by a foolish nation I will anger you.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:1–2; Romans 9:30; Isaiah 55:4–5"> But Isaiah is very bold and says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I was found by those who did not seek Me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I was made manifest to those who did not ask for Me.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 65:2–5; 1 Peter 2:8; Jeremiah 35:15"> But to Israel he says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“the whole day long I have stretched out My hands</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to a disobedient and obstinate people.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>
