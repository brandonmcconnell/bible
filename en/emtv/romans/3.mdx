## God's Judgment Defended

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 2:25–29; Genesis 25:32; Ecclesiastes 6:11"> What then is the superiority of the Jew, or what is the profit of circumcision?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 9:4; Acts 7:38; 1 Corinthians 9:17"> Much in every way! First of all, that they were entrusted with the very words of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:13; Hebrews 4:2; Romans 9:6"> For what if some did not believe? Will their unbelief nullify the faithfulness of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Psalms 116:11; Galatians 2:17"> By no means! Indeed, let God be true but every man a liar, just as it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“That You may be justified in Your words,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and You may prevail when You are judged.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 6:19; Galatians 3:15; Romans 9:18–20"> But if our unrighteousness demonstrates the righteousness of God, what shall we say? Surely God, the One inflicting wrath is not unrighteous, is He? (I speak as a man.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:25; Acts 17:31; Job 8:3"> By no means! For otherwise how shall God judge the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:4; Romans 9:19–20; Acts 13:27–29"> For if by my lie the truth of God has abounded to His glory, why am I also still judged as a sinner?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 6:1; Romans 6:15; Romans 5:20"> And why not say, “Let us do evil so that good may come”?—just as we are slandered, and just as some affirm that we say. The judgment of whom is just.</span>
</div>
</div>

## All Have Sinned

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 6:15; Isaiah 65:5"> What then? Are we any better? Not at all. For we previously charged both Jews and Greeks that they are all under sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 53:1–3; Psalms 14:1–3; Romans 3:23"> Just as it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“There is none righteous, no not one.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 14:2–4; Psalms 53:2; Titus 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is none who understands;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there is none who seeks God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 14:3; Ecclesiastes 7:20; Isaiah 64:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">All have turned aside;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">together they became unprofitable;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there is not one doing kindness, there is not so much as one.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:9; Psalms 140:3; Matthew 23:27–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their throat is an opened grave;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with their tongues they deceived;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the poison of asps is under their lips;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 109:17–18; Psalms 59:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7–8; Proverbs 1:16; Proverbs 6:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their feet are swift to shed blood;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ruin and misery are in their ways;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 59:8; Romans 5:1; Luke 1:79"> <span className="pl pl-1">and the way of peace they did not know.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 36:1; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 16:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is no fear of God before their eyes.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Galatians 3:22–23; Romans 2:12–18"> Now we know that as many things as the law says, it speaks to those under the law, so that every mouth may be stopped, and all the world may be held accountable to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Acts 13:39; Romans 3:28"> Therefore by works of the law no flesh shall be justified in His sight, for through the law comes the knowledge of sin.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Righteousness Through Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 10:43; Jeremiah 33:16; Philippians 3:9"> But now the righteousness of God apart from the law has been revealed, being witnessed by the Law and the Prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Galatians 3:28; Romans 8:1"> even the righteousness of God, through faith<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, faith of Jesus Christ">a</a></sup>in Jesus Christ, to all and upon all those that believe. For there is no distinction;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; 1 John 1:8–10; Romans 3:9"> for all have sinned and come short of the glory of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; Titus 3:5–7; Colossians 1:14"> being justified freely by His grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 3:23–24; 1 Peter 1:18–20; 1 John 4:10"> whom God set forth as a propitiation through faith in His blood, for a demonstration of His righteousness through the passing over of the sins having previously committed, in the forbearance of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Romans 4:5; Romans 3:30"> for a demonstration of His righteousness in the present time, that He might be just and the justifier of he who has faith in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 4:2; Romans 2:23; 1 Corinthians 1:29–31"> Where then is boasting? It is excluded. Through what law? Of works? No, but through the law of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Galatians 3:8; Titus 3:7"> Therefore we conclude that a man is justified by faith apart from works of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Ephesians 3:6; Zechariah 2:11"> Or is He the God of the Jews only? But is He not also the God of the Gentiles? Yes, of the Gentiles also,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Galatians 3:20; Philippians 3:3"> since there is one God who will justify the circumcision by faith, and the uncircumcised through faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Matthew 5:17; Romans 13:8–10"> Therefore do we nullify the law through faith? By no means! On the contrary, we uphold the law.</span>
</div>
</div>
