## Israel's Rejection of Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:7; Romans 1:9; Galatians 1:20"> I am speaking the truth in Christ, I am not lying, my conscience witnessing with me in the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:18; Psalms 119:136; Jeremiah 13:17"> that I have great sorrow and unceasing pain in my heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 32:32; Esther 8:6; Romans 11:14"> For I could wish that I myself to be accursed from Christ on behalf of my brothers, my kinsmen according to the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:12; Psalms 147:19; Romans 9:6"> who are Israelites, of whom are the adoption, and the glory, and the covenants, and the law-giving, and the worship, and the promises;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 10:30; John 1:1–3; Colossians 1:16–19"> of whom are the fathers and from whom Christ came, according to the flesh, He who is God over all, blessed forever. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Israel's Rejection and God's Purpose

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 2:28–29; Galatians 6:16; Romans 11:1–2"> But it is not as though the word of God has failed. For not all those of Israel are of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 21:12; Hebrews 11:18; John 8:37–39"> nor are they all children because they are the seed of Abraham; but, “In Isaac your descendants shall be called.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:13; Romans 4:11–16; Galatians 3:26–29"> That is, those who are the children of the flesh are not the children of God; but it is the children of the promise that are regarded as descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 18:10; Genesis 18:14; Genesis 17:21"> For this is the word of promise: “At this time I will come and Sarah shall have a son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 25:21–23; Luke 16:26; Romans 5:11"> And not only this, but also Rebecca, having conceived from the one man, our father Isaac;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:4–5; 2 Peter 1:10"> (for the children not yet being born, nor having done anything good or evil, that the purpose of God according to election might stand, not of works but of Him who calls),</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 25:22–23; 2 Samuel 8:14; 1 Kings 22:47"> it was said to her, “The older shall serve the younger.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 1:2–3; Deuteronomy 21:15; Genesis 29:33"> As it is written, “Jacob I loved, but Esau I hated.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Israel's Rejection and God's Justice

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:7; Psalms 145:17; Deuteronomy 32:4"> What shall we say then? Is there unrighteousness with God? By no means!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 33:19; Romans 9:16; Micah 7:18"> For He says to Moses, “I will have mercy on whomever I will have mercy, and I will have compassion on whomever I will have compassion.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8; Matthew 11:25–26; 1 Peter 2:9–10"> So then it is not of him that wills, nor of him that runs, but of God who shows mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Proverbs 16:4; Exodus 14:17–18"> For the Scripture says to Pharaoh, “For this very purpose I have raised you up, that I may show My power in you, and that My name may be proclaimed in all the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 11:20; Exodus 4:21; Romans 9:15–16"> So then He has mercy on whom He wills, and whom He wills He hardens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:35; 2 Chronicles 20:6; 1 Corinthians 15:12"> You will say to me then, “Why does He still find fault? For who can resist His will?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 29:16; Isaiah 45:9–11; Isaiah 64:8"> But indeed, O man, who are you to be answering back against God? Shall the thing formed say to him who formed it, “Why have you made me like this?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Proverbs 16:4; 2 Timothy 2:20–21"> Or does not the potter have the right over the clay, from the same lump to make one vessel for honor and the other for dishonor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; Jude 1:4; Romans 9:21"> But what if God, wanting to show His wrath, and to make His power known, endured with much longsuffering the vessels of wrath prepared for destruction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 2:4; 1 Peter 1:2–5; Colossians 1:27"> and so that He might make known the riches of His glory on the vessels of mercy, which He had prepared beforehand for glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Acts 13:47–48; Ephesians 3:6–8"> even us whom He called, not only of the Jews, but also of the Gentiles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; 1 Peter 2:10; John 16:27"> As also in Hosea He says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will call those My people who were not My people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and her beloved, who was not beloved.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 1:9–10; 1 John 3:1–3; Galatians 3:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">“And it shall be in the place where it was said to them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘You are not My people,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there they shall be called sons of the living God.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 10:20–23; Romans 11:4–6; Isaiah 1:9"> Isaiah also cries out concerning Israel:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Though the number of the sons of Israel be as the sand of the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the remnant shall be saved;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 10:23; Revelation 19:11; Acts 17:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">For He is finishing and cutting short an account in righteousness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because the Lord will make a short account upon the earth.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Isaiah 13:19; Amos 4:11"> And just as Isaiah foretold:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“If the Lord of hosts had not left us a seed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">we would have become like Sodom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and would have resembled Gomorrah.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Israel's Unbelief

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Hebrews 11:7; Galatians 2:16"> What shall we say then? That Gentiles, who did not pursue righteousness, have attained righteousness, even the righteousness of faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 11:7; Romans 3:20; James 2:10–11"> but Israel, pursuing a law of righteousness, did not attain to the law of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Peter 2:8; Luke 7:23"> Why? Because they did not seek it by faith, but rather by the works of the law. For they stumbled at that stone of stumbling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Isaiah 8:14–15; Romans 10:11"> Just as it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, I lay in Zion a stone of stumbling and a rock of offense,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and everyone believing on Him will not be put to shame.”</span></span>
</div>
