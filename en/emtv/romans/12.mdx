## Living Sacrifices

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Romans 6:13; Hebrews 13:15–16"> Therefore, brothers, I exhort you through the compassions of God, to present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, well pleasing to God, which is your spiritual act of worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 1:14; Ephesians 4:22–24; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> And do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, that you may prove what is that good and well pleasing and perfect will of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Serve God with Spiritual Gifts

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 6:3; Philippians 2:3–8; Proverbs 26:12"> For I say, through the grace given to me, to everyone who is among you, not to think of himself too highly, beyond what you should think, but to think soberly, as God has imparted to each one a measure of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; 1 Corinthians 12:12–14; Ephesians 4:15–16"> For just as we have many members in one body, but all the members do not have the same function,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:20; 1 Corinthians 10:17; 1 Corinthians 12:27–28"> so we, being many, are one body in Christ, and individually members of one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10–11; 1 Corinthians 7:7; 1 Corinthians 12:28–31"> Having then gifts differing according to the grace having been given to us, let us use them: whether prophecy, let us prophesy according to the proportion of faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11; 2 Timothy 4:2; Acts 13:1"> whether ministry, let us use it in our ministry; whether teaching; in the teaching;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Timothy 5:17; 1 Corinthians 12:28"> whether exhorting, in the exhortation; whether sharing, with simplicity; whether leading, with diligence; whether showing mercy, with cheerfulness.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Love

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 34:14; 1 Peter 4:8; 1 Timothy 1:5"> Let love be without hypocrisy. Hate that which is evil. Cleave to that which is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; John 13:34–35; Hebrews 13:1"> Be warmly affectionate to one another with brotherly love, in honor preferring one another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10–11; Acts 18:25; Revelation 3:15–16"> not lagging in diligence, fervent in spirit, serving the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Hebrews 10:36; Psalms 62:8"> rejoicing in hope, enduring in tribulation, devoted to prayer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 2 Corinthians 9:12; 1 John 3:17"> sharing in the needs of the saints, pursuing hospitality.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Matthew 5:44; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Bless those that persecute you; bless and do not curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Hebrews 13:3; Psalms 35:13–14"> Rejoice with those who rejoice, and weep with those who weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:8; Proverbs 3:7; James 3:13–17"> Live in harmony with one another. Do not be proud, but rather associate with the humble. Do not be wise in your own opinion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; 1 Thessalonians 5:15; Matthew 5:39"> Do not repay anyone evil for evil; providing that which is good before all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; Matthew 5:9; Romans 14:19"> If possible, as much as depends on you, keeping peace with all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35; Hebrews 10:30; Proverbs 24:29"> Beloved, do not avenge yourselves, but give place to wrath; for it is written, “Vengeance is Mine, I will repay,” says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 25:21–22; Matthew 5:44; Luke 6:27"> Therefore<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“If your enemy should hunger, feed him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">if he should thirst, give him drink;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for by doing this you will heap burning coals upon his head.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Luke 6:27–30; Proverbs 16:32"> Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.</span>
</div>
</div>
