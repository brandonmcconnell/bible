## Israel's Rejection Not Total

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:22; 1 Samuel 12:22; Philippians 3:5"> I say then, has God cast away His people? By no means! For I too am an Israelite, of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:14; Romans 8:29–30; Exodus 32:1"> God has not cast away His people whom He foreknew. Or do you not know what the Scripture says in Elijah, how he pleads with God against Israel, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10–18; 1 Kings 18:13; Nehemiah 9:26"> “Lord, they have killed Your prophets and torn down Your altars, and I alone am left, and they are seeking my life”?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 19:18; Numbers 25:3; Zephaniah 1:4"> But what is God's reply to him? “I have reserved for Myself seven thousand men who have not bowed the knee to Baal.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 9:27; Ephesians 1:5–6; Romans 9:11"> So too then, at this present time, there is also a remnant according to the election of grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Romans 4:4–5; 2 Timothy 1:9"> And if by grace, it is no longer of works; otherwise grace is no longer grace. But if it is of works, it is no longer grace; otherwise work is no longer work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Romans 11:5; 2 Corinthians 4:4"> What then? Israel has not obtained what it seeks; but the elect have obtained it, and the rest were blinded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:4; Isaiah 29:10; Ezekiel 12:2"> Just as it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“God has given them a spirit of stupor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">eyes that they should not see,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and ears that they should not hear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">down to this very day.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 69:22–23; Luke 12:20; Psalms 28:4"> And David says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Let their table become a snare and a trap,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a stumbling block and a recompense to them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 69:23; Deuteronomy 28:64–68; 2 Peter 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let their eyes be darkened so that they cannot see,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their backs be bent forever!”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Israel's Rejection Not Permanent

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 13:46–48; Romans 11:31; Romans 10:19"> I say then, have they stumbled that they should fall? By no means! But by their transgression, salvation has come to the Gentiles, to provoke them to jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:25; Colossians 1:27; Romans 11:15"> But if their transgression is riches for the world, and their defeat is riches for the Gentiles, how much more will their fullness be!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 2 Timothy 1:11–12; Ephesians 3:8"> For I speak to you Gentiles; inasmuch as I am indeed an apostle to the Gentiles, I magnify my ministry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:16; 1 Timothy 4:16; Romans 9:3"> if somehow I may provoke to jealousy my fellow Jews, and thus save some of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 1:10; Romans 5:10–11; Colossians 1:20–21"> For if their casting away means reconciliation for the world, what will their acceptance be, if not life from the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:30; James 1:18; Proverbs 3:9"> For if the firstfruit is holy, so also the lump; and if the root is holy, so also the branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 2:11–13; Ephesians 3:6; Matthew 21:43"> But if some of the branches were broken off, and you, being a wild olive tree, were grafted in among them, and became a partaker of the root and of the fatness of the olive tree,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:12; Romans 11:20; Ephesians 2:19–20"> do not be arrogant towards the branches. But if you are arrogant, remember that it is not you that supports the root, but the root supports you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 11:17; Romans 11:11–12; Romans 11:23–24"> You will say then, “Branches were broken off so that I might be grafted in.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:16; 2 Corinthians 1:24; 1 Corinthians 10:12"> That is true. By unbelief they were broken off, but you stand by faith. Do not be arrogant, but fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4–9; Jeremiah 25:29; Jeremiah 49:12"> For if God did not spare the natural branches, perhaps He may not spare you either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 3:6; 1 Corinthians 15:2"> Behold then the kindness and severity of God: upon those that fell, severity; but upon you, kindness, if you continue in His kindness. Otherwise you also shall be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:16; Zechariah 12:10; Matthew 23:39"> And they also, if they do not remain in unbelief, shall be grafted in, for God is able to graft them in again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:17–18; Romans 11:30"> For if you were cut out of the olive tree which is wild by nature, and were grafted contrary to nature into a cultivated olive tree, how much more shall these, the natural ones, be grafted into their own olive tree?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 21:24; 2 Corinthians 3:14–16; Romans 16:25"> For I do not want you to be unaware of this mystery, brothers, lest you be wise in your own opinion, that blindness in part has happened to Israel until the fullness of the Gentiles has come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 59:20; Jeremiah 31:31–37; Psalms 14:7"> And so all Israel shall be saved, as it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The Deliverer shall come out of Zion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and He shall turn away ungodliness from Jacob;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Isaiah 59:21; Ezekiel 36:25–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">For this is My covenant with them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I take away their sins.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:15; Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Matthew 21:43"> As regards to the gospel, they are enemies for your sake; but as regards to election, they are beloved for the sake of the fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 7:21; Numbers 23:19; Malachi 3:6"> For the gifts and the calling of God are irrevocable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 3:7; Ephesians 2:1–2; 1 Corinthians 7:25"> For just as you once were disobedient to God, but now you were shown mercy through their disobedience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 11:15; Romans 10:16; Romans 11:25"> even so these now were disobedient, that through the mercy shown you they also might be shown mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 3:9; 1 Timothy 2:4–6"> For God has consigned all to disobedience, that He might show mercy to all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 5:9; Job 11:7–9; Psalms 92:5"> Oh, the depth of the riches both of the wisdom and knowledge of God! How unsearchable are His judgments and untraceable are His ways!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Job 15:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">“For who has known the mind of the Lord?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or who has been His counselor?”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 41:11; Job 35:7; 1 Corinthians 4:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Or who has first given to Him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and it shall be repaid to him?”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 2:10"> For from Him and through Him and to Him are all things; to Him be the glory forever! Amen.</span>
</div>
