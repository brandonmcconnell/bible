## Submit to Government

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 3:1; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Daniel 2:21"> Let every person submit to the governing authorities. For there is no authority except by God, and the authorities that exist are established by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:13; Titus 3:1; Romans 13:5"> Consequently the one resisting the authority has opposed the ordinance of God, and those opposing shall receive judgment to themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:4–6; 1 Peter 2:13–14; Proverbs 20:2"> For rulers are not a fear to good works, but to evil. And do you wish not to fear the authority? Do what is good, and you shall have praise from the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 12:19; 2 Chronicles 19:6; Proverbs 16:14"> For he is God's servant to you for good. But if you do evil, fear; for he does not bear the sword in vain; for he is God's servant, an avenger for wrath to the one practicing evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:2; Acts 24:16; 1 Peter 2:19"> Therefore it is necessary to be subject, not only on account of wrath, but also for conscience' sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:17–21; Mark 12:14–17; Matthew 17:24–27"> For on account of this you also pay taxes, for they are ministers of God, always giving attention to this very thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; 1 Peter 2:17–18; Luke 20:25"> Render therefore to all their dues: taxes to whom taxes are due, customs to whom customs, fear to whom fear, honor to whom honor.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Love Your Neighbor

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:10; Matthew 22:39–40; Galatians 5:14"> Owe nothing to no one, except to love one another, for he who loves one another has fulfilled the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Matthew 22:39; Luke 10:27"> For, “You shall not commit adultery,” “You shall not murder,” “You shall not steal,” “You shall not covet,” and if there is any other commandment, in this word it is summed up, namely, “You shall love your neighbor as yourself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:39–40; Romans 13:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> Love does no harm to a neighbor; therefore love is the fulfillment of the law.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Put on Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Peter 4:7; Ephesians 5:14"> And do this, knowing the time, that it is already the hour for us to be roused out of sleep; for now our salvation is nearer than when we first believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:31–32; Ephesians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 6:7"> The night is advanced, the day is at hand. Therefore let us put off the works of darkness, and let us put on the armor of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:21; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–5"> Let us walk properly, as in the day, not in revelries and drinking bouts, not in orgies and debaucheries, not in strife and jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Galatians 5:16–17; Galatians 3:27"> But put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the flesh, for its lusts.</span>
</div>
</div>
