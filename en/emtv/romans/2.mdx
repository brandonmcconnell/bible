## God's Righteous Judgment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 6:37; Romans 2:3; John 8:7–9"> Therefore you are inexcusable, O man, everyone who judges, for in whatever you judge another you condemn yourself, for you who judge are practicing the same things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 9:7–8; Zephaniah 3:5; Psalms 145:17"> But we know that the judgment of God is according to truth upon those practicing such things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 16:5; Luke 12:14; Romans 1:32–1"> But do you suppose this, O man, you who judge those practicing such things yet doing them, that you will escape the judgment of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Isaiah 30:18; Exodus 34:6"> Or do you despise the riches of His kindness and tolerance and patience, not knowing that the kindness of God leads you to repentance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Romans 1:18; Romans 2:2–3"> But according to your hardness and impenitent heart you are storing up for yourself wrath in the day of wrath and revelation, and of the righteous judgment of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 62:12; Jeremiah 32:19; Matthew 16:27"> who “will recompense to each one according to his works”:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:7–8; Luke 8:15; 2 Timothy 1:10"> to those who by the endurance of good works are seeking glory and honor and incorruption—eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:18–21; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; 1 Timothy 6:3–4"> But to those who are contentious, and who disobey the truth, but obey unrighteousness—indignation and wrath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17; Ezekiel 18:4; Romans 1:16"> tribulation and anguish, upon every soul of man that works evil, of the Jew first and also of the Greek;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 32:17; Isaiah 26:12; Numbers 6:26"> but glory, honor, and peace to everyone that works good, both to the Jew first and to the Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Acts 10:34; 2 Chronicles 19:7"> For there is no partiality with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:21; Galatians 3:10; Galatians 2:16–19"> For all who have sinned without the law shall also perish without the law, and all who have sinned under the law shall be judged by the law</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 1:22–25; 1 John 3:7; James 2:21–25"> (for it is not the hearers of the law who are righteous before God, but the doers of the law shall be justified;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 2:12; Romans 1:19–20; Romans 2:27"> for whenever Gentiles, who do not have the law, by nature do what the law requires, these, not having the law, are a law unto themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 1:12; Romans 1:18–19"> who show the work of the law written in their hearts, their conscience witnessing with them, and among themselves their thoughts accuse or even defend them),</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Acts 17:31"> in the day when God shall judge the secrets of men according to my gospel through Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Jews Guilty as the Gentiles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 2:23; Micah 3:11; Isaiah 48:1–2"> Behold, you are called a Jew, and rely on the law, and boast in God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Deuteronomy 4:8; James 4:17"> and know His will, and approve the things that are more excellent, being instructed out of the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 3:17–18; Matthew 15:14; Isaiah 49:6"> and are convinced that you are a guide to the blind, a light to those in darkness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 1:13"> an instructor of the foolish, a teacher of the immature, having the embodiment of knowledge and of the truth in the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:27; Matthew 23:3–28; Luke 12:47"> You, therefore, who teach another, do you not teach yourself? You who preach that a man should not steal, do you steal?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Malachi 3:8; Matthew 12:39; Mark 11:17"> You who say, “Do not commit adultery,” do you commit adultery? You who abhor idols, do you commit sacrilege?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 4:16–17; Romans 2:17; Romans 3:2"> You who boast in the law, do you dishonor God through the transgression of the law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:20–23; Isaiah 52:5; 2 Peter 2:2"> For “The name of God is blasphemed among the Gentiles because of you,” just as it is written.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Circumcision of No Profit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Galatians 5:3–6; Acts 7:51"> For indeed circumcision profits if you keep the law; but if you are a transgressor of the law, your circumcision has become uncircumcision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 2:11; 1 Corinthians 7:18–19; Romans 8:4"> Therefore, if an uncircumcised man keeps the righteous requirements of the law, will not his uncircumcision be regarded as circumcision?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 12:41–42; Romans 2:29; 2 Corinthians 3:6"> And will not the physically uncircumcised, who keeps the law, judge you along with your written code and circumcision, as a lawbreaker?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; Matthew 3:9; Romans 9:6–8"> For he is not a Jew who is one outwardly, nor is circumcision that which is outward in flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:6; Philippians 3:3; Deuteronomy 10:16"> but he is a Jew that is one inwardly; and circumcision is of the heart, in the spirit, not in the letter, whose praise is not from men but from God.</span>
</div>
</div>
