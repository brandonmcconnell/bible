## Bearing Others' Burdens

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; Romans 14:1; Galatians 6:1–2"> We then who are strong are obligated to bear with the infirmities of the weak, and not to please ourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 14:19; 1 Corinthians 10:24; Philippians 2:4–5"> Let each of us please his neighbor for his good, leading to edification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:9; Philippians 2:8; John 4:34"> For even Christ did not please Himself; but as it is written, “The reproaches of those reproaching You fell on Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Romans 4:23–24"> For as many things as were previously written, were written for our own instruction, that through the patience and encouragement of the Scriptures we may have hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 2:4–5; 1 Peter 3:8; Romans 12:16"> Now may the God of patience and encouragement grant you to be like-minded among one another, according to Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 10:29–30; Revelation 1:6; Ephesians 1:3"> so that you may with one accord and one mouth glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Glorify God Together

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:34; Romans 5:2"> Therefore receive one another, just as also Christ received you, to the glory of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Matthew 15:24; John 1:11"> Now I say that Christ Jesus has become a servant to the circumcision for the truth of God, in order to confirm the promises given to the fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:50; Psalms 18:49; Romans 3:29"> and that the Gentiles might glorify God on behalf of His mercy, as it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“For this reason I will confess to You among the Gentiles,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will sing praise to Your name.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 67:3–4; Deuteronomy 32:43; Isaiah 42:10–12"> And again he says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Rejoice, O Gentiles, with His people!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 117:1"> And again:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Praise the Lord, all you Gentiles!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Praise Him, all you peoples!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 11:10; Isaiah 11:1; Matthew 12:21"> And again, Isaiah says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“There shall be the root of Jesse;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and He that shall rise to rule over the Gentiles,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in Him the Gentiles shall hope.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:16–17; Romans 12:12; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> Now may the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, that you may abound in hope by the power of the Holy Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 2 Peter 1:12; 1 John 2:21"> Now I myself am confident concerning you, my brothers, that you yourselves are full of goodness, having been filled with all knowledge, able also to admonish one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 15:10; Romans 1:5"> And I write more boldly to you brothers, in part, as reminding you, because of the grace given to me by God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:13; Romans 1:1; Acts 9:15"> that I should be a minister of Jesus Christ to the Gentiles, ministering the gospel of God as a priest, so that the offering of the Gentiles might be acceptable, sanctified by the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14–16; 2 Corinthians 12:11–21; Hebrews 5:1"> Therefore I have something to boast of in Christ Jesus in the things pertaining to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 15:12; Romans 1:5; Acts 21:19"> For I will not dare to speak of any of those things which Christ did not work out through me, in word and deed, for the obedience of the Gentiles—</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:12; Hebrews 2:4; Acts 19:11–12"> in mighty signs and wonders, by the power of the Spirit of God, so that from Jerusalem and as far as Illyricum I have fully preached the gospel of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:13–16; 1 Corinthians 3:9–15; Ephesians 2:20–22"> And so eagerly striving to evangelize not where Christ was named, lest I should build upon the foundation of another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 52:15; Isaiah 65:1"> but as it is written: “To whom He was not announced, they shall see, and those who have not heard shall understand.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Plan to Visit Rome

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 1:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:17–18"> For this reason I also have been hindered many times from coming to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 15:32; Romans 1:10–12; Romans 15:29"> But now no longer having a place in these regions, and having a longing to come to you for many years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:28; Romans 1:12; Acts 15:3"> whenever I travel to Spain, I shall come to you. For I hope while traveling through to see you, and to be sent forward there by you, if I may be filled in part from you first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Acts 19:21; Acts 20:22"> But now I am traveling to Jerusalem, ministering to the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:1–9; Acts 11:27–30; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> For Macedonia and Achaia thought it good to make some contribution for the poor among the saints in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:11; Galatians 6:6; Philemon 1:19"> For they thought it good, and they are their debtors. For if the Gentiles shared in spiritual things, they are obligated also to minister to them in material things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 15:24; Philippians 4:17; Colossians 1:6"> Therefore, having finished and having sealed to them this fruit, I shall go by way of you to Spain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 1:10–12; Psalms 16:11"> But I know that when I come to you, I shall come in the fullness of the blessing of the gospel of Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 1:11; 2 Corinthians 4:11"> Now I beseech you, brothers, through the Lord Jesus Christ, and through the love of the Spirit, to strive together with me in prayers to God on my behalf,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:2; 2 Corinthians 8:4; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> that I may be delivered from those in Judea who do not believe, and that my service for Jerusalem may be acceptable to the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:18; Philemon 1:20; Philemon 1:7"> so that in joy I may come to you through the will of God, and may be refreshed together with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; Philippians 4:9; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> Now the God of peace be with you all. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
