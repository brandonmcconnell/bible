## The Law of Liberty

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:1; 1 Corinthians 8:7–13; 1 Corinthians 9:22"> Receive one that is weak in the faith, not for disputes over opinions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; Romans 14:22–23; Romans 14:14"> One indeed believes that he may eat all things, but another, being weak, eats only vegetables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:10; Colossians 2:16–17; Luke 18:9"> Let not him who eats despise him who does not eat, and let not him who does not eat judge him who eats; for God has received him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 4:11–12; Romans 9:20; Romans 8:31–39"> Who are you to judge another's servant? To his own master he stands or falls. And he shall be made to stand, for God is able to make him stand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:23; Galatians 4:9–10"> One indeed judges one day above another; but another judges every day the same. Let each be fully assured in his own mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:30–31; 1 Timothy 4:3–5; Matthew 14:19"> He that regards the day, regards it to the Lord; and he that does not regard the day, to the Lord he does not regard it. And he that eats, eats to the Lord, for he gives thanks to God; and he that does not eat, to the Lord he does not eat, and he gives thanks to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; 2 Corinthians 5:15; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20"> For none of us lives to himself, and no one dies to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:20; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 15:23"> For whether we live, we live to the Lord; and whether we die, we die to the Lord. Therefore, whether we live, or whether we die, we are the Lord's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; Luke 24:26; Hebrews 12:2"> For to this end Christ died and rose and lived again, so that He might be Lord both of the dead and the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> But why do you judge your brother? Or why do you despise your brother? For we shall all stand before the judgment seat of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:22–25; Philippians 2:10–11; Psalms 72:11"> For it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“As I live, says the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">every knee shall bow to Me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and every tongue shall confess to God.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:5; Matthew 12:36; Matthew 16:27"> So then each of us shall give account concerning himself to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; James 4:11; 1 Corinthians 8:9–13"> Therefore let us no longer judge one another, but judge this rather, not to put a stumbling block or an offense before our brother.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Law of Love

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Romans 14:2; 1 Timothy 4:4"> I know and am convinced in the Lord Jesus that nothing is unclean of itself; except to him considering anything to be unclean, to him it is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:11–12; Philippians 2:2–4; Galatians 5:13"> But if your brother is grieved on account of your food, you are no longer walking according to love. Do not destroy with your food the one on behalf of whom Christ died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:22; 1 Corinthians 10:29–30"> Therefore do not let your good be slandered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Romans 15:13; 1 Corinthians 4:20"> For the kingdom of God is not eating and drinking, but righteousness and peace and joy in the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Corinthians 5:11; 1 Peter 2:20"> For he that serves Christ in these things is well pleasing to God and approved by men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Romans 12:18; Psalms 34:14"> Therefore let us pursue the things which make for peace and the things by which one may edify another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 14:14–15; Matthew 18:6; Titus 1:15"> Do not destroy the work of God for the sake of food. All things indeed are clean, but are evil for the man eating with offense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:13; Romans 14:13; Romans 15:1–2"> It is good neither to eat meat nor drink wine nor to do anything by which your brother stumbles or is offended or becomes weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 3:21; Romans 14:5; James 3:13"> Do you have faith? By yourself, have it before God. Blessed is he that does not condemn himself in what he approves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:6; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 11:29–31"> But he who doubts, if he eats, has been condemned, because it is not of faith; and everything which is not of faith is sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Now to Him who is able to establish you according to my gospel and the proclamation of Jesus Christ, according to the revelation of the mystery kept secret since the world began,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> but now has been made manifest, and by the prophetic Scriptures made known to all nations, according to the commandment of the eternal God, for obedience to the faith—</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> To the only wise God, through Jesus Christ, to whom be the glory forever! Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
