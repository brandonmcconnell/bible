## Unity in the Body of Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Philippians 1:27; 1 Thessalonians 2:12"> Therefore I, the prisoner of the Lord, urge you to walk worthy of the calling with which you were called,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; 1 Corinthians 13:7; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5"> with all humility and meekness, with longsuffering, bearing with one another in love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:10; Colossians 3:13–15; 1 Corinthians 12:12–13"> being diligent to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Romans 12:4–5; 1 Corinthians 12:4–13"> There is one body and one Spirit, just as also you were called in one hope of your calling;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:6; 1 Corinthians 12:13; 1 Peter 3:21"> one Lord, one faith, one baptism;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Malachi 2:10; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> one God and Father of all, who is over all, and through all, and in us all.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Spiritual Gifts

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> But to each one of us grace was given according to the measure of the free gift of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 68:18; Colossians 2:15; 1 Samuel 30:26"> Therefore He says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“When He ascended on high,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He led captivity captive,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and gave gifts to men.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:13; Matthew 12:40; Proverbs 30:4"> But that “He ascended”—what is it but that He also first descended into the lower parts of the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 7:26; Hebrews 4:14; Ephesians 1:20–23"> He that descended, He is also the One who ascended far above all the heavens, that He might fill all things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:28–29; Ephesians 2:20"> And He gave some as apostles, and some as prophets, and some as evangelists, and some as pastors and teachers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11–14; Ephesians 4:16; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> for the perfecting of the saints for the work of ministry, for the building up of the body of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:28; John 17:21; Ephesians 4:5"> until we all arrive to the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the Son of God, to a mature man, to the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:9; Romans 16:17–18; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> that we may no longer be infants, being tossed as by waves, and being carried about by every wind of doctrine, by the trickery of men, by craftiness in regard to deceitful scheming,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 3:18; Ephesians 4:25; Zechariah 8:16"> but speaking the truth in love, we may grow up in all things into Him who is the head—Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 2:19; John 15:5; Colossians 2:2"> from whom the whole body, being fitted and held together by what every joint supplies, according to the working of the measure of each individual part, causes the growth of the body for the edification of itself in love.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The New Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:21; 1 Peter 4:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> This I say, therefore, and testify in the Lord, that you should no longer walk as the rest of the Gentiles walk, in the vanity of their mind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Ephesians 2:12; Romans 1:21–23"> having been darkened in understanding, being estranged from the life of God, because of the ignorance which is in them, because of the hardness of their heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Romans 1:24–26; 1 Timothy 4:2"> who, having become callous, gave themselves over to debauchery, for the working of uncleanness with utmost greediness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 2:11–14; John 6:45; Matthew 11:29"> But you have not so learned Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 5:20; Ephesians 1:13; Hebrews 3:7–8"> if indeed you have heard Him and were instructed by Him, just as the truth is in Jesus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Hebrews 12:1; James 1:21"> that you put off, concerning your former way of life, the old man which is being corrupted according to deceitful lusts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Romans 8:6; Psalms 51:10"> and that you be renewed in the spirit of your mind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:10–14; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ephesians 2:10"> and that you put on the new man which was created in the likeness of God, in true righteousness and holiness.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Do Not Grieve the Holy Spirit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Zechariah 8:16; Proverbs 12:22"> Therefore, having put off falsehood, “Let each one speak truth with his neighbor,” for we are members of one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 1:19; Psalms 4:4; Psalms 37:8"> “Be angry, and do not sin”: do not let the sun set on your anger,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 4:7; 1 Peter 5:8; Ephesians 6:16"> nor give opportunity to the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12; Galatians 6:10"> Let him who stole steal no longer, but rather let him labor, working that which is good with his hands, in order that he may have something to share with him who has need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:11; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> Let no corrupt word proceed from your mouth, but only what is good for edification, that it may give grace to them that hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:19; Isaiah 63:10; Ephesians 1:13–14"> And do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, by whom you were sealed for the day of redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Colossians 3:8; Ecclesiastes 7:9; Titus 3:2–3"> Let all bitterness, wrath, anger, clamor, and blasphemy be removed from you, with all malice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; Matthew 6:14–15; Mark 11:25"> And be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, just as also God in Christ forgave us.</span>
</div>
</div>
