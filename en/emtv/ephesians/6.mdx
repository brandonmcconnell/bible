## Children and Parents

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:20–25; Proverbs 23:22; Proverbs 6:20"> Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 27:16; Matthew 15:4–6"> “Honor your father and mother,” which is the first commandment with a promise:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:16; Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 12:28"> “that it may be well with you, and you may live long on the earth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:21; Proverbs 22:6; Proverbs 29:15"> And fathers, do not provoke your children to anger, but bring them up in the discipline and admonition of the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Slaves and Masters

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:18–21; Titus 2:9–10; Colossians 3:17–24"> Slaves, obey your masters according to the flesh with fear and trembling, in simplicity of heart, as to Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:22–23; 1 Thessalonians 2:4; Galatians 1:10"> not with eye service, as men-pleasers, but as slaves of Christ, doing the will of God from your soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 3:23; Ephesians 6:5–6; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> with good will doing service, as to the Lord, and not to men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; Luke 14:14; Proverbs 11:18"> knowing that whatever good thing anyone does, the same he shall receive back from the Lord, whether slave or free.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 3:25–1; Acts 10:34; Romans 2:11"> And masters, do the same things towards them, giving up threatening, knowing that your own Master is in heaven, and there is no partiality with Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Whole Armor of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Isaiah 40:31; Joshua 1:9"> Finally, my brothers, be strong in the Lord and in the power of His might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; Romans 13:12; Ephesians 6:13"> Put on the full armor of God, so that you may be able to stand against the stratagems of the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 2 Corinthians 4:4; Ephesians 2:2"> For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this age, against spiritual forces of evil in the heavenlies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; Luke 21:36; Ephesians 6:11–17"> Because of this, take up the full armor of God, that you may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having accomplished all things, to stand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:8; Isaiah 11:5"> Stand therefore, having girded your waist with truth, having put on the breastplate of righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Romans 10:15; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> and having shod your feet with the preparation of the gospel of peace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–9; 1 John 5:4–5; Proverbs 18:10"> above all, taking up the shield of faith, with which you will be able to quench all the flaming arrows of the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Isaiah 59:17; Revelation 19:15"> And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:17; Colossians 4:2; Philippians 4:6"> through every prayer and petition, praying in every season in the Spirit, being watchful to this same thing with all perseverance and petition concerning all the saints—</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 4:3; Acts 4:29; 2 Thessalonians 3:1"> and for me, that utterance may be given to me in the opening of my mouth, to make known with boldness the mystery of the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; Colossians 4:4; 2 Timothy 2:9"> for the sake of which I serve as an ambassador in chains; that in it I may speak boldly, as I must speak.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Greetings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:4; 2 Timothy 4:12; Titus 3:12"> But that you also may know my circumstances, and how I am doing, Tychicus, a beloved brother and faithful servant in the Lord, will make all things known to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Colossians 4:7–8; 2 Thessalonians 2:17"> whom I have sent to you for this very purpose, that you may know our situation, and that he may comfort your hearts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 6:16; Galatians 5:6; 1 Peter 5:14"> Peace to the brothers, and love with faith, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 22:37; 2 Corinthians 8:12; Matthew 6:13"> Grace be with all those who love our Lord Jesus Christ in sincerity. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
