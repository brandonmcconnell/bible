<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; 2 Corinthians 1:1; 1 Corinthians 1:1–2"> Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God,<br />To the saints who are at Ephesus, and faithful in Christ Jesus:</span>

## Spiritual Blessings in Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; 2 Corinthians 1:3; Ephesians 2:6"> Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us with every spiritual blessing in the heavenlies in Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 8:28–30; Ephesians 2:10"> even as He chose us in Him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and blameless before Him in love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 8:29–30; Ephesians 1:11; Galatians 4:5–6"> having predestined us to adoption as sons by Jesus Christ to Himself, according to the good pleasure of His will,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 3:22–26; Ephesians 1:12; Colossians 1:13"> to the praise of His glorious grace, by which He bestowed favor upon us in the Beloved.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Romans 3:24; 1 John 1:7–9"> In Him we have redemption through His blood, the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of His grace</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 5:12; Ephesians 3:10; Romans 5:15"> which He lavished on us in all wisdom and insight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:26–28; Romans 16:25–27; Ephesians 3:3–9"> having made known to us the mystery of His will, according to His good pleasure which He purposed in Himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 1:20; Philippians 2:9–10; Galatians 4:4"> for an administration of the fullness of the times, He might bring together all things in Christ, those in heaven and those on the earth—in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:28; 1 Peter 1:4; Isaiah 46:10–11"> In whom also we have obtained an inheritance, having been predestined according to His purpose, who works all things according to the counsel of His will,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 1:6; Jeremiah 17:5–7; Ephesians 1:13–14"> so that we should be to the praise of His glory, we who first trusted in Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:22; Ephesians 4:30; 2 Timothy 2:19"> In whom you also, when you heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation; in whom also, having believed, you were sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:22; 2 Corinthians 5:5; Ephesians 4:30"> who is the guarantee of our inheritance until the redemption of the possession, to the praise of His glory.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul's Prayer for Spiritual Wisdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philemon 1:5; Colossians 1:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> Because of this I also, after I heard of your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love for all the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 1:8–9; Colossians 1:9; Colossians 1:3"> do not cease to give thanks for you, making mention of you in my prayers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; James 3:17–18; Colossians 1:9–10"> that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give to you the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:18; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Luke 24:45"> having the eyes of your heart enlightened; that you may know what is the hope to which He has called you, what are the riches of His glorious inheritance in the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Ephesians 3:20; Colossians 2:12"> and what is the immeasurable greatness of His power toward us who believe, according to the working of His mighty power</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 10:12; Mark 16:19; Psalms 110:1"> which He worked in Christ, having raised Him from the dead, and seated Him at His right hand in the heavenlies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:9–11; Colossians 2:10; Colossians 1:15–16"> above every ruler and authority and power and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this age but also in the coming age.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 1:18; Colossians 2:10; 1 Corinthians 15:25–27"> And He subjected all things under His feet, and gave Him to be head over all things to the church,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:18–19; John 1:16; Colossians 2:9–10"> which is His body, the fullness of Him filling all things in all.</span>
</div>
</div>
