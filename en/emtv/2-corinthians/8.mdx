## A Call to Generous Giving

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 16:9; 2 Corinthians 9:2; 2 Corinthians 9:4"> Now we make known to you brothers, the grace of God which has been given to the churches of Macedonia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 2:5; Proverbs 11:25; 2 Corinthians 6:10"> how that in a great trial of affliction the abundance of their joy and their deep poverty abounded in the riches of their generosity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:5; Exodus 35:21–22"> For I bear witness that according to their ability, and beyond their ability, they were freely willing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> with much appeal, begging us for the grace and the fellowship of the ministry to the saints—</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 12:1; Romans 14:7–9; 2 Chronicles 30:12"> and not as we hoped, but they first gave their own selves to the Lord, and then to us by the will of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 2 Corinthians 8:4"> so that we urged Titus, that just as he had begun before, thus he would also finish this grace also in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8; 2 Peter 1:5–8; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> But as you abound in everything—in faith, in word, in knowledge, in all diligence, and in your love for us—see that you abound in this grace also.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:6; 1 John 3:17–19; James 2:14–16"> I speak not by a command, but I am testing the genuineness of your love by means of the diligence of others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Philippians 2:6–8; Matthew 20:28"> For you know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that though He was rich, yet on account of you He became poor, that you through His poverty might become rich.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18–19; Hebrews 13:16; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> And I give my opinion in this: For this is profitable for you, who not only previously began doing this, but also desired to, since last year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:2"> And now also complete doing it; that as indeed there was the readiness to desire it, thus also there may be the completion from what you have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 12:42–44; 2 Corinthians 9:7; Luke 21:1–4"> For if there is first a readiness, it is acceptable according to what one has, and not according to what he does not have.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 4:34; Romans 15:26–27"> For I do not mean that others should be relieved and you burdened;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:12; Acts 4:34"> but by way of equality, that now at the present time your abundance may supply their need, that their abundance also may supply your need, in order that there may be equality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:18; Luke 22:35"> As it is written, “He who gathered much did not have too much, and he who gathered little had no lack.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Collection for the Judean Saints

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Jeremiah 32:40; 2 Corinthians 2:13–14"> But thanks be to God who puts the same earnest care for you in the heart of Titus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 13:22; 2 Corinthians 8:10"> Because he accepted my appeal, but being more diligent, he came to you of his own accord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> And we sent together with him the brother whose praise is in the gospel throughout all the churches,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:3–4; 2 Corinthians 4:15; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> and not only that, but who was also chosen by the churches as our traveling companion with this gift, which is ministered by us to the glory of the Lord Himself and to show our readiness—</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:22; Romans 14:16; Ephesians 5:15"> avoiding this, lest anyone should blame us in this abundance which is ministered by us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Peter 2:12; Philippians 4:8"> having regard for honorable things, not only before the Lord, but also before men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 2:20–22"> And we sent together with them our brother whom we often approved in many things, being diligent, but now being much more diligent, by his great confidence in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Philemon 1:17; 2 Corinthians 8:6"> If anyone inquires about Titus, he is my partner and fellow worker concerning you; or if our brothers are inquired about, they are messengers of the churches, the glory of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:14; 2 Corinthians 7:4; 2 Corinthians 9:2–4"> Therefore show to them the proof of your love, and of our boasting concerning you, before the churches.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
