## The Vision of Paradise

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:12; 2 Corinthians 12:7; 1 John 5:20"> It is indeed not profitable for me to boast. I will come to visions and revelations of the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 22:17; Ezekiel 8:1–3; 1 Thessalonians 4:17"> I know a man in Christ who fourteen years ago (whether in the body I do not know, or whether out of the body I do not know, God knows), such a one was caught up to the third heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And I know such a man (whether in the body or out of the body I do not know, God knows),</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 23:43; Revelation 2:7; Ezekiel 31:9"> that he was caught up into Paradise and heard unspeakable words, which it is not permitted for a man to utter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:30; 1 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Concerning such a one I will boast, but concerning myself I will not boast, except in my weaknesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:16; 2 Corinthians 11:31; 2 Corinthians 12:11"> For if I should desire to boast, I will not be foolish; for I will speak the truth. But I spare you, lest anyone consider me to be beyond what he sees me to be or hears something from me.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Thorn in the Flesh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:55; Judges 2:3; 2 Chronicles 32:25–26"> And lest I exalt myself above measure by the excellence of the revelations, a thorn in the flesh was given to me, an angel of Satan, that it might beat me, lest I exalt myself above measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Matthew 26:39–44; 1 Samuel 15:11"> Concerning this I pleaded with the Lord three times that it might depart from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 12:10; Isaiah 40:29–31"> And He said to me, “My grace is sufficient for you, for My power is perfected in weakness.” Therefore most gladly I will rather boast in my weaknesses, in order that the power of Christ may rest upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9; Ephesians 6:10; Romans 5:3"> Therefore I delight in weaknesses, in insults, in calamities, in persecutions, in distresses, for Christ's sake. For whenever I am weak, then I am strong.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Concern for the Corinthian Church

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:5; 2 Corinthians 11:1; 1 Corinthians 15:8–10"> I have become a fool in boasting; you have compelled me! For I ought to have been commended by you; for in nothing have I been inferior to the super apostles, though I am nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:18–19; 2 Corinthians 11:4; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> Truly the signs of an apostle were worked among you with all perseverance, in signs and wonders and miracles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Corinthians 9:6; 2 Corinthians 11:7–9"> For what is it in which you were inferior to other churches, except that I myself did not burden you? Forgive me this wrong!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 19:14; Genesis 24:35–36; 1 Corinthians 10:33"> Now for the third time I am ready to come to you, and I will not burden you. For I do not seek what is yours, but you. For the children ought not to store up for the parents, but the parents for the children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:8; 2 Corinthians 1:6; Philippians 2:17"> And I gladly will spend and be expended for your souls; although the more abundantly I love you, the less I am loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:5; 2 Corinthians 10:2–3; 2 Corinthians 12:13"> But be that as it may, I did not burden you. Nevertheless, being crafty, I took you with guile!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:5; 1 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Kings 5:20–27"> Did I take advantage of you by any of those whom I sent to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; Romans 4:12; Acts 20:33–35"> I urged Titus, and I sent with him the brother. Did Titus take advantage of you? Did we not walk in the same Spirit? Did we not walk in the same steps?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 9:1; 1 Corinthians 14:26; 2 Corinthians 10:8"> Again, do you think that we are defending ourselves to you? We speak before God in Christ. But we do all things, beloved, for your edification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 5:19–21; 1 Peter 2:1; Romans 1:29"> For I am afraid, lest perhaps when I come, I will not find you as I desire, and that I will be found by you as you do not desire; lest perhaps there be contentions, jealousies, outbursts of wrath, disputes, slanders, gossipings, pride, disorders;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:2; 1 Peter 4:2–3; Colossians 3:5"> lest, when I come again, my God will humble me before you, and I will mourn for many who have previously sinned and have not repented for the impurity and sexual immorality and lewdness which they have practiced.</span>
</div>
</div>
