## Bear One Another's Burdens

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:1; 2 Timothy 2:25; 2 Thessalonians 3:15"> Brothers, if a man is overtaken in any trespass, you who are spiritual restore such a person in a spirit of gentleness, looking out for yourself lest you also be tempted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 15:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; John 13:34"> Bear one another's burdens, and thus fulfill the law of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 26:12; 1 Corinthians 8:2; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> For if anyone thinks that he is something, when he is nothing, he deceives himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:5; Psalms 26:2; 1 Corinthians 11:28"> But let each person examine his own work, and then he will have boasting in himself alone, and not in another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:19; Romans 14:10–12; 2 Corinthians 5:10–11"> For each one shall bear his own load.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Be Generous and Do Good

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:17–18; Romans 15:27; Deuteronomy 12:19"> Let him who is taught the word share in all good things with him who teaches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6; Hosea 10:12; Job 4:8"> Do not be deceived, God is not mocked; for whatsoever a man sows, this also will he reap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:13; James 3:18; 2 Peter 2:19"> For he who sows to his flesh, from his own flesh will reap corruption, but he who sows to the Spirit, from the Spirit will reap everlasting life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 2 Thessalonians 3:13; Isaiah 40:30–31"> And while we do good, let us not lose heart, for in due season we shall reap if we do not become weary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 3:27; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> So then, as we have opportunity, let us work what is good toward all, but especially toward those who are of the household of faith.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Glory Only in the Cross

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:21–23; Romans 16:22"> See with what large letters I have written to you with my own hand!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:11; Colossians 2:23; Acts 15:1"> As many as wish to make a good showing in the flesh, these compel you to get circumcised, only so that they should not be persecuted for the cross of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 5:6; Romans 2:17–25"> For not even those who are circumcised keep the law themselves, but they wish you to be circumcised that they may boast in your flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Jeremiah 9:23–24; Galatians 2:20"> But God forbid that I should boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, by whom the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Colossians 3:10–11; Ephesians 2:10"> For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision means anything, but a new creation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Romans 9:6–8; Philippians 3:3"> And as many as walk according to this rule, peace and mercy be upon them, and upon the Israel of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:5; Colossians 1:24; 2 Corinthians 11:23–25"> Finally, let no one cause me trouble, for I bear in my body the marks of the Lord Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 16:20; 2 Timothy 4:22; Philemon 1:25"> Brothers, the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
