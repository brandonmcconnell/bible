## Paul's Concerns for the Galatians

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:4; Ephesians 2:11–12; 2 Thessalonians 1:8"> But at one time indeed, when you did not know God, you were slaves to those which by nature were not gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; Galatians 3:3; Romans 8:3"> But now, having known God, or rather, having been known by God, how is it that you turn again to the weak and worthless elements, to which once again you wish to be slaves?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:5; Leviticus 23:1–44"> You observe days and months and seasons and years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:5; Philippians 2:16; Galatians 2:2"> I am afraid for you, lest somehow I have worked hard for you in vain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:5; 1 Kings 22:4; Genesis 34:15"> Brothers, I beseech you, become as I am, because I also am as you are. You have not wronged me at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 12:7–10; 2 Corinthians 11:30"> You know that because of weakness in the flesh I preached the gospel to you the former time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; 1 Thessalonians 2:13; Hebrews 13:2"> And my trial which was in my flesh you did not despise nor disdain, but as an angel of God you received me, even as Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 5:2; 1 John 3:16–18; Colossians 4:13"> What then was your blessing? For I bear you witness that, if possible, you would have torn out your own eyes and given them to me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Proverbs 9:8; John 8:45"> Have I therefore become your enemy by speaking the truth to you?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 16:18; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; 2 Peter 2:3"> They are zealous for you in the wrong way; indeed, they wish to shut you out, so that you will be zealous about them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; Revelation 3:19; Philippians 1:27"> But it is good to be zealous in a good thing always, and not only when I am present with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 8:29; Romans 13:14"> My little children, for whom I labor in birth again until Christ is formed in you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:17–18; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21; 1 Thessalonians 3:9"> And I wanted to be present with you just now and to change my tone; for I am perplexed about you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Two Covenants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Galatians 3:23–24; Romans 7:5–6"> Tell me, you who wish to be under the law, do you not hear the law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 16:15; Genesis 21:10; Genesis 21:1–2"> For it is written that Abraham had two sons: one by the servant girl, and the other by a freewoman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 4:28–29"> But he who was of the servant girl was born according to the flesh, and he of the free woman according to the promise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 21:9–13; Hebrews 10:15–18; Genesis 16:8"> which things are symbolic. For these are two covenants: one in fact from Mount Sinai, bearing children into slavery, which is Hagar—</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 19:44; Acts 1:11; Galatians 1:17"> for Hagar is Mount Sinai in Arabia, and corresponds to the present Jerusalem, and is in slavery with her children—</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 12:22; Philippians 3:20; Micah 4:1–2"> but the Jerusalem above is free, which is the mother of us all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 54:1–5; Psalms 113:9; Ruth 4:14–16"> For it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Rejoice, O barren one, who does not give birth;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">break forth and shout, you who does not have birth pangs;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the children of the desolate are many more</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">than those of her who has a husband.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 4:23; Romans 9:8–9; Romans 4:13–18"> But we, brothers, like Isaac, are children of promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 21:9; Romans 8:13; Galatians 5:11"> But just as then the one who was born according to the flesh persecuted the one born according to the Spirit, so also it is now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 21:10–12; John 8:35; Romans 8:15–17"> But what does the Scripture say? “Cast out the servant girl and her son, for the son of the servant girl shall by no means inherit with the son of the free woman.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 8:36; John 1:12–13; Galatians 5:1"> So then, brothers, we are not children of the servant girl, but of the free woman.</span>
</div>
</div>
