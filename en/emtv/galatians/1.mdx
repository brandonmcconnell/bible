## Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:11–12; 1 Corinthians 1:1; Romans 1:1"> Paul, an apostle (not from men nor through man, but through Jesus Christ and God the Father who raised Him from the dead),</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:1; Philippians 4:21; Acts 16:5–6"> and all the brothers with me,<br />To the churches of Galatia:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 John 1:3; 2 Corinthians 13:14; Romans 1:7–15"> Grace to you and peace from God the Father and our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Romans 4:25; Galatians 2:20"> who gave Himself for our sins, so that He might deliver us from this present evil age, according to the will of our God and Father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Jude 1:25; Psalms 72:19"> to whom be glory forever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## No Other Gospel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:4; Galatians 5:7–8; Isaiah 29:13"> I marvel that you are turning so quickly from Him who called you in the grace of Christ, to a different gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 15:24; Romans 16:17–18; Galatians 5:10"> which is not another; except there are certain people who trouble you and wish to pervert the gospel of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:13–14; Galatians 1:9; Revelation 22:18–19"> But even if we, or an angel from heaven, should preach a gospel to you other than what we preached to you, let him be accursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 16:17; Proverbs 30:6; Deuteronomy 4:2"> As we have said before, and I say again just now, if anyone preaches a gospel to you other than what you received, let him be accursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Acts 5:29; Ephesians 6:6"> For do I now persuade men, or God? Or do I seek to please men? For if I still pleased men, I would not be a bondservant of Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul Called by God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 1:1; 1 Corinthians 2:9–10; 1 Corinthians 11:23"> But I make known to you, brothers, the gospel preached by me is not according to man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 1:16; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Galatians 1:1"> For I neither received it from man, nor was I taught it, but through the revelation of Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Corinthians 15:9; Acts 22:3–5"> For you have heard of my former conduct at one time in Judaism, that beyond measure I used to persecute the church of God, and I was devastating it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Acts 26:5; Philippians 3:4–6"> And I was advancing in Judaism above many my own age, being far more zealous for the traditions of my forefathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 49:5; Isaiah 49:1"> But when it pleased God, who separated me from my mother's womb and called me through His grace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:17; Ephesians 3:5–10; Ephesians 6:12"> to reveal His Son in me, so that I might preach Him among the Gentiles, I did not immediately consult with flesh and blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 9:20–25; 2 Corinthians 11:32–33; Galatians 1:18"> neither did I go up to Jerusalem to those who were apostles before me; but I went to Arabia, and returned once more to Damascus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 9:22–23; Acts 9:26–29; Acts 22:17–18"> Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to visit Peter, and I stayed with him fifteen days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:55; Mark 6:3; Matthew 12:46"> But I saw none of the other apostles except James, the Lord's brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:1; 2 Corinthians 11:31; 2 Corinthians 11:10–11"> Now what I write to you, behold, before God, I do not lie.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 15:41; Acts 9:30; Acts 6:9"> Then I went into the regions of Syria and Cilicia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:14; Romans 16:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:1"> And I was unknown by face to the churches of Judea which are in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:8–10; Acts 9:13; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> But they heard only, “He who persecuted us in times past now preaches the faith which at one time he was devastating.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Acts 11:18; Luke 15:10"> And they glorified God in me.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
