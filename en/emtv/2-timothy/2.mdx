## A Good Soldier of Christ Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Ephesians 6:10; 2 Timothy 1:7"> You therefore, my child, be empowered by the grace which is in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13–14; 1 Timothy 4:6; 1 Timothy 6:12"> And the things which you have heard from me through many witnesses, commit these to faithful men who will be competent to teach others also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2–3; James 1:12; 2 Timothy 4:5"> You therefore endure hardship as a good soldier of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 8:14; 2 Peter 2:20"> No one serving as a soldier entangles himself in the affairs of civilian life, in order that he may please the one having enlisted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Revelation 3:11; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> And also if anyone competes in athletics, he is not crowned unless he competes according to the rules.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; 1 Corinthians 9:7–11; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9"> It is necessary for the hard-working farmer to be first to receive of the fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:5; 1 John 5:20; Luke 24:45"> Consider the things I say, and may the Lord give you understanding in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Romans 2:16; 1 Corinthians 15:11–20"> Remember that Jesus Christ, of the seed of David, was raised from the dead according to my gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Acts 28:31; 2 Timothy 1:8"> in which I endure hardship unto chains as an evildoer; but the word of God is not chained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; Colossians 1:24; 2 Timothy 2:3"> Because of this, I endure all things for the sake of the elect, that they also may obtain the salvation which is in Christ Jesus with eternal glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:8; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:5"> Faithful is this word:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">For if we died with Him, We shall also live with Him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Matthew 10:33; Revelation 20:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">If we endure, we shall also reign with Him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If we deny Him, He also will deny us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 3:3; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">If we are faithless, He remains faithful;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He cannot deny Himself.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Approved and Disapproved Workers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 3:9–11; 2 Timothy 2:23; 2 Timothy 4:1"> Remind them of these things, charging them before the Lord not to dispute about words, useful for nothing, for the ruin of the hearers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; 2 Peter 1:10; 1 Timothy 4:6"> Be diligent to show yourself approved unto God, a worker unashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:14"> But shun profane and empty babblings; for they will advance to more ungodliness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; Nahum 3:15; James 5:3"> And their word will spread like gangrene; of whom are Hymenaeus and Philetus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:12; 1 Timothy 1:19; Hebrews 3:10"> who have strayed concerning the truth, asserting that the resurrection has already occurred; and they overturn the faith of some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 10:14; 1 Corinthians 8:3; Nahum 1:7"> Nevertheless the firm foundation of God stands, having this seal: “The Lord knows those that are His,” and, “Let everyone naming the name of the Lord depart from unrighteousness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:21–23; 1 Peter 2:5; 1 Timothy 3:15"> Now in a great house there are not only vessels of gold and silver, but also of wood and clay, some for honor and some for dishonor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:17; Ephesians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> Therefore if anyone cleanses himself from the latter, he will be a vessel for honor, having been consecrated and useful to the Master, having been prepared for every good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; 1 Peter 3:11; 1 Corinthians 6:18"> Flee youthful lusts; but pursue righteousness, faith, love, peace with those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:14; Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 4:7"> But reject foolish and ignorant disputes, knowing that they bring forth fights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Titus 3:2; James 1:19–20; 1 Timothy 3:2–3"> And a servant of the Lord ought not to fight, but to be gentle to all, skillful at teaching, forbearing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 11:18; Galatians 6:1; 1 Timothy 2:4"> in meekness instructing those that oppose, if God perhaps may give them repentance, to a full knowledge of the truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; Isaiah 49:25–26; Psalms 124:7"> and they regain their senses and escape out of the snare of the devil, having been captured alive by him to do his will.</span>
</div>
