## Preach the Word

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 10:42; 2 Timothy 4:8; 1 Timothy 5:21"> I charge you therefore before God and the Lord Jesus Christ, who is going to judge the living and the dead in accordance with His appearing and His kingdom:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Timothy 4:15–16; Titus 2:15"> preach the word! Be ready in season and out of season. Reprove, rebuke, exhort, with all patience and teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; 2 Timothy 3:1–6; 1 Timothy 4:1–3"> For there will be a time when they will not put up with sound doctrine, but according to their own lusts, they will heap up for themselves teachers, tickling their ears,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 7:11; 1 Timothy 1:4; Acts 7:57"> and they will turn their ears away from the truth, and be turned aside to myths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Peter 1:13; 2 Timothy 2:3"> You however be watchful in all things, endure hardship, do the work of an evangelist, fulfill your ministry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 1:23; Philippians 2:17; 2 Peter 1:14–15"> For I am already being poured out as a drink offering, and the time of my departure has arrived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:12; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Hebrews 12:1–2"> I have fought the good fight. I have finished the race, I have kept the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 1:12; Revelation 2:10; 1 Peter 5:4"> Finally, there is laid up for me the crown of righteousness, which the Lord, the righteous Judge, will give to me on that Day, and not only to me, but also to all those who have loved His appearing.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul's Final Words

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:4; 2 Timothy 4:21"> Be diligent to come to me quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; 2 Timothy 1:15; Colossians 4:14–15"> For Demas has forsaken me, having loved this present world, and traveled to Thessalonica—Crescens to Galatia, Titus to Dalmatia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Philemon 1:24; Acts 12:25"> Luke alone is with me. Take Mark, and bring him with you, for he is useful to me for ministry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 6:21; Acts 20:4; Titus 3:12"> And Tychicus I have sent to Ephesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:11; 2 Corinthians 11:27; Acts 16:11"> Bring the cloak which I left with Carpus at Troas when you come—and the books, especially the parchments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; 2 Thessalonians 1:6; Psalms 28:4"> Alexander the coppersmith did me much harm. May the Lord repay him according to his works;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:8; Philippians 3:2; Matthew 10:16–17"> whom you also should guard against, for he has greatly resisted our words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 7:60; John 16:32; Mark 14:50"> At my first defense no one stood with me, but all forsook me. May it not be reckoned to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Philippians 1:12–14; 1 Samuel 17:37"> But the Lord stood by me and strengthened me, so that through me the proclamation might be fulfilled, and all the Gentiles might hear, and I was delivered out of the mouth of the lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 121:7; 2 Thessalonians 3:3; Jude 1:24–25"> And the Lord shall deliver me from every evil work and preserve me for His heavenly kingdom. To Him be glory forever and ever. Amen!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Greetings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 18:2; 1 Corinthians 16:19; Romans 16:3–4"> Greet Prisca and Aquila, and the household of Onesiphorus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Acts 19:22; Philippians 2:26–27"> Erastus stayed in Corinth, but Trophimus I have left in Miletus sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:9; Philippians 4:22; 3 John 1:14"> Be diligent to come before winter. Eubulus greets you, as well as Pudens, Linus, Claudia, and all the brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philemon 1:25; Galatians 6:18; Colossians 4:18"> The Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Grace be with you. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
