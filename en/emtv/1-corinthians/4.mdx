## The Ministry of Apostles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Titus 1:7; 1 Timothy 3:9"> Let a man so consider us, as ministers of Christ and stewards of the mysteries of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Luke 12:42; Luke 16:10–12"> Now furthermore what is required among stewards, is that one be found faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 7:24; 1 Corinthians 2:15; 1 Samuel 16:7"> But to me it is a very small thing that I should be judged by you, or by a human court. Indeed, I do not even judge myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 143:2; 1 John 3:20–21; Psalms 130:3"> For I am conscious of nothing, but I have not been justified by this; but He who judges me is the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:7; 2 Corinthians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> So then judge nothing before the time, until the Lord comes, who also will bring to light the hidden things of darkness, and will make clear the purposes of the hearts. Then each one's praise shall come from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:6; 2 Corinthians 11:4; 2 Corinthians 12:19"> Now these things, brothers, I have figuratively applied to myself and Apollos for your sakes, in order that you may learn in us not to think above what is written, and that none of you may become arrogant in behalf of one against the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 3:27; 1 Peter 4:10; James 1:17"> For who makes you differ from another? And what do you have that you did not receive? And if you did indeed receive what you have, why do you boast as though you did not receive it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 6:25; Proverbs 13:7; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> You are already full! You are already rich! You have reigned as kings without us; and I wish that indeed you did reign, in order that we also might reign with you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:33; Romans 8:36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–12"> For I think that God has exhibited us, the apostles, last, as men appointed to death; for we have become a spectacle to the world, both to angels and to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:3; Acts 26:24; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> We are fools for Christ's sake, but you are wise in Christ! We are weak, but you are strong! You are lauded, but we are despised!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:35; 2 Corinthians 11:23–27; Philippians 4:12"> Even unto this present hour we both hunger and thirst, and we are naked, and beaten, and homeless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Matthew 5:11; 1 Peter 4:12–14"> And we labor, working with our own hands. Being reviled, we bless; being persecuted, we endure;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Lamentations 3:45; Acts 22:22"> being defamed, we encourage; we have become as the sweepings of the world, the offscouring of all things, to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Corinthians 4:15; 3 John 1:4"> I do not write these things to shame you, but as my beloved children I admonish you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:19; 1 Corinthians 3:10; 1 Corinthians 9:14"> For though you might have ten thousand tutors in Christ, yet you do not have many fathers; for in Christ Jesus I have begotten you through the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Thessalonians 3:9; Philippians 3:17"> Therefore I exhort you, be imitators of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:10; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> Because of this I sent Timothy to you, who is my beloved and faithful child in the Lord, who will remind you of my ways in Christ, as I teach everywhere in every church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:6–8; 1 Corinthians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 4:21"> Now some have become arrogant, as though I were not coming to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:1–4; Acts 18:21; Acts 19:21"> But I will come to you quickly, if the Lord wills, and I will know, not the word of those who are arrogant, but the power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4; 1 Thessalonians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5"> For the kingdom of God is not in word, but in power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:2; 2 Corinthians 13:10; 1 Thessalonians 2:7"> What do you desire? Shall I come to you with a rod, or in love, and a spirit of gentleness?</span>
</div>
