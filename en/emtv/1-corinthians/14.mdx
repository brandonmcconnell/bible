## Prophecy and Tongues

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:39; 1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Corinthians 12:1"> Pursue love, and desire the spiritual gifts, but especially that you may prophesy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Acts 19:6; Acts 10:46"> For he who speaks in a tongue speaks not to men, but to God, for no one understands him; however, in the spirit he speaks mysteries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:12; 1 Timothy 4:13; Jude 1:20"> But he who prophesies speaks edification and exhortation and comfort to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:22; 1 Corinthians 14:26; Mark 16:17"> He who speaks in a tongue edifies himself, but he who prophesies edifies the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:28–30; 1 Corinthians 12:10; Mark 16:17"> Now I wish that you all spoke in tongues, but even more that you should prophesy; for he who prophesies is greater than he who speaks in tongues, unless he interprets, so that the church may be edified.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Tongues Must Be Interpreted

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17; Romans 6:17; 2 Timothy 3:16"> But now, brothers, if I come to you speaking in tongues, what shall I profit you unless I speak to you either in a revelation, or in knowledge, or in a prophecy, or in doctrine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:8; Luke 7:32; 1 Corinthians 13:1"> Likewise the lifeless things, when they make a sound, whether flute or harp, unless they make a distinction in the tones, how will it be known what is being played on the flute, or what is being played on the harp?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 10:9; Ezekiel 33:3–6; Jeremiah 4:19"> For if the trumpet gives an indistinct sound, who will prepare for battle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:26"> So also you, if you do not give a clear word by the tongue, how will it be known what is being spoken? For you will be speaking into the air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> There are, perhaps, so many kinds of languages in the world, and none of them without meaning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 28:2; Romans 1:14; Colossians 3:11"> Therefore, if I do not know the meaning of the language, I shall be as a foreigner to the one speaking, and the one speaking shall be as a foreigner to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:26; 1 Corinthians 14:3–4; 1 Corinthians 14:1"> So also you, since you are zealous of spiritual gifts, seek that you may abound to the edification of the church.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 11:24; 1 Corinthians 12:30; John 14:13–14"> Therefore let him who speaks in a tongue pray that he may interpret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:2; 1 Corinthians 14:15–16; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> For if I pray in a tongue, my spirit prays, but my understanding is unfruitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; John 4:23–24; Ephesians 6:18"> What is the outcome then? I will pray with the spirit, and I will also pray with the mind. I will sing with the spirit, and I will also sing with the mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:36; Psalms 106:48; Revelation 5:14"> Otherwise, if you bless with the spirit, how will he who occupies the place of the uninformed say “Amen” when you give thanks, since he does not know what you are saying?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:4; 1 Corinthians 14:6"> For you indeed give thanks well, but the other is not edified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:4–6; 1 Corinthians 4:7"> I thank my God I speak with tongues more than you all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:21–22; 1 Corinthians 14:4"> but in the church I would rather speak five words with my understanding, in order that I may instruct others, rather than ten thousand words in a tongue.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Tongues a Sign to Unbelievers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:2; 1 Corinthians 13:11; Romans 16:19"> Brothers, do not be children in your understanding; rather, in malice be children, but in understanding be men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 28:11–12; Jeremiah 5:15; Deuteronomy 28:49"> In the law it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“With men of other tongues and by strange lips I will speak to this people;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and yet not even in this way will they hear Me,”</span><br />says the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 2:6–12; Mark 16:17; 1 Corinthians 14:1"> So then tongues are for a sign, not to those who believe but to unbelievers; but prophesying is not for unbelievers but for those who believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 2:13; Acts 26:24; 1 Corinthians 11:18"> If then the whole church comes together at the same place, and all speak in tongues, and there come in those that are unlearned or unbelievers, will they not say that you are mad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 2:37; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Hebrews 4:12–13"> But if all prophesy, and an unbeliever or an unlearned person comes in, he is reproved by all, he is discerned by all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 45:14; Zechariah 8:23; Psalms 72:11"> And so the secrets of his heart become clear; and so, falling down on his face, he will worship God, reporting that God is truly among you!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Order in Church Meetings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 5:19; Romans 14:19; 1 Corinthians 12:7–10"> What is the outcome then, brothers? Whenever you come together, each of you has a song, has a teaching, has a tongue, has a revelation, has an interpretation. Let all things be done for edification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:26"> If anyone speaks in an unknown tongue, let it be by two or at the most three, each in turn, and let one interpret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> But if there is no interpreter, let him be silent in church, and let him speak to himself and to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:19–21; 1 John 4:1–3"> Let two or three prophets speak, and let others discriminate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:19–20; Job 33:31–33; Job 32:11"> But if something is revealed to another who sits by, let the first be silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Corinthians 14:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> For you can all prophesy one by one, that all may learn and all may be encouraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 4:19–20; 1 John 4:1; 1 Samuel 10:10–13"> And the spirits of the prophets are subject to the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 3:17–18; 1 Corinthians 14:40; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> For God is not the author of confusion but of peace, as in all the churches of the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:11–12; Genesis 3:16; Numbers 30:3–13"> Let your women be silent in the churches, for it is not permitted for them to speak, but to be in subjection, just as the law also says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; 1 Peter 3:7; Ephesians 5:25–27"> And if they desire to learn anything, let them ask their own husbands at home; for it is shameful for women to speak in church.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 18:1–17; Zechariah 14:8; Acts 17:15"> Or did the word of God go forth from you? Or did it come to you only?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:7; 2 Peter 3:2"> If anyone thinks that he is a prophet, or is spiritual, let him recognize that the things which I am writing to you are the commandments of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Hosea 4:17; Revelation 22:11–12"> But if anyone is ignorant, let him be ignorant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:31; 1 Corinthians 14:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:20"> Therefore, brothers, seek to prophesy, and do not forbid to speak in tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Colossians 2:5; Titus 1:5; 1 Corinthians 14:26–33"> Let all things be done properly and according to order.</span>
</div>
</div>
