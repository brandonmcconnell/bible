## Warning Against Idolatry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 14:29; Nehemiah 9:11–12; Psalms 78:13–14"> Now I do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, that all our fathers were under the cloud, and all passed through the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 3:2–3; 1 Corinthians 1:13–16; Exodus 14:31"> and all were baptized into Moses in the cloud and in the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Nehemiah 9:15; Exodus 16:35"> and all ate the same spiritual food,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 17:6; John 7:37; Isaiah 48:21"> and all drank the same spiritual drink. For they were drinking from that spiritual Rock that followed them, and that Rock was Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 3:17; Jude 1:5; Numbers 14:37"> Nevertheless, with the majority of them God was not pleased, for their bodies were scattered in the wilderness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:7; 1 Corinthians 10:11; Hebrews 4:11"> Now these things took place as examples for us, in order that we should not lust after evil things, as they also lusted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 32:6–8; Psalms 106:19–20; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> Neither be idolaters, just as some of them; as it is written, “The people sat down to eat and drink, and rose up to play.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 1 Corinthians 6:18; Psalms 106:29"> Neither let us commit sexual immorality, just as some of them did, and in one day twenty-three thousand fell;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 21:5–6; Psalms 78:18; Hebrews 3:8–11"> neither let us tempt Christ, just as also some of them tempted Him, and were destroyed by serpents;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 16:41; Numbers 14:37"> nor murmur, just as also some of them murmured, and were destroyed by the destroyer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 15:4; Romans 13:11; 1 Corinthians 9:10"> Now all these things happened to them as examples, and they were written for our instruction, to whom the ends of the ages have come.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:20; 2 Peter 3:17; Proverbs 16:18"> So then, let him who thinks he stands take heed, lest he fall!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 2:9; 2 Timothy 4:18; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> No temptation has overtaken you except such as is common to man; but God is faithful, who will not permit you to be tempted beyond what you are able, but with the temptation, He will also provide the way out, so as for you to be able to bear it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; 1 John 5:21; 1 Peter 2:11"> Therefore, my beloved, flee from idolatry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 11:13"> I speak as to wise men; judge for yourselves what I say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:26–28; 1 Corinthians 11:23–29; Acts 2:42"> The cup of blessing which we bless, is it not the communion of the blood of Christ? The bread which we break, is it not the communion of the body of Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:5; Colossians 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> Because we, though many, are one bread and one body; for we all partake from the one bread.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 3:3–5; Galatians 6:16; Leviticus 7:6"> Look at Israel according to the flesh: are not those who eat of the sacrifices partakers of the altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:4; 1 Corinthians 3:7; 1 Corinthians 13:2"> What am I saying then? That an idol is anything, or what is offered to idols is anything?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 9:20; Psalms 106:37–39; Deuteronomy 32:16–17"> No, but that the things which the Gentiles sacrifice, they sacrifice to demons and not to God, and I do not desire that you should have fellowship with demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; 1 Kings 18:21; 2 Corinthians 6:15–17"> You cannot drink the cup of the Lord and the cup of demons; you cannot partake of the Lord's table and of the table of demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Deuteronomy 32:16; Hebrews 10:31"> Or do we provoke the Lord to jealousy? Are we stronger than He?</span>
</div>
</div>

## Do All to the Glory of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:12; 1 Corinthians 8:9; Ephesians 4:29"> All things are lawful for me, but not all things are profitable; all things are lawful for me, but not all things edify.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:1–2; 1 Corinthians 10:33; Philippians 2:4–5"> Let no one seek his own concerns, but let each one seek another's concern.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; 1 Corinthians 8:7; Titus 1:15"> Eat everything being sold in the meat market, questioning nothing, for conscience' sake;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 24:1; Exodus 19:5; Psalms 50:12"> for “The earth is the Lord's, and all its fullness.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:25; Luke 10:7–8; 1 Corinthians 5:9–11"> And if any of the unbelievers invites you to dinner, and you desire to go, eat whatever is set before you, questioning nothing, for conscience' sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:10–13; 1 Corinthians 8:7; 1 Corinthians 10:26"> But if anyone says to you, “This was offered to idols,” do not eat it for the sake of the one who told you, and for his conscience' sake; for “The earth is the Lord's, and all its fullness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:19; Romans 14:15–21; 1 Corinthians 8:9–13"> “Conscience,” I say, not your own, but that of the other. For why is my freedom judged by another man's conscience?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 14:6; 1 Timothy 4:3–4"> But if I partake with thanks, why am I defamed for that of which I give thanks?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Colossians 3:23; 1 Peter 4:11"> Therefore, whether you eat or drink, or whatever you do, do all things for the glory of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 20:28; 1 Corinthians 10:33; Romans 14:13"> Be without offense, both toward Jews and toward Greeks, and toward the church of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23"> just as I also please all men in all things, not seeking my own profit, but the profit of many, that they may be saved.</span>
</div>
</div>
