## Christ Crucified

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:17; 1 Corinthians 2:13; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> And I, brothers, when I came to you, did not come with excellence of speech or of wisdom, proclaiming to you the testimony of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 6:14; John 17:3; 1 Corinthians 1:22–25"> For I determined to know nothing among you except Jesus Christ and Him crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:4; 1 Corinthians 4:10–13; 2 Corinthians 12:5–10"> And I was with you in weakness, in fear, and in much trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:20; 1 Thessalonians 1:5; 1 Corinthians 1:17"> And my message and my preaching were not with persuasive words of human wisdom, but in demonstration of the Spirit and of power,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:7; 2 Corinthians 12:9; 2 Corinthians 6:7"> in order that your faith might not be in the wisdom of men, but in the power of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Spiritual Wisdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 5:14; James 3:15; 2 Corinthians 4:4"> However, we speak wisdom among those that are mature, but not the wisdom of this age, nor of the rulers of this age, who are being destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 1:26–27; 2 Peter 1:3; Matthew 11:25"> But we speak the wisdom of God in a mystery, the wisdom having been hidden, which God foreordained before the ages for our glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:27; James 2:1; Acts 7:2"> which none of the rulers of this age understood (for had they understood, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Psalms 31:19; James 1:12"> But as it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Eye has not seen, nor ear heard,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor have entered into the heart of man,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that which God has prepared for those who love Him.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 14:26; Ephesians 3:5; Luke 10:21"> But God has revealed them to us through His Spirit. For the Spirit searches all things, even the deep things of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:27; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Romans 11:33–34"> For who among men knows the things of a man, except the spirit of the man which is in him? Even so, no one knows the things of God except the Spirit of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 2:27; Romans 8:5–6; 1 John 2:20"> Now we did not receive the spirit of the world, but the Spirit which is from God, in order that we might know the things freely given to us by God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4; 1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 1:17"> which things we also speak, not in words taught in human wisdom, but in words taught by the Holy Spirit, comparing spiritual things with spiritual.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:18; Romans 8:5–8; Matthew 13:11–17"> But the natural man does not receive the things of the Spirit of God, for they are foolishness to him; nor can he know them, because they are spiritually discerned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Proverbs 28:5; Hebrews 5:14"> But he that is spiritual discerns all things, yet he himself is discerned by no man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34; John 16:13–16"> For “Who has known the mind of the Lord, that he may instruct Him?” But we have the mind of Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>
