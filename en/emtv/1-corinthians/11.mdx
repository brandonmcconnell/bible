## Head Coverings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:15; 1 Corinthians 4:17; Proverbs 31:28–31"> Now I praise you, brothers, because you remember me in all things, and you hold fast the traditions just as I handed down to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:16; Colossians 1:18; Colossians 3:18"> But I want you to know that the head of every man is Christ, the head of woman is man, and the head of Christ is God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:30; 1 Corinthians 12:28; 1 Corinthians 12:10"> Every man who prays or who prophesies with his head covered, dishonors his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:12; Luke 2:36; Acts 2:17"> But every woman who prays or who prophesies with her head uncovered dishonors her own head, for that is one and the same as if her head were shaved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:5; Numbers 5:18"> For if a woman is not covered, let her also have her hair cut. But if it is a shameful thing for a woman to have her hair cut, or to be shaved, let her be covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3; Genesis 5:1; Psalms 8:6"> For a man indeed ought not to cover his head, since he is the image and glory of God; but woman is the glory of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:13; Genesis 2:21–23"> For man did not come from woman, but woman from man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 2:18; Genesis 2:23–24; Genesis 2:20"> Neither was man created for woman, but woman for man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 24:64–65; Hebrews 1:14; Matthew 18:10"> Because of this, the woman ought to have a symbol of authority on her head, because of the angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; 1 Corinthians 7:10–14; 1 Corinthians 12:12–22"> Nevertheless, neither is the man apart from the woman, nor the woman apart from the man, in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:36; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 1:2–3"> For as the woman is from the man, even so the man is by the woman; but all things are of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:57; John 7:24; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> Judge among yourselves. Is it proper for a woman to pray to God uncovered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:26; 1 Corinthians 14:35"> Does not nature itself teach you, that if a man has long hair, it is a dishonor to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> But if a woman has long hair, it is a glory for her; because her long hair has been given to her in place of a covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Timothy 6:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 2:14"> But if anyone seems to be contentious, we have no such custom, nor do the churches of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lord's Supper

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; 1 Corinthians 14:23; 1 Corinthians 11:22"> Now in giving these instructions I do not praise you, because you are not coming together for the better, but for the worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 1:10–12; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> For first of all, when you come together in church, I hear that there are divisions among you, and in part I believe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 2:19; Titus 3:10; Acts 20:30"> For there must also be sects among you, in order that those who are approved may become recognized among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Therefore when you assemble in the same place, it is not to eat the Lord's Supper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:13; Jude 1:12; 1 Corinthians 11:23–25"> For when you eat, each one takes first his own supper, and one goes hungry and another is drunk!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 20:28; Proverbs 17:5; James 2:5–6"> What? Have you not houses to eat and drink in? Or do you despise the church of God, and disgrace those who do not have? What shall I say to you? Shall I praise you in this? I do not praise you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:19–20; Matthew 26:26–28; 1 Corinthians 11:23–25"> For I received from the Lord that which I also delivered unto you: that the Lord Jesus, during the night in which He was betrayed, took bread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:53–58; 1 Corinthians 11:27–28; 1 Corinthians 10:3–4"> and having given thanks, He broke it and said, “Take, eat; this is My body which has been broken for you; do this in remembrance of Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 22:20; Hebrews 9:15–20; Hebrews 13:20"> Likewise He also took the cup after supper, saying, “This cup is the new covenant in My blood. This do, as often as you drink it, in remembrance of Me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Revelation 1:7; John 14:3"> For as often as you eat this bread and drink this cup, you proclaim the Lord's death until He comes.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Examine Yourself

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 6:51; 1 Corinthians 10:21; Hebrews 10:29"> Therefore whoever eats this bread or drinks this cup of the Lord irreverently will be guilty of the body of the Lord and of the blood of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 6:4; 2 Corinthians 13:5; Lamentations 3:40"> But let a man examine himself, and in this way let him eat from the bread and drink from the cup.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:27; 1 Corinthians 11:30; 1 Corinthians 11:32–34"> For he that eats and drinks irreverently eats and drinks judgment to himself, not discerning the Lord's body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Numbers 20:12; Amos 3:2"> Because of this many among you are weak and sick, and many have died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 1:9; Psalms 32:3–5; Revelation 2:5"> For if we judge ourselves correctly, we would not be judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5–11; Psalms 118:18; Proverbs 3:11–12"> But when we are judged, we are disciplined by the Lord, in order that we may not be condemned along with the world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Therefore, my brothers, when you come together to eat, wait for one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:21–22; 1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Corinthians 4:19"> But if anyone is hungry, let him eat at home, lest you come together for judgment. And the rest I will put in order when I come.</span>
</div>
</div>
