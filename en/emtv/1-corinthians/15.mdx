## The Resurrection of Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:6–12; 1 Peter 5:12; 1 Corinthians 15:3–11"> Now I would remind you, brothers, of the gospel which I preached to you, which also you received, and in which you stand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 3:6; Hebrews 2:1; Hebrews 3:14"> through which also you are saved, if you hold fast that word which I preached to you—unless you believed in vain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Galatians 1:4; Isaiah 53:1–12"> For I delivered to you first of all that which I also received: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 6:2; Matthew 12:40; Psalms 16:10–11"> and that He was buried, and that He was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 16:14; 1 Corinthians 1:12; John 1:42"> and that He appeared to Cephas, then to the twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 28:10; Mark 16:7; Acts 13:36"> Thereafter He appeared to more than five hundred brothers at one time, of whom the majority remain until now, but some have fallen asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 24:50; Acts 1:2–12; Luke 24:33"> Thereafter He appeared to James, then to all the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:1; Acts 26:16; Acts 22:18"> And last of all, as of one born out of due time, He appeared to me also.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 3:7–8; 1 Timothy 1:13–15; Acts 8:3"> For I am the least of the apostles, who am not worthy to be called an apostle, because I persecuted the church of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 3:5; Philippians 2:13"> But by the grace of God I am what I am, and His grace toward me was not in vain; but I labored more abundantly than them all, yet not I, but the grace of God which was with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:3–4; 1 Corinthians 2:2"> Therefore, whether it was I or they, so we preach and so you believed.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Resurrection of the Dead

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 26:8; Acts 17:32; 2 Timothy 2:18"> Now if Christ is preached that He has been raised from the dead, how do some among you say that there is no resurrection of the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Romans 8:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:14"> But if there is no resurrection of the dead, then Christ has not been raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:14; Galatians 2:2"> And if Christ has not been raised, then our preaching is vain and your faith also is vain!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Job 13:7–10; Acts 2:32"> And we are found to be false witnesses of God, because we bore witness concerning God, that He raised Christ, whom He did not raise—if in fact the dead are not raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For if the dead are not raised, then neither has Christ been raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 4:25; 1 Peter 1:3; 1 Corinthians 15:14"> And if Christ has not been raised, your faith is worthless; you are still in your sins!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:13–14; Revelation 14:13; 1 Thessalonians 4:16"> Then also those who have fallen asleep in Christ have perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 1:21; Revelation 14:13; 2 Timothy 1:12"> If in this life only we have hoped in Christ, we are to be pitied above all men.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Last Enemy Destroyed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:23; Acts 26:23; Revelation 1:5"> But now Christ has been raised from the dead, and has become the firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:25; Romans 5:12–17; 1 Corinthians 15:22"> For since by a man came death, also by a Man came the resurrection of the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 5:12–21; 1 Corinthians 15:45–49; John 5:21–29"> For as in Adam all die, even so in Christ all will be made alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; Galatians 3:29; 1 Corinthians 15:52"> But each one in his own order: Christ the firstfruits, then those who belong to Christ at His coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Daniel 7:14; Matthew 28:18"> Then will be the end, when He hands over the kingdom to God, even the Father, when He abolishes every ruler and every authority and power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 10:12–13; Psalms 110:1; Matthew 22:44"> For He must reign till He puts all His enemies under His feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:10; 1 Corinthians 15:55; Revelation 21:4"> The last enemy that will be abolished is death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 8:6; Hebrews 2:8; Matthew 28:18"> For “He has subjected all things under His feet.” But when He says that “all things have been subjected,” it is evident that this is except for Him who subjected all things to Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; 1 Corinthians 3:23; Ephesians 1:23"> Now when all things are made subject to Him, then also the Son Himself will be subjected to Him who subjected all things to Him, in order that God may be all in all.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Effects of Denying the Resurrection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 6:3–4; 1 Corinthians 15:16; Matthew 20:22"> Otherwise, what will they do who are being baptized for the dead, if the dead are not raised at all? Why are they also baptized for the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:9; Galatians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 4:7–12"> And why are we also in danger every hour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:36; 1 Corinthians 4:9–13"> I affirm, by my boasting in you which I have in Christ Jesus our Lord, I die daily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 22:13; Isaiah 56:12; Luke 12:19"> If, in the manner of men, I fought with beasts at Ephesus, what is the benefit to me? If the dead are not raised, “Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Matthew 24:4; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> Do not be deceived: “Evil associations corrupts good habits.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:5; Romans 13:11; Hebrews 5:11–12"> Awake to righteousness, and do not sin! For some have no knowledge of God. I speak this to your shame.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Resurrection Body

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:3; 1 Corinthians 15:38–53; Romans 9:19"> But someone will say, “How are the dead raised? And with what sort of body do they come?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 12:24; Luke 11:40; Ephesians 5:15"> Fool, what you sow is not made alive unless it dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And what you sow, you do not sow that body that will be, but a bare grain—perhaps wheat or some other grains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:7; Isaiah 61:11; Mark 4:26–29"> But God gives to it a body just as He desired, and to each of the seeds its own body.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–26"> All flesh is not the same flesh, but there is a different kind of flesh for men, and another flesh for beasts, another for fish, and another for birds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And there are heavenly bodies, and earthly bodies; but the glory of the heavenly is one, and the glory of the earthly is another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 24:23; Psalms 148:3–5; Deuteronomy 4:19"> There is one glory of the sun, and another glory of the moon, and another glory of the stars; for one star differs from another star in glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Daniel 12:3; Romans 8:21"> So also is the resurrection of the dead. The body is sown in corruption, it is raised in incorruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:14; Colossians 3:4; Philippians 3:20–21"> It is sown in dishonor, it is raised in glory. It is sown in weakness, it is raised in power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:50; Luke 24:31; John 20:19"> It is sown a natural body, it is raised a spiritual body. There is a natural body, and there is a spiritual body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 5:21; Genesis 2:7; Romans 8:2"> Thus also it is written, “The first man Adam became a living being;” the last Adam became a life-giving spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Colossians 3:9–10; Romans 6:6; Ephesians 4:22–24"> However, that which is spiritual is not first, but that which is natural, then that which is spiritual.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:31; Genesis 2:7; Genesis 3:19"> The first man was from earth, made of dust; the second Man is the Lord from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Genesis 5:3; Romans 5:12–21; John 3:6"> Like the man made of dust, so also are those who are made of dust; and as is the heavenly Man, so also are those who are heavenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Romans 8:29; 2 Corinthians 3:18; Genesis 5:3"> And just as we have borne the image of the man of dust, let us also bear the image of the heavenly Man.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Our Final Victory

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 3:3–6; Matthew 16:17; Ephesians 4:17"> Now this I say, brothers, that flesh and blood cannot inherit the kingdom of God; nor can corruption inherit incorruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Philippians 3:21; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> Behold, I tell you a mystery: We shall not all sleep, but we shall all be changed—</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; Matthew 24:31; John 5:28"> in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trumpet. For the trumpet shall sound, and the dead shall be raised incorruptible, and we shall be changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:2–4; 1 John 3:2; Romans 2:7"> For this corruptible must put on incorruption, and this mortal must put on immortality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 20:14; Hebrews 2:14–15"> So when this corruptible should put on incorruption, and this mortal should put on immortality, then will come to pass the word which was written: “Death is swallowed up in victory.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; Psalms 89:48; Acts 2:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">“O Death, where is your sting?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">O Hades, where is your victory?”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Romans 4:15; Galatians 3:10–13; Romans 7:5–13"> Now the sting of death is sin, and the power of sin is the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; Romans 8:37; 1 John 5:4–5"> But thanks be to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Chronicles 15:7; Hebrews 6:10"> So then, my beloved brothers, be steadfast, immovable, always abounding in the work of the Lord, knowing that your labor is not in vain in the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
