## Divisions in the Church

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 5:13; 1 Corinthians 2:14–15; Ephesians 4:13–14"> And I, brothers, was not able to speak to you as to spiritual, but as to carnal, as to babes in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 5:11–14; John 16:12; 1 Peter 2:2"> I gave you milk to drink, and not solid food; for you were not yet able to receive it. Indeed, neither are you now yet able;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:16; Galatians 5:15; Titus 3:3"> for you are still carnal. For where there are envy, strife, and divisions among you, are you not carnal, and walking according to human principles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 4:6"> For when one says, “I am of Paul,” and another, “I am of Apollos,” are you not carnal?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:4; 2 Corinthians 6:1; 1 Peter 4:10"> Who then is Paul, and who is Apollos, but ministers through whom you believed, as the Lord has given to each one?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9–10; Proverbs 11:25; 2 Corinthians 10:14–15"> I planted, Apollos watered, but God gives the increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:5; Galatians 6:3; Psalms 115:1"> So then neither he who plants is anything, nor he who waters, but God who gives the increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 2:6; Matthew 16:27; 2 John 1:8"> Now he who plants and he who waters are one, and each one will receive his own reward according to his own labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> For we are co-workers with God; you are God's field, you are God's building.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:11; Ephesians 2:20; Romans 15:20"> According to the grace of God which was given to me, as a wise architect I have laid the foundation, but another builds on it. But let each one take heed how he builds on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20; Isaiah 28:16; 2 Timothy 2:19"> For no other foundation can anyone lay, other than that which is laid, which is Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3; 2 Timothy 3:7; 2 Timothy 2:20"> Now if anyone builds on this foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, straw,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 1:7; 1 Corinthians 3:14–15; Revelation 20:12"> each one's work shall be made manifest; for the Day shall expose it, because it shall be revealed by fire; and the fire shall test each one's work, as to what sort it is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:8; Matthew 25:21–23; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> If anyone's work which he has built remains, he shall receive a reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:12–13; Jude 1:23"> If anyone's work is burned up, he shall suffer loss; but he himself shall be saved, yet so as through fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; Ezekiel 36:27; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> Do you not know that you are the temple of God, and that the Spirit of God dwells in you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:18–20; Ezekiel 43:12; Numbers 19:20"> If anyone destroys the temple of God, God shall destroy him. For the temple of God is holy, which temple you are.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Avoid Worldly Wisdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Isaiah 5:21; Galatians 6:3"> Let no one deceive himself. If anyone among you seems to be wise in this age, let him become a fool, that he may become wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 5:13; Psalms 141:10; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> For the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God. For it is written, “He catches the wise in their craftiness”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 94:11; Colossians 2:8; Romans 1:21"> and again, “The Lord knows the thoughts of the wise, that they are useless.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Romans 8:28; Jeremiah 9:23–24"> Therefore let no one boast in men. For all things are yours:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–12; Philippians 1:21; 1 Corinthians 9:19–22"> whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas, or the world or life or death, or things present or things to come—all things are yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; 1 Corinthians 11:3; John 17:9–10"> And you are Christ's, and Christ is God's.</span>
</div>
