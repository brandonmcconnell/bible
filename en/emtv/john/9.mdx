## A Man Born Blind Receives His Sight

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And passing by, He saw a man blind from birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 13:2; John 9:34; Acts 28:4"> And His disciples asked Him, saying, “Rabbi, who sinned, this man or his parents, that he was born blind?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:40; Ecclesiastes 9:1–2"> Jesus answered, “Neither this man nor his parents sinned, but so that the works of God should be manifest in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:35; Ephesians 5:16; John 4:34"> I must work the works of Him who sent Me while it is day; the night is coming when no one can work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; Acts 13:47"> While I am in the world, I am the light of the world.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 8:23; Mark 7:33; Revelation 3:18"> Having said these things, He spat on the ground and made clay from the saliva; and He applied the clay on the eyes of the blind man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5; John 9:11; John 11:37"> And He said to him, “Go, wash in the pool of Siloam” (which is translated, Sent). So he went and washed, and came back seeing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; 1 Samuel 21:11; Luke 18:35"> Therefore the neighbors and those who previously saw him, that he was blind, were saying, “Is not this he who sat and begged?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Some said, “This is he.” Others said, “He looks like him.” But he kept saying, “I am he.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 9:26; John 9:21; Ecclesiastes 11:5"> Therefore they said to him, “How were your eyes opened?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 9:27; Jeremiah 36:17"> He answered and said, “A Man called Jesus made clay and anointed my eyes, and He said to me, ‘Go to the pool of Siloam and wash.’ When I had gone and washed, I received my sight.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 5:11–13; Exodus 2:18–20; John 7:11"> Then they said to him, “Where is He?” He said, “I do not know.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Pharisees Excommunicate the Healed Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:57; John 11:46–47; John 12:42"> They brought him who was once blind to the Pharisees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:9; Luke 13:10–17; Matthew 12:1–14"> Now it was the Sabbath day when Jesus made the clay and opened his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:26–27; John 9:10–11"> Then again the Pharisees were asking him how he received his sight. And he said to them, “He put clay on my eyes, and I washed, and I see.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:43; John 10:19; Matthew 12:2"> Therefore some of the Pharisees were saying, “This Man is not from God, because He does not keep the Sabbath.”<br />Others said, “How can a sinful man do such signs?” And there was a division among them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:19; John 6:14; Acts 3:22–26"> They said to the blind man again, “What do you say about Him, since He has opened your eyes?”<br />He said, “He is a prophet.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 3:15–19; John 5:44; Isaiah 26:11"> But the Jews did not believe concerning him, that he was blind and received his sight, until they called the parents of him who had received his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:8–9; Acts 4:14; Acts 3:10"> And they asked them, saying, “Is this your son, who you say was born blind? How then does he now see?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And his parents answered them and said, “We know that this is our son, and that he was born blind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> but how he now sees we do not know, or who opened his eyes we do not know. He is of age; ask him. He will speak for himself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 7:13; John 16:2; Luke 6:22"> These things his parents said because they feared the Jews, for the Jews had agreed already that if anyone should confess that He was Christ, he would be excommunicated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 9:21"> On account of this his parents said, “He is of age; ask him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 7:19; John 9:16; John 8:49"> Therefore they called a second time the man who was blind, and said to him, “Give glory to God! We know that this Man is a sinner.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 5:10; John 9:30; John 5:11"> He answered and said, “Whether He is a sinner or not I do not know. One thing I do know: that although I was blind, now I see.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then they said to him again, “What did He do to you? How did He open your eyes?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:67; John 9:10–15"> He answered them, “I told you already, and you did not listen. Why do you wish to hear it again? Do you also want to become His disciples?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 2:17; John 7:19; Matthew 27:39"> Then they reviled him and said, “You are His disciple, but we are Moses' disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:14; Numbers 12:2–7; Acts 7:35"> We know that God has spoken to Moses; but as for this Man, we do not know where He is from.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; Psalms 119:18; Isaiah 29:18"> The man answered and said to them, “Why, this is a remarkable thing, that you do not know where He is from; yet He opened my eyes!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 28:9"> Now we know that God does not hear sinners; but if anyone is God-fearing and does His will, He hears him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Revelation 16:18; Job 20:4"> Since eternity past it was unheard of that anyone opened the eyes of one having been born blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 9:16; John 3:2; Acts 5:38–39"> If this Man were not from God, He could do nothing.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 9:22; John 9:2; Isaiah 66:5"> They answered and said to him, “You were completely born in sins, and do you teach us?” And they cast him out.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Affirms His Deity

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 20:31; 1 John 5:13; John 10:36"> Jesus heard that they had cast him out; and having found him, He said to him, “Do you believe in the Son of God?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Song of Solomon 5:9; John 1:38"> He answered and said, “And who is He, Lord, that I may believe in Him?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 4:26; Psalms 25:8–9; John 14:21–23"> And Jesus said to him, “You have both seen Him and it is He who is speaking with you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; Matthew 28:9; Luke 24:52"> Then he said, “Lord, I believe!” And he worshiped Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:19; John 12:46; Acts 26:18"> And Jesus said, “For judgment I came into this world, that those who do not see may see, and those who see might become blind.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Matthew 15:12–14; John 7:47–52"> Then some of the Pharisees who were with Him heard these things, and said to Him, “Are we blind also?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; John 15:22–24; 1 John 1:8–10"> Jesus said to them, “If you were blind, you would have no sin; but now you say, ‘We see.’ Therefore your sin remains.</span>
</div>
</div>
