<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then the scribes and Pharisees brought to Him a woman who had been caught in adultery, and having stood her in the midst,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> they said to Him, “Teacher, we found this woman committing adultery, in the very act.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Ezekiel 16:38–40"> Now Moses, in our law, commanded to cast stones at such. Therefore what do You say?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:13; Luke 10:25; Ecclesiastes 3:7"> But this they said, testing Him, that they might have an accusation against Him. But Jesus, stooping down, began to write on the ground with His finger, not taking notice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:1–5; Romans 2:21–25; Romans 2:1–3"> And while they were continuing questioning Him, rising up, He said unto them, “He who is without sin among you, let him be the first to cast a stone at her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And again He stooped down and wrote on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:22; Romans 2:15; 1 John 3:20"> And those who heard it began to go out one by one, beginning with the oldest even to the last. And Jesus was left alone, and the woman being in the midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:11–12"> And standing up and seeing no one but the woman, Jesus said to her, “Woman, where are those accusers of yours? Has no one condemned you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 3:17; John 5:14; Isaiah 1:16–18"> She said, “No one, Lord.” And Jesus said to her, “Neither do I condemn you; go, and from now on sin no more.”</span>

## Jesus is the Light of the World

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 12:46; John 9:5; John 12:35"> Then Jesus spoke to them again, saying, “I am the light of the world. He that follows Me shall by no means walk in darkness, but will have the light of life.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 5:31–47"> Therefore the Pharisees said to Him, “You testify about Yourself; Your testimony is not true.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:28; John 13:3; John 8:42"> Jesus answered and said to them, “Even if I should testify about Myself, My testimony is true, because I know where I came from and where I am going; but you do not know where I come from or where I am going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; John 7:24; John 12:47"> You judge according to the flesh; I judge no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:31; John 8:29; John 16:32"> But even if I do judge, My judgment is true; because I am not alone, but I am with the Father who sent Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:6; Hebrews 10:28"> It is also written in your law that the testimony of two men is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:38; John 8:58; John 8:25"> I am the One testifying about Myself, and the Father who sent Me testifies about Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:3; John 8:54–55; John 14:6–9"> Then they said to Him, “Where is Your Father?” Jesus answered, “You know neither Me nor My Father. If you knew Me, you would also know My Father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:30; John 7:8; Mark 12:41"> These words Jesus spoke in the treasury, while teaching in the temple; and no one seized Him, for His time had not yet come.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Predicts His Departure

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:34; John 8:24; 1 Corinthians 15:17–18"> Then Jesus said to them again, “I am going away, and you will seek Me, and will die in your sin. Where I go you cannot come.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 8:52; Psalms 22:6; John 8:48"> So the Jews said, “Surely He will not kill Himself, will He, since He says, ‘Where I am going you cannot come’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 3:31; John 17:14; John 17:16"> And He said to them, “You are from below; I am from above. You are from this world; I am not from this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; Mark 16:16; John 3:18"> Therefore I said to you that you will die in your sins; for if you do not believe that I AM, you will die in your sins.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:17–29; Luke 22:67; John 1:22"> Then they said to Him, “Who are You?” And Jesus said to them, “Just what I have been saying to you from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 15:15; John 12:47–50; John 8:40"> I have many things to say and to judge concerning you, but He who sent Me is true; and the things which I heard from Him, these things I say to the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; John 8:43; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4"> They did not know that He was speaking to them about the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 5:19; John 5:30; John 6:38"> Therefore Jesus said to them, “When you lift up the Son of Man, then you will know that I AM, and from Myself I do nothing; but just as My Father taught Me, these things I speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:38; John 16:32; John 4:34"> And He who sent Me is with Me. The Father has not left Me alone, because I always do the things that are pleasing to Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 7:31; John 2:23; John 11:45"> While He spoke these words, many believed in Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Truth Shall Set You Free

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 John 1:9; John 15:4–9; 2 Timothy 3:14"> Then Jesus said to those Jews who had believed Him, “If you abide in My word, truly you are My disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 8:2; John 8:36; John 17:17"> And you shall know the truth, and the truth shall set you free.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:9; John 8:39; Genesis 15:13"> They answered Him, “We are Abraham's descendants, and have never been in bondage to anyone. How can you say, ‘You will become free’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 6:16; 2 Peter 2:19; Proverbs 5:22"> Jesus answered them, “Most assuredly I say to you, that everyone that practices sin is a slave of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–7; Luke 15:31; Romans 8:15–17"> And a slave does not remain in the house forever, but a son remains forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Galatians 5:1; Romans 8:2"> Therefore if the Son sets you free, you will be truly free.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Abraham's Seed and Satan's Seed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 8:45–47; John 7:1"> “I know that you are Abraham's seed, but you seek to kill Me, because My word finds no place in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 5:19; John 8:44; John 8:41"> I speak what I have seen with My Father, and you do what you have seen with your father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; John 8:37; Romans 9:7"> They answered and said to Him, “Abraham is our father.” Jesus said to them, “If you were Abraham's children, you would do the works of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 8:26; Galatians 4:29; Galatians 4:16"> But now you seek to kill Me, a Man who has spoken to you the truth which I heard from God. Abraham did not do this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; John 8:38; Isaiah 63:16"> You do the works of your father.” Then they said to Him, “We were not born of fornication; we have one Father—God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:49; John 14:10; John 17:8"> Therefore Jesus said to them, “If God were your Father, you would have loved Me, for I came forth and have come from God; nor have I come from Myself, but He sent Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 12:39–40; Romans 8:7–8; John 7:17"> Why do you not understand what I say? Because you are unable to hear My word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 13:10; 1 John 3:12; 1 John 3:8–10"> You are of your father the devil, and the lusts of your father you desire to do. He was a murderer from the beginning, and has not stood in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he tells a lie, he speaks from his own resources, for he is a liar, and the father of lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Galatians 4:16; John 18:37"> But because I tell the truth, you do not believe Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; John 16:8; 1 Peter 2:22"> Which of you convicts Me of sin? And if I tell the truth, why do you not believe Me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 10:26–27; 2 John 1:9; John 18:37"> He who is of God hears God's words; therefore you do not hear them, because you are not of God.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Before Abraham Was, I AM

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 7:20; John 8:52; Matthew 10:5"> Then the Jews answered and said to Him, “Do we not say well that You are a Samaritan, and You have a demon?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Peter 2:23; Isaiah 49:3; John 13:31–32"> Jesus answered, “I do not have a demon; but I honor My Father, and you dishonor Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 5:41; John 8:54; John 7:18"> And I do not seek My own glory; there is One who seeks it and who judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 5:24; John 3:15–16"> Most assuredly I say to you, if anyone keeps My word he shall by no means experience death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 9:24; John 8:48; Hebrews 2:9"> Then the Jews said to Him, “Now we know that You have a demon! Abraham and the prophets died, and You say, ‘If anyone keeps My word he shall by no means taste death.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 4:12; Romans 9:5; Hebrews 7:1–7"> Are You greater than our father Abraham, who died? Also the prophets died. Whom do You make Yourself out to be?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 8:50; Proverbs 25:27; 2 Peter 1:17"> Jesus answered, “If I glorify Myself, My glory is nothing. It is My Father who glorifies Me, of whom you say that He is our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 8:19; John 8:44; John 7:28–29"> And yet you have not known Him, but I know Him. And if I would say that I do not know Him, I would be like you, a liar; but I do know Him, and I keep His word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13; Genesis 22:18; Galatians 3:7–9"> Your father Abraham rejoiced to see My day, and he saw it and was glad.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> Then the Jews said to Him, “You are not yet fifty years old, and have You seen Abraham?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Exodus 3:14; Colossians 1:17; John 17:5"> Jesus said to them, “Most assuredly I say to you, before Abraham was, I AM.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 11:8; John 10:30–33; John 10:39–40"> Therefore they picked up stones to throw at Him; but Jesus hid Himself and went out of the temple, having gone through their midst, and so passed by.</span>
</div>
