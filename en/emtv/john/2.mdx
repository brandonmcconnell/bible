## Jesus at Cana of Galilee: The First Miracle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 4:46; Proverbs 31:10–12; John 21:2"> And on the third day there was a wedding in Cana of Galilee, and the mother of Jesus was there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:40–49; Matthew 10:40–42; 1 Corinthians 7:39"> Now both Jesus and His disciples were invited to the wedding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Philippians 4:6; Isaiah 24:11"> And when the wine had given out, the mother of Jesus said to Him, “They have no wine.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:6; John 13:1; John 7:30"> Jesus said to her, “Woman, what have I to do with you? My hour has not yet come.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:14; Genesis 6:22; Hebrews 11:8"> His mother said to the servants, “Whatever He may say to you, do.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 3:25; Hebrews 9:10; Ephesians 5:26"> Now there were six stone water jars standing there, according to the purification rites of the Jews, each holding two or three measures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:2–6; John 2:5; John 2:3"> Jesus said to them, “Fill the water jars with water.” And they filled them up to the top.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 2:9; Proverbs 3:5–6; Romans 13:7"> And He said to them, “Draw some out now, and take it to the head steward.” And they took it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 4:46; John 7:17; Psalms 119:100"> Now when the head steward tasted the water which had become wine, and did not know where it came from (but the servants who had drawn the water knew), the head steward called the bridegroom</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 7:16–17; Proverbs 9:1–6; Luke 16:25"> and said to him, “Every man first sets out the good wine, and when they have well drunk, then the inferior. But you have kept the good wine until now!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:30–31; John 1:14; John 3:2"> This beginning of the signs Jesus did in Cana of Galilee, and manifested His glory; and His disciples believed in Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; Matthew 13:55–56; Mark 6:3"> After this He went down to Capernaum, He, His mother, His brothers, and His disciples; and there they remained not many days.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Cleanses the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:41; John 11:55; John 2:23"> Now the Passover of the Jews was near, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:45–46; Mark 11:15; Mark 11:17"> And He found in the temple those who sold oxen and sheep and doves, and the moneychangers sitting there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:6; Zechariah 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> And having made a scourge out of cords, He drove them all out of the temple, with the sheep and the oxen, and He poured out the coins of the moneychangers, and He overturned the tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 21:13; Jeremiah 7:11; Mark 11:17"> And He said to those selling the doves, “Take these things away from here! Do not make My Father's house a house of merchandise!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 69:9; Psalms 119:139"> Then His disciples remembered that it was written, “The zeal for Your house will consume Me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 8:11; Mark 11:27–28"> Therefore the Jews answered and said to Him, “What sign do You show us, seeing that You do these things?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Mark 15:29; Matthew 27:40"> Jesus answered and said to them, “Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then the Jews said, “It has taken forty-six years to build this temple, and will You raise it up in three days?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:20–22"> But He was speaking about the temple of His body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 12:16; John 16:4; Luke 24:7–8"> Therefore, when He was raised up from among the dead, His disciples remembered that He spoke this thing, and they believed the Scripture and the word which Jesus had spoken.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus at the Passover Feast

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 7:31; Luke 8:13; John 6:14"> Now when He was in Jerusalem at the Passover Feast, many believed on His name when they saw the signs that He was doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:64; Hebrews 4:13; Acts 1:24"> But Jesus did not trust Himself to them, because He knew all men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 13:11; Matthew 9:4; John 6:64"> and had no need that anyone should testify concerning man, for He himself knew what was in man.</span>
</div>
</div>
