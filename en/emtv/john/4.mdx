## Jesus and the Samaritan Woman

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 3:26; John 3:22; 2 Corinthians 4:5"> Therefore, when the Lord knew that the Pharisees had heard that Jesus was making and was baptizing more disciples than John</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:48; 1 Corinthians 1:13–17"> (although Jesus Himself was not baptizing, but His disciples),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; John 10:40; John 2:11"> He left Judea and went away into Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 17:11; Luke 2:49; Luke 9:51–52"> But He needed to pass through Samaria.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 24:32; Genesis 33:19; Genesis 48:22"> Therefore He came to a city of Samaria, which is called Sychar, near the piece of land which Jacob gave to his son Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 2:17; Matthew 8:24"> Now Jacob's well was there. Therefore Jesus, having become tired from the journey, was sitting beside the well. It was about the sixth hour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; 1 Kings 17:10; Genesis 24:43"> A woman of Samaria came to draw water. Jesus said to her, “Give Me a drink.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:13; John 4:39; John 4:5"> (For His disciples had gone away into the city, that they might buy provisions).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Matthew 10:5; 2 Kings 17:24–41"> Then the woman of Samaria said to Him, “How is it that You, being a Jew, ask a drink from me, a Samaritan woman?” For Jews do not associate with Samaritans.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:14; Revelation 7:17; John 7:37–39"> Jesus answered and said to her, “If you had known the gift of God, and who it is that says to you, ‘Give Me a drink,’ you would have asked Him, and He would have given to you living water.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 3:4"> The woman said to Him, “Sir, You have nothing to draw with, and the well is deep. Where then do You get that living water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 4:6; John 8:53; Matthew 12:42"> Are You greater than our father Jacob, who gave us the well, and drank of it himself, as well as his sons and his livestock?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:27; Isaiah 65:13–14; Luke 16:24"> Jesus answered and said to her, “Whoever drinks of this water will thirst again,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:38–39; John 6:35; Isaiah 49:10"> but whoever drinks of the water which I shall give him will by no means ever thirst again. But the water which I shall give him will become in him a fountain of water springing up into eternal life.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 6:34; Romans 8:5; Psalms 4:6"> The woman said to Him, “Sir, give me this water, that I may not thirst, nor come here to draw.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23; John 4:18"> Jesus said to her, “Go, call your husband, and come here.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The woman answered and said, “I have no husband.”<br />Jesus said to her, “You have said well, ‘I have no husband,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:20; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Romans 7:3"> for you have had five husbands, and the one whom you now have is not your husband; in this you have said truly.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 6:14; John 9:17"> The woman said to Him, “Sir, I perceive that You are a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 12:6–7; Deuteronomy 27:12; Deuteronomy 11:29"> Our fathers worshiped on this mountain, and you Jews say that in Jerusalem is the place where it is necessary to worship.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:8; Malachi 1:11; John 4:23"> Jesus said to her, “Woman, believe Me, the hour is coming when you will neither on this mountain, nor in Jerusalem, worship the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 17:23; Isaiah 2:3; Romans 9:4–5"> You worship what you do not know; we Jews worship what we know, because salvation is of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 3:3; John 5:25; Matthew 15:7–9"> But the hour is coming, and now is, when the true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and truth; for the Father is seeking such to worship Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Matthew 15:8–9; Philippians 3:3"> God is Spirit, and those who worship Him must worship in spirit and truth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 1:49; Daniel 9:24–26; John 1:41–42"> The woman said to Him, “I know that Messiah is coming” (who is called Christ). “When He comes, He will proclaim to us all things.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:24; Mark 14:61–62; Romans 10:20–21"> Jesus said to her, “I am He, the One speaking to you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Disciples Return

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 4:8–9"> And at this point His disciples came, and they marveled that He was speaking with a woman; however no one said, “What do You seek?” or, “Why are You speaking with her?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 4:7; Luke 24:33; Mark 16:8–10"> The woman then left her water jar, went her way into the city, and said to the men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; 1 Corinthians 14:24–25; John 4:25"> “Come, see a Man who told me everything I ever did. Could this be the Christ?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 5:20; Acts 8:5–8; Luke 17:16–18"> They went out of the city and were coming to Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:38; Genesis 24:33; Acts 16:30–34"> But in the meantime the disciples urged Him, saying, “Rabbi, eat.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 23:12; Psalms 119:103; John 4:34"> But He said to them, “I have food to eat of which you do not know.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 16:6–11; Luke 9:45"> Therefore the disciples were saying to one another, “Has anyone brought Him something to eat?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 6:38; John 5:30; Job 23:12"> Jesus said to them, “My food is to do the will of Him who sent Me, and to finish His work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 10:2–3; Matthew 9:37–38; John 4:30"> Do you not say, ‘There are still four months and the harvest comes’? Behold, I say to you, lift up your eyes and see the fields, for they are already white for harvest!</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="James 5:19–20; Romans 1:13; 1 Corinthians 3:5–9"> And he who reaps receives wages, and gathers fruit unto eternal life, that both he who sows and he who reaps may rejoice together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Job 31:8; Luke 19:21"> For in this the saying is true: ‘One sows and another reaps.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 1:7; 2 Chronicles 36:15; Acts 10:42–43"> I sent you to reap that for which you have not labored; others have labored, and you have entered into their labors.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Christ, Savior of the World

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 4:29; John 4:5; John 10:41–42"> And many of the Samaritans of that city believed in Him because of the word of the woman testifying, “He told me everything I ever did.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Acts 16:15; Luke 24:29"> So when the Samaritans had come to Him, they were asking Him to stay with them; and He stayed there two days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4–5; Acts 1:8; Acts 8:12"> And many more believed because of His word.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 1:29; 1 John 5:20"> Then they were saying to the woman, “No longer because of your speech do we believe, for we ourselves have heard and know that this is truly the Christ, the Savior of the world.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus at Galilee

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 4:13; Romans 15:8; Mark 7:27–28"> Now after the two days He went out from there and went to Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 13:57; Luke 4:24; Mark 6:4"> For Jesus Himself testified that a prophet has no honor in his own country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 2:13–16; Deuteronomy 16:16"> Therefore when He came into Galilee, the Galileans received Him, having seen all the things which He did in Jerusalem at the feast; for they also went to the feast.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 2:1–11; Luke 7:2; Hosea 5:15"> Then Jesus came again to Cana of Galilee where He had turned the water into wine. And there was a certain nobleman whose son was sick in Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 4:3; John 11:21; John 11:32"> When he heard that Jesus had come out of Judea into Galilee, he went to Him and asked Him that He might come down and heal his son, for he was about to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 20:29; Hebrews 2:4; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> Then Jesus said to him, “Unless you people see signs and wonders, you will by no means believe.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 5:23; Psalms 88:10–12; Psalms 40:17"> The nobleman said to Him, “Sir, come down before my child dies!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 8:13; Mark 7:29–30; Romans 4:20–21"> Jesus said to him, “Go; your son lives.” And the man believed the word that Jesus spoke to him, and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Kings 17:23; John 4:50; John 4:53"> And already as he was going, his servants met him and reported, saying, “Your child lives!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> Then he inquired from them the hour at which he got better. And they said to him, “Yesterday at the seventh hour the fever left him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Luke 19:9; Acts 16:34"> Therefore the father knew that it was at the same hour in which Jesus said to him, “Your son lives.” And he himself believed, and his whole household.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 4:45; John 2:1–11"> This was now the second sign that Jesus did, after He had come out of Judea into Galilee.</span>
</div>
</div>
