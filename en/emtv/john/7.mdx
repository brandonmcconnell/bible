<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:16–19; Deuteronomy 16:13–16; Exodus 23:16–17"> Now the Jewish Feast of Tabernacles was near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; John 7:5; Luke 8:19"> His brothers therefore said to Him, “Depart from here and go into Judea, so that Your disciples also may see the works that You do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:20; Proverbs 18:1–2; Matthew 23:5"> For no one does anything in secret while he himself seeks to be known publicly. If You do these things, show Yourself to the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 3:21; John 1:11–13; Micah 7:5–6"> For not even His brothers believed in Him.</span>

## Jesus Sought at the Feast of Tabernacles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Matthew 10:16; Isaiah 42:2–3"> But when His brothers had gone up, then also He Himself went up to the feast, not openly, but in private.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:56"> Then the Jews were seeking Him at the feast, and said, “Where is He?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 7:40–43; John 9:16; John 7:32"> And there was much murmuring among the crowds concerning Him. Some on the one hand were saying, “He is a good Man”; while others were saying, “No, on the contrary, He deceives the people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 9:22; John 19:38; John 20:19"> However, no one spoke openly of Him for fear of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:28; Luke 19:47; John 7:37"> But now about the middle of the feast Jesus went up into the temple and began to teach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54"> And the Jews were marveling, saying, “How has this Man become learned, since He has not been educated?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:10; John 14:24; John 17:8"> Therefore Jesus answered them and said, “My doctrine is not Mine, but His who sent Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:31–32; John 8:43; Jeremiah 31:33–34"> If anyone desires to do His will, he will know about the doctrine, whether it is from God, or whether I am speaking on My own authority.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 5:41; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33; John 13:31–32"> He who speaks from himself is seeking his own glory; but He who seeks the glory of the One who sent Him is true, and there is no unrighteousness in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 1:17; John 11:53; Matthew 12:14"> Has not Moses given you the law, yet none of you keeps the law? Why are you seeking to kill Me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:20; John 8:48; John 8:52"> The crowd answered and said, “You have a demon. Who is seeking to kill You?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:23; John 5:2–11"> Jesus answered and said to them, “I did one work, and you all marvel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Genesis 17:10–14; Romans 4:9–11"> Because of this Moses has given you circumcision (not that it is from Moses, but from the fathers), and you circumcise a man on the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:5; John 5:14–16; Matthew 12:2"> If a man receives circumcision on the Sabbath, so that the law of Moses should not be broken, are you angry with Me because I made a man completely well on the Sabbath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–4; James 2:9; John 8:15"> Do not judge according to appearance, but judge with righteous judgment.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Questions about Jesus' Identity

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 7:10–11"> Therefore some of the people of Jerusalem were saying, “Is this not He whom they are seeking to kill?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7–8; John 7:48; John 12:42"> And look! He is speaking publicly, and they say nothing to Him. Can it be that the rulers know indeed that this is truly the Messiah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:41–42; John 6:42"> However, we know where this Man is from; but whenever the Messiah comes, no one knows where He is from.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:42; John 8:19; John 8:26"> Therefore Jesus cried out, as He was teaching in the temple, saying, “You both know Me, and you know where I am from; and I have not come of my own accord, but He who sent Me is true, whom you do not know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:55; Matthew 11:27; John 17:18"> I know Him, for I am from Him, and He sent Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 8:20; John 10:39; John 7:6"> Therefore they were seeking to arrest Him; but no one laid a hand on Him, because His hour had not yet come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:42; Matthew 12:23; John 10:41–42"> But many of the crowd believed in Him, and were saying, “When the Messiah comes, will He do more signs than these which this Man has done?”</span>
