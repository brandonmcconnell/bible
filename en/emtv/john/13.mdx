## Jesus Washes the Disciples' Feet

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 12:23; 1 John 4:19; John 13:34"> Now before the feast of the Passover, when Jesus knew that His hour had come that He should depart from this world to the Father, having loved His own who were in the world, He loved them to the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:13–17; Acts 5:3; John 13:26–27"> And after supper, the devil having already put it into the heart of Judas Iscariot, the son of Simon, to betray Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 3:35; Matthew 11:27; Hebrews 1:2"> Jesus, knowing that the Father had given all things into His hands, and that He had come from God and was going to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 22:27; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Luke 12:37"> He rose from supper and laid aside His garments, and having taken a towel, He girded Himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:44; Psalms 51:2; Genesis 18:4"> Then He poured water into a basin and began to wash the feet of the disciples, and to dry them with the towel with which He was girded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 3:11–14; John 1:27; Luke 5:8"> Then He came to Simon Peter. And Peter said to Him, “Lord, do You wash my feet?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:8; John 12:16; John 14:26"> Jesus answered and said to him, “What I am doing you do not understand now, but afterward you will understand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25; Acts 22:16; Titus 3:5"> Peter said to Him, “You shall never wash my feet!” Jesus answered him, “Unless I wash you, you have no part with Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 51:7; Psalms 51:2; 1 Peter 3:21"> Simon Peter said to Him, “Lord, not my feet only, but also my hands and my head!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:3; 2 Corinthians 7:1; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> Jesus said to him, “He that is bathed need only to wash his feet, but is completely clean; and you are clean, but not all of you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 13:26; John 13:2; John 13:21"> For He knew who would betray Him; for this reason He said, “You are not all clean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 13:7; John 13:4; Ezekiel 24:24"> So when He had washed their feet, and had taken His garments, He reclined again, and said to them, “Do you know what I have done to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 6:46; 1 Corinthians 12:3; John 11:28"> You call me Teacher and Lord, and you say correctly, for so I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Acts 20:35; Romans 12:16"> Therefore if I, your Lord and your Teacher, have washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another's feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 11:29; 1 Peter 3:17–18"> For I gave you an example, that just as I did to you, you also should do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:40; John 15:20; Matthew 10:24–25"> Most assuredly I say to you, a servant is not greater than his master, nor a messenger greater than he who sent him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 11:28; James 4:17; James 1:25"> If you know these things, blessed are you if you do them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Identifies His Betrayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Matthew 26:23; John 17:12"> “I do not speak concerning all of you. I know those whom I chose; but that the Scripture may be fulfilled, ‘He who eats bread with Me has lifted up his heel against Me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; John 14:29; John 16:4"> From now on I tell you before it happens, so that when it happens, you may believe that I AM.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Luke 9:48; Matthew 25:40"> Most assuredly I say to you, he who receives whomever I send receives Me; and he who receives Me receives Him who sent Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 13:18; Matthew 26:21; Mark 14:18"> When Jesus had said these things, He was troubled in His spirit, and testified and said, “Most assuredly I say to you, one of you will betray Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 22:23; Matthew 26:22; Mark 14:19"> Therefore the disciples were looking at one another, perplexed about whom He was speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 19:26; John 21:7; John 21:20"> Now there was reclining on Jesus' bosom one of His disciples, the one whom Jesus loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Luke 1:22; Acts 13:16"> Simon Peter therefore motioned to him to inquire who it might be about whom He was speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 21:20; Genesis 44:4–12; Esther 7:5"> Then, leaning back thus on Jesus' chest, he said to Him, “Lord, who is it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 26:23; John 13:30; Mark 14:19–20"> Jesus answered, “It is the one to whom I shall give a piece of bread when I have dipped it.” And having dipped the bread, He gave it to Judas Iscariot, the son of Simon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:3; John 13:2; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> And after the piece of bread, then Satan entered him. Therefore Jesus said to him, “What you do, do quickly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> But no one reclining at the table knew for what purpose He said this to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 12:5–6; John 13:1; Ephesians 4:28"> For some were thinking, since Judas had the money box, that Jesus was saying to him, “Buy the things of which we need for the feast,” or that he should give something to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Job 24:13–15; Proverbs 4:16"> Therefore having received the piece of bread, he then went out immediately. And it was night.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The New Commandment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 14:13; 1 Peter 4:11; John 7:39"> When he had gone out, Jesus said, “Now the Son of Man has been glorified, and God has been glorified in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Isaiah 53:10–12; John 17:4–6"> If God has been glorified in Him, God will also glorify Him in Himself, and will glorify Him immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 7:33–34; John 8:21–24; John 16:16–22"> Little children, yet a little while I am with you. You will seek Me; and as I said to the Jews, ‘Where I am going, you cannot come,’ so now I say to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Leviticus 19:18; John 15:17"> A new commandment I give to you, that you love one another; just as I have loved you, that you also love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 4:20–21; 1 John 3:10–14; 1 John 2:10"> By this all will know that you are My disciples, if you have love for one another.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Foretells Peter's Denial

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Peter 1:14; John 13:33; John 21:21–22"> Simon Peter said to Him, “Lord, where are You going?” Jesus answered him, “Where I am going you cannot follow Me now, but later you will follow Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 21:13; Acts 20:24; Mark 14:27–31"> Peter said to Him, “Lord, why can I not follow You now? I will lay down my life for You.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 22:34; Matthew 26:34; Mark 14:30"> Jesus answered him, “Will you lay down your life for Me? Most assuredly I say to you, the rooster shall by no means crow till you deny Me three times.</span>
</div>
