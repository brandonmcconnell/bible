## The Way, the Truth, and the Life

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 26:3; John 16:22–23; Isaiah 12:2–3"> “Let not your heart be troubled; you believe in God, believe also in Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:1; John 13:36; Hebrews 13:14"> In My Father's house are many mansions; if it were not so, I would have told you. I go to prepare a place for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; John 12:26; 2 Timothy 2:12"> And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again and I will receive you unto Myself; so that where I am, there you may be also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 10:9; John 16:28; John 13:3"> And where I am going you know, and the way you know.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 11:16; Mark 9:19; John 20:25–28"> Thomas said to Him, “Lord, we do not know where You are going, and how can we know the way?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 4:12; John 10:9; Ephesians 2:18"> Jesus said to him, “I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; John 8:19; Luke 10:22"> “If you had known Me, you would have known My Father also; and from now on you know Him and you have seen Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 33:18–23; Revelation 22:3–5; John 1:43–46"> Philip said to Him, “Lord, show us the Father, and it is sufficient for us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; John 12:45; John 14:7"> Jesus said to him, “Have I been with you for so long a time, and yet you have not known Me, Philip? He that has seen Me has seen the Father; so how can you say, ‘Show us the Father’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 5:19; John 10:38; John 10:30"> Do you not believe that I am in the Father, and the Father in Me? The words which I speak to you I do not speak on My own authority; but the Father who abides in Me does the works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:38; John 10:25; John 5:36"> Believe Me that I am in the Father and the Father is in Me, but if not, believe Me for the sake of the works themselves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:21; Mark 16:17; Luke 10:17–19"> “Most assuredly I say to you, he that believes in Me, the works which I do he will do also; and greater works than these he will do, because I go to My Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:7; Mark 11:24; Matthew 7:7"> And whatever you may ask in My name, this I will do, so that the Father may be glorified in the Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> If you should ask anything in My name, I will do it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Promise of the Holy Spirit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 2:3–5; 1 John 5:2–3; John 14:21–24"> “If you love Me, keep My commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:26; John 15:26; John 16:7–15"> And I will ask the Father, and He will give you another Helper, so that He may abide with you forever,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; John 16:13; 1 John 2:27"> the Spirit of truth, whom the world cannot receive, because it neither sees Him nor knows Him; but you know Him, because He abides with you and shall be in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 14:3; Psalms 23:4; Matthew 18:20"> I will not leave you orphans; I am coming to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:16; John 7:33; Hebrews 7:25"> “Yet a little while and the world will see Me no more, but you will see Me. Because I live, you will live also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 15:5–7; Galatians 2:20; John 17:21–23"> In that day you will know that I am in My Father, and you in Me, and I in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 14:15; 1 John 5:3; 1 John 2:5"> He that has My commandments and keeps them, it is he who loves Me. And he who loves Me shall be loved by My Father, and I will love him and will manifest Myself to him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Luke 6:16; Matthew 10:3"> Judas (not Iscariot) said to Him, “Lord, and what has happened that You are about to manifest Yourself to us, and not to the world?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 14:21; John 14:15; Revelation 3:20–21"> Jesus answered and said to him, “If anyone loves Me, he will keep My word; and My Father will love him, and We will come to him and we will make Our home with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 7:16; John 14:10; John 8:42"> He who does not love Me does not keep My words; and the word which you hear is not Mine but the Father's who sent Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 17:6–8; John 13:19; John 16:12"> “These things I have spoken to you while I remain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 15:26; John 14:16; 1 John 2:27"> But the Helper, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in My name, He will teach you all things, and will remind you of all things which I said to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Philippians 4:7; John 16:33; Isaiah 41:10"> Peace I leave with you, My peace I give to you; not as the world gives do I give to you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 10:29–30; 1 Corinthians 15:24–28; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> You have heard that I said to you, ‘I am going away and coming back to you.’ If you loved Me, you would have rejoiced that I said, ‘I am going to the Father,’ for My Father is greater than I.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 13:19; John 16:4–31; Matthew 24:24–25"> “And now I have told you before it happens, so that whenever it may happen, you may believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 5:19; 1 John 4:4; Revelation 12:9"> I will no longer speak many things with you, for the ruler of the world is coming, and he has nothing in Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 10:18; Philippians 2:8; John 12:49"> But that the world may know that I love the Father, and as the Father has charged Me, thus I do. Rise up, let us go from here.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
