## Jesus the Eternal Word of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; John 17:5; Revelation 19:13"> In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He was in the beginning with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:16–17; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Revelation 4:11"> All things were made through Him, and without Him nothing was made that was made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; John 5:26"> In Him was life, and the life was the light of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 3:19–20; John 12:36–40; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> And the light shines in the darkness, and the darkness did not comprehend it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## John the Baptist

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; John 3:28; Isaiah 40:3–5"> There was a man sent from God, whose name was John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:4; John 1:36; John 5:33–35"> This man came for a testimony, so that he might testify concerning the Light, so that all through him might believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:20; John 3:28; Acts 19:4"> He was not that Light, but was sent so that he might testify concerning the Light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 12:46; John 1:4; 1 John 2:8"> That was the true Light which lightens every man coming into the world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 17:25; 1 John 3:1; 1 Corinthians 2:8"> He was in the world, and the world was made through Him, and the world did not know Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Isaiah 53:2–3; John 3:32"> He came to His own, and His own did not receive Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 3:26; Romans 8:14; 1 John 3:1"> But as many as received Him, to them He gave the authority to become children of God, even to those who believe in His name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 3:9; James 1:18; 1 Peter 1:23"> who were born, not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God Takes a Body

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:9; 1 Timothy 3:16; Isaiah 40:5"> And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us, and we beheld His glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father, full of grace and truth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Colossians 1:17; Luke 3:16"> John testified concerning Him and has cried out, saying, “This was He of whom I said, ‘He who comes after me ranks before me, because He was before me.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:19; Romans 5:2; Ephesians 2:5–10"> And out of His fullness we have all received, and grace upon grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:14; John 14:6; Romans 3:19–26"> For the law was given through Moses, but grace and truth came through Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; John 6:46; Matthew 11:27"> No one has seen God at any time. The only begotten Son, who is in the bosom of the Father, He has revealed Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Witness of John the Baptist

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 3:15–18; John 10:24; Acts 19:4"> And this is the testimony of John, when the Jews sent priests and Levites from Jerusalem so that they might ask him, “Who are you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 1:7–8; Matthew 3:11–12; John 3:28–36"> He confessed, and did not deny, but confessed, “I am not the Messiah.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Deuteronomy 18:15–18; Matthew 11:14"> And they asked him, “What then? Are you Elijah?”<br />He said, “I am not.”<br />“Are you the Prophet?”<br />And he answered, “No.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:13"> Then they said to him, “Who are you, that we may give an answer to those who sent us? What do you say about yourself?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Mark 1:3; Matthew 3:3"> He said: “I am<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The voice of one crying in the wilderness:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Make straight the way of the Lord,” ’</span><br />as Isaiah the prophet said.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 11:53; Matthew 23:13–15; Luke 11:39–44"> Now those who had been sent were from the Pharisees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18; Deuteronomy 18:15; John 1:20–22"> And they asked him, saying, “Why then do you baptize if you are not the Messiah, nor Elijah, nor the Prophet?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 1:10–11; Luke 3:16; Acts 1:5"> John answered them, saying, “I baptize with water, but there stands One among you whom you do not know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 1:30; Mark 1:7; Matthew 3:11"> He is the One who comes after me, who ranks before me, of whom I am not worthy that I should loose the strap of His sandal.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 10:40; John 3:23; Judges 7:24"> These things happened in Bethany, beyond the Jordan, where John was baptizing.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lamb of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 John 2:2; 1 John 3:5"> The next day he saw Jesus coming toward him, and said, “Behold! The Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:27; John 1:15; Luke 3:16"> This is the One about whom I said, ‘After me comes a Man who ranks before me, because He was before me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Luke 1:17; Malachi 4:2–5"> I did not know Him; but that He should be made manifest to Israel, therefore I came baptizing with water.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 1:10; Luke 3:22; Matthew 3:16"> And John testified, saying, “I saw the Spirit coming down from heaven like a dove, and He remained upon Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:5; Matthew 3:11; Luke 3:16"> I did not know Him, but He who sent me to baptize with water said to me, ‘Upon whom you see the Spirit descending and abiding on Him, this is He who baptizes with the Holy Spirit.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; John 1:49; Psalms 89:26–27"> And I have seen and have testified that this is the Son of God.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The First Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 3:25–26; Malachi 3:16"> Again, the next day, John stood with two of his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 1:29; 1 Peter 1:19–20; Isaiah 65:1–2"> And looking at Jesus as He was walking, he said, “Behold the Lamb of God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Romans 10:17; John 1:43; Zechariah 8:21"> The two disciples heard him speaking, and they followed Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 18:7; John 3:2; John 1:49"> Then Jesus turned and observed them following, and said to them, “What are you seeking?”<br />They said to Him, “Rabbi” (which is translated, Teacher), “where are You staying?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:29; Matthew 11:28–30; John 6:37"> He said to them, “Come and see.” They came and saw where He was staying, and they stayed with Him that day. It was about the tenth hour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 1:16–20; Matthew 4:18–22; John 1:40–42"> One of the two who heard John speak, and followed Him, was Andrew, Simon Peter's brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 4:25; John 1:45; Psalms 89:20"> He first found his own brother Simon, and said to him, “We have found the Messiah” (which is translated, the Christ).</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 21:15–17; 1 Corinthians 3:22; 1 Corinthians 15:5"> And he led him to Jesus.<br />Now when Jesus looked at him, He said, “You are Simon the son of Jonah. You shall be called Cephas” (which means Peter).</span>
</div>
</div>

## Philip and Nathanael

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 9:9; John 14:8; Isaiah 65:1"> On the next day He wanted to go to Galilee, and He found Philip and said to him, “Follow Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 12:21; Matthew 11:21; Mark 8:22"> Now Philip was from Bethsaida, the city of Andrew and Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18–22; Luke 24:27; Micah 5:2"> Philip found Nathanael and said to him, “We have found the One about whom Moses wrote in the Law and the Prophets—Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 7:52; John 7:41–42; John 4:29"> And Nathanael said to him, “Can anything good come out of Nazareth?”<br />Philip said to him, “Come and see.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Revelation 14:5; Psalms 32:2; Psalms 73:1"> Jesus saw Nathanael coming toward Him, and said about him, “Behold, an Israelite indeed, in whom is no guile!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; John 2:25; Psalms 139:1–2"> Nathanael said to Him, “From where do You know me?”<br />Jesus answered and said to him, “Before Philip called you, while you were under the fig tree, I saw you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:15; Zechariah 9:9; Matthew 27:42"> Nathanael answered and said to Him, “Rabbi, You are the Son of God! You are the King of Israel!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 11:40; John 20:29; Luke 1:45"> Jesus answered and said to him, “Because I said to you, ‘I saw you under the fig tree,’ do you believe? You will see greater things than these.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Acts 7:56; Luke 22:43"> And He said to him, “Most assuredly I say to you, from now on you shall see heaven opened, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of Man.”</span>
</div>
</div>
