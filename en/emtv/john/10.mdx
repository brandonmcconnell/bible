## The Parable of the Good Shepherd

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; John 10:8–10; Romans 16:18"> “Most assuredly I say to you, he who does not enter the sheepfold through the door, but climbs up some other way, he is a thief and a robber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 10:14; Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:4"> But he who enters through the door is the shepherd of the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:11–16; 2 Timothy 2:19; John 10:4"> To him the doorkeeper opens, and the sheep hear his voice; and he calls his own sheep by name and he leads them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:30; John 10:16; John 12:26"> And whenever he brings out his own sheep, he goes before them; and the sheep follow him, because they know his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 4:5–6; Colossians 2:6–10; Mark 4:24"> But they will by no means follow a stranger, but they will flee from him, for they do not know the voice of strangers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Psalms 82:5; Proverbs 28:5"> This illustration Jesus spoke to them, but they did not understand the things which He spoke to them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## I AM the Good Shepherd

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 14:6; Ephesians 2:18; John 10:1"> Then Jesus said to them again, “Most assuredly I say to you, I am the door of the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Jeremiah 23:1; John 10:1"> All who came before Me are thieves and robbers, but the sheep did not hear them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 14:6; Ephesians 2:18; John 10:7"> I am the door. If anyone enters by Me, he will be saved, and will go in and go out, and will find pasture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 19:10; John 6:51; John 12:47"> The thief does not come except to steal, and to kill, and to destroy. I have come that they may have life, and that they may have it more abundantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:14–15; Psalms 23:1; John 15:13"> “I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down His life for the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 20:29; Zechariah 11:16–17; Ezekiel 34:2–6"> But a hired hand, he who is not the shepherd, one who does not own the sheep, sees the wolf coming and leaves the sheep and flees; and the wolf catches the sheep and scatters them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:20; John 12:6; Acts 18:17"> But the hired hand flees because he is a hired hand, and is not concerned about the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 10:27; John 10:11; 2 Timothy 2:19"> I am the good shepherd; and I know My own, and I am known by My own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 1:4; John 10:11; Isaiah 53:10"> Just as the Father knows Me, I also know the Father; and I lay down My life for the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 56:8; John 11:52; 1 Peter 2:25"> And other sheep I have which are not of this fold; those also I must bring, and they will hear My voice; and there shall be one flock and one shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 10:18; John 10:11; Hebrews 2:9"> “For this reason My Father loves Me, because I lay down My life that I may take it up again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:9; Titus 2:14; John 2:19–21"> No one takes it from Me, but I lay it down of Myself. I have authority to lay it down, and I have authority to take it up again. This command I have received from My Father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:16; 1 Corinthians 3:3; John 7:40–43"> Therefore there was a division again among the Jews because of these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:20; Mark 3:21; John 8:47–48"> And many of them said, “He has a demon and is raving mad. Why do you listen to Him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 4:11; Isaiah 35:5–6; Psalms 94:9"> Others said, “These are not the words of one being demon-possessed. Can a demon open the eyes of the blind?”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Asserts His Deity

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Now it was the Feast of Dedication in Jerusalem, and it was winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 5:12; Acts 3:11"> And Jesus was walking in the temple, in Solomon's porch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 22:67–70; John 1:19; John 8:25"> Then the Jews encircled Him and said to Him, “How long will You keep us in suspense? If You are the Christ, tell us plainly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 10:38; John 8:58; John 14:11"> Jesus answered them, “I told you, and you do not believe. The works that I do in My Father's name, these things testify about Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:47; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; John 12:37–40"> But you do not believe, because you are not of My sheep, just as I said to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 10:16; John 10:14; Revelation 3:20"> My sheep hear My voice, and I know them, and they follow Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:39–40; John 6:37; John 3:15–16"> And I give to them eternal life, and they shall never perish; and no one will snatch them out of My hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:37; John 17:2; John 17:9"> My Father, who has given them to Me, is greater than all; and no one is able to snatch them out of My Father's hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:1–2; John 14:9; John 17:21–23"> I and the Father are one.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Jews Attempt to Stone Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 5:18; John 8:59; 1 Samuel 30:6"> Therefore the Jews took up stones again to stone Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 John 3:12; John 5:36; Psalms 35:12"> Jesus answered them, “Many good works I have shown you from My Father. For which of those works do you stone Me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 5:18; Leviticus 24:16; John 10:30"> The Jews answered Him, saying, “For a good work we do not stone You, but for blasphemy, and because You, being a Man, make Yourself God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 82:6–7; Exodus 7:1; Psalms 82:1"> Jesus answered them, “Is it not written in your law, ‘I said, “You are gods” ’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Romans 13:1; Luke 16:17"> If He called those gods, to whom the word of God came (and the Scripture cannot be broken),</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 5:17–18; John 3:17; Jeremiah 1:5"> do you say of Him whom the Father sanctified and sent into the world, ‘You are blaspheming,’ because I said, ‘I am the Son of God’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 15:24; John 10:25; John 12:37–40"> If I am not doing the works of My Father, do not believe Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 14:20; John 10:25; John 10:30"> but if I am doing them, even if you do not believe Me, believe the works, so that you may know and believe that the Father is in Me, and I in Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 7:30; John 8:59; John 7:44"> Therefore they were seeking again to seize Him, but He escaped from their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 1:28; John 11:54; John 7:1"> And He went away again beyond the Jordan to the place where John was first baptizing, and He stayed there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 1:33–34; John 1:29–30; Mark 1:37"> And many came to Him and said, “John indeed performed no sign, but everything which John said about this Man was true.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 4:41; John 2:23; John 7:31"> And many believed in Him there.</span>
</div>
</div>
