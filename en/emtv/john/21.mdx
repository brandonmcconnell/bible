## Jesus Appears to Seven Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:23; Matthew 28:16"> After these things Jesus showed Himself again to the disciples at the Sea of Tiberias, and in this manner He showed Himself:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:21–22; John 1:45–1; John 4:46"> Simon Peter, Thomas called the Twin, Nathanael of Cana in Galilee, the sons of Zebedee, and two others of His disciples were together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 5:5; Matthew 4:18–20; 2 Thessalonians 3:7–9"> Simon Peter said to them, “I am going fishing.”<br />They said to him, “We are coming with you also.” They went out and immediately got into the boat, and that night they caught nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 20:14; Luke 24:15–16; Luke 24:31"> But as daybreak had already come, Jesus stood upon the shore; however the disciples did not know that it was Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; Philippians 4:11–13; 1 John 2:13"> Then Jesus said to them, “Children, have you any food?”<br />They answered Him, “No.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:4–7; Acts 2:41; John 2:5"> And He said to them, “Cast the net on the right side of the boat, and you will find some.” Therefore they cast, and they were not able to haul it in because of the multitude of fish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 13:23; John 20:2; Matthew 14:28–29"> Therefore that disciple whom Jesus loved said to Peter, “It is the Lord!” Now when Simon Peter heard that it was the Lord, he put on his outer garment (for he had removed it), and he threw himself into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11"> But the other disciples came in the little boat (for they were not far from the land, but about two hundred cubits), dragging the net with fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 21:13; 1 Kings 19:5–6; John 21:10"> Then as they got off onto the land, they saw a charcoal fire laid there, and fish placed on it, and bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Jesus said to them, “Bring some of the fish which you have just caught.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 5:6–8; Acts 2:41"> Simon Peter went up and hauled the net onto the land, full of large fish, one hundred and fifty-three; and although there were so many, the net was not torn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:41; Luke 9:45; John 16:19"> Jesus said to them, “Come, eat breakfast.” Yet none of the disciples dared ask Him, “Who are You?”—knowing that it was the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 21:9; Acts 10:41; Luke 24:42–43"> Jesus then came and took the bread and gave it to them, and likewise the fish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 20:26; John 20:19"> This was now the third time Jesus was manifested to His disciples, having been raised from the dead.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Restores Peter

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; 1 John 4:19; Romans 15:1"> So when they had eaten breakfast, Jesus said to Simon Peter, “Simon, son of Jonah, do you love Me more than these?”<br />He said to Him, “Yes, Lord; You know that I care for You.”<br />He said to him, “Feed My lambs.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:2; 1 Peter 2:25"> He said to him again a second time, “Simon, son of Jonah, do you love Me?”<br />He said to Him, “Yes, Lord; You know that I care for You.”<br />He said to him, “Shepherd My sheep.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 13:38; John 16:30; Mark 14:72"> He said to him the third time, “Simon, son of Jonah, do you care for Me?” Peter was grieved because He said to him the third time, “Do you care for Me?”<br />And he said to Him, “Lord, You know all things; You know that I care for You.”<br />Jesus said to him, “Feed My sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 13:36; Acts 12:3–4; Acts 21:11"> Most assuredly I say to you, when you were younger, you used to dress yourself and used to walk where you wished; but when you are old, you will stretch out your hands, and another will dress you and will carry you where you do not wish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Peter 1:14; John 21:22; Matthew 10:38"> But this He said, signifying by what kind of death he would glorify God. And when He had spoken this, He said to him, “Follow Me.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus and the Beloved Disciple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 21:7; John 20:2; John 21:24"> Then Peter, turning around, saw the disciple whom Jesus loved following, who also had reclined on His chest at the supper, and said, “Lord, who is the one who betrays You?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 13:23–24; Acts 1:6–7; Matthew 24:3–4"> Peter, seeing him, said to Jesus, “But Lord, what about this man?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 2:25; Revelation 3:11; Mark 9:1"> Jesus said to him, “If I will that he remain till I come, what is that to you? You follow Me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:29; Job 33:13; Daniel 4:35"> Therefore this saying went out among the brothers that this disciple would not die. Yet Jesus did not say to him that he would not die, but, “If I will that he remain till I come, what is that to you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 19:35; John 15:27; 1 John 5:6"> This is the disciple who testifies of these things, and wrote these things; and we know that his testimony is true.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; John 20:30–31; Psalms 71:15"> And there are also many other things which Jesus did, which if they were written one by one, I suppose that not even the world itself would have room for the books which would be written. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
