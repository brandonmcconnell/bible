## The Word of Life

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 4:20; John 20:27; 1 John 4:14"> That which was from the beginning, which we have heard, which we have seen with our eyes, which we have gazed upon, and our hands have handled, concerning the Word of life—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:4; 1 John 5:20; 1 Timothy 3:16"> and the life was manifested, and we have seen and testify and proclaim to you the eternal life which was with the Father and was manifested to us—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:9; John 17:3; John 17:21"> that which we have seen and heard we proclaim to you, in order that you also may have fellowship with us; and indeed our fellowship is with the Father and with His Son Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:11; John 16:24; 2 John 1:12"> And these things we write to you that our joy may be complete.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Walking in the Light

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; James 1:17; Isaiah 60:19"> And this is the message which we have heard from Him and we announce to you, that God is light and in Him there is no darkness at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 2:4; John 3:19–21"> If we claim to have fellowship with Him, and we are walking in darkness, we are lying, and are not practicing the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Romans 13:12; Revelation 1:5"> But if we are walking in the light, as He is in the light, we have fellowship with one another, and the blood of Jesus Christ His Son cleanses us from all sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:23; Ecclesiastes 7:20; James 3:2"> If we claim that we have no sin, we are deceiving ourselves, and the truth is not in us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 32:5; Proverbs 28:13; Jeremiah 33:8"> If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 1:8; 1 John 5:10; 1 John 2:4"> If we claim that we have not sinned, we make Him a liar, and His word is not in us.</span>
</div>
</div>
