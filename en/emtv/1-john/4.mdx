<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:14; 1 Corinthians 12:3; 1 Timothy 3:16"> By this the Spirit of God is known: Every spirit that confesses that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is of God,</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:31; 1 John 5:4; Romans 8:37"> You are of God, little children, and you have overcome them, because He who is in you is greater than he who is in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:23; 2 Peter 2:2–3; John 17:14"> They are of the world: on account of this they speak of the world, and the world hears them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 14:17; John 10:27; 1 Corinthians 2:12–14"> We are of God. He that knows God hears us; he that is not of God does not hear us. By this we know the spirit of truth and the spirit of error.</span>

## Knowing God Through Love

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 4:20–1; 1 John 4:8; 1 Peter 1:22"> Beloved, let us love one another, because love is of God; and everyone that loves has been born of God and knows God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 4:16; Psalms 86:15; 2 Corinthians 13:11"> He that does not love does not know God, because God is love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:16; 1 John 5:11; Romans 8:32"> By this the love of God is manifested in us, that God has sent His only begotten Son into the world, in order that we might live through Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–5; Romans 5:8–10; 1 John 4:19"> In this is love, not that we loved God, but that He loved us and sent His Son to be the propitiation for our sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 15:12–13; Colossians 3:13; John 13:34"> Beloved, if in this way God loved us, indeed we ought to love one another.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Seeing God Through Love

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 1:18; 1 John 2:5; 1 John 4:17–18"> No one has ever seen God. If we love one another, God abides in us, and His love has been perfected in us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 John 3:24; 1 Corinthians 3:16–17"> (By this we know that we abide in Him, and He in us, because He has given us of His Spirit.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:10; John 3:16–17; John 4:42"> And we have seen and testify that the Father has sent the Son as Savior of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 10:9; 1 John 5:5; Matthew 10:32"> Whoever confesses that Jesus is the Son of God, God abides in him, and he in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 3:24; Psalms 36:7–9; 1 John 3:1"> And we have come to know and believe the love which God has in us. God is love, and he that abides in love abides in God, and God abides in him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Consummation of Love

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 4:12; 1 John 2:28; Romans 8:29"> By this love has been perfected with us: that we may have confidence in the Day of Judgment; because just as He is, we also are in this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7; Romans 8:15; 1 John 4:12"> There is no fear in love; but perfect love casts out fear, because fear involves punishment. But he that fears has not been made perfect in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 4:10; John 3:16; John 15:16"> We love Him because He first loved us.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Obedience by Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 John 3:17; 1 John 2:9; 1 John 4:12"> If anyone claims, “I love God,” and hates his brother, he is a liar; for he who does not love his brother whom he has seen, how is it possible for him to love God whom he has not seen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 22:37–39; Leviticus 19:18; 1 John 3:11"> And this commandment we have from Him: that he who loves God should love his brother also.</span>
</div>
