## Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:1; Ephesians 3:1; Philemon 1:9"> Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timothy our brother, To Philemon our beloved and fellow worker,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Colossians 4:17; Romans 16:5"> to Apphia the beloved, and to Archippus our fellow soldier, and to the church at your house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Ephesians 1:2; 2 Corinthians 13:14"> Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Philemon's Love and Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:8; Ephesians 1:16; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> I thank my God, always making mention of you in my prayers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 1:4; Ephesians 1:15; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> hearing of your love and faith which you have toward the Lord Jesus and toward all the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 1:9–11; 2 Peter 1:8; Colossians 1:9"> that the sharing of your faith may become effective in full knowledge of every good thing which is in us in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:9; 2 Corinthians 7:4"> For we have much thanksgiving and encouragement over your love, because the affections of the saints have been refreshed through you, brother.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul's Plea for Onesimus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Corinthians 10:8; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> Therefore, having much boldness in Christ to command you what is fitting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; Ephesians 4:1; Romans 12:1"> rather on account of love I appeal to you—being such a one as Paul, the elder, and now also a prisoner of Jesus Christ—</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 4:9; 1 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Timothy 1:2"> I appeal to you for my son Onesimus, whose father I became in my imprisonment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:11; Job 30:1–2; 1 Peter 2:10"> who formerly was unprofitable to you, but now is profitable both to you and to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; Jeremiah 31:20; Mark 11:25"> whom I am sending back. You therefore receive him, that is, my very heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Ephesians 3:1; 1 Corinthians 16:17"> whom I wished to keep with myself, in order that on your behalf he might serve me in the bonds of the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Chronicles 29:17; 1 Peter 5:2–3"> But I wished to do nothing without your consent, in order that your good might not be by necessity, but rather voluntary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Genesis 45:5–8; Isaiah 20:6"> For perhaps he was taken away for a time on account of this, in order that you might keep him eternally,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Matthew 23:8; 1 Corinthians 7:22"> no longer as a slave but more than a slave—a beloved brother, especially to me but how much more to you, both in the flesh and in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:23; Matthew 10:40; Philemon 1:12"> If then you count me as a partner, receive him as you would me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–7"> But if he has wronged you or owes anything, charge this to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:11; Galatians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 4:15"> I, Paul, am writing with my own hand. I will repay—not to mention that you owe me even your own self!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="3 John 1:4; Philemon 1:7; Philemon 1:12"> Yes, brother, may I have profit from you in the Lord; refresh my heart in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:4; 2 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 7:16"> Having been persuaded of your obedience, I am writing to you, knowing that you will do even beyond what I say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:11; Philippians 2:24; 2 John 1:12"> But meanwhile, also prepare for me a guestroom, for I hope that through your prayers I will be graciously given to you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Greetings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:7; Romans 16:7; Colossians 4:12"> Epaphras, my fellow captive in Christ Jesus, greets you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10–11; Colossians 4:14; Colossians 4:10"> as do Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, Luke, my fellow workers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:22; Romans 16:23; Romans 16:20"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
