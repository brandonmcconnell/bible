<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 24:1–51; Ezekiel 11:22–23; Ezekiel 10:19"> And as He was going out from the temple, one of His disciples said to Him, “Teacher, see what sort of stones and what sort of buildings!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 21:6; Matthew 24:2; 1 Kings 9:7–8"> And Jesus answered and said to him, “Do you see these great buildings? By no means shall one stone be left upon another, which shall in any way escape destruction.”</span>

## The Signs of the Times and the End of the Age

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Mark 4:34; Mark 14:33"> And as He was sitting on the Mount of Olives opposite the temple, Peter, James, John, and Andrew asked Him privately,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 1:6–7; Matthew 24:3; Daniel 12:6"> “Tell us, when will these things be? And what will be the sign when all these things are about to be fulfilled?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Ephesians 5:6; Jeremiah 29:8"> And Jesus, answering them, began to say: “See that no one deceives you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:22; Jeremiah 23:21–25; John 8:24"> For many will come in My name, saying, ‘I AM,’ and they will deceive many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 21:9–11; John 14:27; Matthew 24:6–7"> But whenever you hear of wars and rumors of wars, do not be disturbed; for these things must occur, but the end is not yet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 24:8; Isaiah 37:3; Psalms 48:6"> For nation will be raised up against nation, and kingdom against kingdom. And there will be earthquakes in various places, and there will be famines and disturbances. These are the beginnings of birth pains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 10:17–18; John 16:2; Philippians 1:29"> “But take heed to yourselves, for they will deliver you up to councils, and you will be flogged in the synagogues. You will stand before rulers and kings for My sake, as a testimony to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:14; Colossians 1:23; Romans 10:18"> And the gospel must first be proclaimed to all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 21:14–15; Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–21"> But whenever they lead you forth to deliver you over, do not worry beforehand as to what you will say, nor rack your brains. But whatever is given you in that hour, speak this; for it is not you who is speaking, but the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Luke 12:52–53; Luke 21:16"> Now brother will deliver up brother to death, and a father his child; and children will rise up against parents and will cause them to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:18–19; Matthew 10:22; Matthew 24:13"> And you will be hated by all on account of My name. But he who endures to the end shall be saved.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Great Tribulation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:27; Daniel 12:11; Daniel 11:31"> “So whenever you see the ‘abomination of desolation,’ spoken of by Daniel the prophet, standing where he must not”—he who reads, let him understand—“then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:31–33; Hebrews 11:7; Philippians 3:7–8"> And let him on the housetop not go down into the house, nor enter to take anything out of his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And let him who is in the field not return to the things behind, to take his garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 4:10; Luke 23:29; Hosea 9:14"> But woe to those who are pregnant and to those who are nursing babies in those days!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And pray that your flight does not take place in winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Joel 2:2; Matthew 24:21"> For in those days will be tribulation, such as there has not been the like from the beginning of creation which God created until now, and never again shall be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 24:22; Zechariah 13:8–9; Isaiah 65:8–9"> And unless the Lord had cut short the days, no flesh would be saved; but on account of the elect, whom He chose, He cut short the days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Matthew 24:23–25; Matthew 24:5"> “Then if anyone says to you, ‘Look, here is the Christ!’ or, ‘Look, there He is!’ do not believe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; 2 Thessalonians 2:8–14; Mark 13:6"> For false christs and false prophets will arise, and they will show signs and wonders to deceive, if possible, even the elect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 3:17; Mark 13:5; John 14:29"> But you take heed! See, I have foretold to you all things.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Coming of the Son of Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Acts 2:19–20; Revelation 6:12–14"> “But in those days, after that tribulation, the sun will be darkened, and the moon will not give its radiance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4; Revelation 6:13"> and the stars shall fall out of the heavens, and the powers in the heavens shall be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Matthew 16:27; Matthew 24:30"> And then they shall see the Son of Man coming in the clouds with great power and glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zechariah 2:6; Matthew 24:31; Deuteronomy 30:4"> And then He will send His angels, and they will gather together His elect from the four winds, from the extremity of earth to the extremity of heaven.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Fig Tree

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 21:29–31; Matthew 24:32–33"> “Now learn this parable from the fig tree: When its branch has already become tender, and puts forth its leaves, you know that summer is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; James 5:9; Ezekiel 12:25–28"> So also you, when you see these things coming to pass, know that it is near—even at the doors!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:34; Luke 21:32; Matthew 16:28"> Assuredly I say to you, that by no means will this generation have passed away until all these things have taken place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Isaiah 51:6; Matthew 24:35"> Heaven and earth shall pass away, but My words shall by no means pass away.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Necessity of Watchfulness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Matthew 24:36–42; Matthew 25:13"> “But concerning that day or hour no one knows, not even the angels in heaven, nor the Son, but the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Luke 12:40; Romans 13:11–12"> Take heed, stay awake and pray; for you do not know when the time is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–30; John 10:3; Luke 12:36–40"> It is like a man going away on a journey, having left his house and having given authority to his servants, and to each his work, and commanded the doorkeeper that he should watch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 13:33; Matthew 24:42; Matthew 24:44"> Watch therefore, for you do not know when the master of the house is coming—at evening, or at midnight, or at dawn, or early in the morning—</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6–7; Romans 13:11–14; Matthew 24:48–51"> lest having come suddenly, he should find you sleeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 13:33; Luke 12:41–46; Mark 13:35"> And the things I say to you, I say to all: watch!”</span>
</div>
</div>
