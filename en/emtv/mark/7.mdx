## The Tradition of the Elders

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 15:1–20; Mark 3:22; Luke 5:17"> Then the Pharisees and some of the scribes gathered together to Him, having come from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Matthew 7:3–5; Romans 14:14"> Now when they saw some of His disciples eat bread with ceremonially unclean, that is, with unwashed hands, they found fault.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 1:14; Mark 7:13; Colossians 2:8"> For the Pharisees and all the Jews do not eat unless they wash their hands with their fist, holding the tradition of the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 9:10; Matthew 23:25; Luke 11:38–39"> And coming from the marketplace, unless they bathe, they do not eat. And there are many other things which they have received by tradition, like the washing of cups and pitchers, copper vessels and couches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:6; Acts 21:21; Matthew 15:2"> Then the Pharisees and scribes asked Him, “Why do Your disciples not walk according to the tradition of the elders, but they eat bread with unwashed hands?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 15:7–9"> And answering He said to them, “Well did Isaiah prophesy about you hypocrites, as it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘This people honors Me with their lips,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but their heart is far from Me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Matthew 15:9; James 1:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">And in vain they worship Me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">teaching as doctrines the commandments of men.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Isaiah 1:12"> For having left the commandment of God, you hold to the tradition of men—the washing of pitchers and cups, and many other similar things you do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16–17; Galatians 2:21; Isaiah 29:13"> And He said to them, “Well do you set aside the commandment of God, so that you may keep your tradition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 21:17; Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 5:16"> For Moses said, ‘Honor your father and your mother’; and, ‘Whoever speaks evil of father or mother, let him surely die.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 23:18; 1 Timothy 5:4–8; Matthew 15:5"> But you say, ‘If a man says to his father or to his mother, “Whatever you might be profited by me is Corban” ’—(that is, a gift),</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> then you no longer allow him to do anything for his father or his mother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 7:9; Titus 1:14; Matthew 5:17–20"> thus nullifying the word of God by your tradition which you have handed down. And many similar things you do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:10; Isaiah 6:9; Proverbs 8:5"> And having summoned all the crowd, He said to them, “Hear Me, all of you and understand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 4:23; Leviticus 11:42–47; Titus 1:15"> There is nothing from outside a man, that entering into him, can defile him; but the things which come out of him, those are the things that defile a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> If anyone has ears to hear, let him hear!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Mark 9:28"> When He had entered a house away from the crowd, His disciples were asking Him about the parable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 15:16–17; Hebrews 5:11; Isaiah 28:9–10"> So He said to them, “Are you also without understanding? Do you not understand that everything entering a man from outside cannot defile him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 15:17; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Colossians 2:21–22"> because it does not enter into his heart but into his stomach, and passes into the latrine, thus purifying all foods?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 3:6; James 1:14–15; James 4:1"> And He said, “That which comes out of a man, that defiles a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Romans 8:7–8; James 4:1–3"> For from within, out of the heart of men, come forth evil thoughts, adulteries, fornications, murders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 20:15; Matthew 6:23; 1 Peter 2:15"> thefts, covetousness, wickedness, deceit, lewdness, an evil eye, blasphemy, arrogance, foolishness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 3:17"> All these evil things come from within and defile a man.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Syro-Phoenician Woman's Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:21–28; Matthew 11:21; Ezekiel 28:2"> From there He arose and He went away into the region of Tyre and Sidon. And having entered into a house, He wanted no one to know it, but He was not able to escape notice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Mark 9:17–23; Acts 10:25–26"> For a woman whose young daughter had an unclean spirit heard about Him, and she came and fell at His feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Isaiah 49:12; Galatians 3:28"> Now the woman was a Greek, a Syro-Phoenician by race, and she was asking Him to cast the demon out from her daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:23–28; Matthew 7:6; Acts 22:21"> But Jesus said to her, “Let the children be filled first, for it is not good to take the children's bread and throw it to the little dogs.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Romans 3:29; Acts 11:17–18"> And she answered and said to Him, “Yes, Lord, yet even the little dogs underneath the table eat from the crumbs of the children.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; Matthew 5:3; 1 John 3:8"> Then He said to her, “Because of this saying, go; the demon has gone out of your daughter.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 3:8; John 4:50–52"> And going away to her house, she found the demon having gone out, and her daughter having been placed on the bed.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Heals a Deaf Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 5:20; Matthew 15:29–31; Matthew 4:25"> Again, coming out from the region of Tyre and Sidon, He went to the Sea of Galilee, in the midst of the region of Decapolis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:14; Mark 5:23; Matthew 9:32–33"> Then they brought to Him a deaf man impeded in speech, and they begged Him that He would lay His hand upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:23; John 9:6–7; Mark 5:40"> And when he had taken him aside from the crowd privately, He put His fingers in his ears, and having spit, He touched his tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 11:41; Mark 5:41; Mark 6:41"> Then looking up into heaven, He sighed, and said to him, “Ephphatha,” that is, “Be opened.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5–6; Psalms 33:9; Mark 2:12"> Immediately his ears were opened, and the bond of his tongue was loosed, and he was speaking correctly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Mark 8:26; Mark 3:12"> And He ordered them that they should tell no one; but the more He ordered them, the more exceedingly they would proclaim it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 6:51; Mark 5:42; Exodus 4:10–11"> And they were astonished beyond measure, saying, “He has done all things well. He makes both the deaf to hear and the mute to speak.”</span>
</div>
