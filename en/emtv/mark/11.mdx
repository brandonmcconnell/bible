## The Triumphal Entry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:1–11; John 12:14–19; Luke 19:29–40"> And when they drew near to Jerusalem, to Bethphage and Bethany, towards the Mount of Olives, He sent two of His disciples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 21:2–3; Luke 19:30–31"> and He said to them, “Go into the village opposite you; and immediately entering into it you will find a colt tied up on which no one has sat. When you have untied him, bring him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 10:36; Mark 14:15; Psalms 110:3"> And if anyone says to you, ‘Why are you doing this?’ say, ‘The Lord has need of him,’ and immediately he will send it here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 26:19; Luke 19:32–34; Matthew 21:6–7"> And they departed, and found the colt tied up by the door outside in the street, and they untied him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> But some of those standing there said to them, “What are you doing, loosing the colt?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And they told them just as Jesus had commanded. And they gave them permission.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Luke 19:35–36; Matthew 21:7–8"> Then they led the colt to Jesus, and placed their clothes on him, and He sat on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40"> And many spread their garments on the road, and others were cutting down branches from the trees and were spreading them on the road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 118:25–26; Matthew 21:9; Matthew 23:39"> And those going before, and those that were following were crying out, saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Hosanna!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord!’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Luke 2:14; Luke 19:38–40"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessed is the kingdom of our father David that comes in the name of the Lord!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Hosanna in the highest!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 8:1–2; Luke 19:41–45; Luke 21:37–38"> And Jesus entered into Jerusalem, and into the temple. And having looked around at all things, the hour already being late, He went out to Bethany with the twelve.</span>
</div>

## Jesus Curses the Fig Tree

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:18–22; Matthew 4:2; John 19:28"> Now the next day, after they had come out from Bethany, He was hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Luke 13:6–9; Isaiah 5:7"> And seeing from a distance a fig tree having leaves, He went to see if perhaps He would find something on it. And having come to it, He found nothing but leaves, for it was not the season for figs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Matthew 7:19; Matthew 3:10"> And answering, Jesus said to it, “May no one eat fruit from you ever again.” And His disciples were listening.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Cleanses the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 2:13–17; Matthew 21:12–16; Luke 19:45–47"> And they came to Jerusalem. And when Jesus entered into the temple, He began to throw out those selling and buying in the temple, and He overturned the tables of the moneychangers and the seats of those that sold doves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And He would not permit anyone to carry a vessel through the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Jeremiah 7:11; Luke 19:46"> And He was teaching, saying to them, “Is it not written, ‘My house shall be called a house of prayer for all the nations’? But you have made it a ‘den of thieves!’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Mark 12:12; Mark 14:1–2"> And the scribes and chief priests heard it and they sought how they might destroy Him; for they were afraid of Him, because all the people were astonished at His teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 21:37; Mark 11:11; John 12:36"> And when it became evening, He was going forth outside of the city.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lesson of the Withered Fig Tree

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Mark 11:14; John 15:6"> Now in the morning while passing by, they saw the fig tree dried up from its roots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 3:33; Matthew 23:7; Matthew 25:41"> And Peter, remembering, said to Him, “Rabbi, look! The fig tree which You cursed has dried up!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; John 14:1; Mark 9:23"> So Jesus answered and said to them, “Have faith in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 17:20; John 15:7; Matthew 21:21"> For assuredly I say to you, whoever says to this mountain, ‘Be taken up and cast into the sea,’ and does not doubt in his heart, but believes that the things which he says are coming to pass, whatever he says shall be his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; 1 John 5:14–15; 1 John 3:22"> For this reason I say to you, all things whatsoever you ask for in prayer, believe that you will receive them, and they will be yours.</span>
</div>

## Jesus' Authority Questioned

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 20:1–8; Matthew 21:23–27; Psalms 2:1–5"> Then they came again to Jerusalem. And as He was walking about in the temple, the chief priests, the scribes, and the elders came to Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; Acts 7:38–39; Numbers 16:3"> And they said to Him, “By what authority are You doing these things? And who gave You this authority to do these things?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 20:3–8; Matthew 21:24; Isaiah 52:13"> But Jesus answered and said to them, “I also will ask you one thing, and you answer Me, and I will tell you by what authority I do these things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 3:1–20; John 3:25–36; Mark 1:1–11"> The baptism of John—was it from heaven or from men? Answer Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:29; John 1:34; Matthew 21:31–32"> And they were debating among themselves, saying, “If we say, ‘From heaven,’ He will say, ‘Why then did you not believe him?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 21:46; Mark 12:12; Matthew 14:5"> But should we say, ‘From men’ ”—they feared the people, for all had considered John to have been a prophet indeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12"> And answering they said to Jesus, “We do not know.” And Jesus answered and said to them, “Neither do I tell you by what authority I do these things.”</span>
</div>
