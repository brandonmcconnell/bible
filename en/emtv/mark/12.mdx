## The Parable of the Wicked Tenants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–4; Mark 13:34; Song of Solomon 8:11–12"> Then He began to speak to them in parables: “A man planted a vineyard and set a fence around it, dug a wine vat and built a tower. And he leased it to farmers, and went on a journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:1–8; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 44:4"> And at harvest time he sent a servant to the farmers, in order that he might receive his part from the fruit of the vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15; Nehemiah 9:26; Jeremiah 29:26"> And they took him and beat him and sent him away empty-handed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Again he sent them another servant, and that one they wounded in the head with stones, and they sent him away shamefully treated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:6; Matthew 23:37; Nehemiah 9:30"> And again he sent another, and that one they killed, and so with many others, beating some and killing others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 1:1–2; Matthew 3:17; John 1:34"> Therefore still having one son, his beloved, he even sent him to them last, saying, ‘Surely they will respect my son.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Matthew 2:16; Acts 2:23"> But those farmers said to one another, ‘This is the heir. Come, let us kill him, and the inheritance will be ours.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 21:39; Luke 20:15; Matthew 21:33"> And taking him they killed him, and cast him out of the vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 10:20–12; Matthew 21:43; Luke 19:27"> Therefore what will the owner of the vineyard do? He will come and will destroy the farmers, and give the vineyard to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 28:16"> Have you not even read this Scripture:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The stone which the builders rejected</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">has become the chief cornerstone.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 118:23; Acts 3:12–16; Habakkuk 1:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">This was the Lord's doing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and it is marvelous in our eyes’?”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 22:22; John 7:44"> And they sought to lay hold of Him, but they feared the crowd, for they knew that He had spoken the parable against them. And leaving Him, they went away.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Question about Paying Taxes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Luke 11:54; Mark 3:6"> Then they sent to Him some of the Pharisees and the Herodians, in order that they might catch Him in His discourse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 20:22; John 7:18"> And they came and said to Him, “Teacher, we know that You are true, and it does not make a difference to you about anyone; for You do not look on the face of men, but you teach the way of God in truth. Is it lawful to pay taxes to Caesar, or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:2; Revelation 2:23; Mark 10:2"> Should we give, or should we not give?”<br />But He, knowing their hypocrisy, said to them, “Why do you test Me? Bring Me a denarius, that I may see it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 20:24–26; Revelation 3:12; Matthew 22:19–22"> So they brought one.<br />And He said to them, “Whose image and inscription is this?” And they said to Him, “Caesar's.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:7; 1 Peter 2:17; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> And Jesus answered and said to them, “Render to Caesar the things that are Caesar's, and to God the things that are God's.” And they marveled at Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Question about the Resurrection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 22:23–33; Acts 4:1–2; Acts 23:6–9"> Then some Sadducees came to Him, who say that there is no resurrection, and they asked Him, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Ruth 4:5; Ruth 1:11–13"> “Teacher, Moses wrote to us that if a man's brother dies and leaves behind a wife and leaves no children, that his brother should take his wife and raise up offspring for his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 22:25–28; Luke 20:29–33"> Now there were seven brothers. The first took a wife and he died, and left no offspring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the second took her, and died; nor did he leave any offspring. And the third likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> So the seven had her and left no offspring. Last of all the woman died also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Therefore, in the resurrection, when they shall arise, whose wife of them will she be? For all seven had her as wife.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 20:9; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Matthew 22:29"> Jesus answered and said to them, “Is this not the reason you are mistaken, because you know neither the Scriptures nor the power of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:35–36; Matthew 22:30; 1 John 3:2"> For whenever they rise from among the dead, neither do they marry nor are they given in marriage, but they are like angels in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Luke 20:37; Genesis 17:7–8"> But concerning the dead, that they rise, have you not read in the book of Moses, in connection with the burning bush passage, how God spoke to him, saying, ‘I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 14:9; Romans 4:17; Mark 12:24"> He is not the God of the dead, but the God of the living. Therefore you are greatly mistaken.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The First Commandment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–40; Luke 10:25–28; Luke 20:39"> Then one of the scribes came, after he heard them reasoning together, and perceiving that He had answered them well, asked Him, “Which is the first commandment of all?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:4; Luke 10:27; Galatians 3:20"> Jesus answered him, “The first of all the commandments is: ‘Hear, O Israel, the Lord our God is one Lord.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5"> ‘And you shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, with all your mind, and with all your strength.’ This is the first commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 13:8–9; Matthew 22:39; Leviticus 19:18"> And the second, like it, is this: ‘You shall love your neighbor as yourself.’ There is no other commandment greater than these.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Deuteronomy 4:35; Isaiah 46:9"> So the scribe said to Him, “Well said, Teacher, in truth You said that He is one, and there is no other besides Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Micah 6:6–8; 1 Samuel 15:22"> And to love Him with the whole heart, and with the whole understanding, with all the soul, and with all the strength, and to love one's neighbor as oneself, is more than all the burnt offerings and sacrifices.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 20:40; Matthew 22:46"> Now when Jesus saw that he answered intelligently, He said to him, “You are not far from the kingdom of God.”<br />And no one dared any longer to question Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Question about David's Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Matthew 22:41–45; Matthew 26:55"> Then Jesus answered and said, as He was teaching in the temple, “How is it that the scribes say that the Christ is the Son of David?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Matthew 22:43–45"> For David himself said by the Holy Spirit:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The Lord said to my Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sit at My right hand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Till I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet.” ’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Matthew 11:25; Matthew 1:23"> Therefore David himself calls Him ‘Lord’; how then is He his son?”<br />And the large crowd was hearing Him gladly.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Denounces the Scribes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–7; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 11:43"> Then He said to them in His teaching, “Take heed of the scribes, who like to walk about in long robes, and like greetings in the marketplaces,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="James 2:2–3"> and the first seats in the synagogues, and the first couches at the suppers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Luke 20:47; Matthew 23:33"> who devour widows' houses, and for a pretense make long prayers. These will receive greater condemnation.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Widow's Offering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 21:1–4; 2 Kings 12:9; John 8:20"> And Jesus sat down opposite the treasury, and was observing how the crowd was putting money into the treasury. And many rich people were putting in much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Then one poor widow came and put in two lepta, which equals a quadrans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Acts 11:29"> And having summoned His disciples, He said to them, “Assuredly I say to you that this poor widow has put in more than all those who are putting into the treasury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 1 John 3:17; Ezra 2:68–69"> For they all put in out of their excess, but this woman, out of her destitution, put in everything she had, her whole livelihood.”</span>
</div>
</div>
