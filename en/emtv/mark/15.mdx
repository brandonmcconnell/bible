## Jesus Before Pilate

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 27:1–2; Luke 23:1–5; Luke 22:66"> Immediately, in the morning, the chief priests held a consultation, along with the elders and scribes and the whole Sanhedrin; and having bound Jesus, they led Him away, and delivered Him to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Matthew 27:11–14; 1 Timothy 6:13"> Then Pilate asked Him, “Are You the King of the Jews?” And He answered and said to him, “It is as you say.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; Mark 15:5; John 18:29–31"> And the chief priests were accusing Him of many things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 19:10; Matthew 27:13; Matthew 26:62"> Then Pilate questioned Him again, saying, “Do You answer nothing? See how many things they testify against You!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; Zechariah 3:8; Psalms 71:7"> But Jesus no longer answered anything, so that Pilate marveled.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Pilate Hands Jesus Over to be Crucified

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:39–40; Acts 25:9; Acts 24:27"> Now at the feast he would release one prisoner to them, whomever they would request.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 23:18–19; Matthew 27:16; Luke 23:25"> And there was one called Barabbas, having been bound with his fellow rebels, who had committed murder in the rebellion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then the crowd cried out and began to request that just as always he would do this for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:14–16; John 19:4–5; John 18:39"> But Pilate answered them, saying, “Do you desire that I release to you the King of the Jews?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Titus 3:3; Genesis 4:4–6"> For he knew that because of envy the chief priests had delivered Him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 3:14; John 18:40; Hosea 5:1"> But the chief priests stirred up the crowd, so that he should rather release Barabbas to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; Isaiah 9:6–7; Zechariah 9:9"> But Pilate answered and said to them again, “What then do you desire that I should do with Him whom you call King of the Jews?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And again they cried, “Crucify Him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:41; Luke 23:23–24; Isaiah 53:3"> But Pilate said to them, “Why, what evil did He do?” But they cried out all the more, “Crucify Him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 19:1; Matthew 27:26; Isaiah 50:6"> So Pilate, wanting to gratify the crowd, released Barabbas to them; and he delivered up Jesus, after he had Him flogged, so that He might be crucified.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Soldiers Mock Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 19:9; Matthew 27:27–31; John 18:28"> Then the soldiers led Him away into the courtyard, which is the Praetorium, and assembled the whole garrison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 27:28–30; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> And they clothed Him with purple; and they twisted a crown of thorns, and they put it around His head,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:42–43; Mark 15:2; Genesis 37:10"> and they began to salute Him, “Hail, King of the Jews!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Isaiah 53:3–5; Luke 23:11"> And they were striking His head with a reed and were spitting on Him; and bowing the knee, they mockingly worshiped Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 19:16; Matthew 27:31"> And when they had mocked Him, they stripped Him of the purple, and dressed Him in His own clothing. And they led Him out so that they might crucify Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Crucifixion of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:32; Luke 23:26; Romans 16:13"> And they pressed into service a certain man that was passing by named Simon, a Cyrenian, the father of Alexander and Rufus, as he was coming from the country, that he might carry His cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 27:33–44; John 19:17–27; Luke 23:27–38"> And they brought Him to the place called Golgotha, which is translated, Place of a Skull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 27:34; John 19:28–30; Luke 23:36"> And they gave Him wine mingled with myrrh to drink, but He did not take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 23:34; John 19:23–24; Matthew 27:35–36"> And having crucified Him, they divided His clothes by casting lots over them, to determine who would take what.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 19:14; Mark 15:33; Matthew 27:45"> Now it was the third hour, and they crucified Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:37; Matthew 2:2; John 19:18–22"> And there was the inscription of His charge having been inscribed:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">THE KING OF THE JEWS.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 23:32–33; Matthew 27:38; John 19:18"> And together with Him they crucified two bandits, one on His right side and the one on His left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> So the Scripture was fulfilled which says, “And He was numbered with the transgressors.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Psalms 109:25; Lamentations 1:12"> And those passing by were blaspheming Him, shaking their heads and saying, “Aha! You who destroy the temple and build it in three days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> save Yourself, and come down from the cross!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:23–24; Matthew 27:41–43; Luke 23:35–37"> Likewise the chief priests also, mocking Him to each other with the scribes said, “He saved others; Himself He is not able to save.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Luke 23:39–43; Zechariah 9:9"> Let the Christ, the King of Israel, come down now from the cross, so that we may see and believe.” Even those who were crucified with Him were reviling Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Luke 23:44–45; Amos 8:9–10"> Now when the sixth hour had come, darkness came upon the whole land until the ninth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Matthew 27:46; Hebrews 5:7"> And at the ninth hour Jesus cried out with a loud voice, saying, “Eloi, Eloi, lima sabachthani?” which is translated, “My God, My God, why have You forsaken Me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:47–49; Mark 9:11–13; Matthew 17:11–13"> Some of those standing by when they heard, said, “Look, He is calling Elijah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Mark 15:23; John 19:28–30"> And one, running and having filled a sponge with vinegar, and having put it around a reed, was giving a drink to Him, saying, “Leave Him alone; let us see if Elijah is coming to take Him down.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 19:30; Luke 23:46; Matthew 27:50"> And Jesus, uttering a loud cry, breathed His last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–23; Luke 23:45; Hebrews 9:3–12"> Then the veil of the temple was torn in two from top to bottom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Luke 23:47–48; Matthew 27:43"> Now when the centurion who stood across from Him saw that He cried out like this and breathed His last, he said, “Truly this Man was the Son of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Luke 8:2–3; Matthew 27:55–56"> There were also women looking on from a distance, among whom were both Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James the Less and of Joses, and Salome,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Matthew 27:55–56"> who also would follow Him and would minister to Him when He was in Galilee; and many other women who had come up together with Him to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Burial of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 23:50–56; John 19:38–42; John 19:31"> And now evening having come, since it was Preparation Day, which is the day before the Sabbath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 2:38; Luke 2:25; Luke 23:51"> Joseph of Arimathea, a prominent council member, who was himself waiting for the kingdom of God, coming and taking courage, he went in to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 19:31–37"> And Pilate wondered if He was already dead; and having summoned the centurion, he questioned him if He had been dead long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 27:58; John 19:38; Mark 15:39"> And when he found out from the centurion, he granted the body to Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 22:16"> And having bought linen cloth, and having taken Him down, he wrapped Him in the linen cloth and set Him down in a tomb which had been cut out of a rock, and rolled a stone upon the door of the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 15:40; Mark 16:1; Luke 23:55–2"> And Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joses were observing where He was placed.</span>
</div>
</div>
