## Healing on the Sabbath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:9–14; Luke 6:6–11; Mark 1:21"> And He entered again into the synagogue. And a man was there who had a withered hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:32; Luke 14:1; Luke 6:7"> And they were observing Him, to see if He would heal him on the Sabbath, so that they might accuse Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 1:28–30; Isaiah 42:4; Luke 6:8"> And He said to the one who had the withered hand, “Step forward.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Matthew 12:10–12; Hosea 6:6"> Then He said to them, “Is it lawful on the Sabbath to do good or to do evil, to save life or to kill?” But they kept silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:10; Ephesians 4:18; Romans 11:25"> And looking around at them with anger, being grieved at the hardness of their hearts, He said to the man, “Stretch out your hand.” And he stretched it out, and his hand was restored as whole as the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:16; Mark 12:13; Matthew 12:14"> Then the Pharisees went out with the Herodians and immediately conspired against Him, how they might destroy Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Great Multitude Follows Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 4:25; Luke 6:17; Matthew 12:15"> But Jesus withdrew with His disciples to the sea. And a large multitude from Galilee followed Him, and from Judea</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Isaiah 34:5; Matthew 11:21"> and from Jerusalem and Idumea and beyond the Jordan, and those around Tyre and Sidon, a large multitude, when they heard everything He was doing, came to Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 5:30; John 6:15"> So He told His disciples that a small boat should be kept ready for Him because of the crowd, lest they should crush Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56; Matthew 4:23"> For He had healed many, so that all who had afflictions were crowding upon Him, so that they might touch Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 1:23–24; Luke 8:28"> And the unclean spirits, whenever they saw Him, fell down before Him and would cry out, saying, “You are the Son of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:16; Mark 1:25; Mark 1:34"> And He strictly charged them that they should not make Him known.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Appoints the Twelve

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Luke 6:12–16; Matthew 10:1–4"> And He went up on the mountain and summoned those whom He wanted, and they came to Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Luke 9:1–6; John 15:16"> And He appointed twelve, so that they might be with Him, and so that He might send them out to preach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and to have authority to heal diseases and to cast out demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:42; Acts 1:13; Matthew 16:16–18"> And He gave Simon the name Peter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Mark 14:33; Jeremiah 23:29"> James the son of Zebedee and John the brother of James, He gave them the names Boanerges, which is, “Sons of Thunder”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14; Matthew 9:9"> Andrew, Philip, Bartholomew, Matthew, Thomas, James the son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus, Simon the Cananite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 6:71; John 13:2; Acts 1:16–25"> and Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus and Beelzebul

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 6:31; John 4:31–34; Mark 9:28"> And they came into a house. Then again a crowd assembled, so that they were not even able to eat food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 26:24; John 10:20; Mark 3:31"> But when His relatives heard, they came out to seize Him, for they were saying, “He has lost His mind.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:1; Matthew 10:25; Matthew 9:34"> And the scribes who were from Jerusalem came down and were saying, “He has Beelzebul,” and, “By the ruler of the demons He casts out demons.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:17–23; Matthew 12:25–30; Matthew 13:34"> And having summoned them, he began to say to them in parables: “How can Satan cast out Satan?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Isaiah 19:2–3; 2 Samuel 20:6"> And if a kingdom is divided against itself, that kingdom cannot stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 3:16; Psalms 133:1; Galatians 5:15"> And if a household is divided against itself, that household cannot stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And if Satan has risen up against himself and has become divided, he cannot stand, but is finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–26; Matthew 12:29; Luke 11:21–23"> No one can enter a strong man's house and plunder his possessions, unless he first binds the strong man, and then he may plunder his house.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Unpardonable Sin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; 1 John 5:16"> “Assuredly I say to you, all sins will be forgiven the sons of men, also blasphemies, as many as they may blaspheme;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 12:31–32; Luke 12:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> but whoever blasphemes against the Holy Spirit never has forgiveness, but is in danger of eternal damnation”—</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:20; Mark 3:22"> because they were saying, “He has an unclean spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:19–21; Matthew 12:46–50"> Then His brothers and His mother came, and as they stood outside they sent to Him, calling for Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And a crowd was sitting around Him; and they said to Him, “Look, Your mother and Your brothers and Your sisters are seeking You outside.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 3:21; Deuteronomy 33:9; Mark 6:3"> But He answered them, saying, “Who is My mother, or My brothers?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 12:49–50; Luke 11:27–28; Romans 8:29"> And when He had looked around in a circle at those sitting around Him, He said, “Here are My mother and My brothers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:17; Matthew 7:21; 1 John 3:22–23"> For whoever does the will of God is My brother and My sister and mother.”</span>
</div>
</div>
