## Jesus Heals a Demon-Possessed Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:26–39; Matthew 8:28–34; Mark 4:35"> Then they came to the other side of the sea, to the region of the Gadarenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 1:23; Isaiah 65:4; Mark 5:8"> And when He got out of the boat, immediately there met Him from the tombs a man with an unclean spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 65:4; Luke 8:29; Daniel 4:32–33"> who had his dwelling among the tombs. And no one could bind him, not even with chains,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 3:7–8"> because he often had been bound with shackles and chains, and the chains had been torn apart by him, and the shackles had been broken, and no one was able to subdue him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 2:7–8; 1 Kings 18:28; John 8:44"> And always, night and day, he was in the mountains and in the tombs, crying out and bruising himself with stones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 2:19; Psalms 66:3; Luke 4:41"> But seeing Jesus from a distance, he ran and worshiped Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; Acts 16:17; Matthew 26:63"> and crying out with a great voice he said, “What have I to do with You, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I adjure You by God, do not torment me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 1:25; Mark 9:25–26; Acts 16:18"> For He was saying to him, “Come out from the man, unclean spirit!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 8:30; Mark 5:15; Matthew 26:53"> And He was asking him, “What is your name?” And he answered, saying, “My name is Legion; for we are many.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Mark 5:13"> And he was imploring Him many times that He would not send them out of the region.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 11:7–8; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> Now there was a large herd of swine feeding there near the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:11; Job 1:10–12; Luke 22:31–32"> So all the demons implored Him, saying, “Send us into the swine, that we may enter them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 22:22; 1 Peter 3:22; Job 2:6"> And immediately Jesus gave them permission. And when the unclean spirits came out, they entered into the swine, and the herd rushed down the steep bank into the sea, (there were about two thousand) and drowned in the sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 8:34; Matthew 8:33"> But those feeding the swine fled, and they reported it in the city and in the country. And they came out to see what it was that had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 2 Timothy 1:7; 1 Samuel 6:20–21"> Then they came to Jesus, and observed him who had been demon-possessed and had the legion, sitting and having been clothed and being of sound mind, and they were afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And those who saw it related to them how it happened to him who had been demon-possessed, and about the swine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 8:34; Mark 1:24; Luke 8:37"> Then they began to implore Him to go away from their region.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:38–39; Philippians 1:23–24; Mark 5:17"> And when He got into the boat, he who had been demon-possessed implored Him that he might be with Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 66:16; John 4:29; Isaiah 38:9–20"> But Jesus did not permit him, but said to him, “Go home to your own family, and report to them all the things that the Lord has done for you, and how He had compassion on you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:31; Matthew 4:25"> And he departed and began to proclaim in Decapolis all the things Jesus did for him; and all were marveling.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Girl Restored to Life and a Woman Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 9:1; Mark 4:1; Luke 8:40"> Now when Jesus had crossed over again in the boat to the other side, a large crowd was gathered to Him; and He was beside the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Acts 18:8; Mark 5:35–36"> And behold, there came one of the rulers of the synagogue, named Jairus. And seeing Him, he fell at His feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 13:13; Luke 4:40; Mark 16:18"> and was imploring Him many times, saying, “My little daughter is near death, please come and lay Your hands on her, so that she may be healed, and live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 5:31; Mark 3:20; Luke 7:6"> And He went away with him. And a large crowd was following Him, and they were pressing against Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:25–27; Luke 8:43–44; Matthew 9:20–22"> Now a certain woman, suffering from a flow of blood for twelve years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:12–13; Jeremiah 8:22; Psalms 108:12"> and who had suffered many things under many physicians, and who had spent everything at her disposal, and was no better, but rather having become worse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 19:12; Matthew 14:36; Acts 5:15"> when she heard about Jesus, she came from behind Him in the crowd and touched His clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> For she was saying, “If only I may touch His clothes, I will be healed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Psalms 30:2; Mark 3:10"> And immediately the flow of her blood was dried up, and she knew in her body that she had been healed from her affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 8:46; Luke 5:17"> And Jesus, immediately knowing in Himself that power had gone out of Him, He turned in the crowd and said, “Who touched My clothes?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:45; Luke 9:12"> But His disciples said to Him, “You see the crowd pressing against You, and You say, ‘Who touched Me?’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And He was looking around to see her who had done this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:47; Luke 1:29; Luke 1:12"> But the woman, fearing and trembling, knowing what had happened to her, came and fell down before Him and told Him the whole truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 8:48; Matthew 9:22; Luke 7:50"> And He said to her, “Daughter, your faith has healed you. Go in peace, and be healed of your affliction.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 8:49; John 11:39; Mark 5:22"> While He was still speaking, they came from the synagogue leader, saying, “Your daughter has died. Why do you still trouble the Teacher?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 8:50; Matthew 9:28–29; John 11:40"> But Jesus, immediately when He heard the word being spoken, said to the synagogue leader, “Do not be afraid; only believe.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 9:40; Mark 14:33; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> And He did not permit anyone to follow along with Him except for Peter, James, and John the brother of James.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:23–24; Luke 8:52–53; Mark 5:22"> And He went to the house of the synagogue leader, and observed a commotion, and people weeping and wailing, very much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 20:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–14"> And when He went in, He said to them, “Why are you making such commotion and weeping? The child is not dead, but she is asleep.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; 2 Kings 4:33; Matthew 9:24–25"> And they were ridiculing Him. But putting them all out, He took along the father and the mother of the child, and those with Him, and He went in where the child was lying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Luke 7:14–15; Luke 8:54–55"> And having grasped the hand of the child, He said to her, “Talitha, koumi,” which means, “Little girl, I say to you, arise.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Mark 4:41; Acts 3:10–13; Mark 7:37"> And immediately the little girl arose and was walking about, for she was twelve years old. And they were overcome with great amazement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Mark 1:43"> But He ordered them strictly that no one should know this, and said that something should be given her to eat.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
