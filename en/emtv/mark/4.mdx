## The Parable of the Sower

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 5:1–3; Mark 2:13; Luke 8:4–10"> And again He began to teach beside the sea. And a large crowd was gathered to Him, so that He got aboard into the boat, and sat on the sea; and all the crowd was upon the land by the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; Mark 3:23; Mark 4:11"> And He was teaching them many things by parables, and said to them in His teaching:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 2:5; Matthew 13:26; Mark 4:26–29"> “Listen! Behold, a sower went out to sow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Mark 4:15; Matthew 13:4"> And it happened, as he sowed, that some seed fell alongside the road; and the birds came and devoured it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26; Luke 8:6; Luke 8:13"> And some fell on stony ground, where it did not have much earth; and immediately it sprang up because it had no depth of earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:11; Jeremiah 17:5–8; Jonah 4:8"> But when the sun rose it was scorched, and because it had no root, it withered away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Luke 8:14; Genesis 3:17–18"> And some seed fell among thorns; and the thorns came up and choked it, and it yielded no fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 15:5; Colossians 1:6; Mark 4:20"> But other seed fell on good ground, and it was yielding fruit coming up and growing, and it was producing—some thirtyfold, some sixty, and some a hundred.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Mark 4:23–24; Revelation 3:22"> And He said, “He that has ears to hear, let him hear!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Purpose of Parables

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:10–17; Mark 4:34; Proverbs 13:20"> But when He was by himself, those around Him together with the twelve asked Him about the parable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; Luke 8:10; 1 John 5:20"> And He said to them, “Unto you it has been given to know the mystery of the kingdom of God; but to those who are outside, all things are spoken in parables,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Isaiah 44:18; Matthew 13:14–15"> so that<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Seeing they may see and not perceive,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and hearing they may hear and not understand;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Lest they should turn,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their sins be forgiven them.’ ”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Sower Explained

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Matthew 13:51–52; Mark 7:17–18"> And He said to them, “Do you not know this parable? How then will you know all the parables?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 8:4; Matthew 13:19; Matthew 13:37"> The sower sows the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 13:19; 2 Corinthians 2:11"> And these are the ones beside the road where the word is sown, and whenever they hear, immediately Satan comes and takes away the word which was sown in their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 6:20; Luke 8:13; Matthew 13:20–21"> And these are the ones sown on stony ground; which, whenever they hear the word, immediately they receive it with joy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 1 Thessalonians 3:3–5; Matthew 12:31"> and they have no root in themselves, but are short-lived. Afterward, when trials or persecution arise because of the word, immediately they fall away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:14; Jeremiah 4:3; Matthew 13:22"> And these are the ones sown among thorns; they are those who hear the word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; 1 Timothy 6:17"> and the cares of this world, and the deceitfulness of riches, and the desires for other things enter in and choke the word, and it becomes unfruitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Luke 8:15; John 15:4–5"> And these are the ones sown on good ground, such as who hear the word and welcome it, and bear fruit: some thirtyfold, some sixty, and some a hundred.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Light Under a Basket

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 5:15; Luke 11:33; Luke 8:16"> And He said to them, “A lamp is not brought so that it may be put under a measuring basket or under a bed, is it? Is it not brought so that it may be put on a lampstand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 8:17; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Matthew 10:26–27"> For there is nothing hidden which shall not be made manifest, nor was it hidden but that it might come to light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 4:9; Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:29"> If anyone has ears to hear, let him hear.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Luke 8:18; Matthew 7:2"> Then He said to them, “Watch what you hear. By what measure you measure, it will be measured out to you; and more will be added to you who hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 13:12; Luke 8:18; Matthew 25:28–29"> For whoever has, more will be given to him; and he who does not have, even what he has will be taken away from him.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Growing Seed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 13:24; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9; Luke 8:11"> And He said, “Thus is the kingdom of God as if a man should cast seed on the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:5; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 1 Corinthians 15:37–38"> and should sleep by night and rise by day, and the seed should sprout and should grow, he himself does not know how.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 4:31–32; Hosea 6:3; Philippians 1:9–11"> For the earth produces crops by itself: first a shoot, then a head of grain, then full wheat in the head of grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Matthew 13:30; Matthew 13:40–43"> But whenever the crop permits, immediately he sends forth the sickle, because the harvest has come.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Mustard Seed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 13:31–32; Luke 13:18–21; Lamentations 2:13"> Then He said, “To what shall we compare the kingdom of God? Or by what sort of parable shall we illustrate it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:20; Luke 13:18–19; Daniel 2:44–45"> It is like a mustard seed, which whenever it is sown on the ground, is smaller than all the seeds on the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 4:20–22; Daniel 4:10–14; Ezekiel 31:3–10"> but whenever it is sown, it grows up and becomes greater than all the garden vegetables, and produces great branches, so that the birds of heaven are able to nest under its shade.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; John 16:12; Hebrews 5:11–14"> And by many such parables He would speak the word to them, just as they were able to hear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 16:25; Mark 4:10; Matthew 15:15–20"> And apart from a parable He did not speak to them, however, privately to His disciples, he would explain all things.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Calms a Storm

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 8:18; Luke 8:22; Luke 8:25"> On the same day, when evening had come, He said to them, “Let us go across to the other side.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 3:9; Mark 4:1; Mark 5:2"> And leaving the crowd, they took Him along as He was in the boat. And other little boats were also with Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 8:23–24; Job 1:19; Luke 8:22–23"> And there was a severe windstorm, and the waves dashed up into the boat, so that it was already filling up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Hebrews 4:15; Isaiah 54:6–8"> But He was in the stern, sleeping upon the cushion. And they woke Him up and said to Him, “Teacher! Does it not matter to You that we are perishing?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 107:29; Psalms 89:9; Psalms 65:7"> And having awakened He rebuked the wind, and He said to the sea, “Peace, be still.” And the wind abated and there was a great calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 8:25; Matthew 8:26; Matthew 14:31"> But He said to them, “Why are you so fearful? How is it that you do not have faith?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 8:25; Job 38:11; Psalms 89:7"> And they feared exceedingly, and were saying to one another, “Who then is this, that even the wind and the sea obey Him!”</span>
</div>
</div>
