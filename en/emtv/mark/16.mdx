## The Resurrection of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 23:56–12; John 20:1–10; Matthew 28:1–10"> Now when the Sabbath was past, Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James, and Salome bought spices, so that they might come and anoint Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 20:1; Luke 24:1; Matthew 28:1"> And very early on the first day of the week, they came to the tomb when the sun had risen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 15:46–47; Matthew 27:60–66"> And they said to one another, “Who will roll away the stone from the door of the tomb for us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 28:2–4; Luke 24:2; John 20:1"> And having looked up, they saw that the stone had been rolled away—for it was exceedingly large.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 20:11–12; Luke 24:3–5; John 20:8"> And entering into the tomb, they saw a young man sitting on the right clothed with a white robe, and they were alarmed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; 1 Corinthians 15:3–7; Luke 24:46"> But he said to them, “Do not be alarmed. You are seeking Jesus the Nazarene, who was crucified. He has arisen! He is not here! See the place where they put Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:28; Matthew 26:32; Matthew 28:10"> But go, say to His disciples, and Peter, that He is going before you into Galilee; there you shall see Him, just as He said to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 24:22–24; Luke 24:9–11; Mark 16:5–6"> And going out, they fled from the tomb, but trembling and amazement held them; and they said nothing to anyone, for they were afraid.</span>

## The Great Commission

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5; Luke 24:36–43; Hebrews 3:7–8"> Afterwards He appeared to the eleven as they were reclining at the table; and He rebuked their unbelief and hardness of heart, because they did not believe those who saw Him after He had been raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; Mark 13:10; Acts 1:8"> And He said to them, “Go into all the world and proclaim the gospel to all creation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 3:36; Romans 10:9–14"> He that believes and is baptized shall be saved; but he that does not believe shall be condemned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:16; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Acts 8:7"> And these signs shall accompany those who have believed: In My name they shall cast out demons; they shall speak with new tongues;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 10:19; James 5:14–15; Acts 28:3–6"> they shall pick up serpents; and if they drink anything deadly, it shall by no means hurt them; they shall lay hands on the sick, and they shall be well.”</span>
