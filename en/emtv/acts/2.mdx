## The Coming of the Holy Spirit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:6; Philippians 2:2; Acts 1:13–15"> And when the day of Pentecost had fully come, they were all with one accord in one place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:31; 1 Kings 19:11; Psalms 18:10"> And suddenly a sound came out of heaven, as being borne along by a violent wind, and it filled the whole house where they were sitting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; John 1:32–33; Jeremiah 23:29"> Then there appeared to them divided tongues, as of fire, and rested on each one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Acts 13:52; Acts 4:31"> And they were all filled with the Holy Spirit, and began to speak in different languages, as the Spirit gave them utterance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Acts 8:2; Colossians 1:23"> And there were dwelling in Jerusalem Jews, devout men, from every nation of those under heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 2:3; 1 Corinthians 16:9; Acts 2:2"> And when this sound occurred, the multitude came together, and were confounded, because they were hearing each one speaking in his own language.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 2:12; Acts 1:11; John 7:52"> And they were all amazed and were marveling, saying to one another, “Behold, are not all these who are speaking Galileans?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And how is it that we hear, each in our own language in which we were born?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 1:1; Acts 18:2; Revelation 1:4"> Parthians and Medes and Elamites, those dwelling in Mesopotamia, Judea and Cappadocia, Pontus and Asia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 16:6; Zechariah 8:23; Zechariah 8:20"> Phrygia and Pamphylia, Egypt and the parts of Libya toward Cyrene, visitors from Rome, both Jews and proselytes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 78:4"> Cretans and Arabs—we hear them speaking in our own tongues the mighty deeds of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 15:26; Acts 17:20; Luke 18:36"> So they were all amazed and perplexed, saying to one another, “What does this mean?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:23; Acts 2:15; 1 Samuel 1:14"> Others were mocking and saying, “They are full of new wine.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Peter's Sermon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 51:7; Isaiah 51:1; Deuteronomy 27:9"> But Peter, standing up with the eleven, lifted up his voice and spoke out to them, “Men of Judea and all who dwell in Jerusalem, let this be known to you, and give ear to my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Samuel 1:15; Matthew 20:3"> For these are not drunk, as you suppose, for it is just the third hour of the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32"> But this is what was spoken by the prophet Joel:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32; Isaiah 44:3; Titus 3:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘ “And it shall be in the last days,” says God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“that I will pour out My Spirit on all flesh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and your young men shall see visions,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and your old men shall dream dreams;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11; 1 Corinthians 7:21–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">and on My menservants and on My maidservants</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will pour out My Spirit in those days;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they shall prophesy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:30–31; Zephaniah 1:14–18; Malachi 4:1–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will give wonders in the heaven above,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and signs on the earth below,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">blood and fire and vapor of smoke.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Amos 8:9; Matthew 24:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sun shall be turned into darkness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the moon into blood,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">before the coming of the great and magnificent Day of the Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 10:12–13; Joel 2:32; Psalms 86:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">And it shall be that everyone who calls on the name of the Lord</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shall be saved.” ’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 3:2; John 14:10–11; Matthew 12:28"> “Men of Israel, hear these words: Jesus of Nazareth, a Man attested by God to you by miracles, wonders, and signs which God did through Him in your midst, just as you yourselves also know—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:22; Acts 4:28; Acts 3:18"> Him, being delivered by the determined counsel and foreknowledge of God, you took, and by lawless hands, nailing Him to a cross, you killed Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:14; 1 Peter 1:21; Acts 2:32"> whom God raised, thus doing away with the labor pains of death, because it was not possible for Him to be held by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 16:8–11; Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 62:6"> For David says concerning Him,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘I foresaw the Lord always before me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because He is at my right hand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that I may not be shaken.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 16:9; Psalms 63:5; Psalms 71:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore my heart was glad, and my tongue rejoiced;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">moreover my flesh also will dwell in hope.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Acts 2:31; Revelation 1:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because You will not abandon my soul in Hades,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor will You allow Your Holy One to see corruption.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 25:4; Psalms 21:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have made known to me the ways of life;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You will fill me full of joy with Your presence.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 13:36; 1 Kings 2:10; Acts 7:8–9"> “Men, brothers, let me speak with boldness to you about the patriarch David, that he both died and was buried, and his tomb is with us to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 132:11–18; Psalms 89:3–4; Jeremiah 33:14–15"> Therefore, being a prophet, and knowing that God had sworn with an oath to him that of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh, He would raise up the Christ to seat Him on his throne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 2:27; Psalms 16:10; Acts 13:35"> foreseeing this, he spoke about the resurrection of the Christ, ‘that His soul was not left in Hades, nor did His flesh see corruption.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 3:15; Acts 1:8; Acts 1:22"> This Jesus God has raised up, of which we all are witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:4; Acts 5:31; Acts 2:17"> Therefore having being exalted to the right hand of God, and having received from the Father the promise of the Holy Spirit, He poured out this which you now see and hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Matthew 22:42–45; Hebrews 1:13"> For David did not ascend into the heavens, but he says himself:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The Lord said to my Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sit at My right hand,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 19:19–3; Romans 16:20; Isaiah 60:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">till I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet.” ’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 5:30–31; Romans 14:8–12; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> “Therefore let all the house of Israel know assuredly that God has made this Jesus, whom you crucified, both Lord and Christ.”</span>
</div>

## The First Converts

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 3:10; Acts 22:10; Hebrews 4:12–13"> Now having heard this, they were cut to the heart, and they said to Peter and the rest of the apostles, “Men and brothers, what shall we do?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 22:16; Mark 16:16; Acts 8:12"> Then Peter said to them, “Repent, and let each of you be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of sins; and you shall receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 8:30"> For the promise is for you and for your children, and for all who are far off, as many as the Lord our God will call.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Deuteronomy 32:5; Matthew 17:17"> And with many other words he testified and exhorted them, saying, “Be saved from this crooked generation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 4:4; Acts 2:47; Acts 16:31–34"> Then those who gladly received his word were baptized; and that day about three thousand souls were added.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; Acts 1:14; 1 John 1:3"> And they were continuing in the teaching of the apostles, and in fellowship, and in the breaking of bread, and in prayers.</span>
</div>

## Life Among the Believers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 7:16; John 14:12; Luke 8:37"> Then fear came upon every soul, and many wonders and signs were taking place through the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 4:32; 1 John 3:16–18; 2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> Now all who believed were together, and they had all things in common,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 John 3:17; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; James 2:14–16"> and they were selling their possessions and goods, and were dividing them among all, to the degree that anyone had need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 5:42; Acts 20:7; Acts 2:42"> So continuing daily with one accord in the temple, and breaking bread from house to house, they were sharing food with joy and simplicity of heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 16:5; Acts 5:13–14; Acts 2:41"> praising God and having favor with all the people. And the Lord was adding to the church daily those who were being saved.</span>
</div>
