<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 12:1; Hebrews 11:8; Joshua 24:3"> and said to him, ‘Go out from your land and from your relatives, and come to a land which I will show you.’</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 13:15"> And He gave him no inheritance in it, not even a place to put his foot on, and He promised to give it to him for a possession, and to his descendants after him, though he had no child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 15:13; Exodus 12:40–41; Galatians 3:17"> But God spoke in this way: that his offspring would dwell in a foreign land, and that they would enslave them, and they would treat them badly four hundred years.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 35:23–26; Genesis 17:9–14; Genesis 21:1–4"> Then He gave him the covenant of circumcision; and so Abraham fathered Isaac and circumcised him on the eighth day; and Isaac fathered Jacob, and Jacob fathered the twelve patriarchs.</span>

## The Patriarchs in Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 39:2; Psalms 105:17; Isaiah 43:2"> “And the patriarchs, being jealous of Joseph, sold him into Egypt; and God was with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 3:4; 2 Timothy 4:18; Proverbs 2:6"> and delivered him out of all his afflictions, and gave him favor and wisdom before Pharaoh, king of Egypt; and he made him ruler over Egypt and all his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 41:54–57; Genesis 45:11; Genesis 42:5"> Now a famine came over all the land of Egypt and Canaan, and great affliction, and our fathers could find no provisions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:1–24; Genesis 43:2"> And Jacob, hearing that there was grain in Egypt, he sent out our fathers first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 45:1–18; Genesis 46:31–10"> And on the second visit Joseph was made known to his brothers, and Joseph's family became known to Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 46:26–27; Deuteronomy 10:22; Exodus 1:5"> Then Joseph sent and summoned his father Jacob and all his relatives, seventy-five people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 49:33; Exodus 1:6; Genesis 46:3–7"> So Jacob went down to Egypt; and he died, he and our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 24:32; Genesis 23:16; Exodus 13:19"> And they were brought back to Shechem, and were placed in the tomb which Abraham bought for a price of silver from the sons of Hamor, the father of Shechem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 1:7–12; Acts 7:6; Acts 13:17"> “Now when the time of the promise drew near which God swore to Abraham, the people increased and multiplied in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 1:8"> until there arose a different king, who did not know Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 105:25; Exodus 1:9–22; Revelation 12:4–5"> This king took advantage of our race, and oppressed our fathers, to make their babies exposed, in order that they might not preserve their lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 11:23; Exodus 2:2–10; 1 Samuel 16:12"> At this time Moses was born, and he was well pleasing to God; who was brought up in his father's house for three months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24; Exodus 2:2–10; Deuteronomy 32:26"> But when he was exposed, the daughter of Pharaoh took him up, and brought him up for herself as a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Daniel 1:17–20; 2 Chronicles 9:22"> And Moses was educated in all the wisdom of the Egyptians, and he was mighty in words and deeds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 2:11–12; Proverbs 21:1; Hebrews 11:24–26"> “Now when he had turned forty years old, it came into his heart to look upon his brothers, the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Acts 7:28; John 18:25–27"> And seeing someone being wronged, he retaliated, and avenged him who was being oppressed, and struck the Egyptian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 15:7; Mark 9:32; 2 Kings 5:1"> For he supposed that his brothers understood that God was giving to them deliverance by his hand, but they did not understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 2:13–15; Proverbs 18:19; 1 John 3:11–15"> And on the next day he appeared to them as they were fighting, and tried to reconcile them to peace, saying, ‘Men, you are brothers; why do you wrong one another?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Luke 12:14; Acts 7:54"> But the one wronging his neighbor pushed him away, saying, ‘Who appointed you a ruler and a judge over us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Do you desire to kill me as you killed the Egyptian yesterday?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 18:2–4; Exodus 2:14–22; Exodus 4:19–20"> Now Moses, at this saying, fled and became a stranger in the land of Midian, where he fathered two sons.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 3:1–2; Acts 7:35; Isaiah 43:2"> “And forty years having been fulfilled, an Angel of the Lord appeared to him in a flame of fire in a bush, in the wilderness of Mount Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 3:3–4"> And Moses, seeing it, marveled at the sight; and as he approached it to observe closely, the voice of the Lord came to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Matthew 22:32; Job 4:14"> saying, ‘I am the God of your fathers—the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.’ And Moses was trembling and was not bold enough to look closely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 3:5; Joshua 5:15; Ecclesiastes 5:1"> Then the Lord said to him, ‘Remove your sandals from your feet, for the place where you stand is holy ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 3:7–10; Exodus 4:31; Psalms 106:44"> I have certainly seen the mistreatment of My people in Egypt. I have heard their groaning and I have come down to deliver them. And now come, I will send you to Egypt.’</span>
</div>
</div>

## Israel Rebels Against God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Luke 19:14; Psalms 113:7–8"> “This Moses whom they denied, saying, ‘Who appointed you a ruler and a judge?’ This man God sent as a ruler and redeemer by the hand of the Angel who appeared to him in the bush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 12:41; Exodus 14:21; Exodus 33:1"> He led them out, and did wonders and signs in the land of Egypt, and at the Red Sea, and in the wilderness forty years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–19; Acts 3:22–23; John 18:37"> “This is that Moses who said to the sons of Israel, ‘The Lord our God will raise up for you a Prophet like me from your brothers.’</span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Romans 3:2; Hebrews 5:12; 1 Peter 4:11"> “This is he who was in the congregation in the wilderness with the Angel who was speaking to him on Mount Sinai, and with our fathers, the one who received the living oracles to give to us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 14:3–4; Numbers 11:5; Nehemiah 9:16–17"> whom our fathers did not desire to be obedient, but they rejected him and returned in their heart back to Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 32:1; Exodus 32:23"> saying to Aaron, ‘Make for us gods who will go before us: as for this Moses who led us out of the land of Egypt, we do not know what has happened to him.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 106:19–21; Nehemiah 9:18; Revelation 9:20"> And they made a calf in those days, offered a sacrifice to the idol, and were rejoicing in the works of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Amos 5:25–26; Ezekiel 20:39; Jeremiah 19:13"> But God turned away and gave them over to serve the host of heaven, just as it is written in the book of the Prophets:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Did you offer Me slaughtered animals and sacrifices during forty years in the</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">wilderness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">O house of Israel?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Amos 5:26–27; Exodus 20:4–5; Deuteronomy 5:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Indeed you carried up the tabernacle of Moloch,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the star of your god Remphan,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the images which you made to worship them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will relocate you beyond Babylon.’</span></span>

<div className="bp">

## Stephen Martyred

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Acts 5:33; Psalms 35:16; Matthew 25:30"> Now hearing these things they were infuriated in their hearts, and were gnashing their teeth against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Mark 16:19; Hebrews 1:3; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> But being full of the Holy Spirit, looking intently into heaven, he saw the glory of God, and Jesus standing at the right hand of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 26:64–65; Acts 10:11; Revelation 11:19"> and he said, “Behold! I see the heavens opened, and the Son of Man standing at the right hand of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Zechariah 7:11; Psalms 58:4; Acts 23:27"> Then they cried out with a loud voice, stopped their ears, and ran at him with one purpose;</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Acts 22:20; Acts 8:1; Acts 6:13"> and casting him outside the city, they stoned him. And the witnesses laid down their clothes beside the feet of a young man named Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Luke 23:46; Psalms 31:5; Acts 22:16"> And they stoned Stephen as he was calling on God and saying, “Lord Jesus, receive my spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Luke 23:34; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> Then he knelt down and cried out with a loud voice, “Lord, do not charge them with this sin.” And having said this, he fell asleep.</span>
