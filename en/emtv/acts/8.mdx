## Simon the Sorcerer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 13:6; Acts 5:36; John 7:18"> Now a certain man named Simon was previously in the city practicing magic and astounding the people of Samaria, claiming himself to be someone great,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; 2 Peter 2:2; Revelation 13:3"> to whom they gave heed, from the least to the greatest, saying, “This man is the great power of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 8:9; Galatians 3:1; Isaiah 44:25"> But they gave heed to him because of the long time that he had astonished them with the magic arts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 1:3; Matthew 28:19"> But when they believed Philip as he preached the good news of the things concerning the kingdom of God and the name of Jesus Christ, they were baptized, both men and women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:6–7; John 5:20; Acts 19:11"> Then Simon himself also believed; and having been baptized, he was continuing with Philip, and observing miracles and signs occurring, he was astounded.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 19:2; Matthew 28:19"> For He had not yet fallen upon any of them, but they had only been baptized in the name of Christ Jesus.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Numbers 21:7; Job 42:8"> Then Simon answered and said, “Pray to the Lord for me, that none of the things of which you have spoken may come upon me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Acts 26:22–23; Acts 1:8"> Then solemnly bearing witness to and speaking the word of the Lord, they returned to Jerusalem, and preached the gospel to many villages of the Samaritans.</span>

## Philip and the Ethiopian Eunuch

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 1:3"> Now an angel of the Lord spoke to Philip, saying, “Get up and go toward the south on the road which goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza.” This is desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; Psalms 68:31; Isaiah 56:3–8"> So he got up and went; and behold, a man of Ethiopia, a eunuch, a court official of Candace the queen of the Ethiopians, who was over all her treasury, and had come to Jerusalem to worship,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Isaiah 1:1; Acts 28:25"> and he was returning. And sitting upon his chariot, he was reading the prophet Isaiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:19; Acts 11:12; Acts 20:22–23"> Then the Spirit said to Philip, “Go near, and join yourself to this chariot.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 24:44–45; John 5:39; Ephesians 5:17"> And Phillip, running up to it, heard him reading the prophet Isaiah, and said, “Do you understand what you are reading?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Psalms 73:16–17; Isaiah 29:18–19"> And he said, “How can I, unless someone helps me?” And he urged Philip to come up and sit with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7–8; 1 Peter 2:21–24; Jeremiah 51:40"> Now the portion of the Scripture which he was reading was this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He was led as a sheep to the slaughter;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and as a lamb before its shearer is silent,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so He opened not His mouth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Isaiah 53:8; Zechariah 13:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">In His humiliation His justice was taken away,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and who will recount His generation?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For His life is taken away from the earth.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Matthew 2:2–4"> So the eunuch answered Philip and said, “Please tell me, of whom does the prophet say this; of himself, or of someone else?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Acts 18:28; Acts 28:23"> And Philip, opening his mouth, and beginning from this Scripture, preached the good news about Jesus to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 10:47; John 3:5; Titus 3:5–6"> Now as they were going down the road, they came upon some water. And the eunuch said, “Behold, here is water: what prevents me from being baptized?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:1; John 3:22–23"> And he ordered the chariot to stop, and they both went down into the water, both Phillip and the eunuch, and he baptized him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 18:12; 2 Kings 2:16; Ezekiel 8:3"> Now when they came up out of the water, the Spirit of the Lord caught up Philip, and the eunuch did not see him any longer; and he went on his way rejoicing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 21:8; Acts 23:23; Acts 23:33"> But Philip was found at Azotus. And as he passed through, he preached the gospel to all the cities until he came to Caesarea.</span>
</div>
</div>
