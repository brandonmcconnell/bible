<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 18:22; Acts 24:1; Micah 5:1"> But the high priest Ananias commanded those standing by him to strike him on the mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:1–2; John 7:51; Matthew 23:27–28"> Then Paul said to him, “God is about to strike you, you whitewashed wall! For you sit judging me according to the law, and contrary to the law do you command that I should be struck?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But those standing by said, “Do you revile the high priest of God?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Ecclesiastes 10:20; Jude 1:8–9"> Then Paul said, “I did not know, brothers, that he is the high priest; for it is written, ‘You shall not speak wickedly of a ruler of your people.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 24:15; Acts 24:21; Acts 26:5–8"> But when Paul perceived that one part was of the Sadducees and the other of the Pharisees, he cried out in the Sanhedrin, “Men and brothers, I am a Pharisee, a son of a Pharisee; concerning the hope and resurrection of the dead I am being judged!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 55:9; John 7:40–43; Matthew 10:34"> And when he had spoken this, there came to be a dispute between the Pharisees and the Sadducees, and the assembly was divided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 20:27; Matthew 22:23; Mark 12:18"> For the Sadducees say that there is no resurrection—nor angel nor spirit; but the Pharisees confess both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 22:7; Acts 22:17–18; John 12:29"> And there came to be a great outcry, and the scribes of the Pharisees' party stood up and contended sharply, saying, “We find no evil in this man; but if a spirit or an angel has spoken to him, let us not fight against God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:19; James 3:14–2; Acts 22:24"> And when much dissension developed, the commander, fearing lest Paul might be torn apart by them, commanded the detachment of soldiers to go down and seize him from the midst of them, and to bring him into the barracks.</span>
</div>

## The Plot Against Paul

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 18:9; Acts 19:21; 2 Timothy 4:17"> Now on the next night the Lord stood by him and said, “Take courage, Paul; for as you solemnly testified about the things concerning Me in Jerusalem, so you must also testify in Rome.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 23:30; Isaiah 8:9–10; Acts 23:21"> Now when it was day, some of the Jews joined in a conspiracy and bound themselves by a curse, saying neither would they eat nor drink until they had killed Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 16:2; 2 Samuel 15:31; 2 Samuel 15:12"> And there were over forty who had made this plot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 23:12; Jeremiah 8:12; Isaiah 3:9"> who came to the chief priests and to the elders and said, “We have bound ourselves by oath with a curse to taste nothing until we have killed Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Psalms 37:32–33; Proverbs 1:16"> Now you, therefore, together with the council, suggest to the commander that he be brought down to you tomorrow, as though you are going to determine more accurately the things concerning him; but we, before he comes near, are prepared to kill him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 23:10; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Acts 23:32"> But when Paul's sister's son heard about the ambush, he came and entered into the barracks, and told this to Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 22:3; Matthew 8:8–9; Matthew 10:16"> And Paul, summoning one of the centurions, said, “Lead this young man away to the commander, for he has something to tell him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 3:1; Ephesians 4:1; Acts 28:17"> Therefore taking him along, he brought him to the commander and said, “The prisoner Paul, having summoned me, asked me to bring this young man to you, for he has something to tell you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; Esther 9:12; Esther 7:2"> And the commander, taking him by his hand, withdrew to a private place and inquired, “What is it that you have to tell me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 23:14–15; Acts 23:1; Psalms 12:2"> And he said, “The Jews have agreed to ask that tomorrow you bring Paul down to the Sanhedrin, as though they are going to learn something more accurate concerning him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 23:12–14; Acts 14:5–6; 2 Corinthians 11:26"> Therefore you must not be persuaded by them; for more than forty of them are lying in wait for him, who have bound themselves by a curse neither to eat nor to drink until they have killed him; and now they are prepared, waiting for the promise from you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 1:44; Joshua 2:14"> Then the commander dismissed the young man, and commanded him, “Tell no one that you have made known these things to me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 14:25; Acts 23:33; Luke 12:38"> And having summoned two of the centurions he said, “Make ready two hundred soldiers, and seventy horsemen, and two hundred spearmen, in order that they may go to Caesarea, to leave at the third hour of the night;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 25:14; Acts 23:26; Acts 24:10"> and place mounts at their disposal, in order that they may put Paul on them, and bring him safely to Felix the governor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And he wrote a letter which contained this content:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 15:23; Acts 26:25; Luke 1:3"> Claudius Lysias, to the most excellent governor Felix: Greetings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 22:25–29; Acts 21:31–33; Acts 24:6"> This man, having been arrested by the Jews, was about to be killed by them. I came upon them with the detachment of soldiers and rescued him, having learned that he is a Roman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 22:30"> And desiring to know the cause for which they were accusing him, I took him down to their council;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 26:31; Acts 25:25; Acts 18:15"> and I found that he was accused concerning questions of their law, but had no accusation against him worthy of death or chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 23:35; Acts 25:16; Acts 25:5–6"> And when it became known to me that a plot against the man by the Jews was about to happen, I sent him at once to you, and also commanded the accusers to state the charges against him before you. Farewell.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:3–4; Luke 7:8; Acts 23:23–24"> Then the soldiers, as they were commanded, took Paul and brought him during the night to Antipatris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 23:23; Acts 23:10"> And on the next day they left the horsemen to go on with him, and returned to the barracks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 23:26; Acts 23:23–24; Acts 28:16"> When they entered into Caesarea and delivered the letter to the governor, they also presented Paul to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 21:39; Acts 25:1; Daniel 2:49"> And when the governor read the letter, he asked what province he was from. And learning that he was from Cilicia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 23:30; Acts 25:16; Matthew 27:27"> he said, “I will give you a hearing when your accusers arrive.” And he commanded him to be guarded in Herod's Praetorium.</span>
</div>
</div>
