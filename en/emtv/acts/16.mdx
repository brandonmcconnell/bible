## Timothy Joins Paul and Silas

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:5; 2 Timothy 1:2; 1 Corinthians 4:17"> Now he arrived at Derbe and Lystra. And behold, a certain disciple was there named Timothy, the son of a certain Jewish woman who believed, but his father was Greek,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; 2 Timothy 3:15; Acts 14:21"> who had a good testimony by the brothers in Lystra and Iconium.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 9:20; Galatians 5:6"> Paul wanted Timothy to accompany him, and he took him and circumcised him, because of the Jews who were in those places, for they all knew that his father was Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 15:28–29; Acts 15:2; Acts 11:30"> And as they were going through the cities, they were delivering to them the decrees to keep, which had been decided by the apostles and the elders at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 9:31; Acts 2:47; Acts 6:7"> Therefore the churches were strengthened in the faith, and were growing in number each day.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul's Vision of the Man of Macedonia

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 18:23; Acts 16:7; Acts 13:2–4"> Now when they had gone through Phrygia and the Galatian region, they were forbidden by the Holy Spirit from speaking the word in Asia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:9; Philippians 1:19; Galatians 4:6"> And having come to Mysia they attempted to go to Bithynia, but the Spirit did not permit them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12; 2 Timothy 4:13; Acts 16:11"> So they bypassed Mysia, and went down to Troas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 9:10–12; Acts 19:21; Acts 18:5"> And a vision appeared during the night to Paul. A certain man of Macedonia was standing, urging him, and saying, “Come across to Macedonia and help us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12–13; Acts 14:7; Acts 16:10–17"> Now when he saw the vision, immediately we sought to go out to Macedonia, concluding that the Lord had called us to preach the gospel to them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Lydia Baptized at Philippi

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 21:1; Acts 16:8"> Therefore, putting to sea from Troas, we ran a straight course to Samothrace, and the next day came to Neapolis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:2; Philippians 1:1; Acts 20:6"> and from there to Philippi, which is chief of that part of Macedonia, a colony. And we were spending some days in that very city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 13:14; Colossians 1:23; Acts 17:2"> And on the Sabbath day we went outside the city beside a river, where prayer was customarily made; and having sat down we spoke to the women who assembled together there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; Luke 24:45; Isaiah 50:5"> And a certain woman named Lydia, a dealer of purple cloth of the city of Thyatira, who worshiped God, heard us, whose heart the Lord opened to give heed to the things spoken by Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 11:14; Hebrews 13:2; Acts 16:33"> And when she and her household were baptized, she urged us, saying, “If you have judged me to be faithful to the Lord, come to my house and stay.” So she persuaded us.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul and Silas Imprisoned

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Deuteronomy 18:9–11; Isaiah 8:19"> Now it came to pass, as we went to prayer, that a certain slave girl, having a spirit of Python, met us, who brought much profit to her masters by fortune telling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 14:6; 1 Peter 2:16; Mark 5:7"> This girl, following after Paul and us, was crying out, saying, “These men are the servants of the Most High God, who proclaim to us the way of salvation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Luke 10:17–19; Acts 3:6"> And this she did for many days. But Paul, being greatly annoyed and turning around, said to the spirit, “I command you in the name of Jesus Christ to come out of her.” And he came out in that very hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:6; Acts 21:30; Acts 8:3"> And when her masters saw that the hope of their profit was gone, they took Paul and Silas, and dragged them to the marketplace before the rulers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 4:4; John 15:18–20; Acts 28:22"> And bringing them to the magistrates they said, “These men, being Jews, are agitating our city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esther 3:8; Acts 26:3; Jeremiah 10:3"> and they are proclaiming customs which are not lawful for us to receive or to do, since we are Romans.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:2; 2 Corinthians 6:5; 2 Corinthians 11:23–25"> And the crowd rose up together against them; and the magistrates tore their clothes and ordered men to beat them with rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 2:10; 2 Timothy 2:9; 1 Samuel 23:22–23"> And when they laid many strokes on them, they cast them into prison, commanding the prison keeper to keep them securely;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 13:27; Job 33:11; Jeremiah 29:26"> who, having received such a command, cast them into the inner prison and fastened their feet in the stocks.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Philippian Jailer Saved

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:16–18; Psalms 119:62; James 1:2"> Now about midnight Paul and Silas were praying and singing hymns to God, and the prisoners were listening to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 4:31; Acts 5:19; Acts 12:10"> And suddenly there was a great earthquake, so that the foundations of the prison were shaken; and immediately all the doors were opened and all the bonds were loosed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 12:19; Matthew 27:5; Judges 9:54"> And becoming awake, the prison keeper, seeing the doors of the prison open, drawing a sword, he was about to kill himself, supposing the prisoners to have escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:15; Luke 23:34; Luke 6:27–28"> But Paul called out with a loud voice, saying, “Do not harm yourself, for we are all here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Acts 24:25; Isaiah 60:14"> And asking for a light he rushed in, and he came trembling, and fell before Paul and Silas,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 2:37; John 6:27–29; Isaiah 58:9"> and he brought them forth outside and said, “Sirs, what must I do to be saved?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:12; Acts 2:38–39; John 3:36"> And they said, “Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and you shall be saved, you and your household.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 1:16; Colossians 1:27–28; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> And they spoke the word of the Lord to him and to all those in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:16; Acts 16:15; Acts 16:25"> And he took them along in that same hour of the night and washed their wounds, and immediately he and all his family were baptized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Acts 2:46; Romans 5:11"> And bringing them up into his house, he set a meal before them; and he rejoiced with his whole house, having believed in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:22; Psalms 76:10; Acts 5:40"> Now when it became day, the magistrates sent the officers, saying, “Release those men.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 15:33; John 14:27; 1 Samuel 1:17"> And the prison keeper told these words to Paul, saying, “The magistrates have sent so that you may be released. Now therefore depart, and go in peace.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 22:25–29; Psalms 58:1–2; Matthew 10:16"> But Paul said to them, “They beat us publicly, although we were uncondemned Romans, and they cast us into prison, and now do they cast us out secretly? No indeed! Let them come themselves and lead us out.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 22:29; Matthew 21:46; Matthew 14:5"> And the officers related to the magistrates these words, and they were afraid when they heard that they were Romans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 8:34; Mark 5:17; Daniel 6:23"> And coming, they appealed to them, and bringing them out, they asked them to depart from the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:2–3; Acts 16:14; Acts 14:22"> And coming out from the prison, they entered into the house of Lydia; and seeing the brothers, they encouraged them and departed.</span>
</div>
</div>
