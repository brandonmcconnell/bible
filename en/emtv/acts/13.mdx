## Preaching at Cyprus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 13:2; Acts 11:19"> So these two, having been sent forth by the Holy Spirit, went down to Seleucia, and from there they sailed to Cyprus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:14; Acts 13:46; Acts 14:1"> And arriving in Salamis, they proclaimed the word of God in the synagogues of the Jews. They also had John as their assistant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; 1 John 4:1; 2 Corinthians 11:13"> And going across the island as far as Paphos, they found a certain sorcerer, a false prophet, a Jew whose name was Bar-Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:38; Acts 13:12; Acts 18:12"> who was with the proconsul, Sergius Paulus, an intelligent man. This man summoned Barnabas and Saul, and desired to hear the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:8; Acts 13:6–7; 2 Timothy 4:14–15"> But Elymas the sorcerer (for so his name is translated) resisted them, seeking to turn away the proconsul from the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 3:8; Acts 4:8; Acts 2:4"> But Saul, who also is called Paul, was filled with the Holy Spirit, and gazing intently at him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:44; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Matthew 13:38"> he said, “O full of all guile and all craft, you son of the devil, you enemy of all righteousness, will you not cease perverting the straight ways of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 9:3; Psalms 32:4; Hebrews 10:31"> And now behold, the hand of the Lord is upon you, and you shall be blind, not seeing the sun for a season.” And immediately a mistiness and darkness fell upon him, and going about, he sought someone to lead him by the hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Acts 13:7; Luke 4:22"> Then when the proconsul saw what had happened, he believed, being amazed at the teaching of the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

## John Mark Deserts Paul and Barnabas

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 15:38; Acts 2:10; Acts 14:24–25"> Now Paul and his companions put to sea from Paphos, and came to Perga of Pamphylia; But John deserted them and returned to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 16:13; Acts 14:19; Acts 13:5"> But they went from Perga, and arrived in Antioch of Pisidia, and entering into the synagogue on the Sabbath day, they sat down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:22; Acts 15:21; Mark 5:22"> Now after the reading of the Law and the Prophets, the synagogue leaders sent to them, saying, “Men and brothers, if you have any word of exhortation for the people, speak.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Acts 13:26; Acts 10:2"> And Paul, arising and motioning with his hand, said, “Men, Israelites, and you who fear God, listen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6–8; Isaiah 63:9–14; Psalms 105:23–24"> The God of this people chose our fathers, and lifting up the people during their sojourn in the land of Egypt, and with an uplifted arm brought them out from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Psalms 95:8–11; Psalms 106:13–29"> Now for a time of about forty years He put up with them in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:1; Psalms 78:55; Acts 7:45"> And destroying seven nations in the land of Canaan, He gave their land to them as an inheritance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 2:16; 1 Samuel 3:20; 2 Kings 23:22"> “And after these things, for about four hundred and fifty years, He gave them judges until the time of Samuel the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:1; 1 Samuel 15:1; 1 Samuel 12:12–19"> And then they asked for a king, and God gave them Saul the son of Kish, a man of the tribe of Benjamin, for forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:13–14; 1 Kings 15:5; Psalms 78:70–72"> And removing him, He raised up for them David as king, to whom He also said bearing witness, ‘I have found David the son of Jesse, to be a man after My own heart, who will do all My will.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 1:1; Psalms 132:11; Revelation 22:16"> God, from the seed of this man, according to the promise, has brought to Israel salvation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 1:22; John 1:6–8; Luke 3:2–20"> after John had first proclaimed before His coming, the baptism of repentance to Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Mark 1:7; John 1:26–27"> Now as John was finishing his course, he said, ‘Whom do you suppose me to be? I am not He. But behold, there comes One after me, the sandal of whose feet I am not worthy to loosen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 1:77; Romans 1:16; Luke 1:69"> “Men, brothers, sons of the family of Abraham, and those among you who fear God, to you the message of this salvation has been sent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 3:17; Luke 24:24–27; Luke 24:20"> For those dwelling in Jerusalem, and their rulers, because they did not know Him, nor the voices of the Prophets which are read according to each Sabbath, condemning him, they fulfilled the Scriptures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 23:21–25; Luke 23:14–16; John 19:4"> And finding no cause for death, they asked Pilate that He be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 23:53; John 19:28; John 19:30"> And when they finished all the things having been written about Him, taking Him down from the tree, they put Him in a tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Acts 17:31; Matthew 28:6"> But God raised Him from the dead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:48; Acts 1:3; Acts 1:11"> who appeared for many days more to those going up with Him from Galilee to Jerusalem, who are His witnesses to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 4:13; Acts 26:6; Ezekiel 34:23"> And we proclaim to you the good news of the promise which was made to the fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; Hebrews 5:5; Hebrews 1:5–6"> that God has fulfilled this promise for us to their children, when He raised up Jesus, as it is also written in the second Psalm:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘You are My Son,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">today I have begotten You.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; Psalms 89:2–4; Romans 6:9"> And that He raised Him from the dead, no longer being about to return to corruption, thus He has said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘I will give you the holy and sure blessings of David.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Acts 2:27–31; Psalms 49:9"> Therefore also in another Psalm He says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘You will not allow Your Holy One to see corruption.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 2:29; 2 Samuel 7:12; 1 Kings 2:10"> “For David indeed in his own generation, having served by the purpose of God, died, and was buried with his fathers, and saw corruption;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 13:30; Acts 2:24"> but He whom God raised up did not see corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 24:47; 1 John 2:12; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> Therefore let it be known to you, brothers, that through this Man forgiveness of sins is proclaimed to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Romans 10:4; Galatians 2:16; Romans 8:3"> and from all things from which you could not (by the Law of Moses) be justified, by Him everyone that believes is justified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Hebrews 3:12; Malachi 3:2"> Watch therefore, lest there come upon you that which was spoken of in the prophets:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; 1 Peter 4:17; Romans 11:7–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Behold, you despisers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and marvel and perish!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I will work a work in your days which you will by no means believe,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">though one were to declare it to you.’ ”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Blessing and Conflict at Antioch

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Acts 28:28; Acts 10:33; Matthew 19:30"> Now as the Jews were going out of the synagogue, the Gentiles begged that these words might be spoken to them the next Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 11:23; Acts 14:22; Ephesians 2:8"> And when the synagogue service was broken up, many of the Jews and of the devout proselytes followed Paul and Barnabas, who, speaking to them, persuaded them to continue in the grace of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 110:3; Genesis 49:10; Isaiah 11:10"> On the coming Sabbath, almost all the city was assembled to hear the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Jude 1:10; Acts 18:6; Acts 5:17"> And the Jews, seeing the crowds, were filled with jealousy and spoke against the things being said by Paul, contradicting and blaspheming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 28:28; Matthew 21:43; Acts 18:5–6"> But Paul and Barnabas, speaking boldly said, “It was necessary for the word of God to be spoken to you first; but since you thrust it away, and judge yourselves not worthy of eternal life; behold, we turn to the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Isaiah 49:6; Isaiah 42:6; Luke 2:32"> For so the Lord has commanded us:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘I have set you to be a light of the nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that you should be for salvation to the end of the earth.’ ”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Romans 8:29–30; Ephesians 1:4; Romans 11:7"> Now when the Gentiles heard this, they rejoiced and glorified the word of the Lord. And as many as were appointed to eternal life believed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Acts 12:24; Acts 19:10; Acts 19:26"> And the word of the Lord was being spread throughout all the region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 13:45; Acts 14:2"> But the Jews incited the devout and prominent women and the leading men of the city, and raised up a persecution against Paul and Barnabas, and cast them out from their borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 10:14; Acts 18:6; Luke 9:5"> But shaking off the dust from their feet against them, they went to Iconium.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Romans 15:13; 1 Thessalonians 1:6; Acts 4:31"> And the disciples were filled with joy and with the Holy Spirit.</span>
</div>
</div>
