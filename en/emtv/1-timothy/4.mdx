## The Great Apostasy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:1–9; 2 Peter 2:1; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> But the Spirit explicitly says, that in latter times some shall fall away from the faith, giving heed to deceiving spirits and doctrines of demons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:19; Acts 20:30; Romans 16:18"> speaking lies in hypocrisy, having been seared with a hot iron as to their own conscience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; 1 Timothy 4:4; Romans 14:6"> forbidding to marry, commanding to abstain from foods which God created to be received with thanksgiving by those who are believers and have come to know the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:25; Romans 14:14"> Because every creature of God is good, and not one is to be rejected, being received with thanksgiving,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:3; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Luke 4:4"> for it is sanctified through the word of God and prayer.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Good Servant of Jesus Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:7–10; Colossians 3:16; Matthew 13:52"> Instructing the brothers in these things, you will be a good minister of Jesus Christ, being nourished by the words of faith and of the good doctrine which you have carefully followed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:16; Titus 2:12; 1 Timothy 1:4"> But reject profane and old wives' tales, and exercise yourself unto godliness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; 1 Timothy 6:6; Psalms 37:3–4"> For bodily exercise is profitable a little, but godliness is profitable for all things, having promise of the present life and of the coming life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:15"> Trustworthy is this word and worthy of all acceptance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; 1 John 2:2; 1 Timothy 2:6"> For to this end we both labor and suffer reproach, because we trust in the living God, who is the Savior of all men, especially of believers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:2; 2 Timothy 4:2; Titus 2:15"> These things command and teach.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Titus 2:7; 2 Timothy 2:22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> Let no one look down on your youthfulness, but rather become a pattern for the believers in word, in conduct, in love, in spirit, in faith, in purity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 6:4; Titus 2:15; 2 Timothy 4:2"> Until I come, give attention to public reading, to exhortation, to doctrine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; 1 Peter 4:9–11; 1 Timothy 1:18"> Do not neglect the gift that is in you, which was given to you through prophecy with the laying on of the hands of the eldership.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:16; Philippians 2:15–16; Psalms 143:5"> Meditate on these things; be in these things, so that your progress may be evident to all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 1:9; Titus 2:7; 2 John 1:8–9"> Take heed to yourself and to the doctrine. Continue in them, for by doing this you will save both yourself and those who hear you.</span>
</div>
</div>
