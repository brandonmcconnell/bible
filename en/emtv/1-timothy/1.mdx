## Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 1:3; Colossians 1:27; Acts 9:15"> Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ, according to the commandment of God our Savior and the Lord Jesus Christ, our hope,</span>
</div>

</div>

## Warnings Against False Teachings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:3; Galatians 1:6–7; 2 John 1:9–10"> Just as I urged you to remain in Ephesus as I traveled into Macedonia, that you should command certain people not to teach differently,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:7; Titus 1:14; Titus 3:9"> nor to pay attention to myths and endless genealogies, which cause disputes rather than godly edification which is in faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19; Galatians 5:22; 2 Timothy 2:22"> Now the goal of the commandment is love out of a pure heart, from a good conscience, and from sincere faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 1:10; 2 Timothy 2:23–24; Titus 3:9"> from which some, having strayed, have turned aside to futile talk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 1:22; 1 Timothy 6:4; 2 Peter 2:12"> desiring to be teachers of the law, not understanding either what they say or what they affirm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:12–13; Psalms 19:7–10; Romans 7:16"> But we know that the law is good if one uses it lawfully,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:19; 1 Peter 4:18; Romans 5:20"> knowing this: that the law is not laid down for the righteous, but for the lawless and insubordinate, for the ungodly and for sinners, for unholy and profane, for killers of fathers and killers of mothers, for murderers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Leviticus 18:22; 2 Timothy 4:3"> for fornicators, for homosexuals, for kidnappers, for liars, for perjurers, and if there is any other thing that is opposed to sound doctrine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 2:7; Titus 1:3; 1 Timothy 6:15"> according to the gospel of the glory of the blessed God, with which I was entrusted.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul's Gratitude for God's Mercy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Timothy 4:17; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> And I give thanks to the One who empowers me, Christ Jesus our Lord, because He considered me faithful, putting me into the ministry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Peter 2:10; Hebrews 6:4–8"> I, who was formerly a blasphemer, a persecutor, and an insolent man; but I was shown mercy, because, being ignorant, I did it in unbelief,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13; Ephesians 1:7–8; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> and the grace of our Lord superabounded with faith and love which is in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Luke 19:10; John 3:16–17"> Faithful is this word, and worthy of all acceptance, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am chief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Ephesians 2:7; Luke 7:47"> However, because of this I was shown mercy, so that in me first Jesus Christ might show forth all longsuffering, as a pattern for those who are going to believe on Him for everlasting life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20–21; Jude 1:25; 1 Timothy 6:15–16"> Now to the King of the Ages, immortal, invisible, the only wise God, be honor and glory forever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Fight the Good Fight

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 4:1–3"> This charge I commit to you, my son Timothy, according to the prophecies previously made concerning you, that you may war by them the good warfare,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:9; 1 Peter 3:15–16; Revelation 3:10"> having faith and a good conscience, which some having rejected, concerning the faith have suffered shipwreck,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:4–5; 2 Timothy 2:17; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> of whom are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I handed over to Satan so that they may be taught not to blaspheme.</span>
</div>
</div>
