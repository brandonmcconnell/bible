## Instructions for the Church

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; 1 Peter 5:5–6; Titus 2:2"> Do not sharply rebuke an older man, but rather exhort him as a father, younger men as brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:12; Matthew 12:50; Philippians 4:8"> older women as mothers, younger women as sisters, with all purity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:19; Acts 6:1; Deuteronomy 10:18"> Honor widows who are really widows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; John 19:26–27; Matthew 15:4–6"> But if a certain widow has children or grandchildren, let them first learn to show piety to their own household and to make repayment to their parents; for this is acceptable before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:3; Luke 2:37; 1 Corinthians 7:32"> Now she who is really a widow and having been left alone, has set her hope on God, and continues in supplications and prayers night and day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 5:5; Revelation 18:7; Luke 15:24"> But she who is living for pleasure is dead while she lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:11; Titus 2:15; 2 Timothy 4:1"> And these things command, so that they may be blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Luke 11:11–13"> But if anyone does not provide for his own, and especially for those of his own house, he has denied the faith and is worse than an unbeliever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; 1 Timothy 5:11; Luke 2:36–37"> Let a widow be enrolled having become not less than sixty years, the wife of one man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18; Titus 3:8; Acts 9:36"> having a reputation for good works, if she has brought up children, if she has lodged strangers, if she has washed the saints' feet, if she has relieved the afflicted, if she has diligently followed every good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:14; Hosea 13:6; James 5:5"> But refuse to enroll the younger widows; for whenever they grow lustful against Christ, they desire to marry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 1:6; James 3:1; 1 Corinthians 11:34"> having condemnation because they have set aside their first faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 20:19; 2 Thessalonians 3:6–11; Acts 20:30"> And besides they learn to be idle, going around from house to house, and not only idle but also gossipy and busybodies, speaking things which are not proper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Timothy 6:1; Titus 2:8"> Therefore I desire the younger widows to marry, to bear children, to manage the house, to give no opportunity to the adversary because of reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:2; Jude 1:4–5; 2 Peter 2:20–22"> For already some have turned aside after Satan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:3–5; 1 Timothy 5:8"> If any believing man or believing woman has widows, let that one relieve them, and do not let the church be burdened, that it may relieve those who are really widows.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Honor the Elders

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Galatians 6:6"> Let the elders having ruled well be counted worthy of double honor, especially those who labor in the word and doctrine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Corinthians 9:14; 1 Corinthians 9:7–10"> For the Scripture says, “You shall not muzzle an ox threshing grain,” and, “The worker is worthy of his wages.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 18:16; Deuteronomy 19:15; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> Do not receive an accusation against an elder unless on the basis of two or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Titus 1:13; 1 Timothy 1:20"> Those who are sinning rebuke before all, that the rest also may have fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; 2 Timothy 4:1; 1 Timothy 6:13"> I charge you before God and the Lord Jesus Christ and the elect angels that you observe these things without prejudice, doing nothing according to partiality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; 1 Timothy 3:10; 1 Timothy 3:6"> Lay hands on no one hastily, nor share in the sins of others; keep yourself pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Proverbs 31:4–7; 1 Timothy 4:4"> No longer drink water only, but use a little wine on account of your stomach, and your frequent illnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Acts 8:18; Galatians 5:19–21"> The sins of some men are evident beforehand, preceding them to judgment, but some also follow after.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Luke 11:33; Psalms 37:5–6"> Likewise also the good works of some are evident beforehand, and those which are otherwise cannot be hidden.</span>
</div>
