## Pray for All People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:3; Matthew 6:9–10; James 5:16"> Therefore I exhort first of all that supplications, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgivings be made on behalf of all people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9–13; Jeremiah 29:7; Ezra 6:10"> on behalf of kings and all those who are in authority, that we may lead a tranquil and quiet life in all godliness and dignity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 1 Timothy 1:1; 1 Peter 2:20"> For this is good and acceptable before God our Savior,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:32; 2 Peter 3:9; Ezekiel 18:23"> who desires all people to be saved, and to come to the full knowledge of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:6; Galatians 3:20; Hebrews 9:15"> For there is one God, and there is one Mediator between God and men, the Man Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 John 2:1–2"> who gave Himself as a ransom for all, the testimony in due time;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 2 Timothy 1:11; Romans 9:1"> for which I was appointed a herald and apostle—I speak the truth in Christ, I do not lie—a teacher of Gentiles in faith and truth.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Men and Women in the Church

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 134:2; Psalms 63:4; Hebrews 10:22"> I desire therefore that men pray in every place, lifting up holy hands, without wrath and doubting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Titus 2:3–5; Psalms 149:4"> Likewise also that the women adorn themselves in modest apparel, with propriety and discretion, not with braids or gold or pearls or expensive clothing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 3:3–5; Revelation 2:19"> but which is fitting for women professing godliness, by means of good works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:34–35; Ephesians 5:22–24; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> Let a woman learn in silence with all submission.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:34"> And I do not permit a woman to teach, nor to have authority over a man, but to be in silence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 2:18; 1 Corinthians 11:8–9; Genesis 1:27"> For Adam first was formed, then Eve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:3; Genesis 3:6; Genesis 3:12–13"> And Adam was not deceived, but the woman being deceived, has come to be in transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 2:12; Genesis 3:15–16; 1 Peter 4:7"> Nevertheless she shall be saved through childbirth, if they remain in faith and love, and sanctification, with self-control.</span>
</div>
</div>
