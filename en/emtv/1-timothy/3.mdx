## Qualifications for Overseers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 1:7; Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:2"> Faithful is this word: If anyone aspires to the position of a bishop, he desires a good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:6–9; 2 Timothy 2:24; Romans 12:13"> It is necessary, therefore, for a bishop to be irreproachable, the husband of one wife, temperate, sober-minded, well-behaved, hospitable, skillful at teaching;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 1:7; Ephesians 5:18; Titus 3:2"> not given to wine, not a bully, not greedy for money, but gentle, not quarrelsome, not loving money;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Titus 1:6; Genesis 18:19; 1 Timothy 3:12"> one ruling his own house well, having his children in submission with all reverence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:13; Ephesians 5:24; Acts 20:28"> (for if one does not know how to rule his own house, how will he take care of the church of God?);</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:4; Hebrews 5:12–13; 1 Peter 2:2"> not a new convert, lest being puffed up with pride he fall into the same condemnation as the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Timothy 2:26; Colossians 4:5"> Moreover he must have a good testimony among those outside, lest he fall into reproach and the snare of the devil.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Qualifications for Deacons

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:1; 1 Timothy 5:23; Acts 6:3–6"> Likewise deacons must be reverent, not double-tongued, not being given to much wine, not greedy for money,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19; 2 John 1:9–10; 1 Timothy 1:5"> holding the mystery of the faith with a pure conscience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:22; 1 Timothy 3:13; Titus 1:6–7"> But let these also first be tested; then let them serve as deacons, being irreproachable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Titus 2:3; Titus 3:2; 1 Timothy 3:2"> Likewise their wives must be reverent, not slanderers, temperate, faithful in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; 1 Timothy 3:4–5; Philippians 1:1"> Let deacons be the husbands of one wife, ruling their children well, and their own houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Hebrews 6:10; Acts 21:35"> For those who have served well as deacons obtain a standing for themselves and much boldness in the faith which is in Christ Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Mystery of Godliness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:34; Philemon 1:22; 2 Corinthians 1:15–17"> These things I write to you, hoping to come to you shortly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Ephesians 2:21–22; 1 Thessalonians 1:9"> but if I am delayed, I write so that you may know how it is necessary to conduct oneself in the house of God, which is the church of the living God, the pillar and bulwark of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:14; Hebrews 1:3; Mark 16:19"> And confessedly, great is the mystery of godliness: God was manifested in the flesh, justified in the Spirit, seen by angels, proclaimed among the Gentiles, believed on in the world, and was received up in glory.</span>
</div>
</div>
