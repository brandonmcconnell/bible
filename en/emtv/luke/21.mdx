## The Widow's Two Mites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 12:41–44; John 8:20; Matthew 27:6"> And looking up, He saw the rich putting their gifts into the treasury,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 12:42"> and He saw also a certain poor widow putting in two mites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 10:34; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3"> And He said, “Truly I say to you that this poor widow has put in more than all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 2:44–45; Luke 15:12; Acts 4:34"> For all these out of their abundance have put in offerings for God, but she out of her poverty put in all the livelihood which she had.”</span>

## Jesus Predicts the Destruction of the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 2:20; Matthew 24:1–51; Mark 13:1–37"> Then, as some spoke about the temple, how it was adorned with beautiful stones and donations, He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:2; Mark 13:2; Lamentations 5:18"> “These things which you see—the days will come in which not one stone shall be left upon another that shall not be thrown down.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Signs of the Times and the End of the Age

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 1:6–7; Mark 13:14; Mark 13:3–4"> So they asked Him, saying, “Teacher, when will these things be? And what will be the sign when these things are about to happen?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Mark 13:5–6; Ephesians 5:6"> And He said: “Take heed that no one deceive you. For many will come in My name, saying, ‘I AM,’ and, ‘The time has drawn near.’ Therefore do not go after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 21:28; Matthew 24:6–8; Mark 13:7–8"> But whenever you hear of wars and insurrections, do not be terrified; for these things must happen first, but the end will not come immediately.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:19–20; Mark 13:8; 2 Chronicles 15:5–6"> Then He said to them, “Nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:29–30; Luke 21:25–27"> And there will be great earthquakes in various places, and famines and pestilences; and there will be fearful events and great signs from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12–14; Acts 5:40; Acts 22:30"> But before all these things, they will lay their hands on you and they will persecute you, delivering you up to the synagogues and prisons. You will be brought before kings and rulers on account of My name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:12; Philippians 1:28; 2 Thessalonians 1:5"> But it shall turn out for you for a testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 12:11–12; Mark 13:11; Matthew 10:19–20"> Put it then in your hearts not to practice beforehand giving your defense;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; Ephesians 6:19; Luke 12:12"> for I will give you a mouth and wisdom which all your adversaries will not be able to contradict, nor to withstand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 12:53; Revelation 6:9; Micah 7:5–6"> You will be betrayed even by parents and relatives, friends and brothers; and they will put some of you to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:19; Luke 6:22; John 15:21"> And you will be hated by all people for My name's sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 10:30; 1 Samuel 14:45; Luke 12:7"> But not a hair of your head shall by any means perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Matthew 10:22; James 1:3"> By your patient endurance you shall possess your souls.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Destruction of Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 19:43; Daniel 9:27; Mark 13:14"> “But when you see Jerusalem surrounded by armies, then know that its desolation has drawn near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:1; Genesis 19:17; Luke 17:31–33"> Then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains, let those who are in the midst of her depart, and let not those who are in the fields enter into her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 9:7; Isaiah 65:12–16; Isaiah 63:4"> For these are the days of vengeance, to fulfill all that has been written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 13:17; Matthew 24:19; Luke 23:29"> But woe to those who are pregnant and to those who are nursing a baby in those days! For there will be great distress in the land and wrath among this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:25; Revelation 11:2; Isaiah 63:18"> And they will fall by the edge of the sword, and be led away captive into all nations. And Jerusalem will be trampled by Gentiles until the times of the Gentiles are fulfilled.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Coming of the Son of Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Joel 2:30–31; Revelation 6:12–14"> “And there shall be signs in the sun, in the moon, and in the stars; and on the earth distress of nations, with perplexity, as the sea and the waves roar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Deuteronomy 28:65–67; 2 Peter 3:10–12"> men's hearts failing them from fear and the expectation of those things which are coming upon the earth, for the powers of heaven shall be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 24:30; Revelation 1:7; Mark 13:26"> Then they will see the Son of Man coming in a cloud with power and great glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 8:23; Isaiah 60:1–2; Ephesians 4:30"> Now when these things begin to happen, look up and lift up your heads, because your redemption draws near.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Fig Tree

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:32–35; Mark 13:28–30"> Then He spoke to them a parable: “Look at the fig tree, and all the trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 12:57"> When they are already budding, you see and know for yourselves that summer is now near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 5:9; Hebrews 10:37; 1 Peter 4:7"> So you also, when you see these things coming to pass, know that the kingdom of God is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 13:30; Matthew 24:34; Matthew 23:36"> Assuredly I say to you, this generation shall by no means pass away till all things are fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Mark 13:31; Matthew 24:35"> Heaven and earth shall pass away, but My words shall by no means pass away.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Be Ready!

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 4:19; Luke 12:45–46; Luke 12:40"> “But take heed to yourselves, lest your hearts be weighed down with carousing, drunkenness, and worries of life, and that Day come upon you unexpectedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; Psalms 11:6; Jeremiah 48:43–44"> For it will come as a snare on all those who live on the face of the whole earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 John 2:28; Matthew 25:13; 1 Peter 5:8"> Watch therefore, praying always that you may be counted worthy to escape everything that is about to happen, and to stand before the Son of Man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 22:39; Mark 11:19; Matthew 21:17"> And in the daytime He was teaching in the temple, but at night He went out and stayed on the mountain called Olivet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 8:1–2"> Then early in the morning all the people came to Him in the temple to hear Him.</span>
</div>
