## Jesus Comes to Zacchaeus' House

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 6:1–27; Luke 18:35; 2 Kings 2:18–22"> And having entered, He passed through Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 18:24–27; 2 Chronicles 17:5–6"> And behold, there was a man named Zacchaeus, and he was a chief tax collector, and he was rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 23:8; Luke 9:7–9; John 12:21"> And he was trying to see who Jesus was, but could not because of the crowd, for he was of short stature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 9:10; Luke 5:19; 1 Kings 10:27"> And running up ahead in front, he went up into a sycamore tree so that he might see Him, because He was about to pass by that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; John 14:23; Hebrews 13:2"> And when He came upon that place, Jesus looked up and saw him, and said to him, “Zacchaeus, hurry and come down, for today I must stay in your house.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:29; Acts 16:34; Acts 16:15"> So he hurried and came down, and received Him, rejoicing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 15:2; Luke 7:39; Luke 7:34"> But when they saw it, they all were complaining, saying, “He has gone to be a guest with a sinner.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:6; Numbers 5:7; Luke 18:22–23"> Then Zacchaeus stood and said to the Lord, “Look, Lord, I give half of my possessions to the poor; and if I have extorted anything from anyone, I restore fourfold.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; Luke 13:16; Romans 4:16"> And Jesus said to him, “Today salvation has come to this house, because he also is a son of Abraham;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Matthew 1:21; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> for the Son of Man has come to seek and to save that which was lost.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Minas

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 17:20; Acts 1:6; 2 Thessalonians 2:1–3"> Now as they heard these things, He told another parable, because He was near Jerusalem and because they thought the kingdom of God would appear immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–30; Luke 19:12–27; Luke 20:9"> Therefore He said: “A certain nobleman went into a far country to receive for himself a kingdom and to return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–15; John 12:26; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> And having called ten of his servants, he gave them ten minas, and said to them, ‘Do business till I come.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Acts 7:51–52; Acts 3:14–15"> But his citizens hated him, and sent a delegation after him, saying, ‘We will not have this man to reign over us.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:1–5; Romans 14:10–12; Psalms 2:4–6"> “And so it was that when he returned, having received the kingdom, that he commanded those servants to whom he had given the money, to be called to him, so that he might know who gained what by trading.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 2:18–26; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> Then came the first, saying, ‘Master, your mina has earned ten minas.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 16:10; Matthew 25:21; Revelation 2:26–29"> And he said to him, ‘Well done, good servant; because you were faithful in a very little, have authority over ten cities.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 4:20; Matthew 13:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12"> And the second came, saying, ‘Master, your mina made five minas.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 1 Corinthians 15:41–42; 1 Corinthians 3:8"> Likewise he said to him, ‘You also be over five cities.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:24; Proverbs 26:13–16; James 4:17"> “Then another came, saying, ‘Master, behold your mina, which I had laid away in a handkerchief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:24–25; Romans 8:7; 1 John 4:18"> For I feared you, because you are a harsh man. You take up what you did not deposit, and reap what you did not sow.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:16; Matthew 12:37; Job 15:5–6"> And he said to him, ‘Out of your own mouth I will judge you, you wicked servant. You knew that I was a harsh man, taking up what I did not deposit, and reaping what I did not sow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:25–27; Romans 2:4–5; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> Why then did you not give my money to a bank, and when I came, I would have collected it with interest?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 16:2; Luke 12:20"> “And he said to those standing by, ‘Take the mina away from him, and give it to him who has ten minas.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 16:2; 2 Samuel 7:19; Isaiah 55:8–9"> But they said to him, ‘Master, he has ten minas.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 John 1:8; Luke 8:18; Matthew 21:43"> ‘For I say to you that to everyone who has, more shall be given; and from him who does not have, even what he has shall be taken away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Psalms 21:8–9; Matthew 22:7"> Nevertheless bring here those enemies of mine, who did not want me to reign over them, and slay them before me.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Triumphal Entry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 9:51; Hebrews 12:2; 1 Peter 4:1"> And having said these things, He went on ahead, going up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Matthew 21:1–11"> And it came to pass, as He drew near to Bethphage and Bethany, to the mountain which is called Olivet, that He sent two of His disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 22:8–13; Luke 19:32; 1 Samuel 10:2–9"> saying, “Go into the village opposite you, in which when you enter you will find a colt tied up, on which no one of men has ever sat. Loose him and bring him here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 24:1; Psalms 50:10–12; Mark 11:3–6"> And if anyone asks you, ‘Why are you loosing him?’ thus you shall say to him, ‘Because the Lord has need of him.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 22:13"> So those who were sent departed and found it just as He told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> But as they were loosing the colt, the owners of it said to them, “Why are you loosing the colt?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; John 10:35; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> And they said, “The Lord has need of him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 11:7–8; Matthew 21:7; John 12:14"> Then they brought him to Jesus. And when they threw their own clothes on the colt, they set Jesus on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; Matthew 21:8"> And as He went, they were spreading their clothes under Him on the road.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 12:12–13; Ezra 3:10–13; Matthew 21:1"> And as He was approaching the descent of the Mount of Olives, the whole multitude of the disciples began to rejoice and praise God with a loud voice for all the miracles which they had seen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Luke 13:35; Psalms 118:22–26"> saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“ ‘Blessed is the King who comes in the name of the Lord!’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Peace in heaven and glory in the highest!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 4:1–2; Matthew 23:13; Acts 4:16–18"> And some of the Pharisees said to Him from the crowd, “Teacher, rebuke Your disciples.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:11; Isaiah 55:12; Matthew 3:9"> But He answered and said to them, “I tell you that if these should keep silent, the stones would immediately cry out.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Weeps over Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 13:34–35; John 11:35; Psalms 119:136"> And as He drew near, seeing the city, He wept over it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Hebrews 10:26–29; Isaiah 48:18"> saying, “If you had known, even you, especially in this your day, the things pertaining to your peace! But now they have been hidden from your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 21:20–24; Isaiah 29:1–4; Ezekiel 4:2"> For the days will come upon you when your enemies will build an embankment around you, surround you and hem you in from every side,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 21:6; Luke 13:34–35; John 3:18–21"> and they will level you to the ground, and your children with you; and they will not leave in you one stone upon another, because you did not know the season of your visitation.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Cleanses the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 21:12–13; Mark 11:15–17; John 2:13–17"> And when He entered into the temple, He began to drive out those buying and selling in it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Jeremiah 7:11; Psalms 93:5"> saying to them, “It is written, ‘My house is a house of prayer,’ but you have made it a ‘den of thieves.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 26:55; Mark 11:27–33"> And He was teaching daily in the temple. But the chief priests, the scribes, and the leaders of the people were seeking to destroy Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:3; Acts 16:14; John 7:46–49"> and were unable to do anything; for all the people were very attentive to hear Him.</span>
</div>
</div>
