## Beware of Hypocrisy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:1; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; James 3:17"> Now in the meantime, as an innumerable crowd had gathered together so that they were stepping on one another, He began to say to His disciples first of all, “Beware of the leaven of the Pharisees, which is hypocrisy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 4:22; Luke 8:17; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> For there is nothing covered that shall not be unveiled, and nothing secret that shall not be made known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:27; Matthew 12:36; Ecclesiastes 10:20"> Therefore whatever you have spoken in the dark shall be heard in the light, and what you have spoken in the ear behind closed doors shall be proclaimed on the housetops.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Teaches the Fear of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; Jeremiah 1:8; Revelation 2:10"> “And I say to you, My friends, do not be afraid of those who kill the body, and after that have nothing further they can do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Matthew 10:28; Revelation 14:7"> But I will show you whom you should fear: fear Him who after He kills, has authority to cast into hell; yes, I say to you, fear Him!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:24; Psalms 145:15–16; Psalms 147:9"> “Are not five sparrows sold for two copper coins? And not one of them is forgotten before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:30–31; Matthew 6:26; Isaiah 43:3–4"> But the very hairs of your head have all been numbered. Do not fear therefore; you are of more value than many sparrows.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Whoever Confesses Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 10:32–33; 1 John 2:23; Romans 10:9–10"> “Also I say to you, whoever confesses Me before men, him the Son of Man also will confess before the angels of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:12; Luke 9:26; Mark 8:38"> But he who denies Me before men will be denied before the angels of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 12:31–32; 1 John 5:16; 1 Timothy 1:13"> “And anyone who speaks a word against the Son of Man, it will be forgiven him; but to him who blasphemes against the Holy Spirit, it will not be forgiven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 10:17–20; Luke 21:12–14; Acts 4:5–7"> “Now whenever they bring you to the synagogues and rulers and authorities, do not worry about how or what you should answer, or what you should say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Matthew 10:20; Acts 4:8"> For the Holy Spirit will teach you in that very hour what you must say.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Rich Fool

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 6:45; 1 Timothy 6:5; Ezekiel 33:31"> Then someone from the crowd said to Him, “Teacher, tell my brother to divide the inheritance with me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 2:3; Romans 2:1; Romans 9:20"> But He said to him, “Man, who appointed Me a judge or an arbitrator over you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; 1 Timothy 6:6–10; Psalms 119:36–37"> And He said to them, “Take heed and beware of covetousness, for one's life does not consist in the abundance of his possessions.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 26:12–14; Hosea 2:8; Genesis 41:47–49"> Then He told a parable to them, saying: “The field of a certain rich man produced well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:42; Isaiah 58:7; Romans 12:13"> And he reasoned within himself, saying, ‘What shall I do, because I have nowhere to gather my crops?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 3:15; James 4:15; Luke 12:21"> So he said, ‘I will do this: I will tear down my barns and I will build bigger ones, and there I will gather all my crops and my goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; James 4:13–15; 1 Corinthians 15:32"> And I will say to my soul, “Soul, you have many goods laid away for many years; relax; eat, drink, and be merry.” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 27:8; Jeremiah 17:11; 1 Timothy 6:7"> But God said to him, ‘You fool! This night your soul will be required of you; then whose will those things be which you have prepared?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–20; Luke 12:33; James 2:5"> “So is he who accumulates treasure for himself, and is not rich toward God.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Do Not Worry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 6:25–34; Philippians 4:6; Hebrews 13:5"> Then He said to His disciples, “Therefore I say to you, do not worry about your life, what you will eat; nor about the body, what you will wear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 2:6; Proverbs 13:8; Job 2:4"> Life is more than food, and the body is more than clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:31; Psalms 147:9; Luke 12:7"> Consider the ravens, for they neither sow nor reap, which have neither storeroom nor barn; and yet God feeds them. How much more are you worth than birds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 6:27; Matthew 5:36; Luke 19:3"> And which of you by worrying can add one cubit to his stature?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Psalms 39:6; Ecclesiastes 7:13"> Therefore if you cannot do a very little thing, why do you worry about the rest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 6:28–30; Luke 12:24; 1 Kings 10:1–13"> Consider the lilies, how they grow: they neither toil nor spin; and yet I say to you, not even Solomon in all his glory was arrayed like one of these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 14:31; Isaiah 40:6; Matthew 6:30"> But if God so clothes the grass, although it is in the field today, and tomorrow is cast into the oven, by how much rather will He clothe you, O you of little faith?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 6:31; Luke 12:22; Luke 22:35"> “And do not seek what you may eat or what you may drink, and do not be anxious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 6:32; Matthew 6:8; Ephesians 4:17"> For all these things the nations of the world seek after, but your Father knows that you need these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 34:9"> But seek the kingdom of God, and all these things shall be added to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 12:28; 1 Peter 1:3–5; Revelation 1:6"> “Do not fear, little flock, because your Father is well pleased to give to you the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 19:21; Acts 4:34–35; Acts 2:45"> Sell your possessions and give alms; make for yourselves money bags which do not grow old, an unfailing treasure in the heavens, where a thief does not come near, nor does a moth destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–3; Matthew 6:21; Philippians 3:20"> For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Faithful Servant and the Evil Servant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Peter 1:13; Ephesians 6:14; Matthew 25:4–10"> “Let your waist be girded and your lamps burning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jude 1:20–21; James 5:7–8; Revelation 3:20"> and you yourselves be like men who wait for their master, when he will return from the wedding celebration, so that when he comes and knocks, immediately they may open to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Peter 3:14; Matthew 24:42; John 13:4–5"> Blessed are those servants whom the master, when he comes, will find watching. Assuredly I say to you, that he will gird himself and have them recline to eat, and he will come and serve them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:4–5; Matthew 25:6"> And if he comes in the second watch, or comes in the third watch, and find them so, blessed are those servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Revelation 3:3; Revelation 16:15"> But know this, that if the master of the house had known in what hour the thief was coming, he would have kept watch, and not allowed his house to be broken into.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6; Matthew 25:13; Romans 13:11"> Therefore you also be ready, for in an hour that you think not, the Son of Man comes.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 13:37; 1 Peter 4:7; 1 Peter 5:8"> Then Peter said to Him, “Lord, do You speak this parable only to us, or to all people?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 24:45–46; 1 Peter 5:1–4; Hebrews 13:17"> And the Lord said, “Who then is a faithful and wise manager, whom his master will make ruler over his household, to give them their portion of food in due season?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Blessed is that servant whom his master will find so doing when he comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 22:29–30; Matthew 24:47; Luke 19:17–19"> Truly I say to you, that he will appoint him over all his possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 24:48–50; Revelation 18:7–8; Philippians 3:18–19"> But if that servant says in his heart, ‘My master is delaying his coming,’ and begins to beat the male and female servants, and to eat and drink and be drunk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 12:40; Matthew 24:51; Psalms 11:5"> the master of that servant will come on a day which he does not expect, and at an hour which he does not know, and he will cut him in two and appoint him his portion with the unbelievers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="James 4:17; John 9:41; John 12:48"> And that servant who knew his master's will, and did not get ready or do according to his will, shall be beaten with many blows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="James 3:1; Leviticus 5:17; Luke 16:10–12"> But he who did not know, yet did things worthy of blows, shall be beaten with few. For everyone to whom much is given, from him much will be required; and to whom much has been entrusted, of him they will ask much more.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Christ Brings Division

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Luke 12:51–52; Matthew 3:10–12"> “I have come to cast fire to the earth, and how I wish it were already kindled!</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 12:27–28; John 19:30; John 7:6–8"> But I have a baptism to be baptized with, and how distressed I am till it is completed!</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 10:34–36; Luke 12:49; Matthew 24:7–10"> Do you think that I came to give peace on earth? I tell you, not at all, but rather division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Acts 14:1–4; John 15:18–21; Micah 7:5–6"> For from now on five in one house will be divided: three against two, and two against three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Micah 7:6; Matthew 10:21–22; Matthew 24:10"> Father will be divided against a son and a son against father, a mother against a daughter and a daughter against a mother, a mother-in-law against her daughter-in-law and daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Discerning the Times

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 16:2–4; 1 Kings 18:44–45"> Then He also said to the crowds, “Whenever you see a cloud rising from the west, immediately you say, ‘A rainstorm is coming’; and so it happens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Job 37:17; Matthew 20:12"> And whenever you see the south wind blow, you say, ‘There will be hot weather’; and it happens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 16:3; Matthew 24:32–33; Matthew 11:25"> Hypocrites! You know how to discern the face of the earth and sky, but how is it you do not discern this time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; John 7:24; Acts 13:26–38"> And why do you not judge what is right, even of yourselves?</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Proverbs 25:8–9; Matthew 5:23–26; Luke 13:24–28"> When you go with your adversary to the magistrate, make an effort to make a settlement with him on the road, lest he drag you to the judge, and the judge deliver you to the bailiff, and the bailiff throw you into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Mark 12:42; Matthew 18:34; Matthew 25:46"> I tell you, you shall by no means depart from there till you have paid the very last mite.”</span>
</div>
</div>
