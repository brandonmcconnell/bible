## Healing Again on the Sabbath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:2; Luke 20:20; Psalms 62:4"> Now it came to pass, when He came into the house of one of the rulers of the Pharisees to eat bread on the Sabbath, that they were watching Him closely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And behold, there was a certain man before Him who had dropsy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:10; Mark 3:4; Luke 13:14–16"> And Jesus, answering, spoke to the lawyers and Pharisees, saying, “Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Matthew 21:25–27"> But they were silent. And taking hold of him, He healed him, and let him go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 13:15; Exodus 23:4–5; Matthew 12:11–12"> Then He answered them, saying, “Which of you, having a son or an ox that has fallen into a pit, will not immediately pull him out on the Sabbath day?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 20:40; Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10"> And they were not able to answer Him regarding these things.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Taking the Lowly Place

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Luke 11:43; Matthew 23:6"> So He told a parable to those who were invited, when He noted how they chose the places of honor, saying to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7"> “Whenever you are invited by someone to a wedding celebration, do not sit down in the place of honor, lest one more honorable than you be invited by him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 11:2; Proverbs 3:35"> and he who invited you and him shall come and say to you, ‘Give place to this man,’ and then you begin with shame to take the last place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7; Proverbs 15:33; 1 Samuel 15:17"> But when you are invited, go and sit down in the last place, so that when he who invited you comes, he shall say to you, ‘Friend, go up higher.’ Then you will have glory in the presence of those who recline with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 18:14; Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 29:23"> For everyone who exalts himself shall be humbled, and he who humbles himself shall be exalted.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:46; James 2:1–6; Luke 6:32–36"> Then He also said to him who invited Him, “When you give a dinner or a supper, do not call your friends, nor your brothers, nor your relatives, nor rich neighbors, lest they also invite you back, and you be repaid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 14:21; Isaiah 58:10; Proverbs 14:31"> But whenever you give a banquet, invite the poor, the crippled, the lame, the blind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Acts 24:15; John 5:29"> and you will be blessed, because they do not have anything to repay you; for it will be repaid to you in the resurrection of the just.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Great Supper

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; Luke 22:30; Luke 13:29"> Now when one of those who sat at the table with Him heard these things, he said to Him, “Blessed is he who shall eat breakfast in the kingdom of God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 22:2–14; Revelation 22:17; Isaiah 55:1–7"> Then He said to him, “A certain man gave a great banquet, and he invited many,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:27–29; Matthew 10:1–4; Matthew 22:3–4"> and he sent his servant at supper time to say to those who were invited, ‘Come, for all things are now ready.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 17:26–31; Luke 18:24"> But they all with one accord began to excuse themselves. The first said to him, ‘I bought a field, and I must go and see it. I ask you, have me excused.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And another said, ‘I bought five yoke of oxen, and I am going to test them. I ask you, have me excused.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:5; 1 Corinthians 7:33; Luke 14:26–28"> Still another said, ‘I have married a wife, therefore I cannot come.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 14:13; Matthew 11:5; Luke 14:24"> So that servant came and reported these things to his master. Then the master of the house, being angry, said to his servant, ‘Go out quickly into the streets and lanes of the city, and bring in here the poor and the maimed and the lame and the blind.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 14:2; Ephesians 3:8; 1 Timothy 2:5–6"> And the servant said, ‘Master, it is done as you commanded, and still there is room.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:9–10; Colossians 1:28; Acts 13:47–48"> Then the master said to the servant, ‘Go out into the roads and hedges, and compel them to come in, so that my house may be filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Acts 13:46; John 8:24"> For I say to you that none of those men who were invited shall taste of my supper.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Counting the Cost

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:24–27; Luke 12:1"> Now great crowds were traveling with Him. And turning, He said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; John 12:25; Philippians 3:8"> “If anyone comes to Me and does not hate his father and mother, wife and children, brothers and sisters, yes, and his own life also, he cannot be My disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 9:23–25; Mark 8:34–37"> And whoever does not bear his cross and come after Me cannot be My disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 24:27; Matthew 10:22; Joshua 24:19–24"> For which of you, intending to build a tower, does not first sit down and count up the cost, whether he has the resources to complete it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Lest perhaps after he has laid a foundation, and is not able to finish, all those who see it begin to mock him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 6:11; 2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:11–14"> saying, ‘This man began to build and was not able to finish.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 25:8; 2 Kings 18:20–22"> Or what king, going to engage another king in battle, does not sit down first and consider whether he is able with ten thousand to meet him who comes against him with twenty thousand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 12:20; James 4:6–10; Luke 12:58"> Otherwise, while the other is still far away, he sends a delegation and asks the terms for peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; 1 John 2:15–16; Luke 5:28"> So likewise, every one of you who does not renounce all his possessions cannot be My disciple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:13; Colossians 4:6; Mark 9:49–50"> “Salt is good; but if the salt becomes tasteless, with what shall it be seasoned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Luke 9:44; Revelation 2:11"> It is neither fit for the land nor for the dunghill, but men cast it out. He who has ears to hear, let him hear!”</span>
</div>
</div>
