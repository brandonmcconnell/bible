## Jesus' Authority Questioned

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:23–27; Luke 19:47–48; Acts 6:12"> Now it came to pass on one of those days, as He taught the people in the temple and preached the gospel, that the priests and the scribes, together with the elders, confronted Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; Mark 11:28–33; John 2:18"> and they spoke to Him, saying, “Tell us, by what authority are You doing these things? Or who is he who gave You this authority?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 15:2–3; Luke 22:68; Colossians 4:6"> But He answered and said to them, “I also will ask you one thing, and so tell Me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:18; Matthew 11:7–19; Matthew 17:11–12"> The baptism of John—was it from heaven or from men?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:34; John 1:15–18; John 1:30"> And they debated among themselves, saying, “If we say, ‘From heaven,’ He will say, ‘Why then did you not believe him?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 11:9; John 10:41"> But if we say, ‘From men,’ all the people will stone us, for they are convinced that John was a prophet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; 2 Peter 3:3; Isaiah 41:28"> So they answered that they did not know where it was from.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:4–5; Matthew 21:27; Mark 11:33"> And Jesus said to them, “Neither will I tell you by what authority I do these things.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Wicked Tenants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 21:33–46; Mark 12:1–12; Isaiah 5:1–7"> Then He began to tell the people this parable: “A man planted a vineyard, leased it to farmers, and went on a journey for a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Nehemiah 9:26; Matthew 21:34–36"> Now at vintage-time he sent a servant to the farmers, that they might give him some of the fruit of the vineyard. But the farmers flogged him and sent him away empty-handed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 10:1; Hebrews 11:36–37; 1 Thessalonians 2:2"> And again he sent another servant; and they flogged him also, treated him shamefully, and sent him away empty-handed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And again he sent a third; and they wounded him also and threw him out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 3:17; John 3:16–17; Jeremiah 36:7"> “Then the owner of the vineyard said, ‘What shall I do? I will send my beloved son. Perhaps when they see him they will respect him.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Hebrews 1:2; Acts 2:23"> But when the farmers saw him, they reasoned among themselves, saying, ‘This is the heir. Come, let us kill him, so that the inheritance may be ours.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:12; Mark 12:6–9; Matthew 21:37–40"> And casting him out of the vineyard, they killed him. What then will the owner of the vineyard do to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:27; Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:41"> He will come and destroy those farmers and give the vineyard to others.”<br />And when they heard it they said, “May it never be!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 118:22; Isaiah 28:16; Acts 4:11"> Then He looked at them and said, “What then is this which is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The stone which the builders rejected</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Has become the chief cornerstone’?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; Zechariah 12:3; Daniel 2:44–45"> Everyone who falls on that stone will be broken to pieces; but on whomever it falls, it will grind him to powder.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 19:47–48; Luke 20:14; Matthew 21:45–46"> And the chief priests and the scribes sought to lay hands on Him that very hour, but they were afraid—for they knew that He had spoken this parable against them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Question of Paying Taxes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 11:54; Matthew 22:15–22; Matthew 27:2"> And having watched Him closely, they sent spies who pretended to be righteous, that they might seize on His words, in order to deliver Him to the rule and the authority of the governor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4–5; Matthew 26:49–50; Matthew 22:16"> Then they asked Him, saying, “Teacher, we know that You say and teach rightly, and You do not show favoritism, but teach the way of God in truth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:37; Mark 12:14–17; Acts 5:37"> Is it lawful for us to pay taxes to Caesar or not?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:18; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Luke 5:22"> But perceiving their craftiness, He said to them, “Why do you test Me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 18:28; Acts 11:28; Acts 26:32"> Show Me a denarius. Whose image and inscription does it have?”<br />They answered and said, “Caesar's.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 13:6–7; Mark 12:17; 1 Peter 2:13–17"> And He said to them, “Render therefore to Caesar the things that are Caesar's, and to God the things that are God's.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 20:20; Proverbs 26:4–5; Titus 1:10"> But they could not catch Him in His words in front of the people. And marveling at His answer, they kept silent.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Question About the Resurrection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 4:1–2; 1 Corinthians 15:12; Mark 12:18–27"> Then some of the Sadducees, those who deny that there is a resurrection, coming to Him and asked Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Genesis 38:8; Genesis 38:11"> saying: “Teacher, Moses wrote to us that if a man's brother dies, having a wife, and this man dies childless, that his brother should take his wife and raise up offspring for his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20; Jeremiah 22:30"> Now there were seven brothers. And the first, having taken a wife, died childless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the second took her as wife, and this man died childless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Then the third took her, and in like manner the seven also; and they left no children, and they died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:4; Ecclesiastes 9:5; Hebrews 9:27"> Last of all the woman died also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 22:24–28; Mark 12:19–23"> Therefore, in the resurrection, whose wife will she be? For all seven had her as wife.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 16:8; Luke 17:27; Hebrews 13:4"> And Jesus answered and said to them, “The sons of this age marry and are given in marriage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; John 5:29; Daniel 12:2–3"> But those who have been counted worthy to attain that age, and the resurrection from the dead, neither marry nor are given in marriage;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Philippians 3:21; Matthew 22:30"> nor can they die anymore, for they are equal to the angels and are sons of God, being sons of the resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Mark 12:26–27; Acts 7:30–32"> Now even Moses revealed that the dead are raised, in the burning bush passage, when he called the Lord ‘the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:10–11"> For He is not the God of the dead but of the living, for all are alive to Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 23:9; Mark 12:28–34; Matthew 22:34–40"> Then some of the scribes answered and said, “Teacher, You have spoken well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Mark 12:34; Proverbs 26:5"> But after that they dared not question Him anymore.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Question About the Son of David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 1:1; Matthew 22:41–45"> And He said to them, “How is it that they say the Christ is the Son of David?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Acts 2:34–35; Hebrews 1:13"> Even David himself said in the Book of Psalms,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The Lord said to my Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sit at My right hand,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 19:27; Psalms 110:1; Revelation 19:14–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">till I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet.” ’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Luke 1:31–35; Matthew 1:23"> Therefore David calls Him ‘Lord’; how is He then his Son?”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Beware of the Scribes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 15:10; Mark 12:38–39; Mark 8:34"> And as all the people listened, He said to His disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 11:43; Mark 12:38–39; Luke 14:7"> “Beware of the scribes, who desire to walk about in long robes, and love greetings in the market places, and the first seats in the synagogues, and the places of honor at dinners,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 12:40; James 3:1; Titus 1:16"> who devour widows' houses, and for a show make long prayers. These will receive greater condemnation.”</span>
</div>
