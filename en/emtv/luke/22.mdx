## The Institution of the Lord's Supper

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">

## The Disciples Argue About Greatness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 9:46; Mark 9:34; Mark 10:37–41"> And there arose also a dispute among them, as to which one of them was the greatest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 20:25–28; Mark 10:41–45"> But He said to them, “The kings of the Gentiles lord it over them, and those who exercise authority over them are called ‘benefactors.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Luke 9:48; Matthew 18:3–5"> But it is not this way with you; let the greatest among you, let him be as the youngest, and he who leads as he who serves.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:18; Hebrews 4:15; Acts 1:25"> “Now you are those who have remained with Me in My trials.</span>

<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–10; John 21:15–17; John 17:9–11"> But I have prayed for you, that your faith should not fail; and when you have turned back, strengthen your brothers.”</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 14:31; Matthew 26:33–35; Mark 14:29"> But he said to Him, “Lord, I am ready to go with You, both to prison and to death.”</span>
