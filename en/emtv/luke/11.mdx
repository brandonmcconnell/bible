## Keep Asking, Seeking, Knocking

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 11:24; John 16:23–24; Matthew 21:22"> “So I say to you, ask, and it shall be given to you; seek, and you shall find; knock, and it shall be opened to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:3; Lamentations 3:54–58; Psalms 31:22"> For everyone who asks receives, and he who seeks finds, and to him who knocks it will be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 7:9; Isaiah 49:15"> And which father among you, if his son asks for bread, will he give him a stone? Or if he asks for a fish, will he give him a serpent instead of a fish?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 9:10; Luke 10:19; Ezekiel 2:6"> Or if he asks for an egg, will he give him a scorpion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 7:11; Isaiah 44:3–4; Romans 8:32"> If you then, being evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, by how much more will your heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to those who ask Him!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus and Beelzebub

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 12:22–24; Matthew 9:32–34; Mark 7:32–37"> And He was casting out a demon, and it was mute. So it was, when the demon came out, that the mute man spoke; and the crowds marveled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 9:34; Matthew 12:24–30; John 8:48"> But some of them said, “He casts out demons by Beelzebub, the ruler of the demons.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 6:30; 1 Corinthians 1:22; Mark 8:11–12"> But others, testing Him, were seeking from Him a sign from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 3:23–27; Matthew 12:25–29; Isaiah 19:2–3"> But He, knowing their thoughts, said to them: “Every kingdom divided against itself is made desolate, and a house divided against a house falls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 12:26; Matthew 12:31–34; Luke 11:15"> And if Satan also is divided against himself, how will his kingdom stand? Because you say that I cast out demons by Beelzebub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 9:49; Job 15:6; Romans 3:19"> And if I cast out demons by Beelzebub, by whom do your sons cast them out? Therefore they will be your judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 8:19; Matthew 12:28; Luke 10:9"> But if I cast out demons by the finger of God, then the kingdom of God has come upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 3:27; Matthew 12:29"> When a strong man, fully armed, guards his own palace, his possessions are in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–25; Isaiah 53:12; 1 John 3:8"> But when someone stronger than he comes upon him, he overcomes him, and he takes away his full armor in which he had trusted, and distributes his spoils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:30; Luke 9:50; Mark 9:40"> He who is not with Me is against Me, and he who does not gather with Me scatters.</span>
</div>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–12; 2 Peter 2:10–19; Jude 1:8–13"> And coming, he finds it swept and adorned.</span>

</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 11:16; Mark 8:11–12; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> Now as the crowds gathered even more, He began to say, “This generation is evil. It seeks a sign, and no sign will be given to it except the sign of Jonah the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 12:40–42; Jonah 1:17; Jonah 3:2–10"> For just as Jonah became a sign to the Ninevites, so also the Son of Man will be to this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:1; 1 Kings 10:1–13; Romans 2:27"> The queen of the South shall rise up in the judgment with the men of this generation and shall condemn them, because she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon; and indeed one greater than Solomon is here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 7:26; Luke 11:31; Jonah 3:5–4"> The men of Nineveh shall stand up in the judgment with this generation and shall condemn it, because they repented at the preaching of Jonah; and indeed one greater than Jonah is here.</span>
</div>

## The Lamp of the Body

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:16–17; Mark 4:21–22; Matthew 5:15–16"> “No one, having lit a lamp, puts it in a secret place or under a basket, but on a lampstand, so that those who come in may see the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 6:22–23; Acts 26:18; Jeremiah 5:21"> The lamp of the body is the eye. Therefore, when your eye is clear, your whole body also is full of light. But when your eye is evil, your body also is dark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 16:25; 2 Peter 1:9; James 3:13–17"> Therefore take heed that the light which is in you is not darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 20:27; Proverbs 6:23; Proverbs 4:18–19"> Therefore if your whole body is full of light, having no part dark, the whole body will be full of light, as when the bright shining of a lamp gives light to you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Pronounces More Woes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 7:36; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23; Luke 14:1"> And as He spoke, a certain Pharisee asked that He would eat with him. And having entered, He reclined to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 15:2–3; Mark 7:2–5; John 3:25"> And when the Pharisee saw it, he marveled that He had not first washed before the meal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Proverbs 30:12; Genesis 6:5"> But the Lord said to him, “Now you Pharisees clean the outside of the cup and of the dish, but the inside of you is full of greed and evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 12:20; 1 Corinthians 15:36; Matthew 23:26"> Fools! Did not He who made the outside also make the inside?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 12:33; James 1:27; Titus 1:15"> Nevertheless give what is inside as alms; and see, all things are clean to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 23:23; Luke 18:12; Proverbs 21:3"> “But woe to you Pharisees! Because you pay a tithe of mint and rue and every vegetable, and you pass over justice and the love of God. These things you ought to have done, and not have neglected the others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 20:46; Mark 12:38–39; Luke 14:7–11"> Woe to you Pharisees! Because you love the first seat in the synagogues and greetings in the marketplaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 23:27–28; Psalms 5:9; Acts 23:3"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because you are like unmarked graves, and the men walking over them do not know it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 9:40; Matthew 22:35; Jeremiah 6:10"> Then one of the lawyers answered and said to Him, “Teacher, by saying these things You insult us also.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Isaiah 10:1; Matthew 23:2–4"> And He said, “Woe to you lawyers as well! Because you load men with burdens hard to bear, and you yourselves do not touch the burdens with one of your fingers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15; Acts 7:51; Matthew 23:29–33"> Woe to you! Because you build the tombs of the prophets, and your fathers killed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="James 5:10; Matthew 23:31; 2 Chronicles 36:16"> Consequently, you bear witness to and approve of the deeds of your fathers; because they indeed killed them, and you build their tombs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Colossians 2:3; Ephesians 4:11"> For this reason also the wisdom of God said, ‘I will send to them prophets and apostles, and some of them they will kill and persecute,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 9:5–6; Revelation 18:20–24; 2 Kings 24:4"> so that the blood of all the prophets which was shed from the foundation of the world may be required of this generation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20–22; Matthew 23:35; Hebrews 11:4"> from the blood of Abel to the blood of Zechariah who perished between the altar and the temple. Yes, I say to you, it shall be required of this generation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Malachi 2:7; Luke 11:45–46"> “Woe to you lawyers! Because you have taken away the key of knowledge. You yourselves did not enter in, and those who were entering in you hindered.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:5; Jeremiah 20:10; Luke 20:20"> And as He said these things to them, the scribes and the Pharisees began to be dreadfully hostile, and to cross-examine Him about many things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Mark 12:13; Matthew 22:15; Matthew 22:35"> lying in wait for Him, and seeking to catch Him in something He might say, so that they might accuse Him.</span>
</div>
</div>
