## The Birth of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 24:14; Acts 25:21"> And it came to pass in those days that a decree went out from Caesar Augustus that a census be taken of all the empire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 4:24; Acts 26:30"> This census was the first one while Quirinius was governing Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And all were traveling to be registered, each to his own city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:42; 1 Samuel 16:1"> Joseph also went up from Galilee, out of the city of Nazareth, into Judea, to the city of David, which is called Bethlehem, because he was of the house and family of David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:22–27; Matthew 1:18–19"> to be registered with Mary, his betrothed wife, who was pregnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 33:11; Revelation 12:1–5; Micah 5:2"> So it was, that while they were there, the days were completed for her to bear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 4:4; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Isaiah 53:2–3"> And she bore her firstborn Son, and wrapped Him in swaddling cloths, and laid Him in a manger, because there was no place for them in the inn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 31:39–40; Psalms 78:70–71; 1 Samuel 17:34–35"> And shepherds were in the same country, living in the fields and keeping watch over their flock by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; 2 Corinthians 3:18; 1 Kings 8:11"> And behold, an angel of the Lord stood before them, and the glory of the Lord shone around them, and they were greatly afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 52:7"> Then the angel said to them, “Do not fear, for behold, I bring you good tidings of great joy which will be to all people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Matthew 1:21; Acts 2:36"> For unto you is born this day in the city of David a Savior, who is Christ the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 53:1–2; Isaiah 7:11"> And this will be the sign to you: You will find a baby wrapped in swaddling cloths, lying in a manger.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 103:20–21; Revelation 5:11; Psalms 148:2"> And suddenly there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly host praising God and saying:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 5:1; Luke 19:38; Colossians 1:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Glory to God in the highest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And on earth peace, goodwill toward men!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 2:1–2; Matthew 2:9–11"> So it was, when the angels had departed from them into heaven, that the shepherds said to one another, “Let us go then to Bethlehem and see this thing that has come to pass, which the Lord has made known to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:32; Luke 2:12; Luke 2:7"> And they came with haste and found Mary and Joseph, and the baby lying in a manger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 66:16; Luke 8:39; Psalms 71:17–18"> And when they had seen Him, they made widely known the saying which was told them concerning this Child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 2:47; Luke 4:36; Luke 1:65–66"> And all those who heard it marveled about the things spoken by the shepherds to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 2:51; Luke 1:66; Proverbs 4:4"> But Mary treasured all these things and pondered them in her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 106:48; Isaiah 29:19; Acts 11:18"> Then the shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all the things that they had heard and seen, as it was spoken to them.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:31; Genesis 17:12; Galatians 4:4–5"> And when eight days were completed, so they could circumcise Him, His name was called Jesus, the name given by the angel before He was conceived in the womb.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Presented in the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:1–8"> Now when the days of their purification according to the law of Moses were completed, they brought Him to Jerusalem to present Him to the Lord</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 22:29; Numbers 3:13"> (just as it is written in the law of the Lord, “Every male who opens the womb shall be called holy to the Lord”),</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 12:6–8; Leviticus 12:2; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> and to offer a sacrifice according to what had been said in the law of the Lord, “A pair of doves or two young pigeons.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Song and Prophecy of Simeon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Luke 2:38; Luke 1:6"> And behold, there was a man in Jerusalem whose name was Simeon, and this man was just and devout, waiting for the Consolation of Israel, and the Holy Spirit was upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:51; Hebrews 11:5; Luke 9:27"> And it had been revealed to him by the Holy Spirit that he would not see death before he should see the Lord's Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 4:1; Luke 2:41; Luke 2:51"> So he came by the Spirit into the temple. And when the parents brought in the Child Jesus, that they might do for Him according to the custom of the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 1:64; Luke 2:20; Luke 2:13–14"> he took Him up in his arms and blessed God and said:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Genesis 15:15; Philippians 1:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Lord, now You are releasing Your servant in peace,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">According to Your word;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 49:6; Luke 2:10–11; Luke 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my eyes have seen Your salvation</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 62:1–2; Isaiah 42:1–4; Psalms 98:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Which You have prepared before the face of all peoples,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–3; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 26:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">A light for a revelation to the Gentiles,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And a glory to Your people Israel.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 1:65–66; Isaiah 8:18; Luke 2:48"> And Joseph and His mother were marveling at the things being spoken about Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 8:14–15"> Then Simeon blessed them, and said to Mary His mother, “Behold, this Child is destined for the fall and rise of many in Israel, and for a sign which will be spoken against</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Matthew 12:24–35"> (yes, a sword will pierce through your own soul also), so that the thoughts of many hearts may be revealed.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Testimony of Hannah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Genesis 30:13; Acts 21:9"> Now there was one, Hannah, a prophetess, a daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Asher. She was very old, and had lived with a husband seven years from her virginity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:5; Psalms 23:6; Acts 14:23"> and she was a widow of about eighty-four years, who did not depart from the temple, but served God with fastings and prayers night and day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 2:25; 2 Corinthians 9:15; Mark 15:43"> And coming in that instant she was giving thanks to the Lord, and was speaking about Him to all those who waited for redemption in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 2:4; Galatians 4:4–5; Deuteronomy 12:32"> So when they had performed all things according to the law of the Lord, they returned to Galilee, to their own city, Nazareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 2:52; Luke 1:80; Isaiah 11:1–5"> And the Child was growing and becoming strong in spirit, being filled with wisdom; and the grace of God was upon Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Young Jesus in the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Numbers 28:16; Deuteronomy 12:18"> And His parents traveled to Jerusalem every year to the Feast of the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And when He was twelve years old, when they went up to Jerusalem according to the custom of the feast,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:21–23; Exodus 12:15; 2 Chronicles 25:17"> and when they completed the days, while they were returning, the Child Jesus remained in Jerusalem; and Joseph and His mother did not know it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 122:1–4; Isaiah 2:3; Psalms 42:4"> But supposing Him to be in the caravan, they went a day's journey, and sought Him among their relatives and among their acquaintances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And when they did not find Him, they returned to Jerusalem, seeking Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 5:17; Isaiah 49:1–2; Isaiah 50:4"> Now it came to pass that after three days they found Him in the temple, sitting in the midst of the teachers, both hearing them and questioning them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Luke 4:32; Psalms 119:99"> And those who heard Him were astonished at His understanding and His answers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 2:49; Matthew 12:46; Luke 3:23"> And when they saw Him, they were amazed; and His mother said to Him, “Child, why have You treated us this way? Look, Your father and I were seeking You anxiously.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 6:38; John 8:29; Malachi 3:1"> And He said to them, “Why is it that you were seeking Me? Did you not know that I must be about My Father's business?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Luke 18:34; Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32"> But they did not understand the statement which He spoke to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Daniel 7:28; Genesis 37:11"> Then He went down with them and came to Nazareth, and was subject to them. And His mother kept all these things in her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 2:40; Proverbs 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 2:26"> And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men.</span>
</div>
</div>
