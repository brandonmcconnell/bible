## The Parable of the Persistent Widow

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 4:2; Romans 12:12; 1 Thessalonians 5:17"> Then He spoke a parable to them, that one must always pray and not lose heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; Psalms 8:1–4; Luke 18:4"> saying: “A certain judge was in a certain city, who did not fear God nor have regard for men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Jeremiah 5:28; Deuteronomy 27:19"> Now there was a widow in that city; and she kept coming to him, saying, ‘Give justice to me against my adversary.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12–13; Luke 16:3; Luke 12:17"> And he did not want to for a time; but afterward he said within himself, ‘although I do not fear God, nor have regard for man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 11:8; Luke 18:39; Judges 16:16"> yet because this widow bothers me I will give justice to her, lest she wear me out by forever coming to me.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 7:13"> Then the Lord said, “Hear what the unjust judge said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35–37; 2 Peter 3:9; Luke 11:13"> And God, shall He not execute justice for His own elect, who cry out day and night to Him, and yet He is patient with them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:23–26; 2 Peter 3:8–9; Matthew 24:9–13"> I tell you that He will execute justice for them speedily. Nevertheless, when the Son of Man comes, will He find faith on the earth?”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Pharisee and the Tax Collector

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Proverbs 30:12; Isaiah 65:5"> Also He spoke this parable to some who trusted in themselves that they were righteous, and despised the others:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 21:31–32; 1 Kings 8:30; 2 Kings 20:5"> “Two men went up to the temple to pray, the one a Pharisee and the other a tax collector.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 6:5; Isaiah 65:5; Revelation 3:17"> The Pharisee stood and prayed thus with himself, ‘God, I thank You that I am not like other men—swindlers, unjust, adulterers, or even like this tax collector.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Matthew 23:23–24; Matthew 9:14"> I fast twice a week, I tithe on all things, as many as I acquire.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; 1 John 1:8–10; 1 Timothy 1:15"> And the tax collector, standing far away, would not so much as lift his eyes towards heaven, but beat on his chest, saying, ‘God, be merciful to me a sinner!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 14:11; Luke 16:15; James 4:10"> I say to you, this man went down to his house justified rather than the other; for everyone who exalts himself shall be humbled, and he who humbles himself shall be exalted.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Blesses Little Children

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 10:13–16; Matthew 19:13–15; 1 Samuel 1:24"> And they were bringing infants to Him, that He might touch them; but when the disciples saw it, they rebuked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 18:3–4; 1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 7:14"> But Jesus, summoning them, said, “Allow the little children to come to Me, and do not forbid them; for of such is the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 18:3; Mark 10:15; Psalms 131:1–2"> Assuredly I say to you, whoever does not receive the kingdom of God as a little child will by no means enter into it.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Rich Young Ruler

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 10:17–31; Acts 16:30; Matthew 19:16–30"> Now a certain ruler asked Him, saying, “Good Teacher, what shall I do to inherit eternal life?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 15:14–16; James 1:17; Hebrews 7:26"> So Jesus said to him, “Why do you call Me good? No one is good except One, that is, God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Deuteronomy 5:16–21; Romans 13:9"> You know the commandments: ‘Do not commit adultery,’ ‘Do not murder,’ ‘Do not steal,’ ‘Do not bear false witness,’ ‘Honor your father and your mother.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 3:6; Romans 10:2–3; Mark 10:20–21"> And he said, “All these I have kept from my youth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 12:33; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; Matthew 19:21"> So when Jesus heard these things, He said to him, “Still one thing you lack: sell all that you have and give to the poor, and you shall have treasure in heaven; and come, follow Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; 1 John 2:15; Job 31:24–25"> But when he heard this, he became very sad, for he was extremely rich.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Snare of Riches

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; James 2:5–7; Mark 10:23–27"> And when Jesus saw that he became very sad, He said, “How hard it is for those who have riches to enter the kingdom of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 23:24"> For it is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter the kingdom of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 13:23"> And those who heard it said, “Who then is able to be saved?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Matthew 19:26; Luke 1:37"> But He said, “These things which are impossible with men are possible with God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 10:28; Philippians 3:7; Matthew 19:27"> Then Peter said, “See, we have left all and followed You.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 10:29–31; Matthew 10:37–39; Luke 14:26–28"> So He said to them, “Assuredly I say to you, there is no one who has left house or parents or brothers or wife or children, for the sake of the kingdom of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5–6; Job 42:10; Revelation 2:17"> who shall not receive many times more in this present time, and in the age to come eternal life.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus a Third Time Foretells of His Death and Resurrection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 20:17–19; Isaiah 53:1–12; Psalms 22:1–31"> Then taking the twelve aside, He said to them, “Behold, we are going up to Jerusalem, and all things that are written by the prophets concerning the Son of Man will be accomplished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 23:11; Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3"> For He will be betrayed to the Gentiles, and will be mocked and insulted and spit upon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:3–4; Luke 24:7; Matthew 27:63"> and having scourged Him, they will kill Him. And on the third day He will rise again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32; Luke 24:25"> But they understood none of these things; this saying was hidden from them, and they did not know what was being said.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Heals a Blind Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:29–34; Mark 10:46–52; Luke 16:20–21"> Now it came to pass, as He was coming near Jericho, that a certain blind man sat alongside the road begging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 21:10–11; Luke 15:26"> And hearing a crowd traveling through the city, he asked what this was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; John 19:19; John 12:35–36"> And they reported to him that Jesus the Nazarene was passing by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Luke 18:39; Matthew 15:22"> And he shouted, saying, “Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 32:26–28; Psalms 141:1; Luke 18:15"> Then those who went before warned him that he should be quiet; but he cried out all the more, “Son of David, have pity on me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 20:31–34; Mark 10:48–52"> And having stopped, Jesus commanded him to be brought to Him. And when he drew near, He asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Kings 3:5–15; Matthew 20:21–22; Philippians 4:6"> saying, “What do you desire that I should do for you?” And he said, “Lord, that I may receive my sight.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 8:3; Luke 8:48; Luke 17:19"> Then Jesus said to him, “Receive your sight; your faith has made you well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 146:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:10–12; Isaiah 43:21"> And immediately he received his sight, and was following Him, glorifying God. And all the people, when they saw this, gave praise to God.</span>
</div>
