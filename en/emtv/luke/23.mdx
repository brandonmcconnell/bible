## Jesus Handed Over to Pontius Pilate

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 27:1–2; Matthew 27:11–14; Luke 22:66"> Then the whole multitude of them arose and led Him to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 19:12; Luke 23:14"> And they began to accuse Him, saying, “We found this Man perverting the nation, and forbidding to pay taxes to Caesar, saying that He Himself is Christ, a King.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 1:49; 1 Timothy 6:13; Luke 22:70"> Then Pilate questioned Him, saying, “Are You the King of the Jews?” He answered him and said, “You are saying what is so.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:22"> But Pilate said to the chief priests and the crowds, “I find no guilt in this Man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 1:14; John 1:43; John 2:11"> But they insisted, saying, “He stirs up the people, teaching throughout all Judea, beginning from Galilee to here.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus and Herod

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 5:37; Luke 13:1"> When Pilate heard of Galilee, he asked if the Man were a Galilean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Luke 13:31; Matthew 14:1"> And when he found out that He belonged to Herod's jurisdiction, he sent Him to Herod, who was also in Jerusalem in those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:7–9; Matthew 14:1; Mark 6:14"> Now when Herod saw Jesus, he was exceedingly glad; for he had desired for a long time to see Him, because he had heard many things about Him, and he was hoping to see some miracle done by Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 27:14; Matthew 7:6; 1 Peter 2:23"> Then he questioned Him with many words, but He answered him nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 11:53; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:5"> And the chief priests and scribes stood, vehemently accusing Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Matthew 27:27–30; Psalms 69:19–20"> Then Herod, with his men of war, having treated Him with contempt, and having mocked Him, arrayed Him in a gorgeous robe, and sent Him back to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 4:27; Revelation 17:13–14; Psalms 83:4–6"> That very day Pilate and Herod became friends with each other, for previously they had been at enmity with each other.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus or Barabbas?

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:14; Matthew 27:21–23; John 18:38"> And Pilate, having called together the chief priests, the rulers, and the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:4; Hebrews 7:26; Luke 23:1–2"> said to them, “You have brought this Man to me, on the charge of misleading the people. And indeed, when I examined Him in your presence, I found no guilt in this Man in the matters which you bring as charges against Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and neither did Herod, for I sent you back to him; and indeed nothing worthy of death has been done by Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Luke 23:22; Mark 15:15"> Therefore when I have flogged Him, I will release Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> (For it was necessary for him to release one to them at the feast.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:36; Acts 3:14; Acts 22:22"> But they all cried out together, saying, “Away with this Man, and release to us Barabbas”—</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Luke 23:5; Luke 23:2"> who because of a certain insurrection having occurred in the city had been thrown into prison, and for murder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 15:15; Matthew 14:8–9; Matthew 27:19"> Pilate, therefore, wishing to release Jesus, again called out to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:22–25; Mark 15:13; John 19:15"> But they cried out, saying, “Crucify, crucify Him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 23:14; Luke 23:20; 1 Peter 3:18"> Then he said to them the third time, “Why, what evil has He done? I have found no guilt deserving death in Him. Therefore when I have flogged Him, I will release Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 11:8; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 22:12–13"> But they were insistent, demanding with loud voices that He be crucified. And their voices and those of the chief priests prevailed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 17:15"> So Pilate decided that their request should be carried out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Mark 15:6–7; Mark 15:15"> And he released whom they were requesting, who because of insurrection and murder had been thrown into prison; but he delivered Jesus to their will.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Crucified

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:32–44; Mark 15:21–32; Acts 6:9"> And as they led Him away, they laid hold of a certain man, Simon a Cyrenian, as he was coming from the country, and on him they laid the cross, that he might carry it behind Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 8:52; Luke 8:2; Mark 15:40"> And a great multitude of the people followed Him, and women who also were mourning and lamenting Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:4; Song of Solomon 5:8; Song of Solomon 1:5"> But Jesus, turning to them, said, “Daughters of Jerusalem, do not weep for Me, but weep for yourselves and for your children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:19; Luke 21:23–24; Mark 13:17–19"> For behold, the days are coming in which they will say, ‘Blessed are the barren, and the wombs which did not give birth, and breasts which did not nurse!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 6:16; Hosea 10:8; Isaiah 2:19"> Then they will begin ‘to say to the mountains, “Fall on us!” and to the hills, “Cover us!” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; John 15:6; Proverbs 11:31"> For if they do these things in the green tree, what will happen in the dry?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:27; Isaiah 53:12; Matthew 27:38"> And they also were leading two others with Him, evildoers, to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 15:22–23; John 19:17–18; John 18:32"> And when they came to the place which is called Calvary, there they crucified Him and the evildoers, one on the right, and one on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Acts 7:60; 1 Peter 3:9"> Then Jesus said, “Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do.” And dividing His garments, they cast lots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 22:17; Isaiah 42:1; 1 Peter 2:4"> And the people stood, watching. And the rulers with them also were sneering, saying, “He saved others; let Him save Himself if He is the Christ, the elect of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Matthew 27:48; Matthew 27:34"> The soldiers also mocked Him, coming and offering Him sour wine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and saying, “If You are the King of the Jews, save Yourself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:26; Matthew 27:37; John 19:19–22"> And an inscription also was written over Him, in Greek, Latin, and Hebrew letters:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">THIS IS THE KING OF THE JEWS.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:35"> Then one of the evildoers who were hanged blasphemed Him, saying, “If You are the Christ, save Yourself and us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Luke 12:5; Revelation 15:4"> But the other, answering, rebuked him, saying, “Do you not even fear God, seeing you are under the same condemnation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–41; Ezra 9:13; 1 Peter 1:19"> And we indeed justly, for we are receiving the due reward of our deeds; but this Man did nothing wrong.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Romans 10:9–14; Acts 16:31; 1 John 5:11–13"> Then he said to Jesus, “Lord, remember me when You come in Your kingdom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Psalms 50:15; 1 Timothy 1:15–16"> And Jesus said to him, “Assuredly I say to you, today you shall be with Me in Paradise.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Mark 15:33–34; Amos 8:9"> Now it was about the sixth hour, and darkness came upon the whole land until the ninth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 9:3–8"> Then the sun was darkened, and the veil of the temple was torn in two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 31:5; Acts 7:59; John 19:30"> And Jesus, calling out with a loud voice, said, “Father, ‘into Your hands I will commit My spirit.’ ” And after He said these things, He breathed His last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:39; Luke 23:41"> And when the centurion saw what had happened, he glorified God, saying, “Certainly this was a righteous Man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 18:13; Acts 2:37; Jeremiah 31:19"> And all the crowds which came together for this spectacle, when they saw what had happened, beat their own chests and returned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 8:2; Psalms 38:11; Luke 23:55"> But all His acquaintances, and the women who followed Him from Galilee, stood at a distance, watching these things.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Burial of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Acts 11:24; Acts 10:22"> And behold, there was a man named Joseph, a council member, a good and righteous man</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Luke 2:38; Isaiah 8:12"> (this man had not agreed to their decision and deed), from Arimathea, a city of the Jews, who himself was also waiting for the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 19:38–42"> This man went to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 53:9; Mark 15:46"> And having taken it down, he wrapped it in a linen cloth, and put it in a tomb cut out of rock, where no one had ever lain before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:31; Mark 15:42"> That day was the Preparation; the Sabbath was drawing near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:61; Mark 15:47"> And the women who had come with Him from Galilee followed after, and they observed the tomb and how His body was placed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Luke 24:1; Mark 16:1; Deuteronomy 5:14"> Then they returned and prepared spices and perfumes. And they rested on the Sabbath, according to the commandment.</span>
</div>
