## The Resurrection of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 20:1–2; Mark 16:1–8; Matthew 28:1–8"> And on the first day of the week, very early in the morning, they, and certain other women with them, came to the tomb bringing the spices which they had prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:3–4; Matthew 27:60–66; Mark 15:46–47"> But they found the stone having been rolled away from the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:23; John 20:6–7; Matthew 16:5"> And going in they did not find the body of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 16:5; John 20:11–12; Acts 1:10"> And it happened, as they were greatly perplexed about this, that behold, two men stood by them in dazzling apparel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 16:5–6; Matthew 28:3–5; Revelation 1:18"> Then, as they became afraid and bowed their faces to the ground, they said to them, “Why do you seek the living among the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 17:22–23; Luke 9:22; Mark 8:31"> He is not here, but is risen! Remember how He spoke to you when He was still in Galilee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 16:21"> saying, ‘The Son of Man must be delivered into the hands of sinful men, and be crucified, and on the third day rise again.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 12:16; John 14:26; John 2:19–22"> And they remembered His words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 24:22–24; Mark 16:7–8; Matthew 28:7–8"> And returning from the tomb, they reported all these things to the eleven and to all the rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 27:56; John 20:11–18; Luke 8:2–3"> It was Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Mary the mother of James, and the rest with them, who told these things to the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 9:16; Mark 16:11; Genesis 19:14"> And their words seemed like nonsense before them, and they disbelieved them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 20:3–10"> But Peter rose up and ran to the tomb; and stooping down, he saw the linen strips lying by themselves; and he departed, marveling to himself at what had happened.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Road to Emmaus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 24:18; Mark 16:12–13"> Now behold, two of them were traveling that same day to a village called Emmaus, which was about seven miles from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:45; Deuteronomy 6:7; Malachi 3:6"> And they were conversing with one another about all these things which had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:20; John 14:18–19; Luke 24:36"> So it was, as they were conversing and discussing, that Jesus Himself drew near and started to travel with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 20:14; John 21:4; Luke 24:31"> But their eyes were restrained, so that they did not recognize Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:20–22; John 16:6; Ezekiel 9:4–6"> And He said to them, “What are these words which you are exchanging with one another as you walk, and you have a gloomy look?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 19:25"> Then the one whose name was Cleopas answered and said to Him, “Are You the only stranger in Jerusalem, and do not know what has happened in it in these days?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; Acts 7:22; John 4:19"> And He said to them, “What things?” So they said to Him, “The things concerning Jesus the Nazarene, who proved to be a man who was a Prophet mighty in deed and word before God and all the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:13–15; Acts 4:8–10; Luke 23:13"> and how the chief priests and our rulers handed Him over to be condemned to death, and they crucified Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:68; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Luke 2:38"> But we were hoping that it was He who was going to redeem Israel. Indeed, besides all this, today is the third day since these things happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 16:9–10; Matthew 28:7–8; John 20:1–2"> Moreover certain women of our group astonished us, after they arrived early at the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And not finding His body, they came saying that they had also seen a vision of angels who said He was alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 24:12; John 20:1–10"> And certain of those who were with us went to the tomb, and found it just as the women had said; but Him they did not see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 7:18; Mark 9:19; Hebrews 5:11–12"> Then He said to them, “O you foolish, and slow of heart to believe in all that the prophets have spoken!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:11; Isaiah 53:1–12; Zechariah 13:7"> Ought not the Christ to have suffered these things and to enter into His glory?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Luke 24:44; Deuteronomy 18:15"> And beginning at Moses and all the Prophets, He expounded to them in all the Scriptures the things concerning Himself.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Disciples' Eyes Opened

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 6:48; Genesis 19:2; Genesis 32:26"> Then they drew near to the village where they were going, and He made as though He was going farther.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 4:8; Luke 14:23; Genesis 19:3"> But they constrained Him, saying, “Stay with us, for it is toward evening, and the day is far spent.” And He went in to stay with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; Luke 24:35; Matthew 15:36"> Now it came to pass, as He was reclining to eat with them, having taken bread, He blessed and broke it, and He gave it to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 8:59; Luke 4:30"> Then their eyes were opened and they recognized Him; and He vanished from their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 24:45; Jeremiah 23:29; Hebrews 4:12"> And they said to one another, “Was not our heart burning within us while He was speaking to us on the road, and while He was interpreting the Scriptures to us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 16:13; John 20:19–26; Acts 1:14"> So they rose up that very hour and returned to Jerusalem, and they found the eleven and those who were with them gathered together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5; Mark 16:7; Luke 22:54–62"> saying, “The Lord is risen indeed, and has appeared to Simon!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 16:12–13; Acts 2:42; Luke 24:30–31"> And they described the things that had happened on the road, and how He was known to them in the breaking of bread.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Appears to His Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:16; Isaiah 57:18; Mark 16:14"> Now as they said these things, Jesus Himself stood in the midst of them, and said to them, “Peace to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:13; Matthew 14:26–27; Job 4:14–16"> But being alarmed and becoming fearful, they thought they saw a spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:14; Hebrews 4:13; Daniel 4:19"> And He said to them, “Why are you troubled? And why do doubts arise in your hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 20:27; 1 John 1:1; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> Behold My hands and My feet, that it is I Myself. Handle Me and see, for a spirit does not have flesh and bones as you see I have.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> When He had said this, He showed them His hands and His feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 21:5; Psalms 126:1–2; Genesis 45:26–28"> But as they still disbelieved for joy, and marveled, He said to them, “Do you have any food here?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> So they gave Him a piece of a broiled fish and some honeycomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 10:41"> And taking it, He ate it in their presence.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus' Final Commission

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Luke 24:26–27; Zechariah 9:9"> Then He said to them, “These are the words which I spoke to you while I was still with you, that all things must be fulfilled which were written in the Law of Moses and the Prophets and the Psalms concerning Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Acts 16:14; 1 John 5:20; Psalms 119:18"> And He opened their understanding, that they might comprehend the Scriptures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–12; Isaiah 50:6; Luke 24:44"> Then He said to them, “Thus it is written, and thus it was necessary for the Christ to suffer and to rise from the dead the third day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 5:31; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 2:38"> and that repentance and forgiveness of sins should be preached in His name to all nations, beginning at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 2:32; Acts 13:31"> And you are witnesses of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 1:4; Joel 2:28–32"> And behold, I am sending the Promise of My Father upon you; but tarry in the city of Jerusalem until you are endued with power from on high.”</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Mark 11:1"> And He led them out as far as Bethany, and lifting up His hands, He blessed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Acts 1:9; Mark 16:19; 2 Kings 2:11"> And it happened, while He blessed them, that He was parted from them and was carried up into heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 28:9; John 16:22; Psalms 30:11"> And after they had worshiped Him, they returned to Jerusalem with great joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Mark 16:20; Acts 2:46–47; Acts 5:41–42"> and they were continually in the temple praising and blessing God. Amen.</span>
