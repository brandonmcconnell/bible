## Put on the New Self

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 8:6; Matthew 6:33; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> If then you have been raised with Christ, seek those things which are above, where Christ is, sitting at the right hand of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Chronicles 22:19; Colossians 3:1"> Set your mind on things above, not on things on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; Romans 6:2; Colossians 2:20"> For you died, and your life has been hidden with Christ in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 11:25; 1 John 3:2; Galatians 2:20"> Whenever Christ who is our life appears, then you also will appear with Him in glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 8:13; Galatians 5:19–21; Galatians 5:24"> Therefore put to death your members on the earth: sexual immorality, uncleanness, passion, evil desire, and greed, which is idolatry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 5:6; Romans 1:18; 1 Peter 1:14"> Because of which things the wrath of God is coming on the sons of disobedience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:2; 1 Peter 4:3–4; Romans 6:19–20"> in which you yourselves once walked when you lived in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Ephesians 4:22; James 1:20–21"> But now you yourselves are to put off all these: wrath, anger, malice, blasphemy, filthy language out of your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:22; Ephesians 4:25; Leviticus 19:11"> Do not lie to one another, since you have laid aside the old man with his practices,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 4:23–24; Romans 12:2; Ephesians 2:10"> and have put on the new man who is renewed in full knowledge according to the image of Him who created it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:28–29; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Romans 10:12"> where there is neither Greek nor Jew, circumcised nor uncircumcised, barbarian, Scythian, slave nor free, but Christ is all and in all.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Character of the New Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Ephesians 4:2; 1 John 4:19"> Therefore, as the elect of God, holy and beloved, put on hearts of compassion, kindness, humility, meekness, patience;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Mark 11:25; James 2:13"> forbearing one another and forgiving yourselves, if anyone should have a complaint against any; just as Christ forgave you, so you also do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; Ephesians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 13:1–13"> But above all these things put on love, which is the bond of perfection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 4:6–7; John 14:27; Isaiah 26:3"> And let the peace of God rule in your hearts, to which also you were called in one body; and be thankful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Ephesians 5:19; John 15:7"> Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly in all wisdom, teaching and admonishing one another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing with grace in your hearts to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:31; Colossians 3:23; Proverbs 3:6"> And whatsoever you may do in word or deed, do all things in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God the Father through Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Instructions for Christian Households

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:22–9; Titus 2:4–5; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> Wives, subject yourselves to your own husbands, as is fitting in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Genesis 2:23–24; Colossians 3:21"> Husbands, love your wives and do not be bitter toward them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Ephesians 6:1–3; Proverbs 6:20"> Children, obey your parents in all things, for this is well pleasing in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1–4; Ephesians 6:4; Psalms 103:13"> Fathers, do not provoke your children, lest they become discouraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 6:5–7; 1 Timothy 6:1–2; Galatians 1:10"> Bondservants, obey your masters according to the flesh in all things, not with eye-service, as men-pleasers, but in sincerity of heart, fearing God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Ephesians 6:6–7; Romans 14:8"> And whatever you may do, work it from your soul, as to the Lord and not to men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 6:8; Galatians 1:10; Acts 20:32"> knowing that from the Lord you will receive the reward of the inheritance, for you serve the Lord Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 10:34; Romans 2:11; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> But he who does wrong will be repaid for what he has done, and there is no partiality.</span>
</div>
</div>
