## Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; 1 John 3:18; John 8:32"> The Elder, To the elect lady and her children, whom I love in truth, and not only I, but also all those who have come to know the truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 2:14; 1 John 1:8; 1 John 2:17"> on account of the truth which abides in us and will be with us forever:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 4:10; Romans 1:7; 1 Timothy 1:14"> Grace, mercy, and peace will be with us from God the Father and from the Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in truth and love.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Walking in Truth and Love

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="3 John 1:3–4; Ephesians 5:8; Ephesians 5:2"> I rejoiced greatly that I have found some of your children walking in truth, just as we received commandment from the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 3:11; John 13:34–35; John 15:12"> And now I ask you, lady, not as writing a new commandment to you, but one which we have had from the beginning: that we love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 John 5:3; 1 John 2:24; John 15:10"> This is love, that we walk according to His commandments. This is the commandment, that as you have heard from the beginning, that you should walk in it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Beware of Antichrist Deceivers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 2:26; 1 John 4:1–3; 1 John 2:18–22"> Because many deceivers have gone out into the world who do not confess Jesus Christ as coming in the flesh. This is the deceiver and the Antichrist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Revelation 3:11; 1 Corinthians 3:14"> Look to yourselves, lest we should lose those things for which we worked, but that we should receive a full reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 7:16–17; Colossians 3:16; 1 John 2:22–24"> Everyone who transgresses and does not abide in the doctrine of Christ does not have God. He who abides in the doctrine of Christ, this one has both the Father and the Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Titus 3:10; Romans 16:17–18; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> If anyone comes to you and does not bring this doctrine, do not receive him into your house, and do not greet him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:22; Ephesians 5:11; Revelation 18:4"> for he who greets him shares in his evil works.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Greetings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="3 John 1:13–14; John 16:12; 1 Corinthians 16:5–7"> Although I had many things to write to you, I did not wish to do so with paper and ink; but I hope to come to you and speak face to face, in order that our joy may be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:13; 2 John 1:1–13"> The children of your elect sister greet you. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
