## The Anointing at Bethany

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Luke 7:37–39; John 12:1–8"> And when Jesus was in Bethany in the house of Simon the leper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 12:2–3; Luke 7:46; Luke 7:37–38"> a woman came to Him having an alabaster flask of very expensive perfume, and she began to pour it on His head as He reclined to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 12:4–6; Mark 14:4; Exodus 5:17"> But when His disciples saw it, they became indignant, saying, “Why this waste?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 12:5–6; Mark 14:5; Joshua 7:20–21"> For this perfume could have been sold for much, and given to the poor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Titus 3:8; Hebrews 13:21; 1 Timothy 5:10"> But when Jesus became aware of it, He said to them, “Why do you trouble the woman? For she has done a beautiful work for Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Mark 14:7; John 12:8"> For you have the poor with you always, but Me you do not have always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 14:8; John 12:7; Mark 16:1"> For this woman, having put this perfume on My body, has done this for My burial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 14:9; Matthew 24:14; Psalms 112:6"> Assuredly I say to you, wherever this gospel is proclaimed in the whole world, what this woman has done will be spoken of as a memorial to her.”</span>

## Jesus Celebrates the Passover

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:7–13; Mark 14:12–16; Exodus 12:18–20"> Now on the first day of the Feast of the Unleavened Bread, the disciples came to Jesus, saying to Him, “Where do You desire that we shall prepare for You to eat the Passover?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 14:13–16; John 7:6; John 17:1"> And He said, “Go into the city to a certain man, and say to him, ‘The Teacher says, “My time is near; I will keep the Passover at your house with My disciples.” ’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 2:5; John 15:14; Matthew 21:6"> So the disciples did as Jesus had directed them; and they prepared the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 14:17–21; John 13:21; Luke 22:14–16"> And when it was evening, He was reclining to eat with the twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 13:21; Hebrews 4:13; John 6:70–71"> Now while they were eating, He said, “Assuredly I say to you, that one of you will betray Me.”</span>

## Jesus Prays in Gethsemane

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 14:32–42; Matthew 26:42; Luke 22:39–46"> Then Jesus came with them to a place called Gethsemane, and said to the disciples, “Sit here while I go off and pray over there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Matthew 17:1; Mark 5:37"> And taking along Peter and the two sons of Zebedee, He began to be sorrowful and deeply distressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 12:27; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 Peter 2:24"> Then Jesus said to them, “My soul is deeply grieved, to the point of death. Stay here and keep watch with Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 6:38; Philippians 2:8; Luke 22:41–42"> He went a little farther and fell on His face, and prayed, saying, “O My Father, if it is possible, let this cup pass from Me; nevertheless, not as I will, but as You will.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 14:37; Luke 22:45; Matthew 26:43"> Then He came to the disciples and found them sleeping, and said to Peter, “What? Were you not strong enough to watch with Me one hour?</span>

<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:32; Romans 13:1; Proverbs 23:34"> And He came and found them sleeping again, for their eyes were heavy.</span>

<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 13:1; John 12:27; Mark 14:41–42"> Then He came to His disciples and said to them, “Sleep now, and rest. Behold, the hour is at hand, and the Son of Man is being betrayed into the hands of sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 9:51; John 14:31; 1 Samuel 17:48"> Rise up, let us be going. See, he who betrays Me is at hand.”</span>

## The Betrayal and Arrest of Jesus

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 1:16; Matthew 26:55; Mark 14:43–50"> And while He was still speaking, behold, Judas, one of the twelve came, and with him a great crowd with swords and clubs, sent from the chief priests and elders of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 55:20–21; Psalms 28:3; 2 Samuel 3:27"> Now the one betraying Him had given them a sign, saying, “Whomever I shall kiss, He is the One; seize Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:25; 1 Thessalonians 5:26; Proverbs 27:6"> And immediately he went up to Jesus and said, “Greetings, Rabbi!” and kissed Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 20:13; Matthew 22:12; Psalms 41:9"> But Jesus said to him, “Friend, why have you come?” Then they came and laid hands on Jesus and seized Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 18:36; Mark 14:47; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> And behold, one of those with Jesus stretched out his hand and drew his sword, and striking the servant of the high priest, he cut off his ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Revelation 13:10; Genesis 9:6; Romans 12:19"> But Jesus said to him, “Put your sword back into its place, for all those who take the sword shall perish by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Kings 6:17; Matthew 4:11; Daniel 7:10"> Or do you think that I cannot now call upon My Father, and He will furnish Me with more than twelve legions of angels?</span>

<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; John 16:32; Mark 14:50–52"> But all this has happened so that the Scriptures of the prophets may be fulfilled.” Then all the disciples forsook Him and fled.</span>

## Jesus Before the High Priest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; Mark 14:53–65; Psalms 56:5–6"> And those having seized Jesus led Him away to Caiaphas the high priest, where the scribes and the elders were gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 7:32; John 18:15–16; John 18:25"> But Peter was following Him from a distance, up to the courtyard of the high priest. And entering inside, he sat down with the attendants to see the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Acts 6:11–13; Deuteronomy 19:16–21; Mark 14:55–56"> Now the chief priests, the elders, and all the Sanhedrin were seeking false witnesses against Jesus, so that they might put Him to death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Psalms 35:11; Psalms 27:12"> and they did not find any. And though many false witnesses came forward, they did not find any. But at last two false witnesses came forward</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="John 2:19–21; Matthew 27:40; Mark 15:29"> and said, “This Man said, ‘I am able to destroy the temple of God, and after three days to rebuild it.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="John 19:9–11; John 18:19–24; Mark 14:60"> And standing up the high priest said to Him, “Do You answer nothing? What is it these men are testifying against You?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Matthew 16:16; Isaiah 53:7"> But Jesus kept silent. And the high priest answered and said to Him, “I adjure You by the living God: Tell us if You are the Christ, the Son of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Mark 14:62; Psalms 110:1"> Jesus said to him, “You said it. Nevertheless, I say to you, from now on, you will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of the Power, and coming on the clouds of heaven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:24; John 10:33; Matthew 9:3"> Then the high priest tore his clothes, saying, “He has blasphemed! What further need do we have of witnesses? Look, now you have heard His blasphemy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="John 19:7; Acts 7:52; Leviticus 24:11–16"> What do you think?” They answered and said, “He is deserving of death.”</span>
