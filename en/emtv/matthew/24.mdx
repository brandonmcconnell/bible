<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 13:1–37; Luke 21:5–36; Ezekiel 8:6"> Then Jesus went out and departed from the temple, and His disciples came to Him to point out to Him the buildings of the temple.</span>

## Signs of the End of the Age

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 13:3–4; Acts 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–11"> And as He was sitting on the Mount of Olives, the disciples came to Him privately, saying, “Tell us, when shall these things be? And what shall be the sign of Your coming, and of the end of the age?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Jeremiah 29:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:3"> And Jesus answered and said to them: “Take heed that no one deceives you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 24:11; Matthew 24:24; Jeremiah 14:14"> For many will come in My name, saying, ‘I am the Christ,’ and will deceive many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:7–8; Luke 21:9; 1 Peter 3:14–15"> And you will hear of wars and rumors of wars. See that you are not disturbed; for all these things must come to pass, but the end is not yet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 21:11; Revelation 6:8; Isaiah 19:2"> For nation shall rise up against nation, and kingdom against kingdom, and there will be famines, pestilences, and earthquakes in various places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Deuteronomy 28:59"> But all these things are the beginning of birth pains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 16:2; John 15:19–20; Luke 21:16–17"> “Then they will hand you over to tribulation and they will kill you, and you will be hated by all nations, on account of My name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Luke 21:16; Matthew 11:6"> And then many will fall away, and they will betray one another, and they will hate one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; Matthew 7:15; Matthew 24:5"> Then many false prophets will be raised up, and they will deceive many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 2:4–5; James 4:1–4; Revelation 3:15"> And because lawlessness will increase, the love of many will grow cold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 10:22; Mark 13:13; Revelation 2:10"> But he who endures to the end shall be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 14:6; Romans 10:18; Romans 16:25–26"> And this gospel of the kingdom will be proclaimed in all the world for a testimony to all the nations, and then the end will come.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Great Tribulation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 9:27; Daniel 12:11; Mark 13:14"> “Therefore when you see the ‘abomination of desolation,’ spoken of through Daniel the prophet, standing in the holy place” (whoever reads, let him understand),</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 19:15–17; Luke 21:21–22; Hebrews 11:7"> “then let those who are in Judea flee upon the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 17:31–33; Mark 13:15–16; Matthew 10:27"> Let the one on the housetop not go down to take the things out of his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And let the one in the field not turn back to take his clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 23:29–30; Deuteronomy 28:53–56; Luke 21:23"> But woe to those women who are pregnant, and to the women nursing a baby in those days!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:29; Acts 1:12"> But pray that your flight may not take place in winter, nor on the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Joel 2:2; Malachi 4:1"> For then there shall be great tribulation, such as has not been since the beginning of the world until now, nor by any means shall be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 13:20; 2 Timothy 2:10; Matthew 24:31"> And unless those days were cut short, no flesh would be saved; but for the elect's sake, those days will be cut short.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Luke 17:23–24; Deuteronomy 13:1–3"> “Then if someone says to you, ‘Look, here is the Christ!’ or ‘There!’ do not believe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Mark 13:22; Revelation 13:13–14"> For false christs and false prophets will be raised up, and they will show great signs and wonders so as to deceive, if possible, even the elect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:1; Luke 21:13; Isaiah 48:5–6"> See, I have told you in advance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 3:2–3; Isaiah 40:3; Matthew 3:1"> “Therefore if they should say to you, ‘Look, He is in the desert!’ do not go out; or ‘Look, He is in the inner rooms!’ do not believe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 17:24–37; Zechariah 9:14; Job 37:3"> For as the lightning comes out from the east and flashes to the west, so also will be the coming of the Son of Man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 17:37; Job 39:27–30; Deuteronomy 28:49"> For wherever the carcass may be, there the eagles will be gathered together.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Coming of the Son of Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Amos 8:9; Joel 3:15; Isaiah 13:10"> “Immediately after the tribulation of those days, the sun shall be darkened, and the moon shall not give its light; and the stars shall fall from heaven, and the powers of the heavens shall be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Daniel 7:13; Luke 21:27"> And then shall appear the sign of the Son of Man in heaven, and then all the tribes of the earth shall mourn, and they shall see the Son of Man coming on the clouds of heaven with power and great glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16; Mark 13:27; Isaiah 27:13"> And He shall send His angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they shall gather together His elect from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Fig Tree

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 13:28–29; Luke 21:29–30"> “Now learn this parable from the fig tree: Whenever its branch already becomes tender and puts forth leaves, you know that summer is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 5:9; Hebrews 10:37; Ezekiel 7:2–14"> So you also, whenever you see all these things, know that it is near—at the doors!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 13:30–31; Luke 21:32–33; Matthew 16:28"> Assuredly I say to you, this generation shall by no means pass away till all these things are fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; 1 Peter 1:25; Numbers 23:19"> Heaven and earth shall pass away, but My words shall by no means pass away.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Necessity for Watchfulness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Acts 1:7; Matthew 24:44"> “But concerning that day and hour no one knows, not even the angels of heaven, but My Father only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:1–7; 2 Peter 2:5"> But just as the days of Noah were, so also shall be the coming of the Son of Man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 17:26–28; Romans 13:13–14; Luke 14:18–20"> For just as they were in the days before the flood, eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day that Noah entered into the ark,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:20; Matthew 24:37; Acts 13:41"> and they didn't know a thing until the flood came and took them all away, so also shall be the coming of the Son of Man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 17:34–37; 2 Peter 2:7–9; 2 Peter 2:5"> Then two will be in the field: one will be taken, and one will be left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 11:5; Isaiah 47:2; Luke 17:35"> Two will be grinding at the mill: one will be taken, and one will be left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 21:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:6; Matthew 25:13"> Watch therefore, for you do not know in what hour your Lord is coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 12:39; Matthew 24:44; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–6"> But know this, that if the master of the house had known in what hour the thief was coming, he would have kept watch, and he would not have allowed his house to be broken into.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 25:10; Matthew 24:42; Matthew 25:13"> For this reason you also must be ready, for at an hour that you think not, the Son of Man comes.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Faithful Servant and the Evil Servant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Matthew 25:23; 1 Corinthians 4:1–2"> “Who then is a faithful and wise servant, whom his master appointed over his household, to give them food in due season?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; Luke 12:43; Luke 12:37"> Blessed is that servant whom his master, when he comes, will find so doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 25:23; Matthew 25:21; Luke 12:37"> Assuredly I say to you that he will make him ruler over all his possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 18:32; Isaiah 32:6; Luke 12:45"> But if that evil servant should say in his heart, ‘My master is delaying to come,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Revelation 17:6; Revelation 16:6; Micah 3:5"> and should begin to beat his fellow servants, and to eat and drink with the drunkards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Revelation 3:3; Matthew 24:42–44"> the master of that servant will come on a day which he does not expect, and in an hour which he does not know,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 22:13; Luke 12:46"> and he will cut him in two, and will appoint his share with the hypocrites. There shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.</span>
</div>
