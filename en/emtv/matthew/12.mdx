## Jesus is Lord of the Sabbath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:25; Mark 2:23–28; Luke 6:1–5"> At that time Jesus went through the grain fields on the Sabbath. And His disciples became hungry, and they began to pluck heads of grain and to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:10; John 7:21–24; Luke 14:3"> But when the Pharisees saw it, they said to Him, “Look, Your disciples are doing what is not lawful to do on the Sabbath!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:3–6; Luke 6:3; Mark 2:25–26"> But He said to them, “Have you not read what David did when he became hungry, he and those who were with him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–9; Exodus 25:30; Leviticus 8:31"> how they entered the house of God, and ate the showbread which was not lawful for him to eat, nor for those who were with him, except only for the priests?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 28:9–10; John 7:22–23; Nehemiah 13:17"> Or have you not read in the law that on the Sabbath the priests in the temple profane the Sabbath, and are guiltless?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 12:41–42; 2 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Peter 2:4–5"> Yet I say to you that One greater than the temple is here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Matthew 9:13; Micah 6:6–8"> But if you had known what this means, ‘I desire mercy and not sacrifice,’ you would not have condemned the guiltless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 2:28; Luke 6:5; John 5:17–23"> For the Son of Man is Lord of the Sabbath.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 3:1–6; Luke 6:6–11"> And having departed from there, He went into their synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 13:14; John 9:16; John 8:6"> And behold, there was a man who had a withered hand. And they asked Him, saying, “Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath?”—that they might accuse Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:4; Luke 14:5; Luke 13:15–17"> Then He said to them, “What man is there among you who shall have one sheep, and if it should fall into a ditch on the Sabbath, will not lay hold of it and lift it out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 6:26; Luke 6:9; Mark 3:4"> How much more valuable then is a man than a sheep! So then it is lawful to do good on the Sabbath.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 13:13; Acts 3:7–8"> Then He said to the man, “Stretch out your hand.” And he stretched it out, and it was restored whole like the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 11:53; John 10:39; Mark 3:6"> But the Pharisees went out and conspired against Him, how they might destroy Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 3:7–12; Matthew 10:23; Matthew 19:2"> But when Jesus knew it, He withdrew from there. And great crowds followed Him, and He healed them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Matthew 17:9"> And He warned them that they should not make Him known,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1–4; Isaiah 42:9; Matthew 13:35"> so that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by Isaiah the prophet, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Luke 4:18; Acts 10:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, My Servant whom I have chosen,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">My Beloved in whom My soul is well pleased!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will put My Spirit upon Him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and He will announce justice to the Gentiles.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; John 18:36–38; 2 Timothy 2:24–25"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will not quarrel nor cry out,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor will anyone hear His voice in the streets.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 42:3–4; Ezekiel 34:16; Isaiah 61:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">A bruised reed He shall not break,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and a smoldering wick He shall not quench,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">till He bring forth justice to victory;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 11:10; Romans 15:12–13; Isaiah 42:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">And in His name the nations shall hope.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Pharisees Rebuked

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 11:14–15; Mark 7:35–37; Isaiah 29:18"> Then one was brought to Him who was demon-possessed, blind and mute; and He healed him, so that the blind and mute man both spoke and saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; John 4:29; Matthew 22:42–43"> And all the crowds were amazed and said, “This is not the Son of David, is it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Matthew 9:34; Luke 11:15"> But the Pharisees, having heard, said, “This Man does not cast out demons except by Beelzebub, ruler of the demons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Galatians 5:15; Jeremiah 17:10; Matthew 9:4"> But Jesus, knowing their thoughts, said to them: “Every kingdom divided against itself is made desolate, and every city or house divided against itself shall not stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; Revelation 20:2–3; 1 John 5:19"> And if Satan casts out Satan, he is divided against himself. How then will his kingdom stand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 19:13–16; Mark 9:38–39; Luke 9:49–50"> And if I cast out demons by Beelzebub, by whom do your sons cast them out? Therefore they shall be your judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 11:20; Daniel 7:14; Matthew 6:33"> But if I cast out demons by the Spirit of God, then the kingdom of God has come upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24; Mark 3:27; Luke 11:21–22"> Or how is one able to enter a strong man's house to plunder his goods, unless he first binds the strong man? And then he will plunder his household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 9:40; Luke 11:23; Joshua 24:15"> He who is not with Me is against Me, and he who does not gather with Me scatters.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Unpardonable Sin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Mark 3:28–30; Hebrews 10:29"> “Therefore I say to you, every sin and blasphemy shall be forgiven unto men, but the blasphemy against the Spirit shall not be forgiven unto men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 3:29; Hebrews 10:26–29; Hebrews 6:4–6"> And whoever says a word against the Son of Man, it shall be forgiven him; but whoever speaks against the Holy Spirit, it shall not be forgiven him, either in this age, or in the age to come.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Tree and Its Fruit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 6:43–44; Matthew 7:16–20; James 4:8"> “Either make the tree good and its fruit good, or else make the tree corrupt and its fruit corrupt; for a tree is known by its fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 6:45; Matthew 15:18; Ephesians 4:29"> Brood of vipers! How can you, being evil, speak good things? For out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 15:4; Colossians 4:6; Proverbs 15:28"> A good man out of his good treasure brings forth good things, and an evil man out of his evil treasure brings forth evil things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Romans 2:16; Revelation 20:12"> But I say to you, that for every idle word men may speak, they will give account of it in the Day of Judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Proverbs 13:3; James 2:21–25"> For by your words you shall be justified, and by your words you shall be condemned.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sign of Jonah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:22; Mark 8:11–12; Luke 11:16"> Then some of the scribes and Pharisees answered, saying, “Teacher, we wish to see a sign from You.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 16:4; Luke 11:29–32; Mark 8:38"> But He answered and said to them, “An evil and adulterous generation seeks after a sign, and no sign will be given to it except the sign of the prophet Jonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Jonah 1:17; Matthew 27:63–64; Matthew 16:21"> For just as Jonah was three days and three nights in the belly of the great fish, so will the Son of Man be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; Hebrews 11:7; Matthew 12:6"> The men of Nineveh shall stand up in the judgment with this generation and shall condemn it, because they repented at the preaching of Jonah; and behold, a greater than Jonah is here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Kings 10:1–13; 2 Chronicles 9:1–12; Hebrews 1:2–4"> The queen of the South shall rise up in the judgment with this generation and she shall condemn it, because she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon; and behold, a greater than Solomon is here.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 11:24–26; Luke 8:28–32; Matthew 8:29"> “When an unclean spirit goes out from a man, he goes through dry places, seeking rest, and does not find any.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 John 4:4; Revelation 13:8–9; Jude 1:4–5"> Then he says, ‘I will return to my house from where I came out.’ And when he comes, he finds it unoccupied, swept, and adorned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 11:26; Mark 5:9; Hebrews 6:4–8"> Then he goes and takes along with himself seven other spirits more wicked than himself, and entering in they dwell there; and the last state of that man is worse than the first. Thus it shall also be with this wicked generation.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Changed Relationships

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 13:55; John 2:12; John 7:3"> And while He was still speaking to the crowds, behold, His mother and brothers stood outside, seeking to speak with Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> Then one said to Him, “Look, Your mother and Your brothers are standing outside, seeking to speak with You.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Mark 3:32–33; Matthew 10:37; Luke 2:52"> But He answered and said to him that told Him, “Who is My mother? And who are My brothers?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 3:34; John 17:8–9; John 20:17–20"> And stretching out His hand toward His disciples, He said, “Behold; My mother, and My brothers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Luke 8:21; Mark 3:35; John 15:14"> For whoever does the will of My Father in heaven, he is My brother and sister and mother.”</span>
</div>
