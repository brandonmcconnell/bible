## The Tradition of the Elders

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Matthew 23:2; Luke 5:21"> Then the scribes and Pharisees who were from Jerusalem approached Jesus, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 11:38; Mark 7:2; Colossians 2:20–23"> “Why do Your disciples transgress the tradition of the elders? For they do not wash their hands whenever they eat bread.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:23; Titus 1:14; Colossians 2:8"> He answered and said to them, “Why do you also transgress the commandment of God on account of your tradition?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Leviticus 20:9; Exodus 21:17"> For God commanded, saying, ‘Honor your father and your mother’; and, ‘He who curses father or mother, with death let him die.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 7:10–13; Acts 5:29; Matthew 23:16–18"> But you say, ‘Whoever says to his father or mother, “It is a gift to God, whatever profit you might have received from me”—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; Jeremiah 8:8; Mark 7:13"> then by no means should he honor his father or mother.’ Thus you have invalidated the commandment of God for the sake of your tradition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 7:6; Matthew 23:23–29; Acts 28:25–27"> Hypocrites! Well did Isaiah prophesy about you, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Ezekiel 33:31; Acts 8:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘These people draw near to Me with their mouth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they honor Me with their lips,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but their heart is far away from Me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Mark 7:7; 1 Timothy 1:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">And in vain they worship Me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">teaching as doctrines the commandments of men.’ ”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 7:14–15; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 24:45"> And having summoned the crowd, He said to them, “Hear and understand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Matthew 15:18–20; James 3:5–8"> It is not what goes into the mouth that defiles a man; but what comes out of the mouth, this defiles a man.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 22:13–14; 1 Corinthians 10:32–33; Galatians 2:5"> Then His disciples approached and said to Him, “You know that when the Pharisees heard this message they were offended?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:2; Isaiah 60:21; John 15:6"> But He answered and said, “Every plant which My heavenly Father has not planted will be uprooted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:39; Isaiah 56:10; Matthew 23:16–24"> Leave them alone. They are blind guides of the blind. And if the blind guides the blind, both will fall into a ditch.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 13:36; Mark 4:34; John 16:29"> Then Peter answered and said to Him, “Explain to us this parable.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:9; Mark 7:18; Mark 9:32"> So Jesus said, “Are you also still without understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 6:45; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Matthew 7:19–20"> Do you not yet understand that whatever enters the mouth goes into the stomach, and is cast out into a latrine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Matthew 12:34; James 3:6–10"> But those things which proceed out of the mouth come from the heart, and they defile a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 7:21–23; Galatians 5:19–21; Jeremiah 17:9"> For out of the heart come forth evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false witness, blasphemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Ephesians 5:3–6; 1 Corinthians 6:18–20"> These are the things which defile a man, but to eat with unwashed hands does not defile a man.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Canaanite Woman's Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 7:24–30; Matthew 11:21–23; Judges 1:31"> And going out from there, Jesus withdrew into the region of Tyre and Sidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Mark 7:25–26; Matthew 17:15"> And behold, a Canaanite woman coming forth from that region cried out to Him, saying, “Have mercy on me, O Lord, Son of David! My daughter is severely demon-possessed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 14:15; Mark 10:47–48; Lamentations 3:8"> But He answered her not a word.<br />And His disciples approached and urged Him, saying, “Send her away, for she cries out after us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:5–6; Romans 15:8; Jeremiah 50:6–7"> But He answered and said, “I was not sent except to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 32:26; Matthew 20:31; Matthew 14:33"> Then she came and worshiped Him, saying, “Lord, help me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Matthew 7:6; Philippians 3:2"> But He answered and said, “It is not good to take the children's bread and to throw it to the little dogs.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 16:21; Job 40:4–5"> But she said, “Yes, Lord, yet even the little dogs eat from the crumbs falling from their masters' table.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:23–24; Matthew 8:13; Psalms 145:19"> Then Jesus answered and said to her, “O woman, great is your faith! Let it be to you as you desire.” And her daughter was healed from that very hour.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Heals Many People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 4:18; John 6:1; Matthew 5:1"> And moving on from there, Jesus came alongside the Sea of Galilee, and going up into a mountain, He sat down there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:17–19; Acts 5:15–16; Matthew 14:35–36"> And great crowds came to Him, having with them the lame, blind, mute, maimed, and many others; and they put them down at Jesus' feet, and He healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 18:43; Luke 17:15–18"> So the multitude marveled when they saw the mute speaking, the maimed made whole, the lame walking, and the blind seeing; and they glorified the God of Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Feeds Four Thousand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 27:33; Luke 12:29–30; Matthew 9:36"> Now Jesus, having summoned His disciples, He said, “I have compassion on the crowd, because they have been remaining with Me three days, and they do not have anything to eat. And I do not desire to send them away hungry, lest they faint on the way.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 6:37; Matthew 14:15; Mark 8:4–5"> Then His disciples said to Him, “Where could we get so many loaves of bread in this deserted place, so as to satisfy so great a crowd?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 16:9–10; Luke 24:41–42; John 21:9–10"> Jesus said to them, “How many loaves do you have?”<br />And they said, “Seven, and a few small fish.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 9:14–16; Mark 6:39–40; John 6:10"> So He commanded the crowds to recline on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 22:19; Romans 14:6; John 6:11"> And He took the seven loaves and the fish, and having given thanks, He broke them, and gave them to His disciples; and the disciples gave to the crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 8:19–21; Matthew 16:9–10; Matthew 15:33"> So they all ate and were satisfied. And they took up what they had left of the fragments, seven hampers full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Now those who ate were four thousand men, apart from women and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 8:10; Matthew 14:22"> And having dismissed the crowds, He went up into the boat, and came to the region of Magdala.</span>
</div>
</div>
