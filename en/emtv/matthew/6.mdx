## Concerning Almsgiving

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; John 12:43; Hebrews 6:10"> “Take care not to be doing your charitable giving before men, so as to be seen by them. Otherwise, you have not a reward from your Father in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:5; Matthew 6:16; 2 Corinthians 9:6–15"> Whenever therefore you do charitable giving, do not sound a trumpet before you as the hypocrites do in the synagogues and in the streets, so that they may be praised by men. Assuredly I say to you, they receive their reward in full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 7:4; Matthew 9:30; Mark 1:44"> But when you do charitable giving, do not let your left hand know what your right hand is doing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 6:18; Matthew 6:6; Jeremiah 17:10"> so that your charitable giving may be in secret; and your Father, who sees in secret, will Himself reward you openly.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Concerning Prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:10–11; Matthew 6:2; Luke 18:13"> “And whenever you pray, you shall not be like the hypocrites, because they love to pray standing in the synagogues, and on the street corners, so that they may be seen by men. Assuredly I say to you, that they receive their reward in full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33; Matthew 14:23; Isaiah 65:24"> But you, whenever you pray, enter into your room, and when you have shut your door, pray to your Father who is in secret; and your Father, who sees in secret, will reward you openly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:2–3; Ecclesiastes 5:7; 1 Kings 18:26–29"> But when you pray, do not babble like the heathen, for they think that they will be heard for their many words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:32; Luke 12:30; Philippians 4:6"> Therefore do not be like them. For your Father knows what things you have need of before you ask Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 11:1–4; Revelation 4:11; Romans 8:15"> Therefore pray in this manner:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Our Father in heaven,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Hallowed be Your name.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Matthew 26:42; Matthew 12:50"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your kingdom come,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your will be done,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">On earth as it is in heaven.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 30:8; Luke 11:3; Matthew 4:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give us this day our daily bread.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Mark 11:25; Ephesians 1:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">And forgive us our debts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">As we forgive our debtors.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Revelation 7:14–17; Matthew 26:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">And lead us not into temptation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But deliver us from the evil one.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For Yours is the kingdom and the power and the glory forever. Amen.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Mark 11:25; Colossians 3:13"> “For if you forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:35"> But if you do not forgive men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Concerning Fasting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 58:3–5; Matthew 9:14–15; Daniel 9:3"> “Moreover, when you fast, do not become gloomy, like the hypocrites, for they disfigure their faces that they may appear to men to be fasting. Assuredly I say to you, that they receive their reward in full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ruth 3:3; Ecclesiastes 9:8; 2 Samuel 12:20"> But you, when you fast, anoint your head and wash your face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; Matthew 6:4; 2 Corinthians 10:18"> so that you do not appear to men to be fasting, but to your Father who is in secret; and your Father, who sees in secret, will reward you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Concerning Treasures

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; 1 John 2:15–16; Luke 12:21"> “Do not lay up for yourselves treasures on earth, where moth and rust corrupt, and where thieves dig through and steal;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 12:33; Matthew 19:21; James 2:5"> but lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where neither moth nor rust corrupts, and where thieves do not dig through and steal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–3; Luke 12:34; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sound Eye

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 11:34–36; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Colossians 3:22"> “The lamp of the body is the eye. If therefore your eye is sound, your whole body will be full of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 2:11; Matthew 6:22; Isaiah 5:20–21"> But if your eye is evil, your whole body will be dark. If therefore the light that is in you is darkness, how great is that darkness!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Serving Two Masters

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Luke 16:13; James 4:4"> “No one is able to serve two masters; for either he will hate the one, and he will love the other, or else he will be devoted to the one, and he will despise the other. You cannot serve God and money.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Do Not Worry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; 1 Peter 5:7; Romans 8:32"> “Because of this I say to you, do not worry about your life—what you shall eat, or what you shall drink—nor about your body, what you shall put on. Is not life more than food and the body more than clothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:29–31; Psalms 147:9; Luke 12:24–32"> Look at the birds of the air, for they neither sow nor reap, nor do they gather into barns; yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not worth more than they?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 12:25–26; Ecclesiastes 3:14; Psalms 39:5–6"> Which of you by worrying is able to add one cubit to his stature?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 6:25; Philippians 4:6; Luke 12:27"> “So why do you worry about clothes? Consider the lilies of the field, how they grow; they neither labor, nor spin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:20–22; 2 Chronicles 9:4–6; 1 Peter 3:2–5"> and yet I say to you, that not even Solomon in all his glory was arrayed like one of these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 14:31; Luke 12:28; 1 Peter 1:24"> But if God thus clothes the grass of the field, which exists today, and tomorrow is cast into the oven, will He not much more clothe you, O you of little faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Luke 12:29; Matthew 4:4"> “Therefore do not worry, saying, ‘What shall we eat?’ or ‘What shall we drink?’ or ‘What shall we put on?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 6:8; Luke 12:30; Ephesians 4:17"> For after all these things the Gentiles seek. For your heavenly Father knows that you need all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 12:31; Matthew 5:6; John 6:27"> But seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things shall be added unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 6:25; John 14:27; Hebrews 13:5–6"> Therefore do not worry about tomorrow, for tomorrow will worry about its own affairs. Each day has enough trouble of its own.</span>
</div>
</div>
