## The Temptation of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 2:18; Luke 4:1–13; Mark 1:12–15"> Then Jesus was led up into the wilderness by the Spirit, to be tempted by the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18; Exodus 34:28; 1 Kings 19:8"> And having fasted forty days and forty nights, afterward He was hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:1–5; 1 Thessalonians 3:5; Numbers 11:4–6"> And approaching Him, the tempter said, “If You are the Son of God, command that these stones may become bread.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Luke 4:4; John 6:63"> But He answered and said, “It is written, ‘Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceeds out of the mouth of God.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 4:9; Matthew 27:53; Revelation 11:2"> Then the devil took Him up into the holy city, and set Him on the pinnacle of the temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 91:11–12; Hebrews 1:14; Psalms 34:7"> and said to Him, “If You are the Son of God, throw Yourself down. For it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘He shall give His angels charge concerning you,’</span><br />and,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘In their hands they will lift you up,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">lest you dash your foot against a stone.’ ”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; 1 Corinthians 10:9; Psalms 78:18"> Jesus said to him, “Again, it is written, ‘You shall not tempt the Lord your God.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Revelation 11:15; Psalms 49:16–17"> Again, the devil took Him up on a very high mountain, and showed Him all the kingdoms of the world, and their glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; Daniel 5:18–19; John 16:11"> And he said to Him, “All these things will I give You, if You will fall down and worship me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13–14; Deuteronomy 10:20; Joshua 24:14"> Then Jesus said to him, “Get behind Me, Satan! For it is written, ‘You shall worship the Lord your God, and Him alone you shall serve.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 26:53; Hebrews 1:14; Luke 22:43"> Then the devil left Him, and behold, angels came and ministered to Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Begins His Ministry in Galilee

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 1:14; Luke 3:20; Matthew 14:3"> Now when Jesus heard that John had been imprisoned, He departed into Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 4:46; Mark 1:21; Matthew 11:23"> And leaving Nazareth behind, He came and settled down in Capernaum, which is by the sea, in the regions of Zebulun and Naphtali,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 9:1–2; Matthew 2:23; Luke 24:44"> so that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by Isaiah the prophet, saying:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:1; 2 Kings 15:29; Joshua 21:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">“The land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">by the way of the sea, beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 9:2; Isaiah 42:6–7; Isaiah 60:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">the people sitting in darkness have seen a great light,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and to those who sat in the land and the shadow of death,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">light has dawned.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:38; Mark 1:14–15"> From that time Jesus began to preach and to say, “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Calls the First Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 1:40–42; Matthew 10:2; Luke 6:14"> Now as He was walking alongside the Sea of Galilee, He saw two brothers, Simon called Peter, and Andrew his brother, casting a net into the sea; for they were fishermen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 1:17–18; John 12:26; Matthew 16:24"> Then He said to them, “Come after Me, and I will make you fishers of men.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 10:28–31; Matthew 19:27; Luke 18:28–30"> They immediately left their nets and followed Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 1:19–20; Matthew 10:2; Mark 3:17"> And having gone forth from there, He saw two other brothers, James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, in the boat with Zebedee their father, mending their nets. And He called them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Luke 9:59–60; Deuteronomy 33:9–10"> And immediately they left the boat and their father, and followed Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Ministers to the Multitudes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 9:35; Mark 1:39; Acts 10:38"> And Jesus went about all of Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every disease and every illness among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 8:6; Matthew 17:15; Luke 5:15"> And the report of Him went throughout all Syria; and they brought to Him all those who were sick, and who were suffering with various diseases and torments, and those who were demon-possessed, epileptics, and paralytics; and He healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 6:17; Mark 5:20; Mark 3:7–8"> And large crowds followed Him from Galilee and from Decapolis, and Jerusalem, and Judea, and beyond the Jordan.</span>
</div>
</div>
