## The Parable of the Wedding Banquet

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 20:1–16; Matthew 12:43–45; Luke 14:16"> And Jesus answered and spoke to them again in parables, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–9; Matthew 13:24; Ephesians 5:24–32"> “The kingdom of heaven is like a certain king who arranged a wedding for his son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; John 5:40; Luke 14:15–17"> and sent out his servants to call those who were invited to the wedding; and they did not want to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:36; John 6:50–57"> Again, he sent out other servants, saying, ‘Say to those who are invited, “See, I have prepared my dinner; my oxen and fatted cattle are killed, and all things are ready. Come to the wedding.” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:3; Luke 14:18–20; 1 John 2:15–16"> But not caring, they went away, one to his own field, another to his business.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:19–20; John 16:2–3; Acts 7:51–57"> And the rest seized his servants, mistreated them, and killed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 19:27; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Luke 21:24"> And when that king heard, he was furious. And he sent out his troops, and he destroyed those murderers, and he burned up their city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; Acts 13:46; Revelation 3:4"> Then he said to his servants, ‘The wedding indeed is ready, but those who were invited were not worthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 14:21–24; Proverbs 1:20–23; Mark 16:15–16"> Therefore go into the highways, and as many as you find, invite to the wedding.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Revelation 19:6–9; 2 Corinthians 12:21"> So those servants went out into the highways and gathered together all whom they found, both bad and good. And the wedding hall was filled with guests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 19:8; Hebrews 4:12–13"> “But when the king came in to see the guests, he saw a man there not dressed with wedding clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:9; Job 5:16; Matthew 20:13"> So he said to him, ‘Friend, how did you come in here not having wedding clothes?’ And he was speechless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 25:30; Matthew 24:51"> Then the king said to the servants, ‘Tie him up hands and feet, take him away, and cast him into outer darkness; there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Luke 13:23–24; Matthew 7:13–14"> “For many are called, but few are chosen.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Question about Paying Taxes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Mark 12:13–17; Psalms 2:2"> Then the Pharisees went and plotted how they might trap Him in His speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 3:6; Mark 8:15; Mark 12:14"> And they sent their disciples to Him with the Herodians, saying, “Teacher, we know that You are true, and teach the way of God in truth; nor do You show favoritism to anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 2:1; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 17:25"> Tell us, therefore, what do You think? Is it lawful to pay taxes to Caesar, or not?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:6; Luke 5:22; John 2:25"> But Jesus, knowing their wickedness, said, “Why do you test Me, you hypocrites?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 20:2; Revelation 6:6; Matthew 18:28"> Show Me the tax money.” So they brought Him a denarius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 20:24"> And He said to them, “Whose image and inscription is this?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:7; Proverbs 24:21; Acts 4:19"> They said to Him, “Caesar's.” And He said to them, “Render therefore to Caesar the things that are Caesar's, and to God the things that are God's.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 12:12; Matthew 22:46; Luke 20:25–26"> When they had heard these words, they marveled, and leaving Him, they went away.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Question about the Resurrection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Mark 12:18–27; Acts 23:6–8"> The same day the Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection, came to Him, and they questioned Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Luke 20:28; Ruth 1:11"> saying: “Teacher, Moses said that if a man dies, having no children, his brother shall marry his wife as next of kin, and shall raise up offspring for his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:29–33; Mark 12:19–23; Hebrews 9:27"> Now there were with us seven brothers. The first died, after having married, and having no offspring, he left his wife to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Likewise the second also, and the third, down to the seventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Last of all the woman died also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Therefore, in the resurrection, whose wife of the seven will she be? For they all had married her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 20:9; Romans 15:4; Luke 1:37"> Jesus answered and said to them, “You err, not knowing the Scriptures, nor the power of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 20:34–36; Mark 12:24–25; Matthew 24:38"> For in the resurrection neither do they marry, nor are they given in marriage, but they are like angels of God in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 12:3; Matthew 21:16; Matthew 21:42"> But concerning the resurrection of the dead, have you not read what was spoken to you by God, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Mark 12:26–27; Luke 20:37–38"> ‘I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob’? God is not the God of the dead, but of the living.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 12:17; Mark 6:2; John 7:46"> And when the crowds heard this, they were astonished at His teaching.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Greatest Commandment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 12:28–31; Matthew 25:3–5; Matthew 12:14"> But when the Pharisees heard that He had silenced the Sadducees, they gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 7:30; Luke 10:25–37; Luke 11:52"> Then one of them, a lawyer, asked Him a question, testing Him, and saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 12:28–33; Matthew 5:19–20; Luke 11:42"> “Teacher, which is the great commandment in the law?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 12:29–30; Luke 10:27; Deuteronomy 6:5"> Jesus said to him, “ ‘You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, and with all your mind.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> This is the first and great commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 12:31; Luke 10:27–37; Leviticus 19:18"> And the second is like it: ‘You shall love your neighbor as yourself.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 7:12; 1 John 4:19–21"> On these two commandments hang all the Law and the Prophets.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Question about David's Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 22:34"> And when the Pharisees were gathered together, Jesus asked them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; John 6:68–69; Matthew 16:13–17"> saying, “What do you think about the Christ? Whose Son is He?” They said to Him, “David's.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:2; Revelation 4:2; Mark 12:36"> He said to them, “How then does David in the Spirit call Him ‘Lord,’ saying:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Acts 2:34–35"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘The Lord said to my Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sit at My right hand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">till I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet” ’?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Revelation 22:16; John 8:58"> If David therefore calls Him ‘Lord,’ how is He his Son?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Mark 12:34; Luke 20:40; Luke 14:6"> And no one was able to answer Him a word, nor from that day on did anyone dare question Him any longer.</span>
</div>
