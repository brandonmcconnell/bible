## True Greatness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 9:33–37; Luke 9:46–48; Matthew 20:20–28"> At that time the disciples came to Jesus, saying, “Who then is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 19:13–14; Mark 9:36–37; 1 Kings 3:7"> Then Jesus called a little child, and set him in their midst,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 10:14–15; 1 Peter 2:2; Matthew 19:14"> and said, “Assuredly I say to you, unless you are converted and become as little children, you shall by no means enter the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 4:10; Luke 14:11; Mark 10:43"> Therefore whoever humbles himself as this little child, he is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 25:40; Mark 9:37; John 13:20"> And whoever receives one such little child in My name, receives Me.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Warns of Offenses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 9:42; Luke 17:1–2; Matthew 18:10"> “But whoever causes one of these little ones who believe in Me to stumble, it would be better for him that a huge millstone be hanged on his neck, and he be drowned in the depth of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 17:1; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Matthew 26:24"> Woe to the world because of offenses! For offenses must come, but woe to that man through whom the offense comes!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 5:29–30; Philippians 3:8–9; Romans 13:12"> “And if your hand or your foot causes you to stumble, cut them off and cast them from you. It is better for you to enter into life lame or maimed, than having two hands or two feet, to be thrown into the eternal fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 14:22; Matthew 18:8; Matthew 5:29"> And if your eye causes you to stumble, pluck it out and cast it from you. It is better for you to enter into life with one eye, than having two eyes, to be thrown into the fiery hell.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Lost Sheep

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 91:11; Psalms 34:7; Hebrews 1:14"> “See that you do not despise one of these little ones, for I say to you that in heaven their angels always see the face of My Father who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For the Son of Man came to save that which was lost.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:12; Luke 15:4–7; Ezekiel 34:16"> “What do you think? If a certain man has a hundred sheep, and one of them goes astray, does he not leave the ninety-nine on the mountains, and go and search for the one gone astray?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:17; Luke 15:5–10; James 2:13"> And if he happens to find it, assuredly I say to you that he rejoices over it more than over the ninety-nine which did not go astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:12; John 6:39–40; 2 Peter 3:9"> Thus it is not the will before your Father who is in heaven that one of these little ones should perish.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Reproving Another Who Sins

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 19:17; Luke 17:3–4; Galatians 6:1"> “And if your brother sins against you, go and show him his fault between you and him alone. If he hears you, you have won your brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; 2 Corinthians 13:1; John 8:17"> But if he does not hear, take along with you one or two more, so that ‘by the mouth of two or three witnesses every word may be established.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Corinthians 5:9–7; 2 Thessalonians 3:14–15"> But if he refuses to hear them, tell it to the church. But if he refuses even to hear the church, let him be to you as a heathen and a tax collector.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 16:19; 2 Corinthians 2:10; John 20:23"> “Assuredly I say to you, whatever you bind on earth shall have been bound in heaven, and whatever you loose on earth shall have been loosed in heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 7:7; 1 John 5:14–16; Mark 11:24"> “Again, assuredly I say to you that if two of you agree on earth about anything that they may ask, it will be done for them by My Father in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:4; Revelation 21:3; Matthew 28:20"> For where two or three are gathered together in My name, I am there in their midst.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Forgiveness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 17:3–4; Matthew 18:15"> Then Peter came to Him and said, “Lord, how often shall my brother sin against me, and I forgive him? Up to seven times?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:13; Mark 11:25; Matthew 6:14–15"> Jesus said to him, “I do not say to you, up to seven times, but up to seventy times seven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:24; Romans 14:12; Matthew 3:2"> Therefore the kingdom of heaven is like a certain king who wanted to settle accounts with his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 38:4; Psalms 130:3–4; Luke 16:5"> And when he began to settle up, one debtor was brought to him owing ten thousand talents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 4:1; Nehemiah 5:5; Luke 7:42"> But since he had nothing with which to repay, his master commanded that he be sold, with his wife and children and everything that he had, and to be repaid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 18:29; Luke 7:43; Matthew 8:2"> Therefore falling down before him, the servant said, ‘Master, have patience with me, and I will repay everything to you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 145:8; Nehemiah 9:17; Psalms 86:15"> And having compassion, the master of that servant released him, and forgave him the debt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 20:2; Ezekiel 45:9; Deuteronomy 15:2"> “But when that servant went out, he found one of his fellow servants who owed him a hundred denarii. And taking hold of him, he was choking him, saying, ‘Pay me what you owe!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 18:26; Matthew 6:12; Philemon 1:18–19"> So his fellow servant fell down at his feet and kept begging him, saying, ‘Have patience with me, and I will pay you back.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 22:27; 1 Kings 21:27–29"> But he was not willing, but going off, he cast him into prison till he should pay back what was owed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Hebrews 13:3; Mark 3:5"> So when his fellow servants saw what had been done, they were deeply grieved, and they came and narrated to their master all that had taken place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 19:22; Matthew 25:26; Romans 3:19"> Then summoning him, his master said to him, ‘You wicked servant! I forgave you all that debt because you begged me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Colossians 3:13; Matthew 6:12; Ephesians 4:32–2"> Should you not also have had compassion on your fellow servant, as I also had compassion on you?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:25–26; James 2:13; Luke 12:58–59"> And being angered, his master handed him over to the tormentors until he should pay all that was owed to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; James 2:13; Luke 6:37–38"> “Thus also My heavenly Father will do to you, if you do not forgive each one his brother their trespasses, from your hearts.”</span>
</div>
</div>
