## The Death of John the Baptist

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Mark 8:15; Luke 9:7–9"> At that time Herod the tetrarch heard the report about Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 16:14; John 10:41; Mark 8:28"> and said to his servants, “This is John the Baptist; he has been raised from the dead, and therefore these powers are at work in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 6:17; Luke 3:19–20; Matthew 4:12"> For Herod had laid hold of John and bound him, and put him in prison on account of Herodias, his brother Philip's wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:21; Leviticus 18:16; Mark 6:18"> For John said to him, “It is not lawful for you to have her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 11:9; Mark 6:19–20"> Though desiring to kill him, he feared the crowd, because they regarded him as a prophet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 6:21–23; Genesis 40:20; Hosea 1:5–6"> Now when Herod's birthday was being celebrated, the daughter of Herodias danced before them, and she pleased Herod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 5:3; Esther 5:6; Esther 7:2"> Therefore he promised with an oath to give her whatever she should ask.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 6:24; Proverbs 29:10; Proverbs 1:16"> So she, being urged on by her mother, said, “Give me here on a platter the head of John the Baptist.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:12–16; 1 Samuel 14:28; Mark 6:26"> And the king was grieved, but because of the oaths, and because of those who reclined to eat with him, he commanded it to be given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 17:12; Matthew 21:35–36; Luke 9:9"> And having sent, he beheaded John in the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 16:6; Genesis 49:7; Ezekiel 16:3–4"> And his head was brought on a platter and it was given to the girl, and she brought it to her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 8:2; Matthew 27:58–61"> Then his disciples came forward and took the body and buried it, and coming, they reported it to Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Feeding the Five Thousand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:10–17; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 6:30–44"> When Jesus heard it, He withdrew from there in a boat for a deserted place privately. But when the crowds heard it, they followed Him on foot from the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Matthew 9:36; Hebrews 2:17"> And coming out, Jesus saw a large crowd, and He was moved with compassion towards them, and healed their sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 15:23; Mark 8:3; Mark 6:35–36"> Now when evening came, His disciples came to Him, saying, “The place is deserted, and the hour is already late. Dismiss the crowds, that they may go to the villages and buy food for themselves.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; Ecclesiastes 11:2"> But Jesus said to them, “They do not need to go away. You give them food to eat.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:33–34; Mark 6:37–38; Luke 9:13"> And they said to Him, “We do not have anything here except five loaves of bread and two fish.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But He said, “Bring them here to Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 8:6–7; Luke 24:30; 1 Samuel 9:13"> And commanding the crowds to recline on the grass, taking the five loaves of bread and the two fish, looking up to heaven, He blessed, and breaking them, He gave the loaves to the disciples; and the disciples gave to the crowds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:12; Mark 6:42–44; Matthew 16:8–10"> So they all ate and were satisfied, and they took up what they had left of the fragments, twelve baskets full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 9:8–11; Acts 4:4"> Now those who had eaten were about five thousand men, apart from women and children.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Walks on Water

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 6:45–51; John 6:15–21; Matthew 15:39"> And immediately Jesus compelled the disciples to get into the boat, and to go ahead of Him to the other side, until He could dismiss the crowds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 6:46; Matthew 26:36"> And having dismissed the crowds, He went up on the mountain privately to pray. And when evening had come, He was there alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:18; Mark 6:48; Matthew 8:24"> But the boat was now in the middle of the sea, being harassed by the waves, for the wind was against them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:8; John 6:19; Mark 6:48"> Now in the fourth watch of the night Jesus went to them, walking on the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 6:49–50; Revelation 1:17; Luke 1:11–12"> And when the disciples saw Him walking on the sea, they were terrified, and said, “It is a ghost!” And they cried out for fear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Revelation 1:17–18; John 16:33"> But immediately Jesus spoke to them, saying, “Have courage! It is I AM; do not fear.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:68; Romans 12:3; Matthew 26:33–35"> But Peter answered Him and said, “Lord, if it is You, command me to come to You on the waters.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Mark 9:23; Acts 3:16"> So He said, “Come.” And stepping down from the boat, Peter walked on the waters to go toward Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 3:54–57; 2 Timothy 4:16–17; Mark 14:38"> But seeing the strong wind, he was afraid, and beginning to sink he cried out, saying, “Lord, save me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:6–8; Psalms 138:7; Genesis 22:14"> And immediately, Jesus reaching out His hand, laid hold of him, and said to him, “O you of little faith, why did you doubt?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 107:29–30; Mark 4:41; John 6:21"> And when they had gotten into the boat, the wind ceased.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:27; Psalms 2:7; Romans 1:4"> Then those who were in the boat came and worshiped Him, saying, “Truly You are the Son of God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 6:53–56; Luke 5:1; John 6:24–25"> And having crossed over, they came to the land of Gennesaret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 4:24–25; Mark 6:55; Mark 1:28–34"> And when the men of that place recognized Him, they sent out into all that surrounding region, and they brought to Him all those who were sick,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Mark 3:10; Exodus 28:33–43"> and implored Him that they might only touch the hem of His garment. And as many as touched Him were completely healed.</span>
</div>
</div>
