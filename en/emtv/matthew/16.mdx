## The Demand for a Sign

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 11:16; Matthew 16:6; Acts 4:1"> Then the Pharisees and Sadducees came, and testing Him, they asked Him to show them a sign out of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:54–56"> He answered and said to them, “When evening comes you say, ‘Fine weather, for the sky is red’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 12:56; 1 Chronicles 12:32; Matthew 11:5"> And in the morning, ‘Today will be stormy, for the sky is red and overcast.’ Hypocrites! Indeed you know how to discern the face of the sky, but you cannot discern the signs of the times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 11:29–30; Matthew 12:39–40; Jonah 1:17"> An evil and adulterous generation seeks after a sign, and no sign will be given to it except the sign of the prophet Jonah.” And leaving them behind He went away.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 8:13–14; Matthew 15:39"> Now when His disciples had come to the other side, they forgot to take bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:1; Galatians 5:9; Matthew 16:12"> Then Jesus said to them, “Look out and beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and the Sadducees.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 8:16–18; Matthew 15:16–18; Acts 10:14"> But they were reasoning among themselves, saying, “We did not take bread.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:30; Matthew 14:31; Matthew 8:26"> But Jesus, knowing about it, said to them, “O you of little faith, why are you reasoning among yourselves, because you did not take bread?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 14:17–21; John 6:9–13; Luke 9:13–17"> Do you not yet perceive, or remember the five loaves of the five thousand and how many baskets you took up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 15:34–38; Mark 8:17–21; Mark 8:5–9"> Nor the seven loaves of the four thousand and how many hampers you took up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 16:6; Luke 12:56; Mark 8:21"> How is it you do not perceive that I did not speak to you about bread, but to beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:20; Acts 23:8; Matthew 15:4–9"> Then they understood that He did not say to beware of the leaven of bread, but of the doctrine of the Pharisees and Sadducees.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Peter Confesses Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:18–20; Mark 8:27–29; Daniel 7:13"> When Jesus came into the region of Caesarea Philippi, He was questioning His disciples, saying, “Who do men pronounce Me, the Son of Man, to be?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 6:15; Matthew 14:2; Malachi 4:5"> So they said, “Some say John the Baptist, but others say Elijah, and yet others say Jeremiah, or one of the prophets.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 9:20; Matthew 13:11; Mark 8:29"> He said to them, “But who do you pronounce Me to be?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; John 11:27; 1 John 4:15"> And Simon Peter answered and said, “You are the Christ, the Son of the living God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 6:45; Ephesians 1:17–18; Matthew 11:25–27"> Jesus answered and said to him, “Blessed are you, Simon Bar-Jonah, because flesh and blood did not reveal this to you, but My Father who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 2:19–22; 1 Corinthians 3:9–11; Isaiah 28:16"> And I also say to you that you are Peter, and upon this rock I shall build My church, and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 18:18; Isaiah 22:22; Revelation 1:18"> And I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven, and whatever you bind on the earth will have been bound in heaven, and whatever you loose on the earth will have been loosed in heaven.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 8:30; Luke 9:21; John 1:41"> Then He commanded His disciples that they should tell no one that He was Jesus the Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Foretells His Death and Resurrection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 24:6–7; Matthew 20:17–19; Matthew 17:22–23"> From that time Jesus began to show to His disciples that He must go off to Jerusalem, and suffer many things from the elders and chief priests and scribes, and to be killed, and to be raised the third day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 8:32; John 13:6–8; Matthew 16:16–17"> And taking Him aside, Peter began to rebuke Him, saying, “God be gracious to You, Lord! This shall by no means happen to You!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 8:5–8; Colossians 3:2; Philippians 3:19"> But He turned and said to Peter, “Get behind Me, Satan! You are a stumbling block to Me, because you are not mindful of the things of God, but the things of men.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Cross and Self-Denial

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 14:27; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> Then Jesus said to His disciples, “If anyone desires to come after Me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 10:39; Mark 8:35; Revelation 12:11"> For whoever desires to save his life shall lose it, but whoever loses his life on account of Me shall find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 8:36–37; Job 27:8; Luke 9:25"> For what is a man profited if he gains the whole world, but forfeits his soul? Or what will a man give in exchange for his soul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Psalms 62:12; Romans 2:6"> For the Son of Man is going to come in the glory of His Father with His angels, and then He will render to each according to his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 9:27; Mark 9:1; John 8:52"> Assuredly I say to you, there are some standing here who will by no means taste death till they see the Son of Man coming in His kingdom.”</span>
</div>
</div>
