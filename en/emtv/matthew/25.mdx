## The Parable of the Ten Virgins

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:35–36; Philippians 2:15–16; Matthew 24:42–51"> “Then shall the kingdom of heaven be likened unto ten virgins, who having taken their lamps, went out to meet the bridegroom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:2; 1 Corinthians 10:1–5; Matthew 13:19–23"> Now five of them were wise, and five were foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:15–16; 2 Timothy 3:5; Isaiah 48:1–2"> Those who were foolish, having taken their lamps, did not take oil with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:9; 1 John 2:27; 1 John 2:20"> but the wise took oil in their vessels along with their lamps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:6–8; 2 Peter 3:4–9"> But while the bridegroom was delayed, they all became drowsy and slept.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Matthew 24:31; Matthew 24:44"> “And at midnight a cry was heard: ‘Behold, the bridegroom is coming; go out to meet him!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 12:35; Revelation 3:19–20; 2 Peter 3:14"> Then all those virgins arose, and they trimmed their lamps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Luke 12:35; Hebrews 4:1"> And the foolish said to the wise, ‘Give us some of your oil, for our lamps are going out.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–9; Revelation 3:17–18; Isaiah 55:6–7"> But the wise answered, saying, ‘No, lest there should not be enough for you and us; but go rather to those who sell, and buy for yourselves.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 13:25; Matthew 24:44; Luke 12:36–37"> But as they went away to buy, the bridegroom came, and those who were ready went in with him to the wedding; and the door was shut.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 7:21–23; Hebrews 12:16–17"> “Afterward the remaining virgins came also, saying, ‘Lord, Lord, open to us!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; 2 Timothy 2:19; John 10:27"> But he answered and said, ‘Assuredly I say to you, I do not know you.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:42–44; Revelation 16:15; 1 Thessalonians 5:6"> “Watch therefore, for you do not know the day nor the hour in which the Son of Man is coming.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Talents

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 13:34; Luke 19:12–27; 1 Peter 4:9–11"> “For it is as if a man leaving on a journey, who called his own servants and handed over his possessions to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:24; Luke 19:13–14; Luke 12:48"> And to the one he gave five talents, and to another two, and to another one, to each according to his own ability; and immediately he went on a journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="3 John 1:5–8; 1 Timothy 6:17–18; 2 Timothy 2:6"> Then the one receiving the five talents went and traded with them, and made five other talents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Ephesians 5:16; 1 Peter 4:10"> And likewise the one receiving the two gained two more also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 1:8; Haggai 1:2–4; Malachi 1:10"> But the one receiving one went away and dug in the ground, and hid his lord's money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:12–15; 2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 14:7–12"> Then after much time, the lord of those servants returned and settled accounts with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:24; Luke 19:16–17; Colossians 1:29"> “And the one who had received five talents came and brought five other talents, saying, ‘Lord, you delivered to me five talents; look, I gained five more talents besides them.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:23; Luke 16:10; John 12:26"> His lord said to him, ‘Well done, good and faithful servant; you were faithful over a few things, I will make you ruler over many things. Enter into the joy of your lord.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; 2 Corinthians 8:7–8; 2 Corinthians 8:1–3"> And also the one receiving the two talents came and said, ‘Lord, you delivered to me two talents; look, I have gained two more talents besides them.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Mark 14:8–9; Mark 12:41–44"> His lord said to him, ‘Well done, good and faithful servant; you have been faithful over a few things, I will make you ruler over many things. Enter into the joy of your lord.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 19:20–22; Luke 15:29; Malachi 3:14–15"> “Then the one having received the one talent came and said, ‘Lord, I knew you, that you are a hard man, reaping where you did not sow, and gathering where you did not scatter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6–7; Romans 8:15; Revelation 21:8"> And being afraid, I went off and hid your talent in the ground. Look, you have what is yours.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 18:32; Job 15:5–6"> “But his lord answered and said to him, ‘You wicked and slothful servant, you knew that I reap where I did not sow, and gather where I did not scatter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Romans 3:19; Jude 1:15"> Therefore you should have deposited my money with the bankers, and at my coming I would have received what is mine with interest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 19:24; Luke 10:42"> Therefore take the talent from him, and give it to him who has the ten talents.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 8:18; Mark 4:25; Matthew 13:12"> “ ‘For to everyone who has, more shall be given, and he shall have abundance; but to him who does not have, even what he has shall be taken away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Revelation 3:15–16; Matthew 13:50"> And cast the useless servant into outer darkness. There shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Judgment of the Nations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:7–8; Matthew 19:28"> “And when the Son of Man comes in His glory, and all the holy angels with Him, then will He sit upon His glorious throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Matthew 13:49; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> All the nations will be gathered before Him, and He will separate them one from another, as a shepherd separates the sheep from the goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; John 10:26–28; John 21:15–17"> And He will set the sheep on His right hand, but the goats on His left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–20; James 2:5; Revelation 21:7"> Then the King will say to those on His right hand, ‘Come, you blessed of My Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 3:16–19; James 2:15–16; Deuteronomy 15:7–11"> for I was hungry and you gave Me food; I was thirsty and you gave Me drink; I was a stranger and you took Me in;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="James 1:27; 2 Timothy 1:16–18; James 5:14–15"> I was naked and you clothed Me; I was sick and you visited Me; I was in prison and you came to Me.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 6:3; 1 Chronicles 29:14; 1 Peter 5:5–6"> “Then the righteous will answer Him, saying, ‘Lord, when did we see You hungry and feed You, or thirsty and give You drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> When did we see You a stranger and take You in, or naked and clothe You?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Or when did we see You sick, or in prison, and come to You?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Proverbs 14:31; Matthew 10:42"> And the King will answer and say to them, ‘Assuredly I say to you, inasmuch as you did it to one of the least of these My brothers, you did it to Me.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6–7; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> “Then He will also say to those on the left, ‘Depart from Me, you cursed, into the eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels:</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="James 2:15–24; John 5:23; Matthew 12:30"> for I was hungry and you gave Me no food; I was thirsty and you gave Me no drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> I was a stranger and you did not take Me in, naked and you did not clothe Me, sick and in prison and you did not visit Me.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 7:22; Luke 10:29; Matthew 25:24–27"> “Then they also will answer, saying, ‘Lord, when did we see You hungry or thirsty or a stranger or naked or sick or in prison, and we did not minister to You?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 21:13; Proverbs 17:5; Proverbs 14:31"> Then He will answer them, saying, ‘Assuredly I say to you, inasmuch as you did not do it to one of the least of these, neither did you do it to Me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 3:36; John 5:29"> And these shall go away into eternal punishment, but the righteous into eternal life.”</span>
</div>
</div>
