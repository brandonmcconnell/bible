## The Triumphal Entry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zechariah 14:4; Mark 11:1–10; Luke 21:37"> Now when they drew near Jerusalem, and came to Bethphage, towards the Mount of Olives, then Jesus sent two disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 19:30–32; Mark 11:2–3; John 2:5–8"> saying to them, “Go into the village opposite you, and immediately you will find a donkey tied up, and a colt with her. Loose them and bring them to Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:1–2; Acts 17:25; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> And if anyone asks you anything, you shall say, ‘The Lord has need of them,’ and immediately he will send them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; John 12:15; Matthew 1:22"> Now all this took place so that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet, saying:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9–10; Isaiah 62:11; Isaiah 12:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Say to the daughter of Zion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Behold, your King is coming to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">humble, and sitting on a donkey,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a colt, the foal of a donkey.’ ”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 39:43; John 15:14; Exodus 40:16"> So the disciples went, and did just as Jesus ordered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; Mark 11:4–8; Luke 19:32–35"> They brought the donkey and the colt, and they laid their clothes on them, and they set Him on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; John 12:13; Leviticus 23:40"> And a very large crowd spread their clothes on the road; but others were cutting down branches from the trees and were spreading them on the road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 118:24–26; Luke 2:14; John 12:13–15"> Then the crowds who went before, and those following were crying out, saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Hosanna to the Son of David!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord!’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Hosanna in the highest!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 9:5; Ruth 1:19; John 12:16–19"> And when He entered into Jerusalem, all the city was stirred up, saying, “Who is this?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 6:14; Luke 7:16; John 7:40"> And the crowds were saying, “This is Jesus, the prophet from Nazareth of Galilee.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Cleanses the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 2:14–17; Luke 19:45–47; Mark 11:15–18"> Then Jesus went into the temple of God and He cast out all those buying and selling in the temple, and He overturned the tables of the moneychangers and the seats of those selling doves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:11; Isaiah 56:7; Luke 19:46"> And He said to them, “It is written, ‘My house shall be called a house of prayer,’ but you have made it a ‘den of thieves.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 3:1–9; Acts 10:38; Matthew 11:4–5"> Then the lame and the blind came to Him in the temple, and He healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 21:9; Luke 19:39–40; Isaiah 26:11"> But when the chief priests and scribes saw the wonders which He did, and the children crying out in the temple and saying, “Hosanna to the Son of David,” they became indignant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 8:2; Matthew 11:25; Luke 19:39–40"> and said to Him, “Do You hear what these children are saying?” And Jesus said to them, “Yes. Have you never read, ‘Out of the mouth of babes and nursing infants You have perfected praise’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 11:18; Mark 11:11; Luke 24:50"> And having left them, He went out of the city to Bethany, and lodged there.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Curses the Fig Tree

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 4:2; Hebrews 4:15; Matthew 12:1"> Now in the early morning, returning to the city, He became hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 15:6; Mark 11:14; Titus 1:16"> And seeing a fig tree by the road, He went up to it and found nothing on it but leaves, and said to it, “May no fruit come from you ever again!” And immediately the fig tree withered away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; Mark 11:20–21; James 1:10–11"> And when the disciples saw it, they marveled, saying, “How did the fig tree wither away so soon?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 1:6; Matthew 17:20; Mark 11:22–23"> So Jesus answered and said to them, “Assuredly I say to you, if you have faith and do not doubt, you will not only do what was done to the fig tree, but even if you should say to this mountain, ‘Be taken up and cast into the sea,’ it will happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:7; 1 John 5:14–15; Mark 11:24"> And all things, as many as you may ask in prayer, believing, you shall receive.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Authority of Jesus Questioned

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:7; Mark 11:27–33; 1 Chronicles 24:1–19"> Now as He came into the temple, the chief priests and the elders of the people confronted Him as He was teaching, and said, “By what authority are You doing these things? And who gave You this authority?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Colossians 4:6; Matthew 10:16"> But Jesus answered and said to them, “I also will ask you one thing, which if you tell Me, I likewise will tell you by what authority I do these things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 3:20; Mark 1:1–11; John 3:18"> The baptism of John—where was it from? From heaven or from men?” And they were arguing among themselves, saying, “If we say, ‘From heaven,’ He will say to us, ‘Why then did you not believe him?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 6:20; Matthew 14:5; John 5:35"> But if we should say, ‘From men,’ we fear the crowd, for all regard John as a prophet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 1:18–22; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–10; Luke 20:7–8"> So they answered Jesus and said, “We do not know.” And He said to them, “Neither will I tell you by what authority I do these things.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Two Sons

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Matthew 20:1; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> “But what do you think? A man had two children. And having come to the first, he said, ‘Son, go work today in my vineyard.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:28–32; Matthew 3:2–8; Isaiah 55:6–7"> But he answered and said, ‘I will not,’ but later he regretted it and went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Ezekiel 33:31; Matthew 23:3"> And having come to the other, he said likewise. And he answered and said, ‘I am going, sir,’ but he did not go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 7:29; Luke 7:37–50; Matthew 7:21"> Which of the two did the will of his father?” They said to Him, “The first.” Jesus said to them, “Assuredly I say to you, that tax collectors and harlots enter the kingdom of God before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 7:37–50; Zechariah 7:11–12; Luke 7:29–30"> For John came to you in the way of righteousness, and you did not believe him; but tax collectors and harlots believed him; but you, having seen, did not repent afterwards, that you might believe him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Wicked Tenants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–4; Mark 12:1–12; Luke 20:9–19"> “Hear another parable: There was a certain landowner who planted a vineyard and set a hedge around it, dug a winepress in it and built a tower. And he leased it to farmers and went away on a journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 20:10–19; Isaiah 5:4; Zechariah 1:3–6"> Now when the harvest time of fruits drew near, he sent his servants to the farmers, that they might collect his fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Matthew 5:12; Hebrews 11:36–37"> And the farmers took his servants, and they beat one, and killed another, and stoned another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 22:4"> Again he sent other servants, more than the first, and they did to them likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 3:16; John 3:35–36; Zephaniah 3:7"> And at last he sent to them his son, saying, ‘Surely they will respect my son.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 20:14; Genesis 37:18–20; Mark 12:7–8"> But when the farmers saw the son, they said among themselves, ‘This is the heir. Come, let us kill him, and let us gain possession of his inheritance.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 2:23; James 5:6; Luke 22:52–54"> And taking him, they cast him out of the vineyard and killed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 10:29; Mark 12:9; Luke 20:15–16"> “Therefore, when the owner of the vineyard comes, what will he do to those farmers?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Matthew 21:43; Acts 28:28"> They said to Him, “He will destroy those wicked men miserably, and lease his vineyard to other farmers, who will render to him the fruits in their seasons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Isaiah 28:16; Acts 4:11"> Jesus said to them, “Have you never read in the Scriptures:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The stone which the builders rejected</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">has become the chief cornerstone.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">This was the Lord's doing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and it is marvelous in our eyes’?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 21:41; Matthew 8:11–12; 1 Peter 2:9"> “Therefore I say to you, the kingdom of God shall be taken away from you, and it shall be given to a nation producing the fruits of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; 1 Peter 2:8; Daniel 2:34–35"> And he who falls on this stone will be broken to pieces; but on whomever it falls, it will grind him to powder.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 20:19; Luke 11:45; Matthew 12:12"> Now when the chief priests and Pharisees heard His parables, they knew that He was speaking about them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; Matthew 21:26; Luke 7:16"> And seeking to seize Him, they feared the crowds, since they considered Him to be a prophet.</span>
</div>
</div>
