## Judas Hangs Himself

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; Luke 22:47–48; Job 20:15–29"> Then Judas, the one betraying Him, seeing that He was condemned, feeling regret, returned the thirty silver coins to the chief priests and elders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:30; Titus 1:16; Acts 18:15–17"> saying, “I have sinned by betraying innocent blood.” And they said, “What is that to us? You see to it!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 1:18–19; 2 Samuel 17:23; 1 Kings 16:18"> And throwing the silver coins in the sanctuary, he departed; and going away, he hanged himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:7–9; Deuteronomy 23:18; Isaiah 61:8"> But the chief priests, taking the silver coins said, “It is not lawful to put them into the temple treasury, since they are the price of blood.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And taking counsel, they bought with them the potter's field, to bury strangers in.</span>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 23:2–3; Mark 15:2–5; Matthew 26:25"> Now Jesus stood before the governor. And the governor asked Him, saying, “Are You the King of the Jews?”<br />So Jesus said to him, “It is as you say.”</span>

## Barabbas or Jesus?

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:38–40; Luke 23:16; John 19:16"> Now at the feast, the governor was accustomed to release to the crowd one prisoner whom they wished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 15:7; Acts 3:14; Luke 23:25"> And they had then a notorious prisoner called Barabbas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 19:15; 1 Kings 18:21; Mark 15:9–12"> Therefore when they had assembled, Pilate said to them, “Whom do you wish that I should release to you, Barabbas, or Jesus who is called Christ?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Acts 5:17; Mark 15:10"> For he knew that they had handed Him over because of envy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Proverbs 29:1; Matthew 27:24"> Now as he was sitting on the judgment seat, his wife sent to him, saying, “Have nothing to do with that just Man, for I have suffered many things today in a dream because of Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:14–15; John 19:15–16; John 18:40"> But the chief priests and elders persuaded the crowds that they should ask for Barabbas, but they should destroy Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The governor answered and said to them, “Which of the two do you wish me to release to you?”<br />They said, “Barabbas!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–3; Isaiah 49:7; Acts 13:38"> Pilate said to them, “What then shall I do with Jesus who is called Christ?”<br />They all said to him, “Let Him be crucified!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:18–19; Acts 7:57; Acts 23:12–15"> Then the governor said, “Why, what evil did He do?” But they cried out all the more, saying, “Let Him be crucified!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:4; Psalms 26:6; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> And Pilate, seeing that nothing was being gained, but rather a riot was starting, taking water, he washed his hands clean in front of the crowd, saying, “I am innocent of the blood of this just Man. You see to it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 2:19; Acts 5:28; Acts 7:52"> And all the people answered and said, “His blood be on us and on our children.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; John 19:1; Mark 15:15"> Then he released Barabbas to them; and having scourged Jesus, he delivered Him to be crucified.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Soldiers Mock Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 18:28; John 18:33; Acts 10:1"> Then the soldiers of the governor, having taken Jesus to the Praetorium, gathered the whole garrison around Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 15:17; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> And having stripped Him, they put a scarlet robe on Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 20:19; Psalms 69:19–20"> When they had twisted a crown of thorns, they put it on His head, and a reed in His right hand. And kneeling before Him, they mocked Him, saying, “Hail, King of the Jews!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 53:7"> And spitting on Him, they took the reed and were striking Him on His head.</span>

</div>
</div>

## The Crucifixion of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:21; Matthew 16:24; Luke 23:26"> And going out, they found a man of Cyrene, Simon by name. Him they pressed into service that he might carry His cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 19:17–19; Mark 15:22–32; Mark 15:34"> And coming to a place called Golgotha, that is to say, Place of a Skull,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Mark 15:23; Matthew 27:48"> they gave Him sour wine mingled with gall to drink. But having tasted it, He would not drink.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 22:18; Mark 15:24–32; Luke 23:34"> And having crucified Him, they divided His clothes among themselves, casting lots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:44; Mark 15:39"> And sitting down, they kept watch over Him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 23:38; John 19:19–22; Mark 15:26"> And they put up over His head the crime having been written against Him:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">THIS IS JESUS THE KING OF THE JEWS.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43; Isaiah 53:12"> Then they crucified two thieves with Him, one on His right, and another on His left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 109:25; Lamentations 1:12; Psalms 69:7–12"> And those passing by were blaspheming Him, shaking their heads</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 26:61; Matthew 4:6; Matthew 4:3"> and saying, “You who destroy the temple and build it in three days, save Yourself! If You are the Son of God, come down from the cross.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Isaiah 28:22; Psalms 22:12–13"> Likewise the chief priests also, mocking Him with the scribes and elders, and Pharisees, said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 1:49; Matthew 27:37; Acts 4:14"> “He saved others; Himself He cannot save. If He is the King of Israel, let Him now come down from the cross, and we will believe on Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 22:8; John 10:36; Isaiah 37:10"> He trusted in God; let Him deliver Him now, if He will have Him; for He said, ‘I am the Son of God.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:38; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:39–43"> Even the thieves who were crucified with Him were reviling Him with the same insult.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Amos 8:9; Mark 15:33–34; Luke 23:44–45"> Now from the sixth hour until the ninth hour, darkness came upon all the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 53:10"> And about the ninth hour Jesus cried out with a loud voice, saying, “Eli, Eli, lima sabachthani?” that is, “My God, My God, why have You forsaken Me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Mark 15:35–36; Matthew 11:14"> Some of those standing by, when they heard this, said, “This Man is calling Elijah!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36"> Immediately one of them ran and took a sponge, and filled it with sour wine and put it around a reed, and offered it to Him to drink.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> But the rest said, “Leave Him alone; let us see if Elijah is coming to save Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:30; Isaiah 53:9–12; Psalms 22:14–15"> But Jesus, again crying out with a loud voice, released His spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Mark 15:38; Hebrews 6:19"> And behold, the veil of the temple was torn in two from top to bottom; and the earth was shaken, and the rocks were split,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; 1 Thessalonians 4:14; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> and the tombs were opened; and many bodies of the saints who had passed away were raised;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Isaiah 48:2; Revelation 21:2"> and coming forth out of the tombs after His resurrection, they entered into the holy city and appeared to many.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:36; Mark 15:39; Matthew 27:43"> So when the centurion and those with him guarding Jesus, when they saw the earthquake and the things that had happened, they were greatly afraid, saying, “Truly this was the Son of God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Luke 23:48–49; John 19:25–27"> And there were many women there observing from a distance, who followed Jesus from Galilee, ministering to Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 19:25; Mark 15:47–1; Matthew 28:1"> among whom were Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James and Joses, and the mother of the sons of Zebedee.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Burial of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Mark 15:42–47; Luke 23:50–56"> Now when evening had come, there came a rich man from Arimathea, named Joseph, who himself had also become a disciple of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 23:52–53; Mark 15:44–46"> This man went to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus. Then Pilate commanded the body to be given to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> And taking the body, Joseph wrapped it up in a clean linen cloth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 28:2; Matthew 27:66"> and placed it in his new tomb which he had cut in the rock; and having rolled a large stone against the door of the tomb, he departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Matthew 28:1; Matthew 27:56"> And Mary Magdalene was there, and the other Mary, sitting opposite the grave.</span>
</div>

## The Guard at the Tomb

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 15:42; John 19:14; John 19:42"> On the next day, which is after the Preparation Day, the chief priests and Pharisees were gathered together to Pilate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 9:22; Matthew 16:21; John 2:19"> saying, “Sir, we remember how that deceiver said while He was still alive, ‘After three days I will rise.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Matthew 28:13; Matthew 12:45"> Therefore command that the grave be made secure until the third day, lest His disciples come by night and steal Him, and say to the people, ‘He was raised from the dead.’ So the last deception will be worse than the first.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Matthew 27:66; Matthew 28:11–15; Proverbs 21:30"> Pilate said to them, “You have a guard; go make it as secure as you know how.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Daniel 6:17; Matthew 27:60; 2 Timothy 2:19"> So they went and secured the grave, sealing the stone, with the guard.</span>
</div>
