## Jesus, the Author and Perfecter of Our Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:7; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Philippians 2:16"> So therefore we also, since we are surrounded by so great a cloud of witnesses, let us lay aside every impediment, and the sin which so easily ensnares us, and let us run with endurance the race that is set before us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 7:7; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 Peter 2:23–24"> looking unto Jesus, the author and perfecter of faith, who for the joy that was set before Him endured the cross, despising the shame, and has sat down at the right hand of the throne of God.</span>
</div>

## Do Not Grow Weary

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Revelation 2:3"> For consider Him who endured such hostility from sinners against Himself, lest you be weary and discouraged in your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Revelation 2:13; Hebrews 10:32–34"> You have not yet resisted to the point of bloodshed, struggling against sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 3:11–12; Job 5:17–18; Revelation 3:19"> And you have forgotten the exhortation which speaks to you as sons:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“My son, do not despise the discipline of the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor be discouraged when you are reproved;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 3:12; Psalms 119:75"> <span className="pl pl-1">For whom the Lord loves He disciplines,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and scourges every son whom He receives.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 19:18; Deuteronomy 8:5; Proverbs 13:24"> It is for discipline that you endure; God deals with you as with sons. For what son is there whom a father does not discipline?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 12:6; 1 Peter 5:9–10; Psalms 73:1"> But if you are without discipline, of which all have become partakers, then you are illegitimate children and not sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Numbers 27:16; Isaiah 42:5"> Furthermore, we have had human fathers who disciplined us, and we respected them. Shall we not much more readily be in subjection to the Father of spirits and live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 1:4; Leviticus 11:44–45; Titus 2:14"> For they indeed disciplined us for a few days, as seemed best to them, but He disciplines us for our good, so that we may partake of His holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 5:3–5; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Hebrews 12:5–6"> Now no discipline seems to be joyful for the present, but painful; nevertheless, later it yields the peaceable fruit of righteousness to those having been trained by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; Hebrews 12:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:14"> Therefore strengthen the hands which are weakened, and the feeble knees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 4:26–27; Isaiah 35:3; Galatians 6:1"> and make straight paths for your feet, so that the lame may not be turned aside, but rather be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Peter 1:15–16; 1 Thessalonians 4:7"> Pursue peace and holiness with all people, without which no one shall see the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:12; Deuteronomy 29:18; Galatians 5:4"> looking diligently lest anyone fall from the grace of God; lest any root of bitterness growing up cause trouble, and through this many be defiled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 25:31–34; 1 Corinthians 6:15–20; Revelation 22:15"> lest there be any fornicator or profane person like Esau, who in exchange for one meal gave up his birthright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; Matthew 25:11–12; Matthew 7:23"> For indeed you know that afterward, wishing to inherit the blessing, he was rejected, for he found no place for repentance, having sought it out diligently with tears.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Glorious Company

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:11; Exodus 24:17; Exodus 20:18"> For you have not come to the mountain that may be touched and that burned with fire, and to blackness and darkness and to a whirlwind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:16; 1 Corinthians 15:52; Deuteronomy 4:12"> and to a sound of a trumpet and to a voice of words, which those who heard it begged that no further word be spoken to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 19:12–13; Exodus 19:16; Galatians 3:10"> For they could not bear that which was commanded: “And if so much as a beast touches the mountain, it shall be stoned.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 19:16; Deuteronomy 9:19; Isaiah 6:3–5"> And so fearful was the spectacle, that Moses said, “I am greatly afraid and trembling.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Galatians 4:26; Philippians 3:20; Revelation 14:1"> But you have come to Zion, to the Mountain and city of the living God, the heavenly Jerusalem, and to myriads of angels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 1:18; Hebrews 11:40; Luke 10:20"> to the general assembly and church of the firstborn having been enrolled in the heavens, to God the Judge of all, and to the spirits of just men made perfect,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 9:15; Hebrews 11:28; Hebrews 11:4"> and to Jesus, the mediator of a new covenant, and to the blood of sprinkling which speaks better things than the blood of Abel.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Hear the Heavenly Voice

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Deuteronomy 30:17; Hebrews 2:1–3"> See that you do not refuse Him who speaks. For if they did not escape, having refused Him who warned them, how much more shall we not escape if we turn away from Him who warns from heaven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Haggai 2:6–7; Isaiah 13:13; Exodus 19:18"> whose voice then shook the earth; but now He has promised, saying, “Yet once more I shake not only the earth, but also the heaven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:31; Matthew 24:35; 2 Peter 3:10–11"> Now the phrase, “Yet once more,” signifies the removal of the things being shaken, as having been made, so that the things which cannot be shaken may remain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 12:1–2; Hebrews 13:15; Daniel 2:44"> Therefore, since we are receiving an unshakable kingdom, let us have grace, through which we serve God acceptably with reverence and godly fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Deuteronomy 9:3; Hebrews 10:27"> For our God is a consuming fire.</span>
</div>
</div>
