## Do Not Neglect Salvation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:15; 2 Peter 1:12–13; Proverbs 7:1–2"> On account of this we must give the more earnest heed to the things we have heard, lest we drift away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:53; Galatians 3:19; Hebrews 10:28"> For if the word which was spoken through angels was confirmed, and every transgression and disobedience received a just retribution,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28–29; Hebrews 12:25; Hebrews 5:9"> how shall we escape if we neglect so great a salvation, which in the beginning was spoken by the Lord, and was confirmed to us by those who heard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:3; Ephesians 1:5; Mark 16:20"> God also bearing witness both with signs and wonders, with various miracles, and gifts of the Holy Spirit, according to His own will?</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Son Made Lower than Angels

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; 2 Peter 3:13; Hebrews 6:5"> For He did not subject the world which is to come, concerning which we speak, to angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Psalms 144:3; Job 7:17–18"> But someone somewhere testified, saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“What is man that You are mindful of him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or the son of man that You visit him?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 8:5; Philippians 2:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">You made him a little lower than the angels,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You crowned him with glory and honor,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 13:3; 1 Corinthians 15:27; Psalms 8:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have subjected all things under his feet.”</span><br />For in subjecting to him all things, He left nothing not subjected to him. But now we do not yet see all things having been subjected to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 2:7–9; Revelation 5:9; 1 John 4:9–10"> But we see Jesus, who was made a little lower than the angels, through the suffering of death, having been crowned with glory and honor, so that by the grace of God, He might taste death for everyone.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Bringing Many Sons to Glory

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Acts 5:31; Luke 24:46"> For it was fitting for Him, on account of whom are all things and through whom are all things, in bringing many sons to glory, to perfect the author of their salvation through sufferings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:10; Hebrews 13:12; Hebrews 10:14"> For both He who sanctifies and those who are being sanctified are all of one; for which reason He is not ashamed to call them brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:22; Psalms 40:10; John 18:20"> saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will declare Your name to My brothers;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the midst of the congregation I will sing praise to You.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17–18; Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 91:2"> And again:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will put My trust in Him.”</span><br />And again:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold I and the children whom God has given Me.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:10; Romans 8:3; Colossians 2:15"> Therefore since the children have partaken of flesh and blood, He Himself likewise shared in the same things, in order that through death He might destroy the one having the power of death, that is, the devil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 8:15; 2 Timothy 1:7; Luke 1:74–75"> and that He might set free these, as many as by fear of death through all of their lives, were subject to bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Galatians 3:16; Romans 4:16–25"> For indeed He does not take hold of angels, but He does take hold of the seed of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14–2; Philippians 2:7–8; Hebrews 2:14"> Therefore He was obligated to become like His brothers in all respects, in order that He might become a merciful and faithful High Priest in things pertaining to God, in order that He might make propitiation for the sins of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Hebrews 4:15–16; Jude 1:24"> For in that which He Himself has suffered, being tempted, He is able to help those who are tempted.</span>
</div>
</div>
