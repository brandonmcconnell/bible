## Jesus Christ Still the Same

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 4:20–1; 1 Peter 3:8; 1 Peter 1:22"> Let brotherly love continue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 25:35; Genesis 18:1–3; 1 Peter 4:9"> Do not forget hospitality, for by this some have entertained angels without knowing it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 25:36; Hebrews 10:34; Romans 12:15"> Remember the prisoners as if you were bound with them, and those who are ill treated, as you yourselves are in the body also.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Proverbs 5:15–23; Galatians 5:19"> Marriage is honorable among all, and the bed undefiled; but fornicators and adulterers God will judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:8; Joshua 1:5; Deuteronomy 31:6"> Let your way of life be without loving money, being satisfied with what you have. For He Himself has said, “By no means shall I desert you, nor in any way shall I forsake you;”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 118:6–9; Isaiah 41:10; Romans 8:31"> so that being confident, we may say:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The Lord is my helper;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will not fear.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">What can man do to me?”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Hebrews 13:17; Philippians 3:17"> Remember your leaders, who spoke the word of God to you, of whom considering the result of their conduct, imitate their faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Malachi 3:6; James 1:17; Revelation 1:8"> Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:14; 1 Timothy 6:3–5; Ephesians 5:6"> Do not be carried away by various and strange doctrines. For it is good for the heart to be established by grace, not by foods, by which those having walked were not profited.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:7–8; 1 Corinthians 10:20; 1 Corinthians 9:13"> We have an altar from which those who serve the tabernacle have no right to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 16:27; Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 9:11"> For of the animals, whose blood is brought into the Holies by the high priest as an offering for sin, the bodies of these animals are burned outside the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12–14; John 19:17–18; Acts 7:58"> Therefore Jesus also, so that He might sanctify the people through His own blood, suffered outside the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 11:26; 1 Peter 4:14–16; Acts 5:41"> Therefore let us go out to Him, outside the camp, bearing His reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; Colossians 3:1–3; Hebrews 11:12–16"> For we have no permanent city, but we seek the city which is to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 107:21–22; Psalms 50:14; Romans 12:1"> Therefore through Him let us continually offer up a sacrifice of praise to God, that is, the fruit of our lips, confessing to His name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:13; Galatians 6:10; Romans 12:13"> But do not forget the doing of good and generosity, for with such sacrifices God is well pleased.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; 1 Timothy 5:17; Hebrews 13:7"> Obey your leaders, and submit to them, for they keep watch over your souls, as giving an account, in order that they may do this with joy and not groaning, for this would be unprofitable for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:25; 2 Thessalonians 3:1; Colossians 4:3"> Pray for us; for we are persuaded that we have a good conscience, in all things desiring to live commendably.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philemon 1:22; Romans 1:10–12; Romans 15:31–32"> But I especially urge you to do this, in order that I may be restored to you more quickly.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Benediction and Final Greetings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:11; Hebrews 10:22; Psalms 23:1"> Now may the God of peace, who brought up our Lord Jesus from the dead, that great Shepherd of the sheep, by the blood of the everlasting covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; 2 Thessalonians 2:17; Philippians 4:13"> make you complete in every good work to do His will, working in you what is well pleasing before Him, through Jesus Christ, to whom be glory forever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:1; 1 Peter 5:12; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> And I appeal to you, brothers, bear with this word of exhortation, for I have written to you briefly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philemon 1:1; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; 2 Timothy 1:8"> Know that our brother Timothy has been released, with whom I shall see you, if he comes quickly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 13:17; Acts 18:2"> Greet all your leaders, and all the saints. Those from Italy greet you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Colossians 4:18; 2 Timothy 4:22; Titus 3:15"> Grace be with you all. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
