## The Promise of Rest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 12:15; Hebrews 4:9; Proverbs 28:14"> Therefore let us fear, lest while the promise remains to enter into His rest, any one of you seem to have fallen short.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; Romans 10:16–17; Hebrews 11:6"> For indeed we have had the gospel preached to us just as those also; but the word which they heard did not profit those, not having been mixed with faith in those who heard it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Matthew 11:28–29; 1 Peter 1:20"> For we who have believed enter that rest, as He has said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“So I swore in My wrath,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they shall not enter My rest,”</span><br />Although His works were finished from the foundation of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Exodus 31:17; Genesis 2:1–2"> For He has said somewhere concerning the seventh day thus, “And God rested on the seventh day from all His works;”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Hebrews 4:3; Hebrews 3:11"> and again in this place: “They shall not enter My rest.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Hebrews 3:18–19; Acts 13:46–47"> Therefore since it remains for some to enter into it, and those formerly having the gospel preached to them did not enter on account of disobedience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:7; Hebrews 3:7–8; Hebrews 3:15"> again He appoints a certain day, saying in David, “Today,” after such a time, just as it has been said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Today, if you will hear His voice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">do not harden your hearts.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:15; Joshua 22:4; Deuteronomy 12:9"> For if Joshua had given them rest, then He would not have spoken afterward of another day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10; Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:14–17"> Therefore there remains a Sabbath rest for the people of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Hebrews 4:3–4; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> For he who has entered into His rest also rested himself from his works, as God did from His own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:28–30; Luke 13:24; Hebrews 3:18–1"> Therefore let us be diligent to enter into that rest, lest anyone fall in the same example of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:29; Isaiah 55:11; Ephesians 6:17"> For the word of God is living and active, and sharper than any two-edged sword, piercing even to the division of soul and spirit, and of joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intentions of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:5; Psalms 33:13–15; Proverbs 15:3"> And there is no creature hidden from His sight, but all things are naked and open to His eyes, to whom we must give account.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus the Great High Priest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 9:12"> Therefore having a great High Priest who has passed through the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us hold fast our confession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17–18; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Isaiah 53:4–5"> For we do not have a High Priest who cannot sympathize with our weaknesses, but having been tempted in all respects in quite the same way as we are, yet without sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:19–23"> Therefore let us come boldly to the throne of grace, so that we may receive mercy and find grace to help in time of need.</span>
</div>
