## Christ's Sacrifice Once for All

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Hebrews 9:11; Colossians 2:17"> For the law, having a shadow of the good things to come, and not the image itself of the things, can never with the same sacrifices, which they offer continually every year, make those approaching perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Micah 7:19; Psalms 103:12"> For then would they not have ceased to be offered? For the worshipers, once purified, would have had no more consciousness of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 9:7; Exodus 30:10; Leviticus 23:27–28"> But in them there is a reminder of sins every year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:11; Isaiah 1:11–15; Micah 6:6–8"> For it is not possible for the blood of bulls and goats to take away sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:6–8; Isaiah 1:11; Isaiah 7:14"> Therefore, when He entered into the world, He said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sacrifice and offering You did not desire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but a body You have prepared for Me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 1:10; Matthew 3:17; Psalms 147:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">In burnt offerings and sacrifices for sin</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You had no pleasure.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 40:7–8; John 4:34; John 6:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I said, ‘Behold, I have come—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">In the volume of the book it is written of Me—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">To do Your will, O God.’ ”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5; Mark 12:33"> Earlier saying, “Sacrifice and offering, burnt offerings, and sacrifices for sin You did not desire, nor had pleasure in them” (which are offered according to the law),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 7:18–19; Hebrews 12:27–28; Hebrews 10:7"> then He has said, “Behold, I have come to do Your will, O God.” He takes away the first in order that He may establish the second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:14; Hebrews 10:12; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> By which will we are sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 5:1; Numbers 28:3; Hebrews 10:4"> And every priest stands ministering daily and offering repeatedly the same sacrifices, which are never able to take away sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Colossians 3:1; Romans 8:34"> But He Himself, having offered one sacrifice for sins forever, He sat down at the right hand of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:13; Psalms 110:1; Luke 20:43"> from that time waiting till His enemies are placed as a footstool for His feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:1; Hebrews 9:14"> For by one offering He has perfected forever those who are being sanctified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 3:6"> But the Holy Spirit also witnesses to us; for after He had said before,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33–34; Hebrews 8:8–12; Romans 11:27"> “This is the covenant that I will make with them after those days, says the Lord. I will put My laws on their hearts, and I will inscribe them on their minds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Hebrews 8:12"> and their sins and their lawless deeds I shall by no means remember any longer.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 10:2; Hebrews 10:14"> Now where there is remission of these, there is no longer any offering for sin.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Hold Fast Your Confession

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 4:16"> Therefore, brothers, having boldness to enter the Holiest by the blood of Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 14:6; John 10:9; Hebrews 9:3"> by a new and living way which He inaugurated for us, through the veil, that is, through His flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 4:14–16; Hebrews 8:1"> and having a High Priest over the house of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25; John 3:5; Ephesians 5:26"> let us approach with a true heart in full assurance of faith, having our hearts sprinkled from an evil conscience, and our bodies having been washed with pure water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:24; 1 Corinthians 1:9; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> Let us hold fast the confession of our hope without wavering, for He who promised is faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; 1 John 3:18; Hebrews 13:1"> And let us consider one another for the stirring up of love and of good works,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 2:42; 1 Thessalonians 5:11; Matthew 18:20"> not forsaking the assembling of ourselves, just as is the custom for some, but exhorting one another, and all the more as you see the Day drawing near.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Just Shall Live by Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 2:20–22; Hebrews 6:4–6; John 9:41"> For if we sin willfully after receiving the knowledge of the truth, there no longer remains a sacrifice for sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Hebrews 2:3; Zephaniah 1:18"> but a certain fearful expectation of judgment, and fiery zeal being about to devour the adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:2; Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:2–13"> Anyone disregarding the law of Moses dies without compassions on the testimony of two or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 6:6; Ephesians 4:30; Isaiah 63:10"> By how much worse punishment, do you think, will he be deemed worthy who has trampled the Son of God underfoot, and has regarded as common the blood of the covenant, by which he was sanctified, and has insulted the Spirit of grace?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35–36; Romans 12:19; Nahum 1:2"> For we know Him who said, “Vengeance is Mine. I will repay,” says the Lord. And again, “The Lord will judge His people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; Luke 12:5; Hebrews 12:29"> It is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 6:4; Hebrews 12:4; 2 Timothy 2:3–13"> But remember the former days, in which after you were enlightened, you endured a great conflict of sufferings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:9; Philippians 4:14; Hebrews 11:36"> in part being exposed publicly, both to reproaches and to afflictions, and in part having become partners of those treated in this way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:16; Matthew 5:11–12"> For you sympathized with me in my chains, and you received the plunder of your possessions with joy, knowing that you have for yourselves a better and enduring possession in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Galatians 6:8–10; Hebrews 3:6"> Therefore do not cast away your confidence, which has a great recompense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 12:1; Psalms 37:7; James 5:7–11"> For you have need of endurance, so that having done the will of God, you may receive the promise:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="James 5:7–9; Habakkuk 2:3–4; Luke 18:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">“For yet a little while,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and He who is coming will come and will not delay.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Galatians 3:11; Habakkuk 2:4; Romans 1:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But the just shall live by faith,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and if he withdraws,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">My soul has no pleasure in him.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 John 5:5; 1 Peter 1:5; Mark 16:16"> But we are not of those shrinking back to destruction, but of faith, to the saving of the soul.</span>
</div>
</div>
