## Jesus Greater than Moses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Philippians 3:14; Hebrews 4:14–15"> Therefore, holy brothers, partakers of the heavenly calling, consider the Apostle and High Priest of our confession, Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 12:7; 1 Timothy 3:15; Ephesians 2:22"> who was faithful to Him who appointed Him, as Moses also was faithful in all His house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9; 1 Peter 2:5–7; Zechariah 6:12–13"> For He has been counted worthy of more honor than Moses, inasmuch as He who built the house has more honor than the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 3:3; Hebrews 1:2; Esther 2:10"> For every house is built by someone, but He who built all things is God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 12:7; Hebrews 3:2; Exodus 14:31"> And Moses indeed was faithful in all His house as a servant, for a testimony of the things which would be spoken,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Hebrews 10:35"> but Christ was faithful as a Son over His own house, whose house we are if we hold fast the confidence and the boast of hope firm until the end.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Rest for the People of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:7–11; Hebrews 3:15; Isaiah 55:3"> Therefore, just as the Holy Spirit says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Today, if you will hear His voice,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; Exodus 17:7; Zechariah 7:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the day of testing in the wilderness,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Deuteronomy 4:9; Exodus 19:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Where your fathers tested Me, they tried Me, and saw My works forty years.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 95:10; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Psalms 67:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore I was indignant with that generation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and said, ‘They go astray in their heart, and they did not know My ways.’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:34–35; Hebrews 4:3; Numbers 32:10–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I swore in My wrath,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘They shall not enter My rest.’ ”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 17:9"> Take heed, brothers, lest there be in any one of you a wicked heart of unbelief in departing from the living God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Hebrews 10:24–25; 2 Timothy 4:2"> but encourage yourselves every day, while it is called “Today,” lest any of you be hardened by the deceitfulness of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 3:6; Hebrews 6:11; Hebrews 12:10"> For we have become partakers of Christ, if indeed we hold fast the beginning of the assurance firm until the end,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7–8; Psalms 95:7; Hebrews 10:38"> while it is said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Today, if you hear His voice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 14:30; Numbers 26:65"> For some, having heard, did provoke: but not all who came out of Egypt through Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:29; Numbers 26:64–65; Numbers 14:32–33"> And with whom was He indignant those forty years? Was it not with those who sinned, whose carcasses fell in the wilderness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Hebrews 4:6; Deuteronomy 1:34–35"> And to whom did He swear that they would not enter into His rest, except to those who disobeyed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jude 1:5; John 3:36; Mark 16:16"> And we see that they were not able to enter on account of unbelief.</span>
</div>
</div>
