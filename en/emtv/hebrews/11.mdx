## By Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:7; 2 Corinthians 4:18; Romans 8:24–25"> Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39; Hebrews 11:4"> For by it the elders obtained witness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 33:6; 2 Peter 3:5; John 1:3"> By faith we understand that time itself was framed by a command of God, so that the things which are seen were not made of things which are visible.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 12:24; Genesis 4:3–5; 1 John 3:11–12"> By faith Abel offered to God a more excellent sacrifice than Cain, through which he obtained witness that he was righteous, God testifying concerning his gifts; and through it, although he died, he still speaks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 5:21–24; Jude 1:14; Hebrews 11:6"> By faith Enoch was translated so as not to see death, “and he was not found, because God translated him”; for before his translation he had obtained witness that he was pleasing to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 8:17; Jeremiah 29:13–14; Matthew 6:33"> And without faith it is impossible to please God, for it is necessary for the one approaching God to believe that He is, and that He becomes a rewarder of those who diligently seek Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 6:13–1; 1 Peter 3:20; Ezekiel 14:14"> By faith Noah, having being warned by God concerning the things not yet seen, being reverent, prepared an ark for the salvation of his household, through which he condemned the world and became an heir of the righteousness which is according to faith.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Faith of Abraham

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 12:1–4; Acts 7:2–4; Matthew 7:24–25"> By faith Abraham obeyed when he was called to go forth to the place which he was about to receive as an inheritance. And he went out, not knowing where he was going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 12:8; Genesis 13:18; Genesis 13:3"> By faith he migrated into a land of promise as in a foreign land, dwelling in tents with Isaac and Jacob, the fellow heirs of the same promise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 13:14; Philippians 3:20; Hebrews 12:22"> for he waited for the city having foundations, whose builder and maker is God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 4:20–21; Hebrews 10:23; Genesis 18:11–14"> By faith Sarah herself also received power for conception of seed, and she bore a child past the normal age, because she regarded Him faithful who had promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 32:12; Genesis 15:5"> Therefore even from one man they were begotten, and him as good as dead, just as the stars of the sky in multitude, and innumerable as the sand by the seashore.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Heavenly Hope

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 23:4; Psalms 39:12; Hebrews 11:27"> These all died according to faith, not having received the promises, but having seen them afar off and welcoming them, and confessing that they were strangers and pilgrims on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:18–7; Hebrews 11:16; Hebrews 13:14"> For those saying such things make evident that they seek a fatherland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 24:6–8; Genesis 12:10; Genesis 32:9–11"> And indeed if they had remembered that land from which they went out, they would have had opportunity to turn back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 13:14; Hebrews 11:10; Philippians 3:20"> But now they desire a better land, that is, a heavenly one. Therefore God is not ashamed to be called their God, for He has prepared a city for them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Faith of the Patriarchs

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 22:16; Genesis 22:1–12; James 2:21–24"> By faith Abraham, when he was tested, offered up Isaac, and he that received the promises offered up his only begotten son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 21:12; Romans 9:7; Genesis 17:19"> of whom it was spoken, “In Isaac your seed shall be called,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 4:17–21; Matthew 9:28; Ephesians 3:20"> considering that even from the dead, God was able to raise him up, from which he also received him in a figurative sense.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 27:27–40; Genesis 28:2–3"> By faith Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau concerning things to come.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 47:31–1; Genesis 48:5–22"> By faith Jacob, when he was dying, blessed each of the sons of Joseph, and worshiped, leaning on the top of his staff.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 13:19; Genesis 50:24–25; Joshua 24:32"> By faith Joseph, when he was dying, remembered concerning the exodus of the sons of Israel, and gave orders concerning his bones.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Faith of Moses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Exodus 1:16; Hebrews 13:6"> By faith Moses, when he was born, was hidden for three months by his parents, because they saw a beautiful child; and they did not fear the edict of the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 2:10–11; Acts 7:21–24"> By faith Moses, when he was grown up, refused to be called the son of Pharaoh's daughter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 84:10; 2 Timothy 2:3–10; Job 36:21"> choosing rather to be mistreated with the people of God, than to enjoy the pleasures of sin for a season,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Hebrews 11:6; 1 Peter 4:14"> regarding the reproach of Christ greater riches than the treasures of Egypt; for he was looking to the reward.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:18; Hebrews 11:13; Hebrews 11:1"> By faith he left Egypt, not fearing the anger of the king; for he endured as seeing the Unseen One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 12:21–30; Hebrews 9:19; Hebrews 12:24"> By faith he kept the Passover and the sprinkling of the blood, lest he who destroyed the firstborn should touch them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 14:13–21; Psalms 106:9–11; Psalms 78:13"> By faith they passed through the Red Sea as through dry land, whereas the Egyptians, attempting to do so, were drowned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 6:3–20; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5"> By faith the walls of Jericho fell down, having been encircled for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 2:25; Joshua 6:22–25; Matthew 1:5"> By faith Rahab the harlot did not perish with those who did not believe, when she had received the spies with peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:1; 1 Samuel 1:20; 1 Samuel 16:13"> And what more should I say? For the time would fail me to tell of Gideon, both Barak and Samson and Jephthah, both David and Samuel and the prophets:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 144:1–2; Hebrews 6:12–15; Hebrews 10:36"> who through faith overcame kingdoms, worked righteousness, obtained promises, stopped the mouths of lions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Kings 6:16–18; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; 1 Samuel 17:51–52"> quenched the power of fire, escaped the edge of the sword, out of weakness were made strong, became mighty in battle, turned to flight the armies of foreigners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 4:27–37; 1 Kings 17:22–24; Philippians 3:11"> Women received their dead back by resurrection.<br />And others were tortured, not accepting deliverance, in order that they might obtain a better resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:2; Genesis 39:20; Jeremiah 37:15–21"> Still others took a trial of mockings and scourgings, yes, and of chains and imprisonment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:21; Jeremiah 26:23; 1 Kings 19:10"> They were stoned, they were sawn in two, they were tried, they were slain, being murdered with the sword. They went about in sheepskins and goatskins, being in want, being afflicted, being ill treated,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 18:4; 1 Samuel 22:1; 1 Kings 19:9"> of whom the world was not worthy, wandering in deserts and mountains and caves, and the holes of the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 10:23–24; Hebrews 11:13; Hebrews 11:2"> And all these, having obtained witness through their faith, did not receive the promise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 6:11; Hebrews 8:6; Hebrews 10:11–14"> God having provided something better for us, that they should not be made perfect apart from us.</span>
</div>
</div>
