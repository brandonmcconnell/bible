## God's Supreme Revelation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 1:20–21; Joel 2:28; Numbers 12:6–8"> God, who at various times and in various ways spoke in time past to the fathers by the prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Colossians 1:16–18"> has in these last days spoken to us by His Son, whom He has appointed heir of all things, through whom also He made the worlds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; Revelation 4:11; John 14:9–10"> who being the radiance of His glory and the exact expression of His substance, and upholding all things by the word of His power, when He had by Himself purged our sins, sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:21; Philippians 2:9–11; 1 Peter 3:22"> having become so much better than the angels, as He has inherited a more excellent name than they.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Son Exalted Above Angels

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; Acts 13:33; Hebrews 5:5"> For to which of the angels did He ever say:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are My Son,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">today I have begotten You”?</span><br />And again:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will be a Father to Him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and He shall be a Son to Me”?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:7; Colossians 1:18; Deuteronomy 32:43"> But when He again brings the firstborn into the world, He says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Let all the angels of God worship Him.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 104:4; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 6:17"> And of the angels He says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He who makes His angels spirits</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and His ministers flames of fire.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 45:6–7; Isaiah 9:6–7; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> But of the Son He says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Your throne, O God, is forever and ever;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the scepter of righteousness is the scepter of Your Kingdom.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 45:7; Psalms 23:5; Isaiah 61:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have loved righteousness and hated lawlessness;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">therefore God, Your God, has anointed You</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with the oil of gladness above Your companions.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 102:25–27; Genesis 1:1; John 1:1–3"> And:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You, Lord, in the beginning laid the foundation of the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the heavens are the work of Your hands.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Mark 13:31; Luke 21:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">They shall perish, but You continue;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they all shall grow old like a garment;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 102:26–27; Hebrews 13:8; John 8:58"> <span className="pl pl-1">and like a cloak You will roll them up, and they shall be changed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But You are the same, and Your years shall not fail.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Mark 12:36; Matthew 22:44"> But to which of the angels has He ever said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sit at My right hand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">till I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet”?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 34:7; Psalms 103:20–21; Psalms 91:11–12"> Are they not all ministering spirits being sent out to minister for the sake of those who shall be heirs of salvation?</span>
</div>
</div>
