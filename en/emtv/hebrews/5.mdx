## Qualifications for High Priesthood

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 8:3; Hebrews 10:11; Hebrews 7:27"> For every high priest taken from among men is appointed for men in the things pertaining to God, that he may offer both gifts and sacrifices for sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 2:18; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> being able to deal gently with those who sin in ignorance and go astray, since he himself is also beset with weakness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 9:7; Hebrews 9:7; Leviticus 16:6"> On account of this he is obligated to offer sacrifices for sins, just as concerning the people, so also concerning himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 28:1; Numbers 16:40; Numbers 16:5"> And no man takes this honor upon himself, but he that is called by God, just as Aaron also was.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Priest Forever

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; John 8:54; Acts 13:33"> So also Christ did not exalt Himself to become High Priest, but it was He who said to Him:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are My Son,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Today I have begotten You.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 7:21"> Just as He also says in another place:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are a priest forever</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">according to the order of Melchizedek”;</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:32–39; Luke 22:41–44; John 17:1"> who, in the days of His flesh, when He had offered up both prayers and supplications, with strong crying and tears to the One who was able to save Him from death, and was heard because of His reverence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 2:8; Hebrews 10:5–9; John 4:34"> though He was a Son, He learned obedience by the things which He suffered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:15"> And having been perfected, He became the author of eternal salvation to all who obey Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 5:5–6; Hebrews 6:20"> having been designated by God a High Priest “after the order of Melchizedek,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Acts 28:27; 2 Peter 3:16"> concerning whom we have much to say, and it is hard to explain, since you have become sluggish in hearing.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Warning Against Apostasy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 6:1; 1 Corinthians 3:1–3; 1 Peter 2:2"> For indeed, although you ought to be teachers by this time, again you have need for someone to teach you what are the elements of the beginning of the oracles of God; and you have come to need milk and not solid food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Peter 2:2; 2 Timothy 3:16"> For everyone who partakes only of milk is unacquainted with the word of righteousness, for he is an infant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14–15; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; Ephesians 1:18"> But solid food is for the mature, for those because of their practice have their senses trained for the distinguishing of both good and evil.</span>
</div>
</div>
