<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:6; Genesis 49:23; 1 Kings 22:34"> The battle went hard against Saul, and the archers overtook him; and he was greatly distressed by reason of the archers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 9:54; 2 Samuel 1:6; 2 Samuel 1:9–10"> Then Saul said to his armor bearer, “Draw your sword, and thrust me through with it, lest these uncircumcised come and thrust me through and abuse me!” But his armor bearer would not, for he was terrified. Therefore Saul took his sword and fell on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 10:5"> When his armor bearer saw that Saul was dead, he likewise fell on his sword, and died with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:17; Hosea 13:10–11; 1 Chronicles 10:6"> So Saul died with his three sons, his armor bearer, and all his men that same day together.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Leviticus 26:32; Leviticus 26:36"> When the men of Israel who were on the other side of the valley, and those who were beyond the Jordan, saw that the men of Israel fled and that Saul and his sons were dead, they abandoned the cities and fled; and the Philistines came and lived in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:25; 1 Chronicles 10:8"> On the next day, when the Philistines came to strip the slain, they found Saul and his three sons fallen on Mount Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:20; Judges 16:23–24; 1 Samuel 17:54"> They cut off his head, stripped off his armor, and sent into the land of the Philistines all around, to carry the news to the house of their idols and to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 2:13; Joshua 17:11; 1 Samuel 21:9"> They put his armor in the house of the Ashtaroth, and they fastened his body to the wall of Beth Shan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1–11; 2 Samuel 2:4"> When the inhabitants of Jabesh Gilead heard what the Philistines had done to Saul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:14; Jeremiah 34:5; 2 Samuel 2:4–7"> all the valiant men arose, went all night, and took the body of Saul and the bodies of his sons from the wall of Beth Shan; and they came to Jabesh and burned them there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:12–14; Genesis 50:10; 1 Samuel 22:6"> They took their bones and buried them under the tamarisk<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, salt cedar">a</a></sup>tree in Jabesh, and fasted seven days.</span>
