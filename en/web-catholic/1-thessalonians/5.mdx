<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:9; Matthew 24:36"> But concerning the times and the seasons, brothers, you have no need that anything be written to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; 2 Peter 3:10; Matthew 24:42–44"> For you yourselves know well that the day of the Lord comes like a thief in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 21:34–35; Matthew 24:37–39; Luke 17:26–30"> For when they are saying, “Peace and safety,” then sudden destruction will come on them, like birth pains on a pregnant woman. Then they will in no way escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:8; 2 Peter 3:10; Deuteronomy 19:6"> But you, brothers, aren’t in darkness, that the day should overtake you like a thief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Acts 26:18; John 12:36"> You are all children of light and children of the day. We don’t belong to the night, nor to darkness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 1:13; Matthew 24:42; Luke 22:46"> so then let’s not sleep, as the rest do, but let’s watch and be sober.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 13:13; 1 Corinthians 15:34; 2 Peter 2:13"> For those who sleep, sleep in the night; and those who are drunk are drunk in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:12–13; Ephesians 6:11; 1 Peter 1:13"> But since we belong to the day, let’s be sober, putting on the breastplate of faith and love, and for a helmet, the hope of salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:10; 1 Peter 2:10; 2 Timothy 2:10"> For God didn’t appoint us to wrath, but to the obtaining of salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:8–9; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 8:34"> who died for us, that, whether we wake or sleep, we should live together with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Hebrews 10:25; Romans 15:2"> Therefore exhort one another, and build each other up, even as you also do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; Hebrews 13:7; 1 Timothy 5:17–18"> But we beg you, brothers, to know those who labor among you, and are over you in the Lord and admonish you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 9:50; Hebrews 12:14; Galatians 6:6"> and to respect and honor them in love for their work’s sake.<br />Be at peace among yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Romans 14:1; Acts 20:35"> We exhort you, brothers: Admonish the disorderly; encourage the faint-hearted; support the weak; be patient toward all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Galatians 6:10; Leviticus 19:18"> See that no one returns evil for evil to anyone, but always follow after that which is good for one another and for all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Philippians 4:4; Matthew 5:12"> Always rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Colossians 4:2; Ephesians 6:18"> Pray without ceasing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:20; Philippians 4:6; Colossians 3:17"> In everything give thanks, for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus toward you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; Acts 7:51; 2 Timothy 1:6"> Don’t quench the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:3–6; 1 Corinthians 14:1; Numbers 11:25–29"> Don’t despise prophecies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Romans 12:2; Ephesians 5:10"> Test all things, and hold firmly that which is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; Philippians 4:8; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33"> Abstain from every form of evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jude 1:24; Hebrews 4:12; 1 Corinthians 1:8–9"> May the God of peace himself sanctify you completely. May your whole spirit, soul, and body be preserved blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; Numbers 23:19; 1 Corinthians 1:9"> He who calls you is faithful, who will also do it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 13:18–19; Philippians 1:19; Philemon 1:22"> Brothers, pray for us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 16:16; 1 Corinthians 16:20"> Greet all the brothers with a holy kiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 4:16; Acts 19:13; 1 Timothy 1:3"> I solemnly command you by the Lord that this letter be read to all the holy brothers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 16:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:18; Romans 16:23"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. Amen.</span>
