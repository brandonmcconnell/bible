<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:25; Colossians 4:3; 1 Corinthians 16:9"> Finally, brothers, pray for us, that the word of the Lord may spread rapidly and be glorified, even as also with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3–4; Romans 15:31; Deuteronomy 32:20"> and that we may be delivered from unreasonable and evil men; for not all have faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:18; 1 Thessalonians 5:24; 1 Corinthians 10:13"> But the Lord is faithful, who will establish you and guard you from the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Philemon 1:21; 1 Thessalonians 4:10"> We have confidence in the Lord concerning you, that you both do and will do the things we command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 119:36; 1 Chronicles 29:18; Deuteronomy 30:6"> May the Lord direct your hearts into God’s love and into the perseverance of Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:10–11; 2 Thessalonians 3:7; Romans 16:17"> Now we command you, brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you withdraw yourselves from every brother who walks in rebellion and not after the tradition which they received from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:6; 2 Thessalonians 3:9; 1 Corinthians 4:16"> For you know how you ought to imitate us. For we didn’t behave ourselves rebelliously among you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:11; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; Ephesians 4:28"> neither did we eat bread from anyone’s hand without paying for it, but in labor and travail worked night and day, that we might not burden any of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7; 1 Thessalonians 2:6; 1 Corinthians 9:4–14"> This was not because we don’t have the right, but to make ourselves an example to you, that you should imitate us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 20:4"> For even when we were with you, we commanded you this: “If anyone is not willing to work, don’t let him eat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:13; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Thessalonians 4:11"> For we hear of some who walk among you in rebellion, who don’t work at all, but are busybodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:28; 1 Thessalonians 4:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:8"> Now those who are that way, we command and exhort in the Lord Jesus Christ, that they work with quietness and eat their own bread.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 6:9–10; Hebrews 12:3; 2 Corinthians 4:1"> But you, brothers, don’t be weary in doing what is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Corinthians 5:11"> If any man doesn’t obey our word in this letter, note that man and have no company with him, to the end that he may be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Corinthians 5:5"> Don’t count him as an enemy, but admonish him as a brother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 29:11; John 16:33; Matthew 1:23"> Now may the Lord of peace himself give you peace at all times in all ways. The Lord be with you all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:21; 2 Thessalonians 1:5; Colossians 4:18"> I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand, which is the sign in every letter. This is how I write.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Romans 16:23"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen.</span>
