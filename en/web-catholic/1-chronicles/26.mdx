<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:21"> Meshelemiah had sons: Zechariah the firstborn, Jediael the second, Zebadiah the third, Jathniel the fourth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Elam the fifth, Jehohanan the sixth, and Eliehoenai the seventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 15:24; 1 Chronicles 15:18"> Obed-Edom had sons: Shemaiah the firstborn, Jehozabad the second, Joah the third, Sacar the fourth, Nethanel the fifth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 128:1"> Ammiel the sixth, Issachar the seventh, and Peullethai the eighth; for God blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:28; 1 Timothy 6:12; Judges 6:12"> Sons were also born to Shemaiah his son, who ruled over the house of their father; for they were mighty men of valor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The sons of Shemaiah: Othni, Rephael, Obed, and Elzabad, whose relatives were valiant men, Elihu, and Semachiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:6; 1 Peter 4:11; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11"> All these were of the sons of Obed-Edom with their sons and their brothers, able men in strength for the service: sixty-two of Obed-Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:14; 1 Chronicles 26:1"> Meshelemiah had sons and brothers, eighteen valiant men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2"> Also Hosah, of the children of Merari, had sons: Shimri the chief (for though he was not the firstborn, yet his father made him chief),</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Hilkiah the second, Tebaliah the third, and Zechariah the fourth. All the sons and brothers of Hosah were thirteen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8"> Of these were the divisions of the doorkeepers, even of the chief men, having offices like their brothers, to minister in the LORD’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8; 1 Chronicles 24:31; 1 Chronicles 24:5"> They cast lots, the small as well as the great, according to their fathers’ houses, for every gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The lot eastward fell to Shelemiah. Then for Zechariah his son, a wise counselor, they cast lots; and his lot came out northward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:24; 1 Chronicles 26:17"> To Obed-Edom southward; and to his sons the storehouse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:24; 1 Chronicles 26:10–12; 1 Kings 10:5"> To Shuppim and Hosah westward, by the gate of Shallecheth, at the causeway that goes up, watchman opposite watchman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 9:24"> Eastward were six Levites, northward four a day, southward four a day, and for the storehouse two and two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 23:11"> For Parbar westward, four at the causeway, and two at Parbar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 16:11"> These were the divisions of the doorkeepers; of the sons of the Korahites, and of the sons of Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:22; 1 Chronicles 28:12–19; 1 Chronicles 22:14–16"> Of the Levites, Ahijah was over the treasures of God’s house and over the treasures of the dedicated things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:7; 1 Chronicles 6:17; 1 Chronicles 29:8"> The sons of Ladan, the sons of the Gershonites belonging to Ladan, the heads of the fathers’ households belonging to Ladan the Gershonite: Jehieli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:8; Nehemiah 10:38; 1 Chronicles 26:20"> The sons of Jehieli: Zetham, and Joel his brother, over the treasures of the LORD’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 3:27; 1 Chronicles 23:12; Numbers 3:19"> Of the Amramites, of the Izharites, of the Hebronites, of the Uzzielites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:20; 1 Chronicles 23:15–16"> Shebuel the son of Gershom, the son of Moses, was ruler over the treasuries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:17–18; 1 Chronicles 23:15; Exodus 18:4"> His brothers: of Eliezer, Rehabiah his son, and Jeshaiah his son, and Joram his son, and Zichri his son, and Shelomoth his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:11; 1 Chronicles 29:2–9; 1 Chronicles 22:14"> This Shelomoth and his brothers were over all the treasuries of the dedicated things, which David the king, and the heads of the fathers’ households, the captains over thousands and hundreds, and the captains of the army, had dedicated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 12:14; Joshua 6:19; Nehemiah 10:32–34"> They dedicated some of the plunder won in battles to repair the LORD’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Samuel 17:55; 2 Samuel 10:9–14"> All that Samuel the seer, Saul the son of Kish, Abner the son of Ner, and Joab the son of Zeruiah had dedicated, whoever had dedicated anything, it was under the hand of Shelomoth and of his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:4; Nehemiah 11:16; 2 Chronicles 34:13"> Of the Izharites, Chenaniah and his sons were appointed to the outward business over Israel, for officers and judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:17; 1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> Of the Hebronites, Hashabiah and his brothers, one thousand seven hundred men of valor, had the oversight of Israel beyond the Jordan westward, for all the business of the LORD and for the service of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:19; Joshua 21:39; 1 Chronicles 29:27"> Of the Hebronites, Jerijah was the chief of the Hebronites, according to their generations by fathers’ households. They were sought for in the fortieth year of the reign of David, and mighty men of valor were found among them at Jazer of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:11; 1 Chronicles 12:37; 1 Chronicles 23:24"> His relatives, men of valor, were two thousand seven hundred, heads of fathers’ households, whom King David made overseers over the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of the Manassites, for every matter pertaining to God and for the affairs of the king.</span>
