<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 144:10; 2 Kings 5:1; 1 Samuel 14:23"> They stood in the middle of the plot, defended it, and killed the Philistines; and the LORD saved them by a great victory.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Samuel 13:23"> David was then in the stronghold, and the garrison of the Philistines was in Bethlehem at that time.</span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:23"> Behold, he was more honorable than the thirty, but he didn’t attain to the three; and David set him over his guard.</span>
