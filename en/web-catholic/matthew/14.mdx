<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Mark 8:15; Luke 9:7–9"> At that time, Herod the tetrarch heard the report concerning Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 16:14; John 10:41; Mark 8:28"> and said to his servants, “This is John the Baptizer. He is risen from the dead. That is why these powers work in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 6:17; Luke 3:19–20; Matthew 4:12"> For Herod had arrested John, bound him, and put him in prison for the sake of Herodias, his brother Philip’s wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:21; Leviticus 18:16; Mark 6:18"> For John said to him, “It is not lawful for you to have her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 11:9; Mark 6:19–20"> When he would have put him to death, he feared the multitude, because they counted him as a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 6:21–23; Genesis 40:20; Hosea 1:5–6"> But when Herod’s birthday came, the daughter of Herodias danced among them and pleased Herod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 5:3; Esther 5:6; Esther 7:2"> Therefore he promised with an oath to give her whatever she should ask.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 6:24; Proverbs 29:10; Proverbs 1:16"> She, being prompted by her mother, said, “Give me here on a platter the head of John the Baptizer.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:12–16; 1 Samuel 14:28; Mark 6:26"> The king was grieved, but for the sake of his oaths and of those who sat at the table with him, he commanded it to be given,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 17:12; Matthew 21:35–36; Luke 9:9"> and he sent and beheaded John in the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 16:6; Genesis 49:7; Ezekiel 16:3–4"> His head was brought on a platter and given to the young lady; and she brought it to her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 8:2; Matthew 27:58–61"> His disciples came, took the body, and buried it. Then they went and told Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:10–17; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 6:30–44"> Now when Jesus heard this, he withdrew from there in a boat to a deserted place apart. When the multitudes heard it, they followed him on foot from the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Matthew 9:36; Hebrews 2:17"> Jesus went out, and he saw a great multitude. He had compassion on them and healed their sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 15:23; Mark 8:3; Mark 6:35–36"> When evening had come, his disciples came to him, saying, “This place is deserted, and the hour is already late. Send the multitudes away, that they may go into the villages, and buy themselves food.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; Ecclesiastes 11:2"> But Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“They don’t need to go away. You give them something to eat.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:33–34; Mark 6:37–38; Luke 9:13"> They told him, “We only have here five loaves and two fish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He said,<span className="woj">“Bring them here to me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 8:6–7; Luke 24:30; 1 Samuel 9:13"> He commanded the multitudes to sit down on the grass; and he took the five loaves and the two fish, and looking up to heaven, he blessed, broke and gave the loaves to the disciples; and the disciples gave to the multitudes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:12; Mark 6:42–44; Matthew 16:8–10"> They all ate and were filled. They took up twelve baskets full of that which remained left over from the broken pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 9:8–11; Acts 4:4"> Those who ate were about five thousand men, in addition to women and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 6:45–51; John 6:15–21; Matthew 15:39"> Immediately Jesus made the disciples get into the boat and go ahead of him to the other side, while he sent the multitudes away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 6:46; Matthew 26:36"> After he had sent the multitudes away, he went up into the mountain by himself to pray. When evening had come, he was there alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:18; Mark 6:48; Matthew 8:24"> But the boat was now in the middle of the sea, distressed by the waves, for the wind was contrary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:8; John 6:19; Mark 6:48"> In the fourth watch of the night,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The night was equally divided into four watches, so the fourth watch is approximately 3:00 a.m. to sunrise.">a</a></sup>Jesus came to them, walking on the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 6:49–50; Revelation 1:17; Luke 1:11–12"> When the disciples saw him walking on the sea, they were troubled, saying, “It’s a ghost!” and they cried out for fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Revelation 1:17–18; John 16:33"> But immediately Jesus spoke to them, saying,<span className="woj">“Cheer up! It is I!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or, I AM!">b</a></sup><span className="woj">Don’t be afraid.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:68; Romans 12:3; Matthew 26:33–35"> Peter answered him and said, “Lord, if it is you, command me to come to you on the waters.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Mark 9:23; Acts 3:16"> He said,<span className="woj">“Come!”</span>Peter stepped down from the boat and walked on the waters to come to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 3:54–57; 2 Timothy 4:16–17; Mark 14:38"> But when he saw that the wind was strong, he was afraid, and beginning to sink, he cried out, saying, “Lord, save me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:6–8; Psalms 138:7; Genesis 22:14"> Immediately Jesus stretched out his hand, took hold of him, and said to him,<span className="woj">“You of little faith, why did you doubt?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 107:29–30; Mark 4:41; John 6:21"> When they got up into the boat, the wind ceased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:27; Psalms 2:7; Romans 1:4"> Those who were in the boat came and worshiped him, saying, “You are truly the Son of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 6:53–56; Luke 5:1; John 6:24–25"> When they had crossed over, they came to the land of Gennesaret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 4:24–25; Mark 6:55; Mark 1:28–34"> When the people of that place recognized him, they sent into all that surrounding region and brought to him all who were sick;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Mark 3:10; Exodus 28:33–43"> and they begged him that they might just touch the fringe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, tassel">c</a></sup>of his garment. As many as touched it were made whole.</span>
