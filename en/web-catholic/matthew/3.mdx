</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 1:32–34; Matthew 2:22; Mark 1:9–11"> Then Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e., the Jordan River">b</a></sup>to John, to be baptized by him.</span>
