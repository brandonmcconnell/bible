<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 39:43; John 15:14; Exodus 40:16"> The disciples went and did just as Jesus commanded them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; Mark 11:4–8; Luke 19:32–35"> and brought the donkey and the colt and laid their clothes on them; and he sat on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; John 12:13; Leviticus 23:40"> A very great multitude spread their clothes on the road. Others cut branches from the trees and spread them on the road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 118:24–26; Luke 2:14; John 12:13–15"> The multitudes who went in front of him, and those who followed, kept shouting, “Hosanna<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Hosanna” means “save us” or “help us, we pray”.">b</a></sup>to the son of David! </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 9:5; Ruth 1:19; John 12:16–19"> When he had come into Jerusalem, all the city was stirred up, saying, “Who is this?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 6:14; Luke 7:16; John 7:40"> The multitudes said, “This is the prophet, Jesus, from Nazareth of Galilee.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 2:14–17; Luke 19:45–47; Mark 11:15–18"> Jesus entered into the temple of God and drove out all of those who sold and bought in the temple, and overthrew the money changers’ tables and the seats of those who sold the doves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:11; Isaiah 56:7; Luke 19:46"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“It is written, ‘My house shall be called a house of prayer,’</span><span className="woj">but you have made it a den of robbers!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 3:1–9; Acts 10:38; Matthew 11:4–5"> The lame and the blind came to him in the temple, and he healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 21:9; Luke 19:39–40; Isaiah 26:11"> But when the chief priests and the scribes saw the wonderful things that he did, and the children who were crying in the temple and saying, “Hosanna to the son of David!” they were indignant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 8:2; Matthew 11:25; Luke 19:39–40"> and said to him, “Do you hear what these are saying?”<br />Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Yes. Did you never read, ‘Out of the mouth of children and nursing babies, you have perfected praise’?”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 11:18; Mark 11:11; Luke 24:50"> He left them and went out of the city to Bethany, and camped there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 4:2; Hebrews 4:15; Matthew 12:1"> Now in the morning, as he returned to the city, he was hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 15:6; Mark 11:14; Titus 1:16"> Seeing a fig tree by the road, he came to it and found nothing on it but leaves. He said to it,<span className="woj">“Let there be no fruit from you forever!”</span><br />Immediately the fig tree withered away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; Mark 11:20–21; James 1:10–11"> When the disciples saw it, they marveled, saying, “How did the fig tree immediately wither away?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 1:6; Matthew 17:20; Mark 11:22–23"> Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“Most certainly I tell you, if you have faith and don’t doubt, you will not only do what was done to the fig tree, but even if you told this mountain, ‘Be taken up and cast into the sea,’ it would be done.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:7; 1 John 5:14–15; Mark 11:24"> <span className="woj">All things, whatever you ask in prayer, believing, you will receive.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:7; Mark 11:27–33; 1 Chronicles 24:1–19"> When he had come into the temple, the chief priests and the elders of the people came to him as he was teaching, and said, “By what authority do you do these things? Who gave you this authority?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Colossians 4:6; Matthew 10:16"> Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“I also will ask you one question, which if you tell me, I likewise will tell you by what authority I do these things.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 3:20; Mark 1:1–11; John 3:18"> <span className="woj">The baptism of John, where was it from? From heaven or from men?”</span><br />They reasoned with themselves, saying, “If we say, ‘From heaven,’ he will ask us, ‘Why then did you not believe him?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 6:20; Matthew 14:5; John 5:35"> But if we say, ‘From men,’ we fear the multitude, for all hold John as a prophet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 1:18–22; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–10; Luke 20:7–8"> They answered Jesus, and said, “We don’t know.”<br />He also said to them,<span className="woj">“Neither will I tell you by what authority I do these things.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Matthew 20:1; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> <span className="woj">But what do you think? A man had two sons, and he came to the first, and said, ‘Son, go work today in my vineyard.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:28–32; Matthew 3:2–8; Isaiah 55:6–7"> <span className="woj">He answered, ‘I will not,’ but afterward he changed his mind, and went.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Ezekiel 33:31; Matthew 23:3"> <span className="woj">He came to the second, and said the same thing. He answered, ‘I’m going, sir,’ but he didn’t go.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 7:29; Luke 7:37–50; Matthew 7:21"> <span className="woj">Which of the two did the will of his father?”</span><br />They said to him, “The first.”<br />Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Most certainly I tell you that the tax collectors and the prostitutes are entering into God’s Kingdom before you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 7:37–50; Zechariah 7:11–12; Luke 7:29–30"> <span className="woj">For John came to you in the way of righteousness, and you didn’t believe him; but the tax collectors and the prostitutes believed him. When you saw it, you didn’t even repent afterward, that you might believe him.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–4; Mark 12:1–12; Luke 20:9–19"> <span className="woj">“Hear another parable. There was a man who was a master of a household who planted a vineyard, set a hedge about it, dug a wine press in it, built a tower, leased it out to farmers, and went into another country.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 20:10–19; Isaiah 5:4; Zechariah 1:3–6"> <span className="woj">When the season for the fruit came near, he sent his servants to the farmers to receive his fruit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Matthew 5:12; Hebrews 11:36–37"> <span className="woj">The farmers took his servants, beat one, killed another, and stoned another.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 22:4"> <span className="woj">Again, he sent other servants more than the first; and they treated them the same way.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 3:16; John 3:35–36; Zephaniah 3:7"> <span className="woj">But afterward he sent to them his son, saying, ‘They will respect my son.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 20:14; Genesis 37:18–20; Mark 12:7–8"> <span className="woj">But the farmers, when they saw the son, said among themselves, ‘This is the heir. Come, let’s kill him and seize his inheritance.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 2:23; James 5:6; Luke 22:52–54"> <span className="woj">So they took him and threw him out of the vineyard, then killed him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 10:29; Mark 12:9; Luke 20:15–16"> <span className="woj">When therefore the lord of the vineyard comes, what will he do to those farmers?”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Matthew 21:43; Acts 28:28"> They told him, “He will miserably destroy those miserable men, and will lease out the vineyard to other farmers who will give him the fruit in its season.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Isaiah 28:16; Acts 4:11"> Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Did you never read in the Scriptures,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1 woj">‘The stone which the builders rejected</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">was made the head of the corner.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1 woj">This was from the Lord.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">It is marvelous in our eyes’?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 21:41; Matthew 8:11–12; 1 Peter 2:9"> <span className="woj">“Therefore I tell you, God’s Kingdom will be taken away from you and will be given to a nation producing its fruit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; 1 Peter 2:8; Daniel 2:34–35"> <span className="woj">He who falls on this stone will be broken to pieces, but on whomever it will fall, it will scatter him as dust.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 20:19; Luke 11:45; Matthew 12:12"> When the chief priests and the Pharisees heard his parables, they perceived that he spoke about them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; Matthew 21:26; Luke 7:16"> When they sought to seize him, they feared the multitudes, because they considered him to be a prophet.</span>
