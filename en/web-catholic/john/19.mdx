<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 20:19"> So Pilate then took Jesus and flogged him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Luke 23:11; Matthew 27:27–31"> The soldiers twisted thorns into a crown and put it on his head, and dressed him in a purple garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 27:29; Matthew 26:49; Luke 1:28"> They kept saying, “Hail, King of the Jews!” and they kept slapping him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; John 19:6; Luke 23:4"> Then Pilate went out again, and said to them, “Behold, I bring him out to you, that you may know that I find no basis for a charge against him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:2; Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 7:14"> Jesus therefore came out, wearing the crown of thorns and the purple garment. Pilate said to them, “Behold, the man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:31; John 19:4; Acts 2:23"> When therefore the chief priests and the officers saw him, they shouted, saying, “Crucify! Crucify!” Pilate said to them, “Take him yourselves and crucify him, for I find no basis for a charge against him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 24:16; John 5:18; Matthew 26:63–66"> The Jews answered him, “We have a law, and by our law he ought to die, because he made himself the Son of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 19:13; Acts 14:11–19"> When therefore Pilate heard this saying, he was more afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 18:33; Psalms 38:13–15; Philippians 1:28"> He entered into the Praetorium again, and said to Jesus, “Where are you from?” But Jesus gave him no answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 5:19; John 18:39; Daniel 3:14–15"> Pilate therefore said to him, “Aren’t you speaking to me? Don’t you know that I have power to release you and have power to crucify you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 13:1; John 3:27; Acts 2:23"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“You would have no power at all against me, unless it were given to you from above. Therefore he who delivered me to you has greater sin.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 18:33–36; Mark 6:16–26"> At this, Pilate was seeking to release him, but the Jews cried out, saying, “If you release this man, you aren’t Caesar’s friend! Everyone who makes himself a king speaks against Caesar!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 82:5–7; Matthew 27:19; Amos 4:7"> When Pilate therefore heard these words, he brought Jesus out and sat down on the judgment seat at a place called “The Pavement”, but in Hebrew, “Gabbatha.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:5; Mark 15:25"> Now it was the Preparation Day of the Passover, at about the sixth hour.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“the sixth hour” would have been 6:00 a.m. according to the Roman timekeeping system, or noon for the Jewish timekeeping system in use, then.">a</a></sup>He said to the Jews, “Behold, your King!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 23:18; Acts 21:36; John 19:6"> They cried out, “Away with him! Away with him! Crucify him!” Pilate said to them, “Shall I crucify your King?” The chief priests answered, “We have no king but Caesar!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 23:24–25; Matthew 27:26–31; Mark 15:15–20"> So then he delivered him to them to be crucified. So they took Jesus and led him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:33; Luke 14:27; Luke 23:26"> He went out, bearing his cross, to the place called “The Place of a Skull”, which is called in Hebrew, “Golgotha”,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Mark 15:24–27; Galatians 3:13"> where they crucified him, and with him two others, on either side one, and Jesus in the middle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 27:37; Luke 23:38; Mark 15:26"> Pilate wrote a title also, and put it on the cross. There was written, “JESUS OF NAZARETH, THE KING OF THE JEWS.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 26:14; Revelation 16:16; Acts 22:2"> Therefore many of the Jews read this title, for the place where Jesus was crucified was near the city; and it was written in Hebrew, in Latin, and in Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:19"> The chief priests of the Jews therefore said to Pilate, “Don’t write, ‘The King of the Jews,’ but, ‘he said, “I am King of the Jews.”’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 76:10; Psalms 65:7; Proverbs 8:29"> Pilate answered, “What I have written, I have written.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 23:34; Mark 15:24; Matthew 27:35"> Then the soldiers, when they had crucified Jesus, took his garments and made four parts, to every soldier a part; and also the tunic. Now the tunic was without seam, woven from the top throughout.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 22:18; John 19:28; John 19:36–37"> Then they said to one another, “Let’s not tear it, but cast lots for it to decide whose it will be,” that the Scripture might be fulfilled, which says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“They parted my garments among them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They cast lots for my clothing.”</span>Therefore the soldiers did these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:55–56; John 20:1"> But standing by Jesus’ cross were his mother, his mother’s sister, Mary the wife of Clopas, and Mary Magdalene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 21:24; John 20:2; John 2:4"> Therefore when Jesus saw his mother, and the disciple whom he loved standing there, he said to his mother,<span className="woj">“Woman, behold, your son!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 12:48–50; John 16:32; 1 Timothy 5:2–4"> Then he said to the disciple,<span className="woj">“Behold, your mother!”</span>From that hour, the disciple took her to his own home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Psalms 22:15; John 19:30"> After this, Jesus, seeing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="NU, TR read “knowing” instead of “seeing”">b</a></sup>that all things were now finished, that the Scripture might be fulfilled, said,<span className="woj">“I am thirsty!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 19:18; Luke 23:36; Mark 15:36"> Now a vessel full of vinegar was set there; so they put a sponge full of the vinegar on hyssop, and held it at his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 17:4; Philippians 2:8; Colossians 2:14–17"> When Jesus therefore had received the vinegar, he said,<span className="woj">“It is finished!”</span>Then he bowed his head and gave up his spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:42; Mark 15:42"> Therefore the Jews, because it was the Preparation Day, so that the bodies wouldn’t remain on the cross on the Sabbath (for that Sabbath was a special one), asked of Pilate that their legs might be broken and that they might be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43"> Therefore the soldiers came and broke the legs of the first and of the other who was crucified with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> but when they came to Jesus and saw that he was already dead, they didn’t break his legs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 John 5:6; Zechariah 12:10; Zechariah 13:1"> However, one of the soldiers pierced his side with a spear, and immediately blood and water came out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 21:24; Hebrews 2:3–4; 1 John 1:1–3"> He who has seen has testified, and his testimony is true. He knows that he tells the truth, that you may believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 34:20; Numbers 9:12; Exodus 12:46"> For these things happened that the Scripture might be fulfilled, “A bone of him will not be broken.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Revelation 1:7; Psalms 22:16–17"> Again another Scripture says, “They will look on him whom they pierced.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:42–47; Matthew 27:57–61; Proverbs 29:25"> After these things, Joseph of Arimathaea, being a disciple of Jesus, but secretly for fear of the Jews, asked of Pilate that he might take away Jesus’ body. Pilate gave him permission. He came therefore and took away his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 7:50–52; Song of Solomon 4:14; John 3:1–21"> Nicodemus, who at first came to Jesus by night, also came bringing a mixture of myrrh and aloes, about a hundred Roman pounds.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="100 Roman pounds of 12 ounces each, or about 72 pounds, or 33 Kilograms.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:44; John 20:5–7; Acts 5:6"> So they took Jesus’ body, and bound it in linen cloths with the spices, as the custom of the Jews is to bury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 23:53; Matthew 27:60; John 20:15"> Now in the place where he was crucified there was a garden. In the garden was a new tomb in which no man had ever yet been laid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 19:31; John 19:14; Isaiah 53:9"> Then, because of the Jews’ Preparation Day (for the tomb was near at hand), they laid Jesus there.</span>
