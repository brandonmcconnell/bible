<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Philippians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 1:6; 1 Corinthians 4:16"> Be imitators of me, even as I also am of Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:15; 1 Corinthians 4:17; Proverbs 31:28–31"> Now I praise you, brothers, that you remember me in all things, and hold firm the traditions, even as I delivered them to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:16; Colossians 1:18; Colossians 3:18"> But I would have you know that the head<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, origin">a</a></sup>of every man is Christ, and the head<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or, origin">b</a></sup>of the woman is man, and the head<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, origin">c</a></sup>of Christ is God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:30; 1 Corinthians 12:28; 1 Corinthians 12:10"> Every man praying or prophesying, having his head covered, dishonors his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:12; Luke 2:36; Acts 2:17"> But every woman praying or prophesying with her head uncovered dishonors her head. For it is one and the same thing as if she were shaved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:5; Numbers 5:18"> For if a woman is not covered, let her hair also be cut off. But if it is shameful for a woman to have her hair cut off or be shaved, let her be covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3; Genesis 5:1; Psalms 8:6"> For a man indeed ought not to have his head covered, because he is the image and glory of God, but the woman is the glory of the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:13; Genesis 2:21–23"> For man is not from woman, but woman from man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 2:18; Genesis 2:23–24; Genesis 2:20"> for man wasn’t created for the woman, but woman for the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 24:64–65; Hebrews 1:14; Matthew 18:10"> For this cause the woman ought to have authority over her own head, because of the angels.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; 1 Corinthians 7:10–14; 1 Corinthians 12:12–22"> Nevertheless, neither is the woman independent of the man, nor the man independent of the woman, in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:36; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 1:2–3"> For as woman came from man, so a man also comes through a woman; but all things are from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:57; John 7:24; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> Judge for yourselves. Is it appropriate that a woman pray to God unveiled?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:26; 1 Corinthians 14:35"> Doesn’t even nature itself teach you that if a man has long hair, it is a dishonor to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> But if a woman has long hair, it is a glory to her, for her hair is given to her for a covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Timothy 6:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 2:14"> But if any man seems to be contentious, we have no such custom, neither do God’s assemblies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; 1 Corinthians 14:23; 1 Corinthians 11:22"> But in giving you this command I don’t praise you, because you come together not for the better but for the worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 1:10–12; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> For first of all, when you come together in the assembly, I hear that divisions exist among you, and I partly believe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 2:19; Titus 3:10; Acts 20:30"> For there also must be factions among you, that those who are approved may be revealed among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> When therefore you assemble yourselves together, it is not the Lord’s supper that you eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:13; Jude 1:12; 1 Corinthians 11:23–25"> For in your eating each one takes his own supper first. One is hungry, and another is drunken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 20:28; Proverbs 17:5; James 2:5–6"> What, don’t you have houses to eat and to drink in? Or do you despise God’s assembly and put them to shame who don’t have enough? What shall I tell you? Shall I praise you? In this I don’t praise you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:19–20; Matthew 26:26–28; 1 Corinthians 11:23–25"> For I received from the Lord that which also I delivered to you, that the Lord Jesus on the night in which he was betrayed took bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:53–58; 1 Corinthians 11:27–28; 1 Corinthians 10:3–4"> When he had given thanks, he broke it and said,<span className="woj">“Take, eat. This is my body, which is broken for you. Do this in memory of me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 22:20; Hebrews 9:15–20; Hebrews 13:20"> In the same way he also took the cup after supper, saying,<span className="woj">“This cup is the new covenant in my blood. Do this, as often as you drink, in memory of me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Revelation 1:7; John 14:3"> For as often as you eat this bread and drink this cup, you proclaim the Lord’s death until he comes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 6:51; 1 Corinthians 10:21; Hebrews 10:29"> Therefore whoever eats this bread or drinks the Lord’s cup in a way unworthy of the Lord will be guilty of the body and the blood of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 6:4; 2 Corinthians 13:5; Lamentations 3:40"> But let a man examine himself, and so let him eat of the bread and drink of the cup.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:27; 1 Corinthians 11:30; 1 Corinthians 11:32–34"> For he who eats and drinks in an unworthy way eats and drinks judgment to himself if he doesn’t discern the Lord’s body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Numbers 20:12; Amos 3:2"> For this cause many among you are weak and sickly, and not a few sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 1:9; Psalms 32:3–5; Revelation 2:5"> For if we discerned ourselves, we wouldn’t be judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5–11; Psalms 118:18; Proverbs 3:11–12"> But when we are judged, we are disciplined by the Lord, that we may not be condemned with the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Therefore, my brothers, when you come together to eat, wait for one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:21–22; 1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Corinthians 4:19"> But if anyone is hungry, let him eat at home, lest your coming together be for judgment. The rest I will set in order whenever I come.</span>
</div>
