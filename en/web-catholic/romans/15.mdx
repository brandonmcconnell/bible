<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:9; Philippians 2:8; John 4:34"> For even Christ didn’t please himself. But, as it is written, “The reproaches of those who reproached you fell on me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Romans 4:23–24"> For whatever things were written before were written for our learning, that through perseverance and through encouragement of the Scriptures we might have hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 2:4–5; 1 Peter 3:8; Romans 12:16"> Now the God of perseverance and of encouragement grant you to be of the same mind with one another according to Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 10:29–30; Revelation 1:6; Ephesians 1:3"> that with one accord you may with one mouth glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:34; Romans 5:2"> Therefore accept one another, even as Christ also accepted you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR reads “us” instead of “you”">a</a></sup>to the glory of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Matthew 15:24; John 1:11"> Now I say that Christ has been made a servant of the circumcision for the truth of God, that he might confirm the promises given to the fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:50; Psalms 18:49; Romans 3:29"> and that the Gentiles might glorify God for his mercy. As it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Therefore I will give praise to you among the Gentiles</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and sing to your name.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 67:3–4; Deuteronomy 32:43; Isaiah 42:10–12"> Again he says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Rejoice, you Gentiles, with his people.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 117:1"> Again,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Praise the Lord, all you Gentiles!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Let all the peoples praise him.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 11:10; Isaiah 11:1; Matthew 12:21"> Again, Isaiah says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“There will be the root of Jesse,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he who arises to rule over the Gentiles;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in him the Gentiles will hope.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:16–17; Romans 12:12; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> Now may the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, that you may abound in hope in the power of the Holy Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 2 Peter 1:12; 1 John 2:21"> I myself am also persuaded about you, my brothers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here and where context allows may also be correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">b</a></sup>that you yourselves are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, able also to admonish others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 15:10; Romans 1:5"> But I write the more boldly to you in part as reminding you, because of the grace that was given to me by God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:13; Romans 1:1; Acts 9:15"> that I should be a servant of Christ Jesus to the Gentiles, serving as a priest of the Good News of God, that the offering up of the Gentiles might be made acceptable, sanctified by the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14–16; 2 Corinthians 12:11–21; Hebrews 5:1"> I have therefore my boasting in Christ Jesus in things pertaining to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 15:12; Romans 1:5; Acts 21:19"> For I will not dare to speak of any things except those which Christ worked through me for the obedience of the Gentiles, by word and deed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:12; Hebrews 2:4; Acts 19:11–12"> in the power of signs and wonders, in the power of God’s Spirit; so that from Jerusalem and around as far as to Illyricum, I have fully preached the Good News of Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:13–16; 1 Corinthians 3:9–15; Ephesians 2:20–22"> yes, making it my aim to preach the Good News, not where Christ was already named, that I might not build on another’s foundation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 52:15; Isaiah 65:1"> But, as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“They will see, to whom no news of him came.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They who haven’t heard will understand.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 1:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:17–18"> Therefore also I was hindered these many times from coming to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 15:32; Romans 1:10–12; Romans 15:29"> but now, no longer having any place in these regions, and having these many years a longing to come to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:28; Romans 1:12; Acts 15:3"> whenever I travel to Spain, I will come to you. For I hope to see you on my journey, and to be helped on my way there by you, if first I may enjoy your company for a while.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Acts 19:21; Acts 20:22"> But now, I say, I am going to Jerusalem, serving the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:1–9; Acts 11:27–30; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> For it has been the good pleasure of Macedonia and Achaia to make a certain contribution for the poor among the saints who are at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:11; Galatians 6:6; Philemon 1:19"> Yes, it has been their good pleasure, and they are their debtors. For if the Gentiles have been made partakers of their spiritual things, they owe it to them also to serve them in material things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 15:24; Philippians 4:17; Colossians 1:6"> When therefore I have accomplished this, and have sealed to them this fruit, I will go on by way of you to Spain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 1:10–12; Psalms 16:11"> I know that when I come to you, I will come in the fullness of the blessing of the Good News of Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 1:11; 2 Corinthians 4:11"> Now I beg you, brothers, by our Lord Jesus Christ and by the love of the Spirit, that you strive together with me in your prayers to God for me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:2; 2 Corinthians 8:4; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> that I may be delivered from those who are disobedient in Judea, and that my service which I have for Jerusalem may be acceptable to the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:18; Philemon 1:20; Philemon 1:7"> that I may come to you in joy through the will of God, and together with you, find rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; Philippians 4:9; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> Now the God of peace be with you all. Amen.</span>
</div>
