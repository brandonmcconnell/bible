<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 18:40; 1 Kings 21:25; 1 Kings 21:5–7"> Ahab told Jezebel all that Elijah had done, and how he had killed all the prophets with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ruth 1:17; 2 Kings 6:31; 1 Kings 20:10"> Then Jezebel sent a messenger to Elijah, saying, “So let the gods do to me, and more also, if I don’t make your life as the life of one of them by tomorrow about this time!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Matthew 26:70–74; Matthew 26:56"> When he saw that, he arose and ran for his life, and came to Beersheba, which belongs to Judah, and left his servant there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jonah 4:3; Numbers 11:15; Jonah 4:8"> But he himself went a day’s journey into the wilderness, and came and sat down under a juniper tree. Then he requested for himself that he might die, and said, “It is enough. Now, O LORD, take away my life; for I am not better than my fathers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 34:7; Acts 12:7; Hebrews 13:5"> He lay down and slept under a juniper tree; and behold, an angel touched him, and said to him, “Arise and eat!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 17:6; Matthew 4:11; Matthew 6:32"> He looked, and behold, there was at his head a cake baked on the coals, and a jar of water. He ate and drank, and lay down again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 103:13–14; Deuteronomy 33:25"> The LORD’s angel came again the second time, and touched him, and said, “Arise and eat, because the journey is too great for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 24:18; Exodus 3:1"> He arose, and ate and drank, and went in the strength of that food forty days and forty nights to Horeb, God’s Mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 19:13; Exodus 33:21–22; Jeremiah 2:18"> He came to a cave there, and camped there; and behold, the LORD’s word came to him, and he said to him, “What are you doing here, Elijah?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 18:4; 1 Kings 18:22; Exodus 34:14"> He said, “I have been very jealous for the LORD, the God of Armies; for the children of Israel have forsaken your covenant, thrown down your altars, and killed your prophets with the sword. I, even I only, am left; and they seek my life, to take it away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:4; Psalms 50:3; Job 38:1"> He said, “Go out and stand on the mountain before the LORD.” Behold, the LORD passed by, and a great and strong wind tore the mountains and broke in pieces the rocks before the LORD; but the LORD was not in the wind. After the wind there was an earthquake; but the LORD was not in the earthquake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 4:16; Zechariah 4:6; Deuteronomy 4:33"> After the earthquake a fire passed; but the LORD was not in the fire. After the fire, there was a still small voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 19:9; Isaiah 6:2; Genesis 16:8"> When Elijah heard it, he wrapped his face in his mantle, went out, and stood in the entrance of the cave. Behold, a voice came to him, and said, “What are you doing here, Elijah?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 19:9–10; Jeremiah 22:9; Isaiah 62:1"> He said, “I have been very jealous for the LORD, the God of Armies; for the children of Israel have forsaken your covenant, thrown down your altars, and killed your prophets with the sword. I, even I only, am left; and they seek my life, to take it away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; Jeremiah 1:10; 2 Kings 8:7–15"> The LORD said to him, “Go, return on your way to the wilderness of Damascus. When you arrive, anoint Hazael to be king over Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 9:1–14; 1 Kings 19:19–21; 2 Kings 2:9"> Anoint Jehu the son of Nimshi to be king over Israel; and anoint Elisha the son of Shaphat of Abel Meholah to be prophet in your place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 13:22; 2 Kings 8:12; 2 Kings 13:3"> He who escapes from the sword of Hazael, Jehu will kill; and he who escapes from the sword of Jehu, Elisha will kill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 11:4–5; Isaiah 1:9; Isaiah 10:20–22"> Yet I reserved seven thousand in Israel, all the knees of which have not bowed to Baal, and every mouth which has not kissed him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 2:8; 2 Kings 2:13–14; 1 Samuel 28:14"> So he departed from there and found Elisha the son of Shaphat, who was plowing with twelve yoke of oxen before him, and he with the twelfth. Elijah went over to him and put his mantle on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 8:21–22; Luke 9:61–62; Acts 20:37"> Elisha left the oxen and ran after Elijah, and said, “Let me please kiss my father and my mother, and then I will follow you.” He said to him, “Go back again; for what have I done to you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:22; 2 Timothy 4:11; Philemon 1:13"> He returned from following him, and took the yoke of oxen, killed them, and boiled their meat with the oxen’s equipment, and gave to the people; and they ate. Then he arose, and went after Elijah, and served him.</span>
