</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:36; 2 Samuel 17:17; 2 Samuel 18:27"> While he yet spoke, behold, Jonathan the son of Abiathar the priest came; and Adonijah said, “Come in; for you are a worthy man, and bring good news.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 1:40; Daniel 5:26–28; 1 Samuel 4:5"> Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet have anointed him king in Gihon. They have come up from there rejoicing, so that the city rang again. This is the noise that you have heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:23; Psalms 132:11; 1 Kings 1:13"> Also, Solomon sits on the throne of the kingdom.</span>

<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Kings 3:6; Psalms 132:11–12; Psalms 145:2"> Also thus said the king, ‘Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who has given one to sit on my throne today, my eyes even seeing it.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Isaiah 21:4–5; Proverbs 28:1; Daniel 5:4–6"> All the guests of Adonijah were afraid, and rose up, and each man went his way.</span>

<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> Solomon was told, “Behold, Adonijah fears King Solomon; for, behold, he is hanging onto the horns of the altar, saying, ‘Let King Solomon swear to me first that he will not kill his servant with the sword.’”</span>
