<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 1:28–30; Isaiah 42:4; Luke 6:8"> He said to the man whose hand was withered,<span className="woj">“Stand up.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Matthew 12:10–12; Hosea 6:6"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Is it lawful on the Sabbath day to do good or to do harm? To save a life or to kill?”</span>But they were silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:10; Ephesians 4:18; Romans 11:25"> When he had looked around at them with anger, being grieved at the hardening of their hearts, he said to the man,<span className="woj">“Stretch out your hand.”</span>He stretched it out, and his hand was restored as healthy as the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:16; Mark 12:13; Matthew 12:14"> The Pharisees went out, and immediately conspired with the Herodians against him, how they might destroy him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 4:25; Luke 6:17; Matthew 12:15"> Jesus withdrew to the sea with his disciples; and a great multitude followed him from Galilee, from Judea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Isaiah 34:5; Matthew 11:21"> from Jerusalem, from Idumaea, beyond the Jordan, and those from around Tyre and Sidon. A great multitude, hearing what great things he did, came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 5:30; John 6:15"> He spoke to his disciples that a little boat should stay near him because of the crowd, so that they wouldn’t press on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56; Matthew 4:23"> For he had healed many, so that as many as had diseases pressed on him that they might touch him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 1:23–24; Luke 8:28"> The unclean spirits, whenever they saw him, fell down before him and cried, “You are the Son of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:16; Mark 1:25; Mark 1:34"> He sternly warned them that they should not make him known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Luke 6:12–16; Matthew 10:1–4"> He went up into the mountain and called to himself those whom he wanted, and they went to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Luke 9:1–6; John 15:16"> He appointed twelve, that they might be with him, and that he might send them out to preach</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and to have authority to heal sicknesses and to cast out demons:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:42; Acts 1:13; Matthew 16:16–18"> Simon (to whom he gave the name Peter);</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Mark 14:33; Jeremiah 23:29"> James the son of Zebedee; and John, the brother of James, (whom he called Boanerges, which means, Sons of Thunder);</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14; Matthew 9:9"> Andrew; Philip; Bartholomew; Matthew; Thomas; James, the son of Alphaeus; Thaddaeus; Simon the Zealot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 6:71; John 13:2; Acts 1:16–25"> and Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him. Then he came into a house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 6:31; John 4:31–34; Mark 9:28"> The multitude came together again, so that they could not so much as eat bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 26:24; John 10:20; Mark 3:31"> When his friends heard it, they went out to seize him; for they said, “He is insane.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:1; Matthew 10:25; Matthew 9:34"> The scribes who came down from Jerusalem said, “He has Beelzebul,” and, “By the prince of the demons he casts out the demons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:17–23; Matthew 12:25–30; Matthew 13:34"> He summoned them and said to them in parables,<span className="woj">“How can Satan cast out Satan?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Isaiah 19:2–3; 2 Samuel 20:6"> <span className="woj">If a kingdom is divided against itself, that kingdom cannot stand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 3:16; Psalms 133:1; Galatians 5:15"> <span className="woj">If a house is divided against itself, that house cannot stand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> <span className="woj">If Satan has risen up against himself, and is divided, he can’t stand, but has an end.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–26; Matthew 12:29; Luke 11:21–23"> <span className="woj">But no one can enter into the house of the strong man to plunder unless he first binds the strong man; then he will plunder his house.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; 1 John 5:16"> <span className="woj">“Most certainly I tell you, all sins of the descendants of man will be forgiven, including their blasphemies with which they may blaspheme;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 12:31–32; Luke 12:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> <span className="woj">but whoever may blaspheme against the Holy Spirit never has forgiveness, but is subject to eternal condemnation.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="NU reads, guilty of an eternal sin.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:20; Mark 3:22"> —because they said, “He has an unclean spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:19–21; Matthew 12:46–50"> His mother and his brothers came, and standing outside, they sent to him, calling him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> A multitude was sitting around him, and they told him, “Behold, your mother, your brothers, and your sisters<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="TR omits “your sisters”">b</a></sup>are outside looking for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 3:21; Deuteronomy 33:9; Mark 6:3"> He answered them,<span className="woj">“Who are my mother and my brothers?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 12:49–50; Luke 11:27–28; Romans 8:29"> Looking around at those who sat around him, he said,<span className="woj">“Behold, my mother and my brothers!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:17; Matthew 7:21; 1 John 3:22–23"> <span className="woj">For whoever does the will of God is my brother, my sister, and mother.”</span></span>
