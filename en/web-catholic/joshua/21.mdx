<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 21:20–26; Exodus 6:16–25; 1 Chronicles 6:66–70"> The rest of the children of Kohath had ten cities by lot out of the families of the tribe of Ephraim, out of the tribe of Dan, and out of the half-tribe of Manasseh.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:34–40; 1 Chronicles 6:77–81; Exodus 6:19"> The children of Merari according to their families had twelve cities out of the tribe of Reuben, out of the tribe of Gad, and out of the tribe of Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 21:3; Joshua 18:6; Numbers 33:54"> The children of Israel gave these cities with their pasture lands by lot to the Levites, as the LORD commanded by Moses.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 14:13–15; 1 Chronicles 6:55–57"> But they gave the fields of the city and its villages to Caleb the son of Jephunneh for his possession.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; 1 Chronicles 6:59; Joshua 15:55"> Ain with its pasture lands, Juttah with its pasture lands, and Beth Shemesh with its pasture lands: nine cities out of those two tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 18:24–25; Joshua 9:3; 1 Chronicles 6:60"> Out of the tribe of Benjamin, Gibeon with its pasture lands, Geba with its pasture lands,</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> All the cities of the children of Aaron, the priests, were thirteen cities with their pasture lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:66; Joshua 21:5"> The families of the children of Kohath, the Levites, even the rest of the children of Kohath, had the cities of their lot out of the tribe of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 20:7; Judges 9:1; 1 Kings 12:1"> They gave them Shechem with its pasture lands in the hill country of Ephraim, the city of refuge for the man slayer, and Gezer with its pasture lands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:68; Joshua 16:3; Joshua 18:13–14"> Kibzaim with its pasture lands, and Beth Horon with its pasture lands: four cities.</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 19:42; Joshua 10:12; 1 Chronicles 6:69"> Aijalon with its pasture lands, Gath Rimmon with its pasture lands: four cities.</span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> All the cities of the families of the rest of the children of Kohath were ten with their pasture lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:71; Deuteronomy 1:4; Joshua 20:8"> They gave to the children of Gershon, of the families of the Levites, out of the half-tribe of Manasseh Golan in Bashan with its pasture lands, the city of refuge for the man slayer, and Be Eshterah with its pasture lands: two cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 19:12; 1 Chronicles 6:72–73"> Out of the tribe of Issachar, Kishion with its pasture lands, Daberath with its pasture lands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 10:23; Joshua 12:11; Joshua 10:3"> Jarmuth with its pasture lands, En Gannim with its pasture lands: four cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:74–75; Joshua 19:25–28"> Out of the tribe of Asher, Mishal with its pasture lands, Abdon with its pasture lands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 18:21; 1 Chronicles 6:75; Judges 1:31"> Helkath with its pasture lands, and Rehob with its pasture lands: four cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:76; Joshua 20:7; Joshua 19:37"> Out of the tribe of Naphtali, Kedesh in Galilee with its pasture lands, the city of refuge for the man slayer, Hammothdor with its pasture lands, and Kartan with its pasture lands: three cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> All the cities of the Gershonites according to their families were thirteen cities with their pasture lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:77; Joshua 19:11; Joshua 21:7"> To the families of the children of Merari, the rest of the Levites, out of the tribe of Zebulun, Jokneam with its pasture lands, Kartah with its pasture lands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Dimnah with its pasture lands, and Nahalal with its pasture lands: four cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 20:8; Deuteronomy 4:43; Numbers 21:23"> Out of the tribe of Reuben, Bezer with its pasture lands, Jahaz with its pasture lands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Kedemoth with its pasture lands, and Mephaath with its pasture lands: four cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 32:2; 1 Kings 22:3; 1 Chronicles 6:80"> Out of the tribe of Gad, Ramoth in Gilead with its pasture lands, the city of refuge for the man slayer, and Mahanaim with its pasture lands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 16:8–9; Numbers 32:35; Numbers 32:1"> Heshbon with its pasture lands, Jazer with its pasture lands: four cities in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> All these were the cities of the children of Merari according to their families, even the rest of the families of the Levites. Their lot was twelve cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 35:1–8; Deuteronomy 33:10; Genesis 49:7"> All the cities of the Levites among the possessions of the children of Israel were forty-eight cities with their pasture lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Each of these cities included their pasture lands around them. It was this way with all these cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Psalms 106:42–45; Exodus 23:27–31"> So the LORD gave to Israel all the land which he swore to give to their fathers. They possessed it, and lived in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Joshua 11:23; Hebrews 4:9; Deuteronomy 31:3–5"> The LORD gave them rest all around, according to all that he swore to their fathers. Not a man of all their enemies stood before them. The LORD delivered all their enemies into their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 8:56; Joshua 23:14–15; Numbers 23:19"> Nothing failed of any good thing which the LORD had spoken to the house of Israel. All came to pass.</span>
