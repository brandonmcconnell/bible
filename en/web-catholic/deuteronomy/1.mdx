<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:1; Joshua 22:7; Numbers 33:17–18"> These are the words which Moses spoke to all Israel beyond the Jordan in the wilderness, in the Arabah opposite Suf, between Paran, Tophel, Laban, Hazeroth, and Dizahab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Numbers 13:26; Deuteronomy 9:23"> It is eleven days’ journey from Horeb by the way of Mount Seir to Kadesh Barnea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 33:38; Deuteronomy 4:1–2; Numbers 20:1"> In the fortieth year, in the eleventh month, on the first day of the month, Moses spoke to the children of Israel according to all that the LORD<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="When rendered in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS, “LORD” or “GOD” is the translation of God’s Proper Name (Hebrew “יהוה”, usually pronounced Yahweh).">a</a></sup>had given him in commandment to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Joshua 12:2–6; Joshua 13:10–12"> after he had struck Sihon the king of the Amorites who lived in Heshbon, and Og the king of Bashan who lived in Ashtaroth, at Edrei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:8; Deuteronomy 32:46; Deuteronomy 31:9"> Beyond the Jordan, in the land of Moab, Moses began to declare this law, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Numbers 10:11–13; Exodus 19:1–2"> “The LORD our God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Hebrew word rendered “God” is “אֱלֹהִ֑ים” (Elohim).">b</a></sup>spoke to us in Horeb, saying, ‘You have lived long enough at this mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 10:40; Amos 2:9"> Turn, and take your journey, and go to the hill country of the Amorites and to all the places near there: in the Arabah, in the hill country, in the lowland, in the South, by the seashore, in the land of the Canaanites, and in Lebanon as far as the great river, the river Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 26:3–4"> Behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Behold”, from “הִנֵּה”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. It is often used as an interjection.">c</a></sup>I have set the land before you. Go in and possess the land which the LORD swore to your fathers—to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob—to give to them and to their offspring<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="or, seed">d</a></sup>after them.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 18:18; Numbers 11:17; Numbers 11:11–14"> I spoke to you at that time, saying, “I am not able to bear you myself alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Exodus 32:13; Genesis 22:17"> The LORD your God has multiplied you, and behold, you are today as the stars of the sky for multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 115:14; Genesis 22:17; Numbers 6:27"> May the LORD, the God of your fathers, make you a thousand times as many as you are and bless you, as he has promised you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; 1 Kings 3:7–9; 2 Corinthians 2:16"> How can I myself alone bear your problems, your burdens, and your strife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Acts 1:21–23; Numbers 11:16–17"> Take wise men of understanding who are respected among your tribes, and I will make them heads over you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> You answered me, and said, “The thing which you have spoken is good to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:18; Exodus 18:25–26; Ephesians 4:11"> So I took the heads of your tribes, wise and respected men, and made them heads over you, captains of thousands, captains of hundreds, captains of fifties, captains of tens, and officers, according to your tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:24; Exodus 22:21; 2 Chronicles 19:6–10"> I commanded your judges at that time, saying, “Hear cases between your brothers and judge righteously between a man and his brother, and the foreigner who is living with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 18:26; James 2:9"> You shall not show partiality in judgment; you shall hear the small and the great alike. You shall not be afraid of the face of man, for the judgment is God’s. The case that is too hard for you, you shall bring to me, and I will hear it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:5; Acts 20:20"> I commanded you at that time all the things which you should do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:2; Deuteronomy 8:15; Jeremiah 2:6"> We traveled from Horeb and went through all that great and terrible wilderness which you saw, by the way to the hill country of the Amorites, as the LORD our God commanded us; and we came to Kadesh Barnea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:7–8"> I said to you, “You have come to the hill country of the Amorites, which the LORD our God gives to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Psalms 46:1; Numbers 14:8–9"> Behold, the LORD your God has set the land before you. Go up, take possession, as the LORD the God of your fathers has spoken to you. Don’t be afraid, neither be dismayed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 13:1–20"> You came near to me, everyone of you, and said, “Let’s send men before us, that they may search the land for us, and bring back to us word of the way by which we must go up, and the cities to which we shall come.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 13:3–33"> The thing pleased me well. I took twelve of your men, one man for every tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 13:21–27; Joshua 2:1–2"> They turned and went up into the hill country, and came to the valley of Eshcol, and spied it out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> They took some of the fruit of the land in their hands and brought it down to us, and brought us word again, and said, “It is a good land which the LORD our God gives to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 14:1–4; Acts 7:51; Psalms 106:24–25"> Yet you wouldn’t go up, but rebelled against the commandment of the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:28; Psalms 106:25; Numbers 14:3"> You murmured in your tents, and said, “Because the LORD hated us, he has brought us out of the land of Egypt, to deliver us into the hand of the Amorites to destroy us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 13:28–33; Deuteronomy 9:1–2; Joshua 15:14"> Where are we going up? Our brothers have made our heart melt, saying, ‘The people are greater and taller than we. The cities are great and fortified up to the sky. Moreover we have seen the sons of the Anakim there!’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Then I said to you, “Don’t be terrified. Don’t be afraid of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Nehemiah 4:20; Romans 8:31"> The LORD your God, who goes before you, he will fight for you, according to all that he did for you in Egypt before your eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3–4; Acts 13:18; Hosea 11:3–4"> and in the wilderness where you have seen how that the LORD your God carried you, as a man carries his son, in all the way that you went, until you came to this place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 106:24; Jude 1:5; Hebrews 3:18–19"> Yet in this thing you didn’t believe the LORD your God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 10:33; Psalms 78:14; Nehemiah 9:12"> who went before you on the way, to seek out a place for you to pitch your tents in: in fire by night, to show you by what way you should go, and in the cloud by day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 14:22–30; Psalms 95:11; Ezekiel 20:15"> The LORD heard the voice of your words and was angry, and swore, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 95:11"> “Surely not one of these men of this evil generation shall see the good land which I swore to give to your fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Joshua 14:6–14; Numbers 32:12"> except Caleb the son of Jephunneh. He shall see it. I will give the land that he has trodden on to him and to his children, because he has wholly followed the LORD.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 20:12; Deuteronomy 4:21; Deuteronomy 34:4"> Also the LORD was angry with me for your sakes, saying, “You also shall not go in there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Numbers 14:30; Deuteronomy 31:7–8"> Joshua the son of Nun, who stands before you, shall go in there. Encourage him, for he shall cause Israel to inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15–16; Numbers 14:3; Numbers 14:31"> Moreover your little ones, whom you said would be captured or killed, your children, who today have no knowledge of good or evil, shall go in there. I will give it to them, and they shall possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 14:25"> But as for you, turn, and take your journey into the wilderness by the way to the Red Sea.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 14:39–45; Proverbs 19:3; Numbers 22:34"> Then you answered and said to me, “We have sinned against the LORD. We will go up and fight, according to all that the LORD our God commanded us.” Every man of you put on his weapons of war, and presumed to go up into the hill country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Numbers 14:41–43; Joshua 7:8–13; Isaiah 59:1–2"> The LORD said to me, “Tell them, ‘Don’t go up and don’t fight; for I am not among you, lest you be struck before your enemies.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 14:44; Isaiah 63:10; Acts 7:51"> So I spoke to you, and you didn’t listen; but you rebelled against the commandment of the LORD, and were presumptuous, and went up into the hill country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 118:12; Numbers 14:45; Numbers 21:3"> The Amorites, who lived in that hill country, came out against you and chased you as bees do, and beat you down in Seir, even to Hormah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Psalms 78:34"> You returned and wept before the LORD, but the LORD didn’t listen to your voice, nor turn his ear to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 20:22; Judges 11:16–17"> So you stayed in Kadesh many days, according to the days that you remained.</span>
