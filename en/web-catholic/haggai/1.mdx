<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 2:2; Ezra 3:8; Ezra 3:2"> In the second year of Darius the king, in the sixth month, in the first day of the month, the LORD’s<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="When rendered in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS, “LORD” or “GOD” is the translation of God’s Proper Name (Hebrew “יהוה”, usually pronounced Yahweh).">a</a></sup>word came by Haggai the prophet, to Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezra 4:23–2; Nehemiah 4:10; Ecclesiastes 11:4"> “This is what the LORD of Armies says: These people say, ‘The time hasn’t yet come, the time for the LORD’s house to be built.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 5:1; Zechariah 1:1"> Then the LORD’s word came by Haggai the prophet, saying,</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 119:59–60; Haggai 1:5; Philippians 3:1"> “This is what the LORD of Armies says: ‘Consider your ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 132:13–14; 2 Chronicles 7:16; Haggai 2:7"> Go up to the mountain, bring wood, and build the house. I will take pleasure in it, and I will be glorified,” says the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Haggai 1:4; Haggai 2:16–17; Haggai 1:6"> “You looked for much, and, behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Behold”, from “הִנֵּה”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. Why?” says the LORD of Armies, “Because of my house that lies waste, while each of you is busy with his own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 8:35; Leviticus 26:19; Deuteronomy 28:23–24"> Therefore for your sake the heavens withhold the dew, and the earth withholds its fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Haggai 2:17; Deuteronomy 28:22; 2 Kings 8:1"> I called for a drought on the land, on the mountains, on the grain, on the new wine, on the oil, on that which the ground produces, on men, on livestock, and on all the labor of the hands.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 50:10; Ezra 5:2; Haggai 1:14"> Then Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel and Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, with all the remnant of the people, obeyed the LORD their God’s<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Hebrew word rendered “God” is “אֱלֹהִ֑ים” (Elohim).">c</a></sup>voice, and the words of Haggai the prophet, as the LORD their God had sent him; and the people feared the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Matthew 28:20; Malachi 3:1"> Then Haggai, the LORD’s messenger, spoke the LORD’s message to the people, saying, “I am with you,” says the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:22; Hebrews 13:21; Haggai 2:21"> The LORD stirred up the spirit of Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and the spirit of Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and the spirit of all the remnant of the people; and they came and worked on the house of the LORD of Armies, their God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Haggai 1:1; Haggai 2:20; Haggai 2:1"> in the twenty-fourth day of the month, in the sixth month, in the second year of Darius the king.</span>
