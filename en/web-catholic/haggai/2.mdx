<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Haggai 1:1; Haggai 2:20; Haggai 2:10"> In the seventh month, in the twenty-first day of the month, the LORD’s word came by Haggai the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Haggai 1:1; Nehemiah 8:9; Ezra 2:63"> “Speak now to Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and to the remnant of the people, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 3:12; Haggai 2:9; Ezekiel 7:20"> ‘Who is left among you who saw this house in its former glory? How do you see it now? Isn’t it in your eyes as nothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:20; Joshua 1:9; Zechariah 8:9"> Yet now be strong, Zerubbabel,’ says the LORD. ‘Be strong, Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest. Be strong, all you people of the land,’ says the LORD, ‘and work, for I am with you,’ says the LORD of Armies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 34:10; Exodus 29:45–46; Isaiah 41:13"> This is the word that I covenanted with you when you came out of Egypt, and my Spirit lived among you. ‘Don’t be afraid.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 12:26–28; Isaiah 10:25; Mark 13:24–26"> For this is what the LORD of Armies says: ‘Yet once more, it is a little while, and I will shake the heavens, the earth, the sea, and the dry land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; 1 Kings 8:11; John 7:37–39"> and I will shake all nations. The treasure of all nations will come, and I will fill this house with glory, says the LORD of Armies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 50:10–12; Psalms 24:1; Isaiah 60:17"> The silver is mine, and the gold is mine,’ says the LORD of Armies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 85:8–9; 2 Corinthians 3:9–10; Colossians 1:19–21"> ‘The latter glory of this house will be greater than the former,’ says the LORD of Armies; ‘and in this place I will give peace,’ says the LORD of Armies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Haggai 2:20; Haggai 1:1; Haggai 1:15–1"> In the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, in the second year of Darius, the LORD’s word came by Haggai the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10–11; Malachi 2:7; Deuteronomy 33:10"> “The LORD of Armies says: Ask now the priests concerning the law, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 6:27; Matthew 23:19; Leviticus 7:6"> ‘If someone carries holy meat in the fold of his garment, and with his fold touches bread, stew, wine, oil, or any food, will it become holy?’” The priests answered, “No.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 5:2–3; Numbers 19:11–22; Leviticus 22:4–6"> Then Haggai said, “If one who is unclean by reason of a dead body touches any of these, will it be unclean?” The priests answered, “It will be unclean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 28:9; Titus 1:15; Isaiah 1:11–15"> Then Haggai answered, “‘So is this people, and so is this nation before me,’ says the LORD; ‘and so is every work of their hands. That which they offer there is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Haggai 1:5; Ezra 3:10; Ezra 4:24"> Now, please consider from this day and backward, before a stone was laid on a stone in the LORD’s temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Haggai 1:6; Malachi 2:2; Proverbs 3:9–10"> Through all that time, when one came to a heap of twenty measures, there were only ten. When one came to the wine vat to draw out fifty, there were only twenty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:22; Haggai 1:11; Haggai 1:9"> I struck you with blight, mildew, and hail in all the work of your hands; yet you didn’t turn to me,’ says the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 8:9; Haggai 2:15; Ezra 5:1–2"> ‘Consider, please, from this day and backward, from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, since the day that the foundation of the LORD’s temple was laid, consider it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 3:10; Habakkuk 3:17–18; Proverbs 3:9–10"> Is the seed yet in the barn? Yes, the vine, the fig tree, the pomegranate, and the olive tree haven’t produced. From today I will bless you.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The LORD’s word came the second time to Haggai in the twenty-fourth day of the month, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6–10; Haggai 2:6–7; Haggai 1:1"> “Speak to Zerubbabel, governor of Judah, saying, ‘I will shake the heavens and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 5:10; Judges 7:22; Zephaniah 3:8"> I will overthrow the throne of kingdoms. I will destroy the strength of the kingdoms of the nations. I will overthrow the chariots and those who ride in them. The horses and their riders will come down, everyone by the sword of his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; Isaiah 42:1; Song of Solomon 8:6"> In that day, says the LORD of Armies, I will take you, Zerubbabel my servant, the son of Shealtiel,’ says the LORD, ‘and will make you like a signet ring, for I have chosen you,’ says the LORD of Armies.”</span>
