<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 27:1; Matthew 8:5–13; Matthew 27:27"> Now there was a certain man in Caesarea, Cornelius by name, a centurion of what was called the Italian Regiment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; Acts 10:4; Acts 10:22"> a devout man, and one who feared God with all his house, who gave gifts for the needy generously to the people, and always prayed to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 9:20–21; Acts 3:1; Acts 5:19"> At about the ninth hour of the day,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3:00 p.m.">a</a></sup>he clearly saw in a vision an angel of God coming to him and saying to him, “Cornelius!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; Hebrews 6:10; Acts 10:31"> He, fastening his eyes on him and being frightened, said, “What is it, Lord?” He said to him, “Your prayers and your gifts to the needy have gone up for a memorial before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 16:9; Acts 9:38; Acts 15:7"> Now send men to Joppa, and get Simon, who is also called Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 9:43; Acts 11:13–14; Romans 10:14–17"> He is staying with a tanner named Simon, whose house is by the seaside.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="TR adds “This one will tell you what it is necessary for you to do.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philemon 1:16; 1 Samuel 14:6–7; Matthew 8:9–10"> When the angel who spoke to him had departed, Cornelius called two of his household servants and a devout soldier of those who waited on him continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:36; Psalms 119:59–60; Acts 10:33"> Having explained everything to them, he sent them to Joppa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:5; Psalms 55:17; 1 Samuel 9:25"> Now on the next day as they were on their journey and got close to the city, Peter went up on the housetop to pray at about noon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 22:17; Revelation 4:2–3; 2 Corinthians 12:2–4"> He became hungry and desired to eat, but while they were preparing, he fell into a trance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; John 1:51; Romans 16:25–26"> He saw heaven opened and a certain container descending to him, like a great sheet let down by four corners on the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 7:8–9; John 7:37; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11"> in which were all kinds of four-footed animals of the earth, wild animals, reptiles, and birds of the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 4:31–34; Jeremiah 35:2–5; Acts 10:10"> A voice came to him,<span className="woj">“Rise, Peter, kill and eat!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:25; Ezekiel 4:14; Daniel 1:8"> But Peter said, “Not so, Lord; for I have never eaten anything that is common or unclean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:25; Matthew 15:11; Titus 1:15"> A voice came to him again the second time,<span className="woj">“What God has cleansed, you must not call unclean.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 41:32; John 21:17; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> This was done three times, and immediately the thing was received up into heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 13:12; Acts 10:7–19; Acts 5:24"> Now while Peter was very perplexed in himself what the vision which he had seen might mean, behold, the men who were sent by Cornelius, having made inquiry for Simon’s house, stood before the gate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 10:5–6; Acts 11:11"> and called and asked whether Simon, who was also called Peter, was lodging there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 8:29; Acts 11:12; Acts 21:4"> While Peter was pondering the vision, the Spirit said to him, “Behold, three<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Reading from TR and NU. MT omits “three”">c</a></sup>men seek you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 15:7–9; Zechariah 2:9–11; Isaiah 48:16"> But arise, get down, and go with them, doubting nothing; for I have sent them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 1:38–39; Mark 10:51; Acts 10:29"> Peter went down to the men, and said, “Behold, I am he whom you seek. Why have you come?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 11:14; Acts 10:2; Acts 22:12"> They said, “Cornelius, a centurion, a righteous man and one who fears God, and well spoken of by all the nation of the Jews, was directed by a holy angel to invite you to his house, and to listen to what you say.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 10:45; Acts 11:12; 1 Peter 4:9"> So he called them in and provided a place to stay. On the next day Peter arose and went out with them, and some of the brothers from Joppa accompanied him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 8:40; Zechariah 8:20–23; Zechariah 3:10"> On the next day they entered into Caesarea. Cornelius was waiting for them, having called together his relatives and his near friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Revelation 22:8–9; Daniel 2:46"> When Peter entered, Cornelius met him, fell down at his feet, and worshiped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Revelation 22:8–9; Acts 14:14–15"> But Peter raised him up, saying, “Stand up! I myself am also a man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12; John 4:35; Acts 10:24"> As he talked with him, he went in and found many gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 4:9; Ephesians 3:6–7; Galatians 2:12–14"> He said to them, “You yourselves know how it is an unlawful thing for a man who is a Jew to join himself or come to one of another nation, but God has shown me that I shouldn’t call any man unholy or unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; 1 Peter 3:15; Acts 10:19–20"> Therefore I also came without complaint when I was sent for. I ask therefore, why did you send for me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 10:3; Daniel 9:20–21; Nehemiah 9:1–3"> Cornelius said, “Four days ago, I was fasting until this hour; and at the ninth hour,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3:00 p.m.">d</a></sup>I prayed in my house, and behold, a man stood before me in bright clothing</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 10:4; Daniel 9:23; Luke 1:13"> and said, ‘Cornelius, your prayer is heard, and your gifts to the needy are remembered in the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Send therefore to Joppa and summon Simon, who is also called Peter. He is staying in the house of a tanner named Simon, by the seaside. When he comes, he will speak to you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 1:19; Galatians 4:14; 2 Chronicles 30:12"> Therefore I sent to you at once, and it was good of you to come. Now therefore we are all here present in the sight of God to hear all things that have been commanded you by God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 2:11; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Deuteronomy 10:17"> Peter opened his mouth and said, “Truly I perceive that God doesn’t show favoritism;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:29; Acts 10:2; Romans 10:12–13"> but in every nation he who fears him and works righteousness is acceptable to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Acts 2:36; Revelation 17:14"> The word which he sent to the children of Israel, preaching good news of peace by Jesus Christ—he is Lord of all—</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 4:14; Acts 2:22; Acts 28:22"> you yourselves know what happened, which was proclaimed throughout all Judea, beginning from Galilee, after the baptism which John preached;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 4:18; Acts 2:22; Isaiah 61:1–3"> how God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Spirit and with power, who went about doing good and healing all who were oppressed by the devil, for God was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:48; Acts 1:8; Acts 10:41"> We are witnesses of everything he did both in the country of the Jews and in Jerusalem; whom they also<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="TR omits “also”">e</a></sup>killed, hanging him on a tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Peter 1:21; Acts 13:30–31; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4"> God raised him up the third day and gave him to be revealed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 14:22; Acts 10:39; John 21:13"> not to all the people, but to witnesses who were chosen before by God, to us, who ate and drank with him after he rose from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; 2 Timothy 4:1; 1 Peter 4:5"> He commanded us to preach to the people and to testify that this is he who is appointed by God as the Judge of the living and the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; Acts 2:38; Isaiah 53:11"> All the prophets testify about him, that through his name everyone who believes in him will receive remission of sins.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 11:15; Acts 19:6; Acts 15:8"> While Peter was still speaking these words, the Holy Spirit fell on all those who heard the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Acts 10:23; Acts 2:38; Galatians 3:13–14"> They of the circumcision who believed were amazed, as many as came with Peter, because the gift of the Holy Spirit was also poured out on the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 2:4; Mark 16:17"> For they heard them speaking in other languages and magnifying God. Then Peter answered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 8:36; Romans 10:12; Acts 11:15–17"> “Can anyone forbid these people from being baptized with water? They have received the Holy Spirit just like us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 8:16; Galatians 3:27"> He commanded them to be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ. Then they asked him to stay some days.</span>
