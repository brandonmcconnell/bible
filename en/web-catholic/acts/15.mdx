<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; Acts 15:5; Leviticus 12:3"> Some men came down from Judea and taught the brothers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here and where the context allows may also be correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">a</a></sup>“Unless you are circumcised after the custom of Moses, you can’t be saved.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 15:22–23; Acts 15:6–7; Galatians 2:1–2"> Therefore when Paul and Barnabas had no small discord and discussion with them, they appointed Paul, Barnabas, and some others of them to go up to Jerusalem to the apostles and elders about this question.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 21:5; 1 Corinthians 16:11"> They, being sent on their way by the assembly, passed through both Phoenicia and Samaria, declaring the conversion of the Gentiles. They caused great joy to all the brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 15:12; Acts 15:3"> When they had come to Jerusalem, they were received by the assembly and the apostles and the elders, and they reported everything that God had done with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 5:1–3; Philippians 3:5–8; Acts 15:1"> But some of the sect of the Pharisees who believed rose up, saying, “It is necessary to circumcise them, and to command them to keep the law of Moses.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 15:22; Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 13:17"> The apostles and the elders were gathered together to see about this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 10:17–18; Galatians 2:7–9; 1 Chronicles 28:4–5"> When there had been much discussion, Peter rose up and said to them, “Brothers, you know that a good while ago God made a choice among you that by my mouth the nations should hear the word of the Good News and believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:24; Acts 10:47; Acts 14:3"> God, who knows the heart, testified about them, giving them the Holy Spirit, just like he did to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; Galatians 5:6; Acts 10:28"> He made no distinction between us and them, cleansing their hearts by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 5:1; Matthew 23:4; Galatians 4:9"> Now therefore why do you tempt God, that you should put a yoke on the neck of the disciples which neither our fathers nor we were able to bear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:24; Titus 3:4–7; Titus 2:11"> But we believe that we are saved through the grace of the Lord Jesus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="TR adds “Christ”">b</a></sup>just as they are.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 15:4; Acts 21:19"> All the multitude kept silence, and they listened to Barnabas and Paul reporting what signs and wonders God had done among the nations through them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Galatians 2:12; James 1:1"> After they were silent, James answered, “Brothers, listen to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 1:1; Acts 15:7–9; Luke 2:31–32"> Simeon has reported how God first visited the nations to take out of them a people for his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:8–12; Acts 13:47"> This agrees with the words of the prophets. As it is written,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 9:11–12; Psalms 89:35–49; Luke 1:31–33"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘After these things I will return.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will again build the tabernacle of David, which has fallen.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will again build its ruins.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will set it up</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 2:11; Zechariah 8:20–23; Isaiah 65:1"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">that the rest of men may seek after the Lord:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all the Gentiles who are called by my name,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">says the Lord, who does all these things.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; Ephesians 3:9; Isaiah 46:9–10"> “All of God’s works are known to him from eternity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 15:28; Galatians 1:7–10; Acts 15:24"> Therefore my judgment is that we don’t trouble those from among the Gentiles who turn to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 15:29; Revelation 2:20; Leviticus 3:17"> but that we write to them that they abstain from the pollution of idols, from sexual immorality, from what is strangled, and from blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:15; Acts 13:27; 2 Corinthians 3:14–15"> For Moses from generations of old has in every city those who preach him, being read in the synagogues every Sabbath.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 15:27; Acts 15:40; 1 Thessalonians 1:1"> Then it seemed good to the apostles and the elders, with the whole assembly, to choose men out of their company, and send them to Antioch with Paul and Barnabas: Judas called Barsabbas, and Silas, chief men among the brothers.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here and where the context allows may also be correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 23:26; James 1:1; Galatians 1:21"> They wrote these things by their hand: “The apostles, the elders, and the brothers, to the brothers who are of the Gentiles in Antioch, Syria, and Cilicia: greetings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Galatians 5:12; Galatians 5:10; Acts 15:1"> Because we have heard that some who went out from us have troubled you with words, unsettling your souls, saying, ‘You must be circumcised and keep the law,’ to whom we gave no commandment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Peter 3:15; Acts 1:14; Acts 15:2"> it seemed good to us, having come to one accord, to choose out men and send them to you with our beloved Barnabas and Paul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 9:23–25; Philippians 2:29–30"> men who have risked their lives for the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 15:32; 3 John 1:13"> We have sent therefore Judas and Silas, who themselves will also tell you the same things by word of mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 16:13; Revelation 2:24; Matthew 11:30"> For it seemed good to the Holy Spirit, and to us, to lay no greater burden on you than these necessary things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 15:20; Acts 21:25; Revelation 2:14"> that you abstain from things sacrificed to idols, from blood, from things strangled, and from sexual immorality, from which if you keep yourselves, it will be well with you. Farewell.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 16:4; Acts 6:2; Acts 23:33"> So, when they were sent off, they came to Antioch. Having gathered the multitude together, they delivered the letter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:4–5; Philippians 3:3; Galatians 5:1"> When they had read it, they rejoiced over the encouragement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 11:23; Acts 14:22; Acts 13:1"> Judas and Silas, also being prophets themselves, encouraged the brothers with many words and strengthened them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:11; Acts 16:36; Hebrews 11:31"> After they had spent some time there, they were dismissed in peace from the brothers to the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts add: But it seemed good to Silas to stay there.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Colossians 1:28; Acts 13:1"> But Paul and Barnabas stayed in Antioch, teaching and preaching the word of the Lord, with many others also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 14:6; Acts 13:4; Acts 13:51"> After some days Paul said to Barnabas, “Let’s return now and visit our brothers in every city in which we proclaimed the word of the Lord, to see how they are doing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Philemon 1:24; Acts 13:5"> Barnabas planned to take John, who was called Mark, with them also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 13:13; Proverbs 25:19; Luke 14:27–34"> But Paul didn’t think that it was a good idea to take with them someone who had withdrawn from them in Pamphylia, and didn’t go with them to do the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Colossians 4:10; Ecclesiastes 7:20; Acts 4:36"> Then the contention grew so sharp that they separated from each other. Barnabas took Mark with him and sailed away to Cyprus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 14:26; Acts 11:23"> but Paul chose Silas and went out, being commended by the brothers to the grace of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 15:23; Galatians 1:21; Acts 15:32"> He went through Syria and Cilicia, strengthening the assemblies.</span>
