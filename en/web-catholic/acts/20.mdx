<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:5; Acts 19:21; 1 Timothy 1:3"> After the uproar had ceased, Paul sent for the disciples, took leave of them, and departed to go into Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 4:1; Colossians 1:28"> When he had gone through those parts and had encouraged them with many words, he came into Greece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 20:19; 2 Corinthians 11:26; Acts 9:23–24"> When he had spent three months there, and a plot was made against him by Jews as he was about to set sail for Syria, he determined to return through Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 19:29; Ephesians 6:21; 2 Timothy 4:20"> These accompanied him as far as Asia: Sopater of Beroea, Aristarchus and Secundus of the Thessalonians, Gaius of Derbe, Timothy, and Tychicus and Trophimus of Asia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 16:8; Acts 16:10–11; 2 Timothy 4:13"> But these had gone ahead, and were waiting for us at Troas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 12:3; Acts 16:12; Exodus 23:15"> We sailed away from Philippi after the days of Unleavened Bread, and came to them at Troas in five days, where we stayed seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:2; Acts 2:42; John 20:19"> On the first day of the week, when the disciples were gathered together to break bread, Paul talked with them, intending to depart on the next day; and continued his speech until midnight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Luke 22:12"> There were many lights in the upper room where we<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR reads “they” instead of “we”">a</a></sup>were gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 13:36; Matthew 26:40–41; Jonah 1:5–6"> A certain young man named Eutychus sat in the window, weighed down with deep sleep. As Paul spoke still longer, being weighed down by his sleep, he fell down from the third floor and was taken up dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 5:39; Matthew 9:23–24; 1 Kings 17:21–22"> Paul went down and fell upon him, and embracing him said, “Don’t be troubled, for his life is in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 20:7; Acts 20:9"> When he had gone up, had broken bread and eaten, and had talked with them a long while, even until break of day, he departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:16; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; 2 Corinthians 1:4"> They brought the boy in alive, and were greatly comforted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 6:46; Mark 1:35; Mark 6:31–33"> But we, going ahead to the ship, set sail for Assos, intending to take Paul aboard there; for he had so arranged, intending himself to go by land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When he met us at Assos, we took him aboard and came to Mitylene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:20; Acts 20:17"> Sailing from there, we came the following day opposite Chios. The next day we touched at Samos and stayed at Trogyllium, and the day after we came to Miletus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:8; Acts 2:1; Acts 19:21"> For Paul had determined to sail past Ephesus, that he might not have to spend time in Asia; for he was hastening, if it were possible for him, to be in Jerusalem on the day of Pentecost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Titus 1:5; Acts 11:30; 1 Timothy 5:17"> From Miletus he sent to Ephesus and called to himself the elders of the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:5–6; Acts 18:19; Acts 19:1"> When they had come to him, he said to them, “You yourselves know, from the first day that I set foot in Asia, how I was with you all the time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; 2 Corinthians 2:4; Romans 12:11"> serving the Lord with all humility, with many tears, and with trials which happened to me by the plots of the Jews;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:27; 2 Timothy 4:2; Acts 5:42"> how I didn’t shrink from declaring to you anything that was profitable, teaching you publicly and from house to house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 11:18; 1 John 5:11–13"> testifying both to Jews and to Greeks repentance toward God and faith toward our Lord Jesus.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="TR adds “Christ”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 4:14; 2 Corinthians 5:14; 2 Peter 1:14"> Now, behold, I go bound by the Spirit to Jerusalem, not knowing what will happen to me there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 9:16; 1 Thessalonians 3:3; Acts 21:4"> except that the Holy Spirit testifies in every city, saying that bonds and afflictions wait for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6–8; 2 Corinthians 4:1; 2 Corinthians 12:10"> But these things don’t count; nor do I hold my life dear to myself, so that I may finish my race with joy, and the ministry which I received from the Lord Jesus, to fully testify to the Good News of the grace of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 28:31; Acts 20:38; Matthew 4:23"> “Now, behold, I know that you all, among whom I went about preaching God’s Kingdom, will see my face no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Ezekiel 3:18–21; Ezekiel 33:2–9"> Therefore I testify to you today that I am clean from the blood of all men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 20:20; 1 Thessalonians 2:4; Galatians 1:7–10"> for I didn’t shrink from declaring to you the whole counsel of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 5:2–3; 1 Timothy 4:16; Isaiah 40:11"> Take heed, therefore, to yourselves and to all the flock, in which the Holy Spirit has made you overseers, to shepherd the assembly of the Lord and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="TR, NU omit “the Lord and”">c</a></sup>God which he purchased with his own blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; 2 Peter 2:1; John 10:12"> For I know that after my departure, vicious wolves will enter in among you, not sparing the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19–20; 2 Timothy 4:3–4; Proverbs 23:33"> Men will arise from among your own selves, speaking perverse things, to draw away the disciples after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:8; Acts 20:19; Acts 19:10"> Therefore watch, remembering that for a period of three years I didn’t cease to admonish everyone night and day with tears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 1:12; Ephesians 1:14; Colossians 3:24"> Now, brothers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here and where the context allows may also be correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">d</a></sup>I entrust you to God and to the word of his grace, which is able to build up and to give you the inheritance among all those who are sanctified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; 1 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Corinthians 9:18"> I coveted no one’s silver, gold, or clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 18:3; 1 Corinthians 4:12; 2 Thessalonians 3:8–9"> You yourselves know that these hands served my necessities, and those who were with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 19:17; 2 Corinthians 9:6–12"> In all things I gave you an example, that so laboring you ought to help the weak, and to remember the words of the Lord Jesus, that he himself said,<span className="woj">‘It is more blessed to give than to receive.’</span>”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 21:5; Luke 22:41; Philippians 4:6"> When he had spoken these things, he knelt down and prayed with them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:4; 1 Corinthians 16:20; 1 Thessalonians 5:26"> They all wept freely, and fell on Paul’s neck and kissed him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 15:3; 1 Corinthians 16:11; Acts 20:25"> sorrowing most of all because of the word which he had spoken, that they should see his face no more. Then they accompanied him to the ship.</span>
