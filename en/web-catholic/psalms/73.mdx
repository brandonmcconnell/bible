<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Isaiah 40:29–31; Psalms 138:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">My flesh and my heart fails,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but God is the strength of my heart and my portion forever.</span></span>
