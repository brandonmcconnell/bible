<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 3:11; Genesis 8:21; Numbers 9:2–3"> “Command the children of Israel, and tell them, ‘See that you present my offering, my food for my offerings made by fire, as a pleasant aroma to me, in their due season.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 6:9; Daniel 11:31; John 1:29"> You shall tell them, ‘This is the offering made by fire which you shall offer to the LORD: male lambs a year old without defect, two day by day, for a continual burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 9:21; 1 Kings 18:36; Exodus 12:6"> You shall offer the one lamb in the morning, and you shall offer the other lamb at evening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 16:36; Exodus 29:38–42"> with one tenth of an ephah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1 ephah is about 22 liters or about 2/3 of a bushel">a</a></sup>of fine flour for a meal offering, mixed with the fourth part of a hin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A hin is about 6.5 liters or 1.7 gallons.">b</a></sup>of beaten oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 5:25; Leviticus 6:9; Psalms 50:8"> It is a continual burnt offering which was ordained in Mount Sinai for a pleasant aroma, an offering made by fire to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 29:42; Numbers 28:14; Isaiah 57:6"> Its drink offering shall be the fourth part of a hin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="One hin is about 6.5 liters, so 1/4 hin is about 1.6 liters or 1.7 quarts.">c</a></sup>for each lamb. You shall pour out a drink offering of strong drink to the LORD in the holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The other lamb you shall offer at evening. As the meal offering of the morning, and as its drink offering, you shall offer it, an offering made by fire, for a pleasant aroma to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 58:13; Psalms 92:1–4; Revelation 1:10"> “‘On the Sabbath day, you shall offer two male lambs a year old without defect, and two tenths of an ephah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1 ephah is about 22 liters or about 2/3 of a bushel">d</a></sup>of fine flour for a meal offering mixed with oil, and its drink offering:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:4–5; Numbers 29:22; Numbers 29:31"> this is the burnt offering of every Sabbath, in addition to the continual burnt offering and its drink offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; Ezekiel 46:6; Ezra 3:5"> “‘In the beginnings of your months, you shall offer a burnt offering to the LORD: two young bulls, one ram, seven male lambs a year old without defect,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 15:4–12; Ezekiel 46:5–7; Numbers 29:10"> and three tenths of an ephah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1 ephah is about 22 liters or about 2/3 of a bushel">e</a></sup>of fine flour for a meal offering mixed with oil, for each bull; and two tenth parts of fine flour for a meal offering mixed with oil, for the one ram;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and one tenth part of fine flour mixed with oil for a meal offering to every lamb, as a burnt offering of a pleasant aroma, an offering made by fire to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Their drink offerings shall be half a hin of wine for a bull, the third part of a hin for the ram, and the fourth part of a hin for a lamb. This is the burnt offering of every month throughout the months of the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Numbers 28:22; Numbers 15:24"> Also, one male goat for a sin offering to the LORD shall be offered in addition to the continual burnt offering and its drink offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–8; Exodus 12:18; Leviticus 23:5–8"> “‘In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, is the LORD’s Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 23:6; Exodus 23:15; Exodus 13:6"> On the fifteenth day of this month shall be a feast. Unleavened bread shall be eaten for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 12:16; Leviticus 23:7–8"> In the first day shall be a holy convocation. You shall do no regular work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Ezekiel 45:21–25; Numbers 29:8"> but you shall offer an offering made by fire, a burnt offering to the LORD: two young bulls, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old. They shall be without defect,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> with their meal offering, fine flour mixed with oil. You shall offer three tenths for a bull, and two tenths for the ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> You shall offer one tenth for every lamb of the seven lambs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 8:3"> and one male goat for a sin offering, to make atonement for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Numbers 28:10"> You shall offer these in addition to the burnt offering of the morning, which is for a continual burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> In this way you shall offer daily, for seven days, the food of the offering made by fire, of a pleasant aroma to the LORD. It shall be offered in addition to the continual burnt offering and its drink offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:8; Numbers 28:18; Exodus 13:6"> On the seventh day you shall have a holy convocation. You shall do no regular work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Exodus 34:22; Leviticus 23:10"> “‘Also in the day of the first fruits, when you offer a new meal offering to the LORD in your feast of weeks, you shall have a holy convocation. You shall do no regular work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 23:18–19; Numbers 28:11; Numbers 28:19"> but you shall offer a burnt offering for a pleasant aroma to the LORD: two young bulls, one ram, seven male lambs a year old;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and their meal offering, fine flour mixed with oil, three tenths for each bull, two tenths for the one ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> one tenth for every lamb of the seven lambs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 28:15; Numbers 28:22; 1 Peter 2:24"> and one male goat, to make atonement for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 28:19; Numbers 28:3; Malachi 1:13–14"> Besides the continual burnt offering and its meal offering, you shall offer them and their drink offerings. See that they are without defect.</span>
