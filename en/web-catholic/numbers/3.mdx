<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 6:20; Genesis 10:1; Exodus 6:16"> Now this is the history of the generations of Aaron and Moses in the day that the LORD spoke with Moses in Mount Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:23; Numbers 26:60; Exodus 28:1"> These are the names of the sons of Aaron: Nadab the firstborn, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 28:41; Leviticus 8:30; Leviticus 8:12"> These are the names of the sons of Aaron, the priests who were anointed, whom he consecrated to minister in the priest’s office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–2; Numbers 26:61"> Nadab and Abihu died before the LORD when they offered strange fire before the LORD in the wilderness of Sinai, and they had no children. Eleazar and Ithamar ministered in the priest’s office in the presence of Aaron their father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Numbers 2:33; Numbers 1:49–53"> “Bring the tribe of Levi near, and set them before Aaron the priest, that they may minister to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 1:50; Numbers 8:15; Numbers 8:11"> They shall keep his requirements, and the requirements of the whole congregation before the Tent of Meeting, to do the service of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 4:33; Numbers 10:17; Isaiah 52:11"> They shall keep all the furnishings of the Tent of Meeting, and the obligations of the children of Israel, to do the service of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 8:19; Numbers 18:6–7; Ephesians 4:8"> You shall give the Levites to Aaron and to his sons. They are wholly given to him on the behalf of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 1:51; Numbers 3:38; Romans 12:7"> You shall appoint Aaron and his sons, and they shall keep their priesthood, but the stranger who comes near shall be put to death.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 3:41; Numbers 8:16; Numbers 18:6"> “Behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Behold”, from “הִנֵּה”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. It is often used as an interjection.">a</a></sup>I have taken the Levites from among the children of Israel instead of all the firstborn who open the womb among the children of Israel; and the Levites shall be mine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:23; Exodus 13:12; Exodus 13:2"> for all the firstborn are mine. On the day that I struck down all the firstborn in the land of Egypt I made holy to me all the firstborn in Israel, both man and animal. They shall be mine. I am the LORD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The LORD spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Mark 10:14; 2 Timothy 3:15"> “Count the children of Levi by their fathers’ houses, by their families. You shall count every male from a month old and upward.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 3:39; Numbers 4:37; Numbers 4:49"> Moses counted them according to the LORD’s word, as he was commanded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 46:11; Exodus 6:16–19; Numbers 15:5–23"> These were the sons of Levi by their names: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:17; 1 Chronicles 26:1–32; Exodus 6:17–19"> These are the names of the sons of Gershon by their families: Libni and Shimei.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Chronicles 15:17–21"> The sons of Kohath by their families: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 6:19; 1 Chronicles 6:19; Numbers 3:33"> The sons of Merari by their families: Mahli and Mushi.<br />These are the families of the Levites according to their fathers’ houses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Of Gershon was the family of the Libnites, and the family of the Shimeites. These are the families of the Gershonites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 4:38–40"> Those who were counted of them, according to the number of all the males from a month old and upward, even those who were counted of them were seven thousand five hundred.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:17"> The families of the Gershonites shall encamp behind the tabernacle westward.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Eliasaph the son of Lael shall be the prince of the fathers’ house of the Gershonites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 25:9; 1 Chronicles 23:32; 1 Chronicles 9:14–33"> The duty of the sons of Gershon in the Tent of Meeting shall be the tabernacle, the tent, its covering, the screen for the door of the Tent of Meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Exodus 38:9–16; Exodus 27:9–16"> the hangings of the court, the screen for the door of the court which is by the tabernacle and around the altar, and its cords for all of its service.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:23; Numbers 3:19; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> Of Kohath was the family of the Amramites, the family of the Izharites, the family of the Hebronites, and the family of the Uzzielites. These are the families of the Kohathites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 4:35–36; Numbers 3:7"> According to the number of all the males from a month old and upward, there were eight thousand six hundred keeping the requirements of the sanctuary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:23; Numbers 2:10"> The families of the sons of Kohath shall encamp on the south side of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> The prince of the fathers’ house of the families of the Kohathites shall be Elizaphan the son of Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 36:35–36; Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 26:31–33"> Their duty shall be the ark, the table, the lamp stand, the altars, the vessels of the sanctuary with which they minister, the screen, and all its service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 4:27; Numbers 4:16; Numbers 20:25–28"> Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest shall be prince of the princes of the Levites, with the oversight of those who keep the requirements of the sanctuary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 3:20; 1 Chronicles 6:19; 1 Chronicles 23:21"> Of Merari was the family of the Mahlites and the family of the Mushites. These are the families of Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 2:9; Numbers 2:11; Numbers 1:21"> Those who were counted of them, according to the number of all the males from a month old and upward, were six thousand two hundred.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="+ 22,000 is the sum rounded to 2 significant digits. The sum of the Gershonites, Kohathites, and Merarites given above is 22,300, but the traditional Hebrew text has the number rounded to 2 significant digits, not 3 significant digits.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:25; Numbers 3:28–29"> The prince of the fathers’ house of the families of Merari was Zuriel the son of Abihail. They shall encamp on the north side of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 26:32; Exodus 26:37; Exodus 26:15–29"> The appointed duty of the sons of Merari shall be the tabernacle’s boards, its bars, its pillars, its sockets, all its instruments, all its service,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> the pillars of the court around it, their sockets, their pins, and their cords.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 3:10; Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:3"> Those who encamp before the tabernacle eastward, in front of the Tent of Meeting toward the sunrise, shall be Moses, with Aaron and his sons, keeping the requirements of the sanctuary for the duty of the children of Israel. The outsider who comes near shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Matthew 7:14; Numbers 4:47–48"> All who were counted of the Levites, whom Moses and Aaron counted at the commandment of the LORD, by their families, all the males from a month old and upward, were twenty-two thousand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 3:15; Luke 10:20; Psalms 87:6"> The LORD said to Moses, “Count all the firstborn males of the children of Israel from a month old and upward, and take the number of their names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:45; Numbers 8:16"> You shall take the Levites for me—I am the LORD—instead of all the firstborn among the children of Israel; and the livestock of the Levites instead of all the firstborn among the livestock of the children of Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Moses counted, as the LORD commanded him, all the firstborn among the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 3:39"> All the firstborn males according to the number of names from a month old and upward, of those who were counted of them, were twenty-two thousand two hundred seventy-three.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:40–41"> “Take the Levites instead of all the firstborn among the children of Israel, and the livestock of the Levites instead of their livestock; and the Levites shall be mine. I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 13:13; Numbers 18:15–16"> For the redemption of the two hundred seventy-three of the firstborn of the children of Israel who exceed the number of the Levites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 27:6; Numbers 18:16"> you shall take five shekels apiece for each one; according to the shekel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.35 ounces.">c</a></sup>of the sanctuary you shall take them (the shekel is twenty gerahs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A gerah is about 0.5 grams or about 7.7 grains.">d</a></sup>);</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> and you shall give the money, with which their remainder is redeemed, to Aaron and to his sons.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> Moses took the redemption money from those who exceeded the number of those who were redeemed by the Levites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Numbers 3:46–47; 1 Peter 3:18; Matthew 20:28"> from the firstborn of the children of Israel he took the money, one thousand three hundred sixty-five shekels,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.35 ounces, so 1365 shekels is about 13.65 kilograms or about 30 pounds.">e</a></sup>according to the shekel of the sanctuary;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 3:48; Numbers 16:15; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> and Moses gave the redemption money to Aaron and to his sons, according to the LORD’s word, as the LORD commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
