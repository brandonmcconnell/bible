<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 21:32; Numbers 32:3; Genesis 13:10–11"> Now the children of Reuben and the children of Gad had a very great multitude of livestock. They saw the land of Jazer, and the land of Gilead. Behold, the place was a place for livestock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Then the children of Gad and the children of Reuben came and spoke to Moses, and to Eleazar the priest, and to the princes of the congregation, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:34; Isaiah 16:8–9; Numbers 32:34–38"> “Ataroth, Dibon, Jazer, Nimrah, Heshbon, Elealeh, Sebam, Nebo, and Beon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 21:34; Deuteronomy 2:24–35; Numbers 21:24"> the land which the LORD struck before the congregation of Israel, is a land for livestock; and your servants have livestock.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ruth 2:10; Joshua 7:7; Jeremiah 31:2"> They said, “If we have found favor in your sight, let this land be given to your servants for a possession. Don’t bring us over the Jordan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:5; 2 Samuel 11:11; Philippians 2:4"> Moses said to the children of Gad, and to the children of Reuben, “Shall your brothers go to war while you sit here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 13:27–4; Numbers 32:9; Deuteronomy 1:28"> Why do you discourage the heart of the children of Israel from going over into the land which the LORD has given them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:19–25; Numbers 14:2; Numbers 13:2–26"> Your fathers did so when I sent them from Kadesh Barnea to see the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 13:23–10; Deuteronomy 1:24–28"> For when they went up to the valley of Eshcol, and saw the land, they discouraged the heart of the children of Israel, that they should not go into the land which the LORD had given them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; Psalms 95:11; Ezekiel 20:15"> The LORD’s anger burned in that day, and he swore, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 14:28–30; Numbers 14:24; Numbers 26:2"> ‘Surely none of the men who came up out of Egypt, from twenty years old and upward, shall see the land which I swore to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob; because they have not wholly followed me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:36; Numbers 14:30; Numbers 14:24"> except Caleb the son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite, and Joshua the son of Nun, because they have followed the LORD completely.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5; Numbers 14:33–35; Hebrews 3:16–19"> The LORD’s anger burned against Israel, and he made them wander back and forth in the wilderness forty years, until all the generation who had done evil in the LORD’s sight was consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 11:48; Psalms 78:57; Matthew 23:31–33"> “Behold, you have risen up in your fathers’ place, an increase of sinful men, to increase the fierce anger of the LORD toward Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:11–12; 2 Chronicles 15:2; 2 Chronicles 7:19–22"> For if you turn away from after him, he will yet again leave them in the wilderness; and you will destroy all these people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 33:17; Numbers 34:22"> They came near to him, and said, “We will build sheepfolds here for our livestock, and cities for our little ones;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 4:12–13; Numbers 32:29–32; Deuteronomy 3:18–20"> but we ourselves will be ready armed to go before the children of Israel, until we have brought them to their place. Our little ones shall dwell in the fortified cities because of the inhabitants of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 22:1–5"> We will not return to our houses until the children of Israel have all received their inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 13:8; Joshua 12:1–6; Numbers 34:15"> For we will not inherit with them on the other side of the Jordan and beyond, because our inheritance has come to us on this side of the Jordan eastward.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 4:12–13; Deuteronomy 3:18–20; Joshua 1:13–15"> Moses said to them: “If you will do this thing, if you will arm yourselves to go before the LORD to the war,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and every one of your armed men will pass over the Jordan before the LORD until he has driven out his enemies from before him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 22:9; Joshua 22:4; Deuteronomy 3:20"> and the land is subdued before the LORD; then afterward you shall return, and be clear of obligation to the LORD and to Israel. Then this land shall be your possession before the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 59:12; Proverbs 13:21; Genesis 44:16"> “But if you will not do so, behold, you have sinned against the LORD; and be sure your sin will find you out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 32:16; Numbers 30:2; Numbers 32:34–42"> Build cities for your little ones, and folds for your sheep; and do that which has proceeded out of your mouth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 1:13–14"> The children of Gad and the children of Reuben spoke to Moses, saying, “Your servants will do as my lord commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 1:14"> Our little ones, our wives, our flocks, and all our livestock shall be there in the cities of Gilead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 4:12; Numbers 32:17; Numbers 12:11"> but your servants will pass over, every man who is armed for war, before the LORD to battle, as my lord says.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 1:13"> So Moses commanded concerning them to Eleazar the priest, and to Joshua the son of Nun, and to the heads of the fathers’ households of the tribes of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Moses said to them, “If the children of Gad and the children of Reuben will pass with you over the Jordan, every man who is armed to battle before the LORD, and the land is subdued before you, then you shall give them the land of Gilead for a possession;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 22:19"> but if they will not pass over with you armed, they shall have possessions among you in the land of Canaan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> The children of Gad and the children of Reuben answered, saying, “As the LORD has said to your servants, so will we do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> We will pass over armed before the LORD into the land of Canaan, and the possession of our inheritance shall remain with us beyond the Jordan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 12:6; Deuteronomy 29:8; Deuteronomy 3:12–17"> Moses gave to them, even to the children of Gad, and to the children of Reuben, and to the half-tribe of Manasseh the son of Joseph, the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites, and the kingdom of Og king of Bashan; the land, according to its cities and borders, even the cities of the surrounding land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36; Numbers 32:3; Isaiah 17:2"> The children of Gad built Dibon, Ataroth, Aroer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 32:1; Numbers 32:3"> Atroth-shophan, Jazer, Jogbehah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 32:3"> Beth Nimrah, and Beth Haran: fortified cities and folds for sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 21:27; Numbers 32:3; Isaiah 15:4"> The children of Reuben built Heshbon, Elealeh, Kiriathaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 46:1; Numbers 32:3; Joshua 23:7"> Nebo, and Baal Meon, (their names being changed), and Sibmah. They gave other names to the cities which they built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 50:23; Joshua 17:1; Numbers 26:29"> The children of Machir the son of Manasseh went to Gilead, took it, and dispossessed the Amorites who were therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Joshua 17:1; Joshua 13:29–31; Deuteronomy 3:13–15"> Moses gave Gilead to Machir the son of Manasseh; and he lived therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:14; Judges 10:4; Joshua 13:30"> Jair the son of Manasseh went and took its villages, and called them Havvoth Jair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 49:11; 2 Samuel 18:18"> Nobah went and took Kenath and its villages, and called it Nobah, after his own name.</span>
