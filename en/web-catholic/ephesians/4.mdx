<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Philippians 1:27; 1 Thessalonians 2:12"> I therefore, the prisoner in the Lord, beg you to walk worthily of the calling with which you were called,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; 1 Corinthians 13:7; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5"> with all lowliness and humility, with patience, bearing with one another in love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:10; Colossians 3:13–15; 1 Corinthians 12:12–13"> being eager to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Romans 12:4–5; 1 Corinthians 12:4–13"> There is one body and one Spirit, even as you also were called in one hope of your calling,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:6; 1 Corinthians 12:13; 1 Peter 3:21"> one Lord, one faith, one baptism,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Malachi 2:10; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> one God and Father of all, who is over all and through all and in us all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> But to each one of us, the grace was given according to the measure of the gift of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 68:18; Colossians 2:15; 1 Samuel 30:26"> Therefore he says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“When he ascended on high,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he led captivity captive,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and gave gifts to people.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:13; Matthew 12:40; Proverbs 30:4"> Now this, “He ascended”, what is it but that he also first descended into the lower parts of the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 7:26; Hebrews 4:14; Ephesians 1:20–23"> He who descended is the one who also ascended far above all the heavens, that he might fill all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:28–29; Ephesians 2:20"> He gave some to be apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, shepherds<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, pastors">a</a></sup>and teachers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11–14; Ephesians 4:16; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> for the perfecting of the saints, to the work of serving, to the building up of the body of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:28; John 17:21; Ephesians 4:5"> until we all attain to the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the Son of God, to a full grown man, to the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:9; Romans 16:17–18; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> that we may no longer be children, tossed back and forth and carried about with every wind of doctrine, by the trickery of men, in craftiness, after the wiles of error;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 3:18; Ephesians 4:25; Zechariah 8:16"> but speaking truth in love, we may grow up in all things into him who is the head, Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 2:19; John 15:5; Colossians 2:2"> from whom all the body, being fitted and knit together through that which every joint supplies, according to the working in measure of each individual part, makes the body increase to the building up of itself in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:21; 1 Peter 4:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> This I say therefore, and testify in the Lord, that you no longer walk as the rest of the Gentiles also walk, in the futility of their mind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Ephesians 2:12; Romans 1:21–23"> being darkened in their understanding, alienated from the life of God because of the ignorance that is in them, because of the hardening of their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Romans 1:24–26; 1 Timothy 4:2"> They, having become callous, gave themselves up to lust, to work all uncleanness with greediness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 2:11–14; John 6:45; Matthew 11:29"> But you didn’t learn Christ that way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 5:20; Ephesians 1:13; Hebrews 3:7–8"> if indeed you heard him and were taught in him, even as truth is in Jesus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Hebrews 12:1; James 1:21"> that you put away, as concerning your former way of life, the old man that grows corrupt after the lusts of deceit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Romans 8:6; Psalms 51:10"> and that you be renewed in the spirit of your mind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:10–14; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ephesians 2:10"> and put on the new man, who in the likeness of God has been created in righteousness and holiness of truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Zechariah 8:16; Proverbs 12:22"> Therefore, putting away falsehood, speak truth each one with his neighbor, for we are members of one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 1:19; Psalms 4:4; Psalms 37:8"> “Be angry, and don’t sin.” Don’t let the sun go down on your wrath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 4:7; 1 Peter 5:8; Ephesians 6:16"> and don’t give place<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or, opportunity">b</a></sup>to the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12; Galatians 6:10"> Let him who stole steal no more; but rather let him labor, producing with his hands something that is good, that he may have something to give to him who has need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:11; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> Let no corrupt speech proceed out of your mouth, but only what is good for building others up as the need may be, that it may give grace to those who hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:19; Isaiah 63:10; Ephesians 1:13–14"> Don’t grieve the Holy Spirit of God, in whom you were sealed for the day of redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Colossians 3:8; Ecclesiastes 7:9; Titus 3:2–3"> Let all bitterness, wrath, anger, outcry, and slander be put away from you, with all malice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; Matthew 6:14–15; Mark 11:25"> And be kind to one another, tender hearted, forgiving each other, just as God also in Christ forgave you.</span>
