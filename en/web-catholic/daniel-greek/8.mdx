<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> In the third year of the reign of King Belshazzar, a vision appeared to me, even to me, Daniel, after that which appeared to me at the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> I saw the vision. Now it was so, that when I saw, I was in the citadel of Susa, which is in the province of Elam. I saw in the vision, and I was by the river Ulai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then I lifted up my eyes, and saw, and behold, a ram which had two horns stood before the river. The two horns were high; but one was higher than the other, and the higher came up last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> I saw the ram pushing westward, northward, and southward. No animals could stand before him. There wasn’t anyone who could deliver out of his hand; but he did according to his will, and magnified himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> As I was considering, behold, a male goat came from the west over the surface of the whole earth, and didn’t touch the ground. The goat had a notable horn between his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He came to the ram that had the two horns, which I saw standing before the river, and ran on him in the fury of his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> I saw him come close to the ram, and he was moved with anger against him, and struck the ram, and broke his two horns. There was no power in the ram to stand before him; but he cast him down to the ground, and trampled on him. There was no one who could deliver the ram out of his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The male goat magnified himself exceedingly. When he was strong, the great horn was broken; and instead of it there came up four notable horns toward the four winds of the sky.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Out of one of them came out a little horn, which grew exceedingly great, toward the south, and toward the east, and toward the glorious land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> It grew great, even to the army of the sky; and it cast down some of the army and of the stars to the ground, and trampled on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Yes, it magnified itself, even to the prince of the army; and it took away from him the continual burnt offering, and the place of his sanctuary was cast down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The army was given over to it together with the continual burnt offering through disobedience. It cast down truth to the ground, and it did its pleasure and prospered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then I heard a holy one speaking; and another holy one said to that certain one who spoke, “How long will the vision about the continual burnt offering, and the disobedience that makes desolate, to give both the sanctuary and the army to be trodden under foot be?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He said to me, “To two thousand and three hundred evenings and mornings. Then the sanctuary will be cleansed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> When I, even I Daniel, had seen the vision, I sought to understand it. Then behold, there stood before me something like the appearance of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> I heard a man’s voice between the banks of the Ulai, which called, and said, “Gabriel, make this man understand the vision.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> So he came near where I stood; and when he came, I was frightened, and fell on my face; but he said to me, “Understand, son of man; for the vision belongs to the time of the end.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Now as he was speaking with me, I fell into a deep sleep with my face toward the ground; but he touched me, and set me upright.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> He said, “Behold, I will make you know what will be in the latter time of the indignation; for it belongs to the appointed time of the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The ram which you saw, that had the two horns, they are the kings of Media and Persia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The rough male goat is the king of Greece. The great horn that is between his eyes is the first king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> As for that which was broken, in the place where four stood up, four kingdoms will stand up out of the nation, but not with his power.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> “In the latter time of their kingdom, when the transgressors have come to the full, a king of fierce face, and understanding dark sentences, will stand up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> His power will be mighty, but not by his own power. He will destroy awesomely, and will prosper in what he does. He will destroy the mighty ones and the holy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Through his policy he will cause deceit to prosper in his hand. He will magnify himself in his heart, and he will destroy many in their security. He will also stand up against the prince of princes; but he will be broken without human power.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> “The vision of the evenings and mornings which has been told is true; but seal up the vision, for it belongs to many days to come.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> I, Daniel, fainted, and was sick for some days. Then I rose up, and did the king’s business. I wondered at the vision, but no one understood it.</span>
</div>
</div>
