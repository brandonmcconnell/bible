<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Leviticus 11:44; Ezekiel 20:5"> “Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them, ‘I am the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Exodus 23:24; 1 Peter 4:2–4"> You shall not do as they do in the land of Egypt, where you lived. You shall not do as they do in the land of Canaan, where I am bringing you. You shall not follow their statutes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 18:2; Leviticus 18:26; Ezekiel 20:19"> You shall do my ordinances. You shall keep my statutes and walk in them. I am the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Galatians 3:12"> You shall therefore keep my statutes and my ordinances, which if a man does, he shall live in them. I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17–21; Leviticus 18:7–19; Leviticus 20:11–12"> “‘None of you shall approach any close relatives, to uncover their nakedness: I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; Ezekiel 22:10; Leviticus 18:8–16"> “‘You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father, nor the nakedness of your mother: she is your mother. You shall not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; 1 Corinthians 5:1; Deuteronomy 27:20"> “‘You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father’s wife. It is your father’s nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Deuteronomy 27:22; Ezekiel 22:11"> “‘You shall not uncover the nakedness of your sister, the daughter of your father, or the daughter of your mother, whether born at home or born abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> “‘You shall not uncover the nakedness of your son’s daughter, or of your daughter’s daughter, even their nakedness; for theirs is your own nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> “‘You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father’s wife’s daughter, conceived by your father, since she is your sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 20:19; Exodus 6:20"> “‘You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father’s sister. She is your father’s near kinswoman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> “‘You shall not uncover the nakedness of your mother’s sister, for she is your mother’s near kinswoman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20"> “‘You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father’s brother. You shall not approach his wife. She is your aunt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 20:12; Genesis 38:26; Ezekiel 22:11"> “‘You shall not uncover the nakedness of your daughter-in-law. She is your son’s wife. You shall not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Leviticus 20:21; Matthew 22:24"> “‘You shall not uncover the nakedness of your brother’s wife. It is your brother’s nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Amos 2:7; Deuteronomy 27:23"> “‘You shall not uncover the nakedness of a woman and her daughter. You shall not take her son’s daughter, or her daughter’s daughter, to uncover her nakedness. They are near kinswomen. It is wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachi 2:15; Genesis 30:15; Genesis 29:28"> “‘You shall not take a wife in addition to her sister, to be a rival, to uncover her nakedness, while her sister is still alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 20:18; Leviticus 15:24; Ezekiel 22:10"> “‘You shall not approach a woman to uncover her nakedness, as long as she is impure by her uncleanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; 1 Corinthians 6:9; Hebrews 13:4"> “‘You shall not lie carnally with your neighbor’s wife, and defile yourself with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:31; Leviticus 20:2–5; Deuteronomy 18:10"> “‘You shall not give any of your children as a sacrifice to Molech. You shall not profane the name of your God. I am the LORD.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LORD or GOD in all caps is from the Hebrew יהוה Yahweh except when otherwise noted as being from the short form יה Yah.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:13; Romans 1:26–27; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> “‘You shall not lie with a man as with a woman. That is detestable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15–16; Leviticus 20:12"> “‘You shall not lie with any animal to defile yourself with it. No woman may give herself to an animal, to lie down with it: it is a perversion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:12; Leviticus 18:30; Leviticus 20:22–23"> “‘Don’t defile yourselves in any of these things; for in all these the nations which I am casting out before you were defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 18:28; Leviticus 20:22–23; Jeremiah 2:7"> The land was defiled. Therefore I punished its iniquity, and the land vomited out her inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Leviticus 17:10; Leviticus 18:30"> You therefore shall keep my statutes and my ordinances, and shall not do any of these abominations; neither the native-born, nor the stranger who lives as a foreigner among you</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:14; Leviticus 18:24; Deuteronomy 23:18"> (for the men of the land that were before you had done all these abominations, and the land became defiled),</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 3:16; Leviticus 20:22; Leviticus 18:25"> that the land not vomit you out also, when you defile it, as it vomited out the nation that was before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:6; Exodus 12:15; Leviticus 17:10"> “‘For whoever shall do any of these abominations, even the souls that do them shall be cut off from among their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Deuteronomy 18:9–12; Leviticus 22:9"> Therefore you shall keep my requirements, that you do not practice any of these abominable customs which were practiced before you, and that you do not defile yourselves with them. I am the LORD your God.’”</span>
