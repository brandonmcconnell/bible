They entered into the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 11:9; Deuteronomy 11:30; Genesis 35:4"> Abram passed through the land to the place of Shechem, to the oak of Moreh. At that time, Canaanites were in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 18:1; Genesis 13:15"> The LORD appeared to Abram and said, “I will give this land to your offspring.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, seed">a</a></sup>He built an altar there to the LORD, who had appeared to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:31; Genesis 4:26; Genesis 21:33"> He left from there to go to the mountain on the east of Bethel and pitched his tent, having Bethel on the west, and Ai on the east. There he built an altar to the LORD<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="LORD or GOD in all caps is from the Hebrew יהוה Yahweh except when otherwise noted as being from the short form יה Yah.">b</a></sup>and called on the LORD’s name.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 43:1; Genesis 26:1–3; 1 Kings 17:1–18"> There was a famine in the land. Abram went down into Egypt to live as a foreigner there, for the famine was severe in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 29:17; Genesis 12:14"> When he had come near to enter Egypt, he said to Sarai his wife, “See now, I know that you are a beautiful woman to look at.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 20:11; Genesis 26:7; 1 John 1:8–10"> It will happen that when the Egyptians see you, they will say, ‘This is his wife.’ They will kill me, but they will save you alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 20:12–13; Genesis 20:2"> Please say that you are my sister, that it may be well with me for your sake, and that my soul may live because of you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 6:2; Matthew 5:28; Genesis 39:7"> When Abram had come into Egypt, some Egyptians saw that the woman was very beautiful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 20:2; Genesis 41:1; 2 Kings 18:21"> The princes of Pharaoh saw her, and praised her to Pharaoh; and the woman was taken into Pharaoh’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 20:14; Genesis 13:2; Genesis 24:35"> He dealt well with Abram for her sake. He had sheep, cattle, male donkeys, male servants, female servants, female donkeys, and camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:21; Genesis 20:18; Psalms 105:14–15"> The LORD afflicted Pharaoh and his house with great plagues because of Sarai, Abram’s wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 26:9–11; 1 Samuel 14:43"> Pharaoh called Abram and said, “What is this that you have done to me? Why didn’t you tell me that she was your wife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Why did you say, ‘She is my sister,’ so that I took her to be my wife? Now therefore, see your wife, take her, and go your way.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 105:14–15; Proverbs 21:1; Exodus 18:27"> Pharaoh commanded men concerning him, and they escorted him away with his wife and all that he had.</span>
