<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:17; James 2:21; Deuteronomy 8:2"> After these things, God tested Abraham, and said to him, “Abraham!” He said, “Here I am.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 3:16; Romans 8:32; Hebrews 11:17"> He said, “Now take your son, your only son, Isaac, whom you love, and go into the land of Moriah. Offer him there as a burnt offering on one of the mountains which I will tell you of.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; Matthew 10:37; Luke 14:26"> Abraham rose early in the morning, and saddled his donkey; and took two of his young men with him, and Isaac his son. He split the wood for the burnt offering, and rose up, and went to the place of which God had told him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 13:32; Matthew 17:23; Hosea 6:2"> On the third day Abraham lifted up his eyes, and saw the place far off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:19; Hebrews 12:1"> Abraham said to his young men, “Stay here with the donkey. The boy and I will go over there. We will worship, and come back to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 19:17; 1 Peter 2:24; Isaiah 53:6"> Abraham took the wood of the burnt offering and laid it on Isaac his son. He took in his hand the fire and the knife. They both went together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:15; Matthew 26:39; Genesis 8:20"> Isaac spoke to Abraham his father, and said, “My father?” He said, “Here I am, my son.” He said, “Here is the fire and the wood, but where is the lamb for a burnt offering?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:36; John 1:29; Revelation 5:12"> Abraham said, “God will provide himself the lamb for a burnt offering, my son.” So they both went together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 2:21; Hebrews 11:17–19; Philippians 2:7–8"> They came to the place which God had told him of. Abraham built the altar there, and laid the wood in order, bound Isaac his son, and laid him on the altar, on the wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 2:21–23; Hebrews 11:17–19; Isaiah 53:6–12"> Abraham stretched out his hand, and took the knife to kill his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 21:17; Exodus 3:4; Genesis 16:9–10"> The LORD’s angel called to him out of the sky, and said, “Abraham, Abraham!” He said, “Here I am.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 2:21–22; James 2:18; 1 Samuel 15:22"> He said, “Don’t lay your hand on the boy or do anything to him. For now I know that you fear God, since you have not withheld your son, your only son, from me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Psalms 40:6–8; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> Abraham lifted up his eyes, and looked, and saw that behind him was a ram caught in the thicket by his horns. Abraham went and took the ram, and offered him up for a burnt offering instead of his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 22:13; Exodus 17:15; Genesis 22:8"> Abraham called the name of that place “The LORD Will Provide”.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, the LORD Jireh, or, the LORD Seeing">a</a></sup>As it is said to this day, “On the LORD’s mountain, it will be provided.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The LORD’s angel called to Abraham a second time out of the sky,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13–14; Luke 1:73; Psalms 105:9"> and said, “‘I have sworn by myself,’ says the LORD, ‘because you have done this thing, and have not withheld your son, your only son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; Genesis 24:60; Jeremiah 33:22"> that I will bless you greatly, and I will multiply your offspring greatly like the stars of the heavens, and like the sand which is on the seashore. Your offspring will possess the gate of his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 3:25; Genesis 12:3; Galatians 3:16"> All the nations of the earth will be blessed by your offspring, because you have obeyed my voice.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Judges 20:1; Joshua 15:28"> So Abraham returned to his young men, and they rose up and went together to Beersheba. Abraham lived at Beersheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 11:29; Genesis 24:24; Proverbs 25:25"> After these things, Abraham was told, “Behold, Milcah, she also has borne children to your brother Nahor:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; Job 32:2; Psalms 60:1–12"> Uz his firstborn, Buz his brother, Kemuel the father of Aram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Chesed, Hazo, Pildash, Jidlaph, and Bethuel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 24:15; Genesis 28:5; Genesis 24:51"> Bethuel became the father of Rebekah. These eight Milcah bore to Nahor, Abraham’s brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 16:3; Genesis 25:6; Proverbs 15:25"> His concubine, whose name was Reumah, also bore Tebah, Gaham, Tahash, and Maacah.</span>
