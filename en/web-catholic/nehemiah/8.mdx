<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezra 10:29; Nehemiah 12:13; Nehemiah 10:20"> Ezra the scribe stood on a pulpit of wood, which they had made for the purpose; and beside him stood Mattithiah, Shema, Anaiah, Uriah, Hilkiah, and Maaseiah, on his right hand; and on his left hand, Pedaiah, Mishael, Malchijah, Hashum, Hashbaddanah, Zechariah, and Meshullam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 3:20; 1 Kings 8:14; Luke 4:16–17"> Ezra opened the book in the sight of all the people (for he was above all the people), and when he opened it, all the people stood up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:8; Lamentations 3:41; Exodus 4:31"> Then Ezra blessed the LORD, the great God.<br />All the people answered, “Amen, Amen,” with the lifting up of their hands. <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:4; Leviticus 10:11; Nehemiah 3:23"> Also Jeshua, Bani, Sherebiah, Jamin, Akkub, Shabbethai, Hodiah, Maaseiah, Kelita, Azariah, Jozabad, Hanan, Pelaiah, and the Levites, caused the people to understand the law; and the people stayed in their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 24:45; Acts 28:23; Luke 24:32"> They read in the book, in the law of God, distinctly; and they gave the sense, so that they understood the reading.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

Don’t mourn, nor weep.” For all the people wept when they heard the words of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 28:7–8; Proverbs 17:22; 2 Corinthians 12:8–9"> Then he said to them, “Go your way. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 13:30"> So the Levites calmed all the people, saying, “Hold your peace, for the day is holy. Don’t be grieved.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 119:174; Psalms 119:111; Psalms 119:130"> All the people went their way to eat, to drink, to send portions, and to celebrate, because they had understood the words that were declared to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 2:1–6; Proverbs 8:33–34; Proverbs 12:1"> On the second day, the heads of fathers’ households of all the people, the priests, and the Levites were gathered together to Ezra the scribe, to study the words of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 23:34; Deuteronomy 16:13–15; Leviticus 23:40–43"> They found written in the law how the LORD had commanded by Moses that the children of Israel should dwell in booths in the feast of the seventh month;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40; Deuteronomy 16:16; Leviticus 23:4"> and that they should publish and proclaim in all their cities and in Jerusalem, saying, “Go out to the mountain, and get olive branches, branches of wild olive, myrtle branches, palm branches, and branches of thick trees, to make temporary shelters,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, booths">a</a></sup>as it is written.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:39; 2 Kings 14:13; Nehemiah 12:37"> So the people went out and brought them, and made themselves temporary shelters,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or, booths">b</a></sup>everyone on the roof of his house, in their courts, in the courts of God’s house, in the wide place of the water gate, and in the wide place of Ephraim’s gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:13; Ezra 3:4; 2 Chronicles 7:8–10"> All the assembly of those who had come back out of the captivity made temporary shelters<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, booths">c</a></sup>and lived in the temporary shelters, for since the days of Joshua the son of Nun to that day the children of Israel had not done so. There was very great gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 29:35; Leviticus 23:36; Deuteronomy 31:10–13"> Also day by day, from the first day to the last day, he read in the book of the law of God. They kept the feast seven days; and on the eighth day was a solemn assembly, according to the ordinance.</span>
