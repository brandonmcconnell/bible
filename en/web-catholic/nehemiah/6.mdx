<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; Nehemiah 2:10; Nehemiah 4:1"> Now when it was reported to Sanballat, Tobiah, Geshem the Arabian, and to the rest of our enemies that I had built the wall, and that there was no breach left in it (though even to that time I had not set up the doors in the gates),</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:12; 1 Chronicles 8:12; Nehemiah 11:35"> Sanballat and Geshem sent to me, saying, “Come! Let’s meet together in the villages in the plain of Ono.” But they intended to harm me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 9:4; Matthew 10:16; 1 Timothy 4:15–16"> I sent messengers to them, saying, “I am doing a great work, so that I can’t come down. Why should the work cease while I leave it and come down to you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 16:15–20; Luke 18:5; Galatians 2:5"> They sent to me four times like this; and I answered them the same way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 6:11; 2 Corinthians 2:11; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> Then Sanballat sent his servant to me the same way the fifth time with an open letter in his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; Ezra 4:12; Romans 3:8"> in which was written, “It is reported among the nations, and Gashmu says it, that you and the Jews intend to rebel. Because of that, you are building the wall. You would be their king, according to these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–26; 1 Kings 1:7; 1 Kings 1:18"> You have also appointed prophets to proclaim of you at Jerusalem, saying, ‘There is a king in Judah!’ Now it will be reported to the king according to these words. Come now therefore, and let’s take counsel together.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 52:2; Job 13:4; Matthew 12:34"> Then I sent to him, saying, “There are no such things done as you say, but you imagine them out of your own heart.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 138:3; 1 Peter 5:10; Ephesians 3:16"> For they all would have made us afraid, saying, “Their hands will be weakened from the work, that it not be done.” But now, strengthen my hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:5; Matthew 7:15; Psalms 37:12"> I went to the house of Shemaiah the son of Delaiah the son of Mehetabel, who was shut in at his home; and he said, “Let us meet together in God’s house, within the temple, and let’s shut the doors of the temple; for they will come to kill you. Yes, in the night they will come to kill you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 11:1–2; Acts 21:13; Proverbs 28:1"> I said, “Should a man like me flee? Who is there that, being such as I, would go into the temple to save his life? I will not go in.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:22; 1 Timothy 3:3; Ezekiel 13:7"> I discerned, and behold, God had not sent him, but he pronounced this prophecy against me. Tobiah and Sanballat had hired him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; 2 Corinthians 11:12; 2 Timothy 1:7"> He was hired so that I would be afraid, do so, and sin, and that they might have material for an evil report, that they might reproach me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:29; 2 Timothy 4:14–15; Isaiah 9:14–15"> “Remember, my God, Tobiah and Sanballat according to these their works, and also the prophetess Noadiah and the rest of the prophets that would have put me in fear.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Daniel 9:25; Nehemiah 4:1–2"> So the wall was finished in the twenty-fifth day of Elul, in fifty-two days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 126:2; Joshua 5:1; Nehemiah 4:7"> When all our enemies heard of it, all the nations that were around us were afraid, and they lost their confidence; for they perceived that this work was done by our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Micah 7:1–6; Matthew 24:10–12; Nehemiah 3:5"> Moreover in those days the nobles of Judah sent many letters to Tobiah, and Tobiah’s letters came to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezra 2:5; Nehemiah 7:10; Nehemiah 3:4"> For there were many in Judah sworn to him because he was the son-in-law of Shecaniah the son of Arah; and his son Jehohanan had taken the daughter of Meshullam the son of Berechiah as wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 4:18–21; Nehemiah 6:9; John 7:7"> Also they spoke of his good deeds before me, and reported my words to him. Tobiah sent letters to put me in fear.</span>
