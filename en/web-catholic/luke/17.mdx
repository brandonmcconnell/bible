<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:7; Romans 14:13; 1 Corinthians 11:19"> He said to the disciples,<span className="woj">“It is impossible that no occasions of stumbling should come, but woe to him through whom they come!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 9:42; Matthew 18:14; Matthew 18:3–6"> <span className="woj">It would be better for him if a millstone were hung around his neck, and he were thrown into the sea, rather than that he should cause one of these little ones to stumble.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 19:17; Matthew 18:21; Matthew 18:15–17"> <span className="woj">Be careful. If your brother sins against you, rebuke him. If he repents, forgive him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; Matthew 18:21–22; Romans 12:20"> <span className="woj">If he sins against you seven times in the day, and seven times returns, saying, ‘I repent,’ you shall forgive him.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Philippians 4:13"> The apostles said to the Lord, “Increase our faith.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 17:20; Matthew 21:21; Mark 9:23"> The Lord said,<span className="woj">“If you had faith like a grain of mustard seed, you would tell this sycamore tree, ‘Be uprooted and be planted in the sea,’ and it would obey you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:11; Luke 13:15; Luke 14:5"> <span className="woj">But who is there among you, having a servant plowing or keeping sheep, that will say when he comes in from the field, ‘Come immediately and sit down at the table’?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 12:37; Genesis 43:16; 2 Samuel 12:20"> <span className="woj">Wouldn’t he rather tell him, ‘Prepare my supper, clothe yourself properly, and serve me while I eat and drink. Afterward you shall eat and drink’?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="woj">Does he thank that servant because he did the things that were commanded? I think not.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:16–17; Job 22:2–3; 1 Peter 5:5–6"> <span className="woj">Even so you also, when you have done all the things that are commanded you, say, ‘We are unworthy servants. We have done our duty.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 9:51–52; John 4:3–4"> As he was on his way to Jerusalem, he was passing along the borders of Samaria and Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46; Luke 5:12; 2 Kings 7:3"> As he entered into a certain village, ten men who were lepers met him, who stood at a distance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Matthew 20:30–31; Matthew 9:27"> They lifted up their voices, saying, “Jesus, Master, have mercy on us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:14; Leviticus 13:1–46; 2 Kings 5:14"> When he saw them, he said to them,<span className="woj">“Go and show yourselves to the priests.”</span>As they went, they were cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 30:11–12; Psalms 103:1–4; Psalms 118:18–19"> One of them, when he saw that he was healed, turned back, glorifying God with a loud voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 8:5–25; Matthew 10:5; Revelation 19:10"> He fell on his face at Jesus’ feet, giving him thanks; and he was a Samaritan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:21; Psalms 106:13; Genesis 3:9"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“Weren’t the ten cleansed? But where are the nine?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 50:23; Matthew 15:24–28; Psalms 106:13"> <span className="woj">Were there none found who returned to give glory to God, except this foreigner?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Luke 8:48; Mark 10:52"> Then he said to him,<span className="woj">“Get up, and go your way. Your faith has healed you.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 18:36; Luke 19:11; Daniel 2:44"> Being asked by the Pharisees when God’s Kingdom would come, he answered them,<span className="woj">“God’s Kingdom doesn’t come with observation;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 12:28; Romans 14:17; Mark 13:21"> <span className="woj">neither will they say, ‘Look, here!’ or, ‘Look, there!’ for behold, God’s Kingdom is within you.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 5:35; Matthew 9:15; John 8:21–24"> He said to the disciples,<span className="woj">“The days will come when you will desire to see one of the days of the Son of Man, and you will not see it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Matthew 24:23–26; Luke 17:21"> <span className="woj">They will tell you, ‘Look, here!’ or ‘Look, there!’ Don’t go away or follow after them,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 24:27; Matthew 24:30; Malachi 4:1–2"> <span className="woj">for as the lightning, when it flashes out of one part under the sky, shines to another part under the sky, so will the Son of Man be in his day.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Luke 9:22; Mark 8:31"> <span className="woj">But first, he must suffer many things and be rejected by this generation.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 24:37–39; Luke 17:24; Genesis 7:7–23"> <span className="woj">As it was in the days of Noah, even so it will also be in the days of the Son of Man.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 12:19–20; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–3; Luke 16:19–23"> <span className="woj">They ate, they drank, they married, and they were given in marriage until the day that Noah entered into the ship, and the flood came and destroyed them all.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:49–50; Genesis 18:20–21; James 5:1–5"> <span className="woj">Likewise, even as it was in the days of Lot: they ate, they drank, they bought, they sold, they planted, they built;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6; Zephaniah 2:9; Jude 1:7"> <span className="woj">but in the day that Lot went out from Sodom, it rained fire and sulfur from the sky and destroyed them all.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:7; Revelation 1:7"> <span className="woj">It will be the same way in the day that the Son of Man is revealed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 21:21; Mark 13:14–16; Jeremiah 45:5"> <span className="woj">In that day, he who will be on the housetop and his goods in the house, let him not go down to take them away. Let him who is in the field likewise not turn back.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 19:26; Hebrews 10:38–39; 2 Peter 2:18–22"> <span className="woj">Remember Lot’s wife!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:35–37; Matthew 10:39; John 12:25"> <span className="woj">Whoever seeks to save his life loses it, but whoever loses his life preserves it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 24:40–41; 2 Peter 2:9; Malachi 3:16–18"> <span className="woj">I tell you, in that night there will be two people in one bed. One will be taken and the other will be left.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:41; Exodus 11:5; Judges 16:21"> <span className="woj">There will be two grinding grain together. One will be taken and the other will be left.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some Greek manuscripts add: “Two will be in the field: the one taken, and the other left.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 24:28; Job 39:29–30; Revelation 19:17–18"> They, answering, asked him, “Where, Lord?” He said to them,<span className="woj">“Where the body is, there the vultures will also be gathered together.”</span></span>
