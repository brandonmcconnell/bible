<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 14:12; Luke 10:19; Matthew 16:19"> He called the twelve<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR reads “his twelve disciples” instead of “the twelve”">a</a></sup>together and gave them power and authority over all demons, and to cure diseases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 10:7–8; Luke 10:9; Matthew 3:2"> He sent them out to preach God’s Kingdom and to heal the sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Psalms 37:3; Luke 10:4–12"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Take nothing for your journey—no staffs, nor wallet, nor bread, nor money. Don’t have two tunics each.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:11; Mark 6:10; Luke 10:5–8"> <span className="woj">Into whatever house you enter, stay there, and depart from there.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:51; Acts 18:6; Mark 6:11"> <span className="woj">As many as don’t receive you, when you depart from that city, shake off even the dust from your feet for a testimony against them.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 4:30; Mark 16:20; Acts 5:15"> They departed and went throughout the villages, preaching the Good News and healing everywhere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 6:14–28; Matthew 14:1–12; Luke 3:1"> Now Herod the tetrarch heard of all that was done by him; and he was very perplexed, because it was said by some that John had risen from the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:21; Luke 9:19; Mark 6:15"> and by some that Elijah had appeared, and by others that one of the old prophets had risen again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 23:8; Luke 13:31–32"> Herod said, “I beheaded John, but who is this about whom I hear such things?” He sought to see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 11:21; Hebrews 13:17; Matthew 14:13–21"> The apostles, when they had returned, told him what things they had done. He took them and withdrew apart to a desert region of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="NU omits “a desert region of”.">b</a></sup>a city called Bethsaida.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 9:2; Luke 5:31; Hebrews 4:16"> But the multitudes, perceiving it, followed him. He welcomed them, spoke to them of God’s Kingdom, and he cured those who needed healing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:5–15; Hosea 13:5"> The day began to wear away; and the twelve came and said to him, “Send the multitude away, that they may go into the surrounding villages and farms and lodge and get food, for we are here in a deserted place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; John 6:5–9; 2 Kings 4:42–43"> But he said to them,<span className="woj">“You give them something to eat.”</span>They said, “We have no more than five loaves and two fish, unless we should go and buy food for all these people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 8:6; 1 Corinthians 14:40; Mark 6:39–40"> For they were about five thousand men. He said to his disciples,<span className="woj">“Make them sit down in groups of about fifty each.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> They did so, and made them all sit down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; 1 Timothy 4:4–5; Matthew 15:36"> He took the five loaves and the two fish, and looking up to the sky, he blessed them, broke them, and gave them to the disciples to set before the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 4:44; Matthew 15:37–38; John 6:11–13"> They ate and were all filled. They gathered up twelve baskets of broken pieces that were left over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 16:13–16; Luke 11:1; Matthew 26:36"> As he was praying alone, the disciples were near him, and he asked them,<span className="woj">“Who do the multitudes say that I am?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Luke 9:7–8; Matthew 14:2"> They answered, “‘John the Baptizer,’ but others say, ‘Elijah,’ and others, that one of the old prophets has risen again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 16:15–17; John 4:29; John 7:41"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“But who do you say that I am?”</span>Peter answered, “The Christ of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 16:20; Matthew 17:9; Mark 8:30–31"> But he warned them and commanded them to tell this to no one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Luke 24:7; Luke 18:31–34"> saying,<span className="woj">“The Son of Man must suffer many things, and be rejected by the elders, chief priests, and scribes, and be killed, and the third day be raised up.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 10:38–39; Luke 14:26–27; Romans 8:13"> He said to all,<span className="woj">“If anyone desires to come after me, let him deny himself, take up his cross,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="TR, NU add “daily”">c</a></sup><span className="woj">and follow me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 17:33; John 12:25; Acts 20:23–24"> <span className="woj">For whoever desires to save his life will lose it, but whoever will lose his life for my sake will save it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 8:36; Matthew 16:26; Psalms 49:6–8"> <span className="woj">For what does it profit a man if he gains the whole world, and loses or forfeits his own self?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:32–33; 2 Timothy 2:12; Luke 12:8–9"> <span className="woj">For whoever will be ashamed of me and of my words, of him will the Son of Man be ashamed when he comes in his glory, and the glory of the Father, and of the holy angels.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:28; Mark 9:1; John 21:22–23"> <span className="woj">But I tell you the truth: There are some of those who stand here who will in no way taste of death until they see God’s Kingdom.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 17:1–13; Matthew 26:37–39; Mark 14:33–36"> About eight days after these sayings, he took with him Peter, John, and James, and went up onto the mountain to pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 17:2; Mark 9:2–3; Exodus 34:29–35"> As he was praying, the appearance of his face was altered, and his clothing became white and dazzling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 3:3–6; 2 Corinthians 3:7–11; Luke 1:17"> Behold, two men were talking with him, who were Moses and Elijah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Philippians 3:21; 1 Peter 1:11–12; 1 Peter 5:10"> who appeared in glory and spoke of his departure,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="literally, “exodus”">d</a></sup>which he was about to accomplish at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 8:18; John 1:14; Daniel 10:9"> Now Peter and those who were with him were heavy with sleep, but when they were fully awake, they saw his glory, and the two men who stood with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 9:5–6; Mark 10:38; Psalms 4:6–7"> As they were parting from him, Peter said to Jesus, “Master, it is good for us to be here. Let’s make three tents: one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah,” not knowing what he said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 40:34–38; Matthew 17:5–7; Judges 13:22"> While he said these things, a cloud came and overshadowed them, and they were afraid as they entered into the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 3:22; Acts 3:22–23; Matthew 3:17"> A voice came out of the cloud, saying, “This is my beloved Son. Listen to him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 17:9; Mark 9:9–10; Mark 9:6"> When the voice came, Jesus was found alone. They were silent, and told no one in those days any of the things which they had seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 17:14–20; Mark 9:14–29"> On the next day, when they had come down from the mountain, a great multitude met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; John 4:47; Luke 7:12"> Behold, a man from the crowd called out, saying, “Teacher, I beg you to look at my son, for he is my only born<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The phrase “only born” is from the Greek word “μονογενη”, which is sometimes translated “only begotten” or “one and only”.">e</a></sup>child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 9:20; Luke 8:29; Luke 4:35"> Behold, a spirit takes him, he suddenly cries out, and it convulses him so that he foams; and it hardly departs from him, bruising him severely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Acts 19:13–16; Luke 10:17–19"> I begged your disciples to cast it out, and they couldn’t.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:5; Romans 2:4; John 20:27"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“Faithless and perverse generation, how long shall I be with you and bear with you? Bring your son here.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Mark 9:26–27; Luke 7:15; Revelation 12:12"> While he was still coming, the demon threw him down and convulsed him violently. But Jesus rebuked the unclean spirit, healed the boy, and gave him back to his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:43–45; Luke 5:26; Matthew 17:22"> They were all astonished at the majesty of God. But while all were marveling at all the things which Jesus did, he said to his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 9:22; Acts 2:23; Luke 2:19"> <span className="woj">“Let these words sink into your ears, for the Son of Man will be delivered up into the hands of men.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 18:34; Luke 2:50; John 12:16"> But they didn’t understand this saying. It was concealed from them, that they should not perceive it, and they were afraid to ask him about this saying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 18:1–5; Mark 9:33–37; Matthew 20:20–22"> An argument arose among them about which of them was the greatest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 139:23; John 16:30; Luke 5:22"> Jesus, perceiving the reasoning of their hearts, took a little child, and set him by his side,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Peter 5:6; Luke 14:11; Mark 9:37"> and said to them,<span className="woj">“Whoever receives this little child in my name receives me. Whoever receives me receives him who sent me. For whoever is least among you all, this one will be great.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 9:38–40; Numbers 11:27–29; Mark 10:13–14"> John answered, “Master, we saw someone casting out demons in your name, and we forbade him, because he doesn’t follow with us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 12:30; Luke 11:23; 1 Corinthians 12:3"> Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“Don’t forbid him, for he who is not against us is for us.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Isaiah 50:5–9; Mark 16:19; Luke 13:22"> It came to pass, when the days were near that he should be taken up, he intently set his face to go to Jerusalem</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 10:5; Luke 17:16; Luke 10:1"> and sent messengers before his face. They went and entered into a village of the Samaritans, so as to prepare for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 4:9; John 4:40–42; Luke 9:48"> They didn’t receive him, because he was traveling with his face set toward Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="2 Kings 1:10–14; James 3:14–18; James 1:19–20"> When his disciples, James and John, saw this, they said, “Lord, do you want us to command fire to come down from the sky and destroy them, just as Elijah did?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; 1 Peter 3:9; Jeremiah 17:9"> But he turned and rebuked them,<span className="woj">“You don’t know of what kind of spirit you are.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Romans 12:21; John 3:17"> <span className="woj">For the Son of Man didn’t come to destroy men’s lives, but to save them.”</span>They went to another village.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Matthew 8:19–22; John 13:37; Luke 9:57–60"> As they went on the way, a certain man said to him, “I want to follow you wherever you go, Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Matthew 8:20; Luke 14:26–33; James 2:5"> Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“The foxes have holes and the birds of the sky have nests, but the Son of Man has no place to lay his head.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; Matthew 6:33; 1 Kings 19:20"> He said to another,<span className="woj">“Follow me!”</span>But he said, “Lord, allow me first to go and bury my father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:3–4; 1 Timothy 5:6; John 21:15–17"> But Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“Leave the dead to bury their own dead, but you go and announce God’s Kingdom.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Luke 14:26; Luke 14:18–20; 1 Kings 19:20"> Another also said, “I want to follow you, Lord, but first allow me to say good-bye to those who are at my house.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Philippians 3:13; Hebrews 10:38; 2 Peter 2:20–22"> But Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“No one, having put his hand to the plow and looking back, is fit for God’s Kingdom.”</span></span>
