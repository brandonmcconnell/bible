<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 4:46–53; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Acts 9:36–39"> and Joanna, the wife of Chuzas, Herod’s steward; Susanna; and many others who served them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR reads “him” instead of “them”">a</a></sup>from their possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 4:1–9; Matthew 13:2–9"> When a great multitude came together and people from every city were coming to him, he spoke by a parable:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; James 1:23–24; Mark 4:15"> <span className="woj">“The farmer went out to sow his seed. As he sowed, some fell along the road, and it was trampled under foot, and the birds of the sky devoured it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7–8; Luke 8:13; Mark 4:5–6"> <span className="woj">Some seed fell on the rock, and as soon as it grew, it withered away, because it had no moisture.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Luke 8:14; Luke 21:34"> <span className="woj">Some fell amid the thorns, and the thorns grew with it and choked it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 2:7"> <span className="woj">Some fell into the good ground and grew and produced one hundred times as much fruit.”</span>As he said these things, he called out,<span className="woj">“He who has ears to hear, let him hear!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 4:10; Matthew 13:10; Matthew 13:18"> Then his disciples asked him, “What does this parable mean?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; Mark 4:11; Matthew 11:25"> He said,<span className="woj">“To you it is given to know the mysteries of God’s Kingdom, but to the rest it is given in parables, that ‘seeing they may not see, and hearing they may not understand.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Mark 4:14–20; James 1:21"> <span className="woj">“Now the parable is this: The seed is the word of God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–14; Revelation 12:9; James 1:23–24"> <span className="woj">Those along the road are those who hear; then the devil comes and takes away the word from their heart, that they may not believe and be saved.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 6:4; John 5:35; Proverbs 12:12"> <span className="woj">Those on the rock are they who, when they hear, receive the word with joy; but these have no root. They believe for a while, then fall away in time of temptation.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; 1 Timothy 6:17"> <span className="woj">What fell among the thorns, these are those who have heard, and as they go on their way they are choked with cares, riches, and pleasures of life; and they bring no fruit to maturity.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Galatians 5:22–26; Colossians 1:10"> <span className="woj">Those in the good ground, these are those who with an honest and good heart, having heard the word, hold it tightly, and produce fruit with perseverance.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 11:33; Matthew 5:15–16; Mark 4:21–22"> <span className="woj">“No one, when he has lit a lamp, covers it with a container or puts it under a bed; but puts it on a stand, that those who enter in may see the light.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 12:2–3; Matthew 10:26; Mark 4:22"> <span className="woj">For nothing is hidden that will not be revealed, nor anything secret that will not be known and come to light.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:12; Luke 19:26; Mark 4:23–25"> <span className="woj">Be careful therefore how you hear. For whoever has, to him will be given; and whoever doesn’t have, from him will be taken away even that which he thinks he has.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 12:46–50; Mark 3:31–35; Mark 3:21"> His mother and brothers came to him, and they could not come near him for the crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 1:14; Mark 6:3; Matthew 13:55–56"> Some people told him, “Your mother and your brothers stand outside, desiring to see you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 11:27–28; James 1:22; John 13:17"> But he answered them,<span className="woj">“My mother and my brothers are these who hear the word of God and do it.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 8:23–27; Mark 4:35–41; Mark 6:45"> Now on one of those days, he entered into a boat, himself and his disciples, and he said to them,<span className="woj">“Let’s go over to the other side of the lake.”</span>So they launched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 93:3–4; Psalms 124:2–4; Psalms 148:8"> But as they sailed, he fell asleep. A wind storm came down on the lake, and they were taking on dangerous amounts of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 107:25–29; Luke 4:39; Psalms 104:6–9"> They came to him and awoke him, saying, “Master, Master, we are dying!” He awoke and rebuked the wind and the raging of the water; then they ceased, and it was calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 8:26; Proverbs 30:4; Mark 4:40–41"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Where is your faith?”</span>Being afraid, they marveled, saying to one another, “Who is this then, that he commands even the winds and the water, and they obey him?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 5:1–20; Matthew 8:28–34"> Then they arrived at the country of the Gadarenes, which is opposite Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 5:2–5; 1 Samuel 19:24; Numbers 19:16"> When Jesus stepped ashore, a certain man out of the city who had demons for a long time met him. He wore no clothes, and didn’t live in a house, but in the tombs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; 2 Peter 2:4; Mark 5:6–8"> When he saw Jesus, he cried out and fell down before him, and with a loud voice said, “What do I have to do with you, Jesus, you Son of the Most High God? I beg you, don’t torment me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 5:3–5; Luke 9:39; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> For Jesus was commanding the unclean spirit to come out of the man. For the unclean spirit had often seized the man. He was kept under guard and bound with chains and fetters. Breaking the bonds apart, he was driven by the demon into the desert.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 26:53; Luke 8:2; Matthew 8:29"> Jesus asked him,<span className="woj">“What is your name?”</span><br />He said, “Legion,” for many demons had entered into him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Philippians 2:10–11; Job 2:5"> They begged him that he would not command them to go into the abyss.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3; Revelation 20:7"> Now there was there a herd of many pigs feeding on the mountain, and they begged him that he would allow them to enter into those. Then he allowed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; John 8:44; Luke 8:22–23"> The demons came out of the man and entered into the pigs, and the herd rushed down the steep bank into the lake and were drowned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 5:14; Matthew 8:33; Matthew 28:11"> When those who fed them saw what had happened, they fled and told it in the city and in the country.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 10:39; Acts 22:3; 1 John 3:8"> People went out to see what had happened. They came to Jesus and found the man from whom the demons had gone out, sitting at Jesus’ feet, clothed and in his right mind; and they were afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 4:24"> Those who saw it told them how he who had been possessed by demons was healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 16:39; Luke 8:28; Luke 10:10–11"> All the people of the surrounding country of the Gadarenes asked him to depart from them, for they were very much afraid. Then he entered into the boat and returned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 9:13–16; Mark 5:18–20; Psalms 145:3–12"> But the man from whom the demons had gone out begged him that he might go with him, but Jesus sent him away, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 17:15–18; 1 Timothy 5:8; Deuteronomy 10:21"> <span className="woj">“Return to your house, and declare what great things God has done for you.”</span>He went his way, proclaiming throughout the whole city what great things Jesus had done for him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 9:1; Mark 5:21; Mark 6:20"> When Jesus returned, the multitude welcomed him, for they were all waiting for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 5:22–43; Luke 13:14; Acts 18:8"> Behold, a man named Jairus came. He was a ruler of the synagogue. He fell down at Jesus’ feet and begged him to come into his house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 7:12; Luke 8:45; Job 1:18–19"> for he had an only born<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The phrase “only born” is from the Greek word “μονογενη”, which is sometimes translated “only begotten” or “one and only”.">b</a></sup>daughter, about twelve years of age, and she was dying. But as he went, the multitudes pressed against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 5:25–26; Matthew 9:20–22; Leviticus 15:25–33"> A woman who had a flow of blood for twelve years, who had spent all her living on physicians and could not be healed by any,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Mark 5:27–28; Deuteronomy 22:12; Acts 19:12"> came behind him and touched the fringe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, tassel">c</a></sup>of his cloak. Immediately the flow of her blood stopped.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 5:30–32; Luke 9:13; Luke 5:5"> Jesus said,<span className="woj">“Who touched me?”</span><br />When all denied it, Peter and those with him said, “Master, the multitudes press and jostle you, and you say,<span className="woj">‘Who touched me?’</span>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 5:17; 1 Peter 2:9"> But Jesus said,<span className="woj">“Someone did touch me, for I perceived that power has gone out of me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 5:33; Luke 17:15–16; 1 Corinthians 2:3"> When the woman saw that she was not hidden, she came trembling; and falling down before him declared to him in the presence of all the people the reason why she had touched him, and how she was healed immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Matthew 9:22; Hebrews 4:2"> He said to her,<span className="woj">“Daughter, cheer up. Your faith has made you well. Go in peace.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 5:35–43; Isaiah 7:12; Luke 7:6"> While he still spoke, one from the ruler of the synagogue’s house came, saying to him, “Your daughter is dead. Don’t trouble the Teacher.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 11:40; John 11:25; Mark 5:36"> But Jesus hearing it, answered him,<span className="woj">“Don’t be afraid. Only believe, and she will be healed.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Mark 14:33; Matthew 6:5–6; 1 Kings 17:19–23"> When he came to the house, he didn’t allow anyone to enter in, except Peter, John, James, the father of the child, and her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 23:27; John 11:4; Zechariah 12:10"> All were weeping and mourning her, but he said,<span className="woj">“Don’t weep. She isn’t dead, but sleeping.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Luke 16:14; Isaiah 53:3; John 11:39"> They were ridiculing him, knowing that she was dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; John 11:43; Mark 1:31"> But he put them all outside, and taking her by the hand, he called, saying,<span className="woj">“Child, arise!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 11:44; 1 Kings 17:21–23; Mark 5:43"> Her spirit returned, and she rose up immediately. He commanded that something be given to her to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Luke 5:14"> Her parents were amazed, but he commanded them to tell no one what had been done.</span>
</div>
