</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–39; Luke 18:18; Matthew 19:16–19"> Behold, a certain lawyer stood up and tested him, saying, “Teacher, what shall I do to inherit eternal life?”</span>

</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 3:16–18; Micah 6:8; 1 Peter 2:21"> He said, “He who showed mercy on him.”<br />Then Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“Go and do likewise.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:19–20; John 11:1–5; John 12:1–3"> As they went on their way, he entered into a certain village, and a certain woman named Martha received him into her house.</span>

<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 6:27; Luke 12:29; Matthew 14:15"> But Martha was distracted with much serving, and she came up to him, and said, “Lord, don’t you care that my sister left me to serve alone? Ask her therefore to help me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; Luke 21:34; Matthew 6:25–34"> Jesus answered her,<span className="woj">“Martha, Martha, you are anxious and troubled about many things,</span></span>
