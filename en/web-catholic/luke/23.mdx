<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Neither has Herod, for I sent you to him, and see, nothing worthy of death has been done by him.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 8:52; Luke 8:2; Mark 15:40"> A great multitude of the people followed him, including women who also mourned and lamented him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:4; Song of Solomon 5:8; Song of Solomon 1:5"> But Jesus, turning to them, said,<span className="woj">“Daughters of Jerusalem, don’t weep for me, but weep for yourselves and for your children.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:19; Luke 21:23–24; Mark 13:17–19"> <span className="woj">For behold, the days are coming in which they will say, ‘Blessed are the barren, the wombs that never bore, and the breasts that never nursed.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 6:16; Hosea 10:8; Isaiah 2:19"> <span className="woj">Then they will begin to tell the mountains, ‘Fall on us!’ and tell the hills, ‘Cover us.’</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Acts 7:60; 1 Peter 3:9"> Jesus said,<span className="woj">“Father, forgive them, for they don’t know what they are doing.”</span><br />Dividing his garments among them, they cast lots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 22:17; Isaiah 42:1; 1 Peter 2:4"> The people stood watching. The rulers with them also scoffed at him, saying, “He saved others. Let him save himself, if this is the Christ of God, his chosen one!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Matthew 27:48; Matthew 27:34"> The soldiers also mocked him, coming to him and offering him vinegar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and saying, “If you are the King of the Jews, save yourself!”</span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:35"> One of the criminals who was hanged insulted him, saying, “If you are the Christ, save yourself and us!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–41; Ezra 9:13; 1 Peter 1:19"> And we indeed justly, for we receive the due reward for our deeds, but this man has done nothing wrong.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Romans 10:9–14; Acts 16:31; 1 John 5:11–13"> He said to Jesus, “Lord, remember me when you come into your Kingdom.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Psalms 50:15; 1 Timothy 1:15–16"> Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“Assuredly I tell you, today you will be with me in Paradise.”</span></span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:39; Luke 23:41"> When the centurion saw what was done, he glorified God, saying, “Certainly this was a righteous man.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 8:2; Psalms 38:11; Luke 23:55"> All his acquaintances and the women who followed with him from Galilee stood at a distance, watching these things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Acts 11:24; Acts 10:22"> Behold, there was a man named Joseph, who was a member of the council, a good and righteous man</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Luke 2:38; Isaiah 8:12"> (he had not consented to their counsel and deed), from Arimathaea, a city of the Jews, who was also waiting for God’s Kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 19:38–42"> This man went to Pilate, and asked for Jesus’ body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 53:9; Mark 15:46"> He took it down and wrapped it in a linen cloth, and laid him in a tomb that was cut in stone, where no one had ever been laid.</span>

<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:61; Mark 15:47"> The women who had come with him out of Galilee followed after, and saw the tomb and how his body was laid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Luke 24:1; Mark 16:1; Deuteronomy 5:14"> They returned and prepared spices and ointments. On the Sabbath they rested according to the commandment.</span>
