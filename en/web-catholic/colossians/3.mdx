<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 8:6; Matthew 6:33; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> If then you were raised together with Christ, seek the things that are above, where Christ is, seated on the right hand of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Chronicles 22:19; Colossians 3:1"> Set your mind on the things that are above, not on the things that are on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; Romans 6:2; Colossians 2:20"> For you died, and your life is hidden with Christ in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 11:25; 1 John 3:2; Galatians 2:20"> When Christ, our life, is revealed, then you will also be revealed with him in glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 8:13; Galatians 5:19–21; Galatians 5:24"> Put to death therefore your members which are on the earth: sexual immorality, uncleanness, depraved passion, evil desire, and covetousness, which is idolatry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 5:6; Romans 1:18; 1 Peter 1:14"> For these things’ sake the wrath of God comes on the children of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:2; 1 Peter 4:3–4; Romans 6:19–20"> You also once walked in those, when you lived in them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Ephesians 4:22; James 1:20–21"> but now you must put them all away: anger, wrath, malice, slander, and shameful speaking out of your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:22; Ephesians 4:25; Leviticus 19:11"> Don’t lie to one another, seeing that you have put off the old man with his doings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 4:23–24; Romans 12:2; Ephesians 2:10"> and have put on the new man, who is being renewed in knowledge after the image of his Creator,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:28–29; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Romans 10:12"> where there can’t be Greek and Jew, circumcision and uncircumcision, barbarian, Scythian, bondservant, or free person; but Christ is all, and in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Ephesians 4:2; 1 John 4:19"> Put on therefore, as God’s chosen ones, holy and beloved, a heart of compassion, kindness, lowliness, humility, and perseverance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Mark 11:25; James 2:13"> bearing with one another, and forgiving each other, if any man has a complaint against any; even as Christ forgave you, so you also do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; Ephesians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 13:1–13"> Above all these things, walk in love, which is the bond of perfection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 4:6–7; John 14:27; Isaiah 26:3"> And let the peace of God rule in your hearts, to which also you were called in one body, and be thankful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Ephesians 5:19; John 15:7"> Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly; in all wisdom teaching and admonishing one another with psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs, singing with grace in your heart to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:31; Colossians 3:23; Proverbs 3:6"> Whatever you do, in word or in deed, do all in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God the Father through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:22–9; Titus 2:4–5; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> Wives, be in subjection to your husbands, as is fitting in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Genesis 2:23–24; Colossians 3:21"> Husbands, love your wives, and don’t be bitter against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Ephesians 6:1–3; Proverbs 6:20"> Children, obey your parents in all things, for this pleases the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1–4; Ephesians 6:4; Psalms 103:13"> Fathers, don’t provoke your children, so that they won’t be discouraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 6:5–7; 1 Timothy 6:1–2; Galatians 1:10"> Servants, obey in all things those who are your masters according to the flesh, not just when they are looking, as men pleasers, but in singleness of heart, fearing God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Ephesians 6:6–7; Romans 14:8"> And whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord and not for men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 6:8; Galatians 1:10; Acts 20:32"> knowing that from the Lord you will receive the reward of the inheritance; for you serve the Lord Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 10:34; Romans 2:11; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> But he who does wrong will receive again for the wrong that he has done, and there is no partiality.</span>
