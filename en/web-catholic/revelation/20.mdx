<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; 2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6"> I saw an angel coming down out of heaven, having the key of the abyss and a great chain in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Revelation 12:9; Isaiah 27:1"> He seized the dragon, the old serpent, who is the devil and Satan, who deceives the whole inhabited earth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR and NU omit “who deceives the whole inhabited earth”.">a</a></sup>and bound him for a thousand years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 20:1; Revelation 17:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> and cast him into the abyss, and shut it and sealed it over him, that he should deceive the nations no more until the thousand years were finished. After this, he must be freed for a short time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Daniel 7:18; Matthew 19:28"> I saw thrones, and they sat on them, and judgment was given to them. I saw the souls of those who had been beheaded for the testimony of Jesus and for the word of God, and such as didn’t worship the beast nor his image, and didn’t receive the mark on their forehead and on their hand. They lived and reigned with Christ for a thousand years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:11; Revelation 11:15; Luke 14:14"> The rest of the dead didn’t live until the thousand years were finished. This is the first resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 2:11; Revelation 1:6; 2 Timothy 2:12"> Blessed and holy is he who has part in the first resurrection. Over these, the second death has no power, but they will be priests of God and of Christ, and will reign with him one thousand years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 20:2"> And after the thousand years, Satan will be released from his prison</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 16:14; Ezekiel 38:1; Revelation 20:10"> and he will come out to deceive the nations which are in the four corners of the earth, Gog and Magog, to gather them together to the war, whose number is as the sand of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 39:6; Ezekiel 38:9"> They went up over the width of the earth and surrounded the camp of the saints and the beloved city. Fire came down out of heaven from God and devoured them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Revelation 19:20; Matthew 25:46"> The devil who deceived them was thrown into the lake of fire and sulfur, where the beast and the false prophet are also. They will be tormented day and night forever and ever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 21:1; 2 Peter 3:7; Matthew 25:31"> I saw a great white throne and him who sat on it, from whose face the earth and the sky fled away. There was found no place for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Matthew 16:27; Daniel 7:10"> I saw the dead, the great and the small, standing before the throne, and they opened books. Another book was opened, which is the book of life. The dead were judged out of the things which were written in the books, according to their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 5:28–29; Revelation 6:8; Hosea 13:14"> The sea gave up the dead who were in it. Death and Hades<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or, Hell">b</a></sup>gave up the dead who were in them. They were judged, each one according to his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:26; Revelation 20:6; Revelation 21:8"> Death and Hades<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, Hell">c</a></sup>were thrown into the lake of fire. This is the second death, the lake of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 3:36; Matthew 13:50; Matthew 25:41"> If anyone was not found written in the book of life, he was cast into the lake of fire.</span>
</div>
