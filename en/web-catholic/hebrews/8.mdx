<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Colossians 3:1; Revelation 3:21"> Now in the things which we are saying, the main point is this: we have such a high priest, who sat down on the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 33:7; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Hebrews 9:23–24"> a servant of the sanctuary and of the true tabernacle which the Lord pitched, not man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Hebrews 5:1; Hebrews 9:14"> For every high priest is appointed to offer both gifts and sacrifices. Therefore it is necessary that this high priest also have something to offer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11–15; Hebrews 5:1; Numbers 17:12–13"> For if he were on earth, he would not be a priest at all, seeing there are priests who offer the gifts according to the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Colossians 2:17; Hebrews 10:1"> who serve a copy and shadow of the heavenly things, even as Moses was warned by God when he was about to make the tabernacle, for he said, “See, you shall make everything according to the pattern that was shown to you on the mountain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 7:22; Galatians 3:16–21; Hebrews 12:24"> But now he has obtained a more excellent ministry, by as much as he is also the mediator of a better covenant, which on better promises has been given as law.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11; Galatians 3:21; Hebrews 7:18"> For if that first covenant had been faultless, then no place would have been sought for a second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Hebrews 10:16–17; Hebrews 12:24"> For finding fault with them, he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Behold”, from “ἰδοὺ”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; Galatians 4:24; Mark 8:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">not according to the covenant that I made with their fathers</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the day that I took them by the hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for they didn’t continue in my covenant,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I disregarded them,” says the Lord.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26–28; Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 11:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">“For this is the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">after those days,” says the Lord:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will put my laws into their mind;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will also write them on their heart.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will be their God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will be my people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Isaiah 54:13; John 6:45"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will not teach every man his fellow citizen</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="TR reads “neighbor” instead of “fellow citizen”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and every man his brother, saying, ‘Know the Lord,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for all will know me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from their least to their greatest.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:16–17; Isaiah 43:25; Romans 11:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I will be merciful to their unrighteousness.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will remember their sins and lawless deeds no more.”</span></span>
