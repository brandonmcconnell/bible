<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:6; Hosea 13:4; Leviticus 26:13"> “I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:7; Matthew 4:10; Psalms 81:9"> “You shall have no other gods before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:8; Leviticus 26:1; Deuteronomy 4:15–19"> “You shall not make for yourselves an idol, nor any image of anything that is in the heavens above, or that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the water under the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:9; Numbers 14:18; Deuteronomy 4:24"> you shall not bow yourself down to them, nor serve them, for I, the LORD your God, am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers on the children, on the third and on the fourth generation of those who hate me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; John 14:21; 1 John 5:3"> and showing loving kindness to thousands of those who love me and keep my commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:11; Leviticus 19:12; James 5:12"> “You shall not misuse the name of the LORD<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="LORD or GOD in all caps is from the Hebrew יהוה Yahweh except when otherwise noted as being from the short form יה Yah.">b</a></sup>your God,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, You shall not take the name of the LORD your God in vain">c</a></sup>for the LORD will not hold him guiltless who misuses his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 26:2; Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 31:13–16"> “Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Exodus 34:21; Exodus 23:12"> You shall labor six days, and do all your work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:14–15; Exodus 34:21; Numbers 15:32–36"> but the seventh day is a Sabbath to the LORD your God. You shall not do any work in it, you, nor your son, nor your daughter, your male servant, nor your female servant, nor your livestock, nor your stranger who is within your gates;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:1–2; Genesis 2:2–3; Exodus 31:17"> for in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that is in them, and rested the seventh day; therefore the LORD blessed the Sabbath day, and made it holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; Colossians 3:20; Deuteronomy 5:16"> “Honor your father and your mother, that your days may be long in the land which the LORD your God gives you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Deuteronomy 5:17; Genesis 9:5–6"> “You shall not murder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; Deuteronomy 5:18; Romans 7:2–3"> “You shall not commit adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Ephesians 4:28; Leviticus 19:11"> “You shall not steal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Matthew 19:18; Psalms 15:3"> “You shall not give false testimony against your neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Luke 12:15; Hebrews 13:5"> “You shall not covet your neighbor’s house. You shall not covet your neighbor’s wife, nor his male servant, nor his female servant, nor his ox, nor his donkey, nor anything that is your neighbor’s.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 19:16–18; Jeremiah 23:23; Psalms 139:7–8"> All the people perceived the thunderings, the lightnings, the sound of the trumpet, and the mountain smoking. When the people saw it, they trembled, and stayed at a distance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:16; Acts 7:38; Galatians 3:19"> They said to Moses, “Speak with us yourself, and we will listen; but don’t let God speak with us, lest we die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:3; Proverbs 3:7; 1 Samuel 12:20"> Moses said to the people, “Don’t be afraid, for God has come to test you, and that his fear may be before you, that you won’t sin.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 8:12; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 18:9"> The people stayed at a distance, and Moses came near to the thick darkness where God was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:13; Deuteronomy 4:36; Hebrews 12:25–26"> The LORD said to Moses, “This is what you shall tell the children of Israel: ‘You yourselves have seen that I have talked with you from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:39; Zephaniah 1:5; 2 Kings 17:33"> You shall most certainly not make gods of silver or gods of gold for yourselves to be alongside me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; 2 Chronicles 6:6; Psalms 132:13–14"> You shall make an altar of earth for me, and shall sacrifice on it your burnt offerings and your peace offerings, your sheep and your cattle. In every place where I record my name I will come to you and I will bless you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 8:31; Deuteronomy 27:5–6"> If you make me an altar of stone, you shall not build it of cut stones; for if you lift up your tool on it, you have polluted it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:16; Ecclesiastes 5:1; Leviticus 10:3"> You shall not go up by steps to my altar, that your nakedness may not be exposed to it.’</span>
