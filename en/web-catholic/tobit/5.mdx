<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Tobias answered and said to him, “Father, I will do all things, whatever you have commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> But how could I receive the money, since I don’t know him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> He gave him the handwriting, and said to him, “Seek a man who will go with you, and I will give him wages, while I still live; and go and receive the money.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He went to seek a man, and found Raphael who was an angel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and he didn’t know it. He said to him, “Can I go with you to Rages of Media? Do you know those places well?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The angel said to him, “I will go with you. I know the way well. I have lodged with our brother Gabael.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Tobias said to him, “Wait for me, and I will tell my father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> He said to him, “Go, and don’t wait. And he went in and said to his father, “Behold, I have found someone who will go with me.” But he said, “Call him to me, that I may know of what tribe he is, and whether he be a trustworthy man to go with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> So he called him, and he came in, and they saluted one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Tobit said to him, “Brother, of what tribe and of what family are you? Tell me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> He said to him, “Do you seek a tribe and a family, or a hired man which will go with your son?” And Tobit said to him, “I want to know, brother, your kindred and your name.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he said, “I am Azarias, the son of Ananias the great, of your kindred.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he said to him, “Welcome, brother. Don’t be angry with me, because I sought to know your tribe and family. You are my brother, of an honest and good lineage; for I knew Ananias and Jathan, the sons of Shemaiah the great, when we went together to Jerusalem to worship, and offered the firstborn, and the tenths of our increase; and they didn’t go astray in the error of our kindred. My brother, you are of a great stock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But tell me, what wages shall I give you? A drachma a day, and those things that be necessary for you, as to my son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And moreover, if you both return safe and sound, I will add something to your wages.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And so they agreed. And he said to Tobias, “Prepare yourself for the journey. May God prosper you.” So his son prepared what was needful for the journey, and his father said to him, “Go with this man; but God, who dwells in heaven, will prosper your journey. May his angel go with you.” Then they both departed, and the young man’s dog went with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But Anna his mother wept, and said to Tobit, “Why have you sent away our child? Isn’t he the staff of our hand, in going in and out before us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Don’t be greedy to add money to money; but let it be as refuse compared to our child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For what the Lord has given us to live is enough for us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Tobit said to her, “Don’t worry, my sister. He will return safe and sound, and your eyes will see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For a good angel will go with him. His journey will be prospered, and he will return safe and sound.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> So she stopped weeping.</span>
