<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:31; 1 Kings 2:10; 1 Kings 14:31"> So Abijah slept with his fathers, and they buried him in David’s city; and Asa his son reigned in his place. In his days, the land was quiet ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:11; 2 Chronicles 31:20; 1 Kings 15:14"> Asa did that which was good and right in the LORD his God’s eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:5; Exodus 34:13; 2 Kings 23:14"> for he took away the foreign altars and the high places, broke down the pillars, cut down the Asherah poles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:19; Psalms 119:10; Psalms 101:2–8"> and commanded Judah to seek the LORD, the God of their fathers, and to obey his law and command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:4; 2 Chronicles 34:7"> Also he took away out of all the cities of Judah the high places and the sun images; and the kingdom was quiet before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:15; 1 Chronicles 22:9; Job 34:29"> He built fortified cities in Judah; for the land was quiet, and he had no war in those years, because the LORD had given him rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; 2 Chronicles 14:6; Jeremiah 29:12–14"> For he said to Judah, “Let’s build these cities and make walls around them, with towers, gates, and bars. The land is yet before us, because we have sought the LORD our God. We have sought him, and he has given us rest on every side.” So they built and prospered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:3; 2 Chronicles 17:14–19; 2 Chronicles 11:1"> Asa had an army of three hundred thousand out of Judah who bore bucklers and spears, and two hundred eighty thousand out of Benjamin who bore shields and drew bows. All these were mighty men of valor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:8; 2 Chronicles 12:2–3; 2 Chronicles 11:8"> Zerah the Ethiopian came out against them with an army of a million troops and three hundred chariots, and he came to Mareshah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 1:17; Joshua 19:4"> Then Asa went out to meet him, and they set the battle in array in the valley of Zephathah at Mareshah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:19; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Psalms 22:5"> Asa cried to the LORD his God, and said, “LORD, there is no one besides you to help, between the mighty and him who has no strength. Help us, LORD our God; for we rely on you, and in your name are we come against this multitude. LORD, you are our God. Don’t let man prevail against you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:15; Psalms 136:17–18; Deuteronomy 28:7"> So the LORD struck the Ethiopians before Asa and before Judah; and the Ethiopians fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:19; Genesis 26:1; Genesis 20:1"> Asa and the people who were with him pursued them to Gerar. So many of the Ethiopians fell that they could not recover themselves, for they were destroyed before the LORD and before his army. Judah’s army carried away very much booty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:10; Genesis 35:5; 2 Chronicles 20:29"> They struck all the cities around Gerar, for the fear of the LORD came on them. They plundered all the cities, for there was much plunder in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:30–47; Numbers 31:9; 1 Samuel 30:20"> They also struck the tents of those who had livestock, and carried away sheep and camels in abundance, then returned to Jerusalem.</span>
