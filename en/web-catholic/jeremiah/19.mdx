<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:11–12; Isaiah 30:14; Lamentations 4:2"> Thus said the LORD, “Go, and buy a potter’s earthen container, and take some of the elders of the people and of the elders of the priests;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 15:8; 2 Kings 23:10; Jeremiah 7:31–32"> and go out to the valley of the son of Hinnom, which is by the entry of the gate Harsith, and proclaim there the words that I will tell you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:11; Jeremiah 17:20; Isaiah 28:19"> Say, ‘Hear the LORD’s word, kings of Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem: The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel says, “Behold, I will bring evil on this place, which whoever hears, his ears will tingle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:34; Deuteronomy 28:20; 2 Kings 21:16"> Because they have forsaken me, and have defiled this place, and have burned incense in it to other gods that they didn’t know—they, their fathers, and the kings of Judah—and have filled this place with the blood of innocents,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:35; Deuteronomy 12:31; Leviticus 18:21"> and have built the high places of Baal to burn their children in the fire for burnt offerings to Baal, which I didn’t command, nor speak, which didn’t even enter into my mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 15:8; Isaiah 30:33; Jeremiah 19:2"> Therefore, behold, the days come,” says the LORD, “that this place will no more be called ‘Topheth’, nor ‘The Valley of the son of Hinnom’, but ‘The valley of Slaughter’.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 26:17; Jeremiah 16:4; Psalms 33:10–11"> “‘“I will make the counsel of Judah and Jerusalem void in this place. I will cause them to fall by the sword before their enemies, and by the hand of those who seek their life. I will give their dead bodies to be food for the birds of the sky and for the animals of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:16; Jeremiah 49:13; 1 Kings 9:8"> I will make this city an astonishment and a hissing. Everyone who passes by it will be astonished and hiss because of all its plagues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Leviticus 26:29; Lamentations 4:10"> I will cause them to eat the flesh of their sons and the flesh of their daughters. They will each eat the flesh of his friend in the siege and in the distress with which their enemies, and those who seek their life, will distress them.”’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:63–64; Jeremiah 48:12; Jeremiah 19:1"> “Then you shall break the container in the sight of the men who go with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 2:9; Isaiah 30:14; Lamentations 4:2"> and shall tell them, ‘The LORD of Armies says: “Even so I will break this people and this city as one breaks a potter’s vessel, that can’t be made whole again. They will bury in Topheth until there is no place to bury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:13; Jeremiah 11:5"> This is what I will do to this place,” says the LORD, “and to its inhabitants, even making this city as Topheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:29; 2 Kings 23:12; Zephaniah 1:5"> The houses of Jerusalem and the houses of the kings of Judah, which are defiled, will be as the place of Topheth, even all the houses on whose roofs they have burned incense to all the army of the sky and have poured out drink offerings to other gods.”’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:2; 2 Chronicles 20:5; Jeremiah 19:2–3"> Then Jeremiah came from Topheth, where the LORD had sent him to prophesy, and he stood in the court of the LORD’s house, and said to all the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:26; Jeremiah 17:23; Nehemiah 9:29"> “The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel says, ‘Behold, I will bring on this city and on all its towns all the evil that I have pronounced against it, because they have made their neck stiff, that they may not hear my words.’”</span>
</div>
