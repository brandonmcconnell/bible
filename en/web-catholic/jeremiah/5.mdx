<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 18:23–32; 2 Chronicles 16:9; Ezekiel 22:30"> “Run back and forth through the streets of Jerusalem, and see now, and know, and seek in its wide places, if you can find a man, if there is anyone who does justly, who seeks truth, then I will pardon her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Jeremiah 4:2; Hosea 4:1–2"> Though they say, ‘As the LORD lives,’ surely they swear falsely.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:30; Jeremiah 7:28; Isaiah 9:13"> O LORD, don’t your eyes look on truth? You have stricken them, but they were not grieved. You have consumed them, but they have refused to receive correction. They have made their faces harder than a rock. They have refused to return.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Hosea 4:6; Isaiah 28:9–13"> Then I said, “Surely these are poor. They are foolish; for they don’t know the LORD’s way, nor the law of their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:20; James 2:5–7; Matthew 19:23–26"> I will go to the great men and will speak to them, for they know the way of the LORD, and the law of their God.” But these with one accord have broken the yoke, and burst the bonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Habakkuk 1:8; Zephaniah 3:3"> Therefore a lion out of the forest will kill them. A wolf of the evenings will destroy them. A leopard will watch against their cities. Everyone who goes out there will be torn in pieces, because their transgressions are many and their backsliding has increased.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Joshua 23:7; Jeremiah 2:11"> “How can I pardon you? Your children have forsaken me, and sworn by what are no gods. When I had fed them to the full, they committed adultery, and assembled themselves in troops at the prostitutes’ houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:27; Deuteronomy 5:18; 2 Samuel 11:2–4"> They were as fed horses roaming at large. Everyone neighed after his neighbor’s wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:9; Jeremiah 5:29; Isaiah 1:24"> Shouldn’t I punish them for these things?” says the LORD. “Shouldn’t my soul be avenged on such a nation as this?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:27; Matthew 22:7; Amos 9:8"> “Go up on her walls, and destroy, but don’t make a full end. Take away her branches, for they are not the LORD’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:20; Isaiah 48:8; Hosea 6:7"> For the house of Israel and the house of Judah have dealt very treacherously against me,” says the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Ezekiel 12:22–28; Jeremiah 23:14–17"> They have denied the LORD, and said, “It is not he. Evil won’t come on us. We won’t see sword or famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:15; Job 8:2; Jeremiah 14:13"> The prophets will become wind, and the word is not in them. Thus it will be done to them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:29; Jeremiah 1:9; Hosea 6:5"> Therefore the LORD, the God of Armies says, “Because you speak this word, behold, I will make my words in your mouth fire, and this people wood, and it will devour them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:49; Jeremiah 4:16; Isaiah 33:19"> Behold, I will bring a nation on you from far away, house of Israel,” says the LORD. “It is a mighty nation. It is an ancient nation, a nation whose language you don’t know and don’t understand what they say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 5:28; Psalms 5:9; Romans 3:13"> Their quiver is an open tomb. They are all mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 26:16; Deuteronomy 28:33; Hosea 8:14"> They will eat up your harvest and your bread, which your sons and your daughters should eat. They will eat up your flocks and your herds. They will eat up your vines and your fig trees. They will beat down your fortified cities in which you trust with the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:10; Romans 11:1–5; Jeremiah 4:27"> “But even in those days,” says the LORD, “I will not make a full end of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 9:8–9; Deuteronomy 28:47–48; Jeremiah 22:8–9"> It will happen when you say, ‘Why has the LORD our God done all these things to us?’ Then you shall say to them, ‘Just as you have forsaken me and served foreign gods in your land, so you will serve strangers in a land that is not yours.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> “Declare this in the house of Jacob, and publish it in Judah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 28:26; Ezekiel 12:2; Isaiah 6:9–10"> ‘Hear this now, foolish people without understanding, who have eyes, and don’t see, who have ears, and don’t hear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Psalms 104:9; Job 26:10"> Don’t you fear me?’ says the LORD; ‘Won’t you tremble at my presence, who have placed the sand for the bound of the sea by a perpetual decree, that it can’t pass it? Though its waves toss themselves, yet they can’t prevail. Though they roar, they still can’t pass over it.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:28; Deuteronomy 21:18; Psalms 95:10"> “But this people has a revolting and a rebellious heart. They have revolted and gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Joel 2:23; Genesis 8:22"> They don’t say in their heart, ‘Let’s now fear the LORD our God, who gives rain, both the former and the latter, in its season, who preserves to us the appointed weeks of the harvest.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 59:2; Psalms 107:17; Psalms 107:34"> “Your iniquities have turned away these things, and your sins have withheld good from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 1:11; Psalms 64:5; Ezekiel 22:2–12"> For wicked men are found among my people. They watch, as fowlers lie in wait. They set a trap. They catch men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 1:12; Jeremiah 9:6; Hosea 12:7–8"> As a cage is full of birds, so are their houses full of deceit. Therefore they have become great, and grew rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 1:23; Deuteronomy 32:15; Jeremiah 7:6"> They have grown fat. They shine; yes, they excel in deeds of wickedness. They don’t plead the cause, the cause of the fatherless, that they may prosper; and they don’t defend the rights of the needy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:9; Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 9:9"> “Shouldn’t I punish for these things?” says the LORD. “Shouldn’t my soul be avenged on such a nation as this?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hosea 6:10; Jeremiah 23:14; Isaiah 1:2"> “An astonishing and horrible thing has happened in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6; Jeremiah 14:14; Micah 2:11"> The prophets prophesy falsely, and the priests rule by their own authority; and my people love to have it so. What will you do in the end of it?</span>
</div>
</div>
