<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Zechariah 1:16; Ezekiel 47:4"> I lift vp mine eyes againe and looked, and behold, a man with a measuring line in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:39; Ezekiel 45:6; Revelation 21:15–17"> Then saide I, Whither goest thou? And he saide vnto me, To measure Ierusalem, that I may see what is the breadth thereof, and what is the length thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 1:19; Zechariah 1:8–11; Zechariah 4:5"> And beholde, the Angel that talked with me, went foorth: and another Angel went out to meete him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 1:17; Jeremiah 33:22; Ezekiel 38:11"> And saide vnto him, Runne, speake to this yong man, and say, Ierusalem shalbe inhabited without walles, for the multitude of men and cattell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 4:5; Zechariah 9:8; Psalms 3:3"> For I, saith the Lord, will be vnto her a wall of fire round about, and wil be the glory in the middes of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Ezekiel 17:21; Isaiah 52:11–12"> Ho, ho, come forth, and flee from the land of the North, saith the Lord: for I haue scattered you into the foure winds of the heauen, saith ye Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Genesis 19:17; Isaiah 52:11"> Saue thy selfe, O Zion, that dwellest with the daughter of Babel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 17:8; Deuteronomy 32:10; John 15:21–23"> For thus saith the Lord of hostes, After this glory hath hee sent me vnto the nations, which spoyled you: for he that toucheth you, toucheth the apple of his eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 19:16; Isaiah 14:2; Zechariah 4:9"> For beholde, I will lift vp mine hand vpon them: and they shalbe a spoyle to those that serued them, and ye shall knowe, that the Lord of hostes hath sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; John 1:14; Zephaniah 3:14–15"> Reioyce, and be glad, O daughter Zion: for loe, I come, and will dwell in the middes of thee, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; 1 Peter 2:9–10; Acts 28:28"> And many nations shall be ioyned to the Lord in that day, and shalbe my people: and I will dwell in the middes of thee, and thou shalt knowe that the Lord of hostes hath sent me vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 1:17; Deuteronomy 32:9; Jeremiah 10:16"> And the Lord shall inherite Iudah his portion in the holy lande, and shall chuse Ierusalem againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:20; Psalms 68:5; Psalms 78:65"> Let all flesh be still before the Lord: for he is raised vp out of his holy place.</span>
