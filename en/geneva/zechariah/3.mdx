<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; Revelation 19:8; Ezra 9:15"> Nowe Iehoshua was clothed with filthie garments, and stoode before the Angel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:22; Isaiah 61:10; Ezekiel 36:25"> And he answered and spake vnto those that stoode before him, saying, Take away the filthie garments from him. And vnto him hee saide, Behold, I haue caused thine iniquitie to depart from thee, and I wil clothe thee with change of raiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 6:11; Exodus 29:6; Job 29:14"> And I saide, Let them set a faire diademe vpon his head. So they set a faire diademe vpon his head, and clothed him with garments, and the Angel of the Lord stoode by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–17; Genesis 22:15–16; Zechariah 3:1"> And the Angel of the Lord testified vnto Iehoshua, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Genesis 26:5; 2 Timothy 4:1–2"> Thus saith the Lord of hostes, If thou wilt walke in my wayes, and keepe my watch, thou shalt also iudge mine House, and shalt also keepe my courtes, and I will giue thee place among these that stand by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5; Isaiah 11:1; Zechariah 6:12"> Heare now, O Iehoshua the hie Priest, thou and thy fellowes that sit before thee: for they are monstruous persons: but behold, I wil bring forth the Branche my seruant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 4:10; Jeremiah 50:20; Isaiah 28:16"> For loe, the stone that I haue layd before Iehoshua: vpon one stone shalbe seuen eyes: beholde, I will cut out the grauing thereof, saith the Lord of hostes, and I will take away the iniquitie of this land in one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 4:25; Micah 4:4; Zechariah 2:11"> In that day, saith the Lord of hostes, shall ye call euery man his neighbour vnder the vine, and vnder the figge tree.</span>
