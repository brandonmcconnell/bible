<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 5:16–18; Matthew 7:7–8; Isaiah 44:3"> Aske you of the Lord raine in the time of the latter raine: so shall the Lord make white cloudes, and giue you showers of raine, and to euery one grasse in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Jeremiah 27:9; Ezekiel 34:5"> Surely the idols haue spoken vanitie, and the southsayers haue seene a lye, and the dreamers haue tolde a vaine thing: they comfort in vaine: therefore they went away as sheepe: they were troubled, because there was no shepheard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Ezekiel 34:7–10; Jeremiah 23:1–2"> My wrath was kindled against the shepherdes, and I did visite the goates: but the Lord of hostes will visite his flocke the house of Iudah, and will make them as his beautifull horse in the battell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 9:10; Zechariah 9:8; Numbers 24:17"> Out of him shall the corner come foorth: out of him the nayle, out of him ye bowe of battell, and out of him euery appointer of tribute also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Haggai 2:22; Zechariah 9:13; Psalms 20:7"> And they shalbe as the mightie men, which treade downe their enemies in the mire of the streetes in the battell, and they shall fight, because the Lord is with them, and the riders on horses shall be confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Zechariah 10:12; Jeremiah 3:18"> And I will strengthen the house of Iudah, and I will preserue the house of Ioseph, and I wil bring them againe, for I pitie them: and they shall be as though I had not cast them off: for I am the Lord their God, and will heare them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 9:15; Zechariah 9:17; 1 Samuel 2:1"> And they of Ephraim shall be as a gyant, and their heart shall reioyce as thorowe wine: yea, their children shall see it, and be glad: and their heart shall reioyce in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 5:26; Isaiah 7:18; Jeremiah 33:22"> I will hisse for them, and gather them: for I haue redeemed them: and they shall encrease, as they haue encreased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Hosea 2:23; Esther 8:17"> And I will sowe them among the people, and they shall remember me in farre countreys: and they shall liue with their children and turne againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 11:11; Micah 7:14; Zechariah 8:7"> I will bring them againe also out of the land of Egypt, and gather them out of Asshur: and I will bring them into the land of Gilead, and Lebanon, and place shall not be found for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:13; Micah 5:5–6; Isaiah 43:2"> And he shall goe into the sea with affliction, and shall smite the waues in the sea, and all the depthes of the riuer shall drye vp: and the pride of Asshur shall be cast downe, and the scepter of Egypt shall depart away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Isaiah 2:5; Zechariah 10:6"> And I will strengthen them in the Lord, and they shall walke in his Name, sayth the Lord.</span>
