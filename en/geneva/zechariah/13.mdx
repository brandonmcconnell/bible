<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; John 1:29; Psalms 51:2"> In that day there shall be a fountaine opened to the house of Dauid, and to the inhabitants of Ierusalem, for sinne and for vncleannesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 22:22; Jeremiah 23:14–15; Hosea 2:17"> And in that day, sayth the Lord of hostes, I will cut off the names of the idoles out of the land: and they shall no more be remembred: and I will cause the prophets, and the vncleane spirit to depart out of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:6–11; Deuteronomy 18:20; Ezekiel 14:9"> And when any shall yet prophesie, his father and his mother that begate him, shall say vnto him, Thou shalt not liue: for thou speakest lies in the name of the Lord: and his father and his mother that begat him, shall thrust him through, when he prophesieth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 3:6–7; Matthew 3:4; Revelation 11:3"> And in that day shall the prophetes be ashamed euery one of his vision, when he hath prophesied: neither shall they weare a rough garment to deceiue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 7:14; Acts 19:17–20"> But he shall say, I am no Prophet: I am an husbandman: for man taught me to be an heardman from my youth vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:35; Psalms 22:16; Proverbs 27:5–6"> And one shall say vnto him, What are these woundes in thine hands? Then he shall answere, Thus was I wounded in the house of my friendes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:27; Matthew 26:31; John 16:32"> Arise, O sword, vpon my shepheard, and vpon the man, that is my fellow, sayth the Lord of hostes: smite the shepheard, and the sheepe shall be scattered: and I will turne mine hand vpon the litle ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:12; Revelation 16:19; Ezekiel 5:2–4"> And in all the land, sayeth the Lord, two partes therein shall be cut off, and die: but the third shall be left therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Jeremiah 29:11–12; Acts 2:21"> And I will bring that third part thorowe the fire, and will fine them as the siluer is fined, and will trye them as golde is tryed: they shall call on my Name, and I will heare them: I will say, It is my people, and they shall say, The Lord is my God.</span>
