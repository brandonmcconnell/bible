<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Lamentations 3:27; Ecclesiastes 11:8; Psalms 71:17–18"> Remember nowe thy Creator in the daies of thy youth, whiles the euill daies come not, nor the yeeres approche, wherein thou shalt say, I haue no pleasure in them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:30; 1 Samuel 4:18; 1 Samuel 4:15"> Whiles the sunne is not darke, nor ye light, nor the moone, nor the starres, nor the cloudes returne after the raine:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 90:9–10; Psalms 102:23; 2 Samuel 21:15–17"> When the keepers of ye house shall tremble, and the strong men shall bow them selues, and the grinders shall cease, because they are few, and they waxe darke that looke out by ye windowes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:10; 2 Samuel 19:35; Revelation 18:22"> And the doores shall be shut without by the base sound of the grinding, and he shall rise vp at the voice of the birde: and all the daughters of singing shall be abased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 17:13; Jeremiah 9:17–20; Job 30:23"> Also they shalbe afraide of the hie thing, and feare shalbe in the way, and the almond tree shall flourish, and the grassehopper shall be a burden, and concupiscence shall be driuen away: for man goeth to the house of his age, and the mourners goe about in the streete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 4:2–3"> Whiles the siluer coarde is not lengthened, nor the golden ewer broken, nor the pitcher broken at the well, nor the wheele broken at the cisterne:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Ecclesiastes 3:20–21; Psalms 146:4"> And dust returne to the earth as it was, and the spirit returne to God that gaue it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:2; Ecclesiastes 8:8; Ecclesiastes 1:14"> Vanitie of vanities, saieth the Preacher, all is vanitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 4:32; Proverbs 1:1; 1 Kings 10:8"> And the more wise the Preacher was, the more he taught the people knowledge, and caused them to heare, and searched foorth, and prepared many parables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 1:5; 1 Timothy 1:15; Proverbs 16:21–24"> The Preacher sought to finde out pleasant wordes, and an vpright writing, euen the wordes of trueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 2:37; Hebrews 4:12; Ezekiel 34:23"> The wordes of the wise are like goads, and like nailes fastened by the masters of the assemblies, which are giuen by one pastour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:18; John 21:25; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> And of other things beside these, my sone, take thou heede: for there is none ende in making many bookes, and much reading is a wearines of the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:12; Micah 6:8; Deuteronomy 6:2"> Let vs heare the end of all: feare God and keepe his commandements: for this is the whole duetie of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:16; Matthew 12:36"> For God will bring euery worke vnto iudgement, with euery secret thing, whether it be good or euill.</span>
