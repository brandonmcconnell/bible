<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:16; Ecclesiastes 5:8; Isaiah 5:7"> So I turned and considered all the oppressions that are wrought vnder the sunne, and beholde the teares of the oppressed, and none comforteth them: and lo, the strength is of the hand of them that oppresse them, and none comforteth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 3:11–26; Ecclesiastes 2:17; Ecclesiastes 9:4–6"> Wherefore I praysed the dead which now are dead, aboue the liuing, which are yet aliue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 23:29; Ecclesiastes 6:3–5; Matthew 24:19"> And I count him better then them both, which hath not yet bin: for he hath not seene the euill workes which are wrought vnder the sunne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 3:12; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> Also I beheld all trauaile, and all perfection of workes that this is ye enuie of a man against his neighbour: this also is vanitie and vexation of spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 6:10–11"> The foole foldeth his hands, and eateth vp his owne flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Proverbs 15:16–17; Psalms 37:16"> Better is an handfull with quietnesse, then two handfuls with labour and vexation of spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Psalms 78:33; Zechariah 1:6"> Againe I returned, and sawe vanitie vnder the sunne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 2:16; Proverbs 27:20; Luke 12:20"> There is one alone, and there is not a second, which hath neither sonne nor brother, yet is there none end of all his trauaile, neither can his eye be satisfied with riches: neither doeth he thinke, For whome doe I trauaile and defraude my soule of pleasure? this also is vanitie, and this is an euill trauaile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 27:17; Genesis 2:18; 1 Corinthians 12:18–21"> Two are better then one: for they haue better wages for their labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:18; Isaiah 35:3–4"> For if they fal, the one wil lift vp his felow: but wo vnto him that is alone: for he falleth, and there is not a second to lift him vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 1:1–4"> Also if two sleepe together, then shall they haue heate: but to one how should there be heate?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 3:16–17; Ephesians 4:3; 2 Samuel 23:9"> And if one ouercome him, two shall stand against him: and a threefolde coard is not easily broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:16; 2 Chronicles 16:9–10; Proverbs 19:1"> Better is a poore and wise childe, then an olde and foolish King, which will no more be admonished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Genesis 41:14; Job 5:11"> For out of the prison he commeth forth to reigne: when as he that is borne in his kingdome, is made poore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:6"> I behelde all the liuing, which walke vnder the sunne, with the second childe, which shall stand vp in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; 1 Kings 1:40; Ecclesiastes 2:17"> There is none ende of all the people, nor of all that were before them, and they that come after, shall not reioyce in him: surely this is also vanitie and vexation of spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Take heede to thy foote when thou entrest into the House of God, and be more neere to heare then to giue the sacrifice of fooles: for they knowe not that they doe euil.</span>
