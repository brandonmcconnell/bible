<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:18–20; Ecclesiastes 10:14; Ecclesiastes 10:10"> The heart of a wise man is at his right hand: but the heart of a foole is at his left hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 18:2; Proverbs 13:16; Proverbs 18:6"> And also when the foole goeth by the way, his heart faileth, and he telleth vnto all that he is a foole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 25:15; Ecclesiastes 8:3; 1 Samuel 25:24–44"> If the spirite of him that ruleth, rise vp against thee, leaue not thy place: for gentlenes pacifieth great sinnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13; Ecclesiastes 4:7; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> There is an euil that I haue seene vnder the sunne, as an errour that proceedeth from the face of him that ruleth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 28:12; Proverbs 29:2; Esther 3:1"> Follie is set in great excellencie, and the riche set in the lowe place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Esther 6:8; Proverbs 30:22"> I haue seene seruants on horses, and princes walking as seruants on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:27; Psalms 7:15–16; Esther 7:10"> He that diggeth a pit, shall fal into it, and he that breaketh the hedge, a serpent shall bite him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He that remooueth stones, shall hurt himselfe thereby, and hee that cutteth wood, shall be in danger thereby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:5; Colossians 4:5; Ecclesiastes 10:15"> If the yron be blunt, and one hath not whet the edge, he must then put to more strength: but the excellencie to direct a thing is wisedome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:17; Psalms 58:4–5; Psalms 64:3"> If the serpent bite, when he is not charmed: no better is a babbler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 4:22; Proverbs 22:11"> The words of ye mouth of a wise man haue grace: but the lippes of a foole deuoure himselfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:10–11; Proverbs 29:9; Acts 6:9–11"> The beginning of the wordes of his mouth is foolishnesse, and the latter ende of his mouth is wicked madnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 15:2; Ecclesiastes 6:12; Ecclesiastes 5:3"> For the foole multiplieth woordes, saying, Man knoweth not what shall be: and who can tell him what shall be after him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:4–5; Isaiah 57:1; Isaiah 35:8–10"> The labour of the foolish doeth wearie him: for he knoweth not to goe into the citie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 3:12; Isaiah 5:11–12; Isaiah 3:4–5"> Woe to thee, O lande, when thy King is a childe, and thy princes eate in the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 31:4–5; Proverbs 28:2–3; Isaiah 5:11"> Blessed art thou, O land, when thy King is the sonne of nobles, and thy princes eate in time, for strength and not for drunkennesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; Proverbs 24:30–34; Proverbs 20:4"> By slouthfulnes the roofe of the house goeth to decaie, and by the ydlenesse of the handes the house droppeth through.</span>
