<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:50; 2 Peter 1:20"> Who is as the wise man? and who knoweth the interpretation of a thing? the wisedome of a man doth make his face to shine: and the strength of his face shalbe changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 2:43; Titus 3:1; Proverbs 24:21"> I aduertise thee to take heede to ye mouth of the King, and to the worde of the othe of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:4; Acts 5:8–9; Daniel 4:35"> Haste not to goe forth of his sight: stand not in an euill thing: for he will doe whatsoeuer pleaseth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 9:12; Romans 13:1–4; Daniel 4:35"> Where the word of ye King is, there is power, and who shall say vnto him, What doest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 5:14; 1 Peter 3:13–14; Philippians 1:9–10"> He that keepeth the commandement, shall knowe none euill thing, and the heart of the wise shall knowe the time and iudgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:1; Ecclesiastes 3:17; Ecclesiastes 3:11"> For to euery purpose there is a time and iudgement, because the miserie of man is great vpon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:14; Ecclesiastes 9:12; Proverbs 24:22"> For he knoweth not that which shalbe: for who can tell him when it shalbe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 89:48; Job 14:5; Hebrews 9:27"> Man is not lorde ouer the spirit to retaine the spirite: neither hath hee power in the day of death, nor deliuerance in the battell, neither shall wickednesse deliuer the possessers thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:8; Ecclesiastes 4:7–8; Ecclesiastes 5:13"> All this haue I seene, and haue giuen mine heart to euery worke, which is wrought vnder the sunne, and I sawe a time that man ruleth ouer man to his owne hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Proverbs 10:7"> And likewise I sawe the wicked buried, and they returned, and they that came from the holy place, were yet forgotten in the citie where they had done right: this also is vanitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; Psalms 50:21–22; Romans 2:4–5"> Because sentence against an euill worke is not executed speedily, therefore the heart of the children of men is fully set in them to doe euill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:11; 2 Peter 2:9; Luke 1:50"> Though a sinner doe euill an hundreth times, and God prolongeth his dayes, yet I knowe that it shalbe well with them that feare the Lord, and doe reuerence before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:12; Isaiah 3:11; Job 14:2"> But it shall not be well to the wicked, neither shall he prolong his dayes: he shall be like a shadowe, because he feareth not before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:15; Ecclesiastes 2:14; Malachi 3:15"> There is a vanitie, which is done vpon the earth, that there be righteous men to whom it commeth according to the worke of the wicked: and there be wicked men to whom it commeth according to the worke of the iust: I thought also that this is vanitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:24; Ecclesiastes 5:18; Ecclesiastes 3:12–13"> And I praysed ioy: for there is no goodnesse to man vnder the sunne, saue to eate and to drinke and to reioyce: for this is adioyned to his labour, the dayes of his life that God hath giuen him vnder the sunne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:13; Psalms 127:2; Ecclesiastes 2:23"> When I applied mine heart to knowe wisedome, and to behold the busines that is done on earth, that neither day nor night the eyes of man take sleepe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:11; Psalms 73:16; Romans 11:33"> Then I behelde the whole worke of God, that man cannot finde out ye worke that is wrought vnder the sunne: for the which man laboureth to seeke it, and cannot finde it: yea, and though the wise man thinke to knowe it, he cannot finde it.</span>
