<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Proverbs 22:1; Ecclesiastes 4:2"> Surely there be many things that increase vanitie: and what auaileth it man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 90:12; Deuteronomy 32:29; Hebrews 9:27"> For who knoweth what is good for man in the life and in the nomber of the dayes of the life of his vanitie, seeing he maketh them as a shadowe? For who can shewe vnto man what shall be after him vnder the sunne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:9–11; Romans 5:3–4; James 4:8–10"> A good name is better then a good oyntment, and the day of death, then the day that one is borne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 8:14–16; 1 Samuel 25:36; Luke 7:12–13"> It is better to goe to the house of mourning, then to goe to the house of feasting, because this is the ende of all men: and the liuing shall lay it to his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:31–32; Psalms 141:5; Revelation 3:19"> Anger is better then laughter: for by a sad looke the heart is made better.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 58:9; Psalms 118:12; Ecclesiastes 2:2"> The heart of the wise is in the house of mourning: but the heart of fooles is in the house of mirth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 23:8; Proverbs 17:23"> Better it is to heare ye rebuke of a wise man, then that a man should heare the song of fooles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Proverbs 14:29; Psalms 126:5–6"> For like ye noyse of the thornes vnder the pot, so is the laughter of the foole: this also is vanitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 14:17; Ephesians 4:26–27"> Surely oppression maketh a wise man mad: and the rewarde destroyeth the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 6:13; Psalms 14:2–3; Genesis 6:11–12"> The ende of a thing is better then the beginning thereof, and the pacient in spirit is better then the proude in spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:7; 1 Timothy 6:17–19; Ecclesiastes 2:13"> Be not thou of an hastie spirit to be angry: for anger resteth in the bosome of fooles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 8:35; Proverbs 2:7; Proverbs 3:18"> Say not thou, Why is it that the former dayes were better then these? for thou doest not enquire wisely of this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:15; Job 12:14; Psalms 107:43"> Wisedome is good with an inheritance, and excellent to them that see the sunne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:4; Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 12:13"> For man shall rest in the shadowe of wisedome, and in the shadowe of siluer: but the excellencie of the knowledge of wisedome giueth life to the possessers thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:12–14; Ecclesiastes 6:12; Psalms 73:3–13"> Beholde the worke of God: for who can make straight that which he hath made crooked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:3; Proverbs 25:16; Colossians 2:18"> In the day of wealth be of good comfort, and in the day of affliction consider: God also hath made this contrary to that, to the intent that man shoulde finde nothing after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 10:27; Psalms 55:23; Job 22:16"> I haue seene all things in the dayes of my vanitie: there is a iust man that perisheth in his iustice, and there is a wicked man that continueth long in his malice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:13; Malachi 4:2; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> Be not thou iust ouermuch, neither make thy selfe ouerwise: wherefore shouldest thou be desolate?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 21:22; Colossians 1:9–11; Proverbs 24:5"> Be not thou wicked ouermuch, neither be thou foolish: wherefore shouldest thou perish not in thy time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 3:23; Isaiah 53:6; 1 John 1:8–10"> It is good that thou lay hold on this: but yet withdrawe not thine hand from that: for he that feareth God, shall come forth of them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 30:10; Isaiah 29:21; 2 Samuel 16:10"> Wisedome shall strengthen the wise man more then ten mightie princes that are in ye citie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 3:9; Matthew 18:32–35; John 8:7–9"> Surely there is no man iust in the earth, that doeth good and sinneth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 1:22; Genesis 3:5; 1 Corinthians 1:20"> Giue not thine heart also to all ye wordes that men speake, lest thou doe heare thy seruant cursing thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:33; 1 Timothy 6:16; Isaiah 55:8–9"> For often times also thine heart knoweth that thou likewise hast cursed others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:13; Joshua 7:13; Ecclesiastes 2:20"> All this haue I prooued by wisedome: I thought I will be wise, but it went farre from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 5:3–5; Proverbs 2:18–19"> It is farre off, what may it be? and it is a profound deepenesse, who can finde it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:8–10; Ecclesiastes 1:1–2"> I haue compassed about, both I and mine heart to knowe and to enquire and to search wisedome, and reason, and to knowe the wickednesse of follie, and the foolishnesse of madnesse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 33:23; 1 Kings 11:1–3; Psalms 12:1"> And I finde more bitter then death the woman whose heart is as nettes and snares, and her handes, as bands: he that is good before God, shalbe deliuered from her, but the sinner shall be taken by her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Mark 7:8–9; Titus 3:3"> Beholde, sayth the Preacher, this haue I found, seeking one by one to finde the count:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And yet my soule seeketh, but I finde it not: I haue found one man of a thousand: but a woman among them all haue I not founde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Onely loe, this haue I founde, that God hath made man righteous: but they haue sought many inuentions.</span>
