<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:17; Matthew 16:3; Ecclesiastes 8:5–6"> To all things there is an appointed time, and a time to euery purpose vnder the heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Job 14:5; Matthew 15:13"> A time to bee borne, and a time to die: a time to plant, and a time to plucke vp that which is planted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 6:1–2; 1 Samuel 2:6; Jeremiah 45:4"> A time to slay, and a time to heale: a time to breake downe, and a time to builde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalms 30:5; James 4:9"> A time to weepe, and a time to laugh: a time to mourne, and a time to dance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 3:25; Joshua 10:27; Joshua 4:3–9"> A time to cast away stones, and a time to gather stones: a time to embrace, and a time to be farre from embracing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; Luke 9:24–25; Mark 8:35–37"> A time to seeke, and a time to lose: a time to keepe, and a time to cast away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Micah 7:5; Lamentations 3:28"> A time to rent, and a time to sowe: a time to keepe silence, and a time to speake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25; Ephesians 5:28–29; Luke 14:26"> A time to loue, and a time to hate: a time of warre, and a time of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:3; Ecclesiastes 5:16; Proverbs 14:23"> What profite hath hee that worketh of the thing wherein he trauaileth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:13–14; Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> I haue seene the trauaile that God hath giuen to ye sonnes of men to humble them thereby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Romans 1:19–20; Job 11:7"> He hath made euery thing beautifull in his time: also he hath set the worlde in their heart, yet can not man finde out the worke that God hath wrought from the beginning euen to the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:3; Ecclesiastes 3:22; 1 Timothy 6:18"> I know that there is nothing good in them, but to reioyce, and to doe good in his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Ecclesiastes 2:24"> And also that euery man eateth and drinketh, and seeth the commoditie of all his labour. this is the gift of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:17; Psalms 33:11; Romans 11:36"> I knowe that whatsoeuer God shall doe, it shalbe for euer: to it can no man adde, and from it can none diminish: for God hath done it, that they should feare before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:9–10; Ecclesiastes 6:10"> What is that that hath bene? that is nowe: and that that shalbe, hath now bene: for God requireth that which is past.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Ecclesiastes 5:8; Psalms 94:21–22"> And moreouer I haue seene vnder the sunne the place of iudgement, where was wickednesse, and the place of iustice where was iniquitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Matthew 16:27"> I thought in mine heart, God wil iudge the iust and the wicked: for time is there for euery purpose and for euery worke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; Psalms 49:12; Hebrews 9:27"> I considered in mine heart the state of the children of men that God had purged them: yet to see to, they are in themselues as beastes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 49:12; Psalms 49:20; Psalms 89:47–48"> For the condition of the children of men, and the condition of beasts are euen as one condition vnto them. As the one dyeth, so dyeth the other: for they haue all one breath, and there is no excellency of man aboue ye beast: for all is vanitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Job 34:15; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> All goe to one place, and all was of the dust, and all shall returne to the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; John 14:3; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> Who knoweth whether the spirit of man ascend vpward, and the spirit of the beast descend downeward to the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:24; Ecclesiastes 3:11–12; Ecclesiastes 10:14"> Therefore I see that there is nothing better then that a man shoulde reioyce in his affaires, because that is his portion. For who shall bring him to see what shalbe after him?</span>
