<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Genesis 26:1; Ezekiel 14:13"> In the time that the Iudges ruled, there was a dearth in the lande, and a man of Beth-lehem Iudah went for to soiourne in the countrey of Moab, he, and his wife, and his two sonnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 35:19; Micah 5:2; 1 Samuel 17:21"> And the name of the man was Elimelech, and the name of his wife, Naomi: and the names of his two sonnes, Mahlon, and Chilion, Ephrathites of Beth-lehem Iudah: and when they came into the land of Moab, they continued there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:10–11; Psalms 34:19; Hebrews 12:6"> Then Elimelech the husband of Naomi died, and she remayned with her two sonnes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 1:5; 1 Kings 11:1–2; Deuteronomy 23:3"> Which tooke them wiues of the Moabites: the ones name was Orpah, and the name of ye other Ruth: and they dwelled there about ten yeeres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Psalms 89:30–32; Isaiah 49:21"> And Mahlon and Chilion dyed also both twaine: so the woman was left destitute of her two sonnes, and of her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 132:15; Exodus 3:16; Psalms 111:5"> Then she arose with her daughters in law, and returned from the countrey of Moab: for she had heard say in the countrey of Moab, that the Lord had visited his people, and giuen them bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 8:3; Ruth 1:14; Exodus 18:27"> Wherefore shee departed out of the place where she was, and her two daughters in law with her, and they went on their way to returne vnto the land of Iudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 2:20; Ruth 1:5; Colossians 3:18"> Then Naomi saide vnto her two daughters in lawe, Goe, returne eche of you vnto her owne mothers house: the Lord shew fauour vnto you, as ye haue done with the dead, and with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ruth 3:1; Acts 20:37; Genesis 29:11"> The Lord graunt you, that you may finde rest, either of you in the house of her husband. And when she kissed them, they lift vp their voice and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 8:23; Psalms 16:3; Psalms 119:63"> And they saide vnto her, Surely we will returne with thee vnto thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Genesis 38:11"> But Naomi saide, Turne againe, my daughters: for what cause will you go with me? are there any more sonnes in my wombe, that they may bee your husbands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; 1 Timothy 5:9"> Turne againe, my daughters: go your way: for I am too olde to haue an husband. If I should say, I haue hope, and if I had an husband this night: yea, if I had borne sonnes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 19:21; Judges 2:15; Psalms 32:4"> Would yee tarie for them, till they were of age? would ye be deferred for them from taking of husbands? nay my daughters: for it grieueth me much for your sakes that the hand of the Lord is gone out against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:24; Matthew 16:24; Matthew 10:37"> Then they lift vp their voyce and wept againe, and Orpah kissed her mother in lawe, but Ruth abode still with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 11:24; Joshua 24:15; Zephaniah 1:6"> And Naomi said, Beholde, thy sister in law is gone backe vnto her people and vnto her gods: returne thou after thy sister in lawe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:21; Ruth 2:11–12; 2 Kings 2:2–6"> And Ruth answered, Intreate mee not to leaue thee, nor to depart from thee: for whither thou goest, I will goe: and where thou dwellest, I will dwell: thy people shall be my people, and thy God my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:17; 1 Kings 2:23; 2 Kings 6:31"> Where thou diest, will I die, and there will I be buried. the Lord do so to me and more also, if ought but death depart thee and me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:14; Ephesians 6:10; Acts 2:42"> Whe she saw that she was stedfastly minded to go with her, she left speaking vnto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 21:10; Isaiah 23:7; Lamentations 2:15"> So they went both vntill they came to Beth-lehem: and when they were come to Beth-lehem, it was noysed of them through all the citie, and they said, Is not this Naomi?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 6:4; Hebrews 12:11; Psalms 73:14"> And she answered them, Call me not Naomi, but call me Mara: for the Almightie hath giuen me much bitternes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:7–8; Job 1:21; Job 16:8"> I went out full, and the Lord hath caused me to returne emptie: why call ye me Naomi, seeing the Lord hath humbled me, and the Almightie hath brought me vnto aduersitie?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ruth 2:23; 2 Samuel 21:9; Exodus 9:31–32"> So Naomi returned and Ruth the Moabitesse her daughter in law with her, when she came out of the countrey of Moab: and they came to Beth-lehem in the beginning of barly haruest.</span>
