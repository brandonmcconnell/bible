<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 5:16–18; John 4:3; Matthew 21:38"> After these things, Iesus walked in Galile, and woulde not walke in Iudea: for the Iewes sought to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:16–19; Deuteronomy 16:13–16; Exodus 23:16–17"> Nowe the Iewes feast of the Tabernacles was at hande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; John 7:5; Luke 8:19"> His brethren therefore sayde vnto him, Depart hence, and goe into Iudea, that thy disciples may see thy woorkes that thou doest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:20; Proverbs 18:1–2; Matthew 23:5"> For there is no man that doeth any thing secretely, and hee himselfe seeketh to be famous. If thou doest these things, shewe thy selfe to the worlde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 3:21; John 1:11–13; Micah 7:5–6"> For as yet his brethren beleeued not in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 7:8; John 2:4; John 7:30"> Then Iesus saide vnto them, My time is not yet come: but your time is alway readie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 3:19; Galatians 4:16"> The world can not hate you: but me it hateth, because I testifie of it, that the workes thereof are euill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:6; John 8:20; John 7:30"> Go ye vp vnto this feast: I wil not go vp yet vnto this feast: for my time is not yet fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> These things he sayde vnto them, and abode still in Galile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Matthew 10:16; Isaiah 42:2–3"> But assoone as his brethren were gone vp, then went hee also vp vnto the feast, not openly, but as it were priuilie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:56"> Then the Iewes sought him at the feast, and saide, Where is hee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 7:40–43; John 9:16; John 7:32"> And much murmuring was there of him among the people. Some said, He is a good man: other sayd, Nay: but he deceiueth the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 9:22; John 19:38; John 20:19"> Howbeit no man spake openly of him for feare of the Iewes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:28; Luke 19:47; John 7:37"> Nowe when halfe the feast was done, Iesus went vp into the Temple and taught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54"> And the Iewes marueiled, saving, Howe knoweth this man the Scriptures, seeing that hee neuer learned!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:10; John 14:24; John 17:8"> Iesus answered them, and saide, My doctrine is not mine, but his that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:31–32; John 8:43; Jeremiah 31:33–34"> If any man will doe his will, he shall knowe of the doctrine, whether it be of God, or whether I speake of my selfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 5:41; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33; John 13:31–32"> Hee that speaketh of himselfe, seeketh his owne glorie: but hee that seeketh his glory that sent him, the same is true, and no vnrighteousnes is in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 1:17; John 11:53; Matthew 12:14"> Did not Moses giue you a Law, and yet none of you keepeth the lawe? Why goe ye about to kill me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:20; John 8:48; John 8:52"> The people answered, and said, Thou hast a deuil: who goeth about to kill thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:23; John 5:2–11"> Iesus answered, and saide to them, I haue done one worke, and ye all maruaile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Genesis 17:10–14; Romans 4:9–11"> Moses therefore gaue vnto you circumcision, (not because it is of Moses, but of the fathers) and ye on the Sabbath day circumcise a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:5; John 5:14–16; Matthew 12:2"> If a man on the Sabbath receiue circumcision, that the Lawe of Moses should not be broken, be ye angrie with me, because I haue made a man euery whit whole on the Sabbath day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–4; James 2:9; John 8:15"> Iudge not according to the appearance, but iudge righteous iudgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 7:10–11"> Then saide some of them of Hierusalem, Is not this he, whom they goe about to kill?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7–8; John 7:48; John 12:42"> And beholde, he speaketh openly, and they say nothing to him: doe the rulers know in deede that this is in deede that Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:41–42; John 6:42"> Howbeit we know this man whence he is: but when that Christ commeth, no man shall knowe whence he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:42; John 8:19; John 8:26"> Then cried Iesus in the Temple as hee taught, saying, Ye both knowe mee, and knowe whence I am: yet am I not come of my selfe, but he that sent me, is true, whome ye knowe not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:55; Matthew 11:27; John 17:18"> But I knowe him: for I am of him, and he hath sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 8:20; John 10:39; John 7:6"> Then they sought to take him, but no man layde handes on him, because his houre was not yet come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:42; Matthew 12:23; John 10:41–42"> Now many of the people beleeued in him, and said, When that Christ commeth, will he doe moe miracles then this man hath done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 18:3; Luke 22:52–53; John 11:47–48"> The Pharises heard that the people murmured these thinges of him, and the Pharises, and high Priestes sent officers to take him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 16:5; John 13:33; Mark 16:19"> Then saide Iesus vnto them, Yet am I a little while with you, and then goe I vnto him that sent mee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; John 13:33–36; John 14:6"> Ye shall seeke me, and shall not finde me, and where I am, can ye not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="James 1:1; Isaiah 11:12; Zephaniah 3:10"> Then saide the Iewes amongs themselues, Whither will he goe, that we shall not finde him? Will he goe vnto them that are dispersed among the Grecians, and teache the Grecians?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 3:9; John 7:34; John 12:34"> What saying is this that hee saide, Ye shall seeke mee, and shall not finde mee? and where I am, cannot ye come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 55:1; John 4:14; John 6:35"> Nowe in the last and great day of the feast, Iesus stoode and cried, saying, If any man thirst, let him come vnto me, and drinke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 44:3; Isaiah 58:11"> Hee that beleeueth in mee, as saith the Scripture, out of his bellie shall flowe riuers of water of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 2:17; Joel 2:28; John 14:16–17"> (This spake hee of the Spirite which they that beleeued in him, should receiue: for the holy Ghost was not yet giuen, because that Iesus was not yet glorified.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; John 1:21; John 6:14"> So many of the people, when they heard this saying, said, Of a trueth this is that Prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:52; John 1:46; John 1:49"> Other saide, This is that Christ: and some said, But shall that Christ come out of Galile?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:5; Luke 2:4"> Saith not the Scripture that that Christ shall come of the seede of Dauid, and out of the towne of Beth-leem, where Dauid was?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 7:12"> So was there dissension among the people for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 7:30; Acts 23:11; John 8:20"> And some of them would haue taken him, but no man layde handes on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 7:32; Acts 5:21–27"> Then came the officers to the hie Priests and Pharises, and they said vnto them, Why haue ye not brought him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 7:28–29; Luke 4:22; John 7:26"> The officers answered, Neuer man spake like this man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 7:12; 2 Corinthians 6:8; 2 Kings 18:32"> Then answered them the Pharises, Are yee also deceiued?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 12:42; 1 Corinthians 1:20; 1 Corinthians 2:8"> Doeth any of the rulers, or of the Pharises beleeue in him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20–21; John 9:40; John 9:34"> But this people, which know not the Law, are cursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:39; John 3:1–2"> Nicodemus said vnto them, (he that came to Iesus by night, and was one of them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Deuteronomy 1:17; Deuteronomy 19:15–19"> Doth our Law iudge a man before it heare him, and knowe what he hath done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 7:41; John 1:46; Isaiah 9:1–2"> They answered, and said vnto him, Art thou also of Galile? Searche and looke: for out of Galile ariseth no Prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Job 5:12–13; Psalms 76:10"> And euery man wet vnto his owne house.</span>
