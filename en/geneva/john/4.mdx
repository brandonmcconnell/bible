<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 3:26; John 3:22; 2 Corinthians 4:5"> Nowe when the Lord knew, how the Pharises had heard, that Iesus made and baptized moe disciples then Iohn,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:48; 1 Corinthians 1:13–17"> (Though Iesus himselfe baptized not: but his disciples)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; John 10:40; John 2:11"> Hee left Iudea, and departed againe into Galile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 17:11; Luke 2:49; Luke 9:51–52"> And he must needes goe through Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 24:32; Genesis 33:19; Genesis 48:22"> Then came hee to a citie of Samaria called Sychar, neere vnto the possession that Iacob gaue to his sonne Ioseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 2:17; Matthew 8:24"> And there was Iacobs well. Iesus then wearied in the iourney, sate thus on the well: it was about the sixt houre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; 1 Kings 17:10; Genesis 24:43"> There came a woman of Samaria to drawe water. Iesus sayd vnto her, Giue me drinke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:13; John 4:39; John 4:5"> For his disciples were gone away into the citie, to buy meate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Matthew 10:5; 2 Kings 17:24–41"> Then sayde the woman of Samaria vnto him, Howe is it, that thou being a Iewe, askest drinke of me, which am a woman of Samaria? For the Iewes meddle not with the Samaritans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:14; Revelation 7:17; John 7:37–39"> Iesus answered and saide vnto her, If thou knewest that gift of God, and who it is that saieth to thee, Giue mee drinke, thou wouldest haue asked of him, and hee woulde haue giuen thee, water of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 3:4"> The woman saide vnto him, Sir, thou hast nothing to drawe with, and the well is deepe: from whence then hast thou that water of life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 4:6; John 8:53; Matthew 12:42"> Art thou greater then our father Iacob, which gaue vs the well, and hee himselfe dranke thereof, and his sonnes, and his cattell?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:27; Isaiah 65:13–14; Luke 16:24"> Iesus answered, and said vnto her, Whosoeuer drinketh of this water, shall thirst againe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:38–39; John 6:35; Isaiah 49:10"> But whosoeuer drinketh of the water that I shall giue him, shall neuer be more a thirst: but the water that I shall giue him, shalbe in him a well of water, springing vp into euerlasting life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 6:34; Romans 8:5; Psalms 4:6"> The woman said vnto him, Syr, giue me of that water, that I may not thirst, neither come hither to drawe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23; John 4:18"> Iesus said vnto her, Go, call thine husband, and come hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The woman answered, and saide, I haue no husband. Iesus said vnto her, Thou hast well said, I haue no husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:20; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Romans 7:3"> For thou hast had fiue husbands, and he whom thou nowe hast, is not thine husband: that saidest thou truely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 6:14; John 9:17"> The woman saide vnto him, Sir, I see that thou art a Prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 12:6–7; Deuteronomy 27:12; Deuteronomy 11:29"> Our fathers worshipped in this mountaine, and ye say, that in Ierusalem is the place where men ought to worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:8; Malachi 1:11; John 4:23"> Iesus saide vnto her, Woman, beleeue me, the houre commeth, when ye shall neither in this mountaine, nor at Hierusalem worship ye Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 17:23; Isaiah 2:3; Romans 9:4–5"> Ye worship that which ye knowe not: we worship that which we knowe: for saluation is of the Iewes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 3:3; John 5:25; Matthew 15:7–9"> But the houre commeth, and nowe is, when the true worshippers shall worship the Father in spirit, and trueth: for the Father requireth euen such to worship him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Matthew 15:8–9; Philippians 3:3"> God is a Spirite, and they that worship him, must worship him in spirit and trueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 1:49; Daniel 9:24–26; John 1:41–42"> The woman said vnto him, I knowe well that Messias shall come which is called Christ: when he is come, he will tell vs all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:24; Mark 14:61–62; Romans 10:20–21"> Iesus said vnto her, I am he, that speake vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 4:8–9"> And vpon that, came his disciples, and marueiled that he talked with a woman: yet no man said vnto him, What askest thou? or why talkest thou with her?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 4:7; Luke 24:33; Mark 16:8–10"> The woman then left her waterpot, and went her way into the citie, and said to the men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; 1 Corinthians 14:24–25; John 4:25"> Come, see a man which hath tolde me all things that euer I did: is not he that Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 5:20; Acts 8:5–8; Luke 17:16–18"> Then they went out of the citie, and came vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:38; Genesis 24:33; Acts 16:30–34"> In the meane while, the disciples prayed him, saying, Master, eate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 23:12; Psalms 119:103; John 4:34"> But he said vnto them, I haue meate to eate that ye know not of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 16:6–11; Luke 9:45"> Then said ye disciples betweene themselues, Hath any man brought him meate?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 6:38; John 5:30; Job 23:12"> Iesus saide vnto them, My meate is that I may doe the will of him that sent me, and finish his worke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 10:2–3; Matthew 9:37–38; John 4:30"> Say not ye, There are yet foure moneths, and then commeth haruest? Beholde, I say vnto you, Lift vp your eyes, and looke on the regions: for they are white alreadie vnto haruest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="James 5:19–20; Romans 1:13; 1 Corinthians 3:5–9"> And he that reapeth, receiueth rewarde, and gathereth fruite vnto life eternall, that both he that soweth, and he that reapeth, might reioyce together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Job 31:8; Luke 19:21"> For herein is the saying true, that one soweth, and an other reapeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 1:7; 2 Chronicles 36:15; Acts 10:42–43"> I sent you to reape that, whereon ye bestowed no labour: other men laboured, and ye are entred into their labours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 4:29; John 4:5; John 10:41–42"> Nowe many of the Samaritans of that citie beleeued in him, for the saying of the woman which testified, He hath tolde me all things that euer I did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Acts 16:15; Luke 24:29"> Then when the Samaritans were come vnto him, they besought him, that he woulde tarie with them: and he abode there two dayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4–5; Acts 1:8; Acts 8:12"> And many moe beleeued because of his owne word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 1:29; 1 John 5:20"> And they said vnto the woman, Nowe we beleeue, not because of thy saying: for we haue heard him our selues, and knowe that this is in deede that Christ the Sauiour of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 4:13; Romans 15:8; Mark 7:27–28"> So two dayes after he departed thence, and went into Galile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 13:57; Luke 4:24; Mark 6:4"> For Iesus himselfe had testified, that a Prophet hath none honour in his owne countrey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 2:13–16; Deuteronomy 16:16"> Then when he was come into Galile, the Galileans receiued him, which had seene all the things that he did at Hierusalem at the feast: for they went also vnto the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 2:1–11; Luke 7:2; Hosea 5:15"> And Iesus came againe into Cana a towne of Galile, where he had made of water, wine. And there was a certaine ruler, whose sonne was sicke at Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 4:3; John 11:21; John 11:32"> When he heard that Iesus was come out of Iudea into Galile, he went vnto him, and besought him that he would goe downe, and heale his sonne: for he was euen ready to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 20:29; Hebrews 2:4; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> Then saide Iesus vnto him, Except ye see signes and wonders, ye will not beleeue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 5:23; Psalms 88:10–12; Psalms 40:17"> The ruler said vnto him, Syr, goe downe before my sonne dye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 8:13; Mark 7:29–30; Romans 4:20–21"> Iesus said vnto him, Go thy way, thy sonne liueth: and the man beleeued the worde that Iesus had spoken vnto him, and went his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Kings 17:23; John 4:50; John 4:53"> And as he was nowe going downe, his seruants met him, saying, Thy sonne liueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> Then enquired he of them the houre when he began to amend. And they said vnto him, Yesterday the seuenth houre the feuer left him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Luke 19:9; Acts 16:34"> Then the father knew, that it was the same houre in the which Iesus had said vnto him, Thy sonne liueth. And he beleeued, and all his houshold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 4:45; John 2:1–11"> This second miracle did Iesus againe, after he was come out of Iudea into Galile.</span>
