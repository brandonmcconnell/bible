<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; John 12:17; 2 Kings 20:1–12"> And a certaine man was sicke, named Lazarus of Bethania, the towne of Marie, and her sister Martha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 12:3; Mark 14:3; Matthew 26:6–7"> (And it was that Mary which anointed the Lord with oyntment, and wiped his feete with her heare, whose brother Lazarus was sicke.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:5; Revelation 3:19; 2 Timothy 4:20"> Therefore his sisters sent vnto him, saying, Lord, beholde, he whome thou louest, is sicke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 11:40; John 9:3; 1 Peter 4:14"> When Iesus heard it, he saide, This sickenes is not vnto death, but for the glorie of God, that the Sonne of God might be glorified thereby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:9–13; John 16:27; John 17:26"> Nowe Iesus loued Martha and her sister, and Lazarus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 30:18; Isaiah 55:8–9; Genesis 45:1–5"> And after he had heard that he was sicke, yet abode hee two dayes still in the same place where he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:40–42; Luke 9:51; Acts 20:22–24"> Then after that, said he to his disciples, Let vs goe into Iudea againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:31; John 8:59; Acts 21:12–13"> The disciples saide vnto him, Master, the Iewes lately sought to stone thee, and doest thou goe thither againe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 9:4; John 12:35; Jeremiah 31:9"> Iesus answered, Are there not twelue houres in the day? If a man walke in the day, hee stumbleth not, because he seeth the light of this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:10–11; Proverbs 4:18–19; Jeremiah 13:16"> But if a man walke in the night, hee stumbleth, because there is no light in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 11:13; John 11:43–44"> These things spake he, and after, he said vnto them, Our friend Lazarus sleepeth: but I goe to wake him vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then said his disciples, Lord, if he sleepe, he shalbe safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:24"> Howbeit, Iesus spake of his death: but they thought that he had spoken of the naturall sleepe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:25; John 16:29; John 10:24"> Then saide Iesus vnto them plainely, Lazarus is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; John 14:10–11; Isaiah 54:15"> And I am glad for your sakes, that I was not there, that ye may beleeue: but let vs go vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 13:37; Matthew 10:3; John 21:2"> Then saide Thomas (which is called Didymus) vnto his felow disciples, Let vs also goe, that we may die with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 11:39; Hosea 6:2; Acts 2:27–31"> Then came Iesus, and found that he had lien in the graue foure dayes alreadie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 14:20; Revelation 21:16; John 6:19"> (Nowe Bethania was neere vnto Hierusalem, about fifteene furlongs off.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 2:11; John 11:31; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> And many of ye Iewes were come to Martha and Marie to comfort them for their brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; 1 Thessalonians 4:17; Matthew 25:1"> Then Martha, when shee heard that Iesus was comming, went to meete him: but Mary sate still in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:32; 1 Kings 17:18; Matthew 9:18"> Then said Martha vnto Iesus, Lord, if thou hadst bene here, my brother had not bene dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:31; John 11:41–42; Matthew 28:18"> But now I know also, that whatsoeuer thou askest of God, God will giue it thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:43–44"> Iesus said vnto her, Thy brother shall rise againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Acts 24:15; John 5:28–29"> Martha said vnto him, I know that he shall rise againe in the resurrection at the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14; John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12"> Iesus saide vnto her, I am the resurrection and the life: he that beleeueth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he liue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 10:28; John 5:24; Romans 8:13"> And whosoeuer liueth, and beleeueth in me, shall neuer die: Beleeuest thou this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:16; John 6:69; John 6:14"> She said vnto him, Yea, Lord, I beleeue that thou art that Christ that Sonne of God, which should come into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 11:20; John 13:13; Mark 10:49"> And when she had so saide, she went her way, and called Mary her sister secretly, saying, The Master is come, and calleth for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Psalms 27:8; Song of Solomon 3:1–4"> And when she heard it, shee arose quickly, and came vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:20"> For Iesus was not yet come into the towne, but was in the place where Martha met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:19; Genesis 37:35; 2 Samuel 12:16–18"> The Iewes then which were with her in the house, and comforted her, when they sawe Marie, that she rose vp hastily, and went out, folowed her, saying, She goeth vnto the graue, to weepe there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 11:21; Luke 17:16; Luke 5:8"> Then when Mary was come where Iesus was, and sawe him, she fell downe at his feete, saying vnto him, Lord, if thou haddest bene here, my brother had not bene dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:38; John 12:27; Mark 3:5"> When Iesus therefore saw her weepe, and the Iewes also weepe which came with her, hee groned in the spirit, and was troubled in himselfe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 15:47; Matthew 28:6; John 1:39"> And saide, Where haue ye layde him? They said vnto him, Lord, come and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Hebrews 4:15; John 11:33"> And Iesus wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 11:3; 1 John 3:1; 1 John 4:9–10"> Then saide the Iewes, Beholde, how he loued him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 11:32; Luke 23:39"> And some of them saide, Coulde not he, which opened the eyes of the blinde, haue made also, that this man should not haue died?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:33; Luke 24:2; Matthew 27:60"> Iesus therefore againe groned in himselfe, and came to the graue. And it was a caue, and a stone was layde vpon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 11:17; Mark 16:3; Psalms 49:7"> Iesus saide, Take ye away the stone. Martha the sister of him that was dead, said vnto him, Lord, he stinketh alreadie: for he hath bene dead foure dayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:23–26; Romans 4:17–25"> Iesus saide vnto her, Saide I not vnto thee, that if thou diddest beleeue, thou shouldest see the glorie of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; John 17:1; Psalms 123:1"> Then they tooke away the stone from the place where the dead was layde. And Iesus lift vp his eyes, and saide, Father, I thanke thee, because thou hast heard me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 3:17; John 17:21; 1 John 4:14"> I knowe that thou hearest me alwayes, but because of the people that stand by, I said it, that they may beleeue, that thou hast sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33–36; Acts 9:40; Acts 9:34"> As hee had spoken these things, hee cried with a loude voyce, Lazarus, come foorth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 5:25; John 20:7; John 11:25–26"> Then he that was dead, came forth, bound hande and foote with bandes, and his face was bound with a napkin. Iesus said vnto them, Loose him, and let him goe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 11:19; John 10:41"> Then many of the Iewes, which came to Mary, and had seene the thinges, which Iesus did, beleeued in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 9:13; Acts 5:25; John 12:37"> But some of them went their way to the Pharises, and told them what things Iesus had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 12:19; Acts 4:5–6"> Then gathered the hie Priests, and the Pharises a councill, and said, What shall we doe? For this man doeth many miracles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Matthew 23:35–38; Matthew 27:25"> If we let him thus alone, all men will beleeue in him, and the Romanes will come and take away both our place, and the nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 11:51; John 18:13–14"> Then one of them named Caiaphas, which was the hie Priest that same yere, said vnto them, Ye perceiue nothing at all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:14; Luke 24:46; Romans 3:8"> Nor yet doe you consider that it is expedient for vs, that one man die for the people, and that the whole nation perish not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Exodus 28:30"> This spake hee not of himselfe: but being hie Priest that same yere, he prophecied that Iesus should die for that nation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 10:16; Isaiah 49:6; 1 John 2:2"> And not for that nation onely, but that he shoulde gather together in one the children of God, which were scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Acts 5:33; Acts 9:23; Mark 14:1"> Then from that day foorth they consulted together, to put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 7:1; John 7:4; 2 Chronicles 13:19"> Iesus therefore walked no more openly among the Iewes, but went thence vnto a countrey neere to the wildernes, into a citie called Ephraim, and there continued with his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:17–20; Hebrews 9:13–14; John 6:4"> And the Iewes Passeouer was at hande, and many went out of the countrey vp to Hierusalem before the Passeouer, to purifie themselues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 7:11; John 11:7–8"> Then sought they for Iesus, and spake among themselues, as they stoode in the Temple, What thinke ye, that he cometh not to the feast?</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 9:22; John 8:59; John 5:16–18"> Now both the high Priestes and the Pharises had giuen a commandement, that if any man knew where he were, he should shew it, that they might take him.</span>
