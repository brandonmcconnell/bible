<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 14:3–8; John 11:1; Luke 24:50"> Then Iesus, sixe dayes before the Passeouer, came to Bethania, where Lazarus was, who died, whom he had raised from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 22:27; Luke 10:38–42; Revelation 3:20"> There they made him a supper, and Martha serued: but Lazarus was one of them that sate at the table with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; John 11:2; Luke 10:38–39"> Then tooke Mary a pound of oyntment of Spikenarde very costly, and anoynted Iesus feete, and wiped his feete with her heare, and the house was filled with the sauour of the oyntment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:4; 1 Samuel 17:28–29; John 13:2"> Then said one of his disciples, euen Iudas Iscariot Simons sonne, which should betray him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:22; Luke 12:33; Mark 14:4–5"> Why was not this oyntment sold for three hundreth pence, and giuen to the poore?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 13:29; Psalms 50:16–20; James 2:6"> Nowe he said this, not that he cared for the poore, but because hee was a theefe, and had the bagge, and bare that which was giuen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:12; Mark 14:6; Matthew 27:57–60"> Then said Iesus, Let her alone: against the day of my burying she kept it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Mark 14:7; Matthew 26:11"> For the poore alwayes yee haue with you, but me ye shall not haue alwayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:14; John 11:43–45; Acts 3:10–11"> Then much people of the Iewes knewe that hee was there: and they came, not for Iesus sake onely, but that they might see Lazarus also, whome he had raysed from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Matthew 2:16; John 11:47–53"> The hie Priestes therefore consulted, that they might put Lazarus to death also,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:45; John 12:18; Acts 13:45"> Because that for his sake many of the Iewes went away, and beleeued in Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:7–10; Matthew 21:4–9; Luke 19:35–38"> On the morowe a great multitude that were come to the feast, when they heard that Iesus should come to Hierusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 118:25–26; Revelation 15:3; Matthew 21:9–11"> Tooke branches of palme trees, and went foorth to meete him, and cried, Hosanna, Blessed is the King of Israel that commeth in the Name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Mark 11:1–7; Matthew 21:1–7"> And Iesus found a yong asse, and sate thereon, as it is written,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Isaiah 62:11; Zephaniah 3:16–17"> Feare not, daughter of Sion: behold, thy King commeth sitting on an asses colte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 2:22; John 14:26; John 7:39"> But his disciples vnderstoode not these thinges at the first: but when Iesus was glorified, then remembred they, that these thinges were written of him, and that they had done these things vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 1:2; 1 John 5:9–12; John 19:35"> The people therefore that was with him, bare witnesse that hee called Lazarus out of the graue, and raised him from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 12:11"> Therefore mette him the people also, because they heard that he had done this miracle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:6; Psalms 22:27; 1 John 2:2"> And the Pharises said among themselues, Perceiue ye howe ye preuaile nothing? Beholde, the worlde goeth after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:35; Isaiah 11:10; Acts 17:4"> Nowe there were certaine Greekes among them that came vp to worship at the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Luke 19:2–4; John 6:40"> And they came to Philippe, which was of Bethsaida in Galile, and desired him, saying, Syr, we would see that Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 10:13–14; Luke 9:49–50; John 1:40–41"> Philippe came and tolde Andrew: and againe Andrew and Philippe tolde Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:31–32; John 17:1–5; Isaiah 60:9"> And Iesus answered them, saying, The houre is come, that the Sonne of man must bee glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36–38; Isaiah 53:10–12; Hebrews 2:9–10"> Verely, verely I say vnto you, Except the wheate corne fall into the grounde and die, it bideth alone: but if it die, it bringeth foorth much fruite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 10:39; Luke 9:23–24; Mark 8:35"> He that loueth his life, shall lose it, and he that hateth his life in this world, shall keepe it vnto life eternall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Samuel 2:30"> If any man serue me, let him follow me: for where I am, there shall also my seruant be: and if any man serue me, him will my Father honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 12:23; Hebrews 5:7; Matthew 26:42"> Now is my soule troubled: and what shall I say? Father, saue me from this houre: but therefore came I vnto this houre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 13:31–32; Matthew 26:42; Revelation 5:9–14"> Father, glorifie thy Name. Then came there a voyce from heauen, saying, I haue both glorified it, and will glorifie it againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:2–5; Revelation 6:1; Revelation 14:2"> Then saide the people that stoode by, and heard, that it was a thunder: other said, An Angel spake to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:42; John 11:15; John 5:34"> Iesus answered, and said, This voyce came not because of me, but for your sakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 John 5:19; 1 John 3:8"> Now is the iudgement of this world: nowe shall the prince of this world be cast out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:14; John 8:28; 1 Peter 2:24"> And I, if I were lift vp from the earth, will drawe all men vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 18:32; John 21:19"> Nowe this sayd he, signifying what death he should die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Isaiah 9:7; Daniel 7:14"> The people answered him, We haue heard out of the Law, that that Christ bideth for euer: and howe sayest thou, that that Sonne of man must be lift vp? Who is that Sonne of man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 12:46; John 16:16; Ephesians 5:8"> Then Iesus sayd vnto them, Yet a litle while is the light with you: walke while ye haue that light, lest the darkenes come vpon you: for hee that walketh in the darke, knoweth not whither he goeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; John 8:59; Acts 13:47–48"> While ye haue that light, beleeue in that light, that ye may be the children of the light. These things spake Iesus, and departed, and hid himselfe from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 16:31; John 1:11; John 15:24"> And though he had done so many miracles before them, yet beleeued they not on him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 10:16; Romans 10:20"> That the saying of Esaias the Prophete might be fulfilled, that he sayd, Lord, who beleeued our report? and to whome is the arme of the Lord reueiled?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 10:38; John 5:44; 2 Peter 2:14"> Therefore could they not beleeue, because that Esaias saith againe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Matthew 13:13–15; Luke 8:10"> He hath blinded their eyes, and hardened their heart, that they shoulde not see with their eyes, nor vnderstand with their heart, and should be conuerted, and I should heale them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 6:1–5; Isaiah 6:9–10; John 1:14"> These things sayd Esaias when he sawe his glory, and spake of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 9:22; John 7:13; Isaiah 51:7"> Neuertheles, euen among the chiefe rulers, many beleeued in him: but because of the Pharises they did not confesse him, least they should be cast out of the Synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 5:44; Matthew 23:5–7; John 5:41"> For they loued the prayse of men, more then the prayse of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:20; John 5:24"> And Iesus cryed, and sayd, He that beleeueth in me, beleeueth not in me, but in him that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 14:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Colossians 1:15"> And he that seeth me, seeth him that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 8:12; Psalms 36:9; John 9:5"> I am come a light into the world, that whosoeuer beleeueth in me, should not abide in darkenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:17; John 8:15–16; John 12:48"> And if any man heare my wordes, and beleeue not, I iudge him not: for I came not to iudge the world, but to saue the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Hebrews 9:27–28; Romans 2:16"> He that refuseth me, and receiueth not my wordes, hath one that iudgeth him: the worde that I haue spoken, it shall iudge him in the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 14:10; John 8:26; John 5:30"> For I haue not spoken of my selfe: but the Father which sent me, hee gaue me a commandement what I should say, and what I should speake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 17:3; John 6:68; 1 John 5:20"> And I knowe that his commandement is life euerlasting: the thinges therefore that I speake, I speake them so as the Father sayde vnto me.</span>
