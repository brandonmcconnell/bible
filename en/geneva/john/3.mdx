<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 19:39; John 7:47–50; John 3:10"> There was nowe a man of the Pharises, named Nicodemus, a ruler of the Iewes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 5:36; Acts 10:38; Acts 2:22"> This man came to Iesus by night, and sayd vnto him, Rabbi, we knowe ye thou art a teacher come from God: for no man could do these miracles that thou doest, except God were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; John 3:5–6; 1 Peter 1:23–25"> Iesus answered, and said vnto him, Verely, verely I say vnto thee, except a man be borne againe, he can not see the kingdome of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 1:18; John 3:3"> Nicodemus sayde vnto him, Howe can a man be borne which is olde? can he enter into his mothers wombe againe, and be borne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25–27; Ephesians 5:26; Acts 2:38"> Iesus answered, Verely, verely I say vnto thee, except that a man be borne of water and of the Spirite, hee can not enter into the kingdome of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 5:16–21; Ezekiel 36:26–27; Romans 8:4–9"> That which is borne of the flesh, is flesh: and that that is borne of the Spirit, is spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 3:3; Ephesians 4:22–24; Romans 12:1–2"> Marueile not that I said to thee, Yee must be borne againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:4–5; 1 Corinthians 2:11; Acts 2:2"> The winde bloweth where it listeth, and thou hearest the sound thereof, but canst not tell whence it commeth, and whither it goeth: so is euery man that is borne of the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 6:60; Luke 1:34; Isaiah 42:16"> Nicodemus answered, and said vnto him, Howe can these things be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:29; Jeremiah 8:8–9; Ezekiel 11:19"> Iesus answered, and saide vnto him, Art thou a teacher of Israel, and knowest not these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 8:38; 1 John 5:6–12; John 12:49"> Verely, verely I say vnto thee, wee speake that we know, and testifie that we haue seene: but yee receiue not our witnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:1–2; 1 Corinthians 2:7–9; Hebrews 5:11"> If when I tel you earthly things, ye beleeue not, howe should yee beleeue, if I shall tel you of heauenly things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; John 6:38; Acts 2:34"> For no man ascendeth vp to heauen, but he that hath descended from heauen, that Sonne of man which is in heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 21:7–9; John 8:28; John 12:32–34"> And as Moses lift vp the serpent in the wildernesse, so must that Sonne of man be lift vp,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 3:16; John 6:40; John 12:44–46"> That whosoeuer beleeueth in him, shoulde not perish, but haue eternall life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 5:8; 1 John 4:9–10; John 3:15"> For God so loued the worlde, that hee hath giuen his onely begotten Sonne, that whosoeuer beleeueth in him, should not perish, but haue euerlasting life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 4:14; Luke 19:10; John 12:47–48"> For God sent not his Sonne into the world, that he should condemne the world, but that the world through him might be saued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 16:16; 1 John 5:10; Romans 8:1"> Hee that beleeueth in him, is not condemned: but hee that beleeueth not, is condemned already, because he hath not beleeued in the Name of that onely begotten Sonne of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 8:12; 2 Thessalonians 2:12; John 9:39–41"> And this is the condemnation, that that light came into the worlde, and men loued darknesse rather then that light, because their deedes were euill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11–13; John 7:7; Job 24:13–17"> For euery man that euill doeth, hateth the light, neither commeth to light, least his deedes should be reprooued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 1:6; Psalms 139:23–24; 3 John 1:11"> But he that doeth trueth, commeth to the light, that his deedes might bee made manifest, that they are wrought according to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:1–3; John 3:26; John 2:13"> After these things, came Iesus and his disciples into the lande of Iudea, and there taried with them, and baptized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 1:4–5; Ezekiel 43:2; Matthew 3:5–6"> And Iohn also baptized in Enon besides Salim, because there was much water there: and they came, and were baptized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 4:12; Matthew 14:3; Mark 6:17"> For Iohn was not yet cast into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 2:6; Matthew 3:11; Hebrews 6:2"> Then there arose a question betweene Iohns disciples and the Iewes, about purifying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 1:7; John 1:15; John 12:19"> And they came vnto Iohn, and saide vnto him, Rabbi, he that was with thee beyond Iorden, to whom thou barest witnesse, behold, he baptizeth, and all men come to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 4:7; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> Iohn answered, and saide, A man can receiue nothing, except it be giuen him from heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:20; John 1:23; Matthew 3:11–12"> Yee your selues are my witnesses, that I sayde, I am not that Christ, but that I am sent before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 9:15; Hosea 2:19; Isaiah 54:5"> He that hath the bride, is the bridegrome: but the friend of the bridegrome which standeth and heareth him, reioyceth greatly, because of the bridegromes voyce. This my ioy therefore is fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 1:18; Psalms 72:17–19; Hebrews 3:2–6"> He must increase, but I must decrease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 8:23; Matthew 28:18; Romans 9:5"> Hee that is come from an hie, is aboue all: he that is of the earth, is of the earth, and speaketh of the earth: hee that is come from heauen, is aboue all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:11; John 15:15; John 1:11"> And what hee hath seene and heard, that he testifieth: but no man receiueth his testimonie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 6:17; Ephesians 1:13; Titus 1:1–2"> He that hath receiued his testimonie, hath sealed that God is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 10:38; Matthew 12:18; John 15:26"> For hee whome God hath sent, speaketh the woordes of God: for God giueth him not the Spirit by measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; John 5:22; John 5:20"> The Father loueth the Sonne, and hath giuen all things into his hande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 5:24; John 1:12; 1 Thessalonians 5:9"> Hee that beleeueth in the Sonne, hath euerlasting life, and hee that obeyeth not the Sonne, shall not see life, but the wrath of God abideth on him.</span>
