<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:12; 1 John 4:12; 1 John 4:7–8"> If there be therfore any consolation in Christ, if any comfort of loue, if any fellowship of the Spirit, if any compassion and mercie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 12:16; 1 Peter 3:8–9; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> Fulfill my ioye, that ye be like minded, hauing the same loue, being of one accorde, and of one iudgement,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 12:10; Galatians 5:26; Ephesians 5:21"> That nothing be done through contention or vaine glory, but that in meekenesse of minde euery man esteeme other better then himselfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Romans 15:1; James 2:8"> Looke not euery man on his owne things, but euery man also on the things of other men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 11:29; 1 Peter 2:21"> Let the same minde be in you that was euen in Christ Iesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 5:18; 2 Corinthians 4:4; Hebrews 1:3"> Who being in ye forme of God, thought it no robberie to be equall with God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:9; Matthew 20:28; John 1:14"> But he made himself of no reputation, and tooke on him ye forme of a seruant, and was made like vnto men, and was founde in shape as a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:18; Hebrews 12:2; Romans 5:19"> He humbled himselfe, and became obedient vnto the death, euen the death of the Crosse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Matthew 28:18; 1 Peter 3:22"> Wherefore God hath also highly exalted him, and giuen him a Name aboue euery name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 45:23–25; Matthew 28:18; Revelation 5:13–14"> That at the Name of Iesus shoulde euery knee bowe, both of things in heauen, and things in earth, and things vnder the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Romans 14:11; John 13:13"> And that euery tongue shoulde confesse that Iesus Christ is the Lord, vnto the glory of God the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–10; Luke 13:23–24; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Wherefore my beloued, as ye haue alwayes obeyed me, not as in my presence only, but now much more in mine absence, so make an end of your owne saluation with feare and trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:21; 2 Corinthians 3:5; 2 Timothy 1:9"> For it is God which worketh in you, both the will and the deede, euen of his good pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 4:9; 1 Corinthians 10:10; Philippians 2:3"> Do all things without murmuring and reasonings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:14–16; 1 Peter 2:12; Matthew 5:45"> That ye may be blamelesse, and pure, and the sonnes of God without rebuke in the middes of a naughtie and crooked nation, among whom yee shine as lights in the world,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:26; Galatians 2:2; 1 John 1:1"> Holding forth the worde of life, that I may reioyce in the day of Christ, that I haue not runne in vaine, neither haue laboured in vaine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6; Romans 15:16; Hebrews 13:15–16"> Yea, and though I bee offered vp vpon the sacrifice, and seruice of your faith, I am glad, and reioyce with you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 1:2–4; Philippians 4:4; Philippians 3:1"> For the same cause also be ye glad, and reioyce with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:17; 1 Thessalonians 3:6–8; 1 Thessalonians 3:2"> And I trust in the Lord Iesus, to sende Timotheus shortly vnto you, that I also may be of good comfort, when I knowe your state.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Philippians 2:2; 1 Corinthians 16:10; Philippians 2:22"> For I haue no man like minded, who will faithfully care for your matters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Matthew 16:24; Philippians 2:4"> For all seeke their owne, and not that which is Iesus Christes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Corinthians 2:9; 2 Timothy 1:2"> But yee knowe the proofe of him, that as a sonne with the father, hee hath serued with me in the Gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:3; Philippians 2:19"> Him therefore I hope to send assoone as I knowe howe it will goe with me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="3 John 1:14; Philemon 1:22; Philippians 1:25–26"> And trust in the Lord, that I also my selfe shall come shortly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 4:18; Philemon 1:1–2; Philippians 4:3"> But I supposed it necessarie to sende my brother Epaphroditus vnto you, my companion in labour, and fellowe souldier, euen your messenger, and he that ministred vnto me such things as I wanted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Proverbs 12:25; Ephesians 3:13"> For he longed after all you, and was full of heauinesse, because yee had heard that hee had beene sicke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Philippians 2:30; Psalms 103:3–4"> And no doubt he was sicke, very neere vnto death: but God had mercie on him, and not on him onely, but on me also, least I should haue sorowe vpon sorowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 16:22; 2 Timothy 1:4; Philippians 2:26–27"> I sent him therefore the more diligently, that when yee shoulde see him againe, yee might reioyce, and I might be the lesse sorowfull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12; 1 Corinthians 16:18; 1 Timothy 5:17"> Receiue him therefore in the Lord with all gladnesse, and make much of such:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:17; Acts 20:24; Philippians 4:10"> Because that for the woorke of Christ he was neere vnto death, and regarded not his life, to fulfill that seruice which was lacking on your part towarde me.</span>
