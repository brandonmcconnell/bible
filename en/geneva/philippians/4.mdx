<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:16–18; Romans 12:12; James 1:2–4"> Reioyce in the Lord alway, againe I say, reioyce.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; Galatians 6:6; 2 Corinthians 7:6–7"> Nowe I reioyce also in the Lord greatly, that nowe at the last your care for mee springeth afresh, wherein notwithstanding ye were careful, but yee lacked opportunitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–9; Hebrews 13:5–6; Matthew 6:31–34"> I speake not because of want: for I haue learned in whatsoeuer state I am, therewith to bee content.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; 2 Corinthians 12:7–10; 2 Corinthians 11:27"> And I can be abased, and I can abounde: euery where in all things I am instructed, both to be full, and to be hungrie, and to abounde, and to haue want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Colossians 1:11"> I am able to do al things through the helpe of Christ, which strengtheneth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Philippians 1:7; Hebrews 10:34"> Notwithstanding yee haue well done, that yee did communicate to mine affliction.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; Acts 17:1; 1 Thessalonians 2:18"> For euen when I was in Thessalonica, yee sent once, and afterward againe for my necessitie,</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8–11; Psalms 84:11; 1 Peter 5:10"> And my God shall fulfill all your necessities through his riches with glorie in Iesus Christ.</span>
