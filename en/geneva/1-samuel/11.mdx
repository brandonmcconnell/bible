<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:12; 1 Kings 20:34; Ezekiel 17:13"> Then Nahash the Ammonite came vp, and besieged Iabesh Gilead: and all the men of Iabesh saide vnto Nahash, Make a couenant with vs, and we will be thy seruants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:26; Genesis 34:14; Numbers 16:14"> And Nahash ye Ammonite answered them, On this condition will I make a couenant with you, that I may thrust out all your right eies, and bring that shame vpon all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> To whome the Elders of Iabesh said, Giue vs seuen daies respet, that we may sende messengers vnto all the coastes of Israel: and then if no man deliuer vs, we will come out to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 2:4; 1 Samuel 10:26; 1 Samuel 15:34"> Then came the messengers to Gibeah of Saul, and tolde these tidings in the eares of the people: and all the people lift vp their voices and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 19:19; Judges 18:23; Genesis 21:17"> And behold, Saul came following the cattell out of the fielde, and Saul saide, What aileth this people, that they weepe? And they tolde him the tidings of the men of Iabesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:10; 1 Samuel 16:13; Judges 6:34"> Then the Spirit of God came vpon Saul, when he heard those tidings, and he was exceeding angrie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:29; Judges 20:1; Judges 21:5–11"> And tooke a yoke of oxen, and hewed them in pieces, and sent them throughout all the coastes of Israel by the hands of messengers, saying, Whosoeuer commeth not foorth after Saul, and after Samuel, so shall his oxen be serued. And the feare of the Lord fell on the people, and they came out with one consent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 1:4–5; 2 Samuel 24:9; 2 Chronicles 17:12–19"> And when he nombred them in Bezek, the children of Israel were three hundreth thousande men: and the men of Iudah thirtie thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 18:17"> Then they saide vnto the messengers that came, So say vnto the men of Iabesh Gilead, To morowe by then the sunne be hote, ye shall haue helpe. And the messengers came and shewed it to the men of Iabesh, which were glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:2–3"> Therefore the men of Iabesh sayde, To morowe we will come out vnto you, and yee shall doe with vs all that pleaseth you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 7:16; James 2:13; Exodus 14:24"> And when the morowe was come, Saul put the people in three bandes, and they came in vpon the hoste in the morning watche, and slewe the Ammonites vntill the heate of the day: and they that remained, were scattered, so that two of them were not left together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:27; Luke 19:27; Psalms 21:8"> Then the people said vnto Samuel, Who is he that saide, Shall Saul reigne ouer vs? bring those men that we may slaie them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:22; 1 Samuel 19:5; Exodus 14:13"> But Saul said, There shall no man die this day: for to day the Lord hath saued Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:8; 1 Samuel 7:16; 1 Chronicles 12:38–39"> Then saide Samuel vnto ye people, Come, that we may goe to Gilgal, and renue the kingdome there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:8; 1 Samuel 10:17; 1 Chronicles 29:21–24"> So all the people went to Gilgal, and made Saul King there before the Lord in Gilgal: and there they offered peace offerings before the Lord: and there Saul and all the men of Israel reioyced exceedingly.</span>
