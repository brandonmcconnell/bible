<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:19; Psalms 54:1–7; Joshua 15:24"> Againe the Ziphims came vnto Saul to Gibeah, saying, Doeth not Dauid hide him selfe in the hill of Hachilah before Ieshimon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:2; 1 Samuel 13:2; Psalms 140:4–9"> Then Saul arose, and went downe to the wildernes of Ziph, hauing three thousand chosen men of Israel with him, for to seeke Dauid in the wildernesse of Ziph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:19; 1 Samuel 26:1"> And Saul pitched in the hill of Hachilah, which is before Ieshimon by the way side. Now Dauid abode in the wildernesse, and he sawe that Saul came after him into the wildernesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; Joshua 2:1"> (For Dauid had sent out spies, and vnderstood, that Saul was come in very deede)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:55; 1 Samuel 17:20; 1 Samuel 14:50–51"> Then Dauid arose, and came to the place where Saul had pitched, and when Dauid beheld the place where Saul lay, and Abner the sonne of Ner which was his chiefe captaine, (for Saul lay in the fort, and the people pitched round about him)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 7:10–11; 2 Samuel 2:18; Genesis 15:20"> Then spake Dauid, and said to Ahimelech the Hittite, and to Abishai the sonne of Zeruiah, brother to Ioab, saying, Who will go downe with me to Saul to the hoste? Then Abishai said, I will goe downe with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–3"> So Dauid and Abishai came downe to the people by night: and beholde, Saul lay sleeping within the fort, and his speare did sticke in the ground at his head: and Abner and the people lay round about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:4; 1 Samuel 26:23; Deuteronomy 32:30"> Then saide Abishai to Dauid, God hath closed thine enemie into thine hande this day: now therefore, I pray thee, let me smite him once with a speare to the earth, and I will not smite him againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:14; Psalms 105:15; 2 Samuel 1:16"> And Dauid sayde to Abishai, Destroy him not: for who can lay his hand on the Lordes anoynted, and be giltlesse?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 47:29; Deuteronomy 31:14; Psalms 37:13"> Moreouer Dauid said, As the Lord liueth, eyther the Lord shall smite him, or his day shall come to dye, or he shall descend into battel, and perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:6; 1 Samuel 24:12; 2 Samuel 1:14"> The Lord keepe mee from laying mine hand vpon the Lordes anointed: but, I pray thee, take now the speare that is at his head, and the pot of water, and let vs goe hence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 2:21; Genesis 15:12; Isaiah 29:10"> So Dauid tooke the speare and the pot of water from Sauls head, and they gate them away, and no man saw it, nor marked it, neither did any awake, but they were all asleepe: for the Lord had sent a dead sleepe vpon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:8; Judges 9:7"> Then Dauid went vnto the other side, and stoode on the toppe of an hill a farre off, a great space being betweene them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Dauid cryed to the people, and to Abner the sonne of Ner, saying, Hearest thou not, Abner? Then Abner answered, and said, Who art thou that cryest to the King?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Dauid said to Abner, Art not thou a man? and who is like thee in Israel? wherefore then hast thou not kept thy lorde the King? for there came one of the folke in to destroy the King thy lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 79:11; 2 Samuel 12:5; 1 Samuel 26:9"> This is not well done of thee: as the Lord liueth, ye are worthy to dye, because ye haue not kept your master the Lordes Anointed: and now see where the Kings speare is, and the pot of water that was at his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:16; 1 Samuel 24:8"> And Saul knewe Dauids voyce, and sayde, Is this thy voyce, my sonne Dauid? And Dauid sayde, It is my voyce, my lorde O King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:9; 1 Samuel 24:11–14; John 10:32"> And he sayde, Wherefore doeth my lorde thus persecute his seruant? for what haue I done? or what euill is in mine hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:16; 2 Samuel 16:11; 2 Samuel 20:19"> Now therefore, I beseech thee, let my lord the King heare the wordes of his seruant. If the Lord haue stirred thee vp against me, let him smell the sauour of a sacrifice: but if the children of men haue done it, cursed be they before the Lord: for they haue cast me out this day from abiding in the inheritance of the Lord, saying, Goe, serue other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:14; Matthew 26:55; 1 Samuel 25:29"> Nowe therefore let not my blood fall to the earth before the face of the Lord: for the King of Israel is come out to seeke a flea, as one would hunt a partridge in the mountaines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:24; 1 Samuel 24:17; Exodus 9:27"> Then sayde Saul, I haue sinned: come againe, my sonne Dauid: for I will doe thee no more harme, because my soule was precious in thine eyes this day: behold, I haue done foolishly, and haue erred exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then Dauid answered, and saide, Beholde the Kings speare, let one of the yong men come ouer and set it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 62:12; 1 Samuel 24:6–7; Psalms 18:20–26"> And let the Lord rewarde euery man according to his righteousnesse and faithfulnesse: for the Lord had deliuered thee into mine handes this day, but I woulde not lay mine hand vpon the Lords anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10; Psalms 54:7"> And beholde, like as thy life was much set by this day in mine eyes: so let my life be set by in the eyes of the Lord, that he may deliuer me out of all tribulation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 24:9–10; Proverbs 26:25; 1 Samuel 24:19"> Then Saul said to Dauid, Blessed art thou, my sonne Dauid: for thou shalt doe great things, and also preuaile. So Dauid went his way, and Saul returned to his place.</span>
