<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; 2 Chronicles 28:18; 1 Chronicles 11:13"> Nowe the Philistims gathered their armies to battell, and came together to Shochoh, which is in Iudah, and pitched betweene Shochoh and Azekah, in the coast of Dammim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:9; 1 Samuel 17:19"> And Saul, and the men of Israel assembled, and pitched in the valley of Elah, and put themselues in battell araie to meete the Philistims.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And the Philistims stoode on a mountaine on the one side, and Israel stoode on a mountaine on the other side: so a valley was betweene them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:22; 2 Samuel 21:16–22; 1 Chronicles 20:4–8"> Then came a man betweene them both out of the tents of the Philistims, named Goliath of Gath: his height was sixe cubites and an hande breadth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Aud had an helmet of brasse vpon his head, and a brigandine vpon him: and the weight of his brigandine was fiue thousand shekels of brasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:45; 2 Chronicles 9:15; 1 Kings 10:16"> And he had bootes of brasse vpon his legs, and a shield of brasse vpon his shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:19; 1 Samuel 17:41; 1 Chronicles 11:23"> And the shaft of his speare was like a weauers beame: and his speare head weyed sixe hundreth shekels of yron: and one bearing a shielde went before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:17; 1 Chronicles 21:3; 1 Samuel 17:26"> And he stoode, and cried against the hoste of Israel, and saide vnto them, Why are yee come to set your battell in aray? am not I a Philistim, and you seruaunts to Saul? chuse you a man for you, and let him come downe to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1"> If he be able to fight with me, and kill me, then wil we be your seruants: but if I ouercome him, and kill him, then shall yee be our seruants, and serue vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:25–26; 1 Samuel 17:45; 1 Samuel 17:36"> Also the Philistim saide, I defie the hoste of Israel this day: giue mee a man, that we may fight together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Deuteronomy 31:8; Proverbs 28:1"> When Saul and all Israel heard those wordes of the Philistim, they were discouraged, and greatly afraide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:10–11; Genesis 35:19; 1 Samuel 16:18"> Nowe this Dauid was the sonne of an Ephrathite of Beth-lehem Iudah, named Ishai, which had eight sonnes: and this man was taken for an olde man in the daies of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13; 2 Samuel 21:21; 1 Samuel 17:28"> And the three eldest sonnes of Ishai went and followed Saul to the battel: and the names of his three sonnes that went to battell, were Eliab the Eldest, and the next Abinadab, and the thirde Shammah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:11; Genesis 25:23"> So Dauid was the least: and the three eldest went after Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:11; 1 Samuel 16:19–23"> Dauid also went, but hee returned from Saul to feede his fathers sheepe in Beth-lehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 4:2; Luke 4:2"> And the Philistim drew neere in the morning, and euening, and continued fourtie daies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Samuel 17:28; Ruth 2:14"> And Ishai said vnto Dauid his sone, Take nowe for thy brethren an Ephah of this parched corne, and these ten cakes, and runne to the hoste to thy brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 37:14; 1 Samuel 16:20; 2 Samuel 17:29"> Also carie these ten fresh cheeses vnto the captaine, and looke howe thy brethren fare, and receiue their pledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> (Then Saul and they, and all the men of Israel were in the valley of Elah, fighting with the Philistims)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:5; Luke 19:43; Ephesians 6:1–2"> So Dauid rose vp earely in the morning, and left the sheepe with a keeper, and tooke and went as Ishai had commanded him, and came within the compasse of the hoste: and the hoste went out in araie, and shouted in the battell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For Israel and the Philistims had put themselues in araie, armie against armie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 10:5–6; Judges 18:15; Isaiah 10:28"> And Dauid left the things, which hee bare, vnder the handes of the keeper of the cariage, and ranne into the hoste, and came, and asked his brethren howe they did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:8–10"> And as hee talked with them, beholde, the man that was betweene the two armies, came vp, (whose name was Goliath ye Philistim of Gath) out of the armie of the Philistims, and spake such woordes, and Dauid heard them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:6–7; Leviticus 26:36; Isaiah 7:2"> And all the men of Israel, when they sawe the man, ranne away from him, and were sore afraied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 15:16; Revelation 2:7; Revelation 3:12"> For euery man of Israel saide, Sawe yee not this man that commeth vp? euen to reuile Israel is he come vp: and to him that killeth him, wil the king giue great riches, and will giue him his daughter, yea, and make his fathers house free in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:26; 1 Samuel 11:2; 1 Samuel 14:6"> Then Dauid spake to the men that stoode with him, and sayde, What shall be done to the man that killeth this Philistim, and taketh away the shame from Israel? for who is this vncircumcised Philistim, that he shoulde reuile the hoste of the liuing God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:25"> And the people answered him after this maner, saying, Thus shall it be done to the man that killeth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 10:36; Genesis 37:11; Proverbs 18:19"> And Eliab his eldest brother heard when he spake vnto the men, and Eliab was verie angrie with Dauid, and sayde, Why camest thou downe hither? and with whome hast thou left those fewe sheepe in the wildernesse? I knowe thy pride and the malice of thine heart, that thou art come downe to see the battell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Proverbs 15:1"> Then Dauid sayde, What haue I nowe done? Is there not a cause?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:26–27"> And hee departed from him into the presence of another, and spake of the same maner, and the people answered him according to the former woordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29"> And they that heard the wordes which Dauid spake, rehearsed them before Saul, which caused him to be brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:18; Deuteronomy 20:1–3; Psalms 3:6"> So Dauid saide to Saul, Let no mans heart faile him, because of him: thy seruant wil goe, and fight with this Philistim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 13:31; Deuteronomy 9:2; Revelation 13:4"> And Saul sayde to Dauid, Thou art not able to goe against this Philistim to fight with him: for thou art a boye, and he is a man of warre from his youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And Dauid answered vnto Saul, Thy seruant kept his fathers sheepe, and there came a lyon, and likewise a beare, and tooke a sheepe out of the flocke,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Amos 3:12; 2 Timothy 4:17–18; Daniel 6:22"> And I went out after him and smote him, and tooke it out of his mouth: and when he arose against me, I caught him by the beard, and smote him, and slue him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:10; Acts 5:38–39; Romans 2:28–29"> So thy seruaunt slue both the lyon, and the beare: therefore this vncircumcised Philistim shall be as one of them, seeing hee hath railed on the hoste of the liuing God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17–18; Psalms 138:7–8; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> Moreouer Dauid sayd, The Lord that deliuered me out of the pawe of the lyon, and out of the paw of the beare, he wil deliuer me out of the hand of this Philistim. Then Saul sayd vnto Dauid, Go, and the Lord be with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And Saul put his rayment vpon Dauid, and put an helmet of brasse vpon his head, and put a brigandine vpon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Hosea 1:7"> Then girded Dauid his sword vpon his rayment, and began to go: for he neuer proued it: and Dauid sayde vnto Saul, I can not goe with these: for I am not accustomed. wherefore Dauid put them off him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27–29; Matthew 10:10; Judges 15:15–16"> Then tooke he his staffe in his hand, and chose him fiue smoothe stones out of a brooke, and put them in his shepheards bagge or skrippe, and his sling was in his hand, and he drewe neere to the Philistim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And the Philistim came and drew neere vnto Dauid, and the man that bare the shielde went before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12; 1 Samuel 17:33; Psalms 123:3–4"> Now when the Philistim looked about and saw Dauid, he disdeined him: for he was but yong, ruddie, and of a comely face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:8; 2 Samuel 3:8; 1 Samuel 24:14"> And the Philistim sayde vnto Dauid, Am I a dog, that thou commest to me with staues? And the Philistim cursed Dauid by his gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:9–10; Proverbs 18:12; 1 Kings 20:10–11"> And the Philistim sayd to Dauid, Come to me, and I will giue thy flesh vnto the foules of the heauen, and to the beastes of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; 2 Chronicles 32:8; 2 Corinthians 3:5"> Then sayd Dauid to the Philistim, Thou commest to me with a sword, and with a speare, and with a shield, but I come to thee in the Name of the Lord of hostes, the God of the hoste of Israel, whom thou hast rayled vpon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 52:10; Joshua 4:24; 2 Kings 19:19"> This day shall the Lord close thee in mine hand, and I shall smite thee, and take thine head from thee, and I wil giue the carkeises of the hoste of the Philistims this daye vnto the foules of the heauen, and to the beasts of the earth, that all the world may know that Israel hath a God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 44:6–7; Zechariah 4:6; Hosea 1:7"> And that all this assembly may know, that the Lord saueth not with sworde nor with speare (for the battel is the Lords) and he will giue you into our handes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Psalms 27:1"> And when the Philistim arose to come and drawe neere vnto Dauid, Dauid hasted and ran to fight against the Philistim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27–28; 2 Kings 9:24; 1 Kings 22:34"> And Dauid put his hande in his bagge, and tooke out a stone, and slang it, and smote the Philistim in his forehead, that the stone sticked in his forehead, and he fell groueling to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Judges 15:15; 1 Samuel 17:39; 1 Samuel 23:21"> So Dauid ouercame the Philistim with a sling and with a stone, and smote the Philistim, and slew him, when Dauid had no sword in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Hebrews 11:34; 1 Samuel 21:9; 2 Samuel 23:21"> Then Dauid ranne, and stood vpon the Philistim, and tooke his sword and drew it out of his sheath, and slewe him, and cut off his head therewith. So whe the Philistims saw, that their champion was dead, they fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Joshua 15:11; Joshua 15:33–36; Joshua 15:45–46"> And the men of Israel and Iudah arose, and shouted, and followed after the Philistims, vntill they came to the valley, and vnto the gates of Ekron: and the Philistims fell downe wounded by the way of Shaaraim, euen to Gath and to Ekron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; 2 Kings 7:7–16; Jeremiah 30:16"> And the children of Israel returned from pursuing the Philistims, and spoyled their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 16:33; Joshua 4:7–8; 1 Samuel 21:9"> And Dauid tooke the head of ye Philistim, and brought it to Ierusalem, and put his armour in his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:21–22; 1 Samuel 17:58"> When Saul sawe Dauid go forth against the Philistim, he sayd vnto Abner the captaine of his hoste, Abner, whose sonne is this yong man? and Abner answered, As thy soule liueth, O King, I can not tell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Then the King sayde, Enquire thou whose sonne this yong man is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:54"> And when Dauid was returned from the slaughter of the Philistim, then Abner tooke him, and brought him before Saul with the head of the Philistim in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:12; 1 Samuel 16:18–19"> And Saul sayde to him, Whose sonne art thou, thou yong man? And Dauid answered, I am the sonne of thy seruant Ishai the Bethlehemite.</span>
