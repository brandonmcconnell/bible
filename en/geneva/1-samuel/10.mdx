<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:13; Psalms 78:71; 1 Samuel 9:16"> Then Samuel tooke a viole of oyle and powred it vpon his head, and kissed him, and saide, Hath not the Lord anointed thee to be gouernour ouer his inheritance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:3–5; Genesis 35:19–20; Joshua 18:28"> When thou shalt depart from me this day, thou shalt finde two men by Rahels sepulchre in the border of Beniamin, euen at Zelzah, and they will say vnto thee, The asses which thou wentest to seeke, are founde: and lo, thy father hath left the care of the asses, and soroweth for you, saying, What shall I doe for my sonne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 35:3; Genesis 35:1; Genesis 28:22"> Then shalt thou go forth from thence and shalt come to the plaine of Tabor, and there shall meete thee three men going vp to God to Bethel: one carying three kiddes, and an other carying three loaues of bread, and another carying a bottle of wine:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 18:15"> And they will aske thee if all be well, and will giue thee the two loaues of bread, which thou shalt receiue of their handes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 3:15; 1 Samuel 13:3; 1 Samuel 19:20"> After that shalt thou come to the hill of God, where is the garisons of the Philistims: and when thou art come thither to the citie, thou shalt meete a companie of Prophets comming downe from the hie place with a viole, and a tymbrell, and a pipe, and an harpe before them, and they shall prophecie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:10; 1 Samuel 19:23–24; Numbers 11:25"> Then the Spirit of the Lord will come vpon thee, and thou shalt prophecie with them, and shalt be turned into another man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 6:12; Joshua 1:5; Isaiah 7:14"> Therefore when these signes shall come vnto to thee, doe as occasion shall serue: for God is with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:14–15; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Samuel 13:8–15"> And thou shalt goe downe before me to Gilgal: and I also will come downe vnto thee to offer burnt offerings, and to sacrifice sacrifices of peace. Tary for me seuen dayes, till I come to thee and shewe thee what thou shalt doe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 38:7–8; 1 Samuel 10:2–6; Judges 7:11"> And when he had turned his backe to goe from Samuel, God gaue him another heart: and all those tokens came to passe that same day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5–6; 1 Samuel 19:20–24"> And when they came thither to the hill, beholde, the companie of Prophets meete him, and the Spirit of God came vpon him, and he prophecied among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:24; John 7:15; Matthew 13:54–55"> Therefore all the people that knewe him before, when they saw that he prophecied among the Prophets, saide eche to other, What is come vnto the sonne of Kish? is Saul also among the Prophets?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 54:13; John 6:45; James 1:17"> And one of the same place answered, and said, But who is their father? Therefore it was a prouerbe, Is Saul also among the Prophets?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And when he had made an ende of prophecying, he came to the hie place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:50; 2 Kings 5:25; 1 Samuel 9:3–10"> And Sauls vncle saide vnto him, and to his seruant, Whither went ye? And he saide, To seeke the asses: and when we sawe that they were no where, we came to Samuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Sauls vncle saide, Tell me, I pray thee, what Samuel said vnto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:11; Judges 14:6; 1 Samuel 9:20"> Then Saul saide to his vncle, He tolde vs plainely that the asses were founde: but concerning the kingdome whereof Samuel spake, tolde he him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 1 Samuel 7:5–6"> And Samuel assembled the people vnto the Lord in Mizpeh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 6:8–9; Nehemiah 9:27–28; Judges 2:1"> And he saide vnto the children of Israel, Thus saith ye Lord God of Israel, I haue brought Israel out of Egypt, and deliuered you out of the hand of the Egyptians, and out of the handes of all kingdomes that troubled you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:12; 1 Samuel 8:19; Joshua 24:1"> But ye haue this day cast away your God, who onely deliuereth you out of all your aduersities and tribulations: and ye said vnto him, No, but appoint a King ouer vs. Nowe therefore stand ye before the Lord according to your tribes, and according to your thousands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 7:16–18; Acts 1:24–26; 1 Samuel 14:41"> And when Samuel had gathered together all the tribes of Israel, the tribe of Beniamin was taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Afterwarde he assembled the tribe of Beniamin according to their families, and the familie of Matri was taken. So Saul the sonne of Kish was taken, and when they sought him, he coulde not be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 27:21; Judges 20:18; 1 Samuel 23:2–4"> Therefore they asked the Lord againe, if that man should yet come thither. And the Lord answered, Beholde, he hath hid himselfe among the stuffe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:2; 1 Samuel 16:7; 1 Samuel 17:4"> And they ranne, and brought him thence: and when he stoode among the people, he was hier then any of the people from the shoulders vpwarde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 1:25; 1 Kings 1:39; 2 Samuel 21:6"> And Samuel saide to all the people, See ye not him, whom the Lord hath chosen, that there is none like him among all the people? and all the people shouted and saide, God saue the King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11–18; Deuteronomy 17:14–20; Romans 13:1–7"> Then Samuel tolde the people the duetie of the kingdome, and wrote it in a booke, and laied it vp before the Lord, and Samuel sent all the people away euery man to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:4; Joshua 18:28; 1 Samuel 15:34"> Saul also went home to Gibeah, and there followed him a bande of men, whose heart God had touched,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:13; 2 Chronicles 17:5; 1 Kings 10:25"> But the wicked men saide, Howe shall hee saue vs? So they despised him, and brought him no presents: but he held his tongue.</span>
