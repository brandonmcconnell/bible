<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:17; Proverbs 18:24; 1 Samuel 18:3"> And when he had made an ende of speaking vnto Saul, the soule of Ionathan was knit with the soule of Dauid, and Ionathan loued him, as his owne soule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:15; 1 Samuel 16:21–23"> And Saul tooke him that day, and woulde not let him returne to his fathers house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:8–17; 1 Samuel 20:42; 2 Samuel 21:7"> Then Ionathan and Dauid made a couenant: for he loued him as his owne soule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 41:42; Luke 15:22; Esther 6:8–9"> And Ionathan put off the robe that was vpon him, and gaue it Dauid, and his garments, euen to his sword, and to his bow, and to his girdle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:30; 1 Samuel 18:14–15; Colossians 4:5"> And Dauid went out whithersoeuer Saul sent him, and behaued himselfe wisely: so that Saul set him ouer the men of warre, and he was accepted in the sight of all the people, and also in the sight of Sauls seruants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Judges 11:34; Psalms 68:25"> When they came againe, and Dauid returned from the slaughter of the Philistim, the women came out of all cities of Israel singing and dauncing to meete king Saul, with timbrels, with instruments of ioy, and with rebeckes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:11; 1 Samuel 29:5; Exodus 15:21"> And the women sang by course in their play, and sayd, Saul hath slayne his thousand, and Dauid his ten thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:28; Proverbs 13:10; James 4:5"> Therefore Saul was exceeding wroth, and the saying displeased him, and he sayde, They haue ascribed vnto Dauid ten thousand, and to me they haue ascribed but a thousand, and what can he haue more saue the kingdome?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 7:22; Genesis 31:2; Matthew 20:15"> Wherefore Saul had an eye on Dauid from that day forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:9; 2 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Kings 18:29"> And on the morowe, the euill spirite of God came vpon Saul, and he prophecied in the middes of the house: and Dauid played with his hand like as at other times, and there was a speare in Sauls hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:33; Isaiah 54:17; Luke 4:30"> And Saul tooke the speare, and sayd, I will smite Dauid through to the wall. But Dauid auoyded twise out of his presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:15; 1 Samuel 18:29; 1 Samuel 16:13–14"> And Saul was afrayd of Dauid, because the Lord was with him, and was departed from Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:2; Psalms 121:8; 1 Samuel 18:16–17"> Therefore Saul put him from him, and made him a captaine ouer a thousand, and he went out and in before the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 39:23; Matthew 28:20; 1 Samuel 16:18"> And Dauid behaued himselfe wisely in all his wayes: for the Lord was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:17; Colossians 4:5; James 1:5"> Wherefore when Saul saw that he was very wise, he was afrayde of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:5; Numbers 27:17; 1 Kings 3:7"> For all Israel and Iudah loued Dauid, because he went out and in before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:25; 1 Samuel 25:28; 1 Samuel 18:25"> Then Saul sayd to Dauid, Beholde mine eldest daughter Merab, her I will giue thee to wife: onely be a valiant sonne vnto me, and fight the Lordes battels: for Saul thought, Mine hand shall not be vpon him, but the hand of the Philistims shalbe vpon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:18; 1 Samuel 9:21; 1 Samuel 18:23"> And Dauid answered Saul, What am I? and what is my life, or the family of my father in Israel, that I should be sonne in law to the King?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 2 Samuel 21:8; Judges 14:20"> Howbeit when Merab Sauls daughter should haue bene giuen to Dauid, she was giuen vnto Adriel a Meholathite to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:28"> Then Michal Sauls daughter loued Dauid: and they shewed Saul, and the thing pleased him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:17; Exodus 10:7; Jeremiah 9:8"> Therefore Saul said, I wil giue him her, that she may be a snare to him, and that the hand of the Philistims may bee against him. Wherefore Saul sayde to Dauid, Thou shalt this day be my sonne in law in the one of the twayne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 36:1–3; Psalms 55:21; 2 Samuel 13:28–29"> And Saul commanded his seruants, Speake with Dauid secretly, and say, Behold, ye King hath a fauour to thee, and all his seruants loue thee: be now therefore the Kings sonne in law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:141; Proverbs 14:20; Ecclesiastes 9:15–16"> And Sauls seruantes spake these wordes in the eares of Dauid. And Dauid sayd, Seemeth it to you a light thing to be a Kings sonne in lawe, seeing that I am a poore man and of small reputation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And then Sauls seruats brought him word againe, saying, Such wordes spake Dauid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 34:12; 1 Samuel 18:17; 1 Samuel 14:24"> And Saul sayd, This wise shall ye say to Dauid, The King desireth no dowrie, but an hundred foreskinnes of the Philistims, to bee auenged of the Kings enemies: for Saul thought to make Dauid fall into the handes of the Philistims.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:21"> And when his seruantes tolde Dauid these wordes, it pleased Dauid well, to be the Kings sonne in law: and the dayes were not expired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:14; Judges 14:19; 1 Samuel 18:13"> Afterwarde Dauid arose with his men, and went and slewe of the Philistims two hundreth men: and Dauid brought their foreskinnes, and they gaue them wholly to the King that hee might be the Kings sonne in lawe: therefore Saul gaue him Michal his daughter to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:20; 1 Samuel 26:25; Genesis 37:8–11"> Then Saul sawe, and vnderstoode that the Lord was with Dauid, and that Michal the daughter of Saul loued him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:4; John 11:53; James 2:19"> Then Saul was more and more afrayde of Dauid, and Saul became alway Dauids enemie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:5; 2 Samuel 11:1; 1 Peter 2:4"> And when the Princes of the Philistims went forth, at their going forth Dauid behaued himselfe more wisely then all the seruants of Saul, so that his name was much set by.</span>
