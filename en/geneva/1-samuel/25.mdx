<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:3; Numbers 20:29; Deuteronomy 34:8"> Then Samuel dyed, and all Israel assembled, and mourned for him, and buried him in his owne house at Ramah. And Dauid arose and went downe to the wildernes of Paran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 38:13; 1 Samuel 23:24; Joshua 15:55"> Now in Maon was a man, who had his possessio in Carmel, and the man was exceeding mightie and had three thousand sheepe, and a thousand goates: and he was shering his sheepe in Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; Proverbs 31:10; Isaiah 32:5–7"> The name also of the man was Nabal, and the name of his wife Abigail, and she was a woman of singular wisdome, and beautifull, but the man was churlish, and euil conditioned, and was of the familie of Caleb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 38:13; 2 Samuel 13:23"> And Dauid heard in the wildernesse, that Nabal did shere his sheepe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 43:23; 1 Samuel 17:22"> Therefore Dauid sent tenne yong men, and Dauid said vnto the yong men, Go vp to Carmel, and go to Nabal, and aske him in my name how he doeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 10:5; 1 Chronicles 12:18; Psalms 122:7"> And thus shall ye say for salutation, Both thou, and thine house, and all that thou hast, be in peace, wealth and prosperitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:21; 1 Samuel 25:15–16; 1 Samuel 22:2"> Behold, I haue heard, that thou hast sherers: now thy shepherds were with vs, and we did the no hurt, neyther did they misse any thing all the while they were in Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esther 9:19; Nehemiah 8:10–12; Luke 14:12–14"> Aske thy seruants and they wil shew thee. Wherefore let these yong men finde fauour in thine eyes: (for we come in a good season) giue, I pray thee, whatsoeuer commeth to thine hand vnto thy seruants, and to thy sonne Dauid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 2:15; 2 Chronicles 14:7; Genesis 8:4"> And when Dauids yong men came, they tolde Nabal all those wordes in the name of Dauid, and helde their peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 9:28; Isaiah 32:7; 1 Samuel 22:7–8"> Then Nabal answered Dauids seruantes, and sayd, Who is Dauid? and who is the sonne of Ishai? there be many seruantes nowe a dayes, that breake away euery man from his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 8:6; Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 6:9"> Shall I then take my bread, and my water, and my flesh that I haue killed for my sherers, and giue it vnto men, whom I know not whence they be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 36:21–22; 2 Samuel 24:13; Hebrews 13:17"> So Dauids seruants turned their way, and went againe, and came, and tolde him all those things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:13; Proverbs 25:8; 1 Samuel 24:5–6"> And Dauid said vnto his men, Girde euery man his sword about him. And they girded euery man his sworde: Dauid also girded his sworde. And about foure hundreth men went vp after Dauid, and two hundreth abode by the cariage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:10; Mark 15:29"> Nowe one of the seruantes tolde Abigail Nabals wife, saying, Beholde, Dauid sent messengers out of the wildernesse to salute our master, and he rayled on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:7; 1 Samuel 25:21; Philippians 2:15"> Notwithstanding the men were very good vnto vs, and we had no displeasure, neither missed we any thing as long as we were conuersant with them, when we were in the fieldes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 1:10; Exodus 14:22; Zechariah 2:5"> They were as a wall vnto vs both by night and by day, all the while we were with them keeping sheepe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:7; Deuteronomy 13:13; 1 Samuel 20:9"> Nowe therefore take heede, and see what thou shalt doe: for euill will surely come vpon our master, and vpon all his familie: for he is so wicked that a man can not speake to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:1; Matthew 5:25; Proverbs 6:4–5"> Then Abigail made haste, and tooke two hundreth cakes, and two bottels of wine, and fiue sheepe ready dressed, and fiue measures of parched corne, and an hundreth frailes of raisins, and two hundreth of figs, and laded them on asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 32:20; Genesis 32:16; Proverbs 31:11–12"> Then she said vnto her seruants, Go ye before me: beholde, I will come after you: yet she tolde not her husband Nabal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 4:24"> And as shee rode on her asse, shee came downe by a secret place of the mountaine, and beholde, Dauid and his men came downe against her, and she met them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 17:13; Ephesians 4:31; Romans 12:21"> And Dauid said, In deede I haue kept all in vaine that this fellow had in the wildernesse, so that nothing was missed of all that pertained vnto him: for he hath requited me euill for good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 14:10; 1 Samuel 20:13; 1 Samuel 3:17"> So and more also doe God vnto the enemies of Dauid: for surely I will not leaue of all that he hath, by the dawning of the day, any that pisseth against the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:41; Judges 1:14; Joshua 15:18"> And when Abigail sawe Dauid, she hasted and lighted off her asse, and fell before Dauid on her face, and bowed her selfe to the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esther 8:3; 1 Samuel 25:28; 2 Samuel 14:9"> And fel at his feete, and sayd, Oh, my lord, I haue committed the iniquitie, and I pray thee, let thine handmayde speake to thee, and heare thou the wordes of thine handmayde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:17; Malachi 2:2; 2 Samuel 13:33"> Let not my lorde, I pray thee, regard this wicked man Nabal: for as his name is, so is he: Nabal is his name, and follie is with him: but I thine handmayde sawe not the yong men of my lord whom thou sentest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:32; Romans 12:19–20; Genesis 20:6"> Now therefore my lord, as the Lord liueth, and as thy soule liueth (the Lord, I say, that hath withholden thee from comming to shedde blood, and that thine hand should not saue thee) so now thine enemies shall be as Nabal, and they that intend to doe my lord euill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 33:11; 1 Samuel 30:26; 2 Kings 5:15"> And now, this blessing which thine handmaid hath brought vnto my lorde, let it be giuen vnto the yong men, that follow my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:17; 2 Samuel 7:11; 1 Samuel 24:11"> I pray thee, forgiue the trespasse of thine handmaide: for the Lord will make my lorde a sure house, because my lord fighteth the battels of the Lord, and none euill hath bene found in thee in all thy life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 17:21; Matthew 10:29–30; Malachi 3:17"> Yet a man hath risen vp to persecute thee, and to seeke thy soule, but the soule of my lorde shall be bounde in the bundel of life with the Lord thy God: and the soule of thine enemies shall God cast out, as out of the middle of a sling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:14; Psalms 89:20; 1 Samuel 23:17"> And when the Lord shall haue done to my lord al the good that he hath promised thee, and shall haue made thee ruler ouer Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 40:14; Romans 12:19; Luke 23:42"> Then shall it be no griefe vnto thee, nor offence of minde vnto my lord, that he hath not shed blood causelesse, nor that my lorde hath not preserued him selfe: and when the Lord shall haue dealt well with my lorde, remember thine handmaide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 1:68; Exodus 18:10; Psalms 72:18"> Then Dauid said to Abigail, Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, which sent thee this day to meete me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:26; Proverbs 28:23; Proverbs 9:9"> And blessed be thy counsel, and blessed be thou, which hast kept me this day from comming to shed blood, and that mine hand hath not saued me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:11; 1 Samuel 25:26; Joshua 10:6"> For in deede, as the Lord God of Israel liueth, who hath kept me backe from hurting thee, except thou haddest hasted and met mee, surely there had not bene left vnto Nabal by the dawning of the day, any that pisseth against the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 19:21; 1 Samuel 20:42; 2 Kings 5:19"> Then Dauid receiued of her hande that which she had brought him, and said to her, Goe vp in peace to thine house: beholde, I haue heard thy voyce, and haue graunted thy petition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:19; 2 Samuel 13:23; 2 Samuel 13:28"> So Abigail came to Nabal, and behold, he made a feast in his house, like the feast of a King, and Nabals heart was merie within him, for he was very drunken: wherefore shee tolde him nothing, neither lesse nor more, vntil the morning arose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:28; 1 Samuel 25:34; 1 Samuel 25:22"> Then in the morning when the wine was gone out of Nabal, his wife tolde him those wordes, and his heart died within him, and he was like a stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:10; 2 Kings 15:5; 1 Samuel 6:9"> And about ten dayes after, the Lord smote Nabal, that he dyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:26; 1 Samuel 25:34; Proverbs 22:23"> Now when Dauid heard, that Nabal was dead, he said, Blessed be the Lord that hath iudged the cause of my rebuke of ye hand of Nabal, and hath kept his seruant from euil: for the Lord hath recompensed the wickednesse of Nabal vpon his owne head. Also Dauid sent to commune with Abigail to take her to his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 24:51; Genesis 24:37–38"> And whe the seruants of Dauid were come to Abigail to Carmel, they spake vnto her, saying, Dauid sent vs to thee, to take thee to his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; John 13:3–5; Ruth 2:10"> And she arose, and bowed her selfe on her face to the earth, and said, Behold, let thine handmayde be a seruant to wash the feete of the seruants of my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 24:61–67; Psalms 45:10–11"> And Abigail hasted, and arose, and rode vpon an asse, and her fiue maides folowed her, and she went after the messengers of Dauid, and was his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:5; Joshua 15:56; 1 Samuel 27:3"> Dauid also tooke Ahinoam of Izreel, and they were both his wiues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:14; 1 Samuel 18:27; 1 Samuel 18:20"> Now Saul had giuen Michal his daughter Dauids wife to Phalti the sonne of Laish, which was of Gallim.</span>
