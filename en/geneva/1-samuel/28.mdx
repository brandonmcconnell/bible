<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:1; 1 Samuel 29:1–3; 1 Samuel 7:7"> Nowe at that time the Philistims assembled their bandes and armie to fight with Israel: therfore Achish said to Dauid, Be sure, thou shalt go out with me to the battel, thou, and thy men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 12:9; 2 Samuel 16:16–19; 1 Samuel 27:10"> And Dauid said to Achish, Surely thou shalt knowe, what thy seruant can doe. And Achish sayde to Dauid, Surely I will make thee keeper of mine head for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; 1 Samuel 25:1; Leviticus 20:27"> (Samuel was then dead, and all Israel had lamented him, and buried him in Ramah his owne citie: and Saul had put away the sorcerers, and the soothsayers out of the land)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:1; Joshua 19:18; 2 Kings 4:8"> Then the Philistims assembled themselues, and came, and pitched in Shunem: and Saul assembled all Israel, and they pitched in Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 57:20–21; Daniel 5:6; Isaiah 21:3–4"> And when Saul saw the hoste of the Philistims, he was afraid, and his heart was sore astonied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 12:6; 1 Samuel 14:37; Lamentations 2:9"> Therefore Saul asked counsel of the Lord, and the Lord answered him not, neither by dreames, nor by Vrim, nor yet by Prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 16:16; Joshua 17:11; 1 Chronicles 10:13"> Then saide Saul vnto his seruants, Seeke me a woman that hath a familiar spirite, that I may goe to her, and aske of her. And his seruants said to him, Beholde, there is a woman at En-dor that hath a familiar spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; 1 Chronicles 10:13; 1 Kings 22:30"> Then Saul changed him selfe, and put on other raiment, and he went, and two men with him, and they came to the woman by night: and he saide, I pray thee, coniecture vnto me by the familiar spirite, and bring me him vp whome I shall name vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:3; 2 Kings 5:7; 2 Samuel 18:13"> And the woman saide vnto him, Beholde, thou knowest what Saul hath done, how he hath destroyed the sorcerers, and the southsayers out of the land: wherefore then seekest thou to take me in a snare to cause me to die?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:11; 1 Samuel 19:6; Deuteronomy 18:10–12"> And Saul sware to her by the Lord, saying, As the Lord liueth, no harme shall come to thee for this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then said the woman, Whom shall I bring vp vnto thee? And he answered, Bring me vp Samuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:3; 1 Kings 14:5"> And when the woman sawe Samuel, she cryed with a loude voyce, and the woman spake to Saul, saying, Why hast thou deceiued me? for thou art Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 82:6–7; John 10:34–35; Exodus 22:28"> And the King said vnto her, Be not afraid: for what sawest thou? And the woman said vnto Saul, I saw gods ascending vp out of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:27; 2 Kings 2:8; 1 Samuel 24:8"> Then he said vnto her, What facion is hee of? And she answered, An olde man commeth vp lapped in a mantel: and Saul knewe that it was Samuel, and he enclined his face to the grounde, and bowed him selfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 16:23–26; 1 Samuel 28:6; 1 Samuel 18:12"> And Samuel said to Saul, Why hast thou disquieted me, to bring me vp? Then Saul answered, I am in great distresse: for the Philistims make warre against me, and God is departed from me, and answereth me no more, neither by Prophetes, neither by dreames: therefore I haue called thee, that thou mayest tell me, what I shall doe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 6:27; Lamentations 2:5; Psalms 68:1–3"> Then said Samuel, Wherefore then doest thou aske of me, seeing the Lord is gone from thee, and is thine enemie?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:27–29; 1 Samuel 16:13; 1 Samuel 13:13–14"> Euen the Lord hath done to him, as hee spake by mine hande: for the Lord will rent the kingdome out of thine hande, and giue it thy neighbour Dauid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:9; 1 Kings 20:42; Psalms 50:21–22"> Because thou obeyedst not the voyce of the Lord, nor executedst his fierce wrath vpon the Amalekites, therefore hath the Lord done this vnto thee this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 5:5; Jeremiah 28:16–17; 1 Kings 22:20"> Moreouer the Lord wil deliuer Israel with thee into the handes of the Philistims: and to morowe shalt thou and thy sonnes be with me, and the Lord shall giue the hoste of Israel into the handes of the Philistims.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:5; Job 26:2; Job 15:20–24"> Then Saul fell streight way all along on the earth, and was sore afraide because of the wordes of Samuel, so that there was no strength in him: for he had eaten no bread all the day nor all the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 12:3; Job 13:14; 1 Samuel 19:5"> Then the woman came vnto Saul, and sawe that he was sore troubled, and said vnto him, See, thine handmaide hath obeyed thy voyce, and I haue put my soule in mine hande, and haue obeyed thy wordes which thou saydest vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Now therefore, I pray thee, hearke thou also vnto ye voyce of thine handmaid, and let me set a morsell of bread before thee, that thou mayest eat and get thee strength, and go on thy iourney.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 21:4; Luke 24:29; Luke 14:23"> But he refused, and said, I will not eate: but his seruants and the woman together compelled him, and he obeyed their voyce: so he arose from the earth, and sate on the bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 15:23; Genesis 18:7–8"> Nowe the woman had a fat calfe in the house, and she hasted, and killed it, and tooke floure and kneaded it, and baked of it vnleauened bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Then she brought them before Saul, and before his seruants: and when they had eaten, they stoode, and went away the same night.</span>
