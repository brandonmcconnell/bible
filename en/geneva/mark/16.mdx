<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 20:1; Luke 24:1; Matthew 28:1"> Therefore early in the morning, the first day of the weeke, they came vnto the sepulchre, when the Sunne was nowe risen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 15:46–47; Matthew 27:60–66"> And they saide one to another, Who shall rolle vs away the stone from the doore of the sepulchre?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 28:2–4; Luke 24:2; John 20:1"> And when they looked, they saw that the stone was rolled away (for it was a very great one)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 20:11–12; Luke 24:3–5; John 20:8"> So they went into the sepulchre, and saw a yong man sitting at the right side, clothed in a long white robe: and they were sore troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; 1 Corinthians 15:3–7; Luke 24:46"> But he said vnto them, Be not so troubled: ye seeke Iesus of Nazareth, which hath bene crucified: he is risen, he is not here: behold the place where they put him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:28; Matthew 26:32; Matthew 28:10"> But goe your way, and tell his disciples, and Peter, that he will goe before you into Galile: there shall ye see him, as he said vnto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 24:22–24; Luke 24:9–11; Mark 16:5–6"> And they went out quickly, and fled from the sepulchre: for they trembled, and were amased: neither said they any thing to any man: for they were afraide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 20:14–19; Luke 8:2; John 20:12"> And when Iesus was risen againe, early the first day of the weeke, he appeared first to Marie Magdalene, out of whom he had cast seuen deuils:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 20:18; Luke 24:17; John 16:6"> And shee went and tolde them that had bene with him, which mourned and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 24:11; Mark 16:13–14; Mark 9:19"> And when they heard that he was aliue, and had appeared to her, they beleeued it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:13–32; John 21:14; John 21:1"> After that, he appeared vnto two of them in an other forme, as they walked and went into the countrey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:25; Luke 24:33–35; John 20:8"> And they went, and told it to the remnant, neither beleeued they them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5; Luke 24:36–43; Hebrews 3:7–8"> Finally, he appeared vnto the eleuen as they sate together, and reproched them for their vnbeliefe and hardnesse of heart, because they beleeued not them which had seene him, being risen vp againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; Mark 13:10; Acts 1:8"> And he saide vnto them, Goe ye into all the worlde, and preach the Gospel to euery creature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 3:36; Romans 10:9–14"> He that shall beleeue and be baptized, shalbe saued: but he that will not beleeue, shalbe damned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:16; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Acts 8:7"> And these tokens shall folowe them that beleeue, In my Name they shall cast out deuils, and shall speake with newe tongues,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 10:19; James 5:14–15; Acts 28:3–6"> And shall take away serpents, and if they shall drinke any deadly thing, it shall not hurt them: they shall lay their handes on the sicke, and they shall recouer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; Hebrews 1:3; Hebrews 12:2"> So after ye Lord had spoken vnto them, he was receiued into heauen, and sate at the right hand of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4–5; Acts 14:3; Romans 15:19"> And they went foorth, and preached euery where. And the Lord wrought with them, and confirmed the worde with signes that folowed. Amen.</span>
