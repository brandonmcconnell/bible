<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:26–39; Matthew 8:28–34; Mark 4:35"> And they came ouer to the other side of the sea into the countrey of the Gadarens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 1:23; Isaiah 65:4; Mark 5:8"> And when he was come out of the shippe, there met him incontinently out of the graues, a man which had an vncleane spirit:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 65:4; Luke 8:29; Daniel 4:32–33"> Who had his abiding among the graues, and no man could binde him, no not with chaines:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 3:7–8"> Because that when hee was often bounde with fetters and chaines, he plucked the chaines asunder, and brake the fetters in pieces, neither could any man tame him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 2:7–8; 1 Kings 18:28; John 8:44"> And alwayes both night and day he cryed in the mountaines, and in the graues, and strooke himselfe with stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 2:19; Psalms 66:3; Luke 4:41"> And when he saw Iesus afarre off, he ranne, and worshipped him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; Acts 16:17; Matthew 26:63"> And cryed with a loude voyce, and saide, What haue I to doe with thee, Iesus the Sonne of the most high God? I will that thou sweare to me by God, that thou torment me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 1:25; Mark 9:25–26; Acts 16:18"> (For hee saide vnto him, Come out of the man, thou vncleane spirit.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 8:30; Mark 5:15; Matthew 26:53"> And he asked him, What is thy name? and hee answered, saying, My name is Legion: for we are many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Mark 5:13"> And hee prayed him instantly, that hee would not send them away out of the countrey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 11:7–8; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> Now there was there in the mountaines a great heard of swine, feeding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:11; Job 1:10–12; Luke 22:31–32"> And all ye deuils besought him, saying, Send vs into the swine, that we may enter into them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 22:22; 1 Peter 3:22; Job 2:6"> And incontinently Iesus gaue them leaue. Then the vncleane spirites went out, and entred into the swine, and the heard ranne headlong from the high banke into the sea, (and there were about two thousand swine) and they were choked vp in the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 8:34; Matthew 8:33"> And the swineheards fled, and told it in the citie, and in the countrey, and they came out to see what it was that was done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 2 Timothy 1:7; 1 Samuel 6:20–21"> And they came to Iesus, and sawe him that had bene possessed with the deuil, and had the legion, sit both clothed, and in his right minde: and they were afraide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And they that saw it, tolde them, what was done to him that was possessed with the deuil, and concerning the swine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 8:34; Mark 1:24; Luke 8:37"> Then they began to pray him, that hee would depart from their coastes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:38–39; Philippians 1:23–24; Mark 5:17"> And when he was come into the shippe, he that had bene possessed with the deuil, prayed him that he might be with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 66:16; John 4:29; Isaiah 38:9–20"> Howbeit, Iesus would not suffer him, but said vnto him, Goe thy way home to thy friendes, and shewe them what great thinges the Lord hath done vnto thee, and howe hee hath had compassion on thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:31; Matthew 4:25"> So he departed, and began to publish in Decapolis, what great things Iesus had done vnto him: and all men did marueile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 9:1; Mark 4:1; Luke 8:40"> And when Iesus was come ouer againe by ship vnto the other side, a great multitude gathered together to him, and he was neere vnto the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Acts 18:8; Mark 5:35–36"> And beholde, there came one of the rulers of the Synagogue, whose name was Iairus: and when he sawe him, he fell downe at his feete,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 13:13; Luke 4:40; Mark 16:18"> And besought him instantly, saying, My litle daughter lyeth at point of death: I pray thee that thou wouldest come and lay thine hands on her, that she may be healed, and liue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 5:31; Mark 3:20; Luke 7:6"> Then hee went with him, and a great multitude folowed him, and thronged him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:25–27; Luke 8:43–44; Matthew 9:20–22"> (And there was a certaine woman, which was diseased with an issue of blood twelue yeeres,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:12–13; Jeremiah 8:22; Psalms 108:12"> And had suffred many things of many physicions, and had spent all that she had, and it auailed her nothing, but she became much worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 19:12; Matthew 14:36; Acts 5:15"> When she had heard of Iesus, shee came in the preasse behinde, and touched his garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> For she said, If I may but touch his clothes, I shalbe whole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Psalms 30:2; Mark 3:10"> And straightway the course of her blood was dried vp, and she felt in her body, that she was healed of that plague.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 8:46; Luke 5:17"> And immediatly when Iesus did knowe in himselfe the vertue that went out of him, he turned him round about in the preasse, and said, Who hath touched my clothes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:45; Luke 9:12"> And his disciples said vnto him, Thou seest the multitude throng thee, and sayest thou, Who did touche me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And he looked round about, to see her that had done that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:47; Luke 1:29; Luke 1:12"> And the woman feared and trembled: for she knewe what was done in her, and shee came and fell downe before him, and tolde him the whole trueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 8:48; Matthew 9:22; Luke 7:50"> And hee saide to her, Daughter, thy faith hath made thee whole: go in peace, and be whole of thy plague.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 8:49; John 11:39; Mark 5:22"> While hee yet spake, there came from the same ruler of the Synagogues house certaine which said, Thy daughter is dead: why diseasest thou the Master any further?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 8:50; Matthew 9:28–29; John 11:40"> Assoone as Iesus heard that word spoken, he said vnto the ruler of the Synagogue, Be not afraide: onely beleeue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 9:40; Mark 14:33; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> And he suffered no man to follow him saue Peter and Iames, and Iohn the brother of Iames.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:23–24; Luke 8:52–53; Mark 5:22"> So hee came vnto the house of the ruler of the Synagogue, and sawe the tumult, and them that wept and wailed greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 20:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–14"> And he went in, and said vnto them, Why make ye this trouble, and weepe? the childe is not dead, but sleepeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; 2 Kings 4:33; Matthew 9:24–25"> And they laught him to scorne: but hee put them all out, and tooke the father, and the mother of the childe, and them that were with him, and entred in where the childe lay,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Luke 7:14–15; Luke 8:54–55"> And tooke the childe by the hand, and saide vnto her, Talitha cumi, which is by interpretation, Mayden, I say vnto thee, arise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Mark 4:41; Acts 3:10–13; Mark 7:37"> And straightway the mayden arose, and walked: for shee was of the age of twelue yeeres, and they were astonied out of measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Mark 1:43"> And he charged them straitly that no man should knowe of it, and commanded to giue her meate.</span>
