<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 5:11–12; Matthew 4:3; Matthew 3:17"> The beginning of the Gospel of Iesus Christ, the Sonne of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Matthew 11:10; Luke 1:76"> As it is written in the Prophets, Behold, I send my messenger before thy face, which shall prepare thy way before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Matthew 3:3; Luke 3:4–6"> The voyce of him that cryeth in the wildernesse is, Prepare the way of the Lord: make his paths straight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 19:3–4; Matthew 3:6; Acts 13:24–25"> Iohn did baptize in the wildernesse, and preach the baptisme of amendment of life, for remission of sinnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–42; Acts 19:18; Matthew 3:5–6"> And al ye countrey of Iudea, and they of Hierusalem went out vnto him, and were all baptized of him in the riuer Iordan, confessing their sinnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 1:8; Leviticus 11:22; Matthew 3:4"> Nowe Iohn was clothed with camels heare, and with a girdle of a skinne about his loynes: and he did eate Locusts and wilde hony,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 13:25; Luke 3:16; John 1:27"> And preached, saying, A stronger then I commeth after me, whose shoes latchet I am not worthy to stoupe downe, and vnloose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:5; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Acts 19:4–6"> Trueth it is, I haue baptized you with water: but he will baptize you with the holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 3:13–17; Matthew 2:23; Luke 3:21–22"> And it came to passe in those dayes, that Iesus came from Nazareth, a citie of Galile, and was baptized of Iohn in Iordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:31–34; Luke 3:22; Isaiah 42:1"> And assoone as he was come out of the water, Iohn saw the heauens clouen in twaine, and the holy Ghost descending vpon him like a doue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Mark 9:7; Matthew 3:17"> Then there was a voyce from heauen, saying, Thou art my beloued Sonne, in whome I am well pleased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 4:1–11; Luke 4:1–13"> And immediatly the Spirite driueth him into the wildernesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17–18; Hebrews 4:15; Exodus 34:28"> And he was there in the wildernesse fourtie daies, and was tempted of Satan: hee was also with the wilde beastes, and the Angels ministred vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Matthew 4:12; Luke 8:1"> Now after that Iohn was committed to prison, Iesus came into Galile, preaching the Gospel of the kingdome of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:36–38; Matthew 4:17"> And saying, The time is fulfilled, and the kingdome of God is at hand: repent and beleeue the Gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 4:18–22; John 1:40–42; John 6:8"> And as he walked by the sea of Galile, he saw Simon, and Andrew his brother, casting a net into the sea, (for they were fishers.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 5:10; Matthew 4:19–20; Acts 2:38–41"> Then Iesus said vnto them, Folow me, and I will make you to be fishers of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 18:28–30; Luke 5:11; Mark 10:28–31"> And straightway they forsooke their nets, and folowed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 5:37; Acts 1:13; Mark 10:35"> And when hee had gone a litle further thence, he sawe Iames the sonne of Zebedeus, and Iohn his brother, as they were in the ship, mending their nets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Luke 14:26; Mark 10:29"> And anon hee called them: and they left their father Zebedeus in the shippe with his hired seruants, and went their way after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 6:2; Mark 1:39; Matthew 4:23"> So they entred into Capernaum, and straightway on the Sabbath day hee entred into the Synagogue, and taught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54; Luke 4:32"> And they were astonied at his doctrine, for he taught them as one that had authoritie, and not as the Scribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 9:25; Mark 7:25; Mark 1:34"> And there was in their Synagogue a man in whome was an vncleane spirite, and hee cried out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; James 2:19; Acts 3:14"> Saying, Ah, what haue we to do with thee, O Iesus of Nazareth? Art thou come to destroy vs? I knowe thee what thou art, euen that holy one of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 1:34; Luke 4:41; Acts 16:17"> And Iesus rebuked him, saying, Holde thy peace, and come out of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 9:26; Mark 9:20; Luke 11:22"> And the vncleane spirit tare him, and cried with a loude voyce, and came out of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:31; Mark 7:37; Luke 9:1"> And they were all amased, so that they demaunded one of another, saying, What thing is this? what newe doctrine is this? for he commandeth euen the foule spirites with authoritie, and they obey him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 9:31; Luke 4:37; Mark 1:45"> And immediatly his fame spred abroade throughout all the region bordering on Galile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 4:38–39; Matthew 8:14–15; Mark 1:21"> And assoone as they were come out of the Synagogue, they entred into the house of Symon and Andrew, with Iames and Iohn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:5; Mark 5:23; John 11:3"> And Symons wiues mother lay sicke of a feuer, and anon they told him of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 9:41; Psalms 116:12; Mark 5:41"> And he came and tooke her by the hand, and lifted her vp, and the feuer forsooke her by and by, and shee ministred vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 8:16; Mark 3:2; Luke 4:40"> And whe euen was come, at what time the sunne setteth, they brought to him all that were diseased, and them that were possessed with deuils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 1:5; Acts 13:44"> And the whole citie was gathered together at the doore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 3:12; Matthew 4:23"> And he healed many that were sicke of diuers diseases: and he cast out many deuils, and suffred not the deuils to say that they knewe him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Luke 5:16; Luke 22:39–46"> And in the morning very early before day, Iesus arose and went out into a solitarie place, and there praied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And Simon, and they that were with him, followed carefully after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 12:19; Mark 1:5; Zechariah 11:11"> And when they had found him, they sayde vnto him, All men seeke for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 4:43; John 17:8; Isaiah 61:1–3"> Then he said vnto them, Let vs go into the next townes, that I may preach there also: for I came out for that purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Mark 1:21; Luke 4:43–44"> And hee preached in their Synagogues, throughout all Galile, and cast the deuils out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 5:12–14; Matthew 8:2–4; Leviticus 13:1–14"> And there came a leper to him, beseeching him, and kneeled downe vnto him, and said to him, If thou wilt, thou canst make me cleane.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 4:15"> And Iesus had compassion, and put foorth his hand, and touched him, and said to him, I wil: be thou cleane.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 15:3; Mark 5:29; John 4:50–53"> And assone as he had spoken, immediatly ye leprosie departed from him, and he was made cleane.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 9:30; Mark 7:36; Luke 8:56"> And after he had giue him a streight commandement, he sent him away forthwith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Leviticus 14:1–32; Luke 17:14; Matthew 8:4"> And sayde vnto him, See thou say nothing to any man, but get thee hence, and shewe thy selfe to the Priest, and offer for thy clensing those things, which Moses commanded, for a testimoniall vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 2:13; Matthew 9:31; John 6:2"> But when he was departed, hee began to tel many things, and to publish the matter: so that Iesus could no more openly enter into the citie, but was without in desert places: and they came to him from euery quarter.</span>
