<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 5:1–3; Mark 2:13; Luke 8:4–10"> And hee began againe to teache by the sea side, and there gathered vnto him a great multitude, so that hee entred into a shippe, and sate in the sea, and all the people was by the sea side on the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; Mark 3:23; Mark 4:11"> And he taught them many things in parables, and said vnto them in his doctrine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 2:5; Matthew 13:26; Mark 4:26–29"> Hearken: Beholde, there went out a sower to sowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Mark 4:15; Matthew 13:4"> And it came to passe as he sowed, that some fell by the way side, and the foules of the heauen came, and deuoured it vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26; Luke 8:6; Luke 8:13"> And some fell on stonie grounde, where it had not much earth, and by and by sprang vp, because it had not depth of earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:11; Jeremiah 17:5–8; Jonah 4:8"> But assoone as ye Sunne was vp, it was burnt vp, and because it had not roote, it withered away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Luke 8:14; Genesis 3:17–18"> And some fell among the thornes, and the thornes grewe vp, and choked it, so that it gaue no fruite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 15:5; Colossians 1:6; Mark 4:20"> Some againe fell in good grounde, and did yeelde fruite that sprong vp, and grewe, and it brought foorth, some thirtie folde, some sixtie folde, and some an hundreth folde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Mark 4:23–24; Revelation 3:22"> Then he said vnto them, He that hath eares to heare, let him heare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:10–17; Mark 4:34; Proverbs 13:20"> And whe he was alone, they that were about him with the twelue, asked him of ye parable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; Luke 8:10; 1 John 5:20"> And he saide vnto them, To you it is giuen to knowe the mysterie of the kingdome of God: but vnto them that are without, all thinges bee done in parables,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Isaiah 44:18; Matthew 13:14–15"> That they seeing, may see, and not discerne: and they hearing, may heare, and not vnderstand, least at any time they should turne, and their sinnes should be forgiuen them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Matthew 13:51–52; Mark 7:17–18"> Againe he said vnto them, Perceiue ye not this parable? howe then should ye vnderstand all other parables?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 8:4; Matthew 13:19; Matthew 13:37"> The sower soweth the worde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 13:19; 2 Corinthians 2:11"> And these are they that receiue the seede by the wayes side, in whome the worde is sowen: but when they haue heard it, Satan commeth immediatly, and taketh away the worde that was sowen in their heartes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 6:20; Luke 8:13; Matthew 13:20–21"> And likewise they that receiue the seede in stony ground, are they, which whe they haue heard the word, straightwayes receiue it with gladnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 1 Thessalonians 3:3–5; Matthew 12:31"> Yet haue they no roote in themselues, and endure but a time: for when trouble and persecution ariseth for the worde, immediatly they be offended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:14; Jeremiah 4:3; Matthew 13:22"> Also they that receiue the seede among the thornes, are such as heare the word:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; 1 Timothy 6:17"> But the cares of this world, and the deceitfulnes of riches, and the lustes of other things enter in, and choke the word, and it is vnfruitfull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Luke 8:15; John 15:4–5"> But they that haue receiued seede in good ground, are they that heare the worde, and receiue it, and bring foorth fruite: one corne thirtie, another sixtie, and some an hundreth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 5:15; Luke 11:33; Luke 8:16"> Also he saide vnto them, Commeth the candle in, to be put vnder a bushell, or vnder the bed, and not to be put on a candlesticke?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 8:17; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Matthew 10:26–27"> For there is nothing hid, that shall not be opened: neither is there a secret, but that it shall come to light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 4:9; Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:29"> If any man haue eares to heare, let him heare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Luke 8:18; Matthew 7:2"> And he said vnto them, Take heede what ye heare. With what measure ye mete, it shall be measured vnto you: and vnto you that heare, shall more be giuen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 13:12; Luke 8:18; Matthew 25:28–29"> For vnto him that hath, shall it be giuen, and from him that hath not, shall be taken away, euen that he hath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 13:24; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9; Luke 8:11"> Also he said, So is the kingdome of God, as if a man should cast seede in the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:5; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 1 Corinthians 15:37–38"> And shoulde sleepe, and rise vp night and day, and the seede should spring and growe vp, he not knowing howe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 4:31–32; Hosea 6:3; Philippians 1:9–11"> For the earth bringeth foorth fruite of it selfe, first the blade, then the eares, after that full corne in the eares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Matthew 13:30; Matthew 13:40–43"> And assoone as the fruite sheweth it selfe, anon hee putteth in the sickle, because the haruest is come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 13:31–32; Luke 13:18–21; Lamentations 2:13"> He saide moreouer, Whereunto shall wee liken the kingdome of God? or with what comparison shall we compare it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:20; Luke 13:18–19; Daniel 2:44–45"> It is like a graine of mustarde seede, which when it is sowen in the earth, is the least of all seedes that be in the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 4:20–22; Daniel 4:10–14; Ezekiel 31:3–10"> But after that it is sowen, it groweth vp, and is greatest of all herbes, and beareth great branches, so that the foules of heauen may builde vnder the shadow of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; John 16:12; Hebrews 5:11–14"> And with many such parables he preached the word vnto them, as they were able to heare it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 16:25; Mark 4:10; Matthew 15:15–20"> And without parables spake hee nothing vnto them: but he expounded all thinges to his disciples apart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 8:18; Luke 8:22; Luke 8:25"> Nowe the same day when euen was come, he saide vnto them, Let vs passe ouer vnto the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 3:9; Mark 4:1; Mark 5:2"> And they left the multitude, and tooke him as he was in the shippe, and there were also with him other little shippes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 8:23–24; Job 1:19; Luke 8:22–23"> And there arose a great storme of winde, and the waues dashed into the shippe, so that it was now full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Hebrews 4:15; Isaiah 54:6–8"> And he was in the sterne asleepe on a pillow: and they awoke him, and saide to him, Master, carest thou not that we perish?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 107:29; Psalms 89:9; Psalms 65:7"> And hee rose vp, and rebuked the winde, and saide vnto the sea, Peace, and be still. So the winde ceased, and it was a great calme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 8:25; Matthew 8:26; Matthew 14:31"> Then he saide vnto them, Why are ye so fearefull? how is it that ye haue no faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 8:25; Job 38:11; Psalms 89:7"> And they feared exceedingly, and said one to another, Who is this, that both the winde and sea obey him?</span>
