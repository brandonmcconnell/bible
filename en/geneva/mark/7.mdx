<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 15:1–20; Mark 3:22; Luke 5:17"> Then gathered vnto him the Pharises, and certaine of the Scribes which came from Hierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Matthew 7:3–5; Romans 14:14"> And when they sawe some of his disciples eate meate with common hands, (that is to say, vnwashen) they complained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 1:14; Mark 7:13; Colossians 2:8"> (For the Pharises, and all the Iewes, except they wash their hands oft, eate not, holding the tradition of the Elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 9:10; Matthew 23:25; Luke 11:38–39"> And when they come from the market, except they wash, they eate not: and many other things there be, which they haue taken vpon them to obserue, as the washing of cups, and pots, and of brasen vessels, and of beds.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:6; Acts 21:21; Matthew 15:2"> Then asked him the Pharises and Scribes, Why walke not thy disciples according to the tradition of the Elders, but eate meate with vnwashen hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 15:7–9"> Then hee answered and sayd vnto them, Surely Esay hath prophecied well of you, hypocrites, as it is written, This people honoureth mee with lippes, but their heart is farre away from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Matthew 15:9; James 1:26"> But they worship me in vaine, teaching for doctrines the commandements of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Isaiah 1:12"> For ye lay the commandement of God apart, and obserue the tradition of men, as the washing of pots and of cups, and many other such like things ye doe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16–17; Galatians 2:21; Isaiah 29:13"> And he sayd vnto them, Well, ye reiect the commandement of God, that ye may obserue your owne tradition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 21:17; Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 5:16"> For Moses sayd, Honour thy father and thy mother: and Whosoeuer shall speake euill of father or mother, let him die the death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 23:18; 1 Timothy 5:4–8; Matthew 15:5"> But yee say, If a man say to father or mother, Corban, that is, By the gift that is offered by mee, thou mayest haue profite, hee shall be free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> So ye suffer him no more to doe any thing for his father, or his mother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 7:9; Titus 1:14; Matthew 5:17–20"> Making the worde of God of none authoritie, by your tradition which ye haue ordeined: and ye doe many such like things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:10; Isaiah 6:9; Proverbs 8:5"> Then he called the whole multitude vnto him, and sayd vnto them, Hearken you all vnto me, and vnderstand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 4:23; Leviticus 11:42–47; Titus 1:15"> There is nothing without a man, that can defile him, when it entreth into him: but the things which proceede out of him, are they which defile the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> If any haue eares to heare, let him heare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Mark 9:28"> And when hee came into an house, away from the people, his disciples asked him concerning the parable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 15:16–17; Hebrews 5:11; Isaiah 28:9–10"> And he sayde vnto them, What? are ye without vnderstanding also? Doe ye not knowe that whatsoeuer thing from without entreth into a man, cannot defile him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 15:17; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Colossians 2:21–22"> Because it entreth not into his heart, but into the belly, and goeth out into the draught which is the purging of all meates?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 3:6; James 1:14–15; James 4:1"> Then he sayd, That which commeth out of man, that defileth man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Romans 8:7–8; James 4:1–3"> For from within, euen out of the heart of men, proceede euill thoughtes, adulteries, fornications, murthers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 20:15; Matthew 6:23; 1 Peter 2:15"> Theftes, couetousnes, wickednes, deceite, vncleannes, a wicked eye, backbiting, pride, foolishnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 3:17"> All these euill things come from within, and defile a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:21–28; Matthew 11:21; Ezekiel 28:2"> And from thence he rose, and went into the borders of Tyrus and Sidon, and entred into an house, and woulde that no man should haue knowen: but he could not be hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Mark 9:17–23; Acts 10:25–26"> For a certaine woman, whose litle daughter had an vncleane spirit, heard of him, and came, and fell at his feete,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Isaiah 49:12; Galatians 3:28"> (And the woman was a Greeke, a Syrophenissian by nation) and she besought him that he would cast out the deuill out of her daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:23–28; Matthew 7:6; Acts 22:21"> But Iesus saide vnto her, Let the children first be fedde: for it is not good to take the childrens bread, and to cast it vnto whelpes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Romans 3:29; Acts 11:17–18"> Then shee answered, and saide vnto him, Trueth, Lord: yet in deede the whelpes eate vnder the table of the childrens crommes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; Matthew 5:3; 1 John 3:8"> Then he said vnto her, For this saying goe thy way: the deuil is gone out of thy daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 3:8; John 4:50–52"> And when shee was come home to her house, shee founde the deuill departed, and her daughter lying on the bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 5:20; Matthew 15:29–31; Matthew 4:25"> And hee departed againe from the coastes of Tyrus and Sidon, and came vnto the sea of Galile, through the middes of the coastes of Decapolis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:14; Mark 5:23; Matthew 9:32–33"> And they brought vnto him one that was deafe and stambered in his speache, and prayed him to put his hand vpon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:23; John 9:6–7; Mark 5:40"> Then hee tooke him aside from the multitude, and put his fingers in his eares, and did spit, and touched his tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 11:41; Mark 5:41; Mark 6:41"> And looking vp to heauen, hee sighed, and said vnto him, Ephphatha, that is, Be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5–6; Psalms 33:9; Mark 2:12"> And straightway his eares were opened, and the string of his tongue was loosed, and hee spake plaine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Mark 8:26; Mark 3:12"> And he commanded them, that they should tell no man: but howe much soeuer hee forbad them, the more a great deale they published it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 6:51; Mark 5:42; Exodus 4:10–11"> And were beyonde measure astonied, saying, Hee hath done all thinges well: he maketh both the deafe to heare, and the domme to speake.</span>
