<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:9–14; Luke 6:6–11; Mark 1:21"> And he entred againe into ye Synagogue, and there was a man which had a withered had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:32; Luke 14:1; Luke 6:7"> And they watched him, whether he would heale him on the Sabbath day, that they might accuse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 1:28–30; Isaiah 42:4; Luke 6:8"> Then he saide vnto the man which had the withered hand, Arise: stand forth in the middes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Matthew 12:10–12; Hosea 6:6"> And he saide to them, Is it lawfull to doe a good deede on the Sabbath day, or to doe euil? to saue the life, or to kill? But they held their peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:10; Ephesians 4:18; Romans 11:25"> Then hee looked rounde about on them angerly, mourning also for the hardnesse of their hearts, and saide to the man, Stretch foorth thine hand. And he stretched it out: and his hande was restored, as whole as the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:16; Mark 12:13; Matthew 12:14"> And the Pharises departed, and straightway gathered a councill with the Herodians against him, that they might destroy him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 4:25; Luke 6:17; Matthew 12:15"> But Iesus auoided with his disciples to the sea: and a great multitude followed him from Galile, and from Iudea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Isaiah 34:5; Matthew 11:21"> And from Ierusalem, and from Idumea, and beyonde Iordan: and they that dwelled about Tyrus and Sidon, when they had heard what great things he did, came vnto him in great number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 5:30; John 6:15"> And he commanded his disciples, that a litle shippe should waite for him, because of the multitude, lest they shoulde throng him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56; Matthew 4:23"> For hee had healed many, in so much that they preassed vpon him to touch him, as many as had plagues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 1:23–24; Luke 8:28"> And when the vncleane spirits sawe him, they fel downe before him, and cried, saying, Thou art the Sonne of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:16; Mark 1:25; Mark 1:34"> And he sharply rebuked them, to the ende they should not vtter him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Luke 6:12–16; Matthew 10:1–4"> Then hee went vp into a mountaine, and called vnto him whome he woulde, and they came vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Luke 9:1–6; John 15:16"> And hee appoynted twelue that they should be with him, and that he might send them to preache,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And that they might haue power to heale sicknesses, and to cast out deuils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:42; Acts 1:13; Matthew 16:16–18"> And the first was Simon, and hee named Simon, Peter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Mark 14:33; Jeremiah 23:29"> Then Iames the sonne of Zebedeus, and Iohn Iames brother (and surnamed them Boanerges, which is, the sonnes of thunder,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14; Matthew 9:9"> And Andrew, and Philippe, and Bartlemew, and Matthewe, and Thomas, and Iames, the sonne of Alpheus, and Thaddeus, and Simon the Cananite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 6:71; John 13:2; Acts 1:16–25"> And Iudas Iscariot, who also betraied him, and they came home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 6:31; John 4:31–34; Mark 9:28"> And the multitude assembled againe, so that they could not so much as eate bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 26:24; John 10:20; Mark 3:31"> And when his kinsfolkes heard of it, they went out to laie hold on him: for they sayde that he was beside himselfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:1; Matthew 10:25; Matthew 9:34"> And the Scribes which came downe from Hierusalem, saide, He hath Beelzebub, and through the prince of the deuils he casteth out deuils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:17–23; Matthew 12:25–30; Matthew 13:34"> But he called them vnto him, and said vnto them in parables, How can Satan driue out Satan?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Isaiah 19:2–3; 2 Samuel 20:6"> For if a kingdome bee deuided against it selfe, that kingdome can not stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 3:16; Psalms 133:1; Galatians 5:15"> Or if a house bee deuided against it selfe, that house can not continue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So if Satan make insurrection against himselfe, and be deuided, hee can not endure but is at an ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–26; Matthew 12:29; Luke 11:21–23"> No man can enter into a strong mans house, and take away his goods, except hee first binde that strong man, and then spoyle his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; 1 John 5:16"> Verely I say vnto you, all sinnes shalbe forgiuen vnto the children of men, and blasphemies, wherewith they blaspheme:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 12:31–32; Luke 12:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> But hee that blasphemeth against the holy Ghost, shall neuer haue forgiuenesse, but is culpable of eternall damnation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:20; Mark 3:22"> Because they saide, Hee had an vncleane spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:19–21; Matthew 12:46–50"> Then came his brethren and mother, and stoode without, and sent vnto him, and called him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the people sate about him, and they said vnto him, Beholde, thy mother, and thy brethren seeke for thee without.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 3:21; Deuteronomy 33:9; Mark 6:3"> But hee answered them, saying, Who is my mother and my brethren?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 12:49–50; Luke 11:27–28; Romans 8:29"> And hee looked rounde about on them, which sate in compasse about him, and saide, Beholde my mother and my brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:17; Matthew 7:21; 1 John 3:22–23"> For whosoeuer doeth the will of God, he is my brother, and my sister, and mother.</span>
