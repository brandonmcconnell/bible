<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–4; Mark 13:34; Song of Solomon 8:11–12"> And he began to speake vnto them in parables, A certaine man planted a vineyard, and copassed it with an hedge, and digged a pit for the winepresse, and built a tower in it, and let it out to husbandmen, and went into a strange countrey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:1–8; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 44:4"> And at the time, he sent to the husbandmen a seruant, that he might receiue of the husbandmen of the fruite of the vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15; Nehemiah 9:26; Jeremiah 29:26"> But they tooke him, and beat him, and sent him away emptie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And againe he sent vnto them another seruant, and at him they cast stones, and brake his head, and sent him away shamefully handled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:6; Matthew 23:37; Nehemiah 9:30"> And againe he sent another, and him they slew, and many other, beating some, and killing some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 1:1–2; Matthew 3:17; John 1:34"> Yet had he one sonne, his deare beloued: him also he sent the last vnto them, saying, They will reuerence my sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Matthew 2:16; Acts 2:23"> But ye husbandmen said among themselues, This is the heire: come, let vs kill him, and the inheritance shalbe ours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 21:39; Luke 20:15; Matthew 21:33"> So they tooke him, and killed him, and cast him out of the vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 10:20–12; Matthew 21:43; Luke 19:27"> What shall then the Lord of the vineyard doe? He will come and destroy these husbandmen, and giue the vineyard to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 28:16"> Haue ye not read so much as this Scripture? The stone which the builders did refuse, is made the head of the corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 118:23; Acts 3:12–16; Habakkuk 1:5"> This was done of the Lord, and it is marueilous in our eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 22:22; John 7:44"> Then they went about to take him, but they feared the people: for they perceiued that he spake that parable against them: therefore they left him, and went their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Luke 11:54; Mark 3:6"> And they sent vnto him certaine of the Pharises, and of ye Herodians that they might take him in his talke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 20:22; John 7:18"> And when they came, they saide vnto him, Master, we know that thou art true, and carest for no man: for thou considerest not the person of men, but teachest the way of God truely, Is it lawfull to giue tribute to Cesar, or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:2; Revelation 2:23; Mark 10:2"> Should we giue it, or should we not giue it? but he knew their hypocrisie, and said vnto them, Why tempt ye me? Bring me a peny, that I may see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 20:24–26; Revelation 3:12; Matthew 22:19–22"> So they brought it, and he said vnto them, Whose is this image and superscription? and they said vnto him, Cesars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:7; 1 Peter 2:17; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> Then Iesus answered, and saide vnto them, Giue to Cesar the things that are Cesars, and to God, those that are Gods: and they marueiled at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 22:23–33; Acts 4:1–2; Acts 23:6–9"> Then came the Sadduces vnto him, (which say, there is no resurrection) and they asked him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Ruth 4:5; Ruth 1:11–13"> Master, Moses wrote vnto vs, If any mans brother die, and leaue his wife, and leaue no children, that his brother should take his wife, and rayse vp seede vnto his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 22:25–28; Luke 20:29–33"> There were seuen brethren, and the first tooke a wife, and when he died, left no issue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then the seconde tooke her, and he died, neither did he yet leaue issue, and the third likewise:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> So those seuen had her, and left no yssue: last of all the wife died also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In the resurrection then, when they shall rise againe, whose wife shall she be of them? for seuen had her to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 20:9; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Matthew 22:29"> Then Iesus answered, and saide vnto them, Are ye not therefore deceiued, because ye knowe not the Scriptures, neither the power of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:35–36; Matthew 22:30; 1 John 3:2"> For when they shall rise againe from the dead, neither men marry, nor wiues are married, but are as the Angels which are in heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Luke 20:37; Genesis 17:7–8"> And as touching the dead, that they shall rise againe, haue ye not read in the booke of Moses, howe in the bush God spake vnto him, saying, I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Iacob?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 14:9; Romans 4:17; Mark 12:24"> God is not ye God of the dead, but the God of the liuing. Ye are therefore greatly deceiued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–40; Luke 10:25–28; Luke 20:39"> Then came one of the Scribes that had heard them disputing together, and perceiuing that he had answered them well, he asked him, Which is the first commandement of all?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:4; Luke 10:27; Galatians 3:20"> Iesus answered him, The first of all the commandements is, Heare, Israel, The Lord our God is the onely Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5"> Thou shalt therefore loue the Lord thy God with all thine heart, and with all thy soule, and with all thy minde, and with all thy strength: this is the first commandement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 13:8–9; Matthew 22:39; Leviticus 19:18"> And the second is like, that is, Thou shalt loue thy neighbour as thy selfe. There is none other commandement greater then these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Deuteronomy 4:35; Isaiah 46:9"> Then that Scribe said vnto him, Well, Master, thou hast saide the trueth, that there is one God, and that there is none but he,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Micah 6:6–8; 1 Samuel 15:22"> And to loue him with all the heart, and with all the vnderstanding, and with all the soule, and with all the strength, and to loue his neighbour as himselfe, is more then all whole burnt offerings and sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 20:40; Matthew 22:46"> Then when Iesus saw that he answered discreetely, he saide vnto him, Thou art not farre from the kingdome of God. And no man after that durst aske him any question.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Matthew 22:41–45; Matthew 26:55"> And Iesus answered and said teaching in the Temple, Howe say the Scribes that Christ is the sonne of Dauid?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Matthew 22:43–45"> For Dauid himselfe said by ye holy Ghost, The Lord said to my Lord, Sit at my right hand, till I make thine enemies thy footestoole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Matthew 11:25; Matthew 1:23"> Then Dauid himselfe calleth him Lord: by what meanes is he then his sonne? and much people heard him gladly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–7; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 11:43"> Moreouer he saide vnto them in his doctrine, Beware of the Scribes which loue to goe in long robes, and loue salutations in the markets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="James 2:2–3"> And the chiefe seates in the Synagogues, and the first roumes at feastes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Luke 20:47; Matthew 23:33"> Which deuoure widowes houses, euen vnder a colour of long prayers. These shall receiue the greater damnation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 21:1–4; 2 Kings 12:9; John 8:20"> And as Iesus sate ouer against the treasurie, he beheld how the people cast money into the treasurie, and many rich men cast in much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And there came a certaine poore widowe, and she threw in two mites, which make a quadrin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Acts 11:29"> Then he called vnto him his disciples, and said vnto them, Verely I say vnto you, that this poore widowe hath cast more in, then all they which haue cast into the treasurie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 1 John 3:17; Ezra 2:68–69"> For they all did cast in of their superfluitie: but she of her pouertie did cast in all that she had, euen all her liuing.</span>
