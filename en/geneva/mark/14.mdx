<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:4–5; Matthew 26:8–9; Malachi 1:12–13"> Therefore some disdained among themselues, and sayde, To what ende is this waste of oyntment?</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 3:14; Hebrews 10:24; Hebrews 13:21"> But Iesus saide, Let her alone: why trouble yee her? shee hath wrought a good worke on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Matthew 26:11; James 2:14–16"> For yee haue the poore with you alwaies, and when yee will yee may doe them good, but me yee shall not haue alwaies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; John 12:7; Mark 15:42–1"> She hath done that she coulde: she came afore hand to anoynt my body to the burying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 26:12–13; Mark 16:15; Matthew 24:14"> Verely I say vnto you, wheresoeuer this Gospel shall be preached throughout the whole world, this also that she hath done, shalbe spoken of in remembrance of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 22:3–6; Matthew 26:14–16; John 13:2"> Then Iudas Iscariot, one of the twelue, went away vnto the hie Priestes, to betray him vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Hosea 7:3; Jude 1:11"> And when they heard it, they were glad, and promised that they woulde giue him monie: therefore he sought howe he might conueniently betraie him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 12:6; Exodus 12:18; Matthew 26:17–19"> Nowe the first day of vnleauened bread, when they sacrificed the Passeouer, his disciples sayde vnto him, Where wilt thou that we goe and prepare, that thou mayest eate the Passeouer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 22:10–13; Matthew 26:18–19; Luke 19:30–33"> Then hee sent foorth two of his disciples, and sayde vnto them, Goe yee into the citie, and there shall a man meete you bearing a pitcher of water: follow him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Mark 11:3; John 13:13"> And whithersoeuer he goeth in, say yee to the good man of the house, The Master sayeth, Where is the lodging where I shall eate the Passeouer with my disciples?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Psalms 110:3; Proverbs 21:1–2"> And he wil shewe you an vpper chamber which is large, trimmed and prepared: there make it readie for vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 16:4; Luke 22:13; Luke 22:35"> So his disciples went foorth, and came to the citie, and found as he had said vnto them, and made readie the Passeouer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:14; John 13:21–26; Matthew 26:20–24"> And at euen he came with the twelue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 6:47; John 6:70"> And as they sate at table and did eate, Iesus said, Verely I say vnto you, that one of you shall betray me, which eateth with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 22:21–23; Matthew 26:22; John 13:22"> Then they began to be sorowful and to say to him one by one, Is it I? And another, Is it I?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 13:26; Matthew 26:23; John 6:71"> And he answered and sayde vnto them, It is one of the twelue that dippeth with mee in the platter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 26:24–25; Isaiah 53:1–12; John 19:28"> Truely the Sonne of man goeth his way, as it is written of him: but woe bee to that man, by whome the Sonne of man is betrayed: it had beene good for that man, if hee had neuer beene borne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 26:26–29; Mark 14:24; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> And as they did eate, Iesus tooke the bread, and when hee had giuen thankes, he brake it and gaue it to them, and sayde, Take, eate, this is my bodie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 14:22; Matthew 26:27; 1 Corinthians 10:16"> Also he tooke the cuppe, and when he had giuen thankes, gaue it to them: and they all dranke of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11; Exodus 24:8; Revelation 7:9–17"> And he saide vnto them, This is my blood of that newe Testament, which is shed for many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 26:29; Psalms 104:15; Luke 22:16–18"> Verely I say vnto you, I wil drinke no more of the fruit of ye vine vntill that day, that I drinke it newe in the kingdome of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 26:30; Luke 22:39; Acts 16:25"> And when they had sung a Psalme, they went out to the mount of Oliues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; John 16:32; Matthew 26:31"> Then Iesus said vnto them, Al ye shall be offended by mee this night: for it is written, I will smite the shepheard, and the sheepe shall be scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 28:16; Mark 16:7; Matthew 28:7"> But after that I am risen, I will goe into Galile before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 26:33–35; Luke 22:33–34; John 13:36–38"> And Peter saide vnto him, Although all men should be offended at thee, yet would not I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 13:38; Mark 14:66–72; Genesis 1:5"> Then Iesus saide vnto him, Verely I say vnto thee, this day, euen in this night, before the cocke crowe twise, thou shalt denie me thrise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 19:8; John 13:37; Proverbs 16:18"> But he saide more earnestly. If I shoulde die with thee, I will not denie thee: likewise also saide they all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 14:36; Mark 14:39; Psalms 18:5–6"> After, they came into a place named Gethsemane: then hee saide to his disciples, Sit yee here, till I haue praied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 22:44; Hebrews 5:7; Mark 5:37"> And hee tooke with him Peter, and Iames, and Iohn, and hee began to be troubled, and in great heauinesse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 12:27; Mark 13:35–37; 1 Peter 5:8"> And saide vnto them, My soule is very heauie, euen vnto the death: tarie here, and watch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 14:41; Deuteronomy 9:18; Hebrews 5:7"> So he went forward a litle, and fell downe on the ground, and praied, that if it were possible, that houre might passe from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Galatians 4:6; Romans 8:15–16; John 5:30"> And he saide, Abba, Father, all things are possible vnto thee: take away this cup from me: neuertheles not that I will, but that thou wilt, be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 26:40; Mark 14:29–31; Hebrews 12:3"> Then hee came, and founde them sleeping, and said to Peter, Simon, sleepest thou? couldest not thou watche one houre?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 26:41; Matthew 24:42; Luke 21:36"> Watch ye, and pray, that ye enter not into tentation: the spirite in deede is ready, but the flesh is weake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 26:42–44; 2 Corinthians 12:8; Matthew 6:7"> And againe hee went away, and praied, and spake the same wordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 9:33–34; Romans 3:19; Genesis 44:16"> And he returned, and founde them a sleepe againe: for their eyes were heauie: neither knewe they what they should answere him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 14:35; Mark 9:31; John 17:1"> And he came the third time, and said vnto them, Sleepe henceforth, and take your rest: it is ynough: the houre is come: beholde, the Sonne of man is deliuered into the hands of sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 26:46; John 18:1–2"> Rise vp: let vs go: loe, he that betraieth me, is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 18:3–11; Luke 22:47–53; Matthew 26:47–56"> And immediatly while hee yet spake, came Iudas that was one of the twelue, and with him a great multitude with swordes and staues from the hie Priests, and Scribes, and Elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 26:48–50; Proverbs 27:6; Psalms 55:20–21"> And he that betraied him, had giuen them a token, saying, Whomsoeuer I shall kisse, he it is: take him and leade him away safely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 23:7–10; Malachi 1:6; Mark 12:14"> And assoone as hee was come, hee went straightway to him, and saide, Haile Master, and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 18:12; Lamentations 4:20; Acts 2:23"> Then they laide their handes on him, and tooke him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Matthew 26:51–54; Luke 22:49–51"> And one of them that stoode by, drewe out a sword, and smote a seruant of the hie Priest, and cut off his eare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 22:52–53; 1 Samuel 26:18; 1 Samuel 24:14–15"> And Iesus answered and saide to them, Ye be come out as against a thiefe with swordes, and with staues, to take me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 18:20; John 8:2; Luke 22:37"> I was daily with you teaching in the Temple, and yee tooke me not: but this is done that the Scriptures should be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Mark 14:27; John 16:32; Psalms 88:18"> Then they all forsooke him, and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And there followed him a certaine yong man, clothed in linnen vpon his bare bodie, and the yong men caught him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Mark 13:14–16; Genesis 39:12; Job 2:4"> But he left his linnen cloth, and fled from them naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 26:57–68; John 18:12–14; Luke 22:54–62"> So they led Iesus away to the hie Priest, and to him came together all the hie Priestes, and the Elders, and the Scribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 18:18; Matthew 26:3; John 18:25"> And Peter folowed him afarre off, euen into the hall of the hie Priest, and sate with the seruants, and warmed himselfe at the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16–18; Matthew 26:59–60; Matthew 5:22"> And the hie Priests, and all the Councill sought for witnesse against Iesus, to put him to death, but found none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> For many bare false witnesse against him, but their witnesse agreed not together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Acts 6:13–14; Matthew 26:60–61; John 2:18–21"> Then there arose certaine, and bare false witnesse against him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 2:19; Hebrews 9:11; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> We hearde him say, I will destroy this Temple made with hands, and within three daies I will builde another, made without hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> But their witnesse yet agreed not together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="John 19:9–10; Matthew 26:62–63; Mark 15:3–5"> Then the hie Priest stoode vp amongst them, and asked Iesus, saying, Answerest thou nothing? what is the matter that these beare witnesse against thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Matthew 26:63–64; Mark 15:2; Isaiah 53:7"> But hee helde his peace, and answered nothing. Againe the hie Priest asked him, and sayde vnto him, Art thou that Christ the Sonne of the Blessed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:3"> And Iesus said, I am he, and yee shall see the Sonne of man sitte at the right hande of the power of God, and come in the clouds of heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Matthew 26:65; Isaiah 36:22–1; Numbers 14:6"> Then the hie Priest rent his clothes, and sayd, What haue we any more neede of witnesses?</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Leviticus 24:16; John 10:31–33; John 19:7"> Ye haue heard the blasphemie: what thinke yee? And they all condemned him to be worthie of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Matthew 26:67–68; Acts 23:2; Isaiah 50:6"> And some began to spit at him, and to couer his face, and to beate him with fists, and to say vnto him, Prophesie. And the sergeants smote him with their roddes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Mark 14:54; Matthew 26:69–75; John 18:25–27"> And as Peter was beneath in the hal, there came one of the maides of the hie Priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Mark 14:54; Mark 10:47; Matthew 2:23"> And when shee sawe Peter warming him selfe, shee looked on him, and sayde, Thou wast also with Iesus of Nazareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Mark 14:29–31; Matthew 26:71–72; John 13:36–38"> But he denied it, saying, I knowe him not, neither wot I what thou saiest. Then he went out into the porche, and the cocke crewe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Luke 22:58; Mark 14:38; Galatians 6:1"> Then a maid sawe him againe, and bega to say to them that stood by, This is one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Luke 22:59–60; Matthew 26:73–74; Acts 2:7"> But hee denied it againe: and anon after, they that stoode by, sayde againe to Peter, Surely thou art one of them: for thou art of Galile, and thy speach is like.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; 1 Corinthians 10:12; 2 Kings 10:32"> And he began to curse, and sweare, saying, I knowe not this man of whom ye speake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Mark 14:30; Mark 14:68; Ezekiel 7:16"> Then the seconde time the cocke crewe, and Peter remembred the woorde that Iesus had saide vnto him, Before the cocke crowe twise, thou shalt denie me thrise, and waying that with himselfe, he wept.</span>
