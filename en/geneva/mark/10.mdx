<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 10:40; Mark 4:2; John 11:7"> And he arose from thence, and went into the coastes of Iudea by the farre side of Iordan, and the people resorted vnto him againe, and as he was wont, he taught them againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Luke 11:53–54; John 8:6"> Then the Pharises came and asked him, if it were lawfull for a man to put away his wife, and tempted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:20; Galatians 4:21; Luke 10:25"> And he answered, and sayde vnto them, What did Moses commaund you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1–4; Matthew 5:31–32; Isaiah 50:1"> And they sayd, Moses suffered to write a bill of diuorcement, and to put her away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 19:8; Nehemiah 9:26; Acts 7:51"> Then Iesus answered, and sayd vnto them, For the hardnesse of your heart he wrote this precept vnto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 1:27; Genesis 5:2; Genesis 1:1"> But at the beginning of the creation God made them male and female:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:5–6; Ephesians 5:31"> For this cause shall man leaue his father and mother, and cleaue vnto his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:28; Genesis 2:24; 1 Corinthians 6:16"> And they twaine shalbe one flesh: so that they are no more twaine, but one flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 7:1–3; 1 Chronicles 7:10–13"> Therefore, what God hath coupled together, let not man separate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 9:33; Mark 9:28; Mark 4:10"> And in the house his disciples asked him againe of that matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:31–32; Matthew 19:9; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11"> And he sayd vnto them, Whosoeuer shall put away his wife and marrie another, committeth adulterie against her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:11; 1 Corinthians 7:13"> And if a woman put away her husband, and be married to another, she committeth adulterie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 19:13–15; Luke 18:15–17; Exodus 10:9–11"> Then they brought litle children to him, that he should touch them, and his disciples rebuked those that brought them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 19:14; Matthew 18:10; Matthew 18:4"> But when Iesus sawe it, he was displeased, and said to them, Suffer the litle children to come vnto me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdome of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:3; Luke 18:17; John 3:3–6"> Verely I say vnto you, Whosoeuer shall not receiue the kingdome of God as a litle childe, he shall not enter therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 9:36; Isaiah 40:11; John 21:15–17"> And he tooke them vp in his armes, and put his hands vpon them, and blessed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 19:16–30; Luke 18:18–30; Romans 2:7"> And when hee was gone out on the way, there came one running, and kneeled to him, and asked him, Good Master, what shall I doe, that I may possesse eternall life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 18:19; Matthew 19:17; Romans 3:12"> Iesus sayde to him, Why callest thou me good? there is none good but one, euen God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Romans 13:9; James 2:11"> Thou knowest the comandements, Thou shalt not commit adulterie. Thou shalt not kill. Thou shalt not steale. Thou shalt not beare false witnesse. Thou shalt hurt no man. Honour thy father and mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31–32; 2 Timothy 3:5; Philippians 3:6"> Then he answered, and said to him, Master, all these things I haue obserued from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–21; Revelation 2:4; Luke 18:22"> And Iesus looked vpon him, and loued him, and sayde vnto him, One thing is lacking vnto thee. Go and sell all that thou hast, and giue to the poore, and thou shalt haue treasure in heauen, and come, follow me, and take vp the crosse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 12:15"> But hee was sad at that saying, and went away sorowfull: for he had great possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:26; Luke 18:24; Mark 3:5"> And Iesus looked round about, and sayd vnto his disciples, Howe hardly doe they that haue riches, enter into the kingdome of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 5:1–3; Proverbs 11:28; Psalms 52:7"> And his disciples were afraide at his words. But Iesus answered againe, and sayd vnto them, Children, how hard is it for them that trust in riches, to enter into the kingdome of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 19:24–25; Luke 18:25; Matthew 23:24"> It is easier for a camel to goe through the eye of a needle, then for a riche man to enter into the kingdome of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 16:31; 2 Corinthians 11:23; Romans 10:9–13"> And they were much more astonied, saying with themselues, Who then can be saued?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Jeremiah 32:27; Job 42:2"> But Iesus looked vpon them, and sayd, With men it is impossible, but not with God: for with God all things are possible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 19:27–30; Luke 18:28–30; Luke 14:33"> Then Peter began to say vnto him, Loe, we haue forsaken all, and haue folowed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:23; Matthew 5:10–11; Mark 8:35"> Iesus answered, and sayd, Verely I say vnto you, there is no man that hath forsaken house, or brethren, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or lands for my sake and the Gospels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 18:30; Philippians 3:8; 2 Corinthians 6:10"> But he shall receiue an hundred folde, now at this present, houses, and brethren, and sisters, and mothers, and children, and lands with persecutions, and in the world to come, eternall life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Luke 13:30; Matthew 20:16"> But many that are first, shall be last, and the last, first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 20:17–19; Luke 18:31–34; Luke 9:51"> And they were in the way going vp to Hierusalem, and Iesus went before them and they were troubled, and as they followed, they were afraide, and Iesus tooke the twelue againe, and began to tell them what things should come vnto him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Matthew 20:17–19; Mark 8:31"> Saying, Beholde, we goe vp to Hierusalem, and the Sonne of man shall be deliuered vnto the hie Priests, and to the Scribes, and they shall condemne him to death, and shall deliuer him to the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Matthew 16:21; Mark 14:65"> And they shall mocke him, and scourge him, and spit vpon him, and kill him: but the third day he shall rise againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:20–28; 2 Samuel 14:4–11; 1 Kings 2:20"> Then Iames and Iohn the sonnes of Zebedeus came vnto him, saying, Master, we would that thou shouldest doe for vs that we desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 15:7; 1 Kings 3:5–15; Mark 10:51"> And he sayd vnto them, What would ye I should doe for you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Mark 16:19; Luke 24:26"> And they said to him, Graunt vnto vs, that we may sit, one at thy right hand, and the other at thy left hand in thy glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 12:50; Matthew 20:21–22; Psalms 75:8"> But Iesus sayd vnto them, Ye knowe not what ye aske. Can ye drinke of the cup that I shall drinke of, and be baptized with the baptisme that I shall be baptized with?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 12:2; Revelation 1:9; John 17:14"> And they said vnto him, We can. But Iesus sayd vnto them, Ye shall drinke in deede of the cup that I shall drinke of, and be baptized with the baptisme wherewith I shalbe baptized:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 20:23; Matthew 25:34; John 17:24"> But to sit at my right hand, and at my left, is not mine to giue, but it shalbe giuen to them for whome it is prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 20:24; Proverbs 13:10; Romans 12:10"> And when the ten heard that, they began to disdaine at Iames and Iohn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; Luke 22:25; Matthew 20:25"> But Iesus called them vnto him, and sayd to them, Ye know that they which are princes among the Gentiles, haue domination ouer them, and they that be great among them, exercise authoritie ouer them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 23:8–12; Luke 14:11; Mark 9:35"> But it shall not be so among you: but whosoeuer will be great among you, shall be your seruant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And whosoeuer will be chiefe of you, shall be the seruant of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Philippians 2:5–8; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> For euen the Sonne of man came not to be serued, but to serue, and to giue his life for the raunsome of many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 18:35–1; Matthew 20:29–34; John 9:8"> Then they came to Iericho: and as he went out of Iericho with his disciples, and a great multitude, Bartimeus the sonne of Timeus, a blinde man, sate by the wayes side, begging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 1:24; Matthew 9:27; Revelation 22:16"> And when hee heard that it was Iesus of Nazareth, he began to crye, and to say, Iesus the Sonne of Dauid, haue mercy on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:13; Mark 7:26–29; Hebrews 5:7"> And many rebuked him, because he should holde his peace: but hee cryed much more, O Sonne of Dauid, haue mercy on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 18:40; Matthew 20:32–34; John 11:28"> Then Iesus stood still, and commanded him to be called: and they called the blind, saying vnto him, Be of good comfort: arise, he calleth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–9; Hebrews 12:1"> So he threwe away his cloke, and rose, and came to Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 18:41–43; Mark 10:36; Matthew 7:7"> And Iesus answered, and said vnto him, What wilt thou that I doe vnto thee? And the blinde sayd vnto him, Lord, that I may receiue sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 9:22; Acts 26:18; Mark 8:25"> Then Iesus sayde vnto him, Goe thy way: thy fayth hath saued thee. And by and by, he receiued his sight, and folowed Iesus in the way.</span>
