<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 14:10; Deuteronomy 31:3; Joshua 24:29"> Nowe when Ioshua was olde, and striken in yeeres, the Lord said vnto him, Thou art olde and growen in age, and there remaineth exceeding much land to be possessed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 13:11; 1 Samuel 27:8; Joel 3:4"> This is the land that remaineth, all the regions of the Philistims, and all Geshuri,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:23; Judges 3:3; Jeremiah 2:18"> From Nilus which is in Egypt, euen vnto the borders of Ekron Northward: this is counted of the Canaanites, euen fiue Lordships of the Philistims, the Azzithites, and the Ashdodites, the Eshkelonites, the Gittites, and the Ekronites, and the Auites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 19:30; 1 Samuel 4:1; Judges 1:34–36"> From the South, all the land of the Canaanites, and the caue that is beside the Sidonians, vnto Aphek, and to the borders of the Amorites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 5:18; Joshua 12:7; Psalms 83:7"> And the land of the Giblites, and all Lebanon, toward the sunne rising from Bahal-gad vnder mount Hermon, vntil one come to Hamath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 11:8; Joshua 23:13; Judges 2:21–23"> All the inhabitants of the mountaines from Lebanon vnto Misrephothmaim, and all the Sidonians, I wil cast them out from before the children of Israel: only deuide thou it by lot vnto the Israelites, to inherite, as I haue commanded thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 26:53–56; Numbers 33:54; Ezekiel 48:23–29"> Nowe therefore deuide this lande to inherite, vnto the nine tribes, and to the halfe tribe of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 12:6; Deuteronomy 3:12–17; Joshua 22:4"> For with halfe therof the Reubenites and the Gadites haue receiued their inheritance, which Moses gaue them beyond Iorden Eastward, euen as Moses the seruant of the Lord had giuen them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 13:16; Numbers 21:30; Joshua 12:2"> From Aroer that is on the brinke of the riuer Arnon, and from the citie that is in the mids of the riuer, and all the plaine of Medeba vnto Dibon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 21:24–26"> And all the cities of Sihon King of the Amorites, which reigned in Heshbon, vnto the borders of the children of Ammon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:23; Joshua 12:2–5; Deuteronomy 4:47–48"> And Gilead, and the borders of the Geshurites and of the Maachathites, and all mount Hermon, with all Bashan vnto Salcah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 12:4; Joshua 14:3–4; Numbers 21:23–35"> All the kingdome of Og in Bashan, which reigned in Ashtaroth and in Edrei: (who remained of the rest of the gyants) for these did Moses smite, and cast them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 23:12–13; Joshua 13:11; Numbers 33:55"> But the children of Israel expelled not the Geshurites nor the Maachathites: but the Geshurites and the Maachathites dwell among the Israelites euen vnto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:20–24; Joshua 14:3–4; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> Onely vnto the tribe of Leui he gaue none inheritance, but the sacrifices of the Lord God of Israel are his inheritance, as he said vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Moses then gaue vnto the tribe of the children of Reuben inheritance, according to their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 13:9; Joshua 12:2; Isaiah 16:7–9"> And their coast was from Aroer, that is on the brinke of the riuer Arnon, and from the citie that is in the middes of the riuer, and all the plaine which is by Medeba:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 22:41; Numbers 32:38; Numbers 21:19"> Heshbon with all the cities thereof, that are in the plaine: Dibon and Bamoth-baal, and Bethbaal-meon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 21:23; Joshua 21:36–37; 1 Chronicles 6:78–79"> And Iahazah, and Kedemoth and Mephaath:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 32:37–38"> Kiriathaim also, and Sibmah, and Zerethshahar in the mount of Emek:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 12:3; Deuteronomy 4:46; Ezekiel 25:9"> And Beth-peor, and Ashdoth-pisgah, and Beth-ieshimoth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Deuteronomy 3:10; Deuteronomy 2:30–36"> And all the cities of the plaine: and all the kingdome of Sihon King of the Amorites, which reigned in Heshbon, whome Moses smote with the Princes of Midian, Eui, and Rekem, and Zur, and Hur, and Reba, the dukes of Sihon, dwelling in the countrey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Jude 1:11; 2 Peter 2:15"> And Balaam the sonne of Beor the soothsayer did the children of Israel slay with the sword, among them that were slaine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the border of the children of Reuben was Iorden with the coastes. This was the inheritance of the children of Reuben according to their families, with the cities and their villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 32:34–36"> Also Moses gaue inheritance vnto ye tribe of Gad, euen vnto the children of Gad according to their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:26; 2 Samuel 11:1; Numbers 32:35"> And their coastes were Iazer, and all the cities of Gilead and halfe the lande of the children of Ammon vnto Aroer, which is before Rabbah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:27; 2 Samuel 2:8; Judges 10:17"> And from Heshbon vnto Ramoth, Mizpeh, and Betonim: and from Mahanaim vnto the borders of Debir:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 32:36; Numbers 34:11; Genesis 33:17"> And in the valley Beth-aram, and Bethnimrah, and Succoth, and Zaphon, the rest of the kingdome of Sihon King of Heshbon, vnto Iorden and the borders euen vnto the Sea coast of Cinneereth, beyond Iorden Eastward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> This is the inheritance of the children of Gad, after their families, with the cities, and their villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Also Moses gaue inheritance vnto the halfe tribe of Manasseh: and this belonged to the halfe tribe of the children of Manasseh according to their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 13:26; Deuteronomy 3:13–15; Numbers 32:39–41"> And their border was from Mahanaim, euen all Bashan, to wit, all the kingdome of Og King of Bashan, and all the townes of Iair which are in Bashan, threescore cities,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 12:4; Numbers 32:39–40"> And halfe Gilead, and Ashtaroth, and Edrei, cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan, were giuen vnto the children of Machir the sonne of Manasseh, to halfe of the children of Machir after their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> These are the heritages, which Moses did distribute in the plaine of Moab beyond Iorden, toward Iericho Eastward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 18:7; Joshua 13:14; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> But vnto the tribe of Leui Moses gaue none inheritance: for the Lord God of Israel is their inheritance, as he said vnto them.</span>
