<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 11:10; Psalms 2:1–4; Judges 4:2"> And whe Iabin King of Hazor had heard this, then he sent to Iobab King of Madon, and to the king of Shimron, and to the king of Achshaph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 12:3; Judges 1:27; Joshua 17:11"> And vnto the Kings that were by ye North in the mountaines and plaines toward the Southside of Cinneroth, and in the valleys, and in the borders of Dor Westward,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 3:3; Joshua 18:26; Genesis 31:49"> And vnto the Canaanites, both by East, and by West, and vnto the Amorites, and Hittites, and Perizzites, and Iebusites in the mountaines, and vnto the Hiuites vnder Hermon in the land of Mizpeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 7:12; 1 Samuel 13:5; Genesis 32:12"> And they came out and all their hostes with them, many people as the sande that is on the sea shore for multitude, with horses and charets exceeding many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 118:10–12; Revelation 16:14; Isaiah 8:9"> So all these Kings met together, and came and pitched together at the waters of Merom, for to fight against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 10:8; 2 Samuel 8:4; Judges 20:28"> Then the Lord sayd vnto Ioshua, Be not afrayd for them: for to morowe about this time will I deliuer them all slaine before Israel: thou shalt hough their horses, and burne their charets with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 10:9; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–3"> Then came Ioshua and al the men of warre with him against them by the waters of Merom suddenly, and fell vpon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:6; Joshua 19:28; Zechariah 9:2"> And the Lord gaue them into the hand of Israel: and they smote them, and chased them vnto great Zidon, and vnto Misrephothmaim, and vnto the valley of Mizpeh Eastward, and smote them vntill they had none remaining of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 11:6; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> And Ioshua did vnto them as the Lord bade him: he houghed their horses, and burnt their charets with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 11:1; Judges 4:2"> At that time also Ioshua turned backe, and tooke Hazor, and smote the King thereof with the sword: for Hazor before time was the head of all those kingdomes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 10:40; Deuteronomy 20:16"> Moreouer, they smote all the persons that were therein with the edge of the sworde, vtterly destroying all, leauing none aliue, and hee burnt Hazor with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:16–17; Deuteronomy 7:2; Numbers 33:50–53"> So all ye cities of those Kings, and all the kings of them did Ioshua take, and smote them with the edge of the sword, and vtterly destroyed them, as Moses the seruant of the Lord had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:18"> But Israel burnt none of the cities that stoode still in their strength, saue Hazor onely, that Ioshua burnt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:9; Joshua 8:27"> And all the spoyle of these cities and the cattel the children of Israel tooke for their praye, but they smote euery man with the edge of the sword vntill they had destroyed them, not leauing one aliue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 1:7; Exodus 34:11–13; Acts 20:20"> As the Lord had commanded Moses his seruant, so did Moses commande Ioshua, and so did Ioshua: he left nothing vndone of all that the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 10:41; Joshua 11:21; Joshua 12:8"> So Ioshua tooke all this land of the mountaines, and all the South, and all the lande of Goshen, and the lowe countrey, and the plaine, and the mountaine of Israel, and the lowe countrey of the same,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:24; Joshua 11:3; Joshua 13:5"> From the mount Halak, that goeth vp to Seir, euen vnto Baal-gad in the valley of Lebanon, vnder mount Hermon: and all their Kings he tooke, and smote them, and slewe them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 14:7–10; Joshua 11:23"> Ioshua made warre long time with all those Kings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 9:3–27"> Neither was there any citie that made peace with the children of Israel, saue those Hiuites that inhabited Gibeon: all other they tooke by battell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Deuteronomy 20:16–17; Exodus 4:21"> For it came of the Lord, to harden their heartes that they shoulde come against Israel in battell to the intent that they shoulde destroye them vtterly, and shewe them no mercie, but that they shoulde bring them to nought: as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:2; Numbers 13:22–23; Joshua 15:13–14"> And that same season came Ioshua, and destroyed the Anakims out of the mountaines: as out of Hebron, out of Debir, out of Anab, and out of all the mountaines of Iudah, and out of all the mountaines of Israel: Ioshua destroyed them vtterly with their cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:4; 1 Chronicles 18:1; Joshua 15:46"> There was no Anakim left in the lande of the children of Israel: onely in Azzah, in Gath, and in Ashdod were they left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 21:44–45; Numbers 26:52–55; Joshua 14:1–15"> So Ioshua tooke the whole land, according to all that the Lord had saide vnto Moses: and Ioshua gaue it for an inheritance vnto Israel according to their portion through their tribes: then the land was at rest without warre.</span>
