<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:2; Joshua 3:17"> And when all the people were wholy gone ouer Iorden, (after the Lord had spoken vnto Ioshua, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 3:12; Numbers 1:4–15; 1 Kings 18:31"> Take you twelue me out of the people, out of euery tribe a man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 4:19–20; Joshua 3:13; Joshua 4:8"> And command you them, saying, Take you hence out of the middes of Iorden, out of the place where the Priestes stoode in a readinesse, twelue stones, which ye shall take away with you, and leaue them in the lodging where you shall lodge this night)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 4:2; Mark 3:14–19"> Then Ioshua called the twelue men, whome he had prepared of the children of Israel, out of euery tribe a man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Ioshua said vnto them, Go ouer before the Arke of the Lord your God, euen through the middes of Iorden, and take vp euery man of you a stone vpon his shoulder according vnto the nomber of the tribes of the children of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Joshua 4:21; Psalms 44:1"> That this may bee a signe among you, that whe your children shall aske their fathers in time to come, saying, What meane you by these stones?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Numbers 16:40; Exodus 28:12"> Then ye may answere them, That the waters of Iorden were cut off before the Arke of the couenant of the Lord: for when it passed through Iorden, the waters of Iorden were cut off: therefore these stones are a memoriall vnto the children of Israel for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 4:2–5; Joshua 1:16–18; Joshua 4:20"> Then ye children of Israel did euen so as Ioshua had commanded, and tooke vp twelue stones out of the mids of Iorden as ye Lord had said vnto Ioshua, according to the nomber of the tribes of the children of Israel, and caried them away with them vnto the lodging, and layd them down there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:12; Judges 1:26; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> And Ioshua set vp twelue stones in the middes of Iorden, in the place where the feete of the Priests, which bare the Arke of the couenant stood, and there haue they continued vnto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 3:13; Psalms 119:60; Proverbs 27:1"> So the Priests, which bare ye Arke, stoode in the middes of Iorden, vntill euery thing was finished that ye Lord had comanded Ioshua to say vnto the people, according to all that Moses charged Ioshua: then the people hasted and went ouer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 3:8; Joshua 4:18; Joshua 3:17"> When all the people were cleane passed ouer, the Arke of the Lord went ouer also, and the Priests before the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–32; Joshua 1:14"> And the sonnes of Reuben, and the sonnes of Gad, and halfe the tribe of Manasseh went ouer before the children of Israel armed, as Moses had charged them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 6:11; Jeremiah 39:5; Jeremiah 52:8"> Euen fourty thousand prepared for warre, went before the Lord vnto battel, into ye plaine of Iericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 3:7; 2 Chronicles 30:12; 1 Kings 3:28"> That day the Lord magnified Ioshua in the sight of all Israel, and they feared him, as they feared Moses all dayes of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the Lord spake vnto Ioshua, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Exodus 25:16–22; Joshua 3:3–6"> Commande the Priests that beare ye Arke of the testimonie, to come vp out of Iorden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 8:16–18; Acts 16:35–39; Acts 16:23"> Ioshua therefore commanded the Priests, saying, Come ye vp out of Iorden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 3:15; 1 Chronicles 12:15; Joshua 3:13"> And when the Priests that bare the Arke of the couenant of ye Lord were come vp out of the middes of Iorden, and assoone as the soles of the Priests feete were set on the dry land, the waters of Iorde returned vnto their place, and flowed ouer all the bankes thereof, as they did before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 5:9; Micah 6:5; Exodus 12:2–3"> So the people came vp out of Iorden the tenth day of the first moneth, and pitched in Gilgal, in the Eastside of Iericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 4:3; Joshua 4:8"> Also the twelue stones, which they tooke out of Iorden, did Ioshua pitch in Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 4:6; Psalms 145:4–7; Psalms 105:2–5"> And he spake vnto ye childre of Israel, saying, When your children shall aske their fathers in time to come, and say, What meane these stones?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 3:17; Isaiah 51:10; Isaiah 44:27"> Then ye shall shew your children, and say, Israel came ouer this Iorden on dry land:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Isaiah 43:16; Isaiah 63:12–14"> For the Lord your God dryed vp ye waters of Iorden before you, vntill ye were gone ouer, as the Lord your God did the red Sea, which hee dryed vp before vs, till we were gone ouer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 106:8; Psalms 89:13; 1 Kings 8:42–43"> That all the people of the worlde may know that the hand of the Lord is mightie, that ye might feare the Lord your God continually.</span>
