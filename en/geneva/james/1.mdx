<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:15; 1 Peter 1:1; John 7:35"> Iames a seruant of God, and of the Lord Iesus Christ, to the twelue Tribes, which are scattered abroade, salutation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 1:6–8; James 1:12; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> My brethren, count it exceeding ioy, when ye fall into diuers tentations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 5:3–4; Hebrews 10:36; Colossians 1:11"> Knowing that ye trying of your faith bringeth forth patience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:19; 1 Peter 5:10; Matthew 5:48"> And let patience haue her perfect worke, that ye may be perfect and entier, lacking nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 2:3–6; John 15:7; Proverbs 3:5–7"> If any of you lacke wisedome, let him aske of God, which giueth to all men liberally, and reprocheth no man, and it shalbe giuen him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:21–22; Hebrews 11:6; Mark 11:22–24"> But let him aske in faith, and wauer not: for hee that wauereth, is like a waue of the sea, tost of the winde, and caried away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 4:3; Isaiah 58:3–4; Proverbs 21:27"> Neither let that man thinke that hee shall receiue any thing of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; Matthew 6:24; James 4:8"> A double minded man is vnstable in all his waies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; James 2:5–6; Proverbs 19:1"> Let the brother of lowe degree reioyce in that he is exalted:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; 1 John 2:17; Job 14:2"> Againe hee that is rich, in that hee is made lowe: for as the flower of the grasse, shall he vanish away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7–8; Psalms 37:35–36; Psalms 49:6–14"> For as when the sunne riseth with heate, then the grasse withereth, and his flower falleth away, and the goodly shape of it perisheth: euen so shall the rich man wither away in all his waies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 1 Peter 1:6–8; Revelation 2:10"> Blessed is ye man, that endureth tentation: for when he is tried, hee shall receiue the crowne of life, which the Lord hath promised to them that loue him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:12–13; James 1:12; Romans 9:19–20"> Let no man say when hee is tempted, I am tempted of God: for God can not bee tempted with euill, neither tempteth he any man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 7:21–22; Ephesians 4:22; Matthew 15:18"> But euery man is tempted, when hee is drawen away by his owne concupiscence, and is entised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Job 15:35; Romans 5:12–21"> Then when lust hath conceiued, it bringeth foorth sinne, and sinne when it is finished, bringeth foorth death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 6:7; James 1:19; Philippians 2:12"> Erre not, my deare brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 3:27; Matthew 7:11; Hebrews 13:8"> Euery good giuing, and euery perfect gift is from aboue, and commeth downe from the Father of lights, with whome is no variablenes, neither shadow of turning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:29–31; 1 Peter 1:23; John 3:3–5"> Of his owne will begate hee vs with the woorde of trueth, that we shoulde be as the first fruites of his creatures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 17:27; Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 14:29"> Wherefore my deare brethren, let euery man be swift to heare, slowe to speake, and slowe to wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24–25; Ephesians 4:26; James 3:17–18"> For the wrath of man doeth not accomplish the righteousnesse of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 4:22; Ephesians 1:13; 1 Peter 2:11"> Wherefore lay apart all filthinesse, and superfluitie of maliciousnesse, and receiue with meekenes the word that is graffed in you, which is able to saue your soules.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 2:13; Luke 6:46–48; John 13:17"> And be ye doers of the word, and not hearers onely, deceiuing your owne selues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 7:26–27; Ezekiel 33:31–32; James 2:14–26"> For if any heare the woorde, and doe it not, he is like vnto a man, that beholdeth his naturall face in a glasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Peter 3:11; Luke 1:66; Luke 7:39"> For when he hath considered himselfe, hee goeth his way, and forgetteth immediately what maner of one he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:28; John 13:17; 2 Corinthians 3:17–18"> But who so looketh in the perfect Lawe of libertie, and continueth therein, hee not being a forgetful hearer, but a doer of the woorke, shalbe blessed in his deede.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Psalms 141:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> If any man amog you seeme religious, and refraineth not his tongue, but deceiueth his owne heart, this mans religion is vaine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–17; 1 John 3:17–19; Galatians 6:9–10"> Pure religion and vndefiled before God, euen the Father, is this, to visite the fatherlesse, and widdowes in their aduersitie, and to keepe himselfe vnspotted of the world.</span>
