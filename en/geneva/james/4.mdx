<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Galatians 5:17; Romans 7:23"> From whence are warres and contentions among you? are they not hence, euen of your pleasures, that fight in your members?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 16:24; Matthew 7:7–8; James 1:5"> Ye lust, and haue not: ye enuie, and desire immoderately, and cannot obtaine: ye fight and warre, and get nothing, because ye aske not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 5:14; 1 John 3:22; James 1:6–7"> Ye aske, and receiue not, because ye aske amisse, that ye might lay the same out on your pleasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Romans 8:7; John 15:19"> Ye adulterers and adulteresses, knowe ye not that the amitie of the world is the enimitie of God? Whosoeuer therefore will be a friend of the world, maketh himselfe the enemie of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 6:19; Numbers 11:29"> Doe ye thinke that the Scripture sayeth in vaine, The spirit that dwelleth in vs, lusteth after enuie?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 3:34; 1 Peter 5:5"> But the Scripture offereth more grace, and therefore sayth, God resisteth the proude, and giueth grace to the humble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–9; Ephesians 4:27; Ephesians 6:11–12"> Submit your selues to God: resist the deuill, and he will flee from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; Hebrews 10:22; Malachi 3:7"> Drawe neere to God, and he will drawe neere to you. Clense your handes, ye sinners, and purge your hearts, ye double minded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10–11; Matthew 5:4; Luke 6:25"> Suffer afflictions, and sorrowe ye, and weepe: let your laughter be turned into mourning, and your ioy into heauinesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 147:6; Matthew 23:12; 1 Peter 5:6"> Cast downe your selues before the Lord, and he will lift you vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 2:1; Romans 2:1; James 5:9"> Speake not euill one of another, brethren. He that speaketh euill of his brother, or he that condemneth his brother, speaketh euill of ye Law, and condemneth the Lawe: and if thou condemnest the Lawe, thou art not an obseruer of the Lawe, but a iudge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 14:4; Romans 2:1; Matthew 10:28"> There is one Lawgiuer, which is able to saue, and to destroy. Who art thou that iudgest another man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Luke 12:17–20; Genesis 11:3–4"> Goe to now ye that say, To day or to morowe we will goe into such a citie, and continue there a yeere, and bye and sell, and get gaine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; Psalms 39:5; 1 John 2:17"> (And yet ye cannot tell what shalbe to morowe. For what is your life? It is euen a vapour that appeareth for a litle time, and afterward vanisheth away)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Hebrews 6:3; Lamentations 3:37"> For that ye ought to say, If the Lord will, and, if we liue, we will doe this or that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; 1 Corinthians 5:6; Psalms 52:7"> But now ye reioyce in your boastings: all such reioycing is euill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:41; John 13:17; John 15:22"> Therefore, to him that knoweth how to doe well, and doeth it not, to him it is sinne.</span>
