<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 112:1–3; Galatians 3:9; Matthew 6:33"> Nowe Abraham was olde, and striken in yeeres, and the Lord had blessed Abraham in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 47:29; Genesis 39:4–6; Genesis 15:2"> Therefore Abraham saide vnto his eldest seruant of his house, which had the rule ouer all that he had, Put nowe thine hand vnder my thigh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 28:8"> And I will make thee sweare by ye Lord God of the heauen, and God of the earth, that thou shalt not take a wife vnto my sonne of the daughters of the Canaanites among who I dwel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:2; Genesis 22:20–23; Genesis 12:7"> But thou shalt go vnto my countrey, and to my kinred, and take a wife vnto my sone Izhak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:2; Exodus 9:2; Exodus 20:7"> And the seruant saide to him, What if the woman will not come with me to this land? shall I bring thy sonne againe vnto the lande from whence thou camest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 5:1; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Hebrews 10:39"> To whom Abraham answered, Beware that thou bring not my sonne thither againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 33:2; Genesis 13:15; Hebrews 1:14"> The Lord God of heauen, who tooke me from my fathers house, and from the land where I was borne, and that spake vnto me, and that sware vnto me, saying, Vnto thy seede wil I giue this land, he shall send his Angel before thee, and thou shalt take a wife vnto my sonne from thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 2:17–20; Numbers 30:5; Numbers 30:8"> Neuertheles if the woman wil not follow thee, then shalt thou bee discharged of this mine othe: onely bring not my sonne thither againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 24:2"> Then the seruant put his hand vnder the thigh of Abraham his master, and sware to him for this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 11:31; Deuteronomy 23:4; Genesis 27:43"> So the seruant tooke ten camels of the camels of his master, and departed: (for he had all his masters goods in his hand:) and so he arose, and went to Aram Naharaim, vnto the citie of Nahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:11; Exodus 2:16; John 4:7"> And he made his camels to lye downe without the citie by a well of water, at euentide about the time that the women come out to draw water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 24:27; Exodus 3:6; Genesis 26:24"> And he said, O Lord God of my master Abraham, I beseech thee, send me good speede this day, and shew mercy vnto my master Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 24:43; Psalms 37:5; Exodus 2:16"> Lo, I stand by the well of water, whiles the mens daughters of this citie come out to drawe water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 6:37; Judges 6:17; Genesis 15:8"> Graunt therefore that ye maide, to whom I say, Bowe downe thy pitcher, I pray thee, that I may drinke: if she say, Drinke, and I will giue thy camels drinke also: may be she that thou hast ordeined for thy seruant Izhak: and thereby shall I know that thou hast shewed mercy on my master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 11:29; Genesis 24:45; Isaiah 65:24"> And nowe yer he had left speaking, beholde, Rebekah came out, the daughter of Bethuel, sonne of Milcah the wife of Nahor Abrahams brother, and her pitcher vpon her shoulder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Numbers 31:17–18; Genesis 39:6"> (And the maide was very faire to looke vpon, a virgine and vnknowen of man) and she went downe to the well, and filled her pitcher, and came vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:7; Isaiah 21:14; Genesis 26:1–35"> Then the seruant ranne to meete her, and said, Let me drinke, I pray thee, a litle water of thy pitcher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 31:26; 1 Peter 3:8; Genesis 24:14"> And she said, Drinke sir: and she hasted, and let downe her pitcher vpon her hand and gaue him drinke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 24:14; 1 Peter 4:9; Genesis 24:45–46"> And when she had giuen him drinke, she said, I will drawe water for thy camels also vntill they haue drunken inough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And she powred out her pitcher into the trough speedily; and ranne againe vnto the well to drawe water, and she drewe for all his camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:43; Genesis 24:12; 2 Samuel 7:18–20"> So the man wondred at her, and helde his peace, to knowe whether the Lord had made his iourney prosperous or not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:11–12; Exodus 32:2–3; Genesis 23:15–16"> And when the camels had left drinking, the man tooke a golden abillement of halfe a shekell weight, and two bracelets for her hands, of ten shekels weight of golde:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And he said, Whose daughter art thou? tell me, I pray thee, Is there roume in thy fathers house for vs to lodge in?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 24:15; Genesis 22:23; Genesis 22:20"> Then she said to him, I am the daughter of Bethuel the sonne of Milcah whom she bare vnto Nahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 32:8; 1 Peter 4:9; Judges 19:19–21"> Moreouer she said vnto him, We haue litter also and prouender ynough, and roume to lodge in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 24:48; Exodus 4:31; Genesis 24:52"> And the man bowed himselfe and worshipped the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 24:48; Genesis 24:12; Psalms 98:3"> And said, Blessed be the Lord God of my master Abraham, which hath not withdrawen his mercie and his trueth from my master: for when I was in the way, the Lord brought me to my masters brethrens house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 24:67; Genesis 24:55; Genesis 31:33"> And the maide ranne and tolde them of her mothers house according to these wordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 29:5; Genesis 29:13; Genesis 24:55"> Now Rebekah had a brother called Laban, and Laban ranne vnto the man to the well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For when he had seene the earings and the bracelets in his sisters hands, and when he heard the wordes of Rebekah his sister, saying, Thus said the man vnto me, then he went to the man, and loe, he stoode by the camels at the well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ruth 3:10; Genesis 26:29; Psalms 115:15"> And he saide, Come in thou blessed of the Lord: wherefore standest thou without, seeing I haue prepared the house, and roume for ye camels?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 19:21; Genesis 43:24; Genesis 18:4"> Then the man came into the house, and he vnsadled the camels, and brought litter and prouender for the camels, and water to wash his feete, and the mens feete that were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 132:3–5; 1 Timothy 6:2; Ephesians 6:5–8"> Afterward the meate was set before him: but he saide, I will not eate, vntill I haue saide my message: And he said, Speake on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Then he said, I am Abrahams seruant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 24:1; Genesis 13:2; Proverbs 10:22"> And the Lord hath blessed my master wonderfully, that he is become great: for he hath giuen him sheepe, and beeues, and siluer, and golde, and men seruants, and maide seruants, and camels, and asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 25:5; Genesis 21:10; Genesis 21:1–7"> And Sarah my masters wife hath borne a sonne to my master, when she was olde, and vnto him hath he giuen all that he hath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 27:46; Ezra 9:1–3; Genesis 24:2–9"> Now my master made me sweare, saying, Thou shalt not take a wife to my sonne of the daughters of the Canaanites, in whose land I dwell:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 24:4; Genesis 31:19; Genesis 12:1"> But thou shalt go vnto my fathers house and to my kinred, and take a wife vnto my sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 24:5"> Then I saide vnto my master, What if the woman will not follow me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Psalms 16:8; Genesis 5:24"> Who answered me, The Lord, before who I walke, will send his Angel with thee, and prosper thy iourney, and thou shalt take a wife for my sonne of my kinred and my fathers house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Genesis 24:8; Deuteronomy 29:12"> Then shalt thou be discharged of mine othe, when thou commest to my kinred: and if they giue thee not one, thou shalt be free from mine othe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11; Genesis 24:12–14; Ezra 8:21"> So I came this day to the well, and said, O Lord, the God of my master Abraham, if thou nowe prosper my iourney which I goe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 24:13–14"> Behold, I stand by the well of water: when a virgine commeth forth to drawe water, and I say to her, Giue me, I pray thee, a litle water of thy pitcher to drinke,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 24:14; 1 Peter 3:8; Genesis 2:22"> And she say to me, Drinke thou, and I will also drawe for thy camels, let her be ye wife, which the Lord hath prepared for my masters sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 7:7; Acts 12:12–17; Genesis 24:15–20"> And before I had made an end of speaking in mine heart, beholde, Rebekah came foorth, and her pitcher on her shoulder, and she went downe vnto the well, and drewe water. Then I said vnto her, Giue me drinke, I pray thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Genesis 24:18–19"> And she made haste, and tooke downe her pitcher from her shoulder, and said, Drinke, and I will giue thy camels drinke also. So I dranke, and she gaue the camels drinke also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 45:13–14; Genesis 24:22–24; Isaiah 62:3–5"> Then I asked her, and said, Whose daughter art thou? And she answered, The daughter of Bethuel Nahors sonne, whom Milcah bare vnto him. Then I put the abillement vpon her face, and the bracelets vpon her hands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Genesis 24:26–27; Psalms 48:14"> And I bowed downe and worshipped the Lord, and blessed the Lord God of my master Abraham, which had brought me the right way to take my masters brothers daughter vnto his sone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Genesis 47:29; Joshua 2:14; Proverbs 3:3"> Now therefore, if ye will deale mercifully and truely with my master, tell me: and if not, tell me that I may turne me to the right hand or to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 118:23; Genesis 31:24; Genesis 31:29"> Then answered Laban and Bethuel, and said, This thing is proceeded of the Lord: we cannot therefore say vnto thee, neither euill nor good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 24:15; 2 Samuel 16:10"> Beholde, Rebehak is before thee, take her and goe, that she may be thy masters sonnes wife, euen as the Lord hath said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Genesis 24:26; Genesis 24:48; Psalms 116:1–2"> And when Abrahams seruant heard their wordes, he bowed himselfe toward the earth vnto the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 24:22; Genesis 24:10; Exodus 12:35"> Then the seruant tooke foorth iewels of siluer, and iewels of golde, and raiment, and gaue to Rebekah: also vnto her brother and to her mother he gaue gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Genesis 24:56; Genesis 24:59; Genesis 28:5–6"> Afterward they did eate and drinke, both he, and the men that were with him, and taried all night. and when they rose vp in the morning, he said, Let me depart vnto my master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Genesis 4:3; Leviticus 25:29; Judges 14:8"> Then her brother and her mother answered, Let the maide abide with vs, at the least ten dayes: then shall she goe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Proverbs 25:25; Joshua 1:8; Isaiah 48:15"> But he said vnto them, Hinder you me not, seeing the Lord hath prospered my iourney: send me away, that I may goe to my master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> Then they said, We will call the maide, and aske her consent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 1:38; Psalms 45:10–11"> And they called Rebekah, and saide vnto her, Wilt thou go with this man? And she answered, I will go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Genesis 35:8; Numbers 11:12; Genesis 24:50"> So they let Rebekah their sister goe, and her nourse, with Abrahams seruant and his men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 17:16; Ruth 4:11–12"> And they blessed Rebekah, and sayde vnto her, Thou art our sister, growe into thousande thousands, and thy seede possesse the gate of his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Psalms 45:10; Genesis 2:24; Esther 8:14"> Then Rebekah arose, and her maydes, and rode vpon the camels, and followed the man. and the seruant tooke Rebekah, and departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Genesis 25:11; Genesis 16:14; Genesis 12:9"> Nowe Izhak came from the way of Beer-lahai-roi, (for he dwelt in the South countrey)</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Psalms 119:15; Psalms 1:2; Psalms 104:34"> And Izhak went out. to pray in the fielde toward the euening: who lift vp his eyes and looked, and behold, the camels came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Judges 1:14; Joshua 15:18"> Also Rebekah lift vp her eyes, and when she saw Izhak, she lighted downe from the camel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9; 1 Corinthians 11:5–6; 1 Corinthians 11:10"> (For shee had sayde to the seruant, Who is yonder man, that commeth in the fielde to meete vs? and the seruant had said, It is my master) So she tooke a vaile, and couered her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Mark 6:30"> And the seruant tolde Izhak all things, that he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:2; Ephesians 5:22–33; Genesis 23:1–2"> Afterward Izhak brought her into the tent of Sarah his mother, and he tooke Rebekah, and she was his wife, and he loued her: So Izhak was comforted after his mothers death.</span>
