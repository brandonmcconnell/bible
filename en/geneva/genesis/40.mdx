<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 40:13; Genesis 39:20–23; Esther 6:1"> And after these things, the butler of the King of Egypt and his baker offended their lorde the King of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 16:14; Proverbs 19:12; Psalms 76:10"> And Pharaoh was angrie against his two officers, against the chiefe butler, and against the chiefe baker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 39:20; Genesis 39:23"> Therefore he put them in ward in his chiefe stewardes house, in the prison and place where Ioseph was bound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 39:1; Psalms 37:5; Genesis 37:36"> And the chiefe steward gaue Ioseph charge ouer them, and he serued them: and they continued a season in warde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 41:11; Genesis 40:8; Genesis 37:5–10"> And they both dreamed a dreame, eyther of them his dreame in one night, eche one according to the interpretation of his dreame, both the butler and the baker of the King of Egypt, which were bounde in the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 41:8; Daniel 2:1–3; Genesis 40:8"> And when Ioseph came in vnto them in the morning, and looked vpon them, beholde, they were sad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:2; 1 Samuel 1:8; Luke 24:17"> And he asked Pharaohs officers, that were with him in his masters warde, saying, Wherefore looke ye so sadly to day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 2:47; Genesis 41:15–16; Daniel 2:28"> Who answered him, We haue dreamed, eche one a dreame, and there is none to interprete the same. Then Ioseph saide vnto them, Are not interpretations of God? tell them me nowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 7:13–15; Genesis 37:5–10; Daniel 4:8"> So the chiefe butler tolde his dreame to Ioseph, and said vnto him, In my dreame, behold, a vine was before me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And in the vine were three branches, and as it budded, her flowre came foorth: and the clusters of grapes waxed ripe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11–1; 1 Kings 10:5; 2 Chronicles 9:4"> And I had Pharaohs cup in mine hande, and I tooke the grapes, and wrung the into Pharaohs cup, and I gaue the cup into Pharaohs hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 41:12; Judges 7:14; Genesis 41:25–26"> Then Ioseph sayde vnto him, This is the interpretation of it: The three braunches are three dayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 3:3; Jeremiah 52:31; 2 Kings 25:27"> Within three dayes shall Pharaoh lift vp thine head, and restore thee vnto thine office, and thou shalt giue Pharaohs cup into his hand after the olde maner, when thou wast his butler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Luke 23:42; 1 Samuel 20:14–15"> But haue me in remembrance with thee, when thou art in good case, and shew mercie, I pray thee, vnto me, and make mention of me to Pharaoh, that thou mayest bring me out of this house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17–18; Genesis 39:20; 1 Timothy 1:10"> For I was stollen away by theft out of the land of the Ebrewes, and here also haue I done nothing, wherefore they should put mee in the dungeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 40:1–2"> And when the chiefe baker sawe that the interpretation was good, hee saide vnto Ioseph, Also mee thought in my dreame that I had three white baskets on mine head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 49:20; 1 Chronicles 12:20"> And in the vppermost basket there was of all maner baken meates for Pharaoh: and the birdes did eate them out of the basket vpon mine head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 40:12; Genesis 41:26; 1 Corinthians 11:24"> Then Ioseph answered, and saide, This is the interpretation thereof: The three baskets are three dayes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 40:22; Acts 20:27; 2 Samuel 21:6"> Within three dayes shall Pharaoh take thine head from thee, and shall hang thee on a tree, and the birdes shall eate thy flesh from off thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 40:19; Genesis 40:13; Matthew 14:6"> And so the third day, which was Pharaohs birthday, hee made a feast vnto all his seruants: and hee lifted vp the head of the chiefe butler, and the head of the chiefe baker among his seruants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 40:13; Nehemiah 2:1"> And he restored the chiefe butler vnto his butlershippe, who gaue the cup into Pharaohs hande,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 40:19; Genesis 41:16; Daniel 2:30"> But he hanged the chiefe baker, as Ioseph had interpreted vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 31:12; Job 19:14; Psalms 105:19"> Yet the chiefe butler did not remember Ioseph, but forgate him.</span>
