<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Acts 7:11–13; Lamentations 5:10"> Now great famine was in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–8; Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 15:16"> And when they had eaten vp the vitaile, which they had brought from Egypt, their father sayd vnto them, Turne againe, and bye vs a little foode.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 44:23; Genesis 43:5; 2 Samuel 3:13"> And Iudah answered him, saying, The man charged vs by an othe, saying, Neuer see my face, except your brother be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> If thou wilt sende our brother with vs, we will goe downe, and bye thee foode:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Genesis 44:26; Genesis 42:38"> But if thou wilt not send him, we wil not go downe: for the man said vnto vs, Looke me not in the face, except your brother be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Israel sayd, Wherefore delt ye so euill with me, as to tell the man, whether ye had yet a brother or no?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 43:3; Genesis 42:13; Genesis 43:27"> And they answered, The man asked straitly of our selues and of our kinred, saying, Is your father yet aliue? haue ye any brother? And wee tolde him according to these wordes: could we knowe certainely that he would say, Bring your brother downe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 42:2; Deuteronomy 33:6; Genesis 45:19"> Then sayde Iudah to Israel his father, Send the boy with mee, that we may rise and goe, and that we may liue and not dye, both we, and thou, and our children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 42:37; Philemon 1:18–19; Hebrews 7:22"> I wil be suertie for him: of mine hand shalt thou require him. If I bring him not to thee, and set him before thee, then let me beare the blame for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 19:16"> For except we had made this tarying, doutlesse by this we had returned the second time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 37:25; Jeremiah 8:22; Ezekiel 27:17"> Then their father Israel sayd vnto them, If it must needes be so now, do thus: take of the best fruites of the lande in your vessels, and bring the man a present, a little rosen, and a little hony, spices and myrrhe, nuttes, and almondes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:25; Genesis 42:35; 1 Thessalonians 4:6"> And take double money in your hande, and the money, that was brought againe in your sackes mouthes: cary it againe in your hand, lest it were some ouersight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 42:38"> Take also your brother and arise, and go againe to the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 4:16; Genesis 17:1; Acts 21:14"> And God almightie giue you mercie in the sight of the man, that hee may deliuer you your other brother, and Beniamin: but I shall be robbed of my childe, as I haue bene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Thus the men tooke this present, and tooke twise so much money in their hande with Beniamin, and rose vp, and went downe to Egypt and stoode before Ioseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 44:1; Genesis 43:19; Genesis 24:2–10"> And whe Ioseph saw Beniamin with them, he sayde to his stewarde, Bring these men home and kill meate, and make ready: for the men shall eate with me at noone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the man did as Ioseph bad, and brought the men vnto Iosephs house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 42:35; Romans 7:8; Genesis 42:21"> Nowe when the men were brought into Iosephs house, they were afrayd, and sayd, Because of the money, that came in our sackes mouthes at the first time, are we brought, that hee may picke a quarrell against vs, and lay some thing to our charge, and bring vs in bondage and our asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Therefore came they to Iosephs stewarde, and communed with him at the doore of ye house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 42:3; Genesis 42:10; Genesis 42:27"> And said, Oh syr, we came in deede down hither at the first time to bye foode,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 43:12; Genesis 42:27–35; Romans 13:8"> And as wee came to an ynne and opened our sackes, behold, euery mans money was in his sackes mouth, euen our money in full weight, but we haue brought it againe in our handes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Also other money haue we brought in our handes to bye foode, but we cannot tell, who put our money in our sackes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 20:26; Genesis 42:24; John 14:27"> And he said, Peace be vnto you, feare not: your God and the God of your father hath giuen you that treasure in your sackes, I had your money: and he brought forth Simeon to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 18:4; Genesis 24:32; Luke 7:44"> So the man led them into Iosephs house, and gaue them water to wash their feete, and gaue their asses prouender.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 43:11; Genesis 43:16"> And they made ready their present against Ioseph came at noone, (for they heard say, that they should eate bread there)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Philippians 2:10–11; Genesis 27:29; Genesis 42:6"> When Ioseph came home, they brought the present into the house to him, which was in their handes, and bowed downe to the grounde before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 42:13; 1 Samuel 25:5; 1 Samuel 17:22"> And he asked them of their prosperitie, and sayd, Is your father the olde man, of whome ye tolde me, in good health? is he yet aliue?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 37:7; Genesis 43:26; 2 Samuel 14:4"> Who answered, Thy seruant our father is in good health, he is yet aliue: and they bowed downe, and made obeysance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 42:13; Numbers 6:25; Genesis 30:22–24"> And he lifting vp his eyes, beheld his brother Beniamin his mothers sonne, and sayde, Is this your yonger brother, of whome ye tolde me? And he said, God be merciful vnto thee, my sone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 42:24; Jeremiah 31:20; 1 Kings 3:26"> And Ioseph made haste (for his affection was inflamed towarde his brother, and sought where to weepe) and entred into his chamber, and wept there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 45:1; 1 Peter 3:10; Isaiah 42:14"> Afterward he washed his face, and came out, and refrained himselfe, and sayd, Set on meate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 46:34; Exodus 8:26; Genesis 43:16"> And they prepared for him by himselfe, and for them by themselues, and for the Egyptians, which did eate with him, by themselues, because the Egyptians might not eate bread with the Ebrewes: for that was an abomination vnto the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> So they sate before him: the eldest according vnto his age, and the yongest according vnto his youth. and the men marueiled among themselues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 45:22; 2 Samuel 11:8; 1 Samuel 1:5"> And they tooke meases from before him, and sent to them: but Beniamins mease was fiue times so much as any of theirs: and they drunke, and had of the best drinke with him.</span>
