<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 91:11; Psalms 34:7; Hebrews 1:4"> Nowe Iaakob went forth on his iourney and the Angels of God met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8; Joshua 5:14; Joshua 21:38"> And when Iaakob saw them, he said, This is Gods hoste, and called the name of the same place Mahanaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:30; Joshua 24:4; Deuteronomy 2:5"> Then Iaakob sent messengers before him to Esau his brother, vnto the land of Seir into the countrey of Edom:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:6; Luke 14:11; Genesis 33:8"> To whom he gaue commandement, saying, Thus shall ye speake to my lorde Esau: thy seruant Iaakob sayeth thus, I haue bene a stranger with Laban, and taried vnto this time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:8; Genesis 33:15; Genesis 33:11"> I haue beeues also and Asses, sheepe, and men seruantes, and women seruantes, and haue sent to shew my lord, that I may find grace in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 33:1; Genesis 32:8; Genesis 32:11"> So ye messengers came againe to Iaakob, saying, We came vnto thy brother Esau, and hee also commeth against thee and foure hundreth men with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:3; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10; Exodus 14:10"> Then Iaakob was greatly afraid, and was sore troubled, and deuided the people that was with him, and the sheepe, and the beeues, and the camels into two companies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; Genesis 33:1–3"> For he said, If Esau come to ye one company and smite it, the other companie shall escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:42; Psalms 34:4–6; Genesis 31:3"> Moreouer Iaakob said, O God of my father Abraham, and God of my father Izhak: Lord, which saydest vnto me, Returne vnto thy coutrey and to thy kinred, and I will do thee good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 18:35; Genesis 24:27; 2 Samuel 7:18"> I am not worthy of the least of all the mercies, and al the trueth, which thou hast shewed vnto thy seruant: for with my staffe came I ouer this Iorden, and now haue I gotte two bads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 59:1–2; Proverbs 18:19; Matthew 6:13"> I pray thee, Deliuer me from the hande of my brother, from the hande of Esau: for I feare him, least he will come and smite me, and the mother vpon the children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–15; Hebrews 6:17; Titus 1:2"> For thou saydest; I will surely doe thee good, and make thy seede as the sande of the sea, which can not be nombred for multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 18:16; Genesis 43:11; Proverbs 19:6"> And he taryed there the same night, and tooke of that which came to had, a present for Esau his brother:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 1:3; Deuteronomy 8:18; Job 42:12"> Two hundreth shee goates and twenty hee goates, two hundreth ewes and twentie rammes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Thirtie mylche camels with their coltes, fourtie kine, and ten bullockes, twentie she asses and ten foles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 33:8–9; Genesis 32:20; Proverbs 2:11"> So he deliuered them into the hande of his seruants, euery droue by themselues, and saide vnto his seruants, Passe before me, and put a space betweene droue and droue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 33:3"> And he commanded the formost, saying, If Esau my brother meete thee, and aske thee, saying, Whose seruant art thou? And whither goest thou? And whose are these before thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 32:4–5"> Then thou shalt say, They be thy seruant Iaakobs: it is a present sent vnto my lord Esau: and beholde, he him selfe also is behinde vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> So likewise commanded he the seconde and the thirde, and all that followed the droues, saying, After this maner, ye shall speake vnto Esau, when ye finde him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 42:8–9; Proverbs 21:14; Genesis 43:11"> And ye shall say moreouer, Beholde, thy seruant Iaakob commeth after vs (for he thought, I will appease his wrath with the present that goeth before me, and afterwarde I will see his face: it may be that he will accept me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So went the present before him: but he taried that night with the companie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:16; Deuteronomy 2:37; 1 Timothy 5:8"> And he rose vp the same night, and tooke his two wiues, and his two maides, and his eleuen children, and went ouer the forde Iabbok.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And he tooke them, and sent them ouer the riuer, and sent ouer that he had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 12:3–5; Ephesians 6:12; Luke 13:24"> Now when Iaakob was left him selfe alone, there wrestled a man with him vnto the breaking of the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7–9; Hosea 12:3–4; Matthew 26:41"> And he sawe that he could not preuaile against him: therefore he touched the holowe of his thigh, and the holowe of Iaakobs thigh was loosed, as he wrestled with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 12:4; Psalms 115:12–13; 2 Corinthians 12:8–9"> And he saide, Let me goe, for the morning appeareth. Who answered, I will not let thee go except thou blesse me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Then said he vnto him, What is thy name? And he said, Iaakob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 2:17; Genesis 35:10; Isaiah 62:2–4"> Then said he, Thy name shalbe called Iaakob no more, but Israel: because thou hast had power with God, thou shalt also preuaile with men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Judges 13:16–18; Job 11:7"> Then Iaakob demaded, saying, Tell me, I pray thee, thy name. And he said, Wherefore now doest thou aske my name? and he blessed him there</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 24:10–11; Numbers 12:8; Isaiah 6:5"> And Iaakob called the name of the place, Peniel: for, saide he, I haue seene God face to face, and my life is preserued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7; Genesis 19:15; Judges 8:8"> And the sunne rose vp to him as he passed Peniel, and he halted vpon his thigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:5"> Therefore the children of Israel eate not of the sinewe that shranke in the hollowe of the thigh, vnto this day: because he touched the sinew that shranke in the holow of Iaakobs thigh.</span>
