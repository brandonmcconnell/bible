<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 12:6; Genesis 18:10; Genesis 18:14"> Nowe the Lord visited Sarah, as he had saide, and did vnto her according as he had promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 4:22; Hebrews 11:11; Genesis 18:10"> For Sarah conceiued, and bare Abraham a sonne in his olde age, at the same season that God tolde him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 17:19; Romans 9:7; Genesis 21:6"> And Abraham called his sonnes name that was borne vnto him, which Sarah bare him, Izhak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:8; Deuteronomy 12:32; John 7:22–23"> Then Abraham circumcised Izhak his sonne, when he was eight dayes olde, as God had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; Romans 4:19; Genesis 17:1"> So Abraham was an hundreth yeere olde, when his sonne Izhak was borne vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 126:2; Isaiah 54:1; Genesis 17:17"> Then Sarah said, God hath made me to reioyce: all that heare will reioyce with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 18:11–12; Isaiah 66:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:10"> Againe she said, Who would haue saide to Abraham, that Sarah shoulde haue giuen children sucke? for I haue borne him a sonne in his olde age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:22; Hosea 1:8; Genesis 40:20"> Then the childe grewe and was weaned: and Abraham made a great feast the same day that Izhak was weaned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 4:29; Genesis 16:15; Galatians 4:22"> And Sarah sawe the sonne of Hagar the Egyptian (which she had borne vnto Abraham) mocking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:35; 1 John 2:19; Galatians 4:22–31"> Wherefore she saide vnto Abraham, Cast out this bond woman and her sonne: for ye sonne of this bonde woman shall not be heire with my sonne Izhak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 12:11; Genesis 17:18; Matthew 10:37"> And this thing was very grieuous in Abrahams sight, because of his sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:18; Romans 9:7–8; Genesis 17:19"> But God said vnto Abraham, Let it not be grieuous in thy sight for the childe, and for thy bonde woman: in all that Sarah shall say vnto thee, heare her voyce: for in Izhak shall thy seede be called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 16:10; Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:18"> As for the sonne of the bond woman, I will make him a nation also, because he is thy seede.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Psalms 119:60; Genesis 16:7"> So Abraham arose vp early in ye morning, and tooke bread, and a bottell of water, and gaue it vnto Hagar, putting it on her shoulder, and the childe also, and sent her away: who departing wandred in the wildernesse of Beer-sheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 3:9; Psalms 63:1; Jeremiah 14:3"> And when the water of the bottell was spent, she cast the childe vnder a certaine tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Genesis 29:11; 1 Samuel 24:16"> Then she went and sate her ouer against him a farre off about a bowe shoote: for she said, I will not see the death of the child. and she sate downe ouer against him, and lift vp her voyce and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 16:11; Psalms 50:15; Exodus 3:7"> Then God heard the voyce of ye childe, and the Angel of God called to Hagar from heauen, and said vnto her, What aileth thee, Hagar? feare not, for God hath heard the voyce of the childe where he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 21:13; Genesis 16:10; Genesis 17:20"> Arise, take vp the childe, and holde him in thine hand: for I will make of him a great people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 22:31; 2 Kings 6:17–20; Isaiah 35:5–6"> And God opened her eyes, and she sawe a well of water. so she went and filled the bottell with water, and gaue the boy drinke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Genesis 39:21; Genesis 16:12"> So God was with the childe, and he grewe and dwelt in the wildernesse, and was an archer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 24:3–4; Numbers 12:16"> And he dwelt in the wildernesse of Paran, and his mother tooke him a wife out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 3:7; Genesis 26:26; Genesis 26:28"> And at that same time Abimelech and Phichol his chief captaine spake vnto Abraham, saying, God is with thee in all that thou doest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Genesis 20:14; Hebrews 6:16"> Nowe therefore sweare vnto me here by God, that thou wilt not hurt me, nor my children, nor my childrens children: thou shalt deale with me, and with the countrey, where thou hast bene a stranger, according vnto the kindnesse that I haue shewed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 6:16; Genesis 14:13; Romans 12:18"> Then Abraham said, I will sweare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 26:15–22; Genesis 29:8; Proverbs 25:9"> And Abraham rebuked Abimelech for a well of water, which Abimelechs seruants had violently taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 13:7; 2 Kings 5:20–24"> And Abimelech saide, I knowe not who hath done this thing: also thou toldest me not, neither heard I of it but this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 14:22–23; Isaiah 32:8; Romans 1:31"> Then Abraham tooke sheepe and beeues, and gaue them vnto Abimelech: and they two made a couenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Abraham set seuen lambes of the flocke by themselues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:14; Genesis 33:8; Exodus 12:26"> Then Abimelech said vnto Abraham, What meane these seuen lambes, which thou hast set by themselues?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 31:52; Joshua 24:27; Joshua 22:27–28"> And he answered, Because thou shalt receiue of mine hand these seuen lambes, that it may be a witnes vnto me, that I haue digged this well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 26:33; Genesis 21:14; 1 Kings 4:25"> Wherefore the place is called Beer-sheba, because there they both sware.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 13:17; Genesis 21:27; Judges 13:1"> Thus made they a couenant at Beer-sheba: afterward Abimelech and Phichol his chiefe captaine rose vp, and turned againe vnto the land of the Philistims.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Isaiah 40:28; Psalms 90:2"> And Abraham planted a groue in Beer-sheba, and called there on the Name of ye Lord, the euerlasting God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; 1 Peter 2:11; Hebrews 11:9"> And Abraham was a stranger in the Philistims land a long season.</span>
