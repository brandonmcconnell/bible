<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Psalms 105:17; Genesis 37:36"> Now Ioseph was brought downe into Egypt: and Potiphar an Eunuche of Pharaohs (and his chiefe stewarde an Egyptian) bought him at the hande of the Ishmeelites, which had brought him thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:14; Psalms 91:15; Jeremiah 15:20"> And the Lord was with Ioseph, and he was a man that prospered and was in the house of his master the Egyptian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Genesis 30:27; Matthew 5:16"> And his master sawe that the Lord was with him, and that the Lord made all that hee did to prosper in his hande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 39:21–22; Proverbs 27:18; Genesis 19:19"> So Ioseph founde fauour in his sight, and serued him: and he made him ruler of his house, and put all that he had in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; 2 Samuel 6:11–12; Acts 27:24"> And from that time that he had made him ruler ouer his house and ouer all that he had, the Lord blessed the Egyptians house for Iosephs sake: and the blessing of the Lord was vpon all that he had in the house, and in the fielde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12; Luke 19:17; Proverbs 31:11"> Therefore he left all that he had in Iosephs hand, and tooke accompt of nothing, that was with him, saue onely of the bread, which he did eate. And Ioseph was a faire person, and well fauoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:11; Ezekiel 16:34; 2 Peter 2:14"> Nowe therefore after these thinges, his masters wife cast her eyes vpon Ioseph, and saide, Lye with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 23:26–28; Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 9:13–18"> But he refused and said to his masters wife, Beholde, my master knoweth not what he hath in the house with me, but hath committed all that he hath to mine hande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13; Genesis 20:6; Genesis 42:18"> There is no man greater in this house then I: neither hath he kept any thing from me, but only thee, because thou art his wife: how then can I do this great wickednes and so sinne against God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Genesis 39:8; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> And albeit she spake to Ioseph day by day, yet he hearkened not vnto her, to lye with her, or to be in her company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; Ephesians 5:3; Malachi 3:5"> Then on a certaine day Ioseph entred into the house, to doe his businesse: and there was no man of the houshold in the house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:22; Proverbs 7:13–27; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> Therefore she caught him by his garmet, saying, Sleepe with me: but he left his garment in her hand and fled, and got him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Nowe when she sawe that he had left his garment in her hand, and was fled out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 10:21; 1 Peter 2:20; Psalms 35:11"> She called vnto the men of her house, and tolde them, saying, Beholde, he hath brought in an Ebrewe vnto vs to mocke vs: who came in to me for to haue slept with me: but I cryed with a loude voyce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And when he heard that I lift vp my voice and cryed, he left his garment with me, and fled away, and got him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:32; Jeremiah 4:22; Jeremiah 9:3–5"> So she layde vp his garment by her, vntill her lord came home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 23:1; 1 Kings 18:17; Psalms 37:14"> Then she tolde him according to these words, saying, The Ebrew seruat, which thou hast brought vnto vs, came in to me, to mocke me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But assoone as I lift vp my voyce and cried, he left his garment with me, and fled out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 4:5–6; Proverbs 18:17; Proverbs 6:34–35"> Then when his master heard the wordes of his wife, which she tolde him, saying, After this maner did thy seruant to me, his anger was kindled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 40:15; 2 Timothy 2:9; Psalms 105:18–19"> And Iosephs master tooke him and put him in prison, in the place, where the kings prisoners lay bounde: and there he was in prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 3:21; Isaiah 41:10; 1 Peter 3:17"> But the Lord was with Ioseph, and shewed him mercie, and got him fauour in the sight of the master of the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 40:3–4; Genesis 39:4; 1 Samuel 2:30"> And the keeper of the prison committed to Iosephs hande all the prisoners that were in the prison, and whatsoeuer they did there, that did he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Genesis 39:2–3; Psalms 1:3"> And the keeper of the prison looked vnto nothing that was vnder his hande, seeing that the Lord was with him: for whatsoeuer he did, the Lord made it to prosper.</span>
