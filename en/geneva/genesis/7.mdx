<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Of the foules also of the heauen by seuens, male and female, to keepe seede aliue vpon the whole earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 6:13; Genesis 7:12; Genesis 7:17"> For seuen dayes hence I will cause it raine vpon the earth fourtie dayes and fourtie nightes, and all the substance that I haue made, will I destroy from off the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 6:22; Luke 8:21; Exodus 39:32"> Noah therefore did according vnto all that the Lord commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 5:32; Genesis 8:13"> And Noah was sixe hundreth yeeres olde, when the flood of waters was vpon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 17:27; Genesis 6:18; Matthew 24:38"> So Noah entred and his sonnes, and his wife, and his sonnes wiues with him into the Arke, because of the waters of the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Of the cleane beastes, and of the vncleane beastes, and of the foules, and of all that creepeth vpon the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 7:16; Genesis 2:19; Jeremiah 8:7"> There came two and two vnto Noah into the Arke, male and female, as God had commanded Noah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 6:17; Genesis 7:4; Luke 17:27"> And so after seuen dayes the waters of the flood were vpon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 8:2; Malachi 3:10; Proverbs 8:28–29"> In the sixe hundreth yeere of Noahs life in the second moneth, the seuetenth day of the moneth, in the same day were all the fountaines of the great deepe broken vp, and the windowes of heauen were opened,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Matthew 4:2; Genesis 7:17"> And the raine was vpon the earth fourtie dayes and fourtie nightes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 6:18; Genesis 7:1; Genesis 6:10"> In the selfe same day entred Noah with Shem, and Ham and Iapheth, the sonnes of Noah, and Noahs wife, and the three wiues of his sonnes with them into the Arke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:8–9; Genesis 7:2–3"> They and euery beast after his kinde, and all cattell after their kinde, and euery thing that creepeth and moueth vpon the earth after his kinde, and euery foule after his kinde, euen euery bird of euery fether.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 6:19–20; Isaiah 11:6"> For they came to Noah into ye Arke, two and two, of all flesh wherein is ye breath of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 25:10; Luke 13:25; Psalms 91:1–10"> And they entring in, came male and female of all flesh, as God had commanded him: and the Lord shut him in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:12"> Then ye flood was fourtie dayes vpon the earth, and the waters were increased, and bare vp the Arke, which was lift vp aboue the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; Psalms 69:15; Job 22:16"> The waters also waxed strong, and were increased exceedingly vpon the earth, and the Arke went vpon the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 104:6–9; Psalms 46:2–3; 2 Peter 3:6"> The waters preuailed so exceedingly vpon the earth, that all the high mountaines, that are vnder the whole heauen, were couered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:23; Psalms 104:6"> Fifteene cubites vpwarde did the waters preuaile, when the mountaines were couered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 6:13; Genesis 7:4; Genesis 6:17"> Then all flesh perished that moued vpon the earth, both foule and cattell and beast, and euery thing that creepeth and moueth vpon the earth, and euery man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Genesis 6:17"> Euery thing in whose nostrels the spirit of life did breathe, whatsoeuer they were in the drie land, they died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 2:5; 1 Peter 3:20; Hebrews 11:7"> So he destroyed euery thing that was vpon the earth, from man to beast, to ye creeping thing, and to the foule of the heauen: they were euen destroyed from the earth. And Noah onely remained; and they that were with him in ye Arke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 8:3–4"> And the waters preuailed vpon the earth an hundreth and fiftie dayes.</span>
