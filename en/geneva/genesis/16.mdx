<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 4:24; Genesis 12:16; Genesis 15:2–3"> Nowe Sarai Abrams wife bare him no children, and she had a maide an Egyptian, Hagar by name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 21:4; Genesis 18:10; Genesis 3:17"> And Sarai said vnto Abram, Beholde now, the Lord hath restrained me from childe bearing. I pray thee goe in vnto my maide: it may be that I shall receiue a childe by her. And Abram obeyed the voyce of Sarai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 16:5; Genesis 12:4–5; 2 Samuel 5:13"> Then Sarai Abrams wife tooke Hagar her maide the Egyptian, after Abram had dwelled ten yeere in the land of Canaan, and gaue her to her husband Abram for his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:23; 2 Samuel 6:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5"> And he went in vnto Hagar, and she conceiued. and when she sawe that she had conceiued, her dame was despised in her eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 31:53; Exodus 5:21; Psalms 7:8"> Then Sarai saide to Abram, Thou doest me wrong. I haue giuen my maide into thy bosome, and she seeth that she hath conceiued, and I am despised in her eyes: the Lord iudge betweene me and thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:5; Proverbs 14:29; 1 Peter 3:7"> Then Abram saide to Sarai, Beholde, thy maide is in thine hand: doe with her as it pleaseth thee. Then Sarai dealt roughly with her: wherefore she fled from her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Exodus 15:22; Genesis 21:17"> But the Angel of the Lord founde her beside a fountaine of water in the wildernesse by the fountaine in the way to Shur,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:9; Genesis 4:10; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> And he saide, Hagar Sarais maide, whence commest thou? and whither wilt thou goe? And she said, I flie from my dame Sarai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5–6; Ecclesiastes 10:4; Titus 2:9"> Then the Angel of the Lord saide to her, Returne to thy dame, and humble thy selfe vnder her hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:20; Psalms 83:6–7; Genesis 25:12–18"> Againe the Angel of the Lord saide vnto her, I will so greatly increase thy seede, that it shall not be numbred for multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 2:23–24; Exodus 3:7; Isaiah 7:14"> Also the Angel of the Lord said vnto her, See, thou art with childe, and shalt beare a sonne, and shalt call his name Ishmael: for the Lord hath heard thy tribulation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Genesis 21:20; Job 39:5–8"> And he shalbe a wilde man: his hande shall be against euery man, and euery mans hand against him. and he shall dwell in the presence of all his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 139:1–12; Genesis 32:30; Proverbs 5:21"> Then she called the name of the Lord, that spake vnto her, Thou God lookest on me: for she said, Haue I not also here looked after him that seeth me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 24:62; Genesis 25:11; Genesis 21:31"> Wherefore the well was called, Beerlahai-roi. lo, it is betweene Kadesh and Bered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 25:12; Genesis 21:9–21; Genesis 17:25–26"> And Hagar bare Abram a sonne, and Abram called his sonnes name, which Hagar bare, Ishmael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Abram was foure score and sixe yeere olde, when Hagar bare him Ishmael.</span>
