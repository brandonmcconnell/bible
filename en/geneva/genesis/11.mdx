<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 2:6; Zephaniah 3:9; Isaiah 19:18"> Then the whole earth was of one language and one speache.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 10:10; Daniel 1:2; Genesis 14:1"> And as they went from the East, they found a plaine in the land of Shinar, and there they abode.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:10; Exodus 1:14; Exodus 2:3"> And they said one to another, Come, let vs make bricke, and burne it in the fire. So they had bricke for stone, and slime had they in steade of morter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; John 5:44; Luke 1:51"> Also they said, Goe to, let vs builde vs a citie and a towre, whose top may reache vnto the heauen, that we may get vs a name, lest we be scattered vpon the whole earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 18:21; Psalms 33:13–14; John 3:13"> But the Lord came downe, to see the citie and towre, which the sonnes of men builded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–4; Genesis 3:22; Genesis 11:1"> And the Lord said, Beholde, the people is one, and they all haue one language, and this they begin to doe, neither can they now be stopped from whatsoeuer they haue imagined to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Psalms 55:9; Job 5:12–13"> Come on, let vs goe downe, and there confound their language, that euery one perceiue not anothers speache.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 1:51; Deuteronomy 32:8; Genesis 10:25"> So ye Lord scattered them from thence vpon all the earth, and they left off to build the citie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 10:10; Genesis 10:5; Genesis 10:20"> Therefore the name of it was called Babel, because the Lord did there confounde the language of all the earth: from thence then did the Lord scatter them vpon all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:17–27; Genesis 10:21–22; Luke 3:34–36"> These are the generations of Shem: Shem was an hundreth yeere olde, and begate Arpachshad two yeere after the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 9:7; Psalms 144:12; Genesis 1:28"> And Shem liued, after he begate Arpachshad, fiue hundreth yeeres, and begate sonnes and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 3:36"> Also Arpachshad liued fiue and thirtie yeeres, and begate Shelah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Arpachshad liued, after he begate Shelah, foure hundreth and three yeeres, and begate sonnes and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Shelah liued thirtie yeeres, and begat Eber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> So Shelah liued, after he begat Eber, foure hundreth and three yeeres, and begat sonnes and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 10:21; 1 Chronicles 1:19; Numbers 24:24"> Likewise Eber liued foure and thirtie yeres, and begate Peleg.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> So Eber liued, after he begate Peleg, foure hundreth and thirtie yeeres, and begate sonnes and daughters</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 3:35"> And Peleg liued thirtie yeeres, and begate Reu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Peleg liued, after he begate Reu, two hundreth and nine yeeres, and begate sonnes and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 3:35"> Also Reu liued two and thirtie yeeres, and begate Serug.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So Reu liued, after he begate Serug, two hundreth and seuen yeeres, and begate sonnes and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 24:2"> Moreouer Serug liued thirtie yeeres, and begate Nahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Serug liued, after he begate Nahor, two hundreth yeeres, and begate sonnes and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 3:34; Joshua 24:2"> And Nahor liued nine and twentie yeeres, and begate Terah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> So Nahor liued, after he begate Terah, an hundreth and nineteene yeeres, and begat sonnes and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 24:2; 1 Chronicles 1:26–27; Genesis 22:20–24"> So Terah liued seuentie yeeres, and begate Abram, Nahor, and Haran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 12:4; Genesis 14:12; Genesis 11:31"> Nowe these are the generations of Terah: Terah begate Abram, Nahor, and Haran: and Haran begate Lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 15:7; Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–4"> Then Haran died before Terah his father in the land of his natiuitie, in Vr of the Caldees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 17:15; Genesis 22:20; Genesis 24:15"> So Abram and Nahor tooke them wiues. The name of Abrams wife was Sarai, and the name of Nahors wife Milcah, the daughter of Haran, the father of Milcah, and the father of Iscah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 29:31; Genesis 21:1–2; Luke 1:7"> But Sarai was barren, and had no childe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–4; Genesis 24:10"> Then Terah tooke Abram his sonne, and Lot the sonne of Haran, his sonnes sonne, and Sarai his daughter in lawe, his sonne Abrams wife: and they departed together from Vr of the Caldees, to goe into the land of Canaan, and they came to Haran, and dwelt there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> So the dayes of Terah were two hundreth and fiue yeeres, and Terah died in Haran.</span>
