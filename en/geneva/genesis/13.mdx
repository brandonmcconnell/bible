<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:9–20; Joshua 10:40; 2 Samuel 24:7"> Then Abram went vp from Egypt, he, and his wife, and all that he had, and Lot with him toward the South.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Psalms 112:1–3; Genesis 26:12–13"> And Abram was very rich in cattell, in siluer and in golde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 12:8–9; Genesis 12:6"> And he went on his iourney from ye South toward Beth-el, to the place where his tent had bene at ye beginning, betweene Beth-el and Haai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12; Genesis 12:7–8; Isaiah 58:9"> Vnto the place of the altar, which he had made there at the first: and there Abram called on the Name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:29; Genesis 25:27; Genesis 4:20"> And Lot also, who went with Abram, had sheepe, and cattell and tentes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 36:6–7; Ecclesiastes 5:10–11; 1 Timothy 6:9"> So that the land coulde not beare them, that they might dwell together: for their substance was great, so that they coulde not dwell together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 26:20; Genesis 12:6; James 3:16"> Also there was debate betweene ye heardmen of Abrams cattell, and the heardmen of Lots cattell. (and the Canaanites and the Perizzites dwelled at that time in the land.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:6–7; Acts 7:26; Psalms 133:1"> Then saide Abram vnto Lot, Let there be no strife, I pray thee, betweene thee and me, neither betweene mine heardmen and thine heardmen: for we be brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 34:10; 1 Peter 3:8–12"> Is not the whole land before thee? depart I pray thee from me: if thou wilt take the left hand, then I will goe to the right: or if thou goe to the right hand, then I will take the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:3; Isaiah 51:3; Genesis 14:8"> So when Lot lifted vp his eyes, he saw that all the plaine of Iorden was watered euery where: (for before the Lord destroyed Sodom and Gomorah, it was as the garden of the Lord, like the land of Egypt, as thou goest vnto Zoar)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 13:9; 1 Peter 2:17; Hebrews 10:25"> Then Lot chose vnto him all the plaine of Iorden, and tooke his iourney from the East: and they departed the one from the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 19:29; Genesis 14:12; Genesis 19:1"> Abram dwelled in the lande of Canaan, and Lot abode in the cities of the plaine, and pitched his tent euen to Sodom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 2:10; 2 Peter 2:6–8; Jude 1:7"> Now the men of Sodom were wicked and exceeding sinners against the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 49:18; Deuteronomy 3:27; Genesis 28:14"> Then the Lord saide vnto Abram, (after that Lot was departed from him) Lift vp thine eyes nowe, and looke from the place where thou art, Northward, and Southward, and Eastwarde, and Westward:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:7; Genesis 35:12; Genesis 15:18"> For all the land, which thou seest, will I giue vnto thee and to thy seede for euer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 28:14; Numbers 23:10; Genesis 32:12"> And I will make thy seede, as the dust of the earth: so that if a man can number the dust of the earth, then shall thy seede be numbred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Numbers 13:17–24"> Arise, walke through the land, in ye length thereof, and breadth thereof: for I will giue it vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 18:1; Genesis 8:20; Genesis 35:27"> Then Abram remoued his tent, and came and dwelled in the plaine of Mamre, which is in Hebron, and builded there an altar vnto ye Lord.</span>
