<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 24:14; Jeremiah 23:20; Daniel 10:14"> Then Iaakob called his sonnes, and sayde, Gather your selues together, that I may tell you what shall come to you in the last dayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 34:11; Proverbs 7:1; Proverbs 23:22"> Gather your selues together, and heare, ye sonnes of Iaakob, and hearken vnto Israel your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:17; Psalms 78:51; Psalms 105:36"> Reuben mine eldest sonne, thou art my might, and the beginning of my strength, the excellencie of dignitie, and the excellencie of power:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; 1 Chronicles 5:1; Deuteronomy 27:20"> Thou wast light as water: thou shalt not be excellent, because thou wentest vp to thy fathers bed: then diddest thou defile my bed, thy dignitie is gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 34:25–31; Genesis 46:10–11; Genesis 29:33–34"> Simeon and Leui, brethren in euill, the instruments of crueltie are in their habitations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Psalms 26:9; Psalms 16:9"> Into their secret let not my soule come: my glory, be not thou ioyned with their assembly: for in their wrath they slew a man, and in their selfe will they digged downe a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:1–9; 1 Chronicles 4:24–31; 1 Chronicles 6:65"> Cursed be their wrath, for it was fierce, and their rage, for it was cruell: I will deuide them in Iaakob, and scatter them in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 7:14; Genesis 27:29; 1 Chronicles 5:2"> Thou Iudah, thy brethre shall praise thee: thine hande shalbe in the necke of thine enemies: thy fathers sonnes shall bowe downe vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Numbers 23:24; Revelation 5:5"> Iudah, as a Lions whelpe shalt thou come vp from the spoyle, my sonne. He shall lye downe and couche as a Lion, and as a Lionesse: Who shall stirre him vp?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Numbers 24:17; Isaiah 9:6"> The scepter shall not depart from Iudah, nor a Lawegiuer from betweene his feete, vntill Shiloh come, and the people shall be gathered vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 63:1–3; Joel 3:18; Revelation 7:14"> He shall binde his Asse foale vnto ye vine, and his Asses colte vnto the best vine. hee shall wash his garment in wine, and his cloke in the blood of grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29"> His eyes shalbe red with wine, and his teeth white with milke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:18–19; Genesis 30:20; Joshua 19:10–16"> Zebulun shall dwell by the sea side, and he shalbe an hauen for shippes: and his border shalbe vnto Zidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:32; Genesis 30:18; Deuteronomy 33:18"> Issachar shalbe a strong asse, couching downe betweene two burdens:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 81:6; Matthew 23:4; Joshua 14:15"> And he shall see that rest is good, and that the land is pleasant, and he shall bow his shoulder to beare, and shalbe subiect vnto tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 30:6; Deuteronomy 33:22; Judges 18:26–27"> Dan shall iudge his people as one of the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:35; Judges 18:22–31; Judges 14:1–15"> Dan shall be a serpent by the way, an adder by the path, byting the horse heeles, so that his rider shall fall backward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:174; Psalms 119:166; Micah 7:7"> O Lord, I haue waited for thy saluation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 30:11; 1 Chronicles 5:11–22; Deuteronomy 33:20–21"> Gad, an hoste of men shall ouercome him, but he shall ouercome at the last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:24–25; Genesis 30:13; Genesis 46:17"> Concerning Asher, his bread shalbe fat, and he shall giue pleasures for a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:23; Genesis 30:8; Joshua 19:32–39"> Naphtali shalbe a hinde let goe, giuing goodly wordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 41:52; Psalms 128:3; Psalms 1:1–3"> Ioseph shalbe a fruitefull bough, euen a fruitful bough by the well side: the small boughs shall runne vpon the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:24; Psalms 64:3; Psalms 118:13"> And the archers grieued him, and shotte against him and hated him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 132:2; Isaiah 28:16; Psalms 132:5"> But his bowe abode strong, and the hands of his armes were strengthened, by the handes of the mighty God of Iaakob, of whom was the feeder appointed, by the stone of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 28:13; Genesis 35:11; Philippians 4:19"> Euen by the God of thy father, who shall helpe thee, and by the almightie, who shall blesse thee with heauenly blessinges from aboue, with blessings of the deepe, that lyeth beneath, with blessings of the brestes, and of the wombe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:15–16; Habakkuk 3:6; Isaiah 66:5"> The blessings of thy father shalbe stronger then the blessings of mine elders: vnto the ende of the hilles of the worlde they shall be on the head of Ioseph, and on the top of the head of him that was separate from his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 20:25; Genesis 35:18; Ezekiel 22:27"> Beniamin shall rauine as a wolfe: in the morning he shall deuoure the pray, and at night he shall deuide the spoyle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Esther 8:11; 1 Kings 18:31"> All these are the twelue tribes of Israel, and thus their father spake vnto them, and blessed them: euery one of them blessed hee with a seuerall blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 47:30; Genesis 50:13; Genesis 15:15"> And he charged them and sayd vnto them, I am ready to be gathered vnto my people: burie mee with my fathers in the caue, that is in the fielde of Ephron the Hittite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 23:20; Genesis 23:16–18; Genesis 23:8–9"> In the caue that is in the field of Machpelah besides Mamre in the land of Canaan: which caue Abraham bought with the fielde of Ephron the Hittite for a possession to burie in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; Genesis 35:29; Genesis 50:13"> There they buried Abraham and Sarah his wife: there they buryed Izhak and Rebekah his wife: and there I buried Leah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 23:17–20"> The purchase of the fielde and the caue that is therein, was bought of the children of Heth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 49:29; Genesis 25:8; Genesis 35:29"> Thus Iaakob made an end of giuing charge to his sonnes, and plucked vp his feete into the bed and gaue vp the ghost, and was gathered to his people.</span>
