<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 42:25; Genesis 43:16; Genesis 24:2"> Afterward he commanded his steward, saying, Fill the mens sackes with foode, as much as they can carry, and put euery mans money in his sackes mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:8; Genesis 42:20; Deuteronomy 8:16"> And put my cup, I meane the siluer cup, in the sackes mouth of the yongest, and his corne money. And he did according to the commandement that Ioseph gaue him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And in the morning the men were sent away, they, and their asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 10:32; Psalms 35:12; Proverbs 17:13"> And when they went out of the citie not farre off, Ioseph sayd to his stewarde, Vp, follow after the men: and when thou doest ouertake them, say vnto them, Wherefore haue ye rewarded euill for good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; Leviticus 19:26; 2 Kings 21:6"> Is that not the cuppe, wherein my Lord drinketh? and in the which he doeth deuine and prophecie? ye haue done euill in so doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And when he ouertooke them, he sayde those wordes vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 38:16–18; Genesis 37:18–32; 2 Kings 8:13"> And they answered him, Wherefore sayeth my lorde such wordes? God forbid that thy seruants should do such a thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 43:12; Genesis 42:35; Romans 13:9"> Behold, the money which we found in our sackes mouthes, wee brought againe to thee out of the land of Canaan: how then should we steale out of thy lordes house siluer or golde?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:32; Psalms 7:3–5; Job 31:38–40"> With whomesoeuer of thy seruants it bee found, let him dye, and we also will be my lordes bondmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 22:3; Genesis 44:17; Genesis 44:33"> And he said, Now then let it be according vnto your wordes: he with whome it is found, shall be my seruant, and ye shalbe blamelesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then at once euery man tooke downe his sacke to the grounde, and euery one opened his sacke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 44:26–32; Genesis 42:36–38; Genesis 43:14"> And he searched, and began at the eldest and left at the yongest: and the cuppe was found in Beniamins sacke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:11; Genesis 37:29–34; 2 Samuel 1:2"> Then they rent their clothes, and laded euery man his asse, and went againe into the citie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 50:18; Genesis 43:25; Genesis 43:16"> So Iudah and his brethren came to Iosephs house (for he was yet there) and they fel before him on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 4:10; Genesis 21:26; Genesis 3:13"> Then Ioseph sayd vnto them, What acte is this, which ye haue done? know ye not that such a man as I, can deuine and prophecie?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 44:9; Daniel 9:7; Numbers 32:23"> Then sayd Iudah, What shall we say vnto my lord? what shall we speake? and howe can we iustifie our selues? God hath found out the wickednesse of thy seruants: beholde, we are seruants to my Lord, both wee, and he, with whome the cuppe is founde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; 2 Samuel 23:3; Genesis 18:25"> But he answered, God forbid, that I should doe so, but the man, with whome the cuppe is founde, he shalbe my seruant, and go ye in peace vnto your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 32:22; Genesis 41:40; Genesis 18:30"> Then Iudah drewe neere vnto him, and sayde, O my Lord, let thy seruant nowe speake a worde in my lordes eares, and let not thy wrath be kindled against thy seruant: for thou art euen as Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 43:7; Genesis 43:29; Genesis 42:7–10"> My Lord asked his seruants, saying, Haue ye a father, or a brother?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 37:3; Genesis 42:38; Luke 7:12"> And we answered my Lord, We haue a father that is olde, and a young childe, which he begate in his age: and his brother is dead, and he alone is left of his mother, and his father loueth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 42:15; Genesis 42:20; Amos 9:4"> Now thou saidest vnto thy seruants, Bring him vnto me, that I may set mine eye vpon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 44:30; Genesis 42:38"> And we answered my lord, The childe can not depart from his father: for if he leaue his father, his father would die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 43:5; Genesis 43:3; Genesis 42:15–20"> Then saydest thou vnto thy seruants, Except your yonger brother come downe with you, looke in my face no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 42:29–34"> So when we came vnto thy seruant our father, and shewed him what my lord had sayd,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 43:2; Genesis 43:5"> And our father sayde vnto vs, Goe againe, bye vs a litle foode,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 11:7; Genesis 43:4–5"> Then we answered, We can not go downe: but if our yongest brother go with vs, then will we go downe: for we may not see the mans face, except our yongest brother be with vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 46:19; Genesis 35:16–18; Genesis 30:22–25"> Then thy seruant my father sayde vnto vs, Ye knowe that my wife bare me two sonnes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 37:33; Genesis 37:13–14; Genesis 42:36"> And the one went out from me, and I said, Of a suretie he is torne in pieces, and I sawe him not since.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 42:38; Genesis 43:14; Deuteronomy 31:17"> Nowe yee take this also away from me: if death take him, then yee shall bring my graye head in sorowe to the graue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:1; 2 Samuel 18:33; Genesis 44:31"> Nowe therefore, when I come to thy seruant my father, and the childe be not with vs (seeing that his life dependeth on the childes life)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 44:29; Genesis 37:35; 2 Corinthians 7:10"> Then when hee shall see that the childe is not come, he will die: so shall thy seruants bring the graye head of thy seruant our father with sorowe to the graue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 43:8–9; Genesis 43:16"> Doubtlesse thy seruant became suertie for the childe to my father, and said, If I bring him not vnto thee againe, then I will beare the blame vnto my father for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 John 3:16; Exodus 32:32; Hebrews 7:22"> Nowe therefore, I pray thee, let me thy seruant bide for the childe, as a seruant to my Lord, and let the childe go vp with his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Esther 8:6; Psalms 116:3; Psalms 119:143"> For how can I go vp to my father, if the childe be not with me, vnlesse I woulde see the euil that shall come on my father?</span>
