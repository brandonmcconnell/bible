<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; 1 Kings 13:33–34; 1 Samuel 31:2"> At that time Abiiah the sonne of Ieroboam fell sicke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:8; 1 Kings 11:29–38; 2 Samuel 14:2"> And Ieroboam saide vnto his wife, Vp, I pray thee, and disguise thy selfe, that they know not that thou art the wife of Ieroboam, and goe to Shiloh: for there is Ahiiah the Prophet, which tolde mee that I shoulde bee King ouer this people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:7–8; 1 Kings 13:7; 2 Kings 4:42"> And take with thee tenne loaues and craknels, and a bottell of honie, and go to him: hee shall tell thee what shall become of the yong man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 11:29; 1 Samuel 3:2; 1 Samuel 4:15"> And Ieroboams wife did so, and arose, and went to Shiloh, and came to the house of Ahiiah: but Ahiiah could not see, for his sight was decayed for his age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 6:8–12; Proverbs 21:30; 2 Kings 4:27"> Then the Lord saide vnto Ahiiah, Beholde, the wife of Ieroboam commeth to aske a thing of thee for her sonne, for he is sicke: thus and thus shalt thou say vnto her: for when shee commeth in, shee shall feine her selfe to be another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 5:17–28; Acts 5:9–10; Luke 20:20–23"> Therefore when Ahiiah heard the sounde of her feete as shee came in at the doore, hee saide, Come in, thou wife of Ieroboam: why feinest thou thus thy selfe to bee an other? I am sent to thee with heauie tidings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 16:2; 2 Samuel 12:7–8; 1 Samuel 15:16"> Goe, tel Ieroboam, Thus saith the Lord God of Israel, Forasmuch as I haue exalted thee from among the people, and haue made thee prince ouer my people Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 15:5; 1 Kings 11:33–38; 2 Chronicles 28:1"> And haue rent the kingdome away from the house of Dauid, and haue giuen it thee, and thou hast not bene as my seruant Dauid, which kept my commandements, and followed mee with all his heart, and did onely that which was right in mine eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:35; Nehemiah 9:26; Psalms 50:17"> But hast done euil aboue al that were before thee (for thou hast gone and made thee other gods, and molten images, to prouoke me, and hast cast me behinde thy backe)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; 1 Kings 21:21; 2 Kings 14:26"> Therefore beholde, I will bring euill vpon the house of Ieroboam, and will cut off from Ieroboam him that pisseth against the wall, aswell him that is shut vp, as him that is left in Israel, and will sweepe away the remnant of the house of Ieroboam, as a man sweepeth away doung, till it be all gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 16:4; Jeremiah 15:3; 1 Kings 21:23–24"> The dogges shall eate him of Ieroboams stocke that dyeth in the citie, and the foules of the aire shall eate him that dyeth in the fielde: for the Lord hath said it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 14:3; 1 Kings 14:16–17; 2 Kings 1:6"> Vp therefore and get thee to thine house: for when thy feete enter into the citie, the childe shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:3; 2 Chronicles 12:12; Job 19:28"> And al Israel shall mourne for him, and burie him: for he onely of Ieroboam shall come to the graue, because in him there is found some goodnes towarde the Lord God of Israel in the house of Ieroboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 15:27–29; Ezekiel 7:2–7; Ezekiel 12:22–28"> Moreouer, the Lord shall stirre him vp a King ouer Israel, which shall destroy the house of Ieroboam in that day: what? yea, euen nowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 23:15–16; Psalms 52:5; 2 Kings 15:29"> For the Lord shall smite Israel, as when a reede is shaken in the water, and hee shall weede Israel out of his good lad, which he gaue to their fathers, and shall scatter them beyond the Riuer, because they haue made them groues, prouoking the Lord to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 15:34; 1 Kings 16:2; 1 Kings 13:34"> And he shall giue Israel vp, because of the sinnes of Ieroboam, who did sinne, and made Israel to sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 15:33; 1 Kings 16:15; 1 Kings 16:23"> And Ieroboams wife arose, and departed, and came to Tirzah, and when shee came to the threshold of the house, the yong man dyed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 14:13"> And they buried him, and all Israel lamented him; according to the word of the Lord, which hee spake by the hand of his seruant Ahiiah the Prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:2–20; 1 Chronicles 27:24; 1 Kings 16:20"> And the rest of Ieroboams actes, how hee warred, and howe hee reigned, beholde, they are written in the booke of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:12; Psalms 3:5; 1 Kings 11:43"> And the dayes which Ieroboam reigned, were two and twentie yeere: and he slept with his fathers, and Nadab his sonne reigned in his steade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:13; 1 Kings 11:36; 1 Kings 14:31"> Also Rehoboam the sonne of Salomon reigned in Iudah. Rehoboam was one and fourtie yere olde, when he began to reigne, and reigned seuenteene yere in Ierusalem, the citie which the Lord did chuse out of al the tribes of Israel, to put his Name there: and his mothers name was Naamah an Ammonite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:1; 1 Corinthians 10:22; Psalms 78:58"> And Iudah wrought wickednesse in the sight of the Lord: and they prouoked him more with their sinnes, which they had committed, then all that which their fathers had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:2; Isaiah 57:5; 2 Kings 17:9–10"> For they also made them hie places, and images, and groues on euery hie hill, and vnder euery greene tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:17; 1 Corinthians 6:9; 1 Kings 15:12"> There were also Sodomites in the lande, they did according to all the abominations of the nations, which the Lord had cast out before the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 11:40; 2 Chronicles 12:2–4; 2 Chronicles 12:9"> And in the fift yere of King Rehoboam, Shishak King of Egypt came vp against Ierusale,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 15:18; 2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 7:51"> And tooke the treasures of the house of the Lord, and the treasures of the Kings house, and tooke away all: also he caried away all the shields of golde which Salomon had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11; 1 Samuel 22:17; 1 Kings 18:46"> And King Rehoboam made for them brasen shieldes, and committed them vnto ye hands of the chiefe of the garde, which wayted at the doore of the Kings house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:11"> And when the King went into the house of the Lord, the garde bare them, and brought them againe into the gard chamber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 22:45; 1 Kings 15:23; 1 Kings 14:19"> And the rest of the actes of Rehoboam, and all that hee did, are they not written in the booke of the Chronicles of the Kings of Iudah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 12:24; 1 Kings 12:21; 1 Kings 15:6–7"> And there was warre betweene Rehoboam and Ieroboam continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:16; Matthew 1:7; 1 Kings 15:3"> And Rehoboam slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the citie of Dauid: his mothers name was Naamah an Ammonite. And Abiiam his sonne reigned in his stead.</span>
