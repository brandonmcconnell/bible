<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:17; Proverbs 6:24; Nehemiah 13:23–27"> Bvt King Salomon loued many outlandish women: both the daughter of Pharaoh, and the women of Moab, Ammon, Edom, Zidon and Heth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 34:16; Deuteronomy 7:3–4; Joshua 23:12–13"> Of the nations, whereof the Lord had sayd vnto the children of Israel, Goe not ye in to them, nor let them come in to you: for surely they will turne your hearts after their gods, to them, I say, did Salomon ioyne in loue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:28; 2 Samuel 5:13–16; 2 Chronicles 11:21"> And he had seuen hundreth wiues, that were princesses, and three hundreth concubines, and his wiues turned away his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 9:4; 1 Kings 8:61; 1 Kings 11:6"> For when Salomon was olde, his wiues turned his heart after other gods, so that his heart was not perfect with the Lord his God, as was the heart of Dauid his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 2:13; 1 Kings 11:7; 2 Kings 23:13"> For Salomon followed Ashtaroth the god of the Zidonians, and Milcom the abomination of the Ammonites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Joshua 14:8; Joshua 14:14"> So Salomon wrought wickednesse in the sight of the Lord, but continued not to follow the Lord, as did Dauid his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 21:29; Judges 11:24; Acts 7:43"> Then did Salomon build an hie place for Chemosh the abomination of Moab, in the mountaine that is ouer against Ierusalem, and vnto Molech the abomination of the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11–12; 1 Kings 11:1; Ezekiel 16:22–29"> And so did he for all his outlandish wiues, which burnt incense and offered vnto their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 9:2; 1 Kings 3:5; Deuteronomy 7:4"> Therefore the Lord was angry with Salomon, because hee had turned his heart from the Lord God of Israel, which had appeared vnto him twise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 6:12–13; 2 Chronicles 7:17–22; 1 Kings 9:4–7"> And had giuen him a charge concerning this thing, that he should not follow other gods: but he kept not that, which the Lord had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 12:15–16; 1 Kings 11:31; 2 Kings 17:21"> Wherefore the Lord sayd vnto Salomon, Forasmuch as this is done of thee, and thou hast not kept my couenant, and my statutes (which I commanded thee) I will surely rent the kingdome from thee, and will giue it to thy seruant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 20:19; 1 Samuel 9:4–5; 2 Kings 22:19–20"> Notwithstanding in thy dayes I will not doe it, because of Dauid thy father, but I will rent it out of the hand of thy sonne:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 12:20; Deuteronomy 12:11; 1 Kings 11:32"> Howbeit I wil not rent all the Kingdome, but will giue one tribe to thy sonne, because of Dauid my seruant, and because of Ierusalem which I haue chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; Psalms 89:30–34; Isaiah 13:17"> Then the Lord stirred vp an aduersarie vnto Salomon, euen Hadad the Edomite, of the Kings seede, which was in Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; 1 Chronicles 18:12–13; Deuteronomy 20:13"> For when Dauid was in Edom, and Ioab the captaine of the hoste had smitten all the males in Edom, and was gone vp to bury ye slaine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> (For six moneths did Ioab remaine there, and all Israel, till he had destroyed all the males in Edom)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 2:13–14; Exodus 2:1–10; 2 Kings 11:2"> Then this Hadad fled and certaine other Edomites of his fathers seruants with him, to goe into Egypt, Hadad being yet a litle childe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 10:12; Deuteronomy 33:2; Deuteronomy 1:1"> And they arose out of Midian, and came to Paran, and tooke men with them out of Paran, and came to Egypt vnto Pharaoh King of Egypt, which gaue him an house, and appointed him vitailes, and gaue him lande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 39:4; Jeremiah 43:7–9; Genesis 39:21"> So Hadad found great fauour in the sight of Pharaoh, and he gaue him to wife the sister of his owne wife, euen the sister of Tahpenes the Queene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:7; 1 Samuel 1:24"> And the sister of Tahpenes bare him Genubath his sonne, whome Tahpenes wayned in Pharaohs house: and Genubath was in Pharaohs house among the sonnes of Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 2:10; Exodus 4:19; Matthew 2:20"> And when Hadad heard in Egypt, that Dauid slept with his fathers, and that Ioab the captaine of the hoste was dead, Hadad sayde to Pharaoh, Let me depart, that I may goe to mine owne countrey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:22–23; Luke 22:35; Mark 14:31"> But Pharaoh saide vnto him, What hast thou lacked with me, that thou wouldest thus go to thine owne countrey? And he answered, Nothing, but in any wise let me goe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 11:14; 2 Samuel 8:3; 2 Samuel 10:15–18"> And God stirred him vp another aduersarie, Rezon the sonne of Eliada, which fled from his lorde Hadadezer King of Zobah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:8; 2 Samuel 10:18; 1 Kings 20:34"> And he gathered men vnto him, and had bene captaine ouer the company, when Dauid slew them. And they went to Damascus, and dwelt there, and they made him King in Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Psalms 106:40; 1 Kings 5:4"> Therefore was he an aduersarie to Israel all the daies of Salomon: besides the euil that Hadad did, he also abhorred Israel, and reigned ouer Aram</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:6; 1 Kings 12:2; 1 Kings 11:28"> And Ieroboam the sonne of Nebat an Ephrathite of Zereda Salomons seruant (whose mother was called Zeruah a widowe) lift vp his hand against the King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 9:24; 1 Kings 9:15; Proverbs 30:32"> And this was the cause that he lift vp his hande against the King, When Salomon built Millo, he repared the broken places of the citie of Dauid his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29; 2 Samuel 19:20; Judges 1:22–23"> And this man Ieroboam was a man of strength and courage, and Salomon seeing that the yong man was meete for the worke, he made him ouerseer of all the labour of the house of Ioseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 12:15; 1 Kings 14:2; 2 Chronicles 9:29"> And at that time, when Ieroboam went out of Ierusalem, the Prophet Ahiiah the Shilonite founde him in the way, hauing a newe garment on him, and they two were alone in ye field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:27–28; 1 Samuel 24:4–5"> Then Ahiiah caught the newe garment that was on him, and rent it in twelue pieces,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 11:11–12"> And said to Ieroboam, Take vnto thee ten pieces: for thus saith the Lord God of Israel, Beholde, I wil rent the kingdome out of ye hands of Salomon, and will giue ten tribes to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 11:13; 1 Kings 12:20–21; 1 Kings 14:21"> But he shall haue one tribe for my seruant Dauids sake, and for Ierusalem the citie, which I haue chosen out of all the tribes of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 11:9; 1 Kings 11:5–7; 1 Kings 9:5–7"> Because they haue forsaken me, and haue worshipped Ashtaroth the god of the Zidonians, and Chemosh the god of the Moabites, and Milcom the god of the Ammonites, and haue not walked in my wayes (to do right in mine eyes, and my statutes, and my lawes) as did Dauid his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 11:12–13; 1 Kings 11:31; Job 11:6"> But I will not take the whole kingdome out of his hande: for I will make him prince all his life long for Dauid my seruants sake, whome I haue chosen, and who kept my commandements and my statutes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 12:20; 1 Kings 11:12; Exodus 20:5–6"> But I will take the kingdome out of his sonnes hand, and will giue it vnto thee, euen the ten tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Kings 8:19; 1 Kings 11:13; 1 Kings 15:4"> And vnto his sonne will I giue one tribe, that Dauid my seruant may haue a light alway before me in Ierusalem the citie, which I haue chosen me, to put my Name there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:26; 1 Kings 11:26; 2 Samuel 3:21"> And I wil take thee, and thou shalt reigne, euen as thine heart desireth, and shalt be King ouer Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:11; Joshua 1:5; Deuteronomy 31:8"> And if thou hearken vnto all that I commande thee, and wilt walke in my wayes, and doe right in my sight, to keepe my statutes and my commandements, as Dauid my seruant did, then will I be with thee, and build thee a sure house, as I built vnto Dauid, and wil giue Israel vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Luke 2:11; Isaiah 11:1–10"> And I will for this afflict the seede of Dauid, but not for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:2–9; Isaiah 14:24–27; Lamentations 3:37"> Salomon sought therefore to kill Ieroboam, and Ieroboam arose, and fled into Egypt vnto Shishak King of Egypt, and was in Egypt vntil the death of Salomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:29–31"> And the rest of the wordes of Salomon, and all that he did, and his wisedom, are they not written in the booke of the actes of Salomon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Kings 2:11"> The time that Salomon reigned in Ierusalem ouer all Israel, was fourtie yeere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Kings 21:18; 1 Kings 2:10; Matthew 1:7"> And Salomon slept with his fathers and was buried in the citie of Dauid his father: and Rehoboam his sonne reigned in his steade.</span>
