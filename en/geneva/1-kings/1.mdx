<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 23:1–2; 1 Chronicles 29:27–28; 1 Chronicles 23:1"> Now when King Dauid was olde, and striken in yeeres, they couered him with clothes, but no heate came vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:11; Genesis 16:5; Micah 7:5"> Wherefore his seruants saide vnto him, Let there be sought for my lord ye King a yong virgin, and let her stand before the king, and cherish him: and let her lie in thy bosome, that my lord the King may get heate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 19:18; Esther 2:4; Esther 2:2"> So they sought for a faire young maide thoroughout all the coastes of Israel, and founde one Abishag a Shunamite, and brought her to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 1:25"> And the maid was exceeding faire, and cherished the king, and ministred to him, but the King knew her not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:1; 2 Samuel 3:4; Deuteronomy 17:15–16"> Then Adoniiah the sonne of Haggith exalted himselfe, saying, I will be king. And he gate him charets and horsemen, and fiftie men to run before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 3:13; Hebrews 12:5–6"> And his father would not displease him from his childehood, to say, Why hast thou done so? And hee was a very goodly man, and his mother bare him next after Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:25; 1 Kings 2:22; 1 Samuel 22:20–23"> And he tooke counsel of Ioab the sonne of Zeruiah, and of Abiathar the Priest: and they helped forward Adoniiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 4:18; 2 Samuel 23:8–39; 2 Samuel 20:25"> But Zadok the Priest, and Benaiah the sonne of Iehoiada, and Nathan the Prophet, and Shimei, and Rei, and the men of might, which were with Dauid, were not with Adoniiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:17; 2 Samuel 13:23–27; 2 Samuel 15:11–12"> Then Adoniiah sacrificed sheepe and oxen, and fat cattel by the stone of Zoheleth, which is by En-rogel, and called all his brethren the kings sonnes, and al the men of Iudah ye Kings seruants,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:24; 1 Kings 1:19; 2 Samuel 12:1–15"> But Nathan the Prophet, and Benaiah, and the mightie men, and Salomon his brother hee called not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:4; 2 Samuel 7:12–17; 2 Samuel 12:24–25"> Wherefore Nathan spake vnto Bath-sheba the mother of Salomon, saying, Hast thou not heard, that Adoniiah ye sonne of Haggith doeth reigne, and Dauid our lord knoweth it not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Acts 27:31; Genesis 19:17"> Now therefore come, and I will now giue thee counsell, howe to saue thine owne life, and the life of thy sonne Salomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 1:30; 1 Kings 1:17; Psalms 132:11–12"> Go, and get thee in vnto King Dauid, and say vnto him, Didest not thou, my lorde, O King, sweare vnto thine handmaide, saying, Assuredly Salomon thy sonne shall reigne after me, and he shall sit vpon my throne? why is then Adoniiah King?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:1; 1 Kings 1:17–27"> Behold, while thou yet talkest there with the King, I also will come in after thee, and confirme thy wordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 1:1"> So Bath-sheba went in vnto the King into the chamber, and the King was verie olde, and Abishag the Shunammite ministred vnto ye King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:23; 1 Samuel 24:8; 1 Samuel 20:41"> And Bath-sheba bowed and made obeisance vnto the King. And the King saide, What is thy matter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 1:13; 1 Kings 1:30; 1 Peter 3:6"> And shee answered him, My lorde, thou swarest by the Lord thy God vnto thine handmaide, saying, Assuredly Salomon thy sonne shall reigne after me, and he shall sit vpon my throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 1:24; 1 Kings 1:11; Acts 3:17"> And beholde, nowe is Adoniiah king, and now, my lord, O King, thou knowest it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 1:25; 1 Kings 1:7–10"> And he hath offred many oxen, and fatte cattel, and sheepe, and hath called all the sonnes of the King, and Abiathar the Priest, and Ioab the captaine of the hoste: but Salomon thy seruant hath he not bidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:1; Psalms 123:2; 1 Chronicles 22:8–10"> And thou, my lorde, O King, knowest that the eyes of all Israel are on thee, that thou shouldest tell them, who shoulde sit on the throne of my lord the King after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 2:10; Deuteronomy 31:16; Genesis 15:15"> For els when my lord the King shall sleepe with his fathers, I and my sonne Salomon shalbe reputed vile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 1:16–18; Genesis 24:15; Daniel 9:20"> And lo, while she yet talked with the King, Nathan also the Prophet came in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 1:16; 1 Peter 2:17; Romans 13:7"> And they tolde the King, saying, Beholde, Nathan the Prophet. And when he was come in to the King, hee made obeisance before the King vpon his face to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 1:17–18; 1 Kings 1:5; 1 Kings 1:13–14"> And Nathan saide, My lorde, O King, hast thou said, Adoniiah shall reigne after mee, and he shall sit vpon my throne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Kings 1:19; 1 Kings 1:9"> For hee is gone downe this day, and hath slaine many oxen, and fat cattel, and sheepe, and hath called al the Kings sonnes, and the captaines of the hoste, and Abiathar the Priest: and behold, they eate and drinke before him, and say, God saue King Adoniiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 1:8; 1 Kings 1:10; 2 Samuel 7:2"> But me thy seruant, and Zadok the Priest, and Benaiah the sonne of Iehoiada, and thy seruant Salomon hath he not called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 15:15; 1 Kings 1:24; 2 Kings 4:27"> Is this thing done by my lord the King, and thou hast not shewed it vnto thy seruant, who should sitte on the throne of my lorde the King after him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Then King Dauid answered, and saide, Call me Bath-sheba. And shee came into ye Kings presence, and stoode before the King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 4:9; Psalms 136:24; 1 Samuel 14:45"> And the King sware, saying, As the Lord liueth, who hath redeemed my soule out of all aduersitie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 1:13; 1 Kings 1:17"> That as I sware vnto thee by the Lord God of Israel, saying, Assuredly Salomon thy sonne shall reigne after me, and hee shall sit vpon my throne in my place, so will I certainely doe this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 2:4; Daniel 5:10; Daniel 3:9"> Then Bath-sheba bowed her face to the earth, and did reuerence vnto the King, and said, God saue my lord King Dauid for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 1:8; 1 Kings 1:38; 1 Kings 1:26"> And King Dauid saide, Call mee Zadok the Priest, and Nathan the Prophet, and Benaiah the sonne of Iehoiada. And they came before the King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:30; 1 Kings 1:38; 2 Chronicles 33:14"> Then the King said vnto them, Take with you the seruants of your lorde, and cause Salomon my sonne to ride vpon mine owne mule, and carie him downe to Gihon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 1:25; 2 Samuel 15:10; 1 Samuel 10:1"> And let Zadok the Priest and Nathan the Prophet anoint him there King ouer Israel, and blowe ye the trumpet, and say, God saue king Salomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 1:17; Psalms 72:1–2; 1 Kings 1:13"> Then come vp after him, that hee may come and sit vpon my throne: and hee shall bee King in my steade: for I haue appointed him to be prince ouer Israel and ouer Iudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:6; 1 Corinthians 14:16; Psalms 72:19"> Then Benaiah the sonne of Iehoiada answered the King, and said, So be it, and the Lord God of my lord the King ratifie it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:13; 1 Kings 1:47; Joshua 1:17"> As the Lord hath bene with my lorde the King, so be he with Salomon, and exalt his throne aboue the throne of my lorde king Dauid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 1 Kings 1:8; 1 Kings 1:33"> So Zadok the Priest, and Nathan the Prophet, and Benaiah the sonne of Iehoiada, and the Cherethites and the Pelethites went downe and caused Salomon to ride vpon king Dauids mule, and brought him to Gihon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 89:20; 1 Kings 1:34; 1 Chronicles 29:22"> And Zadok the Priest tooke an horne of oyle out of the Tabernacle, and anointed Salomon: and they blewe the trumpet, and all the people said, God saue king Salomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Kings 11:14; Zechariah 9:9; 2 Kings 11:20"> And all the people came vp after him, and the people piped with pipes, and reioyced with great ioye, so that the earth rang with the sound of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Job 15:21–22; Luke 17:26–29; Acts 21:31"> And Adoniiah and all the ghestes that were with him, heard it: (and they had made an ende of eating) and when Ioab heard the sound of the trumpet, he said, What meaneth this noise and vprore in the citie?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:36; 2 Samuel 17:17; 2 Samuel 18:27"> And as he yet spake, beholde, Ionathan the sonne of Abiathar the Priest came: and Adoniiah said, Come in: for thou art a worthie man, and bringest good tidings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And Ionathan answered, and said to Adoniiah, Verely our lord King Dauid hath made Salomon King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And the King hath sent with him Zadok the Priest, and Nathan the Prophet, and Benaiah the sonne of Iehoiada, and the Cherethites, and the Pelethites, and they haue caused him to ride vpon the Kings mule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 1:40; Daniel 5:26–28; 1 Samuel 4:5"> And Zadok the Priest, and Nathan ye Prophet haue anointed him King in Gihon: and they are gone vp from thence with ioy, and the citie is moued: this is the noise that ye haue heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:23; Psalms 132:11; 1 Kings 1:13"> And Salomon also sitteth on the throne of the kingdome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Genesis 47:31; 1 Kings 1:37; Ezra 6:10"> And moreouer the Kings seruantes came to blesse our lord King Dauid, saying, God make the name of Salomon more famous then thy name, and exalt his throne aboue thy throne: therefore the King worshipped vpon the bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Kings 3:6; Psalms 132:11–12; Psalms 145:2"> And thus sayd the King also, Blessed bee the Lord God of Israel, who hath made one to sit on my throne this day, euen in my sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Isaiah 21:4–5; Proverbs 28:1; Daniel 5:4–6"> Then all the ghestes that were with Adonijah, were afraide, and rose vp, and went euery man his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28; Exodus 21:14; Exodus 27:2"> And Adonijah fearing the presence of Salomon, arose and went, and tooke holde on the hornes of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And one tolde Salomon, saying, Beholde, Adonijah doeth feare King Salomon: for lo, he hath caught holde on the hornes of the altar, saying, Let King Salomon sweare vnto me this day, that he will not slay his seruant with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:45; 2 Samuel 14:11; Proverbs 13:6"> Then Salomon sayd, If he will shewe him selfe a worthy man, there shall not an heare of him fall to the earth, but if wickednes be found in him, he shall dye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 Kings 1:16; 2 Samuel 14:24; 2 Samuel 1:2"> Then King Salomon sent, and they brought him from the altar, and he came and did obeisance vnto King Salomon. And Salomon sayde vnto him, Go to thine house.</span>
