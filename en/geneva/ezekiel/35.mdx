<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:1; Ezekiel 21:1; Ezekiel 34:1"> Moveover the worde of the Lord came vnto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Genesis 32:3; Ezekiel 25:8"> Sonne of man, Set thy face against mount Seir, and prophesie against it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:12; Ezekiel 35:7; Ezekiel 6:14"> And say vnto it, Thus sayth the Lord God, Behold, O mount Seir, I come against thee, and I wil stretch out mine hand against thee, and I will make thee desolate and waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:9; Malachi 1:3–4; Joel 3:19"> I wil lay thy cities waste, and thou shalt be desolate, and thou shalt knowe that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 137:7; Obadiah 1:10–16; Amos 1:11"> Because thou hast had a perpetuall hatred and hast put the children of Israel to flight by the force of the sword in the time of their calamitie, when their iniquitie had an ende,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:2–6; Revelation 18:24; Obadiah 1:15"> Therefore as I liue, sayth the Lord God, I wil prepare thee vnto blood, and blood shall pursue thee: except thou hate blood, euen blood shall pursue thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:11; Ezekiel 35:3; Judges 5:6–7"> Thus will I make mount Seir desolate and waste, and cut off from it him that passeth out and him that returneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:12; Ezekiel 32:4–5; Ezekiel 39:4–5"> And I will fill his mountaines with his slayne men: in thine hilles, and in thy valleys and in all thy riuers shall they fall, that are slayne with the sworde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Malachi 1:3–4; Ezekiel 6:7"> I wil make thee perpetual desolations, and thy cities shall not returne, and ye shall knowe that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:5; Psalms 83:4–12; Ezekiel 48:35"> Because thou hast said, These two nations, and these two countreys shalbe mine, and we wil possesse them (seeing the Lord was there)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 9:16; Matthew 7:2; Psalms 73:17–18"> Therefore as I liue, sayth the Lord God, I wil euen do according to thy wrath, and according to thine indignation which thou hast vsed in thine hatred against them: and I wil make my selfe knowen among them whe I haue iudged thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:2; Psalms 83:12; Ezekiel 6:7"> And thou shalt know, that I the Lord haue heard all thy blasphemies which thou hast spoken against the mountaines of Israel, saying, They lye waste, they are giuen vs to be deuoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 11:36; Isaiah 36:20; Jeremiah 29:23"> Thus with your mouthes ye haue boasted against me, and haue multiplied your words against me: I haue heard them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:48; Isaiah 14:7–8; Isaiah 65:13–15"> Thus sayth the Lord God, So shall all the world reioyce when I shall make thee desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:3–4; Lamentations 4:21; Isaiah 34:5–6"> As thou diddest reioyce at the inheritance of the house of Israel, because it was desolate, so will I doe vnto thee: thou shalt be desolate, O mount Seir, and all Idumea wholly, and they shall know, that I am the Lord.</span>
