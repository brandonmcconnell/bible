<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:7; Isaiah 48:4; Jeremiah 5:3"> For they are impudent children, and stiffe hearted: I do send thee vnto them, and thou shalt say vnto them, Thus saith the Lord God.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:28; Ezekiel 3:17; Jeremiah 1:7"> Therefore thou shalt speake my words vnto them: but surely they will not heare, neither will they in deede cease: for they are rebellious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 10:9; Jeremiah 15:16; Isaiah 50:5"> But thou sonne of man, heare what I say vnto thee: be not thou rebellious, like this rebellious house: open thy mouth, and eate that I giue thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:3; Revelation 5:1–5; Daniel 10:10"> And when I looked vp, beholde, an hande was sent vnto me, and loe, a roule of a booke was therein.</span>
