<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:20; Ezekiel 32:17; Ezekiel 31:1"> And in the twelfth yeere in the twelfth moneth, and in the first day of the moneth, the worde of the Lord came vnto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Nahum 2:11–13; Ezekiel 27:2"> Sonne of man, take vp a lamentation for Pharaoh King of Egypt, and say vnto him, Thou art like a lyon of the nations and art as a dragon in the sea: thou castedst out thy riuers and troubledst the waters with thy feete, and stampedst in their riuers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 16:16; Ecclesiastes 9:12"> Thus sayth the Lord God, I will therefore spread my net ouer thee with a great multitude of people, and they shall make thee come vp into my net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 18:6; Ezekiel 29:5; Ezekiel 31:12–13"> Then will I leaue thee vpon the land, and I will cast thee vpon the open field, and I wil cause all the foules of the heauen to remaine vpon thee, and I will fill all the beastes of the field with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:12"> And I will lay thy flesh vpon the mountaines, and fill the valleys with thine height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 34:3; Isaiah 34:7; Revelation 14:20"> I will also water with thy blood the land wherein thou swimmest, euen to ye mountaines, and the riuers shall be full of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Joel 2:31; Isaiah 13:10"> And when I shall put thee out, I will couer the heauen, and make the starres thereof darke: I will couer the sunne with a cloude, and the moone shall not giue her light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:14; Proverbs 20:20"> All the lightes of heauen will I make darke for thee, and bring darkenesse vpon thy lande, sayeth the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 18:10–15; Ezekiel 30:26; Jeremiah 25:15–25"> I will also trouble the heartes of many people, when I shall bring thy destruction among the nations and vpon the countries which thou hast not knowen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:16; Ezekiel 27:35; 1 Kings 9:8"> Yea, I will make many people amased at thee, and their Kings shalbe astonished with feare for thee, when I shall make my sworde to glitter against their faces, and they shall be afraide at euery moment: euery man for his owne life in the day of thy fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:10; Ezekiel 26:7; Ezekiel 30:22–25"> For thus sayth the Lord God, The sworde of the King of Babel shall come vpon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 31:11"> By the swordes of the mightie will I cause thy multitude to fall: they all shall be terrible nations, and they shall destroy the pompe of Egypt, and all the multitude thereof shalbe consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:11; Ezekiel 29:8; Ezekiel 30:12"> I will destroy also all the beastes thereof from the great watersides, neither shall the foote of man trouble them any more, nor the hooues of beast trouble them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Then will I make their waters deepe, and cause their riuers to runne like oyle, sayeth the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Exodus 7:5; Exodus 14:4"> When I shall make the land of Egypt desolate, and the countrey with all that is therein, shall be laid waste: when I shall smite all them which dwell therein, then shall they know that I am ye Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:17; 2 Chronicles 35:25; Ezekiel 26:17"> This is the mourning wherewith they shall lament her: the daughters of the nations shall lament her: they shall lament for Egypt, and for all her multitude, sayeth the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:1; Ezekiel 33:21; Ezekiel 1:2"> In the twelfth yeere also in the fifteenth day of the moneth, came the worde of the Lord vnto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:14; Ezekiel 32:2; Jeremiah 1:10"> Sonne of man, lament for the multitude of Egypt, and cast them downe, euen them and the daughters of the mighty nations vnto the nether partes of the earth, with them that goe downe into the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:18; Ezekiel 32:29–30; Jeremiah 9:25–26"> Whome doest thou passe in beautie? goe downe and sleepe with the vncircumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 28:3; Ezekiel 32:23–26; Ezekiel 32:29–30"> They shall fall in the middes of them that are slaine by the sword: shee is deliuered to the sword: draw her downe, and all her multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 14:9–10; Ezekiel 32:27; Ezekiel 32:19"> The most mighty and strong shall speake to him out of the mids of hell with them that helpe her: they are gone downe and sleepe with the vncircumcised that be slaine by the sworde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:26; Ezekiel 32:29–30; Ezekiel 32:24"> Asshur is there and all his companie: their graues are about him: all they are slaine and fallen by the sworde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:20; Ezekiel 32:24–27; Psalms 27:13"> Whose graues are made in the side of the pit, and his multitude are rounde about his graue: all they are slaine and fallen by the sworde, which caused feare to be in the land of the liuing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:18; Ezekiel 26:20; Jeremiah 49:34–39"> There is Elam and all his multitude round about his graue: al they are slaine and fallen by the sword which are gone downe with the vncircumcised into the nether parts of the earth, which caused themselues to be feared in the land of the liuing, yet haue they borne their shame with them that are gone downe to the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 139:8; 1 Chronicles 10:4; 2 Samuel 1:20"> They haue made his bed in the mids of the slaine with al his multitude: their graues are round about him: all these vncircucised are slaine by the sworde: though they haue caused their feare in the land of ye liuing, yet haue they borne their shame with them that goe downe to the pitte: they are laide in the middes of them, that be slaine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:13; Genesis 10:2; Ezekiel 38:2–3"> There is Meshech, Tubal, and all their multitude, their graues are round about them: al these vncircumcised were slaine by the sworde, though they caused their feare to be in ye land of the liuing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 14:18–19; Job 3:13–15; Ezekiel 32:21"> And they shall not lie with the valiant of the vncircumcised, that are fallen, which are gone down to the graue, with their weapons of warre, and haue laied their swords vnder their heads, but their iniquitie shalbe vpon their bones: because they were the feare of the mighty in the lande of the liuing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Daniel 2:34–35"> Yea, thou shalt be broken in the middes of the vncircumcised, and lie with them that are slaine by the sworde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:7–22; Malachi 1:3–4; Obadiah 1:1–9"> There is Edom, his Kings, and all his princes, which with their strength are laied by them that were slaine by the sworde: they shall sleepe with the vncircumcised, and with them that goe downe to the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:6; Ezekiel 39:2; Ezekiel 28:21"> There be al the princes of the North, with al the Zidonians, which are gone downe with the slaine, with their feare: they are ashamed of their strength, and the vncircumcised sleepe with them that be slaine by the sword, and beare their shame with them that goe downe to the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:16; Ezekiel 14:22; Lamentations 2:13"> Pharaoh shall see them, and hee shall be comforted ouer all his multitude: Pharaoh, and all his armie shall be slaine by the sword, saieth the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:27; Genesis 35:5; 2 Corinthians 5:11"> For I haue caused my feare to be in the lande of the liuing: and he shall be laid in the mids of the vncircumcised with them, that are slaine by the sword, euen Pharaoh and all his multitude, sayeth the Lord God.</span>
