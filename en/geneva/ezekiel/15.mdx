<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And thee word of the Lord came vnto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 10:1; Psalms 80:8–16; Isaiah 5:1–7"> Sonne of man, what commeth of the vine tree aboue all other trees? and of the vine braunch, which is among the trees of ye forest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 14:34–35; Jeremiah 24:8; Mark 9:50"> Shall wood bee taken thereof to doe any worke? or wil men take a pin of it to hang any vessel thereon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:6; Isaiah 27:11; Psalms 80:16"> Behold, it is cast in the fire to be consumed: the fire consumeth both the endes of it, and the middes of it is burnt. Is it meete for any worke?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:16"> Behold, when it was whole, it was meete for no worke: how much lesse shall it bee meete for any worke, when the fire hath consumed it, and it is burnt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 15:2; Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 24:8–10"> Therefore thus sayth the Lord God, As the vine tree, that is among the trees of the forest, which I haue giuen to the fire to be consumed, so will I giue the inhabitants of Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:8; Psalms 34:16; Isaiah 24:18"> And I will set my face against them: they shall go out from one fire, and another fire shall consume them: and ye shall know, that I am the Lord, when I set my face against them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:14; Zephaniah 1:18; Isaiah 6:11"> And when I make the lande waste, because they haue greatly offended, saith the Lord God.</span>
