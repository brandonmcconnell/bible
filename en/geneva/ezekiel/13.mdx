<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came vnto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:17; Jeremiah 37:19; Ezekiel 22:28"> Sonne of man, prophecie against the prophets of Israel, that prophecie, and say thou vnto them, that prophecie out of their owne hearts, Heare the worde of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 2:14; Jeremiah 23:28–32; Hosea 9:7"> Thus sayth the Lord God, Woe vnto the foolish Prophets that follow their owne spirit, and haue seene nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 19:20; Micah 2:11; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–10"> O Israel, thy Prophets are like the foxes in the waste places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Psalms 106:23; Isaiah 58:12"> Ye haue not risen vp in the gappes, neither made vp the hedge for ye house of Israel, to stand in the battel in the daye of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:8; Ezekiel 22:28; Jeremiah 28:15"> They haue seene vanitie, and lying diuination, saying, The Lord sayth it, and the Lord hath not sent them: and they haue made others to hope that they would cofirme the word of their prophecie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6; Matthew 24:23–24; Ezekiel 13:2–3"> Haue ye not seene a vaine vision? and haue yee not spoken a lying diuination? ye say, The Lord sayth it, albeit I haue not spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:8; Ezekiel 21:3; Ezekiel 28:22"> Therefore thus sayth the Lord God, Because ye haue spoken vanytie and haue seene lyes, therefore beholde, I am agaynst you, sayth the Lord God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 87:6; Psalms 69:28; Ezekiel 20:38"> And mine hande shalbe vpon the Prophets that see vanity, and diuine lies: they shall not be in the assemblie of my people, neither shall they be written in the writing of the house of Israel, neither shall they enter into the land of Israel: and yee shall know that I am the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:28; Ezekiel 13:16; Jeremiah 6:14"> And therefore, because they haue deceiued my people, saying, Peace, and there was no peace: and one buylt vp a wall, and behold, the others daubed it with vntempered morter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Isaiah 28:2; Nahum 1:7–8"> Say vnto them which daube it with vntempered morter, that it shall fall: for there shall come a great showre, and I wil sende haylestones, which shall cause it to fall, and a stormie winde shall breake it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 3:13; Lamentations 2:14–15; Judges 9:38"> Lo, when the wall is fallen, shall it not bee sayd vnto you, Where is the daubing wherewith ye haue daubed it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 30:30; Revelation 16:21; Psalms 148:8"> Therefore thus sayth the Lord God, I will cause a stormie winde to breake foorth in my wrath, and a great showre shall bee in mine anger, and hailestones in mine indignation to consume it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 1:6; Jeremiah 14:15; Jeremiah 6:15"> So I wil destroy the wall that ye haue daubed with vntempered morter, and bring it downe to the ground, so that the fundation thereof shalbe discouered, and it shall fal, and ye shalbe consumed in the middes thereof, and ye shall know, that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 30:13; Psalms 62:3; Nehemiah 4:3"> Thus will I accomplish my wrath vpon the wall, and vpon them that haue daubed it with vntempered morter, and will say vnto you, The wall is no more, neither the daubers thereof,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:10; Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 8:11"> To wit, the Prophets of Israel, which prophesie vpon Ierusalem, and see visions of peace for it, and there is no peace, sayth the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:2; 2 Kings 22:14; Judges 4:4"> Likewise thou sonne of man, set thy face against the daughters of thy people, which prophesie out of their owne heart: and prophesie thou against them, and say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 2:14; Ezekiel 13:20; 2 Timothy 4:3"> Thus sayth the Lord God, Woe vnto the women that sowe pillowes vnder al arme holes, and make vailes vpon the head of euery one that standeth vp, to hunt soules: will yee hunt ye soules of my people, and will yee giue life to the soules that come vnto you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 3:5; Proverbs 28:21; Jeremiah 23:17"> And will ye pollute me among my people for handfuls of barly, and for pieces of bread to slay the soules of them that shoulde not dye, and to giue life to the soules that should not liue in lying to my people, that heare your lies?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:18; 2 Timothy 3:8–9; Ezekiel 13:8–9"> Wherefore thus saith the Lord God, Behold, I will haue to do with your pillowes, wherewith yee hunt the soules to make them to flie, and I will teare them from your armes, and will let the soules goe, euen the soules, that ye hunt to make them to flie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 91:3"> Your vailes also will I teare, and deliuer my people out of your hande, and they shalbe no more in your hands to be hunted, and ye shall knowe that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Jeremiah 14:13–17; 2 Peter 2:18–19"> Because with your lyes yee haue made the heart of the righteous sadde, whome I haue not made sad, and strengthened the hands of the wicked, that he should not returne from his wicked way, by promising him life,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:21; Ezekiel 12:24; Micah 3:6"> Therefore ye shall see no more vanitie, nor diuine diuinatios: for I wil deliuer my people out of your hand, and ye shall know that I am ye Lord.</span>
