<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 7:20; Ezekiel 44:20; Leviticus 21:5"> And thou sonne of man, take thee a sharpe knife, or take thee a barbours rasor and cause it to passe vpon thine head, and vpon thy beard: then take thee balances to weigh, and deuide the heare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:12; Leviticus 26:33; Ezekiel 4:1–8"> Thou shalt burne with fire the thirde part in the middes of the citie, when the dayes of the siege are fulfilled, and thou shalt take the other thirde part, and smite about it with a knife, and the last thirde part thou shalt scatter in the winde, and I will drawe out a sworde after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:16; Jeremiah 40:6; Jeremiah 39:10"> Thou shalt also take thereof a fewe in nomber, and binde them in thy lappe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Jeremiah 41:1–18; 2 Kings 25:25"> Then take of them againe and cast them into the middes of the fire, and burne them in the fire: for thereof shall a fire come foorth into all the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:1; Deuteronomy 4:6; Jeremiah 6:6"> Thus saith the Lord God, This is Ierusalem: I haue set it in the middes of the nations and countreyes, that are rounde about her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:10; Ezekiel 16:47–48; Nehemiah 9:16–17"> And she hath changed my iudgements into wickednes more then the nations, and my statutes more then the countreis, that are round about her: for they haue refused my iudgemets and my statutes, and they haue not walked in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:10–11; 2 Chronicles 33:9; 2 Kings 21:9–11"> Therefore thus saith the Lord God, Because your multitude is greater then the nations that are rounde about you, and ye haue not walked in my statutes, neither haue ye kept my iudgements: no, ye haue not done according to the iudgements of the nations, that are rounde about you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 15:7; Jeremiah 24:9; Ezekiel 21:3"> Therefore thus saith the Lord God, Beholde, I, euen I come against thee, and will execute iudgement in the middes of thee, euen in the sight of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 24:21; Daniel 9:12; Amos 3:2"> And I will doe in thee, that I neuer did before, neither will do any more the like, because of all thine abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 2:6; Deuteronomy 28:64; Ezekiel 12:14"> For in the middes of thee, the fathers shall eate their sonnes, and the sonnes shall eate their fathers, and I will execute iudgement in thee, and the whole remnant of thee will I scatter into all the windes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:20; Ezekiel 7:9; Ezekiel 11:18"> Wherefore, as I liue, saith the Lord God, Surely because thou hast defiled my Sanctuarie with all thy filthinesse, and with all thine abominations, therefore will I also destroy thee, neither shall mine eye spare thee, neither will I haue any pitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:2; Jeremiah 15:2; Jeremiah 43:10–11"> The third part of thee shall die with the pestilence, and with famine shall they bee consumed in the middes of thee: and another third part shall fall by the sword round about thee: and I will scatter the last third part into all windes, and I will drawe out a sword after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:24; Ezekiel 36:5–6; Ezekiel 21:17"> Thus shall mine anger bee accomplished, and I will cause my wrath to cease in them, and I will be comforted: and they shall knowe, that I the Lord haue spoke it in my zeale, when I haue accomplished my wrath in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:4; Nehemiah 2:17; Psalms 79:1–4"> Moreouer, I will make thee waste, and abhorred among the nations, that are round about thee, and in the sight of all that passe by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:17; Jeremiah 22:8–9; 1 Kings 9:7"> So thou shalt bee a reproche and shame, a chastisement and an astonishment vnto the nations, that are rounde about thee, when I shall execute iudgements in thee, in anger and in wrath, and in sharpe rebukes: I the Lord haue spoken it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16; Deuteronomy 32:23–24; Ezekiel 14:13"> When I shall sende vpon them the euill arrowes of famine, which shalbe for their destruction, and which I will sende to destroy you: and I will encrease the famine vpon you, and wil breake your staffe of bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Ezekiel 14:21; Leviticus 26:22"> So will I send vpon you famine, and euill beastes, and they shall spoyle thee, and pestilence and blood shall passe through thee, and I will bring the sworde vpon thee: I the Lord haue spoken it.</span>
