<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:1; Ezekiel 20:1; 2 Kings 6:32"> Then came certaine of the Elders of Israel vnto me, and sate before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 3:7; 1 Kings 14:4"> And the worde of the Lord came vnto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:7; Ezekiel 7:19; Ezekiel 20:3"> Sonne of man, these men haue set vp their idoles in their heart, and put the stumbling blocke of their iniquitie before their face: should I, being required, answere them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 66:4; Ezekiel 14:7; Isaiah 3:11"> Therefore speake vnto them, and say vnto them, Thus saith the Lord God, Euery man of the house of Israel that setteth vp his idols in his heart, and putteth the stumbling blocke of his iniquitie before his face, and commeth to the Prophet, I the Lord will answere him that commeth, according to the multitude of his idoles:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; Zechariah 11:8; Romans 1:28"> That I may take the house of Israel in their owne heart, because they are all departed from me through their idoles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Isaiah 30:22; Ezekiel 36:31–32"> Therfore say vnto the house of Israel, Thus sayth the Lord God, Returne, and withdraw your selues, and turne your faces from your idoles, and turne your faces from all your abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:48; Isaiah 58:1–2; Leviticus 24:22"> For euery one of the house of Israel, or of the stranger that soiourneth in Israel, which departeth from mee, and setteth vp his idoles in his heart, and putteth the stumbling blocke of his iniquitie before his face, and commeth to a Prophet, for to inquire of him for me, I the Lord will answere him for my selfe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 15:7; Jeremiah 44:11; Ezekiel 5:15"> And I will set my face against that man, and will make him an example and prouerbe, and I will cut him off from the middes of my people, and ye shall knowe that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:25; Jeremiah 4:10; Psalms 81:11–12"> And if the Prophet be deceiued, when hee hath spoken a thing, I the Lord haue deceiued that Prophet, and I will stretch out mine hande vpon him, and will destroy him from the middes of my people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:49; Ezekiel 14:4; Jeremiah 8:11–12"> And they shall beare their punishment: the punishment of the Prophet shall bee euen as the punishment of him that asketh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:23; Ezekiel 44:10; Revelation 21:7"> That the house of Israel may go no more astray from mee, neither bee polluted any more with all their transgressions, but that they may be my people, and I may be their God, sayth the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The worde of the Lord came againe vnto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Ezekiel 4:16; Isaiah 24:20"> Sonne of man, when ye land sinneth against me by committing a trespasse, then will I stretch out mine hand vpon it, and will breake the staffe of the bread thereof, and will send famine vpon it, and I will destroy man and beast forth of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:20; Jeremiah 15:1; Ezekiel 28:3"> Though these three men Noah, Daniel, and Iob were among them, they shoulde deliuer but their owne soules by their righteousnes, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:17; Leviticus 26:22; Jeremiah 15:3"> If I bring noysome beastes into the lande and they spoyle it, so that it bee desolate, that no man may passe through, because of beastes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 19:29; James 5:16; Ezekiel 33:11"> Though these three men were in the mids thereof, As I liue, sayth the Lord God, they shall saue neither sonnes nor daughters: they onely shalbe deliuered, but the land shall be waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Ezekiel 21:3–4; Zephaniah 1:3"> Or if I bring a sworde vpon this land, and say, Sword, go through the land, so that I destroy man and beast out of it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Though these three men were in the mids thereof, As I liue, sayth the Lord God, they shall deliuer neither sonnes nor daughters, but they onely shall be deliuered themselues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Ezekiel 5:12; Jeremiah 14:12"> Or if I send a pestilence into this land, and powre out my wrath vpon it in blood, to destroy out of it man and beast,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:14; 1 John 2:29; Isaiah 3:10"> And though Noah, Daniel and Iob were in the middes of it, As I liue, sayth the Lord God, they shall deliuer neither sonne nor daughter: they shall but deliuer their owne soules by their righteousnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:27; Ezekiel 5:17; Ezekiel 14:13"> For thus saith the Lord God, Howe much more when I sende my foure sore iudgements vpon Ierusalem, euen the sworde, and famine, and the noysome beast and pestilence, to destroy man and beast out of it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:16; Ezekiel 20:43; Hebrews 12:6–11"> Yet beholde, therein shalbe left a remnant of them that shalbe caryed away both sonnes and daughters: behold, they shall come forth vnto you, and ye shall see their way, and their enterprises: and ye shall be comforted, concerning the euill that I haue brought vpon Ierusalem, euen concerning al that I haue brought vpon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; Revelation 15:4; Deuteronomy 8:2"> And they shall comfort you, when yee see their way and their enterprises: and ye shall know, that I haue not done without cause all that I haue done in it, saith the Lord God.</span>
