<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esther 9:20; Acts 15:23; Hebrews 13:22"> Now these are the wordes of the booke that Ieremiah the Prophet sent from Ierusalem vnto the residue of the Elders which were caryed away captiues, and to the Priestes, and to the Prophets, and to all the people whome Nebuchad-nezzar had caried away captiue from Ierusalem to Babel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:24–28; Jeremiah 27:20; 2 Chronicles 36:9–10"> (After that Ieconiah the King, and the Queene, and the eunuches, the princes of Iudah, and of Ierusalem, and the workemen, and cunning men were departed from Ierusalem)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:11; 2 Chronicles 34:20; Jeremiah 39:14"> By the hand of Elasah the sonne of Shaphan and Gemariah the sonne of Hilkiah, (whom Zedekiah King of Iudah sent vnto Babel to Nebuchad-nezzar King of Babel) saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:5; Isaiah 10:5–6; Isaiah 45:7"> Thus hath the Lord of hostes the God of Israel spoken vnto all that are caryed away captiues, whome I haue caused to be caryed away captiues from Ierusalem vnto Babel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:28; Jeremiah 29:10; Ezekiel 28:26"> Buylde you houses to dwell in, and plant you gardens, and eate the fruites of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:3–4; Genesis 24:51; Genesis 28:1–4"> Take you wiues, and beget sonnes and daughters, and take wiues for your sonnes, and giue your daughters to husbands, that they may beare sonnes and daughters, that ye may bee increased there, and not diminished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:1–2; Ezra 6:10; 1 Peter 2:13–17"> And seeke the prosperitie of the citie, whither I haue caused you to be caried away captiues, and pray vnto the Lord for it: for in the peace thereof shall you haue peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:6; Jeremiah 5:31; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> For thus saith the Lord of hostes the God of Israel, Let not your prophets, and your southsayers that bee among you, deceiue you, neither giue eare to your dreames, which you dreame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:15; Jeremiah 29:31; Jeremiah 29:23"> For they prophecie you a lie in my Name: I haue not sent them, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 9:2; Zechariah 7:5; Jeremiah 24:6–7"> But thus saith the Lord, That after seuentie yeeres be accomplished at Babel, I will visite you, and performe my good promise toward you, and cause you to returne to this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 55:8–12; Psalms 33:11; Micah 4:12"> For I knowe the thoughtes, that I haue thought towards you, saith the Lord, euen the thoughtes of peace, and not of trouble, to giue you an ende, and your hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; Jeremiah 33:3; Psalms 50:15"> Then shall you crie vnto mee, and ye shall go and pray vnto me, and I will heare you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:29–31; Psalms 119:2; Matthew 7:7"> And ye shall seeke mee and finde mee, because ye shall seeke mee with all your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:1–38; Jeremiah 30:3; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> And I wil be found of you, saith the Lord, and I will turne away your captiuitie, and I will gather you from all the nations, and from all the places, whither I haue cast you, saith the Lord, and will bring you againe vnto the place, whence I caused you to be caryed away captiue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:8–9; Jeremiah 28:1–17; Ezekiel 1:3"> Because ye haue sayd, The Lord hath raised vs vp Prophets in Babel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:2–3; Jeremiah 38:17–23; Ezekiel 17:12–21"> Therefore thus saith the Lord of the King, that sitteth vpon the throne of Dauid, and of all the people, that dwell in this citie, your brethren that are not gone forth with you into captiuitie:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:8–10; Jeremiah 29:18; Jeremiah 24:1–3"> Euen thus sayth the Lord of hostes, Beholde, I will sende vpon them the sworde, the famine, and the pestilence, and will make them like vile figges, that cannot bee eaten, they are so naughtie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:8; Jeremiah 15:4; Jeremiah 42:18"> And I will persecute them with the sword, with the famine, and with the pestilence: and I will make them a terror to all kingdomes of the earth, and a curse, and astonishment and an hissing, and a reproche among all the nations whither I haue cast them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:19; Jeremiah 26:5; Jeremiah 7:24–26"> Because they haue not hearde my words, saith the Lord, which I sent vnto them by my seruantes the Prophetes, rising vp earely, and sending them, but yee woulde not heare, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:5; Micah 4:10; Ezekiel 3:15"> Heare ye therefore the word of the Lord all ye of the captiuitie, whome I haue sent from Ierusalem to Babel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14–15; Lamentations 2:14; Jeremiah 29:8–9"> Thus saith the Lord of hostes, the God of Israel, of Ahab the sonne of Kolaiah, and of Zedekiah the sonne of Maaseiah, which prophecie lyes vnto you in my Name, Beholde, I will deliuer them into the hande of Nebuchad-nezzar King of Babel, and he shall slay them before your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 65:15; Daniel 3:6; Daniel 3:21"> And al they of the captiuitie of Iudah, that are in Babel, shall take vp this curse against them, and say, The Lord make thee like Zedekiah and like Ahab, whome the King of Babel burnt in the fire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Hebrews 4:13; Malachi 3:5"> Because they haue committed vilenie in Israel, and haue committed adulterie with their neighbours wiues, and haue spoken lying words in my Name, which I haue not commanded them, euen I knowe it, and testifie it, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:31–32"> Thou shalt also speake to Shemaiah the Nehelamite, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:3; Jeremiah 29:29; Jeremiah 52:24"> Thus speaketh the Lord of hostes, the God of Israel, saying, Because thou hast sent letters in thy Name vnto all the people, that are at Ierusalem, and to Zephaniah the sonne of Maaseiah the Priest, and to all the Priests, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:1–2; 2 Kings 9:11; John 10:20"> The Lord hath made thee Priest for Iehoiada the Priest, that yee should bee officers in the House of the Lord, for euery man that raueth and maketh himselfe a Prophet, to put him in prison and in the stockes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:16; Jeremiah 43:2–3; Jeremiah 29:26"> Nowe therefore why hast not thou reproued Ieremiah of Anathoth, which prophecieth vnto you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:5; Jeremiah 29:1; Jeremiah 29:10"> For, for this cause hee sent vnto vs in Babel, saying, This captiuitie is long: buyld houses to dwell in, and plant gardens, and eate the fruites of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:25"> And Zephaniah the Priest red this letter in the eares of Ieremiah the Prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Then came the worde of the Lord vnto Ieremiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:9; Jeremiah 29:23–24; Jeremiah 14:14–15"> Send to all them of the captiuitie, saying, Thus saith the Lord of Shemaiah the Nehelamite, Because that Shemaiah hath prophecied vnto you, and I sent him not, and hee caused you to trust in a lye,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:16; 2 Kings 7:19–20; Jeremiah 17:6"> Therefore thus saieth the Lord, Behold, I wil visite Shemaiah the Nehelamite, and his seede: hee shall not haue a man to dwell among this people, neither shall he beholde the good, that I will doe for my people, sayth the Lord, because he hath spoken rebelliously against the Lord.</span>
