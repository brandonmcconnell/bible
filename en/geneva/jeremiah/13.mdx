<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:11; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 27:2"> Thus sayth the Lord vnto mee, Goe, and buy thee a linen girdle, and put it vpon thy loynes, and put it not in water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:8; John 15:14; Proverbs 3:5"> So I bought the girdle according to the commandement of the Lord, and put it vpon my loynes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:8"> And the worde of the Lord came vnto me the second time, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 4:10; Psalms 137:1; Jeremiah 51:63–64"> Take the girdle that thou hast bought, which is vpon thy loynes, and arise, goe towarde Perath, and hide it there in the cleft of the rocke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; Exodus 39:42–43; 2 Timothy 2:3"> So I went, and hid it by Perath, as the Lord had commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And after many dayes, the Lord sayde vnto mee, Arise, goe towarde Perath, and take the girdle from thence, which I commanded thee to hide there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:12; Isaiah 64:6; Jeremiah 13:10"> Then went I to Perath, and digged, and tooke the girdle from the place where I had hid it, and behold, the girdle was corrupt, and was profitable for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then the word of the Lord came vnto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremiah 13:15–17; Zephaniah 3:11"> Thus sayth the Lord, After this maner will I destroy the pride of Iudah, and the great pride of Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; Jeremiah 9:14; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16"> This wicked people haue refused to heare my word, and walke after ye stubbernesse of their owne heart, and walke after other gods to serue them, and to worship them: therefore they shalbe as this girdle, which is profitable to nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Jeremiah 33:9; Psalms 81:11"> For as the girdle cleaueth to the loynes of a man, so haue I tied to me the whole house of Israel, and the whole house of Iudah, saith the Lord, that they might bee my people: that they might haue a name and prayse, and glory, but they would not heare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19"> Therefore thou shalt saye vnto them this word, Thus sayth the Lord God of Israel, Euery bottell shalbe filled with wine, and they shall say vnto thee, Doe we not knowe that euery bottell shalbe filled with wine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 63:6; Psalms 75:8; Psalms 60:3"> Then shalt thou say vnto them, Thus saith the Lord, Behold, I will fill all the inhabitants of this land, euen the Kings that sit vpon the throne of Dauid, and the Priestes and the Prophets and all the inhabitantes of Ierusalem with drunkennesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Psalms 2:9; Jeremiah 19:9–11"> And I wil dash them one against another, euen the fathers and the sonnes together, sayeth the Lord: I will not spare, I will not pitie nor haue compassion, but destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 28:14–22; Revelation 2:29; Acts 4:19–20"> Heare and giue eare, be not proude: for the Lord hath spoken it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:9; Isaiah 8:22; Isaiah 5:30"> Giue glory to the Lord your God before he bring darknes, and or euer your feete stumble in the darke mountaines, and whiles you look for light, he turne it into the shadowe of death and make it as darkenesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 2:2; Jeremiah 9:1; Jeremiah 14:17"> But if ye will not heare this, my soule shall weepe in secrete for your pride, and mine eye shall weepe and drop downe teares, because the Lords flocke is caried away captiue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:26; 2 Kings 24:12; 2 Kings 24:15"> Say vnto the King and to the Queene, Humble yourselues, sit downe, for the crowne of your glory shall come downe from your heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:27; Jeremiah 39:9; Deuteronomy 28:52"> The cities of the South shall be shut vp, and no man shall open them: all Iudah shall be caried away captiue: it shall be wholy caried away captiue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:22; Jeremiah 1:14; Habakkuk 1:6"> Lift vp your eyes and beholde them that come from the North: where is the flocke that was giuen thee, euen thy beautifull flocke?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 13:8; Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 5:31"> What wilt thou saye, when hee shall visite thee? (for thou hast taught them to be captaines and as chiefe ouer thee) shall not sorow take thee as a woman in trauaile?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nahum 3:5; Jeremiah 16:10–11; Jeremiah 5:19"> And if thou say in thine heart, Wherefore come these things vpon me? For the multitude of thine iniquities are thy skirts discouered and thy heeles made bare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; Jeremiah 9:5; Matthew 19:24–28"> Can the blacke More change his skin? or the leopard his spots? then may ye also do good, that are accustomed to do euill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 1:4; Leviticus 26:33; Ezekiel 5:12"> Therefore will I scatter them, as the stubble that is taken away with the South winde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 20:29; Jeremiah 2:32; Psalms 9:17"> This is thy portion, and ye part of thy measures from me, sayth the Lord, because thou hast forgotten me and trusted in lyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Lamentations 1:8; Hosea 2:10; Ezekiel 23:29"> Therefore I haue also discouered thy skirts vpon thy face, that thy shame may appeare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hosea 8:5; Ezekiel 6:13; Ezekiel 24:13"> I haue seene thine adulteries, and thy neiings, the filthinesse of thy whoredome on the hils in the fieldes, and thine abominations. Wo vnto thee, O Ierusalem: wilt thou not bee made cleane? when shall it once be?</span>
