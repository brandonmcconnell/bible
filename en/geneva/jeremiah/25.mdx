<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:1; 2 Kings 24:1–2; Daniel 1:1"> The word that came to Ieremiah, concerning all the people of Iudah in the fourth yeere of Iehoiakim the sonne of Iosiah King of Iudah that was in the first yeere of Nebuchad-nezzar King of Babel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:11; Jeremiah 19:14–15; Mark 7:14–15"> The which Ieremiah the Prophet spake vnto all the people of Iudah, and to all the inhabitants of Ierusalem, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:7; Jeremiah 26:5; Jeremiah 1:2"> From the thirteenth yeere of Iosiah the sonne of Amon King of Iudah, euen vnto this day (that is the three and twentieth yeere) the word of the Lord hath come vnto mee, and I haue spoken vnto you rising earely and speaking, but ye woulde not heare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:3; Jeremiah 26:5; Jeremiah 13:10–11"> And the Lord hath sent vnto you all his seruantes the Prophets, rising early and sending them, but yee would not heare, nor encline your eares to obey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:7; Genesis 17:8; Isaiah 55:6–7"> They sayde, Turne againe now euery one from his euill way, and from the wickednes of your inuentions, and ye shall dwell in the lande that the Lord hath giuen vnto you, and to your fathers for euer and euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:19; Jeremiah 7:6; Jeremiah 35:15"> And go not after other gods to serue them and to worshippe them, and prouoke me not to anger with the workes of your hands, and I will not punish you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 21:15; Proverbs 8:36; Deuteronomy 32:21"> Neuerthelesse ye would not heare me, saith the Lord, but haue prouoked mee to anger with the workes of your hands to your owne hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Therefore thus saith the Lord of hostes, Because ye haue not heard my wordes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 13:3; Jeremiah 18:16; Jeremiah 1:15"> Beholde, I will send and take to mee all the families of the North, saith the Lord, and Nebuchad-nezzar the King of Babel my seruant, and will bring them against this lande, and against the inhabitantes thereof, and against all these nations rounde about, and will destroy them, and make them an astonishment and an hissing, and a continuall desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Revelation 18:22–23; Ezekiel 26:13"> Moreouer I will take from them the voyce of mirth and the voyce of gladnesse, the voyce of the bridegrome and the voyce of the bride, the noise of the milstones, and the light of the candle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 9:2; Zechariah 1:12; 2 Chronicles 36:21–22"> And this whole land shalbe desolate, and an astonishment, and these nations shall serue the King of Babel seuentie yeeres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 9:2; Isaiah 13:19; Jeremiah 29:10"> And when the seuentie yeres are accomplished, I will visite the King of Babel and that nation, saith the Lord, for their iniquities, euen the land of the Caldeans, and will make it a perpetuall desolation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 1:5; Daniel 5:31"> And I will bring vpon that lande all my wordes which I haue pronounced against it, euen all that is written in this booke, which Ieremiah hath prophecied against all nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:6; Jeremiah 27:7; Jeremiah 50:9"> For many nations, and great Kings shall euen serue themselues of them: thus will I recompense them according to their deedes, and according to the workes of their owne handes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 75:8; Isaiah 51:17; Revelation 14:10"> For thus hath the Lord God of Israel spoken vnto me, Take the cuppe of wine of this mine indignation at mine hand, and cause all the nations, to whome I sende thee, to drinke it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nahum 3:11; Jeremiah 51:7; Jeremiah 51:39"> And they shall drinke, and be moued and be mad, because of the sworde that I will sende among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 25:28; Ezekiel 43:3"> Then tooke I the cup at the Lordes hand, and made all people to drinke, vnto whome the Lord had sent me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; Psalms 60:3; Jeremiah 44:22"> Euen Ierusalem, and the cities of Iudah, and the Kings thereof, and the princes thereof, to make them desolate, an astonishment, an hissing, and a curse, as appeareth this day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:13–26; Nahum 3:8–10; Jeremiah 46:2"> Pharaoh also, King of Egypt, and his seruants, and his princes, and all his people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 1:1; Ezekiel 30:5; Isaiah 20:1"> And all sortes of people, and all the Kings of the lande of Vz: and all the Kings of the lande of the Philistims, and Ashkelon, and Azzah, and Ekron, and the remnant of Ashdod:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:1–22; Psalms 137:7; Jeremiah 9:26"> Edom, and Moab, and the Ammonites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:4; Amos 1:9–10; Ezekiel 28:22–23"> And all the Kings of Tyrus, and all the kings of Zidon, and the Kings of the Yles, that are beyonde the Sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:26; Jeremiah 49:32; Jeremiah 49:8"> And Dedan, and Tema, and Buz, and all that dwell in the vttermost corners,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:14; Jeremiah 25:20; Ezekiel 30:5"> And all the Kings of Arabia, and all the Kings of Arabia, that dwell in the desert,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 11:11; Genesis 10:22; Isaiah 13:17"> And all the Kings of Zimri, and all the Kings of Elam, and all the Kings of the Medes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:41; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 50:1–46"> And all the Kings of the North, farre and neere one to another, and all the kingdomes of the worlde, which are vpon the earth, and the king of Sheshach shall drinke after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:16; Habakkuk 2:16; Ezekiel 21:4–5"> Therefore say thou vnto them, Thus saith the Lord of hostes, the God of Israel, Drinke and bee drunken, and spewe and fall, and rise no more, because of the sworde, which I will sende among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 34:33; Jeremiah 51:29; Isaiah 14:24–27"> But if they refuse to take the cuppe at thine hande to drinke, then tell them, Thus saith the Lord of hostes, Ye shall certainely drinke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:21; Proverbs 11:31; 1 Peter 4:17"> For loe, I beginne to plague the citie, where my Name is called vpon, and shoulde you goe free? Ye shall not goe quite: for I will call for a sword vpon al the inhabitants of the earth, saith the Lord of hostes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Amos 1:2; Joel 3:16; Isaiah 42:13"> Therefore prophecie thou against them al these words, and say vnto them, The Lord shall roare from aboue, and thrust out his voyce from his holy habitation: he shall roare vpon his habitation, and crie aloude, as they that presse the grapes, against all the inhabitants of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Joel 3:2; Isaiah 66:16"> The sounde shall come to the endes of the earth: for the Lord hath a controuersie with the nations, and will enter into iudgement with all flesh, and he will giue them that are wicked, to the sworde, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:19; Isaiah 34:2; 2 Chronicles 15:6"> Thus saith the Lord of hostes, Behold, a plague shall goe foorth from nation to nation, and a great whirlewinde shalbe raised vp from the coastes of the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 66:16; Jeremiah 8:2; Psalms 79:3"> And the slaine of the Lord shall be at that day, from one ende of the earth, euen vnto the other ende of the earth: they shall not bee mourned, neither gathered nor buried, but shalbe as the dongue vpon the grounde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Jeremiah 50:27; Psalms 2:9"> Howle, ye shepherdes, and crie, and wallowe your selues in the ashes, ye principall of the flocke: for your dayes of slaughter are accomplished, and of your dispersion, and ye shall fall like precious vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 11:20; Amos 2:14; Jeremiah 52:8–11"> And the flight shall faile from the shepherdes, and the escaping from the principall of the flocke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:8; Jeremiah 25:34"> A voyce of the crye of the shepherdes, and an howling of the principall of the flocke shalbe heard: for the Lord hath destroyed their pasture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 27:10–11; Isaiah 32:14"> And the best pastures are destroyed because of the wrath and indignation of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 5:6; Hosea 13:7–8"> He hath forsaken his couert, as the lyon: for their land is waste, because of the wrath of the oppressor, and because of ye wrath of his indignatio.</span>
