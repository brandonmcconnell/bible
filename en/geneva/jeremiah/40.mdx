<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:15; Acts 12:6–7; Ephesians 6:20"> The worde which came to Ieremiah from the Lord after that Nebuzar-adan the chiefe stewarde had let him goe from Ramath, when hee had taken him being bound in chaines among all that were caried away captiue of Ierusalem and Iudah, which were caried away captiue vnto Babel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:24–28; Jeremiah 22:8–9; Lamentations 2:15–17"> And the chiefe stewarde tooke Ieremiah, and said vnto him, The Lord thy God hath pronounced this plague vpon this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 2:5; Jeremiah 50:7; Daniel 9:11–12"> Nowe the Lord hath brought it, and done according as he hath said: because ye haue sinned against the Lord, and haue not obeyed his voyce, therefore this thing is come vpon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:11–12; Genesis 20:15; Genesis 13:9"> And nowe beholde, I loose thee this day from the chaines which were on thine handes, if it please thee to come with me into Babel, come, and I will looke well vnto thee: but if it please thee not to come with mee into Babel, tarie still: beholde, all the lande is before thee: whither it seemeth good, and conuenient for thee to goe, thither goe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:14; 2 Kings 8:7–9; Jeremiah 41:2"> For yet he was not returned: therefore he said, Returne to Gedaliah the sonne of Ahikam, the sonne of Shaphan, whom the King of Babel hath made gouernour ouer all the cities of Iudah, and dwell with him among the people, or goe wheresoeuer it pleaseth thee to goe. So the chiefe stewarde gaue him vitailes and a rewarde, and let him goe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:14; Judges 20:1; Judges 21:1"> Then went Ieremiah vnto Gedaliah the sonne of Ahikam, to Mizpah, and dwelt there with him among the people that were left in the lande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:10; 2 Kings 25:4; Jeremiah 39:4"> Nowe when all the captaines of the hoste, which were in the fieldes, euen they and their men heard, that the King of Babel had made Gedaliah the sonne of Ahikam gouernour in the land, and that he had committed vnto him, men, and women, and children, and of the poore of the lande, that were not caried away captiue to Babel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:1; Deuteronomy 3:14; Jeremiah 42:8"> Then they came to Gedaliah to Mizpah, euen Ishmael the sonne of Nethaniah, and Iohanan, and Ionathan the sonnes of Kareah, and Seraiah the sonne of Tanehumeth, and the sonnes of Ephai, the Netophathite, and Iezaniah the sonne of Maachathi, they and their men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:11; 2 Kings 25:24; 1 Samuel 20:16–17"> And Gedaliah the sonne of Ahikam, the sonne of Shaphan sware vnto them, and to their men, saying, Feare not to serue the Caldeans: dwell in the lande, and serue the King of Babel, and it shall be well with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:12; Deuteronomy 1:38; Isaiah 16:9"> As for me, Beholde, I will dwell at Mizpah to serue the Caldeans, which will come vnto vs: but you, gather you wine, and sommer fruites, and oyle, and put them in your vessels, and dwell in your cities, that ye haue taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 16:4; Genesis 36:8; Jeremiah 24:9"> Likewise when all the Iewes that were in Moab, and among the Ammonites, and in Edom, and that were in all the countries, heard that the King of Babel had left a remnant of Iudah, and that he had set ouer them Gedaliah the sonne of Ahikam the sonne of Shaphan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:5"> Euen all the Iewes returned out of all places where they were driuen, and came to the land of Iudah to Gedaliah vnto Mizpah, and gathered wine and sommer fruites, very much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:1; Jeremiah 40:8"> Moreouer Iohanan the sonne of Kareah, and all the captaines of the hoste, that were in the fieldes, came to Gedaliah to Mizpah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:10; 2 Samuel 10:1–6; Jeremiah 25:21"> And said vnto him, Knowest thou not that Baalis the King of the Ammonites hath sent Ishmael the sonne of Nethaniah to slay thee? But Gedaliah the sonne of Ahikam beleeued them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:8; 2 Samuel 21:17; Jeremiah 42:2"> Then Iohanan the sonne of Kareah spake to Gedaliah in Mizpah secretly, saying, Let me goe, I pray thee, and I will slay Ishmael the sonne of Nethaniah, and no man shall know it. Wherefore should he kill thee, that all the Iewes, which are gathered vnto thee, shoulde be scattered, and the remnant in Iudah perish?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 10:16–17; Romans 3:8; Jeremiah 41:2"> But Gedaliah the sonne of Ahikam said vnto Iohanan the sonne of Kareah, Thou shalt not doe this thing: for thou speakest falsely of Ishmael.</span>
