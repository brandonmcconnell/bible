<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:28; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 7:1"> Moreover, the woorde of the Lord came vnto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:60; Ezekiel 16:8; Jeremiah 11:6"> Goe, and crie in the eares of Ierusalem, saying, Thus sayeth the Lord, I remember thee, with the kindenes of thy youth and the loue of thy marriage, when thou wentest after me in the wildernes in a lande that was not sowen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 14:4; James 1:18; Isaiah 41:11"> Israel was as a thing halowed vnto the Lord, and his first fruits: all they that eat it, shall offend: euil shall come vpon them, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Micah 6:1; Isaiah 51:1–4"> Heare ye the word of the Lord, O house of Iaakob, and all the families of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 17:15; Romans 1:21; Jeremiah 10:14–15"> Thus sayeth the Lord, What iniquitie haue your fathers founde in mee, that they are gone farre from mee, and haue walked after vanitie, and are become vaine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:10; Deuteronomy 8:14–16; Hosea 13:4"> For they saide not, Where is the Lord that brought vs vp out of the lande of Egypt? that led vs through the wildernesse, through a desert, and waste land, through a drie land, and by the shadow of death, by a land that no man passed through, and where no man dwelt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Jeremiah 16:18; Numbers 13:27"> And I brought you into a plentifull countrey, to eat the fruit thereof, and the commodities of the same: but when yee entred, yee defiled my land, and made mine heritage an abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18; Jeremiah 5:31; Jeremiah 10:21"> The priests said not, Where is the Lord? and they that should minister the Lawe, knewe me not: the pastours also offended against me, and the Prophets prophesied in Baal, and went after things that did not profite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:35–36; Jeremiah 2:35; Exodus 20:5"> Wherefore I wil yet plead with you, saith the Lord, and I will pleade with your childrens children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 120:5; 1 Chronicles 23:1; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> For goe ye to the yles of Chittim, and beholde, and sende vnto Kedar. and take diligent heede, and see whether there be such things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 1:23; Isaiah 37:19; Jeremiah 16:20"> Hath any nation changed their gods, which yet are no gods? but my people haue chaged their glorie, for that which doeth not profite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Jeremiah 22:29; Jeremiah 6:19"> O yee heauens, be astonied at this: bee afraid and vtterly confounded, sayeth the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 4:14; John 7:37; Jeremiah 17:13"> For my people haue committed two euils: they haue forsaken mee the fountaine of liuing waters, to digge them pittes, euen broken pittes, that can holde no water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 4:22; Isaiah 50:1; Genesis 15:3"> Is Israel a seruaunt, or is hee borne in the house? why then is he spoiled?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 50:17; Jeremiah 9:11"> The lions roared vpon him and yelled, and they haue made his land waste: his cities are burnt without an inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 43:7–9; Jeremiah 44:1"> Also the children of Noph and Tahapanes haue broken thine head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:18; Jeremiah 2:19; Jeremiah 2:13"> Hast not thou procured this vnto thy selfe, because thou hast forsaken the Lord thy God, when he led thee by the way?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 13:3; Isaiah 31:1; Jeremiah 2:36"> And what hast thou now to do in the way of Egypt? to drinke the water of Nilus? or what makest thou in the way of Asshur? to drinke the water of the Riuer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 3:9; Jeremiah 4:18; Jeremiah 2:17"> Thine owne wickednes shall correct thee, and thy turnings backe shall reprooue thee: know therefore and beholde, that it is an euil thing, and bitter, that thou hast forsaken the Lord thy God, and that my feare is not in thee, sayeth the Lord God of hostes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:2; Leviticus 26:13; Jeremiah 17:2"> For of olde time I haue broken thy yoke, and burst thy bondes, and thou saidest, I will no more transgresse, but like an harlot thou runnest about vpon al hie hilles, and vnder all greene trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:4; Exodus 15:17; Psalms 44:2"> Yet I had planted thee, a noble vine, whose plants were all natural: howe then art thou turned vnto me into the plants of a strange vine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:1; Job 14:17; Hosea 13:12"> Though thou wash thee with nitre, and take thee much sope, yet thine iniquitie is marked before me, sayeth the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:31; Proverbs 30:12; Proverbs 30:20"> Howe canst thou say, I am not polluted, neither haue I followed Baalim? beholde thy waies in the valley, and know, what thou hast done: thou art like a swift dromedarie, that runneth by his waies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:6; Jeremiah 2:27; Hosea 5:15"> And as a wilde asse, vsed to the wildernesse, that snuffeth vp the winde by occasion at her pleasure: who can turne her backe? all they that seeke her, will not wearie themselues, but wil finde her in her moneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16; Jeremiah 18:12; Jeremiah 3:13"> Keepe thou thy feete from barenes, and thy throte from thirst: but thou saidest desperately, No, for I haue loued strangers, and them will I follow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezra 9:7; Nehemiah 9:32–34; Jeremiah 32:32"> As the theefe is ashamed, when he is foud, so is the house of Israel ashamed, they, their kings, their princes and their Priests, and their Prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:16; Hosea 7:14; Psalms 78:34–37"> Saying to a tree, Thou art my father, and to a stone, Thou hast begotten me: for they haue turned their back vnto me, and not their face: but in ye time of their troble they wil say, Arise, and help vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:13; Deuteronomy 32:37; Judges 10:14"> But where are thy gods, that thou hast made thee? let them arise, if they can helpe thee in the time of thy trouble: for according to the nomber of thy cities, are thy gods, O Iudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:13; Daniel 9:11; Jeremiah 5:1"> Wherefore wil ye pleade with me? ye all haue rebelled against me, sayeth the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26; 1 Thessalonians 2:15; Acts 7:52"> I haue smitten your children in vaine, they receiued no correction: your owne sworde hath deuoured your Prophets like a destroying lyon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:7–9; Deuteronomy 32:15; Micah 6:9"> O generation, take heede to the worde of the Lord: haue I bene as a wildernesse vnto Israel? or a lande of darkenesse? Wherefore sayeth my people then, We are lordes, we will come no more vnto thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 106:21; Jeremiah 13:25; Jeremiah 3:21"> Can a maid forget her ornament, or a bride her attire? yet my people haue forgotten me, daies without number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 57:7–10; Jeremiah 2:36; Ezekiel 16:27"> Why doest thou prepare thy way, to seeke amitie? euen therefore will I teach thee, that thy waies are wickednesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Kings 21:16; Jeremiah 19:4; 2 Kings 24:4"> Also in thy wings is founde the bloud of the soules of ye poore innocents: I haue not found it in holes, but vpon all these places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; 1 John 1:8–10; Job 33:9"> Yet thou saiest, Because I am giltles, surely his wrath shall turne from mee: beholde, I will enter with thee into iudgement, because thou saiest, I haue not sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:22; Hosea 5:13; 2 Chronicles 28:16"> Why runnest thou about so much to change thy waies? for thou shalt be confounded of Egypt, as thou art confounded of Asshur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:19; Jeremiah 37:7–10; Jeremiah 17:5"> For thou shalt goe foorth from thence, and thine hands vpon thine head, because the Lord hath reiected thy confidence, and thou shalt not prosper thereby.</span>
