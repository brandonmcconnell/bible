<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:22; Jeremiah 30:24; Zechariah 13:9"> At the same time, saith the Lord, will I be the God of all the families of Israel, and they shall be my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 10:33; Psalms 95:11; Matthew 11:28"> Thus saith the Lord, The people which escaped the sworde, founde grace in the wildernes: he walked before Israel to cause him to rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 103:17; Isaiah 54:8–9; Hosea 11:4"> The Lord hath appeared vnto me of old, say they: Yea, I haue loued thee with an euerlasting loue, therefore with mercie I haue drawen thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:7; Jeremiah 31:13; Amos 5:2"> Againe I will builde thee, and thou shalt be builded, O virgine Israel: thou shalt stil be adorned with thy timbrels, and shalt goe foorth in the dance of them that be ioyfull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 9:14; Leviticus 19:23–25; Deuteronomy 28:30"> Thou shalt yet plant vines vpon the mountaines of Samaria, and the planters that plant them, shall make them common.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:4–5; Micah 4:1–3; Jeremiah 50:19"> For the dayes shall come that the watchmen vpon the mount of Ephraim shall cry, Arise, and let vs go vp vnto Zion to the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 14:7; Psalms 28:9; Isaiah 37:31"> For thus saith the Lord, Reioyce with gladnesse for Iaakob, and shoute for ioye among the chiefe of the Gentiles: publish praise, and say, O Lord, saue thy people, the remnant of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Isaiah 42:16; Jeremiah 23:8"> Beholde, I will bring them from the North countrey, and gather them from the coastes of the world, with the blinde and the lame among them, with the woman with childe, and her that is deliuered also: a great companie shall returne hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:4; Jeremiah 3:4; Exodus 4:22"> They shall come weeping, and with mercie will I bring them againe: I will lead them by the riuers of water in a straight way, wherein they shall not stumble: for I am a father to Israel, and Ephraim is my first borne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; Ezekiel 34:12; Deuteronomy 30:4"> Heare the worde of the Lord, O ye Gentiles, and declare in the yles afarre off, and say, Hee that scattered Israel, wil gather him and wil keepe him, as a shepheard doeth his flocke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Titus 2:14; Isaiah 44:23"> For the Lord hath redeemed Iaakob, and ransomed him from the hande of him, that was stronger then he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 35:10; Isaiah 58:11; Joel 3:18"> Therefore they shall come, and reioyce in the height of Zion, and shall runne to the bountifulnes of the Lord, euen for the wheat and for the wine, and for the oyle, and for the increase of sheepe, and bullocks: and their soule shalbe as a watered garden, and they shall haue no more sorow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 30:11; Isaiah 61:3; Isaiah 51:11"> Then shall ye virgine reioyce in the dance, and the yong men and the old men together: for I wil turne their mourning into ioy, and wil comfort them, and giue them ioy for their sorowes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:25; 1 Peter 2:9; Jeremiah 33:9"> And I wil replenish the soule of the Priests with fatnesse, and my people shalbe satisfied with my goodnesse, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 2:16–18; Jeremiah 40:1; Psalms 77:2"> Thus saith the Lord, A voyce was heard on hie, a mourning and bitter weeping. Rahel weeping for her children, refused to be comforted for her children, because they were not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ruth 2:12; Jeremiah 30:3; Hebrews 6:10"> Thus saith the Lord, Refraine thy voyce from weeping, and thine eyes from teares: for thy worke shalbe rewarded, saith the Lord, and they shall come againe from the land of the enemie:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 3:21; Jeremiah 29:11–16; Lamentations 3:18"> And there is hope in thine ende, saith the Lord, that thy children shall come againe to their owne borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Psalms 80:3; Job 5:17"> I haue heard Ephraim lamenting thus, Thou hast corrected me, and I was chastised as an vntamed calfe: conuert thou me, and I shalbe conuerted: for thou art the Lord my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:31; Jeremiah 3:25; Ezekiel 21:12"> Surely after that I conuerted, I repented: and after that I was instructed, I smote vpon my thigh: I was ashamed, yea, euen confounded, because I did beare the reproch of my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 55:7; Micah 7:18–19; Isaiah 63:15"> Is Ephraim my deare sonne or pleasant childe? yet since I spake vnto him, I still remembred him: therefore my bowels are troubled for him: I wil surely haue compassion vpon him, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; Isaiah 62:10; Psalms 84:5"> Set thee vp signes: make thee heapes: set thine heart towarde the path and way, that thou hast walked: turne againe, O virgine of Israel: turne againe to these thy cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:4; Jeremiah 7:24; Matthew 1:21"> How long wilt thou goe astray, O thou rebellious daughter? for the Lord hath created a newe thing in the earth: A WOMAN shall compasse a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3; Isaiah 1:26; Jeremiah 50:7"> Thus saith the Lord of hostes, the God of Israel, Yet shall they say this thing in the land of Iudah, and in the cities thereof, when I shall bring againe their captiuitie, The Lord blesse thee, O habitation of iustice and holy mountaine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 8:4–8; Ezekiel 36:10; Zechariah 2:4"> And Iudah shall dwell in it, and all the cities thereof together, the husbandmen and they that goe foorth with the flocke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:6; Matthew 11:28; John 4:14"> For I haue saciate the wearie soule, and I haue replenished euery sorowfull soule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 127:2; Zechariah 4:1–2"> Therefore I awaked and behelde, and my sleepe was sweete vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; Zechariah 10:9; Ezekiel 36:9"> Beholde, the dayes come, saith the Lord, that I will sowe the house of Israel, and the house of Iudah with the seede of man and with the seede of beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 18:7–9"> And like as I haue watched vpon them, to plucke vp and to roote out, and to throw downe, and to destroy, and to plague them, so wil I watch ouer them, to build and to plant them, saith ye Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Lamentations 5:7; Ezekiel 18:2–3; Jeremiah 31:30"> In those dayes shall they say no more, The fathers haue eaten a sowre grape, and the childrens teeth are set on edge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:20; Isaiah 3:11; Deuteronomy 24:16"> But euery one shall die for his owne iniquitie: euery man that eateth the sowre grape, his teeth shalbe set on edge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 10:16–17; Ezekiel 37:26; Jeremiah 31:31–34"> Beholde, the dayes come, saith the Lord, that I will make a new couenant with the house of Israel, and with the house of Iudah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 24:6–8; Deuteronomy 5:3; Exodus 19:5"> Not according to ye couenant that I made with their fathers, when I tooke them by the hand to bring them out of the land of Egypt, the which my couenant they brake, although I was an husband vnto them, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:3; Hebrews 10:16; Hebrews 8:10"> But this shall be the couenant that I will make with the house of Israel, After those dayes, saith the Lord, I will put my Lawe in their inward partes, and write it in their hearts, and wil be their God, and they shalbe my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Hebrews 8:12; 1 John 2:27"> And they shall teach no more euery man his neighbour and euery man his brother, saying, Know the Lord: for they shall all know me from the least of them vnto the greatest of them, saith the Lord: for I wil forgiue their iniquitie, and will remember their sinnes no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Isaiah 51:15; Psalms 136:7–9"> Thus saith the Lord, which giueth the sunne for a light to the day, and the courses of the moone and of the starres for a light to the night, which breaketh the sea, when the waues thereof roare: his Name is the Lord of hostes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 54:9–10; Jeremiah 33:20–26; Psalms 89:36–37"> If these ordinances depart out of my sight, saith the Lord, then shall the seede of Israel cease from being a nation before me, for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:24–26; Isaiah 40:12; Jeremiah 33:22"> Thus saith the Lord, If the heauens can be measured, or the fundations of the earth be searched out beneath, then wil I cast off all the seed of Israel, for all that they haue done, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 14:10; 2 Kings 14:13; Jeremiah 30:18"> Behold, the dayes come, saith the Lord, that the citie shalbe built to the Lord from the tower of Hananeel, vnto the gate of the corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Zechariah 2:1–2; Ezekiel 40:8"> And the line of the measure shall go foorth in his presence vpon the hil Gareb, and shall compasse about to Goath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:23; Joel 3:17; 2 Kings 11:16"> And the whole valley of the dead bodies, and of the ashes, and all the fields vnto the brooke of Kidron, and vnto the corner of the horsegate toward the East, shalbe holy vnto the Lord, neither shall it be plucked vp nor destroyed any more for euer.</span>
