<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:8; Jeremiah 51:63; Jeremiah 42:22"> Nowe when Ieremiah had made an ende of speaking vnto ye whole people all the wordes of the Lord their God, for the which the Lord their God had sent him to them, euen all these wordes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:13; Isaiah 7:9; Jeremiah 40:8"> Then spake Azariah the sonne of Hoshaiah, and Iohanan the sonne of Kareah, and all the proude men, saying vnto Ieremiah, Thou speakest falsely: the Lord our God hath not sent thee to say, Goe not into Egypt to dwell there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 45:1–3; Jeremiah 36:4; Jeremiah 36:26"> But Baruch ye sonne of Neriah prouoketh thee against vs, for to deliuer vs into the hand of the Caldeans, that they might slay vs, and cary vs away captiues into Babel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:5–6; Psalms 37:3; 2 Chronicles 25:16"> So Iohanan the sonne of Kareah, and all the captaines of the hoste, and all the people obeied not the voyce of the Lord, to dwell in the lande of Iudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:11–12; Jeremiah 41:15–16; 1 Samuel 26:19"> But Iohanan the sonne of Kareah, and all the captaines of the hoste tooke all the remnant of Iudah, that were returned from al nations, whither they had bene driuen, to dwel in ye land of Iudah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:10; Jeremiah 40:7; Jeremiah 41:10"> Euen men and women, and children, and the Kinges daughters, and euery person, that Nebuzar-adan the chiefe steward had left with Gedaliah the sonne of Ahikam, ye sonne of Shaphan, and Ieremiah the Prophet, and Baruch the sonne of Neriah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:16; Jeremiah 44:1; Isaiah 30:4"> So they came into the lande of Egypt: for they obeied not the voice of the Lord: thus came they to Tahpanhes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:16; Psalms 139:7; 2 Timothy 2:9"> Then came the worde of the Lord vnto Ieremiah in Tahpanhes, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:2–12; Jeremiah 19:1–15; Jeremiah 51:63–64"> Take great stones in thine hand, and hide them in the claie in the bricke kill, which is at the entrie of Pharaohs house in Tanpanhes in ye sight of the men of Iudah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 44:28–1; Jeremiah 1:15; Psalms 31:20"> And say vnto them, Thus sayeth the Lord of hostes the God of Israel, Beholde, I will sende and bring Nebuchad-nezzar the King of Babel my seruant, and will set his throne vpon these stones that I haue hid, and he shall spread his pauilion ouer them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:2; Jeremiah 44:13; Ezekiel 29:19–20"> And when he shall come, he shall smite the land of Egypt: such as are appoynted for death, to death, and such as are for captiuitie, to captiuitie, and such as are for the sword to the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:13; Psalms 109:18–19; Isaiah 19:1"> And I wil kindle a fire in the houses of the gods of Egypt, and he shall burne them and carie them away captiues, and he shall aray himself with the land of Egypt, as a shepheard putteth on his garment, and shall depart from thence in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 19:18"> He shall breake also ye images of Beth-shemesh, that is in the lande of Egypt, and the houses of the gods of the Egyptians shall he burne with fire.</span>
