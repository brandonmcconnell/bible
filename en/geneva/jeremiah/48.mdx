<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 32:3; Numbers 32:37–38; Jeremiah 48:22–23"> Concerning Moab, thus saith ye Lord of hostes, the God of Israel, Woe vnto Nebo: for it is wasted: Kiriathaim is confounded and taken: Misgab is confounded and afraide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 32:37; Jeremiah 31:36; Isaiah 16:14"> Moab shall boast no more of Heshbon: for they haue deuised euill against it. Come, and let vs destroy it, that it be no more a nation: also thou shalt be destroyed, O Madmen, and the sworde shall pursue thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 15:5; Jeremiah 48:34; Jeremiah 48:5"> A voyce of crying shall be from Horonaim with desolation and great destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 137:9; Numbers 21:27–30; Esther 8:11"> Moab is destroyed: her litle ones haue caused their crie to be heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 15:5"> For at the going vp of Luhith, the mourner shall goe vp with weeping: for in the going downe of Horonaim, the enemies haue heard a cry of destruction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:6; Jeremiah 51:6; Job 30:3–7"> Flee and saue your liues, and be like vnto the heath in the wildernesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 21:29; Jeremiah 9:23; Psalms 52:7"> For because thou hast trusted in thy workes and in thy treasures, thou shalt also be taken, and Chemosh shall goe forth into captiuitie with his Priestes and his princes together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Jeremiah 51:56; Jeremiah 25:9"> And the destroyer shall come vpon all cities, and no citie shall escape: the valley also shall perish and the plaine shalbe destroyed as the Lord hath spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 11:1; Jeremiah 48:28; Isaiah 16:2"> Giue wings vnto Moab, that it may flee and get away: for the cities thereof shalbe desolate, without any to dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; 1 Kings 20:42; Judges 5:23"> Cursed be he that doeth the worke of the Lord negligently, and cursed be he that keepeth backe his sword from blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:12; Zechariah 1:15; Jeremiah 48:29"> Moab hath bene at rest from his youth, and he hath setled on his lees, and hath not bene powred from vessell to vessell, neither hath he gone into captiuitie: therefore his taste remained in him and his sent is not changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nahum 2:2; Isaiah 30:14; Ezekiel 25:9–10"> Therefore beholde, the dayes come, saith the Lord, that I will send vnto him such as shall carie him away, and shall emptie his vessels, and breake their bottels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 45:16; Judges 11:24; Jeremiah 48:39"> And Moab shalbe ashamed of Chemosh as the house of Israel was ashamed of Beth-el their confidence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 33:16; Isaiah 10:13; Isaiah 10:16"> Howe thinke you thus, We are mightie and strong men of warre?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:27; Jeremiah 46:18; Jeremiah 51:57"> Moab is destroyed, and his cities burnt vp, and his chose yong men are gone downe to slaughter, saith ye King, whose name is ye Lord of hostes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 13:22; Jeremiah 1:12; Ezekiel 12:28"> The destruction of Moab is ready to come, and his plague hasteth fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Isaiah 14:4–5; Ezekiel 19:11–14"> All ye that are about him, mourne for him, and all ye that knowe his name, say, Howe is the strong staffe broken, and the beautifull rod!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 21:30; Isaiah 47:1; Isaiah 15:2"> Thou daughter that doest inhabite Dibon, come downe from thy glory, and sit in thirst: for the destroyer of Moab shall come vpon thee, and he shall destroy thy strong holdes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36; 1 Samuel 4:16; 1 Samuel 4:13–14"> Thou that dwellest in Aroer, stand by the way, and beholde: aske him that fleeth and that escapeth, and say, What is done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 16:2; Numbers 21:26–28; Deuteronomy 2:36"> Moab is cofouded: for it is destroied: howle, and cry, tell ye it in Arnon, that Moab is made waste,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 13:18; Isaiah 15:4; Jeremiah 48:8"> And iudgement is come vpon the plaine countrey, vpon Holon and vpon Iahazah, and vpon Mephaath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:18; Numbers 33:46; Numbers 32:34"> And vpon Dibon, and vpon Nebo, and vpon the house of Diblathaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 13:17; Jeremiah 48:1; Numbers 32:38"> And vpon Kiriathaim, and vpon Beth-gamul, and vpon Beth-meon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 2:2; Jeremiah 48:41; Joshua 21:36"> And vpon Kerioth, and vpon Bozrah, and vpon all the cities of ye land of Moab farre or neere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 75:10; Psalms 10:15; Zechariah 1:19–21"> The horne of Moab is cut off, and his arme is broken, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:42; Isaiah 29:9; Jeremiah 51:39"> Make ye him drunken: for he magnified himselfe against the Lord: Moab shall wallowe in his vomite, and he also shalbe in derision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:26; Micah 7:8–10; Zephaniah 2:8"> For diddest not thou deride Israel, as though he had bene found among theeues? for when thou speakest of him, thou art moued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 55:6–7; Judges 6:2; Song of Solomon 2:14"> O ye that dwell in Moab, leaue the cities, and dwell in the rockes, and be like the doue, that maketh her nest in the sides of the holes mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 16:6; Psalms 138:6; Isaiah 2:11–12"> We haue heard the pride of Moab (hee is exceeding proude) his stoutnesse, and his arrogancie, and his pride, and the hautinesse of his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:36; Isaiah 16:6; Isaiah 37:28–29"> I know his wrath, saith ye Lord, but it shall not be so: and his dissimulatios, for they do not right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 15:5; 2 Kings 3:25; Jeremiah 48:36"> Therefore will I howle for Moab, and I will crie out for all Moab: mine heart shall mourne for the men of Kir-heres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 16:8–9; Numbers 21:32; Joshua 13:19"> O vine of Sibmah, I will weepe for thee, as I wept for Iazer: thy plants are gone ouer the sea, they are come to the sea of Iazer: ye destroyer is fallen vpon thy somer fruites, and vpon thy vintage,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 5:10; Haggai 2:16; Jeremiah 25:9–10"> And ioye, and gladnesse is taken from the plentifull fielde, and from the land of Moab: and I haue caused wine to faile from the winepresse: none shall treade with shouting: their shouting shall be no shouting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 15:4–6; Numbers 32:3; Deuteronomy 34:3"> From the cry of Heshbon vnto Elaleh and vnto Iahaz haue they made their noyse from Zoar vnto Horonaim, ye heiffer of three yere old shall go lowing: for ye waters also of Nimrim shalbe wasted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 15:2; Isaiah 16:12; Numbers 28:14"> Moreouer, I will cause to cease in Moab, saith the Lord, him that offered in the high places, and him that burneth incense to his gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 15:7; Isaiah 16:11; Isaiah 15:5"> Therefore mine heart shall sounde for Moab like a shaume, and mine heart shall sound like a shaume for the men of Ker-heres, because the riches that he hath gotten, is perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:5; Genesis 37:34; Jeremiah 47:5"> For euery head shalbe balde, and euery beard plucked: vpon all the handes shall be cuttings, and vpon the loynes sackecloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:28; Jeremiah 25:34; Isaiah 22:1"> And mourning shall be vpon all the house toppes of Moab and in all the streetes thereof: for I haue broken Moab like a vessell wherein is no pleasure, sayeth the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4–6; Lamentations 4:1; Lamentations 2:1"> They shall howle, saying, How is he destroyed? howe hath Moab turned the backe with shame? so shall Moab be a derision, and a feare to all them about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 8:8; Deuteronomy 28:49; Jeremiah 49:22"> For thus saith the Lord, Beholde, he shall flee as an eagle, and shall spread his wings ouer Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 21:3; Isaiah 13:8; Micah 4:9–10"> The cities are taken, and the strong holdes are wonne, and ye mightie mens hearts in Moab at that day shalbe as ye heart of a woman in trauaile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:2; Isaiah 7:8; Isaiah 37:23"> And Moab shall be destroyed from being a people, because he hath set vp himselfe against the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Lamentations 3:47; Isaiah 24:17–18; Deuteronomy 32:23–25"> Feare, and pit and snare shall be vpon thee, O inhabitant of Moab, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:23; 1 Kings 19:17; Jeremiah 46:21"> He that escapeth from the feare, shall fall in the pit, and he that getteth vp out of the pit, shall be taken in the snare: for I will bring vpon it, euen vpon Moab, the yeere of their visitation, sayeth the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; Numbers 21:28; Zechariah 10:4"> They that fled, stoode vnder the shadowe of Heshbon, because of the force: for the fire came out of Heshbon, and a flame from Sihon, and deuoured the corner of Moab, and the top of the seditious children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 21:29; Jeremiah 48:7; Judges 11:24"> Wo be vnto thee, O Moab: the people of Chemosh perisheth: for thy sonnes are taken captiues, and thy daughters led into captiuitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:39; Jeremiah 49:6; Jeremiah 23:20"> Yet will I bring againe the captiuitie of Moab in the latter dayes, sayeth the Lord. Thus farre of the iudgement of Moab.</span>
