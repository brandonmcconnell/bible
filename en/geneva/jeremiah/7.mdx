<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The woordes that came to Ieremiah from the Lord, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:2; Revelation 3:22; Amos 7:16"> Stand in the gate of the Lordes house and crie this woorde there, and say, Heare the woorde of the Lord, all yee of Iudah that enter in at these gates to worship the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:11; Jeremiah 26:13; Isaiah 1:16–19"> Thus sayeth the Lord of hostes, the God of Israel, Amend your waies and your woorkes, and I will let you dwell in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Matthew 3:9–10; Zephaniah 3:11"> Trust not in lying woordes, saying, The Temple of the Lord, the Temple of the Lord: this is the Temple of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:12–13; Isaiah 1:19; Jeremiah 7:3"> For if you amende and redresse your waies and your woorkes: if you execute iudgement betweene a man and his neighbour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:21–24; Zechariah 7:9–12; Jeremiah 13:10"> And oppresse not the stranger, the fatherlesse and the widow and shed no innocent blood in this place, neither walke after other gods to your destruction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Jeremiah 25:5; Jeremiah 3:18"> Then will I let you dwell in this place in the lande that I gaue vnto your fathers, for euer and euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 28:15; Ezekiel 13:6–16; Jeremiah 23:14–16"> Beholde, you trust in lying woordes, that can not profite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:6; Exodus 20:3; James 4:1–4"> Will you steale, murder, and commit adulterie, and sweare falsely and burne incense vnto Baal, and walke after other gods whome yee knowe not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:34; Ezekiel 23:37; Ezekiel 23:39"> And come and stande before mee in this House, whereupon my Name is called, and saye, We are deliuered, though we haue done all these abominations?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:45–46; Isaiah 56:7; Matthew 21:13"> Is this House become a denne of theeues, whereupon my Name is called before your eyes? Beholde, euen I see it, sayeth the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Jeremiah 26:6; 1 Samuel 4:10–11"> But go ye nowe vnto my place which was in Shilo, where I set my Name at the beginning, and beholde, what I did to it for the wickednesse of my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Jeremiah 7:25"> Therefore nowe because yee haue done all these woorkes, sayeth the Lord, (and I rose vp earely and spake vnto you: but when I spake, yee would not heare me, neither when I called, would yee answere).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 78:60; 2 Kings 25:9; Jeremiah 7:4"> Therefore will I do vnto this House, wherupon my Name is called, wherein also yee trust, euen vnto the place that I gaue to you and to your fathers, as I haue done vnto Shilo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:1; 2 Kings 17:23; Jeremiah 52:3"> And I will cast you out of my sight, as I haue cast out all your brethren, euen the whole seede of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:14; Jeremiah 14:11–12; Jeremiah 15:1"> Therfore thou shalt not pray for this people, neither lift vp crie or praier for them neither intreat me, for I will not heare thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:6–18; Ezekiel 14:23; Jeremiah 6:27"> Seest thou not what they doe in the cities of Iudah and in the streetes of Ierusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:13; Jeremiah 44:17–19; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> The children gather wood, and the fathers kindle the fire, and the women knede the dough to make cakes to the Queene of heauen and to powre out drinke offrings vnto other gods, that they may prouoke me vnto anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:11; Jeremiah 2:19; Daniel 9:7–8"> Doe they prouoke me to anger, sayeth the Lord, and not themselues to the confusion of their owne faces?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:18; Lamentations 2:3–5; Isaiah 42:25"> Therefore thus sayeth the Lord God, Beholde, mine anger and my wrath shall be powred vpon this place, vpon man and vpon beast, and vpon the tree of the fielde, and vpon the fruite of the grounde, and it shall burne and not bee quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Jeremiah 6:20; Amos 5:21–23"> Thus sayth the Lord of hostes, the God of Israel, Put your burnt offerings vnto your sacrifices, and eat the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; 1 Samuel 15:22; Psalms 51:16–17"> For I spake not vnto your fathers, nor commanded them, when I brought them out of the land of Egypt, concerning burnt offrings and sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Deuteronomy 5:33; Exodus 19:5–6"> But this thing commanded I them, saying, Obey my voyce, and I will be your God, and yee shalbe my people: and walke yee in all the wayes which I haue commanded you, that it may be well vnto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 81:11–12; Jeremiah 8:5; Jeremiah 7:26"> But they would not obey, nor incline their eare, but went after the counsels and the stubbernesse of their wicked heart, and went backewarde and not forwarde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15; Jeremiah 25:4; Nehemiah 9:26"> Since the day that your fathers came vp out of the lande of Egypt, vnto this day, I haue euen sent vnto you al my seruants the Prophets, rising vp earely euery day, and sending them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Jeremiah 7:24"> Yet would they not heare me nor encline their eare, but hardened their necke and did worse then their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:2; Jeremiah 1:7; Isaiah 65:12"> Therefore shalt thou speake al these words vnto them, but they will not heare thee: thou shalt also crie vnto them, but they will not answere thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 59:14–15; Proverbs 1:7; Isaiah 1:4–5"> But thou shalt say vnto them, This is a nation that heareth not the voice of the Lord their God, nor receiueth discipline: trueth is perished, and is cleane gone out of their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 1:20; Micah 1:16; Jeremiah 6:30"> Cut off thine heare, O Ierusalem, and cast it away, and take vp a complaint on the hie places: for the Lord hath reiected and forsaken the generation of his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:34; Ezekiel 7:20; Daniel 9:27"> For the children of Iudah haue done euill in my sight, sayth the Lord: they haue set their abominations in the House, whereupon my Name is called, to pollute it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 23:10; Jeremiah 32:35; Leviticus 18:21"> And they haue built the hie place of Topheth, which is in the valley of Ben-Hinnom to burne their sonnes and their daughters in the fire, which I commanded them not, neither came it in mine heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:11; Jeremiah 19:6; 2 Kings 23:10"> Therefore beholde, the dayes come, sayeth the Lord, that it shall no more be called Topheth, nor the valley of Ben-Hinnom, but the valley of slaughter: for they shall burie in Topheth til there be no place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:9; Deuteronomy 28:26; Revelation 19:17–18"> And ye carkeises of this people shalbe meat for the foules of the heauen and for the beastes of the earth, and none shall fraie them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Hosea 2:11; Ezekiel 26:13"> Then I will cause to cease from the cities of Iudah and from the streetes of Ierusalem the voice of mirth and the voice of gladnesse, the voice of the bridegrom and the voice of the bride: for the lande shalbe desolate.</span>
