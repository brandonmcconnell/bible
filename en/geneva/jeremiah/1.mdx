<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:60; Jeremiah 11:21; 2 Chronicles 36:21"> The wordes of Ieremiah the sonne of Hilkiah one of the Priests that were at Anathoth in the lande of Beniamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 13:20; Jonah 1:1; Jeremiah 1:11"> To whom the worde of the Lord came in the dayes of Iosiah the sonne of Amon King of Iudah in the thirteenth yeere of his reigne:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:2; 2 Chronicles 36:5–8; 2 Kings 23:34"> And also in the dayes of Iehoiakim the sonne of Iosiah King of Iudah vnto the ende of the eleuenth yeere of Zedekiah, the sonne of Iosiah King of Iudah, euen vnto the carying away of Ierusalem captiue in the fift moneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:16; Ezekiel 1:3; Jeremiah 1:2"> Then the worde of the Lord came vnto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 139:16; Isaiah 49:1; Galatians 1:15–16"> Before I formed thee in the wombe, I knewe thee, and before thou camest out of the wombe, I sanctified thee, and ordeined thee to be a Prophet vnto the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 6:12; Exodus 4:10–16; 1 Kings 3:7–9"> Then said I, Oh, Lord God, behold, I can not speake, for I am a childe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Ezekiel 3:27; Numbers 22:20"> But the Lord said vnto me, Say not, I am a childe: for thou shalt goe to all that I shall send thee, and whatsoeuer I command thee, shalt thou speake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 51:7"> Be not afraide of their faces: for I am with thee to deliuer thee, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Exodus 4:11–12; Luke 12:12"> Then the Lord stretched out his hand and touched my mouth, and the Lord said vnto me, Beholde, I haue put my wordes in thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Isaiah 44:26–28"> Beholde, this day haue I set thee ouer the nations and ouer the kingdomes to plucke vp, and to roote out, and to destroye and throwe downe, to builde, and to plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 7:8; Amos 8:2; Zechariah 5:2"> After this the worde of the Lord came vnto me, saying, Ieremiah, what seest thou? And I said, I see a rod of an almonde tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:28; Ezekiel 12:25; Ezekiel 12:22–23"> Then saide the Lord vnto me, Thou hast seene aright: for I will hasten my worde to performe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 11:7; Zechariah 4:2"> Againe the worde of the Lord came vnto me the second time, saying, What seest thou? And I saide, I see a seething pot looking out of the North.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 41:25; Jeremiah 4:6; Jeremiah 10:22"> Then saide the Lord vnto me, Out of the North shall a plague be spred vpon all the inhabitants of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:10; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 9:11"> For loe, I will call all the families of the kingdomes of the North, saith the Lord, and they shall come, and euery one shall set his throne in the entring of the gates of Ierusalem, and on all the walles thereof rounde about, and in all the cities of Iudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:9; Jeremiah 19:4; Acts 7:41"> And I will declare vnto them my iudgements touching all the wickednesse of them that haue forsaken me, and haue burnt incense vnto other gods, and worshipped the workes of their owne handes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:7–8; 1 Kings 18:46; 1 Peter 1:13"> Thou therefore trusse vp thy loynes, and arise and speake vnto them all that I commaund thee: be not afraide of their faces, lest I destroy thee before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 50:7; Ezekiel 3:8–9; Jeremiah 15:20"> For I, beholde, I this day haue made thee a defenced citie, and an yron pillar and walles of brasse against the whole lande, against the Kings of Iudah, and against the princes thereof, against the Priestes thereof and against the people of the lande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Jeremiah 1:8; Jeremiah 20:11"> For they shall fight against thee, but they shall not preuaile against thee: for I am with thee to deliuer thee, sayth the Lord.</span>
