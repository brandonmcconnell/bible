<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:15; Jeremiah 1:1–2"> The worde, that came to Ieremiah from the Lord, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:32; Isaiah 30:8; Habakkuk 2:2–3"> Thus speaketh the Lord God of Israel, saying, Write thee all the wordes, that I haue spoken vnto thee in a booke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14; Jeremiah 16:15; Jeremiah 30:18"> For loe, the dayes come, saith the Lord, that I wil bring againe the captiuitie of my people Israel and Iudah, saith the Lord: for I will restore them vnto the lande, that I gaue to their fathers, and they shall possesse it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Againe, these are the wordes that the Lord spake concerning Israel, and concerning Iudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:10–11; Amos 8:10; Isaiah 5:30"> For thus saith the Lord, wee haue heard a terrible voyce, of feare and not of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nahum 2:10; Joel 2:6; Jeremiah 4:31"> Demand now and beholde, if man trauayle with childe? wherefore doe I beholde euery man with his hands on his loynes as a woman in trauaile, and all faces are turned into a palenesse?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Zechariah 14:1–2; Malachi 4:1"> Alas, for this day is great: none hath bene like it: it is euen the time of Iaakobs trouble, yet shall he be deliuered from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nahum 1:13; Ezekiel 34:27; Isaiah 9:4"> For in that day, sayth the Lord of hostes, I will breake his yoke from off thy necke, and breake thy bondes, and strangers shall no more serue themselues of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 3:5; Isaiah 55:3–5; Luke 1:69"> But they shall serue the Lord their God, and Dauid their King, whom I will raise vp vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:27–28; Isaiah 43:5; Jeremiah 29:14"> Therefore feare not, O my seruant Iaakob, saith the Lord, neither be afrayde, O Israel: for loe, I will deliuer thee from a farre countrey, and thy seede from the lande of their captiuitie, and Iaakob shall turne againe, and shalbe in rest and prosperitie and none shall make him afraide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:24; Jeremiah 1:19; Isaiah 8:10"> For I am with thee, sayth the Lord, to saue thee: though I vtterly destroy all the nations where I haue scattered thee, yet will I not vtterly destroy thee, but I will correct thee by iudgement, and not vtterly cut thee off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:18; Jeremiah 30:15; Jeremiah 14:17"> For thus saith the Lord, Thy bruising is incurable, and thy wound is dolorous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:11; Jeremiah 8:22; Jeremiah 30:17"> There is none to iudge thy cause, or to lay a plaister: there are no medicines, nor help for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:6; Job 30:21; Lamentations 1:2"> All thy louers haue forgotten thee: they seeke thee not: for I haue striken thee with the wound of an enemie, and with a sharpe chastisement for ye multitude of thine iniquities, because thy sinnes were increased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:14; Jeremiah 30:12; Jeremiah 6:6–7"> Why cryest thou for thine affliction? thy sorowe is incurable, for the multitude of thine iniquities: because thy sinnes were increased, I haue done these things vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:25; Isaiah 33:1; Exodus 23:22"> Therefore all they that deuoure thee, shall be deuoured, and all thine enemies euery one shall goe into captiuitie: and they that spoyle thee, shalbe spoyled, and all they that robbe thee, wil I giue to be robbed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Jeremiah 33:6; 1 Peter 2:24"> For I will restore health vnto thee, and I will heale thee of thy woundes, saith the Lord, because they called thee, The cast away, saying, This is Zion, whom no man seeketh after.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Haggai 2:7–9; Psalms 102:13; Jeremiah 30:3"> Thus saith the Lord, Beholde, I will bring againe the captiuitie of Iaakobs tentes, and haue compassion on his dwelling places: and the citie shalbe builded vpon her owne heape, and the palace shall remaine after the maner thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:22; Isaiah 51:11; Isaiah 60:22"> And out of them shall proceede thankesgiuing, and the voyce of them that are ioyous, and I will multiplie them, and they shall not bee fewe: I will also glorifie them, and they shall not be diminished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Jeremiah 2:3; Jeremiah 50:33–34"> Their children also shall be as afore time, and their congregation shall be established before me: and I will visite all that vexe them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 16:5; Ezekiel 34:23–24; Acts 5:31"> And their noble ruler shall be of themselues, and their gouernour shall proceede from the middes of them, and I will cause him to draw neere, and approche vnto me: for who is this that directeth his heart to come vnto mee, saith the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 21:3; Ezekiel 36:28; Jeremiah 31:33"> And ye shall be my people, and I will bee your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:19–20; Zechariah 9:14; Jeremiah 25:32"> Beholde, the tempest of the Lord goeth foorth with wrath: the whirlewinde that hangeth ouer, shall light vpon the head of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:20; Hosea 3:5; Micah 4:1"> The fierce wrath of the Lord shall not returne, vntill he haue done, and vntill he haue performed the intents of his heart: in the latter dayes ye shall vnderstand it.</span>
