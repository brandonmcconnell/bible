<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:14; Judges 1:21; Joshua 18:21–28"> O ye children of Beniamin, prepare to flee out of the middes of Ierusalem, and blowe the trumpet in Tekoa: set vp a standart vpon Beth-haccerem: for a plague appeareth out of the North and great destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:31; Isaiah 1:8; Isaiah 3:16–17"> I haue compared the daughter of Zion to a beautifull and daintie woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 19:43; Jeremiah 39:1–3; 2 Kings 24:10–12"> The pastors with their flockes shall come vnto her: they shall pitche their tentes rounde about by her, and euery one shall feede in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:8; Zephaniah 2:4; Joel 3:9"> Prepare warre against her: arise, and let vs goe vp toward the South: wo vnto vs: for the day declineth, and the shadowes of the euening are stretched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:13; Isaiah 32:14; 2 Chronicles 36:19"> Arise, and let vs goe vp by night, and destroy her palaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:19–20; Jeremiah 32:24"> For thus hath the Lord of hostes said, Hewe downe wood, and cast a mounte against Ierusalem: this citie must be visited: all oppression is in the middes of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:8; Ezekiel 7:23; Ezekiel 7:11"> As the fountaine casteth out her waters, so she casteth out her malice: crueltie and spoyle is continually heard in her before me with sorowe and strokes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:18; Hosea 9:12; Jeremiah 17:23"> Be thou instructed, O Ierusalem, lest my soule depart from thee, lest I make thee desolate as a land, that none inhabiteth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:9; Jeremiah 16:16; Obadiah 1:5–6"> Thus sayeth the Lord of hostes, They shall gather as a vine, the residue of Israel: turne backe thine hande as the grape gatherer into the baskets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Jeremiah 7:26; John 7:7"> Vnto whome shall I speake, and admonish that they may heare? beholde, their eares are vncircumcised, and they cannot hearken: beholde, the worde of the Lord is vnto them as a reproche: they haue no delite in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:21; Jeremiah 20:9; Micah 3:8"> Therefore I am full of the wrath of the Lord: I am weary with holding it: I will powre it out vpon the children in the streete, and likewise vpon the assembly of the yong men: for the husband shall euen be taken with the wife, and the aged with him that is full of daies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:10; Isaiah 5:25; Deuteronomy 28:39–43"> And their houses with their landes, and wiues also shalbe turned vnto strangers: for I will stretch out mine hande vpon the inhabitants of the land, sayeth the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:11; Jeremiah 8:10; Micah 3:11"> For from the least of them, euen vnto the greatest of them, euery one is giuen vnto couetousnesse, and from the Prophet euen vnto the Priest, they all deale falsely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:10; Jeremiah 23:17; Jeremiah 4:10"> They haue healed also ye hurt of the daughter of my people with sweete woordes, saying, Peace, peace, when there is no peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:12; Matthew 15:14; Jeremiah 3:3"> Were they ashamed when they had committed abomination? nay, they were not ashamed, no neither coulde they haue any shame: therefore they shall fall among the slaine: when I shall visite them, they shall be cast downe, sayth the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 30:21; Deuteronomy 32:7; Jeremiah 18:15"> Thus sayeth the Lord, Stande in the waies and beholde, and aske for the olde way, which is the good way and walke therein, and yee shall finde rest for your soules: but they saide, We will not walke therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:4; Isaiah 58:1; Zechariah 7:11"> Also I set watchmen ouer you, which said, Take heede to the sound of the trumpet: but they said, We will not take heede.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:10; Jeremiah 31:10; Micah 6:5"> Heare therefore, yee Gentiles, and thou Congregation knowe, what is among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Jeremiah 8:9; Jeremiah 17:10"> Heare, O earth, beholde, I will cause a plague to come vpon this people, euen the fruite of their owne imaginations: because they haue not taken heede vnto my woordes, nor to my Lawe, but cast it off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11; Amos 5:21–22; Isaiah 60:6"> To what purpose bringest thou mee incense from Sheba, and sweete calamus from a farre countrey? Your burnt offerings are not pleasant, nor your sacrifices sweete vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; Ezekiel 3:20; Jeremiah 13:16"> Therefore thus sayeth the Lord, Beholde, I will laie stumbling blockes before this people, and the fathers and the sonnes together shall fall vpon them: the neighbour and his friende shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:41–43; Jeremiah 10:22; Jeremiah 6:1"> Thus sayeth the Lord, Beholde, a people commeth from the North countrey, and a great nation shall arise from the sides of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 13:18; Jeremiah 50:42; Luke 21:25–26"> With bowe and shield shall they be weaponed: they are cruell and will haue no compassion: their voyce roareth like the sea, and they ride vpon horses, well appointed, like men of warre against thee, O daughter Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 50:43; Jeremiah 49:24"> We haue heard their fame, and our handes waxe feeble sorrowe is come vpon vs, as the sorrowe of a woman in trauaile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:29; Jeremiah 20:10; Psalms 31:13"> Goe not foorth into the fielde, nor walke by the way: for the sword of the enemie and feare is on euery side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:8; Zechariah 12:10; Isaiah 32:11"> O daughter of my people, girde thee with sackecloth, and wallowe thy selfe in the ashes: make lamentation, and bitter mourning as for thine onely sonne: for the destroier shall suddenly come vpon vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18; Jeremiah 15:20; Ezekiel 3:8–10"> I haue set thee for a defence and fortresse among my people, that thou maiest knowe and trie their waies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:4; Jeremiah 5:23; Jeremiah 18:18"> They are all rebellious traitours, walking craftily: they are brasse, and yron, they all are destroyers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:13; Jeremiah 9:7; 1 Peter 4:12"> The bellowes are burnt: the lead is consumed in the fire: the founder melteth in vaine: for the wicked are not taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hosea 9:17; Psalms 119:119; Isaiah 1:22"> They shall call them reprobate siluer, because the Lord hath reiected them.</span>
