<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 2:32; Revelation 3:12; Revelation 7:3–17"> Then I looked, and lo, a Lambe stood on mount Sion, and with him an hundreth, fourtie and foure thousand, hauing his Fathers Name written in their foreheads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:8; Ezekiel 43:2; Revelation 1:15"> And I heard a voyce from heauen, as the sound of many waters, and as the sound of a great thunder: and I heard the voyce of harpers harping with their harpes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Revelation 14:1; Revelation 15:3"> And they sung as it were a newe song before the throne, and before the foure beasts, and the Elders: and no man could learne that song, but the hundreth, fourtie and foure thousand, which were bought from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 3:4; 2 Corinthians 11:2; James 1:18"> These are they, which are not defiled with women: for they are virgins: these follow the Lambe whithersoeuer he goeth: these are bought from men, being the first fruites vnto God, and to the Lambe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:27; Psalms 32:2; Zephaniah 3:13"> And in their mouthes was found no guile: for they are without spot before the throne of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 16:15; Revelation 8:13; Ephesians 3:9–11"> Then I sawe another Angel flie in the mids of heauen, hauing an euerlasting Gospel, to preach vnto them that dwell on the earth, and to euery nation, and kinred, and tongue, and people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Revelation 15:4; Nehemiah 9:6"> Saying with a loude voyce, Feare God, and giue glory to him: for the houre of his iugdement is come: and woriship him that made heauen and earth, and the sea, and the fountaines of waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 21:9; Jeremiah 51:7–8; Revelation 18:2–3"> And there followed another Angel, saying, Babylon that great citie is fallen, it is fallen: for she made all nations to drinke of the wine of the wrath of her fornication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 14:11; Revelation 14:6–8; Revelation 13:3–6"> And the third Angel followed them, saying with a loude voyce, If any man worship the beast and his image, and receiue his marke in his forehead, or on his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 51:17; Revelation 16:19; Jeremiah 25:15–17"> The same shall drinke of the wine of ye wrath of God, yea, of the pure wine, which is powred into the cup of his wrath, and he shalbe tormented in fire and brimstone before the holy Angels, and before the Lambe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 19:3; Isaiah 34:10; Matthew 25:41"> And the smoke of their torment shall ascend euermore: and they shall haue no rest day nor night, which worship the beast and his image, and whosoeuer receiueth the print of his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 13:10; Revelation 12:17; Revelation 3:10"> Here is the patience of Saints: here are they that keepe the commandements of God, and the fayth of Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 20:6; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; 1 Thessalonians 5:10"> Then I heard a voyce from heauen, saying vnto me, Write, The dead which die in the Lord, are fully blessed. Euen so sayth the Spirit: for they rest from their labours, and their workes follow them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Isaiah 19:1; Matthew 17:5"> And I looked, and behold, a white cloude, and vpon the cloude one sitting like vnto the Sonne of man, hauing on his head a golden crowne, and in his hand a sharpe sickle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Matthew 13:39; Mark 4:29"> And another Angel came out of the Temple, crying with a loude voyce to him that sate on the cloude, Thrust in thy sickle and reape: for the time is come to reape: for the haruest of the earth is ripe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Revelation 14:19; Revelation 14:14"> And he that sate on the cloude, thrust in his sickle on the earth, and the earth was reaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:18; Revelation 14:14–15; Revelation 15:5–6"> Then an other Angel came out of the Temple, which is in heauen, hauing also a sharpe sickle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Revelation 16:8; Revelation 14:15–16"> And another Angel came out from the altar, which had power ouer fire, and cryed with a loude crie to him that had the sharpe sickle, and sayd, Thrust in thy sharpe sickle, and gather the clusters of the vineyard of the earth: for her grapes are ripe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:32–33; Revelation 19:15–21"> And the Angel thrust in his sharpe sickle on the earth, and cut downe the vines of the vineyard of the earth, and cast them into that great wine presse of the wrath of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 1:15; Revelation 19:14–21; Isaiah 63:1–3"> And the wine presse was troden without the citie, and blood came out of the wine presse, vnto the horse bridles, by the space of a thousand and sixe hundreth furlongs.</span>
