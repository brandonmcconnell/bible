<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 7:17; John 4:14; Psalms 36:8–9"> And hee shewed me a pure riuer of water of life, cleare as crystall, proceeding out of the throne of God, and of the Lambe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; 1 Peter 2:24; Revelation 2:7"> In the middes of the street of it, and of either side of ye riuer, was the tree of life, which bare twelue maner of fruits, and gaue fruit euery moneth: and the leaues of the tree serued to heale the nations with.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Revelation 21:3–4; John 12:26"> And there shalbe no more curse, but ye throne of God and of the Lambe shall be in it, and his seruants shall serue him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Revelation 14:1; Revelation 3:12"> And they shall see his face, and his Name shalbe in their foreheads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Daniel 7:18; Revelation 20:4"> And there shalbe no night there, and they neede no candle, neither light of the sunne: for the Lord God giueth them light, and they shall reigne for euermore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:1; Revelation 21:5; Revelation 19:9"> And he said vnto me, These wordes are faithfull and true: and the Lord God of the holy Prophets sent his Angell to shewe vnto his seruants the things which must shortly be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 1:3; Revelation 3:11; Revelation 22:20"> Beholde, I come shortly. Blessed is hee that keepeth the woordes of the prophecie of this booke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Revelation 1:1; Revelation 19:19"> And I am Iohn, which sawe and heard these thinges: and when I had heard and seene, I fell downe to worship before the feete of the Angell which shewed me these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Colossians 2:18–19; Revelation 14:7"> But he sayde vnto me, See thou doe it not: for I am thy fellowe seruaunt, and of thy brethren the Prophets, and of them which keepe the woordes of this booke: worship God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 8:26; Revelation 1:3; 1 Peter 4:7"> And he said vnto me, Seale not the wordes of the prophecie of this booke: for the time is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:10; Proverbs 4:18; Ezekiel 3:27"> He that is vniust, let him be vniust stil and he which is filthie, let him be filthie still: and hee that is righteous, let him be righteous stil: and he that is holy, let him be holy still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Isaiah 62:11; Isaiah 40:10"> And beholde, I come shortly, and my reward is with mee, to giue euery man according as his worke shall be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 21:6; Isaiah 44:6"> I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end, the first and the last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; 1 John 5:3; Revelation 22:7"> Blessed are they, that doe his commandements, that their right may be in the tree of life, and may enter in through the gates into the Citie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; Galatians 5:19–21; Philippians 3:2"> For without shall be dogs and inchanters, and whoremongers, and murtherers, and idolaters, and whosoeuer loueth or maketh lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; 2 Peter 1:19; Isaiah 11:1"> I Iesus haue sent mine Angell, to testifie vnto you these things in the Churches: I am the root and the generation of Dauid, and the bright morning starre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:14; Revelation 21:6; Isaiah 55:1–3"> And the Spirit and the bride say, Come. And let him that heareth, say, Come: and let him that is a thirst, come: and let whosoeuer will, take of the water of life freely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:2; Proverbs 30:6"> For I protest vnto euery man that heareth the words of the prophecie of this booke, If any man shall adde vnto these things, God shall adde vnto him the plagues, that are written in this booke:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:2; Luke 11:52; Revelation 22:2"> And if any man shall diminish of the wordes of the booke of this prophecie, God shall take away his part out of the Booke of life, and out of the holie citie, and from those things which are written in this booke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 22:7; Hebrews 9:28; Revelation 22:12"> He which testifieth these things, saith, Surely, I come quickly. Amen. Euen so, come Lord Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:18; Romans 16:20; 2 Corinthians 13:14"> The grace of our Lord Iesus Christ be with you all, AMEN.</span>
