<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 11:12; Exodus 24:12; Revelation 1:19"> After this I looked, and beholde, a doore was open in heauen, and the first voyce which I heard, was as it were of a trumpet talking with mee, saying, Come vp hither, and I will shewe thee things which must be done hereafter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 22:19; Revelation 1:10; Isaiah 6:1"> And immediatly I was rauished in the spirit, and behold, a throne was set in heauen, and one sate vpon the throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Revelation 21:11; Genesis 9:13–16"> And he that sate, was to looke vpon, like vnto a iasper stone, and a sardine, and there was a rainbowe rounde about the throne, in sight like to an emeraude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Revelation 11:16; Revelation 4:10"> And round about the throne were foure and twentie seates, and vpon the seates I sawe foure and twentie Elders sitting, clothed in white raiment, and had on their heads crownes of golde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Revelation 8:5; Revelation 1:4"> And out of the throne proceeded lightnings, and thundrings, and voyces, and there were seuen lampes of fire burning before the throne, which are the seuen spirits of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 15:2; Revelation 5:6; Revelation 14:3"> And before the throne there was a sea of glasse like vnto chrystall: and in the middes of the throne, and round about the throne were foure beasts full of eyes before and behinde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Ezekiel 1:10; Ezekiel 10:21"> And the first beast was like a lion, and the second beast like a calfe, and the thirde beast had a face as a man, and the fourth beast was like a flying Eagle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:2–13; Revelation 1:8; Hebrews 13:8"> And the foure beasts had eche one of them sixe wings about him, and they were full of eyes within, and they ceased not day nor night, saying, Holy, holy, holy Lord God almighty, Which Was, and Which Is, and Which Is to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 5:13–14; Revelation 7:11–12; Revelation 10:6"> And when those beasts gaue glorie, and honour, and thanks to him that sate on the throne, which liueth for euer and euer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 5:8; Revelation 19:4; Psalms 95:6"> The foure and twentie Elders fell downe before him that sate on the throne and worshipped him that liueth for euermore, and cast their crownes before the throne, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Colossians 1:16–17; Revelation 5:12; Isaiah 40:28"> Thou art worthy, O Lord, to receiue glory and honour, and power: for thou hast created all things, and for thy wils sake they are, and haue beene created.</span>
