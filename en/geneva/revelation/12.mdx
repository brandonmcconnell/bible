<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 21:25; Ephesians 5:32; Luke 21:11"> And there appeared a great wonder in heauen: A woman clothed with the sunne, and the moone was vnder her feete, and vpon her head a crowne of twelue starres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 16:21; Galatians 4:19; Revelation 12:4"> And she was with childe, and cried traueiling in birth, and was pained readie to be deliuered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 17:12; Revelation 12:9; Daniel 7:24"> And there appeared another wonder in heaue: for beholde, a great red dragon hauing seuen heads, and ten hornes, and seuen crownes vpon his heads:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 8:9–12; Revelation 12:9; John 8:44"> And his taile drew the thirde part of the starres of heauen, and cast them to the earth. And the dragon stood before the woman, which was ready to be deliuered, to deuoure her childe, when shee had brought it foorth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 19:15; Revelation 2:26–27; Revelation 12:2"> So shee brought foorth a man childe, which should rule all nations with a rod of yron: and that her childe was taken vp vnto God and to his throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 13:5; Revelation 11:2–3; Revelation 12:14"> And the woman fled into wildernes where she hath a place prepared of God, that they should feede her there a thousande, two hundreth and three score dayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jude 1:9; Daniel 12:1; Ephesians 6:12"> And there was a battell in heauen, Michael and his Angels fought against the dragon, and the dragon fought and his angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 37:10; Revelation 12:11; Jude 1:6"> But they preuailed not, neither was their place found any more in heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 10:18; John 12:31; Revelation 20:10"> And the great dragon, that olde serpent, called the deuil and Satan, was cast out, which deceiueth all the world: he was euen cast into the earth, and his angels were cast out with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 22:31; Zechariah 3:1–2; Revelation 11:15"> Then I heard a loude voyce in heauen, saying, Now is saluation, and strength, and the kingdome of our God, and the power of his Christ: for the accuser of our brethren is cast downe, which accused them before our God day and night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:3–5; John 16:33; 1 John 5:5"> But they ouercame him by that blood of that Lambe, and by that worde of their testimonie, and they loued not their liues vnto the death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Revelation 8:13; Psalms 96:11–13"> Therefore reioyce, ye heauens, and ye that dwell in them. Wo to the inhabitants of the earth, and of the sea: for the deuill is come downe vnto you, which hath great wrath, knowing that he hath but a short time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 3:15; Revelation 12:3–5; John 16:33"> And when the dragon sawe that hee was cast vnto the earth, he persecuted the woman which had brought forth the man childe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Isaiah 40:31; Revelation 12:6"> But to the woman were giuen two wings of a great eagle, that she might flie into the wildernes, into her place, where she is nourished for a time, and times, and halfe a time, from the presence of the serpent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7; Isaiah 59:19; Psalms 18:4"> And the serpent cast out of his mouth water after the woman, like a flood, that he might cause her to be caried away of the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 17:6; Exodus 12:35–36; 2 Kings 8:9"> But the earth holpe the woman, and the earth opened her mouth, and swalowed vp the flood, which the dragon had cast out of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:12; Genesis 3:15; Revelation 11:7"> Then the dragon was wroth with the woman, and went and made warre with the remnant of her seede, which keepe the comaundements of God, and haue the testimonie of Iesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And I stood on the sea sand.</span>
