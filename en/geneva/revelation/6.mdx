<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 6:5; Revelation 5:1; Revelation 6:7"> After, I beheld whe the Lambe had opened one of the seales, and I heard one of the foure beastes say, as it were the noise of thunder, Come and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 6:3–8; Zechariah 1:8; Psalms 45:3–5"> Therefore I behelde, and loe, there was a white horse, and hee that sate on him, had a bowe, and a crowne was giuen vnto him, and he went forth conquering that he might ouercome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 4:7; Revelation 6:1"> And when he had opened the seconde seale, I heard the second beast say, Come and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 6:2; Matthew 10:34; Revelation 13:10"> And there went out an other horse, that was red, and power was giuen to him that sate thereon, to take peace from the earth, and that they should kill one another, and there was giuen vnto him a great sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 6:2; Ezekiel 4:16; Zechariah 6:6"> And when hee had opened the thirde seale, I heard the thirde beast say, Come and see: Then I behelde, and loe, a blacke horse, and he that sate on him, had balances in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 9:4; Revelation 4:6; Revelation 7:3"> And I heard a voice in the mids of the foure beastes say, A measure of wheate for a penie, and three measures of barly for a peny, and oyle, and wine hurt thou not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 4:7; Revelation 6:3; Revelation 6:1"> And when he had opened the fourth seale, I heard the voice of the fourth beast say, Come and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; Jeremiah 15:2–3; Revelation 1:18"> And I looked, and beholde, a pale horse, and his name that sate on him was Death, and Hell folowed after him, and power was giuen vnto them ouer the fourth part of the earth, to kill with sworde, and with hunger, and with death, and with beasts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 20:4; John 16:2; Revelation 1:9"> And when hee had opened the fifth seale, I sawe vnder the altar the soules of them, that were killed for the worde of God, and for the testimonie which they mainteined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 19:2; Psalms 9:12; Revelation 16:5–7"> And they cried with a loud voice, saying, How long, Lord, which art holie and true! doest not thou iudge and auenge our blood on them that dwell on the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Revelation 7:9; Revelation 3:4–5"> And long white robes were giuen vnto euery one, and it was saide vnto them, that they shoulde rest for a litle season vntill their fellow seruants, and their brethren that shoulde bee killed euen as they were, were fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Joel 2:10; Acts 2:19–20"> And I behelde when hee had opened the sixt seale, and loe, there was a great earthquake, and the sunne was as blacke as sackecloth of heare, and the moone was like blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4; Matthew 24:29; Revelation 9:1"> And the starres of heauen fell vnto the earth, as a figge tree casteth her greene figges when it is shaken of a mightie winde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4; Revelation 16:20; 2 Peter 3:10"> And heauen departed away, as a scroule, when it is rolled, and euery mountaine and yle were mooued out of their places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19; Isaiah 2:21; Isaiah 24:21–22"> And the Kinges of the earth, and the great men, and the rich men, and the chiefe captaines, and the mighty men, and euery bondman, and euery free man, hid themselues in dennes, and among the rockes of the mountaines,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 23:30; Hosea 10:8; Revelation 4:2"> And said to the mountaines and rocks, Fal on vs, and hide vs from the presence of him that sitteth on the throne, and from the wrath of the Lambe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 3:2; Joel 2:11; Psalms 76:7"> For the great day of his wrath is come, and who can stand?</span>
