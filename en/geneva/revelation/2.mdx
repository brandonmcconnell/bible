<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 1:20; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 1:11–13"> VNto the Angel of the Church of Ephesus write, These things saieth he that holdeth the seuen starres in his right hand, and walketh in the middes of the seuen golden candlestickes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:1; 2 Peter 2:1–3; 1 Thessalonians 5:21"> I knowe thy workes, and thy labour, and thy patience, and howe thou canst not beare with them which are euill, and hast examined them which say they are Apostles, and are not, and hast found the liars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Galatians 6:9; James 5:7–11"> And thou wast burdened, and hast patience, and for my Names sake hast laboured, and hast not fainted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 24:12–13; Jeremiah 2:2–5; Hebrews 6:10–11"> Neuertheles, I haue somewhat against thee, because thou hast left thy first loue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:2–3; Revelation 3:19; Revelation 2:16"> Remember therefore from whence thou art fallen, and repent, and doe the first workes: or els I will come against thee shortly, and will remooue thy candlesticke out of his place, except thou amend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 101:3; 2 John 1:9–10; Psalms 26:5"> But this thou hast, that thou hatest the workes of the Nicolaitanes, which I also hate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 2:17; Revelation 2:11; Matthew 11:15"> Let him that hath an eare, heare, what the Spirite saith vnto the Churches, To him that ouercommeth, will I giue to eate of the tree of life which is in the middes of the Paradise of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; Revelation 1:8; Revelation 1:11"> And vnto the Angel of the Church of the Smyrnians write, These things saith he that is first, and last, which was dead and is aliue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; 2 Corinthians 8:9; James 2:5–6"> I knowe thy workes and tribulation, and pouertie (but thou art riche) and I knowe the blasphemie of them, which say they are Iewes, and are not, but are the Synagogue of Satan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; Matthew 10:22; Revelation 3:10–11"> Feare none of those things, which thou shalt suffer: beholde, it shall come to passe, that the deuill shall cast some of you into prison, that ye may be tryed, and ye shall haue tribulation tenne dayes: be thou faithfull vnto the death, and I will giue thee the crowne of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 20:6; Revelation 20:14; Revelation 21:8"> Let him that hath an eare, heare what the Spirit saith to the Churches. He that ouercommeth, shall not be hurt of the second death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 2:16; Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 1:16"> And to the Angel of the Church, which is at Pergamus write, This saith he which hath that sharpe sworde with two edges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 2:9–10; 1 Timothy 5:8; Revelation 3:8–9"> I knowe thy workes and where thou dwellest, euen where Satans throne is, and thou keepest my Name, and hast not denied my faith, euen in those dayes when Antipas my faithfull martyr was slaine among you, where Satan dwelleth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 31:16; 2 Peter 2:15; Revelation 2:20"> But I haue a fewe things against thee, because thou hast there them that maintaine the doctrine of Balaam, which taught Balac to put a stumbling blocke before ye children of Israel, that they should eate of things sacrificed vnto Idoles, and commit fornication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 2:6"> Euen so hast thou them, that maintaine the doctrine of the Nicolaitanes, which thing I hate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:8; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 2:5"> Repent thy selfe, or els I will come vnto thee shortly, and will fight against them with the sworde of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 62:2; Isaiah 65:15; Revelation 19:12–13"> Let him that hath an eare, heare what the Spirite saith vnto the Churches. To him that ouercommeth, will I giue to eate of the Manna that is hid, and will giue him a white stone, and in the stone a newe name written, which no man knoweth sauing he that receiueth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 1:14–15; Revelation 1:11; John 3:35–36"> And vnto ye Angel of the Church which is at Thyatira write, These things saith the Sonne of God, which hath his eyes like vnto a flame of fire, and his feete like fine brasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; 2 Peter 1:7; John 15:2"> I knowe thy workes and thy loue, and seruice, and faith, and thy patience, and thy workes, and that they are more at the last, then at the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 2:14; 1 Kings 16:31; 1 Corinthians 10:18–21"> Notwithstanding, I haue a few things against thee, that thou sufferest the woman Iezabel, which calleth her selfe a prophetesse, to teache and to deceiue my seruants to make them commit fornication, and to eate meates sacrificed vnto idoles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Romans 2:4–5; Revelation 9:20–21"> And I gaue her space to repent of her fornication, and she repented not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 17:2; Luke 13:5; Ezekiel 16:37–41"> Beholde, I will cast her into a bed, and them that commit fornication with her, into great affliction, except they repent them of their workes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Romans 8:27; Psalms 7:9"> And I will kill her children with death: and all the Churches shall know that I am he which searche the reines and heartes: and I will giue vnto euery one of you according vnto your workes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:11; Revelation 12:9; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> And vnto you I say, the rest of them of Thyatira, As many as haue not this learning, neither haue knowen the deepenes of Satan (as they speake) I will put vpon you none other burden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 3:11; Revelation 3:3; Hebrews 10:23"> But that which ye haue alreadie, hold fast till I come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 3:21; Daniel 7:27; Revelation 2:7"> For he that ouercommeth and keepeth my workes vnto the end, to him will I giue power ouer nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 2:8–9; Revelation 12:5; Revelation 19:15"> And he shall rule them with a rodde of yron: and as the vessels of a potter, shall they be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; 2 Peter 1:19; Luke 1:78–79"> Euen as I receiued of my Father, so will I giue him the morning starre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 2:7"> Let him that hath an eare, heare what the Spirite saith to the Churches.</span>
