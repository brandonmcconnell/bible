<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 26:21; Revelation 15:6–7; Revelation 21:9"> And I sawe another signe in heauen, great and marueilous, seuen Angels, hauing the seuen last plagues: for by them is fulfilled the wrath of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 4:6; Revelation 12:11; Revelation 19:1–7"> And I sawe as it were a glassie sea, mingled with fire, and them that had gotten victorie of the beast, and of his image, and of his marke, and of the number of his name, stand at the glassie sea, hauing the harpes of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Exodus 15:1–18; Psalms 139:14"> And they sung the song of Moses the seruant of God, and the song of the Lambe, saying, Great and marueilous are thy workes, Lord God almightie: iust and true are thy wayes, King of Saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Malachi 1:11; Jeremiah 10:7"> Who shall not feare thee, O Lord, and glorifie thy Name! for thou onely art holy, and all nations shall come and worship before thee: for thy iudgements are made manifest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Numbers 1:50; Matthew 27:51"> And after that, I looked, and beholde, the Temple of the tabernacle of testimonie was open in heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Revelation 15:1; Ezekiel 44:17–18"> And the seuen Angels came out of the Temple, which had the seuen plagues, clothed in pure and bright linnen, and hauing their breasts girded with golden girdles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 10:6; Revelation 5:8; Revelation 16:2–1"> And one of the foure beastes gaue vnto the seuen Angels seuen golden vials full of the wrath of God, which liueth for euermore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:4; 1 Kings 8:10–11; Exodus 40:34–35"> And the Temple was full of the smoke of the glory of God and of his power, and no man was able to enter into the Temple, till the seuen plagues of the seuen Angels were fulfilled.</span>
