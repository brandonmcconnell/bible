<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Psalms 5:6; Proverbs 28:17"> If one be founde slaine in the lande, which the Lord thy God giueth thee to possesse it, lying in the field, and it is not knowe who hath slaine him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 13:3–4; Deuteronomy 16:18–19"> Then thine Elders and thy Iudges shall come forth, and measure vnto the cities that are round about him that is slayne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 19:2; Matthew 11:28–30; Jeremiah 31:18"> Aud let ye Elders of that citie, which is next vnto the slaine man, take out of the droue an heifer that hath not bene put to labour, nor hath drawen in the yoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:21–24; 1 Peter 3:18"> And let the Elders of that citie bring the heifer vnto a stonie valley, which is neyther eared nor sowen, and strike off the heifers necke there in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:13; Malachi 2:7; Deuteronomy 10:8"> Also the Priests the sonnes of Leui (whom the Lord thy God hath chosen to minister, and to blesse in the name of the Lord) shall come forth, and by their word shall all strife and plague be tried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 73:13; Psalms 26:6; Psalms 51:7"> And all the Elders of that citie that came neere to the slayne man, shall wash their hands ouer the heifer that is beheaded in the valley:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 21:21–23; Job 21:31–34; 2 Samuel 16:8"> And shall testifie, and say, Our handes haue not shed this blood, neither haue our eies seene it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 1:14; 2 Kings 24:4; Ezekiel 23:24–25"> O Lord, be mercifull vnto thy people Israel, whom thou hast redeemed, and lay no innocent blood to the charge of thy people Israel, and the blood shalbe forgiuen them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:12–13; 2 Kings 10:30–31; Deuteronomy 13:18"> So shalt thou take away the cry of innocet blood from thee, when thou shalt do that which is right in the sight of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:10–16; Joshua 21:44"> Whe thou shalt go to warre against thine enemies, and the Lord thy God shall deliuer them into thine hands, and thou shalt take the captiues,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 31:10; Genesis 34:3; Genesis 12:14–15"> And shalt see among the captiues a beautifull woman, and hast a desire vnto her, and wouldest take her to thy wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 14:9; Numbers 6:9; Ephesians 4:22"> Then thou shalt bring her home to thine house, and she shall shaue her head, and pare her nayles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 45:10–11; Luke 14:26–27"> And she shall put off the garment that shee was taken in, and she shall remaine in thine house, and bewaile her father and her mother a moneth long: and after that shalt thou go in vnto her, and marry her, and she shalbe thy wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:29; Genesis 34:2; Deuteronomy 22:19"> And if thou haue no fauour vnto her, then thou mayest let her go whither she will, but thou shalt not sell her for money, nor make marchandise of her, because thou hast humbled her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 29:33; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 29:18"> If a man haue two wiues, one loued and another hated, and they haue borne him children, both the loued and also the hated: if the first borne be the sonne of the hated,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:10; 1 Chronicles 5:2; 2 Chronicles 21:3"> Then when the time commeth, that hee appointeth his sonnes to be heires of that which he hath, he may not make the sonne of the beloued first borne before the sonne of the hated, which is the first borne:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 49:3; Genesis 25:31–34; Psalms 105:36"> But he shall acknowledge the sonne of the hated for the first borne, and giue him double portion of all that he hath: for hee is the first of his strength, and to him belongeth the right of the first borne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 20:12; Amos 4:11–12"> If any man haue a sonne that is stubburne and disobedient, which wil not hearken vnto the voice of his father, nor the voyce of his mother, and they haue chastened him, and he would not obey them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 13:3; Deuteronomy 21:2; Deuteronomy 16:18"> Then shall his father and his mother take him, and bring him out vnto the Elders of his citie, and vnto the gate of the place where he dwelleth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29–35; Proverbs 20:1; Proverbs 23:19–21"> And shall say vnto the Elders of his citie, This our sonne is stubburne and disobedient, and he wil not obey our admonition: he is a riotour, and a drunkard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:19–20; Deuteronomy 13:10–11; Leviticus 24:16"> Then all the men of his citie shall stone him with stones vnto death: so thou shalt take away euill from among you, that all Israel may heare it, and feare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 19:31–38; Acts 26:31; Luke 23:33"> If a man also haue committed a trespasse worthy of death, and is put to death, and thou hangest him on a tree,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 3:13; John 19:31; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> His body shall not remaine all night vpon the tree, but thou shalt bury him the same day: for the curse of God is on him that is hanged. Defile not therfore thy land which the Lord thy God giueth thee to inherite.</span>
