<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:19; Psalms 9:5; Psalms 109:13"> And the first borne which she beareth, shall succeede in the name of his brother which is dead, that his name be not put out of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ruth 4:5–6; Ruth 4:1–2"> And if the man will not take his kinsewoman, then let his kinsewoman goe vp to the gate vnto the Elders, and say, My kinsman refuseth to rayse vp vnto his brother a name in Israel: hee will not doe the office of a kinsman vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 4:6"> Then the Elders of his citie shall call him, and commune with him: if he stand and say, I wil not take her,</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And his name shall be called in Israel, The house of him whose shooe is put off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9; Romans 3:8"> When men striue together, one with another, if the wife of the one come neere, for to ridde her husband out of the handes of him that smiteth him, and put foorth her hand, and take him by his priuities,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:13; Deuteronomy 19:21; Deuteronomy 7:2"> Then thou shalt cut off her hande: thine eye shall not spare her.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Revelation 21:27; Deuteronomy 22:5"> For all that doe such things, and all that doe vnrighteously, are abomination vnto the Lord thy God.</span>
