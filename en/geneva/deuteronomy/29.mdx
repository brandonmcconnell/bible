<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:2–3; Hebrews 8:9; Exodus 24:2–8"> These are the wordes of the couenant which the Lord commanded Moses to make with the children of Israel in the lande of Moab beside the couenant which hee had made with them in Horeb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Exodus 8:12; Joshua 24:5–6"> And Moses called all Israel, and said vnto them, Ye haue seene all that the Lord did before your eyes in the lande of Egypt vnto Pharaoh and vnto all his seruantes, and vnto all his lande,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:32–35; Nehemiah 9:9–11; Deuteronomy 7:18–19"> The great tentations which thine eyes haue seene, those great miracles and wonders:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 4:18; Isaiah 6:9–10; Proverbs 20:12"> Yet the Lord hath not giuen you an heart to perceiue, and eyes to see, and eares to heare, vnto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:4; Nehemiah 9:21; Deuteronomy 8:2"> And I haue led you fourty yere in the wildernesse: your clothes are not waxed olde vpon you, neyther is thy shooe waxed olde vpon thy foote.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Corinthians 9:25"> Ye haue eaten no bread, neither drunke wine, nor strong drinke, that ye might know how that I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:24–17; Numbers 32:33–42; Psalms 135:10–12"> After, ye came vnto this place, and Sihon King of Heshbon, and Og King of Bashan came out against vs vnto battell, and we slewe them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:12–13; Numbers 32:33"> And tooke their lande, and gaue it for an inheritance vnto the Reubenites, and to the Gadites, and to the halfe tribe of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 2:3; Deuteronomy 4:6; Luke 11:28"> Keepe therefore the wordes of this couenant and doe them, that ye may prosper in all that ye shall doe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:16–17; Revelation 20:12; Deuteronomy 31:12–13"> Ye stand this day euery one of you before the Lord your God: your heads of your tribes, your Elders and your officers, eue al ye me of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 12:38; Joshua 9:21–27; Exodus 12:48–49"> Your children, your wiues, and thy stranger that is in thy campe from the hewer of thy wood, vnto the drawer of thy water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:12–15; 2 Kings 11:17; Deuteronomy 5:2–3"> That thou shouldest passe into the couenant of the Lord thy God, and into his othe which the Lord thy God maketh with thee this day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Genesis 17:7; Deuteronomy 28:9"> For to establish thee this day a people vnto him selfe, and that he may be vnto thee a God, as he hath said vnto thee, and as he hath sworne vnto thy fathers, Abraham, Izhak, and Iaakob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Hebrews 8:7–12"> Neither make I this couenant, and this othe with you onely,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:39; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Jeremiah 50:5"> But aswel with him that standeth here with vs this day before the Lord our God, as with him that is not here with vs this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Deuteronomy 2:9; Deuteronomy 2:4"> For ye knowe, how we haue dwelt in the land of Egypt, and how we passed thorowe the middes of the nations, which ye passed by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:36"> And ye haue seene their abominations and their idoles (wood, and stone, siluer and golde) which were among them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:15; Deuteronomy 11:16–17; Deuteronomy 13:1–15"> That there should not be among you man nor woman, nor familie, nor tribe, which should turne his heart away this day from the Lord our God, to goe and serue the gods of these nations, and that there shoulde not be among you any roote that bringeth forth gall and wormewood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Numbers 15:30; Numbers 15:39"> So that when he heareth the words of this curse, he blesse him selfe in his heart, saying, I shall haue peace, although I walke according to the stubburnes of mine owne heart, thus adding drunkennesse to thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; Psalms 79:5; Deuteronomy 9:14"> The Lord will not be mercifull vnto him, but then the wrath of the Lord and his ielousie shall smoke against that man, and euery curse that is written in this booke, shall light vpon him, and the Lord shall put out his name from vnder heauen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Joshua 7:1–26; Ezekiel 13:9"> And the Lord shall separate him vnto euil out of all the tribes of Israel, according vnto all the curses of the couenant, that is written in the booke of this Lawe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:8"> So that the generatio to come, euen your children, that shall rise vp after you, and the stranger, that shall come from a farre lande, shall say, when they shall see the plagues of this lande, and the diseases thereof, wherewith the Lord shall smite it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:9; Jeremiah 17:6; Isaiah 34:9"> (For all that land shall burne with brimstone and salt: it shall not be sowen, nor bring forth, nor any grasse shall growe therein, like as in the ouerthrowing of Sodom, and Gomorah, Admah, and Zeboim, which the Lord ouerthrewe in his wrath and in his anger)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; 1 Kings 9:8–9; Romans 2:5"> Then shall all nations say, Wherefore hath the Lord done thus vnto this lande? how fierce is this great wrath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:9; Isaiah 47:6; Jeremiah 50:7"> And they shall answere, Because they haue forsaken the couenant of the Lord God of their fathers, which he had made with them, when he brought them out of the land of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Judges 2:12–13; Judges 5:8"> And went and serued other gods and worshipped them: euen gods which they knewe not, and which had giuen them nothing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Daniel 9:11–14; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 27:15–26"> Therefore the wrath of the Lord waxed hot against this land, to bring vpon it euery curse that is written in this booke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:20; 1 Kings 14:15; Psalms 52:5"> And ye Lord hath rooted them out of their land in anger, and in wrath, and in great indignation, and hath cast them into another land, as appeareth this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; Acts 1:7; Romans 11:33–34"> The secret things belong to the Lord our God, but the things reueiled belong vnto vs, and to our children for euer, that we may doe all the wordes of this Lawe.</span>
