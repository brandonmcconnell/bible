<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Deuteronomy 4:26; Jeremiah 22:29"> Hearken, ye heauens, and I will speake: and let the earth heare the words of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 55:10–11; Psalms 72:6; Micah 5:7"> My doctrine shall drop as the raine, and my speach shall stil as the dew, as the showre vpon the herbes, and as the great raine vpon the grasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:6; Psalms 150:2; Ephesians 1:19"> For I will publish the name of the Lord: giue ye glorie vnto our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 18:30–31; Psalms 92:15; Isaiah 26:4"> Perfect is the worke of the mighty God: for all his wayes are iudgement. God is true, and without wickednesse: iust, and righteous is he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; Philippians 2:15; Matthew 17:17"> They haue corrupted them selues towarde him by their vice, not being his children, but a frowarde and crooked generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:16; 1 John 3:1; Isaiah 44:2"> Doe ye so rewarde the Lord, O foolish people and vnwise? is not he thy father, that hath bought thee? he hath made thee, and proportioned thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 78:3–4; Job 8:8–10; Psalms 44:1"> Remember the dayes of olde: consider the yeeres of so many generations: aske thy father, and he will shewe thee: thine Elders, and they will tell thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 17:26; Genesis 11:8–9; Daniel 4:17"> When the most hie God deuided to the nations their inheritance, when he separated the sonnes of Adam, he appoynted the borders of the people, according to the nomber of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; 1 Peter 2:9–10; Exodus 19:5–6"> For the Lordes portion is his people: Iaakob is the lot of his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; Psalms 17:8; Hosea 13:5"> He found him in ye land of ye wildernes, in a waste, and roaring wildernes: he led him about, he taught him, and kept him as ye apple of his eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Isaiah 46:4; Isaiah 63:9"> As an eagle stereth vp her nest, flootereth ouer her birdes, stretcheth out her wings, taketh them, and beareth them on her wings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 78:52–53; Isaiah 46:4; Isaiah 44:7–8"> So the Lord alone led him, and there was no strange god with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 58:14; Job 29:6; Psalms 81:16"> He caryed him vp to the hie places of the earth, that he might eate the fruites of the fieldes, and he caused him to sucke hony out of the stone, and oyle out of the hard rocke:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 49:11; Psalms 81:16; Psalms 147:14"> Butter of kine, and milke of sheepe with fat of the lambes, and rammes fed in Bashan, and goates, with the fat of the graines of wheat, and the red licour of the grape hast thou drunke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:20; Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 33:5"> But he that should haue bene vpright, when he waxed fat, spurned with his heele: thou art fat, thou art grosse, thou art laden with fatnes: therefore he forsooke God that made him, and regarded not the strong God of his saluation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 78:58; 1 Corinthians 10:22; Nahum 1:1–2"> They prouoked him with strange gods: they prouoked him to anger with abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 5:8; Deuteronomy 28:64; Leviticus 17:7"> They offred vnto deuils, not to God, but to gods whome they knew not: new gods that came newly vp, whome their fathers feared not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 17:10; Jeremiah 2:32; Psalms 106:21"> Thou hast forgotten the mightie God that begate thee, and hast forgotten God that formed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 106:40; Psalms 5:4; Isaiah 1:2"> The Lord then sawe it, and was angrie, for the prouocation of his sonnes and of his daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:17–18; Luke 7:31–32; Deuteronomy 32:5"> And he said, I will hide my face from the: I will see what their ende shalbe: for they are a frowarde generation, children in who is no faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 10:19; Romans 9:25; Romans 11:11–14"> They haue moued me to ielousie with that which is not God: they haue prouoked me to anger with their vanities: and I will moue them to ielousie with those which are no people: I wil prouoke them to anger with a foolish nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Lamentations 4:11; Jeremiah 15:14; Micah 1:4"> For fire is kindled in my wrath, and shall burne vnto the bottome of hell, and shall consume the earth with her increase, and set on fire the foundations of the mountaines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:16; Psalms 7:12–13; Isaiah 24:17–18"> I will spend plagues vpon them: I will bestowe mine arrowes vpon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 26:22; Ezekiel 5:17; Isaiah 65:25"> They shalbe burnt with hunger, and consumed with heate, and with bitter destruction: I will also sende the teeth of beastes vpon them, with the venime of serpents creeping in the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:15; Lamentations 1:20; Isaiah 30:16"> The sworde shall kill them without, and in the chambers feare: both the yong man and the yong woman, the suckeling with the man of gray heare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:37"> I haue said, I would scatter them abroade: I would make their remembrance to cease from among men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 14:15–16; Exodus 32:12; Zechariah 1:14–15"> Saue that I feared the furie of the enemie, least their aduersaries should waxe proude, and least they should say, Our hie hande and not the Lord hath done all this:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Isaiah 27:11; Job 28:28"> For they are a nation voide of counsel, neither is there any vnderstanding in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 81:13; Deuteronomy 5:29; Hosea 14:9"> Oh that they were wise, then they would vnderstand this: they would consider their latter ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 26:8; Joshua 23:10; Isaiah 30:17"> How should one chase a thousand, and two put ten thousande to flight, except their strong God had sold the, and the Lord had shut them vp?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 14:25; 1 Samuel 2:2; Daniel 2:47"> For their god is not as our God, euen our enemies being iudges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:18; Ezekiel 16:45–51; Isaiah 1:10"> For their vine is of the vine of Sodom, and of the vines of Gomorah: their grapes are grapes of gall, their clusters be bitter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 58:4; Romans 3:13; Psalms 140:3"> Their wine is the poyson of dragons, and the cruel gall of aspes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hosea 13:12; Job 14:17; Jeremiah 2:22"> Is not this laide in store with me, and sealed vp among my treasures?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Hebrews 10:30; Nahum 1:2"> Vengeance and recompence are mine: their foote shall slide in due time: for the day of their destruction is at hand, and the things that shall come vpon them, make haste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 135:14; Psalms 106:45; Judges 2:18"> For the Lord shall iudge his people, and repent towarde his seruants, when hee seeth that their power is gone, and none shut vp in holde nor left abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Judges 10:14; 2 Kings 3:13"> When men shall say, Where are their gods, their mighty God in whome they trusted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:18–19; Psalms 50:13; Judges 10:14"> Which did eate the fat of their sacrifices, and did drinke the wine of their drinke offring? let them rise vp, and help you: let him be your refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:6; Hosea 6:1; Job 5:18"> Behold now, for I, I am he, and there is no gods with me: I kill, and giue life: I wound, and I make whole: neither is there any that can deliuer out of mine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 6:17–18; Genesis 14:22; Revelation 10:5–6"> For I lift vp mine hand to heauen, and say, I liue for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 66:16; Isaiah 34:5–6; Isaiah 27:1"> If I whet my glittering sworde, and mine hand take holde on iudgement, I will execute vengeance on mine enemies, and will rewarde them that hate me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Jeremiah 46:10; Ezekiel 38:21–22"> I will make mine arrowes drunke with blood, (and my sword shall eate flesh) for the blood of the slaine, and of the captiues, when I beginne to take vengeance of the enemie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Revelation 19:2; Romans 12:19; Revelation 6:10"> Ye nations, praise his people: for he will auenge the blood of his seruants, and will execute vengeance vpon his aduersaries, and will bee mercifull vnto his lande, and to his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Numbers 13:8; Numbers 13:16; Deuteronomy 31:30"> Then Moses came and spake all ye words of this song in the audience of the people, he and Hoshea the sonne of Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> When Moses had made an end of speaking all these wordes to all Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:19; Ezekiel 40:4; Proverbs 3:1–4"> Then hee said vnto them, Set your heartes vnto all the wordes which I testifie against you this day, that ye may commande them vnto your children, that they may obserue and doo all the wordes of this Lawe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Proverbs 4:22; Leviticus 18:5; 2 Peter 1:16"> For it is no vaine worde concerning you, but it is your life, and by this worde ye shall prolong your dayes in the land, whither yee go ouer Iorden to possesse it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 27:12–13"> And the Lord spake vnto Moses the selfe same day, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 27:12; Isaiah 33:17; Deuteronomy 34:1–5"> Goe vp into the mountaine of Abarim, vnto the mount Nebo, which is in the lande of Moab, that is ouer against Iericho: and beholde the lande of Canaan, which I giue vnto the children of Israel for a possession,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Numbers 33:38; Numbers 20:24–29"> And die in the mount which thou goest vp vnto, and thou shalt be gathered vnto thy people, as Aaron thy brother died in mount Hor, and was gathered vnto his people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 27:14; Numbers 20:11–14; Deuteronomy 3:23–27"> Because ye trespassed against me among the children of Israel, at the waters of Meribah, at Kadesh in the wildernesse of Zin: for ye sanctified me not among the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:1–4; Deuteronomy 3:27; Hebrews 11:39"> Thou shalt therefore see the lande before thee, but shalt not go thither, I meane, into the land which I giue the children of Israel.</span>
