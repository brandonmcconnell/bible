<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 34:1–2; Exodus 25:10–15; Deuteronomy 10:3–4"> In the same time the Lord said vnto me, Hewe thee two Tables of stone like vnto the first, and come vp vnto me into the Mount, and make thee an Arke of wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 8:9; Deuteronomy 10:5; Deuteronomy 4:13"> And I will write vpon the Tables ye wordes that were vpon the first Tables, which thou brakest, and thou shalt put them in the Arke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 34:4; Exodus 37:1–9; Exodus 25:10"> And I made an Arke of Shittim wood, and hewed two Tables of stone like vnto the first, and went vp into the Mountaine, and the two Tables in mine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:10; Exodus 34:28; Hebrews 12:18–19"> Then he wrote vpon the Tables according to the first writing (the tenne commandements, which the Lord spake vnto you in the Mount out of the middes of the fire, in the day of the assemblie) and the Lord gaue them vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 34:29; Exodus 40:20; Deuteronomy 10:2"> And I departed, and came downe from the Mount, and put the Tables in the Arke which I had made: and there they be, as the Lord commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 33:38; Numbers 20:23–28; Numbers 33:30–33"> And ye children of Israel tooke their iourney from Beeroth of the children of Iaakan to Mosera, where Aaron dyed, and was buried, and Eleazar his sonne became Priest in his steade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:32–34"> From thence they departed vnto Gudgodah, and from Gudgodah to Iotbath a land of running waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Deuteronomy 18:5; Numbers 6:23–26"> The same time ye Lord separated the tribe of Leui to beare the Arke of the couenant of the Lord, and to stand before ye Lord, to minister vnto him, and to blesse in his Name vnto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:28; Numbers 18:20–24; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> Wherefore Leui hath no part nor inheritance with his brethren: for the Lord is his inheritance, as the Lord thy God hath promised him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18–19; Deuteronomy 9:25; Exodus 34:28"> And I taried in the mount, as at ye first time, fourtie dayes and fourtie nightes, and the Lord heard me at that time also, and the Lord would not destroy thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 33:1; Exodus 32:34"> But the Lord said vnto me, Arise, goe forth in the iourney before the people, that they may goe in and possesse the land, which I sware vnto their fathers to giue vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Deuteronomy 6:5; Luke 10:27"> And nowe, Israel, what doth the Lord thy God require of thee, but to feare the Lord thy God, to walke in all his wayes, and to loue him, and to serue the Lord thy God, with all thine heart, and with all thy soule?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Deuteronomy 6:24; James 1:25"> That thou keepe the commandements of the Lord, and his ordinances, which I commaund thee this day, for thy wealth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Psalms 24:1; Psalms 115:16"> Beholde, heauen, and the heauen of heauens is the Lords thy God, and the earth, with all that therein is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:37; Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Romans 9:13–23"> Notwithstanding, the Lord set his delite in thy fathers to loue them, and did choose their seede after them, euen you aboue all people, as appeareth this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Deuteronomy 30:6; Leviticus 26:41"> Circumcise therefore the foreskin of your heart, and harden your neckes no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:34; Romans 2:11; Daniel 2:47"> For the Lord your God is God of gods, and Lord of lordes, a great God, mightie and terrible, which accepteth no persons nor taketh reward:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 146:9; Psalms 68:5; Jeremiah 49:11"> Who doeth right vnto the fatherlesse and widowe, and loueth the stranger, giuing him foode and rayment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 22:21; Leviticus 19:33–34; Luke 17:18"> Loue ye therefore the stranger: for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Deuteronomy 13:4; Luke 4:8"> Thou shalt feare the Lord thy God: thou shalt serue him, and thou shalt cleaue vnto him, and shalt sweare by his Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:14; Psalms 106:21–22; Exodus 15:2"> He is thy praise, and hee is thy God, that hath done for thee these great and terrible things, which thine eyes haue seene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 46:27; Exodus 1:5; Genesis 15:5"> Thy fathers went downe into Egypt with seuentie persons, and now the Lord thy God hath made thee, as ye starres of ye heauen in multitude.</span>
