<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:3; Acts 13:19; Deuteronomy 4:38"> When the Lord thy God shall bring thee into the land whither thou goest to possesse it, and shall roote out many nations before thee: the Hittites, and the Girgashites, and the Amorites, and the Canaanites, and the Perizzites, and the Hiuites, and the Iebusites, seuen nations greater and mightier then thou,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 2:2; Exodus 23:32–33; Deuteronomy 20:16–17"> And the Lord thy God shall giue them before thee, then thou shalt smite them: thou shalt vtterly destroy them: thou shalt make no couenant with them, nor haue compassion on them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 11:2; Joshua 23:12–13; Exodus 34:15–16"> Neither shalt thou make marriages with them, neither giue thy daughter vnto his sonne, nor take his daughter vnto thy sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:15; Judges 2:11; Deuteronomy 4:26"> For they wil cause thy sonne to turne away from me, and to serue other gods: then will the wrath of the Lord waxe hote against you and destroy thee suddenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 23:24; Deuteronomy 12:2–3; Exodus 34:13"> But thus ye shall deale with them, Ye shall ouerthrowe their altars, and breake downe their pillars, and ye shall cut downe their groues, and burne their grauen images with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:2; Exodus 19:5–6; 1 Peter 2:9"> For thou art an holy people vnto the Lord thy God, the Lord thy God hath chosen thee, to be a precious people vnto himselfe, aboue all people that are vpon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Romans 9:11–15; Romans 11:6"> The Lord did not set his loue vpon you, nor chose you, because ye were more in number then any people: for ye were the fewest of all people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Exodus 32:13; Ephesians 2:4–5"> But because the Lord loued you, and because hee would keepe the othe which hee had sworne vnto your fathers, the Lord hath brought you out by a mightie hand, and deliuered you out of the house of bondage from the hand of Pharaoh King of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:23; 2 Timothy 2:13; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> That thou mayest knowe, that the Lord thy God, he is God, the faithfull God which keepeth couenant and mercie vnto them that loue him and keepe his commandements, euen to a thousand generations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 59:18; Nahum 1:2; Romans 12:19"> And rewardeth them to their face that hate him, to bring them to destruction: he wil not deferre to reward him that hateth him, to his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 14:15; Deuteronomy 4:1; Deuteronomy 5:32"> Keepe thou therefore the commandements, and the ordinances, and the lawes, which I commaund thee this day to doe them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:1–14; Deuteronomy 7:9; Leviticus 26:3–13"> For if ye hearken vnto these lawes, and obserue and doe them, then the Lord thy God shall keepe with thee the couenant, and the mercie which he sware vnto thy fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 14:21; John 15:10; Deuteronomy 28:3–5"> And he wil loue thee, and blesse thee, and multiplie thee: he will also blesse the fruite of thy wombe, and the fruite of thy land, thy corne and thy wine, and thine oyle and the increase of thy kine, and the flockes of thy sheepe in the land, which he sware vnto thy fathers to giue thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 23:26–33; Deuteronomy 28:4; Psalms 127:3"> Thou shalt be blessed aboue all people: there shall be neither male nor female barren among you, nor among your cattell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Psalms 105:36–37; Leviticus 26:3–4"> Moreouer, the Lord will take away from thee all infirmities, and will put none of the euill diseases of Egypt (which thou knowest) vpon thee, but wil send them vpon all that hate thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 23:33; Judges 8:27; Deuteronomy 13:8"> Thou shalt therefore consume all people which the Lord thy God shall giue thee: thine eye shall not spare them, neither shalt thou serue their gods, for that shalbe thy destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 13:32; Numbers 33:53; Deuteronomy 18:21"> If thou say in thine heart, These nations are moe then I, how can I cast them out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 105:5; Deuteronomy 31:6; Deuteronomy 1:29"> Thou shalt not feare them, but remember what the Lord thy God did vnto Pharaoh, and vnto all Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Nehemiah 9:10–11; Jeremiah 32:20–21"> The great tentations which thine eyes sawe, and the signes and wonders, and the mighty hand and stretched out arme, whereby the Lord thy God brought thee out: so shall the Lord thy God do vnto all ye people, whose face thou fearest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 24:12; Exodus 23:28–30"> Moreouer, the Lord thy God will send hornets among them vntil they that are left, and hide themselues from thee, be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:5; Deuteronomy 10:17; Nehemiah 9:32"> Thou shalt not feare them: for the Lord thy God is among you, a God mightie and dreadful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 23:29–30; Joshua 15:63"> And the Lord thy God wil roote out these nations before thee by little and little: thou mayest not consume them at once, least the beasts of the fielde increase vpon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:20; Deuteronomy 2:15; Jeremiah 17:18"> But the Lord thy God shall giue them before thee, and shall destroy them with a mightie destruction, vntill they be brought to naught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 10:8; Joshua 23:9; Joshua 1:5"> And he shall deliuer their Kings into thine hand, and thou shalt destroy their name from vnder heauen: there shall no man be able to stand before thee, vntill thou hast destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 32:20; 1 Chronicles 14:12; Deuteronomy 17:1"> The grauen images of their gods shall ye burne with fire, and couet not the siluer and golde, that is on them, nor take it vnto thee, least thou be snared therewith: for it is an abomination before the Lord thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:17; Hosea 14:8; Ezekiel 11:18"> Bring not therefore abomination into thine house, lest, thou be accursed like it, but vtterly abhorre it, and count it most abominable: for it is accursed.</span>
