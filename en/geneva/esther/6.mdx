<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 6:18; Daniel 2:1; Esther 2:23"> The same night the King slept not, and he comanded to bring ye booke of the records, and the chronicles: and they were read before ye King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esther 2:21–22"> Then it was found written that Mordecai had tolde of Bigtana, and Teresh two of the Kings eunuches, keepers of the dore, who sought to lay hands on the King Ahashuerosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:15; Genesis 40:23; Acts 28:8–10"> Then the King sayde, What honour and dignitie hath bene giuen to Mordecai for this? And the Kings seruants that ministred vnto him, sayd, There is nothing done for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 5:14; Esther 4:11; Esther 5:1"> And the King sayde, Who is in the court? (Now Haman was come into the inner court of the Kings house, that he might speake vnto the King to hang Mordecai on the tree that he had prepared for him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the Kings seruants said vnto him, Behold, Haman standeth in the court. And the King sayd, Let him come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Obadiah 1:3; Isaiah 62:4–5"> And when Haman came in, the King saide vnto him, What shalbe done vnto ye man, whom the King will honour? Then Haman thought in his heart, To whom would the King do honour more then to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 6:11; Esther 6:9"> And Haman answered the King, The man whome the King would honour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 1:33; Luke 15:22; Esther 1:11"> Let them bring for him royall apparell, which the King vseth to weare, and the horse that the King rideth vpon, and that the crowne royall may be set vpon his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 41:43; 1 Kings 1:33–34; Zechariah 9:9"> And let the raiment and the horse be deliuered by the hand of one of the Kings most noble princes, and let them apparel the man (whome the King will honour) and cause him to ride vpon the horse thorow the streete of the citie, and proclayme before him, Thus shall it be done vnto the man, whome the King will honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:7; Daniel 4:37; 2 Kings 10:10"> Then the King said to Haman, Make haste, take the rayment and the horse as thou hast said, and doe so vnto Mordecai the Iewe, that sitteth at the Kings gate: let nothing fayle of all that thou hast spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 1:52; Esther 9:3; Revelation 3:9"> So Haman tooke the rayment and the horse, and arayed Mordecai, and brought him on horse backe thorowe the streete of the citie, and proclaymed before him, Thus shall it be done to the man whom the King will honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:30; Jeremiah 14:3–4; Job 20:5"> And Mordecai came againe to the Kings gate, but Haman hasted home mourning and his head couered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Hosea 14:9; Genesis 40:19"> And Haman tolde Zeresh his wife, and all his friends all that had befallen him. Then sayd his wise men, and Zeresh his wife vnto him, If Mordecai be of the seede of the Iewes, before whom thou hast begunne to fall; thou shalt not preuaile against him, but shalt surely fall before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 5:8; Esther 5:14; Deuteronomy 32:35–36"> And while they were yet talking with him, came the Kings eunuches and hasted to bring Haman vnto the banket that Ester had prepared.</span>
