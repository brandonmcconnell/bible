<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 3:9–11; 2 Chronicles 25:8; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> The destroyer is come before thy face: keepe the munition: looke to the way: make thy loynes strong: increase thy strength mightily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 80:12–13; Ezekiel 37:23; Zephaniah 3:11"> For the Lord hath turned away the glorie of Iaakob, as the glorie of Israel: for the emptiers haue emptied them out, and marred their vine branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:14–15; Isaiah 63:1–3; Zechariah 6:2"> The shield of his mightie men is made red: the valiant men are in skarlet: the charets shalbe as in the fire and flames in the day of his preparation, and the firre trees shall tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Ezekiel 26:10; Nahum 3:2–3"> The charets shall rage in the streetes: they shall runne to and from in the hie wayes: they shall seeme like lampes: they shall shoote like the lightning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:12; Jeremiah 51:27–28; Jeremiah 50:29"> He shall remember his strong men: they shall stumble as they goe: they shall make haste to the walles thereof, and the defence shall bee prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1–2; 2 Peter 3:10–11"> The gates of the riuers shalbe opened, and the palace shall melt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Isaiah 59:11; Luke 23:48"> And Huzzab the Queene shalbe led away captiue, and her maides shall leade her as with the voyce of doues, smiting vpon their breastes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:13; Revelation 17:1; Jeremiah 46:5"> But Nineueh is of olde like a poole of water: yet they shall flee away. Stande, stande, shall they crie: but none shall looke backe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:12; Daniel 11:8; Nahum 2:12–13"> Spoyle ye the siluer, spoyle the golde: for there is none ende of the store, and glorie of all the pleasant vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:6; Psalms 22:14; Daniel 5:6"> She is emptie and voyde and waste, and the heart melteth, and the knees smite together, and sorowe is in all loynes, and the faces of the all gather blackenesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:29; Jeremiah 2:15; Isaiah 31:4"> Where is the dwelling of the lyons, and the pasture of the lyons whelpes? where the lyon, and the lionesse walked, and the lyons whelpe, and none made them afrayde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34; Psalms 17:12; Isaiah 10:6–14"> The lyon did teare in pieces ynough for his whelpes, and woryed for his lyonesse, and filled his holes with praye, and his dennes with, spoyle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Psalms 46:9; Nahum 3:5"> Beholde, I come vnto thee, sayeth the Lord of hostes, and I will burne her charets in the smoke, and the sworde shall deuoure thy yong lyons, and I will cut off thy spoyle from the earth, and the voyce of thy messengers shall no more be heard.</span>
