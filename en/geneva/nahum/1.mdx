<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:13; Jonah 1:2; Zechariah 9:1"> The burden of Nineueh. The booke of the vision of Nahum the Elkeshite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 8:2; Exodus 20:5; Psalms 94:1"> God is ielous, and the Lord reuengeth: the Lord reuengeth: euen the Lord of anger, the Lord will take vengeance on his aduersaries, and he reserueth wrath for his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 147:5; Exodus 34:6–7; Nehemiah 9:17"> The Lord is slow to anger, but he is great in power, and will not surely cleare the wicked: the Lord hath his way in ye whirlewind, and in the storme, and the cloudes are the dust of his feete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 33:9; Psalms 106:9; Psalms 114:5"> He rebuketh the sea, and dryeth it, and he dryeth vp all the riuers: Bashan is wasted and Carmel, and the floure of Lebanon is wasted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 1:4; 2 Samuel 22:8; Jeremiah 4:24"> The mountaines tremble for him, and the hilles melt, and the earth is burnt at his sight, yea, the worlde, and all that dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:10; Malachi 3:2; Deuteronomy 32:22–23"> Who can stande before his wrath? or who can abide in the fiercenesse of his wrath? his wrath is powred out like fire, and the rockes are broken by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:27; 2 Timothy 2:19; Lamentations 3:25"> The Lord is good and as a strong hold in the day of trouble, and he knoweth them that trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 28:17; Isaiah 8:22; Ezekiel 13:13"> But passing ouer as with a flood, he will vtterly destroy the place thereof, and darknesse shall pursue his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 21:11; Proverbs 21:30; Psalms 33:10"> What doe ye imagine against the Lord? he wil make an vtter destruction: affliction shall not rise vp the seconde time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 7:4; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 9:18"> For he shall come as vnto thornes folden one in another, and as vnto drunkards in their drunkennesse: they shall be deuoured as stubble fully dryed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; 2 Chronicles 13:7; 2 Kings 18:13–14"> There commeth one out of thee that imagineth euill against the Lord, euen a wicked counsellour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 30:19; Joel 2:19; Isaiah 17:14"> Thus saith the Lord, Though they be quiet, and also many, yet thus shall they be cut off when he shall passe by: though I haue afflicted thee, I will afflict thee no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 10:27; Isaiah 9:4; Jeremiah 2:20"> For nowe I will breake his yoke from thee, and will burst thy bonds in sunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 109:13; Isaiah 46:1–2; Micah 5:13"> And the Lord hath giuen a commandement concerning thee, that no more of thy name be sowen: out of the house of thy gods will I cut off the grauen, and the molten image: I will make it thy graue for thee, for thou art vile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Romans 10:15; Isaiah 40:9–10"> Beholde vpon the mountaines the feete of him that declareth, and publisheth peace: O Iudah, keepe thy solemne feastes, perfourme thy vowes: for the wicked shall no more passe thorowe thee: he is vtterly cut off.</span>
