<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; Exodus 15:20; Numbers 33:36"> Then the children of Israel came with ye whole Congregation to the desert of Zin in the first moneth, and the people abode at Cadesh: where Miriam died, and was buried there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 16:19; Numbers 16:42; Exodus 15:23–24"> But there was no water for the Congregation, and they assembled them selues against Moses and against Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 17:2; Numbers 14:1–2; Numbers 16:31–35"> And the people chode with Moses, and spake, saying, Would God we had perished, when our brethren died before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 17:3; Psalms 106:21; Numbers 16:13–14"> Why haue ye thus brought the Congregation of the Lord vnto his wildernesse, that both we, and our cattell should die there?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:14; Jeremiah 2:2; Jeremiah 2:6"> Wherefore nowe haue yee made vs to come vp from Egypt, to bring vs into this miserable place, which is no place of seede, nor figges, nor vines, nor pomegranates? neither is there any water to drinke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:5; Numbers 16:4; Numbers 16:19"> Then Moses and Aaron went from the assemblie vnto the doore of the Tabernacle of the Congregation, and fell vpon their faces: and the glory of the Lord appeared vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And the Lord spake vnto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:17; Acts 2:1–4; Revelation 22:17"> Take the rod, and gather thou and thy brother Aaron the Congregation together, and speake yee vnto the rocke before their eyes, and it shall giue foorth his water, and thou shalt bring them water out of the rocke: so thou shalt giue the Congregation, and their beastes drinke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 17:10"> Then Moses tooke the rod from before the Lord, as he had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 106:32–33; Acts 14:9–15; Luke 9:54–55"> And Moses and Aaron gathered the Congregation together before the rocke, and Moses sayd vnto them, Heare nowe, ye rebels: shall we bring you water out of this rocke?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:4; Exodus 17:6; Deuteronomy 8:15"> Then Moses lift vp his hande, and with his rod he smote the rocke twise, and the water came out aboundantly: so the Congregation, and their beastes dranke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Numbers 27:14; Ezekiel 36:23"> Againe the Lord spake vnto Moses, and to Aaron, Because ye beleeued me not, to sanctifie mee in the presence of the children of Israel, therefore ye shall not bring this Congregation into the land which I haue giuen them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Psalms 95:8; Deuteronomy 33:8"> This is the water of Meribah, because the children of Israel stroue with the Lord, and hee was sanctified in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 11:16–17; Deuteronomy 23:7; Obadiah 1:10–12"> Then Moses sent messengers from Kadesh vnto the king of Edom, saying, Thus sayth thy brother Israel, Thou knowest all the trauaile that we haue had,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 12:40; Deuteronomy 26:6; Genesis 46:6"> How our fathers went downe into Egypt, and we dwelt in Egypt a long time, where the Egyptians handled vs euill and our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:19; Exodus 23:20; Exodus 33:2"> But when we cried vnto the Lord, he heard our voyce, and sent an Angel, and hath brought vs out of Egypt, and beholde, wee are in the citie Kadesh, in thine vtmost border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:27; Deuteronomy 2:29; Numbers 21:22–24"> I pray thee that we may passe through thy countrey: we will not goe through the fieldes nor the vineyardes, neither will we drinke of the water of the welles: we will goe by the kings way, and neither turne vnto the right hand nor to the left, vntill we be past thy borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And Edom answered him, Thou shalt not passe by mee, least I come out against thee with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:28; Deuteronomy 2:6; Exodus 12:38"> Then the children of Israel said vnto him, We will goe vp by the hie way: and if I and my cattell drinke of thy water, I will then pay for it: I will onely (without any harme) goe through on my feete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 11:17; Amos 1:11; Genesis 32:6"> Hee answered againe, Thou shalt not goe through. The Edom came out against him with much people, and with a mightie power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:18; Deuteronomy 2:29; Deuteronomy 2:4–8"> Thus Edom denyed to giue Israel passage through his countrey: wherefore Israel turned away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 20:14; Numbers 21:4"> And when the children of Israel with al the Congregation departed from Kadesh, they came vnto the mount Hor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 33:37"> And the Lord spake vnto Moses and to Aaron in the mount Hor neere the coast of the land of Edom, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Deuteronomy 32:50; Numbers 31:2"> Aaron shall be gathered vnto his people: for hee shall not enter into the lande, which I haue giuen vnto the children of Israel, because ye disobeyed my commandement at the water of Meribah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 33:38–39"> Take Aaron and Eleazar his sonne, and bring them vp into the mount Hor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 20:24; Isaiah 22:21–22; Hebrews 7:11"> And cause Aaron to put off his garmentes and put them vpon Eleazar his sonne: for Aaron shall be gathered to his fathers, and shall die there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And Moses did as the Lord had commanded: and they went vp into the mount Hor, in the sight of all the Congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:6; Exodus 29:29–30; Deuteronomy 34:9"> And Moses put off Aarons clothes, and put them vpon Eleazar his sonne: so Aaron dyed there in the top of the mount: and Moses and Eleazar came downe from off the mount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:8; 2 Chronicles 35:24–25; Genesis 1:10"> When al the Congregation sawe that Aaron was dead, al the house of Israel wept for Aaron thirtie dayes.</span>
