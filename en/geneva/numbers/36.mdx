<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 27:1; 1 Chronicles 7:14–16; Joshua 17:2–3"> Then the chiefe fathers of the familie of the sonnes of Gilead, the sonne of Machir, the sonne of Manasseh, of the families of the sones of Ioseph, came, and spake before Moses, and before the princes, the chiefe fathers of the children of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 17:3–6; Numbers 27:1–7; Numbers 33:54"> And saide, The Lord commanded my lord to giue the land to inherit by lot to the children of Israel: and my lord was commanded by the Lord, to giue the inheritance of Zelophehad our brother vnto his daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> If they bee married to any of the sonnes of the other tribes of the children of Israel, then shall their inheritance be taken away from the inheritance of our fathers, and shalbe put vnto the inheritance of the tribe whereof they shalbe: so shall it be taken away from the lot of our inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10–18; Isaiah 61:2; Leviticus 25:23"> Also when the Iubile of the children of Israel commeth, then shall their inheritance be put vnto the inheritance of the tribe whereof they shall be: so shall their inheritance be taken away from the inheritance of the tribe of our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 27:7; Deuteronomy 5:28"> Then Moses commanded the children of Israel, according to the word of the Lord, saying, The tribe of the sonnes of Ioseph haue said well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 36:12; 2 Corinthians 6:14; Genesis 24:3"> This is the thing that the Lord hath commanded, concerning the daughters of Zelophehad, saying, They shall be wiues, to whome they thinke best, onely to the familie of the tribe of their father shall they marry:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 21:3; Numbers 36:9"> So shall not the inheritance of the children of Israel remoue from tribe to tribe, for euery one of the children of Israel shall ioyne himselfe to the inheritance of the tribe of his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:22"> And euery daughter that possesseth any inheritance of the tribes of the children of Israel, shalbe wife vnto one of the familie of the tribe of her father: that the children of Israel may enioye euery man the inheritance of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Neither shall the inheritance go about from tribe to tribe: but euery one of the tribes of the childre of Israel shall sticke to his own inheritace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:12; Matthew 28:20; Exodus 39:42–43"> As the Lord commanded Moses, so did the daughters of Zelophehad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 27:1; Numbers 26:33"> For Mahlah, Tirzah, and Hoglah, and Milcah, and Noah the daughters of Zelophehad were married vnto their fathers brothers sonnes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> They were wiues to certaine of the families of the sonnes of Manasseh the sonne of Ioseph: so their inheritance remained in the tribe of the familie of their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:34; Leviticus 26:46; Numbers 22:1"> These are the commandements and lawes which the Lord commanded by the hand of Moses, vnto the children of Israel in the plaine of Moab, by Iorden toward Iericho.</span>
