<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:45; Numbers 11:1–4"> Then all ye Congregation lifted vp their voice, and cryed: and the people wept that night,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 15:24; Exodus 17:3; Numbers 16:41"> And all the children of Israel murmured against Moses and Aaron: and the whole assemblie said vnto them, Would God we had died in the land of Egypt, or in this wildernesse: would God we were dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 14:31–32; Jeremiah 9:3; Psalms 78:40"> Wherefore nowe hath the Lord brought vs into this lande to fall vpon the sworde? our wiues, and our children shall be a pray: were it not better for vs to returne into Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:39; Nehemiah 9:16–17; Deuteronomy 17:16"> And they said one to another, Let vs make a Captaine and returne into Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:4; Numbers 16:22; Numbers 16:45"> Then Moses and Aaron fell on their faces before all the assemblie of the Congregation of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Numbers 13:30; Numbers 14:38"> And Ioshua the sonne of Nun, and Caleb the sonne of Iephunneh two of them that searched the lande, rent their clothes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 13:27; Deuteronomy 1:25; Deuteronomy 8:7–9"> And spake vnto all the assemblie of the childre of Israel, saying, The land which we walked through to search it, is a very good lande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:20; Isaiah 62:4; Numbers 13:27"> If the Lord loue vs, he will bring vs into this land, and giue it vs, which is a land that floweth with milke and honie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:18; Romans 8:31; Deuteronomy 31:6"> But rebell not ye against the Lord, neither feare ye the people of the land: for they are but bread for vs: their shielde is departed from the, and the Lord is with vs, feare them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 17:4; 1 Samuel 30:6; Leviticus 9:23"> And all the multitude saide, Stone them with stones: but the glory of the Lord appeared in the Tabernacle of the Congregation, before all the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 78:32; Psalms 106:24; Psalms 78:22"> And the Lord said vnto Moses, How long will this people prouoke me, and howe long will it be, yer they beleeue me, for al the signes which I haue shewed among them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 32:10; Numbers 25:9; 2 Samuel 24:12–15"> I will smite them with the pestilence and destroy them, and will make thee a greater nation and mightier then they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 106:23; Ezekiel 20:9; Deuteronomy 32:27"> But Moses saide vnto the Lord, When the Egyptians shall heare it, (for thou broughtest this people by thy power from among them)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 15:14; Exodus 33:11; Deuteronomy 5:4"> Then they shall say to the inhabitants of this land, (for they haue heard that thou, Lord, art among this people, and that thou, Lord, art seene face to face, and that thy cloude standeth ouer them, and that thou goest before them by day time in a pillar of a cloude, and in a pillar of fire by night)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 6:16"> That thou wilt kill this people as one man: so the heathen which haue heard the fame of thee, shall thus say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:28; Joshua 7:9; Joshua 7:7"> Because the Lord was not able to bring this people into the lande, which he sware vnto them, therefore hath he slaine them in the wildernesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 9:6; Matthew 9:8; Micah 3:8"> And now, I beseech thee, let the power of my Lord be great, according as thou hast spoken, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 34:6–7; Psalms 145:8; Exodus 20:5"> The Lord is slowe to anger, and of great mercie, and forgiuing iniquitie, and sinne, but not making the wicked innocent, and visiting the wickednes of the fathers vpon the children, in the thirde and fourth generation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 34:9; Psalms 78:38; Psalms 106:45"> Be mercifull, I beseech thee, vnto the iniquitie of this people, according to thy great mercie, and as thou hast forgiuen this people from Egypt, euen vntill nowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 7:18–20; James 5:16; Psalms 106:23"> And the Lord said, I haue forgiuen it, according to thy request.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 72:19; Habakkuk 2:14; Isaiah 6:3"> Notwithstanding, as I liue, all the earth shall be filled with the glory of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 19:3; Genesis 31:7; Exodus 32:1"> For all those men which haue seene my glory, and my miracles which I did in Egypt, and in the wildernes, and haue tempted me this ten times, and haue not obeyed my voyce,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 32:11; Numbers 26:64; Ezekiel 20:15"> Certainely they shall not see the lande, whereof I sware vnto their fathers: neither shall any that prouoke me, see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 14:6–9; Joshua 14:6–14; Ephesians 6:6"> But my seruant Caleb, because he had another spirite, and hath followed me stil, euen him will I bring into the lande, whither he went, and his seede shall inherite it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:40; Numbers 13:29; Psalms 81:11–13"> Nowe the Amalekites and the Canaanites remaine in the valley: wherefore turne backe to morowe, and get you into the wildernesse, by the way of the red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> After, the Lord spake vnto Moses and to Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 16:12; 1 Corinthians 10:10; Exodus 16:28"> How long shall I suffer this wicked multitude to murmure against me? I haue heard the murmurings of the children of Israel, which they murmure against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 14:21; Hebrews 3:17; Numbers 14:2"> Tell them, As I liue (saith the Lord) I wil surely do vnto you, euen as ye haue spoken in mine eares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 1:45; 1 Corinthians 10:5; Hebrews 3:17"> Your carkeises shall fall in this wildernes, and all you that were counted through all your nombers, from twentie yeere olde and aboue, which haue murmured against me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 26:65; Numbers 14:38; Deuteronomy 1:36–38"> Ye shall not doubtles come into the land, for the which I lifted vp mine hande, to make you dwell therein, saue Caleb the sonne of Iephunneh, and Ioshua the sonne of Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:39; Psalms 106:24; Numbers 26:64"> But your children, (which ye said shoulde be a pray) them will I bring in, and they shall knowe the lande which ye haue refused:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5; Numbers 14:29; Hebrews 3:17"> But euen your carkeises shall fall in this wildernes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:35; Numbers 32:13; Deuteronomy 2:14"> And your children shall wander in the wildernesse, fourtie yeeres, and shall beare your whoredomes, vntill your carkeises be wasted in the wildernesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:6; Numbers 13:25; Jeremiah 18:9–10"> After the number of the dayes, in the which ye searched out the lande, euen fourtie dayes, euery day for a yeere, shall ye beare your iniquity, for fourtie yeeres, and ye shall feele my breach of promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; 1 Corinthians 10:5; Hebrews 3:19"> I the Lord haue said, Certainely I will doe so to all this wicked company, that are gathered together against me: for in this wildernesse they shall be consumed, and there they shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 13:31–33; Numbers 13:4–16"> And the men which Moses had sent to search the land (which, when they came againe, made all the people to murmure against him, and brought vp a slander vpon the lande)</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:10; Numbers 16:49; Hebrews 3:17"> Euen those men that did bring vp that vile slander vpon the land, shall die by a plague before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Joshua 14:6–10; Numbers 26:65"> But Ioshua the sonne of Nun, and Caleb the sonne of Iephunneh, of those men that went to search the land, shall liue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 33:4; Matthew 8:12; Hebrews 12:17"> Then Moses tolde these sayings vnto all the children of Israel, and the people sorowed greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:41; Matthew 7:21–23; Luke 13:25"> And they rose vp earely in the morning, and gate them vp into the toppe of the mountaine, saying, Loe, we be readie, to goe vp to the place which the Lord hath promised: for wee haue sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20; Job 4:9; Jeremiah 2:37"> But Moses said, Wherefore transgresse yee thus the commandement of the Lord? it will not so come well to passe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:42; Joshua 7:8; Joshua 7:12"> Goe not vp (for the Lord is not among you) lest ye be ouerthrowe before your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 13:29; Numbers 14:25; 2 Chronicles 15:2"> For the Amalekites and the Canaanites are there before you, and ye shall fall by the sworde: for in as much as ye are turned away from the Lord, the Lord also will not be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:43; 1 Samuel 4:3–11; Numbers 31:6"> Yet they presumed obstinately to goe vp to the top of the mountaine: but the Arke of the couenant of the Lord, and Moses departed not out of the campe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 21:3; Judges 1:17; Deuteronomy 1:44"> Then the Amalekites and the Canaanites, which dwelt in that mountaine, came downe and smote them, and consumed them vnto Hormah.</span>
