<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spake vnto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13; Joel 1:14; Psalms 81:3"> Make thee two trumpets of siluer: of an whole piece shalt thou make the, that thou mayest vse them for the assembling of the Congregation, and for the departure of the campe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:5; Joel 2:15–16"> And when they shall blowe with them, all the Congregation shall assemble to thee before the doore of the Tabernacle of the Cogregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 7:2; Exodus 18:21; Numbers 1:4–16"> But if they blowe with one, then the princes, or heades ouer the thousandes of Israel shall come vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 2:3–9; Numbers 10:14; Isaiah 58:1"> But if ye blow an alarme, then the campe of the that pitch on the East part, shall go forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 10:18; Numbers 2:10–16"> If ye blowe an alarme the second time, then the hoste of them that lie on the Southside shall march: for they shall blowe an alarme when they remoue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joel 2:1; Numbers 10:3–4"> But in assembling the Congregation, ye shall blowe without an alarme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 6:4–16; Numbers 31:6; 1 Chronicles 15:24"> And the sonnes of Aaron the Priest shall blowe the trumpets, and ye shall haue them as a lawe for euer in your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:18; Judges 2:18; Genesis 8:1"> And when ye goe to warre in your lande against the enemie that vexeth you, ye shall blowe an alarme with the trumpets, and ye shall bee remembred before the Lord your God, and shalbe saued from your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 81:3; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; 1 Corinthians 11:24–26"> Also in the day of your gladnesse, and in your feast dayes, and in the beginning of your moneths, ye shall also blow the trumpets ouer your burnt sacrifices, and ouer your peace offrings, that they may be a remembrance for you before your God: I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:17; Numbers 1:1; Numbers 9:11"> And in the seconde yeere, in the seconde moneth, and in the twentieth day of the moneth the cloude was taken vp from the Tabernacle of the Testimonie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 21:21; Numbers 12:16; Numbers 9:5"> And ye children of Israel departed on their iourneys out of the desart of Sinai, and the cloud rested in the wildernesse of Paran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:6; Numbers 9:23"> So they first tooke their iourney at the comandement of the Lord, by ye hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 2:3–9; Numbers 1:7; Numbers 7:12"> In the first place went the standerd of the hoste of the children of Iudah, according to their armies: and Nahshon the sonne of Amminabad was ouer his band.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 7:18; Numbers 1:8"> And ouer the band of the tribe of the children of Issachar was Nethaneel ye sonne of Zuar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 7:24; Numbers 1:9"> And ouer the band of the tribe of the children of Zebulun was Eliab the sonne of Helon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 7:6–8; Numbers 1:51; Hebrews 12:28"> When the Tabernacle was taken downe, then the sonnes of Gershon, and the sonnes of Merari went forward bearing the Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 2:10–16; Numbers 1:5; Numbers 26:5–18"> After, departed the standerd of the hoste of Reuben, according to their armies, and ouer his band was Elizur the sonne of Shedeur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 1:6; Numbers 7:36"> And ouer the band of the tribe of ye children of Simeon was Shelumiel the sonne of Shurishaddai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 1:14; Numbers 2:14; Numbers 7:42"> And ouer the bande of the tribe of ye children of Gad was Eliasaph the sonne of Deuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 10:17; Numbers 7:9; Numbers 4:4–16"> The Kohathites also went forward and bare the Sanctuarie, and the former did set vp the Tabernacle against they came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 2:18–24; Numbers 1:10; Numbers 26:23–41"> Then the standerd of the hoste of the children of Ephraim went forward according to their armies, and ouer his bande was Elishama the sonne of Ammiud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:10; Numbers 7:54"> And ouer the band of the tribe of ye sonnes of Manasseh was Gamliel the sonne of Pedazur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 1:11; Numbers 7:60"> And ouer the band of ye tribe of the sonnes of Beniamin was Abidan the sonne of Gideoni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 6:9; Numbers 1:12; Genesis 49:16–17"> Last, the standerd of the hoste of the children of Dan marched, gathering all ye hostes according to their armies: and ouer his bande was Ahiezer the sonne of Ammishaddai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:13; Numbers 7:72"> And ouer the bande of the tribe of the children of Asher was Pagiel the sonne of Ocran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 1:15; Numbers 7:78"> And ouer the bande of the tribe of the children of Naphtali was Ahira ye sonne of Enan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 2:34; Numbers 24:4–5; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> These were the remouings of the children of Israel according to their armies, whe they marched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Judges 4:11; Exodus 3:1"> After, Moses said vnto Hobab ye sonne of Reuel the Midianite, ye father in law of Moses, We go into the place, of which ye Lord said, I will giue it you: Come thou with vs, and we wil doe thee good: for ye Lord hath promised good vnto Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ruth 1:15–17; Psalms 45:10; Matthew 21:29"> And he answered him, I will not goe: but I will depart to mine owne countrey, and to my kindred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Job 29:15; Galatians 6:2; 1 Corinthians 12:14–21"> Then he sayd, I pray thee, leaue vs not: for thou knowest our camping places in the wildernesse: therefore thou mayest be our guide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Deuteronomy 10:18; 1 John 1:3"> And if thou go with vs, what goodnes the Lord shall shew vnto vs, the same will we shewe vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:33; Exodus 3:1; Psalms 95:11"> So they departed from the mount of the Lord, three dayes iourney: and the Arke of the couenant of the Lord went before them in the three dayes iourney, to searche out a resting place for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Nehemiah 9:19; Psalms 105:39"> And the cloude of the Lord was vpon the by day, when they went out of the campe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 68:1–2; Isaiah 17:12–14; Psalms 132:8"> And when the Arke went forwarde, Moses saide, Rise vp, Lord, and let thine enemies bee scattered, and let them that hate thee, flee before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:10; Psalms 90:13–17; Isaiah 63:17"> And when it rested, hee sayde, Returne, O Lord, to the many thousands of Israel.</span>
