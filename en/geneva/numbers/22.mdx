<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:1; Deuteronomy 3:8; Numbers 21:20"> After, the children of Israel departed and pitched in the plaine of Moab on the other side of Iorden from Iericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:25; Numbers 21:20–35; Numbers 21:3"> Now Balak the sonne of Zippor sawe all that Israel had done to the Amorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 15:15; Isaiah 23:5; Joshua 9:24"> And the Moabites were sore afraide of the people, because they were many, and Moab fretted against the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 25:15–18; Numbers 31:8; Numbers 24:17"> Therfore Moab said vnto the Elders of Midian, Nowe shall this multitude licke vp all that are round about vs, as an oxe licketh vp ye grasse of the fielde: and Balak the sonne of Zippor was King of the Moabites at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:4; Joshua 24:9; Micah 6:5"> Hee sent messengers therefore vnto Balaam the sonne of Beor to Pethor (which is by the riuer of the lande of the children of his folke) to call him, saying, Beholde, there is a people come out of Egypt, which couer the face of the earth, and lye ouer against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Numbers 23:7–8; Acts 16:16"> Come now therefore, I pray thee, and curse me this people (for they are stronger then I) so it may be that I shall be able to smite them, and to driue them out of the land: for I knowe that hee, whome thou blessest, is blessed, and he whom thou cursest, shall be cursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:7–8; Micah 3:11; Romans 16:18"> And the Elders of Moab, and the Elders of Midian departed, hauing the reward of the soothsaying in their hande, and they came vnto Balaam, and tolde him the wordes of Balak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:12; Numbers 22:19–20; Jeremiah 12:2"> Who answered them, Tary here this night, and I will giue you an answere, as the Lord shall say vnto mee. So the princes of Moab abode with Balaam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 20:3; Numbers 22:20; Genesis 16:8"> Then God came vnto Balaam, and sayde, What men are these with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Baalam said vnto God, Balak ye sonne of Zippor, king of Moab hath set vnto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Beholde, there is a people come out of Egypt and couereth the face of the earth: come nowe, curse them for my sake: so it may be that I shalbe able to ouercome them in battell, and to driue them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Ephesians 1:3; Genesis 12:2"> And God said vnto Balaam, Go not thou with them, neither curse the people, for they are blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Numbers 22:14"> And Balaam rose vp in the morning, and sayde vnto ye princes of Balak, Returne vnto your land: for the Lord hath refused to giue me leaue to go with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 22:37; Numbers 22:13"> So the princes of Moab rose vp, and went vnto Balak, and sayd, Balaam hath refused to come with vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 10:7–8; Numbers 22:7–8"> Balak yet sent againe moe princes, and more honourable then they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Who came to Balaam, and sayde to him, Thus saith Balak the sonne of Zippor, Bee not thou staied, I pray thee, from comming vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 24:11; Esther 5:11; Matthew 4:8–9"> For I wil promote thee vnto great honour, and wil do whatsoeuer thou sayest vnto me: come therefore, I pray thee, curse me this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 24:13; 1 Kings 22:14; 2 Chronicles 18:13"> And Balaam answered, and sayde vnto the seruants of Balak, If Balak woulde giue me his house full of siluer and golde, I can not goe beyonde the worde of the Lord my God, to doe lesse or more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jude 1:11; 2 Peter 2:3; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> But nowe, I pray you, tary here this night, that I may wit, what the Lord will say vnto mee more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 23:12; Numbers 24:13; Numbers 23:26"> And God came vnto Balaam by night, and sayd vnto him, If the men come to call thee, rise vp, and goe with them: but onely what thing I say vnto thee, that shalt thou doe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 1:15–16"> So Balaam rose vp early, and sadled his asse, and went with the princes of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 4:24; Lamentations 2:4; Numbers 22:32"> And ye wrath of God was kindled, because he went: and the Angel of the Lord stood in the way to be against him, as he rode vpon his asse, and his two seruants were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jude 1:11; 2 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29"> And when the asse saw the Angel of the Lord stand in the way, and his sworde drawen in his hand, the asse turned out of the way and went into the field, but Balaam smote the asse, to turne her into the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Againe the Angel of the Lord stood in a path of the vineyardes, hauing a wall on the one side, and a wall on the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 47:12; Job 5:13–15"> And when the asse sawe the Angel of the Lord, she thrust her selfe vnto the wall, and dasht Balaams foote against the wall: wherefore hee smote her againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 2:6; Isaiah 26:11"> Then the Angel of the Lord went further, and stoode in a narowe place, where was no way to turne, either to the right hand, or to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 14:16; James 1:19; Proverbs 27:3–4"> And when the asse sawe the Angell of the Lord, she lay downe vnder Balaam: therefore Balaam was very wroth, and smote the asse with a staffe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Peter 2:16; Luke 1:37; Exodus 4:11"> Then the Lord opened the mouth of the asse, and she saide vnto Balaam, What haue I done vnto thee, that thou hast smitten me nowe three times?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 12:10; Matthew 15:19; Proverbs 12:16"> And Balaam saide vnto the asse, Because thou hast mocked me: I woulde there were a sworde in mine hand, for nowe would I kill thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 1:27–28"> And the asse saide vnto Balaam, Am not I thine asse, which thou hast ridden vpon since thy first time vnto this day? haue I vsed at any time to doe thus vnto thee? Who said, Nay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 21:19; Luke 24:31; Acts 26:18"> And the Lord opened the eyes of Balaam, and he sawe the Angel of the Lord standing in the way with his sword drawen in his hande: then he bowed him selfe, and fell flat on his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Acts 13:10; Numbers 22:22"> And the Angel of the Lord said vnto him, Wherefore hast thou nowe smitten thine asse three times? beholde, I came out to withstande thee, because thy way is not straight before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 16:33–35; Numbers 14:37; 1 Kings 13:24–28"> But the asse sawe me, and turned from me now three times: for els, if she had not turned from me, surely I had euen nowe slaine thee, and saued her aliue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 34:31–32; 2 Samuel 12:13; 1 Samuel 15:24"> Then Balaam saide vnto the Angel of the Lord, I haue sinned: for I wist not that thou stoodest in the way against me: now therefore if it displease thee, I will turne home againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–12; Isaiah 37:26–29; Numbers 22:20–21"> But the Angel said vnto Balaam, Go with the men: but what I say vnto thee, that shalt thou speake. So Balaam went with ye princes of Balak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 16:2; Acts 28:15; Jeremiah 48:20"> And when Balak heard that Balaam came, he went out to meete him vnto a citie of Moab, which is in the border of Arnon, euen in the vtmost coast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 24:11; John 5:44; Matthew 4:8–9"> Then Balak saide vnto Balaam, Did I not sende for thee to call thee? Wherefore camest thou not vnto me? am I not able in deede to promote thee vnto honour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 22:18; Proverbs 19:21; Numbers 23:16"> And Balaam made answere vnto Balak, Lo, I am come vnto thee, and can I nowe say any thing at all? the worde that God putteth in my mouth, that shall I speake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> So Balaam went with Balak, and they came vnto the citie of Huzoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 23:14; Proverbs 1:16; Numbers 23:2"> Then Balak offred bullockes, and sheepe, and sent thereof to Balaam, and to the princes that were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 23:13; Numbers 25:2–3; 2 Chronicles 11:15"> And on the morowe Balak tooke Balaam, and brought him vp into the hie places of Baal, that thence hee might see the vtmost part of the people.</span>
