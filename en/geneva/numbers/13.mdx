<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 12:16; Numbers 32:8; Deuteronomy 1:19"> Sende thou men out to search the lande of Canaan which I giue vnto the children of Israel: of euery tribe of their fathers shall ye sende a man, such as are all rulers among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then Moses sent them out of the wildernesse of Paran at the commandement of the Lord: all those men were heades of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Also their names are these: of the tribe of Reuben, Shammua the sonne of Zaccur:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 34:19; Numbers 14:6; Numbers 13:30"> Of the tribe of Simeon, Shaphat the sonne of Hori:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Of the tribe of Iudah, Caleb the sonne of Iephunneh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 24:13; Numbers 11:28; Numbers 13:16"> Of the tribe of Issachar, Igal the sonne of Ioseph:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Of the tribe of Ephraim, Oshea the sone of Nun:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Of the tribe of Beniamin, Palti the sonne of Raphu:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Of the tribe of Zebulun, Gaddiel the sone of Sodi:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Of the tribe of Ioseph, to wit, of the tribe of Manasseh, Gaddi the sonne of Susi:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Of the tribe of Dan, Ammiel the sonne of Gemalli:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Of the tribe of Asher, Sethur the sonne of Michael:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Of the tribe of Naphtali, Nahbi the sonne of Vophsi:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 13:8; Exodus 17:9; Numbers 14:6"> Of the tribe of Gad, Geuel the sonne of Machi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 1:9; Genesis 12:9; Judges 1:19"> These are the names of the men, which Moses sent to spie out the lande: and Moses called ye name of Oshea the sonne of Nun, Iehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:14; Exodus 3:8"> So Moses sent them to spie out the lande of Canaan, and said vnto them, Go vp this way toward the South, and go vp into the moutaines,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And consider the land what it is, and the people that dwel therein, whether they be strong or weake, either fewe or many,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:23; Deuteronomy 31:6–8; Ezekiel 34:14"> Also what the lande is that they dwell in, whether it be good or bad: and what cities they be, that they dwell in, whether they dwell in tents, or in walled townes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 33:36; Numbers 27:14"> And what the land is: whether it be fat or leane, whether there be trees therein, or not. And be of good courage, and bring of the fruite of the lande (for then was the time of the first ripe grapes)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 78:12; Psalms 78:43; Isaiah 19:11"> So they went vp, and searched out the lande, from the wildernesse of Zin vnto Rehob, to go to Hamath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 13:24; Numbers 32:9; Deuteronomy 1:24–25"> And they ascended toward the South, and came vnto Hebron, where were Ahiman, Sheshai and Talmai, the sonnes of Anak. And Hebron was built seuen yeere before Zoan in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Then they came to the riuer of Eshcol, and cut downe thence a branch with one cluster of grapes, and they bare it vpon a barre betweene two, and brought of the pomegranates and of the figges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 24:18; Numbers 14:33–34"> That place was called the riuer Eshcol, because of the cluster of grapes, which the children of Israel cut downe thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Numbers 20:1; Deuteronomy 1:19"> Then after fourtie dayes, they turned againe from searching of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 13:5"> And they went and came to Moses and to Aaron and vnto al the Congregation of the children of Israel, in the wildernesse of Paran, to Kadesh, and brought to the, and to all the Congregation tydings, and shewed them the fruite of the lande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:1–2; Deuteronomy 1:28; Numbers 13:33"> And they tolde him, and saide, We came vnto the land whither thou hast sent vs, and surely it floweth with milke and honie: and here is of the fruite of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 14:43; Judges 6:3; Genesis 15:19–21"> Neuerthelesse the people be strong that dwell in the land, and the cities are walled and exceeding great: and moreouer, we sawe the sonnes of Anak there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Romans 8:31; Philippians 4:13"> The Amalekites dwell in the South countrey, and the Hittites, and the Iebusites, and the Amorites dwell in the mountaines, and the Canaanites dwell by the sea, and by the coast of Iorden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Joshua 14:8; Hebrews 3:19"> Then Caleb stilled the people before Moses, and saide, Let vs go vp at once, and possesse it: for vndoubtedly we shall ouercome it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Numbers 14:36–37; Ezekiel 36:13–14"> But the men, that went vp with him, saide, we be not able to goe vp against the people: for they are stronger then we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Isaiah 40:22; Genesis 6:4"> So they brought vp an euill report of the land which they had searched for the children of Israel, saying, The lande which we haue gone through to search it out, is a land that eateth vp the inhabitants thereof: for all the people that we sawe in it, are men of great stature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> For there we sawe gyants, the sonnes of Anak, which come of the gyants, so that we seemed in our sight like grashoppers: and so wee were in their sight.</span>
