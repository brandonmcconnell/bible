<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spake vnto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 13:5; Amos 2:11–12; Romans 1:1"> Speake vnto the children of Israel, and say vnto them, When a man or a woman doeth separate themselues to vowe a vowe of a Nazarite to separate himselfe vnto the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 1:15; 1 Timothy 5:23; Amos 2:12"> He shall abstaine from wine and strong drinke, and shall drinke no sowre wine nor sowre drinke, nor shall drinke any licour of grapes, neither shall eate fresh grapes nor dryed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 6:5; Numbers 6:21; Numbers 6:8–9"> As long as his abstinence endureth, shall hee eat nothing that is made of the wine of the vine, neither the kernels, nor the huske.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:11; Judges 16:17; Judges 13:5"> While hee is separate by his vowe, the rasor shall not come vpon his head, vntill the dayes be out, in the which he separateth him selfe vnto the Lord, he shalbe holy, and shall let the lockes of the heare of his head growe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 21:11; Leviticus 19:28; Leviticus 21:1–3"> During the time that he separateth himselfe vnto the Lord, he shall come at no dead body:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 9:6; Leviticus 21:1–2; Leviticus 21:10–12"> Hee shall not make himselfe vncleane at the death of his father, or mother, brother, or sister: for the consecration of his God is vpon his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:17–18"> All the dayes of his separation he shalbe holy to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 6:18; Acts 18:18; Leviticus 14:9"> And if any dye suddenly by him, or hee beware, then the head of his consecration shall be defiled, and he shall shaue his head in the day of his clensing: in the seuenth day he shall shaue it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 15:29; Leviticus 1:14; Leviticus 15:14"> And in the eight day hee shall bring two turtles, or two yong pigeons to the Priest, at the doore of the Tabernacle of the Congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 14:30–31; Leviticus 5:8–10"> Then the Priest shall prepare the one for a sinne offering, and the other for a burnt offering, and shall make an atonement for him, because he sinned by the dead: so shall he halowe his head the same day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 5:6; Matthew 24:13; Leviticus 14:24"> And he shall consecrate vnto the Lord the dayes of his separation, and shall bring a lambe of a yeere olde for a trespasse offering, and the first dayes shalbe voide: for his consecration was defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 21:26"> This then is the lawe of the Nazarite: When the time of his consecration is out, he shall come to the doore of the Tabernacle of the Congregation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 3:6; Leviticus 4:32; Leviticus 4:2–3"> And hee shall bring his offering vnto the Lord, an hee lambe of a yeere olde without blemish for a burnt offering, and a shee lambe of a yere olde without blemish for a sinne offring, and a ramme without blemish for peace offrings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 2:4; Exodus 29:2; Numbers 15:10"> And a basket of vnleauened bread, of cakes of fine floure, mingled with oyle, and wafers of vnleauened bread anointed with oile, with their meate offring, and their drinke offrings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The which the Priest shall bring before the Lord, and make his sinne offering and his burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He shall prepare also the ram for a peace offring vnto the Lord, with the basket of vnleauened bread, and the Priest shall make his meate offring, and his drinke offring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:24; Numbers 6:9; Acts 18:18"> And the Nazarite shall shaue the head of his consecration at the doore of the Tabernacle of the Congregation, and shall take the heare of the head of his consecration, and put it in the fire, which is vnder the peace offring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:15; Leviticus 7:30; Exodus 29:23–28"> Then the Priest shall take ye sodden shoulder of the ramme, and an vnleauened cake out of the basket, and a wafer vnleauened, and put them vpon the hands of the Nazarite, after he hath shauen his consecration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Exodus 29:27–28; Numbers 5:25"> And the Priest shall shake them to and from before the Lord: this is an holy thing for the Priest besides the shaken breast, and besides the heaue shoulder: so afterwarde the Nazarite may drinke wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Ezra 2:69; Numbers 5:29"> This is the Lawe of the Nazarite, which he hath vowed, and of his offering vnto the Lord for his consecration, besides that that hee is able to bring: according to the vowe which he vowed, so shall he do after the lawe of his consecration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the Lord spake vnto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; 1 Chronicles 23:13; Genesis 48:20"> Speake vnto Aaron and to his sonnes, saying, Thus shall ye blesse the childre of Israel, and say vnto them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23; Psalms 121:4–7; Philippians 4:7"> The Lord blesse thee, and keepe thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 80:19; Psalms 119:135; Psalms 31:16"> The Lord make his face shine vpon thee, and be merciful vnto thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 4:6; Psalms 29:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> The Lord lift vp his coutenance vpon thee, and giue thee peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; Daniel 9:18–19; Deuteronomy 28:10"> So they shall put my Name vpon the children of Israel, and I wil blesse them.</span>
