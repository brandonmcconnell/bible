<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:18; Isaiah 1:1; Hosea 1:1"> The word of the Lord, that came vnto Micah the Morashite in the dayes of Iotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah Kings of Iudah, which he sawe concerning Samaria, and Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Psalms 11:4; Revelation 3:13"> Heare, al ye people: hearke thou, O earth, and all that therein is, and let the Lord God be witnes against you, euen ye Lord from his holy Teple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 4:13; Isaiah 26:21; Deuteronomy 32:13"> For beholde, the Lord commeth out of his place, and will come downe, and tread vpon the hie places of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nahum 1:5; Psalms 97:5; Judges 5:4"> And the mountaines shall melt vnder him (so shall the valleys cleaue) as waxe before the fire, and as the waters that are powred downewarde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 8:14; Jeremiah 2:19; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16"> For the wickednes of Iaakob is all this, and for the sinnes of the house of Israel: what is the wickednes of Iaakob? Is not Samaria? and which are the hie places of Iudah? Is not Ierusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:14; Micah 3:12; Lamentations 4:1"> Therefore I wil make Samaria as an heape of the fielde, and for the planting of a vineyard, and I will cause the stones thereof to tumble downe into the valley, and I will discouer the foundations thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:18; Hosea 2:12; Hosea 8:6"> And all the grauen images thereof shalbe broken, and all the giftes thereof shalbe burnt with the fire, and all the idoles thereof will I destroy: for she gathered it of the hire of an harlot, and they shall returne to the wages of an harlot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 22:4; Isaiah 20:2–4; Jeremiah 4:19"> Therefore I will mourne and howle: I wil goe without clothes, and naked: I will make lamentation like the dragons, and mourning as the ostriches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:11–15; Isaiah 8:7–8; 2 Chronicles 32:1–23"> For her plagues are grieuous: for it is come into Iudah: the enemie is come vnto the gate of my people, vnto Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:20; Jeremiah 6:26; Joshua 18:23"> Declare ye it not at Gath, neither weepe ye: for the house of Aphrah roule thy selfe in the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 48:9; Micah 1:8"> Thou that dwellest at Shaphir, go together naked with shame: she that dwelleth at Zaanan, shall not come forth in ye mourning of Beth-ezel: the enemie shall receiue of you for his standing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:19; Micah 1:9; Amos 3:6"> For the inhabitant of Maroth wayted for good, but euill came from the Lord vnto the gate of Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; 2 Chronicles 32:9; Joshua 10:3"> O thou inhabitant of Lachish, binde the charet to the beastes of price: she is the beginning of the sinne to the daughter of Zion: for the transgressions of Israel were found in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 15:44; 2 Kings 16:8; 2 Samuel 8:2"> Therefore shalt thou giue presents to Moresheth Gath: the houses of Achzib shalbe as a lye to the Kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 15:44; 1 Samuel 22:1; Joshua 15:35"> Yet will I bring an heire vnto thee, O inhabitant of Mareshah, he shall come vnto Adullam, the glorie of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 22:12; Job 1:20; Jeremiah 7:29"> Make thee balde: and shaue thee for thy delicate children: enlarge thy baldenesse as the eagle, for they are gone into captiuity from thee.</span>
