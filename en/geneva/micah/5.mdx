<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Lamentations 3:30; Job 16:10; Matthew 26:67"> Nowe assemble thy garisons, O daughter of garisons: he hath layed siege against vs: they shall smite the iudge of Israel with a rod vpon the cheeke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:6; John 7:42; Luke 1:31–33"> And thou Beth-leem Ephrathah art litle to bee among the thousandes of Iudah, yet out of thee shall he come forth vnto me, that shalbe the ruler in Israel: whose goings forth haue bene from the beginning and from euerlasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:1–2; Romans 9:27–28; Micah 4:7"> Therefore will he giue them vp, vntill the time that shee which shall beare, shall trauaile: then the remnant of their brethren shall returne vnto the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 40:10–11; Micah 7:14; Luke 1:32"> And he shall stand, and feed in the strength of the Lord, and in the maiestie of the Name of the Lord his God, and they shall dwel still: for now shall he be magnified vnto the ends of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 2:14; Zechariah 10:3; Ephesians 2:14–17"> And hee shall be our peace when Asshur shall come into our lande: when he shall tread in our palaces, then shall we raise against him seuen shepheardes, and eight principall men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 10:8–11; Isaiah 14:25; Isaiah 14:2"> And they shall destroy Asshur with the sword, and the land of Nimrod with their swordes: thus shall he deliuer vs from Asshur, when hee commeth into our lande, and when he shall tread within our borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 5:3; Isaiah 44:3; Psalms 72:6"> And the remnant of Iaakob shalbe among many people, as a dewe from the Lord, and as the showres vpon the grasse, that waiteth not for man, nor hopeth in the sonnes of Adam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 10:5; Micah 4:13; Zechariah 9:15"> And the remnant of Iaakob shalbe among the Gentiles in the middes of many people, as the lyon among the beastes of the forest, and as the lyons whelpe among the flockes of sheepe, who when he goeth thorow, treadeth downe and teareth in pieces, and none can deliuer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Psalms 21:8; Isaiah 14:2–4"> Thine hand shall bee lift vp vpon thine aduersaries, and all thine enemies shalbe cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 9:10; Hosea 14:3; Psalms 20:7–8"> And it shall come to passe in that day, sayth the Lord, that I will cut off thine horses out of the middes of thee, and I will destroy thy charets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 2:12–17; Ezekiel 38:11; Hosea 10:14"> And I will cut off the cities of thy land, and ouerthrowe all thy strong holdes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:10–12; Isaiah 8:19–20; Isaiah 2:20"> And I will cut off thine enchanters out of thine hande: and thou shalt haue no more southsayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Ezekiel 36:25; Hosea 14:8"> Thine idoles also will I cut off, and thine images out of the middes of thee: and thou shalt no more worship the woorke of thine hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 34:13"> And I wil plucke vp thy groues out of the middes of thee: so will I destroy thine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 149:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Micah 5:8"> And I will execute a vegeance in my wrath and indignation vpon the heathen, which they haue not heard.</span>
