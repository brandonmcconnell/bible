<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 28:4; Isaiah 24:13; Hosea 9:10"> Woe is me, for I am as the sommer gatherings, and as the grapes of the vintage: there is no cluster to eate: my soule desired the first ripe fruites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 12:1; Isaiah 57:1; Isaiah 59:7"> The good man is perished out of the earth, and there is none righteous among men: they all lye in wayte for blood: euery man hunteth his brother with a net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Proverbs 4:16–17; Amos 5:12"> To make good for the euil of their hands, the prince asked, and the iudge iudgeth for a reward: therefore the great man he speaketh out the corruption of his soule: so they wrapt it vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 22:5; Ezekiel 2:6; Isaiah 10:3"> The best of them is as a brier, and the most righteous of them is sharper then a thorne hedge: the day of thy watchmen and thy visitation commeth: then shalbe their confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:4; Psalms 118:8–9; Matthew 10:16"> Trust ye not in a friend, neither put ye confidence in a counseller: keepe the doores of thy mouth from her that lyeth in thy bosome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 10:35–36; Luke 12:53; Matthew 10:21"> For the sonne reuileth the father: ye daughter riseth vp against her mother: the daughter in lawe against her mother in lawe, and a mans enemies are the men of his owne house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; Psalms 37:7; 1 John 5:14–15"> Therefore I will looke vnto the Lord: I will waite for God my Sauiour: my God will heare me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:12; Isaiah 9:2; Psalms 27:1"> Reioyce not against me, O mine enemie: though I fall, I shall arise: when I shall sit in darkenesse, the Lord shalbe a light vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 37:6; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Samuel 24:15"> I will beare the wrath of the Lord because I haue sinned against him, vntill he pleade my cause, and execute iudgement for me: then will he bring me foorth to the light, and I shall see his righteousnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 10:5; Jeremiah 50:33–34; Psalms 42:10"> Then she that is mine enemie, shall looke vpon it, and shame shall couer her, which said vnto me, Where is the Lord thy God? Mine eyes shall behold her: now shall she be troden downe as the myre of the streetes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 9:11–15; Nehemiah 2:8; Nehemiah 2:17"> This is ye day, that thy walles shalbe built: this day shall driue farre away the decree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 19:23–25; Isaiah 11:16; Hosea 11:11"> In this day also they shall come vnto thee from Asshur, and from the strong cities, and from the strong holdes euen vnto the riuer, and from Sea to Sea, and from mountaine to mountaine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10–11; Jeremiah 25:11; Micah 6:13"> Notwithstanding, the lande shall be desolate because of them that dwell therein, and for the fruites of their inuentions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 23:1–4; Micah 5:4; Psalms 100:3"> Feed thy people with thy rod, the flocke of thine heritage (which dwell solitarie in the wood) as in the middes of Carmel: let them feede in Bashan and Gilead, as in olde time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 78:12–72; Exodus 3:20; Psalms 68:22"> According to the dayes of thy comming out of the lande of Egypt, will I shewe vnto him marueilous things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Isaiah 52:15; Job 29:9–10"> The nations shall see, and be confounded for all their power: they shall lay their hande vpon their mouth: their eares shall be deafe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Psalms 72:9; Psalms 18:45"> They shall licke the dust like a serpent: they shall mooue out of their holes like wormes: they shalbe afraide of the Lord our God, and shall feare because of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 44:22; Psalms 103:8–9"> Who is a God like vnto thee, that taketh away iniquitie, and passeth by the transgression of the remnant of his heritage! He reteineth not his wrath for euer, because mercie pleaseth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 38:17; Romans 6:14; Isaiah 43:25"> He will turne againe, and haue compassion vpon vs: he will subdue our iniquities, and cast all their sinnes into the bottome of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–14; Luke 1:72–74; Deuteronomy 7:8"> Thou wilt perfourme thy trueth to Iaakob, and mercie to Abraham, as thou hast sworne vnto our fathers in olde time.</span>
