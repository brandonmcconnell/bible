<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 4:11; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Ephesians 4:32"> Bee yee therefore followers of God, as deare children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:34; Galatians 2:20; Colossians 3:14"> And walke in loue, euen as Christ hath loued vs, and hath giuen himselfe for vs, to be an offering and a sacrifice of a sweete smellling sauour to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Ephesians 5:5; Galatians 5:19–21"> But fornication, and all vncleannesse, or couetousnesse, let it not be once named among you, as it becommeth Saintes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:8; Ephesians 4:29; 1 Thessalonians 5:18"> Neither filthinesse, neither foolish talking, neither iesting, which are things not comely, but rather giuing of thankes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Ephesians 5:3"> For this ye know, that no whoremonger, neither vncleane person, nor couetous person, which is an idolater, hath any inheritance in the kingdome of Christ, and of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 1:18; Colossians 2:8; Matthew 24:4"> Let no man deceiue you with vaine wordes: for, for such thinges commeth the wrath of God vpon the children of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Ephesians 5:11; 1 Timothy 5:22"> Be not therefore companions with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:12; 1 John 1:7; Acts 26:18"> For ye were once darkenesse, but are nowe light in the Lord: walke as children of light,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:9–10; Galatians 5:22–23; 3 John 1:11"> (For the fruit of the Spirit is in al goodnes, and righteousnes, and trueth)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:1–2; Hebrews 12:28; Psalms 19:14"> Approuing that which is pleasing to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 13:12; 1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> And haue no fellowship with ye vnfruitfull works of darknes, but euen reproue them rather.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:3; Jeremiah 23:24; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> For it is shame euen to speake of the things which are done of them in secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:20–21; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Hosea 2:10"> But all thinges when they are reproued of the light, are manifest: for it is light that maketh all things manifest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; Romans 13:11–12; Isaiah 26:19"> Wherefore hee sayeth, Awake thou that sleepest, and stande vp from the deade, and Christ shall giue thee light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; James 3:13; Philippians 1:27"> Take heede therefore that yee walke circumspectly, not as fooles, but as wise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; Amos 5:13; John 12:35"> Redeeming ye season: for ye daies are euill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Colossians 1:9"> Wherefore, be ye not vnwise, but vnderstand what the will of the Lord is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Luke 1:15; Romans 13:13"> And be not drunke with wine, wherein is excesse: but be fulfilled with the Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 1 Corinthians 14:26; Psalms 95:2"> Speaking vnto your selues in psalmes, and hymnes, and spirituall songs, singing, and making melodie to the Lord in your hearts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Hebrews 13:15"> Giuing thankes alwaies for all thinges vnto God euen the Father, in the Name of our Lord Iesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Ephesians 5:24; Ephesians 5:22"> Submitting your selues one to another in the feare of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:18–1; 1 Peter 3:1–6; Titus 2:5"> Wiues, submit your selues vnto your husbands, as vnto the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3–10; Colossians 1:18; Ephesians 1:22–23"> For the husband is the wiues head, euen as Christ is the head of the Church, and the same is the sauiour of his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 5:33; Colossians 3:20; Titus 2:7"> Therfore as the Church is in subiection to Christ, euen so let the wiues be to their husbands in euery thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Colossians 3:19; Ephesians 5:28"> Husbands, loue your wiues, euen as Christ loued the Church, and gaue himselfe for it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; John 15:3; Ezekiel 36:25"> That hee might sanctifie it, and clense it by the washing of water through the worde,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 1:22; Hebrews 9:14; 1 Peter 1:19"> That hee might make it vnto him selfe a glorious Church, not hauing spot or wrinkle, or any such thing: but that it shoulde bee holy and without blame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25; Matthew 19:5; Genesis 2:21–24"> So ought men to loue their wiues, as their owne bodies: he that loueth his wife, loueth him selfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:14–15; Ephesians 5:31; Ecclesiastes 4:5"> For no man euer yet hated his owne flesh, but nourisheth and cherisheth it, euen as the Lord doeth the Church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 12:12–27; Romans 12:5"> For we are members of his bodie, of his flesh, and of his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:5; Mark 10:7–8"> For this cause shall a man leaue father and mother, and shall cleaue to his wife, and they twaine shalbe one flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 54:5"> This is a great secrete, but I speake concerning Christ, and concerning the Church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 3:2–7; Esther 1:20; Ephesians 5:25"> Therefore euery one of you, doe ye so: let euery one loue his wife, euen as himselfe, and let the wife see that shee feare her husband.</span>
