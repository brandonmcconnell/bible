<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 15:3; Exodus 15:21; Psalms 106:12"> Then sang Moses and the children of Israel this song vnto the Lord, and sayd in this maner, I will sing vnto the Lord: for he hath triumphed gloriously: the horse and him that rode vpon him hath he ouerthrowen in the Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 118:14; Psalms 18:46"> The Lord is my strength and praise, and he is become my saluation. He is my God, and I will prepare him a tabernacle. he is my fathers God, and I will exalt him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 24:8; Exodus 14:14; Revelation 19:11–21"> The Lord is a man of warre, his Name is Iehouah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 14:13–28; Exodus 14:6–7"> Pharaohs charets and his host hath he cast into the Sea: his chosen captaines also were drowned in the red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:11; Exodus 15:10; Exodus 14:28"> The depths haue couered them, they sanke to the bottome as a stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 118:15–16; Isaiah 51:9; Revelation 2:27"> Thy right hande, O Lord, is glorious in power: thy right hand, O Lord, hath brused the enemie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 47:14; Malachi 4:1"> And in thy great glorie thou hast ouerthrowen them that rose against thee: thou sentest forth thy wrath, which consumed them as the stubble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 4:9; Psalms 78:13; Exodus 14:21–22"> And by the blast of thy nostrels the waters were gathered, the floods stoode still as an heape, the depthes congealed together in the heart of the Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Exodus 14:5; Isaiah 53:12"> The enemie sayd, I wil pursue, I wil ouertake them, I will deuide the spoyle, my lust shall bee satisfied vpon them, I will drawe my sworde, mine hand shall destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Deuteronomy 11:4; Exodus 15:5"> Thou blewest with thy winde, the Sea couered them, they sanke as leade in the mightie waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 8:23; Psalms 89:5–8; 1 Samuel 2:2"> Who is like vnto thee, O Lord, among the Gods! who is like thee so glorious in holinesse, fearefull in prayses, doing wonders!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:6"> Thou stretchedst out thy right hande, the earth swallowed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 77:20; 1 Peter 1:5; Psalms 77:14–15"> Thou wilt by thy mercie cary this people, which thou deliueredst: thou wilt bring them in thy strength vnto thine holy habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Joshua 2:9–10; Psalms 48:6"> The people shall heare and be afraide: sorow shall come vpon the inhabitants of Palestina.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 5:1; Joshua 2:11; Joshua 2:9"> Then the dukes of Edom shalbe amased, and trembling shall come vpon the great men of Moab: all the inhabitantes of Canaan shall waxe faint hearted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Psalms 74:2; Joshua 2:9"> Feare and dread shall fall vpon them: because of the greatnesse of thine arme, they shalbe stil as a stone, till thy people passe, O Lord: til this people passe, which thou hast purchased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 44:2; Psalms 80:8; Psalms 78:54–55"> Thou shalt bring them in, and plant them in the mountaine of thine inheritance, which is the place that thou hast prepared, O Lord, for to dwell in, euen the sanctuarie, O Lord, which thine hands shall establish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 10:16; Psalms 29:10; Psalms 146:10"> The Lord shall reigne for euer and euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:28–29; Exodus 14:22–23; Hebrews 11:29"> For Pharaohs horses went with his charets and horsemen into the Sea, and the Lord brought the waters of the Sea vpon them: but the children of Israel went on drie land in the middes of the Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 150:4; 1 Samuel 18:6; Psalms 68:25"> And Miriam the prophetesse, sister of Aaron tooke a timbrell in her hande, and all the women came out after her with timbrels and daunces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Revelation 15:3; Revelation 7:10–12"> And Miriam answered the men, Sing yee vnto the Lord: for he hath triumphed gloriously: the horse and his rider hath hee ouerthrowen in the Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 16:7; 1 Samuel 15:7; Genesis 25:18"> Then Moses brought Israel from the redde Sea, and they went out into the wildernesse of Shur: and they went three dayes in the wildernesse, and found no waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ruth 1:20; Numbers 33:8"> And whe they came to Marah, they could not drinke of the waters of Marah, for they were bitter: therefore the name of the place was called Marah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:2; Exodus 14:11; Numbers 20:2–5"> Then the people murmured against Moses, saying, What shall we drinke?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; Psalms 66:10; Judges 3:1"> And he cried vnto the Lord, and the Lord shewed him a tree, which when he had cast into the waters, the waters were sweete: there he made them an ordinance and a law, and there he proued them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 103:3; Deuteronomy 7:15; Exodus 23:25"> And sayd, if thou wilt diligently hearken, O Israel, vnto the voyce of the Lord thy God, and wilt do that, which is right in his sight, and wilt giue eare vnto his commandements, and keepe all his ordinances, then will I put none of these diseases vpon thee, which I brought vpon the Egyptians: for I am the Lord that healeth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 22:2; Revelation 7:17; Numbers 33:9"> And they came to Elim, where were twelue fountaines of water, and seuentie palme trees, and they camped thereby the waters.</span>
