<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the Lord spake to Moses and to Aaron in the land of Egypt, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 13:4; Deuteronomy 16:1; Exodus 34:18"> This moneth shalbe vnto you the beginning of moneths: it shalbe to you the first moneth of the yere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 1:2; 1 Corinthians 5:7; John 1:29"> Speake ye vnto all the congregation of Israel, saying, In the tenth of this moneth let euery man take vnto him a lambe, according to the house of the fathers, a lambe for an house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And if the housholde be too litle for the lambe, he shall take his neighbour, which is next vnto his house, according to the nomber of the persons: euery one of you, according to his eating shall make your count for the lambes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; Hebrews 9:13–14; Malachi 1:14"> Your lambe shalbe without blemish, a male of a yeere olde: ye shall take it of the lambes, or of the kiddes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 23:5; 2 Chronicles 30:15–18; Numbers 28:16"> And yee shall keepe it vntill the fourteenth day of this moneth: then al the multitude of the Congregation of Israel shall kill it at euen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:28; Exodus 12:22–23; Hebrews 9:22"> After, they shall take of the blood, and strike it on the two postes, and on the vpper doore post of the houses where they shall eate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 34:25; Deuteronomy 16:7; Exodus 13:3"> And they shall eate the flesh the same night, roste with fire, and vnleauened bread: with sowre herbes they shall eate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 12:8; Deuteronomy 16:7; Lamentations 1:13"> Eate not thereof rawe, boyled nor sodden in water, but rost with fire, both his head, his feete, and his purtenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 23:18; Exodus 34:25; Exodus 29:34"> And ye shall reserue nothing of it vnto the morning: but that, which remaineth of it vnto the morowe, shall ye burne with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 12:27; Ephesians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 5:7"> And thus shall yee eate it, Your loynes girded, your shoes on your feete, and your staues in your handes, and yee shall eate it in haste: for it is the Lords Passeouer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 33:4; Exodus 12:23; Psalms 82:6"> For I will passe through the lande of Egypt the same night, and will smite all the first borne in the land of Egypt, both man and beast, and I will execute iudgement vpon all the gods of Egypt. I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 11:28; Exodus 12:23; 1 John 1:7"> And the blood shalbe a token for you vpon the houses where ye are: so when I see the blood, I will passe ouer you, and the plague shall not be vpon you to destruction, when I smite the lande of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 23:21; Exodus 12:24; Exodus 13:9–10"> And this day shalbe vnto you a remembrance: and ye shall keepe it an holie feast vnto the Lord, throughout your generations: yee shall keepe it holie by an ordinance for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 17:14; Deuteronomy 16:3; Exodus 23:15"> Seuen daies shall ye eat vnleauened bread, and in any case ye shall put away leauen the first day out of your houses: for whosoeuer eateth leauened bread from the first daie vntill the seuenth day, that person shalbe cut off from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 23:7–8; Numbers 28:18; Exodus 16:23"> And in the first day shalbe an holie assemblie: also in the seuenth day shalbe an holy assemblie vnto you: no worke shalbe done in them, saue about that which euery man must eate: that onely may ye do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 7:5; Exodus 13:3; Exodus 12:41"> Ye shall keepe also the feast of vnleauened bread: for that same daye I will bring your armies out of the lande of Egypt: therefore ye shall obserue this day, throughout your posteritie, by an ordinance for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 23:5–8; Exodus 12:15; Exodus 12:1–2"> In the first moneth and the fourteenth day of the moneth at euen, yee shall eate vnleauened bread vnto the one and twentieth day of the moneth at euen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 12:15; Exodus 23:15; Deuteronomy 16:3"> Seuen daies shall no leauen be founde in your houses: for whosoeuer eateth leauened bread, that person shalbe cut off from the Congregation of Israel: whether he bee a stranger, or borne in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Ye shall eate no leauened bread: but in all your habitations shall ye eate vnleauened bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 12:3; Luke 22:7–13; 2 Chronicles 35:5–6"> Then Moses called all the Elders of Israel, and saide vnto them, Choose out and take you for euerie of your housholdes a lambe, and kill the Passeouer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:28; Numbers 19:18; Psalms 51:7"> And take a bunch of hyssop, and dip it in the blood that is in the basen, and strike the lintell, and the doore cheekes with the blood that is in the basen, and let none of you goe out at the doore of his house, vntill the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 7:3; Revelation 9:4; Hebrews 11:28"> For the Lord will passe by to smite the Egyptians: and when he seeth the blood vpon the lintel and on the two doore cheekes, the Lord wil passe ouer the doore, and wil not suffer the destroyer to come into your houses to plague you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Genesis 17:8–10"> Therefore shall ye obserue this thing as an ordinance both for thee and thy sonnes for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 3:17; Exodus 3:8; Deuteronomy 12:8–9"> And when ye shall come into the land, which the Lord will giue you as hee hath promised, then ye shall keepe this seruice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 13:14–15; Deuteronomy 32:7; Psalms 145:4"> And when your children aske you, What seruice is this ye keepe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; Exodus 12:11; 1 Corinthians 5:7"> Then ye shall saye, It is the sacrifice of the Lordes Passeouer, which passed ouer the houses of the children of Israel in Egypt, when he smote the Egyptians, and preserued our houses. Then the people bowed them selues, and worshipped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 11:28"> So the children of Israel went, and did as the Lord had commanded Moses and Aaron: so did they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 78:51; Exodus 4:23; Psalms 105:36"> Nowe at midnight, the Lord smote all the first borne in the lande of Egypt, from the first borne of Pharaoh that sate on his throne, vnto the first borne of the captiue that was in prison, and all the first borne of beastes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 11:6; Amos 5:17; Proverbs 21:13"> And Pharaoh rose vp in the night, he, and all his seruants and all the Egyptians: and there was a great crye in Egypt: for there was no house where there was not one dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 6:1; Exodus 10:9; Exodus 3:19–20"> And hee called to Moses and to Aaron by night, and saide, Rise vp, get you out from among my people, both yee, and the children of Israel, and goe serue the Lord as ye haue sayde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 10:26; Genesis 27:34; Genesis 27:38"> Take also your sheepe and your cattell as yee haue sayde, and depart, and blesse me also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 105:38; Exodus 11:1; Genesis 20:3"> And the Egyptians did force the people, because they would send them out of the land in haste: for they said, We die all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 8:3"> Therfore the people tooke their dough before it was leauened, euen their dough bound in clothes vpon their shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 3:21–22; Exodus 11:2–3; Genesis 15:14"> And the children of Israel did according to the saying of Moses, and they asked of ye Egyptians iewels of siluer and iewels of gold, and raiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 15:14; Acts 7:10; Exodus 3:21–22"> And the Lord gaue the people fauour in the sight of the Egyptians: and they graunted their request: so they spoyled the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Exodus 38:26; Numbers 33:3"> Then the children of Israel tooke their iourney from Rameses to Succoth about sixe hundreth thousand men of foote, beside children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 11:4; Zechariah 8:23"> And a great multitude of sundrie sortes of people went out with them, and sheepe, and beeues, and cattel in great abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 11:1; Exodus 6:1; Exodus 12:31–33"> And they baked the dough which they brought out of Egypt, and made vnleauened cakes: for it was not leauened, because they were thrust out of Egypt, neither coulde they tarie, nor yet prepare themselues vitailes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 15:13; Acts 7:6; Acts 13:17"> So the dwelling of the children of Israel, while they dwelled in Egypt, was foure hundreth and thirtie yeres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; John 7:8; Acts 1:7"> And when the foure hundreth and thirtie yeeres were expired, euen the selfe same day departed all the hostes of the Lord out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–6; Exodus 12:14; Exodus 13:10"> It is a night to be kept holie to the Lord, because he brought them out of the lande of Egypt: this is that night of the Lord, which all the children of Israel must keepe throughout their generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Exodus 12:48; Numbers 9:14; Exodus 12:11"> Also the Lord said vnto Moses and Aaron, This is the Lawe of the Passeouer: no stranger shall eate thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 17:12–13; Genesis 17:23"> But euerie seruant that is bought for money, when thou hast circumcised him, then shall he eat thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 22:10; Ephesians 2:12"> A stranger or an hyred seruant shall not eat thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 9:12; John 19:36; John 19:33"> In one house shall it bee eaten: thou shalt carie none of ye flesh out of the house, neither shall ye breake a bone thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Exodus 12:6; Numbers 9:13; Exodus 12:3"> All the Congregation of Israel shall obserue it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 9:14; Colossians 3:11; Galatians 3:28"> But if a stranger dwell with thee, and will obserue the Passeouer of the Lord, let him circumcise all the males, that belong vnto him, and then let him come and obserue it, and he shall be as one that is borne in the land: for none vncircumcised person shall eate thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 15:15–16; Numbers 15:29; Leviticus 24:22"> One lawe shalbe to him that is borne in the land, and to the stranger that dwelleth among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 7:24–25; Exodus 6:26; Exodus 7:4"> Then all the children of Israel did as the Lord commanded Moses and Aaron: so did they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Exodus 12:41; Exodus 6:26"> And the selfe same day did the Lord bring the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt by their armies.</span>
