<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 10:9; Acts 4:29; Matthew 10:28"> Then afterwarde Moses and Aaron went and said to Pharaoh, Thus saith the Lord God of Israel, Let my people go, that they may celebrate a feast vnto me in the wildernesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 3:19; Job 21:15; 2 Kings 18:35"> And Pharaoh saide, Who is the Lord, that I should heare his voyce, and let Israel go? I knowe not the Lord, neither will I let Israel goe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Zechariah 14:16–19; Ezekiel 6:11"> And they saide, We worship the God of the Ebrewes: we pray thee, let vs goe three daies iourney in the desert, and sacrifice vnto the Lord our God, least he bring vpon vs the pestilence or sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Acts 24:5; Amos 7:10"> Then saide the King of Egypt vnto them, Moses and Aaron, why cause ye the people to cease from their workes? get you to your burdens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 1:7–11; Proverbs 14:28"> Pharaoh saide furthermore, Behold, much people is nowe in the lande, and ye make them leaue their burdens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 5:10; Exodus 5:19; Deuteronomy 16:18"> Therefore Pharaoh gaue commandement the same day vnto the taskemasters of the people, and to their officers, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:19; Genesis 24:25"> Ye shall giue the people no more strawe, to make bricke (as in time past) but let them goe and gather them strawe them selues:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:41"> Notwithstanding lay vpon them the nober of bricke, which they made in time past, diminish nothing thereof: for they be idle, therefore they crie, saying, Let vs go to offer sacrifice vnto our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 18:20; Jeremiah 43:2; Job 16:3"> Lay more worke vpon the men, and cause them to do it, and let the not regard vaine words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Exodus 3:7; Proverbs 29:12"> Then went the taskemasters of the people and their officers out, and tolde the people, saying, Thus saith Pharaoh, I will giue you no more strawe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 5:13–14"> Goe your selues, get you strawe where yee can finde it, yet shall nothing of your labour bee diminished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:7; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 47:14"> Then were the people scattered abroade throughout all the land of Egypt, for to gather stubble in steade of strawe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the taskemasters hasted them, saying, Finish your dayes worke euery dayes taske, as ye did when ye had strawe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 10:24"> And the officers of the children of Israel, which Pharaohs taskemasters had set ouer them, were beaten, and demanded, Wherefore haue ye not fulfilled your taske in making bricke yesterday and to daye, as in times past?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then the officers of the children of Israel came, and cryed vnto Pharaoh, saying, Wherfore dealest thou thus with thy seruants?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> There is no strawe giuen to thy seruantes, and they say vnto vs, Make bricke: and loe, thy seruants are beaten, and thy people is blamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 26:8; John 6:27; Exodus 5:8"> But he said, Ye are to much idle: therfore ye say, Let vs goe to offer sacrifice to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:18; Daniel 2:9–13"> Goe therefore nowe and worke: for there shall no strawe be giuen you, yet shall yee deliuer the whole tale of bricke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:8; Ecclesiastes 4:1; Deuteronomy 32:36"> Then the officers of the children of Israel sawe them selues in an euill case, because it was saide, Ye shall diminish nothing of your bricke, nor of euery dayes taske.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And they met Moses and Aaron, which stood in their way as they came out from Pharaoh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Chronicles 19:6"> To whom they said, The Lord looke vpon you and iudge: for yee haue made our sauour to stinke before Pharaoh and before his seruants, in that ye haue put a sword in their hand to slay vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:7; 1 Kings 19:10; Numbers 11:14–15"> Wherefore Moses returned to the Lord, and saide, Lord, why hast thou afflicted this people? wherefore hast thou thus sent me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:21; Hebrews 10:36–37; Isaiah 28:16"> For since I came to Pharaoh to speake in thy Name, he hath vexed this people, and yet thou hast not deliuered thy people.</span>
