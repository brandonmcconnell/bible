<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the Lord spake vnto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 26:30; Exodus 13:4; Numbers 7:1"> In the first day of the first moneth in the very first of the same moneth shalt thou set vp the Tabernacle, called ye Tabernacle of the Congregation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:12; Numbers 4:5; Revelation 11:19"> And thou shalt put therein the Arke of the Testimonie, and couer the Arke with the vaile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 24:8; Exodus 40:22–25; Exodus 37:10–24"> Also thou shalt bring in the Table, and set it in order as it doth require: thou shalt also bring in the Candlesticke, and light his lampes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Hebrews 9:24; 1 John 2:1–2"> And thou shalt set ye incense Altar of gold before the Arke of the Testimonie, and put the hanging at the doore of the Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 40:29; 1 John 4:9–10; Hebrews 13:10"> Moreouer, thou shalt set the burnt offering Altar before the doore of the Tabernacle, called the Tabernacle of the Congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; 1 John 1:7; Psalms 26:6"> And thou shalt set the Lauer betweene the Tabernacle of the Congregation and the Altar, and put water therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 40:33; Ephesians 4:11–12; Matthew 16:18"> Then thou shalt appoynt the courte round about, and hang vp the hanging at the courte gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 8:10; Numbers 7:1; Matthew 3:16"> After, thou shalt take the anoynting oyle, and anoynt the Tabernacle, and all that is therein, and halowe it with all the instruments thereof, that it may be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 29:36–37; Luke 1:35; Isaiah 61:1"> And thou shalt anoynt the Altar of the burnt offring, and all his instruments, and shalt sanctifie the Altar, that it may bee an altar most holie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Also thou shalt anoynt the Lauer, and his foote, and shalt sanctifie it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 8:1–13; Leviticus 9:1–24; Exodus 29:1–35"> Then thou shalt bring Aaron and his sonnes vnto the doore of the Tabernacle of the Congregation, and wash them with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 28:41; 1 John 2:20; 1 John 2:27"> And thou shalt put vpon Aaron the holy garmentes, and shalt anoynt him, and sanctifie him, that he may minister vnto me in the Priestes office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Romans 8:30; 1 Corinthians 1:30"> Thou shalt also bring his sonnes, and clothe them with garments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 25:13; Hebrews 5:1–14; Hebrews 8:1–10"> And shalt anoynt them as thou diddest anoynt their father, that they may minister vnto mee in the Priestes office: for their anoynting shall be a signe, that the Priesthood shall be euerlasting vnto them throughout their generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Exodus 40:17–32; Deuteronomy 12:32"> So Moses did according to all that ye Lord had commanded him: so did he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 7:1; Exodus 40:1–2; Numbers 9:1"> Thus was the Tabernacle reared vp the first day of the first moneth in the seconde yeere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27–28; 1 Peter 1:5; Leviticus 26:11"> Then Moses reared vp the Tabernacle and fastened his sockets, and set vp the boardes thereof, and put in the barres of it, and reared vp his pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:1–14; Exodus 36:8–19"> And he spred the couering ouer the Tabernacle, and put the couering of that couering on hie aboue it, as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:34; Deuteronomy 10:5; Hebrews 10:19–21"> And he tooke and put the Testimonie in the Arke, and put the barres in the ringes of the Arke, and set the Merciseate on hie vpon the Arke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:33; Exodus 40:3; Exodus 35:12"> He brought also the Arke into the Tabernacle, and hanged vp the couering vaile, and couered the Arke of the Testimonie, as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 26:35; John 6:53–57; Ephesians 3:8"> Furthermore he put the Table in the Tabernacle of the Congregation in the Northside of the Tabernacle, without the vaile,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; Exodus 40:4; Hebrews 9:2"> And set the bread in order before the Lord, as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 26:35; Revelation 2:5; John 1:1"> Also he put the Candlesticke in the Tabernacle of the Congregation, ouer against the Table toward ye Southside of the Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 40:4; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 4:5"> And he lighted the lampes before the Lord, as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Hebrews 7:25; John 11:42"> Moreouer he set the golden Altar in the Tabernacle of the Congregation before the vayle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 30:7"> And burnt sweete incense thereon, as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Hebrews 10:19–20; Ephesians 2:18"> Also he hanged vp the vayle at the doore of the Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 40:6; Hebrews 13:5–6; Hebrews 13:10"> After, he set the burnt offring Altar without the doore of the Tabernacle, called the Tabernacle of the Congregation, and offered the burnt offering and the sacrifice thereon, as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 40:7; Exodus 38:8; Ezekiel 36:25"> Likewise he set the Lauer betweene the Tabernacle of the Congregation and the Altar, and powred water therein to wash with.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 30:19–20; Psalms 26:6; John 13:10"> So Moses and Aaron, and his sonnes washed their handes and their feete thereat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 73:19; Exodus 30:19–20; Exodus 40:19"> When they went into the Tabernacle of the Congregation, and when they approched to the Altar, they washed, as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 40:8; Exodus 27:9–16; John 17:4"> Finally, he reared vp the court rounde about the Tabernacle and the Altar, and hanged vp the vaile at the court gate: so Moses finished the worke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 15:8; Numbers 9:15–23; 2 Chronicles 7:2"> Then the cloud couered the Tabernacle of the Congregation, and the glorie of the Lord filled the Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 8:11; Leviticus 16:2; 2 Chronicles 7:2"> So Moses could not enter into the Tabernacle of the Congregation, because the cloude abode thereon, and the glorie of the Lord filled the Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:19; Numbers 9:17; Exodus 13:21–22"> Nowe when the cloude ascended vp from the Tabernacle, the children of Israel went forward in all their iourneyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 9:19–22; Psalms 31:15"> But if the cloude ascended not, then they iourneyed not till the day that it ascended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 78:14; Numbers 9:15; Exodus 13:21"> For the cloude of the Lord was vpon the Tabernacle by day, and fire was in it by night in the sight of all the house of Israel, throughout all their iourneyes.</span>
