<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–7; Exodus 20:24–26; Exodus 40:10"> Moreouer thou shalt make the altar of Shittim wood, fiue cubites long and fiue cubites broade (the altar shall be foure square) and the height thereof three cubites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:27; Exodus 29:12; Leviticus 4:7"> And thou shalt make it hornes in the foure corners thereof: the hornes shalbe of it selfe, and thou shalt couer it with brasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 7:45; Numbers 4:14; 1 Chronicles 28:17"> Also thou shalt make his ashpannes for his ashes and his besoms, and his basens, and his flesh-hookes, and his censers: thou shalt make all the instruments thereof of brasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 35:16; Exodus 25:12; Exodus 38:4–5"> And thou shalt make vnto it a grate like networke of brasse: also vpon that grate shalt thou make foure brasen rings vpon the foure corners thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 38:4"> And thou shalt put it vnder the compasse of the altar beneath, that the grate may be in the middes of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:13–15; Numbers 4:44; Exodus 30:4"> Also thou shalt make barres for the altar, barres, I say, of Shittim wood, and shalt couer them with brasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:4; Numbers 4:13–14"> And the barres thereof shalbe put in the rings, the which barres shalbe vpon the two sides of the altar to beare it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 25:9; Hebrews 8:5"> Thou shalt make the altar holowe betweene the boardes: as God shewed thee in the mount, so shall they make it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:9–20; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 42:3"> Also thou shalt make the court of the Tabernacle in the Southside, euen full South: the court shall haue curtaines of fine twined linnen, of an hundreth cubites long, for one side,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 26:19–21; Jeremiah 52:21; Exodus 36:38"> And it shall haue twentie pillars, with their twentie sockets of brasse: the heades of the pillars, and their filets shalbe siluer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Likewise on the Northside in length there shalbe hangings of an hundreth cubites long, and the twentie pillars thereof with their twentie sockets of brasse: the heades of the pillars and the filets shalbe siluer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the breadth of the court on the Westside shall haue curtaines of fiftie cubites, with their ten pillars and their ten sockets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the breadth of the court, Eastwarde full East shall haue fiftie cubites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Exodus 26:36"> Also hangings of fifteene cubites shalbe on the one side with their three pillars and their three sockets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Likewise on the other side shalbe hangings of fifteene cubites, with their three pillars and their three sockets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 26:36; Judges 5:30; Exodus 28:39"> And in the gate of the court shalbe a vaile of twentie cubites, of blewe silke, and purple, and skarlet, and fine twined linen wrought with needle, with the foure pillars thereof and their foure sockets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> All the pillars of the court shall haue filets of siluer round about, with their heads of siluer, and their sockets of brasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The length of the court shalbe an hundreth cubites, and the breadth fiftie at either ende, and the height fiue cubites, and the hangings of fine twined linen, and their sockets of brasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Isaiah 22:23–25; Numbers 4:32"> Al the vessels of the Tabernacle for al maner seruice thereof, and all the pinnes thereof, and all the pinnes of the court shalbe brasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 24:1–4; Zechariah 4:11–14; Psalms 23:5"> And thou shalt commande the children of Israel, that they bring vnto thee pure oyle oliue beaten, for the light, that the lampes may alway burne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 19:21; Numbers 18:23; 1 Samuel 3:3"> In the Tabernacle of the Congregation without the vaile, which is before the Testimony, shall Aaron and his sonnes dresse them from euening to morning before the Lord, for a statute for euer vnto their generations, to be obserued by the children of Israel.</span>
