<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; 1 Chronicles 23:12–14; Exodus 6:16–20"> Then there went a man of the house of Leui, and tooke to wife a daughter of Leui,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:23; Acts 7:20; Psalms 112:5"> And the woman coceiued and bare a sonne: and when she saw that he was faire, she hid him three moneths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Acts 7:19; Genesis 6:14"> But when she could no longer hide him, she tooke for him an arke made of reede, and daubed it with slime and with pitch, and laide the childe therein, and put it among the bulrushes by the riuers brinke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; Exodus 15:20; Micah 6:4"> Now his sister stood a farre off, to wit what would come of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 7:21; Psalms 12:5; Psalms 9:9"> Then ye daughter of Pharaoh came downe to wash her in the riuer, and her maidens walked by the riuers side: and when shee sawe the arke among the bulrushes, she sent her maide to fet it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 8:50; 1 Peter 3:8; Acts 7:21"> Then she opened it, and sawe it was a childe: and beholde, the babe wept: so she had compassion on it, and sayde, This is one of the Ebrewes children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 2:4; Numbers 26:59; Numbers 12:1"> Then said his sister vnto Pharaohs daughter, Shall I go and cal vnto thee a nurce of the Ebrew women to nurce thee the childe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3–4; Psalms 27:10; Exodus 6:20"> And Pharaohs daughter sayde to her, Goe. So the maide went and called the childes mother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> To whome Pharaohs daughter sayde, Take this childe away, and nurce it for me, and I wil reward thee. Then the woman tooke the childe and nurced him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24; Genesis 16:11; 1 Samuel 1:20"> Nowe the childe grewe, and she brought him vnto Pharaohs daughter, and he was as her sonne, and she called his name Moses, because, said she, I drewe him out of the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24–26; Acts 7:22–24; Exodus 1:11"> And in those dayes, when Moses was growen, he went forth vnto his brethren, and looked on their burdens: also he sawe an Egyptian smiting an Ebrewe one of his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 7:24–26"> And he looked rounde about, and when he sawe no man, hee slewe the Egyptian, and hid him in the sande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:26–28; 1 Corinthians 6:7–8"> Againe he came forth the second day, and behold, two Ebrewes stroue: and he said vnto him that did the wrong, Wherefore smitest thou thy fellowe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 12:14; Matthew 21:23; Genesis 19:9"> And hee answered, Who made thee a man of authoritie, and a iudge ouer vs? Thinkest thou to kill mee, as thou killedst the Egyptian? Then Moses feared and sayde, Certainly this thing is knowen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 7:29; Hebrews 11:27; Genesis 24:11"> Now Pharaoh heard this matter, and sought to slay Moses: therefore Moses fled from Pharaoh, and dwelt in the lande of Midian, and hee sate downe by a well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 24:11; Exodus 3:1; 1 Samuel 9:11"> And the Priest of Midian had seue daughters, which came and drewe water, and filled the troghes, for to water their fathers sheepe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 29:10; Genesis 21:25; Genesis 26:15–22"> Then the shepherds came and droue them away: but Moses rose vp and defended them, and watered their sheepe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Numbers 10:29; Exodus 4:18"> And whe they came to Reuel their father, he said, Howe are ye come so soone to day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:11; Genesis 29:10"> And they saide, A man of Egypt deliuered vs from the hand of the shepherdes, and also drew vs water ynough, and watered the sheepe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Genesis 43:25; Hebrews 13:2"> Then he saide vnto his daughters, And where is he? why haue ye so left the man? call him that he may eate bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 31:38–40; Hebrews 13:5; Hebrews 11:25"> And Moses agreed to dwell with the man: who gaue vnto Moses Zipporah his daughter:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13–14; Acts 7:29; Exodus 22:21"> And she bare a sonne, whose name he called Gershom: for he said, I haue bene a stranger in a strange lande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 18:20–21; James 5:4; Exodus 3:7–9"> Then in processe of time, the King of Egypt dyed, and the children of Israel sighed for the bondage and cryed: and their crie for the bondage came vp vnto God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 26:3; Psalms 105:42; Exodus 6:5"> Then God heard their mone, and God remembred his couenant with Abraham, Izhak, and Iaakob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; Psalms 55:22; Exodus 3:7–8"> So God looked vpon the children of Israel, and God had respect vnto them.</span>
