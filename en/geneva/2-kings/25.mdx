<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:4–11; 1 Chronicles 6:15; Jeremiah 34:1–6"> And in the ninth yeere of his reigne, the tenth moneth and tenth day of the moneth Nebuchad-nezzar King of Babel came, he, and all his hoste against Ierusalem, and pitched against it, and they built fortes against it round about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> So the citie was besieged vnto the eleueth yeere of King Zedekiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:6; Ezekiel 5:12; Jeremiah 38:2"> And the ninth day of the moneth the famine was sore in the citie, so that there was no bread for the people of the lande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:21; 2 Kings 25:5; Jeremiah 39:2–7"> Then the citie was broken vp, and all the men of warre fled by night, by the way of the gate, which is betweene two walles that was by the Kings garden: nowe the Caldees were by the citie round about: and the King went by the way of the wildernesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 30:16; Jeremiah 39:5; Amos 2:14–16"> But the armie of the Caldees pursued after the King, and tooke him in the desertes of Iericho, and all his hoste was scattered from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33; Jeremiah 34:21–22; Jeremiah 21:7"> Then they tooke the King, and caried him vp to the King of Babel to Riblah, where they gaue iudgement vpon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:4–5; Judges 16:21; Jeremiah 39:6–7"> And they slew the sonnes of Zedekiah before his eyes, and put out the eyes of Zedekiah, and bounde him in chaines, and caried him to Babel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:12–16; Jeremiah 40:1–4; Lamentations 4:12"> And in the fift moneth, and seuenth day of the moneth, which was the nineteenth yere of King Nebuchad-nezzar King of Babel, came Nebuzar-adan chiefe stewarde and seruaunt of the King of Babel, to Ierusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 2:5; Psalms 74:3–7; 1 Kings 9:8"> And burnt the house of the Lord, and the Kings house, and all the houses of Ierusalem, and all the great houses burnt he with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:3; Jeremiah 39:8; Jeremiah 52:14–23"> And all the armie of the Caldees that were with the chiefe stewarde, brake downe the walles of Ierusalem round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:2; Jeremiah 15:1–2; Jeremiah 39:9"> And the rest of the people that were left in the citie, and those that were fled and fallen to the King of Babel, with the remnant of the multitude, did Nebuzar-adan chiefe steward carie away captiue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:7; 2 Kings 24:14; Ezekiel 33:24"> But the chiefe steward left of the poore of the land to dresse the vines, and to till the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:18; Lamentations 1:10; 2 Kings 20:17"> Also the pillars of brasse that were in the house of the Lord, and the bases, and the brasen Sea that was in the house of the Lord, did the Caldees breake, and caried the brasse of them to Babel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:3; 1 Kings 7:47–50; Exodus 38:3"> The pots also and the besomes, and the instruments of musike, and the incense dishes, and al the vessels of brasse that they ministred in, tooke they away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 5:2–3; Numbers 7:13–14; 1 Kings 7:48–51"> And the asshe pannes, and the basens, and all that was of gold, and that was of siluer, tooke the chiefe steward away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 7:47"> With the two pillars, one Sea and the bases, which Salomon had made for the house of the Lord: the brasse of all these vessels was without weight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:15–22; Jeremiah 52:21–23"> The height of the one pillar was eighteene cubits, and the chapiter thereon was brasse, and the height of the chapiter was with networke three cubites, and pomegranates vpon the chapiter rounde about, all of brasse: and likewise was the second pillar with the networke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:25; Ezra 7:1; Jeremiah 21:1"> And the chiefe steward tooke Seraiah the chiefe Priest, and Zephaniah the second Priest, and the three keepers of the doore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esther 1:14"> And out of the citie hee tooke an Eunuch that had the ouersight of the men of warre, and fiue men of them that were in the Kinges presence, which were founde in the citie, and Sopher captaine of the hoste, who mustred the people of the lande, and threescore men of the people of the lande, that were founde in the citie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 4:16; Jeremiah 52:26–27; 2 Kings 23:33"> And Nebuzar-adan the chiefe stewarde tooke them, and brought them to the King of Babel to Riblah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; 2 Kings 23:27; Deuteronomy 4:26"> And the King of Babel smote them, and slew them at Riblah in the land of Hamath. So Iudah was caried away captiue out of his owne land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:14; 2 Kings 22:12; Jeremiah 41:2"> Howbeit there remained people in the land of Iudah, whom Nebuchad-nezzar King of Babel left, and made Gedaliah the sonne of Ahikam the sonne of Shaphan ruler ouer them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:7–9; Jeremiah 40:11–12; Joshua 18:26"> Then when all the captaines of the host and their men heard, that the king of Babel had made Gedaliah gouernour, they came to Gedaliah to Mizpah, to wit, Ishmael the sonne of Nethaniah, and Iohanan the sonne of Kareah, and Seraiah the sonne of Tanhumeth the Netophathite, and Iaazaniah the sonne of Maachathi, they and their men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:9–10; Jeremiah 43:6; 2 Samuel 19:23"> And Gedaliah sware to them, and to their men, and sayd vnto them, Feare not to be the seruants of the Caldees: dwell in the land, and serue the King of Babel, and ye shalbe well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zechariah 7:5; Zechariah 8:19; Jeremiah 40:15"> But in the seuenth moneth Ishmael the sonne of Nethaniah the sonne of Elishama of the Kings seede, came, and ten men with him, and smote Gedaliah, and he died, and so did he the Iewes, and the Caldees that were with him at Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:4–7; Jeremiah 41:16–18; Jeremiah 42:14–22"> Then all ye people both small and great, and the captaines of the armie arose, and came to Egypt: for they were afraide of the Caldees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:31–34; Genesis 40:20; Genesis 40:13"> Notwithstanding in the seuen and thirtieth yeere after Iehoiachin King of Iudah was caried away, in the twelft moneth and the seuen and twentieth day of the moneth, Euil-merodach King of Babel in the yeere that hee began to reigne, did lift vp the head of Iehoiachin King of Iudah out of the prison,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Daniel 2:37; Jeremiah 27:6–11; Daniel 5:18–19"> And spake kindly to him, and set his throne aboue the throne of the Kings that were with him in Babel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:7; Isaiah 61:3; Genesis 41:42"> And changed his prison garments: and he did continually eate bread before him, all the dayes of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 11:3; Matthew 6:11; Nehemiah 11:23"> And his portion was a continual portion giuen him by the King, euery day a certaine, all the dayes of his life.</span>
