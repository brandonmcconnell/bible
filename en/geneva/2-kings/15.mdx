<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 14:3–4; 2 Kings 12:2–3; 2 Chronicles 26:4"> And he did vprightly in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his father Amaziah did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 15:35; 2 Kings 14:4; 2 Kings 12:3"> But the hie places were not put away: for the people yet offered, and burned incense in the hie places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 13:46; Numbers 12:14; 2 Kings 7:3"> And the Lord smote the King: and he was a leper vnto the day of his death, and dwelt in an house apart, and Iotham the kings sonne gouerned the house, and iudged the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:5–15; 2 Kings 14:18"> Concerning the rest of the actes of Azariah, and all that he did, are they not written in the booke of the Chronicles of the Kings of Iudah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:23; Isaiah 6:1"> So Azariah slept with his fathers, and they buryed him with his fathers in the citie of Dauid, and Iotham his sonne reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 14:16–17; 2 Kings 14:29–1; 2 Kings 14:21"> In the eyght and thirtieth yeere of Azariah King of Iudah did Zachariah the sonne of Ieroboam reigne ouer Israel in Samaria sixe moneths,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 14:24; 2 Kings 13:2; 2 Kings 13:11"> And did euill in the sight of the Lord, as did his fathers: for he departed not from the sinnes of Ieroboam the sonne of Nebat, which made Israel to sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 15:28; 2 Kings 15:14; 2 Kings 9:24"> And Shallum the sonne of Iabesh cospired against him, and smote him in the sight of the people, and killed him, and reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 14:28"> Concerning the rest of the actes of Zachariah, behold, they are written in the booke of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 10:30; Mark 13:31; 2 Kings 13:13"> This was the word of the Lord, which he spake vnto Iehu, saying, Thy sonnes shall sit on the throne of Israel vnto the fourth generation after thee. And it came so to passe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 15:1; Matthew 1:8–9; 2 Kings 15:8"> Shallum the sonne of Iabesh began to reigne in the nine and thirtieth yeere of Vzziah King of Iudah: and he reigned the space of a moneth in Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 14:17; 1 Kings 16:17; 1 Kings 16:15"> For Menahem the sonne of Gadi went vp from Tirzah, and came to Samaria, and smote Shallum the sonne of Iabesh in Samaria, and slew him, and reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 14:19; 1 Kings 14:29; 2 Kings 15:11"> Cocerning the rest of the acts of Shallum, and the treason which he wrought, beholde, they are written in the booke of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 1 Kings 4:24; Hosea 13:16"> Then Menahem destroyed Tiphsah, and all that were therein, and the coastes thereof from Tirzah, because they opened not to him, and he smote it, and ript vp all their women with childe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The nine and thirtieth yeere of Azariah King of Iudah, beganne Menahem the sonne of Gadi to reigne ouer Israel, and reigned ten yeeres in Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he did euill in the sight of the Lord, and departed not all his dayes from the sinne of Ieroboam the sonne of Nebat, which made Israel to sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 14:5; 1 Chronicles 5:25–26; 2 Kings 18:16"> Then Pul the king of Asshur came against the land: and Menahem gaue Pul a thousand talents of siluer, that his hande might bee with him, and establish the kingdome in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 18:14–17; 2 Kings 23:35; 2 Kings 17:3–4"> And Menahem exacted the money in Israel, that all men of substance shoulde giue the King of Asshur fiftie shekels of siluer a piece: so the king of Asshur returned and taried not there in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Concerning the rest of the actes of Menahem, and all that he did, are they not written in the booke of the Chronicles of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Menahem slept with his fathers, and Pekahiah his sonne did reigne in his steade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 15:25; 1 Kings 22:51; Job 20:5"> In ye fiftieth yere of Azariah king of Iudah, began Pekahiah the sonne of Menahem to reigne ouer Israel in Samaria, and reigned two yere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 15:9; 2 Kings 15:18"> And he did euill in the sight of the Lord: for he departed not from the sinnes of Ieroboam the sonne of Nebat, which made Israel to sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:6; 2 Kings 9:14; 1 Kings 16:9"> And Pekah the sonne of Remaliah, his captaine conspired against him, and smote him in Samaria in the place of the Kings palace with Argob and Arieh, and with him fiftie men of the Gileadites: so he killed him, and reigned in his steade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Concerning the rest of the actes of Pekahiah, and all that he did, behold, they are written in the booke of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 7:4; Isaiah 7:1; 2 Kings 15:23"> In the two and fiftieth yere of Azariah King of Iudah began Pekah the sonne of Remaliah to reigne ouer Israel in Samaria, and reigned twentie yeere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 15:18; 2 Kings 13:6; 2 Kings 15:9"> And he did euil in the sight of the Lord: for he departed not from the sinnes of Ieroboam the sonne of Nebat, that made Israel to sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; 2 Kings 17:6; 2 Kings 16:7"> In the dayes of Pekah king of Israel came Tiglath Pileser king of Asshur, and tooke Iion, and Abel, Beth-maachah, and Ianoah, and Kedesh, and Hazor, and Gilead, and Galilah, and all the land of Naphtali, and caryed them away to Asshur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 17:1; 2 Chronicles 28:4–6; 2 Kings 16:1"> And Hoshea the sonne of Elah wrought treason against Pekah the sonne of Remaliah, and smote him, and slewe him, and reigned in his stead in the twentieth yeere of Iotham the sonne of Vzziah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Concerning the rest of the actes of Pekah, and all that he did, behold, they are written in the booke of the Chronicles of the kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:12; 2 Kings 15:7; 2 Chronicles 27:1–9"> In the second yere of Pekah the sonne of Remaliah King of Israel, began Iotham sonne of Vzziah King of Iudah to reigne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 27:1"> Fiue and twentie yeere olde was he, when he began to reigne, and he reigned sixteene yeere in Ierusalem: and his mothers name was Ierusha the daughter of Zadok.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:4–5; 2 Kings 15:3–4; 2 Chronicles 27:2"> And hee did vprightly in the sight of the Lord: he did according to all that his father Vzziah had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 12:3; 2 Chronicles 23:20; 2 Chronicles 27:3–9"> But the hie places were not put away: for the people yet offered and burnt incense in the hie places: he buylt the hyest gate of the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Chronicles 27:4–9; 2 Kings 15:6–7"> Concerning the rest of the acts of Iotham, and all that hee did, are they not written in the booke of the Chronicles of the kings of Iudah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Kings 16:5; Isaiah 7:1; Luke 21:28"> In those dayes the Lord began to send against Iudah Rezin the king of Aram, and Pekah the sonne of Remaliah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 14:20; 1 Kings 14:31; 2 Kings 16:1"> And Iotham slept with his fathers, and was buryed with his fathers in the citie of Dauid his father, and Ahaz his sonne reigned in his steade.</span>
