<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:1; 2 Kings 4:29; 1 Kings 1:39"> Then Elisha the Prophet called one of ye children of the Prophets, and sayde vnto him, Gird thy loynes and take this boxe of oyle in thine hand and get thee to Ramoth Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 9:5; 1 Kings 19:16–17; 2 Kings 9:11"> And when thou commest thither, looke where is Iehu ye sonne of Iehoshaphat, the sonne of Nimshi, and go, and make him arise vp from among his brethren, and leade him to a secret chamber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:16; Exodus 29:7; 1 Samuel 16:2"> Then take the boxe of oyle and powre it on his head, and say, Thus sayth the Lord, I haue anointed thee for King ouer Israel. then open the doore, and flee without any tarying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> So the seruat of ye Prophet gate him to Ramoth Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 3:19"> And when he came in, behold, the captaines of the armie were sitting. And he sayde, I haue a message to thee, O captaine. And Iehu sayd, Vnto which of all vs? And he answered, To thee, O captaine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:7; 1 Kings 19:16; 2 Kings 9:3"> And he arose, and went into the house, and he powred the oyle on his head and sayde vnto him, Thus sayth the Lord God of Israel, I haue anointed thee for King ouer the people of the Lord, euen ouer Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 21:15; 1 Kings 18:4; Deuteronomy 32:43"> And thou shalt smite the house of Ahab thy master, that I may auenge the blood of my seruants the Prophets, and the blood of al the seruants of the Lord of the hand of Iezebel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:22; Deuteronomy 32:36; 2 Kings 14:26"> For the whole house of Ahab shalbe destroied: and I will cut off from Ahab, him that maketh water against the wall, as well him that is shut vp, as him that is left in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 15:29; 1 Kings 21:22; 1 Kings 14:10–11"> And I will make the house of Ahab like the house of Ieroboam the sonne of Nebat, and like the house of Baasha the sonne of Ahiiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 21:23; 2 Kings 9:35–36; 2 Kings 9:3"> And the dogges shall eate Iezebel in the fielde of Izreel, and there shalbe none to burie her. And he opened the doore, and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:26; Acts 26:24; Hosea 9:7"> Then Iehu came out to the seruants of his lord. And one sayd vnto him, Is all well? wherefore came this mad fellowe to thee? And hee sayde vnto them, Ye knowe the man, and what his talke was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And they sayde, It is false, tell vs it nowe. Then he sayd, Thus and thus spake he to me, saying, Thus saieth the Lord, I haue anointed thee for King ouer Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 11:7–8; 1 Kings 1:39; 1 Kings 1:34"> Then they made haste, and tooke euerie man his garment, and put it vnder him on the top of the staires, and blewe the trumpet, saying, Iehu is King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 8:28; 1 Kings 22:3; 1 Kings 16:16"> So Iehu ye sonne of Iehoshaphat the sonne of Nimshi conspired against Ioram: (Now Ioram kept Ramoth Gilead, he and all Israel, because of Hazael King of Aram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 8:29; 1 Samuel 27:9–11; 2 Chronicles 22:6"> And King Ioram returned to bee healed in Izreel of the woundes, which the Aramites had giuen him, when hee fought with Hazael King of Aram) and Iehu sayde, If it be your mindes, let no man depart and escape out of the citie, to goe and tell in Izreel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:6–7; 2 Kings 8:28–29"> So Iehu gate vp into a charet, and went to Izreel: for Ioram lay there, and Ahaziah King of Iudah was come downe to see Ioram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:4; Ezekiel 33:2–9; 2 Kings 7:14"> And the watchman that stoode in the towre in Izreel spyed the companie of Iehu as hee came, and sayd, I see a companie. And Iehoram said, Take a horseman and send to meete them, that hee may say, Is it peace?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 9:19; 2 Kings 9:22; Jeremiah 16:5"> So there went one on horseback to meete him, and sayde, Thus sayth the King, Is it peace? And Iehu sayd, What hast thou to do with peace? Turne behinde me. And the watchman tolde, saying, The messenger came to them, but he commeth not againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then hee sent out another on horsebacke, which came to them, and sayde, Thus sayth the King, Is it peace? And Iehu answered, What hast thou to doe with peace? turne behinde me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:27; 1 Kings 19:17; Ecclesiastes 9:10"> And the watchman tolde, saying, He came to them also, but commeth not againe, and the marching is like the marching of Iehu the sonne of Nimshi: for he marcheth furiously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 21:1–7; 2 Chronicles 22:7; Micah 1:13"> Then Iehoram sayd, Make ready: and his charet was made ready. And Iehoram King of Israel and Ahaziah King of Iudah went out eyther of them in his charet against Iehu, and met him in the fielde of Naboth the Izreelite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:13; Nahum 3:4; 1 Kings 16:30–33"> And when Iehoram sawe Iehu, he sayde, Is it peace, Iehu? And he answered, What peace? whiles the whoredomes of thy mother Iezebel, and her witchcraftes are yet in great nomber?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 11:14; 2 Chronicles 23:13"> Then Iehoram turned his hand, and fled, and said to Ahaziah, O Ahaziah, there is treason.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 22:34; Proverbs 21:30; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> But Iehu tooke a bowe in his hande, and smote Iehoram betweene the shoulders, that the arowe went through his heart: and he fell downe in his charet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; 1 Kings 21:24–29; Isaiah 13:1"> Then said Iehu to Bidkar a captaine, Take, and cast him in some place of the fielde of Naboth the Izreelite: for I remember that when I and thou rode together after Ahab his father, the Lord layed this burden vpon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; Deuteronomy 5:9; 2 Chronicles 25:4"> Surely I haue seene yesterday the blood of Naboth, and the blood of his sonnes, saide the Lord, and I will render it thee in this fielde, saith the Lord: nowe therefore take and cast him in the fielde, according to the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 1:27; Joshua 17:11; 2 Kings 8:29"> But when Ahaziah the King of Iudah saw this, he fled by the way of the garden house: And Iehu pursued after him, and sayd, Smite him also in the charet: and they smote him in the going vp to Gur, which is by Ibleam. And hee fled to Megiddo, and there dyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:24; 2 Kings 23:30; 2 Kings 12:21"> And his seruants caried him in a charet to Ierusalem, and buried him in his sepulchre with his fathers in the citie of Dauid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:18–19; 2 Chronicles 22:1–2; 2 Kings 8:24–25"> And in the eleuenth yere of Ioram the sonne of Ahab, began Ahaziah to reigne ouer Iudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:30; Ezekiel 23:40; Ezekiel 24:17"> And when Iehu was come to Izreel, Iezebel heard of it, and painted her face, and tired her head, and looked out at a windowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 16:9–20"> And as Iehu entred at the gate, shee sayde, Had Zimri peace, which slewe his master?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Esther 2:15; Exodus 32:26; 2 Chronicles 11:12"> And he lift vp his eyes to the windowe, and sayd, Who is on my side, who? Then two or three of her Eunuches looked vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Micah 7:10; Lamentations 1:15; Isaiah 25:10"> And he sayde, Cast her downe: and they cast her downe, and hee sprinkled of her blood vpon the wall, and vpon the horses, and he trode her vnder foote.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 16:31; 1 Kings 21:25; Isaiah 65:15"> And when he was come in, he did eate and drinke, and sayde, Visite now yonder cursed woman, and burie her: for she is a Kings daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 12:23; Isaiah 14:18–20; Job 31:3"> And they went to burie her, but they foud no more of her, then the skull and the feete, and the palmes of her handes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 21:23; Leviticus 8:36; 2 Kings 14:25"> Wherefore they came agayne and tolde him. And he said, This is the worde of the Lord, which he spake by his seruant Eliiah the Tishbite, saying, In the fielde of Izreel shall ye dogs eate the flesh of Iezebel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:2; Psalms 83:10; Jeremiah 16:4"> And the carkeis of Iezebel shalbe as doung vpon the ground in the field of Izreel, so that none shall say, This is Iezebel.</span>
