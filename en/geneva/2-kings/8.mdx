<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Haggai 1:11; 2 Kings 4:31–35; Ruth 1:1"> Then spake Elisha vnto the woman, whose sonne he had restored to life, saying, Vp, and goe, thou, and thine house, and soiourne where thou canst soiourne: for the Lord hath called for a famine, and it commeth also vpon the land seuen yeeres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; Judges 3:3; 1 Samuel 27:1–3"> And the woman arose, and did after the saying of the man of God, and went both shee and her housholde and soiourned in the lande of the Philistims seuen yeeres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:16; Luke 18:3–5; 2 Kings 4:13"> And at the seuen yeeres ende, the woman returned out of the lande of the Philistims, and went out to call vpon the King for her house and for her land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 5:20–27; 2 Kings 2:14; 2 Kings 6:32"> And the King talked with Gehazi the seruant of the man of God, saying, Tell mee, I pray thee, all the great actes, that Elisha hath done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 4:35; Matthew 10:29–30; 2 Kings 6:26"> And as he told the King, howe he had restored one dead to life, behold, the woman, whose sonne he had raised to life, called vpon the King for her house and for her land. Then Gehazi sayd, My lorde, O King, this is the woman, and this is her sonne, whom Elisha restored to life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Proverbs 21:1; 2 Samuel 9:7"> And when the King asked the woman, shee told him: so the King appoynted her an Eunuch, saying, Restore thou all that are hers, and all the fruites of her landes since the day shee left the land, euen vntill this time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 11:24; 2 Kings 6:24; 1 Kings 20:1"> Then Elisha came to Damascus, and Ben-hadad the King of Aram was sicke, and one told him, saying, The man of God is come hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 19:15; 2 Kings 1:2; 1 Samuel 9:7"> And the king sayd vnto Hazael, Take a present in thine hande, and goe meete the man of God, that thou mayest inquire of ye Lord by him, saying, Shall I recouer of this disease?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 5:13; 2 Kings 13:14; 1 Kings 19:15"> So Hazael went to meete him, and tooke the present in his hand, and of euery good thing of Damascus, euen the burden of fourtie camels, and came and stood before him, and sayde, Thy sonne Ben-hadad King of Aram hath sent me to thee, saying, Shall I recouer of this disease?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 7:7; Genesis 2:17; Amos 7:1"> And Elisha sayd to him, Goe, and say vnto him, Thou shalt recouer: howbeit the Lord hath shewed me, that he shall surely dye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Romans 9:2; Jeremiah 9:18"> And hee looked vpon him stedfastly, till Hazael was ashamed, and the man of God wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; Nahum 3:10; 2 Kings 15:16"> And Hazael sayde, Why weepeth my lord? And he answered, Because I knowe the euill that thou shalt do vnto the children of Israel: for their strong cities shalt thou set on fire, and their yong men shalt thou slay with the sworde, and shalt dash their infantes against the stones, and rent in pieces their women with child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:43; 1 Kings 19:15; 2 Samuel 9:8"> Then Hazael said, What? is thy seruant a dog, that I should doe this great thing? And Elisha answered, The Lord hath shewed mee, that thou shalt be King of Aram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 8:10; Matthew 26:16; 2 Kings 5:25"> So he departed from Elisha, and came to his master, who said to him, What saide Elisha to thee? And he answered, Hee tolde mee that thou shouldest recouer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 8:13; 1 Kings 15:28; 1 Kings 11:26–37"> And on the morow he tooke a thick cloth and dipt in it water, and spread it on his face, and hee dyed: and Hazael reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 1:17; 2 Kings 3:1; 2 Chronicles 21:1–20"> Now in the fift yere of Ioram ye sonne of Ahab King of Israel, and of Iehoshaphat King of Iudah, Iehoram the sonne of Iehoshaphat King of Iudah began to reigne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:5–10"> He was two and thirtie yere olde, when he began to reigne: and hee reigned eight yeere in Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 8:26; 1 Kings 11:1–5; 2 Chronicles 21:13"> And hee walked in the wayes of the Kings of Israel, as did the house of Ahab: for the daughter of Ahab was his wife, and hee did euill in the sight of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 11:36; 2 Samuel 7:12–13; Luke 1:32–33"> Yet the Lord would not destroy Iudah, for Dauid his seruants sake, as he had promised him to giue him a light, and to his children for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 22:47; 2 Kings 3:9; 2 Kings 8:22"> In those dayes Edom rebelled from vnder the hand of Iudah, and made a King ouer themselues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:17"> Therefore Ioram went to Zair, and all his charets with him, and he arose by night, and smote the Edomites which were about him with the captains of the charets, and the people fled into their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 19:8; Joshua 21:13; Genesis 27:40"> So Edom rebelled from vnder the hand of Iudah vnto this day. then Libnah rebelled at that same time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 15:36; 1 Kings 11:41; 2 Chronicles 21:11–20"> Concerning the rest of the actes of Ioram and all that hee did, are they not written in the booke of the Chronicles of the Kings of Iudah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:6; 2 Chronicles 25:23; 2 Chronicles 21:1"> And Ioram slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the citie of Dauid. And Ahaziah his sonne reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 9:29; 2 Kings 8:16–17; 2 Chronicles 21:20–6"> In the twelft yere of Ioram the sonne of Ahab King of Israel did Ahaziah the sonne of Iehoram King of Iudah begin to reigne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:2; 2 Kings 9:21–27; 2 Kings 11:1"> Two and twentie yeere olde was Ahaziah when he began to reigne, and he reigned one yere in Ierusalem, and his mothers name was Athaliah the daughter of Omri King of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:3–4; 2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> And he walked in the way of the house of Ahab, and did euill in the sight of the Lord, like the house of Ahab: for hee was the sonne in law of the house of Ahab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:5; 1 Kings 4:13; 2 Chronicles 19:2"> And he went with Ioram the sonne of Ahab to warre against Hazael King of Aram in Ramoth Gilead, and the Aramites smote Ioram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 9:15–16; 2 Kings 8:28; 2 Chronicles 22:5–7"> And King Ioram returned to bee healed in Izreel of the wounds which ye Aramites had giuen him at Ramah, whe he fought against Hazael King of Aram. And Ahaziah the sonne of Iehoram King of Iudah went downe to see Ioram the sonne of Ahab in Izreel, because he was sicke.</span>
