<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 21:27; 2 Kings 18:37; 2 Chronicles 32:20–22"> And when King Hezekiah heard it, he rent his clothes and put on sackecloth, and came into the house of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; Isaiah 2:1; Matthew 4:14"> And sent Eliakim which was the stewarde of the house, and Shebnah the chanceller, and the Elders of the Priestes clothed in sackecloth to Isaiah the Prophet the sonne of Amoz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:5–7; Isaiah 66:9; Hosea 13:13"> And they said vnto him, Thus saith Hezekiah, This day is a day of tribulation and of rebuke, and blasphemie: for the childre are come to the birth, and there is no strength to bring foorth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Romans 9:27; Joshua 14:12"> If so be the Lord thy God hath heard all the wordes of Rabshakeh, whome the King of Asshur his master hath sent to raile on the liuing God, and to reproch him with wordes which the Lord thy God hath heard, then lift thou vp thy prayer for the remnant that are left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So the seruants of King Hezekiah came to Isaiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 18:35; 2 Kings 18:17; Isaiah 37:6–38"> And Isaiah said vnto them, So shall ye say to your master, Thus sayeth the Lord, Be not afraide of the words which thou hast heard, wherewith the seruants of the king of Asshur haue blasphemed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Obadiah 1:1; 2 Kings 7:6; Psalms 18:14–15"> Beholde, I will sende a blast vpon him, and he shall heare a noyse, and returne to his owne lande: and I will cause him to fall by the sworde in his owne lande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 18:14; Joshua 10:29; Joshua 12:11"> So Rabshakeh returned, and founde the King of Asshur fighting against Libnah: for he had heard that he was departed from Lachish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:27; Isaiah 37:9; 2 Kings 18:17"> He heard also men say of Tirhakah King of Ethiopia, Beholde, he is come out to fight against thee: he therefore departed and sent other messengers vnto Hezekiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 18:5; 2 Kings 18:29–30; 2 Chronicles 32:15–19"> Thus shall ye speake to Hezekiah King of Iudah, and say, Let not thy God deceiue thee in whome thou trustest, saying, Ierusalem shall not be deliuered into the hande of the King of Asshur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 10:8–11; 2 Chronicles 32:13–14; 2 Kings 19:17–18"> Beholde, thou hast heard what the Kings of Asshur haue done to all landes, how they haue destroyed them: and shalt thou be deliuered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 17:6; Genesis 11:31; Ezekiel 27:23"> Haue the gods of the heathen deliuered them which my fathers haue destroyed? as Gozan, and Haran, and Rezeph, and the children of Eden, which were in Thelasar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 18:34; 2 Kings 17:24; Isaiah 11:11"> Where is the King of Hamath, and the King of Arpad, and the King of the citie of Shepharuaim, Hena and Iuah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 37:14; Psalms 91:1–2; Psalms 123:1–4"> So Hezekiah receiued the letter of the hande of the messengers, and read it: and Hezekiah went vp into the house of the Lord, and Hezekiah spread it before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; 2 Kings 5:15; Daniel 4:34–35"> And Hezekiah prayed before the Lord, and saide, O Lord God of Israel, which dwellest betweene the Cherubims, thou art very God alone ouer all the kingdomes of the earth: thou hast made the heauen and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:40; Psalms 31:2; 2 Kings 19:4"> Lord, bow downe thine eare, and heare: Lord open thine eyes and behold, and heare the wordes of Saneherib, who hath sent to blaspheme the liuing God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; Job 9:2; 1 Corinthians 14:25"> Trueth it is, Lord, that the Kings of Asshur haue destroyed the nations and their landes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 17:29; Isaiah 44:9–20; Jeremiah 10:3–9"> And haue set fire on their gods: for they were no gods, but the worke of mans hands, euen wood and stone: therefore they destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; 1 Kings 8:43; Psalms 67:1–2"> Nowe therefore, O Lord our God, I beseech thee, saue thou vs out of his hande, that all the kingdomes of the earth may knowe, that thou, O Lord, art onely God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 20:5; 2 Chronicles 32:20–21; Jeremiah 33:3"> Then Isaiah the sonne of Amoz sent to Hezekiah, saying, Thus saith the Lord God of Israel, I haue heard that which thou hast prayed me, concerning Saneherib King of Asshur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Lamentations 2:13; Job 16:4; Jeremiah 14:17"> This is the worde that the Lord hath spoken against him, O Virgine, daughter of Zion, he hath despised thee, and laughed thee to scorne: O daughter of Ierusalem, he hath shaken his head at thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Psalms 71:22; Isaiah 30:11–12"> Whome hast thou railed on? and whome hast thou blasphemed? and against whome hast thou exalted thy voyce, and lifted vp thine eyes on hie? euen against the Holie one of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Psalms 20:7; Isaiah 10:18"> By thy messengers thou hast rayled on the Lord, and said, By the multitude of my charets I am come vp to the toppe of the mountaines, by the sides of Lebanon, and will cut downe the hie cedars thereof, and the faire firre trees thereof, and I will goe into the lodging of his borders, and into the forest of his Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 19:6; Exodus 15:9; 2 Samuel 17:13"> I haue digged, and drunke the waters of others, and with the plant of my feete haue I dried all the floods closed in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Isaiah 10:5–6; Isaiah 37:26–27"> Hast thou not heard, howe I haue of olde time made it, and haue formed it long ago? and should I nowe bring it, that it should be destroyed, and laid on ruinous heapes, as cities defensed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Psalms 102:11; Jeremiah 51:32"> Whose inhabitants haue small power, and are afraid, and confounded: they are like the grasse of the field, and greene herbe, or grasse on ye house toppes, or as corne blasted before it be growen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 37:28–29; Jeremiah 23:23–24; Psalms 139:1–11"> I knowe thy dwelling, yea, thy going out, and thy comming in, and thy furie against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:4; Ezekiel 38:4; 2 Kings 19:33"> And because thou ragest against me, and thy tumult is come vp to mine eares, I will put mine hooke in thy nostrels, and my bridle in thy lippes, and will bring thee backe againe the same way thou camest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 25:20–22; 2 Kings 20:8–9; Exodus 3:12"> And this shalbe a signe vnto thee, O Hezekiah, Thou shalt eate this yeere such things as growe of them selues, and the next yeere such as growe without sowing, and the third yeere sowe ye and reape, and plant vineyardes, and eate the fruites thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 19:4; Isaiah 27:6; 2 Chronicles 32:22–23"> And the remnant that is escaped of the house of Iudah, shall againe take roote downewarde, and beare fruite vpwarde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 9:7; Romans 11:5; Romans 9:27"> For out of Ierusalem shall goe a remnant, and some that shall escape out of mount Zion: the zeale of the Lord of hostes shall doe this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–10; 2 Samuel 20:15; Isaiah 37:33–35"> Wherefore thus saith the Lord, concerning the King of Asshur, He shall not enter into this citie, nor shoote an arrowe there, nor come before it with shield, nor cast a mount against it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; 2 Kings 19:36"> But he shall returne the way he came, and shall not come into this citie, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 11:12–13; 2 Kings 20:6; 1 Kings 15:4"> For I will defende this citie to saue it for mine owne sake, and for Dauid my seruants sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:21–22; 2 Samuel 24:16; Exodus 12:29–30"> And the same night the Angell of the Lord went out and smote in the campe of Asshur an hundreth foure score and fiue thousande: so when they rose earely in the morning, behold, they were all dead corpses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jonah 1:2; 2 Kings 19:7; 2 Kings 19:33"> So Saneherib King of Asshur departed, and went his way, and returned, and dwelt in Nineueh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Ezra 4:2; Genesis 8:4; 2 Kings 19:7"> And as he was in the Temple worshipping Nisroch his god, Adramelech and Sharezer his sonnes slewe him with the sworde: and they escaped into the land of Ararat, and Esarhaddon his sonne reigned in his steade.</span>
