<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 13:10; 2 Chronicles 25:1–4; 2 Kings 14:15"> The second yeere of Ioash sonne of Iehoahaz King of Israel reigned Amaziah the sonne of Ioash King of Iudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He was fiue and twentie yeere olde when he began to reigne, and reigned nine and twentie yeere in Ierusalem, and his mothers name was Iehoadan of Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:2–3; Zechariah 1:4–6; 1 Kings 11:4"> And he did vprightly in the sight of the Lord, yet not like Dauid his father, but did according to all that Ioash his father had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 12:3; 2 Kings 16:4; 2 Kings 15:35"> Notwithstanding the hie places were not taken away: for as yet the people did sacrifice and burnt incense in the hie places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:3–4; 2 Kings 12:20–21; Numbers 35:33"> And when the kingdome was confirmed in his hand, he slewe his seruants which had killed the King his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:16; Ezekiel 18:20; Ezekiel 18:4"> But the children of those that did slay him, he slewe not, according vnto that that is written in the booke of the Lawe of Moses, wherein the Lord commanded, saying, The fathers shall not be put to death for the children, nor the children put to death for the fathers: but euery man shall be put to death for his owne sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:13; 1 Chronicles 18:12; Joshua 15:38"> He slew also of Edom in the valley of salt ten thousand, and tooke the citie of Sela by warre, and called the name thereof Ioktheel vnto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:17–24; 2 Samuel 2:14–17; 2 Kings 14:11"> Then Amaziah sent messengers to Iehoash the sonne of Iehoahaz, sonne of Iehu King of Israel, saying, Come, let vs see one another in the face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; 1 Kings 4:33; 2 Chronicles 33:11"> Then Iehoash the King of Israel sent to Amaziah King of Iudah, saying, The thistle that is in Lebanon, sent to the cedar that is in Lebanon, saying, Giue thy daughter to my sonne to wife: and the wilde beast that was in Lebanon, went and trode downe the thistle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:16; Deuteronomy 8:14; 2 Chronicles 32:25"> Because thou hast smitten Edom, thine heart hath made thee proud: bragge of glory, and tary at home. why doest thou prouoke to thine hurt, that thou shouldest fall, and Iudah with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 19:38; 2 Chronicles 25:16; Joshua 15:10"> But Amaziah would not heare: therefore Iehoash King of Israel went vp: and he and Amaziah King of Iudah sawe one another in the face at Beth-shemesh which is in Iudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:17; 1 Samuel 4:10; 1 Kings 22:36"> And Iudah was put to the worse before Israel, and they fledde euery man to their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:16; Nehemiah 12:39; Zechariah 14:10"> But Iehoash King of Israel tooke Amaziah King of Iudah, the sonne of Iehoash the sonne of Ahaziah, at Beth-shemesh, and came to Ierusalem, and brake downe the wall of Ierusalem from the gate of Ephraim to the corner gate, foure hundreth cubites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 14:26; 1 Kings 7:51; 1 Kings 15:18"> And he tooke all the gold and siluer, and all the vessels that were found in the house of the Lord, and in the treasures of the Kings house, and the children that were in hostage, and returned to Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 13:12–13; 1 Kings 14:19–20; 2 Kings 10:34–35"> Concerning the rest of the acts of Iehoash which he did and his valiant deedes, and how he fought with Amaziah King of Iudah, are they not written in the booke of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 13:9; 2 Kings 13:13; 1 Kings 1:21"> And Iehoash slept with his fathers, and was buried at Samaria among the Kings of Israel: and Ieroboam his sonne reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:25–28; 2 Kings 13:10; 2 Kings 14:23"> And Amaziah the sonne of Ioash King of Iudah, liued after the death of Iehoash sonne of Iehoahaz King of Israel fifteene yeere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 11:41; 1 Kings 14:29; 2 Kings 13:8"> Concerning the rest of the actes of Amaziah, are they not written in the booke of the Chronicles of the Kings of Iudah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 10:31; 2 Kings 15:25; 2 Kings 12:20–21"> But they wrought treason against him in Ierusalem, and he fled to Lachish, but they sent after him to Lachish, and slewe him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 9:28; 2 Kings 12:21; 2 Chronicles 33:20"> And they brought him on horses, and he was buried at Ierusalem with his fathers in the citie of Dauid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 21:24; Matthew 1:8–9; 2 Kings 15:13"> Then all the people of Iudah tooke Azariah which was sixteene yeere olde, and made him King for his father Amaziah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 16:6; 1 Kings 9:26; Deuteronomy 2:8"> He built Elath, and restored it to Iudah, after that the King slept with his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hosea 1:1; Amos 7:9–11; Amos 1:1"> In the fifteenth yeere of Amaziah the sonne of Ioash King of Iudah, was Ieroboam the sonne of Ioash made King ouer Israel in Samaria, and reigned one and fourtie yeere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 13:6; 2 Kings 13:11; Genesis 38:7"> And he did euill in the sight of the Lord: for he departed not from all the sinnes of Ieroboam the sonne of Nebat, which made Israel to sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:17; Matthew 12:39–40; Joshua 19:13"> He restored the coast of Israel, from the entring of Hamath, vnto the Sea of the wildernesse, according to the worde of the Lord God of Israel, which he spake by his seruant Ionah the sonne of Amittai the Prophet, which was of Gath Hepher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; Exodus 3:7; 2 Kings 13:4"> For the Lord saw the exceeding bitter affliction of Israel, so that there was none shutte vp, nor any left, neyther yet any that could helpe Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 13:23; 2 Kings 13:5; Romans 11:2–12"> Yet the Lord had not decreed to put out the name of Israel from vnder the heauen: therefore he preserued them by the hand of Ieroboam the sonne of Ioash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 11:24; 1 Chronicles 18:5–6; 2 Samuel 8:6"> Concerning the rest of the actes of Ieroboam, and all that he did, and his valiant deedes, and how he fought, and how he restored Damascus, and Hamath to Iudah in Israel, are they not written in the booke of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 15:8"> So Ieroboam slept with his fathers, euen with the Kings of Israel, and Zachariah his sonne reigned in his steade.</span>
