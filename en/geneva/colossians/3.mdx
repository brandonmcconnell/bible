<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 8:6; Matthew 6:33; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> If yee then bee risen with Christ, seeke those thinges which are aboue, where Christ sitteth at the right hand of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Chronicles 22:19; Colossians 3:1"> Set your affections on things which are aboue, and not on things which are on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; Romans 6:2; Colossians 2:20"> For ye are dead, and your life is hid with Christ in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 11:25; 1 John 3:2; Galatians 2:20"> When Christ which is our life, shall appeare, then shall ye also appeare with him in glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 8:13; Galatians 5:19–21; Galatians 5:24"> Mortifie therefore your members which are on the earth, fornication, vncleannes, the inordinate affection, euill concupiscence, and couetousnes which is idolatrie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 5:6; Romans 1:18; 1 Peter 1:14"> For the which things sake ye wrath of God commeth on the children of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:2; 1 Peter 4:3–4; Romans 6:19–20"> Wherein ye also walked once, when ye liued in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Ephesians 4:22; James 1:20–21"> But now put ye away euen all these things, wrath, anger, maliciousnes, cursed speaking, filthie speaking, out of your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:22; Ephesians 4:25; Leviticus 19:11"> Lie not one to another, seeing that yee haue put off the olde man with his workes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 4:23–24; Romans 12:2; Ephesians 2:10"> And haue put on the newe, which is renewed in knowledge after the image of him that created him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:28–29; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Romans 10:12"> Where is neither Grecian nor Iewe, circumcision nor vncircumcision, Barbarian, Scythian, bond, free: But Christ is all, and in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Ephesians 4:2; 1 John 4:19"> Now therfore as the elect of God holy and beloued, put on the bowels of mercies, kindnesse, humblenesse of minde, meekenesse, long suffering:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Mark 11:25; James 2:13"> Forbearing one another, and forgiuing one another, if any man haue a quarel to another: euen as Christ forgaue, euen so doe ye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; Ephesians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 13:1–13"> And aboue all these thinges put on loue, which is the bond of perfectnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 4:6–7; John 14:27; Isaiah 26:3"> And let the peace of God rule in your hearts, to the which ye are called in one body, and be ye thankfull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Ephesians 5:19; John 15:7"> Let the worde of Christ dwell in you plenteously in all wisdome, teaching and admonishing your owne selues, in Psalmes, and hymnes, and spirituall songs, singing with a grace in your hearts to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:31; Colossians 3:23; Proverbs 3:6"> And whatsoeuer ye shall doe, in worde or deede, doe all in the Name of the Lord Iesus, giuing thankes to God euen the Father by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:22–9; Titus 2:4–5; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> Wiues, submit your selues vnto your husbands, as it is comely in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Genesis 2:23–24; Colossians 3:21"> Husbands, loue your wiues, and be not bitter vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Ephesians 6:1–3; Proverbs 6:20"> Children, obey your parents in all thing for that is well pleasing vnto the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1–4; Ephesians 6:4; Psalms 103:13"> Fathers, prouoke not your children to anger, least they be discouraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 6:5–7; 1 Timothy 6:1–2; Galatians 1:10"> Seruants, be obedient vnto them that are your masters according to the flesh, in all things, not with eye seruice as men pleasers, but in singlenes of heart, fearing God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Ephesians 6:6–7; Romans 14:8"> And whatsoeuer ye doe, doe it heartily, as to the Lord, and not to men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 6:8; Galatians 1:10; Acts 20:32"> Knowing that of the Lord ye shall receiue the reward of the inheritance: for ye serue the Lord Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 10:34; Romans 2:11; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> But he that doeth wrong, shall receiue for the wrong that he hath done: and there is no respect of persons.</span>
