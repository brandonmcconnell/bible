<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:9; 2 Samuel 3:2–3; 2 Samuel 13:15"> Now after this so it was, that Absalom the sonne of Dauid hauing a fayre sister, whose name was Tamar, Amnon the sonne of Dauid loued her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; 1 Kings 21:4; Song of Solomon 5:8"> And Amnon was so sore vexed, that he fell sicke for his sister Tamar: for she was a virgin, and it seemed hard to Amnon to doe any thing to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:9; 1 Corinthians 3:19; James 3:15"> But Amnon had a friend called Ionadab, the sonne of Shimeah Dauids brother: and Ionadab was a very subtile man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Leviticus 18:9; Esther 5:13–14"> Who sayde vnto him, Why art thou the Kings sonne so leane from day to day? wilt thou not tell me? Then Amnon answered him, I loue Tamar my brother Absaloms sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 6:24–25; Proverbs 19:27; Acts 23:15"> And Ionadab sayd vnto him, Lie downe on thy bed, and make thy selfe sicke: and when thy father shall come to see thee, say vnto him, I pray thee, let my sister Tamar come, and giue me meate, and let her dresse meate in my sight, that I may see it, and eate it of her hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:6; Matthew 13:33"> So Amnon lay downe, and made himselfe sicke: and when the King came to see him, Amnon sayde vnto the King, I pray thee, let Tamar my sister come, and make me a couple of cakes in my sight, that I may receiue meate at her hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then Dauid sent home to Tamar, saying, Goe now to thy brother Amnons house, and dresse him meate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> So Tamar went to her brother Amnons house, and he lay downe: and she tooke floure, and knead it, and made cakes in his sight, and did bake the cakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 45:1; John 3:20; Judges 3:19"> And she tooke a pan, and powred them out before him, but he would not eat. Then Amnon saide, Cause ye euery man to goe out from me: so euery man went out from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then Amnon said vnto Tamar, Bring the meate into the chamber, that I may eate of thine hand. And Tamar tooke the cakes which shee had made, and brought them into the chamber to Amnon her brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 39:11–12"> And when she had set them before him to eate, he tooke her, and sayd vnto her, Come, lye with me, my sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 19:23; Leviticus 20:17; Judges 20:6"> But shee answered him, Nay, my brother, doe not force me: for no such thing ought to be done in Israel: commit not this follie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 19:24; Genesis 20:12; Genesis 19:8"> And I, whither shall I cause my shame to goe? and thou shalt be as one of the fooles in Israel: now therefore, I pray thee, speake to the King, for he will not denie me vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:11; Esther 7:8; Judges 20:5"> Howbeit he would not hearken vnto her voyce, but being stronger then she, forced her, and lay with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:17"> Then Amnon hated her exceedingly, so that the hatred wherewith he hated her, was greater then the loue, wherewith hee had loued her: and Amnon sayde vnto her, Vp, get thee hence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And she answered him, There is no cause: this euill (to put mee away) is greater then the other that thou diddest vnto me: but he would not heare her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But called his seruant that serued him, and sayd, Put this woman now out from me, and locke the doore after her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Genesis 37:3; Psalms 45:13–14"> (And she had a garment of diuers coulours vpon her: for with such garments were the Kings daughters that were virgins, apparelled) Then his seruant brought her out, and locked the doore after her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 7:6; Jeremiah 2:37; 2 Samuel 1:11"> And Tamar put ashes on her head and rent the garment of diuers colours which was on her, and layde her hand on her head, and went her way crying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Genesis 46:15; Genesis 34:2"> And Absalom her brother sayd vnto her, Hath Amnon thy brother bene with thee? Now yet be still, my sister: he is thy brother: let not this thing grieue thine heart. So Tamar remayned desolate in her brother Absaloms house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:7; 2 Samuel 3:28–29; 1 Samuel 2:29"> But when King Dauid heard all these things, he was very wroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 19:17–18; Genesis 31:24; Genesis 24:50"> And Absalom sayde vnto his brother Amnon neither good nor bad: for Absalom hated Amnon, because he had forced his sister Tamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 38:12–13; 1 Samuel 25:4; 2 Chronicles 26:10"> And after the time of two yeeres, Absalom had sheepesherers in Baal-hazor, which is beside Ephraim, and Absalom called all the Kings sonnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:8–15; Jeremiah 41:6–7; Psalms 55:21"> And Absalom came to the King and sayd, Beholde now, thy seruant hath sheepesherers: I pray thee, that the King with his seruants would goe with thy seruant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 14:23; Judges 19:7–10; 2 Samuel 14:22"> But the King answered Absalom, Nay my sonne, I pray thee, let vs not goe all, lest we be chargeable vnto thee. Yet Absalom lay sore vpon him: howbeit he would not go, but thanked him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:9; 2 Samuel 3:27; 2 Samuel 11:13–15"> Then sayd Absalom, But, I pray thee, shall not my brother Amnon goe with vs? And the king answered him, Why should he go with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–26"> But Absalom was instant vpon him, and he sent Amnon with him, and all the Kings children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 19:6; Judges 19:22; Judges 19:9"> Now had Absalom commanded his seruants, saying, Marke now when Amnons heart is merry with wine, and when I say vnto you, Smite Amnon, kill him, feare not, for haue not I commanded you? be bold therefore, and play the men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:9; 1 Kings 1:33; 2 Kings 1:9–12"> And the seruantes of Absalom did vnto Amnon, as Absalom had commanded: and al the Kings sonnes arose, and euery man gate him vp vpon his mule, and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And while they were in the way, tydings came to Dauid, saying, Absalom hath slaine al the Kings sonnes, and there is not one of them left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:11; 2 Samuel 12:16; Job 1:20"> Then the King arose, and tare his garments, and lay on the ground, and all his seruants stoode by with their clothes rent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:9; 2 Samuel 13:3; Genesis 27:41"> And Ionadab the sonne of Shimeah Dauids brother answered and sayde, Let not my lord suppose that they haue slayne all the yong men the Kings sonnes: for Amnon onely is dead, because Absalom had reported so, since hee forced his sister Tamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:19"> Nowe therefore let not my lord the King take the thing so grieuously, to thinke that all ye Kings sonnes are dead: for Amnon only is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 4:8–14; Proverbs 28:17; Amos 5:19"> Then Absalom fled: and the yong man that kept the watch, lift vp his eyes, and looked, and behold, there came much people by the way of the hill side behinde him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And Ionadab said vnto the King, Behold, ye Kings sonnes come: as thy seruant sayd, so it is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:33; 2 Samuel 12:21; 2 Samuel 13:15"> And assoone as hee had left speaking, behold, the Kings sonnes came, and lift vp their voyces, and wept: and the King also and all his seruants wept exceedingly sore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:3; 2 Samuel 14:32; 2 Samuel 13:34"> But Absalom fled away, and went to Talmai the sonne of Ammihur King of Geshur: and Dauid mourned for his sonne euery day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:8; 2 Samuel 14:23; 2 Samuel 14:32"> So Absalom fled, and went to Geshur, and was there three yeeres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 24:67; Genesis 38:12; Genesis 37:35"> And King Dauid desired to go forth vnto Absalom, because he was pacified concerning Amnon, seeing he was dead.</span>
