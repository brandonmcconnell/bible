<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:1–4; 2 Samuel 19:17; 2 Samuel 19:27–29"> And there was of the housholde of Saul a seruant whose name was Ziba, and when they had called him vnto Dauid, the King sayd vnto him, Art thou Ziba? And he sayd, I thy seruant am he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 4:4; Titus 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 20:14–17"> Then the King sayd, Remayneth there yet none of the house of Saul, on whome I may shewe the mercie of God? Ziba then answered the King, Ionathan hath yet a sonne lame of his feete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:27–29"> Then the King said vnto him, Where is he? And Ziba said vnto the King, Behold, he is in the house of Machir ye sonne of Ammiel of Lo-debar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then King Dauid sent, and tooke him out of the house of Machir the sonne of Ammiel of Lo-debar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:4; 1 Chronicles 9:40; 1 Chronicles 8:34"> Nowe when Mephibosheth the sonne of Ionathan, the sonne of Saul was come vnto Dauid, he fel on his face, and did reuerence. And Dauid sayde, Mephibosheth? And he answered, Beholde thy seruant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:1; 1 Kings 2:7; 2 Samuel 19:28"> Then Dauid sayd vnto him, Feare not: for I wil surely shewe thee kindnes for Ionathan thy fathers sake, and will restore thee all the fieldes of Saul thy father, and thou shalt eate bread at my table continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:9; Matthew 15:26–27; 1 Samuel 24:14–15"> And he bowed himselfe and sayd, What is thy seruant, that thou shouldest looke vpon such a dead dog as I am?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:29; 2 Samuel 16:4; Isaiah 32:8"> Then the king called Ziba Sauls seruant, and said vnto him, I haue giue vnto thy masters sonne all that perteined to Saul and to all his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:7; 2 Samuel 19:28; 2 Kings 25:29"> Thou therefore and thy sonnes and thy seruantes shall till the lande for him, and bring in that thy masters sonne may haue foode to eate. And Mephibosheth thy masters sonne shall eate bread alway at my table (nowe Ziba had fifteene sonnes, and twentie seruants)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:1–4; 2 Samuel 19:26; 2 Samuel 19:17"> Then sayd Ziba vnto the King, According to all that my lord the King hath commaded his seruant, so shall thy seruat do, that Mephibosheth may eate at my table, as one of the Kings sonnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:40–44; 1 Chronicles 8:8; 1 Chronicles 8:34–40"> Mephibosheth also had a yong sonne named Micha, and all that dwelled in the house of Ziba, were seruants vnto Mephibosheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:7; 2 Samuel 9:3; 2 Samuel 9:10–11"> And Mephibosheth dwelt in Ierusalem: for he did eate continually at the Kings table, and was lame on both his feete.</span>
