<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:1–3; 2 Samuel 19:13; Genesis 29:14"> Then came all the tribes of Israel to Dauid vnto Hebron, and said thus, Beholde, we are thy bones and thy flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:6; Micah 5:4; Ezekiel 34:23"> And in time past when Saul was our King, thou leddest Israel in and out: and the Lord hath sayde to thee, Thou shalt feede my people Israel, and thou shalt be a captaine ouer Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:4; 1 Samuel 16:13; 1 Samuel 23:18"> So all the Elders of Israel came to the King to Hebron: and King Dauid made a couenant with them in Hebron before the Lord: and they anoynted Dauid King ouer Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:27; Luke 3:23; 1 Kings 2:11"> Dauid was thirtie yeere olde when he began to reigne: and hee reigned fortie yeere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:11; 1 Chronicles 3:4; 1 Kings 2:11"> In Hebron hee reigned ouer Iudah seuen yeere, and sixe moneths: and in Ierusalem hee reigned thirtie and three yeeres ouer all Israel and Iudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 1:8; Jeremiah 37:10; 1 Chronicles 11:4–9"> The King also and his men went to Ierusalem vnto the Iebusites, the inhabitants of the land: who spake vnto Dauid, saying, Except thou take away the blinde and the lame, thou shalt not come in hither: thinking that Dauid coulde not come thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 2:10; 2 Samuel 6:16; 2 Samuel 6:12"> But Dauid tooke the fort of Zion: this is the citie of Dauid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 15:16–17; 1 Chronicles 11:6–9; 1 Samuel 17:25"> Nowe Dauid had sayd the same day, Whosoeuer smiteth the Iebusites, and getteth vp to the gutters and smiteth the lame and blinde, which Dauids soule hateth, I will preferre him: therefore they saide, The blinde and the lame shall not come into that house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 9:15; 1 Kings 9:24; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> So Dauid dwelt in that forte, and called it the citie of Dauid, and Dauid built rounde about it, from Millo, and inward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1; Romans 8:31; Psalms 46:11"> And Dauid prospered and grewe: for the Lord God of hostes was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:1; 1 Kings 5:1–2; 1 Kings 5:18"> Hiram also king of Tyrus sent messengers to Dauid, and cedar trees, and carpenters, and masons for walles: and they built Dauid an house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:16; 1 Kings 10:9; 1 Chronicles 14:2"> Then Dauid knewe that the Lord had stablished him King ouer Israel, and that he had exalted his kingdome for his people Israels sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:9; Deuteronomy 17:17; 2 Chronicles 13:21"> And Dauid tooke him more concubines and wiues out of Ierusalem, after hee was come from Hebron, and more sonnes and daughters were borne to Dauid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:5–9; Luke 2:31; 1 Chronicles 14:4"> And these bee the names of the sonnes that were borne vnto him in Ierusale: Shammua, and Shobab, and Nathan, and Salomon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:6; 1 Chronicles 14:5"> And Ibhar, and Elishua, and Nepheg, and Iaphia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:8; 1 Chronicles 14:7"> And Elishama, and Eliada, and Eliphalet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:14; 1 Chronicles 14:8–9; 1 Chronicles 11:16"> But when the Philistims hearde that they had anoynted Dauid King ouer Israel, all the Philistims came vp to seeke Dauid: and when Dauid heard, he went downe to a fort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 15:8; 2 Samuel 23:13; Genesis 14:5"> But the Philistims came, and spred themselues in the valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:1; 1 Samuel 23:2; 2 Samuel 5:23"> Then Dauid asked counsel of the Lord, saying, Shall I goe vp to the Philistims? wilt thou deliuer them into mine handes? And the Lord answered Dauid, Goe vp: for I will doubtlesse deliuer the Philistims into thine handes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 28:21"> Then Dauid came to Baal-perazim, and smote them there, and sayde, The Lord hath deuided mine enemies asunder before mee, as waters be deuided asunder: therefore he called the name of that place, Baal-perazim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:11–12; Deuteronomy 7:5; 1 Samuel 5:2–6"> And there they left their images, and Dauid and his men burnt them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:13; 2 Samuel 5:18; 1 Kings 20:22"> Againe the Philistims came vp, and spred themselues in the valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:14; Matthew 9:29–30; Matthew 8:23–25"> And when Dauid asked counsell of the Lord, hee answered, Thou shalt not goe vp, but turne about behinde them, and come vpon them ouer against the mulberie trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 7:6; Judges 4:14; Judges 7:15"> And when thou hearest the noyse of one going in the toppes of the mulberie trees, then remoue: for then shall the Lord goe out before thee, to smite the hoste of the Philistims.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:16; Joshua 16:10; Isaiah 28:21"> Then Dauid did so as the Lord had commanded him, and smote the Philistims from Geba, vntil thou come to Gazer.</span>
