<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; Psalms 18:10; 1 Samuel 4:4"> And he rode vpon Cherub and did flie, and hee was seene vpon the winges of the winde.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Psalms 144:7; Psalms 18:16"> He sent from aboue, and tooke me: hee drewe me out of many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; 2 Timothy 4:17; Psalms 56:9"> He deliuered me from my strong enemie, and from them that hated me: for they were too strong for me.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 31:8; Psalms 118:5; 2 Samuel 15:26"> And brought me foorth into a large place: he deliuered me, because he fauoured me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 24:4; 1 Samuel 26:23; Psalms 7:8"> The Lord rewarded me according to my righteousnesse: according to the purenesse of mine handes he recompensed me.</span>

<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 144:1; Psalms 18:33–34; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> He teacheth mine handes to fight, so that a bowe of brasse is broken with mine armes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 6:16; Psalms 115:14; Psalms 84:11"> Thou hast also giuen me the shield of thy saluation, and thy louing kindnesse hath caused me to increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Psalms 17:5; 1 Samuel 2:9"> Thou hast inlarged my steppes vnder me, and mine heeles haue not slid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 21:8–9; 2 Samuel 5:18–25; 2 Samuel 10:14"> I haue pursued mine enemies and destroyed them, and haue not turned againe vntill I had consumed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Malachi 4:1; Psalms 110:1"> Yea, I haue consumed them and thrust them through, and they shall not arise, but shall fall vnder my feete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 44:5; Isaiah 60:14; Colossians 1:11"> For thou hast girded me with power to battell, and them that arose against me, hast thou subdued vnder me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; Joshua 10:24; 2 Thessalonians 1:8–9"> And thou hast giuen me the neckes of mine enemies, that I might destroy them that hate me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; 1 Samuel 28:6; Micah 3:4"> They looked about, but there was none to saue them, euen vnto the Lord, but he answered them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Micah 7:10; Isaiah 10:6; 2 Kings 13:7"> Then did I beate them as small as the dust of the earth: I did treade them flat as the clay of the streete, and did spread them abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:1–14; Isaiah 55:5; 2 Samuel 3:1"> Thou hast also deliuered me from the contentions of my people: thou hast preserued me to be the head ouer nations: the people which I knewe not, doe serue me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 81:15; Psalms 66:3; Isaiah 56:3"> Strangers shalbe in subiection to me: assoone as they heare, they shall obey me.</span>

<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 89:26; 2 Samuel 22:3; Job 19:25"> Let the Lord liue, and blessed be my strength: and God, euen the force of my saluation be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 94:1; Psalms 144:2; 2 Samuel 18:31"> It is God that giueth me power to reuenge me, and subdue the people vnder me,</span>
