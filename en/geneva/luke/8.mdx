<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Romans 10:15; Matthew 9:35"> And it came to passe afterwarde, that hee himselfe went through euery citie and towne, preaching and publishing the kingdome of God, and the twelue were with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:9; Matthew 27:55–56; Mark 15:40–41"> And certaine women, which were healed of euill spirites, and infirmities, as Mary which was called Magdalene, out of whom went seuen deuils,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 4:46–53; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Acts 9:36–39"> And Ioanna the wife of Chuza Herods steward, and Susanna, and many other which ministred vnto him of their substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 4:1–9; Matthew 13:2–9"> Nowe when much people were gathered together, and were come vnto him out of all cities, he spake by a parable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; James 1:23–24; Mark 4:15"> A sower went out to sowe his seede, and as he sowed, some fell by the wayes side, and it was troden vnder feete, and the foules of heauen deuoured it vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7–8; Luke 8:13; Mark 4:5–6"> And some fell on the stones, and when it was sprong vp, it withered away, because it lacked moystnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Luke 8:14; Luke 21:34"> And some fell among thornes, and the thornes sprang vp with it, and choked it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 2:7"> And some fell on good ground, and sprang vp, and bare fruite, an hundreth folde. And as hee sayd these things, he cryed, He that hath eares to heare, let him heare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 4:10; Matthew 13:10; Matthew 13:18"> Then his disciples asked him, demaunding what parable that was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; Mark 4:11; Matthew 11:25"> And he sayd, Vnto you it is giuen to know the secrets of ye kingdome of God, but to other in parables, that when they see, they shoulde not see, and when they heare, they should not vnderstand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Mark 4:14–20; James 1:21"> The parable is this, The seede is the worde of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–14; Revelation 12:9; James 1:23–24"> And they that are beside the way, are they that heare: afterward commeth the deuill, and taketh away the worde out of their hearts, least they should beleeue, and be saued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 6:4; John 5:35; Proverbs 12:12"> But they that are on the stones, are they which when they haue heard, receiue ye word with ioy: but they haue no rootes: which for a while beleeue, but in the time of tentation goe away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; 1 Timothy 6:17"> And that which fell among thornes, are they which haue heard, and after their departure are choked with cares and with riches, and voluptuous liuing, and bring forth no fruite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Galatians 5:22–26; Colossians 1:10"> But that which fell in good ground, are they which with an honest and good heart heare the worde, and keepe it, and bring forth fruite with patience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 11:33; Matthew 5:15–16; Mark 4:21–22"> No man when he hath lighted a candle, couereth it vnder a vessell, neither putteth it vnder the bed, but setteth it on a candlesticke, that they that enter in, may see the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 12:2–3; Matthew 10:26; Mark 4:22"> For nothing is secret, that shall not be euident: neither any thing hid, that shall not be knowen, and come to light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:12; Luke 19:26; Mark 4:23–25"> Take heede therefore how ye heare: for whosoeuer hath, to him shall be giuen: and whosoeuer hath not, from him shalbe taken euen that which it seemeth that he hath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 12:46–50; Mark 3:31–35; Mark 3:21"> Then came to him his mother and his brethren, and could not come neere to him for the preasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 1:14; Mark 6:3; Matthew 13:55–56"> And it was tolde him by certaine which said, Thy mother and thy brethren stand without, and would see thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 11:27–28; James 1:22; John 13:17"> But he answered, and sayd vnto them, My mother and my brethren are these which heare the worde of God, and doe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 8:23–27; Mark 4:35–41; Mark 6:45"> And it came to passe on a certaine day, that he went into a ship with his disciples, and he sayd vnto them, Let vs goe ouer vnto the other side of the lake. And they lanched forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 93:3–4; Psalms 124:2–4; Psalms 148:8"> And as they sayled, he fell a sleepe, and there came downe a storme of winde on the lake, and they were filled with water, and were in ieopardie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 107:25–29; Luke 4:39; Psalms 104:6–9"> Then they went to him, and awoke him, saying, Master, Master, we perish. And he arose, and rebuked the winde, and the waues of water: and they ceased, and it was calme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 8:26; Proverbs 30:4; Mark 4:40–41"> Then he sayde vnto them, Where is your fayth? and they feared, and wondered among them selues, saying, Who is this that commandeth both the windes and water, and they obey him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 5:1–20; Matthew 8:28–34"> So they sailed vnto the region of the Gadarenes, which is ouer against Galile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 5:2–5; 1 Samuel 19:24; Numbers 19:16"> And as he went out to land, there met him a certaine man out of the citie, which had deuils long time, and he ware no garment, neither abode in house, but in the graues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; 2 Peter 2:4; Mark 5:6–8"> And when he sawe Iesus, he cryed out and fell downe before him, and with a loude voyce sayd, What haue I to doe with thee, Iesus the Sonne of God the most High? I beseech thee torment me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 5:3–5; Luke 9:39; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> For he commanded ye foule spirit to come out of the man: (for oft times he had caught him: therefore he was bound with chaines, and kept in fetters: but he brake the bands, and was caried of the deuill into wildernesses.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 26:53; Luke 8:2; Matthew 8:29"> Then Iesus asked him, saying, What is thy name? and he sayd, Legion, because many deuils were entred into him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Philippians 2:10–11; Job 2:5"> And they besought him, that he would not commaund them to goe out into the deepe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3; Revelation 20:7"> And there was there by, an hearde of many swine, feeding on an hill: and the deuils besought him, that he would suffer them to enter into them. So he suffered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; John 8:44; Luke 8:22–23"> Then went the deuils out of the man, and entred into the swine: and the hearde was caried with violence from a steepe downe place into the lake, and was choked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 5:14; Matthew 8:33; Matthew 28:11"> When the heardmen sawe what was done, they fled: and when they were departed, they tolde it in the citie and in the countrey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 10:39; Acts 22:3; 1 John 3:8"> Then they came out to see what was done, and came to Iesus, and found the man, out of whom the deuils were departed, sitting at the feete of Iesus, clothed, and in his right minde: and they were afrayd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 4:24"> They also which saw it, tolde them by what meanes he that was possessed with the deuill, was healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 16:39; Luke 8:28; Luke 10:10–11"> Then the whole multitude of the countrey about the Gadarenes, besought him that he would depart from them: for they were taken with a great feare: and he went into the ship, and returned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 9:13–16; Mark 5:18–20; Psalms 145:3–12"> Then the man, out of whome the deuils were departed, besought him that hee might be with him: but Iesus sent him away, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 17:15–18; 1 Timothy 5:8; Deuteronomy 10:21"> Returne into thine owne house, and shewe what great things God hath done to thee. So hee went his way, and preached throughout all the citie, what great things Iesus had done vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 9:1; Mark 5:21; Mark 6:20"> And it came to passe, when Iesus was come againe, that the people receiued him: for they all waited for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 5:22–43; Luke 13:14; Acts 18:8"> And beholde, there came a man named Iairus, and he was the ruler of the Synagogue, who fell downe at Iesus feete, and besought him that he would come into his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 7:12; Luke 8:45; Job 1:18–19"> For he had but a daughter onely, about twelue yeeres of age, and she lay a dying (and as he went, the people thronged him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 5:25–26; Matthew 9:20–22; Leviticus 15:25–33"> And a woman hauing an yssue of blood, twelue yeeres long, which had spent all her substance vpon physicians, and could not be healed of any:</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Mark 5:27–28; Deuteronomy 22:12; Acts 19:12"> When she came behind him, she touched the hemme of his garment, and immediatly her yssue of blood stanched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 5:30–32; Luke 9:13; Luke 5:5"> Then Iesus sayd, Who is it that hath touched me? When euery man denied, Peter sayd and they that were with him, Master, the multitude thrust thee, and tread on thee, and sayest thou, Who hath touched me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 5:17; 1 Peter 2:9"> And Iesus sayde, Some one hath touched me: for I perceiue that vertue is gone out of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 5:33; Luke 17:15–16; 1 Corinthians 2:3"> When the woman sawe that she was not hid, she came trembling, and fell downe before him, and tolde him before all the people, for what cause she had touched him, and how she was healed immediatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Matthew 9:22; Hebrews 4:2"> And he said vnto her, Daughter, be of good comfort: thy faith hath saued thee: go in peace.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 5:35–43; Isaiah 7:12; Luke 7:6"> While he yet spake, there came one from the ruler of the Synagogues house, which sayde to him, Thy daughter is dead: disease not the Master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 11:40; John 11:25; Mark 5:36"> When Iesus heard it, he answered him, saying, Feare not: beleeue onely, and she shall be saued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Mark 14:33; Matthew 6:5–6; 1 Kings 17:19–23"> And when he went into the house, he suffered no man to goe in with him, saue Peter, and Iames, and Iohn, and the father and mother of the maide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 23:27; John 11:4; Zechariah 12:10"> And all wept, and sorowed for her: but he sayd, Weepe not: for she is not dead, but sleepeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Luke 16:14; Isaiah 53:3; John 11:39"> And they laught him to scorne, knowing that she was dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; John 11:43; Mark 1:31"> So he thrust them all out, and tooke her by the hand, and cryed, saying, Maide, arise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 11:44; 1 Kings 17:21–23; Mark 5:43"> And her spirite came againe, and she rose straightway: and he comanded to giue her meate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Luke 5:14"> Then her parents were astonied: but hee commanded them that they should tell no man what was done.</span>
