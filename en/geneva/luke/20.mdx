<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:23–27; Luke 19:47–48; Acts 6:12"> And it came to passe, that on one of those dayes, as he taught the people in the Temple, and preached the Gospel, the hie Priests and the Scribes came vpon him with the Elders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; Mark 11:28–33; John 2:18"> And spake vnto him, saying, Tell vs by what authoritie thou doest these things, or who is hee that hath giuen thee this authoritie?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 15:2–3; Luke 22:68; Colossians 4:6"> And he answered, and sayde vnto them, I also will aske you one thing: tell me therefore:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:18; Matthew 11:7–19; Matthew 17:11–12"> The baptisme of Iohn, was it from heauen, or of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:34; John 1:15–18; John 1:30"> And they reasoned within themselues, saying, If we shall say, From heauen, he will say, Why then beleeued ye him not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 11:9; John 10:41"> But if we shall say, Of men, all the people will stone vs: for they be perswaded that Iohn was a Prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; 2 Peter 3:3; Isaiah 41:28"> Therefore they answered, that they could not tell whence it was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:4–5; Matthew 21:27; Mark 11:33"> Then Iesus sayd vnto them, Neither tell I you, by what authoritie I doe these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 21:33–46; Mark 12:1–12; Isaiah 5:1–7"> Then began he to speake to ye people this parable, A certaine man planted a vineyarde, and let it forth to husbandmen: and went into a strange countrey, for a great time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Nehemiah 9:26; Matthew 21:34–36"> And at the time conuenient he sent a seruant to the husbandmen, that they should giue him of the fruite of the vineyard: but the husbandmen did beate him, and sent him away emptie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 10:1; Hebrews 11:36–37; 1 Thessalonians 2:2"> Againe he sent yet another seruant: and they did beate him, and foule entreated him, and sent him away emptie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Moreouer he sent the third, and him they wounded, and cast out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 3:17; John 3:16–17; Jeremiah 36:7"> Then sayd the Lord of the vineyard, What shall I doe? I will send my beloued sonne: it may be that they will doe reuerence, when they see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Hebrews 1:2; Acts 2:23"> But when the husbandmen sawe him, they reasoned with themselues, saying, This is the heire: come, let vs kill him, that the inheritance may be ours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:12; Mark 12:6–9; Matthew 21:37–40"> So they cast him out of the vineyarde, and killed him. What shall the Lord of the vineyarde therefore doe vnto them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:27; Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:41"> He will come and destroy these husbandmen, and wil giue out his vineyard to others. But when they heard it, they sayd, God forbid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 118:22; Isaiah 28:16; Acts 4:11"> And he beheld them, and said, What meaneth this then that is written, The stone that the builders refused, that is made the head of the corner?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; Zechariah 12:3; Daniel 2:44–45"> Whosoeuer shall fall vpon that stone, shall be broken: and on whomsoeuer it shall fall, it will grinde him to pouder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 19:47–48; Luke 20:14; Matthew 21:45–46"> Then the hie Priests, and the Scribes the same houre went about to lay hands on him: (but they feared the people) for they perceiued that he had spoken this parable against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 11:54; Matthew 22:15–22; Matthew 27:2"> And they watched him, and sent forth spies, which should faine themselues iust men, to take him in his talke, and to deliuer him vnto the power and authoritie of the gouernour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4–5; Matthew 26:49–50; Matthew 22:16"> And they asked him, saying, Master, we know that thou sayest, and teachest right, neither doest thou accept mans person, but teachest the way of God truely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:37; Mark 12:14–17; Acts 5:37"> Is it lawfull for vs to giue Cesar tribute or no?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:18; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Luke 5:22"> But he perceiued their craftines, and sayd vnto them, Why tempt ye me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 18:28; Acts 11:28; Acts 26:32"> Shew me a penie. Whose image and superscription hath it? They answered, and sayd, Cesars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 13:6–7; Mark 12:17; 1 Peter 2:13–17"> Then he sayd vnto them, Giue then vnto Cesar the things which are Cesars, and to God those which are Gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 20:20; Proverbs 26:4–5; Titus 1:10"> And they could not reproue his saying before the people: but they marueiled at his answere, and helde their peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 4:1–2; 1 Corinthians 15:12; Mark 12:18–27"> Then came to him certaine of the Sadduces (which denie that there is any resurrection) and they asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Genesis 38:8; Genesis 38:11"> Saying, Master, Moses wrote vnto vs, If any mans brother die hauing a wife, and hee die without children, that his brother should take his wife, and raise vp seede vnto his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20; Jeremiah 22:30"> Now there were seuen brethren, and the first tooke a wife, and he dyed without children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the second tooke the wife, and he dyed childelesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Then the third tooke her: and so likewise the seuen dyed, and left no children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:4; Ecclesiastes 9:5; Hebrews 9:27"> And last of all the woman dyed also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 22:24–28; Mark 12:19–23"> Therefore at the resurrection, whose wife of them shall she be? for seuen had her to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 16:8; Luke 17:27; Hebrews 13:4"> Then Iesus answered, and sayd vnto them, The children of this world marry wiues, and are married.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; John 5:29; Daniel 12:2–3"> But they which shalbe counted worthy to enioy that world, and the resurrection from the dead, neither marry wiues, neither are married.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Philippians 3:21; Matthew 22:30"> For they can die no more, forasmuch as they are equall vnto the Angels, and are the sonnes of God, since they are the children of the resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Mark 12:26–27; Acts 7:30–32"> And that the dead shall rise againe, euen Moses shewed it besides the bush, when he said, The Lord is the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Iacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:10–11"> For he is not the God of the dead, but of them which liue: for all liue vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 23:9; Mark 12:28–34; Matthew 22:34–40"> Then certaine of the Scribes answered, and sayd, Master, thou hast well sayd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Mark 12:34; Proverbs 26:5"> And after that, durst they not aske him any thing at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 1:1; Matthew 22:41–45"> Then sayd he vnto them, Howe say they that Christ is Dauids sonne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Acts 2:34–35; Hebrews 1:13"> And Dauid himselfe sayth in the booke of the Psalmes, The Lord sayd vnto my Lord, Sit at my right hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 19:27; Psalms 110:1; Revelation 19:14–21"> Till I shall make thine enemies thy footestoole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Luke 1:31–35; Matthew 1:23"> Seeing Dauid called him Lord, howe is he then his sonne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 15:10; Mark 12:38–39; Mark 8:34"> Then in the audience of all the people he sayd vnto his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 11:43; Mark 12:38–39; Luke 14:7"> Beware of the Scribes, which willingly go in long robes, and loue salutations in the markets, and the highest seates in the assemblies, and the chiefe roomes at feastes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 12:40; James 3:1; Titus 1:16"> Which deuoure widowes houses, and in shewe make long prayers: These shall receiue greater damnation.</span>
