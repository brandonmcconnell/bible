<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 11:47–53; Matthew 12:14; Luke 20:19"> And the hie Priests and Scribes sought how they might kill him: for they feared the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 13:2; Mark 14:10–11; John 13:26–27"> Then entred Satan into Iudas, who was called Iscariot, and was of the nomber of the twelue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Acts 5:24; Luke 22:52"> And he went his way, and communed with the hie Priestes and captaines, how he might betray him to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 26:15–16; Acts 1:18; Matthew 27:3–5"> So they were glad, and agreed to giue him money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 14:2; Matthew 26:5"> And he consented, and sought opportunitie to betraye him vnto them, when the people were away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:18; Mark 14:12–16; 1 Corinthians 5:7"> Then came the day of vnleauened bread, when the Passeouer must be sacrificed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 14:13–16; Acts 4:19; Acts 4:13"> And he sent Peter and Iohn, saying, Go and prepare vs the Passeouer, that we may eate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And they saide to him, Where wilt thou, that we prepare it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 16:4; Matthew 26:18–19; Acts 8:26–29"> Then he said vnto them, Beholde, when ye be entred into the citie, there shall a man meete you, bearing a pitcher of water: folowe him into the house that he entreth in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:28; Revelation 3:20; Luke 19:34"> And say vnto the good man of the house, The Master saith vnto thee, Where is the lodging where I shall eate my Passeouer with my disciples?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 16:14–15; Acts 1:13; John 2:25"> Then he shall shewe you a great hie chamber trimmed: there make it ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 21:33; Luke 19:32; John 2:5"> So they went, and found as he had said vnto them, and made readie the Passeouer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 26:20; Mark 14:17; Deuteronomy 16:6–7"> And when the houre was come, hee sate downe, and the twelue Apostles with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 12:50; John 4:34; John 17:1"> Then he saide vnto them, I haue earnestly desired to eate this Passeouer with you, before I suffer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 14:15; Revelation 19:9; Luke 22:30"> For I say vnto you, Hencefoorth I will not eate of it any more, vntill it bee fulfilled in the kingdome of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:19; Psalms 116:13; Deuteronomy 8:10"> And hee tooke the cup, and gaue thankes, and said, Take this, and deuide it among you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 26:29; Luke 22:16; Mark 14:25"> For I say vnto you, I will not drinke of the fruite of the vine, vntill the kingdome of God be come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:23–29; 1 Corinthians 10:16; Mark 14:22–24"> And he tooke bread, and when he had giuen thankes, he brake it, and gaue to them, saying, This is my body, which is giuen for you: doe this in the remembrance of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 26:28; 1 Corinthians 11:25; Zechariah 9:11"> Likewise also after supper he tooke the cup, saying, This cup is that newe Testament in my blood, which is shed for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 13:26; John 13:21–22"> Yet beholde, the hand of him that betrayeth me, is with me at the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 2:23; Psalms 109:6–15; Psalms 55:12–15"> And truely the Sonne of man goeth as it is appointed: but woe be to that man, by whom he is betrayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:22–25; Mark 14:19; Matthew 26:22"> Then they began to enquire among themselues which of them it should be, that should do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 9:46; Mark 9:34; Mark 10:37–41"> And there arose also a strife among them, which of them should seeme to be ye greatest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 20:25–28; Mark 10:41–45"> But hee saide vnto them, The Kings of the Gentiles reigne ouer them, and they that beare rule ouer them, are called bountifull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Luke 9:48; Matthew 18:3–5"> But yee shall not be so: but let the greatest among you be as the least: and the chiefest as he that serueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Luke 12:37; John 13:5–16"> For who is greater, he that sitteth at table, or he that serueth? Is not he that sitteth at table? And I am among you as he that serueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:18; Hebrews 4:15; Acts 1:25"> And yee are they which haue continued with me in my tentations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 25:34; 2 Timothy 2:12; James 2:5"> Therefore I appoint vnto you a kingdome, as my Father hath appointed vnto me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Matthew 8:11; Revelation 2:26–27"> That ye may eate, and drinke at my table in my kingdome, and sit on seates, and iudge the twelue tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Zechariah 3:1; Job 1:6–12"> And the Lord saide, Simon, Simon, beholde, Satan hath desired you, to winowe you as wheate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–10; John 21:15–17; John 17:9–11"> But I haue prayed for thee, that thy faith faile not: therefore when thou art conuerted, strengthen thy brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 14:31; Matthew 26:33–35; Mark 14:29"> And he said vnto him, Lord, I am ready to goe with thee into prison, and to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 13:38; Matthew 26:34; Mark 14:30"> But he said, I tell thee, Peter, the cocke shall not crowe this day, before thou hast thrise denied that thou knewest me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 10:9–10; Luke 10:4; Psalms 23:1"> And he saide vnto them, When I sent you without bagge, and scrip, and shooes, lacked ye any thing? And they said, Nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 10:22–25; 1 Thessalonians 3:4; 1 Thessalonians 2:14–15"> Then he said to them, But nowe he that hath a bagge, let him take it, and likewise a scrip: and hee that hath none, let him sell his coate, and buy a sworde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Luke 23:32; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For I say vnto you, That yet the same which is written, must be perfourmed in me, Euen with the wicked was he nombred: for doubtlesse those things which are written of me, haue an ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 22:49; Matthew 26:52–54; John 18:36"> And they said, Lord, beholde, here are two swordes. And he said vnto them, It is ynough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 21:37; John 18:1–2; Matthew 21:1"> And he came out, and went (as he was wont) to the mount of Oliues: and his disciples also followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Matthew 26:36–46; Luke 22:46"> And when hee came to the place, hee said to them, Pray, lest ye enter into tentation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 26:39; Mark 14:35; Luke 18:11"> And he was drawen aside from them about a stones cast, and kneeled downe, and prayed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:27–28; Matthew 26:39; Matthew 26:42"> Saying, Father, if thou wilt, take away this cuppe from mee: neuerthelesse, not my will, but thine be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 4:11; Matthew 26:53; Psalms 91:11–12"> And there appeared an Angell vnto him from heauen, comforting him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 1:12; Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 22:12–21"> But being in an agonie, hee prayed more earnestly: and his sweate was like drops of blood, trickling downe to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 14:40–41; Matthew 26:43; Matthew 26:40"> And he rose vp from prayer, and came to his disciples, and found them sleeping for heauinesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 22:40; Jonah 1:6; Luke 21:34–36"> And he said vnto them, Why sleepe ye? rise and pray, least ye enter into tentation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 14:41–50; John 18:2–11; Luke 22:3–6"> And while he yet spake, beholde, a companie, and he that was called Iudas one of the twelue, went before them, and came neere vnto Iesus to kisse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Mark 14:44–46; 2 Samuel 20:9–10; Proverbs 27:6"> And Iesus saide vnto him, Iudas, betrayest thou the Sonne of man with a kisse?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 22:38"> Now when they which were about him, saw what would follow, they said vnto him, Lord, shall we smite with sworde?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Mark 14:47; Romans 12:19"> And one of them smote a seruant of the hie Priest, and strooke off his right eare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Romans 12:21; 2 Corinthians 10:1; 1 Peter 2:21–23"> Then Iesus answered, and said, Suffer them thus farre: and he touched his eare, and healed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 22:4; Mark 14:48–49; John 17:12"> Then Iesus said vnto the hie Priests, and captaines of the Temple, and the Elders which were come to him, Bee ye come out as vnto a theefe with swordes and staues?</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 12:27; Ephesians 6:12; Acts 26:18"> When I was dayly with you in the Temple, yee stretched not foorth the handes against mee: but this is your very houre, and the power of darkenesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Mark 14:53–54; Matthew 26:57–58; John 18:12–17"> Then tooke they him, and led him, and brought him to the hie Priestes house. And Peter followed afarre off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Psalms 26:4–5; 2 Corinthians 6:15–17; Mark 14:66–72"> And when they had kindled a fire in the middes of the hall, and were set downe together, Peter also sate downe among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 18:17; Mark 14:6; Mark 14:17"> And a certaine mayde behelde him as hee sate by the fire, and hauing well looked on him, said, This man was also with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="1 John 1:9; John 18:25; Matthew 26:70"> But he denied him, saying, Woman, I know him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 18:25; Matthew 26:71–72; Mark 14:69–70"> And after a little while, another man sawe him, and saide, Thou art also of them. But Peter said, Man, I am not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 18:26–27; Mark 14:69–70; Matthew 26:73–74"> And about the space of an houre after, a certaine other affirmed, saying. Verely euen this man was with him: for he is also a Galilean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Matthew 26:74–75; Luke 22:34; Mark 14:71–72"> And Peter saide, Man, I knowe not what thou sayest. And immediatly while hee yet spake, the cocke crewe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Luke 22:34; Isaiah 57:15–18; Jeremiah 31:18–20"> Then the Lord turned backe, and looked vpon Peter: and Peter remembred the worde of the Lord, how he had said vnto him, Before the cocke crowe, thou shalt denie me thrise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Matthew 26:75; 1 Corinthians 10:12; Mark 14:72"> And Peter went out, and wept bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="John 18:22; Mark 14:55–65; Job 16:9–10"> And the men that helde Iesus, mocked him, and strooke him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Judges 16:25; Judges 16:21"> And when they had blindfolded him, they smote him on the face, and asked him, saying, Prophecie who it is that smote thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; Matthew 27:39"> And many other thinges blasphemously spake they against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Matthew 27:1; Mark 15:1; Acts 22:5"> And assoone as it was day, the Elders of the people, and the hie Priests and the Scribes came together, and led him into their councill,</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Matthew 11:3–5; John 8:43–45; Matthew 26:63–68"> Saying, Art thou that Christ? tell vs. And he said vnto them, If I tell you, ye wil not beleeue it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Luke 20:3–7"> And if also I aske you, you will not answere me, nor let me goe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Psalms 110:1; Mark 16:19"> Hereafter shall the Sonne of man sit at the right hand of the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Luke 23:3; Matthew 27:11; Matthew 26:64"> Then sayd they all, Art thou then ye Sonne of God? And he sayd to them, Ye say, that I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Matthew 26:65–66; Mark 14:63–64"> Then sayd they, What neede we any further witnes? for we our selues haue heard it of his owne mouth.</span>
