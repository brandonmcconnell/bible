<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 14:12; Luke 10:19; Matthew 16:19"> Then called hee his twelue disciples together, and gaue them power and authoritie ouer all deuils, and to heale diseases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 10:7–8; Luke 10:9; Matthew 3:2"> And hee sent them foorth to preach the kingdome of God, and to cure the sicke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Psalms 37:3; Luke 10:4–12"> And he sayd to them, Take nothing to your iourney, neither staues, nor scrip, neither bread, nor siluer, neither haue two coates apiece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:11; Mark 6:10; Luke 10:5–8"> And whatsoeuer house ye enter into, there abide, and thence depart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:51; Acts 18:6; Mark 6:11"> And howe many so euer will not receiue you, when ye goe out of that citie, shake off the very dust from your feete for a testimonie against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 4:30; Mark 16:20; Acts 5:15"> And they went out, and went through euery towne preaching the Gospel, and healing euery where.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 6:14–28; Matthew 14:1–12; Luke 3:1"> Nowe Herod the Tetrarch heard of all that was done by him: and he douted, because that it was sayd of some, that Iohn was risen againe from the dead:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:21; Luke 9:19; Mark 6:15"> And of some, that Elias had appeared: and of some, that one of the olde Prophets was risen againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 23:8; Luke 13:31–32"> Then Herod sayd, Iohn haue I beheaded: who then is this of whome I heare such things? and he desired to see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 11:21; Hebrews 13:17; Matthew 14:13–21"> And when the Apostles returned, they tolde him what great things they had done. Then he tooke them to him, and went aside into a solitarie place, neere to the citie called Bethsaida.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 9:2; Luke 5:31; Hebrews 4:16"> But when the people knewe it, they followed him: and he receiued them, and spake vnto them of the kingdome of God, and healed them that had neede to be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:5–15; Hosea 13:5"> And when the day began to weare away, the twelue came, and sayd vnto him, Sende the people away, that they may goe into the townes and villages round about, and lodge, and get meate: for we are here in a desart place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; John 6:5–9; 2 Kings 4:42–43"> But he sayd vnto them, Giue ye them to eate. And they sayd, We haue no more but fiue loaues and two fishes, except we should go and buy meate for all this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 8:6; 1 Corinthians 14:40; Mark 6:39–40"> For they were about fiue thousand men. Then he sayde to his disciples, Cause them to sit downe by fifties in a company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And they did so, and caused all to sit downe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; 1 Timothy 4:4–5; Matthew 15:36"> Then he tooke the fiue loaues, and the two fishes, and looked vp to heauen, and blessed them, and brake, and gaue to the disciples, to set before the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 4:44; Matthew 15:37–38; John 6:11–13"> So they did all eate, and were satisfied: and there was taken vp of that remained to them, twelue baskets full of broken meate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 16:13–16; Luke 11:1; Matthew 26:36"> And it came to passe, as hee was alone praying, his disciples were with him: and he asked them, saying, Whom say the people that I am?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Luke 9:7–8; Matthew 14:2"> They answered, and sayd, Iohn Baptist: and others say, Elias: and some say, that one of the olde Prophets is risen againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 16:15–17; John 4:29; John 7:41"> And he sayd vnto them, But whom say ye that I am? Peter answered, and sayd, That Christ of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 16:20; Matthew 17:9; Mark 8:30–31"> And he warned and commanded them, that they should tell that to no man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Luke 24:7; Luke 18:31–34"> Saying, The Sonne of man must suffer many things and be reproued of the Elders, and of the hie Priests and Scribes, and be slaine, and the third day rise againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 10:38–39; Luke 14:26–27; Romans 8:13"> And he sayd to them all, If any man will come after me, let him denie himselfe, and take vp his crosse dayly, and follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 17:33; John 12:25; Acts 20:23–24"> For whosoeuer will saue his life, shall lose it: and whosoeuer shall lose his life for my sake, the same shall saue it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 8:36; Matthew 16:26; Psalms 49:6–8"> For what auantageth it a man, if he win the whole worlde, and destroy himselfe, or lose himselfe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:32–33; 2 Timothy 2:12; Luke 12:8–9"> For whosoeuer shall be ashamed of me, and of my wordes, of him shall the Sonne of man be ashamed, when hee shall come in his glorie, and in the glorie of the Father, and of the holy Angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:28; Mark 9:1; John 21:22–23"> And I tell you of a suretie, there be some standing here, which shall not taste of death, till they haue seene the kingdome of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 17:1–13; Matthew 26:37–39; Mark 14:33–36"> And it came to passe about an eyght dayes after those wordes, that he tooke Peter and Iohn, and Iames, and went vp into a mountaine to pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 17:2; Mark 9:2–3; Exodus 34:29–35"> And as he prayed, the fashion of his countenance was changed, and his garment was white and glistered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 3:3–6; 2 Corinthians 3:7–11; Luke 1:17"> And beholde, two men talked with him, which were Moses and Elias:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Philippians 3:21; 1 Peter 1:11–12; 1 Peter 5:10"> Which appeared in glory, and tolde of his departing, which he shoulde accomplish at Hierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 8:18; John 1:14; Daniel 10:9"> But Peter and they that were with him, were heauie with sleepe, and when they awoke, they saw his glorie, and the two men standing with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 9:5–6; Mark 10:38; Psalms 4:6–7"> And it came to passe, as they departed from him, Peter said vnto Iesus, Master, it is good for vs to be here: let vs therefore make three tabernacles, one for thee, and one for Moses, and one for Elias, and wist not what he said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 40:34–38; Matthew 17:5–7; Judges 13:22"> Whiles he thus spake, there came a cloude and ouershadowed them, and they feared when they were entring into the cloude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 3:22; Acts 3:22–23; Matthew 3:17"> And there came a voyce out of the cloud, saying, This is that my beloued Sonne, heare him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 17:9; Mark 9:9–10; Mark 9:6"> And when the voyce was past, Iesus was found alone: and they kept it close, and tolde no man in those dayes any of those things which they had seene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 17:14–20; Mark 9:14–29"> And it came to passe on the next day, as they came downe from the mountaine, much people met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; John 4:47; Luke 7:12"> And beholde, a man of the companie cried out, saying, Master, I beseech thee, beholde my sonne: for he is all that I haue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 9:20; Luke 8:29; Luke 4:35"> And loe, a spirit taketh him, and suddenly he crieth, and he teareth him, that he fometh, and hardly departeth from him, when he hath bruised him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Acts 19:13–16; Luke 10:17–19"> Nowe I haue besought thy disciples to cast him out, but they could not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:5; Romans 2:4; John 20:27"> Then Iesus answered, and said, O generation faithlesse, and crooked, howe long now shall I be with you, and suffer you? bring thy sonne hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Mark 9:26–27; Luke 7:15; Revelation 12:12"> And whiles he was yet comming, the deuill rent him, and tare him: and Iesus rebuked the vncleane spirite, and healed the childe, and deliuered him to his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:43–45; Luke 5:26; Matthew 17:22"> And they were all amased at the mightie power of God: and while they all wondered at al things, which Iesus did, he said vnto his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 9:22; Acts 2:23; Luke 2:19"> Marke these wordes diligently: for it shall come to passe, that the Sonne of man shalbe deliuered into the handes of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 18:34; Luke 2:50; John 12:16"> But they vnderstood not that word: for it was hid from them, so that they could not perceiue it: and they feared to aske him of that worde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 18:1–5; Mark 9:33–37; Matthew 20:20–22"> Then there arose a disputation among them, which of them should be the greatest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 139:23; John 16:30; Luke 5:22"> When Iesus sawe the thoughtes of their heartes, he tooke a litle childe, and set him by him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Peter 5:6; Luke 14:11; Mark 9:37"> And said vnto them, Whosoeuer receiueth this litle childe in my Name, receiueth me: and whosoeuer shall receiue me, receiueth him that sent me: for he that is least among you all, he shall be great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 9:38–40; Numbers 11:27–29; Mark 10:13–14"> And Iohn answered and saide, Master, we sawe one casting out deuils in thy Name, and we forbad him, because he followeth thee not with vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 12:30; Luke 11:23; 1 Corinthians 12:3"> Then Iesus saide vnto him, Forbid ye him not: for he that is not against vs, is with vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Isaiah 50:5–9; Mark 16:19; Luke 13:22"> And it came to passe, when the dayes were accomplished, that he should be receiued vp, he setled himselfe fully to goe to Hierusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 10:5; Luke 17:16; Luke 10:1"> And sent messengers before him: and they went and entred into a towne of the Samaritans, to prepare him lodging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 4:9; John 4:40–42; Luke 9:48"> But they woulde not receiue him, because his behauiour was, as though he would go to Hierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="2 Kings 1:10–14; James 3:14–18; James 1:19–20"> And when his disciples, Iames and Iohn sawe it, they saide, Lord, wilt thou that we commaund, that fire come downe from heauen, and consume them, euen as Elias did?</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; 1 Peter 3:9; Jeremiah 17:9"> But Iesus turned about, and rebuked them, and said, Ye knowe not of what spirit ye are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Romans 12:21; John 3:17"> For the Sonne of man is not come to destroy mens liues, but to saue them. Then they went to another towne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Matthew 8:19–22; John 13:37; Luke 9:57–60"> And it came to passe that as they went in the way, a certaine man said vnto him, I will follow thee, Lord, whithersoeuer thou goest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Matthew 8:20; Luke 14:26–33; James 2:5"> And Iesus saide vnto him, The foxes haue holes, and the birdes of the heauen nestes, but the Sonne of man hath not whereon to lay his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; Matthew 6:33; 1 Kings 19:20"> But he said vnto another, Followe me. And the same said, Lord, suffer me first to goe and burie my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:3–4; 1 Timothy 5:6; John 21:15–17"> And Iesus said vnto him, Let the dead burie their dead: but go thou, and preache the kingdome of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Luke 14:26; Luke 14:18–20; 1 Kings 19:20"> Then another saide, I will follow thee, Lord: but let me first go bid them farewell, which are at mine house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Philippians 3:13; Hebrews 10:38; 2 Peter 2:20–22"> And Iesus saide vnto him, No man that putteth his hand to the plough, and looketh backe, is apt to the kingdome of God.</span>
