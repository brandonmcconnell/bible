<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:2; Luke 20:20; Psalms 62:4"> And it came to passe that when he was entred into the house of one of the chiefe Pharises on the Sabbath day, to eate bread, they watched him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And beholde, there was a certaine man before him, which had the dropsie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:10; Mark 3:4; Luke 13:14–16"> Then Iesus answering, spake vnto the Lawyers and Pharises, saying, Is it lawfull to heale on the Sabbath day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Matthew 21:25–27"> And they held their peace. Then he tooke him, and healed him, and let him goe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 13:15; Exodus 23:4–5; Matthew 12:11–12"> And answered them, saying, Which of you shall haue an asse, or an oxe fallen into a pit, and wil not straightway pull him out on the Sabbath day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 20:40; Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10"> And they could not answere him againe to those things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Luke 11:43; Matthew 23:6"> He spake also a parable to the ghestes, when he marked howe they chose out the chiefe roomes, and said vnto them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7"> When thou shalt be bidden of any man to a wedding, set not thy selfe downe in the chiefest place, lest a more honourable man then thou, be bidden of him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 11:2; Proverbs 3:35"> And he that bade both him and thee, come, and say to thee, Giue this man roome, and thou then begin with shame to take the lowest roome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7; Proverbs 15:33; 1 Samuel 15:17"> But when thou art bidden, goe and sit downe in the lowest roome, that when he that bade thee, cometh, he may say vnto thee, Friende, sit vp hier: then shalt thou haue worship in the presence of them that sit at table with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 18:14; Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 29:23"> For whosoeuer exalteth himselfe, shall be brought lowe, and he that humbleth himselfe, shall be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:46; James 2:1–6; Luke 6:32–36"> Then said he also to him that had bidden him, When thou makest a dinner or a supper, call not thy friendes, nor thy brethren, neither thy kinsemen, nor ye riche neighbours, lest they also bid thee againe, and a recompence be made thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 14:21; Isaiah 58:10; Proverbs 14:31"> But when thou makest a feast, call ye poore, the maimed, the lame, and the blind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Acts 24:15; John 5:29"> And thou shalt be blessed, because they cannot recompense thee: for thou shalt be recompensed at the resurrection of the iust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; Luke 22:30; Luke 13:29"> Nowe when one of them that sate at table, heard these things, he said vnto him, Blessed is he that eateth bread in the kingdome of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 22:2–14; Revelation 22:17; Isaiah 55:1–7"> Then saide he to him, A certaine man made a great supper, and bade many,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:27–29; Matthew 10:1–4; Matthew 22:3–4"> And sent his seruant at supper time to say to them that were bidden, Come: for all things are nowe readie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 17:26–31; Luke 18:24"> But they all with one mind beganne to make excuse: The first saide vnto him, I haue bought a farme, and I must needes goe out and see it: I pray thee, haue me excused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And another said, I haue bought fiue yoke of oxen, and I goe to proue them: I pray thee, haue me excused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:5; 1 Corinthians 7:33; Luke 14:26–28"> And another said, I haue maried a wife, and therefore I can not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 14:13; Matthew 11:5; Luke 14:24"> So that seruaunt returned, and shewed his master these thinges. Then was the good man of the house angrie, and said to his seruant, Goe out quickely into the streetes and lanes of the citie, and bring in hither the poore, and the maimed, and the halt, and the blinde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 14:2; Ephesians 3:8; 1 Timothy 2:5–6"> And the seruaunt saide, Lord, it is done as thou hast commanded, and yet there is roome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:9–10; Colossians 1:28; Acts 13:47–48"> Then the master sayd to the seruaunt, Goe out into the hie wayes, and hedges, and compell them to come in, that mine house may bee filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Acts 13:46; John 8:24"> For I say vnto you, that none of those men which were bidden, shall taste of my supper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:24–27; Luke 12:1"> Nowe there went great multitudes with him, and he turned and sayd vnto them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; John 12:25; Philippians 3:8"> If any man come to mee, and hate not his father, and mother, and wife, and children, and brethren, and sisters: yea, and his owne life also, he can not be my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 9:23–25; Mark 8:34–37"> And whosoeuer beareth not his crosse, and commeth after mee, can not bee my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 24:27; Matthew 10:22; Joshua 24:19–24"> For which of you minding to builde a towre, sitteth not downe before, and counteth the cost, whether hee haue sufficient to performe it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Lest that after he hath laide the foundation, and is not able to performe it, all that behold it, begin to mocke him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 6:11; 2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:11–14"> Saying, This man began to builde, and was not able to make an end?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 25:8; 2 Kings 18:20–22"> Or what King going to make warre against another King, sitteth not downe first, and taketh counsell, whether he be able with ten thousande, to meete him that commeth against him with twentie thousand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 12:20; James 4:6–10; Luke 12:58"> Or els while hee is yet a great way off, hee sendeth an ambassage, and desireth peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; 1 John 2:15–16; Luke 5:28"> So likewise, whosoeuer hee be of you, that forsaketh not all that he hath, he cannot be my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:13; Colossians 4:6; Mark 9:49–50"> Salt is good: but if salt haue lost his sauour, wherewith shall it be salted?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Luke 9:44; Revelation 2:11"> It is neither meete for the land, nor yet for the dunghill, but men cast it out. He that hath eares to heare, let him heare.</span>
