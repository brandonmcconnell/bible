<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:1; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; James 3:17"> In the meane time, there gathered together an innumerable multitude of people, so that they trode one another: and he began to say vnto his disciples first, Take heede to your selues of the leauen of the Pharises, which is hypocrisie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 4:22; Luke 8:17; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> For there is nothing couered, that shall not bee reueiled: neither hidde, that shall not be knowen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:27; Matthew 12:36; Ecclesiastes 10:20"> Wherefore whatsoeuer yee haue spoken in darkenesse, it shall be heard in the light: and that which ye haue spoken in the eare, in secret places, shall be preached on the houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; Jeremiah 1:8; Revelation 2:10"> And I say vnto you, my friendes, be not afraide of them that kill the bodie, and after that are not able to doe any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Matthew 10:28; Revelation 14:7"> But I wil forewarne you, who ye shall feare: feare him which after hee hath killed, hath power to cast into hell: yea, I say vnto you, him feare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:24; Psalms 145:15–16; Psalms 147:9"> Are not fiue sparowes bought for two farthings, and yet not one of them is forgotten before God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:30–31; Matthew 6:26; Isaiah 43:3–4"> Yea, and all the heares of your head are nombred: feare not therefore: yee are more of value then many sparowes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 10:32–33; 1 John 2:23; Romans 10:9–10"> Also I say vnto you, Whosoeuer shall confesse mee before men, him shall the Sonne of man confesse also before the Angels of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:12; Luke 9:26; Mark 8:38"> But hee that shall denie mee before men, shall be denied before the Angels of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 12:31–32; 1 John 5:16; 1 Timothy 1:13"> And whosoeuer shall speake a woorde against the Sonne of man, it shall be forgiuen him: but vnto him, that shall blaspheme ye holy Ghost, it shall not be forgiuen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 10:17–20; Luke 21:12–14; Acts 4:5–7"> And when they shall bring you vnto the Synagogues, and vnto the rulers and Princes, take no thought howe, or what thing ye shall answere, or what yee shall speake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Matthew 10:20; Acts 4:8"> For the holy Ghost shall teache you in the same houre, what yee ought to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 6:45; 1 Timothy 6:5; Ezekiel 33:31"> And one of the companie said vnto him, Master, bidde my brother deuide the inheritance with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 2:3; Romans 2:1; Romans 9:20"> And he said vnto him, Man, who made me a iudge, or a deuider ouer you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; 1 Timothy 6:6–10; Psalms 119:36–37"> Wherefore he said vnto them, Take heede, and beware of couetousnesse: for though a man haue abundance, yet his life standeth not in his riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 26:12–14; Hosea 2:8; Genesis 41:47–49"> And he put foorth a parable vnto them, saying, The grounde of a certaine riche man brought foorth fruites plenteously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:42; Isaiah 58:7; Romans 12:13"> Therefore he thought with himselfe, saying, What shall I doe, because I haue no roume, where I may lay vp my fruites?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 3:15; James 4:15; Luke 12:21"> And he said, This wil I do, I wil pul downe my barnes, and builde greater, and therein will I gather all my fruites, and my goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; James 4:13–15; 1 Corinthians 15:32"> And I wil say to my soule, Soule, thou hast much goods laide vp for many yeeres: liue at ease, eate, drinke and take thy pastime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 27:8; Jeremiah 17:11; 1 Timothy 6:7"> But God said vnto him, O foole, this night wil they fetch away thy soule from thee: then whose shall those things be which thou hast prouided?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–20; Luke 12:33; James 2:5"> So is he that gathereth riches to himselfe, and is not riche in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 6:25–34; Philippians 4:6; Hebrews 13:5"> And he spake vnto his disciples, Therefore I say vnto you, Take no thought for your life, what yee shall eate: neither for your body, what yee shall put on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 2:6; Proverbs 13:8; Job 2:4"> The life is more then meate: and the body more then the raiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:31; Psalms 147:9; Luke 12:7"> Consider the rauens: for they neither sowe nor reape: which neither haue storehouse nor barne, and yet God feedeth them: how much more are yee better then foules?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 6:27; Matthew 5:36; Luke 19:3"> And which of you with taking thought, can adde to his stature one cubite?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Psalms 39:6; Ecclesiastes 7:13"> If yee then bee not able to doe the least thing, why take yee thought for the remnant?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 6:28–30; Luke 12:24; 1 Kings 10:1–13"> Consider the lilies howe they growe: they labour not, neither spin they: yet I say vnto you, that Salomon himselfe in all his royaltie was not clothed like one of these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 14:31; Isaiah 40:6; Matthew 6:30"> If then God so clothe the grasse which is to day in the field, and to morowe is cast into the ouen, howe much more will he clothe you, O yee of litle faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 6:31; Luke 12:22; Luke 22:35"> Therefore aske not what yee shall eate, or what ye shall drinke, neither hag you in suspense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 6:32; Matthew 6:8; Ephesians 4:17"> For all such things the people of the world seeke for: and your Father knoweth that ye haue neede of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 34:9"> But rather seeke ye after the kingdome of God, and all these things shalbe cast vpon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 12:28; 1 Peter 1:3–5; Revelation 1:6"> Feare not, litle flocke: for it is your Fathers pleasure, to giue you the kingdome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 19:21; Acts 4:34–35; Acts 2:45"> Sell that ye haue, and giue almes: make you bagges, which waxe not old, a treasure that can neuer faile in heauen, where no theefe commeth, neither mothe corrupteth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–3; Matthew 6:21; Philippians 3:20"> For where your treasure is, there will your hearts be also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Peter 1:13; Ephesians 6:14; Matthew 25:4–10"> Let your loynes be gird about and your lights burning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jude 1:20–21; James 5:7–8; Revelation 3:20"> And ye your selues like vnto men that waite for their master, when he will returne from the wedding, that when he commeth and knocketh, they may open vnto him immediatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Peter 3:14; Matthew 24:42; John 13:4–5"> Blessed are those seruants, whom the Lord when he commeth shall finde waking: verely I say vnto you, he will girde himselfe about, and make them to sit downe at table, and will come forth, and serue them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:4–5; Matthew 25:6"> And if he come in the seconde watch, or come in the third watch, and shall finde them so, blessed are those seruants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Revelation 3:3; Revelation 16:15"> Nowe vnderstand this, that if the good man of the house had knowen at what houre the theefe would haue come, he would haue watched, and would not haue suffered his house to be digged through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6; Matthew 25:13; Romans 13:11"> Be ye also prepared therefore: for the Sonne of man will come at an houre when ye thinke not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 13:37; 1 Peter 4:7; 1 Peter 5:8"> Then Peter saide vnto him, Master, tellest thou this parable vnto vs, or euen to all?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 24:45–46; 1 Peter 5:1–4; Hebrews 13:17"> And the Lord saide, Who is a faithfull steward and wise, whom the master shall make ruler ouer his householde, to giue them their portion of meate in season?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Blessed is that seruant, whom his master when he commeth, shall finde so doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 22:29–30; Matthew 24:47; Luke 19:17–19"> Of a trueth I say vnto you, that he wil make him ruler ouer all that he hath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 24:48–50; Revelation 18:7–8; Philippians 3:18–19"> But if that seruant say in his heart, My master doeth deferre his comming, and ginne to smite the seruants, and maydens, and to eate, and drinke, and to be drunken,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 12:40; Matthew 24:51; Psalms 11:5"> The master of that seruant will come in a day when he thinketh not, and at an houre when he is not ware of, and will cut him off, and giue him his portion with the vnbeleeuers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="James 4:17; John 9:41; John 12:48"> And that seruant that knewe his masters will, and prepared not himselfe, neither did according to his will, shalbe beaten with many stripes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="James 3:1; Leviticus 5:17; Luke 16:10–12"> But he that knewe it not, and yet did commit things worthy of stripes, shall be beaten with fewe stripes: for vnto whomsoeuer much is giuen, of him shalbe much required, and to whom men much commit, the more of him will they aske.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Luke 12:51–52; Matthew 3:10–12"> I am come to put fire on the earth, and what is my desire, if it be already kindled?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 12:27–28; John 19:30; John 7:6–8"> Notwithstanding I must be baptized with a baptisme, and how am I grieued, till it be ended?</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 10:34–36; Luke 12:49; Matthew 24:7–10"> Thinke ye that I am come to giue peace on earth? I tell you, nay, but rather debate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Acts 14:1–4; John 15:18–21; Micah 7:5–6"> For from hencefoorth there shall be fiue in one house deuided, three against two, and two against three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Micah 7:6; Matthew 10:21–22; Matthew 24:10"> The father shalbe deuided against ye sonne, and the sonne against the father: the mother against the daughter, and the daughter against the mother: the mother in lawe against her daughter in lawe, and the daughter in lawe against her mother in lawe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 16:2–4; 1 Kings 18:44–45"> Then said he to the people, When ye see a cloude rise out of the West, straightway ye say, A shower commeth: and so it is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Job 37:17; Matthew 20:12"> And when ye see the South winde blowe, ye say, that it wilbe hoate: and it commeth to passe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 16:3; Matthew 24:32–33; Matthew 11:25"> Hypocrites, ye can discerne the face of the earth, and of the skie: but why discerne ye not this time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; John 7:24; Acts 13:26–38"> Yea, and why iudge ye not of your selues what is right?</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Proverbs 25:8–9; Matthew 5:23–26; Luke 13:24–28"> While thou goest with thine aduersarie to the ruler, as thou art in the way, giue diligence in the way, that thou mayest be deliuered from him, least he drawe thee to the iudge, and the iudge deliuer thee to the iayler, and the iayler cast thee into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Mark 12:42; Matthew 18:34; Matthew 25:46"> I tell thee, thou shalt not depart thence, till thou hast payed the vtmost mite.</span>
