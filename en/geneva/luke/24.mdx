<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 20:1–2; Mark 16:1–8; Matthew 28:1–8"> Nowe the first day of the weeke early in the morning, they came vnto the sepulchre, and brought the odours, which they had prepared, and certaine women with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:3–4; Matthew 27:60–66; Mark 15:46–47"> And they found the stone rolled away from the sepulchre,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:23; John 20:6–7; Matthew 16:5"> And went in, but found not the body of the Lord Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 16:5; John 20:11–12; Acts 1:10"> And it came to passe, that as they were amased thereat, beholde, two men suddenly stood by them in shining vestures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 16:5–6; Matthew 28:3–5; Revelation 1:18"> And as they were afraide, and bowed downe their faces to the earth, they sayd to them, Why seeke ye him that liueth, among the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 17:22–23; Luke 9:22; Mark 8:31"> He is not here, but is risen: remember how he spake vnto you, when he was yet in Galile,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 16:21"> Saying, that the sonne of man must be deliuered into the hands of sinfull men, and be crucified, and the third day rise againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 12:16; John 14:26; John 2:19–22"> And they remembred his wordes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 24:22–24; Mark 16:7–8; Matthew 28:7–8"> And returned from the sepulchre, and tolde all these things vnto the eleuen, and to all the remnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 27:56; John 20:11–18; Luke 8:2–3"> Now it was Mary Magdalene, and Ioanna, and Mary the mother of Iames, and other women with them, which tolde these things vnto the Apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 9:16; Mark 16:11; Genesis 19:14"> But their wordes seemed vnto them, as a fained thing, neither beleeued they them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 20:3–10"> Then arose Peter, and ran vnto the sepulchre, and looked in, and saw the linnen clothes laide by themselues, and departed wondering in himselfe at that which was come to passe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 24:18; Mark 16:12–13"> And beholde, two of them went that same day to a towne which was from Hierusalem about threescore furlongs, called Emmaus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:45; Deuteronomy 6:7; Malachi 3:6"> And they talked together of al these things that were done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:20; John 14:18–19; Luke 24:36"> And it came to passe, as they communed together, and reasoned, that Iesus himselfe drewe neere, and went with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 20:14; John 21:4; Luke 24:31"> But their eyes were holden, that they could not know him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:20–22; John 16:6; Ezekiel 9:4–6"> And he sayd vnto them, What maner of communications are these that ye haue one to another as ye walke and are sad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 19:25"> And the one (named Cleopas) answered, and sayd vnto him, Art thou onely a stranger in Hierusalem, and hast not knowen the things which are come to passe therein in these dayes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; Acts 7:22; John 4:19"> And he said vnto them, What things? And they sayd vnto him, Of Iesus of Nazareth, which was a Prophet, mightie in deede and in word before God, and all people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:13–15; Acts 4:8–10; Luke 23:13"> And howe the hie Priests, and our rulers deliuered him to be condemned to death, and haue crucified him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:68; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Luke 2:38"> But we trusted that it had bene he that should haue deliuered Israel, and as touching all these things, to day is ye third day, that they were done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 16:9–10; Matthew 28:7–8; John 20:1–2"> Yea, and certaine women among vs made vs astonied, which came early vnto the sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And when they found not his body, they came, saying, that they had also seene a vision of Angels, which sayd, that he was aliue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 24:12; John 20:1–10"> Therefore certaine of them which were with vs, went to the sepulchre, and found it euen so as the women had sayd, but him they saw not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 7:18; Mark 9:19; Hebrews 5:11–12"> Then he sayd vnto them, O fooles and slowe of heart to beleeue all that the Prophets haue spoken!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:11; Isaiah 53:1–12; Zechariah 13:7"> Ought not Christ to haue suffered these things, and to enter into his glory?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Luke 24:44; Deuteronomy 18:15"> And he began at Moses, and at all the Prophets, and interpreted vnto them in all the Scriptures the things which were written of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 6:48; Genesis 19:2; Genesis 32:26"> And they drew neere vnto ye towne, which they went to, but he made as though hee would haue gone further.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 4:8; Luke 14:23; Genesis 19:3"> But they constrained him, saying, Abide with vs: for it is towards night, and the day is farre spent. So he went in to tarie with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; Luke 24:35; Matthew 15:36"> And it came to passe, as hee sate at table with them, he tooke the bread, and blessed, and brake it, and gaue it to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 8:59; Luke 4:30"> Then their eyes were opened, and they knewe him: and he was no more seene of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 24:45; Jeremiah 23:29; Hebrews 4:12"> And they saide betweene themselues, Did not our heartes burne within vs, while he talked with vs by the way, and when he opened to vs the Scriptures?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 16:13; John 20:19–26; Acts 1:14"> And they rose vp the same houre, and returned to Hierusalem, and found the Eleuen gathered together, and them that were with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5; Mark 16:7; Luke 22:54–62"> Which said, The Lord is risen in deede, and hath appeared to Simon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 16:12–13; Acts 2:42; Luke 24:30–31"> Then they tolde what things were done in the way, and howe he was knowen of them in breaking of bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:16; Isaiah 57:18; Mark 16:14"> And as they spake these things, Iesus himselfe stoode in the middes of them, and saide vnto them, Peace be to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:13; Matthew 14:26–27; Job 4:14–16"> But they were abashed and afraide, supposing that they had seene a spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:14; Hebrews 4:13; Daniel 4:19"> Then he saide vnto them, Why are ye troubled? and wherefore doe doutes arise in your hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 20:27; 1 John 1:1; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> Beholde mine handes and my feete: for it is I my selfe: handle me, and see: for a spirit hath not flesh and bones, as ye see me haue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And when he had thus spoken, he shewed them his hands and feete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 21:5; Psalms 126:1–2; Genesis 45:26–28"> And while they yet beleeued not for ioy, and wondred, he saide vnto them, Haue ye here any meate?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And they gaue him a piece of a broyled fish, and of an honie combe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 10:41"> And hee tooke it, and did eate before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Luke 24:26–27; Zechariah 9:9"> And he saide vnto them, These are the wordes, which I spake vnto you while I was yet with you, that all must be fulfilled which are written of me in the Lawe of Moses, and in the Prophets, and in the Psalmes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Acts 16:14; 1 John 5:20; Psalms 119:18"> Then opened he their vnderstanding, that they might vnderstand the Scriptures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–12; Isaiah 50:6; Luke 24:44"> And said vnto them, Thus is it written, and thus it behoued Christ to suffer, and to rise againe from the dead the third day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 5:31; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 2:38"> And that repentance, and remission of sinnes should be preached in his Name among all nations, beginning at Hierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 2:32; Acts 13:31"> Nowe ye are witnesses of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 1:4; Joel 2:28–32"> And beholde, I doe sende the promise of my Father vpon you: but tary ye in the citie of Hierusalem, vntill ye be endued with power from an hie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Mark 11:1"> Afterward he lead them out into Bethania, and lift vp his hands, and blessed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Acts 1:9; Mark 16:19; 2 Kings 2:11"> And it came to passe, that as he blessed them, he departed from them, and was caried vp into heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 28:9; John 16:22; Psalms 30:11"> And they worshipped him, and returned to Hierusalem with great ioy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Mark 16:20; Acts 2:46–47; Acts 5:41–42"> And were continually in the Temple, praysing, and lauding God, Amen.</span>
