<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 1:16–20; Matthew 4:18–22; Mark 3:9"> Then it came to passe, as the people preassed vpon him to heare the word of God, that he stoode by the lake of Gennesaret,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 1:19; Matthew 4:21"> And sawe two shippes stand by the lakes side, but the fishermen were gone out of them, and were washing their nettes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 4:18; Matthew 13:1–2; John 1:41–42"> And he entred into one of the ships, which was Simons, and required him that he would thrust off a litle from the land: and he sate downe, and taught the people out of the ship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 21:6; Matthew 17:27"> Now when he had left speaking, he sayd vnto Simon, Lanch out into the deepe, and let downe your nettes to make a draught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 2:5; John 15:14; Luke 6:46–48"> Then Simon answered, and sayd vnto him, Master, we haue trauailed sore all night, and haue taken nothing: neuerthelesse at thy worde I will let downe the net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; John 21:6–11; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> And when they had so done, they enclosed a great multitude of fishes, so that their net brake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 6:2; Philippians 4:3; Acts 11:25"> And they beckened to their parteners, which were in the other ship, that they shoulde come and helpe them, who came then, and filled both the ships, that they did sinke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:5; Job 42:5–6; Revelation 1:17"> Now when Simon Peter saw it, he fel down at Iesus knees, saying, Lord, go from me: for I am a sinfull man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 4:32; Psalms 8:8; Mark 9:6"> For he was vtterly astonied, and all that were with him, for the draught of fishes which they tooke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 4:19; Matthew 13:47; Ezekiel 47:9–10"> And so was also Iames and Iohn the sonnes of Zebedeus, which were companions with Simon. Then Iesus sayde vnto Simon, Feare not: from henceforth thou shalt catch men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 4:20; Luke 5:28; Matthew 19:27"> And when they had brought the ships to land, they forsooke all, and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Luke 17:16; Matthew 8:2–4"> Nowe it came to passe, as he was in a certaine citie, beholde, there was a man full of leprosie, and when he sawe Iesus, he fell on his face, and besought him, saying, Lord, if thou wilt, thou canst make me cleane.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:29–30; John 4:50–53; Hosea 14:4"> So he stretched forth his hand, and touched him, saying, I will, be thou cleane. And immediately the leprosie departed from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 14:2–32; Luke 17:14; Mark 1:44"> And he commanded him that hee should tell it no man: but Go, sayth he, and shew thy selfe to the Priest, and offer for thy clensing, as Moses hath commanded, for a witnes vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 15:30–31; Luke 12:1; Mark 1:45–2"> But so much more went there a fame abroad of him, and great multitudes came together to heare, and to be healed of him of their infirmities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 1:35–36; Matthew 14:23"> But he kept himselfe apart in the wildernes, and prayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 8:46; Matthew 11:5"> And it came to passe, on a certaine day, as he was teaching, that the Pharises and doctours of the Law sate by, which were come out of euery towne of Galile, and Iudea, and Hierusalem, and the power of the Lord was in him to heale them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 2:3–12; Matthew 9:2–8; John 5:5–6"> Then beholde, men brought a man lying in a bed, which was taken with a palsie, and they sought meanes to bring him in, and to lay him before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 2:4; Matthew 10:27; Matthew 24:17"> And when they could not finde by what way they might bring him in, because of the preasse, they went vp on the house, and let him downe through the tyling, bed and all, in the middes before Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 14:9; Matthew 9:2; Mark 2:5"> And when he sawe their faith, he sayd vnto him, Man, thy sinnes are forgiuen thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Mark 2:6–7; Luke 7:49"> Then the Scribes and the Pharises began to reason, saying, Who is this that speaketh blasphemies? who can forgiue sinnes, but God onely?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 24:38; Isaiah 66:18; Psalms 139:2"> But when Iesus perceiued their reasoning, he answered, and sayd vnto them, What reason ye in your hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 2:9; Matthew 9:5"> Whether is easier to say, Thy sinnes are forgiuen thee, or to say, Rise and walke?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Isaiah 53:11; Luke 8:54"> But that ye may know that that Sonne of man hath authoritie to forgiue sinnes in earth, (he sayd vnto the sicke of the palsie) I say to thee, Arise: take vp thy bed, and goe to thine house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 9:24; Luke 5:13; Psalms 107:20–22"> And immediatly he rose vp before them, and tooke vp his bed whereon he lay, and departed to his owne house, praysing God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Matthew 12:23; Luke 7:16"> And they were all amased, and praysed God, and were filled with feare, saying, Doutlesse we haue seene strange things to day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 2:13–22; Matthew 9:9–17; John 1:43"> And after that, he went foorth and sawe a Publicane named Leui, sitting at the receite of custome, and sayd vnto him, Follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 5:11; Matthew 19:22–27; 1 Kings 19:19–21"> And he left all, rose vp, and folowed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 2:15; Luke 15:1; 1 Corinthians 10:27"> Then Leui made him a great feast in his owne house, where there was a great company of Publicanes, and of other that sate at table with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 21:28–32; Luke 5:17; Mark 7:3"> But they that were Scribes and Pharises among them, murmured against his disciples, saying, Why eate ye and drinke ye with Publicanes and sinners?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Matthew 9:12–13; Jeremiah 8:22"> Then Iesus answered, and sayd vnto them, They that are whole, neede not the Physician, but they that are sicke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Luke 15:10; 1 Timothy 1:15–16"> I came not to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 9:14–17; Mark 2:18–22; John 3:25"> Then they said vnto him, Why do the disciples of Iohn fast often, and pray, and the disciples of the Pharises also, but thine eate and drinke?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 3:29; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 54:5"> And he said vnto them, Can ye make the children of the wedding chamber to fast, as long as the bridegrome is with them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 17:22; Acts 14:23; Acts 3:21"> But the dayes will come, euen when the bridegrome shalbe taken away from them: then shall they fast in those dayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 9:16–17; Mark 2:21–22; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> Againe he spake also vnto them a parable, No man putteth a piece of a newe garment into an olde vesture: for then the newe renteth it, and the piece taken out of the newe, agreeth not with the olde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 9:13; Psalms 119:83; Joshua 9:4"> Also no man powreth newe wine into olde vessels: for then ye new wine wil breake the vessels, and it will runne out, and the vessels will perish:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 8:8–13; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ezekiel 36:26"> But newe wine must be powred into newe vessels: so both are preserued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39; Mark 7:7–13; Jeremiah 6:16"> Also no man that drinketh olde wine, straightway desireth newe: for he sayth, The olde is more profitable.</span>
