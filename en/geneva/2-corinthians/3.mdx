<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:11; 2 Corinthians 10:12; 2 Corinthians 5:12"> Doe we begin to praise our selues againe? or neede we as some other, epistles of recommendation vnto you, or letters of recommendation from you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:1–2; 1 Thessalonians 1:8; Romans 1:8"> Yee are our epistle, written in our hearts, which is vnderstand, and read of all men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Jeremiah 31:33; Hebrews 8:10"> In that yee are manifest, to be the Epistle of Christ, ministred by vs, and written, not with yncke, but with the Spirite of the liuing God, not in tables of stone, but in fleshly tables of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Ephesians 3:12; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> And such trust haue we through Christ to God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9; Philippians 4:13; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> Not that we are sufficient of our selues, to thinke any thing, as of our selues: but our sufficiencie is of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 6:63; Romans 7:6; Jeremiah 31:31"> Who also hath made vs able ministers of the Newe testament, not of the letter, but of the Spirite: for the letter killeth, but the Spirite giueth life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:9–11; Romans 10:4; Exodus 31:18"> If then the ministration of death written with letters and ingrauen in stones, was glorious, so that the children of Israel coulde not beholde the face of Moses, for the glorie of his countenance (which glorie is done away.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:18; Galatians 3:14"> Howe shall not the ministration of the Spirite be more glorious?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 3:21–22; Hebrews 12:18–21; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For if the ministerie of condemnation was glorious, much more doeth the ministration of righteousnesse exceede in glorie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 1:17; Haggai 2:7–9; Acts 26:13"> For euen that which was glorified, was not glorified in this point, that is, as touching the exceeding glorie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 5:20–21; Hebrews 8:13; Hebrews 7:21–25"> For if that which should be abolished, was glorious, much more shall that which remaineth, be glorious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:4; Acts 4:13; Philippians 1:20"> Seeing then that we haue such trust, we vse great boldnesse of speach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 34:33–35; Hebrews 10:1–9; Ephesians 2:14–15"> And we are not as Moses, which put a vaile vpon his face, that the children of Israel should not looke vnto the ende of that which should be abolished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3–4; Acts 26:18; Romans 11:25"> Therefore their mindes are hardened: for vntill this day remaineth the same couering vntaken away in the reading of the olde Testament, which vaile in Christ is put away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 13:27–29"> But euen vnto this day, whe Moses is read, the vaile is laid ouer their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 34:34; Isaiah 29:18; Isaiah 25:7"> Neuertheles when their heart shall be turned to the Lord, the vaile shalbe taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:2; Galatians 5:13; John 8:32"> Nowe the Lord is the Spirite, and where the Spirite of the Lord is, there is libertie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:6; Ephesians 4:22–24; Romans 12:2"> But we al behold as in a mirrour the glory of the Lord with open face, and are changed into the same image, from glorie to glorie, as by the Spirit of the Lord.</span>
