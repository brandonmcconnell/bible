<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:5–7; 2 Kings 24:1–2; 2 Kings 24:13"> In the thirde yeere of the reigne of Iehoiakim king of Iudah, came Nebuchad-nezzar King of Babel vnto Ierusalem and besieged it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:7; Zechariah 5:11; Jeremiah 27:19–20"> And ye Lord gaue Iehoiakim king of Iudah into his hand; with parte of the vessels of the house of God, which he caryed into the land of Shinar, to the house of his god, and he brought the vessels into his gods treasurie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 39:7; 2 Kings 20:17–18; 2 Kings 24:15"> And the King spake vnto Ashpenaz the master of his Eunuches, that he shoulde bring certeine of the children of Israel, of the Kings seede, and of the princes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 5:11; Acts 7:22; Proverbs 22:29"> Children in whome was no blemish, but well fauoured, and instruct in all wisedome, and well seene in knowledge, and able to vtter knowledge, and such as were able to stande in the kings palace, and whome they might teach the learning, and the tongue of the Caldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 1:19; 1 Kings 10:8; Daniel 1:8"> And the King appointed them prouision euery day of a portion of the Kings meate, and and of the wine, which he dranke, so nourishing them three yeere, that at the ende thereof, they might stande before the King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:14; Matthew 24:15; Daniel 2:17"> Nowe among these were certeine of the children of Iudah, Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael and Azariah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 5:12; Daniel 4:8; Daniel 2:49"> Vnto whome the chiefe of the Eunuches gaue other names: for hee called Daniel, Belteshazzar, and Hananiah, Shadrach, and Mishael, Meshach, and Azariah, Abednego.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 141:4; Ezekiel 4:13–14; Leviticus 11:45–47"> But Daniel had determined in his heart, that hee woulde not defile him selfe with the portion of the Kings meate, nor with the wine which he dranke: therefore he required the chiefe of the Eunuches that he might not defile himselfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 39:21; Proverbs 16:7; Psalms 106:46"> (Nowe God had brought Daniel into fauour, and tender loue with the chiefe of the Eunuches)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 6:16–18; John 12:42–43; Proverbs 29:25"> And the chiefe of the Eunuches sayd vnto Daniel, I feare my lord the King, who hath appointed your meate and your drinke: therefore if he see your faces worse liking then the other children, which are of your sort, then shall you make me lose mine head vnto the King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then sayd Daniel to Melzar, whome the chiefe of the Eunuches had set ouer Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 14:2; Daniel 1:16; Genesis 1:29–30"> Proue thy seruants, I beseeche thee, ten dayes, and let them giue vs pulse to eate, and water to drinke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then let our countenances bee looked vpon before thee, and the countenances of the children that eate of the portion of the Kings meate: and as thou seest, deale with thy seruantes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So hee consented to them in this matter, an proued them ten dayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 23:25; Proverbs 10:22; 2 Kings 4:42–44"> And at the end of ten dayes, their countenances appeared fayrer, and in better liking then all the childrens, which did eate the portion of the Kings meate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 1:12"> Thus Melzar tooke away the portion of their meat, and the wine that they should drinke, and gaue them pulse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12; James 1:5; Daniel 2:21"> As for these foure children, God gaue them knowledge, and vnderstanding in al learning and wisedome: also he gaue Daniel vnderstanding of all visions and dreames.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 1:5"> Nowe when the time was expired, that the King had appoynted to bring them in, the chiefe of the Eunuches brought them before Nebuchad-nezzar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29; Genesis 41:46; Daniel 1:5"> And the King communed with them: and among them al was found none like Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah: therefore stoode they before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 8:19; Isaiah 47:12–14; 1 Kings 4:29–34"> And in all matters of wisedome, and vnderstanding that the King enquired of them, hee founde them tenne times better then all the inchanters and astrologians, that were in all his realme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 6:28; Daniel 10:1"> And Daniel was vnto the first yeere of king Cyrus.</span>
