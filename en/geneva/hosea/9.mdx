<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Hosea 4:12; Jeremiah 44:17"> Rejoice not, O Israel for ioy as other people: for thou hast gone a whoring from thy God: thou hast loued a rewarde vpon euery corne floore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 2:9; Micah 6:13–16; Joel 1:3–7"> The floore, and the wine presse shall not feede them, and the newe wine shall faile in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:13; Hosea 8:13; Leviticus 25:23"> They wil not dwel in the Lordes lande, but Ephraim will returne to Egypt, and they will eate vncleane things in Asshur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Deuteronomy 26:14; Jeremiah 6:20"> They shall not offer wine to the Lord, neither shall their sacrifices be pleasant vnto him: but they shall be vnto them as the bread of mourners: al that eate thereof, shalbe polluted: for their bread for their soules shall not come into the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 10:3; Jeremiah 5:31; Hosea 2:11"> What wil ye do then in the solemne day, and in the day of the feast of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 10:8; Isaiah 5:6; Isaiah 7:23"> For loe, they are gone from destruction: but Egypt shall gather them vp, and Memphis shall burie them: the nettle shall possesse the pleasant places of their siluer, and the thorne shall be in their tabernacles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 26:24–25; Micah 7:4; 2 Kings 9:11"> The daies of visitation are come: the daies of recompence are come: Israel shall knowe it: the Prophet is a foole: the spiritual man is mad, for the multitude of thine iniquitie: therefore the hatred is great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 5:1; Ezekiel 3:17; Isaiah 62:6"> The watchman of Ephraim shoulde bee with my God: but the Prophet is the snare of a fouler in all his waies, and hatred in the House of his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 10:9; Judges 19:16–21; Hosea 8:13"> They are deepely set: they are corrupt as in the daies of Gibeah: therefore he will remember their iniquitie, he will visite their sinnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:13; Hosea 4:14; Numbers 25:1–18"> I found Israel like grapes in the wildernes: I saw your fathers as the first ripe in the figge tree at her first time: but they went to Baal-Peor, and separated themselues vnto that shame, and their abominations were according to their louers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:18; Hosea 9:14; Hosea 4:7"> Ephraim their glorie shall flee away like a birde: from the birth and from the wombe, and from the conception.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 7:13; Deuteronomy 32:25; Deuteronomy 31:17"> Though they bring vp their children, yet I will depriue them from being men: yea, woe to them, when I depart from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:1–21; Ezekiel 27:3; Hosea 13:8"> Ephraim, as I sawe, is as a tree in Tyrus planted in a cottage: but Ephraim shall bring forth his children to the murtherer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:29; 1 Corinthians 7:26; Mark 13:17"> O Lord, giue them: what wilt thou giue them? giue them a baren wombe and drie breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Isaiah 1:23; Amos 5:5"> All their wickednesse is in Gilgal: for there doe I hate them: for the wickednesse of their inuentions, I will cast them out of mine House: I will loue them no more: all their princes are rebels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 9:11–13; Ezekiel 24:21; Isaiah 5:24"> Ephraim is smitten, their roote is dried vp: they can bring no fruite: yea, though they bring foorth, yet will I slaie euen the dearest of their bodie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 7:13; Deuteronomy 28:64–65; Acts 3:23"> My God will cast them away, because they did not obey him: and they shall wander among the nations.</span>
