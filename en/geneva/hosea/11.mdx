<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 2:15; Exodus 4:22; Hosea 13:4"> When Israel was a childe, then I loued him, and called my sonne out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:15; Hosea 2:13; Isaiah 65:7"> They called them, but they went thus from them: they sacrificed vnto Baalim, and burnt incense to images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:17; Deuteronomy 1:31; Exodus 15:26"> I ledde Ephraim also, as one shoulde beare them in his armes: but they knewe not that I healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:32; Leviticus 26:13; 2 Corinthians 5:14"> I led them with cordes of a man, euen with bandes of loue, and I was to them, as hee that taketh off the yoke from their iawes, and I laide the meat vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 7:16; Hosea 8:13; 2 Kings 17:13–14"> He shall no more returne into the lande of Egypt: but Asshur shalbe his King, because they refused to conuert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; Hosea 10:6; Hosea 10:14"> And the sworde shall fall on his cities, and shall consume his barres, and deuoure them, because of their owne counsels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:5; Amos 5:14–15; Hosea 7:16"> And my people are bent to rebellion against me: though they called them to the most hie, yet none at all would exalt him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:20; Matthew 23:37; Hosea 6:4"> Howe shall I giue thee vp, Ephraim? howe shall I deliuer thee, Israel? how shall I make thee, as Admah? howe shall I set thee, as Zeboim? mine heart is turned within mee: my repentings are rouled together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 55:8–9; Micah 7:18–20"> I wil not execute ye fiercenesse of my wrath: I will not returne to destroy Ephraim: for I am God, and not man, the holy one in the middes of thee, and I will not enter into the citie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 31:4; Joel 3:16; Amos 1:2"> They shall walke after the Lord: he shall roare like a lyon: when hee shall roare, then the children of the West shall feare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 11:11; Isaiah 60:8; Zechariah 10:10"> They shall feare as a sparrow out of Egypt, and as a doue of the lande of Asshur, and I will place them in their houses, sayth the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 12:7; Revelation 3:21; 2 Chronicles 29:1–32"> Ephraim copasseth me about with lies, and the house of Israel with deceit: but Iudah yet ruleth with God, and is faithfull with the Saints.</span>
