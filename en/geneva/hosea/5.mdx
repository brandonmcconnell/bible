<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 9:8; Micah 7:2; Micah 3:9"> O ye Priestes, heare this, and hearken ye, O house of Israel, and giue ye eare, O house of the King: for iudgement is towarde you, because you haue bene a snare on Mizpah, and a net spred vpon Tabor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 9:15; Isaiah 29:15; Hosea 6:9"> Yet they were profounde, to decline to slaughter, though I haue bene a rebuker of them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 3:2; Hebrews 4:13; Hosea 13:1"> I knowe Ephraim, and Israel is not hid from me: for nowe, O Ephraim thou art become an harlot, and Israel is defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:12; 1 John 2:3–4; Hosea 4:1"> They will not giue their mindes to turne vnto their God: for the spirit of fornication is in the middes of them, and they haue not knowen the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 7:10; Amos 5:2; Ezekiel 23:31–35"> And the pride of Israel doth testifie to his face: therefore shall Israel and Ephraim fall in their iniquitie: Iudah also shall fall with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; John 7:34; Proverbs 1:28"> They shall goe with their sheepe, and with their bullockes to seeke the Lord: but they shall not finde him: for he hath withdrawne himselfe from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 6:7; Isaiah 48:8; Jeremiah 3:20"> They haue transgressed against the Lord: for they haue begotte strange children: now shall a moneth deuoure them with their portions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Hosea 9:9; 1 Samuel 15:34"> Blowe ye the trumpet in Gibeah, and the shaume in Ramah: crie out at Beth-auen, after thee, O Beniamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 1:6; Isaiah 46:10; Hosea 9:11–17"> Ephraim shall be desolate in the day of rebuke: among the tribes of Israel haue I caused to knowe the trueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Deuteronomy 27:17; Psalms 93:3–4"> The princes of Iudah were like them that remoue the bounde: therefore will I powre out my wrath vpon them like water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Micah 6:16; 1 Kings 12:26–33"> Ephraim is oppressed, and broken in iudgement, because he willingly walked after the commandement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 51:8; Job 13:28; Isaiah 50:9"> Therefore wil I be vnto Ephraim as a moth, and to the house of Iudah as a rottennesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 7:11; Hosea 12:1; Hosea 10:6"> When Ephraim sawe his sickenes, and Iudah his wound, then went Ephraim vnto Asshur, and sent vnto King Iareb: yet coulde hee not heale you, nor cure you of your wound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 7:2; Micah 5:8; Hosea 13:7–8"> For I will be vnto Ephraim as a lyon, and as a lyons whelpe to the house of Iudah: I, euen I will spoyle, and goe away: I will take away, and none shall rescue it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 26:16; Jeremiah 29:12–14; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> I will go, and returne to my place, til they acknowledge their fault, and seeke me: in their affliction they will seeke me diligently.</span>
