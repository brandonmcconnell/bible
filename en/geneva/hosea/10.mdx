<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 8:11; Leviticus 26:1; Philippians 2:21"> Israel is a emptie vine, yet hath it brought foorth fruite vnto it selfe, and according to the multitude of the fruite thereof he hath increased the altars: according to the goodnesse of their lande they haue made faire images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 5:13; 1 Kings 18:21; Zephaniah 1:5"> Their heart is deuided: nowe shall they be founde faultie: he shall breake downe their altars: he shall destroy their images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 10:15; Hosea 10:7; Hosea 13:11"> For now they shall say, We haue no King because we feared not the Lord: and what should a King doe to vs?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 5:7; Amos 6:12; Hosea 4:2"> They haue spoken woordes, swearing falsly in making a couenant: thus iudgement groweth as wormewoode in the furrowes of the fielde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 9:11; Hosea 5:8; 2 Kings 23:5"> The inhabitants of Samaria shall feare because of the calfe of Beth-auen: for the people thereof shall mourne ouer it, and the Chemarims thereof, that reioyced on it for the glorie thereof, because it is departed from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 5:13; Isaiah 30:3; Daniel 11:8"> It shall bee also brought to Asshur, for a present vnto King Iareb: Ephraim shall receiue shame, and Israel shall be ashamed of his owne counsell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 10:3; Jude 1:13; 2 Kings 1:3"> Of Samaria, the King thereof is destroyed as the some vpon the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 23:30; Revelation 6:16; Isaiah 2:19"> The hie places also of Auen shall be destroied, euen the sinne of Israel: the thorne and the thistle shall growe vpon their altars, and they shall say to the mountaines, Couer vs, and to the hils, Fall vpon vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Judges 20:5; Judges 19:22–30"> O Israel, thou hast sinned from the daies of Gibeah: there they stoode: the battell in Gibeah against the children of iniquitie did not touch them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Hosea 4:9; Jeremiah 16:16"> It is my desire that I should chastice them, and the people shall be gathered against them, when they shall gather themselues in their two furrowes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 4:16; Jeremiah 50:11; Deuteronomy 25:4"> And Ephraim is as an heifer vsed to delite in threshing: but I will passe by her faire necke: I will make Ephraim to ride: Iudah shall plowe, and Iaakob shall breake his cloddes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3–4; James 3:18; Isaiah 45:8"> Sowe to your selues in righteousnes: reape after the measure of mercy: breake vp your fallowe grounde: for it is time to seeke the Lord, till he come and raine righteousnesse vpon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 4:8; Galatians 6:7–8; Proverbs 1:31"> But you haue plowed wickednesse: ye haue reaped iniquitie: you haue eaten the fruite of lies: because thou didest trust in thine owne waies, and in the multitude of thy strong men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; 2 Kings 18:9–10; Amos 3:8"> Therefore shall a tumult arise among thy people, and all thy munitions shall be destroyed, as Shalman destroyed Beth-arbell in the daie of battell: the mother with the children was dashed in pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Hosea 10:7; Romans 7:13"> So shall Beth-el doe vnto you, because of your malicious wickednes: in a morning shall the King of Israel be destroied.</span>
