<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Jeremiah 13:27; Proverbs 1:22"> Thy calfe, O Samaria, hath cast thee off: mine anger is kindled against them: howe long will they be without innocencie!</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:28; Jeremiah 51:34; Romans 9:22"> Israel is deuoured, now shall they be among the Gentiles as a vessell wherein is no pleasure.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:37; Ezekiel 23:22–26; Isaiah 10:8"> Yet though they haue hired among the nations, nowe will I gather them, and they shall sorowe a litle, for the burden of the King and the princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 12:11; Hosea 10:1–2; Hosea 10:8"> Because Ephraim hath made many altars to sinne, his altars shalbe to sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:6–8; Nehemiah 9:26; Proverbs 22:20"> I haue written to them the great things of my Lawe: but they were counted as a strange thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Hosea 9:6; Amos 8:7"> They sacrifice flesh for ye sacrifices of mine offerings, and eate it: but the Lord accepteth them not: now will he remember their iniquitie, and visite their sinnes: they shall returne to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:18; Jeremiah 17:27; Amos 2:5"> For Israel hath forgotten his maker, and buildeth Temples, and Iudah hath increased strong cities: but I will sende a fire vpon his cities, and it shall deuoure the palaces thereof.</span>
