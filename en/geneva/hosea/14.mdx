<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 6:1; Joel 2:12–13; Hosea 12:6"> Samaria shalbe desolate: for she hath rebelled against her God: they shall fall by the sworde: their infants shalbe dashed in pieces, and their women with childe shalbe ript.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 7:18–19; Hebrews 13:15; Job 34:31–32"> O Israel, returne vnto the Lord thy God: for thou hast fallen by thine iniquitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 68:5; Psalms 10:14; Isaiah 31:1"> Take vnto you words, and turne to the Lord, and say vnto him, Take away all iniquitie, and receiue vs graciously: so wil we render the calues of our lippes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 57:18; Ephesians 2:4–9; Jeremiah 3:22"> Asshur shall not saue vs, neither wil we ride vpon horses, neither will we say any more to the worke of our handes, Ye are our gods: for in thee the fatherlesse findeth mercie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Isaiah 27:6; Ephesians 3:17"> I wil heale their rebellion: I wil loue them freely: for mine anger is turned away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 52:8; John 15:1; 2 Corinthians 2:14–15"> I will be as the dewe vnto Israel: he shall grow as the lilie and fasten his rootes, as the trees of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 91:1; Psalms 85:6; 1 Corinthians 15:36–38"> His branches shall spread, and his beautie shalbe as the oliue tree, and his smell as Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 34:32; Isaiah 55:13; James 1:17"> They that dwel vnder his shadow, shall returne: they shall reuiue as the corne, and florish as the vine: the sent thereof shalbe as the wine of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 10:29; Psalms 107:43; John 8:47"> Ephraim shall say, What haue I to doe any more with idoles? I haue heard him, and looked vpon him: I am like a greene firre tree: vpon me is thy fruite founde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Who is wise, and he shall vnderstande these things? and prudent, and he shall knowe them? for the wayes of the Lord are righteous, and the iust shall walke in them: but the wicked shall fall therein.</span>
