<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 9:25; Micah 1:1; Isaiah 1:1"> The worde of the Lord that came vnto Hosea the sonne of Beeri, in the daies of Vzziah, Iotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah Kings of Iudah, and in the daies of Ieroboam the sonne of Ioash king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 2:4–5; Hosea 3:1; Deuteronomy 31:16"> At the beginning the Lord spake by Hosea, and the Lord said vnto Hosea, Goe, take vnto thee a wife of fornications, and children of fornications: for the lande hath committed great whoredome, departing from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:1–3"> So he went, and tooke Gomer, ye daughter of Diblaim, which conceiued and bare him a sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 10:7–8; 2 Kings 10:10–11; Ezekiel 23:10"> And the Lord said vnto him, Cal his name Izreel: for yet a litle, and I will visite the blood of Izreel vpon the house of Iehu, and will cause to cease the kingdome of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 6:33; Joshua 17:16; Hosea 2:18"> And at that day will I also breake the bowe of Israel in the valley of Izreel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; 1 Peter 2:10; Hosea 2:4"> She conceiued yet againe, and bare a daughter, and God saide vnto him, Call her name Lo-ruhamah: for I will no more haue pitie vpon the house of Israel: but I wil vtterly take them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6; Isaiah 12:2; 2 Kings 19:35"> Yet I will haue mercie vpon the house of Iudah, and wil saue them by the Lord their God, and wil not saue them by bow, nor by sword nor by battell, by horses, nor by horsemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Nowe when she had wained Lo-ruhamah, shee conceiued, and bare a sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:1"> Then saide God, Call his name Lo-ammi: for yee are not my people: therefore will I not be yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:18; Galatians 4:6–7; John 1:12"> Yet the nomber of the children of Israel shall be as the sande of the sea, which can not be measured nor tolde: and in the place where it was saide vnto them, Yee are not my people, it shall be saide vnto them, Yee are the sonnes of the liuing God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 3:5; Isaiah 11:12–13; Jeremiah 50:4–5"> Then shall the children of Iudah, and the children of Israel be gathered together, and appoint them selues one head, and they shall come vp out of the land: for great is the day of Izreel.</span>
