<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 11:12; Hosea 8:7; Ezekiel 17:10"> Ephraim is fed with the winde, and followeth after the East winde: hee increaseth daily lies and destruction, and they do make a couenant with Asshur, and oyle is caried into Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 6:2; Isaiah 10:6; Hosea 4:1"> The Lord hath also a controuersie with Iudah, and will visite Iaakob, according to his waies: according to his workes, wil he recompence him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:26; Genesis 32:24–28; Romans 9:11"> Hee tooke his brother by the heele in the wombe, and by his strength he had power with God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:15; Malachi 3:1; Genesis 35:9–10"> And had power ouer the Angel, and preuailed: he wept and praied vnto him: he founde him in Beth-el, and there he spake with vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:15; Isaiah 42:8; Psalms 135:13"> Yea, the Lord God of hostes, the Lord is himselfe his memoriall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Hosea 14:1; Micah 7:7"> Therefore turne thou to thy God: keepe mercy and iudgement, and hope still in thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 6:10–11; Amos 8:5–6; Proverbs 11:1"> He is Canaan: the balances of deceit are in his hand: he loueth to oppresse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Psalms 62:10; Psalms 52:7"> And Ephraim saide, Notwithstanding I am rich, I haue found me out riches in all my labours: they shall finde none iniquitie in me, that were wickednesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40–43; Nehemiah 8:15–17; Hosea 13:4"> Though I am the Lord thy God, from the land of Egypt, yet will I make thee to dwel in the tabernacles, as in the daies of the solemne feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 17:13; Ezekiel 20:49; Jeremiah 19:1"> I haue also spoken by the Prophets, and I haue multiplied visions, and vsed similitudes by the ministerie of the Prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Hosea 6:8; Hosea 8:11"> Is there iniquitie in Gilead? surely they are vanitie: they sacrifice bullocks in Gilgal, and their altars are as heapes in the furrowes of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:41; Genesis 27:43; Genesis 28:1–22"> And Iaakob fled into the countrey of Aram, and Israel serued for a wife, and for a wife he kept sheepe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:50–51; Acts 3:22–23; Psalms 77:20"> And by a Prophet the Lord brought Israel out of Egypt, and by a Prophet was he reserued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:13; Daniel 11:18; 2 Kings 17:7–18"> But Ephraim prouoked him with hie places: therefore shall his blood be powred vpon him, and his reproche shall his Lord reward him.</span>
