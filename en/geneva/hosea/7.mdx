<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 7:13; Micah 7:3–7; Hosea 6:4"> When I woulde haue healed Israel, then the iniquitie of Ephraim was discouered, and the wickednesse of Samaria: for they haue dealt falsly: and the theefe commeth in, and the robber spoyleth without.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:2; Amos 8:7; Jeremiah 14:10"> And they consider not in their hearts, that I remember all their wickednes: now their owne inuentions haue beset them about: they are in my sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Micah 7:3; Romans 1:32; Jeremiah 28:1–4"> They make the King glad with their wickednesse, and the princes with their lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:2; James 4:4; Jeremiah 5:7–8"> They are all adulterers, and as a very ouen heated by ye baker, which ceaseth from raysing vp, and from kneading ye dough vntill it be leauened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 28:1; Matthew 14:6; Genesis 40:20"> This is the day of our King: the princes haue made him sicke with flagons of wine: he stretcheth out his hand to scorners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 7:7; Hosea 7:4; 1 Samuel 19:11–15"> For they haue made ready their heart like an ouen whiles they lie in waite: their baker sleepeth all the night: in the morning it burneth as a flame of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 15:25; 2 Kings 15:30; 2 Kings 15:10"> They are all hote as an ouen, and haue deuoured their iudges: all their Kings are fallen: there is none among them that calleth vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:35; Ezra 9:12; Nehemiah 13:23–25"> Ephraim hath mixt himselfe among the people. Ephraim is as a cake on the hearth not turned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 8:7; Isaiah 42:22–25; 2 Kings 13:3–7"> Strangers haue deuoured his strength, and he knoweth it not: yea, gray heares are here and there vpon him, yet he knoweth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 5:5; Isaiah 9:13; Amos 4:6–13"> And the pride of Israel testifieth to his face, and they doe not returne to the Lord their God, nor seeke him for all this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 12:1; Hosea 5:13; Hosea 11:11"> Ephraim also is like a doue deceiued, without heart: they call to Egypt: they go to Asshur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 31:16–29"> But when they shall go, I will spred my net vpon them, and drawe them downe as the foules of the heauen: I will chastice them as their congregation hath heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 9:12; Hosea 11:12; Isaiah 63:8"> Wo vnto them: for they haue fled away from me: destruction shalbe vnto them, because they haue transgressed against me: though I haue redeemed them, yet they haue spoken lyes against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 4:3; Job 35:9–10; Jeremiah 3:10"> And they haue not cryed vnto me with their hearts, when they houled vpon their beds: they assembled themselues for corne, and wine, and they rebell against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; Psalms 62:3; Jeremiah 17:9"> Though I haue boud and strengthened their arme, yet doe they imagine mischiefe against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 78:57; Hosea 9:3; Ezekiel 23:32"> They returne, but not to the most high: they are like a deceitfull bowe: their princes shall fall by the sword, for the rage of their tongues: this shall be their derision in the land of Egypt.</span>
