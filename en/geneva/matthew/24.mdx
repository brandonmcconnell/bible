<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 13:1–37; Luke 21:5–36; Ezekiel 8:6"> And Iesus went out, and departed from the Temple, and his disciples came to him, to shewe him the building of the Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 19:44; Jeremiah 26:18; Micah 3:12"> And Iesus sayd vnto them, See ye not all these things? Verely I say vnto you, there shall not be here left a stone vpon a stone, that shall not be cast downe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 13:3–4; Acts 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–11"> And as he sate vpon the mount of Oliues, his disciples came vnto him apart, saying, Tell vs when these things shall be, and what signe shalbe of thy comming, and of the ende of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Jeremiah 29:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:3"> And Iesus answered, and sayd vnto them, Take heede that no man deceiue you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 24:11; Matthew 24:24; Jeremiah 14:14"> For many shall come in my name, saying, I am Christ, and shall deceiue many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:7–8; Luke 21:9; 1 Peter 3:14–15"> And ye shall heare of warres, and rumours of warres: see that ye be not troubled: for all these things must come to passe, but the end is not yet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 21:11; Revelation 6:8; Isaiah 19:2"> For nation shall rise against nation, and realme against realme, and there shalbe famine, and pestilence, and earthquakes in diuers places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Deuteronomy 28:59"> All these are but ye beginning of sorowes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 16:2; John 15:19–20; Luke 21:16–17"> Then shall they deliuer you vp to be afflicted, and shall kill you, and ye shall be hated of all nations for my Names sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Luke 21:16; Matthew 11:6"> And then shall many be offended, and shall betray one another, and shall hate one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; Matthew 7:15; Matthew 24:5"> And many false prophets shall arise, and shall deceiue many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 2:4–5; James 4:1–4; Revelation 3:15"> And because iniquitie shalbe increased, the loue of many shalbe colde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 10:22; Mark 13:13; Revelation 2:10"> But he that endureth to the ende, he shalbe saued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 14:6; Romans 10:18; Romans 16:25–26"> And this Gospel of the kingdome shalbe preached through the whole world for a witnes vnto all nations, and then shall the end come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 9:27; Daniel 12:11; Mark 13:14"> When ye therefore shall see the abomination of desolation spoken of by Daniel the Prophet, set in the holy place (let him that readeth consider it.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 19:15–17; Luke 21:21–22; Hebrews 11:7"> Then let them which be in Iudea, flee into the mountaines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 17:31–33; Mark 13:15–16; Matthew 10:27"> Let him which is on the house top, not come downe to fetch any thing out of his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he that is in the fielde, let not him returne backe to fetch his clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 23:29–30; Deuteronomy 28:53–56; Luke 21:23"> And woe shalbe to them that are with childe, and to them that giue sucke in those dayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:29; Acts 1:12"> But pray that your flight be not in the winter, neither on the Sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Joel 2:2; Malachi 4:1"> For then shall be great tribulation, such as was not from the beginning of the worlde to this time, nor shalbe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 13:20; 2 Timothy 2:10; Matthew 24:31"> And except those dayes should be shortened, there should no flesh be saued: but for the elects sake those dayes shalbe shortened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Luke 17:23–24; Deuteronomy 13:1–3"> Then if any shall say vnto you, Loe, here is Christ, or there, beleeue it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Mark 13:22; Revelation 13:13–14"> For there shall arise false Christes, and false prophets, and shall shewe great signes and wonders, so that if it were possible, they should deceiue the very elect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:1; Luke 21:13; Isaiah 48:5–6"> Beholde, I haue tolde you before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 3:2–3; Isaiah 40:3; Matthew 3:1"> Wherefore if they shall say vnto you, Beholde, he is in the desert, goe not forth: Beholde, he is in the secret places, beleeue it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 17:24–37; Zechariah 9:14; Job 37:3"> For as the lightning commeth out of the East, and is seene into the West, so shall also the comming of the Sonne of man be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 17:37; Job 39:27–30; Deuteronomy 28:49"> For wheresoeuer a dead carkeis is, thither will the Egles be gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Amos 8:9; Joel 3:15; Isaiah 13:10"> And immediatly after ye tribulations of those dayes, shall the sunne be darkened, and the moone shall not giue her light, and the starres shall fal from heauen, and ye powers of heaue shalbe shake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Daniel 7:13; Luke 21:27"> And then shall appeare the signe of the Sonne of man in heauen: and then shall all the kinreds of the earth mourne, and they shall see the Sonne of man come in the cloudes of heauen with power and great glorie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16; Mark 13:27; Isaiah 27:13"> And he shall send his Angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they shall gather together his elect, from the foure windes, and from the one ende of the heauens vnto the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 13:28–29; Luke 21:29–30"> Now learne the parable of the figge tree: when her bough is yet tender, and it putteth foorth leaues, ye knowe that sommer is neere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 5:9; Hebrews 10:37; Ezekiel 7:2–14"> So likewise ye, when ye see all these things, know that the kingdom of God is neere, eue at ye doores.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 13:30–31; Luke 21:32–33; Matthew 16:28"> Verely I say vnto you, this generation shall not passe, till all these things be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; 1 Peter 1:25; Numbers 23:19"> Heauen and earth shall passe away: but my wordes shall not passe away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Acts 1:7; Matthew 24:44"> But of that day and houre knoweth no man, no not the Angels of heauen, but my father only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:1–7; 2 Peter 2:5"> But as the dayes of Noe were, so likewise shall the comming of the Sonne of man be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 17:26–28; Romans 13:13–14; Luke 14:18–20"> For as in the dayes before the flood, they did eate and drinke, marrie, and giue in mariage, vnto the day that Noe entred into the Arke,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:20; Matthew 24:37; Acts 13:41"> And knewe nothing, till the flood came, and tooke them all away, so shall also the comming of the Sonne of man be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 17:34–37; 2 Peter 2:7–9; 2 Peter 2:5"> Then two shall be in the fieldes, the one shalbe receiued, and the other shalbe refused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 11:5; Isaiah 47:2; Luke 17:35"> Two women shalbe grinding at ye mill: the one shalbe receiued, and the other shalbe refused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 21:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:6; Matthew 25:13"> Watch therefore: for ye knowe not what houre your master will come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 12:39; Matthew 24:44; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–6"> Of this be sure, that if the good man of the house knewe at what watch the thiefe would come, he woulde surely watch, and not suffer his house to be digged through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 25:10; Matthew 24:42; Matthew 25:13"> Therefore be ye also ready: for in the houre that ye thinke not, will the Sonne of man come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Matthew 25:23; 1 Corinthians 4:1–2"> Who then is a faithfull seruaunt and wise, whom his master hath made ruler ouer his household, to giue them meate in season?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; Luke 12:43; Luke 12:37"> Blessed is that seruant, whom his master when he commeth, shall finde so doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 25:23; Matthew 25:21; Luke 12:37"> Verely I say vnto you, he shall make him ruler ouer all his goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 18:32; Isaiah 32:6; Luke 12:45"> But if that euil seruant shall say in his heart, My master doth deferre his comming,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Revelation 17:6; Revelation 16:6; Micah 3:5"> And begin to smite his fellowes, and to eate, and to drinke with the drunken,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Revelation 3:3; Matthew 24:42–44"> That seruaunts master will come in a day, when he looketh not for him, and in an houre that he is not ware of,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 22:13; Luke 12:46"> And will cut him off, and giue him his portion with hypocrites: there shalbe weeping, and gnashing of teeth.</span>
