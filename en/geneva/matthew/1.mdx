<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Ruth 1:16–17; James 2:25"> And Salmon begate Booz of Rachab. And Booz begat Obed of Ruth. and Obed begat Iesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 15:5; 1 Samuel 17:12; 2 Samuel 12:24–25"> And Iesse begate Dauid the King. And Dauid the King begate Salomon of her that was the wife of Vrias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:1–15; 1 Kings 14:31; 1 Kings 11:43–24"> And Salomon begate Roboam. And Roboam begate Abia. And Abia begate Asa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:11; 2 Chronicles 26:1–23; 2 Kings 8:16"> And Asa begate Iosaphat. And Iosaphat begate Ioram. And Ioram begate Hozias.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:33–27; 1 Kings 13:2; 1 Chronicles 3:13–15"> And Ezekias begate Manasses. And Manasses begate Amon. And Amon begate Iosias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:20; Jeremiah 52:28–30; Daniel 1:2"> And Iosias begate Iakim. And Iakim begate Iechonias and his brethren about the time they were caried away to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:17; Ezra 3:2; Luke 3:27"> And after they were caried away into Babylon, Iechonias begate Salathiel. And Salathiel begate Zorobabel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Zorobabel begate Abiud. And Abiud begate Eliacim. And Eliacim begate Azor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Azor begate Sadoc. And Sadoc begate Achim. And Achim begate Eliud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Eliud begate Eleazar. And Eleazar begate Matthan. And Matthan begate Iacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:17; John 4:25; Luke 3:23"> And Iacob begat Ioseph ye husband of Mary, of whom was borne Jesus, that is called Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 1:11–12; 2 Kings 24:14; Matthew 11:2"> So all the generations from Abraham to Dauid, are fourtene generations. And from Dauid vntil they were caried away into Babylon, fourtene generations: and after they were caried away into Babylon vntill Christ, fourteene generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Luke 1:27–38; Job 14:4"> Nowe the birth of Jesus Christ was thus, When as his mother Mary was betrothed to Ioseph, before they came together, shee was found with childe of the holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Deuteronomy 24:1–4; John 8:4–5"> Then Ioseph her husband being a iust man, and not willing to make her a publike example, was minded to put her away secretly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Luke 1:19; Matthew 1:18"> But whiles he thought these things, behold, the Angel of the Lord appeared vnto him in a dreame, saying, Ioseph, the sonne of Dauid, feare not to take Mary thy wife: for that which is conceiued in her, is of the holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:23; Acts 4:12; John 1:29"> And she shall bring foorth a sonne, and thou shalt call his name JESUS: for hee shall saue his people from their sinnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 24:44; Matthew 2:23; 1 Kings 8:15"> And al this was done that it might be fulfilled, which is spoken of the Lord by ye Prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 9:6–7; Psalms 46:11"> Behold, a virgine shalbe with childe, and shall beare a sonne, and they shall call his name Emmanuel, which is by interpretation, God with vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 15:14; James 2:21–26; John 2:5–8"> Then Ioseph, being raised from sleepe, did as the Angel of the Lord had inioyned him, and tooke his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 2:21; Exodus 13:2; Matthew 1:21"> But he knew her not, til she had broght forth her first borne sonne, and he called his name JESUS.</span>
