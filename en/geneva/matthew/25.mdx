<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:35–36; Philippians 2:15–16; Matthew 24:42–51"> Then the kingdome of heauen shalbe likened vnto tenne virgins, which tooke their lampes, and went foorth to meete the bridegrome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:2; 1 Corinthians 10:1–5; Matthew 13:19–23"> And fiue of them were wise, and fiue foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:15–16; 2 Timothy 3:5; Isaiah 48:1–2"> The foolish tooke their lampes, but tooke none oyle with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:9; 1 John 2:27; 1 John 2:20"> But the wise tooke oyle in their vessels with their lampes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:6–8; 2 Peter 3:4–9"> Nowe while the bridegrome taried long, all slumbred and slept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Matthew 24:31; Matthew 24:44"> And at midnight there was a crie made, Behold, the bridegrome commeth: goe out to meete him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 12:35; Revelation 3:19–20; 2 Peter 3:14"> Then all those virgins arose, and trimmed their lampes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Luke 12:35; Hebrews 4:1"> And the foolish said to the wise, Giue vs of your oyle, for our lampes are out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–9; Revelation 3:17–18; Isaiah 55:6–7"> But the wise answered, saying, Not so, lest there will not be ynough for vs and you: but goe ye rather to them that sell, and bye for your selues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 13:25; Matthew 24:44; Luke 12:36–37"> And while they went to bye, the bridegrome came: and they that were readie, went in with him to the wedding, and the gate was shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 7:21–23; Hebrews 12:16–17"> Afterwards came also the other virgins, saying, Lord, Lord, open to vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; 2 Timothy 2:19; John 10:27"> But he answered, and said, Verely I say vnto you, I knowe you not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:42–44; Revelation 16:15; 1 Thessalonians 5:6"> Watch therfore: for ye know neither the day, nor the houre, when the sonne of man will come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 13:34; Luke 19:12–27; 1 Peter 4:9–11"> For the kingdome of heauen is as a man that going into a strange countrey, called his seruants, and deliuered to them his goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:24; Luke 19:13–14; Luke 12:48"> And vnto one he gaue fiue talents, and to an other two, and to another one, to euery man after his own ability, and straightway went from home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="3 John 1:5–8; 1 Timothy 6:17–18; 2 Timothy 2:6"> Then he that had receiued the fiue talents, went and occupied with them, and gained other fiue talents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Ephesians 5:16; 1 Peter 4:10"> Likewise also, he that receiued two, he also gained other two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 1:8; Haggai 1:2–4; Malachi 1:10"> But he that receiued that one, went and digged it in the earth, and hid his masters money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:12–15; 2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 14:7–12"> But after a long season, the master of those seruants came, and reckoned with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:24; Luke 19:16–17; Colossians 1:29"> Then came he that had receiued fiue talents, and brought other fiue talents, saying, Master, thou deliueredst vnto me fiue talents: behold, I haue gained with them other fiue talents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:23; Luke 16:10; John 12:26"> Then his master saide vnto him, It is well done good seruant and faithfull, Thou hast bene faithfull in litle, I will make thee ruler ouer much: enter into thy masters ioy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; 2 Corinthians 8:7–8; 2 Corinthians 8:1–3"> Also he that had receiued two talents, came, and said, Master, thou deliueredst vnto me two talents: behold, I haue gained two other talets more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Mark 14:8–9; Mark 12:41–44"> His master saide vnto him, It is well done good seruant, and faithfull, Thou hast bene faithfull in litle, I will make thee ruler ouer much: enter into thy masters ioy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 19:20–22; Luke 15:29; Malachi 3:14–15"> Then he which had receiued the one talent, came, and said, Master, I knewe that thou wast an hard man, which reapest where thou sowedst not, and gatherest where thou strawedst not:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6–7; Romans 8:15; Revelation 21:8"> I was therefore afraide, and went, and hid thy talent in the earth: behold, thou hast thine owne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 18:32; Job 15:5–6"> And his master answered, and said vnto him, Thou euill seruant, and slouthfull, thou knewest that I reape where I sowed not, and gather where I strawed not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Romans 3:19; Jude 1:15"> Thou oughtest therefore to haue put my money to ye exchangers, and then at my comming should I haue receiued mine owne with vantage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 19:24; Luke 10:42"> Take therefore the talent from him, and giue it vnto him which hath tenne talents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 8:18; Mark 4:25; Matthew 13:12"> For vnto euery man that hath, it shall be giuen, and he shall haue abundance, and from him that hath not, euen that he hath, shalbe taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Revelation 3:15–16; Matthew 13:50"> Cast therefore that vnprofitable seruant into vtter darkenes: there shalbe weeping and gnasshing of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:7–8; Matthew 19:28"> And when the Sonne of man commeth in his glory, and all the holy Angels with him, then shall he sit vpon the throne of his glorie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Matthew 13:49; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> And before him shalbe gathered all nations, and he shall seperate them one from another, as a shepheard separateth the sheepe from ye goates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; John 10:26–28; John 21:15–17"> And he shall set the sheepe on his right hand, and the goates on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–20; James 2:5; Revelation 21:7"> Then shall ye king say to them on his right hand, Come ye blessed of my father: take the inheritance of the kingdome prepared for you from the foundation of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 3:16–19; James 2:15–16; Deuteronomy 15:7–11"> For I was an hungred, and ye gaue me meate: I thirsted, and ye gaue me drinke: I was a stranger, and ye tooke me in vnto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="James 1:27; 2 Timothy 1:16–18; James 5:14–15"> I was naked, and ye clothed me: I was sicke, and ye visited me: I was in prison, and ye came vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 6:3; 1 Chronicles 29:14; 1 Peter 5:5–6"> Then shall the righteous answere him, saying, Lord, when sawe we thee an hungred, and fed thee? or a thirst, and gaue thee drinke?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And when sawe we thee a stranger, and tooke thee in vnto vs? or naked, and clothed thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Or when sawe we thee sicke, or in prison, and came vnto thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Proverbs 14:31; Matthew 10:42"> And the King shall answere, and say vnto them, Verely I say vnto you, in as much as ye haue done it vnto one of the least of these my brethre, ye haue done it to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6–7; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> Then shall he say vnto them on ye left hand, Depart from me ye cursed, into euerlasting fire, which is prepared for the deuill and his angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="James 2:15–24; John 5:23; Matthew 12:30"> For I was an hungred, and ye gaue me no meate: I thirsted, and ye gaue me no drinke:</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> I was a stranger, and ye tooke me not in vnto you: I was naked, and ye clothed me not: sicke, and in prison, and ye visited me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 7:22; Luke 10:29; Matthew 25:24–27"> Then shall they also answere him, saying, Lord, when sawe we thee an hungred, or a thirst, or a stranger, or naked, or sicke, or in prison, and did not minister vnto thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 21:13; Proverbs 17:5; Proverbs 14:31"> Then shall he answere them, and say, Verely I say vnto you, in as much as ye did it not to one of the least of these, ye did it not to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 3:36; John 5:29"> And these shall goe into euerlasting paine, and the righteous into life eternall.</span>
