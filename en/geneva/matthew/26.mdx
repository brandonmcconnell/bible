<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Matthew 19:1"> And it came to passe, when Iesus had finished all these sayings, he saide vnto his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 22:1–2; Mark 14:1–2; John 13:1–2"> Ye know that after two dayes is ye Passeouer, and the Sonne of man shalbe deliuered to be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:47–53; John 18:24; Mark 14:54"> Then assembled together the chiefe Priests, and the Scribes, and the Elders of ye people into the hall of the high Priest called Caiaphas:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 12:14; Psalms 2:2; Acts 13:10"> And consulted together that they might take Iesus by subtiltie, and kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:24; Matthew 21:26; Matthew 14:5"> But they sayd, Not on the feast day, least any vprore be among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Luke 7:37–39; John 12:1–8"> And when Iesus was in Bethania, in the house of Simon the leper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 12:2–3; Luke 7:46; Luke 7:37–38"> There came vnto him a woman, which had a boxe of very costly oyntment, and powred it on his head, as he sate at the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 12:4–6; Mark 14:4; Exodus 5:17"> And when his disciples sawe it, they had indignation, saying, What needed this waste?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 12:5–6; Mark 14:5; Joshua 7:20–21"> For this oyntment might haue bene solde for much, and bene giuen to the poore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Titus 3:8; Hebrews 13:21; 1 Timothy 5:10"> And Iesus knowing it, sayde vnto them, Why trouble yee the woman? for shee hath wrought a good woorke vpon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Mark 14:7; John 12:8"> For yee haue the poore alwayes with you, but me shall yee not haue alwaies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 14:8; John 12:7; Mark 16:1"> For in that shee powred this oyntment on my bodie, shee did it to burie me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 14:9; Matthew 24:14; Psalms 112:6"> Verely I say vnto you, wheresoeuer this Gospel shall bee preached throughout all the worlde, there shall also this that shee hath done, be spoken of for a memoriall of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 22:3–6; Matthew 10:4; Mark 14:10–11"> Then one of the twelue, called Iudas Iscariot, went vnto the chiefe Priestes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Genesis 37:26–28; Exodus 21:32"> And said, What will ye giue me, and I will deliuer him vnto you? and they appoynted vnto him thirtie pieces of siluer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:11; Luke 22:6"> And from that time, he sought opportunitie to betraie him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:7–13; Mark 14:12–16; Exodus 12:18–20"> Nowe on the first day of the feast of vnleauened bread the disciples came to Iesus, saying vnto him, Where wilt thou that we prepare for thee to eate the Passeouer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 14:13–16; John 7:6; John 17:1"> And he said, Goe yee into the citie to such a man, and say to him, The master saieth, My time is at hande: I will keepe the Passeouer at thine house with my disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 2:5; John 15:14; Matthew 21:6"> And the disciples did as Iesus had giuen them charge, and made readie the Passeouer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 14:17–21; John 13:21; Luke 22:14–16"> So when the Euen was come, hee sate downe with the twelue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 13:21; Hebrews 4:13; John 6:70–71"> And as they did eate, he sayde, Verely I say vnto you, that one of you shall betraie me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 13:22–25; Luke 22:23; Mark 14:19–20"> And they were exceeding sorowfull, and began euery one of them to say vnto him, Is it I, Master?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:18; Psalms 41:9; John 13:26–28"> And hee answered and sayde, Hee that dippeth his hande with me in the dish, hee shall betraie me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 26:22–23; Luke 24:46; Matthew 18:7"> Surely the Sonne of man goeth his way, as it is written of him: but woe be to that man, by whom the Sonne of man is betrayed: it had bene good for that man, if hee had neuer bene borne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 26:64; Matthew 27:11; Luke 22:70"> Then Iudas which betraied him, answered and sayde, Is it I, Master? He sayde vnto him, Thou hast sayd it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:23–29; Luke 22:18–20; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> And as they did eate, Iesus tooke the bread, and when he had blessed, he brake it, and gaue it to the disciples, and sayd, Take, eate: this is my bodie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; 1 Corinthians 10:16; Psalms 116:13"> Also he tooke the cuppe, and when he had giuen thankes, he gaue it them, saying, Drinke ye all of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–14; Hebrews 9:14–22; Leviticus 17:11"> For this is my blood of the Newe Testament, that is shedde for many, for the remission of sinnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Revelation 7:17; Mark 14:25"> I say vnto you, that I will not drinke henceforth of this fruit of the vine vntil that day, when I shall drinke it new with you in my Fathers kingdome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Ephesians 5:19–20; Luke 22:39"> And when they had sung a Psalme, they went out into the mount of Oliues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Mark 14:27–28; John 16:32"> Then saide Iesus vnto them, All yee shall be offended by me this night: for it is written, I wil smite the shepheard, and the sheepe of the flocke shalbe scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 28:10; Luke 18:33–34; Matthew 28:16"> But after I am risen againe, I will go before you into Galile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 22:33; John 13:36–38; Proverbs 28:25–26"> But Peter aunswered, and sayde vnto him, Though that al men should be offended by thee, yet will I neuer be offended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 13:38; Luke 22:34; Matthew 26:75"> Iesus sayde vnto him, Verely I say vnto thee, that this night, before the cocke crow, thou shalt denie me thrise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Romans 11:20; John 13:37; Proverbs 29:23"> Peter saide vnto him, Though I should die with thee, I will in no case denie thee. Likewise also sayd all the disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 14:32–42; Matthew 26:42; Luke 22:39–46"> Then went Iesus with them into a place which is called Gethsemane, and said vnto his disciples, Sit ye here, while I goe, and pray yonder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Matthew 17:1; Mark 5:37"> And hee tooke vnto him Peter, and the two sonnes of Zebedeus, and began to waxe sorowfull, and grieuously troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 12:27; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 Peter 2:24"> Then sayde Iesus vnto them, My soule is very heauie, euen vnto the death: tarie yee here, and watch with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 6:38; Philippians 2:8; Luke 22:41–42"> So hee went a litle further, and fell on his face, and praied, saying, O my Father, if it be possible, let this cup passe from me: neuerthelesse, not as I will, but as thou wilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 14:37; Luke 22:45; Matthew 26:43"> After, hee came vnto the disciples, and founde them a sleepe, and sayde to Peter, What? coulde yee not watch with me one houre?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 14:38; Luke 22:46; Ephesians 6:18"> Watch, and praie, that yee enter not into tentation: the spirit in deede is readie, but the flesh is weake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 26:39; Mark 14:39–40; Hebrews 4:15"> Againe he went away the second time, and praied, saying, O my Father, if this cuppe can not passe away from mee, but that I must drinke it, thy will be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:32; Romans 13:1; Proverbs 23:34"> And he came, and founde them a sleepe againe, for their eyes were heauie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:8; Luke 18:1; Matthew 6:7"> So he left them and went away againe, and praied the third time, saying the same woordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 13:1; John 12:27; Mark 14:41–42"> Then came he to his disciples, and said vnto them, Sleepe henceforth, and take your rest: behold, the houre is at hand, and the Sonne of man is giuen into the hands of sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 9:51; John 14:31; 1 Samuel 17:48"> Rise, let vs goe: beholde, hee is at hande that betraieth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 1:16; Matthew 26:55; Mark 14:43–50"> And while hee yet spake, loe Iudas, one of the twelue, came, and with him a great multitude with swordes and staues, from the high Priests and Elders of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 55:20–21; Psalms 28:3; 2 Samuel 3:27"> Now he that betraied him, had giuen them a token, saying, Whomesoeuer I shall kisse, that is he, laie holde on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:25; 1 Thessalonians 5:26; Proverbs 27:6"> And forthwith he came to Iesus, and sayd, God saue thee, Master, and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 20:13; Matthew 22:12; Psalms 41:9"> Then Iesus sayde vnto him, Friende, wherefore art thou come? Then came they, and laide hands on Iesus, and tooke him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 18:36; Mark 14:47; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> And behold, one of them which were with Iesus, stretched out his hand, and drewe his sworde, and strooke a seruaunt of the high Priest, and smote off his eare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Revelation 13:10; Genesis 9:6; Romans 12:19"> Then sayde Iesus vnto him, Put vp thy sworde into his place: for all that take the sworde, shall perish with the sworde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Kings 6:17; Matthew 4:11; Daniel 7:10"> Either thinkest thou, that I can not now pray to my Father, and he will giue me moe then twelue legions of Angels?</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Matthew 26:24; Acts 1:16"> Howe then shoulde the Scriptures bee fulfilled, which say, that it must be so?</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Mark 12:35; John 8:2; Luke 22:52–53"> The same houre sayde Iesus to the multitude, Ye be come out as it were against a thiefe, with swordes and staues to take mee: I sate daily teaching in the Temple among you, and yee tooke me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; John 16:32; Mark 14:50–52"> But all this was done, that the Scriptures of the Prophets might be fulfilled. Then all the disciples forsooke him, and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; Mark 14:53–65; Psalms 56:5–6"> And they tooke Iesus, and led him to Caiaphas the hie Priest, where the Scribes and the Elders were assembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 7:32; John 18:15–16; John 18:25"> And Peter followed him a farre off vnto the hie Priestes hall, and went in, and sate with the seruants to see the ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Acts 6:11–13; Deuteronomy 19:16–21; Mark 14:55–56"> Nowe the chiefe Priestes and the Elders, and all the whole councill sought false witnesse against Iesus, to put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Psalms 35:11; Psalms 27:12"> But they founde none, and though many false witnesses came, yet founde they none: but at the last came two false witnesses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="John 2:19–21; Matthew 27:40; Mark 15:29"> And saide, This man saide, I can destroie the Temple of God, and build it in three daies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="John 19:9–11; John 18:19–24; Mark 14:60"> Then the chiefe Priest arose, and sayde to him, Answerest thou nothing? What is the matter that these men witnesse against thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Matthew 16:16; Isaiah 53:7"> But Iesus helde his peace. Then the chiefe Priest answered, and saide to him, I charge thee sweare vnto vs by the liuing God, to tell vs, If thou be that Christ the Sonne of God, or no.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Mark 14:62; Psalms 110:1"> Iesus saide to him, Thou hast saide it: neuerthelesse I say vnto you, hereafter shall ye see the Sonne of man, sitting at the right hande of the power of God, and come in the cloudes of the heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:24; John 10:33; Matthew 9:3"> Then the hie Priest rent his clothes, saying, Hee hath blasphemed, what haue wee any more neede of witnesses? beholde: nowe yee haue heard his blasphemie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="John 19:7; Acts 7:52; Leviticus 24:11–16"> What thinke yee? They answered, and said, He is guiltie of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 27:30"> Then spet they in his face, and buffeted him, and other smote him with roddes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Luke 22:63–65; Mark 14:65; 1 Peter 2:4–8"> Saying, Prophecie to vs, O Christ, Who is he that smote thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="1 Kings 19:13; Matthew 26:71; Acts 5:37"> Peter sate without in the hall, and a maide came to him, saying, Thou also wast with Iesus of Galile:</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:12; Revelation 21:8; Matthew 26:40–43"> But hee denied before them all, saying, I wote not what thou saiest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Mark 14:68–69; John 18:25–27; Luke 22:58"> And when hee went out into the porche, another maide sawe him, and sayde vnto them that were there, This man was also with Iesus of Nazareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Matthew 26:74; Isaiah 48:1; Zechariah 8:17"> And againe he denied with an othe, saying, I knowe not the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73" data-xref="Judges 12:6; John 18:26–27; Nehemiah 13:24"> So after a while, came vnto him they that stoode by, and sayde vnto Peter, Surely thou art also one of them: for euen thy speache bewraieth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; 1 Corinthians 16:22; Mark 14:71–72"> Then began hee to curse himselfe, and to sweare, saying, I knowe not the man. And immediately the cocke crewe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v75" data-v="75" data-xref="Matthew 26:34; Romans 7:18–20; Luke 22:31–34"> Then Peter remembred the wordes of Iesus, which had sayde vnto him, Before the cocke crowe thou shalt denie me thrise. So he went out, and wept bitterly.</span>
