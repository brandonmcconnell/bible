<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–6; John 1:6–8; Luke 1:76"> And in those dayes, Iohn the Baptist came and preached in the wildernes of Iudea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:17; Mark 1:15; Daniel 2:44"> And said, Repent: for the kingdome of heauen is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3; Malachi 3:1; John 1:23"> For this is he of whome it is spoken by the Prophet Esaias, saying, The voyce of him that crieth in the wildernes, Prepare ye the way of the Lord: make his pathes straight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 1:8; Zechariah 13:4; Mark 1:6"> And this Iohn had his garment of camels heare, and a girdle of a skinne about his loynes: his meate was also locusts and wilde hony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 1:5; Luke 3:7; John 3:23"> Then went out to him Ierusalem and all Iudea, and all the region rounde about Iordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 19:18; Matthew 3:13–16; Acts 2:38–41"> And they were baptized of him in Iordan, confessing their sinnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; 1 Thessalonians 1:10; Romans 5:9"> Now when he sawe many of the Pharises, and of the Sadduces come to his baptisme, he said vnto them, O generations of vipers, who hath forewarned you to flee from the anger to come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 3:8; Acts 26:20; 2 Peter 1:4–8"> Bring foorth therefore fruite worthy amendment of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 3:8; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:27–29"> And thinke not to say with your selues, We haue Abraham to our father: for I say vnto you, that God is able euen of these stones to raise vp children vnto Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:6; Luke 3:9; Matthew 7:19"> And now also is the axe put to the roote of the trees: therfore euery tree which bringeth not forth good fruit, is hewen downe, and cast into ye fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 4:4; Luke 3:16; Acts 1:5"> In deede I baptize you with water to amendment of life, but he that commeth after me, is mightier then I, whose shoes I am not worthie to beare: hee will baptize you with the holy Ghost, and with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Matthew 13:30; Luke 3:17"> Which hath his fanne in his hand, and wil make cleane his floore, and gather his wheate into his garner, but will burne vp the chaffe with vnquenchable fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 1:32–34; Matthew 2:22; Mark 1:9–11"> Then came Iesus from Galile to Iordan vnto Iohn, to be baptized of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 3:3–7; Acts 1:5–8; John 13:6–8"> But Iohn earnestly put him backe, saying, I haue neede to be baptized of thee, and commest thou to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:34; John 15:10; Psalms 40:7–8"> Then Iesus answering, saide to him, Let be nowe: for thus it becommeth vs to fulfill all righteousnes. So he suffered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; Mark 1:10; Luke 3:21–22"> And Iesus when hee was baptized, came straight out of the water. And lo, the heaues were opened vnto him, and Iohn saw the Spirit of God descending like a doue, and lighting vpon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Psalms 2:7; Matthew 17:5"> And loe, a voyce came from heauen, saying, This is my beloued Sonne, in whome I am well pleased.</span>
