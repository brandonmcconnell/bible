<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Mark 8:15; Luke 9:7–9"> At that time Herod the Tetrarche heard of the fame of Iesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 16:14; John 10:41; Mark 8:28"> And sayde vnto his seruaunts, This is that Iohn Baptist, hee is risen againe from the deade, and therefore great woorkes are wrought by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 6:17; Luke 3:19–20; Matthew 4:12"> For Herod had taken Iohn, and bounde him, and put him in prison for Herodias sake, his brother Philips wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:21; Leviticus 18:16; Mark 6:18"> For Iohn saide vnto him, It is not lawfull for thee to haue her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 11:9; Mark 6:19–20"> And when hee woulde haue put him to death, hee feared the multitude, because they counted him as a Prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 6:21–23; Genesis 40:20; Hosea 1:5–6"> But when Herods birth day was kept, the daughter of Herodias daunced before them, and pleased Herod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 5:3; Esther 5:6; Esther 7:2"> Wherefore he promised with an othe, that he would giue her whatsoeuer she would aske.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 6:24; Proverbs 29:10; Proverbs 1:16"> And shee being before instructed of her mother, sayde, Giue mee here Iohn Baptists head in a platter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:12–16; 1 Samuel 14:28; Mark 6:26"> And the King was sorie: neuerthelesse because of the othe, and them that sate with him at the table, he commanded it to be giuen her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 17:12; Matthew 21:35–36; Luke 9:9"> And sent, and beheaded Iohn in the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 16:6; Genesis 49:7; Ezekiel 16:3–4"> And his head was brought in a platter, and giuen to the maide, and shee brought it vnto her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 8:2; Matthew 27:58–61"> And his disciples came, and tooke vp the bodie, and buried it, and went, and tolde Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:10–17; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 6:30–44"> And when Iesus heard it, hee departed thence by shippe into a desert place apart. And when the multitude had heard it, they followed him on foote out of the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Matthew 9:36; Hebrews 2:17"> And Iesus went foorth and sawe a great multitude, and was mooued with compassion toward them, and he healed their sicke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 15:23; Mark 8:3; Mark 6:35–36"> And when euen was come, his disciples came to him, saying, This is a desart place, and the time is alreadie past: let the multitude depart, that they may goe into the townes, and bye them vitailes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; Ecclesiastes 11:2"> But Iesus saide to them, They haue no neede to goe away: giue yee them to eate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:33–34; Mark 6:37–38; Luke 9:13"> Then saide they vnto him, Wee haue here but fiue loaues, and two fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he saide, Bring them hither to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 8:6–7; Luke 24:30; 1 Samuel 9:13"> And hee commanded the multitude to sit downe on the grasse, and tooke the fiue loaues and the two fishes, and looked vp to heauen and blessed, and brake, and gaue the loaues to his disciples, and the disciples to the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:12; Mark 6:42–44; Matthew 16:8–10"> And they did all eate, and were sufficed, and they tooke vp of the fragments that remained, twelue baskets full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 9:8–11; Acts 4:4"> And they that had eaten, were about fiue thousande men, beside women and litle children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 6:45–51; John 6:15–21; Matthew 15:39"> And straightway Iesus compelled his disciples to enter into a shippe, and to goe ouer before him, while he sent the multitude away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 6:46; Matthew 26:36"> And assoone as hee had sent the multitude away, he went vp into a moutaine alone to pray: and when the euening was come, hee was there alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:18; Mark 6:48; Matthew 8:24"> And the shippe was nowe in the middes of the sea, and was tossed with waues: for it was a contrarie winde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:8; John 6:19; Mark 6:48"> And in the fourth watch of the night, Iesus went vnto them, walking on the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 6:49–50; Revelation 1:17; Luke 1:11–12"> And when his disciples sawe him walking on the sea, they were troubled, saying, It is a spirit, and cried out for feare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Revelation 1:17–18; John 16:33"> But straight way Iesus spake vnto them, saying, Be of good comfort, It is I: be not afraide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:68; Romans 12:3; Matthew 26:33–35"> Then Peter answered him, and saide, Master, if it be thou, bid me come vnto thee on the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Mark 9:23; Acts 3:16"> And he saide, Come. And when Peter was come downe out of the shippe, he walked on the water, to goe to Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 3:54–57; 2 Timothy 4:16–17; Mark 14:38"> But when he sawe a mightie winde, he was afraide: and as he began to sinke, he cried, saying, Master, saue me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:6–8; Psalms 138:7; Genesis 22:14"> So immediatly Iesus stretched foorth his hande, and caught him, and saide to him, O thou of litle faith, wherefore diddest thou doubt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 107:29–30; Mark 4:41; John 6:21"> And assoone as they were come into the ship, the winde ceased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:27; Psalms 2:7; Romans 1:4"> Then they that were in the ship, came and worshipped him, saying, Of a trueth thou art the Sonne of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 6:53–56; Luke 5:1; John 6:24–25"> And when they were come ouer, they came into the land of Gennezaret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 4:24–25; Mark 6:55; Mark 1:28–34"> And when the men of that place knewe him, they sent out into all that countrey rounde about, and brought vnto him all that were sicke,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Mark 3:10; Exodus 28:33–43"> And besought him, that they might touch the hemme of his garment onely: and as many as touched it, were made whole.</span>
