<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zechariah 14:4; Mark 11:1–10; Luke 21:37"> And when they drewe neere to Hierusalem, and were come to Bethphage, vnto the mount of the Oliues, then sent Iesus two disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 19:30–32; Mark 11:2–3; John 2:5–8"> Saying to them, Goe into the towne that is ouer against you, and anon yee shall finde an asse bounde, and a colt with her: loose them, and bring them vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:1–2; Acts 17:25; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> And if any man say ought vnto you, say ye, that the Lord hath neede of them, and straightway he will let them goe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; John 12:15; Matthew 1:22"> All this was done that it might be fulfilled, which was spoken by the Prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9–10; Isaiah 62:11; Isaiah 12:6"> Tell ye the daughter of Sion, Beholde, thy King commeth vnto thee, meeke and sitting vpon an asse, and a colte, the foale of an asse vsed to the yoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 39:43; John 15:14; Exodus 40:16"> So the disciples went, and did as Iesus had commanded them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; Mark 11:4–8; Luke 19:32–35"> And brought the asse and the colt, and put on them their clothes, and set him thereon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; John 12:13; Leviticus 23:40"> And a great multitude spred their garments in the way: and other cut downe branches from the trees, and strawed them in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 118:24–26; Luke 2:14; John 12:13–15"> Moreouer, the people that went before, and they also that followed, cried, saying, Hosanna to the Sonne of Dauid, Blessed be hee that commeth in the Name of the Lord, Hosanna thou which art in the highest heauens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 9:5; Ruth 1:19; John 12:16–19"> And when he was come into Hierusalem, all the citie was mooued, saying, Who is this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 6:14; Luke 7:16; John 7:40"> And the people said, This is Iesus that Prophet of Nazareth in Galile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 2:14–17; Luke 19:45–47; Mark 11:15–18"> And Iesus went into the Temple of God, and cast out all them that solde and bought in the Temple, and ouerthrew the tables of the money chagers, and the seates of them that sold doues,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:11; Isaiah 56:7; Luke 19:46"> And said to them, it is written, My house shall be called the house of prayer: but ye haue made it a denne of theeues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 3:1–9; Acts 10:38; Matthew 11:4–5"> Then the blinde, and the halt came to him in the Temple, and he healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 21:9; Luke 19:39–40; Isaiah 26:11"> But when the chiefe priestes and Scribes sawe the marueiles that hee did, and the children crying in the Temple, and saying, Hosanna to the Sonne of Dauid, they disdained,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 8:2; Matthew 11:25; Luke 19:39–40"> And said vnto him, Hearest thou what these say? And Iesus said vnto them, Yea: read ye neuer, By the mouth of babes and sucklings thou hast made perfite the praise?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 11:18; Mark 11:11; Luke 24:50"> So hee left them, and went out of the citie vnto Bethania, and lodged there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 4:2; Hebrews 4:15; Matthew 12:1"> And in the morning, as he returned into the citie, he was hungrie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 15:6; Mark 11:14; Titus 1:16"> And seeing a figge tree in the way, he came to it, and found nothing thereon, but leaues onely, and said to it, Neuer fruite grow on thee henceforwards. And anon the figge tree withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; Mark 11:20–21; James 1:10–11"> And when his disciples saw it, they marueiled, saying, How soone is the figge tree withered!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 1:6; Matthew 17:20; Mark 11:22–23"> And Iesus answered and said vnto them, Verely I say vnto you, if ye haue faith, and doubt not, ye shall not only doe that, which I haue done to the figge tree, but also if ye say vnto this mountaine, Take thy selfe away, and cast thy selfe into the sea, it shalbe done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:7; 1 John 5:14–15; Mark 11:24"> And whatsoeuer ye shall aske in prayer, if ye beleeue, ye shall receiue it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:7; Mark 11:27–33; 1 Chronicles 24:1–19"> And whe he was come into the Temple, the chiefe Priestes, and the Elders of the people came vnto him, as he was teaching, and saide, By what authoritie doest thou these things? and who gaue thee this authoritie?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Colossians 4:6; Matthew 10:16"> Then Iesus answered and said vnto them, I also will aske of you a certaine thing, which if ye tell me, I likewise will tell you by what authoritie I doe these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 3:20; Mark 1:1–11; John 3:18"> The baptisme of Iohn, whence was it? from heauen, or of men? Then they reasoned among themselues, saying, If we shall say, From heauen, he will say vnto vs, Why did ye not then beleeue him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 6:20; Matthew 14:5; John 5:35"> And if we say, Of men, we feare the multitude, for all holde Iohn as a Prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 1:18–22; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–10; Luke 20:7–8"> Then they answered Iesus, and said, We can not tell. And he said vnto them, Neither tell I you by what authoritie I doe these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Matthew 20:1; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> But what thinke ye? A certaine man had two sonnes, and came to the elder, and saide, Sonne, goe and worke to day in my vineyarde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:28–32; Matthew 3:2–8; Isaiah 55:6–7"> But he answered, and said, I will not: yet afterward he repented himselfe, and went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Ezekiel 33:31; Matthew 23:3"> Then came he to the second, and said likewise. And he answered, and said, I will, Syr: yet he went not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 7:29; Luke 7:37–50; Matthew 7:21"> Whether of them twaine did the will of the father? They saide vnto him, The first. Iesus saide vnto them, Verely I say vnto you, that the Publicanes and the harlots goe before you into the kingdome of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 7:37–50; Zechariah 7:11–12; Luke 7:29–30"> For Iohn came vnto you in the way of righteousnes, and yee beleeued him not: but the Publicanes, and the harlots beleeued him, and ye, though ye sawe it, were not mooued with repentance afterward, that ye might beleeue him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–4; Mark 12:1–12; Luke 20:9–19"> Heare another parable, There was a certaine housholder, which planted a vineyard, and hedged it round about, and made a winepresse therein, and built a tower, and let it out to husbandmen, and went into a strange countrey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 20:10–19; Isaiah 5:4; Zechariah 1:3–6"> And when the time of the fruite drewe neere, hee sent his seruants to the husbandmen to receiue the fruites thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Matthew 5:12; Hebrews 11:36–37"> And ye husbandmen tooke his seruants and beat one, and killed another, and stoned another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 22:4"> Againe hee sent other seruants, moe then the first: and they did the like vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 3:16; John 3:35–36; Zephaniah 3:7"> But last of all he sent vnto them his owne sonne, saying, They will reuerence my sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 20:14; Genesis 37:18–20; Mark 12:7–8"> But when the husbandmen saw the sonne, they saide among themselues, This is the heire: come, let vs kill him, and let vs take his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 2:23; James 5:6; Luke 22:52–54"> So they tooke him, and cast him out of the vineyarde, and slewe him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 10:29; Mark 12:9; Luke 20:15–16"> When therefore the Lord of the vineyarde shall come, what will hee doe to those husbandmen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Matthew 21:43; Acts 28:28"> They saide vnto him, Hee will cruelly destroy those wicked men, and will let out his vineyard vnto other husbandmen, which shall deliuer him the fruites in their seasons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Isaiah 28:16; Acts 4:11"> Iesus saide vnto them, Read ye neuer in the Scriptures, The stone which the builders refused, the same is made the head of the corner? This was the Lordes doing, and it is marueilous in our eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 21:41; Matthew 8:11–12; 1 Peter 2:9"> Therefore say I vnto you, The kingdome of God shalbe taken from you, and shalbe giuen to a nation, which shall bring foorth the fruites thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; 1 Peter 2:8; Daniel 2:34–35"> And whosoeuer shall fall on this stone, he shalbe broken: but on whomsoeuer it shall fall, it will dash him a pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 20:19; Luke 11:45; Matthew 12:12"> And when the chiefe Priestes and Pharises had heard his parables, they perceiued that hee spake of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; Matthew 21:26; Luke 7:16"> And they seeking to laye handes on him, feared the people, because they tooke him as a Prophet.</span>
