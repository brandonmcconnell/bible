<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1–3; Acts 10:38; Luke 8:1"> And it came to passe that when Iesus had made an ende of commaunding his twelue disciples, hee departed thence to teache and to preach in their cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 6:17; Matthew 14:3; Matthew 9:14"> And when Iohn heard in the prison the woorkes of Christ, he sent two of his disciples, and sayde vnto him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:27; Malachi 3:1; Deuteronomy 18:15–18"> Art thou he that shoulde come, or shall we looke for another?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And Iesus answering, said vnto them, Goe, and shewe Iohn, what things ye heare, and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4–6; Isaiah 61:1–3; Luke 4:18"> The blinde receiue sight, and the halt doe walke: the lepers are clensed, and the deafe heare, the dead are raised vp, and the poore receiue the Gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 26:31"> And blessed is he that shall not be offeded in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:6; John 5:35; Mark 1:3–5"> And as they departed, Iesus beganne to speake vnto the multitude, of Iohn, What went ye out into the wildernes to see? A reede shaken with the winde?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; 2 Kings 1:8; Zechariah 13:4"> But what went ye out to see? A man clothed in soft raiment? Behold, they that weare soft clothing, are in Kings houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:76; Matthew 14:5; Matthew 21:24–26"> But what went ye out to see? A Prophet? Yea, I say vnto you, and more then a Prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 40:3; Malachi 4:5"> For this is he of whom it is written, Beholde, I send my messenger before thy face, which shall prepare thy way before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 7:28; Ephesians 3:8; Matthew 3:11"> Verely I say vnto you, among them which are begotten of women, arose there not a greater then Iohn Baptist: notwithstanding, he that is the least in the kingdome of heauen, is greater then he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 16:16; Luke 13:24; Ephesians 6:11–13"> And from the time of Iohn Baptist hitherto, the kingdome of heauen suffereth violence, and the violent take it by force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:17–18; Romans 3:21; Acts 13:27"> For all the Prophetes and the Lawe prophecied vnto Iohn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Matthew 17:10–13; Mark 9:11–13"> And if ye will receiue it, this is that Elias, which was to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 8:8; Matthew 13:9; Mark 4:23"> He that hath eares to heare, let him heare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 7:31–35; Matthew 12:34; Matthew 23:36"> But whereunto shall I liken this generation? It is like vnto litle children which sit in the markets, and call vnto their fellowes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 28:9–13; Isaiah 30:29; Jeremiah 9:17–20"> And say, We haue piped vnto you, and ye haue not daunced, we haue mourned vnto you, and ye haue not lamented.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Matthew 3:4; John 10:20"> For Iohn came neither eating nor drinking, and they say, He hath a deuill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 9:10–11; Luke 15:1–2; Luke 19:7"> The sonne of man came eating and drinking, and they say, Beholde a glutton and a drinker of wine, a friend vnto Publicanes and sinners: but wisedome is iustified of her children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:13–15; Jeremiah 8:6; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> Then began he to vpbraide the cities, wherein most of his great workes were done, because they repented not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:6–7; Acts 12:20; Luke 9:10"> Woe be to thee, Chorazin: Woe be to thee, Bethsaida: for if ye great workes, which were done in you, had bene done in Tyrus and Sidon, they had repented long agone in sackecloth and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 11:24; Matthew 10:15; Hebrews 10:26–31"> But I say to you, It shalbe easier for Tyrus and Sidon at the day of iudgement, then for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–15; John 4:46–54; Genesis 13:13"> And thou, Capernaum, which art lifted vp vnto heauen, shalt be brought downe to hell: for if the great workes, which haue bin done in thee, had bene done among them of Sodom, they had remained to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:15; Matthew 11:22; Mark 6:11"> But I say vnto you, that it shall be easier for them of the land of Sodom in the day of iudgement, then for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 21:16; Psalms 8:2; Matthew 16:17"> At that time Iesus answered, and saide, I giue thee thankes, O Father, Lord of heauen and earth, because thou hast hid these things from the wise and men of vnderstanding, and hast opened them vnto babes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:11; Job 33:13"> It is so, O Father, because thy good pleasure was such.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:6–9; Matthew 28:18; Luke 10:22"> All things are giuen vnto me of my Father: and no man knoweth the Sonne, but ye Father: neither knoweth any man ye Father, but the Sonne, and he to whom ye Sonne will reueile him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; John 7:37; John 6:37"> Come vnto me, all ye that are wearie and laden, and I will ease you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; 1 John 2:6; Philippians 2:7–8"> Take my yoke on you, and learne of me that I am meeke and lowly in heart: and ye shall finde rest vnto your soules.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 5:3; John 16:33; Philippians 4:13"> For my yoke is easie, and my burden light.</span>
