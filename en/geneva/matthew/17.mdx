<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 9:28–36; Matthew 26:37; Mark 5:37"> And after sixe dayes, Iesus tooke Peter, and Iames and Iohn his brother, and brought them vp into an hie mountaine apart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 104:2; Matthew 28:3; Luke 9:29"> And was transfigured before them: and his face did shine as the Sunne, and his clothes were as white as the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:44; Mark 9:4; Malachi 4:5"> And beholde, there appeared vnto them Moses, and Elias, talking with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 9:5–6; Luke 9:33; John 14:8–9"> Then answered Peter, and saide to Iesus, Master, it is good for vs to be here: if thou wilt, let vs make here three tabernacles, one for thee, and one for Moses, and one for Elias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:17; Matthew 12:18; Deuteronomy 18:15"> While he yet spake, behold, a bright cloude shadowed them: and beholde, there came a voyce out of the cloude, saying, This is that my beloued Sonne, in whom I am well pleased: heare him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 26:14; 2 Peter 1:18; Daniel 8:17"> And when the disciples heard that, they fell on their faces, and were sore afraide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 10:18; Matthew 14:27; Daniel 8:18"> Then Iesus came and touched them, and said, Arise, and be not afraide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:36; Mark 9:8; Acts 12:10–11"> And when they lifted vp their eyes, they sawe no man, saue Iesus onely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 8:30; Mark 9:9–13; Matthew 17:23"> And as they came downe from the moutaine, Iesus charged them, saying, Shewe the vision to no man, vntil the Sonne of man rise againe from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 4:5–6; Matthew 11:14; John 1:21"> And his disciples asked him, saying, Why then say the Scribes that Elias must first come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 3:21; Luke 1:16–17; Malachi 4:6"> And Iesus answered, and saide vnto them, Certeinely Elias must first come, and restore all thinges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3–12; Matthew 16:21; Acts 13:24–28"> But I say vnto you that Elias is come alreadie, and they knewe him not, but haue done vnto him whatsoeuer they would: likewise shall also the Sonne of man suffer of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 11:14"> Then the disciples perceiued that he spake vnto them of Iohn Baptist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 9:14–29; Mark 1:40; Acts 10:25–26"> And when they were come to the multitude, there came to him a certaine man, and fell downe at his feete,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Job 1:10–19; Mark 9:20–22"> And saide, Master, haue pitie on my sonne: for he is lunatike, and is sore vexed: for oft times he falleth into the fire, and oft times into the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Luke 9:40; Acts 19:15–16"> And I brought him to thy disciples, and they could not heale him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; Matthew 6:30; Mark 16:14"> Then Iesus answered, and said, O generation faithlesse, and crooked, how long now shall I be with you! howe long nowe shall I suffer you! bring him hither to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 9:22; Mark 1:34; Mark 5:8"> And Iesus rebuked the deuill, and he went out of him: and the childe was healed at that houre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 9:28; Mark 4:10"> Then came the disciples to Iesus apart, and said, Why could not we cast him out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 17:6; Mark 11:23; Luke 1:37"> And Iesus said vnto them, Because of your vnbeliefe: for verely I say vnto you, if ye haue faith as much as is a graine of mustarde seede, ye shall say vnto this mountaine, Remooue hence to yonder place, and it shall remoue: and nothing shalbe vnpossible vnto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Howbeit this kinde goeth not out, but by prayer and fasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Luke 18:31–34; Matthew 17:23"> And they being in Galile, Iesus said vnto them, The Sonne of man shall be deliuered into the handes of men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Isaiah 53:10–12; Mark 8:31"> And they shall kill him, but the thirde day shall he rise againe: and they were very sorie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 38:26; Exodus 30:13; Mark 9:33"> And when they were come to Capernaum, they that receiued polle money, came to Peter, and sayd, Doeth not your Master pay polle money?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 22:21; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 22:17"> He sayd, Yes. And when he was come into the house, Iesus preuented him, saying, What thinkest thou, Simon? Of whome doe the Kings of the earth take tribute, or polle money? of their children, or of strangers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Peter sayd vnto him, Of strangers. Then said Iesus vnto him, Then are the children free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; 1 Corinthians 8:9; 1 Corinthians 9:19–22"> Neuerthelesse, lest we should offend them: goe to the sea, and cast in an angle, and take the first fish that commeth vp, and when thou hast opened his mouth, thou shalt finde a piece of twentie pence: that take, and giue it vnto them for me and thee.</span>
