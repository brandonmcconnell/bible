<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 9:1–6; Mark 3:13–15; Mark 6:7–13"> And hee called his twelue disciples vnto him, and gaue them power against vncleane spirits, to cast them out, and to heale euery sickenesse, and euery disease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Acts 1:13; Luke 5:10"> Nowe the names of the twelue Apostles are these. The first is Simon, called Peter, and Andrew his brother: Iames the sonne of Zebedeus, and Iohn his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 3:18; Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14"> Philippe and Bartlemewe: Thomas, and Matthewe that Publicane: Iames the sonne of Alpheus, and Lebbeus whose surname was Thaddeus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 26:14; Matthew 26:47; John 13:2"> Simon the Cananite, and Iudas Iscariot, who also betraied him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 4:9; Acts 22:21–23; 2 Kings 17:24–41"> These twelue did Iesus send forth, and commanded them, saying, Goe not into the way of of the Gentiles, and into the cities of the Samaritans enter yee not:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:6; Ezekiel 34:6; Isaiah 53:6"> But goe rather to the lost sheepe of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 28:31; Matthew 3:2; Luke 9:60"> And as ye goe, preach, saying, The kingdome of heauen is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 3:6; Luke 10:9; Acts 4:30"> Heale the sicke: cleanse the lepers: raise vp the dead: cast out the deuils. Freely ye haue receiued, freely giue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Luke 9:3–5; Luke 10:4–12"> Possesse not golde, nor siluer, nor money in your girdels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 10:7–12; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:4–14"> Nor a scrippe for the iourney, neither two coates, neither shoes, nor a staffe: for the workeman is worthie of his meate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 10:7–8; Job 31:32; Judges 19:16–21"> And into whatsoeuer citie or towne ye shall come, enquire who is worthy in it, and there abide till yee goe thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:6; Luke 10:5–6; Acts 10:36"> And when yee come into an house, salute the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 10:6; Psalms 35:13; 2 Corinthians 2:16"> And if the house be worthy, let your peace come vpon it: but if it be not worthie, let your peace returne to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 10:10–11; Acts 18:6; Nehemiah 5:13"> And whosoeuer shall not receiue you, nor heare your woordes, when yee depart out of that house, or that citie, shake off the dust of your feete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6; 1 John 4:17; John 15:22–24"> Truely I say vnto you, it shall be easier for them of the lande of Sodom and Gomorrha in the day of iudgement, then for that citie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:3; Philippians 2:15; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> Behold, I send you as sheepe in the middes of the wolues: be yee therefore wise as serpents, and innocent as doues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 13:9; Matthew 23:34; Acts 26:11"> But beware of men, for they will deliuer you vp to the Councils, and will scourge you in their Synagogues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:25–27; Matthew 8:4; Psalms 2:1–6"> And ye shall be brought to the gouernours and Kings for my sake, in witnes to them, and to the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 4:12; Mark 13:11–13; 2 Timothy 4:17"> But when they deliuer you vp, take no thought howe or what ye shall speake: for it shall be giuen you in that houre, what ye shall say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10; Luke 12:12"> For it is not yee that speake, but the spirite of your father which speaketh in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 21:16–17; Micah 7:5–6; Mark 13:12–13"> And the brother shall betray the brother to death, and the father the sonne, and the children shall rise against their parents, and shall cause them to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 24:13; James 1:12; Revelation 2:10"> And yee shall be hated of all men for my Name: but he that endureth to the end, he shall be saued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:28; Acts 8:1; Mark 13:26"> And when they persecute you in this citie, flee into another: for verely I say vnto you, yee shall not goe ouer all the cities of Israel, till the Sonne of man be come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 15:20; Luke 6:40; John 13:16"> The disciple is not aboue his master, nor the seruant aboue his Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:15; Matthew 12:24; Matthew 9:34"> It is ynough for the disciple to bee as his master is, and the seruaunt as his Lord. If they haue called the master of the house Beel-zebub, howe much more them of his housholde?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 8:17; Mark 4:22; Jeremiah 1:8"> Feare them not therefore: for there is nothing couered, that shall not be disclosed, nor hid, that shall not be knowen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 5:20; John 16:13; John 16:1"> What I tell you in darkenesse, that speake yee in light: and what yee heare in the eare, that preach yee on the houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:4–5; Isaiah 8:12–13; Revelation 2:10"> And feare yee not them which kill the bodie, but are nor able to kill the soule: but rather feare him, which is able to destroy both soule and bodie in hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 12:6–7; Psalms 104:27–30"> Are not two sparrowes sold for a farthing, and one of them shall not fal on the ground without your Father?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 12:7; 1 Samuel 14:45; Acts 27:34"> Yea, and all the heares of your head are nombred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 12:24; Psalms 8:5; Matthew 6:26"> Feare ye not therefore, yee are of more value then many sparowes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 3:5; Romans 10:9–10; Luke 12:8–9"> Whosoeuer therefore shall confesse me before men, him will I confesse also before my Father which is in heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:12; Luke 9:26; Luke 12:9"> But whosoeuer shall denie me before me, him will I also denie before my Father which is in heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 12:49–53; Acts 14:4; Acts 13:45–50"> Thinke not that I am come to sende peace into the earth: I came not to send peace, but the sworde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Matthew 10:21; Luke 21:16"> For I am come to set a man at variance against his father, and the daughter against her mother, and the daughter in law against her mother in lawe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Micah 7:6; Jeremiah 12:6; Psalms 41:9"> And a mans enemies shall be they of his owne housholde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 14:26; Matthew 22:37; Philippians 3:7–9"> He that loueth father or mother more then me, is not worthie of me. And he that loueth sonne, or daughter more then mee, is not worthie of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 14:27; Mark 8:34; Matthew 16:24"> And hee that taketh not his crosse, and followeth after me, is not worthie of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 12:25; Mark 8:35–36; Luke 17:33"> He that will finde his life, shall lose it: and he that loseth his life for my sake, shall finde it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 13:20; Luke 9:48; Luke 10:16"> He that receiueth you, receiueth me: and hee that receiueth mee, receiueth him that hath sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="3 John 1:5–8; Hebrews 6:10; 2 Kings 4:16–17"> Hee that receiueth a Prophet in the name of a Prophet, shall receiue a Prophetes rewarde: and hee that receiueth a righteous man, in the name of a righteous man, shall receiue the rewarde of a righteous man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10; Matthew 25:40; Mark 9:41–42"> And whosoeuer shall giue vnto one of these litle ones to drinke a cuppe of colde water onely, in the name of a disciple, verely I say vnto you, he shall not lose his rewarde.</span>
