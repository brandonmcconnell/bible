<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 88:7; Job 19:21; Psalms 88:15–16"> I am the man, that hath seene affliction in the rod of his indignation.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Job 31:21; Isaiah 1:25"> Surely he is turned against me: he turneth his hand against me all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 38:13; Psalms 51:8; Psalms 31:9–10"> My flesh and my skinne hath he caused to waxe olde, and he hath broken my bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 3:19; Jeremiah 23:15; Job 19:8"> He hath builded against me, and compassed me with gall, and labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 88:5–6; Psalms 143:3; Psalms 143:7"> He hath set me in darke places, as they that be dead for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 19:8; Job 3:23; Jeremiah 40:4"> He hath hedged about mee, that I cannot get out: he hath made my chaines heauy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 30:20; Psalms 22:2; Job 19:7"> Also when I cry and showte, hee shutteth out my prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 63:17; Lamentations 3:11; Isaiah 30:28"> He hath stopped vp my wayes with hewen stone, and turned away my paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 10:9; Amos 5:18–20; Hosea 13:7–8"> He was vnto me as a beare lying in waite, and as a Lion in secret places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 6:1; Job 16:12–13; Lamentations 1:13"> He hath stopped my wayes, and pulled me in pieces: he hath made me desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 38:2; Job 7:20; Job 6:4"> He hath bent his bow and made me a marke for the arrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 6:4; Job 41:28; Deuteronomy 32:23"> Hee caused the arrowes of his quiuer to enter into my reines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:7; Lamentations 3:63; Psalms 22:6–7"> I was a derision to all my people, and their song all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:15; Jeremiah 23:15; Ruth 1:20"> He hath filled me with bitternes, and made me drunken with wormewood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 20:17; Jeremiah 6:26; Psalms 3:7"> He hath also broken my teeth with stones, and hath couered me with ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 59:11; Zechariah 8:10; Jeremiah 8:15"> Thus my soule was farre off from peace: I forgate prosperitie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 17:15; 1 Samuel 27:1; Psalms 116:11"> And I saide, My strength and mine hope is perished from the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Lamentations 3:15; Lamentations 3:5; Psalms 89:50"> Remembring mine affliction, and my mourning, the wormewood and the gall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 42:11; Psalms 42:5–6; Job 21:6"> My soule hath them in remembrance, and is humbled in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; Psalms 130:7; Psalms 77:7–11"> I consider this in mine heart: therefore haue I hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Luke 1:50; Nehemiah 9:31"> It is the Lordes mercies that wee are not consumed, because his compassions faile not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 10:23; Psalms 30:5; Isaiah 33:2"> They are renued euery morning: great is thy faithfulnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Psalms 16:5; Lamentations 3:21"> The Lord is my portion, sayth my soule: therefore wil I hope in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Micah 7:7–8; Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 130:5–6"> The Lord is good vnto them, that trust in him, and to the soule that seeketh him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 37:34; Psalms 130:5"> It is good both to trust, and to waite for the saluation of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:1; Matthew 11:29–30; Psalms 119:71"> It is good for a man that he beare the yoke in his youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:17; Lamentations 2:10; Psalms 102:7"> He sitteth alone, and keepeth silence, because he hath borne it vpon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 40:4; Luke 15:18–19; Romans 3:19"> He putteth his mouth in the dust, if there may be hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 16:10; Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 26:67"> Hee giueth his cheeke to him that smiteth him: he is filled full with reproches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 94:14; Psalms 103:8–10; Psalms 77:7"> For the Lord will not forsake for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 78:38; Psalms 30:5; Psalms 103:11"> But though he sende affliction, yet will he haue compassion according to the multitude of his mercies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:11; Ezekiel 18:32; Hebrews 12:9–10"> For he doeth not punish willingly, nor afflict the children of men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11–12; Isaiah 51:22–23; Psalms 102:20"> In stamping vnder his feete all the prisoners of the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> In ouerthrowing the right of a man before the face of the most high,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 59:15; Habakkuk 1:13; Jeremiah 22:3"> In subuerting a man in his cause: the Lord seeth it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Psalms 33:9–11; Isaiah 46:10; Proverbs 16:9"> Who is he then that sayth, and it commeth to passe, and the Lord commandeth it not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Amos 3:6; Jeremiah 32:42"> Out of the mouth of the most high proceedeth not euill and good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 19:3; Micah 7:9; Genesis 4:5–7"> Wherefore then is the liuing man sorowfull? man suffreth for his sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:5; Psalms 139:23–24; Joel 2:12–13"> Let vs search and try our wayes, and turne againe to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 25:1; Psalms 28:2; Psalms 86:4"> Let vs lift vp our hearts with our handes vnto God in the heauens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 15:18–19; Nehemiah 9:26; 2 Kings 24:4"> We haue sinned, and haue rebelled, therefore thou hast not spared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Lamentations 2:17; Lamentations 2:21; Lamentations 3:66"> Thou hast couered vs with wrath, and persecuted vs: thou hast slaine and not spared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 3:8; Zechariah 7:13; Psalms 97:2"> Thou hast couered thy selfe with a cloude, that our prayer should not passe through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:13; Lamentations 2:15; Lamentations 3:14"> Thou hast made vs as the ofscouring and refuse in the middes of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 22:6–8; Lamentations 2:16; Psalms 44:13–14"> All our enemies haue opened their mouth against vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Isaiah 24:17–18; Isaiah 51:19; Jeremiah 48:43–44"> Feare, and a snare is come vpon vs with desolation and destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Jeremiah 9:18; Jeremiah 9:1"> Mine eye casteth out riuers of water, for the destruction of the daughter of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:17; Psalms 77:2; Lamentations 1:16"> Mine eye droppeth without stay and ceaseth not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Isaiah 63:15; Lamentations 5:1; Daniel 9:16–19"> Till the Lord looke downe, and beholde from heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:9; Luke 19:41–44; Lamentations 1:18"> Mine eye breaketh mine heart because of all the daughters of my citie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Psalms 35:7; Jeremiah 37:15–16"> Mine enemies chased me sore like a birde, without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Daniel 6:17; Jeremiah 38:9; Jeremiah 38:6"> They haue shut vp my life in the dungeon, and cast a stone vpon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Jonah 2:3–5; Ezekiel 37:11; Lamentations 3:18"> Waters flowed ouer mine head, then thought I, I am destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Acts 16:24–28; Psalms 142:3–7; Jonah 2:2–4"> I called vpon thy Name, O Lord, out of the lowe dungeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Isaiah 38:5; Job 34:28; Psalms 6:8–9"> Thou hast heard my voyce: stoppe not thine eare from my sigh and from my cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 41:10; Isaiah 58:9"> Thou drewest neere in the day that I called vpon thee: thou saydest, Feare not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Psalms 34:22–1; Jeremiah 51:36; Psalms 103:4"> O Lord, thou hast maintained the cause of my soule, and hast redeemed my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 9:4; Genesis 31:42"> O Lord, thou hast seene my wrong, iudge thou my cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:19–20; Psalms 10:14; Lamentations 3:59"> Thou hast seene all their vengeance, and all their deuises against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Lamentations 5:1; Psalms 89:50; Zephaniah 2:8"> Thou hast heard their reproch, O Lord, and all their imaginations against me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:3; Psalms 59:7; Psalms 140:3"> The lippes also of those that rose against me, and their whispering against me continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Psalms 139:2; Lamentations 3:14; Job 30:9"> Behold, their sitting downe and their rising vp, how I am their song.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Psalms 28:4; Jeremiah 11:20; 2 Timothy 4:14"> Giue them a recompence, O Lord, according to the worke of their handes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; 1 Corinthians 16:22; Deuteronomy 2:30"> Giue them sorow of heart, euen thy curse to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Psalms 8:3; Lamentations 3:43; Psalms 115:16"> Persecute with wrath and destroy them from vnder the heauen, O Lord.</span>
