<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 12:1; Hosea 9:3; Hosea 7:11"> We haue giuen our handes to the Egyptians, and to Asshur, to be satisfied with bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:29; Jeremiah 16:12; Ezekiel 18:2"> Our fathers haue sinned, and are not, and we haue borne their iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:15; Zechariah 11:6; Proverbs 30:22"> Seruants haue ruled ouer vs, none would deliuer vs out of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16–17; Jeremiah 40:9–12; Ezekiel 12:18–19"> Wee gate our bread with the perill of our liues, because of the sword of the wildernesse.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 13:16; Zechariah 14:2; Deuteronomy 28:30"> They defiled the women in Zion, and the maydes in the cities of Iudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Lamentations 4:16; Isaiah 47:6; Lamentations 2:20"> The princes are hanged vp by their hande: the faces of the elders were not had in honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 16:21; Exodus 11:5; Matthew 23:4"> They tooke the yong men to grinde, and the children fell vnder the wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Isaiah 24:7–11; Ezekiel 26:13"> The Elders haue ceased from the gate and the yong men from their songs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 8:10; Jeremiah 25:10; James 4:9–10"> The ioy of our heart is gone, our daunce is turned into mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 89:39; Jeremiah 13:18; Isaiah 3:9–11"> The crowne of our head is fallen: wo nowe vnto vs, that we haue sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 1:5; Job 17:7; Lamentations 2:11"> Therefore our heart is heauy for these things, our eyes are dimme,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:12; Jeremiah 9:11; Psalms 74:2–3"> Because of the mountaine of Zion which is desolate: the foxes runne vpon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 102:12; Hebrews 1:8–12; Psalms 45:6"> But thou, O Lord, remainest for euer: thy throne is from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 44:24; Psalms 13:1; Jeremiah 14:19–21"> Wherefore doest thou forget vs for euer, and forsake vs so long time?</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 60:1–2; Ezekiel 37:11; Psalms 44:9"> But thou hast vtterly reiected vs: thou art exceedingly angry against vs.</span>
