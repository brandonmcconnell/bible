<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2; Psalms 132:7; Matthew 11:23"> How hath the Lord darkened the daughter of Zion in his wrath! and hath cast downe from heauen vnto the earth the beautie of Israel, and remembred not his footestoole in the day of his wrath!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Lamentations 2:17; Psalms 89:39–40; Lamentations 3:43"> The Lord hath destroyed al the habitations of Iaakob, and not spared: he hath throwen downe in his wrath ye strong holds of the daughter of Iudah: he hath cast the downe to ye ground: he hath polluted the kingdome and the princes thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 74:11; Psalms 75:10; Psalms 75:5"> Hee hath cut off in his fierce wrath all the horne of Israel: he hath drawen backe his right hand from before the enemie, and there was kindled in Iaakob like a flame of fire, which deuoured rounde about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 21:5; Lamentations 3:12–13"> He hath bent his bowe like an enemie: his right hand was stretched vp as an aduersarie, and slewe al that was pleasant to the eye in the tabernacle of the daughter of Zion: he powred out his wrath like fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 2:2; Jeremiah 30:14; Jeremiah 52:13"> The Lord was as an enemie: he hath deuoured Israel, and consumed all his palaces: hee hath destroyed his strong holdes, and hath increased in the daughter of Iudah lamentation and mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Lamentations 1:4; Zephaniah 3:18; Lamentations 4:16"> For hee hath destroyed his Tabernacle, as a garden, hee hath destroyed his Congregation: the Lord hath caused the feastes and Sabbathes to bee forgotten in Zion, and hath despised in the indignation of his wrath the King and the Priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:21; Amos 2:5; Psalms 74:3–8"> The Lord hath forsaken his altar: he hath abhorred his Sanctuarie: he hath giue into the hand of the enemie the walles of her palaces: they haue made a noyse in the House of the Lord, as in the day of solemnitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Isaiah 34:11; Isaiah 3:26"> The Lord hath determined to destroy the wall of the daughter of Zion: hee stretched out a lyne: hee hath not withdrawen his hande from destroying: therefore hee made the rampart and the wall to lament: they were destroyed together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:26; Hosea 3:4; 2 Chronicles 15:3"> Her gates are sunke to the grounde: he hath destroyed and broken her barres: her King and her princes are among the Gentiles: the Lawe is no more, neither can her Prophets receiue any vision from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 3:26; Isaiah 15:3; Job 2:12–13"> The Elders of the daughter of Zion sit vpon the grounde, and keepe silence: they haue cast vp dust vpon their heades: they haue girded them selues with sackecloth: the virgines of Ierusalem hang downe their heades to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 1:20; Job 16:13; Lamentations 1:16"> Mine eyes doe saile with teares: my bowels swell: my liuer is powred vpon the earth, for the destruction of the daughter of my people, because the children and sucklings swoone in the streetes of the citie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Ezekiel 30:24"> They haue sayd to their mothers, Where is bread and drinke? when they swooned as the wounded in the streetes of the citie, and whe they gaue vp the ghost in their mothers bosome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Lamentations 1:12; Jeremiah 8:22; 2 Samuel 5:20"> What thing shall I take to witnesse for thee? what thing shall I compare to thee, O daughter Ierusalem? what shall I liken to thee, that I may comfort thee, O virgine daughter Zion? for thy breach is great like ye sea: who can heale thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:28; Isaiah 58:1; Jeremiah 5:31"> Thy Prophets haue looked out vayne, and foolish things for thee, and they haue not discouered thine iniquitie, to turne away thy captiuitie, but haue looked out for thee false prophesies, and causes of banishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:16; Psalms 48:2; Psalms 50:2"> All that passe by the way, clap their hands at thee: they hisse and wagge their head vpon the daughter Ierusalem, saying, Is this the citie that men call, The perfection of beautie, and the ioye of the whole earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 35:21; Lamentations 3:46; Job 16:9–10"> All thine enemies haue opened their mouth against thee: they hisse and gnashe the teeth, saying, Let vs deuoure it: certainely this is the day that we looked for: we haue founde and seene it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 89:42; Ezekiel 5:11; Deuteronomy 31:16–17"> The Lord hath done that which he had purposed: he hath fulfilled his worde that he had determined of old time: he hath throwen downe, and not spared: hee hath caused thine enemie to reioyce ouer thee, and set vp the horne of thine aduersaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:145; Jeremiah 9:1; Lamentations 2:8"> Their heart cryed vnto the Lord, O wall of the daughter Zion, let teares runne downe like a riuer, day and night: take thee no rest, neither let the apple of thine eye cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Psalms 119:147–148; Isaiah 26:9"> Arise, cry in the night: in the beginning of the watches powre out thine heart like water before the face of the Lord: lift vp thine handes towarde him for the life of thy yong children, that faint for hunger in the corners of all the streetes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:9; Lamentations 4:10; Psalms 78:64"> Beholde, O Lord, and consider to whome thou hast done thus: shall the women eate their fruite, and children of a spanne long? shall the Priest and the Prophet be slaine in the Sanctuarie of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:17; Lamentations 3:43; Zechariah 11:6"> The yong and the olde lie on the ground in the streetes: my virgins and my yong men are fallen by the sworde: thou hast slaine them in the day of thy wrath: thou hast killed and not spared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:25; Psalms 31:13; Jeremiah 16:2–4"> Thou hast called as in a solemne daye my terrours rounde about, so that in the day of the Lordes wrath none escaped nor remained: those that I haue nourished and brought vp, hath mine enemie consumed.</span>
