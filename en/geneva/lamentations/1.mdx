<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 3:26; Ezra 4:20; 1 Kings 4:21"> Howe doeth the citie remaine solitarie that was full of people? she is as a widowe: she that was great among the nations, and princesse among the prouinces, is made tributarie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Micah 7:5; Psalms 77:2–6"> She weepeth continually in the night, and her teares runne downe by her cheekes: among all her louers, she hath none to comfort her: all her friendes haue delt vnfaithfully with her, and are her enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64–67; Lamentations 2:9; Amos 9:1–4"> Iudah is caried away captiue because of affliction, and because of great seruitude: shee dwelleth among the heathen, and findeth no rest: all her persecuters tooke her in the straites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Joel 1:8–13; Jeremiah 10:22"> The wayes of Zion lament, because no man commeth to the solemne feastes: all her gates are desolate: her Priests sigh: her virgins are discomfited, and she is in heauinesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15–27; Jeremiah 30:14–15; Ezekiel 9:9"> Her aduersaries are the chiefe, and her enemies prosper: for the Lord hath afflicted her, for the multitude of her transgressions, and her children are gone into captiuitie before the enemie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:25; Jeremiah 13:18; Deuteronomy 28:25"> And from the daughter of Zion all her beautie is departed: her princes are become like harts that finde no pasture, and they are gone without strength before the pursuer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Deuteronomy 4:34–37; Psalms 42:4"> Ierusalem remembred the dayes of her affliction, and of her rebellion, and all her pleasant things, that shee had in times past, when her people fell into the hande of the enemie, and none did helpe her: the aduersarie sawe her, and did mocke at her Sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 59:2–13; Lamentations 1:20–22; Jeremiah 13:26"> Ierusalem hath grieuously sinned, therefore shee is in derision: all that honoured her, despise her, because they haue seene her filthinesse: yea, she sigheth and turneth backeward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Isaiah 47:7; Psalms 25:18"> Her filthinesse is in her skirts: she remembred not her last ende, therefore she came downe wonderfully: she had no comforter: O Lord, behold mine affliction: for the enemie is proud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:3; Jeremiah 51:51; Psalms 74:4–8"> The enemie hath stretched out his hande vpon al her pleasant things: for she hath seene the heathen enter into her Sanctuarie, whom thou diddest commande, that they shoulde not enter into thy Church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:6; Jeremiah 38:9; Lamentations 2:12"> All her people sigh and seeke their bread: they haue giuen their pleasant thinges for meate to refresh the soule: see, O Lord, and consider: for I am become vile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 23:28–31; Daniel 9:12; Jeremiah 30:24"> Haue ye no regarde, all yee that passe by this way? behold, and see, if there be any sorowe like vnto my sorowe, which is done vnto mee, wherewith the Lord hath afflicted me in the day of his fierce wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 30:30; Habakkuk 3:16; Ezekiel 17:20"> From aboue hath hee sent fire into my bones, which preuaile against them: he hath spred a net for my feete, and turned me backe: hee hath made me desolate, and daily in heauinesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:48; Isaiah 47:6; Proverbs 5:22"> The yoke of my transgressions is bounde vpon his hand: they are wrapped, and come vp vpon my necke: hee hath made my strength to fall: the Lord hath deliuered me into their hands, neither am I able to rise vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Isaiah 28:18; Isaiah 63:3"> The Lord hath troden vnder foote all my valiant men in the middes of me: he hath called an assembly against me to destroy my yong men: the Lord hath troden the wine presse vpon the virgine the daughter of Iudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 1:2; Jeremiah 13:17; Psalms 119:136"> For these things I weepe: mine eye, euen mine eye casteth out water, because the comforter that should refresh my soule, is farre from me: my children are desolate, because the enemie preuailed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; Jeremiah 4:31; Lamentations 1:9"> Zion stretcheth out her handes, and there is none to comfort her: the Lord hath appoynted the enemies of Iaakob rounde about him: Ierusalem is as a menstruous woman in the middes of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:14–15; Zephaniah 3:5; Psalms 119:75"> The Lord is righteous: for I haue rebelled against his commandement: heare, I pray you, all people, and behold my sorowe: my virgins and my yong men are gone into captiuitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Lamentations 1:2; Lamentations 1:11; Lamentations 2:20"> I called for my louers, but they deceiued me: my Priestes and mine Elders perished in the citie while they sought their meate to refresh their soules.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Deuteronomy 32:25; Isaiah 16:11"> Behold, O Lord, howe I am troubled: my bowels swell: mine heart is turned within me, for I am ful of heauinesse: the sword spoyleth abroad, as death doeth at home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Lamentations 1:8; Lamentations 2:15; Lamentations 1:16"> They haue heard that I mourne, but there is none to comfort mee: all mine enemies haue heard of my trouble, and are glad, that thou hast done it: thou wilt bring the day, that thou hast pronounced, and they shalbe like vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:18; Psalms 109:14–15; Revelation 6:10"> Let all their wickednes come before thee: do vnto them, as thou hast done vnto me, for all my transgressions: for my sighes are many, and mine heart is heauy.</span>
