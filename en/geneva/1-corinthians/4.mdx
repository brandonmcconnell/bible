<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Titus 1:7; 1 Timothy 3:9"> Let a man so thinke of vs, as of the ministers of Christ, and disposers of the secrets of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Luke 12:42; Luke 16:10–12"> And as for the rest, it is required of the disposers, that euery man be found faithfull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 7:24; 1 Corinthians 2:15; 1 Samuel 16:7"> As touching me, I passe very litle to be iudged of you, or of mans iudgement: no, I iudge not mine owne selfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 143:2; 1 John 3:20–21; Psalms 130:3"> For I know nothing by my selfe, yet am I not thereby iustified: but he that iudgeth me, is the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:7; 2 Corinthians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> Therefore iudge nothing before the time, vntill the Lord come, who will lighten things that are hid in darkenesse, and make the counsels of the hearts manifest: and then shall euery man haue praise of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:6; 2 Corinthians 11:4; 2 Corinthians 12:19"> Nowe these things, brethren, I haue figuratiuely applied vnto mine owne selfe and Apollos, for your sakes, that ye might learne by vs, that no man presume aboue that which is written, that one swell not against another for any mans cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 3:27; 1 Peter 4:10; James 1:17"> For who separateth thee? and what hast thou, that thou hast not receiued? if thou hast receiued it, why reioycest thou, as though thou haddest not receiued it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 6:25; Proverbs 13:7; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> Nowe ye are full: nowe ye are made rich: ye reigne as kings without vs, and would to God ye did reigne, that we also might reigne with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:33; Romans 8:36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–12"> For I thinke that God hath set forth vs the last Apostles, as men appointed to death: for we are made a gasing stocke vnto the worlde, and to the Angels, and to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:3; Acts 26:24; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> We are fooles for Christes sake, and ye are wise in Christ: we are weake, and ye are strong: ye are honourable, and we are despised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:35; 2 Corinthians 11:23–27; Philippians 4:12"> Vnto this houre we both hunger, and thirst, and are naked, and are buffeted, and haue no certaine dwelling place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Matthew 5:11; 1 Peter 4:12–14"> And labour, working with our owne handes: we are reuiled, and yet we blesse: we are persecuted, and suffer it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Lamentations 3:45; Acts 22:22"> We are euill spoken of, and we pray: we are made as the filth of the world, the offskowring of all things, vnto this time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Corinthians 4:15; 3 John 1:4"> I write not these things to shame you, but as my beloued children I admonish you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:19; 1 Corinthians 3:10; 1 Corinthians 9:14"> For though ye haue tenne thousand instructours in Christ, yet haue ye not many fathers: for in Christ Iesus I haue begotten you through the Gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Thessalonians 3:9; Philippians 3:17"> Wherefore, I pray you, be ye folowers of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:10; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> For this cause haue I sent vnto you Timotheus, which is my beloued sonne, and faithfull in the Lord, which shall put you in remembrance of my wayes in Christ as I teache euery where in euery Church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:6–8; 1 Corinthians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 4:21"> Some are puffed vp as though I woulde not come vnto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:1–4; Acts 18:21; Acts 19:21"> But I will come to you shortly, if the Lord will, and will knowe, not the wordes of them which are puffed vp, but the power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4; 1 Thessalonians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5"> For the kingdome of God is not in worde, but in power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:2; 2 Corinthians 13:10; 1 Thessalonians 2:7"> What will ye? shall I come vnto you with a rod, or in loue, and in ye spirite of meekenes?</span>
