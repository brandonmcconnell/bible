<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:8; 1 Corinthians 7:26–27; 1 Corinthians 7:37–38"> Nowe concerning the thinges whereof ye wrote vnto mee, It were good for a man not to touche a woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 5:28; Ephesians 5:33; 1 Corinthians 7:9"> Neuertheles, to auoide fornication, let euery man haue his wife, and let euery woman haue her owne husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Exodus 21:10"> Let the husband giue vnto the wife due beneuolence, and likewise also the wife vnto the husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 3:3; Matthew 19:9; Mark 10:11–12"> The wife hath not the power of her owne bodie, but ye husband: and likewise also the husband hath not ye power of his own body, but the wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 19:15; Joel 2:16; Zechariah 12:12–14"> Defraude not one another, except it be with consent for a time, that ye may giue your selues to fasting and praier, and againe come together that Satan tempt you not for your incontinecie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:8; 1 Corinthians 7:25; 1 Corinthians 7:12"> But I speake this by permission, not by commandement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 19:11–12; Romans 12:6; 1 Corinthians 9:5"> For I woulde that all men were euen as I my selfe am: but euery man hath his proper gift of God, one after this maner, and another after that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:32; 1 Corinthians 7:34–35; 1 Corinthians 7:26–27"> Therefore I say vnto the vnmaried, and vnto the widowes, It is good for them if they abide euen as I doe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:2; 1 Timothy 5:14; 1 Timothy 5:11"> But if they cannot abstaine, let them marrie: for it is better to marrie then to burne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 5:32; Luke 16:18; Matthew 19:3–9"> And vnto ye maried I comand, not I, but ye Lord, Let not ye wife depart from her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:1; 1 Corinthians 7:10; Isaiah 50:1"> But and if shee depart, let her remaine vnmaried, or be reconciled vnto her husband, and let not the husband put away his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:17; 1 Corinthians 7:6; 1 Corinthians 7:25"> But to ye remnant I speake, and not ye Lord, If any brother haue a wife, ye beleeueth not, if she be content to dwell with him, let him not forsake her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the woman which hath an husband that beleeueth not, if he be content to dwell with her, let her not forsake him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 2:15–16; 1 Timothy 4:5; Titus 1:15"> For the vnbeleeuing husband is sanctified to the wife, and the vnbeleeuing wife is sanctified to the husband, els were your children vncleane: but nowe are they holie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 14:19; 2 Corinthians 13:11; James 3:17–18"> But if the vnbeleeuing depart, let him depart: a brother or a sister is not in subiection in such things: but God hath called vs in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:1–2; 1 Corinthians 9:22; Romans 11:14"> For what knowest thou, O wife, whether thou shalt saue thine husband? Or what knowest thou, O man, whether thou shalt saue thy wife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:17; Romans 12:3–8; 1 Corinthians 7:24"> But as God hath distributed to euery man, as the Lord hath called euery one, so let him walke: and so ordaine I, in all Churches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 15:5; Acts 15:28; Acts 15:24"> Is any man called being circumcised? let him not gather his vncircumcision: is any called vncircumcised? let him not be circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; Galatians 5:6; 1 John 2:3–4"> Circumcision is nothing, and vncircumcision is nothing, but the keeping of the commandements of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:12"> Let euery man abide in the same vocation wherein he was called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 2:18–24; Colossians 3:11; Luke 10:40–41"> Art thou called being a seruant? care not for it: but if yet thou maiest be free, vse it rather.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:16; Philemon 1:16; Colossians 3:22–24"> For he that is called in the Lord, being. a seruant, is the Lords freeman: likewise also he that is called being free, is Christes seruant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:20; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Titus 2:14"> Yee are bought with a price: be not the seruants of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:20; Colossians 3:23–24; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> Brethren, let euery man, wherein hee was called, therein abide with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:6; 1 Corinthians 4:2; 1 Corinthians 7:40"> Nowe concerning virgines, I haue no commandement of the Lord: but I giue mine aduise, as one that hath obtained mercie of the Lord to be faithfull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Corinthians 7:8; 1 Peter 4:17"> I suppose then this to bee good for the present necessitie: I meane that it is good for a man so to be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:20; 1 Corinthians 7:12–14"> Art thou bounde vnto a wife? seeke not to be loosed: art thou loosed from a wife? seeke not a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; 1 Corinthians 7:32–36; 1 Corinthians 7:26"> But if thou takest a wife, thou sinnest not: and if a virgine marrie, shee sinneth not: neuerthelesse, such shall haue trouble in the flesh: but I spare you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 John 2:17; 1 Corinthians 7:31; Romans 13:11–12"> And this I say, brethren, because the time is short, hereafter that both they which haue wiues, be as though they had none:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; Luke 6:25; Luke 6:21"> And they that weepe, as though they wept not: and they that reioyce, as though they reioyced not: and they that bye, as though they possessed not:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 2:17; 1 Peter 4:7; James 4:14"> And they that vse this worlde, as though they vsed it not: for the fashion of this worlde goeth away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 6:25–34; 1 Timothy 5:5; Philippians 4:6"> And I would haue you without care. The vnmaried careth for the things of the Lord, howe he may please the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Colossians 3:19; Ephesians 5:25–33"> But hee that is maried, careth for the things of the world, how he may please his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 10:40–42; 1 Corinthians 6:20; Luke 2:36–37"> There is difference also betweene a virgine and a wife: the vnmaried woman careth for the things of the Lord, that she may be holy, both in body and in spirite: but shee that is maried, careth for the things of the worlde, howe shee may please her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:33–34; 1 Corinthians 7:36; Luke 8:14"> And this I speake for your owne commoditie, not to tangle you in a snare, but that yee follow that, which is honest, and that yee may cleaue fast vnto the Lord without separation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:37; 1 Corinthians 7:9; 1 Samuel 2:33"> But if any man thinke that it is vncomely for his virgine, if shee passe the flower of her age, and neede so require, let him do what he will, he sinneth not: let them be maried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Neuerthelesse, hee that standeth firme in his heart, that hee hath no neede, but hath power ouer his owne will, and hath so decreed in his heart, that hee will keepe his virgine, hee doeth well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; 1 Corinthians 7:1–2; 1 Corinthians 7:8"> So then hee that giueth her to mariage, doeth well, but he that giueth her not to mariage, doeth better.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Romans 7:2–3; 2 Corinthians 6:14–16; 1 Corinthians 7:10"> The wife is bounde by the Lawe, as long as her husband liueth: but if her husband bee dead, shee is at libertie to marie with whome she will, onely in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:35; 2 Peter 3:15–16; 1 Corinthians 7:8"> But shee is more blessed, if she so abide, in my iudgement: and I thinke that I haue also the Spirite of God.</span>
