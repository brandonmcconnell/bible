<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 5:13; 1 Corinthians 2:14–15; Ephesians 4:13–14"> And I could not speake vnto you, brethren, as vnto spirituall men, but as vnto carnall, euen as vnto babes in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 5:11–14; John 16:12; 1 Peter 2:2"> I gaue you milke to drinke, and not meat: for yee were not yet able to beare it, neither yet nowe are yee able.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:16; Galatians 5:15; Titus 3:3"> For yee are yet carnall: for whereas there is among you enuying, and strife, and diuisions, are ye not carnall, and walke as men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 4:6"> For when one sayeth, I am Pauls, and another, I am Apollos, are yee not carnall?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:4; 2 Corinthians 6:1; 1 Peter 4:10"> Who is Paul then? and who is Apollos, but the ministers by whome yee beleeued, and as the Lord gaue to euery man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9–10; Proverbs 11:25; 2 Corinthians 10:14–15"> I haue planted, Apollos watred, but God gaue the increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:5; Galatians 6:3; Psalms 115:1"> So then, neither is hee that planteth any thing, neither hee that watreth, but God that giueth the increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 2:6; Matthew 16:27; 2 John 1:8"> And he that planteth, and he that watreth, are one, and euery man shall receiue his wages, according to his labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> For we together are Gods labourers: yee are Gods husbandrie, and Gods building.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:11; Ephesians 2:20; Romans 15:20"> According to the grace of God giuen to mee, as a skilfull master builder, I haue laide the foundation, and another buildeth thereon: but let euery man take heede how he buildeth vpon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20; Isaiah 28:16; 2 Timothy 2:19"> For other foundation can no man laie, then that which is laied, which is Iesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3; 2 Timothy 3:7; 2 Timothy 2:20"> And if any man builde on this foundation, golde, siluer, precious stones, timber, haye, or stubble,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 1:7; 1 Corinthians 3:14–15; Revelation 20:12"> Euery mans worke shalbe made manifest: for the day shall declare it, because it shalbe reueiled by the fire: and the fire shall trie euery mans worke of what sort it is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:8; Matthew 25:21–23; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> If any mans worke, that he hath built vpon, abide, he shall receiue wages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:12–13; Jude 1:23"> If any mans worke burne, he shall lose, but he shalbe saued himselfe: neuerthelesse yet as it were by the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; Ezekiel 36:27; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> Knowe ye not that ye are the Temple of God, and that the Spirit of God dwelleth in you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:18–20; Ezekiel 43:12; Numbers 19:20"> If any man destroy the Temple of God, him shall God destroy: for the Temple of God is holy, which ye are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Isaiah 5:21; Galatians 6:3"> Let no man deceiue himselfe: If any man among you seeme to be wise in this world, let him be a foole, that he may be wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 5:13; Psalms 141:10; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> For the wisdome of this worlde is foolishnesse with God: for it is written, He catcheth the wise in their owne craftinesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 94:11; Colossians 2:8; Romans 1:21"> And againe, The Lord knoweth that the thoughtes of the wise be vaine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Romans 8:28; Jeremiah 9:23–24"> Therefore let no man reioyce in men: for all things are yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–12; Philippians 1:21; 1 Corinthians 9:19–22"> Whether it be Paul, or Apollos, or Cephas, or the world, or life, or death: whether they be things present, or thinges to come, euen all are yours,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; 1 Corinthians 11:3; John 17:9–10"> And ye Christes, and Christ Gods.</span>
