<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 14:37; 1 Corinthians 14:1–18"> Now concerning spirituall giftes, brethren, I would not haue you ignorant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 4:3; 1 Thessalonians 1:9; Psalms 115:5"> Ye know that ye were Gentiles, and were caried away vnto the dumme Idoles, as ye were led.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 4:2–3; Matthew 16:16–17; Romans 10:9"> Wherefore, I declare vnto you, that no man speaking by the Spirit of God calleth Iesus execrable: also no man can say that Iesus is the Lord, but by the holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Romans 12:4–6"> Now there are diuersities of gifts, but the same Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–12; Romans 12:6–8; Romans 14:8–9"> And there are diuersities of administrations, but the same Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:7; 1 Corinthians 12:11; John 5:17"> And there are diuersities of operations, but God is the same which worketh all in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10–11; Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> But the manifestation of the Spirit is giuen to euery man, to profite withall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 2:6–10; Psalms 143:10"> For to one is giuen by the Spirit the word of wisdome: and to an other the word of knowledge, by the same Spirit:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 5:14–15; 2 Corinthians 4:13; Ephesians 2:8"> And to another is giuen faith by the same Spirit: and to another the giftes of healing, by the same Spirit:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:39; 1 John 4:1; Mark 16:17"> And to another the operations of great workes: and to another, prophecie: and to another, the discerning of spirits: and to another, diuersities of tongues: and to another, the interpretation of tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4; Hebrews 2:4; 2 Corinthians 10:13"> And all these thinges worketh one and the selfe same Spirit, distributing to euery man seuerally as he will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 12:4–5; Colossians 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> For as the body is one, and hath many members, and all the members of the body, which is one, though they be many, yet are but one body: euen so is Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5; Colossians 3:11; Galatians 3:28"> For by one Spirit are we all baptized into one body, whether we bee Iewes, or Grecians, whether we be bonde, or free, and haue bene all made to drinke into one Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27–28; 1 Corinthians 12:12; Ephesians 4:25"> For the body also is not one member, but many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; 2 Kings 14:9"> If the foote would say, Because I am not the hand, I am not of the body, is it therefore not of the body?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:22; Philippians 2:3"> And if the eare would say, Because I am not the eye, I am not of the body, is it therefore not of the body?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:21; Psalms 139:13–16; Proverbs 20:12"> If the whole body were an eye, where were the hearing? If the whole were hearing, where were the smellling?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:28; Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> But nowe hath God disposed the members euery one of them in the bodie at his owne pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For if they were all one member, where were the body?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:14; 1 Corinthians 12:12"> But now are there many members, yet but one body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 10:1–5; Nehemiah 4:16–21; Job 29:11"> And the eye cannot say vnto the hand, I haue no neede of thee: nor the head againe to the feete, I haue no neede of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 14:28; Ecclesiastes 9:14–15; Ecclesiastes 4:9–12"> Yea, much rather those members of the body, which seeme to be more feeble, are necessarie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 3:21; Genesis 3:7"> And vpon those members of the body, which wee thinke most vnhonest, put wee more honestie on: and our vncomely parts haue more comelinesse on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 3:11; Genesis 2:25"> For our comely partes neede it not: but God hath tempered the body together, and hath giuen the more honour to that part which lacked,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; 1 Corinthians 1:10–12; John 17:21–26"> Lest there should be any diuision in the body: but that the members shoulde haue the same care one for another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Galatians 6:2; Hebrews 13:3"> Therefore if one member suffer, all suffer with it: if one member be had in honour, all the members reioyce with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 4:12; Colossians 1:24; Ephesians 5:30"> Now ye are the body of Christ, and members for your part.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–13; Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 12:18"> And God hath ordained some in the Church: as first Apostles, secondly Prophetes, thirdly teachers, then them that doe miracles: after that, the giftes of healing, helpers, gouernours, diuersitie of tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 12:14–20"> Are all Apostles? are all Prophetes? are all teachers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10"> Are all doers of miracles? haue all the gifts of healing? doe all speake with tongues? doe all interprete?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:1; 1 Corinthians 14:39; Matthew 5:6"> But desire you the best giftes, and I will yet shewe you a more excellent way.</span>
