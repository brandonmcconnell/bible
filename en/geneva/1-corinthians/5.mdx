<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:14; 2 Corinthians 6:14"> I wrote vnto you in an Epistle, that ye should not companie together with fornicatours,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:27; John 17:15–16; Matthew 5:14–16"> And not altogether with the fornicatours of this world, or with the couetous, or with extortioners, or with idolaters: for then ye must goe out of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:14; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> But nowe I haue written vnto you, that ye companie not together: if any that is called a brother, be a fornicatour, or couetous, or an idolater, or a rayler, or a drunkard, or an extortioner, with such one eate not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:11; 1 Timothy 3:7; Luke 12:14"> For what haue I to doe, to iudge them also which are without? doe ye not iudge them that are within?</span>
