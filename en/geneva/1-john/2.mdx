<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:5; Romans 8:34; John 14:6"> My little children, these things write I vnto you, that ye sinne not: and if any man sinne, wee haue an Aduocate with the Father, Iesus Christ, the Iust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:14; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 John 4:10"> And he is the reconciliation for our sinnes: and not for ours onely, but also for the sinnes of the whole world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 14:15; John 15:10; Luke 6:46"> And hereby we are sure that we knowe him, if we keepe his commandements.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 1:6; Titus 1:16; James 2:14–16"> Hee that saith, I knowe him, and keepeth not his commandements, is a liar, and the trueth is not in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 14:23; John 14:21; 1 John 4:12–13"> But hee that keepeth his worde, in him is the loue of God perfect in deede: hereby wee knowe that ye are in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:21; John 13:15; 1 Corinthians 11:1"> He that saith he remaineth in him, ought euen so to walke, as he hath walked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 3:11; Leviticus 19:18; Mark 12:29–34"> Brethren, I write no newe commandement vnto you: but an olde commandement, which ye haue had from the beginning: this olde commandement is that worde, which yee haue heard from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Romans 13:12; John 13:34"> Againe, a new comandement I write vnto you, that which is true in him, and also in you: for the darkenes is past, and that true light now shineth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 2:11; 1 John 3:13–17"> He that saith that hee is in that light, and hateth his brother, is in darkenes, vntill this time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 3:14; Romans 9:32–33; Romans 14:13"> Hee that loueth his brother, abideth in that light, and there is none occasion of euil in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 12:35; Titus 3:3; 1 John 1:6"> But he that hateth his brother, is in darkenesse, and walketh in darkenesse, and knoweth not whither hee goeth, because that darkenesse hath blinded his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:43; Acts 13:38; Ephesians 1:7"> Litle children, I write vnto you, because your sinnes are forgiuen you for his Names sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 14:7; 1 John 2:14; Luke 10:22"> I write vnto you, fathers, because yee haue knowen him that is from the beginning. I write vnto you, yong men, because ye haue ouercome that wicked one. I write vnto you, litle children, because ye haue knowen ye Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; 1 John 2:13; Colossians 3:16"> I haue written vnto you, fathers, because ye haue knowen him, that is from the beginning. I haue written vnto you, yong men, because ye are strong, and the worde of God abideth in you, and ye haue ouercome that wicked one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 4:4; Romans 12:2; Matthew 6:24"> Loue not this world, neither the things that are in this world. If any man loue this world, the loue of the Father is not in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 13:14; Galatians 5:17; Psalms 119:36–37"> For all that is in this world (as the lust of the flesh, the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life) is not of the Father, but is of this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Romans 12:2; 1 Corinthians 7:31"> And this world passeth away, and the lust thereof: but he that fulfilleth the will of God, abideth euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 4:3; 2 John 1:7; Matthew 24:24"> Litle children, it is the last time, and as ye haue heard that Antichrist shall come, euen now are there many Antichrists: whereby we know that it is the last time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 20:30; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Luke 8:13"> They went out from vs, but they were not of vs: for if they had bene of vs, they should haue continued with vs. But this cometh to passe, that it might appeare, that they are not all of vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 14:26; 1 John 2:27; 2 Corinthians 1:21–22"> But ye haue an ointment from that Holy one, and know all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 1:12; Jude 1:5; Proverbs 1:5"> I haue not written vnto you, because ye knowe not the trueth: but because ye knowe it, and that no lie is of the trueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 John 1:7; 1 John 4:3; 1 John 2:18"> Who is a liar, but he that denyeth that Iesus is that Christ? the same is that Antichrist that denyeth the Father and the Sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 4:15; John 8:19; John 5:23"> Whosoeuer denyeth the Sonne, the same hath not the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 14:23; John 15:9–10; 1 John 4:13"> Let therefore abide in you that same which ye haue heard from the beginning. If that which ye haue heard from the beginning, shall remaine in you, ye also shall continue in the Sonne, and in the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 10:28; Titus 3:7; Jude 1:21"> And this is the promise that he hath promised vs, euen that eternall life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; 2 John 1:7; Acts 20:29–30"> These things haue I written vnto you, concerning them that deceiue you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:26; 1 John 2:20–21; 1 Corinthians 2:13"> But that anointing which ye receiued of him, dwelleth in you: and ye neede not that any man teach you: but as the same Anoynting teacheth you of all things, and it is true, and is not lying, and as it taught you, ye shall abide in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 John 4:17; Mark 8:38; 1 John 3:2"> And nowe, litle children, abide in him, that when he shall appeare, we may be bolde, and not be ashamed before him at his comming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 3:3–5; 1 John 3:7; 1 John 5:1"> If ye know that he is righteous, know ye that he which doeth righteously, is borne of him.</span>
