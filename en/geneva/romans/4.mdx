<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:2; Romans 4:16; 2 Corinthians 11:22"> What shall we say then, that Abraham our father hath found concerning the flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:31; 2 Corinthians 11:30; Philippians 3:9"> For if Abraham were iustified by workes, he hath wherein to reioyce, but not with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; James 2:23; Galatians 3:6–8"> For what saith the Scripture? Abraham beleeued God, and it was counted to him for righteousnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 11:6; Romans 9:32; Romans 11:35"> Nowe to him that worketh, the wages is not counted by fauour, but by dette:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Romans 3:22; Titus 3:3–7"> But to him that worketh not, but beleeueth in him that iustifieth the vngodly, his faith is counted for righteousnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Philippians 3:9; 1 Corinthians 1:30"> Euen as Dauid declareth the blessednesse of the man, vnto whom God imputeth righteousnes without workes, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 32:1–2; Psalms 130:3–4; Luke 7:47–50"> Blessed are they, whose iniquities are forgiuen, and whose sinnes are couered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 32:2; 1 Peter 2:24; 2 Corinthians 5:19–20"> Blessed is the man, to whom the Lord imputeth not sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:14; Colossians 3:11; Galatians 3:26–28"> Came this blessednesse then vpon the circumcision onely, or vpon the vncircumcision also? For we say, that faith was imputed vnto Abraham for righteousnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; Galatians 5:6; Genesis 17:10"> Howe was it then imputed? when he was circumcised, or vncircumcised? not when he was cricumcised, but when he was vncircumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:22; Luke 19:9; 2 Corinthians 1:22"> After, he receiued the signe of circumcision, as the seale of the righteousnesse of ye faith which he had, when he was vncircumcised, that he should be the father of all them that beleeue, not being circumcised, that righteousnesse might be imputed to them also,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:8; John 8:39–40; Proverbs 2:20"> And the father of circumcision, not vnto them onely which are of the circumcision, but vnto them also that walke in the steppes of the faith of our father Abraham, which he had when he was vncircumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Genesis 22:17–18; Genesis 28:14"> For the promise that he should be the heire of the worlde, was not giuen to Abraham, or to his seede, through the Lawe, but through the righteousnesse of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 3:18–24; Philippians 3:9; Romans 4:16"> For if they which are of the Lawe, be heires, faith is made voide, and the promise is made of none effect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 5:13; Galatians 3:10; Romans 3:19–20"> For the Lawe causeth wrath: for where no Lawe is, there is no transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 3:24–26; Ephesians 2:8; Galatians 3:22"> Therefore it is by faith, that it might come by grace, and the promise might be sure to all the seede, not to that onely which is of the Lawe: but also to that which is of the faith of Abraham, who is the father of vs all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:28; Genesis 17:4–5; John 5:21"> (As it is written, I haue made thee a father of many nations) euen before God whom he beleeued, who quickeneth the dead, and calleth those thinges which be not, as though they were.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:24; Genesis 15:5–6; Acts 27:25"> Which Abraham aboue hope, beleeued vnder hope, that he should be the father of many nations: according to that which was spoken to him, So shall thy seede be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11–19; Romans 4:20–21; Genesis 17:17"> And he not weake in the faith, considered not his owne bodie, which was nowe dead, being almost an hundreth yeere olde, neither the deadnes of Saraes wombe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 1:45; 1 Corinthians 16:13; Daniel 10:19"> Neither did he doubt of the promise of God through vnbeliefe, but was strengthened in the faith, and gaue glorie to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:37; Jeremiah 32:27; Genesis 18:14"> Being fully assured that he which had promised, was also able to doe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 4:6; Romans 4:3"> And therefore it was imputed to him for righteousnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; Romans 15:4; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> Nowe it is not written for him onely, that it was imputed to him for righteousnesse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 2:24; 1 Peter 1:21; Acts 13:30"> But also for vs, to whom it shalbe imputed for righteousnesse, which beleeue in him that raised vp Iesus our Lord from the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 4:9–10; Isaiah 53:5–6; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> Who was deliuered to death for our sinnes, and is risen againe for our iustification.</span>
