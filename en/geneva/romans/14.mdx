<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:10; Colossians 2:16–17; Luke 18:9"> Let not him that eateth, despise him that eateth not: and let not him which eateth not, condemne him that eateth: for God hath receiued him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 4:11–12; Romans 9:20; Romans 8:31–39"> Who art thou that condemnest another mans seruant? hee standeth or falleth to his owne master: yea, he shalbe established: for God is able to make him stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:23; Galatians 4:9–10"> This man esteemeth one day aboue another day, and another man counteth euery day alike: let euery man be fully perswaded in his minde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:30–31; 1 Timothy 4:3–5; Matthew 14:19"> He that obserueth the day, obserueth it to the Lord: and he that obserueth not the day, obserueth it not to the Lord. He that eateth, eateth to the Lord: for he giueth God thankes: and he that eateth not, eateth not to the Lord, and giueth God thankes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; 2 Corinthians 5:15; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20"> For none of vs liueth to himselfe, neither doeth any die to himselfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:20; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 15:23"> For whether wee liue, we liue vnto the Lord: or whether we die, we die vnto the Lord: whether we liue therefore, or die, we are the Lords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; Luke 24:26; Hebrews 12:2"> For Christ therefore died and rose againe, and reuiued, that he might be Lord both of the dead and the quicke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> But why doest thou condemne thy brother? or why doest thou despise thy brother? for we shall all appeare before the iudgement seate of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:22–25; Philippians 2:10–11; Psalms 72:11"> For it is written, I liue, sayth the Lord, and euery knee shall bowe to me, and all tongues shall confesse vnto God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:5; Matthew 12:36; Matthew 16:27"> So then euery one of vs shall giue accounts of himselfe to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; James 4:11; 1 Corinthians 8:9–13"> Let vs not therefore iudge one another any more: but vse your iudgement rather in this, that no man put an occasion to fall, or a stumbling blocke before his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Romans 14:2; 1 Timothy 4:4"> I know, and am perswaded through the Lord Iesus, that there is nothing vncleane of it selfe: but vnto him that iudgeth any thing to be vncleane, to him it is vncleane.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:11–12; Philippians 2:2–4; Galatians 5:13"> But if thy brother be grieued for the meate, nowe walkest thou not charitably: destroy not him with thy meate, for whome Christ dyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:22; 1 Corinthians 10:29–30"> Cause not your commoditie to be euill spoken of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Romans 15:13; 1 Corinthians 4:20"> For the kingdome of God, is not meate nor drinke, but righteousnes, and peace, and ioye in the holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Corinthians 5:11; 1 Peter 2:20"> For whosoeuer in these things serueth Christ, is acceptable vnto God, and is approoued of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Romans 12:18; Psalms 34:14"> Let vs then follow those things which concerne peace, and wherewith one may edifie another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 14:14–15; Matthew 18:6; Titus 1:15"> Destroy not the worke of God for meates sake: all things in deede are pure: but it is euill for the man which eateth with offence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:13; Romans 14:13; Romans 15:1–2"> It is good neither to eate flesh, nor to drinke wine, nor any thing whereby thy brother stumbleth, or is offended, or made weake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 3:21; Romans 14:5; James 3:13"> Hast thou faith? haue it with thy selfe before God: blessed is hee that condemneth not himselfe in that thing which he aloweth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:6; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 11:29–31"> For he that doubteth, is condemned if he eate, because he eateth not of faith: and whatsoeuer is not of faith, is sinne.</span>
