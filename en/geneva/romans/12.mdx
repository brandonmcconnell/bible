<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; 1 Corinthians 12:12–14; Ephesians 4:15–16"> For as wee haue many members in one body, and all members haue not one office,</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10–11; Acts 18:25; Revelation 3:15–16"> Not slouthfull to do seruice: seruent in spirit seruing the Lord,</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Matthew 5:44; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Blesse them which persecute you: blesse, I say, and curse not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Hebrews 13:3; Psalms 35:13–14"> Reioyce with them that reioyce, and weepe with them that weepe.</span>
