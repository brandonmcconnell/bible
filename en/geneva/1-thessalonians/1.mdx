<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:10; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Galatians 4:8–9"> For they themselues shew of vs what maner of entring in we had vnto you, and how ye turned to God from idoles, to serue the liuing and true God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:9; Isaiah 25:8–9; Romans 5:9–10"> And to looke for his sonne from heauen, whome he raised from the dead, euen Iesus which deliuereth vs from that wrath to come.</span>
