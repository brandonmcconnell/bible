<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 14:23; Isaiah 13:1; Jeremiah 51:42"> The burden of the desert Sea. As the whirlewindes in the South vse to passe from the wildernesse, so shall it come from the horrible land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Jeremiah 49:34; Psalms 60:3"> A grieuous vision was shewed vnto me, The transgressour against a transgressour, and the destroyer against a destroyer. Goe vp Elam, besiege Media: I haue caused all the mourning thereof to cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 13:8; Psalms 48:6; Isaiah 26:17"> Therefore are my loynes filled with sorow: sorowes haue taken me as the sorowes of a woman that trauayleth: I was bowed downe when I heard it, and I was amased when I sawe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:34–36; 1 Samuel 25:36–38; Daniel 5:1"> Mine heart failed: fearefulnesse troubled me: the night of my pleasures hath he turned into feare vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 51:57; Jeremiah 51:39"> Prepare thou the table: watch in the watch towre: eate, drinke: arise, ye princes, anoynt the shielde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:2–7; Ezekiel 3:17; Habakkuk 2:1–2"> For thus hath the Lord said vnto me, Go, set a watchman, to tell what he seeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 21:9; Isaiah 37:24; Hebrews 2:1"> And he sawe a charet with two horsemen: a charet of an asse, and a charet of a camel: and he hearkened and tooke diligent heede.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:1–2; Isaiah 56:10; 1 Peter 5:8"> And he cryed, A lyon: my lorde, I stand continually vpon ye watche towre in the day time, and I am set in my watche euery night:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 14:8; Jeremiah 51:8"> And beholde, this mans charet commeth with two horsemen. And he answered and said, Babel is fallen: it is fallen, and all the images of her gods hath he broken vnto the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Micah 4:13; 2 Kings 13:7"> O my threshing, and the corne of my floore. That which I haue heard of the Lord of hostes, the God of Israel, haue I shewed vnto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:14; Obadiah 1:1–16; Amos 1:6"> The burden of Dumah. He calleth vnto me out of Seir, Watchman, what was in ye night? Watchman, what was in the night?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:30–32; Acts 2:37–38; Ezekiel 18:30–32"> The watchman saide, The morning commeth, and also the night. If yee will aske, enquire: returne and come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:23–24; 1 Chronicles 1:32; Ezekiel 27:15"> The burden against Arabia. In the forest of Arabia shall yee tarie all night, euen in the waies of Dedanim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 25:15; Job 6:19; Romans 12:20"> O inhabitants of the lande of Tema, bring foorth water to meete the thirstie, and preuent him that fleeth with his bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:19–20; Isaiah 13:14"> For they flee from the drawen swords, euen from the drawen sword, and from the bent bowe, and from the grieuousnesse of warre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 120:5; Isaiah 60:7; Isaiah 16:14"> For thus hath the Lord sayd vnto me, Yet a yeere according to the yeeres of an hireling, and all the glorie of Kedar shall faile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 1:6; Numbers 23:19; Matthew 24:35"> And the residue of the nomber of ye strong archers of the sonnes of Kedar shall be fewe: for the Lord God of Israel hath spoken it.</span>
