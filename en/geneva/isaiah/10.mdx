<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Micah 3:1–4; Matthew 23:23"> Woe vnto them that decree wicked decrees, and write grieuous things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Isaiah 3:14; Isaiah 29:21"> To keepe backe ye poore from iudgement, and to take away the iudgement of the poore of my people, that widowes may be their pray, and that they may spoyle the fatherlesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 9:7; Luke 19:44; Job 31:14"> What will ye doe nowe in the day of visitation, and of destruction, which shall come from farre? to whom will ye flee for helpe? and where will ye leaue your glorie?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25; Isaiah 9:12; Jeremiah 37:10"> Without me euery one shall fall among them that are bound, and they shall fall downe among the slayne: yet for all this his wrath is not turned away, but his hand is stretched out still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:20–24; Isaiah 14:25; Isaiah 30:30"> O Asshur, the rodde of my wrath: and the staffe in their hands is mine indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 9:17; Jeremiah 25:9; Zechariah 10:5"> I will sende him to a dissembling nation, and I will giue him a charge against the people of my wrath to take the spoyle and to take the pray, and to treade them vnder feete like the mire in the streete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Micah 4:11–12; Acts 2:23"> But he thinketh not so, neither doeth his heart esteeme it so: but he imagineth to destroy and to cut off not a fewe nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 18:24; 2 Kings 19:10; Ezekiel 26:7"> For he sayeth, Are not my princes altogether Kings?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:20; 2 Kings 16:9; Genesis 10:10"> Is not Calno as Carchemish? Is not Hamath like Arpad? Is not Samaria as Damascus?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 10:14; 2 Kings 19:17–19; 2 Kings 18:33–35"> Like as mine hand hath founde the kingdomes of the idoles, seeing their idoles were aboue Ierusalem, and aboue Samaria:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 37:10–13; Isaiah 2:8; Isaiah 36:19–20"> Shall not I, as I haue done to Samaria, and to the idoles thereof, so doe to Ierusalem and to the idoles thereof?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 19:31; Isaiah 65:7; Jeremiah 50:18"> But when the Lord hath accomplished all his worke vpon mount Zion and Ierusalem, I will visite the fruite of the proude heart of the King of Asshur, and his glorious and proud lookes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:17; Daniel 4:30; Ezekiel 26:2"> Because he said, By ye power of mine owne hand haue I done it, and by my wisdome, because I am wise: therefore I haue remooued the borders of the people, and haue spoyled their treasures, and haue pulled downe the inhabitants like a valiant man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 31:25; Proverbs 21:6–7; Nahum 2:9–1"> And mine hand hath found as a nest the riches of the people, and as one gathereth egges that are left, so haue I gathered all the earth: and there was none to mooue the wing or to open the mouth, or to whisper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 9:20–21; Isaiah 45:9; Isaiah 10:5"> Shall the axe boast it selfe against him that heweth therewith? or shall the sawe exalt it selfe against him that moueth it? as if the rod shoulde lift vp it selfe against him that taketh it vp, or the staffe should exalt it selfe, as it were no wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:21; Acts 12:23; Psalms 106:15"> Therefore shall the Lord God of hostes send amog his fat men, leannes, and vnder his glorie he shall kindle a burning, like the burning of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 27:4; Numbers 11:1–3; Isaiah 37:23"> And the light of Israel shalbe as a fire, and the Holy one thereof as a flame, and it shall burne, and deuoure his thornes and his briers in one day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 19:23; Isaiah 10:33–34; Isaiah 9:18"> And shall consume the glory of his forest, and of his fruitfull fieldes both soule and flesh: and he shalbe as ye fainting of a standard bearer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 21:17; Isaiah 37:36"> And the rest of the trees of his forest shalbe fewe, that a childe may tell them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 37:31–32; Isaiah 50:10; Isaiah 1:9"> And at that day shall the remnant of Israel, and such as are escaped of the house of Iaakob, stay no more vpon him that smote them, but shall stay vpon ye Lord, ye Holy one of Israel in trueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; 2 Corinthians 3:14–16; Hosea 7:10"> The remnant shall returne, euen the remnant of Iaakob vnto the mightie God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 9:27–28; Isaiah 6:13; Revelation 20:8"> For though thy people, O Israel, be as the sand of the sea, yet shall the remnant of them returne. The consumption decreed shall ouerflow with righteousnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 28:22; Isaiah 24:1–23; Romans 9:28"> For the Lord God of hostes shall make the consumption, euen determined, in the middes of all the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 12:22–24; Isaiah 37:6; Isaiah 61:3"> Therefore thus saith ye Lord God of hostes, O my people, that dwellest in Zion, be not afraid of Asshur: he shall smite thee with a rod, and shall lift vp his staffe against thee after the maner of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 11:36; 2 Kings 19:35; Hebrews 10:37"> But yet a very litle time, and the wrath shall be consumed, and mine anger in their destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Judges 7:25; 2 Kings 19:35"> And ye Lord of hostes shall raise vp a scourge for him, according to the plague of Midian in the rocke Oreb: and as his staffe was vpon the Sea, so he will lift it vp after the maner of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Luke 4:18; Isaiah 14:25"> And at that day shall his burden be taken away from off thy shoulder, and his yoke from off thy necke: and the yoke shalbe destroied because of the anoynting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:2; 1 Samuel 13:2; 1 Samuel 13:5"> He is come to Aiath: he is passed into Migron: at Michmash shall he lay vp his armour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:23; 1 Samuel 7:17; Joshua 21:17"> They haue gone ouer the foorde: they lodged in the lodging at Geba: Ramah is afraide: Gibeah of Saul is fled away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:44; Jeremiah 1:1; Joshua 21:18"> Lift vp thy voyce, O daughter Gallim, cause Laish to heare, O poore Anathoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 15:31"> Madmenah is remoued: the inhabitants of Gebim haue gathered themselues together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:1; Isaiah 37:22; Isaiah 19:16"> Yet there is a time that he will stay at Nob: he shall lift vp his hand towarde the mount of the daughter Zion, the hill of Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Daniel 4:37; Isaiah 37:38"> Beholde, the Lord God of hostes shall cut off the bough with feare, and they of high stature shalbe cut off, and the hie shalbe humbled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 37:24; Daniel 4:23; Jeremiah 48:2"> And he shall cut away the thicke places of the forest with yron, and Lebanon shall haue a mightie fall.</span>
