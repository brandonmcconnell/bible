<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 1:3–4; Mark 1:10; Revelation 20:11"> Oh, that thou wouldest breake the heauens, and come downe, and that the mountaines might melt at thy presence!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 99:1; Jeremiah 33:9; Ezekiel 38:22–23"> As the melting fire burned, as the fire caused the waters to boyle, (that thou mightest declare thy Name to thy aduersaries) the people did tremble at thy presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 34:10; Psalms 65:5–6; Psalms 66:3"> When thou diddest terrible things, which we looked not for, thou camest downe, and the mountaines melted at thy presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9–10; Psalms 31:19; James 5:7"> For since the beginning of the world they haue not heard nor vnderstande with the eare, neither hath ye eye seene another God beside thee, which doeth so to him that waiteth for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:13–15; Hebrews 4:16; Acts 10:35"> Thou diddest meete him, that reioyced in thee, and did iustly: they remembred thee in thy wayes: beholde, thou art angrie, for we haue sinned: yet in them is continuance, and we shall be saued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 7:18; Romans 7:24; Ephesians 2:1–2"> But we haue all bene as an vncleane thing, and all our righteousnes is as filthie cloutes, and we all doe fade like a leafe, and our iniquities like the winde haue taken vs away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Hosea 7:14; Isaiah 59:16"> And there is none that calleth vpon thy Name, neither that stirreth vp himselfe to take holde of thee: for thou hast hid thy face from vs, and hast consumed vs because of our iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Psalms 119:73; Jeremiah 18:2–6"> But now, O Lord, thou art our Father: we are the clay, and thou art our potter, and we all are the worke of thine hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 7:18–20; Psalms 74:1–2; Psalms 79:13"> Be not angry, O Lord, aboue measure, neither remember iniquitie for euer: lo, we beseech thee beholde, we are all thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; Luke 21:24; 2 Chronicles 36:19–21"> Thine holy cities lye waste: Zion is a wildernes, and Ierusalem a desart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 25:9; Psalms 74:5–7; Lamentations 1:7"> The House of our Sanctuarie and of our glorie, where our fathers praysed thee, is burnt vp with fire and all our pleasant things are wasted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 42:14; Psalms 74:10–11; Psalms 83:1"> Wilt thou holde thy selfe still at these things, O Lord? wilt thou holde thy peace and afflict vs aboue measure?</span>
