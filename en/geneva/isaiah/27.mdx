<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:9; Ezekiel 29:3; Isaiah 66:16"> In that day the Lord with his sore and great and mightie sword shall visite Liuiathan, that pearcing serpent, euen Liuiathan, that crooked serpent, and he shall slay the dragon that is in the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Luke 20:9–18; Isaiah 5:1–7"> In that daye sing of the vineyarde of redde wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; John 15:1–2; 1 Samuel 2:9"> I the Lord doe keepe it: I will water it euery moment: least any assaile it, I will keepe it night and day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:17; Hebrews 6:8; Matthew 3:12"> Anger is not in mee: who would set the briers and the thornes against me in battel? I would go through them, I would burne them together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; Job 22:21; Ephesians 2:16–17"> Or will he feele my strength, that he may make peace with me, and be at one with me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:31; Hosea 14:5–6; Romans 11:16–26"> Hereafter, Iaakob shall take roote: Israel shall florish and growe, and the world shall be filled with fruite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 37:36–38; Jeremiah 51:24; Isaiah 10:20–25"> Hath hee smitten him as hee smote those that smote him? or is hee slaine according to the slaughter of them that were slaine by him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:24; Jeremiah 4:11; Ezekiel 19:12"> In measure in the branches thereof wilt thou contende with it, when he bloweth with his rough winde in the day of the East winde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:27; Isaiah 48:10; Isaiah 17:8"> By this therefore shall the iniquitie of Iaakob be purged, and this is all the fruit, the taking away of his sinne: whe he shall make all the stones of the altars, as chalke stones broken in pieces, that the groues and images may not stand vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:2; Jeremiah 26:18; Isaiah 32:13–14"> Yet the defenced citie shalbe desolate, and the habitation shalbe forsaken, and left like a wildernes. There shall the calfe feede, and there shall he lie, and consume the branches thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Isaiah 1:3; Isaiah 43:1"> When the boughes of it are drie, they shalbe broken: the women come, and set them on fire: for it is a people of none vnderstading: therefore hee that made them, shall not haue compassion of them, and he that formed them, shall haue no mercie on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 30:3–4; Isaiah 56:8"> And in that day shall the Lord thresh from the chanell of the Riuer vnto the riuer of Egypt, and ye shalbe gathered, one by one, O children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Leviticus 25:9; Hebrews 12:22"> In that day also shall the great trumpet be blowen, and they shall come, which perished in the land of Asshur: and they that were chased into the lande of Egypt, and they shall worship the Lord in the holy Mount at Ierusalem.</span>
