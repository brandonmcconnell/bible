<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:35; Isaiah 51:3; Isaiah 55:12–13"> The desert and the wildernes shall reioyce: and the waste ground shalbe glad and florish as the rose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Isaiah 60:13; Amos 9:13–15"> It shall florish abundantly and shall greatly reioyce also and ioye: the glory of Lebanon shalbe giuen vnto it: the beautie of Carmel, and of Sharon, they shall see the glory of the Lord, and the excellencie of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:12; Job 4:3–4; Luke 22:32"> Strengthen the weake handes, and comfort the feeble knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 5:7–9; Isaiah 41:10–14; Haggai 2:4"> Say vnto them that are fearefull, Bee you strong, feare not: beholde, your God commeth with vengeance: euen God with a recompense, he will come and saue you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 42:6–7; Luke 7:20–23; Isaiah 29:18"> Then shall the eyes of the blinde be lightened, and the eares of the deafe be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 5:8–9; John 7:37–39; Luke 11:14"> Then shall ye lame man leape as an hart, and the dumme mans tongue shall sing: for in the wildernes shall waters breake out, and riuers in ye desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 26:18; Isaiah 49:10; John 4:14"> And the dry ground shalbe as a poole, and the thirstie (as springs of water in the habitation of dragons: where they lay) shall be a place for reedes and rushes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 7:13–14; Isaiah 42:16; John 14:6"> And there shalbe a path and a way, and the way shalbe called holy: the polluted shall not passe by it: for he shalbe with them, and walke in the way, and the fooles shall not erre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 62:12; Hosea 2:18; Leviticus 26:6"> There shall be no lyon, nor noysome beastes shall ascend by it, neither shall they be found there, that the redeemed may walke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 30:19; Revelation 21:4; Isaiah 25:8"> Therefore the redeemed of the Lord shall returne and come to Zion with prayse: and euerlasting ioy shall bee vpon their heads: they shall obteine ioye and gladnesse, and sorow and mourning shall flee away.</span>
