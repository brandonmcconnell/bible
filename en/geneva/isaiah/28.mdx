<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 7:5; Isaiah 28:7; 2 Kings 14:25–27"> Woe to the crowne of pride, the drunkards of Ephraim: for his glorious beautie shall be a fading flowre, which is vpon the head of the valley of them that be fat, and are ouercome with wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:30; Nahum 1:8; Ezekiel 13:11"> Beholde, the Lord hath a mightie and strong hoste, like a tempest of haile, and a whirlewinde that ouerthroweth, like a tempest of mightie waters that ouerflowe, which throwe to the ground mightily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 26:6; Hebrews 10:29; Revelation 11:2"> They shall be troden vnder foote, euen the crowne and the pride of the drunkards of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nahum 3:12; Micah 7:1; Isaiah 28:1"> For his glorious beautie shall be a fading floure, which is vpon the head of the valley of them that be fatte, and as the hastie fruite afore sommer, which when hee that looketh vpon it, seeth it, while it is in his hand, he eateth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 5:4; Isaiah 62:3; Isaiah 60:19"> In that day shall the Lord of hostes be for a crowne of glory, and for a diademe of beautie vnto the residue of his people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 18:32–34; John 5:30; Joshua 1:9"> And for a spirite of iudgement to him that sitteth in iudgement, and for strength vnto them that turne away the battell to the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 56:10–12; Luke 21:34; Isaiah 19:14"> But they haue erred because of wine, and are out of the way by strong drinke: the priest and the prophet haue erred by strong drinke: they are swallowed vp with wine: they haue gone astraye through strong drinke: they faile in vision: they stumble in iudgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:26; Proverbs 26:11; Habakkuk 2:15–16"> For all their tables are full of filthy vomiting: no place is cleane.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 131:2; Jeremiah 6:10; John 12:38"> Whome shall he teache knowledge? and whome shall he make to vnderstand the thinges that hee heareth? them that are weyned from the milke, and drawen from the breastes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 28:13; 2 Timothy 3:7; Jeremiah 11:7"> For precept must be vpon precept, precept vpon precept, line vnto line, line vnto line, there a litle, and there a litle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:21; Deuteronomy 28:49; Isaiah 33:19"> For with a stammering tongue and with a strange language shall he speake vnto this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 11:28–29; Isaiah 30:15; Jeremiah 6:16"> Vnto whome hee saide, This is the rest: giue rest to him that is weary: and this is the refreshing, but they would not heare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 13:14; Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Peter 3:16"> Therefore shall the worde of the Lord be vnto them precept vpon precept, precept vpon precept, line vnto line, line vnto line, there a litle and there a litle, that they may goe, and fall backward, and be broken, and be snared, and be taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 28:22; Isaiah 29:20; Isaiah 1:10"> Wherefore, heare the worde of the Lord, ye scornefull men that rule this people, which is at Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Ezekiel 13:22; Amos 2:4"> Because ye haue said, We haue made a couenant with death, and with hell are we at agreement: though a scourge runne ouer, and passe through, it shall not come at vs: for we haue made falshood our refuge, and vnder vanitie are we hid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 118:22; Acts 4:11–12; 1 Peter 2:6–8"> Therefore thus saith the Lord God, Behold, I will laye in Zion a stone, a tried stone, a precious corner stone, a sure foundation. Hee that beleeueth, shall not make haste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Amos 7:7–9; Revelation 16:21"> Iudgement also will I laye to the rule, and righteousnesse to the balance, and the haile shall sweepe away the vaine confidence, and the waters shall ouerflowe the secret place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 7:7; Isaiah 8:10; Isaiah 28:15"> And your couenant with death shalbe disanulled, and your agreement with hell shall not stand: when a scourge shall runne ouer and passe through, then shall ye be trode downe by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Isaiah 36:22; 2 Kings 24:2"> When it passeth ouer, it shall take you away: for it shall passe through euery morning in the day, and in the night, and there shalbe onely feare to make you to vnderstand the hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; 1 Corinthians 1:18–31; Romans 9:30–32"> For the bed is streight that it can not suffice, and the couering narowe that one can not wrappe himselfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:11; Joshua 10:12; 2 Samuel 5:20"> For the Lord shall stand as in mount Perazim: hee shall be wroth as in the valley of Gibeon, that he may do his worke, his strage worke, and bring to passe his acte, his strange acte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22–23; Isaiah 24:1–23; Matthew 27:44"> Nowe therefore be no mockers, least your bondes increase: for I haue heard of the Lord of hostes a consumption, euen determined vpon the whole earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Jeremiah 22:29; Revelation 2:11"> Hearken ye, and heare my voyce: hearken ye, and heare my speach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Hosea 10:11–12"> Doeth the plowe man plowe all the day, to sowe? doeth he open, and breake the clots of his ground?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:9; Matthew 23:23; Exodus 9:31–32"> When he hath made it plaine, wil he not then sowe the fitches, and sowe cummin, and cast in wheat by measure, and the appointed barly and rye in their place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 31:3–6; Exodus 36:2; Job 39:17"> For his God doeth instruct him to haue discretion, and doeth teach him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15; Isaiah 27:7–8; Jeremiah 46:28"> For fitches shall not be threshed with a threshing instrument, neither shall a cart wheele be turned about vpon the cummin: but ye fitches are beaten out with a staffe, and cummin with a rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:9–10; Matthew 3:12; John 12:24"> Bread corne when it is threshed, hee doeth not alway thresh it, neither doeth the wheele of his cart still make a noyse, neither will he breake it with the teeth thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Jeremiah 32:19; Job 5:9"> This also commeth from the Lord of hostes, which is wonderfull in counsell, and excellent in workes.</span>
