<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 102:13; Zechariah 1:17; Zechariah 8:22–23"> For the Lord wil haue compassion of Iaakob, and wil yet chuse Israel, and cause them to rest in their owne lande: and the stranger shall ioyne him selfe vnto them, and they shall cleaue to the house of Iaakob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 61:5; Isaiah 60:14; Jeremiah 30:16"> And the people shall receiue them and bring them to their owne place, and the house of Israel shall possesse them in the land of the Lord, for seruants and handmaids: and they shall take them prisoners, whose captiues they were, and haue rule ouer their oppressours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:10; Isaiah 32:18; Ezra 9:8–9"> And in that day when the Lord shall giue thee rest from thy sorrow, and from thy feare, and from the sore bodage, wherein thou didest serue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Habakkuk 1:2–10; Jeremiah 51:34–35"> Then shalt thou take vp this prouerbe against the King of Babel, and say, Howe hath the oppressor ceased? and the gold thirsty Babel rested?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 125:3; Isaiah 9:4; Jeremiah 48:15–17"> The Lord hath broken the rodde of the wicked, and the scepter of the rulers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 7:19–21; James 2:13; Revelation 17:16–17"> Which smote the people in anger with a continuall plague, and ruled the nations in wrath: if any were persecuted, he did not let.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 126:1–3; Psalms 98:7–9; Psalms 96:11–13"> The whole worlde is at rest and is quiet: they sing for ioye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:16; Isaiah 55:12–13; Zechariah 11:2"> Also the firre trees reioyced of thee, and the cedars of Lebanon, saying, Since thou art laid downe, no hewer came vp against vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:21–32; Jeremiah 50:8; Proverbs 15:24"> Hel beneath is mooued for thee to meete thee at thy comming, raising vp the deade for thee, euen all the princes of the earth, and hath raised from their thrones all the Kinges of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 49:6–14; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Psalms 82:6–7"> All they shall crie, and saie vnto thee, Art thou become weake also as we? art thou become like vnto vs?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 5:25–30; Job 21:11–15; Isaiah 66:24"> Thy pompe is brought downe to ye graue, and the sounde of thy violes: the worme is spred vnder thee, and the wormes couer thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13–17; Revelation 12:7–10; Luke 10:18"> How art thou fallen from heauen, O Lucifer, sonne of the morning? and cutte downe to the grounde, which didest cast lottes vpon the nations?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 8:10–12; Ezekiel 28:2; Ezekiel 28:12–16"> Yet thou saidest in thine heart, I will ascende into heauen, and exalt my throne aboue beside the starres of God: I will sitte also vpon the mount of the Congregation in the sides of the North.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 47:8; Genesis 3:5; 2 Thessalonians 2:4"> I wil ascend aboue ye height of the cloudes, and I will be like the most high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 11:23; Ezekiel 32:23; Acts 12:22–23"> But thou shalt bee brought downe to the graue, to the sides of the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:23; Psalms 58:10–11; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> They that see thee, shall looke vpon thee and consider thee, saying, Is this the man that made the earth to tremble, and that did shake the kingdomes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:3; Isaiah 13:19–22; Isaiah 45:13"> He made the worlde as a wildernesse, and destroied the cities thereof, and opened not the house of his prisoners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:25; Isaiah 22:16; Ezekiel 32:18–32"> All the Kings of the nations, euen they all sleepe in glorie, euery one in his owne house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:9; Jeremiah 41:7; 1 Kings 21:24"> But thou art cast out of thy graue like an abominable branch: like the raiment of those that are slaine, and thrust thorowe with a sword, which goe downe to the stones of the pit, as a carkeise troden vnder feete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 21:10; Job 18:19; Psalms 37:28"> Thou shalt not be ioyned with them in the graue, because thou hast destroied thine owne lande, and slaine thy people: the seede of the wicked shall not be renoumed for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 23:35; Exodus 20:5; Habakkuk 2:8–12"> Prepare a slaughter for his children, for the iniquitie of their fathers: let them not rise vp nor possesse the land, nor fil the face of the world with enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:29–35; Proverbs 10:7; 1 Kings 14:10"> For I wil rise vp against them (sayth the Lord of hostes) and will cut off from Babel the name and the remnant and the sonne, and the nephew, sayth the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:14; Isaiah 13:21–22; 1 Kings 14:10"> And I wil make it a possession to ye hedgehogge, and pooles of water, and I will sweepe it with the besome of destruction, sayeth the Lord of hostes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Job 23:13; Isaiah 46:10–11"> The Lord of hostes hath sworne, saying, Surely like as I haue purposed, so shall it come to passe, and as I haue consulted, it shall stand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Nahum 1:13; Isaiah 37:36–38"> That I will breake to pieces Asshur in my land, and vpon my mountaines will I treade him vnder foote: so that his yoke shall depart from them, and his burden shall be taken from off their shoulder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 23:9; Zephaniah 3:6–8; Exodus 15:12"> This is the counsell that is consulted vpon the whole worlde, and this is the hande stretched out ouer all the nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 43:13; Job 23:13; 2 Chronicles 20:6"> Because the Lord of hostes hath determined it, and who shall disanull it? and his hande is stretched out, and who shall turne it away?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 16:20; Isaiah 13:1; 2 Chronicles 28:27"> In the yeere that King Ahaz died, was this burden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 30:6; Isaiah 11:8; 2 Chronicles 26:6"> Reioyce not, (thou whole Palestina) because the rod of him that did beat thee, is broken for out of the serpents roote shall come forth a cockatrise, and the fruit therof shalbe a firy flying serpent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 7:21–22; Zephaniah 2:4–7; Isaiah 5:17"> For the first borne of the poore shall be fed, and the needie shall lie downe in safetie: and I will kill thy roote with famine, and it shall slay thy remnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:14; Isaiah 20:1; Isaiah 14:29"> Howle, O gate, crie, O citie: thou whole lande of Palestina art dissolued, for there shall come from the North a smoke, and none shalbe alone, at his time appointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 102:16; Psalms 87:5; James 2:5"> What shall then one answere the messengers of the Gentiles? That the Lord hath stablished Zion, and the poore of his people shall trust in it.</span>
