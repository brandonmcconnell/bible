<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Psalms 125:2; Joel 3:12"> The burden of the valley of vision. What aileth thee nowe that thou art wholy gone vp vnto the house toppes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 32:13; Isaiah 23:7; Lamentations 2:20"> Thou that art full of noise, a citie full of brute, a ioyous citie: thy slaine men shall not bee slaine with sworde, nor die in battell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 25:4–7; Isaiah 3:1–8; Jeremiah 39:4–7"> All thy princes shall flee together from the bowe: they shalbe bound: all that shall be found in thee, shall be bound together, which haue fled from farre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:1; Matthew 2:18; Psalms 77:2"> Therefore said I, Turne away from me: I wil weepe bitterly: labour not to comfort mee for the destruction of the daughter of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 7:4; Isaiah 37:3; Luke 23:30"> For it is a day of trouble, and of ruine, and of perplexitie by the Lord God of hostes in the valley of vision, breaking downe the citie: and a crying vnto the mountaines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 16:9; Isaiah 21:2; Amos 1:5"> And Elam bare the quiuer in a mans charet with horsemen, and Kir vncouered the shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Isaiah 10:28–32; Jeremiah 39:1–3"> And thy chiefe valleis were full of charets, and the horsemen set themselues in aray against the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2; 1 Kings 10:17; Isaiah 36:1–3"> And hee discouered the couering of Iudah: and thou didest looke in that day to the armour of the house of the forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 20:20; Nehemiah 3:16; 2 Chronicles 32:1–6"> And ye haue seene the breaches of the citie of Dauid: for they were many, and ye gathered the waters of the lower poole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And yee nombred the houses of Ierusalem, and the houses haue yee broken downe to fortifie the wall,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 7:7; Jeremiah 33:2–3; Isaiah 31:1"> And haue also made a ditche betweene the two walles, for the waters of the olde poole, and haue not looked vnto the maker thereof, neither had respect vnto him that formed it of olde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Micah 1:16; Joel 1:13"> And in that day did the Lord God of hosts call vnto weeping and mourning, and to baldnes and girding with sackecloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 56:12; 1 Corinthians 15:32; Luke 17:26–29"> And beholde, ioy and gladnes, slaying oxen and killing sheepe, eating flesh, and drinking wine, eating and drinking: for to morowe we shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:13; 1 Samuel 3:14; Isaiah 5:9"> And it was declared in ye eares of the Lord of hostes. Surely this iniquitie shall not be purged from you, til ye die, saith the Lord God of hostes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; 2 Kings 18:18; 2 Kings 19:2"> Thus sayeth the Lord God of hostes, Goe, get thee to that treasurer, to Shebna, the steward of the house, and say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:60; 2 Chronicles 16:14; 2 Samuel 18:18"> What haste thou to doe here? and whome hast thou here? that thou shouldest here hewe thee out a sepulchre, as he that heweth out his sepulchre in an hie place, or that graueth an habitation for him selfe in a rocke?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3; Job 9:24; Esther 7:8"> Beholde, the Lord wil carie thee away with a great captiuitie, and will surely couer thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 17:13; Amos 7:17"> He wil surely rolle and turne thee like a bal in a large countrey: there shalt thou die, and there the charets of thy glory shalbe the shame of thy lordes house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; Psalms 75:6–7; Job 40:11–12"> And I wil driue thee from thy station, and out of thy dwelling will he destroy thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; 2 Kings 18:18; Isaiah 37:2"> And in that day will I call my seruant Eliakim the sonne of Hilkiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:8; Isaiah 9:6–7; Genesis 41:42–43"> And with thy garments will I clothe him, and with thy girdle will I strengthen him: thy power also will I commit into his hande, and hee shalbe a father of the inhabitats of Ierusalem, and of the house of Iudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:18–19; Revelation 3:7; Job 12:14"> And the key of the house of Dauid will I lay vpon his shoulder: so hee shall open, and no man shall shut: and he shall shut, and no man shall open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:8; Job 36:7; Zechariah 10:4"> And I will fasten him as a naile in a sure place, and hee shall be for the throne of glorie to his fathers house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 6:1–3; John 20:21–23; Matthew 28:18"> And they shall hang vpon him all the glorie of his fathers house, euen of the nephewes and posteritie all small vessels, from the vessels of the cuppes, euen to all the instruments of musike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 46:11; Esther 9:24–25; Psalms 146:3"> In that day, sayeth the Lord of hostes, shall the naile, that is fastned in the sure place, depart and shall be broken, and fall: and the burden, that was vpon it, shall bee cut off: for the Lord hath spoken it.</span>
