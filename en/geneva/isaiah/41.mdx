<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Habakkuk 2:20; Zechariah 2:13"> Keep silence before mee, O ylands, and let the people renue their strength: let the come neere, and let them speake: let vs come together into iudgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; 2 Samuel 22:43; Ezra 1:2"> Who raised vp iustice from the East, and called him to his foote? and gaue the nations before him, and subdued the Kings? he gaue them as dust to his sword, and as scattered stubble vnto his bowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 5:24; Isaiah 57:2"> He pursued them, and passed safely by the way that he had not gone with his feete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 48:12; Revelation 22:13; Revelation 1:17"> Who hath wrought and done it? he that calleth the generations from the beginning. I the Lord am the first, and with the last I am ye same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:15–16; Psalms 65:8; Psalms 66:3"> The yles sawe it, and did feare, and the ends of the earth were abashed, drew neere, and came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 44:12; Isaiah 40:19; Daniel 3:1–7"> Euery man helped his neighbour, and saide to his brother, Be strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 40:19–20; Daniel 3:1–7; Judges 18:17–18"> So the workeman comforted the founder, and he that smote with ye hammer, him that smote by course, saying, It is ready for the sodering, and he fastened it with nayles that it shoulde not be mooued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 2:23; 2 Chronicles 20:7; Deuteronomy 14:2"> But thou, Israel, art my seruant, and thou Iaakob, whom I haue chosen, the seede of Abraham my friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:22; Psalms 94:14; Deuteronomy 7:6–7"> For I haue taken thee from the endes of the earth, and called thee before the chiefe thereof, and saide vnto thee, Thou art my seruant: I haue chosen thee, and not cast thee away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 43:1–2; Romans 8:31"> Feare thou not, for I am with thee: be not afraide, for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee, and helpe thee, and will susteine thee with the right hand of my iustice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 23:22; Isaiah 45:24; Revelation 3:9"> Beholde, all they that prouoke thee, shalbe ashamed, and confounded: they shalbe as nothing, and they that striue with thee, shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:35–36; Job 20:7–9; Isaiah 17:14"> Thou shalt seeke them and shalt not finde them: to wit, the men of thy strife, for they shall be as nothing, and the men that warre against thee, as a thing of nought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 73:23; Psalms 63:8"> For I the Lord thy God will hold thy right hand, saying vnto thee, Feare not, I wil helpe thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 43:14; Psalms 22:6; Isaiah 54:5"> Feare not, thou worme, Iaakob, and ye men of Israel: I wil helpe thee, sayth the Lord and thy redeemer the holy one of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 4:13; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Zechariah 4:7"> Behold, I wil make thee a roller, and a newe threshing instrument hauing teeth: thou shalt thresh the mountaines, and bring them to pouder, and shalt make the hilles as chaffe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:2; Isaiah 61:10–11; Isaiah 45:24–25"> Thou shalt fanne them, and the winde shall carie them away, and the whirlewinde shall scatter them: and thou shalt reioyce in the Lord, and shalt glory in the holy one of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 55:1; Isaiah 30:19; Isaiah 44:3"> When the poore and the needy seeke water, and there is none (their tongue faileth for thirst: I the Lord will heare them: I the God of Israel will not forsake them)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 107:35; Isaiah 35:6–7; Isaiah 58:11"> I will open riuers in the toppes of the hils, and fountaines in the middes of the valleis: I will make the wildernesse as a poole of water, and the waste land as springs of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 55:13; Isaiah 37:31–32; Isaiah 35:1"> I will set in the wildernesse the cedar, the shittah tree, and the mirre tree, and the pine tree, and I will set in the wildernesse the firre tree, the elme and the boxe tree together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 12:9; Isaiah 44:23; Isaiah 43:21"> Therefore let them see and knowe, and let them consider and vnderstande together that the hand of the Lord hath done this, and the holy one of Israel hath created it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 23:3–4; Job 38:3; Micah 6:1–2"> Stand to your cause, saith the Lord: bring forth your strong reasons, saith ye King of Iaakob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 45:21; Isaiah 48:14; Isaiah 44:7"> Let them bring foorth, and let them tell vs what shall come: let them shew the former things what they be, that wee may consider them, and knowe the latter ende of them: either declare vs things for to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:19; Isaiah 42:9; Jeremiah 10:5"> Shewe the things that are to come hereafter, that we may know that you are gods: yea, doe good or doe euill, that we may declare it, and beholde it together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 115:8; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 41:29"> Beholde, ye are of no value, and your making is of naught: man hath chosen an abomination by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:2; 2 Samuel 22:43; Isaiah 10:6"> I haue raised vp from the North, and he shall come: from the East sunne shall he cal vpon my Name, and shall come vpon princes as vpon clay, and as the potter treadeth myre vnder the foote.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 44:7; Isaiah 41:22; Isaiah 45:21"> Who hath declared from the beginning, that we may knowe? or before time, that we may say, He is righteous? Surely there is none that sheweth: surely there is none that declareth: surely there is none that heareth your wordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Isaiah 52:7; Isaiah 44:28"> I am the first, that saieth to Zion, Beholde, beholde them: and I will giue to Ierusalem one that shall bring good tidings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 63:5; Daniel 2:10–11; Isaiah 40:13–14"> But when I behelde, there was none, and when I inquired of them, there was no counsellor, and when I demaunded of them, they answered not a woorde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:13; Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 10:2–16"> Beholde, they are all vanitie: their worke is of nothing, their images are wind and confusion.</span>
