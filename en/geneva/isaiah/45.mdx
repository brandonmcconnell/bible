<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:20–24; Isaiah 44:28; Isaiah 42:6"> Thus sayeth the Lord vnto Cyrus his anointed, whose right hand I haue holden to subdue nations before him: therefore will I weaken the loynes of Kings and open the doores before him, and the gates shall not be shut:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:4; Luke 3:5; Isaiah 42:16"> I will goe before thee and make the crooked streight: I will breake the brasen doores, and burst the yron barres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 48:15; Isaiah 43:1; Exodus 33:17"> And I will giue thee the treasures of darkenesse, and the things hid in secret places, that thou maist know that I am the Lord which call thee by thy name, euen the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 41:8–9; Acts 17:23; Isaiah 44:1"> For Iaakob my seruants sake, and Israel mine elect, I will euen call thee by thy name and name thee, though thou hast not knowen me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 44:8; Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 46:9"> I am the Lord and there is none other: there is no God besides me: I girded thee though thou hast not knowen me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 1:11; Isaiah 45:5; Psalms 46:10"> That they may knowe from the rising of the sunne and from the West, that there is none besides me. I am the Lord, and there is none other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:13–14; Amos 3:6; Psalms 75:7"> I forme the light and create darkenes: I make peace and create euill: I the Lord doe all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Isaiah 61:11; Psalms 72:6"> Ye heauens, send the dewe from aboue, and let the cloudes droppe downe righteousnesse: let the earth open, and let saluation and iustice growe foorth: let it bring them foorth together: I the Lord haue created him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Romans 9:20–21; Isaiah 10:15"> Woe be vnto him that striueth with his maker, the potsherd with the potsherds of the earth: shall the clay say to him that facioneth it, What makest thou? or thy worke, It hath none hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:16; Hebrews 12:9; Malachi 1:6"> Woe vnto him that sayeth to his father, What hast thou begotten? or to his mother, What hast thou brought foorth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:3; Isaiah 48:17; Mark 11:24"> Thus saith the Lord, the holy one of Israel, and his maker, Aske me of things to come concerning my sonnes, and concerning the workes of mine hands: commande you me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 42:5; Isaiah 44:24; Jeremiah 27:5"> I haue made the earth, and created man vpon it: I, whose hands haue spred out the heauens, I haue euen commanded all their armie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:2; Isaiah 13:17; Isaiah 41:25"> I haue raised him vp in righteousnesse, and I will direct all his wayes: he shall build my citie, and he shall let goe my captiues, not for price nor rewarde, saith the Lord of hostes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; 1 Corinthians 14:25; Jeremiah 16:19"> Thus sayth the Lord, The labour of Egypt, and the marchandise of Ethiopia, and of the Sabeans, men of stature shall come vnto thee, and they shall be thine: they shall follow thee, and shall goe in chaines: they shall fall downe before thee, and make supplication vnto thee, saying, Surely God is in thee, and there is none other God besides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17; Psalms 44:24; Isaiah 57:17"> Verely thou, O God, hidest thy selfe, O God, the Sauiour of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 44:9; Isaiah 44:11; Isaiah 42:17"> All they shalbe ashamed and also confounded: they shall goe to confusion together, that are the makers of images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalms 25:3; Isaiah 54:4"> But Israel shall be saued in the Lord, with an euerlasting saluation: ye shall not be ashamed nor confounded worlde without ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 115:16; Jeremiah 10:12; Isaiah 45:12"> For thus saith the Lord (that created heauen, God himselfe, that formed the earth, and made it: he that prepared it, he created it not in vaine: he formed it to be inhabited) I am the Lord, and there is none other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 48:16; Amos 5:4; Numbers 23:19–20"> I haue not spoken in secrete, neither in a place of darkenes in the earth: I saide not in vaine vnto the seede of Iaakob, Seeke you me: I the Lord doe speake righteousnesse, and declare righteous things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:5; Isaiah 44:17–20; Isaiah 43:9"> Assemble your selues, and come: drawe neere together, ye abiect of the Gentiles: they haue no knowledge, that set vp the wood of their idole, and pray vnto a god, that cannot saue them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 45:5; Isaiah 43:11; Isaiah 44:7–8"> Tell ye and bring them, and let them take counsell together, who hath declared this from the beginning? or hath tolde it of olde? Haue not I the Lord? and there is none other God beside me, a iust God, and a Sauiour: there is none beside me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 6:40; Micah 7:7; Hebrews 12:2"> Looke vnto me, and ye shall be saued: all the endes of the earth shall be saued: for I am God, and there is none other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; Romans 14:10–12; Isaiah 55:11"> I haue sworne by my selfe: the worde is gone out of my mouth in righteousnesse, and shall not returne, That euery knee shall bowe vnto me, and euery tongue shall sweare by me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 41:11; Isaiah 54:17; Ephesians 6:10"> Surely he shall say, In the Lord haue I righteousnesse and strength: he shall come vnto him, and all that prouoke him, shall be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:16; Galatians 3:27–29; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> The whole seede of Israel shall be iustified, and glorie in the Lord.</span>
