<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 6:48–58; Jeremiah 2:13; Psalms 22:26"> Wherefore doe ye lay out siluer and not for bread? and your labour without being satisfied? hearken diligently vnto me, and eate that which is good, and let your soule delite in fatnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:40; Proverbs 4:20; Matthew 11:28"> Encline your eares, and come vnto me: heare, and your soule shall liue, and I will make an euerlasting couenant with you, euen the sure mercies of Dauid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:23–24; Isaiah 9:6–7; Daniel 9:25"> Beholde, I gaue him for a witnes to the people, for a prince and a master vnto the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 60:9; Isaiah 60:5; Zechariah 2:11"> Beholde, thou shalt call a nation that thou knowest not, and a nation that knew not thee, shall runne vnto thee, because of the Lord thy God, and the holy one of Israel: for hee hath glorified thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12–14; Psalms 145:18; Isaiah 45:19"> Seeke ye the Lord while he may be found: call ye vpon him while he is neere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; James 4:8–10; Ezekiel 18:21–23"> Let the wicked forsake his wayes, and the vnrighteous his owne imaginations, and returne vnto the Lord, and he wil haue mercy vpon him: and to our God, for hee is very ready to forgiue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 92:5; Psalms 40:5; Hosea 14:9"> For my thoughtes are not your thoughts, neither are your wayes my wayes, sayth the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:31–36; Psalms 103:11; Matthew 11:25"> For as ye heauens are higher then the earth, so are my wayes higher then your wayes, and my thoughtes aboue your thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:9–11; Deuteronomy 32:2; Isaiah 30:23"> Surely as the raine commeth downe and the snow from heauen, and returneth not thither, but watereth the earth and maketh it to bring forth and bud, that it may giue seede to the sower, and bread vnto him that eateth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Isaiah 46:10; Romans 10:17"> So shall my worde be, that goeth out of my mouth: it shall not returne vnto me voyde, but it shall accomplish that which I will, and it shall prosper in the thing whereto I sent it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:22; Isaiah 44:23; Psalms 105:43"> Therefore ye shall go out with ioy, and be led forth with peace: the mountaines and the hilles shall breake foorth before you into ioye, and all the trees of the fielde shall clap their handes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20–21; Isaiah 41:19; 1 Peter 2:9–10"> For thornes there shall grow firre trees: for nettles shall growe the myrrhe tree, and it shalbe to the Lord for a name, and for an euerlasting signe that shall not be taken away.</span>
