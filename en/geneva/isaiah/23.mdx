<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 3:4–8; Amos 1:9–10; Genesis 10:4"> The burden of Tyrus. Howle, yee shippes of Tarshish: for it is destroied, so that there is none house: none shall come from the lande of Chittim: it is reueiled vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 47:5; Isaiah 41:1; Ezekiel 28:2"> Be still, yee that dwell in the yles: the marchantes of Zidon, and such as passe ouer the sea, haue replenished thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:18; 1 Chronicles 13:5; Ezekiel 27:33"> The seede of Nilus growing by the abundance of waters, and the haruest of the riuer was her reuenues, and she was a marte of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Genesis 10:15; Jeremiah 47:3–4"> Be ashamed, thou Zidon: for the sea hath spoken, euen the strength of the sea, saying, I haue not trauailed, nor brought forth children, neither nourished yong men, nor brought vp virgins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 2:9–11; Exodus 15:14–16; Revelation 18:17–19"> When the fame commeth to the Egyptians, they shall be sorie, concerning the rumour of Tyrus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 23:12; Isaiah 21:15; Isaiah 23:10"> Goe you ouer to Tarshish: howle, yee that dwell in the yles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 22:2; Isaiah 32:13; Joshua 19:29"> Is not this that your glorious citie? her antiquitie is of ancient daies: her owne feete shall leade her afarre off to be a soiourner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 10:8; Jeremiah 50:44–45; Deuteronomy 29:24–28"> Who hath decreed this against Tyrus (that crowneth men) whose marchantes are princes? whose chapmen are the nobles of the worlde?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 4:6; Isaiah 13:11; Job 40:11–12"> The Lord of hostes hath decreed this, to staine the pride of all glorie, and to bring to contempt all them that be glorious in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 12:21; Isaiah 23:14; Psalms 18:32"> Passe through thy lande like a flood to the daughter of Tarshish: there is no more strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 9:3–4; Exodus 14:21; Jeremiah 47:7"> He stretched out his hand vpon the sea: he shooke the kingdomes: the Lord hath giuen a commandement concerning the place of marchandise, to destroy the power thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 18:22; Ezekiel 26:13–14; Isaiah 23:1–2"> And he saide, Thou shalt no more reioyce when thou art oppressed: O virgin daughter of Zidon: rise vp, goe ouer vnto Chittim: yet there thou shalt haue no rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:6; Isaiah 10:5; 2 Kings 17:24"> Behold the lande of the Caldeans: this was no people: Asshur founded it by the inhabitantes of the wildernesse: they set vp the towers thereof: they raised the palaces thereof and hee brought it to ruine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 23:1; Isaiah 23:6; Ezekiel 27:25–30"> Howle yee shippes of Tarshish, for your strength is destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:22; Jeremiah 25:9–11; Daniel 7:14"> And in that day shall Tyrus bee forgotten seuentie yeeres, (according to the yeeres of one King) at the ende of seuentie yeeres shall Tyrus sing as an harlot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:14; Proverbs 7:10–12"> Take an harpe and go about the citie: (thou harlot thou hast beene forgotten) make sweete melodie, sing moe songes that thou maiest be remembred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nahum 3:4; Ezekiel 27:6–36; Revelation 19:2"> And at the ende of seuentie yeres shall the Lord visite Tyrus, and shee shall returne to her wages, and shall commit fornication with all the kingdomes of the earth, that are in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 72:10; Deuteronomy 26:12–14; Philippians 4:17–18"> Yet her occupying and her wages shall bee holy vnto the Lord: it shall not be laied vp nor kept in store, but her marchandise shalbe for them that dwell before the Lord, to eate sufficiently, and to haue durable clothing.</span>
