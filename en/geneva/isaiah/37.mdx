<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 19:1–37; Ezra 9:5; Isaiah 36:22–38"> And when the King Hezekiah heard it, he rent his clothes, and put on sackcloth and came into the House of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; 2 Kings 18:18; Isaiah 36:3"> And he sent Eliakim the stewarde of the house, aud Shebna the chanceller, with the Elders of the Priestes, clothed in sackcloth vnto Isaiah the Prophet, the sonne of Amoz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 66:9; Hosea 13:13; Isaiah 26:17–18"> And they sayd vnto him, Thus saith Hezekiah, This day is a day of tribulation and of rebuke and blasphemie: for the children are come to the birth, and there is no strength to bring foorth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Isaiah 36:20; Isaiah 10:22"> If so be the Lord thy God hath heard the wordes of Rabshakeh, whom the King of Asshur his master hath sent to raile on the liuing God, and to reproch him with wordes which the Lord thy God hath heard, then lift thou vp thy prayer for the remnant that are left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So the seruants of the King Hezekiah came to Isaiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 7:4; Isaiah 35:4; Mark 5:36"> And Isaiah sayde vnto them, Thus say vnto your master, Thus saith the Lord, Be not afrayd of the wordes that thou hast heard, wherewith the seruants of the king of Asshur haue blasphemed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 31:8–9; 2 Chronicles 32:21; Isaiah 33:10–12"> Beholde, I wil send a blast vpon him, and he shall heare a noyse, and returne to his owne land, and I will cause him to fall by the sword in his owne land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 10:29; Numbers 33:20–21; 2 Kings 19:8–9"> So Rabshakeh returned, and found the King of Asshur fighting against Libnah: for he had heard that he was departed from Lachish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 18:1; Isaiah 20:5; Isaiah 37:7"> He heard also men say of Tirhakah, King of Ethiopia, Beholde, he is come out to fight against thee: and when he heard it, he sent other messengers to Hezekiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 36:15; 2 Kings 18:5; Matthew 27:43"> Thus shall ye speake to Hezekiah King of Iudah, saying, Let not thy God deceiue thee, in whom thou trustest, saying, Ierusalem shall not be giuen into the hand of the King of Asshur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 36:18–20; 2 Kings 18:33–35; Isaiah 14:17"> Beholde, thou hast heard what the Kings of Asshur haue done to all lands in destroying them, and shalt thou be deliuered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 11:31; 2 Kings 17:6; 2 Kings 18:11"> Haue the gods of the nations deliuered them, which my fathers haue destroyed? as Gozan, and Haran, and Rezeph, and the children of Eden, which were at Telassar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 19:13; 2 Kings 17:24; Isaiah 36:19"> Where is the King of Hamath, and the King of Arpad, and the King of the citie of Sepharuaim, Hena and Iuah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 19:14; Psalms 123:1–4; Joel 2:17–20"> So Hezekiah receiued the letter of the hand of the messengers and read it, and he went vp into the House of the Lord, and Hezekiah spread it before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:8–9; Daniel 9:3–4; James 5:13"> And Hezekiah prayed vnto the Lord, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Psalms 99:1; Psalms 86:10"> O Lord of hostes, God of Israel, which dwellest betweene the Cherubims, thou art very God alone ouer all the kingdomes of the earth: thou hast made the heauen and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:40; Psalms 17:6; Isaiah 37:4"> Encline thine eare, O Lord, and heare: open thine eyes, O Lord, and see, and heare all the wordes of Saneherib, who hath sent to blaspheme the liuing God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Nahum 2:11–12; 2 Kings 15:29; 2 Kings 17:6"> Trueth it is, O Lord, that the Kings of Asshur haue destroyed all lands, and their countrey,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 44:9–10; Jeremiah 10:11; Hosea 8:6"> And haue cast their gods in ye fire: for they were no gods, but the worke of mans hands, euen wood or stone: therefore they destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 46:10; 1 Kings 18:36–37; Psalms 83:17–18"> Nowe therefore, O Lord our God, saue thou vs out of his hand, that all the kingdomes of the earth may knowe, that thou onely art the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Isaiah 38:3–6; Acts 4:31"> Then Isaiah the sonne of Amoz sent vnto Hezekiah, saying, Thus sayth the Lord God of Israel, Because thou hast prayed vnto me, concerning Saneherib king of Asshur,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 16:4; Lamentations 2:13; Isaiah 1:8"> This is the worde that the Lord hath spoken against him, the virgine, the daughter of Zion, hath despised thee, and laughed thee to scorne: the daughter of Ierusalem, hath shaken her head at thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:7; Daniel 5:20–23; Isaiah 17:7"> Whome hast thou railed on and blasphemed? and against whome hast thou exalted thy voyce, and lifted vp thine eyes on hie? euen against the holy one of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 14:8; Isaiah 10:18; Exodus 15:9"> By thy seruants hast thou railed on the Lord, and sayd, By the multitude of my charets I am come vp to the top of the mountaines to the sides of Lebanon, and will cut downe the hie cedars thereof, and the faire firre trees thereof, and I will goe vp to the heightes of his top and to the forest of his fruitfull places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 20:10; Isaiah 36:12; 2 Kings 19:23–24"> I haue digged and drunke the waters, and with the plant of my feete haue I dryed all the riuers closed in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 4:27–28; Acts 2:23; 1 Peter 2:8"> Hast thou not heard howe I haue of olde time made it, and haue formed it long ago? and should I now bring it, that it should be destroyed, and layde on ruinous heapes, as cities defensed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 129:6; Psalms 37:2; 1 Peter 1:24"> Whose inhabitants haue small power, and are afrayd and confounded: they are like the grasse of the field and greene herbe, or grasse on the house tops, or corne blassed afore it be growen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 2:13; Psalms 139:1–11; Jeremiah 23:23–24"> But I know thy dwelling, and thy going out, and thy comming in, and thy fury against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:4; Isaiah 30:28; Isaiah 37:34"> Because thou ragest against me, and thy tumult is come vnto mine eares, therefore will I put mine hooke in thy nostrels, and my bridle in thy lips, and wil bring thee backe againe the same way thou camest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 19:29; Leviticus 25:4–5; 1 Kings 13:3–5"> And this shalbe a signe vnto thee, O Hezekiah, Thou shalt eate this yeere such as groweth of it selfe: and the second yeere, such things as growe without sowing: and in the third yeere, sowe ye and reape, and plant vineyards, and eate the fruite thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; 2 Kings 19:30–31; Psalms 80:9"> And the remnant that is escaped of the house of Iudah, shall againe take roote downward and beare fruite vpward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 9:7; 2 Kings 19:31; Isaiah 59:17"> For out of Ierusalem shall goe a remnant, and they that escape out of mount Zion: the zeale of the Lord of hostes shall doe this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 17:12; Isaiah 10:32–34; Isaiah 17:14"> Therefore thus sayth the Lord, concerning the King of Asshur, He shall not enter into this citie, nor shoote an arrow there, nor come before it with shield, nor cast a mount against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Proverbs 21:30"> By the same way that he came, he shall returne, and not come into this citie, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 20:6; Isaiah 38:6; Isaiah 31:5"> For I will defend this citie to saue it, for mine owne sake, and for my seruant Dauids sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 10:12; 2 Kings 19:35; Isaiah 10:33–34"> Then the Angel of the Lord went out, and smote in the campe of Asshur an hundreth, fourescore, and fiue thousand: so when they arose early in the morning, beholde, they were all dead corpses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jonah 1:2; Jonah 3:3; Genesis 10:11–12"> So Saneherib king of Asshur departed, and went away and returned and dwelt at Nineueh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 8:4; Jeremiah 51:27; Ezra 4:2"> And as he was in the temple worshipping of Nisroch his god, Adramelech and Sharezer his sonnes slewe him with the sword, and they escaped into the land of Ararat: and Esarhaddon his sonne reigned in his steade.</span>
