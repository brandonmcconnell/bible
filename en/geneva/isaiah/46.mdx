<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:44; Isaiah 21:9; Jeremiah 50:2"> Bel is bowed downe: Nebo is fallen: their idoles were vpon the beastes, and vpon the cattell: they which did beare you, were laden with a wearie burden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:7; Judges 18:17–18; 2 Samuel 5:21"> They are bowed downe, and fallen together: for they coulde not rid them of the burden, and their soule is gone into captiuitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:31; Isaiah 51:7; Isaiah 51:1"> Heare ye me, O house of Iaakob, and all that remaine of the house of Israel, which are borne of me from the wombe, and brought vp of me from the birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 71:18; Psalms 48:14; Psalms 92:14"> Therefore vnto olde age, I the same, euen I will beare you vntill the hoare heares: I haue made you: I will also beare you, and I will cary you and I will deliuer you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:18; Isaiah 40:25; Psalms 89:6"> To whom will ye make me like or make me equall, or copare me, that I should be like him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 17:29; Isaiah 40:19–20; Habakkuk 2:18–20"> They draw gold out of the bagge and weigh siluer in the balance, and hire a goldsmith to make a god of it, and they bowe downe, and worship it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 45:20; Jeremiah 10:5; Isaiah 46:1"> They beare it vpon the shoulders: they carie him and set him in his place: so doeth he stand, and cannot remoue from his place. Though one crie vnto him, yet can he not answere, nor deliuer him out of his tribulation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:18–21; Psalms 115:8; Ezekiel 18:28"> Remember this, and be ashamed: bring it againe to minde, O you transgressers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 111:4; Isaiah 42:9; Isaiah 45:21–22"> Remember the former things of old: for I am God, and there is none other God, and there is nothing like me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Psalms 33:11; Ephesians 1:9–11"> Which declare the last thing from the beginning: and from of olde, the things that were not done, saying, My counsell shall stand, and I will doe whatsoeuer I will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 14:24–27; Job 23:13"> I call a birde from the East, and the man of my counsell from farre: as I haue spoken, so will I bring it to passe: I haue purposed it, and I will doe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:5; Isaiah 46:3; Psalms 76:5"> Heare me, ye stubburne hearted, that are farre from iustice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 62:11; Isaiah 12:2; Isaiah 61:3"> I bring neere my iustice: it shall not be farre off, and my saluation shall not tarie: for I wil giue saluation in Zion, and my glory vnto Israel.</span>
