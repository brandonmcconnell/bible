<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ephesians 5:14; Ephesians 5:8; John 8:12"> Arise, O Ierusalem: be bright, for thy light is come, and the glorie of the Lord is risen vpon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 1 Peter 2:9; Acts 17:30–31"> For beholde, darkenesse shall couer the earth, and grosse darkenesse the people: but the Lord shall arise vpon thee, and his glory shall be seene vpon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 13:47; Isaiah 49:23; Revelation 21:24"> And the Gentiles shall walke in thy light, and Kings at the brightnesse of thy rising vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 42:6; Isaiah 49:20–22; Isaiah 49:18"> Lift vp thine eyes round about, and beholde: all these are gathered, and come to thee: thy sonnes shall come from farre, and thy daughters shalbe nourished at thy side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 61:6; Isaiah 23:18; Isaiah 60:11"> Then thou shalt see and shine: thine heart shall be astonied and enlarged, because the multitude of the sea shalbe conuerted vnto thee, and the riches of the Gentiles shall come vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 2:11; Psalms 72:10; Psalms 72:15"> The multitude of camels shall couer thee: and the dromedaries of Midian and of Ephah: all they of Sheba shall come: they shall bring golde and incense, and shewe foorth the prayses of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Genesis 25:13; Hebrews 13:15–16"> All the sheepe of Kedar shall be gathered vnto thee: the rammes of Nebaioth shall serue thee: they shall come vp to bee accepted vpon mine altar: and I will beautifie the house of my glorie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Luke 13:29; Isaiah 60:4"> Who are these that flee like a cloude, and as the doues to their windowes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 55:5; Isaiah 2:16; Isaiah 51:5"> Surely the yles shall waite for mee, and the shippes of Tarshish, as at the beginning, that they may bring thy sonnes from farre, and their siluer, and their golde with them, vnto the Name of the Lord thy God, and to the Holy one of Israel, because he hath glorified thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 61:5; Isaiah 49:23; Revelation 21:26"> And the sonnes of strangers shall builde vp thy walles, and their Kings shall minister vnto thee: for in my wrath I smote thee, but in my mercie I had compassion on thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 21:25–26; Isaiah 60:5; Isaiah 60:18"> Therefore thy gates shalbe open continually: neither day nor night shall they be shutte, that men may bring vnto thee the riches of the Gentiles, and that their Kings may be brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 14:12–19; Isaiah 54:15; Psalms 2:12"> For the nation and the kingdome, that will not serue thee, shall perish: and those nations shalbe vtterly destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2; Isaiah 35:2; Psalms 132:7"> The glory of Lebanon shall come vnto thee, the firre tree, the elme and the boxe tree together, to beautifie the place of my Sanctuarie: for I will glorifie the place of my feete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Isaiah 49:23; Hebrews 12:22"> The sonnes also of them that afflicted thee, shall come and bowe vnto thee: and all they that despised thee, shall fall downe at the soles of thy feete: and they shall call thee, The citie of the Lord, Zion of the Holy one of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 61:7; Jeremiah 30:17; Isaiah 49:14–23"> Where as thou hast bene forsaken and hated: so that no man went by thee, I will make thee an eternall glorie, and a ioye from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Isaiah 66:11–12; Isaiah 66:14"> Thou shalt also sucke ye milke of the Gentiles, and shalt sucke the breastes of Kings: and thou shalt know, that I the Lord am thy Sauiour, and thy Redeemer, the mightie one of Iaakob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 11:40; Isaiah 30:26; Isaiah 1:26"> For brasse will I bring golde, and for yron will I bring siluer, and for wood brasse, and for stones yron. I will also make thy gouernement peace, and thine exactours righteousnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 26:1; Isaiah 11:9; Zechariah 9:8"> Violence shall no more be heard of in thy land, neither desolation, nor destruction within thy borders: but thou shalt call saluation, thy walles, and praise, thy gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 21:23; Revelation 22:5; Zechariah 2:5"> Thou shalt haue no more sunne to shine by day, neither shall the brightnesse of the moone shine vnto thee: for the Lord shall be thine euerlasting light, and thy God, thy glorie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Psalms 84:11; Isaiah 35:10"> Thy sunne shall neuer goe downe, neither shall thy moone be hid: for the Lord shalbe thine euerlasting light, and the dayes of thy sorowe shalbe ended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Psalms 37:22; Psalms 37:11"> Thy people also shalbe all righteous: they shall possesse the land for euer, the grasse of my planting shalbe the worke of mine handes, that I may be glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; Hebrews 10:36; Luke 18:7"> A litle one shall become as a thousande, and a small one as a strong nation: I the Lord wil hasten it in due time.</span>
