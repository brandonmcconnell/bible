<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:7; Psalms 94:15; Hebrews 12:14"> Heare me, ye that follow after righteousnes, and ye that seeke the Lord: looke vnto the rocke, whence ye are hewen, and to the hole of the pit, whence ye are digged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:24; Galatians 3:9–14; Romans 4:1–5"> Consider Abraham your father, and Sarah that bare you: for I called him alone, and blessed him, and increased him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Joel 2:3; Isaiah 52:9"> Surely the Lord shall comfort Zion: he shall comfort all her desolations, and he shall make her desert like Eden, and her wildernes like the garden of the Lord: ioy and gladnesse shalbe founde therein: praise, and the voyce of singing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Isaiah 2:3; 1 Corinthians 9:21"> Hearken ye vnto me, my people, and giue eare vnto me, O my people: for a Law shall proceede from me, and I will bring foorth my iudgement for the light of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:17–18; Isaiah 46:13; Isaiah 42:4"> My righteousnes is neere: my saluation goeth foorth, and mine armes shall iudge the people: the yles shall waite for me, and shall trust vnto mine arme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Psalms 102:25–26; Isaiah 34:4"> Lift vp your eyes to the heauens, and looke vpon the earth beneath: for the heauens shall vanish away like smoke, and the earth shall waxe olde like a garment, and they that dwell therein, shall perish in like maner: but my saluation shall be for euer, and my righteousnesse shall not bee abolished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 5:11; Psalms 37:31; Ezekiel 2:6"> Hearken vnto me, ye that know righteousnesse, the people in whose heart is my Lawe. Feare ye not the reproche of men, neither be ye afraide of their rebukes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Isaiah 50:9; Hosea 5:12"> For the mothe shall eate them vp like a garment, and the worme shall eate them like wool: but my righteousnesse shalbe for euer, and my saluation from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 74:13–14; Isaiah 52:1; Psalms 89:10"> Rise vp, rise vp, and put on strength, O arme of the Lord: rise vp as in the olde time in the generations of the worlde. Art not thou the same, that hath cutte Rahab, and wounded the dragon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 43:16; Isaiah 63:11–12; Isaiah 50:2"> Art not thou the same, which hath dried the Sea, euen the waters of the great deepe, making the depth of the Sea a way for the redeemed to passe ouer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17"> Therefore the redeemed of the Lord shall returne, and come with ioy vnto Zion, and euerlasting ioy shalbe vpon their head: they shall obtaine ioy, and gladnesse: and sorow and mourning shall flee away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 118:6; 1 Peter 1:24; Isaiah 2:22"> I, euen I am he, that comfort you. Who art thou, that thou shouldest feare a mortall man, and the sonne of man, which shalbe made as grasse?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 45:12; Isaiah 40:22; Psalms 104:2"> And forgettest the Lord thy maker, that hath spred out the heauens, and layde the foundations of the earth? and hast feared continually all the day, because of the rage of the oppressour, which is readie to destroy? Where is now the rage of the oppressour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Zechariah 9:11; Isaiah 52:2"> The captiue hasteneth to be loosed, and that hee should not die in the pitte, nor that his bread should faile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35; Isaiah 54:5; Amos 9:5–6"> And I am the Lord thy God that deuided the Sea, when his waues roared: the Lord of hostes is his Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:21; Isaiah 49:2; John 17:8"> And I haue put my wordes in thy mouth, and haue defended thee in the shadowe of mine hand, that I may plant the heauens, and lay the foundation of the earth, and say vnto Zion, Thou art my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:1; Revelation 14:10; Psalms 60:3"> Awake, awake, and stande vp, O Ierusalem, which hast drunke at the hande of the Lord the cup of his wrath: thou hast drunken the dregges of the cup of trembling, and wrung them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 49:21; Psalms 88:18; Psalms 142:4"> There is none to guide her among all the sonnes, whome she hath brought foorth: there is none that taketh her by the hand of all the sonnes that she hath brought vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Amos 7:2; Isaiah 47:9; Ezekiel 14:21"> These two thinges are come vnto thee: who will lament thee? desolation and destruction and famine, and the sworde: by whome shall I comfort thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11–12; Revelation 16:9–11; Ezekiel 39:19"> Thy sonnes haue fainted, and lye at the head of all the streetes as a wilde bull in a nette, and are full of the wrath of the Lord, and rebuke of thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 29:9; Isaiah 54:11; Isaiah 51:17"> Therefore heare nowe this, thou miserable and drunken, but not with wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 22:23; Isaiah 51:17; Jeremiah 50:34"> Thus saith thy Lord God, euen God that pleadeth the cause of his people, Beholde, I haue taken out of thine hande the cuppe of trembling, euen the dregges of the cuppe of my wrath: thou shalt drinke it no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:24; Zechariah 12:2; Isaiah 49:25–26"> But I will put it into their hande that spoile thee: which haue said to thy soule, Bowe downe, that wee may goe ouer, and thou hast layde thy bodie as the grounde, and as the streete to them that went ouer.</span>
