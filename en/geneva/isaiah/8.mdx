<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 30:8; Job 19:23–24; Habakkuk 2:2–3"> Moreover, the Lord sayd vnto me, Take thee a great roll, and write in it with a mans penne, Make speede to the spoyle: haste to the praye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 16:10–11; 2 Kings 18:2; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> Then I tooke vnto me faithfull witnesses to recorde, Vriah the Priest, and Zechariah the sonne of Ieberechiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 1:3–9; Judges 4:4; Isaiah 7:13–14"> After, I came vnto the Prophetesse, which conceiued, and bare a sonne. Then sayd the Lord to me, Call his name, Mahershalalhash-baz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:11; 2 Kings 16:9; Deuteronomy 1:39"> For before the childe shall haue knowledge to crye, My father, and my mother, he shall take away the riches of Damascus and the spoyle of Samaria, before the King of Asshur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 7:10"> And the Lord spake yet againe vnto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 9:7; Nehemiah 3:15; Isaiah 7:6"> Because this people hath refused the waters of Shiloah that runne softly, and reioyce with Rezin, and the sonne of Remaliah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 17:12–13; Genesis 6:17; Isaiah 7:17"> Nowe therefore, beholde, the Lord bringeth vp vpon them the waters of the Riuer mightie and great, euen the King of Asshur with all his glory, and he shall come vp vpon all their riuers, and goe ouer all their banks,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 30:28; Matthew 1:23"> And shall breake into Iudah, and shall ouerflowe and passe through, and shall come vp to the necke, and the stretching out of his wings shall fill the breadth of thy land, O Immanu-el.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 14:5–6; Proverbs 11:21; Psalms 37:14–15"> Gather together on heapes, O ye people, and ye shalbe broken in pieces, and hearken all ye of farre countreys: gird your selues, and you shalbe broken in pieces: gird your selues, and you shalbe broken in pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 3:37; Romans 8:31; Job 5:12"> Take counsell together, yet it shall be brought to nought: pronounce a decree, yet shall it not stand: for God is with vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Proverbs 1:15; Jeremiah 15:19"> For the Lord spake thus to me in taking of mine hand, and taught me, that I should not walke in the way of this people, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14–15; Luke 21:9; Psalms 53:5"> Say ye not, A confederacie to all them, to whome this people sayth a confederacie, neither feare you their feare, nor be afrayd of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:5; Isaiah 26:3–4; Revelation 15:4"> Sanctifie the Lord of hostes, and let him be your feare, and let him be your dread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 2:8; Romans 9:32–33; Isaiah 28:16"> And he shalbe as a Sanctuarie: but as a stumbling stone, and as a rocke to fall vpon, to both the houses of Israel, and as a snare and as a net to the inhabitants of Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 21:44; Luke 20:17–18; Isaiah 28:13"> And many among them shall stumble, and shall fall and shalbe broken and shalbe snared and shalbe taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 12:4; Hebrews 3:5; Mark 4:10–11"> Binde vp the testimonie: seale vp the Law among my disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36–39; Habakkuk 2:3; Lamentations 3:25–26"> Therefore I will waite vpon the Lord that hath hid his face from the house of Iaakob, and I wil looke for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:13–14; Luke 2:34; Zechariah 8:3"> Beholde, I and the children whome the Lord hath giuen me, are as signes and as wonders in Israel, by the Lord of hostes, which dwelleth in mount Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 10:13; Leviticus 20:6; 2 Chronicles 33:6"> And when they shall say vnto you, Enquire at them that haue a spirit of diuination, and at the soothsayers, which whisper and murmure, Should not a people enquire at their God? from the liuing to the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 1:19; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Isaiah 30:8–11"> To the Law, and to the testimonie, if they speake not according to this worde: it is because there is no light in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Proverbs 19:3; Lamentations 4:4–5"> Then he that is afflicted and famished, shall go to and from in it: and when he shalbe hungry, he shall euen freat himselfe, and curse his King and his gods, and shall looke vpward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 5:30; Matthew 22:13; Jeremiah 13:16"> And when he shall looke to the earth, beholde trouble, and darkenes, vexation and anguish, and he is driuen to darkenes.</span>
