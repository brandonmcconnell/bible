<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:26; Isaiah 41:1; Isaiah 1:2"> Come neere, ye nations and heare, and hearken, ye people: let the earth heare and all that is therein, the world and al that proceedeth thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:15–29; Zephaniah 3:8; Zechariah 14:12–16"> For the indignation of the Lord is vpon all nations, and his wrath vpon all their armies: hee hath destroyed them and deliuered them to the slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 2:20; Ezekiel 39:4; Isaiah 34:7"> And their slaine shalbe cast out, and their stincke shall come vp out of their bodies, and the mountaines shalbe melted with their blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 6:13–14; Matthew 24:29; Ezekiel 32:7–8"> And all the hoste of heauen shalbe dissolued, and the heauens shall be folden like a booke: and all their hostes shall fall as the leafe falleth from the vine, and as it falleth from the figtree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:10; Malachi 1:4; Isaiah 63:1"> For my sword shalbe drunken in the heauen: beholde, it shall come downe vpon Edom, euen vpon the people of my curse to iudgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:1; Jeremiah 49:13; Isaiah 34:5"> The sword of the Lord is filled with blood: it is made fat with the fat and with the blood of the lambes and the goates, with the fat of the kidneis of the rams: for the Lord hath a sacrifice in Bozrah, and a great slaughter in the land of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 68:30; Numbers 23:22; Jeremiah 50:27"> And the vnicorne shall come downe with them and the heiffers with the bulles, and their lande shalbe drunken with blood, and their dust made fat with fatnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 63:4; Isaiah 61:2; Jeremiah 46:10"> For it is the day of the Lordes vengeance, and the yeere of recompence for the iudgement of Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; Psalms 11:6; Genesis 19:28"> And the riuers thereof shall be turned into pitche, and the dust thereof into brimstone, and the land thereof shalbe burning pitch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 19:3; Isaiah 13:20; Malachi 1:3–4"> It shall not be quenched night nor day: the smoke thereof shall goe vp euermore: it shall be desolate from generation to generation: none shall passe through it for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 2:8; 2 Kings 21:13; Zephaniah 2:14"> But the pelicane and the hedgehog shall possesse it, and the great owle, and the rauen shall dwel in it, and he shall stretch out vpon it the line of vanitie, and the stones of emptinesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 3:6–8; 1 Corinthians 13:2; Jeremiah 39:6"> The nobles thereof shall call to the kingdome, and there shalbe none, and all the princes thereof shalbe as nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 10:22; Malachi 1:3"> And it shall bring foorth thornes in the palaces thereof, nettles and thistles in the strong holdes thereof, and it shall be an habitation for dragons, and a court for ostriches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 13:21–22"> There shall meete also Ziim and Iim, and the Satyre shall cry to his fellow, and the shricheowle shall rest there, and shall finde for her selfe a quiet dwelling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:13"> There shall the owle make her nest, and laye, and hatche, and gather them vnder her shadowe: there shall the vultures also bee gathered, euery one with her make.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 33:9; Joshua 1:8; Matthew 5:18"> Seeke in the booke of the Lord, and reade: none of these shall fayle, none shall want her make: for his mouth hath commanded, and his very Spirit hath gathered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 34:10; Acts 17:26; Isaiah 17:14"> And he hath cast the lot for them, and his hand hath deuided it vnto them by line: they shall possesse it for euer: from generation to generation shall they dwell in it.</span>
