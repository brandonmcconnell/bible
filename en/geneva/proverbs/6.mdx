<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 22:26; Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 20:16"> My sonne, if thou be surety for thy neighbour, and hast striken hands with the stranger,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 12:13"> Thou art snared with the wordes of thy mouth: thou art euen taken with the woordes of thine owne mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 4:10; Psalms 31:8; 2 Samuel 24:14"> Doe this nowe, my sonne, and deliuer thy selfe: seeing thou art come into the hande of thy neighbour, goe, and humble thy selfe, and sollicite thy friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 132:4; Proverbs 6:10–11; Mark 13:35–36"> Giue no sleepe to thine eyes, nor slumber to thine eyelids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 91:3; Psalms 124:7; Proverbs 1:17"> Deliuer thy selfe as a doe from the hande of the hunter, and as a birde from the hande of the fouler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 20:4; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 6:9"> Goe to the pismire, O sluggarde: beholde her waies, and be wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:39–12; Proverbs 30:27; Job 39:26–30"> For shee hauing no guide, gouernour, nor ruler,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; 1 Timothy 6:19; Proverbs 30:25"> Prepareth her meat in the sommer, and gathereth her foode in haruest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; Jeremiah 4:14; Ephesians 5:14"> Howe long wilt thou sleepe, O sluggarde? when wilt thou arise out of thy sleepe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; Proverbs 6:6; Proverbs 23:33–34"> Yet a litle sleepe, a litle slumber, a litle folding of the hands to sleepe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 24:34"> Therefore thy pouertie commeth as one that trauaileth by the way, and thy necessitie like an armed man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; 1 Timothy 5:13; Proverbs 16:27"> The vnthriftie man and the wicked man walketh with a froward mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Proverbs 10:10; Job 15:12"> He maketh a signe with his eyes: he signifieth with his feete: he instructeth with his fingers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 16:17; Micah 2:1; Hosea 8:7"> Lewde things are in his heart: he imagineth euill at all times, and raiseth vp contentions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Jeremiah 19:11; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> Therefore shall his destruction come speedily: hee shall be destroyed suddenly without recouerie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 11:5; Proverbs 8:13; Proverbs 11:20"> These sixe things doeth the Lord hate: yea, his soule abhorreth seuen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 120:2–3; Proverbs 12:22; Psalms 101:5"> The hautie eyes, a lying tongue, and the hands that shed innocent blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 6:5; Proverbs 1:16; Romans 3:15"> An heart that imagineth wicked enterprises, feete that be swift in running to mischiefe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Proverbs 6:14; James 3:18"> A false witnesse that speaketh lyes, and him that rayseth vp contentions among brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1; Deuteronomy 27:16; Proverbs 7:1–4"> My sonne, keepe thy fathers commandement, and forsake not thy mothers instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 3:3; Proverbs 7:3–4; 2 Corinthians 3:3"> Binde them alway vpon thine heart, and tye them about thy necke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Proverbs 3:23–24; Psalms 119:24"> It shall leade thee, when thou walkest: it shall watch for thee, when thou sleepest, and when thou wakest, it shall talke with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:105; 2 Peter 1:19; Psalms 19:8"> For the commandement is a lanterne, and instruction a light: and corrections for instruction are the way of life,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 2:16; Proverbs 7:5; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> To keepe thee from the wicked woman, and from ye flatterie of ye tongue of a strange woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 1:14–15; Matthew 5:28; 2 Kings 9:30"> Desire not her beautie in thine heart, neither let her take thee with her eye lids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 15:30; Proverbs 29:3; 1 Samuel 2:36"> For because of the whorish woman a man is brought to a morsell of bread, and a woman wil hunt for the precious life of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 31:9–12; Hosea 7:4–7; James 3:5"> Can a man take fire in his bosome, and his clothes not be burnt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Or can a man go vpon coales, and his feete not be burnt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1; Malachi 3:5; 2 Samuel 12:9–10"> So he that goeth in to his neighbours wife, shall not be innocent, whosoeuer toucheth her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 38:39"> Men do not despise a thiefe, when he stealeth, to satisfie his soule, because he is hungrie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 19:8; Job 20:18; 2 Samuel 12:6"> But if he be founde, he shall restore seuen folde, or he shall giue all the substance of his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 7:7; Hebrews 13:4; Romans 1:22–24"> But he that committeth adulterie with a woman, he is destitute of vnderstanding: he that doeth it, destroyeth his owne soule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 5:9–11; Psalms 51:8; Judges 16:19–21"> He shall finde a wounde and dishonour, and his reproch shall neuer be put away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Numbers 5:14; Song of Solomon 8:6"> For ielousie is the rage of a man: therefore he will not spare in the day of vengeance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 8:25; Malachi 2:9; Proverbs 7:13"> He cannot beare the sight of any raunsome: neither will he consent, though thou augment the giftes.</span>
