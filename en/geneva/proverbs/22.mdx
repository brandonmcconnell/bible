<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:1; Luke 10:20; 1 Kings 1:47"> A good name is to be chosen aboue great riches, and louing fauour is aboue siluer and aboue golde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 31:15; Proverbs 14:31; 1 Samuel 2:7"> The rich and poore meete together: the Lord is the maker of them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:12; Proverbs 14:16; Proverbs 29:1"> A prudent man seeth the plague, and hideth himselfe: but the foolish goe on still, and are punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; James 4:10; Psalms 34:9–10"> The rewarde of humilitie, and the feare of God is riches, and glory, and life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:19; 1 John 5:18; Proverbs 16:17"> Thornes and snares are in the way of the frowarde: but he that regardeth his soule, will depart farre from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 6:4; Deuteronomy 6:7; 2 Timothy 3:15"> Teache a childe in the trade of his way, and when he is olde, he shall not depart from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:1; Proverbs 22:16; Matthew 18:25"> The rich ruleth the poore, and the borower is seruant to the man that lendeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:7–8; Job 4:8; Isaiah 30:31"> He that soweth iniquitie, shall reape affliction, and the rodde of his anger shall faile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Hebrews 6:10; 1 Timothy 6:18–19"> He that hath a good eye, he shalbe blessed: for he giueth of his bread vnto the poore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 26:20–21; Genesis 21:9–10; 1 Corinthians 5:5–6"> Cast out the scorner, and strife shall go out: so contention and reproche shall cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 16:13; Matthew 5:8; Psalms 101:6"> Hee that loueth purenesse of heart for the grace of his lippes, the King shalbe his friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:9; 2 Timothy 3:8–9; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> The eyes of the Lord preserue knowledge: but hee ouerthroweth the wordes of the transgressour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:19; Proverbs 26:13–16; Numbers 13:32–33"> The slouthfull man saith, A lyon is without, I shall be slaine in the streete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 23:27; Proverbs 6:24–29"> The mouth of strage women is as a deepe pit: he with whom the Lord is angry, shall fall therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 19:18"> Foolishnesse is bounde in the heart of a childe: but the rodde of correction shall driue it away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 22:22–23; Psalms 12:5; James 2:13"> Hee that oppresseth the poore to increase him selfe, and giueth vnto the riche, shall surely come to pouertie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:12; Psalms 90:12; Proverbs 3:1"> Incline thine eare, and heare the wordes of the wise, and apply thine heart vnto my knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Proverbs 2:10; Psalms 119:103"> For it shalbe pleasant, if thou keepe them in thy bellie, and if they be directed together in thy lippes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Jeremiah 17:7; 1 Peter 1:21"> That thy confidence may be in the Lord, I haue shewed thee this day: thou therefore take heede.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> Haue not I written vnto thee three times in counsels and knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:3–4; 1 Peter 3:15; John 20:31"> That I might shewe thee the assurance of the wordes of trueth to answere the wordes of trueth to them that sende to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 7:10; Malachi 3:5; Exodus 23:6"> Robbe not the poore, because hee is poore, neither oppresse the afflicted in iudgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 140:12; Proverbs 23:11"> For the Lord will defende their cause, and spoyle the soule of those that spoyle them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 29:22; 2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Proverbs 21:24"> Make no friendship with an angrie man, neither goe with the furious man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 13:20; Psalms 106:35"> Least thou learne his wayes, and receiue destruction to thy soule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 11:15; Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 6:1–5"> Be not thou of them that touch the hand, nor among them that are suretie for debts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 20:16; 2 Kings 4:1; Exodus 22:26–27"> If thou hast nothing to paye, why causest thou that he should take thy bed from vnder thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:17; Deuteronomy 19:14; Proverbs 23:10"> Thou shalt not remooue the ancient bounds which thy fathers haue made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 11:28; Matthew 25:21; Proverbs 12:24"> Thou seest that a diligent man in his businesse standeth before Kings, and standeth not before the base sort.</span>
