<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 16:9; Proverbs 20:24; Ezra 6:22"> The Kings heart is in the hand of the Lord, as the riuers of waters: he turneth it whithersoeuer it pleaseth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 16:2; Proverbs 16:25; Luke 16:15"> Euery way of a man is right in his owne eyes: but the Lord pondereth the hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; Hosea 6:6; Micah 6:6–8"> To doe iustice and iudgement is more acceptable to the Lord then sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 6:17; 1 Peter 5:5; Isaiah 2:17"> A hautie looke, and a proude heart, which is the light of the wicked, is sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4; Ephesians 4:28"> The thoughtes of the diligent doe surely bring abundance: but whosoeuer is hastie, commeth surely to pouertie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 13:11; Proverbs 10:2; Proverbs 20:21"> The gathering of treasures by a deceitfull tongue is vanitie tossed to and from of them that seeke death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 21:21; Ephesians 5:6; Proverbs 1:18–19"> The robberie of the wicked shall destroy them: for they haue refused to execute iudgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 2:15; 1 John 2:29; Psalms 14:2–3"> The way of some is peruerted and strange: but of the pure man, his worke is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 19:13; Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 27:15–16"> It is better to dwell in a corner of the house top, then with a contentious woman in a wide house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 3:2–3; Proverbs 3:29; Psalms 36:4"> The soule of the wicked wisheth euill: and his neighbour hath no fauour in his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 19:25; Proverbs 18:15; Proverbs 9:9"> When the scorner is punished, the foolish is wise: and when one instructeth the wise, he wil receiue knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 14:11; Psalms 37:35–36; 2 Peter 3:6–7"> The righteous teacheth the house of the wicked: but God ouerthroweth the wicked for their euill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 28:27; Matthew 6:14; Proverbs 1:28"> He that stoppeth his eare at the crying of the poore, he shall also cry and not be heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:16; Proverbs 19:6; Proverbs 17:8"> A gift in secret pacifieth anger, and a gift in the bosome great wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 10:29; Matthew 7:23; Matthew 13:41–42"> It is ioye to the iust to doe iudgement: but destruction shalbe to the workers of iniquitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 10:38; Psalms 125:5; Psalms 49:14"> A man that wandreth out of the way of wisdome, shall remaine in the congregation of the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:21; 1 Timothy 5:6; 2 Timothy 3:4"> Hee that loueth pastime, shalbe a poore man: and he that loueth wine and oyle, shall not be riche.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:8; Isaiah 43:3–4; Isaiah 53:4–5"> The wicked shalbe a ransome for the iust, and the transgressour for the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 21:9; Psalms 120:5–6; Psalms 55:6–7"> It is better to dwell in the wildernesse, then with a contentious and angry woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:3–4; Psalms 112:3; Ecclesiastes 7:11"> In the house of the wise is a pleasant treasure and oyle: but a foolish man deuoureth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 5:6; 1 Corinthians 15:58; Proverbs 22:4"> He that followeth after righteousnes and mercy, shall finde life, righteousnes, and glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Proverbs 24:5; Ecclesiastes 9:13–18"> A wise man goeth vp into the citie of the mightie, and casteth downe the strength of the confidence thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 13:3; Proverbs 18:21; James 1:26"> He that keepeth his mouth and his tongue, keepeth his soule from afflictions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 18:12; Proverbs 1:22"> Proude, hautie and scornefull is his name that worketh in his arrogancie wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 12:24; Matthew 25:26"> The desire of the slouthfull slayeth him: for his hands refuse to worke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:26; Acts 20:33–35; Ephesians 4:28"> He coueteth euermore greedily, but the righteous giueth and spareth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 66:3; Proverbs 15:8; Jeremiah 6:20"> The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination: how much more when he bringeth it with a wicked minde?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:13; Proverbs 19:5; Proverbs 19:9"> A false witnes shall perish: but hee that heareth, speaketh continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:11; Proverbs 11:5; Haggai 2:18–19"> A wicked man hardeneth his face: but the iust, he will direct his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Acts 5:39; Jeremiah 9:23"> There is no wisedome, neither vnderstanding, nor counsell against the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 20:7; Isaiah 31:1; Psalms 33:17–18"> The horse is prepared against the day of battell: but saluation is of the Lord.</span>
