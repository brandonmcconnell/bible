<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:7; Psalms 53:5; Isaiah 26:3–4"> The wicked flee when none pursueth: but the righteous are bolde as a lyon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:28; 1 Kings 16:8–29; 2 Kings 15:8–31"> For the transgression of the land there are many princes thereof: but by a man of vnderstanding and knowledge a realme likewise endureth long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 18:28–30"> A poore man, if he oppresse the poore, is like a raging raine, that leaueth no foode.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Ephesians 5:11; Matthew 14:4"> They that forsake the Law, prayse the wicked: but they that keepe the Law, set themselues against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 92:6; James 1:5; 1 John 2:27"> Wicked men vnderstand not iudgemnt: but they that seeke the Lord vnderstand all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 19:1; Proverbs 28:18; Luke 16:19–23"> Better is the poore that walketh in his vprightnesse, then hee that peruerteth his wayes, though he be riche.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:3–4; Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 3:1–35"> He that keepeth the Law, is a childe of vnderstanding: but hee that feedeth the gluttons, shameth his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 13:22; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> He that increaseth his riches by vsurie and interest, gathereth them for him that will be mercifull vnto the poore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 66:18; 2 Timothy 4:3–4; Zechariah 7:11–13"> He that turneth away his eare from hearing the Law, euen his prayer shalbe abominable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 26:27; Psalms 37:25–26; Galatians 2:4"> He that causeth the righteous to go astray by an euill way, shall fall into his owne pit, and the vpright shall inherite good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 18:17; Ecclesiastes 9:15–17; Proverbs 26:16"> The riche man is wise in his owne conceite: but the poore that hath vnderstanding, can trie him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 28:28"> When righteous men reioyce, there is great glory: but when the wicked come vp, the man is tried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 1:8–10; Psalms 32:3–5; Job 31:33"> He that hideth his sinnes, shall not prosper: but he that confesseth, and forsaketh them, shall haue mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 23:17; Psalms 2:11; Isaiah 66:2"> Blessed is the man that feareth alway: but he that hardeneth his heart, shall fall into euill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 2:16; Proverbs 19:12"> As a roaring lyon, and an hungry beare, so is a wicked ruler ouer the poore people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Jeremiah 22:15–17; Ecclesiastes 4:1"> A prince destitute of vnderstanding, is also a great oppressour: but hee that hateth couetousnes, shall prolong his dayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; 1 Kings 21:19; Exodus 21:14"> A man that doeth violence against the blood of a person, shall flee vnto the graue, and they shall not stay him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Proverbs 28:6; Revelation 3:3"> He that walketh vprightly, shalbe saued: but he that is froward in his wayes, shall once fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Judges 9:4; Proverbs 27:23–27"> He that tilleth his land, shall be satisfied with bread: but he that followeth the idle, shall be filled with pouertie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 16:10–12; Proverbs 13:11; Revelation 2:10"> A faythfull man shall abound in blessings, and he that maketh haste to be riche, shall not be innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; Proverbs 18:5; Ezekiel 13:19"> To haue respect of persons is not good: for that man will transgresse for a piece of bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; 1 Timothy 6:9; Proverbs 28:20"> A man with a wicked eye hasteth to riches, and knoweth not, that pouertie shall come vpon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5–6; Matthew 18:15; Psalms 141:5"> He that rebuketh a man, shall finde more fauour at length, then he that flattereth with his tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:4–6; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 19:26"> Hee that robbeth his father and mother, and sayth, It is no transgression, is the companion of a man that destroyeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 11:25; 1 Timothy 6:6; Proverbs 13:10"> He that is of a proude heart, stirreth vp strife: but he that trusteth in the Lord, shall be fatte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Jeremiah 17:9; Job 28:28"> Hee that trusteth in his owne heart, is a foole: but he that walketh in wisdome, shall be deliuered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Deuteronomy 15:7; Psalms 41:1–3"> He that giueth vnto the poore, shall not lacke: but he that hideth his eyes, shall haue many curses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Job 24:4; Acts 12:23–24"> When the wicked rise vp, men hide them selues: but when they perish, ye righteous increase.</span>
