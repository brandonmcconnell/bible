<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 19:11; Proverbs 12:16; Micah 7:5"> A foole powreth out all his minde: but a wise man keepeth it in till afterward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 20:8; 2 Kings 10:6–7; Psalms 52:2–4"> Of a prince that hearkeneth to lyes, all his seruants are wicked.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 17:25; Proverbs 29:17"> The rodde and correction giue wisdome: but a childe set a libertie, maketh his mother ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:36; Psalms 91:8; Psalms 58:10"> When the wicked are increased, transgression increaseth: but ye righteous shall see their fall.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 1:25; Luke 11:28; John 13:17"> Where there is no vision, the people decay: but he that keepeth the Lawe, is blessed.</span>
