<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 37:4–5; Proverbs 3:6; Psalms 55:22"> Commit thy workes vnto the Lord, and thy thoughts shalbe directed.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Proverbs 20:10; Proverbs 20:23"> A true weight and balance are of the Lord: all the weightes of the bagge are his worke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 25:5; Proverbs 29:14; Luke 12:48"> It is an abomination to Kings to commit wickednes: for the throne is stablished by iustice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 14:35; Psalms 101:5–7; Proverbs 22:11"> Righteous lips are the delite of Kings, and the King loueth him that speaketh right things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:2; Proverbs 19:12; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> The wrath of a King is as messengers of death: but a wise man will pacifie it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 72:6; Job 29:23–24; Hosea 6:3"> In the light of the Kings coutenance is life: and his fauour is as a cloude of the latter raine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:10–11; Matthew 16:26; Proverbs 8:19"> Howe much better is it to get wisedome then golde? and to get vnderstanding, is more to be desired then siluer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 35:8; Proverbs 19:16; Proverbs 10:9"> The pathe of the righteous is to decline from euil, and hee keepeth his soule, that keepeth his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:2; Isaiah 2:11–12; Proverbs 18:12"> Pride goeth before destruction, and an high minde before the fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:3; Isaiah 57:15; Psalms 138:6"> Better it is to be of humble minde with the lowly, then to deuide the spoyles with the proude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; Psalms 2:12; Isaiah 30:18"> He that is wise in his busines, shall finde good: and he that trusteth in the Lord, he is blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 16:23–24; Isaiah 50:4; Proverbs 15:7"> The wise in heart shall bee called prudent: and the sweetenesse of the lippes shall increase doctrine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 13:14; John 6:63; John 5:24"> Vnderstading is welspring of life vnto them that haue it: and the instruction of fooles is folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Matthew 12:34–35; Colossians 3:16"> The heart of the wise guideth his mouth wisely, and addeth doctrine to his lippes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Proverbs 24:13–14; Proverbs 25:11–12"> Faire wordes are as an hony combe, sweetenesse to the soule, and health to the bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 14:12; Proverbs 12:15; 2 Corinthians 13:5"> There is a way that seemeth right vnto man: but the issue thereof are the wayes of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:7; Proverbs 14:23; Proverbs 9:12"> The person that traueileth, traueileth for himselfe: for his mouth craueth it of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 3:6; Proverbs 6:14; Isaiah 5:18"> A wicked man diggeth vp euill, and in his lippes is like burning fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 17:9; Proverbs 18:8; Proverbs 15:18"> A frowarde person soweth strife: and a tale teller maketh diuision among princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 2:12–15; Proverbs 12:26; Proverbs 1:10–14"> A wicked man deceiueth his neighbour, and leadeth him into the way that is not good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Micah 7:3; Matthew 14:7–8"> He shutteth his eyes to deuise wickednes: he moueth his lippes, and bringeth euil to passe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 20:29; Leviticus 19:32; Proverbs 3:1–2"> Age is a crowne of glory, when it is founde in the way of righteousnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 15:18; Proverbs 25:28"> He that is slowe vnto anger, is better then the mightie man: and hee that ruleth his owne minde, is better then he that winneth a citie.</span>
