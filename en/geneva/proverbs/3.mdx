<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 16:16; Proverbs 8:19; Job 28:13–19"> For the marchandise thereof is better then the marchandise of siluer, and the gaine thereof is better then golde.</span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 91:3; 1 Samuel 2:9; Proverbs 14:26"> For the Lord shall be for thine assurance, and shall preserue thy foote from taking.</span>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 6:14; Proverbs 16:29–30; Psalms 55:20"> Intend none hurt against thy neighbour, seeing he doeth dwell without feare by thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24; Proverbs 29:22; Romans 12:18–21"> Striue not with a man causelesse, when he hath done thee no harme.</span>
