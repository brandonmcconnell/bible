<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 48:10; Jeremiah 17:10; Psalms 26:2"> As is the fining pot for siluer, and the fornace for golde, so the Lord trieth the heartes.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 14:31; Job 31:29; 1 John 3:17"> Hee that mocketh the poore, reprocheth him, that made him: and he that reioyceth at destruction, shall not be vnpunished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 127:3–5; Proverbs 13:22; Proverbs 16:31"> Childres children are the crowne of the elders: and the glory of ye children are their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:12; Proverbs 12:19; Psalms 50:16–17"> Hie talke becommeth not a foole, much lesse a lying talke a prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 23:8; Micah 7:3; 1 Samuel 25:35"> A rewarde is as a stone pleasant in the eyes of them that haue it: it prospereth, whithersoeuer it turneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 10:12; 1 Peter 4:8; Proverbs 16:28"> Hee that couereth a transgression, seeketh loue: but hee that repeateth a matter, separateth the prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 9:8–9; Proverbs 15:5"> A reproofe entereth more into him that hath vnderstanding, then an hundreth stripes into a foole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 2:46; 2 Samuel 16:5–9; 2 Samuel 18:15"> A sedicious person seeketh onely euill, and a cruel messenger shall be sent against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 13:8; 2 Samuel 17:8; Proverbs 28:15"> It is better for a man to meete a beare robbed of her whelpes, then a foole in his follie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 109:4–13; 1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:17"> He that rewardeth euil for good, euil shall not depart from his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:3; Proverbs 25:8; 2 Timothy 2:23–24"> The beginning of strife is as one that openeth the waters: therefore or the contention be medled with, leaue off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 23:7; Proverbs 18:5; Isaiah 5:23"> He that iustifieth the wicked, and he that condemneth the iust, euen they both are abomination to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 21:25–26; Proverbs 23:23; Proverbs 18:15"> Wherefore is there a price in the hand of the foole to get wisdome, and he hath none heart?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 18:24; John 15:13–14; 1 Samuel 20:17"> A friende loueth at all times: and a brother is borne for aduersitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:15; Proverbs 6:1–5; Proverbs 22:26–27"> A man destitute of vnderstanding, toucheth the hande, and becommeth suretie for his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 18:12; 1 Samuel 25:36–38"> He loueth transgression, that loueth strife: and he that exalteth his gate, seeketh destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 3:6–8; Proverbs 10:31; Proverbs 6:12–15"> The froward heart findeth no good: and he that hath a naughtie tongue, shall fall into euill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 19:13; Proverbs 17:25"> He that begetteth a foole, getteth himselfe sorow, and the father of a foole can haue no ioy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 15:13; Proverbs 12:25; Proverbs 18:14"> A ioyfull heart causeth good health: but a sorowfull minde dryeth the bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 17:8; Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 23:8"> A wicked man taketh a gift out of the bosome to wrest the wayes of iudgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:14; Ecclesiastes 8:1; Proverbs 14:6"> Wisdome is in the face of him that hath vnderstanding: but the eyes of a foole are in the corners of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; 2 Samuel 13:1–22; Proverbs 19:13"> A foolish sonne is a griefe vnto his father, and a heauines to her that bare him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; Proverbs 18:5; John 18:22"> Surely it is not good to condemne the iust, nor that ye princes should smite such for equitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 3:18; James 1:19; Proverbs 10:19"> Hee that hath knowledge, spareth his wordes, and a man of vnderstanding is of an excellent spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 13:5; Proverbs 15:2; Ecclesiastes 10:14"> Euen a foole (when he holdeth his peace) is counted wise, and hee that stoppeth his lips, prudent.</span>
