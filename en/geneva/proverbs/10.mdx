<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:1; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 17:25"> THE PARABLE OF SALOMON. A wise sonne maketh a glad father: but a foolish sonne is an heauines to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Proverbs 12:28; Psalms 49:6–10"> The treasures of wickednesse profite nothing: but righteousnesse deliuereth from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 37:3; Matthew 6:30–33; Psalms 37:25"> The Lord will not famish the soule of the righteous: but he casteth away the substance of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 21:5; Proverbs 20:4; Proverbs 19:15"> A slouthfull hand maketh poore: but the hand of the diligent maketh riche.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 6:8; Proverbs 30:25; Proverbs 17:2"> He that gathereth in sommer, is the sonne of wisdome: but he that sleepeth in haruest, is the sonne of confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:2; Proverbs 28:20; Job 29:13"> Blessings are vpon the head of the righteous: but iniquitie shall couer the mouth of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 112:6; Luke 1:48; Mark 14:9"> The memoriall of the iust shalbe blessed: but the name of the wicked shall rotte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 7:24–25; Proverbs 12:1; James 3:13"> The wise in heart will receiue commandements: but the foolish in talke shalbe beaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Matthew 10:26; Psalms 26:11–12"> He that walketh vprightly, walketh boldely: but he that peruerteth his wayes, shalbe knowen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Proverbs 6:13; 3 John 1:10"> He that winketh with the eye, worketh sorowe, and he yet is foolish in talke, shalbe beaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:14; Ephesians 4:29; Proverbs 10:6"> The mouth of a righteous man is a welspring of life: but iniquitie couereth the mouth of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7; Proverbs 17:9"> Hatred stirreth vp contentions: but loue couereth all trespasses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 26:3; Proverbs 6:32; Isaiah 50:4"> In the lippes of him that hath vnderstanding wisdome is founde, and a rod shalbe for the backe of him that is destitute of wisedome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 10:8; Proverbs 13:3"> Wise men lay vp knowledge: but ye mouth of the foole is a present destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 18:11; Proverbs 19:7; Psalms 52:7"> The riche mans goodes are his strong citie: but the feare of the needie is their pouertie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 6:23; Isaiah 3:10–11; Hebrews 6:10"> The labour of the righteous tendeth to life: but the reuenues of the wicked to sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–11; Luke 11:28; Proverbs 6:23"> He that regardeth instruction, is in the way of life: but he that refuseth correction, goeth out of the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–26; Psalms 55:21; 2 Samuel 3:27"> He that dissembleth hatred with lying lips, and he that inuenteth slaunder, is a foole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 1:19; James 3:2; Proverbs 17:27–28"> In many wordes there cannot want iniquitie: but he that refrayneth his lippes, is wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Proverbs 12:18; Genesis 6:5"> The tongue of the iust man is as fined siluer: but the heart of the wicked is litle worth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; Proverbs 17:16; Proverbs 1:29"> The lippes of the righteous doe feede many: but fooles shall die for want of wisedome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 107:38; Genesis 26:12; 1 Samuel 2:7–8"> The blessing of the Lord, it maketh riche, and he doeth adde no sorowes with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:21; Proverbs 2:14; Proverbs 14:9"> It is as a pastime to a foole to doe wickedly: but wisedome is vnderstanding to a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; Isaiah 66:4; Matthew 5:6"> That which the wicked feareth, shall come vpon him: but God wil graunt the desire of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 15:5; Psalms 58:9; Matthew 7:24–27"> As the whirlewinde passeth, so is the wicked no more: but the righteous is as an euerlasting foundation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 25:13; Proverbs 26:6; Matthew 25:26"> As vineger is to the teeth, and as smoke to the eyes, so is the slouthful to them that send him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 9:11; Proverbs 3:2; Psalms 91:16"> The feare of the Lord increaseth the dayes: but the yeeres of the wicked shalbe diminished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 8:13; Romans 15:13; Psalms 73:24–26"> The patient abiding of the righteous shall be gladnesse: but the hope of the wicked shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 21:15; Romans 2:8–9; Philippians 4:13"> The way of the Lord is strength to the vpright man: but feare shall be for the workers of iniquitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 37:22; Psalms 125:1; Psalms 37:9–10"> The righteous shall neuer be remooued: but the wicked shall not dwell in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 37:30; Proverbs 10:13; Proverbs 10:20–21"> The mouth of the iust shall be fruitfull in wisdome: but the tongue of the froward shall be cut out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Proverbs 12:6; Proverbs 11:11"> The lips of the righteous knowe what is acceptable: but the mouth of the wicked speaketh froward things.</span>
