<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 14:34; Proverbs 29:8; James 3:6"> By the blessing of the righteous, the citie is exalted: but it is subuerted by the mouth of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 10:19; 1 Peter 2:23; Luke 18:9"> He that despiseth his neighbour, is destitute of wisedome: but a man of vnderstanding will keepe silence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 20:19; Proverbs 25:9; Leviticus 19:16"> Hee that goeth about as a slanderer, discouereth a secret: but hee that is of a faithfull heart concealeth a matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 24:6; Proverbs 15:22; Proverbs 20:18"> Where no counsell is, the people fall: but where many counsellers are, there is health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 6:1–5; Proverbs 22:26–27"> Hee shall be sore vexed, that is suretie for a stranger, and he that hateth suretiship, is sure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 31:30–31; Luke 10:42; Acts 16:14–15"> A gracious woman atteineth honour, and the strong men atteine riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Matthew 25:34–40; James 2:13"> Hee that is mercifull, rewardeth his owne soule: but he that troubleth his own flesh, is cruel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 6:8–9; Ecclesiastes 10:8; Hosea 10:12–13"> The wicked worketh a deceitful worke: but hee that soweth righteousnes, shall receiue a sure rewarde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 10:16; Romans 2:8–9; Proverbs 12:28"> As righteousnes leadeth to life: so hee that followeth euill, seeketh his owne death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 119:1; Proverbs 21:29; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> They that are of a froward heart, are abomination to the Lord: but they that are vpright in their way, are his delite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 112:1–2; Acts 2:39; Jeremiah 32:39"> Though hande ioyne in hande, the wicked shall not be vnpunished: but the seede of the righteous shall escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–4; Proverbs 31:30; Nahum 3:4–6"> As a iewell of golde in a swines snoute: so is a faire woman, which lacketh discretion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Romans 2:8–9; Matthew 5:6"> The desire of the righteous is onely good: but the hope of the wicked is indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Luke 6:38; Deuteronomy 15:10"> There is that scattereth, and is more increased: but hee that spareth more then is right, surely commeth to pouertie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; 2 Corinthians 9:6–9; Isaiah 58:7–11"> The liberall person shall haue plentie: and he that watereth, shall also haue raine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 29:13; Genesis 42:6; Amos 8:4–6"> He that withdraweth the corne, the people will curse him: but blessing shalbe vpon the head of him that selleth corne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 7:15–16; Proverbs 17:11; Psalms 9:15–16"> He that seeketh good things, getteth fauour: but he that seeketh euill, it shall come to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Jeremiah 17:8; Psalms 52:7–8"> He that trusteth in his riches, shall fall: but the righteous shall florish as a leafe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:16; Proverbs 14:19; Proverbs 15:27"> He that troubleth his owne house, shall inherite the winde, and the foole shalbe seruant to the wise in heart.</span>
