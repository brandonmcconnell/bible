<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:1; 1 Kings 4:32; Micah 1:1"> THESE ARE ALSO PARABLES of Salomon, which the men of Hezekiah King of Iudah copied out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:29; Romans 11:33–34; Job 11:7–8"> The glorie of God is to conceale a thing secret: but the Kings honour is to search out a thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 55:9; Psalms 103:11; 1 Kings 4:29"> The heaues in height, and the earth in deepenes, and the Kings heart can no man search out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:20–21; Ezekiel 22:18; Proverbs 17:3"> Take the drosse from the siluer, and there shall proceede a vessell for the finer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 16:12; Proverbs 20:8; Psalms 101:7–8"> Take away the wicked from the King, and his throne shall be stablished in righteousnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; Proverbs 25:27; Proverbs 16:19"> Boast not thy selfe before the King, and stand not in the place of great men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; Luke 18:14; 1 Peter 5:5"> For it is better, that it be saide vnto thee, Come vp hither, then thou to be put lower in the presece of the prince whom thine eyes haue seene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 5:25; Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 18:6"> Goe not foorth hastily to strife, least thou know not what to doe in the ende thereof, when thy neighbour hath put thee to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 18:5–17; Proverbs 11:13; Proverbs 20:19"> Debate thy matter with thy neighbour, and discouer not the secret to another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:39"> Least he that heareth it put thee to shame, and thine infamie doe not cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Isaiah 50:4; Ecclesiastes 12:10"> A word spoken in his place, is like apples of golde with pictures of siluer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 141:5; Proverbs 15:31–32; Proverbs 15:5"> He that reprooueth the wise, and the obedient eare, is as a golden earering and an ornament of fine golde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 13:17; Proverbs 25:25; Philippians 2:25–30"> As the colde of the snowe in the time of haruest, so is a faithfull messenger to them that send him: for he refresheth the soule of his masters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:6; Luke 14:11; 2 Peter 2:15–19"> A man that boasteth of false liberalitie, is like cloudes and winde without raine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Ecclesiastes 10:4; Proverbs 16:14"> A Prince is pacified by staying of anger, and a soft tongue breaketh the bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 25:27; Ephesians 5:18; Proverbs 24:13–14"> If thou haue found hony, eate that is sufficient for thee, least thou be ouerfull, and vomit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 19:2–3; Judges 19:18–21; Romans 15:24"> Withdrawe thy foote from thy neighbours house, least he be weary of thee, and hate thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Proverbs 12:18; James 3:6"> A man that beareth false witnes against his neighbour, is like an hammer and a sword, and a sharpe arrowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Ezekiel 29:6–7; Job 6:14–20"> Confidence in an vnfaythfull man in time of trouble, is like a broken tooth and a sliding foote.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Ecclesiastes 3:4; Isaiah 58:7"> Hee that taketh away the garment in the colde season, is like vineger powred vpon nitre, or like him that singeth songs to an heauy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Matthew 5:44; Exodus 23:4–5"> If hee that hateth thee be hungry, giue him bread to eate, and if he be thirstie, giue him water to drinke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Matthew 10:13; 1 Corinthians 15:18"> For thou shalt lay coles vpon his head, and the Lord shall recompense thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 37:22; Psalms 15:3; Romans 1:30"> As the Northwinde driueth away the raine, so doeth an angry countenance the slandering tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 21:9; Proverbs 27:15–16"> It is better to dwell in a corner of the house top, then with a contentious woman in a wide house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 15:30; Romans 10:15; Psalms 63:1"> As are the colde waters to a weary soule, so is good newes from a farre countery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:18–19; 1 Samuel 22:14–18; 1 Thessalonians 2:15"> A righteous man falling downe before the wicked, is like a troubled well, and a corrupt spring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 5:44; Proverbs 27:2; Philippians 2:3"> It is not good to eate much hony: so to search their owne glory is not glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 16:32; Proverbs 22:24; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> A man that refraineth not his appetite, is like a citie which is broken downe and without walles.</span>
