<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; 1 Chronicles 22:9; 1 Chronicles 28:5"> The Parables of Salomon the sonne of Dauid King of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 7:4; Deuteronomy 4:5–6; 2 Timothy 3:15–17"> To knowe wisdome, and instruction, to vnderstand ye wordes of knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:1–9; Job 22:22; Proverbs 8:10–11"> To receiue instruction to do wisely, by iustice and iudgement and equitie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:5; Proverbs 8:17; Psalms 119:9"> To giue vnto the simple, sharpenesse of wit, and to the childe knowledge and discretion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 9:9; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> A wise man shall heare and increase in learning, and a man of vnderstanding shall attayne vnto wise counsels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Mark 4:11; Psalms 49:4"> To vnderstand a parable, and the interpretation, the wordes of ye wise, and their darke sayings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 28:28; Psalms 111:10–1; Ecclesiastes 12:13"> The feare of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge: but fooles despise wisedome and instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:20; Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 5:1–2"> My sonne, heare thy fathers instruction, and forsake not thy mothers teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Proverbs 4:9; Genesis 41:42"> For they shalbe a comely ornament vnto thine head, and as chaines for thy necke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Proverbs 16:29; Psalms 1:1"> My sonne, if sinners doe intise thee, consent thou not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26; Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 12:6"> If they say, Come with vs, we will lay waite for blood, and lie priuilie for the innocent without a cause:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 124:3; Psalms 28:1; Romans 3:13"> We wil swallow them vp aliue like a graue euen whole, as those that goe downe into the pit:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 12:15; Job 24:2–3"> We shall finde all precious riches, and fill our houses with spoyle:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Cast in thy lot among vs: we will all haue one purse:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:101; Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 4:14–15"> My sonne, walke not thou in the way with them: refraine thy foote from their path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 6:18; Proverbs 4:16"> For their feete runne to euill, and make haste to shed blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Proverbs 7:23; Jeremiah 8:7"> Certainely as without cause the net is spred before the eyes of all that hath wing:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 9:16; Esther 7:10; Proverbs 5:22–23"> So they lay waite for blood and lie priuily for their liues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 15:27; 2 Peter 2:3; James 5:1–4"> Such are the wayes of euery one that is greedy of gaine: he would take away the life of the owners thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 9:3; Colossians 2:3; Luke 11:49"> Wisdome cryeth without: she vttereth her voyce in the streetes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 5:20; Proverbs 9:3; Matthew 13:2"> She calleth in the hye streete, among the prease in the entrings of the gates, and vttereth her wordes in the citie, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 1:29; Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 5:12"> O ye foolish, howe long will ye loue foolishnes? and the scornefull take their pleasure in scorning, and the fooles hate knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Ezekiel 33:11; Ezekiel 18:27–30"> (Turne you at my correction: loe, I will powre out my mind vnto you, and make you vnderstand my wordes)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; Romans 10:21; Jeremiah 7:13"> Because I haue called, and ye refused: I haue stretched out mine hand, and none woulde regarde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 81:11; Luke 7:30; Proverbs 1:30"> But ye haue despised all my counsell, and would none of my correction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Proverbs 10:24; Psalms 37:13"> I will also laugh at your destruction, and mocke, when your feare commeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 3:25–26; Luke 21:34–35; Psalms 58:9"> Whe your feare cometh like sudden desolation, and your destruction shall come like a whirle wind: whe affliction and anguish shall come vpon you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Micah 3:4; James 4:3; Jeremiah 11:11"> Then shall they call vpon me, but I will not answere: they shall seeke me early, but they shall not finde me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Job 21:14–15; Proverbs 6:23"> Because they hated knowledge, and did not chuse the feare of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 81:11; Proverbs 1:25; Luke 14:18–20"> They would none of my counsell, but despised all my correction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 14:14; Jeremiah 6:19; Proverbs 22:8"> Therefore shall they eate of ye fruite of their owne way, and be filled with their owne deuises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 8:36; Jeremiah 2:19; John 3:36"> For ease slaieth the foolish, and the prosperitie of fooles destroyeth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 25:12–13; Psalms 112:7–8; Isaiah 26:3"> But he that obeyeth me, shall dwell safely, and be quiet from feare of euill.</span>
