<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 12:6; James 3:5–6; Revelation 12:11"> In the mouth of the foolish is the rod of pride: but the lippes of the wise preserue them.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:5; Matthew 13:11–12; Matthew 11:25–27"> A scorner seeketh wisdome, and findeth it not: but knowledge is easie to him that will vnderstande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Ephesians 5:11; 1 Corinthians 5:11"> Depart from the foolish man, when thou perceiuest not in him the lippes of knowledge.</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:2; James 4:9; Ecclesiastes 2:10–11"> Euen in laughing the heart is sorowful, and the ende of that mirth is heauinesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 6:8; Proverbs 12:14; Hebrews 3:12"> The heart that declineth, shall be saciate with his owne wayes: but a good man shall depart from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 5:17; Proverbs 14:8; 1 John 4:1"> The foolish will beleeue euery thing: but the prudent will consider his steppes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 22:3; Proverbs 3:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> A wise man feareth, and departeth from euill: but a foole rageth, and is carelesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; James 1:19; Ecclesiastes 7:9"> He that is hastie to anger, committeth follie, and a busie body is hated.</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Proverbs 28:27; Proverbs 11:12"> The sinner despiseth his neighbour: but he that hath mercie on the poore, is blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 12:2; John 1:17; Psalms 61:7"> Doe not they erre that imagine euill? but to them that thinke on good things, shalbe mercie and trueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 28:19; Proverbs 12:24; 2 Thessalonians 3:10–12"> In all labour there is abundance: but the talke of the lippes bringeth onely want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:11–12; Luke 12:19–20; Luke 16:19–25"> The crowne of the wise is their riches, and the follie of fooles is foolishnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 14:5; Acts 26:16–20; 2 Peter 3:3"> A faithfull witnes deliuereth soules: but a deceiuer speaketh lyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 19:23; Jeremiah 32:39–40; Psalms 115:13–14"> In the feare of the Lord is an assured strength, and his children shall haue hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 21:6; Proverbs 13:14; Proverbs 2:10–18"> The feare of the Lord is as a welspring of life, to auoyde the snares of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 4:20–21; 2 Kings 13:7; Exodus 1:22"> In the multitude of the people is the honour of a King, and for the want of people commeth the destruction of the Prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 1:19; Ecclesiastes 7:9; Proverbs 14:17"> He that is slowe to wrath, is of great wisdome: but he that is of an hastie minde, exalteth follie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 17:22; Proverbs 4:23; Proverbs 3:8"> A sounde heart is the life of the flesh: but enuie is the rotting of the bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 22:2; Proverbs 17:5; Proverbs 22:16"> He that oppresseth the poore, reprooueth him that made him: but hee honoureth him, that hath mercie on the poore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:18; 2 Corinthians 5:8; Psalms 23:4"> The wicked shall be cast away for his malice: but the righteous hath hope in his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 12:16; Proverbs 13:16; Proverbs 12:23"> Wisedome resteth in the heart of him that hath vnderstanding, and is knowen in the mids of fooles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Deuteronomy 28:1–68; Deuteronomy 29:18–28"> Iustice exalteth a nation, but sinne is a shame to the people.</span>
