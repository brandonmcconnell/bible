<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Proverbs 28:16; Esther 4:6"> As the snowe in the sommer, and as the raine in the haruest are not meete, so is honour vnseemely for a foole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:8; Nehemiah 13:2; Psalms 109:28"> As the sparowe by flying, and the swallow by flying escape, so the curse that is causeles, shall not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 10:13; Psalms 32:9; 1 Corinthians 4:21"> Vnto the horse belongeth a whip, to the asse a bridle, and a rod to the fooles backe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 26:5; 1 Peter 3:9; Jude 1:9"> Answer not a foole according to his foolishnes, least thou also be like him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 12:16; Matthew 16:1–4; Proverbs 28:11"> Answere a foole according to his foolishnes, least he be wise in his owne conceite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 10:26; Proverbs 25:13; Proverbs 13:17"> He that sendeth a message by the hand of a foole, is as he that cutteth off the feete, and drinketh iniquitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 26:9; Psalms 64:8; Proverbs 17:7"> As they that lift vp the legs of the lame, so is a parable in a fooles mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:1; Proverbs 30:22; Proverbs 19:10"> As the closing vp of a precious stone in an heape of stones, so is he that giueth glory to a foole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 23:35"> As a thorne standing vp in the hand of a drunkard, so is a parable in the mouth of fooles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 2:6; Proverbs 11:31"> The excellent that formed all things, both rewardeth the foole and rewardeth the transgressers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:22; Matthew 12:45; Exodus 8:15"> As a dog turneth againe to his owne vomit, so a foole turneth to his foolishnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:20; 1 Corinthians 3:18–19; Proverbs 26:5"> Seest thou a man wise in his owne conceite? more hope is of a foole then of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 22:13; Proverbs 19:15; Proverbs 15:19"> The slouthfull man sayth, A lyon is in the way: a lyon is in the streetes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; Proverbs 12:27; Proverbs 12:24"> As the doore turneth vpon his hinges, so doeth the slouthfull man vpon his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 19:24"> The slouthfull hideth his hand in his bosome, and it grieueth him to put it againe to his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 26:12; 1 Peter 3:15; Proverbs 12:15"> The sluggard is wiser in his owne conceite, then seuen men that can render a reason.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:3; 2 Timothy 2:23–24; Luke 12:14"> He that passeth by and medleth with the strife that belongeth not vnto him, is as one that taketh a dog by the eares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 25:18; Isaiah 50:11; Proverbs 7:23"> As he that faineth himselfe mad, casteth fire brands, arrowes, and mortall things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 5:4; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 15:21"> So dealeth the deceitfull man with his friend and sayth, Am not I in sport?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 22:10; James 3:6; Proverbs 16:28"> Without wood the fire is quenched, and without a talebearer strife ceaseth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Proverbs 29:22; 2 Samuel 20:1"> As ye cole maketh burning coles, and wood a fire, so the contentious man is apt to kindle strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 18:8; Proverbs 20:19; Ezekiel 22:9"> The wordes of a tale bearer are as flatterings, and they goe downe into the bowels of the belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:47–48; Luke 11:39; Proverbs 10:18"> As siluer drosse ouerlayde vpon a potsheard, so are burning lips, and an euill heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:20; Proverbs 10:18; Psalms 41:6"> He that hateth, will counterfaite with his lips, but in his heart he layeth vp deceite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Jeremiah 12:6; Psalms 28:3"> Though he speake fauourably, beleeue him not: for there are seuen abominations in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 55:21–23; 1 Samuel 18:17; 1 Samuel 18:21"> Hatred may be couered by deceite: but the malice thereof shall be discouered in the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 28:10; Psalms 9:15; Psalms 7:15–16"> He that diggeth a pit shall fall therein, and he that rolleth a stone, it shall returne vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:44–49; Luke 20:20–21; John 10:32–33"> A false tongue hateth the afflicted, and a flattering mouth causeth ruine.</span>
