<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 15:10; Proverbs 9:7–8; Psalms 119:97–100"> He that loueth instruction, loueth knowledge: but he that hateth correction, is a foole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 8:35; Acts 11:24; Psalms 112:5"> A good man getteth fauour of the Lord: but the man of wicked immaginations will hee condemne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 10:25; Ephesians 3:17; Proverbs 12:12"> A man cannot be established by wickednesse: but the roote of the righteous shall not be mooued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 14:30; Proverbs 31:10–25; Proverbs 14:1"> A vertuous woman is the crowne of her husband: but she that maketh him ashamed, is as corruption in his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 139:23; Isaiah 55:7; Psalms 140:1–3"> The thoughtes of the iust are right: but the counsels of the wicked are deceitfull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 14:3; Jeremiah 5:26; Proverbs 1:11–19"> The talking of the wicked is to lye in waite for blood: but the mouth of the righteous will deliuer them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:24–27; Proverbs 11:21; Proverbs 10:25"> God ouerthroweth the wicked, and they are not: but the house of the righteous shall stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:18; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Corinthians 3:10–15"> A man shall be commended for his wisedome: but the froward of heart shalbe despised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 14:11; Proverbs 13:7"> He that is despised, and is his owne seruant, is better then he that boasteth himselfe and lacketh bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; Genesis 33:13–14; Numbers 22:28–32"> A righteous man regardeth the life of his beast: but the mercies of the wicked are cruell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 28:19; Proverbs 14:23; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12"> He that tilleth his lande, shalbe satisfied with bread: but he that followeth the idle, is destitute of vnderstanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; John 15:5; Luke 8:13–15"> The wicked desireth the net of euils: but the roote of the righteous giueth fruite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 11:8; Psalms 64:8"> The euill man is snared by the wickednesse of his lips, but the iust shall come out of aduersitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:20–21; Isaiah 3:10–11; Proverbs 13:2"> A man shalbe satiate with good things by the fruite of his mouth, and the recompence of a mans hands shall God giue vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 3:7; Proverbs 26:12; Proverbs 14:16"> The way of a foole is right in his owne eyes: but he that heareth counsell, is wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:11; James 1:19; Proverbs 10:12"> A foole in a day shall be knowen by his anger: but he that couereth shame, is wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 14:5; Proverbs 14:25; Proverbs 24:28"> He that speaketh trueth, will shewe righteousnes: but a false witnes vseth deceite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 15:4; Psalms 57:4"> There is that speaketh wordes like the prickings of a sworde: but the tongue of wise men is health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:9; Psalms 52:4–5; Job 20:5"> The lip of trueth shall be stable for euer: but a lying tongue varieth incontinently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; 1 Peter 3:8–13; Proverbs 12:12"> Deceite is in the heart of them that imagine euill: but to the counsellers of peace shall be ioye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 3:13; Psalms 91:10; Romans 8:28"> There shall none iniquitie come to the iust: but the wicked are full of euill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 5:6; Proverbs 11:20; Revelation 22:15"> The lying lips are an abomination to the Lord: but they that deale truely are his delite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 13:16; Proverbs 11:13; Proverbs 10:19"> A wise man concealeth knowledge: but the heart of the fooles publisheth foolishnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:27; Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 21:25–26"> The hand of the diligent shall beare rule: but the idle shalbe vnder tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 15:13; Proverbs 17:22"> Heauines in the heart of man doeth bring it downe: but a good worde reioyceth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 John 2:26; Matthew 5:46–48; Proverbs 17:27"> The righteous is more excellent then his neighbour: but the way of the wicked will deceiue them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 26:15; Proverbs 15:16"> The deceitfull man rosteth not, that hee tooke in hunting: but the riches of the diligent man are precious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="3 John 1:11; Romans 5:21; Ezekiel 18:20–24"> Life is in the way of righteousnesse, and in that path way there is no death.</span>
