<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:16–19; Acts 12:22–23; Jeremiah 28:2–4"> He that prayseth his friend with a loude voyce, rising earely in the morning, it shall be counted to him as a curse.</span>
