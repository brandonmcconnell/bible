<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:20–21; Matthew 3:3; John 7:37"> Doth not wisedome crie? and vnderstanding vtter her voyce?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 9:14; Proverbs 9:3"> She standeth in the top of the high places by the way in the place of the paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 18:20; Acts 5:20; Job 29:7"> She cryeth besides the gates before the citie at the entrie of the doores,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; John 3:16; Psalms 50:1"> O men, I call vnto you, and vtter my voyce to the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Acts 26:18; Revelation 3:17–18"> O ye foolish men, vnderstand wisedome, and ye, O fooles, be wise in heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 1:26; Matthew 7:28–29; Proverbs 23:16"> Giue eare, for I will speake of excellent things, and the opening of my lippes, shall teache things that be right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:30; John 8:14; John 17:17"> For my mouth shall speake the trueth, and my lippes abhorre wickednesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:46; Psalms 12:6; Proverbs 8:13"> All the wordes of my mouth are righteous: there is no lewdenes, nor frowardnesse in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; James 1:5; John 7:17"> They are all plaine to him that will vnderstande, and streight to them that woulde finde knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 2:4–5; Psalms 119:127; Psalms 119:72"> Receiue mine instruction, and not siluer, and knowledge rather then fine golde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14–15; Proverbs 4:5–7; Proverbs 16:16"> For wisdome is better then precious stones: and all pleasures are not to be compared vnto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 28:26; Exodus 35:30–4; Romans 11:33"> I wisdome dwell with prudence, and I find foorth knowledge and counsels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 97:10; Proverbs 16:6; 1 Samuel 2:3"> The feare of the Lord is to hate euill as pride, and arrogancie, and the euill way: and a mouth that speaketh lewde things, I doe hate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 40:14"> I haue counsell and wisedome: I am vnderstanding, and I haue strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 13:1; Daniel 2:21; Matthew 28:18"> By me, Kings reigne, and princes decree iustice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> By me princes rule and the nobles, and all the iudges of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 14:23; John 14:21; Psalms 91:14"> I loue them that loue me: and they that seeke me earely, shall finde me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:16; Matthew 6:33; Luke 16:11–12"> Riches and honour are with me: euen durable riches and righteousnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14; Proverbs 10:20; Ecclesiastes 7:12"> My fruite is better then golde, euen then fine golde, and my reuenues better then fine siluer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; John 10:27–28; Psalms 32:8"> I cause to walke in the way of righteousnes, and in the middes of the paths of iudgement,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; Proverbs 24:4; Matthew 25:46"> That I may cause them that loue me, to inherite substance, and I will fill their treasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 1:17; John 1:1–2; Proverbs 3:19"> The Lord hath possessed me in the beginning of his way: I was before his workes of olde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 17:24; Psalms 2:6; Genesis 1:26"> I was set vp from euerlasting, from the beginning and before the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:20; Genesis 1:2; 1 John 4:9"> When there were no depths, was I begotten, when there were no fountaines abounding with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Psalms 102:25–28; Job 38:4–11"> Before the mountaines were setled: and before the hilles, was I begotten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 1:1–31"> He had not yet made the earth, nor the open places, nor the height of the dust in the worlde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Job 26:10; Proverbs 3:19"> When hee prepared the heauens, I was there, when he set the compasse vpon the deepe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When he established the cloudes aboue, when he confirmed the fountaines of the deepe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 104:9; Jeremiah 5:22; Genesis 1:9–10"> When he gaue his decree to the Sea, that the waters shoulde not passe his commandement: when he appointed the foundations of the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:1–3; John 1:18; Isaiah 42:1"> Then was I with him as a nourisher, and I was dayly his delight reioycing alway before him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 16:3; John 13:1; Psalms 40:6–8"> And tooke my solace in the compasse of his earth: and my delite is with the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Psalms 119:1–2; Psalms 1:1–4"> Therefore nowe hearken, O children, vnto me: for blessed are they that keepe my wayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1; Psalms 81:11–12; Proverbs 5:1"> Heare instruction, and be ye wise, and refuse it not: blessed is the man that heareth mee, watching dayly at my gates, and giuing attendance at the postes of my doores.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Matthew 7:24; Proverbs 3:13"> For he that findeth me, findeth life, and shall obteine fauour of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12; Proverbs 12:2"> But he that sinneth against me, hurteth his owne soule: and all that hate me, loue death.</span>
