<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jude 1:12; Genesis 43:32–34"> When thou sittest to eate with a ruler, consider diligently what is before thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Matthew 18:8–9; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> And put the knife to thy throte, if thou be a man giuen to the appetite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; Psalms 141:4; Ephesians 4:22"> Be not desirous of his deintie meates: for it is a deceiuable meate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; Proverbs 28:20; Matthew 6:19"> Trauaile not too much to be rich: but cease from thy wisdome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 27:24; 1 John 2:16; James 5:1–2"> Wilt thou cast thine eyes vpon it, which is nothing? for riches taketh her to her wings, as an eagle, and flyeth into the heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 141:4; Proverbs 23:3; Daniel 1:8–10"> Eate thou not the bread of him that hath an euil eye, neither desire his deintie meates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; Psalms 55:21; Judges 16:15"> For as though he thought it in his heart, so will hee say vnto thee, Eate and drinke: but his heart is not with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Thou shalt vomit thy morsels that thou hast eaten, and thou shalt lose thy sweete wordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 9:7–8; Matthew 7:6; Proverbs 26:4–5"> Speake not in the eares of a foole: for hee will despise the wisdome of thy wordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 22:28; Zechariah 7:10; Deuteronomy 27:17"> Remooue not the ancient boundes, and enter not into the fieldes of the fatherlesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 22:23; Job 19:25; Jeremiah 51:36"> For he that redeemeth them, is mightie: he will defend their cause against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 5:1–2; Proverbs 2:2–6; Proverbs 23:19"> Apply thine heart to instruction, and thine eares to the wordes of knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 19:18; Proverbs 29:15"> Withhold not correction from the childe: if thou smite him with the rodde, he shall not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:5; Proverbs 22:15; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> Thou shalt smite him with the rodde, and shalt deliuer his soule from hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:24–25; 2 John 1:4; Proverbs 29:3"> My sonne, if thine heart be wise, mine heart shall reioyce, and I also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6; Ephesians 5:4; James 3:2"> And my reynes shall reioyce, when thy lips speake righteous things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Peter 1:17"> Let not thine heart bee enuious against sinners: but let it bee in the feare of the Lord continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Jeremiah 29:11; Psalms 9:18"> For surely there is an ende, and thy hope shall not be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 23:26; Proverbs 23:12; Proverbs 4:10–23"> O thou my sonne, heare, and bee wise, and guide thine heart in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 21:34; Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:22"> Keepe not company with drunkards, nor with gluttons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 21:17; Proverbs 19:15; Deuteronomy 21:20"> For the drunkard and the glutton shall bee poore, and the sleeper shalbe clothed with ragges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 1:8; John 19:26–27"> Obey thy father that hath begotten thee, and despise not thy mother when she is olde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 18:15; Matthew 13:44; Psalms 119:72"> Bye the trueth, but sell it not: likewise wisdome, and instruction, and vnderstanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 15:20; Proverbs 23:15–16"> The father of the righteous shall greatly reioyce, and hee that begetteth a wise childe, shall haue ioy of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 17:25; Luke 11:27–28; Luke 1:58"> Thy father and thy mother shall be glad, and she that bare thee shall reioyce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:2; Psalms 119:9–11; Deuteronomy 6:5"> My sonne, giue mee thine heart, and let thine eyes delite in my wayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14"> For a whore is as a deepe ditche, and a strange woman is as a narrowe pitte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 7:12; Proverbs 22:14"> Also she lyeth in wait as for a praye, and she increaseth the transgressers among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:11; Proverbs 23:21"> To whome is woe? to whome is sorowe? to whom is strife? to whom is murmuring? to whom are woundes without cause? and to whome is the rednesse of the eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:11; Proverbs 20:1"> Euen to them that tarie long at the wine, to them that goe, and seeke mixt wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 2:16; 2 Samuel 11:2; Psalms 119:37"> Looke not thou vpon the wine, when it is red, and when it sheweth his colour in the cup, or goeth downe pleasantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:8; Isaiah 28:7–8; Amos 9:3"> In the ende thereof it will bite like a serpent, and hurt like a cockatrise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jude 1:12–13; Psalms 69:12; Daniel 5:4"> Thine eyes shall looke vpon strange women, and thine heart shall speake lewde things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–7; 1 Kings 20:16–22; 1 Samuel 30:16–17"> And thou shalt bee as one that sleepeth in the middes of the sea, and as hee that sleepeth in the toppe of the maste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 56:12; Jeremiah 5:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> They haue stricken mee, shalt thou say, but I was not sicke: they haue beaten mee, but I knew not, when I awoke: therefore will I seeke it yet still.</span>
