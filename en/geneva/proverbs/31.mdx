<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 6:20; Proverbs 1:8; 2 Timothy 1:5"> THE WORDS OF KING LEMUEL: The prophecie which his mother taught him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; 1 Samuel 1:11; 1 Samuel 1:28"> What my sonne! and what ye sonne of my wombe! and what, O sonne of my desires!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:17; Nehemiah 13:26; Proverbs 7:26–27"> Giue not thy strength vnto women, nor thy wayes, which is to destroy Kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Ecclesiastes 10:17; Isaiah 5:22"> It is not for Kings, O Lemuel, it is not for Kings to drink wine nor for princes strog drinke,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:5"> Lest he drinke and forget the decree, and change the iudgement of all the children of affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:23; Psalms 104:15; 1 Samuel 1:10"> Giue ye strong drinke vnto him that is readie to perish, and wine vnto them that haue griefe of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18"> Let him drinke, that he may forget his pouertie, and remember his miserie no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 82:3–4; Proverbs 24:11–12; Esther 4:13–16"> Open thy mouth for the domme in the cause of all the children of destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Leviticus 19:15; Amos 5:11–12"> Open thy mouth: iudge righteously, and iudge the afflicted, and the poore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:14; Proverbs 12:4; Proverbs 18:22"> Who shall finde a vertuous woman? for her price is farre aboue the pearles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 3:1–7; 2 Kings 4:22–23; 2 Kings 4:9–10"> The heart of her husband trusteth in her, and he shall haue no neede of spoyle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18–22; 1 Samuel 25:26–27"> She will doe him good, and not euill all the dayes of her life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Timothy 5:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:10–12"> She seeketh wooll and flaxe, and laboureth cheerefully with her handes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 31:24; 1 Kings 9:26–28; Ezekiel 27:3–36"> She is like the shippes of marchants: shee bringeth her foode from afarre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:11; Mark 1:35; Luke 12:42"> And she riseth, whiles it is yet night: and giueth the portion to her houshold, and the ordinarie to her maides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Song of Solomon 8:12; Joshua 15:18"> She considereth a field, and getteth it: and with the fruite of her handes she planteth a vineyarde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 38:3; 1 Peter 1:13; Ephesians 6:10"> She girdeth her loynes with strength, and strengtheneth her armes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; Matthew 25:3–10; Psalms 127:2"> She feeleth that her marchandise is good: her candle is not put out by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:25–26"> She putteth her handes to the wherue, and her handes handle the spindle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 4:28; Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 22:9"> She stretcheth out her hand to the poore, and putteth foorth her hands to the needie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:22; 2 Samuel 1:24; Proverbs 25:20"> She feareth not the snowe for her familie: for all her familie is clothed with skarlet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Proverbs 7:16; Genesis 41:42"> She maketh her selfe carpets: fine linen and purple is her garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 12:4; Ruth 4:1; Deuteronomy 16:18"> Her husband is knowen in the gates, when he sitteth with the Elders of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:16; Luke 16:19; Proverbs 31:19"> She maketh sheetes, and selleth them, and giueth girdels vnto the marchant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 40:10; Romans 13:14; Job 29:14"> Strength and honour is her clothing, and in the latter day she shall reioyce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 10:31"> She openeth her mouth with wisdome, and the lawe of grace is in her tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; Titus 2:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> She ouerseeth the wayes of her housholde, and eateth not the bread of ydlenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:15–17; 2 Timothy 1:5; 1 Kings 2:19"> Her children rise vp, and call her blessed: her husband also shall prayse her, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Song of Solomon 6:8–9; Ephesians 5:27"> Many daughters haue done vertuously: but thou surmountest them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 1:7; 1 Peter 3:4–5; Proverbs 11:22"> Fauour is deceitfull, and beautie is vanitie: but a woman that feareth the Lord, she shall be praysed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Hebrews 6:10; Matthew 7:16"> Giue her of the fruite of her hands, and let her owne workes prayse her in the gates.</span>
