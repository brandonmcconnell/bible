<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 15:5; Proverbs 9:7–8; Proverbs 15:20"> A wise sonne will obey the instruction of his father: but a scorner will heare no rebuke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 12:14; Proverbs 18:20; Proverbs 10:11"> A man shall eate good things by the fruite of his mouth: but the soule of the trespassers shall suffer violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 18:21; Proverbs 18:7"> Hee that keepeth his mouth, keepeth his life: but he that openeth his lips, destruction shall be to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:24; Proverbs 12:11; 2 Peter 1:5–11"> The sluggard lusteth, but his soule hath nought: but the soule of the diligent shall haue plentie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Ephesians 4:25; Psalms 119:163"> A righteous man hateth lying wordes: but the wicked causeth slander and shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:3; Proverbs 11:5–6; Psalms 140:11"> Righteousnesse preserueth the vpright of life: but wickednes ouerthroweth the sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 18:11–14; Revelation 3:17; Proverbs 11:24"> There is that maketh himselfe riche, and hath nothing, and that maketh himselfe poore, hauing great riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Psalms 49:6–10"> A man will giue his riches for the ransome of his life: but the poore cannot heare ye reproch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 4:18; Proverbs 24:20; Proverbs 20:20"> The light of the righteous reioyceth: but the candle of the wicked shall be put out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:20; Proverbs 12:15–16; Proverbs 17:14"> Onely by pride doeth man make contention: but with the well aduised is wisdome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:21; Proverbs 10:2; James 5:1–5"> The riches of vanitie shall diminish: but he that gathereth with the hand, shall increase them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 16:22; Proverbs 13:19; Psalms 119:81–83"> The hope that is deferred, is the fainting of the heart: but when the desire commeth, it is as a tree of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 15:31; 2 Chronicles 36:16; 2 John 1:8"> He that despiseth the worde, hee shall be destroyed: but hee that feareth the commandement he shalbe rewarded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 18:5; Proverbs 10:11; Proverbs 14:27"> The instruction of a wise man is as the welspring of life, to turne away from the snares of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:19; Proverbs 4:19; Acts 7:10"> Good vnderstanding maketh acceptable: but the way of the disobedient is hated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 15:2; Proverbs 12:22–23; Ephesians 5:17"> Euery wise man will worke by knowledge: but a foole will spread abroade folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 25:13; 1 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> A wicked messenger falleth into euill: but a faithfull ambassadour is preseruation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:5; Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 15:31–32"> Pouertie and shame is to him that refuseth instruction: but hee that regardeth correction, shalbe honoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 7:14–17; Proverbs 13:12; Proverbs 29:27"> A desire accomplished deliteth ye soule: but it is an abomination to fooles to depart from euil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33–34; Proverbs 15:31; Proverbs 9:6"> He that walketh with the wise, shalbe wise: but a companion of fooles shalbe afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 32:10; Isaiah 3:10–11; Romans 2:7–10"> Affliction followeth sinners: but vnto the righteous God will recompense good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:26; Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 28:8"> The good man shall giue inheritance vnto his childrens children: and the riches of the sinner is layde vp for the iust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Proverbs 28:19; Ecclesiastes 5:9"> Much foode is in the fielde of the poore: but the fielde is destroyed without discretion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 29:17; Proverbs 23:13–14; Proverbs 29:15"> He that spareth his rodde, hateth his sonne: but he that loueth him, chasteneth him betime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 34:10; Proverbs 10:3; Hebrews 13:5"> The righteous eateth to the contentation of his minde: but the belly of the wicked shall want.</span>
