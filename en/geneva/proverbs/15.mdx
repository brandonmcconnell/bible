<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 10:12; Proverbs 25:15; Proverbs 15:18"> A soft answere putteth away wrath: but grieuous wordes stirre vp anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 12:23; Proverbs 15:28; Proverbs 13:16"> The tongue of the wise vseth knowledge aright: but the mouth of fooles babbleth out foolishnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Jeremiah 16:17; Jeremiah 23:24"> The eyes of the Lord in euery place beholde the euill and the good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 18:8"> A wholesome tongue is as a tree of life: but the frowardnes therof is the breaking of ye minde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:31–32; Proverbs 13:1; Proverbs 13:18"> A foole despiseth his fathers instruction: but he that regardeth correction, is prudent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 8:21; Proverbs 21:20; Proverbs 10:22"> The house of the righteous hath much treasure: but in the reuenues of the wicked is trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; James 3:6; Ephesians 4:29"> The lippes of the wise doe spread abroade knowledge: but ye heart of the foolish doth not so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Proverbs 21:27; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> The sacrifice of the wicked is abomination to the Lord: but the prayer of the righteous is acceptable vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:11; Proverbs 21:21; Matthew 7:13"> The way of the wicked is an abomination vnto the Lord: but he loueth him that followeth righteousnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; John 3:20; Proverbs 13:1"> Instruction is euill to him that forsaketh the way, and he that hateth correction, shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:21; Hebrews 4:13; Psalms 139:8"> Hell and destruction are before the Lord: how much more the hearts of the sonnes of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Job 21:14; Proverbs 9:7–8"> A scorner loueth not him that rebuketh him, neither will he goe vnto the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 17:22; John 14:1; Proverbs 18:14"> A ioyfull heart maketh a chearefull countenance: but by the sorow of the heart the minde is heauie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 9:9; Isaiah 30:10; Proverbs 18:15"> The heart of him that hath vnderstanding, seeketh knowledge: but the mouth of the foole is fedde with foolishnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:10; Romans 12:12; Proverbs 15:13"> All the dayes of the afflicted are euill: but a good conscience is a continuall feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Psalms 37:16; Ecclesiastes 5:10–12"> Better is a litle with the feare of the Lord, then great treasure, and trouble therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 17:1; Proverbs 21:19; 1 John 4:16"> Better is a dinner of greene herbes where loue is, then a stalled oxe and hatred therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Proverbs 26:21; Proverbs 29:22"> An angrie man stirreth vp strife: but hee that is slowe to wrath, appeaseth strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 35:8; Proverbs 22:5; Isaiah 30:21"> The way of a slouthfull man is as an hedge of thornes: but the way of the righteous is plaine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 23:15–16"> A wise sonne reioyceth the father: but a foolish man despiseth his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 5:15; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 14:16"> Foolishnes is ioy to him that is destitute of vnderstanding: but a man of vnderstanding walketh vprightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Proverbs 20:18; Ecclesiastes 8:6"> Without cousel thoughts come to nought: but in the multitude of counsellers there is stedfastnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 50:4; Proverbs 25:11–12; Ephesians 4:29"> A ioy commeth to a man by the answere of his mouth: and how good is a word in due season?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–2; Psalms 16:11; Matthew 7:14"> The way of life is on high to the prudent, to auoyde from hell beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 146:9; Psalms 68:5–6; Proverbs 14:11"> The Lord will destroye the house of the proude men: but hee will stablish the borders of the widowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Jeremiah 4:14; Proverbs 15:23"> The thoughts of ye wicked are abomination to the Lord: but the pure haue pleasant wordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 23:8; Proverbs 1:19; Jeremiah 17:11"> He that is greedie of gaine, troubleth his owne house: but he that hateth giftes, shall liue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15; Proverbs 29:11; Ecclesiastes 5:2"> The heart of the righteous studieth to answere: but the wicked mans mouth babbleth euil thinges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 9:31; Psalms 145:18–19; Proverbs 15:8"> The Lord is farre off from the wicked: but he heareth the prayer of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 21:23"> The light of the eyes reioyceth the heart, and a good name maketh the bones fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:5; Proverbs 25:12; John 15:3–4"> The eare that hearkeneth to the correction of life, shall lodge among the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–33; Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 1:7"> Hee that refuseth instruction, despiseth his owne soule: but he that obeyeth correction, getteth vnderstanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Proverbs 18:12; Proverbs 1:7"> The feare of the Lord is the instruction of wisdome: and before honour, goeth humilitie.</span>
