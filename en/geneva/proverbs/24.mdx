<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 3:31"> Be not thou enuious against euill men, neither desire to be with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 7:14; Psalms 28:3"> For their heart imagineth destruction, and their lippes speake mischiefe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; 1 Corinthians 3:9; Proverbs 9:1"> Through wisdome is an house builded, and with vnderstanding it is established.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 15:6; Proverbs 21:20; Proverbs 20:15"> And by knowledge shall the chambers bee filled with all precious, and pleasant riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Colossians 1:11; Proverbs 21:22"> A wise man is strong: for a man of vnderstanding encreaseth his strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 15:22"> For with counsel thou shalt enterprise thy warre, and in the multitude of them that can giue counsell, is health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; Psalms 10:5; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> Wisdome is hie to a foole: therefore he can not open his mouth in the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 21:11; Ezekiel 38:10–11; Proverbs 14:22"> Hee that imagineth to doe euill, men shall call him an autour of wickednes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; 2 Corinthians 10:5; Isaiah 55:7"> The wicked thought of a foole is sinne, and the scorner is an abomination vnto men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:28–31; Ephesians 3:13; Jeremiah 51:46"> If thou bee faint in the day of aduersitie, thy strength is small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 82:4; Isaiah 58:6–7; 1 John 3:16–17"> Deliuer them that are drawen to death: wilt thou not preserue them that are led to be slaine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 34:11; Revelation 2:23; Jeremiah 17:10"> If thou say, Beholde, we knew not of it: he that pondereth the heartes, doeth not hee vnderstand it? and hee that keepeth thy soule, knoweth he it not? will not he also recompense euery man according to his workes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15; Song of Solomon 5:1; Proverbs 25:16"> My sonne, eate hony, for it is good, and the hony combe, for it is sweete vnto thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:25; Jeremiah 15:16; Proverbs 2:10"> So shall the knowledge of wisdome be vnto thy soule, if thou finde it, and there shall be an ende, and thine hope shall not be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:24; Isaiah 32:18; 1 Samuel 23:20–23"> Laye no waite, O wicked man, against the house of the righteous, and spoyle not his resting place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Psalms 37:24; Micah 7:8–10"> For a iust man falleth seuen times, and riseth againe: but the wicked fall into mischiefe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Obadiah 1:12; Job 31:29; Proverbs 17:5"> Bee thou not glad when thine enemie falleth, and let not thine heart reioyce when hee stumbleth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 1:15–16; Lamentations 4:21–22"> Least the Lord see it, and it displease him, and he turne his wrath from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 119:115; Psalms 37:1; Proverbs 23:17"> Fret not thy selfe because of the malicious, neither be enuious at the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Job 18:5–6; Psalms 11:6"> For there shall bee none ende of plagues to the euill man: the light of the wicked shall bee put out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:1–7; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Titus 3:1"> My sonne feare the Lord, and the King, and meddle not with them that are sedicious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 90:11; Hosea 5:11; Numbers 16:31–35"> For their destruction shall rise suddenly, and who knoweth the ruine of them both?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 28:21; Proverbs 18:5; John 7:24"> ALSO THESE THINGS PERTEINE TO THE WISE, It is not good to haue respect of any person in iudgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; Isaiah 5:23; Proverbs 11:26"> He that saith to the wicked, Thou art righteous, him shall the people curse, and the multitude shall abhorre him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:20; 1 Samuel 3:13; Proverbs 28:23"> But to them that rebuke him, shall be pleasure, and vpon them shall come the blessing of goodnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Genesis 41:38–57; Proverbs 25:11–12"> They shall kisse the lippes of him that answereth vpright wordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 14:28–30; 1 Kings 5:17–18; 1 Kings 6:7"> Prepare thy worke without, and make readie thy thinges in the fielde, and after, builde thine house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Matthew 26:59–60; Proverbs 25:18"> Be not a witnes against thy neighbour without cause: for wilt thou deceiue with thy lippes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; Romans 12:17–21; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Say not, I wil doe to him, as he hath done to mee, I will recompence euery man according to his worke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Proverbs 6:6–19; Job 4:8"> I passed by the fielde of the slouthfull, and by the vineyarde of the man destitute of vnderstanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:18; Isaiah 5:5; Jeremiah 4:3"> And lo, it was al growen ouer with thornes, and nettles had couered the face thereof, and the stone wall thereof was broken downe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; Deuteronomy 13:11; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> Then I behelde, and I considered it well: I looked vpon it, and receiued instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6–8; Proverbs 6:4–11; Romans 13:11"> Yet a litle sleepe, a litle slumber, a litle folding of the handes to sleepe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4"> So thy pouertie commeth as one that traueileth by the way, and thy necessitie like an armed man.</span>
