<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:1; Ephesians 3:1; Philemon 1:9"> Paul a prisoner of Iesus Christ, and our brother Timotheus, vnto Philemon our deare friende, and fellowe helper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Colossians 4:17; Romans 16:5"> And to our deare sister Apphia, and to Archippus our fellowe souldier, and to the Church that is in thine house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Ephesians 1:2; 2 Corinthians 13:14"> Grace be with you, and peace from God our Father, and from the Lord Iesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:8; Ephesians 1:16; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> I giue thanks to my God, making mention alwaies of thee in my praiers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 1:4; Ephesians 1:15; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> (When I heare of thy loue and faith, which thou hast toward the Lord Iesus, and towarde all Saintes)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 1:9–11; 2 Peter 1:8; Colossians 1:9"> That the fellowship of thy faith may bee made effectuall, and that whatsoeuer good thing is in you through Christ Iesus, may be knowen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:9; 2 Corinthians 7:4"> For we haue great ioy and consolation in thy loue, because by thee, brother, the Saintes bowels are comforted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Corinthians 10:8; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> Wherefore, though I bee very bolde in Christ to commaund thee that which is conuenient,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; Ephesians 4:1; Romans 12:1"> Yet for loues sake I rather beseeche thee, though I be as I am, euen Paul aged, and euen nowe a prisoner for Iesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 4:9; 1 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Timothy 1:2"> I beseeche thee for my sonne Onesimus, whome I haue begotten in my bondes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:11; Job 30:1–2; 1 Peter 2:10"> Which in times past was to thee vnprofitable, but nowe profitable both to thee and to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; Jeremiah 31:20; Mark 11:25"> Whome I haue sent againe: thou therefore receiue him, that is mine owne bowels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Ephesians 3:1; 1 Corinthians 16:17"> Whom I woulde haue reteined with mee, that in thy steade he might haue ministred vnto me in the bondes of the Gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Chronicles 29:17; 1 Peter 5:2–3"> But without thy minde woulde I doe nothing, that thy benefite should not be as it were of necessitie, but willingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Genesis 45:5–8; Isaiah 20:6"> It may be that he therefore departed for a season, that thou shouldest receiue him for euer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Matthew 23:8; 1 Corinthians 7:22"> Not now as a seruant, but aboue a seruant, euen as a brother beloued, specially to me: howe much more then vnto thee, both in the flesh and in the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:23; Matthew 10:40; Philemon 1:12"> If therefore thou count our thinges common, receiue him as my selfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–7"> If he hath hurt thee, or oweth thee ought, that put on mine accounts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:11; Galatians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 4:15"> I Paul haue written this with mine owne hande: I will recompense it, albeit I doe not say to thee, that thou owest moreouer vnto me euen thine owne selfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="3 John 1:4; Philemon 1:7; Philemon 1:12"> Yea, brother, let mee obteine this pleasure of thee in the Lord: comfort my bowels in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:4; 2 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 7:16"> Trusting in thine obedience, I wrote vnto thee, knowing that thou wilt do eue more then I say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:11; Philippians 2:24; 2 John 1:12"> Moreouer also prepare mee lodging: for I trust through your prayers I shall be freely giuen vnto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:7; Romans 16:7; Colossians 4:12"> There salute thee Epaphras my felowe prisoner in Christ Iesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10–11; Colossians 4:14; Colossians 4:10"> Marcus, Aristarchus, Demas and Luke, my felowe helpers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:22; Romans 16:23; Romans 16:20"> The grace of our Lord Iesus Christ be with your spirit, Amen. ‘Written from Rome to Philemon, and send by Onesimus a seruant.’</span>
