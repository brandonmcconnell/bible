<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 14:18–20; Psalms 57:2; Hebrews 6:20"> For this Melchi-sedec was King of Salem, the Priest of the most high God, who met Abraham, as he returned from the slaughter of the Kings, and blessed him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 18:21; Psalms 85:10–11; 1 Samuel 8:17"> To whom also Abraham gaue the tithe of all things: who first is by interpretation King of righteousnes: after that, he is also King of Salem, that is, King of peace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 7:23–28; Exodus 6:20–27"> Without father, without mother, without kinred, and hath neither beginning of his dayes, neither ende of life: but is likened vnto the Sonne of God, and continueth a Priest for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 14:20; Acts 2:29; Acts 7:8–9"> Nowe consider how great this man was, vnto whome euen the Patriarke Abraham gaue the tithe of the spoyles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 27:30–33; 2 Chronicles 31:4–6; Numbers 18:21–32"> For verely they which are the childre of Leui, which receiue the office of the Priesthode, haue a commandement to take, according to the Law, tithes of the people (that is, of their bethren) though they came out of ye loynes of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 4:13; Hebrews 7:3–4; Genesis 13:14–17"> But he whose kindred is not couted among them, receiued tithes of Abraham, and blessed him that had the promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:27; Genesis 48:15–20; 1 Timothy 3:16"> And without all contradiction the lesse is blessed of the greater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 6:20; Hebrews 5:6; Revelation 1:18"> And here men that die, receiue tithes: but there he receiueth them, of whome it is witnessed, that he liueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 5:12; Genesis 14:20; Hebrews 7:4"> And to say as the thing is, Leui also which receiueth tithes, payed tithes in Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Hebrews 7:5; Genesis 46:26"> For hee was yet in the loynes of his father Abraham, when Melchi-sedec met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 8:7; Galatians 2:21; Galatians 4:9"> If therefore perfection had bene by the Priesthoode of the Leuites (for vnder it the Lawe was established to the people) what needed it furthermore, that another Priest should rise after the order of Melchi-sedec, and not to be called after the order of Aaron?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Isaiah 66:21; Ezekiel 16:61"> For if the Priesthood be changed, then of necessitie must there be a change of the Lawe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11; Hebrews 7:14; Numbers 17:5"> For hee of whome these things are spoken, perteineth vnto another tribe, whereof no man serued at the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Isaiah 11:1; Luke 3:33"> For it is euident, that our Lord sprung out of Iuda, concerning the which tribe Moses spake nothing, touching the Priesthood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17–21; Hebrews 7:3"> And it is yet a more euident thing, because that after the similitude of Melchi-sedec, there is risen vp another Priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; Colossians 2:20; Hebrews 7:28"> Which is not made Priest after the Law of the carnal commandement, but after the power of the endlesse life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 5:6; Hebrews 7:21"> For hee testifieth thus, Thou art a Priest for euer, after the order of Melchi-sedec.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:3; Acts 13:39; Galatians 4:9"> For the commandement that went afore, is disanulled, because of the weakenes thereof, and vnprofitablenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 13:39; Hebrews 4:16; Hebrews 9:9"> For the Law made nothing perfite, but the bringing in of a better hope made perfite, whereby we drawe neere vnto God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 18:7; Hebrews 5:6; Psalms 110:4"> And for as much as it is not without an othe (for these are made Priestes without an othe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 6:16–18"> But this is made with an othe by him that said vnto him, The Lord hath sworne, and will not repent, Thou art a Priest for euer, after the order of Melchi-sedec)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:25; Genesis 44:32; Hebrews 8:6–12"> By so much is Iesus made a suretie of a better Testament.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:10–11; 1 Chronicles 6:3–14; Hebrews 7:8"> And among them many were made Priests, because they were not suffered to endure, by the reason of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 12:34; Hebrews 7:28; Isaiah 9:6–7"> But this man, because hee endureth euer, hath a Priesthood, which cannot passe from one to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 8:34; 1 John 2:1–2; 1 Timothy 2:5"> Wherefore, hee is able also perfectly to saue them that come vnto God by him, seeing he euer liueth, to make intercession for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 4:14–15; 1 Peter 2:22"> For such an hie Priest it became vs to haue, which is holy, harmelesse, vndefiled, separate from sinners, and made hier then the heauens:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:14"> Which needeth not daily as those hie Priests to offer vp sacrifice, first for his owne sinnes, and then for the peoples: for that did he once, when he offered vp himselfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 7:21; Hebrews 1:2"> For the Law maketh men hie Priestes, which haue infirmitie: but the word of the othe that was since the Lawe, maketh the Sonne, who is consecrated for euermore.</span>
