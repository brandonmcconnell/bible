<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Philippians 3:14; Hebrews 4:14–15"> Therefore, holy brethren, partakers of the heauenly vocation, consider the Apostle and high Priest of our profession Christ Iesus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 12:7; 1 Timothy 3:15; Ephesians 2:22"> Who was faithfull to him that hath appointed him, euen as Moses was in al his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9; 1 Peter 2:5–7; Zechariah 6:12–13"> For this man is counted worthy of more glory then Moses, inasmuch as he which hath builded the house, hath more honour then the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 3:3; Hebrews 1:2; Esther 2:10"> For euery house is builded of some man, and he that hath built all things, is God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 12:7; Hebrews 3:2; Exodus 14:31"> Now Moses verely was faithfull in all his house, as a seruant, for a witnesse of the thinges which should be spoken after.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Hebrews 10:35"> But Christ is as the Sonne, ouer his owne house, whose house we are, if we holde fast that confidence and that reioycing of that hope vnto the ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:7–11; Hebrews 3:15; Isaiah 55:3"> Wherefore, as the holy Ghost sayth, To day if ye shall heare his voyce,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; Exodus 17:7; Zechariah 7:11–12"> Harden not your hearts, as in the prouocation, according to the day of the tentation in the wildernes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Deuteronomy 4:9; Exodus 19:4"> Where your fathers tempted me, prooued me, and sawe my workes fourtie yeeres long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 95:10; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Psalms 67:2"> Wherefore I was grieued with that generation, and sayde, They erre euer in their heart, neither haue they knowen my wayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:34–35; Hebrews 4:3; Numbers 32:10–13"> Therefore I sware in my wrath, If they shall enter into my rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 17:9"> Take heede, brethren, least at any time there be in any of you an euill heart, and vnfaithfull, to depart away from the liuing God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Hebrews 10:24–25; 2 Timothy 4:2"> But exhort one another dayly, while it is called to day, lest any of you be hardened through the deceitfulnes of sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 3:6; Hebrews 6:11; Hebrews 12:10"> For we are made partakers of Christ, if we keepe sure vnto the ende that beginning, wherewith we are vpholden,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7–8; Psalms 95:7; Hebrews 10:38"> So long as it is sayd, To day if ye heare his voyce, harden not your hearts, as in the prouocation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 14:30; Numbers 26:65"> For some when they heard, prouoked him to anger: howbeit, not all that came out of Egypt by Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:29; Numbers 26:64–65; Numbers 14:32–33"> But with whome was he displeased fourtie yeeres? Was hee not displeased with them that sinned, whose carkeises fell in the wildernes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Hebrews 4:6; Deuteronomy 1:34–35"> And to whom sware he that they should not enter into his rest, but vnto them that obeyed not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jude 1:5; John 3:36; Mark 16:16"> So we see that they could not enter in, because of vnbeliefe.</span>
