<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:8; Joshua 1:5; Deuteronomy 31:6"> Let your conuersation be without couetousnesse, and be content with those things that ye haue, for he hath said,</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Hebrews 13:17; Philippians 3:17"> So that we may boldly say, The Lord is mine helper, neither will I feare what man can doe vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Malachi 3:6; James 1:17; Revelation 1:8"> Remember them which haue the ouersight of you, which haue declared vnto you the word of God: whose faith follow, considering what hath bene the ende of their conuersation. Iesus Christ yesterday, and to day, the same also is for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:14; 1 Timothy 6:3–5; Ephesians 5:6"> Be not caried about with diuers and strange doctrines: for it is a good thing that the heart be stablished with grace, and not with meates, which haue not profited them that haue bene occupied therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:7–8; 1 Corinthians 10:20; 1 Corinthians 9:13"> We haue an altar, whereof they haue no authoritie to eate, which serue in the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 16:27; Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 9:11"> For the bodies of those beastes whose blood is brought into the Holy place by the high Priest for sinne, are burnt without the campe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12–14; John 19:17–18; Acts 7:58"> Therefore euen Iesus, that he might sanctifie the people with his owne blood, suffered without the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 11:26; 1 Peter 4:14–16; Acts 5:41"> Let vs goe foorth to him therefore out of the campe, bearing his reproch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; Colossians 3:1–3; Hebrews 11:12–16"> For here haue we no continuing citie: but we seeke one to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 107:21–22; Psalms 50:14; Romans 12:1"> Let vs therefore by him offer the sacrifice of prayse alwaies to God, that is, the fruite of the lippes, which confesse his Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:13; Galatians 6:10; Romans 12:13"> To doe good, and to distribute forget not: for with such sacrifices God is pleased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; 1 Timothy 5:17; Hebrews 13:7"> Obey them that haue the ouersight of you, and submit your selues: for they watche for your soules, as they that must giue accountes, that they may doe it with ioy, and not with griefe: for that is vnprofitable for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:25; 2 Thessalonians 3:1; Colossians 4:3"> Pray for vs: for we are assured that we haue a good conscience in all things, desiring to liue honestly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philemon 1:22; Romans 1:10–12; Romans 15:31–32"> And I desire you somewhat the more earnestly, that yee so doe, that I may be restored to you more quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:11; Hebrews 10:22; Psalms 23:1"> The God of peace that brought againe from the dead our Lord Iesus, the great shepheard of the sheepe, through the blood of the euerlasting Couenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; 2 Thessalonians 2:17; Philippians 4:13"> Make you perfect in all good workes, to doe his will, working in you that which is pleasant in his sight through Iesus Christ, to whom be praise for euer and euer, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:1; 1 Peter 5:12; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> I beseeche you also, brethren, suffer the wordes of exhortation: for I haue written vnto you in fewe wordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philemon 1:1; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; 2 Timothy 1:8"> Knowe that our brother Timotheus is deliuered, with whome (if hee come shortly) I will see you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 13:17; Acts 18:2"> Salute all them that haue the ouersight of you, and all the Saintes. They of Italie salute you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Colossians 4:18; 2 Timothy 4:22; Titus 3:15"> Grace be with you all, Amen. ‘Written to the Hebrewes from Italie, and sent by Timotheus.’</span>
