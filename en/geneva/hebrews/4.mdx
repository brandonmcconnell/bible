<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; Romans 10:16–17; Hebrews 11:6"> For vnto vs was the Gospel preached as also vnto them: but the worde that they heard, profited not them, because it was not mixed with faith in those that heard it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Matthew 11:28–29; 1 Peter 1:20"> For we which haue beleeued, doe enter into rest, as he said to the other, As I haue sworne in my wrath, If they shall enter into my rest: although the workes were finished from the foundation of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Exodus 31:17; Genesis 2:1–2"> For he spake in a certaine place of the seuenth day on this wise, And God did rest the seuenth day from all his workes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Hebrews 4:3; Hebrews 3:11"> And in this place againe, If they shall enter into my rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Hebrews 3:18–19; Acts 13:46–47"> Seeing therefore it remaineth that some must enter thereinto, and they to whom it was first preached, entred not therein for vnbeliefes sake:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:7; Hebrews 3:7–8; Hebrews 3:15"> Againe he appointed in Dauid a certaine day, by To day, after so long a time, saying, as it is sayd, This day, if ye heare his voyce, harden not your hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:15; Joshua 22:4; Deuteronomy 12:9"> For if Iesus had giuen them rest, then would he not after this haue spoke of an other day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10; Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:14–17"> There remaineth therefore a rest to the people of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Hebrews 4:3–4; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> For he that is entred into his rest, hath also ceased from his owne works, as God did from his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:28–30; Luke 13:24; Hebrews 3:18–1"> Let vs studie therefore to enter into that rest, lest any man fall after the same ensample of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:29; Isaiah 55:11; Ephesians 6:17"> For the worde of God is liuely, and mightie in operation, and sharper then any two edged sword, and entreth through, euen vnto the diuiding asunder of the soule and the spirit, and of the ioints, and the marow, and is a discerner of the thoughtes, and the intents of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:5; Psalms 33:13–15; Proverbs 15:3"> Neither is there any creature, which is not manifest in his sight: but all things are naked and open vnto his eyes, with whome we haue to doe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 9:12"> Seeing then that wee haue a great hie Priest, which is entred into heauen, euen Iesus the Sonne of God, let vs holde fast our profession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17–18; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Isaiah 53:4–5"> For we haue not an hie Priest, which can not be touched with the feeling of our infirmities, but was in all things tempted in like sort, yet without sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:19–23"> Let vs therefore goe boldly vnto ye throne of grace, that we may receiue mercy, and finde grace to helpe in time of neede.</span>
