<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:6; Numbers 9:3; 2 Kings 23:21–23"> Moreouer Iosiah kept a Passeouer vnto the Lord in Ierusalem, and they slewe the Passeouer in the fourtenth day of the first moneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:2; 1 Chronicles 22:19; 2 Chronicles 29:5–11"> And he appointed the Priestes to their charges, and incouraged them to the seruice of the house of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:26; Malachi 2:7; Deuteronomy 33:10"> And he sayd vnto the Leuites that taught all Israel and were sanctified vnto the Lord, Put the holy Arke in the house which Salomon the sonne of Dauid King of Israel did build: it shalbe no more a burden vpon your shoulders: serue now the Lord your God and his people Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 23:1–26; Nehemiah 11:10–20"> And prepare your selues by the houses of your fathers according to your courses, as Dauid the King of Israel hath written, and according to the writing of Salomon his sonne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 134:1; Psalms 135:2; Ezra 6:18"> And stande in the Sanctuarie according to the deuision of the families of your brethren the children of the people, and after the deuision of the familie of the Leuites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:5; Hebrews 9:13–14; 2 Chronicles 29:15"> So kill the Passeouer and sanctifie your selues, and prepare your brethren that they may doe according to the worde of the Lord by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 8:63; 2 Chronicles 30:24; Isaiah 32:8"> Iosiah also gaue to the people sheepe, lambs and kiddes, all for the Passeouer, euen to all that were present, to the nomber of thirtie thousand, and three thousande bullocks: these were of the Kings substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:31–33; Jeremiah 29:25–26; 1 Chronicles 24:4–5"> And his princes offred willingly vnto the people, to the Priests and to the Leuites: Hilkiah, and Zechariah, and Iehiel, rulers of the house of God, gaue vnto the Priests for the Passeouer, euen two thousand and sixe hundreth sheepe, and three hundreth bullockes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:21–28; Jeremiah 3:10; Isaiah 1:10–15"> Conaniah also and Shemaiah and Nethaneel his brethren, and Hashabiah and Ieiel, and Iozabad, chiefe of the Leuites gaue vnto the Leuites for the Passeouer, fiue thousand sheepe, and fiue hundreth bullockes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:16; Ezra 6:18; 2 Chronicles 35:4–5"> Thus the seruice was prepared, and the Priests stoode in their places, also the Leuites in their orders, according to the Kings commandement:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; Hebrews 9:21–22; 2 Chronicles 29:22–24"> And they slewe the Passeouer, and the Priests sprinkled the blood with their handes, and the Leuites flayed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 3:9–11; Leviticus 3:3; Leviticus 3:5"> And they tooke away from the burnt offering to giue it according to the deuisions of the families of the children of the people, to offer vnto the Lord, as it is written in the booke of Moses, and so of the bullockes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:8–9; 1 Samuel 2:13–15; Deuteronomy 16:7"> And they rosted the Passeouer with fire, according to ye custome, but the sanctified things they sod in pots, pannes, and cauldrons, and distributed them quickely to all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 6:2–4"> Afterwarde also they prepared for them selues and for the Priestes: for the Priestes the sonnes of Aaron were occupied in offering of burnt offrings, and the fat vntill night: therefore the Leuites prepared for them selues, and for the Priests the sonnes of Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:1–7; 1 Chronicles 26:12–19; 1 Chronicles 9:17–19"> And the singers the sonnes of Asaph stoode in their standing according to the commandement of Dauid, and Asaph, and Heman, and Ieduthun the Kings Seer: and the porters at euery gate, who might not depart from their seruice: therefore their brethren the Leuites prepared for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So all the seruice of the Lord was prepared the same day, to keepe the Passeouer, and to offer burnt offerings vpon the altar of the Lord, according to the commandement of King Iosiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:15–20; 2 Chronicles 30:21–23; Exodus 23:15"> And the children of Israel that were present, kept the Passeouer the same time, and the feast of the vnleauened bread seuen dayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:5; 2 Kings 23:21–23; 2 Chronicles 30:26–27"> And there was no Passeouer kept like that, in Israel, from the dayes of Samuel the Prophet: neyther did all the Kings of Israel keepe such a Passeouer as Iosiah kept, and the Priestes and the Leuites, and all Iudah, and Israel that were present, and the inhabitants of Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> This Passeouer was kept in the eighteenth yeere of the reigne of Iosiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 10:9; 2 Kings 23:29–30; Jeremiah 46:2–12"> After all this, when Iosiah had prepared the Temple, Necho King of Egypt came vp to fight against Carchemish by Perath, and Iosiah went out against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:19; 2 Kings 18:25; Isaiah 36:10"> But he sent messengers to him, saying, What haue I to doe with thee, thou King of Iudah? I come not against thee this day, but against the house of mine enemie, and God commanded me to make haste: leaue of to come against God, which is with me, least he destroy thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 5:19; 2 Chronicles 18:29; 1 Kings 22:30"> But Iosiah would not turne his face from him, but changed his apparel to fight with him, and hearkened not vnto the wordes of Necho, which were of the mouth of God, but came to fight in the valley of Megiddo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 22:34; 2 Kings 9:24; Genesis 49:23"> And the shooters shot at king Iosiah: then the King saide to his seruants, Cary me away, for I am very sicke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 12:11; 2 Kings 23:30; Psalms 36:6"> So his seruants tooke him out of that charet, and put him in the seconde charet which he had, and when they had brought him to Ierusalem, he dyed, and was buryed in the sepulchres of his fathers: and all Iudah and Ierusalem mourned for Iosiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Lamentations 4:20; Jeremiah 22:10; Job 3:8"> And Ieremiah lamented Iosiah, and al singing men and singing women mourned for Iosiah in their lamentations to this day, and made the same for an ordinance vnto Israel: and beholde, they be written in the lamentations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:20; 2 Chronicles 32:32"> Concerning the rest of the actes of Iosiah and his goodnesse, doing as it was written in the Lawe of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:22; 2 Chronicles 25:26; 2 Kings 10:34"> And his deedes, first and last, behold, they are written in the booke of the Kings of Israel and Iudah.</span>
