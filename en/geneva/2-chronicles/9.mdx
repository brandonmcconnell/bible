<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; Luke 11:31; 1 Kings 10:1–13"> And when the Queene of Sheba heard of the fame of Salomon, she came to proue Salomon with hard questions at Ierusalem, with a very great traine, and camels that bare sweete odours and much golde, and precious stones: and when she came to Salomon, she communed with him of all that was in her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:15; 1 Kings 3:12; Mark 4:34"> And Salomon declared her all her questions, and there was nothing hid from Salomon, which he declared not vnto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1–4; 1 Kings 10:3; 1 Kings 6:1–7"> Then the Queene of Sheba sawe the wisedome of Salomon, and the house that he had buylt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 12:37; 1 Kings 4:22–23; Psalms 143:7"> And the meate of his table, and the sitting of his seruants, and the order of his wayters, and their apparel, and his butlers, and their apparel, and his burnt offrings which he offred in the house of the Lord, and she was greatly astonied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 10:6"> And she saide to the King, It was a true worde which I heard in mine owne lande of thy sayings, and of thy wisedome:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 10:7; 2 Chronicles 9:5; 1 Kings 4:34"> Howbeit I beleeued not their report, vntil I came, and mine eyes had seene it: and beholde, the one halfe of thy great wisedom was not told me: for thou exceedest the fame that I heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Proverbs 3:14; Proverbs 10:21"> Happie are thy men, and happie are these thy seruants, which stande before thee alway, and heare thy wisedome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:11; 1 Chronicles 29:23; Isaiah 32:1–2"> Blessed be the Lord thy God, which loued thee, to set thee on his throne as King, in the steade of the Lord thy God: because thy God loueth Israel, to establish it for euer, therefore hath he made thee King ouer them, to execute iudgement and iustice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:1; Psalms 72:10; Psalms 72:15"> Then she gaue the King sixe score talents of golde, and of sweete odours exceeding much and precious stones: neither was there such sweete odours since, as the Queene of Sheba gaue vnto King Salomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:18; 1 Kings 10:11; 1 Kings 10:22"> And the seruants also of Huram, and the seruants of Salomon which brought golde from Ophir, brought Algummim wood and precious stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 10:12; 1 Chronicles 23:5; Psalms 92:1–3"> And the King made of the Algummim wood staires in the house of the Lord, and in the Kings house, and harpes and violes for singers: and there was no such seene before in the lande of Iudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 10:13; Ephesians 3:20; Psalms 20:4"> And King Salomon gaue to the Queene of Sheba euery pleasant thing that she asked, besides for that which she had brought vnto the King: so she returned and went to her owne countrey, both she, and her seruants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 72:15; Psalms 68:29; 1 Kings 10:14–28"> Also the weight of golde that came to Salomon in one yeere, was sixe hundreth three score and sixe talents of golde,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 68:29; Psalms 72:10"> Besides that which chapmen and marchants brought: and all the Kings of Arabia, and the princes of the countrey brought golde and siluer to Salomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 12:9–10"> And King Salomon made two hundreth targets of beaten golde, and sixe hundreth shekels of beaten golde went to one target,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2"> And three hundreth shieldes of beaten golde: three hundreth shekels of golde went to one shielde, and the King put them in the house of the wood of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 10:18–20; Psalms 45:8; Revelation 20:11"> And the King made a great throne of yuorie and ouerlaid it with pure golde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Revelation 5:5; Numbers 23:24"> And the throne had sixe steppes, with a footestoole of gold fastened to the throne, and stayes on either side on the place of the seate, and two lyons standing by the stayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Revelation 21:12"> And twelue lyons stoode there on the sixe steps on either side: there was not the like made in any kingdome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 10:21; Esther 1:7; 2 Chronicles 9:27"> And all King Salomons drinking vessels were of golde, and all the vessels of the house of the wood of Lebanon were of pure gold: for siluer was nothing esteemed in ye dayes of Salomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 22:48; Job 39:13; 1 Kings 10:22"> For the Kings ships went to Tarshish with the seruants of Huram, euery three yeere once came the ships of Tarshish, and brought golde, and siluer, yuorie, and apes, and peacockes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:12; Psalms 89:27; 1 Kings 4:30–31"> So King Salomon excelled all the Kings of the earth in riches and wisedome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 1:5; James 3:17; Proverbs 2:6"> And all the Kings of the earth sought the presence of Salomon, to heare his wisedome that God had put in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 10:25; Job 42:11; Psalms 72:10"> And they brought euery man his present, vessels of siluer, and vessels of golde, and raiment, armour, and sweet odours, horses, and mules, from yeere to yeere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 4:26; 1 Kings 10:26; 2 Chronicles 1:14"> And Salomon had foure thousand stalles of horses, and charets, and twelue thousand horsmen, whom he bestowed in the charet cities, and with the King at Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 4:21; Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 4:24"> And he reigned ouer all the Kings from the Riuer euen vnto the land of the Philistims, and to the border of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:15–17; Isaiah 9:10; 1 Kings 10:27–29"> And the King gaue siluer in Ierusalem, as stones, and gaue cedar trees as the wilde fig trees, that are aboundant in the plaine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:16; 1 Kings 10:28; Isaiah 31:1"> And they brought vnto Salomon horses out of Egypt, and out of all landes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:29; 1 Kings 11:41–43; 1 Kings 11:29"> Concerning the rest of the actes of Salomon first and last, are they not written in the booke of Nathan the Prophet, and in the prophecie of Ahiiah the Shilonite, and in the visions of Ieedo the Seer against Ieroboam the sonne of Nebat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 11:42–43"> And Salomon reigned in Ierusalem ouer all Israel fourtie yeeres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 2:10; 2 Samuel 7:12; 1 Kings 1:21"> And Salomon slept with his fathers, and they buryed him in the citie of Dauid his father: and Rehoboam his sonne reigned in his steade.</span>
