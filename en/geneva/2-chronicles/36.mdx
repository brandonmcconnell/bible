<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:11; 2 Chronicles 33:25; 2 Chronicles 26:1"> Then the people of the lande tooke Iehoahaz the sonne of Iosiah, and made him king in his fathers steade in Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Iehoahaz was three and twentie yeere old when he began to reigne, and he reigned three moneths in Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33"> And the King of Egypt tooke him away at Ierusalem, and condemned the lande in an hundreth talents of siluer, and a talent of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:10–12; 2 Kings 23:34–35; Ezekiel 19:3–4"> And the King of Egypt made Eliakim his brother King ouer Iudah and Ierusalem, and turned his name to Iehoiakim: and Necho tooke Iehoahaz his brother, and caryed him to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 23:36–37; Jeremiah 22:13–19; Jeremiah 26:1"> Iehoiakim was fiue and twentie yeere old, when he began to reigne, and he reigned eleuen yeere in Ierusalem, and did euill in the sight of the Lord his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11; 2 Kings 24:5–6; Ezekiel 19:5–9"> Against him came vp Nebuchadnezzar King of Babel, and bounde him with chaines to cary him to Babel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 24:13; Daniel 5:2–4; Jeremiah 27:16–18"> Nebuchadnezzar also caryed of the vessels of the house of the Lord to Babel, and put them in his temple at Babel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 24:5–6; Matthew 1:11–12; 1 Chronicles 3:16–17"> Concerning the rest of the actes of Iehoiakim, and his abominations which he did, and that which was founde vpon him, behold, they are written in the booke of the Kings of Israel and Iudah, and Iehoiachin his sonne reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 24:8–17"> Iehoiachin was eight yeere olde when he beganne to reigne, and he reigned three moneths and ten dayes in Ierusalem, and did euill in the sight of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:1; 2 Chronicles 36:7; 2 Kings 24:10–17"> And when the yeere was out, King Nebuchadnezzar sent and brought him to Babel with the precious vessels of the house of the Lord, and he made Zedekiah his brother King ouer Iudah and Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 24:18–20; Jeremiah 52:1–3; Jeremiah 27:1"> Zedekiah was one and twentie yeere olde, when he beganne to reigne, and reigned eleuen yeere in Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:23; Jeremiah 37:2–21; Jeremiah 38:14–28"> And he did euill in the sight of the Lord his God, and humbled not himselfe before Ieremiah the Prophet at the commandement of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:2–3; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Ezekiel 17:11–20"> But he rebelled moreouer against Nebuchadnezzar, which had caused him to sweare by God: and he hardened his necke and made his heart obstinate that he might not returne to the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:8; Ezekiel 22:6; Micah 3:9–11"> All the chiefe of the Priestes also and of the people trespassed wonderfully, according to all the abominations of the heathen, and polluted the house of the Lord which he had sanctified in Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:15; Jeremiah 7:13; Jeremiah 25:3–4"> Therefore the Lord God of their fathers sent to them by his messengers, rising earely and sending: for he had compassion on his people, and on his habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12–13; 2 Chronicles 30:10; Luke 22:63–64"> But they mocked the messengers of God and despised his wordes, and misused his Prophets, vntill the wrath of the Lord arose against his people, and till there was no remedie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 74:20; Ezra 9:7; Ezekiel 9:5–7"> For he brought vpon them the King of the Caldeans, who slewe their yong men with the sworde in the house of their Sanctuarie, and spared neither yong man, nor virgin, ancient, nor aged. God gaue all into his hande,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:7; 2 Chronicles 36:10; Isaiah 39:6"> And all the vessels of the house of God great and small, and the treasures of the house of the Lord, and the treasures of the King, and of his princes: all these caryed he to Babel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 79:1; 1 Kings 9:8; Jeremiah 52:13–15"> And they burnt the house of God, and brake downe the wall of Ierusalem, and burnt all the palaces thereof with fire, and all the precious vessels thereof, to destroy all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:7; Ezra 1:1–11; 2 Chronicles 36:22"> And they that were left by the sworde, caryed he away to Babel, and they were seruants to him and to his sonnes, vntill the kingdome of the Persians had rule,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Leviticus 26:34–35; Zechariah 1:12"> To fulfill the worde of the Lord by the mouth of Ieremiah, vntill the lande had her fill of her Sabbaths: for all the dayes that she lay desolate, she kept Sabbath, to fulfill seuentie yeeres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:12; Ezra 1:1–3; Jeremiah 29:10"> But in the first yeere of Cyrus King of Persia (when the worde of the Lord, spoken by the mouth of Ieremiah, was finished) the Lord stirred vp the spirit of Cyrus King of Persia, and he made a proclamation through all his kingdome, and also by writing, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 44:26–28; Ezra 7:13; Romans 8:31"> Thus saith Cyrus King of Persia, All the kingdomes of the earth hath the Lord God of heauen giuen me, and hath commanded me to build him an house in Ierusalem, that is in Iudah. Who is among you of all his people, with whom the Lord his God is? let him goe vp.</span>
