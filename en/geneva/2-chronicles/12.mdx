<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:17; 2 Chronicles 12:13; 2 Chronicles 26:13–16"> And when Rehoboam had established the kingdome and made it strong, hee forsooke the Lawe of the Lord, and all Israel with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 11:40; 2 Chronicles 7:19–20; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> Therefore in the fift yeere of King Rehoboam, Shishak the King of Egypt came vp against Ierusalem (because they had transgressed against the Lord)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nahum 3:9; 2 Chronicles 16:8; Daniel 11:43"> With twelue hundreth charets, and three score thousande horsemen, and the people were without nomber, that came with him from Egypt, euen the Lubims, Sukkiims, and the Ethiopians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:5–12; 2 Kings 18:17; Isaiah 8:8"> And he tooke the strong cities which were of Iudah, and came vnto Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:2; 2 Chronicles 15:2; 1 Kings 12:22"> Then came Shemaiah the Prophet to Rehoboam, and to the princes of Iudah, that were gathered together in Hierusalem, because of Shishak, and sayde vnto them, Thus sayth the Lord, Ye haue forsaken me, therefore haue I also left you in the handes of Shishak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 9:27; Daniel 9:14; Jeremiah 13:18"> Then the princes of Israel, and the King humbled themselues, and sayde, The Lord is iust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:25; Isaiah 42:25; 1 Kings 21:28–29"> And when the Lord sawe that they humbled themselues, the worde of the Lord came to Shemaiah, saying, They haue humbled theselues, therefore I will not destroy them, but I will sende them deliuerance shortly, and my wrath shall not bee powred out vpon Ierusalem by the hand of Shishak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:47–48; Isaiah 26:13; Jeremiah 10:24"> Neuerthelesse they shalbe his sernants: so shall they knowe my seruice, and the seruice of the kingdomes of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Kings 16:8"> Then Shishak King of Egypt came vp against Ierusalem, and tooke the treasures of the house of the Lord, and the treasures of the Kings house he tooke euen all, and hee caried away the shields of golde, which Salomon had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 2 Samuel 23:23; Lamentations 4:1"> In stead whereof King Rehoboam made shieldes of brasse, and committed them to the handes of the chiefe of the garde, that wayted at the doore of the Kings house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And when the King entred into the house of the Lord, the garde came and bare them and brought them againe vnto the garde chamber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:3; 2 Chronicles 12:6–7; 2 Chronicles 33:12–13"> And because hee humbled himselfe, the wrath of the Lord turned from him, that hee woulde not destroy all together. And also in Iudah the things prospered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 14:21; Deuteronomy 12:5; 2 Chronicles 6:6"> So King Rehoboam was strong in Ierusalem and reigned: for Rehoboam was one and fourtie yere olde, when he began to reigne, and reigned seuenteene yeres in Ierusalem, the citie which the Lord had chosen out of all the tribes of Israel to put his Name there. And his mothers name was Naamah an Ammonitesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:3; 1 Corinthians 16:13; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> And he did euill: for hee prepared not his heart to seeke the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:29; 2 Chronicles 12:5; 1 Kings 12:22"> The actes also of Rehoboam, first and last, are they not written in the booke of Shemaiah the Prophet, and Iddo the Seer, in rehearsing the genealogie? and there was warre alway betweene Rehoboam and Ieroboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:10; 2 Chronicles 13:1; Matthew 1:7"> And Rehoboam slept with his fathers, and was buried in the citie of Dauid, and Abiiah his sonne reigned in his stead.</span>
