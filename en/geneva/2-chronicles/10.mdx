<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:6; Joshua 20:7; 1 Chronicles 12:38"> Then Rehoboam went to Shechem: for to Sheche came all Israel to make him king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 11:40; 1 Kings 12:2; 1 Kings 11:28"> And when Ieroboam the sonne of Nebat heard it, (which was in Egypt, whither he had fled from the presence of Salomon the King) he returned out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 12:3"> And they sent and called him: so came Ieroboam and all Israel, and communed with Rehoboam, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 12:4; Exodus 2:23; Matthew 11:29–30"> Thy father made our yoke grieuous: nowe therefore make thou the grieuous seruitude of thy father, and his sore yoke, that he put vpon vs, lighter, and we will serue thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 12:5; Proverbs 3:28"> And he sayde to them, Depart yet three dayes, then come againe vnto me. And the people departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 32:7; Proverbs 19:20; Proverbs 27:10"> And King Rehoboam tooke counsel with the olde men that had stande before Salomon his father, while hee yet liued, saying, What counsell giue ye that I may answere this people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Genesis 49:21; 1 Kings 12:7"> And they spake vnto him, saying, If thou be kinde to this people, and please them, and speake louing words to them, they will be thy seruants for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:14; Proverbs 1:25; Ecclesiastes 10:16"> But hee left the counsel of the ancient men that they had giuen him, and tooke counsell of the yong men that were brought vp with him, and waited on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 10:6; 2 Samuel 17:5–6; 1 Kings 22:6–8"> And he sayd vnto them, What counsel giue ye, that we may answere this people, which haue spoken to mee, saying, Make the yoke which thy father did put vpon vs, lighter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 29:23; Proverbs 13:16; 2 Samuel 17:7–13"> And the yong men that were brought vp with him, spake vnto him, saying, Thus shalt thou answere the people that spake to thee, saying, Thy father made our yoke heauie, but make thou it lighter for vs: thus shalt thou say vnto them, My least part shalbe bigger then my fathers loines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; Isaiah 47:6; Revelation 9:10"> Now whereas my father did burden you with a grieuous yoke, I will yet increase your yoke: my father hath chastised you with roddes, but I will correct you with scourges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 12:12–15; 2 Chronicles 10:5"> Then Ieroboam and all the people came to Rehoboam the third day, as the King had appointed saying, Come againe to me the third day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 10:8; Proverbs 19:27; 1 Samuel 25:10–11"> And the King answered them sharply: and King Rehoboam left the counsel of the ancient men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 12:5; Ecclesiastes 10:16"> And spake to them after ye counsell of the yong men, saying, My father made your yoke grieuous, but I wil incease it: my father chastised you with rods, but I will correct you with scourges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 11:29–39; 1 Samuel 2:25; 2 Chronicles 25:16–20"> So the king hearkened not vnto the people: for it was the ordinance of God that the Lord might performe his saying, which hee had spoken by Ahiiah the Shilonite to Ieroboam the sonne of Nebat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:1; 2 Chronicles 10:19; Psalms 76:10"> So when all Isarael sawe that the King would not heare them, the people answered the King, saying, What portion haue we in Dauid? for we haue none inheritance in the sonue of Ishai. O Israel, euery man to your tents: now see to thine owne house, Dauid. So all Israel departed to their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:1; 1 Kings 12:17; 1 Kings 11:36"> Howbeit Rehoboam reigned ouer the children of Israel, that dwelt in the cities of Iudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 5:14; 1 Kings 4:6; Acts 7:57–58"> Then King Rehoboam sent Hadoram that was ouer the tribute, and the children of Israel stoned him with stones, that he died: then King Rehoboam made speede to get him vp to his charet, to flee to Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 12:19–20; Ezra 9:7; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> And Israel rebelled against the house of Dauid vnto this day.</span>
