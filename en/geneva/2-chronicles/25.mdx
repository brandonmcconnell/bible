<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 14:1–6"> Amaziah was fiue and twentie yere old when he began to reigne, and he reigned nine and twentie yeere in Ierusalem: and his mothers name was Iehoaddan, of Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:14; James 4:8; Acts 8:21"> And he did vprightly in the eyes of the Lord, but not with a perfite heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:25–26; 2 Kings 14:5–22; Genesis 9:5–6"> And when the kingdome was established vnto him, he slewe his seruants, that had slaine the King his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:16; Ezekiel 18:20; 2 Kings 14:5–6"> But he slewe not their children, but did as it is written in the Lawe, and in the booke of Moses, where the Lord commanded, saying, The fathers shall not dye for the children, neyther shall the children die for the fathers, but euery man shall dye for his owne sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 1:3; 2 Chronicles 17:14–18; 2 Chronicles 11:1"> And Amaziah assembled Iudah, and made them captaines ouer thousandes, and captaines ouer hundreths, according to the houses of their fathers, thorowout all Iudah and Beniamin: and he nombred them from twentie yeere olde and aboue, and founde among them three hundreth thousand chosen men, to goe foorth to the warre, and to handle speare and shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He hyred also an hundreth thousand valiant men out of Israel for an hundreth talents of siluer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:2; 1 Kings 12:28; Hosea 9:13"> But a man of God came to him, saying, O King, let not the armie of Israel goe with thee: for the Lord is not with Israel, neither with all the house of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:11; 2 Chronicles 20:6; Philippians 4:19"> If not, goe thou on, doe it, make thy selfe strong to the battel, but God shall make thee fall before the enemie: for God hath power to helpe, and to cast downe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 10:22; Deuteronomy 8:18"> And Amaziah sayde to the man of God, What shall we doe then for the hundreth talents, which I haue giuen to the hoste of Israel? Then the man of God answered, The Lord is able to giue thee more then this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 12:24; 2 Samuel 19:43; Proverbs 29:22"> So Amaziah separated them, to wit, the armie that was come to him out of Ephraim, to returne to their place: wherefore their wrath was kindled greatly against Iudah, and they returned to their places with great anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 14:7; Psalms 60:1; 2 Samuel 8:13"> Then Amaziah was encouraged, and led forth his people, and went to the salt valley, and smote of the children of Seir, ten thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:10; 1 Chronicles 20:3; 2 Samuel 12:31"> And other ten thousand did the children of Iudah take aliue, and caryed them to the top of a rocke, and cast them downe from the top of the rocke, and they all burst to pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:5; 1 Kings 16:29; 1 Kings 9:17"> But the men of the armie, which Amaziah sent away, that they should not goe with his people to battell, fell vpon the cities of Iudah from Samaria vnto Beth-horon, and smote three thousand of them, and tooke much spoyle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:23; Deuteronomy 7:25; Deuteronomy 7:5"> Now after that Amaziah was come from the slaughter of the Edomites, he brought the gods of the children of Seir, and set them vp to be his gods, and worshipped them, and burned incense vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 96:5; 2 Chronicles 25:11–12; 2 Chronicles 25:7"> Wherefore the Lord was wroth with Amaziah, and sent vnto him a Prophet, which sayd vnto him, Why hast thou sought the gods of the people, which were not able to deliuer their owne people out of thine hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:20–21; Jeremiah 29:26; 2 Chronicles 18:25"> And as he talked with him, he said vnto him, Haue they made thee the Kings counseler? cease thou: why should they smite thee? And the Prophet ceased, but said, I knowe that God hath determined to destroy thee, because thou hast done this, and hast not obeyed my counsell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 14:8–14; 2 Chronicles 25:13; Proverbs 20:3"> Then Amaziah King of Iudah tooke counsell, and sent to Ioash the sonne of Iehoahaz, the sonne of Iehu King of Israel, saying, Come, let vs see one another in the face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; 1 Kings 4:33; Psalms 80:13"> But Ioash King of Israel sent to Amaziah King of Iudah, saying, The thistle that is in Lebanon, sent to the cedar that is in Lebanon, saying, Giue thy daughter to my sonne to wife: and the wilde beast that was in Lebanon went and trode downe the thistle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:16; 2 Chronicles 32:25; Proverbs 18:6"> Thou thinkest: lo, thou hast smitten Edom, and thine heart lifteth thee vp to bragge: abide now at home: why doest thou prouoke to thine hurt, that thou shouldest fall, and Iudah with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:7; 1 Kings 12:15; 1 Peter 2:8"> But Amaziah would not heare: for it was of God, that he might deliuer them into his had, because they had sought the gods of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19–20; 1 Samuel 6:9; Joshua 21:16"> So Ioash the King of Israel went vp: and he, and Amaziah King of Iudah saw one another in the face at Bethshemesh, which is in Iudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:10; 2 Chronicles 28:5–6; 1 Kings 22:36"> And Iudah was put to the worse before Israel, and they fled euery man to his tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:17; Nehemiah 8:16; 2 Chronicles 26:9"> But Ioash the King of Israel tooke Amaziah King of Iudah, the sonne of Ioash, the sonne of Iehoahaz in Bethshemesh, and brought him to Ierusalem, and brake downe the wall of Ierusalem, from the gate of Ephraim vnto the corner gate, foure hundreth cubites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:15; 2 Chronicles 12:9; 2 Kings 14:14"> And he tooke all the gold and the siluer, and all the vessels that were found in the house of God with Obed Edom, and in the treasures of the Kings house, and the children that were in hostage, and returned to Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 14:17–22"> And Amaziah the sonne of Ioash King of Iudah liued after the death of Ioash sonne of Iehoahaz King of Israel, fifteene yeere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:34; 2 Kings 14:15"> Concerning the rest of the actes of Amaziah first and last, are they not written in the booke of the Kings of Iudah and Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:25; Joshua 10:31; 2 Kings 14:19"> Nowe after the time that Amaziah did turne away from ye Lord, they wrought treason against him in Ierusalem: and when he was fled to Lachish, they sent to Lachish after him, and slewe him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 14:20"> And they brought him vpon horses, and buried him with his fathers in the citie of Iudah.</span>
