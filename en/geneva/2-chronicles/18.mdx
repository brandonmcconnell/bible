<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:5; 2 Chronicles 21:6; 2 Chronicles 19:2"> And Iehoshaphat had riches and honour in abundance, but he was ioyned in affinitie with Ahab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 9:1; 2 Chronicles 19:2; 1 Kings 1:9"> And after certaine yeeres he went downe to Ahab to Samaria: and Ahab slew sheepe and oxen for him in great nomber, and for the people that he had with him, and entised him to goe vp vnto Ramoth Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 22:4; 2 Kings 3:7; 2 John 1:10–11"> And Ahab King of Israel saide vnto Iehoshaphat King of Iudah, Wilt thou goe with mee to Ramoth Gilead? And hee answered him, I am as thou art, and my people as thy people, and wee will ioyne with thee in the warre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:2; 2 Samuel 5:19; 2 Samuel 2:1"> And Iehoshaphat sayde vnto the King of Israel, Aske counsel, I pray thee, at the worde of the Lord this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3; Micah 3:11; Jeremiah 28:1–17"> Therefore the King of Israel gathered of Prophets foure hundreth men, and sayde vnto them, Shall we goe to Ramoth Gilead to battel, or shall I cease? And they sayd, Go vp: for God shall deliuer it into the Kings hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 22:7–9; 2 Kings 3:11–13"> But Iehoshaphat sayde, Is there heere neuer a Prophet more of the Lord that wee might inquire of him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Micah 2:7; 1 Kings 21:20"> And the King of Israel sayd vnto Iehoshaphat, There is yet one man, by whome wee may aske counsell of the Lord, but I hate him: for he doeth not prophesie good vnto me, but alway euil: it is Michaiah the sonne of Imla. Then Iehoshaphat said, Let not the King say so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 22:9; Isaiah 39:7; 2 Chronicles 18:25–26"> And the King of Israel called an eunuche, and said, Call quickly Michaiah the sonne of Imla.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 14:9; Daniel 7:9; Matthew 19:28"> And the King of Israel, and Iehoshaphat King of Iudah sate either of them on his throne clothed in their apparel: they sate euen in the threshing floore at the entring in of the gate of Samaria: and all the Prophets prophesied before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:17; Zechariah 1:18–21; Jeremiah 28:2–3"> And Zidkiah ye sonne of Chenaanah made him hornes of yron, and sayde, Thus sayth the Lord, With these shalt thou push the Aramites vntill thou hast consumed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 24:24–25; 2 Chronicles 18:33–34; Jude 1:16"> And all the Prophets prophesied so, saying, Go vp to Ramoth Gilead, and prosper: for the Lord shall deliuer it into the hand of the King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 2:6; Psalms 10:11; Micah 2:11"> And the messenger that went to call Michaiah, spake to him, saying, Beholde, the wordes of the Prophets declare good to the King with one accord: let thy word therefore, I pray thee, be like one of theirs, and speake thou good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 22:35; Numbers 24:13; Numbers 22:18–20"> And Michaiah saide, As the Lord liueth, whatsoeuer my God saith, that will I speake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 22:15; Lamentations 4:21; Amos 4:4–5"> So he came to the King, and the King said vnto him, Michaiah, shall we go to Ramoth Gilead to battel, or shall I leaue off? And he said, Goe yee vp, and prosper, and they shalbe deliuered into your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 26:63; 1 Samuel 14:24; 1 Kings 22:16"> And the King sayd to him, Howe oft shall I charge thee, that thou tell mee nothing but the trueth in the Name of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Ezekiel 34:5–6; Numbers 27:17"> Then he said, I saw al Israel scattered in the mountaines, as sheepe that haue no shepheard: and the Lord sayd, These haue no Master: let them returne euery man to his house in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:7; 1 Kings 22:18; Proverbs 29:1"> And the King of Israel sayde to Iehoshaphat, Did I not tell thee, that he would not prophesie good vnto me, but euill?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 7:9; Isaiah 6:1–5; 1 Kings 22:19–23"> Againe hee saide, Therefore heare ye the worde of the Lord: I sawe the Lord sit vpon his throne, and all the hoste of heauen standing at his right hand, and at his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:9; 2 Chronicles 25:19; Proverbs 11:5"> And the Lord sayd, Who shall perswade Ahab King of Israel, that he may go vp, and fall at Ramoth Gilead? And one spake and said thus, and another said that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 1:6; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> Then there came forth a spirit and stoode before the Lord, and said, I will perswade him. And the Lord said vnto him, Wherein?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 8:44; 1 Kings 22:21–22; 1 John 4:6"> And he saide, I will goe out, and bee a false spirit in the mouth of all his Prophets. And hee said, Thou shalt perswade, and shalt also preuaile: goe forth and do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:14; Ezekiel 14:9; Job 12:16"> Now therefore behold, the Lord hath put a false spirit in the mouth of these thy Prophets, and the Lord hath determined euill against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Jeremiah 20:2; 2 Chronicles 18:10"> Then Zidkiah the sonne of Chenaanah came neere, and smote Michaiah vpon the cheeke, and sayde, By what way went the Spirit of the Lord from me, to speake with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:21–22; Isaiah 26:20; Jeremiah 28:16–17"> And Michaiah saide, Behold, thou shalt see that day when thou shalt goe from chamber to chamber to hide thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:8; 2 Chronicles 34:8; Jeremiah 38:6–7"> And the King of Israel sayde, Take ye Michaiah, and cary him to Amon the gouernour of the citie, and to Ioash the Kings sonne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:10; Psalms 80:5; Luke 23:2"> And say, Thus saith the King, Put this man in the prison house, and feede him with bread of affliction and with water of affliction vntil I returne in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 20:45–46; Amos 9:10; Micah 1:2"> And Michaiah said, If thou returne in peace, the Lord hath not spoken by me. And he saide, Heare, all ye people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 22:29–33"> So the King of Israel and Iehoshaphat the King of Iudah went vp to Ramoth Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:8; Jeremiah 23:24; Proverbs 26:25"> And the King of Israel said vnto Iehoshaphat, I will change my selfe, and enter into the battel: but put thou on thine apparel. So the King of Israel changed himselfe, and they went into the battel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:11; Deuteronomy 1:17; 1 Kings 20:42"> And the King of Aram had commanded the captaines of the charets that were with him, saying, Fight you not with small, nor great, but against the King of Israel onely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:14; Psalms 118:13; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> And when the captaines of the charets saw Iehoshaphat, they sayde, It is the King of Israel: and they compassed about him to fight. But Iehoshaphat cryed, and the Lord helped him and moued them to depart from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 22:33"> For when the captaines of the charets saw that hee was not the King of Israel, they turned backe from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:23; 2 Samuel 15:11; 1 Kings 22:34–35"> Then a certaine man drewe a bowe mightily, and smote the King of Israel betweene the ioyntes of his brigandine: Therefore he saide to his charetman, Turne thine hand, and carie mee out of the host: for I am hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 13:21; 2 Chronicles 18:27; Proverbs 28:17"> And the battel increased that day: and the King of Israel stood still in his charet against the Aramites vntil euen, and dyed at the time of the sunne going downe.</span>
