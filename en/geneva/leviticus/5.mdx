<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 5:17; Proverbs 29:24; Leviticus 19:8"> Also if any haue sinned, that is, If he haue heard the voyce of an othe, and hee can be a witnes, whether he hath seene or knowen of it, if hee doe not vtter it, he shall beare his iniquitie:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 5:17; Deuteronomy 14:8; Haggai 2:13"> Either if one touche any vncleane thing, whether it be a carion of an vncleane beast, or a carion of vncleane cattel, or a carion of vncleane creeping things, and is not ware of it, yet he is vncleane, and hath offended:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 19:11–16; Leviticus 15:1–33; Leviticus 12:1–8"> Eyther if hee touche any vncleannesse of man (whatsoeuer vncleannes it be, that hee is defiled with) and is not ware of it, and after commeth to the knowledge of it, he hath sinned:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:2–6; Acts 23:12; Judges 11:31"> Either if any sweare and pronounce with his lippes to do euill, or to doe good (whatsoeuer it bee that a man shall pronounce with an othe) and it be hid from him, and after knoweth that he hath offended in one of these poyntes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Numbers 5:7; Leviticus 26:40"> Whe he hath sinned in any of these things, then he shall confesse that he hath sinned therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 4:28; Leviticus 4:32; Leviticus 4:20"> Therefore shall he bring his trespasse offring vnto the Lord for his sinne which he hath committed, euen a female from ye flocke, be it a lambe or a she goat for a sinne offring, and the Priest shall make an atonement for him, concerning his sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 12:8; Leviticus 14:21–22; Matthew 3:16"> But if he be not able to bring a sheepe, he shall bring for his trespas which he hath committed, two turtle doues, or two yong pigeons vnto the Lord, one for a sinne offring, and the other for a burnt offring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 1:15; Leviticus 1:17; Romans 4:25"> So he shall bring them vnto the Priest, who shall offer the sinne offring first, and wring the necke of it a sunder, but not plucke it cleane off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 4:30; Leviticus 4:34; Leviticus 4:18"> After he shall sprinkle of the blood of the sinne offring vpon the side of the altar, and the rest of the blood shall be shed at the foote of the altar: for it is a sinne offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 1:14–17; Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:20"> Also he shall offer the seconde for a burnt offring as the maner is: so shall the Priest make an atonement for him (for his sinne which hee hath committed) and it shalbe forgiuen him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 5:15; Leviticus 2:1–2; Exodus 16:36"> But if he be not able to bring two turtle doues, or two yong pigeons, then he that hath sinned, shall bring for his offring, the tenth parte of an Ephah of fine floure for a sinne offring, he shall put none oyle thereto, neither put any incense thereon: for it is a sinne offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 4:35; Leviticus 2:9; Leviticus 2:2"> Then shall hee bring it to the Priest, and the Priest shall take his handfull of it for the remembrance thereof, and burne it vpon the altar with the offrings of the Lord made by fire: for it is a sinne offring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Leviticus 4:26; 1 Corinthians 9:13"> So the Priest shall make an atonement for him, as touching his sinne that he hath committed in one of these poyntes, and it shall bee forgiuen him: and the remnant shalbe the Priests, as the meate offring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the Lord spake vnto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 4:2; Leviticus 6:6"> If any person transgresse and sinne through ignorance by taking away things consecrated vnto the Lord, hee shall then bring for his trespasse offring vnto the Lord a ramme without blemish out of the flocke, worth two shekels of siluer by thy estimation after the shekel of the Sanctuarie, for a trespasse offring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 22:14; Numbers 5:7; Leviticus 27:27"> So hee shall restore that wherein hee hath offended, in taking away of the holy thing, and shall put the fift part more thereto, and giue it vnto the Priest: so the Priest shall make an atonement for him with the ram of ye trespasse offring, and it shalbe forgiuen him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27; Leviticus 4:13; Leviticus 4:2–4"> Also if any sinne and doe against any of the commandements of the Lord, which ought not to be done, and knowe not and sinne and beare his iniquitie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 5:15–16; 1 Timothy 2:5–6; Leviticus 1:4"> Then shall he bring a ramme without blemishe out of the flocke, in thy estimation worth two shekels for a trespasse offring vnto ye Priest: and the Priest shall make an atonement for him concerning his ignorance wherein he erred, and was not ware: so it shalbe forgiuen him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Malachi 3:8; 2 Corinthians 5:19–21"> This is the trespasse offring for the trespasse committed against the Lord.</span>
