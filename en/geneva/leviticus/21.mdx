<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:25; Leviticus 21:11; Leviticus 10:6–7"> And the Lord said vnto Moses, Speake vnto the Priestes the sonnes of Aaron, and say vnto them, Let none be defiled by the dead among his people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:13; Leviticus 18:6"> But by his kinseman that is neere vnto him: to wit, by his mother, or by his father, or by his sonne, or by his daughter, or by his brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Or by his sister a maid, that is neere vnto him, which hath not had a husband: for her he may lament.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:16–17"> He shall not lament for the Prince among his people, to pollute him selfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Ezekiel 44:20; Leviticus 19:27–28"> They shall not make balde partes vpon their head, nor shaue off the locks of their beard, nor make any cuttings in their flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 3:11; Leviticus 18:21; 1 Peter 2:9"> They shalbe holy vnto their God, and not pollute the name of their God: for the sacrifices of the Lord made by fire, and the bread of their God they doe offer: therefore they shalbe holie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 21:13–14; Ezekiel 44:22; Leviticus 21:8"> They shall not take to wife an whore, or one polluted, neither shall they marrie a woman diuorced from her husband: for such one is holy vnto his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:7–8; Leviticus 19:2; Leviticus 11:44–45"> Thou shalt sanctifie him therefore, for he offereth the bread of thy God: he shall be holy vnto thee: for I the Lord, which sanctifie you, am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 38:24; Leviticus 19:29; Ezekiel 9:6"> If a Priestes daughter fall to play the whore, she polluteth her father: therefore shall she be burnt with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:6–7; Leviticus 16:32; Matthew 26:65"> Also ye hie Priest among his brethren, (vpon whose head the anointing oyle was powred, and hath consecrated his hand to put on the garments) shall not vncouer his head, nor rent his clothes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 19:14; Leviticus 21:1–2; Numbers 6:7"> Neither shall he goe to any dead bodie, nor make him selfe vncleane by his father or by his mother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 10:7; Exodus 29:6–7; Leviticus 8:9–12"> Neither shall he goe out of the Sanctuarie, nor pollute the holy place of his God: for the crowne of the anoynting oyle of his God is vpon him: I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Revelation 14:4"> Also he shall take a maide vnto his wife:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; Ezekiel 44:22"> But a widowe, or a diuorced woman, or a polluted, or an harlot, these shall he not marrie, but shall take a maide of his owne people to wife:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 11:16; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Nehemiah 13:23–29"> Neyther shall he defile his seede among his people: for I am the Lord which sanctifie him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the Lord spake vnto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 65:4; Numbers 16:5; Leviticus 10:3"> Speake vnto Aaron, and say, Whosoeuer of thy seede in their generations hath any blemishes, shall not prease to offer the bread of his God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2–3; Leviticus 22:19–25; Titus 1:7"> For whosoeuer hath any blemish, shall not come neere: as a man blinde or lame, or that hath a flat nose, or that hath any misshapen member,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Or a man that hath a broken foote, or a broken hande,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:1; Isaiah 56:3"> Or is crooke backt, or bleare eyed, or hath a blemish in his eye, or be skiruie, or skabbed, or haue his stones broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 21:6; Leviticus 21:8; Leviticus 21:17"> None of the seede of Aaron the Priest that hath a blemish, shall come neere to offer the sacrifices of the Lord made by fire, hauing a blemish: he shall not prease to offer the bread of his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Numbers 18:19; Leviticus 24:8–9"> The bread of his God, euen of the most holie, and of the holy shall he eate:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 21:12; Ezekiel 44:9–14; Exodus 30:6–8"> But he shall not goe in vnto the vaile, nor come neere the altar, because hee hath a blemish, least he pollute my Sanctuaries: for I am the Lord that sanctifie them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:18; Malachi 2:1–7; 2 Timothy 2:2"> Thus spake Moses vnto Aaron, and to his sonnes, and to all the children of Israel.</span>
