<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 19:4; Deuteronomy 27:15; Numbers 33:52"> Ye shall make you none idoles nor grauen image, neither reare you vp any pillar, neither shall ye set any image of stone in your land to bow downe to it: for I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 19:30"> Ye shall keepe my Sabbaths, and reuerence my Sanctuarie: I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; Deuteronomy 11:13–15; Deuteronomy 28:1–14"> If ye walke in mine ordinances, and keepe my commandements, and doe them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 8:12; Psalms 67:6; Leviticus 25:21"> I will then sende you raine in due season, and the land shall yelde her increase, and the trees of the fielde shall giue her fruite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 9:13; Joel 2:26; Joel 2:19"> And your threshing shall reache vnto the vintage, and the vintage shall reache vnto sowing time, and you shall eate your bread in plenteousnesse, and dwell in your land safely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 35:9; Zephaniah 3:13; Psalms 147:14"> And I will sende peace in the land, and ye shall sleepe and none shall make you afraid: also I will rid euill beastes out of the lande, and the sworde shall not go through your lande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Also ye shall chase your enemies, and they shall fall before you vpon the sworde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 23:10; Deuteronomy 28:7; Deuteronomy 32:30"> And fiue of you shall chase an hundreth, and an hundreth of you shall put ten thousande to flight, and your enemies shall fall before you vpon the sworde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 17:6–7; Nehemiah 9:23; Ezekiel 16:62"> For I will haue respect vnto you, and make you encrease, and multiplie you, and establish my couenant with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 25:22; 2 Kings 19:29; Luke 12:17"> Ye shall eate also olde store, and cary out olde because of the newe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 25:8; Revelation 21:3; Exodus 29:45"> And I will set my Tabernacle among you, and my soule shall not lothe you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; Jeremiah 30:22; Jeremiah 7:23"> Also I will walke among you, and I wil be your God, and ye shalbe my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:27; Leviticus 25:38; Psalms 116:16"> I am the Lord your God which haue brought you out of the lande of Egypt, that yee should not be their bondmen, and I haue broken ye bonds of your yoke, and made you goe vpright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Leviticus 26:18; Jeremiah 17:27"> But if ye will not obey me, nor do all these commandements,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 17:15; Deuteronomy 31:16; Zechariah 7:11–13"> And if ye shall despise mine ordinances, either if your soule abhorre my lawes, so that yee will not do all my commandements, but breake my couenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 31:8; Jeremiah 5:17; Micah 6:15"> Then wil I also do this vnto you, I wil appoint ouer you fearefulnes, a consumption, and the burning ague to consume the eyes, and make the heart heauie, and you shall sowe your seede in vaine: for your enemies shall eate it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 17:10; Proverbs 28:1; Psalms 53:5"> And I will set my face against you, and ye shall fal before your enemies, and they that hate you, shall raigne ouer you, and yee shall flee when none pursueth you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 26:21; Leviticus 26:28; Leviticus 26:24"> And if ye wil not for these things obey me, then wil I punish you seuen times more, according to your sinnes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:23; 1 Kings 17:1; Daniel 4:37"> And I wil breake the pride of your power, and I will make your heauen as yron, and your earth as brasse:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 127:1; Deuteronomy 11:17; Isaiah 49:4"> And your strength shalbe spent in vaine: neither shall your lande giue her increase, neither shall the trees of the land giue their fruite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 26:18; Leviticus 26:27"> And if ye walke stubburnly against me, and will not obey mee, I will then bring seuen times more plagues vpon you, according to your sinnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:15; Deuteronomy 32:24; Judges 5:6"> I will also sende wilde beastes vpon you, which shall spoyle you, and destroy your cattell, and make you fewe in number: so your hye waies shalbe desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Jeremiah 2:30; Amos 4:6–12"> Yet if by these ye will not be reformed by me, but walke stubburnly against me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 18:26; 2 Samuel 22:27; Job 9:4"> Then wil I also walke stubburnly against you, and I will smite you yet seuen times for your sinnes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 14:12; Ezekiel 5:17; Jeremiah 29:17–18"> And I wil send a sword vpon you, that shall auenge the quarel of my couenant: and when ye are gathered in your cities, I wil send the pestilence among you, and ye shall be deliuered into the hand of the enemie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Micah 6:14; Isaiah 3:1; Psalms 105:16"> When I shall breake the staffe of your bread, then ten women shall bake your breade in one ouen, and they shall deliuer your bread againe by weight, and ye shall eate, but not be satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 26:24; Leviticus 26:21"> Yet if ye will not for this obey mee, but walke against me stubburnly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 59:18; Jeremiah 21:5; Isaiah 66:15"> Then will I walke stubburnly in mine anger against you, and I will also chastice you seuen times more according to your sinnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:10; Lamentations 4:10; Matthew 24:19"> And ye shall eate ye flesh of your sonnes, and the flesh of your daughters shall ye deuoure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 23:20; Isaiah 27:9; Ezekiel 6:13"> I will also destroy your hye places, and cut away your images, and cast your carkeises vpon the bodies of your idoles, and my soule shall abhorre you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:3; Lamentations 1:10; Hebrews 10:26"> And I will make your cities desolate, and bring your Sanctuarie vnto nought, and will not smelll the sauour of your sweete odours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 19:8; Jeremiah 18:16"> I will also bring the land vnto a wildernes, and your enemies, which dwell therein, shalbe astonished thereat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Zechariah 7:14; Ezekiel 20:23; Deuteronomy 28:64–66"> Also I wil scatter you among the heathen, and will drawe out a sworde after you, and your land shalbe waste, and your cities shalbe desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:21; Leviticus 26:43; Leviticus 25:2–4"> Then shall the land inioy her Sabbaths, as long as it lieth voide, and yee shalbe in your enemies land: then shall the land rest, and enioy her Sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 24:5–6; Romans 8:22"> All the dayes that it lieth voide, it shall rest, because it did not rest in your Sabbaths, when ye dwelt vpon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Isaiah 30:17; Leviticus 26:17"> And vpon them that are left of you, I will send euen a faintnes into their hearts in ye land of their enemies, and the sounde of a leafeshaken shall chase them, and they shall flee as fleeing from a sword, and they shall fall, no man pursuing them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 7:12–13; Judges 2:14; Jeremiah 37:10"> They shall fall also one vpon another, as before a sword, though none pursue them, and ye shall not be able to stand before your enemies:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:48; Isaiah 27:13; Jeremiah 42:17–18"> And ye shall perish among the heathen, and the land of your enemies shall eate you vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:17; Ezekiel 24:23; Ezekiel 33:10"> And they that are left of you, shall pine away for their iniquitie, in your enemies landes, and for the iniquities of their fathers shall they pine away with them also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Nehemiah 9:2–5; 1 John 1:8–10"> Then they shall confesse their iniquitie, and the wickednes of their fathers for their trespasse, which they haue trespassed against mee, and also because they haue walked stubburnly against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:7; 2 Chronicles 32:26; 2 Chronicles 12:6–7"> Therefore I wil walke stubburnly against them, and bring them into the land of their enemies: so then their vncircumcised hearts shalbe humbled, and then they shalt willingly beare the punishment of their iniquitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Exodus 6:5; Ezekiel 16:60"> Then I will remember my couenant with Iaakob, and my couenant also with Izhak, and also my couenant with Abraham will I remember, and will remember the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Romans 8:7; Leviticus 26:15; John 7:7"> The land also in the meane season shalbe left of them, and shall enioye her Sabbaths while she lieth waste without them, but they shall willingly suffer the punishment of their iniquitie, because they despised my lawes, and because their soule abhorred mine ordinances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Romans 11:2; Nehemiah 9:31; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> Yet notwithstanding this, when they shalbe in the lande of their enemies, I wil not cast them away, neither will I abhorre them, to destroy them vtterly, nor to breake my couenant with them: for I am the Lord their God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 25:38; Ezekiel 20:22; Ezekiel 20:9"> But I will remember for them the couenant of olde when I brought them out of ye land of Egypt in the sight of the heathen that I might be their God: I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Leviticus 27:34; Deuteronomy 12:1; Deuteronomy 6:1"> These are the ordinances, and the iudgements, and the lawes, which the Lord made betweene him, and the children of Israel in mount Sinai, by the hand of Moses.</span>
