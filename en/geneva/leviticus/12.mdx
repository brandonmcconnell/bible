<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spake vnto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 2:22; Leviticus 15:19; Job 25:4"> Speake vnto the children of Israel, and say, When a woman hath brought forth seede, and borne a manchilde, shee shalbe vncleane seuen dayes, like as she is vncleane when she is put apart for her disease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 2:21; Luke 1:59; John 7:22–23"> (And in the eight day, the foreskin of the childes flesh shalbe circumcised)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 2:22–23; Leviticus 15:25–28; Haggai 2:13"> And she shall continue in the blood of her purifying three and thirtie dayes: she shall touch no halowed thing, nor come into the Sanctuarie, vntil the time of her purifying be out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:14–15; Genesis 3:13; Leviticus 12:2"> But if she beare a mayde childe, then shee shalbe vncleane two weekes, as when shee hath her disease: and she shall continue in the blood of her purifying three score and sixe dayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 2:22; 2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> Nowe when the dayes of her purifying are out, (whether it be for a sonne or for a daughter) shee shall bring to the Priest a lambe of one yeere olde for a burnt offering, and a yong pigeon or a turtle doue for a sinne offring, vnto the doore of the Tabernacle of the Congregation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 15:28–30; Hebrews 9:12–14; Leviticus 4:35"> Who shall offer it before the Lord, and make an atonement for her: so she shalbe purged of the issue of her blood this is the law for her that hath borne a male or female.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 5:7; Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 1:14"> But if she bee not able to bring a lambe, she shall bring two turtles, or two yong pigeons: the one for a burnt offring, and the other for a sinne offring: and the Priest shall make an atonement for her: so she shall be cleane.</span>
