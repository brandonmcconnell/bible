<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:10; Hebrews 11:36; Psalms 35:15–16"> But when Sanballat heard that we builded the wall, then was he wroth and sore grieued, and mocked the Iewes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezra 4:9–10; 1 Corinthians 1:27; Ezekiel 37:3–13"> And sayde before his brethren and the armie of Samaria, thus he sayde, What doe these weake Iewes? wil they fortifie them selues? wil they sacrifice? will they finish it in a day? will they make the stones whole againe out of the heapes of dust, seeing they are burnt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; 1 Kings 20:10; Nehemiah 2:10"> And Tobiah the Ammonite was beside him, and said, Although they buylde, yet if a foxe goe vp, he shall euen breake downe their stonie wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 79:12; Psalms 123:3–4; Proverbs 3:34"> Heare, O our God (for we are despised) and turne their shame vpon their owne head, and giue them vnto a pray in the lande of their captiuitie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 109:14; Jeremiah 18:23; Psalms 69:27–28"> And couer not their iniquitie, neither let their sinne be put out in thy presence: for they haue prouoked vs before the builders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Nehemiah 6:15; Hebrews 13:21"> So we built the wall, and all the wall was ioyned vnto the halfe thereof, and the heart of the people was to worke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:10; Nehemiah 4:1; Acts 5:33"> But when Sanballat, and Tobiah, and the Arabians, and the Ammonites, and the Ashdodims heard that the walles of Ierusalem were repayred, (for the breaches began to be stopped) then they were very wroth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 83:3–11; Isaiah 8:9–10; Jeremiah 20:10"> And conspired all together to come and to fight against Ierusalem, and to hinder them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Acts 4:24–30; Psalms 50:15"> The we prayed vnto our God, and set watchmen by them, day and night, because of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 11:1–2; Haggai 1:2; 2 Chronicles 2:18"> And Iudah said, The strength of the bearers is weakened, and there is much earth, so that we are not able to build the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2; Acts 23:12; Psalms 56:6"> Also our aduersaries had sayde, They shall not knowe, neither see, till we come into the middes of them and slay them, and cause the worke to cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:7; Genesis 31:41; Numbers 14:22"> But when the Iewes (which dwelt beside them) came, they told vs ten times, From all places whence ye shall returne, they wil be vpon vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:17–18; Ephesians 6:11–20; Song of Solomon 3:7–8"> Therefore set I in the lower places behind the wall vpon the toppes of the stones, and placed the people by their families, with their swordes, their speares and their bowes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:12; Numbers 14:9; Isaiah 41:10–14"> Then I behelde, and rose vp, and said vnto the Princes, and to the rulers, and to the rest of the people, Be not afrayde of them: remember the great Lord, and fearefull, and fight for your brethren, your sonnes, and your daughters, your wiues, and your houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:14; Romans 12:11; Job 5:12–13"> And when our enemies heard that it was knowen vnto vs, then God brought their counsell to nought, and we turned all againe to the wall, euery one vnto his worke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:23; Psalms 101:6; Nehemiah 5:15–16"> And from that day, halfe of the yong men did the labour, and the other halfe part of them helde the speares, and shieldes, and bowes, and habergins: and the rulers stoode behinde all the house of Iudah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:7; 2 Timothy 4:7; 2 Timothy 2:3"> They that buylded on the wall, and they that bare burdens, and they that laded, did the worke with one hand, and with the other helde the sworde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 10:9; 2 Chronicles 13:12–17"> For euery one of the buylders had his sworde girded on his loynes, and so buylded: and he that blewe the trumpet, was beside me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then saide I vnto the Princes, and to the rulers, and to the rest of the people, The worke is great and large, and we are separated vpon the wall, one farre from another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Deuteronomy 1:30; Deuteronomy 20:4"> In what place therefore ye heare the sound of the trumpet, resort ye thither vnto vs: our God shall fight for vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; Colossians 1:29; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> So we laboured in the worke, and halfe of them helde the speares, from the appearing of the morning, till the starres came foorth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:1–2"> And at the same time said I vnto the people, Let euery one with his seruant lodge within Ierusalem, that they may be a watch for vs in the night, and labour in the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 9:48; Nehemiah 7:2; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> So neither I, nor my brethren, nor my seruants, nor the men of the warde, (which followed me) none of vs did put off our clothes, saue euery one put them off for washing.</span>
