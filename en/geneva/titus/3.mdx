<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 13:1–7; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> Pvt them in remembrance that they bee subiect to the Principalities and powers, and that they bee obedient, and ready to euery good woorke,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:31; 2 Timothy 2:24–25; Ephesians 4:2"> That they speake euill of no man, that they be no fighters, but soft, shewing all meekenesse vnto all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–3; Romans 6:17; Colossians 1:21"> For wee our selues also were in times past vnwise, disobedient, deceiued, seruing the lustes and diuers pleasures, liuing in maliciousnes and enuie, hatefull, and hating one another:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–10; Titus 2:10–11; 1 Timothy 4:10"> But when that bountifulnesse and that loue of God our Sauiour toward man appeared,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8–9; 2 Timothy 1:9; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> Not by the woorkes of righteousnesse, which we had done, but according to his mercie he saued vs, by the washing of the newe birth, and the renewing of the holy Ghost,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Romans 5:5; Acts 10:45"> Which he shed on vs aboundantly, through Iesus Christ our Sauiour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Titus 2:11; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> That we, being iustified by his grace, should be made heires according to the hope of eternall life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Titus 3:14; Titus 1:9; Psalms 16:2–3"> This is a true saying, and these thinges I will thou shouldest affirme, that they which haue beleeued God, might be carefull to shewe foorth good woorkes. These things are good and profitable vnto men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:14; 2 Timothy 2:23; 1 Timothy 4:7"> But stay foolish questions, and genealogies, and contentions, and brawlings about the Lawe: for they are vnprofitable and vaine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 John 1:10; Matthew 18:15–17"> Reiect him that is an heretike, after once or twise admonition,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26; Luke 19:22; Matthew 25:26–28"> Knowing that hee that is such, is peruerted, and sinneth, being damned of his owne selfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:9; 2 Timothy 4:12; 1 Corinthians 16:8–9"> When I shall send Artemas vnto thee, or Tychicus, be diligent to come to mee vnto Nicopolis: for I haue determined there to winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 18:24; Matthew 22:35; 3 John 1:6–8"> Bring Zenas the expounder of the Lawe, and Apollos on their iourney diligently, that they lacke nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Titus 3:8; Matthew 7:19"> And let ours also learne to shewe foorth good woorkes for necessary vses, that they be not vnfruitfull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Ephesians 6:23–24; 2 John 1:1–2"> All that are with mee, salute thee. Greete them that loue vs in the faith. Grace bee with you all, Amen. ‘To Titus, elect the first bishoppe of the Church of the Cretians, written from Nicopolis in Macedonia.’</span>
