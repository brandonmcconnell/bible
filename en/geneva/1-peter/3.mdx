<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:18; Ephesians 5:33; Ephesians 5:22–24"> Likewise let the wiues bee subiect to their husbands, that euen they which obey not the worde, may without the worde be wonne by the conuersation of the wiues,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 1:15; Philippians 1:27"> While they beholde your pure conuersation, which is with feare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9–10; Romans 12:2; Isaiah 3:18–24"> Whose apparelling, let it not be that outwarde, with broyded heare, and golde put about, or in putting on of apparell:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:12; 1 Samuel 16:7; Romans 2:29"> But let it bee the hidde man of the heart, which consisteth in the incorruption of a meeke and quiet spirite, which is before God a thing much set by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Titus 2:3–4; 1 Timothy 5:5; Luke 2:37"> For euen after this maner in time past did the holy women, which trusted in God, tire them selues, and were subiect to their husbands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:12; Romans 9:7–9; Isaiah 57:11"> As Sara obeyed Abraham, and called him Sir: whose daughters ye are, whiles yee doe well, not being afraide of any terrour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 3:19; Genesis 2:23–24; Matthew 19:3–9"> Likewise ye husbands, dwel with them as men of knowledge, giuing honour vnto the woman, as vnto the weaker vessell, euen as they which are heires together of the grace of life, that your prayers be not interrupted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 12:10; Ephesians 4:31–2; 1 Peter 1:22"> Finally, be ye all of one minde: one suffer with another: loue as brethren: bee pitifull: bee courteous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; Romans 12:17; Romans 12:14"> Not rendring euil for euill, neither rebuke for rebuke: but contrarywise blesse, knowing that ye are thereunto called, that ye should be heires of blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 34:12–16; James 1:26; 1 Peter 2:1"> For if any man long after life, and to see good dayes, let him refraine his tongue from euill, and his lippes that they speake no guile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Psalms 34:14; Hebrews 12:14"> Let him eschew euil, and do good: let him seeke peace, and follow after it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 34:15–16; John 9:31; Proverbs 15:29"> For the eyes of the Lord are ouer the righteous, and his eares are open vnto their prayers: and the face of the Lord is against them that do euil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Romans 8:28; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> And who is it that will harme you, if ye follow that which is good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; 1 Peter 2:19–20; Matthew 5:10–12"> Notwithstanding blessed are ye, if ye suffer for righteousnes sake. Yea, feare not their feare, neither be troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; 2 Timothy 2:25–26; Luke 21:14–15"> But sanctifie the Lord God in your hearts: and be ready alwayes to giue an answere to euery man that asketh you a reason of the hope that is in you, with meekenesse and reuerence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Hebrews 13:18; 1 Peter 2:15"> Hauing a good coscience, that whe they speake euill of you as of euill doers, they may be ashamed, which slander your good conuersation in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 4:19; Matthew 26:39"> For it is better (if the will of God be so) that ye suffer for well doing, then for euil doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Hebrews 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For Christ also hath once suffered for sinnes, the iust for the vniust, that he might bring vs to God, and was put to death concerning the flesh, but was quickened by the spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 4:6; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 42:7"> By the which hee also went, and preached vnto the spirits that are in prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5; Genesis 6:3"> Which were in time passed disobedient, when once the long suffering of God abode in the dayes of Noe, while the Arke was preparing, wherein fewe, that is, eight soules were saued in the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 16:16; Ezekiel 36:25–26; Acts 2:38"> Whereof the baptisme that nowe is, answering that figure, (which is not a putting away of the filth of the flesh, but a confident demaunding which a good conscience maketh to God) saueth vs also by the resurrection of Iesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 10:12"> Which is at the right hand of God, gone into heauen, to whom the Angels, and Powers, and might are subiect.</span>
