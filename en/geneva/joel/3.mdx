<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:19–20; Jeremiah 30:3; Ezekiel 39:25"> For beholde, in those dayes and in that time, when I shall bring againe the captiuitie of Iudah and Ierusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:2–4; Isaiah 66:16; Zephaniah 3:8"> I will also gather all nations, and wil bring them downe into the valley of Iehoshaphat, and will pleade with them there for my people, and for mine heritage Israel, whom they haue scattered among the nations, and parted my land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nahum 3:10; Obadiah 1:11; Amos 2:6"> And they haue cast lottes for my people, and haue giuen the childe for the harlot, and sold the girle for wine, that they might drinke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 1:6–10; Jeremiah 47:4; Isaiah 59:18"> Yea, and what haue you to do with me, O Tyrus and Zidon and all the costes of Palestina? will ye render me a recompence? and if ye recompence mee, swiftly and speedily will I render your recompence vpon your head:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:16–17; 2 Kings 18:15–16; 2 Kings 12:18"> For ye haue taken my siluer and my golde, and haue caried into your temples my goodly and pleasant things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:13; Joel 3:3; Deuteronomy 28:68"> The children also of Iudah and the children of Ierusalem haue you solde vnto the Grecians, that ye might send them farre from their border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5–6; Jeremiah 23:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:6"> Beholde, I will rayse them out of the place where ye haue sold them, and will render your reward vpon your owne head,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 60:14; Job 1:15; Isaiah 14:1–2"> And I will send your sonnes and your daughters into the hande of the children of Iudah, and they shall sell them to the Sabeans, to a people farre off: for the Lord hath spoken it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:3–4; Isaiah 8:9–10; Ezekiel 38:7"> Publish this among the Gentiles: prepare warre, wake vp the mightie men: let all the men of warre drawe neere and come vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 12:8; Micah 4:3; 2 Chronicles 25:8"> Breake your plowshares into swords, and your sithes into speares: let the weake say, I am strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 13:3; Zephaniah 3:8; Ezekiel 38:9–18"> Assemble your selues, and come all yee heathen and gather your selues together round about: there shall the Lord cast downe the mightie men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Isaiah 2:4; Psalms 96:13"> Let the heathen be wakened, and come vp to the valley of Iehoshaphat: for there will I sit to iudge all the heathen round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:15–20; Mark 4:29; Hosea 6:11"> Put in your sithes, for the haruest is ripe: come, get you downe, for the winepresse is full: yea, the winepresses runne ouer, for their wickednesse is great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Philippians 3:2; Isaiah 34:2–8"> O multitude, O multitude, come into the valley of threshing: for the day of the Lord is neere in the valley of threshing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 2:10; Matthew 24:29; Joel 2:31"> The sunne and moone shalbe darkened, and the starres shall withdrawe their light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 1:2; Joel 2:10–11; Haggai 2:6"> The Lord also shall roare out of Zion, and vtter his voyce from Ierusalem, and the heauens and the earth shall shake, but the Lord wil be the hope of his people, and the strength of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:27; Nahum 1:15; Isaiah 52:1"> So shall ye know that I am the Lord your God dwelling in Zion, mine holy Mountaine: then shall Ierusalem bee holy, and there shall no strangers go thorowe her any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:1–12; Zechariah 14:8; Revelation 22:1–2"> And in that day shall the mountaines drop downe newe wine, and the hilles shall flowe with milke, and al the riuers of Iudah shall runne with waters, and a fountaine shall come forth of the House of the Lord, and shall water the valley of Shittim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 19:1–15; Jeremiah 51:35; Zechariah 14:18–19"> Egypt shalbe waste, and Edom shall be a desolate wildernesse, for the iniuries of the childre of Iudah, because they haue shed innocent blood in their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:25; Amos 9:15; Isaiah 33:20"> But Iudah shall dwell for euer, and Ierusalem from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 4:4; Revelation 21:3; Ezekiel 36:25"> For I will clense their blood, that I haue not clensed, and the Lord will dwell in Zion.</span>
