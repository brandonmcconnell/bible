<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Amos 8:1; Joel 2:25"> Thus hath the Lord God shewed vnto mee, and beholde, he formed grashoppers in the beginning of ye shooting vp of the latter grouth: and loe, it was in the latter grouth after the Kings mowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:13; Isaiah 37:4; Jeremiah 42:2"> And when they had made an ende of eating the grasse of the land, then I saide, O Lord God, spare, I beseeche thee: who shall raise vp Iaakob? for he is small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; Jonah 3:10; Hosea 11:8"> So the Lord repented for this. It shall not be, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 7:1; Isaiah 66:15–16; Hebrews 1:7"> Thus also hath the Lord God shewed vnto me, and behold, the Lord God called to iudgement by fire, and it deuoured the great deepe, and did eate vp a part.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 85:4; Isaiah 1:9; Jeremiah 30:19"> Then said I, O Lord God, cease, I beseeche thee: who shall raise vp Iaakob? for he is small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 7:3; Judges 2:18; Psalms 90:13"> So the Lord repented for this. This also shall not be, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Revelation 21:15; Isaiah 28:17"> Thus againe he shewed me, and behold, the Lord stoode vpon a wall made by line with a line in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 8:2; Lamentations 2:8; Ezekiel 7:2–9"> And the Lord saide vnto me, Amos, what seest thou? And I said, A line. Then said the Lord, Beholde, I wil set a line in the middes of my people Israel, and wil passe by them no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 15:8–10; Genesis 46:1; Amos 8:14"> And the hye places of Izhak shalbe desolate, and the temples of Israel shalbe destroyed: and I wil rise against the house of Ieroboam with the sworde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:4; 1 Kings 12:31–32; Jeremiah 26:8–11"> Then Amaziah the Priest of Beth-el sent to Ieroboam King of Israel, saying, Amos hath conspired against thee in the middes of the house of Israel: the lande is not able to beare all his wordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 17:6; Psalms 56:5; Amos 6:7–8"> For thus Amos saith, Ieroboam shall die by the sworde, and Israel shalbe led away captiue out of their owne land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 8:34; 1 Samuel 9:9"> Also Amaziah sayde vnto Amos, O thou the Seer, goe, flee thou away into the land of Iudah, and there eate thy bread and prophecie there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 12:32; Amos 2:12; 1 Kings 12:29"> But prophecie no more at Beth-el: for it is the Kings chappel, and it is the Kings court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 1:1; 2 Kings 4:38; 2 Kings 2:5"> Then answered Amos, and said to Amaziah, I was no Prophet, neither was I a prophets sonne, but I was an herdman, and a gatherer of wilde figs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:8; Ezekiel 2:3–4; Jeremiah 1:7"> And the Lord tooke me as I followed the flocke, and the Lord said vnto me, Go, prophecie vnto my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46; Micah 2:6; Ezekiel 21:2"> Now therefore heare thou the word of the Lord. Thou sayest, Prophecie not against Israel, and speake nothing against the house of Izhak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 9:3; 2 Kings 17:6; Hosea 4:13–14"> Therefore thus sayth the Lord, Thy wife shall be an harlot in the citie, and thy sonnes and thy daughters shall fall by the sword, and thy land shall be deuided by line: and thou shalt die in a polluted land, and Israel shall surely go into captiuitie forth of his land.</span>
