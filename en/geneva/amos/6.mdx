<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 32:9–11; James 5:5; Luke 12:17–20"> Woe to them that are at ease in Zion and trust in the moutaine of Samaria, which were famous at the beginning of the nations: and the house of Israel came to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nahum 3:8; Genesis 10:10; 2 Kings 18:34"> Goe you vnto Calneh, and see: and from thence goe you to Hamath the great: then goe downe to Gath of the Philistims: be they better then these kingdomes? or the border of their land greater then your border,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 9:10; Isaiah 56:12; Ezekiel 12:27"> Ye that put farre away the euill day, and approch to the seate of iniquitie?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 3:12; Luke 16:19; 1 Samuel 25:36–38"> They lie vpon beddes of yuorie, and stretch themselues vpon their beddes, and eate the lambes of the flocke, and the calues out of the stall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:12; Amos 5:23; Amos 8:3"> They sing to the sounde of the viole: they inuent to themselues instruments of musike like Dauid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:22; 1 Corinthians 12:26; Matthew 26:7–9"> They drinke wine in bowles, and anoynt themselues with the chiefe ointments, but no man is sory for the affliction of Ioseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 7:11; Daniel 5:4–6; Amos 5:5"> Therefore nowe shall they go captiue with the first that go captiue, and the sorow of them that stretched themselues, is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:14; Amos 4:2; Amos 8:7"> The Lord God hath sworne by himselfe, saith the Lord God of hostes, I abhorre the excellencie of Iaakob, and hate his palaces: therefore wil I deliuer vp the citie with all that is therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:3; Job 1:2; Psalms 109:13"> And if there remaine ten men in one house, they shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 8:3; 1 Samuel 31:12; Amos 5:13"> And his vncle shall take him vp and burne him to cary out the bones out of the house, and shall say vnto him, that is by ye sides of the house, Is there yet any with thee? And he shall say, None. Then shall he say, Holde thy tongue: for we may not remember the Name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 3:15; Isaiah 55:11; 2 Kings 25:9"> For behold, the Lord commandeth, and he will smite the great house with breaches, and the litle house with clefts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 5:7; Hosea 10:4; Amos 5:11–12"> Shal horses runne vpon the rocke? or wil one plowe there with oxen? for yee haue turned iudgement into gall, and the fruite of righteousnes into wormewood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 28:14–15; Luke 12:19–20; 2 Kings 14:25"> Ye reioyce in a thing of nought: yee say, Haue not wee gotten vs hornes by our owne strength?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:65; 2 Kings 14:25; Jeremiah 5:15–17"> But behold, I wil raise vp against you a nation, O house of Israel, sayeth the Lord God of hostes: and they shall afflict you, from the entring in of Hamath vnto the riuer of the wildernes.</span>
