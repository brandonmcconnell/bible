<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 2:10; Jeremiah 8:3; Micah 3:1"> Heare this worde that the Lord pronounceth against you, O children of Israel, euen against the whole familie which I brought vp from the land of Egypt, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Exodus 19:5–6; Deuteronomy 10:15"> You onely haue I knowen of all the families of the earth: therefore I will visite you for all your iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–16; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 5:22"> Can two walke together except they bee agreed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 11:10; Psalms 104:21; Amos 1:2"> Will a lion roare in ye forest, when he hath no pray? or wil a lions whelpe cry out of his den, if he haue taken nothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:12; Jeremiah 31:28; Daniel 9:14"> Can a birde fall in a snare vpon the earth, where no fouler is? or will he take vp the snare from the earth, and haue taken nothing at all?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Zephaniah 1:16; Isaiah 14:24–27"> Or shall a trumpet be blowen in the citie, and the people be not afraide? or shall there be euil in a citie, and the Lord hath not done it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:15; Genesis 18:17; Psalms 25:14"> Surely the Lord God will doe nothing, but he reueileth his secrete vnto his seruantes the Prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 4:20; Jeremiah 20:9; Amos 3:4"> The lyon hath roared: who will not bee afraide? the Lord God hath spoken: who can but prophecie?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 4:1; Amos 6:1; Amos 1:8"> Proclayme in the palaces at Ashdod, and in the palaces in the lande of Egypt, and saye, Assemble your selues vpon the mountaines of Samaria: so beholde the great tumultes in the middes thereof, and the oppressed in the middes thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Zephaniah 1:9; Zechariah 5:3–4"> For they knowe not to doe right, sayth the Lord: they store vp violence, and robbery in their palaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 18:9–11; 2 Kings 17:3–6; 2 Kings 15:19"> Therefore thus saith the Lord God, An aduersary shall come euen rounde about the countrey, and shall bring downe thy strength from thee, and thy palaces shalbe spoyled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:34–37; Romans 11:4–5; Isaiah 31:4"> Thus saieth the Lord, As the shephearde taketh out of the mouth of the lyon two legges, or a piece of an eare: so shall the children of Israel be taken out that dwell in Samaria in the corner of a bed, and in Damascus, as in a couche.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:18–19; 2 Chronicles 24:19; Acts 20:21"> Heare, and testifie in the house of Iaakob, saith the Lord God, the God of hostes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 23:15; Hosea 10:5–8; Hosea 10:14–15"> Surely in the day that I shall visit the transgressions of Israel vpon him, I wil also visite the altars of Beth-el, and the hornes of the altar shall be broken off, and fall to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 22:39; Jeremiah 36:22; Amos 6:11"> And I wil smite the winter house with the sommer house, and the houses of yuorie shall perish, and the great houses shalbe consumed, sayth the Lord.</span>
