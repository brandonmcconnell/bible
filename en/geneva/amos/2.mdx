<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:8–9; Ezekiel 25:8–9; Amos 1:3"> Thus sayth the Lord, For three transgressions of Moab, and for foure, I will not turne to it, because it burnt the bones of the King of Edom into lime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:24; Jeremiah 48:41; Amos 1:14"> Therefore will I send a fire vpon Moab, and it shall deuoure the palaces of Kerioth, and Moab shall die with tumult, with shouting, and with the sound of a trumpet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; Jeremiah 48:7; Jeremiah 48:25"> And I will cut off the iudge out of the mids thereof, and will slay all the princes thereof with him, sayth the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:24; Ezekiel 20:16; Ezekiel 20:13"> Thus saith the Lord, For three transgressions of Iudah, and for foure, I will not turne to it, because they haue cast away the Lawe of the Lord, and haue not kept his commandementes, and their lies caused them to erre after the which their fathers haue walked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 8:14; Jeremiah 17:27; Jeremiah 21:10"> Therefore will I send a fire vpon Iudah, and it shall deuoure the palaces of Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 3:3; Micah 3:2–3; Amos 5:11–12"> Thus sayth the Lord, For three transgressions of Israel, and for foure, I will not turne to it, because they solde the righteous for siluer and the poore for shooes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:12; Isaiah 10:2; Ezekiel 22:11"> They gape ouer the head of the poore, in the dust of the earth, and peruert the wayes of the meeke: and a man and his father will goe in to a mayde to dishonour mine holy Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:12–17; Exodus 22:26–27; Amos 6:6"> And they lye downe vpon clothes layde to pledge by euery altar: and they drinke the wine of the condemned in the house of their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 18:16; Malachi 4:1; Deuteronomy 3:11"> Yet destroyed I the Amorite before the, whose height was like the height of the cedars, and he was strong as the okes: notwithstanding I destroyed his fruite from aboue, and his roote from beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:7; Exodus 12:51; Amos 3:1"> Also I brought you vp from the land of Egypt, and led you fourtie yeres thorowe the wildernesse, to possesse the land of the Amorite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:25; Numbers 6:2–3; 1 Kings 20:41"> And I raysed vp of your sonnes for Prophets, and of your yong men for Nazarites. Is it not euen thus, O ye children of Israel, sayth the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 7:13; Isaiah 30:10; Jeremiah 11:21"> But ye gaue the Nazarites wine to drinke, and commanded the Prophetes, saying, Prophecie not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:14; Isaiah 43:24; Ezekiel 16:43"> Behold, I am pressed vnder you as a cart is pressed that is full of sheaues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; Psalms 33:16; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> Therefore the flight shall perish from the swift, and the strong shall not strengthen his force, neither shall the mightie saue his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 33:16–17; Ezekiel 39:3"> Nor he that handleth the bowe, shall stand, and he that is swift of foote, shall not escape, neyther shall he that rideth the horse, saue his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:52; Jeremiah 48:41; 2 Kings 7:8–20"> And he that is of a mighty courage among the strong men, shall flee away naked in that day, sayth the Lord.</span>
