<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Bvt Iob answered and sayd,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 32:4; Job 7:11"> Though my talke be this day in bitternes, and my plague greater then my groning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 16:21; Jeremiah 14:7; Job 13:3"> Would God yet I knew how to finde him, I would enter vnto his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 13:18; Daniel 9:18–19; Exodus 32:12–13"> I would pleade the cause before him, and fill my mouth with arguments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 13:22–23; Job 10:2; 1 Corinthians 4:3–4"> I would knowe the wordes, that he would answere me, and would vnderstand what he would say vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 13:21; Job 9:33–34; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Would he plead against me with his great power? No, but he would put strength in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 13:3; Romans 8:1; Isaiah 1:18"> There the righteous might reason with him, so I shoulde be deliuered for euer from my Iudge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 10:1; Job 9:11; Job 35:14"> Behold, if I go to the East, he is not there: if to the West, yet I can not perceiue him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Isaiah 8:17"> If to the North where he worketh, yet I cannot see him: he wil hide himselfe in the South, and I cannot beholde him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; James 1:2–4; 1 Peter 1:7"> But he knoweth my way, and trieth mee, and I shall come forth like the gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:18; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Job 17:9"> My foote hath followed his steps: his way haue I kept, and haue not declined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Psalms 119:103; Psalms 119:11"> Neyther haue I departed from the commandement of his lippes, and I haue esteemed the words of his mouth more then mine appointed foode.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 115:3; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 46:10"> Yet he is in one minde, and who can turne him? yea, he doeth what his minde desireth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:9; Romans 11:33"> For he will performe that, which is decreed of me, and many such things are with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 77:3; Job 31:23"> Therefore I am troubled at his presence, and in considering it, I am afraid of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 22:14; Job 27:2; Psalms 88:16"> For God hath softened mine heart, and the Almightie hath troubled me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 19:8; Isaiah 57:1; 2 Kings 22:20"> For I am not cut off in darknesse, but he hath hid the darkenesse from my face.</span>
