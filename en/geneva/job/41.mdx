<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; Revelation 5:2–3; Jeremiah 9:23"> His heart is as strong as a stone, and as hard as the nether milstone.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> When the sword doeth touch him, he will not rise vp, nor for the speare, dart nor habergeon.</span>
