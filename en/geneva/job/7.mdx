<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 39:4; Job 14:5–6; Job 5:7"> Is there not an appointed time to man vpon earth? and are not his dayes as the dayes of an hyreling?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Deuteronomy 24:15; James 5:4"> As a seruant longeth for the shadowe, and as an hyreling looketh for the ende of his worke,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Psalms 39:5"> So haue I had as an inheritance the moneths of vanitie, and painefull nights haue bene appointed vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:67; Job 7:13–14; Job 17:12"> If I layed me downe, I sayde, When shall I arise? and measuring the euening I am euen full with tossing to and from vnto the dawning of the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 17:14; Isaiah 14:11; Job 2:7–8"> My flesh is clothed with wormes and filthinesse of the dust: my skinne is rent, and become horrible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 9:25; Job 17:15; Job 17:11"> My dayes are swifter then a weauers shittle, and they are spent without hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalms 78:39; Jeremiah 15:15"> Remember that my life is but a wind, and that mine eye shall not returne to see pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 20:9; Job 8:18; Psalms 37:36"> The eye that hath seene me, shall see me no more: thine eyes are vpon me, and I shall be no longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 30:15; Job 10:21; 2 Samuel 14:14"> As the cloude vanisheth and goeth away, so he that goeth downe to the graue, shall come vp no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 8:18; Job 20:9; Psalms 103:16"> He shall returne no more to his house, neither shall his place knowe him any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 40:9; 1 Samuel 1:10; Job 10:1"> Therefore I will not spare my mouth, but will speake in the trouble of my spirite, and muse in the bitternesse of my minde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:2–3; Lamentations 3:7; Job 38:6–11"> Am I a sea or a whalefish, that thou keepest me in warde?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Psalms 77:4; Job 9:27–28"> When I say, My couch shall relieue me, and my bed shall bring comfort in my meditation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 2:1; Genesis 41:8; Genesis 40:5–7"> Then fearest thou me with dreames, and astonishest me with visions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 27:5; 2 Samuel 17:23"> Therefore my soule chuseth rather to be strangled and to die, then to be in my bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Job 10:1; Job 9:21"> I abhorre it, I shall not liue alway: spare me then, for my dayes are but vanitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 2:6; Psalms 8:4; Psalms 144:3"> What is man, that thou doest magnifie him, and that thou settest thine heart vpon him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 38:12–13; Jeremiah 9:7; 1 Peter 1:7"> And doest visite him euery morning, and tryest him euery moment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:18; Job 14:6; Psalms 13:1–3"> Howe long will it be yer thou depart from me? thou wilt not let me alone whiles I may swallowe my spettle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 3:12; Job 7:11–12; Job 3:24"> I haue sinned, what shall I do vnto thee? O thou preseruer of me, why hast thou set me as a marke against thee, so that I am a burden vnto my selfe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Job 10:14; 1 John 1:9"> And why doest thou not pardon my trespasse? and take away mine iniquitie? for nowe shall I sleepe in the dust, and if thou seekest me in the morning, I shall not be found.</span>
