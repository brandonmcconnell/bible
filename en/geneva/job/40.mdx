<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 38:1; Job 40:6"> Againe the Lord answered Iob out of the whirle winde, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:14; Job 33:13"> Girde vp now thy loynes like a man: I will demaunde of thee, and declare thou vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Wilt thou disanul my iudgement? or wilt thou condemne me, that thou mayst be iustified?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:32; Job 29:9; Job 21:5"> Or hast thou an arme like God? or doest thou thunder with a voyce like him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 33:14; Job 9:3; Job 9:15"> Decke thy selfe now with maiestie and excellencie, and aray thy selfe with beautie and glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 38:1; 2 Peter 3:10–12; Psalms 50:3–4"> Cast abroad the indignation of thy wrath, and beholde euery one that is proude, and abase him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:3; Job 42:4; Job 23:3–4"> Looke on euery one that is arrogant, and bring him lowe: and destroy the wicked in their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:4; Isaiah 28:18; Job 32:2"> Hide them in the dust together, and binde their faces in a secret place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:13; Job 37:4–5; Isaiah 45:9"> Then will I confesse vnto thee also, that thy right hand can saue thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 93:1; Psalms 149:4; Isaiah 59:17"> Behold now Behemoth (whom I made with thee) which eateth grasse as an oxe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 4:37; Isaiah 2:11–12; Isaiah 2:17"> Behold now, his strength is in his loynes, and his force is in the nauil of his belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 63:3; Malachi 4:3; Psalms 60:12"> When hee taketh pleasure, his taile is like a cedar: the sinews of his stones are wrapt together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 2:10; Esther 7:8; Job 14:13"> His bones are like staues of brasse, and his small bones like staues of yron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–9; Romans 5:6; Psalms 44:6"> He is the chiefe of the wayes of God: he that made him, will make his sworde to approch vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 40:20; Psalms 104:14; Genesis 1:24–26"> Surely the mountaines bring him foorth grasse, where all the beastes of the fielde play.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Lyeth hee vnder the trees in the couert of the reede and fennes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 41:23"> Can the trees couer him with their shadow? or can the willowes of the riuer compasse him about?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 48:4; Job 7:12"> Behold, he spoyleth the riuer, and hasteth not: he trusteth that he can draw vp Iorden into his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 26:13; Isaiah 27:1"> Hee taketh it with his eyes, and thrusteth his nose through whatsoeuer meeteth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 104:26; Psalms 104:14; Job 40:15"> Canst thou drawe out Liuiathan with an hooke, and with a line which thou shalt cast downe vnto his tongue?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 35:7; Isaiah 19:6–7"> Canst thou cast an hooke into his nose? canst thou perce his iawes with an angle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:5; Leviticus 23:40; Isaiah 15:7"> Will he make many prayers vnto thee, or speake thee faire?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Isaiah 37:25; Isaiah 28:16"> Will hee make a couenant with thee? and wilt thou take him as a seruant for euer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 41:1–2"> Wilt thou play with him as with a bird? or wilt thou bynd him for thy maydes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Shall the companions baket with him? shall they deuide him among the marchants?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Canst thou fill the basket with his skinne? or the fishpanier with his head?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Lay thine hand vpon him: remember the battel, and do no more so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Behold, his hope is in vaine: for shall not one perish euen at the sight of him?</span>
