<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Elihu spake moreouer, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:36–37; Job 19:6–7; Job 16:17"> Thinkest thou this right, that thou hast said, I am more righteous then God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 34:9; Malachi 3:14; Job 9:30–31"> For thou hast said, What profiteth it thee and what auaileth it me, to purge me from my sinne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Job 34:8"> Therefore will I answere thee, and thy companions with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 22:12; Isaiah 55:9; Nahum 1:3"> Looke vnto the heauen, and see and behold the cloudes which are hyer then thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:19; Proverbs 8:36; Proverbs 9:12"> If thou sinnest, what doest thou against him, yea, when thy sinnes be many, what doest thou vnto him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 22:2–3; Psalms 16:2; Luke 17:10"> If thou be righteous, what giuest thou vnto him? or what receiueth he at thine hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Job 42:8; Acts 27:24"> Thy wickednesse may hurt a man as thou art: and thy righteousnes may profite ye sonne of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:23; Job 34:28; Exodus 3:7"> They cause many that are oppressed, to crye, which crye out for ye violence of the mightie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Acts 16:25; Psalms 149:5"> But none saieth, Where is God that made me, which giueth songs in the nyght?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 94:12; Job 36:22; Job 32:8"> Which teacheth vs more then the beastes of the earth, and giueth vs more wisdome then the foules of the heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; John 9:31; Psalms 123:3–4"> Then they crye because of the violence of the wicked, but he answereth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; James 4:3"> Surely God will not heare vanitie, neyther will the Almightie regard it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 9:11; Micah 7:7–9; Psalms 37:5–6"> Although thou sayest to God, Thou wilt not regard it, yet iudgement is before him: trust thou in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 89:32; Job 9:14; Job 13:15"> But nowe because his anger hath not visited, nor called to count the euill with great extremitie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 38:2; Job 33:8–12; Job 34:35–37"> Therfore Iob openeth his mouth in vaine, and multiplieth wordes without knowledge.</span>
