<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Bvt Iob answered, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 13:4–5; Psalms 69:26; Job 11:2–3"> I haue oft times heard such things: miserable comforters are ye all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 15:2; Titus 2:8"> Shall there be none ende of wordes of winde? or what maketh thee bold so to answere?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 109:25; Psalms 22:7; Lamentations 2:15"> I could also speake as yee doe: (but woulde God your soule were in my soules stead) I could keepe you company in speaking, and could shake mine head at you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 27:9; Galatians 6:1; Isaiah 35:3–4"> But I woulde strengthen you with my mouth, and the comfort of my lips should asswage your sorowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 88:15–18; Psalms 77:1–9"> Though I speake, my sorow can not be asswaged: though I cease, what release haue I?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:3; Job 1:15–19; Psalms 6:6–7"> But now hee maketh mee wearie: O God, thou hast made all my congregation desolate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 10:17; Job 19:20; Ruth 1:21"> And hast made me full of wrinkles which is a witnesse thereof, and my leannes ryseth vp in me, testifying the same in my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 35:16; Job 13:24; Lamentations 2:16"> His wrath hath torne me, and hee hateth me, and gnasheth vpon mee with his teeth: mine enemie hath sharpened his eyes against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:15; Psalms 22:13; Isaiah 50:6"> They haue opened their mouthes vpon me, and smitten me on the cheeke in reproch; they gather themselues together against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:12; Psalms 7:14; Psalms 31:8"> God hath deliuered me to the vniust, and hath made mee to turne out of the way by the hands of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 7:20; Lamentations 3:11–12; Lamentations 3:4"> I was in welth, but he hath brought me to nought: he hath taken me by the necke, and beaten me, and set me as a marke for himselfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 20:25; Job 6:4; Lamentations 2:11"> His archers compasse mee rounde about: he cutteth my reines, and doth not spare, and powreth my gall vpon the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 15:8; Lamentations 3:3–5; Joel 2:7"> He hath broken me with one breaking vpon another, and runneth vpon me like a gyant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 37:34; Psalms 7:5; Psalms 75:5"> I haue sowed a sackcloth vpon my skinne, and haue abased mine horne vnto the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:34; Psalms 102:3–5; Lamentations 1:16"> My face is withered with weeping, and the shadow of death is vpon mine eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; Psalms 66:18–19; Job 27:6–7"> Though there be no wickednesse in mine hands, and my prayer be pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Ezekiel 24:7; Genesis 4:11"> O earth, couer not thou my blood, and let my crying finde no place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 11:31"> For lo, now my witnesse is in the heauen, and my record is on hie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 142:2; Job 16:4"> My friends speake eloquently against me: but mine eye powreth out teares vnto God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:10; Isaiah 45:9; Romans 9:20"> Oh that a man might pleade with God, as man with his neighbour!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 14:10; Job 14:14; Job 14:5"> For the yeeres accounted come, and I shall go the way, whence I shall not returne.</span>
