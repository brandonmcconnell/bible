<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Bvt Iob answered, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 64:3; Job 18:2; Job 27:2"> Howe long will yee vexe my soule, and torment me with wordes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 31:7; Nehemiah 4:12; Job 18:4–21"> Ye haue now ten times reproched me, and are not ashamed: ye are impudent toward mee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Proverbs 9:12; Ezekiel 18:4"> And though I had in deede erred, mine errour remaineth with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Psalms 38:16; Psalms 55:12"> But in deede if ye will aduance your selues against me, and rebuke me for my reproche,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 27:2; Job 16:11–14"> Know nowe, that God hath ouerthrowen me, and hath compassed me with his net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:2–3; Lamentations 3:8; Job 31:35–36"> Beholde, I crie out of violence, but I haue none answere: I crie, but there is no iudgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 3:7; Job 3:23; Proverbs 4:19"> Hee hath hedged vp my way that I cannot passe, and he hath set darkenesse in my paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:44; Job 29:7–14; Psalms 89:39"> Hee hath spoyled mee of mine honour, and taken the crowne away from mine head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 24:20; Psalms 37:35–36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> He hath destroyed mee on euery side and I am gone: and he hath remoued mine hope like a tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:24; Job 16:9; Job 33:10"> And he hath kindled his wrath against me, and counteth mee as one of his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 30:12; Job 16:13; Isaiah 51:23"> His armies came together, and made their way vpon me, and camped about my tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 88:8; Psalms 88:18; Psalms 69:8"> He hath remooued my brethre farre from me, and also mine acquaintance were strangers vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Jeremiah 20:10; 2 Samuel 16:23"> My neighbours haue forsaken me, and my familiars haue forgotten me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 31:31–32; Psalms 123:3"> They that dwel in mine house, and my maydes tooke me for a stranger: for I was a stranger in their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 1:15–17; Job 1:19"> I called my seruant, but he would not answere, though I prayed him with my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 2:9–10; Job 17:1"> My breath was strange vnto my wife, though I prayed her for the childrens sake of mine owne body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 2:23; Job 30:12; Job 30:1"> The wicked also despised mee, and when I rose, they spake against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Psalms 109:4–5; Psalms 55:12–14"> All my secret friends abhorred me, and they whome I loued, are turned against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 102:5; Lamentations 4:8; Job 33:19–22"> My bone cleaueth to my skinne and to my flesh, and I haue escaped with the skinne of my teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:11; Psalms 38:2; Job 2:5"> Haue pitie vpon me: haue pitie vpon me, (O yee my friendes) for the hande of God hath touched me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 69:26; Job 31:31; Job 16:13–14"> Why do ye persecute me, as God? and are not satisfied with my flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 30:8; Job 31:35; Isaiah 8:1"> Oh that my wordes were nowe written! oh that they were written euen in a booke,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:1; Exodus 28:21; Exodus 32:16"> And grauen with an yron pen in lead, or in stone for euer!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Isaiah 54:5; Psalms 78:35"> For I am sure, that my Redeemer liueth, and he shall stand the last on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; 1 John 3:2; Psalms 17:15"> And though after my skin wormes destroy this bodie, yet shall I see God in my flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Isaiah 26:19; Numbers 24:17"> Whome I my selfe shall see, and mine eyes shall beholde, and none other for me, though my reynes are consumed within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 19:22; Psalms 69:26; 1 Kings 14:13"> But yee sayde, Why is hee persecuted? And there was a deepe matter in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Psalms 58:10–11"> Be ye afraide of the sworde: for the sworde will be auenged of wickednesse, that yee may knowe that there is a iudgement.</span>
