<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 12:4; Psalms 35:15–16; Psalms 69:12"> Bvt now they that are yonger then I, mocke me: yea, they whose fathers I haue refused to set with the dogges of my flockes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For whereto shoulde the strength of their handes haue serued mee, seeing age perished in them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 24:5; Hebrews 11:38; Job 24:13–16"> For pouertie and famine they were solitary, fleeing into the wildernes, which is darke, desolate and waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:16; 2 Kings 4:38–39; Amos 7:14"> They cut vp nettels by the bushes, and the iuniper rootes was their meate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Daniel 4:32–33; Psalms 109:10"> They were chased forth from among men: they shouted at them, as at a theefe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1–2; Judges 6:2; Isaiah 2:19"> Therfore they dwelt in the clefts of riuers, in the holes of the earth and rockes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 6:5; Job 11:12; Genesis 16:12"> They roared among the bushes, and vnder the thistles they gathered themselues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 16:22; Job 40:4; Isaiah 32:6"> They were the children of fooles and the children of villaines, which were more vile then the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Lamentations 3:63; Job 17:6; Lamentations 3:14"> And now am I their song, and I am their talke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 50:6; Numbers 12:14"> They abhorre me, and flee farre from mee, and spare not to spit in my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:21; Ruth 1:21; Job 12:18"> Because that God hath loosed my corde and humbled mee, they haue loosed the bridle before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 19:12; Isaiah 3:5; Job 19:18"> The youth rise vp at my right hand: they haue pusht my feete, and haue trode on me as on the paths of their destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:12; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 69:26"> They haue destroyed my paths: they tooke pleasure at my calamitie, they had none helpe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Psalms 18:4; Psalms 69:14–15"> They came as a great breach of waters, and vnder this calamitie they come on heapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 13:3; Job 6:4; Isaiah 44:22"> Feare is turned vpon mee: and they pursue my soule as the winde, and mine health passeth away as a cloude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Psalms 22:14; Isaiah 53:12"> Therefore my soule is nowe powred out vpon me, and the dayes of affliction haue taken holde on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 7:4; Job 33:19–21; Isaiah 38:13"> It pearceth my bones in the night, and my sinewes take no rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:5; Job 19:20; Job 2:7"> For the great vehemencie is my garment changed, which compasseth me about as the colar of my coate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:31; Job 42:6; Genesis 18:27"> He hath cast me into the myre, and I am become like ashes and dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 19:7; Psalms 80:4–5; Psalms 22:2"> Whe I cry vnto thee, thou doest not heare me, neither regardest me, when I stand vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 10:14–17; Job 16:9–14; Job 19:6–9"> Thou turnest thy selfe cruelly against me, and art enemie vnto mee with the strength of thine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 27:21; Psalms 1:4; Hosea 4:19"> Thou takest me vp and causest mee to ride vpon the winde, and makest my strength to faile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5–7; Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> Surely I knowe that thou wilt bring mee to death, and to the house appoynted for all the liuing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Matthew 27:39–44; Job 19:7"> Doubtles none can stretch his hand vnto the graue, though they cry in his destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalms 35:13–14; Jeremiah 13:17"> Did not I weepe with him that was in trouble? was not my soule in heauinesse for the poore?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:15; Job 3:25–26; Jeremiah 14:19"> Yet when I looked for good, euill came vnto me: and when I waited for light, there came darkenesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Jeremiah 31:20; Jeremiah 4:19"> My bowels did boyle without rest: for the dayes of affliction are come vpon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 42:9; Psalms 38:6; Psalms 43:2"> I went mourning without sunne: I stood vp in the congregation and cryed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Micah 1:8; Psalms 102:6; Psalms 44:19"> I am a brother to the dragons, and a companion to the ostriches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 4:8; Psalms 102:3; Psalms 119:83"> My skinne is blacke vpon me, and my bones are burnt with heate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 5:15; Isaiah 21:4; Isaiah 22:12"> Therefore mine harpe is turned to mourning, and mine organs into the voyce of them that weepe.</span>
