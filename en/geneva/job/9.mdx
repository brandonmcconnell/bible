<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Iob answered, and sayd,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 4:17; Romans 3:20; Job 25:4"> I knowe verily that it is so: for howe should man compared vnto God, be iustified?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 10:2; Job 40:2; Isaiah 57:15–16"> If I would dispute with him, hee could not answere him one thing of a thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 36:5; Romans 11:33; Ephesians 3:20"> He is wise in heart, and mighty in strength: who hath bene fierce against him and hath prospered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Luke 21:11; Habakkuk 3:10"> He remoueth the mountaines, and they feele not when he ouerthroweth them in his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 2:6; Hebrews 12:26; Psalms 75:3"> Hee remooueth the earth out of her place, that the pillars thereof doe shake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Amos 8:9; Luke 21:25–26"> He commandeth the sunne, and it riseth not: hee closeth vp the starres, as vnder a signet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Isaiah 40:22; Psalms 104:2–3"> Hee himselfe alone spreadeth out the heauens, and walketh vpon the height of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Genesis 1:16; Job 38:31–41"> He maketh the starres Arcturus, Orion, and Pleiades, and the climates of the South.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 5:9; Psalms 72:18; Ephesians 3:20"> He doeth great things, and vnsearcheable: yea, marueilous things without nomber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 23:8–9; Job 35:14; Psalms 77:19"> Lo, when he goeth by me, I see him not: and when he passeth by, I perceiue him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 45:9; Job 11:10; Job 23:13"> Behold, when he taketh a pray, who can make him to restore it? who shall say vnto him, What doest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 89:10; Job 26:12; Isaiah 30:7"> God will not withdrawe his anger, and the most mightie helpes doe stoupe vnder him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:27; Job 9:3; Job 4:19"> Howe much lesse shall I answere him? or howe should I finde out my words with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 10:15; Job 8:5; Job 10:2"> For though I were iust, yet could I not answere, but I would make supplication to my Iudge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 29:24; Exodus 6:9; Psalms 66:18–20"> If I cry, and he answere me, yet woulde I not beleeue, that he heard my voyce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 16:14; Job 2:3; Job 34:6"> For he destroyeth mee with a tempest, and woundeth me without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:19; Lamentations 3:15; Psalms 88:15–18"> He wil not suffer me to take my breath, but filleth me with bitternesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:32–33; Job 9:4; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> If we speake of strength, behold, he is strog: if we speake of iudgement, who shall bring me in to pleade?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:5–6; Philippians 3:12–15; Psalms 143:2"> If I woulde iustifie my selfe, mine owne mouth shall condemne mee: if I would be perfite, he shall iudge me wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; Psalms 139:23–24; Proverbs 28:26"> Though I were perfite, yet I knowe not my soule: therefore abhorre I my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:1–3; Luke 13:2–4; Job 10:8"> This is one point: therefore I said, Hee destroyeth the perfite and the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 24:12; Job 1:13–19; Job 4:7"> If the scourge should suddenly slay, should God laugh at the punishment of the innocent?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 10:3; Jeremiah 12:1–2; Psalms 73:3–7"> The earth is giuen into the hand of ye wicked: he couereth the faces of the iudges therof: if not, where is he? or who is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 7:6–7; James 4:14; Psalms 90:9–10"> My dayes haue bene more swift then a post: they haue fled, and haue seene no good thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Isaiah 18:2; Job 39:27–30"> They are passed as with the most swift ships, and as the eagle that flyeth to the pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 7:13; Jeremiah 8:18; Job 7:11"> If I say, I wil forget my complaynt, I will cease from my wrath, and comfort mee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 88:15–16; Job 21:6"> Then I am afrayd of all my sorowes, knowing that thou wilt not iudge me innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 21:27; Job 10:14–17"> If I be wicked, why labour I thus in vaine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; Psalms 26:6; 1 John 1:8"> If I wash my selfe with snowe water, and purge mine hands most cleane,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; Isaiah 59:6; Job 15:6"> Yet shalt thou plunge mee in the pit, and mine owne clothes shall make me filthie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 9:20; Ecclesiastes 6:10; Psalms 143:2"> For he is not a man as I am, that I shoulde answere him, if we come together to iudgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:25; 1 John 2:1–2; Job 9:19"> Neyther is there any vmpire that might lay his hand vpon vs both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 39:10; Job 13:11; Job 33:7"> Let him take his rod away from me, and let not his feare astonish me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Then will I speake, and feare him not: but because I am not so, I holde me still.</span>
