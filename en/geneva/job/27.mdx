<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 13:7; 2 Corinthians 11:10; John 8:55"> My lips surely shall speake no wickednesse, and my tongue shall vtter no deceite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 2:9; Job 13:15; Job 42:7"> God forbid, that I should iustifie you: vntill I dye, I will neuer take away mine innocencie from my selfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 2:3; 1 John 3:20–21; Acts 24:16"> I will keepe my righteousnesse, and wil not forsake it: mine heart shall not reprooue me of my dayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 4:19; 1 Samuel 25:26; 2 Samuel 18:32"> Mine enemie shall be as the wicked, and he that riseth against me, as the vnrighteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Job 8:13; Job 11:20"> For what hope hath the hypocrite when he hath heaped vp riches, if God take away his soule?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 1:28; Jeremiah 14:12"> Will God heare his cry, when trouble commeth vpon him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 22:26–27; Psalms 37:4; Habakkuk 3:18"> Will he set his delight on the Almightie? will he call vpon God at all times?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 71:17; Isaiah 8:11; Job 4:3–4"> I will teache you what is in the hande of God, and I wil not conceale that which is with the Almightie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 21:3; Job 16:3; Job 19:2–3"> Beholde, all ye your selues haue seene it: why then doe you thus vanish in vanitie?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 20:19–29; Job 15:20–35; Isaiah 3:11"> This is the portion of a wicked man with God, and the heritage of tyrants, which they shall receiue of the Almightie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:41; Job 20:10; Luke 23:29"> If his children be in great nomber, the sworde shall destroy them, and his posteritie shall not be satisfied with bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 78:64; Jeremiah 22:18; 1 Kings 14:10–11"> His remnant shall be buried in death, and his widowes shall not weepe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 9:3; Matthew 6:19; Habakkuk 2:6"> Though he shoulde heape vp siluer as the dust, and prepare rayment as the clay,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 13:22; Ecclesiastes 2:26; Proverbs 28:8"> He may prepare it, but the iust shall put it on, and the innocent shall deuide the siluer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 1:8; Job 8:14–15; Isaiah 51:8"> He buildeth his house as the moth, and as a lodge that the watchman maketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 24:24; Psalms 58:9; Psalms 73:19–20"> When the rich man sleepeth, he shall not be gathered to his fathers: they opened their eyes, and he was gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:21; Job 18:11; Job 21:18"> Terrours shall take him as waters, and a tempest shall cary him away by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Psalms 83:15; Psalms 58:9"> The East winde shall take him away, and he shall depart: and it shall hurle him out of his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4–5; Ezekiel 24:14; Job 11:20"> And God shall cast vpon him and not spare, though he would faine flee out of his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Lamentations 2:15; 1 Kings 9:8; Zephaniah 2:15"> Euery man shall clap their hands at him, and hisse at him out of their place.</span>
