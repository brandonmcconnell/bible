<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:6; Deuteronomy 24:10–13; 1 Samuel 12:3"> They leade away the asse of the fatherles: and take the widowes oxe to pledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 28:28; Job 24:14; Ezekiel 22:29"> They make the poore to turne out of the way, so that the poore of the earth hide themselues together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Job 39:5–7; Psalms 104:23"> Behold, others as wilde asses in the wildernesse, goe forth to their businesse, and rise early for a praye: the wildernesse giueth him and his children foode.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:51; Deuteronomy 28:33"> They reape his prouision in the fielde, but they gather the late vintage of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 22:26–27; Job 22:6; Deuteronomy 24:11–13"> They cause the naked to lodge without garment, and without couering in the colde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 4:5; Song of Solomon 5:2; Hebrews 11:38"> They are wet with the showres of the moutaines, and they imbrace the rocke for want of a couering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; 2 Kings 4:1"> They plucke the fatherles from the breast, and take the pledge of the poore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 2:7–8; Deuteronomy 24:19; Amos 5:11–12"> They cause him to go naked without clothing, and take the glening from the hungrie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 5:4; Jeremiah 22:13; Deuteronomy 25:4"> They that make oyle betweene their walles, and treade their wine presses, suffer thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Malachi 2:17; Romans 2:4–5"> Men cry out of the citie, and the soules of the slayne cry out: yet God doth not charge them with follie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:19–20; Proverbs 4:19; Jude 1:6"> These are they, that abhorre the light: they know not the wayes thereof, nor continue in the paths thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 2:1–2; Psalms 10:8–10; Revelation 3:3"> The murtherer riseth earely and killeth the poore and the needie: and in the night he is as a theefe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 10:11; Proverbs 7:9–10; Psalms 94:7"> The eye also of the adulterer waiteth for the twilight, and sayth, None eye shall see me, and disguiseth his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 3:20; Ephesians 5:11–13; Matthew 24:43"> They digge through houses in the darke, which they marked for themselues in the daye: they knowe not the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 3:5; Jeremiah 2:26; Psalms 73:18–19"> But the morning is euen to them as the shadow of death: if one knowe them, they are in the terrours of the shadowe of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 69:22; Psalms 58:7; Job 9:26"> He is swift vpon the waters: their portion shalbe cursed in the earth: he will not behold the way of the vineyardes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 49:14; Luke 16:22; Proverbs 14:32"> As the dry ground and heate consume the snowe waters, so shall the graue the sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Daniel 4:14; Job 18:16–17"> The pitifull man shall forget him: the worme shall feele his sweetenes: he shalbe no more remembered, and the wicked shalbe broke like a tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:6–7; Job 22:9; Job 29:13"> He doth euil intreat ye barren, that doeth not beare, neither doeth he good to the widowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 6:4–9; Esther 3:8–10; John 19:12–16"> He draweth also the mighty by his power, and when he riseth vp, none is sure of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Revelation 2:23"> Though men giue him assurance to be in safetie, yet his eyes are vpon their wayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 37:10; Psalms 37:35–36; James 5:1–3"> They are exalted for a litle, but they are gone, and are brought lowe as all others: they are destroyed, and cut off as the toppe of an eare of corne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:24; Job 11:2–3; Job 6:28"> But if it be not so, where is he? or who wil proue me a lyer, and make my words of no value?</span>
