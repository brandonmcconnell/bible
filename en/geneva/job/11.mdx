<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 20:1"> Then answered Zophar the Naamathite, and sayde,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 10:19; James 1:19; Job 18:2"> Should not the multitude of wordes be answered? or should a great talker be iustified?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 24:25; Titus 2:8"> Should men holde their peace at thy lyes? and when thou mockest others, shall none make thee ashamed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 6:10; Job 35:2"> For thou hast sayde, My doctrine is pure, and I am cleane in thine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 38:1–2; Job 31:35; Job 33:6–18"> But, oh that God would speake and open his lippes against thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Lamentations 3:22; Daniel 2:28"> That he might shewe thee the secretes of wisedome, howe thou hast deserued double, according to right: know therefore that God hath forgotten thee for thine iniquitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Ecclesiastes 3:11; Psalms 145:3"> Canst thou by searching finde out God? canst thou finde out ye Almighty to his perfection?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 22:12; Psalms 139:6–8; Isaiah 55:9"> The heauens are hie, what canst thou doe? it is deeper then the hell, how canst thou know it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 139:9–10; Job 28:24–25; Psalms 65:5–8"> The measure thereof is longer then the earth, and it is broader then the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:12–13; Job 12:14; Revelation 3:7"> If hee cut off and shut vp, or gather together, who can turne him backe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 10:14; Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23"> For hee knoweth vaine men, and seeth iniquitie, and him that vnderstandeth nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 2:20; Ecclesiastes 3:18; James 3:13–17"> Yet vaine man would be wise, though man new borne is like a wilde asse colte.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 143:6; Psalms 88:9; 1 Samuel 7:3"> If thou prepare thine heart, and stretch out thine hands toward him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:23; James 4:8; Job 4:7"> If iniquitie be in thine hand, put it farre away, and let no wickednesse dwell in thy Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 22:26; 1 John 2:28; 1 John 3:19–22"> The truely shalt thou lift vp thy face without spot, and shalt be stable, and shalt not feare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 65:16; Ecclesiastes 5:20; John 16:21"> But thou shalt forget thy miserie, and remember it as waters that are past.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 58:8–10; Psalms 37:6; Proverbs 4:18"> Thine age also shall appeare more cleare then the noone day: thou shalt shine and bee as the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:24–26; Psalms 43:5; Psalms 3:5"> And thou shalt bee bolde, because there is hope: and thou shalt digge pittes, and shalt lye downe safely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 45:12; Isaiah 60:14; Job 42:8–9"> For when thou takest thy rest, none shall make thee afraide: yea, many shall make sute vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:65; Job 31:16; Job 17:5"> But the eyes of the wicked shall faile, and their refuge shall perish, and their hope shalbe sorow of minde.</span>
