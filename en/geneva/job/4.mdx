<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 19:21; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Job 2:5"> But now it is come vpon thee, and thou art grieued: it toucheth thee, and thou art troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 3:26; Proverbs 14:26; Job 1:1"> Is not this thy feare, thy confidence, thy pacience, and the vprightnesse of thy wayes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:25; 2 Peter 2:9; Job 36:7"> Remember, I pray thee: who euer perished, being an innocent? or where were the vpright destroyed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:7–8; Proverbs 22:8; Hosea 8:7"> As I haue seene, they that plow iniquitie, and sowe wickednesse, reape the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 15:30; Isaiah 11:4; Isaiah 30:33"> With the blast of God they perish, and with the breath of his nostrels are they cosumed.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:10; Job 8:3–4; Job 1:19"> The Lyon perisheth for lacke of pray, and the Lyons whelpes are scattered abroade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 26:14; Psalms 62:11; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> But a thing was brought to me secretly, and mine eare hath receiued a litle thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 33:14–16; Genesis 2:21; Genesis 31:24"> In the thoughtes of ye visions of the night, when sleepe falleth on men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Job 33:19; Luke 1:12"> Feare came vpon me, and dread which made all my bones to tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; Daniel 5:6; Psalms 104:4"> And the wind passed before me, and made the heares of my flesh to stande vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:12"> Then stoode one, and I knewe not his face: an image was before mine eyes, and in silence heard I a voyce, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 9:2; Job 25:4; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> Shall man be more iust then God? or shall a man be more pure then his maker?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 25:5–6; 2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6"> Beholde, he founde no stedfastnesse in his Seruants, and laid follie vpon his Angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 10:9; Genesis 2:7; Genesis 3:19"> Howe much more in them that dwell in houses of clay, whose foundation is in the dust, which shalbe destroyed before the moth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 20:7; Job 14:14; Job 16:22"> They be destroyed from the morning vnto the euening: they perish for euer, without regarde.</span>
