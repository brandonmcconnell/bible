<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 27:1; Job 13:12; Numbers 23:7"> So Iob proceeded and continued his parable, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 1:10; Job 1:1–5; Jeremiah 31:28"> Oh that I were as in times past, when God preserued me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:28; John 8:12; Job 18:6"> When his light shined vpon mine head: and when by his light I walked thorowe the darkenesse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; Proverbs 3:32; Psalms 27:5"> As I was in the dayes of my youth: when Gods prouidence was vpon my tabernacle:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 128:3; Psalms 127:3–5; Deuteronomy 33:27–29"> When the almightie was yet with me, and my children round about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 81:16; Job 20:17; Deuteronomy 32:13–14"> When I washed my pathes with butter, and when the rocke powred me out riuers of oyle:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 8:16; Ruth 4:1–2; Deuteronomy 16:18"> When I went out to the gate, euen to the iudgement seat, and when I caused them to prepare my seate in the streete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; Proverbs 16:31; Romans 13:7"> The yong men saw me, and hid themselues, and the aged arose, and stood vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 10:19"> The princes stayed talke, and layde their hand on their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 137:6; Ezekiel 3:26"> The voyce of princes was hidde, and their tongue cleaued to the roofe of their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Job 31:20; Proverbs 29:2"> And when the eare heard me, it blessed me: and when the eye sawe me, it gaue witnesse to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 72:12; Proverbs 21:13; James 1:27"> For I deliuered the poore that cryed, and the fatherlesse, and him that had none to helpe him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philemon 1:7; Job 22:9; Job 31:19–20"> The blessing of him that was ready to perish, came vpon me, and I caused the widowes heart to reioyce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Isaiah 59:17; Psalms 132:9"> I put on iustice, and it couered me: my iudgement was as a robe, and a crowne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 10:31; Matthew 11:5; 1 Corinthians 12:12–31"> I was the eyes to the blinde, and I was the feete to the lame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; Psalms 68:5; Job 31:18"> I was a father vnto the poore, and when I knewe not the cause, I sought it out diligently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Proverbs 30:14; 1 Samuel 17:35"> I brake also the chawes of the vnrighteous man, and pluckt the praye out of his teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 30:6–7; Genesis 32:12; Job 42:16–17"> Then I sayde, I shall die in my nest, and I shall multiplie my dayes as the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8; Hosea 14:5–7"> For my roote is spread out by the water, and the dewe shall lye vpon my branche.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:24; Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 18:34"> My glory shall renue towarde me, and my bowe shall be restored in mine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Job 29:9–10"> Vnto me men gaue eare, and wayted, and helde their tongue at my counsell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:2; Song of Solomon 4:11; Matthew 22:46"> After my wordes they replied not, and my talke dropped vpon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; Psalms 72:6; Zechariah 10:1"> And they wayted for me, as for the raine, and they opened their mouth as for the latter rayne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 89:15; Psalms 4:6; Psalms 126:1"> If I laughed on them, they beleeued it not: neither did they cause the light of my countenance to fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 3:2–3; Genesis 14:14–17"> I appoynted out their way, and did sit as chiefe, and dwelt as a King in the army, and like him that comforteth the mourners.</span>
