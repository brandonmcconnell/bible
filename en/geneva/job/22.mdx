<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered, and sayde,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:10; Galatians 6:7–8; Job 35:6–8"> May a man be profitable vnto God, as he that is wise, may be profitable to himselfe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Psalms 147:10–11"> Is it any thing vnto the Almightie, that thou art righteous? or is it profitable to him, that thou makest thy wayes vpright?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 14:3; Psalms 143:2; Revelation 3:19"> Is it for feare of thee that he will accuse thee? or go with thee into iudgement?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Job 15:5–6; Job 15:31–34"> Is not thy wickednes great, and thine iniquities innumerable?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:26; Ezekiel 18:12; Ezekiel 18:16"> For thou hast taken the pledge from thy brother for nought, and spoyled the clothes of the naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 31:17; Matthew 25:42; Isaiah 58:7"> To such as were wearie, thou hast not giuen water to drinke, and hast withdrawen bread from the hungrie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 9:15; Job 29:7–17; Job 13:8"> But the mightie man had the earth, and he that was in autoritie, dwelt in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 24:21; Job 24:3; Ezekiel 22:7"> Thou hast cast out widowes emptie, and the armes of the fatherles were broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 13:21; Job 6:4"> Therefore snares are round about thee, and feare shall suddenly trouble thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Lamentations 3:54; Jonah 2:3"> Or darkenes that thou shouldest not see, and abundance of waters shall couer thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 11:8; Isaiah 66:1; Psalms 8:3–4"> Is not God on hie in the heauen? and behold the height of the starres how hie they are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Psalms 10:11; Ezekiel 9:9"> But thou sayest, How should God know? can he iudge through the darke cloude?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Psalms 139:11–12; Psalms 33:14"> The cloudes hide him that he can not see, and he walketh in the circle of heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:5; Genesis 6:11–13"> Hast thou marked the way of the worlde, wherein wicked men haue walked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 15:32; Genesis 7:17–24; Psalms 55:23"> Which were cut downe before the time, whose foundation was as a riuer that ouerflowed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 21:14–15; Romans 1:28; Psalms 4:6"> Which sayd vnto God, Depart from vs, and asked what the Almightie could do for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 21:16; Job 12:6; Acts 14:17"> Yet hee filled their houses with good things: but let the counsell of the wicked be farre from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 58:10; Psalms 107:42; Psalms 52:6"> The righteous shall see them, and shall reioyce, and the innocent shall laugh them to scorne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6–7; Job 1:16; Job 20:26"> Surely our substance is hid: but the fire hath deuoured the remnant of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Isaiah 27:5; Philippians 4:7"> Therefore acquaint thy selfe, I pray thee, with him, and make peace: thereby thou shalt haue prosperitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Job 23:12; Deuteronomy 4:1–2"> Receiue, I pray thee, the law of his mouth, and lay vp his words in thine heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 11:13–14; Zechariah 1:3; Job 8:5–6"> If thou returne to the Almightie, thou shalt be buylt vp, and thou shalt put iniquitie farre from thy tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:12; 1 Kings 9:28; Genesis 10:29"> Thou shalt lay vp golde for dust, and the gold of Ophir, as the flintes of the riuers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 33:6; Psalms 16:5–6; Genesis 15:1"> Yea, the Almightie shalbe thy defence, and thou shalt haue plentie of siluer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Isaiah 58:14; Job 27:10"> And thou shalt then delite in the Almightie, and lift vp thy face vnto God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Psalms 116:1; 1 John 5:14–15"> Thou shalt make thy praier vnto him, and he shall heare thee, and thou shalt render thy vowes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Psalms 90:17; Psalms 20:4"> Thou shalt also decree a thing, and he shall establish it vnto thee, and the light shall shine vpon thy wayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 4:6; Job 5:19–27; Luke 1:52"> When others are cast downe, then shalt thou say, I am lifted vp: and God shall saue the humble person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 42:7–8; James 5:15–16; Genesis 18:26–32"> The innocent shall deliuer the yland, and it shalbe preserued by the purenes of thine hands.</span>
