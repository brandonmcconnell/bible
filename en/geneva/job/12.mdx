<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Iob answered, and sayde,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; Job 8:8–10; 1 Corinthians 6:5"> In deede because that ye are the people onely, wisedome must dye with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 13:2–5; Proverbs 26:4; 2 Corinthians 11:21–23"> But I haue vnderstanding aswel as you, and am not inferior vnto you: yea, who knoweth not such things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 30:1; Job 17:6; Psalms 91:15"> I am as one mocked of his neighbour, who calleth vpon God, and he heareth him: the iust and the vpright is laughed to scorne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 25:8; Proverbs 13:9; Psalms 17:5"> Hee that is readie to fall, is as a lampe despised in the opinion of the riche.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 9:24; Psalms 73:11–12; Psalms 17:14"> The tabernacles of robbers doe prosper, and they are in safetie, that prouoke God, whome God hath enriched with his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:3; Proverbs 6:6; Jeremiah 8:7"> Aske now the beasts, and they shall teach thee, and the foules of the heauen, and they shall tell thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Or speake to the earth, and it shall shewe thee: or the fishes of the sea, and they shall declare vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 41:20; Jeremiah 27:5–6; Romans 11:36"> Who is ignorant of all these, but that the hande of the Lord hath made these?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 17:28; Job 33:4; Acts 17:25"> In whose hande is the soule of euery liuing thing, and the breath of all mankinde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 34:3; Hebrews 5:14; Job 6:30"> Doeth not the eares discerne the words? and the mouth taste meate for it selfe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:7; Job 15:10; Job 8:8"> Among the ancient is wisedome, and in the length of dayes is vnderstanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 9:4; Job 36:5; Proverbs 2:6–7"> With him is wisedome and strength: he hath counsell and vnderstanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:7; Isaiah 22:22; Malachi 1:4"> Beholde, he will breake downe, and it can not be built: he shutteth a man vp, and he can not be loosed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Amos 5:8; Genesis 6:13"> Beholde, he withholdeth the waters, and they drie vp: but when he sendeth them out, they destroy the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:9; Ezekiel 14:9; 1 Kings 22:22–23"> With him is strength and wisedome: hee that is deceiued, and that deceiueth, are his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20; 2 Samuel 17:23"> He causeth the counsellers to goe as spoyled, and maketh the iudges fooles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 2:21; Psalms 116:16; 2 Chronicles 33:11–14"> He looseth the collar of Kings, and girdeth their loynes with a girdle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; Isaiah 37:36–38; Revelation 17:14"> He leadeth away the princes as a pray, and ouerthroweth the mightie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 32:9; Job 17:4; Job 12:24"> He taketh away the speach from the faithfull counsellers, and taketh away the iudgement of the ancient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:40; Isaiah 23:9; 2 Kings 9:34–37"> He powreth contempt vpon princes, and maketh the strength of the mightie weake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Corinthians 2:10"> He discouereth the deepe places from their darkenesse, and bringeth foorth the shadowe of death to light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 9:3; Isaiah 26:15; Isaiah 60:22"> He increaseth the people, and destroyeth them: he inlargeth the nations, and bringeth them in againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 107:40; Job 12:20; Hosea 7:11"> He taketh away the heartes of the that are the chiefe ouer the people of the earth, and maketh them to wander in the wildernes out of the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 5:14; Psalms 107:27; Isaiah 19:14"> They grope in the darke without light: and he maketh the to stagger like a drunken man.</span>
