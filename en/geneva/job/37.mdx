<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 28:2–4; Jeremiah 5:22; Exodus 19:16"> At this also mine heart is astonied, and is mooued out of his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 37:5; Job 36:33; Psalms 104:7"> Heare the sound of his voyce, and the noyse that goeth out of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 38:13; Matthew 24:27; Isaiah 11:12"> He directeth it vnder the whole heauen, and his light vnto the endes of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 29:3–9; Psalms 68:33; Exodus 15:7–8"> After it a noyse soundeth: hee thundereth with the voyce of his maiestie, and hee will not stay them when his voyce is heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 5:9; Romans 11:33; Isaiah 40:28"> God thundereth marueilously with his voyce: he worketh great things, which we know not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 38:22; Job 36:27; Ezekiel 13:13"> For he sayth to the snowe, Be thou vpon the earth: likewise to the small rayne and to the great rayne of his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Psalms 109:27; Isaiah 26:11"> With the force thereof he shutteth vp euery man, that all men may knowe his worke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 104:22; Job 38:40"> Then the beastes go into the denne, and remaine in their places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:9; Psalms 104:3; Isaiah 21:1"> The whirlewind commeth out of the South, and the colde from the North winde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 38:29–30; Psalms 147:16–18; Psalms 78:47"> At the breath of God the frost is giuen, and the breadth of the waters is made narrowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 36:27–30; Matthew 17:5; Isaiah 18:4"> He maketh also the cloudes to labour, to water the earth, and scattereth the cloude of his light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 148:8; Joel 2:23; James 5:17–18"> And it is turned about by his gouernment, that they may doe whatsoeuer he commandeth them vpon the whole worlde:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 18:45; Job 38:26–27; 1 Samuel 12:18–19"> Whether it be for punishment, or for his lande, or of mercie, he causeth it to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Psalms 145:10–12; Exodus 14:13"> Hearken vnto this, O Iob: stand and consider the wonderous workes of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 36:30–32; Isaiah 40:26; Job 37:11"> Diddest thou knowe when God disposed them? and caused the light of his cloud to shine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 36:4; Proverbs 3:19–20; Isaiah 40:22"> Hast thou knowen the varietie of the cloude, and the wonderous workes of him, that is perfite in knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 147:18; Job 6:17; Job 38:31"> Or howe thy clothes are warme, when he maketh the earth quiet through the South winde?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Psalms 104:2; Isaiah 40:22"> Hast thou stretched out the heaues, which are strong, and as a molten glasse?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 38:2; Psalms 73:22; Job 42:3"> Tell vs what we shall say vnto him: for we can not dispose our matter because of darknes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 11:7–8; Psalms 139:4; Job 6:3"> Shall it be told him when I speake? or shall man speake when he shalbe destroyed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 36:32; Job 26:9; Job 38:25"> And nowe men see not the light, which shineth in the cloudes, but the winde passeth and clenseth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 104:1; Jude 1:25; Hebrews 12:29"> The brightnesse commeth out of the North: the praise thereof is to God, which is terrible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:16; Romans 11:33; Psalms 99:4"> It is the Almightie: we can not finde him out: he is excellent in power and iudgement, and aboundant in iustice: he afflicteth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; 1 Corinthians 1:26; Psalms 130:4"> Let men therefore feare him: for he will not regarde any that are wise in their owne conceit.</span>
