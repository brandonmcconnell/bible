<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:12; Zechariah 2:1–2; Job 11:9"> Who hath layde the measures thereof, if thou knowest, or who hath stretched the line ouer it:</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 5:11; Job 1:6; Ezra 3:11–12"> When the starres of the morning praysed me together, and all the children of God reioyced:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:9; Proverbs 8:29; Jeremiah 5:22"> Or who hath shut vp the Sea with doores, when it yssued and came foorth as out of the wombe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 1:2"> When I made the cloudes as a couering thereof, and darkenesse as the swadeling bands thereof:</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 89:9; Proverbs 8:29; Psalms 65:6–7"> And said, Hitherto shalt thou come, but no farther, and here shall it stay thy proude waues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 74:16; Psalms 148:3–5; Genesis 1:5"> Hast thou commanded the morning since thy dayes? hast thou caused the morning to knowe his place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 104:35; Job 37:3; Exodus 14:27"> That it might take hold of the corners of the earth, and that the wicked might be shaken out of it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:6; Psalms 104:2"> It is turned as clay to facion, and all stand vp as a garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 10:15; Psalms 37:17; Job 18:5"> And from the wicked their light shall be taken away, and the hie arme shalbe broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:24; Job 26:5–6; Psalms 77:19"> Hast thou entred into the bottomes of the sea? or hast thou walked to seeke out the depth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 9:13; Psalms 107:18; Job 3:5"> Haue the gates of death bene opened vnto thee? or hast thou seene the gates of the shadowe of death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 89:11–12; Revelation 20:9; Jeremiah 31:37"> Hast thou perceiued the breadth of the earth? tell if thou knowest all this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:8; Genesis 1:14–18; Genesis 1:3–4"> Where is the way where light dwelleth? and where is the place of darkenesse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 10:19; Job 26:10; Genesis 23:17"> That thou shouldest receiue it in the boundes thereof, and that thou shouldest knowe the paths to the house thereof?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 15:7; Job 38:12; Job 38:4"> Knewest thou it, because thou wast then borne, and because the nomber of thy dayes is great?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 37:6; Psalms 135:7; Psalms 33:7"> Hast thou entred into the treasures of the snow? or hast thou seene the treasures of ye haile,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; Isaiah 30:30; Exodus 9:18"> Which I haue hid against the time of trouble, against the day of warre and battell?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 38:12–13; Jonah 4:8; Matthew 24:27"> By what way is the light parted, which scattereth the East winde vpon the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 28:26; Psalms 29:3–10; Job 37:3–6"> Who hath deuided the spowtes for the raine? or the way for the lightning of ye thunders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:22; Hebrews 6:7–8; Psalms 107:35"> To cause it to raine on the earth where no man is, and in the wildernes where there is no man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 107:35; Psalms 104:14"> To fulfil the wilde and waste place, and to cause the bud of the herbe to spring forth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:22; Psalms 147:8; Jeremiah 10:13"> Who is the father of the rayne? or who hath begotten the droppes of the dewe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 147:16–17; Job 37:10; Job 38:8"> Out of whose wombe came the yee? who hath ingendred the frost of the heauen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 37:10"> The waters are hid as with a stone: and the face of the depth is frosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Job 9:9"> Canst thou restraine the sweete influences of the Pleiades? or loose the bandes of Orion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 38:31; Job 9:9; 2 Kings 23:5"> Canst thou bring foorth Mazzaroth in their time? canst thou also guide Arcturus with his sonnes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35–36; Genesis 8:22; Job 38:12–13"> Knowest thou the course of heauen, or canst thou set the rule thereof in the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 22:11; Job 36:27–28; Amos 5:8"> Canst thou lift vp thy voice to the cloudes that the aboundance of water may couer thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 36:32; Job 37:3; Isaiah 65:1"> Canst thou sende the lightenings that they may walke, and say vnto thee, Loe, heere we are?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Job 32:8; Psalms 51:6; Proverbs 2:6"> Who hath put wisedome in the reines? or who hath giuen the heart vnderstanding?</span>
