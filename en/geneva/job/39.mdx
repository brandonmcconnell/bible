<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:24"> When they couch in their places, and remaine in the couert to lye in waite?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Who prepareth for the rauen his meate, when his birdes crie vnto God, wandering for lacke of meate?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Knowest thou the time when the wilde goates bring foorth yong? or doest thou marke when the hindes doe calue?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 11:12; Job 24:5; Job 6:5"> Canst thou nomber the moneths that they fulfill? or knowest thou the time when they bring foorth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Job 24:5; Deuteronomy 29:23"> They bow them selues: they bruise their yong and cast out their sorowes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 5:18; Job 3:18; Exodus 5:13–16"> Yet their yong waxe fatte, and growe vp with corne: they goe foorth and returne not vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 104:27–28; Psalms 145:15–16; Job 40:15"> Who hath set the wilde asse at libertie? or who hath loosed the bondes of the wilde asse?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 23:22; Deuteronomy 33:17; Psalms 92:10"> It is I which haue made the wildernesse his house, and the salt places his dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 10:10–11; Psalms 129:3; Job 39:7"> He derideth the multitude of the citie: he heareth not the crie of the driuer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:26; Psalms 144:14; Genesis 42:26"> He seeketh out the mountaine for his pasture, and searcheth after euery greene thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15; Proverbs 3:16; Amos 2:13"> Will the vnicorne serue thee? or will he tary by thy cribbe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Zechariah 5:9; 2 Chronicles 9:21"> Canst thou binde the vnicorne with his band to labour in the furrowe? or will he plowe the valleyes after thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Wilt thou trust in him, because his strength is great, and cast off thy labour vnto him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Wilt thou beleeue him, that he will bring home thy seede, and gather it vnto thy barne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 4:3; Deuteronomy 28:56–57; 1 Kings 3:26–27"> Hast thou giuen the pleasant wings vnto the peacockes? or winges and feathers vnto the ostriche?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Job 17:4; Isaiah 57:17"> Which leaueth his egges in the earth, and maketh them hote in the dust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 41:29; Job 39:22; Job 5:22"> And forgetteth that the foote might scatter the, or that the wild beast might breake the.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 147:10; Psalms 93:1; Psalms 104:1"> He sheweth himselfe cruell vnto his yong ones, as they were not his, and is without feare, as if he trauailed in vaine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:16; Job 41:20–21; Joel 2:5"> For God had depriued him of wisedom, and hath giuen him no part of vnderstanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:6; Psalms 19:5; 1 Samuel 17:4–10"> When time is, he mounteth on hie: he mocketh the horse and his rider.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 39:16; Job 39:18"> Hast thou giuen the horse strength? or couered his necke with neying?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 41:26–29"> Hast thou made him afraid as the grashopper? his strong neying is fearefull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 3:6; Luke 24:41; Job 37:20"> He diggeth in the valley, and reioyceth in his strength: he goeth foorth to meete the harnest man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 70:3; Ezekiel 26:2; Ezekiel 36:2"> He mocketh at feare, and is not afraid, and turneth not backe from the sworde,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:15; Song of Solomon 2:12; Leviticus 16:11"> Though the quiuer rattle against him, the glittering speare and the shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Obadiah 1:4; Jeremiah 49:16; Exodus 19:4"> He swalloweth the ground for fearcenes and rage, and he beleeueth not that it is the noise of the trumpet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:4"> He sayth among the trumpets, Ha, ha: hee smellleth the battell afarre off, and the noyse of the captaines, and the shouting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 9:26"> Shall the hauke flie by thy wisedome, stretching out his wings toward the South?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:28; Luke 17:37; Ezekiel 39:17–19"> Doeth the eagle mount vp at thy commandement, or make his nest on hie?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Shee abideth and remaineth in the rocke, euen vpon the toppe of the rocke, and the tower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> From thence she spieth for meate, and her eyes beholde afarre off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> His young ones also sucke vp blood: and where the slaine are, there is she.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Moreouer ye Lord spake vnto Iob, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Is this to learne to striue with the Almightie? he that reprooueth God, let him answere to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Then Iob answered the Lord, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Beholde, I am vile: what shall I answere thee? I will lay mine hand vpon my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Once haue I spoken, but I will answere no more, yea twise, but I will proceede no further.</span>
