<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 15:15; Job 15:8–10; Job 4:18"> Call nowe, if any will answere thee, and to which of the Saintes wilt thou turne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:9; Hosea 7:11; Genesis 30:1"> Doubtlesse anger killeth the foolish, and enuie slayeth the idiote.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 73:18–20; Psalms 37:35–36; Psalms 92:7"> I haue seene the foolish well rooted, and suddenly I cursed his habitation, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 127:5; Job 18:16–19; Psalms 119:155"> His children shalbe farre from saluation, and they shall be destroyed in the gate, and none shall deliuer them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Hosea 8:7; Jeremiah 51:44"> The hungrie shall eate vp his haruest: yea, they shall take it from among the thornes, and the thirstie shall drinke vp their substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Lamentations 3:38; 1 Samuel 6:9"> For miserie commeth not foorth of the dust, neither doeth affliction spring out of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 14:1; Genesis 3:17–19; Ecclesiastes 5:15–17"> But man is borne vnto trauaile, as the sparkes flie vpwarde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Job 22:21; Psalms 37:5"> But I would inquire at God, and turne my talke vnto God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; Job 9:10; Psalms 86:10"> Which doeth great things and vnsearchable, and marueilous things without nomber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:24; Acts 14:17; Psalms 147:8"> He giueth raine vpon the earth, and powreth water vpon the streetes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 113:7; 1 Samuel 2:7–8; Luke 6:21"> And setteth vp on hie them that be lowe, that the sorowfull may be exalted to saluation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 33:10–11; Psalms 21:11; Isaiah 8:10"> He scattereth the deuices of the craftie: so that their handes can not accomplish that which they doe enterprise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:19; Psalms 9:15–16; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> He taketh the wise in their craftinesse, and the counsel of the wicked is made foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 12:25; Isaiah 59:10; Deuteronomy 28:29"> They meete with darkenesse in the day time, and grope at noone day, as in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 35:10; Psalms 140:12; Psalms 109:31"> But he saueth the poore from the sword, from their mouth, and from the hande of the violent man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 107:42; 1 Samuel 2:8–9; Psalms 63:11"> So that the poore hath his hope, but iniquitie shall stop her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 1:12; Psalms 94:12; Proverbs 3:11–12"> Beholde, blessed is the man whome God correcteth: therefore refuse not thou the chastising of the Almightie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 6:1; Deuteronomy 32:39; Isaiah 30:26"> For he maketh the wound, and bindeth it vp: he smiteth, and his handes make whole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; 1 Corinthians 10:13; Proverbs 24:16"> He shall deliuer thee in sixe troubles, and in the seuenth the euill shall not touch thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 33:19; Hosea 13:14; Psalms 37:19"> In famine he shall deliuer thee from death: and in battel from the power of the sworde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 31:20; Psalms 91:5–7; Jeremiah 18:18"> Thou shalt be hid from the scourge of the tongue, and thou shalt not be afraid of destruction when it commeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:25; Isaiah 35:9; Psalms 91:13"> But thou shalt laugh at destruction and dearth, and shalt not be afraide of the beast of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 26:6; Hosea 2:18; Daniel 6:22"> For the stones of the fielde shall be in league with thee, and the beastes of the field shall be at peace with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 121:7–8; Psalms 91:10; Isaiah 4:5–6"> And thou shalt knowe, that peace shall be in thy tabernacle, and thou shalt visite thine habitation, and shalt not sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 112:2; Psalms 72:16; Deuteronomy 28:4"> Thou shalt perceiue also, that thy seede shalbe great, and thy posteritie as the grasse of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 42:16–17; Proverbs 9:11; Psalms 91:16"> Thou shalt goe to thy graue in a ful age, as a ricke of corne commeth in due season into the barne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Job 32:11–12; Proverbs 9:12"> Lo, thus haue we inquired of it, and so it is: heare this and knowe it for thy selfe.</span>
