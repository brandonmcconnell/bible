<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Job 22:14; Habakkuk 3:3–5"> He holdeth backe the face of his throne: and spreadeth his cloude vpon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 8:29; Job 38:8–11; Jeremiah 5:22"> He hath set bounds about the waters, vntil the day and night come to an ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; Psalms 18:7; Job 15:15"> The pillars of heauen tremble and quake at his reproofe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 51:15; Jeremiah 31:35; Psalms 89:9–10"> The sea is calme by his power, and by his vnderstanding he smiteth the pride thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 104:30; Psalms 74:13–14"> His Spirite hath garnished the heauens, and his hand hath formed the crooked serpent.</span>
