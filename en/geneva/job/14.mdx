<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 144:3; Psalms 143:2; Psalms 8:4"> And yet thou openest thine eyes vpon such one, and causest me to enter into iudgement with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 3:6; Job 15:14; Ephesians 2:3"> Who can bring a cleane thing out of filthinesse? there is not one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 39:4; Acts 17:26; Hebrews 9:27"> Are not his dayes determined? the nober of his moneths are with thee: thou hast appointed his boundes, which he can not passe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 7:19; Psalms 39:13; Job 7:1–2"> Turne from him that he may cease vntill his desired day, as an hyreling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; Isaiah 11:1; Job 14:14"> For there is hope of a tree, if it bee cut downe, that it will yet sproute, and the branches thereof will not cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36; Isaiah 26:19; John 12:24"> Though the roote of it waxe olde in the earth, and the stocke thereof be dead in ye ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:17–24; Ezekiel 17:3–10; Ezekiel 17:22–24"> Yet by the sent of water it will bud, and bring foorth boughes like a plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 7:7–10; Job 14:12"> But man is sicke, and dyeth, and man perisheth, and where is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 19:5; Jeremiah 15:18; Job 6:15–18"> As the waters passe from the sea, and as the flood decayeth and dryeth vp,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Job 3:13; Ecclesiastes 3:19–21"> So man sleepeth and riseth not: for hee shall not wake againe, nor be raised from his sleepe till the heauen be no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Isaiah 57:1–2; Isaiah 26:20–21"> Oh that thou wouldest hide me in the graue, and keepe me secret, vntill thy wrath were past, and wouldest giue me terme, and remember me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Job 7:1; Acts 26:8"> If a man die, shall he liue againe? All the dayes of mine appointed time will I waite, till my changing shall come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 13:22; Psalms 138:8; Job 10:8"> Thou shalt call me, and I shall answere thee: thou louest the worke of thine own hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 10:6; Job 31:4; Psalms 139:1–4"> But nowe thou nombrest my steppes, and doest not delay my sinnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:34; Hosea 13:12; Job 21:19"> Mine iniquitie is sealed vp, as in a bagge, and thou addest vnto my wickednesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 6:14; Job 18:4; Jeremiah 4:24"> And surely as the mountaine that falleth, commeth to nought, and the rocke that is remooued from his place:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 7:6; Job 27:8; Job 19:10"> As the water breaketh the stones, when thou ouerflowest the things which growe in the dust of ye earth: so thou destroyest ye hope of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:14; Job 2:12; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> Thou preuailest alway against him, so that he passeth away: he changeth his face when thou castest him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Isaiah 63:16"> And he knoweth not if his sonnes shall be honourable, neither shall he vnderstand concerning them, whether they shalbe of lowe degree,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 19:20; Luke 16:23–24"> But while his flesh is vpon him, he shall be sorowfull, and while his soule is in him, it shall mourne.</span>
