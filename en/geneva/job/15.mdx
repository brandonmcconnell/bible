<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 42:7; Job 2:11; Job 22:1"> Then answered Eliphaz the Temanite, and saide,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 8:2; Hosea 12:1"> Shal a wise man speake words of ye winde, and fill his bellie with the East winde?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Matthew 12:36–37; Job 13:4–5"> Shall he dispute with wordes not comely? or with talke that is not profitable?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 3:31; Luke 18:1; Amos 6:10"> Surely thou hast cast off feare, and restrainest prayer before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:3–5; James 3:5–8; Luke 6:45"> For thy mouth declareth thine iniquitie, seeing thou hast chosen ye tongue of the crafty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 19:22; Matthew 12:37; Job 9:20"> Thine owne mouth condemneth thee, and not I, and thy lippes testifie against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Job 12:12; Job 38:4–41"> Art thou the first man, that was borne? and wast thou made before the hils?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 11:34; Jeremiah 23:18; Job 13:5–6"> Hast thou heard the secret counsell of God, and doest thou restraine wisedome to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 11:5; Job 12:3"> What knowest thou that we knowe not? and vnderstandest that is not in vs?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 32:6–7; Job 8:8–10; Job 12:12"> With vs are both auncient and very aged men, farre older then thy father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 1:3–5; 1 Kings 22:24"> Seeme the consolations of God small vnto thee? is this thing strange vnto thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Mark 7:21–22"> Why doeth thine heart take thee away, and what doe thine eyes meane,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:25–27; James 3:2–6; Job 9:4"> That thou answerest to God at thy pleasure, and bringest such wordes out of thy mouth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; Job 14:4; Proverbs 20:9"> What is man, that he should be cleane? and he that is borne of woman, that he shoulde be iust?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 25:5; Job 4:18; Job 5:1"> Beholde, he founde no stedfastnesse in his Saintes: yea, the heauens are not cleane in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 34:7; Proverbs 19:28; Romans 3:9–19"> How much more is man abominable, and filthie, which drinketh iniquitie like water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 36:2; Job 34:2; Job 13:5–6"> I will tell thee: heare me, and I will declare that which I haue seene:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 8:8; Isaiah 38:19; Psalms 71:18"> Which wise men haue tolde, as they haue heard of their fathers, and haue not kept it secret:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Genesis 10:32; Genesis 10:25"> To whome alone the land was giuen and no stranger passed through them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 27:13; James 5:1–6; Luke 12:19–21"> The wicked man is continually as one that traueileth of childe, and the nomber of yeeres is hid from the tyrant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 18:11; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Leviticus 26:36"> A sounde of feare is in his eares, and in his prosperitie the destroyer shall come vpon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 20:24–25; Matthew 27:5; Job 9:16"> He beleeueth not to returne out of darknesse: for he seeth the sworde before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 109:10; Psalms 59:15; Job 18:12"> He wandreth to and from for bread where he may: he knoweth that the day of darkenesse is prepared at hande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 24:34; Proverbs 6:11; Job 6:2–4"> Affliction and anguish shall make him afraide: they shall preuaile against him as a King readie to the battell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Psalms 52:7; Psalms 73:9"> For he hath stretched out his hand against GOD, and made him selfe strong against the Almightie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 18:40; Job 16:12; 2 Chronicles 32:13–17"> Therefore God shall runne vpon him, euen vpon his necke, and against the most thicke part of his shielde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 17:10; Jeremiah 5:28; Psalms 73:7"> Because he hath couered his face with his fatnesse, and hath colloppes in his flancke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 3:14; Jeremiah 51:37; Micah 7:18"> Though he dwell in desolate cities, and in houses which no man inhabiteth, but are become heapes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Psalms 49:16–17; James 1:11"> He shall not be rich, neither shall his substance continue, neither shall he prolong the perfection thereof in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 4:9; Job 15:22; Job 18:5–6"> He shall neuer depart out of darkenesse: the flame shall drie vp his branches, and he shall goe away with the breath of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 59:4; Galatians 6:3; Psalms 62:10"> He beleeueth not that he erreth in vanitie: therefore vanitie shalbe his change.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:17; Psalms 55:23; Hosea 9:16"> His branch shall not be greene, but shall be cut off before his day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:39–40; Isaiah 33:9; Revelation 6:13"> God shall destroy him as the vine her sower grape, and shall cast him off, as the oliue doeth her flowre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 8:13; Job 27:8; Job 8:22"> For the congregation of the hypocrite shalbe desolate, and fire shall deuoure the houses of bribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Hosea 10:13; Isaiah 59:4–5"> For they conceiue mischiefe and bring foorth vanitie, and their bellie hath prepared deceite.</span>
