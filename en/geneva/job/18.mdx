<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 8:1; Job 25:1"> Then answered Bildad the Shuhite, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 13:5–6; Proverbs 18:13; Job 3:17"> When will yee make an ende of your words? cause vs to vnderstande, and then wee will speake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; Job 17:10; Job 17:4"> Wherefore are wee counted as beastes, and are vile in your sight?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 16:9; Job 14:18; Job 13:14"> Thou art as one that teareth his soule in his anger. Shall the earth bee forsaken for thy sake? or the rocke remoued out of his place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Proverbs 24:20; Proverbs 20:20"> Yea, the light of the wicked shalbe quenched, and the sparke of his fire shall not shine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Job 21:17; Psalms 18:28"> The light shalbe darke in his dwelling, and his candle shalbe put out with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Psalms 33:10; Psalms 18:36"> The steps of his strength shalbe restrained, and his owne counsell shall cast him downe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 9:15; Job 22:10; Psalms 35:8"> For hee is taken in the net by his feete, and he walketh vpon the snares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; Job 1:15; Job 1:17"> The grenne shall take him by the heele, and the theefe shall come vpon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Romans 11:9; Psalms 11:6"> A snare is layed for him in the ground, and a trappe for him in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 15:21; Jeremiah 6:25; Leviticus 26:36"> Fearefulnesse shall make him afrayde on euery side, and shall driue him to his feete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Psalms 34:10"> His strength shalbe famine: and destruction shalbe readie at his side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 2:6; Zechariah 14:12; Revelation 6:8"> It shall deuoure the inner partes of his skinne, and the first borne of death shall deuoure his strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 10:28; Psalms 55:4; Job 11:20"> His hope shalbe rooted out of his dwelling, and shall cause him to go to the King of feare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Deuteronomy 29:23; Isaiah 34:9–10"> Feare shall dwell in his house (because it is not his) and brimstone shalbe scattered vpon his habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 9:16; Isaiah 5:24; Job 15:30"> His rootes shalbe dryed vp beneath, and aboue shall his branche be cut downe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:16; Proverbs 10:7; Psalms 109:13"> His remembrance shall perish from the earth, and he shall haue no name in the streete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 20:8; Job 10:22; Job 3:20"> They shall driue him out of the light vnto darkenesse, and chase him out of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:30; Isaiah 14:21–22; Job 20:26–28"> Hee shall neither haue sonne nor nephewe among his people, nor any posteritie in his dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 37:13; Jeremiah 50:27; Ezekiel 21:25"> The posteritie shalbe astonied at his day, and feare shall come vpon the ancient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Jeremiah 9:3; Jeremiah 10:25"> Surely such are the habitations of the wicked, and this is the place of him that knoweth not God.</span>
