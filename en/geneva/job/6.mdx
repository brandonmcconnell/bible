<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:3; Job 23:2; Psalms 40:5"> For it woulde be nowe heauier then the sande of the sea: therefore my wordes are swallowed vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 38:2; Job 30:15; Psalms 88:15–16"> For the arrowes of the Almightie are in me, the venime whereof doeth drinke vp my spirit, and the terrours of God fight against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joel 1:18–20; Jeremiah 14:6; Psalms 42:1"> Doeth the wilde asse bray when he hath grasse? or loweth the oxe when he hath fodder?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 34:3; Luke 14:34; Colossians 4:6"> That which is vnsauerie, shall it be eaten without salt? or is there any taste in the white of an egge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 3:24; Ezekiel 4:16; Daniel 10:3"> Such things as my soule refused to touch, as were sorowes, are my meate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:81; Job 17:14–16; Job 6:11–13"> Oh that I might haue my desire, and that God would grant me the thing that I long for!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Jonah 4:3; Jonah 4:8"> That is, that God would destroy me: that he would let his hand go, and cut me off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 23:12; Leviticus 19:2; Isaiah 57:15"> Then should I yet haue comfort, (though I burne with sorowe, let him not spare) because I haue not denyed the wordes of the Holy one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 103:14–16; Psalms 102:23; Psalms 39:5"> What power haue I that I should endure? or what is mine end, if I should prolong my life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 41:24; Job 40:18"> Is my strength the strength of stones? or is my flesh of brasse?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 26:2; 2 Corinthians 1:12; Job 19:28"> Is it not so, that there is in me no helpe? and that strength is taken from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 17:17; 1 Corinthians 12:26; Galatians 6:2"> He that is in miserie, ought to be comforted of his neighbour: but men haue forsaken the feare of the Almightie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Jeremiah 15:18; Psalms 41:9"> My brethre haue deceiued me as a brook, and as the rising of the riuers they passe away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Which are blackish with yee, and wherein the snowe is hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Job 24:19"> But in time they are dryed vp with heate and are consumed: and when it is hote they faile out of their places,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Or they depart from their way and course, yea, they vanish and perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 21:14; Genesis 25:15; Jeremiah 25:23"> They that go to Tema, considered them, and they that goe to Sheba, waited for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3–4; Romans 5:5; Romans 9:33"> But they were confounded: when they hoped, they came thither and were ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; 2 Timothy 4:16; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> Surely nowe are ye like vnto it: ye haue seene my fearefull plague, and are afraide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 20:33; Job 42:11; 1 Samuel 12:3"> Was it because I said, Bring vnto me? or giue a rewarde to me of your substance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 49:15; Psalms 49:7–8; Psalms 107:2"> And deliuer me from the enemies hande, or ransome me out of the hand of tyrants?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 3:2; Proverbs 9:9; Psalms 39:1–2"> Teach me, and I wil hold my tongue: and cause me to vnderstande, wherein I haue erred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 32:3; Job 13:5; Proverbs 25:11"> Howe stedfast are the wordes of righteousnes? and what can any of you iustly reproue?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 8:2; Ephesians 4:14; Job 42:3"> Doe ye imagine to reproue wordes, that the talke of the afflicted should be as the winde?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 24:9; Job 24:3; 2 Peter 2:3"> Ye make your wrath to fall vpon the fatherlesse, and dig a pit for your friende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 13:4; Job 33:3; Job 27:4"> Nowe therefore be content to looke vpon me: for I will not lie before your face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 17:10; Job 34:5; Job 27:4–6"> Turne, I pray you, let there be none iniquitie: returne, I say, and ye shall see yet my righteousnesse in that behalfe. Is there iniquitie in my tongue? doeth not my mouth feele sorowes?</span>
