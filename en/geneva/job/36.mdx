<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Elihu also proceeded and sayde,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 21:3; 2 Corinthians 5:20; Job 13:7–8"> Suffer me a litle, and I will instruct thee: for I haue yet to speake on Gods behalfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; Deuteronomy 32:4"> I will fetche my knowledge afarre off, and will attribute righteousnes vnto my Maker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:7–8; Job 37:16; Psalms 49:3"> For truely my wordes shall not be false, and he that is perfect in knowledge, speaketh with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 22:24; Psalms 138:6; Jeremiah 10:12"> Behold, the mighty God casteth away none that is mighty and valiant of courage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 72:12–14; Psalms 140:12; 2 Peter 2:9"> He mainteineth not the wicked, but he giueth iudgement to the afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 34:15; Psalms 33:18; 1 Samuel 2:8"> He withdraweth not his eyes from the righteous, but they are with Kings in ye throne, where he placeth them for euer: thus they are exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 5:22; Psalms 107:10; Lamentations 3:9"> And if they bee bound in fetters and tyed with the cordes of affliction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 119:71; Job 15:25; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> Then will he shewe them their worke and their sinnes, because they haue bene proude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 3:8; Job 36:15; Isaiah 55:6–7"> He openeth also their eare to discipline, and commandeth them that they returne from iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:23; Isaiah 1:19; Jeremiah 26:13"> If they obey and serue him, they shall end their dayes in prosperity, and their yeres in pleasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 4:21; Romans 2:8–9; Isaiah 3:11"> But if they wil not obey, they shall passe by the sworde, and perish without knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 2:5; Job 36:8; Job 27:8–10"> But the hypocrites of heart increase the wrath: for they call not when he bindeth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Job 15:32; Deuteronomy 23:17"> Their soule dyeth in youth, and their life among the whoremongers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 36:10; 2 Chronicles 12:8"> He deliuereth the poore in his affliction, and openeth their eare in trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Psalms 118:5; Psalms 18:19"> Euen so woulde he haue taken thee out of the streight place into a broade place and not shut vp beneath: and that which resteth vpon thy table, had bene full of fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Romans 1:32; Job 34:36"> But thou art ful of the iudgement of the wicked, though iudgement and equitie maintaine all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 33:24; Ephesians 5:6; Romans 1:18"> For Gods wrath is, least hee should take that away in thine abundance: for no multitude of giftes can deliuer thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Proverbs 11:21; James 5:3"> Wil he regard thy riches? he regardeth not golde, nor all them that excel in strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 19:35; Job 34:20; Exodus 12:29"> Be not carefull in the night, howe he destroyeth the people out of their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:25; Psalms 66:18; 1 Peter 3:17"> Take thou heede: looke not to iniquitie: for thou hast chosen it rather then affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:7–8; Luke 1:52; Romans 13:1"> Beholde, God exalteth by his power: what teacher is like him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:13–14; Job 8:3"> Who hath appointed to him his way? or who can say, Thou hast done wickedly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 15:3; Psalms 111:2–4; Psalms 72:18"> Remember that thou magnifie his worke, which men behold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> All men see it, and men beholde it afarre off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Psalms 145:3; Job 11:7–9"> Beholde, God is excellent, and we knowe him not, neither can the nomber of his yeres bee searched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Psalms 148:8; Psalms 65:9–13"> When he restraineth the droppes of water, the rayne powreth down by the vapour thereof,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 3:20; Genesis 7:11–12; Job 37:11–13"> Which raine the cloudes do droppe and let fall abundantly vpon man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:16; Job 37:2–5; Psalms 18:13"> Who can know the diuisions of ye clouds and the thunders of his tabernacle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 38:8–11; Job 38:34–35; Genesis 1:9"> Beholde, he spreadeth his light vpon it, and couereth the bottome of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Acts 14:17; Job 37:13"> For thereby hee iudgeth the people, and giueth meate abundantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Psalms 147:8–9; Psalms 148:8"> He couereth the light with the clouds, and commandeth them to go against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 37:2; Job 36:29; 2 Samuel 22:14"> His companion sheweth him thereof, and there is anger in rising vp.</span>
