<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 33:9; Job 10:2"> So these three men ceased to answere Iob, because he esteemed himselfe iust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 22:21; Job 40:8; Job 27:2"> Then the wrath of Elihu the sonne of Barachel the Buzite, of the familie of Ram, was kindled: his wrath, I say, was kindled against Iob, because he iustified himselfe more then God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 8:6; Job 24:25; Job 22:5–30"> Also his anger was kindled against his three friends, because they could not finde an answere, and yet condemned Iob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Proverbs 18:13"> (Now Elihu had wayted til Iob had spoken: for they were more ancient in yeeres then he)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 32:2; Exodus 32:19"> So when Elihu saw, that there was none answere in the mouth of the three men, his wrath was kindled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 15:10; 1 Timothy 5:1; Titus 2:6"> Therefore Elihu the sonne of Barachel, the Buzite answered, and sayd, I am yong in yeres, and ye are ancient: therefore I doubted, and was afraide to shewe you mine opinion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:31; Job 12:12; Hebrews 5:12"> For I said, The dayes shall speake, and the multitude of yeeres shall teach wisedome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 38:36; Job 33:4; 2 Timothy 3:16"> Surely there is a spirite in man, but the inspiration of the Almightie giueth vnderstanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; Job 12:20; Ecclesiastes 4:13"> Great men are not alway wise, neither doe the aged alway vnderstand iudgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:25; 1 Corinthians 7:40"> Therefore I say, Heare me, and I will shew also mine opinion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 29:21; Job 5:27; Proverbs 28:11"> Behold, I did waite vpon your wordes, and hearkened vnto your knowledge, whiles you sought out reasons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:3; 1 Timothy 1:7"> Yea, when I had considered you, lo, there was none of you that reproued Iob, nor answered his wordes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> Lest ye should say, We haue found wisedome: for God hath cast him downe, and no man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Yet hath he not directed his words to me, neyther will I answere him by your wordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:24–25; Matthew 22:46; Job 29:22"> Then they fearing, answered no more, but left off their talke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 1:19; Job 13:5; Amos 5:13"> When I had wayted (for they spake not, but stood still and answered no more)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:3–4; Job 33:12; Job 32:10"> Then answered I in my turne, and I shewed mine opinion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:3; Acts 4:20; 2 Corinthians 5:13–14"> For I am full of matter, and the spirite within me compelleth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 9:17"> Beholde, my belly is as the wine, which hath no vent, and like the new bottels that brast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6–7; Job 21:3; Job 13:13"> Therefore will I speake, that I may take breath: I will open my lippes, and will answere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; Job 13:8; Matthew 22:16"> I will not now accept the person of man, neyther will I giue titles to man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 12:2–3; 1 Thessalonians 2:5; Proverbs 29:5"> For I may not giue titles, lest my Maker should take me away suddenly.</span>
