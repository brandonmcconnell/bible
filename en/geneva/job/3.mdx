<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–18; Jeremiah 15:10; Job 10:18–19"> Let the day perish, wherein I was borne, and the night when it was sayde, There is a man childe conceiued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 27:20; Exodus 10:22–23; Joel 2:2"> Let that day bee darkenesse, let not God regarde it from aboue, neyther let the light shine vpon it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Psalms 23:4; Job 28:3"> But let darkenesse, and the shadowe of death staine it: let the cloude remayne vpon it, and let them make it fearefull as a bitter day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Let darkenesse possesse that night, let it not be ioyned vnto the dayes of the yeere, nor let it come into the count of the moneths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 24:8; Jeremiah 7:34; Revelation 18:22–23"> Yea, desolate be that night, and let no ioy be in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 41:1; Job 41:10; Mark 5:38"> Let them that curse the day, (being readie to renue their mourning) curse it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 41:18; Jeremiah 13:16; Job 30:26"> Let the starres of that twilight be dimme through darkenesse of it: let it looke for light, but haue none: neither let it see the dawning of the day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:17; Ecclesiastes 6:3–5; Genesis 20:18"> Because it shut not vp the dores of my mothers wombe: nor hid sorowe from mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 10:18–19; Isaiah 46:3; Psalms 71:6"> Why died I not in the birth? or why dyed I not, when I came out of the wombe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 66:12; Genesis 30:3; Genesis 50:23"> Why did the knees preuent me? and why did I sucke the breasts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Job 21:13; Job 14:10–12"> For so shoulde I now haue lyen and bene quiet, I should haue slept then, and bene at rest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 15:28; Psalms 89:48; Psalms 49:14"> With the Kings and counselers of the earth, which haue buylded themselues desolate places:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:27; Job 22:25; Zephaniah 1:18"> Or with the princes that had golde, and haue filled their houses with siluer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 58:8; Ecclesiastes 6:3; 1 Corinthians 15:8"> Or why was I not hid, as an vntimely birth, either as infants, which haue not seene the light?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Job 17:16; Hebrews 4:11"> The wicked haue there ceased from their tyrannie, and there they that laboured valiantly, are at rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 39:7; Isaiah 14:3–4; Exodus 5:6–8"> The prisoners rest together, and heare not the voyce of the oppressour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:8; Job 30:23; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> There are small and great, and the seruant is free from his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:18; Job 7:15–16; 1 Samuel 1:10"> Wherefore is the light giuen to him that is in miserie? and life vnto them that haue heauie hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 9:6; Proverbs 2:4; 1 Kings 19:4"> Which long for death, and if it come not, they would euen search it more then treasures:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Which ioy for gladnes, and reioyce, when they can finde the graue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 19:8; Lamentations 3:7; Isaiah 40:27"> Why is the light giuen to the man whose way is hid, and whom God hath hedged in?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 102:9; Psalms 42:3–4; Psalms 80:5"> For my sighing commeth before I eate, and my roarings are powred out like the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 1:5; Job 30:15; Job 31:23"> For the thing I feared, is come vpon me, and the thing that I was afraid of, is come vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 7:14; Job 27:9; Psalms 143:11"> I had no peace, neither had I quietnesse, neither had I rest, yet trouble is come.</span>
