<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Bvt Iob answered, and sayd,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Job 34:2; Job 15:11"> Heare diligently my wordes, and this shalbe in stead of your consolations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 16:10; Job 16:20"> Suffer mee, that I may speake, and when I haue spoken, mocke on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 102:1; Psalms 42:11; Psalms 142:2–3"> Doe I direct my talke to man? If it were so, how should not my spirit be troubled?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 29:9; Job 40:4; Judges 18:19"> Marke mee, and be abashed, and lay your hand vpon your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Psalms 119:120; Psalms 88:15"> Euen when I remember, I am afrayde, and feare taketh hold on my flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 73:3–12; Job 12:6; Jeremiah 12:1–3"> Wherefore do the wicked liue, and waxe olde, and grow in wealth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 17:6; Job 5:3–4; Job 20:10"> Their seede is established in their sight with them, and their generation before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:34; Psalms 73:5; Psalms 73:19"> Their houses are peaceable without feare, and the rod of God is not vpon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 23:26; Luke 16:19; Deuteronomy 7:13–14"> Their bullocke gendreth, and fayleth not: their cow calueth, and casteth not her calfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 107:41; Psalms 127:3–5"> They send forth their children like sheepe, and their sonnes dance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 6:4–6; Isaiah 5:12; Genesis 4:21"> They take the tabret and harpe, and reioyce in the sound of the organs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 36:11; Luke 12:19–20; Psalms 73:4"> They spend their dayes in wealth, and suddenly they go downe to the graue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:17; Romans 8:7; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12"> They say also vnto God, Depart from vs: for we desire not the knowledge of thy wayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Job 34:9; Malachi 1:13–14"> Who is the Almightie, that we should serue him? and what profit should we haue, if we should pray vnto him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; Job 22:18; Genesis 49:6"> Lo, their wealth is not in their hand: therfore let the counsell of the wicked bee farre from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 18:5–6; Proverbs 20:20; Proverbs 13:9"> How oft shall the candle of the wicked be put out? and their destruction come vpon them? he wil deuide their liues in his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 1:4; Psalms 83:13; Job 13:25"> They shall be as stubble before the winde, and as chaffe that the storme carieth away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Ezekiel 18:19–20; Ezekiel 18:2"> God wil lay vp the sorowe of the father for his children: when he rewardeth him, hee shall knowe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 14:10; Isaiah 51:17; Psalms 75:8"> His eyes shall see his destruction, and he shall drinke of the wrath of the Almightie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 14:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Psalms 55:23"> For what pleasure hath he in his house after him, when the nomber of his moneths is cut off?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 11:34; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:13–14"> Shall any teache God knowledge, who iudgeth the highest things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 20:22–23; Luke 12:19–21; Psalms 73:4–5"> One dyeth in his full strength, being in all ease and prosperitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:8; Psalms 17:10; Job 15:27"> His breasts are full of milke, and his bones runne full of marowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16–17; Job 7:11; Proverbs 14:10"> And another dieth in the bitternes of his soule, and neuer eateth with pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:2; Isaiah 14:11; Job 20:11"> They shall sleepe both in the dust, and the wormes shall couer them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 5:22; Psalms 119:86; Psalms 59:4"> Behold, I know your thoughts, and the enterprises, wherewith ye do me wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 8:22; Job 20:7; Habakkuk 2:9–11"> For ye say, Where is the princes house? and where is the tabernacle of the wickeds dwelling?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 129:8"> May ye not aske the that go by the way? and ye can not deny their signes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; 2 Peter 2:9–17; 2 Peter 3:7"> But the wicked is kept vnto the day of destruction, and they shall be brought forth to the day of wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:11; Deuteronomy 7:10; Romans 12:19"> Who shall declare his way to his face? and who shall reward him for that he hath done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 16:22; Psalms 49:14; Ezekiel 32:21–32"> Yet shall he be brought to the graue, and remaine in the heape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Job 24:24; Job 30:23"> The slimie valley shalbe sweete vnto him, and euery man shall draw after him, as before him there were innumerable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 16:2; Job 32:3; Job 13:4"> How then comfort ye me in vaine, seeing in your answeres there remaine but lyes?</span>
