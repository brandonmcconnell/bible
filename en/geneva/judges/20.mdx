<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 11:11; 1 Samuel 3:20; 1 Samuel 10:17"> Then all the children of Israel went out, and the Congregation was gathered together as one man, from Dan to Beersheba, with the land of Gilead, vnto the Lord in Mizpeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 8:10; Judges 20:17; Judges 20:15"> And the chiefe of all the people, and all the tribes of Israel assembled in the Congregation of the people of God foure hundreth thousand footemen that drewe sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 19:22–27; Luke 14:31–32; Luke 12:58–59"> (Now the children of Beniamin heard that the children of Israel were gone vp to Mizpeh) Then the children of Israel saide, Howe is this wickednesse committed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 19:15–28"> And the same Leuite, the womans husband that was slaine, answered and saide, I came vnto Gibeah that is in Beniamin with my concubine to lodge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 19:22; Judges 19:25–26; Genesis 19:4–8"> And the men of Gibeah arose against me, and beset the house round about vpon mee by night, thinking to haue slaine me, and haue forced my concubine that she is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 19:29; Joshua 7:15; Judges 19:23"> Then I tooke my concubine, and cut her in pieces, and sent her throughout all the countrey of the inheritance of Israel: for they haue committed abomination and villenie in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:30; 1 Corinthians 5:6; 1 Corinthians 5:10–12"> Behold, ye are al children of Israel: giue your aduise, and counsell herein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Judges 20:1; Proverbs 21:3"> Then all the people arose as one man, saying, There shall not a man of vs goe to his tent, neither any turne into his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jonah 1:7; Nehemiah 11:1; Joshua 14:2"> But now this is that thing which we will do to Gibeah: we wil goe vp by lot against it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And we wil take ten men of the hundreth throughout al the tribes of Israel, and an hundreth of the thousand, and a thousand of ten thousand to bring vitaile for the people that they may do (when they come to Gibeah of Beniamin) according to all the villeny, that it hath done in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> So all the men of Israel were gathered against the citie, knit together, as one man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:14; Deuteronomy 20:10; Romans 12:18"> And the tribes of Israel sent men through al the tribe of Beniamin, saying, What wickednesse is this that is committed among you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:12; Deuteronomy 13:13; Judges 19:22"> Nowe therefore deliuer vs those wicked men which are in Gibeah, that we may put them to death, and put away euill from Israel: but the children of Beniamin would not obey the voyce of their brethren the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 20:20; Job 15:25–26; Numbers 21:23"> But ye children of Beniamin gathered them selues together out of the cities vnto Gibeah, to come out and fight against the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 26:41; Judges 20:25; Numbers 1:37"> And ye children of Beniamin were nombred at that time out of the cities sixe and twenty thousand men that drewe sworde, beside the inhabitants of Gibeah, which were nombred seuen hundreth chosen men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 3:15; 1 Chronicles 12:2; 1 Samuel 17:49–50"> Of all this people were seuen hundreth chosen men, being left handed: all these could sling stones at an heare breadth, and not faile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; 2 Chronicles 17:14–18; Numbers 26:51"> Also the men of Israel, beside Beniamin, were nombred foure hundreth thousande men that drew sword, euen all men of warre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 20:23; Judges 20:26–27; Numbers 27:21"> And the children of Israel arose, and went vp to the house of God, and asked of God, saying, Which of vs shall goe vp first to fight against the children of Beniamin? And the Lord said, Iudah shalbe first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 7:16; Joshua 3:1; Joshua 6:12"> Then the children of Israel arose vp earely and camped against Gibeah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the men of Israel went out to battell against Beniamin, and the men of Israel put themselues in aray to fight against the beside Gibeah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 20:25; Psalms 73:18–19; 2 Chronicles 28:10"> And the children of Beniamin came out of Gibeah, and slewe downe to the ground of the Israelites that day two and twentie thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 64:5; Judges 20:17; 1 Samuel 30:6"> And the people, the men of Israel plucked vp their hearts, and set their battel againe in aray in the place where they put them in aray the first day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:26–27; Psalms 78:34–36; Joshua 7:6"> (For the children of Israel had gone vp and wept before the Lord vnto the euening, and had asked of the Lord, saying, Shall I goe againe to battel against the children of Beniamin my brethren? and the Lord said, Go vp against them)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Then the children of Israel came neere against the children of Beniamin the second day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 20:21; Genesis 18:25; Romans 2:5"> Also the second day Beniamin came forth to meete them out of Gibeah, and slewe downe to the grounde of the children of Israel againe eighteene thousand men: all they could handle the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Judges 20:23; Joel 2:12–18; Judges 20:18"> Then al the children of Israel went vp and all the people came also vnto the house of God, and wept and sate there before the Lord and fasted that day vnto the euening, and offred burnt offrings and peace offrings before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Judges 20:18; 1 Samuel 4:3–4"> And the children of Israel asked the Lord (for there was the Arke of the couenat of God in those dayes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 7:9; Joshua 24:33; Deuteronomy 18:5"> And Phinehas the sonne of Eleazar, the sonne of Aaron stoode before it at that time) saying, Shall I yet goe anie more to battel against the children of Beniamin my brethren, or shall I cease? And the Lord said, Go vp: for to morowe I will deliuer them into your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 8:4; 2 Samuel 5:23; Judges 20:34"> And Israel set men to lie in waite round about Gibeah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the children of Israel went vp against the children of Beniamin the third day, and put theselues in aray against Gibeah, as at other times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 19:13–14; Joshua 7:5; Isaiah 10:29"> Then the children of Beniamin comming out against the people, were drawen from the citie: and they began to smite of ye people and kill as at other times, euen by the wayes in the fielde (whereof one goeth vp to the house of God, and the other to Gibeah) vpon a thirtie men of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 8:15–16"> (For the children of Beniamin sayd, They are fallen before vs, as at the first. But the children of Israel saide, Let vs flee and plucke them away from the citie vnto the hie wayes)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 8:18–22"> And all the men of Israel rose vp out of their place, and put themselues in aray at Baal-tamar: and the men that lay in wayte of the Israelites came forth of their place, euen out of the medowes of Gibeah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 8:14; Isaiah 47:11; Job 21:13"> And they came ouer against Gibeah, ten thousande chosen men of all Israel, and the battell was sore: for they knewe not that the euill was neere them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 20:5; Judges 20:15; Judges 20:44–46"> And the Lord smote Beniamin before Israel, and the children of Israel destroyed of the Beniamites the same day fiue and twenty thousand and an hundreth men: all they could handle the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 8:15–29"> So the children of Beniamin sawe that they were striken downe: for the men of Israel gaue place to the Beniamites, because they trusted to the men that lay in waite, which they had laide beside Gibeah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 8:19; Joshua 6:5; Exodus 19:13"> And they that lay in wait hasted, and brake forth toward Gibeah, and the ambushment drewe themselues along, and smote all the citie with the edge of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Joshua 8:20; Genesis 17:21; 2 Kings 4:16"> Also the men of Israel had appoynted a certaine time with the ambushmentes, that they should make a great flame and smoke rise vp out of the citie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Judges 20:32"> And when the men of Israel retired in the battel, Beniamin began to smite and kill of the men of Israel about thirtie persons: for they said, Surely they are striken downe before vs, as in the first battell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Joshua 8:20; Revelation 19:3; Genesis 19:28"> But when the flame bega to arise out of the citie, as a pillar of smoke, the Beniamites looked backe, and behold, the flame of the citie began to ascend vp to heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Peter 2:12; Luke 21:26; Revelation 6:15–17"> Then the men of Israel turned againe, and the men of Beniamin were astonied: for they saw that euill was neere vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Joshua 8:15; Hosea 9:9; Lamentations 1:3"> Therefore they fled before the men of Israel vnto the way of the wildernesse, but the battell ouertooke them: also they which came out of the cities, slew them among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 8:20–22"> Thus they compassed the Beniamites about, and chased them at ease, and ouerranne them, euen ouer against Gibeah on the Eastside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And there were slaine of Beniamin eyghteene thousad men, which were all men of warre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Joshua 15:32; Judges 21:13; Zechariah 14:10"> And they turned and fled to the wildernes vnto the rocke of Rimmon: and the Israelites glayned of them by the way fiue thousand men, and pursued after them vnto Gidom, and slewe two thousand men of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Judges 20:35; Judges 20:15"> So that all that were slayne that day of Beniamin, were fiue and twentie thousand men that drewe sword, which were all men of warre:</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Psalms 103:9–10; Habakkuk 3:2"> But sixe hundreth men turned and fled to the wildernesse vnto the rocke of Rimmon, and abode in the rocke of Rimmon foure moneths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:15–17; 2 Chronicles 25:13; 2 Chronicles 28:6–9"> Then the men of Israel returned vnto the children of Beniamin, and smote them with the edge of the sword from the men of the citie vnto the beasts, and all that came to hand: also they set on fire all the cities that they coulde come by.</span>
