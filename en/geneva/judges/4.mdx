<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Judges 3:12; Judges 3:7"> And the children of Israel began againe to do wickedly in the sight of the Lord when Ehud was dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 11:1; Judges 4:16; Judges 4:13"> And the Lord sold them into the hande of Iabin King of Canaan, that reigned in Hazor, whose chiefe Captaine was called Sisera, which dwelt in Harosheth of the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 1:19; Psalms 106:42; Judges 3:9"> Then the children of Israel cryed vnto the Lord: (for he had nine hundreth charets of yron, and twentie yeeres he had vexed the children of Israel very sore)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Luke 2:36; 2 Kings 22:14"> And at that time Deborah a Prophetesse the wife of Lapidoth iudged Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:19; Jeremiah 31:15; Exodus 18:26"> And this Deborah dwelt vnder a palme tree, betweene Ramah and Beth-el in mount Ephraim, and the children of Israel came vp to her for iudgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 13:47; Judges 8:18; Hebrews 11:32"> Then shee sent and called Barak the sonne of Abinoam out of Kadesh of Naphtali, and sayd vnto him, Hath not the Lord God of Israel commanded, saying, Goe, and drawe towarde mount Tabor, and take with thee ten thousande men of the children of Naphtali and of the children of Zebulun?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 18:40; Ezekiel 38:10–16; Psalms 83:9–10"> And I wil drawe vnto thee to the riuer Kishon Sisera, the captaine of Iabins armie with his charets, and his multitude, and wil deliuer him into thine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:10–14; Matthew 14:30–31"> And Barak sayd vnto her, If thou wilt go with me, I will go: but if thou wilt not goe with me, I will not go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 2:14; 1 Samuel 2:30; Judges 4:17–22"> Then shee answered, I will surely goe with thee, but this iourney that thou takest, shall not be for thine honour: for the Lord shall sell Sisera into the hand of a woman. And Deborah arose and went with Barak to Kedesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 5:18; Judges 5:15; Judges 4:14"> And Barak called Zebulun and Naphtali to Kedesh, and he went vp on his feete with ten thousand men, and Deborah went vp with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Joshua 19:33; Numbers 10:29"> (Now Heber the Kenite, which was of the children of Hobab the father in lawe of Moses, was departed from the Kenites, and pitched his tent vntill the playne of Zaanaim, which is by Kedesh)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 89:12; Jeremiah 46:18; Joshua 19:12"> Then they shewed Sisera, that Barak the sonne of Abinoam was gone vp to mout Tabor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 4:2–3; Judges 4:7"> And Sisera called for all his charets, euen nine hundreth charets of yron, and all the people that were with him from Harosheth of the Gentiles, vnto the riuer Kishon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:3; 2 Samuel 5:24; Isaiah 52:12"> Then Deborah sayd vnto Barak, Vp: for this is the day that the Lord hath deliuered Sisera into thine hand. Is not the Lord gone out before thee? So Barak went downe from mount Tabor, and ten thousand men after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 10:10; Psalms 83:9–10; Judges 5:20–21"> And the Lord destroyed Sisera and all his charets, and al his hoste with the edge of the sword before Barak, so that Sisera lighted downe off his charet, and fled away on his feete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 2:12; Leviticus 26:7–8; James 2:13"> But Barak pursued after the charets, and after the hoste vnto Harosheth of the Gentiles: and all the hoste of Sisera fel vpon the edge of the sworde: there was not a man left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 69:22; Isaiah 57:21; Amos 5:19–20"> Howbeit Sisera fled away on his feete to the tent of Iael the wife of Heber the Kenite: (for peace was betweene Iabin the king of Hazor, and betweene the house of Heber the Kenite)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 6:19"> And Iael went out to meete Sisera, and sayd vnto him, Turne in, my lord, turne in to me: feare not. And when he had turned in vnto her into her tent, she couered him with a mantell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 5:25–26; Genesis 24:43; Isaiah 41:17"> And he said vnto her, Giue me, I pray thee, a litle water to drinke: for I am thirstie. And shee opened a bottel of milke, and gaue him drinke, and couered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 2:3–5; 2 Samuel 17:20"> Againe he sayde vnto her, Stande in the doore of the tent, and when any man doth come and enquire of thee, saying, Is any man there? thou shalt say, Nay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 5:26–27; 1 Corinthians 1:27; Judges 3:21"> Then Iael Hebers wife tooke a nayle of the tent, and tooke an hammer in her hande, and went softly vnto him, and smote the nayle into his temples, and fastened it into the grounde, (for he was fast a sleepe and weary) and so he dyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:10–15; 2 Samuel 17:3"> And behold, as Barak pursued after Sisera, Iael came out to meete him, and sayd vnto him, Come, and I wil shewe thee the man, whome thou seekest: and when he came into her tent, behold, Sisera lay dead, and the nayle in his temples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:24; Psalms 18:47; Psalms 18:39"> So God brought downe Iabin the King of Canaan that day before the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:12"> And the hande of the children of Israel prospered, and preuailed against Iabin the King of Canaan, vntill they had destroyed Iabin King of Canaan.</span>
