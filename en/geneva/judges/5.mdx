<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Revelation 19:1–3; Psalms 18:1"> Then sang Deborah, and Barak the sonne of Abinoam the same day, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 5:9; 2 Chronicles 17:16; Psalms 110:3"> Praise ye the Lord for the auenging of Israel, and for the people that offred themselues willingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 138:4–5; Psalms 119:46; Psalms 49:1–2"> Heare, ye Kings, hearken ye princes: I, euen I will sing vnto the Lord: I will sing praise vnto the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Psalms 68:7–8; Psalms 77:17"> Lord, when thou wentest out of Seir, when thou departedst out of the field of Edom, the earth trembled, and the heauens rained, the cloudes also dropped water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 19:18; Psalms 97:5; Isaiah 64:1–3"> The mountaines melted before the Lord, as did that Sinai before the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 33:8; Judges 3:31; Lamentations 1:4"> In the dayes of Shamgar the sonne of Anath, in the dayes of Iael the hie wayes were vnoccupied, and the trauelers walked through by wayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; 2 Samuel 20:19; Esther 9:19"> The townes were not inhabited: they decayed, I say, in Israel, vntill I Deborah came vp, which rose vp a mother in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16–17; Judges 2:12; Judges 2:17"> They chose new gods: then was warre in the gates. Was there a shielde or speare seene among fourtie thousand of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 5:2; 2 Corinthians 8:3–4; 2 Corinthians 9:5"> Mine heart is set on the gouernours of Israel, and on them that are willing among the people: praise ye the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 12:14; Judges 10:4; Psalms 105:2"> Speake ye that ride on white asses, yee that dwel by Middin, and that walke by the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 6:5; Judges 5:8; 1 Samuel 12:7"> For the noyse of the archers appaised among the drawers of water: there shall they rehearse the righteousnesse of the Lord, his righteousnesse of his townes in Israel: then did the people of the Lord goe downe to the gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:8; Psalms 68:18; Psalms 57:8"> Vp Deborah, vp, arise, and sing a song: arise Barak, and leade thy captiuitie captiue, thou sonne of Abinoam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 2:26–27; Romans 8:37; Psalms 75:7"> For they that remaine, haue dominio ouer the mightie of the people: the Lord hath giuen me dominion ouer the strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 12:15; Numbers 32:39–40; Judges 3:27"> Of Ephraim their roote arose against Amalek: and after thee, Beniamin shall fight against thy people, O Amalek: of Machir came rulers, and of Zebulun they that handle the pen of the writer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 4:14; Proverbs 22:13; Acts 20:13"> And the Princes of Issachar were with Deborah, and Issachar, and also Barak: he was set on his feete in the valley: for the diuisions of Reuben were great thoughts of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 32:24; Numbers 32:1–5; Judges 5:15"> Why abodest thou among the sheepefolds, to heare the bleatings of the flockes? for the diuisions of Reuben were great thoughts of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 13:25; Joshua 19:24–31; Joshua 13:31"> Gilead abode beyonde Iorden: and why doeth Dan remayne in shippes? Asher sate on the sea shoare, and taryed in his decayed places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 4:10; Judges 4:6; Esther 4:16"> But the people of Zebulun and Naphtali haue ieopard their liues vnto the death in the hie places of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Judges 1:27; 1 Kings 4:12"> The Kings came and fought: then fought the Kings of Canaan in Taanach by the waters of Megiddo: they receiued no gaine of money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; 1 Samuel 7:10; Psalms 77:17–18"> They fought from heauen, euen the starres in their courses fought against Sisera.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 4:7; Micah 7:10; Psalms 44:5"> The Riuer Kishon swepe them away, that ancient riuer the riuer Kishon. O my soule, thou hast marched valiantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 20:7; Psalms 147:10–11; Jeremiah 47:4"> Then were the horsehooues broken with the oft beating together of their mightie men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:10; Judges 21:9–10; 1 Corinthians 16:22"> Curse ye Meroz: (sayd the Angel of the Lord) curse the inhabitantes thereof, because they came not to helpe the Lord, to helpe the Lord against the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 1:42; Luke 1:28; Judges 4:17"> Iael the wife of Heber the Kenite shall be blessed aboue other women: blessed shall she be aboue women dwelling in tentes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 4:19–21"> He asked water, and shee gaue him milke: she brought forth butter in a lordly dish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:49–51; Judges 4:21; 2 Samuel 20:22"> She put her hand to the naile, and her right hand to the workemans hammer: with the hammer smote she Sisera: she smote off his head, after she had wounded, and pearsed his temples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 52:7; James 2:13; Matthew 7:2"> He bowed him downe at her feete, he fell downe, and lay still: at her feete hee bowed him downe, and fell: and when he had sunke downe, he lay there dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 4:15; James 5:7; Song of Solomon 8:14"> The mother of Sisera looked out at a windowe, and cryed thorowe the lattesse, Why is his charet so long a comming? why tary the wheeles of his charets?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Her wise ladies answered her, Yea. Shee answered her selfe with her owne wordes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 15:9; 2 Samuel 13:18; Genesis 37:3"> Haue they not gotten, and they deuide the spoyle? euery man hath a mayde or two. Sisera hath a praye of diuers coloured garmentes, a pray of sundry colours made of needle worke: of diuers colours of needle worke on both sides, for the chiefe of the spoyle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 37:6; 2 Samuel 23:4; Psalms 19:4–5"> So let all thine enemies perish, O Lord: but they that loue him, shall be as the Sunne when he riseth in his might, and the lande had rest fourtie yeres.</span>
