<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:12–33"> And Iphtah said vnto them, I and my people were at great strife with the children of Ammon, and when I called you, ye deliuered me not out of their handes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:5; 1 Samuel 28:21; Job 13:14"> So when I sawe that ye deliuered me not, I put my life in mine hands, and went vpon the children of Ammon: so the Lord deliuered them into mine handes. Wherefore then are ye come vpon me nowe to fight against me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:10; Deuteronomy 3:12–17; Proverbs 15:1"> Then Iphtah gathered all the men of Gilead, and fought with Ephraim: and the men of Gilead smote Ephraim, because they said, Ye Gileadites are runnagates of Ephraim among the Ephraimites, and among the Manassites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 3:28; Joshua 22:11; Joshua 2:7"> Also the Gileadites tooke the passages of Iorden before the Ephraimites, and when the Ephraimites that were escaped, saide, Let me passe, then the men of Gilead said vnto him, Art thou an Ephraimite? If he said, Nay,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:2; Psalms 69:15; Mark 14:70"> Then said they vnto him, Say nowe Shibboleth: and he said, Sibboleth: for he could not so pronounce: then they tooke him, and slewe him at the passages of Iorden: and there fel at that time of the Ephraimites two and fourtie thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Iphtah iudged Israel sixe yeere: then dyed Iphtah the Gileadite, and was buryed in one of the cities of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 5:2; 1 Samuel 16:1; Matthew 2:1"> After him Ibzan of Beth-lehem iudged Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 10:4; Judges 12:14"> Who had thirtie sonnes and thirtie daughters, which he sent out, and tooke in thirtie daughters from abroade for his sonnes. and he iudged Israel seuen yeere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then Ibzan died, and was buryed at Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And after him iudged Israel Elon, a Zebulonite, and he iudged Israel tenne yeere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:13; 1 Chronicles 6:69; Joshua 19:42"> Then Elon the Zebulonite dyed, and was buryed in Aijalon in the countrey of Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And after him Abdon the sonne of Hillel the Pirathonite iudged Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 5:10; Judges 10:4"> And he had fourty sonnes and thirtie nephewes that rode on seuentie assecoltes: and he iudged Israel eight yeeres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 5:14; Judges 3:13; Judges 3:27"> Then dyed Abdon the sonne of Hillel the Pirathonite, and was buryed in Pirathon, in ye lande of Ephraim, in the Mount of the Amalekites.</span>
