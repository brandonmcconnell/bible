<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 38:17; Genesis 29:21; Luke 15:29"> But within a while after, in the time of wheate haruest, Samson visited his wife with a kid, saying, I wil go in to my wife into the chamber: but her father would not suffer him to goe in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 14:20; Judges 14:16; Genesis 38:14"> And her father sayde, I thought that thou hadst hated her: therefore gaue I her to thy companion. Is not her yonger sister fayrer then shee? take her, I pray thee, in stead of the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 14:15"> Then Samson saide vnto them, Nowe am I more blamelesse then the Philistims: therefore will I doe them displeasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:15; Psalms 63:10; Lamentations 5:18"> And Samson went out, and tooke three hundreth foxes, and tooke firebrands, and turned them taile to taile, and put a firebrand in ye middes betweene two tailes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:30; Exodus 22:6"> And when he had set the brandes on fire, he sent them out into the standing corne of the Philistims, and burnt vp both the rickes and the standing corne with the vineyardes and oliues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 14:15; 1 Thessalonians 4:6; Proverbs 22:8"> Then the Philistims sayde, Who hath done this? And they answered, Samson the sonne in law of the Timnite, because hee had taken his wife, and giuen her to his companion. Then the Philistims came vp and burnt her and her father with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 14:4; Romans 12:19; Judges 14:19"> And Samson saide vnto them, Though yee haue done this, yet wil I be auenged of you, and then I wil cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 63:3; Isaiah 63:6; Isaiah 25:10"> So hee smote them hippe and thigh with a mightie plague: then hee went and dwelt in the top of the rocke Etam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 15:17; Judges 15:19; Judges 15:14"> Then the Philistims came vp, and pitched in Iudah, and were spred abroad in Lehi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the men of Iudah sayde, Why are yee come vp vnto vs? And they answered, To binde Samson are we come vp, and to do to him as hee hath done to vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 13:1; Judges 14:4; Deuteronomy 28:47–48"> Then three thousande men of Iudah went to the top of the rocke Etam, and sayde to Samson, Knowest thou not that the Philistims are rulers ouer vs? Wherefore then hast thou done thus vnto vs? And he answered them, As they did vnto me, so haue I done vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 8:21; Acts 7:25; 1 Kings 2:25"> Againe they sayd vnto him, Wee are come to binde thee, and to deliuer thee into the hande of the Philistims. And Samson sayde vnto them, Sweare vnto me, that yee will not fall vpon me your selues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And they answered him, saying, No, but we will bynde thee and deliuer thee vnto their hande, but we will not kill thee. And they bound him with two newe cordes, and brought him from the rocke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 14:19; Judges 3:10; Psalms 118:11"> When hee came to Lehi, the Philistims shouted against him, and the Spirite of the Lord came vpon him, and the cordes that were vpon his armes, became as flaxe that was burnt with fire: for the bandes loosed from his handes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 23:10; Leviticus 26:8; Judges 3:31"> And he found a new iawebone of an asse, and put forth his hand, and caught it, and slewe a thousand men therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then Samson sayd, With the iaw of an asse are heapes vpon heapes: with the iawe of an asse haue I slaine a thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And when he had left speaking, hee cast away the iawebone out of his hande, and called that place, Ramath-Lehi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7–8; Hebrews 11:32; Judges 8:4"> And he was sore a thirst, and called on the Lord, and sayde, Thou hast giuen this great deliuerance into the hand of thy seruaunt: and nowe shall I dye for thirst, and fall into the handes of the vncircumcised?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Genesis 45:27; Psalms 34:6"> Then God brake the cheeke tooth, that was in the iawe, and water came thereout: and when he had drunke, his Spirit came againe, and he was reuiued: wherefore the name therof is called, Enhakkore, which is in Lehi vnto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 13:1; Judges 16:31; Judges 13:5"> And hee iudged Israel in the dayes of the Philistims twentie yeeres.</span>
