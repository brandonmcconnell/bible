<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:32; Judges 6:12; 2 Kings 5:1"> Then Gilead begate Iphtah, and Iphtah the Gileadite was a valiant man, but the sonne of an harlot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Proverbs 2:16; Deuteronomy 23:2"> And Gileads wife bare him sonnes, and when the womans children were come to age, they thrust out Iphtah, and sayd vnto him, Thou shalt not inherite in our fathers house: for thou art the sonne of a strange woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 9:4; 1 Samuel 22:2; 2 Samuel 10:8"> Then Iphtah fledde from his brethren, and dwelt in the land of Tob: and there gathered idle fellowes to Iphtah, and went out with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 10:9"> And in processe of time the children of Ammon made warre with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Acts 7:35–39; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29"> And when the children of Ammon fought with Israel, the Elders of Gilead went to fet Iphtah out of the land of Tob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And they saide vnto Iphtah, Come and be our captaine, that we may fight with the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Genesis 45:4–5; Isaiah 60:14"> Iphtah then answered the Elders of Gilead, Did not ye hate me, and expell me out of my fathers house? how then come you vnto me now in time of your tribulation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 10:18; 1 Kings 13:6; Exodus 10:17"> Then the Elders of Gilead saide vnto Iphtah, Therefore we turne againe to thee now, that thou mayest goe with vs, and fight against the children of Ammon, and bee our head ouer all the inhabitants of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–29"> And Iphtah said vnto the Elders of Gilead, If ye bring me home againe to fight against the children of Ammon, if the Lord giue them before me, shall I be your head?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:5; Genesis 31:50; Jeremiah 29:23"> And the Elders of Gilead saide to Iphtah, The Lord be witnesse betweene vs, if we doe not according to thy wordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 10:17; 1 Samuel 10:17; Judges 20:1"> Then Iphtah went with the Elders of Gilead, and the people made him head and captaine ouer them: and Iphtah rehearsed all his wordes before the Lord in Mizpeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26; Matthew 18:15–16; Proverbs 25:8–9"> Then Iphtah sent messengers vnto the king of the children of Ammon, saying, What hast thou to doe with me, that thou art come against me, to fight in my lande?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 32:22; Numbers 21:24–26; Deuteronomy 2:37"> And the King of the children of Ammon answered vnto the messengers of Iphtah, Because Israel tooke my lande, when they came vp from Egypt, from Arnon vnto Iabbok, and vnto Iorden: now therefore restore those lands quietly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:11; Romans 12:18; Hebrews 12:14"> Yet Iphtah sent messengers againe vnto the King of the children of Ammon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Deuteronomy 2:9; Numbers 21:27–30"> And said vnto him, Thus saith Iphtah, Israel tooke not the lande of Moab, nor the lande of the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 14:25; Deuteronomy 1:40; Numbers 20:1"> But when Israel came vp from Egypt, and walked through the wildernesse vnto the redde Sea, then they came to Kadesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 20:14–21; Deuteronomy 2:4–9; Numbers 20:1"> And Israel sent messengers vnto the king of Edom, saying, Let me, I pray thee, goe thorowe thy lande: but the King of Edom woulde not consent: and also they sent vnto the King of Moab, but he would not: therefore Israel abode in Kadesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:1–8; Numbers 22:36; Numbers 21:4–13"> Then they went through the wildernesse, and compassed the lande of Edom, and the lande of Moab, and came by the Eastside of the lande of Moab, and pitched on the other side of Arnon, and came not within the coast of Moab: for Arnon was the border of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Deuteronomy 2:26–36; Deuteronomy 3:1–17"> Also Israel sent messengers vnto Sihon, King of the Amorites, the King of Heshbon, and Israel said vnto him, Let vs passe, we pray thee, by thy lande vnto our place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 13:15–32; Numbers 21:23–25; Psalms 136:17–21"> But Sihon consented not to Israel, that he shoulde goe through his coast: but Sihon gathered all his people together, and pitched in Iahaz, and fought with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the Lord God of Israel gaue Sihon and all his folke into the handes of Israel, and they smote them: so Israel possessed all the lande of the Amorites, the inhabitants of that countrey:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36"> And they possessed all the coast of the Amorites, from Arnon vnto Iabbok, and from the wildernesse euen vnto Iorden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Nowe therefore the Lord God of Israel hath cast out the Amorites before his people Israel, and shouldest thou possesse it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 21:29; Psalms 78:55; Micah 4:5"> Wouldest not thou possesse that which Chemosh thy god giueth thee to possesse? So whomesoeuer the Lord our God driueth out before vs, them will we possesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Micah 6:5; Joshua 24:9–10; Deuteronomy 23:3–4"> And art thou nowe farre better then Balak the sonne of Zippor King of Moab? did he not striue with Israel and fight against them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36; Joshua 12:5; Judges 10:8"> When Israel dwelt in Heshbon and in her townes, and in Aroer and in her townes, and in all the cities that are by the coastes of Arnon, three hundreth yeeres? why did ye not then recouer them in that space?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:15; Genesis 16:5; 1 Samuel 24:12"> Wherefore, I haue not offended thee: but thou doest me wrong to warre against me. The Lord the Iudge be iudge this day betweene the children of Israel, and the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 14:11; Proverbs 16:18"> Howbeit the King of the children of Ammon hearkened not vnto the wordes of Iphtah, which he had sent him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 11:25; Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25"> Then the Spirite of the Lord came vpon Iphtah, and he passed ouer to Gilead and to Manasseh, and came to Mizpeh in Gilead, and from Mizpeh in Gilead he went vnto the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 28:20; 1 Samuel 1:11; Ecclesiastes 5:1–2"> And Iphtah vowed a vowe vnto the Lord, and said, If thou shalt deliuer the children of Ammon into mine handes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 66:13–14; Leviticus 27:28–29; 1 Samuel 1:28"> Then that thing that commeth out of the doores of mine house to meete me, when I come home in peace from the children of Ammon, shall be the Lordes, and I will offer it for a burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 1:4; Judges 2:18; Judges 3:10"> And so Iphtah went vnto the children of Ammon to fight against them, and the Lord deliuered them into his handes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:17; Deuteronomy 2:36"> And he smote them from Aroer euen till thou come to Minnith, twentie cities, and so foorth to Abel of the vineyardes, with an exceeding great slaughter. Thus the children of Ammon were humbled before the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:4; Exodus 15:20; Judges 10:17"> Nowe when Iphtah came to Mizpeh vnto his house, beholde, his daughter came out to meete him with timbrels and daunces, which was his onely childe: he had none other sonne, nor daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 14:7–9; Genesis 37:29–30; Genesis 42:36–38"> And when hee sawe her, hee rent his clothes, and saide, Alas my daughter, thou hast brought me lowe, and art of them that trouble me: for I haue opened my mouth vnto the Lord, and can not goe backe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:19; Philippians 2:30; Romans 16:4"> And she said vnto him, My father, if thou hast opened thy mouth vnto the Lord, doe with me as thou hast promised, seeing that the Lord hath auenged thee of thine enemies the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 1:25; 1 Samuel 1:6"> Also she saide vnto her father, Doe thus much for me: suffer me two moneths, that I may goe to the mountaines, and bewaile my virginitie, I and my fellowes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And he sayde, Goe: and he sent her away two moneths: so she went with her companions, and lamented her virginitie vpon the moutaines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 66:3; Judges 11:31; Leviticus 27:28–29"> And after the ende of two moneths, she turned againe vnto her father, who did with her according to his vowe which he had vowed, and she had knowen no man. and it was a custome in Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Kings 9:25; Judges 5:11"> The daughters of Israel went yere by yere to lament the daughter of Iphtah the Gileadite, foure dayes in a yeere.</span>
