<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:28; 1 Chronicles 28:5; 1 Kings 1:33–39"> So when Dauid was olde and full of dayes, he made Salomon his sonne King ouer Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 24:1; 2 Chronicles 34:29–30; 1 Chronicles 13:1"> And hee gathered together all the princes of Israel with the Priestes and the Leuites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Numbers 4:2–49"> And the Leuites were numbred from ye age of thirtie yeere and aboue, and their nomber according to their summe was eight and thirtie thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:8; Deuteronomy 16:18; 1 Chronicles 26:29–31"> Of these foure and twentie thousande were set to aduance the worke of the house of the Lord, and sixe thousand were ouerseers and iudges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:25–26; 2 Chronicles 8:14; Psalms 87:7"> And foure thousand were porters, and foure thousande praised the Lord with instruments which he made to praise the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 6:1; 1 Chronicles 6:16"> So Dauid deuided offices vnto them, to wit, to the sonnes of Leui, to Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:21; Exodus 6:17; 1 Chronicles 6:17–20"> Of the Gershonites were Laadan and Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:33–34; 1 Chronicles 15:20–21; 1 Chronicles 15:7"> The sonnes of Laadan, the chiefe was Iehiel, and Zetham and Ioel, three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The Sonnes of Shimei, Shelomith, and Haziel, and Haram, three: these were the chiefe fathers of Laadan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Also the sonnes of Shimei were Iahath, Zina, Ieush, and Beriah: these foure were ye sonnes of Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Iahath was the chiefe, and Zizah the seconde, but Ieush and Beriah had not many sonnes: therfore they were in the families of their father, counted but as one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; Numbers 3:27; Numbers 26:58"> The sonnes of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron aud Vzziel, foure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Exodus 6:20; 1 Samuel 2:28"> The sonnes of Amram, Aaron and Moses: and Aaron was separated to sanctifie the most holy place, hee and his sonnes for euer to burne incense before the Lord, to minister to him, and to blesse in his Name for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; Psalms 90:1; 1 Chronicles 26:23–25"> Moses also the man of God, and his children were named with the tribe of Leui.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 18:3–4; Exodus 2:22; Exodus 4:20"> The sonnes of Moses were Gershom, and Eliezer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:24; 1 Chronicles 25:20; 1 Chronicles 24:20"> Of the sonnes of Gershom was Shebuel the chiefe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:25"> And the sonne of Eliezer was Rehabiah the chiefe: for Eliezer had none other sonnes: but the sonnes of Rehabiah were very many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:26; 1 Chronicles 24:22"> The sone of Izhar was Shelomith ye chiefe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:23; 1 Chronicles 15:9; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> The sonnes of Hebron were Ieriah the first, Amariah the second, Iahaziel the third, and Iekamiam the fourth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The sones of Vzziel were Michah the first, and Isshiah the second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 6:19; 1 Chronicles 6:20; 1 Chronicles 24:26–30"> The sonnes of Merari were Mahli and Mushi. The sonnes of Mahli, Eleazar and Kish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:28; Numbers 36:6–8"> And Eleazar dyed, and had no sonnes, but daughters, and their brethren the sonnes of Kish tooke them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:30"> The sonnes of Mushi were Mahli, and Eder, and Ierimoth, three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 10:17; Numbers 10:21; 1 Chronicles 23:3"> These were the sonnes of Leui according to the house of their fathers, euen the chiefe fathers according to their offices, according to the nomber of names and their summe that did the worke for the seruice of the house of the Lord from the age of twentie yeeres and aboue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 132:13–14; 1 Chronicles 22:18; 2 Samuel 7:11"> For Dauid sayde, The Lord God of Israel hath giuen rest vnto his people, that they may dwell in Ierusalem for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 4:5; Numbers 7:9; Numbers 4:15"> And also the Leuites shall no more beare the Tabernacle and al the vessels for the seruice thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Psalms 72:20; 2 Samuel 23:1"> Therefore according to the last wordes of Dauid, the Leuites were nombred from twentie yeere and aboue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:3–6; 2 Chronicles 31:11; 2 Chronicles 35:11–14"> And their office was vnder the hand of the sonnes of Aaron, for the seruice of the house of the Lord in the courtes, and chambers, and in the purifiyng of all holy thinges, and in the worke of the seruice of the house of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 19:35–36; Exodus 25:30; Leviticus 6:20–23"> Both for the shewbread, and for the fine floure, for the meate offring, and for the vnleauened cakes, and for the fryed things, and for that which was rosted, and for all measures and cise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 92:1–3; Ezra 3:10–11; 1 Chronicles 9:33"> And for to stand euery morning, to giue thanks and praise to the Lord, and likewise at euen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13–14; Numbers 28:1–29; Numbers 10:10"> And to offer all burnt offrings vnto the Lord, in the Sabbaths, in the moneths, and at the appointed times, according to the nomber and according to their custome continually before the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:38; Numbers 3:6–9"> And that they should keepe the charge of the Tabernacle of the Congregation, and the charge of ye holy place, and the charge of ye sonnes of Aaron their brethren in the seruice of ye house of the Lord.</span>
