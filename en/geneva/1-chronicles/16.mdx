<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:1; Ezra 6:16–18; 2 Chronicles 5:6–7"> So they brought in the Arke of God, and set it in the middes of the Tabernacle that Dauid had pitched for it, and they offred burnt offrings and peace offrings before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 47:10; Genesis 14:19; Leviticus 1:3"> And when Dauid had made an ende of offering the burnt offering and the peace offerings, hee blessed the people in the Name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:10; Ezekiel 45:17; 2 Chronicles 35:7–8"> And he dealt to euery one of Israel both man and woman, to euery one a cake of breade, and a piece of flesh, and a bottel of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:27–32; Psalms 72:18; Psalms 103:2"> And he appointed certaine of the Leuites to minister before the Arke of the Lord, and to rehearse and to thanke and prayse the Lord God of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:39; 2 Chronicles 29:25; 1 Chronicles 15:16–24"> Asaph the chiefe, and next to him Zechariah, Ieiel, and Shemiramoth, and Iehiel, and Mattithiah, and Eliab, and Benaiah, and Obed Edom, euen Ieiel with instruments, violes and harpes, and Asaph to make a sound with cymbales,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:12; 2 Chronicles 29:26–28; 2 Chronicles 5:12–13"> And Benaiah and Iahaziel Priestes, with trumpets continually before the Arke of the couenant of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:1; Psalms 18:1; 2 Samuel 23:1–2"> Then at that time Dauid did appoint at the beginning to giue thankes to the Lord by the hand of Asaph and his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 105:1–15; Isaiah 12:4; 2 Kings 19:19"> Praise the Lord and call vpon his Name: declare his workes among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Psalms 96:1–3; Psalms 98:1–4"> Sing vnto him, sing praise vnto him, and talke of all his wonderfull workes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; Jeremiah 29:13; Psalms 34:2"> Reioyce in his holy Name: let the hearts of them that seeke the Lord reioyce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:8–9; Zephaniah 2:2–3; Psalms 68:35"> Seeke the Lord and his strength: seeke his face continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 111:4; Psalms 119:20; Psalms 103:2"> Remember his marueilous workes that he hath done, his wonders, and the iudgements of his mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Exodus 19:5–6; Psalms 135:4"> O seede of Israel his seruant, O the children of Iaakob his chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 48:10–11; Isaiah 26:9; Exodus 15:2"> He is the Lord our God: his iudgements are throughout all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 105:8; Malachi 4:4; Deuteronomy 7:9"> Remember his couenant for euer, and the worde, which hee commanded to a thousand generations:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 26:3; Genesis 17:2; Genesis 35:11"> Which he made with Abraham, and his othe to Izhak:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Genesis 35:11–12; Joshua 24:11–13"> And hath confirmed it to Iaakob for a Law, and to Israel for an euerlasting couenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Micah 2:5; Genesis 17:8"> Saying, To thee will I giue the land of Canaan, the lot of your inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Deuteronomy 7:7; Acts 7:5"> When ye were fewe in number, yea, a very fewe, and strangers therein,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 46:6; Genesis 20:1; Genesis 46:3"> And walked about from nation to nation, and from one kingdome to another people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 12:17; Genesis 20:3; Exodus 7:15–18"> He suffered no man to do them wrong, but rebuked Kings for their sakes, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 20:7; Psalms 105:15; Genesis 49:8–10"> Touch not mine anoynted, and doe my Prophets no harme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 96:1–13; Psalms 71:15; Isaiah 51:6–8"> Sing vnto the Lord all the earth: declare his saluation from day to day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2–6; Daniel 4:1–3; Psalms 22:27"> Declare his glory among the nations, and his wonderful workes among all people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 89:7; Psalms 76:7; Psalms 66:3–5"> For the Lord is great and much to be praised, and hee is to bee feared aboue all gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 102:25; Leviticus 19:4; Isaiah 42:5"> For all the gods of the people are idoles, but the Lord made the heauens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 96:6; Psalms 28:7–8"> Prayse and glory are before him: power and beautie are in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:10; Philippians 4:13; Psalms 100:1–2"> Giue vnto the Lord, ye families of the people: giue vnto the Lord glory and power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 100:4; Psalms 96:9; Isaiah 6:3"> Giue vnto the Lord ye glory of his Name: bring an offring and come before him, and worship the Lord in the glorious Sanctuarie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 33:9; Psalms 148:5–6; Hebrews 1:3"> Tremble ye before him, al the earth: surely the world shalbe stable and not moue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 96:10; Revelation 19:6; Psalms 97:1"> Let the heauens reioyce, and let the earth be glad, and let them say among the nations, The Lord reigneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 148:9–10; Isaiah 44:23; Psalms 98:7–8"> Let the sea roare, and all that therein is: Let the field be ioyfull and all that is in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 98:9; Psalms 96:12–13; Revelation 11:17–18"> Let the trees of the wood then reioyce at the presence of the Lord: for he commeth to iudge the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 106:1; 2 Chronicles 7:3; Psalms 107:1"> Prayse the Lord, for hee is good, for his mercie endureth for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 106:47–48; 1 Peter 2:9; Psalms 14:7"> And say ye, Saue vs, O God, our saluation, and gather vs, and deliuer vs from the heathen, that we may prayse thine holy Name, and glorie in thy praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 8:15; Psalms 72:18–19; 1 Kings 8:56"> Blessed be the Lord God of Israel for euer and euer: and let all people say, So be it, and praise the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; Ezra 3:4; 1 Chronicles 16:4–6"> Then he left there before the Arke of the Lordes couenant Asaph and his brethren to minister continually before the Arke, that which was to be done euery day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:10; 1 Chronicles 13:14; 1 Chronicles 25:3"> And Obed Edom and his brethren, three score and eight: and Obed Edom the sonne of Ieduthun, and Hosah were porters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 3:4; 1 Chronicles 15:11; 2 Chronicles 1:3–4"> And Zadok the Priest and his brethren the Priestes were before the Tabernacle of ye Lord, in the hie place that was at Gibeon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 28:3–8; Exodus 29:38–42; 2 Chronicles 31:3"> To offer burnt offrings vnto the Lord, vpon the burnt offring altar continually, in the morning and in the euening, euen according vnto all that is written in the law of the Lord, which hee commanded Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:13; 1 Chronicles 25:1–6; 2 Chronicles 20:21"> And with them were Heman, and Ieduthun, and the rest that were chosen (which were appointed by names) to praise the Lord, because his mercie endureth for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 150:3–6; 1 Chronicles 25:6–7; 2 Chronicles 29:25–28"> Euen with them were Heman and Ioduthun, to make a sound with the cornets and with the cymbales, with excellent instruments of musicke: and the sonnes of Ieduthun were at the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:19–20; Genesis 18:19; Psalms 101:2"> And all the people departed, euerie man to his house: and Dauid returned to blesse his house.</span>
