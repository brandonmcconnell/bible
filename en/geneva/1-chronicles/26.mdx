<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:19; Numbers 26:9–11; Psalms 44:1"> Concerning the diuisions of the porters, of the Korhites, Meshelemiah the sonne of Kore of the sonnes of Asaph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:21"> And the sonnes of Meshelemiah, Zechariah the eldest, Iediael the seconde, Zebadiah the third, Iathniel the fourth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Elam the fift, Ichohanan the sixt, and Eliehoenai the seuenth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 15:24; 1 Chronicles 15:18"> And the sonnes of Obed Edom, Shemaiah the eldest, Iehozabad the second, Ioah the third, and Sacar the fourth, and Nethaneel the fift,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 128:1"> Ammiel the sixt, Issachar the seuenth, Peulthai the eight: for God had blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:28; 1 Timothy 6:12; Judges 6:12"> And to Shemaiah his sonne, were sonnes borne, that ruled in the house of their father, for they were men of might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The sonnes of Shemaiah were Othni, and Rephael, and Obed, Elzabad and his brethren, strong men: Elihu also, and Semachiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:6; 1 Peter 4:11; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11"> All these were of the sonnes of Obed Edom, they and their sonnes and their brethren mightie and strong to serue, euen three score and two of Obed Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:14; 1 Chronicles 26:1"> And of Meshelemiah sonnes and brethren, eighteene mightie men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2"> And of Hosah of the sonnes of Merari, the sonnes were Shuri the chiefe, and (though he was not the eldest, yet his father made him the chiefe)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Helkiah the second, Tebaliah the third, and Zechariah the fourth: all the sonnes and the brethren of Hosa were thirteene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8"> Of these were the diuisions of the porters of the chiefe men, hauing the charge against their brethren, to serue in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8; 1 Chronicles 24:31; 1 Chronicles 24:5"> And they cast lottes both small and great for the house of their fathers, for euery gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the lot on the East side fel to Shelemiah: then they cast lottes for Zechariah his sonne a wise couseller, and his lot came out Northward:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:24; 1 Chronicles 26:17"> To Obed Edom Southwarde, and to his sonnes the house of Asuppim:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:24; 1 Chronicles 26:10–12; 1 Kings 10:5"> To Shuppim and to Hosah Westwarde with the gate of Shallecheth by the paued streete that goeth vpward, warde ouer against warde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 9:24"> Eastward were sixe Leuites, and Northwarde foure a day, and Southward foure a day, and toward Asuppim two and two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 23:11"> In Parbar towarde the West were foure by the paued streete, and two in Parbar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 16:11"> These are the diuisions of the porters of the sonnes of Kore, and of the sonnes of Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:22; 1 Chronicles 28:12–19; 1 Chronicles 22:14–16"> And of the Leuites. Ahiiah was ouer the treasures of the house of God, and ouer the treasures of the dedicate things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:7; 1 Chronicles 6:17; 1 Chronicles 29:8"> Of the sonnes of Laadan the sonnes of the Gershunnites descending of Laadan, the chiefe fathers of Laadan were Gershunni and Iehieli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:8; Nehemiah 10:38; 1 Chronicles 26:20"> The sonnes of Iehieli were Zethan and Ioel his brother, appoynted ouer the treasures of the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 3:27; 1 Chronicles 23:12; Numbers 3:19"> Of the Amramites, of the Izharites, of the Hebronites and of the Ozielites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:20; 1 Chronicles 23:15–16"> And Shebuel the sonne of Gershom, the sonne of Moses, a ruler ouer the treasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:17–18; 1 Chronicles 23:15; Exodus 18:4"> And of his brethren which came of Eliezer, was Rehabiah his sonne, and Ieshaiah his sonne, and Ioram his sonne, and Zichri his sonne, and Shelomith his sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:11; 1 Chronicles 29:2–9; 1 Chronicles 22:14"> Which Shelomith and his brethren were ouer al the treasures of the dedicate things, which Dauid the King, and the chiefe fathers, the captaines ouer thousands, and hundreths, and the captaines of the armie had dedicated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 12:14; Joshua 6:19; Nehemiah 10:32–34"> (For of the battels and of the spoyles they did dedicate to maintaine the house of the Lord)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Samuel 17:55; 2 Samuel 10:9–14"> And al that Samuel the Seer had dedicate and Saul the sonne of Kish and Abner the sonne of Ner, and Ioab the sonne of Zeruiah, and whosoeuer had dedicate any thing, it was vnder the hand of Shelomith, and his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:4; Nehemiah 11:16; 2 Chronicles 34:13"> Of the Izharites was Chenaniah and his sonnes, for the busines without ouer Israel, for officers and for Iudges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:17; 1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> Of the Hebronites, Ashabiah and his brethren, men of actiuitie, a thousande, and seuen hundreth were officers for Israel beyonde Iorden Westward, in all the busines of the Lord, and for the seruice of the King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:19; Joshua 21:39; 1 Chronicles 29:27"> Among the Hebronites was Iediiah the chiefest, euen the Hebronites by his generations according to the families. And in the fourtieth yere of the reigne of Dauid they were sought for: and there were founde among them men of actiuitie at Iazer in Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:11; 1 Chronicles 12:37; 1 Chronicles 23:24"> And his brethren men of actiuitie, two thousand and seuen hundreth chiefe fathers, whom King Dauid made rulers ouer the Reubenites, and the Gadites, and the halfe tribe of Manasseh, for euery matter perteining to God, and for the Kings busines.</span>
