<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:2; 1 Chronicles 11:10–47; 1 Chronicles 27:1–34"> Nowe Dauid assembled all the princes of Israel: the princes of the tribes, and the captaines of the bandes that serued the King, and the captaines of thousands and the captaines of hundreths, and the rulers of all the substance and possession of the King, and of his sonnes, with the eunuches, and the mightie, and all the men of power, vnto Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 99:5; Isaiah 66:1; 1 Chronicles 17:1–2"> And King Dauid stoode vp vpon his feete; and saide, Heare ye me, my brethren and my people: I purposed to haue buylt an house of rest for the Arke of the couenant of the Lord, and for a footestoole of our God, and haue made ready for the building,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:8; 1 Chronicles 17:4; 2 Chronicles 6:8–9"> But God sayde vnto me, Thou shalt not buylde an house for my Name, because thou hast bene a man of warre, and hast shed blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:1; 1 Chronicles 5:2; 1 Samuel 16:6–13"> Yet as the Lord God of Israel chose me before all the house of my father, to be King ouer Israel for euer (for in Iudah woulde he chuse a prince, and of the house of Iudah is the house of my father, and among the sonnes of my father he delited in me to make me King ouer all Israel)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:1–9; 1 Chronicles 23:1; 1 Chronicles 22:9–10"> So of all my sonnes (for the Lord hath giuen me many sonnes) he hath euen chosen Salomon my sonne to sit vpon the throne of the kingdome of the Lord ouer Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:13–14; 1 Chronicles 22:9–10; Hebrews 3:6"> And he sayd vnto me, Salomon thy sonne, he shall build mine house and my courtes: for I haue chosen him to be my sonne, and I will be his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:13; 1 Kings 8:61; 1 Kings 6:12–13"> I will stablish therefore his kingdome for euer, if he endeuour himselfe to do my commandements, and my iudgements, as this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Deuteronomy 5:32–3; Proverbs 2:1–5"> Now therefore in the sight of all Israel the Congregation of the Lord, and in the audience of our God, keepe and seeke for all the commandements of the Lord your God, that ye may possesse this good lande, and leaue it for an inheritance for your children after you for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:13; James 4:8–11; Matthew 7:7–8"> And thou, Salomon my sonne, know thou the God of thy father, and serue him with a perfit heart, and with a willing minde: For the Lord searcheth all hearts, and vnderstandeth all the imaginations of thoughts: if thou seeke him, he will be found of thee, but if thou forsake him, he will cast thee off for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:6; 1 Timothy 4:16; 1 Chronicles 22:16–19"> Take heede now, for the Lord hath chosen thee to buylde the house of the Sanctuarie: be strong therefore, and doe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:19; 1 Kings 6:3; Exodus 25:40"> Then Dauid gaue to Salomon his sonne the paterne of the porch and of the houses thereof, and of the closets thereof, and of the galleries thereof, and of the chambers thereof that are within, and of the house of the mercieseate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:20; 1 Kings 14:26; Exodus 25:40"> And the paterne of all that he had in his minde for the courtes of the house of the Lord, and for all the chambers round about, for the treasures of the house of God, and for the treasures of the dedicate things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:29; 1 Chronicles 25:1–31; 1 Kings 7:1–51"> And for the courses of the Priestes, and of the Leuites, and for all the woorke for the seruice of the house of the Lord, and for all the vessels of the ministerie of the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He gaue of golde by weight, for the vessels of gold, for all the vessels of all maner of seruice, and all the vessels of siluer by weight, for all maner vessels of all maner of seruice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Zechariah 4:11–14; Revelation 1:12–13"> The weight also of golde for the candlestickes, and gold for their lampes, with the weight for euery candlesticke, and for the lampes thereof, and for the candlestickes of siluer by the weight of the candlesticke, and the lampes thereof, according to the vse of euery candlesticke,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 7:48; 2 Chronicles 4:8; Exodus 25:23–30"> And the weight of the gold for the tables of shewbread, for euery table, and siluer for the tables of siluer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 10:21; 2 Chronicles 4:20–22; Numbers 7:13–14"> And pure golde for the fleshhookes, and the bowles, and plates, and for basens, golde in weight for euery basen, and for siluer basens, by weight for euery basen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 25:18–22; Exodus 30:1–10; Psalms 80:1"> And for the altar of incense, pure golde by weight, and golde for the paterne of the charet of the Cherubs that spread themselues, and couered the Arke of the couenant of the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:11–12; Exodus 25:40; Ezekiel 3:14"> All, said he, by writing sent to me by the hand of the Lord, which made me vnderstand all the workemanship of the paterne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:13; Joshua 1:5–9; Isaiah 41:10"> And Dauid said to Salomon his sonne, Be strong, and of a valiant courage and doe it: feare not, nor be afraide: for the Lord God, euen my God is with thee: he will not leaue thee nor forsake thee till thou hast finished all the worke for the seruice of the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:1–26; 1 Chronicles 22:17–18; Exodus 31:3"> Beholde also, the companies of the Priests and the Leuites for all the seruice of the house of God, euen they shall be with thee for the whole worke, with euery free heart that is skilfull in any maner of seruice. The princes also and all the people will be wholy at thy commandement.</span>
