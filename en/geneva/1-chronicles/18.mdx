<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:1–18; 1 Samuel 5:8; 2 Samuel 1:20"> And after this Dauid smote the Philistims, and subdued them, and tooke Gath, and the villages thereof out of the hand of the Philistims.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 3:4–5; 1 Kings 10:25; Isaiah 16:1"> And he smote Moab, and the Moabites became Dauids seruants, and brought giftes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 60:1; 2 Samuel 8:3; Genesis 15:18"> And Dauid smote Hadarezer King of Zobah vnto Hamath, as he went to stablish his border by the riuer Perath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:4; 1 Kings 10:26; 1 Kings 4:2"> And Dauid tooke from him a thousand charets, and seuen thousand horsemen, and twentie thousand footemen, and destroyed all the charets, but he reserued of them an hundreth charets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:6; 2 Samuel 8:5–6; 1 Samuel 14:47"> Then came the Aramites of Damascus to succour Hadarezer King of Zobah, but Dauid slewe of the Aramites two and twentie thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:8; Psalms 121:8; Proverbs 21:31"> And Dauid put a garison in Aram of Damascus, and the Aramites became Dauids seruants, and brought giftes: and the Lord preserued Dauid wheresoeuer he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 12:9–10"> And Dauid tooke the shieldes of gold that were of the seruants of Hadarezer, and brought them to Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:8; 2 Chronicles 4:12–18; 1 Kings 7:15–47"> And from Tibhath, and from Chun (cities of Hadarezer) brought Dauid exceeding much brasse, wherewith Salomon made the brasen Sea, and the pillars and the vessels of brasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:9"> Then Tou King of Hamath heard howe Dauid had smitten all the hoste of Hadarezer King of Zobah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:10; Isaiah 39:1; 2 Chronicles 9:1"> Therefore he sent Hadoram his sonne to King Dauid, to salute him, and to reioyce with him, because he had fought against Hadarezer, and beaten him (for Tou had warre with Hadarezer) who brought all vessels of golde and siluer and brasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:13; 1 Chronicles 26:26–27; 1 Kings 7:51"> And King Dauid did dedicate them vnto the Lord, with the siluer and golde that hee brought from all the nations, from Edom, and from Moab, and from the children of Ammon, and from the Philistims, and from Amalek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:11; 2 Samuel 10:10; 2 Samuel 8:13–14"> And Abishai the sonne of Zeruiah smote of Edom in the salt valley eighteene thousand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:6; Genesis 27:37; Psalms 144:10"> And he put a garison in Edom, and all the Edomites became Dauids seruantes: and the Lord preserued Dauid wheresoeuer he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:15; Psalms 89:14; Psalms 78:71–72"> So Dauid reigned ouer all Israel, and executed iudgement and iustice to all his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:6; 1 Kings 4:3; 2 Samuel 8:16"> And Ioab the sonne of Zeruiah was ouer the hoste, and Iehoshaphat the sonne of Ahilud recorder,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; 2 Samuel 20:25; 1 Kings 4:3"> And Zadok the sonne of Ahitub, and Abimelech the sonne of Abiathar were the Priests, and Shausha the Scribe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 2 Samuel 15:18; 1 Kings 2:34–35"> And Benaiah the sonne of Iehoiada was ouer the Cherethites and the Pelethites: and the sonnes of Dauid were chiefe about the King.</span>
