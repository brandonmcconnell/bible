<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:1; 1 Chronicles 17:1–5; Psalms 132:5"> And Dauid made him houses in the citie of Dauid, and prepared a place for the Arke of God, and pitched for it a tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Deuteronomy 31:9; Numbers 18:1–8"> Then Dauid saide, None ought to carie the Arke of God, but the Leuites: for the Lord hath chosen them to beare the Arke of the Lord, and to minister vnto him for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 8:1; 1 Chronicles 13:5; 1 Chronicles 15:1"> And Dauid gathered all Israel together to Ierusalem to bring vp the Arke of the Lord vnto his place, which he had ordeined for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 6:16–22; 1 Chronicles 12:26–28; 1 Chronicles 6:16–30"> And Dauid assembled the sonnes of Aaron, and the Leuites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:22–24"> Of the sonnes of Kohath, Vriel the chiefe, and his brethren sixe score.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:29–30"> Of the sonnes of Merari, Asaiah the chiefe, and his brethren two hundreth and twentie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:11; 1 Chronicles 23:8"> Of the sonnes of Gershom, Ioel the chiefe, and his brethren an hundreth and thirtie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 6:22"> Of the sonnes of Elizaphan, Shemaiah the chiefe, and his brethren two hundreth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 26:30–31"> Of the sonnes of Hebron, Eliel the chiefe, and his brethren fourescore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> Of the sonnes of Vzziel, Amminadab the chiefe, and his brethren an hundreth and twelue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:28; 1 Kings 2:35; 1 Samuel 22:20–23"> And Dauid called Zadok and Abiathar the Priestes, and of the Leuites, Vriel, Asaiah and Ioel, Shemaiah, and Eliel, and Amminadab:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:6; Exodus 19:14–15; 1 Chronicles 15:14"> And he saide vnto them, Ye are the chiefe fathers of the Leuites: sanctifie your selues, and your brethren, and bring vp the Arke of the Lord God of Israel vnto the place that I haue prepared for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:3; 1 Corinthians 14:40; 1 John 1:8–10"> For because ye were not there at the first, the Lord our God made a breach among vs: for we sought him not after due order.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:15; Joel 2:16–17; 2 Chronicles 29:34"> So the Priestes and the Leuites sanctified them selues to bring vp the Arke of the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Numbers 7:9; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> And the sonnes of the Leuites bare the Arke of God vpon their shoulders with the barres, as Moses had commanded, according to the worde of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; 2 Chronicles 5:13; 1 Chronicles 16:42"> And Dauid spake to the chiefe of the Leuites, that they should appoint certaine of their brethren to sing with instruments of musike, with violes and harpes, and cymbales, that they might make a sounde, and lift vp their voyce with ioye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:39; 1 Chronicles 6:44; 1 Chronicles 6:33"> So the Leuites appointed Heman the sonne of Ioel, and of his brethren Asaph the sonne of Berechiah, and of the sonnes of Merari their brethren, Ethan the sonne of Kushaiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4; 1 Chronicles 26:8; 1 Chronicles 13:14"> And with them their brethren in the seconde degree, Zechariah, Ben, and Iaaziel, and Shemiramoth, and Iehiel, and Vnni, Eliab, and Benaiah, and Maaseiah, and Mattithiah, and Elipheleh, and Mikneah, and Obed Edom, and Ieiel the porters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:6; 1 Chronicles 16:5; 1 Chronicles 13:8"> So Heman, Asaph and Ethan were fingers to make a sounde with cymbales of brasse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 46:1–11"> And Zechariah, and Aziel, and Shemiramoth, and Iehiel, and Vnni, and Eliab, and Maaseiah, and Benaiah with violes on Alamoth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5; 1 Chronicles 25:6–7; Psalms 81:1–2"> And Mattithiah, and Elipheleh, and Mikneah, and Obed Edom, and Ieiel, and Azaziah, with harpes vpon Sheminith Ienazzeah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:27; 1 Chronicles 15:16; 1 Chronicles 25:7–8"> But Chenaniah the chiefe of the Leuites had the charge, bearing ye burden in the charge, for he was able to instruct.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 84:10; 2 Kings 22:4; 1 Chronicles 9:21–23"> And Berechiah and Elkanah were porters for the Arke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:6; Numbers 10:8; 1 Chronicles 15:28"> And Shecaniah and Iehoshaphat and Nethaneel and Amasai, and Zechariah, and Benaiah, and Eliezer the Priestes did blowe with trumpets before the Arke of God, and Obed Edom and Ieiiah were porters for the Arke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:12–23; Micah 5:2; Philippians 3:3"> So Dauid and the Elders of Israel and the captaines of thousandes went to bring vp the Arke of the couenant of the Lord from the house of Obed Edom with ioye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 23:29; 1 Chronicles 29:14; Psalms 66:13–15"> And because that God helped the Leuites that bare the Arke of the couenant of the Lord, they offered seuen bullockes and seuen rammes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:18; 2 Samuel 6:14"> And Dauid had on him a linen garment, as all the Leuites that bare the Arke, and the singers and Chenaniah that had the chiefe charge of the singers: and vpon Dauid was a linnen Ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; 1 Chronicles 15:16; 2 Chronicles 5:12–13"> Thus all Israel brought vp the Arke of the Lordes couenant with shouting and sounde of cornet, and with trumpets, and with cymbales, making a sound with violes and with harpes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:20–23; 2 Samuel 6:16; Deuteronomy 31:26"> And when the Arke of the couenant of the Lord came into the citie of Dauid, Michal the daughter of Saul looked out at a windowe, and sawe King Dauid dauncing and playing, and she despised him in her heart.</span>
