<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 22:8–9"> Also Eastwarde he inhabited vnto the entring in of the wildernes from the riuer Perath for they had much cattel in the land of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 21:9; 1 Chronicles 5:18–21; Psalms 83:6"> And in the dayes of Saul they warred with the Hagarims, which fell by their hands: and they dwelt in their tentes in all the East partes of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 13:11; Joshua 13:24–28; Deuteronomy 3:10–17"> And the children of Gad dwelt ouer against them in the land of Bashan, vnto Salchah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Ioel was the chiefest, and Shapham the second, but Iaanai and Shaphat were in Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And their brethren of the house of their fathers were Michael, and Meshullam, and Sheba, and Sorai, and Iacan, and Zia and Eber, seuen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> These are the childre of Abihail, the sonne of Huri, the sonne of Iaroah, the sonne of Gilead, the sonne of Michael, the sonne of Ieshishai, the sonne of Iahdo, the sonne of Buz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Ahi the sonne of Abdiel, the sonne of Guni was chiefe of the houshold of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:29; Song of Solomon 2:1; Isaiah 35:2"> And they dwelt in Gilead in Bashan, and in the townes thereof, and in all the suburbes of Sharon by their borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 14:16; 2 Kings 14:28; 2 Kings 15:32"> All these were rekoned by genealogies in the dayes of Iotham King of Iudah, and in the dayes of Ieroboam King of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:3; Joshua 4:12–13"> The sonnes of Reuben and of Gad, and of halfe the tribe of Manasseh of those that were viliant men, able to beare shield, and sworde, and to draw a bowe, exercised in warre, were foure and fourtie thousand, seuen hundreth and three score, that went out to the warre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 25:15; 1 Chronicles 1:31; 1 Chronicles 5:10"> And they made warre with the Hagarims, with Ietur, and Naphish, and Nodab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 22:4–5; Psalms 9:10; 2 Chronicles 18:31"> And they were holpen against them, and the Hagarims were deliuered into their hande, and all that were with them: for they cryed to God in the battel, and he heard them, because they trusted in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 18:13; Numbers 31:35"> And they led away their cattel, euen their camels fiftie thousand, and two hundreth, and fiftie thousand sheepe, and two thousand asses, and of persons an hundreth thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:8; Romans 8:31; Joshua 23:10"> For many fel downe wounded, because the warre was of God. And they dwelt in their steads vntill the captiuitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:8–9; Song of Solomon 4:8; Psalms 133:3"> And the children of the halfe tribe of Manasseh dwelt in the land, from Baashan vnto Baal Hermon, and Senir, and vnto mount Hermon: for they increased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:38"> And these were the heads of the housholds of their fathers, euen Epher and Ishi, and Eliel and Azriel, and Ieremiah, and Hodauiah, and Iahdiel, strong men, valiant and famous, heades of the housholdes of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 17:5; Psalms 106:34–39; Exodus 34:15"> But they transgressed against the God of their fathers, and went a whoring after the gods of the people of the lande, whome God had destroyed before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 15:19; 2 Kings 17:6; 2 Kings 15:29"> And the God of Israel stirred vp the spirit of Pul king of Asshur, and the spirite of Tilgath Pilneeser king of Asshur, and he caryed them away: euen the Reubenites and the Gadites, and the halfe tribe of Manasseh, and brought them vnto Halah and Habor, and Hara, and to the riuer Gozan, vnto this day.</span>
