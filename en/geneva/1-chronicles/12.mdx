<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 3:15; Joshua 4:18; Jeremiah 49:19"> These are they that went ouer Iorden in the first moneth when he had filled ouer all his bankes, and put to flight all them of the valley, toward the East and the West.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And there came of the children of Beniamin, and Iudah to the hold vnto Dauid,</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 6:34; Judges 3:10; 2 Samuel 17:25"> And the spirit came vpon Amasai, which was the chiefe of thirtie, and he said, Thine are we, Dauid, and with thee, O sonne of Ishai. Peace, peace be vnto thee, and peace be vnto thine helpers: for thy God helpeth thee. Then Dauid receiued them, and made them captaines of the garison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:2–9"> And of Manasseh some fell to Dauid, when he came with the Philistims against Saul to battell, but they helped them not: for the Princes of the Philistims by aduisement sent him away, saying, He will fall to his master Saul for our heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:17; Deuteronomy 1:15; 1 Samuel 29:11"> As he went to Ziklag, there fell to him of Manasseh, Adnah, and Iozabad, and Iediael, and Michael, and Iozabad, and Elihu, and Ziltai, heads of the thousands that were of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:1–17; 1 Chronicles 12:20; 1 Chronicles 5:24"> And they helped Dauid against that bad: for they were all valiant men and were captaines in the hoste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1; Job 17:9; Genesis 32:2"> For at that time day by day there came to Dauid to helpe him, vntill it was a great hoste, like the hoste of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:3–4; 1 Chronicles 11:10; 1 Samuel 16:1"> And these are the nombers of the captaines that were armed to battell, and came to Dauid to Hebron to turne the kingdome of Saul to him, according to the worde of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The children of Iudah that bare shield and speare, were sixe thousand and eight hundreth armed to the warre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Of the children of Simeon valiant men of warre, seuen thousand and an hundreth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Of the children of Leui foure thousande and sixe hundreth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 11:9; 2 Kings 25:18; 1 Chronicles 6:49–57"> And Iehoiada was the chiefe of them of Aaron: and with him three thousande and seuen hundreth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; 1 Chronicles 6:8; 1 Chronicles 6:53"> And Zadok a yong man very valiant, and of his fathers housholde came two and twentie captaines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8–9; 1 Chronicles 12:2; Genesis 31:23"> And of the children of Beniamin the brethren of Saul three thousande: for a great part of them vnto that time kept the warde of the house of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 6:4"> And of the children of Ephraim twentie thousande, and eight hundreth valiant men and famous men in the housholde of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 17:1–18"> And of the halfe tribe of Manasseh eighteene thousand, which were appointed by name to come and make Dauid King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ephesians 5:17; Luke 12:56–57; Esther 1:13"> And of the children of Issachar which were men that had vnderstanding of the times, to knowe what Israel ought to doe: the heades of them were two hundreth, and all their brethren were at their commandement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; John 1:47"> Of Zebulun that went out to battel, expert in warre, and in all instruments of warre, fiftie thousande which could set the battell in aray: they were not of a double heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And of Naphtali a thousand captaines, and with them with shielde and speare seuen and thirtie thousande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And of Dan expert in battell, eyght and twentie thousande, and sixe hundreth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:33; Joel 2:7"> And of Asher that went out to the battell and were trained in the warres, fourtie thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 14:3; Deuteronomy 3:12–16; Joshua 13:7–32"> And of the other side of Iorden of the Reubenites, and of the Gadites, and of the halfe tribe of Manasseh with all instruments of warre to fight with, an hundreth and twentie thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 49:8–10; 1 Kings 8:61; Psalms 101:2"> All these men of warre that coulde leade an armie, came with vpright heart to Hebron to make Dauid King ouer all Israel: and all the rest of Israel was of one accorde to make Dauid King:</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; 2 Samuel 19:42; Genesis 26:30"> And there they were with Dauid three dayes, eating and drinking: for their brethren had prepared for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Samuel 16:1; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> Moreouer they that were neere them vntill Issachar, and Zebulun, and Naphtali brought bread vpon asses, and on camels, and on mules, and on oxen, euen meate, floure, figges, and reisins, and wine and oyle, and beeues and sheepe abundantly: for there was ioy in Israel.</span>
