<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 10:2; Numbers 3:4; Numbers 26:61"> But Nadab and Abihu died before their father, and had no children: therefore Eleazar and Ithamar executed the Priestes office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; 1 Chronicles 24:31; 1 Samuel 21:1"> And Dauid distributed them, euen Zadok of the sonnes of Eleazar, and Ahimelech of the sonnes of Ithamar according to their offices in their ministration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Numbers 25:11–13; 1 Chronicles 15:6–12"> And there were found more of the sonnes of Eleazar by the number of men, then of the sonnes of Ithamar. and they deuided them, to wit, among the sonnes of Eleazar, sixteene heads, according to the houshould of their fathers, and among ye sonnes of Ithamar, according to the housholde of their fathers, eight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:31; Joshua 18:10; Acts 4:1"> Thus they distributed them by lot the one from the other, and so the rulers of the Sanctuarie and the rulers of the house of God were of the sonnes of Eleazar and of the sonnes of Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:16; Matthew 8:19; Nehemiah 8:1"> And Shemaiah the sonne of Nethaneel the scribe of the Leuites, wrote them before ye King and the princes, and Zadok the Priest, and Ahimelech the sonne of Abiathar, and before ye chiefe fathers of the Priests and of the Leuites, one familie being reserued for Eleazar, and another reserued for Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 2:36; Nehemiah 11:10; Nehemiah 12:19"> And the first lot fell to Iehoiarib, and the second to Iedaiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 2:39; Nehemiah 7:35; Nehemiah 12:15"> The third to Harim, the fourth to Seorim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:17"> The fifth to Malchiiah, the sixt to Miiamin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 1:5; Nehemiah 12:4; Nehemiah 12:17"> The seuenth to Hakkoz, the eight to Abiiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezra 2:36; Nehemiah 12:10; Nehemiah 7:39"> The ninth to Ieshua, the tenth to Shecaniah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:10"> The eleuenth to Eliashib, the twelft to Iakim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The thirteenth to Huppa, the fouretenth to Ieshebeab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:40; Ezra 2:37; Ezra 10:20"> The fiftenth to Bilgah, the sixtenth to Immer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The seuententh to Hezir, the eightenth to Happizzer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The ninetenth to Pethahiah, the twentieth to Iehezekel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The one and twentie to Iachin, the two and twentie to Gamul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The three and twentie to Deliah, the foure and twentie to Maaziah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; 1 Corinthians 14:40; Hebrews 7:11"> These were their orders according to their offices, when they entred into the house of the Lord according to their custome vnder the hande of Aaron their father, as the Lord God of Israel had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:24; 1 Chronicles 23:16; 1 Chronicles 6:18"> And of the sonnes of Leui that remained of the sonnes of Amram, was Shubael, of the sonnes of Shubael, Iedeiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:17; 1 Chronicles 26:25"> Of Rehabiah. euen of the sonnes of Rehabiah, the first Isshiiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:18; Numbers 3:27; Numbers 3:19"> Of Izhari, Shelomoth, of the sonnes of Shelomoth, Iahath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 26:31"> And his sonnes Ieriah the first, Amariah the second, Iahaziel the thirde, and Iekameam the fourth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:20"> The sonne of Vzziel was Michah, ye sonne of Michah was Shamir,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:20; 1 Chronicles 15:18; 1 Chronicles 23:20"> The brother of Michah was Isshiiah, the sonne of Isshiiah, Zechariah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:21"> The sonnes of Merari, were Mahli and Mushi, the sonne of Iaaziiah was Beno,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 3:20; 1 Chronicles 23:21; 1 Chronicles 6:19"> The sonnes of Merari of Iahaziah were Beno, and Shoham, and Zaccur and Ibri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:22"> Of Mahli came Eleazar, which had no sonnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Of Kish. the sonne of Kish was Ierahmeel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:23; 1 Chronicles 6:47"> And the sonnes of Mushi were Mahli, and Eder, and Ierimoth: these were sonnes of the Leuites after the houshold of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:5–6; 1 Chronicles 26:13; 1 Chronicles 25:8"> And these also cast lots with their brethren the sonnes of Aaron before King Dauid, and Zadok and Ahimelech and the chiefe fathers of the Priests, and of the Leuites, euen the chiefe of the families against their yonger brethren.</span>
