<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Song of Solomon 6:2; Song of Solomon 4:16; Revelation 22:17"> I am come into my garden, my sister, my spouse: I gathered my myrrhe with my spice: I ate mine hony combe with mine hony, I dranke my wine with my milke: eate, O friends, drinke, and make you merie, O welbeloued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Song of Solomon 2:14; Song of Solomon 6:9"> I sleepe, but mine heart waketh, it is the voyce of my welbeloued that knocketh, saying, Open vnto mee, my sister, my loue, my doue, my vndefiled: for mine head is full of dewe, and my lockes with the droppes of the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 11:7; Romans 7:22–23; Proverbs 13:4"> I haue put off my coate, howe shall I put it on? I haue washed my feete, howe shall I defile them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:4; Genesis 43:30; 2 Corinthians 8:16"> My welbeloued put in his hand by the hole of the doore, and mine heart was affectioned toward him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:13; Ephesians 3:17; Song of Solomon 5:2"> I rose vp to open to my welbeloued, and mine hands did drop downe myrrhe, and my fingers pure myrrhe vpon the handels of the barre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:2; Revelation 3:19; Hosea 5:15"> I opened to my welbeloued: but my welbeloued was gone, and past: mine heart was gone when hee did speake: I sought him, but I coulde not finde him: I called him, but hee answered mee not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Solomon 3:3; Matthew 21:33–41; Acts 5:40–41"> The watchmen that went about the citie, founde me: they smote me and wounded me: the watchmen of the walles tooke away my vaile from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:7; Song of Solomon 2:5; Song of Solomon 3:5"> I charge you, O daughters of Ierusalem, if you finde my welbeloued, that you tell him that I am sicke of loue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:8; Song of Solomon 6:1; Song of Solomon 6:9–10"> O the fairest among women, what is thy welbeloued more then other welbeloued? what is thy welbeloued more then another louer, that thou doest so charge vs?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 45:2; Song of Solomon 2:1; Philippians 2:9–11"> My welbeloued is white and ruddie, the chiefest of ten thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Song of Solomon 7:5; Song of Solomon 5:2; Daniel 7:9"> His head is as fine golde, his lockes curled, and blacke as a rauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:1; Song of Solomon 1:15; Hebrews 4:13"> His eyes are like doues vpon the riuers of waters, which are washt with milke, and remaine by the full vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:10; Song of Solomon 2:1; Song of Solomon 6:2"> His cheekes are as a bedde of spices, and as sweete flowres, and his lippes like lilies dropping downe pure myrrhe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 24:10; Isaiah 54:11; Ezekiel 1:26–28"> His hands as rings of gold set with the chrysolite, his belly like white yuorie couered with saphirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 1:15–16; 1 Kings 4:33; Matthew 17:2"> His legges are as pillars of marble, set vpon sockets of fine golde: his countenance as Lebanon, excellent as the cedars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 45:2; Song of Solomon 7:9; Isaiah 9:6–7"> His mouth is as sweete thinges, and hee is wholy delectable: this is my welbeloued, and this is my louer, O daughters of Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> O the fairest among women, whither is thy welbeloued gone? whither is thy welbeloued turned aside, that we may seeke him with thee?</span>
