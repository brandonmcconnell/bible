<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 45:13; Exodus 35:35; Exodus 28:15"> Howe beautifull are thy goings with shooes, O princes daughter! the ioynts of thy thighs are like iewels: the worke of the hande of a cunning workeman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3; Psalms 45:16; Romans 7:4"> Thy nauel is as a round cuppe that wanteth not licour: thy belly is as an heape of wheat compassed about with lilies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:5; Song of Solomon 6:6"> Thy two breastes are as two young roes that are twinnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:4; Song of Solomon 4:8–9; Psalms 144:12"> Thy necke is like a towre of yuorie: thine eyes are like the fishe pooles in Heshbon by the gate of Bath-rabbim: thy nose is as the towre of Lebanon, that looketh toward Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Song of Solomon 4:1; Micah 7:14"> Thine head vpon thee is as skarlet, and the bush of thine head like purple: the King is tyed in the rafters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:15–16; Psalms 45:11; Song of Solomon 4:10"> Howe faire art thou, and howe pleasant art thou, O my loue, in pleasures!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:5; Song of Solomon 1:13; Ephesians 4:13"> This thy stature is like a palme tree, and thy brestes like clusters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; John 14:21–23; Song of Solomon 2:5"> I saide, I will goe vp into the palme tree, I will take holde of her boughes: thy breastes shall nowe be like the clusters of the vine: and the sauour of thy nose like apples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:16; Isaiah 62:8–9; Acts 4:31–32"> And the roufe of thy mouth like good wine, which goeth straight to my welbeloued, and causeth the lippes of the ancient to speake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 45:11; Song of Solomon 2:16; Song of Solomon 6:3"> I am my welbeloueds, and his desire is toward mee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:8; Song of Solomon 2:10–13; Song of Solomon 1:4"> Come, my welbeloued, let vs go foorth into the fielde: let vs remaine in the villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Song of Solomon 6:11; Ephesians 6:24; Song of Solomon 7:6"> Let vs get vp early to the vines, let vs see if the vine florish, whether it hath budded the small grape, or whether the pomegranates florish: there will I giue thee my loue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 30:14; Matthew 13:52; 1 Peter 4:11"> The mandrakes haue giuen a smelll, and in our gates are all sweete things, new and olde: my welbeloued, I haue kept them for thee.</span>
