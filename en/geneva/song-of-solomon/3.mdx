<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:7; Song of Solomon 8:4; Micah 4:8"> I charge you, O daughters of Ierusalem, by the roes and by the hindes of the fielde, that ye stirre not vp, nor waken my loue vntill she please.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:15–16; Ephesians 3:18–19; Psalms 87:3"> Hee made the pillars thereof of siluer, and the pauement thereof of gold, the hangings thereof of purple, whose middes was paued with the loue of the daughters of Ierusalem.</span>
