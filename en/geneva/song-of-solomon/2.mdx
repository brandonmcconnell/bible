<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Matthew 10:16; Philippians 2:15–16"> Like a lilie amog the thornes, so is my loue among the daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:5; Hebrews 1:1–6; Isaiah 32:2"> Like the apple tree among the trees of the forest, so is my welbeloued among the sonnes of men: vnder his shadow had I delite, and sate downe: and his fruite was sweete vnto my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:28–39; John 15:9–15; Romans 5:8–10"> Hee brought mee into the wine cellar, and loue was his banner ouer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:8; Hosea 3:1; 2 Samuel 6:19"> Stay me with flagons, and comfort me with apples: for I am sicke of loue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:17; Isaiah 54:5–10; Jeremiah 32:41"> His left hande is vnder mine head, and his right hand doeth imbrace me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Solomon 3:5; Song of Solomon 8:4; Song of Solomon 1:5"> I charge you, O daughters of Ierusalem, by the roes and by the hindes of the fielde, that ye stirre not vp, nor waken my loue, vntill she please.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:27; Revelation 3:20; John 3:29"> It is the voyce of my welbeloued: beholde, hee commeth leaping by the mountaines, and skipping by the hilles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:14; Song of Solomon 2:17; John 5:39"> My welbeloued is like a roe, or a yong hart: loe, he standeth behinde our wall, looking forth of the windowes, shewing him selfe through the grates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:13; Song of Solomon 4:7–8; Revelation 22:17"> My welbeloued spake and said vnto me, Arise, my loue, my faire one, and come thy way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–2; Ecclesiastes 3:11; Isaiah 54:6–8"> For beholde, winter is past: the raine is changed, and is gone away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 55:12; Colossians 3:16; Psalms 89:15"> The flowers appeare in the earth: the time of the singing of birdes is come, and the voyce of the turtle is heard in our land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:10; Song of Solomon 7:11–13; Isaiah 55:10–11"> The figtree hath brought foorth her yong figges: and the vines with their small grapes haue cast a sauour: arise my loue, my faire one, and come away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezra 9:5–6; Song of Solomon 5:2; Song of Solomon 1:5"> My doue, that art in the holes of ye rocke, in the secret places of the staires, shewe mee thy sight, let mee heare thy voyce: for thy voyce is sweete, and thy sight comely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 13:32; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Song of Solomon 7:12"> Take vs the foxes, the little foxes, which destroy the vines: for our vines haue small grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Song of Solomon 6:3; Song of Solomon 7:10; Psalms 63:1"> My welbeloued is mine, and I am his: hee feedeth among the lilies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:6; Song of Solomon 2:8–9; 2 Peter 1:19"> Vntil the day breake, and the shadowes flee away: returne, my welbeloued, and be like a roe, or a yong hart vpon the mountaines of Bether.</span>
