<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Song of Solomon 6:6; John 15:7; 2 Peter 1:5–8"> Thy teeth are like a flocke of sheepe in good order, which go vp from the washing: which euery one bring out twinnes, and none is barren among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Song of Solomon 6:7; Song of Solomon 5:16; Joshua 2:18"> Thy lippes are like a threede of scarlet, and thy talke is comely: thy temples are within thy lockes as a piece of a pomegranate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song of Solomon 7:4; Ephesians 4:15–16; Nehemiah 3:19"> Thy necke is as the tower of Dauid builte for defence: a thousand shieldes hang therein, and all the targates of the strong men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:16; Song of Solomon 6:3; Song of Solomon 7:3"> Thy two breastes are as two young roes that are twinnes, feeding among the lilies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:17; Song of Solomon 4:14; Luke 1:78"> Vntill the day breake, and the shadowes flie away, I wil go into the mountaine of myrrhe and to the mountaine of incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:15; Song of Solomon 4:1; Jude 1:24"> Thou art all faire, my loue, and there is no spot in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:9; Song of Solomon 2:13; Psalms 45:10"> Come with me from Lebanon, my spouse, euen with me from Lebanon, and looke from the toppe of Amanah, from the toppe of Shenir and Hermon, from the dennes of the lyons, and from the mountaines of the leopards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:1–2; Song of Solomon 4:12; Proverbs 5:19–20"> My sister, my spouse, thou hast wounded mine heart: thou hast wounded mine heart with one of thine eyes, and with a chaine of thy necke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:2–4; Song of Solomon 7:6; Galatians 5:22"> My sister, my spouse, how faire is thy loue? howe much better is thy loue then wine? and the sauour of thine oyntments then all spices?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:1; Song of Solomon 7:9; Genesis 27:27"> Thy lippes, my spouse, droppe as honie combes: honie and milke are vnder thy tongue, and the sauoure of thy garments is as the sauoure of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 5:15–18; Revelation 21:27; 1 Corinthians 6:13"> My sister my spouse is as a garden inclosed, as a spring shut vp, and a fountaine sealed vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:14; Song of Solomon 6:11; Ecclesiastes 2:5"> Thy plantes are as an orchard of pomegranates with sweete fruites, as camphire, spikenarde,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 30:23; Song of Solomon 4:6; Song of Solomon 6:2"> Euen spikenarde, and saffran, calamus, and cynamon with all the trees of incense, myrrhe and aloes, with all the chiefe spices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:10; John 7:38; John 4:14"> O fountaine of the gardens, O well of liuing waters, and the springs of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Song of Solomon 6:2; Song of Solomon 5:1; Song of Solomon 4:13–14"> Arise, O North, and come O South, and blowe on my garden that the spices thereof may flow out: let my welbeloued come to his garden, and eate his pleasant fruite.</span>
