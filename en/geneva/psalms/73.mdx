<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Psalms 51:10; Luke 12:32"> A Psalme committed to Asaph. Yet God is good to Israel: euen, to the pure in heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:18; 1 Samuel 2:9; Psalms 116:8"> As for me, my feete were almost gone: my steps had well neere slipt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 21:7; Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17"> For I feared at the foolish, when I sawe the prosperitie of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 21:23–24; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Luke 16:22"> For there are no bandes in their death, but they are lustie and strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 21:9; Psalms 73:12; Revelation 3:19"> They are not in trouble as other men, neither are they plagued with other men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 109:18; 1 Peter 5:5; Daniel 4:30"> Therefore pride is as a chayne vnto them, and crueltie couereth them as a garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:28; Psalms 17:10; Job 15:27"> Their eyes stande out for fatnesse: they haue more then heart can wish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:16; Psalms 17:10; Psalms 10:2"> They are licentious, and speake wickedly of their oppression: they talke presumptuously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 13:6; James 3:6; Exodus 5:2"> They set their mouth against heauen, and their tongue walketh through the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 75:8"> Therefore his people turne hither: for waters of a full cup are wrung out to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 7:2; Zephaniah 1:12; Job 22:13–14"> And they say, Howe doeth God know it? or is there knowledge in the most High?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 52:7; Psalms 49:6; Hosea 12:7–8"> Lo, these are the wicked, yet prosper they alway, and increase in riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 21:15; Psalms 26:6; Job 34:9"> Certainely I haue clensed mine heart in vaine, and washed mine hands in innocencie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5; Psalms 94:12; Job 7:3–4"> For dayly haue I bene punished, and chastened euery morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:6–7; Malachi 2:8; 1 Peter 2:9"> If I say, I will iudge thus, beholde the generation of thy children: I haue trespassed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:17; John 16:18–19; Psalms 36:6"> Then thought I to know this, but it was too painefull for me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Psalms 77:13; Ecclesiastes 8:12–13"> Vntill I went into the Sanctuarie of God: then vnderstoode I their ende.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 35:6; Jeremiah 23:12; Deuteronomy 32:35"> Surely thou hast set them in slipperie places, and castest them downe into desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Isaiah 47:11; Daniel 5:6"> How suddenly are they destroyed, perished and horribly consumed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 20:8; Psalms 78:65; Psalms 90:5"> As a dreame when one awaketh! O Lord, when thou raisest vs vp, thou shalt make their image despised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 73:3; Job 16:13"> Certainely mine heart was vexed, and I was pricked in my reines:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18; Psalms 92:6; Psalms 49:10"> So foolish was I and ignorant: I was a beast before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 63:8; Matthew 28:20"> Yet I was alway with thee: thou hast holden me by my right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Isaiah 58:11; Proverbs 3:5–6"> Thou wilt guide me by thy counsell, and afterward receiue me to glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 3:8; Psalms 16:2; Psalms 63:3"> Whom haue I in heauen but thee? and I haue desired none in the earth with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Isaiah 40:29–31; Psalms 138:3"> My flesh fayleth and mine heart also: but God is the strength of mine heart, and my portion for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 4:4; Psalms 119:155; Exodus 34:15"> For loe, they that withdrawe themselues from thee, shall perish: thou destroyest all them that goe a whoring from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="James 4:8; Psalms 40:5; Hebrews 10:19–22"> As for me, it is good for me to draw neere to God: therefore I haue put my trust in the Lord God, that I may declare all thy workes.</span>
