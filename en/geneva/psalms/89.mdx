<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 101:1; Psalms 89:8; Psalms 119:90"> A Psalme to give instruction, of Ethan the Ezrahite. I will sing the mercies of the Lord for euer: with my mouth will I declare thy trueth from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 36:5; Nehemiah 9:31; Psalms 89:5"> For I said, Mercie shalbe set vp for euer: thy trueth shalt thou stablish in ye very heauens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 132:11; 2 Samuel 7:10–16; Psalms 89:34–35"> I haue made a couenant with my chosen: I haue sworne to Dauid my seruant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12–16; Luke 1:32–33; Psalms 132:12"> Thy seede will I stablish for euer, and set vp thy throne from generation to generation. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 97:6; Psalms 19:1; Isaiah 44:23"> O Lord, euen the heauens shall prayse thy wonderous worke: yea, thy trueth in the Congregation of the Saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 86:8; Psalms 71:19; Psalms 113:5"> For who is equall to the Lord in the heauen? and who is like the Lord among the sonnes of the gods?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 47:2; Psalms 76:7–11; Hebrews 12:28–29"> God is very terrible in the assemblie of the Saints, and to be reuerenced aboue all, that are about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 71:19; 1 Samuel 2:2; Jeremiah 32:17"> O Lord God of hostes, who is like vnto thee, which art a mightie Lord, and thy trueth is about thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 65:7; Psalms 107:25–29; Mark 4:41"> Thou rulest the raging of the sea: when the waues thereof arise, thou stillest them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 87:4; Psalms 68:30; Psalms 144:6"> Thou hast beaten downe Rahab as a man slaine: thou hast scattered thine enemies with thy mightie arme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:11; Genesis 1:1; Psalms 115:16"> The heauens are thine, the earth also is thine: thou hast layde the foundation of the world, and all that therein is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 12:1; Job 26:7; Joshua 19:22"> Thou hast created the North and the South: Tabor and Hermon shall reioyce in thy Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 4:34–35; Psalms 89:10; Psalms 62:11"> Thou hast a mightie arme: strong is thine hand, and high is thy right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Proverbs 16:12; Psalms 45:6–7"> Righteousnesse and equitie are the stablishment of thy throne: mercy and trueth goe before thy face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Job 29:3; Psalms 4:6"> Blessed is the people, that can reioyce in thee: they shall walke in the light of thy countenance, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Philippians 4:4; Philippians 3:9; Luke 1:47"> They shall reioyce continually in thy Name, and in thy righteousnes shall they exalt them selues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 75:10; Psalms 92:10; Psalms 148:14"> For thou art the glory of their strength, and by thy fauour our hornes shall be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 47:9; Psalms 71:22; Isaiah 33:22"> For our shield apperteineth to the Lord, and our King to the holy one of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:18; 1 Kings 11:34; Psalms 89:3"> Thou spakest then in a vision vnto thine Holy one, and saydest, I haue layde helpe vpon one that is mightie: I haue exalted one chosen out of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12–13; Isaiah 61:1–3; 1 Samuel 16:1"> I haue found Dauid my seruant: with mine holy oyle haue I anoynted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Psalms 18:32–39; Psalms 89:13"> Therefore mine hande shall be established with him, and mine arme shall strengthen him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:9; 2 Samuel 7:10; Matthew 4:1–10"> The enemie shall not oppresse him, neither shall the wicked hurt him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:9; 2 Samuel 3:1; Psalms 18:40"> But I will destroy his foes before his face, and plague them that hate him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 89:16–17; 2 Corinthians 1:20; Psalms 91:14"> My trueth also and my mercie shall be with him, and in my Name shall his horne be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 80:11; Psalms 2:8; Psalms 72:8–11"> I will set his hand also in the sea, and his right hand in the floods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; 2 Samuel 22:47; Psalms 95:1"> He shall cry vnto mee, Thou art my Father, my God and the rocke of my saluation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; Colossians 1:18; Psalms 2:7"> Also I wil make him my first borne, higher then the Kings of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; Jeremiah 33:20–21; Acts 13:32–34"> My mercie will I keepe for him for euermore, and my couenant shall stande fast with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 89:4; Deuteronomy 11:21; Psalms 89:36"> His seede also will I make to endure for euer, and his throne as the dayes of heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; 1 Chronicles 28:9; Psalms 119:53"> But if his children forsake my Lawe, and walke not in my iudgements:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 55:20"> If they breake my statutes, and keepe not my commandements:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; Hebrews 12:6–11; Proverbs 3:11–12"> Then will I visite their transgression with the rod, and their iniquitie with strokes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 54:8–10; 2 Samuel 7:15; Lamentations 3:31–32"> Yet my louing kindnesse will I not take from him, neither will I falsifie my trueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Matthew 24:35; James 1:17"> My couenant wil I not breake, nor alter the thing that is gone out of my lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 132:11; Hebrews 6:13; Titus 1:2"> I haue sworne once by mine holines, that I will not fayle Dauid, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 72:5; Psalms 89:29; Isaiah 59:21"> His seede shall endure for euer, and his throne shalbe as the sunne before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35–36; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalms 104:19"> He shalbe established for euermore as the moone, and as a faythfull witnes in the heauen. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Deuteronomy 32:19; Psalms 106:40"> But thou hast reiected and abhorred, thou hast bene angry with thine Anoynted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Lamentations 5:16; Psalms 74:7; Psalms 89:34–36"> Thou hast broken the couenant of thy seruant, and profaned his crowne, casting it on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 80:12; Lamentations 2:2; Lamentations 2:5"> Thou hast broken downe all his walles: thou hast layd his fortresses in ruine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Lamentations 5:1; Ezekiel 5:14–15"> All that goe by the way, spoyle him: he is a rebuke vnto his neighbours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 80:6; Revelation 11:10; Deuteronomy 28:43"> Thou hast set vp the right hand of his enemies, and made all his aduersaries to reioyce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 7:8–12; Numbers 14:42; Psalms 44:10"> Thou hast also turned the edge of his sworde, and hast not made him to stand in the battell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 4:1–2; 2 Thessalonians 2:3–10; Psalms 89:39"> Thou hast caused his dignitie to decay, and cast his throne to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 109:29; Psalms 44:15; Micah 7:10"> The dayes of his youth hast thou shortned, and couered him with shame. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 79:5; Psalms 78:63; Psalms 88:14"> Lord, howe long wilt thou hide thy selfe, for euer? shall thy wrath burne like fire?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Job 14:1; Job 10:9; Job 7:7"> Remember of what time I am: wherefore shouldest thou create in vaine all the children of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 49:15; Hebrews 11:5; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> What man liueth, and shall not see death? shall hee deliuer his soule from the hande of the graue? Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:15; Hebrews 7:21; Isaiah 55:3"> Lord, where are thy former mercies, which thou swarest vnto Dauid in thy trueth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 74:22; Psalms 74:18; Psalms 69:9"> Remember, O Lord, the rebuke of thy seruants, which I beare in my bosome of all the mightie people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14–16; Psalms 74:10; 2 Samuel 16:7–8"> For thine enemies haue reproched thee, O Lord, because they haue reproched the footesteps of thine Anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Psalms 72:18–19; Psalms 41:13; 1 Timothy 1:17"> Praised be the Lord for euermore. So be it, euen so be it.</span>
