<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 57:7–11; John 4:24"> A Psalme of David. My soule, prayse thou the Lord, and all that is within me, prayse his holy Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 63:7; Psalms 116:12; Psalms 105:5"> My soule, prayse thou the Lord, and forget not all his benefites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Isaiah 53:5; Jeremiah 17:14"> Which forgiueth all thine iniquitie, and healeth all thine infirmities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 56:13; Psalms 71:23; Psalms 103:12"> Which redeemeth thy life from the graue, and crowneth thee with mercy and compassions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 107:9; 1 Timothy 6:17"> Which satisfieth thy mouth with good things: and thy youth is renued like the eagles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 12:5; Psalms 72:12"> The Lord executeth righteousnes and iudgement to all that are oppressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 33:13; Psalms 147:19; Psalms 78:11"> He made his wayes knowen vnto Moses, and his workes vnto the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Joel 2:13; Exodus 34:6–7"> The Lord is full of compassion and mercie, slowe to anger and of great kindnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 57:16; Psalms 30:5; Micah 7:18–19"> He will not alway chide, neither keepe his anger for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Lamentations 3:22; Nehemiah 9:31"> He hath not dealt with vs after our sinnes, nor rewarded vs according to our iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 57:10; Luke 1:50; Psalms 36:5"> For as high as the heauen is aboue ye earth, so great is his mercie toward them that feare him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; 1 John 1:7; Micah 7:18"> As farre as the East is from the West: so farre hath he remooued our sinnes from vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 11:11–12; Hebrews 12:5–11; Luke 15:21–22"> As a father hath compassion on his children, so hath the Lord compassion on them that feare him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 10:9; Psalms 78:38–39"> For he knoweth whereof we be made: he remembreth that we are but dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; James 1:10–11; Isaiah 51:12"> The dayes of man are as grasse: as a flowre of the fielde, so florisheth he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7; Job 20:9; Job 14:10"> For the winde goeth ouer it, and it is gone, and the place thereof shall knowe it no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 100:5; Exodus 20:6; Ephesians 2:4–7"> But the louing kindnesse of the Lord endureth for euer and euer vpon them that feare him, and his righteousnes vpon childrens children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Psalms 132:12; Luke 1:6"> Vnto them that keepe his couenant, and thinke vpon his commandements to doe them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 47:2; Philippians 2:9–10; Psalms 9:7"> The Lord hath prepared his throne in heauen, and his Kingdome ruleth ouer all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 1:14; Psalms 148:2; Luke 1:19"> Prayse the Lord, ye his Angels, that excell in strength, that doe his commandement in obeying the voyce of his worde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 22:19; Nehemiah 9:6; Luke 2:13"> Prayse the Lord, all ye his hostes, ye his seruants that doe his pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Psalms 145:10; Isaiah 42:10–12"> Prayse the Lord, all ye his workes, in all places of his dominion: my soule, prayse thou the Lord.</span>
