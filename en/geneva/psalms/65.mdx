<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 62:1; Psalms 76:11; Psalms 116:17–18"> To him that excelleth. A Psalme or song of David. O God, praise waiteth for thee in Zion, and vnto thee shall the vowe be perfourmed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; Jeremiah 29:12–13; Psalms 86:9"> Because thou hearest the prayer, vnto thee shall all flesh come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 38:4; Psalms 79:9; Hebrews 9:14"> Wicked deedes haue preuailed against me: but thou wilt be mercifull vnto our transgressions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; Psalms 84:4; Psalms 23:6"> Blessed is he, whom thou chusest and causest to come to thee: he shall dwell in thy courtes, and we shall be satisfied with the pleasures of thine House, euen of thine holy Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:21; Psalms 66:3; Revelation 15:3–4"> O God of our saluation, thou wilt answere vs with fearefull signes in thy righteousnes, O thou the hope of all the ends of the earth, and of them that are farre off in the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 93:1; Micah 6:2; Habakkuk 3:6"> He stablisheth the mountaines by his power: and is girded about with strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 89:9; Isaiah 17:12–13; Psalms 93:3–4"> He appeaseth the noyse of the seas and the noyse of the waues thereof, and the tumults of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 148:3; Psalms 136:8; Psalms 104:20–23"> They also, that dwell in the vttermost parts of the earth, shalbe afraide of thy signes: thou shalt make the East and the West to reioyce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 46:4; Psalms 104:13–15; Psalms 68:9–10"> Thou visitest the earth, and waterest it: thou makest it very riche: the Riuer of God is full of water: thou preparest them corne: for so thou appointest it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; 1 Corinthians 3:6–7; Deuteronomy 32:2"> Thou waterest abundantly the furrowes thereof: thou causest the raine to descende into the valleies thereof: thou makest it soft with showres, and blessest the bud thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joel 2:21–26; Psalms 103:4; Psalms 5:12"> Thou crownest ye yeere with thy goodnesse, and thy steppes droppe fatnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 38:26–27; Psalms 104:10–13; Joel 2:22"> They drop vpon the pastures of the wildernesse: and the hils shalbe compassed with gladnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 55:12; Acts 14:17; Isaiah 35:1–2"> The pastures are clad with sheepe: the valleis also shalbe couered with corne: therefore they shoute for ioye, and sing.</span>
