<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Isaiah 44:22; Isaiah 43:25"> To him that excelleth. A Psalme of David, when the Prophet Nathan came unto him, after he had gone in to Bath-sheba. Have mercie vpon me, O God, according to thy louing kindnes: according to the multitude of thy compassions put away mine iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 1:7–9; Psalms 51:7; Hebrews 10:21–22"> Wash me throughly from mine iniquitie, and clense me from my sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Isaiah 59:12; Psalms 38:18"> For I know mine iniquities, and my sinne is euer before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13–14; Luke 15:21; Genesis 20:6"> Against thee, against thee onely haue I sinned, and done euill in thy sight, that thou mayest be iust when thou speakest, and pure when thou iudgest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 5:12; Job 14:4; Psalms 58:3"> Beholde, I was borne in iniquitie, and in sinne hath my mother conceiued me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 15:2; 1 Samuel 16:7; 1 Peter 3:4"> Beholde, thou louest trueth in the inwarde affections: therefore hast thou taught mee wisedome in the secret of mine heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 1:7; Isaiah 1:18; Hebrews 9:13–14"> Purge me with hyssope, and I shalbe cleane: wash me, and I shalbe whiter then snowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 5:17–18; Hosea 6:1–2; Matthew 5:4"> Make me to heare ioye and gladnes, that the bones, which thou hast broken, may reioyce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:17; Micah 7:18–19; Psalms 51:1"> Hide thy face from my sinnes, and put away all mine iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ephesians 4:22–24"> Create in mee a cleane heart, O God, and renue a right spirit within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; 2 Kings 13:23; Isaiah 63:10–11"> Cast mee not away from thy presence, and take not thine holy Spirit from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 13:5; Isaiah 61:10"> Restore to me the ioy of thy saluation, and stablish me with thy free Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 22:32; Acts 26:18–20; Acts 3:19"> Then shall I teache thy wayes vnto the wicked, and sinners shalbe conuerted vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 35:28; 2 Samuel 12:9; Psalms 26:9"> Deliuer me from blood, O God, which art the God of my saluation, and my tongue shall sing ioyfully of thy righteousnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 63:3–5; Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 9:14"> Open thou my lippes, O Lord, and my mouth shall shewe foorth thy praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 40:6; Hosea 6:6; Hebrews 10:5–6"> For thou desirest no sacrifice, though I would giue it: thou delitest not in burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:18; Isaiah 66:2; Psalms 147:3"> The sacrifices of God are a contrite spirit: a contrite and a broken heart, O God, thou wilt not despise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 102:16; Psalms 122:6–9; Luke 12:32"> Bee fauourable vnto Zion for thy good pleasure: builde the walles of Ierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 4:5; Ephesians 5:2; Malachi 3:3"> Then shalt thou accept ye sacrifices of righteousnes, euen the burnt offering and oblation: then shall they offer calues vpon thine altar.</span>
