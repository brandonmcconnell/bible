<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:12–13; 2 Samuel 8:3; Psalms 44:9"> To him that excelleth upon Shushan Eduth, or Michtam. A Psalme of David to teach. When he fought against Aram Naharaim, and against Aram Zobah, when Joab returned and slew twelve thousand Edomites in the salt valley. O God, thou hast cast vs out, thou hast scattered vs, thou hast bene angry, turne againe vnto vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; Hosea 6:1; Isaiah 30:26"> Thou hast made the land to tremble, and hast made it to gape: heale the breaches thereof, for it is shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 71:20; Isaiah 51:22; Isaiah 51:17"> Thou hast shewed thy people heauy things: thou hast made vs to drinke the wine of giddines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 20:5; Isaiah 11:12; Exodus 17:15"> But now thou hast giuen a banner to them that feare thee, that it may be displayed because of thy trueth. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 108:6–13; Psalms 17:7; Isaiah 41:10"> That thy beloued may be deliuered, helpe with thy right hand and heare me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 13:27; Psalms 89:35; Genesis 12:6"> God hath spoken in his holines: therefore I will reioyce: I shall deuide Shechem, and measure the valley of Succoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 49:10; Deuteronomy 33:17; Joshua 13:31"> Gilead shalbe mine, and Manasseh shalbe mine: Ephraim also shalbe the strength of mine head: Iudah is my lawgiuer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; 2 Samuel 8:1–2; Psalms 108:9–10"> Moab shalbe my wash pot: ouer Edom will I cast out my shoe: Palestina shew thy selfe ioyfull for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:26–31; 2 Samuel 11:1; Judges 1:12"> Who will leade me into the strong citie? who will bring me vnto Edom?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 108:11; Joshua 7:12; Psalms 60:1"> Wilt not thou, O God, which hadest cast vs off, and didest not go forth, O God, with our armies?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 146:3; Psalms 108:12; Psalms 130:8"> Giue vs helpe against trouble: for vaine is the helpe of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 44:5; Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 63:3"> Through God we shall doe valiantly: for he shall tread downe our enemies.</span>
