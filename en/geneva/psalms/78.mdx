<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:4; Isaiah 55:3; Psalms 74:1"> A Psalme to give instruction committed to Asaph. Heare my doctrine, O my people: incline your eares vnto the wordes of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; Mark 4:34; Matthew 13:11–13"> I will open my mouth in a parable: I will declare high sentences of olde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 44:1; Exodus 13:14–15; Exodus 12:26–27"> Which we haue heard and knowen, and our fathers haue tolde vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:19; Psalms 145:4–6; Deuteronomy 4:9"> Wee will not hide them from their children but to the generation to come we wil shewe the praises of the Lord his power also, and his wonderful woorkes that he hath done:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Ephesians 6:4; Deuteronomy 4:45"> How he established a testimonie in Iaakob, and ordeined a Law in Israel, which he commanded our fathers, that they shoulde teache their children:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 102:18; Joel 1:3; Esther 9:28"> That the posteritie might knowe it, and the children, which should be borne, should stand vp, and declare it to their children:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:29; Revelation 14:12; 1 John 5:3"> That they might set their hope on God, and not forget the workes of God but keepe his commandements:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 78:37; Exodus 32:9; 2 Kings 17:14"> And not to bee as their fathers, a disobedient and rebellious generation: a generation that set not their heart aright, and whose spirite was not faithfull vnto God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:41–44; 1 Samuel 31:1; Psalms 78:57"> The children of Ephraim being armed and shooting with the bowe, turned backe in the day of battell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:20; Deuteronomy 31:16; Nehemiah 9:26–29"> They kept not the couenant of God, but refused to walke in his Lawe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 106:13; Deuteronomy 32:18; Psalms 106:21–22"> And forgate his Actes, and his wonderfull woorkes that he had shewed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Ezekiel 30:14; Isaiah 19:11"> Hee did marueilous thinges in the sight of their fathers in the lande of Egypt: euen in the fielde of Zoan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Exodus 15:8; Psalms 66:6"> He deuided the Sea, and led them through: he made also the waters to stand as an heape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 105:39; Exodus 14:24; Exodus 13:21–22"> In the day time also hee led them with a cloude, and all the night with a light of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 105:41; Numbers 20:11; Exodus 17:6"> He claue the rockes in the wildernes, and gaue them drinke as of the great depths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 20:8; Numbers 20:10–11; Deuteronomy 8:15"> He brought floods also out of the stonie rocke; so that hee made the waters to descend like the riuers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; Hebrews 3:16–19; Deuteronomy 9:12–22"> Yet they sinned stil against him, and prouoked the Highest in the wildernesse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; Numbers 11:4; 1 Corinthians 10:9"> And tempted God in their heartes in requiring meate for their lust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 21:5; Numbers 11:4; Exodus 16:3"> They spake against God also, saying, Can God prepare a table in the wildernesse?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; Numbers 11:21–23; Psalms 78:41"> Behold, he smote the rocke, that the water gushed out, and the streames ouerflowed: can hee giue bread also? or prepare flesh for his people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jude 1:5; 1 Corinthians 10:11; Numbers 11:1–3"> Therefore the Lord heard and was angrie, and the fire was kindled in Iaakob, and also wrath came vpon Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:18–19; Hebrews 3:12; Jude 1:5"> Because they beleeued not in God, and trusted not in his helpe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Malachi 3:10; Psalms 33:9"> Yet he had comanded the clouds aboue, and had opened the doores of heauen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:4; Psalms 105:40; John 6:31–71"> And had rained downe MAN vpon them for to eate, and had giuen them of the wheate of heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 14:20; Matthew 15:37; Psalms 103:20"> Man did eate the bread of Angels: hee sent them meate ynough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 11:31; Psalms 135:7"> He caused the Eastwinde to passe in the heauen, and through his power he brought in the Southwinde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 11:18–19; Exodus 16:12–13; Numbers 11:32"> Hee rained flesh also vpon them as dust, and feathered foule as the sand of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And hee made it fall in the middes of their campe euen round about their habitations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 106:15; Numbers 11:19–20"> So they did eate and were well filled: for he gaue them their desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 11:33–34; Luke 16:19–23; Numbers 22:20–22"> They were not turned from their lust, but the meate was yet in their mouthes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16"> When the wrath of God came euen vpon them, and slew the strongest of them, and smote downe the chosen men in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 12:37; Psalms 78:22; Psalms 78:11"> For all this, they sinned stil, and beleeued not his wonderous woorkes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 14:35; Numbers 14:29; Numbers 26:64–65"> Therefore their daies did hee consume in vanitie, and their yeeres hastily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Numbers 21:7; Judges 3:12–15"> And when hee slewe them, they sought him and they returned, and sought God earely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Isaiah 41:14; Exodus 15:13"> And they remembred that God was their strength, and the most high God their redeemer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; Isaiah 29:13; Hosea 11:12"> But they flattered him with their mouth, and dissembled with him with their tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 8:21; Psalms 78:8; Psalms 51:10"> For their heart was not vpright with him: neither were they faithfull in his couenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 48:9; Numbers 14:18–20; Ezekiel 20:17"> Yet he being merciful forgaue their iniquitie, and destroied them not, but oft times called backe his anger, and did not stirre vp all his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 6:3; James 4:14; Job 7:7"> For he remembered that they were flesh: yea, a winde that passeth and commeth not againe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; Psalms 95:8–10; Hebrews 3:15–17"> How oft did they prouoke him in the wildernes? and grieue him in the desert?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Hebrews 3:8–11; Numbers 14:22; Acts 7:39"> Yea, they returned, and tempted God, and limited the Holie one of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Judges 8:34; Psalms 78:11; Jeremiah 32:21"> They remembered not his hand, nor the day when he deliuered them from the enemie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:22; Exodus 4:21; Exodus 3:19–20"> Nor him that set his signes in Egypt, and his wonders in the fielde of Zoan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 105:29; Revelation 16:3–6; Exodus 7:17–21"> And turned their riuers into blood, and their floods, that they could not drinke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Exodus 8:21–24; Psalms 105:30–31; Exodus 8:2–15"> Hee sent a swarme of flies among them, which deuoured them, and frogs, which destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 10:12–15; Psalms 105:34–35; Amos 7:1–2"> He gaue also their fruites vnto the caterpiller, and their labour vnto the grassehopper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 105:32–33; Exodus 9:18–34"> He destroied their vines with haile, and their wilde figge trees with the hailestone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Exodus 9:19; Exodus 9:28"> He gaue their cattell also to the haile, and their flockes to the thunderboltes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Exodus 12:13; 1 Kings 22:21–22; Lamentations 4:11"> Hee cast vpon them the fiercenesse of his anger, indignation and wrath, and vexation by the sending out of euill Angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:4; 2 Peter 2:4–5; Romans 8:32"> He made a way to his anger: he spared not their soule from death, but gaue their life to the pestilence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 105:23; Psalms 135:8; Psalms 106:22"> And smote al the firstborne in Egypt, euen the beginning of their strength in the tabernacles of Ham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Psalms 77:20; John 10:11–42"> But hee made his people to goe out like sheepe, and led them in the wildernes like a flocke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Exodus 14:19–20; Exodus 15:10; Exodus 14:27–28"> Yea, he caried them out safely, and they feared not, and the Sea couered their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Exodus 15:17; Isaiah 11:9"> And he brought them vnto the borders of his Sanctuarie: euen to this Mountaine, which his right hand purchased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Joshua 13:7; Psalms 44:2; Nehemiah 9:22–25"> He cast out the heathe also before them, and caused them to fall to the lot of his inheritance, and made the tribes of Israel to dwell in their tabernacles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Judges 2:11–12; Deuteronomy 32:15–21; Psalms 78:40–41"> Yet they tempted, and prouoked the most high God, and kept not his testimonies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Hosea 7:16; Ezekiel 20:27–28; Psalms 78:8"> But turned backe and delt falsely like their fathers: they turned like a deceitfull bowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Leviticus 26:30; 1 Kings 12:31"> And they prouoked him to anger with their high places, and mooued him to wrath with their grauen images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Psalms 106:40; Lamentations 2:7; Deuteronomy 32:19"> God heard this and was wroth, and greatly abhorred Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; 1 Samuel 4:4–11; Jeremiah 26:6–9"> So that hee forsooke the habitation of Shilo, euen the Tabernacle where hee dwelt among men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Psalms 132:8; 1 Samuel 4:21–2; 1 Samuel 4:17"> And deliuered his power into captiuitie, and his beautie into the enemies hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Psalms 89:38; Isaiah 64:9; 1 Samuel 4:10–11"> And hee gaue vp his people to the sworde, and was angrie with his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Jeremiah 16:9; Deuteronomy 32:22"> The fire deuoured their chosen men, and their maides were not praised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:17; Job 27:15; Ezekiel 24:23"> Their Priestes fell by the sworde, and their widowes lamented not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6; Isaiah 51:9"> But the Lord awaked as one out of sleepe, and as a strong man that after his wine crieth out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:6; Jeremiah 23:40; Job 40:12"> And smote his enemies in the hinder parts, and put them to a perpetuall shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:21–1; 2 Samuel 6:2; 2 Samuel 6:17"> Yet he refused the tabernacle of Ioseph, and chose not the tribe of Ephraim:</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Psalms 87:2; Psalms 132:12–14; Ruth 4:17–22"> But chose the tribe of Iudah, and mount Zion which he loued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Job 26:7; 1 Chronicles 29:19; 1 Samuel 2:8"> And he built his Sanctuarie as an high palace, like the earth, which he stablished for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:11–12; Acts 13:22; Exodus 3:1"> He chose Dauid also his seruant, and tooke him from the shepefolds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:2; 1 Chronicles 11:2; Ezekiel 34:23–24"> Euen from behinde the ewes with yong brought he him to feede his people in Iaakob, and his inheritance in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="1 Kings 9:4; 2 Timothy 2:15; Isaiah 11:2–4"> So he fed them according to the simplicitie of his heart, and guided them by the discretion of his hands.</span>
