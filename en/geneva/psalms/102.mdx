<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Psalms 130:1–2; Psalms 142:2–3"> A prayer of the afflicted, when he shall be in distresse, and pour forth his meditation before the Lord. O Lord, heare my prayer, and let my crye come vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 27:9; Isaiah 8:17"> Hide not thy face from me in the time of my trouble: incline thine eares vnto me: when I call, make haste to heare me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalms 37:20; Job 30:30"> For my dayes are consumed like smoke, and my bones are burnt like an herthe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7; Psalms 37:2; Psalms 77:3"> Mine heart is smitten and withereth like grasse, because I forgate to eate my bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 19:20; Lamentations 4:8; Psalms 6:8"> For the voyce of my groning my bones doe cleaue to my skinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:14; Isaiah 38:14; Isaiah 34:11–15"> I am like a pelicane of the wildernesse: I am like an owle of the deserts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 77:4; Psalms 38:11; Deuteronomy 28:66–67"> I watch and am as a sparrowe alone vpon the house top.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 26:11; Psalms 69:9–10; Isaiah 65:15"> Mine enemies reuile me dayly, and they that rage against me, haue sworne against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Psalms 80:5; Micah 1:10"> Surely I haue eaten asshes as bread, and mingled my drinke with weeping,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 38:3; Psalms 90:7–9; Psalms 147:6"> Because of thine indignation and thy wrath: for thou hast heaued me vp, and cast me downe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 14:2; Psalms 109:23; Psalms 144:4"> My dayes are like a shadowe that fadeth, and I am withered like grasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 135:13; Lamentations 5:19; Exodus 3:15"> But thou, O Lord, doest remaine for euer, and thy remembrance from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; Galatians 4:4; Isaiah 40:2"> Thou wilt arise and haue mercy vpon Zion: for the time to haue mercie thereon, for the appointed time is come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:2; Ezra 7:27; Ezra 3:1–3"> For thy seruants delite in the stones thereof, and haue pitie on the dust thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:43; Psalms 138:4; Isaiah 60:3–22"> Then the heathen shall feare the Name of the Lord, and all the Kings of the earth thy glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–2; Psalms 147:2; Jeremiah 33:7"> When the Lord shall build vp Zion, and shall appeare in his glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:6; Psalms 22:24; Jeremiah 29:11–14"> And shall turne vnto the prayer of the desolate, and not despise their prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 22:30–31; Romans 15:4; Isaiah 43:21"> This shall be written for the generation to come: and the people, which shalbe created, shall prayse the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalms 14:2; 1 Kings 8:43"> For he hath looked downe from the height of his Sanctuarie: out of the heauen did the Lord beholde the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 79:11; Psalms 146:7; Exodus 3:7"> That he might heare the mourning of the prisoner, and deliuer the children of death:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:22; Isaiah 51:11; Psalms 79:13"> That they may declare the Name of the Lord in Zion, and his prayse in Ierusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 8:20–23; Matthew 24:14; Psalms 72:8–11"> When the people shalbe gathered together, and the kingdomes to serue the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 89:38–47; 1 Timothy 4:1–3; 2 Thessalonians 2:3–12"> He abated my strength in the way, and shortened my dayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 39:13; Habakkuk 1:12; Psalms 102:12"> And I sayd, O my God, take me not away in the middes of my dayes: thy yeeres endure from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; Exodus 20:11; Hebrews 1:10–12"> Thou hast aforetime layde the foundation of the earth, and the heauens are the worke of thine hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Matthew 24:35; Isaiah 34:4"> They shall perish, but thou shalt endure: euen they all shall waxe olde as doeth a garment: as a vesture shalt thou change them, and they shall be changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 13:8; James 1:17; Malachi 3:6"> But thou art the same, and thy yeeres shall not fayle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 69:35–36; Psalms 45:16–17; Psalms 22:30–31"> The children of thy seruants shall continue, and their seede shall stand fast in thy sight.</span>
