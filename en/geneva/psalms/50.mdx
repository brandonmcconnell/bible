<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 113:3; Joshua 22:22; Matthew 25:32"> A Psalme of Asaph. The God of Gods, euen the Lord hath spoken and called the earth from the rising vp of the sunne vnto the going downe thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 48:2; Psalms 80:1; Revelation 21:23"> Out of Zion, which is the perfection of beautie, hath God shined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 7:10; Numbers 16:35; Leviticus 10:2"> Our God shall come and shall not keepe silence: a fire shall deuoure before him, and a mightie tempest shall be mooued round about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Isaiah 1:2; Deuteronomy 31:28"> Hee shall call the heauen aboue, and the earth to iudge his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 26:28; Exodus 24:3–8; Hebrews 13:20"> Gather my Saints together vnto me, those that make a couenant with me with sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:6; Psalms 75:7; Psalms 89:5"> And the heauens shall declare his righteousnes: for God is iudge himselfe. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Psalms 81:8; Jeremiah 2:9"> Heare, O my people, and I wil speake: heare, O Israel, and I wil testifie vnto thee: for I am God, euen thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–31; Hebrews 10:4–10; Hosea 6:6"> I wil not reproue thee for thy sacrifices, or thy burnt offerings, that haue not bene continually before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 6:6–8; Hebrews 10:4–6; Psalms 69:31"> I will take no bullocke out of thine house, nor goates out of thy foldes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5–6; Psalms 8:6–8; Psalms 104:14"> For all the beastes of the forest are mine, and the beastes on a thousand mountaines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–22; Matthew 6:26; Luke 12:24"> I knowe all the foules on the mountaines: and the wilde beastes of the fielde are mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Deuteronomy 10:14; Psalms 24:1–2"> If I bee hungry, I will not tell thee: for the world is mine, and all that therein is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Will I eate the flesh of bulles? or drinke the blood of goates?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 56:12; Deuteronomy 23:21"> Offer vnto God praise, and pay thy vowes vnto the most High,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 107:28; Psalms 91:15; Psalms 34:3–4"> And call vpon me in the day of trouble: so will I deliuer thee, and thou shalt glorifie me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Psalms 78:36–38; Romans 2:17–24"> But vnto the wicked said God, What hast thou to doe to declare mine ordinances, that thou shouldest take my couenant in thy mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26; Proverbs 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:3–4"> Seeing thou hatest to bee reformed, and hast cast my wordes behinde thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 1:32; 1 Timothy 5:22; Hebrews 13:4"> For when thou seest a thiefe, thou runnest with him, and thou art partaker with the adulterers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 52:2–4; Psalms 55:21"> Thou giuest thy mouth to euill, and with thy tongue thou forgest deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Proverbs 10:18; Leviticus 19:16"> Thou sittest, and speakest against thy brother, and slanderest thy mothers sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:4–5; Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 57:11"> These things hast thou done, and I held my tongue: therefore thou thoughtest that I was like thee: but I will reproue thee, and set them in order before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 8:13; Psalms 9:17; Hosea 4:6"> Oh cosider this, ye that forget God, least I teare you in pieces, and there be none that can deliuer you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 91:16; James 3:13; Psalms 24:4–5"> He that offereth praise, shall glorifie mee: and to him, that disposeth his way aright, will I shew the saluation of God.</span>
