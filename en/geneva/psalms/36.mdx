<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 3:18; Proverbs 16:6; Psalms 143:12"> To him that excelleth. A Psalme of Dauid, the servant of the Lord. Wickedness sayeth to the wicked man, euen in mine heart, that there is no feare of God before his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:19; Psalms 10:3; Psalms 49:18"> For hee flattereth himselfe in his owne eyes, while his iniquitie is foud worthy to be hated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 55:21; Psalms 10:7; Jeremiah 4:22"> The wordes of his mouth are iniquitie and deceit: hee hath left off to vnderstand and to doe good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Proverbs 4:16; Romans 12:9"> Hee imagineth mischiefe vpon his bed: he setteth himselfe vpon a way, that is not good, and doeth not abhorre euill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 100:5; Psalms 108:4; Psalms 103:11"> Thy mercy, O Lord, reacheth vnto the heauens, and thy faithfulnesse vnto the cloudes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 77:19; Romans 11:33; Psalms 71:19"> Thy righteousnesse is like the mightie moutaines: thy iudgements are like a great deepe: thou, Lord, doest saue man and beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 86:5; Psalms 91:4; Ruth 2:12"> How excellent is thy mercy, O God! therefore the children of men trust vnder the shadowe of thy wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 65:4; Isaiah 58:11"> They shall be satisfied with the fatnesse of thine house, and thou shalt giue them drinke out of the riuer of thy pleasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 8:12; John 4:14; 1 John 1:7"> For with thee is the well of life, and in thy light shall we see light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Hebrews 8:11; Psalms 97:10–11"> Extend thy louing kindnes vnto them that knowe thee, and thy righteousnesse vnto them that are vpright in heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 16:8; Psalms 17:8–14; Psalms 119:51"> Let not ye foote of pride come against me, and let not the hand of ye wicked men moue me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 1:5; Psalms 58:10–11; Psalms 55:23"> There they are fallen that worke iniquity: they are cast downe, and shall not be able to rise.</span>
