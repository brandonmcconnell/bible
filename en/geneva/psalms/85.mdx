<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:2; Psalms 80:18; Isaiah 57:15"> Wilt thou not turne againe and quicken vs, that thy people may reioyce in thee?</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 46:13; Zechariah 2:5; John 7:17"> Surely his saluation is neere to them that feare him, that glory may dwell in our land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 3:3; Psalms 89:14; Psalms 100:5"> Mercie and trueth shall meete: righteousnes and peace shall kisse one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:8; John 14:6; 1 John 5:20–21"> Trueth shall bud out of the earth, and righteousnes shall looke downe from heauen.</span>
