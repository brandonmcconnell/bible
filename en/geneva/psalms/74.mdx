<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Psalms 79:13; Psalms 100:3"> A Psalme to give instruction, committed to Asaph. O God, why hast thou put vs away for euer? why is thy wrath kindled against the sheepe of thy pasture?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Exodus 15:16; Exodus 15:13"> Thinke vpon thy Congregation, which thou hast possessed of olde, and on the rod of thine inheritance, which thou hast redeemed, and on this mount Zion, wherein thou hast dwelt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 1:10; Psalms 79:1; Daniel 9:17"> Lift vp thy strokes, that thou mayest for euer destroy euery enemie that doeth euill to the Sanctuarie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Lamentations 2:7; Matthew 24:15; Daniel 6:27"> Thine aduersaries roare in the middes of thy Congregation, and set vp their banners for signes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 5:6; 2 Chronicles 2:14; Jeremiah 46:22–23"> He that lifted the axes vpon the thicke trees, was renowmed, as one, that brought a thing to perfection:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 6:18; 1 Kings 6:29; 1 Kings 6:35"> But nowe they breake downe the carued worke thereof with axes and hammers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 25:9; Psalms 89:39; Ezekiel 24:21"> They haue cast thy Sanctuarie into the fire, and rased it to the grounde, and haue defiled the dwelling place of thy Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 83:4; 2 Kings 4:23; Esther 3:8–9"> They saide in their hearts, Let vs destroy them altogether: they haue burnt all the Synagogues of God in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 8:11; Ezekiel 7:26; 1 Samuel 3:1"> We see not our signes: there is not one Prophet more, nor any with vs that knoweth howe long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 89:50–51; Psalms 44:16; Psalms 89:46"> O God, howe long shall the aduersarie reproche thee? shall the enemie blaspheme thy Name for euer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 2:3; Psalms 78:65–66; Psalms 44:23"> Why withdrawest thou thine hand, euen thy right hand? drawe it out of thy bosome, and consume them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 44:4; Exodus 19:5–6; Isaiah 33:22"> Euen God is my King of olde, working saluation in the middes of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Psalms 78:13; Ezekiel 29:3"> Thou didest deuide the sea by thy power: thou brakest the heads of the dragons in the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 72:9; Psalms 104:25–26"> Thou brakest the head of Liuiathan in pieces, and gauest him to be meate for the people in wildernesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; Isaiah 48:21; Psalms 105:41"> Thou brakest vp the fountaine and riuer: thou dryedst vp mightie riuers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 136:7–9; Matthew 5:45; Psalms 104:19"> The day is thine, and the night is thine: thou hast prepared the light and the sunne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:8; Acts 17:26; Genesis 8:22"> Thou hast set all the borders of the earth: thou hast made summer and winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:8; Revelation 16:19; Deuteronomy 32:6"> Remember this, that the enemie hath reproched the Lord, and the foolish people hath blasphemed thy Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:14; Matthew 10:16; Psalms 9:18"> Giue not the soule of thy turtle doue vnto the beast, and forget not the Congregation of thy poore for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Genesis 17:7–8; Hebrews 8:10"> Consider thy couenant: for the darke places of the earth are full of the habitations of the cruell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 35:10; Psalms 9:18"> Oh let not the oppressed returne ashamed, but let the poore and needie prayse thy Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 74:18; Psalms 53:1; Isaiah 52:5"> Arise, O God: mainteine thine owne cause: remember thy dayly reproche by the foolish man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Psalms 65:7; Jonah 1:2"> Forget not the voyce of thine enemies: for the tumult of them, that rise against thee, ascendeth continually.</span>
