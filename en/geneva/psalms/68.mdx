<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 10:35; Isaiah 51:9–10; Isaiah 42:13–14"> To him that excelleth. A Psalme or song of David. God will arise, and his enemies shalbe scattered: they also that hate him, shall flee before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 1:4; Psalms 37:20; Isaiah 9:18"> As the smoke vanisheth, so shalt thou driue them away: and as waxe melteth before the fire, so shall the wicked perish at the presence of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 64:10; Psalms 100:1–2; 1 Thessalonians 5:16"> But the righteous shalbe glad, and reioyce before God: yea, they shall leape for ioye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:26; Isaiah 12:4–6; Psalms 66:4"> Sing vnto God, and sing prayses vnto his name: exalt him that rideth vpon the heauens, in his Name Iah, and reioyce before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 10:14; Jeremiah 49:11; Psalms 82:3–4"> He is a Father of the fatherlesse, and a Iudge of the widowes, euen God in his holy habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:33; Psalms 113:9; Psalms 107:10"> God maketh the solitarie to dwell in families, and deliuereth them that were prisoners in stocks: but the rebellious shall dwell in a dry land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Judges 5:4; Judges 4:14"> O God, when thou wentest forth before thy people: when thou wentest through the wildernesse, (Selah)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 19:18; Exodus 19:16; Deuteronomy 5:23–25"> The earth shooke, and the heauens dropped at the presence of this God: euen Sinai was moued at the presence of God, euen the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:14; Psalms 65:9–13; Psalms 78:24–27"> Thou, O God, sendest a gracious raine vpon thine inheritance, and thou didest refresh it when it was wearie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:9–10; Psalms 74:19; Matthew 11:5"> Thy Congregation dwelled therein: for thou, O God, hast of thy goodnesse prepared it for the poore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Psalms 40:3; Exodus 17:9–16"> The Lord gaue matter to the women to tell of the great armie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 10:16; Judges 5:19; 1 Samuel 30:24"> Kings of the armies did flee: they did flee, and she that remained in the house, deuided the spoyle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 105:37; Genesis 49:14; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11"> Though ye haue lien among pots, yet shall ye be as the winges of a doue that is couered with siluer, and whose fethers are like yelowe golde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 9:48; Psalms 51:7; Isaiah 1:18"> When the Almightie scattered Kings in it, it was white as the snowe in Zalmon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 87:1–2; Psalms 78:68–69; Deuteronomy 3:10"> The mountaine of God is like the mountaine of Bashan: it is an high Mountaine, as mount Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Psalms 132:13–14; Revelation 21:2–3"> Why leape ye, ye high mountaines? as for this Mountaine, God deliteth to dwell in it: yea, the Lord will dwell in it for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Daniel 7:10; Revelation 5:11"> The charets of God are twentie thousande thousand Angels, and the Lord is among them, as in the Sanctuarie of Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 4:8–10; 2 Corinthians 6:16; Revelation 21:3"> Thou art gone vp on high: thou hast led captiuitie captiue, and receiued giftes for men: yea, euen the rebellious hast thou led, that the Lord God might dwell there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; Lamentations 3:23; Psalms 55:22"> Praysed be the Lord, euen the God of our saluation, which ladeth vs dayly with benefites. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Revelation 1:18; 1 Samuel 2:6"> This is our God, euen the God that saueth vs: and to the Lord God belong the issues of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:13; Psalms 7:12; Psalms 110:6"> Surely God will wound the head of his enemies, and the hearie pate of him that walketh in his sinnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 21:33; Ezekiel 36:24; Amos 9:2–3"> The Lord hath sayde, I will bring my people againe from Bashan: I will bring them againe from the depths of the Sea:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; Psalms 58:10; 1 Kings 22:38"> That thy foote may bee dipped in blood, and the tongue of thy dogges in the blood of the enemies, euen in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 63:2; 2 Samuel 6:12–17; 1 Chronicles 15:16–24"> They haue seene, O God, thy goings, the goings of my God, and my King, which art in the Sanctuarie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; Judges 11:34; Exodus 15:20"> The singers went before, the players of instruments after: in the middes were the maides playing with timbrels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 48:1; Deuteronomy 33:28; Psalms 26:12"> Praise yee God in the assemblies, and the Lord, ye that are of the fountaine of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:21; Judges 20:35; Judges 21:6–25"> There was litle Beniamin with their ruler, and the princes of Iudah with their assemblie, the princes of Zebulun, and the princes of Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 138:8; Psalms 71:3; Psalms 42:8"> Thy God hath appointed thy strength: stablish, O God, that, which thou hast wrought in vs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 10:10; Psalms 76:11; Isaiah 60:6–11"> Out of thy Temple vpon Ierusalem: and Kings shall bring presents vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 89:10; Psalms 22:12–13; Jeremiah 50:11"> Destroy the company of the spearemen, and multitude of the mightie bulles with the calues of the people, that tread vnder feete pieces of siluer: scatter the people that delite in warre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 45:14; Zephaniah 3:10; Psalms 44:20"> Then shall the princes come out of Egypt: Ethiopia shall hast to stretche her hands vnto God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 67:2–5; Romans 15:10–11; Psalms 117:1–2"> Sing vnto God, O yee kingdomes of the earth: sing praise vnto the Lord, (Selah)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; Psalms 18:10; Psalms 68:4"> To him that rideth vpon ye most high heauens, which were from the beginning: beholde, he will send out by his voice a mightie sound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 19:6; 2 Peter 1:17; 1 Chronicles 16:28–29"> Ascribe the power to God: for his maiestie is vpon Israel, and his strength is in the cloudes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 29:11; Colossians 1:11; Philippians 4:13"> O God, thou art terrible out of thine holie places: the God of Israel is hee that giueth strength and power vnto the people: praised be God.</span>
