<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:23–24; Psalms 100:1; Psalms 98:4"> To him that excelleth. A song or Psalme. Rejoice in God, all ye inhabitants of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 105:2–3; Revelation 5:13; Isaiah 49:13"> Sing forth the glory of his name: make his praise glorious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:44; Psalms 65:5; Psalms 81:15"> Say vnto God, Howe terrible art thou in thy workes! through the greatnesse of thy power shall thine enemies be in subiection vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 22:27; Psalms 117:1; Psalms 96:1–2"> All the worlde shall worship thee, and sing vnto thee, euen sing of thy Name. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 126:1–3; Numbers 23:23; Psalms 66:16"> Come and beholde the workes of God: he is terrible in his doing towarde the sonnes of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; Exodus 15:1–21; Joshua 3:14"> He hath turned the Sea into drie land: they passe through the riuer on foote: there did we reioyce in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 11:4; Matthew 28:18; Psalms 140:8"> He ruleth the worlde with his power: his eyes beholde the nations: the rebellious shall not exalt them selues. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 98:4; Revelation 19:1; Jeremiah 33:11"> Prayse our God, ye people, and make the voyce of his prayse to be heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 121:3; Psalms 112:6; Psalms 94:18"> Which holdeth our soules in life, and suffereth not our feete to slippe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 48:10; Psalms 17:3; 1 Peter 1:6–7"> For thou, O God, hast proued vs, thou hast tryed vs as siluer is tryed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 1:13; Ezekiel 12:13; Lamentations 3:2–66"> Thou hast brought vs into the snare, and layed a strait chaine vpon our loynes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:1–2; Isaiah 51:23; Psalms 40:2–3"> Thou hast caused men to ryde ouer our heads: we went into fire and into water, but thou broughtest vs out into a welthie place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 100:4; Psalms 56:12; Ecclesiastes 5:4"> I will go into thine House with burnt offrings, and will pay thee my vowes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:11; Judges 11:35–36; Psalms 18:6"> Which my lippes haue promised, and my mouth hath spoken in mine affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 51:19; 2 Samuel 6:17–19; 1 Chronicles 16:1–3"> I will offer vnto thee the burnt offerings of fat rammes with incense: I will prepare bullocks and goates. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 71:15; Psalms 34:11; Psalms 71:18"> Come and hearken, all ye that feare God, and I will tell you what he hath done to my soule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:6; Psalms 145:1; Psalms 116:12"> I called vnto him with my mouth, and he was exalted with my tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 9:31; Proverbs 28:9; Proverbs 15:29"> If I regard wickednesse in mine heart, the Lord will not heare me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 116:1–2; Hebrews 5:7; Lamentations 3:55–56"> But God hath heard me, and considered the voyce of my prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 86:12–13; Psalms 68:35; Psalms 51:11"> Praysed be God, which hath not put backe my prayer, nor his mercie from me.</span>
