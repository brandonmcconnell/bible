<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Psalms 27:9; Psalms 13:1–3"> Why standest thou farre off, O Lord, and hidest thee in due time, euen in affliction?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 59:12; Psalms 36:11; Psalms 7:16"> The wicked with pride doeth persecute the poore: let them be taken in the craftes that they haue imagined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 94:4; Luke 12:19; Zechariah 11:5–8"> For the wicked hath made boast of his owne heartes desire, and the couetous blesseth himselfe: he contemneth the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 14:1–2; Acts 8:22; Romans 1:28"> The wicked is so proude that hee seeketh not for God: hee thinketh alwayes, There is no God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 92:5–6; Isaiah 26:11; Psalms 12:5"> His wayes alway prosper: thy iudgements are hie aboue his sight: therefore defieth he all his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:11; Matthew 24:48; Isaiah 56:12"> He saith in his heart, I shall neuer be moued, nor be in danger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Psalms 140:9; Psalms 140:3"> His mouth is full of cursing and deceite and fraude: vnder his tongue is mischiefe and iniquitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:14; 1 Samuel 22:18; Jeremiah 22:17"> He lieth in waite in the villages: in the secret places doeth hee murder the innocent: his eyes are bent against the poore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 17:12; Psalms 59:3; Micah 7:2"> He lyeth in waite secretly, euen as a lyon in his denne: he lyeth in waite to spoyle the poore: he doeth spoyle the poore, when he draweth him into his net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:21–22; 1 Samuel 18:21–26; 2 Samuel 15:5"> He croucheth and boweth: therefore heaps of the poore doe fall by his might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Psalms 94:7; Psalms 73:11"> He hath said in his heart, God hath forgotten, he hideth away his face, and will neuer see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 5:9; Psalms 9:12; Psalms 94:2"> Arise, O Lord God: lift vp thine hande: forget not the poore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:9–10; Psalms 74:10; Genesis 9:5"> Wherefore doeth the wicked contemne God? he saith in his heart, Thou wilt not regard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 68:5; Psalms 146:9; Psalms 55:22"> Yet thou hast seene it: for thou beholdest mischiefe and wrong, that thou mayest take it into thine handes: the poore committeth himselfe vnto thee: for thou art the helper of the fatherlesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:12; Psalms 37:17; Job 38:15"> Breake thou the arme of the wicked and malicious: searche his wickednes, and thou shalt finde none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 29:10; Jeremiah 10:10; 1 Timothy 1:17"> The Lord is King for euer and euer: the heathen are destroyed foorth of his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; 2 Chronicles 34:27; Psalms 34:15"> Lord, thou hast heard the desire of the poore: thou preparest their heart: thou bendest thine eare to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 82:3; Isaiah 11:4"> To iudge the fatherlesse and poore, that earthly man cause to feare no more.</span>
