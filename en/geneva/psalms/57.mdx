<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 138:8; Philippians 1:6; Hebrews 13:21"> I will call vnto the most high God, euen to the God, that performeth his promise toward me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 40:11; Psalms 43:3; Acts 12:11"> He will send from heauen, and saue me from the reproofe of him that would swallowe me. Selah. God wil send his mercy, and his trueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 58:6; Proverbs 30:14; Psalms 35:17"> My soule is among lions: I lie among the children of men, that are set on fire: whose teeth are speares and arrowes, and their tongue a sharpe sworde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Isaiah 6:3; Psalms 72:19"> Exalt thy selfe, O God, aboue the heauen, and let thy glory be vpon all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 145:14; Psalms 7:15–16; Proverbs 28:10"> They haue layd a net for my steps: my soule is pressed downe: they haue digged a pit before me, and are fallen into the mids of it. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 112:7; Ephesians 5:20; Psalms 34:4"> Mine heart is prepared, O God, mine heart is prepared: I will sing and giue prayse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 16:9; Psalms 108:1–3; Psalms 30:12"> Awake my tongue, awake viole and harpe: I wil awake early.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 96:3; Psalms 22:22–23; Psalms 18:49"> I will prayse thee, O Lord, among the people, and I wil sing vnto thee among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 108:4; Psalms 103:11; Psalms 36:5"> For thy mercie is great vnto the heauens, and thy trueth vnto the cloudes.</span>
