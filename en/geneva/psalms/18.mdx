<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:1–51; Colossians 1:11; 1 John 4:19"> To him that excelleth. A Psalme of Dauid the seruant of the Lord, which spake unto the Lord the wordes of this song (in the day that the Lord delivered him for the hande of all this enemies, and form the and of saul) and sayd, I will loue thee dearely, O Lord my strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 144:2; Psalms 62:7; Psalms 19:14"> The Lord is my rocke, and my fortresse, and he that deliuereth me, my God and my strength: in him will I trust, my shield, the horne also of my saluation, and my refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 91:15; Psalms 145:3; Psalms 62:8"> I will call vpon the Lord, which is worthie to be praysed: so shall I be safe from mine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 116:3; Matthew 27:24–25; Mark 14:33–34"> The sorowes of death compassed me, and the floods of wickednes made me afraide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 88:3–8; Acts 2:24"> The sorowes of the graue haue compassed me about: the snares of death ouertooke me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; 2 Samuel 22:7; Psalms 34:15"> But in my trouble did I call vpon the Lord, and cryed vnto my God: he heard my voyce out of his Temple, and my crye did come before him, euen into his eares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 4:31; Psalms 68:7–8; Acts 16:25–26"> Then the earth trembled, and quaked: the foundations also of the mountaines mooued and shooke, because he was angrie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 21:9; Psalms 11:6; Nahum 1:5–6"> Smoke went out at his nostrels, and a consuming fire out of his mouth: coales were kindled thereat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 144:5–15; Deuteronomy 33:26; 2 Samuel 22:10"> He bowed the heauens also and came downe, and darkenes was vnder his feete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; 2 Samuel 22:11–12; Psalms 99:1"> And he rode vpon Cherub and did flie, and he came flying vpon the wings of the winde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Deuteronomy 4:11; Psalms 91:1"> He made darkenes his secrete place, and his pauilion round about him, euen darkenesse of waters, and cloudes of the ayre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; Revelation 16:21; Matthew 17:2"> At the brightnes of his presence his clouds passed, haylestones and coles of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 104:7; 1 Samuel 7:10; Psalms 140:10"> The Lord also thundred in the heauen, and the Highest gaue his voyce, haylestones and coales of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 144:6; Habakkuk 3:11; Numbers 24:8"> Then hee sent out his arrowes and scattred them, and he increased lightnings and destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 106:9; Exodus 15:8; Micah 6:2"> And the chanels of waters were seene, and the foundations of the worlde were discouered at thy rebuking, O Lord, at the blasting of the breath of thy nostrels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:17; Psalms 144:7; Psalms 57:3"> He hath sent downe from aboue and taken mee: hee hath drawen mee out of many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14–15; Psalms 35:10; Psalms 142:6"> He hath deliuered mee from my strong enemie, and from them which hate me: for they were too strong for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Obadiah 1:10–14; Deuteronomy 32:35; Psalms 46:11"> They preuented me in the day of my calamitie: but the Lord was my stay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 118:5; Psalms 31:8; Psalms 18:36"> Hee brought mee foorth also into a large place: hee deliuered mee because hee fauoured me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 24:4; Matthew 6:4; Psalms 58:11"> The Lord rewarded me according to my righteousnes: according to the purenes of mine hands he recompensed me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 119:102; Psalms 17:4; Psalms 26:1"> Because I kept the wayes of the Lord, and did not wickedly against my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:13; Psalms 119:112; Psalms 119:30"> For all his Lawes were before mee, and I did not cast away his commandements from mee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 18:8–9; Psalms 11:7; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> I was vpright also with him, and haue kept me from my wickednes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:41–42; Ruth 2:12; 1 Samuel 26:23"> Therefore the Lord rewarded me according to my righteousnesse, and according to the purenes of mine hands in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Psalms 62:12; Matthew 18:33–35"> With the godly thou wilt shewe thy selfe godly: with the vpright man thou wilt shew thy selfe vpright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 3:34; Leviticus 26:23–24; James 2:13"> With the pure thou wilt shewe thy selfe pure, and with the froward thou wilt shewe thy selfe froward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; James 2:5; Isaiah 57:15"> Thus thou wilt saue the poore people, and wilt cast downe the proude lookes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:29; Job 29:3; Isaiah 42:16"> Surely thou wilt light my candle: the Lord my God wil lighten my darkenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:30; Ephesians 6:10–13; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> For by thee I haue broken through an hoste, and by my God I haue leaped ouer a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Proverbs 30:5; 2 Samuel 22:31"> The way of God is vncorrupt: the worde of the Lord is tried in the fire: he is a shield to all that trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:31; Psalms 86:8; 2 Samuel 22:32"> For who is God besides the Lord? and who is mightie saue our God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 45:5; Psalms 28:7; 2 Samuel 22:33"> God girdeth me with strength, and maketh my way vpright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:19; Deuteronomy 32:13; Deuteronomy 33:29"> He maketh my feete like hindes feete, and setteth me vpon mine high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 144:1; Psalms 46:9; 2 Samuel 22:36"> He teacheth mine hands to fight: so that a bowe of brasse is broken with mine armes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:36; James 3:17–18; Psalms 28:7"> Thou hast also giuen me the shield of thy saluation, and thy right hand hath stayed me, and thy louing kindenes hath caused me to increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; 2 Samuel 22:37; Job 36:16"> Thou hast enlarged my steps vnder mee, and mine heeles haue not slid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 53:10–12; Psalms 44:5; Psalms 35:2"> I haue pursued mine enemies, and taken them, and haue not turned againe till I had consumed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:1–25; 2 Samuel 22:39; 1 Samuel 23:5"> I haue wounded them, that they were not able to rise: they are fallen vnder my feete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:40–41; Psalms 18:32; 1 Corinthians 15:25–28"> For thou hast girded me with strength to battell: them, that rose against me, thou hast subdued vnder me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 21:12; Psalms 94:23"> And thou hast giuen me the neckes of mine enemies, that I might destroy them that hate me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; Job 27:9; Job 35:12–13"> They cryed but there was none to saue them, euen vnto the Lord, but hee answered them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 10:6; Zechariah 10:5; 2 Kings 13:7"> Then I did beate them small as the dust before the winde: I did treade them flat as the clay in the streetes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Isaiah 55:5; 2 Samuel 3:1; Isaiah 52:15"> Thou hast deliuered me from the contentions of the people: thou hast made me the head of the heathen: a people, whom I haue not knowen, shall serue me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 66:3; Psalms 81:15; Deuteronomy 33:29"> As soone as they heare, they shall obey me: the strangers shall be in subiection to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Micah 7:17; James 1:11; Isaiah 24:4"> Strangers shall shrinke away, and feare in their priuie chambers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:47; Psalms 57:5; Psalms 18:2"> Let the Lord liue, and blessed be my strength, and the God of my saluation be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 47:3; Romans 12:19; Nahum 1:2"> It is God that giueth me power to auenge me, and subdueth the people vnder me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 140:1; Psalms 140:4; Psalms 86:14"> O my deliuerer from mine enemies, euen thou hast set mee vp from them, that rose against me: thou hast deliuered mee from the cruell man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Romans 15:9; 2 Samuel 22:50–51; Psalms 72:18–19"> Therefore I will prayse thee, O Lord, among the nations, and wil sing vnto thy Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 144:10; Psalms 132:10; 2 Samuel 7:13"> Great deliuerances giueth hee vnto his King, and sheweth mercie to his anoynted, euen to Dauid, and to his seede for euer.</span>
