<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 143:8; Psalms 86:4; Psalms 24:4"> A Psalme of David. Unto thee, O Lord, lift I vp my soule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalms 41:11; Psalms 71:1"> My God, I trust in thee: let me not be confounded: let not mine enemies reioyce ouer mee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25; Isaiah 40:31; Isaiah 49:23"> So all that hope in thee, shall not be ashamed: but let them be confounded, that transgresse without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 86:11; Psalms 27:11; Psalms 143:8"> Shew me thy waies, O Lord, and teache me thy paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 8:34; Isaiah 30:18; Psalms 25:10"> Leade me foorth in thy trueth, and teache me: for thou art the God of my saluation: in thee doe I trust all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 103:17; Colossians 3:12; Luke 1:50"> Remember, O Lord, thy tender mercies, and thy louing kindnesse: for they haue beene for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 38:17; Job 13:26"> Remember not the sinnes of my youth, nor my rebellions, but according to thy kindenesse remember thou me, euen for thy goodnesse sake, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 1:5; Psalms 32:8; Ephesians 1:17–18"> Gracious and righteous is the Lord: therefore will he teache sinners in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Psalms 23:3; Matthew 5:5"> Them that be meeke, will hee guide in iudgement, and teach the humble his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 5:11; Romans 8:28; Hebrews 8:8–12"> All the pathes of the Lord are mercie and trueth vnto such as keepe his couenant and his testimonies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 79:9; 1 John 2:12; Isaiah 43:25"> For thy Names sake, O Lord, be merciful vnto mine iniquitie, for it is great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:23; Psalms 32:8; John 7:17"> What man is he that feareth the Lord? him wil he teache the way that hee shall chuse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 37:11; Proverbs 1:33; Proverbs 19:23"> His soule shall dwell at ease, and his seede shall inherite the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 15:15; John 14:21–23; John 7:17"> The secrete of the Lord is reueiled to them, that feare him: and his couenant to giue them vnderstanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 141:8; Psalms 124:7–8; Psalms 31:4"> Mine eyes are euer towarde the Lord: for he will bring my feete out of the net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 86:16; Daniel 9:17; Micah 7:19"> Turne thy face vnto mee, and haue mercie vpon me: for I am desolate and poore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 107:6; Psalms 34:19; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> The sorowes of mine heart are enlarged: drawe me out of my troubles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Psalms 119:132; Psalms 51:8–9"> Looke vpon mine affliction and my trauel, and forgiue all my sinnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 56:2; Psalms 52:2; Psalms 38:19"> Beholde mine enemies, for they are manie, and they hate me with cruell hatred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 71:1–2; Psalms 121:7; Joel 2:26–27"> Keepe my soule, and deliuer me: let me not be confounded, for I trust in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 11:3; Psalms 41:12; Proverbs 20:7"> Let mine vprightnes and equitie preserue me: for mine hope is in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 130:8; Psalms 51:18–19; Psalms 14:7"> Deliuer Israel, O God, out of all his troubles.</span>
