<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 69:14–15; Isaiah 43:2; Lamentations 3:54"> To him that excelleth upon Shoshannim. A Psalme of David. Save mee, O God: for the waters are entred euen to my soule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 40:2; Jeremiah 38:6; Matthew 7:25"> I sticke fast in the deepe myre, where no staie is: I am come into deepe waters, and the streames runne ouer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Psalms 119:82; Isaiah 38:14"> I am wearie of crying: my throte is drie: mine eyes faile, whiles I waite for my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:25; Psalms 35:19; Psalms 109:3–5"> They that hate mee without a cause, are moe then the heares of mine heade: they that would destroy mee, and are mine enemies falsly, are mightie, so that I restored that which I tooke not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 38:9; Psalms 17:3; Jeremiah 16:17"> O God, thou knowest my foolishnesse, and my fautes are not hid from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 25:3; Psalms 35:26; Luke 24:19–21"> Let not them that trust in thee, O Lord God of hostes, be ashamed for me: let not those that seeke thee, be confounded through mee, O God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 44:22; Jeremiah 15:15; Isaiah 50:6"> For thy sake haue I suffred reproofe: shame hath couered my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; John 1:11; Matthew 26:70–74"> I am become a stranger vnto my brethren, euen an aliant vnto my mothers sonnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 15:3; John 2:14–17; Psalms 119:139"> For the zeale of thine house hath eaten mee, and the rebukes of them that rebuked thee, are fallen vpon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:13; Psalms 102:8–9; Luke 7:33–34"> I wept and my soule fasted, but that was to my reproofe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 9:7; Jeremiah 24:9; Job 17:6"> I put on a sacke also: and I became a prouerbe vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:15–16; Mark 15:17–19; Daniel 5:23"> They that sate in the gate, spake of mee, and the drunkards sang of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:2; Isaiah 49:8; Psalms 55:16–17"> But Lord, I make my praier vnto thee in an acceptable time, euen in the multitude of thy mercie: O God, heare me in the trueth of thy saluation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 144:7; Psalms 109:21; Jeremiah 38:6–13"> Deliuer mee out of the myre, that I sinke not: let me be deliuered from them that hate me, and out of the deepe waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 12:15–16; Acts 2:24; Jonah 2:2–7"> Let not the water flood drowne mee, neither let the deepe swallowe me vp: and let not the pit shut her mouth vpon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 109:21; Psalms 25:16; Psalms 63:3"> Heare me, O Lord, for thy louing kindnes is good: turne vnto me according to ye multitude of thy tender mercies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 27:9; Psalms 102:2; Psalms 70:1"> And hide not thy face from thy seruant, for I am in trouble: make haste and heare me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 22:19; Psalms 22:1; Psalms 111:9"> Draw neere vnto my soule and redeeme it: deliuer me because of mine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 53:3; 1 Peter 2:23"> Thou hast knowen my reproofe and my shame, and my dishonour: all mine aduersaries are before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 63:5; Psalms 142:4; Mark 14:50"> Rebuke hath broken mine heart, and I am full of heauinesse, and I looked for some to haue pitie on me, but there was none: and for comforters, but I found none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36; Mark 15:23"> For they gaue me gall in my meate, and in my thirst they gaue me vineger to drinke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Romans 11:8–10; 1 Peter 2:8"> Let their table be a snare before them, and their prosperitie their ruine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 5:6; Romans 11:10; Isaiah 6:9–10"> Let their eyes be blinded that they see not: and make their loynes alway to tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 79:6; Hosea 5:10; Exodus 15:15"> Powre out thine anger vpon them, and let thy wrathfull displeasure take them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 1:20; Matthew 23:38; Jeremiah 7:12–14"> Let their habitation be voide, and let none dwell in their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4; Zechariah 1:15; 2 Chronicles 28:9"> For they persecute him, whome thou hast smitten: and they adde vnto the sorrowe of them, whome thou hast wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; Nehemiah 4:5; 2 Timothy 4:14"> Laie iniquitie vpon their iniquitie, and let them not come into thy righteousnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Philippians 4:3; Exodus 32:32–33; Luke 10:20"> Let them be put out of the booke of life, neither let them be written with the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 91:14–16; Psalms 40:17; Psalms 20:1"> When I am poore and in heauinesse, thine helpe, O God, shall exalt me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 28:7; Psalms 34:3; Psalms 118:28–29"> I will praise the Name of God with a song, and magnifie him with thankesgiuing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 50:13–14; Psalms 50:23"> This also shall please the Lord better then a yong bullocke, that hath hornes and hoofes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 22:26; Psalms 34:2; John 16:22"> The humble shall see this, and they that seeke God, shalbe glad, and your heart shall liue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 68:6; Acts 5:18–19; Zechariah 9:11–12"> For the Lord heareth the poore, and despiseth not his prisoners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 96:11; Isaiah 49:13; Psalms 150:6"> Let heauen and earth praise him: the seas and all that moueth in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 44:26; Psalms 51:18; Isaiah 46:13"> For God will saue Zion, and builde the cities of Iudah, that men may dwell there and haue it in possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 102:28; Psalms 37:29; Acts 2:39"> The seede also of his seruants shall inherit it: and they that loue his name, shall dwel therein.</span>
