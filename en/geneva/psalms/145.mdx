<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 12:4; Psalms 119:27; Psalms 72:18"> I wil meditate of the beautie of thy glorious maiestie, and thy wonderfull workes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Daniel 3:28–29; Psalms 107:31–32"> And they shall speake of the power of thy fearefull actes, and I will declare thy greatnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 63:7; Psalms 36:10; 2 Corinthians 9:11–12"> They shall breake out into the mention of thy great goodnes, and shall sing aloude of thy righteousnesse.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; 1 Chronicles 29:11–12; Psalms 93:1–2"> They shewe the glory of thy kingdome, and speake of thy power,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 12:10; Psalms 105:1; Matthew 28:18"> To cause his power to be knowen to the sonnes of men, and the glorious renoume of his kingdome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Daniel 7:14; Psalms 10:16"> Thy kingdome is an euerlasting kingdome, and thy dominion endureth throughout all ages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 146:8; Psalms 37:24; Psalms 94:18"> The Lord vpholdeth all that fall, and lifteth vp all that are ready to fall.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 104:28; Psalms 107:9; Psalms 132:15"> Thou openest thine hand, and fillest all things liuing of thy good pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Zephaniah 3:5; Isaiah 45:21"> The Lord is righteous in all his wayes, and holy in all his workes.</span>
