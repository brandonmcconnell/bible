<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 35:23; Numbers 2:18–24; Psalms 44:23–26"> Before Ephraim and Beniamin and Manasseh stirre vp thy strength, and come to helpe vs.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 85:5; Isaiah 58:2–3; Psalms 59:5"> O Lord God of hostes, how long wilt thou be angrie against the prayer of thy people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Isaiah 30:20; Psalms 102:9"> Thou hast fedde them with the bread of teares, and giuen them teares to drinke with great measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Isaiah 36:8; Isaiah 37:23"> Thou hast made vs a strife vnto our neighbours, and our enemies laugh at vs among themselues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 51:10; Isaiah 64:5; Jeremiah 4:14"> Turne vs againe, O God of hostes: cause thy face to shine, and we shalbe saued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Psalms 44:2; Ezekiel 17:6"> Thou hast brought a vine out of Egypt: thou hast cast out the heathen, and planted it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 24:12; Isaiah 27:6; 1 Kings 4:20"> Thou madest roume for it, and didest cause it to take roote, and it filled the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 104:16"> The mountaines were couered with the shadowe of it, and the boughes thereof were like the goodly cedars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 72:8; 1 Chronicles 18:3; 1 Kings 4:24"> Shee stretched out her branches vnto the Sea, and her boughes vnto the Riuer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 5:5; Psalms 89:40–41; Isaiah 18:5–6"> Why hast thou then broken downe her hedges, so that all they, which passe by the way, haue plucked her?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:6; Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 39:1–3"> The wilde bore out of the wood hath destroyed it, and the wilde beastes of the fielde haue eaten it vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 63:15; Psalms 90:13; Daniel 9:16–19"> Returne we beseech thee, O God of hostes: looke downe from heauen and beholde and visite this vine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 15:1; Psalms 80:8; Jeremiah 2:21"> And the vineyard, that thy right hand hath planted, and the young vine, which thou madest strong for thy selfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 39:11; John 15:6; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> It is burnt with fire and cut downe: and they perish at the rebuke of thy countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Psalms 89:21; Psalms 110:1"> Let thine hande be vpon the man of thy right hande, and vpon the sonne of man, whome thou madest strong for thine owne selfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:107; Psalms 119:25; Psalms 119:40"> So will not we goe backe from thee: reuiue thou vs, and we shall call vpon thy Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:22–23; Psalms 80:3; Psalms 27:4"> Turne vs againe, O Lord God of hostes: cause thy face to shine and we shalbe saued.</span>
