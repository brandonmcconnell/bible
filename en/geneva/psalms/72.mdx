<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; 1 Chronicles 22:12–13; Isaiah 11:2"> A Psalme of Salomon. Give thy iudgements to the King, O God, and thy righteousnesse to the Kings sonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 32:1; Psalms 72:12–14; Revelation 19:11"> Then shall he iudge thy people in righteousnesse, and thy poore with equitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Psalms 96:11–13; Psalms 72:16"> The mountaines and the hilles shall bring peace to the people by iustice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 11:4; Ezekiel 34:15–16; Psalms 109:31"> He shall iudge the poore of the people: he shall saue the children of the needie, and shall subdue the oppressor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:36–37; Psalms 72:17; Daniel 7:14"> They shall feare thee as long as the sunne and moone endureth, from generatio to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; Deuteronomy 32:2; 2 Samuel 23:4"> He shall come downe like the rayne vpon the mowen grasse, and as the showres that water the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:4; Malachi 4:2; Psalms 92:12"> In his dayes shall the righteous florish, and abundance of peace shalbe so long as the moone endureth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Zechariah 9:10; Psalms 80:11"> His dominion shall be also from sea to sea, and from the Riuer vnto the endes of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Micah 7:17; Luke 19:27"> They that dwell in ye wildernes, shall kneele before him, and his enemies shall licke the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6; Psalms 68:29; Isaiah 49:7"> The Kings of Tarshish and of the yles shall bring presents: the Kings of Sheba and Seba shall bring giftes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Isaiah 49:22–23; Revelation 11:15"> Yea, all Kings shall worship him: all nations shall serue him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 29:12; Revelation 3:17–18; Psalms 102:17"> For he shall deliuer the poore when he cryeth: the needie also, and him that hath no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Psalms 109:31; Matthew 5:3"> He shalbe mercifull to the poore and needie, and shall preserue the soules of the poore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 116:15; Revelation 19:2; Psalms 69:18"> He shall redeeme their soules from deceite and violence, and deare shall their blood be in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:14; Revelation 5:8–14; Jude 1:25"> Yea, he shall liue, and vnto him shall they giue of the golde of Sheba: they shall also pray for him continually, and dayly blesse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Psalms 92:12–14; Isaiah 30:23"> An handfull of corne shall be sowen in the earth, euen in the toppe of the mountaines, and the fruite thereof shall shake like the trees of Lebanon: and the children shall florish out of the citie like the grasse of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:23; Psalms 89:36"> His name shall be for euer: his name shall indure as long as the sunne: all nations shall blesse him, and be blessed in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:13; Psalms 106:48; 1 Chronicles 29:10"> Blessed be the Lord God, euen the God of Israel, which onely doeth wonderous things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:21; Matthew 6:10; Psalms 41:13"> And blessed be his glorious Name for euer: and let all the earth be filled with his glorie. So be it, euen so be it. HERE END THE prayers of Dauid, the sonne of Ishai.</span>
