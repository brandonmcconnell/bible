<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 57:1; Psalms 88:9; Ephesians 6:18"> Be mercifull vnto me, O Lord: for I crie vpon thee continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 143:8; Psalms 25:1; Psalms 51:12"> Reioyce the soule of thy seruant: for vnto thee, O Lord, doe I lift vp my soule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 145:8–9; Joel 2:13; Exodus 34:6"> For thou, Lord, art good and mercifull, and of great kindenes vnto all them, that call vpon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 5:1–2; Psalms 17:1; Psalms 130:2"> Giue eare, Lord, vnto my prayer, and hearken to the voyce of my supplication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 34:4–6; Psalms 17:6; Psalms 50:15"> In the day of my trouble I will call vpon thee: for thou hearest me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Deuteronomy 3:24; Psalms 89:6"> Among the gods there is none like thee, O Lord, and there is none that can doe like thy workes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 15:4; Isaiah 66:23; Psalms 66:4"> All nations, whome thou hast made, shall come and worship before thee, O Lord, and shall glorifie thy Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 37:16; Mark 12:29; Psalms 72:18"> For thou art great and doest wonderous things: thou art God alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:38–39; Psalms 25:4–5; Psalms 5:8"> Teach me thy way, O Lord, and I will walke in thy trueth: knit mine heart vnto thee, that I may feare thy Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5; Psalms 146:1–2; Romans 15:6"> I wil prayse thee, O Lord my God, with all mine heart: yea, I wil glorifie thy Name for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 103:8–12; Luke 1:58; Psalms 56:13"> For great is thy mercie toward me, and thou hast deliuered my soule from the lowest graue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 54:3; Psalms 14:4; Psalms 36:1"> O God, the proude are risen against me, and the assemblies of violent men haue sought my soule, and haue not set thee before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–7; Psalms 130:7; Ephesians 1:7"> But thou, O Lord, art a pitifull God and mercifull, slowe to anger and great in kindenes and trueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Philippians 4:13; Isaiah 40:29–31"> Turne vnto me, and haue mercy vpon me: giue thy strength vnto thy seruant, and saue the sonne of thine handmayd.</span>
