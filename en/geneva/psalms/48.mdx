<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 4:1; Zechariah 8:3; Psalms 96:4"> A song or Psalme committed to the sonnes of Korah. Great is the Lord, and greatly to be praysed, in the Citie of our God, euen vpon his holy Mountaine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:2; Lamentations 2:15; Matthew 5:35"> Mount Zion, lying Northwarde, is faire in situation: it is the ioy of the whole earth, and the Citie of the great King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 2:4–5; Psalms 125:1; Psalms 46:7"> In the palaces thereof God is knowen for a refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:6–19; Revelation 17:12–14; Revelation 20:8–9"> For lo, the Kings were gathered, and went together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 14:25; 2 Kings 7:6–7; 2 Kings 19:35–37"> When they sawe it, they marueiled: they were astonied, and suddenly driuen backe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 13:13; Isaiah 13:6–8; Isaiah 21:3"> Feare came there vpon them, and sorowe, as vpon a woman in trauaile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:17; Ezekiel 27:25–26; 1 Kings 22:48"> As with an East winde thou breakest the shippes of Tarshish, so were they destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 87:5; Psalms 78:3–6; Psalms 46:5"> As we haue heard, so haue we seene in the citie of the Lord of hostes, in the Citie of our God: God will stablish it for euer. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 26:3; Psalms 40:10; Psalms 77:10–14"> We waite for thy louing kindnes, O God, in the middes of thy Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 1:11; Deuteronomy 28:58; Psalms 113:3"> O God, according vnto thy Name, so is thy prayse vnto the worlds end: thy right hand is full of righteousnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 97:8; Revelation 16:5–7; Revelation 18:20"> Let mount Zion reioyce, and the daughters of Iudah be glad, because of thy iudgements.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 33:18–20; Nehemiah 12:31–40; Matthew 24:1–2"> Compasse about Zion, and goe round about it, and tell the towres thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 71:18; Psalms 122:7; Deuteronomy 11:19"> Marke well the wall thereof: beholde her towres, that ye may tell your posteritie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; Psalms 23:3–4; Psalms 73:24"> For this God is our God for euer and euer: he shall be our guide vnto the death.</span>
