<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Psalms 86:6; Psalms 61:1"> To him that excelleth on Neginoth. A Psalme of David to give instruction. Heare my prayer, O God, and hide not thy selfe from my supplication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Psalms 66:19; Psalms 77:3"> Hearken vnto me, and answere me: I mourne in my prayer, and make a noyse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:7–8; Psalms 27:12; Psalms 35:11"> For the voyce of the enemie, and for the vexation of ye wicked, because they haue brought iniquitie vpon me, and furiously hate me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 116:3; Mark 14:33–34; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> Mine heart trembleth within mee, and the terrours of death are fallen vpon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Job 21:6; 2 Samuel 15:14"> Feare and trembling are come vpon mee, and an horrible feare hath couered me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 11:1; Revelation 12:14; Psalms 139:9"> And I said, Oh that I had wings like a doue: then would I flie away and rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:2; 2 Samuel 17:21–22; Jeremiah 37:12"> Beholde, I woulde take my flight farre off, and lodge in the wildernes. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 4:6; Matthew 7:25–27; Isaiah 17:12–13"> Hee would make haste for my deliuerance from the stormie winde and tempest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:7; Genesis 11:7–9; Jeremiah 23:14"> Destroy, O Lord, and deuide their tongues: for I haue seene crueltie and strife in the citie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 59:14–15; Isaiah 59:6–15; Ezekiel 9:4"> Day and night they goe about it vpon the walles thereof: both iniquitie and mischiefe are in the middes of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26–27; Psalms 10:7; Psalms 5:9"> Wickednes is in the middes thereof: deceit and guile depart not from her streetes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 13:18; Matthew 26:21–23"> Surely mine enemie did not defame mee: for I could haue borne it: neither did mine aduersarie exalt himselfe against mee: for I would haue hid me from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:12; Micah 7:5; Jeremiah 9:4"> But it was thou, O man, euen my companion, my guide and my familiar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Isaiah 2:3; Ezekiel 33:31"> Which delited in consulting together, and went into the House of God as companions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 59:13; Psalms 64:7; Psalms 9:17"> Let death sense vpon them: let them goe downe quicke into the graue: for wickednes is in their dwellings, euen in the middes of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 109:4; Psalms 73:28"> But I will call vnto God, and the Lord will saue me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; Psalms 141:2"> Euening and morning, and at noone will I pray, and make a noyse, and he wil heare my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 57:3; Psalms 3:6–7; 2 Samuel 22:1"> He hath deliuered my soule in peace from the battel, that was against me: for many were with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Revelation 6:10–11; Jeremiah 48:11"> God shall heare and afflict them, euen hee that reigneth of olde, Selah. because they haue no changes, therefore they feare not God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 12:1; Psalms 89:34; Psalms 7:4"> Hee layed his hande vpon such, as be at peace with him, and he brake his couenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 28:3; Proverbs 5:3–4"> The wordes of his mouth were softer then butter, yet warre was in his heart: his words were more gentle then oyle, yet they were swordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Psalms 37:5; Philippians 4:6–7"> Cast thy burden vpon the Lord, and hee shall nourish thee: he wil not suffer the righteous to fall for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 10:27; Psalms 5:6; Job 15:32"> And thou, O God, shalt bring them downe into the pitte of corruption: the bloudie, and deceitfull men shall not liue halfe their dayes: but I will trust in thee.</span>
