<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 6:1; Isaiah 54:8; Hebrews 12:5–11"> A Psalme of Dauid for remembrance. O Lord, rebuke mee not in thine anger, neither chastise me in thy wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:4; Psalms 32:4; Psalms 64:7"> For thine arrowes haue light vpon me, and thine hand lyeth vpon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:2; Psalms 51:8; Psalms 31:9–10"> There is nothing sound in my flesh, because of thine anger: neither is there rest in my bones because of my sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Ezra 9:6; Matthew 11:28"> For mine iniquities are gone ouer mine head, and as a weightie burden they are too heauie for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 69:5; Jeremiah 8:22; Isaiah 1:5–6"> My woundes are putrified, and corrupt because of my foolishnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 30:28; Psalms 35:14; Psalms 42:9"> I am bowed, and crooked very sore: I goe mourning all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:18–19; Acts 12:23; Psalms 102:3"> For my reines are full of burning, and there is nothing sound in my flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 3:24; Psalms 32:3; Job 30:28"> I am weakened and sore broken: I roare for the very griefe of mine heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:17; Psalms 6:6; 2 Corinthians 5:2"> Lord, I powre my whole desire before thee, and my sighing is not hid from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:7; Psalms 88:9; Psalms 69:3"> Mine heart panteth: my strength faileth me, and the light of mine eyes, euen they are not mine owne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Job 19:13–17; Luke 23:49"> My louers and my friends stand aside from my plague, and my kinsmen stand a farre off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:20; Psalms 140:5; 2 Samuel 17:1–3"> They also, that seeke after my life, laye snares, and they that go about to do me euil, talke wicked things and imagine deceite continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 39:2; Isaiah 53:7; Psalms 39:9"> But I as a deafe man heard not, and am as a dumme man, which openeth not his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:13; John 8:6; Mark 15:3–5"> Thus am I as a man, that heareth not, and in whose mouth are no reproofes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 39:7; Psalms 17:6; Psalms 138:3"> For on thee, O Lord, do I waite: thou wilt heare me, my Lord, my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 94:18; Deuteronomy 32:35; Psalms 35:24–26"> For I said, Heare me, least they reioyce ouer me: for when my foote slippeth, they extol themselues against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 38:6; Psalms 6:6; Psalms 35:15"> Surely I am ready to halte, and my sorow is euer before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 32:5; Proverbs 28:13; Job 33:27"> When I declare my paine, and am sory for my sinne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; John 15:18–25; Psalms 69:4"> Then mine enemies are aliue and are mightie, and they that hate me wrongfully are many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:12; 1 John 3:12; Jeremiah 18:20"> They also, that rewarde euill for good, are mine aduersaries, because I follow goodnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:19; Psalms 22:24; Psalms 22:11"> Forsake me not, O Lord: be not thou farre from me, my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; Psalms 40:13; Isaiah 12:2"> Haste thee to helpe mee, O my Lord, my saluation.</span>
