<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Psalms 89:17; Psalms 45:7"> But thou shalt exalt mine horne, like the vnicornes, and I shalbe anoynted with fresh oyle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 91:8; Psalms 54:7; Psalms 59:10"> Mine eye also shall see my desire against mine enemies: and mine eares shall heare my wish against the wicked, that rise vp against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:8; Psalms 1:3; Psalms 52:8"> The righteous shall flourish like a palme tree, and shall grow like a Cedar in Lebanon.</span>
