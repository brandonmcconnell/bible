<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 24:19"> A Psalme of David. Fret not thy selfe because of the wicked men, neither be enuious for the euill doers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 92:7; Psalms 90:5–6; James 1:10–11"> For they shall soone bee cut downe like grasse, and shall wither as the greene herbe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; Matthew 6:31–33; Psalms 62:8"> Trust thou in the Lord and do good: dwell in the land, and thou shalt be fed assuredly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:7; Psalms 145:19; John 15:16"> And delite thy selfe in the Lord, and hee shall giue thee thine hearts desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 16:3; 1 Peter 5:7; Philippians 4:6–7"> Commit thy way vnto the Lord, and trust in him, and he shall bring it to passe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 54:17; Job 11:17; Isaiah 58:8"> And he shall bring foorth thy righteousnes as the light, and thy iudgement as the noone day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 5:7–11; Psalms 27:14; Psalms 62:5"> Waite patiently vpon the Lord and hope in him: fret not thy selfe for him which prospereth in his way: nor for the man that bringeth his enterprises to passe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Proverbs 16:32; James 1:19–20"> Cease from anger, and leaue off wrath: fret not thy selfe also to doe euill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:21; Psalms 25:13; Psalms 37:29"> For euill doers shalbe cut off, and they that wait vpon the Lord, they shall inherite the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 24:24; Psalms 103:16; Psalms 37:35–36"> Therefore yet a litle while, and the wicked shall not appeare, and thou shalt looke after his place, and he shall not be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:5; Galatians 5:22–23; Isaiah 26:3"> But meeke men shall possesse the earth, and shall haue their delite in the multitude of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Psalms 35:16; Psalms 31:13"> The wicked practiseth against the iust, and gnasheth his teeth against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Proverbs 1:26; 1 Samuel 26:10"> But the Lord shall laugh him to scorne: for he seeth, that his day is comming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 29:27; Psalms 11:2; 1 John 3:12"> The wicked haue drawen their sworde, and haue bent their bowe, to cast downe the poore and needie, and to slay such as be of vpright conuersation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 7:14–15; Psalms 35:8; Jeremiah 51:56"> But their sword shall enter into their owne heart, and their bowes shalbe broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Proverbs 15:16–17; 1 Timothy 6:6"> A small thing vnto the iust man is better, then great riches to the wicked and mightie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 63:8; Psalms 145:14"> For the armes of the wicked shall be broken: but the Lord vpholdeth the iust men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 1:6; Romans 6:23; Psalms 103:17"> The Lord knoweth the dayes of vpright men, and their inheritance shall bee perpetuall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 33:19; Proverbs 10:3; Job 5:20–22"> They shall not be confounded in the perilous time, and in the daies of famine they shall haue ynough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 68:2; Psalms 102:3; Luke 13:3"> But the wicked shall perish, and the enemies of the Lord shall be consumed as the fatte of lambes: euen with the smoke shall they consume away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Psalms 112:5; Proverbs 22:7"> The wicked boroweth and payeth not againe. but the righteous is mercifull, and giueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 3:33; 1 Corinthians 16:22; Galatians 3:13"> For such as be blessed of God, shall inherite the lande, and they that be cursed of him, shalbe cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 16:9; Psalms 119:133; Proverbs 4:26"> The pathes of man are directed by the Lord: for he loueth his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 24:16; Psalms 145:14; Psalms 94:18"> Though he fall, hee shall not be cast off: for the Lord putteth vnder his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 37:28; Psalms 112:2"> I haue beene yong, and am olde: yet I sawe neuer the righteous forsaken, nor his seede begging bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 112:5; Matthew 5:7; Psalms 37:21"> But hee is euer mercifull and lendeth, and his seede enioyeth the blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 34:14; Proverbs 16:6; 1 John 2:16–17"> Flee from euill and doe good, and dwell for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 11:7; Proverbs 2:22; Psalms 21:10"> For the Lord loueth iudgement, and forsaketh not his Saintes: they shall be preserued for euermore: but the seede of the wicked shall be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 2:21; Psalms 37:9; 2 Peter 3:13"> The righteous men shall inherit the lande, and dwell therein for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Proverbs 25:11–13; Ephesians 4:29"> The mouth of the righteous will speake of wisedome, and his tongue will talke of iudgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 40:8; Deuteronomy 6:6; Isaiah 51:7"> For the Lawe of his God is in his heart, and his steppes shall not slide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 19:47–48; Psalms 10:8–10; Psalms 37:12"> The wicked watcheth the righteous, and seeketh to slay him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 109:31; 2 Peter 2:9; Romans 8:1"> But the Lord wil not leaue him in his hand, nor condemne him, when he is iudged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Psalms 37:9; Proverbs 20:22"> Waite thou on the Lord, and keepe his way, and he shall exalt thee, that thou shalt inherite the lande: when the wicked men shall perish, thou shalt see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 5:3; Ezekiel 31:18; Isaiah 14:14–19"> I haue seene the wicked strong, and spreading himselfe like a greene bay tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 37:10; Acts 12:22–23; Isaiah 10:16–19"> Yet he passed away, and loe, he was gone, and I sought him, but he could not be founde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 32:17; Isaiah 57:1–2; 2 Timothy 4:6–8"> Marke the vpright man, and beholde the iust: for the end of that man is peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Proverbs 14:32; Psalms 52:5; Psalms 1:4–6"> But the transgressours shall be destroyed together, and the ende of the wicked shall bee cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 91:15; Isaiah 12:2"> But the saluation of the righteous men shalbe of the Lord: he shalbe their strength in the time of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 31:5; 1 Chronicles 5:20; Daniel 6:23"> For the Lord shall helpe them, and deliuer them: he shall deliuer them from the wicked, and shall saue them, because they trust in him.</span>
