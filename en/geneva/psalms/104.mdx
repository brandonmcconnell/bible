<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 103:22; Psalms 145:3; Psalms 96:6"> My soule, prayse thou the Lord: O Lord my God, thou art exceeding great, thou art clothed with glorie and honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Daniel 7:9; Isaiah 45:12"> Which couereth himselfe with light as with a garment, and spreadeth the heauens like a curtaine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 9:6; 2 Samuel 22:11; Isaiah 19:1"> Which layeth the beames of his chambers in the waters, and maketh the cloudes his chariot, and walketh vpon the wings of the winde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; 2 Kings 2:11; 2 Kings 6:17"> Which maketh his spirits his messengers, and a flaming fire his ministers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 26:7; Psalms 24:2; Psalms 33:9"> He set the earth vpon her foundations, so that it shall neuer moue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:19; 2 Peter 3:5; Genesis 1:2–10"> Thou coueredst it with the deepe as with a garment: the waters woulde stand aboue the mountaines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 18:15; Genesis 8:1; Mark 4:39"> But at thy rebuke they flee: at the voyce of thy thunder they haste away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 8:5; Psalms 33:7"> And the mountaines ascend, and the valleis descend to the place which thou hast established for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Genesis 9:11–15; Jeremiah 5:22"> But thou hast set them a bounde, which they shall not passe: they shall not returne to couer the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:18; Psalms 107:35; Isaiah 35:7"> He sendeth the springs into the valleis, which runne betweene the mountaines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 145:16; Job 39:5–8; Psalms 104:13"> They shall giue drinke to all the beasts of the fielde, and the wilde asses shall quench their thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 50:11; Psalms 148:10; Matthew 8:20"> By these springs shall the foules of the heauen dwell, and sing among the branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Jeremiah 10:13; Jeremiah 14:22"> He watereth the mountaines from his chambers, and the earth is filled with the fruite of thy workes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Job 28:5; Psalms 147:8–9"> He causeth grasse to growe for the cattell, and herbe for the vse of man, that he may bring forth bread out of the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Ecclesiastes 10:19; Ecclesiastes 9:7"> And wine that maketh glad the heart of man, and oyle to make the face to shine, and bread that strengtheneth mans heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 24:6; Psalms 92:2; Psalms 29:5"> The high trees are satisfied, euen the cedars of Lebanon, which he hath planted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 104:12; Leviticus 11:19; Daniel 4:21"> That ye birdes may make their nestes there: the storke dwelleth in the firre trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 30:26; Job 39:1; Leviticus 11:5"> The high mountaines are for the goates: the rockes are a refuge for the conies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalms 136:7–9"> He appoynted the moone for certaine seasons: the sunne knoweth his going downe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Psalms 74:16; Amos 1:13"> Thou makest darkenesse, and it is night, wherein all the beastes of the forest creepe forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 38:39; Joel 1:20; Psalms 34:10"> The lions roare after their praye, and seeke their meate at God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 37:8; Nahum 3:17; John 3:20"> When the sunne riseth, they retire, and couche in their dennes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 5:12; Ephesians 4:28"> Then goeth man forth to his worke, and to his labour vntill the euening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:12; Psalms 40:5; Psalms 50:10–12"> O Lord, howe manifolde are thy workes! in wisdome hast thou made them all: the earth is full of thy riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 69:34; Psalms 95:4–5; Genesis 1:28"> So is this sea great and wide: for therein are things creeping innumerable, both small beastes and great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 107:23; Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 74:14"> There goe the shippes, yea, that Liuiathan, whom thou hast made to play therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Psalms 147:9; Job 38:41"> All these waite vpon thee, that thou maiest giue them foode in due season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 145:16"> Thou giuest it to them, and they gather it: thou openest thine hand, and they are filled with good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 34:14–15; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Psalms 146:4"> But if thou hide thy face, they are troubled: if thou take away their breath, they dye and returne to their dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 33:4; Ezekiel 37:9; Revelation 21:5"> Againe if thou send forth thy spirit, they are created, and thou renuest the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 1:31; 2 Timothy 4:18; Ephesians 3:21"> Glory be to the Lord for euer: let the Lord reioyce in his workes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 144:5; Psalms 97:4–5; Exodus 19:18"> He looketh on the earth and it trembleth: he toucheth the mountaines, and they smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 63:4; Psalms 146:2; Psalms 145:1–2"> I will sing vnto the Lord all my life: I will prayse my God, while I liue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:17–18; Philippians 4:4; Psalms 1:2"> Let my wordes be acceptable vnto him: I will reioyce in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 103:22–1; Psalms 37:38; Psalms 106:48"> Let the sinners be consumed out of the earth, and the wicked till there be no more: O my soule, prayse thou the Lord. Prayse ye the Lord.</span>
