<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 31:1–3; Psalms 25:2–3; 1 Peter 2:6"> In thee, O Lord, I trust: let me neuer be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 31:1; Psalms 17:6; Psalms 116:1–2"> Rescue mee and deliuer me in thy righteousnes: incline thine eare vnto me and saue me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Psalms 91:9; Proverbs 18:10"> Be thou my strong rocke, whereunto I may alway resort: thou hast giuen commandement to saue me: for thou art my rocke, and my fortresse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 140:1–4; Psalms 59:1–2; 2 Samuel 17:1–2"> Deliuer mee, O my God, out of the hande of the wicked: out of the hande of the euill and cruell man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7; Psalms 39:7; Psalms 119:81"> For thou art mine hope, O Lord God, euen my trust from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 22:9–10; Jeremiah 1:5; Psalms 139:15–16"> Vpon thee haue I beene stayed from the wombe: thou art he that tooke me out of my mothers bowels: my praise shalbe alwaies of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; 1 Corinthians 4:9; Acts 4:13"> I am become as it were a monster vnto many: but thou art my sure trust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 35:28; Psalms 146:2; Psalms 104:1"> Let my mouth be filled with thy praise, and with thy glory euery day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; 2 Timothy 1:12; Psalms 71:18"> Cast mee not off in the time of age: forsake me not when my strength faileth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 83:3; Psalms 56:6; Jeremiah 20:10"> For mine enemies speake of mee, and they that lay waite for my soule, take their counsell together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 7:2; Psalms 3:2; Matthew 27:46"> Saying, God hath forsaken him: pursue and take him, for there is none to deliuer him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:22; Psalms 38:21–22; Psalms 70:5"> Goe not farre from me, O God: my God, haste thee to helpe me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:11; Psalms 71:24; Psalms 35:26"> Let them be confounded and consumed that are against my soule: let them be couered with reproofe and confusion, that seeke mine hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 130:7; 1 John 3:3; Psalms 71:6"> But I will waite continually, and will praise thee more and more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; Psalms 35:28; Psalms 71:8"> My mouth shall daily rehearse thy righteousnesse, and thy saluation: for I knowe not the nomber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Psalms 51:14; Philippians 3:9"> I will goe forwarde in the strength of the Lord God, and will make mention of thy righteousnesse, euen of thine onely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 71:5; Psalms 119:9; Psalms 119:102"> O God, thou hast taught me from my youth euen vntill nowe: therefore will I tell of thy wonderous workes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; Psalms 71:9; Psalms 78:4"> Yea, euen vnto mine olde age and graie head, O God: forsake me not, vntill I haue declared thine arme vnto this generation, and thy power to all them, that shall come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 35:10; Psalms 36:5–6; Psalms 57:10"> And thy righteousnes, O God, I wil exalt on high: for thou hast done great thinges: O God, who is like vnto thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 138:7; Psalms 60:3; Psalms 86:13"> Which hast shewed me great troubles and aduersities, but thou wilt returne, and reuiue me, and wilt come againe, and take mee vp from the depth of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 12:1; 2 Samuel 3:1; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> Thou wilt increase mine honour, and returne and comfort me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 92:1–3; 2 Kings 19:22; Psalms 33:2"> Therefore will I praise thee for thy faithfulnesse, O God, vpon instrument and viole: vnto thee will I sing vpon the harpe, O Holy one of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 104:33; Psalms 103:4; Luke 1:46–47"> My lippes will reioyce when I sing vnto thee, and my soule, which thou hast deliuered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 71:13; Psalms 18:37–43; 1 Corinthians 15:25"> My tongue also shall talke of thy righteousnesse daily: for they are confounded and brought vnto shame, that seeke mine hurt.</span>
