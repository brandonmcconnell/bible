<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalms 25:2; Psalms 71:1–3"> To him that excelleth. A Psalme of David. In thee, O Lord, haue I put my trust: let mee neuer be confounded: deliuer me in thy righteousnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 71:2–3; Psalms 102:2; Psalms 86:1"> Bowe downe thine eare to me: make haste to deliuer mee: be vnto me a stronge rocke, and an house of defence to saue me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 143:10–11; Luke 1:79; Psalms 79:9"> For thou art my rocke and my fortresse: therefore for thy Names sake direct mee and guide me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 25:15; Psalms 140:5; Psalms 19:14"> Drawe mee out of the nette, that they haue layde priuilie for mee: for thou art my strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 23:46; Acts 7:59; Deuteronomy 32:4"> Into thine hand I commend my spirit: for thou hast redeemed me, O Lord God of trueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jonah 2:8; Psalms 24:4; Jeremiah 10:15"> I haue hated them that giue them selues to deceitfull vanities: for I trust in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Isaiah 63:9; Psalms 119:153"> I wil be glad and reioyce in thy mercie: for thou hast seene my trouble: thou hast knowen my soule in aduersities,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 36:16; Deuteronomy 32:30; Psalms 18:19"> And thou hast not shut me vp in the hand of the enemie, but hast set my feete at large.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 6:7; Psalms 88:9; Psalms 73:26"> Haue mercie vpon mee, O Lord: for I am in trouble: mine eye, my soule and my bellie are consumed with griefe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 88:15; Psalms 32:3–4; Psalms 38:3"> For my life is wasted with heauinesse, and my yeeres with mourning: my strength faileth for my paine, and my bones are consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Psalms 88:8; Psalms 88:18"> I was a reproch among all mine enemies, but specially among my neighbours: and a feare to mine acquaintance, who seeing me in the streete, fled from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 88:4–5; Psalms 2:9; Romans 9:21–22"> I am forgotten, as a dead man out of minde: I am like a broken vessell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; Matthew 27:1; Lamentations 2:22"> For I haue heard the rayling of great men: feare was on euery side, while they conspired together against mee, and consulted to take my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 56:3–4; Psalms 18:2; Psalms 43:5"> But I trusted in thee, O Lord: I said, Thou art my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Ecclesiastes 3:1–8; John 17:1"> My times are in thine hande: deliuer mee from the hande of mine enemies, and from them that persecute me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 4:6; Psalms 6:4; Daniel 9:9"> Make thy face to shine vpon thy seruant, and saue me through thy mercie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 25:2–3; Jeremiah 20:11; Psalms 34:5"> Let me not be confounded, O Lord: for I haue called vpon thee: let the wicked bee put to confusion, and to silence in the graue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jude 1:15; Psalms 94:4; 1 Samuel 2:3"> Let the lying lips be made dumme, which cruelly, proudly and spitefully speake against the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9; Isaiah 64:4; Lamentations 3:23–25"> Howe great is thy goodnesse, which thou hast layde vp for them, that feare thee! and done to them, that trust in thee, euen before the sonnes of men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 27:5; Psalms 32:7; Psalms 91:1–4"> Thou doest hide them priuily in thy presence from the pride of men: thou keepest them secretly in thy Tabernacle from the strife of tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:7–13; Psalms 17:7; 1 Peter 2:9"> Blessed be the Lord: for hee hath shewed his marueilous kindenesse toward me in a strong citie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 116:11; Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 88:16"> Though I said in mine haste, I am cast out of thy sight, yet thou heardest the voyce of my prayer, when I cryed vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 97:10; Psalms 94:2; Psalms 145:20"> Loue ye the Lord all his Saintes: for the Lord preserueth the faithfull, and rewardeth abundantly the proud doer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Psalms 146:5; Colossians 1:11"> All ye that trust in the Lord, be strong, and he shall establish your heart.</span>
