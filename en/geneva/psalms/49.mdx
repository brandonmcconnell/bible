<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 62:9; Proverbs 22:2; Jeremiah 5:4–5"> As well lowe as hie, both rich and poore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 19:14; Proverbs 22:17; Matthew 12:35"> My mouth shall speake of wisdome, and the meditation of mine heart is of knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Matthew 13:35; Numbers 12:8"> I will incline mine eare to a parable, and vtter my graue matter vpon the harpe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 27:24; Romans 8:33–34; Amos 5:13"> Wherefore should I feare in the euil dayes, when iniquitie shall compasse me about, as at mine heeles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Psalms 52:7; Jeremiah 9:23"> They trust in their goods, and boast them selues in the multitude of their riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Timothy 2:6; 1 Peter 1:18"> Yet a man can by no meanes redeeme his brother: he can not giue his raunsome to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Job 36:18–19"> (So precious is the redemption of their soules, and the continuance for euer)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:48; Psalms 16:10; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> That he may liue still for euer, and not see the graue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Psalms 39:6; Hebrews 9:27"> For he seeth that wise men die, and also that the ignorant and foolish perish, and leaue their riches for others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 4:17; Psalms 5:9; Psalms 64:6"> Yet they thinke, their houses, and their habitations shall continue for euer, euen from generation to generation, and call their lands by their names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18–21; Psalms 49:20; Psalms 82:7"> But man shall not continue in honour: he is like the beastes that die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:20; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Luke 16:27–28"> This their way vttereth their foolishnes: yet their posteritie delite in their talke. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; 1 Corinthians 6:2; Daniel 7:22"> Like sheepe they lie in graue: death deuoureth them, and the righteous shall haue domination ouer them in the morning: for their beautie shall consume, when they shall goe from their house to graue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 73:24; Psalms 56:13; Psalms 86:13"> But God shall deliuer my soule from the power of the graue: for he will receiue me. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 37:1; Revelation 21:26"> Be not thou afrayd when one is made rich, and when the glory of his house is increased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:7; Job 27:19; Luke 16:24"> For he shall take nothing away when he dieth, neither shall his pompe descende after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 12:19; Deuteronomy 29:19; Psalms 10:3"> For while he liued, he reioyced himselfe: and men will prayse thee, when thou makest much of thy selfe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 33:30; Genesis 15:15; Psalms 56:13"> He shall enter into the generation of his fathers, and they shall not liue for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 49:12; Ecclesiastes 3:18–19; Job 4:21"> Man is in honour, and vnderstandeth not: he is like to beasts that perish.</span>
