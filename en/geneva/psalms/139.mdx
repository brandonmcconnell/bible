<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Jeremiah 12:3; Jeremiah 17:9–10"> To him that excelleth. A Psalme of David. O Lord, thou hast tried me and knowen me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:4; 2 Kings 19:27; Psalms 94:11"> Thou knowest my sitting and my rising: thou vnderstandest my thought afarre off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:24; Job 31:4; Genesis 28:10–17"> Thou compassest my pathes, and my lying downe, and art accustomed to all my wayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 19:14; Hebrews 4:12–13; Psalms 50:19–21"> For there is not a word in my tongue, but loe, thou knowest it wholy, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Psalms 34:7; Revelation 1:17"> Thou holdest mee straite behinde and before, and layest thine hand vpon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Job 42:3; Job 26:14"> Thy knowledge is too wonderfull for mee: it is so high that I cannot attaine vnto it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:23–24; Jonah 1:3; Jonah 1:10"> Whither shall I goe from thy Spirite? or whither shall I flee from thy presence?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 9:2–4; Jonah 2:2; Job 34:21–22"> If I ascende into heauen, thou art there: if I lye downe in hell, thou art there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 19:6; Psalms 74:16–17; Malachi 4:2"> Let mee take the winges of the morning, and dwell in the vttermost parts of the sea:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 23:3; Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 73:23"> Yet thither shall thine hand leade me, and thy right hand holde me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:22; Jeremiah 23:24; Psalms 94:7"> If I say, Yet the darkenes shall hide me, euen the night shalbe light about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; Job 34:22; Hebrews 4:13"> Yea, the darkenes hideth not from thee: but the night shineth as the day: the darkenes and light are both alike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Psalms 119:73; Isaiah 44:2"> For thou hast possessed my reines: thou hast couered me in my mothers wombe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 1:26–27; Psalms 111:2; Psalms 104:24"> I will praise thee, for I am fearefully and wonderously made: marueilous are thy workes, and my soule knoweth it well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:5; Psalms 139:13; Job 10:8–11"> My bones are not hid from thee, though I was made in a secret place, and facioned beneath in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 56:8; Malachi 3:16; Revelation 20:12"> Thine eyes did see me, when I was without forme: for in thy booke were all things written, which in continuance were facioned, when there was none of them before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; Jeremiah 29:11; Isaiah 55:8–9"> Howe deare therefore are thy thoughtes vnto me, O God! how great is ye summe of them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 3:5; Psalms 139:3; Psalms 40:5"> If I should count them, they are moe then the sand: when I wake, I am still with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 5:6; Psalms 119:115; Isaiah 11:4"> Oh that thou wouldest slay, O God, the wicked and bloody men, to whom I say, Depart ye from mee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jude 1:15; Exodus 20:7; Psalms 74:18"> Which speake wickedly of thee, and being thine enemies are lifted vp in vaine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 119:158; Psalms 31:6; Psalms 26:5"> Doe not I hate them, O Lord, that hate thee? and doe not I earnestly contend with those that rise vp against thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 101:3–8; Luke 14:26"> I hate them with an vnfained hatred, as they were mine vtter enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 26:2; Job 31:6; 1 Peter 1:7"> Try mee, O God, and knowe mine heart: prooue me and know my thoughtes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 143:10; Jeremiah 17:9–10; Psalms 25:8–9"> And consider if there be any way of wickednes in me, and leade me in the way for euer.</span>
