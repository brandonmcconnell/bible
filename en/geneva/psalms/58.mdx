<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:20; Isaiah 10:1; Ezekiel 22:27"> Yea, rather ye imagine mischiefe in your heart: your hands execute crueltie vpon the earth.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 140:3; James 3:8; Ecclesiastes 10:11"> Their poyson is euen like the poyson of a serpent: like ye deafe adder that stoppeth his eare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:17; Isaiah 19:3; Deuteronomy 18:11"> Which heareth not the voyce of the inchanter, though he be most expert in charming.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 112:10; Joshua 7:5; Psalms 64:3"> Let them melt like the waters, let them passe away: when hee shooteth his arrowes, let them be as broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 3:16; Ecclesiastes 6:3; Psalms 37:35–36"> Let them consume like a snayle that melteth, and like the vntimely fruite of a woman, that hath not seene the sunne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 118:12; Proverbs 10:25; Ecclesiastes 7:6"> As raw flesh before your pots feele the fire of thornes: so let him cary them away as with a whirlewinde in his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 68:23; Psalms 91:8; Psalms 64:10"> The righteous shall reioyce when he seeeth the vengeance: he shall wash his feete in the blood of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 67:4; Isaiah 3:10; Psalms 64:9"> And men shall say, Verily there is fruite for the righteous: doutlesse there is a God that iudgeth in the earth.</span>
