<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 71:2; Psalms 31:1; Psalms 140:6"> A Psalme of David. Heare my prayer, O Lord, and hearken vnto my supplication: answer me in thy trueth and in thy righteousnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; Romans 3:20; Job 25:4"> (And enter not into iudgement with thy seruant: for in thy sight shall none that liueth, be iustified)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:6; Psalms 7:1–2; Psalms 7:5"> For the enemie hath persecuted my soule: he hath smitten my life downe to the earth: he hath layde me in the darkenes, as they that haue bene dead long agoe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 142:3; Psalms 77:3; Psalms 102:3–4"> And my spirit was in perplexitie in me, and mine heart within me was amased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 77:5–6; Psalms 77:10–12; Micah 6:5"> Yet do I remember the time past: I meditate in all thy workes, yea, I doe meditate in the workes of thine hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 63:1; Job 11:13; Psalms 84:2"> I stretche forth mine hands vnto thee: my soule desireth after thee, as the thirstie land. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 28:1; Psalms 27:9"> Heare me spedely, O Lord, for my spirit faeleth: hide not thy face from me, els I shall be like vnto them that go downe into the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; Proverbs 3:5–6; Isaiah 48:17"> Let me heare thy louing kindenes in the morning, for in thee is my trust: shewe mee the waie, that I should walke in, for I lift vp my soule vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 61:3–4; Psalms 59:1"> Deliuer me, O Lord, from mine enemies: for I hid me with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; John 14:26; Hebrews 13:21"> Teach me to do thy will, for thou art my God: let thy good Spirit leade me vnto the land of righteousnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 31:1; Ephesians 2:4–5; Psalms 71:2"> Quicken me, O Lord, for thy Names sake, and for thy righteousnes bring my soule out of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 54:5; Psalms 116:16; Psalms 119:94"> And for thy mercy slay mine enemies, and destrie all them that oppresse my soule: for I am thy seruant.</span>
