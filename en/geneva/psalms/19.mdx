<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 1:19–20; Psalms 8:3; Psalms 50:6"> To him that excelleth. A Psalme of Dauid. The heauens declare the glory of God, and the firmament sheweth ye worke of his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 74:16; Psalms 134:1–3; Genesis 8:22"> Day vnto day vttereth the same, and night vnto night teacheth knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:19"> There is no speach nor language, where their voyce is not heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 10:18; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalms 98:3"> Their line is gone forth through all the earth, and their words into the endes of the world: in them hath he set a tabernacle for the sunne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 62:5; Philippians 3:13–14; 1 Corinthians 9:24–26"> Which commeth forth as a bridegrome out of his chamber, and reioyceth like a mightie man to runne his race.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:5; Job 22:14; Colossians 1:23"> His going out is from the ende of the heauen, and his compasse is vnto the endes of ye same, and none is hid from the heate thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 119:130; Psalms 111:7; Colossians 3:16"> The Lawe of the Lord is perfite, conuerting the soule: the testimonie of the Lord is sure, and giueth wisedome vnto the simple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:105; Psalms 12:6; Jeremiah 15:16"> The statutes of the Lord are right and reioyce the heart: the commandement of the Lord is pure, and giueth light vnto the eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:24; Psalms 119:142; Psalms 119:137–138"> The feare of the Lord is cleane, and indureth for euer: the iudgements of the Lord are trueth: they are righteous altogether,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:103; Proverbs 3:13–15; Psalms 119:72"> And more to be desired then golde, yea, then much fine golde: sweeter also then honie and the honie combe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; 2 John 1:8; Hebrews 11:6–7"> Moreouer by them is thy seruant made circumspect, and in keeping of them there is great reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 90:8; Job 6:24; Psalms 139:23–24"> Who can vnderstand his faultes? clense me from secret fautes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 119:133; Romans 6:12–14; 1 Samuel 25:39"> Keepe thy seruant also from presumptuous sinnes: let them not reigne ouer me: so shall I be vpright, and made cleane from much wickednes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:34; Psalms 51:15; Psalms 119:108"> Let the wordes of my mouth, and the meditation of mine heart be acceptable in thy sight, O Lord, my strength, and my redeemer.</span>
