<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:48; Psalms 140:4; Psalms 71:4"> To him that excelleth. A Psalme of David. Deliuer me, O Lord, from the euill man: preserue me from the cruel man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 56:6; Psalms 36:4; Psalms 64:5–6"> Which imagine euill things in their heart, and make warre continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 58:4; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Romans 3:13–14"> They haue sharpened their tongues like a serpent: adders poyson is vnder their lips. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 36:11; Psalms 71:4; Psalms 17:5"> Keepe mee, O Lord, from the handes of the wicked: preserue mee from the cruell man, which purposeth to cause my steppes to slide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 142:3; Psalms 35:7; Jeremiah 18:22"> The proude haue layde a snare for me, and spred a nette with cordes in my pathway, and set grennes for me. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 16:2; Psalms 31:14; Psalms 119:57"> Therefore I saide vnto the Lord, Thou art my God: heare, O Lord, the voyce of my prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 62:7; Psalms 89:26; Psalms 18:1–2"> O Lord God the strength of my saluation, thou hast couered mine head in the day of battel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 10:2–3; Deuteronomy 32:27; Psalms 27:12"> Let not the wicked haue his desire, O Lord: performe not his wicked thought, least they be proude. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 7:16; Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 12:13"> As for the chiefe of them, that compasse me about, let the mischiefe of their owne lippes come vpon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Psalms 21:9; Revelation 21:8"> Let coles fal vpon them: let him cast them into the fire, and into the deepe pits, that they rise not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:21; Proverbs 18:21; Isaiah 3:11"> For the backbiter shall not be established vpon the earth: euill shall hunt the cruell man to destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 9:4; 1 Kings 8:49; Proverbs 23:10–11"> I know that the Lord will auenge the afflicted, and iudge the poore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 11:7; Isaiah 3:10"> Surely the righteous shall prayse thy Name, and the iust shall dwell in thy presence.</span>
