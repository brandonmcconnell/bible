<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 106:1; 1 Chronicles 16:34; 2 Chronicles 7:3"> Praise the Lord, because he is good: for his mercie endureth for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; Luke 1:74; Galatians 3:13"> Let them, which haue bene redeemed of the Lord, shewe how he hath deliuered them from the hand of the oppressour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 106:47; Isaiah 43:5–6; Revelation 5:9"> And gathered them out of the lands, from the East and from the West, from the North and from the South.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:10; Ezekiel 34:12; Ezekiel 34:6"> When they wandered in the desert and wildernesse out of the waie, and founde no citie to dwell in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 8:2–3; 1 Samuel 30:11–12; Lamentations 2:19"> Both hungrie and thirstie, their soule fainted in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 91:15; Jeremiah 29:12–14"> Then they cried vnto the Lord in their trouble, and he deliuered them from their distresse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Psalms 77:20; Hebrews 11:9–10"> And led them forth by the right way, that they might goe to a citie of habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 107:31; Psalms 147:1; Daniel 4:2–3"> Let them therefore confesse before ye Lord his louing kindnesse, and his wonderfull woorkes before the sonnes of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:6; Jeremiah 31:25; Psalms 34:10"> For he satisfied the thirstie soule, and filled the hungrie soule with goodnesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 1:79; Micah 7:8; Romans 6:20–21"> They that dwell in darkenesse and in the shadowe of death, being bounde in miserie and yron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 106:43; Psalms 78:40; Romans 1:28"> Because they rebelled against the wordes of the Lord, and despised the counsell of the most High,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:11; Exodus 5:18–19; Exodus 2:23"> When he humbled their heart with heauines, then they fell downe and there was no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 107:28; Psalms 107:6; Judges 4:3"> Then they cried vnto the Lord in their trouble, and he deliuered them from their distresse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 42:16; Ephesians 5:8; Psalms 116:16"> He brought them out of darkenes, and out of the shadowe of death, and brake their bandes asunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 107:21; Psalms 107:31; Psalms 107:8"> Let them therefore cofesse before the Lord his louing kindnesse, and his wonderfull woorkes before the sonnes of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1–2; Judges 16:3; Micah 2:13"> For hee hath broken the gates of brasse, and brast the barres of yron asunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 3:39; Proverbs 1:22; Psalms 38:1–8"> Fooles by reason of their transgression, and because of their iniquities are afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 9:13; Psalms 88:3; Job 33:19–22"> Their soule abhorreth al meat, and they are brought to deaths doore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 78:34–35; Psalms 34:4–6; Psalms 107:13"> Then they crie vnto the Lord in their trouble, and he deliuereth them from their distresse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 8:8; Psalms 103:3–4; Psalms 147:15"> He sendeth his worde and healeth them, and deliuereth them from their graues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:8; Psalms 107:15; 2 Chronicles 32:25"> Let them therefore cofesse before the Lord his louing kindnesse, and his wonderful workes before the sonnes of men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 50:14; Psalms 118:17; Psalms 9:11"> And let them offer sacrifices of praise, and declare his workes with reioycing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 18:17; Psalms 48:7; Ezekiel 27:26"> They that goe downe to the sea in ships, and occupie by the great waters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 104:24–27; Psalms 95:5; Job 38:8–11"> They see the woorkes of the Lord, and his wonders in the deepe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jonah 1:4; Matthew 8:24; Psalms 148:8"> For he commaundeth and raiseth the stormie winde, and it lifteth vp the waues thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 22:14; Psalms 119:28; Isaiah 13:7"> They mount vp to the heauen, and descend to ye deepe, so that their soule melteth for trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 12:25; Isaiah 29:9; Isaiah 19:3"> They are tossed to and from, and stagger like a drunken man, and all their cunning is gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 8:25; Psalms 107:13; Psalms 107:19"> Then they crie vnto the Lord in their trouble, and he bringeth them out of their distresse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 8:26; Psalms 65:7; Luke 8:23–25"> He turneth the storme to calme, so that the waues thereof are still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 6:21"> When they are quieted, they are glad, and hee bringeth them vnto the hauen, where they would be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 107:8; Psalms 103:2; Psalms 107:21"> Let them therfore confesse before the Lord his louing kindnesse, and his wonderfull woorkes before the sonnes of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 22:25; Psalms 22:22; Psalms 99:5"> And let them exalt him in the Congregation of the people, and praise him in the assembly of the Elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 50:2; Isaiah 42:15; Amos 4:7–8"> He turneth the floodes into a wildernesse, and the springs of waters into drinesse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Genesis 13:10; Ezekiel 47:11"> And a fruitfull land into barrennes for the wickednes of them that dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 114:8; Isaiah 35:6–7; Isaiah 41:17–19"> Againe hee turneth the wildernesse into pooles of water, and the drie lande into water springs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 107:7; Luke 1:53; Acts 17:26"> And there he placeth the hungrie, and they builde a citie to dwell in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:7; 2 Corinthians 9:10; Jeremiah 29:5"> And sowe the fieldes, and plant vineyardes, which bring foorth fruitfull increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 1:7; Genesis 12:2; Genesis 17:20"> For he blesseth them, and they multiplie exceedingly, and he diminisheth not their cattell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 2:23–24; 2 Kings 10:32; 2 Chronicles 15:5–6"> Againe men are diminished, and brought lowe by oppression, euill and sorowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Job 12:24; Job 12:21; Daniel 4:33"> He powreth contempt vpon princes, and causeth them to erre in desert places out of the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; Job 21:11; Esther 8:15–17"> Yet he raiseth vp the poore out of miserie, and maketh him families like a flocke of sheepe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 63:11; Job 22:19; Romans 3:19"> The righteous shall see it, and reioyce, and all iniquitie shall stoppe her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Hosea 14:9; Jeremiah 9:12; Psalms 64:9"> Who is wise that hee may obserue these things? for they shall vnderstand the louing kindnesse of the Lord.</span>
