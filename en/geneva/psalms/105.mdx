<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 12:4; Psalms 145:11–12; Romans 10:13"> Praise the Lord, and call vpon his Name: declare his workes among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 119:27; Psalms 98:5; Isaiah 12:5–6"> Sing vnto him, sing prayse vnto him, and talke of all his wonderous workes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25; Galatians 6:14; 1 Corinthians 1:29"> Reioyce in his holy Name: let the heart of them that seeke the Lord, reioyce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 27:8; Zephaniah 2:2–3; 2 Chronicles 6:41"> Seeke the Lord and his strength: seeke his face continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 77:11; Psalms 119:13; Deuteronomy 32:7"> Remember his marueilous woorkes, that he hath done, his wonders and the iudgements of his mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 44:1–2; John 15:16; 1 Peter 2:9"> Ye seede of Abraham his seruant, ye children of Iaakob, which are his elect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 26:9; Deuteronomy 26:17–18; Exodus 20:2"> He is the Lord our God: his iudgements are through all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Psalms 106:45; Psalms 111:5"> He hath alway remembred his couenant and promise, that he made to a thousand generations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 17:2; Genesis 22:16–18; Genesis 26:3"> Euen that which he made with Abraham, and his othe vnto Izhak:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; 2 Samuel 23:5; Genesis 28:13–15"> And since hath confirmed it to Iaakob for a lawe, and to Israel for an euerlasting couenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Genesis 15:18; Psalms 78:55"> Saying, Vnto thee will I giue the land of Canaan, the lot of your inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Deuteronomy 7:7; Hebrews 11:9"> Albeit they were fewe in nomber, yea, very fewe, and strangers in the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And walked about from nation to nation, from one kingdome to another people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 35:5; Exodus 7:16–17; Genesis 26:14–33"> Yet suffered he no man to doe them wrong, but reprooued Kings for their sakes, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; Genesis 26:11; Genesis 20:7"> Touche not mine anointed, and doe my Prophets no harme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16; Genesis 41:54; Isaiah 3:1"> Moreouer, he called a famine vpon ye land, and vtterly brake the staffe of bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 7:9; Genesis 37:36; Genesis 45:4–5"> But he sent a man before them: Ioseph was solde for a slaue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 16:24; Genesis 39:20; Genesis 40:15"> They helde his feete in the stockes, and he was laide in yrons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:10; Proverbs 21:1; Genesis 40:20–21"> Vntill his appointed time came, and the counsell of the Lord had tryed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 41:14"> The King sent and loosed him: euen the Ruler of the people deliuered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 41:40–44; Genesis 45:8; Genesis 41:55"> He made him lord of his house, and ruler of all his substance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Genesis 41:33; Genesis 41:38"> That he shoulde binde his princes vnto his will, and teach his Ancients wisedome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 7:11–15; Acts 13:17; Psalms 78:51"> Then Israel came to Egypt, and Iaakob was a stranger in the land of Ham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 46:3; Exodus 1:7–9; Acts 7:17"> And he increased his people exceedingly, and made them stronger then their oppressours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Genesis 15:13; Exodus 9:16"> He turned their heart to hate his people, and to deale craftily with his seruants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 3:10; Exodus 29:5–46; Exodus 28:12"> Then sent he Moses his seruant, and Aaron whom he had chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 78:43–51; Nehemiah 9:10–11; Psalms 105:23"> They shewed among them the message of his signes, and wonders in the land of Ham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 99:7; Joel 2:31; Joel 2:2"> He sent darkenesse, and made it darke: and they were not disobedient vnto his commission.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 7:20–21; Psalms 78:44; Isaiah 50:2"> He turned their waters into blood, and slewe their fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 16:13–14; Exodus 8:3–14; Psalms 78:45"> Their land brought foorth frogs, euen in their Kings chambers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 8:16–18; Exodus 8:21–24; Isaiah 7:18"> He spake, and there came swarmes of flies and lice in all their quarters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 78:47–48; Exodus 9:18–28; Revelation 8:7"> He gaue them haile for raine, and flames of fire in their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 78:47; Revelation 9:4"> He smote their vines also and their figge trees, and brake downe the trees in their coastes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 10:12–15; Psalms 78:46; Joel 1:4–7"> He spake, and the grashoppers came, and caterpillers innumerable,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And did eate vp all the grasse in their land, and deuoured the fruite of their ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 49:3; Exodus 12:29–30; Psalms 78:51"> He smote also all the first borne in their land, euen the beginning of all their strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 12:35–36; Exodus 3:22; Acts 13:17"> He brought them forth also with siluer and golde, and there was none feeble among their tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 12:33; Exodus 15:16; Genesis 35:5"> Egypt was glad at their departing: for the feare of them had fallen vpon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Psalms 78:14; Isaiah 4:5"> He spred a cloude to be a couering, and fire to giue light in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 78:18; Numbers 11:31–33; Joshua 5:12"> They asked, and he brought quailes, and he filled them with the bread of heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; 1 Corinthians 10:4; Exodus 17:6"> He opened the rocke, and the waters flowed out, and ranne in the drye places like a riuer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 2:24; Luke 1:72–73; Genesis 12:7"> For he remembred his holy promise to Abraham his seruant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Isaiah 35:10; Acts 7:36; Jeremiah 31:11–12"> And he brought forth his people with ioy, and his chosen with gladnesse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:10–11; Joshua 24:13; Psalms 78:55"> And gaue them the lands of the heathen, and they tooke the labours of the people in possession,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Titus 2:14; Ephesians 2:8–10"> That they might keepe his statutes, and obserue his Lawes. Prayse ye the Lord.</span>
