<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:4; Psalms 69:13–16; Psalms 59:1"> To him that excelleth. A Psalme of David on Michtam, concerning the dumme doue in a farre countrey, when the Philistims tooke him in Gath. Be mercifull vnto me, O God, for man would swallow me vp: he fighteth continually and vexeth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:10–12; Psalms 57:3; Psalms 92:8"> Mine enemies would dayly swallowe mee vp: for many fight against me, O thou most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 34:4; 1 Samuel 30:6; 2 Corinthians 7:5–6"> When I was afrayd, I trusted in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Psalms 118:6; Romans 8:31–39"> I will reioyce in God, because of his word, I trust in God, and will not feare what flesh can doe vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 11:54; Matthew 22:15; Isaiah 29:20–21"> Mine owne wordes grieue me dayly: all their thoughtes are against me to doe me hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 71:10; Psalms 59:3; Psalms 140:2"> They gather together, and keepe them selues close: they marke my steps, because they waite for my soule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 55:23; Psalms 94:20–21; Isaiah 28:15"> They thinke they shall escape by iniquitie: O God, cast these people downe in thine anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 20:5; Psalms 39:12; Psalms 126:5–6"> Thou hast counted my wandrings: put my teares into thy bottel: are they not in thy register?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Psalms 46:11; Psalms 118:11–13"> When I cry, then mine enemies shall turne backe: this I know, for God is with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; 2 Peter 1:4; Genesis 32:11"> I will reioyce in God because of his worde: in the Lord wil I reioyce because of his worde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; Isaiah 51:12–13; Isaiah 51:7–8"> In God doe I trust: I will not be afrayd what man can doe vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 9:1–3; 1 Samuel 1:24–28; Psalms 50:14"> Thy vowes are vpon me, O God: I will render prayses vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 116:8–9; Job 33:30; Revelation 21:23–24"> For thou hast deliuered my soule from death, and also my feete from falling, that I may walke before God in the light of the liuing.</span>
