<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 103:17; Psalms 94:4; Psalms 107:1"> To him that excelleth. A Psalme of Dauid to giue instruction. When Doeg the Edomite came and shewed Saul, and saide to him, Dauid is come to the house of Abimelech. Why boastest thou thy selfe in thy wickednesse, O man of power? the louing kindenesse of God indureth dayly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 18:21; Psalms 57:4; Jeremiah 9:3–4"> Thy tongue imagineth mischiefe, and is like a sharpe rasor, that cutteth deceitfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:3–5; 2 Timothy 3:4; John 8:44"> Thou doest loue euill more then good, and lies more then to speake the trueth. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 120:3; James 3:6–9; 1 Samuel 22:18–19"> Thou louest all wordes that may destroye, O deceitfull tongue!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 2:22; Psalms 27:13; Revelation 21:8"> So shall God destroy thee for euer: he shall take thee and plucke thee out of thy tabernacle, and roote thee out of ye land of the liuing. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 22:19; Psalms 40:3; Psalms 37:34"> The righteous also shall see it, and feare, and shall laugh at him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Psalms 73:18–20; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> Beholde the man that tooke not God for his strength, but trusted vnto the multitude of his riches, and put his strength in his malice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 92:12–14; Psalms 1:3; Hosea 14:6–8"> But I shall bee like a greene oliue tree in the house of God: for I trusted in the mercie of God for euer and euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 130:5–6; Lamentations 3:25–26; Psalms 54:6"> I will alway praise thee, for that thou hast done this, and I will hope in thy Name, because it is good before thy Saints.</span>
