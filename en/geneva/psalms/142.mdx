<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 30:8; Psalms 57:1; Psalms 141:1"> A Psalme of David, to give instruction, and a prayer, when he was in the cave. I cryed vnto the Lord with my voyce: with my voyce I prayed vnto the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 8:26; Isaiah 26:16; Psalms 62:8"> I powred out my meditation before him, and declared mine affliction in his presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 141:9; Psalms 140:5; Jeremiah 18:22"> Though my spirit was in perplexitie in me, yet thou knewest my path: in the way wherein I walked, haue they priuily layde a snare for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:17; Psalms 88:18; Psalms 31:11"> I looked vpon my right hand, and beheld, but there was none that would knowe me: all refuge failed me, and none cared for my soule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 16:5; Psalms 119:57; Lamentations 3:24"> Then cryed I vnto thee, O Lord, and sayde, thou art mine hope, and my portion in the land of the liuing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 79:8; Psalms 116:6; Psalms 136:23"> Hearken vnto my crye, for I am brought very lowe: deliuer me from my persecuters, for they are too strong for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 143:11; Psalms 146:7; Psalms 7:6–7"> Bring my soule out of prison, that I may prayse thy Name: then shall the righteous come about me, when thou art beneficiall vnto me.</span>
