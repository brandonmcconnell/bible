<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 81:15; Matthew 27:24; Judges 8:28"> For lo, thine enemies make a tumult: and they that hate thee, haue lifted vp the head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 64:2; Psalms 27:5; Psalms 31:20"> They haue taken craftie counsell against thy people, and haue consulted against thy secret ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:2; Jeremiah 11:19; Psalms 74:8"> They haue said, Come and let vs cut them off from being a nation: and let the name of Israel be no more in remembrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:2; Revelation 17:13; Isaiah 8:9–10"> For they haue consulted together in heart, and haue made a league against thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:10; 2 Chronicles 20:1; Psalms 137:7"> The tabernacles of Edom, and the Ishmaelites, Moab and the Agarims:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 13:5; Ezekiel 27:3; Ezekiel 27:9"> Gebal and Ammon, and Amalech, the Philistims with the inhabitants of Tyrus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:9; Genesis 25:3; 2 Kings 15:19"> Asshur also is ioyned with them: they haue bene an arme to the children of Lot. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 4:15–24; Isaiah 9:4; Judges 5:21"> Doe thou to them as vnto the Midianites: as to Sisera and as to Iabin at the riuer of Kishon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:17; Joshua 17:11; 1 Samuel 28:7"> They perished at En-dor, and were dung for the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 7:25; Judges 8:12–21"> Make them, euen their princes like Oreb and like Zeeb: yea, all their princes like Zebah and like Zalmuna.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:11; Psalms 83:4; Psalms 74:7–8"> Which haue said, Let vs take for our possession the habitations of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 21:18; Psalms 35:5; Jeremiah 13:24"> O my God, make them like vnto a wheele, and as the stubble before the winde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:22; Isaiah 9:18; Malachi 4:1"> As the fire burneth the forest, and as the flame setteth the mountaines on fire:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 9:17; Psalms 50:3; Psalms 58:9"> So persecute them with thy tempest, and make them afraide with thy storme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 9:19–20; Psalms 6:10; Psalms 109:29"> Fill their faces with shame, that they may seeke thy Name, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 40:14–15; Psalms 35:26"> Let them be confounded and troubled for euer: yea, let them be put to shame and perish,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 6:3; Psalms 59:13; Jeremiah 16:21"> That they may knowe that thou, which art called Iehouah, art alone, euen the most High ouer all the earth.</span>
