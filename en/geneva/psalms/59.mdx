<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:11–24; Psalms 18:48; Psalms 57:1"> To him that excelleth. Destroy not. A Psalme of David on Michtam. When Saul sent and they did watch the house to kill him. O my God, deliuer mee from mine enemies: defend me from them that rise vp against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 139:19; Psalms 26:9; Psalms 55:23"> Deliuer me from the wicked doers, and saue me from the bloody men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 56:6; Psalms 69:4; 1 Samuel 24:11"> For loe, they haue layd waite for my soule: the mightie men are gathered against me, not for mine offence, nor for my sinne, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 35:23; Isaiah 51:9; Proverbs 1:16"> They runne and prepare themselues without a fault on my part: arise therefore to assist me, and beholde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 84:8; Psalms 55:15; Psalms 9:15"> Euen thou, O Lord God of hostes, O God of Israel awake to visit all the heathen, and be not merciful vnto all that transgresse maliciously. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 59:14; Psalms 22:16; 1 Samuel 19:11"> They goe to and from in the euening: they barke like dogs, and goe about the citie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Proverbs 15:2; Psalms 10:11"> Behold, they brag in their talke, and swords are in their lips: for, Who, say they, doeth heare?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 1:26; Psalms 37:13; Psalms 2:4"> But thou, O Lord, shalt haue them in derision, and thou shalt laugh at all the heathen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 62:2; Psalms 27:14"> He is strong: but I will waite vpon thee: for God is my defence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 54:7; Psalms 59:17; 1 Peter 5:10"> My mercifull God will preuent me: God wil let me see my desire vpon mine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 3:3; Revelation 9:6; Job 40:12"> Slay them not, least my people forget it: but scatter them abroad by thy power, and put them downe, O Lord our shield,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 12:13; Proverbs 18:7; Psalms 10:7"> For the sinne of their mouth, and the words of their lips: and let them be taken in their pride, euen for their periurie and lies, that they speake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; Psalms 7:9; Ezekiel 39:7"> Consume them in thy wrath: consume them that they be no more: and let them knowe that God ruleth in Iaakob, euen vnto the ends of the world. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 22:16; Psalms 59:6"> And in the euening they shall go to and from, and barke like dogs, and go about the citie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 15:23; Psalms 109:10; Job 30:1–7"> They shall runne here and there for meate: and surely they shall not be satisfied, though they tary all night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 21:13; Ephesians 3:20; Romans 15:9"> But I wil sing of thy power, and will prayse thy mercy in the morning: for thou hast bene my defence and refuge in the day of my trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 59:9–10; Psalms 46:1; Psalms 18:1"> Vnto thee, O my Strength, wil I sing: for God is my defence, and my mercifull God.</span>
