<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; Deuteronomy 10:21; Psalms 83:1"> To him that excelleth. A Psalme of David. Holde not thy tongue, O God of my praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 52:4; Proverbs 6:17; Acts 6:13"> For the mouth of the wicked, and the mouth full of deceite are opened vpon me: they haue spoken to me with a lying tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:4; Psalms 35:7; Psalms 35:20"> They compassed me about also with words of hatred, and fought against me without a cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 38:20; Psalms 69:12–13; Psalms 35:7"> For my friendship they were mine aduersaries, but I gaue my selfe to praier.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 17:13; Genesis 44:4; Psalms 38:20"> And they haue rewarded me euil for good, and hatred for my friendship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 3:1; Matthew 5:25; Matthew 27:4"> Set thou the wicked ouer him, and let the aduersarie stand at his right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 28:9; Proverbs 21:27; Proverbs 15:8"> Whe he shalbe iudged, let him be condemned, and let his praier be turned into sinne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 55:23; Acts 1:16–26; Matthew 27:5"> Let his daies be fewe, and let another take his charge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 22:24; Jeremiah 18:21; Lamentations 5:3"> Let his children be fatherlesse, and his wife a widowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:25; Genesis 4:12–14; Isaiah 16:2"> Let his children be vagabonds and beg and seeke bread, comming out of their places destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 5:5; Job 20:18; Job 18:9–19"> Let the extortioner catch al that he hath, and let the strangers spoile his labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Psalms 137:8–9; James 2:13"> Let there be none to extend mercie vnto him: neither let there be any to shewe mercie vpon his fatherlesse children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Psalms 37:28; Job 18:19"> Let his posteritie be destroied, and in the generation following let their name be put out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Nehemiah 4:5; Jeremiah 18:23"> Let the iniquitie of his fathers bee had in remembrance with the Lord: and let not the sinne of his mother be done away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 34:16; Psalms 90:8; Job 18:17"> But let them alway be before the Lord, that he may cut off their memorial from ye earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:18; James 2:13; Psalms 37:14"> Because he remembred not to shew mercie, but persecuted the afflicted and poore man, and the sorowfull hearted to slay him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Proverbs 14:14; Ezekiel 35:6"> As he loued cursing, so shall it come vnto him, and as he loued not blessing, so shall it be farre from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 73:6; Numbers 5:22; Matthew 27:3–5"> As he clothed himselfe with cursing like a rayment, so shall it come into his bowels like water, and like oyle into his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 109:29; Psalms 132:18; Psalms 109:18"> Let it be vnto him as a garment to couer him, and for a girdle, wherewith he shalbe alway girded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:14; 1 Kings 2:44; Psalms 94:23"> Let this be the rewarde of mine aduersarie from the Lord, and of them, that speake euill against my soule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 31:3; Psalms 69:16; Psalms 79:9–10"> But thou, O Lord my God, deale with me according vnto thy Name: deliuer me, (for thy mercie is good)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:1; Psalms 40:17; Psalms 102:4"> Because I am poore and needie, and mine heart is wounded within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 10:19; 1 Chronicles 29:15; Psalms 102:10–11"> I depart like the shadowe that declineth, and am shaken off as the grashopper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 32:3–4; Hebrews 12:12; Matthew 4:2"> My knees are weake through fasting, and my flesh hath lost all fatnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 22:6–7; Psalms 69:19–20; Romans 15:3"> I became also a rebuke vnto them: they that looked vpon me, shaked their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:86; Psalms 57:1; Psalms 69:13"> Helpe me, O Lord my God: saue me according to thy mercie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 37:7; 1 Kings 18:36–37; Exodus 8:19"> And they shall know, that this is thine hand, and that thou, Lord, hast done it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:20; John 16:22; Psalms 109:17"> Though they curse, yet thou wilt blesse: they shall arise and be confounded, but thy seruant shall reioyce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 132:18; Psalms 35:26; Job 8:22"> Let mine aduersaries be clothed with shame, and let them couer themselues with their confusion, as with a cloke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 111:1; Psalms 35:18; Psalms 22:22–25"> I will giue thankes vnto the Lord greatly with my mouth and praise him among ye multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 121:5; Psalms 16:8; Psalms 140:12"> For he will stand at the right hand of the poore, to saue him from them that woulde condemne his soule.</span>
