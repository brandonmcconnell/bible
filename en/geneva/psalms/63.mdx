<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 143:6; Psalms 84:2; Psalms 42:1–2"> A Psalme of David. When he was in the wildernesse of Judah. O God, thou art my God, earely will I seeke thee: my soule thirsteth for thee: my flesh longeth greatly after thee in a barren and drye land without water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Psalms 105:4; Psalms 84:2–11"> Thus I beholde thee as in the Sanctuarie, when I beholde thy power and thy glorie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:16; Psalms 51:15; Philippians 1:23"> For thy louing kindnesse is better then life: therefore my lippes shall prayse thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 104:33; Psalms 134:2; Psalms 28:2"> Thus will I magnifie thee all my life, and lift vp mine hands in thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 36:7–9; Isaiah 25:6; Ezra 3:11–13"> My soule shalbe satisfied, as with marowe and fatnesse, and my mouth shall praise thee with ioyfull lippes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Psalms 119:147–148; Psalms 149:5"> When I remember thee on my bedde, and when I thinke vpon thee in the night watches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Psalms 61:4; Psalms 27:9"> Because thou hast bene mine helper, therefore vnder the shadow of thy wings wil I reioyce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 18:35; Psalms 73:23"> My soule cleaueth vnto thee: for thy right hand vpholdeth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 55:15; Psalms 9:17; Psalms 40:14"> Therefore they that seeke my soule to destroy it, they shall goe into the lowest partes of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; Ezekiel 35:5; Ezekiel 39:4"> They shall cast him downe with the edge of the sword, and they shall be a portion for foxes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Psalms 21:1; Isaiah 65:16"> But the King shall reioyce in God, and all that sweare by him shall reioyce in him: for the mouth of them that speake lyes, shall be stopped.</span>
