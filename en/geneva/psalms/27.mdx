<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Psalms 118:6; Exodus 15:2"> A Psalme of David. The Lord is my light and my saluation, whom shall I feare? the Lord is the strength of my life, of whome shall I be afraide?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:12; Psalms 14:4; Psalms 9:3"> When the wicked, euen mine enemies and my foes came vpon mee to eate vp my flesh; they stumbled and fell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 3:6; 1 Peter 3:14; Romans 8:35–37"> Though an hoste pitched against me, mine heart should not be afraide: though warre be raised against me, I will trust in this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; Psalms 23:6; Psalms 65:4"> One thing haue I desired of the Lord, that I will require, euen that I may dwell in the house of the Lord all the dayes of my life, to beholde the beautie of the Lord, and to visite his Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 31:20; Psalms 138:7"> For in the time of trouble hee shall hide mee in his Tabernacle: in the secrete place of his pauillion shall he hide me, and set me vp vpon a rocke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 3:3; Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 107:22"> And nowe shall hee lift vp mine head aboue mine enemies rounde about mee: therefore wil I offer in his Tabernacle sacrifices of ioy: I wil sing and praise the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 130:2–4; Psalms 13:3; Psalms 5:2"> Hearken vnto my voyce, O Lord, when I crie: haue mercie also vpon mee and heare mee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 105:4; Jeremiah 29:12–13; Isaiah 55:6–7"> When thou saidest, Seeke ye my face, mine heart answered vnto thee, O Lord, I will seeke thy face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 143:7; Psalms 102:2"> Hide not therefore thy face from mee, nor cast thy seruat away in displeasure: thou hast bene my succour: leaue me not, neither forsake mee, O God of my saluation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Isaiah 40:11; John 16:32"> Though my father and my mother shoulde forsake me, yet the Lord will gather me vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; Psalms 5:8; Psalms 86:11"> Teache mee thy way, O Lord, and leade me in a right path, because of mine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 31:8; Psalms 41:11; Matthew 26:59–60"> Giue me not vnto the lust of mine aduersaries: for there are false witnesses risen vp against me, and such as speake cruelly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 142:5; 2 Corinthians 4:16; 2 Corinthians 4:8–14"> I should haue fainted, except I had beleeued to see the goodnes of the Lord in the land of the liuing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 31:24; Isaiah 30:18"> Hope in the Lord: be strong, and he shall comfort thine heart, and trust in the Lord.</span>
