<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 17:23; Daniel 4:1–2; 1 Chronicles 29:10–12"> To him that excelleth on Neginoth. A Psalme or song committed to Asaph. God is knowen in Iudah: his Name is great in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 14:18; Isaiah 12:6; Psalms 132:13–14"> For in Shalem is his Tabernacle, and his dwelling in Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 46:9; Ezekiel 39:3–4; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> There brake he the arrowes of the bowe, the shielde and the sword and the battell. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1–4; Daniel 7:17–28; Ezekiel 19:6"> Thou art more bright and puissant, then the mountaines of pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nahum 3:18; Psalms 13:3; Isaiah 46:12"> The stout hearted are spoyled: they haue slept their sleepe, and all the men of strength haue not found their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Psalms 80:16; Exodus 15:21"> At thy rebuke, O God of Iaakob, both the chariot and horse are cast a sleepe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Psalms 2:12; Psalms 89:7"> Thou, euen thou art to be feared: and who shall stand in thy sight, when thou art angrie!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:29–30; Habakkuk 2:20; Judges 5:20"> Thou didest cause thy iudgement to bee heard from heauen: therefore the earth feared and was still,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:5; Zephaniah 2:3; Isaiah 11:4"> When thou, O God, arose to iudgement, to helpe all the meeke of the earth. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:17; Matthew 24:22; Daniel 3:19–20"> Surely the rage of man shall turne to thy praise: the remnant of the rage shalt thou restrayne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:4–6; Psalms 50:14; Psalms 68:29"> Vowe and performe vnto the Lord your God, all ye that be rounde about him: let them bring presents vnto him that ought to be feared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:6; 2 Chronicles 32:21; Psalms 68:12"> He shall cut off the spirit of princes: he is terrible to the Kings of the earth.</span>
