<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 119:154; Jeremiah 51:36"> A Psalme of Dauid. Pleade thou my cause, O Lord, with them that striue with me: fight thou against them, that fight against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 91:4; Exodus 15:3; Isaiah 42:13"> Lay hand vpon the shielde and buckler, and stand vp for mine helpe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 27:2; Psalms 51:12"> Bring out also the speare and stop the way against them, that persecute me: say vnto my soule, I am thy saluation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 70:2–3; Jeremiah 46:5; Psalms 35:26"> Let them be confounded and put to shame, that seeke after my soule: let them be turned backe, and brought to confusion, that imagine mine hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 21:18; Isaiah 29:5; Isaiah 37:36"> Let them be as chaffe before the winde, and let the Angel of the Lord scatter them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:12; Psalms 73:18; Jeremiah 13:16"> Let their way be darke and slipperie: and let the Angel of the Lord persecute them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:25; Psalms 140:5; Psalms 9:15"> For without cause they haue hid the pit and their net for me: without cause haue they digged a pit for my soule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 141:9–10; Proverbs 5:22; Luke 21:34"> Let destruction come vpon him at vnwares, and let his net, that he hath laid priuilie, take him: let him fall into the same destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Psalms 58:10–11; Luke 1:46–47"> Then my soule shalbe ioyfull in the Lord: it shall reioyce in his saluation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 140:12; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 51:8"> All my bones shall say, Lord, who is like vnto thee, which deliuerest the poore from him, that is too strong for him! yea, the poore and him that is in miserie, from him that spoyleth him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:12; Matthew 26:59–60; Acts 24:5–6"> Cruell witnesses did rise vp: they asked of me things that I knewe not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 10:32; Jeremiah 18:20; Psalms 38:20"> They rewarded me euill for good, to haue spoyled my soule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 69:10–11; Job 30:25; Matthew 5:44"> Yet I, when they were sicke, I was clothed with a sacke: I humbled my soule with fasting: and my praier was turned vpon my bosome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:41–42; 2 Samuel 1:11–12; Genesis 24:67"> I behaued my selfe as to my friend, or as to my brother: I humbled my selfe, mourning as one that bewaileth his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Psalms 7:2; Psalms 35:25–26"> But in mine aduersitie they reioyced, and gathered them selues together: the abiects assembled themselues against me, and knewe not: they tare me and ceased not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Job 16:9; Psalms 37:12"> With the false skoffers at bankets, gnashing their teeth against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:13; Psalms 22:20–21; Psalms 57:4"> Lord, how long wilt thou beholde this? deliuer my soule from their tumult, euen my desolate soule from the lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:12; Psalms 22:22–25; Psalms 116:18"> So will I giue thee thankes in a great Congregation: I will praise thee among much people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 69:4; John 15:25; Proverbs 6:13"> Let not them that are mine enemies, vniustly reioyce ouer mee, neyther let them winke with the eye, that hate mee without a cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 120:5–7; Matthew 12:19; Acts 23:15"> For they speake not as friendes: but they imagine deceitfull woordes against the quiet of the lande.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:13; Psalms 40:15; Psalms 70:3"> And they gaped on mee with their mouthes, saying, Aha, aha, our eye hath seene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 3:7; Psalms 10:1; Psalms 28:1"> Thou hast seene it, O Lord: keepe not silence: be not farre from me, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6; Isaiah 51:9"> Arise and wake to my iudgement, euen to my cause, my God, and my Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 26:1; Psalms 7:8"> Iudge me, O Lord my God, according to thy righteousnesse, and let them not reioyce ouer mee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Psalms 124:3; 2 Samuel 20:19"> Let them not say in their hearts, O our soule reioyce: neither let them say, We haue deuoured him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 38:16; Job 19:5; Psalms 132:18"> Let them bee confounded, and put to shame together, that reioyce at mine hurt: let them bee clothed with confusion and shame, that lift vp themselues against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:40–41; Psalms 149:4; Psalms 40:16"> But let them be ioyful and glad, that loue my righteousnesse: yea, let them say alway, Let the Lord be magnified, which loueth the prosperitie of his seruant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 145:21; Psalms 71:24; Psalms 145:5"> And my tongue shall vtter thy righteousnesse, and thy praise euery day.</span>
