<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 22:2; Luke 18:7"> A song or Psalme of Heman the Ezrahite to give instruction, committed to the sonnes of Korah for him that excelleth upon Malath Leannoth. O Lord God of my saluation, I cry day and night before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 31:2; Psalms 141:1–2; Lamentations 3:8"> Let my prayer enter into thy presence: incline thine eare vnto my cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 107:18; Lamentations 3:15–19; Mark 14:33–34"> For my soule is filled with euils, and my life draweth neere to the graue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; 2 Corinthians 1:9; Psalms 143:7"> I am counted among them that go downe vnto the pit, and am as a man without strength:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:8; Psalms 31:22; Isaiah 14:9–12"> Free among the dead, like the slaine lying in the graue, whome thou remembrest no more, and they are cut off from thine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:15; Psalms 86:13; Psalms 143:3"> Thou hast layde me in the lowest pit, in darkenes, and in the deepe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Job 6:4; Romans 2:5–9"> Thine indignation lyeth vpon me, and thou hast vexed me with all thy waues. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Isaiah 49:7; Jeremiah 32:2"> Thou hast put away mine acquaintance farre from me, and made mee to be abhorred of them: I am shut vp, and cannot get foorth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 11:13; Psalms 38:10; Psalms 86:3"> Mine eye is sorowfull through mine affliction: Lord, I call dayly vpon thee: I stretch out mine hands vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:5; Isaiah 26:19; Psalms 30:9"> Wilt thou shewe a miracle to the dead? or shall the dead rise and prayse thee? Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Proverbs 15:11; Psalms 73:18"> Shall thy louing kindenes be declared in the graue? or thy faithfulnes in destruction?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Isaiah 8:22"> Shall thy wonderous workes be knowen in the darke? and thy righteousnes in the land of obliuion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:3; Psalms 30:2; Mark 1:35"> But vnto thee haue I cryed, O Lord, and early shall my prayer come before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 13:24; Psalms 13:1; Psalms 44:24"> Lord, why doest thou reiect my soule, and hidest thy face from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:4; Luke 22:44; Job 7:11–16"> I am afflicted and at the point of death: from my youth I suffer thy terrours, doubting of my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 90:11; Isaiah 53:4–6; Romans 8:32"> Thine indignations goe ouer me, and thy feare hath cut me off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 22:16; Psalms 116:3; Psalms 69:1–2"> They came round about me dayly like water, and compassed me together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 88:8; Psalms 38:11; Job 19:12–15"> My louers and friends hast thou put away from me, and mine acquaintance hid themselues.</span>
