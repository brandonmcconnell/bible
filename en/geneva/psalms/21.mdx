<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Psalms 62:7; Psalms 28:7"> To him that excelleth. A Psalme of Dauid. The King shall reioyce in thy strength, O Lord: yea how greatly shall he reioyce in thy saluation!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Psalms 20:4–5; Psalms 2:8–9"> Thou hast giuen him his hearts desire, and hast not denyed him the request of his lips. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:30; Ephesians 1:3; Psalms 59:10"> For thou diddest preuent him with liberall blessings, and didest set a crowne of pure gold vpon his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 91:16; Revelation 1:18; Psalms 119:77"> He asked life of thee, and thou gauest him a long life for euer and euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; John 17:22; Revelation 5:8–13"> His glory is great in thy saluation: dignitie and honour hast thou laid vpon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 2:28; Psalms 45:7; Psalms 16:11"> For thou hast set him as blessings for euer: thou hast made him glad with the ioy of thy countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 16:8; Psalms 91:2; Psalms 20:7–8"> Because the King trusteth in the Lord, and in the mercie of the most High, he shall not slide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 10:10; Psalms 72:9; Psalms 110:1–2"> Thine hand shall finde out all thine enemies, and thy right hand shall finde out them that hate thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Psalms 50:3; Psalms 18:8"> Thou shalt make them like a fierie ouen in time of thine anger: the Lord shall destroy them in his wrath, and the fire shall deuoure them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:28; 1 Kings 13:34; Job 18:16–19"> Their fruite shalt thou destroy from the earth, and their seede from the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 10:2; Psalms 2:1; Psalms 31:13"> For they intended euill against thee, and imagined mischiefe, but they shall not preuaile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; Psalms 18:40; Psalms 64:7"> Therefore shalt thou put them aparte, and the strings of thy bowe shalt thou make readie against their faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 16:5–7; Revelation 19:1–6; Revelation 15:3–4"> Be thou exalted, O Lord, in thy strength: so will we sing and prayse thy power.</span>
