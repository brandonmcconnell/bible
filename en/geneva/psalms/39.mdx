<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Psalms 141:3; James 1:26"> To the excellent musician Ieduthun. I thought, I will take heede to my wayes, that I sinne not with my tongue: I will keepe my mouth brideled, while the wicked is in my sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:20; Psalms 38:13–14; Job 32:19–20"> I was dumme and spake nothing: I kept silece euen from good, and my sorow was more stirred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:32; Jeremiah 20:9; Ezekiel 3:14"> Mine heart was hote within me, and while I was musing, the fire kindeled, and I spake with my tongue, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 90:12; Psalms 103:14; Job 14:13"> Lord, let me know mine ende, and the measure of my dayes, what it is: let mee knowe howe long I haue to liue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 90:9–10; Psalms 144:4; Psalms 89:47"> Beholde, thou hast made my dayes as an hand breadth, and mine age as nothing in respect of thee: surely euery man in his best state is altogether vanitie. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:20–21; James 4:14; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> Doubtlesse man walketh in a shadowe, and disquieteth himselfe in vaine: he heapeth vp riches, and cannot tell who shall gather them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Psalms 38:15; Psalms 119:81"> And now Lord, what wait I for? mine hope is euen in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 44:13; Psalms 65:3; Joel 2:17"> Deliuer me from all my transgressions, and make me not a rebuke vnto the foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 2:10; 2 Samuel 16:10; Leviticus 10:3"> I should haue bene dumme, and not haue opened my mouth, because thou didest it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:34; Job 13:21; Psalms 25:16–17"> Take thy plague away from mee: for I am consumed by the stroke of thine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:28; 1 Corinthians 11:30–32; Isaiah 50:9"> When thou with rebukes doest chastise man for iniquitie, thou as a mothe makest his beautie to consume: surely euery man is vanitie. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Hebrews 11:13; 2 Kings 20:5"> Heare my prayer, O Lord, and hearken vnto my cry: keepe not silence at my teares, for I am a strager with thee, and a soiourner as all my fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 10:20–21; Job 14:10–12; Job 14:5–6"> Stay thine anger from me, that I may recouer my strength, before I go hence and be not.</span>
