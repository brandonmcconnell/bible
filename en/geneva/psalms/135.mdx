<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 78:55; Psalms 136:21–22; Joshua 11:23"> And gaue their lande for an inheritance, euen an inheritance vnto Israel his people.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; Jonah 4:2; Psalms 50:4"> For the Lord will iudge his people, and be pacified towardes his seruants.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 13:14–16; Isaiah 6:10"> They haue a mouth, and speake not: they haue eyes and see not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> They haue eares and heare not, neither is there any breath in their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 115:8; Isaiah 44:18–20; Jeremiah 10:8"> They that make them, are like vnto them: so are all that trust in them.</span>
