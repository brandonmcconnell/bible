<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 78:3–6; Exodus 13:14–15; Deuteronomy 6:20"> To him that excelleth. A Psalme to give instruction, committed to the sonnes of Korah. We haue heard with our eares, O God: our fathers haue tolde vs the workes, that thou hast done in their dayes, in the olde time:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 78:55; Exodus 15:17; Joshua 11:23"> Howe thou hast driuen out the heathen with thine hand, and planted them: how thou hast destroyed the people, and caused them to grow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Joshua 24:12"> For they inherited not the lande by their owne sworde, neither did their owne arme saue them: but thy right hand, and thine arme and the light of thy countenance, because thou didest fauour them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 74:12; Psalms 42:8; Psalms 149:2"> Thou art my King, O God: send helpe vnto Iaakob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 108:13; Psalms 60:12; Psalms 118:10–13"> Through thee haue we thrust backe our aduersaries: by thy Name haue we troden downe them that rose vp against vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 20:7; Hosea 1:7; 1 Samuel 17:47"> For I do not trust in my bowe, neither can my sworde saue me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Psalms 132:18; Psalms 136:24"> But thou hast saued vs from our aduersaries, and hast put them to confusion that hate vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 34:2; 1 Corinthians 1:29–31; Jeremiah 9:24"> Therefore will wee praise God continually, and will confesse thy Name for euer. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; Psalms 60:10; Psalms 60:1"> But now thou art farre off, and puttest vs to confusion, and goest not forth with our armies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 7:8; Leviticus 26:17; Joshua 7:12"> Thou makest vs to turne backe from the aduersary, and they, which hate vs, spoile for theselues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:27; Deuteronomy 28:64; Romans 8:36"> Thou giuest vs as sheepe to bee eaten, and doest scatter vs among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:13; Deuteronomy 32:30; Isaiah 52:3–4"> Thou sellest thy people without gaine, and doest not increase their price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Psalms 80:6; Psalms 89:41"> Thou makest vs a reproche to our neighbours, a iest and a laughing stocke to them that are round about vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; 2 Kings 19:21; Job 16:4"> Thou makest vs a prouerbe among the nations, and a nodding of the head among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 69:7; Jeremiah 3:25; Psalms 89:45"> My confusion is dayly before me, and the shame of my face hath couered me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 8:2; Psalms 74:10; Psalms 74:18"> For the voyce of the slaunderer and rebuker, for the enemie and auenger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 9:13; Deuteronomy 8:14; Deuteronomy 6:12"> All this is come vpon vs, yet doe wee not forget thee, neither deale wee falsly concerning thy couenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:51; Psalms 119:157; Psalms 78:57"> Our heart is not turned backe: neither our steps gone out of thy paths,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 3:5; Psalms 23:4; Jeremiah 14:17"> Albeit thou hast smitten vs downe into the place of dragons, and couered vs with the shadow of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 81:9; Job 11:13; Deuteronomy 6:14"> If wee haue forgotten the Name of our God, and holden vp our hands to a strange god,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Jeremiah 23:24; Hebrews 4:12–13"> Shall not God searche this out? for hee knoweth the secrets of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 8:36; Isaiah 53:7; Psalms 44:11"> Surely for thy sake are we slaine continually, and are counted as sheepe for the slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 78:65; Psalms 7:6; Psalms 35:23"> Vp, why sleepest thou, O Lord? awake, be not farre off for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 13:24; Deuteronomy 32:20; Psalms 42:9"> Wherefore hidest thou thy face? and forgettest our miserie and our affliction?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 119:25; Lamentations 4:5; Isaiah 51:23"> For our soule is beaten downe vnto the dust: our belly cleaueth vnto the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 130:7–8; Psalms 25:22; Psalms 26:11"> Rise vp for our succour, and redeeme vs for thy mercies sake.</span>
