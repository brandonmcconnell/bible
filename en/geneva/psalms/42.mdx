<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 63:1–2; Psalms 143:6–7; Psalms 119:131"> To him that excelleth. A Psalme to give instruction, committed to the sonnes of Korah. As the harte brayeth for the riuers of water, so panteth my soule after thee, O God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 63:1; Psalms 84:10; John 7:37"> My soule thirsteth for God, euen for the liuing God: when shall I come and appeare before the presence of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 79:10; Psalms 115:2; Joel 2:17"> My teares haue bin my meate day and night, while they dayly say vnto me, Where is thy God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 30:29; Psalms 122:1; 1 Samuel 1:15–16"> When I remembred these things, I powred out my very heart, because I had gone with the multitude, and ledde them into the House of God with the voyce of singing, and prayse, as a multitude that keepeth a feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Hebrews 10:36–37; Psalms 61:2"> Why art thou cast downe, my soule, and vnquiet within me? waite on God: for I will yet giue him thankes for the helpe of his presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 61:2; Jonah 2:7; Deuteronomy 3:8–9"> My God, my soule is cast downe within me, because I remember thee, from the land of Iorden, and Hermonim, and from the mount Mizar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jonah 2:3; Psalms 69:14–15; Psalms 88:7"> One deepe calleth another deepe by the noyse of thy water spoutes: all thy waues and thy floods are gone ouer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 63:6; Psalms 16:7; Psalms 149:5"> The Lord will graunt his louing kindenesse in the day, and in the night shall I sing of him, euen a prayer vnto the God of my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 38:6; Psalms 43:2; Psalms 18:2"> I wil say vnto God, which is my rocke, Why hast thou forgotten mee? why goe I mourning, when the enemie oppresseth me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Joel 2:17; Luke 2:35"> My bones are cut asunder, while mine enemies reproch me, saying dayly vnto me, Where is thy God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 42:5; Jeremiah 33:6; Psalms 43:5"> Why art thou cast downe, my soule? and why art thou disquieted within mee? waite on God: for I wil yet giue him thankes: he is my present helpe, and my God.</span>
