<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:18; Psalms 105:1; 1 Chronicles 16:34"> Praise ye the Lord. Praise ye the Lord because he is good, for his mercie endureth for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Ephesians 1:19; Psalms 145:3–12"> Who can expresse the noble actes of the Lord, or shewe forth all his prayse?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 15:2; James 1:25; John 15:14"> Blessed are they that keepe iudgement, and doe righteousnesse at all times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:132; Luke 23:42; Nehemiah 13:14"> Remember me, O Lord, with the fauour of thy people: visite me with thy saluation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:16; Psalms 105:43; Psalms 1:3"> That I may see the felicitie of thy chosen, and reioyce in the ioy of thy people, and glorie with thine inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:16; Psalms 78:8; 1 Kings 8:47"> We haue sinned with our fathers: we haue committed iniquitie, and done wickedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 14:11–12; Psalms 78:42; Psalms 78:11"> Our fathers vnderstoode not thy wonders in Egypt, neither remembred they the multitude of thy mercies, but rebelled at the Sea, euen at the red sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Ezekiel 20:9; Ezekiel 20:14"> Neuerthelesse he saued them for his Names sake, that he might make his power to be knowen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 18:15; Isaiah 51:10; Isaiah 63:11–14"> And he rebuked the red Sea, and it was dryed vp, and he led them in the deepe, as in the wildernesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 14:30; Psalms 107:2; Micah 6:4"> And he saued them from ye aduersaries hand, and deliuered them from ye hand of the enemie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 15:5; Psalms 78:53; Exodus 14:13"> And the waters couered their oppressours: not one of them was left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 14:31–21; Luke 8:13; John 8:30–31"> Then beleeued they his wordes, and sang prayse vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 78:11; Exodus 15:24; Isaiah 48:17–18"> But incontinently they forgate his workes: they wayted not for his counsell,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Numbers 11:4; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> But lusted with concupiscence in the wildernes, and tempted God in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 11:31–34; Isaiah 10:16; Psalms 78:29–31"> Then he gaue them their desire: but he sent leannesse into their soule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 16:1–50; Leviticus 21:10–12; Leviticus 21:6–8"> They enuied Moses also in the tentes, and Aaron the holy one of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:6; Numbers 16:29–33; Numbers 26:10"> Therefore the earth opened and swallowed vp Dathan, and couered the companie of Abiram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Numbers 16:35–40; Numbers 16:46"> And the fire was kindled in their assembly: the flame burnt vp the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 32:4–8; Exodus 32:35; Nehemiah 9:18"> They made a calfe in Horeb, and worshipped the molten image.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:11; Exodus 20:4–5; Romans 1:22–23"> Thus they turned their glory into the similitude of a bullocke, that eateth grasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:32; Psalms 78:42–51; Psalms 106:13"> They forgate God their Sauiour, which had done great things in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 105:23; Psalms 78:51; Exodus 14:25–28"> Wonderous woorkes in the lande of Ham, and fearefull things by the red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:13–14; Deuteronomy 9:25; Ezekiel 22:30"> Therefore he minded to destroy them, had not Moses his chosen stand in the breach before him to turne away his wrath, least he shoulde destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:32; Ezekiel 20:6; Hebrews 3:18–19"> Also they contemned that pleasant land, and beleeued not his worde,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 14:22; Deuteronomy 1:26–27; Hebrews 3:15"> But murmured in their tentes, and hearkened not vnto the voice of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:15; Numbers 14:28–35; Hebrews 3:11"> Therefore hee lifted vp his hande against them, to destroy them in the wildernesse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 44:11; Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:64–65"> And to destroy their seede among the nations, and to scatter them throughout the countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hosea 9:10; Numbers 25:1–3; Numbers 25:5"> They ioyned themselues also vnto Baalpeor, and did eate the offrings of the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16–21; 1 Corinthians 10:8; Romans 1:21–24"> Thus they prouoked him vnto anger with their owne inuentions, and the plague brake in vpon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 25:6–8; Joshua 7:12; Numbers 25:14–15"> But Phinehas stoode vp, and executed iudgement, and the plague was staied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 25:11–13; Mark 14:3–9; Deuteronomy 24:13"> And it was imputed vnto him for righteousnes from generation to generation for euer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 20:2–13; Numbers 20:23–24; Deuteronomy 4:21"> They angred him also at the waters of Meribah, so that Moses was punished for their sakes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 20:10–11; Psalms 107:11; James 3:2"> Because they vexed his spirite, so that hee spake vnaduisedly with his lippes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Deuteronomy 7:16; Judges 1:21"> Neither destroied they the people, as the Lord had commanded them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Judges 3:5–6; 1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> But were mingled among the heathen, and learned their workes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:16; Judges 2:3; Exodus 23:33"> And serued their idoles, which were their ruine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Kings 17:17; 2 Kings 16:3; Deuteronomy 32:17"> Yea, they offered their sonnes, and their daughters vnto deuils,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; Deuteronomy 21:9; Isaiah 26:21"> And shed innocent blood, euen the blood of their sonnes, and of their daughters, whome they offred vnto the idoles of Canaan, and the lande was defiled with blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:18; Numbers 15:39; Leviticus 17:7"> Thus were they steined with their owne woorkes, and went a whoring with their owne inuentions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Deuteronomy 9:29; Judges 2:20"> Therefore was the wrath of the Lord kindled against his people, and he abhorred his owne inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Nehemiah 9:27–38; Judges 3:8"> And hee gaue them into the hande of the heathen: and they that hated them, were lordes ouer them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Judges 4:3; Judges 10:12"> Their enemies also oppressed them, and they were humbled vnder their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 81:12; Judges 2:16–18; Psalms 1:1"> Many a time did hee deliuer them, but they prouoked him by their counsels: therefore they were brought downe by their iniquitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Judges 3:9; Judges 4:3; 1 Samuel 7:8–12"> Yet hee sawe when they were in affliction, and he heard their crie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 105:8; Lamentations 3:32; Judges 2:18"> And he remembred his couenant towarde them and repented according to the multitude of his mercies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezra 9:9; Jeremiah 42:12; Jeremiah 15:11"> And gaue them fauour in the sight of all them that lead them captiues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:35–36; Revelation 7:10–12; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> Saue vs, O Lord our God, and gather vs from among the heathen, that we may praise thine holy Name, and glorie in thy praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 41:13; Psalms 89:52; Psalms 105:45–1"> Blessed be the Lord God of Israel for euer and euer, and let all the people say, So be it. Praise yee the Lord.</span>
