<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 3:4; Psalms 142:1–3; Psalms 50:1"> For the excellent musician Ieduthun. A Psalme committed to Asaph. My voyce came to God, when I cryed: my voyce came to God, and he heard me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Genesis 37:35; Isaiah 26:9"> In the day of my trouble I sought ye Lord: my sore ranne and ceased not in the night: my soule refused comfort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 61:2; Psalms 142:2–3; Job 31:23"> I did thinke vpon God, and was troubled: I praied, and my spirit was full of anguish. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 7:13–15; Psalms 6:6; Job 2:13"> Thou keepest mine eyes waking: I was astonied and could not speake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7; Isaiah 51:9; Psalms 143:5"> Then I considered the daies of olde, and the yeeres of ancient time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Psalms 4:4; Psalms 139:23–24"> I called to remembrance my song in the night: I communed with mine owne heart, and my spirit searched diligently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 85:1; Lamentations 3:31–32; Psalms 85:5"> Will the Lord absent him selfe for euer? and will he shewe no more fauour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 9:6; 2 Peter 3:9"> Is his mercie cleane gone for euer? doeth his promise faile for euermore?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 40:11; Isaiah 49:14–15; Isaiah 63:15"> Hath God forgotten to be mercifull? hath he shut vp his teder mercies in displeasure? Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 42:3; Mark 9:24; Exodus 15:6"> And I sayde, This is my death: yet I remembred the yeeres of the right hand of the most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 143:5; Psalms 105:5; 1 Chronicles 16:12"> I remembred the workes of the Lord: certainely I remembred thy wonders of olde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 71:24; Psalms 145:11; Deuteronomy 6:7"> I did also meditate all thy woorkes, and did deuise of thine actes, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 73:17; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 89:6–8"> Thy way, O God, is in the Sanctuarie: who is so great a God as our God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:27; Psalms 136:4; Exodus 15:11"> Thou art ye God that doest wonders: thou hast declared thy power among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 9:29; Deuteronomy 9:26"> Thou hast redeemed thy people with thine arme, euen the sonnes of Iaakob and Ioseph. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Habakkuk 3:15; Joshua 3:15–16"> The waters sawe thee, O God: the waters sawe thee, and were afraide: yea, the depths trembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; 2 Samuel 22:15; Habakkuk 3:11"> The cloudes powred out water: the heauens gaue a sounde: yea, thine arrowes went abroade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 97:4; 2 Samuel 22:14; Revelation 11:19"> The voyce of thy thunder was rounde about: the lightnings lightened the worlde: the earth trembled and shooke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; Psalms 29:10; Nahum 1:3–4"> Thy way is in the Sea, and thy paths in the great waters, and thy footesteps are not knowen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Psalms 78:52; Exodus 14:19"> Thou diddest leade thy people like sheepe by the hand of Moses and Aaron.</span>
