<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 143:7; Psalms 18:2; Proverbs 1:12"> A Psalme of David. Unto thee, O Lord, doe I crie: O my strength, be not deafe toward mee, lest, if thou answere me not, I be like them that goe downe into the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 138:2; Psalms 141:2; Psalms 140:6"> Heare the voyce of my petitions, when I crie vnto thee, when I holde vp mine handes towarde thine holy Oracle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 55:21; Psalms 12:2; Jeremiah 9:8–9"> Drawe mee not away with the wicked, and with the woorkers of iniquitie: which speake friendly to their neighbours, when malice is in their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 22:12; 2 Timothy 4:14; Revelation 18:6"> Reward them according to their deedes, and according to the wickednes of their inuentions: recompense them after the woorke of their handes: render them their reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:12; Romans 1:28; John 12:37"> For they regarde not the woorkes of the Lord, nor the operation of his handes: therefore breake them downe, and builde them not vp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 31:21–22; Psalms 107:19–22; Psalms 118:5"> Praised be the Lord, for he hath heard the voyce of my petitions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 30:11–12; Psalms 56:3–4; Isaiah 61:10"> The Lord is my strength and my shielde: mine heart trusted in him, and I was helped: therfore mine heart shall reioyce, and with my song will I praise him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 20:6; Psalms 140:7; Isaiah 61:1"> The Lord is their strength, and he is the strength of the deliuerances of his anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; Psalms 78:71; Deuteronomy 9:29"> Saue thy people, and blesse thine inheritance: feede them also, and exalt them for euer.</span>
