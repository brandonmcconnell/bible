<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 22:20; Daniel 3:16–18; Acts 5:40"> And Saul consented to his death, and at that time, there was a great persecution against the Church which was at Hierusalem, and they were all scattered abroad thorowe the regions of Iudea and of Samaria, except the Apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 50:10–11; Genesis 23:2; Jeremiah 22:18"> Then certaine men fearing God, caried Steuen amongs them, to be buried, and made great lamentation for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Galatians 1:13; 1 Corinthians 15:9"> But Saul made hauocke of the Church, and entred into euery house, and drewe out both men and women, and put them into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Acts 11:19; Acts 8:1"> Therefore they that were scattered abroad, went to and from preaching the worde.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 6:5; Acts 21:8; Acts 8:35–36"> Then came Philip into the citie of Samaria, and preached Christ vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 4:41–42; 2 Chronicles 30:12; Matthew 20:15–16"> And the people gaue heed vnto those things which Philippe spake, with one accorde, hearing and seeing the miracles which he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:1; Acts 14:8–10; Mark 16:17–18"> For vncleane spirits crying with a loud voyce, came out of many that were possessed of them: and many taken with palsies, and that halted, were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:52; Acts 13:48; Acts 8:39"> And there was great ioy in that citie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 13:6; Acts 5:36; John 7:18"> And there was before in the citie a certaine man called Simon, which vsed witchcraft, and bewitched the people of Samaria, saying that he himselfe was some great man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; 2 Peter 2:2; Revelation 13:3"> To whome they gaue heede from the least to the greatest, saying, This man is that great power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 8:9; Galatians 3:1; Isaiah 44:25"> And they gaue heed vnto him, because that of long time he had bewitched them with sorceries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 1:3; Matthew 28:19"> But assoone as they beleeued Philip, which preached the thinges that concerned the kingdome of God, and the Name of Iesus Christ, they were baptized both men and women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:6–7; John 5:20; Acts 19:11"> Then Simon himselfe beleeued also and was baptized, and continued with Philippe, and wondred, when he sawe the signes and great miracles which were done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; Galatians 2:9"> Nowe when the Apostles, which were at Hierusalem, heard say, that Samaria had receiued the worde of God, they sent vnto them Peter and Iohn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:38; John 16:23–24; John 14:13–14"> Which whe they were come downe, prayed for them, that they might receiue the holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 19:2; Matthew 28:19"> (For as yet, hee was fallen downe on none of them, but they were baptized onely in the Name of the Lord Iesus.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 6:6; 2 Timothy 1:6"> Then layd they their handes on them, and they receiued the holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:5; Matthew 10:8; 2 Kings 8:9"> And when Simon sawe, that through laying on of the Apostles hands the holy Ghost was giuen, he offred them money,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; 3 John 1:9; John 5:44"> Saying, Giue mee also this power, that on whomsoeuer I lay the handes, he may receiue the holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 5:17; Matthew 10:8; Acts 2:38"> Then saide Peter vnto him, Thy money perish with thee, because thou thinkest that the gift of God may be obteined with money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Psalms 36:1; Matthew 6:22–24"> Thou hast neither part nor fellowship in this businesse: for thine heart is not right in the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:25–26; Daniel 4:27; Hebrews 4:12"> Repent therefore of this thy wickednes, and pray God, that if it be possible, the thought of thine heart may be forgiuen thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 58:6; Proverbs 5:22; John 8:34"> For I see that thou art in the gall of bitternes, and in the bonde of iniquitie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Numbers 21:7; Job 42:8"> Then answered Simon, and said, Pray ye to the Lord for me, that none of these things which ye haue spoken, come vpon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Acts 26:22–23; Acts 1:8"> So they, when they had testified and preached the worde of the Lord, returned to Hierusalem, and preached the Gospel in many townes of the Samaritans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 1:3"> Then the Angel of the Lord spake vnto Philip, saying, Arise, and goe towarde the South vnto the way that goeth downe from Hierusalem vnto Gaza, which is waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; Psalms 68:31; Isaiah 56:3–8"> And hee arose and went on: and beholde, a certaine Eunuche of Ethiopia, Candaces the Queene of the Ethiopians chiefe Gouernour, who had the rule of all her treasure, and came to Hierusalem to worship:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Isaiah 1:1; Acts 28:25"> And as he returned sitting in his charet, he read Esaias the Prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:19; Acts 11:12; Acts 20:22–23"> Then the Spirit said vnto Philip, Goe neere and ioyne thy selfe to yonder charet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 24:44–45; John 5:39; Ephesians 5:17"> And Philip ranne thither, and heard him reade the Prophet Esaias, and said, But vnderstandest thou what thou readest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Psalms 73:16–17; Isaiah 29:18–19"> And he saide, Howe can I, except I had a guide? And he desired Philip, that he would come vp and sit with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7–8; 1 Peter 2:21–24; Jeremiah 51:40"> Nowe the place of the Scripture which he read, was this, Hee was lead as a sheepe to the slaughter: and like a lambe domme before his shearer, so opened he not his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Isaiah 53:8; Zechariah 13:7"> In his humilitie his iudgement hath bene exalted: but who shall declare his generation? for his life is taken from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Matthew 2:2–4"> Then the Eunuche answered Philippe, and saide, I pray thee of whome speaketh the Prophet this? of himselfe, or of some other man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Acts 18:28; Acts 28:23"> Then Philip opened his mouth, and began at the same Scripture, and preached vnto him Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 10:47; John 3:5; Titus 3:5–6"> And as they went on their way, they came vnto a certaine water, and the Eunuche said, See, here is water: what doeth let me to be baptized?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And Philippe said vnto him, If thou beleeuest with all thine heart, thou mayest. Then he answered, and saide, I beleeue that that Iesus Christ is that Sonne of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:1; John 3:22–23"> Then he commanded the charet to stand stil: and they went downe both into the water, both Philip and the Eunuche, and he baptized him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 18:12; 2 Kings 2:16; Ezekiel 8:3"> And assoone as they were come vp out of the water, the Spirit of the Lord caught away Philip, that the Eunuche sawe him no more: so he went on his way reioycing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 21:8; Acts 23:23; Acts 23:33"> But Philippe was found at Azotus, and he walked to and from preaching in all the cities, till he came to Cesarea.</span>
