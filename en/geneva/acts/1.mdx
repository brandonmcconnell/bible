<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 1:3; Luke 24:19; Matthew 4:23–24"> I have made the former treatise, O Theophilus, of al that Jesus began to doe and teach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 1:9; John 20:21; Isaiah 59:20–21"> Vntill the day that hee was taken vp, after that hee through the holy Ghost, had giuen commandements vnto the Apostles, whome hee had chosen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5–7; Acts 13:31; John 20:26"> To whome also he presented himselfe aliue after that he had suffered, by many infallible tokens, being seene of them by the space of fourtie daies, and speaking of those thinges which appertaine to the kingdome of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 24:49; John 14:16; John 15:26"> And when he had gathered them together, he commanded them, that they should not depart from Hierusalem, but to waite for the promise of the Father, which sayde hee, yee haue heard of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Acts 11:15–16; Luke 3:16"> For Iohn in deede baptized with water, but ye shall be baptized with the holy Ghost within these fewe daies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 19:11; Luke 17:20; Daniel 7:27"> When they therefore were come together, they asked of him, saying, Lord, wilt thou at this time restore the kingdome to Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–2; Mark 13:32"> And hee saide vnto them, It is not for you to know the times, or the seasons, which the Father hath put in his owne power,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; Mark 16:15; Romans 15:19"> But yee shall receiue power of the holy Ghost, when he shall come on you: and ye shalbe witnesses vnto me both in Hierusalem and in all Iudea, and in Samaria, and vnto the vttermost part of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Luke 24:50–51; Mark 16:19"> And when he had spoken these things, while they behelde, he was taken vp: for a cloude tooke him vp out of their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 24:4; John 20:12; Revelation 7:14"> And while they looked stedfastly towarde heauen, as hee went, beholde, two men stoode by them in white apparell,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; Mark 13:26"> Which also sayde, Yee men of Galile, why stande yee gasing into heauen? This Iesus which is taken vp from you into heauen, shall so come, as yee haue seene him goe into heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:52; Matthew 21:1; Luke 24:50"> Then returned they vnto Hierusalem from the mount that is called the mount of Oliues, which is neere to Hierusalem, being from it a Sabbath daies iourney.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 20:8; Luke 22:12; Mark 14:15"> And when they were come in, they went vp into an vpper chamber, where abode both Peter and Iames, and Iohn, and Andrew, Philip, and Thomas, Bartlemew, and Matthewe, Iames the sonne of Alpheus, and Simon Zelotes, and Iudas Iames brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 6:4; Ephesians 6:18; Colossians 4:2"> These all continued with one accorde in prayer and supplication with the women, and Marie the mother of Iesus, and with his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:6; Acts 21:20; Revelation 3:4"> And in those dayes Peter stoode vp in the middes of the disciples, and sayde (nowe the nomber of names that were in one place were about an hundreth and twentie.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 13:18; Psalms 55:12–15"> Yee men and brethren, this scripture must needes haue beene fulfilled, which the holy Ghost by the mouth of Dauid spake before of Iudas, which was guide to them that tooke Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 1:25; Mark 3:19; John 17:12"> For hee was nombred with vs, and had obteined fellowship in this ministration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:3–10; Matthew 26:14–15; Psalms 55:23"> He therefore hath purchased a field with the reward of iniquitie: and when he had throwen downe himselfe headlong, hee brast asunder in the middes, and all his bowels gushed out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 2:22; Matthew 28:15; Acts 21:40"> And it is knowen vnto all the inhabitants of Hierusalem, in so much, that that field is called in their owne language, Aceldama, That is, the field of blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 69:25; Psalms 109:8–15; Acts 1:25"> For it is written in the booke of Psalmes, Let his habitation be void, and let no man dwel therein: also, Let another take his charge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 15:27; Luke 10:1–2; Numbers 27:17"> Wherefore of these men which haue companied with vs, all the time that the Lord Iesus was conuersant among vs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 1:2; Acts 4:33; Acts 1:8–9"> Beginning from the baptisme of Iohn vnto the day that hee was taken vp from vs, must one of them bee made a witnesse with vs of his resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 1:26"> And they presented two, Ioseph called Barsabas, whose surname was Iustus, and Matthias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Acts 15:8; Hebrews 4:13"> And they praied, saying, Thou Lord, which knowest the hearts of all men, shewe whether of these two thou hast chosen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:2; John 6:70–71; John 17:12"> That he may take the roume of this ministration and Apostleship, from which Iudas hath gone astray, to goe to his owne place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 16:8; Joshua 18:10; 1 Samuel 14:41–42"> Then they gaue foorth their lottes: and the lotte fell on Matthias, and hee was by a common consent counted with the eleuen Apostles.</span>
