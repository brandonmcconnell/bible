<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 18:23–28; 1 Corinthians 16:12; 1 Corinthians 1:12"> And it came to passe, while Apollos was at Corinthus, that Paul when he passed thorow the vpper coasts, came to Ephesus, and found certaine disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 7:39; Acts 11:15–17; Galatians 3:5"> And saide vnto them, Haue ye receiued the holy Ghost since ye beleeued? And they saide vnto him, Wee haue not so much as heard whether there be an holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:25; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Acts 8:16"> And he said vnto them, Vnto what were ye then baptized? And they saide, Vnto Iohns baptisme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 1:29–34; Acts 11:16; Acts 1:5"> Then saide Paul, Iohn verely baptized with the baptisme of repentance, saying vnto the people, that they shoulde beleeue in him, which should come after him, that is, in Christ Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:12; Acts 8:16; Romans 6:3–4"> And when they heard it, they were baptized in the Name of the Lord Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 6:6; Acts 2:4; Mark 16:17"> So Paul layde his handes vpon them, and the holy Ghost came on them, and they spake the tongues, and prophecied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And all the men were about twelue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 28:23; Acts 1:3; Acts 9:20–22"> Moreouer he went into the Synagogue, and spake boldly for the space of three moneths, disputing and exhorting to the things that appertaine to the kingdome of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 19:23; Acts 9:2; Acts 18:6–8"> But when certaine were hardened, and disobeyed, speaking euill of the way of God before the multitude, hee departed from them, and separated the disciples, and disputed dayly in the schoole of one Tyrannus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 20:31; 2 Timothy 1:15; Acts 16:6"> And this was done by the space of two yeeres, so that all they which dwelt in Asia, heard the word of ye Lord Iesus, both Iewes and Grecians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 16:17–20; Acts 5:12; Romans 15:18–19"> And God wrought no small miracles by the handes of Paul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 5:15; 2 Kings 13:20–21; Mark 16:17"> So that from his body were brought vnto the sicke, kerchefs or handkerchefs, and the diseases departed from them, and the euill spirits went out of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 12:27; Mark 9:38; Luke 11:19"> Then certaine of the vagabond Iewes, exorcistes, tooke in hand to name ouer them which had euil spirits, the Name of the Lord Iesus, saying, We adiure you by Iesus, whom Paul preacheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> (And there were certaine sonnes of Sceua a Iewe, the Priest, about seuen which did this)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 16:17–18; Luke 4:33–35; Mark 1:34"> And the euil spirit answered, and said, Iesus I acknowledge, and Paul I know: but who are ye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 8:29; Mark 5:3–4; Mark 5:15"> And the man in whome the euil spirit was, ranne on them, and ouercame them, and preuailed against them, so that they fledde out of that house, naked, and wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:12; Acts 5:5; Philippians 2:9–11"> And this was knowen to all the Iewes and Grecians also, which dwelt at Ephesus, and feare came on them all, and the Name of the Lord Iesus was magnified,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 10:10; Psalms 32:5; Matthew 3:6"> And many that beleeued, came and confessed, and shewed their workes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 13:8; Exodus 32:20; Genesis 35:4"> Many also of them which vsed curious artes, brought their bookes, and burned them before all men: and they counted the price of them, and found it fiftie thousand pieces of siluer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 12:24; Acts 6:7; Isaiah 55:11"> So the worde of God grewe mightily, and preuailed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:22; Acts 20:16; Acts 23:11"> Nowe when these things were accomplished, Paul purposed by the Spirite to passe through Macedonia and Achaia, and to goe to Hierusalem, saying, After I haue bene there, I must also see Rome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 16:23; 2 Timothy 4:20; Acts 16:3"> So sent hee into Macedonia two of them that ministred vnto him, Timotheus and Erastus, but he remained in Asia for a season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 19:9; Acts 24:22; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> And the same time there arose no small trouble about that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Acts 16:16; Acts 16:19"> For a certaine man named Demetrius a siluersmith, which made siluer temples of Diana, brought great gaines vnto the craftesmen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:19; Hosea 12:7–8; Revelation 18:11–19"> Whom he called together, with the workemen of like things, and saide, Syrs, ye knowe that by this craft we haue our goods:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 17:29; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 44:10–20"> Moreouer ye see and heare, that not alone at Ephesus, but almost throughout all Asia this Paul hath perswaded, and turned away much people, saying, That they be not gods which are made with handes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:11; 1 Timothy 6:5; Matthew 23:13"> So that not onely this thing is dangerous vnto vs, that this our portion shall be reproued, but also that the temple of the great goddesse Diana should be nothing esteemed, and that it would come to passe that her magnificence, which all Asia and the world worshippeth, should be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 13:4; Revelation 17:13; Acts 16:19–24"> Now when they heard it, they were full of wrath, and cried out, saying, Great is Diana of the Ephesians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Colossians 4:10; Acts 27:2"> And the whole citie was full of confusion, and they rushed into the common place with one assent, and caught Gaius, and Aristarchus, men of Macedonia, and Pauls companions of his iourney.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 21:39; 2 Samuel 18:2–3; Acts 17:22–31"> And when Paul would haue entred in vnto the people, the disciples suffred him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:10; Acts 21:12; Acts 16:6"> Certaine also of the chiefe of Asia which were his friendes, sent vnto him, desiring him that hee woulde not present him selfe in the Common place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 21:34; Acts 19:40; Matthew 11:7–9"> Some therefore cried one thing, and some another: for the assemblie was out of order, and the more part knewe not wherefore they were come together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 12:17; 2 Timothy 4:14; 1 Timothy 1:20"> And some of the company drew foorth Alexander, the Iewes thrusting him forwards. Alexander then beckened with the hande, and woulde haue excused the matter to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 19:28; Matthew 6:7; Revelation 13:4"> But when they knew that he was a Iewe, there arose a shoute almost for the space of two houres, of all men crying, Great is Diana of the Ephesians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:10–11; 1 Timothy 4:2; Ephesians 2:12"> Then the towne clearke when hee had stayed the people, saide, Ye men of Ephesus, what man is it that knoweth not howe that the citie of the Ephesians is a worshipper of the great goddesse Diana, and of the image, which came downe from Iupiter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Acts 5:35–39; Proverbs 25:8"> Seeing then that no man can speake against these things, ye ought to be appeased, and to doe nothing rashly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; Romans 2:22; Acts 25:8"> For yee haue brought hither these men, which haue neither committed sacrilege, neither doe blaspheme your goddesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 13:7; 1 Corinthians 6:1; Acts 18:14"> Wherefore, if Demetrius and the craftes men which are with him, haue a matter against any man, the lawe is open, and there are Deputies: let them accuse one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> But if ye inquire any thing cocerning other matters, it may be determined in a lawful assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 17:5–8; Matthew 26:5; Acts 20:1"> For we are euen in ieopardie to be accused of this dayes sedition, for as much as there is no cause, whereby we may giue a reason of this concourse of people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:17; Psalms 65:7; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> And when he had thus spoken, hee let the assembly depart.</span>
