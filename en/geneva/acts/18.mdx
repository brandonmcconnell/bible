<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:2; 2 Timothy 4:20; 2 Corinthians 1:1"> After these thinges, Paul departed from Athens, and came to Corinthus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 16:3–4; 1 Corinthians 16:19; 2 Timothy 4:19"> And found a certaine Iewe named Aquila, borne in Pontus, lately come from Italie, and his wife Priscilla (because that Claudius had commanded all Iewes to depart from Rome) and he came vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:12; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; 2 Corinthians 11:9"> And because hee was of the same crafte, he abode with them and wrought (for their crafte was to make tentes.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:11; Acts 17:17; Acts 28:23"> And he disputed in the Synagogue euery Sabbath day, and exhorted the Iewes, and the Grecians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:14; Acts 18:28; Acts 20:21"> Now when Silas and Timotheus were come from Macedonia, Paul, forced in spirit, testified to the Iewes that Iesus was the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:4; 2 Samuel 1:16; Ezekiel 33:8–9"> And when they resisted and blasphemed, he shooke his raiment, and saide vnto them, Your blood be vpon your owne head: I am cleane: from henceforth will I goe vnto the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 16:14; Colossians 4:11; Acts 10:2"> So he departed thence, and entred into a certaine mans house, named Iustus, a worshipper of God, whose house ioyned hard to the Synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:13–17; Romans 10:14–17; Mark 16:15–16"> And Crispus the chiefe ruler of the Synagogue, beleeued in the Lord with all his housholde: and many of the Corinthians hearing it, beleeued and were baptized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Ephesians 6:19–20; Acts 27:23–25"> Then saide the Lord to Paul in the night by a vision, Feare not, but speake, and holde not thy peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9; Exodus 4:12; Isaiah 41:10"> For I am with thee, and no man shall lay handes on thee to hurt thee: for I haue much people in this citie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 14:3; Acts 19:10; Acts 20:31"> So he continued there a yeere and six moneths, and taught ye worde of God among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 13:50; Acts 18:27; Acts 13:7"> Now when Gallio was Deputie of Achaia, the Iewes arose with one accorde against Paul, and brought him to the iudgement seate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 25:8; Acts 24:5–6; Acts 6:13"> Saying, This fellow persuadeth me to worship God otherwise then the Lawe appointeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14–15; Acts 25:11; Mark 9:19"> And as Paul was about to open his mouth, Gallio saide vnto the Iewes, If it were a matter of wrong, or an euill deede, O ye Iewes, I would according to reason maintaine you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 23:29; Acts 25:19; 1 Timothy 6:4"> But if it bee a question of woordes, and names, and of your Lawe, looke yee to it your selues: for I will be no iudge of those things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 12:16; Psalms 76:10; Romans 13:3–4"> And hee draue them from the iudgement seate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:1; Amos 6:6; Acts 17:32"> Then tooke al the Grecians Sosthenes the chiefe ruler of the Synagogue, and beat him before the iudgement seat: but Gallio cared nothing for those things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:24; Numbers 6:18; Numbers 6:5–9"> But when Paul had taried there yet a good while, hee tooke leaue of the brethren, and sailed into Syria (and with him Priscilla and Aquila) after that he had shorne his head in Cenchrea: for he had a vowe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 1:1; 1 Timothy 1:3; 2 Timothy 1:18"> Then hee came to Ephesus, and left them there: but hee entred into the Synagogue and disputed with the Iewes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 1:37–38; 1 Corinthians 16:12; Acts 20:16"> Who desired him to tarie a longer time with them: but he would not consent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:19; James 4:15; Romans 15:32"> But bade the farewel, saying, I must needes keepe this feast that commeth, in Hierusalem: but I will returne againe vnto you, if God will. So he sailed from Ephesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 8:40; Acts 15:4; Acts 23:23"> And when hee came downe to Cesarea, he went vp to Hierusalem: and when he had saluted the Church, he went downe vnto Antiochia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 14:22; Acts 16:6; Acts 15:41"> Nowe when he had taried there a while, he departed, and went thorowe the countrey of Galatia and Phrygia by order, strengthening all the disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:6; Titus 3:13; 1 Corinthians 3:5–6"> And a certaine Iewe named Apollos, borne at Alexandria, came to Ephesus, an eloquent man, and mightie in the Scriptures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 19:3; Romans 12:11; Hosea 14:9"> The same was instructed in the way of the Lord, and hee spake feruently in the Spirite, and taught diligently the things of the Lord, and knew but the baptisme of Iohn onely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 18:25; Acts 14:3; 1 Corinthians 12:21"> And he began to speake boldely in the Synagogue. Whom when Aquila and Priscilla had heard, they tooke him vnto them, and expounded vnto him the way of God more perfectly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:13–14; James 1:16–18; 1 Peter 1:2–3"> And when hee was minded to goe into Achaia, the brethren exhorting him, wrote to the disciples to receiue him: and after hee was come thither, he holpe them much which had beleeued through grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 18:5; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4; Acts 9:22"> For mightily hee confuted publikely the Iewes, with great vehemencie, shewing by the Scriptures, that Iesus was that Christ.</span>
</div>
