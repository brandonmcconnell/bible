<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:2; Acts 28:17; Acts 13:26"> Ye men, brethren and Fathers, heare my defence nowe towards you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 21:40"> (And when they heard that he spake in the Hebrewe tongue to them, they kept the more silence, and he sayd)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 26:5; Philippians 3:5–6; Acts 9:11"> I am verely a man, which am a Iew, borne in Tarsus in Cilicia, but brought vp in this citie at the feete of Gamaliel, and instructed according to the perfect maner of the Lawe of the Fathers, and was zealous toward God, as ye all are this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 22:19–20; 1 Timothy 1:13–15; Acts 26:9–11"> And I persecuted this way vnto the death, binding and deliuering into prison both men and women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 9:1–2; Luke 22:66; Acts 5:21"> As also ye chiefe Priest doeth beare me witnes, and al the company of the Elders: of whom also I receiued letters vnto the brethren, and went to Damascus to bring them which were there, bound vnto Hierusalem, that they might be punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 9:3–8; Acts 26:12–18; Revelation 1:16"> And so it was, as I iourneyed and was come neere vnto Damascus about noone, that suddenly there shone from heauen a great light round about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:9; Matthew 25:45; Genesis 22:11"> So I fell vnto the earth, and heard a voyce, saying vnto me, Saul, Saul, why persecutest thou mee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 26:9; 1 Samuel 8:7; Exodus 16:7–8"> Then I answered, Who art thou, Lord? And he said to me, I am Iesus of Nazareth, whom thou persecutest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 9:7; Daniel 10:7; Acts 26:13"> Moreouer they that were with me, sawe in deede a light and were afraide: but they heard not the voyce of him that spake vnto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 16:30; Acts 22:12–16; Acts 2:37"> Then I sayd, What shall I doe, Lord? And the Lord sayde vnto me, Arise, and goe into Damascus: and there it shall be tolde thee of all things, which are appointed for thee to doe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 9:8–9; Acts 13:11; Isaiah 42:16"> So when I could not see for the glory of that light, I was led by the hand of them that were with me, and came into Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:22; Acts 6:3; 3 John 1:12"> And one Ananias a godly man, as perteining to the Lawe, hauing good report of all the Iewes which dwelt there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 9:17–18; Philemon 1:16"> Came vnto me, and stoode, and sayd vnto me, Brother Saul, receiue thy sight: and that same houre I looked vpon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 15:8; 1 Corinthians 9:1"> And he sayd, The God of our fathers hath appointed thee, that thou shouldest knowe his wil, and shouldest see that Iust one, and shouldest heare the voyce of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Acts 4:20; John 15:27"> For thou shalt be his witnes vnto all men, of the things which thou hast seene and heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 2:21; Titus 3:5"> Now therefore why tariest thou? Arise, and be baptized, and wash away thy sinnes, in calling on the Name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:1–4; Acts 10:9–10; Acts 9:26–28"> And it came to passe, that when I was come againe to Hierusalem, and prayed in the Temple, I was in a traunce,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 9:29; Luke 21:21; Ezekiel 3:6–7"> And saw him saying vnto me, Make haste, and get thee quickly out of Hierusalem: for they will not receiue thy witnes concerning me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 22:4; Matthew 10:17; Acts 8:3"> Then I sayd, Lord, they know that I prisoned, and beat in euery Synagogue them that beleeued in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 7:58; Acts 8:1; Romans 1:32"> And when the blood of thy martyr Steuen was shed, I also stood by, and consented vnto his death, and kept the clothes of them that slew him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Timothy 2:7; Ephesians 3:6–8"> Then he sayd vnto me, Depart: for I will send thee farre hence vnto the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 25:24; Acts 21:36; John 19:15"> And they heard him vnto this worde, but then they lift vp their voyces, and sayd, Away with such a fellow from the earth: for it is not meete that he should liue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:13; Acts 26:11; Ecclesiastes 10:3"> And as they cried and cast off their clothes, and threw dust into the aire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 21:31–32; Acts 21:34; John 19:1"> The chiefe captaine commanded him to be led into the castle, and bade that he should be scourged, and examined, that he might knowe wherefore they cryed so on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:37; Acts 25:16; Acts 27:43"> And as they bound him with thongs, Paul sayd vnto the Centurion that stood by, Is it lawfull for you to scourge one that is a Romane, and not condemned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 23:27; Acts 22:29"> Nowe when the Centurion heard it, hee went, and tolde the chiefe captaine, saying, Take heede what thou doest: for this man is a Romane.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Then the chiefe captaine came, and sayd to him, Tel me, art thou a Romane? And he said, Yea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the chiefe captaine answered, With a great summe obtained I this freedome. Then Paul sayd, But I was so borne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 21:33; Acts 22:24–26; Acts 16:38–39"> Then straightway they departed from him, which should haue examined him: and the chiefe captaine also was afrayd, after he knewe that hee was a Romane, and that he had bound him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 23:28; Acts 21:33; Acts 21:11"> On the next day, because hee would haue knowen the certaintie wherefore he was accused of the Iewes, he loosed him from his bonds, and commanded the hie Priests and all their Councill to come together: and he brought Paul, and set him before them.</span>
