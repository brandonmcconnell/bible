<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:5; 2 Timothy 1:2; 1 Corinthians 4:17"> Then came he to Derbe and to Lystra: and beholde, a certaine disciple was there named Timotheus, a womans sonne, which was a Iewesse and beleeued, but his father was a Grecian,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; 2 Timothy 3:15; Acts 14:21"> Of whom the brethren which were at Lystra and Iconium, reported well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 9:20; Galatians 5:6"> Therefore Paul would that he should go forth with him, and tooke and circumcised him, because of ye Iewes, which were in those quarters: for they knewe all, that his father was a Grecian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 15:28–29; Acts 15:2; Acts 11:30"> And as they went through the cities, they deliuered them the decrees to keepe, ordeined of the Apostles and Elders, which were at Hierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 9:31; Acts 2:47; Acts 6:7"> And so were the Churches stablished in the faith, and encreased in number daily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 18:23; Acts 16:7; Acts 13:2–4"> Nowe when they had gone throughout Phrygia, and the region of Galatia, they were forbidden of the holy Ghost to preache the worde in Asia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:9; Philippians 1:19; Galatians 4:6"> Then came they to Mysia, and sought to go into Bithynia: but the Spirit suffered them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12; 2 Timothy 4:13; Acts 16:11"> Therefore they passed through Mysia, and came downe to Troas,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 9:10–12; Acts 19:21; Acts 18:5"> Where a vision appeared to Paul in the night. There stoode a man of Macedonia, and prayed him, saying, Come into Macedonia, and helpe vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12–13; Acts 14:7; Acts 16:10–17"> And after he had seene the vision, immediatly we prepared to goe into Macedonia, being assured that the Lord had called vs to preache the Gospel vnto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 21:1; Acts 16:8"> Then went we forth from Troas, and with a straight course came to Samothracia, and the next day to Neapolis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:2; Philippians 1:1; Acts 20:6"> And from thence to Philippi, which is the chiefe citie in ye partes of Macedonia, and whose inhabitants came from Rome to dwell there, and we were in that citie abiding certaine dayes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 13:14; Colossians 1:23; Acts 17:2"> And on the Sabbath day, we went out of the citie, besides a Riuer, where they were wont to pray: and we sate downe, and spake vnto the women, which were come together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; Luke 24:45; Isaiah 50:5"> And a certaine woman named Lydia, a seller of purple, of the citie of the Thyatirians, which worshipped God, heard vs: whose heart the Lord opened, that she attended vnto the things, which Paul spake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 11:14; Hebrews 13:2; Acts 16:33"> And when she was baptized, and her houshold, she besought vs, saying, If ye haue iudged me to be faithfull to ye Lord, come into mine house, and abide there: and she constrained vs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Deuteronomy 18:9–11; Isaiah 8:19"> And it came to passe that as we went to prayer, a certaine maide hauing a spirit of diuination, mette vs, which gate her masters much vantage with diuining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 14:6; 1 Peter 2:16; Mark 5:7"> She followed Paul and vs, and cryed, saying, These men are the seruants of the most high God, which shewe vnto you the way of saluation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Luke 10:17–19; Acts 3:6"> And this did she many dayes: but Paul being grieued, turned about, and said to the spirit, I commaund thee in the Name of Iesus Christ, that thou come out of her. And he came out the same houre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:6; Acts 21:30; Acts 8:3"> Nowe when her masters sawe that the hope of their gaine was gone, they caught Paul and Silas, and drewe them into the market place vnto the Magistrates,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 4:4; John 15:18–20; Acts 28:22"> And brought them to the gouernours, saying, These men which are Iewes, trouble our citie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esther 3:8; Acts 26:3; Jeremiah 10:3"> And preache ordinances, which are not lawfull for vs to receiue, neither to obserue, seeing we are Romanes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:2; 2 Corinthians 6:5; 2 Corinthians 11:23–25"> The people also rose vp together against them, and the gouernours rent their clothes, and commanded them to be beaten with roddes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 2:10; 2 Timothy 2:9; 1 Samuel 23:22–23"> And when they had beaten them sore, they cast them into prison, commaunding the Iayler to keepe them surely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 13:27; Job 33:11; Jeremiah 29:26"> Who hauing receiued such commandement, cast them into the inner prison, and made their feete fast in the stockes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:16–18; Psalms 119:62; James 1:2"> Nowe at midnight Paul and Silas prayed, and sung Psalmes vnto God: and the prisoners heard them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 4:31; Acts 5:19; Acts 12:10"> And suddenly there was a great earthquake, so that the foundation of the prison was shaken: and by and by all the doores opened, and euery mans bands were loosed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 12:19; Matthew 27:5; Judges 9:54"> Then the keeper of the prison waked out of his sleepe, and when he sawe the prison doores open, he drewe out his sword and would haue killed himselfe, supposing the prisoners had bin fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:15; Luke 23:34; Luke 6:27–28"> But Paul cryed with a loude voyce, saying, Doe thy selfe no harme: for we are all here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Acts 24:25; Isaiah 60:14"> Then he called for a light, and leaped in, and came trembling, and fell downe before Paul and Silas,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 2:37; John 6:27–29; Isaiah 58:9"> And brought them out, and said, Syrs, what must I doe to be saued?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:12; Acts 2:38–39; John 3:36"> And they saide, Beleeue in the Lord Iesus Christ, and thou shalt be saued, and thine houshold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 1:16; Colossians 1:27–28; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> And they preached vnto him the worde of the Lord, and to all that were in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:16; Acts 16:15; Acts 16:25"> Afterwarde he tooke them the same houre of the night, and washed their stripes, and was baptized with all that belonged vnto him, straigthway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Acts 2:46; Romans 5:11"> And when he had brought them into his house, he set meate before them, and reioyced that he with all his houshold beleeued in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:22; Psalms 76:10; Acts 5:40"> And when it was day, the gouernours sent the sergeants, saying, Let those men goe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 15:33; John 14:27; 1 Samuel 1:17"> Then the keeper of the prison tolde these woordes vnto Paul, saying, The gouerness haue sent to loose you: nowe therefore get you hence, and goe in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 22:25–29; Psalms 58:1–2; Matthew 10:16"> Then sayde Paul vnto them, After that they haue beaten vs openly vncodemned, which are Romanes, they haue cast vs into prison, and nowe would they put vs out priuily? nay verely: but let them come and bring vs out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 22:29; Matthew 21:46; Matthew 14:5"> And the sergeants tolde these woordes vnto the gouernours, who feared whe they heard that they were Romanes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 8:34; Mark 5:17; Daniel 6:23"> Then came they and praied them, and brought them out, and desired them to depart out of the citie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:2–3; Acts 16:14; Acts 14:22"> And they went out of the prison, and entred into the house of Lydia: and when they had seene the brethren, they comforted them, and departed.</span>
