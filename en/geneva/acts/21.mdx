<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:17; Luke 5:4; 1 Samuel 20:41–42"> And as we launched forth, and were departed from them, we came with a straight course vnto Coos, and the day following vnto the Rhodes, and from thence vnto Patara.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jonah 1:3; Acts 11:19; Acts 15:3"> And we found a ship that went ouer vnto Phenice, and went aboard, and set forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 21:16; Acts 4:36; Matthew 4:24"> And whe we had discouered Cyprus, we left it on the left hand, and sailed toward Syria, and arriued at Tyrus: for there the ship vnladed ye burden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 20:22–23; Acts 21:10–12; Acts 20:6–7"> And when we had found disciples, we taried there seuen dayes. And they told Paul through the Spirit, that he should not goe vp to Hierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 20:36; Acts 9:40; Psalms 95:6"> But when the dayes were ended, we departed and went our way, and they all accompanied vs with their wiues and children, euen out of the citie: and we kneeling downe on the shore, prayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 19:27; John 1:11; 2 Corinthians 2:13"> Then when we had embraced one another, we tooke ship, and they returned home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:24; 1 Samuel 10:4; Acts 28:12"> And when we had ended the course from Tyrus, we arriued at Ptolemais, and saluted the brethren, and abode with them one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 6:5; Acts 8:26–40; Ephesians 4:11"> And the next day, Paul and we that were with him, departed, and came vnto Cesarea: and we entred into the house of Philippe the Euangelist, which was one of the seuen Deacons, and abode with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 2:17; Revelation 2:20; Joel 2:28"> Now he had foure daughters virgins, which did prophecie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 11:28; Acts 21:4; Acts 20:16"> And as we taried there many dayes, there came a certaine Prophet from Iudea, named Agabus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 21:33; Jeremiah 13:1–11; 1 Samuel 15:27–28"> And when he was come vnto vs, he tooke Pauls girdle, and bound his owne hands and feete, and sayd, Thus sayth the holy Ghost, So shall the Iewes at Hierusalem binde the man that oweth this girdle, and shall deliuer him into the hands of the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 16:21–23; Acts 20:22; Acts 21:4"> And when we had heard these things, both we and other of the same place besought him that he would not go vp to Hierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 20:24; Philippians 2:17; Philippians 1:20–21"> Then Paul answered, and sayd, What doe ye weeping and breaking mine heart? For I am ready not to be bound onely, but also to die at Hierusalem for the Name of the Lord Iesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 22:42; Matthew 26:42; Matthew 26:39"> So when he would not be perswaded, we ceased, saying, The will of the Lord be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 25:1; Acts 25:6; Acts 25:9"> And after those dayes we trussed vp our fardels, and went vp to Hierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 16:31; Acts 21:8; Psalms 92:14"> There went with vs also certaine of the disciples of Cesarea, and brought with them one Mnason of Cyprus, an olde disciple, with whome we should lodge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 15:4; Romans 15:7; Hebrews 13:1–2"> And when we were come to Hierusalem, the brethren receiued vs gladly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 1:19; Galatians 2:9; Acts 15:13"> And the next day Paul went in with vs vnto Iames: and all the Elders were there assembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 1:17; Romans 15:18–19"> And when he had embraced them, hee tolde by order all things, that God had wrought among the Gentiles by his ministration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 10:2; Galatians 1:14; Acts 22:3"> So when they heard it, they glorified God, and sayd vnto him, Thou seest, brother, how many thousand Iewes there are which beleeue, and they are all zealous of the Law:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 6:12–15; 1 Corinthians 9:19–21; Galatians 5:1–6"> Now they are informed of thee, that thou teachest all the Iewes, which are among the Gentiles, to forsake Moses, and sayest that they ought not to circumcise their sonnes, neither to liue after the customes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 15:12; Acts 15:22; Acts 19:32"> What is then to be done? the multitude must needes come together: for they shall heare that thou art come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 18:18; Numbers 6:2–7"> Doe therefore this that we say to thee. We haue foure men, which haue made a vowe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 21:26; Acts 24:18; Acts 18:18"> Them take, and purifie thy selfe with them, and contribute with them, that they may shaue their heads: and all shall knowe, that those things, whereof they haue bene informed concerning thee, are nothing, but that thou thy selfe also walkest and keepest the Lawe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 15:29; Acts 15:19–20"> For as touching ye Gentiles, which beleeue, we haue written, and determined that they obserue no such thing, but that they keepe themselues from things offred to idoles, and from blood, and from that that is strangled, and from fornication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 24:18; Acts 21:24; Numbers 6:13–20"> Then Paul tooke the men, and the next day was purified with them, and entred into the Temple, declaring the accomplishment of the dayes of the purification, vntill that an offering should be offered for euery one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 26:21; Acts 24:18; Acts 13:50"> And when the seuen dayes were almost ended, the Iewes which were of Asia (when they sawe him in the Temple) moued all the people, and laide hands on him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 24:5–6; Acts 21:21; Acts 26:20–21"> Crying, Men of Israel, helpe: this is the man that teacheth all men euery where against the people, and the Lawe, and this place: moreouer, he hath brought Grecians into the Temple, and hath polluted this holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 20:4; 2 Timothy 4:20; Acts 18:19"> For they had seene before Trophimus an Ephesian with him in the citie, whom they supposed that Paul had brought into the Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 26:21; 2 Corinthians 11:26; Acts 16:19–22"> Then all the citie was moued, and the people ran together: and they tooke Paul and drewe him out of the Temple, and forth with the doores were shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:23–33; Acts 17:5; Acts 26:9–10"> But as they went about to kill him, tydings came vnto the chiefe captaine of the band, that all Hierusalem was on an vproare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 23:27; Isaiah 3:15; Acts 5:40"> Who immediately tooke souldiers and Centurions, and ran downe vnto them: and when they sawe the chiefe Captaine and the souldiers, they left beating of Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; Acts 21:11; Acts 20:23"> Then the chiefe Captaine came neere and tooke him, and commanded him to be bound with two chaines, and demaunded who he was, and what he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 19:32; Acts 23:10; Acts 22:24"> And one cryed this, another that, among the people. So when he could not know the certeintie for the tumult, he commanded him to be led into the castell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:2–3; Psalms 55:9; Psalms 58:2"> And when hee came vnto the grieces, it was so that he was borne of the souldiers, for the violence of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 19:15; Acts 22:22; Luke 23:18"> For the multitude of the people followed after, crying, Away with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 21:34; Luke 21:15; Acts 19:30"> And as Paul should haue bene led into the castell, he sayd vnto the chiefe Captaine, May I speake vnto thee? Who sayd, Canst thou speake Greeke?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 24:26; Acts 5:36–37; Matthew 5:11"> Art not thou the Egyptian who before these dayes raised a sedition, and led out into the wildernesse foure thousande men that were murtherers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 22:3; Acts 9:11; Acts 23:27"> Then Paul sayde, Doubtlesse, I am a man which am a Iewe, and citizen of Tarsus, a famous citie of Cilicia, and I beseech thee, suffer mee to speake vnto the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Acts 22:2; John 5:2"> And when he had giuen him licence, Paul stoode on the grieces, and beckened with the hand vnto the people: and when there was made great silence, hee spake vnto them in the Hebrewe tongue, saying,</span>
