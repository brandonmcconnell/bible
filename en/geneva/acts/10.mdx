<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 27:1; Matthew 8:5–13; Matthew 27:27"> Futhermore there was a certaine man in Cesarea called Cornelius, a captaine of the band called the Italian band,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; Acts 10:4; Acts 10:22"> A deuoute man, and one that feared God with all his housholde, which gaue much almes to the people, and prayed God continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 9:20–21; Acts 3:1; Acts 5:19"> He sawe in a vision euidently (about the ninth houre of the day) an Angel of God comming in to him, and saying vnto him, Cornelius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; Hebrews 6:10; Acts 10:31"> But when hee looked on him, hee was afrayd, and sayd, What is it, Lord? and he sayd vnto him, Thy prayers and thine almes are come vp into remembrance before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 16:9; Acts 9:38; Acts 15:7"> Nowe therefore send men to Ioppa, and call for Simon, whose surname is Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 9:43; Acts 11:13–14; Romans 10:14–17"> Hee lodgeth with one Simon a Tanner, whose house is by the sea side: he shall tell thee what thou oughtest to doe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philemon 1:16; 1 Samuel 14:6–7; Matthew 8:9–10"> And when the Angel which spake vnto Cornelius, was departed, he called two of his seruants, and a souldier that feared God, one of them that waited on him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:36; Psalms 119:59–60; Acts 10:33"> And tolde them all things, and sent them to Ioppa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:5; Psalms 55:17; 1 Samuel 9:25"> On the morow as they went on their iourney, and drew neere vnto the citie, Peter went vp vpon the house to pray, about the sixt houre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 22:17; Revelation 4:2–3; 2 Corinthians 12:2–4"> Then waxed hee an hungred, and would haue eaten: but while they made some thing ready, he fell into a trance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; John 1:51; Romans 16:25–26"> And hee sawe heauen opened, and a certaine vessell come downe vnto him, as it had bene a great sheete, knit at the foure corners, and was let downe to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 7:8–9; John 7:37; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11"> Wherein were all maner of foure footed beastes of the earth, and wilde beastes and creeping things, and foules of the heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 4:31–34; Jeremiah 35:2–5; Acts 10:10"> And there came a voyce to him, Arise, Peter: kill, and eate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:25; Ezekiel 4:14; Daniel 1:8"> But Peter sayd, Not so, Lord: for I haue neuer eaten any thing that is polluted, or vncleane.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:25; Matthew 15:11; Titus 1:15"> And the voyce spake vnto him againe the second time, The things that God hath purified, pollute thou not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 41:32; John 21:17; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> This was so done thrise: and the vessell was drawen vp againe into heauen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 13:12; Acts 10:7–19; Acts 5:24"> Nowe while Peter douted in himselfe what this vision which he had seene, meant, beholde, the men which were sent from Cornelius, had inquired for Simons house, and stoode at the gate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 10:5–6; Acts 11:11"> And called, and asked, whether Simon, which was surnamed Peter, were lodged there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 8:29; Acts 11:12; Acts 21:4"> And while Peter thought on the vision, the Spirit sayde vnto him, Beholde, three men seeke thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 15:7–9; Zechariah 2:9–11; Isaiah 48:16"> Arise therefore, and get thee downe, and goe with them, and doute nothing: For I haue sent them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 1:38–39; Mark 10:51; Acts 10:29"> Then Peter went downe to the men, which were sent vnto him from Cornelius, and sayd, Beholde, I am he whome ye seeke: what is the cause wherefore ye are come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 11:14; Acts 10:2; Acts 22:12"> And they sayd, Cornelius the captaine, a iust man, and one that feareth God, and of good report among all the nation of the Iewes, was warned from heauen by an holy Angel, to send for thee into his house, and to heare thy wordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 10:45; Acts 11:12; 1 Peter 4:9"> Then called he them in, and lodged them, and the next day, Peter went foorth with them, and certaine brethren from Ioppa accompanied him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 8:40; Zechariah 8:20–23; Zechariah 3:10"> And the day after, they entred into Cesarea. Nowe Cornelius waited for them, and had called together his kinsemen, and special friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Revelation 22:8–9; Daniel 2:46"> And it came to passe as Peter came in, that Cornelius met him, and fell downe at his feete, and worshipped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Revelation 22:8–9; Acts 14:14–15"> But Peter tooke him vp, saying, Stand vp: for euen I my selfe am a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12; John 4:35; Acts 10:24"> And as he talked with him, he came in, and found many that were come together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 4:9; Ephesians 3:6–7; Galatians 2:12–14"> And he sayd vnto them, Ye know that it is an vnlawfull thing for a man that is a Iewe, to company, or come vnto one of another nation: but God hath shewed me, that I should not call any man polluted, or vncleane.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; 1 Peter 3:15; Acts 10:19–20"> Therefore came I vnto you without saying nay, when I was sent for. I aske therefore, for what intent haue ye sent for me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 10:3; Daniel 9:20–21; Nehemiah 9:1–3"> Then Cornelius sayd, Foure dayes agoe, about this houre, I fasted, and at the ninth houre I prayed in mine house, and beholde, a man stood before me in bright clothing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 10:4; Daniel 9:23; Luke 1:13"> And sayd, Cornelius, thy prayer is heard, and thine almes are had in remembrance in the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Send therefore to Ioppa, and call for Simon, whose surname is Peter (he is lodged in the house of Simon a Tanner by the sea side) who when he commeth, shall speake vnto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 1:19; Galatians 4:14; 2 Chronicles 30:12"> Then sent I for thee immediately, and thou hast well done to come. Nowe therefore are we all here present before God, to heare all things that are commanded thee of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 2:11; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Deuteronomy 10:17"> Then Peter opened his mouth, and sayd, Of a trueth I perceiue, that God is no accepter of persons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:29; Acts 10:2; Romans 10:12–13"> But in euery nation he that feareth him, and worketh righteousnesse, is accepted with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Acts 2:36; Revelation 17:14"> Ye know the worde which God hath sent to the children of Israel, preaching peace by Iesus Christ, which is Lord of all:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 4:14; Acts 2:22; Acts 28:22"> Euen the worde which came through all Iudea, beginning in Galile, after the baptisme which Iohn preached.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 4:18; Acts 2:22; Isaiah 61:1–3"> To wit, howe God anointed Iesus of Nazareth with the holy Ghost, and with power: who went about doing good, and healing all that were oppressed of the deuill: for God was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:48; Acts 1:8; Acts 10:41"> And we are witnesses of all things which he did both in the land of the Iewes, and in Hierusalem, whom they slewe, hanging him on a tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Peter 1:21; Acts 13:30–31; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4"> Him God raysed vp the third day, and caused that he was shewed openly:</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 14:22; Acts 10:39; John 21:13"> Not to all the people, but vnto the witnesses chosen before of God, euen to vs which did eate and drinke with him, after he arose from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; 2 Timothy 4:1; 1 Peter 4:5"> And he commanded vs to preach vnto the people, and to testifie, that it is he that is ordained of God a iudge of quicke and dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; Acts 2:38; Isaiah 53:11"> To him also giue all the Prophets witnesse, that through his Name all that beleeue in him, shall receiue remission of sinnes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 11:15; Acts 19:6; Acts 15:8"> While Peter yet spake these wordes, the holy Ghost fell on al them which heard the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Acts 10:23; Acts 2:38; Galatians 3:13–14"> So they of the circumcision which beleeued, were astonied, as many as came with Peter, because that on the Gentiles also was powred out the gift of the holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 2:4; Mark 16:17"> For they heard them speake with tongues, and magnifie God. Then answered Peter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 8:36; Romans 10:12; Acts 11:15–17"> Can any man forbid water, that these should not be baptized, which haue receiued the holy Ghost, as well as we?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 8:16; Galatians 3:27"> So he commanded them to be baptized in the Name of the Lord. Then prayed they him to tary certaine dayes.</span>
