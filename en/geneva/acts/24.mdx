<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 24:11; Acts 23:2; Acts 25:2"> Now after fiue dayes, Ananias the hie Priest came downe with the Elders, and with Tertullus a certaine oratour, which appeared before the Gouernour against Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:5; Proverbs 26:28; Acts 24:26–27"> And when he was called foorth, Tertullus began to accuse him, saying, Seeing that we haue obtained great quietnesse through thee, and that many worthy things are done vnto this nation through thy prouidence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 23:26; Acts 26:25; Luke 1:3"> We acknowledge it wholy, and in all places most noble Felix, with all thankes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:32"> But that I be not tedious vnto thee, I pray thee, that thou wouldest heare vs of thy courtesie a fewe wordes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 28:22; Acts 24:14; Acts 26:5"> Certainely we haue found this man a pestilent fellowe, and a moouer of sedition among all the Iewes throughout the world, and a chiefe maintainer of the secte of the Nazarites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 24:12; Acts 21:27–32; John 18:31"> And hath gone about to pollute the Temple: therefore wee tooke him, and woulde haue iudged him according to our Lawe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> But the chiefe captaine Lysias came vpon vs, and with great violence tooke him out of our handes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 23:35; Acts 25:15–16; Acts 25:5"> Commanding his accusers to come to thee: of whom thou mayest (if thou wilt inquire) know all these things whereof we accuse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:16; Micah 7:2–3; Psalms 4:2"> And the Iewes likewise affirmed, saying that it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15; 1 Samuel 2:25; Luke 18:2"> Then Paul, after that the gouernour had beckened vnto him that hee shoulde speake, answered, I do the more gladly answere for my selfe, for as much as I knowe that thou hast bene of many yeres a iudge vnto this nation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 24:1; Acts 21:18; Acts 23:11"> Seeing that thou mayest knowe, that there are but twelue dayes since I came vp to worship in Hierusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 25:8; Acts 28:17; Acts 24:18"> And they neither found mee in the Temple disputing with any man, neither making vproare among the people, neither in the Synagogues, nor in the citie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 25:7; 1 Peter 3:16"> Neither can they proue the things, whereof they now accuse me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 9:2; Romans 3:21; Acts 3:13"> But this I confesse vnto thee, that after the way (which they call heresie) so worship I the God of my fathers, beleeuing all things which are written in the Lawe and the Prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 5:28–29; 1 Thessalonians 4:14–16"> And haue hope towardes God, that the resurrection of the dead, which they themselues looke for also, shalbe both of iust and vniust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 23:1; 1 Timothy 1:5; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> And herein I endeuour my selfe to haue alway a cleare conscience towarde God, and toward men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 2:10; Romans 15:25–28; 1 Corinthians 16:1–4"> Now after many yeres, I came and brought almes to my nation and offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:21; Acts 21:26–30; Acts 24:12"> At what time, certaine Iewes of Asia founde mee purified in the Temple, neither with multitude, nor with tumult.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 23:30; Acts 25:16"> Who ought to haue bene present before thee, and accuse me, if they had ought against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Or let these themselues say, if they haue found any vniust thing in mee, while I stoode in the Council,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 23:6; Acts 4:2; Acts 26:6–8"> Except it be for this one voyce, that I cried standing among them, Of the resurrection of the dead am I accused of you this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 24:6; Acts 24:24; Acts 25:26"> Nowe when Felix heard these things, he deferred them, and said, When I shall more perfectly know the things which concerne this way, by the comming of Lysias the chiefe Captaine, I will decise your matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 28:16; Acts 27:3; Proverbs 16:7"> Then hee commanded a Centurion to keepe Paul, and that he should haue ease, and that he should forbid none of his acquaintance to minister vnto him, or to come vnto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Galatians 2:16; Mark 6:20"> And after certaine dayes, came Felix with his wife Drusilla, which was a Iewesse, and he called foorth Paul, and heard him of the faith in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:8; 1 Peter 3:15; Acts 10:42"> And as he disputed of righteousnes and temperance, and of the iudgement to come, Felix trembled, and answered, Go thy way for this time, and when I haue conuenient time, I will call for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Proverbs 17:23; Isaiah 56:11"> Hee hoped also that money shoulde haue bene giuen him of Paul, that he might loose him: wherefore hee sent for him the oftner, and communed with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 25:9; Acts 25:14; Acts 12:3"> When two yeeres were expired, Porcius Festus came into Felix roume: and Felix willing to get fauour of the Iewes, left Paul bound.</span>
