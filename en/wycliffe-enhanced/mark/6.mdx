<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:35; MATTHEW 8:10; LUKE 13:22"> And he wondered for the unbelief of them. And he went about (the) castles on each side, and taught. (And he wondered at their unbelief. And he went about the villages on each side, and taught.)</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKE 9:3; MATTHEW 10:9–10; LUKE 10:4"> and commanded (to) them, that they should not take anything in the way, (or that they should not take anything on the way, or for the journey), but a staff &#91;or a rod&#93; only, not a scrip, nor bread, neither money in the girdle,</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKE 3:19–20; MATTHEW 11:2; LUKE 3:1"> For that Herod sent (for), and held John, and bound him into prison, for Herodias, the wife of Philip, his brother; for he had wedded her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:21; LEVITICUS 18:16; 1ST KINGS 22:14"> For John said to Herod, It is not leaveful to thee (or It is not lawful for thee), to have the wife of thy brother.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:26; MATTHEW 14:5; 2ND CHRONICLES 24:15–22"> And Herod dreaded John, and knew him a just man and holy, and kept him, (or And Herod feared John, and knew him to be a just and holy man, and kept him in custody, or And Herod revered John, and knew him to be a just and holy man, and kept him safe). <span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ESTHER 7:2; ESTHER 5:3; ESTHER 5:6"> And he swore to her, That whatever thou ask, I shall give (it) to thee, though it be(even) half of my kingdom.</span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOHN 6:2; MATTHEW 15:29–31; MARK 6:54–55"> And they saw them go away (or And the people saw them go away), and many knew, and they went afoot from all (the) cities, and ran &#91;together&#93; thither, and came before them.</span>

<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="LUKE 9:17; MARK 8:8–9; MATTHEW 15:37–38"> and all ate, and were full-filled. &#91;and all ate, and were fulfilled.&#93;(and all ate, and were filled full.)</span>

<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="PSALMS 93:3–4; MARK 4:39; JOHN 6:21"> And he came up to them into the boat, and the wind ceased. And they wondered more within themselves;</span>

<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:34–36; JOHN 6:24–25; LUKE 5:1"> And when they were passed over the sea (or And when they had crossed over the lake), they came into the land of Gennesaret, and setted to land.</span>

<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="MATTHEW 4:24; MARK 2:1–3; MARK 3:7–11"> And they ran through all that country(side), and began to bring sick men in beds on each side, where they heard that he was.</span>
