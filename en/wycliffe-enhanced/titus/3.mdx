<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ROMANS 13:1–7; 1ST PETER 2:13–17; ECCLESIASTES 10:4"> Admonish them to be subjects to princes &#91;or Admonish them to be subject to princes&#93;, and to powers; to obey to that that is said, and to be ready to (do) all good work(s);</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:31; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:24–25; EPHESIANS 4:2"> to blaspheme no man, to be not full of chiding, but temperate &#91;or patient&#93;, showing all mildness to all men (or showing meekness and humility to everyone).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:1–3; ROMANS 6:17; COLOSSIANS 1:21"> For we were sometime unwise, unbelieveful, erring, and serving to desires, and to diverse lusts, doing in malice and envy, worthy to be hated, hating each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:4–10; TITUS 2:10–11; 1ST TIMOTHY 4:10"> But when the benignity and the manhood &#91;or the humanity&#93; of our Saviour God appeared,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:8–9; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:11"> not of works of rightwiseness that we did, but by his mercy he made us safe, by &#91;the&#93; washing, &#91;or (the) baptism&#93;, of (the) again-begetting, and (the) again-newing of the Holy Ghost, (not because of works of righteousness that we did, but by his mercy he saved us, by the washing, or the baptism, of rebirth, and the renewing power of the Holy Spirit,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOEL 2:28; ROMANS 5:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:45"> whom he shedded &#91;out&#93; into us plenteously by Jesus Christ, our Saviour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 8:17; TITUS 2:11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:11"> that we justified by his grace, be heirs by hope of everlasting life. (so that we who be justified by his grace, be heirs through the hope of eternal life.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TITUS 3:14; TITUS 1:9; PSALMS 16:2–3"> A true word is &#91;this&#93;, and of these things I will that thou confirm others, that they that believe in God, be busy to be above others &#91;or to be before others&#93; in good works. These things be good, and profitable to men. (This is a true word, and I desire that thou confirm these things to others, so that they who believe in God, be busy to be before others, or ahead of others, in doing good works. These things be good, and profitable for everyone.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 2:14; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:23; 1ST TIMOTHY 4:7"> And eschew thou foolish questions, and genealogies, and strivings &#91;or and strives&#93;, and fightings of the law; for those be unprofitable and vain. (And shun foolish questions, and genealogies, and fights, and arguments over the Law; for they be empty and useless.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ROMANS 16:17; 2ND JOHN 1:10; MATTHEW 18:15–17"> Eschew thou &#91;or Shun thou&#93; a man heretic, after one and the second correction;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:26; LUKE 19:22; MATTHEW 25:26–28"> witting that he that is such a manner man is subverted, and trespasseth, and is condemned by his own doom (or by his own judgement).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 4:9; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:12; 1ST CORINTHIANS 16:8–9"> When I send to thee Artemas, or Tychicus, hie thou &#91;or (make) haste&#93; to come to me to Nicopolis; for I have purposed to dwell in winter there. (When I send Artemas, or Tychicus, to thee, hasten to come to me at Nicopolis; for I have decided to remain there for the winter.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:24; MATTHEW 22:35; 3RD JOHN 1:6–8"> Busily before send Zenas &#91;or Busily send before Zenas&#93;, a wise man of (the) law, and Apollos, (so) that nothing fail to them. (With whatever assistance you can provide, send forth Zenas, a man wise in the Law, and Apollos, so that nothing is lacking for them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:10; TITUS 3:8; MATTHEW 7:19"> They that be of ours, learn to be governors &#91;or to be before&#93; in good works, (or Those who be of us, learn to be leaders in good works, or the first to do good works), to necessary uses, (and) that they be not without fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 1:5; EPHESIANS 6:23–24; 2ND JOHN 1:1–2"> All men that be with me greet thee well. Greet thou well them, that love us in &#91;the&#93; faith. The grace of God be with you all. Amen. (All who are with me send hearty greetings to thee. Give hearty greetings to those in the faith who love us. The grace of God be with you all. Amen.)</span>
