<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Forsooth Mordecai dwelled (at) that time in the hall of the king, with Bigthan (or Gabatha) and Teresh (or Tharra), the honest servants and chaste (or the honest and chaste servants), &#91;or (the) geldings&#93; of the king, that were porters, (or doorkeepers, or guards), of the palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And when he had understood the thoughts of them, and had before-seen full diligently their busynesses, he learned that they endeavoured them(selves) to set &#91;or to put&#93; their hand(s) upon the king Ahasuerus, and he told of that thing to the king.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the king commanded Mordecai, that he should dwell in the hall of the palace, and he gave to him gifts for the telling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Forsooth Haman, the son of Ham-medatha, a Bougaean (or an Agagite), was most glorious before the king, and he would have annoyed (or harmed) Mordecai, and his people, for the twain honest (and chaste) servants (or the two eunuchs) of the king that were slain.
