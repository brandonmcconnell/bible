<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HOSEA 11:2; JOSHUA 3:7; NUMBERS 13:16"> For Ephraim spake, hideousness assailed Israel; and he trespassed in Baal, and was dead. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:17–20; 1ST KINGS 19:18; ISAIAH 46:6"> And now they added to do sin, and made to them a molten image of their silver, as the likeness of idols; all is the making of craftsmen. To these they say, A! ye men, offer, and worship calves. (And they added to do more sin, and made for themselves a cast image, and idols made out of silver; yea, all the making of craftsmen. To whom they say, O ye men! offer to these, and worship the calves.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HOSEA 6:4; PSALMS 1:4; PSALMS 68:2"> Therefore they shall be as a morrowtide cloud, and as the dew of morrowtide, that passeth forth, as dust ravished by whirlwind from the cornfloor, and as smoke of a chimney. (And so they shall be like a morning cloud, and like the morning dew, that passeth away, and like the dust that is taken up by the whirlwind from the threshing floor, and like the smoke out of a chimney.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 45:21–22; HOSEA 12:9; ISAIAH 43:10–13"> Forsooth I am thy Lord God from the land of Egypt; and thou shalt not know God, except me, and no saviour is, except me. (But I am the Lord thy God since thy days in the land of Egypt; and thou shalt know no other god, except me, and there is no other saviour, except me.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:15; DEUTERONOMY 2:7; DEUTERONOMY 32:10"> I knew thee in the desert, in the land of wilderness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:13–15; DEUTERONOMY 8:12–14; NEHEMIAH 9:35"> By their pastures they were filled, and had abundance; they raised &#91;up&#93; their heart, and forgat me, (or then they raised up their hearts, and forgot me).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HOSEA 5:14; JEREMIAH 5:6; LAMENTATIONS 3:10"> And I shall be as a lioness to them, as a leopard in the way of Assyrians. (And I shall be like a lioness to them, I shall watch them like a leopard by the wayside.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 17:8; ISAIAH 5:29; PSALMS 50:22"> I as a female bear, when the whelps be ravished, shall meet them; and shall all-break the inner things of the maw of them. And I as a lion shall waste them there; a beast of the field shall rend them. (I shall meet them like a female bear, robbed of her cubs, and I shall break the inner things of their maw, or I shall tear apart their innards. And like a lion I shall destroy them there; yea, I shall tear them apart like a wild beast.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:17; JEREMIAH 2:19; DEUTERONOMY 33:26"> Israel, thy perdition is of thee; thine help is only of me. (Israel, thy perdition (is from me); now who (can) help thee? or and now only I (can) help thee!)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HOSEA 8:4; 1ST SAMUEL 8:5–6; HOSEA 10:3"> Where is thy king? mostly save he thee now in all thy cities; and where be thy judges, of which thou saidest, Give thou to me a king and princes? (Where is thy king now? let him save thee in all thy cities; and where be thy rulers, of whom thou saidest, Give thou me a king and princes?)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 10:19; 1ST SAMUEL 15:22–23; 1ST KINGS 14:7–16"> I shall give to thee a king in my strong vengeance, and I shall take away in mine indignation. (I gave thee a king in my strong vengeance, and then in my indignation I took him away.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:34–35; JOB 14:17; ROMANS 2:5"> The wickedness of Ephraim is bound together; his sin is hid. (The wickedness of Ephraim is recorded; and the records of his sins be hidden away.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 37:3; ISAIAH 13:8; PSALMS 48:6"> The sorrows of a woman travailing of child shall come to him; he is a son not wise. For now he shall not stand in the defouling of sons. (The sorrows of a woman labouring with child shall come to him; he is not a wise son, for he would not leave the place of his conceiving.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 25:8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:4; ISAIAH 26:19"> I shall deliver them from the hand of death, and I shall again-buy them from death. Thou death, I shall be thy death; thou hell, I shall be thy morsel. Comfort is hid from mine eyes, (But I shall save, or rescue, them from the hand of death, and I shall buy them back from death. O death, bring on thy death; O Sheol or O grave, bring on thy destruction. Mercy is hid from my eyes,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 19:12; EZEKIEL 17:10; GENESIS 41:52"> for he shall part betwixt brethren. The Lord shall bring a burning wind, going up from desert; and it shall make dry the veins thereof, and it shall make desolate the well(s) thereof; and he shall ravish the treasure of each desirable vessel. (and I shall divide between brothers. Yea, the Lord shall bring in a burning wind, going up from the desert; and it shall dry up their springs, and make their wells desolate; and it shall take away all their valuable treasures.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2ND KI 8:12; 2ND KI 15:16; ISAIAH 13:16"> (Let) Samaria perish, for it stirred his God to bitterness; perish it by sword. The little children of them be hurtled down, and the women with child thereof be carved. (And so Samaria shall perish, for she stirred her God to bitterness; she shall perish by the sword. Their little children shall be hurtled down, and their women with child shall be carved, or cut, up.)</span>
</div>
