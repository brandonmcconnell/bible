(The inhabitants of Samaria worshipped the calf of Bethaven. Thorns and briars, or nettles, shall grow upon their altars). And they shall say to mountains, Cover ye us, and to little hills, Fall ye down on us.</span>

</div>

For it is time to seek the Lord, and when he cometh, he shall rain blessings upon you.)</span>
