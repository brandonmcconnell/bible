<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 9:6; HOSEA 10:12; JOB 4:8"> Do not ye err, God is not scorned; for those things that a man soweth, those things he shall reap, &#91;or for why what things a man soweth, also these things he shall reap&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ROMANS 8:13; JAMES 3:18; 2ND PETER 2:19"> For he that soweth in his flesh, of the flesh he shall reap corruption; but he that soweth in the Spirit, of the Spirit he shall reap everlasting life.</span>

(And so while we have the time, do we good to all; but most of all to those who be members of the household, or the family, of faith.)</span>
