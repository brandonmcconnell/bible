<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GALATIANS 1:11–12; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:1; ROMANS 1:1"> Paul the apostle, not of men, nor by man, but by Jesus Christ, and God the Father, that raised him from death &#91;or that raised him from (the) dead&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 16:1; PHILIPPIANS 4:21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:5–6"> and all the brethren that be with me, to the churches of Galatia, (and all the brothers who be with me, to the churches in Galatia,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND JOHN 1:3; 2ND CORINTHIANS 13:14; ROMANS 1:7–15"> grace (be) to you and (the) peace of God the Father, and of the Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 20:28; ROMANS 4:25; GALATIANS 2:20"> that gave himself for our sins, to deliver us from the present wicked world, by the will of God and our Father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ROMANS 11:36; JUDE 1:25; PSALMS 72:19"> to whom is honour and glory into worlds of worlds. Amen. (to whom be honour and glory forever and ever. Amen.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 11:4; GALATIANS 5:7–8; ISAIAH 29:13"> I wonder, that so soon ye be thus moved from him that called you into the grace of Christ, into another evangel &#91;or into another gospel&#93;; (I am amazed, that so quickly ye be moved away from him who called you into the grace of the Messiah, to another gospel;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:24; ROMANS 16:17–18; GALATIANS 5:10"> which is not another, but that there be some that trouble you, and will mis-turn the evangel of Christ. (which is not truly another gospel, but that there be some who trouble you, and will pervert the Gospel, or the Good News, of the Messiah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 11:13–14; GALATIANS 1:9; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:18–19"> But though we, or an angel of heaven, preached to you, besides that that we have preached to you, be he accursed. &#91;But though we, or an angel of heaven, evangelized to you, besides that that we have evangelized to you, cursed be he.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ROMANS 16:17; PROVERBS 30:6; DEUTERONOMY 4:2"> As I have said before, and now again I say, if any man preach to you besides that that ye have received, be he accursed &#91;or cursed be he&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 2:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:29; EPHESIANS 6:6"> For now whether counsel I men, or God? or whether I seek to please men? If I pleased yet men, I were not Christ’s servant, (or If I still sought to please men, I would not be the Messiah’s servant).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GALATIANS 1:1; 1ST CORINTHIANS 2:9–10; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:23"> For, brethren, I make known to you the evangel &#91;or the gospel&#93;, that was preached of me (or by me), for it is not by man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GALATIANS 1:16; 1ST CORINTHIANS 2:10; GALATIANS 1:1"> nor I took it of man (or nor I received it from any man), nor learned (it from any man), but by &#91;the&#93; revelation of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:3–5"> For ye have heard my conversation sometime in the Jewry, that I pursued (sur)passingly, &#91;or over-manner, or (over-) measure&#93;, the church of God, and fought against it. (For ye have heard about my life before among the Jewry, how I persecuted God’s church beyond measure, and fought so very hard against it.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 2:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:5; PHILIPPIANS 3:4–6"> And I profited in the Jewry above many of mine even-elders in my kindred, and was more abundantly a follower &#91;or a lover&#93; of my fathers’ traditions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:5; ISAIAH 49:5; ISAIAH 49:1"> But when it pleased him, that parted me (or who separated me) from my mother’s womb, and called (me) by his grace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:17; EPHESIANS 3:5–10; EPHESIANS 6:12"> to show his Son in me, that I should preach him among the heathen, anon I drew me not to flesh and blood; (to show his Son to me, so that I would preach him among the Gentiles, at once I drew me not to flesh and blood;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:20–25; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:32–33; GALATIANS 1:18"> nor I came to Jerusalem to the apostles, that were before me, but I went into Arabia, and again I turned again into Damascus. (nor did I come to Jerusalem to those who were apostles before me, but I went to Arabia, and then I returned to Damascus.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:22–23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:26–29; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:17–18"> And since three years after I came to Jerusalem &#91;or Afterward after three years I came to Jerusalem&#93;, to see Peter, and I dwelled with him (for) fifteen days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:55; MARK 6:3; MATTHEW 12:46"> but I saw none other of the apostles, but James, our Lord’s brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 9:1; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:31; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:10–11"> And these things which I write to you, lo! before God I lie not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:41; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:30; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 6:9"> Afterward I came into the coasts of Syria and Cilicia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 2:14; ROMANS 16:7; 2ND THESSALONIANS 1:1"> But I was unknown by face to the churches of Judea, that were in Christ (or who were in the Messiah);</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:8–10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:13; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:13–16"> and they had only an hearing, that he that pursued us sometime (ago), preacheth now the faith, against which he fought sometime (ago); (and they had only heard it said, that he who had persecuted us before, now preacheth the faith, which before he fought so very hard against;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:18; LUKE 15:10"> and in me they glorified God. (and they praised God for me.)</span>
