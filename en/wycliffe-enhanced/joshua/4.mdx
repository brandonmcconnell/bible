(ye shall answer to them, The waters of the Jordan River stopped flowing before the Ark of the Covenant of the Lord, when the Ark crossed over the Jordan River; and so these stones be here to help the Israelites to remember this forevermore.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOSHUA 4:2–5; JOSHUA 1:16–18; JOSHUA 4:20"> Therefore the sons of Israel did as Joshua commanded to them, and bare from the midst of the trough of (the) Jordan twelve stones, as the Lord commanded to him, by the number of the sons of Israel, unto the place in which they setted tents, (or one for each of the tribes of Israel, unto the place where they pitched their tents); and there they putted those stones.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOSHUA 3:13; PSALMS 119:60; PROVERBS 27:1"> Forsooth the priests, that bare the ark, stood in the midst of (the) Jordan, till all things were &#91;ful&#93; filled, which the Lord commanded, that Joshua should speak to the people (or that Joshua should tell the people to do), as Moses had said to him. And the people hasted, and passed over (the) Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOSHUA 3:8; JOSHUA 4:18; JOSHUA 3:17"> And when all men had passed &#91;over&#93;, also the ark of the Lord passed &#91;over&#93;, and (then) the priests went before the people (again).</span>

(And forty thousand fighting men went by their companies, over the plains and fields, near the city of Jericho.)</span>

(On that day the Lord magnified Joshua before all Israel, so that they would fear him, like they had feared Moses, or so that they would revere him, like they had revered Moses, while he was yet alive.)</span>

(Command thou to the priests who carry the Ark of the Covenant, that they come up from the Jordan River.)</span>

(And when they had come up, carrying the Ark of the Covenant of the Lord, and had begun to tread on the dry earth, the waters returned to their place, and flowed again, as they were wont to before.)</span>

(And the people went up from the Jordan River on the tenth day of the first month, and they pitched their tents in Gilgal, east of the city of Jericho.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOSHUA 4:3; JOSHUA 4:8"> Also Joshua putted in Gilgal the twelve stones, which they had taken from the trough of (the) Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOSHUA 4:6; PSALMS 145:4–7; PSALMS 105:2–5"> And he said to the sons of Israel, When your sons shall ask tomorrow their fathers, and shall say to them, What will these stones be mean(ing)? (And he said to the Israelites, When your sons and daughters shall ask their fathers tomorrow, and shall say to them, What mean ye by these stones?)</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 106:8; PSALMS 89:13; 1ST KINGS 8:42–43"> that all the peoples of (the) earth learn, or know, (of) the full strong might of the Lord, and that ye dread your Lord God in all time. (so that all the peoples of the earth can learn, or come to know, of the strong might, or the powerful hand, of the Lord, and so that ye shall fear the Lord your God forevermore or and so that ye shall revere the Lord your God forevermore.)</span>
