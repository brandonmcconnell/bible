<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ISAIAH 57:15; HOSEA 14:5; ISAIAH 35:1–2"> I am a flower of the field, and a lily of great valleys. (I am a flower of Sharon, and a lily of the great valley.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:12; MATTHEW 10:16; PHILIPPIANS 2:15–16"> As a lily among thorns, so is my friendess among daughters. (Like a lily among the thorns, is my friendess among the daughters.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 8:5; HEBREWS 1:1–6; ISAIAH 32:2"> As an apple tree among the trees of woods, so (is) my darling among (the) sons. I sat under the shadow of him, whom I desired; and his fruit was sweet to my throat. (Like an apple tree among the trees of the woods, is my darling among the sons of men. I sat under his shadow, yea, he whom I desired; and his fruit was sweet to my taste.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ROMANS 8:28–39; JOHN 15:9–15; ROMANS 5:8–10"> The king led me into the wine cellar; he ordained charity in me (or he ordained his love upon me).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 5:8; HOSEA 3:1; 2ND SAMUEL 6:19"> Beset ye me with flowers, compass ye me with apples; for I am sick for love. (Put ye flowers about me, yea, surround ye me with apple blossoms; for I am weak, or I faint, because of love.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 3:17; ISAIAH 54:5–10; JEREMIAH 32:41"> His left hand is under mine head; and his right hand shall embrace me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 3:5; SONG OF SONGS 8:4; SONG OF SONGS 1:5"> Ye daughters of Jerusalem, I charge you greatly, by caprets, and harts of fields, that ye raise not, neither make to awake the dearworthy spousess, till she will. (Ye daughters of Jerusalem, I strongly command you, by the gazelles, and the harts of the fields, that ye raise not up, nor awaken the dearworthy spousess, until she desireth it.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 10:27; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:20; JOHN 3:29"> The voice of my darling; lo! this darling cometh leaping in mountains, and skipping over little hills. (The voice of my darling; behold! my (darling) cometh leaping over the mountains, and skipping over the little hills.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 8:14; SONG OF SONGS 2:17; JOHN 5:39"> My darling is like a capret, and a calf of harts; lo! he standeth behind our wall, and beholdeth by the windows, and looketh through the lattice. (My darling is like a gazelle, or like a hart calf; behold! he standeth behind our wall, and seeth in through the windows, and looketh through the lattice.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 2:13; SONG OF SONGS 4:7–8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:17"> Lo! my darling speaketh to me, My love, my culver, my fair spousess, rise thou, haste thou, and come thou; (Behold! my darling speaketh to me, and saith, My love, my dove, my beautiful (spousess), rise thou up, hasten thou, and come thou away;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 60:1–2; ECCLESIASTES 3:11; ISAIAH 54:6–8"> for winter is passed now, rain is gone, and is departed away. (for the winter is now passed, the rains have gone away, yea, they have departed at last.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 55:12; COLOSSIANS 3:16; PSALMS 89:15"> Flowers have appeared in our land, and the time of cutting is come; the voice of a turtle is heard in our land (or the song of a turtledove is heard in our land),</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 2:10; SONG OF SONGS 7:11–13; ISAIAH 55:10–11"> the fig tree hath brought forth his buds; the vineries flowering have given their odour. My love, my fair spousess, rise thou, haste thou, and come thou. (the fig tree hath brought forth its buds; the flowering vines have given forth their aroma. My love, my beautiful spousess, rise thou up, hasten thou, and come thou away.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZRA 9:5–6; SONG OF SONGS 5:2; SONG OF SONGS 1:5"> My culver is in the holes of &#91;the&#93; stone, in the chink of a wall without mortar. Show thy face to me, thy voice sound in mine ears; for thy voice is sweet, and thy face is fair. (My dove is in the holes of the stone, in the chink of a wall made without mortar. Show thy face to me, let thy voice sound in my ears; for thy voice is sweet, and thy face is so beautiful.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKE 13:32; 2ND PETER 2:1–3; SONG OF SONGS 7:12"> Catch ye little foxes to us, that destroy the vineries; for our vinery hath flowered. (Catch ye for us the little foxes, that destroy the vineyards; for our vines have flowered.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 6:3; SONG OF SONGS 7:10; PSALMS 63:1"> My darling is to me, and I am to him, which is fed among lilies; (My darling is for me, and I am for him, who is fed among the lilies;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 4:6; SONG OF SONGS 2:8–9; 2ND PETER 1:19"> till the day spring, and shadows be bowed down. My darling, turn thou again; be thou like a capret, and a calf of harts, on the hills of Bether. (until the day spring forth, and the shadows be bowed down. My darling, return thou; be thou like a gazelle, or a hart calf, on the hills of Bether or upon the rugged hills.)</span>
