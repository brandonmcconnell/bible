<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spake to Moses, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 2:22; LEVITICUS 15:19; JOB 25:4"> Speak thou to the sons of Israel, and thou shalt say to them, If a woman, when she hath received seed, childeth a knave child, she shall be unclean by seven days, by the days of her separating of corruptible blood, that runneth from her by months; (Speak thou to the Israelites, and thou shalt say to them, If a woman, when she hath received seed, beareth a boy, she shall be unclean for seven days, as she is in the days of her separation for the corrupt blood that runneth from her every month;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 2:21; LUKE 1:59; JOHN 7:22–23"> and the young child shall be circumcised in the eighth day. (and the young boy shall be circumcised on the eighth day.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKE 2:22–23; LEVITICUS 15:25–28; HAGGAI 2:13"> Soothly she shall dwell three and thirty days in the blood of her purifying; she shall not touch any holy thing, neither she shall enter into the saintuary, till the days of her cleansing be &#91;ful&#93; filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 2:14–15; GENESIS 3:13; LEVITICUS 12:2"> Soothly if she childeth a female, she shall be unclean (for) two weeks, (as she is) by the custom of &#91;the&#93; flowing (out) of unclean blood, and threescore and six days she shall dwell in the blood of her cleansing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 2:22; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:21; 1ST PETER 1:18–19"> And when the days of her cleansing, for a son, or for a daughter, be fulfilled, she shall bring a lamb of one year into burnt sacrifice, and a culver bird, either a turtle, for sin, to the door of the tabernacle of witnessing; and she shall give to the priest, (And when the days of her cleansing, for a son, or for a daughter, be fulfilled, she shall bring a lamb of one year for a burnt sacrifice, and a young pigeon, or a turtledove, for a sin offering, to the entrance of the Tabernacle of the Witnessing; and she shall give them to the priest,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:28–30; HEBREWS 9:12–14; LEVITICUS 4:35"> which shall offer those before the Lord, and shall pray for her, and so she shall be cleansed from the flowing (out) of her blood. This is the law of a woman that childeth male, or female, (or This is the law for a woman who beareth a boy, or a girl).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:7; LEVITICUS 4:26; LEVITICUS 1:14"> That if her hand find not, neither she may offer a lamb, she shall take two turtles, either two culver birds, one into burnt sacrifice, and the tother for sin; and the priest shall pray for her, and so she shall be cleansed. (But if because of poverty, her hand cannot find a lamb that she may offer, she shall take two turtledoves, or two young pigeons, one for a burnt sacrifice, and the other for a sin offering; and the priest shall pray for her, and then she shall be clean.)</span>
