<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 2:11; DEUTERONOMY 30:6; ROMANS 7:6"> For we be (the) circumcision, which by (the) Spirit serve to God, and glory in Christ Jesus, (or who by the Spirit serve God, and glory in, or boast about, the Messiah Jesus), and have not trust in the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 11:18–22"> though I have trust, yea, in the flesh. If any other man is seen to trust in the flesh, I more,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 11:22; ROMANS 11:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:6"> that was circumcised in the eighth day, of the kin &#91;or of the kindred&#93; of Israel, of the lineage of Benjamin, an Hebrew of Hebrews, by the law a Pharisee, (who was circumcised on the eighth day, of the kindred of Israel, of the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew of Hebrews, a Pharisee by the Law,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GALATIANS 1:13–14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:9–10"> by love pursuing the church of God, by rightwiseness that is in the law living without (com)plaint. <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 14:33; MATTHEW 16:26; MATTHEW 13:44–46"> But which things were to me winnings, I have deemed these impairings for Christ. (And those things which were to me winnings, now I have judged to be losses because of the Messiah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:23–24; ROMANS 8:18; PHILIPPIANS 3:7"> Nevertheless I guess all things to be impairment for the clear science (or for the glorious knowledge) of Jesus Christ my Lord. For whom I made all things impairment, and I deem as drit &#91;or as turds&#93;, that I win Christ (or so that I may win the Messiah),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 5:21; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:30; GALATIANS 2:16"> and that I be found in him, not having my rightwiseness that is of the law, but that that is of the faith of Christ Jesus, that is of God the rightwiseness in faith, (and that I be found in him, not having my righteousness that is from the Law, but the righteousness that is from faith in the Messiah Jesus, which is the righteousness from God through faith,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GALATIANS 2:20; ROMANS 8:17; 1ST PETER 4:1–2"> to know him, and the virtue of his rising again, and the fellowship of his passion, and to be made like &#91;or (to be) configured&#93; to his death, (to know him, and the power of his resurrection, and the sharing of his suffering, and to be made like him in his death,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 20:35–36; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:6"> if on any manner (or in any manner) I come to the resurrection that is from death. &#91;+if on any manner I shall come to the resurrection that is of (the) dead.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 6:11–12; 2ND PETER 1:5–8; PSALMS 138:8"> Not that now I have taken (hold of it), or now I am perfect; but I (pur)sue, if in any manner I comprehend &#91;or I take (hold of)&#93; in which thing also I am comprehended of Jesus Christ. (Not that now I have caught it, or that now I am perfect, or am completed; but I continue to pursue it, if by any means I can catch hold of that for which also I am caught by Jesus Christ.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 9:62; HEBREWS 12:1–2; PHILIPPIANS 3:12"> Brethren, I deem me not that I have comprehended, (or Brothers, I do not reckon that I have taken hold of it, or that I have caught it); but one thing, I forget those things that be behind, and stretching forth myself to those things that be before,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 9:24; 1ST PETER 5:10; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:21"> and pursue to the ordained meed &#91;or to the prize&#93; of the high calling of God in Christ Jesus. (and pursue toward the ordained prize of the high calling of God in the Messiah Jesus.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOHN 7:17; JAMES 1:4–5; PROVERBS 2:3–6"> Therefore whoever we be perfect, feel we this thing. And if ye understand in other manner anything, this thing God shall show to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 2:6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:3; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:4–5"> Nevertheless to what thing we have come, (so) that we understand the same thing, and that we perfectly dwell in the same rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:16; 1ST PETER 5:3"> Brethren, be ye my followers, and wait ye (on) them that walk so, as ye have our form. (Brothers, follow me, and watch those who walk so, so that ye have our form.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:17; ISAIAH 8:11; GALATIANS 6:12"> For many walk, which I have said oft to you, but now I weeping say, &#91;them&#93;, the enemies of Christ’s cross (or the enemies of the Messiah’s cross),</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:19; COLOSSIANS 3:1–3; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:18–1"> But our living is in (the) heavens &#91;or Forsooth our living is in heaven&#93;; from whence also we abide (or we wait for) the Saviour our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
