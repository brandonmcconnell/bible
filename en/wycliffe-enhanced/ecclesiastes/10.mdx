<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:13–16; GALATIANS 2:12–14; NEHEMIAH 13:26"> Flies that die (in it), lose the sweetness of &#91;the&#93; ointment. A little folly at some time is more precious than wisdom and glory. (Flies that die in an ointment can destroy its sweetness. And so a little foolishness can sometimes destroy wisdom and glory.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 12:18–20; ECCLESIASTES 10:14; ECCLESIASTES 10:10"> The heart of a wise man is in his right side; and the heart of a fool is in his left side. (The heart of a wise person is in the right; and the heart of a fool is in the wrong.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:2; PROVERBS 13:16; PROVERBS 18:6"> But also a fool going in the way, when he is unwise, guesseth all men fools. (And a fool, going on the way, since he is unwise, thinketh that all the other people be fools.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 25:15; ECCLESIASTES 8:3; 1ST SAMUEL 25:24–44"> If the spirit of him, that hath power, goeth upon thee, forsake thou not thy place, (or If the spirit of him, who hath power, goeth against thee, do not leave thy position, that is, do not resign thy post); for curing, or taking heed, shall make (the) greatest sins to cease.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 5:13; ECCLESIASTES 4:7; ECCLESIASTES 9:3"> An evil there is, that I saw under the sun, and going out as by error from the face of the prince; (There is an evil that I saw under the sun, and going out as an error from the leader, or from the ruler;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:12; PROVERBS 29:2; ESTHER 3:1"> a fool (is) set in high dignity, and rich men sit beneath.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:10; ESTHER 6:8; PROVERBS 30:22"> I saw servants on horses, and princes as servants going on the earth. (I saw servants riding on horses, and princes, or leaders, walking on the ground like servants.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 26:27; PSALMS 7:15–16; ESTHER 7:10"> He that diggeth a ditch, shall fall into it; and an adder shall bite him, that destroyeth a hedge. (He who diggeth a ditch, shall fall into it; and he who destroyeth a hedge, shall be bitten by a serpent hiding in it.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He that beareth over stones, shall be tormented in those; and he that cutteth trees, shall be wounded of those. (He who carrieth stones, can be hurt by them; and he who cutteth wood, can be injured when cutting it.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JAMES 1:5; COLOSSIANS 4:5; ECCLESIASTES 10:15"> If iron is folded again, and it is not as before, but is made blunt, it shall be made sharp with much travail; and wisdom shall follow after busyness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:17; PSALMS 58:4–5; PSALMS 64:3"> If a serpent biteth, it biteth in silence; he that backbiteth privily, hath nothing less than it, (or he who privately, or secretly, backbiteth someone is no better).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 4:6; LUKE 4:22; PROVERBS 22:11"> The words of the mouth of a wise man be grace; and the lips of an unwise man shall cast him down. (The words out of the mouth of a wise person bring him favour; but the lips of an unwise person shall bring him down.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 25:10–11; PROVERBS 29:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 6:9–11"> The beginning of his words is folly; and the last thing of his mouth is the worst error. (His words begin with foolishness; and the last thing out of his mouth is the worst error of all.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:2; ECCLESIASTES 6:12; ECCLESIASTES 5:3"> A fool multiplieth words; a man knoweth not, what was before him, and who may show to him that, that shall come after him? (or and who can show him what shall come after him?)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 50:4–5; ISAIAH 57:1; ISAIAH 35:8–10"> The travail of fools shall torment them, that know not how to go into the city. (Fools work themselves to exhaustion, yet they do not even know how to go into the city.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 3:12; ISAIAH 5:11–12; ISAIAH 3:4–5"> Land, woe to thee, whose king is a child, and whose princes eat early.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 31:4–5; PROVERBS 28:2–3; ISAIAH 5:11"> Blessed is the land, whose king is noble; and whose princes eat in their time, to (only) sustain the(ir) kind, and not to lechery. (Happy is the land, whose king is well born, or refined; and whose leaders eat at the proper time, only to sustain themselves, and not unto drunkenness.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:1; PROVERBS 24:30–34; PROVERBS 20:4"> The highness of houses shall be made low in sloths; and the house shall drop (rain) in the feebleness of hands (or and a house shall leak due to feeble, or weak, hands).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 104:15; ECCLESIASTES 2:1–2; 1ST PETER 4:3"> In laughing, they dispose bread and wine, that they drinking eat largely; and all things obey to money. (With laughter, they array the table with bread and wine, so that they can enjoy all the abundance; for everything showeth obedience to money.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKE 12:2–3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:5; ECCLESIASTES 7:21–22"> In thy thought backbite thou not the king, and in the private of thy bed, curse thou not a rich man; for the birds of heaven shall bear thy voice, and he that hath pens, shall tell the sentence. (In thy thoughts backbite thou not the king, and in the privacy of thy bed, curse thou not the rich; for the birds of the heavens, or of the air, shall carry thy voice, and he that hath wings, shall tell what thou hast said.)</span>
</div>
</div>
