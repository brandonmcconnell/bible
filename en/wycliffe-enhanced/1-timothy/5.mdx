<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:32; 1ST PETER 5:5–6; TITUS 2:2"> Blame thou not an elder man (or Do not admonish, or rebuke, an older man), but beseech &#91;him&#93; as a father, young men as brethren;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 4:12; MATTHEW 12:50; PHILIPPIANS 4:8"> old women as mothers, young women as sisters, in all chastity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 6:1; DEUTERONOMY 10:18"> Honour thou &#91;the&#93; widows, that be very widows. (Honour the widows, who be true widows, or who be all alone.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:1–3; JOHN 19:26–27; MATTHEW 15:4–6"> But if any widow hath children of sons, learn she first to govern her house, and requite to (her) father and mother; for this thing is accepted before God, (or for this is acceptable before God, or for God approveth of this).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 5:3; LUKE 2:37; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:32"> And she that is a widow verily, and desolate, hope &#91;she&#93; into God, (or And she who is truly a widow, and desolate, let her put her hope in God), and be busy in beseechings and prayers night and day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JAMES 5:5; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:7; LUKE 15:24"> For she that is living in delights, is dead &#91;in (her) soul&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 4:11; TITUS 2:15; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:1"> And command thou this thing, (so) that they be without reproof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:10; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:14; LUKE 11:11–13"> For if any man hath not care of his own, and most(ly) of his household members (or of his own family), he hath denied the faith, and is worse than an unfaithful, &#91;or (a) heathen&#93;, man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 3:2; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:11; LUKE 2:36–37"> A widow be chosen &#91;into the temple&#93; not less than sixty years (old), that was (the) wife of one husband,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 6:18; TITUS 3:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:36"> and hath witnessing in good works (or and hath testimony of good works), (yea), if she nourished children, if she received poor men to harbour, if she hath washed the feet of holy men, if she ministered to men that suffered tribulation, if she followed all good work(s).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 5:14; HOSEA 13:6; JAMES 5:5"> But eschew thou younger widows; for when they have done lechery, they will be wedded in Christ, &#91;or forsooth when they have done lechery in Christ, they will be wedded&#93;, (But shun younger widows; for when, while yet in the Messiah, they shall feel passion, or shall do lechery, and they will be wedded,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GALATIANS 1:6; JAMES 3:1; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:34"> having damnation (or and so shall receive condemnation), for they have made void the(ir) first faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:19; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:6–11; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:30"> Also they (being) idle learn to go about houses, not only idle, but &#91;they be&#93; full of words and curious &#91;or and curiosity&#93;, speaking things that it behooveth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TITUS 2:5; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:1; TITUS 2:8"> Therefore I will (or And so I desire), that younger widows be wedded, and bring forth children, and be housewives, to give none occasion to the adversary, because of (any) cursed thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:2; JUDE 1:4–5; 2ND PETER 2:20–22"> For now some be turned aback after Satan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 5:3–5; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:8"> If any faithful man hath widows, minister he to them, that the church be not charged, that it suffice to them that be very widows. (If anyone in the faith hath a widow in their family, let them minister unto her, so that the church be not burdened, and so that it can provide sufficiently for those who be true widows, or who be all alone.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:17; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:12–13; GALATIANS 6:6"> The priests that be well governors, &#91;that is, (that) truly keep well (the) priesthood&#93;, be they had worthy to double honour; most(ly) they that travail in word and teaching (or and most of all those who labour in speaking and teaching).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 25:4; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:14; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:7–10"> For the scripture saith, Thou shalt not bridle the mouth of the ox threshing, and, A workman is worthy (of) his hire (or A worker is worthy of their wages).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:16; DEUTERONOMY 19:15; 2ND CORINTHIANS 13:1"> Do not thou receive accusing against a priest, &#91;no&#93; but under twain or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 4:2; TITUS 1:13; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:20"> But reprove thou men that sin before all men, that also others have dread. (And rebuke those who sin before everyone, so that others also have fearful reverence.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:4; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:1; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:13"> I pray thee &#91;or I adjure thee&#93; before God, and Jesus Christ, (or I beseech thee before God, and Jesus Christ), and his chosen angels, that thou keep these things without prejudice, and do nothing in bowing to the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:11; 1ST TIMOTHY 3:10; 1ST TIMOTHY 3:6"> Put thou hands to no man, neither anon commune thou with other men’s sins. Keep thyself chaste. &#91;Put thou hands to no man soon, neither commune thou with other men’s sins. Keep thyself chaste.&#93;(Do not at once, or too hastily, lay hands upon any man for ordination, nor share thou in other men’s sinning. Keep thyself pure.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 104:15; PROVERBS 31:4–7; 1ST TIMOTHY 4:4"> Do not thou yet drink water, but use a little wine, for thy stomach, and for thine oft falling infirmities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 4:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:18; GALATIANS 5:19–21"> Some men’s sins be open, before going to doom (or before going unto Judgement); but of some men they come after &#91;or they follow&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:9; LUKE 11:33; PSALMS 37:5–6"> And also good deeds be open, and those that have them in other manner, may not be hid. (And some good deeds be done in the open, but those which be not, cannot be kept hidden forever.)</span>
