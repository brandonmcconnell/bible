<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="TITUS 1:7; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:28; 1ST PETER 5:2"> A faithful word &#91;I shall say&#93;. If any man desireth a bishopric, he desireth a good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="TITUS 1:6–9; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:24; ROMANS 12:13"> Therefore it behooveth a bishop to be without reproof, the husband of one wife, sober, prudent, chaste, virtuous, holding hospitality, a teacher;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TITUS 1:7; EPHESIANS 5:18; TITUS 3:2"> not given much to wine, not a smiter, but temperate &#91;or patient&#93;, not full of chiding &#91;or full of strife&#93;, not covetous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="TITUS 1:6; GENESIS 18:19; 1ST TIMOTHY 3:12"> well-ruling his house, and have sons subject with all chastity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 3:13; EPHESIANS 5:24; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:28"> for if any man know not how to govern his house, how shall he have (the) diligence &#91;or the keeping&#93; of the church of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 6:4; HEBREWS 5:12–13; 1ST PETER 2:2"> not new(ly) converted to the faith, lest he be borne up into pride, and fall into &#91;the&#93; doom of the devil. (not newly converted to the faith, lest he be raised up into pride, and then fall into the judgement of the Devil, or and then fall down under the same condemnation as the Devil.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 8:21; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:26; COLOSSIANS 4:5"> For it behooveth him to have also good witnessing of them that be withoutforth (or And it behooveth him to have good testimony from those who be outside the church), (so) that he fall not into reproof, and into the snare of the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:1; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 6:3–6"> Also it behooveth deacons to be chaste, not double-tongued, not given much to wine &#91;or not given to much wine&#93;, not following foul winning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 1:19; 2ND JOHN 1:9–10; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:5"> that have the mystery of faith in clean conscience. (who hold to the mystery of the faith with a clear conscience.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 5:22; 1ST TIMOTHY 3:13; TITUS 1:6–7"> But be they proved first, and minister they so, having no crime, &#91;or great sin&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TITUS 2:3; TITUS 3:2; 1ST TIMOTHY 3:2"> Also it behooveth women to be chaste, not backbiting, sober, faithful in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 3:2; 1ST TIMOTHY 3:4–5; PHILIPPIANS 1:1"> Deacons be husbands of one wife; which govern well their sons and their houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:21; HEBREWS 6:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:35"> For they that minister well, shall get a good degree to themselves, and much trust in the faith, that is in Christ Jesus (or that is in the Messiah Jesus).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 11:34; PHILEMON 1:22; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:15–17"> Son Timothy, I write to thee these things, hoping that I shall come soon to thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:5; EPHESIANS 2:21–22; 1ST THESSALONIANS 1:9"> but if I tarry, that thou knowest, how it behooveth thee to live in the house of God, that is the church of (the) living God, (as) a pillar and (a) firmness of (the) truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 1:14; HEBREWS 1:3; MARK 16:19"> And openly it is a great sacrament of piety, that thing that was showed in (the) flesh, it is justified in (the) Spirit, it appeared to angels, it is preached to heathen men, it is believed in the world, it is taken up into glory.</span>
