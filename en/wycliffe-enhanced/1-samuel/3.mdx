<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="AMOS 8:11–12; PSALMS 74:9; 1ST SAMUEL 3:21"> Forsooth the child Samuel ministered to the Lord before Eli, and the word of the Lord was precious; in those days was none open revelation (or for in those days there was no open revelation).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 4:15; PSALMS 90:10; GENESIS 27:1"> Therefore it was done in a day, Eli lay in his bed, and his eyes dimmed, and he might not see; (And so it was done one night, when Eli lay on his bed, and his eyes had dimmed, and he could not see;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 27:4; LEVITICUS 24:2–4; EXODUS 27:20–21"> the lantern of the Lord was not yet quenched. And Samuel slept in the temple of the Lord, where the ark of God was. (and Samuel also slept in the Temple of the Lord, where the Ark of God was. And one morning, before that the lantern of God was quenched,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 6:8; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:6–11; EXODUS 3:4"> And the Lord called Samuel; and he answered and said, Lo! I./Lo! I am ready. (the Lord called to Samuel; and he answered and said, Behold! I am here.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he ran to Eli, and said to him, Lo! I; for thou calledest me. And Eli said, I called not thee; turn thou again and sleep. And he went and slept. (And he ran to Eli, and said to him, Behold! I am here; for thou hast called me. And Eli said, I did not call thee; return thou, and go to sleep. And so he went and slept again.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:2; 1ST SAMUEL 4:16; 2ND SAMUEL 18:22"> And the Lord added again to call Samuel; and Samuel rose (up), and went to Eli, and said, Lo! I (am) here; for thou calledest me. And Eli answered, I called not thee, my son; turn thou again and sleep, (or And Eli answered, I did not call thee, my son; return thou, and go back to sleep).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:2; JEREMIAH 9:24"> Forsooth Samuel knew not yet the Lord, neither the word of the Lord was showed to him. (For Samuel did not yet know the Lord, and the word of the Lord was not yet shown to him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 33:14–15; 1ST CORINTHIANS 13:11–12"> And the Lord added, and called yet Samuel the third time; the which rose up and went to Eli, and said, Lo! I; for thou calledest me. Then Eli understood, that the Lord had called the child; (And the Lord added, and called to Samuel yet the third time; and he rose up and went to Eli, and said, Behold! I am here; for thou hast called me. Then Eli understood, that the Lord had called the child;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 20:19; ISAIAH 6:8; PSALMS 85:8"> and Eli said to Samuel, Go thou and sleep; and if he calleth thee afterward, thou shalt say, Speak thou, Lord, for thy servant heareth. Then Samuel went and slept in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 3:8; 1ST SAMUEL 3:4–6"> And the Lord came, and stood, and called as he had called the second time, (or and called to him as he had called the other times, saying), Samuel, Samuel. And Samuel said, Speak thou, Lord, for thy servant heareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HABAKKUK 1:5; JEREMIAH 19:3; 2ND KI 21:12"> And the Lord said to Samuel, Lo! I (shall) make a word, that is, (I shall do) a thing signified by a word, in Israel, which word whoever shall hear, both his ears shall ring, that is, he shall be astonished for wonder and dread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 2:27–36; ZECHARIAH 1:6; JOSHUA 23:15"> In that day I shall raise up against Eli all (the) things that I have spoken upon his house; I shall begin (it), and I shall end (it).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:18; MATTHEW 10:37; 1ST SAMUEL 2:12"> For I before-said to him, that I should deem his house without end for the wickedness thereof; for he knew, that his sons did unworthily, and he chastised not them. (For I said to him before, that I would judge his family forevermore for its wickedness; and he knoweth, that his sons have done unworthily, and yet he hath not chastised them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 22:14; 1ST SAMUEL 2:25; JEREMIAH 15:1"> Therefore I have sworn to the house of Eli, that the wickedness of his family, or his house, shall not be cleansed with sacrifices and gifts till into without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:6–8; MALACHI 1:10; 1ST SAMUEL 1:9"> And then Samuel slept till the morrowtide, and he opened the doors of the house of the Lord; and Samuel dreaded to show the revelation to Eli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Therefore Eli called Samuel, and said, Samuel, my son. And he answered and said, I am ready, (or And he answered, I am here).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 3:35; RUTH 1:17; PSALMS 141:5"> And Eli asked him, What is the word that the Lord hath spoken to thee? I pray thee, hide it not from me; God do to thee these things, and increase these things, if thou hidest from me a word of all &#91;the&#93; words that be said to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOB 2:10; ISAIAH 39:8; JUDGES 10:15"> And Samuel showed to him all the words, and hid not (anything) from him. And Eli answered, He is the Lord; do he that, that is good in his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 39:2; 1ST SAMUEL 9:6; 1ST KINGS 8:56"> Forsooth Samuel increased (or And so Samuel grew up), and the Lord was with him, and none of all his words felled into &#91;the&#93; earth, that is, (were)in vain, for all was (ful) filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JUDGES 20:1; 2ND SAMUEL 3:10; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:12"> And all Israel from Dan to Beersheba knew, that faithful Samuel was a prophet of the Lord. (And all Israel knew, from Dan to Beersheba, that truly Samuel was a prophet of the Lord.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 3:1; GENESIS 12:7; 1ST SAMUEL 3:4"> And the Lord added to appear again in Shiloh, for the Lord was showed to Samuel in Shiloh by the word of the Lord;</span>
