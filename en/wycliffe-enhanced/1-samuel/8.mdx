<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:18–19; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:21; NEHEMIAH 7:2"> And it was done, when Samuel waxed eld, he set his sons to be judges of Israel. (And it was done, when Samuel grew old, he made his sons to be judges in Israel.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 6:38; GENESIS 22:19; 1ST CHRONICLES 6:28"> And the name of his first begotten son was Joel, and the name of the second was Abiah, that were judges in Beersheba (or and they were judges in Beersheba).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:19; PSALMS 15:5; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:10"> And his sons went not in his ways, but they bowed after avarice, and they took gifts, and perverted doom (or and perverted justice, or judgement).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 7:17; 2ND SAMUEL 5:3; EXODUS 3:16"> Therefore all the greater men in birth of Israel were gathered together, and came to Samuel into Ramah. (And so all the men of great age, that is, the elders, of Israel gathered together, and came to Samuel in Ramah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:14–15; HOSEA 13:10–11; 1ST SAMUEL 8:6–8"> And they said to him, Lo! thou hast waxed eld, and thy sons go not in thy ways; ordain thou a king to us, that he deem us, as also all other nations have. (And they said to him, Behold! thou hast grown old, and thy sons do not go in thy ways; ordain thou a king for us, so that he can judge, or can rule, us, like all the other nations have.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 15:11; JAMES 1:5; 1ST SAMUEL 12:17"> And the word displeased in the eyes of Samuel, for they had said, Give thou to us a king, that he deem us. And Samuel prayed to the Lord. (And this request displeased Samuel, for they had said, Give thou a king to us, so that he can judge us. And Samuel prayed to the Lord.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 10:16; 1ST SAMUEL 10:19; EXODUS 16:8"> And the Lord said to Samuel, Hear thou the voice of the people in all things that they speak to thee; for they have not cast away thee (or for they have not thrown thee away), but me, (so) that I reign not upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 106:34–40; EXODUS 32:1; JUDGES 13:1"> By all the works that they have done, from the day in which I led them out of Egypt unto this day, as they have forsaken me, and they have served alien gods, so they do also to thee, (or and they have served foreign, or other, gods, and now they also do to thee).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 10:25; 1ST SAMUEL 8:11–18; EZEKIEL 46:18"> Now therefore hear thou their voice; nevertheless witness thou to them; and before-say thou to them the right of the king, that shall reign upon them, (or and tell thou them, the rights of the king who shall reign over them).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then Samuel said all the words of the Lord to the people, that had asked of him a king (or who had asked him for a king);</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 14:52; DEUTERONOMY 17:14–20; 1ST SAMUEL 10:25"> and he said, This shall be the right of the king, that shall command to you; he shall take your sons, and he shall set them in his chariots; and he shall make them to himself riders, and before-goers of his carts, (or and he shall make them to be his horsemen, or his riders, and to run before his chariots);</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 22:7; 1ST KINGS 4:7; 1ST CHRONICLES 27:1–22"> and he shall ordain to him tribunes, that is, sovereigns of a thousand, and centurions, that is, sovereigns of an hundred, and earers/tillers of his fields, and reapers of his corns, and smiths of his armours, and (smiths) of his chariots. (and he shall ordain his tribunes, that is, the rulers of a thousand, and his centurions, that is, the rulers of a hundred, and the tillers of his fields, and the reapers of his harvest, and the smiths of his arms, or of his weapons, and the smiths of his chariots.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Also he shall make your daughters (to be) makers of his ointments, and his fire-makers (or and his cooks), and (his) bakers/his makers of bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 46:18; 1ST KINGS 21:7; 1ST KINGS 21:19"> And he shall take your fields, and your vineries (or and your vineyards), and the best places of olives, and he shall give those to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DANIEL 1:3; ISAIAH 39:7; GENESIS 37:36"> But also he shall take the tenth part of your corns, and (of) the rents of your vineries, that he give those to his chamberlains, and &#91;to his&#93; servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And he shall take away your servants, and your handmaids, and your best young men, and your asses, and he shall set these in his work. (And he shall take away your servants, and your servantesses, and your best young men, and your donkeys, and he shall make them do his bidding or and he shall make them work for him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Also he shall take the tenth part of your flocks; and ye shall be his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MICAH 3:4; ISAIAH 1:15; PROVERBS 1:25–28"> And ye shall cry in that day from the face of your king, whom ye have chosen to you; and the Lord shall not hear you in that day; for ye &#91;have&#93; asked (for) a king to you. (And ye shall cry out on that day because of your king, whom ye have chosen for yourselves; but the Lord shall not hear you on that day; for ye have asked for a king for yourselves.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 44:16; JEREMIAH 7:13; ISAIAH 66:4"> Soothly the people would not hear the voice of Samuel, but they said, Nay, for a king shall be on us; (But the people would not listen to Samuel, and they said, No! let a king rule over us;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 8:5; PSALMS 106:35; DEUTERONOMY 7:6"> and (then) we also shall be as all folks (or and then we shall be like all the other nations), and our king shall deem us, and he shall go out before us, and he shall fight our battles for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JUDGES 11:11"> And Samuel heard all the words of the people, and he spake them in the ears of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 8:7; HOSEA 13:11"> And the Lord said to Samuel, Hear thou their voices, and ordain thou a king upon them. And Samuel said to the men of Israel, Each man go into his city (or Now everyone go home).</span>
