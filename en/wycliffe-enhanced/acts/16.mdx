<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 1:5; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:2; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:17"> And he came into Derbe and Lystra. And lo! a disciple was there, by (the) name (of) Timothy, the son of a Jewess christian, and of the father heathen (or and of a Gentile father).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 5:10; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:21"> And &#91;the&#93; brethren that were in Lystra and Iconium, yielded good witnessing to him. (And the brothers who were in Lystra and Iconium, testified good things about him, or spoke approvingly about him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GALATIANS 2:3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:20; GALATIANS 5:6"> And Paul would, that this man should go forth with him, and he took, and circumcised him, for (the) Jews that were in those places. For all knew, that his father was heathen. (And Paul wanted, that this man would go forth with him, and he took, and circumcised him, for the Jews who were in those places. For everyone knew, that his father was a Gentile.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:28–29; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:30"> When they passed by (the) cities, they betook to them to keep the teachings, that were deemed of (the) apostles and elder men, that were at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:31; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:47; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 6:7"> And the churches were confirmed in faith, and increased in number each day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:7; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:2–4"> And they passed (through) Phrygia, and the country of Galatia, and were forbidden of the Holy Ghost to speak the word of God in Asia (or but they were forbidden by the Holy Spirit to speak the word of God in Asia).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 8:9; PHILIPPIANS 1:19; GALATIANS 4:6"> And when they came to Mysia, they assayed &#91;or they attempted&#93; to go into Bithynia, and the Spirit of Jesus suffered not them, &#91;or and the Spirit of Jesus suffered them not&#93;, (or but the Spirit of Jesus did not allow them).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 2:12; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:11"> But when they had passed by Mysia, they came down to Troas;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:10–12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:5"> and a vision by night, &#91;or in (the) night&#93;, was showed to Paul. But a man of Macedonia &#91;or Some man of Macedonia&#93; that stood, prayed him (or beseeched him), and said, Go thou into Macedonia, and help us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 2:12–13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:7; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:10–17"> And as he had seen the vision, anon we sought to go forth into Macedonia (or at once we took action to go forth to Macedonia), and were made certain, that God had called us to preach &#91;or to evangelize&#93; to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:8"> And we went by ship from Troas, and came to Samothracia with straight course, &#91;or Soothly we going by boat, or sailing, from Troas, with straight course came to Samothracia&#93;; and the day (pur)suing to Neapolis (or and the following day to Neapolis);</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 2:2; PHILIPPIANS 1:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:6"> and from thence to Philippi, that is the first part of Macedonia, the city colony. And we were in this city some days, and spake together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:14; COLOSSIANS 1:23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:2"> And in the day of sabbaths we went forth without the gate beside the river, where prayer seemed to be &#91;or where prayer was seen to be&#93;; and we sat, and spake to women that came together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:17–18; LUKE 24:45; ISAIAH 50:5"> And a woman, Lydia by name, a purpless of the city of Thyatira, worshipping God, heard; whose heart the Lord opened to give attention to these things, that were said of Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:14; HEBREWS 13:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:33"> And when she was baptized and her house, she prayed (or she beseeched us), and said, If ye have deemed that I am faithful to the Lord, enter ye into mine house, and dwell (there). And she constrained us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:31; DEUTERONOMY 18:9–11; ISAIAH 8:19"> And it was done, when we went to prayer, that a damsel &#91;or a wench&#93; that had a spirit of divination, met us, which gave great winning to her lords in divining, (or who brought much profit to her masters by her divining, or through her fortune-telling).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 14:6; 1ST PETER 2:16; MARK 5:7"> This (woman)(pur)sued Paul and us, and cried, and said, These men be servants of the high God, that tell to you the way of health. (She followed Paul and the rest of us, and cried, and said, These men be servants of the Most High God, who can tell you the way of salvation.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MARK 16:17; LUKE 10:17–19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:6"> And this she did in many days (or And she did this for many days). And Paul sorrowed, and turned, and said to the spirit, I command thee in the name of Jesus Christ, that thou go out of her. And he went out in the same hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:30; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:3"> And the lords of her saw, that the hope of their winning went away, and they took (hold of) Paul and Silas, and led &#91;them&#93; into the chapping, either doom place, to the princes. (And her masters saw, that their hope for more profit went away, and they took hold of Paul and Silas, and led (them) to the market, (or to the seat of judgement), that is, the city square, to the city leaders.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JAMES 4:4; JOHN 15:18–20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:22"> And they brought them to the magistrates, and said, These men disturb &#91;or distrouble&#93; our city, for they be Jews,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ESTHER 3:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:3; JEREMIAH 10:3"> and show a custom, which &#91;it&#93; is not leaveful to us to receive (or which it is not lawful for us to accept), neither do, since we be Romans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 2:2; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:5; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:23–25"> And the people and magistrates ran against them, and when they had rent to pieces the coats of them (or and when they had torn their coats to pieces), they commanded them to be beaten with rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:10; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:9; 1ST SAMUEL 23:22–23"> And when they had given to them many wounds, they sent them into prison, and commanded to the keeper, that he should keep them diligently. (And after they had given them many wounds, they threw them into prison, and commanded the warden of the prison, that he should guard them carefully.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOB 13:27; JOB 33:11; JEREMIAH 29:26"> And when he had taken such a precept, he put them into the inner prison, and restrained the feet of them in a tree (or and restrained their feet in the stocks).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:16–18; PSALMS 119:62; JAMES 1:2"> And at midnight Paul and Silas worshipped, and praised God; and they that were in &#91;the&#93; keeping (or and those who were in the prison), heard them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:31; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:10"> And suddenly a great earth-moving was made, so that the foundaments of the prison were moved. And anon all the doors were opened, and the bonds of all were loosed. (And suddenly there was a great earthquake, so strong that the foundations of the prison were shaken. And at once all the doors were opened, and everyone’s bonds were loosened.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:19; MATTHEW 27:5; JUDGES 9:54"> And the keeper of the prison was awaked (or And the warden of the prison was awakened), and saw the gates of the prison opened, and with a sword drawn out he would have slain himself, and guessed that the men that were bound had fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:15; LUKE 23:34; LUKE 6:27–28"> But Paul cried with a great voice, and said, Do thou none harm to thyself &#91;or Do thou nothing of evil to thyself&#93;, for all we be here. (But Paul cried with a loud voice, and said, Do not harm thyself, for all of us still be here.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:25; ISAIAH 60:14"> And he asked (for) light, and entered &#91;in&#93;, and trembled, and fell down to Paul and to Silas at their feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:37; JOHN 6:27–29; ISAIAH 58:9"> And he brought them withoutforth, and said, Lords, what behooveth me to do, that I be made safe? (And he brought them outside, and said, Lords, what must I do, so that I can be saved?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOHN 1:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:38–39; JOHN 3:36"> And they said, Believe thou in the Lord Jesus, and thou shalt be safe, and thine house. (And they said, Believe in the Lord Jesus, and thou, and thy family, shall be saved.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ROMANS 1:16; COLOSSIANS 1:27–28; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:13–16"> And they spake to him the word of the Lord, with all that were in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:25"> And he took them in the same hour of the night, and washed their wounds. And he was baptized, and all his house anon, (or And he, and his whole family, or and all his household, were baptized at once).</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="ROMANS 15:13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:46; ROMANS 5:11"> And when he had brought them into his house, he set to them a board. And he was glad with all his house, and believed to God. (And when he had brought them into his house, he set a table for them, or he gave them a meal. And he had joy with all his household, and believed in God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:22; PSALMS 76:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:40"> And when (the) day was come, the magistrates sent constables, and said, Deliver thou those men &#91;or Dismiss ye those men&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:33; JOHN 14:27; 1ST SAMUEL 1:17"> And the keeper of the prison told these words to Paul, That the magistrates have sent, that ye be delivered; now therefore go ye out, and go ye in peace. (And the warden of the prison said to Paul, The magistrates have sent word, that ye should be released; and so now go out, and go thy way in peace.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:25–29; PSALMS 58:1–2; MATTHEW 10:16"> And Paul said to them, They sent us men of Rome into prison, that were beaten openly and uncondemned, and now privily they bring us out; not so (fast), but come they themselves, and deliver us out, (or no! let them come here themselves, and release us).</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:29; MATTHEW 21:46; MATTHEW 14:5"> And the constables told these words to the magistrates; and they dreaded, for they heard that they were Romans, (or and they had great fear, when they learned that they were Romans).</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:34; MARK 5:17; DANIEL 6:23"> And they came, and besought them, and they brought them out, and prayed, that they should go out of the city. (And so they came, and found them, and they brought them out, and beseeched them, that they would go away from their city.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 3:2–3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:22"> And they went out of the prison, and entered &#91;in&#93; to (the house of) Lydia. And when they saw (the) brethren, they comforted them, and went forth. (And they left the prison, and went to Lydia’s house. And when they saw the brothers, they strengthened or encouraged them, and then they went forth.)</span>
