<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:8; JOHN 9:8; LUKE 16:20"> And a man, that was lame from the womb of his mother, was borne (there), and was laid &#91;or was put&#93; each day at the gate of the temple, that is said ‘Fair’ (or that is called ‘Beautiful’), to ask (for) alms of men that entered into the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> This (man), when he saw Peter and John beginning to enter into the temple, prayed, that he should take alms. (And when he saw Peter and John entering the Temple, he beseeched, or asked for, some money.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:9–10; JOHN 11:40; LUKE 4:20"> And Peter with John beheld on him, and said, &#91;or Forsooth Peter with John beholding him, said&#93;, Behold thou into us. (And Peter and John looked at him, and Peter said, Look at us.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he beheld into them, and hoped, that he should take somewhat &#91;or something&#93; of them. (And he looked at them, and hoped that he would receive something from them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:38; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:10"> But Peter said, I have neither silver nor gold; but that that I have, I give to thee. In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, rise up, and go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MARK 1:31; MARK 9:27; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:41"> And he took him by the right hand, and lifted him up; and anon (or at once) his legs and his feet were strengthened together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:10; ISAIAH 35:6; PSALMS 107:20–22"> and he leaped, and stood, and wandered (or and walked about). And he entered with them into the temple, and wandered, and leaped, and praised God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:16; LUKE 13:17"> And all the people saw him walking, and praising God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:2; JOHN 5:20; JOHN 9:3"> And they knew him, that he it was that sat at alms at the fair gate of the temple, (or And they knew him, that it was he who sat at the Beautiful Gate of the Temple begging for money). And they were filled with wondering, and astonishing, in that thing that befelled to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:12; JOHN 10:23; LUKE 8:38"> But when they saw Peter and John, all the people ran to them at the porch that was called of Solomon (or that was named for Solomon), and wondered greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 3:5; JOHN 3:27–28; DANIEL 2:28–30"> And Peter saw, and answered to the people, Men of Israel, what wonder ye in this thing? either what behold ye us, as (if) by our (own) virtue, either power, we made this man for to walk? &#91;+or as (if) by our virtue, either piety, we make this man to walk?&#93;(And Peter saw them, and said to the people, Israelites, why wonder ye at this thing? or why look at us, as if by our own power, we made this man to walk?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 22:32; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:32; MATTHEW 27:17–25"> God of Abraham, and God of Isaac, and God of Jacob, &#91;and&#93; God of our fathers, hath glorified his Son Jesus, whom ye betrayed, and denied before the face of Pilate, when he deemed him to be delivered, &#91;or left&#93;, (or to be let go), (or when he judged him, and would have released him).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MARK 1:24; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:52; 1ST PETER 3:18"> But ye denied the holy and the rightful (man), &#91;or Ye forsooth denied the holy and just man&#93;, and asked (for) a manslayer to be given to you. (But ye rejected this holy and just man, and asked for a murderer to be given to you.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:24; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:32"> And ye slew the maker of life, whom God raised from death &#91;or whom God raised from (the) dead&#93;, of whom we be witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:21–22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:9"> And in the faith of his name he hath confirmed this man, whom ye see and know; the name of him, and the faith that is by him, gave to this man full healing &#91;or full health&#93; in the sight of all (of) you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKE 23:34; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:27; JOHN 16:3"> And now, brethren, I wot that by unwitting ye did, as also your princes (did). (And now, brothers, I know, that ye did this by unknowing, or ignorance, as also your leaders did.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:1–12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:22–23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:23"> But God that before-told by the mouth of all (the) prophets, that his Christ should suffer (or that his Messiah should suffer), hath fulfilled so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:38; ISAIAH 44:22; ISAIAH 43:25"> Therefore be ye repentant, and be ye converted, (so) that your sins be done away, (so) that when the times of refreshing shall come from the sight of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:30–36; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:11–16; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:2"> and (then) he shall send that Jesus Christ, that is now preached to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ROMANS 8:21; ISAIAH 1:26; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:11"> Whom it behooveth heaven to receive, into the times of restitution of all things, which the Lord spake by the mouth of his holy prophets from the (beginning of the) world (or of which the Lord spoke by the mouth of his holy prophets from long ago, or from the creation of the world).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:15–19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:37; GALATIANS 4:4"> For Moses said, For the Lord your God shall raise to you a prophet, of your brethren; as me, ye shall hear him by all things &#91;or ye shall hear him upon all things&#93;, whatever he shall speak to you (or whatever he shall say to you).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:19; JOHN 12:48; HEBREWS 10:28–30"> And it shall be, that every man &#91;or every soul&#93; that shall not hear that prophet (or who shall not listen to that prophet), shall be destroyed, &#91;or exiled&#93;, from the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:21; LUKE 24:27; 1ST SAMUEL 3:20"> And all (the) prophets from Samuel and afterward, that spake, told (of) these days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 22:18; GALATIANS 3:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:39"> But ye be the sons of (the) prophets, and of the testament that God ordained to our fathers (or and of the covenant that God ordained to our fathers), and said to Abraham, In thy seed all the families of earth shall be blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTHEW 1:21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:22; JUDE 1:24"> God raised his Son first to you, and sent him blessing you, that each man convert him(self) from his wickedness (or so that each man be turned from his own wickedness).</span>
