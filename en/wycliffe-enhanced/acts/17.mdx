<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:17; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:4"> And by custom Paul entered to them, and by three sabbaths he declared to them of the scriptures, (And as was his custom, Paul went to them, and on three Sabbaths he declared the Scriptures to them,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:28; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:22; LUKE 24:32"> and opened, and showed that it behooved Christ to suffer, and rise again from death, &#91;or to suffer, and rise again from (the) dead&#93;, and that this is Jesus Christ, whom I tell to you. (and expounded upon them, and showed that it behooved the Messiah to suffer, and to rise again from the dead, and that this is Jesus Christ, about whom I tell you.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:43; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:4"> And some of them believed, and were joined to Paul and to Silas; and a great multitude of heathen men worshipped God, and noble women not a few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:26; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:13; ROMANS 16:21"> But the Jews had envy, and took of (or from) the common people some evil men, and when they had made a company, they moved the city. And they came to Jason’s house, and sought to bring them forth among the people (or out to the people).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:19–21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:22"> And when they found them not, they drew Jason and some brethren to the princes of the city, and cried, That these it be, that moved the world, and hither they came, (And when they found them not, they dragged Jason and some of the brothers to the leaders of the city, and cried, These it be, who stirred up the world, and now they have come here,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 23:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:21; JOHN 19:12"> whom Jason received. And these all do against the commandments of the emperor &#91;or against the commandments of Caesar&#93;, and say, that Jesus is another king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 2:3; JOHN 11:48"> And they moved the people, and the princes of the city, hearing these things. (And they stirred up the people, and the leaders of the city, when they heard these things.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:5"> And when satisfaction was taken of Jason, and of others, they let Paul and Silas go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:2; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:13–14"> And anon by night, (the) brethren let &#91;Paul and&#93; Silas go into Berea, &#91;or (the) brethren sent Paul and Silas into Berea&#93;, (or And at once in the night, the brothers sent Paul and Silas to Berea). And when they came thither, they entered into the synagogue of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 34:16; PROVERBS 2:1–5; JOHN 5:39"> But these were the worthier &#91;or the nobler&#93; of them that be at Thessalonica, which received the word with all desire, each day seeking &#91;the&#93; scriptures, if these things had them so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:50"> And many of them believed, and of heathen women honest and men not a few (or and more than a few honourable heathen men and women). &#91;And soothly many of them believed, and of honest heathen women and men not (a) few.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 2:14–16; PROVERBS 28:25; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 6:12"> But when the Jews in Thessalonica had known, that also at Berea the word of God was preached of Paul (or that also at Berea the word of God was preached by Paul), they came thither, moving and disturbing &#91;or distroubling&#93; the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:10; MATTHEW 10:23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:25"> And then anon (the) brethren delivered &#91;or dismissed&#93; Paul, that he should go to the sea; but Silas and Timothy dwelt there. (And then at once the brothers sent off Paul, so that he could go down to the seacoast; but Silas and Timothy remained there.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:5; 1ST THESSALONIANS 3:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:1"> And they that led forth Paul, led him to Athens. And when they had taken a commandment of him to Silas and Timothy (or And when they had received an order from him for Silas and Timothy), that full hieingly they should come to him, they went forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 119:158; 2ND PETER 2:7; PSALMS 119:136"> And while Paul abode them at Athens (or And while Paul waited for them in Athens), his spirit was moved in him, for he saw the city given to idolatry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:1–4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:2–4"> Therefore he disputed in the synagogue with the Jews, and with men that worshipped God, and in the doom place, &#91;or in the chapping&#93;, by all days to them that heard. (And so he disputed in the synagogue with the Jews, and with the men who worshipped God, and also in the city square, or at the market, every day with all those who would listen.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:20–21; COLOSSIANS 2:8; LUKE 11:53"> And some Epicureans, and Stoics, and philosophers disputed with him. And some said, What will this sower of words say? And others said, He seemeth to be a teller of new fiends; for he told to them (of) Jesus, and (of) the again-rising, (or for he told them about Jesus, and about the resurrection).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARK 1:27; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:24"> And they took, and led him to Areopagus, &#91;that is, a common school&#93;, and said, May we know, what is this new doctrine, that is said of thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:23; HOSEA 8:12; 1ST CORINTHIANS 2:14"> For thou bringest in some new things to our ears; therefore we will know, what these things will be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 2:16–17; COLOSSIANS 4:5; EPHESIANS 5:16"> For all men of Athens and comelings (or newcomers) harboured (there), gave attention to none other thing, but either to say, either to hear, some new thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:35; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:16"> And Paul stood in the middle of Areopagus, and said, Men of Athens, by all things I see you as vain (or as futile) worshippers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOHN 4:22; GALATIANS 4:8–9; JOHN 17:3"> For I passed (by), and saw your maumets, &#91;or your simulacra&#93;, (or and I saw your idols), and found an altar, in which was written, To the unknown God. Therefore which thing ye unknowing (or not knowing) worship, (or which ye worship in ignorance), this thing I (shall) show to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST KINGS 8:27; DEUTERONOMY 10:14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:48"> God that made the world and all things that be in it, this (God), for he is (the) Lord of heaven and earth, dwelleth not in temples made with hand &#91;or made by hand&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 50:8–13; GENESIS 2:7; ISAIAH 42:5"> neither is worshipped by man’s hands, neither hath need of anything, for he giveth life to all men, and breathing, and all things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:7–8; GENESIS 3:20; ROMANS 5:12–19"> and made of one all the kind of men to inhabit on all the face of the earth, determining times ordained, and (the) terms of the dwelling &#91;or of the habitation&#93; of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:23–24; DEUTERONOMY 4:7; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:17"> to seek God, if peradventure they feel him, either find (him), though he be not far from each of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOB 12:10; COLOSSIANS 1:17; HEBREWS 1:3"> For in him we live, and move, and be. As also some of your poets said, And we be also the kind &#91;or the kin&#93; of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:25; EXODUS 20:4; PSALMS 115:4–8"> Therefore since we be the kind &#91;or the kin&#93; of God, we shall not deem &#91;or guess&#93; that (a) godly thing is like gold, and silver, either stone, either to (the)(en)graving of craft and thought of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:16; TITUS 2:11–12; LUKE 24:47"> For God despiseth the times of this uncunning, and now showeth to men, that all everywhere do penance; (For God despiseth the times of this unknowing, or this ignorance, and now showeth to all, so that everyone everywhere shall repent;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:31–46; 2ND PETER 3:7; ROMANS 2:16"> for that he hath ordained a day, in which he shall deem the world in equity (or on which he shall judge the world with fairness), in a man in which he ordained, and gave faith to all men, and raised him from death &#91;or raising him from (the) dead&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:19"> And when they had heard (of) the again-rising of dead men, &#91;or Soothly when they had heard (of) the again-rising of (the) dead&#93;, (or And when they had heard of the resurrection of the dead), some scorned, and some said, We shall hear thee again of this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> So Paul went out of the middle of them. (And then Paul went away from their midst.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:19; ROMANS 11:5–6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:48"> But some men drew &#91;or cleaved&#93; to him, and believed. Among which Dionysius Areopagite was, and a woman, by name Damaris, and other men with them.</span>
