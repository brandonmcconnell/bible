<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 27:1; MATTHEW 8:5–13; MATTHEW 27:27"> A man was in Caesarea, Cornelius by name, a centurion of the company of knights, that is said of Italy; (There was a man in Caesarea, named Cornelius, who was a centurion, or the leader, of a group of soldiers, called the Italian Company;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:7–8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:22"> a religious man, and dreading the Lord, with all his household; doing many alms to the people, and praying (to) the Lord (for)evermore. (a religious man, who had fearful reverence for the Lord, with all his family; giving many gifts to the Jewish people, and always praying to the Lord.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DANIEL 9:20–21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:19"> This saw in a vision openly, as in the ninth hour of the day, &#91;or (at) noon&#93;, an angel of God entering in to him, and saying to him, Cornelius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:6; HEBREWS 6:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:31"> And he beheld him, and was adread (or and was afraid), and said, Who art thou, Lord? And he said to him, Thy prayers and thine alms-deeds have ascended up into mind, in(to) the sight of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:38; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:7"> And now send thou men into Joppa, and call one Simon, that is named Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:43; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:13–14; ROMANS 10:14–17"> This is harboured at a man, Simon, (a) currier, &#91;or (a) tanner&#93;, whose house is beside the sea. This shall say to thee, what it behooveth thee to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PHILEMON 1:16; 1ST SAMUEL 14:6–7; MATTHEW 8:9–10"> And when the angel that spake to him, was gone away &#91;or had gone away&#93;, he called two men of his house, and a knight that dreaded the Lord (or and a soldier who had fearful reverence for the Lord), which were at his bidding &#91;or that obeyed to him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:36; PSALMS 119:59–60; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:33"> And when he had told to them all these things, he sent them into Joppa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 1:5; PSALMS 55:17; 1ST SAMUEL 9:25"> And on the day (pur)suing, while they made journey, and nighed to the city, Peter went up into the highest place of the house to pray, about the sixth hour. (And on the following day, while they journeyed, and approached the city, Peter went up to the highest place of the house to pray, at about noon.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:17; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 4:2–3; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:2–4"> And when he was hungered, he would have eaten. But while they made ready, a ravishing of the spirit, &#91;or an excess of soul&#93;, felled on him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EPHESIANS 3:6; JOHN 1:51; ROMANS 16:25–26"> and he saw heaven opened, and a vessel coming down, as a great sheet with four corners &#91;or with four cords&#93;, to be let down from heaven into earth (or to be let down from heaven onto the earth),</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 7:8–9; JOHN 7:37; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:9–11"> in which were all (the) four-footed beasts, and (the) creeping &#91;things&#93; of the earth, and (the) volatiles of (the) heaven(s), &#91;either of the air&#93;, (or and the birds of the air).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 4:31–34; JEREMIAH 35:2–5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:10"> And a voice was made to him, Rise thou, Peter, and slay, and eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:25; EZEKIEL 4:14; DANIEL 1:8"> And Peter said, Lord, forbid &#91;it&#93;, for I never eat any common thing, and unclean (or unclean).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 10:25; MATTHEW 15:11; TITUS 1:15"> And again the second time the voice was made to him, That thing that God hath cleansed, say thou not unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 41:32; JOHN 21:17; 2ND CORINTHIANS 13:1"> And this thing was done by thrice; and anon the vessel was received again into heaven (or and then at once the vessel was taken up again into heaven).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 13:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:7–19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:24"> And while that Peter doubted within himself, what the vision was that he saw, lo! the men, that were sent from Cornelius, sought the house of Simon, and stood at the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:5–6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:11"> And when they had called, they asked if Simon, that is named Peter, had there harbour. (And they called out, and asked if Simon, who was called Peter, was harboured there.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:29; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:4"> And while Peter thought on the vision, the Spirit said to him, Lo! three men seek thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:7–9; ZECHARIAH 2:9–11; ISAIAH 48:16"> Therefore rise thou, and go down, and go with them, and doubt thou nothing, for I sent them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOHN 1:38–39; MARK 10:51; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:29"> And Peter came down to the men, and said, Lo! I am (he), whom ye seek; what is the cause, for which ye be come? &#91;or for which ye have come?&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:12"> And they said, Cornelius, the centurion, a just man, and dreading God, and having good witnessing of all the folk of Jews, took answer of an holy angel, to call thee into his house, and to hear words of thee. (And they said, Cornelius, the centurion, a righteous man, and revering God, and receiving good testimony from all the Jewish people, received a word from a holy angel, to call thee to his house, and to hear some words from thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:45; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:12; 1ST PETER 4:9"> Therefore he led them in, and received (them) in harbour; and that night they dwelled with him. And in the day (pur)suing he rose (or And on the following day he rose up), and went forth with them; and some of the brethren followed him from Joppa &#91;or and some of (the) brethren from Joppa followed him&#93;, (so)that they be witnesses to Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:40; ZECHARIAH 8:20–23; ZECHARIAH 3:10"> And the tother day (or And the next day), he entered into Caesarea. And Cornelius abode them, with his cousins, and necessary friends, that were called together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:10; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:8–9; DANIEL 2:46"> And it was done, when Peter was come in &#91;or when Peter had entered&#93;, Cornelius came meeting him, and fell down at his feet, and worshipped (or and honoured)him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:10; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:8–9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:14–15"> But Peter raised him (up), and said, Arise thou, also I myself am a man, as thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 2:12; JOHN 4:35; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:24"> And he spake with him, and went in, and found many that were come together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOHN 4:9; EPHESIANS 3:6–7; GALATIANS 2:12–14"> And he said to them, Ye know, how abominable it is to a Jew, to be joined either to come to an alien (or to a foreigner); but God showed to me, that no man say a man common, either unclean (or unclean).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 119:60; 1ST PETER 3:15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:19–20"> For which thing I came, when I was called, without doubting. Therefore I ask you, for what cause have ye called me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:3; DANIEL 9:20–21; NEHEMIAH 9:1–3"> And Cornelius said, Today, four days into this hour &#91;or From the fourth day passed till to this hour&#93;, I was praying and fasting in the ninth hour in mine house. And lo! a man stood before me in a white cloth (or a man stood before me in a white cloak),</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:4; DANIEL 9:23; LUKE 1:13"> and said, Cornelius, thy prayer is heard, and thine almsdeeds be in mind in the sight of God (or and thy acts of charity be remembered before God).</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Therefore send thou into Joppa, and call Simon, that is named Peter; this (man) is harboured in the house of Simon (the) currier, beside the sea. This (man), when he shall come, shall speak to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JAMES 1:19; GALATIANS 4:14; 2ND CHRONICLES 30:12"> Therefore anon I sent to thee (or And so at once I sent for thee), and thou didest well in coming to us. Now therefore we all be present in thy sight, to hear the words, whatever be commanded to thee of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="ROMANS 2:11; 2ND CHRONICLES 19:7; DEUTERONOMY 10:17"> And Peter opened his mouth, and said, In truth I have found, that God is no acceptor of persons, (or In truth I have found, that God hath no favourites among people);</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:29; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:2; ROMANS 10:12–13"> but in each folk he that dreadeth God, and worketh rightwiseness, is accept(able) to him. (but among all people and every nation, he who hath fearful reverence for God, and worketh righteousness, is acceptable to him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTHEW 28:18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:36; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 17:14"> God sent a word to the children of Israel, showing peace by Jesus Christ; this is (the) Lord of all things, &#91;or this is the Lord of all men&#93;, (or he is the Lord of everyone).</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKE 4:14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:22"> Ye know the word that is made (or the tidings that be told) through(out) all Judea, and (that) began at Galilee, after the baptism that John preached, (about) Jesus of Nazareth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LUKE 4:18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:22; ISAIAH 61:1–3"> how God anointed him with the Holy Ghost, and virtue; which passed forth in doing well &#91;or which passed forth in well-doing&#93;, and healing all men oppressed of the devil, for God was with him. (how God anointed him with the Holy Spirit, and with power; and he went forth doing good, and healing everyone oppressed by the Devil, for God was with him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LUKE 24:48; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:41"> And we be witnesses of all things, which he did in the country of (the) Jews, and of Jerusalem; whom they slew, hanging on a tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1ST PETER 1:21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:30–31; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:3–4"> And God raised this (man) in (or on) the third day, and gave him to be made known,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="JOHN 14:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:39; JOHN 21:13"> not to all the people, but to witnesses before-ordained of God; to us that ate and drank with him, after that he rose again from death &#91;or after that he rose again from (the) dead&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 5:10; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:1; 1ST PETER 4:5"> And he commanded to us to preach to the people, and to witness, that he it is, that is ordained of God doomsman of the quick and of the dead. (And he commanded us to preach to the people, and to testify, that it is he, who is ordained by God to be the Judge of the living and of the dead.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 13:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:38; ISAIAH 53:11"> To this all (the) prophets bear witnessing &#91;or To this all prophets bear witness&#93;, that all men that believe in him, shall receive remission of sins by his name. (And all the prophets testify about him, that all who believe in him, shall receive forgiveness for their sins through his name.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:8"> And yet while that Peter spake these words, the Holy Ghost felled on all that heard the word. (And while Peter spoke these words, the Holy Spirit fell upon all who heard the word.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:38; GALATIANS 3:13–14"> And the faithful men of (the) circumcision, that came with Peter, wondered, that also into nations the grace of the Holy Ghost is shed out. (And the faithful men of the circumcision, who came with Peter, wondered and were amazed, that the gift of the Holy Spirit was also poured out on heathen men, that is, upon the Gentiles.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:4; MARK 16:17"> For they heard them speaking in languages &#91;or speaking with tongues&#93;, and magnifying God. Then Peter answered (or Then Peter asked),</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:36; ROMANS 10:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:15–17"> Whether any man may forbid water, that these be not baptized, that have also received the Holy Ghost as we? (Would anyone forbid the water, with which these people can be baptized, yea, they who have also received the Holy Spirit like we have?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:38; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:16; GALATIANS 3:27"> And he commanded them to be baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ. Then they prayed him, that he should dwell with them some days.</span>
