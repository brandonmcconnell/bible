<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:30; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:13; 1ST JOHN 2:16"> And on the tother day (or And on the next day), when Agrippa and Bernice came with great desire, &#91;or (with much) pride of state&#93;, and entered into the auditorium, with tribunes and the principal men of the city, when Festus bade, Paul was brought (in).</span>
</div>

(And Festus said, King Agrippa, and all those who be with us, ye see this man, about whom all the multitude of the Jews at Jerusalem beseeched me, and asked, and cried, that he should no longer live.)</span>
