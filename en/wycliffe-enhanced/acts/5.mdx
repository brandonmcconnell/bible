<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:1–3; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:20; MATTHEW 13:47–48"> But a man, Ananias by name, with Sapphira, his wife, sold a field,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:37; 2ND PETER 2:14–15; MATTHEW 23:5"> and defrauded of the price of the field (or and lied about the payment for the field); and his wife was witting. And he brought (only) a part, and laid &#91;it&#93; before the feet of the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:24; ISAIAH 29:15; JOHN 13:2"> And Peter said to him, Ananias, why hath Satan tempted thine heart, that thou lie to the Holy Ghost, and to defraud (God) of the price of the field? (And Peter said to him, Ananias, why hath thou let Satan test thine heart, so that thou lie to the Holy Spirit, and defraud God of the payment for the field?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:3; JOSHUA 7:25–26; 2ND KI 5:25–27"> Whether it unsold was not thine; and when it was sold, it was in thy power? Why hast thou put this thing in thine heart? Thou hast not lied to men, but to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:10–11; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:43; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:13"> Ananias heard these words, and felled down, and was dead &#91;or and died&#93;. And great dread was made on all that heard (about this), (or And great and fearful reverence came upon all who heard about this).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 19:40; 2ND SAMUEL 18:17; DEUTERONOMY 21:23"> And young men rose (up), and moved him away, and bare him out, and buried (him).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And there was made a space of three hours, and his wife knew not that thing that was done, and entered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:2"> And Peter answered to her, Woman, say to me, whether ye sold the field for so much? And she said, Yea, for so much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 10:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:3–4; PSALMS 95:8–11"> And Peter said to her, What befelled to you &#91;or What soothly came together to you, or accorded&#93;, to tempt the Spirit of the Lord? (or What befell to you, to test the Spirit of the Lord?) Lo! the feet of them that have buried thine husband be at the door, and they shall bear thee out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:5"> Anon she felled down at his feet, and died. And the young men entered, and found her dead, and they bare her out, and buried &#91;her&#93; to (or beside) her husband. (At once she fell down at his feet, and died. And the young men returned, and found her dead, and they carried her out, and buried her beside her husband.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:5; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:11–12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:17"> And great dread was made in all the church, and into all that heard (of) these things. (And great and fearful reverence came upon all the church, and upon all who heard about these things.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:11; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:12; HEBREWS 2:4"> And by the hands of the apostles signs (or miracles) and many wonders were made in the people. And all were of one accord in the porch of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:47; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:21; JOHN 12:42"> But no man of others durst (or dared to) join himself with them, but the people magnified them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:47; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:4; ISAIAH 55:11–13"> And the multitude of men and of women believing in the Lord was more increased,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:36; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:11–12; JOHN 14:12"> so that they brought out sick men into (the) streets, and laid (them) in (or on) little beds and couches, (so) that when Peter came, namely the shadow of him should shadow each of them, and they should be delivered from their sicknesses (or and they would be released from their sicknesses). &#91;so that into (the) streets they brought out sick men, and put (them) in little beds and couches, (so) that when Peter came, namely the shadow of him should shadow each of them, and they were delivered from all sicknesses.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MARK 16:17–18; MARK 2:3–4; MATTHEW 15:30–31"> And the multitude(s) of cities nigh to Jerusalem ran, bringing sick men, and (they) that were travailed of unclean spirits (or and those who were troubled by unclean spirits, or demons), which all were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:45; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:5"> But the prince of priests rose up (or But the High Priest rose up), and all that were with him, that is the heresy of (the) Sadducees, and (they) were filled with envy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:3; LUKE 21:12; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:23"> and they laid hands on the apostles, and put them in the common ward &#91;or in the common keeping&#93;. (and they laid their hands on the apostles, and put them in the prison.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:26; PSALMS 34:7; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:7–11"> But the angel of the Lord opened by night the gates of the prison, and led them out, and said, &#91;Forsooth the angel of the Lord by night opened the gates of the prison, and leading them out, said,&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOHN 6:63; JOHN 6:68; 1ST JOHN 5:11–12"> Go ye, and stand ye, and speak in the temple to the people all the words of this life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOHN 8:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:18–19"> Whom when they had heard, they entered early into the temple, and taught. And the prince of priests came (or And the High Priest came), and they that were with him, and called together the council (or the Sanhedrin), and all the elder men of the children of Israel; and sent (men) to the prison, (so) that they should be brought forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And when the ministers came, and found them not, and for the prison was opened, &#91;or Soothly when the ministers came, and, the prison opened, found them not&#93;, they turned again and told (them), (But when the servants, or the officers, came, and opened the prison, and did not find them, they returned, and told them,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 2:4; PSALMS 33:10; DANIEL 6:22–24"> and said, We found the prison shut with all diligence, and the keepers standing at the gates (or and the guards standing at the gates); but we opened (it), and found no man therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:26; JOHN 12:19"> And as the magistrates of the temple, and the princes of (the) priests heard these words, they doubted of them, what was done. (And when the Temple magistrate, and the high priests, heard these words, they wondered, what had become of them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> But a man came, and told to them, For lo! those men which ye have put into prison, be in the temple, and stand (there), and teach the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:21; MATTHEW 26:5"> Then the magistrate went with the ministers, and brought them without violence; for they dreaded the people, lest they should be stoned. (Then the magistrate went with the servants, or the officers, and brought them in without force; because they feared the people, lest they should be stoned.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:7; LUKE 22:66"> And when they had brought them, they set them in the council; and the prince of priests asked them, (And when they had brought them in, they put them before the Council; and the High Priest spoke to them,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:25; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:52; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:10–11"> and said, In commandment we commanded you, that ye should not teach in this name, and lo! ye have filled Jerusalem with your teaching, and ye will bring on us the blood of this man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:19; 1ST SAMUEL 15:24; MARK 7:7–9"> And Peter answered, and the apostles, and said, It behooveth (us) to obey to God, more than to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:13; 1ST PETER 2:24; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:39"> &#91;The&#93; God of our fathers raised Jesus, whom ye slew, hanging in a tree. (The God of our fathers raised Jesus, whom ye killed, by hanging him on a tree, or on the cross.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LUKE 24:47; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:33; HEBREWS 2:10"> God enhanced with his right hand this prince and Saviour, that penance were given to Israel &#91;or for to give penance to Israel&#93;, and remission of sins. (With his right hand God exalted, or raised up, this Leader and Saviour, to give repentance to Israel, and forgiveness for their sins.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JOHN 15:26–27; HEBREWS 2:3–4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:8"> And we be witnesses of these words, and the Holy Ghost (or as is the Holy Spirit), whom God gave to all obeying to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:54; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:37; LUKE 4:28–29"> When they heard these things, they were tormented, and thought to slay them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:3; LUKE 2:46; LUKE 5:17"> But a man rose in the council, a Pharisee, Gamaliel by name, a doctor of the law, a worshipful man to all the people, and (he) commanded the men to be put withoutforth for a while. (But a man stood up in the Council, a Pharisee, named Gamaliel, a teacher of the Law, a man honoured by all the people, and he commanded the men to be put outside for a while.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:26; JEREMIAH 26:19; MATTHEW 27:19"> And he said to them, Ye men of Israel (or Israelites), take attention to yourselves on these men, what ye shall do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:9; 2ND PETER 2:2; GALATIANS 6:3"> For before these days (there was) Theudas, that said himself to be some man, to whom a number of men consented, about four hundred; which was slain, and all that believed to him, were scattered, and brought to nought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKE 2:1–2; PSALMS 7:14–15; MATTHEW 26:52"> After this, Judas of Galilee was in the days of profession, and turned away the people after him; and all how many ever consented to him, were scattered, and he perished. (After this, there was Judas of Galilee during the days of professing, or during the census, and he turned away the people after him; but all whomever consented to him, were scattered, after that he perished.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:13; LAMENTATIONS 3:37; ISAIAH 8:9–10"> And now therefore I say to you, depart ye from these men, and suffer ye them; for if this counsel either work is of men, it shall be undone; (And so now I say to you, go away from these men, and let them be; for if this plan, or this work, is from men, it shall be destroyed, or it shall be ended;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:30; ISAIAH 46:10; MATTHEW 16:18"> but if it is of God, ye may not undo them, lest peradventure ye be found to repugn against God. (but if it is from God, ye cannot stop, or destroy, them, lest perhaps ye be found to fight against God.)&#91;forsooth if it is of God, ye may not undo them. But suffer ye them, lest peradventure ye be found for to repugn God. (but if it is from God, ye cannot stop them. So let them be, lest perhaps ye be found to be fighting with God.)&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:17; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:28; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:17–21"> And they consented to him; and they called together the apostles, and announced (or commanded) to them, (after) that (they) were beaten, that they should no more speak in the name of Jesus, and (then) they let them go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="JAMES 1:2; 1ST PETER 4:13–16; LUKE 6:22"> And they went joying from the sight of the council, that they were had worthy to suffer despising for the name of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:46; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:20; LUKE 21:37"> But each day they ceased not in the temple, and about houses, to teach and preach Jesus Christ. (But each day they did not cease in the Temple, and in their houses, to teach and to preach about Jesus Christ.)</span>
