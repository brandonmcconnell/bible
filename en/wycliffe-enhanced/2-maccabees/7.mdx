<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Forsooth it befell, that seven brethren taken together with the mother, were constrained of (or by) the king, for to taste against the law swine’s flesh; and were tormented with scourgings, and torment made of (or from) bull’s leather.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Forsooth one of them, that was the first, said thus, What seekest thou? and what wilt thou learn of (or from) us? we be ready for to die, more than to break the fathers’ laws of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Therefore the king was wroth, and commanded brazen pans, &#91;or pans of brass&#93;, and brass pots for to be made full hot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And when those anon were made full hot, he commanded the tongue for to be cut off from him that spake first; and when the skin of the head (or his head) was drawn away, he bade (or commanded) both the highest parts of his hands and of his feet for to be cut off, the while the other brethren of him and the mother of him beheld.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And when he was made then &#91;or now&#93; unprofitable by (or in) all things, he commanded fire for to be brought to him, and yet all quick, breathing, or groaning, for to be burnt in the brazen pan; in which when he was long tormented, the others together with the mother, admonished them(selves) together for to die strongly, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The Lord God shall behold truth, and shall give comfort, or give solace, in (or to) us, as Moses declared in before-witnessing of his song, And in (or to) his servants he shall give comfort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Therefore when that first was dead in this manner, they led forth the next for to be scorned; and when the skin of his head was drawn off, with the hairs, they asked, if he would eat, before that he were punished in all the body, by all (the) members by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And he answered by the &#91;country&#93; voice (or language) of (his) fathers, and said, I shall not do it. For which thing, and this in (the) pursuing place, received like torments of (or as) the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And when he was ordained in the last spirit, he said thus, Soothly thou most wicked, or cursed, losest us in this life, but the King of the world shall raise us (up)that be dead for his laws, in again-rising of everlasting life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> After this the third was scorned; and when he was bidden, he proffered soon forth his tongue, and steadfastly held forth his hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and said with trust, Of heaven I wield these limbs, but for the laws of God now I despise these same; for I hope, that I shall receive those &#91;or them&#93; of (or from) him (again).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> So that the king, and they that were with him, wondered on the wisdom of the young man, that he led the torments as nought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And when this was thus dead, they travailed the fourth, and tormented him in like manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And when he was then at the death, he said thus, Well the rather it is need, that men given to death of (or by) men, abide the hope of God, for they shall be raised-again again of (or by) him; forsooth (the) again-rising to life (or resurrection) shall not be to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And when they had brought the fifth, they travailed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And he beheld into him, and said, Thou hast power among men, and though thou be corruptible, thou doest what thou wilt; but do not thou guess, that our kin is forsaken of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But abide thou patiently, and thou shalt see the great power of him, how he shall torment thee, and thy seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> After &#91;this&#93; they brought also the sixth; and this began for to die, and said thus, Do not thou err idly; for we suffer these things for ourselves, sinning against our God, and things worthy of wondering be made in us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> but deem thou not, that it shall be without pain to (or for) thee, that thou hast tempted for to fight against God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Forsooth the mother (of them) is wonderful above manner, and worthy (of) the mind of good men (or worthy to be remembered by all good people), which beheld (her) seven sons perishing under the time of one day, and suffer-ed above manner with good will, for the hope that she had into God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> she admonished each of them by (the) voice (or language) of (her fore)-fathers, &#91;or of (her) country&#93;, and was strongly filled with wisdom, and setted man’s wit to woman’s thought, and said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Sons, I know not how ye appear-ed in my womb; for neither I have given to you spirit, and (or) soul, and (or) life, and I myself joined not to-gether the members of each (of you);</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> but the Maker (out) of nought of the world, that formed the nativity of man, and found (out the) beginning of all, shall yield again to you spirit, and life, with mercy, as now ye despise yourselves for the laws of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Forsooth Antiochus deemed him-(self) for to be despised, and also by (the) despisable voice of a reprover &#91;or despised by voice of the reproving&#93;, and when yet the younger was alive, not only he admonished by words, but with an oath he affirmed to make him rich and blissful &#91;or blessed&#93;, and to have him (for) a friend, translated (or turned) from (the) laws of (his) fathers, and to give to him needful things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> But when the young man was not bowed to these things, the king called the mother, and softly counselled her, that she should be made to the young man into health (or his deliverance).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Forsooth when he admonished her by many words, she promised him for to counsel her son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Therefore she bowed down to him, and scorned the cruel tyrant, and said in (her) country(’s) voice (or language), Son, have mercy on me, that bare thee in (my) womb (for) nine months, and gave (thee) milk by (or for) three years, and nourished or nursed (thee), and fully brought (thee) into this age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> I ask, child, that thou behold to heaven and earth, and all things that be in them, and understand, that God made them (out) of nought, and the kind of men (or and mankind also).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> So it shall be done, that thou dread not this tormentor, but be thou made worthy to (or of) thy brethren, and receive death, that in that mercy doing I receive thee (again) with thy brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> When she said yet these things &#91;or When she yet said these things&#93;, the young man said, Whom abide ye? I obey not to the bidding of the king, but to (the) commandment of the law, that was given to us by Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Forsooth thou, that art made (the) finder of all malice against (the) Hebrews, shalt not escape the hand of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> For we suffer these things for our sins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and though our Lord be a little wroth to (or with) us, for (our) blaming and chastising, but again he shall be reconciled to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Forsooth thou cursed, and most flagitious, or fullest of evil doings, and stirrings, of all men, do not thou vainly be enhanced, that art enflamed by vain hope against his servants;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> for thou hast not escaped yet the doom of Almighty God, and behold-ing all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> For my brethren suffered now a little sorrow, and be made under (the) testament of everlasting life; thou soothly by (the) doom of God shall pay just pains of (or for)(thy) pride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Soothly I, as my brethren, betake my soul and (my) body for the laws of (our) fathers; and I call God to help &#91;or in-calling God&#93;, that more ripely he be made helpful to our folk, and that thou acknowledge with (or after) torments and beatings, that he is God alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Forsooth the wrath of (the) Almighty shall fail, or shall have an end, in me, and in my brethren, which is justly brought in &#91;up&#93; on all our kin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Then the king was kindled with wrath, and was fierce against him more cruelly above all; and bare (it) unworthily, either heavily, himself (to be) scorned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Therefore this (man) was clean (or undefouled), and died, trusting by all things in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Forsooth at the last also the mother was wasted, either died, after the sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Therefore of sacrifices, and over-great cruelties, is enough said.</span>
