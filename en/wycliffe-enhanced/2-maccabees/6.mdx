<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For why two women were accused, that they had circumcised their children; and when they had led them about openly by the city, with &#91;the&#93; infants hanged at their breasts, they casted &#91;them&#93; down by the walls.</span>

<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Therefore I beseech them, that shall read this book, that they dread not for adversities; but areckon they those things that befell to be not to perishing, but to &#91;or for&#93; (the) amending of our kin.</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Soothly these that stood nigh, were moved (al)together by wicked mercy, for eld &#91;or old&#93; friendship of the man, and they took him privily, and prayed that fleshes should be brought, which it was leaveful to him for to eat, (so) that he were feigned to have eaten, as the king commanded, of the flesh of sacrifice;</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> (so) that by this deed he should be delivered from death; and for eld friendship of the man, they did this humanity, or courtesy, in (or to) him.</span>

<div className="bp">
