<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> But a little time after, Lysias, the procurator of the king, and kinsman, and sovereign of offices &#91;or provost of needs&#93;, bare grievously of these things that befell,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and gathered fourscore thousands, and all the multitude of horsemen, and came against (the) Jews, and deemed himself to make the city taken a dwelling to (or for) heathen men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> forsooth to have the temple into winning of money, as other temples of (the) heathen men, and (the) priest-hood set to sale by each year;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and bethought not on the power of God, but in mind, or understanding, he was made without bridle, and trusted in (the) multitude of footmen, and in thousands of horsemen, and in fourscore elephants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Soothly he went into Judea, and came nigh to Bethsura, that was in a strait place, from Jerusalem in (the) space of five furlongs, and fought against that strength (or stronghold).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Soothly when Maccabeus, and they that were with him, knew that (the) strengths (or strongholds) were impugned, with weeping and tears they prayed the Lord, and all the company together, for to send a good angel to the health of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Maccabeus himself took first arms, and admonished others to take together peril with him, and bear help to (or for) their brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And when they went forth to-gether with ready will from Jerusalem, an horseman, that is, an angel in the likeness of an horseman, or knight, appeared going before them in white cloth(es), in golden armours or arms, and flourishing a shaft.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then all together (they) blessed the merciful Lord, and waxed strong in souls (or grew confident); and were ready for to pierce not only men, but and most fierce beasts, and iron walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Therefore they went ready, having an helper of (or from) heaven, and the Lord having mercy on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Soothly by (or in)(the) custom of lions, in fierceness they hurled into (the) enemies, and casted down of them eleven thousands of footmen, and a thousand and six hundred of horsemen. Soothly they turned all (the others) into flight;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> forsooth many of them wounded, escaped naked, but and Lysias him-self foully fleeing escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And for he was not witless, he areckoned with(in) himself the making less done against him, and understood that (the) Hebrews be unovercome, and trust to (the) help of Almighty God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and he sent to them, and promised him(self) to consent to all things that be just, and to compel the king for to be made (a) friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Forsooth Maccabeus granted to the prayers of Lysias, and counselled to profit in all things; and whatever things Maccabeus wrote of (the) Jews to Lysias, the king granted those things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For why epistles were written to (the) Jews from Lysias, containing (or in) this manner. Lysias to the people of (the) Jews, health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> John and Absalom, that were sent from you, betook writs, and asked, that I should &#91;ful&#93; fill those things that were signified by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Therefore whatever things might be brought forth to the king, I expounded, and which the thing suffered, that is, was covenable (or suitable) and just, he granted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Therefore if in needs ye keep faith, also from henceforth I shall endeavour or shall attempt for to be (the) cause of good things to (or for) you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Of other things soothly I com-manded by all words, both to these and to them that be sent of (or from) me, for to speak together with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Fare ye well. In the hundred year and eight and fortieth, in the four and twentieth day of the month Dios-corinthius, that is, in June.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Forsooth the epistle of the king contained these things. King Antiochus to Lysias, brother, health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For our father is translated among (the) gods, we will (or desire) that they that be in our realm do without noise, and give diligence to their things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> we have heard that (the) Jews assented not to the father, to be translated (or brought over) to the custom of (the) Greeks, but will (or desire)&#91;for to&#93; hold their (own) ordinance, and that therefore they ask of us, that their lawful things be granted to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Therefore we will (or desire) that also this folk be quiet, and have ordained and deemed, that the temple be restored to them, (so) that they should do by &#91;or after&#93; the custom of their greater men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Therefore thou shalt do well, if thou shalt send to them, and shalt give (to them)(the) right hand; (so) that, when our will (or desire) is known, they be in (or of) good comfort, and serve to their own profits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Soothly to the Jews the king’s epistle was such. King Antiochus to the senate, or (the) elder men, of (the) Jews, and to (the) other Jews, health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> If ye fare well, so it is as we will, but and we (our)selves fare well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Menelaus came to us, and said, that ye will (or that ye desire to) go down to yours, that be with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Therefore to these that (shall) go together, we give right hands of secureness till to the thirtieth day of the month Xanthicus, that is, April,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> (and) that (the) Jews use their (own) meats, and laws, as and before; and no man of them in any manner suffer dis-ease (because) of these things, that be done by ignorance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Soothly we sent also Menelaus, that shall speak to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Fare ye well. In the hundred year and eight and fortieth, the fifteenth day of the month Xanthicus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> also (the) Romans sent an epistle, having it thus. Quintus Memmius, and Titus Manlius, legates of (the) Romans, to the people of (the) Jews, health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Of these things that Lysias, (the) cousin of the king, hath granted to you, also we granted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Forsooth of which things he deemed to be told again to the king, anon (or at once) send ye some man; and speak ye among you diligentlier, (so) that we deem as it accordeth unto you. For we go to Antioch,</span>
