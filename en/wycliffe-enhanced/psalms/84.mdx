Lord of hosts, how greatly loved be thy tabernacles or how beautiful is thy dwelling place;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 26:9; PSALMS 42:1–2; PSALMS 63:1–2"> my soul coveteth, and faileth into the porches of the Lord. Mine heart, and my flesh; full out joyed into quick God. (my soul desireth, and longeth for, the courtyards of the Lord’s Temple. My heart, and my flesh, rejoiced in the living God.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 90:1; PSALMS 91:1; MATTHEW 8:20"> For why a sparrow findeth an house to itself; and a turtle(dove) findeth a nest to itself, where it shall keep his birds. Lord of virtues, thine altars; my king, and my God. (For there a sparrow findeth a house for itself; and a turtledove findeth a nest for itself, where it shall keep its young. Yea, beside thy altars, Lord of hosts; my King, and my God.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 23:6; PSALMS 65:4; PSALMS 27:4"> Lord, blessed be they that dwell in thine house; they shall praise thee into the worlds of worlds. (Lord, happy be those who live in thy House; they shall praise thee forever.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 28:7–8; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:9; ZECHARIAH 10:12"> Blessed is the man, whose help is of thee; he hath ordained (thy) goings in his heart, (Happy be those whose help is in thee or whose strength is in thee; they have ordained thy ways in their hearts.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 4:17; ROMANS 5:3–5; JOHN 16:33"> in the valley of tears, in the place which he hath set. For the giver of the law shall give blessing, (And as they pass through the dry Baca Valley, they shall find water from a spring. For the Giver of the Law shall give them a blessing.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 4:18; ISAIAH 40:31; 2ND CORINTHIANS 3:18"> they shall go from virtue into virtue; God of gods shall be seen in Zion. (They shall go from strength to strength; and the God of gods shall be seen in Zion.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 59:5"> Lord God of virtues, hear thou my prayer; God of Jacob, perceive thou with ears. (Lord God of hosts, hear thou my prayer; God of Jacob, please listen thou to me.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 2:2; GENESIS 15:1; PSALMS 84:11"> God, our defender, behold thou; and behold into the face of thy christ. (God, our defender, look thou; and look upon the face of thy anointed king.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 27:4; PSALMS 26:8–10; PSALMS 63:2"> For why one day in thine halls is better; than a thousand (elsewhere). I choose to be abject, either an outcast, in the house of my God; more than to dwell in the tabernacles of sinners. (For one day in thy courtyards, is better than a thousand days elsewhere. I would rather choose to be a doorkeeper in the House of my God; than to live in the tents, or in the homes, of the sinners.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 34:9–10; PROVERBS 2:7; MATTHEW 6:33"> For God loveth mercy and truth; the Lord shall give grace and glory. He shall not deprive them from goods, that go in innocence; (For God loveth mercy and faithfulness; and the Lord giveth favour and glory. He will not hold back any good thing, from those who go in innocence or from those who do what is right.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 62:8; JEREMIAH 17:7–8; ISAIAH 30:18"> Lord of virtues, blessed is the man, that hopeth in thee. (Lord of hosts, happy is the person, who trusteth in thee.)</span>
</div>
