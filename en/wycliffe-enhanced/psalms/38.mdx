<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 6:1; ISAIAH 54:8; HEBREWS 12:5–11"> The psalm of David, to bethink on the sabbath. Lord, reprove thou not me in thy strong vengeance; neither chastise thou me in thine ire. (A song by David, to remember the Sabbath. Lord, rebuke thou me not in thy fury; nor chastise thou me in thy anger.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOB 6:4; PSALMS 32:4; PSALMS 64:7"> For thine arrows be fixed in me; and thou hast made steadfast thine hand on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 6:2; PSALMS 51:8; PSALMS 31:9–10"> None health is in my flesh from the face of thine ire; no peace is to my bones from the face of my sins. (There is no health in my flesh, because of thy anger; there is no peace for my bones, because of my sins.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 40:12; EZRA 9:6; MATTHEW 11:28"> For my wickednesses be gone over mine head; as an heavy burden, those be made heavy on me, (or like a heavy weight, they be made heavy upon me).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 69:5; JEREMIAH 8:22; ISAIAH 1:5–6"> Mine healed wounds were rotten, and be broken; from the face of mine unwisdom. (My wounds became rotten, and broken; because of my folly, or my foolishness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOB 30:28; PSALMS 35:14; PSALMS 42:9"> I am made a wretch, and I am bowed down till into the end; all day I entered sorrowful. (I am made a wretch, and I am bent down to the ground; I go about in sorrow all day long.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 21:18–19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:23; PSALMS 102:3"> For my loins be filled with scornings; and health is not in my flesh. (For my loins be filled with sickness; and there is no health in my flesh.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 3:24; PSALMS 32:3; JOB 30:28"> I am tormented, and made low full greatly, (or I am tormented, and brought down so very low); I roared for the wailing of mine heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 10:17; PSALMS 6:6; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:2"> Lord, all my desire is before thee; and my wailing is not hid from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 6:7; PSALMS 88:9; PSALMS 69:3"> Mine heart is troubled in me, my virtue forsook me; and the light of mine eyes, and it is not with me. (My heart is troubled within me, my strength deserted me; and the light in my eyes, it hath also left me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 31:11; JOB 19:13–17; LUKE 23:49"> My friends and my neighbours nighed; and stood (over) against me. And they that were beside me stood afar; (My friends and my neighbours stand far away from me; because of my sickness, or my sores. And even my family members stand far away from me;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 35:20; PSALMS 140:5; 2ND SAMUEL 17:1–3"> and they did violence, that sought my life. And they that sought evils to me, spake vanities; and thought guiles all day. (and they who sought my life, did violence to me. Yea, they who sought evil for me, spoke empty gossip; and they thought up lies and slander about me, all day long.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 39:2; ISAIAH 53:7; PSALMS 39:9"> But I, as a deaf man, heard not; and as a dumb man not opening his mouth. (But I, like a deaf man, did not listen to them; and like a dumb man, I did not open my mouth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="AMOS 5:13; JOHN 8:6; MARK 15:3–5"> And I am made as a man not hearing; and not having reprovings in his mouth. (And I am made like a deaf man; yea, one not speaking any rebuke, or reply.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 39:7; PSALMS 17:6; PSALMS 138:3"> For, Lord, I hoped in thee, (or For, Lord, I trusted in thee); my Lord God, thou shalt hear me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 94:18; DEUTERONOMY 32:35; PSALMS 35:24–26"> For I said, (Hear me), Lest any time mine enemies have joy on me; and the while my feet be moved/and while my feet were moved, they spake great things on me. (For I said, Hear me, lest any time my enemies have joy over me; for when my feet slipped, or stumbled, they spoke many things against me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 38:6; PSALMS 6:6; PSALMS 35:15"> For I am ready to beatings; and my sorrow is ever&#91;more&#93; in my sight. (For I am about to fall; and my sorrow is before me forevermore.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 32:5; PROVERBS 28:13; JOB 33:27"> For I shall tell my wickedness; and I shall think for my sin. (For I shall tell out my wickedness; and I am afraid, when I think of my sin.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 35:19; JOHN 15:18–25; PSALMS 69:4"> But mine enemies live, and they be confirmed on me; and they be multiplied, that hate me wickedly. (But my enemies live, and they be strong against me; yea, there be many who wickedly hate me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 35:12; 1ST JOHN 3:12; JEREMIAH 18:20"> They that yield evils for goods, backbited me; for I followed goodness. (Those who give back evil in return for good, backbite me; because I do what is right, or good.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 22:19; PSALMS 22:24; PSALMS 22:11"> My Lord God, forsake thou not me (or do not thou desert me); go thou not away from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 27:1; PSALMS 40:13; ISAIAH 12:2"> Lord God of mine health; behold thou into mine help. (Lord God of my salvation, or of my deliverance, hasten thou to help me.)</span>
