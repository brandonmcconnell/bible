<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 1:10; EXODUS 34:6–7; PSALMS 40:1–3"> To the victory, in organs, the psalm of David. (For the choirmaster, with stringed instruments, a song by David. Please answer me when I call thee, O God of my righteousness. For when I was in trouble, thou set me free, or at large. So have thou mercy on me; and hear thou my prayer.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:22; HOSEA 4:7; PSALMS 3:3"> Sons of men, how long be ye of heavy heart? why love ye vanity, and seek leasing? <span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOHN 15:16; PSALMS 34:15; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:13–14"> And know ye, that the Lord hath made marvellous his holy man; the Lord shall hear me, when I shall cry to him. <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 9:3; PSALMS 63:2–5; PSALMS 37:4"> thou hast given gladness in mine heart. They be multiplied of the fruit of wheat, and of wine; and of their oil. (But thou hast put more gladness in my heart, than they have ever had; yea, even when their corn, and their wine, were plentiful.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:24; PSALMS 3:5; JOB 11:18–19"> In peace in the same thing; I shall sleep, and take rest.
