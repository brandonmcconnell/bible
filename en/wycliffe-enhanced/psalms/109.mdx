<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 28:1; DEUTERONOMY 10:21; PSALMS 83:1"> To victory, the psalm of David. God, hold thou not still my praising; (For the choirmaster, a song by David. God of my praises, be thou not silent;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 52:4; PROVERBS 6:17; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 6:13"> for the mouth of the sinner, and the mouth of the guileful man, is opened on me. They spake against me with a guileful tongue, (for the mouth of the sinner, and the mouth of the deceitful, were opened against me. Yea, they spoke against me with their lying tongues,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 69:4; PSALMS 35:7; PSALMS 35:20"> and they (en)compassed me with words of hatred; and fought against me without cause. (and they surrounded me with hateful words; and fought against me for no reason.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 38:20; PSALMS 69:12–13; PSALMS 35:7"> For that thing that they should love me (for), they backbited me; but I prayed (for them).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:13; GENESIS 44:4; PSALMS 38:20"> And they setted against me evils for goods (or And they paid me back evil for good); and hatred for my love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 3:1; MATTHEW 5:25; MATTHEW 27:4"> Ordain thou a sinner on him; and the devil stand on his right half. (Ordain thou a sinner over my enemy; and let the devil stand at his right hand.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:9; PROVERBS 21:27; PROVERBS 15:8"> When he is deemed, go he out condemned; and his prayer be made into sin. (When he is judged, let him go out condemned; and let his prayer be made into sin.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 55:23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:16–26; MATTHEW 27:5"> His days be made few; and another take his bishopric. (Let his days be made few; and another take his office.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 22:24; JEREMIAH 18:21; LAMENTATIONS 5:3"> (Let) His sons be made fatherless; and his wife a widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 37:25; GENESIS 4:12–14; ISAIAH 16:2"> His sons trembling be borne over, and beg; and be they cast out of their habitations. (Let his sons and daughters be made vagrants, and go begging; yea, let them be thrown out of their dwelling places.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOB 5:5; JOB 20:18; JOB 18:9–19"> An usurer seek all his chattel; and aliens ravish his travails. (Let an usurer take away all his chattel, or his substance; and let foreigners, or strangers, take all that he hath worked for.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:25; PSALMS 137:8–9; JAMES 2:13"> None helper be to him; neither any be that have mercy on his motherless children. (Let there be no one to help him; nor let anyone have mercy on his motherless children.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:7; PSALMS 37:28; JOB 18:19"> His sons be made into perishing (or Let all his sons and daughters die); (and) the name of him be done away in one generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 20:5; NEHEMIAH 4:5; JEREMIAH 18:23"> The wickedness of his fathers come again into mind in the sight of the Lord; and the sin of his mother be not done away. (Let the wickedness of his forefathers be remembered by the Lord; and let his mother’s sin be not done away,or wiped out.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 34:16; PSALMS 90:8; JOB 18:17"> Be they made ever&#91;more&#93; against the Lord; and the mind of them perish from earth. (Let their sins be before the Lord forevermore; but let the remembrance of these people perish from all the earth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 34:18; JAMES 2:13; PSALMS 37:14"> For that thing that he thought not to do mercy, and he pursued a poor man and a beggar; and to slay a man compunct in heart. (Because he never thought to show mercy, or love, but he persecuted the poor and the needy; and he even killed the broken-hearted.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:2; PROVERBS 14:14; EZEKIEL 35:6"> And he loved cursing, and it shall come to him; and he would not blessing, and it shall be made far from him. (And because he loved to curse others, now let it come to him; and because he delighted not to give anyone a blessing, now let it be made far from him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 73:6; NUMBERS 5:22; MATTHEW 27:3–5"> And he clothed cursing as a cloth, and it entered as water into his inner things; and as oil in his bones. (He clothed himself in cursing, like with a cloak, and it entered into his inner things, like water; yea, like oil into his bones.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 109:29; PSALMS 132:18; PSALMS 109:18"> Be it made to him as a cloth, with which he is covered; and as a girdle, with which he is ever&#91;more&#93; girded. (Let it be made to him like a cloak, with which he is covered; yea, like a girdle, with which he is girded forevermore.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 4:14; 1ST KINGS 2:44; PSALMS 94:23"> This is the work of them that backbite me with the Lord; and that speak evils against my life. (Let this be the reward of those who backbite me with the Lord; yea, those who speak evil against me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 31:3; PSALMS 69:16; PSALMS 79:9–10"> And thou, Lord God, do with me for thy name; for thy mercy is sweet. Deliver thou me, (But thou, Lord God, do with me for the sake of thy name; for thy love is good. Save thou me,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 86:1; PSALMS 40:17; PSALMS 102:4"> for I am needy and poor; and mine heart is troubled within me. (for I am poor and needy; and my heart is troubled within me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EXODUS 10:19; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:15; PSALMS 102:10–11"> I am taken away as a shadow, when it boweth away; and I am shaken away as locusts. (I fade away like a shadow in the evening; and I am shaken off like a bug.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 32:3–4; HEBREWS 12:12; MATTHEW 4:2"> My knees be made feeble with fasting; and my flesh was changed for oil. (My knees be made weak with fasting; and my flesh wasteth away for lack of oil.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 22:6–7; PSALMS 69:19–20; ROMANS 15:3"> And I am made (a) shame to them; they saw me, and moved their heads, (or they saw me, and shook their heads).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 119:86; PSALMS 57:1; PSALMS 69:13"> My Lord God, help thou me; make thou me safe by thy mercy. (My Lord God, help thou me; save thou me in thy mercy or save thou me because of thy love.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOB 37:7; 1ST KINGS 18:36–37; EXODUS 8:19"> And they shall know, that this is thine hand; and (that) thou, Lord, hast done it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:20; JOHN 16:22; PSALMS 109:17"> And they shall curse, and thou shalt bless, they that rise against me, be shamed; but thy servant shall be glad. (And they shall curse, but thou shalt bless, so let those who rise against me, be put to shame; but thy servant shall be glad.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 132:18; PSALMS 35:26; JOB 8:22"> They that backbite me, be clothed with shame; and be they covered with their shame, as with a double cloth. (Let those who backbite me, be clothed with shame; yea, let them be covered with their shame, like with a cloak.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 111:1; PSALMS 35:18; PSALMS 22:22–25"> I shall acknowledge to the Lord greatly with my mouth; and I shall praise him in the middle of many men. (With my mouth I shall greatly thank the Lord; yea, I shall praise him in the midst of many people.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 121:5; PSALMS 16:8; PSALMS 140:12"> Which stood nigh on the right half of a poor man; to make safe my soul from pursuers. (For he standeth close to the right hand of the poor; to save them from their pursuers, or their persecutors.)</span>
