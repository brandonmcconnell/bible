(For the choirmaster, a song by David, when Nathan the prophet came to him, after he had slept with Bathsheba. God, have thou mercy on me; because of thy great love. And in the multitude of thy merciful doings; do thou away my wickedness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST JOHN 1:7–9; PSALMS 51:7; HEBREWS 10:21–22"> Moreover wash thou me from my wickedness; and cleanse thou me from my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:13; ISAIAH 59:12; PSALMS 38:18"> For I acknowledge my wickedness; and my sin is ever&#91;more&#93; against me. (For I confess my sinfulness; yea, my sin is before me forevermore.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 12:13–14; LUKE 15:21; GENESIS 20:6"> I have sinned to thee alone, and I have done evil before thee; that thou be justified in thy words, and overcome when thou art deemed. (I have sinned against thee alone, and I have done evil before thee; so that thou art justified with thy words, and proved right when thou judgest me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ROMANS 5:12; JOB 14:4; PSALMS 58:3"> For lo! I was conceived in wickednesses; and my mother conceived me in sins. (For behold! I was conceived in sinfulness; yea, my mother conceived me in sin.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 15:2; 1ST SAMUEL 16:7; 1ST PETER 3:4"> For lo! thou lovedest truth; thou hast showed to me the uncertain things, and privy things of thy wisdom. (For behold! thou lovedest the truth; and thou hast shown me the uncertain things, and the secret things, of thy wisdom.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST JOHN 1:7; ISAIAH 1:18; HEBREWS 9:13–14"> Lord, sprinkle thou me with hyssop, and I shall be cleansed; wash thou me, and I shall be made white more than snow, (or wash thou me, and I shall be made more white than snow).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 5:17–18; HOSEA 6:1–2; MATTHEW 5:4"> Give thou joy, and gladness to mine hearing; and bones made meek shall full out make joy. (Let me hear the sounds of joy, and of gladness; and then the bones, which thou hast broken and bruised, shall make great joy, or rejoice.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 16:17; MICAH 7:18–19; PSALMS 51:1"> Turn away thy face from my sins; and do away all my wickednesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 11:19; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:17; EPHESIANS 4:22–24"> God, make thou a clean heart in me; and make thou new a rightful spirit in my entrails. (God, make my heart clean in me; and put thou a new and right spirit deep within me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:30; 2ND KI 13:23; ISAIAH 63:10–11"> Cast thou me not away from thy face; and take thou not away from me thine holy spirit. (Do not throw thou me away from before thee; and take thou not away thy holy spirit from me or and take thou not away thy Holy Spirit from me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:10; PSALMS 13:5; ISAIAH 61:10"> Give thou to me the gladness of thine health; and confirm thou me with the principal spirit. (Give thou to me the gladness of thy salvation, or of thy deliverance; and give thou to me a spirit willing to obey thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 22:32; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:18–20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:19"> I shall teach wicked men thy ways; and unfaithful men shall be converted to thee. (I shall teach the wicked thy ways; and the unfaithful shall return to thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 35:28; 2ND SAMUEL 12:9; PSALMS 26:9"> God, the God of mine health, deliver thou me from bloods, or sins; and my tongue shall joyfully sing (of) thy rightfulness. (God, the God of my salvation or the God of my deliverance, save thou me from bloodshed, or from death; and my tongue shall joyfully sing of thy righteousness, or of thy justice.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 63:3–5; HEBREWS 13:15; PSALMS 9:14"> Lord, open thou my lips; and my mouth shall tell (out) thy praising.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 40:6; HOSEA 6:6; HEBREWS 10:5–6"> For if thou haddest would sacrifice, I had given it; truly thou shalt not delight in burnt sacrifice. (For if thou haddest desired a sacrifice, that is, an offering, I would have given it to thee; but truly thou delightest not in burnt sacrifice.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 34:18; ISAIAH 66:2; PSALMS 147:3"> A sacrifice to God is a spirit troubled, that is, (one made) sorry for sin; God, thou shalt not despise a contrite heart, and made meek. (A sacrifice to God is a troubled spirit, that is, one made sorry because of sinning; God, thou shalt not despise a contrite and humble heart.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 102:16; PSALMS 122:6–9; LUKE 12:32"> Lord, do thou benignly in thy good will to Zion; (so) that the walls of Jerusalem (may) be builded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 4:5; EPHESIANS 5:2; MALACHI 3:3"> Then thou shalt take pleasantly the sacrifice of rightfulness, (with) offerings, and burnt sacrifices; then they shall put calves on thine altar. (Then thou shalt be pleased with a righteous sacrifice, yea, with offerings, and burnt sacrifices; and then they shall put calves upon thy altar.)</span>
