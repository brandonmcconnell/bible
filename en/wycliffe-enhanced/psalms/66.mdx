<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 22:27; PSALMS 117:1; PSALMS 96:1–2"> God, all the earth worship thee, and sing to thee; say it psalm to thy name. (God, let all the earth worship thee, and sing to thee; yea, sing they a song to thy name.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 126:1–3; NUMBERS 23:23; PSALMS 66:16"> Come ye and see ye the works of God; fearedful in counsels on the sons of men (or fearful, or awesome, in his counsels toward the sons and daughters of men).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOSHUA 3:16; EXODUS 15:1–21; JOSHUA 3:14"> Which turned the sea into dry land; in the flood they shall pass &#91;through&#93; with foot, there we shall be glad in him. <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 11:4; MATTHEW 28:18; PSALMS 140:8"> The which is Lord in his virtue &#91;into&#93; without end, his eyes behold on folks; they that make sharp be not enhanced in themselves. (Who is the ruler by his might, or his power, forever, and his eyes look on the nations; let not they who rebel against him exalt themselves.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 98:4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:1; JEREMIAH 33:11"> Ye heathen men, bless our God; and make ye heard the voice of his praising. (All ye heathen, bless our God; and make ye the sound of your praises heard.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 121:3; PSALMS 112:6; PSALMS 94:18"> That hath set my soul to life, and gave not my feet into stirring. (He hath given life to my soul or He hath given life to me, and kept my feet from slipping, or stumbling.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 48:10; PSALMS 17:3; 1ST PETER 1:6–7"> For thou, God, hast proved us; thou hast examined us by fire, as silver is examined, (or thou hast assayed us by fire, like silver is proved, or assayed).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 1:13; EZEKIEL 12:13; LAMENTATIONS 3:2–66"> Thou leddest us into a snare, thou puttedest tribulations in our back; (Thou hast led us into a trap, thou hast put troubles upon our backs;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:1–2; ISAIAH 51:23; PSALMS 40:2–3"> thou settedest men on our heads. We passed by fire and water; and thou leddest us out into refreshing. (thou settedest men to ride upon our heads. We passed through fire and water; and then thou leddest us out into refreshing.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 100:4; PSALMS 56:12; ECCLESIASTES 5:4"> I shall enter into thine house in burnt sacrifices; I shall yield to thee my vows, (I shall enter into thy House with burnt sacrifices; I shall pay my vows to thee,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 1:11; JUDGES 11:35–36; PSALMS 18:6"> which my lips spake distinctly. And my mouth spake in my tribulation; (which my lips shall speak clearly, or distinctly. Yea, what I said I would do, when I was in trouble, and you helped me.)</span>

(All ye who fear God, come and listen; and I shall tell you what great things he hath done for my soul./All ye who revere God, come and listen; and I shall tell you what great things he hath done for me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 34:6; PSALMS 145:1; PSALMS 116:12"> I cried to him with my mouth; and I joyed fully under my tongue. (I cried to him with my mouth; and I praised him with my tongue.)</span>
