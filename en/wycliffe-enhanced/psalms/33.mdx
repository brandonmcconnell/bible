<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 32:11; PHILIPPIANS 4:4; PSALMS 147:1"> Ye just men, have fully joy in the Lord; praising (al)together becometh rightful men. (Ye who be righteous, have full out joy, or rejoice, in the Lord; praising him becometh those who be upright, or obedient.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 150:3–6; PSALMS 98:4–5; PSALMS 144:9"> Acknowledge ye to the Lord in an harp; sing ye to him in a psaltery of ten strings. (Praise ye the Lord on a harp; sing ye to him on a ten-stringed lute.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:19; ISAIAH 42:10; 1ST CHRONICLES 25:7"> Sing ye to him a new song; say ye well psalm to him in crying &#91;out&#93;. (Sing ye to him a new song; boldly say ye a psalm to him, and shout out loud.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 12:6; TITUS 1:2; DEUTERONOMY 32:4"> For the word of the Lord is rightful (or For the word of the Lord is true); and all his works be (done) in faithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 11:7; PSALMS 119:64; PSALMS 104:24"> He loveth mercy and doom; the earth is full of the mercy of the Lord. (He loveth righteousness and justice; the earth is full of the love of the Lord.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:3; 2ND PETER 3:5; JOB 33:4"> Heavens be made steadfast by the word of the Lord; and all the virtue of those by the spirit of his mouth. (The heavens were made by the word of the Lord; yea, all the host of them, by the breath from his mouth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 15:8; JOSHUA 3:16; JOSHUA 3:13"> And he gathered together the waters of the sea as in a bouget/as in a bottle; and he setteth deep waters in treasures. (And he gathered the waters of the sea together, like in a bottle; and he put the deep waters in his treasuries, or in his storehouses.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:6–7; PSALMS 96:9–10; PSALMS 22:27"> All earth dread the Lord; soothly all men inhabiting the world be moved of him. (Let all the earth fear the Lord or Let all the earth revere the Lord; let all those who inhabit the world be in awe of him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 1:3; PSALMS 33:6; PSALMS 148:5–6"> For he said, and things were made; he commanded, and things were made of nought, (or he commanded, and everything was made out of nothing!).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 21:11; JOB 5:12–13; ISAIAH 44:25"> The Lord destroyeth the counsels of folks, forsooth he reproveth the thoughts of peoples; and he reproveth the counsels of princes. (The Lord destroyeth the plans of the nations; he thwarteth the plans of the rulers of the peoples.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:21; ISAIAH 46:10; JOB 23:13"> But the counsel of the Lord dwelleth (into) without end; the thoughts of his heart dwell in generation and into generation. (But the plans of the Lord shall stand forever; the thoughts of his heart shall remain for all generations.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 144:15; 1ST PETER 2:9; JOHN 15:16"> Blessed is the folk, whose Lord is his God; the people which he chose into heritage to himself. (Happy is the nation, whose God is the Lord; the people whom he chose for his own inheritance.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOB 28:24; PSALMS 11:4; PSALMS 53:2"> The Lord beheld from heaven; he saw all the sons of men. (The Lord looked down from heaven; he saw all the sons and daughters of men.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST KINGS 8:39; 1ST KINGS 8:27; ISAIAH 57:15"> From his dwelling place made ready before; he beheld on all men, that inhabit the earth, (or he looketh on all those who inhabit the earth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:19; PSALMS 44:21; HOSEA 7:2"> Which made singularly, either each by himself, the souls of them; which understandeth all the works of them. (Who made each one of them individually; and who understandeth all their works, or their deeds.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 44:3; JEREMIAH 9:23; PSALMS 44:6–7"> A king is not saved by much virtue, that is, strength; and a giant shall not be saved in the muchliness of his virtue. (A king is not saved by his large host, or his army; and a mighty man shall not be saved by his great strength.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:31; PSALMS 20:7; PSALMS 147:10"> An horse is false to health; forsooth he shall not be saved in the abundance, either plenty, of his virtue. (A horse is an empty thing for salvation, or deliverance; for it shall not be able to save anyone by the abundance, or the plentifulness, of its strength.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:12; PSALMS 147:11; JOB 36:7"> Lo! the eyes of the Lord be on men dreading him; and in them that hope in his mercy. (Behold! the eyes of the Lord (be) upon those who fear, (or who obey, him); and upon those who hope for his mercy./Behold! the eyes of the Lord (be) upon those who revere him; and upon those who trust in his love.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 37:19; PROVERBS 10:3; JOB 5:19–22"> That he deliver their souls from death; and feed them in hunger. (So that he can save their souls from death; and feed them in times of famine.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:31; PSALMS 27:14; PSALMS 130:5–6"> Our soul suffereth the Lord, that is, abideth patiently his will, (or Our souls wait for the Lord, yea, we wait patiently for his will to be done); for he is our helper and defender.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 13:5; ZECHARIAH 10:7; PSALMS 30:10–12"> For our heart shall be glad in him; and we shall have hope in his holy name. (And our hearts shall be glad in him; for we have trust in his holy name.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 13:5; PSALMS 119:76; MATTHEW 9:29"> Lord, thy mercy be made on us; as we hoped in thee. (Lord, let thy love rest upon us; for we have trusted in thee.)</span>
