<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 18:2; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:8–9; PSALMS 62:6"> For why he is both my God, and mine health; mine up-taker, I shall no more be moved. (For he is both my God, and my deliverer; yea, my defender, and I shall not be shaken, or defeated.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:14; PSALMS 140:2; PSALMS 82:2"> How long fall ye on a man? all ye slain; as to a wall bowed, and as a wall of stone without mortar cast down. <span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 55:21; PSALMS 28:3; PSALMS 5:9"> Nevertheless they thought to put away my price, I ran in thirst; with their mouth they blessed, and in their heart they cursed. <span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MICAH 7:7; PSALMS 27:13–14; LAMENTATIONS 3:24–26"> Nevertheless, my soul, be thou subject to God; for my patience is of him. (Nevertheless, my soul, be thou made subject under God; for my hope of deliverance is in him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 112:6; PSALMS 62:2; PSALMS 16:8"> For he is my God, and my saviour; mine helper, I shall not pass out. (For he is my God, and my saviour; my helper, and I shall not be moved, or shaken.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 3:3; ISAIAH 26:4; PSALMS 94:22"> Mine health, and my glory is in God; God is the giver of mine help, and mine hope is in God. (My salvation, or my deliverance, and my glory be in God; God is the giver of my help, and my trust is in God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:6; LAMENTATIONS 2:19; ISAIAH 26:4"> All the gathering together of the people, hope ye in God, pour ye out your hearts before him; God is our helper &#91;into&#93; without end (All the gathering together of the people, trust ye in God, pour ye out your hearts before him; God shall be our helper forever.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 39:5; ISAIAH 40:15; ISAIAH 40:17"> Nevertheless the sons of men be(all in) vain; the sons of men be liars in balances, that they deceive of vanity into the same thing.
