<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:17; PROVERBS 14:21; 2ND CORINTHIANS 9:8–14"> For victory, the song of David. Blessed is he that understandeth of the needy man and poor; the Lord shall deliver him in the evil day. (For the choirmaster, a song by David. Happy is he who hath concern for the poor and the needy; the Lord shall save him on the evil day or the Lord shall rescue him in his time of trouble.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 45:4–5; PSALMS 27:12; 1ST TIMOTHY 4:8"> The Lord keep him, and quicken him, and make him blessful in the land; and betake not him into the will of his enemies. (The Lord shall keep him safe, and alive, and make him happy in the land; and he shall not deliver him unto the power, or the will, of his enemies.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND KI 20:5–6; PSALMS 73:26; PHILIPPIANS 2:26–27"> The Lord (shall) bear help to him on the bed of his sorrow; thou hast oft turned all his bedstraw in his sickness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 103:3; JAMES 5:15–16; 2ND CHRONICLES 30:18–20"> I said, Lord, have thou mercy on me; heal thou my soul, for I have sinned against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:7; PSALMS 102:8; PSALMS 38:12"> Mine enemies said evils to me; When shall he die, and his name shall perish?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 12:2; LUKE 11:53–54; PROVERBS 26:24–26"> And if he entered for to see (me), he spake vain things (to me); his heart gathered wickedness to himself. He went withoutforth; and spake to the same thing/and spake to the same end. (And when he entered to see me, he spoke to me of empty, or useless, things; and his heart gathered wickedness unto itself. And then he went withoutforth; and spoke the same thing everywhere he went.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:28; PSALMS 56:5–6; PSALMS 31:13"> All mine enemies backbited privily against me; against me they thought evils to me. (All my enemies secretly backbite me; they think up evil things to do to me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 71:11; JOB 2:7–8; MATTHEW 27:41–46"> They ordained an evil word against me; Whether he that sleepeth, shall not lie to, that he rise again? (They ordained an evil word against me; and they said, He who sleepeth, now that he lieth down, shall not rise up again.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 13:18; PSALMS 55:12–14; MATTHEW 26:23"> For why the man of my peace, in whom I hoped, he that ate my loaves; made great deceit on me. (For my friend, in whom I trusted, he who ate my bread; brought forth great deceptions, or lies, against me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 21:8–10; LUKE 19:27; PSALMS 18:37–42"> But thou, Lord, have mercy on me, and raise me (up) again; and I shall yield to them (or and I shall repay them).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 2:15; PSALMS 124:6; PSALMS 147:11"> In this thing I knew, that thou wouldest me; for mine enemy shall not have joy on me. (And by this I shall know that thou delightest in me; and that my enemy shall not have any joy over me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 36:7; PSALMS 73:23–24; PSALMS 37:17"> Forsooth thou hast taken me up for (mine) innocence; and hast confirmed me in thy sight &#91;into&#93; without end. (For thou hast upheld me because of my innocence; and will keep me before thee forever.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 72:18–19; EPHESIANS 1:3; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 4:8"> Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, from the world, and into the world; be it done, be it done. (Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, forever and ever; amen, amen.)</span>
