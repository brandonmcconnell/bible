<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 43:1; PSALMS 119:154; JEREMIAH 51:36"> To David. &#91;The psalm of David.&#93; Lord, deem thou them, that annoy me; overcome thou them, that fight against me. (A song by David. Lord, judge thou them, who harm me; overcome thou them, who fight against me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 91:4; EXODUS 15:3; ISAIAH 42:13"> Take thou armours and shield; and rise up into help to me. (Take thou up arms, or weapons, and shield; and rise up to help me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 12:2; PSALMS 27:2; PSALMS 51:12"> Shed out the sword/Hold out the sword, and close (al)together (the way) against them that pursue me; say thou to my soul, I am thine health. (Draw out the sword, and close up the way against those who persecute me; say thou to me, I am thy salvation, or thy deliverance.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 70:2–3; JEREMIAH 46:5; PSALMS 35:26"> They that seek my life; be shamed, and ashamed. They that think evils to me; be turned away backward, and be they shamed. (Let those who seek my life; be shamed, and ashamed. Let those who plot evil against me; be turned back, and be shamed.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOB 21:18; ISAIAH 29:5; ISAIAH 37:36"> Be they made as dust before the face of the wind; and the angel of the Lord make them strait. (Let them be made like the dust in the wind; and let the angel of the Lord pursue them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:12; PSALMS 73:18; JEREMIAH 13:16"> Their way be made darkness, and sliderness; and the angel of the Lord pursue them. (Let their way be made dark, and slippery; and may the angel of the Lord strike them down.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOHN 15:25; PSALMS 140:5; PSALMS 9:15"> For without cause they hid to me the death of their snare; in vain they despised my soul. (For no reason, they hid their deadly snare for me or they hid their snare for me in a pit; for no reason, they despised me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 141:9–10; PROVERBS 5:22; LUKE 21:34"> The snare which he knoweth not come to him, and the taking which he hid take him; and fall he into the snare in that thing. (But let the snare catch him unawares, or by surprise, yea, let the trap which he himself hid, catch him; and let him fall to his own destruction in that thing.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 61:10; PSALMS 58:10–11; LUKE 1:46–47"> But my soul shall fully have joy in the Lord; and shall delight on his health. (But my soul shall have full out joy, or shall rejoice, in the Lord; and it shall delight in his salvation, or in his deliverance.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 140:12; EXODUS 15:11; PSALMS 51:8"> All my bones shall say, Lord, who is like thee? Thou deliverest a poor man from the hand of his stronger; a needy man and poor from them that diversely ravish him. (All my bones said, Lord, who is like thee? Thou rescuest the poor from the hands of those who be stronger; yea, the poor and the needy from those who oppress them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 27:12; MATTHEW 26:59–60; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:5–6"> Wicked witnesses rising (up) asked me things, which I knew not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOHN 10:32; JEREMIAH 18:20; PSALMS 38:20"> They yielded to me evils for goods; barrenness to my soul. (They gave me evil for good; making my soul barren.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 69:10–11; JOB 30:25; MATTHEW 5:44"> But when they were dis-easeful to me; I was clothed in an hair-shirt. I meeked my soul in fasting; and my prayer shall be turned (again) into my bosom. (But yet when they were sick; I was clothed in a hair-shirt. I humbled myself with fasting; and I prayed to the Lord for them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKE 19:41–42; 2ND SAMUEL 1:11–12; GENESIS 24:67"> I pleased so as our neighbour, as our brother; I was made meek, so as mourning and sorrowful. (I went around, like he was my neighbour, or my brother; I was humbled, like one mourning and sorrowful for his own mother.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MARK 14:65; PSALMS 7:2; PSALMS 35:25–26"> And they were glad, and came together against me; torments were gathered on me, and I knew it not. They were scattered, and not compunct; (But when I was in torment, they were glad, and came together against me; yea, they were gathered together against me, and I knew not why. They tore me apart, and would not stop;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 2:16; JOB 16:9; PSALMS 37:12"> they tempted me, they scorned me with mocking, they gnashed on me with their teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HABAKKUK 1:13; PSALMS 22:20–21; PSALMS 57:4"> Lord, when thou shalt behold, restore thou my soul from the wickedness of them; mine one alone from lions. (Lord, how long shalt thou but look at me? rescue thou me from their wicked assailings, yea, save my life from these lions.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HEBREWS 2:12; PSALMS 22:22–25; PSALMS 116:18"> I shall acknowledge to thee in a great church; I shall praise thee in a firm people. (I shall give thanks to thee in the great congregation; I shall praise thee before many people.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 69:4; JOHN 15:25; PROVERBS 6:13"> They that be adversaries wickedly to me, have not joy on me; that hate me without cause, and beckon with eyes. (Let not those who be my adversaries wickedly have joy over me; let not those who hate me for no reason, leer at me with delight.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 120:5–7; MATTHEW 12:19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:15"> For soothly they spake (not) peaceably to me; and they speaking in wrathfulness of &#91;the&#93; earth, thought guiles. (For they do not speak peacefully; but they tell forth all kinds of lies, against those, who live quietly upon the earth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 22:13; PSALMS 40:15; PSALMS 70:3"> And they made large their mouth on me; they said, Well, well! our eyes have seen. (And they opened their mouths wide against me; and they said, Well, well! now our eyes have seen it all!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 3:7; PSALMS 10:1; PSALMS 28:1"> Lord, thou hast seen, be thou not still; Lord, depart thou not from me. (Lord, thou hast seen all of this, so keep thou not silent; Lord, go thou not away from me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 44:23; PSALMS 7:6; ISAIAH 51:9"> Rise up, and give attention to my doom; my God and my Lord, behold into my cause. (Rise up, and give me justice; my God and my Lord, (please plead) my case!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 43:1; PSALMS 26:1; PSALMS 7:8"> My Lord God, deem thou me by thy rightfulness; and have they not joy on me. (My Lord God, judge thou me by thy righteousness; and do not let them have joy over me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 2:16; PSALMS 124:3; 2ND SAMUEL 20:19"> Say they not in their hearts, Well, well, to our soul; neither say they, We shall devour him. (Let them not say in their hearts, Well, well; nor let them say, We have devoured him!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 38:16; JOB 19:5; PSALMS 132:18"> Shame they, and dread they together; that joy for mine evils. Be they clothed with shame and dread; that speak evil things on me. (Let them all be ashamed, and fearful; who take joy over my suffering. Let them be clothed with shame and fear; who speak evil against me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:40–41; PSALMS 149:4; PSALMS 40:16"> Have they full joy, and be they glad, that will my rightfulness; and say they ever&#91;more&#93;, The Lord be magnified, which desire the peace of his servant. (Have they full out joy,or rejoice, and be they glad, who desire my vindication; and let them say forevermore, The Lord be magnified, who delighteth in his servant’s prosperity.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 145:21; PSALMS 71:24; PSALMS 145:5"> And my tongue shall bethink thy rightfulness; all day thy praising. (And my tongue shall speak of thy righteousness, or thy justice, and thy praises; all day long.)</span>
