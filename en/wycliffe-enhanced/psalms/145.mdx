My God, king, I shall enhance thee; and I shall bless thy name into the world, and into the world of world. (A song by David. My God and my King, I shall exalt thee; and I shall bless thy name forever and ever.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:15; PSALMS 71:6; PSALMS 119:164"> By all days I shall bless thee; and I shall praise thy name into the world, and into the world of the world. (Every day I shall bless thee; and I shall praise thy name forever and ever.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 147:5; ROMANS 11:33; JOB 5:9"> The Lord is great, and worthy to be praised full much; and none end there is of his greatness. (The Lord is great, and worthy to be greatly praised; and there is no end to his greatness.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 78:3–7; PSALMS 71:18; DEUTERONOMY 6:7"> Generation and generation shall praise thy works; and they shall pronounce, either tell (a) far, thy power. (One generation shall praise thy works unto the next; and they shall declare, or shall tell of, thy power.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ISAIAH 12:4; PSALMS 119:27; PSALMS 72:18"> They shall speak (of) the magnificence of the glory of thine holiness; and they shall tell (of all) thy marvels (or and they shall declare all thy marvellous deeds).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:14; DANIEL 3:28–29; PSALMS 107:31–32"> And they shall say (of) the strength of thy fearedful things; and they shall tell (of) thy greatness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 63:7; PSALMS 36:10; 2ND CORINTHIANS 9:11–12"> They shall bring forth the mind of the abundance of thy sweetness; and they shall tell with full out joying (of) thy rightfulness. (They shall bring forth the remembrance of the abundance of thy goodness; and they shall sing with rejoicing about thy righteousness.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:18; PSALMS 103:8; PSALMS 86:5"> The Lord is a merciful doer and merciful in will (or The Lord is a giver of mercy, or of love, and merciful in will); patient, and much merciful.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="NAHUM 1:7; PSALMS 100:5; MATTHEW 5:45"> The Lord is sweet in all things; and his merciful doings be above all his works. (The Lord is good to all; and his merciful doings be over, or upon, all his creatures.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 19:1; PSALMS 103:22; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:9–12"> Lord, all thy works acknowledge to thee; and thy saints bless thee. (Lord, all thy creatures shall praise thee; and thy saints shall bless thee.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 9:6–7; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:11–12; PSALMS 93:1–2"> They shall say &#91;of&#93; the glory of thy realm; and they shall speak (of) thy power. (They shall speak of the glory of thy kingdom; and they shall tell of thy power.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 12:10; PSALMS 105:1; MATTHEW 28:18"> That they make thy power known to the sons of men (or So that they make known thy power to all the people); and the glory of the magnificence of thy realm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 1:17; DANIEL 7:14; PSALMS 10:16"> Thy realm is the realm of all worlds; and thy lordship is in all generation and into generation, (or Thy kingdom is a kingdom forever; and thy lordship, or thy rule, is for all generations). The Lord is faithful in all his words; and holy in all his works.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 146:8; PSALMS 37:24; PSALMS 94:18"> The Lord lifteth up all that fall down; and raiseth up all men hurtled down. (The Lord lifteth up all who fall down; and raiseth up all who be hurtled down.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 136:25; PSALMS 104:27; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:25"> Lord, the eyes of all beasts hope in thee; and thou givest the meat of them in covenable time. (Lord, the eyes of all look with hope to thee; and thou givest them their food at the proper time.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 104:28; PSALMS 107:9; PSALMS 132:15"> Thou openest thine hand; and thou &#91;ful&#93; fillest each beast with blessing. (Thou openest thy hand; and thou fulfillest each living creature with blessing or with what they need, or desire.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:4; ZEPHANIAH 3:5; ISAIAH 45:21"> The Lord is just in all his ways; and holy in all his works.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:12–13; PSALMS 34:18; DEUTERONOMY 4:7"> The Lord is nigh to all that inwardly call him; to all that inwardly call him in truth. (The Lord is near, or close, to all who call to him; to all who call to him in truth, or with sincerity.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 37:4; EPHESIANS 3:16–20; JOHN 15:7"> He shall do the will of them, that dread him; and he shall hear the beseeching of them, and he shall make them safe. (He shall fulfill the desires of those who fear him or of those who revere him; he shall hear their pleas, and he shall save them.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 20:6; JOHN 10:27–29; ROMANS 8:28–30"> The Lord keepeth all men loving him; and he shall lose all sinners. (The Lord keepeth safe all those who love him; but he shall destroy all the sinners.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 71:8; PSALMS 51:15; PSALMS 150:6"> My mouth shall speak the praising of the Lord; and each man bless his holy name into the world, and into the world of world. (My mouth shall declare the praises of the Lord; let everyone bless his holy name forever and ever.)</span>
</div>
