<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 89:1; PSALMS 71:22–23; PSALMS 145:7"> The psalm of David. Lord, I shall sing to thee; mercy and doom. I shall sing, (A song by David. Lord, I shall sing of love and justice or I shall sing of mercy and judgement. I shall sing unto thee,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 119:106; 1ST KINGS 9:4; 1ST TIMOTHY 3:4–5"> and I shall understand in a way without wem; when thou shalt come to me. I went perfectly in the innocence of mine heart; in the middle of mine house. (and I shall go on the way without blemish, or without fault; but when shalt thou come to me? I shall go about with purity in the midst of my house; yea, in the innocence of my heart.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 119:37; JOB 31:1; 2ND SAMUEL 11:2–3"> I setted not forth before mine eyes an unjust thing; I hated them that made trespassings. (I shall not set any impure thing before my eyes; I hate those who trespass, but I know, that that shall not cleave to me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:20; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:14–16; MATTHEW 7:23"> A shrewd heart cleaved not to me; I knew not a wicked man bowing away from me. (I shall turn away from anyone with a depraved heart; and I shall not have any dealings with the wicked.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 15:3; PSALMS 18:27; PROVERBS 30:13"> I pursued him that backbited privily his neighbour. With the proud eye and an heart unable to be filled; I ate not with this. (I shall silence anyone who privately, or secretly, backbiteth his neighbour. And I shall not eat with anyone who hath a proud eye, and a heart unable to be filled, or ever satisfied.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 119:63; LUKE 12:43–44; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:3"> Mine eyes were to the faithful men of earth, that they sit with me; he that went in a way without wem, ministered to me. (But I look favourably upon those who be faithful, or loyal, and they shall sit with me; and he who goeth on the way without blemish, or without fault, shall serve me or shall be my servant.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 4:10–12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:1–10; PSALMS 52:2"> He that doeth pride, shall not dwell in the middle of mine house; he that speaketh wicked things, served not in the sight of mine eyes. (He who hath pride, shall not live in the midst of my house; he who speaketh wicked things, shall not serve me or shall not be my servant.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 75:10; JEREMIAH 21:12; PROVERBS 16:12"> In the morrowtide I killed all the sinners of earth; that I should lose from the city of the Lord all men working wickedness. (In the morning or Morning after morning, I shall kill all the sinners of the land; yea, I shall destroy all who do wickedness in the city of the Lord.)</span>
