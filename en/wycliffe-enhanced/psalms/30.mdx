<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 25:2; DANIEL 4:37; PSALMS 35:19"> The psalm of &#91;the&#93; song, for the hallowing of the house of David. Lord, I shall enhance thee, for thou hast up-taken me; and thou delightedest not mine enemies on me. (A psalm, yea, a song by David, for the dedication of the House or the Temple. Lord, I shall exalt thee, for thou hast taken me up or for thou hast lifted me up; and thou hast not let my enemies have delight, or to gloat, over me.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 103:3–4; 2ND KI 20:5; PSALMS 147:3"> My Lord God, I cried to thee; and thou madest me whole.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 86:13; PSALMS 16:10; JONAH 2:4–6"> Lord, thou leddest out my soul from hell; thou savedest me from them that go down into the pit. (Lord, thou hast led me up from Sheol, or the land of the dead; thou hast pulled me out from among those who go down into the pit.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 97:12; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 4:8; 1ST CHRONICLES 16:4"> Ye saints of the Lord, sing to the Lord; and acknowledge ye to the mind of his holiness (or and give ye thanks at the remembrance of his holiness).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ISAIAH 54:7–8; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:17; PSALMS 126:5–6"> For ire is in his indignation; and life is in his will. Weeping shall dwell at eventide; and gladness at the morrowtide. (For there is anger in his indignation; but there is life in his favour or in his good will. Weeping shall remain through the evening; but gladness shall come in the morning.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 12:19; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:7; PSALMS 119:117"> Forsooth I said in my plenty; I shall not be moved &#91;into&#93; without end. (But I said in the midst of my plenty, or of my abundance, I shall never be shaken, or defeated.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 104:29; PSALMS 143:7; PSALMS 30:5"> Lord, in thy will; thou hast given virtue to my fairness. Thou turnedest away thy face from me; and I am made troubled. (Lord, by thy favour, thou hast protected me; yea, like a mountain stronghold. But then thou hast turned thy face away from me; and I was greatly troubled.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:6–7; PSALMS 34:6; PSALMS 130:1–2"> Lord, I shall cry to thee; and I shall pray to my God. (Lord, I cried to thee; yea, I prayed to my God.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 6:5; PSALMS 115:17–18; ISAIAH 38:18"> What profit is in my blood; while I go down into corruption? Whether dust shall acknowledge to thee; either it shall tell thy truth? (And I said, What profit is there in my death; if I go down into the pit? Shall the dust then praise thee? or can it tell about thy truth?)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 54:4; PSALMS 143:1; PSALMS 143:7–9"> The Lord heard, and had mercy on me; the Lord is made mine helper. (Hear me, Lord, and have mercy on me; Lord, be thou my helper.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:4; JEREMIAH 31:13–14; ECCLESIASTES 3:4"> Thou hast turned my wailing into joy to me; thou hast rent my sackcloth, and hast (en)compassed me with gladness. (And now, thou hast turned my wailing into joy for me; thou hast torn off my sackcloth, and surrounded me with gladness.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 145:2; PSALMS 146:1–2; PSALMS 44:8"> That my glory sing to thee, and I be not compunct; my Lord God, I shall acknowledge to thee &#91;into&#93; without end. (So that my spirit may sing to thee, and I shall never be silent; my Lord God, I shall thank thee forever.)</span>
</div>
