<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ISAIAH 51:4; ISAIAH 55:3; PSALMS 74:1"> The learning of Asaph. My people, perceive ye my law; bow your ear into the words of my mouth. (A teaching by Asaph. My people, listen ye to my teaching; bow down your ear to the words of my mouth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:34–35; MARK 4:34; MATTHEW 13:11–13"> I shall open my mouth in parables; I shall speak perfect reasons from the beginning (or I shall speak perfect reasoning from the old days).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 44:1; EXODUS 13:14–15; EXODUS 12:26–27"> How great things have we heard, and we have known those; and our fathers told to us. (What great things we have heard, and we have known; and our fathers told us.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 11:19; PSALMS 145:4–6; DEUTERONOMY 4:9"> Those be not hid from the sons of them; in another generation. And they told the praisings of the Lord, and the virtues of him; and his marvels, which he did. (We will not hide these things from their sons and daughters; yea, from the generations to come. We shall tell out the praises of the Lord, and his strength, or his power; and the marvellous deeds which he did.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 6:7; EPHESIANS 6:4; DEUTERONOMY 4:45"> And he raised witnessing in Jacob; and he setted law in Israel. How great things commanded he to our fathers, to make those known to their sons; (And he raised up a testimony in Jacob; and he set a law in Israel. He commanded to our fathers to make these things known to their sons and daughters;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 102:18; JOEL 1:3; ESTHER 9:28"> that another generation know. Sons, that shall be born, and shall rise up; shall tell out to their sons. (so that another generation would know them. And so that the sons and daughters who shall be born, shall rise up; and shall tell these things to their sons and daughters.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:29; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:12; 1ST JOHN 5:3"> That they set their hope in God, and forget not the works of God; and that they seek his commandments/and that they search his commandments. (So that they put their trust in God, and forget not the works of God; and that they obey his commandments.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 78:37; EXODUS 32:9; 2ND KI 17:14"> Lest they be made a shrewd generation; and (one) stirring to wrath, as the fathers of them. A generation that (ad)dressed not his heart; and his spirit was not believed with God. (Lest they be made a depraved, and a rebellious generation; like their fathers. For they were a generation who did not direct their hearts towards him; and their spirits were not steadfast with God, or firmly fixed on God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:41–44; 1ST SAMUEL 31:1; PSALMS 78:57"> The sons of Ephraim, bending a bow, and sending arrows; were turned (aback) in the day of battle. (The sons of Ephraim, bending a bow, and sending arrows; turned and ran away on the day of battle.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 31:20; DEUTERONOMY 31:16; NEHEMIAH 9:26–29"> They kept not the testament of God; and they would not go in his law. (They kept not the covenant of God; and they would not follow, or obey, his Law.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 106:13; DEUTERONOMY 32:18; PSALMS 106:21–22"> And they forgat his benificences; and his marvels, which he showed to them. (And they forgot his good gifts; and his marvellous deeds, which he showed to them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:22; EZEKIEL 30:14; ISAIAH 19:11"> He did marvels before the fathers of them, in the land of Egypt; in the field of Tanis. (Yea, he did marvellous deeds before their forefathers, in the land of Egypt; on the plain of Zoan.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 14:21; EXODUS 15:8; PSALMS 66:6"> He brake the sea, and led them through it; and he ordained the waters as in a bouget (or in a bottle). (He broke the sea apart, and led them through it; he made the waters to stand up like walls.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 105:39; EXODUS 14:24; EXODUS 13:21–22"> And he led them forth in a cloud of the day; and all night in the lightening of fire. (And he led them forth by a cloud during the day; and all night long by the light of a fire.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 105:41; NUMBERS 20:11; EXODUS 17:6"> He brake a stone in desert; and he gave water to them as in a much depth. (He broke open a rock in the wilderness; and he gave water to them as if out of the great depths of the ocean.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 20:8; NUMBERS 20:10–11; DEUTERONOMY 8:15"> And he led water out of the stone; and he led forth waters as floods. (Yea, he brought forth water out of that rock; and it came forth like a river.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 63:10; HEBREWS 3:16–19; DEUTERONOMY 9:12–22"> And they putted yet to do sin against him; they excited the high God into ire, in a place without water. (And still they sinned against him; they incited the Most High God to anger, in a place without water.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 6:16; NUMBERS 11:4; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:9"> And they tempted God in their hearts; that they asked meats to their lives. (And they tempted God in their hearts; by demanding food for their hunger.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:5; NUMBERS 11:4; EXODUS 16:3"> And they spake evil of God; they said, Whether God may make ready a board in desert? (And they spoke evil against God; they said, Can God truly prepare a table for us here in the wilderness?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="NUMBERS 20:11; NUMBERS 11:21–23; PSALMS 78:41"> For he smote a stone, and waters flowed; and streams went out in abundance. Whether also he may give bread; either make ready a board to his people? (For he struck a stone, and waters flowed; yea, streams went out in abundance. But can he also give bread, or food, to us? can he also prepare a table here in the wilderness for his people?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JUDE 1:5; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:11; NUMBERS 11:1–3"> Therefore the Lord heard, and delayed; and fire was kindled in Jacob, and the ire of God ascended on Israel. (And so the Lord heard them, and was angry; and fire was kindled against the people of Jacob, yea, God’s anger descended upon the people of Israel.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HEBREWS 3:18–19; HEBREWS 3:12; JUDE 1:5"> For they believed not in God; neither hoped in his health. (For they did not believe in God; nor trusted in his salvation, or his deliverance.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 7:11; MALACHI 3:10; PSALMS 33:9"> And he commanded to the clouds above; and he opened the gates of heaven. (But then he commanded to the clouds above; and he opened the gates of the heavens.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EXODUS 16:4; PSALMS 105:40; JOHN 6:31–71"> And he rained to them manna for to eat; and he gave to them bread of heaven. (And he rained down manna for them to eat; yea, he gave them the bread of heaven.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:20; MATTHEW 15:37; PSALMS 103:20"> Man ate the bread of angels; he sent to them meats in abundance (or he sent them an abundance of food).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="NUMBERS 11:31; PSALMS 135:7"> He turned over the south wind from heaven; and he brought in by his virtue the west wind. (He sent the south wind down from the heavens; and he brought in the west wind by his strength, or his power.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="NUMBERS 11:18–19; EXODUS 16:12–13; NUMBERS 11:32"> And he rained fleshes as dust on them; and also volatiles feathered, as the gravel of the sea. (And he rained down flesh upon them, like a dust storm; yea, as many feathered fowls as the sand of the sea.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And those felled in the midst of their castles; about the tabernacles of them. (And they fell in the midst of their camp; all about their tents.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 106:15; NUMBERS 11:19–20"> And they ate, and were filled greatly, and he brought their desire to them; (And so they ate, and were greatly filled, for he had brought them the desire of their hearts;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="NUMBERS 11:33–34; LUKE 16:19–23; NUMBERS 22:20–22"> they were not defrauded of their desire. Yet their meats were in their mouth; (but their desire, or their yearning, was not yet quenched. And so while the food were yet in their mouths;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ISAIAH 10:16"> and the wrath of God ascended on them. And he killed the fat men of them; and he hindered the chosen men of Israel. (God’s anger descended upon them. And he killed their most stout-hearted men; yea, he struck down Israel’s chosen men.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JOHN 12:37; PSALMS 78:22; PSALMS 78:11"> In all these things they sinned yet; and believed not in the marvels of God. (Yet in spite of all these things, they still sinned; and did not believe or and had no faith in God’s marvellous deeds.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:35; NUMBERS 14:29; NUMBERS 26:64–65"> And the days of them failed in vanity; and the years of them failed with haste. (And so he ended their days like a breath; and their years with sudden disaster.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="HOSEA 5:15; NUMBERS 21:7; JUDGES 3:12–15"> When he killed them, they sought him; and they turned again, and early they came to him. (And after he killed some of them, then they sought him again; and they returned, and they came eagerly to him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:4; ISAIAH 41:14; EXODUS 15:13"> And they bethought (again), that God is the helper of them; and the high God is the again-buyer of them. (And they remembered that God is their helper; and that the Most High God is their redeemer.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:31; ISAIAH 29:13; HOSEA 11:12"> And they loved him in their mouth; and with their tongue they lied to him. (And they loved him with their mouths; but with their tongues they still lied to him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:21; PSALMS 78:8; PSALMS 51:10"> Forsooth the heart of them was not rightful with him; neither they were had faithful in his testament. (And they were not loyal to him in their hearts; nor were they steadfast, or faithful, to obey his covenant.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="ISAIAH 48:9; NUMBERS 14:18–20; EZEKIEL 20:17"> But he is merciful, and he shall be made merciful to the sins of them; and he shall not destroy them. And he did greatly, to turn away his ire; and he kindled not all his ire. (But he was merciful, and he forgave their sins; and so he did not destroy them. And he had great restraint, and turned away his anger; and he did not kindle, or release, all of his wrath.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="GENESIS 6:3; JAMES 4:14; JOB 7:7"> And he bethought, that they be flesh; a spirit going, and not turning again. (And he remembered, that they be but flesh; like the wind passing by, and never returning.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:30; PSALMS 95:8–10; HEBREWS 3:15–17"> How oft made they him wroth in desert; they stirred him into ire in a place without water. (How often they made him angry in the wilderness; yea, they stirred him to anger in a place without water.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="HEBREWS 3:8–11; NUMBERS 14:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:39"> And they were turned, and tempted God; and they wrathed the holy of Israel. (Again and again they tempted God; and they angered the Holy One of Israel.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="JUDGES 8:34; PSALMS 78:11; JEREMIAH 32:21"> They bethought not on his hand; in the day in which he again-bought them from the hand of the troubler. (They did not remember his power; yea, the day when he saved them from the hand of the troubler.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 6:22; EXODUS 4:21; EXODUS 3:19–20"> As he setted his signs in Egypt; and his great wonders in the field of Tanis. (How he showed his signs, or his miracles, in Egypt; yea, his great wonders on the plain of Zoan.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="PSALMS 105:29; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:3–6; EXODUS 7:17–21"> And he turned the floods of them, and the rains of them, into blood; that they should not drink. (And he turned their rivers, and their rains, into blood; so that they could not drink them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="EXODUS 8:21–24; PSALMS 105:30–31; EXODUS 8:2–15"> He sent a flesh fly into them, and it ate them; and he sent a paddock, and it lost them. (He sent swarms of flies into them, and they bit them all over; and he sent frogs among them, and they ruined their land.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="EXODUS 10:12–15; PSALMS 105:34–35; AMOS 7:1–2"> And he gave the fruits of them to rust; and he gave the travails of them to locusts. (And he gave their crops over to mildew; and he gave the produce from their labour over to locusts.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="PSALMS 105:32–33; EXODUS 9:18–34"> And he killed the vines of them with hail; and the (syca)more trees of them with frost. (And he killed their vines with hail; and their sycamore trees with frost.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="EXODUS 9:19; EXODUS 9:28"> And he betook the beasts of them (un)to hail; and the possession(s) of them (un)to fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="EXODUS 12:13; 1ST KINGS 22:21–22; LAMENTATIONS 4:11"> He sent into them the ire of his indignation; indignation, and ire, and tribulation, sendings-in by evil angels. (He sent into them the anger of his indignation; yea, his indignation, and anger, and trouble, by sending in evil angels among them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="EZEKIEL 7:4; 2ND PETER 2:4–5; ROMANS 8:32"> He made (a) way to the path of his ire, and he spared not from the death of their lives; and he closed together in death the beasts of them. (He made a way for the path of his anger, and he did not spare their lives from death; but rather he altogether ended them with a pestilence.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="PSALMS 105:23; PSALMS 135:8; PSALMS 106:22"> And he smote all the first engendered thing(s) in the land of Egypt &#91;or And he smote all the first begotten in the land of Egypt&#93;; the first fruits of all the travail of them in the tabernacles of Ham. (And he struck down all the first-born in the land of Egypt; yea, the first fruits of all their travail in the tents of Ham.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 9:12; PSALMS 77:20; JOHN 10:11–42"> And he took away his people as sheep; and he led them forth as a flock in desert. (But he took away his own people from there, like sheep; yea, he led them forth like a flock in the wilderness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="EXODUS 14:19–20; EXODUS 15:10; EXODUS 14:27–28"> And he led them forth in hope, and they dreaded not; and the sea covered the enemies of them. (And he led them forth in hope, and they were not afraid; and the sea covered their enemies.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="PSALMS 44:3; EXODUS 15:17; ISAIAH 11:9"> And he brought them into the hill of his hallowing; into the hill which his right hand (had) gat. (And he brought them to his holy hill; to the Mount which his right hand, or his power, had gotten or had won.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:7; PSALMS 44:2; NEHEMIAH 9:22–25"> And he casted out heathen men from the face of them; and by lot he parted to them the land in a cord of dealing. And he made the lineages of Israel to dwell in the tabernacles of them. (And he threw out the heathen before them; and by lot he parted the land to them with a measuring cord. And he let the tribes of Israel to live in the tents, or the homes, of the heathen.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="JUDGES 2:11–12; DEUTERONOMY 32:15–21; PSALMS 78:40–41"> And they tempted, and wrathed the high God; and they kept not his witnessings. (And still they tempted, and angered, the Most High God; and they did not obey his teachings, or his commands.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="HOSEA 7:16; EZEKIEL 20:27–28; PSALMS 78:8"> And they turned away themselves, and they kept not covenant; as their fathers, (they) were turned into a shrewd bow. (And they turned themselves away or And they rebelled, and they did not obey the covenant; like their forefathers, they were bent like a crooked bow.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:21; LEVITICUS 26:30; 1ST KINGS 12:31"> They stirred him into ire in their little hills; and they stirred him to indignation in their graven images. (They stirred him to anger with their high places, or their hill shrines; and they stirred him to indignation with their carved images, or their idols.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="PSALMS 106:40; LAMENTATIONS 2:7; DEUTERONOMY 32:19"> God heard, and forsook; and brought to nought Israel greatly. (God saw and heard all of this; and then he abandoned them, and he brought down Israel into nothing.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="JOSHUA 18:1; 1ST SAMUEL 4:4–11; JEREMIAH 26:6–9"> And he putted away the tabernacle of Shiloh; his tabernacle in which he dwelled among men. (And he deserted his Tabernacle at Shiloh; the Tent in which he lived among his people.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="PSALMS 132:8; 1ST SAMUEL 4:21–2; 1ST SAMUEL 4:17"> And he betook the virtue of them into captivity; and the fairness of them into the hands of the enemy. (And he delivered the symbol of his strength, that is, the Ark of the Covenant, into captivity; yea, the symbol of his beauty, or of his glory, into the hands of the enemy.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="PSALMS 89:38; ISAIAH 64:9; 1ST SAMUEL 4:10–11"> And he closed together his people in sword; and he despised his heritage. (And he altogether ended his people by the sword; and he despised his inheritance.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:34; JEREMIAH 16:9; DEUTERONOMY 32:22"> Fire ate the young men of them; and the virgins of them were not bewailed/were not bewept. (The fire ate up their young men; and their virgins did not bewail them or and their virgins did not weep for them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 4:17; JOB 27:15; EZEKIEL 24:23"> The priests of them fell down by sword; and the widows of them were not bewept. (Their priests fell by the sword; and their widows did not weep for them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="PSALMS 44:23; PSALMS 7:6; ISAIAH 51:9"> And the Lord was raised, as sleeping; as mighty greatly filled of wine. (Then the Lord was raised up; like one who had been sleeping, like a mighty man excited by wine.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 5:6; JEREMIAH 23:40; JOB 40:12"> And he smote his enemies on the hinder parts; he gave to them everlasting shame. (And he struck his enemies on their backsides; yea, he gave them up to everlasting shame.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 6:21–1; 2ND SAMUEL 6:2; 2ND SAMUEL 6:17"> And he putted away the tabernacle of Joseph; and he chose not the lineage of Ephraim. (And he rejected the house of Joseph; and he chose not the tribe of Ephraim.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="PSALMS 87:2; PSALMS 132:12–14; RUTH 4:17–22"> But he chose the lineage of Judah; he chose the hill of Zion, which he loved. (But he chose the tribe of Judah; and he chose Mount Zion, which he loved.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="JOB 26:7; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:19; 1ST SAMUEL 2:8"> And he as an unicorn builded his holy place; in the land, which he founded into worlds. (And he built his holy place like his home in heaven or And he built his holy place as high as the heavens; and he founded it like the earth, to last forever.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 16:11–12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:22; EXODUS 3:1"> And he chose David his servant, and took him up from the flocks of sheep; (And he chose David to be his servant, and took him away from the flocks of sheep;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 5:2; 1ST CHRONICLES 11:2; EZEKIEL 34:23–24"> he took him from behind sheep with lambs. <span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="1ST KINGS 9:4; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:15; ISAIAH 11:2–4"> And he fed them in the innocence of his heart; and he led them forth in the understandings of his hands.
