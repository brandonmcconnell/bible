<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND KI 20:5; PSALMS 39:12; PSALMS 126:5–6"> God, I showed my life to thee; thou hast set my tears in thy sight. As and in thy promise, Lord; (God, thou knowest my troubles; thou hast set my tears before thee. Be they not in thy Book, O Lord?)</span>
