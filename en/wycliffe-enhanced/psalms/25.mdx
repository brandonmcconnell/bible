<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 143:8; PSALMS 86:4; PSALMS 24:4"> The song of David. Lord, to thee I have raised my soul; (A song by David. Lord, I raise up my soul to thee;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ROMANS 10:11; PSALMS 41:11; PSALMS 71:1"> my God, I trust in thee, be I not ashamed. Neither mine enemies scorn me; (my God, I put my trust in thee, so do not let me shamed. And do not let my enemies scorn me;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:25; ISAIAH 40:31; ISAIAH 49:23"> for all men that suffer thee shall not be shamed. All men doing wicked things superfluously; be they shamed. (for all those who trust in thee shall not be shamed. But all those who do wicked things without cause, let them be shamed.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 86:11; PSALMS 27:11; PSALMS 143:8"> Lord, show thou thy ways to me; and teach thou me thy paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 8:34; ISAIAH 30:18; PSALMS 25:10"> (Ad)dress thou me in thy truth, and teach thou me, for thou art God, my saviour; and I suffered thee all day. (Direct thou me in thy truth, and teach thou me, for thou art God, my saviour; and I have waited for thee all day long.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 103:17; COLOSSIANS 3:12; LUKE 1:50"> Lord, have thou mind of thy merciful doings; and of thy mercies that be from the world. (Lord, remember thy merciful doings; and thy constant love, which thou hast shown from long ago.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:25; ISAIAH 38:17; JOB 13:26"> Have thou not mind on the trespasses of my youth; and on mine unknowings. Thou, Lord, have mind on me by thy mercy; for thy goodness. (Remember not the trespasses of my youth; and all my ignorance. O Lord, because of thy love, remember me; for the sake of thy goodness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JAMES 1:5; PSALMS 32:8; EPHESIANS 1:17–18"> The Lord is sweet and rightful; for this cause he shall give a law to men trespassing in the way. (The Lord is good and upright; and for this reason he hath given a way forward for those who trespass./The Lord is good and righteous; and for this reason he hath given a way back for those who trespass.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:5–6; PSALMS 23:3; MATTHEW 5:5"> He shall (ad)dress meek men in doom; he shall teach mild men his ways. (He shall direct the humble in their judgement; he shall teach the humble his ways.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JAMES 5:11; ROMANS 8:28; HEBREWS 8:8–12"> All the ways of the Lord be mercy and truth; to men seeking his testament, and his witnessings. (All the ways of the Lord be loving and faithful; for those who keep his covenant, and his teaching, or his commands.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 79:9; 1ST JOHN 2:12; ISAIAH 43:25"> Lord, for thy name, thou shalt do mercy to my sin; for it is much. (Lord, for the sake of thy name, have thou mercy on my sin; although it is great.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 37:23; PSALMS 32:8; JOHN 7:17"> Who is a man, that dreadeth the Lord? he ordaineth to him a law in the way which he (should) choose. (Who is someone, who feareth the Lord? or who hath reverence for the Lord? the Lord shall ordain to him the way that he should choose.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 37:11; PROVERBS 1:33; PROVERBS 19:23"> His soul shall dwell in goods; and his seed shall inherit the land. (He shall live in abundance; and his children shall inherit the land.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 15:15; JOHN 14:21–23; JOHN 7:17"> The Lord is a firmness to men dreading him; and his testament is, that it be showed to them. (The Lord shall share his secrets with those who fear him or with those who revere him; and he shall show his covenant to them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 141:8; PSALMS 124:7–8; PSALMS 31:4"> Mine eyes be ever&#91;more&#93; to(ward) the Lord; for he shall pull away my feet from the snare. (My eyes be upon the Lord forevermore; for he shall pull away my feet from the snare.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 86:16; DANIEL 9:17; MICAH 7:19"> Behold thou to me, and have thou mercy on me; for I am one alone and poor. (Look thou on me, and have thou mercy on me; for I am all alone and poor.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 107:6; PSALMS 34:19; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:8–9"> The tribulations of mine heart be multiplied; deliver thou me of my needs. (The troubles in my heart be multiplied; save thou me from all my troubles.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 16:12; PSALMS 119:132; PSALMS 51:8–9"> See thou my meekness and my travail; and forgive thou all my trespasses. (See my troubles and my trials; and forgive all my sins.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 56:2; PSALMS 52:2; PSALMS 38:19"> Behold thou mine enemies, for they be multiplied; and they hate me by wicked hatred. (Look thou upon my enemies, for they be many; and they hate me with such wicked hatred.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 71:1–2; PSALMS 121:7; JOEL 2:26–27"> Keep thou my soul, and deliver me; be I not ashamed, for I hoped in thee. (Keep thou me alive, and save me; let me not be shamed, for I put my trust in thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:3; PSALMS 41:12; PROVERBS 20:7"> Innocent men and rightful cleaved to me; for I suffered thee. (Let innocence and uprightness, or integrity, cleave to me; for I have waited for thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 130:8; PSALMS 51:18–19; PSALMS 14:7"> God, deliver thou Israel; from all his tribulations. (God, save thou the people of Israel; from all their troubles.)</span>
