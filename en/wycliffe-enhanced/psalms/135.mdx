<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 96:1–4; PSALMS 7:17; PSALMS 33:1–2"> Alleluia. Praise ye the name of the Lord; ye servants of the Lord, praise ye (him).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 116:19; PSALMS 92:13; PSALMS 96:8"> Ye that stand in the house of the Lord; in the halls/in the foreyards of the house of our God. (Ye who stand in the House of the Lord; in the courtyards of the House of our God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 147:1; PSALMS 119:68; PSALMS 106:1"> Praise ye the Lord, for the Lord is good; sing ye to his name, for it is sweet (or for it is pleasant to do so).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:9; DEUTERONOMY 10:15; MALACHI 3:17"> For the Lord chose Jacob to himself; &#91;and&#93; Israel into possession to himself. (For the Lord chose Jacob for himself; yea, he chose Israel as his special possession.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 95:3; PSALMS 48:1; PSALMS 97:9"> For I have known, that the Lord is great; and our God (is) before all gods. (For I know that the Lord is great; and that our God is above all gods.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DANIEL 4:35; PSALMS 115:3; MATTHEW 28:18"> The Lord made all things, whatever things he would, in heaven and in earth; in the sea, and in all depths of waters. (The Lord made everything, whatever he desired, in heaven and on earth; in the sea, and in all the depths of the waters.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:13; ZECHARIAH 10:1; JOB 28:25–26"> He led out clouds from the farthest part of earth; and made lightnings into rain. Which bringeth forth winds from his treasures; (He leadeth out the clouds from the farthest parts of the earth; and maketh lightning for the rains. He bringeth forth the wind out of his treasuries, or out of his storehouses.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 78:51; PSALMS 105:36; EXODUS 12:12"> which killed the first begotten things of Egypt, from man unto beast. (He killed all the first-born in Egypt, both man and beast.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 136:15; DEUTERONOMY 6:22; DEUTERONOMY 4:34"> He sent out signs and great wonders, in the middle of thee, thou Egypt; into Pharaoh, and into all his servants. (He sent out signs and great wonders, into the midst of thee, O Egypt; against Pharaoh, and all his servants.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 136:17–22; NUMBERS 21:24; PSALMS 44:2–3"> Which smote many folks; and killed strong kings. (Who struck down many nations; and killed strong kings.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOSHUA 12:7–24; DEUTERONOMY 29:7; NEHEMIAH 9:22"> Sihon, the king of Amorites, and Og, the king of Bashan; and all the realms of Canaan (or and all the kings in Canaan).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 78:55; PSALMS 136:21–22; JOSHUA 11:23"> And he gave the land of them heritage; to be heritage to Israel, his people. (And he gave their land for an inheritance; yea, to be an inheritance for his people Israel.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 102:12; EXODUS 3:15; PSALMS 8:1"> Lord, thy name is&#91;into&#93; without end; Lord, thy memorial be in generation and into generation. (Lord, thy name shall endure forever; Lord, thou shalt be remembered by all generations.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:36; JONAH 4:2; PSALMS 50:4"> For the Lord shall deem his people; and he shall be prayed in his servants. (For the Lord shall judge his people; and he shall take pity upon his servants.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 115:4–8; HABAKKUK 2:18–19; ISAIAH 37:19"> The simulacra of heathen men be silver and gold; the works of the hands of men. (The idols of the heathenbe but silver and gold; yea, the works of men’s hands.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:14–16; ISAIAH 6:10"> Those images have a mouth, and shall not speak; those have eyes, and shall not see. (Those idols have mouths, but they cannot speak; they have eyes, but they cannot see.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Those have ears, and shall not hear; for there is no spirit in the mouth of them. (They have ears, but they cannot hear; for there is no breath in their mouths.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 115:8; ISAIAH 44:18–20; JEREMIAH 10:8"> They that make those &#91;things&#93;, be made like them; and all that trust in them. (Let they who make those things, be made like them; and all who trust in them as well.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 148:14; PSALMS 118:1–4; PSALMS 147:19–20"> The house of Israel, bless ye the Lord; the house of Aaron, bless ye the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The house of Levi, bless ye the Lord; ye that dread the Lord, bless ye the Lord, (or ye who fear the Lord, or ye who revere the Lord, bless ye the Lord).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 134:3; PSALMS 132:13–14; PSALMS 128:5"> Blessed be the Lord (out) of Zion; that dwelleth in Jerusalem. (Blessed be the Lord in Zion; yea, he who dwelleth in Jerusalem.)</span>
