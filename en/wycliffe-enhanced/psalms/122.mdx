I was glad, when they said to me, We shall go to the House of the Lord.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 100:4; PSALMS 84:7; 2ND CHRONICLES 6:6"> Our feet were standing; in thy halls/in thy foreyards, thou Jerusalem. (And now our feet stand within thy gates, O Jerusalem.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 5:9; EPHESIANS 2:20–21; EPHESIANS 4:4–7"> Jerusalem, which is builded as a city; whose partaking thereof is into the same thing. (Jerusalem is built to be a city, where people be together, yea, with one heart and mind.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:16; PSALMS 118:19; PSALMS 107:1–3"> For the lineages, the lineages of the Lord, ascended thither, the witnessing of Israel; to acknowledge to the name of the Lord. (For the tribes, the Lord’s tribes, come there to give thanks to the Lord; that is Israel’s duty.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:8; 2ND CHRONICLES 19:8; 2ND CHRONICLES 11:22"> For they sat there on seats in doom; seats on the house of David. (For the thrones of judgement, or of justice, be put there; yea, the thrones of the house of David.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 51:18; JEREMIAH 29:7; GENESIS 12:3"> Pray ye those things, that be to the peace of Jerusalem; and abundance be to them that love thee. (Pray ye for the peace of Jerusalem; and say, May those who love thee prosper, or have great abundance.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOHN 14:27; PSALMS 48:3; ISAIAH 54:13"> Peace be made in thy strength; and abundance in thy towers. (May there be peace within thy strongholds, or thy fortresses; and prosperity within thy palaces.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:2–5; 1ST SAMUEL 25:6; JAMES 3:13–18"> For my brethren and my neighbours; I spake peace of thee. (For the sake of my brothers, or my kinsmen, and my neighbours; I say, Peace be with thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 2:10; PSALMS 84:10; ESTHER 10:3"> For the house of our Lord God; I sought goods to thee. (For the sake of the House of the Lord our God; I shall seek thy good, or thy prosperity, or I shall pray for good things for thee.)</span>
