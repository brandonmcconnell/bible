<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 65:2; PSALMS 3:4; ISAIAH 33:22"> My king (or My King), and my God; give thou attention to the voice of my prayer. For, Lord, I shall pray to thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 119:147; PSALMS 88:13; PSALMS 130:6"> hear thou early my voice. Early I shall stand nigh to thee, and I shall see; (hear thou my voice in the morning. Early in the morning I shall stand near to thee, and I shall see thee;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 11:5; HEBREWS 12:14; MALACHI 2:17"> for thou art God not desiring wickedness. Neither an evil-willed man shall dwell beside thee; (for thou art not a God who desireth wickedness. No one with an evil will shall stand beside thee;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 11:5; PSALMS 1:5; HOSEA 9:15"> neither unjust men shall dwell before thine eyes. Thou hatest all them that work wickedness; (nor shall the unrighteous stand before thine eyes. Thou hatest all those who do evil;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 55:23; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:15"> thou shalt lose them that speak leasing. The Lord shall hold abominable a man-queller, and a guileful man. (thou shalt destroy those who tell lies. And the Lord shall hold as abominable man-killers, that is, murderers, and liars.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 132:7; PSALMS 138:2; HEBREWS 4:16"> But Lord, in the multitude of thy mercy, I shall enter into thine house; I shall worship to(ward) thine holy temple in thy dread. (But Lord, because of thy great love, I shall enter into thy House; I shall bow toward thy holy Temple in reverence for thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 27:11; PSALMS 25:4–5; PROVERBS 3:5–6"> Lord, lead thou forth me in thy rightwiseness for mine enemies; (ad)dress thou my way in thy sight. (Lord, lead thou me forth in thy righteousness, because my enemies be all around me; direct thou my way before thee or direct thou me along thy way.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ROMANS 3:13; JEREMIAH 9:3–6; PSALMS 62:4"> For truth is not in their mouth; their heart is vain. Their throat is an open sepulchre, they did guilefully with their tongues; (For truth is not in their mouths; their hearts be empty. Their throats be an open tomb, or an open grave, and everything they say is deceitful, or corrupt;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 63:10; PSALMS 140:9–10; 2ND SAMUEL 17:14"> God, deem thou them. Fall they down from their thoughts; after the multitude of their wickednesses, or unpiousnesses, cast thou them down; for, Lord, they have stirred thee to wrath. (God, judge thou them. Let them fall by their own thoughts; throw thou them down after all their wickednesses; for, Lord, they have rebelled against thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 8:28; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:1–7; PSALMS 68:3"> And all that hope in thee, be they glad; they shall make full out joy &#91;into&#93; without end, and thou shalt dwell in them. And all who love thy name shall have glory in thee;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 84:11; PSALMS 32:10; PSALMS 115:13"> for thou shalt bless the just man. Lord, thou hast crowned us, as with a shield of thy good will.
