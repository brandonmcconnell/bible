<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ROMANS 4:6–8; ISAIAH 44:22; PSALMS 85:2"> Learning to David. &#91;The understanding of David.&#93; Blessed be they, whose wickedness be forgiven; and whose sins be covered. (A teaching by David. Happy be they, whose wickedness be forgiven; and whose sins be covered.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOHN 1:47; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:19–21; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:5"> Blessed is the man, to whom the Lord areckoned not sin; neither guile is in his spirit. (Happy is the person, to whom the Lord did not reckon any sin; and there is no deceit in his spirit.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:13; PSALMS 38:8; PSALMS 38:3"> For I was still, my bones waxed eld; while I cried all day. (For when I kept silent or For when I did not confess my sins, my bones grew old; while I cried all day long.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 5:11; PSALMS 38:2–8; PSALMS 39:10–11"> For by day and night thine hand was made grievous on me (or And day and night thy hand was heavy upon me); I am turned in my wretchedness, while the thorn is set in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:13; 2ND SAMUEL 12:13; PSALMS 38:18"> (Then) I made my sin known to thee; and I hid not my unrightfulness. I said, I shall acknowledge against me mine unrightfulness to the Lord; and thou hast forgiven the wickedness of my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ISAIAH 55:6; ISAIAH 43:2; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:2"> For this thing each holy man shall pray to thee; in covenable time. Nevertheless in the great flood of many waters; they shall not (come) nigh to thee. (For this thing, each holy man shall pray to thee; in his time of need. And then, in the great flood of many troubles, none shall come near him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 91:1; PSALMS 119:114; PSALMS 9:9"> Thou art my refuge from tribulation, that (en)compassed me; thou, my fully joying, deliver me from them that (en)compass me. (Thou art my refuge, from the troubles that surround me; thou, my full out joy, deliver me from those who surround me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:5–6; PSALMS 33:18; PSALMS 25:8–10"> I shall give understanding to thee, and I shall teach thee; in this way in which thou shalt go, I shall make steadfast mine eyes on thee. (And the Lord said, I shall give understanding to thee, and I shall teach thee the way in which thou should go; and I shall put my eyes steadfastly upon thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 26:3; JEREMIAH 4:22; JAMES 3:3"> Do not ye be made as an horse and mule; to which is none understanding. Lord, constrain thou the cheeks of them with a barnacle and bridle; that (they) nigh not to thee. (Do not ye be made like a horse or a mule; which have no understanding, or reasoning ability. Their mouths must be constrained with a bit and a bridle; so that they finally submit to where they should go.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 12:2–3; PROVERBS 16:20; PROVERBS 13:21"> Many beatings be of the sinner; but mercy shall (en)compass him that hopeth in the Lord. (Many beatings be to the sinner; but the love of the Lord shall surround those who trust in him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 64:10; PHILIPPIANS 4:4; PSALMS 97:12"> Ye just men, be glad, and make fully joy in the Lord; and all ye rightful of heart, have glory. (Ye who be righteous be glad, and make full out joy, (or rejoice), in the Lord; and all ye with an upright heart, have glory, (or shout for joy)!)</span>
