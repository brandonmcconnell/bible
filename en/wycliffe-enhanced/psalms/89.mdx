<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 101:1; PSALMS 89:8; PSALMS 119:90"> The learning of Ethan, (the) Ezrahite. I shall sing &#91;into&#93; without end; the mercies of the Lord. In generation and into generation; I shall tell thy truth with my mouth. (A teaching by Ethan, the Ezrahite. I shall sing of the Lord’s constant love forever. To all generations, I shall tell out thy faithfulness with my mouth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 36:5; NEHEMIAH 9:31; PSALMS 89:5"> For thou saidest, &#91;Into&#93; Without end mercy shall be builded in heavens; thy truth shall be made ready in those. (For thy love shall last forever; thy faithfulness is as enduring as the heavens.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 132:11; 2ND SAMUEL 7:10–16; PSALMS 89:34–35"> I disposed a testament to my chosen men; I swore to David, my servant, (Thou saidest, I made a covenant with my chosen man; I swore to my servant David,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 7:12–16; LUKE 1:32–33; PSALMS 132:12"> till into without end I shall make ready thy seed. And I shall build thy seat; in generation, and into generation. (that I shall establish thy children, or thy descendants, forever. And I shall preserve thy throne; for all generations.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 97:6; PSALMS 19:1; ISAIAH 44:23"> Lord, heavens shall acknowledge thy marvels; and thy truth in the church of saints. (Lord, the heavens shall praise thy marvellous deeds; and the council of the saints shall praise thy faithfulness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 86:8; PSALMS 71:19; PSALMS 113:5"> For who in the clouds shall be made even to the Lord; shall be like God among the sons of God? (For who in heaven shall be made equal to the Lord? who shall be like God among the heavenly beings? or who shall be like God in the council of heaven?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 47:2; PSALMS 76:7–11; HEBREWS 12:28–29"> God, that is glorified in the council of saints; is great, and dreadful over all that be in his compass. (God, thou art feared or thou art revered, in the council of the saints; thou art great, and glorified above all who surround thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 71:19; 1ST SAMUEL 2:2; JEREMIAH 32:17"> Lord God of virtues, who is like thee? Lord, thou art mighty, and thy truth is in thy compass. (Lord God of hosts, who is like thee? Lord, thou art mighty, and thy faithfulness surroundeth thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 65:7; PSALMS 107:25–29; MARK 4:41"> Thou art Lord of the power of the sea; forsooth thou assuagest the stirring of the waves thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 87:4; PSALMS 68:30; PSALMS 144:6"> Thou madest low the proud, as (those who be) wounded (and slain); in the arm of thy strength thou hast scattered thine enemies. (Thou hast crushed Rahab to death; thou hast scattered thy enemies with thy strong arm, or with thy power.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 29:11; GENESIS 1:1; PSALMS 115:16"> (The) Heavens be thine, and the earth is thine; thou hast founded the world, and the fullness thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOSHUA 12:1; JOB 26:7; JOSHUA 19:22"> thou madest of nought the north and the sea. Tabor and Hermon shall make full out joy in thy name; (thou madest the north and the south out of nothing. Tabor and Hermon shall make full out joy, or shall rejoice, in thy name.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DANIEL 4:34–35; PSALMS 89:10; PSALMS 62:11"> thine arm with power. Thine hand be made steadfast, and thy right hand be enhanced; (Thy arm hath power; let thy hand be made firm, and thy right hand be lifted high.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 97:2; PROVERBS 16:12; PSALMS 45:6–7"> rightfulness and doom is the making ready of thy seat. Mercy and truth shall go before thy face; (Righteousness and judgement be the foundations of thy throne; love and faithfulness go forth before thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 44:3; JOB 29:3; PSALMS 4:6"> blessed is the people that know (a) hearty song. Lord, they shall go in the light of thy cheer; (Happy be the people who know a hearty song; Lord, they shall go forth by the light of thy face.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:4; PHILIPPIANS 3:9; LUKE 1:47"> and in thy name they shall make full out joy all day; and they shall be enhanced in thy rightfulness. (And in thy name, they shall make full out joy, or shall rejoice, all day long; and they shall be exalted, or shall be lifted up, by thy righteousness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 75:10; PSALMS 92:10; PSALMS 148:14"> For thou art the glory of the virtue of them; and in thy good pleasance our horn shall be enhanced. (For thou art the glory of their strength or For thou art the strength in which they have glory; and in thy good favour our horn shall be exalted.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 47:9; PSALMS 71:22; ISAIAH 33:22"> For our taking up is of the Lord; and of the holy of Israel our king. (For the Lord is our defender; yea, the Holy One of Israel is our King.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 16:18; 1ST KINGS 11:34; PSALMS 89:3"> Then thou spakest in revelation to thy saints, and saidest, I have set help in the mighty (man); and I have enhanced the chosen man of my people. (Then thou spokest in revelation to thy saints, and saidest, I have given help to the mighty man; yea, I have exalted, or lifted up, the chosen man of my people.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 16:12–13; ISAIAH 61:1–3; 1ST SAMUEL 16:1"> I found David, my servant; I anointed him with mine holy oil. (I found my servant David; and I anointed him with my holy oil.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ISAIAH 42:1; PSALMS 18:32–39; PSALMS 89:13"> For mine hand shall help him; and mine arm shall confirm him. (For my hand shall help him; and my arm, or my power, shall strengthen him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 17:9; 2ND SAMUEL 7:10; MATTHEW 4:1–10"> The enemy shall nothing profit in him (or The enemy shall not gain any advantage over him); and the son of wickedness shall not lay to or shall not put to, for to harm him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 7:9; 2ND SAMUEL 3:1; PSALMS 18:40"> And I shall slay his enemies from his face; and I shall turn into flight them that hate him. (And I shall kill all his enemies before his face; and I shall turn to flight, or make to flee, those who hate him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 89:16–17; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:20; PSALMS 91:14"> And my truth and mercy shall be with him; and his horn shall be enhanced in my name. (And my faithfulness and love shall be with him; and in my name his horn shall be exalted or his head shall be lifted up on high.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 80:11; PSALMS 2:8; PSALMS 72:8–11"> And I shall set his hand in the sea; and his right hand in floods. (And I shall extend his power unto the Mediterranean Sea; and his right hand unto the Euphrates River.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 7:14; 2ND SAMUEL 22:47; PSALMS 95:1"> He shall inwardly call me, (saying), Thou art my father; my God, and the up-taker of mine health. (And he shall say to me, Thou art my father; yea, my God, and my defender, my saviour.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:15; COLOSSIANS 1:18; PSALMS 2:7"> And I shall set him the first begotten son; higher than the kings of earth. (And I shall make him my first-born son; higher than all the kings of the earth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="ISAIAH 55:3; JEREMIAH 33:20–21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:32–34"> &#91;Into&#93; Without end I shall keep my mercy to him; and my testament faithful to him. (I shall hold onto my love for him forever; and faithfully keep my covenant with him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 89:4; DEUTERONOMY 11:21; PSALMS 89:36"> And I shall set his seed into the world of world; and his throne as the days of heaven. (And I shall establish his descendants forever and ever; and his throne for as long as the days of the heavens, that is, for as long as the heavenly bodies endure.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 7:14; 1ST CHRONICLES 28:9; PSALMS 119:53"> Forsooth if his sons forsake my law; and go not in my dooms. (But if his sons abandon my Law; and do not go in my judgements.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 55:20"> If they make unholy my rightfulnesses; and keep not my commandments. (If they break my statutes; and do not obey my commandments.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 7:14; HEBREWS 12:6–11; PROVERBS 3:11–12"> I shall visit in a rod the wickednesses of them; and in beatings the sins of them. (Then I shall punish their wickednesses with a rod; and their sins with beatings.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="ISAIAH 54:8–10; 2ND SAMUEL 7:15; LAMENTATIONS 3:31–32"> But I shall not scatter my mercy from him; and in my truth I shall not harm him. (But I shall not take away my love from him; and I shall be faithful to him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:19; MATTHEW 24:35; JAMES 1:17"> Neither I shall make unholy my testament; and I shall not make void those things that come forth of my lips. (Nor shall I break my covenant; and I shall not make void those things which come forth from my lips, that is, I shall not break my promises.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 132:11; HEBREWS 6:13; TITUS 1:2"> Once I swore in mine holiness, I shall not lie to David; (Once I swore by my holiness, that I would never lie to David;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PSALMS 72:5; PSALMS 89:29; ISAIAH 59:21"> his seed shall dwell &#91;into&#93; without end. And his throne as &#91;the&#93; sun in my sight, (his children, that is, his descendants, shall live forever. And his throne shall be before me like the sun,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:35–36; GENESIS 1:14–18; PSALMS 104:19"> and as a perfect moon without end; and a faithful witness in heaven. (and like the moon, which shall endure forever; yea, like a faithful witness in the heavens.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 28:9; DEUTERONOMY 32:19; PSALMS 106:40"> But thou hast put away, and despised; and hast delayed thy christ. (But thou hast rejected, and despised, and hast raged against thy anointed king.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 5:16; PSALMS 74:7; PSALMS 89:34–36"> Thou hast turned away the testament of thy servant; thou madest unholy his saintuary in earth. (Thou hast made void the covenant with thy servant; thou hast defiled his crown, and hast thrown it to the ground.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="PSALMS 80:12; LAMENTATIONS 2:2; LAMENTATIONS 2:5"> Thou destroyedest all the hedges thereof; thou hast set the steadfastness thereof (into) dread (or thou hast brought down his strongholds, or his fortresses, into ruin).</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="PSALMS 79:4; LAMENTATIONS 5:1; EZEKIEL 5:14–15"> All men passing by the way ravished him; he is made (a) shame to his neighbours. (All who pass by him, on the way, rob him; he is shamed by his neighbours.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="PSALMS 80:6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:10; DEUTERONOMY 28:43"> Thou hast enhanced the right hand of men oppressing him; thou hast gladdened all his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:8–12; NUMBERS 14:42; PSALMS 44:10"> Thou hast turned away the help of his sword; and thou helpedest not him in battle. (Thou hast turned away the help from his sword; and thou hast not helped him in the battle.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 4:1–2; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:3–10; PSALMS 89:39"> Thou destroyedest him from cleansing; and thou hast hurled down his seat in earth. (Thou hast destroyed his purity; and thou hast thrown down his throne to the ground.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="PSALMS 109:29; PSALMS 44:15; MICAH 7:10"> Thou hast made less the days of his time; thou hast beshed him with shame. (Thou hast lessened the days of his time; thou hast covered him with shame.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="PSALMS 79:5; PSALMS 78:63; PSALMS 88:14"> Lord, how long turnest thou away, into the end; shall thine ire burn out as fire? (Lord, how long turnest thou away, forever? shall thy anger always burn like fire?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="JOB 14:1; JOB 10:9; JOB 7:7"> Bethink thou what is my substance; for whether thou hast ordained vainly all the sons of men? (Remember thou what my substance is or how short my time is; hast thou ordained all the sons and daughters of men in vain?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="PSALMS 49:15; HEBREWS 11:5; ECCLESIASTES 9:5"> Who is a man, that shall live, and shall not see death; shall (he) deliver his soul from the hand of hell? (What man shall live, and shall not see death? can he save his soul from the power of Sheol, or the land of the dead? or from the power of the grave?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 7:15; HEBREWS 7:21; ISAIAH 55:3"> Lord, where be thine eld mercies; as thou hast sworn to David in thy truth? (Lord, where be the former acts, or the earlier proofs, of thy constant love? yea, those promises that thou hast sworn to David in thy faithfulness?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="PSALMS 74:22; PSALMS 74:18; PSALMS 69:9"> Lord, be thou mindful of the shame of thy servants; (of the curses) of many heathen men, which I held together in my bosom. (Lord, remember thy servant’s shame; remember the curses of many of the heathen, which I carried in my heart.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:14–16; PSALMS 74:10; 2ND SAMUEL 16:7–8"> Which thine enemies, Lord, did shamefully; for they despised the changing of thy christ. (Which thy enemies, Lord, spoke shamefully to me; for they despised the footsteps of thy anointed king or for they despised the successors of thy anointed king.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="PSALMS 72:18–19; PSALMS 41:13; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:17"> Blessed be the Lord &#91;into&#93; without end; be it done, be it done. (Blessed be the Lord forever; amen, amen.)</span>
