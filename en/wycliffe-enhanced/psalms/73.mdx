<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:8; PSALMS 51:10; LUKE 12:32"> The psalm of Asaph. God of Israel is full good; to them that be of rightful heart. (A song by Asaph. The God of Israel is very good; to those who have an upright heart.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 94:18; 1ST SAMUEL 2:9; PSALMS 116:8"> But my feet were moved almost; my steps were shed out almost. (But my feet almost stumbled; my steps almost slipped.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOB 21:7; PSALMS 37:7; PROVERBS 23:17"> For I loved fervently on wicked men; seeing the peace of sinners. (For I envied the wicked; when I saw the prosperity of the sinners.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOB 21:23–24; ECCLESIASTES 2:16; LUKE 16:22"> For beholding is not to the death of them; and steadfastness in the sickness of them. (For it seemed that they never die; yea, they always be strong, and never get sick.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOB 21:9; PSALMS 73:12; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:19"> They be not in travail of (other) men; and they shall not be beaten with men. (They do not have trouble, or tribulation, like other people do; and they be not beaten down like others be.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 109:18; 1ST PETER 5:5; DANIEL 4:30"> Therefore pride hath held them; they were covered with their wickedness and unfaithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:28; PSALMS 17:10; JOB 15:27"> The wickedness of them came forth as of fatness; they went into desire of heart. (Their wickedness came forth like fatness; and they went forth in the desire of their hearts.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JUDE 1:16; PSALMS 17:10; PSALMS 10:2"> They thought and spake waywardness; they spake wickedness on high (or they spoke wickedness out loud).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 13:6; JAMES 3:6; EXODUS 5:2"> They putted their mouth into heaven; and their tongue passed in earth. (They put their mouths against heaven; and their tongues went about over all the earth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 75:8"> Therefore my people shall be turned again here; and full days shall be found in them. (And so my people followed them; and found nothing to condemn them for.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HOSEA 7:2; ZEPHANIAH 1:12; JOB 22:13–14"> And they said, How knoweth God; and whether knowing is on high? (And they said, How could God know? how can the Most High have any knowledge of this?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 52:7; PSALMS 49:6; HOSEA 12:7–8"> Lo! those sinners and having abundance in the world; (they) held riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOB 21:15; PSALMS 26:6; JOB 34:9"> And I said, Therefore without cause I justified mine heart; and washed mine hands among innocents. (And I said, And so I have keep my heart pure, and I have kept my hands clean, all for nothing or all in vain.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:5; PSALMS 94:12; JOB 7:3–4"> And I was beaten all day; and my chastising was in morrowtides. (Yea, all day long I am beaten down; and I am punished every morning.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:6–7; MALACHI 2:8; 1ST PETER 2:9"> If I said, I shall tell thus; lo! I &#91;have&#93; reproved the nation of thy sons. (If I had said, I shall talk as they do; behold! I would have brought reproach upon the nation of thy children.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 8:17; JOHN 16:18–19; PSALMS 36:6"> I guessed, that I should know this; (but too much) travail is before me. (I tried to work through all of this; but it was too much for me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKE 12:20; PSALMS 77:13; ECCLESIASTES 8:12–13"> Till I enter into the saintuary of God; and understand in the last things of them. (Until I entered into the sanctuary of God; and there I understood their last things, that is, their end.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 35:6; JEREMIAH 23:12; DEUTERONOMY 32:35"> Nevertheless for guiles thou hast put to them; thou castedest them down, while they were raised. (For thou shalt put them in slippery places; thou shalt throw them down, after they be raised up.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:1; ISAIAH 47:11; DANIEL 5:6"> How be they made into desolation; they failed suddenly, they perished for their wickedness/for their waywardness. (How they shall go into desolation! they shall suddenly fail, and they shall perish for all their wickedness or for all their wayward ways.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOB 20:8; PSALMS 78:65; PSALMS 90:5"> As the dream of men that (a)rise; Lord, thou shalt drive their image to nought, in thy city. (Like a dream when one awaketh, and it vanisheth; so, Lord, thou shalt drive them down into nothing or until they completely disappear.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 37:7; PSALMS 73:3; JOB 16:13"> For mine heart is enflamed, and my reins be changed; (When my heart was enflamed, and my feelings were hurt;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 3:18; PSALMS 92:6; PSALMS 49:10"> and I am driven to nought, and I knew not. As a work beast I am made with thee; (and I was driven down into nothing, and I knew nothing; yea, I was made like a work beast before thee;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:13; PSALMS 63:8; MATTHEW 28:20"> and I am ever with thee. Thou heldest my right hand, (but still I am ever with thee. Thou holdest my right hand,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 32:8; ISAIAH 58:11; PROVERBS 3:5–6"> and in thy will thou leddest me forth; and with glory thou tookest me up. (and by thy counsel, or thy instruction, thou leadest me forth; and afterward thou shalt receive me with honour.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:8; PSALMS 16:2; PSALMS 63:3"> For why what is to me in heaven; and what would I of thee on earth? (For what is there for me in heaven, but thee? and what else do I desire here on earth, but thee?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 12:9–10; ISAIAH 40:29–31; PSALMS 138:3"> My flesh and mine heart failed; God of mine heart, and my part is God &#91;into&#93; without end. (Though my flesh and my heart fail; but God is my strength, and my portion forever.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JAMES 4:4; PSALMS 119:155; EXODUS 34:15"> For lo! they that draw away far themselves from thee shall perish; thou hast lost all men that do fornication from thee. (For behold! they who take themselves far away from thee, shall perish; thou shalt destroy all those who wantonly abandon thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JAMES 4:8; PSALMS 40:5; HEBREWS 10:19–22"> But it is good to me to cleave to God; and to set mine hope in the Lord God. That I tell all thy preachings, in the gates of the daughter of Zion. (But it is good for me to cleave to God; and to trust in the Lord God. And that I tell out all thy works, or all of thy deeds.)</span>
