<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 145:18; PSALMS 9:9; PROVERBS 18:10"> To the overcomer, the song of the sons of Korah, for youths. Our God, thou art refuge, and virtue; helper in tribulations, that have found us greatly. (For the choirmaster, a song by the Korahites, for youths. O God, thou art our refuge, and our strength; a great helper in the midst of all the troubles, that have found us.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 23:4; HEBREWS 13:6; PSALMS 27:3"> Therefore we shall not dread, while the earth shall be troubled; and the hills shall be borne over into the heart of the sea. (And so we shall not fear, when the earth be troubled; and the mountains be carried over into the heart of the sea.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 93:3–4; JEREMIAH 5:22; JOB 9:5–6"> The waters of them sounded, and were troubled; hills were troubled (al)together in the strength of him. (And when the waters roar, and be troubled; and the mountains be altogether troubled by his strength, or his might.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:1–3; EZEKIEL 47:1–12; PSALMS 36:8–9"> The fierceness of &#91;the&#93; flood maketh glad the city of God; the highest God hath hallowed his tabernacle. (There is a river whose streams make glad the city of God; and the Most High God hath hallowed his Temple.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 2:5; ZEPHANIAH 3:15; JOEL 2:27"> God in the midst thereof shall not be moved; God shall help it early in the gray morrowtide. (God is there in its midst, and that city shall not be shaken, or destroyed; God shall help it early in the gray morning or at the break of day.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="AMOS 9:5; PSALMS 18:13; HABAKKUK 3:5–6"> Heathen men were troubled (al)together, and realms were bowed down; God gave his voice, the earth was moved. (But the heathen were altogether troubled, and kingdoms were bowed down; yea, God gave his voice, and the earth shook.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 9:9; ROMANS 8:31; NUMBERS 14:9"> The Lord of virtues is with us; God of Jacob is our up-taker. (The Lord of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our refuge or the God of Jacob is our stronghold, or our defender.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 66:5; ISAIAH 24:1; ISAIAH 61:4"> Come ye, and see the works of the Lord; which wonders he hath set on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 2:4; MICAH 4:3–4; MICAH 5:10"> He doing away battles till to the end of the land; shall all-bruise bow, and he shall break (al)together armours, and he shall burn shields with fire. (He doeth away, or stoppeth, all the battles unto the ends of the earth; he altogether breaketh the bows, and the arms, or the weapons, and he burneth up the shields with fire.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 100:3; HABAKKUK 2:20; EZEKIEL 38:23"> Give ye attention, and see ye, that I am God; I shall be enhanced among heathen men; and I shall be enhanced in earth.
