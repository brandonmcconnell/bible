<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 146:1–2; PSALMS 57:7–11; JOHN 4:24"> A psalm of David. (A song by David.) My soul, bless thou the Lord, and all things that be within me, bless his holy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 63:7; PSALMS 116:12; PSALMS 105:5"> My soul, bless thou the Lord; and do not thou forget all the yieldings of him. (My soul, bless thou the Lord; and do not thou forget about all his gifts to thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 15:26; ISAIAH 53:5; JEREMIAH 17:14"> Which doeth mercy to all thy wickednesses; which healeth all thy sicknesses. (Who showeth mercy to all thy sins, or all thy wickednesses; who healeth all thy sicknesses.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 56:13; PSALMS 71:23; PSALMS 103:12"> Which again-buyeth thy life from death; which crowneth thee in mercy and merciful doings. (Who redeemeth thy life from death; who crowneth thee with love and merciful doings.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:31; PSALMS 107:9; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:17"> Which &#91;ful&#93; filleth thy desire in goods; thy youth shall be renewed as the youth of an eagle. (Who fulfilleth thy desires with good things; thy youth shall be renewed like the youth of an eagle.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 9:9; PSALMS 12:5; PSALMS 72:12"> The Lord doing mercies; and doom, to all men suffering wrong. (The Lord giveth mercy, and justice, to all who suffer wrong.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 33:13; PSALMS 147:19; PSALMS 78:11"> He made his ways known to Moses; his wills to the sons of Israel (or his deeds to the Israelites).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 86:15; JOEL 2:13; EXODUS 34:6–7"> The Lord is a merciful doer, and merciful in will, (or The Lord is a giver of love, and a giver of mercy); long abiding, and much merciful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 57:16; PSALMS 30:5; MICAH 7:18–19"> He shall not be wroth &#91;into&#93; without end; and he shall not threaten &#91;into&#93; without end. (He shall not be angry forever; and he shall not threaten forever.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZRA 9:13; LAMENTATIONS 3:22; NEHEMIAH 9:31"> He did not to us after our sins; neither he yielded to us after our wickednesses. (He did not do to us what we deserved for our sins; nor did he punish us for all our wickednesses.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 57:10; LUKE 1:50; PSALMS 36:5"> For by the highness of heaven from earth; he made strong his mercy on men dreading him. (For as high as the heavens be above the earth, is how strong his love is, for those who fear him or for those who revere him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:25; 1ST JOHN 1:7; MICAH 7:18"> As much as the east is from the west; he made &#91;a&#93; far our wickednesses from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 11:11–12; HEBREWS 12:5–11; LUKE 15:21–22"> As a father hath mercy on his sons, the Lord had mercy on men dreading him; (Like a father hath love for his sons and daughters, so the Lord hath love for those who fear him or for those who revere him;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 12:7; JOB 10:9; PSALMS 78:38–39"> for he knew our making. He bethought that we be dust (or He remembered that we be but dust),</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST PETER 1:24; JAMES 1:10–11; ISAIAH 51:12"> a man is as hay; his day&#91;s&#93; shall flower out so as a flower of the field. (for a person’s days (be) like the hay, (or the grass); and he shall flower out like the flowers in the field.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:7; JOB 20:9; JOB 14:10"> For the spirit shall pass in him, and it shall not abide; and it shall no more know his place. (For the wind passeth over it, and it shall not remain there long; and then its place shall know it no more.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 100:5; EXODUS 20:6; EPHESIANS 2:4–7"> But the mercy of the Lord is from without beginning, and till into without end; on men dreading him. And his rightfulness is into the sons of sons; (But the Lord’s loveis without beginning, and without end; for those who fear him or for those who revere him. And his righteousness shall never fail the children of their children,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:9; PSALMS 132:12; LUKE 1:6"> to them that keep his testament. And be mindful of his commandments; to do those. (yea, they who keep his covenant; and who remember his commandments, and who do them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 47:2; PHILIPPIANS 2:9–10; PSALMS 9:7"> The Lord hath made ready his seat in heaven; and his realm shall be lord of all. (The Lord hath prepared his throne in heaven; and he shall rule over all as their King.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="HEBREWS 1:14; PSALMS 148:2; LUKE 1:19"> Angels of the Lord, bless ye the Lord; ye mighty in virtue, doing his word, to hear the voice of his words. (Angels of the Lord, bless ye the Lord; ye mighty and strong, who do his word, yea, ye who obey him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST KINGS 22:19; NEHEMIAH 9:6; LUKE 2:13"> All virtues of the Lord, bless ye the Lord; ye ministers of him, that do his will. (All the hosts of the Lord, bless ye the Lord; ye who be his servants, who do his will.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ISAIAH 49:13; PSALMS 145:10; ISAIAH 42:10–12"> All works of the Lord, bless ye the Lord, in each place of his lordship; my soul, bless thou the Lord. (All his creatures, bless ye the Lord, everywhere that he ruleth; my soul, bless the Lord!)</span>
