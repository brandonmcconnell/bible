<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 15:6; ISAIAH 52:10; PSALMS 33:3"> A psalm. Sing ye a new song to the Lord; for he hath done marvels. His right hand and his holy arm; hath made health to him. (A song. Sing ye a new song to the Lord; for he hath done marvellous deeds. His right hand, or his power, and his holy arm, have won him the victory.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 52:10; ISAIAH 62:2; PSALMS 22:31"> The Lord hath made known his health; in the sight of heathen men he hath showed his rightfulness. (The Lord hath made known his victory; yea, he hath shown his righteousness before the heathen.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 22:27; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:47; DEUTERONOMY 4:31"> He bethought on his mercy; and on his truth, to the house of Israel. All the ends of earth; saw the health of our God. (He remembered his love, and his faithfulness, toward the house of Israel. All the ends of the earth; have seen the victory of our God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:23; PSALMS 100:1; PSALMS 66:1"> All earth, make ye heartily joy to God; sing ye, and make ye full out joy, and say ye psalm. (All the earth, heartily make ye joy to God; sing ye, and rejoice, and sing ye a song.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ISAIAH 51:3; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 5:8; PSALMS 33:2"> Sing ye to the Lord in an harp, in harp and in voice of psalm; (Sing ye to the Lord on a harp, yea, on a harp, and with the words of a song or and with the strains of a lute;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 81:2–4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:16; 2ND CHRONICLES 15:14"> in trumps beaten out with hammer, and in voice of a trump of (a) horn. Heartily sing ye in the sight of the Lord, the king; (and with trumpets beaten out with a hammer, yea, with the sound of trumpets and horns. Heartily sing ye before the Lord, the King;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 49:13; PSALMS 24:1; PSALMS 96:11–1"> the sea and the fullness thereof be moved; the world, and they that dwell therein. (let the sea roar, and all the creatures in it; yea, the whole world, and all that live in it!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 55:12; PSALMS 89:12; PSALMS 65:12–13"> Floods shall make joy with hand; together hills shall make full out joy, (The rivers make joy, or clap, with their hands; the hills rejoice together,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 96:13; PSALMS 96:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:31"> for the sight of the Lord; for he cometh to deem the earth. He shall deem the world in rightfulness; and peoples in equity. (before the Lord; for he cometh to judge the earth. He shall judge the world with righteousness; and the peoples with justice, or with fairness.)</span>
