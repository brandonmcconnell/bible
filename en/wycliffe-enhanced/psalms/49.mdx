<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 78:1; PSALMS 33:8; MATTHEW 11:15"> To victory, a psalm to the sons of Korah. All ye folks, hear these things; all ye that dwell in the world, perceive with ears. (For the choirmaster, a song by the Korahites. All ye nations, hear these things; all ye who live in the world, listen!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 62:9; PROVERBS 22:2; JEREMIAH 5:4–5"> All the sons of earth, and the sons of men; together the rich man, and the poor into one. (All the sons and daughters of the earth, the sons and daughters of men; yea, the rich and the poor, together as one.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 19:14; PROVERBS 22:17; MATTHEW 12:35"> My mouth shall speak wisdom; and the thinking of mine heart shall speak prudence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 78:2; MATTHEW 13:35; NUMBERS 12:8"> I shall bow down mine ear into a parable; I shall open my reason set forth in a psaltery. (I shall listen closely to a parable; then I shall set forth my understanding on a lute.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 27:24; ROMANS 8:33–34; AMOS 5:13"> Why shall I dread in the evil day? the wickedness of mine heel shall (en)compass me. (Why should I have fear, or be afraid, on the day of evil, when the wickedness of my enemies shall surround me?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 6:17; PSALMS 52:7; JEREMIAH 9:23"> Which trust in their own virtue (or They who trust in their wealth); and have glory in the multitude of their riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:26; 1ST TIMOTHY 2:6; 1ST PETER 1:18"> A brother again-buyeth not, shall a man again-buy? and he shall not give to God his pleasing. (No one shall ever be able to redeem himself; he shall never be able to pay God the price that he asketh for him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:26; JOB 36:18–19"> And he shall not (be able to) give the price of ransom for his soul; and he shall travail into without end, (Yea, he shall never be able to pay the price of ransom for his own soul; even if he could work forever,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 89:48; PSALMS 16:10; ECCLESIASTES 8:8"> and he shall live yet into the end. He shall not see perishing, (so that he would live forever, and not see perishing, or corruption.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKE 12:20; PSALMS 39:6; HEBREWS 9:27"> when he shall see wise men dying; the unwise man and the fool shall perish together. And they shall leave their riches to aliens; (For he seeth that the wise die; and that the foolish and the ignorant perish together with them. But they all leave their riches to others, even strangers;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 4:17; PSALMS 5:9; PSALMS 64:6"> and the sepulchres of them be the houses of them without end. The tabernacles of them be in generation and in generation; they called their names in their lands. (and their tombs, or their graves, shall be their houses forever. Yea, their dwelling places for all generations; even though their lands were once called by their own names.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 3:18–21; PSALMS 49:20; PSALMS 82:7"> A man/Man, when he was in honour, understood not; he is comparisoned to unwise beasts, and is made like to those. (For anyone, even when he hath been given great honour, liveth not forever; he is comparable to the unthinking beasts, and soon is made like them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 12:20; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:19; LUKE 16:27–28"> This way of them is cause of stumbling to them; and afterward they shall please (al)together in their mouth. (Their way is a trap for themselves; and for all who seek to please them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MALACHI 4:3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:2; DANIEL 7:22"> As sheep they be put in hell; death shall gnaw them. And just men shall be lords of them in the morrowtide; and the help of them shall wax eld in hell, for the glory of them or from the glory of them. (Like sheep they go down to Sheol, or the land of the dead; and death shall gnaw on them. The righteous shall be their lords; and their bodies shall grow old, or rotten, in Sheol, so different from their days of glory.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 73:24; PSALMS 56:13; PSALMS 86:13"> Nevertheless God shall again-buy my soul from the power of hell; when he shall take me. (But God shall redeem my soul; and he shall take me away from the power of Sheol, or the power of death.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 37:7; PSALMS 37:1; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:26"> Dread thou not (or Fear thou not), when a man is made rich; and when the glory of his house is multiplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 6:7; JOB 27:19; LUKE 16:24"> For when he shall die, he shall not take all things with him(or he shall not take anything with him); and his glory shall not go down with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKE 12:19; DEUTERONOMY 29:19; PSALMS 10:3"> For his soul shall be blessed in his life; he shall acknowledge to thee, when thou hast done well to him. (Even though his soul shall be blessed in this life; and people shall praise him, when he hath done well for himself.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOB 33:30; GENESIS 15:15; PSALMS 56:13"> He shall enter till into the generations of his fathers; and till into without end he shall not see light. (He shall go to the generations of his forefathers; and none of them shall ever see the light again.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 49:12; ECCLESIASTES 3:18–19; JOB 4:21"> A man/Man, when he was in honour, understood not; he is comparisoned to unwise beasts, and is made like to those. (Anyone, even when he hath been given great honour, liveth not forever; he is comparable to the unthinking beasts, and soon is made like them.)</span>
