Lord, I have put my trust in thee, let me never be shamed; save thou me in thy righteousness.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 71:2–3; PSALMS 102:2; PSALMS 86:1"> Bow down thine ear to me; haste thou to deliver me. Be thou to me into God a defender, and into an house of refuge; that thou make me safe. (Bow down thy ear to me; hasten thou to rescue me. God, be my defender, and a house of refuge; so that thou keep me safe or so that thou save me.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 143:10–11; LUKE 1:79; PSALMS 79:9"> For thou art my strength and my refuge; and for (the sake of) thy name, thou shalt lead me forth, and shalt nourish me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 25:15; PSALMS 140:5; PSALMS 19:14"> Thou shalt lead me out of the snare, which they hid to me (or which they have hid for me); for thou art my defender.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKE 23:46; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:59; DEUTERONOMY 32:4"> I betake my spirit into thine hands; Lord God of truth, thou hast again-bought me. (I commit, or I entrust, my spirit into thy hands; Lord God of truth, thou hast redeemed me.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JONAH 2:8; PSALMS 24:4; JEREMIAH 10:15"> Thou hatest them that keep vanities superfluously. Forsooth I hoped in the Lord; (Thou hatest them who worship false gods, or useless idols. But I put my trust in the Lord;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 49:13; ISAIAH 63:9; PSALMS 119:153"> I shall have fully joy, and shall be glad in thy mercy. For thou beheldest my meekness; thou savedest my life from needs. (I shall have full out joy, or rejoice, and shall be glad for thy love. For thou hast beheld my troubles; thou hast saved my life from dis-eases, or distress.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 36:16; DEUTERONOMY 32:30; PSALMS 18:19"> And thou closedest not me (al)together within the hands of the enemy; thou hast set my feet in a large place. (And thou enclosedest me not in the hands of the enemy; thou hast set me free in a large place.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 6:7; PSALMS 88:9; PSALMS 73:26"> Lord, have thou mercy on me, for I am troubled; mine eye is troubled in ire, my soul and my womb also. (Lord, have thou mercy on me, for I am in trouble; my eyes be tired from so much grief, and my soul, and my womb, also be tired.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 88:15; PSALMS 32:3–4; PSALMS 38:3"> For why my life failed in sorrow; and my years in wailings. My virtue is made feeble in poverty; and my bones be troubled. (For my life is failing, because of sorrows; and my years, because of wailings. My strength is made feeble, or weak, by poverty; and my bones be diseased.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 38:11; PSALMS 88:8; PSALMS 88:18"> Over all mine enemies I am made (a) shame, &#91;and&#93; greatly to my neighbours; and dread to my known. They that saw me withoutforth, fled from me; (I am shamed before all my enemies, and especially my neighbours; and I am thought of as someone to be feared by my acquaintances. Those who saw me withoutforth, fled from me;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 88:4–5; PSALMS 2:9; ROMANS 9:21–22"> I am given to forgetting, as a dead man from the heart. I am made as a (for)lorn vessel; (I am forgotten, like a dead man out of mind. I am made like a forlorn vessel;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 20:10; MATTHEW 27:1; LAMENTATIONS 2:22"> for I heard despising of many men dwelling in compass. In that thing while they came together against me; they counselled to take my life. (for I have heard the despising of many men who live all around me. And when they came together against me; they plotted to take away my life.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 56:3–4; PSALMS 18:2; PSALMS 43:5"> But, Lord, I hoped in thee, (or But, Lord, I put my trust in thee); I said, Thou art my God;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:7; ECCLESIASTES 3:1–8; JOHN 17:1"> my times be in thine hands. Deliver thou me from the hands of mine enemies; and from them that pursue me. (my life is in thy hands. Rescue thou me from the power of my enemies; and from those who persecute me.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 4:6; PSALMS 6:4; DANIEL 9:9"> Make thou clear thy face on thy servant; Lord, make thou me safe in thy mercy; (Make thy face to shine upon thy servant; Lord, save thou me in thy love;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 25:2–3; JEREMIAH 20:11; PSALMS 34:5"> be I not shamed, for I inwardly called thee. Unpious men be ashamed, and be they led forth into hell; (let me not be shamed, for I have called upon thee. Let the unpious, or the unrighteous, be ashamed, and let them go down to Sheol, or the land of the dead;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JUDE 1:15; PSALMS 94:4; 1ST SAMUEL 2:3"> guileful lips be made dumb. That speak wickedness against a just man; in pride, and in mis-using/in pride, and in abusing. (and let deceitful lips be made dumb. Yea, those who speak wickedness against the righteous; in pride, and in mis-using, or in pride, and in abusing.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 2:9; ISAIAH 64:4; LAMENTATIONS 3:23–25"> Lord, the multitude of thy sweetness is full great; which thou hast hid to men dreading thee. Thou hast made a perfect thing to them that hope in thee; in the sight of the sons of men. (Lord, the multitude of thy goodness is very great; which thou hast kept safe for those who fear thee or for those who revere thee. Thou hast made a perfect thing for those who put their trust in thee; before the sons and daughters of men.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 27:5; PSALMS 32:7; PSALMS 91:1–4"> Thou shalt hide them in the private of thy face; from &#91;the&#93; troubling of men, (or Thou shalt hide them in the secret of thy presence; from all the troubles caused by people). Thou shalt defend them in thy tabernacle; from &#91;the&#93; against-saying of tongues.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 23:7–13; PSALMS 17:7; 1ST PETER 2:9"> Blessed be the Lord; for he hath made wonderful his mercy to me in a strengthened city. (Blessed be the Lord; for he hath shown his wonderful love to me, like a city that is strengthened, or fortified.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 116:11; HEBREWS 5:7; PSALMS 88:16"> Forsooth I said in the out-passing of my soul; I am cast out from the face of thine eyes. Therefore thou heardest the voice of my prayer; while I cried to thee. (For I said in the going forth of my soul, I am cast out from before thine eyes. But thou heardest the words of my prayer; when I cried to thee for help.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 97:10; PSALMS 94:2; PSALMS 145:20"> All ye holy men of the Lord, love him; for the Lord shall seek truth, and he shall yield plenteously to them that do pride. (All ye holy people of the Lord, love him; for the Lord preserveth the faithful, but he shall strongly punish the proud.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 27:14; PSALMS 146:5; COLOSSIANS 1:11"> All ye that hope in the Lord, do ye manly; and your heart be comforted. (All ye who hope in the Lord, be ye encouraged; and let your hearts be strengthened.)</span>
