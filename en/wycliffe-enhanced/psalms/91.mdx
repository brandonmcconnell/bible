<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 18:2; ISAIAH 26:3–4; PROVERBS 18:10"> He shall say to the Lord, Thou art mine up-taker, and my refuge; my God, I shall hope in him. (He shall say to the Lord, Thou art my defender, and my refuge; my God, I trust in thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 124:7; PSALMS 141:9; JOB 5:10–22"> For he delivered me from the snare of hunters; and from a sharp word. (For he shall save me from the hunter’s snare; and from a sharp word.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 61:4; PSALMS 57:1; PSALMS 17:8"> With his shoulders he shall make shadow to thee; and thou shalt have hope under his feathers. His truth shall (en)compass thee with a shield; (With his feathers he shall make a shadow for thee; and thou shalt have hope under his wings. His faithfulness shall surround thee like a shield.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:2; JOB 5:19–27; PROVERBS 3:23–25"> thou shalt not dread of the night’s dread. Of an arrow flying in the day, (Thou shalt not fear the terror in the night; nor an arrow flying in the day.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 121:5–6; NUMBERS 16:48; MATTHEW 24:6–7"> of a goblin going in darknesses; of assailing, and of a midday fiend. (Nor the pestilence going in darkness; nor the assailing of the plague at midday.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 12:12–13; GENESIS 7:23; PSALMS 32:6"> A thousand shall fall down from thy side, and ten thousand from thy right side; forsooth it shall not nigh to thee. (A thousand shall fall at thy side, and ten thousand at thy right side; but it shall not come even close to thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 37:34; PROVERBS 3:25–26; PSALMS 92:11"> Nevertheless thou shalt behold with thine eyes; and thou shalt see the yielding of sinners. (Nevertheless thou shalt see with thine eyes; yea, thou shalt see the punishment of the sinners.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 71:3; PSALMS 90:1; PSALMS 91:1–2"> For thou, Lord, art mine hope; thou hast set thine help (to be the) alder-Highest. (For thou hast made the Lord to be thy hope; yea, the Most High to be thy help.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:21; PSALMS 121:7; DEUTERONOMY 7:15"> Evil shall not come to thee; and a scourge shall not (come) nigh to thy tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 4:10–11; PSALMS 34:7; HEBREWS 1:14"> For God hath commanded to his angels of thee; that they keep thee in all thy ways. (For God hath commanded his angels to be all around thee; so that they keep thee safe on all thy ways.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 37:24; PROVERBS 3:23; LUKE 4:11"> They shall bear thee in the hands; lest peradventure thou hurt thy foot at a stone. (They shall lift thee up with their hands; lest thou hurt thy foot on a stone.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 10:19; DANIEL 6:22; MARK 16:18"> Thou shalt go upon a snake, and a cockatrice; and thou shalt defoul a lion, and a dragon, (or and thou shalt trample upon a lion, and a dragon).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 9:10; JOHN 14:23; JAMES 1:12"> (For God saith), For he hoped in me, I shall deliver him, (or For God saith, Because he loved me, I shall save him); I shall defend him, for he knew my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:3; ISAIAH 65:24; PSALMS 50:15"> He cried to me, and I shall hear him; I am with him in tribulation; I shall deliver him, and I shall glorify him. (When he crieth to me, I shall answer him; I shall be with him in all his troubles; I shall rescue him, and I shall honour him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:2; PROVERBS 3:16; PSALMS 21:4"> I shall &#91;ful&#93; fill him with the length of days; and I shall show mine health to him.
