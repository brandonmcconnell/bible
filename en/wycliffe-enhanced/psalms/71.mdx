</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 22:9–10; JEREMIAH 1:5; PSALMS 139:15–16"> In thee I am confirmed, that is, defended, from the womb; thou art my defender from the womb of my mother. My singing is ever&#91;more&#93; in thee (or My praises shall be of thee forevermore);</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 8:18; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:13"> I am made as a great wonder to many men; and thou art a strong helper. (I am made a great example to many men; but thou art my strong helper.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 35:28; PSALMS 146:2; PSALMS 104:1"> My mouth be filled with praising; that I sing thy glory, all day thy greatness. (My mouth shall be filled with praise; and I shall sing of thy glory, and of thy greatness, all day long.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 46:4; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:12; PSALMS 71:18"> Cast thou not away me in the time of eld (age)/in the time of oldness; when my strength faileth, forsake thou not me. (Cast thou me not away in the time of old age; when my strength faileth, desert thou me not.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 83:3; PSALMS 56:6; JEREMIAH 20:10"> For mine enemies said of me; and they that kept my life made counsel together. (For my enemies spoke against me; those who laid ambush for me took counsel together.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 7:2; PSALMS 3:2; MATTHEW 27:46"> Saying, God hath forsaken him; pursue ye, and take him; for none there is that shall deliver. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 35:22; PSALMS 38:21–22; PSALMS 70:5"> God, be thou not made afar from me; my God, behold thou into mine help. (God, be thou not made far away from me; my God, hasten thou to help me.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 20:11; PSALMS 71:24; PSALMS 35:26"> Men that backbite my soul, be shamed, and fail they; and be they covered with reproof and shame, that seek evils to me. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 130:7; 1ST JOHN 3:3; PSALMS 71:6"> But I shall hope ever&#91;more&#93;; and I shall add to ever over all thy praising. (But I shall have hope in thee forevermore; and I shall praise thee more and more.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

For I know not by literature, (that is, by man’s teaching, or by reading and study, but by the revelation of God).)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:13; PSALMS 51:14; PHILIPPIANS 3:9"> I shall enter into the powers of the Lord; Lord, I shall bethink on thy rightfulness alone. (I shall walk in the Lord’s strength; I shall tell out thy righteousness, thine alone.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 71:5; PSALMS 119:9; PSALMS 119:102"> God, thou hast taught me from my youth, and till to now; I shall tell out thy marvels. (God, thou hast taught me from my youth; and I have told out all thy marvellous deeds.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 46:4; PSALMS 71:9; PSALMS 78:4"> And till into eld (age)/into oldness, and the last age; God, forsake thou not me. And I shall tell of thy power, or of thy might, to each generation yet to come.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 35:10; PSALMS 36:5–6; PSALMS 57:10"> Till I tell (of) thy might, and thy rightfulness, God, till into the highest great deeds which thou hast done; God, who is like thee? (Yea, until I tell of thy might, or of thy power, and thy righteousness, O God; and of the greatest deeds which thou hast done! O God, who is like thee!)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 138:7; PSALMS 60:3; PSALMS 86:13"> How great tribulations, many and evil, hast thou showed to me; and thou converted, hast quickened me, and hast again-brought me again from the depths of earth. (What great troubles, many and evil, thou hast sent me! and then turned, thou hast granted me life, and hast brought me up again from the watery depths of the earth or and hast brought me up again from the grave.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ISAIAH 12:1; 2ND SAMUEL 3:1; 2ND CORINTHIANS 2:14"> Thou hast multiplied thy great doing; and thou converted (or and thou turned), hast comforted me.</span>
