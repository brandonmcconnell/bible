<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 33:27; PSALMS 71:3; 1ST JOHN 4:16"> The prayer of Moses, the man of God. Lord, thou art made (a) help to us; from generation into generation. (A prayer of Moses, a man of God. Lord, thou hast helped us; from generation unto generation.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:8; GENESIS 1:1; PSALMS 102:24–27"> Before that hills were made, either the earth and the world was formed; from the world and into the world, thou art God. (Before that the mountains were made, and the earth and the world was formed; forever and ever, thou art God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 3:19; ECCLESIASTES 12:7; PSALMS 146:4"> Turn thou not away a man into lowness; and thou saidest, Ye sons of men, be turned again. (Thou hast turned people back to the lowness from whence they came; yea, thou sayest, Ye sons and daughters of men, return to dust.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND PETER 3:8; PSALMS 39:5; MATTHEW 24:43"> For a thousand years be before thine eyes; as yesterday, which is passed, and as &#91;the&#93; keeping in the night. (For in thine eyes a thousand years be like yesterday, which is just passed; yea, as fleeting as the night watch.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:6; PSALMS 73:20; JOB 22:16"> The years of them shall be; that be had for nought. Early pass he, as an herb, (And their years shall be as if nothing. In the morning they shall come forth, like a blade of grass,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOB 14:2; PSALMS 92:7; JAMES 1:11"> early flourish he, and pass; in the eventide fall he down, be he hard, and wax he dry. (yea, that flourisheth in the morning, and groweth up; and then, in the evening, it falleth down, and hardeneth, and groweth dry.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 39:11; PSALMS 59:13; EXODUS 14:24"> For we have failed in thine ire; and we be troubled in thy strong vengeance. (For we be brought to an end by thy anger; and we be dis-eased, or distressed, by thy fury.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 16:17; ECCLESIASTES 12:14; HEBREWS 4:12–13"> Thou hast set our wickednesses in thy sight; our world in the lightening of thy cheer. (Thou hast set our wickednesses before thee; our secret sins in the full light of thy face.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 78:33; PSALMS 39:5; PSALMS 90:4"> For all our days have failed; and we have failed in thine ire. Our years (we) shall bethink upon as a spider; (For all our days be brought to an end by thy anger. All our years we shall remember as but a whisper;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JAMES 4:14; PSALMS 78:39; 2ND SAMUEL 19:35"> the days of our years be those seventy years. Forsooth, if fourscore years/if eighty years be in mighty men; and (yet) the more time of them is travail and sorrow. For mildness came above; and we shall be chastised. (and the days of our years be those seventy years. For strong people, they be eighty years; yet most of that time is trouble, or labour, and sorrow. For life is short; and then we be gone.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:24; ISAIAH 33:14; NAHUM 1:6"> Who knew the power of thine ire; and durst number thine ire for thy dread? (Who knoweth the power of thy anger? and who knoweth thy anger better than those who fear thee?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:16–17; PSALMS 39:4; JOHN 9:4"> Make thy right hand so known; and make men learned in heart by wisdom. (Make thy right hand, or thy power, known to us, or Teach us that our days be short; and so make people learned in their hearts with thy wisdom.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 106:45; PSALMS 135:14; DEUTERONOMY 32:36"> Lord, be thou converted some-deal; and be thou able to be prayed of (by) thy servants. (Lord, how long until thou be turned somewhat; and then thou be able to be prayed to, or petitioned by, thy servants?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 23:6; PSALMS 86:4; PSALMS 31:7"> We were &#91;ful&#93; filled early with thy mercy; we made full out joy, and we delighted in all our days. (Fulfill us or Fill us full each morning with thy love; so that we can rejoice, and be glad, in all our days.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 126:5–6; PSALMS 30:5; JOHN 16:20"> We were glad for the days in which thou madest us meek; for the years in which we saw evils. (Make us glad now in recompense for the days in which thou hast humbled us; yea, for the years in which we endured evils.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HABAKKUK 3:2; PSALMS 44:1; DEUTERONOMY 32:4"> Lord, behold thou into thy servants, and into thy works; and (ad)dress thou, (or direct thou), the sons of them. (Lord, let thy servants see thy mighty works; and our sons and daughters see thy glory.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:7; JOB 22:28"> And the shining of our Lord God be on us; and (ad)dress thou the works of our hands on us; and (ad)dress thou the works of our hands. (And let the shining of the Lord our God be upon us, and direct thou the works of our hands for us; yea, direct thou the works of our hands.)</span>
