<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 22:1–51; COLOSSIANS 1:11; 1ST JOHN 4:19"> To victory, the word of the Lord to David, which spake the words of this song, in the day in which the Lord delivered him from the hand of all his enemies, and from the hand of Saul; and he said: Lord, my strength, I shall love thee<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Compare Psalm 18:1-50to 2nd Samuel 22:1-51.">a</a></sup>; (For the choirmaster, the word of the Lord to David, who spoke the words of this song, on the day in which the Lord rescued him from the power of all his enemies, and from the power of Saul; and he said, O Lord, my strength, I shall love thee;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 144:2; PSALMS 62:7; PSALMS 19:14"> the Lord is my steadfastness, and my refuge, and my deliverer. My God is mine helper; and I shall hope in him. My defender, and the horn of mine health; and mine up-taker, (or My God is my helper; and I shall trust him. My defender, and the horn of my salvation, or of my deliverance; and my rescuer).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 91:15; PSALMS 145:3; PSALMS 62:8"> I shall praise, and inwardly call the Lord; and I shall be safe from mine enemies. (I shall praise him, and I shall call on the Lord; and I shall be saved from my enemies.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 116:3; MATTHEW 27:24–25; MARK 14:33–34"> The sorrows of death (en)compassed me; and the strands of wickedness have troubled me. (The sorrows of death surrounded me; and the streams of wickedness have flowed over me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 86:13; PSALMS 88:3–8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:24"> The sorrows of hell (en)compassed me; the snares of death before-occupied me. (The sorrows of Sheol, or of the land of the dead, surrounded me./The sorrows of the grave surrounded me; the snares of death were set for me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; 2ND SAMUEL 22:7; PSALMS 34:15"> In my tribulation I inwardly called the Lord; and I cried to my God. And he heard my voice from his holy temple; and my cry in his sight entered into his ears. (In my trouble I called to the Lord; yea, I cried to my God. And he heard my voice in his holy Temple; yea, my cry came before him, and entered into his ears.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:31; PSALMS 68:7–8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:25–26"> The earth was moved together, and trembled greatly; the foundaments of hills were troubled together, and moved together, for he was wroth to them. (The earth was altogether shaken, and greatly trembled; the foundations of the mountains were altogether troubled, and altogether shaken, for he was angry.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 21:9; PSALMS 11:6; NAHUM 1:5–6"> Smoke went up in the ire of the Lord, and fire burnt out from his face; coals were kindled of him. (Smoke went up from his nostrils, and devouring fire came out of his mouth; and coals were kindled by it.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 144:5–15; DEUTERONOMY 33:26; 2ND SAMUEL 22:10"> He bowed down (the) heavens, and came down; and darkness was under his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 104:3; 2ND SAMUEL 22:11–12; PSALMS 99:1"> And he ascended on cherubim, and flew; he flew over the pens of winds. (And he went up on cherubim, and flew; yea, he flew upon the wings of the wind.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 97:2; DEUTERONOMY 4:11; PSALMS 91:1"> And he setted darknesses his hiding place, his tabernacle in his compass; and dark water was in the clouds of the air. (And he made the darkness his hiding place; and the clouds of the heavens, which were filled with water, surrounded him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:21; MATTHEW 17:2"> Full clear clouds passed in his sight; hail and the coals of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 104:7; 1ST SAMUEL 7:10; PSALMS 140:10"> And the Lord thundered from heaven; and the highest gave his voice, hail and coals of fire. (And the Lord thundered from the heavens; yea, the Most High sent forth his voice, and hail, and coals of fire.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 144:6; HABAKKUK 3:11; NUMBERS 24:8"> And he sent his arrows, and destroyed those men; he multiplied lightnings, and troubled those men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 106:9; EXODUS 15:8; MICAH 6:2"> And the wells of waters appeared; and the foundaments of the earth were showed. Lord, of thy blaming; of the breathing of the spirit of thine ire. (And the seabed appeared; and the foundations of the earth were uncovered. Yea, by the Lord’s rebuke; by the blast of the breath from his nostrils.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 22:17; PSALMS 144:7; PSALMS 57:3"> He sent from the highest place, and took me; and he took me from many waters. (He reached down from the highest place, and took hold of me; yea, he took me out of the deep waters.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HEBREWS 2:14–15; PSALMS 35:10; PSALMS 142:6"> He delivered me from my strongest enemies; and from them that hated me, for they were comforted on me. (He rescued me from my strongest enemies; yea, from those who hated me, for they were too strong for me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="OBADIAH 1:10–14; DEUTERONOMY 32:35; PSALMS 46:11"> They came before me in the day of my torment; and the Lord was made my defender.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 118:5; PSALMS 31:8; PSALMS 18:36"> And he led out me into breadth; he made me safe, for he would me. (And he led me out into an open place; he saved me, for he delighted in me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 24:4; MATTHEW 6:4; PSALMS 58:11"> And the Lord shall yield to me by my rightfulness; and he shall yield to me by the cleanness of mine hands. (And the Lord rewarded me according to my righteousness; he rewarded me according to the cleanness of my hands.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 119:102; PSALMS 17:4; PSALMS 26:1"> For I (have) kept the ways of the Lord; and I did not (go away) unfaithfully from my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 119:13; PSALMS 119:112; PSALMS 119:30"> For all his dooms be in my sight; and I putted not away from me his rightfulnesses. (For all his laws were before me; and I did not go away from his commands.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:8–9; PSALMS 11:7; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:17"> And I shall be unwemmed with him; and I shall keep me from my wickedness. (And I was without blemish, or without fault, before him; for I have kept myself from my own wickedness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:41–42; RUTH 2:12; 1ST SAMUEL 26:23"> And the Lord shall yield to me by my rightfulness; and by the cleanness of mine hands in the sight of his eyes. (And so the Lord rewarded me according to my righteousness; and according to the cleanness of my hands before him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:7; PSALMS 62:12; MATTHEW 18:33–35"> With the holy, thou shalt be holy; and with an innocent man, thou shalt be innocent. (With the holy, O Lord, thou shalt be holy; and with the innocent, thou shalt be innocent.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:34; LEVITICUS 26:23–24; JAMES 2:13"> And with a chosen man, thou shalt be chosen; and with a wayward man, thou shalt be wayward. (And with the chosen, thou shalt be chosen; but to the wicked, thou shalt be vengeful, or punishing.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ISAIAH 66:2; JAMES 2:5; ISAIAH 57:15"> For thou shalt make safe a meek people; and thou shalt make meek the eyes of proud men. (And thou shalt save, or help, the poor; but thou shalt humble those who be high in their own eyes or but thou shalt look with contempt upon the proud.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 22:29; JOB 29:3; ISAIAH 42:16"> For thou, Lord, lightenest my lantern; my God, lighten thou my darknesses. (For thou, Lord, brightenest my lantern; my God, thou lightest up my darknesses.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 22:30; EPHESIANS 6:10–13; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:9–10"> For by thee I shall be delivered from temptation; and in my God I shall go over the wall. (For I shall be rescued,or saved, from temptation by thee; and by my God or and with God’s help, I shall go over the wall.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:4; PROVERBS 30:5; 2ND SAMUEL 22:31"> My God, his way is undefouled; the speeches of the Lord be examined by fire; he is &#91;the&#93; defender of all men hoping in him. (My God, his way is undefiled; the words of the Lord be assayed by fire, that is, they be tried and tested, and found to be true; he is the defender of all who put their trust in him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:31; PSALMS 86:8; 2ND SAMUEL 22:32"> For why, who is God, except the Lord? either who is God, except our God? (For who else is God, except the Lord? yea, who else is God, except our God?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ISAIAH 45:5; PSALMS 28:7; 2ND SAMUEL 22:33"> God that hath girded me with virtue; and hath set my way unwemmed. (God who hath girded me with strength; and hath made my way without blemish, or without fault.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="HABAKKUK 3:19; DEUTERONOMY 32:13; DEUTERONOMY 33:29"> Which made perfect my feet as the feet of harts; and ordaining me on high things. (Who made my feet like the feet of harts; and hath ordained me upon the high places.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 144:1; PSALMS 46:9; 2ND SAMUEL 22:36"> Which teacheth mine hands to battle; and thou hast set mine arms as a brazen bow. (Who teacheth my hands in battle; and now my arms can break a bronze bow.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 22:36; JAMES 3:17–18; PSALMS 28:7"> And thou hast given to me the covering of thine health; and thy right hand hath up-taken me, (or And thou hast given me the covering of thy salvation, or thy deliverance; and thy right hand hath taken me up). And thy chastising amended me into the end; and that chastising of thee shall teach me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PROVERBS 4:12; 2ND SAMUEL 22:37; JOB 36:16"> Thou alargedest my paces under me; and my steps be not made unsteadfast. (Thou hast enlarged my steps under me; and my feet have not slipped, or stumbled.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:10–12; PSALMS 44:5; PSALMS 35:2"> I shall pursue mine enemies, and I shall take (hold of) them; and I shall not turn (again) till they fail (or and I shall not return until they fall).</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 5:1–25; 2ND SAMUEL 22:39; 1ST SAMUEL 23:5"> I shall all-break them, and they shall not be able to stand; they shall fall under my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 22:40–41; PSALMS 18:32; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:25–28"> And thou hast girded me with virtue to battle; and thou hast overturned under me men rising against me. (And thou hast girded me with strength for the battle; and thou hast put beneath me those who rose up against me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="PSALMS 21:12; PSALMS 94:23"> And thou hast given mine enemies aback to me; and thou hast destroyed men hating me/and thou hast destroyed them that hated me. (And thou hast given me the backs of my enemies; and I have destroyed those who hated me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:28; JOB 27:9; JOB 35:12–13"> They cried, and none there was that made them safe; they cried to the Lord, and he heard not them. (They cried, but there was no one to save them; they cried to the Lord, but he did not answer them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="ISAIAH 10:6; ZECHARIAH 10:5; 2ND KI 13:7"> And I shall all-break them, as dust before the face of the wind; I shall do them away, as the clay of streets. (And I broke them all in pieces, so that they became like the dust in the wind; I did them away, and trampled them underfoot, like the clay in the streets.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="ISAIAH 55:5; 2ND SAMUEL 3:1; ISAIAH 52:15"> Thou shalt deliver me from &#91;the&#93; against-sayings of the people; thou shalt set me into the head of folks. The people, which I knew not, hath served me; (Thou delivered me from the gainsaying of the people; thou put me as the head of the nations. The people, whom I knew not, now serve me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="PSALMS 66:3; PSALMS 81:15; DEUTERONOMY 33:29"> in the hearing of ear it obeyed to me. Alien sons lied (down) to me, (The sons of foreigners, or of strangers, now bow down to me; and after hearing me speak, they obey me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MICAH 7:17; JAMES 1:11; ISAIAH 24:4"> alien sons waxed eld; and (went) crooked from thy paths. (The courage of the sons of foreigners, or of strangers, faded away; and they slunk out of their strongholds together.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 22:47; PSALMS 57:5; PSALMS 18:2"> The Lord liveth, and my God be blessed; and the God of mine health be enhanced (or let the God of my salvation be exalted!).</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="PSALMS 47:3; ROMANS 12:19; NAHUM 1:2"> God, that givest vengeances to me, and makest subject peoples under me; (O God, who givest vengeance to me, and makest the nations subject under me;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="PSALMS 140:1; PSALMS 140:4; PSALMS 86:14"> thou art my deliverer from my wrathful enemies. And thou shalt enhance me from them, that rise against me; thou shalt deliver me from a wicked man. (thou art my deliverer from all my enemies. And thou hast raised me up over those who rose up against me; thou hast rescued me from violent men.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="ROMANS 15:9; 2ND SAMUEL 22:50–51; PSALMS 72:18–19"> Therefore, Lord, I shall acknowledge to thee among nations; and I shall say psalm to thy name. (And so, Lord, I shall praise thee before the nations; and I shall sing to thy name.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="PSALMS 144:10; PSALMS 132:10; 2ND SAMUEL 7:13"> Magnifying the healths of his king; and doing mercy to his christ David, and to his seed till into the world. (He who giveth victories to his king; who showeth love to his anointed king, yea, to David, and to his children, or his descendants, forever.)</span>
