My God, deliver thou me from mine enemies; and deliver thou me from them that rise against me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 56:6; PSALMS 69:4; 1ST SAMUEL 24:11"> For lo! they have taken my soul; strong men fell in on me. Neither (for) my wickedness, neither (for) my sin; (For behold! they lie in wait for me; strong men have fallen in on me. But not because of my wickedness, nor for my sin.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 35:23; ISAIAH 51:9; PROVERBS 1:16"> Lord, I ran without wickedness, and (ad)dressed my works. Rise up into my meeting, and see; (Lord, though I am innocent, they run into position against me. Rise up to help me, and see;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 84:8; PSALMS 55:15; PSALMS 9:15"> and thou, Lord God of virtues, art God of Israel. Give thou attention to visit all folks; do thou not mercy to all that work wickedness. (for thou, Lord God of hosts, art the God of Israel. Give thou attention to punishing all the nations; do not thou give mercy to any who do evil.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 59:14; PSALMS 22:16; 1ST SAMUEL 19:11"> They shall be turned (again) at eventide, and they as dogs shall suffer hunger; and they shall compass, that is, go abegging, (in) the city. (They shall return in the evening, and they shall suffer hunger like dogs; and they shall go about begging in the city.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 57:4; PROVERBS 15:2; PSALMS 10:11"> Lo! they shall speak in their mouth, and a sword in their lips; for who heard? (Behold! they shall speak out insults with their mouths, and their lips, (or their tongues), shall be like swords; for they say, Who shall hear us?)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:26; PSALMS 37:13; PSALMS 2:4"> And thou, Lord, shalt scorn them; thou shalt bring all folks to nought. (But thou, Lord, shalt scorn them; thou shalt bring down all these nations to nothing.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 9:9; PSALMS 62:2; PSALMS 27:14"> I shall keep my strength to thee; for God is mine up-taker, (O my strength, I shall turn to thee; for God is my defender,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 54:7; PSALMS 59:17; 1ST PETER 5:10"> my God, his mercy shall come before me. God showed to me (my desire) on mine enemies, (my God, his love shall come to me. God shall show me my heart’s desire for all my enemies.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 3:3; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 9:6; JOB 40:12"> slay thou not them; lest any time my peoples forget. Scatter thou them in thy virtue; and, Lord, my defender, put thou them down. (But kill thou them not; lest any time my people forget. Rather, scatter thou them by thy strength; and, O Lord, my defender, put thou them down.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:13; PROVERBS 18:7; PSALMS 10:7"> Put down the trespass of their mouth, and the word of their lips; and be they taken in their pride. And of cursing and of leasing; they shall be showed (up) in the ending. (And for the trespasses of their mouths, and the words of their lips; let them be taken in their pride. And for their cursing and their lies; let them be shown up in the end.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 83:18; PSALMS 7:9; EZEKIEL 39:7"> (Destroy thou them) In the ire of ending, and they shall not be; and they shall know, that the Lord shall be Lord of Jacob, and of the ends of earth. (Destroy thou them in thy anger, yea, destroy them altogether, and they shall be no more; and then they shall know, that the Lord is the lord, or the ruler, in Jacob, and unto the ends of the earth.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 22:16; PSALMS 59:6"> They shall be turned (again) at eventide, and they as dogs shall suffer hunger; and they shall compass, that is, go abegging, (in) the city. (They shall return in the evening, and they shall suffer hunger like dogs; and they shall go about begging in the city.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOB 15:23; PSALMS 109:10; JOB 30:1–7"> They shall be scattered abroad, for to eat; soothly if they be not &#91;ful&#93; filled, and they shall grutch. (They shall be scattered abroad, searching for food; and truly they shall grumble, if they be not satisfied, or fulfilled.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

For thou art made mine up-taker; and my refuge, in the day of my tribulation. (But I shall sing of thy strength; and in the morning I shall sing of thy love. For thou art my defender; and my refuge, in the days of my trouble.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 59:9–10; PSALMS 46:1; PSALMS 18:1"> Mine helper, I shall sing to thee; for thou art God, mine up-taker, my God, my mercy. (My helper, I shall sing to thee; for thou art God, my defender, the God who loveth me.)</span>
