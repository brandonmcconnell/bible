<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ROMANS 3:18; PROVERBS 16:6; PSALMS 143:12"> To victory, to David&#91;or the psalm of David&#93;, the servant of the Lord. The wicked man said, My sin, or my trespass, is my concern alone; and so the fear of God is not in his heart or and so reverence for God is not in his heart.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:19; PSALMS 10:3; PSALMS 49:18"> For he did guilefully in the sight of God; that his wickedness be found to (be) hatred. (But he did deceitfully before God; until his wickedness was found to be hateful.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 55:21; PSALMS 10:7; JEREMIAH 4:22"> The words of his mouth be wickedness and guile; he would not understand to do well. (The words of his mouth be wicked and deceitful; he desireth not to understand, or to do good.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MICAH 2:1; PROVERBS 4:16; ROMANS 12:9"> He thought wickedness in his bed; he stood nigh (to) all way(s)(that be) not good; forsooth he hated not malice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 100:5; PSALMS 108:4; PSALMS 103:11"> Lord, thy mercy is in heaven; and thy truth is unto &#91;the&#93; clouds. (Lord, thy love reacheth up to the heavens; and thy faithfulness up to the clouds.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 77:19; ROMANS 11:33; PSALMS 71:19"> Thy rightfulness is as the hills of God; thy dooms be(as) much depth of waters. Lord, thou shalt save men and beasts; (Thy righteousness is as high as the mountains; thy judgements, or thy just acts, be as deep as the water. Lord, thou shalt save people and beasts;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 86:5; PSALMS 91:4; RUTH 2:12"> as thou, God, hast multiplied thy mercy. But the sons of men shall hope in the covering of thy wings. (for thou, God, hast multiplied thy love. And so the sons and daughters of men shall hope for or shall trust in the covering of thy wings.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 16:11; PSALMS 65:4; ISAIAH 58:11"> They shall be (ful)filled greatly of the plenty of thine house; and thou shalt give drink to them of the stiff stream of thy liking. (They shall be filled from the rich plenty of thy House; and thou shalt give drink to them out of the flowing stream of thy delights, or of thy goodness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 8:12; JOHN 4:14; 1ST JOHN 1:7"> For the well of life is with thee; and in thy light we shall see light.</span>

(Lord, continue to show thy love to those who know thee; and thy righteousness to those who have an upright heart.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 16:8; PSALMS 17:8–14; PSALMS 119:51"> The foot of pride come not to me; and the hand of the sinner move me not. (Let not the foot of pride come against me; nor let the hand of the sinner upset me.)</span>
