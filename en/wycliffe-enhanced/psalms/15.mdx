</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ROMANS 13:10; PSALMS 101:5–8; MATTHEW 7:12"> which did not guile in his tongue. Nor did evil to his neighbour; and took not reproof against his neighbours. (and did not deceive with his tongue. Yea, he who did no evil to his neighbours; nor took up any reproach, or spreadeth any rumour, against them.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JUDGES 11:35; MATTHEW 5:33; PSALMS 101:6"> A wicked man is brought to nought in his sight; but he glorifieth them that dread the Lord. He who sweareth an oath, or who promiseth, to his neighbour, and deceiveth him not;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 22:25; DEUTERONOMY 16:19; EZEKIEL 22:12"> which gave not his money to usury; and took not gifts upon the innocent. He, who doeth these things, shall never be moved, or shaken, from his secure place in the Lord.)</span>
