<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 69:14–15; ISAIAH 43:2; LAMENTATIONS 3:54"> To victory, on the roses of David. God, make thou me safe; for waters have entered unto my soul. (For the choirmaster, to the tune of ‘The lilies’, (a song) by David. God, save thou me; for the waters have entered unto my soul!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 40:2; JEREMIAH 38:6; MATTHEW 7:25"> I am set in the slime of the depth; and there is no substance. I came into the depth of the sea; and the tempest drenched me &#91;down&#93;. (I am sinking in the slime of the depth; and there is no firm ground beneath me. I came into the depth of the sea; for the tempest hath drowned me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 6:6; PSALMS 119:82; ISAIAH 38:14"> I travailed crying, my cheeks were made hoarse; mine eyes failed, the while I hope/d into my God. (I am made weary from crying, my voice is made hoarse; my eyes have failed, yet all the while I have hope/d in my God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 15:25; PSALMS 35:19; PSALMS 109:3–5"> They that hated me without cause; were multiplied above the hairs of mine head. Mine enemies that pursued me unjustly were comforted; I paid then (for) those things, which I ravished not. (They who hated me for no reason, were greater in number than the hairs of my head. My enemies who unjustly persecuted me were strong; and I was forced to pay for what I did not steal.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 38:9; PSALMS 17:3; JEREMIAH 16:17"> God, thou knowest mine unknowing, (or God, thou knowest my foolishness, or my ignorance); and my trespasses be not hid from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 25:3; PSALMS 35:26; LUKE 24:19–21"> Lord, Lord of virtues; they, that abide thee, be not ashamed in me. God of Israel; they, that seek thee, be not shamed on me. (Lord, Lord of hosts; let they, who wait for thee, be not shamed by me. God of Israel; let they, who seek thee, be not shamed by me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 44:22; JEREMIAH 15:15; ISAIAH 50:6"> For I suffered shame for thee; shame covered my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 31:11; JOHN 1:11; MATTHEW 26:70–74"> I am made a stranger to my brethren; and a pilgrim to the sons of my mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ROMANS 15:3; JOHN 2:14–17; PSALMS 119:139"> For the fervent love of thine house ate me (or For my fervent love for thy House ate me up, or devoured me); and the shames of men saying shames to thee fell on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 35:13; PSALMS 102:8–9; LUKE 7:33–34"> And I covered my soul with fasting; and it was made into shame to me. (I humbled myself by fasting; and I was reproached for even that.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST KINGS 9:7; JEREMIAH 24:9; JOB 17:6"> And I putted (for) my cloth an hair-shirt; and I am made to them into a parable. (I had a hair-shirt for my cloak; and for that, I am made into their parable.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 35:15–16; MARK 15:17–19; DANIEL 5:23"> They, that sat in the gate, spake against me; and they, that drank wine, sang of me. (They, who sat by the gate, spoke against me; and they, who drank wine, sang about me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 6:2; ISAIAH 49:8; PSALMS 55:16–17"> But Lord, I (ad) dress my prayer to thee; God, I abide the time of good pleasance. Hear thou me in the multitude of thy mercy; in the truth of thine health. (But Lord, I direct my prayer to thee; O God, I wait for the time of thy good pleasure. Hear thou me because of thy great love; because of the truth of thy salvation or because of the surety of thy deliverance.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 144:7; PSALMS 109:21; JEREMIAH 38:6–13"> Deliver thou me from the clay, that I be not fast set-in; deliver thou me from them that hate me, and from &#91;the&#93; depths of waters/and from &#91;the&#93; deepness of waters. (Rescue thou me from the clay, so that I do not get stuck in it; save thou me from those who hate me, and from the depths of the waters or and from the deep waters.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 12:15–16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:24; JONAH 2:2–7"> The tempest of water drench not me &#91;down&#93;, neither the depth swallow me; neither the pit make strait his mouth on me. (Let not the tempest of the water drown me, nor let the depths swallow me; let not the pit close its mouth upon me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 109:21; PSALMS 25:16; PSALMS 63:3"> Lord, hear thou me, for thy mercy is benign; after the multitude of thy merciful doings behold thou into me. (Lord, hear thou me, out of the goodness of thy love; and according to the multitude of thy merciful doings, look thou upon me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 27:9; PSALMS 102:2; PSALMS 70:1"> And turn not away thy face from thy servant; for I am in tribulation, hear thou me swiftly, (or for I am in trouble, so answer thou me swiftly).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 22:19; PSALMS 22:1; PSALMS 111:9"> Give thou attention to my soul, and deliver thou it; for mine enemies, deliver thou me. (Give thou attention to me, and save me; save thou me from my enemies or save thou me, for I have many enemies.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:2; ISAIAH 53:3; 1ST PETER 2:23"> Thou knowest my reproof, and my despising; and my shame. All that trouble me be in thy sight; (Thou knowest the reproaches, and the despisings, and the shames that I bear. All who trouble me be before thee;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ISAIAH 63:5; PSALMS 142:4; MARK 14:50"> mine heart abode (in) shame, and wretchedness. And I abode him, that was sorry together (with me), and none was; and that should comfort me, and I found not. (shame and wretchedness live in my heart. And I waited for someone, who would be sad together with me, but there was no one; and someone who would comfort me, but I found no one.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:34; LUKE 23:36; MARK 15:23"> And they gave gall into my meat; and in my thirst they gave to me drink with vinegar. (And they gave me gall for my food; and for my thirst, they gave me vinegar to drink.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:3; ROMANS 11:8–10; 1ST PETER 2:8"> The board of them be made before them into a snare; and into yieldings, and into cause of stumbling. (May their own table be made into a snare before them; and may their sacred feasts be made into a trap, yea, the reason for their downfall, or ruin.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DANIEL 5:6; ROMANS 11:10; ISAIAH 6:9–10"> Their eyes be made dark, that they see not; and ever&#91;more&#93; bow down the back of them. (May their eyes be made dark, or dim, so that they cannot see; and bow down their backs forevermore or and make their backs crooked forevermore.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 79:6; HOSEA 5:10; EXODUS 15:15"> Shed out thine ire on them; and the strong vengeance of thine ire take them. (Pour out thy anger upon them; and let the fury of thy anger take hold of them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:20; MATTHEW 23:38; JEREMIAH 7:12–14"> The habitation of them be made forsaken; and be there none that dwell in the tabernacles of them. (May their homes be made empty; and may there be none of them left to live in their tents.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:4; ZECHARIAH 1:15; 2ND CHRONICLES 28:9"> For they pursued him, whom thou hast smitten; and they added on(to) the sorrow of my wounds. (For they pursued after those whom thou hast struck down; yea, they added to the sorrow of those whom thou hast wounded.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ISAIAH 26:10; NEHEMIAH 4:5; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:14"> Add thou wickedness on(to) the wickedness of them; and enter they not into thy rightwiseness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:3; EXODUS 32:32–33; LUKE 10:20"> Be they done away from the book of living men; and be they not written with just men. (Let them be done away from The Book of the Living; and let their names be not written in The List of the Righteous.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 91:14–16; PSALMS 40:17; PSALMS 20:1"> I am poor and sorrowful; God, thine health took me up. (I am in pain and full of sorrow; God, lift me up, and save me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 28:7; PSALMS 34:3; PSALMS 118:28–29"> I shall praise the name of God with song; and I shall magnify him with praising.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:15; PSALMS 50:13–14; PSALMS 50:23"> And it shall please God more than a new calf bringing forth horns and claws. (And this shall please God more than the offering of a new calf that hath horns and claws.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 22:26; PSALMS 34:2; JOHN 16:22"> Poor men see, and be glad; seek ye God, and your soul shall live. (Let the poor see this, and be glad; seek ye God, and ye shall live.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 68:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:18–19; ZECHARIAH 9:11–12"> For the Lord heard poor men; and despised not his bound men. (For the Lord heareth the poor; and despiseth not his people who be imprisoned or and despiseth not those who be bound to his service.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 96:11; ISAIAH 49:13; PSALMS 150:6"> Heavens and earth, praise him; the sea, and all creeping beasts in those, praise him. (Let the heavens and the earth praise him; and the seas, and all the things that move in them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:26; PSALMS 51:18; ISAIAH 46:13"> For God shall make safe Zion; and the cities of Judah shall be builded. And they shall dwell there; and they shall get it by heritage. (For God shall save Zion; and he shall rebuild the cities of Judah. And his people shall live there; and they shall have it as their possession.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PSALMS 102:28; PSALMS 37:29; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:39"> And the seed of his servants shall have it in possession; and they that love his name, shall dwell therein. (And the children, or the descendants, of his servants shall get it by inheritance; and they who love his name shall live there.)</span>
