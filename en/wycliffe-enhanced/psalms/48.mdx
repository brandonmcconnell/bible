The Lord is great, and worthy to be greatly praised; in the city of our God, on his holy hill.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 50:2; LAMENTATIONS 2:15; MATTHEW 5:35"> It is founded in the full out joying of all earth; the hill of Zion, the sides of the north, the city of the great king. (Well-placed, it is the full out joy, or the rejoicing, of all the earth; Mount Zion, on the sides of the north, the city of the great King.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 2:4–5; PSALMS 125:1; PSALMS 46:7"> God shall be known in the houses thereof; when he shall take it. (And God is known as a refuge, or a stronghold, in its palaces.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 10:6–19; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 17:12–14; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:8–9"> For lo! the kings of (the) earth were gathered together (against it); they came into one place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 14:25; 2ND KI 7:6–7; 2ND KI 19:35–37"> They seeing, so wondered; they were troubled, they were moved (al)together, (But when they saw it, they wondered about it or they were full of wonder, or amazement; and they were troubled, and ran away,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HOSEA 13:13; ISAIAH 13:6–8; ISAIAH 21:3"> trembling took them. There sorrows, as of a woman travailing of child; (for panic, or trembling, took hold of them there. And sorrows, like when a woman laboureth with child;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 18:17; EZEKIEL 27:25–26; 1ST KINGS 22:48"> in a great spirit thou shalt all-break the ships of Tarshish. (or when a great east wind all-breaketh the ships from Tarshish.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 87:5; PSALMS 78:3–6; PSALMS 46:5"> As we heard, so we saw, in the city of the Lord of virtues, in the city of our God; God hath founded that city &#91;into&#93; without end. (All that we had heard about, we saw with our own eyes, in the city of the Lord of hosts, in the city of our God; and God hath established that city forever.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 26:3; PSALMS 40:10; PSALMS 77:10–14"> God, we have received thy mercy; in the midst of thy temple. (God, we think about thy love; in the midst of thy Temple.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MALACHI 1:11; DEUTERONOMY 28:58; PSALMS 113:3"> After thy name, God, so thy praising is spread abroad into the ends of earth; thy right hand is full of rightwiseness. (The praising of thy name, O God, is spread abroad unto the ends of the earth; thy right hand is full of righteousness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 97:8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:5–7; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:20"> The hill of Zion be glad, and the daughters of Judah be fully joyful; for thy dooms, Lord. (Let Mount Zion be glad, or rejoice, and let the daughters of Judah make full out joy; because of thy judgements, Lord.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 33:18–20; NEHEMIAH 12:31–40; MATTHEW 24:1–2"> Compass ye Zion, and embrace ye it; tell ye in the towers thereof. (March ye around Zion, ye people; and count ye up its towers.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 71:18; PSALMS 122:7; DEUTERONOMY 11:19"> Set ye your hearts in the virtue of him; and part ye the houses of him, that ye tell out in another generation. (Put ye its strongholds, or its fortresses, in your hearts; and think ye about its palaces, so that ye can tell about it to the next generation.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 58:11; PSALMS 23:3–4; PSALMS 73:24"> For this is God, our God, into without end, and into the world of world; he shall govern us into worlds. (For this God is our God, forever and ever; yea, he shall govern us forever.)</span>
