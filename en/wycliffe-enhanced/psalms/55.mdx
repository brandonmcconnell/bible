<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:12; PSALMS 86:6; PSALMS 61:1"> To victory, in organs, the learning of David. God, hear thou my prayer, and despise thou not my beseeching; (For the choirmaster, with stringed instruments, a teaching by David. God, hear thou my prayer, and despise thou not my plea;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 38:14; PSALMS 66:19; PSALMS 77:3"> give thou attention to me, and hear thou me. I am sorrowful in mine exercising (or I am grieved by my distress);</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 16:7–8; PSALMS 27:12; PSALMS 35:11"> and I am disturbed of the face of the enemy, and of the tribulation of the sinner. For they bowed wickednesses into me; and in ire they were dis-easeful to me. (and I am made afraid by my enemies, and by the trouble which the sinners have brought upon me. For they bowed down wickednesses upon me; and with great anger they were very hateful to me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 116:3; MARK 14:33–34; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:8–10"> Mine heart was troubled in me; and the dread of death felled on me. (My heart was troubled within me; and the fear of death fell upon me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 119:120; JOB 21:6; 2ND SAMUEL 15:14"> Dread and trembling came on me (or Fear and trembling came upon me); and darknesses covered me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 11:1; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 12:14; PSALMS 139:9"> And I said, Who shall give to me feathers, as of a culver; and I shall fly, and shall take rest? (And I said, Who shall give me wings like a dove? and then I can fly away, and get some rest.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:2; 2ND SAMUEL 17:21–22; JEREMIAH 37:12"> Lo! I went far away, and fled; and I dwelled in wilderness. (Behold! I would flee far away; and I would live in the wilderness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 4:6; MATTHEW 7:25–27; ISAIAH 17:12–13"> I abode him, that made me safe from the littleness, either dread, of spirit; and from tempest. (I would hasten, and make myself safe from the wind, and from the tempest.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:7; GENESIS 11:7–9; JEREMIAH 23:14"> Lord, cast thou down, &#91;and&#93; part thou the tongues of them; for I saw wickedness and against-saying in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 59:14–15; ISAIAH 59:6–15; EZEKIEL 9:4"> By day and night wickedness shall (en)compass it on the walls thereof; and travail and unrightfulness be in the midst of them. (Day and night they surround the city, all along its walls; and trouble and unrighteousness be in its midst.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:26–27; PSALMS 10:7; PSALMS 5:9"> And usury and guile failed not; from the streets thereof. (And greed, and deceit, always be found in its streets.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 41:9; JOHN 13:18; MATTHEW 26:21–23"> For if mine enemy had cursed me; soothly I had suffered. And if he, that hated me, had spoken great things on me; in hap I had hid me from him. (For if my enemy had cursed me; truly I would have suffered it. And if he, who hated me, had spoken great things against me; I would have hid myself from him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 15:12; MICAH 7:5; JEREMIAH 9:4"> But thou art a man of one will (with me); my leader, and my known. (But thou art a man of one mind with me; my leader, and my dear friend.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 42:4; ISAIAH 2:3; EZEKIEL 33:31"> Which tookest together sweet meats with me; we went with consent in(to) the house of God. (Who shared his good counsel with me; and we went together to the House of God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 59:13; PSALMS 64:7; PSALMS 9:17"> Death come on them; and go they down quick into hell, (or May death come upon them; and may they go down alive into Sheol, or the land of the dead). For waywardnesses be in the dwelling places of them; (and) in the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; PSALMS 109:4; PSALMS 73:28"> But I cried to thee, Lord; and the Lord saved me. (But I shall cry to thee, Lord; and the Lord will save me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:18; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:17; PSALMS 141:2"> In the eventide, and &#91;the&#93; morrowtide, and in midday (or and at midday), I shall tell, and show (him); and he shall hear my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 57:3; PSALMS 3:6–7; 2ND SAMUEL 22:1"> He shall again-buy my soul in peace from them, that nigh to me; for among many they were with me. (And he shall deliver my soul unto peace again, from those who come against me; for there were many against me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 33:27; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:10–11; JEREMIAH 48:11"> God shall hear; and he that is before the worlds shall make them low. For changing is not to them, and they dreaded not God; (God shall hear; and he who was before the worlds, shall humble them. For changing is not to them, and they do not fear God;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:1; PSALMS 89:34; PSALMS 7:4"> he holdeth forth his hand in yielding. They defouled his testament, (indeed, they put forth their hands, against those who be at peace with them. They have broken their promise, or their covenant,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 57:4; PSALMS 28:3; PROVERBS 5:3–4"> the cheers thereof were parted from ire; and his heart nighed. The words thereof were softer than oil; and they be darts. (their words were smoother than butter; but battle was in their hearts. Their words were softer than oil; but they were as sharp as darts.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:7; PSALMS 37:5; PHILIPPIANS 4:6–7"> Cast thy care, or thought, &#91;(or) thy busyness&#93;, on the Lord, and he shall fully nourish thee; and he shall not give &#91;into&#93; without end fluttering to a just man. (Cast thy burdens upon the Lord, and he shall nourish and protect thee; yea, he shall not allow the righteous to be moved, or shaken.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:27; PSALMS 5:6; JOB 15:32"> But thou, God, shalt lead them forth; into the pit of death. Men-quellers and beguilers shall not have half their days; but, Lord, I shall hope in thee. (But murderers and deceivers shall not have half their days; for thou, God, shalt lead them forth, into the pit of death. But, O Lord, I shall put my trust in thee.)</span>
