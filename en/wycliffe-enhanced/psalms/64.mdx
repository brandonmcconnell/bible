<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 34:4; PSALMS 143:1–3; LAMENTATIONS 3:55–56"> To victory, the psalm of David. God, hear thou my prayer, when I beseech; deliver thou my soul from dread of the enemy. (For the choirmaster, a song by David. God, hear thou my prayer, when I beseech thee; save thou me from the threats of my enemies.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 143:9; PSALMS 59:2; PSALMS 109:2–3"> Thou hast defended me from the covent of evil-doers; from the multitude of them that work wickedness. (Thou hast protected me from the plots of the evil-doers; yea, from the many who do evil.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 57:4; PSALMS 58:7; JAMES 3:6–8"> For they sharpened their tongues as a sword, they bend their bow, a bitter thing; (For they sharpened their tongues like swords, yea, they bent their bows, and shot out bitter words;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 55:19; PSALMS 11:2; 1ST PETER 2:22–23"> for to shoot in huddles, or privates, him that is unwemmed. Suddenly they shall shoot him, and they shall not dread; (to secretly attack he who is without blemish, or without fault. Suddenly they shall shoot at him, and they shall not fear;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 140:5; PSALMS 10:11; EZEKIEL 8:12"> they made steadfast to themselves a wicked word. They told, that they should hide snares; they said, Who shall see them? (for they encouraged each other in their evil plots. And they told one another to hide their snares; even though they also said, But who shall see them?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:9–10; MATTHEW 26:59; PSALMS 5:9"> They sought wickednesses; they sought, and failed (not) in seeking. A man nighed to &#91;a&#93; deep heart; (They sought out wickednesses; yea, they sought them out, and failed not in finding them, for their hearts be dark and deep.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 7:12–13; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:2–3; PROVERBS 6:15"> and God shall be enhanced. The arrows of little men, that is, (of) envious men, be made the wounds of them; (But God shall shoot his arrows at them; and then suddenly they shall be the ones who be wounded.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:7; PROVERBS 12:13; PSALMS 140:9"> and the tongues of them be made sick against them. All men be troubled, that saw them; (And their tongues, that is, their own evil words, shall be their own undoing. And all who see them shall shake their heads;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:10; JEREMIAH 50:28; PSALMS 40:3"> and each man dreaded. And they told the works of God; and they understood the deeds of him. (and every person shall be afraid. And they shall tell about the works of God; for they shall understand his deeds, that is, why he hath done all this.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 32:11–1; PSALMS 40:3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:30–31"> The just man shall be glad in the Lord, and shall hope in him; and all men of rightful heart shall be praised. (The righteous shall be glad in the Lord, and shall trust him; and all who have upright hearts shall praise him.)</span>
