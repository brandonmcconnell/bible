<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 79:9–10; ISAIAH 48:11; PSALMS 96:8"> (Alleluia.) Lord, not to us, Lord, not to us; but give thou glory to thy name. Of thy mercy, and of thy truth; (Alleluia. Lord, not to us, Lord, not to us; but give thou glory to thy name. For thy love, and for the sake of thy faithfulness;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 42:3; PSALMS 79:10; PSALMS 42:10"> lest any time heathen men say, Where is the God of them? (lest any time the heathen say, Where is their God?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 135:6; DANIEL 4:35; ISAIAH 46:10"> Forsooth our God in heaven; did all things, whichever he would. (For our God is in heaven; and he doeth whatever he desireth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:28; ISAIAH 46:6–7; JEREMIAH 10:3–5"> The simulacra of heathen men be silver and gold (or The idols of the heathen be made out of silver and gold); the works of men’s hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:5"> They have mouth(s), and shall not speak; they have eyes, and shall not see. (They have mouths, but they cannot speak; they have eyes, but they cannot see.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> They have ears, and shall not hear; they have nostrils, and shall not smell. (They have ears, but they cannot hear; they have nostrils, but they cannot smell.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> They have hands, and shall not grope; they have feet, and shall not go; they shall not cry in their throat. (They have hands, but they cannot feel; they have feet, but they cannot move; and they cannot make any sound.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 135:18; HABAKKUK 2:18–19; JEREMIAH 10:8"> They that make those simulacra be made like those; and all that trust in them. (Let all who make those idols be made like them; and also all who trust in them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 62:8; PSALMS 37:3; PSALMS 118:2–4"> The house of Israel hoped in the Lord; he is the helper of them, and the defender of them. (But let the house of Israel trust in the Lord; he is their helper, and their defender.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 118:3; NUMBERS 18:7; EXODUS 28:1"> The house of Aaron hoped in the Lord; he is the helper of them, and the defender of them. (And let the house of Aaron trust in the Lord; he is their helper, and their defender.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:26; PROVERBS 30:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:35"> They that dread the Lord, hoped in the Lord; he is the helper of them, and the defender of them. (Let all who fear the Lord or Let all who revere the Lord, trust in the Lord; he is their helper, and their defender.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 136:23; EPHESIANS 1:3; ISAIAH 44:21"> The Lord was mindful of us; and blessed us. He blessed the house of Israel; he blessed the house of Aaron. (The Lord remembereth us; and will bless us. He will bless the house of Israel; he will bless the house of Aaron.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 112:1; LUKE 1:50; PSALMS 128:1"> He blessed all men that dread the Lord; both little and greater. (He will bless all those who fear the Lord or all those who revere the Lord; both small and great alike.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:11; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:39; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:25"> The Lord add, either increase, on you; on you, and on your sons. (May the Lord give you increase; yea, you, and your sons and your daughters.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 134:3; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:7; GENESIS 1:1"> Blessed be ye of the Lord; that made heaven and earth. (May ye be blessed by the Lord; who made heaven and earth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 8:6; PSALMS 89:11; GENESIS 1:28–30"> Heaven of heaven is to the Lord/Heaven of heavens to the Lord; but he gave the earth to the sons of men. (Heaven and the heavensbe the Lord’s; but he gave the earth to the sons and daughters of men.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 6:5; ISAIAH 38:18–19; PSALMS 88:10–12"> Lord, not dead men shall praise thee; neither all men that go down into hell. (Lord, the dead shall not praise thee; yea, none who go down into Sheol, or the land of the dead, or none who go down into the grave.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 113:2; DANIEL 2:20; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 5:13"> But we that live, bless the Lord; from this time now, and till into the world. (But we who live, bless the Lord; from this time now, and forever.)</span>
