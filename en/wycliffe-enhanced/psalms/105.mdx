<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ISAIAH 12:4; PSALMS 145:11–12; ROMANS 10:13"> Alleluia. Acknowledge ye to the Lord, and inwardly call ye his name; tell ye his works among heathen men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Compare Psalm 105:1-15to 1st Chronicles 16:8-22.">a</a></sup>. (Alleluia. Give ye thanks to the Lord, and call ye on his name; tell ye about his works among the heathen.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 119:27; PSALMS 98:5; ISAIAH 12:5–6"> Sing ye to him, and say ye psalm to him, and tell ye all his marvels (or and tell ye of all his marvellous deeds);</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:25; GALATIANS 6:14; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:29"> be ye praised in his holy name. The heart of men seeking the Lord be glad; (glory ye in his holy name. Let the hearts of the people seeking the Lord be glad;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 27:8; ZEPHANIAH 2:2–3; 2ND CHRONICLES 6:41"> seek ye the Lord, and be ye confirmed; seek ye ever&#91;more&#93; his face. (seek ye the Lord, who is your strength; seek ye his face forevermore.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 77:11; PSALMS 119:13; DEUTERONOMY 32:7"> Have ye mind on his marvels, which he did; on his great wonders, and dooms of his mouth. (Remember the marvellous deeds, which he hath done; and the judgements, which came forth from his mouth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:1–2; JOHN 15:16; 1ST PETER 2:9"> The seed of Abraham, his servant; the sons of Jacob, his chosen man. (Ye descendants of his servant Abraham; ye sons and daughters of his chosen man Jacob.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 26:9; DEUTERONOMY 26:17–18; EXODUS 20:2"> He is our Lord God; his dooms be in all the earth. (He is the Lord our God; his judgements be for all the earth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:9; PSALMS 106:45; PSALMS 111:5"> He was mindful of his testament into the world; of the word which he commanded into a thousand generations. (He shall remember his covenant forever; yea, the word which he commanded to a thousand generations.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 17:2; GENESIS 22:16–18; GENESIS 26:3"> Which he disposed to Abraham; and of his oath to Isaac. (The covenant which he made with Abraham; and his oath to Isaac.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 17:7–8; 2ND SAMUEL 23:5; GENESIS 28:13–15"> And he ordained it to Jacob into a commandment; and to Israel into everlasting testament. (And he ordained it to Jacob for a law; yea, to Israel for an everlasting covenant.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 13:15; GENESIS 15:18; PSALMS 78:55"> And he said, I shall give to thee the land of Canaan; the cord of your heritage (or yea, your possession, your inheritance).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 34:30; DEUTERONOMY 7:7; HEBREWS 11:9"> When they were in a little number; and the comelings of them were full few. (When they were little in number; yea, very few, and newcomers, or strangers, in that land.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And they passed from folk into folk; and from a realm into another people. (And they went from one nation to another; and from one kingdom to another.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 35:5; EXODUS 7:16–17; GENESIS 26:14–33"> He left not a man to annoy them; and he chastised kings for them. (He did not let anyone harm them; and he admonished, or warned, kings about them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 2:8; GENESIS 26:11; GENESIS 20:7"> (Saying), Do not ye touch my christs; and do not ye do wickedly among my prophets. (Saying, Do not ye touch my anointed servants; and do not ye harm my prophets.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 4:16; GENESIS 41:54; ISAIAH 3:1"> And God called hunger on earth (or And God called for hunger, or a famine, on the earth); and he wasted all the steadfastness of bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:9; GENESIS 37:36; GENESIS 45:4–5"> He sent a man before them; Joseph was sold into a servant (or Joseph was sold as a slave).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:24; GENESIS 39:20; GENESIS 40:15"> They made low his feet in fetters, iron passed by his soul; (They put his feet in fetters, and an iron collar around his neck;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:10; PROVERBS 21:1; GENESIS 40:20–21"> till the word of him came. The speech of the Lord enflamed him; (until the word of the Lord came as predicted. The word of the Lord proved him right;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 41:14"> the king sent and unbound him; the prince of peoples sent and delivered him. (then the king sent and unbound him; yea, the ruler of the nations sent word, and set him free.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 41:40–44; GENESIS 45:8; GENESIS 41:55"> He ordained him lord of his house; and prince of all his possession. (He ordained him lord of his house; and the ruler of all his possessions.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ISAIAH 19:11; GENESIS 41:33; GENESIS 41:38"> That he should learn his princes as himself (or And that he should teach his leaders, or his officials, as he desired); and that he should teach his eld men prudence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:11–15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:17; PSALMS 78:51"> And Israel entered into Egypt; and Jacob was a comeling in the land of Ham. (And then Jacob, or Israel, came down to Egypt; and he was a newcomer, or a stranger, in the land of Ham.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 46:3; EXODUS 1:7–9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:17"> And God increased his people greatly; and made them steadfast on his enemies. (And God greatly increased his people; and he made them stronger than their enemies.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:19; GENESIS 15:13; EXODUS 9:16"> He turned the heart of them, that they hated his people; and did guile against his servants. (And then he turned the hearts of their enemies, so that they hated his people; and they were deceitful with his servants.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 3:10; EXODUS 29:5–46; EXODUS 28:12"> He sent Moses, his servant; &#91;and&#93; that Aaron, whom he chose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 78:43–51; NEHEMIAH 9:10–11; PSALMS 105:23"> He putted in them (to tell and to do) the words of his miracles; and of his great wonders in the land of Ham. (And they told of his miracles beforehand; and then they performed his great wonders in the land of Egypt.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 99:7; JOEL 2:31; JOEL 2:2"> He sent darknesses, and made (it) dark; and he made not bitter his words. (He sent darkness, and all the land was made dark; but still the Egyptians resisted his commands.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EXODUS 7:20–21; PSALMS 78:44; ISAIAH 50:2"> He turned the waters of them into blood; and he killed the fishes of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:13–14; EXODUS 8:3–14; PSALMS 78:45"> And the land of them gave paddocks; in the privy places of the kings of them. (And their land brought forth frogs; even the king’s private rooms were filled with them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EXODUS 8:16–18; EXODUS 8:21–24; ISAIAH 7:18"> God said, and a flesh fly came; and gnats in all the coasts of them. (And God spoke, and then swarms of flies came; and gnats as well, in all their coasts.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 78:47–48; EXODUS 9:18–28; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 8:7"> He setted their rains (into) hail; (and) fire burning in the land of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 78:47; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 9:4"> And he smote the vines of them, and the fig trees of them; and all-brake the trees of the coasts of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="EXODUS 10:12–15; PSALMS 78:46; JOEL 1:4–7"> He said, and the locust(s) came; and a bruchus of which (there) was no number (or and innumerable bruchi).</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And it ate all the hay in the land of them; and it ate all the fruit of the land of them. (And they ate all the plants in their land; and they ate all the fruit of their land.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="GENESIS 49:3; EXODUS 12:29–30; PSALMS 78:51"> And he killed each the first engendered thing in the land of them (or And he killed all their first-born in their land); the first fruits of all the travail of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="EXODUS 12:35–36; EXODUS 3:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:17"> And he led out them with silver and gold; and none was sick in the lineages of them. (And he led them out with silver and gold; and no one was weak, or feeble, in all their tribes.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="EXODUS 12:33; EXODUS 15:16; GENESIS 35:5"> Egypt was glad in the going out of them; for the dread of them lay on Egyptians. (The Egyptians were glad that they left; for the Egyptians feared them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 9:12; PSALMS 78:14; ISAIAH 4:5"> He spreaded abroad a cloud, into the covering of them (or for their covering); and (a) fire, that it shined to them by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="PSALMS 78:18; NUMBERS 11:31–33; JOSHUA 5:12"> They asked, and a curlew came; and he &#91;ful&#93; filled them with the bread of heaven. (They asked for food, and the curlews came; and then he fulfilled them with bread, or manna, from heaven.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="NUMBERS 20:11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:4; EXODUS 17:6"> He brake the stone, and waters flowed; floods went forth in the dry place. (He broke open a rock, and waters flowed out; yea, a river went forth there in the desert, or in the wilderness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="EXODUS 2:24; LUKE 1:72–73; GENESIS 12:7"> For he was mindful of his holy word; which he had (said) to Abraham, his servant. (For he remembered his solemn promise; which he had made to his servant Abraham.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="ISAIAH 35:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:36; JEREMIAH 31:11–12"> And he led out his people in full out joying; and his chosen men in gladness. (And so he led out his people rejoicing; and his chosen in gladness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 6:10–11; JOSHUA 24:13; PSALMS 78:55"> And he gave to them the countries of heathen men; and they had in possession the travails of (other) peoples. (And he gave them the countries of the heathen; and they had in possession what other peoples had toiled for, or had worked so hard for.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:40; TITUS 2:14; EPHESIANS 2:8–10"> That they keep his justifyings; and keep his law. (So that they would obey his statutes; and obey his laws.)</span>
