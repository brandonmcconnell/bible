</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOB 32:11–12; PROVERBS 18:13"> Therefore Elihu abode Job speaking, for they, that spake, were elder men. (And so Elihu waited for Job to finish speaking, for all those, who spoke, were older than he was.)</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOB 32:2; EXODUS 32:19"> But when he had seen, that these three men might not answer Job, he was wroth greatly (or he was very angry).</span>

(And Elihu, the son of Barachel the Buzite, answered, and said, I am younger in age, and ye be older; and so with my face cast down, I feared to tell you my thinking.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:31; JOB 12:12; HEBREWS 5:12"> For I hoped that &#91;the&#93; longer age should speak, and that the multitude of years should teach wisdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 38:36; JOB 33:4; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:16"> But as I see now, a spirit is in men, and the inspiration, either revelation, of Almighty God giveth understanding.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:25; JOB 12:20; ECCLESIASTES 4:13"> Men of long life be not (always) wise, and eld men understand not doom. (Men of long life do not always be wise, nor do old men always have judgement, or know what is right.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:25; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:40"> Therefore I shall say, Hear ye me, and I also shall show my knowing to you. (And so I say, Listen ye to me, and I shall show my knowledge to you.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOB 29:21; JOB 5:27; PROVERBS 28:11"> For I abode your words, I heard your prudence, as long as ye disputed in your words. (For I waited on your words, and I listened to your thinking, for as long as ye disputed with your words.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 32:3; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:7"> And as long as I guessed you to say anything, I beheld; but as I perceive, there is none of you, that may reprove Job, and answer to his words; (And for as long as I thought, that any of you had something of value to say, I listened; but now I perceive, that none of you can rebuke Job, or make an answer to his words;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:23; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:27–29; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:18"> lest peradventure ye say, We have found wisdom; God, and not man, hath cast him away. (lest perhaps ye say, We have found wisdom; but it is God who hath thrown him away, and not man.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Job spake nothing to me, and I not by your words shall answer him. (Job spoke to you, and said nothing to me, but I shall not shall answer him with words like yours.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOB 6:24–25; MATTHEW 22:46; JOB 29:22"> They &#91;much&#93; dreaded, and answered no more, and took away speech from themselves. (For, Job, they be afraid, and have no more answers, and nothing left to say.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JAMES 1:19; JOB 13:5; AMOS 5:13"> Therefore since I abode, and they spake not, they stood, and answered no more; (And so, since I waited, and now they speak no more, but stand silent, and have no more answers,)</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 39:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:20; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:13–14"> For I am full of words, and the spirit of my womb, that is, (my) mind, constraineth me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:17"> Lo! my womb is as must without (a) spigot/without (a) faucet, either a venting, that bursteth new vessels.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 8:6–7; JOB 21:3; JOB 13:13"> I shall speak, and (then) breathe again a little; I shall open my lips, and I shall answer (you).</span>
