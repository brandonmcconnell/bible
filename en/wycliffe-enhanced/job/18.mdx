<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOB 2:11; JOB 8:1; JOB 25:1"> Then Bildad (the) Shuhite answered, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOB 13:5–6; PROVERBS 18:13; JOB 3:17"> Unto what end shalt thou boast with words? Understand thou us first, and so speak we together. (Unto what end shalt thou continue to boast with your words? First listen to us, and then we shall speak together.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 73:22; JOB 17:10; JOB 17:4"> Why be we areckoned as beasts, and why have we been (judged) foul before thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOB 16:9; JOB 14:18; JOB 13:14"> What, or why, losest thou thy soul in thy strong vengeance? Whether the earth shall be forsaken for thee, and hard stones shall be borne over from their place, (just for thee)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:9; PROVERBS 24:20; PROVERBS 20:20"> Whether the light of a wicked man shall not be quenched; and the flame of his fire shall not shine? (The light of the wicked shall be quenched; and the flame of his fire shall not shine!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:23; JOB 21:17; PSALMS 18:28"> Light shall wax dark in his tabernacle; and the lantern, which is on him, shall be quenched. (The light shall grow dark in his tent; and the lantern, which shineth on him, shall be quenched.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 4:12; PSALMS 33:10; PSALMS 18:36"> The steps of his strength shall be made strait; and his (own) counsel shall cast him down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 9:15; JOB 22:10; PSALMS 35:8"> For he hath sent, or put, his feet into a net; and he goeth in the meshes, or knittings, thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 8:14–15; JOB 1:15; JOB 1:17"> His foot shall be holden with a snare; and thirst shall burn out against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 12:13; ROMANS 11:9; PSALMS 11:6"> The foot-trap of him is hid in the earth, and his snare is laid on the path. (A foot-trap is hid on the ground for him, and a snare is laid on the path for him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOB 15:21; JEREMIAH 6:25; LEVITICUS 26:36"> Dreads shall make him afeared on every side, and shall bewrap his feet. (Terror shall make him afraid on every side, and shall enwrap, or shall bind, his feet.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 2:36; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:3; PSALMS 34:10"> His strength &#91;shall&#93; be made feeble by hunger; and poverty (shall) assail his ribs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JONAH 2:6; ZECHARIAH 14:12; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:8"> Devour it the fairness of his skin; the first engendered (of) death waste his arms. (Disease shall devour the beauty of his skin; and Death’s first-born shall destroy,or shall eat up, his arms and his legs.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:28; PSALMS 55:4; JOB 11:20"> His trust be taken away from his tabernacle; and perishing, as a king, above-tread on him. (He shall be torn away from the safety of his tent; and perishing, or destruction, shall tread all over him, like a king.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 11:6; DEUTERONOMY 29:23; ISAIAH 34:9–10"> The fellows of him that is not, that is, the fellows of a dead man, dwell in his tabernacle; and brimstone be sprinkled in his tabernacle. (The fellows, or friends, of him who is not, that is, the friends of he who is dead, shall live in his tent; and brimstone, or sulphur, shall be sprinkled all around it to ward off evil.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HOSEA 9:16; ISAIAH 5:24; JOB 15:30"> The roots of him be made dry beneath; and be his ripe corn all-broken above. (His roots shall be made dry beneath; and all his branches shall wither above.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 34:16; PROVERBS 10:7; PSALMS 109:13"> His mind perish from the earth; and his name be not made solemn in streets. (His memory shall perish from the earth; and his name shall be forgotten in the streets.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOB 20:8; JOB 10:22; JOB 3:20"> He shall put him out from light into darknesses; and he shall bear him over from the world. (He shall be put out from the light into the darkness; and he shall be carried over from this world into the next.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:30; ISAIAH 14:21–22; JOB 20:26–28"> Neither his seed, neither his kindred, shall be in his people, neither any remnants of them (shall be) left in his countries. (Neither his descendants, nor his kindred, shall be among his own people, nor shall any remnant of them be left in his land.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 37:13; JEREMIAH 50:27; EZEKIEL 21:25"> The last men (of him) shall wonder in his days; and hideousness shall assail the first men. (The people who come after him shall wonder at his day; just as horror assailed those who came before him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="TITUS 1:16; JEREMIAH 9:3; JEREMIAH 10:25"> Therefore these be the tabernacles of a wicked man; and this is the place of him, that knoweth not God. (And so such shall be the fate of the wicked; the end, or the place, of those, who do not know God.)</span>
