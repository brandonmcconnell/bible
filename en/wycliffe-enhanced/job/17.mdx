<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:25; MATTHEW 13:11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:20"> Thou hast made the heart of them far from doctrine, that is, (far) from knowing of (the) truth; therefore they shall not be enhanced (or and so thou shalt not let them triumph).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 12:2–3; PROVERBS 20:19; PROVERBS 29:5"> He promiseth prey to his fellows, and the eyes of his sons shall fail (or yet the eyes of his sons shall fail).</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 5:8; HABAKKUK 1:13; JOB 22:19"> Just men shall wonder on this thing; and an innocent shall be raised up against an hypocrite.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOB 42:7; JOB 6:29; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:5"> Therefore all ye be turned again, and come ye; and I shall not find in you any wise man. (And so come now, let all of you try again; for thus far, I have not found a wise man among you.)</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 7:3–4; JOB 7:13–14; DEUTERONOMY 28:67"> Those have turned the night into day; and again after darknesses hope (for) light. (They have turned the night into day; and then again after darkness, hope for some light.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOB 10:21–22; JOB 17:1; JOB 30:23"> If I sustain, either suffer patiently, hell is mine house; and I have arrayed my bed in darknesses. (If I endure, Sheol, or the grave, shall be my house; and I shall array my bed in darkness.)</span>
