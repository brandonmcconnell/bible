<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 58:10; PSALMS 107:42; PSALMS 52:6"> Just men shall see, and shall be glad; and an innocent man shall scorn them. (The righteous shall see, and shall be glad; and the innocent shall scorn them.)</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:6–7; JOB 1:16; JOB 20:26"> Whether the up-raising of them is not cut down, and fire shall devour the remnants of them? (And whatever they have raised up, shall be cut down, and fire shall devour whatever is left.)</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 28:9; ISAIAH 27:5; PHILIPPIANS 4:7"> Therefore assent thou to God, and have thou peace; and by these things thou shalt have (the) best fruits.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 119:11; JOB 23:12; DEUTERONOMY 4:1–2"> Take thou the law of his mouth (or Take thou the Law from his mouth), and set thou his words in thine heart.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOB 11:13–14; ZECHARIAH 1:3; JOB 8:5–6"> If thou turnest again to Almighty God, thou shalt be builded &#91;up&#93;; and thou shalt make wickedness far from thy tabernacle. (If thou turnest again to Almighty God, thou shalt be built up; and thou shalt make wickedness to go far away from thy home.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ISAIAH 13:12; 1ST KINGS 9:28; GENESIS 10:29"> He shall give a flint for earth, and golden strands for a flint. (And thou shalt treat a stone like the dust, and the gold of Ophir like the stones from the stream.)</span>
