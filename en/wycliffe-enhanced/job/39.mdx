<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 29:9; 1ST SAMUEL 24:2; PSALMS 104:18"> Whether thou knowest the time of birth of wild goats in (the) stones, either hast thou espied hinds bringing forth calves? (Knowest thou the time of birth for the wild mountain goats, or hast thou seen the deer bringing forth their calves?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:24"> Hast thou numbered the months of their conceiving, and hast thou known the time of their calving?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> They be bowed down to &#91;the&#93; calf, and so calve; and they send out then roarings (or and then they send out their roarings).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Their calves be separated from them, and go forth to pasture; they go out, and they turn not again to their mothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOB 11:12; JOB 24:5; JOB 6:5"> Who hath let go the wild ass free, and who hath loosed the bonds of him? (Who hath let the wild donkey go free, and who hath loosened his bonds?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 107:34; JOB 24:5; DEUTERONOMY 29:23"> To whom I have given an house in (the) wilderness, and the tabernacles of him in the land of saltness. (To whom I have given a home in the desert, and his dwelling places in the salty land.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 5:18; JOB 3:18; EXODUS 5:13–16"> He despiseth the multitude of the city; he heareth not the cry of the asker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 104:27–28; PSALMS 145:15–16; JOB 40:15"> He looketh about the hills of his pasture, and he seeketh (after) all green things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:22; DEUTERONOMY 33:17; PSALMS 92:10"> Whether an unicorn shall desire to serve thee, either shall dwell at thy cratch? (Shall a wild ox desire to serve thee, or shall he stay in thy stall?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HOSEA 10:10–11; PSALMS 129:3; JOB 39:7"> Whether thou shalt bind the unicorn with thy chain, for to ear thy land, either shall he break the clots of the valleys after thee? (Shalt thou bind the wild ox with thy chain, to plow thy land, or shall he break up the clods of the valleys after thee?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 1:26; PSALMS 144:14; GENESIS 42:26"> Whether thou shalt have trust in his great strength, and shalt thou leave to him thy travails? (Shalt thou have trust in his great strength, and shalt thou leave thy work for him to do?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 13:15; PROVERBS 3:16; AMOS 2:13"> Whether thou shalt believe to him, that he shall yield seed to thee, and shall gather (it) together (for) thy cornfloor? (Shalt thou trust him, that he shall bring in thy seed to thee, and shall gather it unto thy threshing floor?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:7; ZECHARIAH 5:9; 2ND CHRONICLES 9:21"> The feather of an ostrich is like the feathers of a gyrfalcon, and of an hawk;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> the which ostrich forsaketh his eggs in the earth, in hap thou shalt make those hot in the dust. (who forsaketh his eggs in the earth, and warmeth them under the sand.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He forgetteth, that a foot treadeth those eggs, either that a beast of the field all-breaketh them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 4:3; DEUTERONOMY 28:56–57; 1ST KINGS 3:26–27"> He is made hard to his young, as if they were not his; he travailed in vain, while no dread constrained him. (He is hardened against his young, as if they were not his; he laboured in vain, while no fear constrained him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOB 35:11; JOB 17:4; ISAIAH 57:17"> For God hath deprived him from wisdom, and he hath not given understanding to him. (For God hath deprived him of wisdom, and he hath not given him understanding.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOB 41:29; JOB 39:22; JOB 5:22"> When time is, he raiseth the wings on high, (or And then the time cometh that he raise up his wings on high); he scorneth the horse, and his rider.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 147:10; PSALMS 93:1; PSALMS 104:1"> Whether thou shalt give strength to an horse, either shalt give neighing about his neck? (Hast thou given strength to a horse, and hast thou clothed his neck with a mane?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:16; JOB 41:20–21; JOEL 2:5"> Whether thou shalt raise him as locusts? The glory of his nostrils is dreaded. (Hast thou made that horse as frightening as a horde of locusts? The glory of his nostrils (is) fearful indeed!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:6; PSALMS 19:5; 1ST SAMUEL 17:4–10"> He diggeth &#91;the&#93; earth with his foot, he full out joyeth; and he goeth boldly against &#91;the&#93; armed men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 41:33; JOB 39:16; JOB 39:18"> He despiseth fearedfulness, and he giveth not stead to &#91;the&#93; sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOB 41:26–29"> An arrow case shall sound upon him; a spear and a shield shall shine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="AMOS 3:6; LUKE 24:41; JOB 37:20"> He is hot, or fervent, and gnasheth, and swalloweth the earth; and he areckoneth not that the cry of the trump soundeth (or and he cannot be held when the cry of the trumpet soundeth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 70:3; EZEKIEL 26:2; EZEKIEL 36:2"> When he heareth a clarion, he saith, Joy! he smelleth (the) battle afar (off); the exciting of dukes, and the yelling of the host, (or the orders of the leaders, and the yelling of the army).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 14:15; SONG OF SONGS 2:12; LEVITICUS 16:11"> Whether an hawk spreading abroad his wings to the south, beginneth to have feathers by thy wisdom? (Be it by thy wisdom that a hawk haveth feathers, and spreadeth his wings towards the south?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="OBADIAH 1:4; JEREMIAH 49:16; EXODUS 19:4"> Whether an eagle shall be raised up at thy commandment, and shall set his nest in high places?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 14:4"> He dwelleth in stones, and he abideth in flints broken before, and in rocks, to which men may not nigh. (He liveth on the stones, yea, on broken stones and rocks, to which men cannot come near.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOB 9:26"> From thence he beholdeth (his) meat (or From there he seeth his prey), and his eyes look from &#91;a&#93; far.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:28; LUKE 17:37; EZEKIEL 39:17–19"> His young suck blood, and wherever a carrion is, anon he is present. (His young suck up the blood, and wherever there is a carcass, at once he is present.)</span>
