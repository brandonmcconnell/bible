<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOB 2:11"> Then Bildad (the) Shuhite answered, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOB 6:26; JOB 15:2; 1ST KINGS 19:11"> How long shalt thou speak such things? The spirit of the word of thy mouth is manyfold (or The many words from thy mouth be just so much wind).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:4; 2ND CHRONICLES 19:7; GENESIS 18:25"> Whether God supplanteth, either deceiveth, doom, and whether Almighty God destroyeth that, that is just? (Did God ever pervert judgement, or did Almighty God ever destroy what is fair?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOB 1:5; JOB 1:18–19; JOB 5:4"> Yea, though thy sons sinned against him, and he left them in the hand of their wickedness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JAMES 4:7–10; HEBREWS 3:7–8; JOB 11:13"> nevertheless, if thou risest early to God, and beseechest Almighty God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ISAIAH 51:9; ISAIAH 3:10; PROVERBS 15:8"> if thou goest clean and rightful, anon he shall wake fully to thee, and shall make peaceable the dwelling place of thy rightfulness; (if thou goest clean and upright, at once he shall watch over thee, and he shall make the dwelling place of thy righteousness prosperous;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOB 42:12–13; MATTHEW 13:31–32; PROVERBS 19:20"> in so much that thy former things were little, and that thy last things be multiplied greatly. (in so much that though thy first things were few, but thy last things shall be greatly multiplied.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:7; DEUTERONOMY 4:32; JOB 15:18"> For why, ask thou the former generations, and seek thou diligently the mind of &#91;the&#93; fathers (or and diligently seek thou the wisdom of the forefathers).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 29:15; PSALMS 144:4; JOB 14:2"> For we be men of yesterday, and know not (anything); for our days be as (a) shadow on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:15; MATTHEW 12:35; JOB 12:7–8"> And they shall teach thee, &#91;and&#93; they shall speak to thee, and (out) of their heart they shall bring forth true speeches (or and they shall bring forth truth from their hearts).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 2:3; ISAIAH 19:5–7"> Whether a (bul)rush may grow without moisture? either a reed (can grow) without water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:6; PSALMS 129:6–7; MATTHEW 13:20"> When it is yet in the flower, neither it is taken with hand, it waxeth dry before all herbs. (When it is still flowering, and it hath not yet been picked by hand, it groweth dry before all the other plants.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 9:17; JOB 11:20; JOB 13:16"> So be the ways of all men, that forget God (or who forget God); and the hope of an hypocrite shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 59:5–6"> His cowardice shall not please God, and his trust shall be as a web of spiders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOB 27:18; LUKE 6:47–49; MATTHEW 7:24–27"> He shall lean, either rest, upon his house, and it shall not stand; he shall underset it, and it shall not rise up altogether. (He shall lean, or shall rest, upon his house, but it shall not stand; he shall undergird it, but it shall not endure.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 80:11; JOB 21:7–15; JOB 5:3"> The (bul)rush seemeth moist, before that the sun come; and in the rising of the sun, the seed thereof shall go out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 5:24; MARK 11:20; JUDE 1:12"> The roots thereof shall be made thick upon an heap of stones, and it shall dwell among &#91;the&#93; stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOB 7:10; PSALMS 37:36; JOB 7:8"> If a man draweth it out of the place thereof, that place shall deny it, and it shall say (in effect), I know thee not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOB 20:5; PSALMS 113:7; 1ST SAMUEL 2:8"> For this is the gladness of his way, that again other rushes spring out of the earth. (For this is the joy of its way, that other bulrushes shall spring out of the earth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOB 4:7; PSALMS 37:37; PSALMS 37:24"> Forsooth God shall not cast away a simple man, neither he shall address his hand to (help) wicked men; (But God shall not throw away a person who is without guile, nor shall he direct his hand to help the wicked;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 126:2; GENESIS 21:6; PSALMS 126:6"> till thy mouth be filled with laughter, and thy lips with hearty song.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 132:18; PSALMS 109:29; PSALMS 35:26"> They that hate thee shall be clothed with shame; and the tabernacle of wicked men shall not stand (or and the tents, or the homes, of the wicked shall not stand).</span>
