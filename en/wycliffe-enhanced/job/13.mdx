<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOB 5:9–16; PSALMS 78:3–4; JOB 42:3–6"> Lo! mine eye, saith Job, hath seen all things, and mine ear hath heard (it all); and I understood all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOB 12:3; JOB 42:7; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:11"> Even with your knowing, (that) also I know, and I am not lower than ye. (What you know, I also know, and I am not lower than ye.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:21; JOB 31:35; JOB 13:22"> But nevertheless I shall speak to Almighty God, and I covet to dispute with God (or and I desire to dispute with God);</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 119:69; JOB 16:2; JEREMIAH 23:32"> and first I (shall) show you (to be) makers of lies, and lovers/favourers of wayward teachings. (but first I shall show you to be liars, and lovers of wayward teaching.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:28; JOB 13:13; ECCLESIASTES 5:3"> And I would, that ye were still, that ye were guessed to be wise men. (And I wish, that ye held your peace, so that ye could be thought to be wise.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOB 34:2; JOB 21:2–3; PROVERBS 8:6–7"> Therefore hear ye my chastisings; and perceive ye the doom of my lips. (And so hear ye my arguments; and understand ye the judgement from my lips.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOB 36:4; JOB 27:4; JOB 32:21–22"> Whether God hath need to your leasing, that ye speak guileful things for him? (Hath God a need for your lies, so that ye speak these guileful things for him?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 24:23; EXODUS 23:2–3; MALACHI 2:9"> Whether ye take his face, and enforce to deem for God? (Do ye take his place, and endeavour to judge for God?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:7–8; JEREMIAH 17:10; PSALMS 44:21"> Either it shall please him, from whom nothing may be hid? Whether he, as a man, shall be deceived by your falsenesses? (or Shall he, like a man, be deceived by your falseness, or your duplicity?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JAMES 2:9; PSALMS 82:2; PSALMS 50:21–22"> He shall reprove you; for ye take his face in huddles. (He shall rebuke you, if ye secretly accuse me, and judge me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOB 31:23; PSALMS 119:120; EXODUS 15:16"> Anon as he shall stir him, he shall trouble you; and his dread shall fall upon you. (At once he shall stir himself, and he shall trouble you; and the fear of him, or his terror, shall come upon you.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 18:17; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:1; ISAIAH 26:14"> Your mind shall be comparisoned to ashes; and your nolls shall be driven down into (the) clay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOB 7:11; JOB 21:3; JOB 13:5"> Be ye still a little, that I speak (or so that I can speak), whatever thing my mind hath showed to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JUDGES 12:3; PSALMS 119:109; ECCLESIASTES 4:5"> Why rend I my flesh with my teeth, and bear my life in mine hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 23:4; JOB 23:10; JOB 19:25–28"> Yea, though God slay me, I shall hope in him; nevertheless I shall prove my ways in his sight. (Yea, even if God shall kill me, I shall still hope, or trust, in him; and I shall still argue my case before him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 12:1–2; PSALMS 118:21; ISAIAH 33:14"> And he shall be my saviour; for why each hypocrite shall not come in his sight (or for no hypocrite shall ever come before him).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOB 21:2; JOB 33:1; JOB 13:6"> Hear ye my word, and perceive ye with &#91;your&#93; ears (my) dark and hard privy speeches/(my) dark and hard figurative speeches. (Hear ye my voice, and perceive ye with your ears, my words of explanation.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOB 23:4; JOB 9:2–3; JOB 16:21"> If I shall be deemed, I know that I shall be found just. (If I shall be judged, I know that I shall be justified, or found acquitted.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 50:7–8; JOB 7:11; JOB 33:32"> Who is he that is deemed with me? Come he; why am I still, and am wasted? (Who is he who is judged with me? Come he; for if I hold my peace, I shall be destroyed.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 139:12; JOB 9:34–35; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:15–16"> Do thou not to me two things only; and then I shall not be hid from thy face. (Do thou only two things for me; and then I shall not hide from thy face.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOB 9:34; JOB 33:7; PSALMS 39:10"> Make thine hand far from me; and thy dread make not me afeared. (Take thy hand far away from me; and do not let my fear of thee make me afraid.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 14:15; JOB 9:32; JOB 42:3–6"> Call thou me, and I shall answer thee; either certainly I shall speak, and (then) thou shalt answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 44:20–21; PSALMS 139:23; JOB 36:8–9"> How great sins and wickednesses have I? (or How many sins and wickednesses have I?) Show thou to me my felonies, and my trespasses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOB 19:11; PSALMS 13:1; LAMENTATIONS 2:5"> Why hidest thou thy face, and deemest me thine enemy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:36; JOB 21:18; 1ST SAMUEL 24:14"> Thou showest thy might against a leaf, that is ravished away with the wind; and thou pursuest dry stubble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 25:7; JOHN 5:14; PROVERBS 5:11–13"> For thou writest bitternesses against me; and wilt waste me with the sins of my young waxing age. (For thou writest bitter charges against me; and wilt destroy me for the sins of my youth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOB 33:11; JOB 10:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:24"> Thou hast set my foot in a stock (or Thou hast set my feet in the stocks), and thou hast kept (watch over) all my paths; and thou hast beheld the steps of my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 39:11; JOB 4:19; JOB 30:17–19"> And I shall be wasted as rot, and as a cloth, that is eaten of a moth. (And I shall be wasted by rot, and like a cloak, that is eaten by a moth.)</span>
