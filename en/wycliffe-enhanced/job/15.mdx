<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOB 42:7; JOB 2:11; JOB 22:1"> Then Eliphaz (the) Temanite answered, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOB 6:26; JOB 8:2; HOSEA 12:1"> Whether a wise man shall answer, as speaking against the wind, and shall fill his stomach with burning, that is ire? (Would a wise man answer someone, whose speaking is merely wind, and who filleth his stomach with hot air?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 4:6; MATTHEW 12:36–37; JOB 13:4–5"> For thou reprovest him by words, which is not like thee, and thou speakest that, that speedeth not to thee. (For thou rebukest him with worthless words, and thou speakest, what profiteth thee not.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ROMANS 3:31; LUKE 18:1; AMOS 6:10"> As much as is in thee, thou hast avoided dread; and thou hast taken away thy prayers before God. (As much as is possible, thou hast avoided fearing God or revering God; and thou hast not presented thy prayers to God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:3–5; JAMES 3:5–8; LUKE 6:45"> For &#91;thy&#93; wickedness hath taught thy mouth, and thou followest the tongue of blasphemers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 19:22; MATTHEW 12:37; JOB 9:20"> Thy tongue &#91;or Thy mouth&#93;, and not I, shall condemn thee, and thy lips shall answer (against) thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 90:2; JOB 12:12; JOB 38:4–41"> Whether thou art born the first man, and whether thou art formed before (the) little hills? (Art thou the first man born, and wast thou formed before the little hills?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ROMANS 11:34; JEREMIAH 23:18; JOB 13:5–6"> Whether thou hast heard the counsel of God, and whether his wisdom is lower than thou? (or and is his wisdom less than thine?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOB 13:2; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:5; JOB 12:3"> What thing knowest thou, which we know not? What thing understandest thou, which we know not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOB 32:6–7; JOB 8:8–10; JOB 12:12"> Both wise men and eld (men), much elder than thy fathers (or much older than thy father), be among us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOB 13:2; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:3–5; 1ST KINGS 22:24"> Whether it is great, that God comfort thee? But thy shrewd words forbid this. (Is it not wonderful, that God comforteth thee? But thy depraved words forbid this.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 35:19; ECCLESIASTES 11:9; MARK 7:21–22"> What raiseth thine heart thee, and thou as thinking great things hast eyes astonished? (What raiseth up thy heart, to think such great things? and why be thine eyes astonished?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOB 15:25–27; JAMES 3:2–6; JOB 9:4"> What swelleth thy spirit against God, that thou bring forth of thy mouth such words? (What swelleth thy spirit against God, so that thou bring forth such words out of thy mouth?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:20; JOB 14:4; PROVERBS 20:9"> What is a man, that he be without wem, and that he, born of a woman, appear just? (What is a man, is he ever pure, or without fault? yea, he, who is born of a woman, is he ever truly right before God?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOB 25:5; JOB 4:18; JOB 5:1"> Lo! none among his saints is unchangeable, (or Behold! no one among his saints is trustworthy), and (even the) heavens be not clean in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOB 34:7; PROVERBS 19:28; ROMANS 3:9–19"> How much more is a man abominable and unprofitable, that drinketh wickedness as water? (Then how much more abominable and unprofitable is man, who drinketh wickedness like water?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOB 36:2; JOB 34:2; JOB 13:5–6"> I shall show to thee, hear thou me; I shall tell to thee that, that I saw, (or I shall tell thee, what I saw).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOB 8:8; ISAIAH 38:19; PSALMS 71:18"> Wise men acknowledge, and hide (it) not their fathers. (The wise acknowledge it, and their forefathers hide it not.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOEL 3:17; GENESIS 10:32; GENESIS 10:25"> To which wise men alone the earth is given, and an alien shall not pass by them. (To whom alone the land was given; and no foreigner lived among them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOB 27:13; JAMES 5:1–6; LUKE 12:19–21"> A wicked man is proud in all his days; and the number of his years and of his tyranny is uncertain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOB 18:11; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:3; LEVITICUS 26:36"> The sound of dread is ever&#91;more&#93; in his ears, and when peace is, he supposeth ever&#91;more&#93; treasons. (A fearful sound is forever in his ears, and even when there is peace, he always expecteth an attack.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 20:24–25; MATTHEW 27:5; JOB 9:16"> He believeth not that he may turn again from darknesses to light; and he beholdeth about on each side (for) a sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 109:10; PSALMS 59:15; JOB 18:12"> When he stirreth him&#91;self&#93; to seek bread, he knoweth, that the day of darknesses is made ready in his hand, (or he knoweth that the day of darkness is at hand).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 24:34; PROVERBS 6:11; JOB 6:2–4"> Tribulation shall make him afeared, and anguish shall (en)compass him, as a king which is made ready to battle. (Tribulation shall make him afraid, and anguish shall surround him, like a king who is made ready for battle.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DANIEL 5:23; PSALMS 52:7; PSALMS 73:9"> For he held forth his hand against God, and he was made strong against Almighty God. (For he put forth his hand against God, and he made himself strong against Almighty God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 18:40; JOB 16:12; 2ND CHRONICLES 32:13–17"> He ran with his neck raised up against God, and he was armed with a fat noll. (He ran with his head raised up against God, and he was armed with a stiff neck, or he was stubborn.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 17:10; JEREMIAH 5:28; PSALMS 73:7"> Fatness, that is, pride of temporal abundance, covered his face, or understanding, and outward fatness, that is, unshamefastness, hangeth down of his sides (or hangeth down on his sides). &#91;Fatness covered his face, and of his sides grease hangeth. (Fatness covered his face, and grease hangeth down on his sides.)&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOB 3:14; JEREMIAH 51:37; MICAH 7:18"> He shall dwell in desolate cities (or He shall live in empty cities), and in deserted houses, that be turned into burials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOB 27:16–17; PSALMS 49:16–17; JAMES 1:11"> He shall not be made rich, neither his chattel shall dwell steadfastly (nor shall his substance, or his possessions, endure); neither he shall send his root into the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOB 4:9; JOB 15:22; JOB 18:5–6"> neither he shall go away from darknesses. Flame shall make dry his branches, and he shall be taken away by the spirit of his mouth. (nor shall he go away from darkness. The flame shall dry up his branches, and then he shall be taken away by the wind.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ISAIAH 59:4; GALATIANS 6:3; PSALMS 62:10"> Believe he not vainly which is deceived by error, that he shall be again-bought by any price. (He, who is deceived by error, vainly believeth that he shall be bought back, or redeemed, at any price.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:17; PSALMS 55:23; HOSEA 9:16"> Before that his days be &#91;ful&#93; filled, he shall perish, and his hands shall wax dry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:39–40; ISAIAH 33:9; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:13"> he shall be hurt as a vine in the first flower of his grape, and as an olive tree casting away his flower. (he shall be like a vine that droppeth off its unripe grapes, or like an olive tree that casteth away its flowering buds.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JOB 8:13; JOB 27:8; JOB 8:22"> For the gathering together of an hypocrite is barren, and fire shall devour the tabernacles of them, that take gifts willfully. (For hypocrites, one and all, be barren, and fire shall devour the tents of those, who willingly take gifts.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 7:14; HOSEA 10:13; ISAIAH 59:4–5"> He conceived sorrow, and childed wickedness, and his womb maketh ready treacheries.</span>
