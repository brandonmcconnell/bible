<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Therefore Elihu spake again, (and said) these things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:36–37; JOB 19:6–7; JOB 16:17"> Whether thy thought seemeth even, either rightful, to thee, that thou shouldest say, I am right-fuller than God? &#91;or I am more rightwise than God?&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOB 34:9; MALACHI 3:14; JOB 9:30–31"> For thou saidest, That, that is good, pleaseth not thee; either what profiteth it to thee, if I do sin? (For thou hast said, What is good, pleaseth thee not; or what profiteth it to me, if I do not sin?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:20; JOB 34:8"> Therefore I shall answer to thy words, and to thy friends with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOB 22:12; ISAIAH 55:9; NAHUM 1:3"> See thou, and behold (the) heaven(s) (or and look at the sky), and behold thou the air, and know that God is higher than thou. &#91;Behold heaven, and look, and mindfully see the clouds, (all) that is higher than thou.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:19; PROVERBS 8:36; PROVERBS 9:12"> If thou sinnest, what shalt thou harm him? and if thy wickednesses be multiplied, what shalt thou do against him? (If thou sinnest, what harm shalt thou do to him? and if thy wickednesses be multiplied, what shalt thou do to him?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOB 22:2–3; PSALMS 16:2; LUKE 17:10"> Certainly if thou doest justly, what shalt thou give to him; either what shall he take of thine hand? (And if thou doest righteously, what shalt thou give him? or what shall he receive from thy hand?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:30; JOB 42:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 27:24"> Thy wickedness shall annoy a man, which is like thee, (or Thy wickedness shall harm a man, who is just like thee); and thy rightwiseness shall help the son of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 2:23; JOB 34:28; EXODUS 3:7"> Men shall cry for the multitude of false challengers, and they shall wail for the violence of the power of tyrants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 42:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:25; PSALMS 149:5"> And Job said not, Where is God, that made me, and that gave songs in the night? (And no one hath said, Where is God, who made me, and gave songs in the night, that is, hope in the darkness?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 94:12; JOB 36:22; JOB 32:8"> And the which teacheth us above the beasts of &#91;the&#93; earth, and he shall teach us above the birds of (the) heaven(s). (And who teacheth us more than the beasts of the earth, and the birds of the air.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:28; JOHN 9:31; PSALMS 123:3–4"> There they shall cry, and God shall not hear them, for the pride of evil men. (And they shall cry for help, but God shall not hear them, because of the pride of evil people.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:29; ISAIAH 1:15; JAMES 4:3"> For God shall not hear without cause, and Almighty God shall behold the causes of each man. (For God shall not listen to vanity, that is, to vain, or empty, things, nor shall Almighty God consider the importuning of these people.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOB 9:11; MICAH 7:7–9; PSALMS 37:5–6"> Yea, when thou sayest, He beholdeth not; be thou deemed before him, and abide thou him (or wait thou for him, and be thou judged before him).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 89:32; JOB 9:14; JOB 13:15"> For now the Lord bringeth not in his strong vengeance, (n)either he vengeth felonies greatly here. (But here and now, the Lord bringeth not in his strong vengeance, nor greatly avengeth felonies;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOB 38:2; JOB 33:8–12; JOB 34:35–37"> Therefore Job openeth his mouth in vain, and multiplieth words without knowing. (and so Job openeth his mouth in vain, or in futility, and multiplieth words without knowledge.)</span>
