</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 97:2; JOB 22:14; HABAKKUK 3:3–5"> He holdeth the cheer of his seat, and spreadeth abroad thereon his cloud. (He spreadeth his cloud upon the surface of his throne.)</span>
</div>

</div>
