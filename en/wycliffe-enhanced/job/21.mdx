<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Forsooth Job answered, and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HEBREWS 2:1; JOB 34:2; JOB 15:11"> I pray you, hear ye my words, and do ye penance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOB 17:2; JOB 16:10; JOB 16:20"> Suffer ye me, that I speak; and laugh ye after my words, if it shall seem to you worthy to do so. (Allow ye me, that I speak; and then laugh ye after my words, if it shall seem worthy for you to do so.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 102:1; PSALMS 42:11; PSALMS 142:2–3"> Whether my disputing is against man, that skillfully I ought not to be &#91;made&#93; sorry? (Is my disputing against man? have I not good cause, or a good reason, to be impatient?)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOB 29:9; JOB 40:4; JUDGES 18:19"> Perceive ye me, and be ye astonished; and set ye your finger upon your mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HABAKKUK 3:16; PSALMS 119:120; PSALMS 88:15"> And when I bethink me, I dread, and trembling shaketh my flesh. (And when I think about all of this, I am afraid, and my flesh shaketh and trembleth.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 73:3–12; JOB 12:6; JEREMIAH 12:1–3"> Why therefore live wicked men? They be enhanced, and strengthened with riches. (And so why do the wicked live so long? Indeed they be exalted, and can enjoy their riches.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:6; JOB 5:3–4; JOB 20:10"> Their seed dwelleth before them (or Their descendants, or their children, live with them); the company of their kinsmen, and of the sons of their sons, dwelleth in their sight.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOB 9:34; PSALMS 73:5; PSALMS 73:19"> Their houses be secure, and peaceable; and the rod, or scourge, of God is not upon them. (Their houses be secure, and at peace; and the scourge of God is not upon them.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 23:26; LUKE 16:19; DEUTERONOMY 7:13–14"> The cow of them conceived, and calved not a dead calf; (yea), the cow calved, and is not deprived of her calf.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 107:41; PSALMS 127:3–5"> Their little children go out as flocks; and their young children full out joy with playings. (Their little children go out to play like lambs; and their young children dance with joy.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="AMOS 6:4–6; ISAIAH 5:12; GENESIS 4:21"> They hold the tympan, and (the) harp; and they (dance with) joy at the sound of the organ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOB 36:11; LUKE 12:19–20; PSALMS 73:4"> They lead in goods (all) their days; and in a point, (or suddenly), they go down to hells, that is, to burials, or the grave. (They get good things for themselves all their days; and then, in a moment, that is, without pain, or suffering, they go down to Sheol, in peace.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOB 22:17; ROMANS 8:7; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:10–12"> Which men said to God, Go thou away from us; we desire not the knowing of thy ways. (And they said to God, Go thou away from us; we do not desire to know of thy ways.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 5:2; JOB 34:9; MALACHI 1:13–14"> Who is Almighty God, that we serve him? and what profiteth it to us, if we pray (to) him?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 1:1; JOB 22:18; GENESIS 49:6"> Nevertheless for their goods be not in their hand, or power, the counsel of wicked men be far from me. (And (they say), that all their good things come from their own hands, (or their own effort); may the counsel of the wicked be far from me!)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOB 18:5–6; PROVERBS 20:20; PROVERBS 13:9"> How oft shall the lantern of wicked men be quenched, and flowing shall come upon them, and God shall part with them the sorrows of his strong vengeance? (How often is the lantern of the wicked quenched, and destruction shall come upon them? how often shall God impart to them the sorrows of his strong vengeance?)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 1:4; PSALMS 83:13; JOB 13:25"> They shall be as chaff before the face of the wind; and as a dead spark, that the whirlwind scattereth abroad. (Be they ever like chaff before the face of the wind? or like a dead spark that the whirlwind scattereth abroad?)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 20:5; EZEKIEL 18:19–20; EZEKIEL 18:2"> (Ye say), God shall keep the sorrow of the father to his sons; and when he hath yielded to them vengeance, then he shall know it. (Ye say, God shall put the father’s punishment upon his sons; (but I say), No! he shall yield vengeance to him who deserveth it, and he shall know (it).)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:10; ISAIAH 51:17; PSALMS 75:8"> His eyes shall see their slaying (or His eyes shall see his own slaughter); and he shall drink of the strong vengeance of Almighty God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOB 14:5; ECCLESIASTES 2:18–19; PSALMS 55:23"> For why what pertaineth it to him of his house(hold) after him, though the number of his months be half taken away? (For what careth him about his family, or his children, after him, when his own days and months be numbered?)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ROMANS 11:34; 1ST CORINTHIANS 2:16; ISAIAH 40:13–14"> Whether any man shall teach God knowing, which deemeth them that be (on) high? (Shall anyone teach God knowledge, he who even judgeth those who be on high?)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOB 20:22–23; LUKE 12:19–21; PSALMS 73:4–5"> This evil man dieth strong and whole, rich and blessful to the world (or rich and blessed before the world).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:8; PSALMS 17:10; JOB 15:27"> His entrails be full of fatness; and his bones be moisted with marrow.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EZEKIEL 4:16–17; JOB 7:11; PROVERBS 14:10"> And another man dieth in the bitterness of his soul, and without any riches.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:2; ISAIAH 14:11; JOB 20:11"> Nevertheless they shall sleep together in (the) dust, and worms shall cover them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKE 5:22; PSALMS 119:86; PSALMS 59:4"> Certainly I know your wicked thoughts, and your sentences against me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOB 8:22; JOB 20:7; HABAKKUK 2:9–11"> For ye say, Where is the house of the prince? and where be the tabernacles of wicked men? (or and where be the homes of the wicked?)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 129:8"> Ask ye this of each way-goer; and ye shall know, that he knoweth these same things,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:4; 2ND PETER 2:9–17; 2ND PETER 3:7"> that is, that an evil man shall be kept (safe) into the day of perdition, and he shall be led (forth unscathed un)to the day of strong vengeance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GALATIANS 2:11; DEUTERONOMY 7:10; ROMANS 12:19"> Who shall reprove his ways before him? (or Who shall rebuke him for his ways?) and who shall yield to him (for) those things, which he hath done?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LUKE 16:22; PSALMS 49:14; EZEKIEL 32:21–32"> He shall be led to the sepulchres; and he shall wake in the heap of dead men. (He shall be led to the graves, or to the tombs; and he shall stand watch over many of the dead or and many shall stand watch at his tomb.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:27; JOB 24:24; JOB 30:23"> He was sweet to the stones, either filths, of hell; and he draweth each man after him, and unnumberable men went before him. (The dust of the earth shall be sweet to him or shall lie gently upon him; and he draweth each person after him, like the innumerable people who went before him.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JOB 16:2; JOB 32:3; JOB 13:4"> How therefore comfort ye me in vain, since your answers be showed to (be) contrary (to) the truth? (And so your comfort for me is in vain, for your answers be shown to be contrary to the truth!)</span>
</div>
