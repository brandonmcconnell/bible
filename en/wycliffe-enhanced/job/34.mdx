<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:5; 1ST CORINTHIANS 14:20; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:15"> Wise men, hear ye my words, and learned men, harken ye (to) me;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOB 12:11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 2:15; JOB 33:2"> for the ear proveth words, and the throat deemeth meat by taste (or and the tongue judgeth food by taste).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:21; JUDGES 19:30; ROMANS 12:2"> Choose we doom to us (or Let us use judgement); and see we among us, what is the better.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOB 27:2; JOB 33:9; JOB 16:17"> For Job said, I am just, and God hath turned my justness upside down. (For Job said, I am innocent, but God hath turned my justice, or my sentence, upside down.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOB 6:4; JOB 27:4–6; JOB 16:13"> For why leasing is in deeming me, and mine arrow is violent without any sin. (For lies be used in judging me, and my wound is fatal, yet I am without any sin.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOB 15:16; DEUTERONOMY 29:19; PROVERBS 1:22"> Who is a man, as Job is, that drinketh scorning as water? (Who is such a man like Job, who drinketh scorning like water?)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 1:1; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:33; PROVERBS 13:20"> that goeth with men working wickedness, and goeth with unfaithful men? (who goeth with those working wickedness, and with the unfaithful?)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOB 21:14–16; JOB 35:3; MALACHI 3:14"> For he said, A man shall not please God, yea, though he run with God. (For he said, Yea, though one may run with God, he still shall not please God.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ROMANS 9:14; DEUTERONOMY 32:4; JOB 8:3"> Therefore ye wise men, that is, (ye with) understanding, hear ye me; unpiety, either cruelty, be far from God (or is far from God), and wickedness from Almighty God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 5:10; ROMANS 2:6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:12"> For he shall yield (after) the work of (a) man to him (or For he shall yield to someone after their work, or their deeds); and by the ways of each man he shall restore to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 8:3; PSALMS 11:7; PSALMS 145:17"> For verily God shall not condemn without cause; neither Almighty God shall destroy doom. (For truly God shall not condemn without a reason; nor shall Almighty God destroy, or pervert, justice.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 29:11; ISAIAH 40:13–14; ROMANS 11:34–36"> What other man hath he ordained upon earth? either whom hath he set upon the world, that he hath made? (But hath someone else ordained God upon the earth? or hath someone set him upon the world which he himself hath made? No!)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 104:29; JOB 7:17; JOB 9:4"> If God (ad)dresseth his heart to him, he shall draw to himself his spirit and blast. (If God directed his heart towards people, and he drew back his spirit and his breath unto himself;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 3:19; ECCLESIASTES 12:7; ISAIAH 57:16"> Each flesh shall fail together in dying; and a man shall turn again into ashes. (then all flesh would fail together, that is, they would all die, and everyone would return to the dust.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOB 13:2–6; JOB 12:3"> Therefore if thou hast understanding, hear thou that that is said, and harken (to) the voice of my speech.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOB 40:8; GENESIS 18:25; 2ND SAMUEL 23:3"> Whether he that loveth not doom may be made whole? and how then condemnest thou so much him, that is just? (Can he who loveth not justice be made whole? so how then condemnest thou him, who is so just, or so fair?)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 22:28; PROVERBS 17:26; ROMANS 13:7"> He it is that saith to a king, Thou art apostate, either (a) breaker of religion, when he keepeth not rightfulness and the common good; which calleth the dukes unpious, either unfaithful. (It is he who saith to a king, Thou art an apostate, that is, a breaker of religion, when he keepeth not righteousness and the common good; he who calleth the leaders unpious, or unfaithful.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:17; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:34; JAMES 2:5"> He accepteth not the persons of princes (or He favoureth not princes, or rulers), neither he knoweth a tyrant to spare him, when he striveth against a poor man; for all men be the work of his hands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 73:19; 1ST SAMUEL 25:37–39; 1ST SAMUEL 26:10"> They shall die suddenly, and at midnight peoples shall be troubled; and shall pass, and shall take away a violent man without hand. (And then suddenly they shall die, yea, at midnight these people shall be troubled, and shall pass away; he shall take away a mighty man, without even raising a hand.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:3; JOB 31:4; JEREMIAH 16:17"> For the eyes of God be on the ways of men, and he beholdeth all the goings of them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:13; AMOS 9:2–3; PROVERBS 10:29"> No darknesses be, neither no shadow of death is, that they, that work wickedness, be hid there; (There is no darkness, nor shadow of death, where they, who work wickedness, can be hid;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZRA 9:13; PSALMS 119:137; ROMANS 9:20"> for it is no more in the power of man, that he come to God into doom. (for it is not in the power of man, to set the time, when he come before God for judgement.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 113:7–8; PSALMS 94:5; DANIEL 2:21"> God shall all-break many men and unnumberable (or God shall all-break innumerable powerful men); and shall make other men to stand for them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="HOSEA 7:2; JOB 34:20; AMOS 8:7"> For he knoweth the works of them; &#91;and&#93; therefore he shall bring in (the) night upon them, and they shall be all-broken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 58:10–11; DEUTERONOMY 21:21; ISAIAH 66:24"> He smote them, as unpious men, in the place of seeing men. (He striketh them, like unpious men, where all can see.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 28:5; 1ST SAMUEL 15:11; ISAIAH 5:12"> Which went away from him by casting afore/by forecasting (or For they went away from him by intention, that is, willfully), and would not understand all his ways.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JAMES 5:4; EXODUS 3:7; JOB 35:9"> That they should make the cry of a needy man to come to him, and that he should hear the voice of poor men. (Yea, they who maketh the cry of the needy to come to him, so that he heareth the voice of the poor.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOB 23:13; JOHN 14:27; PSALMS 27:9"> For when he granteth peace, who is it that condemneth him? And since he hideth his cheer, who is (it) that seeth him? And on folks, and on all men, he hath power to do such things. (And if he granteth peace, who is it that condemneth him? But when he hideth his face, then who can find him? And he hath power to do such things over the nations, yea, over all people.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:2–12; JOB 34:21; 2ND KI 21:9"> Which maketh a man, hypocrite, to reign, for the sins of the people. (And he maketh a man, who is a hypocrite, to reign, for the sins of the people.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MICAH 7:9; JOB 33:27; JOB 42:6"> Therefore for I have spoken to God, I shall not forbid thee to speak. (And so now I have spoken for God, and remember, I did not forbid thee to speak for him.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 32:8; PSALMS 25:4–5; PSALMS 139:23–24"> If I have erred, teach thou me; if I have spoken wickedness, I shall no more add to (it).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOB 33:32; ISAIAH 45:9; PSALMS 135:6"> Whether God asketh that wickedness of thee, for it displeased thee? For thou hast begun to speak, and not I; (so) that if thou knowest anything better, speak thou that.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 10:15; JOB 34:4; JOB 34:16"> Men (of) understanding, speak to me; and a wise man, hear me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JOB 35:16; JOB 38:2; JOB 15:2"> Forsooth Job hath spoken follily, and his words sound not (like) teaching. (For Job hath spoken foolishly, and his words be not sound teaching.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JOB 21:7; JOB 23:16; JOB 12:6"> My father (God), be Job proved unto the end; cease thou not from the man of wickedness, (My father God, let Job be proved evil unto the end; cease thou not from this wicked man,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JOB 27:23; ISAIAH 1:19–20; 1ST SAMUEL 15:23"> that addeth blasphemy over his sins. Be he constrained among us in the meantime; and then by his words stir he God to the doom. (who addeth blasphemy on top of his sins. Let him be constrained among us in the meantime; and then, with his words, stir he God to judgement.)</span>
</div>
