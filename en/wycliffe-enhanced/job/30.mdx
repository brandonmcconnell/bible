<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOB 24:5; HEBREWS 11:38; JOB 24:13–16"> They were barren for neediness and hunger; they gnawed in (the) wilderness, and were (made) pale for poverty and wretchedness;</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKE 15:16; 2ND KI 4:38–39; AMOS 7:14"> and they ate herbs, and the rinds of trees; and the root of junipers was their meat. (and they ate grass, and the bark of trees; and juniper roots were their food.)</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DANIEL 4:25; DANIEL 4:32–33; PSALMS 109:10"> The which men ravished these things from great valleys; and when(ever) they had found any of all these things, they ran with (a) cry to them. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 22:1–2; JUDGES 6:2; ISAIAH 2:19"> They dwelled in deserts of strands, and in caves of &#91;the&#93; earth, either on gravel. (They lived in dried up riverbeds, and in caves of the earth, and on rocks.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOB 6:5; JOB 11:12; GENESIS 16:12"> Which were glad among such things (or And they were happy there), and they areckoned (it) as delights to be under bushes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:22; JOB 40:4; ISAIAH 32:6"> These were the sons of fools, and of unnoble men, and utterly appearing not on &#91;the&#93; earth. (They were the sons of fools, and of unnoble men, and were soon driven out of the land.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:63; JOB 17:6; LAMENTATIONS 3:14"> But now I am turned into the song of them, and I am made a proverb to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:67; ISAIAH 50:6; NUMBERS 12:14"> They hold me abominable, and they flee far from me, and dread not to spit on my face (or and do not fear to spit in my face).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOB 12:21; RUTH 1:21; JOB 12:18"> For God hath opened his arrow case, and he hath tormented me, and he hath put a bridle into my mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 19:12; ISAIAH 3:5; JOB 19:18"> At the right side of the east my wretchednesses have risen up at once; they turned upside down my feet, and they oppressed me with their paths as with floods. (On my right side they attack me in a mob; they set my feet running, and they raise against me the ways of their destruction.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 3:12; ZECHARIAH 1:15; PSALMS 69:26"> They destroyed my ways; they setted treason to me, and they had the mastery; and there was none that helped me (or and there was no one who helped me).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOB 22:16; PSALMS 18:4; PSALMS 69:14–15"> They felled in upon me as by a broken wall, and by (a) gate opened (or and by an opened gate), and (they) were stretched forth to my wretchednesses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HOSEA 13:3; JOB 6:4; ISAIAH 44:22"> I am driven into nought; he took away my desire as &#91;the&#93; wind, and mine help passed away as a cloud. (I am driven down into nothing; these torments have swept away my hope like the wind, and my help hath passed away like a cloud.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 42:4; PSALMS 22:14; ISAIAH 53:12"> But now my soul fadeth in myself, and &#91;the&#93; days of torment hold me steadfastly. (But now my life fadeth in myself, and the days of torment strongly hold me.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOB 7:4; JOB 33:19–21; ISAIAH 38:13"> In &#91;the&#93; night my bone is pierced with sorrows; and they, that eat me (or who eat me), sleep not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOB 7:5; JOB 19:20; JOB 2:7"> In the multitude of those my cloth is wasted (or In the multitude of them my cloak is destroyed), and they have girded me (about) as with the collar of a coat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOB 9:31; JOB 42:6; GENESIS 18:27"> I am comparisoned to clay, and I am made like to a dead spark and ashes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOB 19:7; PSALMS 80:4–5; PSALMS 22:2"> I shall cry to thee, and thou shalt not hear me; I stand, and thou beholdest not me. (I cry to thee, but thou hearest me not, or but thou answerest me not; I stand up, but thou seest me not.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOB 10:14–17; JOB 16:9–14; JOB 19:6–9"> Thou art changed into cruel to me, and in the hardness of thine hand thou art adversary to me. (Thou art cruel towards me, and with thy hard hand thou hast become an adversary to me.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 27:21; PSALMS 1:4; HOSEA 4:19"> Thou hast raised me, and hast set me as on (the) wind; and hast hurtled me down strongly. (Thou hast raised me up, and set me on the wind; and then thou hast strongly hurtled me down.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 12:5–7; HEBREWS 9:27; ECCLESIASTES 9:5"> I know, that thou shalt betake me to death, where an house is ordained to each living man. (I know that thou shalt deliver me unto death, where a house is ordained for each man who liveth.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JUDGES 5:31; MATTHEW 27:39–44; JOB 19:7"> Nevertheless thou sendest not out thine hand to the wasting of them; and if they fall down, thou shalt save them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ROMANS 12:15; PSALMS 35:13–14; JEREMIAH 13:17"> I wept sometime on him that was tormented, and my soul had compassion on a poor man. (There were times when I wept over those who were tormented, and my soul had compassion for the poor.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:15; JOB 3:25–26; JEREMIAH 14:19"> I abode goods, and evils be come to me; I abode light, and darknesses brake out. (And I waited for good things, but only evil came to me; I waited for the light, but only darkness broke out.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 2:11; JEREMIAH 31:20; JEREMIAH 4:19"> Mine inner things boiled out without my rest (or My innards, or my bowels, boiled without any rest); and &#91;the&#93; days of torment came before me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 42:9; PSALMS 38:6; PSALMS 43:2"> I went mourning, and I rose up without strong vengeance in the company, and I cried. (I went in mourning, and without any sunshine, or comfort; I rose up in the congregation, and I cried for help.)</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MICAH 1:8; PSALMS 102:6; PSALMS 44:19"> I was the brother of dragons, and the fellow of ostriches.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 4:8; PSALMS 102:3; PSALMS 119:83"> My skin was made black upon me, and my bones dried for heat (or and my bones dried up from the heat).</span>
