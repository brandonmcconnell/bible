<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 28:2–4; JEREMIAH 5:22; EXODUS 19:16"> Mine heart dreaded of this thing, and is moved out of his place. (My heart feareth this thing, and is given a start, or a shock, in its place or and is startled out of its place.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOB 37:5; JOB 36:33; PSALMS 104:7"> It shall hear an hearing in the fearedfulness of his voice, and a sound coming forth &#91;out&#93; of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOB 38:13; MATTHEW 24:27; ISAIAH 11:12"> He beholdeth over all (the) heavens; and his light is over the terms of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 29:3–9; PSALMS 68:33; EXODUS 15:7–8"> (A)Great sound shall roar after him, and he shall thunder with the voice of his greatness; and it shall not be sought out, when his voice is heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOB 5:9; ROMANS 11:33; ISAIAH 40:28"> God shall thunder in his voice wonderfully, which maketh great things that may not be sought out. (God shall thunder wonderfully with his voice, and he maketh great things which cannot be understood.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOB 38:22; JOB 36:27; EZEKIEL 13:13"> He it is that commandeth the snow to come down upon the earth (or It is he who commandeth the snow to come down on the earth), and to the rains of winter, and to the rains of his strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 111:2; PSALMS 109:27; ISAIAH 26:11"> He marketh in the hand of all men, that all men know their works (or so that all can know his works).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 104:22; JOB 38:40"> An unreasonable beast shall go into his den, and shall dwell in his cave, either dark place. (An unreasoning beast shall go into its den, and shall live there in its cave, or that dark place.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOB 9:9; PSALMS 104:3; ISAIAH 21:1"> Tempest shall go out from the inner things, and cold from Arcturus, that is, a sign of five stars in the north. (The tempest shall go out from the south, and the cold shall come from the north.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOB 38:29–30; PSALMS 147:16–18; PSALMS 78:47"> When God maketh blowing, frost waxeth (al)together; and again full broad waters be poured out thereof. (When God maketh blowing, the frost cometh; and very broad waters be poured out again.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOB 36:27–30; MATTHEW 17:5; ISAIAH 18:4"> Wheat desireth clouds, and (the) clouds spread abroad their light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 148:8; JOEL 2:23; JAMES 5:17–18"> The which clouds compass all things about by compass (or Which clouds go about everywhere), whither ever the will of the governor leadeth them, to all thing to which he commandeth them upon the face of the world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST KINGS 18:45; JOB 38:26–27; 1ST SAMUEL 12:18–19"> whether in one lineage, either in his land, (or whether for just one tribe, or over all his land), either in whatever place of his mercy he commandeth those to be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 111:2; PSALMS 145:10–12; EXODUS 14:13"> Job, harken thou (to) these things; stand thou, and behold the marvels of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOB 36:30–32; ISAIAH 40:26; JOB 37:11"> Whether thou knowest, when God commanded to the rains, that those shall show the light of his clouds? (Knowest thou, that when God commandeth to the rains, they show the lightning in his clouds?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOB 36:4; PROVERBS 3:19–20; ISAIAH 40:22"> Whether thou knowest the great ways of the clouds, and the perfect knowings of those? (Knowest thou the great ways of the clouds, which be made by his perfect knowledge?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 147:18; JOB 6:17; JOB 38:31"> Whether thy clothes be not hot, when the earth is blown with the south (wind)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:24; PSALMS 104:2; ISAIAH 40:22"> In hap thou madest with him (the) heavens, which most firm be founded, as of brass. (Perhaps thou madest the heavens with him, which be created most firm, like bronze.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOB 38:2; PSALMS 73:22; JOB 42:3"> Show thou to us, what we shall say to him; for we be wrapped in darknesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOB 11:7–8; PSALMS 139:4; JOB 6:3"> Who shall tell to him, what things I speak? yea, if he speaketh, a man shall be devoured. (Who shall tell him what things he should speak? yea, if a man speaketh, he shall be devoured!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOB 36:32; JOB 26:9; JOB 38:25"> And now men see not (the) light; the air shall be made thick suddenly into clouds, and wind passing shall drive away those. (And now people do not see the light; and then suddenly the air shall be made thick with clouds, but the passing wind shall drive them away.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 104:1; JUDE 1:25; HEBREWS 12:29"> Gold shall come from the north, and the fearedful praising of God. (And then a golden glow shall come from the north, from the terrible, or the wonderful, majesty of God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 6:16; ROMANS 11:33; PSALMS 99:4"> For we may not find him worthily; he is great in strength, and in doom, and in rightfulness, and he may not be told out. (For we be not able to find him; yea, he is great in strength, and in justice, and in righteousness, and he cannot be described, or understood.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:28; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:26; PSALMS 130:4"> Therefore men shall dread him; and all men, that seem to themselves to be wise, shall not be (so fool-)hardy to behold God. (And so people shall fear him; and all, who shall be wise, shall look to God.)</span>
