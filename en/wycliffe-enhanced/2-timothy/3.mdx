<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2ND PETER 3:3; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:3; 1ST TIMOTHY 4:1–3"> But know thou this thing, that in the last days perilous times shall nigh, (But know this, that in the Last Days perilous times shall approach,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:21; ROMANS 1:29–31; JUDE 1:16"> and men shall be loving themselves, covetous, high of bearing, proud, blasphemers, not obedient to (their) father and mother, unkind, cursed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JUDE 1:18; ROMANS 1:31; MATTHEW 10:21"> without affection, &#91;or good will&#93;, without peace, false blamers &#91;or false challengers&#93;, uncontinent, &#91;or unchaste&#93;, unmild, without benignity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:18–19; 1ST TIMOTHY 3:6; JUDE 1:4"> traitors, over-thwart &#91;or froward&#93;, swollen &#91;or blown&#93;with proud thoughts, blind, lovers of lusts more than of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ISAIAH 29:13; TITUS 1:16; MATTHEW 23:27–28"> having the likeness of piety, but denying the virtue of it (or but denying its power). And eschew thou (or shun) these men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JUDE 1:4; 2ND PETER 3:17; TITUS 3:3"> Of these they be that pierce houses, and lead women captives &#91;or and lead little women captive&#93;, charged (or burdened) with sins, which be led with diverse desires,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 4:3–4; EPHESIANS 4:14; 1ST TIMOTHY 2:4"> (for)evermore learning, and never perfectly coming to the science, &#91;or to the cunning&#93;, (or to the knowing), of truth. (always learning, and never perfectly coming to the knowledge of the truth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:8–11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:20; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:5"> And as Jannes and Jambres against-stood Moses, so these against-stand the truth, men corrupt in understanding &#91;or in soul&#93;, reproved about the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 3:8; EXODUS 7:12; JEREMIAH 28:15–17"> But further they shall not profit, for the unwisdom of them shall be known to all men, as theirs was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 6:11; TITUS 2:7; 2ND PETER 1:5–7"> But thou hast gotten my teaching &#91;or my doctrine&#93;, (or But thou hast received my teaching), ordinance, purposing &#91;or purpose&#93;, faith, long abiding, love, patience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 34:19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:5–6; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:23–28"> persecutions, passions, which were made to me at Antioch, at Iconium, at Lystra, what manner persecutions I suffered, and the Lord hath delivered me of all (or but the Lord hath delivered me from all of them).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:14; 1ST PETER 4:12–16; 1ST PETER 2:20–21"> And all men that will live faithfully &#91;or piously&#93; in Christ Jesus, shall suffer persecution. (And all who will live faithfully, or piously, in the Messiah Jesus, shall suffer persecution.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 4:1; 2ND PETER 2:20; TITUS 3:3"> But evil men and deceivers shall increase into worse, erring &#91;themselves&#93;, and sending &#91;others&#93; into error.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 2:13; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:2; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:13"> But dwell thou in these things that thou hast learned, and that be betaken to thee, witting of whom thou hast learned (them); (But remain thou in these things that thou hast learned, and that be delivered to thee, knowing from whom thou hast learned them;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 119:98–99; LUKE 24:45; PROVERBS 22:6"> for thou hast known holy letters from thy youth, which may learn thee &#91;or which may inform thee&#93; to health, by (the) faith that is in Christ Jesus. (for thou hast known the holy Scriptures from thy youth, which can lead thee to salvation, through faith that is in the Messiah Jesus.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ROMANS 15:4; HEBREWS 4:12; 2ND PETER 1:19–21"> For all scripture inspired of (or by) God is profitable to teach, to reprove, to chastise, &#91;for&#93; to learn in rightwiseness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 2:21; EPHESIANS 2:10; 2ND CORINTHIANS 9:8"> (so) that the man of God be perfect, learned to (do) all good work(s).</span>
