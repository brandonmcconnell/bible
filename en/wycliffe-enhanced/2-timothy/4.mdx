<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:14; 1ST TIMOTHY 4:15–16; TITUS 2:15"> preach the word, be thou busy covenably without rest, (or be thou suitably busy, or be thou busy at every opportunity, without any rest, or without ceasing), reprove thou, beseech thou, blame thou in all patience and doctrine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:1–3; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:1–6; 1ST TIMOTHY 4:1–3"> For (the) time shall be, when men shall not suffer &#91;or shall not sustain&#93; wholesome teaching (or when men shall not allow or permit wholesome teaching), but at their desires they shall gather together to themselves masters (or teachers) itching &#91;or pleasing&#93; to the ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 7:11; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:57"> And truly they shall turn away the(ir) hearing from (the) truth, but to fables they shall turn (or and instead they shall turn their attention to fables).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 1:8; 1ST PETER 1:13; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:3"> But wake thou (or Watch), in all things travail thou, do &#91;thou&#93; the work of an evangelist, fulfill thy service, &#91;or (thine) office&#93;, be thou sober (or be resolute).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:23; PHILIPPIANS 2:17; 2ND PETER 1:14–15"> For I am sacrificed now, and the time of my departing is nigh (or and the time of my departure is near).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 6:12; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:24–27; HEBREWS 12:1–2"> I have striven a good strife (or I have fought a good fight), I have ended the course, I have kept the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JAMES 1:12; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:10; 1ST PETER 5:4"> In the tother time a crown of rightwiseness is kept to me, which the Lord, a just doomsman, shall yield to me in that day; and not only to me, but also to these that love his coming. (In the time to come a crown of righteousness is kept for me, which the Lord, a righteous Judge, shall give to me on that Day; and not only to me, but also to those who await his coming with love.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 1:4; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:21"> Hie thou to come to me soon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:15–16; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:15; COLOSSIANS 4:14–15"> For Demas, loving this world, hath forsaken me, and went to Thessalonica, Crescens into Galatia, Titus into Dalmatia;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:12; PHILEMON 1:24; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:25"> Luke alone is with me. Take thou Mark, and bring (him) with thee; for he is profitable to me into service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:4; TITUS 3:12"> Forsooth I sent Tychicus to Ephesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 4:11; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:27; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:11"> The cloth which I left at Troas at Carpas (or The cloak which I left with Carpas in Troas), when thou comest, bring with thee, and the books, but most(ly) the parchments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 1:20; 2ND THESSALONIANS 1:6; PSALMS 28:4"> Alexander, the treasurer, showed to me much evil; the Lord shall yield to him after his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 3:8; PHILIPPIANS 3:2; MATTHEW 10:16–17"> Whom also thou eschew; for he against-stood full greatly our words. (Whom also thou should shun; for he very strongly stood against our words.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:60; JOHN 16:32; MARK 14:50"> In my first defence no man helped me, but all forsook me; be it not areckoned to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:8; PHILIPPIANS 1:12–14; 1ST SAMUEL 17:37"> But the Lord helped me &#91;or Forsooth the Lord stood nigh to me&#93;, and comforted me, that the preaching be &#91;full-&#93;filled by me, and that all folks hear, (and) that I am delivered from the mouth of the lion. (But the Lord stood near to me, or by me, and strengthened me, so that the preaching was fully proclaimed by me, and all the nations heard it, and I was delivered from the lion’s mouth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 121:7; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:3; JUDE 1:24–25"> And the Lord delivered me from all evil work, and shall make me safe into his heavenly kingdom, to whom be glory into worlds of worlds. Amen. (And the Lord shall deliver me from all evil works, and shall bring me safely into his heavenly kingdom, to whom be glory forever and ever. Amen.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:2; 1ST CORINTHIANS 16:19; ROMANS 16:3–4"> Greet well Prisca, and Aquila, and the house of Onesiphorus. (Give hearty greetings to Prisca, and Aquila, and Onesiphorus’ household or family.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:22; PHILIPPIANS 2:26–27"> Erastus (was) left &#91;or dwelt&#93; at Corinth, and I left Trophimus sick at Miletum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 4:9; PHILIPPIANS 4:22; 3RD JOHN 1:14"> Hie thou to come before winter. Eubulus, and Pudens, and Linus, and Claudia, and all brethren, greet thee well, (or and all the brothers, send hearty greetings).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PHILEMON 1:25; GALATIANS 6:18; COLOSSIANS 4:18"> Our Lord Jesus Christ be with thy spirit. The grace of God be with you. Amen.</span>
