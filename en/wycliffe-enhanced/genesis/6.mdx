<div className="bp">

</div>

(and he said, I shall do away man, whom I made out of nothing, from the face of the earth; from man to beast, from the reptiles to the birds of the air; for I repent that I ever made them.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 19:19; HEBREWS 4:16; LUKE 1:30"> Forsooth Noe found grace before the Lord. (But Noah found grace before the Lord.)</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 5:32"> and begat three sons, Shem, Ham, and Japheth.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 53:2–3; PROVERBS 15:3; PSALMS 14:1–3"> And when God saw, that the earth was corrupt, for each flesh, or man, had corrupted his way on &#91;the&#93; earth (or for all people had corrupted their ways upon the earth),</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HOSEA 4:1–2; 1ST PETER 4:7; EZEKIEL 7:2–6"> he said to Noe, The end of all flesh is come before me; the earth is filled with wickedness of the face of them, and I shall destroy them with the earth. (he said to Noah, The end of all flesh hath come before me, for they have filled the earth full of wickedness; and so I shall destroy them all, and the earth with them.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:20; LUKE 17:27; MATTHEW 24:38"> Make thou to thee a ship<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Throughout Genesis, whenever the “Later Version” refers to Noah’s ‘ship’, the “Early Version” refers to Noah’s ‘ark’.">a</a></sup>of wood hewn and planed (or Make thou a ship for thyself out of hewn and planed wood); thou shalt make dwelling places in the ship, and thou shalt anoint it with pitch within and withoutforth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 3:11; GENESIS 7:20"> And so thou shalt make it. The length of the ship shall be of three hundred cubits, the breadth shall be of fifty cubits, and the highness thereof shall be of thirty cubits. (And thou shalt make it thus. The length of the ship shall be three hundred cubits, and the breadth shall be fifty cubits, and the height of it shall be thirty cubits.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 8:6; GENESIS 7:16; EZEKIEL 41:16"> Thou shalt make a window in the ship, and thou shalt end the highness thereof in a cubit (or and the distance from the top of it to the roof shall be one cubit); soothly thou shalt set the door of the ship in the side beneath; thou shalt make solars, and places of three chambers in the ship.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 7:4; 2ND PETER 2:5; GENESIS 7:21–23"> Lo! I shall bring waters of deluge, or great flood, on the earth, and I shall slay each flesh in which is the spirit of life under heaven, and all things that be in &#91;the&#93; earth, shall be wasted. (Behold! and then I shall bring in waters of a deluge, or of a great flood, on the earth, and I shall kill all flesh under heaven in which is the spirit of life, and all the things that be on the earth shall be destroyed.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:7; GENESIS 7:7; GENESIS 17:7"> And I shall set my covenant of peace with thee (or But I shall make my covenant of peace with thee); and thou shalt enter into the ship, &#91;thou&#93;, and thy sons, and thy wife, and the wives of thy sons shall enter with thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 7:15–16; GENESIS 7:8–9; GENESIS 8:17"> And of all living beasts of all flesh, thou shalt bring into the ship twain and twain, of male kind and female, that they live (along) with thee; (And of all living beasts of all flesh, thou shalt bring into the ship two of each kind, male and female, so that they can continue to live, along with thee;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:11–12; GENESIS 2:19; GENESIS 7:8–16"> of birds by their kind, and of work beasts in their kind, and of each creeping beast of &#91;the&#93; earth, by their kind; twain and twain of all shall enter with thee, that they may live. (of birds by their kind, and of work beasts by their kind, and of reptiles by their kind; two of each kind shall enter with thee, so that they will continue to live.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 1:29–30; PSALMS 145:16; PSALMS 136:25"> Therefore thou shalt take with thee of all meats that may be eaten, and thou shalt bear together at thee, and those shall be as well to thee as to the beasts into meat.
