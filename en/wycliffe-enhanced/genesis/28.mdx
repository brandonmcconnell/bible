<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 25:20; HOSEA 12:12; GENESIS 24:50"> but go thou, and walk forth into Mesopotamia of Syria, to the house of Bethuel, &#91;the&#93; father of thy mother, and take to thee from thence a wife of the daughters of Laban, thine uncle. (but go thou forth to Paddan-aram, to the house of Bethuel, the father of thy mother, and get a wife for thyself from there, of one of the daughters of thy uncle Laban.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 48:3; GENESIS 35:11; GENESIS 1:28"> Soothly Almighty God bless thee (or And may Almighty God bless thee), and make thee to increase, and multiply thee, (so) that thou be into companies of peoples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:14; GENESIS 12:7; EPHESIANS 1:3"> and God give to thee the blessing of Abraham, and to thy seed after thee, that thou wield the land of thy pilgrimage, which he promised to thy grand-sire. (and may God give thee the blessing of Abraham, and thy descendants after thee, so that thou possessest this land where thou art now living, which he promised to thy grandfather.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And when Isaac had let go Jacob, Jacob went forth, and came into Mesopotamia of Syria, to Laban, the son of Bethuel of Syria, the brother of Rebecca, his mother. (And so when Isaac had let Jacob go, he went forth, and came to Paddan-aram, and to Laban, the son of Bethuel the Syrian, the brother of his mother Rebecca.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 27:33; GENESIS 28:1"> Forsooth Esau saw that his father had blessed Jacob, and had sent him (away) into Mesopotamia of Syria, that he should wed a wife of thence, and that after the blessing he commanded to Jacob, and said, Thou shalt not take a wife of the daughters of Canaan; (And Esau saw that his father had blessed Jacob, and had sent him away to Paddan-aram, so that he would wed a wife from there, and that after his blessing he had commanded to Jacob, and said, Thou shalt not take a wife from the daughters of Canaan;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:1; EPHESIANS 6:3; COLOSSIANS 3:20"> and that Jacob obeyed to his father and mother, and went into Syria; (and that Jacob had obeyed his father and mother, and had gone away to Paddan-aram;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 24:3; GENESIS 26:34–35; 1ST SAMUEL 8:6"> also Esau proved thereby that his father beheld not gladly the daughters of Canaan. (and so Esau understood by this that his father did not approve of the daughters of Canaan.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 36:3; GENESIS 36:18; GENESIS 36:13"> And (so) Esau went to Ishmael, and wedded a(nother) wife, without these which he had before (or in addition to the two whom he had already wed), Mahalath<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Mahalath is also called Bashemath, but she is a different person than Esau’s 2nd wife, who was also called Bashemath(!).">a</a></sup>, the daughter of Ishmael, son of Abraham, the sister of Nebajoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:2; GENESIS 11:31; GENESIS 32:10"> Therefore Jacob went out of Beersheba, and went to Haran. (And so Jacob left Beersheba, and went toward Haran.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:20; GENESIS 28:18; GENESIS 31:46"> And when he had come to some place, and would rest therein after the going down of the sun, he took (some) of the stones that lay there, and he put (them) under his head, and slept in the same place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOHN 1:51; NUMBERS 12:6; GENESIS 32:1–2"> And he saw in &#91;his&#93; sleep a ladder standing on the earth, and the top thereof touching heaven; and he saw God’s angels going up and going down thereby,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 13:15; GENESIS 48:3; GENESIS 35:12"> and the Lord nighed to the ladder, saying to him, I am the Lord God of Abraham, thy father, and (the) God of Isaac; I shall give to thee and to thy seed the land in which thou sleepest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 12:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:25; GENESIS 18:18"> And thy seed shall be as the dust of &#91;the&#93; earth, (and) thou shalt be alarged to the east, and west, and to the north, and south; and all the lineages of &#91;the&#93; earth shall be blessed in thee and in thy seed, (or and all the families of the earth shall pray to be blessed as thee and thy descendants be blessed, or and through thee and thy descendants, I shall bless all the families of the earth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:5; ISAIAH 41:10; JEREMIAH 1:19"> And I shall be thy keeper, whither ever thou shalt go; and I shall lead thee again into this land, and I shall not leave thee, no but I shall fulfill all &#91;the&#93; things which I have said, (or and I shall not leave thee, until I have fulfilled all the things which I have promised).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOSHUA 5:15; EXODUS 3:5; EXODUS 15:11"> And when Jacob had waked of &#91;the&#93; sleep, he said, Verily the Lord is in this place, and I knew not. (And when Jacob had awakened from his sleep, he said, Truly the Lord is in this place, and I did not know it.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 3:15; GENESIS 35:1–13; ECCLESIASTES 5:1"> And he said dreading, How fearedful, or worshipful, is this place! Here is none other thing, no but the house of God, and the gate of heaven. (And he said with fear, or with reverence, How fearful, or how worshipful, is this place! This is nothing else, but the House of God, or Bethel, and the gateway to heaven, or and heaven’s gate!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 35:14; GENESIS 31:45; GENESIS 31:13"> Therefore Jacob rose early, and took the stone which he had put under his head, and raised (it) up into a title, or (a) sign, and poured out oil (from) above. (And so Jacob rose up early, and took the stone which he had put under his head, and set it up as a sacred pillar, and poured oil on top of it.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="HOSEA 12:4–5; GENESIS 35:1; GENESIS 48:3"> And he called the name of that city Bethel, which was called Luz before. (And he called the name of that place Bethel; but the city that was there before was called Luz.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 6:8; 2ND SAMUEL 15:8; GENESIS 31:13"> Also Jacob avowed a vow, and said, If God is with me, and keepeth me in the way in which I go, (or And Jacob vowed a vow, and said, If God is with me, and keepeth me safe on the way in which I go), and giveth to me loaves to eat, and clothes to be clothed with,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JUDGES 11:31; DEUTERONOMY 26:17; 2ND SAMUEL 19:24"> and I turn again in prosperity to the house of my father, the Lord shall be into God to me. (and I return safety to my father’s house, then the Lord shall be my God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 35:7; GENESIS 14:20; DEUTERONOMY 14:22–23"> And this stone, which I raised into a title, shall be called the house of God; and I shall offer tithes to thee of all things which thou shalt give to me. (And this stone, which I raised up as a sacred pillar, shall be called the House of God; and I shall offer a tithe, or a tenth, to thee, of all the things which thou shalt give me.)</span>
