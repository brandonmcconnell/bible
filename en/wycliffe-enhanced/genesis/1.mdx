<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOHN 1:1–3; HEBREWS 11:3; ISAIAH 45:18"> In the beginning God made of nought heaven and earth. (In the beginning God made out of nothing the heavens and the earth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:23; PSALMS 104:30; ISAIAH 45:18"> Forsooth the earth was idle and void, and darknesses were on the face of (the) depth; and the Spirit of the Lord was borne on the waters &#91;or and the Spirit of God was borne upon the waters&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 4:6; JOHN 1:5; ISAIAH 45:7"> And God said, Light be made, and the light was made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:13; GENESIS 1:18; ECCLESIASTES 11:7"> And God saw the light, that it was good, and he parted the light from (the) darknesses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 74:16; ISAIAH 45:7; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:5"> and he called the light, day, and the darknesses, night. And the eventide and the morrowtide was made, one day (or the first day).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:15; PSALMS 33:6; JOB 37:18"> And God said, The firmament be made in the midst of (the) waters, and part (the) waters from (the) waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 8:28–29; PSALMS 148:4; MATTHEW 8:27"> And God made the firmament, and parted the waters that were under the firmament, from the waters that were on the firmament &#91;or from these that were above the firmament&#93;; and it was done so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 1:5; GENESIS 1:19; GENESIS 1:10"> And God called the firmament, (the) heaven(s). And the eventide and the morrowtide was made, the second day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND PETER 3:5; JONAH 1:9; JEREMIAH 5:22"> Forsooth God said, The waters, that be under (the) heaven(s), be gathered into one place, and a dry place appear; and it was done so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:4; PSALMS 104:31; GENESIS 1:4"> And God called the dry place, (the) earth; and he called the gatherings together of waters, the seas. And God saw that it was good;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 104:14–17; HEBREWS 6:7; PSALMS 147:8"> and said, The earth bring forth green herb, and making seed, and an apple tree making fruit by his kind, whose seed be in itself, on &#91;the&#93; earth; and it was done so. (and said, Let the earth bring forth green herbs, which make seed after their kind, and trees making fruit after their kind, whose seed be in them, all over the earth; and it was done so.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MARK 4:28; ISAIAH 55:10–11; MATTHEW 13:24–26"> And the earth brought forth green herb and making seed by his kind, and a tree making fruit, and each having seed by his kind. And God saw that it was good. (And the earth brought forth green herbs, which make seed after their kind, and trees making fruit, and each having seed after its kind. And God saw that it was good.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the eventide and the morrowtide was made, the third day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 104:19–20; DEUTERONOMY 4:19; JEREMIAH 31:35"> Forsooth God said, Lights be made in the firmament of (the) heaven(s), and part they the day and (the) night; and be they into signs, and times, and days, and years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and shine those in the firmament of (the) heaven(s), and lighten they the earth; and it was done so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 136:7–9; DEUTERONOMY 4:19; PSALMS 8:3"> And God made two great lights, the greater light that it should be before to the day, and the less(er) light that it should be before to the night; and God made (the) stars;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 8:3; PSALMS 8:1; JOB 38:12"> and setted them in the firmament of (the) heaven(s), (so) that they should shine on &#91;the&#93; earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:35; PSALMS 19:6"> and that they should be before to the day and (before)&#91;to the&#93; night, and should part (the) light and (the) darkness. And God saw that it was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And the eventide and the morrowtide was made, the fourth day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 2:19; PSALMS 104:24–25; 1ST KINGS 4:33"> Also God said (or And God said), The waters bring forth a reptile, either a creeping beast, of living soul, and a volatile, either a bird flying above &#91;the&#93; earth, under the firmament of (the) heaven(s).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 104:24–26; GENESIS 1:25; GENESIS 8:19"> And God made of nought great whales, and each living soul and movable, which the waters have brought forth in their kinds; and God made of nought each volatile by his kind. And God saw that it was good; (And God made out of nothing great whales, and each living soul that moveth, which the waters brought forth after their kind; and God made out of nothing each bird after its kind. And God saw that it was good;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 40:15; GENESIS 8:17; PSALMS 107:38"> and blessed them, and said, Wax ye, and be ye multiplied, and fill ye the waters of the sea, and &#91;the&#93; birds be multiplied on &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the eventide and the morrowtide was made, the fifth day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 50:9–10; GENESIS 6:20; GENESIS 8:19"> And God said, The earth bring forth a living soul in his kind, work beasts, and reptiles, either creeping beasts, and unreasonable beasts of &#91;the&#93; earth, by their kinds; and it was done so. (And God said, Let the earth bring forth living souls after their kind, yea, work beasts, and reptiles, or creeping beasts, and unreasoning beasts of the earth, all after their kind; and it was done so.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JEREMIAH 27:5; JOB 12:8–10; GENESIS 2:19–20"> And God made unreasonable beasts of the earth by their kinds, and work beasts, and each creeping beast of the earth in his kind. And God saw that it was good; (And God made the unreasoning beasts of the earth after their kind, and the work beasts, and the reptiles of the earth, each after its kind. And God saw that it was good;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:10; EPHESIANS 4:24; PSALMS 8:4–8"> and said, Make we man to our image and likeness, and be he sovereign to the fishes of the sea, and to the volatiles of (the) heaven(s), and to &#91;the&#93; unreasonable beasts of &#91;the&#93; earth, and to each creature, and to each creeping beast/each reptile, which is moved in &#91;the&#93; earth. (and said, Let us make man in our image and likeness, and be he sovereign over the fishes of the sea, and over the birds of the air, and over the unreasoning beasts of the earth, yea, over each creature, and over each reptile which creepeth on the earth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:4; EPHESIANS 2:10; GENESIS 5:1–2"> And God made of nought a man to his image and likeness; God made of nought a man, to the image of God; God made of nought them, male and female. (And so God made out of nothing a man in his image and likeness; God made out of nothing a man in the image of God; yea, God made them out of nothing, male and female.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 9:1; GENESIS 9:7; LEVITICUS 26:9"> And God blessed them, and said, Increase ye, and be ye multiplied, and fill ye the earth, and make ye it subject; and be ye lords to the fishes of the sea, and to &#91;the&#93; volatiles of (the) heaven(s), and to all living beasts that be moved on &#91;the&#93; earth, (or and be ye lords, or rule ye, over the fishes of the sea, and the birds of the air, and all the living beasts that move on the earth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 9:3; PSALMS 104:14–15; PSALMS 145:15–16"> And God said, Lo! I have given to you each herb bearing seed on &#91;the&#93; earth, and all (the) trees that have in themselves the seed of their kind, that they be into meat to you (or so that they can be food for you);</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 104:14; PSALMS 145:15–16; PSALMS 147:9"> and to all living beasts of &#91;the&#93; earth, and to each bird of (the) heaven(s), and to all things that be moved in &#91;the&#93; earth, and in which is a living soul, that they have (them) to eat; and it was done so. (and also for all the living beasts of the earth, and for each bird of the air, and for all the things that creep on the earth, and in which is a living soul, so that they have them to eat; and it was done so.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 4:4; PSALMS 104:24; PSALMS 19:1–2"> And God saw all (the) things which he (had) made, and they were full good (or and they were very good). And the eventide and the morrowtide was made, the sixth day.</span>
