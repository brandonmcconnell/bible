<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 25:34; PROVERBS 21:1; 1ST SAMUEL 25:26"> And the Lord said to him, And I know that thou didest by simple heart, and therefore I kept thee, lest thou didest sin against me, and I suffered not that thou touchedest her; (And the Lord said to him, Yea, I know that thou didest this with a pure heart, and so I kept thee, so that thou didest not sin against me, and I did not allow thee to touch her;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOB 42:8; 1ST SAMUEL 7:5; 2ND SAMUEL 24:17"> now therefore yield thou the wife to her husband, for he is a prophet; and he shall pray for thee, and thou shalt live; soothly if thou wilt not yield her, know thou that thou shalt die by death, thou, and all things that be thine, (or but if thou wilt not give her back to him, know thou that thou shalt die, thou, and all who be thine).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And at once Abimelech rose (up) by night, and called (for) all his servants, and spake all these words in the ears of them; and all men dreaded greatly (or and all the men had great fear).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 12:18; GENESIS 26:10; GENESIS 39:9"> Soothly Abimelech called also Abraham (or And then Abimelech called for Abraham), and said to him, What hast thou done to us? what sinned we against thee, that thou hast brought in on me and on my realm (such) a great sin? thou hast done to us which things thou oughtest not to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And again Abimelech asked, and said, What thing sawest thou, that thou wouldest do this? (And again Abimelech asked Abraham, Why did thou do this?)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 26:7; GENESIS 12:12; JOB 28:28"> (And) Abraham answered, I thought within me, and said, In hap the dread of God is not in this place; and they shall slay me for my wife;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 11:29; GENESIS 12:13; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:22"> in other manner forsooth and she is my sister verily, (for she is) the daughter of my father, and not the daughter of my mother; and I wedded her into wife; (and truly in one way she is my sister, for she is the daughter of my father, but not the daughter of my mother; and so I wedded her for a wife;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 12:1; GENESIS 12:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:3–5"> soothly after that God led me out of the house of my father, I said to her, Thou shalt do this mercy with me in each place to which we shall enter; thou shalt say, that I am thy brother. (and after that God led me out of my father’s house, I said to her, Thou shalt do this mercy for me in each place to which we shall enter; thou shalt say that I am thy brother.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 12:16; GENESIS 20:2; GENESIS 20:7"> Therefore Abimelech took sheep, and oxen, and servants, and handmaids, and gave to Abraham; and he yielded to him Sarah, his wife, (And so Abimelech took sheep, and oxen, and male and female slaves, and gave them to Abraham; and he gave him back his wife Sarah,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 34:10; GENESIS 13:9; GENESIS 47:6"> and said, The land is before you; dwell thou, wherever it pleaseth thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:5; GENESIS 20:5; GENESIS 24:65"> Forsooth Abimelech said to Sarah, Lo! (And Abimelech said to Sarah, Behold! I have given a thousand pieces of silver to thy brother; this shall be proof to all thy own people that thou art guiltless; but wherever thou goest, remember that thou art already taken.)</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
