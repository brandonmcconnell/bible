<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:1; GENESIS 27:13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:29"> Now therefore, my son, assent to my counsels,</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and (so) that when thou hast brought in those meats, and he hath eaten, he (shall) bless thee before that he die.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:18; GENESIS 25:27; MALACHI 1:14"> (so) if my father shall touch (me), or draw me to him, and feel me, I dread lest he guess that I would scorn him, and he bring in cursing on me for blessing (or and I bring in a curse upon myself, and not a blessing).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:25; 1ST SAMUEL 25:24; 2ND SAMUEL 14:9"> To whom his mother said, My son, this cursing be in me, (or My son, any curse shall be upon me); only hear thou my voice, and go, and bring that that I said.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and she gave to him the stew (or and then she gave him the stew), and betook (to) him &#91;the&#93; loaves, which she had baked.</span>

I have done for thee as thou commandedest me; rise thou up, and sit, and eat my venison, and then afterward thou can bless me.)</span>

And Jacob answered, It was God’s will, that what I desired, should come so soon to me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JAMES 4:8; ISAIAH 57:19; GENESIS 27:12"> And Isaac said, My son, come thou hither, (so) that I (can) touch thee, and that I prove whether thou be my son Esau, or nay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> (And) Jacob nighed to his father; and when Isaac had feeled him, he said, Soothly the voice is the voice of Jacob, but the hands be the hands of Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 27:16; HEBREWS 11:20; ROMANS 9:11–12"> And Isaac knew not Jacob, for the hairy hands showed the likeness of the elder son. Therefore Isaac blessed Jacob (or And so that is why Isaac would soon bless Jacob),</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:9; EPHESIANS 4:25; PROVERBS 12:22"> and (so he) said (again), Art thou my son Esau? (And) Jacob answered, I am.</span>

(And Jacob came over, and kissed him; and when Isaac smelled the aroma of his clothes, at once he blessed him, saying, Behold! the aroma of my son is like the aroma of a plentiful field which the Lord hath blessed.)</span>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 12:3; NUMBERS 24:9; ISAIAH 49:23"> and (may) peoples serve thee, and lineages worship thee; be thou lord of thy brethren, and the sons of thy mother be bowed (low) before thee; be he cursed that curseth thee, and he that blesseth thee, be he &#91;full-&#93;filled with blessings, (or be they cursed who curse thee, but let those who bless thee, be filled full with blessings).</span>

<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GENESIS 27:4"> and brought in meats sodden of the hunting to the father, and said, My father, rise thou, and eat of the hunting of thy son, that thy soul bless me. <span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GENESIS 27:18"> And Isaac said, Who art thou? Which answered, I am Esau, thy first begotten son. (And Isaac said, Who art thou? And Esau answered, I am Esau, thy first-born son.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="GENESIS 28:3–4; ROMANS 11:29; EPHESIANS 1:3"> Isaac dreaded with a great astonishing; and he wondered more than it may be believed, and said, Who therefore is he which a while ago brought to me venison taken, and I ate of all things before that thou camest; and I blessed him? and he shall be blessed. (And Isaac dreaded with great astonishment; and he wondered more than it can be imagined, and he said, Then who was it, who just a short while ago, brought me the newly caught venison, and I ate all of it before that thou camest in; and I blessed him? and yea, he shall be blessed.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:17; PROVERBS 1:31; 1ST SAMUEL 30:4"> When the words of the father were heard, Esau roared with a great cry, and was astonished, and said, My father, bless thou also me. (And when he heard his father’s words, Esau roared with a great cry, and was astonished, and said, My father, thou must also bless me!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 4:6; JOB 13:7; MALACHI 2:10"> Which said (or And Jacob said), Thy brother came prudently &#91;or Thy brother came (be)guilingly&#93;, and took (away) thy blessing.</span>

And again he said to the father, Whether thou hast not reserved a blessing also to me? (And Esau added, His name is rightly called Jacob, that is, the Heel, or the Supplanter, for behold! now he hath supplanted me the second time; first he took away my birthright as the first-born son, and now he hath cheated me out of my blessing. And again he said to his father, Hast thou not reserved a blessing for me?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 8:14; GENESIS 27:28–29; GENESIS 25:23"> Isaac answered, I have made him thy lord, and I have made subject all his brethren to his servage; I have stablished him in wheat, and wine, and oil; and (so), my son, what shall I do to thee after these things? (Isaac answered, I have made him thy lord, and I have made all of his brothers to be in servitude to him, that is, to be his slaves; I have established him with corn, or with grain, and wine, and oil; and so now, my son, after all these things, what is left that I can do for thee?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:17; GENESIS 27:34; ISAIAH 65:14"> To whom Esau said, Father, whether thou hast only one blessing? I beseech thee, that also thou bless me. And when Esau wept with great yelling, (To whom Esau said, Father, hast thou only one blessing? I beseech thee, that thou also bless me. And when Esau wept with great yelling,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="GENESIS 27:28; HEBREWS 11:20; JOSHUA 24:4"> Isaac was stirred, and said to him, Thy blessing shall be (not) in the fatness of &#91;the&#93; earth, and in the dew of heaven from above; (Isaac was stirred, and said to him, Thy dwelling shall be far from the fatness of the earth, and far from the dew of heaven above;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2ND KI 8:20–22; GENESIS 25:23; 2ND CHRONICLES 25:11–12"> thou shalt live by (the) sword, and thou shalt serve thy brother, and (then the) time shall come when thou shalt shake away, and unbind his yoke from &#91;off&#93; thy nolls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1ST JOHN 3:12–15; GENESIS 37:4; GENESIS 50:3–4"> Therefore Esau hated evermore Jacob for the blessing by which the father had blessed him; and Esau said in his heart, The days of mourning of my father shall come, and (then) I shall slay Jacob, my brother. (And so Esau hated Jacob even more for the blessing with which his father had blessed him; and Esau said in his heart, The days of mourning for my father shall come, and then I shall kill my brother Jacob.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="GENESIS 37:18–20; 1ST SAMUEL 30:5; GENESIS 42:21–22"> These things were told to Rebecca, and she sent, and called (for) her son Jacob, and said to him, Lo! Esau, thy brother, menaceth to slay thee, (or Behold! Esau, thy brother, hath threatened to kill thee);</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="GENESIS 27:8; GENESIS 11:31; GENESIS 27:13"> now therefore, my son, hear thou my voice, and rise thou up, and flee to Laban, my brother, into Haran, (or and flee to my brother Laban, in Haran);</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="GENESIS 31:38; GENESIS 31:41"> and thou shalt dwell with him (for) a few days, till the strong vengeance of thy brother rest, and his indignation cease,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:21; GENESIS 4:8–16; GENESIS 9:5–6"> and till he forget those things which thou hast done against him. And then afterward I shall send for thee, and I shall bring thee back here. For why should I be deprived of both sons in one day?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="GENESIS 24:3; GENESIS 26:34–35; GENESIS 28:8"> And Rebecca said to Isaac, It annoyeth me of my life for the daughters of Heth (or I am weary to death of the daughters of the Hittites); if Jacob take a wife of the kindred of this land, I will not live.</span>
