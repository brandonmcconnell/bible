And &#91;the&#93; waters were decreased, or assuaged, (And the Lord remembered Noah, and all the living beasts, and all the work beasts, that were with him in the ship; and he brought forth a wind on the earth. And the waters decreased,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 7:11; MATTHEW 8:26–27; PROVERBS 8:28"> and the wells of the sea were closed, and the windows of (the) heaven(s) were closed, and (the) rains of (the) heaven(s) were ceased. (and the wells of the sea were closed, and the windows of the sky were closed, and the rains from the sky ceased.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 7:24; GENESIS 7:11"> And &#91;the&#93; waters turned again from (off) the earth, and went again, and began to be decreased, or assuaged, after an hundred and fifty days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND KI 19:37; JEREMIAH 51:27; ISAIAH 37:38"> And the ship rested in the seventh month, in the seven and twentieth day of the month, on the hills of Armenia. (And the ship rested in the seventh month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on Mount Ararat.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 7:11"> And soothly the waters went and decreased till to the tenth month, for in the tenth month, in the first day of the month (or on the first day of the month), the tops of &#91;the&#93; hills appeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 6:16; DANIEL 6:10"> And when forty days had passed, Noe opened the window of the ship which he had made,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 147:9; 1ST KINGS 17:6; 1ST KINGS 17:4"> and sent out a crow, which went out, and turned not again till the waters were dried on &#91;the&#93; earth. (and sent out a crow, which went out, and did not return until the waters were dried up from off the earth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 2:14; SONG OF SONGS 1:15; MATTHEW 10:16"> Also Noe sent out a culver after him, to see if the waters had ceased then on the face of the earth; (And then Noah sent out a dove after him, to see if the waters had now gone from off the face of the earth;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:28; PSALMS 116:7; DEUTERONOMY 28:65"> and when the culver found not where her foot should rest, she turned again to him into the ship, for the waters were on all &#91;the&#93; earth; and Noe held forth his hand, and brought the culver, (once) taken, (back) into the ship. (but when the dove found nowhere to rest her feet, she returned to him in the ship, for the waters were still over all the earth; and Noah held forth his hand, and caught her, and brought the dove back into the ship.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 7:4; GENESIS 7:10; GENESIS 8:12"> Soothly when other seven days were abided afterward, again he sent out a culver from the ship; (And he waited another seven days, and then again he sent out the dove from the ship;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 8:15; ZECHARIAH 4:12–14; ROMANS 10:15"> and she came (back) to him at eventide, and bare in her mouth a branch of (an) olive tree with green leaves. Therefore Noe understood that the waters had ceased (from flowing) on (the) earth (or And so Noah understood that the waters had now gone from off the face of the earth);</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 25:9; ISAIAH 30:18; GENESIS 8:10"> and nevertheless he abode seven other days, and (then) sent out a culver, which turned not again to him. (nevertheless he waited another seven days, and then again sent out the dove, but this time she did not return to him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 7:11"> Therefore in the six hundred and one year of the life of Noe, in the first month, in the first day of the month, &#91;the&#93; waters were decreased on (the) earth; and Noe opened the roof of the ship, and beheld, and saw that the face of the earth was dried. (And so in the six hundred and first year of Noah’s life, in the first month, on the first day of the month, the waters were gone from off the face of the earth; and Noah opened the roof of the ship, and looked, and saw that the face of the earth was becoming dry.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 7:13–14; GENESIS 7:11"> In the second month, in the seven and twentieth day of the month, the earth was made dry. (And by the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the whole earth was made dry.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Soothly the Lord spake to Noe; and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 7:13; PSALMS 121:8; PSALMS 91:11"> Go out of the ship, thou, and thy wife, and thy sons, and the wives of thy sons with thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 1:22; GENESIS 9:1; JEREMIAH 31:27–28"> and lead out with thee all living beasts that be with thee of each flesh, as well in volatiles, as in unreasonable beasts, and all reptiles/all creeping beasts that creep on &#91;the&#93; earth; and enter ye on the earth, increase ye, and be ye multiplied on (the) earth. (and lead out with thee all the living beasts that be with thee of all flesh, yea, the birds, and the unreasoning beasts, and all the reptiles that creep on the earth; and go they over the earth, and increase they, and be they multiplied on the earth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 121:8"> Therefore Noe went out, and his sons, and his wife, and the wives of his sons with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> but also all (the) living beasts, and work beasts, (and birds), and reptiles that creep on &#91;the&#93; earth, (all) by their kind, went out of the ship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:15–16; ROMANS 12:1; GENESIS 22:9"> Forsooth Noe builded an altar to the Lord, and he took of all clean beasts and birds, and offered burnt sacrifices on the altar. (And Noah built an altar to the Lord, and he took one of each kind of the clean beasts and birds, and offered burnt sacrifices on the altar.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 2:15; EPHESIANS 5:2; ISAIAH 54:9–10"> And the Lord savoured the odour of sweetness, and said to him(self), I shall no more curse the earth for men, for (I know that) the wit and thought of man’s heart be ready, either prone, into evil from (a) young waxing age; therefore I shall no more smite each living soul, as I did; (And the Lord savoured the aroma of sweetness, and said to himself, I shall no more curse the earth for man’s sake, for I know that the wit and the thought of man’s heart be prone toward evil from a young age; and so I shall no more strike down each living soul, as I have done;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JAMES 5:7; PSALMS 74:16–17; JEREMIAH 5:24"> (and so) in all the days of &#91;the&#93; earth, seed and ripe corn, cold and heat, summer and winter, night and day, shall not rest. (and so for all the days that remain for the earth, seed-time and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, night and day, shall never cease.)</span>
