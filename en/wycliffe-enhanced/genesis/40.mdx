</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:14; PROVERBS 19:12; PSALMS 76:10"> And Pharaoh was wroth against them, for the one was (the) master butler, and the tother was (the) master baker.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 39:20; GENESIS 39:23"> And he sent them into the prison of the prince of knights, in which also Joseph was bound. (And he sent them into the prison of the captain of the guard, where Joseph was also kept.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 39:1; PSALMS 37:5; GENESIS 37:36"> And the keeper of the prison betook them to Joseph, which also served, or kept, them. Somewhat of time passed, and they were holden in keeping, (And the ruler of the prison delivered them unto Joseph, who served them. Some time passed, and they were still held in the prison,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 41:11; GENESIS 40:8; GENESIS 37:5–10"> and both saw a dream in one night, by covenable expounding to them. (when both of them had a dream one night, and they needed their dreams to be interpreted.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 41:8; DANIEL 2:1–3; GENESIS 40:8"> And when Joseph had entered to them early, and had seen them sorry, (And when Joseph came to them in the morning, and saw them looking sorrowful, or sad,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 2:2; 1ST SAMUEL 1:8; LUKE 24:17"> he asked them, and said, Why is your cheer heavier today than it is wont (to be)? (he asked them, Why are your faces so heavy, or so unhappy, today?)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DANIEL 2:47; GENESIS 41:15–16; DANIEL 2:28"> Which answered, We (each) saw a dream, and there is no man that expoundeth it to us. And Joseph said to them, Whether the expounding is not of God? Tell ye to me what ye have seen. (And they answered, We have each had a dream, and there is no one to interpret it for us. And Joseph said to them, Should not the interpretation come from God? Tell me what ye have seen.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JUDGES 7:13–15; GENESIS 37:5–10; DANIEL 4:8"> The master butler told first his dream; I saw before me that a vine, (So the master butler told his dream first, and he said, I saw that there was a vine before me,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> in which were three scions, waxed little and little into burgeonings, and that after the flowers (came), the grapes waxed ripe, (which had three branches, that over time had more and more buds, and after the flowers came, the grapes ripened,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 1:11–1; 1ST KINGS 10:5; 2ND CHRONICLES 9:4"> and (then) the cup of Pharaoh was in mine hand; therefore I took the grapes, and pressed them out into the cup that I held, and I gave (the) drink to Pharaoh (or and I gave it to Pharaoh to drink).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 41:12; JUDGES 7:14; GENESIS 41:25–26"> Joseph answered, This is the expounding of the dream; three scions be yet three days, (Joseph answered, This is the dream’s interpretation; the three branches be three days yet to come,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 3:3; JEREMIAH 52:31; 2ND KI 25:27"> after which Pharaoh shall have mind of thy service, and he shall restore thee into the first degree, and thou shalt give to him the cup, by thine office, as thou were wont to do before. (and then Pharaoh shall remember thy service, and he shall restore thee to thy place, and thou shalt give him the cup, as per thy duties, yea, as thou were wont to do before.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOSHUA 2:12; LUKE 23:42; 1ST SAMUEL 20:14–15"> Only have thou mind of me, when it is well to thee, and thou shalt do mercy with me, that thou make suggestion to Pharaoh, that he lead me out of this prison; (And when it is well with thee, remember me, and do thou mercy to me, so that thou make a suggestion to Pharaoh, that he release me from this prison;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:17–18; GENESIS 39:20; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:10"> for thiefly, (that is, by thievery), I am taken away from the land of Hebrews, and (once) here I am sent innocent into prison. (for I was stolen away from the land of the Hebrews, and then once here, I was sent into prison, though innocent.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 40:1–2"> The master baker saw that Joseph had declared prudently the dream, and he said, And I saw a dream, that I had three baskets of meal on mine head, (The master baker saw that Joseph had prudently interpreted the dream, and he said, And I had a dream, where I had three baskets of meal on my head,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 49:20; 1ST CHRONICLES 12:20"> and I guessed that I bare in one basket, that was highest, all (the) (bake)meats that be made (for Pharaoh) by the craft of bakers, and that birds ate thereof. (and I saw that I carried in the first basket, that was on top, all the baked goods that the baker made for Pharaoh, and the birds were eating them.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 40:12; GENESIS 41:26; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:24"> Joseph answered, This is the expounding of the dream; three baskets be yet three days, (Joseph answered, This is the dream’s interpretation; the three baskets be three days yet to come,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 40:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:27; 2ND SAMUEL 21:6"> after which Pharaoh shall take away thine head, and he shall hang thee in a cross, and birds shall draw thy flesh. (after which Pharaoh shall take away thy head, and he shall hang thee on a tree, and then the birds shall draw off thy flesh.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 40:19; GENESIS 40:13; MATTHEW 14:6"> From thence the third day was the day of the birth of Pharaoh, which made a great feast to his servants, and he had mind among the meats, of the master butler, and of the prince of (the) bakers; (And three days later was Pharaoh’s birthday, and he made a great feast for his servants, and during the feast he remembered the master butler, and the master baker;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 40:13; NEHEMIAH 2:1"> and he restored the one into his place, (so) that he should direct the cup, either (the) drink, to the king,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 40:19; GENESIS 41:16; DANIEL 2:30"> and he hanged the tother in a gibbet, that the truth of Joseph(’s) declaring (of) the dreams should be proved. (but he hanged the other man on a tree, and so the truth of Joseph’s interpretations of the dreams was shown.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 31:12; JOB 19:14; PSALMS 105:19"> And nevertheless when prosperities befelled to the master butler, he forgat Joseph that declared his dream. (And yet when good things happened to the master butler, he forgot about Joseph, who had interpreted his dream.)</span>
</div>
