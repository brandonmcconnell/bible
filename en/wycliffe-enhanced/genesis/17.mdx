<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:13; EPHESIANS 3:20; LUKE 1:6"> Forsooth after that Abram began to be of ninety years and nine, the Lord appeared to him, and said to him, I am Almighty God; go thou before me, and be thou perfect; (Now when Abram was ninety-nine years old, the Lord appeared to him, and said to him, I am Almighty God; go thou before me, and do thou what is right;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 17:4–6; GENESIS 12:2; PSALMS 105:8–11"> and I shall set my covenant of peace betwixt me and thee; and I shall multiply thee full greatly (or and I shall greatly multiply thee).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JUDGES 13:20; GENESIS 17:17; NUMBERS 16:45"> And Abram felled down low on his face. And God said to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 35:11; GALATIANS 3:28–29; GENESIS 12:2"> I am, and my covenant of peace is with thee, and thou shalt be the father of many folks (or and thou shalt be the father of many nations);</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 1:21–23; NEHEMIAH 9:7; GENESIS 17:15"> and thy name shall no more be called Abram, but thou shalt be called Abraham, for I have made thee &#91;the&#93; father of many folks (or for I have made thee the father of many nations);</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 17:4; MATTHEW 1:6–17; GENESIS 35:11"> and I shall make thee to wax full greatly, and I shall set thee in folks (or and I shall make nations to come out of thee), and kings shall go out of thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 9:7–9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:39; GENESIS 26:24"> and I shall make my covenant between me and thee, and between thy seed after thee, in their generations, by everlasting bond of peace, that I be thy God, and of thy seed after thee; (and I shall make my covenant between me and thee, and thy descendants after thee, in all their generations, an everlasting covenant, that I be thy God, and the God of thy descendants after thee;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:12; PSALMS 105:11; EXODUS 6:7"> and I shall give to thee, and to thy seed after thee, the land of thy pilgrimage, all the land of Canaan, into everlasting possession, and I shall be the God of them. (and I shall give to thee, and to thy descendants after thee, this land where thou art now living, yea, all the land of Canaan, for an everlasting possession, and I shall be their God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 56:4–5; PSALMS 25:10; PSALMS 103:18"> God said again to Abraham, And therefore thou shalt keep my covenant, and thy seed after thee, in their generations. (And God said to Abraham, And so thou shalt keep my covenant, thou, and thy descendants after thee, in all generations.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 2:11–12; ROMANS 4:9–11; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:8"> This is my covenant, which ye shall keep, betwixt me and you, and thy seed after thee; each male kind of you shall be circumcised (or every male among you shall be circumcised),</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 4:11; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:8; DEUTERONOMY 10:16"> and ye shall circumcise the flesh of your man’s rod, that it be into a sign of bond of peace betwixt me and you (or so that it be a sign of the covenant between me and you).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 12:3; LUKE 2:21; EXODUS 12:48–49"> A young child of eight days shall be circumcised in you, all male kind in your generations, as well a born servant &#91;of your household&#93;, as a servant bought, shall be circumcised, (A newborn child among you shall be circumcised on the eighth day, yea, all the males of every generation, as well as a slave born in your household, and a slave who is bought, yea, all the males shall be circumcised,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 12:44; GENESIS 37:36; NEHEMIAH 5:8"> and whoever is of your kindred, he (also) shall be circumcised; and my covenant shall be in your flesh into everlasting bond of peace (or and so my covenant shall be made in your flesh as an everlasting covenant).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 4:24–26; NUMBERS 15:30–31; EXODUS 12:19"> A(ny) man whose flesh of his rod shall not be circumcised, that man shall be done away from his people; for he made void my covenant (or for he hath broken my covenant).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 12:25; GENESIS 32:28; GENESIS 17:5"> Also God said to Abraham (or And God said to Abraham), Thou shalt not call Sarai, thy wife, Sarai, but Sarah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 35:11; GENESIS 17:6; ISAIAH 49:23"> and I shall bless her, and of her I shall give to thee a son, whom I shall bless, and he shall be into nations, and kings of peoples shall be born of him. (and I shall bless her, and I shall give thee a son by her; yea, I shall bless her, and she shall be the mother of nations, and kings of many people shall be born from her.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:16; GENESIS 21:6; GENESIS 18:12"> Abraham felled down on his face, and laughed in his heart, and said, Guessest thou, whether a child shall be born to a man of an hundred years, and Sarah of ninety years shall bear a child? (And Abraham fell down on his face, and laughed in his heart, and said, Thinkest thou, that a child shall be born to a man who is a hundred years old, and that Sarah, who is ninety years old, shall yet bear a child?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:39; PSALMS 41:12; PSALMS 4:6"> And he said to the Lord, I would that Ishmael might live before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKE 1:13–20; GENESIS 21:2–3; 2ND KI 4:16–17"> And the Lord said to Abraham, Sarah, thy wife, shall bear a son to thee, and thou shalt call his name Isaac, and I shall make my covenant with him into everlasting bond of peace, and to his seed after him; (And the Lord said to Abraham, No, thy wife Sarah shall bear a son for thee, and thou shalt call his name Isaac, and I shall make my covenant with him, and his descendants after him, yea, an everlasting covenant;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 21:18; GENESIS 21:13; GENESIS 25:12–18"> also on Ishmael I have heard thee, lo! I shall bless him, and I shall increase (him), and I shall multiply him greatly; he shall engender twelve dukes, and I shall make him into a great folk. (and regarding Ishmael, I have heard thee, behold! I shall bless him, and I shall increase him, and I shall greatly multiply him; he shall beget twelve princes, and I shall make him into a great nation.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 21:2–3; GENESIS 26:2–5; GALATIANS 3:29"> Forsooth I shall make my covenant with Isaac, whom Sarah shall child to thee in this time in the tother year. (But I shall make my covenant with Isaac, whom Sarah shall bear for thee at this same time next year.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 18:33; GENESIS 35:9–15; JUDGES 6:21"> And when the word of the speaker with him was ended (or And when he was finished speaking), God ascended from Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 119:60; GENESIS 18:19; GENESIS 17:10–14"> Forsooth Abraham took Ishmael, his son, and all the born servants of his house, and all which he had bought, all the males of all men of his house, and circumcised the flesh of their rod(s), anon in that day, as the Lord commanded to him. (Then Abraham took his son Ishmael, and all the slaves born in his household, and all the slaves he had bought, yea, all the males of his household, and at once circumcised the flesh of their rods on that day, as the Lord had commanded him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ROMANS 4:11; GENESIS 17:17; ROMANS 4:19–20"> Abraham was of ninety years and nine when he circumcised the flesh of his rod, (Abraham was ninety-nine years old when he circumcised the flesh of his own rod,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and Ishmael, his son, had filled thirteen years in the time of his circumcision. (and his son Ishmael was thirteen years old at the time of his circumcision.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 119:60; GENESIS 22:3–4; GENESIS 12:4"> Abraham was circumcised in the same day, and Ishmael his son, (And Abraham and his son Ishmael were circumcised on the same day,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GENESIS 18:19"> and all the men of his house, as well born servants, as (those) bought and aliens, were circumcised together. (and all the men of his household, yea, the slaves born in his household, and those bought from foreigners, were circumcised with him.)</span>
