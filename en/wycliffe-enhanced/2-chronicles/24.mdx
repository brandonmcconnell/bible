And burnt sacrifices were offered in the house of the Lord continually, in all the days of Jehoiada.</span>
</div>

<div className="bp">

(Then the Spirit of the Lord came upon Zechariah, the son of the High Priest Jehoiada; and he stood before the people, and said to them, The Lord saith these things, Why have ye broken the Lord’s commands? truly that shall not profit you; but because ye have abandoned the Lord, now he hath abandoned you.)</span>
</div>

(And King Joash forgot about the loyalty which Jehoiada, Zechariah’s father, had shown to him; and so he had Jehoiada’s son killed. <div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 22:8; LEVITICUS 26:37; LEVITICUS 26:8"> And certain(ly), when a full little number of men of Syria was come into Judah, the Lord betook in(to) their hands a multitude of Jews without number, for they had forsaken the Lord God of their fathers.
