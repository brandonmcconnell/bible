<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST KINGS 15:24; EPHESIANS 6:10; MATTHEW 1:8"> Forsooth Jehoshaphat, his son, reigned for him; and he had the mastery against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 15:8; 2ND CHRONICLES 11:5; 2ND CHRONICLES 11:11–12"> And he set numbers of knights in all the cities of Judah, that were (en)compassed with walls, (or And he put numbers of horsemen in all the cities of Judah, that were surrounded with walls), and he disposed strongholds in the land of Judah, and in the cities of Ephraim, which Asa, his father, had taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND KI 22:2; ISAIAH 41:10; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:22"> And the Lord was with Jehoshaphat, which went in the first ways of David, his father; he hoped not in Baalim, (And the Lord was with Jehoshaphat, who went in the early ways of his father Asa; he did not trust in, or follow, the Baalim,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST KINGS 12:28; LUKE 1:6; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:12"> but he hoped in the Lord God of David, his father, (or but he hoped in the Lord God of his father Asa), and he went in the commandments of God, and not after the sins of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 18:1; MATTHEW 6:33; PSALMS 127:1"> And the Lord confirmed the realm in his hand; and all Judah gave gifts to Jehoshaphat, and riches without number, and much glory was made to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HOSEA 14:9; 2ND CHRONICLES 15:17; 2ND CHRONICLES 20:33"> And when his heart had taken hardiness for the ways of the Lord, he took away also (the) high places and &#91;maumet&#93; woods from Judah. (And he took pride in following the ways of the Lord, and he also did away the hill shrines and the idol groves, or the sacred poles, in Judah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 35:3; NEHEMIAH 8:7–8; ECCLESIASTES 12:9–10"> And in the third year of his realm, he sent of his princes (or he sent his officials), Benhail, and Obadiah, and Zechariah, and Nethaneel, and Michaiah, that they should teach in the cities of Judah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZRA 7:1–6; 2ND CHRONICLES 19:8; MALACHI 2:7"> and with them he sent nine deacons, (or Levites), that is, Shemaiah, and Nethaniah, and Zebadiah, and Asahel, and Shemiramoth, and Jehonathan, and Adonijah, and Tobijah, and Tobadonijah, deacons (or all Levites); and with them Elishama and Jehoram, priests;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 5:46; ROMANS 3:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:40"> and they taught the people in Judah, and had the book of the law of the Lord; and they compassed all the cities of Judah, and taught all the people. (and they taught the people in Judah, and had The Book of the Law of the Lord; and they went all around the cities of Judah, and taught all the people.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 14:14; EXODUS 15:14–16; PROVERBS 16:7"> Therefore the dread of the Lord was made upon all the realms of (the) lands, that were (round) about Judah; and those durst not fight against Jehoshaphat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 26:8; 2ND CHRONICLES 9:14; 2ND SAMUEL 8:2"> But also &#91;the&#93; Philistines brought gifts to Jehoshaphat, and toll, or tribute, of silver; and men of Arabia brought to him sheep, seven thousand and seven hundred wethers, and so many bucks of goats (or and as many goat bucks).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 8:2–6; 2ND CHRONICLES 14:6–7; 2ND CHRONICLES 32:5"> Then Jehoshaphat increased, and was magnified till to on high; and he builded in Judah houses at the likeness of towers, and full strong cities;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 27:25–31; 2ND CHRONICLES 26:10–15"> and he made ready many works in the cities of Judah. Also men warriors and strong men were in Jerusalem; (and he had much work done in the cities of Judah. And there were men of war and strong men in Jerusalem;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 11:1; GENESIS 15:5; NUMBERS 1:18"> of which this is the number, by the houses and families of all (the) men (who were) in Judah. Duke Adnah was prince of the host (or Adnah was the leader of the army), and with him were three hundred thousand full strong men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And after him was Jehohanan (the) prince (or And second in command was Jehohanan), and with him were two hundred thousand and fourscore thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JUDGES 5:9; JUDGES 5:2; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:9"> After this also Amasiah, the son of Zichri, was hallowed to the Lord, and with him were two hundred thousand of strong men. (And after him was Amasiah, the son of Zichri, who was dedicated to the Lord, and with him were two hundred thousand strong men.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 14:8; 2ND SAMUEL 1:21–22"> (And of Benjamin); Eliada, a mighty man to battles, followed this Amasiah, and with him were two hundred thousand of men holding bow and shield. (And of Benjamin; Eliada, a mighty man of battles, and with him were two hundred thousand men holding bow and shield.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> After this was also Jehozabad, and with him were an hundred thousand and fourscore thousand of ready knights. (And after him was Jehozabad, and with him were a hundred and eighty thousand fully-armed horsemen.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 17:2; 2ND CHRONICLES 11:12; 2ND CHRONICLES 11:23"> All these were at the hand of the king, besides others, which he had put in walled cities in all Judah. (All of these were at the hand of the king, besides others, whom he had put in the fortified cities in all of Judah.)</span>
