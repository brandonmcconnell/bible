<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST KINGS 7:38; EXODUS 30:18–21; 1ST KINGS 7:40"> Also he made ten hollow vessels, and setted five at the right side, and five at the left side, that they should wash in those all things, which they should offer into burnt sacrifice; soothly the priests were washed in the sea. (And he made ten hollow vessels, and put five at the right side, and five at the left side, so that they could wash all the things in them that they would offer for the burnt sacrifice; but the priests washed in the Sea.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 25:31–40; 1ST KINGS 7:49; JOHN 8:12"> Soothly he made ten golden candlesticks by the likeness which he had commanded to be made, and he setted those in the temple, five at the right side, and five at the left side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST KINGS 7:48; MALACHI 1:12; EXODUS 25:23–30"> And he made also ten tables, and he setted those in the temple, five at the right side, and five at the left side. <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST KINGS 6:36; 2ND KI 21:5; 1ST KINGS 7:12"> Also he made a large place of (the) priests, and a great house, and &#91;the&#93; doors in the great house, which he covered with brass. (And he made a courtyard for the priests, and the great courtyard, and he covered the doors of both courtyards with bronze.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST KINGS 7:39"> And he setted the sea in the right side of the porch against the east at the south. (And he put the Sea on the right side of the courtyard toward the east at the south.)</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST KINGS 7:20; 1ST KINGS 7:42; JEREMIAH 52:23"> also he made forty pomegranates, and two works like nets, so that the two orders of pomegranates were joined to each work like nets, which covered the pommels, and the heads of the pillars. (and he made four hundred pomegranates on the two networks, with two rows of pomegranates on each network, that covered the bowl-shaped capitals, that were on the tops of the pillars.)</span>

Hiram, the father of Solomon, so-called because of his age, or because of the excellence of his craftsmanship, made all the vessels for Solomon for the House of the Lord out of burnished bronze.)</span>
