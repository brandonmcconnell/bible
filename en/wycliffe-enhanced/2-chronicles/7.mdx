<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 15:8; 2ND CHRONICLES 5:14; EXODUS 24:17"> And the priests might not enter into the temple of the Lord; for the mighty shining of the Lord had full-filled the temple of the Lord. <span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 5:13; 2ND CHRONICLES 20:21; LUKE 1:50"> But also all the sons of Israel saw fire coming down, and the glory of the Lord upon the house, and they felled down low to the earth, upon the pavement arrayed, or paved, with stone, and they worshipped, and praised the Lord, (and said), For he is good, for his mercy is into the world. (And all the Israelites saw the fire coming down, and the glory of the Lord on the House, and they fell down low to the ground, on the pavement paved with stone, and they worshipped, and praised the Lord, and said, For he is good, for his love is forevermore.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST KINGS 8:62–63"> And the king and all the people offered slain sacrifices before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOHN 10:22; EZEKIEL 45:17; 2ND CHRONICLES 30:24"> Therefore king Solomon killed sacrifices of oxen two and twenty thousand, of wethers sixscore thousand; and the king and all the people hallowed the house of God. (And so King Solomon and the people killed for a sacrifice twenty-two thousand oxen, and one hundred and twenty thousand rams; and the king and all the people dedicated the House of God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 5:12; 1ST CHRONICLES 15:16–21; 2ND CHRONICLES 7:3"> And the priests stood in their offices, and &#91;the&#93; deacons in organs of songs of the Lord, which king David made to praise the Lord (with), For his mercy is into the world; and they sang the hymns of David by their hands in organs and other instruments; and the priests sang with trumps before them, and all the people of Israel stood. (And the priests stood in their places, and the Levites with the musical instruments for the Lord, which King David had made to praise the Lord with, For his love is forevermore; and they sang the hymns of David with the instruments; and the priests blew their trumpets opposite them, and all the people of Israel stood.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST KINGS 8:64–66; HEBREWS 13:10–12; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:14"> Therefore Solomon hallowed the middle of the large place before the temple of the Lord; for he had offered there burnt sacrifices, and the inner fatnesses of peaceable sacrifices, for the brazen altar which he had made might not sustain, or hold, the burnt sacrifices, and slain sacrifices, and inner fatnesses of peaceable sacrifices. (And so Solomon consecrated the centre of the courtyard, in front of the Temple of the Lord; for he offered the burnt sacrifices, and the inner fatnesses of the peace offerings there, because the bronze altar which he had made could not hold all the burnt sacrifices, and the slain sacrifices, and the inner fatnesses of the peace offerings.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 15:18; 1ST KINGS 8:65; NEHEMIAH 8:13–18"> Therefore Solomon made a solemnity in that time in seven days, and all Israel with him, a full great church, or congregation, from the entering of Hamath unto the strand of Egypt. (And so Solomon made a feast at that time for seven days, and all Israel with him, the full great congregation, from the entrance to Hamath unto the River of Egypt, that is, unto the Nile.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:36; 1ST KINGS 8:65; JOEL 1:14"> And in the eighth day he made a gathering of money, that is, for necessaries of the temple, for he had hallowed the altar in seven days, and had made &#91;the&#93; solemnity in seven (more) days. (And on the eighth day they gathered money for the necessities of the Temple, for they had celebrated the dedication of the altar for seven days, and then had kept the feast for another seven days.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST KINGS 8:66; PSALMS 100:2; DEUTERONOMY 12:18"> Therefore in the three and twentieth day of the seventh month, he let the peoples go to their tabernacles (or he let the people go back to their tents, or their homes), joying and gladding upon the goodness that God had done to David, and to Solomon, and to his people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST KINGS 9:1–9; ECCLESIASTES 2:10–11; 2ND CHRONICLES 2:1"> And Solomon performed the house of the Lord, and the house of the king, and all things which he had disposed in his heart for to do in the house of the Lord, and in his own house; and he had prosperity. (And Solomon finished the House of the Lord, and the king’s house, and all the things that he had disposed in his heart to do for the House of the Lord, and for his own house; and he prospered.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 17:1; 1ST KINGS 9:2; 2ND CHRONICLES 7:16"> Forsooth the Lord appeared to him in the night, and said, I have heard thy prayer, and I have chosen this place to me into an house of sacrifice. (And the Lord appeared to him in the night, and said, I have heard thy prayer, and I have chosen this place to be a House of sacrifice to me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 6:26–28; DEUTERONOMY 11:17; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:6"> If I close heaven, and rain cometh not down, and if I send, and command to the locust, that he devour the land, and if I send pestilence into my people; (And if I close up the heavens, and rain cometh not down, or if I command to the locusts, that they devour the land, or if I send pestilence into my people;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:40–41; JAMES 4:9–10; ISAIAH 55:6–7"> forsooth if my people is converted, on which my name is called, and if it beseecheth me, and seeketh my face, and doeth penance of his full evil ways, then I shall hear from heaven, and I shall be merciful to the sins of them, and I shall heal the land of them. (and if my people, who be called by my name, be turned, and if they beseech me, and seek my face, and do penance for their full evil ways, then I shall hear from heaven, and I shall be merciful to their sins, and I shall heal their land.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:12; 2ND CHRONICLES 6:40; NEHEMIAH 1:6"> And mine eyes shall be opened, and mine ears shall be raised up to the prayer of him, that prayeth in this place (or who prayeth in this place);</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 7:15; 2ND CHRONICLES 7:12; 2ND CHRONICLES 6:5–6"> for I have chosen, and hallowed this place, that my name be there without end, and that mine eyes and mine heart dwell there in all days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 3:7; EZEKIEL 36:27; 1ST KINGS 11:38"> Also if thou goest before me, as David thy father went, and doest by all those things which I commanded to thee, and keepest my rightfulnesses and my dooms, (And if thou goest before me, as thy father David went, and doest by all those things which I commanded to thee, and obeyest my statutes and my judgements,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 6:16; JEREMIAH 33:20–21; 2ND SAMUEL 7:13–16"> I shall raise up the throne of thy realm, as I promised to David thy father, and said, A man of thy generation shall not be taken away, that shall be prince in Israel. (I shall raise up the throne of thy kingdom, as I promised to thy father David, and said, There shall always be a man of thy descendants, who shall be the leader of Israel.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:14; DEUTERONOMY 28:15; LEVITICUS 26:33–46"> But if ye turn away, and forsake my rightfulnesses and my commandments, which I have set forth to you, and ye go, and serve alien gods, and worship them, (But if ye turn away, and abandon my statutes and my commandments, which I have set forth to you, and ye go, and serve other gods, and worship them,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2ND KI 17:20; DEUTERONOMY 28:37; JEREMIAH 24:9"> I shall draw you away from my land, which I gave to you, and I shall cast away from my face this house which I have builded to my name, and I shall give it into a parable, and into ensample to all peoples. (I shall draw you away from my land, which I gave you, and this House, which I have consecrated in honour of my name, I shall throw it away from my face, and I shall make it into a parable, and into an example, for all the peoples.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:8–9; JEREMIAH 16:10–12; JEREMIAH 19:8"> And this house shall be into a proverb to all men passing forth; and they shall say, wondering in themselves, Why did the Lord so to this land, and to this house? (And this House shall be like a proverb for all people passing forth; and they shall say, wondering to themselves, Why hath the Lord done so to this land, and to this House?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DANIEL 9:12; LAMENTATIONS 2:16–17; EZEKIEL 14:23"> And they shall answer, For they forsook the Lord God of their fathers, that led them out of the land of Egypt, and they took (hold of) alien gods, and worshipped &#91;them&#93;, and praised them; therefore all these evils came upon them. (And they shall answer to them, For they deserted the Lord God of their fathers, who led them out of the land of Egypt, and they clung to foreign, or other, gods, and worshipped them, and praised them; and so all these evils came upon them.)</span>
