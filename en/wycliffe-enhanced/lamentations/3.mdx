<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 88:7; JOB 19:21; PSALMS 88:15–16"> I am a man seeing my poverty in the rod of his indignation. (I am a man who seeth my poverty, I have felt the rod of his indignation, or of his anger.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOB 30:26; ISAIAH 59:9; AMOS 5:18–20"> He drove me, and brought (me) into darknesses, and not into light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:20; JOB 31:21; ISAIAH 1:25"> Only he turned into me, and turned together his hand all day. (He turned only against me, and he turned his hand against me all day long.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 38:13; PSALMS 51:8; PSALMS 31:9–10"> He made eld my skin, and my flesh; he all-brake my bones. (He made old, or wasted, my skin, and my flesh; he broke all my bones.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:19; JEREMIAH 23:15; JOB 19:8"> He builded in my compass, and he compassed me with gall and travail. (He built all around me, yea, he surrounded me with gall and tribulation.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 88:5–6; PSALMS 143:3; PSALMS 143:7"> He setted me in dark places, as everlasting dead men. (He put me in dark places, like the everlasting dead.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOB 19:8; JOB 3:23; JEREMIAH 40:4"> He builded about against me, (so) that I go not out; he aggrieved my gyves (or he made my chains heavy).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 30:20; PSALMS 22:2; JOB 19:7"> But and when I cry and pray, he hath excluded my prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 63:17; LAMENTATIONS 3:11; ISAIAH 30:28"> He closed together my ways with square stones; he destroyed my paths. (He altogether enclosed my ways with square stones; he destroyed my paths.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 10:9; AMOS 5:18–20; HOSEA 13:7–8"> He is made (like) a bear setting ambush to me, (like) a lion in hid places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HOSEA 6:1; JOB 16:12–13; LAMENTATIONS 1:13"> He destroyed my paths, and brake me; he setted me desolate (or he left me desolate).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 38:2; JOB 7:20; JOB 6:4"> He bent his bow, and setted me as a sign to an arrow. (He bent his bow, and made me a target for his arrows.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOB 6:4; JOB 41:28; DEUTERONOMY 32:23"> He sent in(to) my reins the daughters of his arrow case.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 20:7; LAMENTATIONS 3:63; PSALMS 22:6–7"> I am made into scorn to all the people (or I am mocked by everyone), the song of them all day (long).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:15; JEREMIAH 23:15; RUTH 1:20"> He filled me with bitternesses; he greatly filled me with wormwood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:17; JEREMIAH 6:26; PSALMS 3:7"> He brake at number my teeth (or He broke my teeth on gravel); he fed me with ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 59:11; ZECHARIAH 8:10; JEREMIAH 8:15"> And my soul is put away; I have forgotten goods. (And I have given up; I have forgotten the good things or I have forgotten whatever was good.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOB 17:15; 1ST SAMUEL 27:1; PSALMS 116:11"> And I said, Mine end perished, and mine hope, from the Lord. (And I said, My strength, and my hope in the Lord, have all perished.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:15; LAMENTATIONS 3:5; PSALMS 89:50"> Have thou mind on my poverty, and going over, and on wormwood and gall. (Remember my poverty, and my goings about, yea, the wormwood and the gall.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 42:11; PSALMS 42:5–6; JOB 21:6"> By mind I shall be mindful; and my soul shall fail in me. (Remember, O remember; even though my soul shall fail in me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:3; PSALMS 130:7; PSALMS 77:7–11"> I bethink these things in mine heart, I shall hope in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 86:15; LUKE 1:50; NEHEMIAH 9:31"> The mercies of the Lord be many, for we be not wasted; for why his merciful doings failed not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:23; PSALMS 30:5; ISAIAH 33:2"> I knew in the morrowtide; thy faith is much &#91;or much is thy faith&#93;. (They be new in the morning; great is thy faithfulness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 73:26; PSALMS 16:5; LAMENTATIONS 3:21"> My soul said, The Lord is my part; therefore I shall abide him. (My soul said, The Lord is my portion; and so I shall wait for him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MICAH 7:7–8; ISAIAH 40:31; PSALMS 130:5–6"> The Lord is good to them that hope into him, to a soul seeking him. (The Lord is good to those who hope in him, to someone seeking him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 37:7; PSALMS 37:34; PSALMS 130:5"> It is good to abide with stillness the health of God. (It is good to silently wait for the salvation of God./It is good to patiently wait for the deliverance of God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 12:1; MATTHEW 11:29–30; PSALMS 119:71"> It is good to a man (or It is good for a person), when he hath borne the yoke of his youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JEREMIAH 15:17; LAMENTATIONS 2:10; PSALMS 102:7"> He shall sit alone, and he shall be still; for he raised himself above himself. (He shall sit alone, and he shall be silent or and he shall be patient; and he shall carry it by himself.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOB 40:4; LUKE 15:18–19; ROMANS 3:19"> He shall set his mouth in (the) dust, if peradventure hope is. (He shall put his face upon the ground, for perhaps there may still be hope.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOB 16:10; ISAIAH 50:6; MATTHEW 26:67"> He shall give the cheek to a man that smiteth him; he shall be filled with shames. (He shall give his cheek to the one who striketh him; he shall be laden with abuse.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 94:14; PSALMS 103:8–10; PSALMS 77:7"> For the Lord shall not put away &#91;into&#93; without end. (For the Lord shall not cast us away forever.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 78:38; PSALMS 30:5; PSALMS 103:11"> For if he casted away, and he shall do mercy after the multitude of his mercies. (For even if he hath cast us away, he shall still do mercy after the multitude of his mercies.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:11; EZEKIEL 18:32; HEBREWS 12:9–10"> For he maked not low of his heart; and casted not away the sons of men. (For he did not make his heart low; and did not cast away the sons and daughters of men forever.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 9:11–12; ISAIAH 51:22–23; PSALMS 102:20"> That he should all-foul under his feet all the bound men of &#91;the&#93; earth. (So that he would all-foul all the bound, or all the imprisoned, on earth, under his feet.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> That he should bow down the doom of (a) man, in the sight of the cheer of the Highest. (So that he would deny someone justice, before the very face of the Most High.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="ISAIAH 59:15; HABAKKUK 1:13; JEREMIAH 22:3"> That he should pervert a man in his doom, the Lord knew not. (So that he would pervert a person in his judgement; no, the Lord desireth none of this.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="PSALMS 33:9–11; ISAIAH 46:10; PROVERBS 16:9"> Who is this that said (or Who is this who said), that a thing should be done, when the Lord commandeth (it) not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="ISAIAH 45:7; AMOS 3:6; JEREMIAH 32:42"> Neither goods neither evils shall go out of the mouth of the Highest. (Do not both good and evil go out from the mouth of the Most High?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:3; MICAH 7:9; GENESIS 4:5–7"> What grutched a man living, a man (punished) for his sins? (Why grumble a living man, when he is punished for his sins?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 13:5; PSALMS 139:23–24; JOEL 2:12–13"> Search we our ways, and seek we, and turn we again to the Lord. (Consider all that we do, and seek we the truth about it, and then turn we again to the Lord.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="PSALMS 25:1; PSALMS 28:2; PSALMS 86:4"> Raise we our hearts with hands, to the Lord into heavens. (Raise we up our hearts with our hands, to the Lord in heaven.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="LUKE 15:18–19; NEHEMIAH 9:26; 2ND KI 24:4"> We have done wickedly, and have stirred thee to wrath; therefore thou art not able to be prayed (to).</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 2:17; LAMENTATIONS 2:21; LAMENTATIONS 3:66"> Thou coveredest in strong vengeance, and smitedest us (or and hast struck us); thou killedest (us), and sparedest (us) not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:8; ZECHARIAH 7:13; PSALMS 97:2"> Thou settedest a cloud to thee, that prayer pass not. (Thou hast set a cloud before thee, that prayers cannot pass through.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 4:13; LAMENTATIONS 2:15; LAMENTATIONS 3:14"> Thou settedest me, drawing up by the root, and casting out, in the midst of &#91;the&#93; peoples. (Thou hast made us, as if drawn up by the root, and cast out, in the midst of the peoples.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="PSALMS 22:6–8; LAMENTATIONS 2:16; PSALMS 44:13–14"> All &#91;the&#93; enemies opened their mouth on us. (All the enemies have opened their mouths against us.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="ISAIAH 24:17–18; ISAIAH 51:19; JEREMIAH 48:43–44"> Inward dread and snare is made to us, prophecy and defouling. (Inward fear and snare be made upon us, and prophecy and defiling.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 2:11; JEREMIAH 9:18; JEREMIAH 9:1"> Mine eyes led down partings of waters, for the defouling of the daughter of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="JEREMIAH 14:17; PSALMS 77:2; LAMENTATIONS 1:16"> Mine eye was tormented, and was not still; for no rest was. (My eyes were tormented, and were not still; for there was no rest,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="ISAIAH 63:15; LAMENTATIONS 5:1; DANIEL 9:16–19"> Until the Lord beheld, and saw from heavens. (until the Lord beheld, and saw from heaven.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="JEREMIAH 19:9; LUKE 19:41–44; LAMENTATIONS 1:18"> Mine eye robbed my soul in all the daughters of my city. (My heart is grieved at what befell all the daughters of my city.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="PSALMS 35:19; PSALMS 35:7; JEREMIAH 37:15–16"> Mine enemies took me without cause, by hunting (me) as a bird. (My enemies had no reason to be against me, yet they hunted me down like a bird.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="DANIEL 6:17; JEREMIAH 38:9; JEREMIAH 38:6"> My life slid into a pit; and they putted a stone on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="JONAH 2:3–5; EZEKIEL 37:11; LAMENTATIONS 3:18"> Waters flowed over mine head; I said, I perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:24–28; PSALMS 142:3–7; JONAH 2:2–4"> Lord, I called to help thy name, from the last pit. (Lord, I called thy name for help, from the deepest pit or from the bottom of the pit.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="ISAIAH 38:5; JOB 34:28; PSALMS 6:8–9"> Thou heardest my voice; turn thou not away thine ear from my sobbing and cries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="JAMES 4:8; ISAIAH 41:10; ISAIAH 58:9"> Thou nighedest to me in the day, wherein I called thee to help; thou saidest, Dread thou not. (Thou came to me on the day, when I called to thee for help; thou saidest, Fear thou not.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="PSALMS 34:22–1; JEREMIAH 51:36; PSALMS 103:4"> Lord, again-buyer of my life, thou deemedest the cause of my soul. (Lord, the Redeemer of my life, thou hast pleaded the cause of my soul or thou hast pleaded my soul’s case.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="PSALMS 43:1; PSALMS 9:4; GENESIS 31:42"> Lord, thou sawest the wickedness of them against me; deem thou my doom (or judge thou my judgement or judge thou my justice).</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="JEREMIAH 11:19–20; PSALMS 10:14; LAMENTATIONS 3:59"> Thou sawest all the strong vengeance, all the thoughts of them against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 5:1; PSALMS 89:50; ZEPHANIAH 2:8"> Lord, thou heardest the shames of them, (or Lord, thou heardest their abuse); all the thoughts of them against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:3; PSALMS 59:7; PSALMS 140:3"> The lips of men rising against me, and the thoughts of them against me all day (long).</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="PSALMS 139:2; LAMENTATIONS 3:14; JOB 30:9"> See thou the sitting and (the) rising again of them; I am the psalm of them (or I am their song of mocking).</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="PSALMS 28:4; JEREMIAH 11:20; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:14"> Lord, thou shalt yield while to them, by the works of their hands. (Lord, thou shalt punish them, according to the works of their hands.)</span>
