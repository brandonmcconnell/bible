<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:11; EZEKIEL 35:3; JUDGES 5:6–7"> And I shall give the hill of Seir desolate and forsaken (or And I shall make the hill country of Seir a desolate wilderness), and I shall take away from it a goer and a comer-again;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 31:12; EZEKIEL 32:4–5; EZEKIEL 39:4–5"> and I shall fill the hills thereof with the carrions of their slain men. People killed by the sword shall fall down in thy little hills, and in thy valleys, and by thy streams.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 25:13; MALACHI 1:3–4; EZEKIEL 6:7"> I shall give thee into everlasting wildernesses, and thy cities shall not be inhabited, (or I shall make thee into everlasting wildernesses, and thy cities shall not be inhabited); and ye shall know, that I am the Lord God.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 9:16; MATTHEW 7:2; PSALMS 73:17–18"> therefore I live, saith the Lord God, for I shall do by thy wrath, and by thine envy, which thou didest, hating them (my people), and I shall be made known by them, when I shall deem thee; (and so as I live, saith the Lord God, I shall do by thy anger, and by thy envy, which thou didest, in hating my people, and I shall be known by them, when I shall judge thee;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:2; PSALMS 83:12; EZEKIEL 6:7"> and (then) thou shalt know, that I am the Lord. (Like thou haddest joy over the inheritance of the house of Israel, for it was destroyed, so I shall do to thee; the hill country of Seir shall be destroyed, and all of Edom; and they shall know, that I am the Lord.)</span>
