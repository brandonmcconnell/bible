<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 10:4; EZEKIEL 11:22–23; EZEKIEL 43:2–4"> And the glory of the Lord of Israel was taken up from cherub&#91;im&#93;, which glory was on it, to the threshold of the house (of the Lord); and the Lord called the man that was clothed with linen clothes, and had a penner of a writer in his loins. (Then the glory of the Lord of Israel was taken up from above the cherubim, which glory was upon them, to the threshold of the House of the Lord; andthe Lord called to the man who was clothed in linen clothes, and had a writer’s inkhorn at his side.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 9:4; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:22; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:1"> And the Lord said to him, Pass thou by the midst of the city, in the midst of Jerusalem, and mark thou Tau on the foreheads of men wailing and sorrowing on all &#91;the&#93; abominations that be done in the midst thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 5:11; EZEKIEL 8:18; EZEKIEL 7:9"> And he said to them in mine hearing, Go ye through the city, and follow ye him, and smite ye; your eye spare not, neither do ye mercy. (And he said to the others in my hearing, Go ye through the city, and follow ye him, and strike ye them down; do not let your eye spare them, nor have ye any mercy on them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 9:4; EXODUS 12:23; JEREMIAH 25:29"> Slay ye till to death, an eld man, a young man, and a virgin, a little child, and women; but slay ye not any man, on whom ye see Tau; and begin ye at my saintuary. Therefore they began at the elder men, that were before the face of the house, (or And so they began with the elders, who were in front of the House of the Lord).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 36:17; EZEKIEL 7:20–22; LAMENTATIONS 2:4–7"> And he said to them, Defoul ye the house (of the Lord), and fill ye the foreyards with slain men; go ye out. And they went out, and killed them that were in the city. (And he said to them, Defile ye the House of the Lord, and fill ye the courtyards with the slain; go ye out. And they went out, and killed those who were in the city.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 11:13; NUMBERS 16:45; JOSHUA 7:6"> And lo! when the slaying was &#91;ful&#93; filled, I was left (alone). (And behold! when the killing was finished, I was left alone. And I fell down on my face, and I cried, and said, Alas! alas! alas! Lord God, and so shalt thou destroy all the remnants of Israel, and shalt thou pour out thy strong vengeance upon Jerusalem?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOB 22:13; EZEKIEL 8:12; EZEKIEL 7:23"> And he said to me, The wickedness of the house of Israel and of Judah is full great, and the land is filled of bloods, and the city is filled with turning away; for they said, The Lord hath forsaken the land, and the Lord seeth not. (And he said to me, The wickedness of the house of Israel and Judah is very great, and the land is filled with bloodshed, or of murder, and the city is filled with turning away from righteousness; for they said, The Lord hath abandoned the land, and the Lord seeth not.)</span>
