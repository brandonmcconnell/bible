<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:10; PHILIPPIANS 1:27; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:12"> Therefore I bound for the Lord beseech you, that ye walk worthily in the calling, in which ye be called,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:12–13; 1ST CORINTHIANS 13:7; 1ST CORINTHIANS 13:4–5"> with all meekness and mildness, with patience supporting each other in charity, (in all humbleness and humility, with forbearance supporting each other in love,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:10; COLOSSIANS 3:13–15; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:12–13"> busy to keep (the) unity of (the) Spirit in the bond of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:18; ROMANS 12:4–5; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:4–13"> One body and one Spirit, as ye be called in one hope of your calling;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 8:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:13; 1ST PETER 3:21"> one Lord, one faith, one baptism,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ROMANS 11:36; MALACHI 2:10; 1ST CORINTHIANS 8:6"> one God and Father of all, which is above all men, and by all things, and in us all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:10; ROMANS 12:3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:7–11"> But to each of us grace is given by &#91;or after&#93; the measure of the giving of Christ; (But each of us is given grace according to the measure of the giving of the Messiah;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 68:18; COLOSSIANS 2:15; 1ST SAMUEL 30:26"> for which thing he saith, He ascending on high, led captivity captive, he gave gifts to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 3:13; MATTHEW 12:40; PROVERBS 30:4"> But what is it, that he ascended up, no but that also he came down first into the lower parts of the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HEBREWS 7:26; HEBREWS 4:14; EPHESIANS 1:20–23"> He it is that came down, and that ascended &#91;up&#93; on (or above) all (the) heavens, (so) that he should fill all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 3:15; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:28–29; EPHESIANS 2:20"> And he gave some apostles (or And he made some of us apostles), some prophets, others evangelists, others shepherds and teachers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:11–14; EPHESIANS 4:16; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:27"> to the full ending of (the) saints, into the work of (the) ministry, into &#91;the&#93; edification of Christ’s body, (for the perfection or the completion of God’s people, for the work of the ministry, for the instruction of the Messiah’s body,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:28; JOHN 17:21; EPHESIANS 4:5"> till we run all, into (the) unity of (our) faith and of (our) knowing of God’s Son, &#91;or till we run all, in unity of faith and of knowing of God’s Son&#93;, into a perfect man, after the measure of the age of the plenty of Christ (or according to the measure of the stature of the fullness of the Messiah);</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:9; ROMANS 16:17–18; 1ST CORINTHIANS 14:20"> (so) that we be not now little children, moving as (the) waves, and be not borne about with each wind of teaching, in the waywardness of men, in subtle wit, to the deceiving of error.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST JOHN 3:18; EPHESIANS 4:25; ZECHARIAH 8:16"> But do we (the) truth in charity, and wax in him by all things, that is Christ our head; (But speak we the truth in love, and grow in him in every way, who is the Messiah our head;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 2:19; JOHN 15:5; COLOSSIANS 2:2"> of whom all the body set together, and bound together by each jointure of under-serving, by (the) working into the measure of each member, maketh increasing of the body, into &#91;the&#93; edification of itself in charity (or in love).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ROMANS 1:21; 1ST PETER 4:3–4; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:1–2"> Therefore I say and witness (to) this thing in the Lord, that ye walk not now, as heathen men walk, in the vanity of their wit; (And so I say and testify to this in the Lord, that ye walk not now, like the Gentiles walk, in the emptiness and uselessness of their reasoning or of their thinking;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:15; EPHESIANS 2:12; ROMANS 1:21–23"> that have understanding darkened with darknesses (or who have their understanding darkened with darkness), and be aliened, (or alienated), &#91;or made far&#93;, from the life of God, by (the) ignorance that is in them, for the blindness of their heart(s).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:5; ROMANS 1:24–26; 1ST TIMOTHY 4:2"> Which despairing betook (or delivered) themselves to unchastity, into the working of all uncleanness in covetousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TITUS 2:11–14; JOHN 6:45; MATTHEW 11:29"> But ye have not so learned Christ, (But ye have not so learned the Messiah,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST JOHN 5:20; EPHESIANS 1:13; HEBREWS 3:7–8"> if nevertheless ye heard him, and be taught in him, as is (the) truth in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ROMANS 6:6; HEBREWS 12:1; JAMES 1:21"> Do ye away by the old living, &#91;or after the first living&#93;, the old man, that is corrupt by the desires of error;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ROMANS 12:2; ROMANS 8:6; PSALMS 51:10"> and be ye renewed &#91;or made new again&#93; in the spirit of your soul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:10–14; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:17; EPHESIANS 2:10"> and clothe ye the new man, which is made after God in rightwiseness and (in the) holiness of truth. &#91;and clothe ye the new man, which after God is made of nought (or out of nothing) in rightwiseness and holiness of truth.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:9; ZECHARIAH 8:16; PROVERBS 12:22"> For which thing put ye away leasing (or lying), and speak ye (the) truth each man with his neighbour, for we be members each to (the) other, &#91;or together&#93;, (or for we be members with one another of one body).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JAMES 1:19; PSALMS 4:4; PSALMS 37:8"> Be ye wroth, and do not do sin &#91;or and do not ye sin&#93;; the sun fall not down on your wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JAMES 4:7; 1ST PETER 5:8; EPHESIANS 6:16"> Do not ye give stead (or a place) to the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 6:18; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:11–12; GALATIANS 6:10"> He that stole, now steal he not; but more rather travail he in working with his hands that that is good, (so) that he have whereof he shall give to the needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 4:6; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:11; ECCLESIASTES 10:12"> Each evil word go not out of your mouth; but if any (word) is good to the edification of (the) faith, (so) that it give grace to men that hear (it).</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:19; ISAIAH 63:10; EPHESIANS 1:13–14"> And do not ye make the Holy Ghost of God sorry, &#91;or heavy&#93;, in which ye be marked in the day of redemption. (And do not make the Holy Spirit of God sorrowful, in whom, and by whom, ye be sealed unto the Day of Redemption.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:8; ECCLESIASTES 7:9; TITUS 3:2–3"> All bitterness, and wrath, and indignation, and cry, and blasphemy be taken away from you, with all malice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:12–13; MATTHEW 6:14–15; MARK 11:25"> and be ye together benign, &#91;or of good will&#93;, merciful, forgiving together, as also God forgave to you in Christ. (and be benign, or have good will, with one another, merciful, and forgiving each other, as also God forgave you in the Messiah.)</span>
