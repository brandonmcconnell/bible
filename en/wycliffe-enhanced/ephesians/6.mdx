<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 20:12; DEUTERONOMY 27:16; MATTHEW 15:4–6"> Honour thou thy father and mother, that is the first commandment in &#91;the&#93; promise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:16; DEUTERONOMY 4:40; DEUTERONOMY 12:28"> (so) that it be well to thee, and that thou be long living on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:21; PROVERBS 22:6; PROVERBS 29:15"> And, fathers, do not ye provoke your sons to wrath; but nourish ye them in the teaching and (the) chastising of the Lord &#91;or but nourish ye them in the discipline and (the) correction of the Lord&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:18–21; TITUS 2:9–10; COLOSSIANS 3:17–24"> Servants, obey ye to fleshly lords with dread and trembling, in simpleness of your heart, as to Christ; (Servants, obey your human lords with fearful reverence and trembling, and with sincerity in your hearts, as unto the Messiah;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:22–23; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:4; GALATIANS 1:10"> not serving at the eye, as pleasing to men, but as servants of Christ (or but as the Messiah’s servants); doing the will of God by discretion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:23; EPHESIANS 6:5–6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:31"> with good will serving as to the Lord, and not as to men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:24; LUKE 14:14; PROVERBS 11:18"> witting (or knowing) that each man, whatever good thing he shall do, he shall receive this of the Lord, whether servant, whether free man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:25–1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:34; ROMANS 2:11"> And, ye lords, do the same things to them, forgiving menacings; witting that both their Lord and yours is in heavens, and the taking of persons is not with God. (And, ye lords, do the same thing for them, forgiving threats; knowing that both their Lord and yours is in heaven, and that the favouring of persons is not done by God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:13; ISAIAH 40:31; JOSHUA 1:9"> Here afterward, brethren, be ye comforted in the Lord, and in the might of his virtue. (Here afterward or Henceforth, brothers, be strengthened in the Lord, and in the power of his might.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 10:4; ROMANS 13:12; EPHESIANS 6:13"> Clothe you(rselves) with the armour of God, that ye may stand against the ambushings, &#91;or (the) assailings&#93;, of the devil. (Clothe yourselves with the armour of God, so that ye can stand against the Devil’s assaults.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:13; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:4; EPHESIANS 2:2"> For why striving &#91;or battle&#93; is not to us against flesh and blood, but against &#91;the&#93; princes and (the) potentates, against (the) governors of the world of these darknesses, (or against the rulers of the darkness in this world, or the rulers of this dark world), against spiritual things of wickedness, in heavenly things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 10:4; LUKE 21:36; EPHESIANS 6:11–17"> Therefore take ye the armour of God, that ye may against-stand in the evil day, (or And so take or put on God’s armour, so that ye can withstand, or stand against,the Devil on the Day of Evil); and in all things stand perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 59:17; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:8; ISAIAH 11:5"> Therefore stand ye, and be girded about your loins in soothfastness, and clothed with the habergeon of rightwiseness, (And so stand, with your loins girded in truthfulness, or with truth, and clothed with the breastplate of righteousness,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 52:7; ROMANS 10:15; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:18–21"> and your feet shod in (the) making ready of the gospel of peace. (and your feet shod in the preparation of the Gospel, or the Good News, of peace.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:8–9; 1ST JOHN 5:4–5; PROVERBS 18:10"> In all things take ye the shield of faith, in which ye may quench all the fiery darts of him that is(the) most wicked or (of him who is) the worst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:12; ISAIAH 59:17; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:15"> And take ye the helmet of health, and the sword of the Ghost, that is, the word of God. (And take or put on the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Holy Spirit, that is, the word of God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:17; COLOSSIANS 4:2; PHILIPPIANS 4:6"> By all prayer and beseeching pray ye all time in (the) Spirit, and in him waking in all busyness, and beseeching for all holy men &#91;or for all (the) saints&#93;. (By all prayer and beseeching pray all the time in the Spirit, and in him keeping watch with all diligence, and beseeching for all of God’s people.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 4:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:29; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:1"> and for me; that word be given to me in (the) opening of my mouth, with trust to make known the mystery of the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 5:20; COLOSSIANS 4:4; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:9"> for which I am set in message in a chain (or for which I am an ambassador in a chain); so that in it I be hardy (or I be bold) to speak, as it behooveth me &#91;to speak out&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:4; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:12; TITUS 3:12"> And (so that) ye know, what things be about me, (and) what I do, Tychicus, my most dear brother, and true minister in the Lord (or and a true servant in the Lord), shall make all things known to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:25; COLOSSIANS 4:7–8; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:17"> whom I sent to you for this same thing, (so) that ye know what things be about us, and that he comfort your hearts (or and so that he strengthen your hearts).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:16; GALATIANS 5:6; 1ST PETER 5:14"> Peace to brethren, and charity, (or Peace be to the brothers, and love), with (the) faith of God our Father, and of the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 22:37; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:12; MATTHEW 6:13"> Grace (be) with all men that love our Lord Jesus Christ in uncorruption. Amen, that is, So be it.</span>
