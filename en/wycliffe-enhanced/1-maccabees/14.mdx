<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> In the hundred and two and seventy &#91;or seventieth&#93; year, king Demetrius gathered his host, and went to Media, for to draw together helps to him(self), for to overcome Tryphon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And as Arsaces, king of Persia and Media, heard that Demetrius entered into his nigh coasts, he sent one of his princes, for to take him quick, and that he should bring him to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And he went, and smote the host of Demetrius, and took him, and led him to Arsaces, and he putted him into keeping (or into prison).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And the land of Judea was still, either peaceable, in all the days of Simon, and he sought good things of (or for) his folk; and his power and his glory pleased them in all days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And with all his glory he took Joppa into haven, and made entry into (the) isles of the sea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and alarged (or enlarged) the coasts of his people, and wielded the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And he gathered much captivity, and was lord in Gazara, and Beth-sura, and the high tower; and he did away (the) uncleannesses of it, and there was not that against-stood him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And each man tilled his own land in peace, and the land of Judea gave his (or its) fruits, and (the) trees of (the) fields their fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> (The) Elder men sat all in (the) streets, and treated of (the) goods of the land; and young men clothed them-(selves) in glory, and stoles of battle, that is armours, in the time of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And to the cities he gave foods, and ordained those &#91;or them&#93;, that those &#91;or they&#93; were vessels of strengthening, till that the name of his glory was named till to the last of (the) earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> He made peace on the land, and Israel was glad with great gladness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and each man sat under his vine, and under his fig tree, neither there was that feared them (or made them afraid).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The fighting man against them failed on (the) earth; (the) kings were all-broken in those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And he confirmed all meek men of his people, and he sought out the law, and did away all evil and wickedness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and he glorified holy things, and multiplied vessels of holy things. (and he beautified the Temple, and multi-plied the vessels of the Temple.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And it was heard at Rome, that Jonathan was dead, and till into (the) Spartans, and they were full sorrowful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Forsooth as they heard, that Simon, his brother, was made highest priest in his stead, and he wielded the country, and (the) cities in it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> they wrote to him in brazen tables (or on brass or bronze tablets), for to renew (the) friendship, and fellowship, that they made with Judas and Jonathan, his brethren;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and they were read in the sight of the church (or the congregation) in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And this is the ensample (or a copy) of (the) epistles, that (the) Spartans (or Lacedaemonians) sent. The prince and the cities, that is, (the) governors of the cities, of (the) Spartans to Simon, (the) great priest, and to the elder men, and priests, and to (the) other people of (the) Jews, brethren, health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> (The) Legates that were sent to our people, told to us of your glory, and honour, and gladness, and we joyed in (or at) the entry of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And we have written what things were said of them in (the) councils &#91;or in (the) council&#93; of (the) people, thus. Numenius, the son of Antiochus, and Antipater, the son of Jason, (the) legates of (the) Jews, came to us, and renewed with us the former friendship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And it pleased to the people, for to receive the men gloriously, and to put (an) ensample of their words in departed books of the people (or and to put a copy of their address in the public records), that it be to mind to the people of (the) Spartans (or so that it be remembered by the Lacedaemon-ians); forsooth we have written (an) ensample (or a copy) of these things to Simon, the great priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Forsooth after these things, Simon sent Numenius to Rome, having a great golden shield, in (the) weight of a thousand bezants, for to ordain fellowship with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Soothly when the people of Rome heard these words, they said, What doing of thankings shall we yield to Simon, and his sons?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> For he restored his brethren, and overcame the enemies of Israel from them. And they ordained to him liberty &#91;or they ordained to them (their) liberty&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and wrote (it) in brazen tables (or on brass or bronze tablets), and (the)Jews putted in titles (or on pillars), in the mount of Zion. And this is (an) en-sample (or a copy) of (the) writing. In the eighteenth day of the month Elul, that is, August, in the hundred and two and seventy &#91;or seventieth&#93; year, the third year under Simon, (the) great priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> in Saramel, in the great coming together of (the) priests, (and) of the people, and (the) princes, and (the) folk, and the elder men &#91;or seniors&#93; of the country, these things were made known;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> for many times battles were done &#91;or be made&#93; in your country. Forsooth Simon, the son of Mattathias, of the sons of Jarib, and his brethren, gave themselves to peril, and against-stood (the) adversaries of their folk, (so) that their holy things (or their Temple) and (the) law should stand; and by great glory they glorified their folk (or they brought great glory to their people).</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Jonathan gathered his folk (together), and was made to them a great priest, and is put to his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the enemies of them would defoul holy things (or defile the Temple), and destroy the country of them, and stretch forth hands into holy things of them (or upon their Temple).</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Then Simon against-stood, and fought for his people, and gave many riches &#91;or much money&#93;, and armed (the) men of virtue of his folk, and gave to them solds (or wages);</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and strengthened the cities of Judea, and Bethsura, that was in the ends of Judea, where before were (the) armours of (the) enemies, and he put-ted there (a) help, &#91;or (a) strength&#93;, (or a stronghold), (with) men of (the) Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And he strengthened Joppa, that was at the sea, and Gazara, that was in the coasts of Azotus, in which (the) enemies (had) dwelt before; and he setted there Jews, and whatever things were able to (the) amending of them, he putted in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And the people saw the doing of Simon, and (the) glory that he thought for to do to his folk, and they made him their duke, and prince of priests, for that he had done all these things, and rightwiseness, and faith that he kept to his folk; and he sought out in all manner for to raise (up) his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And in his days it had prosperity in his hands, (so) that heathen men were taken away from the country of them, (and) which were (also) in the city of David in Jerusalem, in the high tower, from which they came out, and defouled all things that were in compass of holy things (or around the Temple), and gave great wound to (its) chastity (or its purity).</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And he setted therein men Jews, to defending of the country, and (the) city, and raised (up) the walls in (or of) Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And king Demetrius ordained to him the highest priesthood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> by this he made him his friend, and glorified him in great glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> For he heard, that (the) Jews were called of (or by the) Romans (their) friends, and fellows, and brethren, and that they received (the) legates (or the ambassadors) of Simon gloriously;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> and that (the) Jews, and (the) priests of them, consented, him for to be their duke, and highest priest &#91;into&#93; without end, till there rise a faithful prophet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> and that he be duke on them, and care, or busyness, were to him for (the) holy things; and that he should ordain governors &#91;or provosts&#93; on the works of them, and on the country, and on arms, and on strengths (or strongholds); and care be to him of (the) holy things (or the Temple);</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> and that he be heard of (or obey-ed by all) men, and all (the) writings in the country be written together under the name of him, and that he be covered with purple and gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and that it be not leaveful to (or lawful for) any of the people, and to priests, for to make anything of these void, and against-say (or gainsay) to these things that be said of him, either for to call together (a) covent, or convent, (or an assembly) in the country without him; and for to be clothed in purple, and for to use a golden lace, or (an) ouch (or clasp).</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> Soothly he that shall do without this, either &#91;or&#93; shall make void any of these (things), shall be guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And (so) it pleased (al)together to all the people, for to ordain Simon, and do by &#91;or after&#93; these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> And Simon received (this), and it pleased him, that he should use &#91;or that he was set in&#93;(the) high priesthood or (the) highest priesthood, and be duke and prince of the folk of (the) Jews, and priests, and be the sovereign of all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And they ordained for to put this writing in brazen tables (or on brass tablets), and put them in the wall about the compassing &#91;or the walking place&#93; of (the) holy things (or the Temple precincts), in (a) solemn place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> forsooth for to put (an) ensample (or copy) of these in the treasury, (so) that Simon have (them) and his sons.</span>
