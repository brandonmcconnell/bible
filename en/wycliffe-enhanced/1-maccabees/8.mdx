<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Judas (had) heard the name (or the fame) of (the) Romans, that they be mighty in strengths, and accord to all things that be asked of them; and whoever went to them, they ordained with them friendships;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and that they be mighty in strengths. And they heard (of the) battles of them, and good virtues, that they did in Galatia, for they wielded them, and led them under tribute;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and how many things they did in the country of Spain, and that they brought into power metals of silver and gold that be there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and (that) they wielded each place with their counsel, and (with) patience, or wisdom, places that were full far from them; and they all-brake kings that came on them from the utmost places of (the) earth, and they smited them with (a) great wound; forsooth others give to them tribute by all years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And they all-brake in battle Philip, and Perseus, kings &#91;or king&#93; of Kittim, and others that bare arms (or weapons) against them, and wielded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And they wielded Antiochus, the great king of Asia, that gave battle to them, and had an hundred and twenty elephants, and multitude of horsemen, and chariots, and full great host all-broken of (or by) them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and that they took him quick (or captured him alive), and ordained to him, that he should give great tribute, and they that reigned after him; and that he should give pledges and ordinance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> in the country of India; and they putted out men of Media, and of Lydia, from the best countries of them, and they gave those countries taken of (or from) them to king Eumenes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and that they that were with (the) Greeks, would go, and take away them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and the word was known to these Romans, and they sent to them one duke, and they fought against them; and many of them fell, and they led their wives captives, and sons, and robbed them; and wielded the land of them, and destroyed the walls of them, and brought them into servage (or into servitude), till into this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And they destroyed other realms and isles, that sometime against-stood them, and brought &#91;them&#93; into (or under their) power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Forsooth with their friends, and (they) that had rest in them, they kept friendship, and they wielded realms that were next (or were near), and that were far; for whoever heard the name of them, dreaded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For they reigned, to whom they would be in help for to reign; forsooth which they would, they disturbed (or displaced) from (their) realm; and they were greatly enhanced (or exalted).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> (Yet) In all these Romans, no man bare (a) diadem, neither was clothed in purple, for to be magnified therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And &#91;for&#93; they made to (or for) them(selves) a court, and each day they counselled three hundred and twenty, doing counsel evermore of (the) multitude, (so) that they do what things be worthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And they betake to one man their mastery, either chief governance, by each year, for to be lord of all their land; and all obeish (or obey) to one, and envy is not, neither wrath among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Judas chose Eupolemus, the son of John, son of Accos, and Jason, the son of Eleazar, and sent them to Rome, for to ordain with them friend-ship and fellowship;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and (so) that they should take away from them the yoke of (the) Greeks, for they saw that they oppressed the realm of Israel into servage (or into servitude).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And they went to Rome, a full great way, and they entered into the court, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Judas Maccabeus, and his brethren, and the people of (the) Jews, sent us to you, for to ordain with you fellow-ship and peace, and for to write together us (as) your fellows and friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the word pleased in the sight of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And this is the again-writing, which they again-writed in brazen tables (or wrote upon brass or bronze tablets), and sent into Jerusalem, (so) that it were there a memorial, either a thing of mind, of peace and fellowship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Be it well to (the) Romans, and to the folk of (the) Jews, in the sea and (the) land, without end; and sword and enemy be far from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> That if battle befall to (the) Romans before, &#91;or former&#93;, (or first), either to all fellows of them in all the lordship of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> the folk of (the) Jews shall bear help, as time asketh, with (their) full heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and the Romans shall not give, neither privily minister to the Jews fighting, wheat, armours, (or arms, or weapons), money, ships, as it pleased to (the) Romans; and they shall keep the commandments of them, and take nothing of (or from) them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Forsooth in like manner, and if battle falleth before (or first) to the folk of (the) Jews, (the) Romans shall help of (or with)(a)&#91;good&#93; heart, as time suffereth (or alloweth) them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and to (the)Romans helping, wheat shall not be given, neither armours, (nor arms, or weapons), money, neither ships, as it pleased to (the) Romans; and they shall keep the command-ments of them without guile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> By &#91;or After&#93; these words the Romans ordained to the people of (the) Jews,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> That if after these words, these either they will put anything to, either do away, they shall do of (or by) their common assent; and whatever things they shall put to, either do away, they shall be steadfast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> But also of (the) evils which king Demetrius hath done against them, we have written to him, and we said Why hast thou grieved thy yoke on our friends and fellows, the Jews?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Therefore if again they shall come to us against thee, we shall do doom to them, and shall fight with thee by land and (by) sea.</span>
