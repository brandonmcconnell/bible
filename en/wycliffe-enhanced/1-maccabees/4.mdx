<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Gorgias came into the tents of Judas by night, and found no man; and they sought them in (the) hills, for he said, These flee from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And when day was made, Judas appeared in the field with three thousands of men only, which had not coverings and swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And they saw the tents of (the) heathen men strong, and (the) men habergeoned, and the multitude of horsemen in compass of them, and these were taught to battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Judas said to his men, that were with him, Dread ye not the multitude of them, and dread ye not inwardly the fierceness of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Bethink ye how our fathers were made safe in the Red Sea, when Pharaoh pursued them with much host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And now cry we to heaven, and the Lord shall have mercy on us, and shall be mindful of the testament of our fathers, and shall all-break this host before our face today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And (then) all (the) folks shall know, that it is God, that shall again-buy (or who shall redeem), and deliver Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And (the) aliens raised (up) their eyes, and saw them coming of (or from) the contrary part, &#91;or even against (them)&#93;,</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And they went together, and (the) heathen men were all-broken, and fled into the field;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> forsooth the last fell down by (the) sword. And they pursued them till to Gazara, and till into the fields of Idumea (or Edom), and Azotus, and Jamnia; and there fell down of them till to three thousands of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Judas (re)turned again, and his host pursuing him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and Gorgias and his host be in the hill nigh us; but stand ye now against our enemies, and overcome them, and after these things ye shall take preys securely (or boldly take the prey).</span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And great health (or deliverance) was made in Israel in that day.</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And when he heard these things, he was astonished in soul, and failed; for not what manner things he would, such befell in Israel, and what manner things the king commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And in the year pursuing, Lysias gathered of chosen men sixty thousand, and of horsemen five thousand, for to overcome them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And they came into Judea, and setted tents in Bethhoron (or And they came into Idumea, and set up their tents in Bethsura); and Judas ran to them with ten thousand of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And they saw (the) strong host, and he prayed, and said, Blessed art thou, (O) Saviour of Israel, that hast all-broken the fierceness of the mighty Goliath in the hand of thy servant David, and betookest the castles &#91;or tents&#93;, or hosts, of (the) aliens into the hands of Jonathan, son of Saul, and of his squire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Close thou (up)(al)together also this host in the hand of thy people Israel, and be they confounded in their host, and horsemen.</span>

<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Forsooth Judas said to his brethren &#91;or Forsooth Judas said, and his brethren&#93;, Lo! our enemies be all-broken; go we up or ascend we now, for to cleanse (the) holy things (or the Temple), and make (it) new (again).</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And all the host was gathered, and they went up into the hill of Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And they saw (the) hallowing (or Temple) desert(ed), either forsaken, and the altar unhallowed, or defouled by idolatry, and the gates burnt, and in the porches tender trees grown, as in (the) wild wood, or (the) mountains, and (the) little cells destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And they rent their clothes, and wailed with great wailing; and putted ashes on their head(s),</span>

<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Then Judas ordained men, for to fight against them that were in the high tower, as long as (or until) they cleansed holy things (or the Temple).</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And he chose priests without wem, having will in (or for) the law of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> and they cleansed (the) holy things (or Temple), and took away (the) stones of defouling into an unclean place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And he thought on the altar of burnt sacrifices, that was unhallowed, (and) what he should do thereof.</span>

<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And (so) they destroyed it, and kept &#91;or putted&#93;(the) stones in the hill of the house, in (a) covenable (or a suitable) place, till that a prophet came, and answered of (or for) them.</span>

<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And they builded holy things (or the Temple), and the things that were within the house withinforth; and they hallowed the house, and the porches.</span>

<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> And &#91;they&#93; putted incense on the altar, and tended (the) lanterns, that were on the candlestick, and gave light in the temple.</span>

<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And before morrowtide they rose (up), in the five and twentieth day of the ninth month, this is the month Kislev, that is, November-December, of the hundred and eight and fortieth year.</span>

<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> And they builded in that time the hill of Zion, and by compass high walls, and firm towers, lest any time (the) heathen men would come, and defoul it, as they did before.</span>
