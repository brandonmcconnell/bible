<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And then it shall be need(ed), that the spyers &#91;or the spies&#93; of the land flee to raise up their prince to battle.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And when ye know that they flee, go ye securely after them, for God shall all-break them &#91;or tread them&#93; under your feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then Achior saw the virtue that God of Israel had done, and he forsook the custom of heathenness, and he believed to (or in) God; and he cir-cumcised the flesh of his rod, and he was put (or was joined) to the people of Israel, and all the after-coming of his kin till into this day, or unto this time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Forsooth anon (or at once) as the day rose, they hanged the head of Holofernes on the wall; and each man took his armours, (or his arms, or weapons), and they went out with great noise and yelling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Which thing the spyers &#91;or the spies&#93; saw, and they ran to the tabernacle of Holofernes.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For no man was hardy, (or bold enough), to open the tabernacle of the virtue, that is, of the prince of the chivalry (or cavalry), of (the) Assyrians by knocking, either by entering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> But when his dukes, and his tribunes &#91;or the leaders of thousands&#93;, and all the greater men of the host of the king of Assyria were come, they said to the chamberlains,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Enter ye, and raise ye him up; for mice be gone out of the caves, and they dare (to) move us &#91;or to call forth us&#93; to battle.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Bagoas entered into the tabernacle of Judith, and found not her &#91;or (and) found her not&#93;, and anon (or at once) he went out to the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and said, An Hebrew woman hath made confusion in the house of king Nebuchadnezzar; for lo! Holofernes lieth in the earth (or on the ground), and his head is not with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And when the princes of the virtue, or the power, of (the) Assyrians had heard this thing, all they rent their clothes, and unsufferable &#91;or intolerable&#93; dread and trembling felled down upon them, and their souls were troubled greatly.</span>
