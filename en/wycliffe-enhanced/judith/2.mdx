<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> In the thirteenth year of king Nebuchadnezzar, in the two and twentieth day of the first month, a word was made &#91;or is done&#93; in the house of Nebuchadnezzar, king of (the) Assyrians, that he would defend him(self)from his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And he called to him(self) all his elder men, and all the dukes, (and) his warriors &#91;or his fighters&#93;; and had with them the private of his counsel (or his secret counsel).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And he said, that his thought and intent was set in that thing, to make subject each land to his empire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And when this saying had pleased all (his) men, king Nebuchadnezzar called Holofernes, the prince of his chivalry, and said to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Go thou out against each realm of the west, and against them principally (or specifically), that despised my commandment &#91;or mine empire&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Thine eye shall not spare any realm, and thou shalt make subject to me each strengthened (or fortified) city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then Holofernes called the dukes and (the) magistrates of the power of (the) Assyrians, and he numbered men into the making ready, &#91;or the speeding, (or expediting)&#93;, of (the) battle, as the king commanded to him, sixscore thousand of footmen fighters, and twelve thousand horsemen and archers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And he made all his purveyance to go before him in multitude of unnumberable camels, with those things that sufficed plenteously to his hosts, and he sent droves of oxen, and flocks of sheep, of which (there) was none number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He ordained wheat to be made ready of (or from) all Syria in his passage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And he took with him much gold and silver of (or from) the king’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Holofernes, and all his host, went forth with chariots &#91;or four-horsed carts&#93;, and horsemen, and archers, which covered the face of the earth, as locusts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And when he had passed the ends &#91;or the coasts&#93; of (the) Assyrians, he came to the great hills called Auge, that be at the left half &#91;or side&#93; of Cilicia; and he went up into all the castles (or the villages) of them, and he got &#91;or wielded&#93; each strong place there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he brake and destroyed the most strong city called Bectileth, and he robbed all the sons of Rassis, and the sons of Ishmael, that were against (or towards) the face of (the) desert, and at the south coast of the land of (the) Cheleans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And he passed &#91;over&#93;(the) Eu-phrates, and came into Mesopotamia, and he brake, and wasted, all the high cities that were there, from the strand &#91;or the stream&#93; of Abron till that men come to the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he occupied the ends or the coasts thereof from Cilicia unto the ends of Japheth, that be at the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And he brought with him all the sons of Midian (or the Midianites), and he took from them all their riches; and he killed &#91;or he slew&#93; by the sharpness of sword all men against-standing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And after these things he came down into the fields of Damascus, in the days of ripe corn &#91;or of reap(ing)&#93;, that is, in harvest, and he burnt all those corns &#91;or all the sown things&#93;, and he made all the trees and vines to be cut down;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and his dread &#91;or the dread of him&#93; fell upon all men inhabiting the land or dwelling upon (the) earth &#91;or in the land&#93;.</span>
