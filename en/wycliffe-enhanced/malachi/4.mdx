(For behold! a day shall come, burning like a chimney; and all who be proud, and all who do unrighteousness, (or wickedness), shall become stubble; and this coming day shall enflame them, (or shall set all of them ablaze), saith the Lord of hosts, and it shall not leave them with any roots, or with any burgeoning.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:6; PSALMS 84:11; ISAIAH 30:26"> And to you dreading my name the sun of rightwiseness shall rise, and health in (the) pens of him; and ye shall go out, and shall leap, as a calf of the drove. <span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOB 40:12; ZECHARIAH 10:5; ROMANS 16:20"> And ye shall tread the unpious men, when they shall be ashes under the sole(s) of your feet, in the day in which I do, saith the Lord of hosts. (And ye shall tread upon the unrighteous, or upon the wicked, when they shall be ashes under the soles of your feet, on the day in which I shall do this, saith the Lord of hosts.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:10; JAMES 2:9–13; LUKE 16:29–31"> Bethink ye on the law of my servant Moses, which I commanded to him in Horeb, to all Israel commandments and dooms. (Behold! I shall send you the prophet Elijah, before that the great and terrible day of the Lord shall come.)</span>

Amen. (And he shall turn the hearts of the fathers toward their sons, and the hearts of the sons toward their fathers, lest perhaps I come, and strike the land with a curse, and destroy it.
