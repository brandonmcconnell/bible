<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 18:11; JEREMIAH 19:14–15; MARK 7:14–15"> which word Jeremy, the prophet, spake to all the people of Judah, and to all the dwellers of Jerusalem, and said, (which word the prophet Jeremiah spoke to all the people of Judah, and to all the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and said,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 11:7; JEREMIAH 26:5; JEREMIAH 1:2"> From the thirteenth year of the realm of Josiah, the son of Amon, the king of Judah, unto this day, this is the three and twentieth year, (that) the word of the Lord was made to me; and I spake to you, and I rose (up) by night and spake, and ye heard not (or but ye would not listen).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:3; JEREMIAH 26:5; JEREMIAH 13:10–11"> And the Lord sent to you all his servants (the) prophets, and rose (up) full early, and sent, and ye heard not (or but you would not listen), neither ye bowed &#91;in&#93; your ears, for to hear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:7; GENESIS 17:8; ISAIAH 55:6–7"> when he said, Turn ye again, each man from his evil way, and from your worst thoughts, and ye shall dwell in the land which the Lord gave to you, and to your fathers, from the world and till into the world. (when they said, Turn ye, each person, from your evil ways, and from your worst thoughts, and ye shall live in the land which the Lord gave to you, and to your forefathers, forever and ever.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:19; JEREMIAH 7:6; JEREMIAH 35:15"> And do not ye go after alien gods, (saith the Lord), that ye serve them, and worship them, neither stir ye me to wrathfulness, in the works of your hands, and I shall not torment you. (And do not ye go after strange, or foreign, gods, saith the Lord, so that ye serve them, and worship them, nor stir ye me to anger, with the works of your hands, and I shall not torment you.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND KI 21:15; PROVERBS 8:36; DEUTERONOMY 32:21"> And ye heard not me, saith the Lord, that ye stirred me to wrathfulness in the works of your hands, into your &#91;own&#93; evil. (But ye would not listen to me, saith the Lord, and ye stirred me to anger with the works of your hands, to your own harm.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Therefore the Lord of hosts saith these things, For that that ye heard not my words, (And so the Lord of hosts saith these things, Because ye did not listen to, or obey, my words,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 13:3; JEREMIAH 18:16; JEREMIAH 1:15"> lo! I shall send (for), and take all the kindreds of the north, saith the Lord, and Nebuchadnezzar, my servant, the king of Babylon; and I shall bring them on this land, and on the dwellers thereof, and on all nations, that be in the compass thereof, (or behold! I shall send for all the tribes of the north, saith the Lord, and my servant Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon; and I shall bring them against this land, and its inhabitants, and all the nations that be all around it); and I shall slay them, and I shall set them into wondering, and into hissing, and into everlasting wildernesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:34; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:22–23; EZEKIEL 26:13"> And I shall lose of them the voice of joy, and the voice of gladness, the voice of spouse, and the voice of spousess, the voice of &#91;the&#93; quern, and the light of the lantern. (And I shall take from them the sound of joy, and the sound of happiness, the voice of the spouse, and the voice of the spousess, the sound of the quern, and the light of the lantern.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DANIEL 9:2; ZECHARIAH 1:12; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:21–22"> And all the land thereof shall be into wilderness, and into wondering; and all these folks shall serve the king of Babylon seventy years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DANIEL 9:2; ISAIAH 13:19; JEREMIAH 29:10"> And when seventy years be &#91;ful&#93; filled, I shall visit on the king of Babylon, and on that folk, (for) the wickedness of them, saith the Lord, and on the land of Chaldees, and I shall set it into everlasting wildernesses. (And when seventy years be fulfilled, I shall punish the king of Babylon, and that nation, saith the Lord, for their wickedness, and I shall make the land of the Chaldeans into an everlasting wilderness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:10; JEREMIAH 1:5; DANIEL 5:31"> And I shall bring on that land all my words which I spake against it, all thing that is written in this book; whatever things Jeremy prophesied against all folks (or whatever Jeremiah prophesied against all the nations);</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:6; JEREMIAH 27:7; JEREMIAH 50:9"> for they served to them, when they were many folks, and great kings; and I shall yield to them after the works of them, and after the deeds of their hands. (and then they shall serve many nations, and great kings; and so I shall yield to them after their works, and after the deeds of their own hands.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 75:8; ISAIAH 51:17; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:10"> For the Lord of hosts, God of Israel, saith thus, Take thou the cup of wine of this strong vengeance from mine hand, and thou shall pour out drink thereof to all heathen men, to which I shall send thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NAHUM 3:11; JEREMIAH 51:7; JEREMIAH 51:39"> And they shall drink, and shall be troubled, and shall be mad, of the face of sword, which I shall send among them. (And they shall drink it, and shall be troubled, and shall go mad, in the face of the sword, which I shall send among them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:10; JEREMIAH 25:28; EZEKIEL 43:3"> And I took the cup from the hand of the Lord, and I poured out drink to all folks, to which the Lord sent me; (And I took the cup from the Lord’s hand, and I poured out drink to all the nations, to which the Lord had sent me;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 24:9; PSALMS 60:3; JEREMIAH 44:22"> to Jerusalem, and to all the cities of Judah, and to the kings thereof, and to the princes thereof; that I should give them into wilderness, and into wondering, and into hissing, and into cursing, as this day is, (or yea, to make them into wilderness, and into wondering, and into hissing, and into cursing, as it is this day);</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 46:13–26; NAHUM 3:8–10; JEREMIAH 46:2"> to Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, and to his servants, and to his princes, and to all his people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOB 1:1; EZEKIEL 30:5; ISAIAH 20:1"> and to all men generally, to all the kings of the land (of) Uz, and to all the kings of the land of Philistines, and to Ashkelon, and to Azzah, and to Ekron, and to the residues of Ashdod (or and to the remnant of Ashdod);</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:1–22; PSALMS 137:7; JEREMIAH 9:26"> to Idumea, and to Moab, and to the sons of Ammon; (to Edom, and to Moab, and to the Ammonites;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 47:4; AMOS 1:9–10; EZEKIEL 28:22–23"> and to all the kings of Tyrus (or and to all the kings of Tyre), and to all the kings of Sidon, and to the kings of the land of isles that be beyond the sea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:26; JEREMIAH 49:32; JEREMIAH 49:8"> and to Dedan, and Tema, and Buz, and to all men that be clipped on the long hair (or and to all the men who clip their long hair);</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 9:14; JEREMIAH 25:20; EZEKIEL 30:5"> and to all the kings of Arabia, and to all the kings of the west, that dwell in desert (or who live in the desert);</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ISAIAH 11:11; GENESIS 10:22; ISAIAH 13:17"> and to all the kings of Zimri, and to all the kings of Elam, and to all the kings of Medes (or and to all the kings of Media);</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:41; JEREMIAH 25:9; JEREMIAH 50:1–46"> and to all the kings of the north, of nigh and of far, to each man against his brother; and to all the realms of earth, that be on the face thereof; and (the) king (of) Sheshach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Sheshach is another name for Babylon.">a</a></sup>shall drink after them. (and to all the kings of the north, of near and of far away, to each person and their kin; and to all the kingdoms of the earth, that be on the face of it; and the king of Sheshach, that is, the king of Babylon, shall drink after them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:16; HABAKKUK 2:16; EZEKIEL 21:4–5"> And thou shalt say to them, The Lord of hosts, God of Israel, saith these things, Drink ye, and be ye drunken, and spew ye, and fall ye down, and do not ye rise (up) from the face of &#91;the&#93; sword which I shall send among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOB 34:33; JEREMIAH 51:29; ISAIAH 14:24–27"> And when they will not take the cup from thine hand (or And if they refuse to take the cup from thy hand), (so) that they drink, thou shalt say to them, The Lord of hosts saith these things, Ye drinking shall drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:21; PROVERBS 11:31; 1ST PETER 4:17"> for lo! in the city in which my name is called to help, I begin to torment, and shall ye as innocents be without pain? ye shall not be without pain, for I call (for the) sword on all the dwellers of earth, saith the Lord of hosts. (for behold! in the city in which my name is called on for help or in the city which is called by my name, I shall begin to torment it, and shall ye, as innocents, be without pain? ye shall not be without pain, for I shall call for the sword on all the inhabitants of the earth, saith the Lord of hosts.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="AMOS 1:2; JOEL 3:16; ISAIAH 42:13"> And thou shalt prophesy to them all these words, and thou shalt say to them, The Lord shall roar from on high, and from his holy dwelling place he shall give his voice; he roaring shall roar on his fairness; a merry song, as of men treading in pressers, shall be sung against all &#91;the&#93; dwellers of earth. (And thou shalt prophesy to them all these words, and thou shalt say to them, The Lord shall roar from on high, and he shall give his voice from his holy dwelling place; he roaring shall roar across the heavens; and a happy song, like when people tread the winepresses, shall be sung against all the inhabitants of the earth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="HOSEA 4:1; JOEL 3:2; ISAIAH 66:16"> &#91;The&#93; Sound is come unto the last parts of &#91;the&#93; earth, for why doom is to the Lord with folks, he is deemed with each flesh; the Lord saith, I have given wicked men to the sword. (The sound shall come unto the last parts of the earth, for the Lord shall judge the nations, yea, he shall judge all flesh, and he shall give the wicked ones to the sword. Thus saith the Lord.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:19; ISAIAH 34:2; 2ND CHRONICLES 15:6"> The Lord of hosts saith these things, Lo! torment shall go out from folk into folk, and a great whirlwind shall go out from the ends of &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="ISAIAH 66:16; JEREMIAH 8:2; PSALMS 79:3"> And the slain men of the Lord shall be in that day from the end of the earth unto the end thereof; they shall not be bewailed, neither shall be gathered together, neither shall be buried; they shall lie into a dunghill on the face of &#91;the&#93; earth. (And on that day, the people killed by the Lord shall lie flat on the earth from one end to the other; they shall not be bewailed, nor shall they be gathered together, nor shall they be buried, but they shall lie upon the face of the earth like a mound of dung.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:26; JEREMIAH 50:27; PSALMS 2:9"> Yell, ye shepherds, and cry, and, ye principals of the flock, besprinkle you with ashes; for your days be &#91;ful&#93; filled, that ye be slain, and your scatterings be (ful) filled, and ye shall fall as precious vessels. (Yell, ye shepherds of the people, and cry aloud, and besprinkle yourselves with ashes, ye principal men, or ye leaders, of the flock; for your days be fulfilled, and now ye shall be killed, and your scatterings be fulfilled, and now ye shall fall like precious vessels.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JOB 11:20; AMOS 2:14; JEREMIAH 52:8–11"> And fleeing shall perish from &#91;the&#93; shepherds, and saving shall perish from the principals of the flock. (And there shall be no escape for the shepherds of the people, yea, no salvation for the principal men, or the leaders, of the flock.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:8; JEREMIAH 25:34"> The voice of the cry of shepherds, and the yelling of the principals of the flock, for the Lord hath wasted the pastures of them. (Hear the sound of the cry of the shepherds of the people, and the yelling of the principal men, or of the leaders, of the flock, for the Lord hath destroyed their pastures.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="ISAIAH 27:10–11; ISAIAH 32:14"> And the fields of peace were still(ed), for the face of &#91;the&#93; wrath of the strong vengeance of the Lord. (And the peaceful fields were wasted, by the anger of the strong vengeance of the Lord.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:7; JEREMIAH 5:6; HOSEA 13:7–8"> He as a lion hath forsaken his tabernacle, for the land of them is made into desolation, of the face of (the) wrath of the culver, and of the face of (the) wrath of the strong vengeance of the Lord. (They have deserted their tents like a lion hath deserted his den, for their land is made into desolation, by the cruel sword, and by the anger of his strong vengeance.)</span>
