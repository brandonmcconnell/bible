<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 3:16; EZEKIEL 1:3; JEREMIAH 1:2"> And the word of the Lord was made to me, and said,</span>

(Before that I formed thee in the womb, I knew thee; and before that thou wentest out of the womb, I consecrated, or dedicated, thee; and I gave thee to be a prophet among the nations.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 6:12; EXODUS 4:10–16; 1ST KINGS 3:7–9"> And I said, A! A! A! Lord God, lo! I cannot speak, for I am a child. (And I said, O! O! O! Lord God, behold! I cannot speak, for I am but a child.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MARK 16:15–16; EZEKIEL 3:27; NUMBERS 22:20"> And the Lord said to me, Do not thou say, that I am a child; for thou shalt go to all things, to which I shall send thee, and thou shalt speak all things, whatever things I shall command to thee. (And the Lord said to me, Do not thou say, that I am but a child; for thou shalt go to whatever people I shall send thee, and thou shalt say whatever words I shall command thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:5; JOSHUA 1:9; ISAIAH 51:7"> Dread thou not of the face of them; for I am with thee, to deliver thee, saith the Lord. (Fear thou not them; for I am with thee, to keep thee safe, saith the Lord./Do not thou fear them, for I am with thee, to protect thee, saith the Lord.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 21:15; EXODUS 4:11–12; LUKE 12:12"> And the Lord sent his hand, and touched my mouth; and the Lord said to me, Lo! I have given my words in thy mouth; (And the Lord put forth his hand, and touched my mouth; and the Lord said to me, Behold! I have put my words in thy mouth;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:28; 2ND CORINTHIANS 10:4–5; ISAIAH 44:26–28"> lo! <span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="AMOS 7:8; AMOS 8:2; ZECHARIAH 5:2"> And the word of the Lord was made to me, and said, What seest thou, Jeremy? And I said, I see a rod wakening. <span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 12:28; EZEKIEL 12:25; EZEKIEL 12:22–23"> And the Lord said to me, Thou hast seen well, for I shall wake<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This word in Hebrew sounds like the Hebrew for ‘almond’.">a</a></sup>on my word, to do it. (And the Lord said to me, Thou hast seen well, for I am on watch, to carry out my word.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 11:3; EZEKIEL 11:7; ZECHARIAH 4:2"> And the word of the Lord was made the second time to me, and said, What seest thou? &#91;And I said&#93;, I see a pot boiling, and the face thereof from the face of the north, (or And I said, I see a boiling pot, and its face is towards the north).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:25; JEREMIAH 4:6; JEREMIAH 10:22"> And the Lord said to me, From the north shall be showed all evil on all the dwellers of the land. (And the Lord said to me, From the north shall come all evil for all the inhabitants of this land.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 43:10; JEREMIAH 25:9; JEREMIAH 9:11"> For lo! I shall call together all the nations of &#91;the&#93; realms of the north, saith the Lord; and they shall come, and set each man his seat in the entering of the gates of Jerusalem, and on all the walls thereof in compass, and on all the cities of Judah. (For behold! I shall call together all the nations of the kingdoms of the north, saith the Lord; and they shall come, and each king shall set up his throne before the gates of Jerusalem, and against the walls all around it, and in all the cities of Judah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:9; JEREMIAH 19:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:41"> And I shall speak my dooms with them on all the malice of them, that forsook me, and made sacrifice to alien gods, and worshipped the work of their hands. (And I shall tell out my judgement of them, for all the malice of those who have deserted me, and made sacrifice to strange, or foreign, gods, and who worshipped the work of their own hands.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:7–8; 1ST KINGS 18:46; 1ST PETER 1:13"> Therefore gird &#91;up&#93; thou thy loins, and rise thou (up), and speak to them all things which I command to thee; dread thou not of the face of them, for I shall not make thee for to dread the cheer of them. (And so gird thou up thy loins, and rise thou up, and say to them all the things which I command thee; fear thou not to go before them, or else I shall truly make thee afraid when thou art before them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 50:7; EZEKIEL 3:8–9; JEREMIAH 15:20"> For I gave thee today into a strong city, and into an iron pillar, and into a brazen wall, on all the land, to the kings of Judah, and to the princes thereof, and to the priests thereof, and to all the people of the land. (For I have made thee this day like a strong city, and like an iron pillar, and like a bronze wall, to stand against all the land, yea, against the kings of Judah, and its princes, or its leaders, and its priests, and all the people of the land.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:9; JEREMIAH 1:8; JEREMIAH 20:11"> And they shall fight against thee, and they shall not have the mastery; for I am with thee, saith the Lord, that I deliver thee. (And they shall fight against thee, but they shall not have the mastery; for I am with thee, saith the Lord, and I shall keep thee safe.)</span>
