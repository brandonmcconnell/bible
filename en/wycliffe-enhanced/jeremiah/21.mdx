<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:25; JEREMIAH 37:3; JEREMIAH 52:24"> The word which was made of the Lord to Jeremy (or The word of the Lord which was made to Jeremiah), when king Zedekiah sent to him Pashur, the son of Melchiah, and Zephaniah, the priest, the son of Maaseiah, and (they) said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 37:3; JEREMIAH 42:4–6; JEREMIAH 37:7"> Ask thou the Lord for us, for Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon, fighteth against us; if in hap the Lord do with us by all his marvels, and he go away from us, (or if perhaps the Lord shall do one of his marvels, or one of his miracles, for us, and he shall go away from us).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And Jeremy said to them, Thus ye shall say to Zedekiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:5; JEREMIAH 37:8–10; ISAIAH 5:5"> The Lord God of Israel saith these things, Lo! I shall turn (back upon you) the instruments of battle that be in your hands, and with which ye fight against the king of Babylon, and against Chaldees, that besiege you in the compass of &#91;the&#93; walls, (or and against the Chaldeans, who besiege you all around the walls); and I shall gather those together in the midst of this city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ISAIAH 63:10; EXODUS 6:6; ISAIAH 5:25"> And I shall overcome you in &#91;a&#93; hand stretched forth, and in &#91;a&#93; strong arm, (or And I shall overcome you with a hand stretched forth, and with a strong arm), and in strong vengeance, and in indignation, and in great wrath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 1:3; EZEKIEL 14:13; JEREMIAH 12:3–4"> and I shall smite the dwellers of this city (or and I shall strike down the inhabitants of this city), (yea), men and beasts shall die by great pestilence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 37:17; 2ND KI 25:5–7; HABAKKUK 1:6–10"> And after these things, saith the Lord, I shall give Zedekiah, king of Judah, and his servants, and his people, and (those) that be left in this city from pestilence, and sword, and hunger, in(to) the hand of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, and in(to) the hand of their enemies, and in(to) the hand of men seeking the life of them; and he shall smite them by the sharpness of sword (or and he shall strike them down by the sharpness of the sword); and he shall not be bowed, neither shall spare, neither shall have mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 30:19; DEUTERONOMY 30:15; DEUTERONOMY 11:26"> And thou shalt say to this people, The Lord God saith these things, Lo! I give before you the way of life, and the way of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 38:2; JEREMIAH 45:5; JEREMIAH 39:18"> He that dwelleth in this city, shall die by sword, and hunger, and pestilence; but he that goeth out, and fleeth over to &#91;the&#93; Chaldees that besiege you, shall live, and his life shall be as a prey to him. (He who liveth in this city, shall die by the sword, and hunger, and pestilence; but he who goeth out, and fleeth over to the Chaldeans who besiege you, shall live, but his own life shall be his only prey.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 44:11; AMOS 9:4; JEREMIAH 44:27"> For I have set my face on this city into evil, and not into good, saith the Lord, (or For I have set my face against this city for evil, and not for good, saith the Lord); it shall be given in(to) the hand of the king of Babylon, and he shall burn it with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:20; MICAH 3:1; JEREMIAH 13:18"> And thou shall say to the house of the king of Judah, the house of David, Hear ye the word of the Lord. (And thou shall say to the house of the king of Judah, Ye house of David, hear ye the word of the Lord.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:17; PROVERBS 31:8–9; JEREMIAH 4:4"> The Lord saith these things, Deem ye early doom, and deliver ye him that is oppressed by violence from the hand of the false challenger; lest peradventure mine indignation go out as fire, and be kindled, and none be that quench, for the malice of your studies. (The Lord saith these things, Early judge ye judgement, and rescue ye him who is oppressed with violence from the hand of the oppressor; lest perhaps my indignation go out like a fire, and be kindled, and there is no one who can quench it, for the malice of your deeds.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:8; LAMENTATIONS 4:12; OBADIAH 1:3–4"> Lo! I do to thee, dwelleress of the firm valley, and plain, saith the Lord, &#91;ye&#93; which say, Who shall smite us, and who shall enter into our houses? (Behold! I am against thee, O dwelleress of the firm valley, and O rock of the plain, saith the Lord, Yea, ye who say, Who shall strike us, and who shall enter into our houses?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 3:10–11; JEREMIAH 32:19; PROVERBS 1:31"> And I shall visit on you by the fruit of your studies, saith the Lord; and I shall kindle fire in the forest thereof, and it shall devour all things in the compass thereof. (And I shall punish you for the fruit of your deeds, saith the Lord; and I shall kindle a fire in the forest there, and it shall devour everything all around it.)</span>
