We shall obey the voice of the Lord our God, to whom we send thee, so that it may be well with us, when we have heard the voice of the Lord our God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 27:14; ISAIAH 28:16; HABAKKUK 2:3"> Forsooth when ten days were &#91;ful&#93; filled, the word of the Lord was made to Jeremy.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND KI 19:4; JEREMIAH 42:2; 2ND KI 19:6"> and he said to them, The Lord God of Israel saith these things, to whom ye sent me, that I should meekly set forth your prayers in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:28; EZEKIEL 36:36; JEREMIAH 24:6"> If ye rest, and dwell in this land, I shall build you, and I shall not destroy; I shall plant, and I shall not draw out; for now I am pleased on the evil which I did to you. (If ye rest, and live in this land, I shall build you, and I shall not destroy you; I shall plant you, and I shall not draw you out; for now I repent for the evil which I have done to you.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 8:31; JEREMIAH 1:19; JEREMIAH 1:8"> Do not ye dread of the face of the king of Babylon, whom ye that be fearful, dread; do not ye dread him, saith the Lord, for I am with you, to make you safe, and to deliver &#91;you&#93; from his hand. (Do not ye fear the king of Babylon, whom ye who be fearful, do fear; do not ye fear him, saith the Lord, for I am with you, to make you safe, and to deliver you out of his hands.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 1:11; PSALMS 106:45–46; PROVERBS 16:7"> And I shall give mercies to you, and I shall have mercy on you, and I shall make you dwell in your land (or and I shall help you to live in your own land).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 44:16; EXODUS 5:2; JEREMIAH 42:10"> Forsooth if ye say, We shall not dwell in this land, neither we shall hear the voice of our Lord God, (But if ye say, We shall not live in this land, nor shall we listen to the voice of the Lord our God,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 41:17; JEREMIAH 4:19; ISAIAH 31:1"> and say, Nay, but we shall go to the land of Egypt, where we shall not see battle, and shall not hear the noise, either sound, of trump, and we shall not suffer hunger, and there we shall dwell; (and say, No! but we shall go to the land of Egypt, where we shall not see battle, and shall not hear the sound of the trumpet, and we shall not suffer hunger, and we shall live there;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:16; JEREMIAH 44:12–14; JEREMIAH 42:17"> for this thing, ye remnants of Judah, hear now the word of the Lord. The Lord of hosts, God of Israel, saith these things, If ye set your face, for to enter into Egypt, and if ye enter, to dwell there, (or If ye set your face, to enter into Egypt, and if ye go, to live there),</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 44:27; EZEKIEL 11:8; AMOS 9:1–4"> the sword which ye dread shall take you there in the land of Egypt, and the hunger for which ye be anguished shall cleave to you in Egypt; and there ye shall die (or and ye shall die there).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 42:22; JEREMIAH 44:28; JEREMIAH 44:13–14"> And all the men that setted their face, to enter into Egypt, and to dwell there, shall die by sword, and hunger, and pestilence; no man of them shall dwell still, neither shall escape from the face of &#91;the&#93; evil, which I shall bring on them. (And all those who set their face, to enter into Egypt, and to live there, shall die by the sword, and hunger, and pestilence; not one of them shall still remain, nor shall escape from the face of the evil, which I shall bring upon them.)</span>

(For the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, saith these things, As my strong vengeance and my indignation is welled together upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so my indignation shall be welled together upon you, when ye have entered into Egypt; and ye shall be into swearing, and into wondering, and into cursing, and into shame; and ye shall no longer see this place.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:16; ISAIAH 30:1–7; NEHEMIAH 9:26"> The word of the Lord is on you, ye remnants of Judah; do not ye enter into Egypt; ye (that be) witting shall know, that I have witnessed (against that) to you today; (This word of the Lord is for you, ye remnant of Judah; do not ye enter into Egypt; ye who be witting, or who understand, now know, that I have witnessed against that to you today;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EZEKIEL 14:3–4; EZEKIEL 33:31; MATTHEW 22:15–18"> for ye have deceived your souls, for ye sent me to your Lord God, and said, Pray thou for us to our Lord God, and by all things whatever things our Lord &#91;God&#93; shall say to thee, so tell thou to us, and we shall do. (for ye have deceived your own souls, when ye sent me to the Lord your God, and said, Pray thou for us to the Lord our God, and everything that the Lord our God shall say to thee, tell thou to us, and we shall do it.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 2:7; ZECHARIAH 7:11–12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:26–27"> And I told to you today, and ye heard not the voice of your Lord God, on all things for which he sent me to you. (And so I told it to you today, but ye have not listened to, or obeyed, the voice of the Lord your God, regarding anything for which he hath sent me to you.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 42:17; EZEKIEL 6:11; HOSEA 9:6"> Now therefore, ye (that be) witting shall know, for ye shall die by sword, and hunger, and pestilence, in the place to which ye would enter, to dwell there. (And so, ye who be witting now know, or understand, that ye shall die by the sword, and hunger, and pestilence, in the place where ye would go, to live there.)</span>
