<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 38:6; JEREMIAH 38:10–13; LAMENTATIONS 3:53"> Therefore Jeremy entered into the house of the pit, and into the prison of travail; and Jeremy sat there many days (or and Jeremiah sat there for many days).</span>

(And so King Zedekiah sent for Jeremiah, and had him brought to his house, and privately asked him, Knowest thou, is there a word from the Lord? And Jeremiah said, There is. And Jeremiah said, Thou shalt be delivered into the hands of the king of Babylon.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:8; JOHN 10:32; DANIEL 6:22"> And Jeremy said to Zedekiah, the king, What have I sinned to thee, and to thy servants, and to thy people, for thou hast sent me into the house of &#91;the&#93; prison? (And Jeremiah said to King Zedekiah, What have I sinned against thee, and thy officers, and thy people, so that thou hast sent me into the prison house?)</span>

(Where be your prophets, who prophesied to you, and said, The king of Babylon shall not come against you, or against this land?)</span>
</div>

(And so Zedekiah commanded that Jeremiah should be taken to the courtyard of the prison, and that a loaf of bread should be given to him each day, but without stew, until all the loaves of the city were gone; and so Jeremiah lived in the courtyard of the prison.)</span>
