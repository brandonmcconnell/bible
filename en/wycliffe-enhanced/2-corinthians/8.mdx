<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:9; 2ND CORINTHIANS 9:2; 2ND CORINTHIANS 9:4"> But, brethren, we make known to you the grace of God, that is given in the churches of Macedonia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JAMES 2:5; PROVERBS 11:25; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:10"> that in much assaying of tribulation, the plenty &#91;or the abundance&#93; of the joy of them was, and the highest poverty of them was plenteous into the riches of the simpleness of them (or their sincerity).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 35:29; EXODUS 35:5; EXODUS 35:21–22"> For I bear witnessing to them, after might, &#91;or after power&#93;, and above might they were willful, (For I testify about them, yea, about their power, and even over their power, or beyond their limit, they were willing,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:17; GALATIANS 6:10; 2ND CORINTHIANS 9:1"> with much admonishing beseeching us (for) the grace and the communing of (the) ministering &#91;or of the ministry&#93; that is made to holy men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ROMANS 12:1; ROMANS 14:7–9; 2ND CHRONICLES 30:12"> And not as we hoped, but they gave themselves first to the Lord, afterward to us by the will of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 12:18; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:19; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:4"> So that we prayed Titus, that as he began, so also he perform in you this grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 9:8; 2ND PETER 1:5–8; 2ND THESSALONIANS 1:3"> But as ye abound in all things, in faith, and word, and cunning, (or and knowing), &#91;or and science&#93;, and all busyness, moreover and in your charity into us, that also in this grace ye abound. (But as ye abound in all things, in faith, and word, and knowledge, and all zeal, and moreover in your love for us, so that also in this gift ye abound.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:6; 1ST JOHN 3:17–19; JAMES 2:14–16"> I say not as commanding, but by the busyness of other men proving also the good wit of your charity. (I say not as commanding, but by telling you of the concern of other men, I am proving, or testing, the good thoughts or the sincerity of your love.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ROMANS 8:32; PHILIPPIANS 2:6–8; MATTHEW 20:28"> And ye know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, for he was made needy for you, when he was rich, (so) that ye should be made rich by his neediness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 6:18–19; HEBREWS 13:16; 2ND CORINTHIANS 9:2"> And I give counsel in this thing; for this is profitable to you, that not only have begun to do, but also ye began to have will (or to have desire) from the former year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 9:2"> But now perform ye in deed, that as the discretion of will is ready &#91;to desire good&#93;, so be it also of performing of that that ye have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MARK 12:42–44; 2ND CORINTHIANS 9:7; LUKE 21:1–4"> For if the will be ready, it is accepted after that that it hath, not after that that it hath not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:34; ROMANS 15:26–27"> And not that it be (a) remission, &#91;or idleness, or sloth&#93;, to other men, and to you tribulation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 9:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:34"> but of evenness in the present time your abundance fulfill the need of them, (so) that also the abundance of them be a fulfilling of your need, (so) that evenness be made;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 16:18; LUKE 22:35"> as it is written, He that gathered much, was not increased, and he that gathered little, had not less.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 17:17; JEREMIAH 32:40; 2ND CORINTHIANS 2:13–14"> And I do thankings to God, that gave the same busyness for you in the heart of Titus, (And I give thanks to God, who put the same concern for you in Titus’ heart,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 8:6; HEBREWS 13:22; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:10"> for he received exhortation; but when he was busier, by his will he went forth to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 12:18; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:19; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:17"> And we sent with him a brother, whose praising is in the gospel by all (the) churches. (And we sent a brother with him, who is praised by all the churches for his service to, or his preaching of, the Gospel or the Good News.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 16:3–4; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:15; 2ND CORINTHIANS 9:8"> And not only praised, but also he is ordained of churches (as) the fellow of our pilgrimage into this grace, that is ministered of us, to the glory of the Lord, and to our ordained will; (And not only praised, but he is also ordained by the churches as the partner of our pilgrimage in this grace, that is administered by us, for the glory of the Lord, and unto our ordained will;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:22; ROMANS 14:16; EPHESIANS 5:15"> eschewing this thing, that no man blame us in this plenty, that is ministered of us, to the glory of the Lord. (shunning this, so that no one can reproach us regarding this plenty, that is administered by us, for the glory of the Lord.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ROMANS 12:17; 1ST PETER 2:12; PHILIPPIANS 4:8"> For we purvey good things, not only before God, but also before all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:20–22"> For we sent with them also our brother, whom we have proved in many things oft, that he was busy, but now much busier, for much trust in you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:25; PHILEMON 1:17; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:6"> either for Titus, that is my fellow and helper in you, either for our brethren, (the) apostles of the churches of the glory of Christ. (either for Titus, who is my fellow and helper with you, or for our brothers, the apostles of the churches to the glory of the Messiah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 7:14; 2ND CORINTHIANS 7:4; 2ND CORINTHIANS 9:2–4"> Therefore show ye to them in the face of (the) churches, that showing that is of your charity (or that showing of your love), and of our glory for you.</span>
