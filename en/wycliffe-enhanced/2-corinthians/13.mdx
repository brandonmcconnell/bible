<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:16; DEUTERONOMY 19:15; DEUTERONOMY 17:6"> Lo! this third time I come to you, and in the mouth of two or of three witnesses every word shall stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 1:23; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:20–21; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:5"> I said before, and say before, as present twice, and now absent, to them that before have sinned, and to all others; for if I come again, I shall not spare (you).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:20; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:12; LUKE 21:15"> Whether ye seek the proof &#91;or (the) assaying&#93; of that Christ, that speaketh in me, which is not feeble in you, &#91;but is mighty in you&#93;? (Do ye seek the proof of that Messiah, who speaketh through me, who is not frail or weak in you, but is mighty and powerful?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:18; ROMANS 6:4; 2ND CORINTHIANS 10:3–4"> For though he was crucified of infirmity, but he liveth of the virtue of God. For also we be sick in him, but we shall live with him of the virtue of God in us. (For though he was crucified in weakness, now he liveth by the power of God. For we also be frail or weak in him, but we shall live with him by the power of God in us.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:40; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:28; PSALMS 139:23–24"> Assay yourselves, if ye be in the faith; ye yourselves prove (it). Whether ye know not yourselves, for Christ Jesus is in you? &#91;no&#93; but in hap ye be reprovable. (Test yourselves, if ye be in the faith; ye yourselves prove it. Do ye not know, that the Messiah Jesus is in you? unless perhaps ye be reproachable.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 12:20; 2ND CORINTHIANS 13:10; 2ND CORINTHIANS 13:3–4"> But I hope, that ye know, that we be not reprovable. (But I hope, that ye know, that we be not reproachable.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JAMES 1:12; 1ST TIMOTHY 2:2; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:8–9"> And we pray the Lord, that ye do nothing of evil; not that we seem proved (or not that we be seen as to have been proven correct), but that ye do that that is good, and that we be as reprovable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:30; MARK 9:39; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:28–30"> For we may do nothing against (the) truth, but, (rather), for the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 13:11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:10; 1ST THESSALONIANS 3:10"> For we joy, when we be sick, but ye be mighty; and we pray (for) this thing, (yea), your perfection. (For we rejoice, when we be frail or weak, but ye be strong or powerful; and we pray for this thing, yea, for your completion.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 10:8; 2ND CORINTHIANS 2:3; TITUS 1:13"> Therefore I absent write these things, that (when) I (am) present do not harder, (or so that when I come there, I do not have to be harsher), by the power, which the Lord gave to me into edification, and not into your destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:14; ROMANS 12:16; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:10"> Brethren (or Brothers), henceforward joy ye, be ye perfect, excite ye &#91;or and teach ye&#93;; understand ye the same thing; have ye peace, and (the) God of peace and of love shall be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ROMANS 16:16; 1ST PETER 5:14; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:26"> Greet ye well together in holy kiss. (Give hearty greetings to one another with a holy kiss.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ROMANS 16:16; ROMANS 16:21–23; HEBREWS 13:24"> All holy men greet you well. (All the saints, or All of God’s people, send you hearty greetings.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:22; ROMANS 5:5; ROMANS 16:20"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, and the charity of God, and the communing of the Holy Ghost, be with you all. Amen. (The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the communing of the Holy Spirit, be with you all. Amen.)</span>
