<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:3; LUKE 11:28"> My son, keep thou my words; and keep my behests to thee. (My son, remember my words; and keep my commands with thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 17:8; PROVERBS 4:4; LEVITICUS 18:5"> Keep thou my behests, and thou shalt live; and my law, as the apple of thine eye. (Keep thou my commands, and thou shalt live; and my instructions, or my teaching, as the apple of thine eyes.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 11:18–20; PROVERBS 3:3; DEUTERONOMY 6:8–9"> Bind thou it in thy fingers; write thou it in the tables of thine heart. (Bind thou it on thy fingers; write thou it on the tablets of thy heart.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 4:6–8; PROVERBS 2:2–4; SONG OF SONGS 8:1"> Say thou to wisdom, Thou art my sister; and call thou prudence thy love (or and call thou understanding, thy friend).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 2:16; PROVERBS 6:24; PROVERBS 5:3"> That it keep thee from a strange woman; and from an alien woman, that maketh her words sweet. (So that they keep thee safe from a strange woman; yea, from an unknown woman, who maketh her words sweet.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 26:8; JUDGES 5:28; 2ND SAMUEL 6:16"> (For she saith), For why from the window of mine house, by the lattice, I beheld;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:32; PROVERBS 1:22; PROVERBS 1:32"> and I see little children, that is, fools that have little wit. I behold a young man coward, (and I see fools, who have little intelligence. I behold a cowardly young man,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 5:8; PROVERBS 7:12; JUDGES 16:1"> that passeth by the streets, beside the corner, (or who passeth along the street, beside the corner); and he goeth nigh the way of her house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOB 24:13–15; ROMANS 13:12–14; EPHESIANS 5:11"> in dark time, when the day draweth to night, in the darkness and mist in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 2:9; ISAIAH 3:16–24; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:2–3"> And lo! a woman, made ready with (the) ornament of an whore to deceive souls, meeteth him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 9:13; TITUS 2:5; PROVERBS 31:10–31"> and she is a jangler, and going about, and unpatient of rest, and may not stand in the house with her feet; (and she is a gossip, who goeth about restlessly, yea, who cannot stand still in her own house;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 23:28; PROVERBS 9:14; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:3"> and now withoutforth, now in &#91;the&#93; streets, now beside &#91;the&#93; corners, she ambusheth (him).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 39:12; NUMBERS 25:6–8; GENESIS 39:7"> And she taketh (hold of), and kisseth the young man; and flattereth (him) with wooing cheer, that is, unrestful (ly), and without shame, and saith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:27; LEVITICUS 7:11; PROVERBS 17:1"> I owed sacrifices for health (or I have paid my offerings for my deliverance); today I have yielded my vows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Therefore I went out into thy meeting, and I desired to see thee; and I have found thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 19:9; EZEKIEL 27:7; 1ST KINGS 10:28"> I have made (ready) my bed with cords, I have arrayed it with tapets painted of Egypt; (I have prepared my bed, yea, I have arrayed it with coloured tapestries from Egypt;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 45:8; ISAIAH 57:7–9; SONG OF SONGS 4:13–14"> I have besprinkled my bed with myrrh, and aloes, and canel (or and cinnamon).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Come thou, be we filled with touching of teats, and use we embracings that be coveted (or and with desired embraces); till the day begin to be clear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 20:11; LUKE 12:45–46; LUKE 12:39"> For mine husband is not in his house; he is gone (away) a full long way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 2:4"> He took with him a bag of money; he shall turn again in to his house in the day of &#91;the&#93; full moon. (He took a bag of money with him; and he shall not return to his house until the day of the full moon.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PROVERBS 5:3; PROVERBS 7:5; 2ND KI 4:8"> She bound him with many words; and she drew forth him with flatterings of lips. (And so she bound him with many words; and she drew him forth with the flattery from her lips.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 13:27; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:24; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:13"> Anon he as an ox led to slain sacrifice followeth her, and as a jolly lamb and unknowing; and the fool knoweth not, that he is drawn to bonds, (And so at once he followeth her, like an ox led away to be slain for the offering, and like a jolly, and unknowing, lamb; and the fool knoweth not, that he is drawn into bonds,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:12; PROVERBS 9:18; PROVERBS 1:17"> till an arrow pierce his maw. As if a bird hasteth to the snare; and knoweth not, that it is done of the peril of his life. (until an arrow pierce his belly. Yea, like a bird that hasteneth to the snare, and knoweth not that it is done at the peril of its own life.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 5:7; PROVERBS 4:1; 1ST JOHN 2:1"> Now therefore, my son, hear thou me; and perceive the words of my mouth. (And so now, my son, listen thou to me; and understand the words that I speak.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PROVERBS 5:8; ISAIAH 53:6; PROVERBS 6:25"> Lest thy soul be drawn away in the ways of her; neither be thou deceived in the paths of her. (Lest thy life be drawn away by her ways; go thou not forth on her deceptive paths.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:11; NEHEMIAH 13:26; 1ST KINGS 11:1–2"> For she hath cast down many wounded men; and all &#91;the&#93; strongest men were slain of her. (For she hath wounded, and cast down, many men; yea, even the strongest men have been slain by her.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PROVERBS 5:5; PROVERBS 9:18; PROVERBS 2:18–19"> The ways of hell is her house; and pierce(th) into the inner things of death. (Her house is the way, or the entrance, to Sheol or to hell; yea, it leadeth down to the land of the dead.)</span>
