<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:20; PROVERBS 1:8; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:5"> The words of Lemuel, the king; the vision by which his mother taught him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 49:15; 1ST SAMUEL 1:11; 1ST SAMUEL 1:28"> What, my darling? what, the darling of my womb? what, the darling of my desires? (What, my darling? what is it, the darling of my womb? what is it, the answer to my prayers?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:17; NEHEMIAH 13:26; PROVERBS 7:26–27"> Give thou not thy chattel to women (or Give thou not all thy substance to women), and thy riches to do away kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:1; ECCLESIASTES 10:17; ISAIAH 5:22"> A! Lemuel, do not thou give wine to kings; for no private there is, where drunkenness reigneth. (O Lemuel! do not thou give wine to kings; for there is no secret place, where drunkenness can reign.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:5"> Lest peradventure they drink, and forget dooms, and change the cause of the sons of a poor man. (Lest perhaps they drink, and forget justice, and pervert the cause of the poor.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 5:23; PSALMS 104:15; 1ST SAMUEL 1:10"> Give ye cider to them that mourn, and wine to them that be of bitter soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:18"> Drink they, and forget they their neediness; and think they no more on their sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 82:3–4; PROVERBS 24:11–12; ESTHER 4:13–16"> Open thy mouth for a dumb man, and open thy mouth for the causes of all sons that pass forth. (Speak thou for the dumb, and for the causes of all those who pass forth before thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:17; LEVITICUS 19:15; AMOS 5:11–12"> Deem thou that that is just, and deem thou a needy man and a poor man. (Judge thou with judgement, or with discernment, and give thou justice to the needy and to the poor.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:14; PROVERBS 12:4; PROVERBS 18:22"> Who shall find a strong woman? the price of her is far, and from the last ends. (Who shall find a woman of virtue? her value is far above anything else.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:1–7; 2ND KI 4:22–23; 2ND KI 4:9–10"> The heart of her husband trusteth in her; and he shall not have need to robberies. (Her husband trusteth her in his heart; and he shall have no need for robbery, or for theft.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 25:18–22; 1ST SAMUEL 25:26–27"> She shall yield to him good, and not evil, in all the days of her life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TITUS 2:5; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:10; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:10–12"> She sought wool and flax; and wrought by the counsel of her hands. (She sought out wool and flax; and skillfully worked them with her hands.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 31:24; 1ST KINGS 9:26–28; EZEKIEL 27:3–36"> She is made as the ship of a merchant (or She is made like a merchant’s ship), that beareth his bread from &#91;a&#93; far.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ROMANS 12:11; MARK 1:35; LUKE 12:42"> And she rose by night, and gave lifelode to her menials, and meats to her handmaidens. (And she arose at night, and gave sustenance to her servants, and food to her servantesses.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:44; SONG OF SONGS 8:12; JOSHUA 15:18"> She beheld a field, and bought it; of the fruit of her hands she planted a vinery. (She saw a field, and bought it; and from the fruit of her hands, or out of her earnings, she planted a vineyard.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOB 38:3; 1ST PETER 1:13; EPHESIANS 6:10"> She girded her loins with strength, and made strong her arm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 2:9; MATTHEW 25:3–10; PSALMS 127:2"> She tasted, and saw, that her merchandise was good; her lantern shall not be quenched in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 35:25–26"> She put her hands to the wharve, and her fingers took the spindle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:28; HEBREWS 13:16; PROVERBS 22:9"> She opened her hand to the needy man, and stretched forth her hands to a poor man. (She opened her hands to the needy, and stretched forth her hands to the poor.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 45:22; 2ND SAMUEL 1:24; PROVERBS 25:20"> She shall not dread for her house of the colds of snow (or She shall not have fear for her household concerning the cold and the snow); for all her menials be clothed with double clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:8; PROVERBS 7:16; GENESIS 41:42"> She made to her a ray-cloth; bis, either white silk, and purple is the cloth of her. (She made a ray-cloth for herself; bis, or white silk, and purple maketh her cloak.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:4; RUTH 4:1; DEUTERONOMY 16:18"> Her husband is noble in the gates, when he sitteth with the senators of &#91;the&#93; earth. (Her husband is well-regarded at the city gates, when he sitteth with the elders of the land.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:16; LUKE 16:19; PROVERBS 31:19"> She made linen cloth, and sold it; and gave a girdle to a merchant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOB 40:10; ROMANS 13:14; JOB 29:14"> Strength and fairness is the clothing of her; and she shall laugh in the last day. (Strength and beauty be her clothing; and she shall laugh at tomorrow or and she shall laugh on the last day, as she did on this day.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:29; PROVERBS 16:24; PROVERBS 10:31"> She opened her mouth to wisdom; and the law of mercy is in her tongue. (She opened her mouth with wisdom; and the law of love, or of kindness, is upon her tongue.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 5:10; TITUS 2:4; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:6"> She beheld the paths of her house; and she ate not bread idly (or and she did not eat the bread of idleness).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 3:15–17; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:5; 1ST KINGS 2:19"> Her sons rose up, and preached her most blessed; her husband rose(up), and praised her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 6:8–9; EPHESIANS 5:27"> Many daughters gathered riches; thou passedest all. (Many women have gathered in riches; but thou hast surpassed them all.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:7; 1ST PETER 3:4–5; PROVERBS 11:22"> Fairness is deceivable grace, and vain; that woman, that dreadeth the Lord, &#91;she&#93; shall be praised. (Beauty is deceptive, and will not last; but that woman, who feareth the Lord or who revereth the Lord, yea, she shall be praised.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 128:2; HEBREWS 6:10; MATTHEW 7:16"> Give ye to her of the fruit of her hands; and her works praise her in the gates. (Give ye her credit for the fruit of her hands, or for all that she hath done; and may her works bring her praise at the city gates.)</span>
