<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:20–21; MATTHEW 3:3; JOHN 7:37"> Whether wisdom crieth not oft; and prudence giveth her voice? (Whether not wisdom crieth often, and understanding giveth forth her voice?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 9:14; PROVERBS 9:3"> In most sovereign and high tops, above the way, in the midst of paths, (On hilltops, on the way, in the midst of paths,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOHN 18:20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:20; JOB 29:7"> and it standeth beside the gate(s) of the city, in those enclosings, and speaketh, and saith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:15; JOHN 3:16; PSALMS 50:1"> A! ye men, I cry oft to you; and my voice is to the sons of men. (O ye people! I often cry to you; and my words (be) for the sons and daughters of men.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:18; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:17–18"> Little children, that is, little of wit, understand ye wisdom; and ye unwise men, perceiveth in heart. (Ye of little intelligence, understand ye wisdom; and ye who be unwise, think in your hearts.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:26; MATTHEW 7:28–29; PROVERBS 23:16"> Hear ye (or Listen), for I shall speak of great things; and my lips shall be opened, to preach rightful things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 37:30; JOHN 8:14; JOHN 17:17"> My throat shall bethink truth; and my lips shall curse a wicked man. (My mouth shall speak the truth; and my lips shall curse the wicked or for my lips hate wicked words.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 7:46; PSALMS 12:6; PROVERBS 8:13"> My words be just; no shrewd thing, neither wayward is in those. (My words be right, or true; there is nothing depraved, or wicked, in them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:6; JAMES 1:5; JOHN 7:17"> They be rightful to them that understand; and they be even to them that find knowing. (They be right to those who understand; and they be obvious to those who find knowledge.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 2:4–5; PSALMS 119:127; PSALMS 119:72"> Take ye my chastising, and not money, (or Receive, or accept, my discipline, and not money); choose ye teaching, more than treasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:14–15; PROVERBS 4:5–7; PROVERBS 16:16"> For wisdom is better than all riches most precious (or For wisdom is better than all of the most precious riches); and all desirable thing may not be comparisoned thereto.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 28:26; EXODUS 35:30–4; ROMANS 11:33"> I, wisdom, dwell in counsel (or live in good advice); and I am among learned thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 97:10; PROVERBS 16:6; 1ST SAMUEL 2:3"> The dread of the Lord hateth evil; I curse boast, and pride, and a shrewd way, and a double-tongued mouth. (The fear of the Lord is to hate evil or To have reverence for the Lord is to hate evil; I curse boast, and pride, and a depraved way, and speaking with a double-tongue, or with duplicity.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:19; ISAIAH 9:6; ISAIAH 40:14"> Counsel is mine, and equity; prudence is mine, and strength. (Forethought is mine, and fairness, or equality; understanding is mine, and strength.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ROMANS 13:1; DANIEL 2:21; MATTHEW 28:18"> Kings reign by me; and makers of laws deem just things by me. (Kings reign by me; and law-makers justly judge by me or and rulers make just laws by me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Princes command by me; and mighty men deem rightfulness by me. (Leaders command by me; and mighty men judge rightly, or with justice, by me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 14:23; JOHN 14:21; PSALMS 91:14"> I love them that love me; and they that wake early to me, shall find me. (I love them who love me; and they who wake up early to seek me, shall find me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:16; MATTHEW 6:33; LUKE 16:11–12"> With me be riches, and glory; sovereign riches, and rightfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:14; PROVERBS 10:20; ECCLESIASTES 7:12"> My fruit is better than gold, and precious stone(s); and my seeds &#91;or burgeonings&#93;be better than chosen silver (or and my recompense is better than choice silver).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:32; JOHN 10:27–28; PSALMS 32:8"> I go in the ways of rightfulness, in the midst of the paths of doom; (I go on the ways of righteousness, in the midst of the paths of justice;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST PETER 1:4; PROVERBS 24:4; MATTHEW 25:46"> that I make rich them that love me (or so that I make rich those who love me), and that I fill their treasures/their treasuries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:17; JOHN 1:1–2; PROVERBS 3:19"> The Lord wielded me in the beginning of his ways (or The Lord created me at the beginning of his ways); before that he made anything, (yea), at the (very) beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOHN 17:24; PSALMS 2:6; GENESIS 1:26"> From without beginning I was ordained; and from eld times, before that the earth was made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:20; GENESIS 1:2; 1ST JOHN 4:9"> &#91;The&#93; Depths of waters were not yet (made); and I was conceived then. The wells of waters had not broken out yet (or The water wells had not yet broken forth),</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 90:2; PSALMS 102:25–28; JOB 38:4–11"> and hills stood not (al)together yet by firm heaviness; before little hills I was born. (and the mountains did not yet stand with firm heaviness; yea, I was born before the little hills.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 1:1–31"> Yet he had not made &#91;the&#93; earth; and floods, and the earths of the world. (He had not yet made the earth; and the rivers, and the dust of the world.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:22; JOB 26:10; PROVERBS 3:19"> When he made ready heavens, I was present, (or When he prepared the heavens, I was there); (and) when he compassed, &#91;or (en)closed&#93;, the depths of (the) waters by certain law and compass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When he made steadfast the air above; and weighed the wells of waters (or and set in place the water wells).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 104:9; JEREMIAH 5:22; GENESIS 1:9–10"> When he compassed to the sea his mark (or When he set the boundaries for the seas); and setted law to (the) waters, that they should not pass their coasts. When he weighed the foundaments of &#91;the&#93; earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOHN 1:1–3; JOHN 1:18; ISAIAH 42:1"> I was making all (these) things with him. And I delighted by all days, and played before him in all time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 16:3; JOHN 13:1; PSALMS 40:6–8"> and I played in the world; and my delights be to be with the sons of men (or and my delight is to be with the sons and daughters of men).</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LUKE 11:28; PSALMS 119:1–2; PSALMS 1:1–4"> Now, therefore, sons, hear ye me; blessed be they that keep my ways. (And so now, sons and daughters, listen ye to me; blessed be those who follow my ways.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PROVERBS 4:1; PSALMS 81:11–12; PROVERBS 5:1"> Hear ye teaching, and be ye wise men; and do not ye cast it away. (Listen ye to my teaching, and be ye wise; and do not ye throw it away.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LUKE 11:28; MATTHEW 7:24; PROVERBS 3:13"> Blessed is the man that heareth me, and that watcheth at my gates all day, (or Happy is the person who listeneth to me, and who standeth watch at my gates all day long); and keepeth (guard) at the posts of my door&#91;s&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JOHN 3:36; 1ST JOHN 5:11–12; PROVERBS 12:2"> He that findeth me, shall find life; and he shall draw health of the Lord. (He who findeth me, shall find life; and he shall obtain favour from the Lord.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:32; PROVERBS 1:31; 1ST CORINTHIANS 16:22"> But he that sinneth against me shall hurt his soul; all that hate me, love death. (But he who sinneth against me shall hurt his own soul; all who hate me, love death.)</span>
