<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 31:10–31; PROVERBS 24:3–4; PROVERBS 21:9"> A wise woman buildeth her house; and an unwise woman shall destroy with hands, that is, with her evil works, an house builded. (A wise woman buildeth up her own house; but an unwise woman shall destroy with her own hands, that is, with her own evil works, the house that she hath built.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 3:2–3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:35; PROVERBS 28:6"> A man going in rightful way, and dreading God, is despised of him that goeth in the way of evil fame. (A person who doeth right, and feareth God or and revereth God, is despised by him who goeth in evil ways.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:6; JAMES 3:5–6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 12:11"> The rod of pride is in the mouth of a fool; the lips of wise men keep them (or but the lips of the wise keep them safe).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 9:9–11; PROVERBS 13:23; AMOS 4:6"> Where oxen be not, the cratch is void (or the stall is empty); but where full many corns appear, there the strength of the ox is &#91;made&#93; open.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:19; PROVERBS 19:5; EXODUS 23:1"> A faithful witness shall not lie; a guileful witness bringeth forth a leasing (or a deceitful witness bringeth forth many lies).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JAMES 1:5; MATTHEW 13:11–12; MATTHEW 11:25–27"> A scorner seeketh wisdom, and he findeth it not, (or A mocker seeketh wisdom, but never findeth it); the teaching of prudent men is easy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:20; EPHESIANS 5:11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:11"> Go thou against a man a fool; and he shall not know the lips of prudence. (Go thou away from a foolish person; for he shall not speak words of understanding, that is, sensible words.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:17; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:13; JAMES 3:13"> The wisdom of a fell man is to understand his way; and the unwariness of fools erreth. (The wisdom of a clever person is to understand his own way; but the carelessness of fools maketh them to err.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 26:18–19; PROVERBS 10:23; ROMANS 14:17–18"> A fool scorneth sin; grace shall dwell among just men. (A fool mocketh sin; favour shall dwell among the righteous.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:7; PROVERBS 18:14; PROVERBS 15:13"> The heart that knoweth the bitterness of his soul; a stranger shall not be meddled in the joy thereof. (The heart knoweth the bitterness of its own soul; a stranger cannot be mixed in, or mingled, with its joy.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:33; PROVERBS 12:7; MATTHEW 7:26–27"> The house of wicked men shall be done away; the tabernacles of just men shall burgeon. (The houses of the wicked shall be done away; but the homes of the righteous shall flourish.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:25; MATTHEW 7:13–14; PROVERBS 12:15"> Soothly a way is, that seemeth just to a man, (or Truly there is a way, that seemeth right to a person); but the last things thereof lead forth to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:2; JAMES 4:9; ECCLESIASTES 2:10–11"> Laughing shall be meddled with sorrow (or Laughter shall be mixed, or mingled, with sorrow); and mourning occupieth the last things of joy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:8; PROVERBS 12:14; HEBREWS 3:12"> A fool shall be filled with his ways; and a good man shall be above him. (A fool shall reap the fruit of his foolish ways; and a good person that of his deeds, or of his labour.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:17; PROVERBS 14:8; 1ST JOHN 4:1"> An innocent man believeth to each word; a fell man beholdeth his goings. (An innocent person believeth each and every word; a clever person regardeth, or watcheth, his own steps.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 22:3; PROVERBS 3:7; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:22"> A wise man dreadeth, and boweth away from evil; a fool skippeth over, and trusteth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:29; JAMES 1:19; ECCLESIASTES 7:9"> A man unpatient shall work folly; and a guileful man is odious. (An impatient person will act foolishly; and a deceitful person is odious.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST PETER 1:18; 1ST PETER 5:4; JEREMIAH 44:17"> Little men of wit shall hold folly; and fell men shall abide knowing. (People of little wit, or of low intelligence, shall hold fast to foolishness; but clever people shall gain understanding.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 42:6; MALACHI 4:3; EXODUS 8:8"> Evil men shall lie (down) before good men; and unpious men before the gates of just men. (Evil people shall lie down before good people; and the wicked shall bow before the gates of the righteous.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:4; PROVERBS 10:15; PROVERBS 19:6–7"> A poor man shall be (thought) hateful, yea, (even) to his neighbour; but many men be (the) friends of rich men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:17; PROVERBS 28:27; PROVERBS 11:12"> He that despiseth his neighbour, doeth sin; but he that doeth mercy to a poor man, shall be blessed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:2; JOHN 1:17; PSALMS 61:7"> He that believeth in the Lord, loveth mercy; they err that work evil. Mercy and truth make ready goods (or Mercy and truth bring forth good things);</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:19; PROVERBS 12:24; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:10–12"> abundance shall be in each good work/in every good work. Soothly where full many words be, there neediness is oft, (or Truly, where there be a great many words, there often is neediness, or lack, or want).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:11–12; LUKE 12:19–20; LUKE 16:19–25"> The crown of wise men is the riches of them; the folly of fools is unwariness. (The reward of the wise is their riches; the foolishness of fools is their recklessness.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:16–20; 2ND PETER 3:3"> A faithful witness delivereth souls; and a false man bringeth forth leasings. (A faithful witness saveth lives; but a false accuser bringeth forth only lies.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:23; JEREMIAH 32:39–40; PSALMS 115:13–14"> In the dread of the Lord is trust of strength; and hope shall be to the sons of him. (He who feareth the Lord or He who revereth the Lord hath trust in his strength; and his sons and daughters shall have hope and security.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:6; PROVERBS 13:14; PROVERBS 2:10–18"> The dread of the Lord is a well of life; that it bow away from the falling of death. (The fear of the Lord or Reverence for the Lord is a well of life; yea, so that thou turn away from deadly traps, or snares.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1ST KINGS 4:20–21; 2ND KI 13:7; EXODUS 1:22"> The dignity of the king is in the multitude of (his) people; and the shame of a prince is in the fewness of (his) people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JAMES 1:19; ECCLESIASTES 7:9; PROVERBS 14:17"> He that is patient, is governed by much wisdom; but he that is unpatient, enhanceth his folly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:22; PROVERBS 4:23; PROVERBS 3:8"> (The) Health of (the) heart is the life of (the) flesh; envy is (the) rot of (the) bones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PROVERBS 22:2; PROVERBS 17:5; PROVERBS 22:16"> He that falsely challengeth a needy man, despiseth his maker; but he that hath mercy on a poor man, honoureth his maker. (He who oppresseth the needy, despiseth his Maker; but he who hath mercy on the poor, honoureth his Maker.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 4:18; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:8; PSALMS 23:4"> A wicked man is put out for his malice; but a just man hopeth in his death. (A wicked person is brought down by his own malice; but a righteous person hath hope in his death.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:16; PROVERBS 13:16; PROVERBS 12:23"> Wisdom resteth in the heart of a wise man; and he shall teach all unlearned men. (Wisdom resteth in the hearts of the wise; and they shall teach all the unlearned.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 107:34; DEUTERONOMY 28:1–68; DEUTERONOMY 29:18–28"> Rightfulness raiseth &#91;up&#93; a folk; sin maketh peoples wretches. (Uprightness raiseth up the nation; sin maketh people into wretches.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:45–51; LUKE 12:42–48; MATTHEW 25:23"> A servant understanding is acceptable to a king; a servant unprofitable shall suffer the wrathfulness of him. (An understanding servant shall receive the king’s favour; an unprofitableservant shall suffer his wrath.)</span>
</div>
</div>
