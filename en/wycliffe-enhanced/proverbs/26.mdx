<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:10; PROVERBS 28:16; ESTHER 4:6"> As snow in summer, and rain in harvest; so glory is unseemly to a fool. (Like snow in summer, and rain at harvest; so glory is unbecoming to a fool.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:8; NEHEMIAH 13:2; PSALMS 109:28"> For as a bird flying over to high things, and as a sparrow going into uncertain; so cursing brought forth without reasonable cause shall come above into some man. (Like a bird flying over to high places, and like a sparrow going into uncertainty; so cursing brought forth without a reasonable cause, shall simply go over someone, and not touch them.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:13; PSALMS 32:9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:21"> Beating be to an horse, and a bridle to an ass; and a rod to the back of unprudent men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 26:5; 1ST PETER 3:9; JUDE 1:9"> Answer thou not to a fool after his folly, lest thou be made like him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ROMANS 12:16; MATTHEW 16:1–4; PROVERBS 28:11"> Answer thou (to) a fool after his folly, lest he seem to himself to be wise (or lest he thinketh himself to be wise).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:26; PROVERBS 25:13; PROVERBS 13:17"> (As) An halting man in feet, and drinking wickedness, that is, drink harmful to himself, (is) he that sendeth words by a fond messenger. (Like a person who is lame, and like someone who drinketh a drink that is harmful to himself, is he who sendeth words by a foolish messenger.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 26:9; PSALMS 64:8; PROVERBS 17:7"> As an halting man hath fair legs in vain; so a parable is unseemly in the mouth of fools.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 26:1; PROVERBS 30:22; PROVERBS 19:10"> As he that sendeth a stone into the broad place of the sling; so he that giveth honour to an unwise man. (Like he who sendeth a stone into the broad place of a sling, is he who giveth honour to an unwise person.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 23:35"> As if a thorn groweth in the hand of a drunken man; so is a parable in the mouth of fools. (Like a thorn that groweth in the hand of a drunk, is a parable in the mouth of a fool.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ROMANS 2:6; PROVERBS 11:31"> Doom determineth causes; and he that setteth silence to a fool, assuageth ires. (Judgement decideth a person’s case; and he who telleth a fool to be silent, lesseneth anger.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:22; MATTHEW 12:45; EXODUS 8:15"> As a dog that turneth again to his spewing &#91;or As an hound that turneth again to his vomit&#93;; so is an unprudent man, that rehearseth his folly. (Like a dog that returneth to his vomit, is an imprudent person, who repeateth his own foolishness.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:20; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:18–19; PROVERBS 26:5"> Thou hast seen a man seem wise to himself; an unknowing man shall have hope more than he. (Thou hast seen a person who taketh himself to be wise; but a person without knowledge, yea, a fool, shall have more hope than him.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 22:13; PROVERBS 19:15; PROVERBS 15:19"> A slow man saith, A lion is in the way, a lioness is in the footpaths. (A lazy person saith, A lion is there on the way, a lioness is there on the footpaths!)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:12; PROVERBS 12:27; PROVERBS 12:24"> As a door is turned in his hinges; so a slow man in his bed. (Like a door turning on its hinges, is a lazy person turning in his bed.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:24"> A slow man hideth his hands under his armpit; and he travaileth, if he turneth them up to his mouth. (A lazy person hideth his hands under his armpit; and he laboureth, if he turneth them up to his mouth.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 26:12; 1ST PETER 3:15; PROVERBS 12:15"> A slow man seemeth wiser to himself, than seven men speaking sentences. (A lazy person seemeth wiser to himself, than seven people speaking forth their thoughts.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:3; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:23–24; LUKE 12:14"> As he that taketh a dog by the ears; so he that passeth, and is unpatient, and is meddled with the chiding of another man. (Like he who taketh a dog by the ears, is he who passeth by, and is impatient, and is mixed in, or mingled, with the argument of another man.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 25:18; ISAIAH 50:11; PROVERBS 7:23"> As he is guilty, that sendeth spears and arrows into death, (Like he is guilty, who sendeth spears and arrows into uncertainty, causing death,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:4; PROVERBS 10:23; PROVERBS 15:21"> so a man that harmeth guilefully his friend, and when he is taken, he shall say, I did playing. (is a person who deceitfully harmeth his friend, and when he is caught, he saith, I was just playing.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 22:10; JAMES 3:6; PROVERBS 16:28"> When trees fail &#91;or When woods shall fail&#93;, the fire shall be quenched; and when a privy backbiter is withdrawn, strives rest. (When there is no more wood, the fire shall be quenched; and when a gossip departeth, or stoppeth speaking, the argument shall be ended.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:18; PROVERBS 29:22; 2ND SAMUEL 20:1"> As dead coals at quick coals, and trees at the fire, &#91;or As dead coals to quick coals, and wood to fire&#93;; so a wrathful man (that) raiseth chidings. (Like dead coals to burning coals, and wood to fire, is an angry person who raiseth up arguments, or strife.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:8; PROVERBS 20:19; EZEKIEL 22:9"> The words of a privy backbiter be as simple (or The words of a gossip be tasty); and those come till to the innerest things of the heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKE 22:47–48; LUKE 11:39; PROVERBS 10:18"> As if thou wouldest adorn a vessel of earth, (that is, a cheap pot made out of clay), with the dross of silver, so be swelling lips fellowshipped with a full wicked heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:20; PROVERBS 10:18; PSALMS 41:6"> An enemy is understood by his lips, when he treateth guiles in his heart. (An enemy can be understood by his words, when he treateth deceitfulness in his heart.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:16–19; JEREMIAH 12:6; PSALMS 28:3"> When he maketh low his voice, believe thou not to him; for seven wickednesses be in his heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 55:21–23; 1ST SAMUEL 18:17; 1ST SAMUEL 18:21"> The malice of him that covereth hatred guilefully, shall be showed in a council. (The malice of one who deceitfully covereth hatred, shall be shown before the assembly.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:10; PSALMS 9:15; PSALMS 7:15–16"> He that delveth a ditch, shall fall into it; and if a man walloweth a stone, it shall turn again (on)to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOHN 8:44–49; LUKE 20:20–21; JOHN 10:32–33"> A false tongue loveth not &#91;the&#93; truth; and a slippery mouth worketh fallings.</span>
</div>
</div>
