<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 22:26; PROVERBS 17:18; PROVERBS 20:16"> My son, if thou hast promised for thy friend, thou hast fastened thine hand at a stranger. (My son, if thou hast pledged yourself, or made a guarantee, for thy friend, then thou hast fastened thy hand to a stranger.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:7; PROVERBS 12:13"> Thou art bound by the words of thy mouth; and thou art taken with thine own words (or and thou art caught by thy own words, or thy promises).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JAMES 4:10; PSALMS 31:8; 2ND SAMUEL 24:14"> Therefore, my son, do thou that that I say, and deliver thyself; for thou hast fallen into the hand of thy neighbour. Run thou about, haste thou, raise thy friend; (And so my son, do thou what I say, and save thyself; for thou hast fallen into the hand of thy neighbour. Run thou about, haste thou, implore thy friend;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 132:4; PROVERBS 6:10–11; MARK 13:35–36"> give thou not sleep to thine eyes, neither (let) thine eyelids nap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 91:3; PSALMS 124:7; PROVERBS 1:17"> Be thou ravished, as a doe from the hand; and as a bird from &#91;the&#93; ambushings of the fowler. (Be thou released, like a doe from the hand of the hunter; and like a bird from the ambush of the fowler.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:4; PROVERBS 18:9; PROVERBS 6:9"> O! thou slow man (or O thou lazy one!), go to the ant; and behold thou his ways, and learn thou wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOB 38:39–12; PROVERBS 30:27; JOB 39:26–30"> Which when he hath no duke, neither commander, nor prince (nor leader);</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:5; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:19; PROVERBS 30:25"> maketh ready in summer meat to himself, and gathereth together in harvest that, that he shall eat. (prepareth food for himself in the summer, and gathereth together at harvest time what he shall eat.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 24:33–34; JEREMIAH 4:14; EPHESIANS 5:14"> How long shalt thou, slow man, sleep? (or How long shalt thou, O lazy one, sleep?) when shalt thou rise from thy sleep?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 24:33–34; PROVERBS 6:6; PROVERBS 23:33–34"> A little thou shalt sleep, a little thou shalt nap; (and) a little thou shalt join together thine hands that thou sleep (again).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:4; PROVERBS 10:4; PROVERBS 24:34"> And then neediness, as a waygoer, shall come to thee; and poverty, as an armed man. Forsooth if thou art not slow (or But if thou art not lazy), thy ripe corn shall come as a well; and neediness shall flee far from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:34; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:13; PROVERBS 16:27"> A man apostate, is a man unprofitable, he goeth with a wayward mouth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 35:19; PROVERBS 10:10; JOB 15:12"> he beckoneth with (the) eyes, he trampeth with the foot, he speaketh with the finger,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ROMANS 16:17; MICAH 2:1; HOSEA 8:7"> by shrewd heart he imagineth evil, and in all time he soweth dissensions. (with a depraved heart he imagineth all kinds of evil, and at all times he soweth discord.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 36:16; JEREMIAH 19:11; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:3"> His perdition shall come to him anon, and he shall be broken suddenly; and he shall no more have medicine. (His perdition shall come upon him at once, and suddenly he shall be broken; and then he shall be without any remedy.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 11:5; PROVERBS 8:13; PROVERBS 11:20"> Six things there be, which the Lord hateth; and his soul curseth the seventh thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 120:2–3; PROVERBS 12:22; PSALMS 101:5"> High eyes, a tongue liar, that is, accustomable to deadly leasing, (or a lying tongue, that is, one accustomed to deadly lies), hands shedding out innocent blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 6:5; PROVERBS 1:16; ROMANS 3:15"> an heart imagining full wicked thoughts, feet swift to run into evil (or feet quick to run to evil),</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:5; PROVERBS 6:14; JAMES 3:18"> a man bringing forth leasing, a false witness; and him that soweth discord among brethren. (a liar, that is, a false witness; and he who soweth discord among brothers, or among friends.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:1; DEUTERONOMY 27:16; PROVERBS 7:1–4"> My son, keep the commandments of thy father; and forsake &#91;thou&#93; not the law of thy mother. (My son, obey thy father’s commands; and do not thou abandon thy mother’s instructions.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:3; PROVERBS 7:3–4; 2ND CORINTHIANS 3:3"> Bind thou those continually in thine heart; and encompass to thy throat. (Bind thou them continually to thy heart; and tie them about thy neck.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 119:11; PROVERBS 3:23–24; PSALMS 119:24"> When thou goest, go they with thee; when thou sleepest, keep they thee (safe); and thou waking, speak with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 119:105; 2ND PETER 1:19; PSALMS 19:8"> For the commandment of God is a lantern, and the law is light, and the blaming of teaching is the way of life; (For the commandment of God is a lantern, and the Law is a light, and the rebukes of discipline pointeth to the way of life;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 2:16; PROVERBS 7:5; ECCLESIASTES 7:26"> (so) that they keep thee from an evil woman, and from a flattering tongue of a strange woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JAMES 1:14–15; MATTHEW 5:28; 2ND KI 9:30"> Thine heart covet not the fairness of her; neither be thou taken (in) by the beckonings of her. (Do not let thy heart desire her beauty; nor be thou taken in by her beckonings.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LUKE 15:30; PROVERBS 29:3; 1ST SAMUEL 2:36"> For the price of a whore is scarcely a gobbet of bread; but (such) a woman taketh (away) the precious soul of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOB 31:9–12; HOSEA 7:4–7; JAMES 3:5"> Whether a man may hide fire in his bosom, that his clothes burn not; (Can a man hide fire in his bosom, and his clothes not burn?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> either go on coals, and his feet be not burnt? (or go upon coals, and his feet not be burned?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:1; MALACHI 3:5; 2ND SAMUEL 12:9–10"> So he that entereth to the wife of his neighbour; (he) shall not be clean, when he hath touched her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOB 38:39"> It is no great sin, when a man stealeth; for he stealeth to fill an hungry soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LUKE 19:8; JOB 20:18; 2ND SAMUEL 12:6"> And he taken shall yield the seventhfold (or And if he is caught, he shall give back sevenfold); and he shall give all the chattel of his house, and (so he) shall deliver himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PROVERBS 7:7; HEBREWS 13:4; ROMANS 1:22–24"> But he that is an adulterer, shall lose his soul, for the poverty of heart, that is, (for the) wanting of reason.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PROVERBS 5:9–11; PSALMS 51:8; JUDGES 16:19–21"> He gathereth filth, and scandal to himself; and his shame shall not be done away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:4; NUMBERS 5:14; SONG OF SONGS 8:6"> For the fervent love and strong vengeance of the man shall not spare in the day of vengeance, (For the husband’s fervent love, and desire for revenge, shall not spare him on the day of vengeance,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PROVERBS 8:25; MALACHI 2:9; PROVERBS 7:13"> neither shall (the husband) assent to the prayers of any; neither he shall take many gifts for ransom (nor shall he take any number of gifts for recompense).</span>
