<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="wycliffe-enhanced" data-translation-id="eng_wyc2018" data-translation-label="Wycliffe (Enhanced)" data-book="PRO" data-book-slug="proverbs" data-book-name="PROVERBS" data-chapter="13" data-verses="25">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:5; PROVERBS 9:7–8; PROVERBS 15:20"> A wise son is the teaching of the father; but he that is a scorner, heareth not, when he is reproved. (A wise sonlisteneth to his father’s teaching; but he who is a mocker, heareth not, when he is rebuked.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:14; PROVERBS 18:20; PROVERBS 10:11"> A man shall be filled with goods of the fruit of his mouth; but the soul of unpious men is wicked. (A good person shall be filled with things from the fruit of his mouth; but the souls of evil people be wicked.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:23; PROVERBS 18:21; PROVERBS 18:7"> He that keepeth his mouth, keepeth his soul; but he that is unwary to speak, shall feel evils. (He who guardeth his mouth, keepeth his soul safe; but he who is careless when he speaketh, shall experience evil.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:24; PROVERBS 12:11; 2ND PETER 1:5–11"> A slow man will, and will not; but the soul of them that work shall be made fat. (A lazy person desireth, and desireth not; but those who do work shall prosper.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:9; EPHESIANS 4:25; PSALMS 119:163"> A just man shall loathe a false word; but a wicked man shameth, and shall be shamed. (The righteous loathe a lie; but the wicked shame, and shall be shamed.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:3; PROVERBS 11:5–6; PSALMS 140:11"> Rightfulness keepeth (safe) the way of an innocent man; but wickedness deceiveth a sinner.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 18:11–14; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:17; PROVERBS 11:24"> A man is as rich, when he hath nothing; and a man is as poor, when he is in many riches. (A person can be rich, even when he hath nothing; and a person can be poor, even when he hath many riches.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:26; 1ST PETER 1:18–19; PSALMS 49:6–10"> &#91;The&#93; Redemption of the soul of &#91;a&#93; man is his riches; but he that is poor, suffereth not blaming. (The ransom for a man’s lifeis his riches; but he who is poor, shall not even suffer threats.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 4:18; PROVERBS 24:20; PROVERBS 20:20"> The light of just men maketh glad; but the lantern of wicked men shall be quenched. (The light of the righteous maketh happy, or inspireth; but the lantern of the wicked shall be quenched.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:20; PROVERBS 12:15–16; PROVERBS 17:14"> Strives be ever among proud men; but they that do all things with counsel, be governed by wisdom. (There is always strife, or arguments, among the proud; but they who do all things with advice, be governed by wisdom.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:21; PROVERBS 10:2; JAMES 5:1–5"> Hasted chattel, that is, gotten hastily, shall be made less; but that that is gathered little and little with hand, shall be multiplied, (or but that which is gathered little by little, shall be multiplied).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOHN 16:22; PROVERBS 13:19; PSALMS 119:81–83"> Hope which is delayed, tormenteth the soul; a tree of life is (a) desire (that is) coming.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:31; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:16; 2ND JOHN 1:8"> He that backbiteth anything, bindeth himself into &#91;the&#93; time to coming; but he that dreadeth the commandment, shall live in peace. (He who backbiteth for something said to him, bindeth himself to the time to come; but he who feareth the commandment, shall live in peace.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 18:5; PROVERBS 10:11; PROVERBS 14:27"> The law of a wise man is a well of life; that he bow away from the falling of death, that is, of sin, and of hell. (The teaching of the wise is a well of life; so that thou bow away from the falling of death, that is, from sin, and from hell.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:19; PROVERBS 4:19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:10"> Good teaching shall give grace; a swallow is in the way of despisers. (Good teaching shall bring favour; a pit is on the way of despisers.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:2; PROVERBS 12:22–23; EPHESIANS 5:17"> A fell man doeth all things with counsel; but he that is a fool, shall open folly. (A clever person doeth everything with forethought, or only after advice; but he who is a fool, shall lay open his foolishness for all to see.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 25:13; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:2; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:20"> The messenger of a wicked man shall fall into evil; a faithful messenger is health. (A wicked messenger shall fall into evil; but a faithful messenger bringeth health, or victory, or salvation, or deliverance.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:5; PROVERBS 12:1; PROVERBS 15:31–32"> Neediness and shame is to him that forsaketh teaching; but he that assenteth to a blamer, shall be glorified. (Neediness and shame shall be for him who abandoneth his teaching, or his instruction; but he who assenteth to a rebuker, shall be honoured.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:14–17; PROVERBS 13:12; PROVERBS 29:27"> Desire, if it is &#91;ful&#93; filled, delighteth the soul; fools loathe them that flee evils.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:33–34; PROVERBS 15:31; PROVERBS 9:6"> He that goeth with wise men, shall be wise, (or He who goeth with the wise, shall be wise); the friend of fools shall be made like them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 32:10; ISAIAH 3:10–11; ROMANS 2:7–10"> Evil pursueth sinners; and goods shall be given to just men (or but good things shall be given to the righteous).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:26; JOB 27:16–17; PROVERBS 28:8"> A good man shall leave after him heirs, sons, and the sons of sons; and the chattel of a sinner is kept to a just man. (A good person shall leave heirsafter him, yea, sons and daughters, and their sons and daughters; but a sinner’s possessions shall be given to the righteous.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:11; PROVERBS 28:19; ECCLESIASTES 5:9"> Many meats be in the new(ly)-tilled fields of fathers; and be gathered to other men without doom. (Much food for the poor could be in untilled fields; but other men unjustly keep that land from being worked.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:17; PROVERBS 23:13–14; PROVERBS 29:15"> He that spareth the rod, hateth his son; but he that loveth him, teacheth busily, (or but he who loveth him, busily teacheth him).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 34:10; PROVERBS 10:3; HEBREWS 13:5"> A just man eateth, and &#91;ful&#93; filleth his soul; but the womb of wicked men is unable to be filled. (The righteous eat, and fill their hunger; but the bellies of the wicked be unable to be filled.)</span>
</div>
</div>
