<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:15–17; PROVERBS 2:1–6; PROVERBS 20:3"> He that will go away from a friend, seeketh occasions, that is, feigneth causes, (or looketh for reasons); in all time he shall be despisable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:7; PROVERBS 12:23; PSALMS 1:1–2"> A fool receiveth not the words of prudence; no but thou say those things, that be turned (over) in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 22:10; 1ST PETER 4:14; PROVERBS 11:2"> A wicked man, when he cometh into (the) depth of sins, despiseth wholesome lore, (or discipline), and commandment; but scandal and shame followeth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:5; PROVERBS 10:11; COLOSSIANS 4:6"> Deep water is the words of the mouth of a man; and a strand floating over is the well of wisdom. (Words from a person’s mouth (can be) like deep water or (can be) as deep as the water; and the well of wisdom (is) like a stream flowing over.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:15; DEUTERONOMY 16:19; PROVERBS 28:21"> It is not good to take the person of a wicked man in doom, that thou bow away from the truth of doom. (It is not good to favour a wicked person, so that thou turn away from the truth in judgement.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:3; PROVERBS 16:27–28; PROVERBS 22:24–25"> The lips of a fool meddle themselves with chidings; and his mouth stirreth strives. (A fool’s lips mix,or mingle, themselves in with arguments; and his mouth stirreth up strife.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:3; PROVERBS 12:13; PROVERBS 10:14"> The mouth of a fool is&#91;the&#93; defouling of him; and his lips be the falling of his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:18; LEVITICUS 19:16; PROVERBS 16:28"> The words of a double-tongued man be as simple; and they come unto the inner things of the womb. Dread casteth down a slow man; forsooth the souls of men turned into women’s condition shall have hunger. (A gossip’s wordsbe tasty; and they go down into the innermost parts. Fear casteth down the lazy; and those who be timid shall have hunger.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:4; HEBREWS 6:12; PROVERBS 24:30–34"> He that is nesh, and dissolute, either unsteadfast, in his work, is the brother of a man destroying his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 22:3; PSALMS 91:2; ISAIAH 26:4"> A full strong tower is the name of the Lord; a just man runneth to him, and &#91;he&#93; shall be enhanced. (The Lord’s nameis a full strong tower; the righteous run to it, and they shall be safe.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:15; PSALMS 52:5–7; ECCLESIASTES 7:12"> The chattel of a rich man is the city of his strength; and as a strong wall compassing him. (A rich person’s possessions (is) the city of his strength; and (they be) like a strong wall surrounding him, (or so he thinketh).)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:33; 1ST PETER 5:5; EZEKIEL 28:2"> The heart of man is enhanced, before that it be broken; and it is made meek, before that it be glorified. (A person’s heart is raised upin pride, before that it is broken; and it is humbled, before that it is glorified.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 7:51; DEUTERONOMY 13:14; PROVERBS 20:25"> He that answereth before that he heareth, showeth himself to be a fool; and worthy of shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:13; PROVERBS 17:22; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:9–10"> The spirit of a man sustaineth (him in) his feebleness; but who may sustain a spirit light to be wroth? (A person’s spirit can sustain him in sickness; but who can endure with a broken spirit?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JAMES 1:5; PROVERBS 15:14; PROVERBS 1:5"> The heart of a prudent man shall hold steadfastly knowing; and the ear of wise men seeketh teaching. (The heart of a person with understanding shall steadfastly hold onto knowledge; and the ears of the wise seek out teaching, or instruction.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:6; GENESIS 43:11; PROVERBS 17:8"> The gift of a man alargeth his way; and maketh space to him before princes. (Giving a gift openeth, (or smootheth), one’s way; and maketh an opening for thee among the leaders, (or among the powerful).)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:12–13; 2ND SAMUEL 19:24–27"> A just man is the first accuser of himself; his friend cometh, and shall search him. (He who is the first to speak for a cause seemeth right; but then his opponent shall come, and shall search him out.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:33; NEHEMIAH 11:1; 1ST CHRONICLES 24:31"> Lot ceaseth against-sayings; and deemeth also among mighty men. (Casting lots ceaseth arguments; and also judgeth, or decideth, among the mighty.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:32; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:39; PROVERBS 6:19"> A brother that is helped of a brother, is as a steadfast city; and &#91;the&#93; dooms be as the bars of cities. (A brother helped by a brother, is like protection from the wall of a steadfast city; but arguments be like the bars of a city, preventing entry.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:13–14; PROVERBS 25:11–12; PROVERBS 13:2"> A man’s womb shall be &#91;ful&#93; filled of the fruit of his mouth (or A man’s belly shall be fulfilled,or satisfied, by the fruit of his mouth); and the seeds of his lips shall fill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:29; MATTHEW 12:35–37; COLOSSIANS 4:6"> Death and life be in the works of &#91;the&#93; tongue; they that love it, shall eat the fruits thereof, (or they who nurture it, shall eat its fruits).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:14; PROVERBS 31:10–31; PROVERBS 12:4"> He that findeth a good woman, findeth a good thing; and of the Lord he shall draw up mirth (or and he receiveth favour from the Lord). He that putteth away a good woman, putteth away a good thing; but he that holdeth (onto an) adulteress, is a fool and unwise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JAMES 2:3; JAMES 1:9–11; RUTH 2:7"> A poor man shall speak with beseechings, (or with pleadings); and a rich man shall speak sternly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:17; JOHN 15:13–15; PROVERBS 27:9"> A man friendly to fellowship shall more be a friend, than a brother. (A man given to fellowship with thee, can be more of a friend, than even thy own brother.)</span>
