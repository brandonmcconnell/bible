<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:12; PROVERBS 25:15; PROVERBS 15:18"> A soft answer breaketh ire; an hard word raiseth strong vengeance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:23; PROVERBS 15:28; PROVERBS 13:16"> The tongue of wise men honoureth knowing (or The speaking of the wise honoureth knowledge); the mouth of fools boileth out folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:13; JEREMIAH 16:17; JEREMIAH 23:24"> In each place the eyes of the Lord behold good men, and evil men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:18; PROVERBS 16:24; PROVERBS 18:8"> A pleasant tongue is the tree of life; but the tongue that is unmeasurable &#91;or untempered&#93;, shall defoul the spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:31–32; PROVERBS 13:1; PROVERBS 13:18"> A fool scorneth the teaching of his father; but he that keepeth blamings (or but he who receiveth rebukes), shall be made wiser. Most virtue shall be in plenteous rightfulness; but the thoughts of wicked men shall be drawn up by the root.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 8:21; PROVERBS 21:20; PROVERBS 10:22"> (In) The house of a just man is full great strength; and troubling is in the fruits of a wicked man. (In the house of the righteous is very great wealth; and trouble is in the fruits of the wicked.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:34; JAMES 3:6; EPHESIANS 4:29"> The lips of wise men shall sow abroad knowing (or The lips of the wise sow abroad knowledge); (but) the heart(s) of fools shall be unlike (them, that is, they shall be full of ignorance).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:29; PROVERBS 21:27; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:17"> The sacrifices of wicked men be abominable to the Lord; the avows of just men be pleasant. (The sacrifices of the wicked be abominable to the Lord; but the vows of the righteous be pleasing to him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 6:11; PROVERBS 21:21; MATTHEW 7:13"> The life of the unpious man is (an) abomination to the Lord; he that followeth rightfulness, shall be loved of the Lord. (The life of an evil person is an abomination to the Lord; but he who followeth righteousness, shall be loved by the Lord.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:1; JOHN 3:20; PROVERBS 13:1"> Evil teaching is of men that forsake the way of life; he that hateth blamings shall die. (Evil teaching is from those who abandon the way of life; he who hateth rebukes shall die.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 44:21; HEBREWS 4:13; PSALMS 139:8"> Hell and perdition be open before the Lord; how much more the hearts of &#91;the&#93; sons (and daughters) of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="AMOS 5:10; JOB 21:14; PROVERBS 9:7–8"> A man full of pestilence loveth not him that reproveth him; and he goeth not to wise men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:22; JOHN 14:1; PROVERBS 18:14"> A joyful heart maketh glad the face; (but) the spirit is cast down in the mourning of (the) soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 9:9; ISAIAH 30:10; PROVERBS 18:15"> The heart of a wise man seeketh teaching; and the mouth of fools is fed with unknowing, (or with ignorance).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 6:10; ROMANS 12:12; PROVERBS 15:13"> All the days of a poor man be evil; a secure soul is a continual feast. (All the days of the poor be evil; the life of a secure soul is a continual feast.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:8; PSALMS 37:16; ECCLESIASTES 5:10–12"> Better is a little with the dread of the Lord, than many treasures and unfillable. (Better is a little with the fear of the Lord or with reverence for the Lord, than many treasures and troubles.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:1; PROVERBS 21:19; 1ST JOHN 4:16"> It is better to be called to worts with charity, than with hatred to a calf made fat. (It is better to be warmly, or cordially, called to herbs, or vegetables, than to be hatefully invited to share a fattened calf.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:1; PROVERBS 26:21; PROVERBS 29:22"> A wrathful man raiseth chidings; he that is patient, assuageth chidings that were raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 35:8; PROVERBS 22:5; ISAIAH 30:21"> The way of slow men is as an hedge of thorns; the way of just men is without &#91;thing of&#93; hurting. (A lazy person’s way (is) a hedge of thorns; but the way of the righteous (is) without a cause of stumbling.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:3; PROVERBS 10:1; PROVERBS 23:15–16"> A wise son maketh glad his father; but a fond man despiseth his mother. (A wise son maketh his father happy; but a fool despiseth his mother.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:15; PROVERBS 10:23; PROVERBS 14:16"> Folly is joy to a fool; but a prudent man shall (ad)dress his steps. (Foolishness bringeth joy to a fool; but a prudent person shall direct his steps.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:14; PROVERBS 20:18; ECCLESIASTES 8:6"> Thoughts be destroyed, where no counsel is; but where many counsellors be, they be confirmed. (Plans come to naught, where there is no wise advice; but where there be many counsellors, the plans succeed.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ISAIAH 50:4; PROVERBS 25:11–12; EPHESIANS 4:29"> A man is glad in the sentence of his mouth; and a covenable word is best (or but a suitable word is always best).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:1–2; PSALMS 16:11; MATTHEW 7:14"> The path of life is on a learned man; that he bow away from the last hell. (The path of life leadeth upward for the learned; yea, so that they turn away from Sheol, or the land of the dead, or so that they turn away from hell, which is below.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 146:9; PSALMS 68:5–6; PROVERBS 14:11"> The Lord shall destroy the house of proud men; and he shall make steadfast the coasts of a widow. (The Lord shall destroy the house of the proud; but he shall make firm the property lines, or the boundary stones, of a widow.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:16–19; JEREMIAH 4:14; PROVERBS 15:23"> Evil thoughts is abomination of the Lord; and a clean word most fair shall be made steadfast of him. (Evil thoughts be an abomination to the Lord; but a most fair, and clean, word is his delight.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EXODUS 23:8; PROVERBS 1:19; JEREMIAH 17:11"> He that followeth avarice, troubleth his house; but he that hateth gifts shall live. Sins be purged by mercy and faith; each man boweth away from evil by the dread of the Lord, (or Sins be cleansed by love and faith; all can turn away from evil by the fear of the Lord or with reverence for the Lord).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:15; PROVERBS 29:11; ECCLESIASTES 5:2"> The soul of a just man bethinketh obedience; the mouth of wicked men is full of evils. (The righteous think before that they answer; but the ready reply of the wicked is usually full of evil.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOHN 9:31; PSALMS 145:18–19; PROVERBS 15:8"> The Lord is far from wicked men; and he shall hear the prayers of just men. (The Lord is far from the wicked; but he shall hear the prayers of the righteous.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="ISAIAH 58:11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:5; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:23"> The light of (the) eyes maketh glad the soul; good fame maketh fat the bones. (Sparkling eyes make a person happy; good news fatteneth up the bones.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:5; PROVERBS 25:12; JOHN 15:3–4"> The ear that heareth the blamings of life, shall dwell in the midst of wise men. (The person who listeneth to rebukes, shall live in the midst of the wise.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:24–33; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:19; PROVERBS 1:7"> He that casteth away chastising, despiseth his soul; but he that assenteth to blamings, is a peaceable holder of the heart. (He who casteth away discipline, despiseth his own soul; but he who accepteth rebukes, shall become wiser.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:5; PROVERBS 18:12; PROVERBS 1:7"> The dread of the Lord is teaching of wisdom; and meekness goeth before glory. (The fear of the Lord or Reverence for the Lord is the teaching of wisdom; and humility goeth before honour.)</span>
