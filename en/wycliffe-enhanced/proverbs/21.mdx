<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:9; PROVERBS 20:24; EZRA 6:22"> As partings of waters, so the heart of the king is in the power of the Lord; whither ever he will, he shall bow it. (Like out-flowings of water, (is) the king’s heart in the Lord’s hand; he shall turn it wherever he will, (or desireth).)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:2; PROVERBS 16:25; LUKE 16:15"> Each way of a man seemeth rightful to himself; but the Lord weigheth the hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 15:22; HOSEA 6:6; MICAH 6:6–8"> To do mercy and doom, pleaseth more the Lord, than sacrifices. (To do mercy and justice, that pleaseth the Lord more than sacrifices, or burnt offerings.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:17; 1ST PETER 5:5; ISAIAH 2:17"> Enhancing of eyes is &#91;the&#93; alarging of the heart; the lantern of wicked men is sin. (The raising up of the eyes, and the enlarging of the heart; such sins be the lantern of the wicked.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:4; PROVERBS 13:4; EPHESIANS 4:28"> The thoughts of a strong man be ever in abundance; but each slow man is ever in neediness. (The thoughts of a strong person (be) forevermore on abundance, (or on plenty); but those of each person in haste (be) forevermore on neediness./Carefully-made plans shall be successful; but each person in haste shall be forever in need.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:11; PROVERBS 10:2; PROVERBS 20:21"> He that gathereth treasures by the tongue of a lie maker, (or of a liar), is vain, and without heart; and he shall be hurled to the snares of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:21; EPHESIANS 5:6; PROVERBS 1:18–19"> The ravens of unpious men shall draw them down; for they would not do doom. (The robberies of the wicked shall pull them down; for what they do is wrong.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 2:15; 1ST JOHN 2:29; PSALMS 14:2–3"> The wayward way of a man is alien from God; but the work of him that is clean of sin, is rightful. (The evil way of a person is alien to God; but the work of him who is clean of sin, is upright.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:13; PROVERBS 21:19; PROVERBS 27:15–16"> It is better to sit in the corner of an house without roof, than with a woman full of chiding, and in a common house. (It is better to sit in the corner of a house without a roof, than with a woman full of arguments, or of bickering, in a house together.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MICAH 3:2–3; PROVERBS 3:29; PSALMS 36:4"> The soul of an unpious man desireth evil; he shall not have mercy on his neighbour. (The soul of a wicked person desireth evil; he shall not even give mercy to his friend.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:25; PROVERBS 18:15; PROVERBS 9:9"> When a man full of pestilence is punished, a little man of wit shall be wiser (or even someone of little wit, or of low intelligence, shall be made the wiser); and if he followeth a wise man, he shall take knowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:11; PSALMS 37:35–36; 2ND PETER 3:6–7"> A just man of the house of a wicked man thinketh, to withdraw wicked men from evil. (The righteous think about the wicked in their houses, but it is God who shall throw down the wicked for their evil./The just God thinketh about the house of the wicked, and he shall throw down the wicked for their evil.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:27; MATTHEW 6:14; PROVERBS 1:28"> He that stoppeth his ear at the cry of a poor man, shall cry also (or shall also cry), and he shall not be heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:16; PROVERBS 19:6; PROVERBS 17:8"> A gift hid quencheth chidings (or A secret gift quencheth arguments); and a gift in &#91;the&#93; bosom quencheth most indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:29; MATTHEW 7:23; MATTHEW 13:41–42"> It is joy to a just man to make doom; and it is dread to them that work wickedness. (It is a joy for the righteous to see justice done; but judgement bringeth ruin to those who work wickedness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:38; PSALMS 125:5; PSALMS 49:14"> A man that erreth from the way of doctrine, shall dwell in the company of giants, that is, of men evil ruled, either of fiends. (Those who err, or who stray, from the way of doctrine, shall dwell in the company of the dead.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 23:21; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:6; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:4"> He that loveth meats shall be in neediness (or He who loveth too much food shall be in want); he that loveth wine and fat things, shall not be made rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:8; ISAIAH 43:3–4; ISAIAH 53:4–5"> An unpious man shall be given for a just man; and a wicked man shall be given for a rightful man. (An evil person shall be given as a ransom for a righteous person; and a wicked person shall be given as a ransom for an upright person.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:9; PSALMS 120:5–6; PSALMS 55:6–7"> It is better to dwell in a desert land, than with a woman full of chiding, and wrathful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:3–4; PSALMS 112:3; ECCLESIASTES 7:11"> Desirable treasure and oil is in the dwelling place of a just man; and an unprudent man shall destroy it. (Desirable treasure and oil be in the dwelling place of the wise; but an imprudent person shall destroy it all.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:58; PROVERBS 22:4"> He that followeth rightfulness and mercy, shall find life, &#91;and rightwiseness&#93;, and glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:19; PROVERBS 24:5; ECCLESIASTES 9:13–18"> A wise man ascended into the city of (the) strong men, and destroyed the strength of &#91;the&#93; trust thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:3; PROVERBS 18:21; JAMES 1:26"> He that keepeth his mouth and his tongue (under control), keepeth his soul from anguishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 1:1; PROVERBS 18:12; PROVERBS 1:22"> A proud man and (a) boaster is called a fool, (for) that (he) worketh (his) pride in ire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:4; PROVERBS 12:24; MATTHEW 25:26"> Desires slay a slow man; for his hands would not (do) work (of) anything. (Desires shall slay a lazy person; for his hands shall not do any work.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 37:26; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:33–35; EPHESIANS 4:28"> All day he coveteth and desireth; but he that is a just man, shall give, and shall not cease. (All day long he wanteth, and longeth for; but he who is righteous, shall give, and shall never cease to give.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ISAIAH 66:3; PROVERBS 15:8; JEREMIAH 6:20"> The offerings of wicked men (or The offerings of the wicked), that be offered (out) of great trespass, be abominable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 4:13; PROVERBS 19:5; PROVERBS 19:9"> A false witness shall perish; a man obedient shall speak victory (or but an honest person shall speak, and be rewarded).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 3:11; PROVERBS 11:5; HAGGAI 2:18–19"> A wicked man maketh firm his cheer unshamefastly; but he that is rightful, amendeth his way. (A wicked person unashamedly maketh firm his face, or is stubborn; but he who is upright, mendeth his way.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:39; JEREMIAH 9:23"> No wisdom there is, no prudence there is, no counsel there is, against the Lord. (There is no wisdom, no understanding, no good advice, in being against the Lord.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 20:7; ISAIAH 31:1; PSALMS 33:17–18"> An horse is made ready to the day of battle; but the Lord shall give health. (A horse can be prepared for the day of battle; but victory cometh only from the Lord.)</span>
