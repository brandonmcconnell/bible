<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:1; PROVERBS 29:15; PROVERBS 17:25"> The parables of Solomon. A wise son maketh glad his father; but a fond son is the sorrow of his mother. (The proverbs of Solomon. A wise son maketh his father happy; but a foolish son is the sorrow of his mother.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:4; PROVERBS 12:28; PSALMS 49:6–10"> Treasures of wickedness shall not profit; but rightfulness shall deliver from death. (Treasures gotten by wickedness shall not profit thee; but uprightness shall deliver thee from death.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 37:3; MATTHEW 6:30–33; PSALMS 37:25"> The Lord shall not torment the soul of a just man with hunger; and he shall destroy the treasons of unpious men. (The Lord shall not torment the righteous with hunger; but he shall destroy the desires of the wicked.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:5; PROVERBS 20:4; PROVERBS 19:15"> A slow hand hath wrought neediness; but the hand of strong men maketh ready riches. Forsooth he that enforceth to get anything by leasings, feedeth the winds; soothly the same man followeth birds flying. (Lazy hands create neediness; but strong hands create riches. He who endeavoureth to get anything by lying, feedeth the wind; yea, that person runneth after birds in flight.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:8; PROVERBS 30:25; PROVERBS 17:2"> He that gathereth together in harvest, is a wise son; but he that sleepeth in summer, is a son of confusion. (He who gathereth together at harvest time, is wise; but he who sleepeth in summer, is a disgrace.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:2; PROVERBS 28:20; JOB 29:13"> The blessing of God is over the head of a just man; but wickedness covereth the mouth of wicked men. (The blessing of God is upon the heads of the righteous; but wickedness covereth the mouths of the wicked.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 112:6; LUKE 1:48; MARK 14:9"> The mind of a just man shall be with praisings; and the name of wicked men shall wax rotten. (The righteous shall be remembered with praise or The memory of the righteous is blessed; but the names of the wicked shall grow rotten.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:24–25; PROVERBS 12:1; JAMES 3:13"> A wise man shall receive commandments with heart (or A wise person shall take commandments to heart); a fool is beaten with (his own) lips.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:18; MATTHEW 10:26; PSALMS 26:11–12"> He that goeth simply, goeth trustily; but he that maketh shrewd his ways, shall be &#91;made&#93; open. (He who goeth with honesty, or with integrity, goeth with security; but he who maketh his ways depraved, shall be exposed.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 35:19; PROVERBS 6:13; 3RD JOHN 1:10"> He that beckoneth with the eye, shall give sorrow; a fool shall be beaten with (his own) lips.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:14; EPHESIANS 4:29; PROVERBS 10:6"> The vein of life is the mouth of a just man; but the mouth of wicked men covereth wickedness. (The words of the righteous be a fountain of life; but wickedness covereth the mouths of the wicked.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:8; 1ST CORINTHIANS 13:4–7; PROVERBS 17:9"> Hatred raiseth up chidings; and charity covereth all sins. (Hatred raiseth up arguments, or strife; but love covereth all sins.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 26:3; PROVERBS 6:32; ISAIAH 50:4"> Wisdom is found in the lips of a wise man; and a rod in the back of him that is needy of heart. (Wisdom is found on the lips of the wise; and a rod on the back of him who is foolish.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:7; PROVERBS 10:8; PROVERBS 13:3"> Wise men hide knowing (or The wise store up knowledge); but the mouth of a fool is next to confusion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:11; PROVERBS 19:7; PSALMS 52:7"> The chattel of a rich man is the city of his strength; the dread of poor men is the neediness of them. (The substance of a rich man is his strong city; the fear of the poor is their own neediness.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ROMANS 6:23; ISAIAH 3:10–11; HEBREWS 6:10"> The work of a just man is to life; but the fruit of a wicked man is to sin. (The work of a good person serveth unto life; but the fruit of the wicked is sin.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND PETER 1:5–11; LUKE 11:28; PROVERBS 6:23"> The way of life is to him that keepeth chastising; but he that forsaketh blamings, erreth. (The way of life is for him who receiveth discipline; but he who forsaketh rebukes, erreth.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 26:24–26; PSALMS 55:21; 2ND SAMUEL 3:27"> False lips hide hatred; he that bringeth forth despising is unwise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JAMES 1:19; JAMES 3:2; PROVERBS 17:27–28"> Sin shall not fail in much speaking; but he that measureth his lips, is most prudent. (Sin shall abound with much speaking; but he who ruleth his lips, is most prudent.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:9; PROVERBS 12:18; GENESIS 6:5"> Chosen silver is the tongue of a just man; the heart of wicked men is for nought. (Choice silver is like the tongue of a righteous person; but the hearts of the wicked be worth nothing.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HOSEA 4:6; PROVERBS 17:16; PROVERBS 1:29"> The lips of a just man teach full many men; but they that be unlearned shall die in neediness of heart. (The lips of the righteous teach a great many people; but they who be ignorant, shall die without any understanding.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 107:38; GENESIS 26:12; 1ST SAMUEL 2:7–8"> The blessing of the Lord maketh rich men (or The blessing of the Lord maketh people rich); and torment shall not be fellowshipped to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:21; PROVERBS 2:14; PROVERBS 14:9"> A fool worketh wickedness as by laughing; but wisdom is prudence to a man, or but prudence is to a wise man. (A fool worketh wickedness with laughter; but wisdom is a delight to those with understanding.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 145:19; ISAIAH 66:4; MATTHEW 5:6"> That that a wicked man dreadeth, shall come &#91;up&#93; on him; the desire of just men shall be given to them. (That which the wicked fear, shall come upon them; and the desire of the righteous shall be given to them.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 15:5; PSALMS 58:9; MATTHEW 7:24–27"> As a tempest passing, a wicked man shall not be; but a just man shall be as an everlasting foundament. (Like a tempest passing by, a wicked person shall soon not be; but the foundations of the righteous be everlasting.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PROVERBS 25:13; PROVERBS 26:6; MATTHEW 25:26"> As vinegar harmeth the teeth, and smoke harmeth the eyes; so a slow man harmeth them that sent him in the way. (Like vinegar harmeth the teeth, and smoke harmeth the eyes; so a lazy man harmeth those who sent him on the way.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PROVERBS 9:11; PROVERBS 3:2; PSALMS 91:16"> The dread of the Lord increaseth days; and the years of wicked men shall be made short. (The fear of the Lord or Reverence for the Lord increaseth days; and the years of the wicked shall be made short.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOB 8:13; ROMANS 15:13; PSALMS 73:24–26"> Abiding of just men is gladness; but the hope of wicked men shall perish. (Hopeful waiting by the righteous shall lead to gladness; but all the hopes of the wicked shall perish.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:15; ROMANS 2:8–9; PHILIPPIANS 4:13"> The strength of a simple man is the way of the Lord; and dread to them that work evil. (The way of the Lord giveth strength to an honest person; but fear to those who work evil.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 37:22; PSALMS 125:1; PSALMS 37:9–10"> A just man shall not be moved &#91;into&#93; without end; but wicked men shall not dwell on the earth. (The righteous shall not be moved forever; but the wicked shall not remain on the earth, or in the land.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 37:30; PROVERBS 10:13; PROVERBS 10:20–21"> The mouth of a just man shall bring forth wisdom; the tongue of shrews shall perish. (The mouths of the righteous shall bring forth wisdom; and the tongues of the depraved shall be stopped.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:28; PROVERBS 12:6; PROVERBS 11:11"> The lips of a just man behold pleasant things; and the mouth of wicked men beholdeth wayward things. (The lips of the righteous always know what is kind, or pleasant, to say; but the mouths of the wicked speak forth only hurtful things.)</span>
</div>
</div>
