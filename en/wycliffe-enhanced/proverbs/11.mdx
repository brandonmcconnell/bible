<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 25:13–16; PROVERBS 16:11; PROVERBS 20:23"> A guileful balance is abomination with God; and an even weight is his will. (A deceitful scale is an abomination to God; and equal, or honest, weights be his desire.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:23; PROVERBS 15:33; PROVERBS 16:18–19"> Where pride is, there also despising shall be; but where meekness is, there also is wisdom. (Where there is pride, there shall also be despising; but where there is humility, there is also wisdom.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:6; PROVERBS 28:18; PSALMS 26:1"> The simpleness of just men shall (ad)dress them; and the deceiving of wayward men shall destroy them. (The honesty, or the integrity, of the righteous shall direct them; and the deceptions of the wayward shall destroy them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 1:18; PROVERBS 10:2; GENESIS 7:1"> Riches shall not profit in the day of vengeance; but rightfulness shall deliver from death. (Riches shall not profit anyone on the day of vengeance; but uprightness shall deliver one from death.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 5:22; PSALMS 9:15–16; PROVERBS 1:31–32"> The rightfulness of a simple man shall (ad)dress his way; and a wicked man shall fall in his wickedness. (The uprightness of an honest person shall direct his way; and a wicked person shall fall because of his wickedness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 10:8; 1ST KINGS 2:44; PSALMS 7:15–16"> The rightfulness of rightful men shall deliver them; and wicked men shall be taken in their ambushings. (The uprightness of the righteous shall save them; but the wicked shall be caught in their own ambushings.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:28; JOB 8:13–14; JOB 11:20"> When a wicked man is dead, none hope shall be further of him; and &#91;the&#93; abiding of busy men in evil shall perish. (When the wicked die, they shall have no further hope; and the abundance, or the wealth, of those busy with evil shall perish.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DANIEL 6:23–24; PROVERBS 21:18; ESTHER 7:9–10"> A just man is delivered from anguish; and a wicked man shall be given for him. (The righteous person is delivered from all his anguish; and it is given to a wicked person instead.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 2:10–16; PROVERBS 4:5–6; 1ST JOHN 2:27"> A feigner by mouth deceiveth his friend; but just men shall be delivered by knowing. (A faker, or a hypocrite, deceiveth his friend with his mouth; but the righteous rescue others with their wisdom.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:12; ESTHER 8:15–16; PSALMS 58:10–11"> A city shall be enhanced in the goods of just men; and praising shall be in the perdition of wicked men. (A city shall rejoice for the prosperity of the righteous; and praising shall be in the perdition, or in the perishing, of the wicked.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:34; PROVERBS 29:8; JAMES 3:6"> A city shall be enhanced by &#91;the&#93; blessing of just men; and it shall be destroyed by the mouth of wicked men. (A city shall be enhanced by the blessing of the righteous; and it shall be destroyed by the words of the wicked.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:19; 1ST PETER 2:23; LUKE 18:9"> He that despiseth his friend, is needy in heart; but a prudent man shall be still. (He who despiseth his friend is foolish; but a man of understanding shall be silent.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:19; PROVERBS 25:9; LEVITICUS 19:16"> He that goeth guilefully, showeth privates, (or He who goeth deceitfully, telleth secrets); but he that is faithful, covereth the private (matter) of a friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 24:6; PROVERBS 15:22; PROVERBS 20:18"> Where a governor is not, the people shall fall; but health is, where be many counsels. (Without a leader, or good governance, the people shall fall; but there is victory or but there is salvation, or deliverance, where there be many counsellors, or much planning, or good advice.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:18; PROVERBS 6:1–5; PROVERBS 22:26–27"> He that maketh faith, that is, (an) obligation, for a stranger, shall be tormented with evil; but he that escheweth snares, shall be secure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 31:30–31; LUKE 10:42; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:14–15"> A gracious woman shall find glory (or A gracious woman shall receive honour); and strong men shall have riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:7; MATTHEW 25:34–40; JAMES 2:13"> A merciful man doeth well to his soul (or A merciful person doeth good to his own soul); but he that is cruel, casteth away, yea, (even his own) kinsmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:8–9; ECCLESIASTES 10:8; HOSEA 10:12–13"> A wicked man maketh unstable work; but faithful meed is to him, that soweth rightfulness. (There is no surety with the work of the wicked; but there is a sure reward for those who sow goodness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:16; ROMANS 2:8–9; PROVERBS 12:28"> Mercy shall make ready life; and the following of evil, death. (Mercy bringeth forth life; but death followeth after evil.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 119:1; PROVERBS 21:29; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:17"> A shrewd heart is abominable to the Lord; and his will is in them, that go simply. (A depraved heart is abominable to the Lord; but his delight is in those who do things honestly, or with integrity.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 112:1–2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:39; JEREMIAH 32:39"> Though hand be in hand, an evil man shall not be innocent; but the seed of just men shall be saved. (Be assured, that the evil shall be punished; but the children, or the descendants, of the righteous shall be saved.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:3–4; PROVERBS 31:30; NAHUM 3:4–6"> A golden ring in the nostrils of a sow, (is like) a woman fair and fool. (A gold ring in the nostrils of a pig, is like a woman who is comely, but foolish.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 37:4; ROMANS 2:8–9; MATTHEW 5:6"> The desire of just men is all good; (but the) abiding of wicked men is strong vengeance. (The desire of the righteous is only for good; but the longing of the wicked is for strong vengeance.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:17; LUKE 6:38; DEUTERONOMY 15:10"> Some men part their own things, and be made richer; other men ravish things, that be not theirs, and they be ever in neediness. (Some people share their own things, and grow richer; other people steal things, that be not theirs, and still they always be in need.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:7; 2ND CORINTHIANS 9:6–9; ISAIAH 58:7–11"> A soul that blesseth, shall be made fat; and he that filleth, shall be filled also. (A person who blesseth, or who shareth, shall be made prosperous; and he who filleth, shall also be filled.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOB 29:13; GENESIS 42:6; AMOS 8:4–6"> He that hideth wheat, shall be cursed among the peoples; but blessing shall come&#91;up&#93; on the head(s) of sellers. (He who hideth corn, shall be cursed by the people; but blessing, or praise, shall come to the sellers.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 7:15–16; PROVERBS 17:11; PSALMS 9:15–16"> Well (be) he (who) riseth early, that seeketh good things; but he that is a searcher of evils, shall be oppressed of those. (He who riseth early, to seek out the good, shall find favour; but he who searcheth out evils, shall be oppressed by them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 6:17; JEREMIAH 17:8; PSALMS 52:7–8"> He that trusteth in his riches, shall fall; but just men shall burgeon as a green leaf (or but the righteous shall flourish like the green leaves).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 5:16; PROVERBS 14:19; PROVERBS 15:27"> He that troubleth his house, shall have (the) winds in possession; and he that is a fool, shall serve a wise man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DANIEL 12:3; JAMES 5:20; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:19–23"> The fruit of a rightful man is the tree of life; and he that taketh souls, is a wise man. (The fruit of righteousness is the tree of life; and he who winneth souls, is wise.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:17–18; JEREMIAH 25:29; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:30–32"> If a just man receiveth in &#91;the&#93; earth, how much more an unfaithful man, and a sinner. (If the righteous receive recompense here on earth, how much more shall the unfaithful, and the sinners!)</span>
</div>
