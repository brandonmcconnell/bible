<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:15; 1ST SAMUEL 2:25; PROVERBS 1:24–31"> Sudden perishing shall come &#91;up&#93; on that man, that with hard noll, that is, (with) an obstinate soul, despiseth a blamer (or despiseth a rebuker); and health shall not follow him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:12; PROVERBS 11:10; PROVERBS 28:28"> The commonality shall be glad in the multiplying of just men; when wicked men have taken princehood, the people shall wail. (The community shall be glad when the righteous rule; but when the wicked rule, then the people shall wail.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:11; PROVERBS 10:1; PROVERBS 15:20"> A man that loveth wisdom, maketh glad his father; but he that nourisheth a strumpet, shall lose his chattel. (A man who loveth wisdom, maketh his father glad; but he who feedeth a whore, shall lose his possessions.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:14; PROVERBS 20:8; DANIEL 11:20"> A just king raiseth &#91;up&#93; the land; an avaricious man shall destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HOSEA 5:1; JOB 17:5; PSALMS 5:9"> A man that speaketh by flattering and feigned words to his friend, spreadeth abroad a net to his steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:12; EXODUS 15:1; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:26"> A snare shall wrap a wicked man doing sin; and a just man shall praise, and make joy. (A snare shall enwrap the wicked doing sin; but the righteous shall give praise, and make joy in their lives.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOB 29:16; PSALMS 41:1; PROVERBS 21:13"> A just man knoweth the cause of poor men; and an unpious man knoweth not knowing. (The righteous know of the concerns of the poor; but the wicked do not care to know.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:11; JEREMIAH 15:1; JAMES 5:15–18"> Men full of pestilence destroy a city; but wise men turn away strong vengeance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 26:4; ECCLESIASTES 10:13; MATTHEW 7:6"> If a wise man striveth with a fool, whether he be wroth, or laugh, he shall not find rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 15:18–19; 1ST JOHN 3:12–13; GENESIS 4:5–8"> Men-quellers hate a simple man; but just men seek his soul. (Murderers hate honest people; but the righteous seek them out.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:11; PROVERBS 12:16; MICAH 7:5"> A fool bringeth forth all his spirit; a wise man delayeth, and reserveth into (the) time (to) coming afterward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:8; 2ND KI 10:6–7; PSALMS 52:2–4"> A prince that heareth willfully the words of leasing, shall have all his servants unfaithful. (A ruler who willingly listeneth to lies, shall have servants who all be unfaithful./If a ruler willingly listeneth to lies, then all of his servants shall be unfaithful.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:45; PROVERBS 22:2; PSALMS 13:3"> A poor man and a lender met themselves; the Lord is &#91;the&#93; lightener of ever either. (A poor person and a lender met together; the Lord lighteneth the burdens of both of them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:12; ISAIAH 11:4; PSALMS 72:2–4"> If a king deemeth poor men in truth, his throne shall be made steadfast &#91;into&#93; without end. (If a king judgeth the poor with honesty, his throne shall be made secure forever.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:1; PROVERBS 17:25; PROVERBS 29:17"> A rod and chastising shall give wisdom; but a child, that is left to his own will, shameth his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 37:36; PSALMS 91:8; PSALMS 58:10"> Great trespasses shall be multiplied in the multiplying of wicked men; and just men shall see the fallings of them. (Great trespasses shall be multiplied when the wicked rule; but the righteous shall see the falling of them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:1; PROVERBS 29:15; PROVERBS 23:13–14"> Teach thy son, and he shall comfort thee (or and he shall be a comfort to thee); and he shall give delights to thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JAMES 1:25; LUKE 11:28; JOHN 13:17"> When prophecy faileth, the people shall be destroyed; but he that keepeth the law, is blessed, (or but he who obeyeth the Law, is blessed).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PROVERBS 26:3; JOB 19:16; PROVERBS 30:22"> A servant may not be taught (only) by words; for he understandeth that that thou sayest, and he despiseth to answer. (A servant will not be corrected with words alone; for he understandeth what thou sayest, but he still despiseth to answer thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JAMES 1:19; PROVERBS 26:12; PROVERBS 14:29"> Thou hast seen a man swift to speak; folly shall be hoped more than his amending. (Thou hast seen a person quick to speak; more shall be hoped of from a fool, than for that person’s correction.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He that nourisheth his servant delicately from childhood, shall find him (a) rebel afterward. (He who delicately careth for his servant from childhood, shall find him to be rebellious later.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:18; PROVERBS 17:19; PROVERBS 26:21"> A wrathful man stirreth chidings; and he that is light to have indignation, shall be more inclined to sins. (An angry person stirreth up arguments; and he who is quick to have indignation, shall be more inclined to sin.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:12; LUKE 14:11; PROVERBS 11:2"> Lowness followeth a proud man; and glory shall up-take a meek man of spirit. (Lowness followeth the proud; but honour shall come to those with a humble spirit.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:1; PROVERBS 1:11–19; PROVERBS 8:36"> He that taketh part with a thief, hateth his soul; he heareth a man charging greatly, and showeth not. (He who taketh part with a thief, hateth his own soul; he heareth someone telling many lies, but he declareth it not.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 118:8; LUKE 12:4; PROVERBS 18:10"> He that dreadeth a man, shall fall soon; he that hopeth in the Lord, shall be raised &#91;up&#93;. (He who feareth someone, shall soon fall; but he who hopeth in the Lord, shall be raised up.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:6; PROVERBS 19:21; PSALMS 20:9"> Many men seek the face of the prince; and the doom of all men shall go forth of the Lord. (Many people seek favour from a leader; but for all people judgement, or justice, shall come from the Lord.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1ST JOHN 3:13; JOHN 15:17–19; PSALMS 139:21"> Just men have abomination of a wicked man; and wicked men have abomination of them, that be in a rightful way. (The righteous have abomination for the wicked; and the wicked have abomination for those who be on the right way.)</span>
