<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:21; PROVERBS 16:9; LUKE 21:14–15"> It pertaineth to man to make ready the soul; and it pertaineth to the Lord to govern the tongue. (Each person must prepare their own soul, or their own life; but it is God who governeth their tongue, or telleth them what to say.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:2; 1ST SAMUEL 16:7; LUKE 16:15"> All the ways of men be open to the eyes of God (or All the ways of people be before God); the Lord is a weigher of spirits, that is, of wills, yielding to man after his deservings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 37:4–5; PROVERBS 3:6; PSALMS 55:22"> Show thy works to the Lord; and thy thoughts shall be (ad)dressed. (Commit thy plans to the Lord; and they shall succeed.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:9; ROMANS 9:22; ISAIAH 43:21"> The Lord wrought all things for himself; and he made ready a wicked man to the evil day. (The Lord made everything for its own purpose; and he made the wicked for the day of evil.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:16–17; PROVERBS 11:21; PROVERBS 8:13"> Abomination of the Lord is each proud man (or Each proud person is an abomination to the Lord); yea, though the hand is to the hand, he shall not be innocent. The beginning of &#91;the&#93; good way is to do rightwiseness; forsooth it is more acceptable with God, than to offer sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:16; JOB 28:28; 2ND CORINTHIANS 7:1"> Wickedness is again-bought by mercy and truth; and men boweth away from evil by the dread of the Lord. (Wickedness is redeemed by love and faithfulness; and people can turn away from evil by the fear of the Lord or through reverence for the Lord.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 8:31; 1ST JOHN 3:22; COLOSSIANS 1:10"> When the ways of (a) man please the Lord, he shall convert, yea, his enemies to peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:16; PSALMS 37:16; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:6–9"> Better is a little with rightfulness, than many fruits with wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:21; PSALMS 37:23; PROVERBS 16:1"> The heart of a man shall dispose his way; but it pertaineth to the Lord to (ad)dress his steps. (A person’s heart shall ordain his way; but it pertaineth to the Lord to direct his steps.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 32:1–2; GENESIS 44:5; GENESIS 44:15"> Divining is in the lips of a king; his mouth shall not err in doom. (The king speaketh with divine authority; his mouth shall not err in any judgement.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:1; PROVERBS 20:10; PROVERBS 20:23"> The dooms of the Lord be weight and balance; and his works be all the stones of the world. (The Lord’s justice be equal weights and an honest balance; yea, each of the stones in the worldis his own work.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 25:5; PROVERBS 29:14; LUKE 12:48"> They that do wickedly be abominable to the king; for the throne of the realm is made steadfast by rightfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:35; PSALMS 101:5–7; PROVERBS 22:11"> The will of kings is just lips; he that speaketh rightful things, shall be (ad)dressed. (The desire of kings is just, or truthful, words; he who speaketh upright things shall be favoured.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:2; PROVERBS 19:12; ECCLESIASTES 10:4"> &#91;The&#93; Indignation of the king is(like) messengers of death; and a wise man shall please him (or but a wise person shall please him).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 72:6; JOB 29:23–24; HOSEA 6:3"> Life is in gladness of the king’s cheer; and his mercy is as rain coming late. (Life (is) in the gladness of the king’s face; and his mercy (is) like the rain that cometh late.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 8:10–11; MATTHEW 16:26; PROVERBS 8:19"> Wield thou wisdom, for it is better than gold; and get thou prudence, for it is preciouser than silver. (Possess thou wisdom, for it is better than gold; and get thou prudence, for it is more precious than silver.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 35:8; PROVERBS 19:16; PROVERBS 10:9"> The path of just men boweth away (from) evils (or The path of the righteous turneth away from evil); the keeper of his soul keepeth his way (safe).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:2; ISAIAH 2:11–12; PROVERBS 18:12"> Pride goeth before sorrow; and the spirit shall be enhanced before falling (or and the spirit shall be raised up before a fall).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:3; ISAIAH 57:15; PSALMS 138:6"> It is better to be made meek with mild men, than to part spoils with proud men. (It is better to be made poor with the humble, than to part spoils with the proud.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:7–8; PSALMS 2:12; ISAIAH 30:18"> A learned man in word shall find goods (or A person learned in understanding shall obtain good things); and he that hopeth in the Lord is blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:23–24; ISAIAH 50:4; PROVERBS 15:7"> He that is wise in heart, shall be called prudent; and he that is sweet in speech, shall find greater things. (He who is wise in heart, shall be called prudent; and he who useth sweet, or pleasant, speech, shall increase his knowledge or shall help others to learn.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:14; JOHN 6:63; JOHN 5:24"> The well of life is the learning of him that wieldeth (it); the teaching of fools is folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:28; MATTHEW 12:34–35; COLOSSIANS 3:16"> The heart of a wise man shall teach his mouth; and shall increase grace to his lips. (The heart of a wise person shall teach his mouth; and his lips shall bring an increase of favour.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:18; PROVERBS 24:13–14; PROVERBS 25:11–12"> Words well-set together is a comb of honey; health of bones is the sweetness of soul. (Kind words be like a honeycomb; sweet to the soul, and health for the bones.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:12; PROVERBS 12:15; 2ND CORINTHIANS 13:5"> A way there is that seemeth rightful to a man (or There is a way that seemeth right to someone); and the last things thereof lead to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 6:7; PROVERBS 14:23; PROVERBS 9:12"> The soul of a man travailing travaileth to himself; for his mouth compelled him. (A person labouring laboureth for himself; for his hunger compelled him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JAMES 3:6; PROVERBS 6:14; ISAIAH 5:18"> An unwise man diggeth evil; and fire burneth in his lips. (The unwise person diggeth up evil; and fire burneth on his lips.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:9; PROVERBS 18:8; PROVERBS 15:18"> A wayward man raiseth strives; and a man full of words separateth princes. (A wayward person raiseth up strife, or arguments; and a person full of words separateth leaders.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PROVERBS 2:12–15; PROVERBS 12:26; PROVERBS 1:10–14"> A wicked man flattereth his friend; and leadeth him by a way not good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:15; MICAH 7:3; MATTHEW 14:7–8"> He that thinketh shrewd things with eyes astonied, biteth his lips, and performeth evil. (With astonished eyes, he thinketh depraved things, and biteth his lips, and performeth evil.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:29; LEVITICUS 19:32; PROVERBS 3:1–2"> A crown of dignity is eld (age), that shall be found in the ways of rightfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JAMES 1:19; PROVERBS 15:18; PROVERBS 25:28"> A patient man is better than a strong man; and he that is lord of his soul, is better than an overcomer of cities. (A patient person is better than a strong person; and he who is lord over himself, or over his own nature, is better than an overcomer of cities.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:18; JONAH 1:7; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:26"> Lots be sent into the bosom; but they be tempered of the Lord. (Lots can be cast into the lap; but they shall be decided, or determined, by the Lord.)</span>
