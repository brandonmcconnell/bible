<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:18; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:12; 1ST KINGS 7:2–3"> Wisdom builded an house to himself (or Wisdom built a house for himself); he hewed out seven pillars,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 22:3–14; ISAIAH 25:6; LUKE 14:16–17"> he offered his slain sacrifices, he meddled wine (or he mixed in spices with the wine), and setted forth his table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 9:14; PROVERBS 8:1–3; MATTHEW 22:3–4"> He sent his handmaids (or He sent forth his servantesses), that they should call to the tower; and to the walls of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:32; PROVERBS 9:16; PROVERBS 8:5"> If any man is little (in wit), come he to me. And wisdom spake to unwise men, (and said), (And that they should say, If any one hath little intelligence, come he to me. And then wisdom spoke to the unwise, and said,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOHN 6:27; SONG OF SONGS 5:1; PROVERBS 9:17"> Come ye, eat ye my bread; and drink ye the wine, which I have meddled to you, (or and drink ye the wine, in which I have mixed in spices for you).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:20; PROVERBS 4:11; LUKE 13:24"> Forsake ye young childhood, and live ye; and go ye by the way of prudence. (Abandon ye foolishness, and have life; and go ye on the way of understanding.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:12; PROVERBS 23:9; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:16"> He that teacheth a scorner, doeth wrong to himself; and he that reproveth a wicked man, engendereth a wem to himself. (He who trieth to teach a mocker, only wrongeth himself; and he who reproveth the wicked, begetteth a wound for himself.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 141:5; PROVERBS 13:18; PROVERBS 23:9"> Do not thou reprove a scorner; lest he hate thee. Reprove thou a wise man; and he shall love thee. (Do not thou rebuke a mocker; lest he hate thee. Rebuke thou a wise person; and he shall love thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:5; MATTHEW 13:11–12; 1ST JOHN 5:13"> Give thou occasion to a wise man; and wisdom shall be increased to him. Teach thou a just man; and he shall hasten to take it(to heart). (Give thou advice, or instruction, to a wise person; and his wisdom shall be increased. Teach thou a righteous person; and he shall hasten to take it to heart.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 111:10; JOB 28:28; PROVERBS 1:7"> The beginning of wisdom is the dread of the Lord; and prudence is the knowing of saints. (The beginning of wisdom (is) the fear of the Lord; and knowledge of the Holy One is understanding./The beginning of wisdom (is) reverence for the Lord; and to know the Holy One is to have understanding.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:27; PROVERBS 3:16; PROVERBS 3:2"> For thy days shall be multiplied by me; and &#91;the&#93; years of (thy) life shall be increased to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 22:2–3; 2ND PETER 3:3–4; EZEKIEL 18:20"> If thou art wise, thou shalt be wise to thyself, and to thy neighbours. Forsooth if thou art a scorner, thou alone shalt bear evil. (If thou art wise, thou shalt be wise, or gain advantage, for thyself, and for thy friends as well. But if thou art a mocker, thou alone shalt bear the evil.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 7:11; PROVERBS 5:6; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:4"> A fond woman, and full of cry, and full of unleaveful lusts, and that knoweth nothing utterly, (A loud and foolish woman, full of unlawful lusts, who knoweth utterly nothing,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 9:3; PROVERBS 7:10–12"> sitteth in the doors of her house (or sitteth at the door of her house), on a seat, in an high place of the city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 7:25–27; PROVERBS 23:27–28; PROVERBS 7:13–15"> to call men passing by the way, and men going in their journey. (to call to men passing by the way, and to men going on their journey.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 9:4"> Who is a little man in wit; bow he to me, (or And she saith to herself, Who is a man of little intelligence; turn he unto me). And she spake to a coward, (and said),</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:17; PROVERBS 23:31–32; JAMES 1:14–15"> Waters of theft be sweeter, and bread hid is sweeter. (Stolen water is sweeter, and hidden bread is sweeter.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 7:27; PROVERBS 2:18–19; PROVERBS 5:5"> And &#91;he&#93; knew not that giants be there; and the guests of her be in the depths of hell. Soothly he that shall be applied, either fastened, to her, shall go down to hells. For why he that goeth away from her shall be saved. (And he knew not that death is there; and that her guests be in the depths of hell. Truly, he who shall be fastened to her, shall go down to Sheol, that is, to the land of the dead, or shall go down to hell. But he who goeth away from her, shall be saved.)</span>
