<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="wycliffe-enhanced" data-translation-id="eng_wyc2018" data-translation-label="Wycliffe (Enhanced)" data-book="PRO" data-book-slug="proverbs" data-book-name="PROVERBS" data-chapter="12" data-verses="28">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:10; PROVERBS 9:7–8; PSALMS 119:97–100"> He that loveth chastising, loveth knowing; but he that hateth blamings, is unwise. (He who loveth discipline, loveth knowledge; but he who hateth rebukes, is unwise.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 8:35; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:24; PSALMS 112:5"> He that is good, shall draw to himself (the) grace of the Lord, (or He who is good, shall draw favour to himself from the Lord); but he that trusteth in his (own) thoughts, doeth wickedly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:25; EPHESIANS 3:17; PROVERBS 12:12"> A man shall not be made strong by wickedness; and the root of just men shall not be moved. (No one is made strong by wickedness; but the roots of the righteous shall not be uprooted.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:30; PROVERBS 31:10–25; PROVERBS 14:1"> A diligent woman is a crown to her husband; and rot is in the bones of that woman, that doeth things worthy of confusion. (A diligent woman is a crown to her husband; but rot is in the bones of her, who doeth shameful things.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 139:23; ISAIAH 55:7; PSALMS 140:1–3"> The thoughts of just men be dooms; and the counsels of wicked men be guileful. (The thoughts of the righteous be lawful; and the plans of the wicked be deceitful or and the advice of the wicked is deceptive.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:3; JEREMIAH 5:26; PROVERBS 1:11–19"> The words of wicked men set treason to blood; the mouth of just men shall deliver them. (The words of the wicked set ambush for blood; the words of the righteous shall save them.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:24–27; PROVERBS 11:21; PROVERBS 10:25"> (Over)Turn thou wicked men, and they shall not be; but the houses of just men shall dwell perfectly. (Overturn thou, or bring thou down, the wicked, and they shall not be; but the families of the righteous shall continue.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 10:18; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:5; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:10–15"> A man shall be known by his teaching; but he that is vain and heartless, shall be open to despising.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 14:11; PROVERBS 13:7"> Better is a poor man, and sufficient (un)to himself, than (to be) a (self-)glorious man, and needy of bread.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 25:4; GENESIS 33:13–14; NUMBERS 22:28–32"> A just man knoweth the lives of his work beasts; but the entrails of wicked men be cruel. (A righteous person careth for the lives of his work beasts; but the hearts of the wicked be cruel.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:19; PROVERBS 14:23; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:11–12"> He that worketh his land, shall be filled with loaves; but he that followeth idleness, is most fool(ish). He that is sweet, that is mild, liveth in temperances; and in his admonishings he forsaketh despisings, (or He who is humble, liveth temperately; and he forsaketh despisings in all of his admonishings).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:7–8; JOHN 15:5; LUKE 8:13–15"> The desire of a wicked man is the memorial of worst things; but the root of just men shall increase. (The desires of the wicked be a memorial of the worst things; but the roots of the righteous shall increase, or shall go deeper.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:23; PROVERBS 11:8; PSALMS 64:8"> For the sins of (his) lips, falling down nigheth to an evil man; but a just man shall escape from anguish (or but the righteous shall escape from anguish).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:20–21; ISAIAH 3:10–11; PROVERBS 13:2"> Of the fruit of his mouth each man shall be &#91;ful&#93; filled with goods (or A person shall be fulfilled with good things by the fruit of his mouth); and by the works of his hands it shall be yielded to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:7; PROVERBS 26:12; PROVERBS 14:16"> The way of a fool is rightful in his eyes; but he that is wise, heareth counsels, (or but he who is wise, listeneth to good advice).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:11; JAMES 1:19; PROVERBS 10:12"> A fool showeth anon his ire; but he that dissembleth wrongs, is wise. (A fool showeth his anger at once; but he who overlooketh wrongs, is wise.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:5; PROVERBS 14:25; PROVERBS 24:28"> He that speaketh that, that he knoweth, is a judge of rightfulness; but he that lieth, is a guileful witness. (He who speaketh what he knoweth, is a just judge or is a judge of righteousness; but he who lieth, is a deceitful witness.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:24; PROVERBS 15:4; PSALMS 57:4"> A man is that promiseth, and he is pricked as with the sword of conscience; but the tongue of wise men is health. (There is a person who speaketh, and his words cut like a sword; but the tongues of the wise give health.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:9; PSALMS 52:4–5; JOB 20:5"> The lip of truth shall be steadfast &#91;into&#93; without end; but he that is a sudden witness, maketh ready the tongue of leasing, (or but he who speaketh a ready answer, often haveth a lying tongue).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:9; 1ST PETER 3:8–13; PROVERBS 12:12"> Guile is in the heart of them that think evils; but joy followeth them that make counsels of peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:13; PSALMS 91:10; ROMANS 8:28"> Whatever befalleth to a just man, it shall not make him sorry; but wicked men shall be filled with evil. (Whatever befalleth to the righteous, shall not bring them sorrow, or grief; but the wicked shall be filled with evil.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 5:6; PROVERBS 11:20; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:15"> False lips is abomination to the Lord; but they that do faithfully, please him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:16; PROVERBS 11:13; PROVERBS 10:19"> A fell man covereth knowing; and the heart of unwise men stirreth folly. (A clever person concealeth his knowledge; but the unwise tell out their foolishness.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:27; PROVERBS 10:4; PROVERBS 21:25–26"> The hand of strong men shall have lordship; but the hand that is slow, shall serve to tributes. (The hand of the strong shall rule; but those who be lazy, shall become slaves.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:24; PROVERBS 15:13; PROVERBS 17:22"> Mourning in the heart of a just man shall make him meek; and he shall be made glad by a good word. (Mourning in the heart of a righteous person shall make him weak; but he shall be made happy by a good, or by a kind, word.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:26; MATTHEW 5:46–48; PROVERBS 17:27"> He that despiseth harm for a friend, is a just man; but the way of wicked men shall deceive them. (He who despiseth harm to a friend, is a righteous person; but the way of the wicked shall deceive them.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:4; PROVERBS 26:15; PROVERBS 15:16"> A guileful man shall not find winning; and the substance of (a diligent) man shall be (equal to) the price of gold, that is, preciouser than gold. (A deceitful, or a lazy, person shall not find winning; but the substance, or the reward, of a diligent person shall be equal to the price of gold, that is, shall be more precious than gold.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="3RD JOHN 1:11; ROMANS 5:21; EZEKIEL 18:20–24"> Life is in the path of rightwiseness (or Life is on the path of righteousness); but the wrong way leadeth to death.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
