<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:7; PSALMS 53:5; ISAIAH 26:3–4"> A wicked man fleeth, when no man pursueth; but a just man as a lion trusting shall be without fearedfulness. (A wicked person fleeth, when no one pursueth him; but a righteous person shall be without fear, like a trusting lion.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST KINGS 15:28; 1ST KINGS 16:8–29; 2ND KI 15:8–31"> For the sins of the land there be many princes of it; and for the wisdom of a man, and for the knowing of these things that be said, the life of the duke shall be the longer. (For the sins of the land there shall be many leaders of it; but with the wisdom and knowledge of a good ruler, the nation shall endure.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:28–30"> A poor man falsely challenging poor men, is like a great rain, wherein hunger is made ready. (A poor person who oppresseth the poor or A tyrant who oppresseth the poor, is like a great rain that destroyeth the crops.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ROMANS 1:32; EPHESIANS 5:11; MATTHEW 14:4"> They that forsake the law, praise the wicked man; they that keep the law, be kindled, or stirred&#91;up&#93;, against him. (They who desert the law, praise the wicked; they who keep the law, be kindled, or stirred up, against them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 92:6; JAMES 1:5; 1ST JOHN 2:27"> Wicked men think not on doom; but they that seek the Lord, perceive all things. (The wicked do not think about justice; but they who seek the Lord, understand everything about it or understand it well.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:1; PROVERBS 28:18; LUKE 16:19–23"> Better is a poor man going in his simpleness, than a rich man in &#91;his&#93; shrewd ways. (Better is a poor person going in his honesty, or in his integrity, than a rich person in all his depraved ways.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:3–4; PROVERBS 29:3; PROVERBS 3:1–35"> He that keepeth the law, is a wise son; but he that feedeth gluttons, shameth his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 27:16–17; PROVERBS 13:22; ECCLESIASTES 2:26"> He that gathereth together riches by usuries, and free(ly)(made) increases, gathereth those together against poor men. (He who gathereth together riches from high interest rates, and exorbitant increases, gathereth them together for him who will give them to the poor.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 66:18; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:3–4; ZECHARIAH 7:11–13"> His prayer shall be made cursed, that boweth away his ear (or who turneth away his ear), (so) that he hear not the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 26:27; PSALMS 37:25–26; GALATIANS 2:4"> He that deceiveth just men in an evil way, shall fall in his perishing; and just men shall wield his goods. (He who deceiveth the righteous in an evil way, shall fall in his perishing; and the righteous shall possess his goods.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:17; ECCLESIASTES 9:15–17; PROVERBS 26:16"> A rich man seemeth wise to himself; but a poor man prudent shall search him. (A rich person seemeth wise to himself; but a prudent poor person shall search him out, and find him wanting.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:2; PROVERBS 11:10; PROVERBS 28:28"> In &#91;the&#93; enhancing of just men is much glory; when wicked men reign, fallings of men be. (When the righteous be advanced, or promoted, there is much honour; but when the wicked reign, or rule, there shall be the falling of many people.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST JOHN 1:8–10; PSALMS 32:3–5; JOB 31:33"> He that hideth his great trespasses, shall not be made rightful; but he that acknowledgeth and forsaketh them, shall get mercy. (He who hideth his great trespasses, shall not be made upright; but he who acknowledgeth and abandoneth them, shall get mercy.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 23:17; PSALMS 2:11; ISAIAH 66:2"> Blessed is the man, which is ever dreadful; but he that is of hard heart, shall fall into evil. (Blessed is the person, who is ever fearful of the Lord or who is always reverential toward the Lord; but he who hath a hard heart, shall fall into evil.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:8; MATTHEW 2:16; PROVERBS 19:12"> A roaring lion, and an hungry bear, is a wicked prince on a poor people. (Like a roaring lion, and a hungry bear, is a wicked ruler over the poor.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 18:21; JEREMIAH 22:15–17; ECCLESIASTES 4:1"> A duke needy of prudence shall oppress many men by false challenge; but the days of him that hateth avarice shall be made long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 9:6; 1ST KINGS 21:19; EXODUS 21:14"> No man sustain a man that falsely challengeth the blood of a man, (even) if he fleeth till to the pit. (Let no one protect someone who oppresseth unto the blood, that is, unto the murder, of another person, yea, let him flee into the pit.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:9; PROVERBS 28:6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:3"> He that goeth simply shall be safe (or He who goeth honestly, or with integrity, shall be safe); (but) he that goeth by wayward ways, shall fall down &#91;at&#93; once.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:11; JUDGES 9:4; PROVERBS 27:23–27"> He that worketh his land, shall be filled with loaves; he that followeth idleness (or but he who is idle, or lazy), shall be filled with neediness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKE 16:10–12; PROVERBS 13:11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:10"> A faithful man shall be praised much; but he that hasteth to be made rich, shall not be innocent. (A faithful person shall be much praised; but he who hasteneth to be made rich, shall not be innocent.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PROVERBS 24:23; PROVERBS 18:5; EZEKIEL 13:19"> He that knoweth a face in doom, doeth not well; this man forsaketh truth, yea, for a morsel of bread. (He who knoweth the person whom he is judging, doeth wrongly; this person deserteth the truth, yea, for a morsel of bread.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 23:6; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:9; PROVERBS 28:20"> A man that hasteth to be made rich, and hath envy to other men (or and hath envy of others), knoweth not that neediness shall come &#91;up&#93; on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:5–6; MATTHEW 18:15; PSALMS 141:5"> He that reproveth a man, shall find grace afterward with him, (or He who rebuketh someone, shall find favour afterward with him); more than he that deceiveth by flatterings of (the) tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:4–6; PROVERBS 18:9; PROVERBS 19:26"> He that withdraweth anything from his father and from his mother, and saith that this is no sin, is partner of a man-queller (or is the partner of, or no better than, a murderer).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:25; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:6; PROVERBS 13:10"> He that avaunteth himself, and alargeth, raiseth up strives; but he that hopeth in the Lord, shall be saved. (He who vaunteth, and enlargeth, himself, raiseth up arguments, or contention; but he who trusteth in the Lord, shall be saved.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:5; JEREMIAH 17:9; JOB 28:28"> He that trusteth in his (own) heart, is a fool; but he that goeth wisely, shall be praised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:17; DEUTERONOMY 15:7; PSALMS 41:1–3"> He that giveth to a poor man, shall not be needy; he that despiseth a poor man beseeching, shall suffer neediness. (He who giveth to the poor, shall not be needy; but he who despiseth the pleading of the poor, shall suffer want, or lack.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:2; JOB 24:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:23–24"> When unpious men rise, men shall be hid; and when they have perished, just men shall be multiplied. (When the wicked rise up, people shall be hidden; but when they have perished, the righteous shall be multiplied.)</span>
