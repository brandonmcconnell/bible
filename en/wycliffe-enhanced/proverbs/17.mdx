<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:17; PSALMS 37:16; PROVERBS 21:19"> Better is a dry morsel with joy, than an house full of sacrifices with chiding, (or with arguments).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:5; ECCLESIASTES 4:13; PROVERBS 14:35"> A wise servant shall be lord of fond sons; and he shall part heritage among brethren. (A wise servant shall be lord over his master’s foolish sons and daughters; and in time he shall have part of the inheritance, along with the brothers and the sisters.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 48:10; JEREMIAH 17:10; PSALMS 26:2"> As silver is proved by fire, and gold is proved by a chimney, so the Lord proveth hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 4:3–4; 1ST JOHN 4:5; PROVERBS 28:4"> An evil man obeyeth to a wicked tongue; and a false man obeyeth to false lips. (An evil person obeyeth a wicked tongue; and a liar obeyeth lies.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:31; JOB 31:29; 1ST JOHN 3:17"> He that despiseth a poor man, reproveth his maker; and he that is glad in the falling of another man, shall not be unpunished. (He who despiseth the poor, rebuketh his Maker; and he who is glad in the falling of another person, shall not go unpunished.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 127:3–5; PROVERBS 13:22; PROVERBS 16:31"> The crown of eld men is the sons of sons; and the glory of sons is the fathers of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:12; PROVERBS 12:19; PSALMS 50:16–17"> Words well-set together beseem not a fool; and a lying lip becometh not a prince. (Words put together well become not a fool; and lies become not a leader.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 23:8; MICAH 7:3; 1ST SAMUEL 25:35"> A precious stone most acceptable is the abiding of him that seeketh; whither ever he turneth himself, he understandeth prudently. (A gift, or a bribe, is like a most acceptable precious stone in the eyes of him who receiveth it; wherever it goeth, it bringeth prosperity to he who giveth it.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:12; 1ST PETER 4:8; PROVERBS 16:28"> He that covereth trespass, seeketh friendships; he that rehearseth by an high word (or but he who remembereth a wrong), separateth them that (should) be knit together in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:19; PROVERBS 9:8–9; PROVERBS 15:5"> A blaming profiteth more at a prudent man, than an hundred wounds at a fool. (A rebuke more profiteth a prudent man, than an hundred wounds to a fool.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST KINGS 2:46; 2ND SAMUEL 16:5–9; 2ND SAMUEL 18:15"> Ever an evil man seeketh strives; forsooth a cruel angel shall be sent against him. (An evil person forevermore seeketh out strife, or arguments; but a cruel messenger shall be sent against him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HOSEA 13:8; 2ND SAMUEL 17:8; PROVERBS 28:15"> It speedeth more to meet a female bear, when her whelps be ravished, than a fool trusting to himself in his folly. (It is better to meet a female bear after she hath been robbed of her whelps, than a fool trusting himself in his own foolishness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 109:4–13; 1ST PETER 3:9; ROMANS 12:17"> Evil shall not go away from the house of him, that yieldeth evils for goods (or who giveth evil for good).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:3; PROVERBS 25:8; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:23–24"> He that letteth (out) water, is the head of strives, (or of arguments); and before that he suffereth wrong, he forsaketh doom (or he deserteth judgement).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 23:7; PROVERBS 18:5; ISAIAH 5:23"> Both he that justifieth a wicked man, and he that condemneth a just man, ever either is abominable to God. (Both he who justifieth a wicked person, and he who condemneth a righteous person, be abominable to God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:25–26; PROVERBS 23:23; PROVERBS 18:15"> What profiteth it to a fool to have riches, since he may not buy wisdom? (or since he cannot buy wisdom?) He that maketh his house high, seeketh falling; and he that escheweth to learn, shall fall into evils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:24; JOHN 15:13–14; 1ST SAMUEL 20:17"> He that is a friend, loveth in all time; and a brother is proved in anguishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:15; PROVERBS 6:1–5; PROVERBS 22:26–27"> A fond man shall make joy with hands (or A foolish person shall clap his hands, or shall rejoice), when he hath promised for his friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:18; PROVERBS 18:12; 1ST SAMUEL 25:36–38"> He that bethinketh discords, loveth chidings; and he that enhanceth his mouth, seeketh falling. (He who thinketh up discord, loveth arguments; and he who exalteth his own mouth, seeketh his own downfall.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JAMES 3:6–8; PROVERBS 10:31; PROVERBS 6:12–15"> He that is of wayward heart, shall not find good; and he that (mis-)turneth the tongue, shall fall into evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:1; PROVERBS 19:13; PROVERBS 17:25"> A fool is born in his shame; but neither the father shall be glad in a fool. (A fool is born to his parents? shame; and the father shall never be glad, or have joy, over his foolish son.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:13; PROVERBS 12:25; PROVERBS 18:14"> A joyful soul maketh liking age (or A joyful soul maketh a good life); a sorrowful spirit maketh dry bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:8; DEUTERONOMY 16:19; EXODUS 23:8"> A wicked man taketh gifts from the bosom, to mis-turn the paths of doom. (A wicked person accepeth secret gifts, or bribes, to pervert the course of justice.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:14; ECCLESIASTES 8:1; PROVERBS 14:6"> Wisdom shineth in the face of a prudent man; the eyes of fools be in the ends of earth. (Wisdom shineth in the face of the prudent; the eyes of fools be upon the ends of the earth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:1; 2ND SAMUEL 13:1–22; PROVERBS 19:13"> A fond son is the ire of the father, and the sorrow of the mother that bare him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:15; PROVERBS 18:5; JOHN 18:22"> It is not good to bring in harm to a just man; neither to smite the prince that deemeth rightfully. (It is not good to harm the righteous; nor to strike the leader who ruleth rightfully.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JAMES 3:18; JAMES 1:19; PROVERBS 10:19"> He that measureth his words, is wise and prudent; and a learned man is of precious spirit. (He who measureth his words, is wise and prudent; and a learned person hath a precious spirit.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOB 13:5; PROVERBS 15:2; ECCLESIASTES 10:14"> Also a fool, if he is still, shall be guessed a wise man, (or Even a fool, if he is silent, shall be thought to be wise); and, if he presseth together his lips, he is guessed an understanding man.</span>
