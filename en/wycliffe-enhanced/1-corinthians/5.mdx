<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 20:23; COLOSSIANS 3:17; MATTHEW 16:19"> when ye be gathered together in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, and my spirit, with the virtue of the Lord Jesus (or with the power of the Lord Jesus),</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:33; JAMES 4:16"> Your glorying is not good. For Christ offered is our pask, &#91;or Forsooth Christ is offered our pask&#93;, (or For the sacrificed Messiah is our Passover).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:1–2; DEUTERONOMY 16:3; EXODUS 12:15"> Therefore eat we, not in old sourdough, neither in sourdough of malice and waywardness, but in therf things of clearness, and of truth. (And so, let us not eat the old yeast, yea, the yeast of malice and wickedness, but the unleavened things of sincerity, and of truth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:11; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:14; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:14"> I wrote to you in an epistle, that ye be not meddled, &#91;or mingled, or commune not&#93;, with lechers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 10:27; JOHN 17:15–16; MATTHEW 5:14–16"> not with (the) lechers of this world, nor with covetous men, nor raveners, nor with men serving to maumets &#91;or to idols&#93;, else ye should have gone out of this world (or else ye would have had to have gone out of this world).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 16:17; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:14; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:6"> But now I have written to you, that ye be not meddled, &#91;or mingled, or commune not with such&#93;.
