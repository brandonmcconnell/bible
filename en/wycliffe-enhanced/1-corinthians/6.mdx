(Dare any of you who hath a case against another, be judged by wicked men, and not by God’s people?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:28; DANIEL 7:22; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:4"> Whether ye know not, that (the) saints shall deem this world? And if the world shall be deemed by you, be ye unworthy to deem the least things? (Do ye not know, that God’s people shall judge this world? And so if the world shall be judged by you, be ye unworthy to judge these least things?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JUDE 1:6; MATTHEW 25:41; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:4"> Know ye not, that we shall deem angels? (or Do ye not know, that we shall judge the angels?)(and so then) how much more (the) worldly things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 5:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 6:2–4"> Therefore if ye have worldly dooms (or And so if ye have worldly judgements to make), ordain ye those contemptible men, &#91;or of little reputation&#93;, that be in the church, to deem (them).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:34; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:14; JAMES 3:13–18"> I say (this) to make you ashamed &#91;or I say (this) to your shame&#93;. So there is not any wise man, that may deem betwixt a brother and his brother, (or So is there not any wise man, who can judge between a brother and his brother);</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:14–15; 1ST JOHN 2:9–11; 1ST JOHN 3:11–15"> but brother with brother striveth in doom, and that among unfaithful men. (but a brother must fight, or argue, with another brother in court, and in front of men who be out of the faith?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 12:17–19; PROVERBS 20:22; 1ST PETER 2:19–23"> And (so) now trespass is always among you, for ye have dooms among you (or because ye have legal wranglings among you). Why rather take ye not (the) wrong? why rather suffer ye not (the) deceit &#91;or (the) fraud&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 4:6; COLOSSIANS 3:25; MARK 10:19"> But also ye do wrong, and do fraud &#91;or and defraud&#93;, and that to brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 1:9–10; GALATIANS 5:19–21; EPHESIANS 5:4–5"> Whether ye know not, that wicked men shall not wield the kingdom of God? Do not ye err; neither lechers, neither men that serve maumets, neither adulterers, neither lechers against kind, neither they that do lechery with men, (Do ye not know, that the wicked shall not possess the Kingdom of God? Do not err; neither lechers, nor men who serve idols, nor adulterers, nor lechers against nature, nor those who do lechery with men,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 5:11; GALATIANS 5:21; EZEKIEL 22:29"> neither thieves, neither avaricious &#91;or covetous&#93; men, neither men full of drunkenness, neither cursers, neither raveners, shall wield the kingdom of God (or shall possess the Kingdom of God).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:30; COLOSSIANS 3:5–7; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:2"> And ye were sometime these things; but ye be washed, but ye be hallowed (or but ye be consecrated), but ye be justified in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, and in the Spirit of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 10:23–33; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:27; HEBREWS 12:15–16"> All things be leaveful to me, but not all things be speedful. All things be leaveful to me, but I shall not be brought down under any man’s power. (All things be lawful for me, but not all things be expedient. All things be lawful for me, but I shall not be brought down under the power of any man.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 6:19; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:16; ROMANS 6:12"> Meat to the womb, and the womb to meats; and God shall destroy both this and that. And the body not to fornication, but to the Lord, and the Lord to the body. (Food for the belly, and the belly for food; and God shall destroy both this and that. And the body is not for fornication, but for the Lord, and the Lord is for the body.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 4:14; ROMANS 8:11; EPHESIANS 1:19–20"> For God raised the Lord, and shall raise us by his virtue. (For God raised the Lord, and he shall also raise us by his power.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:30; ROMANS 12:5; EPHESIANS 4:15–16"> Know ye not, that your bodies be members of Christ? Shall I then take the members of Christ, and shall I make them the members of a whore? God forbid. (Do ye not know, that your bodies be parts of the Messiah? Shall I then take the parts of the Messiah, and make (them), or join (them), to the parts of a whore? God forbid.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 2:24; EPHESIANS 5:31; MARK 10:8"> Whether ye know not, that he that cleaveth to a whore, is made one body? For he saith, There shall be twain in one flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 17:21–23; GALATIANS 2:20; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:13"> And he that cleaveth to the Lord, is one Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:11; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:3; COLOSSIANS 3:5"> Flee ye fornication; all sin whatever sin a man doeth, is without the body (or is outside the body); but he that doeth fornication, sinneth against his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 3:16; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:16; 1ST PETER 2:5"> Whether ye know not, that your members be the temple of the Holy Ghost, that is in you, whom ye have of God, and ye be not your own? (Do ye not know, that your bodies be the temple of the Holy Spirit, who is in you, whom ye have received from God, and ye be not your own?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:23; ROMANS 12:1; 1ST PETER 2:9"> For ye be bought with (a) great price. Glorify ye, and bear ye God in your body, (or Glorify, and carry or bear about God in your body).</span>
