<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:10; TITUS 1:7; 1ST TIMOTHY 3:9"> So a man guess, &#91;or deem&#93;, us as ministers of Christ, (or So think of us as servants of the Messiah), and (as) dispensers of the mysteries of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:21; LUKE 12:42; LUKE 16:10–12"> Now it is sought here among the dispensers, that a man be found true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOHN 7:24; 1ST CORINTHIANS 2:15; 1ST SAMUEL 16:7"> And to me it is for the least thing, that I be deemed of you (or if I be judged by you), or of man’s day; but neither I deem (or I judge) myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 143:2; 1ST JOHN 3:20–21; PSALMS 130:3"> For I am nothing over-trusting, &#91;or guilty&#93;, to myself, but not in this thing I am justified; for he that deemeth me, is the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JAMES 5:7; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:10; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:13"> Therefore do not ye deem before the time, till that the Lord come, which shall lighten the hid things of (the) darknesses, and shall show the counsels of (the) hearts; and then praising shall be to each man of God. (And so do not judge before the time, until that the Lord come, who shall lighten things hidden in the darkness, and shall reveal the plans in the hearts; and then praises shall be to each person in God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 12:6; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:4; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:19"> And, brethren, I have transfigured these things into me and into Apollos, for you; that in us ye learn (or so that ye can learn from us), lest over that it is written &#91;or lest over that that is written&#93;, one against another be blown with pride for another &#91;man&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOHN 3:27; 1ST PETER 4:10; JAMES 1:17"> Who deemeth thee? (or Who judgeth thee?) And what hast thou, that thou hast not received? And if thou hast received (it), what gloriest thou (or why boastest thou), as (if) thou haddest not received (it)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKE 6:25; PROVERBS 13:7; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:6"> Now ye be &#91;full-&#93;filled, now ye be made rich; ye reign without us; and I would that ye reign, (so) that also we (could) reign with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:33; ROMANS 8:36; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:8–12"> And I guess, that God showed us the last apostles, (or For I think, that God hath made us apostles the last, or the lowest), (yea), as those that be sent to the death; for we be made a spectacle to the world, and to angels, and to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 2:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:24; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:18"> We fools for Christ, but ye prudent in Christ; we sick, but ye strong; ye noble, but we unnoble. (We be fools for the Messiah, but ye be wise in the Messiah; we be frail or weak, but ye be strong; ye be noble, but we be ignoble.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 8:35; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:23–27; PHILIPPIANS 4:12"> Till into this hour we hunger, and thirst, and be naked, and be smitten with buffets, and we be unstable, &#91;moving from place to place&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:9; MATTHEW 5:11; 1ST PETER 4:12–14"> and we travail working with our hands; we be cursed, and we bless; we suffer persecution, and we abide long;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:45; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:22"> we be blasphemed, and we beseech; as cleansings of this world we be made the out-casting(s) of all things till yet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 2:11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:15; 3RD JOHN 1:4"> I write not these things, (so) that I confound you, but (that) I warn you as my most dearworthy sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GALATIANS 4:19; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:10; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:14"> For why if ye have ten thousand of under-masters in Christ, but not many fathers; for in Christ Jesus I have (en)gendered you by the gospel. (For ye may have ten thousand tutors in the Messiah, but only one father; for in the Messiah Jesus I have begat you, by preaching the Gospel or the Good News.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 11:1; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:9; PHILIPPIANS 3:17"> Therefore, brethren, I pray you, be ye followers of me, as I of Christ. (And so brothers, I beseech you, be followers of me, like I am of the Messiah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 1:2; 1ST CORINTHIANS 16:10; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:17"> Therefore I sent to you Timothy, which is my most dearworthy son, and faithful in the Lord, which shall teach you &#91;or shall admonish you (in)&#93; my ways, that be in Christ Jesus, (or who shall teach you my way of life, that is in the Messiah Jesus); as I teach everywhere in each church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 4:6–8; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:2; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:21"> As though I should not come to you, so some be blown with pride;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 13:1–4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:21"> but I shall come to you soon, if God will (or God willing); and I shall know not the word(s) of them that be blown with pride, but the virtue (or but the power).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 2:4; 1ST THESSALONIANS 1:5; 2ND CORINTHIANS 10:4–5"> For the realm of God is not in word(s), but in virtue. (For the Kingdom of God is not of words, but of power.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 13:2; 2ND CORINTHIANS 13:10; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:7"> What will ye? Shall I come to you in a rod, or in charity, and in a spirit of mildness? (What desire ye? Shall I come to you with a rod, or with love, and in a spirit of meekness and humility?)</span>
