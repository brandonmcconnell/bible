<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ROMANS 1:1; EPHESIANS 1:1; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:1"> Paul, called (an) apostle of Jesus Christ, by the will of God, and Sosthenes, (a) brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ROMANS 1:7; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:9–11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:30"> to the church of God that is at Corinth, to them that be hallowed in Christ Jesus, and called saints, (or to those who be consecrated in the Messiah Jesus, and be called God’s people), with all that inwardly call the name of our Lord Jesus Christ &#91;or with all that in-call the name of our Lord Jesus Christ&#93;, in each place of them and of ours (or in every place of theirs and of ours),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ROMANS 1:7; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:2; 1ST PETER 1:2"> grace (be) to you and (the) peace of God, our Father, and of the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ROMANS 1:8; JOHN 14:26; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:14"> I do thankings to my God (for)evermore for you, in the grace of God that is given to you in Christ Jesus. (I give thanks always to my God for you, for the grace of God which is given to you in the Messiah Jesus.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 9:11; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:7; COLOSSIANS 2:3"> For in all things ye be made rich in him, in each word, and in each cunning, (or knowing), &#91;or science&#93;, (or in all knowledge),</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:2; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:8; 1ST TIMOTHY 2:6"> as the witnessing of Christ is confirmed in you; (as the testimony of the Messiah is confirmed in you;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:20; 2ND PETER 3:12; HEBREWS 9:28"> so that nothing fail to you in any grace (or of any blessing), that abide the showing &#91;or the revelation&#93; of our Lord Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:23–24; 1ST THESSALONIANS 3:13; COLOSSIANS 1:22"> which also shall confirm you into the end without crime, &#91;or great sin&#93;, in the day of the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 3:3; HEBREWS 10:23; DEUTERONOMY 7:9"> A true God &#91;or Forsooth God is true&#93;, by whom ye be called into the fellowship of his Son Jesus Christ our Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:1–4; EPHESIANS 4:1–7; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:25"> But, brethren, I beseech you, by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that ye all say the same thing, and that dissensions, &#91;or schisms, or divisions, or discords&#93;, be not among you; but be ye perfect in the same wit, (or in the same thinking, or of the same mind), and in the same cunning, (or the same knowing, or with the same knowledge).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:6; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:4; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:23–25"> For, my brethren, it is told to me of them that be at Chloe’s, that strives (or arguments) be among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 3:21–23; JOHN 1:42; MATTHEW 23:9–10"> And I say that, that each of you saith, For I am of Paul, and I am of Apollos, and I am of Cephas, &#91;that is, Peter&#93;, but I am of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:5; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:38"> Whether Christ is parted? (or Is the Messiah divided?) whether Paul was crucified for you, either ye be baptized in the name of Paul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:8; ROMANS 16:23; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:4"> I do thankings to my God (or I give thanks to my God), that I baptized none of you, but Crispus and Gaius;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 11:2; JOHN 3:28–29; JOHN 7:18"> lest any man say, that ye be baptized in my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 16:15; 1ST CORINTHIANS 16:17; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:33"> And I baptized also the house of Stephanas, but I know not, that I baptized any other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 2:1; 1ST CORINTHIANS 2:13; 2ND PETER 1:16"> For Christ sent me not to baptize, but to preach the gospel &#91;or to evangelize&#93;; not in (the) wisdom of word(s), that the cross of Christ be not voided away (or so that the cross of the Messiah be made of no consequence).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 2:14; ROMANS 1:16; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:10"> For the word of the cross is folly to them that perish; but to them that be made safe, that is to say, to us, it is the virtue of God. (For the word of the cross is foolishness to those who perish; but for those who be saved, that is to say, for us, it is the power of God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 29:14; JEREMIAH 8:9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:19"> For it is written, I shall destroy the wisdom of wise men, and I shall reprove the prudence of prudent men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 1:22; JOB 12:17; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:19"> Where is the wise man? where is the wise lawyer? &#91;or where is the writer, or the man of (the) law?&#93; where is the purchaser of this world? Whether God hath not made the wisdom of this world fond &#91;or foolish&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKE 10:21; ROMANS 1:20–22; MATTHEW 11:25"> For the world, in (the) wisdom of God, knew not God by wisdom, &#91;or For why for in the wisdom of God, the world knew not God by wisdom&#93;, it pleased to God, by &#91;the&#93; folly of preaching, to make them safe that believed, (or it pleased God, by the foolishness of preaching, to save those who believed).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:1–4; MATTHEW 12:38–39; MARK 8:11"> For Jews seek signs, and Greeks seek wisdom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 2:14; 1ST CORINTHIANS 2:2; 1ST PETER 2:8"> but we preach Christ crucified, to the Jews (a) cause of stumbling, and to heathen men folly; (but we preach the crucified Messiah, to the Jews a stumbling block, and to the Gentiles foolishness;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:30; COLOSSIANS 2:3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:18"> but to those Jews and Greeks that be called, we preach Christ the virtue of God, and the wisdom of God. (but to those Jews and Greeks who be called, we preach the Messiah to be the power of God, and the wisdom of God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ROMANS 11:33–36; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:18; ZECHARIAH 4:6–7"> For that that is (a) folly thing of God, is wiser than men; and that that is the feeble, &#91;or (the) frail&#93;, thing of God, is stronger than men. (For that which is a foolish thing of God, is wiser than men; and that which is the frail or the weak thing of God, is stronger than men.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JAMES 2:5; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:18–20; JAMES 1:9–11"> But, brethren, see ye your calling, (or But, brothers, see to your calling); for not many (of you be) wise men after the flesh, not many mighty, not many noble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JAMES 2:5; MATTHEW 11:25; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:20"> But God chose those things that be fond &#91;or that be foolish&#93; of the world, to confound wise men; and God chose the feeble, &#91;or (the) frail&#93;, things of the world, to confound the strong things; (But God chose those things that be foolish in the world, to shame the wise; and God chose the frail or the weak things of the world, to shame the strong;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOB 34:24; ROMANS 4:17; ISAIAH 2:11"> and God chose the unnoble things and (the) despisable things of the world, and those things that be not, to destroy those things that be;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ROMANS 3:27; JEREMIAH 9:23; EPHESIANS 2:9"> that each man have not glory in his sight. (so that no one can boast in the presence of God or before God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:7; PHILIPPIANS 3:9; ROMANS 8:1"> But of him ye be in Christ Jesus, which is made of God to us wisdom, and rightwiseness, and holiness, and again-buying; (For ye be in the Messiah Jesus, whom God made to be for us our wisdom, and righteousness, and holiness, and redemption;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:23–24; 2ND CORINTHIANS 10:17; PHILIPPIANS 3:3"> that, as it is written, He that glorieth, have glory in the Lord. (so that, as it is written, He who boasteth, boast of the Lord.)</span>
