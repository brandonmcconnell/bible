<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Do not thou for a friend (or instead of a friend) be made (an) enemy to thy neighbour; for why an evil man shall inherit upbraiding and despising, and each sinner envious and double-tongued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Enhance thee not in the thought of thy soul, as a bull doeth; lest thy virtue &#91;or strength&#93; be hurtled down by folly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and it eat thy leaves, and lose (or destroy) thy fruits, and thou be left as a dry tree in (the) desert &#91;or (the) wilderness&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Forsooth a wicked, &#91;or shrewd, (or depraved)&#93; soul shall lose &#91;or destroy&#93; him that hath it, and it giveth him into the joy of the enemy, and it shall lead forth into the part of wicked men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> A sweet word multiplieth friends, and assuageth enemies; and a tongue well gracious shall be plenteous &#91;or abound&#93; in a good man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Many peaceable men be to thee, and (or but) one (out) of a thousand be a counsellor to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> If thou hast a friend, have him in temptation, that is, prove thou him in thine adversity, and betake not lightly &#91;or not lightly open, or trust&#93;, thyself to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> For there is a friend by (or for) his (own) time, and he shall not dwell &#91;or abide&#93; in the day of tribulation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And there is a friend which is turned to enmity; and there is a friend, that shall show openly hatred, and chiding &#91;or strife&#93;, and despisings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Forsooth there is a friend, (a) fellow of (the) table, and (he) dwell-eth not in the day of need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> If a friend dwelleth steadfast, he shall be as a man even with thee, and he shall do trustily in (or with) thy menial &#91;or homely&#93; things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> If he meeketh himself before thee, and hideth him(self) from thy face, thou shalt have good friendship of one accord &#91;or of one will&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Be thou parted from thine enemies, and take heed of thy friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> A faithful friend is a strong defending &#91;or strong protection&#93;; for-sooth he that findeth him, findeth treasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> No comparison is to a faithful friend; weighing of gold and of silver is not worthy against the goodness of his faithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> A faithful friend is(the) medicine of life, and of undeadliness (or of immortality); and they that dread the Lord, shall find him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He that dreadeth the Lord, shall have evenly good friendship; for why his friend shall be at the likeness of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Son, from thy youth take thou doctrine, and till to &#91;thine&#93; hoar hairs thou shalt find wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> As he that eareth (or ploweth), and that soweth, nigh thou to it, and abide thou (for) the good fruits there-of. For thou shalt travail a little in the work thereof, and thou shalt eat soon of the generations thereof &#91;or the gettings of it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Wisdom is over-sharp or is full-sharp to untaught men, and an heart-less man shall not dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> As the virtue (or the strength) of a stone, proving shall be in them; and they shall not tarry to cast away it &#91;or to throw it afar&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Forsooth the wisdom of teaching is by the name thereof, and it is not open to many men; but it dwelleth with them, of whom it is known, till to the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Son, hear thou, and take the counsel of understanding, and cast thou not away my counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Set in thy foot into the stocks thereof, and thy neck into the bies, (or the bands), &#91;or the collars&#93; thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Make subject thy shoulder, and bear it, and be thou not annoyed (or harmed) in the bonds thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> In all thy will go to it, and in all thy virtue keep the ways thereof &#91;or of it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Inquire thou (of or about) it &#91;or Ensearch it&#93;, and it shall be made open to thee; and thou made holding wisdom forsake not it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> For in the last things thou shalt find rest therein, and it shall turn &#91;or shall be turned&#93; to thee into delight-ing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And the stocks thereof shall be to thee in defence &#91;or protection&#93; of strength, and the foundaments of virtue, and the bie(s), (or the bands), &#91;or the collars&#93; thereof, in(to) a stole of glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For why the fairness of life is in wisdom, and the bonds thereof be heal(th)ful, &#91;or wholesome&#93; binding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Thou shalt wear it as a stole of glory, and thou shalt set &#91;or shalt put&#93; on thee a crown of thanking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Son, if thou takest heed to me, thou shalt learn wisdom; and if thou givest thy will, thou shalt be wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> If thou bowest down thine ear, thou shalt take (or receive) teaching; and if thou lovest for to hear, thou shalt be wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Stand thou in the multitude of prudent priests, and be thou joined of (or in) heart to the wisdom of them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> (so) that thou mayest hear each telling of God, and the proverbs of praising flee or fly not away from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And if thou seest a wise man, wake thou to him, and thy foot &#91;often&#93; tread on the grees (or the steps) of his doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Have thou thought in (or on) the commandments of God, and be thou most busy in his behests; and he shall give to thee &#91;an&#93; heart, and covetous-ness of wisdom shall be given to thee.</span>
