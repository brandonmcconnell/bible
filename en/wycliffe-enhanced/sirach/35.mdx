<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that keepeth the word of God, multiplieth prayer. Wholeful &#91;or Wholesome&#93; sacrifice is to take heed to (or of) the commandments, and to depart &#91;or go away&#93; from all wicked-ness. And to offer the pleasing of sacrifice for unrightfulnesses &#91;or (for) unrightwisenesses&#93;, and beseeching for sins, is to go away from unrightful-ness &#91;or unrightwiseness&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He that offereth purest flour of wheat, shall yield grace, that is, shall give a pleasant (or a pleasing) service to God; and he that doeth mercy, offereth a sacrifice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> It is well pleasant &#91;or well-pleas-ing&#93; to the Lord, to go away from wickedness; and prayer is to go away from unrightfulness &#91;or unrightwise-ness&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Thou shalt not appear void (or empty) before the sight of God;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> for why all these things be done for the behests, (or for the commands, or the commandments) of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The offering of a just man &#91;or of the rightwise&#93; maketh fat the altar, and is (the) odour of sweetness in the sight of the Highest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The sacrifice of a just man is acceptable, and the Lord shall not for-get the mind (or the memory) of him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> With good will yield thou glory to God, and make thou not less the first fruits of thine hands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> In each gift &#91;or In all gifts&#93; make glad thy cheer, and in full out joying hallow thy tithes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Give thou to the Highest after his &#91;free&#93; gift, that is, after the quantity of thy goods, which thou hast of (or from) God; and with good eye make thou the findings of thine hands, that is, with rightful intent, ordain thy good works, to the praising of God and not of man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For why the Lord is a yielder, and he shall yield sevenfold so much &#91;or as much&#93; to thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Do not thou offer shrewd (or de-praved) gifts; for he shall not receive those &#91;or them&#93;. And do not thou behold an unjust &#91;or the unrightwise&#93; sacrifice; for the Lord is judge, and glory, or taking, (or favouring) of person(s) is not at &#91;or with&#93; him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The Lord shall not take a person against a poor man (or The Lord will not favour some person over a poor person); and he shall hear the prayer of him that is hurt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He shall not despise the prayers of a fatherless child, neither a widow, if she sheddeth &#91;or poured&#93; out (the) speech of wailing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Whether the tears of a widow go not down to the cheek, and the crying of her on him that leadeth forth those tears? For why those ascend from the cheek unto heaven, and the Lord hearing shall not delight in those &#91;or in them&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He that worshippeth God in delighting, shall be received; and his prayer shall nigh unto the clouds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The prayer of him that meeketh himself shall pierce (the) clouds, and till it nigheth (or approacheth), he shall not be comforted, and he shall not go away, till the Highest behold (him). And the Lord shall not be far &#91;or long away&#93;, but he shall judge just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men, and shall make doom;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and the Strongest shall not have patience in those &#91;or (towards) them&#93;, that (or till) he trouble the back(s) of them. And he shall yield vengeance to (the) folks &#91;or (the) Gentiles&#93;, till he take away the fullness &#91;or the plenty&#93; of proud men, and trouble (al)to-gether the sceptres of wicked men;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> till he yield to men after their deeds, and after the works of Adam<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="By (or For) Hebrews, this word ‘Adam’ signifieth man commonly.">a</a></sup>, and after the presumption of him; till he deem the doom of his people, and shall delight just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men in his mercy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The mercy of God is fair in the time of tribulation, as clouds of rain in the time of dryness &#91;or of drought&#93;.</span>
</div>
