<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Love thou not jealously the woman of thy bosom &#91;or Be thou not jealous to the woman of thy bosom&#93;; lest she show on thee the malice of evil doctrine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Give thou not to a woman the power of (or over) thy soul; lest she enter in thy virtue, and thou be shamed &#91;or confounded&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Behold thou not a woman of many wills (or desires), that coveteth now this man, now that man; lest peradventure thou fall into the snares of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Be thou not customable with a danceress, neither hire thou her; lest peradventure thou perish in the speedy work of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Behold thou not a virgin &#91;or a maiden&#93;; lest peradventure thou be caused to stumble in (or by) the fairness of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Give thou not thy soul to whores in anything; lest thou lose thee, and thy soul, and thine heritage, (or lest thou destroy thyself, and thy soul, and thy inheritance).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Do not thou behold about in the lanes of the city; neither err thou (or wander) in the large streets thereof. &#91;Do thou not behold about in the ways of the city; nor err thou about in the streets of it.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Turn away thy face from a woman well-arrayed; and behold thou not about the fairness of another or of another man’s wife. Many men have perished for the fairness of a woman; and thereby covetousness &#91;or lust&#93; burneth on high as fire or burneth out as fire. Each woman which is an whore, either customable to fornication, shall be defouled as a fen, or a turd, in the way. Many men wondering on the fairness of an alien woman were made reprovable, for why the speech of her burneth on high as fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Sit thou not in any manner with an alien woman, neither rest thou with her on a bed &#91;or nor lie thou with her upon the arm&#93;; and jangle &#91;or strive&#93; thou not with her in wine, lest peradventure thine heart bow &#91;or she bow down thine heart&#93; into her, and thou fall &#91;or slide&#93; into perdition by thy blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Forsake thou not an eld &#91;or old&#93; friend; for a new friend shall not be like him. New wine is(like) a new friend; (after) it shall wax eld &#91;or old&#93;, and (then) thou shalt drink it with sweetness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Covet thou not the glory and riches of a sinner; for thou knowest not, what destroying of him shall come. &#91;Love thou not the glory and riches of the sinner; forsooth thou knowest not, what be to come (of) the turning upside-down of him.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The wrong of unjust &#91;or unright-wise&#93; men please not thee, and know thou that a wicked man &#91;or the unpious&#93; shall not please till to hells, &#91;or unto hell&#93;, (or the grave).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Be thou far from a man that hath power to slay, that is, from a cruel tyrant, and thou shalt not have suspicion of the dread of death; and if thou nighest to him, do not thou do any trespass &#91;or anything do amiss&#93;, lest peradventure he take away thy life. Know thou the communing of death; for thou shalt enter into the midst of snares, and thou shalt go on the armours, &#91;or arms&#93;, (or weapons) of them that sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> By thy virtue, keep thee (away) from thy neighbour that may speak against thee to a tyrant; and treat thou with wise men and prudent men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Just &#91;or Rightwise&#93; men be guests, or meat-frères, (or meal companions) to thee; and thy glorying be in the dread of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the thought of God be to thee in wit, that is, apply thy wit to think on God; and all thy telling-&#91;out&#93;be in the behests (or about the commands) of the Highest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Works shall be praised in (or from) the hand of craftsmen, and the prince of the people in the wisdom of his word; forsooth in the wit of elder men &#91;or of elders&#93; a word shall be praised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> A man, a jangler, &#91;or a tonguey man&#93;, is dreadful &#91;or fearful&#93; in his city; and a foolhardy man in his word shall be hateful.</span>
