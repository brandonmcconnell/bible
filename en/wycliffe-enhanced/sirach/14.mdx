<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Blessed is the man, that stood not by the word(s) of &#91;or from&#93; his mouth, and was not pricked in (or with) the sorrow of trespass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He is blessed, that hath not sorrow of (or in) his soul, and falleth not down &#91;or away&#93; from his hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Chattel, that is, riches, is without reason to a covetous man, and hard niggard, (or stingy, or miserly); and whereto is gold to an envious man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He that gathereth of his will un-justly, gathereth to (or for) other men; and another man shall make waste &#91;or do lechery&#93; in (or with) his goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> To what other man shall he be good, which is wicked to himself? and he shall not be merry in his goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Nothing is worse, than he that hath envy to himself; and this is the yielding of his malice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And if he doeth good, he doeth (it) unwittingly, and not willfully (or willingly); and at the last he showeth his malice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The eye of an envious man is wicked, and turning away the face, and despising his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The eye of the covetous man is never filled; he shall not be filled into the part of wickedness, till he perform unrightfulness &#91;or unrightwiseness&#93;, and make dry his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> An evil eye to evils, and the needy man shall not be filled of &#91;or with&#93; bread; and he shall be in sorrow on (or at) his table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Son, if thou hast, do well with thyself, and offer thou worthy offerings to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Be thou mindful that death shall not tarry, and the testament of hells &#91;or of hell&#93;, that is, the ordinance of God, of the death of each man, which is showed to thee; for why the testament of this world shall die by death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Before (thy) death do thou good to (or for) thy friend, and by thy mights &#91;or strengths&#93; stretch thou forth, and give to a poor man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Be thou not deceived &#91;or beguiled&#93; of (or from) a good day, and a little part of a good day pass not thee (unfulfilled or unappreciated).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Whether thou shalt not leave to other men thy sorrows, and &#91;thy&#93; travails?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> In the parting of lot give thou, and take, and justify thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Before thy death work thou rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;; for at hells, &#91;or hell&#93;, (or in the grave), it is not to find meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Each man shall wax eld &#91;or old&#93; as hay, and as a leaf bringing fruit in a green tree. Others be engendered, and others be cast &#91;or fall&#93; down; so the generation of flesh and blood, another is ended, and another is born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Each corruptible work shall fail in the end; and he that worketh it, shall go with it. And all &#91;or each&#93; chosen work shall be justified; and he that worketh it, shall be honoured in (or by) it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Blessed is the man, that shall dwell in wisdom, and that shall be-think in rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;, and shall think in wit (about) the beholding of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Which &#91;or Who&#93; thinketh out, either findeth out, the ways of him in his heart, and shall be understanding in the hid things of him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> going as a searcher after it, and standing in the ways of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Which beholdeth by the windows thereof, and heareth in (or at) the gates thereof; &#91;Who beholdeth by the windows of it, and in the gates of it is hearing;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> which &#91;or who&#93; resteth nigh the house thereof, and setteth a stake in the walls thereof. &#91;who resteth beside the house of it, and in the walls of it pitcheth a pale.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> He shall set his little house at the hands of him, and goods (or good things) shall rest in his little house, by (the) enduring of the world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> he shall set his sons under the covering thereof &#91;or the roof of it&#93;, and he shall dwell under the boughs &#91;or branches&#93; thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> he shall be covered under the covering thereof &#91;or the roof of it&#93; from heat, and he shall rest in the glory thereof.</span>
