<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Son, thou hast done sin? &#91;or Son, hast thou sinned?&#93; add thou (to it) not again; but beseech thou for the former sins, that those &#91;or they&#93; be forgiven to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> As from the face of a serpent &#91;or a shadow adder&#93; flee thou sins; and if thou nighest (or approachest) to them, those &#91;or they&#93; shall take thee. The teeth of a lion be(as) the teeth there-of, that slay the souls of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> All wickedness is as a sharp sword on either side; health is not to the wound thereof &#91;or of it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Chidings and wrongs shall destroy chattel &#91;or substance&#93;; and an house that is over-rich, shall be destroyed by pride; so the chattel &#91;or substance&#93; of a proud man shall be drawn up by the root.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The prayer of a poor man shall come from the mouth unto (the) ears of God; and doom shall come to him hastily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He that hateth reproving, is (in) a step of the sinner; and (or but) he that dreadeth God, shall be turned &#91;or converted&#93; to (him in) his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> A mighty man with an hardy tongue is known afar; and a witty (or a witting or a knowing) man can or knoweth how to keep himself &#91;or to slide&#93; from that man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> He that buildeth his house with other men’s costs, is as he that gather-eth his stones in winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Sheaves, or stubble, &#91;or flax top(s)&#93;, gathered together is the synagogue of sinners; and the ending of them is the flame of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The way of sinners is set (al)to-gether &#91;or planted&#93; with stones; and in the end of them be hells<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="It is said ‘hells’ in plural number, for many places be there.">a</a></sup>, &#91;or hell&#93;, and darknesses, and pains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> He that keepeth rightfulness, shall hold the wit (or the understanding) thereof. The perfection of God’s dread is wisdom and wit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> He shall not be taught, which is not wise in (what is) good. Forsooth unwisdom is, which is plenteous in evil; and wit is not, where (there) is bitterness. &#91;He shall not be learned, that is not wise in good. There is forsooth unwisdom, that aboundeth in evil; and there is not wit, where is bitterness.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The cunning, (or knowing, or the knowledge) of a wise man shall be plenteous as flowing (water); and the counsel of him dwelleth as a well of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The heart of a fool is as a broken vessel; and it shall not hold any wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Whatever wise word a cunning (or a knowing) man heareth, he shall praise (it), and lay to (or add to it). A lecherous man heard (it), and it shall displease him; and he shall cast &#91;or throw&#93; it away behind his back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The telling of a fool is as a burden in (or on) the way; for why grace shall be found in (or on) the lips of a wise man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The mouth of a prudent man is sought in the church; and men shall think (on) his words in their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> As an house destroyed, so is wisdom to a fool; and the cunning (or the knowing) of an unwise man is words that may not be or be not able to be told out &#91;or (that be) untellable&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Stocks in (or on) the feet is teach-ing &#91;or doctrine&#93; to a fool; and as bonds of hands on the right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> A fool enhanceth his voice in laughing; but a wise man shall laugh scarcely still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Teaching is a golden ornament to a prudent man; and as an ornament of the arm &#91;or an arm-circle&#93; in (or on) the right arm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The foot of a fool is light (or easily) into the house of a neighbour; and a wise man shall be ashamed of the person of a mighty man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> A fool beholdeth from the window into the house; but a learned man shall stand withoutforth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> It is folly of a man to harken by the door; and a prudent man shall be grieved by despising &#91;or with strife&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The lips of unprudent men shall tell fond things &#91;or follies&#93;; but the words of prudent men shall be weigh-ed in a balance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The heart of fools is in their mouth; and the mouth of wise men is in their heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> When a wicked man curseth the devil, he curseth his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> A privy backbiter shall defoul his soul, and in all things he shall be hated, and he that dwelleth, shall be hated; a still man and wise shall be honoured.</span>
