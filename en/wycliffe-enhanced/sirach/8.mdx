<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Chide &#91;or Strive&#93; thou not with a mighty man, lest thou fall into his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Strive thou not with a rich man, lest peradventure he make (a) play again-ward to (or towards) thee &#91;or he set strife to thee&#93;. For why gold and silver hath lost (or destroyed) many men; and it stretcheth forth till to the heart&#91;s&#93; of kings, and turneth (them).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Chide thou not with a man, a jangler, &#91;or Strive thou not with a tonguey man&#93;, and lay thou not trees into his fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Commune thou not with an untaught man, lest he speak evil of thy kindred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Despise thou not a man turning away himself from sin, neither upbraid thou him &#91;nor put thou reproof to him&#93;; have thou mind, that all we be in corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Despise thou not a man in his eld (age); for why (some) of us men wax eld &#91;or old&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Do not thou make joy of (or over) thine enemy (being) dead, witting that all we die (or knowing that we all die), and will (or desire) not (to) come into joy of (or with) our enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Despise thou not the telling of wise priests, and be thou conversant &#91;or (al)together dwell&#93; in the proverbs of them; for of (or from) them thou shalt learn wisdom, and teaching of understanding, and to serve without (com)plaint to great men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The telling of elder men pass not (by) thee; for they have learned of (or from) their fathers. For of (or from) them thou shalt learn understanding; and in the time of need thou shalt give &#91;an&#93; answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Kindle thou not the coals of sinners, and reprove them; and be thou not burnt with the flame of &#91;the&#93; fire of their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Stand thou not against the face of a man full of despising &#91;or of the strifeful&#93;; lest he sit as an espyer to thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Do not thou lend to a man stronger than thou &#91;or than thyself&#93;; that (or but) if thou hast lent, have thou it as lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Promise thou not above &#91;or over&#93; thy power or virtue; that (or but) if thou hast promised, bethink thou as yielding, for thou art holden to do thy might, (that is, thou art held to pay it back).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Deem thou not against a judge; for he deemeth after that, that is just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Go thou not in the way with an hardy man, lest peradventure he aggregate (or bring together all) his evils in (or on) thee; for he goeth after his (own) will (or desire), and thou shalt perish together with (him through) his folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Make thou not chiding &#91;or jangling&#93; with a wrathful man, and go thou not into (a) desert with an hardy man; for why blood, that is, shedding out of innocent blood, is as nought before him, and where none help is, (or where there is no help), he shall hurtle thee down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Have thou not counsel with fools; for they may not (or be not able to) love, no but those things that please them(selves).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Make thou not a counsel before a stranger; for thou knowest not, what he shall bring forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Make not thine heart known to each man &#91;or To all men thine heart open thou not&#93;, but only to a very (or a true) friend, and proved; lest peradventure he bring to thee false grace, that is, feigned friendship, and despise thee.</span>
