<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> The power of (the) earth is in the hand of God, and all the wickedness of heathen men &#91;or the Gentiles&#93; is abominable; and he shall raise (up) a profitable governor at a time &#91;or in (due) time&#93; on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The power of man is in the hand of God; and he shall set his honour on the face of a wise man in the law &#91;or the scribe&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Have thou not mind on all the wrong of the neighbour; and do thou nothing in the works of wrong-(doing).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Pride is hateful before God and men; and all the wickedness of (the) heathen men &#91;or (the) Gentiles&#93;is abominable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> A realm is translated, either taken away, &#91;or borne over&#93;, from a folk into folk for unrightfulnesses, and wrongs, and despisings, and diverse guiles, &#91;or for unrightwisenesses, and wrongs, and strives, and diverse treacheries&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Nothing is curseder than an avarice man. What (or Why) art thou proud, thou earth and ashes? <span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Each power is short life (or short-lived); long sickness &#91;or infirmity&#93; grieveth the leech (or the physician). A leech (or The physician) cutteth away &#91;or cutteth off&#93; short sickness; so and a king is today &#91;or today is&#93;, and tomorrow he shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Forsooth when a man shall die, he shall inherit serpents, and beasts, and worms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The beginning of &#91;the&#93; pride of man was to be apostate &#91;or to go backward&#93; from God; for his heart went away from him that made him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For why pride is the beginning of all sin &#91;or For the beginning of all sin is pride&#93;; he that holdeth it, shall be filled with cursings, and it shall destroy him into the end. <span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> God destroyed the seats (or the thrones) of proud dukes; and made mild men to sit for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> God made dry the roots of proud folks; and planted meek men of those folks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The Lord destroyed the lands of folks &#91;or Gentiles&#93;; and lost those &#91;or destroyed them&#93; unto the foundament (or unto the foundation of the earth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He made dry the roots of them, and lost (or destroyed) them; and made the mind of them (or their memory) to cease from the earth. <span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Pride was not made to (or for) men; neither wrathfulness to (or for) the nation of women, that is, to all men born of women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> This seed of men that dreadeth God, shall be honoured; but this seed shall be dishonoured, that over-pass-eth (or that passeth over) the commandments of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> In the midst of brethren the governor of them is in honour; and they that dread God, shall be in his eyes, that is, shall be honourable, and please him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The glory of rich men honoured and of poor men is the dread of God.</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The judge is great, and he is mighty in honour; and (or but) he is not greater than that man that dread-eth God. &#91;Great is a judge, and the mighty is in worship; and (or but) he is not more than he that dreadeth God.&#93;</span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> A poor man hath glory by his learning and dread; and there is a man that is honoured for his chattel &#91;or his substance&#93;.</span>
