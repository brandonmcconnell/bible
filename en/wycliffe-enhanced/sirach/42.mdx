<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Be thou not (a)shamed for all these things; and take &#91;or accept&#93; thou not a person, (so) that thou do trespass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Be thou ware of the law and testament of the Highest, of doom to justify a wicked man; &#91;of the law of the Highest, and of the testament of doom, to justify the unpious;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> of the word of fellows, and of way-goers, and of the giving of (the) heritage of friends;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> of the evenness of balance, and of weights, of the getting of many things, and of few things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> of (the) corruption of buying, and of merchants, and of much chastising &#91;or much discipline&#93; of sons; and of a worst servant, to make the side to bleed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> A sealing, either closing, is good on a wicked woman. Where be many hands, close thou (up);</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and whatever thing thou shalt betake (or receive), number thou, and weigh thou; forsooth describe thou, either write (down), each gift, and taking (or receiving in).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Abstain thou from the teaching of an unwitty (or unwise) man, and fool, and of elder men that be deemed of (or by the) young men; and thou shalt be learned in all things, and thou shalt be commendable &#91;or (ap)prov-able (or approved)&#93; in the sight of all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> An hid daughter of a father is waking &#91;or watch(ing)&#93; and busyness of (or for) him; she shall take away sleep; lest peradventure she be made (an) adulteress in her young waxing age, and lest she dwelling with the husband, be made hateful;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> lest anytime she be defouled in her virginity &#91;or be polluted in her maidenhood&#93;, and be found with child in the keeping of her father; lest peradventure she dwelling with the husband, do trespass, either certainly be made barren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Ordain thou keeping &#91;or ward&#93; on a lecherous daughter, lest any time she make thee to come into shame &#91;or reproof&#93; to &#91;thine&#93; enemies, of backbiting in the city, and of casting out of the people; and she make thee ashamed in the multitude of people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Do not thou take heed to each man (or to each person) in the fair-ness, that is, in delighting in the beholding of his (or their) fairness; and do not thou dwell in the midst of women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For why a moth cometh forth of (or from) clothes, and (the) wicked-ness of a man cometh forth of (or from) a woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For why the wickedness of a man is better, that is, less evil, than a woman doing well, and a woman shaming into shame (or reproach)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See note above, for Chapter 25, verse 13.">a</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Therefore be thou mindful of the works of the Lord; and I shall tell (out) the works of the Lord, which I saw, in the words of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The sun lightening &#91;or shining through&#93; beheld by all things; and the work thereof is full of the glory of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Whether the Lord made not holy men to tell out all his marvels, which the Lord Almighty steadfast in his glory shall confirm?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He shall ensearch the depth, and the heart of men; and he shall think in (or on) the fellness, &#91;or the sly wit, or guile&#93;, of them. For the Lord knew all cunning (or all knowing or knowl-edge), and beheld into the sign(s) of the world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> telling &#91;out&#93; those things that be passed, and those things that shall come; showing the steps of hid things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And no thought passeth him (by), and no word hideth itself from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He made fair the great works of his wisdom, which is before the world, and till into the world; neither anything is increased &#91;or added&#93;, neither is decreased &#91;or less(en)ed&#93;, and he hath no need to the counsel of any &#91;man&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> All his works be full desirable, and to behold, as a sparkle which is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> All these things live, and dwell into the world (or evermore); and all things obey to him in all need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> All things be double, one against one; and he made not anything to fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> He shall confirm the goods of each &#91;or of everything&#93;; and who shall be filled, seeing his glory?</span>
