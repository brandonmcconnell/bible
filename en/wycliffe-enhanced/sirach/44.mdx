<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Praise we glorious men, and our fathers &#91;and mothers&#93; in their gener-ation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The Lord made much glory by his great doing, from the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Great men in virtue were lords in their powers, and rich in their prudence; telling in (or by the) prophets, (or by prophecies), the dignity of (the) prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and commanding in (the) present people, and telling holiest words to (the) peoples, by the virtue of prudence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Seeking manners of music in their cunning (or in their knowing), and telling songs &#91;or ditties&#93; of scriptures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Rich men in virtue, having the study of fairness, making peace in their houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> All these men got glory in the generations of their folk; and be had in praisings in their days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> They that were born of them, left a name to tell the praisings of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And some there be, of which is no mind (or memory); they perished as they that were not, and they were born as not born; and their sons perished with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But also the men of mercy be, whose pieties &#91;or piousnesses&#93; failed not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and good heritage dwelled continually with the seed of them. And the seed of their sons’ sons stood in (the) testament,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and the heritage of their sons dwelleth for them, till into without end;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> the seed of them, and the glory of them, shall not be forsaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The bodies of them be buried in peace; and the name of them shall live into generations and generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> (The) Peoples (shall) tell (of) the wisdom of them; and the church telleth the praising of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Enoch<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="He shall come again from paradise about the world’s end, with Elijah, to preach against (the) Antichrist.">a</a></sup>pleased God, and was translated into paradise, (so) that he give wisdom to folks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Noah was found perfect and just &#91;or rightwise&#93;, and he was made reconciling in the time of wrathful-ness. Therefore residue seed was left to (the) earth, when the great flood was made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> (The) Testaments of the world were set with him, lest all flesh might be done away by the great flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Abraham was the great father of the multitude of folks; and none was found like him in glory, that is, in virtuous work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> which kept the law of (the) high God&#91;or of the Highest&#93;, and was in testament (or in covenant) with him. He made a testament to stand in his flesh; and he was found faithful in temptation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Therefore God with an oath gave to him glory in his folk; God made him to increase, as an heap of earth, and to enhance his seed as (the) stars, and to inherit them from the sea unto the sea, from the flood (or the river) unto the ends of (the) earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And to (or for) Isaac God did in the same manner, (as) for Abraham, his father. The Lord gave to him the blessing of all folks; and confirmed his testament</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> on the head of Jacob. He knew him in his blessings, and gave heritage to him; and parted &#91;or divided&#93; to him a part in (the) twelve lineages.</span>
