<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The wisdom of a man made meek shall enhance his head; and shall make him to sit in the midst of great men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Praise thou not a man in (or for) his fairness; neither despise thou a man in (or for) his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> A bee is little among birds; and his fruit hath the beginning of sweet-ness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Have thou never glory in cloth-ing, and be thou not enhanced (or exalted) in the day of thine honour; for why the works of the Highest alone be wonderful, and his works be glorious, and hid, and unseen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Many tyrants have set in (or upon a) throne; and a man of whom was no supposing bare the diadem (or crown).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Many mighty men be oppressed strongly; and glorious men be given into the hands of other men. &#91;Many mighty men be oppressed greatly; and the glorious be taken into the hands of other men.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Before that thou ask, blame thou not any man; and when thou hast asked, blame thou justly &#91;or chastise thou rightly&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Before that thou hear, answer thou not a word; and in the midst of elder men add thou not to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Strive thou not, of (or in) that thing that dis-easeth &#91;or grieveth&#93; not thee; and stand thou not in the doom of sins &#91;or (with) sinners&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Son, thy deeds be not in many things; and if thou art rich, thou shalt not be without part of guilt. For if thou pursuest &#91;or shalt follow&#93;(after), thou shalt not &#91;over&#93;-take; and thou shalt not escape, if thou runnest before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> There is a man travailing, and hast(en)ing, and sorrowing, and unpious; and by so much the more he shall not have plenty &#91;or he shall not abound&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> There is a man fade, that is, feeble, failing more than others, needy of recovering, failing more in virtue, and plenteous in poverty; and the eye of God beheld him in good, and raised him (up) from his lowness, &#91;There is a man withered, needing recovering, more failing in virtue, and abounding in poorness; and the eye of God beheld him in good, and reared him (up) from his lowness;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and enhanced his head; and many men wondered &#91;or marvelled&#93; in (or about) him, and honoured &#91;or worshipped&#93; God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Goods and evils, that is, prosperities and adversities, life and death, poverty and honesty (or honours or riches), be of (or from) God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Wisdom, and learning, and cunning (or knowing) of the law be with the Lord; love &#91;or loving&#93; and the ways of good men be at, &#91;or with&#93;, (or from) him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Error and darknesses be made together to (or with) sinners; forsooth they that make full out joy in evil, wax eld &#91;or old&#93; together into evils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The gift of God dwelleth to (or with) just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men; and (the) increasings of him shall have prosperities &#91;into&#93; without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Some man there is made rich in doing scarcely &#91;or (in) scarcely doing&#93;, and this is the part of his meed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> in that that he saith, I have found rest to (or for) me, and now I alone shall eat of my goods. And he know not that time passeth him, and death nigheth (or approacheth), and he shall leave all things to other men, and shall die &#91;or and die&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Stand thou in thy testament, and speak thou (al)together in it; and wax thou eld &#91;or old&#93; in the work of thy behests (or of thy commands).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Dwell &#91;or Abide&#93; thou not in the works of sinners; but trust thou in God, and dwell in thy place. For it is easy in the eyes of God, suddenly to make honest (or rich) a poor man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The blessing of God hast(en)eth into the meed of a just man &#91;or the rightwise&#93;; and the going forth of him maketh fruit in swift honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Say thou not, What is need to me (or What do I need)? and what goods (or good things) shall be (for) me hereafter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Say thou not, I am sufficient, and what shall I be made worse hereafter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> In the day of goods (or good things), be thou not unmindful of evils, and in the day of evils, be thou not unmindful of goods (or good things); (On a good day do not forget the bad days, and on a bad day do not forget the good days;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> for it is easy before God to yield in the day of death, to each man after his ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> The malice of one hour maketh forgetting of most lechery; and in the end of a man is making naked of his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Praise thou not any man before his death; for why a man is known in (or by) his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Bring thou not each man into thine house; for why many treasons be of a guileful man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For why as the entrails of stinking things break out, and as a partridge is led into a trap, either (a) net, and as a capret is led into a snare, so and the heart of proud men; and as a behold-er seeing the fall of his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> For he turneth goods &#91;or good things&#93; into evils, and setteth treasons, and putteth a wem, (or a spot, or a blemish) on chosen men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Fire is increased of a sparkle (or by a spark), and blood is increased of (or by) a guileful (or treacherous) man; for why a sinful man setteth treason &#91;or waiteth&#93; to (or lieth in wait for) blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Take heed to thee from a guileful man, for he maketh &#91;or forgeth&#93; evils; lest peradventure he bring in on thee scorning without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Receive thou an alien to thee, and he shall destroy thee in a whirl-wind, and he shall make thee alien (or alienated) from thine own ways.</span>
