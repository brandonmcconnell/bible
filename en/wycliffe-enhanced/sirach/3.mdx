<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Dearworthy sons, hear ye the doom of the father; and do ye so, (so) that ye be safe (or be saved).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For why God honoured the father in (or over the) sons, and he seeketh, and hath made steadfast the doom of the mother into (or over the) sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> He that loveth God, shall pray for (his) sins, and he shall abstain himself from those &#91;or from them&#93;, and he shall be heard in the prayer of days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And as he that treasureth, so he that honoureth his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> He that honoureth his father, shall be made merry in sons, and he shall be heard in the day of his prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He that honoureth his father, shall live by &#91;or with&#93; longer life; and he that obeyeth to the father, shall refresh the mother, that is, shall comfort her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> He that dreadeth the Lord, honoureth father and mother; and he shall serve in work, and word, and in all patience to them that engendered &#91;or begat&#93; him, as to lords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Honour thy father, (so) that the blessing of God come to thee; and his blessing dwelleth in the last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The blessing of the father maketh steadfast the houses of sons; but the cursing of the mother draweth out the foundaments (or the foundations)&#91;by the root&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Have thou not glory in the des-pising of thy father; for it is not glory to thee, but confusion &#91;or shame&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For why the glory of a man is of the honour of his father; and the shame of the son is a father without honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Son, receive the eld &#91;or the last age&#93; of thy father, and make thou not him sorry &#91;or (have) sorrow&#93; in his life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and if he faileth in wit, give thou forgiveness, and despise thou not him in thy virtue (or in thy strength);</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> for why the alms-&#91;deed(s)&#93; of (or for) the father shall not be &#91;in&#93; forget-ting (or forgotten). For why good shall be restored to thee for the sin of the mother, and building &#91;up&#93; shall be made to thee in rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and it shall remember of thee in the day of tribulation, and thy sins shall be released &#91;or loos(en)ed&#93;, as ice in (the) clearness, either heat, of the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He is of full evil fame, that forsaketh the father; and he that wratheth the mother, is cursed of (or by) God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Son, perform thy works in mild-ness (or in meekness), and thou shalt be loved over the glory of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> In as much as thou art great, make thee meek in all things, and thou shalt find grace before God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> (This verse is omitted in the original text.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> for why the power of God alone is great, and he is honoured of (or by) meek men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Seek thou not higher things than thou &#91;or thyself&#93;, and inquire &#91;or search&#93; thou not (about) stronger things than thou;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> but ever&#91;more&#93; think thou (upon) those things, which God commanded to thee; and be thou not curious in (or about the) full many works of him. For it is not needful to (or for) thee to see with thine eyes those things, that be hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In superfluous things do not thou seek &#91;or ensearch&#93; manyfold; and be thou not curious in (the) many works of him; for why full many things above the wit of men be showed to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> For the suspicion of many men hath deceived them(selves), and with-held their wits in vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> (This verse is omitted in the original text.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> An hard heart shall have evil in the last time; and he that loveth peril shall perish therein. An heart that entereth &#91;or going&#93; by two ways, that is, that hath the knowing of good in understanding, and malice in will, shall not have prosperities, either rest; and a man of shrewd (or depraved) heart, shall be slandered in those (or shall be caused to stumble by them).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> A wicked heart shall be grieved in sorrows; and a sinner shall heap to, or add to, &#91;or lay to&#93;, to do sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Health (or Deliverance) shall not be to the synagogue of proud men; for why the thick wood &#91;or bush&#93; of sin shall be drawn out &#91;or taken up&#93; by the root in them, and it shall not be understood, of (or by) sinners who will not think on God’s dooms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The heart of a wise man is understood in wisdom, and a good ear shall hear wisdom with all covetousness. A wise heart and able to understand shall abstain itself from sins, and shall have prosperities in the works of rightfulness &#91;or rightwise-ness&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Water quencheth (a) fire burning, and alms-&#91;deeds&#93; against-standeth sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And God, the beholder of him that yieldeth grace, that is, (that) doeth alms-(deeds), hath mind after-ward; and he shall find steadfastness in the time of his fall.</span>
