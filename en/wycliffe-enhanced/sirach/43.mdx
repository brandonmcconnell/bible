<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The firmament of highness is the fairness thereof &#91;or The firmament of the height is the fairness of him&#93;; the fairness of heaven in the sight of glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The sun in beholding, telling in going out, is a wonderful vessel, the work of (the) high God&#91;or the (Most) High&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> In the time of midday it burneth the earth; and who shall be able to suffer (or to endure) in the sight of his (or its) heat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Keeping a furnace in the works of heat; the sun burning hills in three manners, sending out beams of fire &#91;or blasting out fiery beams&#93;, and shining again with his (or its) beams, blindeth (the) eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The Lord is great, that made it; and in the words of him it hast(en)ed (its) journey &#91;or hied the way&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And the moon in all men&#91;or things&#93; in his (or its) time is(a) show-ing of time, and a sign of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> A sign of the feast &#91;or holy&#93; day is taken of (or from) the moon; the light which is made little in the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The month is increasing by &#91;or after&#93; the name thereof, wonderfully into the ending. A vessel of castles (or of the hosts) in high things, shining gloriously in the firmament of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The fairness of heaven is the glory of stars; the Lord on high lighteneth the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> In (or At) the words of the Holy (One), those (or they) shall stand at the doom; and those shall not fail in their wakings &#91;or their watches&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> See thou the rainbow, and bless thou him that made it; it is full fair in his (or its) shining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> It went about heaven in the compass &#91;or the circuit&#93; of his (or its) glory; the hands of (the) high God&#91;or the (Most) High&#93; opened it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> By his commandment he hast(en)-ed the snow; and he hast(en)eth to send out the lightnings of his doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Therefore (the) treasures were opened, and clouds fled out &#91;or flew away&#93; as bees &#91;or birds&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> In his greatness he setted &#91;the&#93; clouds; and stones of hail were broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Hills shall be moved in (or at) his sight; and the south wind shall blow in (or at) his will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The voice of his thunder shall beat &#91;or beateth&#93; the earth; the tem-pest of the north, and the gathering together of (the) wind. And as a bird putting down to sit sprinkleth (or scattereth the) snow, and the coming down of that snow is as a locust drenching (or drowning) down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The eye shall wonder &#91;or marvel&#93; on the fairness of (the) whiteness thereof; and an heart dreadeth &#91;or the heart quaketh&#93; on the rain thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> He shall shed &#91;or pour&#93; out frost as salt on the earth; and while the wind bloweth, it shall be made as (the) cops (or tops) of a briar &#91;or the bramble (bush)&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The cold northern wind blew, and (the) crystal of (the) water froze together; it resteth on all the gather-ing together of waters, and it clotheth itself with waters, as with an habergeon (or breastplate).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And it shall devour hills, and it shall burn the desert; and it shall quench green thing as fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The medicine of all things is in the hast(en)ing of a cloud; a dew, meeting the heat coming of (or after the) burning, shall make it low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The wind was still &#91;or held his peace&#93; in (or by) the word of God; by his thought he made peaceable the depth of (the) waters; and the Lord Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Though the author of this book, that was an Hebrew man, wrote this book in Greek, nevertheless he setted in here an Hebrew name. (This gloss from the “Wycliffe Bible” notwithstanding, modern translations do not have this or any name here. T.P.N.)">a</a></sup>, that is, God, which is (the) Saviour of all men, planted it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> They that sail in (or on) the sea, tell out the perils thereof; and we hearing with our ears, shall wonder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> There be full clear works, and wonderful &#91;or marvellous&#93;, diverse kinds of beasts, and of all little beasts, and the creature(s), (or the creation), of wonderful fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The end of (the) way is confirmed for it &#91;or (by) him&#93;; and all things be made in (or by) the word of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> We say many things, and (yet) we fail in words; forsooth he is the (full) ending of words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> To what thing shall we be mighty, that have (or who hath) glory in all things? for he is all-mighty above &#91;or over&#93; all his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The Lord is fearedful, and full great; and his power is wonderful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Glorify ye the Lord as much as ever ye may or as ye be able to, yet he shall be mightier; and his great doing is wonderful &#91;or marvellous&#93;. Ye blessing the Lord, enhance (or exalt) him as much as ye may or as ye be able to; for he is more than all praising. Ye enhancing him shall be filled with virtue; travail ye not to know God perfectly in this life, for ye shall not take (or shall not receive it) perfectly, that is, for it is unpossible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Who saw him, and shall tell (it) out? and who shall magnify him, as he is from the beginning?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Many things greater than these be hid from us; for we have seen (only a) few things of his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Forsooth the Lord made all things; and he gave wisdom to men doing faithfully &#91;or piously&#93;.</span>
