<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> All wisdom is of (or from) the Lord God, and was ever with him, and is before the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Who numbered the gravel of the sea, and the drops of rain, and the days of the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Who measured the highness &#91;or the height&#93; of heaven, and the breadth of (the) earth, and the depth of the sea? Who ensearched (or searched for) the wisdom of God, that goeth before all things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Wisdom was formed first of all things, and the understanding of prudence, from the world, that is, from without beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The well of wisdom is the son of God in high things; and the entering of that wisdom is (the) everlasting commandments. &#91;The well of wisdom (is) the word of God in heights; and the in-going of it (is) everlasting commandments.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> To whom was the root of wisdom showed? and who knew the subtleties thereof?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> To whom was the lore &#91;or the discipline&#93; of wisdom showed, and made open? and who understood the multiplying of the entering thereof, that is, of the work thereof?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> One is the highest Creator &#91;or the maker (out) of nought&#93; of all things, almighty, and a mighty king, and worthy to be dreaded full much, sitting on the throne of that wisdom, and God having lordship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He formed it in the Holy Ghost, and he saw, and numbered, and he measured (it). And he shedded &#91;or poured&#93; out it &#91;up&#93; on all his works,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and on each flesh by his gift &#91;or and upon all flesh after his gift&#93;; he giveth it to them that love him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The dread of the Lord is glory, and glorying &#91;or joying&#93;, and gladness, and a crown of full out joying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The dread of the Lord shall delight the heart; and shall give gladness and joy into &#91;the&#93; length of days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> To him that dreadeth God, it shall be well in the last things, or in the last days; and he shall be blessed in the day of his death. Forsooth they to whom wisdom appeareth in sight, that is, by (the) revelation of prophecy, love it in sight, and in (the) knowing of his great things. The love of God is honourable wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The beginning of wisdom is the dread of the Lord; and it is formed together in the womb with faithful men, and it goeth with chosen women, and it is known with just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men and faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The dread (or fear) of the Lord is religiosity of cunning, (or knowing, or knowledge). Religiosity shall keep, and shall justify the heart; and shall give mirth and joy. It shall be well to him that dreadeth God; and he shall be blessed in the days of his comfort &#91;or (the) ending of him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The fullness of wisdom is for to dread God; and fullness is of the fruits thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> It shall fill each gift &#91;or each house&#93; of him of (or for) generations, and (the) receipts of (or the recep-tacles with) the treasures thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The crown of wisdom is the dread of the Lord, and filleth peace &#91;or ful-filling peace&#93;, and the fruit of health. And he saw, and numbered it; for-sooth ever either be the gifts of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Wisdom shall part the cunning (or the knowing) and understanding of prudence; and it enhanceth the glory of them, that hold it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The root of wisdom is for to dread God; forsooth the branches thereof be long enduring &#91;or long living&#93;. Understanding and religiosity of cunning, (or knowing, or knowl-edge)be in the treasures of wisdom; but wisdom is abomination to sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The dread of the Lord putteth away sin, for he that is without dread of God, may not be &#91;or shall not be able to be&#93; justified;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> for why the wrathfulness of his pride &#91;or willfulness&#93; is the destroying of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> A patient man shall suffer the dis-eases of a proud man till into (a) time; and afterward there shall be yielding of mirth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Good wit shall hide the words of him till into a time; and the lips of many men shall tell out the wit of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> In the treasures of wisdom is signifying of cunning (or of knowing); but the worshipping of God is (an) abomination to a sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> A! (or O) son, coveting wisdom, keep thou rightfulness &#91;or rightwise-ness&#93;, and God shall give it to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> For why the dread of the Lord is wisdom, and cunning (or knowing), &#91;or discipline&#93;, and that that is well pleasant (or well-pleasing) to him is faith and mildness (or meekness); and God&#91;or it&#93; shall &#91;full&#93;-fill the treasures of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Be thou not rebel(lious), and unbelieveful to the dread of the Lord; and nigh (or approach) thou not to him in &#91;or with&#93;(a) double heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Be thou not an hypocrite in the sight of men; and be thou not slandered in (or caused to stumble by) thy lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Take thou keep (or care) to those &#91;things&#93;, lest thou fall, and bring dishonor to thy soul; and lest God show thy privates (or thy secrets), &#91;or hid things&#93;, and hurtle thee down in the midst of the synagogue, that is, of (the) gathering together of faithful men; for thou nighedest wickedly &#91;or maliciously&#93; to the Lord, and thine heart was full of guile and of falseness.</span>
