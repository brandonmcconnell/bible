<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that liveth without beginning and end, made (out) of nought all things together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> God alone shall be justified,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and he dwelleth a King unover-come &#91;or unvanquished&#93; without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Who shall suffice to tell out his works? for why who shall seek (out) the great &#91;worthy&#93; things of him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> But who shall tell out the virtue (or the strength) of his greatness? either &#91;or&#93; who shall lay to for to tell out his mercy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> It is not to make less, neither to lay to; neither it is to find the great things of God. &#91;There is not to lessen, nor to add (to); nor there is to find the great worthy things of God.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> When a man hath ended, then he shall begin &#91;or beginneth&#93;; and when he hath rested, (then) he shall work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> What is a man, and what is the glory of him? and what is good, either &#91;or&#93; what is the wicked thing of him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The number of the days of men, that be commonly &#91;or as much (as)&#93; an hundred years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> be areckoned as the drops of the water of the sea; and as the stone (or a stone) of gravel, so a few years in the day of everlastingness &#91;or the spiritual world&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For this thing God is patient in (or with) them, and sheddeth &#91;or he shall pour&#93; out on them his mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> He saw the presumption, &#91;or (the) pride&#93;, of their heart, for it was evil; and he knew the destroying of them, for it was wicked, &#91;or shrewd, (or depraved)&#93;. Therefore he filled his mercy in them, and showed to them the way of equity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The merciful doing of man is about his neighbour; but the mercy of the Lord is over each &#91;or upon all&#93; flesh. He that hath mercy, and teach-eth, and chastiseth as a shepherd his flock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> do he mercy, taking the teaching of merciful doing; and he that hast(en)-eth in the dooms thereof &#91;or of him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Son, in goods &#91;or good things&#93; give thou not (com)plaint, and in (or with) each gift give thou not heaviness of an evil word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Whether dew shall not cool heat? so and a word is better than (a) gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Lo! whether a word is not above &#91;or over&#93; a good gift? but ever either is with a man justified &#91;or a justified man&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> A fool shall upbraid sharply &#91;or shall give reproof&#93;; and the gift of an untaught man maketh (the) eyes to fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Before the doom make thou ready rightfulness to thee; and learn thou, before that thou speak. Before sickness give thou &#91;or take&#93; medicine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> and before the doom ask thyself, and thou shalt find mercy in the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Before sickness make thee meek, and in the time of sickness show thy living. &#91;Before sickness meek thee, and in the time of infirmity show thou thy conversation or thy living.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Be thou not hindered to pray ever&#91;more&#93;, and dread thou not to be justified till to &#91;or unto the&#93; death; for why the meed of God dwelleth without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Before prayer make ready &#91;or prepare&#93; thy soul; and do not thou be as a man that tempteth God, that is, that a man betake himself to peril, and believe that that he may do reasonably, and (then) abide to be delivered of (or by) God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Have thou mind of (or remember the) ire &#91;or wrath&#93; in the day of ending; and make thou in living &#91;or conversation&#93; the time of yielding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Have thou mind of (or remember) poverty in the day of abundance &#91;or the time of plenty&#93;; and the need of poverty in the time &#91;or day&#93; of riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> From the morrowtide unto the eventide the time shall be changed; and all these things be swift &#91;or hasted&#93; in the eyes of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> A wise man shall dread in all things; and in the days of trespasses he shall flee from uncunning (or unknowing), either sloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Each fell, &#91;or witty, (or witting, or knowing)&#93; man, that is, attentive to eschew evils, by God’s dread, know-eth wisdom; and to him that findeth it, he shall give acknowledging (to it).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Witty, (or Witting, or knowing) men in words also they did wisely, and understood truth, and rightful-ness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;; and besought proverbs and dooms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Go thou not after thy covetous-nesses; and be thou turned away from thy will. &#91;After thy lusts go thou not; and from thy will turn thee away.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> If thou givest to thy soul the covetousnesses thereof &#91;or (its) lusts&#93;, it shall make thee into joy to thine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Delight thou not in companies (or crowds), neither in little companies&#91;or in small things&#93;; for why the sinning &#91;or the trespassing&#93; of them is continual.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Be thou not mean in the striving of love &#91;or for money&#93;, and something is &#91;not&#93; to thee in the bag; for why thou shalt be envious to thy (own) soul.</span>
