<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOSHUA 22:8–9"> and he dwelled against the east coast, till to the entering of (the) desert, and to the flood Euphrates. <span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 21:9; 1ST CHRONICLES 5:18–21; PSALMS 83:6"> Forsooth in the days of Saul the sons of Reuben fought against (the) Hagarites, and killed them; and dwelled for them in the tabernacles of them (or and they lived in their tents, or their camps), in all the coast that beholdeth to the east of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:11; JOSHUA 13:24–28; DEUTERONOMY 3:10–17"> Soothly the sons of Gad even against them dwelled in the land of Bashan till to Salcah; (And the sons of Gad lived beside them in the land of Bashan unto Salcah;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Joel was in the beginning, and Shapham was the second; also Jaanai and Shaphat were in Bashan. (Joel was the leader, and Shapham was the second; and Jaanai and Shaphat were in Bashan.)</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Also the brethren of the son of Abdiel, son of Guni, was prince of the house in his families. (And Ahi, the son of Abdiel, the son of Guni, was the leader of the house of their family.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 27:29; SONG OF SONGS 2:1; ISAIAH 35:2"> And they dwelled in Gilead (or And they lived in Gilead), and in Bashan, and in the towns thereof, (and) in all the suburbs of Sharon, till to the ends.</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 25:15; 1ST CHRONICLES 1:31; 1ST CHRONICLES 5:10"> and fought against Hagarites. Forsooth Jetur, and Nephish, and Nodab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 22:4–5; PSALMS 9:10; 2ND CHRONICLES 18:31"> gave help to them; and Hagarites, and all men that were with them, were betaken into the hands of Reuben, and Gad, and Manasseh; for they called inwardly the Lord, while they fought, and the Lord heard them, for they believed in to him. (gave them help; and the Hagarites, and all the men who were with them, were delivered into the hands of Reuben, and Gad, and eastern Manasseh; for they inwardly called on the Lord, while they fought, and the Lord heard them, for they believed in him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:13; NUMBERS 31:35"> And they took (away) all things which (the) Hagarites had in possession, fifty thousand of camels, and two hundred and fifty thousand of sheep, two thousand of asses (or two thousand donkeys), and an hundred thousand persons of men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 32:8; ROMANS 8:31; JOSHUA 23:10"> for many men were wounded and felled down; for it was the battle of the Lord. And they dwelled (there) for (the) Hagarites till to the conquest. (for many men were killed and fell down; for the battle was the Lord’s making. And they lived there in place of the Hagarites until the captivity.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 3:8–9; SONG OF SONGS 4:8; PSALMS 133:3"> Also the sons of the half lineage of Manasseh had in possession the land, from the ends of Bashan till to Baalhermon, and Senir, and the hill of Hermon; for it was a great number. (And the sons of the eastern half of the tribe of Manasseh, had in possession the land from Bashan to Baalhermon, and Senir, and Mount Hermon; for they were great in number.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 4:38"> And these were the princes of the house of their kindred; Epher, and Ishi, and Eliel, and Azriel, and Jeremy, and Hodaviah, and Jahdiel, full strong men and mighty, and named dukes in their families. (And these were the leaders of the house of their kindred; Epher, and Ishi, and Eliel, and Azriel, and Jeremiah, and Hodaviah, and Jahdiel, very strong and mighty men, with famous names, yea, the leaders of their families.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 17:5; PSALMS 106:34–39; EXODUS 34:15"> Forsooth they forsook the God of their fathers, and did fornication after the gods of &#91;the&#93; peoples of the land, which the Lord took away before them. (But they left the God of their fathers, and did idolatry, worshipping the gods of the peoples of the land, whom the Lord had destroyed before them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2ND KI 15:19; 2ND KI 17:6; 2ND KI 15:29"> And the Lord God of Israel raised (up) the spirit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Yea, ‘the spirit’, that is, (the) will to fight against the children of Israel.">a</a></sup>of Pul, king of Assyrians, and the spirit of Tilgathpilneser, king of Assur; and he translated Reuben, and Gad, and the half lineage of Manasseh, and brought them into Halah, and Habor, and Hara, and into the river of Gozan, till to this day. (And the Lord God of Israel stirred up the will, or the desire, of Pul, the king of Assyria, who is also known as Tilgathpilneser, the king of Assyria, to fight against Israel; and he carried away Reuben, and Gad, and the eastern half of the tribe of Manasseh, and brought them to Halah, and Habor, and Hara, and the Gozan River, where they live unto this day.)</span>
