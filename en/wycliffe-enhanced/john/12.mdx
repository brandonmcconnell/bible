<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:4; 1ST SAMUEL 17:28–29; JOHN 13:2"> Therefore Judas Iscariot, one of his disciples, that was to betray him, said,</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 13:29; PSALMS 50:16–20; JAMES 2:6"> But he said this thing, not for it pertained to him of needy men, but for he was a thief, and had the purses, and bare those things that were sent (into them), (or and carried off whatever was put into them).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:12; MARK 14:6; MATTHEW 27:57–60"> Therefore Jesus said, Suffer ye her, that into the day of my burying she keep that (which is left); (And so Jesus said, Allow her, or Let her, do this, so that on the day of my burial she can use what is left;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 15:11; MARK 14:7; MATTHEW 26:11"> for ye shall (for)evermore have poor men with you, but ye shall not (for)evermore have me. (for ye shall always have the poor with you, but ye shall not always have me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:14; JOHN 11:43–45; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:10–11"> Therefore much people of the Jews knew, that Jesus was there; and they came, not only for Jesus, but to see Lazarus, whom he had raised from death &#91;or whom he raised from (the) dead&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKE 16:31; MATTHEW 2:16; JOHN 11:47–53"> But the princes of (the) priests thought, or sought, to slay Lazarus, (And so the high priests also made plans to kill Lazarus,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOHN 11:45; JOHN 12:18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:45"> for many of the Jews went away for him, and believed in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MARK 11:7–10; MATTHEW 21:4–9; LUKE 19:35–38"> But on the morrow much people, that came together to the feast day, when they had heard, that Jesus came to Jerusalem, (But the next morning, many people who had come for the Feast Day, or the Festival, when they had heard, that Jesus had come to Jerusalem,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 118:25–26; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 15:3; MATTHEW 21:9–11"> took branches of palms, and came forth to meet him, and cried, Hosanna, blessed is the king of Israel, that cometh in the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 9:9; MARK 11:1–7; MATTHEW 21:1–7"> And Jesus found a young ass, and sat on him, as it is written,</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 2:22; JOHN 14:26; JOHN 7:39"> His disciples knew not (at) first these things, but when Jesus was glorified, then they had mind, that these things were written of him, and these things they did to him. (And so the people, who were with him, when he called Lazarus out of the tomb, and raised him from the dead, testified about that.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOHN 12:11"> And therefore the people came, and met with him, for they heard that he had done this sign (or this miracle).</span>

(And there were some Gentiles, among those who had come to worship on the Feast Day or at the Festival.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 2:2; LUKE 19:2–4; JOHN 6:40"> And these came to Philip, that was of Bethsaida of Galilee, and prayed him, and said, Sire, we will see Jesus, &#91;or Sire, we would (like to) see Jesus&#93;. (And they came to Philip, who was from Bethsaida in Galilee, and beseeched him, and said, Sir, we want to see Jesus.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MARK 10:13–14; LUKE 9:49–50; JOHN 1:40–41"> Philip cometh, and saith to Andrew; and again Andrew and Philip said to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOHN 13:31–32; JOHN 17:1–5; ISAIAH 60:9"> And Jesus answered to them, and said, The hour cometh, that man’s Son be clarified, (or The time hath come, for man’s Son to be glorified).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:36–38; ISAIAH 53:10–12; HEBREWS 2:9–10"> Truly, truly, I say to you, &#91;no&#93; but a corn of wheat fall into the earth, and be dead, (or Truly, I tell you the truth, unless a kernel, or a grain, of wheat fall into the earth, and it die), it dwelleth alone; but if it be dead, it bringeth &#91;forth&#93; much fruit.</span>

If any man serve me, my Father shall worship him (or my Father shall honour him).</span>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOHN 13:31–32; MATTHEW 26:42; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 5:9–14"> Father, clarify thy name. And a voice came from heaven, and said, I have glorified it, and I shall glorify it again.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOB 37:2–5; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:1; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:2"> Therefore the people that stood (there), and heard, said, that thunder was made; other men said, an angel spake to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOHN 11:42; JOHN 11:15; JOHN 5:34"> Jesus answered, and said, This voice came not for me, but for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 4:4; 1ST JOHN 5:19; 1ST JOHN 3:8"> Now is the doom of the world, now the prince of this world shall be cast out. (Now is the Judgement of this world, now the Prince of this world shall be thrown out.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JOHN 3:14; JOHN 8:28; 1ST PETER 2:24"> And if I shall be enhanced from the earth, I shall draw all things to myself. (And if I shall be raised or lifted up from the earth, then I shall draw everyone to me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOHN 18:32; JOHN 21:19"> And he said this thing, signifying by what death he was to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:24–25; ISAIAH 9:7; DANIEL 7:14"> And the people answered to him, We have heard of the law, that Christ dwelleth &#91;into&#93; without end; and how sayest thou, It behooveth man’s Son to be areared? Who is this man’s Son? (And the people said to him, We have heard in the Law, that the Messiah remaineth forever; so how sayest thou, It behooveth the Son of man to be raised up? Who is this Son of man?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JOHN 12:46; JOHN 16:16; EPHESIANS 5:8"> And then Jesus saith to them, Yet a little light is in you; walk ye, the while ye have light, that (the) darkness(es) catch you not; he that wandereth in (the) darknesses, knoweth not whither he goeth. (And then Jesus said to them, Yet a little light is in you, or There is but a little light left to you; walk ye, the while ye have the light, so that the darkness catch you not; he who walketh, or who goeth, in the darkness, knoweth not where he goeth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:8; JOHN 8:59; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:47–48"> While ye have (the) light, believe ye in the light, (so) that ye be the children of (the) light. Jesus spake these things, and went, and hid him(self) from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKE 16:31; JOHN 1:11; JOHN 15:24"> And when he had done so many miracles &#91;or so many signs&#93; before them, they believed not in him; (And though he had done so many miracles before them, they still did not believe in him;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:1; ROMANS 10:16; ROMANS 10:20"> that the word of Esaias, the prophet, should be fulfilled, which he said, Lord, who hath believed to our hearing, and to whom is the arm of the Lord showed? (so that the word of the prophet Isaiah would be fulfilled, when he said, Lord, who hath believed our message, and to whom is the Lord’s power shown or revealed?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="JOHN 10:38; JOHN 5:44; 2ND PETER 2:14"> Therefore they might not believe, for again Esaias said, (And so they could not believe, for again Isaiah said,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="ISAIAH 6:10; MATTHEW 13:13–15; LUKE 8:10"> He hath blinded their eyes, and hath made hard the heart(s) of them, (so) that they see not with (their) eyes, and understand &#91;not&#93; with (their) heart(s); and that they be converted, and I heal them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="ISAIAH 6:1–5; ISAIAH 6:9–10; JOHN 1:14"> Esaias said these things, when he saw the glory of him, and spake of him. (Isaiah said these things, when he saw his glory, and spoke about him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="JOHN 9:22; JOHN 7:13; ISAIAH 51:7"> Nevertheless of the princes, many believed in him, but for the Pharisees they acknowledged not, that they should not be put out of the synagogue; (Nevertheless, among the leaders, many believed in him, but because of the Pharisees they did not acknowledge him publicly, so that they would not be put out of the synagogue;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="JOHN 5:44; MATTHEW 23:5–7; JOHN 5:41"> for they loved the glory of men, more than the glory of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:40; JOHN 13:20; JOHN 5:24"> And Jesus cried, and said, He that believeth in me, believeth not in me, but in him that sent me (or but in him who sent me).</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="JOHN 14:9–10; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:6; COLOSSIANS 1:15"> He that seeth me, seeth him that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="JOHN 8:12; PSALMS 36:9; JOHN 9:5"> I (as a) light came into the world, that each that believeth in me, dwell not in (the) darknesses. (I came as a light into the world, so that everyone who believeth in me, remain not in darkness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="JOHN 3:17; JOHN 8:15–16; JOHN 12:48"> And if any man heareth my words, and keepeth them not, I deem him not; for I came not, that I deem the world, but that I make the world safe. (And if anyone heareth my words, and obeyeth them not, I do not judge that person; for I came not, that I judge the world, but that I save the world.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="LUKE 10:16; HEBREWS 9:27–28; ROMANS 2:16"> He that despiseth me, and taketh not my words, hath him that shall judge him; that word that I have spoken &#91;or the word that I have spoken&#93;, shall deem him in the last day. (He who despiseth me, and receiveth not my words, hath that which shall judge him; yea, the words that I have spoken, will judge him on the Last Day.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="JOHN 14:10; JOHN 8:26; JOHN 5:30"> For I have not spoken of myself, but that Father that sent me, &#91;he&#93; gave to me a commandment, what I shall say, and what I shall speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="JOHN 17:3; JOHN 6:68; 1ST JOHN 5:20"> And I know, that his commandment is everlasting life (or that his command bringeth eternal life); therefore those things that I speak, as the Father said to me, so I speak.</span>
