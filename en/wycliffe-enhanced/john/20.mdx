<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 24:12"> Therefore Peter went out, and that other disciple, and they came to the grave. (And so Peter went out, and that other disciple, and they came to the tomb.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 18:23; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:12; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:24"> And they twain ran together, and that other disciple ran before Peter, and came first to the grave. <span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOHN 19:40; JOHN 20:11; JOHN 11:44"> And when he stooped (down), he saw the sheets lying (there), nevertheless he entered not (or but he did not go in).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 18:17; JOHN 21:7; MATTHEW 16:15–16"> Therefore Simon Peter came (pur)suing him, and he entered into the grave, and he saw the sheets laid (there), (And so Simon Peter came following behind him, and he entered into the tomb, and he saw the sheets laid there,)</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 20:25; JOHN 20:4; JOHN 1:50"> Therefore then that disciple that came first to the grave, entered, and saw, and believed. (And then that disciple who first came to the tomb, entered, and saw everything, and believed.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 16:10; ISAIAH 25:8; ISAIAH 53:10–12"> For they knew not yet the scripture, that it behooved him to rise again from death. &#91;Forsooth they wist not yet the scripture, for it behooved him to rise again from (the) dead.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 7:53; JOHN 16:32"> Therefore the disciples went again to themselves. (And so the disciples went off by themselves.)</span>

(But Mary stood at the tomb outside weeping. And while she wept, she bowed herself down, and looked into the tomb.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 24:3–7; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:4; MARK 16:5–6"> And she saw two angels sitting in white, one at the head and one at the feet, where the body of Jesus was laid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 20:15; JOHN 2:4; 1ST SAMUEL 1:8"> And they say to her, Woman, what weepest thou? (or And they said to her, Woman, why weepest thou?) She said to them, For they have taken away my Lord, and I know not, where they have laid him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKE 24:16; JOHN 21:4; MATTHEW 28:9"> When she had said these things, she turned backward &#91;or she turned aback&#93;, and saw Jesus standing (there), and knew not that it was Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOHN 20:13; JOHN 18:7; JOHN 1:38"> Jesus saith to her, Woman, what weepest thou? whom seekest thou? (Jesus said to her, Woman, why weepest thou? whom seekest thou? She thinking that he was the gardener, said to him, Sir, if thou hast taken him some place, tell me, where thou hast laid him, and I shall take him away.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 10:3; JOHN 1:38; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:3"> Jesus saith to her, Mary. She turned, and saith to him, Rabboni, that is to say, Master (or Teacher).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 16:28; EPHESIANS 4:8–10; GALATIANS 4:6–7"> Jesus saith to her, Do not thou touch me, for I have not yet ascended to my Father; but go to my brethren, and say to them, I ascend to my Father and to your Father, to my God and to your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKE 24:10; MATTHEW 28:10; JOHN 20:1"> Mary Magdalene came, telling to the disciples, That I saw the Lord, and these things he said to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOHN 20:26; JOHN 14:27; JOHN 20:21"> Therefore when it was even(ing) in that day, &#91;in&#93; one of the sabbaths, and the gates were shut, where the disciples were gathered, for dread of the Jews, Jesus came, and stood in the middle of the disciples, and he saith to them, Peace &#91;be&#93; to you. (And so when it was evening on that day, on one of the Sabbaths (or on the first day of the week), and the doors were shut, where the disciples were gathered, for fear of the Jews, Jesus came, and stood in the midst of the disciples, and he said to them, Peace be to you.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOHN 16:22; JOHN 16:20; JOHN 20:27"> And when he had said this, he showed to them &#91;his&#93; hands and side; therefore the disciples joyed (or and so the disciples rejoiced), for the Lord was seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOHN 17:18–19; JOHN 13:20; JOHN 3:17"> And he saith to them again, Peace (be) to you; as the Father sent me, I send you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 33:4; GENESIS 2:7; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:15"> When he had said this, he blew on them, and said, Take ye the Holy Ghost (or and said, Receive the Holy Spirit);</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:18; MATTHEW 16:19; MARK 2:5–10"> whose sins ye forgive, those be forgiven to them; and whose sins ye withhold, those be withheld.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOHN 11:16; JOHN 6:66–67; HEBREWS 10:25"> But Thomas, one of the twelve, that is said Didymus (or who is called the Twin), was not with them, when Jesus came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOHN 6:30; MARK 16:11; HEBREWS 3:12"> Therefore the other disciples said, We have seen the Lord. And he said to them, &#91;No&#93; But I see in his hands the printing of the nails, (or And he said to them, Unless I see the scarring, or the marks, from the nails in his hands), and put my finger into the place of the nails, and put mine hand into his side, I shall not believe (it).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOHN 20:19; ISAIAH 54:10; JOHN 20:21"> And after eight days again his disciples were within, and Thomas with them. Jesus came, while the gates were shut, and stood in the middle, and said, Peace (be) to you, (or And Jesus came, while the doors were shut, and stood in their midst, and said, Peace be to you).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOHN 20:25; 1ST JOHN 1:1–2; JOHN 20:20"> Afterward he saith to Thomas, Put in here thy finger, and see mine hands, and put hither thine hand, and put &#91;it&#93; into my side, and do not thou be unbelieveful, but faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOHN 1:1; ISAIAH 9:6; ISAIAH 7:14"> Thomas answered, and said to him, My Lord and my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1ST PETER 1:8; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:7; HEBREWS 11:1"> Jesus saith to him, Thomas, for thou hast seen me, thou believedest; blessed be they, that saw not, and have believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOHN 21:25; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:15–17; ROMANS 15:4"> And Jesus did many other signs (or miracles) in the sight of his disciples, which be not written in this book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:43; JOHN 3:15–16; 1ST JOHN 5:1"> But these be written, that ye believe, that Jesus is Christ, the Son of God, and that ye believing have life in his name.
