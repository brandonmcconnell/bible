<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 1:1; JOHN 17:5; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:13"> In the beginning was the word, &#91;that is, God’s son, (or God’s Son)&#93;, and the word was at God, and God was the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> This (Word) was in the beginning at (or with) God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:16–17; 1ST CORINTHIANS 8:6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 4:11"> All things were made by him, and without him was made nothing &#91;or nought&#93;, (of) that thing that was made. (Everything was made by him, and without him nothing was made, out of all that was made.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 8:12; JOHN 12:46; JOHN 5:26"> In him was life, and the life was the light of men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOHN 3:19–20; JOHN 12:36–40; 1ST CORINTHIANS 2:14"> and the light shineth in darknesses, and &#91;the&#93; darknesses comprehended not it. (and the light shineth in the darkness, but the darkness did not understand it.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MALACHI 3:1; JOHN 3:28; ISAIAH 40:3–5"> A man was sent from God, to whom the name was John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:4; JOHN 1:36; JOHN 5:33–35"> This man came into witnessing, that he should bear witnessing of the light, that all men should believe by him. (This man came as a witness, that he would testify about the light, so that all men could believe through him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 1:20; JOHN 3:28; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:4"> He was not that light, but that he should bear witnessing of the light. (He was not that light, but he testified about the light.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 12:46; JOHN 1:4; 1ST JOHN 2:8"> There was a very light (or There was a true light), which (en)lighteneth each man that cometh into this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 17:25; 1ST JOHN 3:1; 1ST CORINTHIANS 2:8"> He was in the world, and the world was made by him, and the world knew him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 19:14; ISAIAH 53:2–3; JOHN 3:32"> He came into his own things, and his (own) received him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:26; ROMANS 8:14; 1ST JOHN 3:1"> But how many ever received him, he gave to them power to be made the sons of God, to them that believe in his name (or to those who believe in him);</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST JOHN 3:9; JAMES 1:18; 1ST PETER 1:23"> the which not of bloods, neither of the will of (the) flesh, neither of the will of man, but be born of God. (who be born not of blood, nor by the desire of the flesh, nor by the desire of man, but be born of God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST JOHN 4:9; 1ST TIMOTHY 3:16; ISAIAH 40:5"> And the word was made man, and dwelled among us, and we have seen the glory of him, as the glory of the one begotten Son of the Father, full of grace and of truth. &#91;And the word, that is, God’s son, (or God’s Son), is made flesh, or man, and hath dwelled in us, and we have seen the glory of him, the glory as of the one begotten of the Father, the son (or the Son) full of grace and truth.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:11; COLOSSIANS 1:17; LUKE 3:16"> John beareth witnessing of him, and crieth, and saith, (or John testified about him, and cried out, and said), This (man) is he of whom I said, He that shall come after me, is made before me, for he was before me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:19; ROMANS 5:2; EPHESIANS 2:5–10"> and of the plenty of him we all have taken, and grace for grace. (and all of us have received so much from him, blessing upon blessing.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ROMANS 6:14; JOHN 14:6; ROMANS 3:19–26"> For the law was given by Moses; but grace and truth is made by Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:15; JOHN 6:46; MATTHEW 11:27"> No man saw ever God &#91;or No man ever saw God&#93;, &#91;no&#93; but the one begotten Son, that is in the bosom of the Father, he hath told out (about him).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKE 3:15–18; JOHN 10:24; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:4"> And this is the witnessing of John, when (the) Jews sent from Jerusalem priests and deacons to him, that they should ask him, Who art thou? (And this is the testimony of John, when the Jews sent priests and Levites from Jerusalem to him, so that they could ask him, Who art thou?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MARK 1:7–8; MATTHEW 3:11–12; JOHN 3:28–36"> He acknowledged, and denied not, and he acknowledged, For I am not Christ. (He confessed, and did not deny it, yea, he confessed, I am not the Messiah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MALACHI 4:5; DEUTERONOMY 18:15–18; MATTHEW 11:14"> And they asked him, What then? Art thou Elias? (or Elijah?) And he said, I am not. Art thou a prophet? &#91;or Art thou the prophet?&#93;(or Art thou the Prophet?) And he answered, Nay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 24:13"> Therefore they said to him, Who art thou? (So) That we (can) give an answer to these that sent us. What sayest thou of thyself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:3–5; MARK 1:3; MATTHEW 3:3"> He said, I am a voice of a crier in desert, Dress ye the way of the Lord, as Esaias the prophet, said. &#91;He saith, I am a voice of a man crying in desert, Dress ye the way of the Lord, as Esaias, the prophet, said.&#93;(He said, I (am) a voice of a (man) crying in the desert, Align or Make straight the way of the Lord, as the prophet Isaiah said.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKE 11:53; MATTHEW 23:13–15; LUKE 11:39–44"> And they that were sent, were of the Pharisees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:18; DEUTERONOMY 18:15; JOHN 1:20–22"> And they asked him, and said to him, What then baptizest thou, if thou art not Christ, neither Elias, neither a prophet? (And they asked him, Why then do thou baptize, if thou art not the Messiah, nor Elijah, nor the Prophet?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOHN 1:10–11; LUKE 3:16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:5"> John answered to them, and said, I baptize in water, but in the middle of you hath stand &#91;or stood&#93; one, that ye know not; (John answered them, and said, I baptize in water, but in the midst of you hath stood one, whom ye know not;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOHN 1:30; MARK 1:7; MATTHEW 3:11"> he it is, that shall come after me, that was made before me, of whom I am not worthy to loose(n) the thong of his shoe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOHN 10:40; JOHN 3:23; JUDGES 7:24"> These things were done in Bethany beyond, &#91;or over&#93;, (the) Jordan, where John was baptizing. (These things were done in Bethany on the other side of the Jordan River, where John was baptizing.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:7; 1ST JOHN 2:2; 1ST JOHN 3:5"> Another (or The next) day John saw Jesus coming to him, and he said, Lo! the lamb of God; lo! he that doeth away the sins of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOHN 1:27; JOHN 1:15; LUKE 3:16"> This is he, that I said of, After me is come a man &#91;or After me cometh a man&#93;, which was made before me; for he was rather &#91;or former&#93; than I (or for he already was, before that I was).</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MALACHI 3:1; LUKE 1:17; MALACHI 4:2–5"> And I knew him not, but that he be showed in Israel, therefore I came baptizing in water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MARK 1:10; LUKE 3:22; MATTHEW 3:16"> And John bare witnessing, and said, I saw the Spirit coming down as a culver from heaven, and (it) dwelled on him &#91;or and dwelling upon him&#93;. (And John testified, and said, I saw the Spirit coming down like a dove from heaven, and dwelling upon him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:5; MATTHEW 3:11; LUKE 3:16"> And I knew him not; but he that sent me to baptize in water, said to me, On whom thou seest the Spirit coming down, and dwelling on him, this is he, that baptizeth in the Holy Ghost, (or this is he, who baptizeth with the Holy Spirit).</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 2:7; JOHN 1:49; PSALMS 89:26–27"> And I saw, and bare witnessing (or and I testify), that this is the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JOHN 3:25–26; MALACHI 3:16"> Another day John stood, and two of his disciples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JOHN 1:29; 1ST PETER 1:19–20; ISAIAH 65:1–2"> and he beheld Jesus walking, and saith, Lo! the lamb of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="ROMANS 10:17; JOHN 1:43; ZECHARIAH 8:21"> And (the) two disciples heard him speaking, and &#91;they&#93; followed Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JOHN 18:7; JOHN 3:2; JOHN 1:49"> And Jesus turned, and saw them (pur)suing him, and saith to them, What seek ye? And they said to him, Rabbi, that is to say, Master, where dwellest thou? (And Jesus turned, and saw them following him, and said to them, What do ye seek? And they said to him, Teacher, where do you live?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LUKE 24:29; MATTHEW 11:28–30; JOHN 6:37"> And he saith to them, Come ye, and see. And they came, and saw where he dwelled; and dwelt with him that day. And it was as the tenth hour. (And he said to them, Come, and see. And they came, and saw where he lived; and remained with him that day. And it was about four o’clock in the afternoon.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MARK 1:16–20; MATTHEW 4:18–22; JOHN 1:40–42"> And Andrew, the brother of Simon Peter, was one of the twain, that heard of John, and had (pur)sued him. (And Andrew, the brother of Simon Peter, was one of the two, who had heard that from John, and had followed him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="JOHN 4:25; JOHN 1:45; PSALMS 89:20"> This found first his brother Simon, and he said to him, We have found Messias, that is to say, Christ; (And at once he found his brother Simon, and he said to him, We have found the Messiah, that is to say, the Christ;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="JOHN 21:15–17; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:22; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:5"> and he led him to Jesus. And Jesus beheld him, and said, Thou art Simon, the son of Johanna (or the son of John); thou shalt be called Cephas, that is to say, Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:9; JOHN 14:8; ISAIAH 65:1"> And on the morrow he would go out into Galilee, and he found Philip; and he saith to him, (Pur)Sue thou me, (or and Jesus said to him, Follow me).</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="JOHN 12:21; MATTHEW 11:21; MARK 8:22"> Philip was of Bethsaida, the city of Andrew and of Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:18–22; LUKE 24:27; MICAH 5:2"> Philip found Nathanael, and said to him, We have found Jesus, the son of Joseph, of Nazareth, (about) whom Moses wrote in the law and the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="JOHN 7:52; JOHN 7:41–42; JOHN 4:29"> And Nathanael said to him, Of Nazareth may some good thing be? (or Can anything good come from Nazareth?) Philip said to him, Come, and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:5; PSALMS 32:2; PSALMS 73:1"> Jesus saw Nathanael coming to him, and said of him, Lo! verily a man of Israel, in whom is no guile. (Jesus saw Nathanael coming to him, and said of him, Behold! truly a man of Israel, in whom there is no deceit.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="ISAIAH 65:24; JOHN 2:25; PSALMS 139:1–2"> Nathanael said to him, Whereof hast thou known me? Jesus answered, and said to him, Before that Philip called thee, when thou were under the fig tree, I saw thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 3:15; ZECHARIAH 9:9; MATTHEW 27:42"> Nathanael answered to him, and said, Rabbi (or Teacher), thou art the Son of God, thou art (the) king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="JOHN 11:40; JOHN 20:29; LUKE 1:45"> Jesus answered, and said to him, For I said to thee, I saw thee under the fig tree, thou believest; thou shalt see more than these things &#91;or thou shalt see more things than these&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="GENESIS 28:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:56; LUKE 22:43"> And he said to them, Truly, truly, I say to you, (or Truly, I tell you the truth), ye shall see heaven opened, and the angels of God ascending up and coming down on man’s Son.</span>
