<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:16; EXODUS 23:14–17; EXODUS 34:23"> After these things there was a feast day of the Jews, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 12:39; NEHEMIAH 3:1; NEHEMIAH 3:32"> And in Jerusalem is a washing place (or a pool), that in Hebrew is called Bethesda, and (it) hath five porches. &#91;Forsooth at Jerusalem is a standing water of beasts (or a watering pool for beasts), that in Hebrew is called Bethesda, having five little gates, or entries.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MARK 3:1–4; LUKE 7:22; MATTHEW 15:30"> In these lay a great multitude of sick men, blind, crooked (or lame), and dry (or withered), abiding (or waiting for) the moving &#91;or the stirring&#93; of the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For the angel of the Lord came down (at) certain times into the &#91;standing&#93; water (or into the pool), and the water was moved; and he that first came down into the cistern, after the moving of the water, was made whole (or was healed) of whatever sickness he was held (by).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:2; LUKE 8:43; MARK 9:21"> And a man was there, having eight and thirty years in his sickness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 18:41; HEBREWS 4:15; HEBREWS 4:13"> And when Jesus had seen him lying (there), and had known, that he had much time (there), he saith to him, Wilt thou be made whole?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 72:12; DEUTERONOMY 32:36; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:24"> The sick man answered to him, Lord, I have no man, (so) that when the water is moved &#91;or (it) is troubled&#93;, to put me into the cistern (or into the pool); for while I come, another goeth down before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKE 5:24; MATTHEW 9:6; MARK 2:11"> Jesus saith to him, Rise up, take thy bed, and go. (Jesus said to him, Get up, pick up thy bed, and go.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:7–8; JOHN 9:14; MARK 3:2–4"> And anon the man was made whole, and took up his bed, and went forth. And it was sabbath in that day. (And at once the man was healed, and picked up his bed, and went forth. And that day was the Sabbath.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:21; LUKE 6:2; JOHN 9:16"> Therefore the Jews said to him that was made whole, It is (the) sabbath, it is not leaveful to thee, to take away thy bed. (And so the Jews said to him who was healed, It is the Sabbath, and it is not lawful for thee to carry thy bed.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MARK 2:9–11; JOHN 9:16"> He answered to them, He that made me whole, said to me, Take (up) thy bed, and go. (He answered to them, He who healed me, said to me, Pick up thy bed, and go.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:23; JUDGES 6:29; ROMANS 10:2"> Therefore they asked him, What man is that &#91;or Who is that man&#93;, that said to thee, Take up thy bed, and go? (or Who said to thee, Pick up thy bed, and go?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 4:30; LUKE 24:31; JOHN 8:59"> But he that was made whole, wist not who it was, (or But he who was healed, did not know who it was). And Jesus (had) bowed away from the people, that was set in the place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 8:11; LEVITICUS 26:23–24; PSALMS 118:18"> Afterward Jesus found him in the temple, and said to him, Lo! thou art made whole, (or Behold! thou art healed); now do not thou do sin &#91;or now do not thou sin&#93;, lest any worse thing befall to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOHN 9:11–12; JOHN 4:29; JOHN 9:25"> (Then) That man went, and told to the Jews, that it was Jesus that made him whole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 10:39; JOHN 7:19–20; MARK 3:6"> Therefore the Jews pursued Jesus, for he did this thing in the sabbath. (And so the Jews persecuted Jesus, because he did this miracle on the Sabbath.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 9:4; JOHN 14:10; COLOSSIANS 1:16"> And Jesus answered to them, My Father worketh till now, and I work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOHN 10:30; JOHN 10:33; JOHN 19:7"> Therefore the Jews sought more to slay him, for not only he brake the sabbath, but he said that God was his Father, and made him(self) even (or equal) to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOHN 12:49; JOHN 8:28; JOHN 14:10"> Therefore Jesus answered, and said to them, Truly, truly, I say to you, (or Truly, I tell you the truth), the Son may not of himself do anything, &#91;no&#93; but that thing that he seeth the Father doing; for whatever things he doeth, the Son doeth in like manner those things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOHN 3:35; MATTHEW 3:17; JOHN 17:26"> For the Father loveth the Son, and showeth to him all (the) things that he doeth; and he shall show to him greater works than these, (so) that ye wonder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ROMANS 8:11; DEUTERONOMY 32:39; JOHN 11:25"> For as the Father raiseth dead men, and quickeneth (them), so the Son quickeneth whom he will. (For as the Father raiseth the dead, and giveth them life, so the Son giveth life to whomever he desireth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOHN 5:27; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:31; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:10"> For neither the Father judgeth any man, but hath given every doom to the Son, (For the Father judgeth no one, but hath given every judgement to the Son,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:23; LUKE 10:16; MATTHEW 11:27"> (so) that all men honour the Son, as they honour the Father. He that honoureth not the Son, honoureth not the Father that sent him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOHN 3:36; JOHN 6:40; 1ST JOHN 5:11–13"> Truly, truly, I say to you, (or Truly, I tell you the truth), that he that heareth my word, and believeth in him that sent me, hath everlasting life, and he cometh not into doom, (or hath eternal life, and he cometh not unto judgement), but passeth from death into life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOHN 5:28; EPHESIANS 5:14; EPHESIANS 2:5"> Truly, truly, I say to you, (or Truly, I tell you the truth), for the hour cometh, and now it is, when dead men (or when the dead) shall hear the voice of the Son of God, and they that hear (it), shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOHN 11:26; JOHN 1:4; EXODUS 3:14"> For as the Father hath life in himself, so he gave to the Son, to have life in himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:42; JOHN 5:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:31"> and he gave to him power to make doom, (or and he gave him the authority to pass judgement), for he is man’s Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:12; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:14–17; ISAIAH 26:19"> Do not ye wonder in this, for the hour cometh, in which all men that be in burials, shall hear the voice of God’s Son. (Do not wonder at this, for the hour cometh, in which all those who be in graves, shall hear the voice of God’s Son.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DANIEL 12:2–3; ROMANS 2:6–10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:15"> And they that have done good things, shall go into again-rising of life &#91;or shall come forth into rising again of life&#93;; but they that have done evil things, into again-rising of doom. (And they who have done good, shall be resurrected for life; but they who have done evil, shall be resurrected for judgement or for condemnation.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOHN 6:38; JOHN 5:19; JOHN 4:34"> I may nothing do of myself, but as I hear, I deem, &#91;or I may not of myself do anything, but as I hear, I judge&#93;, and my doom is just (or and my judgement is just and fair), for I seek not my will, but the will of the Father that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:2; JOHN 8:54; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:14"> If I bear witnessing of myself, my witnessing is not true; (If I testify about myself, my testimony is not accepted as true;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JOHN 1:33; JOHN 12:28–30; JOHN 5:36–37"> another is that beareth witnessing of me, and I know that his witnessing is true, that he beareth of me. (but there is another who testifieth about me, and I know that his testimony is true, what he testifieth about me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOHN 1:29–34; JOHN 3:26–36; JOHN 1:15–27"> Ye sent to John, and he bare witnessing to &#91;the&#93; truth. (Ye sent messengers to John, and he testified about the truth, or and he testified truthfully.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LUKE 19:41–42; JOHN 20:31; 1ST JOHN 5:9"> But I take not witnessing of (any) man; but I say these things, that ye be safe. (But I do not need the testimony of anyone; but I say these things, so that ye can be saved.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2ND PETER 1:19; JOHN 1:7–8; MATTHEW 11:11"> He was a lantern burning and shining, &#91;or giving light&#93;; but ye would (be) glad, or(would have)joy, (or rejoice), at an hour in his light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JOHN 10:25; JOHN 10:37–38; JOHN 15:24"> But I have more witnessing than John, for the works that my Father gave to me to perform them &#91;or forsooth the works that my Father gave me that I perform them&#93;, those works that I do bear witnessing of me, that the Father sent me. (But I have a greater witness than John, or But I have better testimony than John’s, for the works that my Father gave to me to complete, or to finish, those works that I do testify about me, that the Father sent me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JOHN 8:18; JOHN 1:18; MATTHEW 3:17"> And the Father that sent me, he bare witnessing of me, (or And the Father who sent me, he testified about me). (But) Neither ye heard ever his voice, neither ye saw his likeness, &#91;or (his) form&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:14; DEUTERONOMY 6:6–9; JOHN 12:44–48"> And ye have not his word dwelling in you; for ye believe not to him, whom he sent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LUKE 24:27; LUKE 24:44; 2ND PETER 1:19–21"> Seek ye the scriptures, in which ye guess to have everlasting life; and those it be, that bear witnessing of me. (Ye study the Scriptures, through which ye hope to gain eternal life; and they do indeed testify about me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="JOHN 3:19; JOHN 1:11; PSALMS 81:11"> And (yet) ye will not come to me, (so) that ye (can) have (eternal) life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="JOHN 7:18; JOHN 5:34; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:6"> I take not clearness of men; (I do not receive my glory, or my honour, from people;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1ST JOHN 4:20; 1ST JOHN 3:17; JOHN 2:25"> but I have known you, that ye have not the love of God in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="JOHN 10:25; MATTHEW 24:5; JOHN 17:4–6"> I came in the name of my Father, and ye took not me (or but ye did not receive me). If another come in his own name, ye shall receive him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="JOHN 12:43; ROMANS 2:29; PHILIPPIANS 2:3"> How may ye believe, that receive glory each of (the) other, and ye seek not the glory that is of God alone? (How can ye believe, ye who receive your glory from one another, and seek not the glory that is from God alone?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="ROMANS 3:19–20; GALATIANS 3:10; 2ND CORINTHIANS 3:7–11"> Do not ye guess, that I am to accuse you with the Father; it is Moses that accuseth you, in whom ye hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:15; DEUTERONOMY 18:18–19; JOHN 1:45"> For if ye believed to Moses, peradventure ye should believe also to me; for he wrote of me. (For if ye believed Moses, perhaps ye would also believe me; for he wrote about me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="LUKE 16:31; LUKE 16:29"> But if ye believe not to his letters, how shall ye believe to my words? (But if ye do not believe what he wrote, how shall ye believe what I say?)</span>
