<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 1:5; 1ST KINGS 11:1–2; DEUTERONOMY 23:3"> and they took wives of Moab, of which wives one was called Orpah, the tother Ruth. And the sons dwelled there ten years (or And the sons lived there for ten years),</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:39; PSALMS 89:30–32; ISAIAH 49:21"> and both died, that is, Mahlon and Chilion; and the woman (was) left, and was made bare of her two free sons, and her husband. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 132:15; EXODUS 3:16; PSALMS 111:5"> And she rose to go with ever either wife of her sons into her country from the country of Moab; for she had heard, that the Lord had beheld his people, and had given meats to them. (And she rose up to go with the wives of both of her sons back to her country from the country of Moab; for she had heard, that the Lord had looked kindly again upon his people, and had given them food.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND KI 8:3; RUTH 1:14; EXODUS 18:27"> And so she went out from the place of her pilgrimage with ever either wife of her sons; and now when she was set in the way of turning again into the land of Judah, (And so she went out from the place where she lived with the wives of both of her sons; and now when she was set on the way, returning to the land of Judah,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="RUTH 2:20; RUTH 1:5; COLOSSIANS 3:18"> she said to them, Go ye (again) into the house of your mother; the Lord do mercy with you, as ye did with the dead men, and with me;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="RUTH 3:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:37; GENESIS 29:11"> the Lord give to you to find rest in the houses of &#91;the&#93; husbands which ye shall take. And she kissed them. And they began to weep with (a) high voice,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 8:23; PSALMS 16:3; PSALMS 119:63"> and to say, We shall go with thee to thy people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 25:5; GENESIS 38:11"> To whom she answered, My daughters, turn ye again, why come ye with me? I have no more sons in my womb, that ye may have any hope of husbands from me;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 17:17; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:9"> my daughters of Moab, turn ye again, and go, (or my daughters of Moab, return ye, and go home); for now I am made eld, and I am not able to the bond of marriage; yea, though I might conceive in this night, and bear sons,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOB 19:21; JUDGES 2:15; PSALMS 32:4"> though ye will abide till they waxed, and &#91;ful&#93; fill the years of marriage, ye shall sooner be eld women than ye shall be wedded; I beseech (thee), my daughters, mourn ye not, for your anguish oppresseth me the more, and the hand of the Lord is gone out against me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JUDGES 11:24; JOSHUA 24:15; ZEPHANIAH 1:6"> To whom Naomi said, Lo! thy kinswoman turned again to her people, and to her gods; go thou with her.</span>

<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 3:17; 1ST KINGS 2:23; 2ND KI 6:31"> what land shall receive thee dying, I shall die therein also, and there I shall take place of burying; God do to me these things, and add these things, if death alone shall not part me and thee. (whatever land shall receive thee when thou diest, I shall die there also, and that shall be the place where I am buried; yea, God do to me terrible things, and add other things as well, if anything but death separate me and thee.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:14; EPHESIANS 6:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:42"> Therefore Naomi saw, that Ruth had deemed with steadfast soul to go with her, and she would not be against her, neither counsel further turning again to her countrymen, (or and so she would not be against her, nor further counsel her to return to her own people).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:10; ISAIAH 23:7; LAMENTATIONS 2:15"> And (so) they went forth together, and came into Bethlehem; and when they entered into the city, swift fame (a)rose with all men, and women said, This is that Naomi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOB 6:4; HEBREWS 12:11; PSALMS 73:14"> To whom she said, Call ye not me Naomi, that is, fair, but call ye me Mara, that is, bitter; for Almighty God hath filled me greatly with bitterness. (To whom she said, Do not ye call me Naomi, or Delightful, or Pleasant, but call ye me Mara, or Bitter; for Almighty God hath filled me with great bitterness.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="RUTH 2:23; 2ND SAMUEL 21:9; EXODUS 9:31–32"> Therefore Naomi came with Ruth of Moab, the wife of her son, from the land of her pilgrimage, and turned again into Bethlehem, when barley was reaped first. (And so Naomi came with Ruth the Moabite, her son’s wife, from the land where she had lived, and returned to Bethlehem with her, when the barley was first harvested.)</span>
