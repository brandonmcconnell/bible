</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then Tobias went forth, and he found a shining young man, standing, tucked up &#91;or gird(ed)&#93;, and as ready to go;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and he knew not that it was the angel of God &#91;or an angel of God&#93;. And he greeted the young man, and said, Of whence have we thee, good young man?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And he answered, I am of the sons of Israel. And Tobias said to him, Knowest thou the way, that leadeth into the country of Media?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> To whom he answered, I know it, and I have gone often all the ways thereof, and I have dwelled with Gabael, your cousin or your brother, that dwelleth in Rages, a city of Media, which city is set in Ecbatana.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> To whom Tobias said, I beseech thee, abide thou me here, till I tell these things to my father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then Tobias entered, and told all these things to his father; on the which things his father wondered, and he prayed the young man, that he would enter in to him &#91;or that he should come in to him&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then he entered, and greeted, or saluted, Tobit, and said to him, Joy be ever&#91;more&#93; to thee!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And Tobit said, What manner joy shall be to me, that sit in darknesses, and see not the light of heaven?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> To whom the young man said, Be thou of strong wit; it is in the most nigh time, or full nigh (time), that thou be healed of (or by) God &#91;or full nigh it is that of God thou be cured&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Then Tobit said to him, Whether thou mayest lead my son to Gabael into Rages, a city of Media, and when thou comest again, I shall restore &#91;or I shall yield&#93; thy meed to thee?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the angel said to him, I shall lead him thither, and bring again him to thee or and I shall lead again him whole to thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> To whom Tobit answered, I pray thee, show to me, of what house, either &#91;or&#93; of what lineage thou art?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> To whom Raphael, the angel, said, Askest thou the kin &#91;or the kindred&#93; of the hired man, either askest thou who is the hired man himself, that shall go with thy son?</span>
