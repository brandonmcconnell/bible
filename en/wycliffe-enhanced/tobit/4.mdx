<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Therefore when Tobit guessed his prayer to be heard, that he might die, he called Tobias, his son, to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and said to him, My son, hear thou the words of my mouth, and build or ground thou them as a foundament (or as a foundation) in thine heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> When God hath taken my soul, bury thou my body; and thou shalt have (or thou shalt give) honour to thy mother in all the days of her life or and in all the days of thy life thou shalt do honour to thy mother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> for thou owest to be mindful (or thou ought to remember), what perils and how great perils she suffered for thee in her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And when also she hath &#91;full&#93;-filled or hath ended the time of her life, thou shalt bury her beside me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And in all the days of thy life have thou God in mind, and be thou ware &#91;or beware&#93;, lest anytime thou consent to sin, and forsake the behests (or the commandments) of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And of thy own chattel &#91;or thy substance&#93; do thou alms-&#91;deed(s)&#93;, and do not thou turn away thy face from any poor man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="If thou mayest not give chattel, give thou will (or a resolve to help), and (a) sign of compassion.">a</a></sup>; and so it shall be done, that the face of the Lord be not turned away from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> As thou mayest, that is, after thy power, be thou merciful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> If thou hast much, give thou plenteously &#91;or abundantly&#93;; if thou hast a little, also be thou busy to part willfully, (or willingly), or gladly a little.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Certainly then thou treasurest to thee a good meed (or a good reward), in the day of need;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> for why alms-&#91;deed (s)&#93; delivereth from all sin and from death, and shall not suffer (or allow) the soul to go into darknesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Alms-&#91;deed(s)&#93; shall be the great trust before the highest God to all men doing it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Son, take heed to thyself, and flee from all fornication, and besides thy wife, suffer (or allow) thou never to know (that) sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And suffer (or allow) thou never pride to have lordship in thy wit, neither in thy word; for all loss &#91;or all perdition&#93;, either damnation, took beginning in pride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Whoever worketh anything to (or for) thee, yield thou anon his meed (that is, give him his reward at once), and utterly the hire of thine hired man, or thy workman, dwell it not with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> That that thou hatest to be done to thee of (or by) another man, see thou, lest anytime thou do that thing to another man. &#91;That of another thou hatest to be done to thee, look thou, that anytime thou do it not to another.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Eat thy bread with hungry men and needy, and with thy clothes cover thou naked men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Ordain thy bread and thy wine on the sepulchre, or the burying, of a just &#91;or (a) rightwise&#93; man, and do not thou eat and drink thereof with sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Ever&#91;more&#93; seek thou perfectly counsel of (or from) a wise man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> In all time bless thou God, and ask thou of (or from) him, that he (ad)dress, (or he direct), &#91;or make ready&#93; thy ways, and all thy counsels dwell in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Also, my son, I show (or I tell) to thee, that while thou were yet a little child &#91;or a little infant&#93;, I gave ten talents of silver to Gabael, in Rages, a city of Media; and I have his obligation &#91;or the writ of it&#93; with me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> therefore inquire thou busily, how thou shalt come to him, and receive thou of (or from) him the foresaid weight of silver, and restore to him his obligation &#91;or his writ&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> My son, do not thou dread; for-sooth we lead a poor life, but we shall have many goods (or many good things, or rewards), if we dread God, and go away from all sin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, deadly (or mortal) sin, for we may not utterly eschew all (that is) venial in this life.">b</a></sup>, and do well.</span>
