<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Tobit called to him his son (Tobias), and said to him, What may we give to this holy man, that came with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And Tobias answered, and said to his father, Father, what meed (or what reward) shall we give to him, either what thing may be worthy to (or for) his benefices, &#91;or his benefits&#93;, or his goodnesses?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> He led me forth, and he hath brought me again whole; and he received of (or from) Gabael the money that he owed to thee; and he made me to have a wife, and he drove away the fiend &#91;or the devil&#93; from her; he made joy to (or for) her father and mother; he delivered me from the devouring of a fish; and he made thee to see the light of heaven; and we be full-filled with all goods by him; what thing worthy to these things may we give to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But, father, I ask thee, that is, I beseech thee, that thou pray him, if peradventure he shall vouchsafe to take to (or for) himself the half part of all (the) things, whatever things be brought hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the father and the son called him, (that is, Raphael the angel), and took him asides half, and they began to pray him, that he would vouchsafe to have acceptable the half part of all (the) things, which they had brought thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then Raphael said to them privily, Bless ye God of heaven, and acknowl-edge ye to him before all men living, &#91;or before all things living&#93;, (or before all those who live), for he hath done his mercy with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For it is good to hide the private (or the secret) of an earthly king; but it is worshipful to show and acknowledge the works of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Prayer is good with fasting, and with alms-&#91;deed(s)&#93;, more than to hide treasures of gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> for why alms-&#91;deed(s)&#93; delivereth from death, and those alms-(deeds) it is that purgeth sins, and maketh man to find everlasting life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Forsooth they that do sin and wickedness, be enemies of their (own) soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Therefore I (shall) show (or I shall tell)&#91;the&#93; truth to you, and I shall not hide from you a privy word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> When thou, Tobit, prayedest with tears, and buriedest dead men &#91;or and thou buriedest the dead&#93;, and leftest thy meat (or thy meal), and hidest those dead men by day in thine house, and buriedest them in the night, I offered thy prayers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="For angels offer to God the prayers of just men; they be spirits of service sent into service, for them that take the heritage of health (or who receive the inheritance of deliverance); to (or see the Book of) Hebrews, in the first Chapter.">a</a></sup>to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And for thou were acceptable to, &#91;or accepted (by)&#93; the Lord, it was need-ful that temptation should prove thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And now (or And so) the Lord sent me for to cure &#91;or to heal&#93; thee, and to deliver Sarah, the wife of thy son, from the fiend &#91;or the devil&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For I am Raphael, the angel, one of the seven that be present &#91;or that stand&#93; before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And when they had heard this, they were disturbed, or troubled, that is, they wondered and were astonish-ed, and they fell down trembling upon their face(s).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the angel said to them, Peace be to you, do not ye dread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> for when I was with you, I was (there) by God’s will. Therefore bless ye him, and sing ye to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Certainly I seemed to eat and to drink with you; but I use invisible meat, and drink that may not be seen of (or by) men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Therefore it is time, that I (re)turn again to him, that sent me; but bless ye God, and tell ye out all his marvels; bless ye him, and sing ye to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And when he had said these things, he was taken away &#91;or borne away&#93; from their sight; and they might no more see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then they felled down upon their face(s) the space of three hours, and they blessed God; and (then) they rising up, told (out) all his marvels (to everyone).</span>
