<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And now, Lord Almighty, God of Israel, a soul in anguishes, and a spirit annoyed (or harmed), crieth to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Lord, hear thou, and have mercy, for thou art merciful God; and have thou mercy on us, for we have sinned before thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> (for) thou sittest without end, and we shall not perish without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Lord God Almighty, God of Israel, hear thou now the prayer of the dead men of Israel, and of the sons of them, that &#91;have&#93; sinned before thee, and heard not the voice of their Lord God, and evils be fastened &#91;or joined&#93; to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Do not thou have mind on (or remember) the wickedness of our fathers, but have thou mind on thine hand, and on thy name, in this time;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> for thou art our Lord God, and, Lord, we shall praise thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For why for this thing thou hast given thy dread in our hearts, (so) that we call (on) thy name to (or for) help, and praise thee in our captivity; for we shall be converted &#91;or turned&#93; from the wickedness of our fathers, that sinned against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And lo! we be in our captivity today, whither thou scatteredest us, into shame, and into cursing, and into sin, by &#91;or after&#93; all the wickedness of our fathers, that went away from thee, thou our Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Israel, hear thou the command-ments of life; perceive thou with ears, (so) that thou know prudence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Israel, what (or why) is it, that thou art in the land of (thy) enemies? Thou waxedest (or grewest) eld in an alien land, thou art defouled with dead men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> (and) thou art areckoned with them, that go down into hell (or the grave)?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Thou hast forsaken the well of wisdom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> for why if thou haddest gone in the ways of God, soothly thou haddest dwelled in peace on (the) earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Learn thou, where is wisdom, where is prudence, where is virtue, where is understanding, (so) that thou know (al)together, where is long enduring of life, and lifelode, (or livelihood), (and) where is (the) light of (the) eyes, and peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Who found the place thereof, and who entered into the treasures thereof?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Where be the princes of (the) heathen men, and that be lords over the beasts, that be on (the) earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Which played with the birds of heaven; which treasure silver and gold, in which men trust, and none end &#91;there&#93; is of the purchasing of them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Which make silver, and be busy, and no finding is of their works?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> They be destroyed, and went down to hells, &#91;or to hell&#93;, (or to the grave); and other men rose (up) in the place of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The young men of them saw light, and dwelled on (the) earth. But they knew not the way of wisdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> neither understood the paths thereof; neither the sons of them received it. It was made far from the face of them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> it is not heard (of) in the land of Canaan, neither &#91;it&#93; is seen in Teman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Also the sons of Hagar, that sought out prudence which is of (or on the) earth, the merchants of (the) earth, and of Teman, and the tale tellers &#91;or the fablers, or janglers&#93;, and seekers out of prudence, and of understand-ing. But they knew not the way of wisdom, neither had mind on the paths thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> O! Israel, the house of God is full great, and the place of his possession is great;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> it is great and hath none end, high and great without measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> (The) Named giants were there; they that were of great stature at the beginning, and knew (or knowing in) battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> The Lord chose not these, neither they found the way of wisdom &#91;or of discipline&#93;; therefore they perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And for they had not wisdom, they perished for their unwisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Who ascended, or went up, into heaven, and took that wisdom, and brought it down from the clouds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Who (hath) passed over the sea, and found it, and brought it more than chosen gold?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> None is &#91;or There is not&#93;, that may know the way thereof, neither that seeketh &#91;out&#93; the paths thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> but he that have all things &#91;or that knoweth all things&#93;, knew it, and found it by his prudence. Which made ready the earth in everlasting time, and filled it with two-footed beasts, and (with) four-footed beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Which sendeth out (the) light, and it goeth, and called it (again); and it obeyeth to him in trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Forsooth (the) stars gave light in their keepings, and were glad; those &#91;or they&#93; were called, and those said, We be present &#91;or they said, We come to (thee)&#93;; and those (or they) shined to him with mirth, that made those &#91;or them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> This is our God, and none other shall be guessed against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> This &#91;or He&#93; found each way of wisdom, and gave it to Jacob, his child (or his servant), and to Israel, his darling &#91;or his dearworth(y)&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> After these things he was seen in (many) lands, and lived with men.</span>
