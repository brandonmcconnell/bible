<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ROMANS 2:5; JAMES 5:7–8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:8"> Your gold and silver hath rusted, and the rust of them shall be to you into witnessing, and shall eat your fleshes, as fire. <span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:13; JEREMIAH 22:13; DEUTERONOMY 24:14–15"> Lo! the hire of your workmen, that reaped your fields, which is defrauded of you, crieth; and the cry of them hath entered into the ears of the Lord of hosts. <span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:22–23; JOHN 19:9–11; ISAIAH 53:7"> ye brought, and slew the just Man, and he against-stood not you. &#91;ye led to, and slew the just man, and he withstood you not (or and he did not withstand you).&#93;(ye condemned, and killed the Just Man, and he did not oppose you, or and he did not stand against you.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:1–3; JAMES 5:8–9; JOEL 2:23"> Therefore, brethren, be ye patient, till to the coming of the Lord. Lo! an earth-tiller abideth &#91;the&#93; precious fruit of the earth, patiently suffering, till he receive timeful and lateful fruit. (And so brothers, endure, until the coming of the Lord. Behold! a farmer waiteth for the precious fruit from the earth, yea, patiently waiting, until he receive (the fruit) in its time, and then even a later harvest.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JAMES 5:9; GALATIANS 5:22; PSALMS 37:7"> And be ye patient, and confirm ye your hearts, for the coming of the Lord shall nigh. (And so be patient, and make your hearts firm, for the coming of the Lord shall approach, or it is near.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:18; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:5; JAMES 4:11"> Brethren, do not ye be sorrowful &#91;or do not ye be scornful&#93; each to (the) other, that ye be not deemed (or so that ye be not judged). Lo! the judge standeth nigh before the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:32–38; MATTHEW 5:11–12; HEBREWS 13:7"> Brethren, take ye (an) ensample of evil going out, and of long abiding, and travail, &#91;or and of (the) long abiding of travail&#93;, and of patience, the prophets, that spake to you in the name of the Lord, (or the prophets, who spoke to you in the name of the Lord).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JAMES 1:12; EXODUS 34:6; JOB 1:21–22"> Lo! we bless them that suffered. Ye have heard (of) the patience of Job, and ye saw the end of the Lord (or and ye saw his end with the Lord), for the Lord is merciful, and doing mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:33–37; MATTHEW 23:16–22; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:17–20"> Before all things, my brethren, do not ye swear, neither by heaven, neither by earth, neither by whatever other oath. But be your word Yea, yea, Nay, nay, that ye fall not under doom (or so that ye do not fall under judgement).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; JONAH 2:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:24–25"> And if any of you is sorrowful, &#91;or (is) heavy&#93;, pray he with (a) patient soul, and say he a psalm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MARK 6:13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:8; MARK 16:18"> If any of you is sick, lead he in (some) priests of the church (or send he for some priests from the church), and pray they for him, and anoint him with oil in the name of the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST JOHN 5:14–16; JAMES 5:13; JAMES 5:16"> and the prayer of faith shall save the sick man&#91;or and the prayer of faith shall save the sick&#93;, and the Lord shall make him light (or and the Lord shall put him at ease); and if he be in sins, they shall be forgiven to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:12–13; PROVERBS 15:29; MATTHEW 21:22"> Therefore acknowledge ye each to (the) other your sins, and pray ye each for (the) other, (so) that ye be saved. For the continual prayer of a just man is (of) much worth.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST KINGS 18:42–45; JEREMIAH 14:22; 1ST KINGS 18:18"> And again he prayed, and heaven gave rain, and the earth gave his fruit (or and the land brought forth its fruit).</span>
