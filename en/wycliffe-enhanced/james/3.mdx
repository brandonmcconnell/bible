<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 12:47–48; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:7; 1ST PETER 5:3"> My brethren, do not ye be made (into) many masters, witting that ye take the more doom. (My brothers, do not let many of you become teachers, knowing that if ye do, ye shall receive a sterner judgement, or a greater condemnation.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JAMES 1:26; PROVERBS 13:3; 1ST PETER 3:10"> For all we offend in many things. If any man offendeth not in word, this is a perfect man, (or If some man offendeth not by speaking unkindly, or harshly, then he is a perfect man); for also he may lead about all the body with a bridle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 32:9; PSALMS 39:1; JAMES 1:26"> For if we put bridles into horses’ mouths, for to consent to us, and (so) we lead about all the body of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 107:25–27; MATTHEW 8:24; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 27:14–38"> And lo! ships, when they be great, and be driven of strong winds, yet they be borne about of a little rudder, where the moving of the governor will. (And behold! ships, although they be great, and be driven by strong winds, yet they can be turned about by a little rudder, wherever the captain desireth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:21; JEREMIAH 9:3–8; PROVERBS 12:18"> So also the tongue is but a little member, and raiseth great things. Lo! how little fire burneth &#91;or kindleth&#93; a full great wood, (or Behold! how a little fire burneth a very large forest).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 120:2–3; PROVERBS 16:27; MARK 7:15"> And our tongue is (a) fire, the university of wickedness. The tongue is ordained in our members, which defouleth all the body; and it is enflamed, &#91;or set afire&#93;, of hell (or and it is set on fire from hell), and enflameth the wheel of our birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MARK 5:4"> And all the kind(s) of beasts, and of fowls, and of serpents, and of others is chastised, and those be made tame of man’s kind (or and they all can be tamed by mankind); &#91;Soothly all kind(s) of beasts, and fowls, and serpents, and of others, be overcome, or under-yoked, and be made tame, of mankind, (or and can be made tame by mankind);&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ROMANS 3:13; PSALMS 140:3; PSALMS 55:21"> but no man may chastise the tongue (or but no one can discipline the tongue), for it is an unpeaceable evil, and full of deadly venom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 10:7; GENESIS 1:26–27; PSALMS 62:4"> In it we bless God, the Father, and in it we curse men, that be made to the likeness of God. (With it we bless God the Father, and with it we curse men, who be made in the likeness of God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ROMANS 12:14; 1ST PETER 3:9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:3"> (Out) Of the same mouth passeth forth &#91;or cometh forth&#93; blessing and cursing. My brethren (or My brothers), it behooveth not that these things be done so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Whether a well of the same hole bringeth forth sweet (water), and salt water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 6:43–44; MATTHEW 12:33; MATTHEW 7:16–20"> My brethren (or My brothers), whether a fig tree may make grapes, either a vine figs? So neither salt water may make sweet water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:23; JAMES 3:17; MATTHEW 5:5"> Who is wise, and taught among you? show he of good living his working &#91;or show he of good living his work&#93;, in mildness of his wisdom. (Who is wise, and learned among you? show he by good living his work, along with the humility and the meekness of his wisdom.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JAMES 3:16; 1ST CORINTHIANS 13:4; PHILIPPIANS 2:3"> (So) That if ye have bitter envy, and strivings &#91;or strives&#93; be in your hearts, do not ye have glory (or do not boast), and be liars against the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JAMES 3:17; JAMES 1:17; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:19"> For this wisdom is not from above coming down, but earthly, and beastly, and fiendly (or and devilish).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JAMES 3:14; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 14:33"> For where (there) is envy and strife, there is unsteadfastness and all shrewd work (or and every depraved work).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:22–23; PROVERBS 2:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 13:4–7"> But wisdom that is from above, first it is chaste, afterward peaceable, mild (or meek and humble), able to be counselled &#91;or persuadable&#93;, consenting to good things, full of mercy and of good fruits, deeming without feigning (or judging with sincerity).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:9; HOSEA 10:12; PHILIPPIANS 1:11"> And the fruit of rightwiseness is sown in peace, to men that make peace. (And the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace, by men who make peace.)</span>
