<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:23–24; JAMES 2:5–6; PROVERBS 19:1"> And a meek brother have glory in his enhancing, (And a humble man have glory in his exalting,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST PETER 1:24; 1ST JOHN 2:17; JOB 14:2"> and a rich man in his lowness; for as the flower of grass he shall pass (away).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:7–8; PSALMS 37:35–36; PSALMS 49:6–14"> The sun rose up with heat, and dried the grass &#91;or the hay&#93;, and the flower of it felled down, and the fairness of his cheer perished (or and the beauty of his face perished); and so a rich man withereth in his ways.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:10; 1ST PETER 1:6–8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:10"> Blessed is the man, that suffereth temptation, (or Happy is the man, who undergoeth testing); for when he shall be proved, he shall receive the crown of life, which God promised to men that love him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 7:14; JOB 15:35; ROMANS 5:12–21"> Afterward coveting &#91;or Then coveting&#93;, when it hath conceived, bringeth forth sin; but sin, when it is &#91;ful&#93; filled, (en)gendereth death.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 3:27; MATTHEW 7:11; HEBREWS 13:8"> Each good gift, and each perfect gift is from above, and cometh down from the Father of lights, with whom is none other changing, nor overshadowing of reward. &#91;+Each best thing given, and all perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of lights, with whom is not any changing, neither shadowing of whileness, or (of) time.&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ROMANS 8:29–31; 1ST PETER 1:23; JOHN 3:3–5"> For willfully he begat us by the word of truth, that we be a beginning of his creature(s). (For willingly, or by free will, he begat us by the Word of Truth, so that we hold the first rank among all his creatures.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:27; PROVERBS 21:23; PROVERBS 14:29"> Know ye, my brethren most (be)loved, be each man swift to hear, but slow to speak, and slow to wrath;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 2:24–25; EPHESIANS 4:26; JAMES 3:17–18"> for the wrath of man worketh not the rightwiseness of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:22; EPHESIANS 1:13; 1ST PETER 2:11"> For which thing cast ye away all uncleanness, and plenty of malice, and in mildness (or and in meekness and humility), receive ye the word that is planted, that may save your souls (or that can save your souls).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ROMANS 2:13; LUKE 6:46–48; JOHN 13:17"> But be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving yourselves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:26–27; EZEKIEL 33:31–32; JAMES 2:14–26"> For if any man is an hearer of the word, and not a doer, this shall be likened to a man that beholdeth the cheer of his birth in a mirror (or he shall be like a man who seeth his face in a mirror);</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2ND PETER 3:11; LUKE 1:66; LUKE 7:39"> for he beheld himself, and went away, and anon he forgot which &#91;or what&#93; he was (or and at once he forgot what he was).</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKE 11:28; JOHN 13:17; 2ND CORINTHIANS 3:17–18"> But he that beholdeth into the law of perfect freedom, and dwelleth in it, and is not made a forgetful hearer, but a doer of work(s), this shall be blessed in his deed(s).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:29; PSALMS 141:3; 1ST PETER 3:10"> And if any man guesseth himself to be religious, and refraineth not his tongue, but deceiveth his heart, the religion of him is vain (or his religion is empty and useless).</span>
