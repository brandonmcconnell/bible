<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 12:51; DEUTERONOMY 7:8; AMOS 2:10"> For I led thee out of the land of Egypt, and of the house of servage I delivered thee; and I sent before thy face Moses, and Aaron, and Mary. <span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOSHUA 5:9–10; NUMBERS 25:1; JUDGES 5:11"> My people, bethink, I pray, what Balak, king of Moab, thought, and what Balaam, son of Beor, of Shittim, answered to him till to Gilgal, that thou shouldest know the rightwiseness of the Lord. (My people, remember, I pray thee, what Balak, the king of Moab, thought,or planned, to do against you, and what Balaam, the son of Beor, answered to him; remember the journey from Shittim to Gilgal, so that thou shalt know the Lord’s righteousness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 51:16–17; ROMANS 10:2–3; PSALMS 40:6–8"> What worthy thing shall I offer to the Lord? shall I bow the knee to the high God? Shall I offer him burnt sacrifices, and one year old calves?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND KI 16:3; 1ST SAMUEL 15:22; PSALMS 51:16"> Whether God may be satisfied in thousands of wethers, either in many thousands of fat goat bucks? Whether I shall give my first begotten for my great trespass, the fruit of my womb for sin of my soul? (Can God truly be satisfied even with thousands of rams, or with many thousands of fat goat bucks? Shall I give my first begotten son for my great trespass, yea, the fruit of my womb for the sin of my soul?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:12–13; PROVERBS 21:3; COLOSSIANS 3:12"> I shall show to thee, thou man, what is good, and what the Lord asketh of thee; forsooth for to do doom, and for to love mercy, and be busy for to walk with thy God. (I shall tell thee, ye people, what is good, and what the Lord asketh of thee; and that is, to act rightly, or with justice, and to love mercy, or and to love mercifully, and to be busy, or committed, to walk with thy God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:19; HOSEA 14:9; ISAIAH 30:27"> The voice of the Lord crieth to the city, and health shall be to all men dreading thy name. Listen, ye tribes, to him who hath gathered thee together, or Listen, ye tribes, all ye who gather together in the city.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="AMOS 3:10; AMOS 8:5–6; JEREMIAH 5:26–27"> Yet fire is in the house of unpious men, the treasures of wickedness, and a less measure, (which make me) full of wrath. (Yet in the house of the unrighteous, or of the wicked, there is fire, yea, the treasures of wickedness, and a false measure, which make me very angry.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HOSEA 12:7; LEVITICUS 19:36; PROVERBS 16:11"> Whether I shall justify the wicked balance, and the guileful weights of a little sack, (Can I justify a wicked, or a false, balance, and a bag of deceitful weights,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:23; JEREMIAH 9:8; HOSEA 7:13"> in which rich men thereof be filled with wickedness? And those living there spoke lies, and their tongues were deceitful in their mouths.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:23; DEUTERONOMY 28:21–22; HOSEA 13:16"> And I therefore began for to smite thee, in(to) perdition on thy sins. (And so I began to strike thee down unto perdition, for thy sins.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HOSEA 4:10; LEVITICUS 26:26; ISAIAH 65:13"> Thou shalt eat, and shalt not be &#91;ful&#93; filled, and thy meeking is in the middle of thee (or and thy humbling is in thy midst); and thou shalt take (hold), and shalt not save; and (that) which thou shalt save, I shall give into (the) sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 1:13; AMOS 5:11; JEREMIAH 12:13"> Thou shalt sow, and shalt not reap; thou shalt tread the olive, and shalt not be anointed with oil; and (make) must, and shalt not drink (the) wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:51; 1ST KINGS 16:25–33; JEREMIAH 7:24"> And thou keptest the behests of Omri, and all the work of the house of Ahab, and hast walked in the lusts of them, that I should give thee into perdition, and men dwelling in it into hissing, either scorning, and ye shall bear the shame of my people. (And thou hast followed the ways of Omri, and all the work of the house of Ahab, and hast walked in their lusts, so that now I shall give thee unto perdition, and the people living there unto hissing, or mocking, and ye shall bear the shame of my people.)</span>
