</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> But the manyfold engendered multitude of wicked &#91;or unpious&#93; men shall not be profitable; and (as) plantings of adultery they shall not give deep roots, neither shall set stable steadfastness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Though they burgeon in boughs &#91;or (in) branches&#93; for a time, they set unsteadfastly shall be moved of (or by) the wind, and they shall be drawn out by the root of the greatness of winds &#91;or and of the muchliness of winds (they shall be) pulled up by the roots&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For why boughs &#91;or branches&#93; unperfect shall be broken (al)together; and the fruits of them be unprofitable, and sour to eat, and covenable to (or suitable for) nothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For why all (the) sons, that be born of wicked men, be witnesses of wickedness against fathers and mothers, in their asking.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> But a just man &#91;or The rightwise&#93;, though he be before-occupied by death, shall be in refreshing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> For why worshipful eld (age) is not of long time &#91;or long enduring&#93;, neither it is reckoned by the number of years;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> the wits of a man be hoary, and the age of eld (or old age) is life without wem, (or spot), &#91;or undefouled&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He pleased God, and was made dearworth(y), &#91;or loved&#93;, and he living among sinners was translated, or borne over;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> he was ravished, lest malice should change his understanding, either lest feigning should deceive &#91;or beguile&#93; his soul.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For why deceiving of trifling maketh dark good things, and the unstable-ness of covetousness turneth over &#91;or overturneth&#93; the wit without malice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> He was ended in short time, and &#91;ful&#93; filled many times;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> for why his soul was pleasant to &#91;or pleased&#93; God; for this thing God hast(en)ed to lead him out from the midst of wickednesses;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> but peoples saw and understood not, neither setted &#91;or putted&#93; such things in their hearts or entrails. For the grace and mercy of God is on (or with) his saints, and (the) beholding &#91;or (the) respect&#93; of God is on (or for) his chosen men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Forsooth a just man dead condemn-eth quick wicked men; and youth ended swiftlier condemneth the long life of an unjust man. &#91;Forsooth the rightwise dead condemneth the un-pious men alive; and youth swiftlier ended, the long life of the unrightwise.&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For they shall see the end of a wise man, and they shall not under-stand, what thing God thought of him, and why the Lord made him less &#91;or diminished him&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For they shall see, and shall despise him; but the Lord shall scorn them. And after these things they shall be falling down without honour, and in despising among dead men without end.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For he shall all-break them swollen without voice, and he shall move them from the foundaments (or the foundations); and they shall be desolate till to the last thing. And they shall be wailing, and the mind (or the memory) of them shall perish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> They shall come fearedful in the thought of their sins; and their wickednesses on the contrary side shall lead them over.</span>
