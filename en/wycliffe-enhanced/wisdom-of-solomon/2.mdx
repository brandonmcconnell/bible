<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Forsooth wicked men said, thinking with themselves not rightfully, The time of our life is little, and with annoy-ance (or harm); no refreshing is in the end of a man, and none there is, that is known, that (re)turned again from hells (or from the grave)&#91;or from hell&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For we were born (out) of nought, and after this time we shall be, as if we had not been; for why smoke is blown out in (or of) our nostrils, and a word of sparkle to stir our heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For our body shall be quenched ashes, and the spirit shall be scattered abroad as soft air; and our life shall pass as the step of a cloud, and it shall be departed as a mist, which is driven away of (or by) the beams of the sun, and is grieved of (or by) the heart thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And our name shall take forget-ting by the passing of time; and no man shall have mind (or remembrance) of our works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For why our time is the passing of a shadow, and no (re)turning again of (or after) our end there is &#91;or and there is not turning again of (or after) our end&#93;; for it is asealed, and no man (re)turneth again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Therefore come ye, and use we the goods that be, and use we a creature, as in youth, swiftly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Fill we us with precious wine and ointments; and the flower of time pass not (by) us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Crown we us with roses, before that they wither; no meadow be, that our lechery pass not by &#91;or through&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> No man of us be there without part of our lechery; everywhere leave we the signs of gladness; for this is our part, and this is our heritage, (or our inheritance), &#91;or our lot&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Oppress we a poor just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man, and spare we not a widow, neither reverence we hoar hairs of an old man of much time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> But our strength be the law of rightfulness; for why that that is feeble, is found unprofitable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Therefore deceive we a just &#91;or the rightwise&#93; man, for he is unprofit-able to us, and he is contrary to our works; and he upbraideth &#91;or reprovingly putteth&#93; to us the sins of (the) law, and he defameth on &#91;or against&#93; us the sins of our teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> He promiseth that he hath the cunning (or the knowing) of God, and he nameth himself the son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He is made to us into showing of our thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He is grievous to us, yea, to see; for why his life is unlike to other men, and his ways be changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> We be guessed of him to be triflers, that is, men of no virtue, and he abstaineth himself from our ways, as from uncleannesses; and he before-setteth &#91;or telleth before&#93; the last things of just men, and he hath glory, that he hath God (as)his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Therefore see we, if his words be true; and assay we, what things shall come to him; and we shall know, what shall be the last things of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For if he is the very (or the true) son of God, he shall up-take him, and shall deliver him from the hands of them that be contrary to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Ask we him by despising and tor-ment, (so) that we know his reverence, and that we prove his patience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> By most foul death condemn we him, for why beholding &#91;or respect&#93; shall be of his words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> They thought these things, and they erred; for why their malice blinded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And they knew not the sacra-ments of God, neither they hoped (for) the meed (or the reward) of rightful-ness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;, neither they deemed the honour of holy souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For why God made man unable to be destroyed, &#91;or undeadly&#93;, (or immortal), and God made man to the image of his likeness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> But by envy of (or from) the devil death entered into the world; forsooth they pursue him, that be of his part.</span>
