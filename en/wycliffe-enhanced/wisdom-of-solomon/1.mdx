<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Ye that deem the earth, love rightfulness; feel ye of the Lord in goodness, and seek ye him in the simpleness of heart. &#91;Love ye right-wiseness, that deem the earth; feeleth of the Lord in goodness, and in simpleness of heart seeketh him.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For he is found of them, that tempt not him &#91;or that tempt him not&#93;; for-sooth he appeareth to them, that have faith into him. (For he is found by those, who do not test him; he appeareth to those, who have faith in him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For why wayward thoughts part &#91;or sever&#93;(one) from God; but proved virtue reproveth unwise men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For why wisdom shall not enter into an evil-willed soul; neither it shall dwell in a body subject to sins. &#91;For into an evil-willing soul shall not go in wisdom; nor shall dwell in a body subject to sins.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Forsooth the Holy Ghost of wisdom shall fly or flee away from a feigned man, and he shall take away himself from thoughts, that be with-out understanding; and the unwise man shall be punished of (or by) wick-edness coming above (or upon him). &#91;An holy spirit forsooth shall flee the feigner, or the feigned thing, of dis-cipline, and shall take himself away from thoughts, that be without under-standing; and he shall be chastised from the overcoming wickedness.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For the spirit of wisdom is benign, and he shall not deliver a cursed man from his lips; for why God is witness of his reins, and the searcher of his heart is true, and the hearer of his tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For why the Spirit of the Lord hath filled the world; and this thing, that containeth all things, hath the cun-ning (or the knowing) of (the) voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Therefore this he that speaketh wicked thing &#91;or For that he that speaketh wicked things&#93;, may not be hid; and doom and punishing shall not pass (by) him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For why asking shall be in the thoughts of a wicked man &#91;or the unpious&#93;. Forsooth the hearing of his words shall come to God, and to the punishing &#91;or the correction&#93; of his wickednesses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> for the ear of fervent love heareth all things &#91;or for the ear of the jealous heareth all things&#93;, and the noise of grutchings (or of grumblings) shall not be hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Therefore keep ye you from grutch-ing (or from grumbling), that profiteth nothing, and from back-biting spare ye the tongue; for a dark word shall not go into vain; forsooth the mouth that lieth, slayeth the soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Do not ye covet death, in the error of your life, neither get ye perdition in (or with) the works of your hands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> for God made not death, neither he is glad in the perdition of living men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For why God made (out) of nought all things &#91;or Forsooth God made, that all things were&#93;, that those should be; and he made the nations of the world able to be healed. For why medicine of destroying is not in those men, neither the realm of hells &#91;or of hell&#93; is in (the) earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For rightfulness is everlasting, and undeadly (or immortal); but unrightful-ness is(the) getting of death. &#91;Right-wiseness forsooth is perpetual, and undeadly; unrightwiseness forsooth purchasing of death.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Forsooth wicked &#91;or unpious&#93; men called that unrightfulness by hands and words, and they guessed &#91;or esteemed&#93; it a friend, and floated away, and they putted promises to it; for they be worthy (of) the death, that be of the part thereof.</span>
