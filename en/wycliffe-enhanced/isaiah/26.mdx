</div>

When thou shalt make thy judgements upon the earth, all the inhabitants of the world shall learn righteousness.)</span>
</div>

And so thou hast punished them, and hast all-broken them, and thou hast destroyed all the memory of them;)</span>
</div>

We have not won any victory in the land, and the inhabitants of the earth have not fallen.)</span>
