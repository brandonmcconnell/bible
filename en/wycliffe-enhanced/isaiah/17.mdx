</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="AMOS 3:11–15; ISAIAH 27:10; ISAIAH 24:1–12"> In that day the cities of strength thereof shall be forsaken as plows, and &#91;the&#93; corns that were forsaken of the face of the sons of Israel; and thou shalt be forsaken. (On that day its strong cities shall be deserted, like the countryside, and also the cornfields that were left for the Israelites, yea, all shall be made desolate.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 106:21; DEUTERONOMY 32:18; ISAIAH 26:4"> For thou hast forgotten God, thy saviour, and haddest not mind on thy strong helper; therefore thou shalt plant a faithful planting, and thou shalt sow an alien seed. (For thou hast forgotten God, thy Saviour, and did not remember thy strong helper; and so thou shalt plant a garden dedicated to idols, and thou shalt sow a strange, or a foreign, seed.)</span>
</div>

(On the day of thy planting, it shall become a wild vine, and thy seed shall flower the next day; but the ripe corn shall be taken away on the day of harvest, and Israel shall grieve, and shall have sorrow.)</span>

(The peoples shall sound like the sound of flowing water, and God shall rebuke them; and they shall flee far away, and they shall be driven like the dust on the hills by the face of the wind, and like a whirlwind before the tempest.)</span>
