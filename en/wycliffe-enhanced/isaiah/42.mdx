</div>

(So that thou shouldest open the eyes of the blind; and that thou shouldest lead out the bound, or the prisoners, from being enclosed, yea, those sitting in darkness, from out of the prison house.)</span>
</div>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:19; ISAIAH 46:9–10; JOHN 13:19"> Lo! those things that were the first, be come, and (now) I tell new things; I shall make heard to you, before that those begin to be made. </div>

(Sing ye a new song to the Lord; his praises come from the ends of the earth; ye who go down to the sea, and its fullness, the islands, and their inhabitants.)</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ROMANS 15:9–11; PSALMS 117:1–2; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:9–12"> They shall set glory to the Lord, and they shall tell his praising in isles. (They shall give glory to the Lord, and they shall tell out his praises on the islands.)</span>
</div>

(The Lord shall go out like a strong man, he shall stir up fervour like a man of war; he shall speak, and he shall cry out; he shall be strong against his enemies.)</span>
</div>

</div>

</div>

(And I shall lead out the blind by the way, which they know not, and I shall make them to go on paths, which they knew not; I shall turn their darkness into light before them, and make depraved, or crooked, ways into straight ways; I shall do these things for them, and I shall not desert them.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 97:7; ISAIAH 1:29; ISAIAH 44:11"> (But others), They (shall) be turned aback; (yea), be they shamed with shame, that trust in a graven image; which say to a molten image, Ye be our gods.</span>
</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HEBREWS 8:10; ROMANS 3:31; ISAIAH 46:12–13"> And the Lord would, that he should hallow it, and magnify the law, and enhance it. (And the Lord desired, that he would hallow the Law, and magnify it, and exalt it, or lift it up.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 102:20; JEREMIAH 50:17; ISAIAH 51:23"> But that people was ravished, and wasted; all they be the snare of young men, and be hid in the houses of prisons. (But that people was robbed, and taken as prey; they all be ensnared, and be hid in prison houses. They be made into raven, or spoils, and there is no one who rescueth them; yea, into robbing, and there is no one who saith, Yield thou or Give thou them back!)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:22–23; ISAIAH 48:18; DEUTERONOMY 32:29"> Who is among you, that heareth this, (that) perceiveth, and hearkeneth (to) things to coming?</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ISAIAH 47:6; ISAIAH 10:5–6; MATTHEW 22:7"> Who gave Jacob into ravishing, and Israel to destroyers? Whether not the Lord? He it is, against whom they sinned; and they would not go in his ways, and they heard not his law. (Who gave up Jacob for robbing, and Israel over to destroyers? Did not the Lord? It is he whom they sinned against; and they would not go in his ways, and they would not listen to his Law.)</span>
</div>
