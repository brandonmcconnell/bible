(The Lord, the King of Israel, and its Redeemer, the Lord of hosts saith these things, I am the first, and I am the last, and there is no God besides me.)</span>

Whether a God is without me, and a former, whom I knew not? (Do not ye fear, nor be ye troubled; from that time I have made thee to hear it, and I have told thee; ye be my witnesses. Is there any God besides me, or any Creator, or any Maker, whom I knew not?)</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:5; HABAKKUK 2:18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:26"> Who formed a god, and melted out an image, not profitable to anything? (Who formed a god, and melted out an idol, not profitable for anything?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 42:17; ISAIAH 45:16; PSALMS 97:7"> Lo! all the partners thereof shall be shamed; for the smiths be of men (or for these smiths be but men). When (they) all shall come, they shall stand, and shall dread, and shall be shamed together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:6–7; ISAIAH 40:19; ISAIAH 46:6–7"> A smith wrought with a file; he formed it in coals, and in hammers (or and with hammers), and he wrought with the arm of his strength. He shall be hungry, and he shall fail; he shall not drink water, and he shall be faint.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JUDGES 17:4–5; PSALMS 115:5–7; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:29"> A carpenter stretched forth a rule, he formed it with an adze or with an awl, either a joiner’s hook; he made it in the corner places, and he turned it in compass, (or he made the corners, and he turned it all around); and he made the image of a man, as a fair man, dwelling in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:20; JEREMIAH 10:3–8; HOSEA 4:12"> He cutted down cedars, he took an hawthorn, and an oak, that stood among the trees of the forest; he planted a pineapple tree &#91;or he planted the pine tree&#93;, which he nourished with rain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 25:14; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 9:20; JUDGES 2:19"> and it was made into fire to men. (and it was made into fire for people. He took some of it, and was warmed, and he burned it, and baked loaves; but with the rest of it he made a god, and then worshipped it, and he made a carved idol, and then he was bowed down before that.)</span>

I am warmed; I saw &#91;the&#93; fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 45:20; 1ST KINGS 18:26; DANIEL 6:27"> Forsooth the residue thereof he made a god, and a graven image to himself; he is bowed before that, and worshippeth that, and beseecheth, and saith, Deliver thou me, for thou art my god. (But with the rest of it he made a god, yea, a carved idol for himself; and he is bowed down before that, and worshippeth that, and beseecheth, and saith, Save thou me, for thou art my god.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 6:9–10; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:3–4; JOHN 12:39–40"> They knew not, neither understood, for they have forgotten, that their eyes see not, and that they understand not with their heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:15; 1ST KINGS 11:7; 1ST KINGS 11:5"> They bethink not in their soul, neither they know, neither they feel, that they say, I burnt the half thereof in fire, and I baked loaves on the coals thereof, and I seethed fleshes, and ate; and of the residue thereof shall I make an idol? shall I fall down before the stock of a tree? (They do not think in their soul, nor do they know, nor do they feel, so that they might say, I burned half of it in the fire, and then I baked some loaves on the coals there, and I boiled some meat, and ate; and with the rest of it shall I make an idol? and then shall I fall down before a piece of a tree? or before a piece of wood?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 1:28; JOB 15:31; HOSEA 12:1"> A part thereof is ashes; an unwise heart shall worship it, and he shall not deliver his soul, neither he shall say, A strong leasing is in my right hand. (A part of it is ashes; and an unwise heart shall worship it, but it shall not save his soul, nor shall he say, Only a big lie, or a great falsehood, is in my right hand.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:1–2; ISAIAH 49:15–16; ISAIAH 43:15"> Thou, Jacob, and Israel, have mind of these things, (or Thou, Jacob, O Israel, remember these things), for thou art my servant; I formed thee, Israel, thou art my servant; thou shalt not forget me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:25; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:18–19"> I did away thy wickednesses as a cloud, and thy sins as a mist; turn thou again to me, for I again-bought thee. (I did away thy wickednesses like a cloud, and thy sins like a mist; return thou to me, for I have bought thee back, that is, I have ransomed, or I have redeemed, thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:6–7; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 5:8–14; PSALMS 69:34"> Ye heavens, praise, for the Lord hath done mercy; the last parts of &#91;the&#93; earth, sing ye heartily song, (or the ends of the earth, heartily sing ye songs); hills, sound ye praising; the forest, and each tree thereof, praise God; for the Lord again-bought Jacob, and Israel shall have glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ISAIAH 45:12; ISAIAH 42:5; ISAIAH 54:5"> The Lord, thine again-buyer, and thy former from the womb (or and thy Maker from the womb), saith these things, I am the Lord, making all things, and I alone stretch forth (the) heavens, and stablish the earth, and none is with me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 33:10; JOB 5:12–14; JEREMIAH 50:36"> and I make void the signs of false diviners, and I turn into madness diviners that divine by sacrifices offered to fiends; and I turn wise men backward, and I make their science fond (or foolish).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1ST KINGS 18:36–38; ISAIAH 42:9; NEHEMIAH 1:3"> And the Lord raiseth the word of his servant, and &#91;ful&#93; filleth the counsel of his messengers; and I say to Jerusalem, Thou shalt be inhabited; and to the cities of Judah, Ye shall be builded (again), and I shall raise (up) the deserts thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ISAIAH 42:15; JEREMIAH 51:36; JEREMIAH 50:38"> and I say to the depth, Be thou desolate, and I shall make dry thy floods; (and I say to the ocean depth, Be thou made dry, and I shall make thy rivers run dry or and I shall also dry up thy rivers;)</span>

(and I say to Cyrus, Thou art my shepherd, and thou shalt fulfill, or thou shalt do, all my will; and I say to Jerusalem, Thou shalt be rebuilt; and to the Temple, Thy foundations shall be laid again.)</span>
