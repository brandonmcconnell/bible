<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ISAIAH 9:6–7; JEREMIAH 33:15; HEBREWS 1:8–9"> Lo! the king shall reign in rightfulness, and the princes shall be sovereigns in doom. (Behold! the king shall reign with righteousness, and the leaders shall rule with justice.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 25:4; ISAIAH 44:3; ISAIAH 9:6"> And a man shall be, as he that is hid from &#91;the&#93; wind, and hideth himself from tempest; as streams of waters in thirst (or like streams of water in thirst), and (like) the shadow of a stone standing far out in a desert land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 29:18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:18; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:6"> The eyes of seers, that is, prophets, shall not dim, and the ears of hearers shall harken diligently;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 29:24; LUKE 21:14–15; MATTHEW 16:17"> and the heart of fools shall understand knowing, and the tongue of stuttering men shall speak swiftly, and plainly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ISAIAH 5:20; PSALMS 15:4; MALACHI 3:18"> He that is unwise shall no more be called prince, and a guileful man shall not be called the greater. (He who is unwise shall no more be called a leader, and the deceitful shall no more be called great, or honourable.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:19; MATTHEW 23:13; ISAIAH 3:15"> Forsooth a fool shall speak folly things, and his heart shall do wickedness, that he perform feigning, and speak to the Lord guilefully, (or so that he act falsely, and speak deceitfully, even to the Lord); and he shall make void the soul of an hungry man, and shall take away drink from a thirsty man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:26–28; MATTHEW 26:59–60; MATTHEW 26:14–16"> The vessels of a guileful man be worst; for he shall make ready thoughts to lose mild men in the word of a lie, (even) when a poor man spake doom. (The ways of the deceitful be the worst, or evil; they have thoughts to destroy the meek, or the humble, with lies, even when the poor speak justly, or rightly.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 8:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:29–30; LUKE 6:33–35"> Forsooth a prince shall think those things that be worthy to a prince, and he shall stand over dukes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:9; ISAIAH 3:16; ISAIAH 28:23"> Rich women, rise ye (up), and hear my voice; daughters trusting, perceive ye with ears my speech, (or trusting daughters, listen to what I have to say).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 7:23; JEREMIAH 8:13; ZEPHANIAH 1:13"> For why after days and a year, and ye that trust shall be troubled; for why &#91;the&#93; vintage is ended, (the) gathering shall no more come (or there shall be no more grapes to gather in).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 3:24; ISAIAH 33:14; ISAIAH 20:4"> Ye rich women, be astonied; ye that trust, be troubled, (or Ye rich women, be astonished; ye who be so trusting, be troubled); unclothe ye you, and be ye ashamed; gird your loins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:15; EZEKIEL 20:6; NAHUM 2:7"> wail ye on breasts, on the desirable country(side), on the plenteous vinery. (beat ye your breasts, and wail ye, for the desirable land, and for the plentiful vines.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 22:2; HOSEA 9:6; ISAIAH 7:23"> Thorns and briars shall ascend on the earth of my people; how much more on all the houses of joy of the city making full out joy? (Thorns and briars shall grow on the land of my people, and how much more in all the houses of joy, in the city making full out joy?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 24:12; ISAIAH 24:10; ISAIAH 25:2"> For why the house is left, the multitude of the city is forsaken; darknesses and groping be made on &#91;the&#93; dens, till into without end. The joy of wild asses is the pasture of flocks; (For the palace is left empty, and all the city is deserted; darkness and groping be made on the glens forever. The place of joy for the wild donkeys is now the pasture for flocks;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 29:17; EZEKIEL 39:29; ISAIAH 44:3"> till the spirit be shed out on us from on high, and the desert shall be into Carmel, and Carmel shall be areckoned into a forest. (until the Spirit is poured out on us from on high, and the wilderness shall be turned into a plentiful land, and the plentiful land shall reckoned as a forest.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 94:14–15; 1ST PETER 2:9–12; ISAIAH 60:21"> And doom shall dwell in wilderness, and rightfulness shall sit in Carmel; (And justice, or judgement, shall dwell in the wilderness, and righteousness shall be in the plentiful land;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 26:3; PSALMS 119:165; PHILIPPIANS 4:6–9"> and the work of rightfulness shall be peace, and the tilth of rightfulness shall be stillness and secureness, till into without end. (and the work of righteousness shall be peace, and the fruit, or the harvest, of righteousness shall be peace and security forever.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 2:5; HOSEA 2:18–23; JEREMIAH 33:16"> And my people shall sit in the fairness of peace, and in the tabernacles of trust, and in rich rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 28:17; ISAIAH 30:30; ISAIAH 28:2"> But hail shall be in the coming down of the forest, and by lowness the city shall be made low (or and the city shall be made low in a low place).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 11:1; ISAIAH 30:23–24; ISAIAH 55:10–11"> Blessed be ye, that sow on all waters, and send in the foot of an ox and of an ass. (Blessed be ye, who sow by all the waters, yea, send in there the foot of an ox and the foot of a donkey.)</span>
