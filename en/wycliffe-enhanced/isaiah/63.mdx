<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:13; ISAIAH 34:5–6; PSALMS 137:7"> Who is this that cometh from Edom, in dyed clothes from Bozrah? this fair man in his stole, either long cloth (or cloak), going in the multitude of his strength? I that speak rightfulness, and am a for-fighter for to save, (or It is I who speak righteously, or It is I who speak of right over wrong, and am a fighter for you, and will save you).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:13; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:15"> Why therefore is thy clothing red? and thy clothes as of men stamping in a presser? (And so why is thy clothing red? and thy clothes like those who stamp at a winepress?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:13–15; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:19–20; MALACHI 4:3"> I alone stamped the press, and of folks, either Gentiles, no man is, either was, with me, (or I stamped the winepress alone, and no one from the nations, or from the Gentiles, was with me); (and so) I stamped them in my strong vengeance, and I defouled them in my wrath; and their blood is sprinkled on my clothes, and I made foul all my clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 34:8; JEREMIAH 51:6; ISAIAH 35:4"> For why a day of vengeance is in mine heart, and the year of my yielding cometh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 44:3; ISAIAH 40:10; PSALMS 98:1"> I looked about, and none helper was; I sought, and none was that helped; and mine arm saved to me, and mine indignation, that helped me. (I looked about, but no one would help me; I sought everywhere, but no one would help; so my own arm saved me, and my anger, that upheld me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:10; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:19; ISAIAH 49:26"> And I defouled peoples in my strong vengeance; and I made them drunken in mine indignation, and I drew down their strength into the earth. (And I defiled the nations in my strong vengeance; and I made them drunk in my indignation, and I poured out their blood onto the ground.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:4; PSALMS 86:5; PSALMS 86:15"> I shall have mind on the merciful doings of the Lord, I shall preach the praising of the Lord, on all things which the Lord &#91;hath&#93; yielded to us, and the multitude of &#91;the&#93; goods of the house of Israel, which he gave to them, by his forgiveness, and by the multitude of his mercies. (I shall remember the merciful doings of the Lord, I shall preach the praises of the Lord, for all the things which the Lord hath given to us, and for the multitude of his goodness to the house of Israel, which he gave to them, by his forgiveness, and by the multitude of his mercies.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 17:7; ISAIAH 12:2; EXODUS 6:7"> And the Lord said, Nevertheless it is my people, sons not denying (me), and (so) he was made a saviour to them, (And the Lord said, Yea, they be my people, and my sons and daughters shall not deceive me, and so he was made their Saviour,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 33:14; EXODUS 23:20–21; MALACHI 3:1"> in all the tribulation of them. It was set in tribulation, and the angel of his face saved them. In his love and in his forgiveness he again-bought them, and he bare them, and raised them in all &#91;the&#93; days of the world. (in all their trials and tribulation. For he was set in tribulation with them, and it was not the angel sent from him, but he himself who saved them. In his love and in his forgiveness he redeemed, or he rescued, them, and he carried them, and he raised them up in all the days of the world.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:51; EPHESIANS 4:30; PSALMS 78:56"> Forsooth they excited him to wrathfulness, and tormented the spirit of his holy; and he was turned into an enemy of them, and he overcame them in battle. (But they rebelled against him, and tormented his Holy Spirit; and he was turned into their enemy, and he defeated them in battle.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NUMBERS 11:17; EXODUS 14:22; PSALMS 77:20"> And he had mind on the days of the world, of Moses, and of his people. Where is he, that led them out of the sea, with the shepherds of his flock? Where is he, that setted the spirit of his holy in the middle thereof; (And then they remembered the old days, the days of Moses, and his people, and they said, Where is he, who led them out of the Red Sea, or out of the Sea of Reeds, or who led them up from the Nile River, with the shepherds of his flock? Where is he, who put his Holy Spirit within him?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 15:16; PSALMS 78:13; JOSHUA 3:16"> which led out Moses to the right half in the arm of his majesty? which parted waters before them, that he should make to himself a name everlasting; (who led them out by the right hand of Moses with his majestic arm? who parted the waters before them, so that he would make an everlasting name for himself?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:9; PSALMS 106:9; HABAKKUK 3:15"> which led them out through depths of waters, as an horse not stumbling in desert, (who led them out through the depths of the waters, like a horse not stumbling in the wilderness,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 63:12; HEBREWS 4:8–11; JOSHUA 23:1"> as a beast going down in the field? The Spirit of the Lord was the leader thereof; so thou leddest thy people, that thou madest to thee a name of glory. (like a beast going down into the field? The Spirit of the Lord was their leader; so thou leddest thy people, and thou madest a glorious name for thyself.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:20; DEUTERONOMY 26:15; PSALMS 80:14"> Behold thou from heaven, and see from thine holy dwelling place, and from the seat of thy glory. Where is thy fervent love, and thy strength, the multitude of thine entrails, and of thy merciful doings? They withheld themselves on me (or Why hast thou withheld thyself from us?).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 64:8; MALACHI 2:10; JEREMIAH 3:19"> Forsooth thou art our father, and Abraham knew not us, and Israel knew not us. Thou, Lord, art our father, and our again-buyer; thy name is from the world. (For thou art our father, though Abraham knew us not, and Israel, or Jacob, knew us not. Thou, Lord, art our father, and our Redeemer; thy name is from forever.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 6:10; JOSHUA 11:20; EZEKIEL 14:7–9"> Lord, why hast thou made us to err from thy ways? thou hast made hard our heart, that we dreaded not thee? be thou converted, for thy servants, the lineages of thine heritage. (Lord, why hast thou let us go astray from thy ways? hast thou hardened our hearts, so that we do not fear thee? or so that we do not have reverence for thee? return thou for the sake of thy servants, the tribes of thy inheritance.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 74:3–7; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:2; DEUTERONOMY 7:6"> They had as nought thine holy people in possession, and our enemies defouled thine hallowing. (For a little while they had thy holy people in possession, and our enemies defiled thy sanctuary.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 135:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:16; ROMANS 9:4"> We be made as in the beginning, when thou were not Lord of us, neither thy name was called to help on us. (And we were made like in the beginning, when thou were not Lord of us, and we did not call on thy name for help or and we were not called by thy name.)</span>
