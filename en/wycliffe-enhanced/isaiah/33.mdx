</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 112:1–3; PROVERBS 19:23; 1ST TIMOTHY 4:8"> And faith shall be in thy times; the riches of health is wisdom and knowing; the dread of the Lord, that is the treasure of him. (And faith shall be in thy times; wisdom and knowledge be the riches of salvation, or of deliverance; the fear of the Lord is thy treasure.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND KI 18:18; 2ND KI 18:37–3; ISAIAH 36:22"> Lo! the seers withoutforth shall cry, &#91;the&#93; angels of peace shall weep bitterly. (Behold! the brave shall cry outside, (or aloud), the messengers of peace shall weep bitterly.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JUDGES 5:6; ISAIAH 35:8; LAMENTATIONS 1:4"> &#91;The&#93; Ways be destroyed, a goer by the path ceased; the covenant is made void, he casted down &#91;the&#93; cities, he areckoned not men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 35:2; NAHUM 1:4; ISAIAH 65:10"> The land mourned, and was sick; the Lebanon was shamed, and was foul; and Sharon is made as desert, and Bashan is shaken, and Carmel. (The land mourned, and was sick; Lebanon was shamed, and defiled; and Sharon is made a desert, or a wilderness, and Bashan, and Carmel, be shaken bare.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 12:5; PSALMS 102:13–18; AMOS 6:1"> Now I shall rise (up), saith the Lord, now I shall be enhanced (or now I shall be exalted), and now I shall be raised up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 7:14; ISAIAH 59:4; JAMES 1:15"> Ye shall conceive heat, ye shall bring forth stubble; your spirit, as fire, shall devour you. (Ye shall conceive chaff, ye shall bring forth stubble; your spirit, like a fire, shall devour you.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 27:4; 2ND SAMUEL 23:6–7; ISAIAH 10:17"> And peoples shall be as ashes of the burning; thorns gathered together shall be burnt in fire. (And the peoples, or the nations, shall be like the ashes from burning; yea, like thorns gathered together that be burned in the fire.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 48:10; EPHESIANS 2:11–18; DANIEL 3:27–3"> Ye that be far (or Ye who be far away), hear what things I have done; and, ye neighbours, know my strength.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:29; MATTHEW 25:46; ISAIAH 66:24"> Sinners be all-broken in Zion, trembling wielded (the) hypocrites; who of you may dwell with fire devouring? who of you shall dwell with everlasting burnings? (The sinners in Zion be all-broken, fear and trembling held the hypocrites; who of you can live with devouring fire? who of you can live with everlasting burning?)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 119:37; 1ST JOHN 3:7; EPHESIANS 5:11–13"> He that goeth in rightfulnesses, and speaketh truth; he that casteth away (the) avarice of false challenge, and shaketh away his hands from all gift(s), (or bribes); he that stoppeth his ears, that he hear not (of) blood, and closeth his eyes, that he see not evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 12:29–31; ISAIAH 32:18; PROVERBS 1:33"> This man shall dwell in high things, the strongholds of stones be the highness of him; bread is given to him, his waters be faithful. (This man shall live in the heights, yea, the strongholds of stones be his high places of safety; bread is given to him, his water never faileth, that is, it is always plentiful.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 9:17; JOHN 14:21; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:18"> They shall see the king in (all) his fairness; the eyes of him shall behold the land from &#91;a&#93; far.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:20; PSALMS 31:7–8; ISAIAH 38:9–22"> Eliakim, thine heart shall bethink dread; where is the lettered man? Where is he that weigheth the words of the law? where is the teacher of little children? (Thy heart shall remember what thou feared, and thou shalt ask, Now where is he who counted? where is he who weighed? yea, where is he who counted out the treasures?)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 28:11; DEUTERONOMY 28:49–50; JEREMIAH 5:15"> Thou shalt not see a people unwise, a people of deep word, so that thou mayest not understand the fair speaking of his tongue, in which people is no wisdom. (Thou shalt no longer see a fierce people, a people of deep words, those whom thou cannot understand the strange speaking of their tongues, in which people there is no wisdom.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 46:5; ISAIAH 32:18; ISAIAH 54:2"> Behold thou Zion, the city of your solemnity, (or Behold thou Zion, the city of our solemn, or our sacred, feasts); thine eyes shall see Jerusalem, a rich city, a tabernacle that may not be borne over, neither the nails thereof shall be taken away without end; and all the cords thereof shall not be broken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 46:4–5; ISAIAH 48:18; ISAIAH 66:12"> For only the worshipful doer, our Lord God, is there; the place of floods is strands full large and open (or a place of very large rivers and wide streams); the ship of rowers shall not enter by it, neither a great ship shall pass over (to) it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:31; JAMES 4:12; ZEPHANIAH 3:15–17"> For why the Lord is our judge, the Lord is our lawgiver, the Lord is our king; he shall save us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2ND KI 7:8; PSALMS 68:12; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:27"> Thy ropes be slacked, but those shall not avail; thy mast shall be so, that thou may not alarge a sign. Then the spoils of many preys shall be parted, crooked men shall ravish (the) raven. (Thy ropes be slackened, and so they cannot hold thy masts firm, and thou shalt not be able to spread the sails. Then the spoils of many preys shall be divided, and even the lame shall share in the taking.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 50:20; 1ST JOHN 1:7–9; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:4"> And a neighbour shall say, I was not sick; (and for) the people that dwelleth in that Jerusalem, wickedness shall be taken away from it. (And no one there shall say, I am sick; and for the people who liveth in that Jerusalem, their wickednesses, that is, their sins, shall be taken away.)</span>
