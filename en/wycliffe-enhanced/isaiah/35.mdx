<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:35; ISAIAH 51:3; ISAIAH 55:12–13"> The forsaken Judah and (the place) without (a) way shall be glad, and &#91;the&#93; wilderness shall make full out joy, and shall flower as a lily. (The deserted Judah and the place without a way shall be happy, and the desert shall rejoice, and shall flower like a lily.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 25:9; ISAIAH 60:13; AMOS 9:13–15"> It burgeoning shall burgeon, and it glad and praising shall make full out joy. The glory of Lebanon is given to it, the fairness of Carmel and of Sharon; they shall see the glory of the Lord, and the fairness of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:12; JOB 4:3–4; LUKE 22:32"> Comfort ye &#91;the&#93; clumsid hands (or Strengthen ye the benumbed hands), and make ye strong &#91;the&#93; feeble knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JAMES 5:7–9; ISAIAH 41:10–14; HAGGAI 2:4"> Say ye, Men of little comfort, be ye comforted, and do not ye dread; lo! our God shall bring the vengeance of yielding, God himself shall come, and shall save us. (Say ye, People of little courage, be ye strengthened, and do not ye fear; behold! our God shall bring the vengeance of punishment, (or of retribution), God himself shall come, and shall save us.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ISAIAH 42:6–7; LUKE 7:20–23; ISAIAH 29:18"> Then the eyes of blind men shall be opened, and the ears of deaf men shall be open(ed). (Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall be opened.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 5:8–9; JOHN 7:37–39; LUKE 11:14"> Then a crooked man shall skip as an hart, and the tongue of dumb men shall be opened, (or Then the lame shall skip like a hart, and the tongues of the dumb shall be loosened, or shall be freed); for why waters be broken out in (the) desert, and streams in (the) wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:18; ISAIAH 49:10; JOHN 4:14"> And that that was dry, is made into a pond, and the thirsty is made into wells of waters. &#91;The&#93; Greenness of &#91;the&#93; reed, and of &#91;the&#93; spire shall grow in &#91;the&#93; dens, in which dwelled dragons before (or where jackals lived before).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:13–14; ISAIAH 42:16; JOHN 14:6"> And a path and a way shall be there, and it shall be called an holy way, (and) he that is defouled shall not pass thereby; and this shall be a straight way to you, so that fools err not thereby. (And a path and a way shall be there, and it shall be called a holy way, and he who is defiled shall not go along it; yea, it shall be a strait, or a narrow, way for you, but fools cannot stray there.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 62:12; HOSEA 2:18; LEVITICUS 26:6"> A lion shall not be there, and an evil beast shall not ascend thereby, neither shall be found there. And they shall go, that be delivered, (or But they who be rescued, shall go there);</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 30:19; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:4; ISAIAH 25:8"> and (those) again-bought of the Lord; and they shall be converted, and shall come into Zion with praising, (or and those bought back, or redeemed, by the Lord, shall return, and shall come into Zion with praising); and everlasting gladness shall be on the head(s) of them; they shall have joy and gladness, and sorrow and wailing shall flee away.</span>
