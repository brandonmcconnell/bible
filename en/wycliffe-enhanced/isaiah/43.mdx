<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 100:3; ISAIAH 43:7; ZECHARIAH 13:9"> And now the Lord God, making of nought thee, Jacob, and forming thee, Israel, saith these things, Do not thou dread, for I again-bought thee, and I called thee by thy name; thou art my servant. (And now the Lord God, making thee, Jacob, out of nothing, and forming thee, Israel, saith these things, Do not thou fear, for I have ransomed, or redeemed, thee, and I have called thee by thy name; thou art my servant.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:10; JOSHUA 1:9; PSALMS 66:12"> When thou shalt go by waters, I shall be with thee, and floods shall not cover thee; when thou shalt go in fire, thou shalt not be burnt, and flame shall not burn in thee (or and the flame shall not burn thee).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 60:16; ISAIAH 45:15; 2ND CHRONICLES 14:9–14"> For I am thy Lord God, the Holy of Israel, thy saviour. I gave (for) thy mercy Egypt; (yea, also) Ethiopia, and Seba, for thee. (For I am the Lord thy God, the Holy One of Israel, thy Saviour. I gave up Egypt for thy ransom, yea, also Ethiopia, and Seba, for thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:6–8; 1ST PETER 2:9; JEREMIAH 31:3"> Since thou art made honourable, and glorious in mine eyes; I (have) loved thee, and I shall give (up) men for thee, and peoples for thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 30:3; ISAIAH 49:12; LUKE 13:29"> Do not thou dread, for I am with thee; I shall bring thy seed from the east, and I shall gather thee together from the west. (Do not thou fear, for I am with thee; I shall bring thy descendants from the east, and I shall gather thee together from the west.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 6:17–18; JEREMIAH 3:14; JEREMIAH 3:18–19"> I shall say to the north, Give thou (them up), and to the south, Do not thou forbid (them); bring thou my sons from afar, and my daughters from the last parts of &#91;the&#93; earth (or and my daughters from the ends of the earth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:10; ISAIAH 43:21; EPHESIANS 1:12"> And each that calleth my name to help, into my glory I made him of nought; I formed him, and made him. (Yea, each person who is called by my name, for I made them out of nothing, for my glory; I formed them, and I made them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 6:9; EZEKIEL 12:2; ISAIAH 42:18–20"> Lead thou forth the blind people, and having eyes; the deaf people, and ears be to it. (Lead thou forth the people who have eyes, but be blind; the people who have ears, but be deaf.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 46:10; ISAIAH 44:7–9; ISAIAH 41:21–26"> All heathen men be gathered together, and lineages be gathered together. Who among you, who shall tell this, and shall make you to hear those things, that be the first? give they &#91;the&#93; witnesses of them, and be they justified, and hear they, and say, (That is the truth). (All the heathen be gathered together, and the tribes be gathered together. Who among you, who shall tell out this, and shall help us to understand the former things? bring they in their witnesses, and be they justified, or proven right, and listen they, and say, That is the truth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:4; ISAIAH 45:6; ISAIAH 46:8–9"> Verily ye be my witnesses, saith the Lord, and my servant(s), whom I chose; that ye know, and believe to me, and understand, for I myself am; before me is no God former, and after me shall none be. (Truly ye be my witnesses, saith the Lord, and my servants, whom I chose; so that ye would know me, and believe me, and understand, that I am he; before me no God was formed, and there shall be no one after me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HOSEA 13:4; ISAIAH 44:6; ISAIAH 45:21–22"> I am, I am the Lord, and without me is no saviour. (I am, yea, I am the Lord, and there is no other saviour besides me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:12; PSALMS 81:9–10; ISAIAH 43:10"> I told, and saved; I made hearing, and none alien God was among you, (or I have told it out, and I have saved; I have made it heard, when there was no foreign, or strange, god among you). Ye be my witnesses, saith the Lord; and I am God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 14:27; ISAIAH 46:10; PSALMS 90:2"> from the beginning, I myself am, and none there is that delivereth from mine hand; I shall work, and who shall destroy it? (yea, from the beginning, I am he, and there is no one who taketh anything from my hand; I shall do my work, and who shall be able to destroy it?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:6; ISAIAH 23:13; JEREMIAH 51:24"> The Lord, your again-buyer, the Holy of Israel, saith these things, For you I sent out into Babylon, and I drew down all bars, and &#91;the&#93; Chaldees having glory in their ships. (The Lord, your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel, saith these things, For your sakes I have sent to Babylon, and I have brought down all their bars, or all their gates, and the Chaldeans having glory in their ships.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:3; MATTHEW 25:34; ISAIAH 48:17"> I am the Lord, your Holy, your king, making Israel of nought. (I am the Lord, your Holy One, your King, making Israel out of nothing.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 51:10; ISAIAH 43:2; PSALMS 77:19"> The Lord saith these things, that gave &#91;a&#93; way in the sea, and a path in running waters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EXODUS 14:4–9; ISAIAH 1:31; EZEKIEL 38:8–18"> which led out a cart, and horse, a company, and (a) strong man; they slept together, neither they shall rise again; they be all-broken as flax, and be quenched (like a wick). (who led out chariots, and horses, yea, a company of strong men; they all sleep now, or be dead, and they shall never rise again; they be all-broken like flax, and be quenched, or snuffed out, like a wick.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 65:17; 2ND CORINTHIANS 3:10; 1ST CHRONICLES 16:12"> Think ye not on the former things, and behold ye not (on the) old things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 48:6; ISAIAH 42:9; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:17"> Lo! I make new things, and now those shall begin to be made; soothly ye shall know them. I shall set &#91;a&#93; way in (the) desert, and floods in a land without (a) way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:17; ISAIAH 48:21; ISAIAH 49:10"> And a beast of the field shall glorify me, (the) dragons and ostriches shall glorify me; for I gave waters in desert, and floods in the land without (a) way, that I should give drink to my people, to my chosen people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:9; HEBREWS 13:15; PSALMS 102:18"> I formed this people to me, it shall tell my praising. (I formed this people for myself, and they shall tell out my praises.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MICAH 6:3; MALACHI 3:14; MALACHI 1:13"> Jacob, thou calledest not me to help; and thou, Israel, travailedest not for me. (Yet Jacob, thou hast not called upon me for help; yea, Israel, thou hast not laboured for me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="AMOS 5:25; ZECHARIAH 7:5–6; MALACHI 3:8"> Thou offeredest not to me the ram of thy burnt sacrifice, and thou glorifiedest not me with thy slain sacrifices. I made not thee to serve in offering, neither I gave to thee travail in incense. (Thou hast not offered me the ram of thy burnt sacrifice, and thou hast not glorified me with thy slain sacrifices. And I did not ask thee to serve me with offerings, nor did I ask thee to offer incense to me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ISAIAH 7:13; JEREMIAH 6:20; ISAIAH 1:14"> Thou boughtest not to me sweet smelling spicery for silver, and thou filledest not me with &#91;the&#93; fatness of thy slain sacrifices; nevertheless thou madest me to serve in thy sins, thou gavest travail to me in thy wickednesses. (Thou hast not brought me sweet smelling spices with thy silver, and thou hast not fulfilled, or satisfied, me, or and thou hast not filled me with the fatness of thy slain sacrifices; nevertheless thou hast burdened me with thy sins, and thou hast given me trials and tribulation with thy wickednesses.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="HEBREWS 8:12; MICAH 7:18–19; HEBREWS 10:17"> I am, I myself am, that do away thy wickednesses for me, and I shall not have mind on thy sins. (But I am, yea, I am he who do away thy wickednesses for my own sake, and I shall not remember thy sins.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:18; ISAIAH 43:9; ROMANS 8:33"> Bring me again into mind, and be we deemed together; tell thou, if thou hast anything, that thou be justified. (Bring me again into mind, and be we judged together, or let us argue it out; tell thou it out, if thou hast anything, by which thou can be justified.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ISAIAH 28:7; JEREMIAH 5:31; EZEKIEL 22:25–28"> Thy first father sinned, and thine interpreters trespassed against me. (Thy first father sinned, and thy expounders trespassed against me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JEREMIAH 24:9; ISAIAH 65:15; ZECHARIAH 8:13"> And I made foul (the) holy princes, and I gave Jacob to death, and Israel into blasphemy. (And I defiled the princes of the sanctuary, and I gave Jacob unto death, and Israel unto reproach./And thy leaders defiled my sanctuary, and so I gave Jacob unto death, and Israel unto reproach.)</span>
