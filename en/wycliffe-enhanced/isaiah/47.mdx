<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 20:4; MATTHEW 24:41; EXODUS 11:5"> Take thou a quernstone, and grind thou meal; make thou naked thy filth(hood), discover the shoulder, show the hips, pass thou &#91;over the&#93; floods. (Take thou a millstone, and grind thou some meal with it; make thou thyself naked, yea, uncover the shoulder, show the hips, and cross thou over the rivers.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NAHUM 3:5; JEREMIAH 51:20–24; JEREMIAH 50:27–28"> Thy shame shall be showed, and thy shame shall be seen; I shall take vengeance, and no man shall against-stand me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:6; ISAIAH 43:14; ISAIAH 49:26"> Our again-buyer, the Lord of hosts is his name, the Holy of Israel. (Our Redeemer, the Lord of hosts is his name, the Holy One of Israel.)</span>

(Daughter of the Chaldeans, sit thou, be thou silent, and enter into the darkness, or into the shadows, for thou shalt no more be called the lady, or the queen, of many kingdoms.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 28:9; ZECHARIAH 1:15; DEUTERONOMY 28:50"> I was wroth on my people, I defouled mine heritage, and I gave them in thine hand, and thou settedest not mercies to them; thou madest grievous the yoke greatly on an eld man, (I was angry with my people, I defiled my inheritance, and I gave them into thy hands, or into thy power, and thou gavest no mercy to them; thou madest the yoke greatly grievous upon an old man,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:29; ISAIAH 47:5; JEREMIAH 5:31"> and thou saidest, Without end I shall be &#91;a&#93; lady, (or and thou saidest, I shall be a lady forever); thou puttedest not these things on thine heart, neither thou bethoughtest on thy last thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 2:15; ISAIAH 32:9; ISAIAH 47:10"> And now, thou delicate, and dwelling trustily, hear these things, which sayest in thine heart, I am, and besides me there is no more; I shall not sit &#91;a&#93; widow, and I shall not know barrenness. (And now, O delicate one, and trustily dwelling, hear ye these things, thou who sayest in thy heart, Here I am, and there is no other besides me; I shall never be a widow, and I shall never know barrenness.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 73:19; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:3; ISAIAH 47:12–13"> These two things, barrenness and widowhood, shall come to thee suddenly in one day; all things came on thee for the multitude of thy witchcrafts, and for the great hardness of thine enchanters, either tregetours. (But these two things, barrenness and widowhood, shall suddenly come upon thee in a single day; they shall come upon thee for the multitude of thy witchcrafts, and for the great willfulness of thy enchanters, or of thy conjurers.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 5:21; ISAIAH 29:15; ROMANS 1:22"> And (for) thou haddest trust in thy malice, and saidest, None is that seeth me; this, thy wisdom and thy knowing, (hath) deceived thee; and thou saidest in thine heart, I am, and besides me there is none other, (or I am, and there is no one else besides me).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:3; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:9–10; DANIEL 5:25–30"> Evil shall come &#91;up&#93; on thee, and thou shalt not know the beginning thereof; and mischief shall fall &#91;up&#93; on thee, which thou shalt not be able to cleanse; wretchedness which thou knowest not, shall come &#91;up&#93; on thee suddenly, (or and such wretchedness, which thou knowest not, shall suddenly come upon thee).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:8–12; NAHUM 3:4; JEREMIAH 2:28"> Stand thou with thine enchanters, and with the multitude of thy witches, in which thou travailedest from thy youth; if in hap they profit anything to thee, either if thou mayest be made the stronger. (Stand thou with thy enchanters, and with the multitude of thy witches, with whom thou hast laboured from thy youth; if perhaps they profit anything to thee, or if thou mayest be made stronger, or more awesome.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:25; DANIEL 2:2–10; EZEKIEL 24:12"> Thou failedest in the multitude of thy counsels; the false diviners of heaven stand, and save thee, which beheld stars, and numbered months, that they should tell by them things to coming to thee. (Thou hast failed, despite the multitude of thy advice; let the false diviners of the heavens stand up, and save thee, they who looked at the stars, and numbered the months, so that they could tell by them the things that would come to thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MALACHI 4:1; NAHUM 1:10; EZEKIEL 15:7"> Lo! they be made as stubble, the fire hath burnt them; they shall not deliver their life from the power of flame; coals be not, by which they shall be warmed, neither fire, that they sit at it. (Behold! they shall be made like stubble, and the fire shall burn them up; they shall not be able to save, (or to rescue), their own lives from the power of the flame; there shall not be coals by which they shall be warmed, nor a fire, that they can sit before.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 56:11; EZEKIEL 27:12–25; JEREMIAH 51:6–9"> So those things be made to thee in which ever thou travailedest; thy merchants from thy youth erred, each man in his way; none is, that shall save thee. (So shall these enchanters be to thee, they with whom thou hast laboured all thy life; they have wandered off, each in his own way, and there is no one, who can save thee.)</span>
