<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST KINGS 15:26; 1ST KINGS 14:16; 1ST KINGS 12:28"> in his sins which he sinned, doing evil before the Lord, and going in the way of Jeroboam, and in his sins, by which he made Israel to do sin.</span>

(And he bought the hill of Samaria from Shemer, for two talents of silver, and built a city on that hill; and he called the name of the city, which he built, Samaria, after the name of Shemer, the former lord of that hill.)</span>

(And he went in all the ways of Jeroboam, the son of Nebat, and in his sins, by which he made Israel to do sin, so that they stirred the Lord God of Israel to anger with their worthless idols.)</span>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Omri slept with his fathers, and was buried in Samaria; and Ahab, his son, reigned for him.</span>

(Then Ahab, the son of Omri, began to reign upon Israel in the thirty-eighth year of Asa, the king of Judah; and Ahab, the son of Omri, reigned upon Israel, in Samaria, for twenty-two years.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1ST KINGS 16:25; 1ST KINGS 21:25; 1ST KINGS 14:9"> And Ahab, the son of Omri, did evil in the sight of the Lord, over all men that were before him; (And Ahab, the son of Omri, did evil before the Lord, more than all the kings who were before him;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2ND KI 17:16; 2ND KI 10:18; JUDGES 18:7"> and it sufficed not to him that he went in the sins of Jeroboam, the son of Nebat, furthermore and he wedded a wife, Jezebel, the daughter of Ethbaal, king of Sidonians; and he went and served Baal, and worshipped him. (and it was not sufficient for him that he merely went in the sins of Jeroboam, the son of Nebat, but moreover he wedded Jezebel for a wife, Ethbaal’s daughter, the king of the Sidonians; and he went and served Baal, and worshipped him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2ND KI 10:21; 2ND KI 10:26–27"> And he set up an altar to Baal in the temple of Baal, which he had builded in Samaria,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2ND KI 13:6; 1ST KINGS 21:25; 2ND KI 21:3"> and he planted a maumet wood; and Ahab added to (that) in his work, and stirred to ire the Lord God of Israel, more than all &#91;the&#93; kings of Israel that were before him. (and he planted an idol grove or and he put up a sacred pole; and Ahab did more with his deeds, to stir the Lord God of Israel to anger, than all the kings of Israel who were before him.)</span>
