</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>

(And he answered, With fervent love, that is, with all of my heart, I have fervently loved for the Lord God of hosts; but the Israelites have deserted the covenant of the Lord; they have destroyed thy altars, and killed thy prophets with the sword; and I alone am left, and now they seek my life, so that they can do me away.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:4; PSALMS 50:3; JOB 38:1"> And he said to Elijah, Go thou out, and stand in the hill, before the Lord. And after the wind there was a great shaking; but the Lord was not in that shaking.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 4:16; ZECHARIAH 4:6; DEUTERONOMY 4:33"> And after the stirring is a fire; not in the fire is the Lord. And after the fire there was a hissing of the wind, as if softly breathing; and there was the Lord or and the Lord was there.)</span>
</div>

And a voice spake to him, and said, Elijah, what doest thou here?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST KINGS 19:9–10; JEREMIAH 22:9; ISAIAH 62:1"> And he answered, With fervent love I have loved fervently, for the Lord God of hosts; for the sons of Israel have forsaken thy covenant; they have destroyed thine altars, and they have killed with (the) sword thy prophets; and I am left alone, and they seek my life, that they do it away. (And he answered, and said, With fervent love I have fervently loved for the Lord God of hosts; but the Israelites have deserted thy covenant; they have destroyed thy altars, and they have killed thy prophets with the sword; and I alone am left, and now they seek my life, so that they can do me away.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 45:1; JEREMIAH 1:10; 2ND KI 8:7–15"> And the Lord said to him, Go, and turn again into thy way, by the desert, into Damascus, (or And the Lord said to him, Go, and return on the way, by the wilderness, to Damascus); and when thou shalt come thither, thou shalt anoint Hazael king upon Syria;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2ND KI 9:1–14; 1ST KINGS 19:19–21; 2ND KI 2:9"> and thou shalt anoint (unto) king upon Israel, Jehu, the son of Nimshi; and thou shalt anoint a prophet for thee, Elisha, the son of Shaphat, that is of Abelmeholah. (and thou shalt anoint Jehu, the son of Nimshi, to be king upon Israel; and thou shalt anoint Elisha, the son of Shaphat, of Abelmeholah, to be the prophet in thy place.)</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND KI 13:22; 2ND KI 8:12; 2ND KI 13:3"> And it shall be, whoever shall flee the sword of Hazael, Jehu shall slay him; and whoever shall flee the sword of Jehu, Elisha shall slay him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ROMANS 11:4–5; ISAIAH 1:9; ISAIAH 10:20–22"> And I shall leave to me in Israel seven thousand of men, of which the knees be not bowed before Baal, and each mouth that worshipped not him, and kissed not his hand. (But I shall leave for me seven thousand men in Israel, who have not bowed their knees before Baal, and whose mouths have not worshipped him, nor kissed his hand.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2ND KI 2:8; 2ND KI 2:13–14; 1ST SAMUEL 28:14"> Therefore Elijah went forth from thence, and found Elisha, the son of Shaphat, earing in twelve yokes of oxen; and he was one in the twelve yokes of oxen, earing. And when Elijah had come to him, Elijah casted his mantle upon him. (And so Elijah went forth from there, and found Elisha, the son of Shaphat, plowing with twelve yokes of oxen; and he was plowing alongside the twelfth yoke of them. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:21–22; LUKE 9:61–62; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:37"> And he ran anon after Elijah, when the oxen were left, and said, I pray thee, kiss I my father and my mother, and so I shall follow thee. And Elijah said to him, Go thou, and turn again, for I have done to thee that that was mine (to do). (And leaving the oxen behind, Elisha ran at once after Elijah, and said, I pray thee, let me kiss my father and my mother good-bye, and then I shall follow thee. And Elijah said to him, Go thou, and return, for I have done to thee what was mine to do.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 24:22; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:11; PHILEMON 1:13"> Soothly he turned again from Elijah, and took twain oxen, and killed them; and with the plow of the oxen he seethed the flesh, and gave (the meat) to the people, and they ate; and he rose (or and then he rose up), and went, and followed Elijah, and ministered to him.</span>
