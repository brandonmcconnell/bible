<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 19:8; 2ND SAMUEL 12:6; PROVERBS 6:31"> If any man stealeth a sheep, or (an) ox, and slayeth, or selleth (it), he shall restore five oxen for one ox, and four sheep for one sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:43; MATTHEW 6:19–20; NUMBERS 35:27"> And if a night thief breaking (into) an house, either undermining (it), is found (out), and he taken is (made) dead by a wound, or hurt, (or and when he is caught, he dieth from a wound), the smiter shall not be guilty of his blood, or death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 21:2; ISAIAH 50:1; MATTHEW 18:25"> that if he did this when the sun was risen, he did manslaying, and he shall die, (or but if the lord of the house did this when the sun was up, he hath done manslaughter, and he shall be put to death). If a thief have not that, that he shall yield for &#91;the&#93; theft, he shall be sold (to make recompense);</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 22:7; EXODUS 22:1; EXODUS 22:9"> if that thing that he stole, is found quick at him, either ox, either ass, either sheep, he shall restore the double. (if what he stole, is found alive with him, either an ox, or a donkey, or a sheep, he shall restore double what he stole.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 22:3; EXODUS 22:12; JOB 20:18"> If a man harmeth a field, or a vinery, and suffereth his beast, that it waste other men’s things, (or If anyone harmeth a field, or a vineyard, by allowing his beast to destroy another person’s things), he shall restore for the value of &#91;the&#93; harm (done), (with) the best thing(s)(of) whatever he hath in his (own) field, either in his (own) vinery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 22:9; EXODUS 21:33–34; 2ND SAMUEL 14:30–31"> If fire goeth out, and findeth, or burneth, ears of corn, and catcheth heaps of corn, or corns standing in (the) fields, he that kindled the fire shall yield (for) the harm (or he who kindled the fire shall make recompense for the harm done).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 22:4; PROVERBS 6:30–31; JEREMIAH 2:26"> If a man betaketh into keeping money to a friend, or a vessel, and it is taken away by theft from him that received it, if the thief is found, he shall restore the double. (If anyone taketh money, or a vessel, to a friend for safe keeping, and it is taken away by theft from him who received it, if the thief is found, he shall restore double what he stole.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 22:28; DEUTERONOMY 16:18; EXODUS 21:6"> (But) If the thief is hid(den), or unknown, the lord of the house that received that good shall be brought to the gods, that is, to (the) judges, and he shall swear, that he held not forth his hand into his neighbour’s thing, to defraud, (or and he shall swear, that he did not put forth his hand to his neighbour’s thing, to defraud him);</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 25:1; EXODUS 22:4; NUMBERS 5:6–7"> as well in ox, as in ass, and in sheep, and in cloth; and in whatever thing may bring in harm, the cause of ever either shall come to the judges, and if they deem him guilty, he shall restore the double to his neighbour. (and with any ox, or donkey, or sheep, or cloak, or whatever thing which may be claimed by two people, the case shall come to the judges, and whom they judge guilty, he shall restore double to his neighbour.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKE 12:48; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:12; LUKE 16:11"> If any man betaketh to his neighbour ox, ass, sheep, and all work beast to keeping, and it is dead, or is made feeble, or is taken of enemies, and no man seeth this, (If anyone taketh an ox, a donkey, a sheep, or any work beast, to his neighbour for safe keeping, and it dieth, or is hurt, or is taken by enemies, but no one seeth this,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:16; LEVITICUS 5:1; 1ST KINGS 2:42–43"> an oath shall be in the midst, that he held not forth his hand to the impairing of his neighbour’s thing; and the lord that owned that good shall receive his oath, and he to whom it was taken shall not be compelled to yield, or restore it. (he shall make an oath before all, that he did not put forth his hand to the impairment of his neighbour’s thing; and the lord who owned that thing shall receive his oath, and then he to whom the thing was given shall not be compelled to yield any recompense for it, or to restore it.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 31:39; EXODUS 22:7"> That if it is taken away by theft, he shall restore the harm to the lord; (But if it is taken away by theft, he shall yield recompense to its lord for it;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="NAHUM 2:12; MICAH 5:8; EZEKIEL 4:14"> if it is eaten of a beast (or and if it is eaten by a wild beast), he shall bring to the lord that that is slain, and he shall not (have to) restore it otherwise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:19–20; EXODUS 21:34; DEUTERONOMY 15:2"> He that asketh of his neighbour anything of these foresaid things by borrowing, and it is enfeebled, either dead, while the lord thereof is not present, he shall be constrained to yield (for) it; (He who asketh to borrow from his neighbour any of these foresaid things, and it is enfeebled, or dead, while its lord is not present, he shall be compelled to yield recompense for it;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 8:10"> that if the lord is in presence, he shall not restore it, mostly if it came hired, that is, if to hire he took it, for meed of his work. (but if its lord was present, he shall not have to restore it, and if it was hired, that is, if he took it to hire, only the wages for its work shall be due.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:28–29; GENESIS 34:2–4"> If a man deceiveth a virgin not yet wedded, and sleepeth with her, he shall give dower to her (or he shall give her a dowry), and shall have her to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 34:12; 1ST SAMUEL 18:25; DEUTERONOMY 22:29"> If the father of the virgin will not give her to him, he shall (still) give (the) money, by the manner of dower (or in the manner of a dowry), which virgins were wont to take.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:27; DEUTERONOMY 18:10–11; 1ST SAMUEL 28:3"> Thou shalt not suffer witches to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:23; DEUTERONOMY 27:21; LEVITICUS 20:15–16"> He that doeth lechery with a beast, die he by death (or shall be put to death).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:2–5; NUMBERS 25:7–8; DEUTERONOMY 18:20"> He that offereth to (any) gods, except to the Lord alone, be he slain (or shall be put to death).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 23:9; DEUTERONOMY 10:19; LEVITICUS 19:33"> Thou shalt not make sorrowful a comeling, neither thou shalt torment him; for also ye were comelings in the land of Egypt (or for ye were also newcomers in the land of Egypt).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 24:17; ISAIAH 1:17; ISAIAH 1:23"> Ye shall not harm a widow, and a fatherless or a motherless child. (Ye shall not harm a widow, or a fatherless or a motherless child.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 18:6; PSALMS 10:17–18; LUKE 18:7"> If ye hurt them, they shall cry to me, and I shall hear the cry of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 109:9; JEREMIAH 15:8; PSALMS 76:7"> and my great vengeance shall have indignation on you, and I shall smite you with sword (or and I shall strike you down with a sword), and your wives shall be widows, and your sons shall be fatherless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 15:5; LEVITICUS 25:35–37; DEUTERONOMY 23:19–20"> If thou givest money to loan to my poor people, that dwelleth with thee, thou shalt not constrain him as an extortioner doeth, neither thou shalt oppress him by usuries. (If thou lendest money to anyone of my poor people, who liveth with thee, thou shalt not compel him like an extortioner doeth, nor shalt thou oppress him with usury.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:16; AMOS 2:8; DEUTERONOMY 24:6"> If thou takest of thy neighbour a cloth to wed (or If thou takest a cloak from thy neighbour for a pledge), thou shalt yield it (back) to him before the going down of the sun;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EXODUS 34:6; 2ND CHRONICLES 30:9; PSALMS 136:10–11"> for that alone is the clothing of his flesh, with which he is covered, neither he hath another, in which he shall sleep; if he crieth to me, I shall hear him; for I am merciful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:5; ECCLESIASTES 10:20; EXODUS 22:8–9"> Thou shalt not backbite &#91;the&#93; gods, that is, (the) priests, or (the) judges, and thou shalt not curse the prince of thy people (or and thou shalt not curse the leaders of thy people).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EXODUS 23:16; EXODUS 13:2; JAMES 1:18"> Thou shalt not tarry to offer to the Lord thy tithes, and thy first fruits. Thou shalt give to me the first begotten of thy sons (or Thou shalt give me thy first-born sons);</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:27; DEUTERONOMY 15:19; EXODUS 13:12"> also of &#91;thine&#93; oxen, and of (thy) sheep, thou shalt do in like manner; seven days be he with his mother, in the eighth day thou shalt yield him to me. (and thou shalt do likewise with thy oxen, and with thy sheep; for seven days let him be with his mother, then on the eighth day thou shalt yield him to me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EZEKIEL 4:14; LEVITICUS 19:2; DEUTERONOMY 14:21"> Ye shall be holy men to me; ye shall not eat the flesh that is before-tasted of (other) beasts, but ye shall cast it forth to hounds. (Ye shall be holy before me; ye shall not eat the flesh of that which is killed by another beast, but ye shall throw it to the hounds.)</span>
