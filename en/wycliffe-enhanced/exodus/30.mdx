<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 25:11; EXODUS 25:24"> And thou shalt clothe it with cleanest gold, as well the roof thereof, that is, the higher part, as the walls, and &#91;the&#93; corners by compass thereof; and thou shalt make to the altar a little golden crown by compass, (And thou shalt cover it with pure gold, the top of it, and its walls, and the horns on its corners; and thou shalt put a gold band all around the altar,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 25:27; EXODUS 26:29; EXODUS 27:7"> and two golden circles under the crown by all sides, that bars be put into those rings, and so the altar be borne. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 25:13; EXODUS 25:27"> Also thou shalt make the bars of the wood of shittim, and thou shalt overgild them; (And thou shalt make the bars out of shittim wood, or acacia, and thou shalt gild them with gold;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 25:21–22; EXODUS 30:36; LEVITICUS 16:13"> and thou shalt set the altar against the veil, that hangeth before the ark of witnessing, (and) before the propitiatory, with which the witnessing is covered, where I shall speak to thee. (and thou shalt put the altar outside the Veil, that hangeth in front of the Ark of the Witnessing, and in front of the propitiatory, that is, the mercy seat, or the lid, that covereth the Ark of the Witnessing, where I shall speak to thee.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 2:28; 1ST CHRONICLES 23:13; EXODUS 27:20–21"> And Aaron shall burn thereon incense smelling sweetly early (or And early each day, Aaron shall burn sweet smelling incense on it); when he shall array the lanterns, he shall burn it;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:24; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:17; HEBREWS 7:25"> and when he setteth the lanterns at eventide, he shall burn everlasting incense before the Lord, into your generations. (and when he setteth the lanterns in the evening, he shall also burn everlasting incense on it before the Lord, and so shall all your generations to come.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:1"> Ye shall not offer thereon incense of (any) other making, neither offering, nor slain sacrifice, neither ye shall offer flowing offerings thereon (nor shall ye offer any wine offerings on it).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 16:18; HEBREWS 9:22–23; LEVITICUS 16:29–30"> And Aaron shall pray on the horns thereof once by the year, in the blood which is offered for sin, and he shall please (the Lord) thereon in your generations; it shall be the holy of holy things to the Lord. (And once every year Aaron shall put blood on its horns, from the yearly sin offering, and for all your generations to come, the priest shall cleanse, or shall purify, the altar in this way; for it is most holy to the Lord.)</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 31:50; 1ST CHRONICLES 27:24; EXODUS 38:25–26"> When thou shalt take the sum of the sons of Israel, all by themselves shall give by the number (the) price for their souls to the Lord, and vengeance shall not be in them, when they be numbered. (When thou shalt take the sum of the sons of Israel, that is, when thou shalt register them, each one shall give a ransom for his life to the Lord, and so vengeance shall not come upon them, as they be listed.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:25; NUMBERS 3:47; EZEKIEL 45:12"> Soothly each that passeth to the name(d), that is, of twenty years, shall give this price, (or And each one who passeth over to the named, that is, he who is twenty years of age, or older, shall pay this amount of money), half a shekel by the measure of the temple; a shekel hath twenty half-pence; the half part of a shekel shall be offered to the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:20; NUMBERS 1:18; NUMBERS 1:3"> He that is had in the number, from twenty years and above, shall give this price; (He that is had in the number, from twenty years of age and older, shall pay this amount of money;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 31:50; LEVITICUS 17:11; EXODUS 30:12"> a rich man shall not add to the half of a shekel, and a poor man shall nothing abate (or and a poor man shall not pay less).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 10:32–33; NUMBERS 16:40; EXODUS 38:25–31"> And thou shalt betake into the uses of the tabernacle of witnessing the money taken, which is gathered of the sons of Israel, that it be a mind of them before the Lord, and he shall be merciful to their souls. (And thou shalt use the money, which is gathered from the sons of Israel, for the needs of the Tabernacle of the Witnessing; and it shall be a reminder of them to the Lord, and so I shall be merciful to their souls.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the Lord spake to Moses, and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 40:7; EXODUS 38:8; 1ST KINGS 7:23"> Also thou shalt make a great vessel of brass with his foundament, to wash in, and thou shalt set it betwixt the tabernacle of witnessing and the altar; and when water is put therein, (And thou shalt make a great bronze vessel, with a bronze foundation, or a bronze base, to wash in, and thou shalt set it between the Tabernacle of the Witnessing and the altar; and when water is put in it,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 52:11; EXODUS 40:31–32; PSALMS 26:6"> Aaron and his sons shall wash therein their hands and their feet,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:1–3; HEBREWS 12:28–29; 1ST CHRONICLES 13:10"> when they shall enter into the tabernacle of witnessing, and when they shall nigh to the altar, that they offer therein incense to the Lord, lest peradventure they die; (whenever they shall go into the Tabernacle of the Witnessing, or when they shall approach the altar to offer incense on it to the Lord, otherwise they shall die or they shall be killed;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 28:43; EXODUS 27:21"> it shall be a lawful thing everlasting to him and to his seed by successions. (this shall be an everlasting law for him and for his descendants by succession.)</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:19; SONG OF SONGS 4:14; EZEKIEL 27:22"> Take to thee sweet smelling spiceries, of the first and chosen myrrh, (equal to the weight of) five hundred shekels; and of canel the half (or and half as much canel), that is, (the weight of) two hundred and fifty shekels; &#91;and&#93; in like manner of calamus, that is, a sweet smelling tree, small and full of knots, (the weight of) two hundred and fifty shekels;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 45:8; EXODUS 29:40; NUMBERS 3:47"> also of cassia five hundred shekels, in the weight of &#91;the&#93; saintuary; the oil of olives trees, the measure of hin; (and of cassia, the weight of five hundred shekels, all by the measure of the sanctuary; and the oil of olives trees, the measure of a hin, that is, two pounds;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 37:29; 1ST CHRONICLES 9:30; EXODUS 40:9"> and thou shalt make the holy oil of anointing, an ointment made by the craft of an ointment maker.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="NUMBERS 7:1; 1ST JOHN 2:20; ISAIAH 61:1"> And thou shalt anoint thereof the tabernacle of witnessing, and the ark of the testament, (And thou shalt anoint with it the Tabernacle of the Witnessing, and the Ark of the Witnessing,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and the board with his vessels, &#91;and&#93; the candlestick, and the purtenances thereof, (and) the altars of incense, (and the table, and its vessels, and the lampstand, and its purtenances, and the altar of incense,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and of burnt sacrifice, and all the purtenance, that pertaineth to the adorning of those things. (and the altar of burnt sacrifice, and the purtenances of the altars, and the great washing vessel, and its foundation, or its base.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:19; EXODUS 29:37; LEVITICUS 6:18"> And thou shalt hallow all (these) things, and they shall be the holy of holy things; he that shall touch those, shall be hallowed beforehand. (And thou shalt hallow all these things, and they shall be most holy; anyone who is unclean who toucheth these things, shall be harmed.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LEVITICUS 8:12; EXODUS 28:3; LEVITICUS 8:30"> Thou shalt anoint Aaron, and his sons, and thou shalt hallow them, that they be set in priesthood to me (or so that they can serve as my priests).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EXODUS 37:29; LEVITICUS 21:10; PSALMS 89:20"> And thou shalt say to the sons of Israel, This oil of anointing shall be holy to me into your generations. </div>

<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EXODUS 30:25; EXODUS 30:37–38; LEVITICUS 21:10"> The flesh of (a) man shall not be anointed thereof, and by the making thereof ye shall not make another such oil, for it is hallowed, and it shall be holy to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="EXODUS 30:38; GENESIS 17:14; EXODUS 12:15"> Whatever man maketh such oil, and giveth thereof to an alien, he shall be put out of his people. (Whoever maketh such an oil, or giveth it to anyone, shall be put out, or shall be cut off, from his people.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="EXODUS 37:29; EXODUS 25:6; EXODUS 30:23"> Forsooth the Lord said to Moses, Take to thee sweet smelling spiceries, stacte, and onycha, galbanum of good odour, and most pure (frank)incense, all these shall be of even weight (or and they all shall be of equal weight).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:13; EXODUS 30:25; JOHN 12:3"> And thou shalt make (the) incense, made by the craft of an ointment maker, meddled together diligently (or diligently mixed together), and pure, and most worthy of hallowing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="EXODUS 25:22; EXODUS 30:6; EXODUS 16:34"> And when thou hast pounded all these things into (the) smallest powder, thou shalt put thereof before the tabernacle of witnessing, in which place I shall appear to thee; the made incense shall be to you the holy of holy things. (And when thou hast pounded all these things into the smallest powder, thou shalt sprinkle it in front of the Ark of the Witnessing in the Tabernacle, where I shall appear to thee; this incense shall be most holy to you.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:3; EXODUS 29:37; EXODUS 30:32–33"> Ye shall not make such a making into your &#91;own&#93; uses, for it is holy to the Lord. (Ye shall not make such incense for your own uses, for it is holy to the Lord.)</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="EXODUS 30:33"> Whatever man maketh like thing to it, that he use the odour thereof, he shall perish from his people.
