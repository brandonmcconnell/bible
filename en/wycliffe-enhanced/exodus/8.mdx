<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 3:18; EXODUS 3:12; EXODUS 5:1"> Also the Lord said to Moses, Enter thou to Pharaoh, and thou shalt say to him, The Lord saith these things, Deliver thou my people, that it make sacrifice to me; (And then the Lord said to Moses, Go to Pharaoh, and thou shalt say to him, The Lord saith these things, Let my people go, so they can worship me;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 78:45; EXODUS 9:2; EXODUS 7:14"> soothly if thou wilt not deliver (them), lo! I shall smite all thy terms, or coasts, with paddocks; (and if thou wilt not let them go, behold! I shall cover all thy land with frogs,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 105:30; EXODUS 12:34"> and the flood shall boil out paddocks, that shall go up, and enter into thine house, and into the closet of thy bed, and on thy bed, and into the house(s) of thy servants, and into thy people, and into thine ovens, and into the remnants of thy meats; (and the (Nile) River shall boil out frogs, that shall go up, and enter into thy house, and into thy bed-closet, and onto thy bed, and into thy servants’ houses, and onto thy people, and into thy ovens, and even onto thy food;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 19:11; DANIEL 4:37; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:22–23"> and the paddocks shall enter to thee, and to thy people, and to all thy servants. (and the frogs shall jump on thee, and on thy people, and on all thy servants.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 7:19"> And the Lord said to Moses, Say thou to Aaron, Hold forth thine hand on the floods, and on the streams, and on the marshes; and bring out paddocks on the land of Egypt. (And the Lord said to Moses, Say thou to Aaron, Stretch forth thy hand over the rivers, and the steams, and the marshes; and bring out frogs onto the land of Egypt.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 105:30; PSALMS 78:45; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:13"> And Aaron held forth the hand on the waters of Egypt; and paddocks went up, and covered the land of Egypt. (And Aaron stretched forth his hand over the waters of Egypt; and frogs went out, and covered the land of Egypt.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 7:11; EXODUS 7:22; DEUTERONOMY 13:1–3"> Forsooth and the witches did in like manner by their enchantments; and they brought forth paddocks on the land of Egypt. (And the witches did likewise with their enchantments; and they also brought forth frogs onto the land of Egypt.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 10:17; EXODUS 9:28; EXODUS 8:25–28"> Forsooth Pharaoh called (for) Moses and Aaron, and said to them, Pray ye the Lord, that he do away the paddocks from me, and from my people; and I shall deliver the people, that it make sacrifice to the Lord, (or and then I shall let the people go, so that they can offer sacrifices to the Lord).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST KINGS 18:25; ISAIAH 10:15; JUDGES 7:2"> And Moses said to Pharaoh, Ordain thou a time to me, when I shall pray for thee, and for thy servants, and for thy people, that the paddocks be driven away from thee, and from thine house(s), and from thy servants, and from thy people; and &#91;they&#93; dwell only in the flood. (And Moses said to Pharaoh, Ordain thou a time for me, when I shall pray for thee, and for thy servants, and for thy people, so that the frogs be driven away from thee, and from thy houses, and from thy servants, and from thy people; and so that they live only in the (Nile) River.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 46:9; PSALMS 86:8; 1ST CHRONICLES 17:20"> And he answered, Tomorrow. And Moses said, I shall do by thy word, (so) that thou know, that none is as our Lord God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 8:3; EXODUS 8:9"> and the paddocks shall go away from thee, and from thine house(s), and from thy children, and from thy servants, and from thy people; and they shall dwell only in the flood (or and they shall live only in the (Nile) River).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JAMES 5:16–18; EXODUS 8:30; EXODUS 10:18"> And Moses and Aaron went out from Pharaoh. And Moses cried to the Lord, for the promise of (the) paddocks, which he had said to Pharaoh, (or And Moses cried to the Lord, to take away the frogs which he had brought upon Pharaoh).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 34:10–12"> And the Lord did by the word of Moses; and the paddocks were dead from &#91;the&#93; houses, and from &#91;the&#93; towns, and from &#91;the&#93; fields; (And the Lord did what Moses asked; and the frogs were dead in the houses, and in the towns, and in the fields;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 8:24; EXODUS 7:21; JOEL 2:20"> and they gathered them into great heaps, and the land was rotten, or corrupted with stink. &#91;and they gathered them together into great heaps without number, and the earth stank.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 26:10; ECCLESIASTES 8:11; HEBREWS 3:8"> Soothly Pharaoh saw that rest was given, and he made grievous his heart, and he heard not them, as the Lord commanded. (But when Pharaoh saw that the trouble had ceased, he hardened his heart, and he would not listen to them, as the Lord had said.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 8:5; EXODUS 8:17"> And the Lord said to Moses, Speak thou to Aaron, Hold forth thy rod, and smite the dust of the earth, (or Say thou to Aaron, Stretch forth thy staff, and strike the dust of the earth), and little flies, or gnats, be in all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 105:31; ISAIAH 23:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:23"> And they did so; and Aaron held forth the hand, and held the rod, and smote the dust of &#91;the&#93; earth; and gnats were made in men, and in work beasts; all the dust of the earth was turned into gnats by all the land of Egypt. (And they did so; and Aaron stretched forth the staff with his hand, and struck the dust of the earth; and gnats came on people, and on the work beasts; yea, all the dust of the earth was turned into gnats in all the land of Egypt.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 7:11; EXODUS 9:11; DANIEL 5:8"> And the witches did in like manner by their enchantments, that they should bring forth gnats, and they might not; and gnats were as well in men as in work beasts. (And the witches did likewise with their enchantments, so that they would also bring forth gnats, but they could not do so; still, gnats covered all the people, and the work beasts as well.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKE 11:20; PSALMS 8:3; EXODUS 7:5"> And the witches said to Pharaoh, This is the finger of God. And the heart of Pharaoh was made hard, and he heard not them, as the Lord commanded, (or But Pharaoh’s heart was hardened, and he would not listen to them, as the Lord had said).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 7:15; EXODUS 9:13; EXODUS 8:1"> And the Lord said to Moses, Rise thou (up) early, and stand before Pharaoh, for he shall go out to the waters; and thou shalt say to him, The Lord saith these things, Deliver thou my people, that it make sacrifice to me, (or The Lord saith these things, Let my people go, so that they can worship me);</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 78:45; ISAIAH 7:18; PSALMS 105:31"> that if thou wilt not deliver the people (or but if thou wilt not let the people go), lo! I shall send into thee, and into thy servants, and into thy people, and into thine houses, all the kind(s) of flies; and the houses of the Egyptians shall be full-filled with flies of diverse kinds, and all the land in which they shall be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 9:4; MALACHI 3:18; EXODUS 10:23"> And in that day I shall make wonderful the land of Goshen, in which my people is, that flies be not there; and that thou know that I am the Lord in the midst of &#91;the&#93; earth; (But on that day I shall protect the land of Goshen, where my people be, so that there shall be no flies there; and so that thou shalt know that I, the Lord, am in the midst of the land;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and I shall set parting betwixt my people and thy people; this sign shall be tomorrow (or this miracle shall come tomorrow).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 105:31; PSALMS 78:45; EXODUS 8:21"> And the Lord did so. And a most grievous fly, that is, (a) multitude of flies, came into the house of Pharaoh, and into the houses of his servants, and into all the land of Egypt; and the land was corrupted of such flies (or and the land was ruined by such a multitude of flies).</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 8:8; EXODUS 9:27; EXODUS 10:16"> And Pharaoh called (for) Moses and Aaron, and said to them, Go ye, make ye sacrifice to the Lord your God, in this land, (or Go ye, and offer your sacrifices to the Lord your God, here in this land).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 43:32; GENESIS 46:34; DEUTERONOMY 7:25–26"> And Moses said, It may not be &#91;done&#93; so, for why shall we offer to the Lord our God the abominations of Egyptians; that if we shall slay before the Egyptians those things which they worship, they shall throw us down with stones. (And Moses said, It cannot be done so, for we shall not offer to the Lord our God the abominations of the Egyptians; and if, in front of the Egyptians, we kill those things which they worship, they shall throw us down with stones.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EXODUS 3:18; EXODUS 3:12; EXODUS 34:11"> We shall go the way of three days into wilderness, and we shall make sacrifice to our Lord God, as he commanded us. (We shall make a journey for three days into the wilderness, and we shall offer sacrifices to the Lord our God, as he commanded us.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EXODUS 8:8; EXODUS 9:28; 1ST KINGS 13:6"> And Pharaoh said, I shall deliver you, that ye make sacrifice to the Lord your God in desert, (or I shall let you go, so that ye can offer sacrifices to the Lord your God in the wilderness); nevertheless go ye not (any) further; (and) pray ye for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EXODUS 8:15; JEREMIAH 42:20–21; EXODUS 8:8"> And Moses said, I shall go out from thee, and I shall pray (to) the Lord; and the fly, that is, the multitude of flies, shall go away from Pharaoh, and from his servants, and (from) his people, tomorrow; nevertheless do not thou more deceive me, that thou deliver not the people to make sacrifice to the Lord, (or but do not thou deceive me again, and not allow the people to go, and offer sacrifices to the Lord).</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EXODUS 8:12; EXODUS 9:33; JAMES 5:16"> And Moses went out from Pharaoh, and prayed the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> the which did by the word of Moses (or and he did what Moses asked), and took away the flies from Pharaoh, and from his servants, and from his people; none (was) left, soothly not one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EXODUS 8:15; EXODUS 4:21; ROMANS 2:5"> And the heart of Pharaoh was made hard, so that he delivered not the people, soothly neither in this time. (But again Pharaoh’s heart was hardened, and he would not let the people go, yea, truly not at that time.)</span>
