<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:22; DEUTERONOMY 5:4; DEUTERONOMY 4:36"> And the Lord spake all these words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:6; HOSEA 13:4; LEVITICUS 26:13"> I am thy Lord God, that led thee out of the land of Egypt, from the house of servage. (I am the Lord thy God, who led thee out of the land of Egypt, from the house of servitude, or of slavery.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:7; MATTHEW 4:10; PSALMS 81:9"> Thou shalt not have alien gods before me. (Thou shalt not have foreign, or other, gods in place of me or instead of me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:8; LEVITICUS 26:1; DEUTERONOMY 4:15–19"> Thou shalt not make to thee a graven image, neither any likeness of (any) thing that is in heaven above, and that is in (the) earth beneath, neither of those things that be in waters under the earth; (Thou shalt not make a carved image for thyself, nor any likeness of anything that is in the heavens above, or on the earth beneath, or of those things that be in the waters under the earth;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:9; NUMBERS 14:18; DEUTERONOMY 4:24"> thou shalt not bow down to them, neither worship them; for I am thy Lord God, a strong(ly) jealous lover; I visit the wickedness of (the) fathers in (the) children into the third and fourth generation of them that hated me, (thou shalt not bow down to them, nor worship them; for I, the Lord thy God, am a jealous God; I punish the children for the wickedness of their fathers, unto the third and fourth generations of those who hate me,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:9; JOHN 14:21; 1ST JOHN 5:3"> and I do mercy into thousands, to them that love me, and keep my behests. (but I do mercy unto thousands, to those who love me, and who keep my commandments.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:11; LEVITICUS 19:12; JAMES 5:12"> Thou shalt not take in vain the name of thy Lord God, for the Lord shall not have him guiltless, that taketh in vain the name of his Lord God. (Thou shalt not take the name of the Lord thy God in vain, for the Lord shall not hold guiltless he who taketh the name of the Lord his God in vain.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:2; LEVITICUS 19:3; EXODUS 31:13–16"> Have thou mind, that thou hallow the sabbath day; (Remember, to keep the Sabbath day holy, or sacred;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 13:14; EXODUS 34:21; EXODUS 23:12"> in six days thou shalt work, and do all thy works; (for six days thou shalt work, and do all thy works;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:14–15; EXODUS 34:21; NUMBERS 15:32–36"> forsooth in the seventh day is the sabbath of thy Lord God; thou shalt not do any work (on that day), thou, and thy son, and thy daughter, and thy manservant, and thine handmaid, thy work beast, and the comeling that is within thy gates; (but on the seventh day is a Sabbath of the Lord thy God; thou shalt not do any work on that day, thou, and thy son, and thy daughter, and thy male slave, and thy slave-girl, and thy work beast, and the newcomer who is within thy gates;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 1:1–2; GENESIS 2:2–3; EXODUS 31:17"> for in six days God made (the) heaven(s) and (the) earth, the sea, and all things that be in those, and rested in the seventh day (or and then he rested on the seventh day); therefore the Lord blessed the sabbath day, and hallowed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:1–3; COLOSSIANS 3:20; DEUTERONOMY 5:16"> Honour thy father and thy mother, that thou be long living on the land (or so that thou can live a long life in the land), which thy Lord God shall give to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ROMANS 13:9; DEUTERONOMY 5:17; GENESIS 9:5–6"> Thou shalt not slay. (Thou shalt not kill.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:4; DEUTERONOMY 5:18; ROMANS 7:2–3"> Thou shalt not do lechery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ROMANS 13:9; EPHESIANS 4:28; LEVITICUS 19:11"> Thou shalt not do theft.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:5; MATTHEW 19:18; PSALMS 15:3"> Thou shalt not speak false witnessing against thy neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ROMANS 13:9; LUKE 12:15; HEBREWS 13:5"> Thou shalt not covet the house of thy neighbour, neither thou shalt desire his wife, nor his (man)servant, nor his handmaid, nor his ox, nor his ass, neither (anything of) all (the) things that be his. (Thou shalt not covet thy neighbour’s house, nor shalt thou desire his wife, or his slave, or his slave-girl, or his ox, or his donkey, or anything of all the things that be his.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 19:16–18; JEREMIAH 23:23; PSALMS 139:7–8"> Forsooth all the people heard voices, (that is, the thunder), and saw lamps, that is, shining lights, and the sound of a clarion, and the hill smoking; and they were afeared, and shaken with inward dread, and stood afar, (And all the people heard the thunder, and saw the lightening, and heard the sound of the trumpet, and saw the mountain smoking; and they were afraid, and shaken with inward fear, and stood afar off,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:38; GALATIANS 3:19"> and (they) said to Moses, Speak thou to us, and we shall hear; (but) the Lord speak not to us, lest peradventure we die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 13:3; PROVERBS 3:7; 1ST SAMUEL 12:20"> And Moses said to the people, Do not ye dread, for God came to prove you, and that his dread should be in you, and that ye should not do sin. (And Moses said to the people, Do not ye fear or Fear not, for God came to prove you, so that his fear would be in you, and then ye shall not sin.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST KINGS 8:12; PSALMS 97:2; PSALMS 18:9"> And the people stood afar (off); and Moses nighed to the darkness, wherein God was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 9:13; DEUTERONOMY 4:36; HEBREWS 12:25–26"> And the Lord said furthermore to Moses, Thou shalt say these things to the sons of Israel, Ye saw that from heaven I have spoken to you; (And the Lord also said to Moses, Thou shalt say these things to the Israelites, Ye have seen that I have spoken to you from heaven;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:39; ZEPHANIAH 1:5; 2ND KI 17:33"> ye shall not make gods of silver, neither ye shall make to you gods of gold. (ye shall not make gods out of silver, or gods out of gold, to be worshipped in addition to me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:5; 2ND CHRONICLES 6:6; PSALMS 132:13–14"> Ye shall make an altar of earth to me, and ye shall offer thereon your burnt sacrifices, and peaceable sacrifices, your sheep, and oxen; in each place in which the mind of my name shall be, I shall come to thee, and I shall bless thee. (Ye shall make an altar out of earth for me, and ye shall offer on it your burnt sacrifices, and your peace offerings, your sheep, and your oxen; and in each place where I ask thee to remember my name, I shall come to thee, and I shall bless thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOSHUA 8:31; DEUTERONOMY 27:5–6"> That if thou shalt make an altar of stone to me, thou shalt not build it of stones hewn, (or And if thou make an altar out of stones for me, thou shalt not build it out of cut stones); for if thou shalt raise thy knife thereupon, or (any) other instrument wherewith blood may be shed out, it shall be polluted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1ST PETER 1:16; ECCLESIASTES 5:1; LEVITICUS 10:3"> Thou shalt not go up by degrees to mine altar, lest thy filth(hood) be showed. (Thou shalt not go up to my altar by steps, lest thy nakedness should be shown.)</span>
