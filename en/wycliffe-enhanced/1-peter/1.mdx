</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 20:29; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:7; ROMANS 15:13"> Whom when ye have not seen, ye love; into whom also now ye not seeing, believe; but ye that believe shall have joy, and gladness that may not be told out, (or but ye who believe shall have joy, yea, joy that cannot be told out), and ye shall be glorified,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ROMANS 6:22; JAMES 1:21; HEBREWS 11:13"> and have &#91;or bring again&#93; the end of your faith, the health of your souls. (and receive the completion of your faith, yea, the salvation of your souls.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:17; LUKE 10:24; LUKE 24:25–27"> Of which health prophets sought &#91;out&#93;, (or Of which salvation the prophets sought out), and searched into, that prophesied of the grace to coming in you, &#91;Of which health prophets sought out, and ensearched, that prophesied of the grace to come into you,&#93;</span>

</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:48; 2ND CORINTHIANS 7:1; HEBREWS 12:14"> but like him that hath called you holy; (so) that also yourselves be holy in all living;</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:44; LEVITICUS 19:2; LEVITICUS 20:7"> for it is written, Ye shall be holy, for I am holy.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 6:20; TITUS 2:14; PSALMS 49:7–8"> witting that not by corruptible gold, either silver, ye be bought again of your vain living of (your) fathers’ tradition, (knowing that not by corruptible gold, or silver, ye be redeemed, or released, from the empty and useless living of your fathers’ traditions,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST JOHN 1:7; 1ST PETER 2:22–24; JOHN 1:29"> (no) but by the precious blood as of the lamb undefouled and unspotted, Christ Jesus, (but rather, by the precious blood as of an undefiled and unspotted lamb, yea, the Messiah Jesus,)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:4; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:9–10; HEBREWS 9:26"> that was known before the making of the world, but he is showed in the last times, for you (who was known before the creation of the world, but he was shown in these Last Times, for you)</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:24; HEBREWS 2:9; JOHN 12:44"> that by him be faithful in God; that raised him from death &#91;or that raised him from (the) dead&#93;, and gave to him everlasting glory, (so) that your faith and hope were in God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 1:5; HEBREWS 13:1; ROMANS 12:9–10"> And make ye chaste your souls in obedience of charity, in love of (the) brotherhood; of simple heart love ye together more busily (or with a sincere heart love one another more diligently).</span>
</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:8; MATTHEW 5:18; PSALMS 119:89"> but the word of the Lord dwelleth &#91;into&#93; without end (or but the word of the Lord remaineth forever). And this is the word, that is preached to you.</span>
