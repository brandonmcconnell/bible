<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:5; ROMANS 6:13; HEBREWS 13:15–16"> Therefore, brethren, I beseech you by the mercy of God, that ye give your bodies (as) a living sacrifice, holy, pleasing to God, and your service reasonable (or which is your reasonable service).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST PETER 1:14; EPHESIANS 4:22–24; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:17"> And do not ye be conformed to this world, but be ye reformed in (the) newness of your wit, that ye prove which is the will of God, (or but be ye reformed in the renewing of your mind, or in your thinking, so that ye prove what is the will of God), good, and well pleasing, and perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:3; PHILIPPIANS 2:3–8; PROVERBS 26:12"> For I say, by the grace that is given to me, to all that be among you, that ye understand not more than it behooveth to understand, but for to understand to soberness, &#91;or to not savour, or know, more than it behooveth to know, but to know to soberness&#93;; and to each man, as God hath parted the measure of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 12:27; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:12–14; EPHESIANS 4:15–16"> For as in one body we have many members, but all the members have not the same deed &#91;or the same act&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 12:20; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:17; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:27–28"> so we many be one body in Christ, and each be members one of another. (so we many bodies be but one body in the Messiah, and each of us be members of one another.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:10–11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:7; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:28–31"> Therefore we that have gifts diversing &#91;or Therefore having gifts diversing&#93;, after the grace that is given to us, either prophecy, after the reason of faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:11; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:1"> either service &#91;or ministry&#93;, in ministering; either he that teacheth, in teaching;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 9:7; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:17; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:28"> he that stirreth softly, in admonishing &#91;or in exhortation&#93;; he that giveth, in simpleness (or with sincerity); he that is sovereign, &#91;or is (a) prelate, or is before&#93;, in busyness (or with zeal and concern); he that hath mercy, in gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 34:14; 1ST PETER 4:8; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:5"> Love without feigning, hating evil, drawing &#91;or fast(ly) cleaving&#93; to (the) good;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:3; JOHN 13:34–35; HEBREWS 13:1"> loving together the charity of brotherhood. Each come before to worship (the) other &#91;or Coming before together in honour&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:10–11; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:25; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:15–16"> not slow in busyness, fervent in spirit, serving to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ROMANS 15:13; HEBREWS 10:36; PSALMS 62:8"> joying in hope, patient in tribulation, busy in prayer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:16; 2ND CORINTHIANS 9:12; 1ST JOHN 3:17"> giving good to the needs of (the) saints, keeping hospitality. (giving what is needed to God’s people, being hospitable.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:9; MATTHEW 5:44; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:15"> Bless ye men that pursue you; bless ye, and do not ye curse (them); (Bless those who persecute you; yea, bless them, and do not curse them;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 12:26; HEBREWS 13:3; PSALMS 35:13–14"> for to joy with men that joy, for to weep with men that weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:8; PROVERBS 3:7; JAMES 3:13–17"> Feel ye the same thing together; not understanding high things, &#91;or not savouring, or cunning, (or knowing), high things&#93;, but consenting to meek (or to humble) things, &#91;following meek (or humble) fathers&#93;. Do not ye be prudent with yourselves (or Do not think yourselves to be overly wise);</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:22; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:15; MATTHEW 5:39"> to no man yielding evil for evil, but purvey ye good things, not only before God, but also before all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:14; MATTHEW 5:9; ROMANS 14:19"> If it may be done, that that is of you, have ye peace with all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:35; HEBREWS 10:30; PROVERBS 24:29"> Ye most dear brethren, not defending, &#91;or venging&#93;, yourselves, but give ye place to wrath &#91;or ire&#93;; for it is written, The Lord saith, To me vengeance, and I shall yield (it).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 25:21–22; MATTHEW 5:44; LUKE 6:27"> But if thine enemy hungereth, feed thou him; if he thirsteth, give thou drink to him; for thou doing this thing shalt gather together coals on his head &#91;or forsooth doing these things thou shalt gather together coals on his head&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:9; LUKE 6:27–30; PROVERBS 16:32"> Do not thou be overcome of evil, but overcome thou evil by good. (Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.)</span>
