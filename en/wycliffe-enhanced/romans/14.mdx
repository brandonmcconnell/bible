</div>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 8:21; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:11; 1ST PETER 2:20"> And he that in this thing serveth Christ, pleaseth God, and is proved to men. (And he who doeth this serveth the Messiah, pleaseth God, and is approved by men.)</span>
