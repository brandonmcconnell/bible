<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARK 6:7–13; LUKE 9:52; NUMBERS 11:24–26"> And after these things the Lord Jesus ordained also other seventy and twain (or two), and sent them by twain and twain before his face into every city and place, whither he was to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:36–38; JOHN 4:35–38; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:6–9"> And he said to them, There is much ripe corn, and few workmen; therefore pray ye the Lord of the ripe corn, that he send workmen into his ripe corn. (And he said to them, There is a great harvest, but few workers; and so beseech the Lord of the harvest, that he send workers to gather in his harvest.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:29; EZEKIEL 2:3–6"> Go ye, lo! I send you as lambs among wolves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKE 22:35; 2ND KI 4:29; LUKE 10:4–12"> Therefore do not ye bear a satchel, neither scrip, neither shoes, and greet ye no man by the way (or and greet no one on the way).</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 25:6; MATTHEW 10:12–13; ISAIAH 57:19"> Into what&#91;ever&#93; house that ye enter, first say ye, Peace to this house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 3:16; 2ND CORINTHIANS 2:15–16; EPHESIANS 5:6"> And if a son of peace be there, your peace shall rest on him; but if none, it shall turn again to you, (or but if not, it shall return to you).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 5:17–18; MATTHEW 10:10–11; GALATIANS 6:6"> And dwell ye in the same house, eating and drinking those things that be at them; for a workman is worthy (of) his hire. Do not ye pass from house into house. (And live in the same house, eating and drinking those things that be there with them; for a workman is worthy of his wages. Do not go from house to house.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 10:27; MATTHEW 10:40; LUKE 10:10"> And into whatever city ye enter, and they receive you, eat ye those things that be set &#91;or that be put&#93; to you (or eat those things that be put before you);</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:2; JOHN 3:5; LUKE 10:11"> and heal ye the sick men (or the sick people) that be in that city. And say ye to them, The kingdom of God shall &#91;come&#93; nigh to you, (or The Kingdom of God hath come near to you).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:6; MATTHEW 10:14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:51"> And into what&#91;ever&#93; city ye enter, and they receive you not, go ye out into the streets of it, and say ye,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 10:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:51; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:26"> We wipe off against you the powder that cleaved to us of your city; nevertheless know ye this thing, that the realm of God shall come nigh. (We wipe off against you the dust that cleaved to us from your city; nevertheless know ye this thing, that the Kingdom of God hath come near to you.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:15; MATTHEW 11:24; LAMENTATIONS 4:6"> I say to you, that to Sodom it shall be easier &#91;or it shall be less pain(ful)&#93; than to that city in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 23:1–18; JOEL 3:4–8; EZEKIEL 26:1–26"> Woe to thee, Chorazin; woe to thee, Bethsaida; for if in Tyre and Sidon the virtues had been done, which have been done in you, sometime they would have sat in haircloth and ashes, and have done penance. (Woe to thee, Chorazin! woe to thee, Bethsaida! for if in Tyre and Sidon the works of power, or the miracles, had been done, which have been done in you, long ago they would have sat in sackcloth and ashes, and have repented.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 15:22–25; ROMANS 2:1; LUKE 12:47–48"> Nevertheless to Tyre and Sidon it shall be easier in the doom, than to you. (Nevertheless for Tyre and Sidon, it shall be easier at the Judgement, than for you.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 14:13–15; MATTHEW 4:13; LUKE 13:28"> And thou, Capernaum, art enhanced till to heaven; thou shalt be drenched &#91;down&#93; till into hell. (And thou, Capernaum, art exalted unto heaven? or art raised up into the sky? thou shalt be drowned down till into hell!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 13:20; MATTHEW 10:40; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:8"> He that heareth you, heareth me; and he that despiseth you, despiseth me; and he that despiseth me, despiseth him that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ROMANS 16:20; MARK 16:17; LUKE 9:1"> And the two and seventy disciples turned again with joy (or And the seventy-two disciples returned with joy), and said, Lord, also devils be subject to us in thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 12:7–9; ISAIAH 14:12; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 9:1"> And he said to them, I saw Satan falling down from heaven, as (or like) lightning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 91:13; MARK 16:18; ROMANS 16:20"> And lo! I have given to you power to tread on serpents, and scorpions, and on all the virtue of the enemy (or and over all the power of the enemy), and nothing shall harm you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:15; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:12; HEBREWS 12:23"> Nevertheless do not ye &#91;have&#93; joy in this thing, that spirits be subject to you; but joy ye, that your names be written in heavens. (Nevertheless do not rejoice over this, that the spirits, or the devils and the demons, be subject to you; rather rejoice, that your names be written in heaven.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 4:3; MATTHEW 13:11–16; MATTHEW 11:25–27"> In that hour he gladded in the Holy Ghost, and said, I acknowledge to thee, Father, Lord of heaven and of earth, for thou hast hid these things from wise men and prudent, and hast showed them to small &#91;or to little&#93; children. Yea, Father, for so it pleased before thee &#91;or for so it pleased to thee&#93;. (At that time he was filled with gladness by the Holy Spirit, or At that time he rejoiced in the Holy Spirit, and said, I acknowledge to thee, Father, Lord of heaven and of earth, for thou hast hidden these things from wise and prudent men, and thou hast shown them to little children. Yes, Father, for so it pleased thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:27; JOHN 1:18; JOHN 6:44–46"> All things be given to me of my Father (or Everything is given to me by my Father), and no man knoweth who is the Son, but the Father; and who is the Father, but the Son, and to whom the Son will show (it).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:16–17"> And he turned to his disciples, and said, Blessed be the eyes, that see those things that ye see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOHN 8:56; HEBREWS 11:13; 1ST PETER 1:10–12"> For I say to you, that many prophets and kings would have seen those things, that ye see, and they saw not; and hear those things, that ye hear, and they heard not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 22:34–39; LUKE 18:18; MATTHEW 19:16–19"> And lo! a wise man of the law rose up, tempting him, and saying, Master, what thing shall I do to have everlasting life? (And behold! a man wise in the Law rose up to test him, and asked, Teacher, what should I do to get eternal life?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ROMANS 10:5; ISAIAH 8:20; GALATIANS 3:21–22"> And he said to him, What is written in the law? how readest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MARK 12:30–31; MATTHEW 22:37–40; DEUTERONOMY 6:5"> He answered, and said, Thou shalt love thy Lord God of all thine heart &#91;or Thou shalt love the Lord thy God of all thine heart&#93;, and of all thy soul, and of all thy strengths, and of all thy mind; and thy neighbour as thyself. (He answered, Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thine heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy strength, and with all thy mind; and thy neighbour as thyself.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:11; LEVITICUS 18:5; MATTHEW 19:17"> And Jesus said to him, Thou hast answered rightly (or Thou hast answered correctly); do this thing, and thou shalt live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LUKE 16:15; ROMANS 4:2; LEVITICUS 19:34"> But he willing to justify himself, said to Jesus, And who is my neighbour? (But desiring to justify himself, he said to Jesus, And who is my neighbour?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 88:4; LUKE 18:31; LAMENTATIONS 2:12"> And Jesus beheld (him), and said, A man came down from Jerusalem into Jericho &#91;or Some man came down from Jerusalem to Jericho&#93;, and fell among thieves, and they robbed him, and wounded him, and went away, and left the man half alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1ST JOHN 3:16–18; PSALMS 69:20; JEREMIAH 5:31"> And it befell, that a priest came down the same way, and passed forth (or and passed by him), when he had seen him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:17; PSALMS 109:25"> Also a deacon, when he was beside the place, and saw him, passed forth. (Also a Levite, when he was beside the place, and saw him, passed by him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1ST KINGS 8:50; JEREMIAH 38:7–13; LUKE 7:13"> But a Samaritan, going the way, came beside him; and he saw him, and had ruth on him (or and had compassion for him); &#91;Forsooth some Samaritan, making journey, came beside the way; and he seeing him, was stirred by mercy;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="ROMANS 12:20; PSALMS 147:3; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:15"> and came to him, and bound together his wounds, and poured in oil and wine; and laid him on his beast, and led him in to an hostelry, and did the care of him (or and took care of him).</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:17; LUKE 14:13; ROMANS 16:23"> And another day (or And the next day), he brought forth two pence, and gave (them) to the hosteller, and said, Have the care of him (or Take care of him); and whatever thou shalt give over, I shall yield to thee, when I come again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LUKE 7:42; MATTHEW 21:28–31; MATTHEW 17:25"> Who of these three, seemeth to thee, was (a) neighbour to him, that fell among &#91;the&#93; thieves?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1ST JOHN 3:16–18; MICAH 6:8; 1ST PETER 2:21"> And he said (to him), He that did mercy into him, (or He who did mercy, or who was kind to him). And Jesus said to him, Go thou, and do thou in like manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JOHN 11:19–20; JOHN 11:1–5; JOHN 12:1–3"> And it was done, while they went, he entered into a castle (or he went into a village); and a woman, Martha by name, received him into her house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="PROVERBS 8:34; LUKE 8:35; JOHN 12:3"> And to this was a sister, Mary by name, which also sat beside the feet of the Lord, and heard his word(s).</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="JOHN 6:27; LUKE 12:29; MATTHEW 14:15"> But Martha busied (herself) about the oft service &#91;or Forsooth Martha busied (herself) about much service&#93;. And she stood, and said, Lord, takest thou no keep (or carest thou not), that my sister hath left me alone to serve? therefore say thou to her, that she help me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:6; LUKE 21:34; MATTHEW 6:25–34"> And the Lord answered, and said to her, Martha, Martha, thou art busy, and art troubled about full many things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="PSALMS 27:4; PSALMS 16:5–6; JOHN 10:27–28"> but one thing is necessary. Mary hath chosen the best part, which shall not be taken away from her.</span>
