<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 4:2; ROMANS 12:12; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:17"> And he said to them also a parable, that it behooveth to pray (for)evermore (or that it behooveth to always pray), and not fail;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:7; PSALMS 8:1–4; LUKE 18:4"> and said, There was a judge in a city, that dreaded not God, neither shamed of men. (and said, There was a judge in a city, who did not fear God, nor could be shamed by any man.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:17; JEREMIAH 5:28; DEUTERONOMY 27:19"> And a widow was in that city, and she came to him, and said, (A)Venge me of mine adversary;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:12–13; LUKE 16:3; LUKE 12:17"> and he would not (for a) long time. But after these things he said within himself, Though I dread not God, and shame not of man, (or Though I do not fear God, and I cannot be shamed by any man),</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKE 11:8; LUKE 18:39; JUDGES 16:16"> nevertheless for this widow is heavy &#91;or is dis-easeful&#93; to me, I shall (a)venge her; lest at the last she coming condemn me &#91;or she strangle me&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 7:13"> And the Lord said, Hear ye, what the doomsman of wickedness saith (or Listen to what the wicked judge saith);</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:35–37; 2ND PETER 3:9; LUKE 11:13"> and whether God shall not do &#91;the&#93; vengeance of his chosen, crying to him day and night, and shall have patience in them? (and so shall God not take vengeance for his chosen, those crying to him day and night, and shall also have patience with them?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:23–26; 2ND PETER 3:8–9; MATTHEW 24:9–13"> Soothly I say to you, for soon he shall do &#91;the&#93; vengeance of them. Nevertheless guessest thou, that man’s Son coming shall find faith in earth? (Truly I say to you, or I tell you the truth, that soon he shall take vengeance for them. Nevertheless guessest thou, that when man’s Son cometh, he shall find faith on the earth?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 16:15; PROVERBS 30:12; ISAIAH 65:5"> And he said also to some men, that trusted in themselves, as they were rightful, &#91;or that trusted in themselves, as rightful&#93;, (or who believed themselves to be good and righteous), and despised others, this parable, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:31–32; 1ST KINGS 8:30; 2ND KI 20:5"> Two men went up into the temple to pray; the one a Pharisee, and the other a publican (or and the other one a tax-collector).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:5; ISAIAH 65:5; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:17"> And the Pharisee stood, and prayed by himself these things, and said, God, I do thankings to thee, (or God, I give thanks to thee), for I am not as other men, raveners, unjust, adulterers, as also this publican (is);</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 11:42; MATTHEW 23:23–24; MATTHEW 9:14"> I fast twice in the week, I give tithes of all (the) things that I have in possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZRA 9:6; 1ST JOHN 1:8–10; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:15"> And the publican stood afar (or And the tax-collector stood far off), and would not raise &#91;up&#93; his eyes to heaven, but smote his breast, and said, God be merciful to me, (a) sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKE 14:11; LUKE 16:15; JAMES 4:10"> Truly I say to you, this went down into his house, and was justified from the other. For each that enhanceth himself, shall be made low, and he that meeketh himself, shall be enhanced. (I tell you the truth, this man went down to his house, and was justified rather than the other man. For everyone who exalteth himself, or who raiseth himself up, shall be made low, and he who humbleth himself, shall be exalted, or shall be raised up.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MARK 10:13–16; MATTHEW 19:13–15; 1ST SAMUEL 1:24"> And they brought to him young children, that he should touch them; and when the disciples saw this thing, they blamed them. (And they brought young children to him, so that he would touch them; and when the disciples saw this, they rebuked them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:3–4; 1ST CORINTHIANS 14:20; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:14"> But Jesus called together them, and said, Suffer ye (the) children to come to me, and do not ye forbid them, for of such is the kingdom of heavens. (But Jesus called them together, and said, Allow the children to come to me, and do not forbid them, for of such is the Kingdom of Heaven.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:3; MARK 10:15; PSALMS 131:1–2"> Truly I say to you, whoever shall not take the kingdom of God as a child, he shall not enter into it. (I tell you the truth, whoever shall not receive, or shall not accept, the Kingdom of God like a child, he shall not enter into it.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MARK 10:17–31; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:30; MATTHEW 19:16–30"> And a prince asked him, and said, Good master, in what thing doing shall I wield everlasting life? (And a leader of the synagogue asked him, and said, Good Teacher, what must I do to get, or to possess, eternal life?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOB 15:14–16; JAMES 1:17; HEBREWS 7:26"> And Jesus said to him, What sayest thou me (to be) good? (or Why sayest thou that I am good?) No man is good, but God alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 20:12–17; DEUTERONOMY 5:16–21; ROMANS 13:9"> Thou knowest the commandments, Thou shalt not slay, Thou shalt not do lechery, Thou shalt not do theft, Thou shalt not say false witnessing, Worship thy father and thy mother. (Thou knowest the commandments, Thou shalt not kill, Thou shalt not do adultery, Thou shalt not do theft, Thou shalt not give false testimony, or Thou shalt not lie, Honour thy father and thy mother.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:6; ROMANS 10:2–3; MARK 10:20–21"> Which said (or And he replied), I have kept all these things from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKE 12:33; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:18–19; MATTHEW 19:21"> And when this thing was heard, Jesus said to him, Yet one thing faileth to thee; sell thou all (the) things that thou hast, and give to poor men, and thou shalt have treasure in heaven; and come, and (pur)sue thou me, &#91;or and come, and (pur)sue me&#93;, (or and come, and follow me).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:31; 1ST JOHN 2:15; JOB 31:24–25"> And when these things were heard, he was sorrowful, for he was full rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 30:9; JAMES 2:5–7; MARK 10:23–27"> And Jesus seeing him made sorry &#91;or made sorrowful&#93; said, How hard they that have money &#91;or riches&#93; shall enter into the kingdom of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:24"> for it is lighter &#91;or forsooth it is easier&#93;, (for) a camel to pass through a needle’s eye, than (for) a rich man to enter into the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LUKE 13:23"> And they that heard these things said, Who may be made safe? (And they who heard these things said, Then who can be saved?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:17; MATTHEW 19:26; LUKE 1:37"> And he said to them, Those things that be impossible with men, be possible with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MARK 10:28; PHILIPPIANS 3:7; MATTHEW 19:27"> But Peter said, Lo! we have left all things, and have (pur)sued thee. (But Peter said, Behold! we have left everything, and have followed thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MARK 10:29–31; MATTHEW 10:37–39; LUKE 14:26–28"> And he said to him, Truly I say to you (or I tell you the truth), there is no man that shall forsake house, or father and mother, or brethren, or wife, or children, or fields, for the realm of God (or for the Kingdom of God),</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:5–6; JOB 42:10; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:17"> and shall not receive many more things in this time, and in the world to coming everlasting life, &#91;or and in the world to come everlasting life&#93;, (or and in the world to come eternal life).</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTHEW 20:17–19; ISAIAH 53:1–12; PSALMS 22:1–31"> And Jesus took his twelve disciples, and said to them, Lo! we go up to Jerusalem, and (there) all things shall be ended, that be written by the prophets of man’s Son (or which be written by the prophets about man’s Son).</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LUKE 23:11; ISAIAH 50:6; ISAIAH 53:3"> For he shall be betrayed to heathen men (or For he shall be handed over to the Gentiles), and he shall be scorned, and scourged, and bespat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:3–4; LUKE 24:7; MATTHEW 27:63"> and after that they have scourged him, they shall slay him, and the third day he shall rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LUKE 9:45; MARK 9:32; LUKE 24:25"> And they understood nothing of these; and this word was hid from them (or and the meaning was hidden from them), and they understood not those things that were said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTHEW 20:29–34; MARK 10:46–52; LUKE 16:20–21"> But it was done, when Jesus came nigh to Jericho (or when Jesus approached Jericho), a blind man sat beside the way, and begged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:10–11; LUKE 15:26"> And when he heard the people passing (by), he asked, what this was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MATTHEW 2:23; JOHN 19:19; JOHN 12:35–36"> And they said to him, that Jesus of Nazareth passed (by).</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:27; LUKE 18:39; MATTHEW 15:22"> And he cried, and said, Jesus, the son of David, have mercy on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="GENESIS 32:26–28; PSALMS 141:1; LUKE 18:15"> And they that went before, blamed him, that he should be still, (or And they who went ahead of Jesus, rebuked him, saying that he should be quiet); but he cried much the more &#91;or soothly he cried much more&#93;, Thou son of David, have mercy on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTHEW 20:31–34; MARK 10:48–52"> And Jesus stood, and commanded him to be brought forth to him. And when he came nigh, he asked him, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1ST KINGS 3:5–15; MATTHEW 20:21–22; PHILIPPIANS 4:6"> What wilt thou that I shall do to thee? (or What desirest thou that I shall do for thee?) And he said, Lord, that I see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:3; LUKE 8:48; LUKE 17:19"> And Jesus said to him, Behold; thy faith hath made thee safe, (or Behold; thy faith hath saved thee, or thy faith hath healed thee).</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="PSALMS 146:8; 2ND THESSALONIANS 1:10–12; ISAIAH 43:21"> And anon he saw, and (pur)sued him, and magnified God. And all the people, as it saw, gave praising to God. (And at once he saw, and followed him, and praised God. And all the people, who saw it happen, also praised God.)</span>
