<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 12:16; JOHN 14:26; JOHN 2:19–22"> And they bethought on his words. (And they remembered, and thought on his words.)</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:56; JOHN 20:11–18; LUKE 8:2–3"> And there was Mary Magdalene, and Joanna, and Mary of James, and other women that were with them, that said to the apostles these things &#91;or that said these things to the apostles&#93;.</span>

(and how the high priests and our leaders delivered him unto the damnation of death, and crucified him.)</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and when his body was not found, they came, and said, that they saw also a sight of angels, which said (or who said), that he liveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKE 24:12; JOHN 20:1–10"> And some &#91;men&#93; of ours went to the grave, and they found so as the women said, but they found not him. <span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MARK 6:48; GENESIS 19:2; GENESIS 32:26"> And they came nigh to the castle, whither they went, (or And they came near to the village, where they were going). And he made countenance that he would go further.</span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:19; LUKE 24:35; MATTHEW 15:36"> And it was done, while he sat at the meat with them (or while he sat down for the meal with them), he took bread, and blessed (it), and brake (it), and (then) took (it) to them &#91;or and (then) gave (it) to them&#93;.</span>

<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LUKE 24:45; JEREMIAH 23:29; HEBREWS 4:12"> And they said together, Whether our heart was not burning in us, while he spake to us in the way, and &#91;he&#93; opened to us &#91;the&#93; scriptures? (And they said to each other, Were not our hearts burning in us, while he spoke to us on the way, and he opened the Scriptures to us?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MARK 16:13; JOHN 20:19–26; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:14"> And they rose up in the same hour, and went again into Jerusalem &#91;or (and) went again to Jerusalem&#93;, and found the eleven gathered together, and them that were with them, (And they rose up at once, and returned to Jerusalem, and found the Eleven gathered together, and those who were with them,)</span>

<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MARK 16:12–13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:42; LUKE 24:30–31"> And they told what things were done in the way (or And then they told them what had happened on the way), and how they knew him in (the) breaking of (the) bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 3:16; ISAIAH 57:18; MARK 16:14"> And while they spake these things, Jesus stood in the middle of them, and said to them, Peace (be) to you; I am (here), do not ye dread. (And while they spoke of these things, suddenly Jesus stood in their midst, and said to them, Peace be to you; I am here, do not fear, or do not be afraid.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 28:13; MATTHEW 14:26–27; JOB 4:14–16"> But they were afraid and aghast, and guessed them(selves) to see a spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:14; HEBREWS 4:13; DANIEL 4:19"> And he said to them, What be ye troubled (or Why be ye troubled), and thoughts come up into your hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="JOHN 20:27; 1ST JOHN 1:1; ECCLESIASTES 12:7"> See ye my hands and my feet, for I myself am (or for I am here). Feel ye, and see ye; for a spirit hath not flesh and bones, as ye see that I have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And when he had said this thing, he showed (his) hands and feet to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="JOHN 21:5; PSALMS 126:1–2; GENESIS 45:26–28"> And yet while they believed not, and wondered for joy, he said, Have ye here anything that shall be eaten? &#91;or Have ye anything here that shall be eaten?&#93;</span>

<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:41"> And when he had eaten before them, he took that that (was) left, and gave (it) to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 12:10; LUKE 24:26–27; ZECHARIAH 9:9"> and &#91;he&#93; said to them, These be the words that I spake to you, when I was yet with you; for it is need(ful) that all things be fulfilled, that be written in the law of Moses, and in (the) prophets, and in (the) psalms, of me (or about me).</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:14; 1ST JOHN 5:20; PSALMS 119:18"> Then he opened to them wit, that they should understand &#91;the&#93; scriptures. (Then he opened their minds, or their thinking, so that they could understand the Scriptures.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:2–12; ISAIAH 50:6; LUKE 24:44"> And he said to them, For thus it is written, and thus it behooved Christ to suffer (or and thus it behooved the Messiah to suffer), and rise again from death the third day &#91;or and rise again from (the) dead the third day&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:31; ISAIAH 49:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:38"> and penance, and remission of sins to be preached in his name to all folks, beginning at Jerusalem. (and repentance, and forgiveness of sins to be preached in his name to all the nations and all the peoples, beginning at Jerusalem.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:32; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:31"> And ye be (the) witnesses of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:4; JOEL 2:28–32"> And I shall send the promise of my Father into you; but sit ye in the city, till ye be clothed with virtue from on high, (or but remain in the city, until ye be clothed with power from on high).</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:12; MATTHEW 21:17; MARK 11:1"> And he led them forth into Bethany, and when his hands were lifted up, he blessed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:9; MARK 16:19; 2ND KI 2:11"> And it was done, the while he blessed them &#91;or while he blessed them&#93;, he departed from them, and was borne (up) into heaven.</span>
