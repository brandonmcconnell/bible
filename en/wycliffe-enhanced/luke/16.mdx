<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:10; MATTHEW 25:14–30; JAMES 4:3"> He said also to his disciples, There was a rich man, that had a bailiff, &#91;or a farmer&#93;, (or a steward, or a manager); and this was denounced to him, as he had wasted his goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 11:9–10; 1ST PETER 4:5; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:12"> And he called him, and said to him, What hear I this thing of thee? (or Why do I hear this about thee?) yield (the) reckoning (or the accounting) of thy bailiffship, for thou might not now be bailiff, &#91;+or yield reason of thy farm, for now thou shalt no more hold the farm&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:31; ISAIAH 10:3; PROVERBS 18:9"> And the bailiff said within himself, What shall I do, for my lord taketh away from me the bailiffship? delve may I not, I shame to beg, (or I cannot dig, and I am ashamed to beg). &#91;Forsooth the farmer said within himself, What shall I do, for my lord taketh away from me the farm? I may not delve, I am ashamed to beg.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 30:9; JEREMIAH 4:22; JAMES 3:15"> I know what I shall do, (so) that when I am removed from the bailiffship &#91;or (so) that when I shall be removed from the farm&#93;, they receive me into their houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKE 7:41–42; MATTHEW 18:24"> Therefore when all the debtors of his lord were called together, he said to the first, How much owest thou to my lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TITUS 2:10; LUKE 16:12; LUKE 16:9"> And he said, An hundred barrows of oil. And he said to him, Take thy caution &#91;or Take thine obligation&#93;, and sit soon, and write fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 8:11–12; LUKE 20:9; LUKE 20:12"> Afterward he said to another, And how much owest thou? Which answered, An hundred cors of wheat &#91;or An hundred measures of wheat&#93;. And he said to him, Take thy letters, and write fourscore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:5; EPHESIANS 5:8; PROVERBS 6:6–8"> And the lord praised the bailiff of wickedness &#91;or And the lord praised the farmer of wickedness&#93;, for he had done prudently; for the sons of this world be more prudent in their generation, than the sons of light. (And the lord praised the steward for his wickedness, or his shrewdness, for he had done prudently; for the sons of this world be more prudent with their affairs, than the sons of the light be.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:21; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:17–19; DANIEL 4:27"> And I say to you, make ye to you friends of the riches of wickedness, (so) that when ye shall fail, they receive you into everlasting tabernacles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKE 16:11–12; MATTHEW 25:21; LUKE 19:17"> He that is true in the least thing, is true also in the more (thing), &#91;or He that is faithful in the least thing, is faithful also in the more&#93;; and he that is wicked in a little thing, is wicked also in the more (things). (He who is true in the least thing, is also true in the greater thing; and he who is wicked in a little thing, is also wicked in the greater things.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 12:33; JAMES 2:5; PROVERBS 8:18–19"> Therefore if ye were not true in the wicked thing of riches &#91;or Therefore if ye were not true in the wicked riches&#93;, who shall betake to you that that is very &#91;or (that) that is sooth&#93;? (or who shall deliver unto you that which is truly valuable?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 19:13–26; MATTHEW 25:14–29; JOB 1:21"> And if ye were not true in other men’s thing(s), who shall give to you that that is yours?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:15–16; MATTHEW 6:24; JAMES 4:4"> No servant may serve to two lords; for either he shall hate the one, and love the other; either he shall draw to the one, and despise the other. Ye may not serve to God and to riches (or Ye cannot serve God and money, or wealth). &#91;No manservant may serve two lords; forsooth either he shall hate one, and love the other; either he shall cleave to one, and despise the other. Ye may not serve to God and riches.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKE 23:35; LUKE 20:47; JEREMIAH 6:13"> But the Pharisees, that were covetous, heard all these things, and they scorned him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 16:7; PROVERBS 21:2; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:5"> And he said to them, Ye it be, that justify you(rselves) before men; but God hath known your hearts &#91;or soothly God knoweth your hearts&#93;, for that that is high to men, is (an) abomination before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:9–14; MATTHEW 21:32; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:24–25"> The law and the prophets till to John; from that time the realm of God is evangelized &#91;or is preached&#93;, and each man doeth violence into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:18; LUKE 21:33; 1ST PETER 1:25"> Forsooth it is lighter (for) heaven and earth to pass (away), than that one tittle fall from the law. (For it is easier for heaven and earth to pass away, than for one dot to fall away from the Law.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:9; MARK 10:11–12; MATTHEW 5:31–32"> Every man that forsaketh his wife, and weddeth another, doeth lechery &#91;or doeth adultery&#93;; and he that weddeth the wife forsaken of the husband, doeth adultery, (or and he who weddeth the wife left by her husband, doeth adultery).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 16:49; AMOS 6:4–6; LUKE 18:24–25"> There was a rich man, and (he) was clothed in purple, and white silk, and ate every day shiningly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:2; 1ST SAMUEL 2:8; JAMES 2:5"> And there was a beggar, Lazarus by name, that lay at his gate full of boils,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:27; MARK 7:28; JOHN 6:12"> and coveted to be fulfilled of the crumbs &#91;+or coveting to be filled with the crumbs&#93;, that fell down from the rich man’s board (or table), and no man gave to him; but (the) hounds came, and licked his boils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:24; PSALMS 49:16–19; JAMES 1:11"> And it was done, that the beggar died, and was borne of (the) angels (or was carried by the angels) into Abraham’s bosom. And the rich man was dead also, and was buried in hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:4; LUKE 16:28; MATTHEW 18:9"> And he raised up his eyes, when he was in torments, and saw Abraham afar, and Lazarus in his bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOHN 4:10; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:1; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:16–17"> And he cried, and said, Father Abraham, have mercy on me, and send Lazarus, that he dip the end of his finger in water, and cool my tongue; for I am tormented in this flame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKE 6:24; 1ST JOHN 2:15; PSALMS 17:14"> And Abraham said to him, Son, have mind (or remember), for thou hast received good things in thy life, and Lazarus also evil things; but he is now comforted, and thou art tormented.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOHN 3:36; PSALMS 49:14; PSALMS 50:22"> And in all these things a great dark place &#91;or a great dark depth&#93; is stablished betwixt us and you; that they that will from hence pass to you, may not, (or so that they who want to pass over from here to you, cannot), neither from thence (to) pass over hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And he said, Then I pray thee, father, that thou send him into the house of my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:40; PSALMS 49:12–13; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:6"> For I have five brethren, that he witness to them, (or For I have five brothers, so that he can testify to them), lest also they come into this place of torments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:21; LUKE 24:27; LUKE 16:16"> And Abraham said to him, They have Moses and the prophets; hear they them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:9–11; LUKE 16:24; LUKE 13:5"> And he said, Nay, father Abraham, but if any of dead men go to them &#91;or but if any of (the) dead shall go to them&#93;, they shall do penance (or then they shall repent).</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 4:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:8; JOHN 11:43–53"> And he said to him, If they hear not Moses and the prophets, neither if any of (the) dead men rise again, they shall believe to him. (And he said to him, If they will not listen to Moses and the prophets, even if a dead man shall rise again, they shall not believe him.)</span>
