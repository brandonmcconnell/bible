(I shall do them away like the dust of the earth; I shall pound them, and I shall do them away like the fen, or the clay, of the streets.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 8:1–14; ISAIAH 55:5; 2ND SAMUEL 3:1"> Thou shalt save me from &#91;the&#93; against-sayings of my people; thou shalt keep me into the head of folks (or thou shalt make me the head of the nations, or thou shalt keep me the head of the nations); the people, whom I know not, shall serve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="PSALMS 81:15; PSALMS 66:3; ISAIAH 56:3"> Alien sons shall (not) against-stand me; by hearing of &#91;the&#93; ear, they shall obey to me. (The sons of foreigners shall bow low to me; and after hearing me, they shall obey me.)</span>

<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="PSALMS 94:1; PSALMS 144:2; 2ND SAMUEL 18:31"> God, that givest vengeances to me, and hast cast down peoples under me. (The God, who grantest me vengeance, and hast thrown down peoples, or nations, under me.)</span>
