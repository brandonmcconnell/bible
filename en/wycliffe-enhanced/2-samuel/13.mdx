<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 3:9; 2ND SAMUEL 3:2–3; 2ND SAMUEL 13:15"> And it was done after these things, that Amnon, the son of David, loved the fairest sister, Tamar by name, of Absalom, the son of David. (And it was done after these things, that Amnon, one of David’s sons, loved Tamar, the fairest sisterof Amnon, and of Absalom, another of David’s sons.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 7:10; 1ST KINGS 21:4; SONG OF SONGS 5:8"> And Amnon perished greatly for her, so that he was sick for her love. For since she was a virgin, it seemed hard to him, that he should do anything unhonestly with her. (And Amnon greatly burned for her, so that he was sick for her love. But since she was a virgin, it was hard for him to do anything dishonourable to her.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 16:9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:19; JAMES 3:15"> But there was a friend to Amnon, Jonadab by name, the son of Shimeah, the brother of David; and Jonadab was a full prudent man &#91;(or) a full sly man&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:17; LEVITICUS 18:9; ESTHER 5:13–14"> Which said to Amnon, Son of the king, why art thou made feeble so by leanness, by all days? why showest thou not to me? (or And he said to Amnon, Son of the king, why art thou made so weak and thin, day after day? why not tellest thou to me?) And Amnon said to him, I love Tamar, the sister of my brother Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MARK 6:24–25; PROVERBS 19:27; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:15"> And Jonadab answered to him, Lie thou on thy bed, and feign thou sickness; and when thy father cometh, that he visit thee, say thou to him, I pray, come Tamar, my sister, that she give meat to me, and make a stew, that I eat it of her hand. (And Jonadab answered to him, Do thou this. Lie thou on thy bed, and pretend to be sick; and when thy father cometh to visit thee, say thou to him, I pray thee, let my sister Tamar come, and give food to me, yea, to make me a stew, and I shall eat it by her hand or and she shall serve it to me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 18:6; MATTHEW 13:33"> Therefore Amnon lay down, and feigned to be sick. And when the king had come to visit him, Amnon said to the king, I beseech, come Tamar, my sister, that she make two suppings before my eyes, and that I take of her hand the meat made ready. (And so Amnon lay down, and pretended to be sick. And when the king had come to visit him, Amnon said to the king, I beseech thee, that my sister Tamar come, and make supper for me, and when the food is ready, I shall eat it by her hand or she shall serve it to me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Therefore David sent to the house of Tamar, and said, Come thou into the house of Amnon, thy brother, and make thou a stew to him. (And so David sent word to Tamar’s house, and said, Go thou to thy brother Amnon’s house, and make thou a stew for him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Tamar came into the house of Amnon, her brother. And he lay down; and she took meal, and mixed (it) together, and made (it) moist before his eyes, and seethed &#91;the&#93; suppings (or and boiled the supper);</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 45:1; JOHN 3:20; JUDGES 3:19"> and she took that, that she had sodden, and poured it out, and set it before him, and he would not eat, (or and she took what she had boiled, and poured it out, and set it before him, but he would not eat it). And Amnon said, Put ye out all men from me. And when they had put out all (the) men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Amnon said to Tamar, Bear the meat into the (bed-)closet, that I eat of thine hand. Therefore Tamar took the suppings which she had made, and brought in to Amnon, her brother, in the (bed-)closet. (Amnon said to Tamar, Bring the food into the bed-chamber, so that I can eat it by thy hand or so that thou can serve it to me. And so Tamar took the supper which she had made, and brought it to her brother Amnon, in the bed-chamber.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 39:11–12"> And when she had proffered the meat to him, he took her, and said, Come thou, my sister, lie thou with me. (And when she offered him the food, he took hold of her, and said, Come thou, my sister, lie thou with me or sleep with me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JUDGES 19:23; LEVITICUS 20:17; JUDGES 20:6"> And she answered to him, My brother, do not thou, do not thou oppress me, for this is not leaveful in Israel (or for this is not lawful in Israel); do not thou do this folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JUDGES 19:24; GENESIS 20:12; GENESIS 19:8"> For I shall not be able to bear my shame, and thou shalt be as one of the unwise men, (or the fools), in Israel; but rather speak thou to the king, and he shall not deny me to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 12:11; ESTHER 7:8; JUDGES 20:5"> Soothly he would not assent to her prayers; but he was stronger in mights, and oppressed her, and lay with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:17"> And then (afterward), with full great hatred Amnon hated her, so that the hatred was greater, by which he hated her, than the love by which he (had) loved her before. And Amnon said to her, Rise thou (up), and go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And she answered to him, This evil is more which thou doest now against me, and puttest me out, than that, that thou didest before. And he would not hear her; (And she answered to him, This evil which thou now doest against me, by putting me out, is worse, than what thou didest before. But he would not listen to her;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> but when the servant was called, that ministered to him (or who served him), he said, Put thou out this woman from me, and close thou the door after her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JUDGES 5:30; GENESIS 37:3; PSALMS 45:13–14"> And she was clothed with a coat down to the heel; for the king’s daughters (who were) virgins used such clothes. Then the servant of Amnon put her out, and closed the door after her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:6; JEREMIAH 2:37; 2ND SAMUEL 1:11"> And she sprinkled ashes (on)to her head, and when her long coat was rent, and her hands put on her head, she went entering &#91;in&#93; and crying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 12:19; GENESIS 46:15; GENESIS 34:2"> And Absalom, her brother, said to her, Whether Amnon, thy brother, hath lain with thee? But now, sister, be still; he is thy brother, and torment thou not thine heart for this thing. Therefore Tamar dwelled mourning in the house of Absalom, her brother, (or And so Tamar stayed in the house of her brother Absalom, and mourned her state).</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 34:7; 2ND SAMUEL 3:28–29; 1ST SAMUEL 2:29"> Forsooth when king David had heard these words, he was full sorry, and he would not make sore the spirit of Amnon, his son, (or And when King David heard about this, he was very upset, but he would not punish his son Amnon); for he loved Amnon, for he was his first begotten son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:17–18; GENESIS 31:24; GENESIS 24:50"> And Absalom spake not to Amnon, neither evil nor good; for Absalom hated Amnon, for he had defouled Tamar, his sister, (or for he had defiled his sister Tamar).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 38:12–13; 1ST SAMUEL 25:4; 2ND CHRONICLES 26:10"> And it was done after the time of two years, that the sheep of Absalom were shorn in Baalhazor, which is beside Ephraim. And Absalom called all the sons of the king (or And Absalom invited all the king’s sons to be there).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 11:8–15; JEREMIAH 41:6–7; PSALMS 55:21"> And he came to the king, and said to him, Lo! the sheep of thy servant be shorn; I pray (thee), come the king with his servants to his servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKE 14:23; JUDGES 19:7–10; 2ND SAMUEL 14:22"> And the king said to Absalom, Do not thou, my son, do not thou pray, that all we come, and charge thee. And when he constrained David, and he would not go, he blessed Absalom. (And the king said to Absalom, Do not thou, my son, do not thou pray, that we all come, and be a burden to thee. And when Absalom pressed David, he still would not go, but he blessed Absalom.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 20:9; 2ND SAMUEL 3:27; 2ND SAMUEL 11:13–15"> And Absalom said to David, If thou wilt not come, I beseech thee, come namely Amnon, my brother, with us, (or And Absalom said to David, If thou wilt not come, I beseech thee, then let my brother Amnon come with us). And the king said to him, It is no need, that he go with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PROVERBS 26:24–26"> Therefore Absalom constrained him; and he delivered with him Amnon, and all the sons of the king. (But Absalom pressed him; and so he let Amnon, and all his other sons, go with him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JUDGES 19:6; JUDGES 19:22; JUDGES 19:9"> And Absalom had made a feast as the feast of a king. And Absalom &#91;had&#93; commanded to his servants, and said, Espy ye, when Amnon is drunken of wine, and when I say to you, Smite ye, and slayeth him. Do not ye dread, for I am that command to you; be ye strengthened, and be ye strong men. (And Absalom made a feast like the feast of a king. And Absalom commanded to his servants, and said, Watch ye, so that when Amnon is drunk with wine, and I say to you, Strike ye him! that you kill him. Do not ye fear, for I am the one who command you to do this; be ye of good courage, and be ye strong men.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 18:9; 1ST KINGS 1:33; 2ND KI 1:9–12"> Therefore the servants of Absalom did against Amnon, as Absalom had commanded to them; and (then) all the sons of the king (swiftly) rose up, and ascended each upon his mule, and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And when they went yet in the way, (the) fame came thereof to the king, and it was said, Absalom hath slain all the sons of the king, and namely not one (is) left of them. (And when they were yet on the way, the report came to the king, and it was said, Absalom hath killed all of the king’s sons, and not one of them is left alive.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 1:11; 2ND SAMUEL 12:16; JOB 1:20"> Therefore the king rose up, and rent his clothes, and felled down on the earth (or and fell down on the ground); and all his servants that stood nigh to him, rent their clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 16:9; 2ND SAMUEL 13:3; GENESIS 27:41"> But Jonadab, the son of Shimeah, brother of David, answered and said, My lord the king, guess thou not, that all the young men, and sons of the king, be slain; Amnon alone is dead, for he was set in hatred to Absalom, from the day in which he oppressed Tamar, his sister. (But Jonadab, the son of Shimeah, David’s brother, said, My lord the king, think thou not, that all of the king’s sons be killed; no, only Amnon is dead, for Absalom hath hated him, from the day that he oppressed his sister Tamar.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 19:19"> Now therefore, my lord the king, set not this word on his heart, and say, All the sons of the king be slain; for Amnon alone is dead. (And so now, my lord the king, put not this thing upon thy heart, and say, All the king’s sons be killed; for only Amnon is dead.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="GENESIS 4:8–14; PROVERBS 28:17; AMOS 5:19"> Forsooth Absalom fled. And a young man, (an) espyer, raised &#91;up&#93; his eyes, and beheld, and lo! much people came by a way out of the common way, by the side of the hill. (And so Absalom fled away. And a young man, a watchman, raised up his eyes, and looked, and behold! a crowd of people came by the road, on the side of the hill behind him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And Jonadab said to the king, Lo! the sons of the king come; after the word of thy servant, so it is done, (or so it is done, just as thy servant hath said).</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 18:33; 2ND SAMUEL 12:21; 2ND SAMUEL 13:15"> And when he had ceased to speak, also the sons of the king appeared; and they entered, and raised up their voice, and wept; but also the king and all his servants wept with full great weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 3:3; 2ND SAMUEL 14:32; 2ND SAMUEL 13:34"> Forsooth Absalom fled, and went to Talmai, the son of Ammihud, the king of Geshur. Therefore David bewailed his son Amnon in many days (or And so David bewailed his son Amnon for many days).</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 15:8; 2ND SAMUEL 14:23; 2ND SAMUEL 14:32"> Forsooth Absalom, when he had fled, and had come into Geshur, was there (for) three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="GENESIS 24:67; GENESIS 38:12; GENESIS 37:35"> And &#91;king&#93; David ceased to pursue Absalom, for he was comforted upon the death of Amnon. (And after King David resigned himself to Amnon’s death, he longed for his son Absalom.)</span>
