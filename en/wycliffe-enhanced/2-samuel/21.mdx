Soothly when David failed, (And again the Philistines made battle against Israel; and David and his men went down, and fought against the Philistines. And when David grew weary,)</span>

(Ishbibenob, who was a descendant of Harapha, that is, the father of the giants, whose iron of his spear weighed three hundred ounces, and who was girded with a new sword, endeavoured to strike down David.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST KINGS 11:36; 2ND SAMUEL 18:3; PSALMS 132:17"> And Abishai, the son of Zeruiah, was in help to David; and he smote and killed the Philistine. (But Abishai, the son of Zeruiah, helped David; and he struck and killed the Philistine. And then the men of David swore, and said, From now on, thou shalt not go out with us into battle, lest thou quench Israel’s lantern.)</span>

(And there was a second battle against the Philistines at Gob; there Sibbechai of the Hushathites struck down Saph, a descendant of Harapha, that is, the father of the giants.)</span>

(And the third battle against the Philistines was also at Gob; in which battle Elhanan, the son of Jair or the son of Jaareoregim, a man of Bethlehem, struck downthe brother of Goliath of Gath, whose spear shaft was like a weaver’s beam.)</span>
