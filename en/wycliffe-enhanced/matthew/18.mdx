<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:13–14; MARK 9:36–37; 1ST KINGS 3:7"> And Jesus called (for) a little child, and put him in the middle &#91;or in the midst&#93; of them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MARK 10:14–15; 1ST PETER 2:2; MATTHEW 19:14"> and said, I say truth to you &#91;or Truly I say to you&#93;, &#91;no&#93; but ye be turned, and &#91;be&#93; made as little children, ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heavens. (and said, I tell you the truth, unless ye be changed, and be made like little children, ye shall not enter into the Kingdom of Heaven.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JAMES 4:10; LUKE 14:11; MARK 10:43"> Therefore whoever meeketh him&#91;self&#93; as this little child, he is (the) greater in the kingdom of heavens. (And so whoever humbleth himself, like this little child, he is the greatest in the Kingdom of Heaven.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:40; MARK 9:37; JOHN 13:20"> And he that receiveth one such little child in my name, receiveth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MARK 9:42; LUKE 17:1–2; MATTHEW 18:10"> But whoso causeth to stumble one of these small (ones), &#91;or these little (ones)&#93;, that believe in me, it speedeth to him, that a millstone of asses be hanged in his neck, and he be drenched in the deepness of the sea. (But whosoever causeth to stumble one of these little ones, who believe in me, it is more expedient for him, that a donkey’s millstone be hung about his neck, and he be drowned in the depth of the sea.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 17:1; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:19; MATTHEW 26:24"> Woe to the world, for causes of stumbling; for it is need(ful), (or it is necessary), that causes of stumbling come; nevertheless woe to that man by whom a cause of stumbling cometh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:29–30; PHILIPPIANS 3:8–9; ROMANS 13:12"> And if thine hand or thy foot cause thee to stumble, cut it off, and cast it away from thee. It is better to thee to enter &#91;in&#93; to life feeble, either crooked, (or It is better for thee to enter into (eternal) life maimed, or lame), than having twain hands or two feet to be sent into (the) everlasting fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:22; MATTHEW 18:8; MATTHEW 5:29"> And if thine eye cause thee to stumble, pull it out, and cast it away from thee. It is better to thee, with one eye to enter into life, (or It is better for thee to enter into (eternal) life with one eye), than having twain eyes to be sent into the fire of hell &#91;or into hellfire&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 91:11; PSALMS 34:7; HEBREWS 1:14"> See ye, that ye despise not one of these little (ones). For I say to you &#91;or Truly I say to you&#93;, that the angels of them in heavens see (for)evermore the face of my Father that is in heavens, (or For I tell you the truth, that their guardian angels in heaven always see the face of my Father who is in heaven).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For man’s Son came to save that thing that perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:12; LUKE 15:4–7; EZEKIEL 34:16"> What seemeth to you? If there were to a man an hundred sheep, and one of them hath erred, whether he shall not leave (the) ninety and nine in (the) desert &#91;or in the hills&#93;, and shall go to seek that that erred? (What seemeth to you? If a man hath a hundred sheep, and one of them hath wandered off, shall he not leave the ninety-nine on the hills, and go to seek the one which hath wandered off?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 3:17; LUKE 15:5–10; JAMES 2:13"> And if it fall that he find it, truly I say to you (or I tell you the truth), that he shall have joy thereof, &#91;or for he shall joy thereon&#93;, more than on (the) ninety and nine that erred not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 17:12; JOHN 6:39–40; 2ND PETER 3:9"> So it is not the will of your Father that is in heavens &#91;or that is in heaven&#93;, that one of these little (ones) perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:17; LUKE 17:3–4; GALATIANS 6:1"> But if thy brother sinneth against thee, go thou, and reprove him, betwixt thee and him alone, (or And if thy brother sinneth against thee, go, and rebuke him, between thee and him alone); if he heareth thee, thou hast won thy brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 19:15; 2ND CORINTHIANS 13:1; JOHN 8:17"> And if he heareth thee not, take with thee one or twain, (so) that every word stand in the mouth of twain or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 3:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:9–7; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:14–15"> And if he heareth not them, say thou to the church. But if he heareth not the church, be he as an heathen and (as) a publican to thee, &#91;or be he to thee as an heathen man and a publican&#93;, (or be he like a Gentile and a tax collector to thee).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:19; 2ND CORINTHIANS 2:10; JOHN 20:23"> I say to you truly, (or Truly I say to you, or I tell you the truth), whatever things ye bind on earth, those shall be bound also in heaven; and whatever things ye unbind on earth, those shall be unbound also in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:7; 1ST JOHN 5:14–16; MARK 11:24"> Again I say to you, that if twain of you consent on earth, of everything whatever they ask, it shall be done to them of my Father that is in heavens. (Again I tell you, that if two of you consent, or agree, on the earth, about anything, whatever they ask, it shall be done for them by my Father who is in heaven.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 5:4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:3; MATTHEW 28:20"> For where twain or three be gathered in my name, there I am in the middle of them. &#91;For where two or three be gathered in my name, there I am in the midst of them.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKE 17:3–4; MATTHEW 18:15"> Then Peter came to him, and said, Lord, how oft shall my brother sin against me, and I shall forgive him? Whether till seven times?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:13; MARK 11:25; MATTHEW 6:14–15"> Jesus saith to him, I say not to thee, till seven times; but till seventy times seven times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:24; ROMANS 14:12; MATTHEW 3:2"> Therefore the kingdom of heavens is likened to a king, that would reckon with his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 38:4; PSALMS 130:3–4; LUKE 16:5"> And when he began to reckon, one that owed to him ten thousand talents, was brought to him. &#91;+And when he began to put reason, one was brought to him, that owed to him ten thousand bezants, or talents.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2ND KI 4:1; NEHEMIAH 5:5; LUKE 7:42"> And when he had not whereof to yield, his lord commanded him to be sold, and his wife, and children, and all things that he had, and (so) to be paid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:29; LUKE 7:43; MATTHEW 8:2"> But that servant felled down, and prayed him, and said, Have patience in me (or Have patience with me), and I shall yield to thee all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 145:8; NEHEMIAH 9:17; PSALMS 86:15"> And the lord had mercy on that servant, and suffered him to go, (or and allowed him to go), &#91;or (and) delivered him, (or let him go)&#93;, and forgave him the debt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTHEW 20:2; EZEKIEL 45:9; DEUTERONOMY 15:2"> But that servant went out, and found one of his even-servants, that owed him an hundred pence, (or But that servant went out, and found one of his fellow servants, who owed him a hundred pennies); and he held him, and strangled him, and said, Yield that that thou owest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:26; MATTHEW 6:12; PHILEMON 1:18–19"> And his even-servant fell down, and prayed him, and said, Have patience in me, and I shall requite all things to thee. (And his fellow servant fell down at his feet, and beseeched him, and said, Have patience with me, and I shall repay everything to thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1ST KINGS 22:27; 1ST KINGS 21:27–29"> But he would not; but went out, and put him &#91;or sent him&#93; into prison, till he paid all the debt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ROMANS 12:15; HEBREWS 13:3; MARK 3:5"> And his even-servants, seeing the things that were done, sorrowed greatly, (or And his fellow servants, seeing what was done, greatly sorrowed). And they came, and told to their lord all the things that were done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LUKE 19:22; MATTHEW 25:26; ROMANS 3:19"> Then his lord called him, and said to him, Wicked servant, I forgave to thee all the debt, for thou prayedest me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:13; MATTHEW 6:12; EPHESIANS 4:32–2"> Therefore whether it behooved not also thee to have mercy on thine even-servant, as I had mercy on thee? (And so did it not also behoove thee to have mercy on thy fellow servant, like I had mercy on thee?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:25–26; JAMES 2:13; LUKE 12:58–59"> And his lord was wroth, and took him to tormentors, till he paid all the debt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:14–15; JAMES 2:13; LUKE 6:37–38"> So my Father of heaven shall do to you, if ye forgive not every man to his brother, of your hearts. (So shall my Father in heaven do to you, if any of you do not forgive his brother, from your heart.)</span>
