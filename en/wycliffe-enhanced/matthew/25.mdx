<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 8:12; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:7–8; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:1–3"> And he that had taken two bezants, or two talents, came, and said, Lord, thou betookest to me two bezants; lo! <span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKE 19:20–22; LUKE 15:29; MALACHI 3:14–15"> But he that had taken one bezant &#91;or one talent&#93;, came, and said, Lord, I know that thou art an hard man; thou reapest where thou hast not sown, and thou gatherest together where thou hast not spread abroad; (But he who had received one talent, came, and said, Lord, I know that thou art a hard man; thou harvestest where thou hast not sown, and thou gatherest together where thou hast not spread abroad;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 1:6–7; ROMANS 8:15; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:8"> and I dreading (or and I greatly fearing thee), went, and hid thy bezant &#91;or thy talent&#93; in the earth; lo! thou hast that that is thine.</span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:12; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:15–16; MATTHEW 13:50"> And cast ye out the unprofitable servant into outer-more &#91;or uttermore&#93; darknesses; there shall be weeping, and grinding of teeth.</span>

(Then the King shall say to those, who shall be on his right side, Come, the blessed of my Father, take into possession the Kingdom prepared for you from the creation of the world.)</span>

<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:3; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:14; 1ST PETER 5:5–6"> Then just men shall answer to him, and say (or Then the righteous shall say to him), Lord, when saw we thee hungry, and we fed thee; thirsty, and we gave to thee, &#91;or and we gave thee&#93;, (something to) drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and when saw we thee harbourless, and we harboured thee, &#91;or when forsooth saw we thee harbourless, and we gathered thee to harbour&#93;; or naked, and we covered thee?</span>

<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:4; JUDE 1:6–7; 2ND THESSALONIANS 1:9"> Then the king shall say also to them, that shall be on his left half, Depart from me, ye cursed, into (the) everlasting fire, that is made ready to the devil and his angels. (Then the King shall say to those, who shall be on his left side, Go away from me, ye cursed, into the eternal fire, which is prepared for the Devil and his angels.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="JAMES 2:15–24; JOHN 5:23; MATTHEW 12:30"> For I hungered, and ye gave not me (anything) to eat; I thirsted, and ye gave not me (anything) to drink; &#91;+For I hungered, and ye gave not to me (anything) to eat; I thirsted, and ye gave not to me (anything) to drink;&#93;</span>
