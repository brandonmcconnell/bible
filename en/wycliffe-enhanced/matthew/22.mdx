<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:17; JOHN 5:40; LUKE 14:15–17"> And he sent his servants to call men that were bidden to the weddings, and they would not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:46; MATTHEW 21:36; JOHN 6:50–57"> Again he sent other servants, and said, Say to the men that be bidden to the feast, Lo! <span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HEBREWS 2:3; LUKE 14:18–20; 1ST JOHN 2:15–16"> But they despised, and went forth, one into his town, another to his merchandise. &#91;Soothly they despised, or reckoned not, and they went away, one into his vineyard, forsooth another to his merchandise.&#93;</span>

(But the king, when he had heard, was enraged; and he sent his army, or his soldiers, and destroyed those murderers, and burned down their city.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 1:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:46; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:4"> Then he said to his servants, The weddings be ready, but they that were called to the feast, were not worthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 14:21–24; PROVERBS 1:20–23; MARK 16:15–16"> Therefore go ye into the ends of ways, and whomever ye find, call ye to the weddings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 6:9–11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:6–9; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:21"> And his servants went out into the ways, and gathered together all that they found, good and evil; and the bridal was &#91;or the weddings be&#93; full-filled with men sitting at the meat (or and the wedding feast was filled full with people come for the meal).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:4–5; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:8; HEBREWS 4:12–13"> And the king entered, to see men sitting at the meat (or to see those who had come for the meal); and he saw there a man not clothed with bride clothes &#91;or and he saw there a man not clothed with bridal clothes&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 2:9; JOB 5:16; MATTHEW 20:13"> And he said to him, Friend, how enteredest thou hither without bride clothes? And he was dumb (or And he did not answer).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:12; MATTHEW 25:30; MATTHEW 24:51"> Then the king bade his ministers, &#91;or Then the king said to his ministers&#93;, (or Then the king said to his servants), Bind him both hands and feet, and send ye him into outer-more &#91;or uttermore&#93; darknesses; there shall be weeping, and grinding of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND PETER 1:10; LUKE 13:23–24; MATTHEW 7:13–14"> For many be called, but few be chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKE 20:20–26; MARK 12:13–17; PSALMS 2:2"> Then (the) Pharisees went away, and took a counsel &#91;or took counsel&#93; to take Jesus in word. (Then the Pharisees went away, and made a plan to catch Jesus with his own words.)</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKE 2:1; ROMANS 13:6–7; MATTHEW 17:25"> Therefore say to us, what seemeth to thee. <span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOHN 8:6; LUKE 5:22; JOHN 2:25"> And when Jesus had known the wickedness of them, he said, Hypocrites, what tempt ye me? (And when Jesus had perceived their malice, he said, Hypocrites, why do you test me? or why do you try to trap me?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 20:2; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:6; MATTHEW 18:28"> Show ye to me the print of the money. And they brought to him a penny.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKE 20:24"> And Jesus said to them, Whose is this image, and the writing above? (or Whose image is this, and the writing upon it?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ROMANS 13:7; PROVERBS 24:21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:19"> They say to him, The emperor’s. Then he said to them, Therefore yield ye to the emperor those things that be the emperor’s, and to God those things that be of God. &#91;+They say to him, Of Caesar. Then he saith to them, Therefore yield ye to Caesar those things that be of Caesar/those things that be Caesar’s, and to God those things that be of God.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MARK 12:12; MATTHEW 22:46; LUKE 20:25–26"> And they heard, and wondered; and they left him, and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:1; MARK 12:18–27; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:6–8"> In that day (some) Sadducees, that say there is no rising again to life(or who say that there is no resurrection), came to him, and asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 25:5–10; LUKE 20:28; RUTH 1:11"> and said, Master, (or Teacher), Moses said, if any man is dead, not having a son, that his brother wed his wife, and raise seed to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKE 20:29–33; MARK 12:19–23; HEBREWS 9:27"> And there were seven brethren to us; and the first wedded a wife, and is dead. And he had no seed, and left his wife to his brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> also the second, and the third, till to the seventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> But the last of all, &#91;also&#93; the woman is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Also &#91;or Therefore&#93; in the rising again to life(or And so in the resurrection), whose wife of the seven shall she be? for all had her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOHN 20:9; ROMANS 15:4; LUKE 1:37"> Jesus answered, and said to them, Ye err, not knowing the scriptures, nor the virtue of God. (Jesus answered, and said to them, Ye err, not knowing the Scriptures, nor the power of God.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LUKE 20:34–36; MARK 12:24–25; MATTHEW 24:38"> For in the rising again to life(or For in the resurrection to life), neither they shall wed, neither shall be wedded; but they be as the angels of God in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:3; MATTHEW 21:16; MATTHEW 21:42"> And of the rising again of dead men (or And about the resurrection of the dead), have ye not read, that &#91;it&#93; is said of the Lord, that saith to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EXODUS 3:6; MARK 12:26–27; LUKE 20:37–38"> I am God of Abraham, and God of Isaac, and God of Jacob? he is not (the) God of dead men, but of living men, (or he is not the God of the dead, but of the living).</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MARK 12:17; MARK 6:2; JOHN 7:46"> And the people hearing, wondered on his teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MARK 12:28–31; MATTHEW 25:3–5; MATTHEW 12:14"> And the Pharisees heard that he had put silence to (the) Sadducees, and came together (to him).</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LUKE 7:30; LUKE 10:25–37; LUKE 11:52"> And one of them, a teacher of the law, asked Jesus, and tempted him, (or and tested him, or and tried to trap him),</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MARK 12:28–33; MATTHEW 5:19–20; LUKE 11:42"> Master, which is a great commandment in the law? (Teacher, which is the greatest, or the most important, commandment in the Law?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MARK 12:29–30; LUKE 10:27; DEUTERONOMY 6:5"> Jesus said to him, Thou shalt love thy Lord God &#91;or Thou shalt love the Lord thy God&#93;, of all thine heart, and in all thy soul, and in all thy mind. (Jesus said to him, Thou shalt love the Lord thy God, with all thine heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy mind.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> This is the first and the most (important) commandment. (This is the first and the greatest commandment.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MARK 12:31; LUKE 10:27–37; LEVITICUS 19:18"> And the second is like to this; Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="ROMANS 13:9; MATTHEW 7:12; 1ST JOHN 4:19–21"> In these two commandments hangeth all the law and the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LUKE 20:41–44; MARK 12:35–37; MATTHEW 22:34"> And when the Pharisees were gathered together, Jesus asked them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:5–6; JOHN 6:68–69; MATTHEW 16:13–17"> and said, What seemeth to you of Christ (or What seemeth to you regarding the Messiah), whose son is he? They say to him, Of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 23:2; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 4:2; MARK 12:36"> He saith to them, How then David in spirit calleth him Lord, and saith, (He said to them, How then did David by the Holy Spirit call him Lord, and say,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="PSALMS 110:1; HEBREWS 1:13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:34–35"> The Lord said to my Lord, Sit &#91;thou&#93; on my right half, till I put thine enemies a stool of thy feet? (The Lord said to my Lord, Sit at my right hand, or on my right side, until I make thine enemies thy footstool?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="ROMANS 1:3–4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:16; JOHN 8:58"> Then if David calleth him Lord, how (then) is he his son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="MARK 12:34; LUKE 20:40; LUKE 14:6"> And no man might answer a word to him, neither any man was hardy from that day (on), (or nor was any man bold enough from that day on), to ask him (anything) more.</span>
