<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARK 13:1–37; LUKE 21:5–36; EZEKIEL 8:6"> And Jesus went out of the temple; and his disciples came to him, to show him the buildings of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 19:44; JEREMIAH 26:18; MICAH 3:12"> But he answered, and said to them, See ye all these things? Truly I say to you (or I tell you the truth), a stone shall not be left on a stone/a stone shall not be left here on one stone, that not it shall be destroyed &#91;or which shall not be destroyed&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MARK 13:3–4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:7; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:1–11"> And when he sat on the mount of Olives, his disciples came to him privily (or in private), and said, Say to us, when these things shall be, and what (is the) token (or the sign) of thy coming, and of the ending of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST JOHN 4:1; JEREMIAH 29:8; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:3"> And Jesus answered, and said to them, Look ye &#91;or See ye&#93;, that no man deceive you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:11; MATTHEW 24:24; JEREMIAH 14:14"> For many shall come in my name, and shall say, I am Christ (or I am the Messiah); and they shall deceive many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MARK 13:7–8; LUKE 21:9; 1ST PETER 3:14–15"> For ye shall hear battles, and opinions of battles; see ye that ye be not disturbed &#91;or that ye be not distroubled&#93;; for it behooveth these things to be done, but not yet is the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 21:11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:8; ISAIAH 19:2"> For folk shall rise together against folk, and realm against realm, and pestilences, and hungers, and earth-movings shall be by places; (For nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom, and plagues, and famines, and earthquakes shall be in places;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:3; 1ST PETER 4:17–18; DEUTERONOMY 28:59"> and all these be (but the) beginnings of (the) sorrows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 16:2; JOHN 15:19–20; LUKE 21:16–17"> Then men shall betake you into tribulation, and shall slay you, and ye shall be in hate &#91;or in hatred&#93; to all folks for my name. (Then men shall deliver you unto trials and troubles, and shall kill you, and ye shall be hated by all the nations and all the peoples, for my name’s sake.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MICAH 7:5–6; LUKE 21:16; MATTHEW 11:6"> And then many shall be caused to stumble, and betray each other, and they shall hate each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:24; MATTHEW 7:15; MATTHEW 24:5"> And many false prophets shall rise, and deceive many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:4–5; JAMES 4:1–4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:15"> And for wickedness shall be plenteous, the charity of many shall wax cold (or the love of many shall grow cold);</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:22; MARK 13:13; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:10"> but he that shall dwell still &#91;or steadfast&#93; into the end, shall be safe. (but he who shall endure unto the end, shall be saved.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:6; ROMANS 10:18; ROMANS 16:25–26"> And this gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in all the world, in&#91;to&#93; witnessing to all folks; and then the end shall come. (And this Gospel, or Good News, of the Kingdom shall be preached in all the world, as a testimony to all the nations and all the peoples; and then the end shall come.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DANIEL 9:27; DANIEL 12:11; MARK 13:14"> Therefore when ye see the abomination of discomfort, that is said of (or by) Daniel, the prophet, standing in the holy place; he that readeth (this), understand he;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 19:15–17; LUKE 21:21–22; HEBREWS 11:7"> then they that be in Judea, flee to the mountains;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKE 17:31–33; MARK 13:15–16; MATTHEW 10:27"> and he that is in the house roof (or and he who is on the roof of the house), come not down to take anything (out) of his house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and he that is in the field, turn not again to take his coat (or do not return to get his coat).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKE 23:29–30; DEUTERONOMY 28:53–56; LUKE 21:23"> But woe to them that be with child, and nourishing &#91;or nursing&#93; in those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 16:29; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:12"> Pray ye, that your flying be not made in winter, or in the sabbath. (Pray ye, that your fleeing be not made in winter, or on the Sabbath.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DANIEL 12:1; JOEL 2:2; MALACHI 4:1"> For then shall be great tribulation, what manner was not from the beginning of the world to now &#91;or till now&#93;, neither shall be made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MARK 13:20; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:10; MATTHEW 24:31"> And but those days had been abridged (or shortened), each flesh, &#91;or all flesh, that is, mankind&#93;, should not be made safe (or none of mankind could be saved); but those days shall be made short, for the chosen men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKE 21:8; LUKE 17:23–24; DEUTERONOMY 13:1–3"> Then if any man say to you, Lo! here is Christ, or there, do not ye believe (him). (Then if anyone say to you, Behold! here is the Messiah, or there, do not believe them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 2:9–11; MARK 13:22; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 13:13–14"> For false Christs and false prophets shall rise (up), (or For false Messiahs and false prophets shall rise up), and they shall give great tokens &#91;or great signs&#93; and wonders; so that also the chosen be led into error, if it may be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOHN 16:1; LUKE 21:13; ISAIAH 48:5–6"> Lo! I have before-said to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LUKE 3:2–3; ISAIAH 40:3; MATTHEW 3:1"> Therefore if they say to you, Lo! he is in (the) desert, do not ye go out; lo! he is in privy places, (or behold! (he is) in private, or secret, places), do not ye believe (them).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKE 17:24–37; ZECHARIAH 9:14; JOB 37:3"> For as lightning goeth out from the east, and appeareth into the west, so shall be also the coming of man’s Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKE 17:37; JOB 39:27–30; DEUTERONOMY 28:49"> Wherever the body shall be, also the eagles shall be gathered thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="AMOS 8:9; JOEL 3:15; ISAIAH 13:10"> And anon (or at once) after the tribulation of those days, the sun shall be made dark, and the moon shall not give her light, and the stars shall fall from (the) heaven(s), and the virtues (or the powers) of (the) heavens shall be moved &#91;or shall be stirred&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:7; DANIEL 7:13; LUKE 21:27"> And then the token of man’s Son shall appear in (the) heaven(s), and then all the kindreds, &#91;or all the lineages&#93;, (or all the tribes), of the earth shall wail; and they shall see man’s Son coming in the clouds of (the) heaven(s), with much virtue, and majesty. (And then the sign of man’s Son shall appear in the heavens, and then all the peoples of the earth shall wail; and they shall see man’s Son coming on the clouds of the heavens, with great power, and majesty.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 4:16; MARK 13:27; ISAIAH 27:13"> And he shall send (out) his angels with a trumpet, and a great voice (or and a great blast); and they shall gather his chosen from (the) four winds, from the highest things of heaven (un)to the ends of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MARK 13:28–29; LUKE 21:29–30"> And learn ye the parable of the fig tree. When his branch is now tender, and the leaves be sprung, ye know that summer is nigh, (or When its branch is now tender, and the leaves be sprung out, ye know that summer is near);</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JAMES 5:9; HEBREWS 10:37; EZEKIEL 7:2–14"> and so ye when ye shall see all these things, know ye that it is nigh, (yea), in the gates, (or know that it is near, yes, even at the door).</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MARK 13:30–31; LUKE 21:32–33; MATTHEW 16:28"> Truly I say to you (or I tell you the truth), for this generation shall not pass (away), till all (these) things be done;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:8; 1ST PETER 1:25; NUMBERS 23:19"> heaven and earth shall pass (away), but my words shall not pass (away).</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MARK 13:32; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:7; MATTHEW 24:44"> But of that day and hour no man knoweth, neither &#91;the&#93; angels of heaven, &#91;no&#93; but the Father alone (or but only the Father).</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKE 17:26–27; GENESIS 6:1–7; 2ND PETER 2:5"> But as it was in the days of Noe (or Noah), so shall be the coming of man’s Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LUKE 17:26–28; ROMANS 13:13–14; LUKE 14:18–20"> For as in the days before the great flood, they were eating and drinking, wedding and taking to wedding, till that day, that Noe (or Noah) entered into the ship;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="JOHN 3:20; MATTHEW 24:37; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:41"> and they knew not, till the great flood came, and took (away) all men, so shall be the coming of man’s Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LUKE 17:34–37; 2ND PETER 2:7–9; 2ND PETER 2:5"> Then twain shall be in a field, one shall be taken, and the other left;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="EXODUS 11:5; ISAIAH 47:2; LUKE 17:35"> two women shall be grinding in a quern &#91;or (at a) mill&#93;, one shall be taken, and the other left; twain in a bed, the one shall be taken, and the other left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="LUKE 21:36; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:6; MATTHEW 25:13"> Therefore wake ye, (or And so be on watch, or stay awake), for ye know not in what hour the Lord shall come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LUKE 12:39; MATTHEW 24:44; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:2–6"> But know ye this, that if the husbandman knew in what hour the thief were to come, certainly he would wake, and suffer not his house to be undermined (or and would not allow his house to be robbed).</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:10; MATTHEW 24:42; MATTHEW 25:13"> And therefore be ye ready, for in what hour ye guess not, man’s Son shall come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:21; MATTHEW 25:23; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:1–2"> Who guessest thou is a true &#91;or is a faithful&#93; servant and prudent, whom his lord ordained on his household, to give them meat in (its) time? (Then who thinkest thou, is a faithful and a wise servant, whom his lord hath ordained upon, or over, his household, to give them their food at the proper time?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:15; LUKE 12:43; LUKE 12:37"> Blessed is that servant, whom his lord, when he shall come, shall find so doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:23; MATTHEW 25:21; LUKE 12:37"> Truly I say to you, for on all his goods he shall ordain him. (I tell you the truth, that he shall put him in charge over all of his property.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:32; ISAIAH 32:6; LUKE 12:45"> But if that evil servant say in his heart, My lord tarrieth to come,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 17:6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:6; MICAH 3:5"> and beginneth to smite his even-servants, and to eat, and drink with drunken men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:2–3; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:3; MATTHEW 24:42–44"> the lord of that servant shall come in the day &#91;in&#93; which he hopeth not, and in the hour that he knoweth not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:12; MATTHEW 22:13; LUKE 12:46"> and shall part him (or and shall divide him in pieces), and put his part with (the) hypocrites; there shall be weeping, and grinding of teeth.</span>
