<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:10; JOHN 7:21–24; LUKE 14:3"> And the Pharisees, seeing, said to him, Lo! thy disciples do that thing that is not leaveful to them to do in &#91;the&#93; sabbaths. (And the Pharisees, seeing that, said to him, Behold! thy disciples do what is not lawful for them to do on the Sabbath.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 21:3–6; LUKE 6:3; MARK 2:25–26"> And he said to them, Whether ye have not read, what David did, when he hungered, and they that were with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 24:5–9; EXODUS 25:30; LEVITICUS 8:31"> how he entered into the house of God, and ate (the) loaves of proposition, &#91;either of setting forth&#93;, which loaves it was not leaveful to him to eat, neither to them that were with him, &#91;no&#93; but to (the) priests alone? (or which bread it was not lawful for him to eat, nor for those who were with him, but only for the priests?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:9–10; JOHN 7:22–23; NEHEMIAH 13:17"> Or whether ye have not read in the law, that in (or on) the sabbaths (the) priests in the temple defoul the sabbaths (or defile the Sabbath), and they be without blame?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:41–42; 2ND CHRONICLES 6:18; 1ST PETER 2:4–5"> And I say to you, that here is a greater than the temple. (And I tell you, that here is something greater than the Temple.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HOSEA 6:6; MATTHEW 9:13; MICAH 6:6–8"> And if ye knew, what it is, I will mercy, and not sacrifice, ye should never have condemned innocents. (And if ye only knew, what is meant by, I want mercy, or kindness, and not sacrifice, ye would never have condemned the innocent.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MARK 2:28; LUKE 6:5; JOHN 5:17–23"> For man’s Son is Lord, yea, of the sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MARK 3:1–6; LUKE 6:6–11"> And when he passed from thence, he came into the synagogue of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKE 13:14; JOHN 9:16; JOHN 8:6"> And lo! a man that had a dry hand. And they asked him, and said, Whether it be leaveful to heal in the sabbath? that they should accuse him. (And behold! a man who had a withered hand. And they asked him, and said, Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath? so that they could accuse him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:4; LUKE 14:5; LUKE 13:15–17"> And he said to them, What man of you shall there be, that hath one sheep, and if it fall into a ditch in (or on) the sabbaths, whether he shall not (take) hold (of it), and lift it up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:26; LUKE 6:9; MARK 3:4"> How much more is a man better than a sheep? Therefore it is leaveful to do good in the sabbaths, &#91;or And so it is leaveful to do good in the sabbath&#93;, (or And so it is lawful to do good on the Sabbath).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 13:13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:7–8"> Then he said to the man, Stretch forth thine hand. And he stretched (it) forth; and it was restored to health as the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 11:53; JOHN 10:39; MARK 3:6"> And the Pharisees went out, and made a counsel (or a plan) against him, (as to) how they should destroy him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MARK 3:7–12; MATTHEW 10:23; MATTHEW 19:2"> And Jesus knew it, and went away from thence; and many (pur)sued him (or and many followed him), and he healed them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:4; MATTHEW 9:30; MATTHEW 17:9"> And he commanded to them, that they should not make him known;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 42:1–4; ISAIAH 42:9; MATTHEW 13:35"> that that thing were fulfilled, that was said by Esaias, the prophet, saying, (so that it was fulfilled, which was said by the prophet Isaiah, saying,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 42:1; LUKE 4:18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:38"> Lo! my child, whom I have chosen, my darling, in whom it hath well pleased to my soul; I shall put my Spirit on him, and he shall tell doom to (the) heathen men (or and he shall tell out judgement to the Gentiles).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:29; JOHN 18:36–38; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:24–25"> He shall not strive, nor cry, neither any man shall hear his voice in (the) streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ISAIAH 42:3–4; EZEKIEL 34:16; ISAIAH 61:1–3"> A bruised reed he shall not break &#91;or He shall not break (al)together a shaken reed&#93;, and he shall not quench smoking flax, till he cast out doom (or judgement), (un)to victory;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ISAIAH 11:10; ROMANS 15:12–13; ISAIAH 42:4"> and heathen men shall (have) hope in his name. (and the Gentiles shall have hope in his name.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKE 11:14–15; MARK 7:35–37; ISAIAH 29:18"> Then a man blind and dumb, that had a fiend &#91;or a devil&#93;, was brought to him; and he healed him, so that he spake, and saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:27; JOHN 4:29; MATTHEW 22:42–43"> And all the people wondered, and said, Whether this be the son of David?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MARK 3:22; MATTHEW 9:34; LUKE 11:15"> But the Pharisees heard, and said, He this (man) casteth not out fiends, (no) but in Beelzebub, (the) prince of (the) fiends. &#91;+But the Pharisees hearing, said, This (man) casteth not out fiends, no but in Beelzebub, prince of devils&#93;. (But the Pharisees heard, and said, He throweth out demons by Beelzebub, the Prince of demons.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:15; JEREMIAH 17:10; MATTHEW 9:4"> And Jesus, witting their thoughts, said to them, Each kingdom parted against itself, shall be desolated, &#91;or shall be desolate&#93;, (or Each kingdom divided against itself, shall be destroyed), and each city, or house, parted against itself, shall not stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:13; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:2–3; 1ST JOHN 5:19"> And if Satan casteth out Satan, he is parted against himself, (or And if Satan throweth out Satan, he is divided against himself); therefore how shall his kingdom stand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:13–16; MARK 9:38–39; LUKE 9:49–50"> And if I in Beelzebub cast out devils, in whom, &#91;or by whose might&#93;, (do) your sons cast (them) out? Therefore they shall be your doomsmen (or And so they shall be your judges).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKE 11:20; DANIEL 7:14; MATTHEW 6:33"> But if I in the Spirit of God cast out fiends, then the kingdom of God is come into you &#91;or is come among you&#93;. (But if I by the Spirit of God throw out devils and demons, then the kingdom of God is come unto you.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ISAIAH 49:24; MARK 3:27; LUKE 11:21–22"> Either how may any man enter into the house of a strong man, and take away his vessels, &#91;no&#93; but he first bind the strong man, and then he shall spoil his house? (Or how can any man enter into the house of a strong man, and take away his things? first he must bind up the strong man, and then he can rob his house.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MARK 9:40; LUKE 11:23; JOSHUA 24:15"> He that is not with me, is against me; and he that gathereth not together with me, scattereth abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LUKE 12:10; MARK 3:28–30; HEBREWS 10:29"> Therefore I say to you, all sin and blasphemy shall be forgiven to men, but &#91;the&#93; blasphemy of the Spirit shall not be forgiven (or but blasphemy against the Holy Spirit shall not be forgiven).</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MARK 3:29; HEBREWS 10:26–29; HEBREWS 6:4–6"> And whoever saith a word against man’s Son, it shall be forgiven to him; but who that saith a word against the Holy Ghost (or but who that saith a word against the Holy Spirit), it shall not be forgiven to him, neither in this world, nor in the tother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKE 6:43–44; MATTHEW 7:16–20; JAMES 4:8"> Either make ye the tree good, and his fruit good; either make ye the tree evil and his fruit evil; for a tree is known of his fruit (or for a tree is known by its fruit).</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LUKE 6:45; MATTHEW 15:18; EPHESIANS 4:29"> Ye generation of adders, how may ye speak good things, when ye be evil? (or Ye children of snakes, how can ye say anything good, when ye yourselves be evil?) For the mouth speaketh (out) of the plenty &#91;or (out) of the great abundance&#93; of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:4; COLOSSIANS 4:6; PROVERBS 15:28"> A good man bringeth forth good things of good treasure, and an evil man bringeth forth evil things of evil treasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 12:14; ROMANS 2:16; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:12"> And I say to you, that of every idle word, that men speak, they shall yield reason thereof in the day of doom; (And I tell you, that for every idle word, that men speak, they shall have to give a reason for it on the Day of Judgement;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:3; JAMES 2:21–25"> for of thy words thou shalt be justified, and of thy words thou shalt be condemned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:22; MARK 8:11–12; LUKE 11:16"> Then some of the scribes and the Pharisees answered to him, and said, Master, we will see a token of thee, (or Teacher, we desire to see a sign from thee).</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:4; LUKE 11:29–32; MARK 8:38"> Which answered, and said to them, An evil kindred and a spouse-breaker seeketh a token &#91;or An evil generation and adulterous seeketh a token&#93;, and a token shall not be given to it, &#91;no&#93; but the token of Jonas, the prophet. (And he answered, and said to them, An evil and adulterous generation seeketh a sign, and no sign shall be given to it, except for the sign of the prophet Jonah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="JONAH 1:17; MATTHEW 27:63–64; MATTHEW 16:21"> For as Jonas was in the womb of a whale three days and three nights (or For as Jonah was in the belly of a whale for three days and three nights), so man’s Son shall be in the heart of the earth three days and three nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:42; HEBREWS 11:7; MATTHEW 12:6"> Men of Nineveh shall rise in (the) doom with this generation, and shall condemn it; for they did penance in the preaching of Jonas, and lo! here &#91;is&#93; a greater (man) than Jonas. (Men of Nineveh shall rise at the Judgement with this generation, and they shall condemn it; for they repented after the preaching of Jonah, and behold! here (is) something greater than Jonah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1ST KINGS 10:1–13; 2ND CHRONICLES 9:1–12; HEBREWS 1:2–4"> The queen of the south shall rise in doom with this generation, and (she) shall condemn it; for she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and lo! here &#91;is&#93; a greater (man) than Solomon. (The Queen of the South shall rise at the Judgement with this generation, and she shall condemn it; for she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and behold! here (is) something greater than Solomon.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LUKE 11:24–26; LUKE 8:28–32; MATTHEW 8:29"> When an unclean spirit goeth out from a man, he goeth by dry places, seeking rest, and findeth not &#91;or and he findeth none&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1ST JOHN 4:4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 13:8–9; JUDE 1:4–5"> Then he saith, I shall turn again into mine house, from whence I went out. And he cometh, and findeth it void, and cleansed with besoms, and made fair. (Then he saith, I shall return to my house, from where I went out. And he cometh, and findeth it empty, and swept clean, and made neat and tidy.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="LUKE 11:26; MARK 5:9; HEBREWS 6:4–8"> Then he goeth (out), and taketh (back) with him seven other spirits worse than himself; and they enter &#91;in&#93;, and dwell there. And the last things of that man be made worse than the former. So it shall be to this worst generation, (or And so the last things of that man be made worse than the first things. So it shall be with this evil generation).</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:55; JOHN 2:12; JOHN 7:3"> Yet while he spake to the people, lo! his mother and his brethren stood withoutforth, seeking to speak with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> And a man said to him, Lo! thy mother and thy brethren stand withoutforth, seeking thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="MARK 3:32–33; MATTHEW 10:37; LUKE 2:52"> And he answered to the man, that spake to him, and said, Who is my mother? and who be my brethren?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="MARK 3:34; JOHN 17:8–9; JOHN 20:17–20"> And he held forth his hand into his disciples, and said, Lo! my mother and my brethren;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="LUKE 8:21; MARK 3:35; JOHN 15:14"> for whoever doeth the will of my Father that is in heavens, he is my brother, and sister, and mother.</span>
