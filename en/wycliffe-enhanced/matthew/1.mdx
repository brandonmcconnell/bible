<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:8; HEBREWS 11:17–18; GENESIS 46:8–27"> Abraham begat Isaac. Isaac begat Jacob. Jacob begat Judas and his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 46:12; GENESIS 38:29–30; 1ST CHRONICLES 4:1"> Judas begat Phares and Zara, of Thamar. Phares begat Esrom. Esrom begat Aram.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 7:1–13; 2ND CHRONICLES 26:21; 2ND CHRONICLES 27:1–32"> Ozias begat Joatham. Joatham begat Achaz. Achaz begat Ezekias.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 27:20; JEREMIAH 52:28–30; DANIEL 1:2"> Josias begat Jechonias and his brethren, into the transmigration of Babylon (or until the exile to Babylon).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 3:17; EZRA 3:2; LUKE 3:27"> And after the transmigration of Babylon (or And after the exile to Babylon), Jechonias begat Salathiel. Salathiel begat Zorobabel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Zorobabel begat Abiud. Abiud begat Eliakim. Eliakim begat Azor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Azor begat Sadoc. Sadoc begat Achim. Achim begat Eliud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Eliud begat Eleazar. Eleazar begat Matthan. Matthan begat Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:17; JOHN 4:25; LUKE 3:23"> Jacob begat Joseph, the husband of Mary, of whom Jesus was born, that is called Christ (or who is called the Messiah).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 1:11–12; 2ND KI 24:14; MATTHEW 11:2"> And so all the generations from Abraham to David be fourteen generations, and from David to the transmigration of Babylon be fourteen generations, and from the transmigration of Babylon to Christ be fourteen generations. (And so all the generations from Abraham to David be fourteen generations, and from David to the exile to Babylon be fourteen generations, and from the exile to Babylon to the Messiah be fourteen generations.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GALATIANS 4:4–5; LUKE 1:27–38; JOB 14:4"> But the generation of Christ was thus (or But the birth of the Messiah was thus). When Mary, the mother of Jesus, was espoused to Joseph, before that they came together, she was found having of the Holy Ghost in the womb, &#91;or she was found having in the womb of the Holy Ghost&#93;, (or she was found being with child by the Holy Spirit).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:21–24; DEUTERONOMY 24:1–4; JOHN 8:4–5"> And Joseph, her husband, for he was rightful &#91;or when he was a just man&#93;, and would not publish her, he would privily have left her. (And her husband Joseph, for he was a good man, and would not make her public, he wanted to privately let her go.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:5–6; LUKE 1:19; MATTHEW 1:18"> But while he thought (on) these things, lo! the angel of the Lord appeared to him in sleep, and said, Joseph, the son of David, do not thou dread to take Mary, (to be) thy wife; for that thing that is born in her is of the Holy Ghost. (But while he thought on these things, behold! the angel of the Lord appeared to him in sleep, and said, Joseph, the son of David, do not fear to take Mary to be thy wife; for that which is born in her is by the Holy Spirit.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:12; JOHN 1:29"> And she shall bear a son, and thou shalt call his name Jesus; for he shall make his people safe from their sins (or for he shall save his people from their sins).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKE 24:44; MATTHEW 2:23; 1ST KINGS 8:15"> For all this thing was done, that it should be fulfilled, that was said of the Lord by a prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ISAIAH 7:14; ISAIAH 9:6–7; PSALMS 46:11"> Lo! a virgin shall have in &#91;the&#93; womb, and she shall bear a son, and they shall call his name Emmanuel, that is to say, God with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOHN 15:14; JAMES 2:21–26; JOHN 2:5–8"> And Joseph rose &#91;up&#93; from sleep, and did as the angel of the Lord commanded him, and took Mary, (to be) his wife;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKE 2:21; EXODUS 13:2; MATTHEW 1:21"> and he knew her not, till she had born her first begotten son, and he called his name Jesus.</span>
