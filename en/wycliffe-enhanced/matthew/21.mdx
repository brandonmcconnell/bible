<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JOHN 3:16; JOHN 3:35–36; ZEPHANIAH 3:7"> And at the last he sent his son to them, and said, They shall dread my son. <span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:29; MARK 12:9; LUKE 20:15–16"> Therefore when the lord of the vineyard shall come, what shall he do to those earth-tillers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:6; MATTHEW 21:43; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:28"> They say to him, He shall lose evil (or He shall destroy evil), (yea), the evil men, and he shall set to hire his vineyard to other earth-tillers, which shall yield to him (the) fruit in their times.</span>

<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:41; MATTHEW 8:11–12; 1ST PETER 2:9"> Therefore I say to you, that the kingdom of God shall be taken from you, and &#91;it&#93; shall be given to a folk doing &#91;the&#93; fruits of it.
