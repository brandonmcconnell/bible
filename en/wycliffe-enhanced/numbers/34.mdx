<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:8; JOSHUA 15:3–4; NUMBERS 13:26"> the which terms shall compass the south coast by the going up of the hill Scorpion, so that those terms pass into Zin, and come to the south, unto Kadeshbarnea; from whence the terms shall go forth to the town, Addar by name, and they shall stretch forth unto Azmon; (the southern border shall then go along the ascent of Mount Akrabbim, and then down through Zin, as far south as Kadeshbarnea; from there the border shall go west to Hazaraddar, and then to Azmon;)</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NUMBERS 33:37; NUMBERS 34:6; NUMBERS 34:3"> Soothly at the north coast, the terms shall begin at the great sea, and they shall come unto the highest hill, (The northern border shall begin at the Mediterranean Sea, and it shall go forth to Mount Hor,)</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:21; 2ND KI 14:25; JOSHUA 13:5–6"> from which hill those terms shall come into Hamath, unto the terms of Zedad; (from which mountain the border shall go forth to the Hamath Pass, and then to Zedad;)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 47:17"> and the coasts shall go unto Ziphron, and to the town of Enan. These shall be the terms in the north part. (and then the border shall go to Ziphron, and to Hazarenan. This shall be the northern border.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> From thence they shall mete the coasts against the east coast, from the town (of) Enan to Shepham; (From there the eastern border shall run from Hazarenan to Shepham;)</span>

</div>

Ye shall have all the land within these borders.)</span>
</div>
