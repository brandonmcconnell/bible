<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spake to Moses, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JUDGES 13:5; AMOS 2:11–12; ROMANS 1:1"> Speak thou to the sons of Israel, and thou shalt say to them, When a man either a woman maketh a vow, that they be hallowed, and they will hallow themselves to the Lord, (Say thou to the Israelites, When a man or a woman maketh a vow, that they will become a Nazarite, and they will consecrate, or will dedicate, themselves to the Lord,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 1:15; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:23; AMOS 2:12"> they shall abstain from wine, and from all thing that may make drunken; they shall not drink vinegar of wine, and of anything able to make drunken, and whatever thing is pressed out of the grape; they shall not eat fresh grapes and dry, (they shall abstain from wine, and from all things that can make them drunk; they shall not drink wine vinegar, or any other thing that is able to make them drunk, or whatever is pressed out of the grape; they shall not eat fresh grapes, or dried grapes,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 6:5; NUMBERS 6:21; NUMBERS 6:8–9"> all &#91;the&#93; days in which they be hallowed by a vow to the Lord; they shall not eat whatever thing may be of the vinery, from the rind till to the little grains that be in the midst of the grape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 1:11; JUDGES 16:17; JUDGES 13:5"> All the time of his separating, or of his avow holding, a razor shall not pass upon his head, unto the day(s) be fulfilled in which he is hallowed to the Lord; he shall be holy, and the hair of his head shall wax. (All the time of his separation, or of the keeping of his vow, a razor shall not pass over his head, until the days be fulfilled in which he is consecrated, or is dedicated, to the Lord; he shall be holy, and his head hair shall grow.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:11; LEVITICUS 19:28; LEVITICUS 21:1–3"> In all the time of his hallowing (or In all the time of his consecration, or his dedication), he shall not enter upon a dead body,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NUMBERS 9:6; LEVITICUS 21:1–2; LEVITICUS 21:10–12"> and soothly he shall not be defouled upon the dead body of his father and of his mother, of brother and of sister, for the hallowing of his God is upon his head; (yea, he shall not even be defiled with the dead body of his own father or his mother, or his brother or his sister, for the consecration of his God is upon his head;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 6:17–18"> each day of his separating, or avowing, shall be holy to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="NUMBERS 6:18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:18; LEVITICUS 14:9"> But if any man is dead suddenly before him, the head of his hallowing shall be defouled, which he shall shave anon in the same day of his cleansing, and again in the seventh day; (But if anyone is suddenly dead before him, the hair of his consecration shall be defiled, and he shall shave it off on the day of his cleansing, that is, on the seventh day afterward;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:29; LEVITICUS 1:14; LEVITICUS 15:14"> forsooth in the eighth day he shall offer two turtles, either two birds of a culver, to the priest, in the entering of the bond of peace of witnessing. (then on the eighth day, he shall offer two turtledoves, or two young pigeons, to the priest, at the entrance to the Tabernacle of the Covenant, that is, the Tabernacle of the Witnessing.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:30–31; LEVITICUS 5:8–10"> And the priest shall make, or offer, one for sin, and the tother into burnt sacrifice; and the priest shall pray for him, for he sinned upon a dead body, and he shall hallow his head in that day. (And the priest shall offer one of the birds for a sin offering, and the other for a burnt sacrifice, and so the priest shall make amends for him, for he sinned through contact with a dead body; and then he shall consecrate his head again on that day.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:6; MATTHEW 24:13; LEVITICUS 14:24"> And he shall hallow to the Lord the days of his separating, and he shall offer a lamb of one year for his sin, so nevertheless that the former days be made void, for his hallowing is defouled. (And he shall rededicate the days of his separation to the Lord, and he shall offer a one-year-old lamb for his sin, nevertheless the former days shall be made void, for the hair of his consecration was defiled.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:26"> This is the law of hallowing. When the days shall be fulfilled, which he deemed to fulfill by a vow, the priest shall bring him to the door of the tabernacle of &#91;the&#93; bond of peace, (This is the law of consecration, or of dedication or This is the law for the Nazarite. And when the days shall be fulfilled, which he committed to fulfill by a vow, the priest shall bring him to the entrance of the Tabernacle of the Covenant,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:6; LEVITICUS 4:32; LEVITICUS 4:2–3"> and he shall offer his offering to the Lord, a lamb of one year without wem, into burnt sacrifice, and a sheep of one year without wem, for sin, and a ram without wem, (as) a peaceable sacrifice; (and he shall offer his offering to the Lord, a one-year-old lamb without blemish, or without fault, for a burnt sacrifice, and a one-year-old sheep without blemish, for a sin offering, and a ram without blemish, for a peace offering;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:4; EXODUS 29:2; NUMBERS 15:10"> also a basket of therf loaves, that be sprinkled (al)together with oil, and cakes sodden in water, and after anointed with oil, without sourdough, and &#91;the&#93; flowing sacrifices of all these by themselves; (and a basket of unleavened bread, that is sprinkled with oil, and cakes made without yeast, boiled in water, and anointed with oil, and the grain and wine offerings for all of these;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> which the priest shall offer before the Lord, and he shall make, or offer these, as well for sin as into burnt sacrifice. (which the priest shall offer before the Lord, and he shall offer these for his sin offering, and for his burnt sacrifice.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Soothly he shall offer the ram (as) a peaceable sacrifice to the Lord, and he shall offer therewith a basket of therf loaves, and flowing sacrifices, that be due by custom. (And he shall offer the ram as a peace offering to the Lord, and he shall offer a basket of unleavened bread with it, and the grain and wine offerings, that be due by custom.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:24; NUMBERS 6:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:18"> Then the Nazarite, or he that is hallowed, shall be shaven from the hair of his hallowing, before the door of the tabernacle of &#91;the&#93; bond of peace; and the priest shall take his hairs, and he shall put them upon the fire, which is put under the sacrifice of peaceable things. (Then the Nazarite, that is, he who is consecrated, shall shave off the hair of his consecration, at the entrance to the Tabernacle of the Covenant; and the priest shall take his hair, and he shall put it on the fire which is under the peace offering.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 2:15; LEVITICUS 7:30; EXODUS 29:23–28"> And he shall take the shoulder sodden of the ram, and one therf cake from the basket, and one &#91;thin&#93; therf cake first sodden in water and afterward fried in oil, and he shall betake them into the hands of the Nazarite, after that his head is shaven. (And he shall take the boiled shoulder of the ram, and one unleavened cake from the basket, and one thin unleavened wafer first boiled in water and then fried in oil, and he shall put them into the hands of the Nazarite, after that he hath shaved his head.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:7; EXODUS 29:27–28; NUMBERS 5:25"> And the priest shall raise (up) in the sight of the Lord the things taken again of him. And those things hallowed shall be the priest’s part, as the breast which is commanded to be separated, and the hip. After these things the Nazarite may (again) drink wine. (And then the priest shall take these things from him, and raise them up as a special gift before the Lord. And these consecrated things shall be the priest’s portion, including the special gift of the breast, and the special contribution of the leg. And after these things be done, the Nazarite can drink wine again.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:16; EZRA 2:69; NUMBERS 5:29"> This is the law of the Nazarite, when he hath avowed his offering to the Lord, in the time of his consecration, or hallowing, besides these things which his hand findeth. By this that he &#91;hath&#93; avowed in soul, or in will, so he shall do, to the perfection of his hallowing. (This is the law for the Nazarite, when he hath vowed his offering to the Lord, at the time of his consecration, besides these things which his hand findeth. By this that he hath vowed with his soul, or with his will, so he shall do, to the perfection, or the completion, of his dedication.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the Lord spake to Moses and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 21:5; 1ST CHRONICLES 23:13; GENESIS 48:20"> Speak thou to Aaron and to his sons, (and say), Thus ye shall bless the sons of Israel (or Thus shall ye bless the Israelites), and ye shall say to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:23; PSALMS 121:4–7; PHILIPPIANS 4:7"> The Lord bless thee, and keep thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 80:19; PSALMS 119:135; PSALMS 31:16"> the Lord show his face to thee, and have mercy upon thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 4:6; PSALMS 29:11; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:16"> the Lord turn his cheer to thee, and give peace to thee. (the Lord turn his face toward thee, and give peace to thee.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 7:14; DANIEL 9:18–19; DEUTERONOMY 28:10"> They shall call inwardly my name on the sons of Israel, and I shall bless them. (Yea, they shall call my name upon, or over, the Israelites, and I shall bless them.)</span>
