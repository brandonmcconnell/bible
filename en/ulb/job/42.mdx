<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered Yahweh and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Mark 10:27; Genesis 18:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I know that you can do all things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that no purpose of yours can be stopped.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 131:1; Psalms 139:6; Job 38:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">'Who is this who without knowledge conceals plans?'</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Indeed, I have spoken things that I did not understand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">things too difficult for me to understand, which I did not know about.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 40:7; Job 38:3; Genesis 18:30–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">You said to me, 'Listen, now, and I will speak;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will ask you things, and you will tell me.'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; Romans 10:17; John 12:45"> <span className="pl pl-1">I had heard about you by my ear's hearing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but now my eye sees you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; 1 Timothy 1:13–16; James 4:7–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I despise myself;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I repent in dust and ashes.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 11:5–6; Job 32:2–3; Job 32:5"> It came about that after he had said these words to Job, Yahweh said to Eliphaz the Temanite, “My wrath is kindled against you and against your two friends, for you have not spoken of me what is right, as my servant Job has done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:1; James 5:16; Psalms 103:10"> Now therefore, take for yourselves seven bulls and seven rams, go to my servant Job, and offer up for yourselves a burnt offering. My servant Job will pray for you, and I will accept his prayer, so that I may not deal with you after your folly. You have not said what is right about me, as my servant Job has done.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Acts 9:6; Acts 10:33"> So Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite went and did as Yahweh had commanded them, and Yahweh accepted Job.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:3; Job 8:6–7; Proverbs 22:4"> When Job prayed for his friends, Yahweh restored his fortunes. Yahweh gave him twice as much as he had possessed before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 19:13–14; Proverbs 16:7"> Then all Job's brothers, and all his sisters, and all who knew him before, came to him and ate food with him in his house. They showed him sympathy and comforted him for all the disasters that Yahweh had brought upon him, and each of them gave Job a piece of silver and a ring of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 8:7; Ecclesiastes 7:8; Proverbs 10:22"> Yahweh blessed the final end of Job's life more than the first; he had fourteen thousand sheep, six thousand camels, one thousand yoke of oxen, and one thousand female donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 1:2; Psalms 107:41; Psalms 127:3"> He also had seven sons and three daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He named the first daughter Jemimah, the second Keziah, and the third Keren-Happuch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 27:7; Psalms 144:12; Acts 7:20"> In all the land no women were found as beautiful as Job's daughters. Their father gave them an inheritance along with their brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 17:6; Psalms 128:6; Genesis 50:23"> After this, Job lived 140 years; he saw his sons and his sons' sons, up to four generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Genesis 15:15; Job 5:26"> Then Job died, being old and full of days.</span>
