<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 5:11; Ezekiel 14:14; Ezekiel 14:20"> There was a man in the land of Uz whose name was Job; and Job was blameless and upright, one who feared God and turned from evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 127:3–5; Job 42:13; Psalms 128:3"> There were born to him seven sons and three daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 29:25; Job 42:12; Judges 6:3"> He possessed seven thousand sheep, three thousand camels, five hundred pairs of oxen, and five hundred donkeys and a great many servants. He was the man who was the greatest of all the people of the East.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:1; Psalms 133:1"> On each son's assigned day, he would give a feast in his house. They would send and call for their three sisters to eat and drink with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 42:8; Genesis 8:20; 1 Samuel 16:5"> When the days of the feast were over, Job would send for them and he would consecrate them. He would rise early in the morning and offer burnt offerings for each of his children, for he would say, “It may be that my children have sinned and cursed God in their hearts.” Job always did this.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 2:1; Job 38:7; Zechariah 3:1"> Then it was the day when the sons of God came to present themselves before Yahweh. Satan also came with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Revelation 12:12–17; Matthew 12:43"> Yahweh said to Satan, “From where have you come?” Then Satan answered Yahweh and said, “From wandering on the earth, from going back and forth on it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 2:3; Job 1:1; Numbers 12:7–8"> Yahweh said to Satan, “Have you considered my servant Job? For there is no one like him on the earth, a blameless and upright man, one who fears God and turns from evil.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Timothy 4:8; Job 2:10"> Then Satan answered Yahweh and said, “Does Job fear God without reason?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 34:7; Zechariah 2:5; 1 Peter 1:5"> Have you not put a barrier around him, around his house, and around all that is his from every side? You have blessed the deeds of his hands, and his cattle have burst forth in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 2:5; Job 19:21; Revelation 16:11"> But now stretch out your hand and touch all that he has, and see if he does not curse you to your face.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 22:31–32; John 3:35–36; 2 Corinthians 12:7"> Yahweh said to Satan, “Behold, all that he has is in your hand. Only against him himself do not stretch out your hand.” Then Satan went away from the presence of Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 21:34; Proverbs 27:1; Job 1:4"> It came about that on a certain day, his sons and his daughters were eating and drinking wine in their oldest brother's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:13; Jeremiah 51:31; 1 Samuel 4:17"> A messenger came to Job and said, “The oxen were plowing and the donkeys were feeding beside them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Job 6:19; Isaiah 45:14"> Then the Sabeans fell on them and took them away. As for the servants, they have struck them with the edge of the sword. I alone have escaped to tell you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 1:12; Genesis 19:24; 1 Kings 18:38"> While he was still speaking, another also came and said, “The fire of God fell from the heavens and burned up the sheep and the servants. I alone have escaped to tell you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 11:28; Habakkuk 1:6; Genesis 11:31"> While he was still speaking, another also came and said, “The Chaldeans formed three groups, attacked the camels, and have taken them away. As for the servants, they have struck them with the edge of the sword. I alone have escaped to tell you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 1:4; Job 1:13; Job 27:14"> While he was yet speaking, another also came and said, “Your sons and your daughters were eating and drinking wine in their oldest brother's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 42:36; Luke 13:1–5; Judges 16:30"> A strong wind came from the wilderness and struck the four corners of the house. It fell on the young people, and they died. I alone have escaped to tell you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 5:6; 2 Samuel 12:16–20; Genesis 37:34"> Then Job rose, tore his robe, shaved his head, lay facedown on the ground, and worshiped God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:15; 1 Timothy 6:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:18"> He said, “I was naked when I came out of my mother's womb, and I will be naked when I will return there. It is Yahweh who gave, and it is Yahweh who has taken away. May the name of Yahweh be blessed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 1:12; Job 2:10; 1 Peter 1:7"> In all this matter, Job did not sin, nor did he accuse God of wrongdoing.</span>
</div>
</div>
