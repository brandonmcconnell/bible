<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Philippians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 1:6; 1 Corinthians 4:16"> Be imitators of me, just as I am an imitator of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:15; 1 Corinthians 4:17; Proverbs 31:28–31"> Now I praise you because you remember me in everything. I praise you because you hold fast to the traditions just as I delivered them to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:16; Colossians 1:18; Colossians 3:18"> Now I want you to understand that Christ is the head of every man, that a man is the head of a woman, and that God is the head of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:30; 1 Corinthians 12:28; 1 Corinthians 12:10"> Any man who prays or prophesies with his head covered dishonors his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:12; Luke 2:36; Acts 2:17"> But any woman who prays or prophesies with her head uncovered dishonors her head. For it is the same thing as if her head were shaved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:5; Numbers 5:18"> For if a woman will not cover her head, she should cut her hair short. If it is disgraceful for a woman to have her hair cut off or for her to shave her head, let her cover her head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3; Genesis 5:1; Psalms 8:6"> For a man should not have his head covered, since he is the image and glory of God. But the woman is the glory of the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:13; Genesis 2:21–23"> For man was not made from woman. Instead, woman was made from man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 2:18; Genesis 2:23–24; Genesis 2:20"> For neither was man created for woman. Instead, woman was created for man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 24:64–65; Hebrews 1:14; Matthew 18:10"> This is why the woman ought to have a symbol of authority on her head, because of the angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; 1 Corinthians 7:10–14; 1 Corinthians 12:12–22"> Nevertheless, in the Lord, the woman is not independent from the man, nor is the man independent from the woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:36; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 1:2–3"> For as the woman comes from the man, so does the man come from the woman. And all things come from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:57; John 7:24; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> Judge for yourselves: Is it proper for a woman to pray to God with her head uncovered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:26; 1 Corinthians 14:35"> Does not even nature itself teach you that if a man has long hair, it is a disgrace for him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Does not nature teach you that if a woman has long hair, it is her glory? For her hair has been given to her as a covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Timothy 6:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 2:14"> But if anyone wants to argue about this, we do not have any other practice, nor do the churches of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; 1 Corinthians 14:23; 1 Corinthians 11:22"> But in the following instructions, I do not praise you. For when you come together, it is not for the better but for the worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 1:10–12; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> For in the first place, I hear that when you come together in the church, there are divisions among you, and in part I believe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 2:19; Titus 3:10; Acts 20:30"> For there must also be factions among you, so that those who are approved may be recognized among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For when you come together, it is not the Lord's Supper that you eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:13; Jude 1:12; 1 Corinthians 11:23–25"> When you eat, each one eats his own food before the others have their meal. One is hungry, and another becomes drunk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 20:28; Proverbs 17:5; James 2:5–6"> Do you not have houses to eat and to drink in? Do you despise the church of God and humiliate those who have nothing? What should I say to you? Should I praise you? I will not praise you for this!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:19–20; Matthew 26:26–28; 1 Corinthians 11:23–25"> For I received from the Lord what I also passed on to you, that the Lord Jesus, on the night when he was betrayed, took bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:53–58; 1 Corinthians 11:27–28; 1 Corinthians 10:3–4"> After he had given thanks, he broke it and said, “This is my body, which is for you. Do this to remember me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 22:20; Hebrews 9:15–20; Hebrews 13:20"> In the same way he took the cup after supper, and he said, “This cup is the new covenant in my blood. Do this as often as you drink it, to remember me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Revelation 1:7; John 14:3"> For every time you eat this bread and drink this cup, you proclaim the Lord's death until he comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 6:51; 1 Corinthians 10:21; Hebrews 10:29"> Whoever, therefore, eats the bread or drinks the cup of the Lord in an unworthy manner, will be guilty of the body and the blood of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 6:4; 2 Corinthians 13:5; Lamentations 3:40"> Let a person examine himself first, and in this way let him eat of the bread and drink of the cup.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:27; 1 Corinthians 11:30; 1 Corinthians 11:32–34"> For he who eats and drinks without discerning the body, eats and drinks judgment to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Numbers 20:12; Amos 3:2"> That is why many among you are weak and ill, and some of you have fallen asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 1:9; Psalms 32:3–5; Revelation 2:5"> But if we examine ourselves, we will not be judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5–11; Psalms 118:18; Proverbs 3:11–12"> But when we are judged by the Lord, we are disciplined, so that we may not be condemned along with the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Therefore, my brothers, when you come together to eat, wait for one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:21–22; 1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Corinthians 4:19"> If anyone is hungry, let him eat at home, so that when you come together it will not be for judgment. And about the other things you wrote, I will give directions when I come.</span>
