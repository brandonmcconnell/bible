<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:39; 1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Corinthians 12:1"> Pursue love and be zealous for spiritual gifts, especially that you may prophesy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Acts 19:6; Acts 10:46"> For the one who speaks in a tongue does not speak to people but to God. For no one understands him because he speaks hidden things in the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:12; 1 Timothy 4:13; Jude 1:20"> But the one who prophesies speaks to people to build them up, to encourage them, and to comfort them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:22; 1 Corinthians 14:26; Mark 16:17"> The one who speaks in a tongue builds up himself, but the one who prophesies builds up the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:28–30; 1 Corinthians 12:10; Mark 16:17"> Now I wish that you all spoke in tongues. But even more than that, I wish that you would prophesy. The one who prophesies is greater than the one who speaks in tongues (unless someone interprets so that the church may be built up).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17; Romans 6:17; 2 Timothy 3:16"> But now, brothers, if I come to you speaking in tongues, how will I benefit you? I cannot, unless I speak to you with revelation, or knowledge, or prophecy, or teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:8; Luke 7:32; 1 Corinthians 13:1"> If lifeless instruments are producing sounds—like the flute or the harp—and they do not produce different tones, how will anyone know what tune the flute or harp is playing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 10:9; Ezekiel 33:3–6; Jeremiah 4:19"> For if the trumpet is played with an uncertain sound, how will anyone know when it is time to prepare for battle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:26"> So it is with you. If you utter speech that is unintelligible, how will anyone understand what you have said? You will be speaking, and no one will understand you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> There are doubtless many different languages in the world, and none is without meaning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 28:2; Romans 1:14; Colossians 3:11"> But if I do not know the meaning of a language, I will be a foreigner to the speaker, and the speaker will be a foreigner to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:26; 1 Corinthians 14:3–4; 1 Corinthians 14:1"> So it is with you. Since you are eager for the manifestations of the Spirit, try to excel in the gifts that build up the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 11:24; 1 Corinthians 12:30; John 14:13–14"> So the one who speaks in a tongue should pray that he may interpret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:2; 1 Corinthians 14:15–16; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> For if I pray in a tongue, my spirit prays, but my mind is unfruitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; John 4:23–24; Ephesians 6:18"> What am I to do? I will pray with my spirit, but I will also pray with my mind. I will sing with my spirit, and I will also sing with my mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:36; Psalms 106:48; Revelation 5:14"> Otherwise, if you praise God with the spirit, how will the outsider say “Amen” when you are giving thanks if he does not know what you are saying?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:4; 1 Corinthians 14:6"> For you certainly give thanks well enough, but the other person is not built up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:4–6; 1 Corinthians 4:7"> I thank God that I speak in tongues more than all of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:21–22; 1 Corinthians 14:4"> But in the church I would rather speak five words with my understanding so that I might instruct others, than ten thousand words in a tongue.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:2; 1 Corinthians 13:11; Romans 16:19"> Brothers, do not be children in your thinking. Rather, in regard to evil, be like infants. But in your thinking be mature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 28:11–12; Jeremiah 5:15; Deuteronomy 28:49"> In the law it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“By men of strange tongues and by the lips of strangers</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will speak to this people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Even then they will not hear me,”</span><br />says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 2:6–12; Mark 16:17; 1 Corinthians 14:1"> So then, tongues are a sign, not to believers, but to unbelievers. But prophesying is for a sign, not for unbelievers, but for believers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 2:13; Acts 26:24; 1 Corinthians 11:18"> If, therefore, the whole church comes together and all speak in tongues, and outsiders and unbelievers come in, would they not say that you are insane?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 2:37; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Hebrews 4:12–13"> But if you all were prophesying and an unbeliever or an outsider came in, he would be convicted by all he hears. He would be judged by all that is said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 45:14; Zechariah 8:23; Psalms 72:11"> The secrets of his heart would be revealed. As a result, he would fall on his face and worship God. He would declare that God is really among you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 5:19; Romans 14:19; 1 Corinthians 12:7–10"> What is next then, brothers? When you come together, each one has a psalm, a teaching, a revelation, a tongue, or an interpretation. Do everything so that you build up the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:26"> If anyone speaks in a tongue, let there be two or at most three, and each one in turn, then someone should interpret what is said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> But if there is no one to interpret, let each of them keep silent in the church. Let each one speak to himself alone and to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:19–21; 1 John 4:1–3"> Let two or three prophets speak, and let the others listen with discernment to what is said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:19–20; Job 33:31–33; Job 32:11"> But if an insight is given to one who is sitting in the service, let the one who had been speaking be silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Corinthians 14:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> For each of you can prophesy one by one so that each one may learn and all may be encouraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 4:19–20; 1 John 4:1; 1 Samuel 10:10–13"> For the spirits of the prophets are under the control of the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 3:17–18; 1 Corinthians 14:40; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> For God is not a God of confusion, but of peace.<br />This is the rule in all the churches of the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:11–12; Genesis 3:16; Numbers 30:3–13"> The women should keep silent in the churches. For they are not permitted to speak. Instead, they should be in submission, as also the law says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; 1 Peter 3:7; Ephesians 5:25–27"> If there is anything they desire to learn, let them ask their husbands at home. For it is disgraceful for a woman to speak in the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 18:1–17; Zechariah 14:8; Acts 17:15"> Did the word of God come from you? Are you the only ones it has reached?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:7; 2 Peter 3:2"> If anyone thinks himself to be a prophet or spiritual, he should acknowledge that the things I write to you are a command of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Hosea 4:17; Revelation 22:11–12"> But if anyone does not recognize this, let him not be recognized.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Most versions, including the ULB and UDB, read, But if anyone does not recognize this, let him not be recognized . Some older versions read, But if anyone is ignorant of this, let him be ignorant .">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:31; 1 Corinthians 14:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:20"> So then, brothers, earnestly desire to prophesy, and do not forbid anyone from speaking in tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Colossians 2:5; Titus 1:5; 1 Corinthians 14:26–33"> But let all things be done properly and in order.</span>
</div>
