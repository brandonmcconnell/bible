<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:17; 1 Corinthians 2:13; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> When I came to you, brothers, I did not come with eloquence of speech or wisdom as I proclaimed hidden truths about God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Many other versions read, as I proclaimed the testimony about God .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 6:14; John 17:3; 1 Corinthians 1:22–25"> For I decided to know nothing when I was among you except Jesus Christ, and him crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:4; 1 Corinthians 4:10–13; 2 Corinthians 12:5–10"> And I was with you in weakness, and in fear, and in much trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:20; 1 Thessalonians 1:5; 1 Corinthians 1:17"> And my message and my proclamation were not with persuasive words of wisdom. Instead, they were with the demonstration of the Spirit and of power,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:7; 2 Corinthians 12:9; 2 Corinthians 6:7"> so that your faith might not be in the wisdom of humans, but in the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 5:14; James 3:15; 2 Corinthians 4:4"> Now we do speak wisdom among the mature, but not the wisdom of this world, or of the rulers of this age, who are passing away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 1:26–27; 2 Peter 1:3; Matthew 11:25"> Instead, we speak God's wisdom in hidden truth, the hidden wisdom that God predestined before the ages for our glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:27; James 2:1; Acts 7:2"> None of the rulers of this age understood it, for if they had understood it, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Psalms 31:19; James 1:12"> But as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Things that no eye has seen,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">no ear has heard,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">no mind has imagined,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the things that God has prepared for those who love him.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 14:26; Ephesians 3:5; Luke 10:21"> These are the things that God has revealed to us through the Spirit. For the Spirit searches everything, even the deep things of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:27; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Romans 11:33–34"> For who knows a person's thoughts except the spirit of the person in him? So also, no one knows the deep things of God except the Spirit of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 2:27; Romans 8:5–6; 1 John 2:20"> But we did not receive the spirit of the world, but the Spirit who is from God, so that we might know the things freely given to us by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4; 1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 1:17"> We speak about these things in words that man's wisdom cannot teach, but which the Spirit teaches us. The Spirit interprets spiritual words with spiritual wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:18; Romans 8:5–8; Matthew 13:11–17"> The unspiritual person does not receive the things that belong to the Spirit of God, for they are foolishness to him. He cannot know them because they are spiritually discerned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Proverbs 28:5; Hebrews 5:14"> The one who is spiritual judges all things, but he is not subject to the judgment of others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34; John 16:13–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">“For who can know the mind of the Lord, that he can instruct him?”</span>But we have the mind of Christ.</span>
