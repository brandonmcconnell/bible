<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 4:32; Leviticus 23:24; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> It was the seventh month after the people of Israel came back to their cities, when they gathered together as one man in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:17; Ezra 2:2; Haggai 1:1"> Jeshua son of Jozadak and his brothers the priests, and Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, and his brothers rose up and built the altar of the God of Israel to offer burnt offerings as it is written in the law of Moses the man of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 4:4; Numbers 28:2–8; Ezra 8:21–22"> Then they established the altar on its stand, for dread was on them because of the people of the land. They offered burnt offerings to Yahweh at dawn and evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:14–17; Numbers 29:12–38; Exodus 23:16"> They also observed the Festival of Shelters as it is written and offered burnt offerings day by day according to the decree, each day's duty on its day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 29:39; Numbers 28:19; Exodus 29:38–42"> Accordingly, there were daily burnt offerings and monthly ones and offerings for all the fixed feasts of Yahweh, along with all the freewill offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> They began to offer up burnt offerings to Yahweh on the first day of the seventh month, although the temple had not been founded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 5:6; Acts 9:36; Acts 12:20"> So they gave silver to the stoneworkers and craftsmen, and they gave food, drink, and oil to the people of Sidon and Tyre, so they would bring cedar trees by sea from Lebanon to Joppa, as authorized for them by Cyrus, king of Persia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 4:3; 1 Chronicles 23:4; 1 Chronicles 23:24–32"> Then in the second month of the second year after they came to the house of God in Jerusalem, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, Jeshua son of Jozadak, the rest of the priests, the Levites, and those who came from captivity back to Jerusalem began the work. They assigned the Levites twenty years old and older to oversee the work of Yahweh's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezra 2:40; Ezra 3:8; Nehemiah 7:43"> Jeshua and his sons and brothers, and Kadmiel and his sons, who were descendants of Hodaviah), and the sons of Henadad and their sons and brothers-all of them were Levites-joined together in supervising those working on the house of God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="There are some textual issues here regarding the phrase the sons of Judah or the descendants of Hodviah and other matters.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:1–7; 1 Chronicles 15:27; 1 Chronicles 16:37"> The builders laid a foundation for Yahweh's temple. This enabled the priests to stand in their garments with trumpets, and the Levites, sons of Asaph, to praise Yahweh with cymbals, just as the hand of David, king of Israel had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:34; Psalms 106:1; Psalms 107:1"> They sang with praise and thankfulness to Yahweh, “He is good! His covenant faithfulness to Israel endures forever.” All the people cried out with a great shout of joy in praise of Yahweh because the temple's foundations had been laid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Haggai 2:3; Matthew 13:31–32; Isaiah 60:22"> But many of the priests, Levites, and chief patriarchs, those who were old and had seen the first house, when this house's foundations were laid before their eyes, wept loudly. But many people had shouts of joy with gladness and an excited sound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 19:37–40; 1 Samuel 4:5; Nehemiah 12:43"> As a result, people were not able to distinguish the joyful and glad sounds from the sound of people weeping, for the people were crying out with great joy, and the sound was heard from far away.</span>
