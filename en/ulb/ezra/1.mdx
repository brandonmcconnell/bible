<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; 2 Chronicles 36:22–23; Jeremiah 25:12–14"> In the first year of Cyrus, king of Persia, Yahweh fulfilled his word that came from the mouth of Jeremiah, and stirred Cyrus's spirit. Cyrus' voice went out over his entire kingdom. This is what was written and spoken:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 45:12–13; Isaiah 44:26–1; Daniel 5:23"> “Cyrus, king of Persia, says: Yahweh, God of Heaven, gave me all the kingdoms of the earth, and he appointed me to build for him a house in Jerusalem in Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 6:26; Jeremiah 10:10; Isaiah 45:5"> Whoever is from his people (may his God be with him) may go up to Jerusalem and build a house for Yahweh, the God of Israel, the God who is in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:3; Ezra 7:16–18; Ezra 2:68–70"> People of any part of the kingdom where survivors of that land are living should provide them with silver and gold, possessions and animals, as well as a freewill offering for God's house in Jerusalem.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezra 1:1; 2 Chronicles 36:22; Philippians 2:13"> Then the heads of the ancestors' clans of Judah and Benjamin, the priests and Levites, and everyone whose spirit God stirred to go and build his house arose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Ezra 1:4; Ezra 8:33"> Those around them supported their work with silver and gold objects, possessions, animals, valuables, and freewill offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:7; Ezra 5:14; 2 Kings 24:13"> Cyrus king of Persia also released the objects belonging to Yahweh's house that Nebuchadnezzar had brought from Jerusalem and put in his own gods' houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 5:14; Zechariah 4:6–10; Ezra 1:11"> Cyrus put them into the hand of Mithredath the treasurer, who counted them out for Sheshbazzar, Judea's leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 7:50; Numbers 7:19–89; 2 Chronicles 4:8"> This was their number: thirty gold basins, one thousand silver basins, twenty-nine other basins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> thirty gold bowls, 410 small silver bowls, and one thousand additional objects.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:19–21; Romans 9:23; Matthew 1:11–12"> There were 5,400 gold and silver items in all. Sheshbazzar brought all of them when the exiles went from Babylon to Jerusalem.</span>
