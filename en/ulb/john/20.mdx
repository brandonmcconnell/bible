<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:23; John 20:13; John 21:24"> So she ran and came to Simon Peter and to the other disciple whom Jesus loved, and she said to them, “They took away the Lord out from the tomb, and we do not know where they have laid him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:12"> Then Peter and the other disciple went out, and they went to the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12; 1 Corinthians 9:24"> They both ran together, and the other disciple quickly ran ahead of Peter and arrived at the tomb first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:40; John 20:11; John 11:44"> Then stooping down, he saw the linen cloths lying there, but he did not go inside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:17; John 21:7; Matthew 16:15–16"> Simon Peter then arrived after him and went into the tomb. He saw the linen cloths lying there</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 11:44"> and the cloth that had been on his head. It was not lying with the linen cloths but was folded up in a place by itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 20:25; John 20:4; John 1:50"> Then the other disciple, the one who first arrived at the tomb, also went in, and he saw and believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Isaiah 25:8; Isaiah 53:10–12"> For until that time they still did not know the scripture that he should rise from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 7:53; John 16:32"> So the disciples went back home again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:5"> But Mary was standing outside the tomb weeping. As she wept, she stooped down into the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:3–7; Revelation 3:4; Mark 16:5–6"> She saw two angels in white sitting, one at the head, and one at the foot of where the body of Jesus had lain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:15; John 2:4; 1 Samuel 1:8"> They said to her, “Woman, why are you weeping?” She said to them, “Because they took away my Lord, and I do not know where they have put him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 21:4; Matthew 28:9"> When she said this, she turned around and saw Jesus standing there, but she did not know that it was Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 20:13; John 18:7; John 1:38"> Jesus said to her, “Woman, why are you weeping? Whom are you looking for?” She thought that he was the gardener, so she said to him, “Sir, if you have taken him away, tell me where you have put him, and I will take him away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:3; John 1:38; Acts 10:3"> Jesus said to her, “Mary.” She turned, and said to him in Hebrew, “Rabboni” (which means “Teacher”).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:28; Ephesians 4:8–10; Galatians 4:6–7"> Jesus said to her, “Do not touch me, for I have not yet gone up to the Father, but go to my brothers and say to them that I will go up to my Father and your Father, and my God and your God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 24:10; Matthew 28:10; John 20:1"> Mary Magdalene came and told the disciples, “I have seen the Lord,” and that he had said these things to her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 20:26; John 14:27; John 20:21"> When it was evening, on that day, the first day of the week, and the doors of where the disciples were, were closed for fear of the Jews, Jesus came and stood in the middle of them and said to them, “Peace to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 16:22; John 16:20; John 20:27"> After he said this, he showed them his hands and his side. The disciples rejoiced when they saw the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 17:18–19; John 13:20; John 3:17"> Jesus then said to them again, “Peace to you. As the Father has sent me, so I am sending you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 33:4; Genesis 2:7; Acts 8:15"> When Jesus had said this, he breathed on them and said to them, “Receive the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 18:18; Matthew 16:19; Mark 2:5–10"> Whoever's sins you forgive, they are forgiven; whoever's sins you keep back, they are kept back.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 11:16; John 6:66–67; Hebrews 10:25"> Thomas, one of the twelve, called Didymus, was not with them when Jesus came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:30; Mark 16:11; Hebrews 3:12"> The other disciples later said to him, “We have seen the Lord.” He said to them, “Unless I see in his hands the mark of the nails, and put my finger into the mark of the nails, and put my hand into his side, I will not believe.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 20:19; Isaiah 54:10; John 20:21"> After eight days his disciples were inside again, and Thomas was with them. Jesus came while the doors were closed, and stood among them, and said, “Peace to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 20:25; 1 John 1:1–2; John 20:20"> Then he said to Thomas, “Reach here with your finger and see my hands. Reach here with your hand and put it into my side. Do not be unbelieving, but believe.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:1; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 7:14"> Thomas answered and said to him, “My Lord and my God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Hebrews 11:1"> Jesus said to him, “Because you have seen me, you have believed. Blessed are those who have not seen, and believed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 21:25; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Romans 15:4"> Now Jesus did many other signs in the presence of the disciples, signs that have not been written in this book,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 10:43; John 3:15–16; 1 John 5:1"> but these have been written so that you would believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and so that believing, you would have life in his name.</span>
</div>
