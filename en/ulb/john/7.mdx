<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 5:16–18; John 4:3; Matthew 21:38"> After these things Jesus traveled about in Galilee, for he did not want to go into Judea because the Jews were seeking to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:16–19; Deuteronomy 16:13–16; Exodus 23:16–17"> Now the Jewish Festival of Shelters was near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; John 7:5; Luke 8:19"> His brothers therefore said to him, “Leave this place and go to Judea, so that your disciples also may see the works that you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:20; Proverbs 18:1–2; Matthew 23:5"> No one does anything in secret if he himself wants to be known openly. If you do these things, show yourself to the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 3:21; John 1:11–13; Micah 7:5–6"> For even his brothers did not believe in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 7:8; John 2:4; John 7:30"> Jesus therefore said to them, “My time has not yet come, but your time is always ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 3:19; Galatians 4:16"> The world cannot hate you, but it hates me because I testify about it that its works are evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:6; John 8:20; John 7:30"> You go up to the festival; I am not going to this festival because my time has not yet been fulfilled.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> After he said these things to them, he stayed in Galilee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Matthew 10:16; Isaiah 42:2–3"> But when his brothers had gone up to the festival, then he also went up, not publicly but in secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:56"> The Jews were looking for him at the festival and said, “Where is he?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 7:40–43; John 9:16; John 7:32"> There was much discussion among the crowds about him. Some said, “He is a good man.” Others said, “No, he leads the crowds astray.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 9:22; John 19:38; John 20:19"> Yet no one spoke openly about him for fear of the Jews.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:28; Luke 19:47; John 7:37"> When the festival was already half over, Jesus went up into the temple and began to teach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54"> Then the Jews marveled, saying, “How does this man know so much? He has never been educated.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:10; John 14:24; John 17:8"> Jesus answered them and said, “My teaching is not mine, but is of him who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:31–32; John 8:43; Jeremiah 31:33–34"> If anyone wishes to do his will, he will know about this teaching, whether it comes from God, or whether I speak from myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 5:41; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33; John 13:31–32"> Whoever speaks from himself seeks his own glory, but whoever seeks the glory of him who sent him, that person is true, and there is no unrighteousness in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 1:17; John 11:53; Matthew 12:14"> Did not Moses give you the law? Yet none of you keeps the law. Why do you seek to kill me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:20; John 8:48; John 8:52"> The crowd answered, “You have a demon. Who seeks to kill you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:23; John 5:2–11"> Jesus answered and said to them, “I did one work, and you all marvel because of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Genesis 17:10–14; Romans 4:9–11"> Moses gave you circumcision (not that it is from Moses, but from the ancestors), and on the Sabbath you circumcise a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:5; John 5:14–16; Matthew 12:2"> If a man receives circumcision on the Sabbath so that the law of Moses is not broken, why are you angry with me because I made a man completely healthy on the Sabbath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–4; James 2:9; John 8:15"> Do not judge according to appearance, but judge righteously.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 7:10–11"> Some of them from Jerusalem said, “Is not this the one they seek to kill?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7–8; John 7:48; John 12:42"> See, he speaks openly, and they say nothing to him. It cannot be that the rulers indeed know that this is the Christ, can it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:41–42; John 6:42"> Yet we know where this one is from. But when the Christ comes, no one will know where he is from.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:42; John 8:19; John 8:26"> Then Jesus cried out in the temple, teaching and saying, “You both know me and know where I come from. I have not come of myself, but he who sent me is true, and you do not know him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:55; Matthew 11:27; John 17:18"> I know him because I come from him and he sent me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 8:20; John 10:39; John 7:6"> They were trying to arrest him, but no one laid a hand on him because his hour had not yet come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:42; Matthew 12:23; John 10:41–42"> But many in the crowd believed in him, and they said, “When the Christ comes, will he do more signs than what this one has done?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 18:3; Luke 22:52–53; John 11:47–48"> The Pharisees heard the crowds whispering these things about Jesus, and the chief priests and the Pharisees sent officers to arrest him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 16:5; John 13:33; Mark 16:19"> Jesus then said, “I am still with you for a short amount of time, and then I go to him who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; John 13:33–36; John 14:6"> You will seek me but you will not find me; where I go, you will not be able to come.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="James 1:1; Isaiah 11:12; Zephaniah 3:10"> The Jews therefore said among themselves, “Where will this man go that we will not be able to find him? Will he go to the dispersion among the Greeks and teach the Greeks?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 3:9; John 7:34; John 12:34"> What is this word that he said, 'You will seek me but will not find me; where I go, you will not be able to come'?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 55:1; John 4:14; John 6:35"> Now on the last, great day of the festival, Jesus stood and cried out, saying, “If anyone is thirsty, let him come to me and drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 44:3; Isaiah 58:11"> He who believes in me, just as the scripture says, rivers of living water will flow from his stomach.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 2:17; Joel 2:28; John 14:16–17"> But he said this about the Spirit, whom those who believed in him would receive; the Spirit had not yet been given because Jesus was not yet glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; John 1:21; John 6:14"> Some of the crowd, when they heard these words, said, “This is indeed the prophet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:52; John 1:46; John 1:49"> Others said, “This is the Christ.” But some said, “Does the Christ come from Galilee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:5; Luke 2:4"> Have the scriptures not said that the Christ will come from the descendants of David and from Bethlehem, the village where David was?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 7:12"> So there arose a division in the crowds because of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 7:30; Acts 23:11; John 8:20"> Some of them would have arrested him, but no one laid hands on him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 7:32; Acts 5:21–27"> Then the officers came back to the chief priests and Pharisees, who said to them, “Why did you not bring him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 7:28–29; Luke 4:22; John 7:26"> The officers answered, “Never has anyone spoken like this.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 7:12; 2 Corinthians 6:8; 2 Kings 18:32"> So the Pharisees answered them, “Have you also been deceived?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 12:42; 1 Corinthians 1:20; 1 Corinthians 2:8"> Have any of the rulers believed in him, or any of the Pharisees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20–21; John 9:40; John 9:34"> But this crowd that does not know the law, they are cursed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:39; John 3:1–2"> Nicodemus (one of the Pharisees, who came to him earlier) said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Deuteronomy 1:17; Deuteronomy 19:15–19"> “Does our law judge a man before hearing from him and knowing what he does?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 7:41; John 1:46; Isaiah 9:1–2"> They answered and said to him, “Are you also from Galilee? Search and see that no prophet comes from Galilee.”</span>
</div>
