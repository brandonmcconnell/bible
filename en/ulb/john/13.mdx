<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 12:23; 1 John 4:19; John 13:34"> Now it was before the Festival of the Passover. Jesus knew that his hour had come to go out of this world and go to the Father. Having loved his own who were in the world, he loved them to the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:13–17; Acts 5:3; John 13:26–27"> Now the devil had already put it into the heart of Judas Iscariot son of Simon, to betray Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 3:35; Matthew 11:27; Hebrews 1:2"> He knew that the Father had given everything over into his hands and that he had come from God and was going back to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 22:27; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Luke 12:37"> He got up from dinner and took off his outer clothing. Then he took a towel and wrapped it around himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:44; Psalms 51:2; Genesis 18:4"> Then he poured water into a basin and began to wash the feet of the disciples and dry them with the towel that he had put around himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 3:11–14; John 1:27; Luke 5:8"> He came to Simon Peter, and Peter said to him, “Lord, are you going to wash my feet?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:8; John 12:16; John 14:26"> Jesus answered and said to him, “What I am doing you do not understand now, but you will understand this later.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25; Acts 22:16; Titus 3:5"> Peter said to him, “You will never wash my feet.” Jesus answered him, “If I do not wash you, you have no share with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 51:7; Psalms 51:2; 1 Peter 3:21"> Simon Peter said to him, “Lord, do not only wash my feet, but also my hands and my head.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:3; 2 Corinthians 7:1; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> Jesus said to him, “He who is bathed has no need, except to wash his feet, but he is completely clean; you are clean, but not everyone.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 13:26; John 13:2; John 13:21"> (For Jesus knew who would betray him; that is why he said, “Not all of you are clean.”)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 13:7; John 13:4; Ezekiel 24:24"> So when Jesus had washed their feet and taken his garments and sat down again, he said to them, “Do you know what I have done for you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 6:46; 1 Corinthians 12:3; John 11:28"> You call me 'teacher' and 'Lord,' and you are speaking correctly, because so I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Acts 20:35; Romans 12:16"> If I then, the Lord and the Teacher, have washed your feet, you should also wash the feet of one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 11:29; 1 Peter 3:17–18"> For I have given you an example so that you should also do just as I did for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:40; John 15:20; Matthew 10:24–25"> Truly, truly, I say to you, a servant is not greater than his master; nor is a messenger greater than he who sent him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 11:28; James 4:17; James 1:25"> If you know these things, you are blessed if you do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Matthew 26:23; John 17:12"> I am not speaking about all of you; I know those whom I have chosen—but this so that the scripture will be fulfilled: 'He who eats my bread lifted up his heel against me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; John 14:29; John 16:4"> I tell you this now before it happens so that when it happens, you may believe that I AM.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Luke 9:48; Matthew 25:40"> Truly, truly, I say to you, he who receives whomever I sent, receives me, and he who receives me, receives him who sent me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 13:18; Matthew 26:21; Mark 14:18"> When Jesus said this, he was troubled in spirit. He testified and said, “Truly, truly, I say to you that one of you will betray me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 22:23; Matthew 26:22; Mark 14:19"> The disciples looked at each other, wondering of whom he was speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 19:26; John 21:7; John 21:20"> One of his disciples, whom Jesus loved, was lying down at the table against Jesus' side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Luke 1:22; Acts 13:16"> Simon Peter motioned to this disciple and said, “Ask him who he is speaking about.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 21:20; Genesis 44:4–12; Esther 7:5"> So he leaned back against the side of Jesus and said to him, “Lord, who is it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 26:23; John 13:30; Mark 14:19–20"> Then Jesus answered, “It is the one for whom I will dip the piece of bread and give it him.” So when he had dipped the bread, he gave it to Judas son of Simon Iscariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:3; John 13:2; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> Then after the bread, Satan entered into him, so Jesus said to him, “What you are doing, do it quickly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Now no one who was lying down at the table knew why he said this to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 12:5–6; John 13:1; Ephesians 4:28"> Some thought that, since Judas had the moneybag, Jesus said to him, “Buy what we need to have for the festival,” or that he should give something to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Job 24:13–15; Proverbs 4:16"> After Judas received the bread, he went out immediately. It was night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 14:13; 1 Peter 4:11; John 7:39"> When Judas was gone, Jesus said, “Now the Son of Man is glorified, and God is glorified in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Isaiah 53:10–12; John 17:4–6"> God will glorify him in himself, and he will glorify him immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 7:33–34; John 8:21–24; John 16:16–22"> Little children, I am with you for still a short amount of time. You will look for me, and as I said to the Jews, 'Where I am going, you cannot come.' Now I also say this to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Leviticus 19:18; John 15:17"> I am giving you a new commandment, that you should love one another; as I have loved you, so also you should love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 4:20–21; 1 John 3:10–14; 1 John 2:10"> By this everyone will know that you are my disciples, if you have love one for another.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Peter 1:14; John 13:33; John 21:21–22"> Simon Peter said to him, “Lord, where are you going?” Jesus answered, “Where I am going, you cannot follow me now, but you will follow later.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 21:13; Acts 20:24; Mark 14:27–31"> Peter said to him, “Lord, why can I not follow you now? I will lay down my life for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 22:34; Matthew 26:34; Mark 14:30"> Jesus answered, “Will you lay down your life for me? Truly, truly, I say to you, the rooster will not crow before you have denied me three times.”</span>
