<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 14:3–8; John 11:1; Luke 24:50"> Six days before the Passover, Jesus came to Bethany, where Lazarus, whom Jesus had raised from the dead, was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 22:27; Luke 10:38–42; Revelation 3:20"> So they made him a dinner there, and Martha was serving, but Lazarus was one of those who was lying down at the table with Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; John 11:2; Luke 10:38–39"> Then Mary took a litra of perfume made of very precious pure nard, anointed the feet of Jesus with it, and wiped his feet with her hair. The house was filled with the fragrance of the perfume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:4; 1 Samuel 17:28–29; John 13:2"> Judas Iscariot, one of his disciples, the one who would betray him, said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:22; Luke 12:33; Mark 14:4–5"> “Why was this perfume not sold for three hundred denarii and given to the poor?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 13:29; Psalms 50:16–20; James 2:6"> Now he said this, not because he cared about the poor, but because he was a thief. He had the moneybag and would steal from what was put in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:12; Mark 14:6; Matthew 27:57–60"> Jesus said, “Allow her to keep what she has for the day of my burial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Mark 14:7; Matthew 26:11"> You will always have the poor with you. But you will not always have me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:14; John 11:43–45; Acts 3:10–11"> Now a large crowd of the Jews learned that Jesus was there, and they came, not only for Jesus, but also to see Lazarus, whom Jesus had raised from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Matthew 2:16; John 11:47–53"> The chief priests conspired together so that they might also put Lazarus to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:45; John 12:18; Acts 13:45"> for it was because of him that many of the Jews went away and believed in Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:7–10; Matthew 21:4–9; Luke 19:35–38"> On the next day a great crowd came to the festival. When they heard that Jesus was coming to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 118:25–26; Revelation 15:3; Matthew 21:9–11"> they took the branches of the palm trees and went out to meet him and cried out, “Hosanna! Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord, the King of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Mark 11:1–7; Matthew 21:1–7"> Jesus found a young donkey and sat on it; as it was written,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Isaiah 62:11; Zephaniah 3:16–17"> “Do not fear, daughter of Zion; see, your King is coming, sitting on the colt of a donkey.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 2:22; John 14:26; John 7:39"> His disciples did not understand these things at first; but when Jesus was glorified, they remembered that these things had been written about him and that they had done these things to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 1:2; 1 John 5:9–12; John 19:35"> Now the crowd testified that they had been with him when he called Lazarus out of the tomb and raised him up from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 12:11"> It was also for this reason that the crowd went out to meet him, because they heard that he had done this sign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:6; Psalms 22:27; 1 John 2:2"> The Pharisees therefore said among themselves, “Look, you can do nothing; see, the world has gone after him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:35; Isaiah 11:10; Acts 17:4"> Now certain Greeks were among those who were going up to worship at the festival.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Luke 19:2–4; John 6:40"> These went to Philip, who was from Bethsaida in Galilee, and asked him, saying, “Sir, we want to see Jesus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 10:13–14; Luke 9:49–50; John 1:40–41"> Philip went and told Andrew; Andrew went with Philip, and they told Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:31–32; John 17:1–5; Isaiah 60:9"> Jesus answered them and said, “The hour has come for the Son of Man to be glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36–38; Isaiah 53:10–12; Hebrews 2:9–10"> Truly, truly, I say to you, unless a grain of wheat falls into the earth and dies, it remains by itself alone; but if it dies, it will bear much fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 10:39; Luke 9:23–24; Mark 8:35"> He who loves his life will lose it; but he who hates his life in this world will keep it for eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Samuel 2:30"> If anyone serves me, let him follow me; and where I am, there will my servant also be. If anyone serves me, the Father will honor him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 12:23; Hebrews 5:7; Matthew 26:42"> Now my soul is troubled and what should I say? 'Father, save me from this hour'? But for this reason I came to this hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 13:31–32; Matthew 26:42; Revelation 5:9–14"> Father, glorify your name.” Then a voice came from heaven and said, “I have glorified it and I will glorify it again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:2–5; Revelation 6:1; Revelation 14:2"> Then the crowd that stood by and heard it said that it had thundered. Others said, “An angel has spoken to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:42; John 11:15; John 5:34"> Jesus answered and said, “This voice did not come for me, but for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 John 5:19; 1 John 3:8"> Now is the judgment of this world: Now will the ruler of this world be thrown out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:14; John 8:28; 1 Peter 2:24"> When I am lifted up from the earth, I will draw everyone to myself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 18:32; John 21:19"> He said this to indicate what kind of death he would die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Isaiah 9:7; Daniel 7:14"> The crowd answered him, “We have heard from the law that the Christ will stay forever. How can you say, 'The Son of Man must be lifted up'? Who is this Son of Man?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 12:46; John 16:16; Ephesians 5:8"> Jesus then said to them, “The light will still be with you for a short amount of time. Walk while you have the light, so that darkness does not overtake you. He who walks in the darkness does not know where he is going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; John 8:59; Acts 13:47–48"> While you have the light, believe in the light so that you may be sons of light.”<br />Jesus said these things and then departed and hid from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 16:31; John 1:11; John 15:24"> Although Jesus had done so many signs before them, yet they did not believe in him</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 10:16; Romans 10:20"> so that the word of Isaiah the prophet would be fulfilled, in which he said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Lord, who has believed our report,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and to whom has the arm of the Lord been revealed?”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 10:38; John 5:44; 2 Peter 2:14"> For this reason they could not believe, for Isaiah had also said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Matthew 13:13–15; Luke 8:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">“He has blinded their eyes, and he has hardened their hearts;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">otherwise they would see with their eyes and understand with their hearts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and turn,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I would heal them.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 6:1–5; Isaiah 6:9–10; John 1:14"> Isaiah said these things because he saw the glory of Jesus and spoke of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 9:22; John 7:13; Isaiah 51:7"> But despite that, many of the rulers believed in Jesus; but because of the Pharisees, they did not admit it so that they would not be banned from the synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 5:44; Matthew 23:5–7; John 5:41"> They loved the praise that comes from people more than the praise that comes from God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:20; John 5:24"> Jesus cried out and said, “The one who believes in me, believes not only in me but also in him who sent me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 14:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Colossians 1:15"> and the one who sees me sees him who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 8:12; Psalms 36:9; John 9:5"> I have come as a light into the world, so that whoever believes in me may not remain in the darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:17; John 8:15–16; John 12:48"> If anyone hears my words but does not keep them, I do not judge him; for I have not come to judge the world, but to save the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Hebrews 9:27–28; Romans 2:16"> The one who rejects me and who does not receive my words, has one who judges him. The word I have spoken will judge him on the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 14:10; John 8:26; John 5:30"> For I did not speak for myself, but it is the Father who sent me, who has given me the command about what to say and what to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 17:3; John 6:68; 1 John 5:20"> I know that his command is eternal life, so that is what I say—just as the Father has spoken to me, so I speak.”</span>
</div>
