<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; John 12:17; 2 Kings 20:1–12"> Now a certain man named Lazarus was sick. He was from Bethany, the village of Mary and her sister Martha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 12:3; Mark 14:3; Matthew 26:6–7"> It was Mary who anointed the Lord with myrrh and wiped his feet with her hair, whose brother Lazarus was sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:5; Revelation 3:19; 2 Timothy 4:20"> The sisters then sent for Jesus, saying, “Lord, see, he whom you love is sick.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 11:40; John 9:3; 1 Peter 4:14"> When Jesus heard it, he said, “This sickness is not to death, but instead it is for the glory of God so that the Son of God may be glorified by it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:9–13; John 16:27; John 17:26"> Now Jesus loved Martha and her sister and Lazarus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 30:18; Isaiah 55:8–9; Genesis 45:1–5"> So when he heard that Lazarus was sick, Jesus stayed two more days in the place where he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:40–42; Luke 9:51; Acts 20:22–24"> Then after this, he said to the disciples, “Let us go to Judea again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:31; John 8:59; Acts 21:12–13"> The disciples said to him, “Rabbi, right now the Jews are trying to stone you, and you are going back there again?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 9:4; John 12:35; Jeremiah 31:9"> Jesus answered, “Are there not twelve hours of light in a day? If someone walks in the daytime, he will not stumble, because he sees by the light of this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:10–11; Proverbs 4:18–19; Jeremiah 13:16"> However, if he walks at night, he will stumble because the light is not in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 11:13; John 11:43–44"> He said these things, and after these things, he said to them, “Our friend Lazarus has fallen asleep, but I am going so that I may wake him out of sleep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The disciples therefore said to him, “Lord, if he has fallen asleep, he will recover.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:24"> Now Jesus had spoken of his death, but they thought that he was speaking about the sleep of resting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:25; John 16:29; John 10:24"> Then Jesus said to them plainly, “Lazarus is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; John 14:10–11; Isaiah 54:15"> I am glad, for your sakes, that I was not there so that you may believe. Let us go to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 13:37; Matthew 10:3; John 21:2"> Thomas, who was called Didymus, said to his fellow disciples, “Let us also go so that we may die with Jesus.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 11:39; Hosea 6:2; Acts 2:27–31"> When Jesus came, he found that Lazarus had already been in the tomb for four days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 14:20; Revelation 21:16; John 6:19"> Now Bethany was near Jerusalem, about fifteen stadia away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 2:11; John 11:31; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> Many of the Jews had come to Martha and Mary, to comfort them about their brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; 1 Thessalonians 4:17; Matthew 25:1"> Then Martha, when she heard that Jesus was coming, went to meet him, but Mary was sitting in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:32; 1 Kings 17:18; Matthew 9:18"> Martha then said to Jesus, “Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:31; John 11:41–42; Matthew 28:18"> Even now, I know that whatever you ask from God, he will give to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:43–44"> Jesus said to her, “Your brother will rise again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Acts 24:15; John 5:28–29"> Martha said to him, “I know that he will rise again in the resurrection on the last day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14; John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12"> Jesus said to her, “I am the resurrection and the life; he who believes in me, even if he dies, will live;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 10:28; John 5:24; Romans 8:13"> and whoever lives and believes in me will never die. Do you believe this?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:16; John 6:69; John 6:14"> She said to him, “Yes, Lord, I believe that you are the Christ, the Son of God, who is coming into the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 11:20; John 13:13; Mark 10:49"> When she had said this, she went away and called her sister Mary privately. She said, “The Teacher is here and is calling for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Psalms 27:8; Song of Songs 3:1–4"> When she heard this, she got up quickly and went to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:20"> Now Jesus had not yet come into the village but was still in the place where Martha had met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:19; Genesis 37:35; 2 Samuel 12:16–18"> So when the Jews, who were with her in the house and who were comforting her, saw Mary getting up quickly and going out, they followed her, thinking that she was going to the tomb to weep there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 11:21; Luke 17:16; Luke 5:8"> When Mary came to the place where Jesus was and saw him, she fell down at his feet and said to him, “Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:38; John 12:27; Mark 3:5"> When Jesus saw her weeping, and the Jews who came with her also weeping, he was deeply moved in his spirit and was troubled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 15:47; Matthew 28:6; John 1:39"> he said, “Where have you laid him?” They said to him, “Lord, come and see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Hebrews 4:15; John 11:33"> Jesus wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 11:3; 1 John 3:1; 1 John 4:9–10"> Then the Jews said, “See how much he loved Lazarus!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 11:32; Luke 23:39"> But some of them said, “Could not this man, who opened the eyes of a blind man, also have made this man not die?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:33; Luke 24:2; Matthew 27:60"> Then Jesus again, being deeply moved in himself, went to the tomb. Now it was a cave, and a stone lay against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 11:17; Mark 16:3; Psalms 49:7"> Jesus said, “Take away the stone.” Martha, the sister of Lazarus, the one who had died, said to Jesus, “Lord, by this time the body will be decaying, for he has been dead for four days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:23–26; Romans 4:17–25"> Jesus said to her, “Did I not say to you that, if you believed, you would see the glory of God?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; John 17:1; Psalms 123:1"> So they took away the stone. Jesus lifted up his eyes and said, “Father, I thank you that you listened to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 3:17; John 17:21; 1 John 4:14"> I knew that you always listen to me, but it is because of the crowd that is standing around me that I said this, so that they may believe that you have sent me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33–36; Acts 9:40; Acts 9:34"> After he had said this, he cried out with a loud voice, “Lazarus, come out!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 5:25; John 20:7; John 11:25–26"> The dead man came out; his feet and hands were bound with cloths, and his face was bound about with a cloth. Jesus said to them, “Untie him and let him go.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 11:19; John 10:41"> Then many of the Jews who came to Mary and saw what Jesus did, believed in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 9:13; Acts 5:25; John 12:37"> But some of them went away to the Pharisees and told them the things that Jesus had done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 12:19; Acts 4:5–6"> Then the chief priests and the Pharisees gathered the council together and said, “What will we do? This man does many signs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Matthew 23:35–38; Matthew 27:25"> If we leave him alone like this, all will believe in him; the Romans will come and take away both our place and our nation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 11:51; John 18:13–14"> However, a certain man among them, Caiaphas, who was high priest that year, said to them, “You know nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:14; Luke 24:46; Romans 3:8"> You do not consider that it is better for you that one man dies for the people than that the whole nation perishes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Exodus 28:30"> Now this he said not from himself. Instead, being high priest that year, he prophesied that Jesus should die for the nation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 10:16; Isaiah 49:6; 1 John 2:2"> and not only for the nation, but so that the children of God who are scattered would be gathered together into one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Acts 5:33; Acts 9:23; Mark 14:1"> So from that day onward they planned how to put Jesus to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 7:1; John 7:4; 2 Chronicles 13:19"> No longer did Jesus walk openly among the Jews, but he departed from there into the country near to the wilderness into a town called Ephraim. There he stayed with the disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:17–20; Hebrews 9:13–14; John 6:4"> Now the Passover of the Jews was near, and many went up to Jerusalem from the country before the Passover in order to purify themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 7:11; John 11:7–8"> They were looking for Jesus, and speaking one with another as they stood in the temple, “What do you think? That he will not come to the festival?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 9:22; John 8:59; John 5:16–18"> Now the chief priests and the Pharisees had given an order that if anyone knew where Jesus was, he should report it so that they might seize him.</span>
</div>
