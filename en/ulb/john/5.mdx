<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 23:14–17; Exodus 34:23"> After this there was a Jewish festival, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:39; Nehemiah 3:1; Nehemiah 3:32"> Now in Jerusalem by the sheep gate, there is a pool, which in Hebrew is called Bethesda, and it has five roofed porches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 3:1–4; Luke 7:22; Matthew 15:30"> A large number of people who were sick, blind, lame, or paralyzed were lying there.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The best ancient copies omit the phrase, waiting for the moving of the water .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The best ancient copies omit vs. 4, For an angel of the Lord went down and stirred up the water at certain times and whoever stepped in while the water was stirring was healed from whatever disease he suffered from .">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 3:2; Luke 8:43; Mark 9:21"> A certain man was there who had been sick for thirty-eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 18:41; Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 4:13"> When Jesus saw him lying there, and after he realized that he had been there a long time, he said to him, “Do you want to be healthy?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 72:12; Deuteronomy 32:36; 1 Corinthians 9:24"> The sick man replied, “Sir, I do not have anyone to put me into the pool when the water is stirred up. When I come, another steps down before me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 5:24; Matthew 9:6; Mark 2:11"> Jesus said to him, “Get up, take up your bed, and walk.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 3:7–8; John 9:14; Mark 3:2–4"> Immediately the man was healed, and he took up his bed and walked.<br />Now that day was a Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:21; Luke 6:2; John 9:16"> So the Jews said to him who was healed, “It is the Sabbath and you are not permitted to carry your mat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 2:9–11; John 9:16"> He replied, “He who made me healthy said to me, 'Pick up your mat and walk.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Judges 6:29; Romans 10:2"> They asked him, “Who is the man that said to you, 'Pick it up and walk'?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:30; Luke 24:31; John 8:59"> However, the one who was healed did not know who it was because Jesus had gone away secretly, for there was a crowd in the place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 8:11; Leviticus 26:23–24; Psalms 118:18"> Afterward, Jesus found him in the temple and said to him, “See, you have become healthy! Do not sin anymore, so that something worse will not happen to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:11–12; John 4:29; John 9:25"> The man went away and reported to the Jews that it was Jesus who had made him healthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:39; John 7:19–20; Mark 3:6"> Now because of these things the Jews persecuted Jesus, because he did these things on the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:4; John 14:10; Colossians 1:16"> Jesus replied to them, “My Father is working even now, and I, too, am working.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 10:30; John 10:33; John 19:7"> Because of this, the Jews sought even more to kill him because he not only broke the Sabbath, but also called God his own Father, making himself equal to God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 12:49; John 8:28; John 14:10"> Jesus answered them, “Truly, truly, the Son can do nothing of himself, except only what he sees the Father doing, for whatever the Father is doing, the Son does these things also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 3:35; Matthew 3:17; John 17:26"> For the Father loves the Son and he shows him everything that he himself does, and he will show him greater things than these so that you will be amazed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:11; Deuteronomy 32:39; John 11:25"> For as the Father raises the dead and gives them life, so also the Son gives life to whomever he wishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 5:27; Acts 17:31; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> For the Father judges no one, but he has given all judgment to the Son</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 2:23; Luke 10:16; Matthew 11:27"> so that everyone will honor the Son just as they honor the Father. The one who does not honor the Son does not honor the Father who sent him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; John 6:40; 1 John 5:11–13"> Truly, truly, he who hears my word and believes him who sent me has eternal life and will not be condemned, but he has passed from death to life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:28; Ephesians 5:14; Ephesians 2:5"> Truly, truly, I tell you the time is coming, and is now, when the dead will hear the voice of the Son of God, and those who hear will live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 11:26; John 1:4; Exodus 3:14"> For just as the Father has life in himself, so he has also given to the Son so that he has life in himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 10:42; John 5:22; Acts 17:31"> and the Father has given the Son authority to carry out judgment because he is the Son of Man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 20:12; 1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Isaiah 26:19"> Do not be amazed at this, for there is a time coming in which everyone who is in the tombs will hear his voice</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Romans 2:6–10; Acts 24:15"> and will come out. Those who have done good to the resurrection of life, and those who have done evil to the resurrection of judgment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 6:38; John 5:19; John 4:34"> I can do nothing from myself. As I hear, I judge, and my judgment is righteous because I am not seeking my own will but the will of him who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; John 8:54; Revelation 3:14"> If I should testify about myself, my testimony would not be true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 1:33; John 12:28–30; John 5:36–37"> There is another who testifies about me, and I know that the testimony that he gives about me is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 1:29–34; John 3:26–36; John 1:15–27"> You have sent to John, and he has testified the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 19:41–42; John 20:31; 1 John 5:9"> But the testimony that I receive is not from man. I say these things that you might be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Peter 1:19; John 1:7–8; Matthew 11:11"> John was a lamp that was burning and shining, and you were willing to rejoice in his light for a while.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 10:25; John 10:37–38; John 15:24"> Yet the testimony that I have is greater than that of John, for the works that the Father has given me to accomplish, the very works that I do, testify about me that the Father has sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 8:18; John 1:18; Matthew 3:17"> The Father who sent me has himself testified about me. You have neither heard his voice nor seen his form at any time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 John 2:14; Deuteronomy 6:6–9; John 12:44–48"> You do not have his word remaining in you, for you are not believing in the one whom he has sent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Luke 24:44; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> You search the scriptures because you think that in them you have eternal life, and these same scriptures testify about me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 3:19; John 1:11; Psalms 81:11"> and you are not willing to come to me so that you may have life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:18; John 5:34; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> I do not receive praise from men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 3:17; John 2:25"> but I know that you do not have the love of God in yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 10:25; Matthew 24:5; John 17:4–6"> I have come in my Father's name, and you do not receive me. If another should come in his own name, you would receive him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 12:43; Romans 2:29; Philippians 2:3"> How can you believe, you who accept praise from one another but are not seeking the praise that comes from the only God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 3:19–20; Galatians 3:10; 2 Corinthians 3:7–11"> Do not think that I myself will accuse you before the Father. The one who accuses you is Moses, in whom you have put your hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15; Deuteronomy 18:18–19; John 1:45"> If you believed Moses, you would believe me, because he wrote about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Luke 16:29"> If you do not believe his writings, how are you going to believe my words?”</span>
</div>
