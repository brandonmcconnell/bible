<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Now as Jesus passed by, he saw a man blind from birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 13:2; John 9:34; Acts 28:4"> His disciples asked him, “Rabbi, who sinned, this man or his parents, so that he was born blind?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:40; Ecclesiastes 9:1–2"> Jesus answered, “Neither did this man sin, nor his parents, but so that the works of God would be revealed in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:35; Ephesians 5:16; John 4:34"> We must work the works of him who sent me while it is day. Night is coming when no one will be able to work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; Acts 13:47"> While I am in the world, I am the light of the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 8:23; Mark 7:33; Revelation 3:18"> After Jesus said these things, he spit on the ground, made mud with the saliva, and smeared the mud on his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5; John 9:11; John 11:37"> He said to him, “Go, wash in the pool of Siloam,” (which is translated “Sent”). So the man went away, washed, and came back seeing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; 1 Samuel 21:11; Luke 18:35"> Then the man's neighbors and those who had seen him previously as a beggar were saying, “Is not this the man that used to sit and beg?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Some said, “It is he.” Others said, “No, but he is like him.” But he said, “It is me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 9:26; John 9:21; Ecclesiastes 11:5"> They said to him, “Then how were your eyes opened?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 9:27; Jeremiah 36:17"> He answered, “The man who is called Jesus made mud and smeared it on my eyes and said to me, 'Go to Siloam and wash.' So I went and washed, and I received my sight.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 5:11–13; Exodus 2:18–20; John 7:11"> They said to him, “Where is he?” He replied, “I do not know.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:57; John 11:46–47; John 12:42"> They brought the man who used to be blind to the Pharisees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:9; Luke 13:10–17; Matthew 12:1–14"> Now it was the Sabbath day when Jesus made the mud and opened his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:26–27; John 9:10–11"> Then again the Pharisees asked him how he had received his sight. He said to them, “He put mud on my eyes, I washed, and I now can see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:43; John 10:19; Matthew 12:2"> Some of the Pharisees said, “This man is not from God because he does not keep the Sabbath.” Others said, “How can a man who is a sinner do such signs?” So there was a division among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:19; John 6:14; Acts 3:22–26"> So they asked the blind man again, “What do you say about him, since he opened your eyes?” The blind man said, “He is a prophet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 3:15–19; John 5:44; Isaiah 26:11"> Now the Jews still did not believe about him that he was blind and had received his sight until they called the parents of him who had received his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:8–9; Acts 4:14; Acts 3:10"> They asked the parents, “Is this your son whom you say was born blind? How then does he now see?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So his parents answered them, “We know that this is our son and that he was born blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> How he now sees, we do not know, and who opened his eyes, we do not know. Ask him, he is an adult. He can speak for himself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 7:13; John 16:2; Luke 6:22"> His parents said these things, because they were afraid of the Jews. For the Jews had already agreed that if anyone would confess him to be the Christ, he would be thrown out of the synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 9:21"> Because of this, his parents said, “He is an adult, ask him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 7:19; John 9:16; John 8:49"> So for a second time they called the man who had been blind and said to him, “Give glory to God. We know that this man is a sinner.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 5:10; John 9:30; John 5:11"> Then that man replied, “I do not know if he is a sinner. One thing I do know: I was blind, and now I see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then they said to him, “What did he do to you? How did he open your eyes?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:67; John 9:10–15"> He answered, “I have told you already, and you did not listen! Why do you want to hear it again? You do not want to become his disciples too, do you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 2:17; John 7:19; Matthew 27:39"> They insulted him and said, “You are his disciple, but we are disciples of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:14; Numbers 12:2–7; Acts 7:35"> We know that God has spoken to Moses, but we do not know where this one is from.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; Psalms 119:18; Isaiah 29:18"> The man answered and said to them, “This is remarkable, that you do not know where he is from, and yet he opened my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 28:9"> We know that God does not listen to sinners, but if someone is devout and does his will, he listens to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Revelation 16:18; Job 20:4"> Since the world began it has never been heard that anyone opened the eyes of a man born blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 9:16; John 3:2; Acts 5:38–39"> If this man were not from God, he could do nothing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 9:22; John 9:2; Isaiah 66:5"> They answered and said to him, “You were completely born in sins, and you are teaching us?” Then they threw him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 20:31; 1 John 5:13; John 10:36"> Jesus heard that they had cast him out of the synagogue. He found him and said, “Do you believe in the Son of Man?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Song of Songs 5:9; John 1:38"> He replied and said, “Who is he, Lord, that I may believe in him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 4:26; Psalms 25:8–9; John 14:21–23"> Jesus said to him, “You have seen him, and it is the one who is speaking with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; Matthew 28:9; Luke 24:52"> The man said, “Lord, I believe” and he worshiped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:19; John 12:46; Acts 26:18"> Jesus said, “For judgment I came into this world so that those who do not see may see and so that those who see may become blind.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Matthew 15:12–14; John 7:47–52"> Some of the Pharisees who were with him heard these things and asked him, “Are we also blind?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; John 15:22–24; 1 John 1:8–10"> Jesus said to them, “If you were blind, you would have no sin, but now you say, 'We see,' so your sin remains.”</span>
