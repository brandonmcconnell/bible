<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:8; Matthew 13:21; Matthew 26:31–33"> “I have spoken these things to you so that you will not fall away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 9:22; Acts 26:9–11; Romans 10:2–3"> They will throw you out of the synagogues. But the hour is coming when everyone who kills you will think that he is offering a service to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 15:21; John 17:25; John 8:55"> They will do these things because they have not known the Father nor me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 14:29; Mark 13:23; John 17:12–13"> I have spoken these things to you so that when their hour comes, you will remember that I told you about them. I did not tell you about these things in the beginning, because I was with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 13:36; John 7:33; John 16:28"> But now I go to him who sent me, yet none of you asks me, 'Where are you going?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 14:1; John 16:20–22; Luke 24:17"> But because I have said these things to you, sadness has filled your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 14:26; John 14:16–17; John 15:26"> But truly I tell you, it is better for you that I go away. For if I do not go away, the Comforter will not come to you, but if I go, I will send him to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:37; Jude 1:15; Zechariah 12:10"> When he comes, the Comforter will prove the world to be wrong about sin, about righteousness, and about judgment—</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 15:22–25; John 8:23–24; John 12:47–48"> about sin, because they do not believe in me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 10:3–4"> about righteousness, because I am going to the Father, and you will no longer see me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 12:31; 1 John 3:8; Hebrews 2:14"> and about judgment, because the ruler of this world has been judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:33; John 15:15; 1 Corinthians 3:1–2"> I have many things to say to you, but you would not understand them now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 14:26; 1 Corinthians 2:10–13; John 14:17"> But when he, the Spirit of Truth, comes, he will guide you into all the truth, for he will not speak from himself. But he will say whatever he hears, and he will tell you things that are to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:13–14; 2 Corinthians 3:14–18; John 15:26"> He will glorify me, because he will take from what is mine and he will tell it to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 17:10; Matthew 11:27; John 3:35"> Everything that the Father has is mine. Therefore, I said that the Spirit will take from what is mine and he will tell it to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:33; Acts 1:3; John 14:18–24"> In a short amount of time you will no longer see me, and after another short amount of time you will see me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:16; Mark 9:32; John 16:5"> Then some of his disciples said to one another, “What is this that he says to us, 'A short amount of time you will no longer see me and after another short amount of time you will see me,' and, 'Because I go to the Father'?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 24:25; Matthew 16:9–11; Hebrews 5:12"> Therefore they said, “What is this that he says, 'A short amount of time'? We do not know what he is talking about.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:30; John 16:16; John 14:19"> Jesus saw that they wanted to ask him, and he said to them, “Is this what you are asking yourselves, what I meant by saying, 'A little while and you will no longer see me, and again a little while and you will see me'?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 1:2; Matthew 5:4; Psalms 126:5–6"> Truly, truly, I say to you, you will weep and lament, but the world will be glad. You will become full of sadness, but your sorrow will be turned into joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 113:9; Isaiah 13:8; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> When a woman gives birth she has sadness because her hour has come, but when she has given birth to the child, she no longer remembers her pain because of her joy that a man has been born into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; Acts 13:52; John 16:6"> So you have sadness now, but I will see you again, and your heart will be glad, and no one will be able to take away your joy from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 15:7; John 14:13–14; Matthew 21:22"> On that day you will not ask me anything. Truly, truly, I say to you, if you ask anything of the Father in my name, he will give it to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; James 4:2–3; John 15:11"> Until now you have not asked anything in my name. Ask, and you will receive so that your joy will be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:12; Mark 4:13; Matthew 13:34–35"> I have said these things to you in figures of speech, but the hour is coming when I will no longer speak to you in figures of speech, but instead I will tell you plainly about the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 8:34; John 17:24; John 17:19"> On that day you will ask in my name and I do not say to you that I will pray to the Father for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:21; John 14:23; John 17:23"> for the Father himself loves you because you have loved me and because you have believed that I came from the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 13:1; John 13:3; John 8:14"> I came from the Father, and I have come into the world. Again, I am leaving the world and I am going to the Father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 16:25"> His disciples said, “See, now you are speaking plainly and you are not using figures of speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 21:17; John 17:8; John 16:17"> Now we know that you know all things, and you do not need anyone to ask you questions. Because of this, we believe that you have come from God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 9:44–45; John 13:38"> Jesus answered them, “Do you believe now?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Matthew 26:31; John 8:29"> See, the hour is coming, yes, and has indeed come, when you will be scattered, everyone to his own home, and you will leave me alone. Yet I am not alone because the Father is with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 14:27; 1 John 5:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> I have spoken these things to you so that you will have peace in me. In the world you have troubles, but have courage, I have conquered the world.”</span>
