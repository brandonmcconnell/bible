<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 6:31–44; John 6:23; Luke 5:1"> After these things, Jesus went away to the other side of the Sea of Galilee, also called the Sea of Tiberias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:24–25; Matthew 12:15; Matthew 15:30–31"> A great crowd was following him because they saw the signs that he was doing on those who were sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 6:15; Luke 9:28; Luke 6:12–13"> Jesus went up the mountain and there he sat down with his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 2:13; John 11:55; John 5:1"> (Now the Passover, the Jewish festival, was near.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 15:33; Luke 9:12–13; Matthew 14:14–15"> When Jesus looked up and saw a great crowd coming to him, he said to Philip, “Where are we going to buy bread so that these may eat?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 22:1; Deuteronomy 13:3; 2 Chronicles 32:31"> (But Jesus said this to test Philip, for he himself knew what he was going to do.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 11:21–22; Mark 6:37; John 12:5"> Philip answered him, “Two hundred denarii worth of bread would not be sufficient for each one to have even a little.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:40–44; Matthew 4:18"> One of the disciples, Andrew, the brother of Simon Peter, said to Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; Matthew 14:17; Mark 6:38"> “There is a boy here who has five bread loaves of barley and two fish, but what are these among so many?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 9:14–16; Matthew 15:35–36; Mark 6:39–41"> Jesus said, “Make the people sit down.” (Now there was a lot of grass in the place.) So the men sat down, about five thousand in number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 6:23; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Matthew 15:36"> Then Jesus took the loaves and after giving thanks, he gave it to those who were sitting. He did the same with the fish, as much as they wanted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 1:53; Nehemiah 9:25; Nehemiah 8:10"> When the people were filled, he said to his disciples, “Gather up the broken pieces which remain, so that nothing will be lost.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; 2 Kings 4:2–7; 2 Corinthians 9:8–9"> So they gathered them up and filled twelve baskets with broken pieces from the five barley loaves left over by those who had eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–18; Matthew 21:11; Acts 7:37"> Then, when the people saw this sign that he did, they said, “This truly is the prophet who is to come into the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:36; John 6:15–21; John 7:3–4"> When Jesus realized that they were about to come and seize him by force to make him king, he withdrew again up the mountain by himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> When it became evening, his disciples went down to the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 6:45; John 6:24–25; John 2:12"> They got into a boat, and were going over the sea to Capernaum. It was dark by this time, and Jesus had not yet come to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 107:25; Psalms 135:7; Matthew 14:24"> A strong wind was blowing, and the sea was getting rough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 6:47–49; Matthew 14:25–26; Job 9:8"> When they had rowed about twenty-five or thirty stadia, they saw Jesus walking on the sea and coming near the boat, and they were afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 14:27–31; Isaiah 43:1–2; Isaiah 44:8"> But he said to them, “It is I! Do not be afraid.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 6:51; Matthew 14:32–33; Revelation 3:20"> Then they were willing to receive him into the boat, and immediately the boat reached the land where they were going.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 6:45; Matthew 14:22; John 6:2"> The next day, the crowd that had been standing on the other side of the sea saw that there was no other boat there except the one, and that Jesus had not entered it with his disciples but that his disciples had gone away alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:11–12"> However, there were some boats that came from Tiberias close to the place where they had eaten the bread loaves after the Lord had given thanks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:17; Luke 8:40; Mark 6:53"> When the crowd discovered that neither Jesus nor his disciples were there, they themselves got into the boats and went to Capernaum seeking Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 1:38–39; Matthew 23:7"> After they found him on the other side of the sea, they said to him, “Rabbi, when did you come here?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 4:3–4; Philippians 3:19; Philippians 2:21"> Jesus replied to them, saying, “Truly, truly, you seek me, not because you saw signs, but because you ate some of the bread loaves and were filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 3:2; Isaiah 55:2; John 6:54"> Do not work for the food that perishes, but work for the food that endures to eternal life which the Son of Man will give you, for God the Father has set his seal on him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 16:30; Acts 9:6; Matthew 19:16"> Then they said to him, “What must we do, so that we may do the works of God?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 John 3:23; Acts 16:31; John 3:36"> Jesus replied and said to them, “This is the work of God: That you believe in the one whom he has sent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; John 10:38; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> So they said to him, “What sign then will you do, so that we may see and believe you? What will you do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:15; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:24–25"> Our fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, as it is written, 'He gave them bread from heaven to eat.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 15:1; John 6:58; Exodus 16:4"> Then Jesus replied to them, “Truly, truly, it was not Moses who gave you the bread out of heaven, but it is my Father who is giving you the true bread from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 8:42; John 17:8; John 3:13"> For the bread of God is that which comes down from heaven and gives life to the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 4:15; John 6:26; Psalms 4:6"> So they said to him, “Sir, give us this bread always.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 6:48–58; Revelation 22:17; John 6:41"> Jesus said to them, “I am the bread of life; he who comes to me will not be hungry, and he who believes in me will never be thirsty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8–9; Luke 16:31; John 12:37"> But I told you that indeed you have seen me, and you do not believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; John 6:39; John 17:24"> Everyone whom the Father gives me will come to me, and he who comes to me I will certainly not throw out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 5:30; John 4:34; John 3:31"> For I have come down from heaven, not to do my own will, but the will of him who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 17:12; John 6:40; John 18:9"> This is the will of him who sent me, that I would lose not one of all those whom he has given me, but will raise them up on the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 6:54; John 5:24; Romans 6:23"> For this is the will of my Father, that everyone who sees the Son and believes in him would have eternal life and I will raise him up on the last day.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 6:51–52; John 6:48; John 6:33"> Then the Jews grumbled about him because he had said, “I am the bread that has come down from heaven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Mark 6:3; Matthew 13:55–56"> They said, “Is not this Jesus son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know? How then does he now say, 'I have come down from heaven'?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; John 6:64; Mark 9:33"> Jesus replied and said to them, “Stop grumbling among yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 6:65; John 6:39–40; Matthew 11:25–27"> No one can come to me unless the Father who sent me draws him, and I will raise him up on the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Isaiah 54:13; Jeremiah 31:33–34; Hebrews 10:16"> It is written in the prophets, 'Everyone will be taught by God.' Everyone who has heard and learned from the Father comes to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 1:18; John 7:29; Matthew 11:27"> Not that anyone has seen the Father, except he who is from God—he has seen the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:36; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 5:9–10"> Truly, truly, he who believes has eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 6:51; 1 Corinthians 11:24–25; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> I am the bread of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3–5; Jude 1:5; Zechariah 1:5"> Your fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, and they died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 6:33; John 6:51; Romans 8:10"> This is the bread which comes down from heaven, so that a person may eat some of it and not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 22:19; John 6:33; Hebrews 10:5–12"> I am the living bread that came down from heaven. If anyone eats some of this bread, he will live forever. The bread that I will give is my flesh for the life of the world.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 6:41"> The Jews became angry among themselves and began to argue, saying, “How can this man give us his flesh to eat?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 John 5:12; Matthew 26:26–28; John 6:55"> Then Jesus said to them, “Truly, truly, unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink his blood, you will not have life in yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 6:39–40; John 6:27; Philippians 3:7–10"> Whoever eats my flesh and drinks my blood has everlasting life, and I will raise him up at the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 15:1; John 8:31; John 6:32"> For my flesh is true food, and my blood is true drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="1 John 3:24; John 15:4–5; John 14:20"> He who eats my flesh and drinks my blood remains in me, and I in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; John 5:26; John 17:21"> As the living Father sent me, and as I live because of the Father, so he who eats me, he will also live because of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 3:36; John 6:47–51; John 6:41"> This is the bread that has come down from heaven, not as the fathers ate and died. He who eats this bread will live forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 6:24; Proverbs 8:1–3; Luke 4:31"> But Jesus said these things in the synagogue while he was teaching in Capernaum.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="John 6:66; John 8:43; John 8:31"> Then many of his disciples who heard this said, “This is a difficult teaching; who can accept it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="John 2:24–25; John 6:64; John 21:17"> Jesus, because he knew in himself that his disciples were grumbling at this, said to them, “Does this offend you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 16:19; John 3:13; John 17:4–5"> Then what if you should see the Son of Man going up to where he was before?</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Galatians 5:25; Hebrews 4:12; Psalms 119:93"> It is the Spirit who makes alive; the flesh profits nothing. The words that I have spoken to you are spirit, and they are life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="John 10:26; 2 Timothy 2:19; Hebrews 4:13"> Yet there are some of you who do not believe.” For Jesus knew from the beginning who were the ones that would not believe and who it was who would betray him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="John 6:44–45; John 6:37; John 3:27"> He said, “It is because of this that I said to you that no one can come to me unless it is granted to him by the Father.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Luke 9:62; 1 John 2:19; Hebrews 10:38"> Because of this, many of his disciples went away and no longer walked with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Ruth 1:11–18; John 6:70; Luke 14:25–33"> Then Jesus said to the twelve, “You do not want to go away also, do you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Psalms 73:25; John 5:24; 1 John 5:11–13"> Simon Peter answered him, “Lord, to whom shall we go? You have words of eternal life,</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="John 11:27; Luke 9:20; Mark 8:29"> and we have believed and come to know that you are the Holy One of God.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The phrase the Holy One of God is in the best ancient copies. Some later copies add an additional description and they read: the Christ, the Holy One of God .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="John 13:27; John 13:2; John 17:12"> Jesus said to them, “Did not I choose you, the twelve, and one of you is a devil?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Matthew 26:14–16; Jude 1:4; Acts 1:16–20"> Now he spoke of Judas son of Simon Iscariot, for it was he, one of the twelve, who would betray Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>
