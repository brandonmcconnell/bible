<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Colossians 1:10; John 15:16"> I am the vine, you are the branches. He who remains in me and I in him, he bears much fruit, for without me you can do nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:19; Hebrews 6:7–8; Matthew 13:41"> If anyone does not remain in me, he is thrown away like a branch and dries up, and they gather the branches and throw them into the fire, and they are burned up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; 1 John 5:14; John 14:13"> If you remain in me, and if my words remain in you, ask whatever you wish, and it will be done for you.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 17:13; Romans 15:13; John 16:24"> I have spoken these things to you so that my joy will be in you and so that your joy will be complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 4:21; 1 Peter 4:8; John 13:34"> This is my commandment, that you love one another as I have loved you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 10:11; Ephesians 5:2; Romans 5:6–8"> No one has greater love than this—that he lay down his life for his friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 12:50; 1 John 5:3; James 2:23"> You are my friends if you do the things that I command you.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 15:19; Jeremiah 1:5–7; Ephesians 2:10"> You did not choose me, but I chose you and appointed you so that you would go and bear fruit, and that your fruit should remain. <span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:12; 1 John 3:14–17; 1 Peter 2:17"> These things I command you, so that you love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 3:13; John 7:7; Mark 13:13"> If the world hates you, know that it has hated me before it hated you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 4:4–5; John 17:14–16; Ephesians 1:4–11"> If you were of the world, the world would love you as its own. But because you are not of the world and because I chose you out of the world, therefore the world hates you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:12; John 13:16; Matthew 10:24"> Remember the word that I said to you, 'A servant is not greater than his master.' If they persecuted me, they will also persecute you; if they kept my word, they will also keep yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 10:22; John 16:3; Matthew 10:18"> They will do all these things to you because of my name, because they do not know him who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:41; James 4:17; Acts 17:30"> If I had not come and spoken to them, they would not have sin, but now they have no excuse for their sin.</span>
