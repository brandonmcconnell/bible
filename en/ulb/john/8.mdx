<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Mark 11:1; Luke 19:37"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See the note about Jhn. 7:53-8:11 above">a</a></sup>Jesus went to the Mount of Olives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 26:55; Luke 4:20; Luke 21:37"> Early in the morning he came to the temple again, and all the people came; he sat down and taught them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The scribes and the Pharisees brought a woman caught in the act of adultery. They placed her in the middle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See the note about John 7:53-8:11 above">b</a></sup>Then they said to him, “Teacher, this woman has been caught in the act of adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Ezekiel 16:38–40"> Now in the law, Moses commanded us to stone such people; what do you say about her?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:13; Luke 10:25; Ecclesiastes 3:7"> They said this in order to trap him so that they might have something to accuse him about, but Jesus bent down and wrote on the ground with his finger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:1–5; Romans 2:21–25; Romans 2:1–3"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See the note about Jhn. 7:53-8:11 above">c</a></sup>When they continued asking him questions, he stood up and said to them, “The one among you who has no sin, let him be the first to throw a stone at her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Again he stooped down, and wrote on the ground with his finger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:22; Romans 2:15; 1 John 3:20"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See the note about Jhn. 7:53-8:11 above">d</a></sup>When they heard it, they left one by one, beginning with the oldest. Finally Jesus was left alone, with the woman who had been in the middle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:11–12"> Jesus stood up and said to her, “Woman, where are your accusers? Did no one condemn you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 3:17; John 5:14; Isaiah 1:16–18"> She said, “No one, Lord.” Jesus said, “Neither do I condemn you. Go and sin no more.”&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 12:46; John 9:5; John 12:35"> Again Jesus spoke to them, saying, “I am the light of the world; he who follows me will not walk in the darkness but will have the light of life.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 5:31–47"> The Pharisees said to him, “You bear witness about yourself; your witness is not true.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:28; John 13:3; John 8:42"> Jesus answered and said to them, “Even if I bear witness about myself, my witness is true. I know where I came from and where I am going, but you do not know where I came from or where I am going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; John 7:24; John 12:47"> You judge according the flesh; I judge no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:31; John 8:29; John 16:32"> Yet if I judge, my judgment is true because I am not alone, but I am with the Father who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:6; Hebrews 10:28"> Yes, and in your law it is written that the testimony of two men is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:38; John 8:58; John 8:25"> I am he who bears witness about myself, and the Father who sent me bears witness about me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:3; John 8:54–55; John 14:6–9"> They said to him, “Where is your father?” Jesus answered, “You know neither me nor my Father; if you had known me, you would have known my Father also.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:30; John 7:8; Mark 12:41"> He said these words in the treasury as he taught in the temple, and no one arrested him because his hour had not yet come.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:34; John 8:24; 1 Corinthians 15:17–18"> So again he said to them, “I am going away; you will seek me and will die in your sin. Where I am going, you cannot come.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 8:52; Psalms 22:6; John 8:48"> The Jews said, “Will he kill himself? Is that why he says, 'Where I am going you cannot come'?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 3:31; John 17:14; John 17:16"> Jesus said to them, “You are from below; I am from above. You are of this world; I am not of this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; Mark 16:16; John 3:18"> Therefore, I said to you that you will die in your sins. For unless you believe that I AM, you will die in your sins.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:17–29; Luke 22:67; John 1:22"> They said therefore to him, “Who are you?” Jesus said to them, “What I have said to you from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 15:15; John 12:47–50; John 8:40"> I have many things to speak and to judge about you. However, he who sent me is true; and the things that I heard from him, these things I say to the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; John 8:43; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4"> They did not understand that he was speaking to them about the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 5:19; John 5:30; John 6:38"> Jesus said, “When you have lifted up the Son of Man, then will you know that I AM, and that I do nothing of myself. As the Father taught me, I speak these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:38; John 16:32; John 4:34"> He who sent me is with me, and he has not left me alone, because I always do what is pleasing to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 7:31; John 2:23; John 11:45"> As Jesus was saying these things, many believed in him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 John 1:9; John 15:4–9; 2 Timothy 3:14"> Jesus said to those Jews who had believed him, “If you remain in my word, then you are truly my disciples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 8:2; John 8:36; John 17:17"> and you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:9; John 8:39; Genesis 15:13"> They answered him, “We are descendants of Abraham and have never been slaves of anyone; how can you say, 'You will be set free'?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 6:16; 2 Peter 2:19; Proverbs 5:22"> Jesus answered them, “Truly, truly, I say to you, everyone who commits sin is the slave of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–7; Luke 15:31; Romans 8:15–17"> The slave does not remain in the house forever; the son remains forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Galatians 5:1; Romans 8:2"> Therefore, if the Son sets you free, you will be truly free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 8:45–47; John 7:1"> I know that you are Abraham's descendants; you seek to kill me because my word has no place in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 5:19; John 8:44; John 8:41"> I say what I have seen with my Father, and you also do what you heard from your father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; John 8:37; Romans 9:7"> They answered and said to him, “Our father is Abraham.” Jesus said to them, “If you were Abraham's children, you would do the works of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 8:26; Galatians 4:29; Galatians 4:16"> Yet, now you seek to kill me, a man who has told you the truth that I heard from God. Abraham did not do this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; John 8:38; Isaiah 63:16"> You do the works of your father.” They said to him, “We were not born in sexual immorality; we have one Father: God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:49; John 14:10; John 17:8"> Jesus said to them, “If God were your Father, you would love me, for I came from God and am here; for neither have I come of myself, but he sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 12:39–40; Romans 8:7–8; John 7:17"> Why do you not understand my words? It is because you cannot hear my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 13:10; 1 John 3:12; 1 John 3:8–10"> You are of your father, the devil, and you wish to do the desires of your father. He was a murderer from the beginning and does not stand in the truth because there is no truth in him. When he speaks a lie, he speaks from his own nature because he is a liar and the father of lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Galatians 4:16; John 18:37"> Yet, because I speak the truth, you do not believe me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; John 16:8; 1 Peter 2:22"> Which one of you convicts me of sin? If I speak the truth, why do you not believe me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 10:26–27; 2 John 1:9; John 18:37"> He who is of God hears the words of God; you do not hear them because you are not of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 7:20; John 8:52; Matthew 10:5"> The Jews answered and said to him, “Do we not truly say that you are a Samaritan and have a demon?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Peter 2:23; Isaiah 49:3; John 13:31–32"> Jesus answered, “I do not have a demon, but I honor my Father, and you dishonor me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 5:41; John 8:54; John 7:18"> I do not seek my glory; there is one seeking and judging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 5:24; John 3:15–16"> Truly, truly, I say to you, if anyone keeps my word, he will never see death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 9:24; John 8:48; Hebrews 2:9"> The Jews said to him, “Now we know that you have a demon. Abraham and the prophets died; but you say, 'If anyone keeps my word, he will never taste death.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 4:12; Romans 9:5; Hebrews 7:1–7"> You are not greater than our father Abraham who died, are you? The prophets also died. Who do you make yourself out to be?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 8:50; Proverbs 25:27; 2 Peter 1:17"> Jesus answered, “If I glorify myself, my glory is nothing; it is my Father who glorifies me—about whom you say that he is your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 8:19; John 8:44; John 7:28–29"> You have not known him, but I know him. If I would say, 'I do not know him,' I would be like you, a liar. However, I know him and keep his word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13; Genesis 22:18; Galatians 3:7–9"> Your father Abraham rejoiced at seeing my day; he saw it and was glad.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> The Jews said to him, “You are not yet fifty years old, and you have seen Abraham?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Exodus 3:14; Colossians 1:17; John 17:5"> Jesus said to them, “Truly, truly, I say to you, before Abraham was, I AM.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 11:8; John 10:30–33; John 10:39–40"> Then they picked up stones to throw at him, but Jesus hid himself and went out of the temple.</span>
