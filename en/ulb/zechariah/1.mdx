<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:4; Zechariah 7:1; Zechariah 1:7"> In the eighth month of the second year of Darius' reign, the word of Yahweh came to Zechariah son of Berechiah son of Iddo, the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:6; Lamentations 5:7; Ezekiel 22:31"> “Yahweh was exceedingly angry with your fathers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Malachi 3:7; Jeremiah 3:22; Jeremiah 4:1"> Say to them, 'Yahweh of hosts says this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Turn to me!—this is the declaration of Yahweh of hosts—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will return to you, says Yahweh of hosts.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 78:8; Psalms 106:6–7; Jeremiah 35:15"> Do not be like your fathers to whom the prophets cried out previously, saying, “Yahweh of hosts says this: Turn from your evil ways and wicked practices!” But they would not hear and did not pay attention to me—this is Yahweh's declaration.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5; Job 14:10–12; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your fathers, where are they? Where are the prophets, are they here forever?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Lamentations 2:17; Deuteronomy 28:15; Ezekiel 12:25–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">But my words and my decrees that I commanded my servants the prophets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">have they not overtaken your fathers?</span>So they repented and said, 'Just as Yahweh of hosts planned to do to us what our ways and actions deserved, so he has dealt with us.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 1:1"> On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month, which is the month of Shebat, in the second year of Darius' reign, the word of Yahweh came to Zechariah son of Berechiah son of Iddo, the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 6:4; Isaiah 55:13; Zechariah 13:7"> “I saw in the night, and, look! a man was riding on a red horse, and he was among the myrtle trees that are in the valley; and behind him there were red, reddish-brown, and white horses.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 4:4–5; Zechariah 5:5; Zechariah 2:3"> I said, “What are these things, Lord?” Then the angel who talked with me said to me, “I will show you what these things are.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 1:11; Zechariah 6:5–8; Hebrews 1:14"> Then the man who stood among the myrtle trees answered and said, “These are those Yahweh has sent out to roam throughout the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 1:10; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 103:20–21"> They answered the angel of Yahweh who stood among the myrtle trees; they said to him, “We have been roaming throughout the earth; see, all the earth sits still and is at rest.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 7:5; Jeremiah 29:10; Psalms 102:13"> Then the angel of Yahweh answered and said, “Yahweh of hosts, how long will you show no compassion to Jerusalem and to the cities of Judah, which you have been angry with these seventy years?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Isaiah 40:1–2; Jeremiah 30:10–22"> Yahweh answered the angel who had talked with me, with good words, words of comfort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 2:18; Isaiah 40:1; Zechariah 1:17"> So the angel who had talked with me said to me, “Call out and say, 'Yahweh of hosts says this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have been jealous for Jerusalem and for Zion with a great passion!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:12–17; Jeremiah 51:24; Isaiah 54:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am very angry with the nations that are at ease.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When I was only a little angry with them, they made the disaster worse.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3; Ezra 6:14–15; Zechariah 2:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore Yahweh of hosts says this:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have returned to Jerusalem with mercies. My house will be built within her</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">—this is the declaration of Yahweh of hosts—and the measuring line will be stretched out over Jerusalem!'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Zechariah 2:12; Zephaniah 3:15–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Again call out, saying,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">'Yahweh of hosts says this: My cities will once again overflow with goodness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Yahweh will again comfort Zion, and he will once again choose Jerusalem.'”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 5:13; 2 Kings 24:1–20; Zechariah 5:9"> Then I lifted up my eyes and saw four horns!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 1:21; Zechariah 8:14; Zechariah 2:2"> I spoke to the angel who talked with me, “What are these?” He answered me, “These are the horns that have scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 10:3–5; Zechariah 12:2–6; Zechariah 9:12–16"> Then Yahweh showed me four craftsmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 75:10; Zechariah 1:19; Psalms 75:4–5"> I said, “What are these people coming to do?” He answered, and said, “These are the horns that scattered Judah so that no man would lift up his head. But the blacksmiths have come to terrify them, to cast down the horns of the nations that lifted up a horn against the land of Judah to scatter her.”</span>
