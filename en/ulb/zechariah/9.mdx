<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 1:3–5; Jeremiah 49:23–27; Amos 3:12"> “This is a declaration of Yahweh's word concerning the land of Hadrak and Damascus, its resting place; for the eyes of all humanity and all the tribes of Israel are toward Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:23; Ezekiel 28:12; Ezekiel 28:2–5"> This declaration also concerns Hamath, which borders on Damascus, and it concerns Tyre and Sidon, for they are very wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:33; Job 27:16; Ezekiel 28:4–5"> Tyre has built herself a stronghold and heaped up silver like dust and refined gold like mud in the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:18; Ezekiel 26:17; Isaiah 23:1–7"> Look! The Lord will dispossess her and destroy her strength on the sea, so she will be devoured by fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:26; Revelation 18:9–17; Jeremiah 47:1"> Ashkelon will see and be afraid! Gaza also will tremble greatly! Ekron, her hopes will be disappointed! The king will perish from Gaza, and Ashkelon shall no longer be inhabited!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 1:8; Zephaniah 2:10; 1 Peter 5:5"> Strangers will make their homes in Ashdod, and I will cut off the pride of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 3:12; Ezekiel 16:57–61; Isaiah 60:14–16"> For I will remove their blood from their mouths and their abominations from between their teeth. Then they will become a remnant for our God like a clan in Judah, and Ekron will become like the Jebusites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 54:14; Isaiah 60:18; Isaiah 52:1"> I will camp around my land against enemy armies,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Most interpreters translate the Hebrew words as against enemy armies and some translate the Hebrew as a guard,and so translate the phrase, I will surround my temple as a guard .">a</a></sup>so no one can pass through or return, for no oppressor will overrun them, for now I see with my own eyes!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 21:4–7; Zephaniah 3:14–15; John 12:13–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Shout with great joy, daughter of Zion! Shout with happiness, daughter of Jerusalem!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Behold! Your king is coming to you with righteousness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and is rescuing you. He is humble and is riding on a donkey, on the colt of a donkey.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 1:7; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Hosea 2:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I will cut off the chariot from Ephraim and the horse from Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the bow will be cut off from battle; for he will speak peace to the nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and his dominion will be from sea to sea, and from the River to the ends of the earth!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:14; Exodus 24:8; Psalms 102:19–21"> As for you, because of the blood of my covenant with you, I will set your prisoners free from the pit where there is no water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 42:10; Isaiah 61:7; Lamentations 3:21–22"> Return to the stronghold, prisoners of hope! Even today I am declaring that I will return double to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; Zechariah 10:3–7; Isaiah 49:2"> for I have bent Judah as my bow. I have filled my quiver with Ephraim. I have roused your sons, Zion, against your sons, Greece, and have made you, Zion, like a warrior's sword!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; Isaiah 66:15; Psalms 144:5–6"> Yahweh will appear to them, and his arrows will shoot out like lightning! For my Lord Yahweh will blow the trumpet and will advance with the storms from Teman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Zechariah 10:7; Zechariah 10:5"> Yahweh of hosts will defend them, and they will devour them and defeat the stones of the slings. Then they will drink and shout like men drunk on wine, and they will be filled with wine like bowls, like the corners of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 62:3; Luke 12:32; Haggai 2:23"> So Yahweh their God will rescue them on that day, as the flock of his people. They are the jewels of a crown that will shine on his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 33:17; Isaiah 62:8–9; 1 John 4:8–11"> How good and how beautiful they will be! The young men will flourish on grain and the virgins on sweet wine!”</span>
</div>
