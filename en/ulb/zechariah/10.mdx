<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Zechariah 10:12; Jeremiah 3:18"> I will strengthen the house of Judah and save the house of Joseph; for I will restore them and have mercy on them. <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 9:15; Zechariah 9:17; 1 Samuel 2:1"> Then Ephraim will be like a warrior, and their hearts will rejoice as with wine; their children will see and rejoice. Their hearts will rejoice in me!</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:13; Micah 5:5–6; Isaiah 43:2"> I will pass through the sea of their affliction; I will strike the waves of that sea and will dry up all the depths of the Nile. <span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Isaiah 2:5; Zechariah 10:6"> I will strengthen them in myself, and they will walk in my name—this is Yahweh's declaration.”</span>
