<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 4:3; Malachi 1:13–14; Exodus 12:5"> Now this is what you must do to set them apart to me so that they may serve me as priests. Take one young bull and two rams without blemish,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 6:15; Leviticus 2:15; Leviticus 7:12"> bread without yeast, and cakes without yeast mixed with oil. Also take wafers without yeast rubbed with oil. Make the wafers using fine wheat flour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 6:17; Leviticus 8:26; Leviticus 8:2"> You must put them into a single basket, bring them in the basket, and present them with the bull and the two rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 40:12; Hebrews 10:22; Exodus 26:36"> You must present Aaron and his sons at the entrance to the tent of meeting. You must wash Aaron and his sons in water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 28:2–8; Leviticus 8:7–8"> You must take the garments and clothe Aaron with the coat, the robe of the ephod, the ephod, and the breastpiece, fastening the finely-woven waistband of the ephod around him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 8:9; Exodus 28:36–39"> You must set the turban on his head and put the holy crown on the turban.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 35:25; Leviticus 21:10; John 3:34"> Then take the anointing oil and pour it on his head, and in this way anoint him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 8:13; Exodus 28:40"> You must bring his sons and put coats on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 18:7; Leviticus 8:22–28; Deuteronomy 18:5"> You must clothe Aaron and his sons with sashes and put headbands on them. The work of the priesthood will belong to them by permanent law. In this way you must consecrate Aaron and his sons for them to serve me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 1:4; Leviticus 8:14; Exodus 29:15"> You must all bring the bull before the tent of meeting, and Aaron and his sons must lay their hands on its head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 9:8; Leviticus 9:12; Exodus 29:4"> You must kill the bull before Yahweh at the entrance to the tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 27:2; Leviticus 9:9; Leviticus 8:15"> You must take some of the bull's blood and put it on the horns of the altar with your finger, and you must pour out the rest of the blood at the base of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 2:16; Leviticus 9:10"> You must take all the fat that covers the inner parts, and also take the covering of the liver and the two kidneys with the fat that is on them; burn it all on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 4:11–12; Leviticus 4:21; Exodus 30:10"> But as for the bull's flesh, as well as its skin and dung, you must burn it up outside the camp. It will be a sin offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 29:19; Exodus 29:3; Leviticus 8:18–21"> You must also take the one ram, and Aaron and his sons must lay their hands on its head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 29:11–12"> You must kill the ram, then take its blood and sprinkle it on all sides of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:14; Leviticus 1:13; Leviticus 1:9"> You must cut the ram into pieces and wash its inner parts and its legs, and you must put the inner parts, together with its pieces and with its head,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 8:21; Philippians 4:18; Genesis 22:7"> on the altar. Then burn the whole ram. It will be a burnt offering to Yahweh, a sweet aroma, an offering made to Yahweh by fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 29:3; Leviticus 8:22–29"> You must then take the other ram, and Aaron and his sons must lay their hands on its head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 50:5; Hebrews 10:22; Leviticus 14:16"> Then you must kill the ram and take some of its blood. Put it on the tip of Aaron's right ear, and on the tip of his sons' right ears, on the thumb of their right hands, and on the great toe of their right feet. Then you must sprinkle the blood against the altar on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 9:22; Exodus 29:7; Exodus 29:1"> You must take some of the blood that is on the altar and some of the anointing oil, and sprinkle it all on Aaron and on his garments, and also on his sons and on their garments. Aaron will then be set apart for me, as well as his garments, his sons and his sons' garments with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:13; Leviticus 9:21; Leviticus 9:19"> You must take the ram's fat, the fat tail, the fat that covers the inner parts, the covering of the liver, the two kidneys and the fat on them, and the right thigh—for this ram is for the priests' consecration to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 29:2–3; Leviticus 8:26"> Take one loaf of bread, one cake of bread made with oil, and one wafer out of the basket of bread without yeast that is before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30; Leviticus 8:27; Leviticus 9:21"> You must put these in Aaron's hands and in the hands of his sons and wave them before me for a wave offering before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 8:28; Exodus 29:41; Leviticus 3:16"> You must then take the food from their hands and burn it on the altar with the burnt offering. It will produce a sweet aroma for me; it will be an offering made to me by fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 8:29; Leviticus 7:31; Leviticus 7:34"> You must take the breast of Aaron's ram of dedication and wave it for a wave offering before Yahweh, and it will be your share.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 10:15; Numbers 18:11; Deuteronomy 18:3"> You must set apart to me the breast of the wave offering that is waved, and the thigh that is the contribution for the priests—both the breast that was waved and the thigh that was contributed for Aaron and his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 15:19–20; Leviticus 7:11–38; Leviticus 10:14–15"> This will be a perpetual ordinance for Aaron and his sons. It will be a contribution from the people of Israel to give to Yahweh from their peace offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 18:8; Exodus 28:3–4; Exodus 30:30"> The holy garments of Aaron must also be reserved for his sons after him. They are to be anointed in them and ordained to me in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 20:28; Leviticus 13:5; Acts 20:6–7"> The priest who succeeds him from among his sons, who comes into the tent of meeting to serve me in the holy place, is to wear those garments for seven days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 8:31; 1 Samuel 2:13; Ezekiel 46:20–24"> You must take the ram for the installation of the priests to me and boil its meat in a holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 12:4; Exodus 29:23; Exodus 29:2–3"> Aaron and his sons must eat the ram's meat and the bread that is in the basket at the entrance to the tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 18:4; Leviticus 10:13–18; Psalms 22:26"> They must eat the meat and bread that were given to atone for them and to ordain them, to be set apart to me. No one else may eat that food, because they must treat it as consecrated to me, reserved for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 12:10; Leviticus 8:32; Exodus 29:22"> If any of the meat of the ordination offering, or any of the bread, remains to the next morning, then you must burn it. It must not be eaten because it has been set apart to me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 29:30; Leviticus 14:8–11; Leviticus 8:4–36"> In this way, by following all that I have commanded you to do, you must treat Aaron and his sons. For seven days you must prepare them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 10:11; Hebrews 9:22–23; Exodus 30:28–29"> Every day you must offer a bull as a sin offering for atonement. You must purify the altar by making atonement for it, and you must anoint it in order to set it apart to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 40:10; Matthew 23:19; Exodus 30:28–29"> For seven days you must make atonement for the altar and set it apart it to Yahweh. Then the altar will be completely set apart to me. Whatever touches the altar will be set apart to Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4; 1 Chronicles 16:40; Numbers 28:3–8"> You must regularly offer on the altar every day two lambs a year old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:13–15; 2 Kings 16:15; Psalms 5:3"> One lamb you must offer in the morning, and the other lamb you must offer about sundown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 2:17; Ezekiel 46:14; Joel 2:14"> With the first lamb, offer a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with the fourth part of a hin of oil from pressed olives, and the fourth part of a hin of wine as a drink offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:13–15; 1 Kings 18:29; Psalms 141:2"> You must offer the second lamb about sunset. You must offer the same grain offering as in the morning, and the same drink offering. These will produce a sweet aroma for me; it will be an offering made to me by fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 25:22"> These must be regular burnt offerings throughout your generations, at the entrance to the tent of meeting before Yahweh, where I will meet with you to speak to you there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Kings 8:11; 2 Chronicles 7:1–3; Malachi 3:1"> That is where I will meet with the Israelites; the tent will be set apart for me by my glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Leviticus 21:15; Leviticus 22:9; Leviticus 22:16"> I will set apart the tent of meeting and the altar for these to belong to me alone. I will also set apart Aaron and his sons to serve me as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 26:12; 2 Corinthians 6:16; Exodus 25:8"> I will live among the Israelites and will be their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 20:5"> They will know that I am Yahweh, their God, who brought them out from the land of Egypt so that I might live among them. I am Yahweh, their God.</span>
</div>
