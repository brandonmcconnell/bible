<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–20; Exodus 26:33; Numbers 10:33–36"> Bezalel made the ark of acacia wood. Its length was two and a half cubits; its width was one cubit and a half; and its height was one cubit and a half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 30:3"> He covered it inside and out with pure gold and made for it a border of gold around its top.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> He cast four rings of gold for its four feet, with two rings on one side of it, and two rings on the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Peter 1:7; Numbers 4:14–15"> He made poles of acacia wood and covered them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; 2 Samuel 6:3–7; Numbers 1:50"> He put the poles into the rings on the ark's sides, in order to carry the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Titus 2:14; Romans 3:25"> He made an atonement lid of pure gold. Its length was two and a half cubits, and its width was one and a half cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:2; Psalms 104:4; Psalms 80:1"> Bezalel made two cherubim of hammered gold for the two ends of the atonement lid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> One cherub was for one end of the atonement lid, and other cherub was for the other end. They were made as one piece with the atonement lid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 1:51; Hebrews 1:14; Genesis 28:12"> The cherubim spread out their wings upward and overshadowed the atonement lid with them. The cherubim faced one another and looked toward the center of the atonement lid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:14; Ezekiel 40:39–42; Malachi 1:12"> Bezalel made the table of acacia wood. Its length was two cubits, its width was one cubit, and its height was one and a half cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> He covered it with pure gold and put a border of pure gold around the top.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> He made a surrounding frame for it one handbreadth wide, with a surrounding border of gold for the frame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> He cast for it four rings of gold and attached the rings to the four corners, where the four feet were.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The rings were attached to the frame to provide places for the poles, in order to carry the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He made the poles out of acacia wood and covered them with gold, in order to carry the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:29; 1 Kings 7:50; 2 Kings 12:13"> He made the objects that would be on the table—the dishes, spoons, the bowls, and pitchers to be used to pour out the offerings. He made them out of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Hebrews 9:2; 2 Chronicles 13:11"> He made the lampstand of pure hammered gold. He made the lampstand with its base and shaft. Its cups, its leafy bases, and its flowers were all made of one piece with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Six branches extended out from its sides—three branches extended from one side, and three branches of the lampstand extended from the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The first branch had three cups made like almond blossoms, with a leafy base and a flower, and three cups made like almond blossoms in the other branch, with a leafy base and a flower. It was the same for all six branches extending out from the lampstand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5; Jeremiah 1:11; Exodus 25:33"> On the lampstand itself, the central shaft, there were four cups made like almond blossoms, with their leafy bases and the flowers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:35"> There was a leafy base under the first pair of branches—made as one piece with it, and a leafy base under the second pair of branches—also made as one piece with it. In the same way there was a leafy base under the third pair of branches, made as one piece with it. It was the same for all six branches extending out from the lampstand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Exodus 25:31; Psalms 51:17"> Their leafy bases and branches were all one piece with it, one beaten piece of work of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 1:12; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 1:20–1"> Bezalel made the lampstand and its seven lamps, its tongs and their trays of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He made the lampstand and its accessories with one talent of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:1–5; Hebrews 7:25; Revelation 8:3–4"> Bezalel made the incense altar. He made it with acacia wood. Its length was one cubit, and its width one cubit. It was square, and its height was two cubits. Its horns were made as one piece with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> He covered the incense altar with pure gold—its top, its sides, and its horns. He also made a surrounding border of gold for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> He made two golden rings to be attached to it under its border on its two opposite sides. The rings were holders for poles to carry the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> He made the poles of acacia wood, and he covered them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Hebrews 7:25; 1 John 2:20"> He made the holy anointing oil and the pure fragrant incense, the work of a perfumer.</span>
