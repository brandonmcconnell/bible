<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 28:20; Genesis 35:23"> Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Dan, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Genesis 46:26–27; Judges 8:30"> All the people who were descendants of Jacob were seventy in number. Joseph was already in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 50:26; Genesis 50:24; Acts 7:14–16"> Then Joseph, all his brothers, and all that generation died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 46:3; Genesis 1:28; Deuteronomy 26:5"> The Israelites were fruitful, increased greatly in numbers, and became very strong; the land was filled with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 7:18; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Ecclesiastes 9:15"> Now then a new king arose over Egypt, one who did not know about Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 105:24–25; Ecclesiastes 4:4; James 3:14–16"> He said to his people, “Look, the Israelites are more numerous and stronger than we are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Psalms 83:3–4; Proverbs 1:11"> Come, let us deal with them wisely, otherwise they will continue to grow in numbers, and if war breaks out, they will join our enemies, fight against us, and leave the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 15:13; Genesis 47:11; Exodus 3:7"> So they put taskmasters over them to oppress them with hard labor. The Israelites built store cities for Pharaoh: Pithom and Rameses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 105:24; Exodus 1:9; Romans 8:28"> But the more the Egyptians oppressed them, the more the Israelites increased in numbers and spread. So the Egyptians began to dread the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:20"> The Egyptians made the Israelites work rigorously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Numbers 20:15; Exodus 2:23"> They made their lives bitter with hard service with mortar and brick, and with all kinds of work in the fields. All their required work was hard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then the king of Egypt spoke to the Hebrew midwives; the name of the one was Shiphrah, and the other Puah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Matthew 21:38; Revelation 12:4"> He said, “When you assist the Hebrew women on the birthstool, observe when they give birth. If it is a son, then you must kill him; but if it is a daughter, then she may live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Exodus 1:21; Matthew 10:28"> But the midwives feared God and did not do as the king of Egypt ordered them; instead, they let the baby boys live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:4; 2 Samuel 13:28"> The king of Egypt summoned the midwives and said to them, “Why have you done this, and let the baby boys live?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 2:4–24; 2 Samuel 17:19–20; 1 Samuel 21:2"> The midwives answered Pharaoh, “The Hebrew women are not like the Egyptian women. They are vigorous and have finished giving birth before a midwife comes to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10; Ecclesiastes 8:12; Proverbs 11:18"> God protected these midwives. The people increased in numbers and became very strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 11:38; 1 Samuel 2:35; Psalms 37:3"> Because the midwives feared God, he gave them families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Proverbs 1:16; Exodus 1:16"> Pharaoh ordered all his people, “You must throw every son that is born into the river, but every daughter you will let live.”</span>
