<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 33:10–12; Exodus 15:27; Exodus 17:1"> The people journeyed on from Elim, and all the community of Israelites came to the wilderness of Sin, which is between Elim and Sinai, on the fifteenth day of the second month after their departure from the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:10; Exodus 15:24; Psalms 106:25"> The whole community of Israelites complained against Moses and Aaron in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 11:4–5; Numbers 20:3–5; Exodus 17:3"> The Israelites said to them, “If only we had died by Yahweh's hand in the land of Egypt when we were sitting by the pots of meat and were eating bread to the full. For you have brought us out into this wilderness to kill our whole community with hunger.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3; Psalms 78:24–25; Deuteronomy 8:2"> Then Yahweh said to Moses, “I will rain down bread from heaven for you. The people will go out and gather a day's portion every day so that I may test them to see whether or not they will walk in my law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 16:22–23; Exodus 35:2–3; Leviticus 25:21–22"> It will come about on the sixth day, that they will gather twice as much as what they gathered every day before, and they will cook what they bring in.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Numbers 16:28; Exodus 16:12–13"> Then Moses and Aaron said to all the people of Israel, “In the evening you will know that it is Yahweh who has brought you out from the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 40:5; Numbers 16:11; John 11:40"> In the morning you will see Yahweh's glory, for he hears your complaining against him. Who are we for you to complain against us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:2; 1 Samuel 8:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> Moses also said, “You will know this when Yahweh gives you meat in the evening and bread in the morning to the full—for he has heard the complaints that you speak against him. Who are Aaron and I? Your complaints are not against us; they are against Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 16:16; Exodus 16:2; Exodus 16:8"> Moses said to Aaron, “Say to all the community of the people of Israel, 'Come near before Yahweh, for he has heard your complaints.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 16:7; Numbers 16:19; Numbers 16:42"> It came about, as Aaron spoke to the whole community of the people of Israel, that they looked toward the wilderness, and, behold, Yahweh's glory appeared in the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Ezekiel 39:22; Exodus 6:7"> “I have heard the complaints of the people of Israel. Speak to them and say, 'In the evening you will eat meat, and in the morning you will be filled with bread. Then you will know that I am Yahweh your God.'”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 11:9; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:27–28"> It came about in the evening that quails came up and covered the camp. In the morning the dew lay round about the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 11:7–9; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:24"> When the dew was gone, there on the surface of the wilderness were thin flakes like frost on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:4; John 6:58; Exodus 16:31"> When the people of Israel saw it, they said one to another, “What is it?” They did not know what it was. Moses said to them, “It is the bread that Yahweh has given you to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 16:36; Exodus 16:18; Exodus 16:32–33"> This is the command that Yahweh has given: 'You must gather, each one of you, the amount you need to eat, an omer for each person of the number of your people. This is how you will gather it: Gather enough to eat for every person who lives in your tent.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The people of Israel did so. Some gathered more, some gathered less.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> When they measured it with an omer measure, those who had gathered much had nothing left over, and those who had gathered little had no lack. Each person gathered enough to meet their need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 12:10; Exodus 23:18; Matthew 6:34"> Then Moses said to them, “No one must leave any of it until morning.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 10:14; Numbers 16:15; Ephesians 4:26"> However, they did not listen to Moses. Some of them left some of it until morning, but it bred worms and became foul. Then Moses became angry with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–11; Ecclesiastes 12:1; 2 Corinthians 6:2"> They gathered it morning by morning. Each person gathered enough to eat for that day. When the sun became hot, it melted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 16:5; Leviticus 25:22; Exodus 16:16"> It came about that on the sixth day they gathered twice as much bread, two omers for each person. All the leaders of the community came and told this to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 23:3; Exodus 31:15; Exodus 20:8–11"> He said to them, “This is what Yahweh has said: 'Tomorrow is a solemn rest, a holy Sabbath in Yahweh's honor. Bake what you want to bake, and boil what you want to boil. All that remains over, set it aside for yourselves until morning.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:20; Exodus 16:33"> So they set it aside until morning, as Moses had instructed. It did not become foul, nor was there any worm in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 16:23; Exodus 16:29; Nehemiah 9:14"> Moses said, “Eat that food today, for today is a day reserved as a Sabbath to honor Yahweh. Today you will not find it in the fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:13; Exodus 20:9–11; Ezekiel 46:1"> You will gather it during six days, but the seventh day is the Sabbath. On the Sabbath there will be no manna.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 20:4"> It came about on the seventh day that some of the people went out to gather manna, but they found none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 78:10; Psalms 106:13; Numbers 14:11"> Then Yahweh said to Moses, “How long will you refuse to keep my commandments and my laws?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 31:13; Isaiah 58:13–14; Ezekiel 20:12"> See, Yahweh has given you the Sabbath. So on the sixth day he is giving you bread for two days. Each of you must stay in his own place; no one must go out from his place on the seventh day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:12–14; Leviticus 23:3; Hebrews 4:9"> So the people rested on the seventh day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Song of Songs 2:3; Numbers 11:6–9; Exodus 16:15"> The people of Israel called that food “manna.” It was white like coriander seed, and its taste was like wafers made with honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 105:5; Luke 22:19; Psalms 111:4–5"> Moses said, “This is what Yahweh has commanded: 'Let an omer of manna be kept throughout your people's generations so that your descendants might see the bread with which I fed you in the wilderness, after I brought you out from the land of Egypt.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4"> Moses said to Aaron, “Take a pot and put an omer of manna into it. Preserve it before Yahweh to be kept throughout the people's generations.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 27:21; Exodus 25:21; Exodus 25:16"> As Yahweh commanded Moses, Aaron stored it beside the covenant decrees in the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:20–21; Joshua 5:12; Deuteronomy 8:2–3"> The people of Israel ate manna forty years until they came to inhabited land. They ate it until they came to the borders of the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 16:16; Exodus 16:32–33"> Now an omer is a tenth of an ephah.</span>
</div>
