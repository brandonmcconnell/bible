<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Exodus 32:13; Exodus 32:7"> Then Yahweh spoke to Moses, “Go from here, you and the people whom you have brought up out of the land of Egypt. Go to the land about which I made an oath to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, when I said, 'I will give it to your descendants.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 32:34; Joshua 24:11; Exodus 23:27–31"> I will send an angel before you, and I will drive out the Canaanites, Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 32:9–10; Exodus 3:8; Acts 7:51"> Go to that land, which is flowing with milk and honey, but I will not go up with you, because you are a stubborn people. I might destroy you on the way.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 14:39; Numbers 14:1; Ezekiel 26:16"> When the people heard these troubling words, they mourned, and no one put on any jewelry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:21; Numbers 16:45–46; Exodus 33:3–4"> Yahweh had said to Moses, “Say to the Israelites, 'You are a stubborn people. If I went among you for even one moment, I would destroy you. So now, take off your jewelry so that I may decide what to do with you.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 33:4; Exodus 32:3; Jeremiah 2:19"> So the Israelites wore no jewelry from Mount Horeb onward.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6–7; Exodus 29:42–43; Proverbs 15:29"> Moses took a tent and pitched it outside the camp, some distance from the camp. He called it the tent of meeting. Everyone who asked Yahweh for anything went out to the tent of meeting, outside the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 16:27"> When Moses would go out to the tent, all the people would stand up at their tent entrances and look at Moses until he had gone inside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 99:7; Exodus 31:18; Exodus 13:21–22"> Whenever Moses entered the tent, the pillar of cloud would come down and stand at the tent entrance, and Yahweh would speak with Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 8:22; 1 Kings 8:14; Exodus 4:31"> Whenever all the people saw the pillar of cloud stand at the entrance to the tent, they would get up and worship, every man at his own tent entrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 12:8; Deuteronomy 34:10; Exodus 24:13"> Yahweh would speak to Moses face to face, as a man speaks to his friend. Then Moses would return to the camp, but his servant Joshua son of Nun, a young man, would stay in the tent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 33:17; Jeremiah 1:5; John 10:14–15"> Moses said to Yahweh, “See, you have been saying to me, 'Take this people on their journey,' but you have not let me know whom you will send with me. You have said, 'I know you by name, and you have also found favor in my eyes.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 119:33; Psalms 25:4; Psalms 27:11"> Now if I have found favor in your eyes, show me your ways so that I may know you and continue to find favor in your eyes. Remember that this nation is your people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 21:44; Matthew 11:28; Joshua 1:5"> Yahweh answered, “My own presence will go with you, and I will give you rest.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:9; Psalms 4:6; Exodus 33:3"> Moses said to him, “If your presence does not go with us, do not take us up from here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:7; Numbers 14:14; 1 Kings 8:53"> For otherwise, how will it be known that I have found favor in your eyes, I and your people? Will it not only be if you go with us so that I and your people are different from all the other peoples that are on the surface of the earth?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; 1 John 5:14–15; Exodus 33:12"> Yahweh said to Moses, “I will also do this thing that you have requested, for you have found favor in my eyes, and I know you by name.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:23; Exodus 33:20; Titus 2:13"> Moses said, “Please show me your glory.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 9:15–18; Ephesians 1:6–8; Jeremiah 31:14"> Yahweh said, “I will make all my goodness pass before you, and I will proclaim my name 'Yahweh' before you. I will be gracious to whom I will be gracious, and I will show mercy on whom I will show mercy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 32:30; John 1:18; 1 Timothy 6:16"> But Yahweh said, “You may not see my face, for no one can see me and live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:31; Zechariah 3:7; Joshua 20:4"> Yahweh said, “See, here is a place by me; you will stand on this rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 91:1; Isaiah 2:21; Psalms 91:4"> While my glory passes by, I will put you in a crevice of the rock and cover you with my hand until I have passed by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 33:20; John 1:18; 1 Timothy 6:16"> Then I will take away my hand, and you will see my back, but my face will not be seen.”</span>
</div>
