<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 28:3; Exodus 35:30–35; Exodus 31:1–6"> So Bezalel and Oholiab and every skilled person to whom Yahweh has given skill and ability to know how to do any work in the construction of the holy place are to do the work according to all that Yahweh has commanded.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 4:17; Hebrews 5:4; Exodus 31:6"> Moses summoned Bezalel, Oholiab, and every skillful person in whose mind Yahweh had given skill, and whose heart stirred within him to come and do the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:27; Exodus 35:5–21"> They received from Moses all the offerings that the Israelites had brought for constructing the holy place. The people were still bringing freewill offerings every morning to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:10; Luke 12:42; Matthew 24:45"> So all the skilled people working on the holy place came from the work that they had been doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:6–10; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 2 Chronicles 24:14"> The craftsmen told Moses, “The people are bringing much more than enough for doing the work that Yahweh has commanded us to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> So Moses instructed that no one in the camp should bring any more offerings for the construction of the holy place. Then the people stopped bringing these gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:10"> They had more than enough materials for all the work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 6:23; Exodus 26:1–37; Ezekiel 1:5–28"> So all the craftsmen among them constructed the tabernacle with ten curtains made from fine linen and blue, purple, and scarlet wool with the designs of cherubim. This was the work of Bezalel, the very skilled craftsman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The length of each curtain was twenty-eight cubits, the width four cubits. All the curtains were of the same size.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; Exodus 26:3; Psalms 122:3"> Bezalel joined five curtains to each other, and the other five curtains he also joined to each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:4"> He made loops of blue along the outer edge of the end curtain of one set, and he did the same along the outer edge of the end curtain in the second set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 26:5; Exodus 26:10"> He made fifty loops on the first curtain and fifty loops on the edge of the end curtain in the second set. So the loops were opposite to each another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 12:20"> He made fifty gold clasps and joined the curtains together with them so that the tabernacle became united.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–13"> Bezalel made curtains of goat hair for a tent over the tabernacle; he made eleven of these curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The length of each curtain was thirty cubits, and the width of each curtain was four cubits. Each of the eleven curtains was of the same size.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He joined five curtains to each other and the other six curtains to each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He made fifty loops on the edge of the end curtain of the first set, and fifty loops along the edge of the end curtain that joined the second set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Bezalel made fifty bronze clasps to join the tent together so that it might be one piece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:14"> He made for the tabernacle a covering of ram skins dyed red, another covering of fine leather to go above that.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 25:5; Numbers 25:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> Bezalel made vertical frames out of acacia wood for the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The length of each frame was ten cubits, and the width of each frame was one and a half cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Each frame had two wooden pegs for joining the frames together. He did this for all the frames of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> He made the frames for the tabernacle in this way: twenty frames for the south side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Bezalel made forty silver bases to go under the twenty frames. There were two bases under one frame to join the frames together, and also two bases under each of the other frames to join frames together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For the second side of the tabernacle, on the north side, he made twenty frames</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and their forty silver bases. There were two bases under the first frame, two bases under the next frame, and so on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:22; Exodus 26:27"> For the back of the tabernacle on the west, Bezalel made six frames.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> He made two frames for the back corners of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:32; Ephesians 3:18–19; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> These frames were separate at the bottom, but joined at the top in one ring. He made two of them in this way for the two corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:25"> There were eight frames, together with their silver bases. There were sixteen bases in all, two bases under the first frame, two bases under the next frame, and so on.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 26:26–29; Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:5"> Bezalel made crossbars of acacia wood—five for the frames of the one side of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 26:26"> five crossbars for the frames of the other side of the tabernacle, and five crossbars for the frames for the back side of the tabernacle to the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> He made the crossbar in the center of the frames, that is, halfway up, to reach from end to end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> He covered the frames with gold. He made their rings of gold, for them to serve as holders for the crossbars, and he covered the bars with gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 10:20; Exodus 40:21"> Bezalel made the curtain of blue, purple, and scarlet wool, and of fine linen, with designs of cherubim, the work of a skillful workman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18"> He made for the curtain four pillars of acacia wood, and he covered them with gold. He also made gold hooks for the pillars, and he cast for them four silver bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 26:36–37; Exodus 40:28"> He made a hanging for the tent entrance. It was made of blue, purple, and scarlet wool, using fine linen, the work of an embroiderer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 27:10"> He also made the hanging's five pillars with hooks. He covered their tops and their rods with gold. Their five bases were made of bronze.</span>
</div>
