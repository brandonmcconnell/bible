<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:14; Deuteronomy 6:20; Exodus 24:3–4"> “Now these are the decrees that you must set before them:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12–15; Exodus 12:44; Deuteronomy 15:18"> 'If you buy a Hebrew servant, he is to serve for six years, and in the seventh year he will go free without paying anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12–14"> If he came by himself, he must go free by himself; if he is married, then his wife must go free with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> If his master gave him a wife and she bore him sons or daughters, the wife and her children will belong to her master, and he must go free by himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:14–15; Deuteronomy 15:16–17; Isaiah 26:13"> But if the servant plainly says, “I love my master, my wife, and my children; I will not go out free,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:17; Leviticus 25:40; Deuteronomy 19:17–18"> then his master must bring him to God. The master must bring him to a door or doorpost, and his master must bore his ear through with an awl. Then the servant will serve him for life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; Exodus 21:2–3"> If a man sells his daughter as a female servant, she must not go free as the male servants do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 28:8; Deuteronomy 20:7; Malachi 2:11–15"> If she does not please her master, who has designated her for himself, then he must let her be bought back. He has no right to sell her to a foreign people. He has no such right, since he has treated her deceitfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> If her master designates her as a wife for his son, he must treat her the same as if she were his daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1–6"> If he takes another wife for himself, he must not diminish her food, clothing, or her marital rights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> But if he does not provide these three things for her, then she can go free without paying any money.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; Matthew 26:52; Leviticus 24:17"> Whoever strikes a man so that he dies, that person must surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 20:2–9; 1 Samuel 24:18; Deuteronomy 4:41–43"> If the man did not do it with premeditation, but instead by accident, then I will fix a place to where he can flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28–34; Hebrews 10:26; Numbers 15:30–31"> If a man willfully attacks his neighbor and kills him according to a cunning plan, then you must take him, even if he is at God's altar, so that he may die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:18–21; Proverbs 30:17; 1 Timothy 1:9"> Whoever hits his father or mother must surely be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:7; 1 Timothy 1:10; Exodus 22:4"> Whoever kidnaps a person—whether the kidnapper sells him, or that person is found in his hand—that kidnapper must be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:20; Mark 7:10–11; Deuteronomy 27:16"> Whoever curses his father or his mother must surely be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Deuteronomy 25:11; 2 Samuel 14:6"> If men fight and one hits the other with a stone or with his fist, and that person does not die, but is confined to his bed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:29; Zechariah 8:4"> then if he recovers and is able to walk about using his staff, the man who struck him must pay for the loss of his time; he must also pay for his complete recovery. But that man is not guilty of murder.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 29:19; Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:19"> If a man hits his male servant or his female servant with a staff, and if the servant dies as a result of the blow, then that man must surely be punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 25:44–46"> However, if the servant lives for a day or two, the master must not be punished, for he will have suffered the loss of the servant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:18–19; Exodus 21:30; Deuteronomy 16:18"> If men fight together and hurt a pregnant woman so that she miscarries, but there is no other injury to her, then the guilty man must surely be fined as the woman's husband demands it from him, and he must pay as the judges determine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:31; Leviticus 24:19"> But if there is serious injury, then you must give a life for a life,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Matthew 5:38–40; Matthew 7:2"> an eye for an eye, a tooth for a tooth, a hand for a hand, a foot for a foot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> a burn for a burn, a wound for a wound, or a bruise for a bruise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Job 31:13–15; Psalms 72:12–14"> If a man hits the eye of his male servant or of his female servant and destroys it, then he must let the servant go free in compensation for his eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> If he knocks out a tooth of his male servant or female servant, he must let the servant go free as compensation for the tooth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 21:32; Leviticus 20:15–16; Genesis 9:5–6"> If an ox gores a man or a woman to death, the ox must surely be stoned, and its flesh must not be eaten; but the ox's owner must be acquitted of guilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:1–9"> But if the ox had a habit of goring in the past, and its owner was warned but did not keep it in, and the ox has killed a man or a woman, that ox must be stoned, and its owner also must be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 21:22; Exodus 30:12; Numbers 35:31–33"> If a payment is required for his life, he must pay whatever he is required to pay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> If the ox has gored a man's son or daughter, the ox's owner must do what this decree requires him to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 27:3–9"> If the ox gores a male servant or a female servant, the ox's owner must pay thirty shekels of silver, and the ox must be stoned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 28:10; Jeremiah 18:20; Ecclesiastes 10:8"> If a man opens a pit, or if a man digs a pit and does not cover it, and an ox or a donkey falls into it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 22:6; Exodus 21:29–30; Exodus 22:14"> the owner of the pit must repay the loss. He must give money to the dead animal's owner, and the dead animal will become his.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> If one man's ox hurts another man's ox so that it dies, then they must sell the live ox and divide its price, and they must also divide the dead ox.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> But if it was known that the ox had a habit of goring in time past, and its owner has not kept it in, he must surely pay ox for ox, and the dead animal will become his own.</span>
</div>
</div>
