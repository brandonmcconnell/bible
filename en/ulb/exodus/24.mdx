<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 11:16; Exodus 6:23; Leviticus 10:1–2"> Then Yahweh said to Moses, “Come up to me—you, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy of Israel's elders, and worship me at a distance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 24:15; Exodus 24:13; Hebrews 10:21–22"> Moses alone may come near to me. The others must not come near, nor may the people come up with him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 19:8; Exodus 24:7; Joshua 24:22"> Moses went and told the people all of Yahweh's words and decrees. All the people answered with one voice and said, “We will do all the words that Yahweh has said.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:18; Deuteronomy 31:9; Joshua 4:8–9"> Then Moses wrote down all of Yahweh's words. Early in the morning, Moses built an altar at the foot of the mountain and arranged twelve stone pillars, so that the stones would represent the twelve tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:12; Leviticus 3:1–17; Exodus 19:22"> He sent some Israelite young men to offer burnt offerings and sacrifice fellowship offerings of oxen to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19; Exodus 24:8; Hebrews 9:18"> Moses took half of the blood and put it into basins; he sprinkled the other half onto the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:11–13; 1 Thessalonians 5:27; Exodus 24:3–4"> He took the book of the Covenant and read it aloud to the people. They said, “We will do all that Yahweh has spoken. We will be obedient.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–5; Exodus 24:6; Ezekiel 36:25"> Then Moses took the blood and sprinkled it onto the people. He said, “This is the blood of the covenant that Yahweh has made with you by giving you this promise with all these words.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 24:1"> Then Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy of Israel's elders went up the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:18; Ezekiel 10:1; Genesis 32:30"> They saw the God of Israel. Under his feet there was a pavement made of sapphire stone, as clear as the sky itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 15:23–24; Exodus 18:12; Genesis 31:54"> God did not lay a hand on the Israelite leaders. They saw God, and they ate and drank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:22; Exodus 31:18; Exodus 32:15–16"> Yahweh said to Moses, “Come up to me on the mountain and stay there. I will give you the tablets of stone and the law and commandments that I have written, so that you may teach them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 33:11; Numbers 11:28; Exodus 3:1"> So Moses set out with his assistant Joshua and went up the mountain of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 17:10; Exodus 17:12; Genesis 22:5"> Moses had said to the elders, “Stay here and wait for us until we come to you. Aaron and Hur are with you. If anyone has a dispute, let him go to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Matthew 17:5; Exodus 19:16"> So Moses went up the mountain, and the cloud covered it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 16:10; Numbers 16:42; Ezekiel 1:28"> Yahweh's glory settled on Mount Sinai, and the cloud covered it for six days. On the seventh day he called to Moses from within the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Deuteronomy 4:24; Exodus 3:2"> The appearance of Yahweh's glory was like a devouring fire on the top of the mountain in the eyes of the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:9; Exodus 34:28; Deuteronomy 10:10"> Moses entered the cloud and went up the mountain. He was up the mountain for forty days and forty nights.</span>
