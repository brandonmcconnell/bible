<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 31:10; Exodus 35:23; Exodus 35:19"> With the blue, purple, and scarlet wool, they made finely-woven garments for service in the holy place. They made Aaron's garments for the holy place, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 28:6–12; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 25:7"> Bezalel made the ephod of gold, of blue, purple, and scarlet wool, and of fine twined linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 36:8; Exodus 26:1"> They hammered gold sheets and cut them into wires, to work them into the blue, purple, and scarlet wool, and into the fine linen, the work of a skillful workman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> They made shoulder pieces for the ephod, attached at its two upper corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Matthew 28:20; Isaiah 11:5"> Its finely-woven waistband was like the ephod; it was made of one piece with the ephod, made of fine twined linen that was gold, blue, purple, and scarlet, just as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 28:9; Ezekiel 28:13; Job 28:16"> They crafted the onyx stones, enclosed in settings of gold, set with engravings as on a signet, and engraved with the names of Israel's twelve sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 4:7; Exodus 28:12; Exodus 28:29"> Bezalel put them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod, as stones to remind Yahweh of Israel's twelve sons, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Leviticus 8:8–9; Isaiah 59:17"> He made the breastpiece, the work of a skillful workman, fashioned like the ephod. He made it of gold, of blue, purple, and scarlet wool, and of fine linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> It was square. They folded the breastpiece double. It was one span long and one span wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 28:21; Exodus 28:16–17; Revelation 21:19–21"> They set in it four rows of precious stones. The first row had a ruby, a topaz, and a garnet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 28:18; Ezekiel 28:13"> The second row had an emerald, a sapphire, and a diamond.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The third row had a jacinth, an agate, and an amethyst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The fourth row had a beryl, an onyx, and a jasper. The stones were mounted in gold settings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:12"> The stones were arranged by the names of Israel's twelve sons, each in order by name. They were like the engraving on a signet ring, each name standing for one of the twelve tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 10:28; John 17:12; 1 Peter 1:5"> On the breastpiece they made chains like cords, braided work of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:12"> They made two settings of gold and two gold rings, and they attached the two rings to the two corners of the breastpiece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> They put the two braided chains of gold in the two rings at the corners of the breastpiece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 28:14; Song of Songs 1:10"> They attached the other two ends of the braided chains to the two settings. They attached them to the shoulder pieces of the ephod at its front.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> They made two rings of gold and put them on the two other corners of the breastpiece, on the edge next to the inner border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 26:3"> They made two more gold rings and attached them to the bottom of the two shoulder pieces of the front of the ephod, close to its seam above the finely-woven waistband of the ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; 1 Corinthians 1:25; 1 Corinthians 1:27"> They tied the breastpiece by its rings to the ephod's rings with a blue cord, so that it might be attached just above the ephod's finely-woven waistband. This was so that the breastpiece might not become unattached from the ephod. This was done as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 28:31–35"> Bezalel made the robe of the ephod entirely of blue fabric, the work of a weaver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> It had an opening for the head in the middle. The opening had a woven edge round about so that it did not tear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 28:33; Galatians 5:22"> On the bottom hem, they made pomegranates of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and of fine linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 28:33–34; Song of Songs 4:13; Psalms 89:15"> They made bells of pure gold, and they put the bells between the pomegranates all around on the bottom edge the robe, between the pomegranates—</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 28:34; Song of Songs 4:13; Song of Songs 6:7"> a bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate—on the edge of the robe for Aaron to serve in. This was as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:18; 1 Peter 1:13; Isaiah 61:10"> They made the coats of fine linen for Aaron and for his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Ezekiel 44:18; Exodus 28:39"> They made the turban of fine linen, the ornate headbands of fine linen, the linen undergarments of fine linen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 28:39"> and the sash of fine linen and of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, the work of an embroiderer. This was as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Hebrews 7:26; Hebrews 1:3"> They made the plate of the holy crown of pure gold; they engraved on it, like the engraving on a signet, “Holy to Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> They attached to the turban a blue cord to the top of the turban. This was as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 39:42–43; Matthew 28:20; Exodus 25:40"> So the work on the tabernacle, the tent of meeting, was finished. The people of Israel did everything. They followed all the instructions that Yahweh had given to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 35:11–19; Exodus 31:7–11; Exodus 36:1–38"> They brought the tabernacle to Moses—the tent and all its equipment, its clasps, frames, bars, posts, and bases;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> the covering of ram skins dyed red, the covering of sea cow leather, and the curtain to conceal</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 25:17; Hebrews 9:8"> the ark of the testimony, as well as the poles and the atonement lid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; 1 Kings 7:48"> They brought the table, all its utensils, and the bread of the presence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Exodus 27:21; Matthew 5:14–16"> the lampstand of pure gold and its lamps in a row, with its accessories and the oil for the lamps;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:7; Exodus 37:29; Exodus 30:3"> the golden altar, the anointing oil and the fragrant incense; the hanging for the tabernacle entrance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 8:64; Exodus 38:30"> the bronze altar with its bronze grate and its poles and utensil and the large basin with its base.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 27:9–16"> They brought the hangings for the courtyard with its posts and bases, and the curtain for the courtyard entrance; its ropes and tent pegs; and all the equipment for the service of the tabernacle, the tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 28:2; Exodus 31:10; Exodus 39:1"> They brought the fine woven garments for serving in the holy place, the holy garments for Aaron the priest and his sons, for them to serve as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 35:10; Exodus 23:21–22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> Thus the people of Israel did all the work as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27; Leviticus 9:22–23; 2 Samuel 6:18"> Moses examined all the work, and, behold, they had done it. As Yahweh had commanded, in that way they did it. Then Moses blessed them.</span>
