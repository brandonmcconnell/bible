<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 8:3; Exodus 37:25–28; Exodus 40:5"> You must make an altar to burn incense. You must make it with acacia wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 27:2"> Its length must be one cubit, and its width one cubit. It must be square, and its height must be two cubits. Its horns must be made as one piece with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; Exodus 25:24"> You must cover the incense altar with pure gold—its top, its sides, and its horns. You must make a surrounding border of gold for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:27; Exodus 26:29; Exodus 27:7"> You must make two golden rings to be attached to it under its border on its two opposite sides. The rings must be holders for poles to carry the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:13; Exodus 25:27"> You must make the poles of acacia wood, and you must cover them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:21–22; Exodus 30:36; Leviticus 16:13"> You must put the incense altar before the curtain that is by the ark of the testimony. It will be before the atonement lid that is over the ark of the testimony, where I will meet with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:28; 1 Chronicles 23:13; Exodus 27:20–21"> Aaron must burn fragrant incense every morning. He must burn it when he tends the lamps,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; Hebrews 7:25"> and Aaron lights the lamps again in the evening so incense will burn on it regularly before Yahweh, throughout your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1"> But you must offer no other incense on the incense altar, nor any burnt offering or grain offering. You must pour no drink offering on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:18; Hebrews 9:22–23; Leviticus 16:29–30"> Aaron must make atonement on its horns once a year. With the blood of the sin offering he will make atonement for it once a year throughout your generations. It is completely set apart to Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; 1 Chronicles 27:24; Exodus 38:25–26"> “When you take a census of the Israelites, then each person must give a ransom for his life to Yahweh. You must do this after you count them, so that there will be no plague among them when you count them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:25; Numbers 3:47; Ezekiel 45:12"> Everyone who is counted in the census is to pay half a shekel of silver, according to the weight of the shekel of the sanctuary (a shekel is the same as twenty gerahs). This half shekel will be an offering to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:20; Numbers 1:18; Numbers 1:3"> Everyone who is counted, from twenty years old and up, must give this offering to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; Leviticus 17:11; Exodus 30:12"> When the people give this offering to me to make atonement for their lives, the rich must not give more than the half shekel, and the poor must not give less.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:32–33; Numbers 16:40; Exodus 38:25–31"> You must receive this atonement money from the Israelites and you must allocate it to the work of the tent of meeting. It must be a reminder to the Israelites before me, to make atonement for your lives.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 40:7; Exodus 38:8; 1 Kings 7:23"> “You must also make a large bronze basin with a bronze stand, a basin for washing. You must put it between the tent of meeting and the altar, and you must put water in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Exodus 40:31–32; Psalms 26:6"> Aaron and his sons must wash their hands and their feet with the water in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–3; Hebrews 12:28–29; 1 Chronicles 13:10"> When they go into the tent of meeting or when they go near to the altar to serve me by burning an offering, they must wash with water so that they do not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 28:43; Exodus 27:21"> They must wash their hands and feet so that they do not die. This must be a permanent law for Aaron and his descendants throughout their people's generations.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:19; Song of Songs 4:14; Ezekiel 27:22"> “Take these fine spices: five hundred shekels of flowing myrrh, 250 shekels of sweet-smelling cinnamon, 250 shekels of sweet-smelling cane,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 45:8; Exodus 29:40; Numbers 3:47"> five hundred shekels of cassia, measured by the weight of the shekel of the sanctuary, and one hin of olive oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; 1 Chronicles 9:30; Exodus 40:9"> You must make holy anointing oil with these ingredients, the work of a perfumer. It will be a holy anointing oil, reserved for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 7:1; 1 John 2:20; Isaiah 61:1"> You must anoint the tent of meeting with this oil, as well as the ark of the testimony,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> the table and all its utensils, the lampstand and its equipment, the incense altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> the altar for burnt offerings with all its equipment, and the basin with its stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Exodus 29:37; Leviticus 6:18"> You must set them apart to me so that they may be holy to me. Anything that touches them will also be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 8:12; Exodus 28:3; Leviticus 8:30"> You must anoint Aaron and his sons and set them aside to me so that they may serve me as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Leviticus 21:10; Psalms 89:20"> You must say to the Israelites, 'This must be an anointing oil that is set apart to Yahweh throughout your people's generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 30:25; Exodus 30:37–38; Leviticus 21:10"> It must not be applied to people's skin, nor must you make any oil like it with the same formula, because it is set apart to Yahweh. You must regard it in this manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 30:38; Genesis 17:14; Exodus 12:15"> Whoever makes perfume like it, or whoever puts any of it on someone, that person must be cut off from his people.'”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Exodus 25:6; Exodus 30:23"> Yahweh said to Moses, “Take spices—stacte, onycha, and galbanum—sweet spices along with pure frankincense, each in equal amounts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 2:13; Exodus 30:25; John 12:3"> Make it into the form of incense, blended by a perfumer, seasoned with salt, pure and set apart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Exodus 30:6; Exodus 16:34"> You will grind it into a very fine mixture. Put part of it in front of the ark of the testimony, which is in the tent of meeting, where I will meet with you. You will regard it as very holy to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:32–33"> As for this incense that you will make, you must not make any with the same formula for yourselves. It must be most holy to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:33"> Whoever makes anything like it to use as a perfume must be cut off from his people.”</span>
</div>
