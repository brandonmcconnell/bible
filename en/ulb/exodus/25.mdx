<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Nehemiah 11:2; Ezra 2:68"> “Tell the Israelites to take an offering for me from every person who is motivated by a willing heart. You must receive these offerings for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:9; Job 28:2"> These are the offerings that you must receive from them: gold, silver, and bronze;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Genesis 41:42; Ezekiel 16:10"> blue, purple, and scarlet material; fine linen; goats' hair;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 26:37–1; Exodus 26:14–15; Exodus 36:20"> ram skins dyed red and sea cow hides; acacia wood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:37; Exodus 40:24–25"> oil for the sanctuary lamps; spices for the anointing oil and the fragrant incense;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Exodus 28:6–30"> onyx stones and other precious stones to be set for the ephod and breastpiece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Kings 6:13; Revelation 21:3"> Let them make me a sanctuary so that I may live among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Hebrews 8:5; Acts 7:44"> You must make it exactly as I will show you in the plans for the tabernacle and for all its equipment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Exodus 37:1–3; Deuteronomy 10:1–3"> They are to make an ark of acacia wood. Its length must be two and a half cubits; its width will be one cubit and a half; and its height will be one cubit and a half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 25:24; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:4"> You must cover it inside and out with pure gold, and you must make on it a border of gold around its top.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 27:7; Exodus 25:26; Exodus 25:15"> You must cast four rings of gold for it, and put them on the ark's four feet, with two rings on one side of it, and two rings on the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:15; Numbers 4:11; Exodus 27:6"> You must make poles of acacia wood and cover them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> You must put the poles into the rings on the ark's sides, in order to carry the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:8; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> The poles must remain in the rings of the ark; they must not be taken from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:26; 1 Kings 8:9; Hebrews 9:4"> You must put into the ark the covenant decrees that I will give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 37:6; Hebrews 4:16"> You must make an atonement lid of pure gold. Its length must be two and a half cubits, and its width must be a cubit and a half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:20; Hebrews 9:5; 1 Kings 8:6–7"> You must make two cherubim of hammered gold for the two ends of the atonement lid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Make one cherub for one end of the atonement lid, and the other cherub for the other end. They must be made as one piece with the atonement lid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:18; 1 Kings 8:7; Hebrews 9:5"> The cherubim must spread out their wings upward and overshadow the atonement lid with them. The cherubim must face one another and look toward the center of the atonement lid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:34; Romans 10:4; Exodus 25:16–17"> You must put the atonement lid on top of the ark, and you must put into the ark the covenant decrees that I am giving you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:42–43; Numbers 7:89; Isaiah 37:16"> It is at the ark that I will meet with you. I will speak with you from my position above the atonement lid. It will be from between the two cherubim over the ark of the testimony that I will speak to you about all the commands I will give you for the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 9:2; Exodus 37:10–16; 1 Kings 7:48"> You must make a table of acacia wood. Its length must be two cubits; its width must be one cubit, and its height must be a cubit and a half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; 1 Kings 6:20–22"> You must cover it with pure gold and put a border of gold around the top.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:2; Exodus 30:3"> You must make a surrounding frame for it one handbreadth wide, with a surrounding border of gold for the frame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> You must make for it four rings of gold and attach the rings to the four corners, where the four feet were.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Exodus 25:28"> The rings must be attached to the frame to provide places for the poles, in order to carry the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Acts 9:15; Numbers 10:17"> You must make the poles out of acacia wood and cover them with gold so that the table may be carried with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 4:7; Exodus 37:16; Revelation 3:20"> You must make the dishes, spoons, pitchers, and bowls to be used to pour out drink offerings. You must make them of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–9; Matthew 12:4; Exodus 39:36"> You must regularly set the bread of the presence on the table before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 7:49; Zechariah 4:2; Exodus 37:17–24"> You must make a lampstand of pure hammered gold. The lampstand is to be made with its base and shaft. Its cups, its leafy bases, and its flowers are to be all made of one piece with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Six branches must extend out from its sides—three branches must extend from one side, and three branches of the lampstand must extend from the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 17:4–8; Jeremiah 1:11–12; Zechariah 4:3"> The first branch must have three cups made like almond blossoms, with a leafy base and a flower, and three cups made like almond blossoms in the other branch, with a leafy base and a flower. It must be the same for all six branches extending out from the lampstand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> On the lampstand itself, the central shaft, there must be four cups made like almond blossoms, with their leafy bases and the flowers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> There must be a leafy base under the first pair of branches—made as one piece with it, and a leafy base under the second pair of branches—also made as one piece with it. In the same way there must be a leafy base under the third pair of branches, made as one piece with it. It must be the same for all six branches extending out from the lampstand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:18; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 9:15"> Their leafy bases and branches must all be one piece with it, one beaten piece of work of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 8:2; Revelation 1:20–1; Leviticus 24:2–4"> You must make the lampstand and its seven lamps, and set up its lamps for them to give light from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Kings 12:13; Jeremiah 52:18; 2 Kings 25:14"> The tongs and their trays must be made of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Use one talent of pure gold to make the lampstand and its accessories.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Exodus 26:30; Acts 7:44"> Be sure to make them after the pattern that you are being shown on the mountain.</span>
