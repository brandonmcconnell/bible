<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 11:32; Luke 11:29–30; 2 Kings 14:25"> Now the word of Yahweh came to Jonah son of Amittai, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 18:20–21; Genesis 10:11; Isaiah 58:1"> “Get up and go to Nineveh, that great city, and speak out against it, because their wickedness has risen up before me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 139:7–12; Genesis 4:16; Joshua 19:46"> But Jonah got up to run away from the presence of Yahweh and go to Tarshish. He went down to Joppa and found a ship going to Tarshish. So he paid the fare and boarded the ship to go with them to Tarshish, away from the presence of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 107:23–31; Matthew 8:24–27; Exodus 10:19"> But Yahweh sent out a great wind on the sea and it became a mighty storm on the sea. Soon it appeared that the ship was going to be broken up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Acts 27:18–19; 1 Kings 18:26"> Then the sailors became very afraid and each man cried out to his own god. They threw the ship's cargo into the sea to lighten it. But Jonah had gone down into the innermost parts of the ship, and he was lying there deeply asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jonah 3:9; Psalms 107:28–29; Ephesians 5:14"> So the captain came to him and said to him, “What are you doing sleeping? Get up! Call upon your god! Maybe your god will notice us and we will not perish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:33; Joshua 7:13–18; 1 Samuel 10:20–21"> They all said to each other, “Come, let us cast lots, so that we may know who is the cause of this evil that is happening to us.” So they threw lots, and the lot fell to Jonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 47:3; 1 Samuel 30:13; Joshua 7:19"> Then they said to Jonah, “Please tell us who is the cause of this evil that is happening to us. What is your occupation, and where did you come from? What is your country, and from which people are you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 146:5–6; Psalms 95:5–6; Nehemiah 9:6"> Jonah said to them, “I am a Hebrew; and I fear Yahweh, the God of heaven, who has made the sea and the dry land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jonah 1:3; Job 27:22; Joshua 7:25"> Then the men were even more afraid and said to Jonah, “What is this that you have done?” For the men knew that he was running away from the presence of Yahweh, because he had told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:1–6; Micah 6:6–7; 1 Samuel 6:2–3"> Then they said to Jonah, “What should we do to you so that the sea will calm down for us?” For the sea became more and more violent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:17; 1 Chronicles 21:17; Joshua 7:12"> Jonah said to them, “Pick me up and throw me into the sea. Then the sea will be calm for you, for I know that it is because of me that this great storm is happening to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; Job 34:29"> Nevertheless, the men rowed hard to get them back to the land, but they could not do it because the sea was becoming more and more violent against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 115:3; Psalms 135:6; Ephesians 1:11"> Therefore they cried out to Yahweh and said, “We beg you, Yahweh, we beg you, do not let us perish on account of this man's life, and do not lay upon us the guilt of his death, because you, Yahweh, have done just as it pleased you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 8:24; Psalms 93:3–4; Psalms 107:29"> So they picked up Jonah and threw him into the sea, and the sea stopped raging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 50:14; Genesis 8:20; Daniel 6:26"> Then the men feared Yahweh very much. They offered sacrifices to Yahweh and made vows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 12:40; Luke 11:30; Matthew 16:4"> Now Yahweh had prepared a great fish to swallow Jonah, and Jonah was in the stomach of the fish three days and three nights.</span>
