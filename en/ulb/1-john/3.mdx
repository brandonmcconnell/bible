<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 1:12; Galatians 3:26; 2 Corinthians 6:18"> See what kind of love the Father has given to us, that we should be called children of God, and this is what we are. For this reason, the world does not know us, because it did not know him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some older versions leave out, and this is what we are .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:21; Colossians 3:4; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> Beloved, we are now children of God, and it has not yet been revealed what we will be. We know that when Christ appears, we will be like him, for we will see him just as he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 John 2:6; 2 Peter 3:14"> Everyone who has this hope fixed on him purifies himself just as he is pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 5:17; Romans 3:20; Romans 4:15"> Everyone who sins is committing acts of lawlessness, for sin is lawlessness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 9:28; 1 Peter 3:18"> You know that Christ was revealed in order to take away sins, and in him there is no sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 John 1:11; 1 John 2:4; 1 John 3:9"> No one who remains in him will keep on sinning. No one who continues to sin has seen him or known him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 2:29; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Romans 2:13"> Dear children, do not let anyone lead you astray. The one who does righteousness is righteous, just as Christ is righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14; John 8:44; Colossians 2:15"> The one who commits sin is from the devil, for the devil has sinned from the beginning. For this reason the Son of God was revealed, so that he would destroy the devil's works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 5:18; 1 Peter 1:23; John 1:13"> Whoever has been born from God does not continue to sin because God's seed remains in him. He cannot continue to sin because he has been born of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 4:8; 3 John 1:11; Luke 6:35"> In this the children of God and children of the devil are revealed: Whoever does not do what is righteous is not from God, neither is the one who does not love his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 15:12; 2 John 1:5; 1 John 4:7"> For this is the message that you have heard from the beginning: We should love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Genesis 4:4–15; Proverbs 29:27"> We should not be like Cain, who was from the evil one and murdered his brother. Why did he kill him? Because his works were evil and his brother's righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 17:14; Luke 21:17"> Do not be amazed, my brothers, if the world hates you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 13:35; 1 John 5:2; 1 John 4:20–21"> We know that we have passed out of death into life, because we love the brothers. Anyone who does not love remains in death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:21–22; Proverbs 26:24–26; Revelation 21:8"> Anyone who hates his brother is a murderer. You know that no murderer has eternal life residing in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:9–11; Ephesians 5:2; John 13:34"> By this we know love, because Christ laid down his life for us. We also ought to lay down our lives for the brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Isaiah 58:7–10; Deuteronomy 15:7–11"> But whoever has the world's goods, sees his brother in need, and shuts up his heart of compassion from him, how does the love of God remain in him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 2:15–16; Romans 12:9; 1 Peter 1:22"> My dear children, let us not love in word nor in tongue, but in actions and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; 1 John 3:21; Hebrews 6:10–11"> It is by this we know that we are from the truth, and we assure our hearts before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 139:1–4; Jeremiah 17:10; Hebrews 4:13"> For if our hearts condemn us, God is greater than our hearts, and he knows all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:28; Hebrews 4:16; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> Beloved, if our hearts do not condemn us, we have confidence toward God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 15:7; John 9:31; 1 John 5:14"> Whatever we ask we will receive from him, because we keep his commandments and do the things that are pleasing before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 6:29; John 13:34; 1 Peter 1:22"> This is his commandment: that we should believe in the name of his Son Jesus Christ and love one another, just as he gave us this commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16; 1 John 4:15–16; 1 John 4:12–13"> The one who keeps God's commandments remains in him, and God remains in him. By this we know that he remains in us, by the Spirit whom he gave to us.</span>
