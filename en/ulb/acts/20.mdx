<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:5; Acts 19:21; 1 Timothy 1:3"> After the uproar was over, Paul sent for the disciples and after he encouraged them, he said farewell and left to go into Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 4:1; Colossians 1:28"> When he had gone through those regions and had spoken many words of encouragement to them, he came to Greece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 20:19; 2 Corinthians 11:26; Acts 9:23–24"> After he had spent three months there, a plot was formed against him by the Jews as he was about to sail for Syria, so he decided to return through Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 19:29; Ephesians 6:21; 2 Timothy 4:20"> Accompanying him as far as Asia were Sopater son of Pyrrhus from Berea; Aristarchus and Secundus, both from the Thessalonian believers; Gaius of Derbe; Timothy; and Tychicus and Trophimus from Asia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 16:8; Acts 16:10–11; 2 Timothy 4:13"> But these men had gone before us and were waiting for us at Troas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 12:3; Acts 16:12; Exodus 23:15"> We sailed away from Philippi after the days of unleavened bread, and in five days we came to them in Troas. There we stayed for seven days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:2; Acts 2:42; John 20:19"> On the first day of the week, when we were gathered together to break bread, Paul spoke to the believers. He was planning to leave the next day, so he kept speaking until midnight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Luke 22:12"> There were many lamps in the upper room where we had come together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 13:36; Matthew 26:40–41; Jonah 1:5–6"> In the window was sitting a young man named Eutychus, who fell into a deep sleep. As Paul spoke even longer, this young man, still sleeping, fell down from the third story and was picked up dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 5:39; Matthew 9:23–24; 1 Kings 17:21–22"> But Paul went down, stretched himself out on him, and embraced him. Then he said, “Do not be upset any more, for he is alive.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 20:7; Acts 20:9"> Then he went upstairs again and broke bread and ate. After talking with them much longer until dawn, he left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:16; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; 2 Corinthians 1:4"> They brought back the boy alive and were greatly comforted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 6:46; Mark 1:35; Mark 6:31–33"> We ourselves went ahead of Paul by ship and sailed away to Assos, where we planned to take Paul on board. This is what he himself desired to do, because he planned to go by land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When he met us at Assos, we took him onto the ship and went to Mitylene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:20; Acts 20:17"> Then we sailed from there and arrived the next day opposite the island of Chios. The following day we touched at the island of Samos, and the day after we came to the city of Miletus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:8; Acts 2:1; Acts 19:21"> For Paul had decided to sail past Ephesus, so that he would not spend any time in Asia; for he was hurrying to be in Jerusalem for the day of Pentecost, if it were at all possible for him to do so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Titus 1:5; Acts 11:30; 1 Timothy 5:17"> From Miletus he sent men to Ephesus and called to himself the elders of the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:5–6; Acts 18:19; Acts 19:1"> When they had come to him, he said to them,<br />“You yourselves know, from the first day that I set foot in Asia, how I always spent my time with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; 2 Corinthians 2:4; Romans 12:11"> I kept serving the Lord with all lowliness of mind and with tears, and in sufferings that happened to me because of the plots of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:27; 2 Timothy 4:2; Acts 5:42"> You know how I did not keep back from declaring to you anything that was useful, and how I taught you in public and from house to house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 11:18; 1 John 5:11–13"> testifying both Jews and Greeks about repentance toward God and of faith in our Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 4:14; 2 Corinthians 5:14; 2 Peter 1:14"> Now look, I am going to Jerusalem, compelled by the Spirit, not knowing what will happen to me there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 9:16; 1 Thessalonians 3:3; Acts 21:4"> except that the Holy Spirit warns to me in every city that chains and sufferings await me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6–8; 2 Corinthians 4:1; 2 Corinthians 12:10"> But I do not consider my life is valuable to myself, if only I may finish the race and complete the ministry that I received from the Lord Jesus, to testify to the gospel of the grace of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 28:31; Acts 20:38; Matthew 4:23"> Now look, I know that you all, among whom I went about proclaiming the kingdom, will see my face no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Ezekiel 3:18–21; Ezekiel 33:2–9"> Therefore I testify to you this day, that I am innocent of the blood of any man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 20:20; 1 Thessalonians 2:4; Galatians 1:7–10"> For I did not hold back from declaring to you the whole will of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 5:2–3; 1 Timothy 4:16; Isaiah 40:11"> Therefore be careful about yourselves, and about all the flock of which the Holy Spirit has made you overseers. Be careful to shepherd the church of God, which he purchased with his own blood.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Instead of with his own blood , some ancient copies read, with the blood of his own Son .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; 2 Peter 2:1; John 10:12"> I know that after my departure, vicious wolves will come in among you and will not spare the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19–20; 2 Timothy 4:3–4; Proverbs 23:33"> I know that from even among you some men shall come and distort the truth in order to draw away the disciples after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:8; Acts 20:19; Acts 19:10"> So be on guard. Remember that for three years I did not stop instructing every one of you with tears night and day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 1:12; Ephesians 1:14; Colossians 3:24"> Now I entrust you to God and to the word of his grace, which is able to build you up and to give you the inheritance among all those who are being sanctified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; 1 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Corinthians 9:18"> I coveted no man's silver, gold, or clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 18:3; 1 Corinthians 4:12; 2 Thessalonians 3:8–9"> You yourselves know that these hands served my own needs and the needs of those who were with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 19:17; 2 Corinthians 9:6–12"> In all things I gave you an example of how you should help the weak by working, and of how you should remember the words of the Lord Jesus, words that he himself said: “It is more blessed to give than to receive.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 21:5; Luke 22:41; Philippians 4:6"> After he had spoken in this way, he knelt down and prayed with them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:4; 1 Corinthians 16:20; 1 Thessalonians 5:26"> There was a lot of crying and they embraced Paul and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 15:3; 1 Corinthians 16:11; Acts 20:25"> They were sad most of all because of what he had said, that they would never see his face again. Then they escorted him to the ship.</span>
</div>
