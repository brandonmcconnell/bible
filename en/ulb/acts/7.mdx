<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 18:19–21; Matthew 26:61–62; Acts 6:13–14"> The high priest said, “Are these things true?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 22:1; Genesis 11:31; Psalms 29:3"> Stephen said, “Brothers and fathers, listen to me: The God of glory appeared to our father Abraham when he was in Mesopotamia, before he lived in Haran;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 12:1; Hebrews 11:8; Joshua 24:3"> he said to him, 'Leave your land and your relatives, and go into the land that I will show you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 12:4–5; Genesis 11:31–32; Isaiah 41:9"> Then he left the land of the Chaldeans and lived in Haran; from there, after his father died, God brought him into this land, where you live now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 13:15"> He gave none of it as an inheritance to him, no, not even enough to set a foot on. But he promised—even though Abraham had no child yet—that he would give the land as a possession to him and to his descendants after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 15:13; Exodus 12:40–41; Galatians 3:17"> God was speaking to him like this, that his descendants would live for a while in a foreign land, and that the inhabitants there would bring them into slavery and treat them badly for four hundred years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 3:12; Psalms 105:27–36; Nehemiah 9:9–11"> 'But I will judge the nation that they serve,' said God, 'and after that they will come out and worship me in this place.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 35:23–26; Genesis 17:9–14; Genesis 21:1–4"> Then God gave Abraham the covenant of circumcision, so Abraham became the father of Isaac and circumcised him on the eighth day; Isaac became the father of Jacob, and Jacob the father of the twelve patriarchs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 39:2; Psalms 105:17; Isaiah 43:2"> Because the patriarchs were jealous of Joseph, they sold him into Egypt; but God was with him</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 3:4; 2 Timothy 4:18; Proverbs 2:6"> and rescued him from all his troubles. He gave Joseph favor and wisdom in the presence of Pharaoh, king of Egypt, who had made him governor over Egypt and over all his household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 41:54–57; Genesis 45:11; Genesis 42:5"> Now there came a famine over all Egypt and Canaan, and great suffering, and our fathers could find no food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:1–24; Genesis 43:2"> But when Jacob heard that there was grain in Egypt, he sent out our fathers on their first trip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 45:1–18; Genesis 46:31–10"> On their second trip Joseph made himself known to his brothers, and Joseph's family became known to Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 46:26–27; Deuteronomy 10:22; Exodus 1:5"> Joseph sent his brothers back to tell Jacob his father to come to Egypt, along with all his relatives, seventy-five persons in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 49:33; Exodus 1:6; Genesis 46:3–7"> So Jacob went down into Egypt, and he died, he and our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 24:32; Genesis 23:16; Exodus 13:19"> They were carried over to Shechem and laid in the tomb that Abraham had bought for a price in silver from the sons of Hamor in Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 1:7–12; Acts 7:6; Acts 13:17"> As the time of the promise approached, the promise that God had made to Abraham, the people grew and multiplied in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 1:8"> until there arose another king over Egypt, a king who did not know about Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 105:25; Exodus 1:9–22; Revelation 12:4–5"> He deceived our people and forced our fathers to expose their newborn infants so they would not be kept alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 11:23; Exodus 2:2–10; 1 Samuel 16:12"> At that time Moses was born; he was very beautiful before God and was nourished for three months in his father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24; Exodus 2:2–10; Deuteronomy 32:26"> When he was placed outside, Pharaoh's daughter adopted him and raised him as her own son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Daniel 1:17–20; 2 Chronicles 9:22"> Moses was educated in all the wisdom of the Egyptians, and he was mighty in his words and works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 2:11–12; Proverbs 21:1; Hebrews 11:24–26"> But when he was about forty years old, it came into his heart to visit his brothers, the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Acts 7:28; John 18:25–27"> Seeing an Israelite being mistreated, Moses defended him and avenged him who was oppressed by striking the Egyptian:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 15:7; Mark 9:32; 2 Kings 5:1"> he thought that his brothers would understand that God by his hand was rescuing them, but they did not understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 2:13–15; Proverbs 18:19; 1 John 3:11–15"> On the next day he came to some Israelites as they were quarreling; he tried to put them at peace with each other; he said, 'Men, you are brothers; why are you hurting one another?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Luke 12:14; Acts 7:54"> But the one who had wronged his neighbor pushed him away, and said, 'Who made you a ruler and a judge over us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Would you like to kill me, as you killed the Egyptian yesterday?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 18:2–4; Exodus 2:14–22; Exodus 4:19–20"> Moses ran away after hearing this; he became a foreigner in the land of Midian, where he became the father of two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 3:1–2; Acts 7:35; Isaiah 43:2"> When forty years were past, an angel appeared to him in the wilderness of Mount Sinai, in a flame of fire in a bush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 3:3–4"> When Moses saw the fire, he marveled at the sight; and as he approached to look at it, there came a voice of the Lord, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Matthew 22:32; Job 4:14"> 'I am the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, and of Isaac, and of Jacob.' Moses trembled and did not dare to look.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 3:5; Joshua 5:15; Ecclesiastes 5:1"> The Lord said to him, 'Take off the sandals from your feet, for the place where you are standing is holy ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 3:7–10; Exodus 4:31; Psalms 106:44"> I have certainly seen the suffering of my people who are in Egypt; I have heard their groaning, and I have come down to rescue them; now come, I will send you to Egypt.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Luke 19:14; Psalms 113:7–8"> This Moses whom they rejected, when they said, 'Who made you a ruler and a judge?'—he was the one whom God sent as both a ruler and deliverer. God sent him by the hand of the angel who appeared to Moses in the bush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 12:41; Exodus 14:21; Exodus 33:1"> Moses led them out of Egypt, after doing miracles and signs in Egypt and at the Sea of Reeds, and in the wilderness during forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–19; Acts 3:22–23; John 18:37"> It is the same Moses who said to the people of Israel, 'God will raise up a prophet for you from among your brothers, a prophet like me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Romans 3:2; Hebrews 5:12; 1 Peter 4:11"> This is the man who was in the assembly in the wilderness with the angel who had spoken to him on Mount Sinai. This is the man who was with our fathers; this is the man who received living words to give to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 14:3–4; Numbers 11:5; Nehemiah 9:16–17"> This is the man whom our fathers refused to obey; they pushed him away from themselves, and in their hearts they turned back to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 32:1; Exodus 32:23"> At that time they said to Aaron, 'Make us gods who will lead us. As for this Moses, who led us out of the land of Egypt, we do not know what has happened to him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 106:19–21; Nehemiah 9:18; Revelation 9:20"> So they made a calf in those days and brought a sacrifice to the idol, and rejoiced because of the work of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Amos 5:25–26; Ezekiel 20:39; Jeremiah 19:13"> But God turned and gave them up to worship the stars in the sky, as it is written in the book of the prophets,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'Did you offer to me slain beasts and sacrifices</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for forty years in the wilderness, house of Israel?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Amos 5:26–27; Exodus 20:4–5; Deuteronomy 5:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">You accepted the tabernacle of Molech</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the star of the god Rephan,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the images that you made to worship them:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will carry you away beyond Babylon.'</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 38:21; Hebrews 8:5"> Our fathers had the tabernacle of the testimony in the wilderness, just as God commanded when he spoke to Moses, that he should make it like the pattern that he had seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 44:2; Joshua 18:1; Joshua 23:9"> Later, our fathers, under Joshua, received the tabernacle and brought it with them when they took possession of the land. God took the land from the nations and drove them out before the face of our fathers. The tabernacle remained in the land until the time of David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 132:1–5; Acts 13:22; 2 Samuel 7:1–5"> who found favor in the sight of God, and he asked if he might find a dwelling place for the God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Kings 8:20; 1 Kings 6:37–38; 2 Samuel 7:13"> But it was Solomon who built the house for God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 17:24–25; Isaiah 66:1–2; 1 Kings 8:27"> However, the Most High does not live in houses made with hands, as the prophet says,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Isaiah 66:1; Psalms 11:4; John 4:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">'Heaven is my throne, and the earth is the footstool for my feet.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">What kind of house can you build for me? says the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or what is the place for my rest?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Psalms 33:6–9; Acts 14:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Did my hand not make all these things?'</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:16; Ephesians 4:30; Isaiah 63:10"> You people who are stiff-necked and uncircumcised in heart and ears, you always resist the Holy Spirit; you act just as your fathers acted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 5:12; 2 Chronicles 36:16; 1 Kings 19:10"> Which of the prophets did your fathers not persecute? They killed the prophets who appeared in advance of the coming of the Righteous One; and you have now become the betrayers and murderers of him also,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Hebrews 2:2; Galatians 3:19; Acts 7:38"> you people who received the law that angels had established, but you did not keep it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Acts 5:33; Psalms 35:16; Matthew 25:30"> Now when the council members heard these things, they were cut to the heart, and they ground their teeth at Stephen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Mark 16:19; Hebrews 1:3; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> But he, being full of the Holy Spirit, looked up intently into heaven and saw the glory of God; and he saw Jesus standing at the right hand of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 26:64–65; Acts 10:11; Revelation 11:19"> Stephen said, “Look, I see the heavens opened, and the Son of Man standing at the right hand of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Zechariah 7:11; Psalms 58:4; Acts 23:27"> At this the council members covered their ears, and shouting out with a loud voice, altogether they rushed at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Acts 22:20; Acts 8:1; Acts 6:13"> They dragged him out of the city where they began to stone him, and the witnesses laid down their outer clothing at the feet of a young man named Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Luke 23:46; Psalms 31:5; Acts 22:16"> As they were stoning Stephen, he was calling out to the Lord and saying, “Lord Jesus, receive my spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Luke 23:34; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> He knelt down and called out with a loud voice, “Lord, do not hold this sin against them.” When he had said this, he fell asleep.</span>
