<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5–7; Acts 13:31; John 20:26"> After his suffering, he presented himself alive to them with many convincing proofs. For forty days he appeared to them, and he spoke about the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 24:49; John 14:16; John 15:26"> When he was meeting together with them, he commanded them not to leave Jerusalem, but to wait for the promise of the Father, about which, he said, “You heard from me</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Acts 11:15–16; Luke 3:16"> that John indeed baptized with water, but you shall be baptized in the Holy Spirit in a few days.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 19:11; Luke 17:20; Daniel 7:27"> When they were assembled together they asked him, “Lord, is this the time you will restore the kingdom to Israel?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–2; Mark 13:32"> He said to them, “It is not for you to know the times or the seasons which the Father has determined by his own authority.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; Mark 16:15; Romans 15:19"> But you will receive power, when the Holy Spirit comes upon you, and you will be my witnesses both in Jerusalem and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Luke 24:50–51; Mark 16:19"> When the Lord Jesus had said these things, as they were looking up, he was raised up, and a cloud hid him from their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 24:4; John 20:12; Revelation 7:14"> While they were looking intensely to heaven as he went, suddenly, two men stood by them in white clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; Mark 13:26"> They said, “You men of Galilee, why do you stand here looking into heaven? This Jesus who has been taken up from you into heaven, will return in the same manner as you saw him going into heaven.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:52; Matthew 21:1; Luke 24:50"> Then they returned to Jerusalem from the Mount of Olives, which is near to Jerusalem, a Sabbath day's journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 20:8; Luke 22:12; Mark 14:15"> When they arrived, they went up into the upper chamber, where they were staying. They were Peter, John, James, Andrew, Philip, Thomas, Bartholomew, Matthew, James son of Alphaeus, Simon the Zealot, and Judas son of James.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 6:4; Ephesians 6:18; Colossians 4:2"> They were all united as one, as they diligently continued in prayer. Included were the women, Mary the mother of Jesus, and his brothers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:6; Acts 21:20; Revelation 3:4"> In those days Peter stood up in the midst of the brothers, about 120 people, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 13:18; Psalms 55:12–15"> “Brothers, it was necessary that the scripture should be fulfilled, that the Holy Spirit spoke before by the mouth of David concerning Judas, who guided the ones who arrested Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 1:25; Mark 3:19; John 17:12"> For he was one of us and received his share of the benefits of this ministry.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:3–10; Matthew 26:14–15; Psalms 55:23"> (Now this man bought a field with the earnings he received for his wickedness, and there he fell headfirst, and his body burst open, and all his intestines poured out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 2:22; Matthew 28:15; Acts 21:40"> All those living in Jerusalem heard about this, so they called that field in their language “Akeldama,” that is, “Field of Blood.”)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 69:25; Psalms 109:8–15; Acts 1:25"> “For it is written in the Book of Psalms,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'Let his field be made desolate,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and do not let even one person live there';</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">'Let someone else take his position of leadership.'</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 15:27; Luke 10:1–2; Numbers 27:17"> It is necessary, therefore, that one of the men who accompanied us all the time the Lord Jesus went in and out among us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 1:2; Acts 4:33; Acts 1:8–9"> beginning from the baptism of John to the day that he was taken up from us, must be a witness with us of his resurrection.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 1:26"> They put forward two men, Joseph called Barsabbas, who was also named Justus, and Matthias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Acts 15:8; Hebrews 4:13"> They prayed and said, “You, Lord, know the hearts of all people, so reveal which of these two is the one whom you have chosen</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:2; John 6:70–71; John 17:12"> to take the place in this ministry and apostleship from which Judas turned away to go to his own place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 16:8; Joshua 18:10; 1 Samuel 14:41–42"> They cast lots for them, and the lot fell to Matthias, and he was numbered with the eleven apostles.</span>
</div>
