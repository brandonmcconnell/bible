<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 22:4; Acts 6:12; Acts 23:6–9"> As Peter and John were speaking to the people, the priests and the captain of the temple and the Sadducees came upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 17:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–14; Acts 24:14–15"> They were deeply troubled because Peter and John were teaching the people and proclaiming in Jesus the resurrection from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 5:18; Matthew 10:16–17; Acts 16:19–24"> They arrested them and put them in jail until the next morning, since it was now evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 2:41; 2 Corinthians 2:14–17; 2 Timothy 2:9–10"> But many of the people who had heard the message believed; and the number of the men who believed was about five thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 4:8; Matthew 27:1–2; Luke 20:1"> It came about on the next day that their rulers, elders and scribes gathered together in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 3:2; John 18:24; Matthew 26:3"> Annas the high priest was there, and Caiaphas, and John, and Alexander, and all who were relatives of the high priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 11:28; Acts 5:40"> When they had set Peter and John in their midst, they asked them, “By what power, or in what name, have you done this?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–20; Acts 2:4"> Then Peter, filled with the Holy Spirit, said to them, “You rulers of the people, and elders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15–17; Acts 3:7; John 10:32"> if we this day are being questioned concerning a good deed done to a sick man, by what means was this man made well?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:29–32; Acts 3:6; Acts 2:36"> May this be known to you all and to all the people of Israel, that in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, whom you crucified, but whom God raised from the dead, that this man stands here before you healthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Isaiah 28:16; Mark 12:10–12"> Jesus Christ is the stone which you as builders despised but which has been made the head cornerstone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 14:6; 1 Corinthians 3:11; John 3:36"> There is no salvation in any other person, for there is no other name under heaven given among men by which we must be saved.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27; John 7:15; Matthew 11:25"> Now when they saw the boldness of Peter and John, and realized that they were ordinary, uneducated men, they were surprised, becoming aware that Peter and John had been with Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 19:36; Acts 3:8–12; Acts 4:21"> Because they saw the man who was healed standing with them, they had nothing to say against this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 26:30–32; Acts 5:34–42; Matthew 5:22"> But after they had commanded the apostles to leave the council meeting, they talked among themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10; John 12:18"> They said, “What shall we do to these men? For the fact that a remarkable miracle has been done through them is known to everyone who lives in Jerusalem; we cannot deny it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:28; Romans 15:18–22; Acts 5:24"> But in order that it spreads no further among the people, let us warn them not to speak anymore to anyone in this name.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:40; Luke 24:46–48; Acts 5:20"> They called Peter and John in and commanded them not to speak at all nor teach in the name of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Exodus 1:17; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> But Peter and John answered and said to them, “Whether it is right in the sight of God to obey you rather than him, you judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 22:15; Jeremiah 20:9; 1 Corinthians 9:16–17"> We are not able to stop speaking about the things we have seen and heard.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 5:26; Matthew 21:46; Luke 22:2"> After further warning Peter and John, they let them go. They were unable to find any excuse to punish them, because all of the people were praising God for what had been done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 5:5; Acts 9:33; Luke 13:11"> The man who had experienced this miracle of healing was more than forty years old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Acts 12:11–12; Psalms 119:63"> After they were set free, Peter and John came to their own people and reported all that the chief priests and the elders had said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Jeremiah 32:17; Nehemiah 9:6"> When they heard it, they raised their voices together to God and said, “Lord, you made the heavens and the earth and the sea, and all that is in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–6; Acts 1:16; Acts 2:30"> You spoke by the Holy Spirit through the mouth of your servant, our father David,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'Why did the Gentile nations rage,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the peoples imagine useless things?'</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 2:2; Revelation 17:12–14; Daniel 9:24"> You said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'The kings of the earth set themselves together,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the rulers gathered together</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">against the Lord, and against his Christ.'</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Acts 4:30; Luke 9:22"> Indeed, both Herod and Pontius Pilate, together with the Gentiles and the people of Israel, gathered together in this city against your holy servant Jesus, whom you anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:23; Isaiah 46:10; Genesis 50:20"> They gathered together to do all that your hand and your plan had decided in advance would happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:13; Acts 14:3; Philippians 1:14"> Now, Lord, look upon their warnings and grant to your servants to speak your word with all boldness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 4:27; Acts 3:6; Acts 5:12"> Stretch out your hand to heal, and to give signs and wonders through the name of your holy servant Jesus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Acts 2:2; Acts 2:4"> When they finished praying, the place where they were gathered together was shaken, and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit and they spoke the word of God with boldness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:10; Ephesians 4:2–6; 1 Peter 3:8"> The great number of those who believed were of one heart and soul. No one said that anything he possessed was his own, but they had everything in common.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 1:16; Acts 1:22; Acts 1:8"> With great power the apostles were proclaiming their testimony about the resurrection of the Lord Jesus, and great grace was upon them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 2:45; Luke 12:33; Luke 16:9"> There was no person among them who lacked anything, for all who owned title to lands or houses sold them and brought the money of the things that were sold</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 5:2; Acts 2:45; 2 Corinthians 8:20–21"> and laid it at the apostles' feet, and it was distributed to each one according to their need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Galatians 2:9; Galatians 2:13; Galatians 2:1"> Joseph, a Levite, a man from Cyprus whom the apostles called Barnabas (which means Son of Encouragement),</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 5:1–2; Acts 4:34–35; Matthew 19:29"> sold a field and brought the money and laid it at the apostles' feet.</span>
