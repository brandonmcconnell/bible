<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:1; Philippians 4:16; Acts 20:4"> Now when they had passed through the cities of Amphipolis and Apollonia, they came to the city of Thessalonica, where there was a synagogue of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 9:20; Acts 17:17; Acts 18:4"> Paul, as his custom was, went to them, and for three Sabbath days reasoned with them from the scriptures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:28; Acts 9:22; Luke 24:32"> He was opening the scriptures and explaining that it was necessary for the Christ to suffer and to rise again from the dead. He said, “This Jesus whom I proclaim to you is the Christ.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 17:12; Acts 13:43; Acts 14:4"> Some of the Jews were persuaded and joined Paul and Silas, including a large number of devout Greeks, and not a few of the leading women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 5:26; Acts 17:13; Romans 16:21"> But the unbelieving Jews, being moved with jealousy, took certain wicked men from the marketplace, gathered a crowd together, and set the city in an uproar. Assaulting the house of Jason, they wanted to bring Paul and Silas out to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 24:5; Acts 16:19–21; Acts 28:22"> But when they did not find them, they dragged Jason and certain other brothers before the officials of the city, crying, “These men who have turned the world upside down have come here also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 23:2; Acts 16:21; John 19:12"> These men whom Jason has welcomed act against the decrees of Caesar; they say that there is another king—Jesus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 2:3; John 11:48"> The crowd and the officials of the city were disturbed when they heard these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 17:5"> But after the officials made Jason and the rest pay money as security, then they let them go.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 17:2; 1 Thessalonians 2:2; Acts 17:13–14"> That night the brothers sent Paul and Silas to Berea. When they arrived there, they went into the synagogue of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 34:16; Proverbs 2:1–5; John 5:39"> Now these people were more noble than those in Thessalonica, for they received the word with all readiness of mind, examining the scriptures daily to see whether these things were so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:14; Acts 14:1; Acts 13:50"> Therefore many of them believed, including some influential Greek women and many men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:14–16; Proverbs 28:25; Acts 6:12"> But when the Jews of Thessalonica learned that Paul was also proclaiming the word of God at Berea, they went there and stirred up and troubled the crowds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 17:10; Matthew 10:23; Acts 9:25"> Then immediately, the brothers sent Paul to go to the sea, but Silas and Timothy stayed there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 18:5; 1 Thessalonians 3:1; Acts 18:1"> Those who were leading Paul took him as far as the city of Athens. As they left Paul there, they received from him instructions for Silas and Timothy to come to him as quickly as possible.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 119:158; 2 Peter 2:7; Psalms 119:136"> Now while Paul was waiting for them in Athens, his spirit was provoked within him as he saw the city full of idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 13:16; Acts 14:1–4; Acts 17:2–4"> So he reasoned in the synagogue with the Jews and others who worshiped God, as well as in the marketplace with those who happened to be there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20–21; Colossians 2:8; Luke 11:53"> But also some of the Epicurean and Stoic philosophers encountered him. Some said, “What is this babbler trying to say?” Others said, “He seems to be one who calls people to follow strange gods,” because he was proclaiming Jesus and the resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 1:27; Acts 17:22; Acts 24:24"> They took Paul and brought him to the Areopagus, saying, “May we know this new teaching which you were speaking?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; Hosea 8:12; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> For you bring some strange things to our ears. Therefore, we want to know what these things mean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:16–17; Colossians 4:5; Ephesians 5:16"> (Now all the Athenians and the strangers living there spent their time in nothing but either telling or listening about something new.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 25:19; Acts 19:35; Acts 17:16"> So Paul stood in the middle of the Areopagus and said,<br />“You men of Athens, I see that you are very religious in every way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 4:22; Galatians 4:8–9; John 17:3"> For as I passed along and observed the objects of your worship, I found an altar with this inscription, “To an Unknown God.” What therefore you worship in ignorance, this I announce to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 8:27; Deuteronomy 10:14; Acts 7:48"> The God who made the world and everything in it, since he is Lord of heaven and earth, does not live in temples built with hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 50:8–13; Genesis 2:7; Isaiah 42:5"> Neither is he served by men's hands, as though he needed anything, since he himself gives people life and breath and everything else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7–8; Genesis 3:20; Romans 5:12–19"> From one man he made every nation of people to live on the surface of the earth, having determined their appointed seasons and the boundaries of their living areas,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:23–24; Deuteronomy 4:7; Acts 14:17"> so that they should search for God and perhaps they may feel their way toward him and find him. Yet he is not far from each one of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 12:10; Colossians 1:17; Hebrews 1:3"> For in him we live and move and have our being, just as one of your own poets has said,<br />'For we also are his offspring.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 40:25; Exodus 20:4; Psalms 115:4–8"> Therefore, since we are God's offspring, we ought not to think that the qualities of deity are like gold, or silver, or stone—images created by the art and imagination of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 14:16; Titus 2:11–12; Luke 24:47"> Therefore God overlooked the times of ignorance, but now he commands all men everywhere to repent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 25:31–46; 2 Peter 3:7; Romans 2:16"> This is because he has set a day when he will judge the world in righteousness by the man he has chosen. God has given proof of this man to everyone by raising him from the dead.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 17:18; Acts 26:8; Acts 25:19"> Now when the men of Athens heard of the resurrection of the dead, some mocked Paul; but others said, “We will listen to you again about this matter.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> After that, Paul left them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 17:19; Romans 11:5–6; Acts 13:48"> But certain men joined him and believed, including Dionysius the Areopagite, a woman named Damaris, and others with them.</span>
</div>
</div>
