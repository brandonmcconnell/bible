<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 24:9; John 15:20; Matthew 10:17–18"> Now about that time Herod the king laid hands on some who belonged to the church so that he might mistreat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:37; Matthew 20:23; Matthew 4:21–22"> He killed James the brother of John with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 23:15; John 12:43; Acts 24:27"> After he saw that this pleased the Jews, he proceeded to arrest Peter also. That was during the days of unleavened bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Luke 22:33; Luke 21:12"> After arresting him, he put him in prison, assigning him over to four squads of soldiers to guard him; he was intending to bring him to the people after the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; James 5:16"> So Peter was kept in the prison, but prayer was made earnestly to God for him by those in the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 21:33; Hebrews 13:6; Philippians 4:6–7"> On the night before Herod was going to bring him out for trial, Peter was sleeping between two soldiers, bound with two chains, while guards in front of the door were keeping watch over the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Acts 16:26; Psalms 146:7"> Behold, an angel of the Lord suddenly appeared by him, and a light shone in the prison cell. He struck Peter on the side and woke him and said, “Get up quickly,” and his chains fell off his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The angel said to him, “Dress yourself and put on your sandals.” Peter did so. The angel said to him, “Put on your outer garment and follow me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 126:1; Hebrews 11:8; Genesis 45:26"> So Peter followed the angel and went out. He did not know that what was done by the angel was real. He thought he was seeing a vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Acts 16:26; Isaiah 45:1–2"> After they had passed by the first guard and the second, they came to the iron gate that led into the city; it opened for them by itself. They went out and went down a street, and the angel left him right away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 6:22; Daniel 3:28; 2 Peter 2:9"> When Peter came to himself, he said, “Now I truly know that the Lord has sent his angel and delivered me out of the hand of Herod, and from everything the Jewish people were expecting.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 5:14–15; Acts 12:5; Matthew 18:19–20"> When he realized this, he went to the house of Mary the mother of John, also called Mark, where many people had gathered and were praying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:16; Luke 13:25; John 18:16–17"> When he knocked at the door of the gate, a servant girl named Rhoda came to answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 24:41; Matthew 28:8"> When she recognized Peter's voice, out of joy she failed to open the door; instead, she came running into the room; she reported that Peter was standing at the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:10; Luke 24:11; Mark 16:14"> So they said to her, “You are insane.” But she insisted that it was so. They said, “It is his angel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> But Peter continued knocking, and when they had opened the door, they saw him and were amazed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 21:40; Acts 13:16; Acts 19:33"> Peter motioned to them with his hand to be silent, and he told them how the Lord had brought him out of prison. He said, “Report these things to James and the brothers.” Then he left and went to another place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:22–25; Acts 16:27; Acts 19:23"> Now when it became day, there was no small disturbance among the soldiers over what had happened to Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 16:27; Psalms 37:32–33; John 12:10–11"> After Herod had searched for him and could not find him, he questioned the guards and ordered them to be put to death. Then he went down from Judea to Caesarea and stayed there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezra 3:7; Matthew 11:21–22; Ezekiel 27:17"> Now Herod was very angry with the people of Tyre and Sidon. They went to him together. They persuaded Blastus, the king's assistant, to help them. Then they asked for peace, because their country received its food from the king's country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> On a set day Herod dressed himself in royal clothing and sat on a throne; he made a speech to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 14:10–13; Revelation 13:4; Jude 1:16"> The people shouted, “This is the voice of a god, not of a man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 115:1; Acts 10:25–26; 2 Thessalonians 2:4"> Immediately an angel of the Lord struck him, because he did not give God the glory; he was eaten by worms and died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 6:7; Acts 19:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:1"> But the word of God increased and multiplied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 12:12; 1 Peter 5:13; Acts 15:37"> So when Barnabas and Saul had completed their mission, they returned from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some ancient copies read, they returned to Jerusalem .">a</a></sup>Jerusalem, bringing with them John, also called Mark.</span>
</div>
