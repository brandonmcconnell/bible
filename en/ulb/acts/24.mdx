<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 24:11; Acts 23:2; Acts 25:2"> After five days, Ananias the chief priest, certain elders, and an orator named Tertullus went there. These men brought charges against Paul before the governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:5; Proverbs 26:28; Acts 24:26–27"> When Paul stood before the governor, Tertullus began to accuse him and said to the governor, “Because of you we have great peace, and your foresight brings good reform to our nation;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 23:26; Acts 26:25; Luke 1:3"> so with all thankfulness we welcome everything that you do, most excellent Felix.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:32"> So that I detain you no more, I ask you to briefly listen to me with kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 28:22; Acts 24:14; Acts 26:5"> For we have found this man to be a pest and one who causes all the Jews throughout the world to rebel. He is a leader of the Nazarene sect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 24:12; Acts 21:27–32; John 18:31"> He even tried to desecrate the temple, so we arrested him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some ancient copies add, “We wanted to judge him according to our our law .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some ancient copies have vs 7, But Lysias, the officer, came and took him by force out of our hands .">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 23:35; Acts 25:15–16; Acts 25:5"> When you question Paul about all these matters, you will be able to learn about these charges we are bringing against him.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some ancient copies add to the first part of vs 8, sending us to you .">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:16; Micah 7:2–3; Psalms 4:2"> The Jews also joined in the accusation, affirming that these charges were true.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15; 1 Samuel 2:25; Luke 18:2"> But when the governor motioned for Paul to speak, Paul answered, “I understand that for many years you have been a judge to this nation, and so I gladly explain myself to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 24:1; Acts 21:18; Acts 23:11"> You will be able to find out that it has not been more than twelve days since I went up to worship in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 25:8; Acts 28:17; Acts 24:18"> When they found me in the temple, I did not argue with anyone, and I did not stir up a crowd, either in the synagogues, or in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 25:7; 1 Peter 3:16"> They cannot prove to you the accusations they are now making against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 9:2; Romans 3:21; Acts 3:13"> But I admit this to you, that according to the Way that they call a sect, in that same way I serve the God of our fathers. I am faithful to all that is in the law and the writings of the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 5:28–29; 1 Thessalonians 4:14–16"> I have the same confident hope in God as these men, that there will be a resurrection of both the righteous and the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 23:1; 1 Timothy 1:5; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> So I always strive to have a clear conscience before God and human beings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 2:10; Romans 15:25–28; 1 Corinthians 16:1–4"> Now after many years I came to bring help to my nation and gifts of money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:21; Acts 21:26–30; Acts 24:12"> When I did this, certain Jews from Asia found me in a purification ceremony in the temple, not with a crowd or an uproar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 23:30; Acts 25:16"> These men ought to be before you now and say what they have against me, if they have anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Or else, these same men should say what wrong they found in me when I stood before the Jewish council,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 23:6; Acts 4:2; Acts 26:6–8"> unless it is about this one thing that I shouted out when I stood among them, 'It is concerning the resurrection of those who have died that I am on trial before you today.'”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 24:6; Acts 24:24; Acts 25:26"> Then Felix, who was well informed about the Way, ajourned the hearing. He said, “When Lysias the commander comes down from Jerusalem, I will decide your case.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 28:16; Acts 27:3; Proverbs 16:7"> Then he commanded the centurion that Paul should be kept under guard, but to have some freedom so that none of his friends would be prevented from attending to his needs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Galatians 2:16; Mark 6:20"> After some days, Felix returned with Drusilla his wife, a Jewess, and he sent for Paul and he heard from him about faith in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:8; 1 Peter 3:15; Acts 10:42"> But when Paul reasoned with him about righteousness, self-control, and the coming judgment, Felix became frightened and said, “Go away for now. But when I have the opportunity later on, I will send for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Proverbs 17:23; Isaiah 56:11"> At the same time he wanted Paul to give money to him, so he often sent for him and spoke with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 25:9; Acts 25:14; Acts 12:3"> But when two years passed, Porcius Festus became the governor after Felix, but Felix wanted to gain favor with the Jews, so he left Paul to continue under guard.</span>
</div>
</div>
