<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:31; 1 Kings 19:11; Psalms 18:10"> Suddenly there came from heaven a sound like the rush of a violent wind, and it filled the whole house where they were sitting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; John 1:32–33; Jeremiah 23:29"> There appeared to them tongues like fire that were distributed, and they sat upon each one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Acts 13:52; Acts 4:31"> They were all filled with the Holy Spirit and began to speak with other languages, as the Spirit gave them to speak.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Acts 8:2; Colossians 1:23"> Now there were Jews who were living in Jerusalem, godly men, from every nation under heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 2:3; 1 Corinthians 16:9; Acts 2:2"> When this sound was heard, the multitude came together and was confused because everyone heard them speaking in his own language.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 2:12; Acts 1:11; John 7:52"> They were amazed and marveled; they said, “Really, are not all these who are speaking Galileans?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Why is it that we are hearing them, each in our own language in which we were born?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 1:1; Acts 18:2; Revelation 1:4"> Parthians and Medes and Elamites, and those who live in Mesopotamia, in Judea and Cappadocia, in Pontus and Asia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 16:6; Zechariah 8:23; Zechariah 8:20"> Phrygia and Pamphylia, in Egypt and the parts of Libya toward Cyrene, and visitors from Rome,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 78:4"> Jews and proselytes, Cretans and Arabians, we hear them telling in our languages about the mighty works of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 15:26; Acts 17:20; Luke 18:36"> They were all amazed and perplexed; they said to one another, “What does this mean?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:23; Acts 2:15; 1 Samuel 1:14"> But others mocked and said, “They are full of new wine.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 51:7; Isaiah 51:1; Deuteronomy 27:9"> But Peter stood with the eleven, raised his voice, and said to them, “Men of Judea and all of you who live at Jerusalem, let this be known to you; pay attention to my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Samuel 1:15; Matthew 20:3"> For these people are not drunk as you assume, for it is only the third hour of the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32"> But this is what was spoken through the prophet Joel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32; Isaiah 44:3; Titus 3:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">'It will be in the last days,' God says,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">'I will pour out my Spirit on all people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your sons and your daughters will prophesy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">your young men will see visions,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and your old men will dream dreams.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11; 1 Corinthians 7:21–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Also on my servants and my female servants in those days</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will pour out my Spirit, and they will prophesy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:30–31; Zephaniah 1:14–18; Malachi 4:1–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will show wonders in the sky above</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and signs on the earth below,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">blood, fire, and vapor of smoke.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Amos 8:9; Matthew 24:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sun will be turned to darkness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the moon to blood,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">before the great and remarkable day of the Lord comes.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 10:12–13; Joel 2:32; Psalms 86:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will be that everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.'</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 3:2; John 14:10–11; Matthew 12:28"> Men of Israel, hear these words: Jesus of Nazareth, a man accredited to you by God by the mighty deeds, the wonders, and the signs which God did through him in your midst, as you yourselves know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:22; Acts 4:28; Acts 3:18"> This man was handed over by God's predetermined plan and foreknowledge; and you, by the hand of lawless men, put him to death by nailing him to a cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:14; 1 Peter 1:21; Acts 2:32"> But God raised him up, freeing him from the pains of death, because it was impossible for him to be held by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 16:8–11; Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 62:6"> For David says about him,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'I saw the Lord always before my face,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for he is beside my right hand so that I should not be moved.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 16:9; Psalms 63:5; Psalms 71:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore my heart was glad and my tongue rejoiced.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Also, my flesh will live in certain hope.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Acts 2:31; Revelation 1:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">For you will not abandon my soul to Hades,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">neither will you allow your Holy One to see decay.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 25:4; Psalms 21:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You revealed to me the ways of life;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you will make me full of gladness with your face.'</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 13:36; 1 Kings 2:10; Acts 7:8–9"> Brothers, I can speak to you confidently about the patriarch David, he both died and was buried, and his tomb is with us to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 132:11–18; Psalms 89:3–4; Jeremiah 33:14–15"> Therefore, he was a prophet and knew that God had sworn with an oath to him, that he would set one of the fruit of his body upon his throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 2:27; Psalms 16:10; Acts 13:35"> He saw what was to happen in the future and spoke about the resurrection of the Christ,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'He was neither abandoned to Hades, nor did his flesh see decay.'</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 3:15; Acts 1:8; Acts 1:22"> This Jesus—God raised him up, of which we all are witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:4; Acts 5:31; Acts 2:17"> Therefore having been exalted to the right hand of God and having received the promised Holy Spirit from the Father, he has poured out what you see and hear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Matthew 22:42–45; Hebrews 1:13"> For David did not ascend to the heaven, but he says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'The Lord said to my Lord, “Sit at my right hand,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 19:19–3; Romans 16:20; Isaiah 60:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">until I make your enemies the stool for your feet.”'</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 5:30–31; Romans 14:8–12; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> Therefore, let all the house of Israel certainly know that God has made him both Lord and Christ, this Jesus whom you crucified.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 3:10; Acts 22:10; Hebrews 4:12–13"> Now when they heard this, they were pierced in their hearts, and said to Peter and the rest of the apostles, “Brothers, what shall we do?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 22:16; Mark 16:16; Acts 8:12"> Then Peter said to them, “Repent and be baptized, each of you, in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 8:30"> For the promise is to you and to your children and to all that are far off, as many people as the Lord our God will call.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Deuteronomy 32:5; Matthew 17:17"> With many other words he testified and urged them; he said, “Save yourselves from this wicked generation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 4:4; Acts 2:47; Acts 16:31–34"> Then they received his word and were baptized, and there were added in that day about three thousand souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; Acts 1:14; 1 John 1:3"> They continued in the apostles' teaching and fellowship, in the breaking of bread and in prayers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 7:16; John 14:12; Luke 8:37"> Fear came upon every soul, and many wonders and signs were done through the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 4:32; 1 John 3:16–18; 2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> All who believed were together and had all things in common,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 John 3:17; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; James 2:14–16"> and they sold their property and possessions and distributed them to all, according to the needs anyone had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 5:42; Acts 20:7; Acts 2:42"> So day after day they continued with one purpose in the temple, and they broke bread in homes, and they shared food with glad and humble hearts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 16:5; Acts 5:13–14; Acts 2:41"> praising God and having favor with all the people, and every day the Lord added to their number those who were being saved.</span>
</div>
