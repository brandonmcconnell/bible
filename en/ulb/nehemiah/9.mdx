<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 13:2–3; Ezra 8:23; Nehemiah 8:2"> Now on the twenty-fourth day of the same month the people of Israel were assembled and they were fasting, and they were wearing sackcloth, and they put dust on their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 9:3–10; Nehemiah 13:3; Ezra 10:11"> The descendants of Israel separated themselves from all the foreigners. They stood and confessed their own sins and the evil actions of their ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:7–8; Nehemiah 8:3–4"> They stood up in their places, and for one-fourth of the day they read from the book of the law of Yahweh their God. For another fourth of the day they were confessing and bowing down before Yahweh their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:7; Nehemiah 12:8; Nehemiah 9:5"> The Levites, Jeshua, Bani, Kadmiel, Shebaniah, Bunni, Sherebiah, Bani, and Kenani, stood on the stairs and they called out with a loud voice to Yahweh their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 103:1–2; 1 Peter 1:3; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> Then the Levites, Jeshua, and Kadmiel, Bani, Hashabneiah, Sherebiah, Hodiah, Shebaniah, and Pethahiah said, “Stand up and give praise to Yahweh your God forever and ever.” “May they bless your glorious name, and may it be exalted above every blessing and praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:16; Genesis 1:1; Revelation 4:11"> You are Yahweh. You alone. You have made heaven, the highest heavens, with all their host, and the earth and everything on it, and the seas and all that is in them. You give life to them all, and the host of heaven worship you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 11:31; Genesis 17:5; Isaiah 51:2"> You are Yahweh, the God who chose Abram, and brought him out of Ur of the Chaldees, and gave him the name Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Genesis 15:18–21; Hebrews 6:18"> You found his heart was faithful before you, and you made with him the covenant to give to his descendants the land of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Jebusites, and the Girgashites. You have kept your promise because you are righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 3:7–9; Acts 7:34; Exodus 3:16"> You saw the affliction of our forefathers in Egypt and you heard their cry by the Sea of Reeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:20; Daniel 9:15; Exodus 18:11"> You gave signs and wonders against Pharaoh, and all his servants, and on all the people of his land, for you knew that the Egyptians acted with arrogance against them. But you made a name for yourself which stands to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 78:13; Exodus 14:21–22; Exodus 14:27–28"> Then you divided the sea before them, so that they went through the middle of the sea on the dry land; and threw those who pursued them into the depths, as a stone into deep waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 13:21–22; Nehemiah 9:19; Psalms 107:7"> You led them by a pillar of cloud during by day, and by a pillar of fire during the night to light the way for them to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 119:160; Exodus 20:1; Psalms 119:127–128"> On Mount Sinai you came down and you spoke with them from heaven and gave to them righteous decrees and true laws, good statutes and commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 20:8–11; Genesis 2:3; Ezekiel 20:12"> You made your holy Sabbath known to them, and you gave them commandments and statutes and a law through Moses your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3–4; Deuteronomy 1:8; Exodus 17:6"> You gave them bread from heaven for their hunger, and water from a rock for their thirst, and you said to them to go in to possess the land you swore on oath to give them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:29; Psalms 81:11–14; Hebrews 3:15"> But they and our ancestors acted disrespectfully, and they were stubborn, and did not listen to your commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 86:5; Joel 2:13; Psalms 145:8–9"> They refused to listen, and they did not think about the wonders that you had done among them, but became stubborn, and in their rebellion they appointed a leader to return to their slavery. But you are a God who is full of forgiveness, gracious and compassionate, slow to anger, and abounding in steadfast love. You did not abandon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 106:19–23; Exodus 32:4–8; Ezekiel 20:7–44"> Even when they had cast a calf out of molten metal and said, 'This is your God who brought you up out of Egypt,' and had committed great blasphemies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Nehemiah 9:27; Malachi 3:6"> you, in your compassion, did not abandon them in the wilderness. The pillar of cloud to lead them on the way did not leave them during day, neither did the pillar of fire by night to light the way for them to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 143:10; Numbers 11:17; Isaiah 63:11–14"> Your good Spirit you gave them to instruct them, and your manna you did not withhold from their mouths, and water you gave them for their thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:7; Deuteronomy 8:4; Psalms 34:10"> For forty years you provided for them in the wilderness, and they lacked nothing. Their clothes did not wear out and their feet did not swell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Deuteronomy 3:1–17; Deuteronomy 2:26–36"> You gave them kingdoms and peoples, assigning to them every corner of the land. Then they took possession of the land of Sihon king of Heshbon and the land of Og king of Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 15:5; 1 Chronicles 27:23"> You made their children as numerous as the stars of heaven, and you brought them into the land that you told their ancestors to go in and possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 21:43; Joshua 11:23; Psalms 44:2–3"> So the people went in and possessed the land and you subdued before them the inhabitants of the land, the Canaanites. You gave them into their hands, with their kings and the peoples of the land, that Israel might do with them as they pleased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Nehemiah 9:35; Deuteronomy 3:5"> They captured the fortified cities and a productive land, and they captured houses full of all good things, cisterns already cut out, vineyards and olive orchards, and fruit trees in abundance. So they ate and were satisfied and grew fat and enjoyed themselves in your great goodness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 14:9; Judges 2:11–12; Nehemiah 9:18"> Then they became disobedient and they rebelled against you. They threw your law behind their backs. They murdered your prophets who had warned them to turn back to you, and they committed great blasphemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 106:41–45; 2 Kings 14:27; 2 Kings 13:5"> So you gave them into the hand of their enemies, who made them suffer. In the time of their suffering, they cried out to you, and you heard them from heaven, and because of your great mercies you sent them rescuers who rescued them out of the hand of their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 5:31–1; Judges 4:1; 1 Kings 8:39"> But after they had rest, they did evil again before you, and you abandoned them to the hand of their enemies, so their enemies ruled over them. Yet when they returned and cried out to you, you heard from heaven, and many times because of your compassion you rescued them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:16; Leviticus 18:5; Nehemiah 9:26"> You warned them so they might turn back to your law. Yet they acted arrogantly and did not listen to your commands. They sinned against your decrees which give life to anyone who obeys them. They gave the stubborn shoulder-blade and stiffened their neck and refused to listen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 2:4; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Isaiah 42:24"> For many years you put up with them and warned them by your Spirit through your prophets. Yet they did not listen. So you gave them into the hand of the neighboring peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 3:22; Jeremiah 4:27; Nehemiah 9:17"> But in your great mercies you did not make a complete end of them, or forsake them, for you are a gracious and merciful God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Nehemiah 1:5; Daniel 9:4"> Now therefore, our God—you great, mighty, and awesome God who keep your covenant and steadfast love—do not let all this hardship seem little to you that has come on us, on our kings, on our princes, and on our priests, and on our prophets, and on our ancestors, and on all your people from the days of the kings of Assyria until today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 18:25; Psalms 106:6; Job 34:23"> You are just in everything that has come on us, for you have dealt faithfully, but we have acted wickedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:19; 2 Kings 17:13; Deuteronomy 31:21"> Our kings, our princes, our priests, and our ancestors have not kept your law, nor paid attention to your commandments or your covenant decrees by which you warned them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:47; Deuteronomy 32:12–15; Jeremiah 5:19"> Even in their own kingdom, while they enjoyed your great goodness to them, in the large and productive land you set before them, they did not serve you or turn away from their evil ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezra 9:9; Deuteronomy 28:48; 2 Chronicles 12:8"> Now we are slaves in the land you gave our ancestors to enjoy its fruit and its good gifts, and behold, we are slaves in it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Deuteronomy 28:48; Ezra 7:24"> The rich yield from our land goes to the kings you have set over us because of our sins. They rule over our bodies and over our livestock as they please. We are in great distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:1; 2 Chronicles 34:31; Ezra 10:3"> Because of all this, we make a firm covenant in writing. On the sealed document are the names of our princes, Levites, and priests.”</span>
</div>
