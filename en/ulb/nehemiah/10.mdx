<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Psalms 119:106; Titus 2:11–14"> they joined together with their brothers, their nobles, and bound themselves with both a curse and an oath to walk in God's law, which was given by Moses the servant of God, and to observe and obey all the commandments of Yahweh our Lord and his decrees and his statutes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3; Exodus 34:16; Ezra 9:1–3"> We promised that we would not give our daughters to the people of the land or take their daughters for our sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15–22; Leviticus 23:3; Exodus 23:10–11"> We also promised that if the people of the land bring goods or any grain to sell on the Sabbath day, we would not buy from them on the Sabbath or on any holy day. Every seventh year we will let our fields rest, and we will cancel all debts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 17:24–27; Exodus 30:11–16; Proverbs 3:9"> We accepted the commands to give a third of a shekel each year for the service of the house of our God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4; Leviticus 24:5–6; Numbers 28:1–29"> to provide for the bread of the presence, and for the regular grain offering, the burnt offerings on the Sabbaths, the new moon festivals and appointed feasts, and for the holy offerings, and for the sin offerings to make atonement for Israel, as well as for all the work of the house of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:31; Isaiah 40:16; Leviticus 6:12–13"> The priests, the Levites, and the people cast lots for the wood offering. The lots would select which of our families would bring wood into the house of our God at the appointed times each year, to be burned on the altar of Yahweh our God, as it is written in the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:2; Exodus 23:19; Exodus 34:26"> We promised to bring to the house of Yahweh the firstfruits grown from our soil, and the firstfruits of every tree each year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Leviticus 27:26–27; Exodus 13:12–15"> As it is written in the law, we promised to bring to the house of God and to the priests who serve there, the firstborn of our sons and of our herds and flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 23:17; Numbers 18:21; Nehemiah 13:9"> We will bring the first of our dough and our grain offerings, and the fruit of every tree, and the new wine and the oil we will bring to the priests, to the storerooms of the house of our God. We will bring to the Levites the tithes from our soil because the Levites collect the tithes in all the towns where we work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:12–13; Numbers 18:26–28; 2 Chronicles 31:11–12"> A priest, a descendant of Aaron, must be with the Levites when they receive the tithes. The Levites must bring a tenth of the tithes to the house of our God to the storerooms of the treasury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:12; Hebrews 10:25; Deuteronomy 14:23–27"> For the people of Israel and the descendants of Levi are to bring the contributions of grain, new wine, and oil to the storerooms where the articles of the sanctuary are kept and where the priests who are serving, and the gatekeepers, and the singers stay.<br />We will not neglect the house of our God.</span>
