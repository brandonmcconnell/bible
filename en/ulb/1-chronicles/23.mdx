<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:28; 1 Chronicles 28:5; 1 Kings 1:33–39"> When David was old and near the end of his life, he made Solomon his son king over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 24:1; 2 Chronicles 34:29–30; 1 Chronicles 13:1"> He gathered together all the leaders of Israel, with the priests and Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Numbers 4:2–49"> The Levites who were thirty years old and older were counted. They numbered thirty-eight thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:8; Deuteronomy 16:18; 1 Chronicles 26:29–31"> “Of these, twenty-four thousand were to oversee the work of Yahweh's house, and six thousand were officers and judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:25–26; 2 Chronicles 8:14; Psalms 87:7"> Four thousand were gatekeepers, and four thousand were to praise Yahweh with the instruments that I made to give praise,” David said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 6:1; 1 Chronicles 6:16"> He divided them into groups that corresponded to Levi's sons: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:21; Exodus 6:17; 1 Chronicles 6:17–20"> From the clans descended from Gershon, there were Ladan and Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:33–34; 1 Chronicles 15:20–21; 1 Chronicles 15:7"> There were three of Ladan's sons: Jehiel the leader, Zetham, and Joel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> There were three of Shimei's sons: Shelomoth, Haziel, and Haran. These were the leaders of the clans of Ladan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> There were four of Shimei's sons: Jahath, Ziza, Jeush, and Beriah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Jahath was the oldest, and Ziza the second, but Jeush and Beriah did not have many sons, so they were considered as one clan with the same duties.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; Numbers 3:27; Numbers 26:58"> There were four of Kohath's sons: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Exodus 6:20; 1 Samuel 2:28"> These were Amram's sons: Aaron and Moses. Aaron was chosen to set apart the most holy things, that he and his descendants would offer incense before Yahweh, to serve him and to give blessings in his name forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; Psalms 90:1; 1 Chronicles 26:23–25"> But as for Moses the man of God, his sons were considered to be Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 18:3–4; Exodus 2:22; Exodus 4:20"> Moses' sons were Gershom and Eliezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:24; 1 Chronicles 25:20; 1 Chronicles 24:20"> Gershom's descendant was Shubael the oldest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:25"> Eliezer's descendant was Rehabiah. Eliezer had no other sons, but Rehabiah had many descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:26; 1 Chronicles 24:22"> Izhar's son was Shelomith the leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:23; 1 Chronicles 15:9; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> Hebron's descendants were Jeriah, the oldest, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, and Jekameam the fourth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Uzziel's sons were Micah the oldest, and Ishijah the second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 6:19; 1 Chronicles 6:20; 1 Chronicles 24:26–30"> Merari's sons were Mahli and Mushi. Mahli's sons were Eleazar and Kish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:28; Numbers 36:6–8"> Eleazar died without having any sons. He had only daughters. The sons of Kish married them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:30"> Mushi's three sons were Mahli, Eder, and Jerimoth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 10:17; Numbers 10:21; 1 Chronicles 23:3"> These were Levi's descendants corresponding to their clans. They were the leaders, counted and listed by name, of the clans that did the work in the service of Yahweh's house, from twenty years old and upward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 132:13–14; 1 Chronicles 22:18; 2 Samuel 7:11"> For David said, “Yahweh, the God of Israel, has given rest to his people. He makes his home in Jerusalem forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 4:5; Numbers 7:9; Numbers 4:15"> The Levites will no longer need to carry the tabernacle and all the equipment used in its service.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Psalms 72:20; 2 Samuel 23:1"> For by David's last words the Levites were counted, from twenty years old and upward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:3–6; 2 Chronicles 31:11; 2 Chronicles 35:11–14"> Their duty was to assist Aaron's descendants in the service of Yahweh's house. They were to care for the courtyards, the rooms, the ceremonial purification of all the things that belong to Yahweh, and other work in the service of God's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 19:35–36; Exodus 25:30; Leviticus 6:20–23"> They also took care of the bread of the presence, the fine flour for grain offerings, the unleavened wafers, the baked offerings, the offerings mixed with oil, and all the measuring of the amounts and sizes of things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 92:1–3; Ezra 3:10–11; 1 Chronicles 9:33"> They also stood every morning to thank and praise Yahweh. They also did this in the evening</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13–14; Numbers 28:1–29; Numbers 10:10"> and whenever burnt offerings were offered to Yahweh, on the Sabbath and at the new moon festivals and feast days. A fixed number, assigned by decree, always had to be present before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:38; Numbers 3:6–9"> They were in charge of the tent of meeting, the holy place, and helped their fellow descendants of Aaron in the service of Yahweh's house.</span>
</div>
</div>
