<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:1–16; 1 Chronicles 12:23–40; Judges 9:2"> Then all Israel came to David at Hebron and said, “Look, we are your flesh and bone.</span>

Yahweh your God said to you, 'You will shepherd my people Israel, and you will become a ruler over my people Israel.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12–13; 1 Samuel 16:1; 2 Samuel 5:3"> So all the elders of Israel came to the king at Hebron, and David made a covenant with them before Yahweh. They anointed David king over Israel. In this way, the word of Yahweh that had been declared by Samuel came true.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 1:21; Genesis 15:21; Joshua 15:8"> David and all Israel went to Jerusalem (that is, Jebus). Now the Jebusites, the inhabitants of the land, were there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:2; 1 Kings 8:1; Psalms 87:2"> The inhabitants of Jebus said to David, “You will not come in here.” But David took the stronghold of Zion, that is, the city of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16; 2 Samuel 20:23; 2 Samuel 3:27"> David had said, “Whoever attacks the Jebusites first will become chief and commander.” So Joab son of Zeruiah attacked first, so he was made the chief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:5; 2 Samuel 5:7; Psalms 2:6"> Then David began to live in the stronghold. So they called it the city of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 9:6; 1 Kings 11:27; 2 Kings 12:20"> He built the city all around from the Millo and back to the surrounding wall. Joab restored the rest of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1; Isaiah 41:10; 1 Chronicles 9:20"> David became greater and greater because Yahweh of hosts was with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:3; 1 Samuel 16:1; 1 Chronicles 12:38"> These were the leaders David had, who showed themselves strong with him in his kingdom, together with all Israel, to make him king, obeying the word of Yahweh concerning Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:8; 1 Chronicles 27:2; 1 Chronicles 12:18"> This is a list of David's mighty men: Jashobeam, the son of a Hachmonite, was commander of the officers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Hebrew could also read of the thirty or of the three .">a</a></sup>. He killed three hundred men with his spear on one occasion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:4; 2 Samuel 23:17–19; 2 Samuel 23:23"> After him was Eleazar son of Dodo, the Ahohite, who was one of the three mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:11–12; 1 Samuel 17:1"> He was with David at Pas Dammim, and there the Philistines assembled together for battle, where there was a barley field and the army fled from the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 144:10; 2 Kings 5:1; 1 Samuel 14:23"> They stood in the middle of the field. They defended it and cut down the Philistines and Yahweh rescued them with a great victory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:9; 2 Samuel 5:18; Isaiah 17:5"> Then three of the thirty leaders went down to the rock to David, to the cave of Adullam. The army of the Philistines was camped in the Valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Samuel 13:23"> At that time David was in his stronghold, a cave, while the Philistines had established their camp at Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 12:3; 2 Samuel 23:15–16"> David was longing for water and said, “If only someone would give me water to drink from the well at Bethlehem, the well that is by the gate!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:13; 1 Samuel 19:5; Acts 20:24"> So these three mighty men broke through the army of the Philistines and drew water out of the well of Bethlehem, the well at the gate. They took the water and brought it to David, but he refused to drink it. Instead, he poured it out to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:17; Psalms 72:14; Judges 9:17"> Then he said, “May it be that I should never do this! Should I drink the blood of these men who have risked their lives?” Because they had put their lives at risk, David refused to drink it. These were the deeds of the three mighty men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:6; 1 Chronicles 2:16; 2 Samuel 20:6"> Abishai brother of Joab was captain over the Three. He once used his spear against three hundred and killed them. He is mentioned along with the Three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:41; Matthew 13:8"> Of the Three, he was given double honor and became their captain, even though he was not one of them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:20–23; 2 Samuel 8:18; Joshua 15:21"> Benaiah son of Jehoiada was a brave warrior from Kabzeel, who did great deeds. He killed the two sons of Ariel of Moab. He also went down into a pit and killed a lion on a day when the snow was falling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:7; 1 Samuel 17:4; Deuteronomy 3:11"> He even killed an Egyptian, a man five cubits tall. The Egyptian had a spear like a weaver's beam, but he went down to him with only a staff. He seized the spear out of the Egyptian's hand and killed him with his own spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Benaiah son of Jehoiada did these feats, and he was named alongside the three mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:23"> He was more highly regarded than the thirty soldiers in general, but he was not regarded quite as highly as the three mighty men. Yet David put him in charge of his bodyguard.</span>
</div>
