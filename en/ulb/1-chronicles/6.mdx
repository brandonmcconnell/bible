<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Leviticus 10:16; 1 Chronicles 24:1; Numbers 20:26–28"> Aaron's descendants are reckoned as follows: Aaron's son was Eleazar. Eleazar's son was Phinehas. Phinehas's son was Abishua.</span>
