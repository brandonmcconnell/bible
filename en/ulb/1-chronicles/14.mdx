<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:7; Psalms 89:20–37; 2 Samuel 7:16"> David knew that Yahweh had established him as king over Israel, and that his kingdom was exalted on high for the sake of his people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 5:18–19; 2 Samuel 5:13; Ecclesiastes 7:26–29"> In Jerusalem, David took more wives, and he became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:5–9; 2 Samuel 5:14; Luke 3:31"> These were the names of the children who were born to him in Jerusalem: Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, Solomon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:15; 1 Chronicles 3:6"> Ibhar, Elishua, Elpelet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:8; 2 Samuel 5:16"> Elishama, Beeliada, and Eliphelet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:11; Revelation 11:15–18; Psalms 2:1–6"> Now when the Philistines heard that David had been anointed as king over all Israel, they all went out looking for him. But David heard about it and went out against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:15; 1 Chronicles 14:13; 2 Samuel 23:13"> Now the Philistines had come and made a raid in the Valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 3:6; 1 Chronicles 14:14; 1 Chronicles 13:3"> Then David asked for help from God. He said, “Should I attack the Philistines? Will you give victory over them?” Yahweh said to him, “Attack, for I will certainly give them to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:21; Exodus 14:28; 2 Samuel 5:20"> So they came up to Baal Perazim, and there he defeated them. He commented, “God has burst through my enemies by my hand like a bursting flood of water.” So the name of that place became Baal Perazim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 32:20; 1 Samuel 5:2–6; 2 Kings 19:18"> The Philistines abandoned their gods there, and David gave an order that they should be burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:9; 1 Kings 20:22; 2 Samuel 5:22–25"> Then the Philistines raided the valley yet again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 9:6–7; 1 Chronicles 14:10; Psalms 27:4"> So David asked for help from God again. God said to him, “You must not attack their front, but rather circle around behind them and come on them through the balsam woods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 13:4; Acts 2:2; Philippians 2:12–13"> When you hear the sound of marching in the wind blowing through the balsam treetops, then attack with force. Do this because God will have gone out before you to attack the army of the Philistines.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 15:14; John 13:17; Genesis 6:22"> So David did as God had commanded him. He defeated the army of the Philistines from Gibeon all the way to Gezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Joshua 6:27; Exodus 15:14–16"> Then David's fame went out into all lands, and Yahweh caused all nations to fear him.</span>
