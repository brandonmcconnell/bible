<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:19; Numbers 26:9–11; Psalms 44:1"> Here were the divisions of the gatekeepers: From the Korahites, Meshelemiah son of Kore, a descendant of Asaph<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Asaph is the same person referred to as Ebiasaph in 1 Chron. 9:19.">a</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:21"> Meshelemiah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Meshelemiah is the same person referred to as Shelemiah in 1 Chron. 26:14.">b</a></sup>had sons: Zechariah the firstborn, Jediael the second, Zebadiah the third, Jathniel the fourth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Elam the fifth, Jehohanan the sixth, Eliehoenai the seventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 15:24; 1 Chronicles 15:18"> Obed Edom had sons: Shemaiah the firstborn, Jehozabad the second, Joah the third, and Sakar the fourth, and Nethanel the fifth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 128:1"> Ammiel the sixth, Issachar the seventh, Peullethai the eighth, for God had blessed Obed Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:28; 1 Timothy 6:12; Judges 6:12"> To Shemaiah his son were born sons who ruled over their families; they were men with many abilities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The sons of Shemaiah were Othni, Rephael, Obed, and Elzabad. His relatives Elihu and Semakiah were also men with many abilities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:6; 1 Peter 4:11; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11"> All these were descendants of Obed Edom. They and their sons and relatives were men capable of doing their duties in the tabernacle service. There were sixty-two of them related to Obed Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:14; 1 Chronicles 26:1"> Meshelemiah had sons and relatives, capable men, eighteen in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2"> Hosah, a descendant of Merari, had sons: Shimri the leader (although he was not the firstborn, his father made him leader),</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Hilkiah the second, Tabaliah the third, Zechariah the fourth. All of Hosah's sons and kinsmen were thirteen in number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8"> These divisions of the gatekeepers, corresponding to their leaders, had responsibilities, like their relatives, to serve in Yahweh's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8; 1 Chronicles 24:31; 1 Chronicles 24:5"> They threw lots, both young and old, corresponding to their families, for every gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When the lot was cast for the east gate, it fell to Shelemiah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Shelemiah is the same person referred to as Meshelemiah in 1CH 26:2.">c</a></sup>. They then cast lots for Zechariah his son, a prudent advisor, and his lot came out for the north gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:24; 1 Chronicles 26:17"> To Obed Edom was assigned the south gate, and his sons were assigned the storehouses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:24; 1 Chronicles 26:10–12; 1 Kings 10:5"> Shuppim and Hosah were assigned the west gate along with the gate of Shalleketh, on the upper road. Watches were established for each family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 9:24"> On the east were six Levites, on the north four a day, on the south four a day, and at the storehouses two pairs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 23:11"> At the pillar to the west there were four stationed at the road and two at the pillar<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The word translated “pillar” is translated in some versions as “parbar.” This word appears only here and in 2 Kings 23:11 in the Old Testament. The meaning of this word is not clear.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 16:11"> These were the gatekeepers' divisions. They were filled with descendants of Korah and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:22; 1 Chronicles 28:12–19; 1 Chronicles 22:14–16"> Among the Levites, Ahijah was in charge of the treasures of God's house, and of the treasures of the things that belong to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:7; 1 Chronicles 6:17; 1 Chronicles 29:8"> The descendants of Ladan, descended from Gershon through him and who were leaders of the families of Ladan the Gershonite, were Jehieli,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:8; Nehemiah 10:38; 1 Chronicles 26:20"> the sons of Jehieli, Zetham, and Joel his brother. They were in charge of the storehouses of the house of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 3:27; 1 Chronicles 23:12; Numbers 3:19"> From the clans of Amram, the clans of Izhar, the clans of Hebron, and the clans of Uzziel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:20; 1 Chronicles 23:15–16"> Shubael son of Gershom son of Moses, was supervisor over the storehouses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:17–18; 1 Chronicles 23:15; Exodus 18:4"> His relatives from the clan of Eliezer were his son Rehabiah, Rehabiah's son Jeshaiah, Jeshaiah's son Joram, Joram's son Zichri, and Zichri's son Shelomith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:11; 1 Chronicles 29:2–9; 1 Chronicles 22:14"> Shelomith and his relatives were over all the storehouses holding the things that belong to Yahweh, that David the king, the family leaders, commanders over thousands and hundreds, and the army commanders had set apart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 12:14; Joshua 6:19; Nehemiah 10:32–34"> They set apart the plunder won in battles for the upkeep of the house of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Samuel 17:55; 2 Samuel 10:9–14"> They were also in charge of everything that was set apart to Yahweh by Samuel the prophet, Saul son of Kish, Abner son of Ner, and Joab son of Zeruiah. Everything that was set apart to Yahweh was under the guard of Shelomith and his relatives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:4; Nehemiah 11:16; 2 Chronicles 34:13"> Of Izhar's descendants, Kenaniah and his sons were in charge of the civil affairs of Israel. They were officers and judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:17; 1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> Of Hebron's descendants, Hashabiah and his brothers, 1,700 capable men, were in charge of Yahweh's work and the king's work. They were on the west side of the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:19; Joshua 21:39; 1 Chronicles 29:27"> From Hebron's descendants, Jeriah was the leader of his descendants, counted from the lists of their families. In the fortieth year of the reign of David they examined the records and found among them men of ability in Jazer of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:11; 1 Chronicles 12:37; 1 Chronicles 23:24"> Jeriah had 2,700 relatives, who were capable family leaders. David made them overseers over the tribes of Reuben and Gad, and the half tribe of Manasseh, for every matter pertaining to God, and for the king's affairs.</span>
