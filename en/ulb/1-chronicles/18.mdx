<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:1–18; 1 Samuel 5:8; 2 Samuel 1:20"> After this it came about that David attacked the Philistines and defeated them. He took Gath and its villages out of the Philistines' control.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 3:4–5; 1 Kings 10:25; Isaiah 16:1"> Then he defeated Moab, and the Moabites became servants to David and paid him tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 60:1; 2 Samuel 8:3; Genesis 15:18"> David then defeated Hadadezer, king of Zobah at Hamath, as Hadadezer was traveling to establish his rule by the Euphrates River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:4; 1 Kings 10:26; 1 Kings 4:2"> David captured from him a thousand chariots, seven thousand horsemen, and twenty thousand footmen. David hamstrung all the chariot horses, but reserved enough of them for a hundred chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:6; 2 Samuel 8:5–6; 1 Samuel 14:47"> When the Arameans of Damascus came to help Hadadezer king of Zobah, David killed twenty-two thousand Aramean men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:8; Psalms 121:8; Proverbs 21:31"> Then David put garrisons in Aram of Damascus, and the Arameans became servants to him and brought him tribute. Yahweh gave victory to David wherever he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 12:9–10"> David took the golden shields that were on Hadadezer's servants and brought them to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:8; 2 Chronicles 4:12–18; 1 Kings 7:15–47"> From Tebah and Kun, cities of Hadadezer, David took very much bronze. It was with this bronze that Solomon later made the bronze basin called “The Sea,” the pillars, and the bronze equipment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:9"> When Tou, king of Hamath, heard that David had defeated all the army of Hadadezer king of Zobah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:10; Isaiah 39:1; 2 Chronicles 9:1"> and so Tou sent Hadoram his son to King David to greet him and to bless him. He did this because David had fought against Hadadezer and defeated him, and because Tou had often been at war with Hadadezer. Tou also sent David many different sorts of articles made of gold and silver and bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:13; 1 Chronicles 26:26–27; 1 Kings 7:51"> King David set these objects apart to Yahweh, together with the silver and the gold that he carried away from all the nations: Edom, Moab, the people of Ammon, the Philistines, and Amalek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:11; 2 Samuel 10:10; 2 Samuel 8:13–14"> Abishai son of Zeruiah killed eighteen thousand Edomites in the Valley of Salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:6; Genesis 27:37; Psalms 144:10"> He placed garrisons in Edom, and all the Edomites became David's servants. Yahweh gave victory to David wherever he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:15; Psalms 89:14; Psalms 78:71–72"> David reigned over all Israel, and he administered justice and righteousness to all his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:6; 1 Kings 4:3; 2 Samuel 8:16"> Joab son of Zeruiah was the commander of the army, and Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was recorder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; 2 Samuel 20:25; 1 Kings 4:3"> Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar were priests, and Shavsha was scribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 2 Samuel 15:18; 1 Kings 2:34–35"> Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the Kerethites and Pelethites, and David's sons were the chief officials at the hand of the king.</span>
