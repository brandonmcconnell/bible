<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 3:15; 1 Chronicles 6:33; 1 Chronicles 6:39"> David and the leaders of the army selected some of the sons of Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun to prophesy with harps, with stringed instruments, and with cymbals. Here is the list of the men who performed this service:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:6; 1 Chronicles 16:5; 1 Chronicles 25:1"> From the sons of Asaph: Zaccur, Joseph, Nethaniah, and Asharelah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This is the same person referred to in 1 Chron. 25:14 as Jesarelah">a</a></sup>the sons of Asaph, under the direction of Asaph, who prophesied under the king's supervision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:41–42; 1 Chronicles 9:16; 1 Chronicles 15:18"> From the sons of Jeduthun: Gedaliah, Zeri,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="this is a variant of the name Izri, which is found in 1 Chron. 25:11, and refers to the same person">b</a></sup>Jeshaiah, Shimei, Hashabiah, and Mattithiah, six in all, under the direction of their father Jeduthun, who played the harp for giving thanks and praising Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 88:1; 1 Chronicles 6:33; 1 Chronicles 24:24"> From the sons of Heman: Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Uzziel is the same person referred to in 1 Chron. 25:18 as Azarel.">c</a></sup>Shubael, and Jerimoth, Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamti-Ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, and Mahazioth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:9; Psalms 127:3; 1 Samuel 9:9"> All these were the sons of Heman the king's prophet. God gave Heman fourteen sons and three daughters to lift up his horn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:1–3; Colossians 3:16; 1 Chronicles 23:5"> All these were under the direction of their fathers. They were musicians in Yahweh's house, with cymbals and stringed instruments as they served in God's house. Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman were under the king's supervision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:5"> They and their brothers who were skilled and trained to make music to Yahweh numbered 288.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:13; Proverbs 16:33; 1 Chronicles 26:16"> They cast lots for their duties, all alike, the same for the young as well as the old, the teacher as well as the student.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Now regarding Asaph's sons: The first lot fell to Joseph's family; the second fell to Gedaliah's family, twelve persons in number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> the third fell to Zaccur, his sons and his relatives, twelve persons in number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> the fourth fell to Izri,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="this is a variant of Zeri, found in 1 Chron. 25:3, and refers to the same person">d</a></sup>his sons and his relatives, twelve persons in number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> the fifth fell to Nethaniah, his sons and his relatives, twelve persons in number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> the sixth fell to Bukkiah, his sons and his relatives, twelve persons in number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> the seventh fell to Jesarelah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="This is the same person referred to in 1 Chron. 25:2 as Asharelah">e</a></sup>his sons and his relatives, twelve persons in number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> the eighth fell to Jeshaiah, his sons and his relatives, twelve persons in number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> the ninth fell to Mattaniah, his sons and his relatives, twelve persons in number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> the tenth fell to Shimei, his sons and his relatives, twelve persons in number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> the eleventh fell to Azarel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="This is the same person referred to in 1 Chron. 25:4 as Uzziel">f</a></sup>his sons and his relatives, twelve persons in number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> the twelfth fell to Hashabiah, his sons and his relatives, twelve persons in number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> the thirteenth fell to Shubael, his sons and his relatives, twelve persons in number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> the fourteenth fell to Mattithiah, his sons and his relatives, twelve persons in number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> the fifteenth fell to Jerimoth, his sons and his relatives, twelve persons in number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> the sixteenth fell to Hananiah, his sons and his relatives, twelve persons in number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> the seventeenth fell to Joshbekashah, his sons and his relatives, twelve persons in number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> the eighteenth fell to Hanani, his sons and his relatives, twelve persons in number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> the nineteenth fell to Mallothi, his sons and his relatives, twelve persons in number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> the twentieth fell to Eliathah, his sons and his relatives, twelve persons in number;</span>
