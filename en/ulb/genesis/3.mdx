<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 58:4"> The woman said to the serpent, “We may eat the fruit from the trees of the garden,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 2:16–17; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Exodus 19:12–13"> but concerning the fruit of the tree which is in the middle of the garden, God said, 'You may not eat it, nor may you touch it, or you will die.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 8:44; 2 Corinthians 11:3; 1 Timothy 2:14"> The serpent said to the woman, “You will surely not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; 2 Thessalonians 2:4; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> For God knows that the day you eat it your eyes will be opened, and you will be like God, knowing good and evil.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 John 2:16; James 1:14–15; 1 Timothy 2:14"> When the woman saw that the tree was good for food, and that it was a delight to the eyes, and that the tree was desirable to make one wise, she took some of its fruit and ate it. Then she also gave some to her husband who was with her, and he ate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 59:6; Genesis 2:25; Genesis 3:5"> The eyes of both of them were opened, and they knew that they were naked. They sewed fig leaves together and made coverings for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:24; Proverbs 15:3; Hebrews 4:13"> They heard the sound of Yahweh God walking in the garden in the cool of the day, so the man and his wife hid themselves from the presence of Yahweh God among the trees of the garden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 4:9; Genesis 16:8; Genesis 18:20–21"> Yahweh God called to the man and said to him, “Where are you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 2:25; Psalms 119:120; Isaiah 47:3"> The man said, “I heard you in the garden, and I was afraid, because I was naked. So I hid myself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:20; Genesis 4:10; Psalms 50:21"> God said, “Who told you that you were naked? Have you eaten from the tree that I commanded you not to eat from?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; James 1:13–15; Job 31:33"> The man said, “The woman whom you gave to be with me, she gave me fruit from the tree, and I ate it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:3; 1 Timothy 2:14; Genesis 4:10–12"> Yahweh God said to the woman, “What is this you have done?” The woman said, “The serpent lied to me, and I ate.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 7:17; Isaiah 65:25; Psalms 72:9"> Yahweh God said to the serpent,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Because you have done this,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">cursed are you alone among all the livestock</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and all the beasts of the field.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is on your stomach that you will go,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and it is dust that you will eat all the days of your life.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:20; 1 John 3:8; Revelation 12:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will put hostility between you and the woman,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and between your seed and her seed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will bruise your head, and you will bruise his heel.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 2:5; Colossians 3:18; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> To the woman he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will greatly multiply your pain in having children;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it is in pain that you will give birth to children.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your desire will be for your husband, but he will rule over you.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:20–22; Genesis 5:29; Isaiah 24:5–6"> To Adam he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Because you have listened to the voice of your wife,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and have eaten from the tree, concerning which I commanded you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">saying, 'You may not eat from it,'</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">cursed is the ground because of you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">through painful work you will eat from it all the days of your life.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:7; Job 5:5; Isaiah 7:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will produce thorns and thistles for you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you will eat the plants of the field.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 34:15; Psalms 104:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">By the sweat of your face you will eat bread,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">until you return to the ground, for out of it you were taken.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For dust you are, and to dust you will return.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:23; Matthew 1:23; Genesis 5:29"> The man called his wife's name Eve because she was the mother of all the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; 2 Corinthians 5:21; 2 Corinthians 5:2–3"> Yahweh God made for Adam and for his wife garments of skins and clothed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Revelation 22:14; Proverbs 3:18"> Yahweh God said, “Now the man has become like one of us, knowing good and evil. So now he must not be allowed to reach out with his hand, take from the tree of life, eat it, and live forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 4:2; Genesis 3:19; Genesis 4:12"> Therefore Yahweh God sent him out from the garden of Eden, to cultivate the ground from which he had been taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; Psalms 104:4; Exodus 25:18–22"> So God drove the man out of the garden, and he placed cherubim at the east of the garden of Eden, and a flaming sword that turned every way, in order to guard the way to the tree of life.</span>
