<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 12:6; Genesis 18:10; Genesis 18:14"> Yahweh paid attention to Sarah as he had said he would, and Yahweh did for Sarah just as he had promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 4:22; Hebrews 11:11; Genesis 18:10"> Sarah conceived and bore a son to Abraham in his old age, at the set time of which God had spoken to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 17:19; Romans 9:7; Genesis 21:6"> Abraham named his son, the one who had been born to him, whom Sarah bore to him, Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:8; Deuteronomy 12:32; John 7:22–23"> Abraham circumcised his son Isaac when he was eight days old, just as God had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; Romans 4:19; Genesis 17:1"> Abraham was one hundred years old when his son Isaac was born to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 126:2; Isaiah 54:1; Genesis 17:17"> Sarah said, “God has made me laugh; every one who hears will laugh with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 18:11–12; Isaiah 66:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:10"> She also said, “Who would have said to Abraham that Sarah would nurse children, and yet I have borne him a son in his old age!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:22; Hosea 1:8; Genesis 40:20"> The child grew and was weaned, and Abraham made a great feast on the day that Isaac was weaned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 4:29; Genesis 16:15; Galatians 4:22"> Sarah saw the son of Hagar the Egyptian, whom she had borne to Abraham, mocking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:35; 1 John 2:19; Galatians 4:22–31"> So she said to Abraham, “Drive out this slave woman and her son, for the son of this slave woman will not be heir with my son, with Isaac.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 12:11; Genesis 17:18; Matthew 10:37"> This thing was very grievous to Abraham because of his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:18; Romans 9:7–8; Genesis 17:19"> But God said to Abraham, “Do not be grieved because of the lad, and because of your servant woman. Listen to her words in all she says to you about this matter, because it is through Isaac that your descendants will be named.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 16:10; Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:18"> I will also make the son of the servant woman into a nation, because he is your descendant.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Psalms 119:60; Genesis 16:7"> Abraham rose up early in the morning, took bread and a skin of water, and gave it to Hagar, putting it on her shoulder. He gave her the boy and sent her away. She departed and wandered in the wilderness of Beersheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 3:9; Psalms 63:1; Jeremiah 14:3"> When the water in the waterskin was gone, she abandoned the child under one of the bushes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Genesis 29:11; 1 Samuel 24:16"> Then she went, and sat down a short distance from him, about the distance of a bowshot away, for she said, “Let me not look upon the death of the child.” As she sat there across from him, she lifted up her voice and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 16:11; Psalms 50:15; Exodus 3:7"> God heard the voice of the lad, and the angel of God called to Hagar out of heaven, and said to her, “What troubles you, Hagar? Do not be afraid, for God has heard the voice of the lad where he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 21:13; Genesis 16:10; Genesis 17:20"> Get up, raise up the lad, and encourage him; for I will make him into a great nation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 22:31; 2 Kings 6:17–20; Isaiah 35:5–6"> Then God opened her eyes, and she saw a well of water. She went and filled the skin with water, and gave the lad a drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Genesis 39:21; Genesis 16:12"> God was with the lad, and he grew. He lived in the wilderness and became an archer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 24:3–4; Numbers 12:16"> He lived in the wilderness of Paran, and his mother got a wife for him from the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 3:7; Genesis 26:26; Genesis 26:28"> It came about at that time that Abimelech and Phicol the captain of his army spoke to Abraham, saying, “God is with you in all that you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Genesis 20:14; Hebrews 6:16"> Now therefore swear to me here by God that you will not deal falsely with me, nor with my offspring, nor with my descendants. Show to me and to the land in which you have been staying the same covenant faithfulness that I have shown to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 6:16; Genesis 14:13; Romans 12:18"> Abraham said, “I swear.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 26:15–22; Genesis 29:8; Proverbs 25:9"> Abraham also complained to Abimelech concerning a well of water that Abimelech's servants had seized from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 13:7; 2 Kings 5:20–24"> Abimelech said, “I do not know who has done this thing. You did not tell me before now; I have not heard of it until today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 14:22–23; Isaiah 32:8; Romans 1:31"> So Abraham took sheep and oxen and gave them to Abimelech, and the two men made a covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Then Abraham set seven female lambs of the flock by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:14; Genesis 33:8; Exodus 12:26"> Abimelech said to Abraham, “What is the meaning of these seven female lambs that you have set by themselves?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 31:52; Joshua 24:27; Joshua 22:27–28"> He replied, “These seven female lambs you will receive from my hand, so that it may be a witness for me, that I dug this well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 26:33; Genesis 21:14; 1 Kings 4:25"> So he called that place Beersheba, because there they both swore an oath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 13:17; Genesis 21:27; Judges 13:1"> They made a covenant at Beersheba, and then Abimelech and Phicol, the captain of his army, returned to the land of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Isaiah 40:28; Psalms 90:2"> Abraham planted a tamarisk tree in Beersheba. There he worshiped Yahweh, the eternal God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; 1 Peter 2:11; Hebrews 11:9"> Abraham remained as a foreigner in the land of the Philistines many days.</span>
</div>
