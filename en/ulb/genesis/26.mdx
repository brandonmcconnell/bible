<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Genesis 25:11; Genesis 21:22–32"> Now a famine happened in the land, besides the first famine that had been in the days of Abraham. Isaac went to Abimelech, king of the Philistines at Gerar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Psalms 37:3; Genesis 17:1"> Now Yahweh appeared to him and said, “Do not go down to Egypt; live in the land that I tell you to live in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Psalms 105:9; Genesis 17:8"> Stay in this very land, and I will be with you and will bless you; for to you and to your descendants, I will give all these lands, and I will fulfill the oath that I swore to Abraham your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 3:25; Genesis 15:5; Genesis 22:17–18"> I will multiply your descendants like the stars of heaven, and will give to your descendants all these lands. Through your descendants all the nations of the earth will be blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 7:24; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 22:16"> I will do this because Abraham obeyed my voice and kept my instructions, my commandments, my statutes, and my laws.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 20:1"> So Isaac settled in Gerar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Genesis 12:13; Genesis 20:2"> When the men of the place asked him about his wife, he said, “She is my sister.” He feared to say, “She is my wife,” because he thought, “The men of this place will kill me to get Rebekah, because she is so beautiful.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 62:5; Proverbs 5:18–19; Proverbs 7:6"> After Isaac had been there a long time, Abimelech king of the Philistines happened to look out of a window. He saw, behold, Isaac was caressing Rebekah, his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Abimelech called Isaac to him and said, “Look, certainly she is your wife. Why did you say, 'She is my sister'?” Isaac said to him, “Because I thought someone might kill me to get her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 12:18–19"> Abimelech said, “What is this you have done to us? One of the people might easily have slept with your wife, and you would have brought guilt upon us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 105:15; Genesis 20:6; Proverbs 6:29"> So Abimelech warned all the people and said, “Whoever touches this man or his wife will surely be put to death.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:8; Genesis 24:1; Genesis 26:3"> Isaac planted crops in that land and reaped in the same year a hundredfold, because Yahweh blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 112:3; Proverbs 10:22; Genesis 24:35"> The man became rich, and grew more and more until he became very great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 37:11; Genesis 13:2; Proverbs 27:4"> He had many sheep and cattle, and a large household. The Philistines envied him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 21:30; Genesis 21:25"> Now all the wells that his father's servants had dug in the days of Abraham his father, the Philistines stopped them up by filling them with earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 1:9"> Abimelech said to Isaac, “Go away from us, for you are much mightier than we.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> So Isaac departed from there and settled in the Valley of Gerar, and lived there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 13:2; Hosea 2:17; Numbers 32:38"> Once again Isaac dug out the wells of water, which they had dug in the days of Abraham his father. The Philistines had stopped them up after Abraham's death. Isaac called the wells by the same names that his father had called them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 4:10–11; John 7:38; Song of Songs 4:15"> When Isaac's servants dug in the valley, they found there a well of flowing water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:25"> The herdsmen of Gerar quarreled with Isaac's herdsmen, and said, “This water is ours.” So Isaac called that well “Esek,” because they had quarreled with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 4:6"> Then they dug another well, and they quarreled over that, too, so he gave it the name of “Sitnah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 17:6; Exodus 1:7; Psalms 118:5"> He left there and dug yet another well, but they did not quarrel over that one. So he called it Rehoboth, and he said, “Now Yahweh has made room for us, and we will prosper in the land.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Genesis 21:31; Genesis 46:1"> Then Isaac went up from there to Beersheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 15:1; Exodus 3:6; Isaiah 43:1–2"> Yahweh appeared to him that same night and said, “I am the God of Abraham your father. Do not fear, for I am with you and will bless you and multiply your descendants, for my servant Abraham's sake.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Psalms 116:17; Genesis 12:7–8"> Isaac built an altar there and called on the name of Yahweh. There he pitched his tent, and his servants dug a well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–32; Genesis 20:3"> Then Abimelech went to him from Gerar, with Ahuzzath, his friend, and Phicol, the captain of his army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 26:16; Judges 11:7; Acts 7:14"> Isaac said to them, “Why are you coming to me, since you hate me and have sent me away from you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–23; Isaiah 45:14; Isaiah 61:9"> Then they said, “We have clearly seen that Yahweh has been with you. So we decided that there should be an oath between us, yes, between us and you. So let us make a covenant with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 24:31; Genesis 21:22; Genesis 22:17"> that you will do us no harm, just as we have not harmed you, and as we have treated you well and have sent you away in peace. Indeed, you are blessed by Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:3; Romans 12:18; Hebrews 12:14"> So Isaac made a feast for them, and they ate and drank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:16–17; Hebrews 6:16; Genesis 25:33"> They rose early in the morning and swore an oath with each other. Then Isaac sent them away, and they left him in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 2:4–5"> That same day Isaac's servants came and told him about the well that they had dug. They said, “We have found water.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Genesis 26:28"> He called the well Shibah, so the name of that city is Beersheba to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 36:13; Exodus 34:16"> When Esau was forty years old, he took a wife, Judith the daughter of Beeri the Hittite, and also Basemath the daughter of Elon the Hittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 27:46–2; Genesis 28:8; Genesis 6:2"> They brought sorrow to Isaac and Rebekah.</span>
</div>
