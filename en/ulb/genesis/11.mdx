<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 2:6; Zephaniah 3:9; Isaiah 19:18"> Now the whole earth used one language and had the same words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 10:10; Daniel 1:2; Genesis 14:1"> As they journeyed in the east, they found a plain in the land of Shinar and they settled there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:10; Exodus 1:14; Exodus 2:3"> They said to one another, “Come, let us make bricks and bake them thoroughly.” They had brick instead of stone and tar as mortar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; John 5:44; Luke 1:51"> They said, “Come, let us build ourselves a city and a tower whose top will reach to the sky, and let us make a name for ourselves. If we do not, we will be scattered across the surface of the whole earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 18:21; Psalms 33:13–14; John 3:13"> So Yahweh came down to see the city and the tower which the descendants of Adam had built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–4; Genesis 3:22; Genesis 11:1"> Yahweh said, “Look, they are one people with the same language, and they are beginning to do this! Soon nothing that they intend to do will be impossible for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Psalms 55:9; Job 5:12–13"> Come, let us go down and confuse their language there, so that they may not understand each other.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 1:51; Deuteronomy 32:8; Genesis 10:25"> So Yahweh scattered them from there across the surface of all the earth and they stopped building the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 10:10; Genesis 10:5; Genesis 10:20"> Therefore, its name was called Babel, because there Yahweh confused the language of the whole earth and from there Yahweh scattered them abroad over the surface of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:17–27; Genesis 10:21–22; Luke 3:34–36"> These were the descendants of Shem. Shem was a hundred years old, and he became the father of Arphaxad two years after the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 9:7; Psalms 144:12; Genesis 1:28"> Shem lived five hundred years after he became the father of Arphaxad. He also became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 3:36"> When Arphaxad had lived thirty-five years, he became the father of Shelah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Arphaxad lived 403 years after he became the father of Shelah. He also became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When Shelah had lived thirty years, he became the father of Eber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Shelah lived 403 years after he became the father of Eber. He also became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 10:21; 1 Chronicles 1:19; Numbers 24:24"> When Eber had lived thirty-four years, he became the father of Peleg.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Eber lived 430 years after he became the father of Peleg. He also became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 3:35"> When Peleg had lived thirty years, he became the father of Reu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Peleg lived 209 years after he became the father of Reu. He also became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 3:35"> When Reu had lived thirty-two years, he became the father of Serug.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Reu lived 207 years after he became the father of Serug. He also became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 24:2"> When Serug had lived thirty years, he became the father of Nahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Serug lived two hundred years after he became the father of Nahor. He also became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 3:34; Joshua 24:2"> When Nahor had live twenty-nine years, he became the father of Terah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Nahor lived 119 years after he became the father of Terah. He also became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 24:2; 1 Chronicles 1:26–27; Genesis 22:20–24"> After Terah had lived seventy years, he became the father of Abram, Nahor, and Haran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 12:4; Genesis 14:12; Genesis 11:31"> Now these were the descendants of Terah. Terah became the father of Abram, Nahor, and Haran, and Haran became the father of Lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 15:7; Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–4"> Haran died in the presence of his father Terah in the land of his birth, in Ur of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 17:15; Genesis 22:20; Genesis 24:15"> Abram and Nahor took wives. The name of Abram's wife was Sarai and the name of Nahor's wife was Milkah, a daughter of Haran, who was the father of Milkah and Iskah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 29:31; Genesis 21:1–2; Luke 1:7"> Now Sarai was barren; she had no child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–4; Genesis 24:10"> Terah took Abram his son, Lot the son of his son Haran, and Sarai his daughter-in-law, his son Abram's wife, and together they left Ur of the Chaldeans, to go into the land of Canaan. But they came to Haran and stayed there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Terah lived 205 years and then died in Haran.</span>
