<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 30:21; Jeremiah 2:36; Genesis 30:13"> Now Dinah, Leah's daughter whom she bore to Jacob, went out to meet the young women of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 5:28; Job 31:1; Job 31:9"> Shechem son of Hamor the Hivite, the prince of the land, saw her and he grabbed her, assaulted her, and slept with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 2:14; Isaiah 40:2; 2 Chronicles 30:22"> He was drawn to Dinah, the daughter of Jacob. He loved the young woman and spoke tenderly to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 14:2; 2 Samuel 13:13; Genesis 21:21"> Shechem spoke to his father Hamor, saying, “Get this young woman for me as a wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:22; Leviticus 10:3; 1 Samuel 10:27"> Now Jacob heard that he had defiled Dinah his daughter. His sons were with his livestock in the field, so Jacob held his peace until they came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Hamor the father of Shechem went out to Jacob to speak with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 7:15; Judges 20:6; Deuteronomy 22:21"> The sons of Jacob came in from the field when they heard of the matter. The men were offended. They were very angry because he had disgraced Israel by forcing himself on Jacob's daughter, for such a thing should not have been done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 11:2; Psalms 119:20; Psalms 84:2"> Hamor spoke with them, saying, “My son Shechem loves your daughter. Please give her to him as a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 19:14"> Intermarry with us, give your daughters to us, and take our daughters for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 47:27; Genesis 42:34"> You will live with us, and the land will be open to you to live and trade in, and to acquire property.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 33:15; Genesis 18:3"> Shechem said to her father and to her brothers, “Let me find favor in your eyes, and whatever you tell me I will give.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 22:16–17; Deuteronomy 22:28–29; 1 Samuel 18:25–27"> Ask me for as great a bride price and gift as you will, and I will give whatever you say to me, but give me the young woman as a wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 25:27–34; Isaiah 59:13; Job 13:4"> The sons of Jacob answered Shechem and Hamor his father with deceit, because Shechem had defiled Dinah their sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–39; 2 Samuel 15:7; Genesis 17:11"> They said to them, “We cannot do this thing, to give our sister to anyone who is uncircumcised; for that would be a disgrace to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:12"> Only on this condition will we agree with you: If you will become circumcised as we are, if every male among you is circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then will we give our daughters to you, and we will take your daughters to ourselves, and we will live with you and become one people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But if you do not listen to us and become circumcised, then we will take our sister and we will leave.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Their words pleased Hamor and his son Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:12; Acts 13:50; 1 Samuel 22:14"> The young man did not delay to do what they said, because he delighted in Jacob's daughter, and because he was the most honored person in all his father's household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; Proverbs 31:23; Amos 5:12"> Hamor and Shechem his son went to the gate of their city and spoke with the men of their city, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> “These men are at peace with us, so let them live in the land and trade in it for, really, the land is large enough for them. Let us take their daughters as wives, and let us give them our daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Only on this condition will the men agree to live with us and become one people: If every male among us is circumcised, as they are circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 1:12–13; John 6:26–27; John 2:16"> Will not their livestock and their property—all their animals be ours? So let us agree with them, and they will live among us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 23:10; 1 Corinthians 7:19; Isaiah 1:10–16"> All the men of the city listened to Hamor and Shechem, his son. Every male was circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 49:5–7; Genesis 29:33–34; Numbers 31:7"> On the third day, when they were still in pain, two of the sons of Jacob (Simeon and Levi, Dinah's brothers), each took his sword and they attacked the city that was certain of its security, and they killed all the males.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:26; Deuteronomy 32:42; Isaiah 31:8"> They killed Hamor and his son Shechem with the edge of the sword. They took Dinah from Shechem's house and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 7:13; Joshua 7:21; Exodus 2:14"> The other sons of Jacob came to the dead bodies and looted the city, because the people had defiled their sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 1:15–16; Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Job 20:5"> They took their flocks, their herds, their donkeys, and everything in the city and in the surrounding fields with</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> all their wealth. All their children and their wives, they captured. They even took everything that was in the houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 105:12; Joshua 7:25; Exodus 5:21"> Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, “You have brought trouble on me, to make me stink to the inhabitants of the land, the Canaanites and the Perizzites. I am few in number. If they gather themselves together against me and attack me, then I will be destroyed, I and my household.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 6:34; Genesis 34:13; Genesis 49:7"> But Simeon and Levi said, “Should Shechem have dealt with our sister as with a prostitute?”</span>
