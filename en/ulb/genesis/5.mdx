<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 4:25; Job 25:4; Psalms 51:5"> When Adam had lived 130 years, he became the father of a son in his own likeness, after his image, and he called his name Seth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:1–3; Luke 3:36–38; Genesis 5:26"> After Adam became the father of Seth, he lived eight hundred years. He became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 4:26"> When Seth had lived 105 years, he became the father of Enosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> After he became the father of Enosh, he lived 807 years and became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Seth lived 912 years, and then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 3:37; 1 Chronicles 1:2"> When Enosh had lived ninety years, he became the father of Kenan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> After he became the father of Kenan, Enosh lived 815 years. He became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Enosh lived 905 years, and then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 3:37"> When Kenan had lived seventy years, he became the father of Mahalalel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> After he became the father of Mahalalel, Kenan lived 840 years. He became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Kenan lived 910 years, and then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:2"> When Mahalalel had lived sixty-five years, he became the father of Jared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> After he became the father of Jared, Mahalalel lived 830 years. He became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Mahalalel lived 895 years, and then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 4:17; 1 Chronicles 1:3; Jude 1:14–15"> When Jared had lived 162 years, he became the father of Enoch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> After he became the father of Enoch, Jared lived eight hundred years. He became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Jared lived 962 years, and then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 3:37"> When Enoch had lived sixty-five years, he became the father of Methuselah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 6:9"> Enoch walked with God three hundred years after he became the father of Methuselah. He became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Enoch lived 365 years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 11:5–6; 1 John 1:7; Genesis 5:22"> Enoch walked with God, and then he was gone, for God took him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 4:18"> When Methuselah had lived 187 years, he became the father of Lamech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> After he became the father of Lamech, Methuselah lived 782 years. He became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Methuselah lived 969 years. Then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When Lamech had lived 182 years, he became the father of a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 2:5; Hebrews 11:7; Matthew 24:37"> He called his name Noah, saying, “This one will give us rest from our work and from the painful labor of our hands, which we must do because of the ground that Yahweh has cursed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Lamech lived 595 years after he became the father of Noah. He became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Lamech lived 777 years. Then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 6:10; Genesis 10:21; Genesis 9:18–19"> After Noah had lived five hundred years, he became the father of Shem, Ham, and Japheth.</span>
