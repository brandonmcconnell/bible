<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 3:12; Genesis 3:15; Genesis 4:25"> The man slept with Eve his wife. She conceived and gave birth to Cain. She said, “I have produced a man with Yahweh's help.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 3:15; 1 John 3:12; Luke 11:51"> Then she gave birth to his brother Abel. Now Abel became a shepherd, but Cain cultivated the soil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 18:12; Leviticus 2:1–11; 1 Kings 17:7"> It came about that in the course of time Cain brought some of the fruit of the ground as an offering to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Proverbs 3:9; Exodus 13:12"> As for Abel, he brought some of the firstborn of his flock and some of the fat. Yahweh accepted Abel and his offering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Job 5:2; Luke 15:28–30"> but Cain and his offering he did not accept. So Cain was very angry, and he scowled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Job 5:2; Luke 15:31–32"> Yahweh said to Cain, “Why are you angry and why are you scowling?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:15; Romans 2:6–10; Isaiah 3:10–11"> If you do what is right, will you not be accepted? But if you do not do what is right, sin crouches at the door and desires to control you, but you must rule over it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 23:35; 1 John 3:12–15; Jude 1:11"> Cain spoke to Abel his brother. It came about that while they were in the fields, Cain rose up against Abel his brother and killed him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The best ancient copies read in this way. However, some old translations and some modern translations read, Cain said to Abel his brother, “Let us go into the fields.” It came about that while they were in the fields, Cain rose up against Abel his brother and killed him .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; John 8:44; Psalms 9:12"> Then Yahweh said to Cain, “Where is Abel your brother?” He said, “I do not know. Am I my brother's keeper?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 12:24; Psalms 72:14; Hebrews 11:4"> Yahweh said, “What have you done? Your brother's blood is calling out to me from the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Job 16:18; Genesis 3:14"> Now cursed are you from the ground, which has opened its mouth to receive your brother's blood from your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 9:17; Leviticus 26:36; Leviticus 26:20"> When you cultivate the ground, from now on it will not yield to you its strength. A fugitive and a wanderer you will be in the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:22; Revelation 16:9; Revelation 16:11"> Cain said to Yahweh, “My punishment is greater than I can bear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 35:19; Psalms 143:7; Job 15:20–24"> Indeed, you have driven me out this day from this ground, and I will be hidden from your face. I will be a fugitive and a wanderer in the earth, and whoever finds me will kill me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:4; Psalms 79:12; Genesis 4:24"> Yahweh said to him, “If anyone kills Cain, vengeance will be taken on him sevenfold.” Then Yahweh put a mark on Cain, so that if anyone found him, that person would not attack him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:39; Job 2:7; Genesis 4:14"> So Cain went out from the presence of Yahweh and lived in the land of Nod, on the east of Eden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 49:11; Ecclesiastes 2:4–11; Daniel 4:30"> Cain slept with his wife and she conceived. She gave birth to Enoch. He built a city and named it after his son Enoch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 5:21"> To Enoch was born Irad. Irad became the father of Mehujael. Mehujael became the father of Methushael. Methushael became the father of Lamech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:8; Genesis 2:18"> Lamech took for himself two wives. The name of the one was Adah, and the name of the other was Zillah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 4:2; Jeremiah 35:9–10; John 8:44"> Adah gave birth to Jabal. He was the father of those who lived in tents who have livestock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 21:12; Isaiah 5:12; Genesis 31:27"> His brother's name was Jubal. He was the father of those who play the harp and pipe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:25; Deuteronomy 8:9; 2 Chronicles 2:7"> As for Zillah, she bore Tubal-Cain, the forger of tools of bronze and iron. The sister of Tubal-Cain was Naamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 23:18; Exodus 20:13; Leviticus 19:18"> Lamech said to his wives,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Adah and Zillah, listen to my voice; you wives of Lamech, listen to what I say.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I have killed a man for wounding me, a young man for bruising me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 18:22; Genesis 4:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">If Cain is avenged seven times, then Lamech will be avenged seventy-seven times.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 4:8; Genesis 4:10–11; 1 Chronicles 1:1"> Adam slept with his wife again, and she bore another son. She called his name Seth and said, “God has given me another son in the place of Abel, for Cain killed him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 116:17; 1 Kings 18:24; Joel 2:32"> A son was born to Seth and he called his name Enosh. At that time people began to call on the name of Yahweh.</span>
