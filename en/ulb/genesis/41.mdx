<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 20:3; Genesis 29:14; Exodus 4:9"> It came about at the end of two full years that Pharaoh had a dream. Behold, he stood by the Nile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 19:6"> Behold, seven cows came up out of the Nile, desirable and fat, and they grazed in the reeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 41:20–21; Genesis 41:4"> Behold, seven other cows came up after them out of the Nile, undesirable and thin. They stood by the other cows on the bank of the river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 3:15"> Then the undesirable and thin cows ate the seven desirable and fat cows. Then Pharaoh woke up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:14"> Then he slept and dreamed a second time. Behold, seven heads of grain came up on one stalk, wholesome and good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Hosea 13:15; Ezekiel 19:12"> Behold, seven heads, thin and scorched by the east wind, sprouted up after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 37:5; Genesis 20:3"> The thin heads swallowed up the seven wholesome and full heads. Pharaoh woke up, and, behold, it was a dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 4:7; Exodus 7:22; Matthew 2:1"> It came about in the morning that his spirit was troubled. He sent and called for all the magicians and wise men of Egypt. Pharaoh told them his dreams, but there was no one who could interpret them to Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 40:14; Genesis 40:23; Genesis 40:1–3"> Then the chief cupbearer said to Pharaoh, “Today I am thinking about my offenses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 39:20; Genesis 40:2–3; Genesis 37:36"> Pharaoh was angry with his servants, and put me in custody in the house of the captain of the guard, the chief baker and me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 40:5–8"> We dreamed a dream the same night, he and I. We dreamed each man according to the interpretation of his dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 40:12–19; Genesis 37:36; Genesis 39:1"> There was with us there a young Hebrew man, a servant of the captain of the guard. We told him and he interpreted for us our dreams. He interpreted for each of us according to his dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:3; Genesis 40:20–22; Genesis 40:12"> It came about as he interpreted for us, so it happened. Pharaoh restored me to my post, but the other one he hanged.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 2:25; Psalms 113:7–8; Psalms 105:19–22"> Then Pharaoh sent and called for Joseph. They quickly took him out of the dungeon. He shaved himself, changed his clothes, and came in to Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 5:16; Daniel 5:12; Psalms 25:14"> Pharaoh said to Joseph, “I had a dream, but there is no interpreter for it. But I have heard about you, that when you hear a dream you can interpret it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; Genesis 40:8; Daniel 2:47"> Joseph answered Pharaoh, saying, “It is not in me. God will answer Pharaoh with favor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Pharaoh spoke to Joseph, “In my dream, behold, I stood on the bank of the Nile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:8; Jeremiah 24:1–3; Jeremiah 24:5"> Behold, seven cows came up out of the Nile, fat and desirable, and they grazed among the reeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Behold, seven other cows came up after them, weak, very undesirable, and thin. I never saw in all the land of Egypt such undesirableness like them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The thin and undesirable cows ate up the first seven fat cows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:3; Psalms 37:19; Revelation 10:9–10"> When they had eaten them up, it could not be known that they had eaten them, for they were still as undesirable as before. Then I awoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> I looked in my dream, and, behold, seven heads came up upon one stalk, full and good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 129:6–7; Hosea 13:15; 2 Kings 19:26"> Behold, seven more heads—withered, thin, and scorched by the east wind—sprang up after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 4:7; Genesis 41:8; Exodus 8:19"> The thin heads swallowed up the seven good heads. I told these dreams to the magicians, but there was none that could explain it to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 2:45; Revelation 4:1; Ephesians 1:17"> Joseph said to Pharaoh, “The dreams of Pharaoh are the same. What God is about to do, he has declared to Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 41:47; Genesis 41:2; Genesis 41:53"> The seven good cows are seven years, and the seven good heads are seven years. The dreams are the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 8:1; 2 Samuel 24:19"> The seven thin and undesirable cows that came up after them are seven years, and also the seven thin heads scorched by the east wind will be seven years of famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 41:25; Genesis 41:16"> That is the thing which I spoke to Pharaoh. What God is about to do he has revealed to Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 41:26; Genesis 41:46–47; Genesis 41:49"> Look, seven years of great abundance will come throughout all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 47:13; Genesis 41:54; James 5:17"> Seven years of famine will come after them, and all the abundance will be forgotten in the land of Egypt, and the famine will devastate the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 24:20; 1 Samuel 5:6"> The abundance will not be remembered in the land because of the famine that will follow, for it will be very severe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 46:10–11; Isaiah 14:24–27"> That the dream was repeated to Pharaoh is because the matter has been established by God, and God will soon do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 41:39; Acts 6:3; Deuteronomy 1:13"> Now let Pharaoh look for a man discerning and wise, and put him over the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 22:3; Luke 16:5; Proverbs 6:6–8"> Let Pharaoh appoint officials over the land, and let them take a fifth of the crops of Egypt in the seven abundant years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 45:6–7; Genesis 41:56; Exodus 4:13"> Let them gather all the food of these good years that are coming and store up grain under the authority of Pharaoh, for food to be used in the cities. They should preserve it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 47:13–25"> The food will be a supply for the land for the seven years of famine which will be in the land of Egypt. In this way the land will not be devastated by the famine.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 7:10; 1 Kings 21:2; 2 Samuel 3:36"> This advice was good in the eyes of Pharaoh and in the eyes of all his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Job 32:8; Daniel 5:14; Numbers 27:18"> Pharaoh said to his servants, “Can we find such a man as this, in whom is the Spirit of God?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 41:33; Genesis 41:28; Genesis 41:25"> So Pharaoh said to Joseph, “Since God has shown you all this, there is none so discerning and wise as you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29; Acts 7:10; Psalms 105:21–22"> You will be over my house, and according to your word will all my people be ruled. Only in the throne will I be greater than you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Philippians 2:9–11; Genesis 42:6"> Pharaoh said to Joseph, “See, I have put you over all the land of Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Daniel 5:7; Esther 3:10"> Pharaoh took off his signet ring from his hand and put it upon Joseph's hand. He clothed him with clothes of fine linen, and put a gold chain on his neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 7:10; Genesis 45:8; Genesis 45:26"> He had him ride in the second chariot which he possessed. Men shouted before him, “Bend the knee.” Pharaoh put him over all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 105:22; Exodus 11:7"> Pharaoh said to Joseph, “I am Pharaoh, and apart from you, no man will lift his hand or his foot in all the land of Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Genesis 46:20; Ezekiel 30:17; Exodus 2:16"> Pharaoh called Joseph's name “Zaphenath-Paneah.” He gave him Asenath, the daughter of Potiphera priest of On, as a wife. Joseph went out over the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Daniel 1:19; 1 Kings 12:6; 1 Samuel 16:21"> Joseph was thirty years old when he stood before Pharaoh, king of Egypt. Joseph went out from the presence of Pharaoh, and went throughout all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Genesis 26:12; Psalms 72:16"> In the seven bountiful years the land produced abundantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Genesis 41:34–36; Genesis 47:21"> He gathered up all the food of the seven years that was in the land of Egypt and put the food in the cities. He put into each city the food from the fields that surrounded it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 78:27; Judges 6:5; Genesis 22:17"> Joseph stored up grain like the sand of the sea, so much that he stopped counting, because it was beyond counting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 46:20; Genesis 48:5; 2 Samuel 8:18"> Joseph had two sons before the years of famine came, whom Asenath, the daughter of Potiphera priest of On, bore to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; Proverbs 31:7; Deuteronomy 33:17"> Joseph called the name of his firstborn Manasseh, for he said, “God has made me forget all my trouble and all my father's household.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Genesis 49:22; Genesis 50:23; Genesis 17:6"> He called the name of the second son Ephraim, for he said, “God has made me fruitful in the land of my affliction.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 41:29–31; Psalms 73:20; Luke 16:25"> The seven years of abundance that was in the land of Egypt came to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Genesis 45:11; Acts 7:11; Psalms 105:16"> The seven years of famine began, as Joseph had said. There was famine in all lands, but in all the land of Egypt there was food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Matthew 17:5; Matthew 3:17; Jeremiah 14:1–6"> When all the land of Egypt was famished, the people loudly called on Pharaoh for food. Pharaoh said to all the Egyptians, “Go to Joseph and do what he says.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Zechariah 5:3; Luke 21:35"> The famine was over all the face of the whole land. Joseph opened all the storehouses and sold to the Egyptians. The famine was severe in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Genesis 41:54; Genesis 42:5; Genesis 41:56"> All the earth was coming to Egypt to buy grain from Joseph, because the famine was severe in all the earth.</span>
