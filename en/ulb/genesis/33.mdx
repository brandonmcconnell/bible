<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Luke 14:11; Genesis 18:2"> He himself went on ahead of them. He bowed toward the ground seven times, until he came near to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 45:14–15; Psalms 34:4; Luke 15:20"> Esau ran to meet him, embraced him, hugged his neck, and kissed him. Then they wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 127:3; Isaiah 8:18; Genesis 48:9"> When Esau looked up, he saw the women and the children. He said, “Who are these people with you?” Jacob said, “The children whom God has graciously given your servant.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then the female servants came forward with their children, and they bowed down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Next Leah also and her children came forward and bowed down. Finally Joseph and Rachel came forward and bowed down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:5; Genesis 39:5; Genesis 32:13–20"> Esau said, “What do you mean by all these groups that I met?” Jacob said, “To find favor in the sight of my master.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 27:39; Philemon 1:7; Genesis 27:41"> Esau said, “I have enough, my brother. Keep what you have for yourself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 33:26; Revelation 22:4; Genesis 32:30"> Jacob said, “No, please, if I have found favor in your eyes, then accept my gift from my hand, for indeed, I have seen your face, and it is like seeing the face of God, and you have accepted me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:27; Philippians 4:18; Genesis 30:43"> Please accept my gift that was brought to you, because God has dealt graciously with me, and because I have enough.” Thus Jacob urged him, and Esau accepted it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then Esau said, “Let us be on our way. I will go before you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; Ezekiel 34:15–16; Ezekiel 34:23–25"> Jacob said to him, “My master knows that the children are young, and that the sheep and the cattle are nursing their young. If they are driven hard even one day, all the animals will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 32:3; Isaiah 40:11; Romans 15:1"> Please let my master go on ahead of his servant. I will travel more slowly, at the pace of the livestock that are before me, and at the pace of the children, until I come to my master in Seir.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Genesis 47:25; Genesis 34:11"> Esau said, “Let me leave with you some of my men who are with me.” But Jacob said, “Why do that? Let me find favor in the sight of my lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So Esau that day started on his way back to Seir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 60:6; Joshua 13:27; Judges 8:5"> Jacob traveled to Succoth, built himself a house, and made shelters for his livestock. Therefore the name of the place is called Succoth.</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:16; John 4:5; Joshua 24:32"> Then he bought the piece of ground where he had pitched his tent from the sons of Hamor, Shechem's father, for a hundred pieces of silver.</span>
