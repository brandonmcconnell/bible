<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 31:17; Exodus 20:11; Psalms 33:6"> Then the heavens and the earth were finished, and all the living things that filled them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:4; Exodus 31:17; Deuteronomy 5:14"> On the seventh day God came to the end of his work which he had done, and so he rested on the seventh day from all his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:12; Mark 2:27; Leviticus 23:3"> God blessed the seventh day and sanctified it, because in it he rested from all his work which he had done in his creation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 5:1; Revelation 1:8; Genesis 1:4"> These were the events concerning the heavens and the earth, when they were created, on the day that Yahweh God made the earth and the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 3:23; Hebrews 6:7; Job 5:10"> No bush of the field was yet in the earth, and no plant of the field had yet sprouted, for Yahweh God had not caused it to rain upon the earth, and there was no man to cultivate the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But a mist went up from the earth and watered the whole surface of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 33:4; 1 Corinthians 15:45"> Yahweh God formed man from the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life, and man became a living being.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Genesis 3:24; Genesis 13:10"> Yahweh God planted a garden eastward, in Eden, and there he put the man whom he had formed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 3:22; Revelation 2:7; Revelation 22:14"> Out of the ground Yahweh God made every tree to grow that is pleasant to the sight and good for food. This included the tree of life that was in the midst of the garden, and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Psalms 46:4"> A river went out of Eden to water the garden. From there it divided and became four rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Genesis 10:29; 1 Samuel 15:7"> The name of the first is Pishon. It is the one which flows throughout the whole land of Havilah, where there is gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 28:20; Numbers 11:7; Ezekiel 28:13"> The gold of that land is good. There are also bdellium and the onyx stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:6; Isaiah 11:11"> The name of the second river is Gihon. This one flows throughout the whole land of Cush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 10:4; Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 1:7"> The name of the third river is Tigris, which flows east of Ashur. The fourth river is the Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Genesis 2:8; Ephesians 4:28"> Yahweh God took the man and put him into the garden of Eden to work it and to maintain it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; 1 Timothy 4:4; 1 Timothy 6:17"> Yahweh God commanded the man, saying, “From every tree in the garden you may freely eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:23; James 1:15; Genesis 3:19"> But from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil you may not eat, for on the day that you eat from it, you will surely die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:9–12; 1 Corinthians 11:7–12; Proverbs 18:22"> Then Yahweh God said, “It is not good that the man should be alone. I will make him a helper suitable for him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Genesis 1:28; Genesis 9:2"> Out of the ground Yahweh God formed every animal of the field and every bird of the sky. Then he brought them to the man to see what he would call them. Whatever the man called each living creature, that was its name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:18"> The man gave names to all the livestock, to all the birds of the sky, and to every beast of the field. But for the man himself there was found no helper suitable for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:12; Job 4:13; 1 Samuel 26:12"> Yahweh God caused a deep sleep to fall upon the man, so the man slept. Yahweh God took one of his ribs and closed up the flesh where he took the rib.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:8–9; 1 Timothy 2:13; Hebrews 13:4"> With the rib that Yahweh God had taken from the man, he made a woman and brought her to the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 5:28–30; 1 Corinthians 11:8–9; 2 Samuel 5:1"> The man said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“This time, this one is bone of my bones, and flesh of my flesh.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">She will be called 'woman,' because she was taken out of man.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 19:3–9; Ephesians 5:28–31; Mark 10:6–12"> Therefore a man will leave his father and his mother, he will be united to his wife, and they will become one flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 3:7; Genesis 3:10–11; Romans 10:11"> They were both naked, the man and his wife, but were not ashamed.</span>
