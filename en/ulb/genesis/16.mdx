<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 16:5; Genesis 12:4–5; 2 Samuel 5:13"> It was after Abram had lived ten years in the land of Canaan that Sarai, Abram's wife, gave Hagar, her Egyptian servant, to her husband as a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:23; 2 Samuel 6:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5"> So he had relations with Hagar, and she conceived. When she saw that she had conceived, she looked with contempt on her mistress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 31:53; Exodus 5:21; Psalms 7:8"> Then Sarai said to Abram, “This wrong on me is because of you. I gave my servant woman into your embrace, and when she saw that she had conceived, I was despised in her eyes. Let Yahweh judge between me and you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:5; Proverbs 14:29; 1 Peter 3:7"> But Abram said to Sarai, “See here, your servant woman is in your power, do to her what you think best.” So Sarai dealt harshly with her, and she fled from her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Exodus 15:22; Genesis 21:17"> The angel of Yahweh found her by a spring of water in the wilderness, the spring that is on the way to Shur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:9; Genesis 4:10; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> He said, “Hagar, Sarai's servant, where did you come from and where are you going?” Then she said, “I am fleeing from my mistress Sarai.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5–6; Ecclesiastes 10:4; Titus 2:9"> The angel of Yahweh said to her, “Return to your mistress, and submit yourself to her authority.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:20; Psalms 83:6–7; Genesis 25:12–18"> Then the angel of Yahweh said to her, “I will greatly multiply your descendants, so that they will be too numerous to count.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 2:23–24; Exodus 3:7; Isaiah 7:14"> The angel of Yahweh also said to her,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, you are pregnant and will bear a son,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you will call his name Ishmael,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because Yahweh has heard your affliction.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Genesis 21:20; Job 39:5–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will be a wild donkey of a man.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will be hostile against every man,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and every man will be hostile to him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he will live apart from all his brothers.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 139:1–12; Genesis 32:30; Proverbs 5:21"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then she gave this name to Yahweh who spoke to her, “You are the God who sees me,” for she said, “Do I really continue to see, even after he has seen me?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 24:62; Genesis 25:11; Genesis 21:31"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">Therefore the well was called Beer Lahai Roi; behold, it is between Kadesh and Bered.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 25:12; Genesis 21:9–21; Genesis 17:25–26"> Hagar gave birth to Abram's son, and Abram named his son, whom Hagar bore, Ishmael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Abram was eighty-six years old when Hagar bore Ishmael to Abram.</span>
