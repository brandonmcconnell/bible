<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–6"> Now Yahweh said to Abram, “Go from your country, and from your relatives, and from your father's household, to the land that I will show you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:9; Genesis 18:18; Galatians 3:14"> I will make of you a great nation, and I will bless you, and make your name great, and you will be a blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 27:29"> I will bless those who bless you, but whoever dishonors you I will curse. Through you will all the families of the earth be blessed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Genesis 11:31; Genesis 11:27"> So Abram went, as Yahweh had told him to do, and Lot went with him. Abram was seventy-five years old when he left Haran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 14:14; Genesis 11:31; Hebrews 11:8–9"> Abram took Sarai, his wife, Lot, his brother's son, all their possessions that they had accumulated, and the people that they had acquired in Haran. They left to go into the land of Canaan, and came to the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 11:9; Deuteronomy 11:30; Genesis 35:4"> Abram passed through the land as far as Shechem, to the oak of Moreh. At that time the Canaanites lived in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 18:1; Genesis 13:15"> Yahweh appeared to Abram, and said, “To your descendants I will give this land.” So there Abram built an altar to Yahweh, who had appeared to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:31; Genesis 4:26; Genesis 21:33"> From there he moved to the hill country to the east of Bethel, where he pitched his tent, with Bethel to the west and Ai to the east. There he built an altar to Yahweh and called on the name of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 13:3; Genesis 24:62; Genesis 13:1"> Then Abram continued journeying, going toward the Negev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 43:1; Genesis 26:1–3; 1 Kings 17:1–18"> There was a famine in the land, so Abram went down into Egypt to stay, for the famine was severe in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 29:17; Genesis 12:14"> When he was about to enter into Egypt, he said to Sarai his wife, “See here, I know that you are a beautiful woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 20:11; Genesis 26:7; 1 John 1:8–10"> When the Egyptians see you they will say, 'This is his wife,' and they will kill me, but they will keep you alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 20:12–13; Genesis 20:2"> Say that you are my sister, so that it may be well with me because of you, and so that my life will be spared because of you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 6:2; Matthew 5:28; Genesis 39:7"> It came about that when Abram entered into Egypt, the Egyptians saw that Sarai was very beautiful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 20:2; Genesis 41:1; 2 Kings 18:21"> The princes of Pharaoh saw her, and praised her to Pharaoh, and the woman was taken into Pharaoh's household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 20:14; Genesis 13:2; Genesis 24:35"> Pharaoh treated Abram well for her sake, and gave him sheep, oxen, male donkeys, male servants, female servants, female donkeys, and camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:21; Genesis 20:18; Psalms 105:14–15"> Then Yahweh afflicted Pharaoh and his house with great plagues because of Sarai, Abram's wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 26:9–11; 1 Samuel 14:43"> Pharaoh summoned Abram, and said, “What is this that you have done to me? Why did you not tell me that she was your wife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Why did you say, 'She is my sister,' so that I took her to be my wife? Now therefore, here is your wife. Take her, and go your way.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 105:14–15; Proverbs 21:1; Exodus 18:27"> Then Pharaoh gave orders to his men concerning him, and they sent him away, along with his wife and all that he had.</span>
