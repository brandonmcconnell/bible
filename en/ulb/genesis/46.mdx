<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 12:2; Deuteronomy 26:5; Isaiah 43:1–2"> He said, “I am God, the God of your father. Do not fear to go down to Egypt, for there I will make you a great nation.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 45:21; Genesis 45:19; Genesis 45:27"> Jacob rose up from Beersheba. The sons of Israel transported Jacob their father, their children, and their wives, in the carts that Pharaoh had sent to carry him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 24:4; Isaiah 52:4; Acts 7:15"> They took their livestock and their possessions that they had accumulated in the land of Canaan. They came into Egypt, Jacob and all his descendants with him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 29:34; 1 Chronicles 6:16; Numbers 8:1–26"> and the sons of Levi, Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:21; Genesis 38:10; Genesis 29:35"> The sons of Judah were Er, Onan, Shelah, Perez, and Zerah, (but Er and Onan had died in the land of Canaan). The sons of Perez were Hezron and Hamul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:32; Genesis 35:23; Genesis 30:14–18"> The sons of Issachar were Tola, Puah, Lob, and Shimron;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:18–19; Numbers 26:26–27; Numbers 1:30–31"> The sons of Zebulun were Sered, Elon, and Jahleel</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 29:32–35; Genesis 30:17–21; Genesis 34:1–31"> These were the sons of Leah whom she bore to Jacob in Paddan Aram, along with his daughter Dinah. His sons and his daughters numbered thirty-three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 26:15–17; Genesis 30:11; Numbers 1:24–25"> The sons of Gad were Zephon, Haggi, Shuni, Ezbon, Eri, Arodi, and Areli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 49:20; Genesis 30:13; Numbers 1:13"> The sons of Asher were Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, and Beriah; and Serah was their sister. The sons of Beriah were Heber and Malkiel</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 29:24; Genesis 35:26; Genesis 30:9–13"> These were the sons of Zilpah, whom Laban had given to Leah his daughter. These sons she bore to Jacob—sixteen in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 44:27; Genesis 29:18; Genesis 35:24"> The sons of Jacob's wife Rachel were Joseph and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 41:50–52; Genesis 41:45; Deuteronomy 33:13–17"> In Egypt Manasseh and Ephraim were born to Joseph by Asenath, the daughter of Potiphera priest of On.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:6–12; 1 Chronicles 8:1–7; Numbers 26:38–40"> The sons of Benjamin were Bela, Beker, Ashbel, Gera, Naaman, Ehi, Rosh, Muppim, Huppim, and Ard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> These were the sons of Rachel who were born to Jacob—fourteen in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:38–39; Genesis 49:16–17; Numbers 1:12"> The son of Dan was Hushim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:13; Numbers 1:42–43; Genesis 35:25"> The sons of Naphtali were Jahziel, Guni, Jezer, and Shillem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 29:29; Genesis 35:25; Genesis 30:3–8"> These were the sons born to Jacob by Bilhah, whom Laban gave to Rachel his daughter—seven in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 1:5; Genesis 35:11; Judges 8:30"> All those who went to Egypt with Jacob, who were his descendants, not counting Jacob's sons' wives, were sixty-six in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Acts 7:14; Exodus 1:5"> With the two sons of Joseph who were born to him in Egypt, the members of his family who went to Egypt were seventy in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 45:10; Genesis 44:16–34; Genesis 49:8"> Jacob sent Judah ahead of him to Joseph to show the way before him to Goshen, and they came to the land of Goshen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 15:20; Genesis 45:14–15; Genesis 33:4"> Joseph prepared his chariot and went up to meet Israel his father in Goshen. He saw him, hugged his neck, and wept on his neck a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 2:29–30; Genesis 45:28"> Israel said to Joseph, “Now let me die, since I have seen your face, that you are still alive.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 47:1–3; Hebrews 2:11; Acts 18:3"> Joseph said to his brothers and to his father's house, “I will go up and tell Pharaoh, saying, 'My brothers and my father's house, who were in the land of Canaan, have come to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 47:3; 1 Kings 9:27; 1 Kings 18:5–6"> The men are shepherds, for they have been keepers of livestock. They have brought their flocks, their herds, and all that they have.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 46:32; Genesis 47:2–4; Jonah 1:8"> It will come about, when Pharaoh calls you and asks, 'What is your occupation?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 8:26; Genesis 43:32; Genesis 46:32"> that you should say, 'Your servants have been keepers of livestock from our youth until now, both we, and our forefathers.' Do this so that you may live in the land of Goshen, for every shepherd is an abomination to the Egyptians.”</span>
