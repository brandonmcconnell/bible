<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Ecclesiastes 1:17; Proverbs 31:4–5"> I explored in my heart how to gratify my desires with wine. I let my mind guide me with wisdom although I was still holding on to folly. I wanted to find out what is good for people to do under heaven during the days of their lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 7:1–12; 2 Chronicles 8:11; 2 Chronicles 26:10"> I accomplished great things. I built houses for myself and planted vineyards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 17:27–29; Song of Songs 4:12–1; Jeremiah 39:4"> I built for myself gardens and parks; I planted all kinds of fruit trees in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:14; Psalms 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8"> I created pools of water to water a forest where trees were grown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 3:4; Genesis 15:3; 1 Kings 4:23"> I purchased male slaves and female slaves; I had slaves born in my palace. I also had large herds and flocks of livestock, much more than any king who ruled before me in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 10:10; 1 Kings 9:28; 2 Samuel 19:35"> I also accumulated for myself silver and gold, the treasures of kings and provinces. I got male and female singers for myself—the delights of the children of humanity—and many concubines.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Modern versions interpret the last part of this verse in different ways: everything that pleases people , concubines and everything that pleases men , etc.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:25; Ecclesiastes 1:16; 1 Kings 10:23"> So I became greater and wealthier than all who were before me in Jerusalem, and my wisdom remained with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:9; Ecclesiastes 5:18; Ecclesiastes 3:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whatever my eyes desired,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I did not withhold from them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I did not withhold my heart from any pleasure,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because my heart rejoiced in all my labor</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and pleasure was my reward for all my work.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; 1 John 2:16–17; Ecclesiastes 1:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I looked on all the deeds that my hands had accomplished,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and on the work that I had done,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but again, everything was vapor and an attempt to shepherd the wind.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">There was no profit under the sun in it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:17; Ecclesiastes 7:25; Ecclesiastes 1:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I turned to consider wisdom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and also madness and folly.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For what can the next king do who comes after the king,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">which has not already been done?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Ecclesiastes 7:11–12; Matthew 6:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I began to understand</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that wisdom has advantages over folly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">just as light is better than darkness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 49:10; Proverbs 17:24; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wise man uses his eyes in his head to see where he is going,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the fool walks in darkness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">although I know the same event happens to all of them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:8; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Ecclesiastes 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I said in my heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“What happens to the fool,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will also happen to me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So what difference does it make if I am very wise?”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I concluded in my heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“This too is only vapor.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:11; Ecclesiastes 9:5; Psalms 88:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the wise man, like the fool, is not remembered for very long.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">In the days to come everything will have been long forgotten.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The wise man dies just like the fool dies.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:11; Jonah 4:8; Ecclesiastes 4:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I detested life because all the work done under the sun was evil to me. This was because everything is vapor and an attempt to shepherd the wind.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:6; Psalms 49:10; Luke 16:27–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">I hated all my accomplishments for which I had worked under the sun because I must leave them behind to the man who comes after me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 3:17; Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 9:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">For who knows whether he will be a wise man or a fool? Yet he will be master over everything under the sun that my work and wisdom have built. This also is vapor.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 43:14; Job 17:11–15; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore my heart began to despair over all the work under the sun that I did.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:17–18; 2 Chronicles 31:20–21; 2 Chronicles 34:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">For there might be someone who works with wisdom, with knowledge, and skill, but he will leave everything he has to a man who has not made any of it. This also is vapor and a great tragedy.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 127:2; Ecclesiastes 1:3; Philippians 4:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For what profit does the person gain who works so hard and tries in his heart to complete his labors under the sun?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 14:1; Job 5:7; Psalms 6:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Every day his work is painful and stressful, so at night his soul does not find rest. This also is vapor.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 8:15; 1 Timothy 6:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is nothing better for anyone than to simply eat and drink and be satisfied with what is good in his work. I saw that this truth comes from God's hand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:1–12; 1 Kings 4:21–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">For who can eat or who can have any kind of pleasure apart from God?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 13:22; Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 28:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">For to anyone who pleases him, God gives wisdom and knowledge and joy. However, to the sinner he gives the work of gathering and storing up so that he may give it away to someone who pleases God. This also amounts to vapor and an attempt to shepherd the wind.</span></span>
