<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 6:1–2; 1 Samuel 2:6; Jeremiah 45:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">a time to kill and a time to heal,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a time to tear down and a time to build up.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalms 30:5; James 4:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is a time to weep and a time to laugh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a time to mourn and a time to dance,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 3:25; Joshua 10:27; Joshua 4:3–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">a time to throw away stones and a time to gather stones,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a time to embrace other people, and a time to refrain from embracing.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; Luke 9:24–25; Mark 8:35–37"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is a time to look for things and a time to stop looking,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a time to keep things and a time to throw away things,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Micah 7:5; Lamentations 3:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">a time to tear clothing and a time to repair clothing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a time to keep silent and a time to speak.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25; Ephesians 5:28–29; Luke 14:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is a time to love and a time to hate,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a time for war and a time for peace.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:3; Ecclesiastes 5:16; Proverbs 14:23"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">What profit does the worker gain in his labor?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:13–14; Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have seen the work that God has given to human beings to complete.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Romans 1:19–20; Job 11:7"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">God has made everything suitable for its own time. <span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Ecclesiastes 2:24"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">and that everyone should eat and drink, and should understand how to enjoy the good that comes from all his work. This is a gift from God.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; Psalms 49:12; Hebrews 9:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">I said in my heart, “God tests human beings to show them that they are like animals.”</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Job 34:15; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Everything is going to the same place. Everything comes from the dust, and everything returns to the dust.</span></span>
