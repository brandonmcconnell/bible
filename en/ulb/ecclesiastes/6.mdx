<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13"> There is an evil that I have seen under the sun, and it weighs heavy on men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:19; Psalms 17:14; Psalms 73:7"> God might give riches, wealth, and honor to a man so that he lacks nothing that he desires for himself, but then God gives him no ability to enjoy it. Instead, someone else uses his things. This is vapor, an evil affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 3:16; Jeremiah 22:19; Ecclesiastes 4:3"> If a man fathers a hundred children and lives many years, so that the days of his years are many, but if his heart is not satisfied with good and he is not buried, then I say that a baby that is born dead is better off than he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 109:13"> Even such a baby is born in futility and passes away in darkness, and its name remains hidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 58:8; Job 14:1; Job 3:10–13"> Although this child does not see the sun or know anything, it has rest even though that man did not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 3:20; Job 30:23"> Even if a man should live for two thousand years but does not learn to enjoy good things, he goes to the same place as everyone else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 6:27; Proverbs 16:26; 1 Timothy 6:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">All a man's work is for his mouth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">yet his appetite is not satisfied.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 19:1; Ecclesiastes 2:14–16; Psalms 101:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Indeed, what advantage has the wise person over the fool?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">What advantage does the poor man have</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">even if he knows how to act in front of other people?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; Job 31:7; Ecclesiastes 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is better to be satisfied with what the eyes see</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">than to desire what a wandering appetite craves,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">which is also vapor and an attempt to shepherd the wind.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:32; Isaiah 45:9–10; Job 40:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whatever has existed has already been given its name, and what mankind is like has already been known. So it has become useless to dispute with the one who is the mighty judge of all.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:16; Ecclesiastes 5:7; Ecclesiastes 2:3–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The more words that are spoken, the more futility increases,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so what advantage is that to a man?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 4:14; Job 14:2; Ecclesiastes 3:22"> <span className="pl pl-2">For who knows what is good for man in his life during his futile, numbered days through which he passes like a shadow? Who can tell a man what will come under the sun after he passes?</span></span>
