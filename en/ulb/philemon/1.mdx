<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:1; Ephesians 3:1; Philemon 1:9"> Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and the brother Timothy to Philemon, our dear friend and fellow worker,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Colossians 4:17; Romans 16:5"> and to Apphia our sister, and to Archippus our fellow soldier, and to the church that meets in your home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Ephesians 1:2; 2 Corinthians 13:14"> May grace be to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:8; Ephesians 1:16; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> I always thank my God. I mention you in my prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 1:4; Ephesians 1:15; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> I have heard of the love and the faith that you have in the Lord Jesus and for all the believers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 1:9–11; 2 Peter 1:8; Colossians 1:9"> I pray that the fellowship of your faith may be effective for the knowledge of everything good that is among us in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:9; 2 Corinthians 7:4"> For I have had much joy and comfort because of your love, because the hearts of the saints have been refreshed by you, brother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Corinthians 10:8; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> Therefore, although I have all the boldness in Christ to command you to do what you should do,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; Ephesians 4:1; Romans 12:1"> yet because of love, I appeal to you instead—I, Paul, an old man, and now a prisoner for Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 4:9; 1 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Timothy 1:2"> I am asking you concerning my child Onesimus, whom I have fathered in my chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:11; Job 30:1–2; 1 Peter 2:10"> For he once was useless to you, but now he is useful both to you and to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; Jeremiah 31:20; Mark 11:25"> I have sent him back to you, he who is my very heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Ephesians 3:1; 1 Corinthians 16:17"> I wish I could have kept him with me, so he could serve me for you, while I am in chains for the sake of the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Chronicles 29:17; 1 Peter 5:2–3"> But I did not want to do anything without your consent. I did not want your good deed to be from necessity but from good will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Genesis 45:5–8; Isaiah 20:6"> Perhaps for this he was separated from you for a time, so that you might have him back forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Matthew 23:8; 1 Corinthians 7:22"> No longer would he be a slave, but better than a slave, a beloved brother. He is beloved especially to me, and much more so to you, in both the flesh and in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:23; Matthew 10:40; Philemon 1:12"> So if you have me as a partner, receive him as me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–7"> If he has wronged you or owes you anything, charge that to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:11; Galatians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 4:15"> I, Paul, write this with my own hand. I myself will pay it back—not to mention that you owe me your own self!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="3 John 1:4; Philemon 1:7; Philemon 1:12"> Yes, brother, do me a favor in the Lord; refresh my heart in Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:4; 2 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 7:16"> Confident about your obedience, I am writing to you. I know that you will do even more than I ask.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:11; Philippians 2:24; 2 John 1:12"> At the same time, prepare a guest room for me, for I hope that through your prayers I will be given back to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:7; Romans 16:7; Colossians 4:12"> Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, greets you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10–11; Colossians 4:14; Colossians 4:10"> So do Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke, my fellow workers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:22; Romans 16:23; Romans 16:20"> May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
