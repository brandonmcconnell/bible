<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:13; Jonah 1:2; Zechariah 9:1"> The declaration about Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum, the Elkoshite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 8:2; Exodus 20:5; Psalms 94:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh is a jealous God and avenges; Yahweh avenges and is full of wrath;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh takes vengeance on his adversaries, and he continues his anger for his enemies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 147:5; Exodus 34:6–7; Nehemiah 9:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh is slow to anger and great in power; he will not allow the wicked to go unpunished.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh makes his way in the whirlwind and the storm, and the clouds are the dust of his feet.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 33:9; Psalms 106:9; Psalms 114:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">He rebukes the sea and makes it dry; he dries up all the rivers.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Bashan is weak, and Carmel also; the flowers of Lebanon are weak.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 1:4; 2 Samuel 22:8; Jeremiah 4:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The mountains shake in his presence, and the hills melt;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the earth collapses in his presence, indeed, the world and all people who live in it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:10; Malachi 3:2; Deuteronomy 32:22–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who can stand before his wrath? Who can resist the fierceness of his anger?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His wrath is poured out like fire, and the rocks are broken apart by him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:27; 2 Timothy 2:19; Lamentations 3:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh is good, a stronghold in the day of trouble; and he is faithful to those who take refuge in him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 28:17; Isaiah 8:22; Ezekiel 13:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">But he will make a full end to his enemies with an overwhelming flood; he will pursue them into darkness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 21:11; Proverbs 21:30; Psalms 33:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">What are you people plotting against Yahweh?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will make a full end to it; trouble will not rise up a second time.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 7:4; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 9:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they will become tangled up like thornbushes; they will be saturated in their own drink;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they will be completely devoured by fire like dry stubble.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; 2 Chronicles 13:7; 2 Kings 18:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Someone arose among you, Nineveh, who planned evil against Yahweh, someone who promoted wickedness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 30:19; Joel 2:19; Isaiah 17:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is what Yahweh says,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Even if they are at their full strength and full numbers, they will nevertheless be sheared;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their people will be no more. But you, Judah: Though I have afflicted you, I will afflict you no more.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 10:27; Isaiah 9:4; Jeremiah 2:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now will I break that people's yoke from off you; I will break your chains.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 109:13; Isaiah 46:1–2; Micah 5:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh has given a command about you, Nineveh:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“There will be no more descendants bearing your name.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will cut off the carved figures and the cast metal figures from the houses of your gods.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will dig your graves, for you are contemptible.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Romans 10:15; Isaiah 40:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look, on the mountains there are the feet of someone who is bringing good news, who is announcing peace!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Celebrate your festivals, Judah, and keep your vows,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the wicked one will invade you no more; he is completely cut off.</span></span>
