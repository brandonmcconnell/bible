<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Ephesians 6:10; 2 Timothy 1:7"> You therefore, my child, be strengthened in the grace that is in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13–14; 1 Timothy 4:6; 1 Timothy 6:12"> The things you heard from me among many witnesses, entrust them to faithful people who will be able to teach others also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2–3; James 1:12; 2 Timothy 4:5"> Suffer hardship with me, as a good soldier of Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 8:14; 2 Peter 2:20"> No soldier serves while entangled in the affairs of this life, so that he may please his superior officer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Revelation 3:11; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> Also, if someone competes as an athlete, he is not crowned unless he competes by the rules.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; 1 Corinthians 9:7–11; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9"> It is necessary that the hardworking farmer receive his share of the crops first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:5; 1 John 5:20; Luke 24:45"> Think about what I am saying, for the Lord will give you understanding in everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Romans 2:16; 1 Corinthians 15:11–20"> Remember Jesus Christ, from David's seed, who was raised from the dead. This is according to my gospel message,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Acts 28:31; 2 Timothy 1:8"> for which I am suffering to the point of being bound with chains as a criminal. But the word of God is not bound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; Colossians 1:24; 2 Timothy 2:3"> Therefore I endure all things for those who are chosen, so that they also may obtain the salvation that is in Christ Jesus, with eternal glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:8; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:5"> This is a trustworthy saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“If we have died with him, we will also live with him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Matthew 10:33; Revelation 20:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">If we endure, we will also reign with him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If we deny him, he also will deny us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 3:3; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">if we are unfaithful, he remains faithful,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for he cannot deny himself.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 3:9–11; 2 Timothy 2:23; 2 Timothy 4:1"> Keep reminding them of these things. Warn them before God against quarreling about words; it is of no value, and only ruins those who listen.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some versions read, Warn them before the Lord .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; 2 Peter 1:10; 1 Timothy 4:6"> Do your best to present yourself to God as one approved, a worker who has no reason to be ashamed, who accurately teaches the word of truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:14"> Avoid profane talk, which leads to more and more godlessness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; Nahum 3:15; James 5:3"> Their talk will spread like cancer. Among them are Hymenaeus and Philetus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:12; 1 Timothy 1:19; Hebrews 3:10"> who have gone astray from the truth. They say that the resurrection has already happened, and they destroy the faith of some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 10:14; 1 Corinthians 8:3; Nahum 1:7"> However, the firm foundation of God stands. It has this inscription: “The Lord knows those who are his” and “Everyone who names the name of the Lord must depart from unrighteousness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:21–23; 1 Peter 2:5; 1 Timothy 3:15"> In a wealthy home, there are not only containers of gold and silver. There are also containers of wood and clay. Some of these are for honorable use, and some for dishonorable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:17; Ephesians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> If someone cleans himself from dishonorable use, he is an honorable container. He is set apart, useful to the Master, and prepared for every good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; 1 Peter 3:11; 1 Corinthians 6:18"> Flee youthful lusts. Pursue righteousness, faith, love, and peace with those who call on the Lord out of a clean heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:14; Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 4:7"> But refuse foolish and ignorant questions. You know that they give birth to arguments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Titus 3:2; James 1:19–20; 1 Timothy 3:2–3"> The Lord's servant must not quarrel. Instead he must be gentle toward all, able to teach, and patient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 11:18; Galatians 6:1; 1 Timothy 2:4"> He must in meekness educate those who oppose him. God may perhaps give them repentance for the knowledge of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; Isaiah 49:25–26; Psalms 124:7"> They may become sober again and leave the devil's trap, after they have been captured by him for his will.</span>
