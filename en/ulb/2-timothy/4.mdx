<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 10:42; 2 Timothy 4:8; 1 Timothy 5:21"> I give this solemn command before God and Christ Jesus, who will judge the living and the dead, and because of his appearing and his kingdom:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Timothy 4:15–16; Titus 2:15"> Preach the word; be ready when it is convenient and when it is not. Reprove, rebuke, exhort, with all patience and teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; 2 Timothy 3:1–6; 1 Timothy 4:1–3"> For the time will come when people will not endure sound teaching. Instead, they will heap up for themselves teachers according to their own desires, who say what their itching ears want to hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 7:11; 1 Timothy 1:4; Acts 7:57"> They will turn their hearing away from the truth, and they will turn aside to myths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Peter 1:13; 2 Timothy 2:3"> But you, be sober-minded in all things. Suffer hardship; do the work of an evangelist; fulfill your service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 1:23; Philippians 2:17; 2 Peter 1:14–15"> For I am already being poured out. The time of my departure has come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:12; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Hebrews 12:1–2"> I have competed in the good contest; I have finished the race; I have kept the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 1:12; Revelation 2:10; 1 Peter 5:4"> The crown of righteousness has been reserved for me, which the Lord, the righteous judge, will give to me on that day, and not to me only, but also to all those who have loved his appearing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:4; 2 Timothy 4:21"> Do your best to come to me quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; 2 Timothy 1:15; Colossians 4:14–15"> For Demas has left me. He loves this present world and has gone to Thessalonica. Crescens went to Galatia, and Titus went to Dalmatia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Philemon 1:24; Acts 12:25"> Only Luke is with me. Get Mark and bring him with you because he is useful to me in the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 6:21; Acts 20:4; Titus 3:12"> Tychicus I sent to Ephesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:11; 2 Corinthians 11:27; Acts 16:11"> The cloak that I left at Troas with Carpus, bring it when you come, and the books, especially the parchments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; 2 Thessalonians 1:6; Psalms 28:4"> Alexander the coppersmith displayed many evil deeds against me. The Lord will repay him according to his deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:8; Philippians 3:2; Matthew 10:16–17"> You also should guard yourself against him, because he greatly opposed our words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 7:60; John 16:32; Mark 14:50"> At my first defense, no one stood with me. Instead, everyone left me. May it not be counted against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Philippians 1:12–14; 1 Samuel 17:37"> But the Lord stood by me and strengthened me so that, through me, the message might be fully proclaimed, and that all the Gentiles might hear it; and that I was rescued out of the lion's mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 121:7; 2 Thessalonians 3:3; Jude 1:24–25"> The Lord will rescue me from every evil deed and will save me for his heavenly kingdom. To him be the glory forever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 18:2; 1 Corinthians 16:19; Romans 16:3–4"> Greet Priscilla, Aquila, and the house of Onesiphorus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Acts 19:22; Philippians 2:26–27"> Erastus remained at Corinth, but Trophimus I left sick at Miletus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:9; Philippians 4:22; 3 John 1:14"> Do your best to come before winter. Eubulus greets you, also Pudens, Linus, Claudia, and all the brothers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philemon 1:25; Galatians 6:18; Colossians 4:18"> May the Lord be with your spirit. May grace be with you.</span>
</div>
</div>
