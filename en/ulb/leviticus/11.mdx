<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 15:11; Leviticus 11:11; Hebrews 9:10"> “Speak to the people of Israel, saying, 'These are the living things which you may eat among all the animals that are on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:10; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Deuteronomy 6:6–7"> You may eat any animal that has a split hoof and that also chews the cud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> However, some animals either chew the cud or have a split hoof, and you must not eat them, animals such as the camel, because it chews the cud but does not have a split hoof. So the camel is unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 104:18; Proverbs 30:26; Matthew 7:26"> Also the rock badger, because it chews the cud but does not have a split hoof, it is also unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:7"> The rabbit, because it chews the cud, but does not have a split hoof, is unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 66:17; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> The pig, although it has a split hoof, does not chew the cud, is unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Colossians 2:16; Hebrews 9:10"> You must not eat any of their meat, nor touch their carcasses. They are unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; James 2:18; Acts 20:21"> The animals living in the water that you may eat are all those that have fins and scales, whether in the ocean or in the rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 29:27; Leviticus 7:18; Psalms 139:21–22"> But all living creatures that do not have fins and scales in the ocean or rivers, including all that move in the water and all the living creatures that are in the water— they must be detested by you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Since they must be detested, you must not eat of their meat; also, their carcasses must be detested.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Whatever has no fins or scales in the water must be detested by you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 39:27–30; Jeremiah 4:22; Titus 3:3"> The birds you must detest and that you must not eat are these: the eagle, the vulture,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> the kite, any kind of falcon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 12:24; 1 Kings 17:6; Proverbs 30:17"> every kind of raven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 34:11–15; Ephesians 5:7–11; Ephesians 2:2–3"> the horned owl and the screech owl, the seagull, and any kind of hawk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> You must also detest the little owl and the great owl, the cormorant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> the white owl and the barn owl, the osprey,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> the stork, any kind of heron, the hoopoe, and also the bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Philippians 3:18–19; Leviticus 11:23"> All winged insects that walk on four legs are detestable to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Yet you may eat any of the flying insects that also walk on four legs if they have jointed legs for hopping on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; Mark 1:6; Exodus 10:4–5"> You may also eat any kind of locust, katydid, cricket, or grasshopper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> But all the flying insects that have four feet must be detested by you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Ephesians 2:1–3; 2 Corinthians 6:17"> You will become unclean until evening by these animals if you touch a carcass of one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Leviticus 14:8; Numbers 31:24"> Whoever picks up one of their carcasses must wash his clothes and remain unclean until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Every animal which has a split hoof that is not completely divided or which does not chew the cud is unclean to you. Everyone who touches them will be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 11:23; Leviticus 11:20"> Whatever walks on its paws among all the animals that walk on all four legs, they are unclean to you. Whoever touches such a carcass will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25"> Whoever picks up such a carcass must wash his clothes and be unclean until the evening. These animals will be unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 11:41–42; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Psalms 10:3"> Of the animals that creep on the ground, these are the animals that will be unclean to you: the weasel, the rat, every kind of large lizard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> the gecko, the monitor lizard, the lizard, the skink, and the chameleon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25; Leviticus 11:8"> Of all the animals that creep, these are the animals which will be unclean to you. Whoever touches them when they are dead will be unclean until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Titus 3:5; Titus 2:14"> If any of them dies and falls on anything, that thing will be unclean, whether it is made of wood, cloth, leather, or sackcloth. Whatever it is and whatever it is used for, it must be put into water; it will be unclean until evening. Then it will be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 14:45"> For every clay pot into or onto which any unclean animal falls, whatever is in the pot will become unclean, and you must destroy that pot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Proverbs 21:4; Proverbs 21:27"> Any food that could be eaten but has water on it from such a pot is unclean. Any liquid that is for drinking from such a pot is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 11:33"> Anything that one of their carcasses falls on becomes unclean; if it is an oven or small stove, it must be broken to pieces. They are unclean and they must remain unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; John 4:14"> A spring or cistern for collecting water remains clean; but anyone who touches their carcass is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:37; 1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:18"> If any part of their carcass falls upon any seeds for planting, those seeds will still be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> But if water is put on the seeds, and if any part of their carcass falls on them, then they will be unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Leviticus 11:40; Leviticus 11:24; Leviticus 15:7"> If any animal that you may eat dies, then he who touches the carcass will be unclean until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Leviticus 22:8; Ezekiel 44:31; Deuteronomy 14:21"> Whoever eats any of that carcass must wash his clothes and be unclean until evening. Anyone who picks up such a carcass will wash his clothes and be unclean until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 11:29; Leviticus 11:20; Leviticus 11:23"> Every animal that creeps on the ground is to be detested; it must not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Titus 1:12; Isaiah 65:25; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> Whatever crawls on its belly, and whatever walks on all four legs, or whatever has many feet—all the animals that creep on the ground, these you must not eat, for they are to be detested.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 20:25; Leviticus 11:41–42"> You must not make yourselves unclean with any living creatures that creep; you must not make yourselves unclean with them, that you should be made impure by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Leviticus 19:2"> For I am Yahweh your God. You are to keep yourselves holy, therefore, and be holy, because I am holy. You must not defile yourselves with any kind of animal that moves about on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Exodus 20:2; Exodus 6:7"> For I am Yahweh, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt, to be your God. You must therefore be holy, for I am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Leviticus 14:54; Leviticus 15:32; Ezekiel 43:12"> This is the law regarding the animals, the birds, every living creature that moves in the waters, and of every creature that creeps on the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Ezekiel 44:23; Romans 14:13–23"> for which a distinction is to be made between the unclean and the clean, and between the living things that may be eaten and the living things that may not be eaten.'”</span>
</div>
