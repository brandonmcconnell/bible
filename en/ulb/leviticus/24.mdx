<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:6; Ephesians 5:8–14; Exodus 27:20–21"> “Command the people of Israel to bring you pure oil beaten from olives to be used in the lamp, that the light may burn continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Outside the curtain before the covenant decrees in the tent of meeting, Aaron must continually, from evening to morning, keep the lamp lit before Yahweh. This will be a permanent statute throughout your people's generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 31:8; Exodus 37:17–24; Exodus 39:37"> The high priest must always keep the lamps lit before Yahweh, the lamps on the lampstand of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; 1 Kings 18:31; Exodus 40:23"> You must take fine flour and bake twelve loaves with it. There must be two-tenths of an ephah in each loaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 7:48; Exodus 25:23–24; 2 Chronicles 4:19"> Then you must set them in two rows, six in a row, on the table of pure gold before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Revelation 8:3–4; Genesis 9:16"> You must put pure incense along each row of loaves as a representative offering. This incense will be burnt for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4; Numbers 4:7; 1 Chronicles 9:32"> Every Sabbath day the high priest must regularly set out the bread before Yahweh on behalf of the people of Israel, as a sign of an everlasting covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 8:31; Luke 6:4; Mark 2:26"> This offering will be for Aaron and his sons, and they are to eat it in a place that is holy, for it is a portion from the offerings to Yahweh made by fire.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 11:4; Exodus 12:38"> Now it happened that the son of an Israelite woman, whose father was an Egyptian, went among the people of Israel. This son of the Israelite woman fought against an Israelite man in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Leviticus 24:15–16; Exodus 3:15"> The son of the Israelite woman blasphemed the name of Yahweh and cursed God, so the people brought him to Moses. His mother's name was Shelomith, the daughter of Dibri, from the tribe of Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 18:15–16; Numbers 15:34; Numbers 27:5"> They held him in custody until Yahweh himself should declare his will to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:7; Leviticus 20:27; Deuteronomy 21:21"> “Take the man who has cursed God outside the camp. All who heard him must lay their hands on his head, and then the entire assembly must stone him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 9:13; Exodus 22:28; Leviticus 5:1"> You must explain to the people of Israel and say, 'Whoever curses his God must carry his own guilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 12:31; James 2:7; John 10:33–36"> He who blasphemes the name of Yahweh must surely be put to death. All the assembly must certainly stone him, whether he is a foreigner or a native-born Israelite. If anyone blasphemes the name of Yahweh, he must be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:5–6; Deuteronomy 19:11–12; Deuteronomy 27:24"> If anyone strikes down another human being, he must certainly be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 24:21; Exodus 21:34–36"> If anyone strikes down someone's animal, he must pay it back, life for life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:38; Matthew 7:2; Deuteronomy 19:21"> If anyone injures his neighbor, it must be done to him as he did to his neighbor:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Matthew 5:38; Exodus 21:23–25"> fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth. As he has caused an injury to a person, so must it also be done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17–18; Exodus 21:33"> Anyone who kills an animal must pay it back, and anyone who kills a person must be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 12:49; Numbers 15:15–16; Numbers 9:14"> You must have the same law for both the foreigner and the native-born Israelite, for I am Yahweh your God.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28–29; Hebrews 2:2–3; Numbers 15:35–36"> So Moses spoke to the people of Israel, and the people brought the man outside the camp, the one who had cursed Yahweh. They stoned him with stones. The people of Israel carried out the command of Yahweh to Moses.</span>
