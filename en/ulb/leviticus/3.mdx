<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 6:14; Leviticus 1:3; Romans 5:1–2"> If someone offers a sacrifice which is a fellowship offering of an animal from the herd, whether male or female, he must offer an animal without blemish before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 29:10; Leviticus 1:4–5; Acts 2:36–38"> He will lay his hand on the head of his offering and kill it at the door of the tent of meeting. Then Aaron's sons the priests will sprinkle its blood on the sides of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:13; Leviticus 4:8–9; Exodus 29:22"> The man will offer the sacrifice of a fellowship offering by fire to Yahweh. The fat that covers or is connected to the inner parts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 3:10; Leviticus 8:25; Exodus 29:22"> and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them by the loins, and the lobe of the liver, with the kidneys—he will remove all of this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 6:12; Exodus 29:13; 1 Peter 2:5"> Aaron's sons will burn that on the altar with the burnt offering, which is on the wood that is on the fire. This will produce a sweet aroma for Yahweh; it will be an offering made to him by fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:11–12; Galatians 3:28; Leviticus 1:2"> If the man's sacrifice of a fellowship offering to Yahweh is from the flock; male or female, he must offer a sacrifice without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 8:62; Ephesians 5:12; Leviticus 17:8–9"> If he offers a lamb for his sacrifice, then he must offer it before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 1:5; Ephesians 3:12; Leviticus 3:13"> He will lay his hand on the head of his sacrifice and kill it before the tent of meeting. Then Aaron's sons will sprinkle its blood on the sides of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 29:22; Leviticus 9:19; Leviticus 8:25"> The man will offer the sacrifice of fellowship offerings as an offering made by fire to Yahweh. The fat, the entire fat tail cut away close to the backbone, and the fat that covers the inner parts and all the fat that is near the inner parts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 3:4"> and the two kidneys and the fat that is with them, which is by the loins, and the lobe of the liver, with the kidneys—he will remove all of this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 21:17; Leviticus 21:6; Leviticus 3:16"> Then the priest will burn it all on the altar as a burnt offering of food to Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 9:15; Isaiah 53:2; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> If the man's offering is a goat, then he will offer it before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 3:8; Romans 5:15–21; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> He must lay his hand on the head of the goat and kill it before the tent of meeting. Then the sons of Aaron will sprinkle its blood on the sides of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 26:38; Matthew 22:37; Leviticus 3:9–11"> The man will offer his sacrifice made by fire to Yahweh. He will remove the fat that covers the inner parts, and all the fat near the inner parts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He will also remove the two kidneys and the fat that is with them, which is by the loins, and the lobe of the liver with the kidneys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 7:23–25; Matthew 22:37; Isaiah 53:10"> The priest will burn all that on the altar as a burnt offering of food, to produce a sweet aroma. All the fat belongs to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:4; Matthew 26:28; Deuteronomy 15:23"> It will be a permanent statute throughout your people's generations in every place you make your home, that you must not eat fat or blood.'”</span>
