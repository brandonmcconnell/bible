<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:12; 1 Kings 20:34; Ezekiel 17:13"> Then Nahash the Ammonite went and laid seige to Jabesh Gilead. All the men of Jabesh said to Nahash, “Make a covenant with us, and we will serve you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:26; Genesis 34:14; Numbers 16:14"> Nahash the Ammonite replied, “On this condition will I make a treaty with you, that I gouge out all of your right eyes, and in this way bring disgrace on all Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then the elders of Jabesh replied to him, “Leave us alone for seven days, so that we may send messengers to all the territory of Israel. Then, if there is no one to save us, we will surrender to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 2:4; 1 Samuel 10:26; 1 Samuel 15:34"> The messengers came to Gibeah, where Saul lived, and told the people what had happened. All the people wept loudly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 19:19; Judges 18:23; Genesis 21:17"> Now Saul was following the oxen out of the field. Saul said, “What is wrong with the people that they are weeping?” They told Saul what the men of Jabesh had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:10; 1 Samuel 16:13; Judges 6:34"> When Saul heard what they said, the Spirit of God rushed upon him, and he was very angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:29; Judges 20:1; Judges 21:5–11"> He took a yoke of oxen, cut them into pieces, and sent them throughout all the territory of Israel with the messengers. He said, “Whoever does not come out after Saul and after Samuel, this is what will be done to his oxen.” Then the terror of Yahweh fell on the people, and they came out together as one man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 1:4–5; 2 Samuel 24:9; 2 Chronicles 17:12–19"> When he mustered them at Bezek, the people of Israel were three hundred thousand, and the men of Judah thirty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 18:17"> They said to the messengers that came, “You will tell the men of Jabesh Gilead, 'Tomorrow, by the time the sun is hot, you will be rescued.'” So the messengers went and told the men of Jabesh, and they were glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:2–3"> Then the men of Jabesh said to Nahash, “Tomorrow we will surrender to you, and you can do to us whatever seems good to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 7:16; James 2:13; Exodus 14:24"> The next day Saul put the people in three groups. They came into the middle of the camp during the morning watch, and they attacked and defeated the Ammonites until the heat of the day. Those who survived were scattered, so that no two of them were left together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:27; Luke 19:27; Psalms 21:8"> Then the people said to Samuel, “Who was it who said, 'Will Saul reign over us?' Bring the men, so we can put them to death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:22; 1 Samuel 19:5; Exodus 14:13"> But Saul said, “No one must be put to death this day, because today Yahweh has rescued Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:8; 1 Samuel 7:16; 1 Chronicles 12:38–39"> Then Samuel said to the people, “Come, let us go to Gilgal and renew the monarchy there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:8; 1 Samuel 10:17; 1 Chronicles 29:21–24"> So all the people went to Gilgal and made Saul king before Yahweh in Gilgal. There they sacrificed peace offerings before Yahweh, and Saul and all the men of Israel rejoiced greatly.</span>
