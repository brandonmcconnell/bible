<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:5; Psalms 11:1; 1 Samuel 23:15"> David's men said to him, “See, we are afraid here in Judah. How much more then if we go to Keilah against the armies of the Philistines?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 7:7; Joshua 8:7; 2 Kings 3:18"> Then David prayed to Yahweh for help yet again. Yahweh answered him, “Arise, go down to Keilah. For I will give you victory over the Philistines.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> David and his men went to Keilah and fought with the Philistines. He led away their cattle and struck them with a great slaughter. So David saved the inhabitants of Keilah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:20; 1 Samuel 14:3; 1 Samuel 14:18"> When Abiathar son of Ahimelech had fled to David at Keilah, he came down with an ephod in his hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:4–6; Job 20:5; 1 Samuel 26:8–9"> Saul was told that David had gone to Keilah. Saul said, “God has given him into my hand. For he is shut in because he has entered a city that has gates and bars.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Saul summoned all his forces for battle, to go down to Keilah, to besiege David and his men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:7; 1 Samuel 23:6; Numbers 27:21"> David knew that Saul was plotting harm against him. He said to Abiathar the priest, “Bring the ephod here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 3:15–16; 1 Samuel 23:8; Genesis 18:24"> Then David said, “Yahweh, the God of Israel, your servant has indeed heard that Saul seeks to come to Keilah, to destroy the city for my sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:3; Psalms 50:15; Matthew 7:7–8"> Will the men of Keilah surrender me into his hand? Will Saul come down, as your servant has heard? Yahweh, the God of Israel, I beg you, please tell your servant.” Yahweh said, “He will come down.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:20; Psalms 31:8; Psalms 62:1"> Then David said, “Will the men of Keilah surrender me and my men into the hand of Saul?” Yahweh said, “They will surrender you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:2; 1 Samuel 25:13; 1 Samuel 30:9–10"> Then David and his men, who were about six hundred, got up and went away from Keilah, and they went from place to place. It was told Saul that David had escaped from Keilah, and he stopped the pursuit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 54:3–4; Psalms 32:7; Joshua 15:24"> David stayed in the strongholds in the wilderness, in the hill country in the wilderness of Ziph. Saul looked for him every day, but God did not give him into his hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> David saw that Saul had come out to seek his life; now David was in the wilderness of Ziph at Horesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3–4; Hebrews 12:12–13; Job 4:3–4"> Then Jonathan, Saul's son, got up and went to David at Horesh, and strengthened his hand in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:31; 1 Samuel 24:20; Acts 28:16"> He said to him, “Do not be afraid. For the hand of Saul my father will not find you. You will be king over Israel, and I will be next to you. Saul my father also knows this.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:7; 1 Samuel 20:42; 1 Samuel 18:3"> They made a covenant before Yahweh. David remained at Horesh, and Jonathan went home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:1; 1 Samuel 26:3; Psalms 54:1–7"> Then Ziphites came to Saul at Gibeah and said, “Is not David hiding among us in the strongholds at Horesh, on the hill of Hakilah, which is south of Jeshimon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:12; Proverbs 29:26; Proverbs 11:23"> Now come down, king! According to your desire, come down! Our part will be to surrender him into the king's hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:8; Isaiah 66:5; Micah 3:11"> Saul said, “May you be blessed by Yahweh. For you have had compassion on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 5:13"> Go, make even more sure. Learn and find out where his hiding place is and who has seen him there. It is told to me that he is very crafty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 3:15–16; Mark 14:1; Numbers 10:36"> So look, and learn all of the places where he hides himself. Come back to me with sure information, and then I will return with you. If he is in the land, I will search him out among all the thousands of Judah.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:2; Joshua 15:55"> Then they rose up and went to Ziph ahead of Saul. Now David and his men were in the wilderness of Maon, in the Arabah to the south of Jeshimon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 15:8; 1 Samuel 23:28"> Saul and his men went to seek him. But David was told of it, so he went down to a rocky hill and lived in the wilderness of Maon. When Saul heard it, he chased David in the wilderness of Maon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 17:9; 2 Corinthians 1:8; Psalms 140:1–9"> Saul went on one side of the mountain, and David and his men were going on the other side of the mountain. David hurried to get away from Saul. As Saul and his men were surrounding David and his men to take them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 19:9; Psalms 116:3; Deuteronomy 32:36"> a messenger came to Saul and said, “Hurry and come, for the Philistines have made a raid against the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> So Saul returned from pursuing David and went against the Philistines. Therefore that place was called the Rock of Escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:2; Joshua 15:62; Ezekiel 47:10"> David went up from there and lived in the strongholds of Engedi.</span>
</div>
