<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:16; 1 Samuel 12:14; Psalms 2:10–11"> Samuel said to Saul, “Yahweh sent me to anoint you king over his people Israel. Now listen to the words of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:17–19; Exodus 17:8–16; Numbers 24:20"> This is what Yahweh of hosts says, 'I have noted what Amalek did to Israel in opposing them on the way, when they came up from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:16–18; Numbers 24:20; Numbers 31:17"> Now go and attack Amalek and completely destroy all that they have. Do not spare them, but kill both man and woman, child and infant, ox and sheep, camel and donkey.'”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 15:24; 1 Samuel 11:8; 1 Samuel 13:15"> Saul summoned the people and numbered them at the city of Telaim—two hundred thousand men on foot, and ten thousand men of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then Saul came to the city of Amalek and waited in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Numbers 10:29–32; Exodus 18:19"> Then Saul said to the Kenites, “Go, depart, come out from among the Amalekites, so I do not destroy you along with them. For you showed kindness to all the people of Israel, when they came from Egypt.” So the Kenites moved away from the Amalekites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 16:7; Genesis 25:18; 1 Samuel 14:48"> Then Saul attacked the Amalekites, from Havilah as far as Shur, which is east of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:1; Esther 3:1; Numbers 24:7"> Then he took Agag the king of the Amalekites alive; he completely destroyed all the people with the edge of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:15; 1 Samuel 15:19; 1 Samuel 15:3"> But Saul and the people spared Agag, as well as the best of the sheep, oxen, fattened calves, and the lambs. Everything that was good, they did not destroy. But they completely destroyed anything that was despised and worthless.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then the word of Yahweh came to Samuel, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:13; Genesis 6:6; 2 Samuel 24:16"> “It grieves me that I have made Saul king, for he has turned back from following me and has not performed my commandments.” Samuel was angry; he cried out to Yahweh all night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:55; 2 Samuel 18:18; 1 Samuel 25:2"> Samuel got up early to meet Saul in the morning. Samuel was told, “Saul came to Carmel and he set up a monument to himself, then turned and proceeded on down to Gilgal.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 17:2; Ruth 3:10; Proverbs 31:31"> Then Samuel came to Saul, and Saul said to him, “Blessed are you by Yahweh! I have fulfilled the command of Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 3:13–15; Jeremiah 2:18–19; Jeremiah 2:34–37"> Samuel said, “What then is this bleating of sheep in my ears, and the lowing of the oxen that I hear?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:21; 1 Samuel 15:9; Exodus 32:22–23"> Saul replied, “They have brought them from the Amalekites. For the people spared the best of the sheep and oxen, to sacrifice to Yahweh your God. The rest we have completely destroyed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:27; 1 Kings 22:16; 1 Samuel 12:7"> Then Samuel said to Saul, “Wait, and I will tell you what Yahweh has said to me tonight.” Saul said to him, “Speak!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:22; 1 Samuel 9:21; 1 Samuel 15:1–3"> Samuel said, “Though you are little in your own sight, were you not made the head of the tribes of Israel? Then Yahweh anointed you king over Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:29; Genesis 13:13; Genesis 15:16"> and Yahweh sent you on your way and said, 'Go and completely destroy the sinners, the Amalekites, and fight against them until they are destroyed.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:32; 2 Chronicles 33:6; 2 Chronicles 36:12"> Why did you not obey the voice of Yahweh, but instead you seized the booty and did what was evil in the sight of Yahweh?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:13; Matthew 19:20; Romans 10:3"> Then Saul said to Samuel, “I have indeed obeyed the voice of Yahweh, and have gone on the way that Yahweh sent me. I have captured Agag, the king of Amalek, and have completely destroyed the Amalekites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:15; Exodus 32:22–23; Genesis 3:13"> But the people took some of the booty—sheep and oxen, the best of the things devoted to destruction, to sacrifice to Yahweh your God in Gilgal.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Psalms 51:16–17; Proverbs 21:3"> Samuel replied, “Has Yahweh as much delight in burnt offerings and sacrifices, as in obeying the voice of Yahweh? Obedience is better than sacrifice, and to listen is better than the fat of rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:16; 1 Samuel 13:14; Leviticus 20:6"> For rebellion is like the sin of divination, and stubbornness is like wickedness and iniquity. Because you have rejected the word of Yahweh, he has also rejected you from being king.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13; Isaiah 51:12–13; Numbers 22:34"> Then Saul said to Samuel, “I have sinned; for I have broken Yahweh's commandment and your words, because I was afraid of the people and obeyed their voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 10:17"> Now, please pardon my sin, and return with me so that I may worship Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; 1 Samuel 13:14; 1 Samuel 16:1"> Samuel said to Saul, “I will not go back with you; for you have rejected the word of Yahweh, and Yahweh has rejected you from being king over Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 11:30–31"> As Samuel turned to leave, Saul took hold of the hem of his robe, and it tore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:17–18; Acts 13:22; Daniel 4:32"> Samuel said to him, “Yahweh has torn the kingdom of Israel from you today and has given it to a neighbor of yours, one who is better than you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:14; Numbers 23:19; Titus 1:2"> Also, the Strength of Israel will not lie nor change his mind; for he is not a man, that he should change his mind.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 5:44; John 12:43; Isaiah 29:13"> Then Saul said, “I have sinned. But please honor me now before the elders of my people and before Israel. Turn again with me, that I may worship Yahweh your God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> So Samuel turned again after Saul, and Saul worshiped Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 18:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Jeremiah 48:44"> Then Samuel said, “Bring Agag the king of the Amalekites here to me.” Agag came to him confined with chains and said, “Surely the bitterness of death has past.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Judges 1:7; Genesis 9:6; Matthew 7:2"> Samuel replied, “As your sword has made women childless, so shall your mother be childless among women.” Then Samuel chopped Agag to pieces before Yahweh at Gilgal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:4; 1 Samuel 7:17"> Samuel went to Ramah, and Saul went up to his house at Gibeah of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:11; 1 Samuel 19:24; 1 Samuel 16:1"> Samuel did not see Saul until the day of his death, for he mourned for Saul. Yahweh was grieved that he had made Saul king over Israel.</span>
</div>
