<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:10; 1 Samuel 25:13; 1 Kings 2:39–40"> David arose and passed over, he and the six hundred men who were with him, to Achish son of Maok, the king of Gath.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:28; Joshua 15:31; 1 Chronicles 12:20"> So that day Achish gave him Ziklag; that is why Ziklag belongs to the kings of Judah to this very day.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:7–8; Joshua 13:2; Joshua 13:13"> David and his men attacked various places, making raids on the Geshurites, the Girzites, and the Amalekites; for those nations were the inhabitants of the land, as you go to Shur, as far as the land of Egypt. They had been living there in the land from ancient times.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Instead of the Girzites which is found in some ancient Hebrew copies, some modern versions have the Gizrites which is found in the margin of some Hebrew manuscripts.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Joshua 6:21; Deuteronomy 25:17–19"> David attacked the land and saved neither man nor woman alive; he took away the sheep, the oxen, the donkeys, the camels, and the clothing; he would return and come again to Achish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 1:16; 1 Chronicles 2:9; 1 Chronicles 2:25"> Achish would say, “Against whom have you made a raid today?” David would answer, “Against the south of Judah,” or “Against the south of the Jerahmeelites,” or “Against the south of the Kenites.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Proverbs 12:19; 1 Samuel 22:22"> David would keep neither man nor woman alive to bring them to Gath, saying, “So that they cannot say about us, 'David did such and such.'” This was what he did all the while he was living in the country of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; 1 Samuel 13:4"> Achish believed David, saying, “He has made his people Israel utterly abhor him; he will therefore be my servant forever.”</span>
