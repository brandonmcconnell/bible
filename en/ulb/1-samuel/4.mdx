<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:1; 1 Samuel 7:12; 1 Samuel 5:1"> The word of Samuel came to all of Israel.<br />Now Israel went out to battle against the Philistines. They set up camp at Ebenezer, and the Philistines set up camp at Aphek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 Samuel 17:8; Joshua 7:5–8"> The Philistines lined up for battle against Israel. When the battle spread, Israel was defeated by the Philistines, who killed about four thousand men on the field of battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Numbers 10:35; 1 Corinthians 10:1–5"> When the people came into the camp, the elders of Israel said, “Why has Yahweh defeated us today before the Philistines? Let us bring the ark of the covenant of Yahweh here from Shiloh, that it may be here with us, that it might keep us safe from the hands of our enemies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:2; Numbers 7:89; Psalms 80:1"> So the people sent men to Shiloh; from there they carried the ark of the covenant of Yahweh of hosts, who sits above the cherubim. The two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, were there with the ark of the covenant of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 6:5; Job 20:5; Amos 6:3"> When the ark of the covenant of Yahweh came into the camp, all of the people of Israel gave a great shout, and the earth resounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 32:17–18"> When the Philistines heard the noise of the shouting, they said, “What does this loud shouting in the camp of the Hebrews mean?” Then they realized that the ark of Yahweh had come into the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:30; Exodus 14:25; Exodus 15:14–16"> The Philistines were afraid; they said, “A god has come into the camp.” They said, “Woe to us! Nothing like this has happened before!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 9:14; Exodus 7:5; Psalms 78:43–51"> Woe to us! Who will protect us from the strength of these mighty gods? These are the gods who attacked the Egyptians with many different kinds of plagues in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:13; Judges 13:1; 2 Samuel 10:12"> Take courage, and be men, you Philistines, or you will become slaves to the Hebrews, as they have been slaves to you. Be men, and fight.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 26:17; 2 Kings 14:12; Deuteronomy 28:25"> The Philistines fought, and Israel was defeated. Every man fled to his house, and the slaughter was very great; for thirty thousand foot soldiers from Israel fell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:34; Psalms 78:64; 1 Samuel 2:32"> The ark of God was taken, and the two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 7:6; Nehemiah 9:1; 2 Samuel 15:32"> A man of Benjamin ran from the battle line and came to Shiloh the same day, arriving with his clothes torn and earth on his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:9; 1 Samuel 4:18; Joshua 7:9"> When he arrived, Eli was sitting on his seat by the road watching because his heart trembled with concern for the ark of God. When the man entered the city and told the news, the whole city cried out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When Eli heard the noise of the outcry, he said, “What is the meaning of this uproar?” The man quickly came and told Eli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:2; Psalms 90:10; 1 Kings 14:4"> Now Eli was ninety-eight years old; his eyes did not focus, and he could not see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:4; 1 Samuel 3:6; Joshua 7:19"> The man said to Eli, “I am the one who came from the battle line. I fled from the battle today.” Eli said, “How did it go, my son?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:11; 1 Samuel 4:10–11"> The man who brought the news answered and said, “Israel fled from the Philistines. Also, there has been a great defeat among the people. Also, your two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, are dead, and the ark of God has been taken.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:21–22; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Psalms 69:9"> When he mentioned the ark of God, Eli fell over backward from his seat by the side of the gate. His neck was broken, and he died, because he was old and heavy. He had judged Israel for forty years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Now his daughter-in-law, Phinehas' wife, was pregnant and about to give birth. When she heard the news that the ark of God was captured and that her father-in-law and her husband were dead, she knelt down and gave birth, but her labor pains overwhelmed her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:17–18; Psalms 77:2; John 16:21"> About the time of her death the women attending to her said, “Do not be afraid, for you have given birth to a son.” But she did not answer or take what they said to heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; Jeremiah 2:11; Psalms 78:61"> She named the child Ichabod, saying, “The glory has gone away from Israel!” for the ark of God had been captured, and because of her father-in-law and her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 137:5–6; John 2:17"> She said, “The glory has gone away from Israel, because the ark of God has been captured.”</span>
</div>
