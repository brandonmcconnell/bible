<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:1; 1 Samuel 29:1–3; 1 Samuel 7:7"> It came about in those days that the Philistines gathered their armies together for battle to fight with Israel. Achish said to David, “Know for certain that you will go out with me in the host, you and your men.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 12:9; 2 Samuel 16:16–19; 1 Samuel 27:10"> David said to Achish, “So you will know what your servant can do.” Achish said to David, “So I will make you my bodyguard all your days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; 1 Samuel 25:1; Leviticus 20:27"> Now Samuel had died, and all Israel had lamented him and buried him in Ramah, in his own city. Also, Saul had banned from the land those who talked with the dead or with spirits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:1; Joshua 19:18; 2 Kings 4:8"> Then the Philistines gathered themselves together and came and camped at Shunem; and Saul gathered all Israel together, and they camped at Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 57:20–21; Daniel 5:6; Isaiah 21:3–4"> When Saul saw the army of the Philistines, he was afraid, and his heart trembled very much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 12:6; 1 Samuel 14:37; Lamentations 2:9"> Saul prayed to Yahweh for help, but Yahweh did not answer him—neither by dreams, nor by Urim, nor by prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 16:16; Joshua 17:11; 1 Chronicles 10:13"> Then Saul said to his servants, “Find me a woman who talks with the dead, so that I may go to her and seek her advice.” His servants said to him, “See, there is a woman in Endor who claims to talk with the dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; 1 Chronicles 10:13; 1 Kings 22:30"> Saul disguised himself, put on other clothing, and went, he and two men with him; they went to the woman by night. He said, “Divine for me, I beg you, with a spirit, and bring me up whomever I name to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:3; 2 Kings 5:7; 2 Samuel 18:13"> The woman said to him, “See, you know what Saul has done, how he has banned from the land those who talk with the dead or with spirits. So why are you setting a trap for my life, to make me die?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:11; 1 Samuel 19:6; Deuteronomy 18:10–12"> Saul swore to her by Yahweh and said, “As Yahweh lives, no punishment will happen to you for this thing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then the woman said, “Whom should I bring up to you?” Saul said, “Bring up Samuel for me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:3; 1 Kings 14:5"> When the woman saw Samuel, she cried with a loud voice and spoke to Saul, saying, “Why have you deceived me? For you are Saul.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 82:6–7; John 10:34–35; Exodus 22:28"> The king said to her, “Do not be afraid. What do you see?” The woman said to Saul, “I see a god coming up out of the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:27; 2 Kings 2:8; 1 Samuel 24:8"> He said to her, “What does he look like?” She said, “An old man is coming up; he is clothed with a robe.” Saul perceived that it was Samuel, and he bowed with his face to the ground and showed respect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 16:23–26; 1 Samuel 28:6; 1 Samuel 18:12"> Samuel said to Saul, “Why have you disturbed me and brought me up?” Saul answered, “I am very distressed, for the Philistines are waging war against me, and God has left me and does not answer me any more, neither by prophets, nor by dreams. Therefore I have called you, that you may make known to me what I will do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 6:27; Lamentations 2:5; Psalms 68:1–3"> Samuel said, “What then do you ask me, since Yahweh has left you, and he has become your enemy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:27–29; 1 Samuel 16:13; 1 Samuel 13:13–14"> Yahweh has done to you what he said he would. Yahweh has torn the kingdom out of your hand and he has given it to someone else—to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:9; 1 Kings 20:42; Psalms 50:21–22"> Because you did not obey the voice of Yahweh and did not carry out his fierce wrath on Amalek, he has therefore done this today to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 5:5; Jeremiah 28:16–17; 1 Kings 22:20"> Yahweh will give Israel along with you into the hand of the Philistines, and tomorrow you and your sons will be with me. Yahweh will also give the army of Israel into the hand of the Philistines.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:5; Job 26:2; Job 15:20–24"> Then Saul immediately fell his full length on the ground and was very afraid because of the words of Samuel. There was no strength in him, for he had eaten no food all that day, neither that whole night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 12:3; Job 13:14; 1 Samuel 19:5"> The woman came to Saul and saw that he was very troubled, She said to him, “See, your woman servant has listened to your voice; I have put my life in my hand and have listened to the words that you said to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Now therefore, I beg you, listen also to the voice of your woman servant, and let me set a little food in front of you. Eat so that you may gain strength for when you go on your way.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 21:4; Luke 24:29; Luke 14:23"> But Saul refused and said, “I will not eat.” But his servants, together with the woman, compelled him, and he listened to their voice. So he rose from the ground and sat on the bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 15:23; Genesis 18:7–8"> The woman had a fatted calf in the house; she hurried and killed it; she took flour, kneaded it, and baked unleavened bread with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> She brought it before Saul and his servants, and they ate. Then they got up and left that night.</span>
