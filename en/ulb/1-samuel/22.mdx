<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 57:1–11; Psalms 142:1–7; 1 Chronicles 11:15"> So David left there and escaped to the cave of Adullam. When his brothers and all his father's house heard it, they went down there to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; 1 Samuel 30:22–24; Psalms 72:12–14"> Everyone who was in distress, everyone who was in debt, and everyone who was discontented—they all gathered to him. David became captain over them. There were about four hundred men with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:18; 2 Samuel 15:25–26; Judges 11:29"> Then David went from there to Mizpah in Moab. He said to the king of Moab, “Please let my father and my mother go out with you until I know what God will do for me.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some ancient versions have, Please let my father and my mother stay with you, and some modern versions read this way.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 11:1; 1 Chronicles 12:16; 1 Chronicles 29:29"> He left them with the king of Moab. His father and mother stayed with him the whole time that David was in his stronghold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:25; 1 Chronicles 21:9; 1 Chronicles 29:29"> Then the prophet Gad said to David, “Do not stay in your stronghold. Leave and go into the land of Judah.” So David left there and went into the forest of Hereth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:9; 1 Samuel 18:10; 1 Samuel 20:33"> Saul heard that David had been discovered, along with the men who were with him. Now Saul was sitting in Gibeah under the tamarisk tree in Ramah, with his spear in his hand, and all his servants were standing around him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 12:16; 1 Samuel 8:14–15; 1 Samuel 22:13"> Saul said to his servants who stood around him, “Listen now, people of Benjamin! Will the son of Jesse give every one of you fields and vineyards? Will he make you all captains of thousands and captains of hundreds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:3; 1 Samuel 23:21; 1 Samuel 20:13–17"> in exchange for all of you plotting against me? None of you informs me when my son makes a covenant with the son of Jesse. None of you is sorry for me. None of you informs me that my son has incited my servant David against me. Today he hides and waits for me so he may attack me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 52:1–9; 1 Samuel 14:3; Ezekiel 22:9"> Then Doeg the Edomite, who stood by the servants of Saul, answered, “I saw the son of Jesse come to Nob, to Ahimelech son of Ahitub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 27:21; 1 Samuel 30:8; 1 Samuel 23:4"> He prayed to Yahweh that he might help him, and he gave him provisions and the sword of Goliath the Philistine.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:15"> Then the king sent someone to summon the priest Ahimelech son of Ahitub and all his father's house, the priests who were in Nob. All of them came to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:6; 1 Samuel 22:7; Isaiah 65:1"> Saul said, “Listen now, son of Ahitub.” He answered, “Here I am, my master.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:8; Luke 23:2–5; Amos 7:10"> Saul said to him, “Why have you plotted against me, you and the son of Jesse, in that you have given him bread, and a sword, and have prayed to God that he might help him, so that he might rise up against me, to hide in secret, as he does today?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:32; 1 Samuel 19:4–5; 1 Samuel 22:13"> Then Ahimelech answered the king and said, “Who among all your servants is so faithful as David, who is the king's son-in-law and is over your bodyguard, and is honored in your house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:36; 1 Peter 3:16–17; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> Is today the first time I have prayed to God to help him? Far be it from me! Do not let the king impute anything to his servant or to all the house of my father. For your servant knows nothing of this whole matter.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 28:15; Daniel 3:19–20; 1 Kings 19:2"> The king replied, “You will surely die, Ahimelech, you and all your father's house.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 1:17; 1 Samuel 14:45; 2 Kings 1:13–14"> The king said to the guard that stood around him, “Turn and kill the priests of Yahweh. Because their hand also is with David, and because they knew that he fled, but did not reveal it to me.” But the servants of the king would not put out their hand to kill the priests of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:10–11; Hosea 7:3; 1 Samuel 2:18"> Then the king said to Doeg, “Turn and kill the priests.” So Doeg the Edomite turned and attacked the priests; he killed eighty-five persons who wore a linen ephod that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Hosea 10:14; 1 Samuel 21:1"> He also put to the sword Nob, the city of the priests; both men and women, children and infants, and oxen and donkeys and sheep he put to the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:6; 1 Samuel 30:7; 1 Kings 2:26–27"> But one of the sons of Ahimelech son of Ahitub, named Abiathar, escaped and fled after David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Abiathar told David that Saul had killed Yahweh's priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:1–9; Psalms 44:22"> David said to Abiathar, “I knew on that day, when Doeg the Edomite was there, that he would surely tell Saul. I am responsible for every death in your father's family!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 2:26; Hebrews 12:1–3; John 10:28–30"> Stay with me and do not be afraid. For the one who seeks your life seeks mine as well. You will be safe with me.”</span>
