<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:12; 1 Samuel 4:1; Joshua 13:3"> Now the Philistines had captured the ark of God, and they brought it from Ebenezer to Ashdod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 16:23; Daniel 5:2; 1 Chronicles 10:10"> The Philistines took the ark of God, brought it into the house of Dagon, and set it up beside Dagon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 19:1; Isaiah 46:1–2; Isaiah 46:7"> When the people of Ashdod got up early the next day, behold, Dagon had fallen facedown on the ground before the ark of Yahweh. So they took Dagon and set him up in his place again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 1:7; Isaiah 2:18–19; Jeremiah 10:11"> But when they got up early the next morning, behold, Dagon had fallen facedown on the ground before the ark of Yahweh. The head of Dagon and both of his hands were lying cut off in the doorway. Only the trunk of Dagon remained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:9; Psalms 115:4–7; Joshua 5:15"> This is why, even today, the priests of Dagon and anyone who comes into Dagon's house does not step on the doorway of Dagon in Ashdod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:11; Acts 13:11; Exodus 9:3"> Yahweh's hand was heavy upon the people of Ashdod. He destroyed them and afflicted them with tumors, both Ashdod and its territories.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:33; Jeremiah 48:7; 1 Chronicles 15:13"> When the men of Ashdod realized what was happening, they said, “The ark of the God of Israel must not stay with us, because his hand is hard against us and against Dagon our god.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:11; 1 Samuel 17:4; Zechariah 12:3"> So they sent for and gathered together all of the rulers of the Philistines; they said to them, “What should we do with the ark of the God of Israel?” They answered, “Let the ark of the God of Israel be brought around to Gath.” So they carried the ark of the God of Israel there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:6; 1 Samuel 7:13; Deuteronomy 2:15"> But after they brought it around, Yahweh's hand was against the city, causing a very great confusion. He afflicted the men of the city, both small and great; and tumors broke out on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 1:8; Judges 1:18; Joshua 15:45"> So they sent the ark of God to Ekron. But as soon as the ark of God came into Ekron, the Ekronites cried out, saying, “They have brought to us the ark of the God of Israel to kill us and our people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:6; Jeremiah 48:42–44; Isaiah 13:7–9"> So they sent for and gathered together all of the rulers of the Philistines; they said to them, “Send away the ark of the God of Israel, and let it return to its own place, so that it does not kill us and our people.” For there was a deathly panic throughout the city; the hand of God was very heavy there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 12:30; Amos 5:19; 1 Kings 19:17"> The men who did not die were afflicted with the tumors, and the cry of the city went up to the heavens.</span>
