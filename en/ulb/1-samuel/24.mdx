<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:28–29; Ezekiel 22:9; 1 Samuel 23:19"> When Saul returned from chasing the Philistines, he was told, “David is in the wilderness of Engedi.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:2; Psalms 104:18; Psalms 37:32"> Then Saul took three thousand chosen men from all Israel and went to seek David and his men on the Rocks of the Wild Goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 3:24; Psalms 57:1–11; Psalms 142:1–7"> He came to sheep pens on the way, where there was a cave. Saul went inside to cover his feet. Now David and his men were sitting far back in the cave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:8–11; 1 Samuel 24:10; 1 Samuel 26:23"> David's men said to him, “This is the day of which Yahweh spoke when he said to you, 'I will give your enemy into your hand, for you to do with him as you wish.'” Then David arose and quietly crept forward and cut off the corner of Saul's robe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:10; 2 Kings 22:19; 1 John 3:20–21"> Afterward David's heart afflicted him because he had cut a corner off Saul's robe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:14; Job 31:29–30; 1 Samuel 26:9–11"> He said to his men, “May Yahweh forbid that I should do this thing to my master, Yahweh's anointed, to put out my hand against him, seeing he is Yahweh's anointed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Psalms 7:4; Romans 12:17–21"> So David rebuked his men with these words, and did not permit them to attack Saul. Saul stood up, left the cave, and went on his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:23–24; Romans 13:7; Exodus 20:12"> Afterward, David also stood up, left the cave, and called out after Saul: “My master the king.” When Saul looked behind him, David bowed with his face to the ground and showed him respect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:21–22; Proverbs 29:12; Proverbs 16:28"> David said to Saul, “Why do you listen to the men who say, 'See, David is seeking your harm?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 105:15; 1 Samuel 24:4; 1 Samuel 26:8–9"> Today your eyes have seen how Yahweh put you into my hand when we were in the cave. Some told me to kill you, but I spared you. I said, 'I will not put out my hand against my master; for he is Yahweh's anointed.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:20; 1 Samuel 23:14; 1 Samuel 23:23"> See, my father, see the corner of your robe in my hand. For the fact that I cut off the corner of your robe and did not kill you, you may know and see that there is no evil or treason in my hand, and I have not sinned against you, even though you hunt my life to take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Judges 11:27; Psalms 35:1"> May Yahweh judge between you and me, and may Yahweh avenge me against you, but my hand shall not be against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 7:20; Matthew 7:16–18; Matthew 12:33–34"> As the proverb of the ancients says, 'Out of the wicked comes wickedness.' But my hand will not be against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:20; 1 Samuel 17:43; 2 Samuel 9:8"> After whom has the king of Israel come out? After whom do you pursue? After a dead dog! After a flea!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 35:1; Psalms 119:154"> May Yahweh be judge and give judgment between you and me, and see to it, and plead my cause and permit me to escape from your hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:17; Genesis 33:4; Luke 21:15"> When David had finished speaking these words to Saul, Saul said, “Is this your voice, my son David?” Saul lifted up his voice and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; 1 Samuel 26:21; Genesis 38:26"> He said to David, “You are more righteous than I am. For you have repaid me good, where I have repaid you evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:23; Psalms 31:8; 1 Samuel 23:12"> You have declared today how you have done good to me, for you did not kill me when Yahweh had put me at your mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 18:20; 1 Samuel 23:21; Proverbs 25:21–22"> For if a man finds his enemy, will he let him go safely? May Yahweh reward you with good for what you have done to me today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:17; Matthew 2:3–6; 2 Samuel 3:17–18"> Now, I know that you will surely be king and that the kingdom of Israel will be established in your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 21:23; 2 Samuel 21:6–8; 1 Samuel 20:14–17"> Swear to me by Yahweh that you will not cut off my descendants after me, and that you will not destroy my name out of my father's house.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:29; John 2:24; Matthew 10:16–17"> So David made an oath to Saul. Then Saul went home, but David and his men went up to the stronghold.</span>
