<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 2:9; 2 Corinthians 1:12; 1 John 3:21"> Then David fled from Naioth in Ramah and came and said to Jonathan, “What have I done? What is my iniquity? What is my sin before your father, that he seeks to take my life?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:15; John 17:8; John 15:15"> Jonathan said to David, “Far from it; you will not die. My father does nothing either great or small without telling it to me. Why should my father hide this thing from me? It is not so.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:26; Deuteronomy 6:13; 2 Kings 2:6"> Yet David vowed again and said, “Your father knows well that I have found favor in your eyes. He has said, 'Do not let Jonathan know this, or he will be grieved.' But as truly as Yahweh lives, and as you live, there is but a step between me and death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then Jonathan said to David, “Whatever you say, I will do for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; 1 Samuel 19:2; Numbers 28:11"> David said to Jonathan, “Tomorrow is the new moon, and I ought to sit down to eat with the king. But let me go, so that I may hide myself in the field until the third day at evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:58; 1 Samuel 9:12; Deuteronomy 12:5"> If your father misses me at all, then say, 'David earnestly asked leave of me that he might run to Bethlehem his city; because it is the yearly sacrifice there for all the clan.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:17; Esther 7:7; Deuteronomy 1:23"> If he says, 'It is well,' your servant will have peace. But if he is very angry, then know that he has decided on evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:32; 1 Samuel 18:3; 1 Samuel 23:18"> Therefore deal kindly with your servant. For you have brought your servant into a covenant of Yahweh with you. But if there is sin in me, kill me yourself; for why then should you bring me to your father?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:42; 1 Samuel 19:2; 1 Samuel 20:38"> Jonathan said, “Far be it from you! If I learned my father decided harm to come upon you, would I not tell you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:14; 1 Samuel 25:17; Genesis 42:30"> Then David said to Jonathan, “Who will tell me if by chance your father should answer you roughly?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Jonathan said to David, “Come, let us go out into the field.” So they both went out into the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 22:22; Job 31:4; Proverbs 25:2–3"> Jonathan said to David, “May Yahweh, the God of Israel, be witness. When I have questioned my father around this time tomorrow, or the third day, see, if there is good will toward David, will I not then send to you and make it known to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ruth 1:17; 1 Chronicles 22:11; Joshua 1:5"> If it pleases my father to do you harm, may Yahweh do to Jonathan and more also if I do not make it known to you and send you away, so that you may go in peace. May Yahweh be with you, as he has been with my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:3; Ephesians 5:1–2"> If I am still alive, will you not show me the covenant faithfulness of Yahweh, that I may not die?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:7; 1 Samuel 24:21; 2 Samuel 9:1–7"> Do not cut off your covenant faithfulness from my house forever—not even when Yahweh cuts off every one of the enemies of David from the face of the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:22; 1 Samuel 18:3; 2 Samuel 21:8"> So Jonathan made a covenant with the house of David and said, “May Yahweh require an accounting from the hand of the enemies of David.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 18:24; 1 Samuel 18:1; 1 Samuel 18:3"> Jonathan made David vow again because of the love that he had for him, because he loved him as he loved his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:25; 1 Samuel 20:5"> Then Jonathan said to him, “Tomorrow is the new moon. You will be missed because your seat will be empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:2; 1 Samuel 20:5"> When you have stayed three days, go down quickly and come to the place where you hid yourself when the business was in hand, and stay by the stone Ezel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> I will shoot three arrows to the side of it, as though I were shooting at a target.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:2; Amos 8:14; Jeremiah 12:16"> Then I will send my young man and say to him, 'Go find the arrows.' If I say to the young boy, 'Look, the arrows are on this side of you; get them,” then come; for there will be safety for you and not harm, as Yahweh lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:37"> “But if I say to the young man, 'Look, the arrows are beyond you,' then go your way, for Yahweh has sent you away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:14–15; 1 Samuel 20:42; Genesis 31:50"> As for the agreement of which you and I have spoken, see, Yahweh is between you and me forever.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 4:17; Isaiah 1:11–15; Proverbs 21:3"> So David hid himself in the field. When the new moon came, the king sat down to eat food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:18; Judges 16:20"> The king sat on his seat, as usual, on the seat by the wall. Jonathan stood up, and Abner sat by Saul's side. But David's place was empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Leviticus 11:24; Leviticus 11:27"> Yet Saul did not say anything that day, because he thought, “Something has happened to him. He is not clean; surely he is not clean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4; 1 Samuel 19:15; 1 Samuel 18:11"> But on the second day, the day after the new moon, David's place was empty. Saul said to Jonathan his son, “Why has the son of Jesse not come to the meal either yesterday or today?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:6"> Jonathan answered Saul, “David earnestly asked permission from me to go to Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:28"> He said, 'Please let me go. For our family has a sacrifice in the city, and my brother has ordered me to be there. Now, if I have found favor in your eyes, please let me go and see my brothers.' For this reason he has not come to the king's table.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 21:24; James 1:19–20; Proverbs 25:28"> Then Saul's anger burned against Jonathan, and he said to him, “You son of a perverse, rebellious woman! Do I not know that you have chosen the son of Jesse to your own shame, and to the shame of your mother's nakedness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:6; 1 Samuel 20:8; 1 Samuel 19:11–15"> For as long as the son of Jesse lives on the earth, neither you nor your kingdom will be established. Now then, send and bring him to me, for he must surely die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 27:23; 1 Samuel 19:5; Luke 23:22"> Jonathan answered Saul his father, “For what reason should he be put to death? What has he done?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:7; 1 Samuel 18:11; 1 Samuel 19:10"> Then Saul threw his spear at him to kill him. So Jonathan knew that his father was determined to put David to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ephesians 4:26; Mark 3:5; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> Jonathan got up from the table in fierce anger and ate no food the second day of the month, for he was grieved over David, because his father had dishonored him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:5; 1 Samuel 20:19"> In the morning, Jonathan went out into the field to the appointment with David, and a young man was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:20–22"> He said to his young man, “Run and find the arrows that I shoot.” As the young man ran, he shot an arrow beyond him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:22"> When the young man came to the place where the arrow that Jonathan shot had landed, Jonathan called after the young man, and said, “Is not the arrow beyond you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 55:6–9; Proverbs 6:4–5; Mark 13:14–16"> Then Jonathan called after the young man, “Hurry, be quick, do not stay!” So Jonathan's young man gathered up the arrows and came to his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> But the young man did not know anything. Only Jonathan and David knew the matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Jonathan gave his weapons to his young man and said to him, “Go, take them to the city.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Genesis 29:11; 1 Samuel 18:3; 2 Samuel 9:6"> As soon as the young man was gone, David stood up from behind the mound, lay facedown on the ground, and bowed himself three times. They kissed one another and wept together, with David weeping the more.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Hebrew text reads, David came out from beside the south . Modern versions interpret this passage in different ways because the Hebrew text is difficult to understand.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:17; 1 Samuel 23:18; 1 Samuel 20:22–23"> Jonathan said to David, “Go in peace, because we have both sworn in the name of Yahweh and said, 'May Yahweh be between you and me, and between my descendants and your descendants, forever.'” Then David stood up and left, and Jonathan returned to the city.</span>
