<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 2:26; Nehemiah 11:32; Isaiah 10:32"> Then David came to Nob to see Ahimelech the priest. Ahimelech came to meet David trembling and said to him, “Why are you alone and have no one with you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 2:12; Colossians 3:9; 1 Samuel 22:22"> David said to Ahimelech the priest, “The king has sent me on a mission and has said to me, 'Let no one know anything about the business I am sending you, and what I have commanded you.' I have directed the young men to a certain place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 3:6; Judges 9:29; 1 Samuel 21:4"> Now then what do you have on hand? Give me five loaves of bread, or whatever is here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 19:15; Matthew 12:3–4; Exodus 25:30"> The priest answered David and said, “There is no ordinary bread on hand, but there is holy bread—if the young men have kept themselves from women.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 24:9; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–4; 2 Timothy 2:20–21"> David answered the priest, “Surely women have been kept from us for the past three days, as usual when I set out. The things belonging to the men have been set apart even on ordinary missions. How much more today will what they have be set apart!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:3–4; Leviticus 24:5–9; Matthew 12:3–4"> So the priest gave him the bread that was set apart. For there was no bread there except the bread of the presence, which was removed from before Yahweh, in order to put hot bread in its place on the day it was taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:9; Psalms 52:1–9; 1 Chronicles 27:29"> Now one of the servants of Saul was there that day, detained before Yahweh. His name was Doeg the Edomite, the chief of Saul's herdsmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> David said to Ahimelech, “Now is there not here on hand any spear or sword? For I brought neither my sword nor my weapons with me, because the king's business was urgent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:2; 1 Samuel 17:50–54; Exodus 28:6–14"> The priest said, “The sword of Goliath the Philistine, whom you killed in the Valley of Elah, is here wrapped in a cloth behind the ephod. If you want to take that, take it, for there is no other weapon here.” David said, “There is no other sword like that one; give it to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:21; 1 Kings 19:3; 1 Samuel 27:1–2"> David arose and fled that day from Saul and went to Achish, the king of Gath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:5; 1 Samuel 18:7–8; 1 Samuel 16:1"> Achish's servants said to him, “Is not this David, the king of the land? Did they not sing to one another about him in dances,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'Saul has killed his thousands,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and David his ten thousands?'”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Psalms 56:3; Psalms 34:4"> David took these words to heart and was very afraid of Achish, the king of Gath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Ecclesiastes 7:7; Psalms 34:1–22"> He changed his behavior before them and pretended to be insane in their hands; he made marks on the doors of the gate and let his saliva run down his beard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:7"> Then Achish said to his servants, “Look, you see the man is mad. Why have you brought him to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Do I lack madmen, so that you have brought this fellow to behave like one in my presence? Will this fellow really come into my house?”</span>
