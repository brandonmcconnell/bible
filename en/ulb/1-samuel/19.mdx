<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:1; 1 Samuel 18:9"> Saul said to Jonathan his son and to all his servants that they should kill David. But Jonathan, Saul's son, took great pleasure in David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 17:17; Acts 9:24; 1 Samuel 18:1–3"> So Jonathan told David, “Saul my father seeks to kill you. Therefore be on your guard in the morning and hide yourself in a secret place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:9; 1 Samuel 20:13"> I will go out and stand beside my father in the field where you are, and I will speak with my father about you. If I learn anything, I will tell you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:20; Proverbs 31:8–9; Genesis 42:22"> Jonathan spoke well of David to Saul his father and said to him, “Do not let the king sin against his servant David. For he has not sinned against you, and his deeds have brought you good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:13; 1 Chronicles 11:14; Judges 9:17"> For he took his life in his hand and killed the Philistine. Yahweh brought about a great victory for all Israel. You saw it and rejoiced. Why would you sin against innocent blood by killing David for no reason?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–25; Jeremiah 5:2; Psalms 15:4"> Saul listened to Jonathan. Saul swore, “As Yahweh lives, he will not be put to death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:2; 1 Samuel 16:21; 1 Samuel 18:13"> Then Jonathan called David, and Jonathan told him all these things. Jonathan brought David to Saul, and he was in his presence as before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 27:3; Psalms 18:32–50"> There was war again, and David went out and fought with the Philistines and defeated them with a great slaughter. They fled before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:14; 1 Samuel 18:10–11; 1 Samuel 16:16"> A harmful spirit from Yahweh came on Saul as he sat in his house with his spear in his hand, and as David was playing his instrument.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:33; 1 Samuel 18:11; Proverbs 21:30"> Saul tried to pin David to the wall with the spear, but he slipped away from Saul's presence, so that Saul drove the spear into the wall. David fled and escaped that night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 59:1–17; Judges 16:2"> Saul sent messengers to David's house to watch him that he might kill him in the morning. Michal, David's wife, told him, “If you do not save your life tonight, tomorrow you will be killed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 2:15; Acts 9:24–25; Psalms 34:19"> So Michal let David down through the window. He went and fled, and escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 31:19; Judges 18:17; Judges 18:14"> Michal took a household idol and laid it in the bed. Then she put a pillow of goats' hair at its head, and covered it with the clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 2:5; 2 Samuel 16:17–19; 2 Samuel 17:20"> When Saul sent messengers to take David, she said, “He is sick.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 27:3–4; Psalms 37:12; Job 31:31"> Then Saul sent the messengers to see David; he said, “Bring him up to me in the bed, so that I may kill him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> When the messengers came in, behold, the household idol was in the bed along with the pillow of goats' hair at its head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:22; 1 Samuel 22:17; Exodus 1:17–19"> Saul said to Michal, “Why have you deceived me and let my enemy go, so that he has escaped?” Michal answered Saul, “He said to me, 'Let me go. Why should I kill you?'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:17; Psalms 116:11; 1 Samuel 15:34"> Now David fled and escaped, and went to Samuel in Ramah and told him all that Saul had done to him. Then he and Samuel went and stayed in Naioth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:19; 1 Samuel 22:9–10; 1 Samuel 26:1"> It was told to Saul, saying, “See, David is at Naioth in Ramah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joel 2:28; John 7:32; 1 Samuel 10:5–6"> Then Saul sent messengers to capture David. When they saw the company of the prophets prophesying, and Samuel standing as head over them, the Spirit of God came on the messengers of Saul, and they also prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 27:22; Jeremiah 13:23; Joel 2:28"> When Saul was told this, he sent other messengers, and they also prophesied. So Saul sent messengers again the third time, and they also prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then he also went to Ramah and came to the deep well that is in Seku. He asked, “Where are Samuel and David?” Someone said, “See, they are at Naioth in Ramah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 16:9; 1 Samuel 10:10; 1 Samuel 19:20"> Saul went to Naioth in Ramah. Then the Spirit of God came upon him, and as he went he prophesied until he came to Naioth in Ramah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:20; Isaiah 20:2; Micah 1:8"> He stripped off his clothes and also prophesied before Samuel. He lay naked all that day and all that night. This is why they ask, “Is Saul also among the prophets?”</span>
