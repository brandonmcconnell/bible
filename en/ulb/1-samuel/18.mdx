<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:17; Proverbs 18:24; 1 Samuel 18:3"> When he had finished speaking to Saul, the soul of Jonathan was bound to the soul of David, and Jonathan loved him as his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:15; 1 Samuel 16:21–23"> Saul took David into his service that day; he did not let him return to his father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:8–17; 1 Samuel 20:42; 2 Samuel 21:7"> Then Jonathan and David made a covenant of friendship because Jonathan loved him as his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 41:42; Luke 15:22; Esther 6:8–9"> Jonathan took off the robe that he was wearing and gave it to David with his armor, as well as his sword, bow, and belt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:30; 1 Samuel 18:14–15; Colossians 4:5"> David went out wherever Saul sent him, and he succeeded. Saul set him over the men of war. This was pleasing in the eyes of all the people and also in the sight of Saul's servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Judges 11:34; Psalms 68:25"> As they came home from defeating the Philistines, the women came from all the cities of Israel, singing and dancing, to meet King Saul, with tambourines, with joy, and with musical instruments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:11; 1 Samuel 29:5; Exodus 15:21"> The women sang one to another as they played. They sang:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Saul has killed his thousands,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and David his ten thousands.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:28; Proverbs 13:10; James 4:5"> Saul was very angry, and this song displeased him. He said, “They have ascribed to David ten thousands, but they have ascribed only thousands to me. What more can he have but the monarchy?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 7:22; Genesis 31:2; Matthew 20:15"> Saul looked at David with suspicion from that day on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:9; 2 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Kings 18:29"> The next day a harmful spirit from God rushed upon Saul and he raved within the house. So David played his instrument, as he did each day. Saul had a spear in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:33; Isaiah 54:17; Luke 4:30"> Saul threw the spear, for he thought, “I will pin David to the wall.” But David escaped from Saul's presence twice in this way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:15; 1 Samuel 18:29; 1 Samuel 16:13–14"> Saul was afraid of David, because Yahweh was with him, but was no longer with Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:2; Psalms 121:8; 1 Samuel 18:16–17"> So Saul removed him from his presence and appointed him a commander of a thousand. In this way David went out and came in before the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 39:23; Matthew 28:20; 1 Samuel 16:18"> David was prospering in all his ways, for Yahweh was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:17; Colossians 4:5; James 1:5"> When Saul saw that he prospered, he stood in awe of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:5; Numbers 27:17; 1 Kings 3:7"> But all Israel and Judah loved David, for he went out and came in before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:25; 1 Samuel 25:28; 1 Samuel 18:25"> Then Saul said to David, “Here is my oldest daughter Merab. I will give her to you as a wife. Only be courageous for me and fight Yahweh's battles.” For Saul thought, “Let not my hand be on him, but let the hand of the Philistines be on him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:18; 1 Samuel 9:21; 1 Samuel 18:23"> David said to Saul, “Who am I, and who are my relatives, or my father's clan in Israel, that I should be son-in-law to the king?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 2 Samuel 21:8; Judges 14:20"> But at the time when Merab, Saul's daughter, should have been given to David, she was given to Adriel the Meholathite as a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:28"> But Michal, Saul's daughter, loved David. They told Saul, and this pleased him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:17; Exodus 10:7; Jeremiah 9:8"> Then Saul thought, “I will give her to him, so that she can be a trap for him, and that the hand of the Philistines may be against him.” So Saul said to David a second time, “You will be my son-in-law.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 36:1–3; Psalms 55:21; 2 Samuel 13:28–29"> Saul commanded his servants, “Speak with David in private, and say, 'See, the king takes pleasure in you, and all his servants love you. Now then, become the king's son-in-law.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:141; Proverbs 14:20; Ecclesiastes 9:15–16"> So Saul's servants spoke these words to David. Then David said, “Is it a small matter to you to be the king's son-in-law, since I am a poor man, and lightly esteemed?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The servants of Saul reported to him the words which David spoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 34:12; 1 Samuel 18:17; 1 Samuel 14:24"> Then Saul said, “Say this to David, 'The king does not desire any price for the bride except a hundred foreskins of the Philistines, to be avenged from the king's enemies.'” Now Saul thought to make David fall by the hand of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:21"> When his servants told David these words, it pleased David to be the king's son-in-law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:14; Judges 14:19; 1 Samuel 18:13"> Before those days had expired, David went with his men and killed two hundred Philistines. David brought their foreskins, and they gave them in full number to the king, so that he might be the king's son-in-law. So Saul gave him Michal his daughter as his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:20; 1 Samuel 26:25; Genesis 37:8–11"> When Saul saw, and he knew that Yahweh was with David, and that Michal, Saul's daughter, loved him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:4; John 11:53; James 2:19"> Saul was even more afraid of David. Saul was continually David's enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:5; 2 Samuel 11:1; 1 Peter 2:4"> Then the princes of the Philistines came out for battle, and as often as they came out, David succeeded more than all the servants of Saul, so that his name was highly regarded.</span>
