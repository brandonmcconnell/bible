<div className="hs">For the chief musician. A psalm of David, the servant of Yahweh, when he sang to Yahweh the words of this song on the day that Yahweh rescued him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul. He sang:</div>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:13; Psalms 119:112; Psalms 119:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">For all his righteous decrees have been before me; as for his statutes, I have not turned away from them.</span><br /></span>
