<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 6:6–8; Hebrews 10:4–6; Psalms 69:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will take no bull out of your house,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or male goats out of your folds.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5–6; Psalms 8:6–8; Psalms 104:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">For every beast of the forest is mine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the cattle on a thousand hills.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–22; Matthew 6:26; Luke 12:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">I know all the birds of the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the wild beasts of the field are mine.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Deuteronomy 10:14; Psalms 24:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I were hungry, I would not tell you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the world is mine, and everything in it.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 56:12; Deuteronomy 23:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Offer to God the sacrifice of thanksgiving,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and pay your vows to the Most High.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 1:32; 1 Timothy 5:22; Hebrews 13:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">When you see a thief, you agree with him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you participate with those who commit adultery.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 52:2–4; Psalms 55:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">You give your mouth to evil,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and your tongue expresses deceit.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Proverbs 10:18; Leviticus 19:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">You sit and speak against your brother;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you slander your own mother's son.</span><br /></span>
