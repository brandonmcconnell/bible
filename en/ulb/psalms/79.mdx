<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:4; Jeremiah 14:16; Revelation 11:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have shed their blood like water around Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and there was none to bury them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 80:6; Lamentations 5:1; Psalms 89:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">We have become a reproach for our neighbors,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">mocking and derision to those who are around us.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 64:9; Psalms 142:6; Psalms 116:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not hold the sins of our forefathers against us;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">may your merciful actions come to us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for we are very low.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:7; Psalms 25:11; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Help us, God of our salvation, for the sake of the glory of your name;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">save us and forgive our sins for your name's sake.</span><br /></span>
