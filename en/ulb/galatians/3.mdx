<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 5:7–8; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Galatians 1:6"> Foolish Galatians! Who has put a spell on you? It was before your eyes that Jesus Christ was publicly displayed as crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:16–17; Galatians 3:14; Acts 15:8"> This is the only thing I want to learn from you: Did you receive the Spirit by the works of the law or by believing what you heard?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 5:4–8; Galatians 4:7–10; Hebrews 7:16–19"> Are you so foolish? Having begun by the Spirit, are you now going to finish by the flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 15:2; Hebrews 10:32–39"> Have you suffered so many things for nothing—if indeed it was for nothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 3:2; Acts 14:9–10; Acts 19:11–12"> Does he who gives the Spirit to you and works miracles among you do so by the works of the law, or by hearing with faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Romans 4:21–22; James 2:23"> Just as Abraham “believed God and it was credited to him as righteousness,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:9; Galatians 3:26–29"> in the same way, understand, then, that those of faith are the children of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 28:14"> The scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the Gentiles by faith, preached the gospel beforehand to Abraham, saying, “In you all the nations will be blessed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 4:16; Galatians 3:29; Romans 4:24"> So then, those of faith are blessed along with Abraham, the man of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:26; Romans 8:7; Romans 3:19–20"> All who rely on the works of the law are under a curse; and so it is written, “Cursed is everyone who does not abide by all the things written in the book of the law, and do them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:4; Romans 1:17; Hebrews 10:38"> Now it is clear that no one is justified before God by the law, because “the righteous will live by faith.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Romans 10:5–6; Nehemiah 9:29"> But the law is not of faith, rather, “The person who does the works of the law must live by them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Deuteronomy 21:23; Hebrews 9:12"> Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us—for it is written, “Cursed is everyone who hangs on a tree”—</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 3:28–29; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Ezekiel 36:26–27"> so that the blessing of Abraham might come to the Gentiles in Christ Jesus, so that by faith we might receive the promise of the Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 9:17; Romans 6:19; Romans 3:5"> Brothers, let me speak in human terms. In a man-made contract, no one can set it aside or add to it, once it is established by law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 4:13; Luke 1:55"> Now the promises were spoken to Abraham and to his descendant. It does not say, “to descendants,” referring to many, but instead to only one, “and to your descendant,” who is Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39–40; Genesis 15:13; Romans 4:13–14"> Now what I mean is this: The law, which came 430 years afterward, does not set aside the covenant previously established by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12–15; Galatians 2:21; Romans 4:13–16"> For if the inheritance comes by the law, then it no longer comes by promise. But God gave it to Abraham by a promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:53; Romans 7:7–13; Hebrews 2:2"> What, then, was the purpose of the law? It was added because of trespasses, until the descendant of Abraham would come to whom the promise had been made. The law was put into force through angels by a mediator.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:5; Deuteronomy 6:4; Job 9:33"> Now a mediator implies more than one person, but God is one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Romans 9:31; Romans 3:20–22"> So is the law against the promises of God? Absolutely not! For if a law had been given that could give life, then righteousness would certainly have come by the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 11:32; Hebrews 9:15; Galatians 3:23"> But the scripture imprisoned everything under sin. God did this so that the promise to save us by faith in Jesus Christ might be given to those who believe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 5:18; Galatians 3:24–25; Galatians 3:19"> Now before faith came, we were held captive under the law, imprisoned until faith should be revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 10:4; Acts 13:38–39; Romans 7:24–25"> So then the law became our guardian until Christ came, so that we might be justified by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:4; Hebrews 10:15–18"> But now that faith has come, we are no longer under a guardian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 6:18; John 1:12–13"> For you are all sons of God through faith in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 13:14; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Romans 6:3–4"> For as many of you who were baptized into Christ have clothed yourselves with Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:12–13; Colossians 3:11; Romans 3:29–30"> There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither slave nor free, there is neither male nor female, for you are all one in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:7"> Then if you are Christ's, you are Abraham's descendants, and heirs according to promise.</span>
</div>
</div>
