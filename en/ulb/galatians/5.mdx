<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Galatians 5:13; 1 Peter 2:16"> For freedom Christ has set us free. Stand firm, therefore, and do not again be put under the control of a yoke of slavery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; Acts 15:1; Romans 9:31–32"> Look, I, Paul, say to you that if you let yourselves be circumcised, Christ will not benefit you in any way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Romans 2:25; James 2:10–11"> I testify again to every man who lets himself be circumcised that he is obligated to obey the whole law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:31–32; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Romans 11:6"> You are cut off from Christ, you who would be justified by the law; you no longer experience grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 130:5; 1 Thessalonians 1:10; Psalms 62:5"> For through the Spirit, by faith, we eagerly wait for the hope of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 7:19; James 2:14–26"> In Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision means anything, but only faith working through love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24; Galatians 3:1; Hebrews 12:1"> You were running well. Who prevented you from obeying the truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 1:6; Romans 8:28"> This persuasion does not come from him who calls you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 5:6–7; Mark 8:15"> A little yeast makes the whole batch of dough rise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 5:12; 2 Corinthians 2:3; Galatians 1:7"> I have confidence in the Lord that you will take no other view. The one who is troubling you will pay the penalty, whoever he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; Galatians 6:12; Galatians 4:29"> Brothers, if I still proclaim circumcision, why am I still being persecuted? In that case the stumbling block of the cross has been removed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 5:13; Titus 3:10"> As for those who are disturbing you, I wish they would castrate themselves!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 9:19; 1 Corinthians 8:9"> For you were called to freedom, brothers. But do not use your freedom as an opportunity for the sinful nature; rather, through love serve one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Leviticus 19:18; Galatians 6:2"> For the whole law is fulfilled in one command: “You must love your neighbor as yourself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:3; James 3:14–3; Galatians 5:26"> But if you bite and devour one another, watch out that you are not consumed by one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 5:24–25; Romans 8:12–14; Galatians 6:8"> But I say, walk by the Spirit and you will not carry out the desires of the sinful nature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:13; John 3:6; Romans 8:5–8"> For the desires of the sinful nature are against the Spirit, and the desires of the Spirit are against the sinful nature. For these are in conflict with each other, so that you cannot do the things you want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:14; Romans 6:14–15; Galatians 5:25"> But if you are led by the Spirit, you are not under the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Colossians 3:5–8; Matthew 15:18–19"> Now the works of the sinful nature are evident: sexual immorality, impurity, depravity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Revelation 21:8"> idolatry, sorcery, hostilities, strife, jealousy, outbursts of anger, rivalry, dissension, divisions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Romans 8:13; Romans 13:13"> envy, drunkenness, drunken celebrations, and things like these. I warn you, as I warned you before, that those who practice such things will not inherit the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–17; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7; John 15:5"> But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:9; Acts 24:25; Titus 2:2"> gentleness, and self-control; against such things there is no law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Romans 13:14; Romans 8:13"> Those who belong to Christ Jesus have crucified the sinful nature with its passions and desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Galatians 5:16; Romans 8:4–5; Romans 8:2"> If we live by the Spirit, let us also walk by the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 3:14–16; Philippians 2:1–3; James 4:16"> Let us not become conceited, provoking one another, envying one another.</span>
