<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 2:3; Acts 14:12; 2 Corinthians 8:23"> Then after fourteen years I went up again to Jerusalem with Barnabas, taking Titus along with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 2:6; Philippians 2:16; 1 Thessalonians 3:5"> I went up because of a revelation and set before them the gospel that I proclaim among the Gentiles. I spoke privately to those who seemed to be important, in order to make sure that I was not running—or had not run—in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 5:2–6; Acts 16:3; 1 Corinthians 9:20–21"> But not even Titus, who was with me and who was a Greek, was forced to be circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jude 1:4; Galatians 5:1; Galatians 4:3"> The false brothers came in secretly to spy on the liberty we have in Christ Jesus. They desired to make us slaves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 2:14; Galatians 4:16; Colossians 1:5"> but we did not yield in submission to them for a moment, so that the truth of the gospel would remain with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 2:2; Acts 10:34; 2 Corinthians 12:11"> But those who seemed to be important (whatever they were does not matter to me, God shows no partiality)—those, I say, who seemed important added nothing to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Timothy 2:7; Galatians 1:16"> On the contrary, they saw that I had been entrusted with the gospel to those who are uncircumcised, just as Peter had been entrusted with the gospel to those who are circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Acts 22:21; Acts 2:14–41"> For God, who worked in Peter for the apostleship to those who are circumcised, also worked in me to the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 12:3; Revelation 3:12; Galatians 2:2"> When James, Cephas, and John, who were recognized as those who built up the church, understood the grace that had been given to me, they gave the right hand of fellowship to Barnabas and me. They did this so that we should go to the Gentiles, and so that they should go to those who are circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 24:17; 1 John 3:17; Hebrews 13:16"> They requested only that we remember the poor, the very thing that I was eager to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Corinthians 5:16; Acts 15:1"> But when Cephas came to Antioch, I opposed him to his face because he was declared to be in the wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Luke 15:2; Acts 11:2–3"> Before certain men came from James, Cephas was eating with the Gentiles. But when these men came, he stopped and kept away from the Gentiles. He was afraid of those who were demanding circumcision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Ephesians 4:14; Hebrews 13:9"> Also the rest of the Jews joined in this hypocrisy. Even Barnabas was led astray with them by their hypocrisy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 2:5; Acts 10:28; Acts 15:10–11"> But when I saw that their behavior was not following the truth of the gospel, I said to Cephas in front of all of them, “If you are a Jew but are living like a Gentile and not like a Jew, how can you force the Gentiles to live like Jews?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Ephesians 2:3; Ephesians 2:11–12"> We ourselves are Jews by birth and not Gentile sinners;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Psalms 143:2; Romans 9:30"> yet we know that no person is justified by the works of the law but through faith in Christ Jesus. We also came to faith in Christ Jesus so that we might be justified by faith in Christ and not by the works of the law. For by the works of the law no flesh will be justified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:1–2; 1 John 3:8–10; 1 John 3:5"> But if, while we seek to be justified in Christ, we too, were found to be sinners, does Christ then promote sin? Absolutely not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 4:9–12; Galatians 2:4–5; Galatians 2:12–16"> For if I rebuild those things I once destroyed, I prove myself to be a lawbreaker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 7:4; Romans 6:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:10"> For through the law I died to the law, so that I might live for God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 5:24; 2 Corinthians 5:15; Romans 6:4–6"> I have been crucified with Christ. It is no longer I who live, but Christ lives in me. The life I now live in the flesh I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave himself for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 11:6; Galatians 3:21; Hebrews 7:11"> I do not set aside the grace of God, for if righteousness could be gained through the law, then Christ died for nothing!</span>
</div>
