<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:11–12; 1 Corinthians 1:1; Romans 1:1"> Paul, an apostle—not an apostle from men nor by human agency, but through Jesus Christ and God the Father, who raised him from the dead—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:1; Philippians 4:21; Acts 16:5–6"> and all the brothers with me, to the churches of Galatia:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 John 1:3; 2 Corinthians 13:14; Romans 1:7–15"> Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Romans 4:25; Galatians 2:20"> who gave himself for our sins so that he might deliver us from this present evil age, according to the will of our God and Father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Jude 1:25; Psalms 72:19"> to him be the glory forever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:4; Galatians 5:7–8; Isaiah 29:13"> I am amazed that you are turning away so quickly from him who called you by the grace of Christ. I am amazed that you are turning to a different gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 15:24; Romans 16:17–18; Galatians 5:10"> This is not to say that there is another gospel, but there are some men who cause you trouble and want to change the gospel of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:13–14; Galatians 1:9; Revelation 22:18–19"> But even if we or an angel from heaven should proclaim to you a gospel other than the one we proclaimed to you, let him be cursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 16:17; Proverbs 30:6; Deuteronomy 4:2"> As we have said before, so now I say again, “If someone proclaims to you a gospel other than the one you received, let him be cursed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Acts 5:29; Ephesians 6:6"> For am I now seeking the approval of men or God? Am I seeking to please men? If I am still trying to please men, I am not a servant of Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 1:1; 1 Corinthians 2:9–10; 1 Corinthians 11:23"> For I want you to know, brothers, that the gospel I proclaimed is not man's gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 1:16; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Galatians 1:1"> I did not receive it from any man, nor was I taught it. Instead, it was by revelation of Jesus Christ to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Corinthians 15:9; Acts 22:3–5"> You have heard about my former life in Judaism, how I was persecuting the church of God beyond measure and that I was trying to destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Acts 26:5; Philippians 3:4–6"> I advanced in Judaism beyond many of those who were my own age, from out of my own people. That is how extremely zealous I was for the traditions of my fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 49:5; Isaiah 49:1"> But when God, who had set me apart from my mother's womb, and who called me through his grace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:17; Ephesians 3:5–10; Ephesians 6:12"> was pleased to reveal his Son in me, so that I would proclaim him among the Gentiles, I did not immediately consult with flesh and blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 9:20–25; 2 Corinthians 11:32–33; Galatians 1:18"> I did not go up to Jerusalem to those who had become apostles before me. Instead, I went to Arabia and then returned to Damascus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 9:22–23; Acts 9:26–29; Acts 22:17–18"> Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to get to know Cephas and I stayed with him fifteen days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:55; Mark 6:3; Matthew 12:46"> But I saw none of the other apostles except James, the Lord's brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:1; 2 Corinthians 11:31; 2 Corinthians 11:10–11"> In what I write to you, I assure you before God, that I am not lying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 15:41; Acts 9:30; Acts 6:9"> Then I went to the regions of Syria and Cilicia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:14; Romans 16:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:1"> I was still not personally known to the churches of Judea that are in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:8–10; Acts 9:13; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> They only heard it being said, “The man who once persecuted us is now proclaiming the faith he once tried to destroy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Acts 11:18; Luke 15:10"> So they glorified God because of me.</span>
</div>
</div>
