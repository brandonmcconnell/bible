<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 34:18; Exodus 12:2–20; Exodus 13:4"> Observe the month of Abib, and keep the Passover to Yahweh your God, for in the month of Aviv Yahweh your God brought you out of Egypt by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 12:26; Luke 22:15"> You will sacrifice the Passover to Yahweh your God with some of the flock and the herd in the place that Yahweh will choose as his sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 12:39; Exodus 34:18; Exodus 12:19–20"> You will eat no leavened bread with it; seven days will you eat unleavened bread with it, the bread of affliction; for you came out of the land of Egypt in haste. Do this all the days of your life so that you may call to mind the day when you came out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 34:25; Exodus 12:10; Exodus 13:7"> No yeast must be seen among you within all your borders during seven days; nor must any of the meat that you sacrifice in the evening on the first day remain until the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:2; Deuteronomy 12:5–6"> You may not sacrifice the Passover within any of your city gates that Yahweh your God is giving you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Exodus 12:6–9; Numbers 9:11"> Instead, sacrifice at the place that Yahweh your God will choose as his sanctuary. There you will perform the sacrifice of the Passover in the evening at the going down of the sun, at the time of year that you came out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:13; Exodus 12:8–9; John 2:23"> You must roast it and eat it at the place that Yahweh your God will choose; in the morning you will turn and go to your tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 13:6–8; Leviticus 23:6–8; Leviticus 23:36"> For six days you will eat unleavened bread; on the seventh day there will be a solemn assembly for Yahweh your God; on that day you must do no work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 34:22; Exodus 23:16; Acts 2:1"> You will count seven weeks for yourselves; from the time you begin to put the sickle to the standing grain you must start counting seven weeks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:2; Leviticus 12:8; Malachi 3:10–11"> You must keep the Festival of Weeks for Yahweh your God with the contribution of a freewill offering from your hand that you will give, according as Yahweh your God has blessed you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:7; Deuteronomy 12:12; Deuteronomy 12:18"> You will rejoice before Yahweh your God—you, your son, your daughter, your male servant, your female servant, the Levite who is within your city gates, and the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow who are among you, at the place that Yahweh your God will choose for his sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:15; Lamentations 3:19–20; Romans 6:17–18"> You will call to mind that you were a slave in Egypt; you must observe and do these statutes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; John 7:2; 2 Chronicles 5:3"> You must keep the Festival of Shelters for seven days after you have gathered in the harvest from your threshing floor and from your winepress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:9–12; Deuteronomy 16:11; Isaiah 30:29"> You will rejoice during your festival—you, your son, your daughter, your male servant, your female servant, the Levite, and the foreigner, and the fatherless and the widow who are within your gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 23:36–42; Deuteronomy 30:16; Deuteronomy 16:10"> For seven days you must observe the festival for Yahweh your God at the place that Yahweh will choose, because Yahweh your God will bless you in all your harvest and all the work of your hands, and you must be completely joyful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Psalms 96:8; Haggai 1:9"> Three times in a year all your males must appear before Yahweh your God at the place that he will choose: at the Festival of Unleavened Bread, at the Festival of Weeks, and at the Festival of Shelters. No one will appear before Yahweh empty-handed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–7; 2 Corinthians 8:12; Deuteronomy 16:10"> instead, every man will give as he is able, that you might know the blessing that Yahweh your God has given to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:4; Exodus 18:25–26; Romans 13:1–6"> You must make judges and officers within all your city gates that Yahweh your God is giving you; they will be taken from each of your tribes, and they must judge the people with righteous judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 17:23"> You must not take justice away by force; you must not show partiality nor take a bribe, for a bribe blinds the eyes of the wise and perverts the words of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Philippians 4:8; Ezekiel 18:9; Ezekiel 18:5"> You must follow after justice, after justice alone, so that you may live and inherit the land that Yahweh your God is giving you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:3; 2 Kings 17:16; Exodus 34:13"> You must not set up for yourselves an Asherah, any sort of pole, beside the altar of Yahweh your God that you will make for yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 26:1; Exodus 20:4; Zechariah 8:17"> Neither must you set up for yourself any sacred stone pillar, which Yahweh your God hates.</span>
</div>
