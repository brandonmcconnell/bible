<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Romans 9:8; Galatians 3:26"> You are the people of Yahweh your God. Do not cut yourselves, nor shave any part of your face for the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Leviticus 20:26; Exodus 19:5–6"> For you are a nation that is set apart to Yahweh your God, and Yahweh has chosen you to be a people for his own possession, more than all peoples that are on the surface of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:14; Romans 14:14; Leviticus 20:25"> You must not eat any abominable thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 11:2–45; Acts 10:14; 1 Kings 4:23"> These are the animals that you may eat: the ox, the sheep, and the goat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> the deer, the gazelle, the roebuck, the wild goat, and ibex, and the antelope, and the mountain sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 18:1; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Psalms 1:1–2"> You may eat any animals that parts the hoof, that is, that has the hoof divided in two, and that chews the cud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:22–23; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 3:5"> Nevertheless, you must not eat some animals that chew the cud or that have the hoof divided in two: the camel, the rabbit, and the rock badger; because they chew the cud but do not part the hoof, they are unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 11:26–27; Isaiah 66:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> The pig is unclean to you as well because he parts the hoof but does not chew the cud; he is unclean to you. Do not eat pig meat, and do not touch their carcasses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 11:9–12"> Of these things that are in water you may eat: whatever has fins and scales;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> but whatever has no fins and scales you must not eat; they are unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> All clean birds you may eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 11:13–19"> But these are the birds that you must not eat: the eagle, the vulture, the osprey,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> the red kite and black kite, any kind of falcon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> You must not eat any kind of raven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 30:29"> and the ostrich, and the night hawk, the sea gull, any kind of hawk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> the little owl, the great owl, the white owl,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> the pelican, the carrion vulture, the cormorant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> You must not eat the stork, any kind of heron, the hoopoe, and the bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Leviticus 11:20–23"> All winged, swarming things are unclean to you; they must not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> You may eat all clean flying things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Exodus 34:26; Leviticus 22:8"> You must not eat of anything that dies of itself; you may give it to the foreigner who is within your towns, that he may eat it; or you may sell it to a foreigner. For you are a nation that is set apart to Yahweh your God. You must not boil a young goat in its mother's milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:37; Deuteronomy 12:17; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> You must surely tithe all the yield of your seed, that which comes out from the field year after year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5–7; Deuteronomy 4:10; Deuteronomy 12:17–18"> You must eat before Yahweh your God, in the place that he will choose as his sanctuary, the tithe of your grain, of your new wine, and of your oil, and the firstborn of your herd and your flock; that you may learn to always honor Yahweh your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:21; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 11:24"> If the journey is too long for you so that you are not able to carry it, because the place that Yahweh your God will choose as his sanctuary is too far from you, then, when Yahweh God blesses you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> you will convert the offering into money, tie up the money in your hand, and go to the place that Yahweh your God will choose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Deuteronomy 12:7; Matthew 21:12"> There you will spend the money for whatever you desire: for oxen, or for sheep, or for wine, or for strong drink, or for whatever you desire; you will eat there before Yahweh your God, and you will rejoice, you and your household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 18:20; Deuteronomy 14:29; Deuteronomy 12:12"> The Levite who is within your gates—do not forsake him, for he has no portion nor inheritance with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:22; Amos 4:4; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> At the end of every three years you will present all the tithe of your produce in the same year, and you will store it up within your gates;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 14:27; Proverbs 19:17"> and the Levite, because he has no portion nor inheritance with you, and the foreigner, and the fatherless, and the widow who are within your gates, will come and eat and be satisfied. Do this so that Yahweh your God may bless you in all the work of your hand that you do.</span>
