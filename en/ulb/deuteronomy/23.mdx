</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:1–2; Nehemiah 13:23; Ruth 4:6"> An Ammonite or a Moabite may not belong to the assembly of Yahweh; as far as to the tenth generation of his descendants, none of them may belong to the assembly of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:2; 2 Peter 2:15; 1 Kings 18:4"> This is because they did not meet you with bread and with water on the road when you had come out of Egypt, and because they hired against you Balaam son of Beor from Pethor in Aram Naharaim, to curse you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Romans 8:31; Ephesians 2:4–5"> But Yahweh your God would not listen to Balaam; instead, Yahweh your God turned the curse into a blessing for you, because Yahweh your God loved you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:12; 2 Samuel 12:31; Nehemiah 13:23–25"> You must never seek their peace or prosperity, during all your days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:19; Exodus 22:21; Exodus 23:9"> You must not detest an Edomite, for he is your brother; you must not abhor an Egyptian, because you were a foreigner in his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 20:5–6; Ephesians 2:12–13; Deuteronomy 23:1–3"> The descendants of the third generation that are born to them may belong to the assembly of Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 6:18; Judges 20:26; Revelation 19:11–14"> When you march out as an army against your enemies, then you must keep yourselves from every evil thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 15:16; Numbers 5:2–3; 1 Corinthians 5:11–13"> If there is among you any man who is unclean because of what happened to him at night, then he must go out of the army's camp; he must not come back into the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; Ephesians 5:26–27; Leviticus 14:9"> When evening comes, he must bathe himself in water; when the sun goes down, he will come back inside the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> You must have a place also outside the camp to which you will go;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:6–8"> and you will have something among your tools to dig with; when you squat down to relieve yourself, you must dig with it and then put back the earth and cover up what has come out from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Leviticus 26:12; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> For Yahweh your God walks in the midst of your camp to give you victory and to give your enemies into your hand. Therefore your camp must be holy, so that he may not see any unclean thing among you and turn away from you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:15; Obadiah 1:14"> You must not give back to his master a slave who has escaped from his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:21; Zechariah 7:10; Jeremiah 7:6"> Let him live with you, in whatever town he chooses. Do not oppress him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 23:7; 1 Kings 14:24; Leviticus 19:29"> There must be no cultic prostitute among any of the daughters of Israel, neither must there be a cultic prostitute among the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 3:2; Matthew 7:6; Revelation 22:15"> You must not bring the wages of a prostitute or the wages of a dog into the house of Yahweh your God for any vow; for both these are abominations to Yahweh your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Leviticus 25:35–37; Psalms 15:5"> You must not lend on interest to your fellow Israelite—interest of money, interest of food, or the interest of anything that is lent on interest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 15:3; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> To a foreigner you may lend on interest; but to your fellow Israelite you must not lend on interest, so that Yahweh your God may bless you in all that you put your hand to, in the land which you are going in to possess.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:4–5; Psalms 66:13–14; Psalms 116:18"> When you make a vow to Yahweh your God, you must not be slow in fulfilling it, for Yahweh your God will surely require it of you; it would be sin for you not to fulfill it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But if you will refrain from making a vow, it will be no sin for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 20:25; Judges 11:30–31; 1 Samuel 1:11"> That which has gone out from your lips you must observe and do; according as you have vowed to Yahweh your God, anything that you have freely promised with your mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:26; Romans 12:13; Hebrews 13:5"> When you go into your neighbor's vineyard, you may eat as many grapes as you desire, but do not put any in your basket.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 12:1–2; Mark 2:23; Luke 6:1–2"> When you go into your neighbor's ripe grain, you may pluck the heads of grain with your hand, but do not put a sickle to your neighbor's ripe grain.</span>
</div>
