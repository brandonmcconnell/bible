<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:14; Deuteronomy 13:9; Deuteronomy 6:1–10"> When you have come into the land that Yahweh your God is giving you as an inheritance, and when you possess it and live in it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Exodus 23:16; Ezekiel 44:30"> then you must take some of the first of all the harvest of the land that you have brought in from the land that Yahweh your God is giving you. You must put it in a basket and go to the place that Yahweh your God will choose as his sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 105:9–10; Deuteronomy 19:17; Genesis 26:3"> You must go to the priest who will be serving in those days and say to him, 'I acknowledge today to Yahweh your God that I have come to the land that Yahweh swore to our ancestors to give us.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Matthew 5:23–24; Hebrews 13:10–12"> The priest must take the basket out of your hand and set it down before the altar of Yahweh your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Genesis 46:27; Genesis 31:20"> You must say before Yahweh your God, 'My ancestor was a wandering Aramean. He went down into Egypt and stayed there, and his people were few in number. There he became a great, mighty, and populous nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Exodus 1:14; Exodus 5:23"> The Egyptians treated us badly and afflicted us. They made us do the work of slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 2:23–4; Ephesians 3:20–21; Exodus 3:9"> We cried out to Yahweh, the God of our fathers, and he heard our voice and saw our affliction, our labor, and our oppression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Deuteronomy 5:15; Psalms 78:12–13"> Yahweh brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand, with an outstretched arm, with great fearsomeness, with signs, and with wonders;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Psalms 105:44; Psalms 107:7–8"> and he has brought us to this place and has given us this land, a land that flows with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:2; Deuteronomy 18:4; Revelation 22:9"> Now look, I have brought the first of the harvest of the land that you, Yahweh, have given me.' You must set it down before Yahweh your God and worship before him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:11; Deuteronomy 12:7; Philippians 4:4"> and you must rejoice in all the good that Yahweh your God has done for you, for your house—you, and the Levite, and the foreigner who is among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 27:30; Numbers 18:24; Philippians 4:18–19"> When you have finished giving all the tithe of your harvest in the third year, that is, the year of tithing, then you must give it to the Levite, to the foreigner, to the fatherless, and to the widow, so that they may eat within your city gates and be filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 119:176; Psalms 119:153; Psalms 119:141"> You must say before Yahweh your God, 'I have brought out of my house the things that belong to Yahweh, and have given them to the Levite, to the foreigner, to the fatherless, and to the widow, according to all your commandments that you have given me. I have not transgressed any of your commandments, neither have I forgotten them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 7:20; Leviticus 21:11; Hosea 9:4"> I have not eaten any of it in my mourning, nor have I put it somewhere else when I was impure, nor have I given any of it in honor of the dead. I have listened to the voice of Yahweh my God; I have obeyed everything you have commanded me to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 63:15; Zechariah 2:13; Jeremiah 31:23"> Look down from the holy place where you live, from heaven, and bless your people Israel, and the land that you have given us, as you had sworn to our fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:1; John 14:15; 1 John 5:2–3"> Today Yahweh your God is commanding you to obey these statutes and decrees; you will therefore keep them and do them with all your heart and with all your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 24:7; Isaiah 12:2; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20"> You have declared today that Yahweh is your God, and that you will walk in his ways and keep his statutes, his commandments, and his decrees, and that you will listen to his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 14:2; Deuteronomy 28:9"> Today Yahweh has declared that you are a people who are his own possession, as he had promised you, and that you are to keep all his commandments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 28:1"> and he will set you high above all the other nations that he has made, and you will receive praise, fame, and honor. You will be a people that is set apart to Yahweh your God, just as he said.”</span>
