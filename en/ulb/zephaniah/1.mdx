<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Micah 5:13; 2 Kings 23:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will reach out with my hand over Judah and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will cut off every remnant of Baal from this place and the names of the idolatrous people among the priests,</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:20; Zechariah 2:13; Zephaniah 1:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be silent before the Lord Yahweh! <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:5; Amos 3:10; 1 Samuel 2:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">On that day I will punish all those who leap over the threshold,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">those who fill their master's house with violence and deceit.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Ezekiel 30:3; Zephaniah 1:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The great day of Yahweh is near, near and hurrying quickly!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The sound of the day of Yahweh will be that of a warrior crying bitterly!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 2:2; Isaiah 22:5; Revelation 6:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">That day will be a day of fury, a day of distress and anguish,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a day of storm and devastation, a day of darkness and gloom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a day of clouds and thick darkness.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Psalms 83:10; Jeremiah 2:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I will bring distress upon mankind, so that they will walk about like blind men</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">since they have sinned against Yahweh.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their blood will be poured out like dust, and their inner parts like dung.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:8; Ezekiel 7:19; Proverbs 11:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Neither their silver nor their gold will be able to save them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on the day of Yahweh's fury.
