<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 1:13; Psalms 83:4–7; Jeremiah 48:27–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I have heard the taunts of Moab and the reviling of the people of Ammon</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when they taunted my people and violated their borders.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 4:1–3; Zechariah 13:2; Psalms 138:4"> Then they will fear Yahweh, for he will taunt all the gods of the earth. Everyone will worship him, everyone from his own place, from every seashore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:4–9; Isaiah 43:3; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">You Cushites also will be pierced by my sword,</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Isaiah 22:2; Isaiah 47:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is the exultant city that lived without fear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that said in her heart, “I am, and nothing is my equal.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">How she has become a horror, a place for beasts to lie down in.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Everyone that passes by her will hiss and shake his fist at her.</span></span>
