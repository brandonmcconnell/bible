<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 24:14; Acts 25:21"> Now in those days, it came about that Caesar Augustus sent out a decree ordering that a census be taken of all the people living in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 4:24; Acts 26:30"> This was the first census made while Quirinius was governor of Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> So everyone went to his own city to be registered for the census.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:42; 1 Samuel 16:1"> Joseph also went up from Galilee, from the city of Nazareth, to Judea, to the city of David which is called Bethlehem, because he was of the house and family line of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:22–27; Matthew 1:18–19"> He went there to register along with Mary, who was engaged to him and was pregnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 33:11; Revelation 12:1–5; Micah 5:2"> Now it came about that while they were there, the time came for her to deliver her baby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 4:4; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Isaiah 53:2–3"> She gave birth to a son, her firstborn child, and she wrapped him in long strips of cloth and laid him in a manger, because there was no room for them in the inn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 31:39–40; Psalms 78:70–71; 1 Samuel 17:34–35"> There were shepherds in that region who were staying in the fields, guarding their flock at night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; 2 Corinthians 3:18; 1 Kings 8:11"> An angel of the Lord appeared to them, and the glory of the Lord shone around them, and they were filled with great fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 52:7"> Then the angel said to them, “Do not be afraid, because I bring you the good news that will bring great joy to all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Matthew 1:21; Acts 2:36"> Today a savior was born for you in the city of David! He is Christ the Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 53:1–2; Isaiah 7:11"> This is the sign that will be given to you: You will find a baby wrapped in strips of cloth and lying in a manger.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 103:20–21; Revelation 5:11; Psalms 148:2"> Suddenly there was together with the angel a great, heavenly army praising God and saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 5:1; Luke 19:38; Colossians 1:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Glory to God in the highest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and may there be peace on earth among people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with whom he is pleased.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 2:1–2; Matthew 2:9–11"> It came about that when the angels had gone away from them into heaven, the shepherds said to each other, “Let us now go to Bethlehem, and see this thing that has happened, which the Lord has made known to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:32; Luke 2:12; Luke 2:7"> They hurried there and found Mary and Joseph, and saw the baby lying in the manger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 66:16; Luke 8:39; Psalms 71:17–18"> After they had seen him, they made known what had been said to them about this child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 2:47; Luke 4:36; Luke 1:65–66"> All who heard it were amazed at what was spoken to them by the shepherds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 2:51; Luke 1:66; Proverbs 4:4"> But Mary kept thinking about all the things she had heard, treasuring them in her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 106:48; Isaiah 29:19; Acts 11:18"> The shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for everything that they had heard and seen, just as it had been spoken to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:31; Genesis 17:12; Galatians 4:4–5"> When it was the end of the eighth day, when he was circumcised, he was named Jesus, the name he had been given by the angel before he was conceived in the womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:1–8"> When the required number of days for their purification had passed, according to the law of Moses, Joseph and Mary<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The proper names Joseph and Mary were not in the original text, and are supplied for the reader from the third person plural verb.">a</a></sup>brought him up to the temple in Jerusalem to present him to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 22:29; Numbers 3:13"> As it is written in the law of the Lord, “Every male who opens the womb will be set apart to the Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 12:6–8; Leviticus 12:2; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> So they offered a sacrifice according to what was said in the law of the Lord, “a pair of doves or two young pigeons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Luke 2:38; Luke 1:6"> Behold, there was a man in Jerusalem, whose name was Simeon, and this man was righteous and devout. He was looking for the consolation of Israel, and the Holy Spirit was upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:51; Hebrews 11:5; Luke 9:27"> It had been revealed to him by the Holy Spirit that he would not see death before he had seen the Lord's Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 4:1; Luke 2:41; Luke 2:51"> Led by the Spirit, Simeon came into the temple. When the parents brought in the infant Jesus, to do for him according to the custom of the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 1:64; Luke 2:20; Luke 2:13–14"> he took him into his arms and praised God and he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Genesis 15:15; Philippians 1:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Now let your servant depart in peace, Lord, according to your word.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 49:6; Luke 2:10–11; Luke 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my eyes have seen your salvation,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 62:1–2; Isaiah 42:1–4; Psalms 98:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">which you have prepared in the presence of all peoples:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–3; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 26:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">A light for revelation to the Gentiles and glory to your people Israel.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 1:65–66; Isaiah 8:18; Luke 2:48"> His father and mother<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some ancient copies add to the text the name of his father, Joseph ; and some omit the mention of the mother. The most reliable copies have his father and mother .">b</a></sup>were amazed at what was said about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 8:14–15"> Simeon blessed them and said to Mary his mother, “Behold, this child is appointed for the downfall and rising up of many people in Israel and for a sign that is rejected—</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Matthew 12:24–35"> and a sword will pierce your own soul—so that the thoughts of many hearts may be revealed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Genesis 30:13; Acts 21:9"> A prophetess named Anna was there. She was the daughter of Phanuel from the tribe of Asher. She was very old. She had lived with her husband for seven years after her virginity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:5; Psalms 23:6; Acts 14:23"> and was a widow for eighty-four years. She never left the temple but was serving with fastings and prayers, night and day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 2:25; 2 Corinthians 9:15; Mark 15:43"> At that very hour she came near to them and began giving thanks to God and she spoke about the child to everyone who had been waiting for the redemption of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 2:4; Galatians 4:4–5; Deuteronomy 12:32"> When they had finished everything they were required to do according to the law of the Lord, they returned to Galilee, to their own town of Nazareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 2:52; Luke 1:80; Isaiah 11:1–5"> The child grew and became strong, increasing in wisdom, and the grace of God was upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Numbers 28:16; Deuteronomy 12:18"> His parents went every year to Jerusalem for the Festival of the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> When he was twelve years old, they again went up at the customary time for the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:21–23; Exodus 12:15; 2 Chronicles 25:17"> After they had stayed the full number of days for the feast, they began to return home. But the boy Jesus stayed behind in Jerusalem and his parents did not know it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 122:1–4; Isaiah 2:3; Psalms 42:4"> They assumed that he was with the group that was traveling with them, so they traveled a day's journey. Then they started looking for him among their relatives and friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> When they did not find him, they returned to Jerusalem and started looking for him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 5:17; Isaiah 49:1–2; Isaiah 50:4"> It came about that after three days, they found him in the temple, sitting in the middle of the teachers, listening to them and asking them questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Luke 4:32; Psalms 119:99"> All who heard him were amazed at his understanding and his answers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 2:49; Matthew 12:46; Luke 3:23"> When they saw him, they were astonished. His mother said to him, “Son, why have you treated us this way? Listen, your father and I have been anxiously searching for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 6:38; John 8:29; Malachi 3:1"> He said to them, “Why were you searching for me? Did you not know that I had to be about my Father's business?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some scholars translate “about my Father's business” as in my Father's house .">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Luke 18:34; Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32"> But they did not understand what he meant by those words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Daniel 7:28; Genesis 37:11"> Then he went back home with them to Nazareth and was obedient to them. His mother treasured all these things in her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 2:40; Proverbs 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 2:26"> But Jesus continued to grow in wisdom and stature, and increased in favor with God and people.</span>
