<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 23:24; Genesis 49:10; Luke 9:7"> In the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar—while Pontius Pilate was governor of Judea, Herod was tetrarch of Galilee, his brother Philip was tetrarch of the region of Ituraea and Trachonitis, and Lysanias was tetrarch of Abilene,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:6; John 18:24; Luke 1:80"> during the high priesthood of Annas and Caiaphas—the word of God came to John son of Zechariah in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 1:77; Matthew 3:5–6; Acts 13:24"> He went into all the region around the Jordan, preaching a baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; John 1:23; Luke 1:76–79"> As it is written in the book of the words of Isaiah the prophet,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“A voice of one calling out in the wilderness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">'Make ready the way of the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">make his paths straight.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:4; Isaiah 42:16; Isaiah 45:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Every valley will be filled,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and every mountain and hill will be made low,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the crooked roads will be made straight,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the uneven places will be built into roads,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 52:10; Isaiah 40:5; Psalms 98:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">and all flesh will see the salvation of God.'”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 23:33; Matthew 12:34; 1 Thessalonians 1:10"> So John said to the crowds who were coming out to be baptized by him, “You offspring of vipers! Who warned you to run away from the wrath that is coming?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:27–31; Acts 26:20; Isaiah 1:16–18"> Therefore, produce fruits that are worthy of repentance, and do not begin to say within yourselves, 'We have Abraham for our father,' for I tell you that God is able to raise up children for Abraham from these stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 7:19; John 15:6; Luke 13:9"> Even now the ax is set against the root of the trees. So every tree that does not produce good fruit is chopped down and thrown into the fire.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:37; Luke 3:14; Acts 16:30"> Then the crowds kept asking him, saying, “What then are we to do?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 58:7–11; 1 John 3:17; 1 Timothy 6:18"> He answered and said to them, “If someone has two tunics, he should share it with a person who has none, and the one having food should do the same.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 7:29; Luke 15:1–2; Luke 18:13"> Tax collectors also came to be baptized, and they said to him, “Teacher, what must we do?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 19:8; Ephesians 4:28; 1 Corinthians 6:10"> He said to them, “Do not collect more money than you have been ordered to collect.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5–6; Exodus 20:16; Exodus 23:1"> Some soldiers also asked him, saying, “What about us? What must we do?” He said to them, “Do not take money from anyone by force, and do not accuse anyone falsely. Be content with your wages.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 10:24; John 3:28–29; John 1:19–28"> Now as the people were eagerly expecting the Christ to come, everyone was wondering in their hearts concerning John, whether he might be the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Acts 1:5; Acts 11:15–16"> John answered by saying to them all, “As for me, I baptize you with water, but someone is coming who is more powerful than I, and I am not worthy even to untie the strap of his sandals. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:7; Matthew 13:30; Micah 4:12"> His winnowing fork is in his hand to thoroughly clear off his threshing floor and to gather the wheat into his storehouse. But he will burn up the chaff with fire that can never be put out.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:29–36; John 1:34; John 1:29"> With many other exhortations also, John preached good news to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 11:2; Mark 6:17–18"> When Herod the tetrarch had been reproved for marrying his brother's wife Herodias, and for all the other evil things that Herod had done,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:17–22; John 3:24; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16"> he added this to them all, that he locked John up in prison.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 1:32–34; Matthew 3:13–17; John 12:27–28"> Now it came about, when all the people were baptized, Jesus also was baptized, and while he was praying, the heavens opened,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; 2 Peter 1:17–18; John 1:32"> and the Holy Spirit in bodily form came down on him like a dove, and a voice came from heaven, “You are my Son, whom I love. I am pleased with you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Matthew 13:55; Numbers 4:3"> When Jesus began his ministry, he was about thirty years of age. He was the son (as it was assumed) of Joseph, the son of Heli,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> the son of Matthat, the son of Levi, the son of Melchi, the son of Jannai, the son of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Joseph was the son of Mattathias, the son of Amos, the son of Nahum, the son of Esli, the son of Naggai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> the son of Maath, the son of Mattathias, the son of Semein, the son of Josech, the son of Joda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 1:12"> Joda was the son of Joanan, the son of Rhesa, the son of Zerubbabel, the son of Salathiel, the son of Neri,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> the son of Melchi, the son of Addi, the son of Cosam, the son of Elmadam, the son of Er,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> the son of Joshua, the son of Eliezer, the son of Jorim, the son of Matthat, the son of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Levi was the son of Simeon, the son of Judah, the son of Joseph, the son of Jonam, the son of Eliakim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:5; 2 Samuel 5:14; Zechariah 12:12"> the son of Melea, the son of Menna, the son of Mattatha, the son of Nathan, the son of David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ruth 4:18–22; Matthew 1:3–6; Isaiah 11:1–2"> the son of Jesse, the son of Obed, the son of Boaz, the son of Salmon, the son of Nahshon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ruth 4:19–20; Genesis 49:8; Genesis 38:29"> Nahshon was the son of Amminadab, the son of Admin, the son of Arni, the son of Hezron, the son of Perez, the son of Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 21:3; Joshua 24:2; 1 Chronicles 1:24–28"> the son of Jacob, the son of Isaac, the son of Abraham, the son of Terah, the son of Nahor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 11:12–21; Genesis 10:24–25"> the son of Serug, the son of Reu, the son of Peleg, the son of Eber, the son of Shelah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 5:28–32; Genesis 10:21–22; Genesis 9:1"> Shelah was the son of Cainan, the son of Arphaxad, the son of Shem, the son of Noah, the son of Lamech,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:1–3; Genesis 5:6–28"> the son of Methuselah, the son of Enoch, the son of Jared, the son of Mahalaleel, the son of Cainan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Isaiah 64:8; 1 Corinthians 15:45"> the son of Enos, the son of Seth, the son of Adam, the son of God.</span>
</div>
