<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Matthew 25:14–30; James 4:3"> Jesus also said to the disciples, “There was a certain rich man who had a manager, and it was reported to him that this manager was wasting his possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:9–10; 1 Peter 4:5; Revelation 20:12"> So the rich man called him and said to him, 'What is this that I hear about you? Give an account of your management, for you can no longer be manager.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Isaiah 10:3; Proverbs 18:9"> The manager said to himself, 'What should I do, since my master is taking away my management job? I do not have strength to dig, and I am ashamed to beg.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; Jeremiah 4:22; James 3:15"> I know what I will do, so that when I am removed from my management job, people will welcome me into their houses.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:41–42; Matthew 18:24"> Then the manager called for each one of his master's debtors, and he asked the first one, 'How much do you owe to my master?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:10; Luke 16:12; Luke 16:9"> He said, 'A hundred baths of olive oil.' He said to him, 'Take your bill, sit down quickly, and write fifty.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Songs 8:11–12; Luke 20:9; Luke 20:12"> Then the manager said to another, 'How much do you owe?' He said, 'A hundred cors of wheat.' He said to him, 'Take your bill, and write eighty.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5; Ephesians 5:8; Proverbs 6:6–8"> The master then commended the unrighteous manager because he had acted shrewdly. For the children of this world are more shrewd in dealing with their own people than are the children of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 19:21; 1 Timothy 6:17–19; Daniel 4:27"> I say to you, make friends for yourselves by means of unrighteous wealth, so that when it is gone, they may welcome you into the eternal dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 16:11–12; Matthew 25:21; Luke 19:17"> He who is faithful in very little is also faithful in much, and he who is unrighteous in very little is also unrighteous in much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 12:33; James 2:5; Proverbs 8:18–19"> If you have not been faithful in using unrighteous wealth, who will trust you with true wealth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 19:13–26; Matthew 25:14–29; Job 1:21"> If you have not been faithful in using other people's property, who will give you money of your own?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Matthew 6:24; James 4:4"> No servant can serve two masters, for either he will hate the one and love the other, or else he will be devoted to one and despise the other. You cannot serve God and wealth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:35; Luke 20:47; Jeremiah 6:13"> Now the Pharisees, who were lovers of money, heard all these things, and they ridiculed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Proverbs 21:2; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> He said to them, “You justify yourselves in the sight of men, but God knows your hearts. That which is exalted among men is detestable in the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 11:9–14; Matthew 21:32; Acts 3:24–25"> The law and the prophets were in effect until John came. From that time on, the gospel of the kingdom of God is preached, and everyone tries to force their way into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:18; Luke 21:33; 1 Peter 1:25"> But it is easier for heaven and earth to pass away than for one stroke of a letter of the law to become invalid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 19:9; Mark 10:11–12; Matthew 5:31–32"> Everyone who divorces his wife and marries another commits adultery, and he who marries one who is divorced from her husband commits adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:49; Amos 6:4–6; Luke 18:24–25"> Now there was a certain rich man who was clothed in purple and fine linen and was enjoying every day his great wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:2; 1 Samuel 2:8; James 2:5"> A certain beggar named Lazarus was laid at his gate, covered with sores,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 15:27; Mark 7:28; John 6:12"> and longing to eat what fell from the rich man's table. Even the dogs came and licked his sores.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Psalms 49:16–19; James 1:11"> It came about that the beggar died and was carried away by the angels to Abraham's side. The rich man also died and was buried,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Luke 16:28; Matthew 18:9"> and in Hades, being in torment, he lifted up his eyes and saw Abraham far away and Lazarus at his side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 4:10; Revelation 22:1; Revelation 7:16–17"> So he cried out and said, 'Father Abraham, have mercy on me and send Lazarus, that he may dip the tip of his finger in water and cool my tongue, for I am in anguish in this flame.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 6:24; 1 John 2:15; Psalms 17:14"> But Abraham said, 'Child, remember that in your lifetime you received your good things, and Lazarus in like manner evil things. But now he is comforted here, and you are in agony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 3:36; Psalms 49:14; Psalms 50:22"> Besides all this, a great chasm has been put in place, so that those who want to cross over from here to you cannot, and no one can cross over from there to us.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> The rich man said, 'I beg you, Father Abraham, that you would send him to my father's house—</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:40; Psalms 49:12–13; 1 Thessalonians 4:6"> for I have five brothers—in order that he may warn them, so that it may not be that they come into this place of torment.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 15:21; Luke 24:27; Luke 16:16"> But Abraham said, 'They have Moses and the prophets; let them listen to them.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 16:9–11; Luke 16:24; Luke 13:5"> The rich man replied, 'No, Father Abraham, but if someone would go to them from the dead, they will repent.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3; Acts 19:8; John 11:43–53"> But Abraham said to him, 'If they do not listen to Moses and the prophets, neither will they be persuaded if someone rises from the dead.'”</span>
