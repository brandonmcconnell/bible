<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 6:1–27; Luke 18:35; 2 Kings 2:18–22"> Jesus entered and was passing through Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 18:24–27; 2 Chronicles 17:5–6"> Behold, there was a man there named Zacchaeus. He was a chief tax collector and was rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 23:8; Luke 9:7–9; John 12:21"> He was trying to see who Jesus was, but could not see over the crowd, because he was small in height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 9:10; Luke 5:19; 1 Kings 10:27"> So he ran on ahead of the people and climbed up into a sycamore tree to see him, because Jesus was about to pass that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; John 14:23; Hebrews 13:2"> When Jesus came to the place, he looked up and said to him, “Zacchaeus, come down quickly, for today I must stay at your house.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:29; Acts 16:34; Acts 16:15"> So he hurried and came down and welcomed him joyfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 15:2; Luke 7:39; Luke 7:34"> When everyone saw this, they all complained, saying, “He has gone in to visit a man who is a sinner.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:6; Numbers 5:7; Luke 18:22–23"> Zacchaeus stood and said to the Lord, “Look, Lord, the half of my goods I give to the poor, and if I have cheated anyone of anything, I will restore four times the amount.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; Luke 13:16; Romans 4:16"> Jesus said to him, “Today salvation has come to this house, because he too is a son of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Matthew 1:21; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> For the Son of Man came to seek and to save the people who are lost.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 17:20; Acts 1:6; 2 Thessalonians 2:1–3"> As they heard these things, he continued speaking and told a parable, because he was near to Jerusalem, and they thought that the kingdom of God was about to appear immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–30; Luke 19:12–27; Luke 20:9"> He said therefore, “A certain nobleman went into a far country to receive for himself a kingdom and then to return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–15; John 12:26; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> He called ten of his servants, and gave them ten minas, and said to them, 'Conduct business until I come back.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Acts 7:51–52; Acts 3:14–15"> But his citizens hated him and sent a delegation after him, saying, 'We will not have this man rule over us.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:1–5; Romans 14:10–12; Psalms 2:4–6"> It happened when he came back again, having received the kingdom, he commanded the servants to whom he had given the money to be called to him, that he might know what profit they had made by doing business.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 2:18–26; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> The first came before him, saying, 'Lord, your mina has made ten minas more.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 16:10; Matthew 25:21; Revelation 2:26–29"> The nobleman said to him, 'Well done, good servant. Because you were faithful in very little, you will have authority over ten cities.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 4:20; Matthew 13:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12"> The second came, saying, 'Your mina, lord, has made five minas.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 1 Corinthians 15:41–42; 1 Corinthians 3:8"> The nobleman said to him, 'You take charge over five cities.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:24; Proverbs 26:13–16; James 4:17"> Another came, saying, 'Lord, here is your mina, which I kept safely in a cloth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:24–25; Romans 8:7; 1 John 4:18"> for I was afraid of you, because you are a demanding person. You take up what you did not put in, and you reap what you did not sow.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:16; Matthew 12:37; Job 15:5–6"> The nobleman said to him, 'By your own words I will judge you, you wicked servant. You knew that I am a demanding person, that I take up what I did not put in, and that I reaped what I did not sow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:25–27; Romans 2:4–5; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> Then why did you not put my money in the bank, so that when I returned I would have collected it with interest?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 16:2; Luke 12:20"> The nobleman said to them that stood by, 'Take away from him the mina, and give it to him that has the ten minas.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 16:2; 2 Samuel 7:19; Isaiah 55:8–9"> They said to him, 'Lord, he has ten minas.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 John 1:8; Luke 8:18; Matthew 21:43"> 'I say to you, that everyone who has will be given more, but from him that has not, even that which he has will be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Psalms 21:8–9; Matthew 22:7"> But these enemies of mine, those who did not want me to reign over them, bring them here and kill them before me.'”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 9:51; Hebrews 12:2; 1 Peter 4:1"> When he had said these things, he went on ahead, going up to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Matthew 21:1–11"> It came about that when he came near to Bethphage and Bethany, to the hill that is called Olivet, he sent two of the disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 22:8–13; Luke 19:32; 1 Samuel 10:2–9"> saying, “Go into the next village. As you enter, you will find a colt that has never been ridden. Untie it and bring it to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 24:1; Psalms 50:10–12; Mark 11:3–6"> If anyone asks you, 'Why are you untying it?' say, 'The Lord has need of it.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 22:13"> Those who were sent went and found the colt just as Jesus had told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> As they were untying the colt, the owners said to them, “Why are you untying the colt?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; John 10:35; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> They said, “The Lord has need of it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 11:7–8; Matthew 21:7; John 12:14"> They brought it to Jesus, and they threw their cloaks upon the colt and set Jesus on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; Matthew 21:8"> As he went, they spread their cloaks on the road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 12:12–13; Ezra 3:10–13; Matthew 21:1"> As he was now approaching the place where the Mount of Olives descends, the whole multitude of the disciples began to rejoice and praise God with a loud voice for all the mighty works which they had seen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Luke 13:35; Psalms 118:22–26"> saying, “Blessed is the king who comes in the name of the Lord! Peace in heaven and glory in the highest!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 4:1–2; Matthew 23:13; Acts 4:16–18"> Some of the Pharisees in the multitude said to him, “Teacher, rebuke your disciples.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:11; Isaiah 55:12; Matthew 3:9"> Jesus answered and said, “I tell you, if these were silent, the stones would cry out.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 13:34–35; John 11:35; Psalms 119:136"> When Jesus approached the city, he wept over it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Hebrews 10:26–29; Isaiah 48:18"> saying, “If only you had known in this day, even you, the things which bring you peace! But now they are hidden from your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 21:20–24; Isaiah 29:1–4; Ezekiel 4:2"> For the days will come upon you when your enemies will build a barricade around you, and surround you, and press in on you from every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 21:6; Luke 13:34–35; John 3:18–21"> They will strike you down to the ground and your children with you. They will not leave one stone upon another, because you did not recognize it when God was trying to save you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 21:12–13; Mark 11:15–17; John 2:13–17"> Jesus entered the temple and began to cast out those who were selling,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Jeremiah 7:11; Psalms 93:5"> saying to them, “It is written, 'My house will be a house of prayer,' but you have made it a den of robbers.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 26:55; Mark 11:27–33"> So Jesus was teaching daily in the temple. The chief priests and the scribes and the leaders of the people wanted to kill him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:3; Acts 16:14; John 7:46–49"> but they could not find a way to do it, because all the people were listening to him intently.</span>
</div>
</div>
