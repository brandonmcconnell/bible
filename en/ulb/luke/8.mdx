<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Romans 10:15; Matthew 9:35"> It happened soon afterward that Jesus began traveling around to different cities and villages, preaching and proclaiming the good news about the kingdom of God. The twelve were with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:9; Matthew 27:55–56; Mark 15:40–41"> as well as certain women who had been healed of evil spirits and diseases: Mary who was called Magdalene, from whom seven demons had been driven out;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 4:46–53; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Acts 9:36–39"> Joanna, the wife of Chuza, Herod's manager; Susanna; and many others, who, out of their possessions, provided for their needs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 4:1–9; Matthew 13:2–9"> While a large crowd of people was gathering, and people were coming to him from town after town, he told a parable:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; James 1:23–24; Mark 4:15"> “A farmer went out to sow his seed. As he sowed, some fell beside the road and it was trampled underfoot, and the birds of the sky devoured it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7–8; Luke 8:13; Mark 4:5–6"> Some fell on the rock, and as soon as it grew up, it withered away, because it had no moisture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Luke 8:14; Luke 21:34"> Some fell among thorns, and the thorns grew up together with the seed and choked it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 2:7"> But some fell on good soil and produced a crop that was a hundred times greater.” After Jesus had said these things, he called out, “Whoever has ears to hear, let him hear.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 4:10; Matthew 13:10; Matthew 13:18"> His disciples asked him what this parable meant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; Mark 4:11; Matthew 11:25"> He said, “The knowledge of the secrets of the kingdom of God has been given to you, but for others I speak in parables, so that 'seeing they may not see, and hearing they may not understand.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Mark 4:14–20; James 1:21"> Now this is the meaning of the parable: The seed is the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–14; Revelation 12:9; James 1:23–24"> The ones along the path are those who have heard, but then the devil comes and takes away the word from their hearts, so they may not believe and be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 6:4; John 5:35; Proverbs 12:12"> The ones on the rock are those who, when they hear the word, receive it with joy. But they have no root; they believe for a while, and in a time of testing they fall away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; 1 Timothy 6:17"> The seeds that fell among the thorns are people who hear the word, but as they go on their way, they are choked by the cares and riches and pleasures of this life, and their fruit does not mature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Galatians 5:22–26; Colossians 1:10"> But the seed that fell on the good soil, these are the ones who, hearing the word with an honest and good heart, hold it securely and bear fruit with patient endurance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 11:33; Matthew 5:15–16; Mark 4:21–22"> No one lights a lamp and covers it with a bowl or puts it under a bed. Rather, he puts it on a lampstand so that everyone who enters may see the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 12:2–3; Matthew 10:26; Mark 4:22"> For nothing is hidden that will not be made known, nor is anything secret that will not be known and come into the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:12; Luke 19:26; Mark 4:23–25"> So listen carefully, for to the one who has, more will be given to him, but the one who does not have, even what he thinks he has will be taken away from him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 12:46–50; Mark 3:31–35; Mark 3:21"> Then his mother and brothers came to him, but they could not get near him because of the crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 1:14; Mark 6:3; Matthew 13:55–56"> He was told, “Your mother and your brothers are standing outside, wanting to see you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 11:27–28; James 1:22; John 13:17"> But Jesus answered and said to them, “My mother and my brothers are those who hear the word of God and do it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 8:23–27; Mark 4:35–41; Mark 6:45"> Now one day he got into a boat with his disciples, and he said to them, “Let us go over to the other side of the lake.” They set sail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 93:3–4; Psalms 124:2–4; Psalms 148:8"> But as they sailed he fell asleep. A terrible windstorm came down on the lake, and their boat was filling with water, and they were in danger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 107:25–29; Luke 4:39; Psalms 104:6–9"> Then Jesus' disciples came over to him and woke him up, saying, “Master! Master! We are about to die!” He awoke and rebuked the wind and the raging of the water, and they ceased, and there was a calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 8:26; Proverbs 30:4; Mark 4:40–41"> Then he said to them, “Where is your faith?” and they were afraid. While they were afraid they were also amazed, and they asked one another, “Who then is this, that he commands even the winds and the water, and they obey him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 5:1–20; Matthew 8:28–34"> They sailed to the region of the Gerasenes, which is across the lake from Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 5:2–5; 1 Samuel 19:24; Numbers 19:16"> When Jesus stepped on the land, he was met by a certain man from the city who had demons. For a long time he had worn no clothes, and he did not live in a house but among the tombs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; 2 Peter 2:4; Mark 5:6–8"> When he saw Jesus, he cried out and fell down before him and he said with a loud voice, “What have you to do with me, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I beg you, do not torment me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 5:3–5; Luke 9:39; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> For Jesus had commanded the unclean spirit to come out of the man. For many times it had seized him, and though he was bound with chains and shackles and kept under guard, he had broken his chains and he would be driven by the demon into the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 26:53; Luke 8:2; Matthew 8:29"> Then Jesus asked him, “What is your name?” and he said, “Legion,” for many demons had entered into him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Philippians 2:10–11; Job 2:5"> They kept begging him not to send them into the abyss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3; Revelation 20:7"> Now a large herd of pigs was there feeding on the hillside. The demons begged him to let them go into them, and he gave them permission.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; John 8:44; Luke 8:22–23"> So the demons came out of the man and went into the pigs, and the herd rushed down the steep slope into the lake and was drowned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 5:14; Matthew 8:33; Matthew 28:11"> When those tending the pigs saw what had happened, they ran off and told about it in the city and countryside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 10:39; Acts 22:3; 1 John 3:8"> So the people went out to see what had happened, and they came to Jesus and found the man from whom the demons had gone out. He was sitting at the feet of Jesus, clothed and in his right mind; and they were afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 4:24"> Then those who had seen it told them how the man who had been possessed by demons had been healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 16:39; Luke 8:28; Luke 10:10–11"> Then all the people of the region of the Gerasenes asked Jesus to depart from them, for they were overwhelmed with great fear. So he got into the boat so they could start back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 9:13–16; Mark 5:18–20; Psalms 145:3–12"> The man from whom the demons had gone out begged him to let him go with him, but Jesus sent him away, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 17:15–18; 1 Timothy 5:8; Deuteronomy 10:21"> “Return to your home and give a full account of what God has done for you.” The man went on his way, proclaiming throughout the whole city what Jesus had done for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 9:1; Mark 5:21; Mark 6:20"> Now when Jesus returned, the crowd welcomed him, for they were all expecting him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 5:22–43; Luke 13:14; Acts 18:8"> Behold, a man named Jairus, who was one of the leaders of the synagogue, came and fell down at Jesus' feet, and he begged him to come to his house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 7:12; Luke 8:45; Job 1:18–19"> because his only daughter, a girl of about twelve years of age, was dying. As Jesus was on his way, the crowds of people pressed together around him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 5:25–26; Matthew 9:20–22; Leviticus 15:25–33"> Now a woman was there who had been bleeding for twelve years and had spent all her money<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Scholars are divided whether the phrase [she] had spent all her money should be included here.">a</a></sup>, and could not be healed by anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Mark 5:27–28; Deuteronomy 22:12; Acts 19:12"> She came behind Jesus and touched the edge of his coat, and immediately her bleeding stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 5:30–32; Luke 9:13; Luke 5:5"> Jesus said, “Who was it who touched me?” When all denied it, Peter said, “Master, the crowds of people are all around you and they are pressing in against you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 5:17; 1 Peter 2:9"> But Jesus said, “Someone did touch me, for I know that power has gone out from me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 5:33; Luke 17:15–16; 1 Corinthians 2:3"> When the woman saw that she could not escape notice, she came trembling and fell down before him. In the presence of all the people she declared why she had touched him and how she had been immediately healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Matthew 9:22; Hebrews 4:2"> Then he said to her, “Daughter, your faith has made you well. Go in peace.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 5:35–43; Isaiah 7:12; Luke 7:6"> While he was still speaking, someone came from the synagogue leader's house, saying, “Your daughter is dead. Do not trouble the teacher any longer.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 11:40; John 11:25; Mark 5:36"> But when Jesus heard this, he answered Jairus, “Do not be afraid; only believe, and she will be healed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Mark 14:33; Matthew 6:5–6; 1 Kings 17:19–23"> When he came to the house, he allowed no one to enter with him, except Peter and John and James, and the father of the child and her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 23:27; John 11:4; Zechariah 12:10"> Now all were mourning and wailing for her, but he said, “Do not weep; she is not dead but asleep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Luke 16:14; Isaiah 53:3; John 11:39"> But they laughed at him, knowing that she was dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; John 11:43; Mark 1:31"> But he took her by the hand and called out, saying, “Child, get up!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 11:44; 1 Kings 17:21–23; Mark 5:43"> Her spirit returned, and she rose up immediately. He ordered them to get her something to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Luke 5:14"> Her parents were astonished, but he ordered them to tell no one what had happened.</span>
