<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:23–27; Luke 19:47–48; Acts 6:12"> It came about one day, as Jesus was teaching the people in the temple and preaching the gospel, the chief priests and the scribes came to him with the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; Mark 11:28–33; John 2:18"> They spoke, saying to him, “Tell us by what authority you do these things, or who it is who gave you this authority.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 15:2–3; Luke 22:68; Colossians 4:6"> He answered and said to them, “I will also ask you a question, and you tell me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:18; Matthew 11:7–19; Matthew 17:11–12"> The baptism of John: was it from heaven or from men?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:34; John 1:15–18; John 1:30"> They reasoned with themselves, saying, “If we say, 'From heaven,' he will say, 'Then why did you not believe him?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 11:9; John 10:41"> But if we say, 'From men,' all the people will stone us, for they are persuaded that John was a prophet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; 2 Peter 3:3; Isaiah 41:28"> So they answered that they did not know where it came from.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:4–5; Matthew 21:27; Mark 11:33"> Jesus said to them, “Neither will I tell you by what authority I do these things.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 21:33–46; Mark 12:1–12; Isaiah 5:1–7"> He told the people this parable, “A man planted a vineyard, rented it out to vine growers, and went into another country for a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Nehemiah 9:26; Matthew 21:34–36"> At the appointed time he sent a servant to the vine growers, that they should give him of the fruit of the vineyard. But the vine growers beat him, and sent him away empty-handed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 10:1; Hebrews 11:36–37; 1 Thessalonians 2:2"> He then sent yet another servant and they also beat him, treated him shamefully, and sent him away empty-handed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> He also sent yet a third and they also wounded him, and threw him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 3:17; John 3:16–17; Jeremiah 36:7"> So the lord of the vineyard said, 'What will I do? I will send my beloved son. Maybe they will respect him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Hebrews 1:2; Acts 2:23"> But when the vine growers saw him, they discussed among themselves, saying, 'This is the heir. Let us kill him, that the inheritance may be ours.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:12; Mark 12:6–9; Matthew 21:37–40"> They threw him out of the vineyard and killed him. What then will the lord of the vineyard do to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:27; Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:41"> He will come and destroy these vine growers, and will give the vineyard to others.” When they heard it, they said, “May it never be!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 118:22; Isaiah 28:16; Acts 4:11"> But Jesus looked at them, and said, “What is the meaning of that which is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'The stone that the builders rejected has become the cornerstone'?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; Zechariah 12:3; Daniel 2:44–45"> Every one who falls on that stone will be broken to pieces. But on whomever it falls, it will crush.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 19:47–48; Luke 20:14; Matthew 21:45–46"> So the scribes and the chief priests sought to lay hands on him in that very hour, for they knew that he had spoken this parable against them. But they were afraid of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 11:54; Matthew 22:15–22; Matthew 27:2"> Watching him carefully, they sent out spies who pretended to be righteous, that they might find fault with his speech, so as to deliver him up to the rule and to the authority of the governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4–5; Matthew 26:49–50; Matthew 22:16"> They asked him, saying, “Teacher, we know that you say and teach rightly, and are not influenced by anyone's position, but you teach the truth about the way of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:37; Mark 12:14–17; Acts 5:37"> Is it lawful for us to pay taxes to Caesar, or not?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:18; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Luke 5:22"> But Jesus understood their craftiness, and said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 18:28; Acts 11:28; Acts 26:32"> “Show me a denarius. Whose image and name is on it?” They said, “Caesar's.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 13:6–7; Mark 12:17; 1 Peter 2:13–17"> He said to them, “Then give to Caesar the things that are Caesar's, and to God the things that are God's.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 20:20; Proverbs 26:4–5; Titus 1:10"> They were not able to find fault with what he had said in front of the people, but marveling at his answer, they were silent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 4:1–2; 1 Corinthians 15:12; Mark 12:18–27"> When some of the Sadducees came to him, the ones who say that there is no resurrection,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Genesis 38:8; Genesis 38:11"> they asked him, saying, “Teacher, Moses wrote to us that if a man's brother dies, having a wife, and being childless, the man should take the brother's wife, and have a child for his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20; Jeremiah 22:30"> There were seven brothers and the first took a wife, and died childless,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and the second as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> The third took her, and likewise the seven also left no children, and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:4; Ecclesiastes 9:5; Hebrews 9:27"> Afterward the woman also died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 22:24–28; Mark 12:19–23"> In the resurrection then, whose wife will she be? For the seven had her as their wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 16:8; Luke 17:27; Hebrews 13:4"> Jesus said to them, “The sons of this world marry and are given in marriage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; John 5:29; Daniel 12:2–3"> But those who are regarded as worthy in that age to receive the resurrection from the dead will neither marry nor be given in marriage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Philippians 3:21; Matthew 22:30"> Neither can they die anymore, for they are equal to the angels and are sons of God, being sons of the resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Mark 12:26–27; Acts 7:30–32"> But that the dead are raised, even Moses showed, in the place concerning the bush, where he calls the Lord the God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:10–11"> Now he is not the God of the dead, but of the living, because all live to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 23:9; Mark 12:28–34; Matthew 22:34–40"> Some of the scribes answered, “Teacher, you have answered well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Mark 12:34; Proverbs 26:5"> For they did not dare ask him any more questions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 1:1; Matthew 22:41–45"> Jesus said to them, “How do they say that the Christ is David's son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Acts 2:34–35; Hebrews 1:13"> For David himself says in the Book of Psalms,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">The Lord said to my Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">'Sit at my right hand,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 19:27; Psalms 110:1; Revelation 19:14–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">until I make your enemies your footstool.'</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Luke 1:31–35; Matthew 1:23"> David therefore calls the Christ 'Lord', so how is he David's son?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 15:10; Mark 12:38–39; Mark 8:34"> In the hearing of all the people he said to his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 11:43; Mark 12:38–39; Luke 14:7"> “Beware of the scribes, who desire to walk in long robes, and love special greetings in the marketplaces, and chief seats in the synagogues, and chief places at feasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 12:40; James 3:1; Titus 1:16"> They also devour widows' houses, and for a show they make long prayers. Men like this will receive greater condemnation.”</span>
</div>
</div>
