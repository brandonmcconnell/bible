<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 27:1–2; Matthew 27:11–14; Luke 22:66"> The whole company of them rose up and brought Jesus before Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 19:12; Luke 23:14"> They began to accuse him, saying, “We found this man perverting our nation, forbidding to give tribute to Caesar, and saying that he himself is Christ, a king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 1:49; 1 Timothy 6:13; Luke 22:70"> Pilate asked him, saying, “Are you the King of the Jews?” Jesus answered him and said, “You say so.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:22"> Pilate said to the chief priests and the multitudes, “I find no fault in this man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 1:14; John 1:43; John 2:11"> But they were insisting, saying, “He stirs up the people, teaching throughout all Judea, beginning from Galilee even to this place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 5:37; Luke 13:1"> So when Pilate heard this, he asked whether the man was a Galilean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Luke 13:31; Matthew 14:1"> When he discovered that he was under Herod's authority, he sent Jesus to Herod, who himself also was at Jerusalem in those days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:7–9; Matthew 14:1; Mark 6:14"> When Herod saw Jesus, he was very glad, because he had wanted to see him for a long time. He had heard about him and he hoped to see some miracle done by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 27:14; Matthew 7:6; 1 Peter 2:23"> Herod questioned Jesus in many words, but Jesus answered him nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 11:53; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:5"> The chief priests and the scribes stood, violently accusing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Matthew 27:27–30; Psalms 69:19–20"> Herod with his soldiers insulted Jesus and they mocked him. Then they dressed him in elegant clothes and sent him back to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 4:27; Revelation 17:13–14; Psalms 83:4–6"> For Herod and Pilate had become friends with each other that very day, for before this they had been enemies with each other.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:14; Matthew 27:21–23; John 18:38"> Pilate then called together the chief priests and the rulers and the crowd of people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:4; Hebrews 7:26; Luke 23:1–2"> and said to them, “You brought to me this man like a man who is leading the people to act badly, and see, I, having questioned him before you, find no fault in this man concerning those things of which you accuse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> No, nor does Herod, for he sent him back to us, and see, nothing worthy of death has been done by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Luke 23:22; Mark 15:15"> I will therefore punish him, and release him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The best ancient copies omit Luk. 23:17, Now Pilate was obligated to release to the Jews one prisoner at the feast .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:36; Acts 3:14; Acts 22:22"> But they cried out all together, saying, “Away with this man, and release to us Barabbas!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Luke 23:5; Luke 23:2"> Barabbas was a man who had been put into prison for a certain rebellion in the city and for murder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 15:15; Matthew 14:8–9; Matthew 27:19"> Pilate addressed them again, desiring to release Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:22–25; Mark 15:13; John 19:15"> But they shouted, saying, “Crucify him, crucify him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 23:14; Luke 23:20; 1 Peter 3:18"> He said to them a third time, “Why, what evil has this man done? I have found nothing deserving the death penalty in him. Therefore after punishing him, I will release him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 11:8; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 22:12–13"> But they were insistent with loud voices, demanding for him to be crucified. Their voices convinced Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 17:15"> So Pilate decided to grant their demand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Mark 15:6–7; Mark 15:15"> He released the one they asked for who had been put in prison for rioting and murder. But he delivered up Jesus to their will.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:32–44; Mark 15:21–32; Acts 6:9"> As they led him away, they seized one Simon of Cyrene, coming from the country, and they laid the cross on him to carry, following Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 8:52; Luke 8:2; Mark 15:40"> A great crowd of the people, and of women who grieved and mourned for him, were following him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Song of Songs 8:4; Song of Songs 5:8; Song of Songs 1:5"> But turning to them, Jesus said, “Daughters of Jerusalem, do not weep for me, but weep for yourselves and for your children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:19; Luke 21:23–24; Mark 13:17–19"> For see, the days are coming in which they will say, 'Blessed are the barren and the wombs that did not bear, and the breasts that did not nurse.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 6:16; Hosea 10:8; Isaiah 2:19"> Then they will begin to say to the mountains, 'Fall on us,' and to the hills, 'Cover us.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; John 15:6; Proverbs 11:31"> For if they do these things while the tree is green, what will happen when it is dry?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:27; Isaiah 53:12; Matthew 27:38"> Other men, two criminals, were led away with him to be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 15:22–23; John 19:17–18; John 18:32"> When they came to the place that is called “The Skull,” there they crucified him, and the criminals—one on his right and one on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Acts 7:60; 1 Peter 3:9"> Jesus said, “Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing.” Then they cast lots, dividing up his garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 22:17; Isaiah 42:1; 1 Peter 2:4"> The people stood watching while the rulers also were mocking him, saying, “He saved others. Let him save himself, if he is the Christ of God, the chosen one.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Matthew 27:48; Matthew 27:34"> The soldiers also ridiculed him, approaching him, offering him vinegar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and saying, “If you are the King of the Jews, save yourself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:26; Matthew 27:37; John 19:19–22"> There was also a sign over him, “This is the King of the Jews.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:35"> One of the criminals who was hanging there insulted him by saying, “Are you not the Christ? Save yourself and us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Luke 12:5; Revelation 15:4"> But the other rebuked him, saying, “Do you not fear God, since you are under the same sentence?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–41; Ezra 9:13; 1 Peter 1:19"> We indeed are here justly, for we are receiving what we deserve for our deeds. But this man did nothing wrong.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Romans 10:9–14; Acts 16:31; 1 John 5:11–13"> Then he said, “Jesus, remember me when you come into your kingdom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Psalms 50:15; 1 Timothy 1:15–16"> Jesus said to him, “Truly I say to you, today you will be with me in paradise.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Mark 15:33–34; Amos 8:9"> It was now about the sixth hour, and darkness came over the whole land until the ninth hour</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 9:3–8"> as the sun's light failed. Then the curtain of the temple was split in two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 31:5; Acts 7:59; John 19:30"> Crying with a loud voice, Jesus said, “Father, into your hands I commit my spirit.” Having said this, he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:39; Luke 23:41"> When the centurion saw what was done, he glorified God, saying, “Surely this was a righteous man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 18:13; Acts 2:37; Jeremiah 31:19"> When all the multitudes who came together to witness this sight saw the things that were done, they returned beating their breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 8:2; Psalms 38:11; Luke 23:55"> But all those who knew him, and the women who followed him from Galilee, stood at a distance, watching these things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Acts 11:24; Acts 10:22"> Behold, there was a man named Joseph, who was a member of the Council. He was a good and righteous man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Luke 2:38; Isaiah 8:12"> This man had not agreed with the decision of the Council and their action. He was from the Judean town of Arimathea; and he was looking for the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 19:38–42"> This man, approaching Pilate, asked for the body of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 53:9; Mark 15:46"> He took it down, wrapped it in fine linen, and placed it in a tomb that was cut in stone, where no one had ever been laid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:31; Mark 15:42"> It was the Day of the Preparation, and the Sabbath was about to begin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:61; Mark 15:47"> The women who had come with Jesus out of Galilee followed and saw the tomb and how his body was laid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Luke 24:1; Mark 16:1; Deuteronomy 5:14"> They returned, and prepared spices and ointments.<br />Then on the Sabbath they rested according to the commandment.</span>
</div>
