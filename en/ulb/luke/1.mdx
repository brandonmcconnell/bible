<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:10; Matthew 2:1; Nehemiah 12:4"> In the days of Herod, king of Judea, there was a certain priest named Zechariah, from the division of Abijah. His wife was from the daughters of Aaron, and her name was Elizabeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:1; Genesis 17:1; Philippians 2:15"> They were both righteous before God, obeying all the commandments and ordinances of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Genesis 18:11; 1 Samuel 1:2"> But they had no child, because Elizabeth was barren, and they were both very old by this time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 24:19; 2 Chronicles 31:2"> Now it came about that Zechariah was in God's presence, carrying out the priestly duties in the order of his division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:11; Exodus 30:7–8; 1 Chronicles 23:13"> According to the customary way of choosing which priest would serve, he had been chosen by lot to enter into the temple of the Lord to burn incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:17; Revelation 8:3; Hebrews 4:14"> The whole crowd of people was praying outside at the hour when the incense was burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:26–27; Acts 10:3–4; Judges 13:3"> Now an angel of the Lord appeared to him and stood at the right side of the incense altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:22; Judges 6:22; Luke 2:9–10"> When Zechariah saw him, he was terrified and fear fell on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:21; Acts 10:31; Genesis 25:21"> But the angel said to him, “Do not be afraid, Zechariah, because your prayer has been heard. Your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son. You will call his name John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:58; Proverbs 23:24; Genesis 21:6"> You will have joy and gladness, and many will rejoice at his birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Numbers 6:2–4; Luke 7:33"> For he will be great in the sight of the Lord. He must never drink wine or strong drink, and he will be filled with the Holy Spirit from his mother's womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Matthew 21:32; Malachi 3:1"> Many of the people of Israel will be turned to the Lord their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 4:5–6; Matthew 11:14; Luke 1:76"> He will go before the face of the Lord in the spirit and power of Elijah, to turn the hearts of the fathers to the children and the disobedient to the wisdom of the righteous—to make ready for the Lord a people prepared for him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 38:22; Genesis 17:17; Luke 1:7"> Zechariah said to the angel, “How can I know this? For I am an old man and my wife is very old.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 1:26; Matthew 18:10; Daniel 9:21–23"> The angel answered and said to him, “I am Gabriel, who stands in the presence of God. I was sent to speak to you, to bring you this good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:26; Ezekiel 24:27; Luke 1:22"> Behold! You will be silent, unable to speak, until the day these things take place. This is because you did not believe my words, which will be fulfilled at the right time.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27"> Now the people were waiting for Zechariah. They were surprised that he was spending so much time in the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 1:62; John 13:24; Acts 21:40"> But when he came out, he could not speak to them. They realized that he had seen a vision while he was in the temple. He kept on making signs to them and remained silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; 2 Kings 11:5–7"> It came about that when the days of his service were over, he went to his house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> After these days, his wife Elizabeth conceived and for five months she kept herself hidden. She said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 30:22–23; Isaiah 54:1–4; Genesis 21:1–2"> “This is what the Lord has done for me when he looked at me with favor in order to take away my shame before people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; Luke 1:19; John 7:41"> In the sixth month, the angel Gabriel was sent from God to a city in Galilee named Nazareth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:16"> to a virgin engaged to a man whose name was Joseph. He belonged to the house of David, and the virgin's name was Mary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 6:12; Daniel 10:19; Luke 1:30"> He came to her and said, “Greetings, you who are highly favored! The Lord is with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:17; Luke 1:12; Acts 10:4"> But she was very confused by his words and she wondered what kind of greeting this could be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Romans 8:31; Luke 12:32"> The angel said to her, “Do not be afraid, Mary, for you have found favor with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:21; Matthew 1:25"> See, you will conceive in your womb and bear a son. You will call his name 'Jesus.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; Isaiah 16:5; 2 Samuel 7:11–13"> He will be great and will be called the Son of the Most High. The Lord God will give him the throne of his ancestor David.</span>

<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Judges 13:8–12; Acts 9:6"> Mary said to the angel, “How will this happen, since I have not slept with any man?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; John 1:49; Matthew 1:20"> The angel answered and said to her, “The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and the power of the Most High will come over you. So the holy one to be born will be called the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> See, your relative Elizabeth has also conceived a son in her old age. This is the sixth month for her, she who was called barren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Jeremiah 32:27; Mark 10:27"> For nothing will be impossible for God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Romans 4:20–21; Psalms 119:38; Psalms 116:16"> Mary said, “See, I am the female servant of the Lord. Let it be for me according to your message.” Then the angel left her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> She went into the house of Zechariah and greeted Elizabeth.</span>

<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 20:29; John 11:40; Luke 11:27–28"> Blessed is she who believed that there would be a fulfillment of the things that were told her from the Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:1–10; Psalms 34:2–3; Psalms 35:9"> Mary said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“My soul praises the Lord,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 35:9; 1 Timothy 2:3; Habakkuk 3:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">and my spirit has rejoiced in God my savior.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 138:6; Malachi 3:12; 1 Corinthians 1:26–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">For he has looked at the low condition of his female servant.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For see, from now on all generations will call me blessed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 126:2–3; Psalms 111:9; Ephesians 3:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">For he who is mighty has done great things for me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and his name is holy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 103:17–18; Exodus 20:6; Genesis 17:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">His mercy lasts from generation to generation for those who fear him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 98:1; Isaiah 52:10; 1 Samuel 2:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has displayed strength with his arm;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he has scattered those who were proud about the thoughts of their hearts.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; James 4:10; Psalms 107:40–41"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has thrown down princes from their thrones</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he has raised up those of low condition.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 107:8–9; Luke 6:21; Psalms 34:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has filled the hungry with good things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the rich he has sent away empty.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Psalms 98:3; Micah 7:20; Isaiah 44:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has given help to Israel his servant,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so as to remember to show mercy</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Psalms 105:6–10; Genesis 17:19; Psalms 132:11–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">(as he said to our fathers) to Abraham and his descendants forever.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Mary stayed with Elizabeth about three months and then returned to her house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Genesis 21:2–3; Luke 2:6–7"> Now the time had come for Elizabeth to deliver her baby and she gave birth to a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Luke 1:14; Romans 12:15"> Her neighbors and her relatives heard that the Lord had shown his great mercy to her, and they rejoiced with her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Genesis 17:12; Leviticus 12:3; Philippians 3:5"> Now it happened on the eighth day that they came to circumcise the child. They would have called him “Zechariah,” after the name of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Luke 1:13; 2 Samuel 12:25; Luke 1:63"> But his mother answered and said, “No. He will be called John.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> They said to her, “There is no one among your relatives who is called by this name.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Luke 1:22"> They made signs to his father as to how he wanted him to be named.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 1:60; Isaiah 30:8; Luke 1:13"> His father asked for a writing tablet and wrote, “His name is John.” They all were astonished at this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Luke 1:20; Exodus 4:15–16; Ezekiel 33:22"> Immediately his mouth was opened and his tongue was freed. He spoke and praised God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Luke 1:39; Acts 5:11; Luke 7:16"> Fear came on all who lived around them. All these matters were spread throughout all the hill country of Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Acts 11:21; Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51"> All who heard them stored them in their hearts, saying, “What then will this child become?” For the hand of the Lord was with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Luke 1:41; Luke 1:15"> His father Zechariah was filled with the Holy Spirit and prophesied, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Psalms 106:48; Luke 7:16; Psalms 41:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Praised be the Lord, the God of Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for he has come to help and he has accomplished redemption for his people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Ezekiel 29:21; 2 Samuel 22:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has raised up a horn of salvation for us</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the house of his servant David</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Acts 3:21–24; Romans 1:2; 2 Peter 1:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">(as he spoke by the mouth of his holy prophets from long ago),</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Psalms 106:10; Jeremiah 23:6; Zephaniah 3:15–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">salvation from our enemies</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and from the hand of all who hate us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13–18; Micah 7:20; Psalms 106:45"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will do this to show mercy to our fathers</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and to remember his holy covenant,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73" data-xref="Genesis 22:16–18; Psalms 105:9; Deuteronomy 7:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">the oath that he spoke to Abraham our father.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7; Zephaniah 3:15–17; Isaiah 54:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">He swore to grant to us that we, having been delivered out of the hand of our enemies,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">would serve him without fear,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v75" data-v="75" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Ephesians 2:10; Titus 2:11–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">in holiness and righteousness before him all our days.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v76" data-v="76" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 40:3–5; Mark 1:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yes, and you, child, will be called a prophet of the Most High,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for you will go before the face of the Lord to prepare his paths,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to prepare people for his coming,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v77" data-v="77" data-xref="Acts 5:31; Acts 2:38; Acts 10:43"> <span className="pl pl-1">to give knowledge of salvation to his people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">by the forgiveness of their sins.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v78" data-v="78" data-xref="Malachi 4:2; 2 Peter 1:19; Revelation 22:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">This will happen because of the tender mercy of our God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because of which the sunrise from on high will come to help us,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v79" data-v="79" data-xref="Isaiah 9:2; Matthew 4:16; Acts 26:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">to shine on those who sit in darkness and in the shadow of death.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will do this to guide our feet into the path of peace.”</span></span>
</div>
