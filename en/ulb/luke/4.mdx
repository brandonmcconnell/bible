<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:1–11; Acts 10:38; Mark 1:12–13"> Then Jesus, being full of the Holy Spirit, returned from the Jordan River, and was led by the Spirit in the wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Hebrews 4:15; Deuteronomy 9:9"> where for forty days he was tempted by the devil. He ate nothing during those days, and at the end of that time he was hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 4:3; Luke 3:22"> The devil said to him, “If you are the Son of God, command this stone to become bread.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Matthew 4:4; Matthew 6:31"> Jesus answered him, “It is written, 'Man does not live on bread alone.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; 1 Corinthians 15:52; Matthew 4:8"> Then the devil led Jesus up and showed him all the kingdoms of the world in an instant of time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 12:31; 1 John 5:19; John 14:30"> The devil said to him, “I will give to you all this authority and all their splendor, for they have been given to me, and I can give it to anyone I want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 8:28; Psalms 72:11; Isaiah 45:14"> So then, if you will bow down and worship me, it will be yours.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Deuteronomy 10:20; Matthew 4:10"> But Jesus answered and said to him, “It is written, 'You will worship the Lord your God, and you will serve only him.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 4:5–6; Luke 4:3; Matthew 8:29"> Then the devil led Jesus to Jerusalem and put him on the very highest point of the temple building, and said to him, “If you are the Son of God, throw yourself down from here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 91:11–12; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Corinthians 11:14"> For it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'He will give orders to his angels regarding you, to protect you,'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 91:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">and, 'They will lift you up in their hands,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so that you will not strike your foot against a stone.'”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; Hebrews 3:8–9; 1 Corinthians 10:9"> Answering him, Jesus said, “It is said, 'Do not put the Lord your God to the test.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; James 4:7; Matthew 4:11"> When the devil had finished testing Jesus, he went away and left him until another time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 4:23–25; Acts 10:37; Matthew 4:12"> Then Jesus returned to Galilee in the power of the Spirit, and news about him spread throughout the entire surrounding region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Matthew 9:8; Matthew 9:35"> Then he began to teach in their synagogues and he was praised by all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:2; Acts 13:14–16; Matthew 2:23"> He came into Nazareth, where he had been raised, and as was his custom, he entered the synagogue on the Sabbath day and he stood up to read aloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1–3; Acts 13:15; Acts 7:42"> The scroll of the prophet Isaiah was handed to him. He opened the scroll and found the place where it was written,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1; Matthew 11:5; Psalms 146:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">“The Spirit of the Lord is upon me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because he anointed me to tell good news to the poor.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He has sent me to proclaim freedom to the captives,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and recovery of sight to the blind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to set free those who are oppressed,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 61:2; Isaiah 63:4; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">to proclaim the year of the Lord's favor.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 4:17; John 8:2; Luke 19:48"> Then he rolled up the scroll, gave it back to the attendant, and sat down. The eyes of all in the synagogue were fixed on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 4:25–26; Acts 3:18; Acts 2:16–18"> He began to speak to them, “Today this scripture has been fulfilled in your hearing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 13:54–56; Psalms 45:2; Acts 6:10"> Everyone there spoke well of him and they were amazed at the gracious words which were coming out of his mouth, and they asked, “Is this not the son of Joseph?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 4:46–53; Matthew 11:23–24; Mark 2:1–12"> He said to them, “Surely you will say this proverb to me, 'Doctor, heal yourself. Whatever we heard that you did in Capernaum, do the same in your hometown.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 13:57; John 4:44; Mark 6:4–5"> But he said, “Truly I say to you, no prophet is received in his own hometown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 5:17; 1 Kings 17:1; Matthew 20:15"> But in truth I tell you that there were many widows in Israel during the time of Elijah, when the sky was shut up for three years and six months, and a great famine came upon all the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 17:9–24; Matthew 11:21; Obadiah 1:20"> But Elijah was sent to none of them, but only to Zarephath in Sidon, to a widow living there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 5:1–27; John 17:12; Matthew 12:4"> There were many lepers in Israel during the time of Elisha the prophet, but none of them were healed except Naaman the Syrian.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 5:33; 2 Chronicles 24:20–21; Jeremiah 38:6"> All the people in the synagogue were filled with rage when they heard these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:59; Acts 21:28–32; Numbers 15:35"> They got up, forced him out of the town, and led him to the cliff of the hill on which their town was built, so they might throw him off the cliff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:39; John 8:59; John 18:6–7"> But he passed through the middle of them and he went to another place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 1:21–28; Matthew 4:13; Acts 13:50–2"> Then he went down to Capernaum, a city in Galilee, and he began to teach them on the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 4:36; Matthew 7:28–29; John 6:63"> They were astonished at his teaching, because he spoke with authority.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 1:23"> Now in the synagogue there was a man who had the spirit of an unclean demon, and he cried out with a loud voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 1:24; James 2:19; Matthew 8:29"> “Ah! What do we have to do with you, Jesus of Nazareth? Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are—the Holy One of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Luke 4:39; Matthew 8:26"> Jesus rebuked the demon, saying, “Do not speak! Come out of him!” When the demon had thrown the man down in the middle of them, he came out of him, and did not harm him in any way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 4:32; 1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 12:22–23"> All the people were very amazed, and they kept talking about it with one another. They said, “What kind of words are these? He commands the unclean spirits with authority and power and they come out.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 1:45; Luke 4:14; Isaiah 52:13"> So news about him began to spread into every part of the surrounding region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 8:14–15; Mark 1:29–31; John 11:22"> Then Jesus left the synagogue and entered into the house of Simon. Now Simon's mother-in-law was suffering with a high fever, and they pleaded with him on her behalf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Luke 4:35; Luke 8:24"> So he stood over her and rebuked the fever, and it left her. Immediately she got up and started serving them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 1:32–34; Matthew 8:16–17; Mark 5:23"> When the sun was setting, people brought to Jesus everyone who was sick with various kinds of diseases. He laid his hands on every one of them and healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:34; James 2:19; Luke 4:34–35"> Demons also came out from many of them, crying out and saying, “You are the Son of God!” Jesus rebuked the demons and would not let them speak, because they knew that he was the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 1:35–38; John 4:34"> When daybreak came, he went out into a solitary place. Crowds of people were looking for him and came to the place where he was. They tried to keep him from going away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 1:38–39; 2 Timothy 4:2; John 9:4"> But he said to them, “I must also preach the good news about the kingdom of God to many other cities, because this is the reason I was sent here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Mark 1:39; Luke 4:15"> Then he continued to preach in the synagogues throughout Judea.</span>
