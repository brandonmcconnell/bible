<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 12:41–44; John 8:20; Matthew 27:6"> Jesus looked up and saw the rich men who were putting their gifts into the treasury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 12:42"> He saw a certain poor widow putting in two mites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 10:34; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3"> So he said, “Truly I say to you, this poor widow put in more than all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 2:44–45; Luke 15:12; Acts 4:34"> All of these gave gifts out of their abundance. But this widow, out of her poverty, put in all she had to live on.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 2:20; Matthew 24:1–51; Mark 13:1–37"> As some spoke of the temple, how it was decorated with beautiful stones and offerings, he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:2; Mark 13:2; Lamentations 5:18"> “As for these things that you see, the days will come when not one stone will be left on another which will not be torn down.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 1:6–7; Mark 13:14; Mark 13:3–4"> So they asked him, saying, “Teacher, when will these things happen? What will be the sign when these things are about to happen?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Mark 13:5–6; Ephesians 5:6"> Jesus answered, “Be careful that you are not deceived. For many will come in my name, saying, 'I am he,' and, 'The time is near.' Do not go after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 21:28; Matthew 24:6–8; Mark 13:7–8"> When you hear of wars and riots, do not be terrified, for these things must happen first, but the end will not happen immediately.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:19–20; Mark 13:8; 2 Chronicles 15:5–6"> Then he said to them, “Nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:29–30; Luke 21:25–27"> There will be great earthquakes, and in various places famines and plagues. There will be terrifying events and great signs from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12–14; Acts 5:40; Acts 22:30"> But before all of these things, they will lay their hands on you and will persecute you, delivering you over to the synagogues and prisons, bringing you before kings and governors because of my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:12; Philippians 1:28; 2 Thessalonians 1:5"> It will lead to an opportunity for your testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 12:11–12; Mark 13:11; Matthew 10:19–20"> Therefore resolve in your hearts not to prepare your defense ahead of time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; Ephesians 6:19; Luke 12:12"> for I will give you words and wisdom that all your adversaries will not be able to resist or contradict.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 12:53; Revelation 6:9; Micah 7:5–6"> But you will be delivered up also by parents, brothers, relatives, and friends, and they will put some of you to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:19; Luke 6:22; John 15:21"> You will be hated by everyone because of my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 10:30; 1 Samuel 14:45; Luke 12:7"> But not a hair of your head will perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Matthew 10:22; James 1:3"> In your endurance you will gain your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 19:43; Daniel 9:27; Mark 13:14"> When you see Jerusalem surrounded by armies, then know that its destruction is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:1; Genesis 19:17; Luke 17:31–33"> Then let those in Judea flee to the mountains, let those who are in the city leave it, and those who are out in the country must not enter the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 9:7; Isaiah 65:12–16; Isaiah 63:4"> For these are days of vengeance, so that all the things that are written will be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 13:17; Matthew 24:19; Luke 23:29"> Woe to them who are pregnant and to them who are nursing in those days! For there will be great distress upon the land, and wrath to this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:25; Revelation 11:2; Isaiah 63:18"> They will fall by the edge of the sword, and they will be led captive into all the nations, and Jerusalem will be trampled by the Gentiles until the times of the Gentiles are fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Joel 2:30–31; Revelation 6:12–14"> There will be signs in the sun, in the moon, and in the stars, and on the earth. The nations will be in distress, anxious because of the roar of the sea and waves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Deuteronomy 28:65–67; 2 Peter 3:10–12"> There will be men fainting from fear and from expectation of the things which are coming upon the world. For the powers of the heavens will be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 24:30; Revelation 1:7; Mark 13:26"> Then they will see the Son of Man coming in a cloud with power and great glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 8:23; Isaiah 60:1–2; Ephesians 4:30"> But when these things begin to happen, stand up and lift up your heads, because your deliverance is coming near.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:32–35; Mark 13:28–30"> Jesus told them a parable, “Look at the fig tree, and all the trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 12:57"> When they sprout buds, you see for yourselves and know that summer is already near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 5:9; Hebrews 10:37; 1 Peter 4:7"> So also, when you see these things happening, you know that the kingdom of God is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 13:30; Matthew 24:34; Matthew 23:36"> Truly I say to you, this generation will not pass away until all these things take place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Mark 13:31; Matthew 24:35"> Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will never pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 4:19; Luke 12:45–46; Luke 12:40"> But pay attention to yourselves, so that your hearts are not burdened with the effects of drinking and intoxication, and the worries of life, and then that day will close on you suddenly like a trap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; Psalms 11:6; Jeremiah 48:43–44"> For it will come upon everyone living on the face of the whole earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 John 2:28; Matthew 25:13; 1 Peter 5:8"> But be alert at all times, praying that you may be strong enough to escape all these things that will take place, and to stand before the Son of Man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 22:39; Mark 11:19; Matthew 21:17"> So during the days he was teaching in the temple, and at night he went out and stayed on the Mount of Olives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 8:1–2"> All of the people came early in the morning to hear him in the temple.</span>
