<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:6–23; Matthew 26:2–5; John 11:55–57"> Now the Festival of Unleavened Bread was approaching, which is called the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 11:47–53; Matthew 12:14; Luke 20:19"> The chief priests and the scribes discussed how they could put Jesus to death, for they were afraid of the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 13:2; Mark 14:10–11; John 13:26–27"> Then Satan entered into Judas Iscariot, who was one of the twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Acts 5:24; Luke 22:52"> Judas went to the chief priests and captains and discussed with them how he would betray Jesus to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 26:15–16; Acts 1:18; Matthew 27:3–5"> They were glad, and agreed to give him money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 14:2; Matthew 26:5"> He consented, and looked for an opportunity to deliver him to them away from the crowd.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:18; Mark 14:12–16; 1 Corinthians 5:7"> Then came the day of unleavened bread, on which the Passover lamb had to be sacrificed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 14:13–16; Acts 4:19; Acts 4:13"> So Jesus sent Peter and John, saying, “Go and prepare for us the Passover meal, so that we may eat it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> They said to him, “Where do you want us to make preparations?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 16:4; Matthew 26:18–19; Acts 8:26–29"> He answered them, “Listen, when you have entered the city, a man bearing a pitcher of water will meet you. Follow him into the house that he goes into.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:28; Revelation 3:20; Luke 19:34"> Then say to the master of the house, 'The Teacher says to you, “Where is the guest room, where I will eat the Passover with my disciples?”'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 16:14–15; Acts 1:13; John 2:25"> He will show you a large furnished upper room. Make the preparations there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 21:33; Luke 19:32; John 2:5"> So they went, and found everything as he had said to them. Then they prepared the Passover meal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 26:20; Mark 14:17; Deuteronomy 16:6–7"> When the time came, he sat down with the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 12:50; John 4:34; John 17:1"> Then he said to them, “I have greatly desired to eat this Passover with you before I suffer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 14:15; Revelation 19:9; Luke 22:30"> For I say to you, I will not eat it again, until it is fulfilled in the kingdom of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:19; Psalms 116:13; Deuteronomy 8:10"> Then Jesus took a cup, and when he had given thanks, he said, “Take this, and share it among yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 26:29; Luke 22:16; Mark 14:25"> For I say to you, I will not drink of the fruit of the vine again until the kingdom of God comes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:23–29; 1 Corinthians 10:16; Mark 14:22–24"> Then he took bread, and when he had given thanks, he broke it, and gave to them, saying, “This is my body which is given for you. Do this in remembrance of me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 26:28; 1 Corinthians 11:25; Zechariah 9:11"> He took the cup in the same way after supper, saying, “This cup is the new covenant in my blood, which is poured out for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 13:26; John 13:21–22"> But pay attention. The one who betrays me is with me at the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 2:23; Psalms 109:6–15; Psalms 55:12–15"> For the Son of Man indeed goes as it has been determined. But woe to that man through whom he is betrayed!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:22–25; Mark 14:19; Matthew 26:22"> They began to discuss among themselves which one of them it might be who would do this.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 9:46; Mark 9:34; Mark 10:37–41"> Then there arose also a quarrel among them about which of them was considered to be greatest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 20:25–28; Mark 10:41–45"> He said to them, “The kings of the Gentiles are masters over them, and the ones who have authority over them are referred to as those who do good to their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Luke 9:48; Matthew 18:3–5"> But it must not be like this with you. Instead, let the one who is the greatest among you become like the youngest, and let the one who is the most important become like the one who serves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Luke 12:37; John 13:5–16"> For who is greater, the one who sits at the table, or the one who serves? Is it not the one who sits at the table? Yet I am among you as one who serves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:18; Hebrews 4:15; Acts 1:25"> But you are the ones who have continued with me in my temptations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 25:34; 2 Timothy 2:12; James 2:5"> I give to you a kingdom, even as my Father has given a kingdom to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Matthew 8:11; Revelation 2:26–27"> that you may eat and drink at my table in my kingdom, and you will sit on thrones judging the twelve tribes of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Zechariah 3:1; Job 1:6–12"> Simon, Simon, be aware, Satan asked to have you, that he might sift you as wheat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–10; John 21:15–17; John 17:9–11"> But I have prayed for you, that your faith may not fail. After you have turned back again, strengthen your brothers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 14:31; Matthew 26:33–35; Mark 14:29"> Peter said to him, “Lord, I am ready to go with you both to prison and to death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 13:38; Matthew 26:34; Mark 14:30"> Jesus replied, “I tell you, Peter, the rooster will not crow this day, before you deny three times that you know me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 10:9–10; Luke 10:4; Psalms 23:1"> Then Jesus said to them, “When I sent you out without a purse, a bag of provisions, or shoes, did you lack anything?” They answered, “Nothing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 10:22–25; 1 Thessalonians 3:4; 1 Thessalonians 2:14–15"> Then he said to them, “But now, the one who has a purse, let him take it, and also a bag of provisions. The one who does not have a sword should sell his cloak and buy one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Luke 23:32; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For I say to you, what is written about me must be fulfilled, 'He was counted with the lawless ones.' For what is predicted about me is being fulfilled.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 22:49; Matthew 26:52–54; John 18:36"> Then they said, “Lord, look! Here are two swords.” He said to them, “It is enough.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 21:37; John 18:1–2; Matthew 21:1"> Jesus went, as he often did, to the Mount of Olives, and the disciples followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Matthew 26:36–46; Luke 22:46"> When they arrived, he said to them, “Pray that you do not enter into temptation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 26:39; Mark 14:35; Luke 18:11"> He went away from them about a stone's throw, and he knelt down and prayed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:27–28; Matthew 26:39; Matthew 26:42"> saying, “Father, if you are willing, remove this cup from me. Nevertheless not my will, but yours be done.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 4:11; Matthew 26:53; Psalms 91:11–12"> Then an angel from heaven appeared to him, strengthening him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 1:12; Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 22:12–21"> Being in agony, he prayed more earnestly, and his sweat became like great drops of blood falling down upon the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 14:40–41; Matthew 26:43; Matthew 26:40"> When he rose up from his prayer, he came to the disciples, and found them sleeping because of their sorrow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 22:40; Jonah 1:6; Luke 21:34–36"> and asked them, “Why are you sleeping? Rise and pray, that you may not enter into temptation.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 14:41–50; John 18:2–11; Luke 22:3–6"> While he was still speaking, behold, a crowd appeared, with Judas, one of the twelve, leading them. He came near to Jesus to kiss him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Mark 14:44–46; 2 Samuel 20:9–10; Proverbs 27:6"> but Jesus said to him, “Judas, are you betraying the Son of Man with a kiss?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 22:38"> When those who were around Jesus saw what was happening, they said, “Lord, should we strike with the sword?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Mark 14:47; Romans 12:19"> Then one of them struck the servant of the high priest, and cut off his right ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Romans 12:21; 2 Corinthians 10:1; 1 Peter 2:21–23"> Jesus said, “That is enough!” He touched his ear, and healed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 22:4; Mark 14:48–49; John 17:12"> Jesus said to the chief priests, to the captains of the temple, and to elders who came against him, “Do you come out as against a robber, with swords and clubs?</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 12:27; Ephesians 6:12; Acts 26:18"> When I was daily with you in the temple, you did not lay your hands on me. But this is your hour, and the authority of darkness.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Mark 14:53–54; Matthew 26:57–58; John 18:12–17"> Seizing him, they led him away, and brought him into the high priest's house. But Peter followed from a distance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Psalms 26:4–5; 2 Corinthians 6:15–17; Mark 14:66–72"> After they had kindled a fire in the middle of the courtyard and had sat down together, Peter sat in the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 18:17; Mark 14:6; Mark 14:17"> A certain female servant saw him as he sat in the light of the fire, and looked straight at him and said, “This man also was with him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="1 John 1:9; John 18:25; Matthew 26:70"> But Peter denied it, saying, “Woman, I do not know him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 18:25; Matthew 26:71–72; Mark 14:69–70"> After a little while someone else saw him, and said, “You are also one of them.” But Peter said, “Man, I am not.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 18:26–27; Mark 14:69–70; Matthew 26:73–74"> After about an hour another man insisted and said, “Truly this man also was with him, for he is a Galilean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Matthew 26:74–75; Luke 22:34; Mark 14:71–72"> But Peter said, “Man, I do not know what you are saying.” Immediately, while he was speaking, a rooster crowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Luke 22:34; Isaiah 57:15–18; Jeremiah 31:18–20"> Turning, the Lord looked at Peter, and Peter remembered the word of the Lord, when he said to him, “Before a rooster crows today you will deny me three times.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Matthew 26:75; 1 Corinthians 10:12; Mark 14:72"> Peter went outside and wept bitterly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="John 18:22; Mark 14:55–65; Job 16:9–10"> Then the men guarding Jesus mocked and beat him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Judges 16:25; Judges 16:21"> They put a cover over him and asked him, saying, “Prophesy! Who is the one who hit you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; Matthew 27:39"> They spoke many other things against Jesus, blaspheming him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Matthew 27:1; Mark 15:1; Acts 22:5"> As soon as it was day, the elders of the people gathered together, both chief priests and scribes. They led him into the council</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Matthew 11:3–5; John 8:43–45; Matthew 26:63–68"> and said, “If you are the Christ, tell us.” But he said to them, “If I tell you, you will not believe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Luke 20:3–7"> and if I ask you, you will not answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Psalms 110:1; Mark 16:19"> But from now on, the Son of Man will be seated at the right hand of the power of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Luke 23:3; Matthew 27:11; Matthew 26:64"> They all said, “Then you are the Son of God?” Jesus said to them, “You say that I am.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Matthew 26:65–66; Mark 14:63–64"> They said, “Why do we still need a witness? For we ourselves have heard from his own mouth.”</span>
</div>
