<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 4:2; Romans 12:12; 1 Thessalonians 5:17"> Then he spoke a parable to them about how they should always pray and not become discouraged,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; Psalms 8:1–4; Luke 18:4"> saying, “In a certain city there was a judge who did not fear God and did not respect people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Jeremiah 5:28; Deuteronomy 27:19"> Now there was a widow in that city, and she came often to him, saying, 'Help me get justice against my opponent.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12–13; Luke 16:3; Luke 12:17"> For a long time he was not willing to help her, but after a while he said to himself, 'Though I do not fear God or respect man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 11:8; Luke 18:39; Judges 16:16"> yet because this widow causes me trouble, I will help her get justice, so that she does not wear me out by her constant coming.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 7:13"> Then the Lord said, “Listen to what the unjust judge says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35–37; 2 Peter 3:9; Luke 11:13"> Now will not God also bring justice to his chosen ones who cry out to him day and night? Will he delay long over them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:23–26; 2 Peter 3:8–9; Matthew 24:9–13"> I say to you that he will bring justice to them speedily. Even so, when the Son of Man comes, will he indeed find faith on the earth?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Proverbs 30:12; Isaiah 65:5"> Then he also spoke this parable to some who were persuaded in themselves that they were righteous and who despised other people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 21:31–32; 1 Kings 8:30; 2 Kings 20:5"> “Two men went up into the temple to pray—the one was a Pharisee and the other was a tax collector.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 6:5; Isaiah 65:5; Revelation 3:17"> The Pharisee stood and prayed these things about himself, 'God, I thank you that I am not like other people—robbers, unrighteous people, adulterers—or even like this tax collector.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Matthew 23:23–24; Matthew 9:14"> I fast two times every week. I give tithes of all that I get.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; 1 John 1:8–10; 1 Timothy 1:15"> But the tax collector, standing at a distance, would not even lift up his eyes to heaven, but hit his breast, saying, 'God, have mercy on me, a sinner.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 14:11; Luke 16:15; James 4:10"> I say to you, this man went back down to his house justified rather than the other, because everyone who exalts himself will be humbled, but everyone who humbles himself will be exalted.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 10:13–16; Matthew 19:13–15; 1 Samuel 1:24"> The people were also bringing to him their infants so that he might touch them, but when the disciples saw it, they rebuked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 18:3–4; 1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 7:14"> But Jesus called them to him, saying, “Permit the little children to come to me, and do not forbid them. For the kingdom of God belongs to such ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 18:3; Mark 10:15; Psalms 131:1–2"> Truly I say to you, whoever will not receive the kingdom of God like a child will definitely not enter it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 10:17–31; Acts 16:30; Matthew 19:16–30"> A certain ruler asked him, saying, “Good teacher, what must I do to inherit eternal life?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 15:14–16; James 1:17; Hebrews 7:26"> Jesus said to him, “Why do you call me good? No one is good, except God alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Deuteronomy 5:16–21; Romans 13:9"> You know the commandments—do not commit adultery, do not kill, do not steal, do not testify falsely, honor your father and mother.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 3:6; Romans 10:2–3; Mark 10:20–21"> The ruler said, “All these things I have obeyed from the time I was a youth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 12:33; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; Matthew 19:21"> When Jesus heard that, he said to him, “One thing you still lack. You must sell all that you have and distribute it to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven—and come, follow me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; 1 John 2:15; Job 31:24–25"> But when the ruler heard these things, he became extremely sad, for he was very rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; James 2:5–7; Mark 10:23–27"> Then Jesus, seeing him, became very sad<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Scholars are divided whether the phrase: [he] became very sad should be included here. Compare vs 23.">a</a></sup>and said, “How difficult it is for those who are rich to enter the kingdom of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 23:24"> For it is easier for a camel to go through a needle's eye, than for a rich person to enter the kingdom of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 13:23"> Those hearing it said, “Then who can be saved?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Matthew 19:26; Luke 1:37"> Jesus answered, “The things which are impossible with people are possible with God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 10:28; Philippians 3:7; Matthew 19:27"> Peter said, “Well, we have left everything that is our own and have followed you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 10:29–31; Matthew 10:37–39; Luke 14:26–28"> Jesus then said to them, “Truly, I say to you that there is no one who has left house, or wife, or brothers, or parents, or children, for the sake of the kingdom of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5–6; Job 42:10; Revelation 2:17"> who will not receive much more in this world, and in the world to come, eternal life.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 20:17–19; Isaiah 53:1–12; Psalms 22:1–31"> After he gathered the twelve to himself, he said to them, “See, we are going up to Jerusalem, and all the things that have been written by the prophets about the Son of Man will be accomplished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 23:11; Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3"> For he will be given over to the Gentiles, and will be mocked, and shamefully treated, and spit upon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:3–4; Luke 24:7; Matthew 27:63"> After whipping him, they will kill him, and on the third day he will rise again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32; Luke 24:25"> They understood none of these things, and this word was hidden from them, and they did not understand the things that were said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:29–34; Mark 10:46–52; Luke 16:20–21"> It came about that, as Jesus approached Jericho, a certain blind man was sitting by the road begging,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 21:10–11; Luke 15:26"> and hearing a crowd going by, he asked what was happening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; John 19:19; John 12:35–36"> They told him that Jesus of Nazareth was passing by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Luke 18:39; Matthew 15:22"> So the blind man cried out, saying, “Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 32:26–28; Psalms 141:1; Luke 18:15"> The ones who were walking ahead rebuked the blind man, telling him to be quiet. But he cried out all the more, “Son of David, have mercy on me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 20:31–34; Mark 10:48–52"> Jesus stood still and commanded that the man be brought to him. Then when the blind man was near, Jesus asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Kings 3:5–15; Matthew 20:21–22; Philippians 4:6"> “What do you want me to do for you?” He said, “Lord, I want to receive my sight.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 8:3; Luke 8:48; Luke 17:19"> Jesus said to him, “Receive your sight. Your faith has healed you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 146:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:10–12; Isaiah 43:21"> Immediately he received his sight and followed him, glorifying God. All the people, when they saw this, gave praise to God.</span>
</div>
