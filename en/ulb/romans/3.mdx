<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 2:25–29; Genesis 25:32; Ecclesiastes 6:11"> Then what advantage does the Jew have? And what is the benefit of circumcision?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 9:4; Acts 7:38; 1 Corinthians 9:17"> It is great in every way. First of all, the Jews were entrusted with revelation from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:13; Hebrews 4:2; Romans 9:6"> For what if some Jews were without faith? Will their unbelief abolish God's faithfulness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Psalms 116:11; Galatians 2:17"> May it never be. Instead, let God be found to be true, even though every man is a liar. As it has been written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“That you might be shown to be righteous in your words,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and that you might prevail when you come into judgment.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 6:19; Galatians 3:15; Romans 9:18–20"> But if our unrighteousness shows the righteousness of God, what can we say? Can we say that God is unrighteous to bring his wrath upon us? (I am using a human argument.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:25; Acts 17:31; Job 8:3"> May it never be! For then how would God judge the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:4; Romans 9:19–20; Acts 13:27–29"> But if the truth of God through my lie provides abundant praise for him, why am I still being judged as a sinner?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 6:1; Romans 6:15; Romans 5:20"> Why not say, as we are falsely reported to say, and as some affirm that we say, “Let us do evil, so that good may come”? The judgment on them is just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 6:15; Isaiah 65:5"> What then? Are we excusing ourselves? Not at all. For we have already accused both Jews and Greeks, all of them, of being under sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 53:1–3; Psalms 14:1–3; Romans 3:23"> This is as it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“There is no one righteous, not one.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 14:2–4; Psalms 53:2; Titus 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is no one who understands.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There is no one who seeks after God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 14:3; Ecclesiastes 7:20; Isaiah 64:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have all turned away. They together have become useless.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There is no one who does good, no, not even one.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:9; Psalms 140:3; Matthew 23:27–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Their throat is an open grave.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their tongues have deceived.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The poison of snakes is under their lips.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 109:17–18; Psalms 59:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Their mouths are full of cursing and bitterness.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7–8; Proverbs 1:16; Proverbs 6:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Their feet are swift to pour out blood.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Destruction and suffering are in their paths.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 59:8; Romans 5:1; Luke 1:79"> <span className="pl pl-1">These people have not known a way of peace.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 36:1; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 16:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">“There is no fear of God before their eyes.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Galatians 3:22–23; Romans 2:12–18"> Now we know that whatever the law says, it speaks to the ones who are under the law. This is in order that every mouth may be shut, and the whole world held accountable to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Acts 13:39; Romans 3:28"> This is because no flesh will be justified by the works of the law in his sight. For through the law comes the knowledge of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 10:43; Jeremiah 33:16; Philippians 3:9"> But now apart from the law the righteousness of God has been made known. It was witnessed by the Law and the Prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Galatians 3:28; Romans 8:1"> that is, the righteousness of God through faith in Jesus Christ for all those who believe. For there is no distinction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; 1 John 1:8–10; Romans 3:9"> For all have sinned and come short of the glory of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; Titus 3:5–7; Colossians 1:14"> and they are freely justified by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 3:23–24; 1 Peter 1:18–20; 1 John 4:10"> For God provided Christ Jesus as a propitiation through faith in his blood. He offered Christ as proof of his justice, because of his disregard of previous sins</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Romans 4:5; Romans 3:30"> in his patience. This all happened for the demonstration of his righteousness at this present time. This was so he could prove himself just, and to show that he justifies anyone because of faith in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 4:2; Romans 2:23; 1 Corinthians 1:29–31"> Where then is boasting? It is excluded. On what grounds? Of works? No, but on the grounds of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Galatians 3:8; Titus 3:7"> We conclude then that a person is justified by faith without works of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Ephesians 3:6; Zechariah 2:11"> Or is God the God of Jews only? Is he not also the God of Gentiles? Yes, of Gentiles also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Galatians 3:20; Philippians 3:3"> If, indeed, God is one, he will justify the circumcision by faith, and the uncircumcision through faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Matthew 5:17; Romans 13:8–10"> Do we then nullify the law through faith? May it never be! Instead, we uphold the law.</span>
