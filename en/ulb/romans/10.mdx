<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:20–22; Romans 9:1–3; John 5:34"> Brothers, my heart's desire and my request to God is for them, for their salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 21:20; Romans 9:31–32; Proverbs 19:2"> For I testify about them that they have a zeal for God, but not according to knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Luke 16:15; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For they do not know of God's righteousness, and they seek to establish their own righteousness. They did not submit to the righteousness of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 3:24; Romans 8:3–4; Matthew 5:17–18"> For Christ is the fulfillment of the law for righteousness for everyone who believes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Nehemiah 9:29"> For Moses writes about the righteousness that comes from the law: “The man who does the righteousness of the law will live by this righteousness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:11–14; Proverbs 30:4; Ephesians 4:8–10"> But the righteousness that comes from faith says this, “Do not say in your heart, 'Who will ascend into heaven?' (that is, to bring Christ down);</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Revelation 1:18; Romans 4:25"> and do not say, 'Who will descend into the abyss?'” (that is, to bring Christ up from the dead).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:14; Romans 1:16–17; 1 Timothy 4:6"> But what does it say?<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart.”</span>That is the word of faith, which we proclaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 12:8; Acts 16:31; Philippians 2:11"> For if with your mouth you acknowledge Jesus as Lord, and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Romans 10:9; Galatians 2:16"> For with the heart man believes for righteousness, and with the mouth he acknowledges for salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Romans 9:33; 1 Peter 2:6"> For scripture says, “Everyone who believes on him will not be put to shame.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; Revelation 17:14; Galatians 3:28"> For there is no difference between Jew and Greek. For the same Lord is Lord of all, and he is rich to all who call upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:21; Joel 2:32"> For everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 26:17–18; Titus 1:3"> How then can they call on him in whom they have not believed? How can they believe in him of whom they have not heard? How can they hear without a preacher?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Nahum 1:15; Ephesians 2:17"> Then how can they preach, unless they are sent?—As it is written, “How beautiful are the feet of those who proclaim glad tidings of good things!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 3:3; John 12:38–40"> But they did not all listen to the gospel. For Isaiah says, “Lord, who has believed our message?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 3:2; Galatians 3:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:13"> So faith comes from hearing, and hearing by the word of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 19:4; Romans 1:8; Matthew 24:14"> But I say, “Did they not hear?” Yes, most certainly.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Their sound has gone out into all the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their words to the ends of the world.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Romans 11:11; Romans 11:14"> Moreover, I say, “Did Israel not know?” First Moses says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will provoke you to jealousy by what is not a nation.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">By means of a nation without understanding, I will stir you up to anger.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:1–2; Romans 9:30; Isaiah 55:4–5"> Then Isaiah was very bold when he says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I was found by those who did not seek me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I appeared to those who did not ask for me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 65:2–5; 1 Peter 2:8; Jeremiah 35:15"> But to Israel he says, “All the day long I reached out my hands to a disobedient and stubborn people.”</span>
