<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:7; Romans 1:9; Galatians 1:20"> I tell the truth in Christ. I do not lie, and my conscience bears witness with me in the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:18; Psalms 119:136; Jeremiah 13:17"> that for me there is great sorrow and unceasing pain in my heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 32:32; Esther 8:6; Romans 11:14"> For I could wish that I myself would be cursed and set apart from Christ for the sake of my brothers, those of my own race according to the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:12; Psalms 147:19; Romans 9:6"> They are Israelites. They have adoption, the glory, the covenants, the gift of the law, the worship of God, and the promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 10:30; John 1:1–3; Colossians 1:16–19"> Theirs are the ancestors from whom Christ has come with respect to the flesh—he who is God over all. May he be praised forever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 2:28–29; Galatians 6:16; Romans 11:1–2"> But it is not as though the promises of God have failed. For it is not everyone in Israel who truly belongs to Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 21:12; Hebrews 11:18; John 8:37–39"> Neither are all Abraham's descendants truly his children. But, “It is through Isaac that your descendants will be called.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:13; Romans 4:11–16; Galatians 3:26–29"> That is, the children of the flesh are not the children of God. But the children of the promise are regarded as descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 18:10; Genesis 18:14; Genesis 17:21"> For this is the word of promise: “At this time I will come, and a son will be given to Sarah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 25:21–23; Luke 16:26; Romans 5:11"> Not only this, but after Rebekah also had conceived by one man, our father Isaac—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:4–5; 2 Peter 1:10"> for the children were not yet born and had not yet done anything good or bad, so that the purpose of God according to choice might stand, not because of actions, but because of him who calls—</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 25:22–23; 2 Samuel 8:14; 1 Kings 22:47"> it was said to her, “The older will serve the younger.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 1:2–3; Deuteronomy 21:15; Genesis 29:33"> It is just as had been written: “Jacob I loved, but Esau I hated.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:7; Psalms 145:17; Deuteronomy 32:4"> What then will we say? Is there unrighteousness with God? May it never be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 33:19; Romans 9:16; Micah 7:18"> For he says to Moses, “I will have mercy on whom I will have mercy, and I will have compassion on whom I will have compassion.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8; Matthew 11:25–26; 1 Peter 2:9–10"> So then, it is not because of him who wills, nor because of him who runs, but because of God, who shows mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Proverbs 16:4; Exodus 14:17–18"> For the scripture says to Pharaoh, “For this very purpose I raised you up, so that I might demonstrate my power in you, and so that my name might be proclaimed in all the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 11:20; Exodus 4:21; Romans 9:15–16"> So then, God has mercy on whom he wishes, and whom he wishes, he makes stubborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:35; 2 Chronicles 20:6; 1 Corinthians 15:12"> You will say then to me, “Why does he still find fault? For who has ever withstood his will?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 29:16; Isaiah 45:9–11; Isaiah 64:8"> On the contrary, man, who are you who answers against God? Will what has been molded say to the one who molds it, “Why did you make me this way?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Proverbs 16:4; 2 Timothy 2:20–21"> Does the potter not have the right over the clay to make from the same lump a container for special use, and another container for daily use?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; Jude 1:4; Romans 9:21"> What if God, who is willing to show his wrath and to make his power known, endured with much patience containers of wrath prepared for destruction?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 2:4; 1 Peter 1:2–5; Colossians 1:27"> What if he did this in order that he might make known the riches of his glory upon containers of mercy, which he had previously prepared for glory?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Acts 13:47–48; Ephesians 3:6–8"> What if he did this also for us, whom he also called, not only from among the Jews, but also from among the Gentiles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; 1 Peter 2:10; John 16:27"> As he says also in Hosea:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will call my people who were not my people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and her beloved who was not beloved.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 1:9–10; 1 John 3:1–3; Galatians 3:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then it will be that where it was said to them, 'You are not my people,'</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there they will be called 'sons of the living God.'”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 10:20–23; Romans 11:4–6; Isaiah 1:9"> Isaiah cries out concerning Israel,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Though the number of the children of Israel were as the sand of the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it will be a remnant that will be saved,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 10:23; Revelation 19:11; Acts 17:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">for the Lord will carry out his sentence on the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">completely and without delay.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Isaiah 13:19; Amos 4:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">As Isaiah had said previously,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“If the Lord of hosts had not left us descendants,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">we would be like Sodom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and we would have become like Gomorrah.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Hebrews 11:7; Galatians 2:16"> What will we say then? That the Gentiles, who were not pursuing righteousness, obtained righteousness, the righteousness by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 11:7; Romans 3:20; James 2:10–11"> But Israel, who did pursue a law of righteousness, did not arrive at it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Peter 2:8; Luke 7:23"> Why not? Because they did not pursue it by faith, but by works. They stumbled over the stone of stumbling,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Isaiah 8:14–15; Romans 10:11"> as it has been written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Look, I am laying in Zion a stone of stumbling and a rock of offense.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He who believes in it will not be ashamed.”</span></span>
