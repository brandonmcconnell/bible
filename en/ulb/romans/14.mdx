<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:23; Galatians 4:9–10"> One person values one day above another. Another values every day equally. Let each person be convinced in his own mind.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:20; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 15:23"> For if we live, we live for the Lord, and if we die, we die for the Lord. Then whether we live or die, we are the Lord's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; Luke 24:26; Hebrews 12:2"> For to this purpose Christ died and lived again, that he might be Lord of both the dead and those who are living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> But you, why do you judge your brother? And you, why do you despise your brother? For we will all stand before the judgment seat of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:22–25; Philippians 2:10–11; Psalms 72:11"> For it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“As I live,” says the Lord, “to me every knee will bend,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and every tongue will give praise to God.”</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:11–12; Philippians 2:2–4; Galatians 5:13"> If because of food your brother is hurt, you are no longer walking in love. Do not destroy with your food one for whom Christ died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:22; 1 Corinthians 10:29–30"> So do not allow what you consider to be good to be spoken of as evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Romans 15:13; 1 Corinthians 4:20"> For the kingdom of God is not about food and drink, but about righteousness, peace, and joy in the Holy Spirit.</span>
