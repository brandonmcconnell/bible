<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 6:3; Philippians 2:3–8; Proverbs 26:12"> For I say, because of the grace that was given to me, that everyone who is among you should not think more highly of themselves than they ought to think. <span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; 1 Corinthians 12:12–14; Ephesians 4:15–16"> For we have many members in one body, but not all the members have the same function.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:20; 1 Corinthians 10:17; 1 Corinthians 12:27–28"> In the same way, we who are many are one body in Christ, and are individually members of each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10–11; 1 Corinthians 7:7; 1 Corinthians 12:28–31"> We have different gifts according to the grace that was given to us. If one's gift is prophecy, let it be done according to the proportion of his faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11; 2 Timothy 4:2; Acts 13:1"> If one's gift is service, let him serve. If one has the gift of teaching, let him teach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Timothy 5:17; 1 Corinthians 12:28"> If one's gift is encouragement, let him encourage. <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 34:14; 1 Peter 4:8; 1 Timothy 1:5"> Let love be without hypocrisy. Abhor what is evil; hold on to that which is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; John 13:34–35; Hebrews 13:1"> Concerning love of the brothers, be affectionate to one another. Concerning honor, respect one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10–11; Acts 18:25; Revelation 3:15–16"> Concerning diligence, do not be hesitant. Concerning the spirit, be eager. Concerning the Lord, serve him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Hebrews 10:36; Psalms 62:8"> Rejoice in certain hope, be patient in suffering, be faithful in prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 2 Corinthians 9:12; 1 John 3:17"> Share in the needs of the saints. Find many ways to show hospitality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Matthew 5:44; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Bless those who persecute you; bless and do not curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Hebrews 13:3; Psalms 35:13–14"> Rejoice with those who rejoice; weep with those who weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:8; Proverbs 3:7; James 3:13–17"> Be of the same mind toward one another. Do not think in proud ways, but accept lowly people. Do not be wise in your own thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; 1 Thessalonians 5:15; Matthew 5:39"> Repay no one evil for evil. Do good things in the sight of all people.</span>
