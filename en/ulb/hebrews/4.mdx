<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; Romans 10:16–17; Hebrews 11:6"> For we were told the good news just as they were. But that message did not benefit those who did not unite in faith with those who obeyed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Other versions read, But that message did not benefit those who heard it without joining faith to it .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Matthew 11:28–29; 1 Peter 1:20"> For we who have believed enter that rest, just as he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“As I swore in my wrath,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will never enter my rest.”</span>Even so, his works were finished from the foundation of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Exodus 31:17; Genesis 2:1–2"> For he has somewhere spoken about the seventh day:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“And God rested on the seventh day from all his works.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Hebrews 4:3; Hebrews 3:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">And again in this same passage he said,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“They will never enter my rest.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Hebrews 3:18–19; Acts 13:46–47"> Therefore, because it is still reserved for some to enter his rest, and since many Israelites who heard the good news did not enter it because of disobedience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:7; Hebrews 3:7–8; Hebrews 3:15"> God has again set a certain day calling it “Today.” After many days, he spoke through David, as it was earlier said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Today if you hear his voice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">do not harden your hearts.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:15; Joshua 22:4; Deuteronomy 12:9"> For if Joshua had given them rest, God would not have spoken about another day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10; Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:14–17"> Therefore there is still a Sabbath rest reserved for God's people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Hebrews 4:3–4; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> For he who enters into God's rest has himself also rested from his deeds, just as God did from his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:28–30; Luke 13:24; Hebrews 3:18–1"> Therefore let us be eager to enter that rest, so that no one will fall into the kind of disobedience that they did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:29; Isaiah 55:11; Ephesians 6:17"> For the word of God is living and active and sharper than any two-edged sword. It pierces even to the division of soul from spirit, and joints from marrow. It is able to know the heart's thoughts and intentions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:5; Psalms 33:13–15; Proverbs 15:3"> Nothing created is hidden before God. Instead, everything is bare and open to the eyes of the one to whom we must give account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 9:12"> Therefore, since we have a great high priest who has passed through the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us firmly hold to our beliefs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17–18; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Isaiah 53:4–5"> For we do not have a high priest who cannot feel sympathy for our weaknesses. Instead, we have someone who has in all ways been tempted as we are, except that he is without sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:19–23"> Let us then go with confidence to the throne of grace, so that we may receive mercy and find grace to help in time of need.</span>
