<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Hebrews 9:11; Colossians 2:17"> For the law is only a shadow of the good things to come, not the real forms of those things themselves. Those who approach God can never be made perfect by the same sacrifices that the priests continually bring year after year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Micah 7:19; Psalms 103:12"> Otherwise, would the sacrifices not have ceased to be offered? For the worshipers would have been cleansed one time and would no longer have any consciousness of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 9:7; Exodus 30:10; Leviticus 23:27–28"> But with those sacrifices there is a reminder of sins year after year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:11; Isaiah 1:11–15; Micah 6:6–8"> For it is impossible for the blood of bulls and goats to take away sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:6–8; Isaiah 1:11; Isaiah 7:14"> When Christ came into the world, he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sacrifices and offerings</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you did not desire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but a body you have prepared for me;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 1:10; Matthew 3:17; Psalms 147:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">with burnt offerings and sin offerings</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you did not take pleasure.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 40:7–8; John 4:34; John 6:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I said, 'See, here I am—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as it is written about me in the scroll—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to do your will.'”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5; Mark 12:33"> First he said, “It was neither sacrifices, nor offerings, nor whole burnt offerings, nor sacrifices for sin that you desired. Nor did you take pleasure in them.” These are sacrifices that are offered according to the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 7:18–19; Hebrews 12:27–28; Hebrews 10:7"> Then he said, “See, here I am to do your will.” He takes away the first practice in order to establish the second practice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:14; Hebrews 10:12; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> By that will, we have been sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 5:1; Numbers 28:3; Hebrews 10:4"> Day after day every priest stands and performs his service to God. He offers the same sacrifices again and again—sacrifices that can never take away sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Colossians 3:1; Romans 8:34"> But when Christ offered for all time one sacrifice for sins, he sat down at the right hand of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:13; Psalms 110:1; Luke 20:43"> He is waiting until his enemies are made a stool for his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:1; Hebrews 9:14"> For by one offering he has perfected forever those who are being sanctified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 3:6"> The Holy Spirit also testifies to us. First he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33–34; Hebrews 8:8–12; Romans 11:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">“This is the covenant that I will make with them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">after those days, says the Lord.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will put my laws in their hearts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will write them on their minds.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Hebrews 8:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their sins and lawless deeds</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will remember no longer.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 10:2; Hebrews 10:14"> Now where there is forgiveness for these, there is no longer any sacrifice for sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 4:16"> Therefore, brothers, we have confidence to enter into the most holy place by the blood of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 14:6; John 10:9; Hebrews 9:3"> That is the new and living way that he has opened for us through the curtain, that is, by means of his flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 4:14–16; Hebrews 8:1"> Because we have a great priest over the house of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25; John 3:5; Ephesians 5:26"> let us approach with true hearts in the full assurance of faith, having our hearts sprinkled clean from an evil conscience and having our bodies washed with pure water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:24; 1 Corinthians 1:9; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> Let us also hold tightly to the confession of our hope without wavering, because God, who has promised, is faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; 1 John 3:18; Hebrews 13:1"> Let us consider how to motivate one another to love and good deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 2:42; 1 Thessalonians 5:11; Matthew 18:20"> Let us not stop meeting together, as some have done. Instead, encourage one another more and more, and all the more as you see the day coming closer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 2:20–22; Hebrews 6:4–6; John 9:41"> For if we deliberately go on sinning after we have received the knowledge of the truth, a sacrifice for sins no longer exists.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Hebrews 2:3; Zephaniah 1:18"> Instead, there is only a certain fearful expectation of judgment, and a fury of fire that will consume God's enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:2; Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:2–13"> Anyone who has rejected the law of Moses dies without mercy at the testimony of two or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 6:6; Ephesians 4:30; Isaiah 63:10"> How much worse punishment do you think one deserves who has trampled underfoot the Son of God, who treated the blood of the covenant as unholy—the blood by which he was sanctified—and insulted the Spirit of grace?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35–36; Romans 12:19; Nahum 1:2"> For we know the one who said, “Vengeance belongs to me; I will pay back.” And again, “The Lord will judge his people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; Luke 12:5; Hebrews 12:29"> It is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 6:4; Hebrews 12:4; 2 Timothy 2:3–13"> But remember the former days, after you were enlightened, how you endured a great struggle in suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:9; Philippians 4:14; Hebrews 11:36"> You were exposed to public ridicule by insults and persecution, and you were sharing with those who went through such suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:16; Matthew 5:11–12"> For you had compassion on those who were prisoners, and you accepted with joy the seizure of your possessions. You knew that you yourselves had a better and everlasting possession.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some older versions read, For you had compassion on me in my chains .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Galatians 6:8–10; Hebrews 3:6"> So do not throw away your confidence, which has a great reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 12:1; Psalms 37:7; James 5:7–11"> For you need patience, so that you may receive what God has promised, after you have done his will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="James 5:7–9; Habakkuk 2:3–4; Luke 18:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">“For in a very little while,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the one who is coming will indeed come and not delay.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Galatians 3:11; Habakkuk 2:4; Romans 1:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">My righteous one will live by faith.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If he shrinks back, I will not be pleased with him.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 John 5:5; 1 Peter 1:5; Mark 16:16"> But we are not any of those who turn back to destruction. Instead, we are some of those who have faith for keeping our soul.</span>
