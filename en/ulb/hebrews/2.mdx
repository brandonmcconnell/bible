<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:15; 2 Peter 1:12–13; Proverbs 7:1–2"> Therefore we must give far more attention to what we have heard, so that we do not drift away from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:53; Galatians 3:19; Hebrews 10:28"> For if the message that was spoken through the angels proved to be so reliable, and every trespass and disobedience receives just punishment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28–29; Hebrews 12:25; Hebrews 5:9"> how then can we escape if we ignore so great a salvation? This is salvation that was first announced by the Lord and confirmed to us by those who heard it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:3; Ephesians 1:5; Mark 16:20"> At the same time God testified to it by signs, wonders, and various powerful deeds, and by distributing the gifts of the Holy Spirit according to his will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; 2 Peter 3:13; Hebrews 6:5"> For it was not to the angels that God subjected the world to come, about which we are speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Psalms 144:3; Job 7:17–18"> Instead, someone has somewhere testified, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“What is man, that you are mindful of him?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or a son of man, that you care for him?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 8:5; Philippians 2:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">You made man a little lower than the angels;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you crowned him with glory and honor.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some older version add, and you have put him over the works of your hands .">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 13:3; 1 Corinthians 15:27; Psalms 8:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You put everything in subjection under his feet.”</span>For it was to him that God has subjected all things. He did not leave anything not subjected to him. But now we do not yet see everything subjected to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 2:7–9; Revelation 5:9; 1 John 4:9–10"> But we see him who was made lower than the angels for a little while, Jesus, crowned with glory and honor because of his suffering and death, so that by God's grace he might taste death for everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Acts 5:31; Luke 24:46"> For it was proper for God, because everything exists for him and through him, to bring many sons to glory. It was proper for him to make the leader of their salvation complete through his sufferings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:10; Hebrews 13:12; Hebrews 10:14"> For both the one who sanctifies and those who are sanctified have one source. So he is not ashamed to call them brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:22; Psalms 40:10; John 18:20"> He says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will proclaim your name to my brothers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will sing about you from inside the assembly.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17–18; Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 91:2"> And again,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will trust in him.”</span>And again,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“See, here am I and the children whom God has given me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:10; Romans 8:3; Colossians 2:15"> Therefore, since the children of God share flesh and blood, Jesus also shared the same things with them, so that through death he would do away with the one who had the power of death, that is, the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 8:15; 2 Timothy 1:7; Luke 1:74–75"> This was so that he would free all those who through fear of death lived all their lives in slavery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Galatians 3:16; Romans 4:16–25"> For of course it is not with angels that he is concerned; instead, it is with the seed of Abraham that he is concerned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14–2; Philippians 2:7–8; Hebrews 2:14"> So it was necessary for him to become like his brothers in all ways, so that he would be a merciful and faithful high priest in relation to the things of God, and so that he would bring about the pardon of the people's sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Hebrews 4:15–16; Jude 1:24"> Because Jesus himself has suffered and was tempted, he is able to help those who are tempted.</span>
