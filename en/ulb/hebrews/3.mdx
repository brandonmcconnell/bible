<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Philippians 3:14; Hebrews 4:14–15"> Therefore, holy brothers, you share in a heavenly calling. Think about Jesus, the apostle and high priest of our confession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 12:7; 1 Timothy 3:15; Ephesians 2:22"> He was faithful to God, who appointed him, just as Moses was also faithful in God's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9; 1 Peter 2:5–7; Zechariah 6:12–13"> For Jesus has been considered worthy of greater glory than Moses, because the one who builds a house has more honor than the house itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 3:3; Hebrews 1:2; Esther 2:10"> For every house is built by someone, but the one who built everything is God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 12:7; Hebrews 3:2; Exodus 14:31"> For Moses was faithful as a servant in God's entire house, bearing witness about the things that were to be spoken of in the future.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Hebrews 10:35"> But Christ is faithful as a Son who is in charge of God's house. We are his house if we hold fast to our courage and the hope of which we boast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:7–11; Hebrews 3:15; Isaiah 55:3"> Therefore, it is just as the Holy Spirit says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Today, if you hear his voice</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; Exodus 17:7; Zechariah 7:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">do not harden your hearts</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as in the rebellion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the time of testing in the wilderness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Deuteronomy 4:9; Exodus 19:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">This was when your ancestors rebelled by testing me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and when, during forty years, they saw my deeds.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 95:10; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Psalms 67:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore I was displeased with that generation.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I said, 'They have always gone astray in their hearts.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They have not known my ways.'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:34–35; Hebrews 4:3; Numbers 32:10–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is just as I swore in my anger:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">'They will never enter my rest.'”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 17:9"> Be careful, brothers, so that among you there will not be anyone with an evil heart of unbelief, a heart that turns away from the living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Hebrews 10:24–25; 2 Timothy 4:2"> Instead, encourage one another daily, as long as it is called “today,” so that no one among you will be hardened by the deceitfulness of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 3:6; Hebrews 6:11; Hebrews 12:10"> For we have become partners of Christ if we firmly hold to our confidence in him from the beginning to the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7–8; Psalms 95:7; Hebrews 10:38"> About this it has been said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Today, if you hear his voice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">do not harden your hearts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as in the rebellion.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 14:30; Numbers 26:65"> Who was it who heard God and rebelled? Was it not all those who came out of Egypt through Moses?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:29; Numbers 26:64–65; Numbers 14:32–33"> With whom was he angry for forty years? Was it not with those who sinned, whose dead bodies fell in the wilderness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Hebrews 4:6; Deuteronomy 1:34–35"> To whom did he swear that they would not enter his rest, if it was not to those who disobeyed him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jude 1:5; John 3:36; Mark 16:16"> We see that they were not able to enter his rest because of unbelief.</span>
