<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 1:20–21; Joel 2:28; Numbers 12:6–8"> Long ago God spoke to our ancestors through the prophets at many times and in many ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Colossians 1:16–18"> But in these last days, he has spoken to us through a Son, whom he appointed to be the heir of all things. It is through him that God also made the universe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; Revelation 4:11; John 14:9–10"> He is the brightness of God's glory, the exact representation of his being. He even holds everything together by the word of his power. After he had made cleansing for sins, he sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:21; Philippians 2:9–11; 1 Peter 3:22"> He has become just as superior to the angels as the name he has inherited is more excellent than their name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; Acts 13:33; Hebrews 5:5"> For to which of the angels did God ever say,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are my son,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">today I have become your father”?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or to which of the angels did God ever say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will be a father to him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he will be a son to me”?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:7; Colossians 1:18; Deuteronomy 32:43"> But again, when God brings the firstborn into the world, he says, “All God's angels must worship him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 104:4; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 6:17"> About the angels he says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He is the one who makes his angels spirits,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and his servants flames of fire.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 45:6–7; Isaiah 9:6–7; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> But to the Son he says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Your throne, God, is forever and ever.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The scepter of your kingdom is the scepter of justice.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 45:7; Psalms 23:5; Isaiah 61:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have loved righteousness and hated lawlessness.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Therefore God, your God, has anointed you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with the oil of joy more than your companions.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 102:25–27; Genesis 1:1; John 1:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">“In the beginning, Lord, you laid the earth's foundation.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The heavens are the work of your hands.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Mark 13:31; Luke 21:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will perish, but you will continue.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will all wear out like a piece of clothing.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 102:26–27; Hebrews 13:8; John 8:58"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will roll them up like a cloak,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they will be changed like a piece of clothing.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But you are the same,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and your years do not end.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Mark 12:36; Matthew 22:44"> But to which of the angels has God said at any time,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sit at my right hand</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">until I make your enemies a stool for your feet”?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 34:7; Psalms 103:20–21; Psalms 91:11–12"> Are not all angels spirits who serve, and who are sent to care for those who will inherit salvation?</span>
