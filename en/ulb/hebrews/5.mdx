<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 8:3; Hebrews 10:11; Hebrews 7:27"> For every high priest, chosen from among people, is appointed to act on the behalf of people in the things concerning God, so that he may offer both gifts and sacrifices for sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 2:18; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> He can deal gently with those who are ignorant and who have been deceived, because he himself is subject to weakness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 9:7; Hebrews 9:7; Leviticus 16:6"> Because of this, he also is required to offer sacrifices for his own sins, just as he does for the people's sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 28:1; Numbers 16:40; Numbers 16:5"> It is not for himself that anyone takes this honor. Instead, he is called by God, just as Aaron was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; John 8:54; Acts 13:33"> In the same way, neither did Christ honor himself by making himself high priest. Instead, the one speaking to him said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are my Son;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">today I have become your Father.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 7:21"> It is just as he also says in another place,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are a priest forever</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">after the manner of Melchizedek.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:32–39; Luke 22:41–44; John 17:1"> During the days of his flesh, Christ offered up both prayers and requests with loud cries and tears to God, the one able to save him from death, and he was heard because of his godly life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 2:8; Hebrews 10:5–9; John 4:34"> Even though he was a son, he learned obedience from what he suffered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:15"> He was made perfect and became, for everyone who obeys him, the cause of eternal salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 5:5–6; Hebrews 6:20"> He was designated by God as high priest after the manner of Melchizedek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Acts 28:27; 2 Peter 3:16"> We have much to say about Jesus, but it is hard to explain since you have become dull in hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 6:1; 1 Corinthians 3:1–3; 1 Peter 2:2"> For though by this time you should be teachers, you still have need for someone to teach you the basic principles of God's messages. You need milk, not solid food!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Peter 2:2; 2 Timothy 3:16"> For anyone who only takes milk is inexperienced with the message of righteousness, because he is still a little child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14–15; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; Ephesians 1:18"> But solid food is for adults. These are those who because of their maturity have their understanding trained for distinguishing good from evil.</span>
