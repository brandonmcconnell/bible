<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Colossians 3:1; Revelation 3:21"> Now the point of what we are saying is this: We have a high priest who has sat down at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 33:7; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Hebrews 9:23–24"> He is a servant in the holy place, the true tabernacle that the Lord, not a man, set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Hebrews 5:1; Hebrews 9:14"> For every high priest is appointed to offer both gifts and sacrifices. Therefore it is necessary to have something to offer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11–15; Hebrews 5:1; Numbers 17:12–13"> Now if Christ were on earth, he would not be a priest at all, since there are those who offer the gifts according to the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Colossians 2:17; Hebrews 10:1"> They serve in a tabernacle that is a copy and shadow of the heavenly things. It is just as Moses was warned by God when he was about to construct the tabernacle: God said, “See that you make everything according to the pattern that was shown to you on the mountain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 7:22; Galatians 3:16–21; Hebrews 12:24"> But now Christ has received a much better ministry, just as he is also the mediator of a better covenant, which is based on better promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11; Galatians 3:21; Hebrews 7:18"> For if that first covenant had been faultless, there would have been no need for a second covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Hebrews 10:16–17; Hebrews 12:24"> For when God found fault with the people, he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“See, the days are coming—says the Lord—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I will make a new covenant</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; Galatians 4:24; Mark 8:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will not be like the covenant</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that I made with their ancestors</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on the day that I took them by their hand</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to lead them out of the land of Egypt.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For they did not carefully obey my covenant,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I disregarded them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">—says the Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26–28; Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 11:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is the covenant</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that I will make with the house of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">after those days</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">—says the Lord.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will put my laws into their minds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will also write them on their hearts.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will be their God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they will be my people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Isaiah 54:13; John 6:45"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will not teach each one his neighbor</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and each one his brother, saying, 'Know the Lord.'</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For they will all know me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from the least of them to the greatest.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:16–17; Isaiah 43:25; Romans 11:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I will be merciful toward their evil deeds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their sins I will not remember any longer.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Hebrews 7:18–19; Hebrews 7:11–12"> By calling this covenant “new,” he declared the first covenant to be old, and what has become old and obsolete will soon disappear.</span>
