<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 3:18; Romans 16:23; 1 Corinthians 1:14"> The elder to beloved Gaius, whom I love in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:3–9; 2 Peter 3:18; Colossians 1:4–6"> Beloved, I pray that all may go well with you and that you may be healthy, just as it is well with your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 John 1:4; 2 John 1:2; Psalms 119:11"> For I rejoiced greatly when brothers came and bore witness to your truth, just as you walk in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 4:19; 2 Timothy 1:2; Proverbs 23:24"> I have no greater joy than this, to hear that my children walk in the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; Luke 12:42; Luke 16:10–12"> Beloved, you practice faithfulness whenever you work for the brothers and for strangers</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; Titus 3:13; Acts 15:3"> who have borne witness of your love in the presence of the church. You do well to send them off on their journey in a manner worthy of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:5; Revelation 2:3; 2 Kings 5:15–16"> because it was for the sake of the name that they went out, taking nothing from the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–9; 1 Corinthians 16:10–11; 2 Corinthians 7:2–3"> We therefore should welcome such as these, so that we will be fellow workers for the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 1:7–16; Luke 22:24–27; Matthew 23:4–8"> I wrote something to the congregation, but Diotrephes, who loves to be first among them, does not receive us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 66:5; 2 Corinthians 13:2; Luke 6:22"> So if I come, I will call attention to what he is doing, talking wicked nonsense against us. Not satisfied with that, he not only refused to welcome the brothers himself, but he also stops those who want to welcome them and puts them out of the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–17; Psalms 37:27; Ephesians 5:1"> Beloved, do not imitate what is evil but what is good. The one who does good is of God; the one who does evil has not seen God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; John 21:24; Acts 22:12"> Demetrius is borne witness to by all and by the truth itself. We also bear witness, and you know that our testimony is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 John 1:12"> I had many things to write to you, but I do not wish to write them to you with pen and ink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 43:23; Ephesians 6:23; Romans 16:1–16"> But I expect to see you soon, and we will speak face to face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 10:3"> May peace be with you. The friends greet you. Greet our friends there by name.</span>
