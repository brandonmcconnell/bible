<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:47–48; 1 Timothy 1:7; 1 Peter 5:3"> Not many of you should become teachers, my brothers, for you know that we who teach will be judged more strictly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:26; Proverbs 13:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> For we all stumble in many ways. If anyone does not stumble in words, he is a perfect man, able to control even his whole body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 32:9; Psalms 39:1; James 1:26"> Now if we put bits into horses' mouths for them to obey us, we can also direct their whole bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 107:25–27; Matthew 8:24; Acts 27:14–38"> Notice also that ships, although they are so large and are driven by strong winds, are steered by a very small rudder to wherever the pilot desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:21; Jeremiah 9:3–8; Proverbs 12:18"> Likewise the tongue is a small body part, yet it boasts great things. Notice also how small a fire sets on fire a large forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 120:2–3; Proverbs 16:27; Mark 7:15"> The tongue is also a fire, a world of sinfulness set among our body parts. It stains the whole body and sets on fire the course of life. It is itself set on fire by hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 5:4"> For every kind of wild animal, bird, reptile, and sea creature is being tamed and has been tamed by mankind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalms 140:3; Psalms 55:21"> But no human being can tame the tongue. It is a restless evil, full of deadly poison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Genesis 1:26–27; Psalms 62:4"> With it we praise the Lord and Father, and with it we curse men, who have been made in God's likeness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:14; 1 Peter 3:9; 1 Corinthians 3:3"> Out of the same mouth come blessing and cursing. My brothers, these things should not happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Does a spring pour out from its opening both sweet and bitter water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:43–44; Matthew 12:33; Matthew 7:16–20"> Does a fig tree, my brothers, make olives? Or a grapevine, figs? Neither can salty water produce sweet water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; James 3:17; Matthew 5:5"> Who is wise and understanding among you? Let that person show a good life by his works in the humility of wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 3:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4; Philippians 2:3"> But if you have bitter jealousy and ambition in your heart, do not boast and lie against the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> This is not the wisdom that comes down from above. Instead, it is earthly, unspiritual, demonic.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 3:14; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> For where there are jealousy and ambition, there is confusion and every evil practice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 5:22–23; Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> But the wisdom from above is first pure, then peace-loving, gentle, reasonable, full of mercy and good fruits, impartial and sincere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Hosea 10:12; Philippians 1:11"> The fruit of righteousness is sown in peace among those who make peace.</span>
