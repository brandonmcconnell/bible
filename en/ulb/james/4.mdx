<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Galatians 5:17; Romans 7:23"> Where do quarrels and disputes among you come from? Do they not come from your desires that fight among your members?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 16:24; Matthew 7:7–8; James 1:5"> You desire, and you do not have. You kill and covet, and you are not able to obtain. You fight and quarrel. You do not possess because you do not ask.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 5:14; 1 John 3:22; James 1:6–7"> You ask and do not receive because you ask badly, in order that you may use it for your desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Romans 8:7; John 15:19"> You adulteresses! Do you not know that friendship with the world is hostility against God? So whoever wants to be a friend of the world makes himself an enemy of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 6:19; Numbers 11:29"> Or do you think the scripture says in vain, “The Spirit he caused to live in us is deeply jealous”?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 3:34; 1 Peter 5:5"> But God gives more grace, so the scripture says, “God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–9; Ephesians 4:27; Ephesians 6:11–12"> So submit to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; Hebrews 10:22; Malachi 3:7"> Come close to God, and he will come close to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners, and purify your hearts, you double-minded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10–11; Matthew 5:4; Luke 6:25"> Grieve, mourn, and cry! Let your laughter turn into sadness and your joy into gloom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 147:6; Matthew 23:12; 1 Peter 5:6"> Humble yourselves before the Lord, and he will lift you up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 2:1; Romans 2:1; James 5:9"> Do not speak against one another, brothers. The person who speaks against a brother or judges his brother speaks against the law and judges the law. If you judge the law, you are not a doer of the law, but a judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 14:4; Romans 2:1; Matthew 10:28"> Only one is the lawgiver and judge. He is the one who is able to save and to destroy. Who are you, you who judge your neighbor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Luke 12:17–20; Genesis 11:3–4"> Now listen, you who say, “Today or tomorrow we will go into this city, spend a year there, trade, and make a profit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; Psalms 39:5; 1 John 2:17"> Who knows what will happen tomorrow, and what is your life? For you are a mist that appears for a little while and then disappears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Hebrews 6:3; Lamentations 3:37"> Instead, you should say, “If the Lord wishes, we will live and do this or that.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; 1 Corinthians 5:6; Psalms 52:7"> But now you are boasting about your arrogant plans. All such boasting is evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:41; John 13:17; John 15:22"> So for anyone who knows to do good but does not do it, for him it is sin.</span>
