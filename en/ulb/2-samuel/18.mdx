<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 7:16; 2 Samuel 15:19–22; 2 Samuel 10:7–10"> Then David sent out the army, one-third under the command of Joab, another third under the command of Abishai son of Zeruiah, Joab's brother, and still another third under the command of Ittai the Gittite. The king said to the army, “I will certainly go out with you myself, too.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:17; Exodus 17:10–12; 1 Kings 22:31"> But the men said, “You must not go to battle, for if we flee away they will not care about us, or if half of us die they will not care. But you are worth ten thousand of us! Therefore it is better that you be ready to help us from the city.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:24; 2 Samuel 18:1; 1 Samuel 29:2"> So the king answered them, “I will do whatever seems best to you.” The king stood by the city gate while all the army went out by hundreds and by thousands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:18–21; 2 Samuel 16:11; Psalms 103:13"> The king commanded Joab, Abishai, and Ittai saying, “Deal gently for my sake with the young man, with Absalom.” All the people heard that the king had given the captains this command about Absalom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 17:18; Joshua 17:15; Judges 12:4–6"> So the army went out into the countryside against Israel; the battle spread into the forest of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; 2 Samuel 2:31; 2 Samuel 2:26"> The army of Israel was defeated there before the soldiers of David; there was a great slaughter there that day of twenty thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 15:10; Joshua 10:11; Psalms 43:1"> The battle spread throughout the whole countryside, and more men were consumed by the forest than by the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:26; Proverbs 30:17; Job 31:3"> Absalom happened to meet some of David's soldiers. Absalom was riding his mule, and the mule went under the thick branches of a large oak tree, and his head was caught up in the tree branches. He was left dangling between the ground and the sky while the mule he was riding kept going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Someone saw this and told Joab, “Look, I saw Absalom hanging in an oak tree!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Joab said to the man who told him about Absalom, “Look! You saw him! Why did you not strike him down to the ground? I would have given you ten silver shekels and a belt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:5"> The man replied to Joab, “Even if I received a thousand silver shekels, still I would not have reached out my hand against the king's son, because we all heard the king command you, Abishai, and Ittai, saying, 'No one must touch the young man Absalom.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:19–20; Hebrews 4:13; 2 Samuel 4:10–12"> If I had risked my life by a falsehood (and there is nothing hidden from the king), you would have abandoned me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 45:5; Matthew 12:40; 2 Samuel 14:30"> Then Joab said, “I will not wait for you.” So Joab took three javelins in his hand and thrust them through the heart of Absalom, while he was still alive and hanging from the oak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then ten young men who carried Joab's armor surrounded Absalom, attacked him, and killed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:28; 2 Samuel 20:22; 1 Corinthians 14:8"> Then Joab blew the trumpet, and the army returned from pursuing Israel, for Joab held back the army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 7:26; Joshua 8:29; 2 Samuel 19:8"> They took Absalom and threw him into a large pit in the forest; they buried his body under a very large pile of stones, while all Israel fled, every man to his own home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 14:17; 2 Samuel 14:27; 1 Samuel 15:12"> Now Absalom, while still alive, had built for himself a large stone pillar in the King's Valley, for he said, “I have no son to carry along the memory of my name.” He named the pillar after his own name, so it is called Absalom's Monument to this very day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:36; 2 Samuel 18:31; Psalms 9:4"> Then Ahimaaz son of Zadok said, “Let me now run to the king with the good news, how Yahweh has rescued him from the hand of his enemies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:5; 2 Samuel 18:33; 2 Samuel 17:16–21"> Joab answered him, “You will not be the bearer of news today; you must do it another day. Today you will bear no news because the king's son is dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then Joab said to a Cushite, “Go, tell the king what you have seen.” The Cushite bowed down to Joab, and ran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 1:28; Ephesians 5:4"> Then Ahimaaz son of Zadok said again to Joab, “Regardless of what may happen, please let me also run and follow the Cushite.” Joab replied, “Why do you want to run, my son, seeing that you will have no reward for the news?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 20:4"> “Whatever happens,” said Ahimaaz, “I will run.” So Joab answered him, “Run.” Then Ahimaaz ran by the way of the plain, and outran the Cushite.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:8; 2 Samuel 13:34; Ezekiel 33:2–7"> Now David was sitting between the inner and outer gates. The watchman had gone up to the roof of the gate to the wall and raised his eyes. As he looked, he saw a man approaching, running alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The watchman shouted out and told the king. Then the king said, “If he is alone, there is news in his mouth.” The runner came closer and neared the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then the watchman noticed another man running, and the watchman called to the gatekeeper; he said, “Look, there is another man running alone.” The king said, “He is also bringing news.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 9:20; 1 Kings 1:42; Romans 10:15"> So the watchman said, “I think the running of the man in front is like the running of Ahimaaz son of Zadok.” The king said, “He is a good man and is coming with good news.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:4; Genesis 14:20; Genesis 24:27"> Then Ahimaaz called out and said to the king, “All is well.” He bowed himself before the king with his face to the ground and said, “Blessed be Yahweh your God! He has delivered the men who lifted up their hand against my master the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:22; 2 Samuel 20:9; 2 Samuel 18:19–20"> So the king replied, “Is it well with the young man Absalom?” Ahimaaz answered, “When Joab sent me, the king's servant, to you, king, I saw a great disturbance, but I did not know what it was.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Then the king said, “Turn aside and stand here.” So Ahimaaz turned aside, and stood still.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:19; 2 Samuel 18:28; Luke 18:7–8"> Immediately then the Cushite arrived and said, “There is good news for my master the king, for Yahweh has avenged you today from all who rose up against you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 5:31; 1 Samuel 25:26; Daniel 4:19"> Then the king said to the Cushite, “Is it well with the young man Absalom?” The Cushite answered, “The enemies of my master the king, and all who rise up against you to do harm to you, should be as that young man is.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:4; 2 Samuel 12:10–23; Proverbs 17:25"> Then the king was deeply unnerved, and he went up to the room over the gate and wept. As he went he grieved, “My son Absalom, my son, my son Absalom! I wish I had died instead of you, Absalom, my son, my son!”</span>
</div>
