<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:47–48; Matthew 26:48–49; Micah 7:2"> So Joab said to Amasa, “Is it well with you, my cousin?” Joab took Amasa by the beard with his right hand to kiss him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:2; Ecclesiastes 9:14–18; 1 Samuel 25:32–33"> Then a wise woman cried out of the city, “Listen, please listen, Joab! Come near me so I may speak with you.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:10–11"> Then she spoke, “They used to say in old times, 'Surely seek advice at Abel,' and that advice would end the matter.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:29; Proverbs 28:13; Jeremiah 17:9"> So Joab answered and said, “Far be it, far be it from me, that I should swallow up or destroy.</span>

Give up him alone, and I will withdraw from the city.” The woman said to Joab, “His head will be thrown to you over the wall.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:1; Ecclesiastes 7:19; 2 Samuel 11:6–21"> Then the woman went to all the people in her wisdom. They cut off the head of Sheba son of Bikri, and threw it out to Joab. Then he blew the trumpet and Joab's men left the city, every man to his home. Then Joab returned to Jerusalem to the king.</span>
</div>
