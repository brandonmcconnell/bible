<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 4:4; Isaiah 13:7; Jeremiah 6:24"> When Ishbosheth, Saul's son, heard that Abner was dead in Hebron, his hands became weak, and all Israel was troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 18:25; Joshua 9:17; 2 Kings 6:23"> Now Saul's son had two men who were captains of groups of soldiers. The name of one was Baanah and the other Recab, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite of the people of Benjamin (for Beeroth is also considered part of Benjamin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:33; 1 Samuel 31:7"> and the Beerothites fled to Gittaim and have been living there until this very time).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:3; 2 Samuel 9:6; 1 Chronicles 9:40"> Now Jonathan, Saul's son, had a son who was crippled in his feet. He was five years old when the news about Saul and Jonathan came from Jezreel. His nurse picked him up to flee. But as she was running, Jonathan's son fell and became lame. His name was Mephibosheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; 2 Chronicles 25:27; 1 Kings 16:9"> So the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, Recab and Baanah, traveled during the heat of the day to the house of Ishbosheth, as he was resting at noon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:23; 2 Samuel 20:10; 2 Samuel 3:27"> The woman guarding the door had fallen asleep while sifting wheat, and Recab and Baanah walked in quietly and passed her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 14:11; Mark 6:28–29; 1 Samuel 17:54"> So after they entered the house, they attacked him and killed him as he was lying on his bed in his room. Then they cut off his head and carried it away, traveling on the road all night to the Arabah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:29; 2 Samuel 22:48; 1 Samuel 19:2–11"> They brought the head of Ishbosheth to David at Hebron, and they said to the king, “Look, this is the head of Ishbosheth son of Saul, your enemy, who sought your life. Today Yahweh has avenged our master the king against Saul and his descendants.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 1:29; Genesis 48:16; Psalms 107:2"> David answered Recab and Baanah his brother, the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite; he said to them, “As Yahweh lives, who delivered my life from every trouble,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:2–16"> when someone told me, 'Look, Saul is dead,' thinking he was bringing good news, I seized him and killed him at Ziklag. That was the reward I gave him for his news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 9:12; Numbers 35:31–34; Genesis 9:5–6"> How much more, when wicked men have killed an innocent person in his own house on his bed, should I not now require his blood from your hand, and remove you from the earth?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:15; 2 Samuel 3:32; Matthew 7:2"> Then David gave orders to the young men, and they killed them and cut off their hands and feet and hung them up beside the pool at Hebron. But they took the head of Ishbosheth and buried it in the grave of Abner in Hebron.</span>
