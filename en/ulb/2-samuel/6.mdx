<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:1–4; 1 Kings 8:1; 1 Chronicles 13:6–14"> Now David again gathered together all the chosen men of Israel, thirty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:4; 1 Samuel 7:1; Psalms 80:1"> David arose and went with all his men who were with him from Baalah in Judah to bring up from there the ark of God, which is called by the name of Yahweh of hosts, who sits enthroned over the cherubim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:7; Numbers 7:4–9; Numbers 4:5–12"> They set the ark of God on a new cart. They brought it out of Abinadab's house, which was on a hill. Uzzah and Ahio, his sons, were guiding the new cart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:7; 1 Samuel 7:1–2"> They brought the cart out of Abinadab's house on the hill with the ark of God on it. Ahio was walking in front of the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; Psalms 150:3–5; Amos 5:23"> Then David and all the house of Israel began to play before Yahweh, celebrating with wooden instruments, harps, lyres, tambourines, rattles, and cymbals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:9; Numbers 4:15; Numbers 4:19–20"> When they came to the threshing floor of Nakon, the oxen stumbled, and Uzzah reached out with his hand to grab the ark of God, and he took hold of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19; 1 Chronicles 15:13; 1 Chronicles 13:10"> Then the anger of Yahweh burned against Uzzah. God attacked him there for his sin. Uzzah died there by the ark of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 4:1; 1 Chronicles 13:11–12; Jonah 4:9"> David was angry because Yahweh had attacked Uzzah, and he called the name of that place Perez Uzzah. That place is called Perez Uzza to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; 1 Chronicles 13:11–12; Numbers 17:12–13"> David was afraid of Yahweh that day. He said, “How can the ark of Yahweh come to me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4–8; 2 Samuel 15:19; 1 Chronicles 15:18"> So David was not willing to take the ark of Yahweh with him into the city of David. Instead, he put it aside in the house of Obed Edom the Gittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 39:5; Genesis 30:27; Proverbs 3:9–10"> The ark of Yahweh remained in the house of Obed Edom the Gittite for three months. So Yahweh blessed him and all his household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:25–3; 1 Kings 8:1; Matthew 10:42"> Now King David was told, “Yahweh has blessed Obed Edom's house and everything that belongs to him because of the ark of God.” So David went and brought up the ark of God from Obed Edom's house to the city of David with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 8:5; 1 Chronicles 15:15; Numbers 4:15"> When those who were carrying the ark of Yahweh had gone six steps, he sacrificed an ox and a fattened calf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:18; Exodus 15:20; Psalms 150:4"> David danced before Yahweh with all his might; he was wearing only a linen ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 68:24–27; 1 Chronicles 15:28; 1 Chronicles 15:25"> So David and all the house of Israel brought up the ark of Yahweh with shouting and the sound of trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Chronicles 15:29; Psalms 69:7"> Now as the ark of Yahweh came into the city of David, Michal, daughter of Saul, looked out the window. She saw King David leaping and dancing before Yahweh. Then she despised him in her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:1; 2 Chronicles 1:4; 1 Kings 8:5"> They brought in the ark of Yahweh and put it in its place, in the middle of the tent that David had set up for it. Then David offered burnt offerings and fellowship offerings before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 8:55; 1 Chronicles 16:2; Genesis 14:19"> When David had finished sacrificing the burnt offerings and the fellowship offerings, he blessed the people in the name of Yahweh of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:10; 2 Chronicles 35:7–8; 1 Chronicles 16:3"> Then he distributed among all the people, the whole multitude of Israel, both to men and women, a loaf of bread, a portion of meat, and a cake of raisins. Then all the people left; each one returned to his own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:16; 2 Samuel 6:14; Judges 9:4"> Then David returned to bless his family. Michal, the daughter of Saul, came out to meet David and said, “How honored the king of Israel was today, who undressed himself today before the eyes of the slave girls among his servants, like one of the crude fellows who shamelessly undresses himself!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:22; Psalms 89:19–20; 1 Samuel 13:14"> David responded to Michal, “I did that before Yahweh, who chose me above your father and above all his family, who appointed me leader over the people of Yahweh, over Israel. Before Yahweh I will be joyful!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14; Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 51:7"> I will be even more undignified than this, and I will be humiliated in my own eyes. But by these slave girls you have spoken about, I will be honored.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 1:25; Isaiah 22:14; Isaiah 4:1"> So Michal, the daughter of Saul, had no children to the day of her death.</span>
