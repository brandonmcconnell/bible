<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Luke 1:74–75; 1 Kings 5:4"> It happened that after the king had settled in his house, and after Yahweh had given him rest from all his surrounding enemies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:1; 2 Samuel 5:11; Haggai 1:4"> the king said to Nathan the prophet, “Look, I am living in a house of cedar, but the ark of God is staying in the middle of a tent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:7; 1 Kings 8:17–18; 2 Kings 4:27"> Then Nathan said to the king, “Go, do what is in your heart, for Yahweh is with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:3; Numbers 12:6; Amos 3:7"> But that same night the word of Yahweh came to Nathan, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:3–32; 1 Kings 5:3–4; 1 Kings 8:16–19"> “Go and tell David my servant, 'This is what Yahweh says: Will you build me a house in which to live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 8:16; Joshua 18:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> For I have not lived in a house from the day that I brought up the people of Israel out of Egypt until this present day; instead, I have been moving about in a tent, a tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:6; 2 Samuel 5:2; Leviticus 26:11–12"> In all places where I have moved among all the people of Israel, did I ever say anything to any of Israel's leaders whom I appointed to shepherd my people Israel, saying, “Why have you not built me a house of cedar?”'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 78:70–71; 2 Samuel 6:21; 2 Samuel 12:7"> Now then, tell my servant David, “This is what Yahweh of hosts says: 'I took you from the pasture, from following the sheep, so that you would be ruler over my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:10; 1 Chronicles 17:8; 1 Samuel 18:14"> I have been with you wherever you went. I have cut off all your enemies from before you. Now I will make your name great, like the names of the great ones of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 9:15; 1 Chronicles 17:9; Jeremiah 24:6"> I will appoint a place for my people Israel and will plant them there, so that they may live in their own place and be troubled no more. No longer will wicked people oppress them, as they did before,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:27; 2 Samuel 7:1; 1 Chronicles 17:10"> as they were doing from the days that I commanded judges to be over my people Israel. Now I will give you rest from all your enemies. Moreover, I, Yahweh declare to you that I will make you a house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 8:20; Acts 2:30; Acts 13:36"> When your days are fulfilled and you lie down with your fathers, I will raise up a descendant after you, one who will come out from your body, and I will establish his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:31–33; 1 Kings 8:19; 2 Samuel 7:16"> He will build a house for my name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 1:5; Revelation 3:19; 1 Chronicles 17:13"> I will be a father to him, and he will be my son. When he sins, I will discipline him with the rod of men and with the whipping of the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:23; 1 Samuel 15:28; Psalms 89:28"> But my covenant faithfulness will not leave him, as I took it from Saul, whom I removed from before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Psalms 89:36–37; 2 Samuel 7:13"> Your house and kingdom will be confirmed forever before you. Your throne will be established forever.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:15; Acts 20:27; 1 Corinthians 15:3"> Nathan spoke to David and reported to him all these words, and he told him about the entire vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 8:4; 1 Chronicles 17:16; 1 Samuel 18:18"> Then David the king went in and sat before Yahweh and said, “Who am I, Yahweh God, and what is my family that you have brought me to this point?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 55:8–9; 1 Chronicles 17:17; Psalms 36:7"> Now this was a small thing in your sight, Lord Yahweh. You have even spoken about your servant's family for a great while to come, and have shown me future generations, Lord Yahweh!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 21:17; 1 Samuel 16:7; Psalms 139:1"> What more can I, David, say to you? You have known your servant, Lord Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 115:1; Psalms 138:2; Ephesians 1:9"> For your word's sake, and to fulfill your own purpose, you have done this great thing and revealed it to your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:10; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 86:8"> Therefore you are great, Lord Yahweh, for there is no one like you, and there is no God besides you, as we have heard with our own ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:26; Deuteronomy 10:21; Deuteronomy 15:15"> What nation is like your people Israel, the one nation on earth whom you, God, went and rescued for yourself? You did this so that they would become a people for yourself, to make a name for yourself, and to do great and fearful deeds for your land. You drove out nations and their gods from before your people, whom you rescued from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:18; 1 Peter 2:10; Genesis 17:7"> You established Israel as your own people forever, and you, Yahweh, became their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 119:49; Ezekiel 36:37; Genesis 32:12"> So now, Yahweh God, may the promise that you made concerning your servant and his family be established forever. Do as you have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:23–24; Psalms 72:18–19; Matthew 6:9"> May your name be forever great, so the people will say, 'Yahweh of hosts is the God of Israel,' while the house of me, David, your servant is established before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:25–26; Psalms 10:17; Ruth 4:4"> For you, Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel, have revealed to your servant that you will build him a house. That is why I, your servant, have found courage to pray to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 17:17; Exodus 34:6; Numbers 23:19"> Now, Lord Yahweh, you are God, and your words are trustworthy, and you have made this good promise to your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 6:24–26; 1 Chronicles 17:27; Psalms 115:12–15"> Now then, let it please you to bless the house of your servant, so that it may continue forever before you. For you, Lord Yahweh, have said these things, and with your blessing your servant's house will be blessed forever.”</span>
