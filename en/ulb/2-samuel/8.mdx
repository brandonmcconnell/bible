</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:47; 2 Samuel 10:16; 2 Samuel 10:19"> David then defeated Hadadezer son of Rehob, the king of Zobah, as Hadadezer was traveling to recover his rule by the Euphrates River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:6; Joshua 11:9; 1 Chronicles 18:4"> David captured from him 1,700 chariots and twenty thousand footmen. David hamstrung all the chariot horses, but reserved enough of them for a hundred chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 11:23–25; Isaiah 7:8; Job 9:13"> When the Arameans of Damascus came to help Hadadezer king of Zobah, David killed twenty-two thousand Aramean men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; 2 Samuel 7:9; Psalms 18:34–46"> Then David put garrisons in Aram of Damascus, and the Arameans became servants to him and brought him tribute. Yahweh gave victory to David wherever he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 14:26–27; 1 Kings 10:16–17"> David took the golden shields that were on Hadadezer's servants and brought them to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:8; 1 Chronicles 22:16; Ezekiel 47:16"> From Tebah and Berothai, cities of Hadadezer, King David took very much bronze.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 8:65; 2 Chronicles 8:4; 1 Chronicles 18:9"> When Tou, king of Hamath, heard that David had defeated all the army of Hadadezer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:10; 1 Chronicles 18:10; Psalms 129:8"> Tou sent Hadoram his son to King David to greet him and to bless him, because David had fought against Hadadezer and defeated him, and because Hadadezer had waged war against Tou. Hadoram brought with himself objects of silver, gold, and bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 7:51; 1 Chronicles 29:2; 1 Chronicles 22:14–16"> King David set aside these objects to Yahweh, together with the silver and gold from all the nations that he conquered—</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:14; 2 Samuel 8:2; 1 Chronicles 18:11"> from Aram, Moab, the people of Ammon, the Philistines, and Amalek, along with all of the plundered goods of Hadadezer son of Rehob, the king of Zobah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 14:7; 2 Samuel 7:9; 1 Chronicles 18:12"> David's name was well known when he returned from conquering the Arameans in the Valley of Salt, with their eighteen thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 27:29; Genesis 25:23; 2 Samuel 8:6"> He placed garrisons throughout all of Edom, and all the Edomites became servants to him. Yahweh gave victory to David wherever he went.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 5:15; Psalms 89:14; Psalms 72:2"> David reigned over all Israel, and he administered justice and righteousness to all his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 4:3; 1 Chronicles 11:6; 2 Samuel 19:13"> Joab son of Zeruiah was the commander of the army, and Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was recorder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:8; 1 Chronicles 18:16; 1 Chronicles 24:3–4"> Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar were priests, and Seraiah was scribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:14; 2 Samuel 20:7; 2 Samuel 20:23"> Benaiah son of Jehoiada was in charge of the Kerethites and Pelethites, and David's sons were the chief officials at the king's hand.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Comparing this verse with 1 Chron. 18:17, were the chief officials at the king's hand ; but the MT has, were priests .">a</a></sup></span>
