<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:9–14; Luke 6:6–11; Mark 1:21"> Again Jesus walked into the synagogue and there was a man with a withered hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:32; Luke 14:1; Luke 6:7"> Some people watched him closely to see if he would heal him on the Sabbath so that they could accuse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 1:28–30; Isaiah 42:4; Luke 6:8"> Jesus said to the man with the withered hand, “Get up and stand here in the middle of everyone.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Matthew 12:10–12; Hosea 6:6"> Then he said to the people, “Is it lawful to do good on the Sabbath day or to do harm; to save a life or to kill?” But they were silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:10; Ephesians 4:18; Romans 11:25"> He looked around at them with anger, and he was grieved by their hardness of heart, and he said to the man, “Stretch out your hand.” He stretched it out, and his hand was restored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:16; Mark 12:13; Matthew 12:14"> The Pharisees went out and immediately began to plot with the Herodians as to how they might put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 4:25; Luke 6:17; Matthew 12:15"> Then Jesus, with his disciples, went to the sea, and a great crowd of people followed from Galilee and from Judea</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Isaiah 34:5; Matthew 11:21"> and from Jerusalem and from Idumea and beyond the Jordan and around Tyre and Sidon. When they heard about the things he was doing, a great crowd came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 5:30; John 6:15"> He asked his disciples to have a small boat ready for him because of the crowd, so that they would not crush him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56; Matthew 4:23"> For he healed many, so that everyone who had afflictions eagerly approached him in order to touch him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 1:23–24; Luke 8:28"> Whenever the unclean spirits saw him, they fell down before him and cried out, and they said, “You are the Son of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:16; Mark 1:25; Mark 1:34"> He strictly ordered them not to make him known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Luke 6:12–16; Matthew 10:1–4"> He went up on the mountain, and he called for those he wanted, and they came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Luke 9:1–6; John 15:16"> He appointed the twelve (whom he named apostles) so that they might be with him and he might send them to proclaim the message,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and to have authority to cast out demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:42; Acts 1:13; Matthew 16:16–18"> Then he appointed the twelve: Simon, to whom he gave the name Peter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Mark 14:33; Jeremiah 23:29"> James son of Zebedee, and John the brother of James, to whom he gave the name Boanerges, that is, sons of thunder;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14; Matthew 9:9"> and Andrew, Philip, Bartholomew, Matthew, Thomas, James son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus, Simon the Zealot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 6:71; John 13:2; Acts 1:16–25"> and Judas Iscariot, who would betray him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 6:31; John 4:31–34; Mark 9:28"> Then he went home, and the crowd came together again, so that they could not even eat bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 26:24; John 10:20; Mark 3:31"> When his family heard about it, they went out to seize him, for they said, “He is out of his mind.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:1; Matthew 10:25; Matthew 9:34"> The scribes who came down from Jerusalem said, “He is possessed by Beelzebul” and “By the ruler of the demons he drives out demons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:17–23; Matthew 12:25–30; Matthew 13:34"> Jesus called them to him and said to them in parables, “How can Satan cast out Satan?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Isaiah 19:2–3; 2 Samuel 20:6"> If a kingdom is divided against itself, that kingdom cannot stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 3:16; Psalms 133:1; Galatians 5:15"> If a house is divided against itself, that house will not be able to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> If Satan has risen up against himself and is divided, he is not able to stand, but has come to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–26; Matthew 12:29; Luke 11:21–23"> But no one can enter into the house of a strong man and steal his belongings without tying up the strong man first, and then he will plunder his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; 1 John 5:16"> Truly I say to you, all sins of the sons of men will be forgiven, even all the blasphemies which they utter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 12:31–32; Luke 12:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> but whoever blasphemes against the Holy Spirit will never have forgiveness, but is guilty of an eternal sin.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:20; Mark 3:22"> Jesus said this because they were saying, “He has an unclean spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:19–21; Matthew 12:46–50"> Then his mother and his brothers came and stood outside. They sent for him, summoning him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> A crowd was sitting around him and they said to him, “Your mother and your brothers are outside, and they are looking for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 3:21; Deuteronomy 33:9; Mark 6:3"> He answered them, “Who are my mother and my brothers?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 12:49–50; Luke 11:27–28; Romans 8:29"> He looked around at those who were sitting in a circle around him and said, “See, here are my mother and my brothers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:17; Matthew 7:21; 1 John 3:22–23"> For whoever does the will of God, that person is my brother, and sister, and mother.”</span>
