<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 9:27; Hebrews 2:9; Luke 22:30"> He said to them, “Truly I say to you, there are some of you who are standing here who will not taste death before they see the kingdom of God come with power.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 9:28–36; Isaiah 33:17; Matthew 17:1–8"> Six days later, Jesus took Peter and James and John with him up a high mountain, alone by themselves. Then he was transfigured before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 28:3; Daniel 7:9; Revelation 7:14"> His garments became radiantly brilliant, extremely white, whiter than any bleacher on earth could bleach them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 9:30–31; Matthew 17:3–4; Acts 3:21–24"> Then Elijah with Moses appeared to them, and they were talking with Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 1:23; John 14:8–9; Revelation 22:3–4"> Peter answered and said to Jesus, “Rabbi, it is good for us to be here, and so let us make three shelters, one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Daniel 10:15–19; Mark 16:5–8"> (For he did not know what to say, for they were terrified.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Peter 1:17; Matthew 3:17; Mark 1:11"> A cloud came and overshadowed them. Then a voice came out of the cloud, “This is my beloved Son. Listen to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 10:16; Acts 8:39–40; Luke 24:31"> Suddenly, when they looked around, they no longer saw anyone with them, but only Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 12:40; Matthew 17:9–13; Mark 5:43"> As they were coming down the mountain, he commanded them to tell no one what they had seen, until the Son of Man had risen from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 17:18; Luke 24:7–8; Luke 18:33–34"> So they kept the matter to themselves, but they discussed among themselves what “rising from the dead” could mean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Malachi 3:1; Matthew 11:14"> They asked him, “Why do the scribes say that Elijah must come first?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 52:14"> He said to them, “Elijah does come first to restore all things. Why then is it written that the Son of Man must suffer many things and be despised?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 14:3–11; Matthew 17:12–13; Matthew 11:14"> But I say to you that Elijah has come, and they did whatever they wanted to him, just as scripture says about him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 9:37–42; Matthew 17:14–20; Luke 11:53–54"> When they came to the disciples, they saw a great crowd around them and scribes were arguing with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:30; Mark 9:2–3"> As soon as they saw Jesus, the whole crowd was amazed and as they ran up to him they greeted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 5:30–32; Mark 8:11"> He asked his disciples, “What are you arguing with them about?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 9:25; Luke 11:14; John 4:47"> Someone in the crowd answered him, “Teacher, I brought my son to you. He has a spirit that makes him unable to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Mark 11:23; Matthew 17:16"> It seizes him and it throws him down and he foams at the mouth, grinds his teeth, and he becomes rigid. I asked your disciples to drive it out of him, but they could not.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; John 20:27; Numbers 14:27"> He answered them, “Unbelieving generation, how long will I have to stay with you? How long will I bear with you? Bring him to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 1:26; Luke 4:35; John 8:44"> They brought the boy to him. When the spirit saw Jesus, it immediately threw him into a convulsion. The boy fell on the ground and foamed at the mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 9:33; Acts 4:22; Mark 5:25"> Jesus asked his father, “How long has he been like this?” The father said, “Since childhood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:22–28; Mark 5:19; Matthew 20:34"> It has often thrown him into the fire or into the waters and tried to destroy him. If you are able to do anything, have pity on us and help us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:40; Hebrews 11:6; Mark 11:23"> Jesus said to him, “'If you are able'? All things are possible for the one who believes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 17:5; Hebrews 12:2; Ephesians 2:8"> Immediately the father of the child cried out and said, “I believe! Help my unbelief!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:18; Luke 9:42; Mark 9:15"> When Jesus saw the crowd running to them, he rebuked the unclean spirit and said, “You mute and deaf spirit, I command you, come out of him, and never enter into him again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 9:20; Mark 1:26; Mark 9:18"> It cried out and convulsed the boy greatly and then came out. The boy looked like one who was dead, so that many said, “He is dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Acts 3:7; Mark 1:41"> But Jesus took him by the hand and lifted him up, and the boy stood up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Mark 7:17; Matthew 15:15"> When Jesus came into the house, his disciples asked him privately, “Why could we not cast it out?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 5:15; Daniel 9:3; Matthew 17:20"> He said to them, “This kind cannot be cast out except by prayer.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 17:22–23; Luke 9:43–45; Mark 6:31–32"> They went out from there and passed through Galilee. He did not want anyone to know where they were,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Mark 9:12; Luke 24:44–46"> for he was teaching his disciples. He said to them, “The Son of Man will be delivered into the hands of men, and they will put him to death. When he has been put to death, after three days he will rise again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 2:50; Luke 9:45; Luke 18:34"> But they did not understand this statement, and they were afraid to ask him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 18:1–5; Hebrews 4:13; Luke 9:46–48"> Then they came to Capernaum. After he entered the house he asked them, “What were you discussing on the way?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 9:50; Matthew 20:21–24; Matthew 18:1–5"> But they were silent. For they had been arguing with one with another on the way about who was the greatest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:25–28; Mark 10:42–45; Luke 22:26"> Sitting down, he called the twelve together and he said to them, “If anyone wants to be first, he must be last of all and servant of all.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 10:16; Matthew 19:14–15; Matthew 18:2"> He took a little child and placed him in their midst. He took him in his arms and said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 18:3–5; Luke 10:16; Matthew 10:40–42"> “Whoever receives such a child in my name receives me; and whoever receives me, does not receive me but the one who sent me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 9:49–50; Numbers 11:26–29; Luke 11:19"> John said to him, “Teacher, we saw someone driving out demons in your name and we stopped him, because he does not follow us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:3; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Matthew 13:28–29"> But Jesus said, “Do not stop him, for there is no one who will do a mighty work in my name and can soon afterwards say anything bad about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 12:30; Luke 11:23"> Whoever is not against us is for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; Matthew 25:40; 1 Corinthians 15:23"> Whoever gives you a cup of water to drink because you belong to Christ, truly I say to you, he will not lose his reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 18:6; Luke 17:1–2; Matthew 18:10"> Whoever causes one of these little ones who believes in me to stumble, it would be better for him to have a large millstone tied around his neck and be thrown into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 5:29–30; Matthew 18:8–9; Matthew 25:41"> If your hand causes you to stumble, cut it off. It is better for you to enter into life maimed than to have two hands and to go into hell, into the unquenchable fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The best ancient copies omit this phrase from verses 44 and 46, where their worm does not die, and the fire is not put out .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 18:8; Matthew 5:22; Mark 9:43"> If your foot causes you to stumble, cut it off. It is better for you to enter into life lame than to have your two feet and be thrown into hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The best ancient copies omit this phrase from verses 44 and 46, where their worm does not die, and the fire is not put out .">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 18:9; Mark 9:43; Matthew 5:22"> If your eye causes you to stumble, tear it out. It is better for you to enter into the kingdom of God with one eye than to have two eyes and to be thrown into hell,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Isaiah 66:24; Mark 9:43–45; Matthew 25:41"> where their worm does not die, and the fire is not put out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Leviticus 2:13; Ezekiel 43:24"> For everyone will be salted with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Romans 12:18; Matthew 5:13"> Salt is good, but if the salt has lost its saltiness, how can you make it salty again? Have salt among yourselves, and be at peace with one another.”</span>
