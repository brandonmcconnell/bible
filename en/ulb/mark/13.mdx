<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 24:1–51; Ezekiel 11:22–23; Ezekiel 10:19"> As Jesus was walking away from the temple, one of his disciples said to him, “Teacher, look at the wonderful stones and wonderful buildings!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 21:6; Matthew 24:2; 1 Kings 9:7–8"> He said to him, “Do you see these great buildings? Not one stone will be left on another which will not be torn down.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Mark 4:34; Mark 14:33"> As he sat on the Mount of Olives opposite the temple, Peter, James, John, and Andrew asked him privately,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 1:6–7; Matthew 24:3; Daniel 12:6"> “Tell us, when will these things happen? What will be the sign when all these things are about to happen?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Ephesians 5:6; Jeremiah 29:8"> Jesus began to say to them, “Be careful that no one leads you astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:22; Jeremiah 23:21–25; John 8:24"> Many will come in my name and say, 'I am he,' and they will lead many astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 21:9–11; John 14:27; Matthew 24:6–7"> When you hear of wars and rumors of wars, do not worry; these things must happen, but the end is not yet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 24:8; Isaiah 37:3; Psalms 48:6"> For nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom. There will be earthquakes in many places, and famines. These are the beginnings of birth pains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 10:17–18; John 16:2; Philippians 1:29"> Be on your guard. They will deliver you up to councils, and you will be beaten in synagogues. You will stand before both governors and kings for my sake, as a testimony to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:14; Colossians 1:23; Romans 10:18"> But the gospel must first be proclaimed to all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 21:14–15; Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–21"> When they arrest you and hand you over, do not worry about what you should say. For in that hour, what you should say will be given to you; it will not be you who speak, but the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Luke 12:52–53; Luke 21:16"> Brother will deliver up brother to death, and a father his child. Children will rise up against their parents and cause them to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:18–19; Matthew 10:22; Matthew 24:13"> You will be hated by everyone because of my name. But whoever endures to the end, that person will be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:27; Daniel 12:11; Daniel 11:31"> When you see the abomination of desolation standing where it should not be standing (let the reader understand), let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:31–33; Hebrews 11:7; Philippians 3:7–8"> let him who is on the housetop not go down into the house or take anything out of it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and let him who is in the field not return to take his cloak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 4:10; Luke 23:29; Hosea 9:14"> But woe to those who are with child and to those who are nursing infants in those days!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Pray that it might not occur in the winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Joel 2:2; Matthew 24:21"> For there will be great tribulation, such as has not been from the beginning, when God created the world, until now, no, nor ever will be again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 24:22; Zechariah 13:8–9; Isaiah 65:8–9"> Unless the Lord had shortened the days, no flesh would be saved. But for the sake of the elect, those whom he chose, he cut short the days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Matthew 24:23–25; Matthew 24:5"> Then if anyone says to you, 'Look, here is the Christ!' or 'Look, there he is!' do not believe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; 2 Thessalonians 2:8–14; Mark 13:6"> For false Christs and false prophets will appear and will give signs and wonders, so as to deceive, if possible, even the elect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 3:17; Mark 13:5; John 14:29"> Be on guard! I have told you all these things ahead of time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Acts 2:19–20; Revelation 6:12–14"> But after the tribulation of those days,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">the sun will be darkened,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the moon will not give its light,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4; Revelation 6:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">the stars will fall from the sky,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the powers that are in the heavens will be shaken.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Matthew 16:27; Matthew 24:30"> Then they will see the Son of Man coming in the clouds with great power and glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zechariah 2:6; Matthew 24:31; Deuteronomy 30:4"> Then he will send his angels and he will gather together his elect from the four winds, from the ends of the earth to the ends of the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 21:29–31; Matthew 24:32–33"> Learn a lesson from the fig tree. As soon as the branch becomes tender and puts out its leaves, you know that summer is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; James 5:9; Ezekiel 12:25–28"> So also, when you see these things happening, you know that he is near, close to the gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:34; Luke 21:32; Matthew 16:28"> Truly I say to you, this generation will not pass away until all of these things occur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Isaiah 51:6; Matthew 24:35"> Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will never pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Matthew 24:36–42; Matthew 25:13"> But concerning that day or that hour, no one knows, not even the angels in heaven, nor the Son, but the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Luke 12:40; Romans 13:11–12"> Be alert! Watch, because you do not know what time it is.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some ancient authorities read: Be alert! Watch and pray .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–30; John 10:3; Luke 12:36–40"> It is like a man who goes on a journey—he leaves his house and puts his servants in charge of the house, each one with his work, and he commands the watchman to stay awake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 13:33; Matthew 24:42; Matthew 24:44"> Therefore stay alert, because you do not know when the master of the house will come home; it could be in the evening, at midnight, when the rooster crows, or in the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6–7; Romans 13:11–14; Matthew 24:48–51"> If he comes suddenly, do not let him find you sleeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 13:33; Luke 12:41–46; Mark 13:35"> What I say to you I say to everyone: Watch!”</span>
