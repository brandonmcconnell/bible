<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:26–39; Matthew 8:28–34; Mark 4:35"> They came to the other side of the sea, to the region of the Gerasenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 1:23; Isaiah 65:4; Mark 5:8"> When Jesus was getting out of the boat, immediately there came up to him out of the tombs a man with an unclean spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 65:4; Luke 8:29; Daniel 4:32–33"> The man lived in the tombs. No one could restrain him anymore, not even with a chain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 3:7–8"> He had been bound many times with shackles and with chains. He tore the chains apart and his shackles were shattered. No one had the strength to subdue him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 2:7–8; 1 Kings 18:28; John 8:44"> Every night and day in the tombs and in the mountains, he cried out and cut himself with sharp stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 2:19; Psalms 66:3; Luke 4:41"> When he saw Jesus from a distance, he ran to him and bowed down before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; Acts 16:17; Matthew 26:63"> He cried out with a loud voice, “What do I have to do with you, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I beg you by God himself, do not torment me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 1:25; Mark 9:25–26; Acts 16:18"> For he had been saying to him, “Come out of the man, you unclean spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 8:30; Mark 5:15; Matthew 26:53"> He asked him, “What is your name?” He answered him, “My name is Legion, for we are many.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Mark 5:13"> He begged him again and again not to send them out of the region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 11:7–8; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> Now a great herd of pigs was there feeding on the hill,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:11; Job 1:10–12; Luke 22:31–32"> and they begged him, saying, “Send us into the pigs; let us enter into them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 22:22; 1 Peter 3:22; Job 2:6"> So he allowed them; the unclean spirits came out and entered into the pigs, and they rushed down the steep hill into the sea, and about two thousand pigs drowned in the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 8:34; Matthew 8:33"> Then those who were feeding the pigs ran away and reported what had happened in the city and in the countryside, and so people went out to see what had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 2 Timothy 1:7; 1 Samuel 6:20–21"> Then they came to Jesus and they saw the demon-possessed man, the one who had been possessed by Legion, sitting there, clothed and in his right mind; and they were afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Those who had seen what happened to the demon-possessed man told them about it in detail, and they also them told about the pigs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 8:34; Mark 1:24; Luke 8:37"> Then they started to beg him to leave their region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:38–39; Philippians 1:23–24; Mark 5:17"> When he was getting into the boat, the demon-possessed man begged him that he might be with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 66:16; John 4:29; Isaiah 38:9–20"> But Jesus did not permit him, but said to him, “Go to your house and to your people and tell them what the Lord has done for you, and how he has shown you mercy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:31; Matthew 4:25"> So he went away and began to proclaim the great things that Jesus had done for him in the Decapolis, and everyone was amazed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 9:1; Mark 4:1; Luke 8:40"> Now when Jesus had crossed over again to the other side, in the boat, a great crowd gathered around him, as he was beside the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Acts 18:8; Mark 5:35–36"> Then one of the leaders of the synagogue, named Jairus, came, and when he saw him, fell at his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 13:13; Luke 4:40; Mark 16:18"> He begged again and again, saying, “My little daughter is near death. I beg you, come and lay your hands on her that she may be made well and live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 5:31; Mark 3:20; Luke 7:6"> So he went with him, and a great crowd followed him and pressed close around him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:25–27; Luke 8:43–44; Matthew 9:20–22"> Now a woman was there who had a flow of blood for twelve years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:12–13; Jeremiah 8:22; Psalms 108:12"> She had suffered much from many doctors and had spent everything that she had, but instead of getting better she grew worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 19:12; Matthew 14:36; Acts 5:15"> When she had heard the reports about Jesus, she came up behind him in the crowd and touched his cloak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> For she said, “If I touch just his clothes, I will be healed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Psalms 30:2; Mark 3:10"> When she touched him, the bleeding stopped, and she felt in her body that she was healed from her affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 8:46; Luke 5:17"> Jesus immediately realized in himself that power had gone out from him. He turned around in the crowd and said, “Who touched my clothes?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:45; Luke 9:12"> His disciples said to him, “You see this crowd pressed around you, and you say, 'Who touched me?'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> But Jesus looked around to see who had done it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:47; Luke 1:29; Luke 1:12"> The woman, knowing what had happened to her, feared and trembled. She came and fell down before him and told him the whole truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 8:48; Matthew 9:22; Luke 7:50"> He said to her, “Daughter, your faith has made you well. Go in peace and be healed from your disease.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 8:49; John 11:39; Mark 5:22"> While he was speaking, some people came from the leader of the synagogue, saying, “Your daughter is dead. Why trouble the teacher any longer?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 8:50; Matthew 9:28–29; John 11:40"> But when Jesus overheard what they said, he said to the leader of the synagogue, “Do not be afraid. Just believe.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 9:40; Mark 14:33; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> He did not permit anyone to accompany him, except Peter, James, and John, the brother of James.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:23–24; Luke 8:52–53; Mark 5:22"> They came to the house of the leader of the synagogue and he saw there people making a lot of noise; they were weeping and wailing loudly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 20:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–14"> When he entered the house, he said to them, “Why are you upset and why do you weep? The child is not dead but sleeps.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; 2 Kings 4:33; Matthew 9:24–25"> They laughed at him. But he put them all outside and took the father of the child and the mother and those who were with him, and he went in where the child was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Luke 7:14–15; Luke 8:54–55"> He took the hand of the child and said to her, “Talitha, koum!” which being translated would be, “Little girl, I say to you, get up.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Mark 4:41; Acts 3:10–13; Mark 7:37"> Immediately the child got up and walked (for she was twelve years of age). They were immediately astonished with overwhelming amazement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Mark 1:43"> He strictly ordered them that no one should know about this. Then he told them to give her something to eat.</span>
