<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 22:1–2; Exodus 12:6–20; John 13:1"> It was now two days before the Passover and the Festival of Unleavened Bread. The chief priests and the scribes were considering how they might stealthily arrest Jesus and then kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 7:40; Proverbs 21:30; John 12:19"> For they were saying, “Not during the feast, so that a riot does not arise among the people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 7:37–39; John 12:1–8; John 11:2"> While Jesus was in Bethany in the house of Simon the leper, as he was reclining at the table, a woman came to him having an alabaster jar of very costly liquid, which was pure nard. She broke the jar and poured it on his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:4–5; Matthew 26:8–9; Malachi 1:12–13"> But there were some who were angry. They spoke among themselves and said, “What is the reason for this waste?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 13:29; Ephesians 4:28; 1 Corinthians 10:10"> This perfume could have been sold for more than three hundred denarii, and given to the poor.” Then they scolded her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 3:14; Hebrews 10:24; Hebrews 13:21"> But Jesus said, “Leave her alone. Why are you troubling her? She has done a beautiful thing for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Matthew 26:11; James 2:14–16"> You always have the poor with you, and whenever you desire you can do good to them, but you will not always have me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; John 12:7; Mark 15:42–1"> She has done what she could. She has anointed my body for burial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 26:12–13; Mark 16:15; Matthew 24:14"> Truly I say to you, wherever the gospel is preached in the whole world, what this woman has done will be spoken of, in memory of her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 22:3–6; Matthew 26:14–16; John 13:2"> Then Judas Iscariot, one of the twelve, went away to the chief priests so that he might deliver him over to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Hosea 7:3; Jude 1:11"> When the chief priests heard it, they were glad and promised to give him money. He began looking for an opportunity to deliver him to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 12:6; Exodus 12:18; Matthew 26:17–19"> On the first day of unleavened bread, when they sacrificed the Passover lamb, his disciples said to him, “Where do you want us to go to prepare, so you may eat the Passover meal?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 22:10–13; Matthew 26:18–19; Luke 19:30–33"> He sent two of his disciples and said to them, “Go into the city, and a man bearing a pitcher of water will meet you. Follow him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Mark 11:3; John 13:13"> Where he enters a house, follow him in and say to the owner of that house, 'The Teacher says, “Where is my guest room where I will eat the Passover with my disciples?”'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Psalms 110:3; Proverbs 21:1–2"> He will show you a large furnished upper room that is ready. Make the preparations for us there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 16:4; Luke 22:13; Luke 22:35"> The disciples left and went to the city. They found everything as he had said to them, and they prepared the Passover meal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:14; John 13:21–26; Matthew 26:20–24"> When it was evening, he came with the twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 6:47; John 6:70"> As they were lying down at the table and eating, Jesus said, “Truly I say to you, one of you eating with me will betray me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 22:21–23; Matthew 26:22; John 13:22"> They were all very sorrowful, and one by one they said to him, “Surely not I?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 13:26; Matthew 26:23; John 6:71"> Jesus answered and said to them, “It is one of the twelve, the one now dipping bread with me in the bowl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 26:24–25; Isaiah 53:1–12; John 19:28"> For the Son of Man will go the way that the scripture says about him. But woe to that man through whom the Son of Man is betrayed! It would have been better for him if he had not been born.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 26:26–29; Mark 14:24; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> As they were eating, Jesus took bread, blessed it, and broke it. He gave it to them and said, “Take this. This is my body.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 14:22; Matthew 26:27; 1 Corinthians 10:16"> He took a cup, gave thanks, and gave it to them, and they all drank from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11; Exodus 24:8; Revelation 7:9–17"> He said to them, “This is my blood of the covenant, the blood that is poured out for many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 26:29; Psalms 104:15; Luke 22:16–18"> Truly I say to you, I will not drink again of this fruit of the vine until that day when I drink it new in the kingdom of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 26:30; Luke 22:39; Acts 16:25"> When they had sung a hymn, they went out to the Mount of Olives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; John 16:32; Matthew 26:31"> Jesus said to them, “All of you will fall away, for it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'I will strike the shepherd</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the sheep will be scattered.'</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 28:16; Mark 16:7; Matthew 28:7"> But after I am raised up, I will go ahead of you into Galilee.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 26:33–35; Luke 22:33–34; John 13:36–38"> Peter said to him, “Even if all fall away, I will not.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 13:38; Mark 14:66–72; Genesis 1:5"> Jesus said to him, “Truly I say to you, today—yes, this very night—before the rooster crows twice you will deny me three times.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 19:8; John 13:37; Proverbs 16:18"> But Peter said, “If I must die with you, I will not deny you.” They all made the same promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 14:36; Mark 14:39; Psalms 18:5–6"> They came to the place called Gethsemane, and Jesus said to his disciples, “Sit here while I pray.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 22:44; Hebrews 5:7; Mark 5:37"> He took Peter, James, and John with him and began to be distressed and deeply troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 12:27; Mark 13:35–37; 1 Peter 5:8"> He said to them, “My soul is deeply grieved, even to the point of death. Remain here and watch.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 14:41; Deuteronomy 9:18; Hebrews 5:7"> Going a little farther, Jesus fell to the ground and prayed that if it were possible the hour might pass from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Galatians 4:6; Romans 8:15–16; John 5:30"> He said, “Abba, Father, all things are possible with you. Remove this cup from me. But not my will, but yours.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 26:40; Mark 14:29–31; Hebrews 12:3"> He came back and found them sleeping, and he said to Peter, “Simon, are you asleep? Could you not watch for one hour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 26:41; Matthew 24:42; Luke 21:36"> Watch and pray that you do not enter into temptation. The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 26:42–44; 2 Corinthians 12:8; Matthew 6:7"> Again he went away and prayed, and he used the same words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 9:33–34; Romans 3:19; Genesis 44:16"> When he came back again, he found them sleeping, for their eyes were heavy. They did not know what to say to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 14:35; Mark 9:31; John 17:1"> He came the third time and said to them, “Are you still sleeping and taking your rest? Enough! The hour has come. Look! The Son of Man is being betrayed into the hands of sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 26:46; John 18:1–2"> Get up; let us go. Look, the one who is betraying me is near.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 18:3–11; Luke 22:47–53; Matthew 26:47–56"> While he was still speaking, Judas, one of the twelve, arrived, and a large crowd was with him with swords and clubs, from the chief priests, the scribes, and the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 26:48–50; Proverbs 27:6; Psalms 55:20–21"> Now his betrayer had given them a sign, saying, “Whomever I kiss, he is the one. Seize him and lead him away under guard.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 23:7–10; Malachi 1:6; Mark 12:14"> When Judas arrived, immediately he came up to Jesus and said, “Rabbi,” and he kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 18:12; Lamentations 4:20; Acts 2:23"> Then they laid hands on him and seized him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Matthew 26:51–54; Luke 22:49–51"> But one of them who stood by drew his sword and struck the servant of the high priest and cut off his ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 22:52–53; 1 Samuel 26:18; 1 Samuel 24:14–15"> Jesus said to them, “Do you come out, as against a robber, with swords and clubs to capture me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 18:20; John 8:2; Luke 22:37"> When I was daily with you and I was teaching in the temple, you did not arrest me. But this was done that the scriptures might be fulfilled.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Mark 14:27; John 16:32; Psalms 88:18"> All those with Jesus left him and ran away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> A young man, wearing only a linen garment that was wrapped around him, was following Jesus. When the men seized him</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Mark 13:14–16; Genesis 39:12; Job 2:4"> he left the linen garment and ran away naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 26:57–68; John 18:12–14; Luke 22:54–62"> They led Jesus to the high priest. There were gathered with him all the chief priests, the elders, and the scribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 18:18; Matthew 26:3; John 18:25"> Now Peter followed him from a distance, as far as the courtyard of the high priest. He sat among the guards, who were near a fire to keep warm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16–18; Matthew 26:59–60; Matthew 5:22"> Now the chief priests and the entire Jewish council were seeking testimony against Jesus so they might put him to death. But they did not find any.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> For many brought false testimony against him, but even their testimony did not agree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Acts 6:13–14; Matthew 26:60–61; John 2:18–21"> Some stood up and brought false testimony against him; they said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 2:19; Hebrews 9:11; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> “We heard him say, 'I will destroy this temple made with hands, and in three days I will build another made without hands.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> Yet even their testimony did not agree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="John 19:9–10; Matthew 26:62–63; Mark 15:3–5"> The high priest stood up among them and asked Jesus, “Have you no answer? What is it these men testify against you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Matthew 26:63–64; Mark 15:2; Isaiah 53:7"> But he was silent and answered nothing. Again the high priest questioned him and said, “Are you the Christ, the Son of the Blessed?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:3"> Jesus said, “I am;<br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you will see the Son of Man</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when he sits at the right hand of power</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and comes with the clouds of heaven.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Matthew 26:65; Isaiah 36:22–1; Numbers 14:6"> The high priest tore his garments and said, “Do we still need witnesses?</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Leviticus 24:16; John 10:31–33; John 19:7"> You have heard the blasphemy. What is your decision?” They all condemned him as one who deserved death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Matthew 26:67–68; Acts 23:2; Isaiah 50:6"> Some began to spit on him and to cover his face and strike him and say to him, “Prophesy!” The officers took him and beat him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Mark 14:54; Matthew 26:69–75; John 18:25–27"> While Peter was below in the courtyard, one of the servant girls of the high priest came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Mark 14:54; Mark 10:47; Matthew 2:23"> She saw Peter warming himself, and she looked closely at him and said, “You were also with the Nazarene, Jesus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Mark 14:29–31; Matthew 26:71–72; John 13:36–38"> But he denied it, saying, “I neither know nor understand what you are talking about.” Then he went out into the courtyard.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some ancient copies add, “Then the rooster crowed,” but the best ancient copies do not have this phrase.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Luke 22:58; Mark 14:38; Galatians 6:1"> But the servant girl there saw him and began to say again to those who stood there, “This man is one of them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Luke 22:59–60; Matthew 26:73–74; Acts 2:7"> But he denied it again. After a little while those who stood there were saying to Peter, “Surely you are one of them, for you also are a Galilean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; 1 Corinthians 10:12; 2 Kings 10:32"> But he began to put himself under curses and to swear, “I do not know this man you are talking about.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Mark 14:30; Mark 14:68; Ezekiel 7:16"> The rooster immediately crowed a second time. Then Peter remembered the words that Jesus had said to him: “Before the rooster crows twice, you will deny me three times,” and he broke down and wept.</span>
