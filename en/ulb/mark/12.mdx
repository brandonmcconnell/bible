He said, “A man planted a vineyard, put a hedge around it, and dug a pit for a winepress. He built a watchtower and then leased the vineyard to vine growers. Then he went away on a journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:1–8; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 44:4"> At the right time, he sent a servant to the vine growers to receive from them some of the fruit of the vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15; Nehemiah 9:26; Jeremiah 29:26"> But they took him, beat him, and sent him away with nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Again he sent to them another servant, and they wounded him in the head and treated him shamefully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:6; Matthew 23:37; Nehemiah 9:30"> He sent yet another, and this one they killed. They treated many others in the same way, beating some and killing others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 1:1–2; Matthew 3:17; John 1:34"> He had still one more person to send, a beloved son. He was the last one he sent to them. He said, 'They will respect my son.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Matthew 2:16; Acts 2:23"> But the vine growers said to one another, 'This is the heir. Come, let us kill him, and the inheritance will be ours.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 21:39; Luke 20:15; Matthew 21:33"> They seized him, killed him, and threw him out of the vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 10:20–12; Matthew 21:43; Luke 19:27"> Therefore, what will the owner of the vineyard do? He will come and destroy the vine growers and will give the vineyard to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 28:16"> Have you not read this scripture?<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'The stone which the builders rejected</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">has been made the cornerstone.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 118:23; Acts 3:12–16; Habakkuk 1:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">This was from the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and it is marvelous in our eyes.'”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 22:22; John 7:44"> They sought to arrest Jesus, but they feared the crowd, for they knew that he had spoken this parable against them. So they left him and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Luke 11:54; Mark 3:6"> Then they sent some of the Pharisees and the Herodians to him to trap him with words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 20:22; John 7:18"> When they came, they said to him, “Teacher, we know that you care for no one's opinion, and you do not show partiality between people. You truly teach the way of God. Is it lawful to pay taxes to Caesar or not? Should we pay or not?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:2; Revelation 2:23; Mark 10:2"> But Jesus knew their hypocrisy and said to them, “Why do you test me? Bring me a denarius so I can look at it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 20:24–26; Revelation 3:12; Matthew 22:19–22"> They brought one to Jesus. He said to them, “Whose likeness and inscription is this?” They said, “Caesar's.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:7; 1 Peter 2:17; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> Jesus said, “Give to Caesar the things that are Caesar's, and to God the things that are God's.” They marveled at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 22:23–33; Acts 4:1–2; Acts 23:6–9"> Then Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection, came to him. They asked him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Ruth 4:5; Ruth 1:11–13"> “Teacher, Moses wrote for us, 'If a man's brother dies and leaves a wife behind him, but no child, the man should take the brother's wife, and raise up a descendant for his brother.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 22:25–28; Luke 20:29–33"> There were seven brothers; the first took a wife and then died, leaving no children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then the second took her and died, leaving no children, and the third likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The seven left no children. Last of all, the woman also died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In the resurrection, when they rise again, whose wife will she be? For all seven brothers had her as their wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 20:9; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Matthew 22:29"> Jesus said, “Is this not the reason you are mistaken, because you do not know the scriptures nor the power of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:35–36; Matthew 22:30; 1 John 3:2"> For when they rise from the dead, they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but they are like angels in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Luke 20:37; Genesis 17:7–8"> But concerning the dead that are raised, have you not read in the Book of Moses, in the account about the bush, how God spoke to him and said, 'I am the God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob'?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 14:9; Romans 4:17; Mark 12:24"> He is not the God of the dead, but of the living. You are quite mistaken.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–40; Luke 10:25–28; Luke 20:39"> One of the scribes came and heard their discussion; he saw that Jesus answered them well. He asked him, “What commandment is the most important of all?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:4; Luke 10:27; Galatians 3:20"> Jesus answered, “The most important is, 'Hear, Israel, the Lord our God, the Lord is one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5"> You must love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, with all your mind, and with all your strength.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 13:8–9; Matthew 22:39; Leviticus 19:18"> The second commandment is this, 'You must love your neighbor as yourself.' There is no other greater commandment than these.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Deuteronomy 4:35; Isaiah 46:9"> The scribe said, “Good, Teacher! You have truly said that God is one, and that there is no other besides him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Micah 6:6–8; 1 Samuel 15:22"> To love him with all the heart and with all the understanding and with all the strength, and to love one's neighbor as oneself, is even more than all burnt offerings and sacrifices.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 20:40; Matthew 22:46"> When Jesus saw that he had given a wise answer, he said to him, “You are not far from the kingdom of God.” After that, no one dared to ask Jesus any more questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Matthew 22:41–45; Matthew 26:55"> While Jesus was teaching in the temple courts, he said, “How is it that the scribes say the Christ is the son of David?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Matthew 22:43–45"> David himself, in the Holy Spirit, said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'The Lord said to my Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sit at my right hand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">until I make your enemies your footstool.”'</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Matthew 11:25; Matthew 1:23"> David himself calls him 'Lord,' so how can the Christ be David's son?” The large crowd gladly listened to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–7; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 11:43"> In his teaching Jesus said, “Beware of the scribes, who like to walk in long robes and they like the greetings they receive in the marketplaces</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="James 2:2–3"> and they like to have the chief seats in the synagogues and chief places at feasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Luke 20:47; Matthew 23:33"> They also devour widows' houses, and they pray long prayers for people to see. These men will receive greater condemnation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 21:1–4; 2 Kings 12:9; John 8:20"> Then Jesus sat down across from an offering box in the temple area; he was watching people as they dropped their money into the box. Many rich people put in large amounts of money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Then a poor widow came and put in two mites, worth about a penny.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Acts 11:29"> He called his disciples and said to them, “Truly I say to you, this poor widow has put in more than all of them who contributed to the offering box.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 1 John 3:17; Ezra 2:68–69"> For all of them gave out of their abundance. But this widow, out of her poverty, put in all of the money which she had to live on. “</span>
