<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 27:1–2; Luke 23:1–5; Luke 22:66"> Early in the morning, the chief priests met together with the elders and scribes and the entire Jewish council. Then they bound Jesus and led him away. They handed him over to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Matthew 27:11–14; 1 Timothy 6:13"> Pilate asked him, “Are you the King of the Jews?” He answered him, “You say so.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; Mark 15:5; John 18:29–31"> The chief priests were presenting many charges against Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 19:10; Matthew 27:13; Matthew 26:62"> Pilate again asked him, “Do you give no answer? See how many charges they are bringing against you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; Zechariah 3:8; Psalms 71:7"> But Jesus no longer answered Pilate, and that amazed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:39–40; Acts 25:9; Acts 24:27"> Now at the time of the feast, Pilate usually released to them one prisoner, a prisoner they requested.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 23:18–19; Matthew 27:16; Luke 23:25"> There with the rebels in prison, among the murderers held for their part in the rebellion, was a man named Barabbas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The crowd came to Pilate and began to ask him to do for them as he had done in the past.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:14–16; John 19:4–5; John 18:39"> Pilate answered them and said, “Do you want me to release to you the King of the Jews?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Titus 3:3; Genesis 4:4–6"> For he knew that it was because of envy that the chief priests had handed Jesus over to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 3:14; John 18:40; Hosea 5:1"> But the chief priests stirred up the crowd to cry out that Barabbas should be released instead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; Isaiah 9:6–7; Zechariah 9:9"> Pilate answered them again and said, “What then should I do with the King of the Jews?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> They shouted again, “Crucify him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:41; Luke 23:23–24; Isaiah 53:3"> Pilate said to them, “What wrong has he done?” But they shouted more and more, “Crucify him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 19:1; Matthew 27:26; Isaiah 50:6"> Pilate wanted to satisfy the crowd, so he released Barabbas to them. He scourged Jesus and then handed him over to be crucified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 19:9; Matthew 27:27–31; John 18:28"> The soldiers led him inside the courtyard (which is the government headquarters), and they called together the whole cohort of soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 27:28–30; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> They put a purple robe on Jesus, and they twisted together a crown of thorns and put it on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:42–43; Mark 15:2; Genesis 37:10"> They began to salute him and say, “Hail, King of the Jews!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Isaiah 53:3–5; Luke 23:11"> They struck his head with a reed staff and they spat on him. They bent their knees before him to pretend to worship him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 19:16; Matthew 27:31"> When they had mocked him, they took off of him the purple robe and put his own garments on him, and then led him out to crucify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:32; Luke 23:26; Romans 16:13"> A certain man, Simon of Cyrene, was coming in from the country (he was the father of Alexander and Rufus), and they forced him to carry his cross.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 27:33–44; John 19:17–27; Luke 23:27–38"> The soldiers brought Jesus to the place called Golgotha (which interpreted means, “Place of a Skull”).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 27:34; John 19:28–30; Luke 23:36"> They offered him wine mixed with myrrh, but he did not drink it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 23:34; John 19:23–24; Matthew 27:35–36"> They crucified him and divided up his garments by casting lots to determine what piece each soldier would take.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 19:14; Mark 15:33; Matthew 27:45"> It was the third hour when they crucified him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:37; Matthew 2:2; John 19:18–22"> On a sign they wrote the charge against him, “The king of the Jews.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 23:32–33; Matthew 27:38; John 19:18"> With him they crucified two robbers, one on the right of him and one on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The best ancient copies omit Mrk. 15:28, The scripture was fulfilled that says, 'He was counted with the lawless ones,' (compare LUK 22:37) .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Psalms 109:25; Lamentations 1:12"> Those who passed by insulted him, shaking their heads and saying, “Aha! You who would destroy the temple and rebuild it in three days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> save yourself and come down from the cross!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:23–24; Matthew 27:41–43; Luke 23:35–37"> In the same way the chief priests were mocking him with each other, along with the scribes, and said, “He saved others, but he cannot save himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Luke 23:39–43; Zechariah 9:9"> Let the Christ, the King of Israel, come down now from the cross, that we may see and believe,” and those who were crucified with him also taunted him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Luke 23:44–45; Amos 8:9–10"> At the sixth hour, darkness came over the whole land until the ninth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Matthew 27:46; Hebrews 5:7"> At the ninth hour Jesus cried out with a loud voice, “Eloi, Eloi, lama sabachthani?” which is interpreted, “My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:47–49; Mark 9:11–13; Matthew 17:11–13"> Some of those standing by heard his words and said, “Look, he is calling for Elijah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Mark 15:23; John 19:28–30"> Someone ran, put sour wine on a sponge, put it on a reed staff, and gave it to him to drink. The man said, “Let us see if Elijah comes to take him down.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 19:30; Luke 23:46; Matthew 27:50"> Then Jesus cried out with a loud voice and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–23; Luke 23:45; Hebrews 9:3–12"> The curtain of the temple was split in two from the top to the bottom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Luke 23:47–48; Matthew 27:43"> When the centurion who stood and faced Jesus saw that he had died in this way, he said, “Truly this man was the Son of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Luke 8:2–3; Matthew 27:55–56"> There were also women who looked on from a distance. Among them were Mary Magdalene, Mary (the mother of James the younger and of Joses), and Salome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Matthew 27:55–56"> When he was in Galilee they followed him and served him. Many other women also came up with him to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 23:50–56; John 19:38–42; John 19:31"> When evening had come, because it was the Day of Preparation, that is, the day before the Sabbath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 2:38; Luke 2:25; Luke 23:51"> Joseph of Arimathea came there. He was a respected member of the council, who was waiting for the kingdom of God. He boldly went in to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 19:31–37"> Pilate was amazed that Jesus was already dead; he called the centurion and asked him if Jesus was dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 27:58; John 19:38; Mark 15:39"> When Pilate learned from the centurion that Jesus was dead, he gave the body to Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 22:16"> Joseph had bought a linen cloth. He took him down from the cross, wrapped him in the linen cloth, and laid him in a tomb that had been cut out of a rock. Then he rolled a stone against the entrance of the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 15:40; Mark 16:1; Luke 23:55–2"> Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joses saw the place where Jesus was buried.</span>
</div>
