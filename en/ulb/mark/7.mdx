<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Matthew 7:3–5; Romans 14:14"> They saw that some of his disciples ate bread with hands that were defiled, that is, unwashed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 1:14; Mark 7:13; Colossians 2:8"> (For the Pharisees and all the Jews do not eat unless they wash their hands, because they hold to the tradition of the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 9:10; Matthew 23:25; Luke 11:38–39"> When the Pharisees come from the marketplace, they do not eat unless they bathe themselves, and they hold fast to many other traditions, such as the washing of cups, pots, copper vessels, and the couches upon which they eat.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:6; Acts 21:21; Matthew 15:2"> The Pharisees and the scribes asked Jesus, “Why do your disciples not walk according to the tradition of the elders, for they eat their bread with unwashed hands?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 15:7–9"> But he said to them, “Isaiah prophesied well about you hypocrites. He wrote,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'This people honors me with their lips,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but their heart is far from me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Matthew 15:9; James 1:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Empty worship they offer me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">teaching the rules of men as their doctrines.'</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Isaiah 1:12"> You abandon the commandment of God and hold fast to the tradition of men.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16–17; Galatians 2:21; Isaiah 29:13"> He also said to them, “How well you reject the commandment of God so you may keep your tradition!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 21:17; Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 5:16"> For Moses said, 'Honor your father and your mother,' and, 'He who speaks evil of his father or mother will surely die.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 23:18; 1 Timothy 5:4–8; Matthew 15:5"> But you say, 'If a man says to his father or mother, “Whatever help you would have received from me is Corban”' (that is to say, 'Given to God'),</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> then you no longer permit him to do anything for his father or his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 7:9; Titus 1:14; Matthew 5:17–20"> You are making the commandment of God void by your tradition which you have handed down. And many similar things you do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:10; Isaiah 6:9; Proverbs 8:5"> He called the crowd again and said to them, “Listen to me, all of you, and understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 4:23; Leviticus 11:42–47; Titus 1:15"> There is nothing from outside of a person that can defile him when it enters into him. It is what comes out of the person that defiles him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The best ancient copies omit vs. 16. If any man has ears to hear, let him hear .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Mark 9:28"> Now when Jesus left the crowd and entered the house, his disciples asked him about the parable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 15:16–17; Hebrews 5:11; Isaiah 28:9–10"> Jesus said, “Are you also still without understanding? Do you not see that whatever enters into a person from outside cannot defile him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 15:17; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Colossians 2:21–22"> because it cannot go into his heart, but it goes into his stomach and then passes out into the latrine?” With this statement Jesus declared all foods clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 3:6; James 1:14–15; James 4:1"> He said, “It is that which comes out of the person that defiles him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Romans 8:7–8; James 4:1–3"> For from within a person, out of the heart, proceed evil thoughts, sexual immorality, theft, murder,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 20:15; Matthew 6:23; 1 Peter 2:15"> adultery, coveting, wickedness, deceit, sensuality, envy, slander, pride, folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 3:17"> All these evils come from within, and they are what defile a person.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:21–28; Matthew 11:21; Ezekiel 28:2"> He got up from there and went away to the region of Tyre and Sidon. There he went into a house and he wanted no one to know where he was, but he could not be hidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Mark 9:17–23; Acts 10:25–26"> In fact<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some ancient copies have the word, But as an alternative reading.">b</a></sup>, as soon a woman whose little daughter had an unclean spirit heard about him, she came and fell down at his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Isaiah 49:12; Galatians 3:28"> Now the woman was a Greek, a Syrophoenician by descent. She begged him to cast out the demon from her daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:23–28; Matthew 7:6; Acts 22:21"> He said to her, “Let the children first be fed. For it is not right to take the children's bread and throw it to the dogs.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Romans 3:29; Acts 11:17–18"> But she answered and said to him, “Yes, Lord, even the dogs under the table eat the children's crumbs.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; Matthew 5:3; 1 John 3:8"> He said to her, “Because you have said this, you are free to go. The demon has gone out of your daughter.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 3:8; John 4:50–52"> She went back to her house and found the child lying on the bed, and the demon was gone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 5:20; Matthew 15:29–31; Matthew 4:25"> Then he went out again from the region of Tyre, and went through Sidon to the Sea of Galilee up into the region of the Decapolis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:14; Mark 5:23; Matthew 9:32–33"> They brought to him someone who was deaf and had difficulty speaking, and they begged him to lay his hand on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:23; John 9:6–7; Mark 5:40"> He took him aside from the crowd privately, and he put his fingers into his ears, and after spitting, he touched his tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 11:41; Mark 5:41; Mark 6:41"> He looked up to heaven, he sighed and said to him, “Ephphatha,” that is to say, “Open!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5–6; Psalms 33:9; Mark 2:12"> At once his ears were opened, his tongue was released, and he began to speak plainly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Mark 8:26; Mark 3:12"> Jesus ordered them to tell no one. But the more he ordered them, the more abundantly they proclaimed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 6:51; Mark 5:42; Exodus 4:10–11"> They were extremely astonished, saying, “He has done all things well. He even makes the deaf hear and the mute speak.”</span>
</div>
