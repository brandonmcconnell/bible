<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 6:14–18; Esther 7:10; Esther 1:12–21"> After these things, when the anger of King Ahasuerus subsided, he thought about Vashti and what she had done. He also thought about the decree that he had made against her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 1:2; Genesis 12:14; Esther 6:14"> Then the king's young men who served him said, “Let a search be made on the king's behalf for beautiful young virgins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esther 2:12–15; Esther 2:8–9; Esther 1:1–2"> Let the king appoint officers in all the provinces of his kingdom, to gather together all the beautiful young virgins to the harem in the fortress in Susa. Let them be put under the care of Hegai, the king's official, who is in charge of the women, and let him give them their cosmetics.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 14:6; Matthew 20:16; Esther 1:21"> Let the young girl who pleases the king become queen in the place of Vashti.” This advice pleased the king, and he did so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:5; 1 Samuel 9:1; Esther 10:3"> There was a certain Jew in the fortress of Susa whose name was Mordecai son of Jair son of Shimei son of Kish, who was a Benjamite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 24:14–15; 2 Chronicles 36:20; Jeremiah 24:1"> He had been taken away from Jerusalem with the exiles along with those taken with Jehoiachin, king of Judah, whom Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylonia carried away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 2:15; Esther 1:11; 2 Corinthians 6:18"> He was caring for Hadassah, that is, Esther, his uncle's daughter, because she had neither father nor mother. The young woman had a beautiful figure and was lovely in appearance. Mordecai took her as his own daughter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esther 2:3; Esther 2:15"> When the king's order and decree were proclaimed, many young women were brought to the fortress of Susa. They were put under Hegai's care. Esther also was taken into the king's palace and put under the care of Hegai, the overseer of the women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 39:21; Proverbs 16:7; Esther 2:12"> The young girl pleased him, and she found favor with him. Immediately he provided her with cosmetics and her portion of food. He assigned to her seven servant girls from the king's palace, and he moved her and the servant girls to the best place in the house of the women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esther 2:20; Matthew 10:16; Esther 3:8"> Esther had not told anyone who her people or relatives were, for Mordecai had instructed her not to tell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 37:14; 1 Samuel 17:18; Acts 15:36"> Every day Mordecai walked back and forth in front of the courtyard outside the house of the women, to learn about Esther's welfare, and about what would be done with her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:4–5; Proverbs 7:17; Isaiah 57:9"> When the turn came for each girl to go to King Ahasuerus—complying with the regulations for the women, each girl had to complete twelve months of beauty treatments, six months with oil of myrrh, and six with perfumes and cosmetics—</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> when a young woman went to the king, whatever she desired was given to her from the house of the women, for her to take to the palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 4:11; Genesis 34:19; Isaiah 62:4–5"> In the evening she would go in, and in the morning she would return to the second house of the women, and to the custody of Shaashgaz, the king's official, who was in charge of the concubines. She would not return to the king again unless he had taken great pleasure in her and called for her again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Song of Songs 6:9; Esther 2:3; Esther 9:29"> Now when the time came for Esther (daughter of Abihail, the uncle of Mordecai, who had taken her as his own daughter) to go in to the king, she did not ask for anything but what Hegai the king's official, who was in charge of the women, suggested. Now Esther received the favor of all who saw her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esther 8:9; Esther 2:3; Esther 2:1"> Esther was taken to King Ahasuerus into the royal residence on the tenth month, which is the month of Tebeth, in the seventh year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Luke 1:48–52; Ezekiel 17:24"> The king loved Esther more than all the other women and she received acceptance and favor before him, more than all the other virgins. So he set the royal crown on her head and made her queen instead of Vashti.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Song of Songs 3:11; Luke 14:8; Judges 14:10–17"> The king gave a great feast for all his officials and his servants, “Esther's feast,” and he granted relief from taxation to the provinces. He also gave gifts with royal generosity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esther 2:21; Esther 2:3–4; Esther 5:13"> Now when the virgins had been gathered together a second time, Mordecai was sitting at the king's gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; Esther 2:10; Esther 2:7"> Esther had not yet told anyone about her relatives or her people, as Mordecai had instructed her. She continued to follow Mordecai's advice, as she had done when she was raised by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esther 6:2; 1 Kings 16:9; 2 Kings 21:23"> In those days, while Mordecai was sitting at the king's gate, two of the king's officials, Bigthana and Teresh, who guarded the doorway, became angry and sought to do harm to King Ahasuerus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esther 6:1–2; Romans 11:33; Ecclesiastes 10:20"> When the matter was revealed to Mordecai, he told Queen Esther, and Esther spoke to the king in the name of Mordecai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 40:22; Genesis 40:19; Esther 10:2"> The report was investigated and confirmed, and both the men were hanged from a gallows. This account was written in the book of the chronicles in the presence of the king.</span>
</div>
</div>
