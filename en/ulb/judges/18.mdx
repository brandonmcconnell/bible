<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 17:6; Judges 19:1; Judges 1:34"> In those days there was no king in Israel. The tribe of the descendants of Dan was looking for a territory to live in, for up to that day they had not received any inheritance from among the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 13:25; Joshua 2:1; Judges 18:11"> The people of Dan sent five men from the whole number of their tribe, men who were experienced warriors from Zorah and from Eshtaol, to scout the land on foot, and to look it over. They said to them, “Go and look over the land.” They came to the hill country of Ephraim, to the house of Micah, and they spent the night there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 12:6; Genesis 27:22; Matthew 26:73"> When they were near Micah's house, they recognized the speech of the young Levite. So they stopped and asked him, “Who brought you here? What are you doing in this place? Why are you here?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 17:10; Ezekiel 13:19; Proverbs 28:21"> He said to them, “This is what Micah has done for me: He has hired me to become his priest.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:10; Isaiah 30:1; 1 Kings 22:5"> They said to him, “Please seek the advice of God, so we may know whether the journey we are going on will be successful.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 22:6; 1 Kings 22:15; Psalms 33:18"> The priest said to them, “Go in peace. Yahweh will lead you in the way you should go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:27–28; 1 Samuel 3:13"> Then the five men left and came to Laish, and they saw that the people were living in safety, in the same way the Sidonians lived, undisturbed and secure. There was no one who conquered them or who oppressed them in any way in the land. They lived far away from the Sidonians and had no dealings with anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 18:11; Judges 18:2; Judges 13:2"> They returned to their tribe in Zorah and Eshtaol. Their relatives asked them, “What is your report?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 2:23–24; Numbers 13:30; Joshua 18:3"> They said, “Come! Let us attack them! We have seen the land and it is very good. Are you doing nothing? Do not be slow to attack and conquer the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 18:27; Judges 18:7; Deuteronomy 2:29"> When you go, you will come to a people who think they are secure, and the land is wide! God has given it to you—a place that does not lack anything in the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 18:16"> Six hundred men of the tribe of Dan, armed with weapons of war, set out from Zorah and Eshtaol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:25; Joshua 15:60; 2 Chronicles 1:4"> They went up and camped at Kiriath Jearim, in Judah. This is why people called that place Mahaneh Dan to this day; it is west of Kiriath Jearim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 24:30; Judges 19:1; Judges 18:2–3"> They went away from there to the hill country of Ephraim and came to the house of Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 17:5; Judges 18:3–4; 1 Samuel 14:28"> Then the five men who had gone to scout the country of Laish said to their relatives, “Do you know that in these houses there are an ephod, household gods, a carved figure, and a cast metal figure? Decide now what you will do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 43:27; 1 Samuel 17:22; John 14:27"> So they turned in there and came to the house of the young Levite, at the house of Micah, and they greeted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 18:11"> Now the six hundred Danites, armed with weapons of war, stood at the entrance of the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 18:14; Judges 17:4–5; Judges 18:2"> The five men who had gone to scout out the land went there and they took the carved figure, the ephod, the household gods, and the cast metal figure, while the priest stood by the opening of the gate with the six hundred men armed with weapons of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> When these went into Micah's house and took the carved figure, the ephod, the household gods, and the cast metal figure, the priest said to them, “What are you doing?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 29:9; Job 21:5; Judges 17:10"> They said to him, “Be quiet! Put your hand on your mouth and come with us, and be to us a father and a priest. Is it better for you to be priest for the house of one man, or to be priest for a tribe and a clan in Israel?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Ezekiel 13:19; Proverbs 30:15"> The priest's heart was glad. He took the ephod, the household gods, and the carved figure, and went along with the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So they turned and went away. They put the small children in front of themselves, as well as the cattle and their possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> When they were a good distance from the house of Micah, the men who were in the houses near Micah's house were called together, and they caught up with the Danites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 114:5; 2 Samuel 14:5; 1 Samuel 11:5"> They shouted to the Danites, and they turned and said to Micah, “Why have you been called together?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18–19; Isaiah 44:18–20; Psalms 115:8"> He said, “You stole the gods that I made, you have taken my priest, and you are leaving. What else do I have left? How can you ask me, 'What is bothering you?'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 27:2; Job 3:5; 1 Samuel 30:6"> The people of Dan said to him, “You should not let us hear you say anything, or some very angry men will attack you, and you and your family will be killed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then the people of Dan went their way. When Micah saw that they were too strong for him, he turned and went back to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:7; Judges 18:10"> The people of Dan took what Micah had made, as well as his priest, and they came to Laish, to a people who were undisturbed and secure and they struck them with the edge of the sword and burned the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:6; Judges 18:7; Numbers 13:21"> There was no one to rescue them because it was a long way from Sidon, and they had no dealings with anyone. It was in the valley that is near Beth Rehob. The Danites rebuilt the city and lived there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Genesis 14:14; 1 Kings 15:20"> They named the city Dan, the name of Dan their ancestor, who was one of Israel's sons. But the name of the city used to be Laish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:10–11; Judges 13:1; Judges 17:5"> The people of Dan set up the carved figure for themselves. Jonathan son of Gershom, son of Moses, he and his sons were priests for the tribe of the Danites until the day of the land's captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; 1 Samuel 1:3; Judges 19:18"> So they worshiped Micah's carved figure that he made as long as the house of God was at Shiloh.</span>
