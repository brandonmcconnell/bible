<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Genesis 17:7–8; Leviticus 26:42"> The angel of Yahweh went up from Gilgal to Bokim, and said, “I brought you up from Egypt, and have brought you to the land I swore to give to your fathers. I said, 'I will never break my covenant with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2–4; Jeremiah 2:36; Ezra 9:1–3"> You must make no covenant with those who live in this land. You must tear down their altars.' But you have not listened to my voice. What is this that you have done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 23:13; Numbers 33:55; Deuteronomy 7:16"> So now I say, 'I will not drive the Canaanites out before you, but they will become thorns in your sides, and their gods will become a trap for you.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Proverbs 17:10; Zechariah 12:10"> When the angel of Yahweh spoke these words to all the people of Israel, the people shouted and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:9; Genesis 35:8; Joshua 7:26"> They called that place Bokim. There they offered sacrifices to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 24:28–31; Joshua 22:6"> Now when Joshua had sent the people on their way, the people of Israel each went to the place assigned, to take ownership of their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; Joshua 24:31; 2 Chronicles 24:2"> The people served Yahweh during the lifetime of Joshua and of the elders who outlived him, those who had seen all of Yahweh's great deeds he had done for Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 24:29–30"> Joshua son of Nun the servant of Yahweh, died at the age of 110 years old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 19:50; Joshua 24:30"> They buried him within the border of the land he was assigned in Timnath Heres, in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:12; Titus 1:16; Exodus 5:2"> All that generation was also gathered to their fathers. Another generation grew up after them who did not know Yahweh or what he had done for Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 4:1; Judges 6:1; Judges 3:7"> The people of Israel did what was evil in the sight of Yahweh and they served the Baals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 13:5; Deuteronomy 6:14–15"> They broke away from Yahweh, the God of their fathers, who had brought them out of the land of Egypt. They went after other gods, the very gods of the peoples who were around them, and they bowed down to them. They provoked Yahweh to anger because</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 10:6; Psalms 106:36; Judges 3:7"> they broke away from Yahweh and worshiped Baal and the Ashtoreths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 17:20; Psalms 106:40–42; Deuteronomy 32:30"> The anger of Yahweh burned against Israel, and he gave them to the raiders who stole their possessions from them. He sold them as slaves who were held by the strength of their enemies around them, so they could no longer defend themselves against their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Joshua 23:15–16; Leviticus 26:14–46"> Wherever Israel went out to fight, Yahweh's hand was against them to defeat them, just as he had sworn to them and they were in terrible distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 13:20; Psalms 106:43–45; 1 Samuel 12:11"> Then Yahweh raised up judges, who saved them out of the hand of those who were stealing their possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 1:6; Deuteronomy 9:12; Judges 2:7"> Yet they would not listen to their judges. They were unfaithful to Yahweh and gave themselves like prostitutes to other gods and worshiped them. They soon turned aside from the way their fathers had lived—those who had obeyed the commandments of Yahweh—but they themselves did not do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; Joshua 1:5; Exodus 2:24"> When Yahweh raised up judges for them, Yahweh helped the judges and rescued them from the hand of their enemies all the days the judge lived. Yahweh had pity on them as they groaned because of those who oppressed them and afflicted them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 8:33; Judges 4:1; Matthew 23:32"> But when the judge died, they would turn away and do things that were even more corrupt than their fathers had done. They would go after other gods to serve them and worship them. They refused to give up any of their evil practices or their stubborn ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Joshua 23:16; Exodus 32:10–11"> The anger of Yahweh burned against Israel; he said, “Because this nation has broken the terms of my covenant that I had set in place for their fathers—because they have not listened to my voice—</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 23:13; Judges 2:3; Judges 3:3"> I will not, from now on, drive out from before them any of the nations that Joshua left when he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; Deuteronomy 8:16; Deuteronomy 13:3"> I will do this so that I may test Israel, whether or not they will keep the way of Yahweh and walk in it, as their fathers kept it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> That is why Yahweh left those nations and did not drive them out quickly and give them into the hand of Joshua.</span>
