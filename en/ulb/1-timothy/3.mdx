<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 1:7; Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:2"> This saying is trustworthy: If someone desires to be an overseer, he desires a good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:6–9; 2 Timothy 2:24; Romans 12:13"> Therefore the overseer must be without reproach. He must be a husband of one wife. He must be moderate, sensible, orderly, and hospitable. He must be able to teach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 1:7; Ephesians 5:18; Titus 3:2"> He must not be addicted to wine, not a brawler, but instead, gentle, peaceful. He must not be a lover of money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Titus 1:6; Genesis 18:19; 1 Timothy 3:12"> He should manage his own household well, and his children should obey him with all respect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:13; Ephesians 5:24; Acts 20:28"> For if a man does not know how to manage his own household, how will he care for a church of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:4; Hebrews 5:12–13; 1 Peter 2:2"> He should not be a new convert, so that he does not swell with pride and might fall into condemnation as the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Timothy 2:26; Colossians 4:5"> He must also have a good reputation with those outside, so that he does not fall into disgrace and the trap of the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:1; 1 Timothy 5:23; Acts 6:3–6"> Deacons, likewise, should be dignified, not double-talkers. They should not drink too much wine or be greedy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19; 2 John 1:9–10; 1 Timothy 1:5"> They should keep the revealed truth of the faith with a clean conscience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:22; 1 Timothy 3:13; Titus 1:6–7"> They should also be approved first, then they should serve because they are blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Titus 2:3; Titus 3:2; 1 Timothy 3:2"> Women in the same way should be dignified. They should not be slanderers. They should be moderate and faithful in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; 1 Timothy 3:4–5; Philippians 1:1"> Deacons must be husbands of one wife. They must manage well their children and household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Hebrews 6:10; Acts 21:35"> For those who have served well acquire for themselves a good standing and great confidence in the faith that is in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:34; Philemon 1:22; 2 Corinthians 1:15–17"> I am writing these things to you, and I expect to come to you soon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Ephesians 2:21–22; 1 Thessalonians 1:9"> But if I delay, I am writing so that you may know how to behave in the household of God, which is the church of the living God, the pillar and support of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:14; Hebrews 1:3; Mark 16:19"> It is undeniable that the revealed truth of godliness is great:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He appeared in the flesh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">was justified by the Spirit,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">was seen by angels,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">was proclaimed among nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">was believed on in the world,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and was taken up in glory.”</span></span>
