<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:3; Matthew 6:9–10; James 5:16"> Therefore first of all, I urge that requests, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgivings be made for all people,</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 1 Timothy 1:1; 1 Peter 2:20"> This is good and acceptable before God our savior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:32; 2 Peter 3:9; Ezekiel 18:23"> He desires all people to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:6; Galatians 3:20; Hebrews 9:15"> For there is one God, and there is one mediator for God and man, the man Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 John 2:1–2"> He gave himself as a ransom for all, as the testimony at the right time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 2 Timothy 1:11; Romans 9:1"> For this purpose, I myself, was made a herald and an apostle. I am telling the truth. I am not lying. I am a teacher of the Gentiles in faith and truth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 134:2; Psalms 63:4; Hebrews 10:22"> Therefore, I want men in every place to pray and to lift up holy hands without anger or arguing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Titus 2:3–5; Psalms 149:4"> Likewise, I want the women to dress themselves in proper clothing, with modesty and self-control. They should not have braided hair, or gold, or pearls, or expensive clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 3:3–5; Revelation 2:19"> I want them to dress with what is appropriate for women who profess godliness through good works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:34–35; Ephesians 5:22–24; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> A woman should learn in silence and with all submission.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:34"> I do not permit a woman to teach or to exercise authority over a man, but to live in quietness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 2:18; 1 Corinthians 11:8–9; Genesis 1:27"> For Adam was formed first, then Eve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:3; Genesis 3:6; Genesis 3:12–13"> Adam was not deceived, but the woman was deceived and became a transgressor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 2:12; Genesis 3:15–16; 1 Peter 4:7"> However, she will be saved through bearing children, if they continue in faith and love and sanctification with soundness of mind.</span>
