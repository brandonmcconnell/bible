<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:1; 1 Corinthians 1:1; Philemon 1:1"> Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through the will of God, and Timothy our brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:2; Ephesians 1:1; Romans 1:7"> to the believers and faithful brothers in Christ who are at Colossae. May grace be to you, and peace from God our Father.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some versions add, and the Lord Jesus Christ .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:2; Colossians 1:9; 2 Timothy 1:3"> We give thanks to God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, and we always pray for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:15; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 1 Thessalonians 1:3"> We have heard of your faith in Christ Jesus and of the love that you have for all those set apart for God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3–4; Ephesians 1:13; 2 Timothy 4:8"> You have this love because of the certain hope reserved for you in heaven. You heard about this confident expectation before in the word of truth, the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:16; Philippians 1:11; Colossians 1:23"> which has come to you. This gospel is bearing fruit and is growing in all the world. It has been doing this in you also from the day you heard it and learned about the grace of God in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 4:12; Philemon 1:23; Colossians 4:7"> This is the gospel as you learned it from Epaphras, our beloved fellow servant, who is a faithful servant of Christ on our behalf.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some versions read, on your behalf .">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 15:30; 2 Timothy 1:7; Galatians 5:22"> Epaphras has made known to us your love in the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 3:17; Philippians 1:9–11; James 1:5"> Because of this love, from the day we heard this we have not stopped praying for you. We have been asking that you will be filled with the knowledge of his will in all wisdom and spiritual understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 4:1; Hebrews 13:21; 2 Peter 1:2–3"> We have been praying that you will walk worthily of the Lord in pleasing ways. We have been praying that you will bear fruit in every good deed and that you will grow in the knowledge of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 3:16; Philippians 4:13; Ephesians 4:2"> We pray you may be strengthened in every ability according to the power of his glory into all perseverance and patience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 20:32; Ephesians 1:11; Ephesians 1:18"> We pray that you will joyfully give thanks to the Father, who has made you able to have a share in the inheritance of the saints in light.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some versions read, who has made us able to have a share .">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Acts 26:18; 1 Peter 2:9"> He has rescued us from the dominion of darkness and transferred us to the kingdom of his beloved Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Romans 3:24–25; 1 Peter 1:19–20"> In his Son we have redemption, the forgiveness of sins.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some versions read, In his Son we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of sins .">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; John 14:9; John 1:1"> The Son is the image of the invisible God. He is the firstborn of all creation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:36; John 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> For by him all things were created, those in the heavens and those on the earth, the visible and the invisible things. Whether thrones or dominions or governments or authorities, all things were created by him and for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Acts 17:28"> He himself is before all things, and in him all things hold together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 1:22–23; Revelation 1:5; Ephesians 4:15–16"> He is the head of the body, the church. He is the beginning and the firstborn from among the dead, so he has first place among all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 2:9; John 1:16; Ephesians 1:23"> For God was pleased that all his fullness should live in him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:18–21; Ephesians 1:10; 1 John 4:9–10"> and to reconcile through the Son all things to himself. God made peace through the blood of his cross. God reconciled all things to himself, whether the things on the earth or the things in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 2:12; Titus 3:3–7; Ephesians 2:1–3"> At one time, you also were strangers to God and were his enemies in mind and in evil deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 5:27; Jude 1:24; Ephesians 1:4"> But now he has reconciled you by his physical body through death. He did this in order to present you holy, blameless, and above reproach before him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 3:6; Matthew 24:13–14"> if you continue in the faith, established and firm, not moved away from the certain hope of the gospel that you heard. This is the gospel that was proclaimed to every person created under heaven. This is the gospel of which I, Paul, became a servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 5:3; 2 Timothy 1:8; 2 Corinthians 1:5–8"> Now I rejoice in my sufferings for you, and I fill up in my flesh what is lacking of the afflictions of Christ for the sake of his body, which is the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ephesians 3:2; Colossians 1:23; Galatians 2:7–8"> It is of this church that I am a servant, according to the responsibility from God that was given to me for you, to fulfill the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 8:10; Romans 16:25–26; Ephesians 3:3–10"> This is the secret truth that was hidden for ages and for generations. But now it has been revealed to those who believe in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:20; Romans 8:10; Ephesians 3:16–17"> It is to them that God wanted to make known the riches of the glory of this secret truth among the Gentiles, which is Christ in you, the hope of glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 5:27; Jeremiah 3:15; Ephesians 4:11–13"> This is he whom we proclaim. We admonish every person, and we teach every person with all wisdom, so that we may present every person complete in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:10; Ephesians 1:19; 1 Corinthians 9:25–27"> For this I labor and strive according to his energy that is at work in me in power.</span>
</div>
