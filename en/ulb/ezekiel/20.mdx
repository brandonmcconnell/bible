<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:1; Ezekiel 30:20; Ezekiel 24:1"> It came about in the seventh year, on the tenth day of the fifth month, that elders of Israel came to inquire of Yahweh and sat before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Then the word of Yahweh came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 28:9; Matthew 15:8–9; Psalms 50:15–21"> “Son of man, declare to the elders of Israel and say to them, 'The Lord Yahweh says this: Have you come to inquire of me? As I live, I will not be inquired of by you!—this is the Lord Yahweh's declaration.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:2; Ezekiel 23:36; Ezekiel 14:20"> Will you judge them? Will you judge, son of man? Let them know about their fathers' abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Ezekiel 20:15; Exodus 3:8"> Say to them, 'The Lord Yahweh says this: On the day that I chose Israel and raised my hand to swear an oath to the descendants of the house of Jacob, and made myself known to them in the land of Egypt, when I raised my hand to swear an oath to them. I said, “I am Yahweh your God”—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Jeremiah 32:22; Ezekiel 20:15"> on that day I raised my hand to swear an oath to them that I would bring them out of the land of Egypt into a land that I had carefully selected for them. It was flowing with milk and honey; it was the most beautiful ornament among all the lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 18:3; Ezekiel 20:8; Leviticus 11:44"> I said to them, “Let each man throw away the detestable things from before his eyes and the idols of Egypt. Do not make yourselves unclean; I am Yahweh your God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:7; Isaiah 63:10; Ezekiel 20:21"> But they rebelled against me and were unwilling to listen to me. Each man did not throw away the detestable things from before his eyes nor forsake the idols of Egypt, so I determined to pour out my fury upon them to satisfy my wrath among them in the middle of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:7; Ezekiel 20:22; Exodus 32:12"> I acted for my name's sake so it would not be profaned in the eyes of the nations among whom they were staying. I made myself known to them, in their eyes, by bringing them out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 15:22; Exodus 13:17–18; Exodus 14:17–22"> So I sent them out of the land of Egypt and brought them into the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Leviticus 18:5; Deuteronomy 4:8"> Then I gave them my statutes and made my decrees known to them, by which a man will live if he obeys them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:20; Ezekiel 37:28; Leviticus 21:23"> I also gave them my Sabbaths as a sign between myself and them, for them to know that I am Yahweh who makes them holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:21; Ezekiel 20:8; Isaiah 56:6"> But the house of Israel rebelled against me in the wilderness. They did not walk in my statutes; instead, they rejected my decrees, by which a man will live if he obeys them. They greatly profaned my Sabbaths, so I said I would pour out my fury upon them in the wilderness in order to end them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:9; Ezekiel 36:22–23; Ezekiel 20:22"> But I acted for my name's sake so it would not be profaned in the eyes of the nations, in whose sight I had brought them out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Psalms 106:26; Hebrews 3:18"> So I myself also raised my hand to swear an oath to them in the wilderness not to bring them into the land that I had been going to give them, a land flowing with milk and honey, which was the most beautiful ornament among all the lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 15:39; Ezekiel 20:8; Ezekiel 14:3–4"> I swore this because they had rejected my decrees and did not walk in my statutes, and they profaned my Sabbaths, since their hearts walked after their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:13; Jeremiah 4:27; Jeremiah 5:18"> But my eye spared them from their destruction and I did not annihilate them in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 1:2–4; Psalms 78:6–8; 1 Peter 1:18"> I said to their children in the wilderness, “Do not walk in the statutes of your fathers, or keep their rules, or defile yourselves with their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Deuteronomy 5:32–8; Ezekiel 37:24"> I am Yahweh your God, walk in my statutes; keep my decrees and obey them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 58:13; Jeremiah 17:22; Exodus 31:13–17"> Keep my Sabbaths holy so that they will be a sign between me and you, so that you will know that I am Yahweh your God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:13; Numbers 21:5; Ezekiel 20:8"> But their sons and daughters rebelled against me. They did not walk in my statutes or keep my decrees, by which a man might live if he obeys them. They profaned my Sabbaths, so I determined to pour out my fury upon them to satisfy my wrath against them in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:14; Ezekiel 20:9; Jeremiah 14:21"> But I turned my hand away and acted for my name's sake, so it would not be profaned in the eyes of the nations in whose sight I had brought out the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:64–68; Deuteronomy 32:26–27"> I myself also lifted up my hand to swear to them in the wilderness, that I would scatter them among the nations and disperse them among the lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:16; Ezekiel 6:9; Amos 2:4"> I decided to do this since they had not obeyed my decrees, and since they had rejected my statutes and profaned my Sabbaths. Their eyes were longing after their fathers' idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 81:12; Isaiah 66:4; Ezekiel 20:39"> Then I also gave them statutes that were not good, and decrees by which they could not live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Leviticus 18:21; Ezekiel 20:31"> I declared them to be unclean through their gifts—they made a sacrifice of every firstborn of the womb and caused them to pass through the fire—that I might fill them with horror so they will know that I am Yahweh!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Ezekiel 2:7; Ezekiel 3:11"> Therefore, son of man, speak to the house of Israel and say to them, 'The Lord Yahweh says this: In this also your fathers blasphemed me when they were unfaithful to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:13; Nehemiah 9:22–26; Jeremiah 3:6"> When I brought them into the land that I had sworn to give them, and then whenever they saw any high hill and leafy tree, they offered their sacrifices, they provoked me by their offerings, and there they also burned their fragrant incense and poured out their drink offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:24–25; Ezekiel 16:31"> Then I said to them, “What is this high place where you bring offerings there?” So the name is called Bamah to this day.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; Jeremiah 7:26; Judges 2:19"> Therefore say to the house of Israel, 'The Lord Yahweh says this: Why do you make yourselves unclean with the ways of your fathers? So why do you act like prostitutes, searching for disgusting things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:26; Psalms 106:37–39; Jeremiah 7:31"> When you offer up your gifts—when you cause your sons to pass through the fire—to this day you defile yourselves with all your idols. So why should I let you seek me, house of Israel? As I live—this is the Lord Yahweh's declaration—I will not allow you to seek me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:5; Jeremiah 44:17; Psalms 139:2"> The thought that is forming in your mind will never happen. You say, “Let us be like the other nations, like the clans in the other lands who worship wood and stone.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:5; Ezekiel 8:18; Jeremiah 44:6"> As I live—this is the Lord Yahweh's declaration—I will certainly reign over you with a mighty hand, a raised arm, and fury that will be poured out on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:38; Amos 9:9–10; Ezekiel 34:16"> I will bring you out from the other peoples and will gather you out of the countries among whom you have been scattered. I will do this with a mighty hand and with fury poured out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hosea 2:14; Jeremiah 2:35; Ezekiel 17:20"> Then I will bring you into the wilderness of the peoples, and there I will judge you face to face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5–10; Numbers 11:1–35; Ezekiel 20:13"> As I judged your fathers in the wilderness of the land of Egypt, so also I will judge you—this is the Lord Yahweh's declaration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:13; Leviticus 27:32; Matthew 25:32–33"> I will cause you to pass under my rod, and I will make you obey the requirements of the covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:9; Amos 9:9–10; Psalms 95:11"> I will purge from among you the rebels and those who revolt against me. I will send them out from the land where they are staying as foreigners, but they will not enter the land of Israel. Then you will know that I am Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Hosea 4:17; Isaiah 1:13–15; Ezekiel 43:7"> So to you, house of Israel, the Lord Yahweh says this: Each of you should go to his own idols. Worship them if you will refuse to listen to me, but you must no longer profane my holy name with your gifts and your idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Malachi 3:4; Isaiah 60:7"> For on my holy mountain, on the mountain peak of Israel—this is the Lord Yahweh's declaration—all of the house of Israel will worship me there in the land. I will be pleased to require your offerings there, and also the firstfruits of your tribute with all your holy things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:25; Ezekiel 11:17; Ezekiel 38:23"> I will accept you like fragrant incense when I bring you out from the peoples and gather you out of the countries where you have been scattered. I will show myself as holy among you for the nations to see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:13; Ezekiel 38:23; Ezekiel 36:23–24"> Then, when I bring you to the land of Israel, to the land that I have lifted up my hand to swear to give to your fathers, you will know that I am Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Hosea 5:15; Jeremiah 31:18"> There you will call to mind your conduct and all of your deeds by which you defiled yourselves, and you will despise yourselves in your own eyes for all your evil deeds that you have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:24; Psalms 115:1; Ezekiel 20:9"> So you will know that I am Yahweh when I do this to you because of my name's sake, and not because of your evil deeds, and not because of your corrupt deeds, house of Israel—this is the Lord Yahweh's declaration.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> Then the word of Yahweh came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Amos 7:16; Ezekiel 21:2; Jeremiah 13:19"> “Son of man, set your face toward the southern lands, and speak against the south; prophesy against the forest of the Negev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:14; Isaiah 24:1–6; Ezekiel 17:24"> Say to the forest of the Negev, 'This is Yahweh's declaration—the Lord Yahweh says this: See, I will set a fire among you. It will devour every fresh tree and every dried tree among you. The fiery flame will not be quenched; every face from the south to the north will be burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:2–3; Jeremiah 7:20; Deuteronomy 29:24–28"> Then all flesh will see that I am Yahweh when I light the fire, and it will not be quenched.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 16:25; Acts 17:18; Ezekiel 17:2"> Then I said, “Alas! Lord Yahweh, they are saying of me, 'Is he not a mere teller of parables?'”</span>
