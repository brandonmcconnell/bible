<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the word of Yahweh came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46; Ezekiel 4:7; Ezekiel 25:2"> “Son of man, set your face toward Jerusalem, and speak against the sanctuaries; prophesy against the land of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 9:22; Jeremiah 21:13; Ezekiel 5:8"> Say to the land of Israel, 'Yahweh says this: Behold! I am against you! I will draw my sword from its sheath and cut off both the righteous person and the wicked person from you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:47; Ezekiel 7:2; Ezekiel 6:11–14"> In order for me to cut off both the righteous and the wicked from you, my sword will go out from its sheath against all flesh from the south to the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:30; Nahum 1:9; 1 Samuel 3:12"> Then all flesh will know that I, Yahweh, have drawn my sword from its sheath. It will no longer hold back!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 22:4; Ezekiel 6:11; Jeremiah 19:10"> As for you, son of man, groan as your loins break! In bitterness groan before their eyes!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:17; Ezekiel 7:26; Isaiah 13:7"> Then it will happen that they will ask you, 'For what reason are you groaning?' Then you will say, 'Because of the news that is coming, for every heart will faint away, and every hand will falter! Every spirit will grow faint, and every knee will flow like water. Behold! It is coming and it will be like this!—this is the Lord Yahweh's declaration.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then the word of Yahweh came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 34:5–6; Ezekiel 21:28; Ezekiel 21:3"> “Son of man, prophesy and say, 'The Lord says this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Say: A sword! A sword! It will be sharpened and polished!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 110:5–6; Ezekiel 20:47; Ezekiel 21:25–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will be sharpened in order to engage in great slaughter! It will be polished in order to be like lightning!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Should we rejoice in my son's scepter? The coming sword hates every such rod!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:19; Jeremiah 25:33; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">So the sword will be given to be polished, and then to be grasped by the hand!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The sword is sharpened and it is polished and it is to be given into the hand of the one who kills!”'”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:6; Joel 1:13; Ezekiel 21:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Call for help and lament, son of man! For that sword has come against my people! It is against all the leaders of Israel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are thrown against the sword with my people. Therefore, slap your thigh!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:10; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Ezekiel 21:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">For there is a trial, but what if the scepter does not last?—this is the Lord Yahweh's declaration.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 24:1; Numbers 24:10; Leviticus 26:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now you, son of man, prophesy and hit your two hands together, for the sword will attack even a third time!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">A sword for the ones to be slaughtered! It is a sword for the many to be slaughtered, piercing them everywhere!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:10; Jeremiah 17:27; Ezekiel 21:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">In order to melt their hearts and to multiply their fallen, I have set the sword for slaughter at all their gates. Ah! It is made like lightning, it is grasped for slaughter.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; Ezekiel 21:4; Ezekiel 14:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">You, sword! Strike to the right! Strike to the left! Go wherever your face is turned.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Ezekiel 21:14; Zechariah 6:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I will also hit my two hands together, and then I will bring my fury to rest! I, Yahweh, declare it!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The word of Yahweh came to me again, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:1–3; Jeremiah 1:10; Ezekiel 5:1–17"> “Now you, son of man, assign two roads for the sword of the king of Babylon to come. The two roads will start in the same land, and a signpost will mark one of them as leading to a city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Amos 1:14; Jeremiah 49:2; Ezekiel 25:5"> Mark one road for the Babylonian army to come to Rabbah, the city of the Ammonites. Mark the other to lead the army to Judah and the city of Jerusalem, which is fortified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 18:20; Proverbs 16:33; Judges 17:5"> For the king of Babylon will stop at the crossroads, at the junction, in order to practice divination. He will shake some arrows and ask direction from some idols and he will examine a liver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:2; Ezekiel 26:9; Jeremiah 51:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">In his right hand will be an omen about Jerusalem, to set battering rams against it, to open his mouth to order the slaughter,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to sound a war cry, to set battering rams against the gates,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to build a ramp, and to erect siege towers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 5:15; Ezekiel 21:24; Ezekiel 29:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will seem to be a useless omen in the eyes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of the ones in Jerusalem, those who had sworn an oath to the Babylonians! But the king will accuse them of violating their treaty in order to besiege them!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 4:2; Micah 3:10–12; Jeremiah 3:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore the Lord Yahweh says this: Because you have made your guilt to be remembered,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">making your transgressions to be revealed, so that in all your actions your sins will be seen—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because you have done this you will be taken in hand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:5; Ezekiel 21:29; Ezekiel 17:19"> As for you, profane and wicked ruler of Israel, whose day of punishment has come, and whose time of committing iniquity has ended,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; Psalms 75:7; Jeremiah 13:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">the Lord Yahweh says this to you: Remove the turban and take off the crown! Things will no longer be the same! Exalt the lowly and humble the exalted!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Zechariah 9:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">A ruin! A ruin! I will make it a ruin! It will not be restored until the one comes who is assigned to execute judgment.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; Ezekiel 21:9–10; Zephaniah 2:8–10"> So you, son of man, prophesy and say, 'The Lord Yahweh says this to the people of Ammon concerning their coming disgrace: A sword, a sword is drawn! It is sharpened for the slaughter in order to devour, so it will be like lightning!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:25; Ezekiel 22:28; Psalms 37:13"> While prophets see empty visions for you, while they perform rituals to come up with lies for you, this sword will lie on the necks of the wicked who are about to be killed, whose day of punishment has come and whose time of iniquity is about to end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:38; Genesis 15:14; Jeremiah 47:6–7"> Return the sword to its sheath. In the place of your creation, in the land of your origin, I will judge you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Jeremiah 6:22–23; Ezekiel 14:19"> I will pour out my indignation on you! I will fan the fire of my rage against you and put you into the hand of cruel men, craftsmen of destruction!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:10; Malachi 4:1; Ezekiel 20:47–48"> You will become fuel for the fire! Your blood will be in the midst of the land. You will not be remembered, for I, Yahweh have declared this!'”</span>
