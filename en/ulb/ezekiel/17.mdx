<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word of Yahweh came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:49; Matthew 13:35; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> “Son of man, present a riddle and speak a parable to the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Jeremiah 48:40; Ezekiel 17:7"> Say, 'The Lord Yahweh says this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">A large eagle with large wings and long pinions, full of feathers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and that was multicolored went to Lebanon and took hold of the top of a cedar tree.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 43:14; Isaiah 47:15; Revelation 18:11–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">It broke off the tips of the branches and took them to the land of Canaan;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he planted it in a city of merchants.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 44:4; Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Jeremiah 37:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">He also took some seed of the land and planted it in fertile soil.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He placed it beside a large body of water like a willow.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:14; Proverbs 16:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then it sprouted and became a spreading vine low to the ground.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Its branches turned toward him, and its roots grew under it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So it became a vine and produced branches and sent out shoots.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:4; Ezekiel 17:15; 2 Kings 24:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">But there was another great eagle with large wings and many feathers.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Behold! This vine turned its roots toward the eagle,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and it spread out its branches toward the eagle from the place it had been planted so it would be watered.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">It had been planted in good soil beside a large body of water</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so it would produce branches and sprout fruit, to become a magnificent vine.'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Jeremiah 32:5; Isaiah 30:1–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Say to the people, 'The Lord Yahweh says this: Will it prosper?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Will it not be uprooted and stripped of its fruit so that it withers, and all its fresh growth will wither?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">No strong arm or many people will be needed to pull it out by its roots.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 13:15; John 15:6; Matthew 21:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">So behold! After it has been planted, will it grow? Will it not wither when the eastern wind touches it?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will completely wither away in the garden plot where it sprouted.'”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then the word of Yahweh came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19; Ezekiel 17:3; Ezekiel 1:2"> “Speak to the rebellious house, 'Do you not know what these things mean? Behold! The king of Babylon came to Jerusalem and took her king and her princes and brought them to him in Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:13; 2 Kings 24:15–17; Ezekiel 17:5"> Then he took a royal descendant, made a covenant with him, and brought him under an oath. He took away the powerful people of the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:14; Deuteronomy 28:43; 1 Samuel 2:30"> so the kingdom might become lowly and not lift itself up. By keeping his covenant the land will survive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:3; Jeremiah 38:18; Ezekiel 17:7"> But the king of Jerusalem rebelled against him by sending his ambassadors to Egypt to acquire horses and an army. Will he succeed? Will the one doing these things escape? If he breaks the covenant, will he escape?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 52:11; Ezekiel 17:18–19"> As I live!—this is the Lord Yahweh's declaration—he will surely die in the land of the king who made him king, the king whose oath he despised, and whose covenant he broke. He will die in the middle of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:7; Ezekiel 4:2; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> Pharaoh with his mighty army and a great company of men will not help him in battle, when ramps are constructed and siege walls are built up to cut off many lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:24; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Lamentations 5:6"> For the king despised his oath by breaking the covenant. Behold, he reached out with his hand to make a promise and yet he did all these things. He will not escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:11; Jeremiah 5:9; Jeremiah 5:2"> Therefore the Lord Yahweh says this: As I live, was it not my oath that he despised and my covenant that he broke? So I will bring his punishment on his head!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Ezekiel 32:3; Jeremiah 2:35"> I will spread my net out over him, and he will be caught in my hunting net. Then I will bring him to Babylon and execute judgment on him there for his treason that he committed when he betrayed me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 25:5; 2 Kings 25:11; Ezekiel 12:14"> All of his refugees in his armies will fall by the sword, and the ones who remain will be scattered in every direction. Then you will know that I am Yahweh; I have declared this would happen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2; Zechariah 6:12–13; Isaiah 11:1–5"> The Lord Yahweh says this, 'So I myself will take away the highest part of the cedar tree, and I will plant it away from its tender branches. I will break it off, and I myself will plant it on a high mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:32; Ezekiel 31:6; Isaiah 2:2"> I will plant it on the mountains of Israel so it will bear branches and produce fruit, and it will become a majestic cedar so that every winged bird will live under it. They will nest in the shade of its branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 9:11; 1 Corinthians 1:27–28; Ezekiel 24:14"> Then all the trees of the field will know that I am Yahweh. I bring down the high trees and I raise up the low trees. I wither the watered tree and I cause the dried tree to bloom. I am Yahweh, I have declared that this will happen; and I have done it.'”</span>
