<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 10:9–10; Ezekiel 3:17–21; Ezekiel 2:8–9"> He said to me, “Son of man, what you have found, eat. Eat this scroll, then go speak to the house of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:17; Acts 26:19"> So I opened my mouth, and he fed me that scroll.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Revelation 10:9–10; Psalms 119:103"> He said to me, “Son of man, feed your belly and fill your stomach with this scroll that I have given to you!” So I ate it, and it was as sweet as honey in my mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:5–6; Acts 1:8; Ezekiel 2:3"> Then he said to me, “Son of man, go to the house of Israel and speak my words to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jonah 1:2; Isaiah 33:19; Jonah 3:2–4"> For you are not being sent to a people of strange speech or difficult language, but to the house of Israel—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 11:20–24; Romans 9:30–33; Jonah 3:5–10"> not to many peoples of strange speech or a difficult language, whose words you cannot understand. Surely if I sent you to them, they would have listened to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Ezekiel 2:4; 1 Samuel 8:7"> But the house of Israel will not be willing to listen to you, for they are not willing to listen to me. So all the house of Israel is strong browed and hard hearted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18; Acts 7:51–56; Micah 3:8"> Behold! I have made your face as stubborn as their faces and your brow as hard as their brows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:6; Jeremiah 1:8; Isaiah 50:7"> I have made your brow like a diamond, harder than flint! Do not fear them or be discouraged by their faces, since they are a rebellious house.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Job 22:22; Ezekiel 3:1–3"> Then he said to me, “Son of man, all the words that I announce to you—take them into your heart and hear them with your ears!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:7; Ezekiel 3:27; Ezekiel 33:30"> Then go to the captives, to your people, and speak to them. Say to them, 'This is what the Lord Yahweh says,' whether they will listen or not.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:3; Acts 8:39; Ezekiel 11:1"> Then the Spirit lifted me up, and I heard behind me the sound of a great earthquake: “Blessed be the glory of Yahweh from his place!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:24; Ezekiel 10:16–17; Ezekiel 10:5"> It was the sound of the wings of the living creatures as they touched one another, and the sound of the wheels that were with them, and the sound of a great earthquake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:1; Jeremiah 20:7–9; 2 Kings 3:15"> The Spirit lifted me up and took me away, and I went with bitterness in my spirit's rage, for the hand of Yahweh was powerfully pressing on me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 137:1; Job 2:13; Genesis 50:10"> So I went to the captives at Tel Aviv who lived along the Kebar Canal, and I stayed there among them for seven days, overwhelmed in amazement.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:7"> Then it happened after seven days that the word of Yahweh came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:8; Hebrews 13:17; 2 Chronicles 19:10"> “Son of man, I have made you a watchman for the house of Israel, so listen to the word from my mouth, and give them my warning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:6; James 5:19–20; Ezekiel 3:20"> When I say to the wicked, 'You will surely die' and you do not warn him or speak a warning to the wicked about his evil deeds so he might live—the wicked one will die for his sin, but I will require his blood from your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:9; Ezekiel 14:20; 1 Timothy 4:16"> But if you warn the wicked, and he does not turn from his wickedness or from his wicked deeds, then he will die for his sin, but you will have rescued your own life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:24; Ezekiel 18:26; Ezekiel 33:12–13"> If a righteous man turns from his righteousness and acts unjustly, and I set a stumbling block before him, he will die. Because you did not warn him, he will die in his sin, and I will not call to mind the righteous deeds that he performed, but I will require his blood from your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:16; Proverbs 9:9; Titus 2:15"> But if you warn the righteous man to stop sinning so that he no longer sins, he will surely live since he was warned; and you will have rescued your own life.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 9:6; Ezekiel 37:1; Ezekiel 8:4"> So the hand of Yahweh was on me there, and he said to me, “Arise! Go out into the plain, and I will speak with you there!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Acts 7:55; Revelation 1:17"> I arose and went out into the plain, and there the glory of Yahweh was staying, like the glory that I had seen beside the Kebar Canal; so I fell on my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:2; Daniel 10:19; Daniel 10:8–10"> The Spirit came to me and stood me up on my feet; and he spoke with me, and said to me, “Go and shut yourself up within your house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:8; Acts 9:16; Mark 3:21"> for now, son of man, they will place ropes upon you and tie you so you cannot go out among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:27; Micah 3:6–7; Amos 8:11–12"> I will make your tongue stick to the roof of your mouth, so you will be mute, and you will not be able to rebuke them, since they are a rebellious house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:2–3; Ezekiel 24:27; Ezekiel 3:11"> But when I speak with you, I will open your mouth so you will say to them, 'This is what the Lord Yahweh says.' The one who will hear will hear; the one who will not listen will not listen, for they are a rebellious house!”</span>
</div>
