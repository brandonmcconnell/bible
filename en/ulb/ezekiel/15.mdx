<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the word of Yahweh came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 10:1; Psalms 80:8–16; Isaiah 5:1–7"> “Son of man, how is a vine better than any tree with branches that is among the trees in a forest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 14:34–35; Jeremiah 24:8; Mark 9:50"> Do people take wood from a vine to make anything? Or do they make a peg from it to hang anything on it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:6; Isaiah 27:11; Psalms 80:16"> See! If it is thrown into a fire as fuel, and if the fire has burned both of its ends and also the middle, is it good for anything?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:16"> See! When it was complete, it could not make anything; surely then, when the fire has burned, then it still will not make anything useful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 15:2; Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 24:8–10"> Therefore the Lord Yahweh says this: Unlike the trees in the forests, I have given the vine as fuel for fires; I will act in the same way toward the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:8; Psalms 34:16; Isaiah 24:18"> For I will set my face against them. Though they come out from the fire, yet the fire will consume them; so you will know that I am Yahweh, when I set my face against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:14; Zephaniah 1:18; Isaiah 6:11"> Then I will make the land into an abandoned wasteland because they have committed sin—this is the Lord Yahweh's declaration.”</span>
