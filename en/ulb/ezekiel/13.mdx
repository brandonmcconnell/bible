<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Again, the word of Yahweh came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:17; Jeremiah 37:19; Ezekiel 22:28"> “Son of man, prophesy against the prophets who are prophesying in Israel, and say to those who are prophesying out of their own imaginations, 'Listen to the word of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 2:14; Jeremiah 23:28–32; Hosea 9:7"> The Lord Yahweh says this: Woe to the foolish prophets who follow their own spirit, but who have seen nothing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 19:20; Micah 2:11; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–10"> Israel, your prophets have been like jackals in the wastelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Psalms 106:23; Isaiah 58:12"> You have not gone to the breaks in the wall around the house of Israel in order to repair it, in order to resist in battle on the day of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:8; Ezekiel 22:28; Jeremiah 28:15"> The people have false visions and make false predictions, those who say, “Such and such is Yahweh's declaration.” Yahweh has not sent them, but they nevertheless have made people hope that their messages would come true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6; Matthew 24:23–24; Ezekiel 13:2–3"> Have you not had false visions and made false predictions, you who say, “Such and such is Yahweh's declaration” when I myself have not spoken?'</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:8; Ezekiel 21:3; Ezekiel 28:22"> Therefore the Lord Yahweh says this, 'Because you have had false visions and have told lies—therefore this is the Lord Yahweh's declaration against you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 87:6; Psalms 69:28; Ezekiel 20:38"> My hand will be against the prophets who have lying visions and who make false predictions. They will not be in the assembly of my people, or enrolled in the record of the house of Israel; they must not go to the land of Israel. For you will know that I am the Lord Yahweh!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:28; Ezekiel 13:16; Jeremiah 6:14"> Because of this, and because they led my people astray and said, “Peace!” when there is no peace, they are building a wall that they will paint with whitewash.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Isaiah 28:2; Nahum 1:7–8"> Say to those who are whitewashing the wall, 'It will fall down; there will be a downpour of rain, and I will send hailstones to make it fall down, and a windstorm wind to break it down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 3:13; Lamentations 2:14–15; Judges 9:38"> See, the wall will fall down. Have others not said to you, “Where is the whitewash that you put on it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 30:30; Revelation 16:21; Psalms 148:8"> Therefore the Lord Yahweh says this: I will bring a windstorm in my rage, and there will be floods of rain in my wrath! Hailstones in my rage will completely destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 1:6; Jeremiah 14:15; Jeremiah 6:15"> For I will tear down the wall that you have covered with whitewash, and I will demolish it to the ground and lay bare its foundations. So it will fall, and you will be annihilated in the middle of it all. Then you will know that I am Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 30:13; Psalms 62:3; Nehemiah 4:3"> For I will annihilate in my fury the wall and those who whitewashed it. I will say to you, “The wall exists no more, nor do the people who whitewashed it—</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:10; Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 8:11"> the prophets of Israel who prophesied about Jerusalem and who had visions of peace for her. But there is no peace!—this is the Lord Yahweh's declaration.”'</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:2; 2 Kings 22:14; Judges 4:4"> So you, son of man, set your face against the daughters of your people who prophesy out of their own minds, and prophesy against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 2:14; Ezekiel 13:20; 2 Timothy 4:3"> Say, 'The Lord Yahweh says this: Woe to the women who sew magic charms onto every part of their hand and make veils for their heads of every size, used to hunt down people. Will you hunt down my people but save your own lives?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 3:5; Proverbs 28:21; Jeremiah 23:17"> You have profaned me among my people for handfuls of barley and crumbs of bread, to kill people who should not die, and to preserve the lives of those who should not continue to live, because of your lies to my people who heard you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:18; 2 Timothy 3:8–9; Ezekiel 13:8–9"> Therefore the Lord Yahweh says this: I am against the magic charms that you have used to ensnare the people's lives as if they were birds. Indeed, I will tear them from your arms; and the people whom you have trapped like birds—I will let them go free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 91:3"> I will tear away your veils and rescue my people from your hand, so they will no longer be trapped in your hands. You will know that I am Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Jeremiah 14:13–17; 2 Peter 2:18–19"> Because you discourage the heart of the righteous person with lies, even though I did not desire his discouragement, and because you encourage instead the actions of the wicked person so that he will not turn from his way to save his life—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:21; Ezekiel 12:24; Micah 3:6"> therefore you will no longer have false visions or continue to make predictions, for I will rescue my people out of your hand. You will know that I am Yahweh.'”</span>
</div>
</div>
