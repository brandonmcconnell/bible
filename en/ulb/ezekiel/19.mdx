<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:2; Ezekiel 26:17; 2 Kings 24:6"> “Now you, take up a lamentation against the leaders of Israel</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 11:3; Isaiah 5:29; Job 4:11"> and say,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'Who was your mother? A lioness, she lived with a lion's son;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the midst of young lions, she nurtured her cubs.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:25; 2 Kings 23:31–32; 2 Chronicles 36:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">She is the one who raised up one of her cubs to become a young lion, a lion who learned to tear apart his victims, and then he devoured men.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:4; 2 Kings 23:33–34; 2 Chronicles 36:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then the nations heard about him. He was caught in their trap, and they brought him with hooks to the land of Egypt.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:3; 2 Kings 23:34–37"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then she saw that although she had waited for his return, her expectation was now gone, so she took another of her cubs and raised him to become a young lion.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:5; 2 Chronicles 36:9; Jeremiah 26:1–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">This young lion roamed about in the midst of lions. He was a young lion and learned to tear his victims; he devoured men.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:12; Ezekiel 12:19; Micah 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">He seized their widows and ruined their cities.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The land and its fullness were abandoned because of the sound of his roaring.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Instead of He seized their widows , some ancient copies and many modern versions have He destroyed their fortresses .">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 4:20; Ezekiel 19:4; Ezekiel 12:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">But the nations came against him from the surrounding provinces;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they spread their nets over him. He was caught in their trap.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:6; Ezekiel 6:2; Jeremiah 36:30–31"> <span className="pl pl-1">With hooks they put him in a cage and then they brought him to the king of Babylon.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They brought him to the strongholds so that his voice would no longer be heard on the mountains of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 80:8–11; Deuteronomy 8:7; Numbers 24:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your mother was like a vine planted in your blood beside the water.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Instead of in your blood , some ancient copies and many modern versions have in your vineyard .">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it was fruitful and full of branches because of the abundance of water.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:3; Psalms 80:15; Daniel 4:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">It had strong branches that were used for rulers' scepters,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and its size was exalted above the branches,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and its height was seen by the greatness of its foliage.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Hosea 13:15; Isaiah 27:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">But the vine was uprooted in fury and thrown down to the earth, and an eastern wind dried out its fruit.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Its strong branches were broken off and withered and fire consumed them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Ezekiel 19:10; Psalms 63:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">So now it is planted in the wilderness, in a land of drought and thirst.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 80:15–16; Ezekiel 19:11; 2 Kings 24:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">For fire went out from her large branches and consumed its fruit.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There is no strong branch on it, no scepter to rule.' This is a lamentation and will be sung as a lamentation.”</span></span>
