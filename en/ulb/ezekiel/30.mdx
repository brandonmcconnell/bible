<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word of Yahweh came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 13:6; Ezekiel 21:12; Joel 1:11"> “Son of man, prophesy and say, 'The Lord Yahweh says this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Wail, “Woe is the coming day.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Obadiah 1:15; Joel 2:1–2; Joel 1:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">The day is near. The day is near for Yahweh. It will be a day of clouds, a time of doom for nations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 29:8; Isaiah 16:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then a sword will come against Egypt, and there will be anguish in Cush when the killed people fall in Egypt—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when they take her wealth, and when her foundations are ruined.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:20; Ezekiel 27:10; Jeremiah 25:24"> Cush and Put, Lydia and all Arabia, and Libya, together with the people belonging to the covenant—they will all fall by the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:10; Isaiah 20:3–6; Isaiah 31:3"> Yahweh says this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">So the ones who support Egypt will fall, and the pride of her strength will go down.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">From Migdol to Syene their soldiers will fall by the sword—this is the Lord Yahweh's declaration.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:12; Jeremiah 25:18–26; Ezekiel 32:18–32"> They will be appalled in the midst of the abandoned lands, and their cities will be among all the ruined cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:6; Ezekiel 29:16; Amos 1:7"> Then they will know that I am Yahweh, when I set fire in Egypt, and when all of her helpers are destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 18:1–2; Isaiah 23:5; Ezekiel 32:9–10"> In that day messengers will go out from before me in ships to terrorize a secure Cush, and there will be anguish among them on the day of Egypt's doom. For behold! It is coming.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 32:11–16; Ezekiel 29:4–5"> The Lord Yahweh says this: I will make an end of the multitudes of Egypt by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Ezekiel 32:12; Ezekiel 39:11–20"> He and his army with him, the terror of nations, will be brought to destroy the land; they will draw out their swords against Egypt and fill the land with those who have been killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Nahum 1:4; Revelation 16:12"> I will make the rivers into dry ground, and I will sell the land into the hand of wicked men. I will make the land and its fullness desolate by the hand of strangers—I, Yahweh, have spoken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 10:11; Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 2:16"> The Lord Yahweh says this: I will destroy idols, and I will bring an end to the worthless idols of Memphis. There will no longer be a prince in the land of Egypt, and I will put terror on the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:14; Jeremiah 46:25; Psalms 78:43"> Then I will make Pathros desolate and set fire in Zoan, and I will execute acts of judgment on Thebes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 16:1; Nahum 1:6; Psalms 11:6"> For I will pour out my fury on Pelusium, the stronghold of Egypt, and cut off the multitude of Thebes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:8–9; Ezekiel 28:18"> Then I will set fire in Egypt; Pelusium will be in great agony, Thebes will be broken up, and Memphis will face enemies every day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 41:45"> The young men in Heliopolis and Bubastis will fall by the sword, and their cities will go into captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:3; Ezekiel 29:15; Isaiah 10:27"> In Tahpanhes, the day will hold back its light when I break the yoke of Egypt there, and the pride of her strength will be finished. There will be a cloud covering her, and her daughters will walk into captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:14; Revelation 17:1; Ezekiel 5:8"> I will execute acts of judgment in Egypt, so they will know that I am Yahweh.'”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:17; Ezekiel 26:1; Ezekiel 29:1"> Then it came about in the eleventh year, in the first month, in the seventh day of the month, that the word of Yahweh came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:13; Jeremiah 46:11; Psalms 37:17"> “Son of man, I have broken the arm of Pharaoh, the king of Egypt. Behold! It has not been bound up, or set to heal with a bandage, so that it will become strong enough to grasp a sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 37:17; Jeremiah 37:7; 2 Kings 24:7"> Therefore the Lord Yahweh says this, 'Behold, I am against Pharaoh, the king of Egypt. For I will break his arm, both the strong one and the broken one, and I will make the sword fall from his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:26; Ezekiel 30:17–18; Ezekiel 29:12–13"> Then I will scatter Egypt among the nations and disperse them among the lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:25; Zephaniah 2:12; Isaiah 45:5"> I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon and place my sword in his hand so that I might destroy Pharaoh's arms. He will groan before the king of Babylon with the groans of a dying man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:21; Ezekiel 32:15; Ezekiel 29:16"> For I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon, while Pharaoh's arms will fall. Then they will know that I am Yahweh, when I put my sword into the hand of the king of Babylon; for he will attack the land of Egypt with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 11:42; Ezekiel 29:12; Ezekiel 30:23"> So I will scatter Egypt among the nations and disperse them through the lands. Then they will know that I am Yahweh.'”</span>
</div>
</div>
