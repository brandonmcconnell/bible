<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Ezekiel 11:21; Ezekiel 5:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my eyes will not pity you, and I will not spare you. <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:28; Ezekiel 7:12; Isaiah 22:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your doom is coming to you who inhabit the land.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The time has come; the day of destruction is near, and the mountains will no longer be joyful.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:8; Ezekiel 14:19; Ezekiel 20:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now before long I will pour out my fury against you and fill up my wrath upon you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I judge you according to your ways and bring all your abominations upon you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 20:13; Micah 6:9; Isaiah 9:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my eye will not look compassionately, and I will not spare you. As you have done, I will do to you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and your abominations will be in your midst so you will know that I am Yahweh, the one punishing you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 10:5; James 4:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, the day! Behold, it is coming!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Doom has gone out! The rod has blossomed, arrogance has budded!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:18; Ezekiel 7:23; Isaiah 59:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Violence has grown up into a rod of wickedness—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">none of them, and none of their multitude, none of their wealth, and none of their importance will last!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13–14; 1 Corinthians 7:29–31; Ezekiel 7:5–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The time is coming; the day has come close. <span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 25:24–28; Leviticus 25:31; Job 15:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the seller will not return to the land he sold as long as they both live,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because the vision concerning the entire multitude will not be reversed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and because of their sins, none of them will be strengthened!</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:18; Ezekiel 5:12; Lamentations 1:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sword is on the outside, and plague and famine are inside the building.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Those who are in the field will die by the sword, while famine and plague will consume those in the city.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Hebrews 12:12; Jeremiah 6:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Every hand will falter and every knee will be weak as water,</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Zephaniah 1:18; Luke 12:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will throw their silver into the streets and their gold will be like refuse. Their silver and their gold</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will not be able to rescue them in the day of Yahweh's rage. Their lives will not be saved,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their hunger will not be satisfied, because their iniquity has become a stumbling block.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:21; Jeremiah 7:30; Ezekiel 8:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">In their pride they took the beauty of his jeweled ornaments, and with them they made their idolatrous figures, and their detestable things. Therefore, I am turning these into an unclean thing to them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 24:13; Psalms 74:2–8; 2 Chronicles 36:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I will give those things into the hand of strangers as plunder and to the wicked of the earth as plunder,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they will defile them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:17; Psalms 74:18–23; Psalms 74:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I will turn my face away from them when they defile my cherished place;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">bandits will enter it and defile it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:9; Jeremiah 27:2; Ezekiel 11:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Make a chain, because the land is filled with the judgment of blood,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the city is full of violence.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Psalms 106:41; Ezekiel 21:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I will bring the most wicked of the nations, and they will possess their houses,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will bring an end to the pride of the mighty, for their holy places will be defiled!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:15–16; Isaiah 57:21; Lamentations 4:17–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Fear will come! They will seek peace, but there will be none.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; Ezekiel 14:1; Micah 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Disaster upon disaster will come, and there will be rumor after rumor.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then they will seek a vision from the prophet, but the law will perish from the priest and advice from the elders.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Ezekiel 7:4–8; Ezekiel 26:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The king will mourn and the prince will dress in despair,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">while the hands of the people of the land will tremble in fear.
