<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:1; Ezekiel 40:17; Ezekiel 42:14"> Then the man brought me back to the outer sanctuary gate that faced east; it was closed tightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2–4; Exodus 24:10; Isaiah 6:1–5"> Yahweh said to me, “This gate has been sealed shut; it will not be opened. No man will go through it, for Yahweh, the God of Israel, has come through it, so it has been closed tightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:2; Ezekiel 34:24; Ezekiel 37:25"> The ruler of Israel will sit in it to eat food before Yahweh. He will enter by way of the gate's portico and go out the same way.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Revelation 1:17; Ezekiel 3:23"> Then he brought me by way of the northern gate to the front of the temple, and I looked, and behold, the glory of Yahweh filled the house of Yahweh, and I fell on my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:4; Ezekiel 43:10–11; Deuteronomy 12:32"> Then Yahweh said to me, “Son of man, set your heart and look with your eyes and listen with your ears to all that I am declaring to you, to all the statutes of the house of Yahweh and all its regulations. Think about the house's entrance and exits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:9; Ezekiel 45:9; 1 Peter 4:3"> Then say to the rebellious ones, the house of Israel, 'The Lord Yahweh says this: Let all of your disgusting actions be enough for you, house of Israel—</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 22:25; Jeremiah 9:26; Ezekiel 44:9"> that you brought foreigners with uncircumcised hearts and uncircumcised flesh to be in my sanctuary, profaning my house, while you were offering me food, fat and blood—you have broken my covenant by all your disgusting actions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:14; Numbers 18:3–5; Ezekiel 40:45–46"> You have not carried out your duties regarding my holy things, but you appointed others to take up your duties, and you assigned them to care for my holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Ezekiel 44:7; Zechariah 14:21"> The Lord Yahweh says this: No foreigner, uncircumcised in heart and flesh, from any of those who live among the people of Israel, may enter my holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:26; Ezekiel 48:11; 2 Kings 23:8–9"> Yet the Levites went far from me—they wandered away from me, going after their idols—but they will pay for their sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 16:9; 2 Chronicles 29:34; 2 Chronicles 30:17"> They are servants in my sanctuary, watching the gates of the house and serving in the house and they slaughter the burnt offerings and the people's sacrifices, and they will stand before the people and serve them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 16:10–16; Psalms 106:26; Ezekiel 14:3–4"> But because they performed the sacrifices before their idols, they became stumbling blocks for sin for the house of Israel. Therefore I will lift up my hand to swear an oath against them—this is the Lord Yahweh's declaration—they will bear their punishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 18:3; 2 Kings 23:9; Ezekiel 16:61"> They will not come near me to act as my priests or to approach any of my holy things, the most holy things. Instead, they will bear their reproach and their guilt for the disgusting actions that they have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:28–32; Numbers 18:4"> But I will place them as keepers of the work in the house, for all of its duties and everything that is done in it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Ezekiel 48:11; Ezekiel 44:7"> Then the Levitical priests, those sons of Zadok who fulfilled the duties of my sanctuary when the people of Israel were wandering away from me—they will come near me to worship me. They will stand before me to offer me the fat and the blood—this is the Lord Yahweh's declaration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:22; Malachi 1:12; Numbers 18:7–8"> They will come to my sanctuary; they will approach my table to worship me and to fulfill their duties to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Exodus 28:43; Exodus 39:27–29"> So it will be that when they come to the gates of the inner courtyard, they will have to dress in linen clothes, for they must not come in wool inside the gates of the courtyard and its house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 39:28; 1 Corinthians 11:4–10; Isaiah 3:20"> There should be linen turbans on their heads and linen underclothes on their hips. They must not dress in clothes that make them sweat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:20; Leviticus 6:27; Leviticus 6:10–11"> When they go out to the outer courtyard, to the outer courtyard in order to go to the people, they must take off the clothes they wore when they served; they must take them off and lay them down in a holy room, so they do not make other people holy by contact with their special clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:14; Numbers 6:5; Leviticus 21:5–24"> Also they must neither shave their heads nor allow their hair to hang loosely, but they must trim the hair of their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 10:9; Titus 1:7–8; 1 Timothy 5:23"> No priest may drink wine when he comes to the inner court,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; Leviticus 21:13–14; 1 Timothy 3:2"> nor take a widow or a divorced woman as a wife for himself, but only a virgin from the line of the house of Israel or a widow who was previously married to a priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:26; Leviticus 10:10–11; 2 Timothy 2:24–25"> For they will teach my people the difference between the holy and the profane; they will make them know the unclean from the clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:4; Ezekiel 22:26; 2 Chronicles 19:8–10"> In a dispute they will stand to judge with my decrees; they must be just. They will keep my law and my statutes in every feast, and they will celebrate my holy Sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:16; Leviticus 22:4; Matthew 8:21–22"> They will not go to a dead person so as to become unclean, unless it is their father or mother, son or daughter, brother or a sister who has not slept with a man; otherwise, they will become unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 9:13–14; Numbers 19:11–14; Numbers 6:10–21"> After a priest has become unclean, they will count off a period of seven days for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 6:9–11; Ezekiel 44:17; Leviticus 4:3–35"> On the day he enters the holy place, into the inner courtyard to serve in the holy place, he must bring a sin offering for himself—this is the Lord Yahweh's declaration.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 13:33; Numbers 18:20; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> This will be their inheritance: I am their inheritance, and you must give them no property in Israel; I will be their property!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 27:21; Leviticus 27:28"> They will eat the food offerings, the sin offerings, the guilt offerings, and everything devoted to Yahweh in Israel, will be theirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:35–37; 2 Chronicles 31:10; Deuteronomy 18:4"> The best of the firstfruits of all things and every contribution, anything from all of your contributions will belong to the priests, and you will give the best of your meal offerings to the priests so that blessing may rest on your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 22:8; Exodus 22:31; Deuteronomy 14:21"> The priests will not eat any carcass or animal torn by a beast, whether bird or beast.</span>
</div>
