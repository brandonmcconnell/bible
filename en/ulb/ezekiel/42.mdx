<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:15–16; Ezekiel 41:10; Song of Songs 7:5"> Some of those rooms faced the inner courtyard and were twenty cubits away from the sanctuary. There were three levels of rooms, and the ones above looked down on the ones below and were open to them, having a walkway. Some of the rooms looked out onto the outer courtyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:19; Luke 13:24; Matthew 7:14"> A passage ten cubits in width and one hundred cubits in length ran in front of the rooms. <span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:6; 1 Kings 6:8"> For the halls on the third story had no columns, unlike the courtyards, which did have columns. So the highest level's rooms were smaller in size compared to the rooms in the lowest and middle levels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:12; Ezekiel 42:10"> The outside wall ran along the rooms toward the outer courtyard, the courtyard that was in front of the rooms. That wall was fifty cubits in length.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:13–14"> The length of the rooms of the outer courtyard was fifty cubits, and the rooms facing the sanctuary were one hundred cubits in length.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:19; Ezekiel 44:5"> There was an entrance to the lowest rooms from the east side, coming from the outer courtyard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:1; Ezekiel 42:13; Ezekiel 41:12–15"> Along the wall of the outer courtyard on the eastern side of the outer courtyard, in front of the sanctuary's inner courtyard, there were also rooms<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew and some modern versions read on the eastern side . <span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> On the south side were doors into rooms that were just the same as on the north side. A passage on the inside had a door at its head, and the passage opened into the various rooms. On the east side there was a doorway into the passage at one end.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 10:17; Leviticus 7:6; Ezekiel 40:46"> Then the man said to me, “The northern rooms and the southern rooms that are in front of the outer courtyard are holy rooms where the priests who work nearest to Yahweh may eat the most holy food. They will put the most holy things there—the food offering, the sin offering, and the guilt offering—for this is a holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:19; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 29:4–9"> When the priests enter there, they must not go out of the holy place to the outer court, without laying aside the clothes in which they served, since these are holy. So they must dress in other clothes before going near the people.”</span>
</div>
