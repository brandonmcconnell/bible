<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 66:23; Ezekiel 44:1–2; Exodus 20:9"> The Lord Yahweh says this: The gate of the inner courtyard, facing east, will be shut for the six days of work, but on the Sabbath it will be opened, and on the day of the new moon it will be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:3; Ezekiel 46:8; 1 Chronicles 29:10–12"> The prince will enter the outer courtyard by the way of the gate and its portico from outside, and he will stand before the doorposts of the inner gate while the priests perform his burnt offering and peace offering. Then he will worship at the threshold of the inner gate and go out, but the gate will not be shut until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 1:10; John 10:9; Hebrews 10:19–22"> The people of the land will also worship before Yahweh at the entrance to this gate on the Sabbaths and new moons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:17; Numbers 28:9–10"> The burnt offering that the prince offers to Yahweh on the Sabbath day will be six unblemished lambs and an unblemished ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:24; Ezekiel 46:7; Deuteronomy 16:17"> The grain offering with the ram will be an ephah, and the grain offering with the lambs will be what he wishes to give, and a hin of oil with each ephah of grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:1"> On the day of the new moon he must offer an unblemished bull from a herd, six lambs, and an unblemished ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:5"> He must make a grain offering of an ephah for the bull and an ephah for the ram, and what he wishes to give for the lambs, and a hin of oil for every ephah of grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:2; Ezekiel 44:1–3; Colossians 1:18"> When the prince enters by the way of the gate and its portico, he must leave by the same way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 23:14–17; 2 Peter 2:20–21"> But when the people of the land come before Yahweh at the appointed festivals, anyone entering through the north gate to worship must leave through the south gate; and anyone entering through the south gate must leave through the north gate. No one may turn back to the gate through which he entered, for he must go out straight ahead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; 1 Chronicles 29:20; 1 Chronicles 29:22"> The prince must be in their midst; when they go in, he must go in, and when they leave, he must leave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:5; Ezekiel 46:7; Numbers 15:1–41"> At the festivals, the grain offering must be an ephah of grain for the bull and an ephah for the ram, and whatever he wishes to give with the lambs, and a hin of oil for every ephah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:1–2; Ezekiel 45:17; Ezekiel 44:3"> When the prince gives a freewill offering, either a burnt offering or a peace offering to Yahweh, the gate facing east will be opened for him. He will offer his burnt offering offering or his peace offering as he does it on the Sabbath day. Then he must go out, and after he has gone out the gate will be shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 50:4; John 1:29; Exodus 12:5"> In addition, you will give an unblemished lamb one year old as a burnt offering to Yahweh daily; you will do this morning after morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 28:5"> You will give a grain offering with it morning after morning, a sixth of an ephah and a third of a hin of oil to moisten the flour of the grain offering for Yahweh, according to a permanent statute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 10:1–10; Numbers 28:6; Exodus 29:42"> They will prepare the lamb, the grain offering, and the oil morning after morning, a permanent burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:3; Galatians 4:7; Genesis 25:5–6"> The Lord Yahweh says this: If the prince gives a gift to any of his sons, it is his inheritance. It will be the property of his sons, it is an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Luke 19:25–26; Galatians 4:30–31"> But if he gives a gift from his inheritance to one of his servants, then it will be that servant's until the year of liberty, and then it will return to the prince. His inheritance will certainly be for his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:8; 1 Kings 21:19; Ezekiel 22:27"> The prince will not take the people's inheritance away from their own property; he must provide for his sons from his own property so that my people will not be scattered, each man from his own property.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:9; Ezekiel 44:4–5; Ezekiel 40:44–46"> Next the man brought me through the entrance at the gate to the holy rooms for the priests, which faced north and behold! There was a place toward the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:13; Ezekiel 44:29; Ezekiel 44:19"> He said to me, “This is the place where the priests must boil the guilt offering and the sin offering and where they must bake the grain offering. They must not bring the offerings into the outer courtyard, for then the people would be consecrated.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then he brought me to the outer courtyard and he led me past the four corners of that courtyard, and I saw that in at every corner of the courtyard there was a another court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> In the four corners of the outer courtyard there were four small courtyards, forty cubits in length and thirty in width. There the same dimensions for all four of the courtyards.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Hebrew word translated here as small is unclear. It is translated in this way by some ancient and some modern versions. However, other modern versions translate it differently.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> There was a row made of stone all around the four of them, and cooking hearths were under the stone row.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:20; John 21:15–17; 1 Peter 5:2"> The man said to me, “These are the places where the temple servants will boil the people's sacrifices.”</span>
