<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:1; Matthew 2:18; Psalms 77:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore I said, “Do not look at me, I will weep bitterly;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">do not try to comfort me concerning the destruction of the daughter of my people.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 7:4; Isaiah 37:3; Luke 23:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">For there is a day of tumult, treading down, and confusion for the Lord Yahweh of hosts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the Valley of Vision, a breaking down of the walls, and people crying out to the mountains.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 16:9; Isaiah 21:2; Amos 1:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Elam takes up the quiver, with chariots of men and horsemen,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Kir lays the shield bare.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Isaiah 10:28–32; Jeremiah 39:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will come about that your choicest valleys</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will be full of chariots, and the horsemen will take their positions at the gate.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2; 1 Kings 10:17; Isaiah 36:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">He took away the protection of Judah;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you looked in that day to the weapons in the Palace of the Forest.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 20:20; Nehemiah 3:16; 2 Chronicles 32:1–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You saw the breaches of the city of David, that they were many,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you collected the water of the lower pool.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">You counted the houses of Jerusalem, and you tore down the houses to fortify the wall.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 7:7; Jeremiah 33:2–3; Isaiah 31:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">You made a reservoir between the two walls for the water of the old pool.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But you did not consider the city's maker, who had planned it long ago.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Micah 1:16; Joel 1:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord Yahweh of hosts called on that day</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for weeping, for mourning, for shaved heads, and the wearing of sackcloth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 56:12; 1 Corinthians 15:32; Luke 17:26–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">But look, instead, celebration and gladness, killing cattle and slaughtering sheep,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">eating meat and drinking wine; let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we will die.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:13; 1 Samuel 3:14; Isaiah 5:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">This was revealed in my ears by Yahweh of hosts:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Surely this iniquity will not be forgiven you, even when you die,” says the Lord Yahweh of hosts.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:60; 2 Chronicles 16:14; 2 Samuel 18:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">'What are you doing here and who gave you permission to cut out a tomb for yourself,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">hewing out a grave on the heights and carving out a resting place in the rock?'”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3; Job 9:24; Esther 7:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">See, Yahweh is about to throw you, a mighty man, about to throw you down; he will grasp you tightly.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 17:13; Amos 7:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will surely wind you round and round, and toss you like a ball into a vast country.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There you will die, and there your glorious chariots will be; you will be the shame of your master's house!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; Psalms 75:6–7; Job 40:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I will thrust you from your office and from your station. You will be pulled down.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; 2 Kings 18:18; Isaiah 37:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will come about on that day that I will call my servant Eliakim son of Hilkiah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:8; Isaiah 9:6–7; Genesis 41:42–43"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will clothe him with your tunic and put on him your sash, and I will transfer your authority into his hand.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will be a father to the inhabitants of Jerusalem and to the house of Judah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:18–19; Revelation 3:7; Job 12:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will place the key of the house of David on his shoulder;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he will open, and none will shut; he will shut, and none will open.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:8; Job 36:7; Zechariah 10:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will fasten him, a peg in a secure place,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he will become a seat of glory for his father's house.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 6:1–3; John 20:21–23; Matthew 28:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will hang on him all the glory of his father's house, the offspring and descendants, every small container</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from the cups to all the jugs.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 46:11; Esther 9:24–25; Psalms 146:3"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">On that day—this is the declaration of Yahweh of hosts—the peg driven in a firm place will give way, break off, and fall, and the weight that was on it will be cut off—for Yahweh has spoken.</span></span>
