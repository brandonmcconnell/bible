<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 15:37; 2 Kings 16:1; Isaiah 1:1"> During the days of Ahaz son of Jotham son of Uzziah, king of Judah, Rezin the king of Aram, and Pekah son of Remaliah, king of Israel, went up to Jerusalem to war against it, but they could not prevail against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 8:12; Isaiah 7:13; Isaiah 9:9"> It was reported to the house of David that Aram was allied with Ephraim. His heart trembled, and the heart of his people, as the trees of the forest shake in the wind.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Instead of that Aram was allied with Ephraim , some versions have that Aram had camped in Ephraim .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Isaiah 36:2; Romans 9:27"> Then Yahweh said to Isaiah, “Go out with your son Shear-Jashub to meet Ahaz at the end of the conduit of the upper pool, on the road to Launderer's Field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; Lamentations 3:26; Deuteronomy 20:3"> Tell him, 'Be careful, remain calm, do not be afraid or intimidated by these two smoldering firebrands, by the fierce anger of Rezin and Aram, and of Pekah son of Remaliah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 83:3–4; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 2:2"> Aram, Ephraim, and the son of Remaliah have planned evil against you; they have said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> “Let us attack Judah and terrify her, and let us break into her and set up our king there, the son of Tabeel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:10; Lamentations 3:37; Isaiah 46:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord Yahweh says, “It will not take place; it will not happen,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 17:1–3; Genesis 14:15; 2 Samuel 8:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">because the head of Aram is Damascus, and the head of Damascus is Rezin.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Within sixty-five years, Ephraim will be shattered and will no longer be a people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 11:6; Romans 11:20; 2 Chronicles 20:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">The head of Ephraim is Samaria, and the head of Samaria is Remaliah's son.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If you do not remain firm in faith, surely you will not remain secure.”'”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 8:5; Isaiah 1:5; Isaiah 10:20"> The Lord spoke again to Ahaz,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Instead of The Lord spoke , some versions have Yahweh spoke .">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 37:30; Isaiah 38:7–8; Isaiah 38:22"> “Ask a sign of Yahweh your God; ask for it in the depths or in the height above.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; 2 Chronicles 28:22; Malachi 3:15"> But Ahaz said, “I will not ask, nor will I test Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 43:24; Isaiah 63:10; Ezekiel 34:18"> So Isaiah replied, “Listen, house of David. Is it not enough for you people to test the patience of people? Must you also test the patience of my God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 1:23; Isaiah 9:6; Luke 1:31"> Therefore the Lord himself will give you people a sign: See, the young woman will conceive, bear a son, and will call his name Immanuel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Instead of the young woman , some versions have a young woman .">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 7:22; Amos 5:15; Matthew 3:4"> He will eat curds and honey when he knows to refuse the evil and choose the good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 8:4; Deuteronomy 1:39; Isaiah 9:11"> For before the child knows to refuse the evil and choose the good, the land whose two kings you dread will be desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Isaiah 10:5–6; 2 Chronicles 28:19–21"> Yahweh will bring on you, on your people, and on your father's house days unlike any since Ephraim seceded from Judah—he will bring on you the king of Assyria.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 5:26; Joshua 24:12; Deuteronomy 1:44"> <span className="pl pl-1">At that time</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh will whistle for a fly from the distant streams of Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and for a bee from the land of Assyria.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19; Jeremiah 16:16; Isaiah 2:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will all come and settle down into all the gorges, into the clefts of the rocks,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on all the thornbushes, and onto all the pastures.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7; Ezekiel 29:18; Isaiah 10:15"> At that time the Lord will shave with a razor that was hired beyond the Euphrates River—the king of Assyria—<br /><span className="pl pl-1">the head and the hair of the legs; it will also sweep away the beard.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:17; Jeremiah 39:10; Isaiah 37:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">On that day, a man will keep alive a young cow and two sheep,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15; Matthew 3:4; 2 Samuel 17:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">and because of the abundance of milk which they shall give, he will eat curds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for everyone left in the land will eat curds and honey.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 5:6; Song of Songs 8:11–12; Matthew 21:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">At that time, where there were a thousand vines worth a thousand silver shekels,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there will be nothing but briers and thorns.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 27:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Men will go there to hunt with bows, because all the land will be briers and thorns.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 7:21–22; Isaiah 13:20–22; Isaiah 5:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will stay away from all the hills that were cultivated with the hoe, for fear of the briers and thorns;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but it will be a place where cattle and sheep graze.</span></span>
