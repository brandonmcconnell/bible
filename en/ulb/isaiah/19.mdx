<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:12; Revelation 1:7; Joshua 2:11"> A declaration about Egypt.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">See, Yahweh rides on a swift cloud and is coming to Egypt;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the idols of Egypt quake before him, and the hearts of the Egyptians melt within themselves.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 1 Samuel 14:20; Matthew 10:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I will stir up Egyptians against Egyptians: A man will fight against his brother, and a man against his neighbor;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">city will be against city, and kingdom against kingdom.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; Daniel 2:2; 1 Chronicles 10:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The spirit of Egypt will be weakened from within. I will destroy his advice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">though they sought the advice of idols, dead men's spirits, mediums, and spiritualists.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 46:26; Ezekiel 29:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will give the Egyptians into the hand of a harsh master, and a strong king will rule over them—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">this is the declaration of the Lord Yahweh of hosts.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:36; Ezekiel 30:12; Zechariah 14:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">The waters of the sea will dry up, and the river will dry up and become empty.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:25; Exodus 7:18; 2 Kings 19:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The rivers will become foul; the streams of Egypt will dwindle and dry up;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the reeds and flags will wither away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 32:20; Joel 1:17–18; Isaiah 23:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The reeds along the Nile,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">at the mouth of the Nile,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and every sown field beside the Nile will become parched,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will be driven away, and will be no more.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:10; Habakkuk 1:15; Numbers 11:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The fishermen will wail and mourn, and all who cast a hook into the Nile will mourn,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and those who spread nets on the waters will grieve.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:7; Proverbs 7:16; 1 Kings 10:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">The workers in combed flax and those who weave white cloth will turn pale.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:10; Exodus 7:19; Exodus 8:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The cloth workers of Egypt will be crushed; all who work for hire will be grieved within themselves.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Acts 7:22; 1 Kings 4:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">The princes of Zoan are completely foolish. The advice of the wisest advisors of Pharaoh has become senseless.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">How can you say to Pharaoh, “I am the son of wise men, a son of ancient kings?”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20; Isaiah 14:24; Judges 9:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">Where then are your wise men?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let them tell you and make known what Yahweh of hosts plans concerning Egypt.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:13; Jeremiah 2:16; Jeremiah 46:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">The princes of Zoan have become fools, the princes of Memphis are deceived; they have made Egypt go astray, who are the cornerstones of her tribes.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Instead of Memphis , some versions have Noph , the Hebrew name for the same city in Egypt.">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Kings 22:20–23; Ezekiel 14:7–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh has mixed a spirit of distortion into her midst,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they have led Egypt astray in all she does, like a drunk staggering in his vomit.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:14–15; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12; Proverbs 14:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is nothing anyone can do for Egypt, whether head or tail, palm branch or reed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Nahum 3:13; Isaiah 11:15"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">In that day, the Egyptians will be like women. They will tremble and fear because of the upraised hand of Yahweh of hosts that he raises over them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 14:24; Daniel 4:35; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">The land of Judah will become a cause of staggering to Egypt. Whenever anyone reminds them of her, they will be afraid, because of the plan of Yahweh, that he is planning against them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:9; Zechariah 2:11; Isaiah 19:21"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">In that day there will be five cities in the land of Egypt that speak the language of Canaan and swear allegiance to Yahweh of hosts. One of these will be called The City of the Sun.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Instead of The City of the Sun , which probably refers to the Egyptian city of Heliopolis, some ancient and modern versions have The City of Destruction .">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 28:18; Joshua 22:10; Joshua 22:26"> In that day there will be an altar to Yahweh in the middle of the land of Egypt, and a stone pillar at the border to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Titus 2:13; Isaiah 45:21–22"> It will be as a sign and a witness to Yahweh of hosts in the land of Egypt. When they cry to Yahweh because of oppressors, he will send them a savior and a defender, and he will deliver them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; Malachi 1:11; John 17:3"> Yahweh will become known to Egypt, and the Egyptians will acknowledge Yahweh on that day. They will worship with sacrifices and offerings, and will make vows to Yahweh and fulfill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Hebrews 12:11; Hosea 14:1"> Yahweh will afflict Egypt, afflicting and healing. They will return to Yahweh; he will hear their prayer and will heal them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 11:16; Ephesians 2:18–22; Isaiah 35:8–10"> In that day there will be a highway from Egypt to Assyria, and the Assyrian will come to Egypt, and the Egyptian to Assyria; and the Egyptians will worship with the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 65:8; Genesis 12:2; Galatians 3:14"> In that day, Israel will be the third with Egypt and Assyria, a blessing in the midst of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 29:23; Hosea 2:23; Romans 9:24–25"> Yahweh of hosts will bless them and say, “Blessed be Egypt, my people; Assyria, the work of my hands; and Israel, my inheritance.”</span>
