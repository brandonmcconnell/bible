<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 9:27–28; Isaiah 6:13; Revelation 20:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">For though your people, Israel, are like the sand of the seashore, only a remnant of them will return. <span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:23; 1 Samuel 7:17; Joshua 21:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have crossed over the pass and they lodge at Geba.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Ramah trembles and Gibeah of Saul has fled.</span><br /></span>
