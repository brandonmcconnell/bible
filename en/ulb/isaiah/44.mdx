<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; Psalms 1:3; Psalms 92:13–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will spring up among the grass, like willows by the streams of water.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 41:4; Isaiah 43:10–11; Revelation 1:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is what Yahweh says—the King of Israel and his Redeemer, Yahweh of hosts:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I am the first, and I am the last; and there is no God but me.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; 1 Samuel 2:2; Deuteronomy 4:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not fear or be afraid. <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 10:14–15; Psalms 97:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">All who fashion idols are nothing; the things they delight in are worthless;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their witnesses cannot see or know anything, and they will be put to shame.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:5; Habakkuk 2:18; Acts 19:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who would form a god or cast an idol that is worthless?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:17; Isaiah 45:16; Psalms 97:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look, all his associates will be put to shame; the craftsmen are only men.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let them take their stand together; they will cower and be put to shame.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; John 12:39–40"> <span className="pl pl-1">They do not know, nor do they understand, for their eyes are blind and cannot see, and their hearts cannot perceive.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:15; 1 Kings 11:7; 1 Kings 11:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">No one thinks, nor do they comprehend and say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I have burned part of the wood in the fire; yes, I have also baked bread upon its coals. <span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:28; Job 15:31; Hosea 12:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is as if he were eating ashes; his deceived heart misleads him. He cannot rescue himself, nor does he say, “This thing in my right hand is a false god.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 44:1–2; Isaiah 49:15–16; Isaiah 43:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Think about these things, Jacob, and Israel, for you are my servant:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have formed you; you are my servant: Israel, you will not be forgotten by me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; 1 Corinthians 6:20; Acts 3:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have blotted out, like a thick cloud, your rebellious deeds, and like a cloud, your sins;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">return to me, for I have redeemed you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 1:6–7; Revelation 5:8–14; Psalms 69:34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sing, you heavens, for Yahweh has done this; shout, you depths of the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Break out into singing, you mountains, you forest with every tree in it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for Yahweh has redeemed Jacob, and will show his glory in Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 45:12; Isaiah 42:5; Isaiah 54:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is what Yahweh says, your Redeemer, he who formed you from the womb:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I am Yahweh, who made everything,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who alone stretched out the heavens, who alone fashioned the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Job 5:12–14; Jeremiah 50:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">I who frustrate the omens of the empty talkers and who disgrace those who read omens;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I who overturn the wisdom of the wise and make their advice foolish.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 18:36–38; Isaiah 42:9; Nehemiah 1:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">I, Yahweh, who confirmed the words of his servant and brings to pass the predictions of his messengers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who says of Jerusalem, 'She will be inhabited,' and of the towns of Judah, 'They will be built again, and I will raise up their ruins';</span><br /></span>
