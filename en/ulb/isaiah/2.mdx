<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:4–5; Luke 24:47; Zechariah 8:20–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Many peoples will come and say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Come, let us go up to the mountain of Yahweh, to the house of the God of Jacob,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so he may teach us some of his ways, and we may walk in his paths.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For out of Zion will go the law, and the word of Yahweh from Jerusalem.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:20; 2 Kings 1:2; Psalms 106:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">For you have abandoned your people, the house of Jacob,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they are filled with customs from the east and they practice divination like the Philistines,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they shake hands with sons of foreigners.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16–17; Isaiah 30:16; Isaiah 31:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their land is full of silver and gold, and is there no limit to their wealth;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their land also is full of horses, nor is there limit to their chariots.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 115:4–8; Revelation 9:20; Jeremiah 2:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their land also is full of idols;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they worship the craftsmanship of their own hands, things that their own fingers have made.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 5:15; Joshua 24:19; Romans 3:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people will be bowed down, and individuals will fall down; therefore do not raise them up.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 6:15–16; Isaiah 2:19–21; Luke 23:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Go into the rocky places and hide in the ground</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from the terror of Yahweh and from the glory of his majesty.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:15–16; Psalms 18:27; Isaiah 2:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">The lofty gaze of man will be brought low, and the pride of men will be brought down,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Yahweh alone will be exalted on that day.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 23:9; Proverbs 16:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">For there will be a day of Yahweh of hosts</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">against everyone who is proud and raised up, and against everyone who is arrogant—and he will be brought down—</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 14:8; Zechariah 11:1–2; Ezekiel 31:3–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">and against all the cedars of Lebanon that are high and lifted up,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and against all the oaks of Bashan.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 30:25; Isaiah 40:4; 2 Corinthians 10:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">That day of Yahweh of hosts will be against all the high mountains, and against all the hills that are lifted up,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 25:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">and against every high tower, and against every impregnable wall,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 10:22; Isaiah 23:1; Numbers 33:52"> <span className="pl pl-1">and against all the ships of Tarshish, and against all beautiful sailing vessels.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:29–30; Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 13:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The pride of man will be brought low, and the haughtiness of men will fall;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh alone will be exalted on that day.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 27:9; Ezekiel 36:25; Isaiah 21:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">The idols will completely pass away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 2:10; Luke 23:30; Hosea 10:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Men will go into the caves of the rocks and the holes of the ground,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from the terror of Yahweh, and from the glory of his majesty, when he rises to terrify the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 30:22; Isaiah 31:7; Isaiah 46:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">On that day people will throw away their idols of silver and of gold</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that they have made for themselves to worship—they will throw them away to the moles and bats.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19; Song of Songs 2:14; Isaiah 2:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people will go into the crevices in the rocks and into the clefts of the ragged rocks,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from the terror of Yahweh and from the glory of his majesty, when he rises to terrify the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Psalms 146:3; James 4:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Stop trusting in man, whose life-breath is in his nostrils,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for what does he amount to?</span></span>
