<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 9:11; Isaiah 8:4; Isaiah 7:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Fortified cities will disappear from Ephraim, the kingdom from Damascus,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the remnant of Aram—they will be like the glory of the people of Israel—this is the declaration of Yahweh of hosts.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16; Deuteronomy 32:15–27; Isaiah 24:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will come about on that day</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that the glory of Jacob will become thin, and the fatness of his flesh will become lean.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Joel 3:13; Jeremiah 51:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will be as when a harvester gathers the standing grain, and his arm reaps the heads of grain.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will be as when one gleans heads of grain in the Valley of Rephaim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 24:13; Deuteronomy 4:27; Romans 9:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Gleanings will be left, however, as when the olive tree is shaken:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">two or three olives in the top of the uppermost bough, four or five in the highest branches of a fruitful tree—this is the declaration of Yahweh, the God of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 7:7; Hosea 6:1; Hosea 3:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">On that day men will look toward their Maker, and their eyes will look to the Holy One of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 30:22; Isaiah 27:9; Isaiah 2:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will not look to the altars, the work of their hands, nor will they look to what their fingers have made, the Asherah poles or the sun images.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 3:11–15; Isaiah 27:10; Isaiah 24:1–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">On that day their strong cities will be like the abandoned wooded slopes on the hill summits,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that were forsaken because of the people of Israel and that will become a desolation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 106:21; Deuteronomy 32:18; Isaiah 26:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">For you have forgotten the God of your salvation, and have ignored the rock of your strength.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So you plant pleasant plants, and set out vine branches received from a stranger,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 4:8; Hosea 8:7; Galatians 6:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">on the day you plant and hedge and cultivate. <span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:23; Luke 21:25; Psalms 18:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe! <span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 9:5; Psalms 1:4; Isaiah 41:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The nations will roar like the rushing of many waters,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he will rebuke them and they will flee far away,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they will be chased before the wind like chaff on the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and like weeds whirling before a storm.</span><br /></span>
