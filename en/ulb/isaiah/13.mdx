<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 3:11; Jeremiah 51:20–24; Revelation 17:12–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have commanded my holy ones,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">yes, I have called my mighty men to execute my anger, even my proudly exulting ones.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joel 3:14; Revelation 19:11–21; Joel 2:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">The noise of a multitude in the mountains, as of many people!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The noise of a tumult of the kingdoms like many nations gathered together!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh of hosts is mustering the army for the battle.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 1:15; Isaiah 13:9; Amos 5:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Howl, for the day of Yahweh is near; it comes with destruction from the Almighty.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Nahum 2:10; Nahum 1:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore all hands hang limp, and every heart melts.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 26:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Nahum 2:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will be terrified; pain and agony will seize them, like a woman in labor.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will look in astonishment at one another; their faces will be aflame.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 13:15–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">See, the day of Yahweh comes with cruel wrath and overflowing anger,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to make the land a desolation and to destroy the sinners from it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:31; Matthew 24:29; Mark 13:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The stars of heaven and the constellations will not give their light.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The sun will be darkened even from dawn, and the moon will not shine.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 5:15; Daniel 5:22–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will punish the world for its evil and the wicked for their iniquity.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will put an end to the arrogance of the proud and will bring down the arrogance of the ruthless.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 4:1; Isaiah 24:6; Job 28:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will make men more rare than fine gold and mankind harder to find than the pure gold of Ophir.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 12:26–27; Joel 3:16; Matthew 24:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore I will make the heavens tremble, and the earth will be shaken out of its place,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">by the fury of Yahweh of hosts, and on the day of his fierce anger.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 22:17; Jeremiah 50:16; 1 Kings 22:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">Like a hunted gazelle or like a sheep with no shepherd,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">every man will turn toward his own people and will flee to his own land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 14:19–22; Jeremiah 50:27; Isaiah 47:9–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Every one who is found will be killed, and every one who is captured will die by the sword.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nahum 3:10; Hosea 10:14; Psalms 137:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their infants also will be dashed in pieces before their eyes.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their houses will be plundered and their wives raped.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 5:28–31; Jeremiah 51:11; Isaiah 41:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look, I am about to stir up the Medes to attack them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who will not be concerned about silver, nor do they delight in gold.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 2 Chronicles 36:17; Isaiah 13:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their bows will strike down the young men;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they will have no mercy on infants and they will not look with pity on children.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:30; Zephaniah 2:9; Jeremiah 51:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then Babylon, the most admired of kingdoms, the splendor of Chaldean pride,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will be overthrown by God like Sodom and Gomorrah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:43; Isaiah 14:23; Jeremiah 50:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will not be inhabited or lived in from generation to generation.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The Arab will not pitch his tent there, nor will shepherds have their flocks rest there.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Isaiah 34:11–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">But wild animals of the desert will lie there. Their houses will be full of owls;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and ostriches and wild goats will skip about there.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Isaiah 25:2; Isaiah 35:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hyenas will cry in their fortresses, and jackals in the beautiful palaces. Her time is near, and her days will not be delayed.</span></span>
