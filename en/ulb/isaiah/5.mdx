<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Deuteronomy 32:32–33; Mark 11:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">He spaded it, removed the stones, and planted it with an excellent kind of vine.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He built a tower in the middle of it, and also built a winepress.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He waited for it to produce grapes, but it only produced wild grapes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 2:5; Psalms 51:4; Micah 6:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">So now, inhabitant of Jerusalem and man of Judah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">judge between me and my vineyard.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 23:37; Micah 6:3–4; Jeremiah 2:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">What more could have been done for my vineyard, that I have not done for it?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When I looked for it to produce grapes, why did it produce wild grapes?</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 7:23–25; Jeremiah 25:11; Isaiah 24:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will lay it waste, and it will not be pruned nor hoed. <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 80:8–11; Jeremiah 12:10; James 5:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the vineyard of Yahweh of hosts is the house of Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the man of Judah his pleasant planting;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he waited for justice, but instead, there was killing; for righteousness, but, instead, a shout for help.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 2:2; Habakkuk 2:9–12; Jeremiah 22:13–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to those who join house to house, who join field to field,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">until no room remains, and you alone remain in the land!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 23:38; Isaiah 22:14; Amos 5:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh of hosts told me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">many houses will be empty, even great and impressive ones, without any inhabitant.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Haggai 2:16; Haggai 1:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For a ten-yoke vineyard will yield only one bath, and one homer of seed will yield only an ephah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29–30; Romans 13:13; Proverbs 20:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to those who rise up early in the morning to obtain strong drink,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">those who linger late into the night until wine inflames them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 21:11–14; Psalms 28:5; Amos 6:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">They banquet with harp, lute, tambourine, flute, and wine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but they do not recognize the work of Yahweh, nor have they considered the doings of his hands.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; Isaiah 1:3; Isaiah 27:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore my people have gone into captivity for lack of understanding;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their honored leaders go hungry, and their common people have nothing to drink.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 30:16; Luke 21:34; Habakkuk 2:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore Sheol has made its appetite greater and has opened its mouth very wide;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their elite, the people, their leaders, and the revelers and those who are happy among them, descend into Sheol.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 2:9; Jeremiah 5:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Man will be forced to bend down, and mankind will be humbled; the eyes of the lofty will be cast down.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 46:10; Isaiah 12:4; 1 Chronicles 29:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh of hosts will be exalted in his justice, and God the Holy One will show himself holy by his righteousness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:6; Zephaniah 2:14; Isaiah 7:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then the sheep will feed as in their own pasture, and in the ruins, lambs will graze as foreigners.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some modern versions have and in the ruins of the rich, lambs will graze .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Ezekiel 13:22; Jeremiah 5:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to those who pull along iniquity with useless cords and who pull along sin as if it were with a cart rope.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:22; Jeremiah 17:15; Jeremiah 23:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to those who say, “Let God hurry, let him act quickly, so we can see it happen;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and let the plans of the Holy One of Israel come, so that we may know them.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; 2 Timothy 3:1–5; Luke 16:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to those who call evil good, and good evil;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who represent darkness as light, and light as darkness;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who represent bitter as sweet, and sweet as bitter!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:18–20; Proverbs 3:7; Romans 12:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to those who are wise in their own eyes, and prudent in their own understanding!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 5:11; Habakkuk 2:15; Isaiah 28:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to those who are champions at drinking wine, and masters at mixing strong drinks;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 10:2; James 5:6; Psalms 94:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">who acquit the wicked for payment, and deprive the innocent of his rights!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 30:12; Job 18:16; Joel 2:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore as the tongue of fire devours stubble, and as the dry grass goes down in flame,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so their root will rot, and their blossom will blow away like dust.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">This will happen because they have rejected the law of Yahweh of hosts, and because they have despised the word of the Holy One of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 10:4; Isaiah 9:21; Isaiah 9:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore the anger of Yahweh is kindled against his people. He has reached out with his hand against them and has punished them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The mountains tremble, and their corpses are like garbage in the streets.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">In all these things, his anger does not subside; instead, his hand is still stretched out.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Zechariah 10:8; Deuteronomy 28:49; Isaiah 7:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will lift up a signal flag for far away nations and will whistle for those at the end of the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Look, they will come speedily and promptly.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joel 2:7–8; Job 12:18; Job 12:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">None tire or stumble among them; none slumbers or sleeps.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Nor are their belts loose, or the thongs of their sandals broken.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 45:5; Nahum 2:3–4; Ezekiel 21:9–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their arrows are sharp and all their bows are bent;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their horses' hooves are like flint, and their chariot wheels like storms.</span><br /></span>
