Now give my your attention, you distant peoples.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh has called me from birth by name, when my mother brought me into the world.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 1:16; Isaiah 50:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has made my mouth like a sharp sword; he hid me in the shadow of his hand;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he has made me into a polished arrow; in his quiver he has hidden me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; 1 Peter 2:9; Isaiah 44:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">He said to me, “You are my servant, Israel, through whom I show my glory.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; Galatians 4:11; Isaiah 53:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I replied, “Though I thought I have labored in vain, I have spent my energy for nothing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">yet my justice is with Yahweh, and my reward is with my God.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Matthew 17:5; John 3:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now Yahweh has spoken—he who formed me from birth to be his servant,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to restore Jacob again to himself, so that Israel would be gathered to him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for I am honored in the eyes of Yahweh, and my God has become my strength—</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 13:47; Isaiah 42:6; Luke 2:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">and he says, “It is too small a thing for you to be my servant to reestablish the tribes of Jacob,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and to restore the survivors of Israel. I will make you a light to the Gentiles,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that you may be my salvation to the ends of the earth.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; 1 Peter 2:4; Isaiah 53:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is what Yahweh says, the Redeemer of Israel, their Holy One,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to the one whose life is despised, hated by the nations, and a slave of rulers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Kings will see you and arise, and princes will see you and bow down,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because of Yahweh who is faithful, even the Holy One of Israel, who has chosen you.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:2; Isaiah 42:6; Psalms 69:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is what Yahweh says,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“At a time I decide to show my favor I will answer you, and in a day of salvation I will help you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will protect you, and give you as a covenant for the people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to rebuild the land, to reassign the desolate inheritance.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 42:7; Isaiah 42:16; 1 Peter 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will say to the prisoners, 'Come out;' to those in dark dungeons, 'Show yourselves.'</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will graze along the roads, and on all the bare slopes will be their pasture.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 7:16–17; Matthew 5:6; Ezekiel 34:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will not be hungry or thirsty; nor will the heat or sun beat on them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for he who has mercy on them will lead them; he will guide them to springs of water.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 11:16; Isaiah 62:10; Isaiah 35:8–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I will make all my mountains into a road, and make my highways level.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5–6; Matthew 8:11; Revelation 11:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look, these will come from far away, some from the north and the west; and others from the land of Sinim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 44:23; Isaiah 51:3; Psalms 96:11–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sing, heavens, and be joyful, earth; break into singing, you mountains!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For Yahweh comforts his people, and will have compassion on his afflicted.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:27; Romans 11:1–5; Psalms 22:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">But Zion said, “Yahweh has forsaken me, and the Lord has forgotten me.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 44:21; Psalms 103:13; Matthew 7:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Can a woman forget her baby, nursing at her breast, so she does not have compassion on the son she has borne?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yes, they may forget, but I will not forget you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Haggai 2:23; Isaiah 62:6; Isaiah 60:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look, I have inscribed your name on my palms; your walls are continually before me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 51:13; Isaiah 62:5; Ezekiel 28:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your children are hurrying back, while those who destroyed you are going away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 54:1–3; Isaiah 66:12–13; Isaiah 61:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look around and see, they are all gathering and coming to you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">As surely as I live—this is Yahweh's declaration—you will surely wear them like jewelry, and you will put them on like a bride.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 10:10; Isaiah 54:1–2; Jeremiah 30:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Though you were a waste and desolate, a land that was in ruins,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">now you will be too small for the inhabitants, and those who devoured you will be far away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hosea 1:10; Joshua 17:14–16; Galatians 4:26–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">The children born during the time of your bereavement will say in your hearing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">'The place is too cramped for us, make room for us, so we may live here.'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13; Isaiah 3:26; Lamentations 1:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then you will ask yourself, 'Who has borne these children for me?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I was bereaved and barren, exiled and divorced. Who has raised these children?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Look, I was left all alone; where did these come from?'”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Luke 13:29; Isaiah 14:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is what the Lord Yahweh says,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Look, I will raise my hand to the nations; I will raise my signal flag to the peoples.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will bring your sons in their arms and carry your daughters on their shoulders.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:14; Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 60:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Kings will be your foster fathers, and their queens your nursemaids;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they will bow down to you with their faces to the earth and lick the dust of your feet;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you will know that I am Yahweh; those who wait for me will not be put to shame.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 11:21–22; Psalms 124:6–7; Matthew 12:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">Can the spoils be taken from the warrior, or captives be rescued from the fierce?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Instead of from the fierce , the Hebrew text has from the righteous , but most modern versions correct it to from the fierce .">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 54:15–17; Isaiah 35:4; Isaiah 41:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">But this is what Yahweh says,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Yes, the captives will be taken away from the warrior, and spoils will be rescued;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for I will oppose your adversary and save your children.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Isaiah 41:14–20; Revelation 16:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will feed your oppressors with their own flesh; and they will get drunk on their own blood, as if it were wine.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then all mankind will know that I, Yahweh, am your Savior and your Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob.”</span></span>
