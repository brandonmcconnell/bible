<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Joel 2:3; Isaiah 52:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yes, Yahweh will comfort Zion; he will comfort all her waste places;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">her wilderness he made like Eden, and her desert plains beside the Jordan River valley like the garden of Yahweh;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">joy and gladness will be found in her, thanksgiving, and the sound of singing.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Isaiah 2:3; 1 Corinthians 9:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Be attentive to me, my people; and listen to me, my people!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I will issue a decree, and I will make my justice to be a light for the nations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:17–18; Isaiah 46:13; Isaiah 42:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">My righteousness is near; my salvation will go out, and my arm will judge the nations;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the coastlands will wait for me; for my arm they will eagerly wait.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Psalms 102:25–26; Isaiah 34:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lift up your eyes to the sky, and look at the earth beneath,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the heavens will vanish away like smoke, the earth will wear out like a garment, and its inhabitants will die like flies.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But my salvation will continue forever, and my righteousness will never stop working.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 5:11; Psalms 37:31; Ezekiel 2:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen to me, you who know what is right, you people who have my law in your heart:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not fear the insults of men, nor be disheartened by their abuse.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Isaiah 50:9; Hosea 5:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the moth will eat them up like a garment, and the worm will eat them like wool;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but my righteousness will be forever, and my salvation to all generations.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 74:13–14; Isaiah 52:1; Psalms 89:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Awake, awake, clothe yourself with strength, arm of Yahweh.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Awake as in the days of old, the generations of ancient times.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Is it not you who crushed Rahab, you who pierced the monster?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 43:16; Isaiah 63:11–12; Isaiah 50:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Did you not dry up the sea, the waters of the great deep,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and make the depths of the sea into a way for the redeemed to pass through?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">The ransomed of Yahweh will return and come to Zion with cries of joy</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and with gladness forever on their heads; and gladness and joy will overtake them, and sorrow and mourning will flee away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 118:6; 1 Peter 1:24; Isaiah 2:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I, I, am he who comforts you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why are you afraid of men, who will die, the sons of mankind, who are made like grass?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 45:12; Isaiah 40:22; Psalms 104:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why have you forgotten Yahweh your Maker, who stretched out the heavens</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and laid the foundations of the earth? You are in constant dread every day</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because of the hot fury of the oppressor when he decides to destroy. Where is the fury of the oppressor?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Zechariah 9:11; Isaiah 52:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">The one who is bent down, Yahweh will hurry to release; he will not die and go down to the pit, nor will he lack bread.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35; Isaiah 54:5; Amos 9:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I am Yahweh your God, who churns up the sea so that its waves roar—Yahweh of hosts is his name.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:21; Isaiah 49:2; John 17:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have placed my words in your mouth, and I have covered you in the shadow of my hand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that I may plant the heavens, lay the foundations of the earth, and say to Zion, 'You are my people.'”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:1; Revelation 14:10; Psalms 60:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Awake, awake, stand up, Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you who have drunk out of the hand of Yahweh from the bowl of his anger;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you who have drunk out of the bowl, down to the dregs from the cup of staggering.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 49:21; Psalms 88:18; Psalms 142:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is no one among all the sons she has born to guide her;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there is no one among all the sons that she has raised to take her by the hand.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Amos 7:2; Isaiah 47:9; Ezekiel 14:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">These two troubles happened to you—who will grieve with you?—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">desolation and destruction, and the famine and the sword. Who will comfort you?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Hebrew text and some modern versions have How will I comfort you? , most modern versions correct this passage to Who will comfort you?">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11–12; Revelation 16:9–11; Ezekiel 39:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your sons have fainted; they lie at every street corner, like an antelope in a net;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they are filled with the anger of Yahweh, the rebuke of your God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 29:9; Isaiah 54:11; Isaiah 51:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But now hear this, you oppressed one and drunken one, but not drunk with wine:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 22:23; Isaiah 51:17; Jeremiah 50:34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your Lord Yahweh, your God, who pleads the cause of his people, says this,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“See, I have taken the cup of staggering from your hand—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the bowl, which is the cup of my anger—so that you will not drink it again.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:24; Zechariah 12:2; Isaiah 49:25–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will put it into the hand of your tormentors, those who have said to you, 'Lie down, that we may walk over you';</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you made your back like the ground and like the street for them to walk on.”</span></span>
