<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 66:9; Hosea 13:13; Isaiah 26:17–18"> They said to him, “Hezekiah says, 'This day is a day of distress, rebuke, and disgrace, like when a child is ready to be born, but the mother has no strength to give birth to her child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Isaiah 36:20; Isaiah 10:22"> It may be Yahweh your God will hear the words of the chief commander, whom the king of Assyria his master has sent to defy the living God, and will rebuke the words which Yahweh your God has heard. Now lift up your prayer for the remnant that is still here.'”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So the servants of King Hezekiah came to Isaiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 7:4; Isaiah 35:4; Mark 5:36"> and Isaiah said to them, “Say to your master: 'Yahweh says, “Do not be afraid of the words that you have heard, with which the servants of the king of Assyria have insulted me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 31:8–9; 2 Chronicles 32:21; Isaiah 33:10–12"> Look, I will put a spirit in him, and he will hear a certain report and go back to his own land. I will cause him to fall by the sword in his own land.”'”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 10:29; Numbers 33:20–21; 2 Kings 19:8–9"> Then the chief commander returned and found the king of Assyria fighting against Libnah, for he had heard that the king had gone away from Lachish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 18:1; Isaiah 20:5; Isaiah 37:7"> Then Sennacherib heard that Tirhakah king of Cush and Egypt had mobilized to fight against him, so he sent messengers again to Hezekiah with a message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 36:15; 2 Kings 18:5; Matthew 27:43"> “Say to Hezekiah, king of Judah, 'Do not let your God in whom you trust deceive you, saying, “Jerusalem will not be given into the hand of the king of Assyria.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 36:18–20; 2 Kings 18:33–35; Isaiah 14:17"> See, you have heard what the kings of Assyria have done to all lands by destroying them completely. So will you be rescued?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 11:31; 2 Kings 17:6; 2 Kings 18:11"> Have the gods of the nations rescued them, the nations that my fathers destroyed: Gozan, Haran, Rezeph, and the people of Eden in Tel Assar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 19:13; 2 Kings 17:24; Isaiah 36:19"> Where is the king of Hamath, the king of Arpad, the king of the cities of Sepharvaim, of Hena, and Ivvah?'”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 19:14; Psalms 123:1–4; Joel 2:17–20"> Hezekiah received this letter from hand of the messengers and read it. Then he went up to the house of Yahweh and spread it before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:8–9; Daniel 9:3–4; James 5:13"> Hezekiah prayed to Yahweh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Psalms 99:1; Psalms 86:10"> “Yahweh of hosts, God of Israel, you who sit above the cherubim, you are God alone over all the kingdoms of the earth. You made the heavens and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:40; Psalms 17:6; Isaiah 37:4"> Turn your ear, Yahweh, and listen. Open your eyes, Yahweh, and see, and hear the words of Sennacherib, which he has sent to mock the living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Nahum 2:11–12; 2 Kings 15:29; 2 Kings 17:6"> It is true, Yahweh, the kings of Assyria have destroyed all the nations and their lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 44:9–10; Jeremiah 10:11; Hosea 8:6"> They have put their gods into the fire, for they were not gods but the work of men's hands, just wood and stone. So the Assyrians have destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 46:10; 1 Kings 18:36–37; Psalms 83:17–18"> So now, Yahweh our God, save us from his power, so that all the kingdoms of the earth may know that you are Yahweh alone.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Isaiah 38:3–6; Acts 4:31"> Then Isaiah son of Amoz sent a message to Hezekiah, saying, “Yahweh, the God of Israel says, 'Because you have prayed to me concerning Sennacherib king of Assyria,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 16:4; Lamentations 2:13; Isaiah 1:8"> this is the word that Yahweh has spoken about him:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The virgin daughter of Zion despises you and laughs you to scorn;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the daughter of Jerusalem shakes her head at you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:7; Daniel 5:20–23; Isaiah 17:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whom have you defied and insulted? Against whom have you exalted your voice</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and lifted up your eyes in pride? Against the Holy One of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 14:8; Isaiah 10:18; Exodus 15:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">By your servants you have defied the Lord and have said, 'With the multitude of my chariots</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have gone up to the heights of the mountains, to the highest elevations of Lebanon.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will cut down its tall cedars and choice cypress trees there,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will enter into its farthest high places, its most fruitful forest.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 20:10; Isaiah 36:12; 2 Kings 19:23–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have dug wells and drunk water;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some ancient and modern versions have I have dug wells and drunk water in foreign lands .">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I dried up all the rivers of Egypt under the soles of my feet.'</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 4:27–28; Acts 2:23; 1 Peter 2:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Have you not heard how I determined it long ago</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and worked it out in ancient times? Now I am bringing it to pass.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You are here to reduce impregnable cities into heaps of ruins.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 129:6; Psalms 37:2; 1 Peter 1:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their inhabitants, of little strength, are shattered and ashamed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are plants in the field, green grass,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the grass on the roof or in the field,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">before the east wind.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 2:13; Psalms 139:1–11; Jeremiah 23:23–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I know your sitting down, your going out, your coming in, and your raging against me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:4; Isaiah 30:28; Isaiah 37:34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because of your raging against me, and because your arrogance has reached my ears,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will put my hook in your nose, and my bit in your mouth;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will turn you back the way you came.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 19:29; Leviticus 25:4–5; 1 Kings 13:3–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">This will be the sign for you:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">This year you will eat what grows wild, and in the second year what grows from that.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But in the third year you must plant and harvest, plant vineyards and eat their fruit.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; 2 Kings 19:30–31; Psalms 80:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">The remnant of the house of Judah that survives will again take root and bear fruit.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 9:7; 2 Kings 19:31; Isaiah 59:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">For from Jerusalem a remnant will come out; from Mount Zion survivors will come.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The zeal of Yahweh of hosts will do this.'”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 17:12; Isaiah 10:32–34; Isaiah 17:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore Yahweh says this about the king of Assyria:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He will not come into this city and he will not shoot an arrow here.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will not come before it with shield or build up a siege ramp against it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Proverbs 21:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">The way by which he came will be the same way he will leave; he will not enter this city—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 20:6; Isaiah 38:6; Isaiah 31:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I will defend this city and rescue it, for my own sake and for David my servant's sake.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 10:12; 2 Kings 19:35; Isaiah 10:33–34"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then the angel of Yahweh went out and attacked the camp of the Assyrians, putting to death 185,000 soldiers. When the men arose early in the morning, dead bodies lay everywhere.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jonah 1:2; Jonah 3:3; Genesis 10:11–12"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">So Sennacherib king of Assyria left Israel and went home and stayed in Nineveh.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 8:4; Jeremiah 51:27; Ezra 4:2"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">Later, as he was worshiping in the house of Nisrok his god, his sons Adrammelek and Sharezer killed him with the sword. Then they escaped into the land of Ararat. Then Esarhaddon his son reigned in his place.</span></span>
