<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 5:24; 1 Peter 3:18; Colossians 3:3–5"> Therefore, because Christ suffered in the flesh, arm yourselves with the same intention. For whoever has suffered in the flesh has ceased from sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:22–24; 1 Peter 1:14; Titus 3:3–8"> As a result, such a person, for the rest of his time in the flesh, no longer lives for men's desires, but for God's will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:5"> You have spent enough time doing what the Gentiles want to do, living in sensuality, passion, drunkenness, drunken celebrations, having wild parties, and committing disgusting acts of idolatry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; 1 Peter 2:12; Jude 1:10"> They think it is strange that you do not join with them in these floods of reckless behavior, so they speak evil about you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:9; Acts 10:42; 2 Timothy 4:1"> They will give an account to the one who is ready to judge the living and the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 8:2; 1 Peter 3:19; Romans 8:9–11"> For this purpose the gospel was preached also to the dead, so that, although they have been judged in the flesh as humans, they may live in the spirit the way God does.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 5:8–9; 1 Peter 1:13; 1 Peter 5:8"> The end of all things is coming near. Therefore be of sound mind, and be sober in your thinking for the sake of prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:12; Colossians 3:14; Proverbs 17:9"> Above all things, have fervent love for one another, for love covers a multitude of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Romans 12:13; Philippians 2:14"> Be hospitable to one another without complaining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 4:1–2"> As each one of you has received a gift, use it to serve one another, faithfully administering God's grace in its various forms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:31; Ephesians 6:10; 1 Peter 4:10"> If anyone speaks, let it be with God's words. If anyone serves, let it be from the strength that God supplies. Do these things so that in all ways God would be glorified through Jesus Christ. May there be to Jesus Christ glory and power forever and ever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:12; 1 Peter 1:6–7; 1 Peter 5:9"> Beloved, do not regard as strange the testing in the fire that has happened to you, as if something strange were happening to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 8:17; James 1:2–3; Romans 5:3"> Instead, however much you experience the sufferings of Christ, rejoice, so that you may also rejoice and be glad at the revealing of his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; Luke 6:22; Matthew 5:11"> If you are insulted for Christ's name, you are blessed, because the Spirit of glory and the Spirit of God is resting on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:11; 1 Timothy 5:13"> But let none of you suffer as a murderer, a thief, an evildoer, or a meddler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17–18; Philippians 1:29; 1 Peter 4:19"> But if anyone suffers as a Christian, let him not be ashamed; instead, let him glorify God with that name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:29; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Romans 2:9"> For it is time for judgment to begin with the household of God. If it begins with us, what will be the outcome for those who do not obey God's gospel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:31; Ezekiel 18:24; Luke 23:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">And “If it is difficult for the righteous to be saved,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">what will become of the ungodly and the sinner?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 37:5; 2 Timothy 1:12; 1 Peter 3:17"> Therefore let those who suffer because of God's will entrust their souls to the faithful Creator in well-doing.</span>
