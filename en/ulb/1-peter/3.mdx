<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:18; Ephesians 5:33; Ephesians 5:22–24"> In this way, you who are wives should submit to your own husbands. Do this so even if some men are disobedient to the word, they may be won without a word, through their wives' behavior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 1:15; Philippians 1:27"> For they will have seen your sincere behavior with respect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9–10; Romans 12:2; Isaiah 3:18–24"> Let it be done not with outward ornaments such as braided hair, jewels of gold, or fashionable clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:12; 1 Samuel 16:7; Romans 2:29"> Instead, let it be done with the inner person of the heart, and the lasting beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit, which is precious before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Titus 2:3–4; 1 Timothy 5:5; Luke 2:37"> For this is how holy women long ago who hoped in God adorned themselves, by submitting to their husbands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:12; Romans 9:7–9; Isaiah 57:11"> In this way Sarah obeyed Abraham and called him her lord. You are now her children if you do what is good and if you are not afraid of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 3:19; Genesis 2:23–24; Matthew 19:3–9"> In the same way, you husbands should live with your wives according to understanding, as with a weaker container, a woman. You should give them honor as fellow heirs of the grace of life. Do this so that your prayers will not be hindered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 12:10; Ephesians 4:31–2; 1 Peter 1:22"> Finally, all of you, be likeminded, compassionate, loving as brothers, tenderhearted, and humble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; Romans 12:17; Romans 12:14"> Do not pay back evil for evil or insult for insult. On the contrary, continue to bless, because for this you were called, that you might inherit a blessing.<br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 34:12–16; James 1:26; 1 Peter 2:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">“The one who wants to love life and see good days</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">should stop his tongue from evil and his lips from speaking deceit.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Psalms 34:14; Hebrews 12:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let him turn away from what is bad and do what is good.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let him seek peace and pursue it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 34:15–16; John 9:31; Proverbs 15:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">The eyes of the Lord see the righteous, and his ears hear their requests.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But the face of the Lord is against those who do evil.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Romans 8:28; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Who is the one who will harm you if you are eager to do what is good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; 1 Peter 2:19–20; Matthew 5:10–12"> But if you suffer because of righteousness, you are blessed. Do not fear what they fear. Do not be troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; 2 Timothy 2:25–26; Luke 21:14–15"> Instead, set apart the Lord Christ in your hearts as holy. Always be ready to answer everyone who asks you why you have confidence in God. Do this with meekness and respect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Hebrews 13:18; 1 Peter 2:15"> Have a good conscience so that the people who insult your good life in Christ may be ashamed because they are speaking against you as if you were evildoers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 4:19; Matthew 26:39"> It is better, if God desires, that you suffer for doing good than for doing evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Hebrews 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> Christ also suffered once for sins. He who is righteous suffered for us, who were unrighteous, so that he would bring us to God. He was put to death in the flesh, but he was made alive by the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 4:6; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 42:7"> By the Spirit, he went and preached to the spirits who are now in prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5; Genesis 6:3"> They were disobedient when the patience of God was waiting in the days of Noah, in the days of the building of an ark, and God saved a few people—eight souls—by means of the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 16:16; Ezekiel 36:25–26; Acts 2:38"> This is a symbol of the baptism that saves you now—not as a washing away of dirt from the body, but as the appeal of a good conscience to God—through the resurrection of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 10:12"> Christ is at the right hand of God. He went into heaven. Angels, authorities, and powers must submit to him.</span>
