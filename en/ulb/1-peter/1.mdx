<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 1:1; Acts 2:5–11; Hebrews 11:13"> Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the foreigners of the dispersion, the chosen ones, throughout Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:13; Ephesians 1:4–5; Hebrews 12:24"> This is according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, set apart by the Spirit for obedience and for the sprinkling of the blood of Jesus Christ. May grace be to you, and may your peace increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:4; Romans 15:13"> May the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ be praised! In his great mercy, he has given us new birth to a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Hebrews 9:15; 1 Peter 5:4"> This is for an inheritance that will not perish, will not become stained, and will not fade away. It is reserved in heaven for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Ephesians 2:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:13–14"> You are protected by God's power through faith, for the salvation that is ready to be revealed in the last times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 5:10; Romans 12:12"> You are very glad about this, even though it is now necessary for you to feel sadness in many different troubles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 23:10; Isaiah 48:10; 1 Peter 4:12"> This is for the proving of your faith, which is more precious than gold that perishes, even though it is tested by fire. This happens so that your faith will be found to result in praise, glory, and honor at the revealing of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 20:29; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Romans 15:13"> You have not seen him, but you love him. You do not see him now, but you believe in him and rejoice with joy that is inexpressible and filled with glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 6:22; James 1:21; Hebrews 11:13"> You are now receiving for yourselves the result of your faith, the salvation of your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:17; Luke 10:24; Luke 24:25–27"> The prophets searched and inquired carefully about this salvation, about the grace that would be yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 52:13–14; 2 Peter 1:21; Luke 24:25–27"> They searched to know about whom and when the Spirit of Christ in them was speaking to them. This was happening as he was telling them in advance about the sufferings of Christ and the glorious things after that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 15:26; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Ephesians 3:10"> It was revealed to them that they were not serving themselves, but you, when they spoke of the things that have now been told to you by those who preached the gospel to you by the Holy Spirit sent from heaven—things into which angels long to look.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Luke 12:35; 1 Peter 4:7"> So gird up the loins of your mind. Be sober. Put your hope fully on the grace that will be brought to you when Jesus Christ is revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Titus 3:3–5; 1 Peter 4:2–3"> As obedient children, do not conform yourselves to the desires that you followed when you were ignorant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:48; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Hebrews 12:14"> But as the one who called you is holy, you, too, be holy in your whole behavior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Leviticus 19:2; Leviticus 20:7"> For it is written, “Be holy, because I am holy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:34–35; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Matthew 6:9"> So if you call “Father” the one who judges impartially and according to each person's work, go through the time of your journey in reverence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:20; Titus 2:14; Psalms 49:7–8"> You know that it was not with perishable silver or gold that you have been redeemed from the foolish behavior that you learned from your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 1:7; 1 Peter 2:22–24; John 1:29"> Instead, you have been redeemed with the precious blood of Christ, who was like a lamb without blemish or spot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:9–10; Hebrews 9:26"> Christ was chosen before the foundation of the world, but now he has been revealed to you in these last times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Hebrews 2:9; John 12:44"> Through him you believe in God, who raised him from the dead and gave him glory, so that your faith and hope would be in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Hebrews 13:1; Romans 12:9–10"> You made your souls pure by obedience to the truth. This was for the purpose of sincere brotherly love; so love one another earnestly from the heart.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some older versions read, You made your souls pure by obedience to the truth through the Spirit .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; Hebrews 4:12; John 1:13"> You have been born again, not from perishable seed, but from imperishable seed, through the living and remaining word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; Psalms 103:15; James 4:14"> For,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“All flesh is like grass,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and all its glory is like the wild flower of the grass.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The grass dries up, and the flower falls off,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Matthew 5:18; Psalms 119:89"> <span className="pl pl-1">but the word of the Lord remains forever.”</span>This is the good news that was announced to you.</span>
