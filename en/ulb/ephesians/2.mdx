<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 2:13; Ephesians 2:5–6; Colossians 3:1–4"> And as for you, you were dead in your trespasses and sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 6:12; 1 John 5:19; Ephesians 2:3"> It was in these trespasses and sins that you once lived according to the ways of this world. You were living according to the ruler of the authorities of the air, the spirit that is working in the sons of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Ephesians 4:22; Galatians 5:16–24"> Once we all lived among these people, fulfilling the evil desires of our sinful nature, and carrying out the desires of the body and of the mind. We were by nature children of wrath, like the rest of humanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; Ephesians 1:7; Psalms 86:15"> But God is rich in mercy because of his great love with which he loved us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 3:24; Ephesians 2:8; Romans 5:10"> While we were dead in trespasses, he made us alive together in Christ—by grace you have been saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–3; Ephesians 1:3; Romans 6:4–5"> God raised us up together with Christ, and God made us sit together in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:16–17; Titus 3:4; Ephesians 2:4"> so that in the ages to come he might show to us the immeasurably great riches of his grace expressed in his kindness toward us in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:22–26; Ephesians 2:5; Romans 4:16"> For by grace you have been saved through faith, and this did not come from you, it is the gift of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 11:6; Titus 3:3–5"> not from works and so no one may boast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Colossians 1:10; Hebrews 13:21"> For we are God's workmanship, created in Christ Jesus to do good deeds that God planned long ago for us, so that we would walk in them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Colossians 2:11; Colossians 2:13; Colossians 3:11"> Therefore remember that once you were Gentiles in the flesh. You are called “uncircumcision” by what is called the “circumcision” in the flesh made by human hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Colossians 1:21; Jeremiah 31:31–34"> For at that time you were separated from Christ, foreigners to the people of Israel, strangers to the covenants of the promise, having no hope and without God in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:39; Colossians 1:20–22; Colossians 1:13–14"> But now in Christ Jesus you who once were far away from God have been brought near by the blood of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11; Colossians 3:15"> For he is our peace. He made the two one. By his flesh he destroyed the wall of hostility that divided us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 2:14; Galatians 3:10; Colossians 1:21–22"> That is, he abolished the law of commandments and regulations so that he might create one new man in himself, and so making peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:20–22; Romans 5:10; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> Christ reconciles both peoples into one body to God through the cross, putting to death the hostility.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 57:19–21; Acts 10:36; Romans 5:1"> Jesus came and proclaimed peace to you who were far away and peace to those who were near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:13; Ephesians 3:12; Romans 5:2"> For through Jesus we both have access in one Spirit to the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; 1 John 3:1; Galatians 6:10"> So then, now you Gentiles are no longer strangers and foreigners. Instead you are fellow citizens with the saints and members of God's household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Isaiah 28:16; 1 Peter 2:7–8"> You have been built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, and Christ Jesus himself was the cornerstone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16–17; 1 Corinthians 3:9; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> In him the whole building fits together and grows as a temple in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; 1 Corinthians 3:16; 1 John 4:16"> It is in him that you also are being built together as a dwelling place for God in the Spirit.</span>
