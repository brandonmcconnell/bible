<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 5:28–29; Isaiah 26:19; Matthew 25:46"> Many of those who sleep in the dust of the earth will rise up, some to everlasting life and some to shame and everlasting contempt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Proverbs 4:18; James 5:19–20"> Those who are wise will shine like the brightness of the sky above, and those who turn many to righteousness, are like the stars forever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 12:9; Revelation 22:10; Daniel 8:26"> But you, Daniel, close up these words; keep the book sealed until the time of the end. Many will run here and there, and knowledge will increase.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 10:4–6; Daniel 10:16; Daniel 10:10"> Then I, Daniel, looked, and there were two others standing. One stood on the bank on this side of the river, and one stood on the bank on the other side of the river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 8:13; Ezekiel 9:2; Zechariah 1:12–13"> One of them said to the man clothed in linen, the one who was upstream along the river, “How long will it be to the end of these amazing events?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 7:25; Revelation 12:14; Daniel 8:24"> I heard the man clothed in linen, who was upstream along the river—he raised his right hand and his left hand to heaven and swore by the one who lives forever that it would be for a time, times, and half a time. When the power of the holy people has been finally broken, all these things will be completed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:7; 1 Peter 1:11; Daniel 10:14"> I heard, but I did not understand. So I asked, “My master, what will be the outcome of all these things?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 12:4; Daniel 8:26; Revelation 10:4"> He said, “Go your way, Daniel, for the words are shut up and sealed until the time of the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:11; Daniel 11:35; Hosea 14:9"> Many will be purified, cleansed, and refined, but the wicked will act wickedly. None of the wicked will understand, but those who are wise will understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 11:31; Matthew 24:15; Mark 13:14"> From the time that the regular burnt offering is taken away and the abomination that causes complete desolation is set up, there will be 1,290 days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 30:18; Daniel 8:14; Romans 11:15"> Blessed is the one who waits until the end of the 1,335 days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; Zechariah 3:7"> You must go your way until the end, and you will rest. You will rise in the place assigned to you, at the end of days.”</span>
