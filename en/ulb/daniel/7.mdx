<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 1:17; Amos 3:7; Daniel 5:1"> In the first year of Belshazzar king of Babylonia, Daniel had a dream and visions in his mind while lying on his bed. Then he wrote down what he had seen in the dream. He wrote down the most important events:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 7:1; Revelation 17:15"> Daniel explained, “In my vision at night I saw that the four winds of heaven were stirring up the great sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 13:1; Daniel 7:17; Daniel 2:32–33"> Four large animals, each one different from the other, came up out of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Isaiah 14:13–17; Ezekiel 17:3"> The first was like a lion but had eagle's wings. As I was looking, its wings were torn off and it was lifted from the ground and made to stand on two feet, like a man. The mind of a man was given to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 2:39; Isaiah 13:17–18; Proverbs 17:12"> Then there was a second animal, like a bear, and it was bending over; it had three ribs between its teeth in its mouth. It was told, 'Get up and devour many people.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 13:2; Daniel 8:20–22; Daniel 8:5–8"> After this I looked again. There was another animal, one that looked like a leopard. On its back it had four wings like bird wings, and it had four heads. It was given authority to rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 13:1; Revelation 12:3; Revelation 17:12"> After this I saw in the visions of the night a fourth animal, terrifying, frightening, and very strong. It had large iron teeth; it devoured, broke in pieces, and trampled underfoot what was left. It was different from the other animals, and it had ten horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 13:5–6; Revelation 13:1; Psalms 12:3"> While I was considering the horns, I looked and saw another horn grow up among them, a little horn. Three of the first horns were wrenched out by the roots. I saw in this horn eyes like the eyes of a man and a mouth that was boasting about great things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 1:14; Mark 9:3; Daniel 7:13"> As I looked,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">thrones were set in place,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the Ancient of Days took his seat.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His clothing was as white as snow,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the hair of his head was like pure wool.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His throne was flames of fire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and its wheels were burning fire.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 30:27; Deuteronomy 33:2; Revelation 20:11–15"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">A river of fire flowed out from before him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">millions served him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and one hundred million were standing before him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The court was in session,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the books were opened.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 19:20; Revelation 20:10; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">I continued to look because of the boastful words spoken by the horn. I watched while the animal was killed, and its body was destroyed, and it was given over to be burned up.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 8:7; Daniel 7:4–6"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">As for the rest of the four animals, their authority to rule was taken away, but their lives were prolonged for a period of time.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:64; Revelation 1:7; Luke 21:27"> In my visions that night,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">I saw one coming with the clouds of heaven like a son of man;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he came to the Ancient of Days</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and was presented before him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; Daniel 7:27; John 3:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Authority to rule and glory and royal power were given to him</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so that all the peoples, nations, and languages should serve him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His authority to rule is an everlasting authority that will not pass away,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and his kingdom is one that will never be destroyed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 7:28; Jeremiah 17:16; Genesis 40:7–8"> As for me, Daniel, my spirit was grieved inside of me, and the visions I saw in my mind troubled me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:13–14; Zechariah 2:3; Revelation 5:5"> I approached one of them standing there and asked him to show me the meaning of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 8:19–22; Daniel 2:37–40; John 18:36"> 'These large animals, four in number, are four kings that will arise from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Isaiah 60:12–14; Revelation 5:10"> But the holy people of the Most High will receive the kingdom, and they will possess it forever and ever.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 7:7; Daniel 2:40–43"> Then I wanted to know more about the fourth animal—it was so different from the others and very horrifying with its iron teeth and bronze claws; it devoured, broke into pieces, and trampled on what was left with its feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 7:23; Daniel 7:8; Daniel 7:11"> I wanted to know about the ten horns on its head, and about the other horn that grew up, and before which the three horns fell down. I wanted to know about the horn that had eyes and about the mouth that boasted about great things and that seemed greater than its companions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Revelation 17:14; Daniel 8:24"> As I looked, this horn waged war against the holy people and was defeating them</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 7:18; Revelation 20:4; Revelation 5:10"> until the Ancient of Days came, and justice was given to the holy people of the Most High. Then the time came when the holy people received the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 2:40; Daniel 7:7; Luke 2:1"> This is what that person said, 'As for the fourth animal,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">it will be a fourth kingdom on earth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that will be different from all the other kingdoms.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will devour the whole earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and it will trample it down</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and break it into pieces.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 7:20; Daniel 11:36; Revelation 13:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">As for the ten horns,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">out of this kingdom ten kings will arise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and another will arise after them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will be different from the previous ones,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he will conquer the three kings.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Revelation 12:14; 2 Thessalonians 2:4"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will speak words against the Most High</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and will oppress the holy people of the Most High God.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will try to change the festivals and the law.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">These things will be given into his hand</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for one year, two years, and half a year.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 20:10–11; 2 Thessalonians 2:8; Revelation 11:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">But the court will be in session,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they will take his royal power away</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to be consumed and destroyed at the end.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 72:11; Luke 1:33; Revelation 11:15"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">The kingdom and the dominion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the greatness of the kingdoms under the whole heaven,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will be given to the people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who belong to the holy people of the Most High.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His kingdom is an everlasting kingdom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and all other kingdoms will serve and obey him.'</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51; Daniel 10:8"> Here is the end of the matter. As for me, Daniel, my thoughts greatly alarmed me and my face changed in appearance. But I kept these things to myself.”</span>
