<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 9:1; Daniel 5:31; Daniel 10:18"> In the first year of Darius the Mede, I myself came to support and protect Michael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezra 4:5–6; Daniel 8:26; Revelation 21:5"> Now I will tell you the truth. Three kings will arise in Persia, and a fourth will be far richer than all the others. When he has gained power through his riches, he will stir up everyone against the kingdom of Greece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 11:16; Daniel 11:36; Daniel 8:21"> A mighty king will rise up who will rule a very great kingdom, and he will act according to his desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 8:22; Daniel 8:8; Jeremiah 12:17"> When he has risen up, his kingdom will be broken and divided to the four winds of heaven, but not to his own descendants, and not with his power when he had been ruling. For his kingdom will be uprooted for others besides his descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 11:14; Daniel 11:11; Daniel 11:25"> The king of the South will become strong, but one of his commanders will become even stronger than he and will rule his kingdom with great power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 11:13; Daniel 11:40; Daniel 11:7"> After a few years, when the time is right, they will make an alliance. The daughter of the king of the South will come to the king of the North to confirm the agreement. But she will not keep her arm's strength, nor will he stand, or his arm. She will be abandoned—she and those who brought her, and her father, and the one who supported her in those times.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Instead of his arm , which the Hebrew text has, some ancient and many modern versions read his seed , that is, his children .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 11:38–39; Daniel 11:19–20; Isaiah 9:14"> But a branch from her roots will rise up in her place. He will attack the army and enter the fortress of the king of the North. He will fight them, and he will conquer them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 37:19; Isaiah 46:1–2; Jeremiah 43:12–13"> He will carry off to Egypt their gods along with their cast metal figures and their precious vessels of silver and of gold. For some years he will stay away from the king of the North.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then the king of the North will invade the realm of the king of the South, but he will withdraw to his own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:42; Daniel 11:7; Daniel 11:26"> His sons will get ready and assemble a great army. It will keep coming and will flood everything; it will pass through all the way to his fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 8:7; Daniel 11:5; Psalms 33:16–17"> Then the king of the South will become very angry; he will go and fight against him, the king of the North. The king of the North will raise up a great army, but the army will be given into his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 12:22–23; 2 Kings 14:10; Isaiah 10:7–12"> The army will be carried off, and the heart of the king of the South will be lifted up, and he will make tens of thousands to fall, but he will not be victorious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 4:16; Daniel 12:7; Daniel 11:6–7"> Then the king of the North will raise up another army, greater than the first. After some years, the king of the North will surely come with a great army supplied with much equipment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Acts 4:25–28"> In those times many will rise against the king of the South. Sons of the violent among your people will set themselves in order to fulfill a vision, but they will stumble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:6; Ezekiel 17:17; Ezekiel 4:2"> The king of the North will come, pour out earth for siege mounds, and capture a city with fortifications. The forces of the South will not be able to stand, not even their best soldiers. There will be no strength to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 8:9; Daniel 11:36; Daniel 11:3"> Instead, the one who comes will act according to his desires against him; no one will stand in his way. He will stand in the land of beauty, and destruction will be in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 12:17; Ezekiel 4:3; Ezekiel 4:7"> The king of the North will set his face to come with the strength of his entire kingdom, and with him will be an agreement that he will make with the king of the South. He will give him a daughter of women in marriage to ruin the kingdom of the South. But the plan will not succeed or help him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 12:14; Jeremiah 31:10; Zephaniah 2:11"> After this, the king of the North will pay attention to the coastlands and will capture many of them. But a commander will end his arrogance and will cause his arrogance to turn back upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:21; Psalms 37:36; Job 20:8"> Then he will pay attention to the fortresses of his own land, but he will stumble and fall; he will not be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 60:17; Daniel 11:7; Deuteronomy 15:2–3"> Then someone will rise up in his place who will make a tax collector pass through for the sake of the splendor of the kingdom. But in the next days he will be broken, but not in anger or in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 11:34; Daniel 8:25; Judges 9:1–20"> In his place will rise up a despised person to whom the people will not have given the honor of royal power; he will come unexpectedly and will take over the kingdom by trickery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 8:10–11; Daniel 11:10; Daniel 9:26"> An army will be swept away like a flood from before him. Both that army and the leader of the covenant will be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 8:25; 2 Thessalonians 2:9; Psalms 52:2"> From the time an alliance is made with him, he will act deceitfully; with only a small number of people he will become strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 7:25; Proverbs 23:7; Matthew 9:4"> Without warning he will come into the richest part of the province, and he will do what neither his father nor his father's father did. He will spread among his followers the booty, the plunder, and the wealth. He will plan the overthrow of fortresses, but only for a time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Daniel 11:2; Proverbs 28:25"> He will wake up his power and his heart against the king of the South with a great army. The king of the South will wage war with a large, very powerful army, but he will not stand because others will make plots against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 11:10; Daniel 11:40; 2 Samuel 4:2–12"> Even those who eat his fine food will try to destroy him. His army will be swept away like a flood, and many of them will fall killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; Jeremiah 9:3–5; Habakkuk 2:3"> Both these kings, with their hearts set on evil against each other, will sit at the same table and lie to each other, but it will be of no use. For the end will come at the time that has been fixed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Daniel 11:30–32; Daniel 8:24; Acts 3:25"> Then the king of the North will go back into his land with great riches, with his heart set against the holy covenant. He will act and then will return to his own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 17:26; Isaiah 14:31; Daniel 11:25"> At the appointed time he will return and come against the South again. But this time it will not be as before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Numbers 24:24; Jeremiah 2:10"> For ships of Kittim will come against him, and he will become afraid and will turn back. He will be furious against the holy covenant, and he will show favor to those who forsake the holy covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 12:11; Daniel 9:27; Matthew 24:15"> His forces will rise up and profane the fortress sanctuary. They will take away the regular burnt offering, and they will set up the abomination that causes desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Psalms 9:10; John 17:3"> As for those who acted wickedly against the covenant, he will deceive them and corrupt them. But the people who know their God will be strong and will take action.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 24:9; John 16:2; Malachi 2:7"> The wise among the people will make many understand. But they will stumble by the sword and by flame; they will stumble into captivity and into being robbed for days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; Romans 16:18; Acts 20:29–30"> In their stumbling, they will be helped with a little help. In hypocrisy many will join themselves with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Malachi 3:2–4; Revelation 2:10"> Some of the wise will stumble so that refining will happen to them, and cleansing, and purifying, until the time of the end. For the appointed time is still to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Revelation 13:5–6; 2 Thessalonians 2:4; Deuteronomy 10:17"> The king will act according to his desires. He will lift himself up and make himself great above every god. Against the God of gods he will say astonishing things, for he will succeed until the wrath is completed. For what has been decreed will be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:4; Isaiah 14:13; 1 Timothy 4:3"> He will pay no attention to the gods of his ancestors or to the god desirable to women. Neither will he will pay attention to any other god. For all above every one he will make himself great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Revelation 17:1–5; Isaiah 44:9; Revelation 13:12–17"> He will honor the god of fortresses instead of these. It is a god whom his fathers did not acknowledge that he will honor with gold and silver, with precious stones and valuable gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Revelation 18:9–13"> He will attack the strongest fortresses with the help of a foreign god. To anyone who acknowledges him, he will give much honor. He will make them rulers over many people, and he will divide up the land as a reward.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Instead of He will attack the strongest fortresses , some modern versions have He will defend his fortress .">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Daniel 11:35; Daniel 11:10; Isaiah 5:28"> At the time of the end the king of the South will attack. The king of the North will storm against him with chariots and horsemen, and with many ships. He will go against lands, flood them, and pass through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:47; Jeremiah 49:6; Isaiah 11:13–15"> He will go into the land of beauty, and tens of thousands of Israelites will fall. But these will escape from his hand: Edom, Moab, and the remainder of the people of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Zechariah 10:10–11; Ezekiel 29:14; Revelation 11:8"> He will extend his hand into lands; the land of Egypt will not be rescued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:3; Nahum 3:9; Ezekiel 30:4–5"> He will have control over the treasures of gold and of silver, and over all the riches of Egypt; the Libyans and the Cushites will be in his footsteps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Revelation 19:19–21; Revelation 16:12; Revelation 17:13"> But news from the east and the north will frighten him, and he will go out with great rage to completely destroy and to set many apart for destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:4; Daniel 8:25; Revelation 19:19–21"> He will set up the tent of his royal residence between the seas and the mountain of the beauty of holiness. He will come to his end, and there will be no helper for him.</span>
