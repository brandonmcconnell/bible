<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 55:9–11; Jeremiah 9:2–6; Jeremiah 20:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why do you make me see iniquity and look upon wrongdoing?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Destruction and violence are before me; there is strife, and contention rises up.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:126; Isaiah 1:21–23; Job 21:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore the law is weakened, and justice does not last for any time.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the wicked surround the righteous; therefore false justice goes out.”</span>Yahweh replies to Habakkuk</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:40–41; Lamentations 4:12; Isaiah 29:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Look at the nations and examine them; be amazed and astonished!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I am surely about to do something in your days that you will not believe when it is reported to you.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:5–9; Jeremiah 52:9–11; Deuteronomy 5:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">They are terrifying and fearsome; their judgment and splendor proceed from themselves.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Zephaniah 3:3; Hosea 8:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their horses also are swifter than leopards, quicker than the evening wolves.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So their horses stamp,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their horsemen come from a great distance—they fly like an eagle hurrying to eat.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Hosea 13:15; Hosea 1:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">They all come for violence;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their multitudes go like the desert wind, and they gather captives like sand.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Hebrew text translated here as their multitudes go like the desert wind is very difficult; many modern versions have other interpretations.">a</a></sup><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Jeremiah 30:11; Jeremiah 46:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Are you not from ancient times, Yahweh my God, my Holy One? <span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:4–5; Psalms 34:15–16; Jeremiah 12:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your eyes are too pure to gaze upon evil, and you are not able to look on wrongdoing with favor;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">why then have you looked favorably on those who betray?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why are you silent while the wicked swallow up those more righteous than they are?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 6:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">You make men like fish in the sea, like creeping things without a ruler over them.</span><br /></span>
