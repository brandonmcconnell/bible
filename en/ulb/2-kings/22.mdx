<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:39; 2 Chronicles 34:1–33; Jeremiah 1:2"> Josiah was eight years old when he began to reign; he reigned thirty-one years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Jedidah (she was the daughter of Adaiah of Bozkath).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; Joshua 1:7; 2 Kings 18:3"> He did what was right in the eyes of Yahweh. He walked in all the way of David his ancestor, and he did not turn away either to the right or to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:3–33"> It came about that in the eighteenth year of King Josiah, he sent Shaphan son of Azaliah son of Meshullam, the scribe, to the house of Yahweh, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 12:4; Mark 12:41–42; 2 Chronicles 34:9–18"> “Go up to Hilkiah the high priest and tell him to count the money that has been brought into the house of Yahweh, which the temple guards have gathered from the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 12:11–14; 2 Kings 12:5; 2 Chronicles 24:12–13"> Let it be given into the hand of the workmen who are in charge of the house of Yahweh, and let them give it to the workmen who are in the house of Yahweh, for them to make repairs to damage in the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Let them give money to the carpenters, the builders, and the masons, and also to buy timber and cut stone to repair the temple.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 12:15; 1 Corinthians 4:2; Nehemiah 7:2"> But no accounting was required for the money that was given to them, because they handled it faithfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:24–26; 2 Chronicles 34:14–28"> Hilkiah the high priest said to Shaphan the scribe, “I have found the book of the law in the house of Yahweh.” So Hilkiah gave the book to Shaphan, and he read it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:11; 2 Kings 25:22; 2 Kings 18:18"> Shaphan went and took the book to the king, and also reported to him, saying, “Your servants have spent the money that was found in the temple and they have given it into the hand of the workmen who supervise the care for the house of Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:21; Jeremiah 13:18; Deuteronomy 17:18–20"> Then Shaphan the scribe said to the king, “Hilkiah the priest has given me a book.” Then Shaphan read it to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:19; Joel 2:13; 2 Kings 22:19"> It came about that when the king had heard the words of the law, he tore his clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:24; 2 Kings 25:22; 2 Kings 19:2–3"> The king commanded Hilkiah the priest, Ahikam son of Shaphan, Akbor son of Micaiah, Shaphan the scribe, and Asaiah, his own servant, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23–28; Deuteronomy 31:17–18; Jeremiah 37:17"> “Go and consult with Yahweh for me, and for the people and for all Judah, because of the words of this book that has been found. For great is the anger of Yahweh that has been kindled against us because our ancestors have not listened to the words of this book so as to obey all that was written concerning us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 4:4; 2 Chronicles 34:22; Micah 6:4"> So Hilkiah the priest, Ahikam, Akbor, Shaphan, and Asaiah went to Huldah the prophetess, the wife of Shallum son of Tikvah son of Harhas, keeper of the wardrobe (she lived in Jerusalem in the second quarter), and they spoke with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 1:6; 2 Kings 1:16; Jeremiah 23:28"> She said to them, “This is what Yahweh, the God of Israel, says: 'Tell the man who sent you to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 9:11–14; Joshua 23:15; Deuteronomy 29:27"> “This is what Yahweh says: 'See, I will bring disaster to this place and to its inhabitants, according to everything written in the book that the king of Judah has read.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 21:22; Jeremiah 7:20; Deuteronomy 29:24–28"> Because they have forsaken me and have burned incense to other gods, so that they might provoke me to anger with all the deeds they have committed—therefore my anger has been kindled against this place, and it will not be extinguished.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:26–28; Malachi 3:16–17; Isaiah 3:10"> But to the king of Judah, who sent you to ask Yahweh's will, this is what you will say to him: “Yahweh, the God of Israel says this: 'About the words that you heard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 51:17; 1 Kings 21:29; Isaiah 57:15"> because your heart was tender, and because you have humbled yourself before Yahweh, when you heard what I said against this place and its inhabitants, that they would become a desolation and a curse, and because you have torn your clothes and wept before me, I also have listened to you—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 37:37; Isaiah 57:1–2; 1 Chronicles 17:11"> See, I will gather you to your ancestors, and you will be gathered to your grave in peace. Your eyes will not see all the disaster that I will bring upon this place.'”'” So the men took this message back to the king.</span>
