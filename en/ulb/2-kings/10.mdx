<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 8:30; 1 Kings 13:32; 1 Kings 21:8–14"> Now Ahab had seventy descendants in Samaria. Jehu wrote letters and sent them to Samaria, to the rulers of Jezreel, including the elders and the guardians of Ahab's descendants, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 5:6"> “Your master's descendants are with you, and you also have chariots and horses and a fortified city and armor. So then, as soon as this letter comes to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8–9; 1 Kings 12:20–21; 2 Samuel 2:12–17"> select the best and most deserving of your master's descendants and set him on his father's throne, and fight for your master's royal line.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 9:24; 2 Kings 9:27; Isaiah 27:4"> But they were terrified and said among themselves, “See, the two kings could not stand before Jehu. So how can we stand?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 20:32; 1 Kings 20:4; Joshua 9:11"> Then the man who was in charge of the palace, and the man who was over the city, and the elders also, and they who raised the children, sent word back to Jehu, saying, “We are your servants. We will do everything that you command us. We will not make any man king. Do what is good in your eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 2:20–23; Deuteronomy 5:9; Numbers 25:4"> Then Jehu wrote a letter the second time to them, saying, “If you are on my side, and if you will listen to my voice, you must take the heads of the men of your master's descendants, and come to me to Jezreel by tomorrow this time.” Now the king's descendants, seventy in number, were with the important men of the city, who were bringing them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 21:21; 2 Kings 11:1; 2 Kings 10:9"> So when the letter came to them, they took the king's sons and killed them, seventy persons, put their heads in baskets, and sent them to Jehu in Jezreel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:23; 2 Samuel 11:18–21; Mark 6:28"> A messenger came to Jehu, saying, “They have brought the heads of the king's sons.” So he said, “Put them in two heaps at the entrance of the gate until the morning.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 9:14–24; Isaiah 5:3; Hosea 1:4"> In the morning Jehu went out and stood, and said to all the people, “You are innocent. See, I plotted against my master and killed him, but who killed all these?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 21:29; 1 Kings 21:19; 2 Kings 9:7–10"> Now you should certainly realize that no part of Yahweh's word, the word that he spoke concerning the family of Ahab, will fall to the ground, for Yahweh has done what he spoke about through his servant Elijah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 18:19; 1 Kings 18:19; 1 Kings 22:6"> So Jehu killed all who remained in the family of Ahab in Jezreel, and all his important men, his close friends, and his priests, until none of them remained.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then Jehu arose and left; he went to Samaria. As he was arriving at Beth Eked of the shepherd,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 8:29; 2 Kings 8:24; 2 Chronicles 21:17"> he met brothers of Ahaziah king of Judah. Jehu said to them, “Who are you?” They answered, “We are brothers of Ahaziah, and we are going down to greet the children of the king and the children of Queen Jezebel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:10; 2 Kings 10:6; 2 Kings 10:10–11"> Jehu said to his own men, “Take them alive.” So they took them alive and killed them at the well of Beth Eked, all forty-two men. He did not leave any of them alive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:14–19; Ezra 10:19; Ezekiel 17:18"> When Jehu had left there, he met Jehonadab son of Recab coming to meet him. Jehu greeted him and said to him, “Is your heart with me, as my heart is with yours?” Jehonadab answered, “It is.” Jehu said, “If it is, give me your hand.” So he gave Jehu his hand, and Jehu took Jehonadab up with him into the chariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10; Matthew 6:5; 2 Kings 10:31"> Jehu said, “Come with me and see my zeal for Yahweh.” So he had Jonadab ride along with him in his chariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 9:8; 2 Chronicles 22:8; Malachi 4:1"> When he came to Samaria, Jehu killed all who remained from Ahab's descendants in Samaria, until he had destroyed Ahab's royal line, just as was told them before by the word of Yahweh, which he had spoken to Elijah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 16:31–32; 2 Kings 3:2; Romans 3:8"> Then Jehu gathered all the people together and said to them, “Ahab served Baal a little, but Jehu will serve him much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 22:6; 1 Kings 18:19; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> Now therefore call to me all the prophets of Baal, all his worshipers, and all his priests. Let no one be left out, for I have a great sacrifice to offer to Baal. Whoever does not come will not live.” But Jehu did this deceitfully, with the intent to kill the worshipers of Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joel 1:14; Exodus 32:5; 1 Kings 18:19–20"> Jehu said, “Set a time to have an assembly for Baal.” So they announced it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 16:32; 2 Kings 11:18; Joel 3:2"> Then Jehu sent throughout all Israel and all the worshipers of Baal came, so that there was not a man left who did not come. They came into the temple of Baal, and it was filled from one end to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 28:2; Matthew 22:11–12"> Jehu said to the man who kept the priest's wardrobe, “Bring out robes for all the worshipers of Baal.” So the man brought out robes to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Matthew 25:32–33; Matthew 13:41"> So Jehu went with Jehonadab son of Recab into the house of Baal, and he said to the worshipers of Baal, “Search and make sure that there is no one here with you from the servants of Yahweh, but the worshipers of Baal alone.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 20:30–42"> Then they went in to offer sacrifices and burnt offerings. Now Jehu had chosen eighty men who were standing outside, and he told them, “If any of the men whom I bring into your hands escapes, whoever lets that man escape, his life will be taken for the life of the one who escaped.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 16:6–7; Ezekiel 22:21–22; 1 Kings 18:40"> So then as soon as Jehu finished offering the burnt offering, he said to the guard and to the captains, “Go in and kill them. Let no one come out.” So they killed them with the edge of the sword, and the guard and the captains threw them out and went into the inner room of the house of Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 14:23; 2 Kings 3:2; 2 Kings 19:18"> They dragged out the stone pillars that were in the house of Baal, and they burned them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Daniel 3:29; Ezra 6:11; Daniel 2:5"> Then they broke down the pillar of Baal, and destroyed the house of Baal and made it a latrine, which it is to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> That is how Jehu destroyed Baal worship from Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28–30; 1 Kings 14:16; 1 Kings 13:33–34"> But Jehu did not leave the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he made Israel sin—that is, the worship of the golden calves in Bethel and Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 10:35; 2 Kings 13:1; 2 Kings 13:10"> So Yahweh said to Jehu, “Because you have done well in executing what was right in my eyes, and have done to the house of Ahab according to all that was in my heart, your descendants will sit on the throne of Israel to the fourth generation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 10:29; Proverbs 4:23; Hebrews 2:1"> But Jehu took no care to walk in the law of Yahweh, the God of Israel, with all his heart. He did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam, by which he made Israel sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 1 Kings 19:17; 2 Kings 13:22"> In those days Yahweh began to cut off regions from Israel, and Hazael defeated the Israelites at the borders of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Amos 1:3–4; Deuteronomy 2:36; Joshua 13:9–12"> from the Jordan eastward, all the land of Gilead, the Gadites, and the Reubenites, and the Manassites, from Aroer, which is by the Valley of the Arnon, through Gilead to Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 11:41; 1 Kings 14:29; 1 Kings 14:19"> As for the other matters concerning Jehu, and all that he did, and all his power, are they not written in the book of the events of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 14:20; 1 Kings 14:31; 1 Kings 1:21"> Jehu slept with his ancestors, and they buried him in Samaria. Then Jehoahaz his son became king in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> The time that Jehu reigned over Israel in Samaria was twenty-eight years.</span>
</div>
