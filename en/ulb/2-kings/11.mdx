<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:1; 2 Kings 25:25; 2 Chronicles 22:10–12"> Now when Athaliah, the mother of Ahaziah, saw that her son was dead, she arose and killed all the royal children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:26; Jeremiah 35:2; Isaiah 7:6–7"> But Jehosheba, a daughter of King Jehoram and sister of Ahaziah, took Joash son of Ahaziah, and hid him away from among the king's sons who were killed, along with his nurse; she put them into a bedroom. They hid him from Athaliah so that he was not killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 12:8; 2 Chronicles 22:12; Malachi 3:15"> He remained with her six years, hidden in the house of Yahweh, while Athaliah reigned over the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 11:19; 2 Kings 11:9; 2 Kings 11:17"> In the seventh year, Jehoiada sent messages and brought the commanders of hundreds of the Carites and of the guard, and brought them to himself, into the temple of Yahweh. He made a covenant with them, and he made them swear an oath in the house of Yahweh. Then he showed them the king's son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; Ezekiel 44:2–3; 1 Chronicles 23:32"> He commanded them, saying, “This is what you must do. A third of you who come on the Sabbath will keep watch over the king's house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:4–5; 1 Chronicles 26:13–19"> and a third will be at the Sur Gate, and a third at the gate behind the guardhouse.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:6; 2 Kings 11:5"> The two other groups who are not serving on the Sabbath, you must keep the watch over the house of Yahweh for the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 27:17; 2 Chronicles 23:7; 1 Kings 2:28–31"> You must surround the king, every man with his weapons in his hand. Whoever enters within your ranks, let him be killed. You must stay with the king when he goes out, and when he comes in.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:8; 2 Kings 11:4; 1 Chronicles 26:26"> So the commanders of hundreds obeyed everything Jehoiada the priest commanded. Each one took his men, those who were to come in to serve on the Sabbath, and those who were to stop serving on that Sabbath; and they came to Jehoiada the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:7; 1 Chronicles 18:7; 2 Chronicles 5:1"> Then Jehoiada the priest gave the commanders of hundreds the spears and shields that belonged to King David and that were in the house of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 11:8; Ezekiel 8:16; 2 Kings 11:10"> So the guards stood, each man with his weapon in his hand, from the right side of the temple to the left side, near the altar and the temple, surrounding the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 25:16; 1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Kings 1:39"> Then Jehoiada brought out the king's son Joash, put the crown on him, and gave him the covenant decrees. Then they made him king and anointed him. They clapped their hands and said, “Long live the king!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:12–15"> When Athaliah heard the noise of the guard and of the people, she came to the people in the house of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:31; 2 Kings 23:3; 2 Kings 9:23"> She looked, and, behold, the king was standing by the pillar, as the custom was, and the captains and the trumpeters were by the king. All the people of the land were rejoicing and blowing trumpets. Then Athaliah tore her clothes and shouted, “Treason! Treason!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:7; 2 Chronicles 23:14; 2 Chronicles 23:9"> Then Jehoiada the priest ordered the commanders of hundreds who were over the army, saying, “Bring her out between the ranks. Anyone who follows her, kill him with the sword.” For the priest had said, “Do not let her be killed in the house of Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; 2 Chronicles 23:15; Matthew 7:2"> So they seized her as she reached the place where the horses enter the palace grounds, and there she was killed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:3; 2 Chronicles 15:12–14; 2 Chronicles 34:31"> Then Jehoiada made a covenant between Yahweh and the king and people, that they should be Yahweh's people, and also between the king and the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:3; 2 Kings 10:26; 1 Kings 18:40"> So all the people of the land went to the house of Baal and tore it down. They smashed Baal's altars and his idol figures to pieces, and they killed Mattan, the priest of Baal, in front of those altars. Then Jehoida the priest appointed guards over the temple of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:25; Jeremiah 22:4; 2 Chronicles 23:19"> Jehoida took with him the commanders of hundreds, the Carites, the guard, and all the people of the land, and together they brought down the king from the house of Yahweh and they went into the king's house, entering by way of the gate of the guards. Joash took his place on the royal throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 29:2; 2 Kings 11:14"> So all the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet after Athaliah had been killed with the sword at the king's house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:1–14; 2 Kings 22:1; 2 Kings 11:4"> Joash was seven years old when he began to reign.</span>
</div>
