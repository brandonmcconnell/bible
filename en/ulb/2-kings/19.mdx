<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:5–7; Isaiah 66:9; Hosea 13:13"> They said to him, “Hezekiah says, 'This day is a day of distress, rebuke, and disgrace, for the children have come to the time of birth, but there is no strength for them to be born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Romans 9:27; Joshua 14:12"> It may be that Yahweh your God will hear all the words of the chief commander, whom the king of Assyria his master has sent to defy the living God, and will rebuke the words which Yahweh your God has heard. <span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So the servants of King Hezekiah came to Isaiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 18:35; 2 Kings 18:17; Isaiah 37:6–38"> and Isaiah said to them, “Say to your master: 'Yahweh says, “Do not be afraid of the words that you have heard, with which the servants of the king of Assyria have insulted me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Obadiah 1:1; 2 Kings 7:6; Psalms 18:14–15"> Look, I will put a spirit in him, and he will hear a certain report and go back to his own land. I will cause him to fall by the sword in his own land.”'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 18:14; Joshua 10:29; Joshua 12:11"> Then the chief commander returned and found the king of Assyria fighting against Libnah, for he had heard that the king had gone away from Lachish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:27; Isaiah 37:9; 2 Kings 18:17"> Then Sennacherib heard that Tirhakah king of Cush and Egypt had mobilized to fight against him, so he sent messengers again to Hezekiah with a message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 18:5; 2 Kings 18:29–30; 2 Chronicles 32:15–19"> “Say to Hezekiah king of Judah, 'Do not let your God in whom you trust deceive you, saying, “Jerusalem will not be given over into the hand of the king of Assyria.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 10:8–11; 2 Chronicles 32:13–14; 2 Kings 19:17–18"> See, you have heard what the kings of Assyria have done to all lands by destroying them completely. So will you be rescued?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 17:6; Genesis 11:31; Ezekiel 27:23"> Have the gods of the nations rescued them, the nations that my fathers destroyed: Gozan, Haran, Rezeph, and the people of Eden in Tel Assar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 18:34; 2 Kings 17:24; Isaiah 11:11"> Where are the king of Hamath, the king of Arpad, the king of the cities of Sepharvaim, of Hena, and Ivvah?'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 37:14; Psalms 91:1–2; Psalms 123:1–4"> Hezekiah received this letter from the messengers and read it. Then he went up to the house of Yahweh and spread it before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; 2 Kings 5:15; Daniel 4:34–35"> Then Hezekiah prayed before Yahweh and said, “Yahweh of hosts, God of Israel, you who sit above the cherubim, you are God alone over all the kingdoms of the earth. You made the heavens and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:40; Psalms 31:2; 2 Kings 19:4"> Turn your ear, Yahweh, and listen. Open your eyes, Yahweh, and see, and hear the words of Sennacherib, which he has sent to mock the living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; Job 9:2; 1 Corinthians 14:25"> Truly, Yahweh, the kings of Assyria have destroyed the nations and their lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 17:29; Isaiah 44:9–20; Jeremiah 10:3–9"> They have put their gods into the fire, for they were not gods but the work of men's hands, just wood and stone. So the Assyrians have destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; 1 Kings 8:43; Psalms 67:1–2"> Now then, Yahweh our God, save us, I implore you, from his power, so that all the kingdoms of the earth may know that you, Yahweh, are God alone.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 20:5; 2 Chronicles 32:20–21; Jeremiah 33:3"> Then Isaiah son of Amoz sent a message to Hezekiah, saying, “Yahweh, the God of Israel says, 'Because you have prayed to me concerning Sennacherib king of Assyria, I have heard you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Lamentations 2:13; Job 16:4; Jeremiah 14:17"> This is the word that Yahweh has spoken about him:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The virgin daughter of Zion</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">despises you and laughs you to scorn.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The daughter of Jerusalem</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">shakes her head at you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Psalms 71:22; Isaiah 30:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whom have you defied and insulted?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Against whom have you exalted your voice</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and lifted up your eyes in pride?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Against the Holy One of Israel!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Psalms 20:7; Isaiah 10:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">By your messengers</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you have defied the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and have said,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">'With the multitude of my chariots</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have gone up to the heights of the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to the highest elevations of Lebanon.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will cut down the tall cedars</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the choice cypress trees there.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will enter into its farthest parts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">its most fruitful forest.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 19:6; Exodus 15:9; 2 Samuel 17:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have dug wells</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and have drunk foreign waters.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I dried up all the rivers of Egypt</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">under the soles of my feet.'</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Isaiah 10:5–6; Isaiah 37:26–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Have you not heard how I</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">determined it long ago,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and worked it out in ancient times?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Now I am bringing it to pass.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You are here to reduce impregnable cities</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">into heaps of ruins.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Psalms 102:11; Jeremiah 51:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their inhabitants, of little strength,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">are shattered and ashamed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are plants in the field,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">green grass,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the grass on the roof or in the field,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">burned before it has grown up.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 37:28–29; Jeremiah 23:23–24; Psalms 139:1–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I know your sitting down,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your going out, your coming in,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your raging against me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:4; Ezekiel 38:4; 2 Kings 19:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because of your raging against me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and because your arrogance has reached my ears,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will put my hook in your nose,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and my bit in your mouth;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will turn you back</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the way you came.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 25:20–22; 2 Kings 20:8–9; Exodus 3:12"> This will be the sign for you:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">This year you will eat what grows wild,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and in the second year what grows from that.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But in the third year you must plant and harvest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">plant vineyards and eat their fruit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 19:4; Isaiah 27:6; 2 Chronicles 32:22–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">The remnant of the house of Judah that survives</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will again take root and bear fruit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 9:7; Romans 11:5; Romans 9:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">For from Jerusalem a remnant will come out,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from Mount Zion survivors will come. The zeal of Yahweh of hosts will do this.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–10; 2 Samuel 20:15; Isaiah 37:33–35"> <span className="pl pl-2">Therefore Yahweh says this about the king of Assyria:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He will not come into this city</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor shoot an arrow here.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Neither will he come before it with shield</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or build up a siege ramp against it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; 2 Kings 19:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">The way by which he came</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will be the same way he will leave;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will not enter this city—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">this is Yahweh's declaration.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 11:12–13; 2 Kings 20:6; 1 Kings 15:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I will defend this city and rescue it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for my own sake and for my servant David's sake.'”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:21–22; 2 Samuel 24:16; Exodus 12:29–30"> It came about that night that the angel of Yahweh went out and attacked the camp of the Assyrians, putting to death 185,000 soldiers. When the men arose early in the morning, dead bodies lay everywhere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jonah 1:2; 2 Kings 19:7; 2 Kings 19:33"> So Sennacherib king of Assyria left Israel and went home and stayed in Nineveh.</span>

Then they escaped into the land of Ararat. Then Esarhaddon his son became king in his place.</span>
