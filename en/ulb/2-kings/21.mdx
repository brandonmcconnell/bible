<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 62:4; 2 Chronicles 32:33–9; Matthew 1:10"> Manasseh was twelve years old when he began to reign; he reigned fifty-five years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Hephzibah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:31; 2 Chronicles 36:14; 2 Chronicles 33:2–4"> He did what was evil in the sight of Yahweh, like the disgusting things of the nations whom Yahweh had driven out before the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 18:4; 2 Kings 17:16; Deuteronomy 17:3"> For he rebuilt the high places that Hezekiah his father had destroyed, and he built altars for Baal, made an Asherah pole, as Ahab king of Israel had done, and he bowed down to all the stars of heaven and worshiped them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 8:29; 2 Samuel 7:13; Jeremiah 32:34"> Manasseh built altars in the house of Yahweh, although Yahweh had commanded, “It is in Jerusalem that my name will be forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 7:12; 2 Kings 23:4; 2 Kings 23:12"> He built altars for all the stars of heaven in the two courtyards of the house of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 17:17; Leviticus 19:31; Leviticus 18:21"> He caused his son to pass through the fire, he performed sorcery and divination and consulted with those who talked with the dead and with those who talked with spirits. He did much evil in the sight of Yahweh, provoking him to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:16; 1 Kings 9:3; 2 Samuel 7:13"> The carved figure of Asherah that he had made, he placed it in the house of Yahweh. It was about this house that Yahweh had spoken to David and Solomon his son; he had said: “It is in this house and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen from all the tribes of Israel, that I will put my name forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:10; Deuteronomy 5:28–29; Psalms 81:11–16"> I will not cause the feet of Israel to wander any more out of the land that I gave to their ancestors, if they will only be careful to obey all that I have commanded them, and to follow all the law that my servant Moses commanded them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 29:12; Nehemiah 9:26; 1 Kings 14:16"> But the people did not listen, and Manasseh led them to do evil even more than the nations that Yahweh had destroyed before the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 23:34–37; 2 Chronicles 33:10; 2 Chronicles 36:15"> So Yahweh spoke by his servants the prophets, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 24:3–4; 1 Kings 21:26; 2 Kings 21:9"> “Because Manasseh king of Judah has done these disgusting things, and has acted wickedly more than all that the Amorites who were before him did, and has also made Judah sin with his idols,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:3; 1 Samuel 3:11; Daniel 9:12"> therefore Yahweh, the God of Israel, says this: Look, I am about to bring such evil on Jerusalem and Judah that whoever hears of it, both his ears will tingle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 7:7–8; Lamentations 2:8; Isaiah 34:11"> I will stretch over Jerusalem the measuring line used against Samaria, and the plumb line used against the house of Ahab; I will wipe Jerusalem clean, as a man wipes a dish, wiping it and turning it upside down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:33; 2 Kings 19:4; Psalms 89:38–45"> I will throw off the remnant of my inheritance and give them into the hand of their enemies. They will become victims and plunder for all their enemies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:30; Deuteronomy 31:27; Ezekiel 20:21"> because they have done what is evil in my sight, and have provoked me to anger, since the day their ancestors came out of Egypt, to this day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; 2 Chronicles 33:9; 2 Kings 21:11"> Moreover, Manasseh shed much innocent blood, until he had filled Jerusalem from one end to another with death. This was in addition to the sin by which he made Judah to sin, when they did what was evil in the sight of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 20:20–21; 2 Chronicles 33:1–20"> As for the other matters concerning Manasseh, all that he did, and the sin that he committed, are they not written in the book of the events of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:20; 2 Kings 21:26; 2 Chronicles 32:33"> Manasseh slept with his ancestors and was buried in the garden of his own house, in the garden of Uzza. Amon his son became king in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:21–23; 1 Kings 22:51; 1 Chronicles 3:14"> Amon was twenty-two years old when he began to reign; he reigned two years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Meshullemeth; she was the daughter of Haruz of Jotbah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 23:32; 2 Kings 21:11; Acts 7:51"> He did what was evil in the sight of Yahweh, as Manasseh his father had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Amon followed in all the way that his father had walked in and worshiped the idols that his father worshiped, and bowed down to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 22:17; 1 Kings 11:33; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> He abandoned Yahweh, the God of his fathers, and did not walk in the way of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 12:20; 2 Kings 14:19; 2 Chronicles 33:24–25"> The servants of Amon conspired against him and put the king to death in his own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 14:5; 2 Kings 11:17; 2 Kings 14:21"> But the people of the land killed all those who had conspired against King Amon, and they made Josiah his son king in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 20:20; 2 Kings 21:17"> As for the other matters concerning Amon that he did, are they not written in the book of the events of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 21:18; Matthew 1:10; 1 Kings 13:5"> The people buried him in his tomb in the garden of Uzza, and Josiah his son became king in his place.</span>
