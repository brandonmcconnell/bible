<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:25; 1 Kings 2:12; Genesis 39:2"> Solomon son of David was strengthened in his rule, and Yahweh his God was with him and made him very powerful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:1; 1 Chronicles 27:1; 2 Chronicles 30:2"> Solomon spoke to all Israel, to the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and to the judges, and to every prince in all Israel, the heads of the fathers' houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:29; 1 Chronicles 16:39; Leviticus 1:1"> So Solomon and all the assembly with him went to the high place that was at Gibeon, for there was the tent of meeting of God, which Moses the servant of Yahweh, had made in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:17; 1 Chronicles 15:25–28; 2 Samuel 6:2"> But David had brought up the ark of God from Kiriath Jearim to the place that he had prepared for it, for he had pitched a tent for it in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 31:2; Exodus 38:1–7; 1 Chronicles 2:19–20"> In addition, the bronze altar made by Bezalel son of Uri son of Hur was there before the tabernacle of Yahweh; Solomon and the assembly went to it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some older versions have, the bronze altar made by Bezalel son of Uri son of Hur, he placed there before the tabernacle of Yahweh , where he refers to David.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 3:4; 1 Kings 8:63; 1 Chronicles 29:21"> Solomon went up there to the bronze altar before Yahweh, which was at the tent of meeting, and offered one thousand burnt offerings on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; 1 John 5:14–15; John 16:23"> God appeared to Solomon that night and said to him, “Ask! What should I give you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:5; Isaiah 55:3; 2 Samuel 22:51–1"> Solomon said to God, “You have shown great covenant faithfulness to David my father, and have made me king in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12–16; Genesis 13:16; Psalms 132:11–12"> Now, Yahweh God, let your promise to David my father be carried out, for you have made me king over a people as numerous as the dust of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 3:9; Proverbs 4:7; Proverbs 2:2–6"> Now give me wisdom and knowledge, so that I may lead this people, for who can judge your people, who are so many in number?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 3:17; Proverbs 23:7; 1 Samuel 16:7"> God said to Solomon, “Because this was in your heart, and because you have not asked for riches, wealth, or honor, nor for the life of those who hate you, nor for long life for yourself, but you have asked for wisdom and knowledge for yourself, so that you might rule my people, over whom I have made you king, and this is what I will do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 1:5; 2 Chronicles 9:22; Matthew 6:33"> I will now give you wisdom and knowledge. I will also give you riches, wealth, and honor, as none of the kings had before you, and none after you will have.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:3; 1 Kings 4:24–25"> So Solomon came to Jerusalem from the high place that was at Gibeon, from before the tent of meeting; he reigned over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 4:26; 1 Kings 10:26–29; 2 Chronicles 9:25"> Solomon gathered chariots and horsemen, and he had 1,400 chariots and twelve thousand horsemen that he placed in the chariot cities, and with himself, the king in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:27; Isaiah 9:10; 1 Kings 10:27–29"> The king made silver and gold as common in Jerusalem as the stones, and he made cedar wood as common as the sycamore trees that are in the lowlands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:28; 1 Kings 10:28–29"> As for the importation of horses from Egypt and Kue for Solomon, his merchants bought them from Kue at a price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 10:29"> They imported a chariot from Egypt for six hundred shekels of silver, and a horse for 150 shekels. They also exported them to the kings of the Hittites and the Arameans.</span>
