<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 83:5–8; Isaiah 16:6; 2 Chronicles 32:1"> It came about after this, that the people of Moab and Ammon, and with them some Meunites came against Jehoshaphat to do battle.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The reading some Meunites represents a correction to the Hebrew text, which reads, some Ammonites . It is thought that Meunites was original, and that a copyist changed it to some Ammonites . But this last reading makes no sense, because Ammonites have already been mentioned in this verse. However, different versions deal with this problem in different ways.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 14:7; 1 Samuel 23:29; Joshua 15:62"> Then some came who told Jehoshaphat, saying, “A large multitude is coming against you from beyond the Dead Sea, from Edom. See, they are in Hazezon Tamar,” that is, Engedi.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Instead of Edom , some ancient and modern versions read, Aram .">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 9:3; 2 Chronicles 19:3; 1 Samuel 7:6"> Jehoshaphat became afraid and set himself to seek Yahweh. He proclaimed a fast throughout all Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 60:10–12; Psalms 69:35"> Judah gathered together to seek Yahweh; they came to seek Yahweh from all the cities of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 19:15–19; 2 Chronicles 6:12–13; 2 Chronicles 34:31"> Jehoshaphat stood in the assembly of Judah and Jerusalem, at the house of Yahweh, in front of the new courtyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; 1 Chronicles 29:11–12; Daniel 4:17"> He said, “Yahweh, the God of our ancestors, are you not God in heaven? Are you not the ruler over all the kingdoms of the nations? Power and might are in your hand, so no one is able to resist you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 41:8; James 2:23; Psalms 44:2"> Our God, did you not drive out the inhabitants of this land before your people Israel, and give it forever to the descendants of Abraham?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:10; 2 Chronicles 2:4"> They lived in it and built you a holy place in it for your name, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:28–30; 2 Chronicles 6:20; 1 Kings 8:33"> 'If disaster comes on us—the sword, judgment, or disease, or famine—we will stand before this house, and before you (for your name is in this house), and we will cry to you in our affliction, and you will hear us and save us.'<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Instead of judgment , some ancient and modern versions read flood .">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Deuteronomy 2:4–5; Deuteronomy 2:9"> See now, here are the people of Ammon, Moab, and Mount Seir whom you would not let Israel invade when they came out of the land of Egypt; instead, Israel turned away from them and did not destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 83:3–12; Judges 11:23–24; Jeremiah 18:20"> See how they are rewarding us; they are coming to drive us out of your land that you have given us to inherit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 121:1–2; Psalms 25:15; Psalms 141:8"> Our God, will you not judge them? For we have no power against this great army that is coming against us. We do not know what to do, but our eyes are on you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 10:1; Acts 21:5; Deuteronomy 29:10"> All Judah stood before Yahweh, with their little ones, wives, and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:1; 2 Chronicles 24:20; Acts 10:31"> Then in the middle of the assembly, the Spirit of Yahweh came on Jahaziel, son of Zechariah, son of Benaiah, son of Jeiel, son of Mattaniah, the Levite, one of the sons of Asaph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:6; Exodus 14:13–14"> Jahaziel said, “Listen, all Judah and you inhabitants of Jerusalem, and King Jehoshaphat. This is what Yahweh says to you, 'Do not fear; do not be discouraged because of this great army, for the battle does not belong to you, but to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> You must go down against them tomorrow. See, they are coming up by way of the pass of Ziz. You will find them at the end of the valley, before the wilderness of Jeruel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:13–14; 2 Chronicles 15:2; Isaiah 41:10"> You will not need to fight in this battle. Stand in your positions, stand still, and see the rescue of Yahweh with you, Judah and Jerusalem. Do not fear nor be discouraged. Go out against them tomorrow, for Yahweh is with you.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; 2 Chronicles 7:3; Genesis 24:26"> Jehoshaphat bowed his head with his face to the ground. All Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem fell down before Yahweh, worshiping him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:16–22; 1 Chronicles 16:42; 1 Chronicles 16:5"> The Levites, those of the descendants of the Kohathites and Korahites, stood up to praise Yahweh, the God of Israel, with a very loud voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 8:31; John 14:1; John 11:40"> Early in the morning they arose and went out into the wilderness of Tekoa. As they went out, Jehoshaphat stood and said, “Listen to me, Judah, and you inhabitants of Jerusalem! Trust in Yahweh your God, and you will be supported. Trust in his prophets, and you will succeed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:1; Psalms 106:1; 2 Chronicles 5:13"> After he consulted with the people, he appointed those who sang to Yahweh and who praised him for his majestic splendor as they went out before the army, saying, “Give thanks to Yahweh, for his covenant faithfulness endures forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:20; Isaiah 19:2; Ezekiel 38:21"> When they began to sing and to praise, Yahweh set men in ambush against the people of Ammon, Moab, and Mount Seir, who were coming against Judah. They were defeated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 1 Samuel 14:20; Ezekiel 35:2–3"> For the people of Ammon and Moab rose to fight the inhabitants of Mount Seir, in order to completely kill them and destroy them. When they had finished with the inhabitants of Mount Seir, they all helped to destroy each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 37:36; Psalms 110:6; 1 Chronicles 5:22"> When Judah came to a place overlooking the wilderness, they looked out on the army. Behold, they were dead, fallen to the ground; none had escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:19–20; 2 Kings 7:9–16; Numbers 31:15"> When Jehoshaphat and his people came to take plunder from them, they found among them abundant goods, clothing, and valuable articles, which they took for themselves, more than they could carry away. It took them three days to carry off the plunder, there was so much of it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Instead of among them , some ancient and modern versions read cattle . Instead of clothing , some ancient and modern versions read dead bodies .">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 17:15; Genesis 32:20; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> On the fourth day they assembled in the Valley of Berakah. There they praised Yahweh, so the name of that place is the “Valley of Berakah” to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:43; Hebrews 6:20; Psalms 30:1"> Then they returned, every man of Judah and Jerusalem, and Jehoshaphat in their lead, to go again to Jerusalem with joy, for Yahweh had made them rejoice over their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 14:2–3; Psalms 57:8; Psalms 92:3"> They came to Jerusalem and to the house of Yahweh with lyres, harps, and trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:10; 2 Kings 7:6; Exodus 23:27"> The terror of God was on all the kingdoms of the nations when they heard that Yahweh had fought against Israel's enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:15; 2 Chronicles 14:6–7; 2 Samuel 7:1"> So Jehoshaphat's kingdom was quiet, for his God gave him peace all around him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 22:41–44"> Jehoshaphat reigned over Judah: He was thirty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned in Jerusalem for twenty-five years. His mother's name was Azubah, the daughter of Shilhi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:2–5; 1 Kings 15:11; 2 Chronicles 14:11–13"> He walked in the ways of Asa, his father; he did not turn away from them; he did what was right in the eyes of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:6; 2 Chronicles 19:3; 2 Chronicles 12:14"> However, the high places were not taken away. The people still had not directed their hearts to the God of their ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 16:1; 2 Chronicles 19:2; 1 Kings 16:7"> As for the other matters concerning Jehoshaphat, first and last, behold, they are written in the history of Jehu son of Hanani, which is recorded in the book of the kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 22:48–49; 2 Kings 1:2–16"> After this Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, allied himself with Ahaziah, king of Israel, who committed much wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 10:22; 2 Chronicles 9:21; 1 Kings 9:26"> He allied himself with him to build ships to go to Tarshish. They built the ships at Ezion Geber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; 2 Chronicles 19:2; 2 Chronicles 9:21"> Then Eliezer son of Dodavahu of Mareshah, prophesied against Jehoshaphat; he said, “Because you have allied yourself with Ahaziah, Yahweh has destroyed your projects.” The ships were wrecked so that they were not able to sail.</span>
