<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 18:4; 2 Chronicles 32:12; Genesis 19:15"> Now when all this was finished, all the people of Israel who were there went out to the cities of Judah and broke to pieces the stone pillars and they cut down the Asherah poles, and they broke down the high places and the altars in all of Judah and Benjamin, and in Ephraim and Manasseh, until they had destroyed them all. Then all the people of Israel returned, every one to his own possession and his own city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:1; 1 Chronicles 16:4–6; 2 Chronicles 29:24–26"> Hezekiah assigned the divisions of the priests and the Levites organized by their divisions, each man assigned to his work, both the priests and the Levites. He assigned them to make the burnt offerings and fellowship offerings, to serve, to give thanks, and to praise at the gates of Yahweh's temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:17; 2 Chronicles 35:7; Colossians 2:16–17"> He also assigned the king's portion for the burnt offerings from his own possessions, that is, for the morning and evening burnt offerings, and the burnt offerings for the Sabbath days, the new moons, and the fixed festivals, as it was written in the law of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Malachi 2:7; Numbers 18:8–21; Nehemiah 13:10–13"> Moreover, he commanded the people who lived in Jerusalem to give the portion for the priests and the Levites, so that they might concentrate on obeying the law of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:12; 2 Chronicles 24:10–11; Nehemiah 10:35–39"> As soon as the command was sent out, the people of Israel generously gave the firstfruits of grain, new wine, oil, honey, and from all their harvest of the field. They brought in a tithe of everything, which was a great quantity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 27:30; 2 Chronicles 11:16–17; Deuteronomy 14:28"> The people of Israel and Judah who lived in the cities of Judah also brought in the tithe of cattle and sheep, and the tithe of the holy things that were set apart to Yahweh their God, and they piled them up in heaps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 23:16–24"> It was in the third month when they began piling up their contribution in heaps, and they finished in the seventh month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 1:3; Genesis 14:19–20; Philippians 4:10"> When Hezekiah and the leaders came and saw the heaps, they blessed Yahweh and his people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then Hezekiah questioned the priests and the Levites about the heaps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 3:10; 1 Chronicles 6:8; 2 Corinthians 9:8–11"> Azariah, the chief priest, of the house of Zadok, answered him and said, “Since the people began to bring the offerings into the house of Yahweh, we have eaten and had enough, and have plenty left over, for Yahweh has blessed his people. What was left over is this large amount here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:38–39; Nehemiah 13:5; Nehemiah 13:12–13"> Then Hezekiah commanded storerooms to be prepared in the house of Yahweh, and they prepared them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:9; 2 Kings 12:15; 1 Chronicles 26:20–26"> Then they faithfully brought in the offerings, the tithes and the things that belonged to Yahweh. Konaniah the Levite was the manager in charge of them, and his brother Shimei was second to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:5; 2 Chronicles 31:10–11; 1 Chronicles 9:11"> Jehiel, Azaziah, Nahath, Asahel, Jerimoth, Jozabad, Eliel, Ismakiah, Mahath, and Benaiah were managers under the hand of Konaniah and Shimei his brother, by appointment of Hezekiah, the king, and Azariah, the official over the house of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 23:38; 1 Chronicles 26:12; Leviticus 27:28"> Kore son of Imnah the Levite, the porter at the east gate, was over the free will offerings of God, in charge of distributing the offerings to Yahweh and the most holy offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 21:9–19; 1 Chronicles 9:22; 2 Chronicles 29:12"> Under him were Eden, Miniamin, Jeshua, Shemaiah, Amariah, and Shecaniah, in the cities of the priests. They filled offices of trust, in order to give these offerings to their brothers division by division, to both the important and the unimportant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezra 3:4; Leviticus 21:22–23; 1 Chronicles 23:3"> They also gave to those males three years old and up, who were listed in the records of their ancestors who entered the house of Yahweh, as required by the daily schedule, to do the work in their offices and their divisions.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Instead of males three years old and up , some modern versions have males thirty years old and up .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; 1 Chronicles 23:27; Numbers 3:15"> They distributed to the priests according to the records of their ancestors, and the same to the Levites twenty years old and more, according to their offices and their divisions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 15:16; Isaiah 5:16; 2 Chronicles 31:15"> They included all their little ones, their wives, their sons, and their daughters, through the whole community, for they were faithful in keeping themselves holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:12–15; Leviticus 25:34; Numbers 35:2–5"> For the priests, the descendants of Aaron, who were in the fields of the villages belonging to their cities, or in every city, there were men assigned by name to give portions to all the males among the priests, and to all who were listed in the records of their ancestors as being among the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 20:3; 2 Kings 22:2; Acts 24:16"> Hezekiah did this throughout all Judah. He accomplished what was good, right, and faithful before Yahweh, his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Jeremiah 29:13; Joshua 1:7–8"> In every project that he began in the service of the house of God, the law, and the commandments, to seek his God, he performed it with all his heart, and he succeeded.</span>
