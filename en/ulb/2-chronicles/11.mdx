<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 12:21–24; Psalms 33:10; Psalms 33:16"> When Rehoboam arrived in Jerusalem, he assembled the house of Judah and Benjamin, 180,000 chosen men who were soldiers, to fight against Israel, to restore the kingdom to Rehoboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:15; 2 Chronicles 12:5–7; 2 Chronicles 8:14"> But the word of Yahweh came to Shemaiah the man of God, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 17:34; Genesis 49:28; Revelation 7:4–8"> “Say to Rehoboam son of Solomon, king of Judah, and to all Israel in Judah and Benjamin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; 1 Kings 11:29–38; 2 Chronicles 10:15–16"> 'Yahweh says this, “You must not attack or make war against your brothers. Everyone must return to his own house, for I have caused this to happen.”'” So they obeyed the words of Yahweh and turned back from attacking Jeroboam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:2–6; 2 Chronicles 14:6–7; Isaiah 22:8–11"> Rehoboam lived in Jerusalem and built cities in Judah for defense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:5; Amos 1:1; 1 Chronicles 4:32"> He built Bethlehem, Etam, Tekoa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; 1 Samuel 22:1; Joshua 15:58"> Beth Zur, Soko, Adullam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 54:1; Joshua 15:24; 1 Samuel 23:19"> Gath, Mareshah, Ziph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 15:39; Joshua 15:35; Joshua 10:5"> Adoraim, Lachish, Azekah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Joshua 15:33; Joshua 14:14"> Zorah, Aijalon, and Hebron. These are fortified cities in Judah and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:19; Isaiah 22:10–11; 2 Chronicles 11:23"> He fortified the fortresses and put commanders in them, with stores of food, oil, and wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:22; 2 Chronicles 26:14–15; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> He put shields and spears in all the cities and made them very strong. So Judah and Benjamin belonged to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The priests and the Levites who were in all Israel went over to him from within their borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:9; Numbers 35:2–5; 1 Kings 12:28–33"> For the Levites left their pasturelands and property in order to come to Judah and Jerusalem, for Jeroboam and his sons had driven them away, so that they could no longer perform priestly duties for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; 1 Kings 12:31; 1 Kings 13:33"> Jeroboam appointed for himself priests for the high places and the goat and calf idols he had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:9; Acts 11:23; Psalms 84:5–7"> People from all the tribes of Israel came after them, those who set their hearts to seek Yahweh, the God of Israel; they came to Jerusalem to sacrifice to Yahweh, the God of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:1; 2 Chronicles 1:1–12; 2 Chronicles 7:17–19"> So they strengthened the kingdom of Judah and made Rehoboam son of Solomon strong during three years, and they walked for three years in the way of David and Solomon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:6; 1 Chronicles 27:18; 1 Samuel 17:13"> Rehoboam took a wife for himself: Mahalath, the daughter of Jerimoth, David's son, and of Abihail, the daughter of Eliab, Jesse's son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> She bore him sons: Jeush, Shemariah, and Zaham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:2; 2 Chronicles 11:21; 2 Chronicles 12:16"> After Mahalath, Rehoboam took Maacah, Absalom's daughter; she bore him Abijah, Attai, Ziza, and Shelomith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 8:30; Deuteronomy 17:17; 1 Kings 11:3"> Rehoboam loved Maacah, Absalom's daughter, more than all his other wives and his concubines (he took eighteen wives and sixty concubines, and became the father of twenty-eight sons and sixty daughters).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:15–17; 1 Chronicles 29:1; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2"> Rehoboam appointed Abijah son of Maacah to be chief, a leader among his brothers; he had the thought of making him king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:3; Genesis 25:6; Luke 16:8"> Rehoboam ruled wisely; he scattered all his sons throughout all the land of Judah and Benjamin to every fortified city. He also gave them food in abundance and looked for many wives for them.</span>
</div>
