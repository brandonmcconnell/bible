<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 3:5; Zechariah 13:1; Exodus 30:18–21"> He also made the round sea of cast metal, ten cubits from brim to brim. Its height was five cubits, and the sea was thirty cubits in circumference.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:10; Ezekiel 10:14; 1 Corinthians 9:9–10"> Under the brim encircling the sea were bulls, ten to each cubit, cast in one piece with the sea when the sea itself was cast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 28:19–20; Revelation 21:14; Mark 16:15"> The large basin known as “The Sea” was set upon twelve oxen, three looking toward the north, three looking toward the west, three looking toward the south, and three looking toward the east. “The Sea” was set on top of them, and all their hindquarters were toward the inside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 7:26"> “The Sea” was as thick as the width of a hand, and its brim was forged like the brim of a cup, like a lily blossom. “The Sea” held three thousand baths of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 7:38; Exodus 30:18–21; 1 Kings 7:40"> He made also ten basins for washing things; he put five on the south side, and five on the north; items used in performing the burnt offering were to be washed in them. The large basin known as “The Sea” was used by the priests for them to wash.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–40; 1 Kings 7:49; John 8:12"> He made the ten lampstands of gold that were made from the instructions for their design; he placed them in the temple, five on the right hand, and five on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 7:48; Malachi 1:12; Exodus 25:23–30"> He made ten tables and placed them in the temple, five on the right side, and five on the left. He made one hundred basins of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 6:36; 2 Kings 21:5; 1 Kings 7:12"> Furthermore he made the courtyard of the priests, and the great court and the doors for the court and overlaid their doors with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 7:39"> He placed the basin known as “The Sea” on the east side of the temple, facing toward the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 7:40–51; 1 Kings 7:14"> Huram made the pots, the shovels, and the sprinkling bowls.<br />So Huram finished the work that he did for King Solomon in the house of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 7:41; 2 Chronicles 3:15–17"> the two pillars, the bowl-like capitals that were on top of the two pillars, and the two sets of decorative latticework to cover the two bowl-like capitals that were on top of the pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 7:20; 1 Kings 7:42; Jeremiah 52:23"> He had made the four hundred pomegranates for the two sets of decorative latticework: two rows of pomegranates for each set of latticework to cover the two bowl-like capitals that were on the pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 7:27–43"> He also made the stands and the basins to go on the stands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> one sea and the twelve bulls under it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:13; 1 Kings 7:13–14; 2 Chronicles 4:11"> also the pots, shovels, meat forks, and all the other implements that Huram-Abi made of polished bronze for King Solomon, for the house of Yahweh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Instead of meat forks , some versions have bowels .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:46"> The king had cast them in the plain of the Jordan, in the clay ground between Succoth and Zarethan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:47; Jeremiah 52:20; 1 Chronicles 22:14"> Thus Solomon made all these vessels in great abundance; indeed, the weight of the bronze could not be known.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 9:13; Daniel 5:2–3; Exodus 25:23–30"> Solomon made all the furnishings that were in the house of God, the golden altar also, and the tables on which the bread of the presence was to be placed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:7; Exodus 25:31–37; 1 Kings 6:5"> the lampstands with their lamps, that were designed to burn before the inner room—these were made of pure gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 6:29; Exodus 37:20; 1 Kings 6:35"> and the flowers, the lamps, and the tongs, of gold, pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 12:13; 2 Kings 25:14; Jeremiah 52:18"> Also the lamp trimmers, basins, spoons, and incense burners were all made of pure gold. As for the entrance into the house, its inner doors into the most holy place and the doors of the house, that is, of the temple, were made of gold.</span>
