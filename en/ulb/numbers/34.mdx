<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh spoke to Moses and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Psalms 105:11; Deuteronomy 1:7–8"> “Command the people of Israel and say to them, 'When you enter the land of Canaan, the land that will belong to you, the land of Canaan and its borders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Ezekiel 47:13; Joshua 3:16"> your southern border will extend from the wilderness of Zin along the border of Edom. The eastern end of the southern border will be on a line that ends at the southern end of the Salt Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Joshua 15:3–4; Numbers 13:26"> Your border will turn south from the hill of Akrabbim and pass along through the wilderness of Zin. From there, it will run south of Kadesh Barnea and continue to Hazar Addar and further to Azmon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 15:4; Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 8:65"> From there, the border will turn from Azmon toward the brook of Egypt and follow it to the sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 1:4; Ezekiel 47:20; Ezekiel 47:15"> The western border will be the coastline of the Great Sea. This will be your western border.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:37; Numbers 34:6; Numbers 34:3"> Your northern border will extend along a line that you must mark out from the Great Sea to Mount Hor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; 2 Kings 14:25; Joshua 13:5–6"> then from Mount Hor to Lebo Hamath, then on to Zedad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:17"> Then the border will continue to Ziphron and end at Hazar Enan. This will be your northern border.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then you must mark out your eastern border from Hazar Enan south to Shepham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33; Deuteronomy 3:17; Joshua 11:2"> Then the eastern border will go down from Shepham to Riblah, on the east side of Ain. The border will continue along the east side of the Sea of Kinnereth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 34:3; Genesis 13:10; Genesis 19:24–26"> Then the border will continue south along the Jordan River to the Salt Sea and continue down the eastern border of the Salt Sea. This will be your land, following its borders all around.'”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 34:1; Joshua 14:1–5"> Then Moses commanded the people of Israel and said, “This is the land that you will receive by lot, which Yahweh has commanded to give to the nine tribes and to the half tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 32:33; Numbers 32:23; Joshua 14:2–3"> The tribe of the descendants of Reuben, following the assignment of property to their ancestor's tribe, and the tribe of the descendants of Gad, following the assignment of property to their ancestor's tribe, and the half tribe of Manasseh have all received their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 32:32"> The two tribes and the half tribe have received their share of land beyond the Jordan at Jericho eastward, toward the sunrise.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Yahweh spoke to Moses and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Joshua 19:51; Numbers 13:8"> “These are the names of the men who will divide the land for your inheritance: Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:4–16"> You must choose one leader from every tribe to divide the land for their clans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 26:65; Numbers 13:30; Numbers 14:24"> These are the names of the men:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">From the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">From the tribe of the descendants of Simeon, Shemuel son of Ammihud.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Psalms 68:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">From the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad son of Kislon.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <span className="pl pl-1">From of the tribe of the descendants of Dan a leader, Bukki son of Jogli.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">From the descendants of Joseph, of the tribe of the descendants of Manasseh a leader, Hanniel son of Ephod.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> <span className="pl pl-1">From the tribe of the descendants of Ephraim a leader, Kemuel son of Shiphtan.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> <span className="pl pl-1">From the tribe of the descendants of Zebulun a leader, Elizaphan son of Parnak.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> <span className="pl pl-1">From the tribe of the descendants of Issachar a leader, Paltiel son of Azzan.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> <span className="pl pl-1">From the tribe of the descendants of Asher a leader, Ahihud son of Shelomi.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> <span className="pl pl-1">From the tribe of the descendants of Naphtali a leader, Pedahel son of Ammihud.”</span></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:51; Numbers 34:18"> Yahweh commanded these men to divide the land of Canaan and to give each of the tribes of Israel their share.</span>
