<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 22:1; Numbers 31:12; Numbers 33:50"> Yahweh spoke to Moses on the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 14:3–4; Ezekiel 48:8; Joshua 21:2–42"> “Command the people of Israel to give some of their own shares of land to the Levites. They must give them cities to live in and pastureland surrounding those cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:14; Joshua 21:11; Ezekiel 45:2"> The Levites will have these cities to live in. The pastureland will be for their cattle, their flocks, and all their animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> The pasturelands around the cities that you will give to the Levites must extend from the city walls for one thousand cubits in every direction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> You must measure two thousand cubits from outside the city on the east side, and two thousand cubits to the south side, two thousand cubits to the west side, and two thousand cubits to the north side. This will be the pasturelands for their cities. The cities will be in the center.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 21:13; Joshua 21:3; Psalms 9:9"> Six of the cities that you will give to Levites must serve as cities of refuge. You must provide these as places to which a person who has killed someone can flee. Also provide forty-two other cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:3–42; 1 Chronicles 6:54–81"> The cities that you give to the Levites will total forty-eight. You must give their pasturelands with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 26:54; Numbers 33:54; Joshua 21:1–42"> The larger tribes of the people of Israel, the tribes that have more land, must provide more cities. The smaller tribes will provide fewer cities. Each tribe must provide for the Levites according to the share that it has received.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then Yahweh spoke to Moses and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:9; Deuteronomy 19:1–2; Leviticus 14:34"> “Speak to the people of Israel and say to them, 'When you cross over the Jordan into the land of Canaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:2; Numbers 35:6"> then you must choose cities to serve as cities of refuge for you, a place to which a person who has killed someone unintentionally may flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 20:3–6; Deuteronomy 19:6; Joshua 20:9"> These cities must be your refuge from the avenger, so that the accused man will not be killed without first standing trial before the community.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> You must choose six cities as cities of refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:8–10; Deuteronomy 4:41–43; Joshua 20:7–9"> You must provide three cities beyond the Jordan and three in the land of Canaan. They will be cities of refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 15:16; Galatians 3:28; Romans 3:29"> For the people of Israel, for the foreigners, for anyone living among you, these six cities will serve as a refuge to which anyone who kills someone unintentionally can flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17; Genesis 9:5–6; Deuteronomy 19:11–13"> But if an accused man has struck his victim with an instrument of iron, and if his victim dies, then the accused is indeed a murderer. He must certainly be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 21:18"> If an accused man has struck his victim with a stone in his hand that might kill the victim, and if his victim dies, then the accused is indeed a murderer. He must certainly be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> If an accused man has struck his victim with a wooden weapon that might kill the victim, and if the victim dies, then the accused is indeed a murderer. He must certainly be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 20:3; Deuteronomy 19:6; Deuteronomy 19:12"> The avenger of blood may put the murderer to death. When he meets him, the avenger of blood must put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 21:14; Deuteronomy 19:11; 2 Samuel 3:27"> If he strikes another in hatred or throws something at him, while hiding to ambush him, so that the victim dies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> or if he strikes him down in hatred with his hand so that the victim dies, then the accused who struck him must surely be put to death. He is a murderer. The avenger of blood may put the murderer to death when he meets him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 35:11; Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:3"> But if an accused man suddenly hits a victim without premeditated hate or throws something that hits the victim without lying in wait</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> or if he throws a stone that could kill a victim without seeing the victim, then the accused was not the victim's enemy; he was not trying to hurt the victim. But this is what to do if the victim dies anyway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 20:6; Numbers 35:12"> In that case, the community must judge between the accused and the avenger of blood on the basis of these rules.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 29:7; Leviticus 4:3; Romans 3:24–26"> The community must rescue the accused from the power of the avenger of blood. The community must return the accused to the city of refuge to which he had originally fled. He must live there until the death of the current high priest, the one who was anointed with the holy oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> But if the accused man at any time goes beyond the border of the city of refuge to which he fled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 22:2; Deuteronomy 19:10; Deuteronomy 19:6"> and if the avenger of blood finds him outside the border of his city of refuge, and if he kills the accused man, the avenger of blood will not be guilty of murder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 10:26–30; Hebrews 6:4–8"> This is because the accused man should have remained in his city of refuge until the death of the high priest. After the death of the high priest, the accused may return to the land where he has his own property.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 27:11; Numbers 27:1"> These laws must be statutes for you through all your people's generations in all the places where you live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28; Deuteronomy 19:15; Matthew 18:16"> Whoever kills any person, the murderer must be killed, as testified to by the words of witnesses. But one witness' word alone may not cause any person to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:11–13; Genesis 9:5–6; Psalms 51:14"> Also, you must not accept ransom for the life of a murderer who is guilty of murder. He must certainly be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 4:12; Galatians 3:10–13; Galatians 2:21"> You must not accept ransom for the one who has fled to a city of refuge. You must not in this way allow him to reside on his own property until the high priest dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 11:50–51; Psalms 106:38; Hosea 4:2–3"> Do not pollute in this way the land where you live, because blood from murder pollutes the land. No atonement can be made for the land when blood has been shed on it, except by the blood of the one who shed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 5:3; Psalms 135:21; Revelation 21:27"> So you must not defile the land in which you live because I am living in it. I, Yahweh, live among the people of Israel.'”</span>
</div>
