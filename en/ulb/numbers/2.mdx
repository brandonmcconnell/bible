<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh spoke again to Moses and Aaron. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:40; Numbers 10:22; Numbers 1:50"> “Each one of the Israelites must camp around his standard, with the banners of their fathers' houses. They will camp around the tent of meeting on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:10; Ruth 4:20; Luke 3:32–33"> Those will be camping on the east of the tent of meeting, where the sun rises, they are the camp of Judah and they are camping under their standard. Nahshon son of Amminadab is the leader of the people of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 26:22; Numbers 1:27"> The number of the people of Judah is 74,600.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 1:8; Numbers 7:18; Numbers 7:23"> The tribe of Issachar must camp next to Judah. Nethanel son of Zuar must lead the army of Issachar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 26:25; Numbers 1:29"> The number in his division is 54,400 men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 1:9; Numbers 7:24; Numbers 10:16"> The tribe of Zebulun must camp next to Issachar. Eliab son of Helon must lead the army of Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 26:26–27; Numbers 1:31"> The number in his division is 57,400.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 10:14"> All the number of the camp of Judah is 186,400. They will set out first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 1:5; Genesis 49:3–4; Numbers 7:35"> On the south side will be the camp of Reuben under their standard. The leader of the camp of Reuben is Elizur son of Shedeur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 26:7; Numbers 1:21"> The number in his division is 46,500.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 1:6; Numbers 10:19; Numbers 7:41"> Simeon is camping next to Reuben. The leader of the people of Simeon is Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 26:14; Numbers 1:23"> The number in his division is 59,300.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:14; Numbers 7:42; Numbers 7:47"> The tribe of Gad is next. The leader of the people of Gad is Eliasaph son of Deuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 26:18; Numbers 1:25"> The number in his division is 45,650.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 10:18; Numbers 2:9; Numbers 2:24"> The number of all the men assigned to the camp of Reuben, according to their divisions, is 151,450. They will set out second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 10:21; Numbers 10:17; Numbers 3:38"> Next, the tent of meeting must go out from the camp with the Levites in the middle of all the camps. They must go out from the camp in the same order as they come into the camp. Every man must be in his place, by his banner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:10; Numbers 10:22; Genesis 48:14–20"> The divisions of the camp of Ephraim under their standard. The leader of the people of Ephraim is Elishama son of Ammihud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 1:33; Numbers 26:37"> The number in his division is 40,500.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 1:10; Numbers 7:54; Numbers 10:23"> Next to them is the tribe of Manasseh. The leader of Manasseh is Gamaliel son of Pedahzur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 26:34; Numbers 1:35"> The number in his division is 32,200.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 1:11; Numbers 7:60; Numbers 10:24"> Next will be the tribe of Benjamin. The leader of Benjamin is Abidan son of Gideoni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:37; Numbers 26:41"> The number in his division is 35,400.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 10:22; Numbers 2:31; Psalms 80:2"> All those numbered in the camp of Ephraim is 108,100. They will set out third.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 1:12; Numbers 7:66; Numbers 10:25"> On the north will be the divisions of the camp of Dan. The leader of the people of Dan is Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 26:43; Numbers 1:39"> The number in his division is 62,700.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 1:13; Numbers 7:77; Numbers 7:72"> The people of the tribe of Asher camp next to Dan. The leader of Asher is Pagiel son of Okran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 1:41; Numbers 26:47"> The number in his division is 41,500.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 1:15; Numbers 10:27; Numbers 26:48–50"> The tribe of Naphtali is next. The leader of Naphthali is Ahira son of Enan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 1:42–43; Numbers 26:50"> The mnumber in his division is 53,400.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 10:25; Numbers 2:16; Numbers 2:9"> All those numbered in the camp with Dan is 157,600. They will go out from the camp last, under their banner.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Exodus 38:26; Numbers 2:9"> These are the Israelites, numbered according to their families. All those counted in their camps, by their divisions, are 603,550.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 26:57–62; Numbers 1:47–49"> But Moses and Aaron did not count the Levites among the people of Israel. This was as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 119:6; Numbers 24:2; Numbers 24:5–6"> The people of Israel did everything that Yahweh commanded Moses. They camped by their banners. They went out from the camp by their clans, in the order of their ancestor's families.</span>
