<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 21:32; Numbers 32:3; Genesis 13:10–11"> Now the descendants of Reuben and of Gad had large numbers of livestock. When they saw the land of Jazer and Gilead, the land was a wonderful place for livestock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> So the descendants of Gad and Reuben came and spoke to Moses, to Eleazar the priest, and to the leaders of the community. They said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:34; Isaiah 16:8–9; Numbers 32:34–38"> “This is a list of places we have surveyed: Ataroth, Dibon, Jazer, Nimrah, Heshbon, Elealeh, Sebam, Nebo, and Beon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 21:34; Deuteronomy 2:24–35; Numbers 21:24"> These are the lands that Yahweh attacked before the community of Israel, and they are good places for livestock. We, your servants, have a lot of livestock.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ruth 2:10; Joshua 7:7; Jeremiah 31:2"> They said, “If we have found favor in your eyes, let this land be given to us, your servants, as a possession. Do not make us cross over the Jordan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:5; 2 Samuel 11:11; Philippians 2:4"> Moses replied to the descendants of Gad and Reuben, “Should your brothers go to war while you settle down here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 13:27–4; Numbers 32:9; Deuteronomy 1:28"> Why discourage the hearts of the people of Israel from going over into the land that Yahweh has given them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:19–25; Numbers 14:2; Numbers 13:2–26"> Your fathers did the same thing when I sent them from Kadesh Barnea to examine the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 13:23–10; Deuteronomy 1:24–28"> They went up to the Valley of Eshkol. They saw the land and then discouraged the hearts of the people of Israel so that they refused to enter the land that Yahweh had given them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; Psalms 95:11; Ezekiel 20:15"> Yahweh's anger was kindled on that day. He took an oath and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 14:28–30; Numbers 14:24; Numbers 26:2"> 'Surely none of the men who came up out of Egypt, from twenty years old and up, will see the land about which I swore to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, because they have not completely followed me, except for</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:36; Numbers 14:30; Numbers 14:24"> Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite, and Joshua son of Nun. Only Caleb and Joshua have completely followed me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5; Numbers 14:33–35; Hebrews 3:16–19"> So Yahweh's anger was kindled against Israel. He made them wander around in the wilderness for forty years until all the generation who had done evil in his sight was destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 11:48; Psalms 78:57; Matthew 23:31–33"> Look, you have risen up in your fathers' place, like just more sinful men, to add to Yahweh's burning anger toward Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:11–12; 2 Chronicles 15:2; 2 Chronicles 7:19–22"> If you turn away from following him, he will again leave Israel in the wilderness and you will have destroyed all this people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 33:17; Numbers 34:22"> So they came near Moses and said, “Allow us to build fences here for our cattle and cities for our families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 4:12–13; Numbers 32:29–32; Deuteronomy 3:18–20"> However, we ourselves will be ready and armed to go with Israel's army until we have led them into their place. But our families will live in the fortified cities because of the other people who still live in this land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 22:1–5"> We will not return to our houses until every one of the people of Israel has obtained his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 13:8; Joshua 12:1–6; Numbers 34:15"> We will not inherit the land with them on the other side of the Jordan, because our inheritance is here on the east side of the Jordan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 4:12–13; Deuteronomy 3:18–20; Joshua 1:13–15"> So Moses replied to them, “If you do what you say, if you arm yourselves to go before Yahweh to war,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> then every one of your armed men must cross over the Jordan before Yahweh, until he has driven out his enemies from before him</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 22:9; Joshua 22:4; Deuteronomy 3:20"> and the land is subdued before him. Then afterward you may return. You will be guiltless toward Yahweh and toward Israel. This land will be your possession before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 59:12; Proverbs 13:21; Genesis 44:16"> But if you do not do so, look, you will have sinned against Yahweh. Be sure that your sin will find you out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 32:16; Numbers 30:2; Numbers 32:34–42"> Build cities for your families and pens for your sheep; then do what you have said.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 1:13–14"> The descendants of Gad and Reuben spoke to Moses and said, “Your servants will do as you, our master, commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 1:14"> Our little ones, our wives, our flocks, and all our livestock will stay there in the cities of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 4:12; Numbers 32:17; Numbers 12:11"> However, we, your servants, will cross over before Yahweh to battle, every man who is armed for war, as you, our master, say.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 1:13"> So Moses gave instructions concerning them to Eleazar the priest, to Joshua son of Nun, and to the leaders of the ancestor's clans in the tribes of the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Moses said to them, “If the descendants of Gad and Reuben cross over the Jordan with you, every man who is armed to battle before Yahweh, and if the land is subdued before you, then you will give them the land of Gilead as a possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 22:19"> But if they do not cross over with you armed, then they will acquire their possessions among you in the land of Canaan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> So the descendants of Gad and Reuben answered and said, “As Yahweh has said to us, your servants, this is what we will do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> We will cross over armed before Yahweh into the land of Canaan, but our possessed inheritance will remain with us on this side of the Jordan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 12:6; Deuteronomy 29:8; Deuteronomy 3:12–17"> So to the descendants of Gad and Reuben, and also to the half tribe of Manasseh son of Joseph, Moses gave the kingdom of Sihon, king of the Amorites, and of Og, king of Bashan. He gave to them the land, and distributed to them all its cities with their borders, the cities of the land around them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36; Numbers 32:3; Isaiah 17:2"> The descendants of Gad rebuilt Dibon, Ataroth, Aroer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 32:1; Numbers 32:3"> Atroth Shophan, Jazer, Jogbehah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 32:3"> Beth Nimrah, and Beth Haran as fortified cities with pens for sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 21:27; Numbers 32:3; Isaiah 15:4"> The descendants of Reuben rebuilt Heshbon, Elealeh, Kiriathaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 46:1; Numbers 32:3; Joshua 23:7"> Nebo, Baal Meon—their names were later changed, and Sibmah. They gave other names to the cities that they rebuilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 50:23; Joshua 17:1; Numbers 26:29"> The descendants of Machir son of Manasseh went to Gilead and took it away from the Amorites who were in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Joshua 17:1; Joshua 13:29–31; Deuteronomy 3:13–15"> Then Moses gave Gilead to Machir son of Manasseh, and his people settled there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:14; Judges 10:4; Joshua 13:30"> Jair son of Manasseh went and captured its towns and called them Havvoth Jair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 49:11; 2 Samuel 18:18"> Nobah went and captured Kenath and its villages, and he called it Nobah, after his own name.</span>
