<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 22:31; Numbers 24:16; Numbers 23:7"> He received this prophecy and said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Balaam son of Beor is about to speak,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the man whose eyes are wide open.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> <span className="pl pl-1">How beautiful are your tents, Jacob,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the place where you live, Israel!</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 45:5; Psalms 2:9; Numbers 14:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">God brings him out of Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with strength like a wild ox.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He will eat up the nations who fight against him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He will break their bones to pieces.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He will shoot them with his arrows.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Genesis 27:29; Genesis 49:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">He crouches down like a lion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like a lioness. Who dares disturb him?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May everyone who blesses him be blessed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may everyone who curses him be cursed.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 23:11; Ezekiel 21:14; Ezekiel 21:17"> Balak's anger was kindled against Balaam and he struck his hands together in anger. Balak said to Balaam, “I called you to curse my enemies, but look, you have blessed them three times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 22:17; Numbers 22:37; Hebrews 11:24–26"> So leave me right now and go home. I said I would greatly reward you, but Yahweh has kept you from getting any reward.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:18; Numbers 22:38"> Then Balaam replied to Balak, “I said to the messengers that you sent to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 16:28; Numbers 22:18; Numbers 22:20"> 'Even if Balak gave me his palace full of silver and gold, I could not go beyond Yahweh's word and anything bad or good, or anything at all that I might want to do. I can say only what Yahweh tells me to say.' Did I not say this to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 6:5; Revelation 2:14; Genesis 49:1"> So now, look, I will go back to my people. But first let me warn you what this people will do to your people in the days ahead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 24:3–4; Numbers 23:7; Matthew 13:35"> Balaam began this prophecy. He said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Balaam son of Beor speaks,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The man whose eyes are wide open.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Luke 1:32–33; Genesis 49:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I see him, but he is not here now.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I look at him, but he is not near.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">A star will come out of Jacob,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a scepter will rise out of Israel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will shatter Moab's leaders</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and destroy all the descendants of Seth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 9:12; Genesis 27:29; 2 Samuel 8:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then Edom will become a possession of Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Seir will also become their possession,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">enemies of Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whom Israel will conquer with force.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 49:10; Micah 5:2; Psalms 2:1–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Out of Jacob a king will come who will have dominion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he will destroy the survivors of their city.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 17:14; Judges 6:3; 1 Chronicles 4:43"> Then Balaam looked at Amalek and began his prophecy. He said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Amalek was once the greatest of nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but his final end will be destruction.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:19; Judges 1:16; Job 29:18"> Then Balaam looked toward the Kenites and began his prophecy. He said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The place where you live is strong,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your nest is in the rocks.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 83:8; Genesis 10:22; Ezra 4:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Nevertheless you Kenites will be consumed by fire</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when Assyria carries you away captive.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 23:23; Malachi 3:2; 2 Kings 5:1"> Then Balaam began his final prophecy. He said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Woe! Who will survive when God does this?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Numbers 24:20; Daniel 10:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ships will come from the coast of Kittim;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will attack Assyria and will conquer Eber,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they, too, will end in destruction.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Joshua 13:22; Numbers 24:11"> Then Balaam got up and left. He returned to his home, and Balak also went away.</span>
