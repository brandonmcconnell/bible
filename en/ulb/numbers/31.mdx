<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh spoke to Moses and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 27:13; Numbers 25:14–18; Revelation 6:10"> “Take vengeance on the Midianites for what they did to the Israelites. After doing that, you will die and be gathered to your people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Numbers 25:11; 2 Kings 10:30"> So Moses spoke to the people. He said, “Arm some of your men for war so they may go against Midian and carry out Yahweh's vengeance on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:8; 1 Samuel 14:6; Judges 7:2"> Every tribe throughout Israel must send a thousand soldiers to war.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So out of Israel's thousands of men, one thousand was provided from each tribe, twelve thousand men armed for war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:44; Numbers 10:8–9; 2 Chronicles 13:12–15"> Then Moses sent them to battle, a thousand from every tribe, along with Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest, and with some articles from the holy place and the trumpets in his possession for sounding signals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 21:11; 1 Kings 11:15–16; 1 Samuel 27:9"> They fought against Midian, as Yahweh had commanded Moses. They killed every man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:21–22; Numbers 25:15; Revelation 2:14"> They killed the kings of Midian with the rest of their dead: Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba, the five kings of Midian. They also killed Balaam son of Beor, with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; 2 Chronicles 28:5"> The army of Israel took captive the women of Midian, their children, all their cattle, all their flocks, and all their goods. They took these as plunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; 1 Samuel 30:1; Joshua 6:24"> They burned all their cities where they lived and all their camps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 8:2"> They took all the plunder and prisoners, both people and animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:1"> They brought the prisoners, the plunder, and the captured things to Moses, to Eleazar the priest, and to the community of the people of Israel. They brought these to the camp in the plains of Moab, by the Jordan near Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; 1 Samuel 30:21; Numbers 31:12"> Moses, Eleazar the priest, and all the leaders of the community went to meet them outside the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 13:19; 1 Samuel 15:13–14; Numbers 31:48"> But Moses was angry with the officers of the army, the commanders of thousands and the captains of hundreds, who came from battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Jeremiah 48:10; Deuteronomy 2:34"> Moses said to them, “Have you let all the women live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 2 Peter 2:15; Revelation 2:14"> Look, these women caused the people of Israel, through Balaam's advice, to commit sin against Yahweh in the matter of Peor, when the plague spread among Yahweh's community.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 21:11–12; Deuteronomy 20:16–18; Deuteronomy 7:2"> Now then, kill every male among the little ones, and kill every woman who has ever slept with a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:10–14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; Deuteronomy 20:14"> But take for yourselves all the young girls who have never slept with a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 19:11–22; Numbers 5:2; 1 Chronicles 22:8"> You must camp outside the camp of Israel for seven days. All of you who have killed anyone and or have touched any dead person—you must purify yourselves on the third day and on the seventh day—you and your prisoners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 19:22; Genesis 35:2; Exodus 19:10"> You must purify every garment and everything made of animal hide and goats' hair, and everything made of wood.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 30:16"> Eleazar the priest said to the soldiers who had gone to war, “This is a decreed law that Yahweh has given to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The gold, silver, bronze, iron, tin, and lead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 19:9; Numbers 19:17; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> and everything that resists fire, you must put it through the fire, and it will become clean. You must then purify those things with the water of cleansing. Whatever cannot go through the fire you must cleanse with that water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 15:13; Leviticus 14:9"> You must wash your clothes on the seventh day, and then you will become clean. Afterward you may come into Israel's camp.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Then Yahweh spoke to Moses and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> “Count all the plundered things that were taken, both people and animals. You, Eleazar the priest, and the leaders of the community's ancestor's clans</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 22:8; Psalms 68:12; 1 Samuel 30:24–25"> must divide the plunder into two parts. Divide it between the soldiers who went out to battle and all the rest of the community.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 18:26; Numbers 31:30; Numbers 31:47"> Then levy a tax to be given to me from the soldiers who went out to battle. This tax must be one out of every five hundred, whether persons, cattle, donkeys, sheep, or goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 29:27; Deuteronomy 12:19; Deuteronomy 12:12"> Take this tax from their half and give it to Eleazar the priest for an offering to be presented to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 3:7–8; Numbers 3:25"> Also from the people of Israel's half, you must take one out of every fifty—from the persons, cattle, donkeys, sheep, and goats. Give these to the Levites who take care of my tabernacle.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> So Moses and Eleazar the priest did as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Now the plunder that remained of what the soldiers had taken was 675,000 sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> seventy-two thousand oxen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> sixty-one thousand donkeys,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and thirty-two thousand women who had never slept with any man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> The half that was kept for the soldiers numbered 337,000 sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Yahweh's part of the sheep was 675.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> The oxen were thirty-six thousand which Yahweh's tax was seventy-two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> The donkeys were 30,500 from which Yahweh's part was sixty-one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> The persons were sixteen thousand women of whom Yahweh's tax was thirty-two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 18:8; Numbers 18:19; Hebrews 7:9–12"> Moses took the tax that was to be an offering presented to Yahweh. He gave it to Eleazar the priest, as Yahweh commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> As for the people of Israel's half that Moses had taken from the soldiers who had gone to war—</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> the community's half was 337,500 sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> thirty-six thousand oxen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> 30,500 donkeys,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> and sixteen thousand women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 31:30; Luke 10:1–8; Isaiah 56:10–11"> From the people of Israel's half, Moses took one out of every fifty, both of people and animals. He gave them to the Levites who kept care of Yahweh's tabernacle, as Yahweh had commanded him to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> Then the officers of the army, the commanders over thousands and the captains over hundreds, came to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 18:9; 1 Samuel 30:18–19; Psalms 72:14"> They said to him, “Your servants have counted the soldiers who are under our command, and not one man is missing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 30:12; Exodus 30:15–16; Psalms 116:12"> We have brought Yahweh's offering, what each man found, articles of gold, armlets and bracelets, signet rings, earrings, and necklaces, to make atonement for ourselves before Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 7:2–6"> Moses and Eleazar the priest received from them the gold and all the articles of craftsmanship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> All the gold of the offering that they gave to Yahweh—the offerings from the commanders of thousands and from the captains of hundreds—weighed 16,750 shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:32"> Each soldier had taken plunder, each man for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 30:16; Zechariah 6:14; Acts 10:4"> Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold from the commanders of thousands and captains of hundreds. They took it into the tent of meeting as a reminder of the people of Israel for Yahweh.</span>
</div>
