<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 23:24–25; 1 Chronicles 15:28; Isaiah 27:13"> “In the seventh month, on the first day of the month, you must have a holy assembly to honor Yahweh. You must not do regular work on that day. It will be a day when you blow trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 28:27; Numbers 28:19; Numbers 29:36"> You must offer a burnt offering to produce a sweet aroma for Yahweh. You must offer one young bull, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old, each without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> You must offer with them their grain offering, fine flour mixed with oil, three-tenths of an ephah for the bull, two-tenths for the ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and one-tenth for each lamb of the seven lambs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 28:30; Numbers 28:15; Numbers 28:22"> You must offer one male goat as a sin offering to make atonement for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 28:3–8; Numbers 28:11–15; Numbers 29:18"> Make these offerings in the seventh month in addition to all of the offerings you will make on the first of each month: the special burnt offering and the grain offering to go with it. These must be in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offerings. As you make these offerings, you will obey what has been decreed to produce a sweet aroma, an offering made by fire to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 5:4; Leviticus 16:29–31; Zechariah 12:10"> On the tenth day of the seventh month you must have a holy assembly to honor Yahweh. You must humble yourselves and do no work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 28:19; Numbers 29:13; Numbers 29:2"> You must offer a burnt offering to produce a sweet aroma for Yahweh. You must offer one young bull, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old. They must each be without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 15:3–12"> You must offer with them a grain offering, fine flour mixed with oil, three-tenths of an ephah for the bull, two-tenths for the one ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and a tenth of an ephah for each of the seven lambs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 16:3; Leviticus 16:5; Numbers 28:3–8"> You must offer one male goat as a sin offering. This will be in addition to the sin offering of atonement, the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and their drink offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:13–14; Zechariah 14:16–19; Exodus 34:22"> On the fifteenth day of the seventh month you must have a holy assembly to honor Yahweh. You must not do regular work on that day, and you must keep the festival for him seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 28:11; Ezra 3:4; Numbers 29:2"> You must offer a burnt offering, a sacrifice made by fire to produce a sweet aroma for Yahweh. You must offer thirteen young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old. Each must be without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> You must offer with them a grain offering, fine flour mixed with oil, three-tenths of an ephah for every bull of the thirteen bulls, two-tenths for each ram of the two rams,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and a tenth of an ephah for each of the fourteen lambs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> You must offer one male goat as a sin offering in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and the drink offering with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 8:13; Psalms 50:8–9; Psalms 69:31"> On the second day of the assembly, you must offer twelve young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, each without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 28:7; Numbers 15:4–12; Numbers 28:14"> You must make with them a grain offering and the drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, making as many offerings as were commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 29:11; Numbers 29:25; Numbers 29:22"> You must offer one male goat as a sin offering in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and their drink offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> On the third day of the assembly, you must offer eleven bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, each without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> You must make with them a grain offering and the drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, making as many offerings as were commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joel 1:13; Psalms 16:4; Joel 2:14"> You must offer one male goat as a sin offering in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and their drink offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> On the fourth day of the assembly, you must offer ten bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, each without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> You must make with them a grain offering and the drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, making as many offerings as were commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 13:43; Galatians 2:5; Galatians 6:9"> You must offer one male goat as a sin offering in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and their drink offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> On the fifth day of the assembly, you must offer nine bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, each without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> You must make with them a grain offering and the drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, making as many offerings as were commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> You must offer one male goat as a sin offering in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and their drink offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> On the sixth day of the assembly, you must offer eight bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, each without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> You must make with them a grain offering and the drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, making as many offerings as were commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> You must offer one male goat as a sin offering in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and their drink offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> On the seventh day of the assembly, you must offer seven bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, each without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> You must make with them a grain offering and the drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, making as many offerings as were commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> You must offer one male goat as a sin offering in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and their drink offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 23:36; John 7:37–39; Revelation 7:9–17"> On the eighth day you must have another solemn assembly. You must not do regular work on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> You must make a burnt offering, an offering made by fire to produce a sweet aroma for Yahweh. You must offer one bull, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old, each without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> You must offer their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bull, for the ram, and for the lambs, making as many offerings as were commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> You must offer one male goat as a sin offering in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and their drink offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:3; Leviticus 23:2; 1 Chronicles 23:31"> These are what you must offer to Yahweh at your fixed festivals. These must be in addition to your vows and freewill offerings. You must offer these as your burnt offerings, grain offerings, drink offerings, and fellowship offerings.”</span>
