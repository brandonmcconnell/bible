<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 1:1; Exodus 40:2"> Yahweh spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the first month of the second year after they came out from the land of Egypt. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–2; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; 2 Chronicles 35:1"> “Let the people of Israel keep the Passover at its fixed time of year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 12:6–11; Leviticus 23:5; 2 Chronicles 30:2"> On the fourteenth day of this month, at evening, you must keep the Passover at its fixed time of year. You must keep it, follow all the regulations, and obey all the decrees that are related to it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> So, Moses told the people of Israel that they should keep the Festival of the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:10; Matthew 28:20; Exodus 12:1–13"> So they kept the Passover in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, at evening, in the wilderness of Sinai. The people of Israel obeyed everything that Yahweh commanded Moses to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 27:2; Exodus 18:15; Numbers 5:2"> There were certain men who became unclean by the body of a dead man. They could not keep the Passover on that day. They went before Moses and Aaron on that same day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:2; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Exodus 12:27"> Those men said to Moses, “We are unclean because of the dead body of a man. Why do you keep us from offering the sacrifice to Yahweh at the fixed time of year among the people of Israel?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 85:8; Numbers 27:5; 1 Corinthians 4:4"> Moses said to them, “Wait for me to hear what Yahweh will instruct about you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Yahweh spoke to Moses. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–2; Ephesians 2:12–13; 1 Corinthians 11:28"> “Speak to the people of Israel. Say, 'If any of you or your descendants are unclean because of a dead body, or are on a long journey, he may still keep the Passover to Yahweh.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:2–15; Exodus 12:43–49; John 19:36"> In the second month on the fourteenth day at evening, they will eat the Passover meal. They must eat the Passover lamb with bread that is made without yeast and with bitter herbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 19:36; Exodus 12:46; Exodus 12:10"> They must not leave any of it until the morning, or break any of its bones. They must follow all the regulations for the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 17:14; Exodus 12:15; Numbers 9:7"> But any person who is clean and is not on a journey, but who fails to keep the Passover, that person must be cut off from his people because he did not offer the sacrifice that Yahweh requires at the fixed time of year. That man must carry his sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 12:48–49; Leviticus 24:22; Isaiah 56:3–7"> If a stranger lives among you and keeps the Passover in Yahweh's honor, he must keep it and do all he commands, keeping the rules of the Passover, and obeying the laws for it. You must have the same law for the foreigner and for all who have been born in the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Exodus 40:34; Nehemiah 9:19"> On the day that the tabernacle was set up, the cloud covered the tabernacle, the tent of the covenant decrees. At evening the cloud was over the tabernacle. It appeared like fire until morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Exodus 40:38; 1 Corinthians 10:1"> It continued that way. The cloud covered the tabernacle and appeared like fire at night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 40:36–38; Psalms 32:8; John 10:9"> Whenever the cloud was taken up from over the tent, the people of Israel would set out on their journey. Wherever the cloud stopped, the people would camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:1; 2 John 1:6; Exodus 17:1"> At Yahweh's command, the people of Israel would travel, and at his command, they would camp. While the cloud stopped over the tabernacle, they would stay in their camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 3:7; Numbers 1:52–53; Numbers 3:8"> When the cloud remained on the tabernacle for many days, then the people of Israel would obey Yahweh's instructions and not travel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Sometimes the cloud remained a few days on the tabernacle. In that case, they would obey Yahweh's command—they would make camp and then travel on again at his command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:19; Nehemiah 9:12"> Sometimes the cloud was present in camp from evening until morning. When the cloud lifted in the morning, they journeyed. If it continued for a day and for a night, only when the cloud lifted would they journey on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Exodus 40:36–37; Exodus 40:16"> Whether the cloud stayed on the tabernacle for two days, a month, or a year, for as long as it stayed there, the people of Israel would stay in their camp and not travel. But whenever the cloud was taken up, they would set out on their journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 22:3; Numbers 9:19; Genesis 26:5"> They would camp at Yahweh's command, and they would travel at his command. They obeyed Yahweh's command given through Moses.</span>
