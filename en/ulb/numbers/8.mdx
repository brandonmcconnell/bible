<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh spoke to Moses. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 25:37; Exodus 40:25; Leviticus 24:1–2"> “Speak to Aaron. Say to him, 'The seven lamps must give light in front of the lampstand when you light them.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Aaron did this. He lit the lamps on the lampstand to give light toward the front of it, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–40; Exodus 25:9; Exodus 25:18"> The lampstand was made in this way and Yahweh showed Moses the pattern for it. It was to be hammered gold from its base to its top, with hammered cups like blossoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Again, Yahweh spoke to Moses. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; James 4:8; Exodus 19:15"> “Take the Levites from among the people of Israel and purify them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 19:13; Numbers 19:7–10; Leviticus 8:6"> Do this to them to purify them: Sprinkle the water of atonement on them. Make them shave their entire body, wash their clothes, and in this way purify themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 29:1; Leviticus 16:3"> Then have them take a young bull and its grain offering of fine flour mingled with oil. Let them take another young bull as a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 40:12; Leviticus 8:3; Exodus 29:4–37"> You will bring the Levites in front of the tent of meeting and assemble the whole community of the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 1:4; Acts 6:6; 1 Timothy 4:14"> When you bring the Levites before Yahweh, the people of Israel must lay their hands on the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30; Leviticus 8:29; Numbers 1:49–53"> Aaron must offer the Levites before Yahweh, as a wave offering from the people of Israel so that they may do the service of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–10; Exodus 29:10; Leviticus 1:4"> The Levites must place their hands on the heads of the bulls. You must offer one bull for a sin offering and the other bull for a burnt offering to me, to atone for the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 8:21; Numbers 8:11; Romans 15:16"> Present the Levites before Aaron and before his sons, and lift them up as a wave offering to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 3:45; Malachi 3:17; Numbers 18:6"> In this way you must separate the Levites from among the people of Israel. The Levites will belong to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 8:11; Numbers 8:13; Numbers 3:23–37"> After that, the Levites must go in to serve in the tent of meeting. You must purify them. You must offer them as a wave offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:45; Numbers 3:9"> Do this, because they are entirely mine from among the people of Israel. They will take the place of each male child who opens the womb, the firstborn of all the descendants of Israel. I have taken the Levites for myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Luke 2:23; Exodus 13:12–15"> All the firstborn from among the people of Israel are mine, both of people and of animals. On the day that I took the lives of all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, I set them apart for myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> I have taken the Levites from among the people of Israel instead of all the firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 16:46; 2 Chronicles 26:16–20"> I have given the Levites as a gift to Aaron and his sons. I have taken them from among the people of Israel to do the work of the people of Israel in the tent of meeting. I have given them to atone for the people of Israel so that no plague will harm the people when they come near to the holy place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Moses, Aaron, and the whole community of the people of Israel did this with the Levites. They did everything that Yahweh had commanded Moses concerning the Levites. The people of Israel did this with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 8:7; Numbers 19:19; Numbers 8:15"> The Levites purified themselves and washed their clothing, and Aaron presented them as a wave offering to Yahweh and he made atonement for them to cleanse them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:2; 2 Chronicles 35:8–15; Numbers 8:15"> After that, the Levites went in to do their service in the tent of meeting before Aaron and before Aaron's sons. This was as Yahweh had commanded Moses about the Levites. They treated all the Levites in this way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Yahweh spoke again to Moses. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 4:3; 1 Chronicles 23:3; Numbers 4:23"> “All of this is for the Levites who are twenty-five years old and more. They must join the company to serve in the tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 4:23; 2 Timothy 4:7"> They must stop serving in this way at the age of fifty years. At that age they must not serve any longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:32; 1 Timothy 4:15"> They may help their brothers who continue to work at the tent of meeting, but they must serve no more. You must direct the Levites in all these matters.”</span>
