<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Yahweh spoke to Moses. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10; Numbers 15:18; Leviticus 25:2"> “Speak to the people of Israel and say to them, 'When you go into the land where you will live, which Yahweh will give to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:18; Genesis 8:21; Philippians 4:18"> you are to prepare an offering by fire to Yahweh, either a burnt offering or a sacrifice to fulfill a vow or a freewill offering, or an offering at your feasts, to produce a pleasing aroma for Yahweh from the herd or the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:40; Leviticus 23:13; Leviticus 6:14"> You must offer to Yahweh a burnt offering as well as a grain offering of a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with one-fourth of a hin of oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 28:7; Numbers 28:14; Judges 9:13"> You must also offer with the burnt offering, or for the sacrifice, one-fourth of a hin of wine for the drink offering for each lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 28:12–14; Numbers 15:4"> If you are offering a ram, you must prepare as a grain offering two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with a third of a hin of oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For the drink offering, you must offer a third of a hin of wine. It will produce a sweet aroma for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 3:1; Leviticus 7:11–18; Leviticus 1:3"> When you prepare a bull as a burnt offering or as a sacrifice to fulfill a vow, or as a fellowship offering to Yahweh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 28:14; Numbers 28:12; Leviticus 6:14"> then you must offer with the bull a grain offering of three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with half a hin of oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 15:5; Numbers 6:15"> You must offer as the drink offering half a hin of wine, as an offering made by fire, to produce a sweet aroma for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> It must be done this way for each bull, for each ram, and for each of the male lambs or young goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Every sacrifice that you prepare and offer must be done as described here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> All who are native-born Israelites must do these things in this way, when anyone brings an offering made by fire, to produce an aroma that is pleasing to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> If a foreigner is staying with you, or whoever may live among you throughout your people's generations, he must make an offering made by fire, to produce a sweet aroma for Yahweh. He must act as you act.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 9:14; Numbers 15:29; Exodus 12:49"> There must be the same law for the community and for the foreigner who stays with you, a permanent law throughout your people's generations. As you are, so also must be the traveler staying with you. He must act as you act before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The same law and decree must apply to you and to the foreigner who is staying with you.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Again Yahweh spoke to Moses. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 15:2; Deuteronomy 26:1–15"> “Speak to the people of Israel and say to them, 'When you come into the land where I will take you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 5:11–12"> when you eat the food produced in the land, you must offer an offering and present it to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 2:14; Exodus 34:26; Deuteronomy 26:2–10"> From the first of your dough you must offer a loaf to raise it up as a raised offering from the threshing floor. You must raise it up in this way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 29:28; Numbers 18:26"> You must give to me a raised offering throughout your people's generations from the first of your dough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:2; Leviticus 4:27; Leviticus 5:15–17"> You will sometimes sin without intending to do so, when you do not obey all these commands that I have spoken to Moses—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> everything that I have commanded you through Moses from the day that I began to give you commands and onward throughout your people's generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 4:23; Leviticus 4:13–21; Leviticus 5:15"> In the case of unintentional sin without the community's knowledge, then all the community must offer one young bull as a burnt offering to produce a sweet aroma for Yahweh. Along with this must be made a grain offering and drink offering, as commanded by the decree, and one male goat as a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 4:20; Romans 3:25; Hebrews 2:17"> The priest must make atonement for all the community of the people of Israel. They will be forgiven because the sin was an error. They have brought their sacrifice, an offering made by fire to me. They have brought their sin offering before me for their error.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 15:24"> Then all the community of the people of Israel will be forgiven, and also the foreigners who are staying with them, because all the people committed the sin unintentionally.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27–28; Acts 17:30; Acts 3:17"> If a person sins unintentionally, then he must offer a female goat a year old as a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 4:35"> The priest must make atonement before Yahweh for the person who sins unintentionally. That person will be forgiven when atonement has been made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 15:15; Leviticus 16:29; Leviticus 17:15"> You must have the same law for the one who does anything unintentionally, the same law for the one who is native born among the people of Israel and for the foreigners who are staying among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26; Psalms 19:13; Deuteronomy 1:43"> But the person who does anything in defiance, whether he is native born or a foreigner, blasphemes me. That person must be cut off from among his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:9; Proverbs 13:13; Ezekiel 18:20"> Because he has despised my word and has broken my commandment, that person must be cut off completely. His sin will be on him.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 35:2–3; Exodus 31:14–15; Exodus 20:8–10"> While the people of Israel were in the wilderness, they found a man gathering wood on the Sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 8:3–20"> Those who found him brought him to Moses, Aaron, and all the community.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 24:12; Numbers 9:8"> They kept him in custody because it had not been declared what should be done with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 7:58; Exodus 31:14–15; 1 Kings 21:13"> Then Yahweh said to Moses, “The man must surely be put to death. All the community must stone him with stones outside the camp.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 7:25"> So all the community brought him outside the camp and stoned him to death as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Again Yahweh spoke to Moses. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; Deuteronomy 22:12; Matthew 9:20"> “Speak to the descendants of Israel and command them to make for themselves tassels to hang from the borders of their garments, to hang them from each border by a blue cord. They must do this throughout their people's generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 106:39; Proverbs 28:26; Psalms 73:27"> It will be a special reminder to you, when you may look at it, of all my commandments, to carry them out so that you do not look to your own heart and your own eyes and prostitute yourselves to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 12:1; 1 Thessalonians 4:7; Leviticus 11:44–45"> Do this so that you may call to mind and obey all my commandments, and so that you may be holy, reserved for me, your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9–10; Ezekiel 36:25–27; Hebrews 11:16"> I am Yahweh your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, to become your God. I am Yahweh your God.”</span>
