<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 6:20; Genesis 10:1; Exodus 6:16"> Now this is the history of the descendants of Aaron and Moses when Yahweh spoke with Moses on Mount Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:23; Numbers 26:60; Exodus 28:1"> The names of Aaron's sons were Nadab the firstborn, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 28:41; Leviticus 8:30; Leviticus 8:12"> These are the names of the sons of Aaron, the priests who were anointed and who were ordained to serve as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–2; Numbers 26:61"> But Nadab and Abihu fell dead before Yahweh when they offered to him unacceptable fire in the wilderness of Sinai. Nadab and Abihu had no children, so just Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests with Aaron their father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Yahweh spoke to Moses. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Numbers 2:33; Numbers 1:49–53"> “Bring the tribe of Levi and present them to Aaron the priest for them to help him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 1:50; Numbers 8:15; Numbers 8:11"> They must perform the duties on behalf of Aaron and the whole community before the tent of meeting. They must serve in the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 4:33; Numbers 10:17; Isaiah 52:11"> They must care for all the furnishings in the tent of meeting, and they must help the tribes of Israel to carry out the tabernacle service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 8:19; Numbers 18:6–7; Ephesians 4:8"> You must give the Levites to Aaron and his sons. They are wholly given to help him serve the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 1:51; Numbers 3:38; Romans 12:7"> You must appoint Aaron and his sons as priests, but any foreigner who comes near must be put to death.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Yahweh spoke to Moses. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 3:41; Numbers 8:16; Numbers 18:6"> “Look, I have taken the Levites from among the people of Israel. I have done this instead of taking each firstborn male who is born among the people of Israel. The Levites belong to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:23; Exodus 13:12; Exodus 13:2"> All the firstborn belong to me. On the day that I attacked all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, I set apart for myself all the firstborn in Israel, both people and animals. They belong to me. I am Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Yahweh spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Mark 10:14; 2 Timothy 3:15"> “Count the descendants of Levi in each family, in their clans. Count every male who is one month old and older.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 3:39; Numbers 4:37; Numbers 4:49"> Moses counted them, following the word of Yahweh, just as he was commanded to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 46:11; Exodus 6:16–19; Numbers 15:5–23"> The names of Levi's sons were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:17; 1 Chronicles 26:1–32; Exodus 6:17–19"> The clans coming from Gershon's sons were Libni and Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Chronicles 15:17–21"> The clans coming from Kohath's sons were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 6:19; 1 Chronicles 6:19; Numbers 3:33"> The clans coming from Merari's sons were Mahli and Mushi. These are the clans of the Levites, listed clan by clan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The clans of the Libnites and the Shimeites come from Gershon. These are the clans of the Gershonites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 4:38–40"> All the males from a month old and older were counted, totaling 7,500.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:17"> The clans of the Gershonites must camp on the west side of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Eliasaph son of Lael must lead the clans of the descendants of the Gershonites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 25:9; 1 Chronicles 23:32; 1 Chronicles 9:14–33"> The family of Gershon must care for the tent of meeting including the tabernacle. They must care for the tent, its covering, and the curtain used as the entrance to the tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Exodus 38:9–16; Exodus 27:9–16"> They must care for the courtyard hangings, the curtain at the courtyard entrance—the courtyard that surrounds the sanctuary and the altar. They must care for the ropes of the tent of meeting and for everything in it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:23; Numbers 3:19; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> These clans come from Kohath: the clan of the Amramites, the clan of the Izharites, the clan of the Hebronites, and the clan of the Uzzielites. These clans belong to the Kohathites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 4:35–36; Numbers 3:7"> 8,600 males have been counted aged one month old and older to take care of the things that belong to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:23; Numbers 2:10"> The Kohath clans must camp on the south side of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Elizaphan son of Uzziel must lead the clans of the Kohathites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 36:35–36; Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 26:31–33"> They must care for the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars, the holy things that are used in their service, the curtain, and all the work around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 4:27; Numbers 4:16; Numbers 20:25–28"> Eleazar son of Aaron the priest must lead the men who lead the Levites. He must supervise the men who care for the holy place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 3:20; 1 Chronicles 6:19; 1 Chronicles 23:21"> Two clans have come from Merari: the clan of the Mahlites and the clan of the Mushites. These clans have come from Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 2:9; Numbers 2:11; Numbers 1:21"> 6,200 males have been counted aged one month old and older.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:25; Numbers 3:28–29"> Zuriel son of Abihail must lead the clans of Merari. They must camp on the north side of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 26:32; Exodus 26:37; Exodus 26:15–29"> The descendants of Merari must care for the framing of the tabernacle, the crossbars, posts, bases, all the hardware, and everything related to them, including</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> the pillars and posts of the courtyard that surround the tabernacle, with their sockets, pegs, and ropes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 3:10; Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:3"> Moses and Aaron and his sons must camp on the east side of the tabernacle, in front of the tent of meeting, toward the sunrise. They are responsible for the fulfillment of the duties of the sanctuary and the duties of the people of Israel. Any foreigner who approaches the sanctuary must be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Matthew 7:14; Numbers 4:47–48"> Moses and Aaron counted all the males in the clans of Levi who were aged one month old and older, just as Yahweh commanded. They counted twenty-two thousand men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 3:15; Luke 10:20; Psalms 87:6"> Yahweh said to Moses, “Count all the firstborn males of the people of Israel who are aged one month old and older. List their names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:45; Numbers 8:16"> You must take the Levites for me—I am Yahweh—instead of all the firstborn of the people of Israel, and the livestock of the Levites instead of the firstborn of the livestock of the descendants of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Moses counted all the firstborn people of Israel as Yahweh had commanded him to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 3:39"> He counted all the firstborn males by name, aged one month old and older. He counted 22,273 men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Again, Yahweh spoke to Moses. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:40–41"> “Take the Levites instead of all the firstborn among the people of Israel, and take the Levites' livestock instead of the people's livestock. The Levites belong to me—I am Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 13:13; Numbers 18:15–16"> You must collect five shekels for the redemption of each of the 273 firstborn people of Israel who exceed the number of the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 27:6; Numbers 18:16"> You must use the shekel of the sanctuary as your standard weight. The shekel equals twenty gerahs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> You must give the price of redemption that you paid to Aaron and his sons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> So Moses collected the payment of redemption from those who exceeded the number of those redeemed by the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Numbers 3:46–47; 1 Peter 3:18; Matthew 20:28"> Moses collected the money from the firstborn of the people of Israel. He collected 1,365 shekels, weighing with the shekel of the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 3:48; Numbers 16:15; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> Moses gave the redemption money to Aaron and to his sons. Moses did everything he was told to do by Yahweh's word, as Yahweh had commanded him.</span>
</div>
</div>
