<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 21:25; Ephesians 5:32; Luke 21:11"> A great sign was seen in heaven: a woman clothed with the sun, and with the moon under her feet, and a crown of twelve stars was on her head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 16:21; Galatians 4:19; Revelation 12:4"> She was pregnant, and she was crying out in birth pains, in the anguish of childbirth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 17:12; Revelation 12:9; Daniel 7:24"> Then another sign was seen in heaven: Look! There was a huge red dragon that had seven heads and ten horns, and there were seven crowns on his heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 8:9–12; Revelation 12:9; John 8:44"> His tail swept away a third of the stars in heaven and hurled them down to the earth. The dragon stood before the woman who was about to give birth, so that when she gave birth, he would devour her child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 19:15; Revelation 2:26–27; Revelation 12:2"> She gave birth to a son, a male child, who would rule all the nations with an iron rod. Her child was snatched away to God and to his throne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 13:5; Revelation 11:2–3; Revelation 12:14"> and the woman fled into the wilderness, where God had prepared a place for her, so she could be taken care of for 1,260 days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jude 1:9; Daniel 12:1; Ephesians 6:12"> Now there was war in heaven. Michael and his angels fought against the dragon; and the dragon and his angels fought back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 37:10; Revelation 12:11; Jude 1:6"> But the dragon was not strong enough to win. So there was no longer any place in heaven for him and his angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 10:18; John 12:31; Revelation 20:10"> The great dragon—that old serpent called the devil or Satan, who deceives the whole world—was thrown down to the earth, and his angels were thrown down with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 22:31; Zechariah 3:1–2; Revelation 11:15"> Then I heard a loud voice in heaven:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Now have come the salvation and the power</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the kingdom of our God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the authority of his Christ.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the accuser of our brothers has been thrown down,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the one who accused them before our God day and night.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:3–5; John 16:33; 1 John 5:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">They conquered him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for they did not love their lives even to death.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Revelation 8:13; Psalms 96:11–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore rejoice you heavens,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all who reside in them!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But woe to the earth and to the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because the devil has gone down to you!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He is filled with terrible anger</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because he has only a little time!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 3:15; Revelation 12:3–5; John 16:33"> When the dragon realized he had been thrown down to the earth, he pursued the woman who had given birth to the male child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Isaiah 40:31; Revelation 12:6"> But the woman was given the two wings of a great eagle, so that she would flee to the place prepared for her in the wilderness. This was the place where she would be taken care of, for a time, times, and half a time—out of the serpent's presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7; Isaiah 59:19; Psalms 18:4"> The serpent poured water out of his mouth like a river, that he would make a flood to sweep her away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 17:6; Exodus 12:35–36; 2 Kings 8:9"> But the earth helped the woman. The earth opened its mouth and swallowed the river that the dragon was pouring out of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:12; Genesis 3:15; Revelation 11:7"> Then the dragon was enraged at the woman and went off to wage war with the rest of her descendants, those who obey God's commandments and hold to the testimony about Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Then the dragon stood on the sand of the seashore.</span>
