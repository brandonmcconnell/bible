<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 21:15; Ezekiel 42:15–20; Zechariah 2:1–2"> A reed was given to me to use like a measuring rod. I was told, “Get up and measure the temple of God and the altar, and those who worship in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Daniel 7:25; Luke 21:24"> But do not measure the courtyard outside the temple, for it has been given over to the Gentiles. They will trample the holy city for forty-two months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Revelation 11:2; Deuteronomy 19:15"> I will give my two witnesses authority to prophesy for 1,260 days, clothed in sackcloth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 4:11–14; Zechariah 4:2–3; Jeremiah 11:16"> These witnesses are the two olive trees and the two lampstands that have stood before the Lord of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:14; 2 Kings 1:10–12; Hosea 6:5"> If anyone chooses to harm them, fire comes out of their mouth and devours their enemies. Anyone who wishes to harm them must be killed in this way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Luke 4:25; Psalms 105:26–36"> These witnesses have authority to close up the sky so that no rain will fall during the time that they prophesy. They have power to turn the waters to blood and to strike the earth with every kind of plague whenever they wish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 13:7; Daniel 8:23–24; Daniel 7:25"> When they will have finished their testimony, the beast that comes up from the bottomless pit will wage war against them. He will conquer them and kill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Revelation 18:10; Revelation 18:21"> Their bodies will lie in the street of the great city (which is symbolically called Sodom and Egypt) where their Lord was crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 79:2–3; Revelation 17:15; Revelation 10:11"> For three and a half days some from every people, tribe, language, and nation will look at their bodies. They will not permit them to be placed in a tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 18:17; Revelation 3:10; Acts 5:33"> Those who live on the earth will rejoice over them and celebrate. They will even send gifts to one another because these two prophets tormented those who lived on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Romans 8:11; Romans 8:2"> But after three and a half days a breath of life from God will enter them, and they will stand on their feet. Great fear will fall on those who see them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 4:1; 2 Kings 2:11; Acts 1:9"> Then they will hear a loud voice from heaven say to them, “Come up here!” Then they will go up into heaven in a cloud, while their enemies look on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:7; Revelation 6:12; Joshua 7:19"> At that hour there will be a major earthquake, and a tenth part of the city will collapse. Seven thousand people will be killed in the earthquake, and the survivors will be terrified and give glory to the God of heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 8:13; Revelation 9:12; Revelation 15:1"> The second woe is past. Look! The third woe is coming quickly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 7:14; Revelation 12:10; Daniel 7:27"> Then the seventh angel sounded his trumpet, and loud voices spoke in heaven and said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord and of his Christ.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will reign forever and ever.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 4:4; Revelation 4:10; Revelation 7:11"> Then the twenty-four elders, who sit on their thrones in the presence of God, fell upon their faces and worshiped God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 16:5; Revelation 4:8–9"> They said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“We give thanks to you, Lord God Almighty, the one who is and who was,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because you have taken your great power and have begun to reign.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some copies read the ruler over all, the one who is and who was, and who is to come, but most modern versions do not.">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 19:5; Revelation 20:12; Revelation 6:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The nations were enraged,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but your wrath has come.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The time has come for the dead to be judged</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and for you to reward your servants the prophets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">those who are believers, and those who feared your name,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">both the unimportant and the mighty.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The time has come for you to destroy those who are destroying the earth.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 16:21; Revelation 15:5–8; Ezekiel 38:22"> Then God's temple in heaven was opened and the ark of his covenant was seen within his temple. There were flashes of lightning, rumblings, crashes of thunder, an earthquake, and a great hailstorm.</span>
