<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Revelation 22:6; Galatians 1:12"> This is the revelation of Jesus Christ that God gave him to show his servants what must soon take place. He made it known by sending his angel to his servant John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:6; John 19:35; 1 John 1:1"> John testified about the word of God and the testimony of Jesus Christ, all the things that he saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Revelation 22:10; Romans 13:11"> Blessed is the one who reads aloud this prophecy, and those who listen to the words of this prophecy and who obey what is written in it, because the time is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; John 1:1; Hebrews 13:8"> John, to the seven churches in Asia: May grace be to you and peace from the one who is, and who was, and who is to come, and from the seven spirits who are before his throne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:27; Hebrews 9:14; 1 John 1:7"> and from Jesus Christ, who is the faithful witness, the firstborn from the dead, and the ruler of the kings of the earth. To the one who loves us and has released us from our sins by his blood—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Revelation 20:6; Exodus 19:6"> he has made us a kingdom, priests for his God and Father—to him be the glory and the power forever and ever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:30; Zechariah 12:10; Daniel 7:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look, he is coming with the clouds;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">every eye will see him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">including those who pierced him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">All the tribes of the earth will mourn because of him.</span>Yes, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Revelation 22:13; Isaiah 43:10"> “I am the alpha and the omega,” says the Lord God, “the one who is, and who was, and who is to come, the Almighty.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some versions read, I am the alpha and the omega, the beginning and the end, says the Lord God .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 14:22; Revelation 3:10; John 16:33"> I, John—your brother and the one who shares with you in the suffering and kingdom and patient endurance that are in Jesus—was on the island called Patmos because of the word of God and the testimony about Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:10; 2 Corinthians 12:2–4; Revelation 4:1–2"> I was in the Spirit on the Lord's day. I heard behind me a loud voice like a trumpet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:1; Jeremiah 30:2; Habakkuk 2:2"> It said, “Write what you see in a book, and send it to the seven churches—to Ephesus, to Smyrna, to Pergamum, to Thyatira, to Sardis, to Philadelphia, and to Laodicea.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 4:2; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 1:20–1"> I turned around to see whose voice was speaking to me, and as I turned I saw seven golden lampstands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Daniel 10:5–6; Daniel 10:16"> In the middle of the lampstands there was one like a son of man, wearing a long robe that reached down to his feet and a golden sash around his chest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 10:6; Daniel 7:9; Revelation 19:12"> His head and hair were as white as wool—as white as snow—and his eyes were like a flame of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2; Daniel 10:6; Revelation 14:2"> His feet were like polished bronze, like bronze that had been refined in a furnace, and his voice was like the sound of many rushing waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 19:15; Revelation 2:12"> He had seven stars in his right hand, and a sword with two sharp edges was coming out of his mouth. His face was shining like the sun at its strongest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Revelation 22:13; Daniel 10:8–10"> When I saw him, I fell at his feet like a dead man. He placed his right hand on me and said, “Do not be afraid. I am the first and the last,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 6:9; Hebrews 7:25; Romans 14:8–9"> and the one who lives. I was dead, but look, I live forever! And I have the keys of death and of Hades.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 4:1–11; Revelation 1:11–3"> Therefore write down what you have seen, what is now, and what will take place after this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 2:1; Revelation 3:1; Revelation 1:16"> As for the hidden meaning about the seven stars you saw in my right hand, and the seven golden lampstands: The seven stars are the angels of the seven churches, and the seven lampstands are the seven churches.”</span>
