<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 29:11–12; Revelation 5:5; Revelation 10:1"> I saw a mighty angel proclaiming with a loud voice, “Who is worthy to open the scroll and break its seals?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 5:13; Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34"> No one in heaven or on the earth or under the earth was able to open the scroll or to read it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 12:8–9; Revelation 4:1"> I wept bitterly because no one was found worthy to open the scroll or to read it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 11:1; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Revelation 22:16"> But one of the elders said to me, “Do not weep. Look! The Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has conquered. He is able to open the scroll and its seven seals.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 4:10; John 1:29; Revelation 5:12"> I saw a Lamb standing in the middle of the throne area and among the four living creatures and among the elders. He looked as though he had been killed. He had seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven spirits of God sent out into all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 5:1; Revelation 4:2–3"> He went and took hold of the scroll out of the right hand of the one who was seated on the throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Revelation 8:3–4; Philippians 2:9–11"> When he had taken the scroll, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell down before the Lamb. Each of them had a harp and a golden bowl full of incense, which are the prayers of the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; 1 John 1:7; Matthew 26:28"> They sang a new song:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are worthy to take the scroll</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to open its seals.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For you were slaughtered, and with your blood you purchased people for God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from every tribe, language, people, and nation.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 1:6; Daniel 7:27; Revelation 20:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You made them a kingdom and priests to serve our God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will reign on the earth.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 7:10; Hebrews 12:22; Revelation 4:4"> Then I looked and heard the sound of many angels who encircled the throne and the living creatures and the elders. Their total number was ten thousands of ten thousands and thousands of thousands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Revelation 19:1; Revelation 4:11"> They said in a loud voice, “Worthy is the Lamb who has been slaughtered to receive power, wealth, wisdom, strength, honor, glory, and praise.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; 1 Chronicles 29:11; Psalms 148:2–13"> I heard every created thing that was in heaven and on the earth and under the earth and on the sea—everything in them—saying, “To the one who sits on the throne and to the Lamb be praise, honor, glory, and the power to rule, forever and ever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 19:4; Revelation 4:9–11; Revelation 5:8"> The four living creatures said, “Amen!” and the elders fell down and worshiped.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some older versions read, the twenty-four elders fell down and worshiped the one who lives forever and ever .">a</a></sup></span>
