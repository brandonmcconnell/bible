<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 2:4–5; Hosea 3:1; Deuteronomy 31:16"> When Yahweh spoke at first through Hosea, he said to him,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Go, take for yourself a wife who is a prostitute.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">She will have children who are the result of her prostitution.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the land is committing great prostitution</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">by forsaking Yahweh.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:1–3"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">So Hosea went and married Gomer daughter of Diblaim, and she conceived and bore him a son.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 10:7–8; 2 Kings 10:10–11; Ezekiel 23:10"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh said to Hosea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Call his name Jezreel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For in a little while</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will punish the house of Jehu</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the bloodshed at Jezreel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will put an end</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to the kingdom of the house of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 6:33; Joshua 17:16; Hosea 2:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will happen on the day</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that I break the bow of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the Valley of Jezreel.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; 1 Peter 2:10; Hosea 2:4"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">Gomer conceived again and bore a daughter. Then Yahweh said to Hosea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Call her name Lo-Ruhamah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for I will no longer have mercy</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on the house of Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that I should forgive them at all.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6; Isaiah 12:2; 2 Kings 19:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet I will have mercy on the house of Judah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will save them by myself, Yahweh their God.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will not save them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">by bow, sword, battle, horses, or horsemen.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">Now after Gomer had weaned Lo-Ruhamah, she conceived and bore another son.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:1"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then Yahweh said,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Call his name Lo-Ammi,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for you are not my people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I am not your God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:18; Galatians 4:6–7; John 1:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet the number of the people of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will be like the sand of the seashore,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">which cannot be measured or counted.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will be that where it was said to them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">'You are not my people,'</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it will be said to them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">'You are people of the living God.'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 3:5; Isaiah 11:12–13; Jeremiah 50:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people of Judah and the people of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will be gathered together.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will appoint one leader for themselves,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they will go up from the land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for great will be the day of Jezreel.</span></span>
