<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:12; 2 Corinthians 12:7; 1 John 5:20"> I must boast, but nothing is gained by it. But I will go on to visions and revelations from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 22:17; Ezekiel 8:1–3; 1 Thessalonians 4:17"> I know a man in Christ who fourteen years ago who—whether in the body or out of the body, I do not know, God knows—was caught up into the third heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And I know that this man—whether in the body, or out of the body, I do not know, God knows—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 23:43; Revelation 2:7; Ezekiel 31:9"> was caught up into paradise and heard things too sacred for anyone to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:30; 1 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> On behalf of such a person I will boast. But on behalf of myself I will not boast, except about my weaknesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:16; 2 Corinthians 11:31; 2 Corinthians 12:11"> If I should choose to boast, I will not be a fool, because I would be speaking the truth. But I will keep from boasting, so that no one will think more of me than what he sees in me or hears from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:55; Judges 2:3; 2 Chronicles 32:25–26"> To keep me from boasting because of the surpassing greatness of the revelations, a thorn in the flesh was given to me, a messenger from Satan to afflict me—so I would not become overly proud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Matthew 26:39–44; 1 Samuel 15:11"> Three times I begged the Lord about this, for him to take it away from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 12:10; Isaiah 40:29–31"> But he said to me, “My grace is enough for you, for power is made perfect in weakness.” So I would much rather boast about my weakness, so that the power of Christ might reside on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9; Ephesians 6:10; Romans 5:3"> Therefore I am content for Christ's sake in weaknesses, in insults, in troubles, in persecutions and distressing situations. For whenever I am weak, then I am strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:5; 2 Corinthians 11:1; 1 Corinthians 15:8–10"> I have become a fool! You forced me to this, for I should have been praised by you. For I was not at all inferior to the so-called super-apostles, even though I am nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:18–19; 2 Corinthians 11:4; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> The true signs of an apostle were performed among you with complete patience, signs and wonders and mighty deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Corinthians 9:6; 2 Corinthians 11:7–9"> For how were you less important than the rest of the churches, except that I was not a burden to you? Forgive me for this wrong!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 19:14; Genesis 24:35–36; 1 Corinthians 10:33"> Look! I am ready to come to you a third time. I will not be a burden to you, for I do not want what is yours. I want you. For children should not save up for the parents. Instead, the parents should save up for the children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:8; 2 Corinthians 1:6; Philippians 2:17"> I will most gladly spend and be spent for your souls. If I love you more, am I to be loved less?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:5; 2 Corinthians 10:2–3; 2 Corinthians 12:13"> But as it is, I did not burden you. But, since I am so crafty, I am the one who caught you by deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:5; 1 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Kings 5:20–27"> Did I take advantage of you by anyone I sent to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; Romans 4:12; Acts 20:33–35"> I urged Titus to go to you, and I sent the other brother with him. Did Titus take advantage of you? Did we not walk in the same way? Did we not walk in the same steps?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 9:1; 1 Corinthians 14:26; 2 Corinthians 10:8"> Do you think all of this time we have been defending ourselves to you? In the sight of God, we have in Christ been saying everything for your strengthening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 5:19–21; 1 Peter 2:1; Romans 1:29"> For I fear that when I come I may not find you as I wish. I fear that you might not find me as you wish. I fear that there may be quarreling, jealousy, outbursts of anger, rivalries, slander, gossip, arrogance, and disorder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:2; 1 Peter 4:2–3; Colossians 3:5"> I fear that when I come back, my God might humble me before you. I fear that I might be grieved by many of those who have sinned before now, and who did not repent of the impurity and sexual immorality and lustful indulgence that they practiced.</span>
