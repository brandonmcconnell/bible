<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; 2 Corinthians 7:14; Titus 3:1"> Now I have sent the brothers so that our boasting about you may not be futile, and so that you would be ready, as I said you would be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 15:26; 2 Corinthians 11:17; 2 Corinthians 8:24"> Otherwise, if any Macedonians come with me and find you unprepared, we would be ashamed—I say nothing about you—for being so confident in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:11; 1 Samuel 25:27; Philippians 4:17"> So I thought it was necessary to urge the brothers to come to you and to make arrangements in advance for the gift you have promised. <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 20:35; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> Let each one give as he has planned in his heart, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20; Philippians 4:18–19; Proverbs 10:22"> And God is able to make all grace overflow for you, so that, always, in all things, you may have all you need. This will be so that you may multiply every good deed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 112:9; Psalms 112:3; Proverbs 21:21"> It is as it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He has distributed his riches and given them to the poor.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His righteousness lasts forever.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 55:10; 2 Corinthians 9:6; Hosea 10:12"> He who supplies seed to the farmer who sows and bread for food, will also supply and multiply your seed for sowing. He will increase the harvest of your righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Chronicles 29:12–14; 1 Timothy 6:17–18"> You will be enriched in every way so you can be generous. This will bring about thanksgiving to God through us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14–15; Philemon 1:4–7; Philippians 2:25"> For carrying out this service not only meets the needs of the saints but is also overflowing into many acts of thanksgiving to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Matthew 5:16; 2 Corinthians 2:12"> Because of your being tested and proved by this service, you will also glorify God by obedience to your confession of the gospel of Christ. You will also glorify God by the generosity of your gift to them and to everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:14; Philippians 4:1; Philippians 4:18–19"> They long for you, and they are praying for you. They do this because of the very great grace of God that is on you.</span>
