<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:11; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 27:2"> Yahweh said this to me, “Go and buy a linen undergarment and put it on around your waist, but do not put it in water first.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:8; John 15:14; Proverbs 3:5"> So I bought an undergarment as Yahweh directed, and I put in on around my waist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:8"> Then the word of Yahweh came to me a second time, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 4:10; Psalms 137:1; Jeremiah 51:63–64"> “Take the undergarment that you purchased that is around your waist, get up and go now to Perath.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some transate Perath as the Euphrates .">a</a></sup>Hide it there in a rock crevice.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; Exodus 39:42–43; 2 Timothy 2:3"> So I went and hid it in Perath, just as Yahweh had commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> After many days, Yahweh said to me, “Get up and go back to Perath. Take from there the undergarment that I had told you to hide.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:12; Isaiah 64:6; Jeremiah 13:10"> So I went back to Perath<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some transate Perath as the Euphrates .">b</a></sup>and dug out the undergarment where I had hid it, and behold, it was destroyed and completely useless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then the word of Yahweh came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremiah 13:15–17; Zephaniah 3:11"> “Yahweh says this: In the same way I will destroy the great arrogance of Judah and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; Jeremiah 9:14; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16"> This wicked people who refuses to listen to my word, who walk in the hardness of their heart, who go after other gods to worship them and bow down to them—they will be like this undergarment that is good for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Jeremiah 33:9; Psalms 81:11"> For just as an undergarment clings to someone's hips, so I have made all the house of Israel and all the house of Judah cling to me—this is Yahweh's declaration—to be my people, to bring me fame, praise, and honor. But they would not listen to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19"> So you must speak this word to them, 'Yahweh, the God of Israel, says this: Every jar will be filled with wine.' They will say to you, 'Do we not indeed know that every jar will be filled with wine?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 63:6; Psalms 75:8; Psalms 60:3"> So say to them, 'Yahweh says this: See, I am about to fill with drunkenness every inhabitant of this land, the kings who sit on David's throne, the priests, prophets, and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Psalms 2:9; Jeremiah 19:9–11"> Then I will smash each man against the other, fathers and children together—this is Yahweh's declaration—I will not pity them or have compassion, and I will not spare them from destruction.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 28:14–22; Revelation 2:29; Acts 4:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen and pay attention. Do not be arrogant, for Yahweh had spoken.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:9; Isaiah 8:22; Isaiah 5:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give honor to Yahweh your God before he brings darkness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and before he causes your feet to stumble on the mountains at twilight.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For you are hoping for light, but he will turn the place into a deep darkness, into a dark cloud.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 2:2; Jeremiah 9:1; Jeremiah 14:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">So if you will not listen, I will weep alone because of your arrogance.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">My eyes will certainly weep and flow with tears, for Yahweh's flock has been taken captive.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:26; 2 Kings 24:12; 2 Kings 24:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Say to the king and to the queen mother, 'Come down from your thrones,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for your splendid crowns have fallen from your heads.'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:27; Jeremiah 39:9; Deuteronomy 28:52"> <span className="pl pl-1">The cities in the Negev will be shut up, with no one to open them. All Judah will be taken captive, completely taken captive.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:22; Jeremiah 1:14; Habakkuk 1:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lift up your eyes and look at the ones coming from the north.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Where is the flock he gave to you, the flock that was so beautiful to you?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 13:8; Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 5:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">What will you say when God sets over you those you had trained to be your special allies?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Are these not the beginnings of the labor pains that will seize you just like a woman in childbirth?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nahum 3:5; Jeremiah 16:10–11; Jeremiah 5:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then you might say in your heart, 'Why are these things happening to me?'</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will be for the multitude of your iniquities that your skirts are raised up and you have been violated.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; Jeremiah 9:5; Matthew 19:24–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Can the people of Cush change their skin color, or a leopard change its spots?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If so, then you yourself, although accustomed to wickedness, would be able to do good.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 1:4; Leviticus 26:33; Ezekiel 5:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I will scatter them like chaff that perishes in the desert wind.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 20:29; Jeremiah 2:32; Psalms 9:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is what I have given to you, the portion I have decreed for you—this is Yahweh's declaration—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because you have forgotten me and trusted in deceit.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Lamentations 1:8; Hosea 2:10; Ezekiel 23:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">So also I myself will strip your skirts off you, and your private parts will be seen.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hosea 8:5; Ezekiel 6:13; Ezekiel 24:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have seen your adultery and neighing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the wickedness of your prostitution on the hills and in the fields,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I have seen these detestable things!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Woe to you, Jerusalem! How long until you are made clean again?”</span></span>
