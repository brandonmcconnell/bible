<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:1; 2 Kings 24:1–2; Daniel 1:1"> This is the word that came to Jeremiah about all the people of Judah. It came in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah. That was the first year of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:11; Jeremiah 19:14–15; Mark 7:14–15"> Jeremiah the prophet proclaimed this to all the people of Judah and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:7; Jeremiah 26:5; Jeremiah 1:2"> He said, “For twenty-three years, from the thirteenth year of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah until this day, Yahweh's words have been coming to me and I have spoken to you again and again, but you have not listened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:3; Jeremiah 26:5; Jeremiah 13:10–11"> Yahweh sent out all his servants the prophets to you again and again, but you have not listened or paid any attention.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:7; Genesis 17:8; Isaiah 55:6–7"> These prophets said, 'Let each man turn from his wicked way and the corruption of his practices and return to the land that Yahweh gave in ancient times to your ancestors and to you, as a permanent gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:19; Jeremiah 7:6; Jeremiah 35:15"> So do not walk after other gods to worship them or bow down to them, and do not provoke him with the work of your hands so that he does you harm.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 21:15; Proverbs 8:36; Deuteronomy 32:21"> But you have not listened to me—this is Yahweh's declaration—so you have provoked me with the work of your hands to do harm to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> So Yahweh of hosts says this, 'Because you did not listen to my words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 13:3; Jeremiah 18:16; Jeremiah 1:15"> see, I am about to send out a command to gather all the peoples of the north—this is Yahweh's declaration—with Nebuchadnezzar my servant, king of Babylon, and bring them against this land and its inhabitants, and against all the nations around you. For I will set them apart for destruction. I will turn them into a horror, an object for hissing, and an unending desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Revelation 18:22–23; Ezekiel 26:13"> I will put an end to the sound of joy and sound of gladness, the sound of the groom and the sound of the bride, the sound of the millstones and the light of the lamp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 9:2; Zechariah 1:12; 2 Chronicles 36:21–22"> Then all of this land will become a desolation and a horror, and these nations will serve the king of Babylon for seventy years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 9:2; Isaiah 13:19; Jeremiah 29:10"> Then it will happen when seventy years have been completed, that I will punish the king of Babylon and that nation, the land of the Chaldeans—this is Yahweh's declaration—for their iniquity and make it an unending desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 1:5; Daniel 5:31"> Then I will carry out against that land all the words that I had spoken, and everything written in this book that Jeremiah has prophesied against all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:6; Jeremiah 27:7; Jeremiah 50:9"> For also many other nations and great kings will make slaves out of these nations. I will repay them for their deeds and the works of their hands.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 75:8; Isaiah 51:17; Revelation 14:10"> For Yahweh, God of Israel, said this to me, “Take this cup of the wine of fury from my hand and make all the nations to which I am sending you drink it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nahum 3:11; Jeremiah 51:7; Jeremiah 51:39"> For they will drink and then stagger about and rant madly before the sword that I am sending out among them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 25:28; Ezekiel 43:3"> So I took the cup from Yahweh's hand, and I made all the nations to which Yahweh had sent me drink it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; Psalms 60:3; Jeremiah 44:22"> Jerusalem, the cities of Judah and her kings and officials—to turn them into ruins and something terrifying, and into an object for hissing and cursing, as they are at this present day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:13–26; Nahum 3:8–10; Jeremiah 46:2"> Other nations also had to drink it: Pharaoh king of Egypt and his servants; his officials and all his people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 1:1; Ezekiel 30:5; Isaiah 20:1"> all people of mixed heritage and all the kings of the land of Uz; all the kings of the land of Philistia—Ashkelon, Gaza, Ekron, and the remnant of Ashdod;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:1–22; Psalms 137:7; Jeremiah 9:26"> Edom and Moab and the people of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:4; Amos 1:9–10; Ezekiel 28:22–23"> The kings of Tyre and Sidon, the kings of the coasts on the other side of the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:26; Jeremiah 49:32; Jeremiah 49:8"> Dedan, Tema, and Buz with all the ones who cut the hair on the sides of their heads, they also had to drink it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:14; Jeremiah 25:20; Ezekiel 30:5"> All the kings of Arabia and all the kings of people of mixed heritage who live in the wilderness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 11:11; Genesis 10:22; Isaiah 13:17"> all the kings of Zimri, all the kings of Elam, and all the kings of the Medes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:41; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 50:1–46"> all the kings of the north, the ones close by and the ones far away—everyone with his brother and all the kingdoms of the world that are on the surface of the earth, all of them had to drink the cup from Yahweh's hand. Finally, the king of Babylon will also drink from that cup.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:16; Habakkuk 2:16; Ezekiel 21:4–5"> Yahweh said to me, “Now you must say to them, 'Yahweh of hosts, God of Israel, says this: Drink and become drunk, then vomit, fall down, and do not rise before the sword that I am sending among you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 34:33; Jeremiah 51:29; Isaiah 14:24–27"> Then it will happen that if they refuse to take the cup from your hand to drink, you will say to them, 'Yahweh of hosts says this: You must certainly drink it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:21; Proverbs 11:31; 1 Peter 4:17"> For see, I am about to bring disaster on the city that is called by my name, and should you yourselves be free from punishment? You will not be free, for I am calling a sword against all the inhabitants of the land!—this is the declaration of Yahweh of hosts.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Amos 1:2; Joel 3:16; Isaiah 42:13"> You must prophesy all these words against them, and say to them,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'Yahweh will roar from the heights</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he will shout with his voice from his holy dwelling,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he will roar mightily against his fold;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he will shout, like those who tread the grapes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">against all those who live on the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Joel 3:2; Isaiah 66:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sound of battle will resound to the ends of the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for Yahweh brings charges against the nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he brings judgment on all flesh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the wicked ones he will put to the sword—this is Yahweh's declaration.'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:19; Isaiah 34:2; 2 Chronicles 15:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh of hosts says this,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">'See, disaster is going out from nation to nation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and a great storm is beginning from the farthest parts of the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 66:16; Jeremiah 8:2; Psalms 79:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then those killed by Yahweh will on that day extend from one end of the earth to the other;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they will not be mourned, gathered, or buried.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will be like dung on the ground.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Jeremiah 50:27; Psalms 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wail, shepherds, and shout for help!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Roll about in the dust,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you leaders of the flock,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the days of your slaughter have come;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you will be scattered when you fall like fine pottery.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 11:20; Amos 2:14; Jeremiah 52:8–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is no refuge for the shepherds, there will be no escape for the leaders of the flock.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:8; Jeremiah 25:34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear the cries of the shepherds and the wails of the leaders of the flock,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for Yahweh is destroying their pastures.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 27:10–11; Isaiah 32:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">So the peaceful pastures will be devastated because of Yahweh's fierce anger.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 5:6; Hosea 13:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Like a young lion, he has left his den, for their land will become a horror because of the oppressor's anger,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because of his angry wrath.'”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Instead of the oppressor's anger , some ancient copies and modern versions have the oppressor's sword .">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
