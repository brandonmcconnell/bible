<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 2:37–38; 1 Chronicles 24:14; 2 Kings 25:18"> Pashhur son of Immer the priest—he was a leading officer—heard Jeremiah prophesying these words before Yahweh's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:13; Zechariah 14:10; Jeremiah 1:19"> So Pashhur beat Jeremiah the prophet and then placed him in the stocks that were at the Upper Gate of Benjamin in Yahweh's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; Jeremiah 46:5; Lamentations 2:22"> It happened on the next day that Pashhur brought Jeremiah out of the stocks. Then Jeremiah said to him, “Yahweh has not called your name Pashhur, but you are Magor Missabib.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:21; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 21:4–10"> For Yahweh says this, 'Look, I will make you an object of horror, you and all of your loved ones, for they will fall by the sword of their enemies and your eyes will see it. I will give all of Judah into the hand of the king of Babylon. He will make them captives in Babylon or attack them with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:13; Ezekiel 22:25; Lamentations 1:10"> I will give him all the wealth of this city and all of its riches, all of its precious items and all the treasures of the kings of Judah. I will place these things in the hand of your enemies, and they will seize them. They will take them and bring them to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14–15; Lamentations 2:14; Ezekiel 22:28"> But you, Pashhur, and all the inhabitants of your house will go into captivity. You will go to Babylon and die there. You and all of your loved ones to whom you prophesied deceitful things will be buried there.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 9:7; Jeremiah 20:9; Lamentations 3:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Yahweh, you deceived me, and I was deceived. You are stronger than I, and you overpowered me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have become a laughingstock all day long; everyone mocks me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Jeremiah 6:10; Jeremiah 5:15–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">For whenever I have spoken, I have called out and proclaimed, 'Violence and destruction.'</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then Yahweh's word has become for me reproach and mocking every day.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 39:3; Job 32:18–20; Acts 4:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I say, 'I will not think about Yahweh anymore. I will not speak any longer in his name.'</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then it is like a fire in my heart, held within my bones. So I struggle to contain it but I cannot.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Psalms 31:13; Isaiah 29:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have heard rumors of terror from many people all around. 'Report! We must report it!'</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Those who are close to me watch to see if I will fall. 'Perhaps he can be tricked.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If so, we can overpower him and take our revenge on him.'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:19; Jeremiah 1:8; Jeremiah 15:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">But Yahweh is with me like a powerful warrior, so the ones pursuing me will stagger.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will not defeat me. They will be greatly ashamed, because they will not succeed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will have unending shame, it will never be forgotten.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Jeremiah 11:20; Psalms 54:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">But Yahweh of hosts, you examine the righteous and see the mind and the heart.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let me see your vengeance on them for I have committed my cause to you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; Psalms 35:9–11; Psalms 69:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sing to Yahweh! Praise Yahweh!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For he has rescued the lives of those who are oppressed from the hand of evildoers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:10; Job 3:3–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the day when I was born be cursed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not let the day that my mother bore me be blessed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 21:5–6; Luke 1:14; Jeremiah 1:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the man who informed my father be cursed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the one who said, 'A male child has been born to you,' causing great joy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:22; Jeremiah 48:3–4; Deuteronomy 29:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let that man be like the cities that Yahweh overthrew and he did not have compassion on them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let him hear a cry for help in the dawn, a battle cry at noontime,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 10:18–19; Job 3:10–11; Ecclesiastes 6:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">because he did not kill me in the womb, making my mother to be my tomb,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a womb that was pregnant forever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 3:20; Lamentations 3:1; 1 Peter 4:14–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why is it that I came out from the womb to see troubles and agony,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so that my days are filled with shame?”</span></span>
