<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:8"> This is the word of Yahweh that came to Jeremiah regarding the drought,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 3:26; Jeremiah 8:21; 1 Samuel 5:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Let Judah mourn; let her gates fall apart. They are wailing for the land;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their cries for Jerusalem are going up.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:30; Psalms 40:14; 2 Kings 18:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their mighty ones send out their servants for water.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When they go to the trenches, they cannot find water. They all return unsuccessful;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they cover their heads ashamed and dishonored.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joel 1:19–20; Joel 1:11; Jeremiah 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because of this the ground is cracked, for there is no rain in the land.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The plowmen are ashamed and cover their heads.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 29:9; Job 39:1–4; Isaiah 15:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For even the doe leaves her young in the fields and abandons them, for there is no grass.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:24; Job 39:5–6; Joel 1:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wild donkeys stand on the bare plains and they pant in the wind like jackals.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their eyes fail to work, for there is no vegetation.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 25:11; Hosea 5:5; Jeremiah 14:20–21"> <span className="pl pl-2">Even though our iniquities testify against us, Yahweh, act for the sake of your name.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">For our faithless actions increase; we have sinned against you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:13; Jeremiah 50:7; Isaiah 43:3"> <span className="pl pl-2">You are the Hope of Israel, the one who saves him in the time of distress,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">why will you be like a stranger in the land, like a foreign wanderer who stretches out and spends just one night?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 63:19; Isaiah 59:1; Jeremiah 15:16"> <span className="pl pl-2">Why are you like someone who is astounded, or like a warrior who has no power to rescue?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You are in our midst, Yahweh, and your name is called over us. Do not leave us!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Hosea 8:13; Psalms 119:101"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh says this to this people: “Since they love to wander, they have not held back their feet from doing so.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh is not pleased with them. Now he calls to mind their iniquity and has punished their sins.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:16; Jeremiah 11:14; Exodus 32:32–34"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh said to me, “Do not pray for good on behalf of this people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:11; Ezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 1:28"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">For if they fast, I will not listen to their wailing, and if they offer up burnt offerings and food offerings, I will not take pleasure in them. For I will put an end to them by sword, famine, and plague.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 4:10; 2 Peter 2:1"> Then I said, “Oh, Lord Yahweh! Behold! The prophets are saying to the people, 'You will not see the sword; there will be no famine for you, for I will give you true security in this place.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6–7; Ezekiel 12:24; Jeremiah 29:8–9"> Yahweh said to me, “The prophets prophesy deceit in my name. I did not send them out, nor did I give them any command or speak to them. But deceitful visions and useless, deceitful divination coming from their own minds are what they are prophesying to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12–13; Ezekiel 14:10; Jeremiah 23:14–15"> Therefore Yahweh says this, “About the prophets prophesying in my name but whom I did not send out—those who say there will be no sword or famine in this land: These prophets will perish by sword and famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Proverbs 1:31; Jeremiah 15:2–3"> Then the people to whom they prophesied will be thrown out in the streets of Jerusalem because of famine and sword, for there will be no one to bury them—them, their wives, their sons, or their daughters—for I will pour out their wickedness on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:21; Jeremiah 9:1; Jeremiah 13:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Say this word to them: 'Let my eyes flow with tears, night and day.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not let them stop, for there will be a great collapse of the virgin daughter of my people—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a great and incurable wound.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:15; Lamentations 1:20; Jeremiah 8:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I go out to the field, there are the ones who were killed by the sword!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If I come to the city, there are the diseases that are caused by famine.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Both the prophet and the priest wander about the land, and they do not know.'”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Lamentations 5:22; Jeremiah 8:15; Jeremiah 6:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Have you completely rejected Judah? Do you hate Zion?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why will you afflict us when there is no healing for us? We hoped for peace, but there was nothing good—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and for a time of healing, but see, there is only terror.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:25; Psalms 32:5; 1 John 1:7–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">We admit, Yahweh, our offenses, the iniquity of our ancestors, for we have sinned against you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:7; Psalms 106:45; Jeremiah 3:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not reject us! For the sake of your name, do not make your glorious throne a disgrace.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Remember and do not break your covenant with us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joel 2:23; Jeremiah 5:24; Psalms 135:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do any of the worthless idols of the nations bring rain? Or can the skies themselves send down showers?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Are you not the one, Yahweh our God? We wait for you, for you are the one who does all these things.</span></span>
