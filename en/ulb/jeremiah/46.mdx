<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Zechariah 2:8; Jeremiah 4:7"> This is the word of Yahweh that came to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 23:29; Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 25:19"> For Egypt: “This is about the army of Pharaoh Necho, king of Egypt that was at Carchemish by the Euphrates river. This was the army that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon defeated in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 21:5; Joel 3:9; Nahum 2:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Get the small shields and the large shields ready, and go forward to fight.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:9–11; Jeremiah 51:3; 1 Samuel 17:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Put the harness on the horses; mount up on the horses and take your stand with your helmets on;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">polish the spears and put on your armor.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:29; Jeremiah 6:25; Jeremiah 46:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">What am I seeing here? They are filled with terror and are running away, for their soldiers are defeated.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are running for safety and are not looking back. Terror is all around—this is Yahweh's declaration—</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 11:19; Jeremiah 46:12; Psalms 27:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">the swift cannot run away, and the soldiers cannot escape.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They stumble in the north and fall beside the Euphrates River.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:2; Isaiah 8:7–8; Daniel 11:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who is this who rises like the Nile, whose waters toss up and down like the rivers?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 10:13–16; Ezekiel 32:2; Isaiah 37:24–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Egypt rises like the Nile, like rivers of water that rise and fall.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Egypt says, 'I will go up and I will cover the earth. I will destroy cities and their inhabitants.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 66:19; Ezekiel 27:10; Nahum 3:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Go up, horses. Be angry, you chariots. Let the soldiers go out,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Cush and Put, men skillful with a shield, and Ludim, men skillful at bending their bows.'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:42; Joel 1:15; Jeremiah 46:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">That day will be the day of vengeance for the Lord Yahweh of hosts, and he will avenge himself on his foes.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The sword will devour and be satisfied. It will drink its fill of their blood. For there will be a sacrifice</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to the Yahweh Lord of hosts in the northern land by the Euphrates River.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:22; Micah 1:9; Nahum 3:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Go up to Gilead and obtain medicine, virgin daughter of Egypt.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is useless that you put much medicine on yourself. There is no cure for you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nahum 3:8–10; Isaiah 19:2; Jeremiah 46:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The nations have heard of your disgrace. The earth is filled with your laments,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for soldier stumbles against soldier; both of them fall together.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:30; Jeremiah 43:10–13; Isaiah 19:1–25"> This is the word that Yahweh told Jeremiah the prophet when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came and attacked the land of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:10; Jeremiah 44:1; Jeremiah 46:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Announce in Egypt, and proclaim it in Migdol, Memphis, and Tahpanhes.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">'Take your station and prepare yourself, for the sword will devour those around you.'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 66:15–16; Psalms 68:2; Psalms 18:39"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why are your mighty ones face-down on the ground? They will not stand, because I, Yahweh, have pushed them to the ground.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 26:36–37; Jeremiah 51:9; Jeremiah 46:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">He increases the numbers of those who stumble. Each soldier falls against the next one. They are saying, 'Get up. Let us go home.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let us go back to our own people, to our native land. Let us leave this sword that is beating us down.'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11–16; Exodus 15:9; 1 Kings 20:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">They proclaimed there, 'Pharaoh the king of Egypt is only a noise, one who has let his opportunity slip away.'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:15; Judges 4:6; Joshua 19:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">As I live—declares the King, whose name is Yahweh of hosts—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">someone will come like Mount Tabor and Mount Carmel by the sea.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 48:18; Ezekiel 30:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pack for yourselves baggage to carry into exile, you who live in Egypt.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For Memphis will become a waste, it will lie in ruins and no one will live there.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hosea 10:11; Jeremiah 47:2; Jeremiah 1:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Egypt is a very beautiful young cow, but a stinging insect is coming from the north. It is coming.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:5; Obadiah 1:13; 2 Kings 7:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The hired soldiers in her midst are like a fattened bull, but they will also turn away and run away.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will not stand together, for the day of their disaster is coming against them, the time of their punishment.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 14:8; Isaiah 29:4; Micah 1:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Egypt hisses like a snake and crawls away, for her enemies are marching against her.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are going toward her like woodcutters with axes.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 6:5; Joel 2:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will cut down the forests—this is Yahweh's declaration—although it is very dense.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the enemies will be more numerous than locusts, unable to be counted.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:15; Ezekiel 29:1–21; Jeremiah 46:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The daughter of Egypt will be made ashamed. She will be given into the hand of people from the north.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:11; Jeremiah 43:12–13; Isaiah 20:5–6"> Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel, says, “See, I am about to punish Amon of Thebes, Pharaoh, Egypt and her gods, her kings the Pharaohs, and those who trust in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:11; Jeremiah 44:30; Ezekiel 29:8–14"> I am giving them into the hand of the ones seeking their lives, and into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and his servants. Then after this Egypt will be inhabited as in previous days—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5; Jeremiah 30:10–11; Isaiah 43:1"> But you, my servant Jacob, do not fear. Do not be dismayed, Israel, for see, I am about to bring you back from far away, and your offspring from the land of their captivity. Then Jacob will return, find peace, and be secure, and there will be no one to terrify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:32; Revelation 3:19; Jeremiah 10:24"> You, my servant Jacob, do not fear—this is Yahweh's declaration—for I am with you, so I will bring complete destruction against all the nations where I scattered you. But I will not destroy you completely. Yet I will discipline you justly and will certainly not leave you unpunished.”</span>
