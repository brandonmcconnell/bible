<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Jeremiah 4:2; Hosea 4:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Although they say, 'As Yahweh lives,' yet they are swearing falsely.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:30; Jeremiah 7:28; Isaiah 9:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, do your eyes not look for faithfulness? <span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Hosea 4:6; Isaiah 28:9–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I said, “Surely these are only poor people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are foolish, for they do not know Yahweh's ways, nor their God's decrees.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:20; James 2:5–7; Matthew 19:23–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will go to the important people and declare God's messages to them, for they at least know Yahweh's ways, the decrees of their God.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But they all broke their yoke together; they all tore apart the chains that bound them to God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Habakkuk 1:8; Zephaniah 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">So a lion from a thicket will attack them. Anyone who goes outside his city will be torn apart.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For their transgressions increase. Their acts of faithlessness are unlimited.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Joshua 23:7; Jeremiah 2:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why should I pardon these people?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your sons have abandoned me and have made oaths by what are not gods.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I fed them fully, but they committed adultery and walked in great numbers to the houses of prostitutes.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:27; Deuteronomy 5:18; 2 Samuel 11:2–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">They were horses in heat. They roamed about wanting to mate. Each man neighed to his neighbor's wife.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:9; Jeremiah 5:29; Isaiah 1:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">So should I not punish them—this is Yahweh's declaration—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and should I not avenge myself on a nation that is like this?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:27; Matthew 22:7; Amos 9:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Go up onto her vineyards' terraces and destroy. But do not bring complete destruction to them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Trim their vines, since those vines do not come from Yahweh.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:20; Isaiah 48:8; Hosea 6:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the houses of Israel and Judah have completely betrayed me—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Ezekiel 12:22–28; Jeremiah 23:14–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have spoken falsely about Yahweh and they said, “He will do nothing;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">no harm will come upon us, and we will not see sword or famine.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:15; Job 8:2; Jeremiah 14:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The prophets will become wind, the word is not in them, so let what they say be done to them.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:29; Jeremiah 1:9; Hosea 6:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">So Yahweh, the God of hosts says this, “Because you have said this, see,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I am about to place my word in your mouth. It will be like a fire, and this people will be like wood! For it will consume them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:49; Jeremiah 4:16; Isaiah 33:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold! I am about to bring a nation against you from far away, house of Israel—this is Yahweh's declaration—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it is a lasting nation, an ancient nation! It is a nation whose language you do not know,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor will you understand what they say.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 5:28; Psalms 5:9; Romans 3:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Its quiver is like an open tomb. They are all soldiers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 26:16; Deuteronomy 28:33; Hosea 8:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">So your harvest will be consumed, your sons and daughters also, and your food.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will eat your flocks and cattle; they will eat the fruit from your vines and fig trees.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will beat down with a sword your fortified cities that you trusted in.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:10; Romans 11:1–5; Jeremiah 4:27"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">But even in those days—this is Yahweh's declaration—I do not intend to destroy you completely.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 9:8–9; Deuteronomy 28:47–48; Jeremiah 22:8–9"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">When you, Israel and Judah, say, 'Why has Yahweh our God done all these things to us?' then you, Jeremiah, will say to them, 'Just as you abandoned Yahweh and served foreign gods in your land, so you must also serve strangers in a land that is not your own.'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Report this to the house of Jacob and let it be heard in Judah. Say,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 28:26; Ezekiel 12:2; Isaiah 6:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">'Hear this, you foolish people who have no understanding;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who have eyes but you cannot see, and you have ears but you cannot hear.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Psalms 104:9; Job 26:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do you not fear me—this is Yahweh's declaration—or tremble before my face?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have placed a border of sand against the sea, an ongoing decree that it does not violate—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">even though the sea rises and falls, still it does not violate it. Even though its waves roar, they do not cross it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:28; Deuteronomy 21:18; Psalms 95:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">But this people has a stubborn heart. It turns away in rebellion and goes away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Joel 2:23; Genesis 8:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they do not say in their hearts, “Let us fear Yahweh our God, the one who brings the rain</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">—the early rain and the late rains—in their right time, keeping the fixed weeks of the harvest for us.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 59:2; Psalms 107:17; Psalms 107:34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your iniquities kept these things from happening. Your sins have stopped good from coming to you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 1:11; Psalms 64:5; Ezekiel 22:2–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">For wicked men are found with my people. They watch as someone crouches to capture birds;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they set a trap and catch people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 1:12; Jeremiah 9:6; Hosea 12:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Like a cage full of birds, their houses are full of deceit.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So they grow large and become rich.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 1:23; Deuteronomy 32:15; Jeremiah 7:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have become fat; they shine with well-being. They crossed over all bounds of wickedness. They do not plead the cause of the people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or the cause of the orphan. They prosper even though they have not given justice to the needy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:9; Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 9:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Should I not punish them for these things—this is Yahweh's declaration—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and will I not take vengeance for myself on a nation like this?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hosea 6:10; Jeremiah 23:14; Isaiah 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Atrocities and horrors have occurred in the land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6; Jeremiah 14:14; Micah 2:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The prophets prophesy with deceit, and the priests rule with their own power.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">My people love it this way, but what will happen in the end?'”</span></span>
