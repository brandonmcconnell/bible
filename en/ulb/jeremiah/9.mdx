<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:9; Jeremiah 5:29; Isaiah 1:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Should I not punish them because of these things—this is Yahweh's declaration—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and should I not avenge myself on a nation that is like this?</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Ezekiel 7:16–18; Deuteronomy 28:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the sound of wailing is heard in Zion, 'How we are devastated.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">We are greatly ashamed, for we have abandoned the land since they tore down our houses.'</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:11; Ezekiel 9:5–6; Jeremiah 15:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">For death has come through our windows; it goes into our palaces.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It destroys children from outside, and young men in the city squares.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:2; Jeremiah 25:33; Psalms 83:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Declare this, 'This is Yahweh's declaration—the corpses of men will fall like dung in the fields,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and like grain stalks after the reapers, and there will be no one to gather them.'”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27–29; Isaiah 5:21; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh says this, “Do not let the wise man take pride in his wisdom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or the warrior in his might. Do not let the wealthy man take pride in his riches.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:17; Galatians 6:14; 1 Corinthians 1:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">For if a man takes pride in anything, let it be in this, that he has insight and knows me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I am Yahweh, who acts with covenant loyalty, justice and righteousness on earth. For it is in these that I take pleasure</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">—this is Yahweh's declaration.”</span></span>
