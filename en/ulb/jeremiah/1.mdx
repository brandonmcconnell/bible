<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:60; Jeremiah 11:21; 2 Chronicles 36:21"> These are the words of Jeremiah son of Hilkiah, one of the priests at Anathoth in the land of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 13:20; Jonah 1:1; Jeremiah 1:11"> The word of Yahweh came to him in the days of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:2; 2 Chronicles 36:5–8; 2 Kings 23:34"> It also came in the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, until the fifth month of the eleventh year of Zedekiah son of Josiah, king of Judah, when the people of Jerusalem were taken away as prisoners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:16; Ezekiel 1:3; Jeremiah 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">The word of Yahweh came to me, saying,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 139:16; Isaiah 49:1; Galatians 1:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Before I formed you in the womb, I chose you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">before you came out from the womb I set you apart; I made you a prophet to the nations.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 6:12; Exodus 4:10–16; 1 Kings 3:7–9"> “Ah, Lord Yahweh!” I said, “I do not know how to speak, for I am too young.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Ezekiel 3:27; Numbers 22:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">But Yahweh said to me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Do not say, 'I am too young.' You must go everywhere I send you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you must say whatever I command you!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 51:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not be afraid of them, for I am with you to rescue you—this is Yahweh's declaration.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Exodus 4:11–12; Luke 12:12"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then Yahweh reached out with his hand, touched my mouth, and said to me, “Now, I have placed my word in your mouth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Isaiah 44:26–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am appointing you today over nations and over kingdoms,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to uproot and break down, to destroy and overthrow, to build and plant.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 7:8; Amos 8:2; Zechariah 5:2"> The word of Yahweh came to me, saying, “What do you see, Jeremiah?” I said, “I see an almond branch.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:28; Ezekiel 12:25; Ezekiel 12:22–23"> Yahweh said to me, “You have seen well, for I am watching over my word to carry it out.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 11:7; Zechariah 4:2"> The word of Yahweh came to me a second time, saying, “What do you see?” I said, “I see a heated pot, whose surface is churning, tipping away from the north.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 41:25; Jeremiah 4:6; Jeremiah 10:22"> Yahweh said to me, “Disaster will be opened up out of the north on all who live in this land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:10; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 9:11"> For I am calling all the tribes of the northern kingdoms, declares Yahweh. They will come, and everyone will set his throne at the entrance of the gates of Jerusalem, against all the walls that surround it, and against all the cities of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:9; Jeremiah 19:4; Acts 7:41"> I will pronounce sentence against them for all their evil in forsaking me, in burning incense to other gods, and in worshiping what they made with their own hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:7–8; 1 Kings 18:46; 1 Peter 1:13"> Get yourself ready! Stand up and say to them whatever I command you. Do not be shattered before them, or I will shatter you before them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 50:7; Ezekiel 3:8–9; Jeremiah 15:20"> Behold! Today I have made you a fortified city, an iron pillar, and bronze walls against the whole land—against the kings of Judah, its officials, its priests, and the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Jeremiah 1:8; Jeremiah 20:11"> They will fight against you, but they will not defeat you, for I will be with you to rescue you—this is Yahweh's declaration.”</span>
