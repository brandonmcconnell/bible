<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 65:16; Jeremiah 9:24; Genesis 22:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">You must be truthful, just, and righteous when you swear, 'As Yahweh lives.'</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then the nations will bless themselves in him, and in him they will glory.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Luke 8:7; Luke 8:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">For Yahweh says this to each person in Judah and Jerusalem:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">'Plow your own ground,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and do not sow among thorns.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:16; Deuteronomy 30:6; Romans 2:28–29"> Circumcise yourselves to Yahweh,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">and remove the foreskins of your heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">men of Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem, or else my fury will break out like fire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and burn with no one to quench it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because of the wickedness of your deeds.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:14; Jeremiah 6:1; Joshua 10:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Report in Judah and let it be heard in Jerusalem.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Say, “Blow the trumpet in the land.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Proclaim, “Gather together. Let us go to the fortified cities.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:2; Jeremiah 1:13–15; Jeremiah 51:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lift up the signal flag and point it toward Zion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and run for safety!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not stay, for I am bringing disaster</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from the north and a great collapse.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; Jeremiah 5:6; Isaiah 6:11"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">A lion is coming out from his thicket</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and someone who will destroy nations is setting out.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He is leaving his place to bring horror to your land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to turn your cities into ruins, where no one will live.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 22:12; Jeremiah 6:26; Isaiah 10:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because of this, wrap yourself in sackcloth, lament and wail.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the force of Yahweh's anger has not turned away from us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:9–16; Isaiah 29:9–10; Isaiah 22:3–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then it will happen in that day—this is Yahweh's declaration—that the hearts of the king and his officials will die. The priests will be appalled, and the prophets will be horrified.'”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–12; Isaiah 37:35"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">So I said, “Ah! Lord Yahweh. Surely you have completely deceived this people and Jerusalem by saying, 'There will be peace for you.' Yet the sword is striking against their life.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 13:15; Ezekiel 17:10; Jeremiah 23:19"> In that time it will be said of this people and Jerusalem, “A burning wind from the plains of the desert will make its way to the daughter of my people. It will not winnow or cleanse them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:16; Ezekiel 5:8; Ezekiel 6:11–13"> A wind far stronger than that will come at my command, and I will now pass sentence against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 19:1; Isaiah 66:15; Habakkuk 1:8"> See, he is attacking like clouds, and his chariots are like a storm. His horses are faster than eagles. Woe to us, for we will be devastated!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 55:7; Acts 8:22"> Cleanse your heart from wickedness, Jerusalem, so that you might be saved. How long will your deepest thoughts be about how to sin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:16; Joshua 17:15; Judges 18:29"> For a voice is bringing news from Dan, and the coming disaster is heard from the mountains of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:15; Ezekiel 21:22; Isaiah 39:3"> Make the nations think about this: See, announce to Jerusalem that besiegers are coming from a distant land to shout in battle against the cities of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:23; 2 Kings 25:1–4; Nehemiah 9:30"> They will be like the watchmen of a cultivated field against her all around, since she has been rebellious against me—this is Yahweh's declaration—</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:19; Psalms 107:17; Proverbs 1:31"> and your conduct and your deeds have done these things to you. This will be your punishment. How terrible it will be! It will strike your very heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Isaiah 16:11; Isaiah 22:4"> My heart! My heart! I am in anguish in my heart. My heart is turbulent within me. I cannot keep quiet for I hear the sound of the horn, an alarm for battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Matthew 10:28; Joel 1:15"> Disaster follows after disaster; for all the land lies in ruins. Suddenly my tents are destroyed, my curtains in a moment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:10; 2 Chronicles 36:3; 2 Chronicles 36:6–7"> How long will I see the standard? Will I hear the sound of the horn?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; Romans 16:19; Jeremiah 5:21"> For the foolishness of my people—they do not know me. They are idiotic people and they have no understanding. They have skill at evil, but do not know to do good.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Mark 13:24–25; Isaiah 13:10"> I saw the land. Behold! It was formless and empty. For there was no light for the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:20; Isaiah 5:25; Nahum 1:5–6"> I looked at the mountains. Behold, they were trembling, and all the hills were shaking about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:2–3; Jeremiah 12:4; Jeremiah 9:10"> I looked. Behold, there was no one, and all the birds of the heavens had fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Jeremiah 12:4; Jeremiah 14:2–6"> I looked. Behold, the orchards were a wilderness and all the cities had been pulled down before Yahweh, before the fury of his wrath.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:28; Jeremiah 5:10; Jeremiah 5:18"> This is what Yahweh says, “All the land will become a devastation, but I will not completely destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 50:3; Hosea 4:3"> For this reason, the land will mourn, and the heavens above will darken. For I have declared my intentions; I will not hold back; I will not turn from carrying them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19–21; Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 52:7"> Every city will flee from the noise of the cavalry and the archers with a bow; they will run into the forests. Every city will climb up into the rocky places. The cities will be abandoned, for there will be no one to inhabit them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 9:30; Lamentations 1:2; Lamentations 1:19"> Now that you have been devastated, what will you do? For though you dress in scarlet, adorn yourself with gold jewelry, and make your eyes look bigger with paint, the men who lusted for you now reject you. Instead, they are trying to take away your life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 1:17; Isaiah 1:15; Jeremiah 13:21"> So I hear the sound of anguish, distress as in the birth of a firstborn child, the sound of the daughter of Zion. She is gasping for breath. She spreads out her hands, 'Woe to me! I am fainting because of these murderers.'”</span>
</div>
