<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 44:9–20; Leviticus 18:30; 1 Peter 1:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the religious customs of these people are worthless.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They cut down a tree in the forest, and the craftsman carves the wood.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 46:7; Isaiah 40:19–20; Psalms 115:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then they decorate it with silver and gold. They strengthen it with hammer and nails so it will not fall over.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 46:7; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Isaiah 41:23–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">What they make with their hands is like scarecrows in a cucumber field, because they, too, can say nothing, and they have to be carried because they cannot walk.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not fear them, for they cannot bring about evil, nor are they able to do anything good.'”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 96:4; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 48:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is no one like you, Yahweh. You are great, and your name is great in power.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 15:4; Psalms 22:28; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who does not fear you, king of the nations? <span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 10:2; Jeremiah 10:14; Psalms 115:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">They are all the same, they are brutish and stupid, disciples of idols that are nothing but wood.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 115:4; Daniel 10:5; 1 Kings 10:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">They bring hammered silver from Tarshish, and gold from Uphaz made by artificers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the hands of refiners. <span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Isaiah 57:15; Nahum 1:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">But Yahweh is the true God. <span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 96:5; Isaiah 2:18; Lamentations 3:66"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will speak to them like this, “The gods that did not make the heavens and earth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will perish from the earth and from under these heavens.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; Job 9:8; Proverbs 3:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">But it was he who made the earth by his power, and he established the world by his wisdom, and by his understanding he stretched out the heavens.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Job 38:22; Job 38:34–35"> <span className="pl pl-1">His voice makes the roar of waters in the heavens, and he brings up the mists from the ends of the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He makes lightning for the rain and sends out wind from his storehouse.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 135:16–18; Psalms 115:4–8; Isaiah 46:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Every man has become ignorant, without knowledge. <span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:22; Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 51:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">They are useless, the work of mockers; they will perish at the time of their punishment.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:9; Jeremiah 32:18; Jeremiah 31:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">But God, the portion of Jacob, is not like these, for he is the molder of all things.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Israel is the tribe of his inheritance; Yahweh of hosts is his name.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:3–12; Jeremiah 6:1; Matthew 24:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Gather your bundle and leave the land, you people who have been living under the siege.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:29; Zechariah 1:6; Deuteronomy 28:63–64"> <span className="pl pl-1">For Yahweh says this, “See, I am about to throw the inhabitants of the land out this time.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will cause them distress, and they will find it to be so.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 7:9; Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 14:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to me! Because of my broken bones, my wound is infected.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So I said, “Surely this is agony, but I must bear it.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; Jeremiah 31:15; Isaiah 54:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">My tent is devastated, and all of my tent cords are cut in two.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They have taken my children away from me, so they no longer exist. There is no longer anyone to spread out my tent or to raise up my tent curtains.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2–10; Jeremiah 12:10; Jeremiah 23:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the shepherds are stupid and they do not seek Yahweh;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so they have not prospered, and all their flock has been scattered.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Habakkuk 1:6–9; Jeremiah 6:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">The report of news has arrived, “See! It is coming, a great earthquake is coming from the land of the north</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">To make the cities of Judah into ruins, hideouts for jackals.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 20:24; Proverbs 16:1; Psalms 37:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">I know, Yahweh, that the way of a man does not come from himself. No person walking directs his own steps.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 38:1; Psalms 6:1; Jeremiah 30:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Discipline me, Yahweh, with justice, not in your anger or you would destroy me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 79:6–7; Jeremiah 8:16; Job 18:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pour your fury on the nations that do not know you and on the families that do not call on your name.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For they have devoured Jacob and consumed him so as to completely destroy him and demolish his habitation.</span></span>
