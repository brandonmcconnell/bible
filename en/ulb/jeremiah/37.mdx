<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:24; 2 Kings 24:17; 2 Kings 24:12"> Now Zedekiah son of Josiah reigned as king instead of Jehoiachin son of Jehoiakim. Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had made Zedekiah king over the land of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 24:19–20; Proverbs 29:12; 2 Chronicles 36:12–16"> But Zedekiah, his servants, and the people of the land did not listen to the words of Yahweh that he proclaimed by the hand of Jeremiah the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:1–2; Jeremiah 29:25; Jeremiah 42:20"> So King Zedekiah, Jehukal son of Shelemiah, and Zephaniah son of Maaseiah the priest sent a message to Jeremiah the prophet. They said, “Pray on our behalf to Yahweh our God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:15; Jeremiah 32:2–3"> Now Jeremiah was coming and going among the people, for he had not yet been put in prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:15; Jeremiah 37:11; Jeremiah 34:21"> Pharaoh's army came out from Egypt, and the Chaldeans who were besieging Jerusalem heard the news about them and left Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then the word of Yahweh came to Jeremiah the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Lamentations 4:17; 2 Kings 22:18; Ezekiel 17:17"> “Yahweh, God of Israel, says this: This is what you will say to the king of Judah, because he has sent you to seek advice from me, 'See, Pharaoh's army, which came to help you, is about to go back to Egypt, its own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:2–8; Jeremiah 38:23; Jeremiah 34:21–22"> The Chaldeans will return. They will fight against this city, capture it, and burn it.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Obadiah 1:3; Jeremiah 29:8; Galatians 6:3"> Yahweh says this: Do not deceive yourselves by saying, 'Surely the Chaldeans are leaving us,' for they will not leave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:45; Isaiah 30:17; Joel 2:11"> Even if you had defeated the entire Chaldean army that is fighting you so that only wounded men were left in their tents, they would get up and burn this city.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:5"> So it was when the Chaldean army had left Jerusalem as Pharaoh's army was coming,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:1; Nehemiah 6:11; 1 Kings 19:3"> then Jeremiah went out from Jerusalem to go to the land of Benjamin. He wanted to take possession of a tract of land there among his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 14:10; Jeremiah 38:7; Luke 23:2"> As he was in the Benjamin Gate, a chief guard was there. His name was Irijah son of Shelemiah son of Hananiah. He grabbed hold of Jeremiah the prophet and said, “You are deserting to the Chaldeans.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 52:1–2; Jeremiah 40:4–6; Psalms 27:12"> But Jeremiah said, “That is not true. I am not deserting to the Chaldeans.” But Irijah did not listen to him. He took Jeremiah and brought him to the officials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:26; Jeremiah 20:1–3; Jeremiah 26:16"> The officials were angry with Jeremiah. They beat him and put him in prison, which had been the house of Jonathan the scribe, for they had turned it into a prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:6; Jeremiah 38:10–13; Lamentations 3:53"> So Jeremiah was put into an underground cell, where he stayed for many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:7; Jeremiah 24:8; 1 Kings 14:1–4"> Then King Zedekiah sent someone who brought him to the palace. In his house, the king asked him privately, “Is there any word from Yahweh?” Jeremiah answered, “There is a word: You will be given into the hand of the king of Babylon.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 25:8; John 10:32; Daniel 6:22"> Then Jeremiah said to King Zedekiah, “How have I sinned against you, your servants, or this people so that you have placed me in prison?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Jeremiah 28:10–17; Jeremiah 6:14"> Where are your prophets, the ones who prophesied for you and said the king of Babylon will not come against you or against this land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:7; Jeremiah 38:6–9; Acts 28:18–19"> But now listen, my master the king! Let my pleas come before you. Do not return me to the house of Jonathan the scribe, or I will die there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:13; Jeremiah 38:28; Jeremiah 38:9"> So King Zedekiah gave an order. His servants confined Jeremiah in the courtyard of the guard. A loaf of bread was given him every day from the street of the bakers, until all the bread in the city was gone. So Jeremiah stayed in the courtyard of the guard.</span>
