<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word that came to Jeremiah from Yahweh, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 1:1; Acts 9:6; Jeremiah 19:1–2"> “Arise and go out to the potter's house, for I will have you hear my word there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 15:14; Jonah 1:3; Acts 26:19"> So I went out to the potter's house, and behold! The potter was working on the potter's wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:20–23; Jeremiah 18:6; Isaiah 45:9"> But the object of clay that he was molding was ruined in his hand, so he changed his mind and made another object that seemed good in his eyes to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then the word of Yahweh came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Romans 9:20–21; Jeremiah 18:4"> “Should I not be able to act like this potter with you, house of Israel?—this is Yahweh's declaration. Behold! Like clay in a potter's hand—that is how you are in my hand, house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 12:14–17; Jeremiah 45:4"> At one moment, I may proclaim something about a nation or a kingdom, that I will drive it out, tear it down, or destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:21; Jeremiah 26:13; Jonah 3:9–10"> But if the nation about which I have made that proclamation turns from its evil, then I will relent from the disaster that I was planning to bring upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Amos 9:11–15; Jeremiah 1:10"> At another moment, I may proclaim something about a nation or a kingdom, that I will build it up or plant it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:18; 1 Samuel 2:30; Ezekiel 18:24"> But if it does evil in my eyes by not listening to my voice, then I will stop the good that I had said I would do for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 17:13; Isaiah 55:6–7; Jeremiah 35:15"> So now, speak to the men of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem and say, 'Yahweh says this: See, I am about to form disaster against you. I am about to devise a plan against you. Repent, each person from his wicked way, so your ways and your practices will bring good to you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:25; Jeremiah 7:24; Deuteronomy 29:19"> But they will say, 'This is no use. We will act according to our own plans. Each one of us will do what his evil, stubborn heart desires.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:30; Hosea 6:10; Jeremiah 23:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore Yahweh says this, 'Ask the nations, who has ever heard of such a thing as this?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The virgin Israel has committed a horrible act.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 6:68"> <span className="pl pl-1">Does the snow in Lebanon ever leave the rocky hills on its sides?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Are the mountain streams coming from far away ever destroyed, those cold streams?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; Jeremiah 2:32; Hosea 11:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet my people have forgotten me. They have made offerings to useless idols and been made to stumble in their paths;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they have left the ancient paths to walk lesser paths.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 22:7; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 19:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their land will become a horror, an object of everlasting hissing.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Everyone who passes by her will shudder and shake his head.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:24; Jeremiah 2:27; Job 27:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will scatter them before their enemies like an eastern wind. I will show them my back, and not my face, on the day of their disaster.'”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 52:2; Jeremiah 11:19; Malachi 2:7"> So the people said, “Come, let us make plots against Jeremiah, since the law will never perish from the priests, or advice from the wise men, or words from the prophets. Come, let us attack him with our words and no longer pay attention to anything he proclaims.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:9; Jeremiah 20:12; Psalms 55:16–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pay attention to me, Yahweh, and listen to the voice of my enemies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:7; Psalms 57:6; Psalms 106:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will disaster from them really be my reward for being good to them? For they have dug a pit for me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Remember how I stood before you to speak for their welfare, to cause your fury to turn away from them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 109:9–20; Lamentations 5:3; Amos 4:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore give their children over to famine, and give them to the hands of those who use the sword.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So let their women become bereaved and widows, and their men be killed, and their young men killed by the sword in battle.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Psalms 140:5; Zephaniah 1:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let a distressed shout be heard from their houses, as you suddenly bring raiders against them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For they have dug a pit to capture me and have hidden traps for my feet.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 69:22–28; Nehemiah 4:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you, Yahweh, you know all of their plans against me to kill me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not forgive their iniquities and sins. Do not wipe their sins away from you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Instead, let them be overthrown before you. Act against them in the time of your wrath.</span></span>
