<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:8; Jeremiah 40:6; Psalms 41:9"> But it happened that in the seventh month Ishmael son of Nethaniah son of Elishama, from the royal family, and some officers of the king, came—ten men were with him—to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, at Mizpah. They ate food together there in Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 25:25; Jeremiah 40:5; Psalms 109:5"> But Ishmael son of Nethaniah, and the ten men who were with him rose up and attacked Gedaliah son of Ahikam son of Shaphan, with the sword. Ishmael killed Gedaliah, whom the king of Babylon had put in charge of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 25:25; Lamentations 1:2; Jeremiah 41:11–12"> Then Ishmael killed all the Judeans who were with Gedaliah in Mizpah and the Chaldean fighting men found there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:11; Psalms 52:1–2"> Then it was the second day after the killing of Gedaliah, but no one knew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 16:24; Deuteronomy 14:1; Joshua 18:1"> Some men came from Shechem, from Shiloh, and from Samaria—eighty men who had shaved their beard, torn their clothes, and cut themselves—with food offerings and frankincense in their hands to go to Yahweh's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:16; Jeremiah 50:4; 2 Samuel 1:2–16"> So Ishmael son of Nethaniah went out from Mizpah to meet them as they went, walking and weeping. Then it happened that as he encountered them, he said to them, “Come to Gedaliah son of Ahikam!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Psalms 55:23; Ezekiel 22:27"> It came about that when they came into the city, Ishmael son of Nethaniah slaughtered them and threw them into a pit, he and the men who were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 2:4; Proverbs 13:8; Philippians 3:7–9"> But there were ten men among them who said to Ishmael, “Do not kill us, for there are provisions of ours in a field: Wheat and barley, oil and honey.” So he did not kill them with their other companions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 6:2; 1 Kings 15:17–22; 1 Samuel 13:6"> The cistern where Ishmael threw all the dead bodies that he had killed, was a large cistern that King Asa dug to make a defense against King Baasha of Israel. Ishmael son of Nethaniah filled it in with the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:14; Jeremiah 40:7; Nehemiah 2:10"> Next Ishmael captured all the other people who were in Mizpah, the king's daughters and all the people who were left in Mizpah whom Nebuzaradan the chief guard had assigned to Gedaliah son of Ahikam. So Ishmael son of Nethaniah captured them and went to cross over to the people of Ammon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:13–16; Jeremiah 40:7–8; Jeremiah 42:1"> But Johanan son of Kareah and all the army commanders with him heard of all the harm that Ishmael son of Nethaniah had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:13; Genesis 14:14–16; 1 Samuel 30:1–8"> So they took all their men and went to fight against Ishmael son of Nethaniah. They found him at the great pool of Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then it happened that when all the people who were with Ishmael saw Johanan son of Kareah and all the army commanders who were with him, they were very happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So all the people whom Ishmael had captured at Mizpah turned around and went to Johanan son of Kareah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 21:30; Proverbs 28:17; 1 Kings 20:20"> But Ishmael son of Nethaniah fled with eight men from Johanan. He went to the people of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:4–7; Jeremiah 42:8; Jeremiah 41:10"> Johanan son of Kareah and all the army commanders with him took from Mizpah all the people who had been rescued from Ishmael son of Nethaniah. This was after Ishmael had killed Gedaliah son of Ahikam. Johanan and his companions took the strong men, the fighting men, the women and children, and the eunuchs who had been rescued at Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:37–38; Jeremiah 42:14; Jeremiah 43:7"> Then they went and stayed for a while in Geruth Kimham, which is near Bethlehem. They were going to go to Egypt</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:5; Luke 12:4–5; Jeremiah 42:16"> because of the Chaldeans. They were afraid of them since Ishmael son of Nethaniah had killed Gedaliah son of Ahikam, whom the king of Babylon had put in charge of the land.</span>
</div>
