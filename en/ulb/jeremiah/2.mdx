<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 14:4; James 1:18; Isaiah 41:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Israel was set apart to Yahweh, the firstfruits of his harvest!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All who ate from the firstfruits were held guilty; disaster came upon them—this is Yahweh's declaration.'”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Micah 6:1; Isaiah 51:1–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear the word of Yahweh, house of Jacob, all you clans of the house of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 17:15; Romans 1:21; Jeremiah 10:14–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh says this, “What did your fathers find wrong with me, that they went far from following me?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That they went after useless idols and became useless themselves?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:10; Deuteronomy 8:14–16; Hosea 13:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">They did not say, 'Where is Yahweh, who brought us up from the land of Egypt?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Where is Yahweh, who led us to the wilderness, into the land of the Arabah and pits,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in a land of drought and deep darkness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a land through which no one passes and where no one lives?'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Jeremiah 16:18; Numbers 13:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I brought you to the land of Carmel, to eat its fruit and other good things!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yet when you came, you defiled my land, you made my inheritance an abomination!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18; Jeremiah 5:31; Jeremiah 10:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">The priest did not say, 'Where is Yahweh?' and the experts in the law did not care about me!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The shepherds transgressed against me. The prophets prophesied for Baal and walked after unprofitable things.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:35–36; Jeremiah 2:35; Exodus 20:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I will still accuse you—this is Yahweh's declaration—and I will accuse your sons' sons.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 120:5; 1 Chronicles 23:1; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">For cross over to the coasts of Kittim and look. Send messengers out to Kedar and find out</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and see if there has ever before been anything like this.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 1:23; Isaiah 37:19; Jeremiah 16:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Has a nation exchanged gods, even though they were not gods?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But my people have exchanged their glory for what cannot help them.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Instead of their glory , some ancient copies have my glory .">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Jeremiah 22:29; Jeremiah 6:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Shudder, heavens, because of this! Be shocked and horrified—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 4:14; John 7:37; Jeremiah 17:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my people have committed two evils against me: They have abandoned the springs of living waters,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they have dug out cisterns for themselves,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">broken cisterns that cannot hold water.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 4:22; Isaiah 50:1; Genesis 15:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Is Israel a slave? Was he born in his master's home? So why has he become plunder?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 50:17; Jeremiah 9:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Young lions roared against him. They made a lot of noise and made his land a horror.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His cities are destroyed without any inhabitants.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 43:7–9; Jeremiah 44:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Also, the people of Memphis and Tahpanhes will shave your skull.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:18; Jeremiah 2:19; Jeremiah 2:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Did you not do this to yourselves</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when you abandoned Yahweh your God, while he was leading you along the way?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 13:3; Isaiah 31:1; Jeremiah 2:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">So now, why take the road to Egypt and drink the waters of Shihor?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why take the road to Assyria and drink the waters of the Euphrates River?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 3:9; Jeremiah 4:18; Jeremiah 2:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your wickedness rebukes you, and your faithlessness punishes you. So think about it and understand that it is wicked and bitter</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when you forsake Yahweh your God, and have no fear of me—this is the declaration of Lord Yahweh of hosts.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:2; Leviticus 26:13; Jeremiah 17:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I broke your yoke that you had in ancient days; I tore your fetters off you. Still you said,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">'I will not serve!' since you bowed down on every high hill and beneath every leafy tree, you adulterer.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:4; Exodus 15:17; Psalms 44:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I planted you as a choice vine, completely from pure seed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">How then have you have changed yourself into a corrupt, worthless vine?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:1; Job 14:17; Hosea 13:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">For even if you clean yourself in the river or wash with strong soap,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">your iniquity is a stain before me—this is the declaration of the Lord Yahweh.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:31; Proverbs 30:12; Proverbs 30:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">How can you say, 'I am not defiled! I have not walked after the Baals'?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Look at what you did in the valleys! Consider what you have done—you are a swift female camel running here and there,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:6; Jeremiah 2:27; Hosea 5:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">a wild donkey accustomed to the wilderness, in her heat sniffing the wind!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Who can restrain her lust? None of the males need wear themselves out pursuing her; at mating time they will find her.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16; Jeremiah 18:12; Jeremiah 3:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">You must restrain your feet from becoming bare and your throat from being thirsty!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But you have said, 'It is hopeless! No, I love strangers and go after them!'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezra 9:7; Nehemiah 9:32–34; Jeremiah 32:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Like the shame of a thief when he is found, so the house of Israel will be ashamed—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they, their kings, their princes, and their priests and prophets!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:16; Hosea 7:14; Psalms 78:34–37"> <span className="pl pl-1">These are the ones who say to the tree, 'You are my father,' and to the stone, 'You gave birth to me.'</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For their back faces me and not their faces. Nevertheless, they say in the time of troubles, 'Arise and save us!'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:13; Deuteronomy 32:37; Judges 10:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet where are the gods that you made for yourselves? Let them arise if they wish to save you in your time of troubles,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for your idols equal your cities in number, Judah!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:13; Daniel 9:11; Jeremiah 5:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">So why do you accuse me of doing wrong? All of you have sinned against me—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26; 1 Thessalonians 2:15; Acts 7:52"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have punished your people in vain. They would not accept discipline.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your sword has devoured your prophets like a destructive lion!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:7–9; Deuteronomy 32:15; Micah 6:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">You who belong to this generation! Pay attention to my word, the word of Yahweh! Have I been a wilderness to Israel?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or a land of deep darkness? Why would my people say, 'Let us wander around, we will not go to you anymore'?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 106:21; Jeremiah 13:25; Jeremiah 3:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will a virgin forget her jewelry, a bride her veils?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yet my people have forgotten me for days without number!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 57:7–10; Jeremiah 2:36; Ezekiel 16:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">How well you make your way to look for love.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You have even taught your ways to wicked women.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Kings 21:16; Jeremiah 19:4; 2 Kings 24:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">The blood that was the life of innocent, poor people has been found on your clothes.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">These people were not discovered in acts of burglary.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; 1 John 1:8–10; Job 33:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet you say, 'I am innocent; surely his anger has turned away from me.'</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But look! I will bring down judgment on you because you say, 'I have not sinned.'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:22; Hosea 5:13; 2 Chronicles 28:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why do you treat so very lightly this change in your ways?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You will also be disappointed by Egypt, just as you were by Assyria.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:19; Jeremiah 37:7–10; Jeremiah 17:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will also go out from there dejected, with your hands on your head,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for Yahweh has rejected the ones whom you trusted, so you will not be helped by them.”</span></span>
