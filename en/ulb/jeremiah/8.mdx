<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:5; 2 Kings 23:16; Jeremiah 7:32–34"> “At that time—this is Yahweh's declaration—they will bring out from the graves the bones of the kings of Judah and its officials, the bones of the priests and the prophets, and the bones of the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 23:5; Jeremiah 9:22; Deuteronomy 4:19"> Then they will spread them out in the light of the sun and moon and all the stars of the skies; these things in the sky that they have followed and served, that they have walked after and sought, and that they have worshiped. The bones will not be gathered or buried again. They will be like dung on the surface of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 9:6; Jeremiah 29:14; Jeremiah 23:3"> In every remaining place where I have driven them, they will choose death instead of life for themselves, all who are still left over from this evil nation—this is the declaration of Yahweh of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 24:16; Micah 7:8; Hosea 6:1"> So say to them, 'Yahweh says this: Does anyone fall and not get up? Does anyone get lost and not try to return?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:6; Jeremiah 5:3; Zechariah 7:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why has this people, Jerusalem, turned away in permanent faithlessness?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They hold on to treachery and refuse to repent.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 14:2; Ezekiel 18:28; Luke 15:17–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">I paid attention and listened, but they did not speak right; no one was sorry for his wickedness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">no one who says, “What have I done?” All of them go where they wish,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like a stallion rushing toward battle.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:3; Jeremiah 5:4–5; Proverbs 6:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even the stork in heaven knows the right times; and the doves, swifts, and cranes.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They go on their migrations at the right time, but my people do not know Yahweh's decrees.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 9:41; Job 5:12–13; Isaiah 10:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">How can you say, “We are wise, for the law of Yahweh is with us”?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Indeed, see! The deceitful pen of the scribes has created deceit.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:15; Psalms 119:98–100; Job 5:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wise men will be ashamed. They are dismayed and trapped.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Behold! They reject Yahweh's word, so what use is their wisdom?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:12–13; Ezekiel 33:31; Isaiah 56:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I will give their wives to others, and their fields to those who will possess them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because from the least to the greatest, all of them are greedy for dishonest gain! From the prophet to the priest, all of them practice deceit.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Ezekiel 13:10–16; Lamentations 2:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">They healed the wounds of my people lightly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">saying, “Peace, Peace,” when there was no peace.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:3; Isaiah 3:9; Jeremiah 6:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Were they ashamed when they practiced abominations? They were not ashamed; they did not know how to blush! So they will fall among the fallen; they will be brought down when they are punished, says Yahweh.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Joel 1:7; Jeremiah 17:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will remove them completely—this is Yahweh's declaration—there will be no grapes on the vine, nor will there be figs on the fig trees. For the leaf will wither, and what I have given to them will pass away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:15; Jeremiah 9:15; Lamentations 3:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why are we sitting here? Come together; let us go to the fortified cities, and we will become silent there in death.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For Yahweh our God will silence us. He will make us drink poison, since we have sinned against him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:19; Jeremiah 4:10; Jeremiah 8:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">We are hoping for peace, but there will be nothing good.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">We are hoping for a time of healing, but see, there will be terror.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 18:29; Judges 5:22; Jeremiah 4:15–16"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">The snorting of his stallions is heard from Dan. The whole earth shakes at the sound of the neighing of his strong horses.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For they will come and consume the land and its wealth, the city and the ones living in it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:11; Psalms 58:4–5; Deuteronomy 32:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">For see, I am sending out snakes among you, vipers that you cannot charm.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will bite you—this is Yahweh's declaration.'”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 1:16–17; Jeremiah 6:24; Isaiah 22:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">My sorrow has no end, and my heart is sick.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 39:3; Isaiah 13:5; Psalms 135:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold! The screaming voice of the daughter of my people from a land far away! Is Yahweh not in Zion?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Is her king no longer there? Why then do they provoke me to anger with their carved figures and their worthless foreign idols?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; Matthew 25:1–12; Luke 13:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">The harvest has passed on, summer is over. But we have not been saved.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:17; Joel 2:6; Nahum 2:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am hurt because of the hurt of the daughter of my people. I mourn at the horrible things that have happened to her; I am dismayed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:11; Genesis 37:25; Jeremiah 51:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Is there no medicine in Gilead? Is there no healer there?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why will the healing of the daughter of my people not happen?</span></span>
