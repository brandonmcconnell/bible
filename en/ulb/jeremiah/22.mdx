<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:2; Luke 3:19–20; 2 Samuel 24:11–12"> This is what Yahweh says, “Go down to the house of the king of Judah and proclaim this word there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 1:32; Isaiah 9:7; Jeremiah 22:4"> Say, 'King of Judah, listen to the word of Yahweh—you who sit on David's throne—you, and your servants, and your people who come through these gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:12; Micah 6:8; Jeremiah 22:17"> Yahweh says this, “Perform justice and righteousness, and anyone who has been robbed—rescue him from the hand of the oppressor. Do not mistreat any foreigner in your land, or any orphan or widow. Do not commit violence or pour out innocent blood in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:25"> For if you truly do these things, then kings sitting on David's throne will enter the gates of this house riding in a chariot and on horses, he, his servants, and his people!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 22:16"> But if you do not listen to these words from me that I have announced—this is Yahweh's declaration—then this royal house will become a ruin.”'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Isaiah 6:11; Isaiah 27:10"> For Yahweh says this concerning the house of the king of Judah,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'You are like Gilead, or like the summit of Lebanon to me. Yet I will turn you into a wilderness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">into cities with no inhabitants.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 37:24; Isaiah 10:33–34; Jeremiah 4:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I have designated destroyers to come against you! Men with their weapons</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will cut off the best of your cedars and let them fall into the fire.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 9:8–9; 2 Chronicles 7:20–22; Deuteronomy 29:23–26"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then many nations will pass by this city. Each person will say to the next, “Why has Yahweh acted in this way toward this great city?”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 22:17; 2 Chronicles 34:25; Jeremiah 50:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then the other will answer, “Because they abandoned the covenant of Yahweh their God and bowed down to other gods and worshiped them.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:2; Jeremiah 22:11; Isaiah 57:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not weep for the one who is dead or mourn for him; but weep bitterly for him who is about to go away,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because he will never return and see his native land again.'</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:15; 2 Chronicles 36:1–4; 2 Kings 23:34"> For Yahweh says this about Jehoahaz son of Josiah king of Judah, who served as king instead of Josiah his father, 'He has gone from this place and will not come back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 23:34; Jeremiah 22:18"> He will die there in the place to where they have exiled him, and he will never again see this land.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 5:4; Micah 3:10; Leviticus 19:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to him who builds his house by unrighteousness, his upper rooms by injustice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who makes his neighbor work for him for nothing, and he does not give him his wages;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Isaiah 5:8–9; Haggai 1:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">he says, 'I will build for myself a large house with spacious upper rooms.'</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So he cuts out large windows for it, and he panels it with cedar, and he paints it red.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 23:25; Isaiah 3:10; Ecclesiastes 10:17"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">Is this what makes you a good king, that you wanted to have boards of cedar?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Did not your father also eat and drink, yet do justice and righteousness? Then things went well for him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:24; Isaiah 1:17; 1 John 2:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">He judged in favor of the poor and needy. It was good then. Is this not what it means to know me?—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 2:14; 2 Kings 24:4; Jeremiah 22:3"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">But there is nothing in your eyes and heart except worry for your unjust profit and for pouring out innocent blood,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for producing oppression and crushing of others.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 13:30; Jeremiah 16:6; Jeremiah 16:4"> Therefore this is what Yahweh says about Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will not lament for him, saying,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">'Woe, my brother!' or 'Woe, my sister!'</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will not lament for him, saying,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">'Woe, master!' or 'Woe, majesty!'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:30; 1 Kings 21:23–24; 2 Chronicles 36:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will be buried with a donkey's burial,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">dragged away and thrown out beyond the gates of Jerusalem.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 27:12; Ezekiel 23:22; Lamentations 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Go up Lebanon's mountains and shout. Lift your voice in Bashan.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Shout from the Abarim mountains, for all of your friends will be destroyed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:30; Jeremiah 3:25; Jeremiah 7:22–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">I spoke to you when you were safe, but you said, 'I will not listen.'</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">This was your custom since your youth, for you have not listened to my voice.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:30–31; Jeremiah 12:10; Jeremiah 23:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wind will shepherd away all your shepherds, and your friends will go into captivity.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then you will certainly be ashamed and humiliated by all of your evil deeds.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:24; Jeremiah 4:30–31; Jeremiah 22:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You who live in 'Lebanon,' who is nestled in cedar buildings,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">how you will be pitied when the labor pains come upon you, pain like that of a woman in labor!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:1; Song of Songs 8:6; 1 Chronicles 3:16"> “As I live—this is Yahweh's declaration—even if you, Jehoiachin son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, were the signet on my right hand, I would tear you off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:20–21; Jeremiah 21:7; 2 Kings 24:15–16"> For I have given you to the hand of the ones seeking your life and to the hand of those before whom you are afraid, even to the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 24:15; Jeremiah 15:2–4; Isaiah 22:17"> I will throw you and your mother who bore you into another land, a country where you were not born, and there you will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:14; 2 Kings 25:27–30; Psalms 86:4"> About this land to which they will want to return, they will not come back here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 31:12; Hosea 8:8; Jeremiah 48:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">Is this a despised and shattered vessel? Is this man Jehoiachin a pot that pleases no one?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why have they thrown him and his descendants out, and have poured them out into a land that they did not know?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Micah 1:2; Jeremiah 6:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Land, Land, Land! Hear the word of Yahweh!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:30; Psalms 94:20; Matthew 1:11–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh says this, 'Write about this man Jehoiachin: He will be childless.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will not prosper during his days, and no one among his descendants will achieve success</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or ever again sit on David's throne and rule over Judah.'”</span></span>
</div>
