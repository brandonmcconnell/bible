<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 137:9; Numbers 21:27–30; Esther 8:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Moab has been destroyed. Her children have made their cries heard.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 45:16; Judges 11:24; Jeremiah 48:39"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then Moab will be ashamed of Chemosh just as the house of Israel was ashamed of Bethel, their object of trust.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 13:22; Jeremiah 1:12; Ezekiel 12:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Moab's disaster is soon to happen; calamity is hurrying quickly.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 13:17; Jeremiah 48:1; Numbers 32:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">to Kiriathaim, Beth Gamul, and Beth Meon,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 2:2; Jeremiah 48:41; Joshua 21:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">To Kerioth and Bozrah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and to all the cities in the land of Moab—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the farthest and the closest cities.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 75:10; Psalms 10:15; Zechariah 1:19–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">The horn of Moab has been hacked off; its arm has been broken—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:42; Isaiah 29:9; Jeremiah 51:39"> Make him drunk, because he acted proudly against Yahweh. Let Moab wallow in his vomit, and let him be an object of ridicule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:26; Micah 7:8–10; Zephaniah 2:8"> For did not Israel become an object for laughter to you? Was he found among thieves, so that you shook your head at him as often as you spoke about him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 55:6–7; Judges 6:2; Song of Songs 2:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Abandon the cities and camp on the cliffs, inhabitants of Moab.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Become like a dove that is nesting over the mouth of a hole in the rocks.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 16:6; Psalms 138:6; Isaiah 2:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">We have heard of Moab's pride—his arrogance,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">his haughtiness, his pride, his self-glory and the conceit in his heart.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:36; Isaiah 16:6; Isaiah 37:28–29"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">This is Yahweh's declaration—I myself know his defiant speech, which amounts to nothing, like his deeds.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4–6; Lamentations 4:1; Lamentations 2:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">How it has been shattered! How they howl in their lamenting! <span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 8:8; Deuteronomy 28:49; Jeremiah 49:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">For Yahweh says this, “See, the enemy will come flying like an eagle, spreading out his wings over Moab.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 21:3; Isaiah 13:8; Micah 4:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Kerioth has been captured, and its strongholds have been seized.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For in that day the hearts of Moab's soldiers will be like the hearts of women in birth labor.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:2; Isaiah 7:8; Isaiah 37:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">So Moab will be destroyed and be no longer a people, because he made himself to be great against Yahweh.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Lamentations 3:47; Isaiah 24:17–18; Deuteronomy 32:23–25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Terror and the pit, and a trap are coming on you, inhabitant of Moab—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; Numbers 21:28; Zechariah 10:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">The ones who flee will stand in the shadow of Heshbon without any strength, for fire will go out from Heshbon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">flame from the middle of Sihon. It will devour the forehead of Moab and the top of the heads of the boastful people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 21:29; Jeremiah 48:7; Judges 11:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to you, Moab! Chemosh's people are destroyed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For your sons are taken as captives and your daughters into captivity.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:39; Jeremiah 49:6; Jeremiah 23:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I will restore the fortunes of Moab in later days—this is Yahweh's declaration.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The judgment on Moab ends here.</span></span>
