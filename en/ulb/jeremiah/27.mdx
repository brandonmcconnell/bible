<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:3; Jeremiah 28:1; Jeremiah 27:12"> In the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah son of Josiah, king of Judah, this word came to Jeremiah from Yahweh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Although most Hebrew copies have Jehoiakim , most modern versions have Zedekiah , because the events in this chapter occur during his reign.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 7:1; Amos 7:4; Jeremiah 27:12"> This is what Yahweh said to me, “Make fetters and a yoke for yourself. Place them on your neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 1:9–3; Ezekiel 25:1–17; Ezekiel 17:15–21"> Then send them out to the king of Edom, the king of Moab, the king of the people of Ammon, the king of Tyre, and to the king of Sidon. Send them by the hand of those kings' ambassadors who have come to Jerusalem to Zedekiah king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:19; Jeremiah 10:10; Jeremiah 25:27"> Give commands to them for their masters and say, 'Yahweh of hosts, God of Israel, says this: This is what you must say to your masters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Jeremiah 51:15; Psalms 115:15–16"> “I myself made the earth by my great strength and my raised arm. I also made the people and animals on the earth, and I give it to anyone who is right in my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:14; Jeremiah 25:9; Daniel 2:37–38"> So now, I myself am giving all these lands into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, my servant. Also, I am giving the living things in the fields to him to serve him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:31; Revelation 13:5–10; Daniel 5:25–31"> For all the nations will serve him, his son, and his grandson until the time for his land comes. Then many nations and great kings will subdue him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:21; Ezekiel 17:19–21; Jeremiah 38:17–19"> So the nation and the kingdom that does not serve Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, and that does not put its neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon—I will punish that nation with the sword, with famine, and with the plague—this is Yahweh's declaration—until I have destroyed it by his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14; Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 29:8"> So do not listen to your prophets, your diviners, your seers, your soothsayers, and sorcerers, who have been speaking to you and saying, 'Do not serve the king of Babylon.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:31; Jeremiah 28:16; Jeremiah 23:25"> For they are prophesying deceit to you in order to send you far away from your lands, for I will drive you away, and you will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:8; Jeremiah 27:2; Jeremiah 38:2"> But the nation that places its neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon and serves him, I will allow it to rest in its land—this is Yahweh's declaration—and they will cultivate it and make their homes in it.”'”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:1; Jeremiah 38:17; 2 Chronicles 36:11–13"> So I spoke to Zedekiah king of Judah and gave him this message, “Place your necks under the yoke of the king of Babylon and serve him and his people, and you will live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:31; Jeremiah 27:8; Proverbs 8:36"> Why will you die—you and your people—by the sword, famine, and plague, just as I have declared about the nation that refuses to serve the king of Babylon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; Jeremiah 29:8–9"> Do not listen to the words of the prophets who speak to you and say, 'Do not serve the king of Babylon,' for they are prophesying lies to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:10; 2 Chronicles 25:16; Jeremiah 6:13–15"> 'For I have not sent them out—this is Yahweh's declaration—for they are prophesying deceit in my name so that I will drive you out and you will perish, both you and the prophets who are prophesying to you.'”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 24:13; Jeremiah 28:3; Daniel 1:2"> I proclaimed this to the priests and all the people and said, “Yahweh says this: Do not listen to the words of your prophets who prophesy to you and say, 'Look! The objects belonging to Yahweh's house are being returned from Babylon now!' They are prophesying lies to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:17; Jeremiah 38:23; Jeremiah 7:34"> Do not listen to them. You should serve the king of Babylon and live. Why should this city become a ruin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:8; Jeremiah 18:20; 1 Samuel 12:19"> If they are prophets, and if the word of Yahweh has truly come to them, let them beg Yahweh of hosts not to send to Babylon the objects that remain in his house, the house of the king of Judah, and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 25:13; Jeremiah 52:17–23; 2 Kings 25:17"> Yahweh of hosts says this about the pillars, the large basin known as “The Sea” and its base, and the rest of the objects that remain in this city—</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:1; 2 Chronicles 36:10; 2 Kings 24:14–16"> the objects that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon did not take when he carried Jehoiachin son of Jehoiakim, the king of Judah, into captivity from Jerusalem to Babylon with all the nobles of Judah and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Yahweh of hosts, God of Israel, says this about the objects that remain in the house of Yahweh, the house of the king of Judah, and Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Ezra 7:19; Jeremiah 32:5"> 'They will be brought to Babylon, and they will remain there until the day I have set to come for them—this is Yahweh's declaration—then I will bring them up and restore them to this place.'”</span>
</div>
