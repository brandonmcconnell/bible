<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:21; Zechariah 11:15–17; Ezekiel 34:2–10"> “Woe to the shepherds who destroy and scatter the sheep of my pasture—this is Yahweh's declaration.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 32:34; Jeremiah 21:12; Micah 7:4"> Therefore Yahweh, the God of Israel, says this concerning the shepherds who are shepherding his people, “You have scattered my flock and have driven them away. You have not cared for them. So I am about to punish you for the evil you have done—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 9:14–15; Jeremiah 32:37; Isaiah 43:5–6"> I myself will gather the remnant of my flock from all of the lands where I have driven them, and I will return them to a grazing place, where they will be fruitful and increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:14–15; 1 Peter 1:5; Ezekiel 34:23–31"> Then I will raise up shepherds over them who will shepherd them so they will no longer fear or be shattered. None of them will go missing—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Isaiah 4:2; Isaiah 9:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">See, the days are coming—this is Yahweh's declaration—when I will raise up for David a righteous branch.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will reign as king; he will act wisely and cause justice and righteousness in the land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Jeremiah 33:16; Philippians 3:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">In his days Judah will be rescued, and Israel will live in security.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then this is the name by which he will be called: Yahweh is our righteousness.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:14–15; Jeremiah 23:3; Isaiah 43:18–19"> Therefore see, days are coming—this is Yahweh's declaration—when they will no longer say, 'As Yahweh lives, who brought the people of Israel up from the land of Egypt.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5–6; Amos 9:14–15; Ezekiel 34:13"> Instead they will say, 'As Yahweh lives, who brought up and who led back the descendants of the house of Israel from the northern land and all the lands where they had been driven.' Then they will live in their own land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Lamentations 3:15; Psalms 60:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Regarding the prophets, my heart is broken in me, and all of my bones tremble. I have become like a drunk man,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like a man whom wine has overpowered, because of Yahweh and his holy words.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 4:2–3; Psalms 107:34; Jeremiah 9:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the land is full of adulterers. Because of these the land is dried up.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The meadows in the wilderness dry up. These prophets' paths are wicked; their power is not used in a right manner.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:4; Jeremiah 6:13; Matthew 21:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">“For both the prophets and the priests are polluted. I even found their wickedness in my house!—this is Yahweh's declaration—</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:16; Jeremiah 11:23; Psalms 35:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">therefore their way will be like a slippery place in the darkness. They will be pushed down. They will fall in it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I will send disaster against them in the year of their punishment—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:8; Isaiah 9:16; 1 Kings 18:18–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I have seen the prophets in Samaria doing what is repulsive:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They prophesied by Baal and led my people Israel astray.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9–10; Jeremiah 29:23; Ezekiel 22:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Among the prophets in Jerusalem I have seen horrible things:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They commit adultery and walk in deceit.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They strengthen the hands of evildoers; no one turns back from his evildoing.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All of them have become like Sodom to me and its inhabitants like Gomorrah!”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:14; Jeremiah 9:15; Lamentations 3:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore Yahweh of hosts says this concerning the prophets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Look, I am about to make them eat wormwood and drink poisonous water,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for pollution has gone out from the prophets of Jerusalem to all the land.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14; Ezekiel 13:6; Galatians 1:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh of hosts says this, “Do not listen to the words of the prophets who prophesy to you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They have deluded you! They are announcing visions from their own minds, not from Yahweh's mouth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Jeremiah 13:10; Amos 9:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">They are constantly saying to those who dishonor me, 'Yahweh declares there will be peace for you.'</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For everyone walking in the stubbornness of his own heart says, 'Disaster will not come upon you.'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:22; Isaiah 40:13–14; Amos 3:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet who has stood in Yahweh's council meeting? Who sees and hears his word?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Who pays attention to his word and listens?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:23; Jeremiah 25:32; Amos 1:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">See, there is a storm coming from Yahweh! His fury is going out, and a tempest is whirling about.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is whirling around the heads of the wicked.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:24; Isaiah 55:11; Zechariah 1:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh's wrath will not return until it has carried out and brought into being his heart's intentions.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">In the final days, you will understand it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14; Jeremiah 27:15; Jeremiah 23:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">I did not send out these prophets. They just appeared.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I did not proclaim anything to them, but they have still prophesied.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:18; Zechariah 1:4; Jeremiah 25:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">For if they had stood in my council meeting, they would have caused my people to hear my word;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they would have caused them to turn from their wicked words and corrupt practices.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 139:1–10; 1 Kings 20:23; 1 Kings 20:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Am I only a God nearby—this is Yahweh's declaration—and not also a God far away?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Psalms 139:7; Psalms 90:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Can anyone hide in a secret place so I cannot see him?—this is Yahweh's declaration—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and do I not fill the heavens and the earth?—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:32; Jeremiah 23:28; Jeremiah 29:8"> I have heard what the prophets have said, those who were prophesying deceit in my name. They said, 'I had a dream! I had a dream!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:1–2; Jeremiah 14:14; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> How long will this go on, prophets who prophesy lies from their minds, and who prophesy from the deceit in their hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 8:33–34; Judges 3:7; Acts 13:8"> They are planning on making my people forget my name with the dreams that they report, each one to his neighbor, just as their ancestors forgot my name in favor of Baal's name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 1 Timothy 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:12–13"> The prophet who has a dream, let him report the dream. But the one to whom I have declared something, let him declare my word truthfully. What does straw have to do with grain?—this is Yahweh's declaration—</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Jeremiah 5:14; Luke 24:32"> Is not my word like fire?—this is Yahweh's declaration—and like a hammer that shatters a rock into pieces?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; Ezekiel 13:8; Jeremiah 14:14–15"> So see, I am against the prophets—this is Yahweh's declaration—anyone who steals words from another person and says they come from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:17; 2 Chronicles 18:10–12; 2 Chronicles 18:5"> See, I am against the prophets—this is Yahweh's declaration—who use their tongues to prophesy proclamations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:7–18; Zephaniah 3:4; Lamentations 2:14"> See, I am against the prophets who dream deceitfully—this is Yahweh's declaration—and then proclaim them and in this way mislead my people with their deceit and boasting. I am against them, for I have not sent them out nor given them commands. So they will certainly not help this people—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Malachi 1:1; Jeremiah 12:7; Habakkuk 1:1"> When these people, or a prophet, or a priest asks you, 'What is the burden of Yahweh?' you will say to them, 'You are the burden, and I will cast you off'—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 13:3; Lamentations 2:14"> As for the prophets, priests, and people who are saying, 'This is the burden of Yahweh' I will punish that man and his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Jeremiah 42:4; Jeremiah 33:3"> You continue to say, each person to his neighbor and each man to his brother, 'What did Yahweh answer?' and 'What did Yahweh declare?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:10; 2 Peter 3:16; Isaiah 3:8"> But you must no longer talk about the 'burden of Yahweh,' for the burden is every man's own word, and you have perverted the words of the living God, Yahweh of hosts, our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> This is what you will say to the prophet, 'What answer did Yahweh give you? or 'What did Yahweh say?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:13–14"> But if you say, 'The burden of Yahweh', this is what Yahweh says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'Because you have said these words, 'The burden of Yahweh,' when I sent to you, saying, 'You will not say, “The burden of Yahweh,”'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:33; Ezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 13:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">therefore, behold, I am about to pick you up and throw you away from me, along with the city that I gave you and your ancestors.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:11; Ezekiel 5:14–15; Jeremiah 42:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I will put everlasting shame and insult on you that will not be forgotten.'”</span></span>
</div>
