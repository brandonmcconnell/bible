<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:22; Jeremiah 30:24; Zechariah 13:9"> “At that time—this is Yahweh's declaration—I will be the God of all the clans of Israel, and they will be my people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 10:33; Psalms 95:11; Matthew 11:28"> Yahweh says this,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The people who have survived the sword have found favor in the wilderness; I will go out to give rest to Israel.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 103:17; Isaiah 54:8–9; Hosea 11:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh appeared to me in the past and said, “I have loved you, Israel, with everlasting love.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So I have drawn you toward myself with covenant faithfulness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:7; Jeremiah 31:13; Amos 5:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will build you up again so you will be built, virgin Israel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You will again pick up your tambourines and go out with happy dances.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 9:14; Leviticus 19:23–25; Deuteronomy 28:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will plant vineyards again on the mountains of Samaria; the farmers will plant and put the fruit to good use.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:4–5; Micah 4:1–3; Jeremiah 50:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">For a day will come when the watchmen in the mountains of Ephraim will proclaim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">'Arise, let us go up to Zion to Yahweh our God.'</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 14:7; Psalms 28:9; Isaiah 37:31"> For Yahweh says this, “Shout for joy over Jacob! Shout in gladness for the chief people of the nations! Let praise be heard. Say, 'Yahweh has rescued his people, the remnant of Israel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Isaiah 42:16; Jeremiah 23:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">See, I am about to bring them from the northern lands. I will gather them from the farthest parts of the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The blind and lame will be among them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">pregnant women and those who are about to give birth will be with them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">A great assembly will return here.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:4; Jeremiah 3:4; Exodus 4:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will come weeping; I will lead them as they make their pleas. I will have them journey to streams of water</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on a straight road. They will not stumble on it, for I will be a father to Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Ephraim will be my firstborn.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; Ezekiel 34:12; Deuteronomy 30:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Hear the word of Yahweh, nations. Report along the coasts in the distance.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You nations must say, 'The one who scattered Israel is gathering her up and keeping her as a shepherd keeps his sheep.'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Titus 2:14; Isaiah 44:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">For Yahweh has ransomed Jacob and has redeemed him from the hand that was too strong for him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 35:10; Isaiah 58:11; Joel 3:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then they will come and rejoice on the heights of Zion. Their faces will shine because of Yahweh's goodness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">over the corn and the new wine, over the oil and the offspring of the flocks and herds.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For their lives will become like a watered garden, and they will never again feel any more sorrow.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 30:11; Isaiah 61:3; Isaiah 51:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then virgins will rejoice with dancing, and young and old men will be together.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I will change their mourning into celebration. I will have compassion on them and cause them to rejoice instead of sorrowing.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:25; 1 Peter 2:9; Jeremiah 33:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I will saturate the lives of the priests in abundance.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">My people will fill themselves with my goodness—this is Yahweh's declaration.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 2:16–18; Jeremiah 40:1; Psalms 77:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh says this: “A voice is heard in Ramah, wailing and bitter weeping.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is Rachel weeping for her children. She refuses to be comforted over them, for they live no longer.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ruth 2:12; Jeremiah 30:3; Hebrews 6:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh says this, “Hold your voice back from weeping and your eyes from tears;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there is a reward for your work—this is Yahweh's declaration—your children will return from the land of the enemy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 3:21; Jeremiah 29:11–16; Lamentations 3:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is hope for your future—this is Yahweh's declaration—your descendants will return inside their borders.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Psalms 80:3; Job 5:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I have certainly heard Ephraim sorrowing, 'You punished me, and I have been punished like an untrained calf.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Bring me back and I will be brought back, for you are Yahweh my God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:31; Jeremiah 3:25; Ezekiel 21:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">For after I turned back to you, I was sorry; after I was trained, I slapped my thigh.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I was ashamed and humiliated, for I have borne the guilt of my youth.'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 55:7; Micah 7:18–19; Isaiah 63:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Is not Ephraim my precious child? Is he not my dear, delightful son?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For whenever I speak against him, I certainly still call him to my loving mind. In this way my heart longs for him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will certainly have compassion on him—this is Yahweh's declaration.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; Isaiah 62:10; Psalms 84:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Place road signs for yourself. Set up guideposts for yourself. Set your mind on the right path,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the way you should take. Come back, virgin Israel! Come back to these cities of yours.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:4; Jeremiah 7:24; Matthew 1:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">How long will you waver, faithless daughter?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For Yahweh has created something new on earth—a woman surrounds a strong man.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3; Isaiah 1:26; Jeremiah 50:7"> Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel, says this, “When I bring back the people to their land, they will say this in the land of Judah and its cities, 'May Yahweh bless you, you righteous place where he lives, you holy mountain.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 8:4–8; Ezekiel 36:10; Zechariah 2:4"> For Judah and all its cities will live together there, as will farmers and those who set out with flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:6; Matthew 11:28; John 4:14"> For I will cause those who are weary to drink, and I fill up those who are faint.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 127:2; Zechariah 4:1–2"> After this I awoke, and I realized that my sleep had been refreshing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; Zechariah 10:9; Ezekiel 36:9"> “Look, the days are coming—this is Yahweh's declaration—when I will sow the houses of Israel and Judah with the descendants of man and beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 18:7–9"> In the past, I kept them under surveillance in order to uproot them and to tear them down, to overthrow, destroy, and bring them harm. But in the coming days, I will watch over them, in order to build them up and to plant them—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Lamentations 5:7; Ezekiel 18:2–3; Jeremiah 31:30"> In those days no one will say any longer,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'Fathers have eaten sour grapes, but the children's teeth are dulled.'</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:20; Isaiah 3:11; Deuteronomy 24:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">For each man will die in his own iniquity; everyone who eats sour grapes, his teeth will be dulled.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 10:16–17; Ezekiel 37:26; Jeremiah 31:31–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look, the days are coming</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">—this is Yahweh's declaration—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I will establish a new covenant</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the house of Israel and the house of Judah.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 24:6–8; Deuteronomy 5:3; Exodus 19:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will not be like the covenant</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that I established with their fathers in the days</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I took them by their hand</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to bring them out from the land of Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they broke my covenant,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">although I was a husband for them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:3; Hebrews 10:16; Hebrews 8:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is the covenant</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that I will establish with the house of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">after these days</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will place my law within them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and will write it on their heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for I will be their God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will be my people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Hebrews 8:12; 1 John 2:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then each man will no longer teach his neighbor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or a man teach his brother and say, 'Know Yahweh!'</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For all of them will know me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the smallest of them to the greatest</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">—this is Yahweh's declaration—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for I will forgive their iniquity</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and will no longer call their sins to mind.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Isaiah 51:15; Psalms 136:7–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh says this,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is he who makes the sun to shine by day</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and fixes the moon and stars to shine by night.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He is the one who sets the sea in motion</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that its waves roar—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh of hosts is his name.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 54:9–10; Jeremiah 33:20–26; Psalms 89:36–37"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Only if these permanent things vanish from my sight—this is Yahweh's declaration—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will Israel's descendants ever stop from forever being a nation before me.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:24–26; Isaiah 40:12; Jeremiah 33:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh says this, “Only if the highest heavens can be measured,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and only if the earth's foundation below can be discovered, will I reject all of Israel's descendants</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because of all that they have done—this is Yahweh's declaration.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 14:10; 2 Kings 14:13; Jeremiah 30:18"> “Look, the days are coming—this is Yahweh's declaration—when the city will be rebuilt for me, from the Tower of Hananel to the Corner Gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Zechariah 2:1–2; Ezekiel 40:8"> Then the measuring line will go out again farther, to the hill of Gareb and around Goah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:23; Joel 3:17; 2 Kings 11:16"> The whole valley of the dead bodies and the ashes, and all the terraced fields going out to the Kidron Valley as far as the corner of the Horse Gate on the east, will be set apart for Yahweh. The city will not be pulled up or overthrown again, forever.”</span>
