<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:14; Ezekiel 14:20–21; Jeremiah 14:11"> Then Yahweh said to me, “Even if Moses or Samuel were standing in front of me, I would still not be in favor of this people. Send them out from before me, for them to go away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:11; Zechariah 11:9; Revelation 13:10"> It will happen that they will say to you, 'Where should we go?' Then you must say to them, 'Yahweh says this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Those who are for death should go to death; those who are for the sword should go to the sword.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Those who are for famine should go to famine; and those who are for captivity should go to captivity.'</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:26; Leviticus 26:22; Leviticus 26:16"> For I will assign them to four groups—this is Yahweh's declaration—the sword to slaughter some, the dogs to drag some away, the birds of the skies and the beast of the earth to consume and destroy some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; 2 Kings 23:26–27; 2 Kings 24:3–4"> I will make of them a horrifying thing to all the kingdoms of the earth, because of what Manasseh son of Hezekiah, king of Judah, did in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 51:19; Nahum 3:7; Jeremiah 21:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">For who will have compassion for you, Jerusalem? Who will grieve for you?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Zephaniah 1:4; Isaiah 1:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have forsaken me—this is Yahweh's declaration—you have gone back from me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So I will strike you with my hand and destroy you. I am tired of having mercy on you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 3:12; Jeremiah 51:2; Isaiah 41:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I will winnow them with a pitchfork at the gates of the land.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will bereave them. I will destroy my people since they will not turn from their ways.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 3:25–1; Jeremiah 6:4–5; Luke 21:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will make their widows number more than the sands of the seashore. Against the mothers of young men I will send</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the destroyer at noonday. I will make shock and horror suddenly fall on them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:5; Isaiah 47:9; Lamentations 1:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">The mother who has borne seven children will waste away. She will gasp. Her sun will set while it is still day.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">She will be ashamed and embarrassed, for I will give those who remain to the sword in the presence of their enemies</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">—this is Yahweh's declaration.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 6:22; Exodus 22:25; Proverbs 26:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to me, my mother! For you have borne me, I who am a man of controversy and argument through all the land.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have not lent, nor has anyone lent to me, but they all curse me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Jeremiah 29:11–14; Psalms 106:46"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh said: “Will I not rescue you for good?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will certainly make your enemies beg for help in the time of calamity and distress.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 40:9; Jeremiah 21:4–5; Isaiah 45:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Can one smash iron? Especially iron from the north that is mixed with bronze?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:3; Psalms 44:12; Isaiah 52:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will give to your enemies your wealth and treasures as free plunder.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will do this because of all your sins committed within all your borders.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:13; Deuteronomy 32:22; Jeremiah 17:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I will make you serve your enemies in a land that you do not know,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for a fire will ignite, kindled in my wrath against you.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Instead of Then I will make you serve your enemies , some ancient copies have Then I will make your enemies take you .">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:3; Psalms 69:7–9; Luke 21:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, you know! Remember me and help me. Bring vengeance for me against those who persecute me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You are patient, but do not allow them to take me away; know that I suffer reproach for your sake.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 10:9–10; Ezekiel 3:1–3; Psalms 119:101–103"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your words have been found, and I consumed them. Your words became to me a joy</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the delight of my heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for I bear your name, Yahweh, God of hosts.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; Lamentations 3:28; Psalms 26:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I did not sit in the circle of those who celebrated or rejoiced.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I sat in solitude because of your powerful hand, for you filled me with indignation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:15; Job 34:6; Micah 1:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why is my pain ongoing and my wound incurable, refusing to be healed?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Will you be like deceitful waters to me, waters that dry up?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 3:7; Luke 21:15; Leviticus 10:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore Yahweh said this, “If you repent, Jeremiah, then I will restore you, and you will stand before me and serve me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For if you separate the foolish things from the precious things, you will be like my mouth. The people will come back to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but you yourself must not go back to them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18–19; Acts 18:9–10; Ezekiel 3:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will make you like an impenetrable bronze wall to this people, and they will wage war against you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But they will not defeat you, for I am with you to save and rescue you—this is Yahweh's declaration—</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Isaiah 49:24–25; Jeremiah 50:34"> <span className="pl pl-1">for I will rescue you from the hand of the wicked and redeem you from the hand of the tyrant.”</span></span>
