<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremiah 14:4; Deuteronomy 28:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">So the showers have been withheld and the spring rains have not come;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">yet you have the forehead of a prostitute; you refuse to be ashamed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:2; Jeremiah 3:19; Psalms 71:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Have you not just now called to me: 'My father! My closest friend even from my youth!</span><br /></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 17:7–17; Ezekiel 16:31; Jeremiah 7:24"> Then Yahweh said to me in the days of Josiah the king, “Do you see what faithless Israel has done? <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:46; Hosea 6:1–4; Hosea 14:1"> I said, 'After she does all these things, she will return to me,' but she did not return. Then her faithless sister Judah saw these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1; Hosea 4:15–17; Isaiah 50:1"> So I saw that, in the same way that faithless Israel had committed adultery and I had sent her away and had given a bill of divorce against her, her faithless sister Judah did not fear; she also went out and acted like a prostitute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:27; Isaiah 57:6; Jeremiah 3:2"> Her prostitution was nothing to her; she defiled the land, and she committed adultery with stones and trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 7:14; Jeremiah 12:2; 2 Chronicles 34:33–18"> Then after all of this, her faithless sister Judah returned to me, not with all her heart, but with a lie—this is Yahweh's declaration.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:11; Ezekiel 16:51–52; Hosea 11:7"> Then Yahweh said to me, “Faithless Israel has been more righteous than faithless Judah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Ezekiel 33:11; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> Go and proclaim these words to the north. Say,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'Return, faithless Israel!—this is Yahweh's declaration—I will not always be angry with you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Since I am faithful—this is Yahweh's declaration—I will not stay angry forever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:25; Jeremiah 3:6; Deuteronomy 12:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Acknowledge your iniquity, for you have transgressed against Yahweh your God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you have shared your ways with strangers under every leafy tree!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For you have not listened to my voice!—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:4; Acts 20:28; Ephesians 4:11–12"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will give you shepherds after my heart, and they will shepherd you with knowledge and insight.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; Amos 9:14–15; Ezekiel 37:26"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then it will happen that you will increase and bear fruit in the land in those days—this is Yahweh's declaration-they will no longer say, “The ark of the covenant of Yahweh!” This matter will no longer come up in their hearts or be remembered; it will not be missed, and another one will not be made.'</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:7; Micah 4:1–5; Jeremiah 17:12"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">In that time they will proclaim about Jerusalem, 'This is Yahweh's throne,' and all the other nations will gather at Jerusalem in Yahweh's name. They will no longer walk in the stubbornness of their wicked hearts.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:8; Hosea 1:11; Amos 9:15"> In those days, the house of Judah will walk with the house of Israel. They will come together from the land of the north to the land that I gave your ancestors as an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:4; Jeremiah 31:9; Isaiah 63:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">As for me, I said, 'How I want to treat you as my son and give you a pleasant land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">an inheritance more beautiful than what is in any other nation!' I would have said, 'You will call me “my father”.'</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I would have said that you would not turn from following me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 48:8; Jeremiah 5:11; Hosea 5:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">But like a woman faithless to her husband,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you have betrayed me, house of Israel—this is Yahweh's declaration.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; Jeremiah 31:9; Jeremiah 2:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">A voice is heard on the plains, the weeping and pleading of the people of Israel!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For they have changed their ways; they have forgotten Yahweh their God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 14:1; Hosea 14:4; Zechariah 13:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Return, faithless people! I will heal you of treachery!”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold! We will come to you, for you are Yahweh our God!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 121:1–2; Psalms 3:8; Isaiah 12:2"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">Surely lies come from the hills, a confusing noise from the mountains;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">surely Yahweh our God is the salvation of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 9:10; Ezekiel 16:63; Ezekiel 16:61"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet shameful idols have consumed what our ancestors have worked for—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their flocks and cattle, their sons and daughters!</span><br /></span>
