<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> These are the words that Yahweh declared concerning Israel and Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:10–11; Amos 8:10; Isaiah 5:30"> “For Yahweh says this,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'We have heard a trembling voice of dread and not of peace.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Zechariah 14:1–2; Malachi 4:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe! <span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nahum 1:13; Ezekiel 34:27; Isaiah 9:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">For it will be in that day—this is the declaration of Yahweh of hosts—that I will break the yoke off your neck, and I will shatter your chains, so foreigners will no longer enslave you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 3:5; Isaiah 55:3–5; Luke 1:69"> <span className="pl pl-1">But they will worship Yahweh their God and serve David their king, whom I will make king over them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:27–28; Isaiah 43:5; Jeremiah 29:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">So you, my servant Jacob, do not fear—this is Yahweh's declaration—and do not be dismayed, Israel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For see, I am about to bring you back from far away, and your descendants from the land of captivity.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Jacob will return and be at peace; he will be secure, and there will be no more terror.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:24; Jeremiah 1:19; Isaiah 8:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I am with you—this is Yahweh's declaration—to save you. Then I will bring a complete end</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to all the nations where I have scattered you. But I will certainly not put an end to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">though I discipline you justly and will certainly not leave you unpunished.'</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:18; Jeremiah 30:15; Jeremiah 14:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">For Yahweh says this, 'Your injury is incurable; your wound is infected.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:11; Jeremiah 8:22; Jeremiah 30:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is no one to plead your case; there is no remedy for your wound to heal you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:6; Job 30:21; Lamentations 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">All of your lovers have forgotten you. They will not look for you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for I have wounded you with the wound of an enemy and the discipline of a cruel master</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because of your many iniquities and your innumerable sins.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:14; Jeremiah 30:12; Jeremiah 6:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why do you call for help for your injury? <span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:25; Isaiah 33:1; Exodus 23:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">So everyone who consumes you will be consumed, and all of your adversaries will go into captivity.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the ones who have plundered you will become plunder, and I will make all of the ones despoiling you a spoil.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Jeremiah 33:6; 1 Peter 2:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I will bring healing on you; I will heal you of your wounds—this is Yahweh's declaration—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will do this because they called you: Outcast. No one cares for this Zion.'”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Haggai 2:7–9; Psalms 102:13; Jeremiah 30:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh says this, “See, I am about to bring back the fortunes of Jacob's tents and have compassion on his homes.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then a city will be built on the heap of ruins, and a stronghold will exist again where it used to be.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:22; Isaiah 51:11; Isaiah 60:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then a song of praise and a sound of merriment will go out from them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for I will increase them and not diminish them; I will honor them so they will not be humbled.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Jeremiah 2:3; Jeremiah 50:33–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then their people will be like before, and their assembly will be established before me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I punish all the ones who are now tormenting them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 16:5; Ezekiel 34:23–24; Acts 5:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their leader will come from among them. He will emerge from their midst</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I draw him near and when he approaches me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If I do not do this, who would dare come close to me?—this is Yahweh's declaration.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 21:3; Ezekiel 36:28; Jeremiah 31:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then you will be my people, and I will be your God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:19–20; Zechariah 9:14; Jeremiah 25:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">See, the tempest of Yahweh, his fury, has gone out. It is a continual tempest.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will whirl on the heads of the wicked people.</span><br /></span>
