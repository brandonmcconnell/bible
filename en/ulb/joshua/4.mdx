<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 3:12; Numbers 1:4–15; 1 Kings 18:31"> “Choose twelve men for yourselves from among the people, one man from each tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 4:19–20; Joshua 3:13; Joshua 4:8"> Give them this command: 'Take up twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan where the priests are standing on the dry ground, and bring them over with you and lay them down in the place where you will spend the night tonight.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 4:2; Mark 3:14–19"> Then Joshua called the twelve men whom he had chosen from the tribes of Israel, one from each tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Joshua said to them, “Go over before the ark of Yahweh your God into the middle of the Jordan. Each of you is to take up a stone upon his shoulder, according to the number of the tribes of the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Joshua 4:21; Psalms 44:1"> This will be a sign in your midst for you when your children ask in days to come, 'What do these stones mean to you?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Numbers 16:40; Exodus 28:12"> Then you will say to them, 'The waters of the Jordan were cut off before the ark of the covenant of Yahweh. When it passed over the Jordan, the waters of the Jordan were cut off. So these stones will be a memorial to the people of Israel forever.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 4:2–5; Joshua 1:16–18; Joshua 4:20"> The people of Israel did just as Joshua commanded, and they picked up twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan, as Yahweh said to Joshua. They set the stones up according to the number of the tribes of the people of Israel. They carried the stones with them, over to the place where they camped and they set them down there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:12; Judges 1:26; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> Then Joshua set up twelve stones in the middle of the Jordan River, in the place where the feet of the priests that carried the ark of the covenant stood. The memorial is there to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 3:13; Psalms 119:60; Proverbs 27:1"> The priests that carried the ark stood in the middle of the Jordan until everything that Yahweh commanded Joshua to tell the people was completed, according to all that Moses had commanded Joshua. The people hurried and they crossed over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 3:8; Joshua 4:18; Joshua 3:17"> When all the people had finished crossing over, the ark of Yahweh and the priests crossed over before the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–32; Joshua 1:14"> The tribe of Reuben, the tribe of Gad, and the half tribe of Manasseh passed before the people of Israel formed up as an army, just as Moses said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 6:11; Jeremiah 39:5; Jeremiah 52:8"> About forty thousand men equipped for war passed before Yahweh, for battle on the plains of Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 3:7; 2 Chronicles 30:12; 1 Kings 3:28"> On that day Yahweh made Joshua great in the eyes of all Israel. They honored him—just as they honored Moses— all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then Yahweh spoke to Joshua,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Exodus 25:16–22; Joshua 3:3–6"> “Command the priests who carry the ark of the testimony to come up out of the Jordan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 8:16–18; Acts 16:35–39; Acts 16:23"> So, Joshua commanded the priests, “Come up out of the Jordan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 3:15; 1 Chronicles 12:15; Joshua 3:13"> When the priests carrying the ark of the covenant of Yahweh came up out of the middle of the Jordan, and the soles of their feet were lifted up out on dry ground, then the waters of the Jordan returned to their place and overflowed its banks, just as they were four days before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 5:9; Micah 6:5; Exodus 12:2–3"> The people came up out of the Jordan on the tenth day of the first month. They stayed in Gilgal, east of Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 4:3; Joshua 4:8"> The twelve stones that they took out of the Jordan, Joshua set up in Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 4:6; Psalms 145:4–7; Psalms 105:2–5"> He said to the people of Israel, “When your descendants ask their fathers in times to come, 'What are these stones?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 3:17; Isaiah 51:10; Isaiah 44:27"> Tell your children, 'This is where Israel crossed over the Jordan on dry ground.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Isaiah 43:16; Isaiah 63:12–14"> Yahweh your God dried up the waters of the Jordan for you, until you had crossed over, just as Yahweh your God did to the Sea of Reeds, which he dried up for us until we passed over,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 106:8; Psalms 89:13; 1 Kings 8:42–43"> so that all the peoples of the earth may know that the hand of Yahweh is mighty, and that you will honor Yahweh your God forever.”</span>
