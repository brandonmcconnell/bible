<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 6:21; Hebrews 7:1; Joshua 8:2"> Now when Adoni-Zedek, king of Jerusalem, heard that Joshua had captured Ai and had completely destroyed it (just as he had done to Jericho and its king), he also heard how the people of Gibeon had made peace with Israel and were living among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:25; Joshua 2:24; Exodus 15:14–16"> The people of Jerusalem were very afraid because Gibeon was a large city, like one of the royal cities. It was larger than Ai, and all its men were mighty warriors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:9; Micah 1:13; Joshua 10:1"> So Adoni-Zedek, king of Jerusalem, sent a message to Hoham, king of Hebron, to Piram, king of Jarmuth, to Japhia, king of Lachish, and to Debir, king of Eglon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 9:15; Joshua 10:1; Isaiah 8:9–10"> “Come up to me and help me. Let us attack Gibeon because they have made peace with Joshua and with the people of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 8:9–10; Genesis 15:16; Joshua 10:6"> The five kings of the Amorites, the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish, and the king of Eglon came up, they and all their armies. They set up their positions against Gibeon, and they attacked it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 9:6; Joshua 5:10; Deuteronomy 1:15"> The people of Gibeon sent a message to Joshua and to the army at Gilgal. They said, “Hurry! Do not withdraw your hands from your servants. Come up to us quickly and save us. Help us, for all the kings of the Amorites who live in the hill country have gathered together to attack us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 8:1; Isaiah 8:12; Isaiah 8:14"> Joshua went up from Gilgal, he and all the men of war with him, and all the fighting men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Deuteronomy 3:2; Psalms 27:1–2"> Yahweh said to Joshua, “Do not be afraid of them. I have given them into your hand. Not one of them will be able to stop your attack.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; 2 Timothy 2:3; Proverbs 24:11–12"> Joshua came upon them suddenly, having marched all night from Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; Joshua 16:3; Joshua 16:5"> Yahweh confused the enemy before Israel, and Israel killed them with a great slaughter at Gibeon and pursued them on the road going up to Beth Horon, and they killed them on the road to Azekah and Makkedah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:2; Judges 5:20; Psalms 18:12–14"> As they ran away from Israel, down the hill from Beth Horon, Yahweh threw large stones down from heaven upon them all the way to Azekah, and they died. There were more who died because of the hailstones than who were killed with the sword by the men of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:11; Isaiah 38:8; Isaiah 60:20"> Then Joshua spoke to Yahweh on the day Yahweh gave the men of Israel victory over the Amorites. This is what Joshua said to Yahweh before Israel,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sun, be still at Gibeon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and moon, in the Valley of Aijalon.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:11; Isaiah 38:8; Joshua 10:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sun stood still, and the moon stopped moving</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">until the nation took vengeance on their enemies.</span><br />Is this not written in the Book of Jashar?<br /><span className="pl pl-1">The sun stayed in the middle of the sky;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it did not go down for about a whole day.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:30; Exodus 14:14; Joshua 10:42"> There has been no other day like it before or after it, when Yahweh obeyed the voice of a human being. For Yahweh was waging war on behalf of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 10:43; Joshua 10:6"> Joshua and all Israel with him returned to the camp at Gilgal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:8; Amos 9:2; Psalms 139:7–10"> Now the five kings had escaped and hidden themselves in the cave at Makkedah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> It was told to Joshua, “They have been found!—the five kings hidden in the cave at Makkedah!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:66; Job 21:30; Amos 9:1"> Joshua said, “Roll large stones against the mouth of the cave and place soldiers there to guard them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 18:37–41; Jeremiah 8:14; Isaiah 9:14–15"> Do not stay yourselves. Pursue your enemies and attack them from the rear. Do not permit them to enter into their cities, because Yahweh your God has given them into your hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 8:24; 2 Samuel 20:6; Joshua 10:10"> Joshua and the sons of Israel had finished slaughtering them with a very great slaughter, until they were almost completely destroyed; only a few survivors who escaped reached the fortified cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 11:7; Isaiah 54:17; Isaiah 57:4"> Then the whole army returned in peace to Joshua at the camp at Makkedah. No one dared to say one word against any of the people of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:32"> Then Joshua said, “Open the mouth of the cave and out of the cave bring to me these five kings.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:1; Joshua 10:5; Joshua 10:3"> They did as he said. They brought to him these five kings from the cave—the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish, and the king of Eglon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Romans 16:20; Revelation 2:26–27"> When they brought the kings to Joshua, he summoned every man of Israel. He said to the commanders of the soldiers who had gone into battle with him, “Put your feet on their necks.” So they came up and put their feet on their necks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:10"> Then he said to them, “Do not be afraid and do not be dismayed. Be strong and courageous. This is what Yahweh will do to all your enemies you are going to fight.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 8:29; 2 Samuel 21:9; Matthew 27:25"> Then Joshua attacked and killed the kings. He hung them on five trees. They hung on the trees until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 8:29; Deuteronomy 21:22–23; Joshua 7:26"> When it was sunset, Joshua gave orders, and they took them down from the trees and threw them into the cave where they had hidden themselves. They put large stones over the mouth of the cave. Those stones remain there to this very day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 6:21; Deuteronomy 7:16; Deuteronomy 20:16–17"> In this way, Joshua captured Makkedah on that day and killed everyone there with the sword, including its king. He completely destroyed everyone in it. He left no survivor in it. He did to the king of Makkedah just as he had done to the king of Jericho.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 21:13; Joshua 15:42; Jeremiah 52:1"> Joshua and all Israel passed on from Makkedah to Libnah. He went into battle against Libnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Yahweh also gave it into the hand of Israel—along with their king. Joshua struck it with the edge of the sword and every person in it. He left no survivor in it. He did to its king just as he had done to the king of Jericho.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 10:3; Joshua 12:11; Joshua 10:5"> Then Joshua and all Israel with him passed on from Libnah to Lachish. He camped by it and waged war against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Yahweh gave Lachish into the hand of Israel. Joshua captured it on the second day and struck it with the edge of the sword, and every person in it, just as he had done to Libnah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; Judges 1:29; Joshua 16:3"> Then Horam, king of Gezer, came up to help Lachish. Joshua attacked him and his army until there was not even one survivor left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 10:3; Joshua 12:12; Joshua 15:39"> Then Joshua and all Israel passed on from Lachish to Eglon. They camped by it and waged war against it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 26:44; Joshua 10:37; Job 19:10"> and captured it that same day. They struck it with the edge of the sword and they completely destroyed everyone in it, as Joshua had done to Lachish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Judges 1:10; Joshua 15:13; Joshua 14:13–14"> Then Joshua and all Israel passed on from Eglon to Hebron. They waged war against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> They captured it and struck it with the edge of the sword, and its king and its villages, and everyone in it. They left no survivor it, as they had done to Eglon, they totally destroyed it and every person in it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Joshua 15:15; Joshua 12:13; Joshua 15:49"> Then Joshua turned, and all the army of Israel with him, and they passed on to Debir and waged war against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Obadiah 1:18; Joshua 11:8; Deuteronomy 3:3"> They captured it, its king and all its nearby villages. They struck them with the edge of the sword and completely destroyed every person in it. They left no survivor. They did to Debir and its king as they had done to Libnah and its king and to Hebron.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2–16; Exodus 23:31–33; Joshua 8:27"> Joshua conquered all the land, the hill country, the Negev, the lowlands, and the foothills. Of all their kings he left not one survivor. He completely destroyed every living thing, just as Yahweh, the God of Israel, had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Joshua 11:16; Joshua 15:51; Deuteronomy 9:23"> Joshua struck them from Kadesh Barnea to Gaza, and all the country of Goshen to Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Joshua 10:14; Psalms 46:7; Psalms 80:3"> Joshua captured all these kings and their land at one time because Yahweh, the God of Israel, fought for Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 10:15; Joshua 4:19; 1 Samuel 11:14"> Then Joshua, and all Israel with him, returned to the camp at Gilgal.</span>
</div>
</div>
