<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 34:17–29"> These are the areas of land that the people of Israel received as their inheritance in the land of Canaan, which Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the the tribal leaders of Israel allotted to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 33:54; Numbers 34:13; Numbers 26:55–56"> Their inheritance was selected by lot for the nine and one-half tribes, just as Yahweh had commanded by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 13:14; Joshua 13:8; Joshua 13:32–33"> For Moses had given the inheritance of the two and one-half tribes beyond the Jordan, but to the Levites he gave no inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 48:5; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2; Numbers 35:2–8"> The tribe of Joseph was actually two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim. The Levites were given no portion of the inheritance in the land, but only certain cities to live in, with their pasturelands for livestock and for their own material resources.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 21:2; Numbers 35:1–2"> The people of Israel did as Yahweh commanded Moses, so they assigned the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Numbers 13:6; Numbers 14:24"> Then the tribe of Judah came to Joshua at Gilgal. Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite, said to him, “You know what Yahweh said to Moses the man of God concerning you and me at Kadesh Barnea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 13:6; Numbers 13:26–33; Numbers 14:6–10"> I was forty years old when Moses the servant of Yahweh sent me from Kadesh Barnea to spy on the land. I brought him a report again as it was in my heart to make.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Deuteronomy 1:36; Numbers 13:31"> But my brothers who went up with me made the heart of the people melt with fear. But I completely followed Yahweh my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 1:3; Deuteronomy 1:36; Numbers 14:22–24"> Moses swore on that day, saying, 'Surely the land on which your foot has walked will be an inheritance for you and for your children forever, because you have completely followed Yahweh my God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:33–34; Numbers 14:30; Joshua 11:18"> Now, look! Yahweh has kept me alive these forty-five years, just as he said—from the time when Yahweh spoke this word to Moses, while Israel walked in the wilderness. Now, look! I am this day eighty-five years old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:7; Psalms 103:5; Deuteronomy 31:2"> I am still as strong this day as I was in the day that Moses sent me out. My strength is now as my strength was then, for war and for going and coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:33; Philippians 4:13; Numbers 13:33"> Now therefore give me this hill country, which Yahweh promised me on that day. For you heard on that day that the Anakim were there with great fortified cities. It may be that Yahweh will be with me and that I will drive them out, just as Yahweh said.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 1:20; Joshua 22:6; 1 Chronicles 6:55–56"> Then Joshua blessed him and gave Hebron as an inheritance to Caleb son of Jephunneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 14:8–9; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Therefore Hebron became the inheritance of Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite to this day, because he completely followed Yahweh, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 11:23; Genesis 23:2; Joshua 15:13"> Now the name of Hebron formerly was Kiriath Arba. (Arba had been the greatest man among the Anakim.) Then the land had rest from war.</span>
