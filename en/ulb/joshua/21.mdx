<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Joshua 19:51; Joshua 17:4"> Then the tribal leaders of the Levites came to Eleazar the priest, to Joshua son of Nun, and to the leaders of the families of their ancestors within the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Numbers 35:2–8; Matthew 10:10"> They said to them at Shiloh in the land of Canaan, “Yahweh commanded you by the hand of Moses to give to us cities to live in, with the pasturelands for our livestock.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 49:7; Deuteronomy 33:8–10; 1 Chronicles 6:54–81"> So by the command of Yahweh, the people of Israel gave out of their inheritance the following cities, including their pasturelands, to the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:54–60; Joshua 24:33; Joshua 21:8–19"> The casting of lots for the clans of the Kohathites gave this result: The priests—the descendants of Aaron who were from the Levites—received thirteen cities given from the tribe of Judah, from the tribe of the Simeon, and from the tribe of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 21:20–26; Exodus 6:16–25; 1 Chronicles 6:66–70"> The rest of the Kohathites were allotted ten cities from the clans of the tribes of Ephraim, Dan, and from the half tribe of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 21:27–33; 1 Chronicles 6:71–76; Exodus 6:16–17"> Then the people descended from Gershon were given, by the casting of lots, thirteen cities from the clans of the tribes of Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and the half tribe of Manasseh in Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:34–40; 1 Chronicles 6:77–81; Exodus 6:19"> The people who were descendants of Merari, clan by clan, received twelve cities from the tribes of Reuben, Gad, and Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 21:3; Joshua 18:6; Numbers 33:54"> So the people of Israel gave, by casting lots, these cities (including their pasturelands) to the Levites, just as Yahweh had commanded by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:65; Joshua 21:13–18"> From the tribes of Judah and Simeon, they assigned land to the following cities, here listed by name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 21:4; Numbers 3:27; Exodus 6:20–26"> These cities were given to the descendants of Aaron, who were among the clans of the Kohathites, who in turn were from the tribe of Levi. For the first casting of lots had fallen to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:55; Luke 1:39; 2 Samuel 5:1–5"> The Israelites gave them Kiriath Arba (Arba had been the father of Anak), the same place as Hebron, in the hill country of Judah, with the pasturelands around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 14:13–15; 1 Chronicles 6:55–57"> But the fields of the city, including their villages, were already given to Caleb son of Jephunneh, as his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 15:42; Joshua 10:29; Joshua 15:54"> To the descendants of Aaron the priest they gave Hebron with its pasturelands—which was a city of refuge for any who killed another unintentionally—and Libnah with its pasturelands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 15:48; Joshua 15:50; 1 Samuel 30:27–28"> Jattir with its pasturelands, and Eshtemoa with its pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 15:49; Joshua 15:51; Joshua 12:13"> They also gave Holon with its pasturelands, Debir with its pasturelands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; 1 Chronicles 6:59; Joshua 15:55"> Ain with its pasturelands, Juttah with its pasturelands, and Beth Shemesh with its pasturelands. There were nine cities that were given from these two tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 18:24–25; Joshua 9:3; 1 Chronicles 6:60"> From the tribe of Benjamin were given Gibeon with its pasturelands, Geba with its pasturelands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 10:30; 1 Chronicles 6:60; Jeremiah 1:1"> Anathoth with its pasturelands, and Almon with its suburbs—four cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The cities given to the priests, the descendants of Aaron, were thirteen cities in all, including their pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:66; Joshua 21:5"> As for the rest of Kohathites who belong to the Kohathite clans of the Levites—they had cities given to them from the tribe of Ephraim by the casting of lots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 20:7; Judges 9:1; 1 Kings 12:1"> To them were given Shechem with its pasturelands in the hill country of Ephraim—a city of refuge for any who killed a person unintentionally—Gezer with its pasturelands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:68; Joshua 16:3; Joshua 18:13–14"> Kibzaim with its pasturelands, and Beth Horon with its pasturelands—four cities in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 19:44–45"> From the tribe of Dan, the clan of Kohath was given Eltekeh with its pasturelands, Gibbethon with its pasturelands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 19:42; Joshua 10:12; 1 Chronicles 6:69"> Aijalon with its pasturelands, and Gath Rimmon with its pasturelands—four cities in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 17:11; Judges 5:19"> From the half tribe of Manasseh, the clan of Kohath was given Taanach with its pasturelands and Gath Rimmon with its pasturelands—two cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> There were ten cities in all for the rest of the clans of the Kohathites, including their pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:71; Deuteronomy 1:4; Joshua 20:8"> From the half tribe of Manasseh, to clans of Gershon, these were other Levite clans, and they gave Golan in Bashan with its pasturelands—a city of refuge for anyone who killed another unintentionally, along with Be Eshterah with its pasturelands—two cities in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 19:12; 1 Chronicles 6:72–73"> To the clans of Gershon they also gave Kishion from the tribe of Issachar, along with its pasturelands, Daberath with its pasturelands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 10:23; Joshua 12:11; Joshua 10:3"> Jarmuth with its pasturelands, and En Gannim with its pasturelands—four cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:74–75; Joshua 19:25–28"> From the tribe of Asher, they gave Mishal with its pasturelands, Abdon with its pasturelands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 18:21; 1 Chronicles 6:75; Judges 1:31"> Helkath with its pasturelands, and Rehob with its pasturelands—four cities in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:76; Joshua 20:7; Joshua 19:37"> From the tribe of Naphtali, they gave the clans of Gershon Kedesh in Galilee with its pasturelands—a city of refuge for anyone who killed another unintentionally; Hammoth Dor with its pasturelands, and Kartan with its pasturelands—three cities in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> There were thirteen cities in all, out of the clans of Gershon, including their pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:77; Joshua 19:11; Joshua 21:7"> To the rest of the Levites—the clans of Merari—were given out of the tribe of Zebulun: Jokneam with its pasturelands, Kartah with its pasturelands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Dimnah with its pasturelands, and Nahalal with its pasturelands—four cities in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 20:8; Deuteronomy 4:43; Numbers 21:23"> To the clans of Merari were given from the tribe of Reuben: Bezer with its pasturelands, Jahaz with its pasturelands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Kedemoth with its pasturelands, and Mephaath with its pasturelands—four cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 32:2; 1 Kings 22:3; 1 Chronicles 6:80"> Out of the tribe of Gad they were given Ramoth in Gilead with its pasturelands—a city of refuge for any who killed another unintentionally—and Mahanaim with its pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 16:8–9; Numbers 32:35; Numbers 32:1"> The clans of Merari were also given Heshbon with its pasturelands, and Jazer with its pasturelands. These were four cities in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> All these were the cities of the several clans of Merari, who were from the tribe of Levi—twelve cities in all were given to them by the casting of lots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 35:1–8; Deuteronomy 33:10; Genesis 49:7"> The cities of the Levites taken from the middle of the land possessed by the people of Israel were forty-eight cities, including their pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> These cities each had its surrounding pasturelands. It was this way with all these cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Psalms 106:42–45; Exodus 23:27–31"> So Yahweh gave to Israel all the land that he swore to give to their ancestors. The Israelites took possession of it and settled there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Joshua 11:23; Hebrews 4:9; Deuteronomy 31:3–5"> Then Yahweh gave them rest on every side, just as he had sworn to their ancestors. Not one of their enemies could defeat them. Yahweh gave all their enemies into their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 8:56; Joshua 23:14–15; Numbers 23:19"> Not one thing among all the good promises that Yahweh had spoken to the house of Israel failed to come true. All of them came to be.</span>
