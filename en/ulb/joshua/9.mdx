<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 34:6; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 23:23"> Then all the kings who lived beyond the Jordan in the hill country, and in the lowlands along the shore of the Great Sea toward Lebanon—the Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and the Jebusites—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 16:14; Joel 3:9–13; Psalms 2:1–2"> these joined together under one command, to wage war against Joshua and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 10:2; Joshua 9:17; 2 Samuel 21:1–2"> When the inhabitants of Gibeon heard what Joshua had done to Jericho and to Ai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 16:8; Matthew 10:16; Mark 2:22"> they acted with a cunning plan. They went as messengers. They took worn-out sacks and put them on their donkeys. They also took old wineskins that were worn, torn, and had been repaired.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Instead of They went as messengers, some versions have, They got provisions ready .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 15:22; Deuteronomy 33:25; Deuteronomy 29:5"> They put old and patched sandals on their feet, and dressed in old, worn-out clothing. All the bread in their food supply was dry and moldy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 5:10; 2 Kings 20:14; Joshua 9:9"> Then they went to Joshua in the camp at Gilgal and said to him and to the men of Israel, “We have traveled from a very far country, so now make a covenant with us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 11:19; Judges 2:2; Exodus 34:12"> The men of Israel said to the Hivites, “Perhaps you live near us. How can we make a covenant with you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:11; 2 Kings 10:5; Joshua 9:11"> They said to Joshua, “We are your servants.” Joshua said to them, “Who are you? Where did you come from?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:15; Joshua 9:24; Isaiah 55:5"> They said to him, “Your servants have come here from a land very far away, because of the name of Yahweh your God. We have heard a report about him and about everything that he did in Egypt—</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 21:24–35; Deuteronomy 1:4; Joshua 12:4"> and everything that he did to the two kings of the Amorites on the other side of the Jordan—to Sihon king of Heshbon, and to Og king of Bashan who was at Ashtaroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 9:8; Esther 8:17; Joshua 1:11"> Our elders and all the inhabitants of our country said to us, 'Take provisions in your hand for the journey. Go to meet them and say to them, “We are your servants. Make a treaty with us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 9:4–5"> This is our bread, it was still warm when we took it from our houses on the day we set out to come to you. But now, see, it is dry and moldy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> These wineskins were new when we filled them, and look, now they are leaking. Our garments and our sandals are worn-out from a very long journey.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Numbers 27:21; 1 Chronicles 10:13–14"> So the Israelites took some of their provisions, but they did not consult with Yahweh for guidance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:2; Deuteronomy 20:10–11; Joshua 11:19"> Joshua made peace with them and made a covenant with them, to let them live. The leaders of the people also made a vow to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 12:19"> Three days after the Israelites made this covenant with them, they learned that they were their neighbors and that they lived nearby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezra 2:25; Joshua 18:25–28; Joshua 18:14"> Then the people of Israel set out and came to their cities on the third day. Their cities were Gibeon, Kephirah, Beeroth, and Kiriath Jearim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 15:4; 2 Samuel 21:7; Ecclesiastes 5:6"> The people of Israel did not attack them because their leaders had taken an oath about them before Yahweh, the God of Israel. All the Israelites were grumbling against their leaders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:2; Joshua 9:20; Ecclesiastes 9:2"> But all the leaders said to all the people, “We have taken an oath concerning them by Yahweh, the God of Israel, and now we cannot harm them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Proverbs 20:25; 1 Timothy 1:10"> This is what we will do to them: To avoid any wrath that may come on us because of the oath we swore to them, we will let them live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:11; Joshua 9:27; Joshua 9:23"> The leaders said to their people, “Let them live.” So, the Gibeonites became cutters of wood and drawers of water for all the Israelites, just as the leaders said about them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 9:6; Joshua 9:16; Genesis 3:13–14"> Joshua called for them and said, “Why did you deceive us when you said, 'We are very far from you', when you live right here among us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 9:21; Genesis 9:25–26; Joshua 9:27"> Now, because of this, you are cursed and some of you will always be slaves, those who cut wood and draw water for the house of my God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:15–17; Deuteronomy 7:1–2; Exodus 23:31–33"> They answered Joshua and said, “Because it was told to your servants that Yahweh your God commanded his servant Moses to give you all the land, and to destroy all the inhabitants of the land before you—so we were very afraid for our lives because of you. That is why we did this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 16:6; Judges 10:15; Jeremiah 26:14"> Now, look, you hold us in your power. Whatever seems good and right for you to do to us, do it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So Joshua did this for them: He delivered them out of the control of the people of Israel, so that the Israelites did not kill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5"> That day Joshua made the Gibeonites cutters of wood and drawers of water for the community, and for the altar of Yahweh, to this day, in the place that Yahweh chooses.</span>
