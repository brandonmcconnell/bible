<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 13:1; Joshua 21:44; Joshua 11:23"> After many days, when Yahweh had given rest to Israel from all their enemies that around them, and Joshua was old and well advanced in years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 24:1; Deuteronomy 31:28; 1 Chronicles 28:1"> Joshua called for all Israel—for their elders, for their leaders, for their judges, and for their officers—and he said to them, “I am very old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Joshua 10:42; Joshua 10:14"> You have seen everything that Yahweh your God has done to all these nations for your sake, for it is Yahweh your God who has fought for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 13:6–7; Joshua 18:10; Joshua 13:2"> Look! I have assigned to you the nations that remain to be conquered as an inheritance for your tribes, along with all the nations I have already destroyed, from the Jordan to the Great Sea in the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:23; Joshua 13:6; Exodus 33:2"> Yahweh your God will drive them out. He will push them out from you. He will seize their land, and you will take possession of their land, just as Yahweh your God promised to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; Deuteronomy 28:14; Proverbs 4:26–27"> So be very strong, so that you keep and do all that is written in the Book of the Law of Moses, turning aside from it neither to the right hand nor to the left,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 23:13; Psalms 16:4; Deuteronomy 7:2–3"> so you may not mix with these nations that remain among you or mention the names of their gods, swear by them, worship them, or bow down to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:20; Joshua 22:5; Deuteronomy 13:4"> Instead, you must cling to Yahweh your God just as you have done to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Deuteronomy 11:23; Joshua 23:5"> For Yahweh has driven out before you large, strong nations. As for you, no one has been able to stand before you to this present day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:30; Leviticus 26:8; Deuteronomy 3:22"> Any single man of your number will make a thousand run away, for Yahweh your God, is the one who fights for you, just as he promised you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 22:5; Deuteronomy 6:5–12; Proverbs 4:23"> Pay particular attention, so that you love Yahweh your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3; Ezekiel 18:24; Zephaniah 1:6"> But if you turn back and cling to the survivors of these nations who remain among you, or if you intermarry with them, or if you come together with them and they with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:16; Numbers 33:55; Exodus 23:33"> then know for certain that Yahweh your God will no longer drive these nations out from among you. Instead, they will become a snare and a trap for you, whips on your backs and thorns in your eyes, until you perish from this good land that Yahweh your God has given you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 21:33; 1 Kings 2:2; Leviticus 26:3–13"> Now I am going the way of all the earth, and you know with all your hearts and souls that not one word has failed to come true of all the good things that Yahweh your God promised about you. All these things have come about for you. Not one of them has failed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Leviticus 26:14–46; Judges 3:8"> But just as every word Yahweh your God promised you has been fulfilled, so Yahweh will bring on you all the evil things until he has destroyed you from this good land that Yahweh your God has given you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:25–26; 2 Kings 24:20"> He will do this if you break the covenant of Yahweh your God, which he commanded you to keep. If you go and worship other gods and bow down to them, then the anger of Yahweh will be kindled against you, and you will quickly perish from the good land that he has given you.”</span>
