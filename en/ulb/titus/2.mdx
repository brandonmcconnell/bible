<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13; 1 Timothy 6:3; Titus 2:11–14"> But you, speak what fits with faithful instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; 1 Peter 4:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:8"> Teach older men to be temperate, dignified, sensible, sound in faith, in love, and in perseverance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 2:4; 1 Timothy 3:11; 1 Peter 3:3–5"> Teach older women likewise to be reverent in behavior, not slanderers or being slaves to much wine, but to be teachers of what is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:14; 1 Timothy 5:2; 1 Timothy 5:11"> In this way they may train the younger women to love their own husbands and children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 31:10–31; Colossians 3:18; 1 Timothy 5:10"> to be sensible, pure, good housekeepers, and obedient to their own husbands. They should do these things so that God's word may not be insulted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:1; 1 Peter 5:5; Joel 2:28"> In the same way, encourage the younger men to use good sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; 1 Timothy 4:12; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> In all ways present yourself as an example of good works. In your teaching, show integrity, dignity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 2:15; 1 Peter 3:16"> and a correct message, so that anyone who opposes you may be ashamed because they have nothing bad to say about us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:18–25; 1 Timothy 6:1–2; Colossians 3:22–25"> Teach slaves to obey their masters in everything, to please them and not argue with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 5:16; 1 Peter 2:12; 2 John 1:9"> to not steal from them, but instead to demonstrate all good faith, so that in every way they may bring credit to the teaching about God our Savior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8; 1 Timothy 2:4; Isaiah 45:22"> For the grace of God has appeared for the salvation of all people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Matthew 16:24; Romans 6:19"> It trains us to reject godlessness and worldly passions, and to live self-controlled, upright, and godly lives in this age,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:8; 1 Corinthians 1:7; Matthew 16:27"> while we look forward to receiving our blessed hope, the appearance of the glory of our great God and Savior Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:14; Ephesians 2:10; 1 Peter 2:9"> Jesus gave himself for us in order to redeem us from all lawlessness and to make pure, for himself, a special people who are eager to do good works.</span>
