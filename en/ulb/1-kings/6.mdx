<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1–2; Acts 7:47; 1 Chronicles 29:19"> So Solomon began to build the temple of Yahweh. This happened in the 480th year after the people of Israel came out of the land of Egypt, in the fourth year of Solomon's reign over Israel, in the month of Ziv, which is the second month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:1–41; Ezekiel 41:1–15; Revelation 21:16–17"> The temple that King Solomon built for Yahweh was sixty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and thirty cubits high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:11; 2 Chronicles 3:3–4; Ezekiel 41:15"> The portico in front of the temple's main hall was twenty cubits in length, equal to the width of the temple, and ten cubits deep in front of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 41:26; Ezekiel 41:16"> For the house he made windows with frames that made them more narrow at the outside than on the inside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:16; 1 Kings 6:19–21; 1 Kings 6:31"> Against the walls of the main chamber he built rooms around it, around both the outer room and the inner room. He built rooms all around the sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The lowest story was five cubits wide, the middle was six cubits wide, and the third was seven cubits wide. For on the outside he made offsets in the wall of the house all around so that the beams would not be inserted in the walls of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:5–6; 1 Peter 2:5; Exodus 20:25"> The house was built of stones prepared at the quarry. No hammer, ax, or any iron tool was heard in the house while it was being built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:6–7"> On the south side of the temple there was an entrance at the ground level, then one went up by stairs to the middle level, and from the middle to the third level.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 6:14; 1 Kings 6:38"> So Solomon built the temple and finished it; he covered the house with beams and planks of cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He built the side rooms against the inner chambers of the temple, each side five cubits high; they were joined to the house with timbers of cedar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The word of Yahweh came to Solomon, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:10; Colossians 1:23; 1 Kings 3:14"> “Concerning this temple which you are building, if you walk in my statutes and do justice, keep all my commandments and walk in them, then I will confirm my promise with you that I had made to David your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:6; Exodus 25:8; Leviticus 26:11"> I will live among the people of Israel and will not forsake them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 6:9; 1 Kings 6:38; Acts 7:47–48"> So Solomon built the house and finished it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 7:7"> Then he built the interior walls of the house with boards of cedar. From the floor of the house to the ceiling, he covered them on the inside with wood, and he covered the floor of the house with cypress boards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:8; 1 Kings 8:6; Hebrews 9:3"> He built twenty cubits onto the rear of the house with boards of cedar from the floor to the ceiling. He built this room to be the inner room, the most holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The main hall, that is, the holy place that was in front of the most holy place, was forty cubits long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:24"> There was cedar inside the house, carved in the shape of gourds and open flowers. All was cedar inside. No stonework was visible on the inside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 8:6–10; Psalms 28:2; 1 Kings 6:5"> Solomon prepared the inner room inside the house in order to place the ark of the covenant of Yahweh there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 6:2–3; 1 Kings 6:22; 1 Kings 7:48"> The inner room was twenty cubits in length, twenty cubits in width, and twenty cubits in height. Solomon overlaid the walls with pure gold and covered the altar with cedar wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:7–9; 2 Chronicles 3:14–16; 1 Kings 6:5"> Solomon overlaid the inside of the temple with pure gold, and he placed chains of gold across the front of the inner room, and overlaid the front with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 30:1; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:7–14"> He overlaid the entire interior with gold until all the temple was finished. He also overlaid with gold the whole altar that belonged to the inner room.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 37:7–9; Genesis 3:24; 1 Peter 1:12"> Solomon made two cherubim of olivewood, each ten cubits high, for the inner room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> One wing of the first cherub was five cubits long and its other wing was also five cubits long. So from the tip of one wing to the tip of the other there was a distance of ten cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The other cherub also had a wingspan ten cubits. Both the cherubim were of the same dimensions and shape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The height of one cherub was ten cubits and the other cherub was the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 37:9; Exodus 25:20; 2 Chronicles 5:8"> Solomon placed the cherubim in the innermost room. The wings of the cherubim were stretched out so that the wing of one touched one wall and the wing of the other cherub touched the other wall. Their wings touched one another in the middle of the most holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Solomon overlaid the cherubim with gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 6:32; Psalms 92:12–15; 2 Chronicles 4:2–5"> He carved all the walls of the house around about with figures of cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers, in the outer and inner rooms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 21:18–21; Isaiah 60:17; Isaiah 54:11–12"> Solomon overlaid the floor of the house with gold, in both the outer and inner rooms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–20; Ephesians 3:18; John 14:6"> Solomon made doors of olivewood for the entrance to the inner room. The lintel and doorposts had five indented sections.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 6:18; 1 Kings 6:29"> So he made two doors of olivewood, and he made on them carvings of cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers. He overlaid them with gold and he spread the gold on the cherubim and palm trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> In this way, Solomon also made for the temple entrance doorposts of olive wood having four indented sections</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:23–25; 1 Kings 5:8"> and two doors of cypress wood. The two leaves of the one door were folding and the two leaves of the other door were folding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> He carved on them cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers, and he evenly overlaid gold on the carved work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 7:12; 2 Chronicles 7:7; Exodus 27:9–19"> He built the inner courtyard with three rows of cut stone and a row of cedar beams.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Kings 6:1; 2 Chronicles 3:2"> The foundation of the house of Yahweh was laid in the fourth year, in the month of Ziv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezra 6:14–15; 1 Kings 7:1; Zechariah 4:9"> In the eleventh year, in the month of Bul, which is the eighth month, the house was finished in all its parts and conforming to all its specifications. Solomon took seven years to build the temple.</span>
</div>
