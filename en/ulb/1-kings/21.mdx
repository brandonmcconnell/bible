<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 6:33; 2 Chronicles 28:22; Joshua 19:18"> Now some time later, Naboth the Jezreelite had a vineyard in Jezreel, near the palace of Ahab, king of Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:14; James 1:14–15; 1 Timothy 6:9"> Ahab spoke to Naboth, saying, “Give me your vineyard, so I can have it as a vegetable garden, because it is near my house. In exchange, I will give you a better vineyard, or, if you prefer, I will pay you its value in money.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 36:7; Leviticus 25:23; Ezekiel 46:18"> Naboth replied to Ahab, “May Yahweh forbid that I should give the inheritance of my ancestors to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 20:43; James 1:14; Jonah 4:9"> So Ahab went into his palace resentful and angry because of the answer Naboth the Jezreelite gave him when he said, “I will not give you the inheritance of my ancestors.” He lay down on his bed, turned away his face, and refused to eat any food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 16:31; Genesis 3:6; 1 Kings 19:2"> Jezebel his wife came to him and said to him, “Why is your heart so sad, so that you eat no food?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esther 5:9–14; James 4:2–7; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> He replied to her, “I spoke to Naboth the Jezreelite and said to him, 'Give me your vineyard for money, or if it pleases you, I will give you another vineyard to be yours.' Then he answered me, 'I will not give you my vineyard.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:14; Micah 7:3; 2 Samuel 13:4"> So Jezebel his wife replied to him, “Do you not still rule the kingdom of Israel? Get up and eat; let your heart be happy. I will obtain for you the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:5; 2 Kings 10:11; Ezra 4:7–8"> So Jezebel wrote letters in Ahab's name, sealed them with his seal, and sent them to the elders and to the wealthy who sat with him in meetings, and who lived near Naboth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 58:4; Matthew 23:13; Luke 20:47"> She wrote in the letters, saying, “Proclaim a fast and seat Naboth above the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Acts 6:11; Leviticus 24:15–16"> Also place two dishonest men with him and let them testify against him, saying, 'You cursed God and the king.'” Then take him out and stone him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 4:19; Leviticus 19:15; 2 Kings 10:6–7"> So the men of his city, the elders and the wealthy who lived in Naboth's city, did as Jezebel had described to them, as was written in the letters that she had sent to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 58:4; 1 Kings 21:8–10"> They proclaimed a fast and seated Naboth above the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 9:26; Deuteronomy 22:24; Ecclesiastes 10:20"> The two dishonest men came in and sat before Naboth; they testified against Naboth in the presence of the people, saying, “Naboth cursed both God and the king.” Then they carried him out of the city and stoned him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:14; 2 Samuel 11:14–24; Ecclesiastes 5:8"> Then the elders sent word to Jezebel, saying, “Naboth has been stoned and is dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 1:10–16; Proverbs 4:17; 1 Kings 21:7"> So when Jezebel heard that Naboth had been stoned and was dead, she said to Ahab, “Get up and take possession of the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite, which he refused to give you for money, because Naboth is not alive, but dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Obadiah 1:12–14; Romans 1:32; 2 Samuel 11:25–27"> When Ahab heard that Naboth was dead, he rose up to go down to the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite and take possession of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 9:12; Isaiah 26:21; 2 Kings 5:26"> Then the word of Yahweh came to Elijah the Tishbite, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 13:32; 2 Chronicles 22:9"> “Get up and go meet Ahab king of Israel, who lives in Samaria. He is in the vineyard of Naboth, where he has gone to take possession of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 22:38; 2 Samuel 12:9; Habakkuk 2:9"> You must speak to him and say that Yahweh says, 'Have you killed and also taken possession?' Then you will tell him that Yahweh says, 'In the place where dogs licked the blood of Naboth, the dogs will lick your blood, yes, your blood.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 17:17; Romans 7:14; 1 Kings 21:25"> Ahab said to Elijah, “Have you found me, my enemy?” Elijah answered, “I have found you, because you have sold yourself to do what is evil in the sight of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 14:10; 1 Samuel 25:34; 2 Kings 10:1–7"> Yahweh says this to you: 'See, I will bring disaster on you and will completely consume and cut off from you every male child and slave and free man in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 16:3; 1 Kings 14:16; 1 Kings 16:11"> I will make your family like the family of Jeroboam son of Nebat, and like the family of Baasha son of Ahijah, because you have provoked me to anger and have led Israel to sin.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 9:10; 2 Kings 9:30–37; 1 Kings 21:25"> Yahweh has also spoken concerning Jezebel, saying, 'The dogs will eat Jezebel beside the wall of Jezreel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 16:4; 1 Kings 14:11; Jeremiah 15:3"> Anyone who belongs to Ahab and dies in the city, the dogs will eat; and the birds of the sky will eat anyone who dies in the field.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14; 1 Kings 21:20; 1 Kings 16:30–33"> There was no one like Ahab, who sold himself to do what was evil in the sight of Yahweh, whom Jezebel his wife incited to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 21:11; Genesis 15:16; Leviticus 18:25–30"> Ahab did disgusting deeds for the idols he followed, just as all that the Amorites had done, those whom Yahweh had removed before the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 6:30; Genesis 37:34; 2 Samuel 3:31"> When Ahab heard these words, he tore his clothes and put sackcloth on his body and fasted, and lay in sackcloth and became very sad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Then the word of Yahweh came to Elijah the Tishbite, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Micah 7:18; Psalms 86:15"> “Do you see how Ahab humbles himself before me? Because he humbles himself before me, I will not bring the coming disaster in his days; it is in his son's day that I will bring disaster on his family.”</span>
