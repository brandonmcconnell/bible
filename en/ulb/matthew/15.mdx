<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Matthew 23:2; Luke 5:21"> Then some Pharisees and scribes came to Jesus from Jerusalem. They said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 11:38; Mark 7:2; Colossians 2:20–23"> “Why do your disciples violate the traditions of the elders? For they do not wash their hands when they eat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:23; Titus 1:14; Colossians 2:8"> He answered and said to them, “Then why do you violate the commandment of God for the sake of your traditions?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Leviticus 20:9; Exodus 21:17"> For God said, 'Honor your father and your mother,' and 'He who speaks evil of his father or mother, will surely die.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 7:10–13; Acts 5:29; Matthew 23:16–18"> But you say, 'Whoever says to his father or mother “Whatever help you would have received from me is now a gift given to God,”'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; Jeremiah 8:8; Mark 7:13"> that person does not need to honor his father. In this way you have made void the word of God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The best ancient copies have the word of God some other ancient copies have the commandment.It is difficult to choose the better reading.">a</a></sup>for the sake of your traditions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 7:6; Matthew 23:23–29; Acts 28:25–27"> You hypocrites! Well did Isaiah prophesy about you when he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Ezekiel 33:31; Acts 8:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">'This people honors me with their lips,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but their heart is far from me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Mark 7:7; 1 Timothy 1:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">They worship me in vain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they teach as their doctrines the commandments of people.'”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 7:14–15; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 24:45"> Then he called the crowd to himself and said to them, “Listen and understand—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Matthew 15:18–20; James 3:5–8"> Nothing that enters into the mouth defiles a person. Instead, what comes out of the mouth, this is what defiles a person.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 22:13–14; 1 Corinthians 10:32–33; Galatians 2:5"> Then the disciples came and said to Jesus, “Do you know that the Pharisees were offended when they heard this statement?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:2; Isaiah 60:21; John 15:6"> Jesus answered and said, “Every plant that my heavenly Father has not planted will be rooted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:39; Isaiah 56:10; Matthew 23:16–24"> Let them alone, they are blind guides. If a blind person guides another blind person, both will fall into a pit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 13:36; Mark 4:34; John 16:29"> Peter responded and said to Jesus, “Explain this parable to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:9; Mark 7:18; Mark 9:32"> Jesus said, “Are you also still without understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 6:45; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Matthew 7:19–20"> Do you not see that whatever goes into the mouth passes into the stomach and is then passed out into the latrine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Matthew 12:34; James 3:6–10"> But the things that come out of the mouth come from the heart. They are the things that defile a person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 7:21–23; Galatians 5:19–21; Jeremiah 17:9"> For from the heart proceed evil thoughts, murder, adultery, sexual immorality, theft, false witness, and slander.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Ephesians 5:3–6; 1 Corinthians 6:18–20"> These are the things that defile a person. But to eat with unwashed hands does not defile a person.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 7:24–30; Matthew 11:21–23; Judges 1:31"> Then Jesus went away from there and withdrew toward the regions of the cities of Tyre and Sidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Mark 7:25–26; Matthew 17:15"> Behold, a Canaanite woman came out from that region. She shouted out and said, “Have mercy on me, Lord, Son of David! My daughter is severely demon-possessed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 14:15; Mark 10:47–48; Lamentations 3:8"> But Jesus answered her not a word. His disciples came and begged him, saying, “Send her away, for she is shouting after us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:5–6; Romans 15:8; Jeremiah 50:6–7"> But Jesus answered and said, “I was not sent to anyone except to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 32:26; Matthew 20:31; Matthew 14:33"> But she came and bowed down before him, saying, “Lord, help me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Matthew 7:6; Philippians 3:2"> He answered and said, “It is not right to take the children's bread and throw it to the little dogs.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 16:21; Job 40:4–5"> She said, “Yes, Lord, but even the little dogs eat some of the crumbs that fall from their masters' tables.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:23–24; Matthew 8:13; Psalms 145:19"> Then Jesus answered and said to her, “Woman, great is your faith; let it be done for you just as you wish.” At that hour her daughter was healed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 4:18; John 6:1; Matthew 5:1"> Jesus left that place and went near to the Sea of Galilee. Then he went up a hill and sat there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:17–19; Acts 5:15–16; Matthew 14:35–36"> Large crowds came to him. They brought with them lame, blind, mute, and crippled people, and many others who were sick. They presented them at Jesus' feet, and he healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 18:43; Luke 17:15–18"> So the crowd marveled when they saw the mute persons speak, the crippled made well, the lame walking, and the blind seeing. They praised the God of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 27:33; Luke 12:29–30; Matthew 9:36"> Jesus called his disciples to him and said, “I have compassion on the crowd, because they have stayed with me for three days already and have nothing to eat. I do not want to send them away without eating, or they may faint on the way.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 6:37; Matthew 14:15; Mark 8:4–5"> The disciples said to him, “Where can we get enough loaves of bread in such a deserted place to satisfy so large a crowd?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 16:9–10; Luke 24:41–42; John 21:9–10"> Jesus said to them, “How many loaves do you have?” They said, “Seven, and a few small fish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 9:14–16; Mark 6:39–40; John 6:10"> Then Jesus commanded the crowd to sit down on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 22:19; Romans 14:6; John 6:11"> He took the seven loaves and the fish, and after giving thanks, he broke the loaves and gave them to the disciples. The disciples gave them to the crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 8:19–21; Matthew 16:9–10; Matthew 15:33"> The people all ate and were satisfied. Then they gathered up seven baskets full of the broken pieces that were left over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Those who ate were four thousand men, besides women and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 8:10; Matthew 14:22"> Then Jesus sent the crowds away and got into the boat and went into the region of Magadan.</span>
</div>
