<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 6:37; Romans 2:1–2; James 4:11–12"> Do not judge, and you will not be judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 2:13; Mark 4:24; Luke 6:38"> For with the judgment you judge, you will be judged, and with the measure that you measure, it will be measured out to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 8:7–9; Galatians 6:1; Luke 6:41–42"> Why do you look at the tiny piece of straw that is in your brother's eye, but you do not notice the log that is in your own eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> How can you say to your brother, 'Let me take out the piece of straw that is in your eye,' while the log is in your own eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:42; Psalms 51:9–13; Matthew 23:13–28"> You hypocrite! First take the log out of your own eye, and then you will see clearly to take out the piece of straw that is in your brother's eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 9:7–8; Proverbs 23:9; Matthew 10:14–15"> Do not give what is holy to the dogs, and do not throw your pearls in front of the hogs. Otherwise they may trample them underfoot, and then turn and tear you to pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; 1 John 3:22; John 15:7"> Ask, and it will be given to you. Seek, and you will find. Knock, and it will be opened to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 81:10; Psalms 81:16; Luke 23:42–43"> For everyone who asks, receives; everyone who seeks, finds; and to the person who knocks, it will be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13"> Or which one of you, if his son asks for a loaf of bread, will give him a stone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Or if he asks for a fish, will give him a snake?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13; James 1:17; Romans 8:32"> Therefore, if you who are evil know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father in heaven give good things to those who ask him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:31; Romans 13:8–10; Galatians 5:13–14"> Therefore, whatever things you want people to do to you, you should also do to them, for this is the law and the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 13:24–25; Proverbs 16:25; John 14:6"> Enter through the narrow gate. For wide is the gate and broad is the way that leads to destruction, and there are many people who go through it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 13:23–30; Matthew 22:14; Matthew 16:24–25"> But the gate is narrow and the way is difficult that leads to life, and there are few who find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 4:1; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Matthew 24:11"> Beware of false prophets, who come to you in sheep's clothing but are truly ravenous wolves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:43–45; Matthew 12:33; James 3:12"> By their fruits you will know them. Do people gather grapes from a thornbush or figs from thistles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 12:33–35; Galatians 5:22–24; Ephesians 5:9"> In the same way, every good tree produces good fruit, but the bad tree produces bad fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; 1 John 3:9–10"> A good tree cannot produce bad fruit, nor can a bad tree produce good fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 3:10; Hebrews 6:8; John 15:2–6"> Every tree that does not produce good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 7:16; Acts 5:38"> So then, you will recognize them by their fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:13; Luke 6:46; James 1:22"> Not everyone who says to me, 'Lord, Lord,' will enter into the kingdom of heaven, but only those who do the will of my Father who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 13:25–27; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Malachi 3:17–18"> Many people will say to me in that day, 'Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy in your name, in your name drive out demons, and in your name do many mighty deeds?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Psalms 6:8; Luke 13:25"> Then will I openly declare to them, 'I never knew you! Get away from me, you evildoers!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 6:47–49; John 13:17; 1 Corinthians 3:10–11"> Therefore, everyone who hears my words and obeys them will be like a wise man who built his house upon a rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 1:12; Psalms 125:1–2; 1 Peter 1:7"> The rain came down, the floods came, and the winds blew and beat upon that house, but it did not fall down, for it was built on the rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:9; Luke 6:49; James 2:20"> But everyone who hears my words and does not obey them will be like a foolish man who built his house upon the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 13:19–22; 2 Peter 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> The rain came down, the floods came, and the winds blew and struck that house, and it fell, and its destruction was complete.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 4:32; Mark 1:22; John 7:46"> It came about that when Jesus finished speaking these words, the crowds were astonished by his teaching,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12–13; Luke 20:8; Deuteronomy 18:18–19"> for he taught them as one who had authority, and not as their scribes.</span>
