<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:47–53; John 18:24; Mark 14:54"> Then the chief priests and the elders of the people were gathered together in the palace of the high priest, who was named Caiaphas.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Luke 7:37–39; John 12:1–8"> Now while Jesus was in Bethany in the house of Simon the leper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 12:2–3; Luke 7:46; Luke 7:37–38"> as he was reclining at table, a woman came to him having an alabaster jar of very expensive ointment, and she poured it upon his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 12:4–6; Mark 14:4; Exodus 5:17"> But when his disciples saw it, they became angry and said, “What is the reason for this waste?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 12:5–6; Mark 14:5; Joshua 7:20–21"> This could have been sold for a large amount and given to the poor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Titus 3:8; Hebrews 13:21; 1 Timothy 5:10"> But Jesus, knowing this, said to them, “Why are you troubling this woman? For she has done a beautiful thing for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Mark 14:7; John 12:8"> You always have the poor with you, but you will not always have me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 14:8; John 12:7; Mark 16:1"> For when she poured this ointment on my body, she did it for my burial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 14:9; Matthew 24:14; Psalms 112:6"> Truly I say to you, wherever this gospel is preached in the whole world, what this woman has done will also be spoken of in memory of her.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 22:3–6; Matthew 10:4; Mark 14:10–11"> Then one of the twelve, who was named Judas Iscariot, went to the chief priests</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Genesis 37:26–28; Exodus 21:32"> and said, “What are you willing to give me to deliver him to you?” They weighed out thirty pieces of silver for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:11; Luke 22:6"> From that moment he sought an opportunity to deliver him to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:7–13; Mark 14:12–16; Exodus 12:18–20"> Now on the first day of unleavened bread the disciples came to Jesus and said, “Where do you want us to prepare for you to eat the Passover meal?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 14:13–16; John 7:6; John 17:1"> He said, “Go into the city to a certain man and say to him, 'The Teacher says, “My time is at hand. I will keep the Passover at your house with my disciples.”'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 2:5; John 15:14; Matthew 21:6"> The disciples did as Jesus directed them, and they prepared the Passover meal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 14:17–21; John 13:21; Luke 22:14–16"> When evening came, he sat down to eat with the twelve disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 13:21; Hebrews 4:13; John 6:70–71"> As they were eating, he said, “Truly I say to you that one of you will betray me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 13:22–25; Luke 22:23; Mark 14:19–20"> They were very sorrowful, and each one began to ask him, “Surely not I, Lord?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:18; Psalms 41:9; John 13:26–28"> He answered, “The one who dips his hand with me in the dish is the one who will betray me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 26:22–23; Luke 24:46; Matthew 18:7"> The Son of Man will go, just as it is written about him. But woe to that man by whom the Son of Man is betrayed! It would be better for that man if he had not been born.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 26:64; Matthew 27:11; Luke 22:70"> Judas, who would betray him said, “Is it I, Rabbi?” He said to him, “You have said it yourself.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:23–29; Luke 22:18–20; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> As they were eating, Jesus took bread, blessed it, and broke it. He gave it to the disciples and said, “Take, eat. This is my body.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; 1 Corinthians 10:16; Psalms 116:13"> He took a cup and gave thanks, and gave it to them and said, “Drink it, all of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–14; Hebrews 9:14–22; Leviticus 17:11"> For this is my blood of the covenant that is poured out for many for the forgiveness of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Revelation 7:17; Mark 14:25"> But I say to you, I will not drink again of this fruit of the vine, until that day when I drink it new with you in my Father's kingdom.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Ephesians 5:19–20; Luke 22:39"> When they had sung a hymn, they went out to the Mount of Olives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Mark 14:27–28; John 16:32"> Then Jesus said to them, “All of you will fall away tonight because of me, for it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'I will strike the shepherd</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the sheep of the flock will be scattered.'</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 28:10; Luke 18:33–34; Matthew 28:16"> But after I am raised up, I will go ahead of you into Galilee.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 22:33; John 13:36–38; Proverbs 28:25–26"> But Peter said to him, “Even if all fall away because of you, I will never fall away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 13:38; Luke 22:34; Matthew 26:75"> Jesus said to him, “Truly I say to you, this very night, before the rooster crows, you will deny me three times.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Romans 11:20; John 13:37; Proverbs 29:23"> Peter said to him, “Even if I must die with you, I will not deny you.” All the other disciples said the same thing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 14:32–42; Matthew 26:42; Luke 22:39–46"> Then Jesus went with them to a place called Gethsemane and said to his disciples, “Sit here while I go over there and pray.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Matthew 17:1; Mark 5:37"> He took Peter and the two sons of Zebedee with him and began to become sorrowful and troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 12:27; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 Peter 2:24"> Then he said to them, “My soul is deeply sorrowful, even to death. Remain here and watch with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 6:38; Philippians 2:8; Luke 22:41–42"> He went a little farther, fell on his face, and prayed. He said, “My Father, if it is possible, let this cup pass from me. Yet, not as I will, but as you will.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 14:37; Luke 22:45; Matthew 26:43"> He came to the disciples and found them sleeping, and he said to Peter, “What, could you not watch with me for one hour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 14:38; Luke 22:46; Ephesians 6:18"> Watch and pray that you do not enter into temptation. The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 26:39; Mark 14:39–40; Hebrews 4:15"> He went away a second time and prayed. He said, “My Father, if this cannot pass away unless I drink it, your will be done.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:32; Romans 13:1; Proverbs 23:34"> He came again and found them sleeping, for their eyes were heavy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:8; Luke 18:1; Matthew 6:7"> So leaving them again, he went away and prayed a third time, saying the same words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 13:1; John 12:27; Mark 14:41–42"> Then Jesus came to the disciples and said to them, “Are you still sleeping and taking your rest? Look, the hour is at hand, and the Son of Man is being betrayed into the hands of sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 9:51; John 14:31; 1 Samuel 17:48"> Arise, let us go. Look, the one who is betraying me is near.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 1:16; Matthew 26:55; Mark 14:43–50"> While he was still speaking, Judas, one of the twelve, came. A large crowd came with him from the chief priests and elders of the people. They came with swords and clubs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 55:20–21; Psalms 28:3; 2 Samuel 3:27"> Now the man who was going to betray Jesus had given them a signal, saying, “Whomever I kiss, he is the one. Seize him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:25; 1 Thessalonians 5:26; Proverbs 27:6"> Immediately he came up to Jesus and said, “Greetings, Rabbi!” and he kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 20:13; Matthew 22:12; Psalms 41:9"> Jesus said to him, “Friend, do what you have come to do.” Then they came, laid hands on Jesus, and seized him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 18:36; Mark 14:47; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> Behold, one of those who was with Jesus stretched out his hand, drew his sword, and struck the servant of the high priest, and cut off his ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Revelation 13:10; Genesis 9:6; Romans 12:19"> Then Jesus said to him, “Put your sword back in its place, for all those who take up the sword will perish by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Kings 6:17; Matthew 4:11; Daniel 7:10"> Do you think that I could not call upon my Father, and he would send me more than twelve legions of angels?</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Matthew 26:24; Acts 1:16"> But how then would the scriptures be fulfilled, that this must happen?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Mark 12:35; John 8:2; Luke 22:52–53"> At that time Jesus said to the crowd, “Have you come out with swords and clubs to seize me like a robber? Every day I sat teaching in the temple, and you did not arrest me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; John 16:32; Mark 14:50–52"> But all this has happened so that the writings of the prophets might be fulfilled.” Then all the disciples left him and fled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; Mark 14:53–65; Psalms 56:5–6"> Those who had seized Jesus led him away to Caiaphas the high priest, where the scribes and the elders had gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 7:32; John 18:15–16; John 18:25"> But Peter followed him from a distance to the courtyard of the high priest. He went inside and sat down with the guards to see the outcome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Acts 6:11–13; Deuteronomy 19:16–21; Mark 14:55–56"> Now the chief priests and the whole council were looking for false testimony against Jesus so that they might put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Psalms 35:11; Psalms 27:12"> They did not find any, even though many false witnesses came forward. But later two came forward</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="John 2:19–21; Matthew 27:40; Mark 15:29"> and said, “This man said, 'I am able to destroy the temple of God and rebuild it in three days.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="John 19:9–11; John 18:19–24; Mark 14:60"> The high priest stood up and said to him, “Do you have no answer? What is it that they are testifying against you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Matthew 16:16; Isaiah 53:7"> But Jesus was silent. The high priest said to him, “I command you by the living God, tell us whether you are the Christ, the Son of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Mark 14:62; Psalms 110:1"> Jesus replied to him, “You have said it yourself. But I tell you, from now on you will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of Power, and coming on the clouds of heaven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:24; John 10:33; Matthew 9:3"> Then the high priest tore his clothes and said, “He has spoken blasphemy! Why do we still need witnesses? Look, now you have heard the blasphemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="John 19:7; Acts 7:52; Leviticus 24:11–16"> What do you think?” They answered and said, “He is worthy of death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 27:30"> Then they spit in his face and beat him, and struck him with the palms of their hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Luke 22:63–65; Mark 14:65; 1 Peter 2:4–8"> and said, “Prophesy to us, you Christ. Who is it that struck you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="1 Kings 19:13; Matthew 26:71; Acts 5:37"> Now Peter was sitting outside in the courtyard, and a servant girl came to him and said, “You were also with Jesus of Galilee.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:12; Revelation 21:8; Matthew 26:40–43"> But he denied it in front of them all, saying, “I do not know what you are talking about.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Mark 14:68–69; John 18:25–27; Luke 22:58"> When he went out to the gateway, another servant girl saw him and said to those there, “This man was also with Jesus of Nazareth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Matthew 26:74; Isaiah 48:1; Zechariah 8:17"> He again denied it with an oath, “I do not know the man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73" data-xref="Judges 12:6; John 18:26–27; Nehemiah 13:24"> After a little while those who were standing by came and said to Peter, “Surely you are also one of them, for the way you speak gives you away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; 1 Corinthians 16:22; Mark 14:71–72"> Then he began to curse and swear, “I do not know the man,” and immediately a rooster crowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v75" data-v="75" data-xref="Matthew 26:34; Romans 7:18–20; Luke 22:31–34"> Peter remembered the words that Jesus had said, “Before the rooster crows you will deny me three times.” Then he went outside and wept bitterly.</span>
</div>
