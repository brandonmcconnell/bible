</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; Luke 22:47–48; Job 20:15–29"> Then when Judas, who had betrayed him, saw that Jesus had been condemned, he repented and returned the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and elders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:30; Titus 1:16; Acts 18:15–17"> and said, “I have sinned by betraying innocent blood.” But they said, “What is that to us? See to that yourself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 1:18–19; 2 Samuel 17:23; 1 Kings 16:18"> Then he threw down the pieces of silver in the temple, and departed, and went out and hanged himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:7–9; Deuteronomy 23:18; Isaiah 61:8"> The chief priests took the pieces of silver and said, “It is not lawful to put this into the treasury, because it is the price of blood.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> They discussed the matter together and with the money bought the potter's field in which to bury strangers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:19; Matthew 28:15; Joshua 4:9"> For this reason that field has been called, “The Field of Blood” to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 1:22; Leviticus 27:2–7"> Then that which had been spoken by Jeremiah the prophet was fulfilled, saying, “They took the thirty pieces of silver, the price set on him by the people of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 11:13"> and they gave it for the potter's field, as the Lord had directed me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 23:2–3; Mark 15:2–5; Matthew 26:25"> Now Jesus stood before the governor, and the governor asked him, “Are you the King of the Jews?” Jesus answered him, “You say so.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:63; Isaiah 53:7; Matthew 27:14"> But when he was accused by the chief priests and elders, he answered nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:62; John 18:35; Acts 22:24"> Then Pilate said to him, “Do you not hear all the charges against you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; Psalms 71:7; 1 Corinthians 4:9"> But he did not answer even one word, so that the governor was greatly amazed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:38–40; Luke 23:16; John 19:16"> Now at the feast it was the custom of the governor to release one prisoner chosen by the crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 15:7; Acts 3:14; Luke 23:25"> At that time they had a notorious prisoner named Barabbas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 19:15; 1 Kings 18:21; Mark 15:9–12"> So when they were gathered together, Pilate said to them, “Who do you want me to release for you? Barabbas, or Jesus who is called Christ?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Acts 5:17; Mark 15:10"> He knew that they had handed Jesus over to him because of envy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Proverbs 29:1; Matthew 27:24"> While he was sitting on the judgment seat, his wife sent word to him and said, “Have nothing to do with that innocent man. For I have suffered much today because of a dream I had about him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:14–15; John 19:15–16; John 18:40"> Now the chief priests and the elders persuaded the crowd to ask for Barabbas, and have Jesus killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The governor asked them, “Which of the two do you want me to release to you?” They said, “Barabbas.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–3; Isaiah 49:7; Acts 13:38"> Pilate said to them, “What should I do with Jesus who is called Christ?” They all answered, “Crucify him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:18–19; Acts 7:57; Acts 23:12–15"> Then he said, “Why, what crime has he done?” But they cried out even louder, “Crucify him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:4; Psalms 26:6; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> So when Pilate saw that he could not do anything, but instead a riot was starting, he took water, washed his hands in front of the crowd, and said, “I am innocent of the blood of this innocent man. See to it yourselves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 2:19; Acts 5:28; Acts 7:52"> All the people said, “May his blood be on us and our children.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; John 19:1; Mark 15:15"> Then he released Barabbas to them, but he scourged Jesus and handed him over to be crucified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 18:28; John 18:33; Acts 10:1"> Then the soldiers of the governor took Jesus into the government headquarters and gathered the whole company of soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 15:17; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> They stripped him and put a scarlet robe on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 20:19; Psalms 69:19–20"> They made a crown of thorns and put it on his head, and placed a staff in his right hand. They knelt down before him and mocked him, saying, “Hail, King of the Jews!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 53:7"> They spat on him, and they took the staff and struck him on the head again and again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 Kings 21:13; 1 Kings 21:10"> When they had mocked him, they took the robe off him and put his own garments on him, and led him away to crucify him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:21; Matthew 16:24; Luke 23:26"> As they came out, they found a man from Cyrene named Simon, whom they forced to go with them so that he might carry his cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 19:17–19; Mark 15:22–32; Mark 15:34"> They came to a place called Golgotha, which means “The Place of a Skull.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Mark 15:23; Matthew 27:48"> They gave him wine to drink mixed with gall. But when he tasted it, he would not drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 22:18; Mark 15:24–32; Luke 23:34"> When they had crucified him, they divided up his garments by casting lots,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:44; Mark 15:39"> and they sat and kept guard over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 23:38; John 19:19–22; Mark 15:26"> Above his head they put the charge against him, which read “This is Jesus, the king of the Jews.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43; Isaiah 53:12"> Two robbers were crucified with him, one on the right of him and one on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 109:25; Lamentations 1:12; Psalms 69:7–12"> Those who passed by insulted him, shaking their heads</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 26:61; Matthew 4:6; Matthew 4:3"> and saying, “You who were going to destroy the temple and rebuild it in three days, save yourself! If you are the Son of God, come down from the cross!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Isaiah 28:22; Psalms 22:12–13"> In the same way the chief priests were mocking him, along with the scribes and elders, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 1:49; Matthew 27:37; Acts 4:14"> “He saved others, but he cannot save himself. He is the King of Israel. Let him come down off the cross, and then we will believe in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 22:8; John 10:36; Isaiah 37:10"> He trusts in God, let God rescue him now, if God consents to release him. For he even said, 'I am the Son of God.'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:38; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:39–43"> In the same way the robbers who were crucified with him also spoke insults to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Amos 8:9; Mark 15:33–34; Luke 23:44–45"> Now from the sixth hour darkness came over the whole land until the ninth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 53:10"> About the ninth hour, Jesus cried with a loud voice and said, “Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani?” which means, “My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Mark 15:35–36; Matthew 11:14"> When some of those who were standing there heard it, they said, “He is calling for Elijah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36"> Immediately one of them ran and took a sponge, filled it with sour wine, put it on a reed staff, and gave it to him to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> The rest of them said, “Leave him alone. Let us see whether Elijah comes to save him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:30; Isaiah 53:9–12; Psalms 22:14–15"> Then Jesus cried out again with a loud voice and gave up his spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Mark 15:38; Hebrews 6:19"> Behold, the curtain of the temple was split in two from the top to the bottom, and the earth shook, and the rocks split apart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; 1 Thessalonians 4:14; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> The tombs were opened, and the bodies of the saints who had fallen asleep were raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Isaiah 48:2; Revelation 21:2"> They came out of the tombs after his resurrection, entered the holy city, and appeared to many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:36; Mark 15:39; Matthew 27:43"> Now when the centurion and those who were watching Jesus saw the earthquake and the things that had happened, they became very afraid and said, “Truly this was the Son of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Luke 23:48–49; John 19:25–27"> Many women who had followed Jesus from Galilee to care for him were there looking on from a distance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 19:25; Mark 15:47–1; Matthew 28:1"> Among them were Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James and Joseph, and the mother of the sons of Zebedee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Mark 15:42–47; Luke 23:50–56"> When it was evening, there came a rich man from Arimathea, named Joseph, who was also a disciple of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 23:52–53; Mark 15:44–46"> He approached Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus. Then Pilate ordered it to be given to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> Joseph took the body, wrapped it in a clean linen cloth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 28:2; Matthew 27:66"> and laid it in his own new tomb that he had cut into the rock. Then he rolled a large stone against the door of the tomb and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Matthew 28:1; Matthew 27:56"> Mary Magdalene and the other Mary were there, sitting opposite the tomb.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 15:42; John 19:14; John 19:42"> The next day, which was the day after the Preparation, the chief priests and the Pharisees were gathered together with Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 9:22; Matthew 16:21; John 2:19"> They said, “Sir, we remember that when that deceiver was alive, he said, 'After three days will I rise again.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Matthew 28:13; Matthew 12:45"> Therefore command that the tomb be made secure until the third day, otherwise his disciples may come and steal him and say to the people, 'He has risen from the dead,' and the last deception will be worse than the first.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Matthew 27:66; Matthew 28:11–15; Proverbs 21:30"> Pilate said to them, “Take a guard. Go and make it as secure as you can.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Daniel 6:17; Matthew 27:60; 2 Timothy 2:19"> So they went and made the tomb secure, sealing the stone and placing the guard.</span>
</div>
