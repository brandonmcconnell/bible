<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:4–10; Matthew 13:1–15; Mark 4:1–12"> On that day Jesus went out of the house and sat beside the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 5:3; Matthew 4:25; Mark 4:1"> A very large crowd gathered around him, so he got into a boat and sat in it, while the whole crowd stood on the beach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 4:2–9; Mark 4:13; Psalms 78:2"> Then Jesus said many things to them in parables. He said, “Behold, a farmer went out to sow seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:18–19"> As he sowed, some seeds fell beside the road, and the birds came and devoured them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Ezekiel 36:26; Zechariah 7:12"> Other seeds fell on rocky ground, where they did not have much soil. Immediately they sprang up, because the soil had no depth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 13:21; James 1:11–12; Colossians 1:23"> But when the sun had risen, they were scorched because they had no root, and they withered away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 4:18–19; Jeremiah 4:3–4; Genesis 3:18"> Other seeds fell among the thorn plants. The thorn plants grew up and choked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 13:23; Luke 8:15; Genesis 26:12"> Other seeds fell on good soil and produced a crop, some one hundred times as much, some sixty, and some thirty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 2:11; Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:7"> He who has ears, let him listen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 4:33–34; Mark 4:10"> The disciples came and said to Jesus, “Why do you talk to the crowd in parables?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9–10; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 19:11"> Jesus answered and said to them, “You have been given the privilege of understanding mysteries of the kingdom of heaven, but to them it is not given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:29; Mark 4:24–25; Luke 8:18"> For whoever has will be given more, and he will have an abundance. But whoever does not have, even what he has will be taken away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:2; Jeremiah 5:21; Isaiah 42:18–20"> This is why I talk to them in parables: Though they are seeing, they do not see; and though they are hearing, they do not hear, or understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Ezekiel 12:2; 2 Corinthians 3:14"> To them the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled, that which says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'While hearing you will hear, but you will in no way understand;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">while seeing you will see, but you will in no way perceive.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Mark 4:12; Zechariah 7:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">For this people's heart has become dull,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they are hard of hearing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they have closed their eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so they should not see with their eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or hear with their ears,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or understand with their hearts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so they would turn again,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I would heal them.'</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:23–24; Ephesians 1:17–18; Matthew 16:17"> But blessed are your eyes, for they see; and your ears, for they hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:56; Hebrews 11:13; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> Truly I say to you that many prophets and righteous men desired to see the things that you see, and did not see them. They desired to hear the things that you hear, and did not hear them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:11–12; Luke 8:11–15; Mark 4:13–20"> Listen then to the parable of the farmer who sowed his seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 8:11–15; Matthew 13:38; Hebrews 2:1"> When anyone hears the word of the kingdom but does not understand it, the evil one comes and snatches away what has been sown in his heart. This is the seed that was sown beside the road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31–32; Psalms 106:12–13; John 5:35"> What was sown on rocky ground is the person who hears the word and immediately receives it with joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 2 Peter 1:8–9; Ephesians 3:17"> yet he has no root in himself and he endures for a while. When tribulation or persecution arises because of the word, he quickly falls away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Luke 8:14; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> What was sown among the thorn plants, this is the person who hears the word, but the cares of the world and the deceitfulness of riches choke the word, and he becomes unfruitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 1:11; Matthew 12:33; John 15:16"> What was sown on the good soil, this is the person who hears the word and understands it. He bears fruit and makes a crop, some yielding one hundred times as much as was planted, some sixty, and some thirty times as much.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 25:1; Matthew 13:47; Matthew 20:1"> Jesus presented another parable to them. He said, “The kingdom of heaven is like a man who sowed good seed in his field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; 2 Peter 2:1; Acts 20:30–31"> But while people slept, his enemy came and also sowed weeds among the wheat and then went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 4:26–29"> When the blades sprouted and then produced their crop, then the weeds appeared also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 Corinthians 6:1; 2 Corinthians 5:18–20"> The servants of the landowner came and said to him, 'Sir, did you not sow good seed in your field? How does it now have weeds?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:3–7; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; Luke 9:49–54"> He said to them, 'An enemy has done this.' The servants said to him, 'So do you want us to go and pull them out?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The landowner said, 'No. Because while you are pulling out the weeds, you might uproot the wheat with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 3:12; 1 Timothy 5:24; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> Let both grow together until the harvest. At the time of the harvest I will say to the reapers, “First pull out the weeds and tie them in bundles to burn them, but gather the wheat into my barn.”'”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 13:18–19; Matthew 17:20; Mark 4:30–32"> Then Jesus presented another parable to them. He said, “The kingdom of heaven is like a mustard seed which a man took and sowed in his field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:6; Daniel 4:12; Ezekiel 17:23–24"> This seed is indeed the smallest of all seeds. But when it has grown, it is greater than the garden plants. It becomes a tree, so that the birds of the air come and nest in its branches.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Galatians 5:9; 1 Corinthians 5:6–7; Luke 13:21"> Jesus then told them another parable. “The kingdom of heaven is like yeast that a woman took and mixed with three measures of flour until all the dough had risen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 4:33–34; John 16:25; Matthew 13:13"> All these things Jesus said to the crowds in parables; and he said nothing to them without a parable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Ephesians 3:9; 1 Peter 1:20–21"> This was in order that what had been said through the prophet might come true, when he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will open my mouth in parables.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will say things that were hidden from the foundation of the world.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 16:17–20; Matthew 13:11; Matthew 9:28"> Then Jesus left the crowds and went into the house. His disciples came to him and said, “Explain to us the parable of the weeds of the field.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–7; Matthew 13:41; Luke 10:16"> Jesus answered and said, “He who sows the good seed is the Son of Man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 8:44; Philippians 3:18–19; Acts 13:10"> The field is the world; and the good seed, these are the sons of the kingdom. The weeds are the sons of the evil one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 13:49; Revelation 14:15–19; Matthew 25:31"> and the enemy who sowed them is the devil. The harvest is the end of the age, and the reapers are angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 13:39"> Therefore, as the weeds are gathered up and burned with fire, so will it be at the end of the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Matthew 18:7; 2 Peter 2:1–2"> The Son of Man will send out his angels, and they will gather out of his kingdom all the things that cause sin and those who commit iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 13:50; Luke 16:23–24; Matthew 8:12"> They will throw them into the furnace of fire, where there will be weeping and grinding of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; Matthew 11:15; Luke 12:32"> Then will the righteous people shine like the sun in the kingdom of their Father. He who has ears, let him listen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Proverbs 2:2–5; Colossians 2:3; Philippians 3:7–9"> The kingdom of heaven is like a treasure hidden in a field. A man found it and hid it. In his joy he goes, sells everything he possesses, and buys that field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 3:13–18; Matthew 16:26; Matthew 13:24"> Again, the kingdom of heaven is like a man who is a merchant looking for valuable pearls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 18:28–30; Proverbs 2:4"> When he found one pearl of very great value, he went and sold everything that he possessed and bought it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 15:6; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Matthew 22:9–10"> Again, the kingdom of heaven is like a net that was cast into the sea, and that gathered creatures of every kind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Matthew 13:40–43; Matthew 3:12"> When it was filled, the fishermen drew it up on the beach. Then they sat down and gathered the good things into containers, but the worthless things they threw away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Revelation 20:12–15; Matthew 24:31; Matthew 13:39"> It will be this way at the end of the age. The angels will come and separate the wicked from among the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 13:42; Matthew 8:12; Revelation 14:10–11"> They will throw them into the furnace of fire, where there will be weeping and grinding of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Matthew 13:11; Mark 4:34"> Have you understood all these things?” The disciples said to him, “Yes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 John 2:7–8; 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Titus 1:9"> Then Jesus said to them, “Therefore every scribe who has become a disciple to the kingdom of heaven is like a man who is the owner of a house, who draws out old and new things from his treasure.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Mark 4:33–35"> Then it came about that when Jesus had finished these parables, he departed from that place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; John 7:15–16; Mark 6:1–6"> Then Jesus entered his own region and taught the people in their synagogue. The result was that they were astonished and said, “Where does this man get his wisdom and these miracles from?</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 6:42; Mark 6:3; Luke 3:23"> Is not this man the carpenter's son? Is not his mother called Mary? Are not his brothers James, Joseph, Simon, and Judas?</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Are not all his sisters with us? Where did he get all these things?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 4:44; Matthew 11:6; Luke 4:24"> They were offended by him. But Jesus said to them, “A prophet is not without honor except in his own country and in his own family.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Mark 6:5–6; Romans 11:20; Hebrews 3:12–19"> He did not do many miracles there because of their unbelief.</span>
