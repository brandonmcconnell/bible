<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:1; Luke 20:45–46; Matthew 15:10–20"> Then Jesus spoke to the crowds and to his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 20:46; Mark 12:38; Malachi 2:7"> He said, “The scribes and the Pharisees sit in Moses' seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:16; Romans 2:19–24"> Therefore whatever they command you to do, do these things and observe them. But do not imitate their deeds, for they say things but then do not do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 11:46; Acts 15:10; Galatians 6:13"> Yes, they bind heavy burdens that are difficult to carry, and then they put them on people's shoulders. But they themselves will not move a finger to carry them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 6:1–16; John 12:43; Philippians 2:3"> They do all their deeds to be seen by people. For they make their phylacteries wide, and they enlarge the edges of their garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 John 1:9; Luke 11:43–54; Mark 12:38–39"> They love the chief places at feasts and the chief seats in the synagogues,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 1:38; John 1:49; John 3:26"> and special greetings in the marketplaces, and to be called 'Rabbi' by people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 3:1; 1 Corinthians 1:12–13; 1 Peter 5:3"> But you must not be called 'Rabbi,' for you have only one teacher, and all you are brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:1; Hebrews 12:9; Malachi 1:6"> And call no man on earth your father, for you have only one Father, and he is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Neither must you be called 'teacher,' for you have only one teacher, the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:19; Galatians 5:13; John 13:14–15"> But he who is greatest among you will be your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; James 4:6; Proverbs 29:23"> Whoever exalts himself will be humbled, and whoever humbles himself will be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Matthew 23:29; Matthew 23:23"> But woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! You shut the kingdom of heaven against people. For you do not enter it yourselves, and neither do you allow those about to enter to do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The best ancient copies omit verse 14 (some copies add the verse after verse 12). Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you devour widows' houses, while you make a show of long prayers. You will therefore receive greater condemnation .">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 2:3; Acts 13:10; Acts 13:43"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you go over sea and land to make one convert, and when he has become one, you make him twice as much a son of hell as you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:14; Matthew 5:33–35; James 5:12"> Woe to you, you blind guides, you who say, 'Whoever swears by the temple, it is nothing. But whoever swears by the gold of the temple, he is bound to his oath.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Numbers 16:38–39; Psalms 94:8"> You blind fools! Which is greater, the gold or the temple that makes the gold holy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And, 'Whoever swears by the altar, it is nothing. But whoever swears by the gift that is on it, he is bound to his oath.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:29"> You blind people! Which is greater, the gift or the altar that makes the gift holy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Therefore, he who swears by the altar swears by it and by everything on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; 1 Kings 8:13; 2 Chronicles 6:2"> The one who swears by the temple swears by it and by the one who lives in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 5:34; Psalms 11:4; Isaiah 66:1"> And the one who swears by heaven swears by the throne of God and by him who sits on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Micah 6:8; Hosea 6:6"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you tithe mint and dill and cumin, but you have left undone the weightier matters of the law—justice and mercy and faith. But these you ought to have done and not to have left the other undone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 19:24; Matthew 27:6–8; Matthew 15:2–6"> You blind guides, you who strain out a gnat but swallow a camel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:39–40; Matthew 15:19–20; Mark 7:4–13"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you clean the outside of the cup and of the plate, but inside they are full of greed and self-indulgence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; James 4:8; Hebrews 10:22"> You blind Pharisee! Clean first the inside of the cup and of the plate, so that the outside may become clean also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 11:44; Acts 23:3; Numbers 19:16"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you are like whitewashed tombs, which on the outside look beautiful, but on the inside are full of dead men's bones and everything unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Psalms 51:6; Matthew 23:5"> In the same way, you also outwardly appear righteous to men, but on the inside you are full of hypocrisy and lawlessness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 11:47–48; Acts 2:29"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you build the tombs of the prophets and decorate the tombs of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:30; Matthew 23:34–35; Matthew 21:35–36"> You say, 'If we had lived in the days of our fathers, we would not have been participants with them in shedding the blood of the prophets.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 7:51–52; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Joshua 24:22"> Therefore you testify against yourselves that you are sons of those who killed the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 15:16; Numbers 32:14; 1 Thessalonians 2:16"> You also fill up the measure of your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:7; Matthew 12:34; Matthew 5:22"> You serpents, you offspring of vipers, how will you escape the judgment of hell?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 10:16–17; Matthew 13:52; Colossians 1:28"> Therefore, see, I am sending you prophets and wise men and scribes. Some of them you will kill and crucify, and some you will whip in your synagogues and drive them out from city to city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20–22; Zechariah 1:1; Hebrews 11:4"> The result is that upon you will come all the righteous blood that has been shed on the earth, from the blood of righteous Abel, to the blood of Zechariah son of Barachiah, whom you killed between the sanctuary and the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Matthew 24:34; Mark 13:30–31"> Truly I say to you, all these things will come upon this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 13:34–35; Ruth 2:12; Psalms 91:4"> Jerusalem, Jerusalem, you who kill the prophets and stone those who are sent to you! How often did I long to gather your children together, just as a hen gathers her chicks under her wings, but you were not willing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:5; Luke 13:35; Luke 21:24"> See, your house is left to you desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 118:26; Matthew 21:9; Romans 11:25"> For I say to you, You will not see me from now on until you say, 'Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord.'”</span>
