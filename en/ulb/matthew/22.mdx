<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 20:1–16; Matthew 12:43–45; Luke 14:16"> Jesus spoke to them again in parables, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–9; Matthew 13:24; Ephesians 5:24–32"> “The kingdom of heaven is like a certain king who prepared a marriage feast for his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; John 5:40; Luke 14:15–17"> He sent out his servants to call those who had been invited to come to the marriage feast, but they would not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:36; John 6:50–57"> Again the king sent other servants, saying, 'Tell them who are invited, “See, I have prepared my dinner. My oxen and fattened calves have been killed, and all things are ready. Come to the marriage feast.”'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:3; Luke 14:18–20; 1 John 2:15–16"> But they paid no attention and went away, one to his farm, another to his business.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:19–20; John 16:2–3; Acts 7:51–57"> The others seized the king's servants, treated them shamefully, and killed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 19:27; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Luke 21:24"> The king was angry, and he sent his soldiers and they killed those murderers and burned their city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; Acts 13:46; Revelation 3:4"> Then he said to his servants, 'The wedding is ready, but those who were invited were not worthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 14:21–24; Proverbs 1:20–23; Mark 16:15–16"> Therefore go to the highway crossings and invite as many people to the marriage feast as you can find.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Revelation 19:6–9; 2 Corinthians 12:21"> The servants went out to the highways and gathered together all the people they found, both bad and good. So the wedding hall was filled with guests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 19:8; Hebrews 4:12–13"> But when the king came in to look at the guests, he saw a man there who was not wearing wedding clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:9; Job 5:16; Matthew 20:13"> The king said to him, 'Friend, how did you come in here without wedding clothes?' But the man was speechless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 25:30; Matthew 24:51"> Then the king said to the servants, 'Bind this man hand and foot, and throw him into the outer darkness, where there will be weeping and the grinding of teeth.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Luke 13:23–24; Matthew 7:13–14"> For many people are called, but few are chosen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Mark 12:13–17; Psalms 2:2"> Then the Pharisees went and planned how they might entrap Jesus in his own talk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 3:6; Mark 8:15; Mark 12:14"> Then they sent to him their disciples, together with the Herodians. They said to Jesus, “Teacher, we know that you are truthful, and that you teach God's way in truth. You care for no one's opinion, and you do not show partiality between people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 2:1; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 17:25"> So tell us, what do you think? Is it lawful to pay taxes to Caesar or not?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:6; Luke 5:22; John 2:25"> But Jesus understood their wickedness and said, “Why are you testing me, you hypocrites?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 20:2; Revelation 6:6; Matthew 18:28"> Show me the coin for the tax.” Then they brought a denarius to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 20:24"> Jesus said to them, “Whose image and name are these?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:7; Proverbs 24:21; Acts 4:19"> They said to him, “Caesar's.” Then Jesus said to them, “Then give to Caesar the things that are Caesar's, and to God the things that are God's.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 12:12; Matthew 22:46; Luke 20:25–26"> When they heard it, they marveled. Then they left him and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Mark 12:18–27; Acts 23:6–8"> On that day some Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection, came to him. They asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Luke 20:28; Ruth 1:11"> saying, “Teacher, Moses said, 'If a man dies, having no children, his brother must marry his wife and have children for his brother.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:29–33; Mark 12:19–23; Hebrews 9:27"> There were seven brothers. The first married and then died. Having left no children, he left his wife to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then the second brother did the same thing, then the third, all the way to the seventh brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> After them all, the woman died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Now in the resurrection, whose wife will she be of the seven brothers? For they all had married her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 20:9; Romans 15:4; Luke 1:37"> But Jesus answered and said to them, “You are mistaken, because you do not know the scriptures or the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 20:34–36; Mark 12:24–25; Matthew 24:38"> For in the resurrection they neither marry nor are given in marriage. Instead, they are like angels in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 12:3; Matthew 21:16; Matthew 21:42"> But concerning the resurrection of the dead, have you not read what was spoken to you by God, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Mark 12:26–27; Luke 20:37–38"> 'I am the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob'? God is not the God of the dead, but of the living.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 12:17; Mark 6:2; John 7:46"> When the crowds heard this, they were astonished at his teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 12:28–31; Matthew 25:3–5; Matthew 12:14"> But when the Pharisees heard that Jesus had silenced the Sadducees, they gathered themselves together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 7:30; Luke 10:25–37; Luke 11:52"> One of them, a lawyer, asked him a question, testing him—</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 12:28–33; Matthew 5:19–20; Luke 11:42"> “Teacher, which is the greatest commandment in the law?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 12:29–30; Luke 10:27; Deuteronomy 6:5"> Jesus said to him, “'Love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> This is the great and first commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 12:31; Luke 10:27–37; Leviticus 19:18"> And a second commandment is like it—'Love your neighbor as yourself.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 7:12; 1 John 4:19–21"> On these two commandments depend the whole law and the prophets.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 22:34"> Now while the Pharisees were still gathered together, Jesus asked them a question.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; John 6:68–69; Matthew 16:13–17"> He said, “What do you think about the Christ? Whose son is he?” They said to him, “The son of David.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:2; Revelation 4:2; Mark 12:36"> Jesus said to them, “How then does David in the Spirit call him Lord, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Acts 2:34–35"> <span className="pl pl-1">'The Lord said to my Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sit at my right hand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">until I make your enemies your footstool”'?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Revelation 22:16; John 8:58"> If David then calls the Christ 'Lord,' how is he David's son?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Mark 12:34; Luke 20:40; Luke 14:6"> No one was able to answer him a word, and no man dared ask him any more questions from that day on.</span>
