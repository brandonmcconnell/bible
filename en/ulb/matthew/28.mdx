<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 9:3; Acts 1:10; Mark 16:5"> His appearance was like lightning, and his clothing as white as snow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Daniel 10:7; Matthew 27:65–66"> The guards shook with fear and became like dead men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; Hebrews 1:14; Isaiah 41:10"> The angel addressed the women and said to them, “Do not be afraid, for I know that you seek Jesus, who has been crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 16:6; Matthew 12:40; Luke 24:6–8"> He is not here, but is risen, just as he said. Come see the place where the Lord lay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Luke 24:22–24; Matthew 28:10"> Go quickly and tell his disciples, 'He has risen from the dead. See, he is going ahead of you to Galilee. There you will see him.' See, I have told you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 2:11; Mark 16:8; Luke 24:36–41"> The women quickly left the tomb with fear and great joy, and ran to tell his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 28:17; John 20:14–17; Matthew 14:33"> Behold, Jesus met them and said, “Greetings!” The women came, took hold of his feet and worshiped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:29; John 20:17; Matthew 28:7"> Then Jesus said to them, “Do not be afraid. Go tell my brothers to leave for Galilee. There they will see me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 27:65–66; Matthew 28:4"> Now while the women were going, behold, some of the guards went into the city and told the chief priests all the things that had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:3–4; Acts 4:5–22; Matthew 27:1–2"> When the priests had met with the elders and discussed the matter with them, they gave a large amount of money to the soldiers</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:64"> and told them, “Say to others, 'Jesus' disciples came by night and stole his body while we were sleeping.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Acts 12:19"> If this report reaches the governor, we will persuade him and take any worries away from you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 27:8; Matthew 26:15"> So the soldiers took the money and did as they had been instructed. This report spread widely among the Jews and continues even today.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Matthew 28:10; Matthew 28:7"> But the eleven disciples went to Galilee, to the mountain to which Jesus had directed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 45:11; Psalms 2:12; John 5:23"> When they saw him, they worshiped him, but some doubted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:35; 1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 11:27"> Jesus came to them and spoke to them and said, “All authority has been given to me in heaven and on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 2:38–39; Acts 1:8"> Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations. Baptize them into the name of the Father, of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Joshua 1:5; Acts 18:9–10"> Teach them to obey all the things that I have commanded you. See, I am with you always, even to the end of the age.”</span>
