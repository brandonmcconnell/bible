<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Mark 8:15; Luke 9:7–9"> About that time, Herod the tetrarch heard the news about Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 16:14; John 10:41; Mark 8:28"> He said to his servants, “This is John the Baptist; he has risen from the dead. Therefore these powers are at work in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 6:17; Luke 3:19–20; Matthew 4:12"> For Herod had arrested John, bound him, and put him in prison because of Herodias, his brother Philip's wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:21; Leviticus 18:16; Mark 6:18"> For John had said to him, “It is not lawful for you to have her as your wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 11:9; Mark 6:19–20"> Herod would have put him to death, but he feared the people, because they regarded him as a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 6:21–23; Genesis 40:20; Hosea 1:5–6"> But when Herod's birthday came, the daughter of Herodias danced in the midst and pleased Herod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 5:3; Esther 5:6; Esther 7:2"> In response, he promised with an oath to give her whatever she should ask.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 6:24; Proverbs 29:10; Proverbs 1:16"> After being instructed by her mother, she said, “Give me here, on a platter, the head of John the Baptist.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:12–16; 1 Samuel 14:28; Mark 6:26"> The king was very upset by her request, but because of his oath and because of all those at dinner with him, he ordered that it should be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 17:12; Matthew 21:35–36; Luke 9:9"> He sent and beheaded John in the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 16:6; Genesis 49:7; Ezekiel 16:3–4"> Then his head was brought on a platter and given to the girl and she took it to her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 8:2; Matthew 27:58–61"> Then his disciples came, took up the corpse, and buried it. After this, they went and told Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:10–17; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 6:30–44"> Now when Jesus heard this, he withdrew from there in a boat to an isolated place. When the crowds heard of it, they followed him on foot from the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Matthew 9:36; Hebrews 2:17"> Then Jesus came before them and saw the large crowd. He had compassion on them and healed their sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 15:23; Mark 8:3; Mark 6:35–36"> When the evening had come, the disciples came to him and said, “This is a deserted place, and the day is already over. Dismiss the crowds, so that they can go into the villages and buy food for themselves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; Ecclesiastes 11:2"> But Jesus said to them, “They have no need to go away. You give them something to eat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:33–34; Mark 6:37–38; Luke 9:13"> They said to him, “We have here only five loaves of bread and two fish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Jesus said, “Bring them to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 8:6–7; Luke 24:30; 1 Samuel 9:13"> Then Jesus ordered the crowd to sit down on the grass. He took the five loaves and the two fish. Looking up to heaven, he blessed and broke the loaves and gave them to the disciples, and the disciples gave them to the crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:12; Mark 6:42–44; Matthew 16:8–10"> They all ate and were filled. Then they took up what remained of the broken pieces of food—twelve baskets full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 9:8–11; Acts 4:4"> Those who ate were about five thousand men, besides women and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 6:45–51; John 6:15–21; Matthew 15:39"> Immediately he made the disciples get into the boat and go before him to the other side, while he sent away the crowds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 6:46; Matthew 26:36"> After he had sent away the crowds, he went up on the mountain by himself to pray. When evening came, he was there alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:18; Mark 6:48; Matthew 8:24"> But the boat was now a long way from land, being tossed about by the waves, for the wind was blowing against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:8; John 6:19; Mark 6:48"> In the fourth watch of the night Jesus approached them, walking on the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 6:49–50; Revelation 1:17; Luke 1:11–12"> When the disciples saw him walking on the sea, they were terrified and said, “It is a ghost,” and they cried out in fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Revelation 1:17–18; John 16:33"> But Jesus spoke to them right away and said, “Be brave! It is I! Do not be afraid.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:68; Romans 12:3; Matthew 26:33–35"> Peter answered him and said, “Lord, if it is you, command me to come to you on the water.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Mark 9:23; Acts 3:16"> Jesus said, “Come.” So Peter got out from the boat and walked on the water to go to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 3:54–57; 2 Timothy 4:16–17; Mark 14:38"> But when Peter saw the wind, he became afraid. As he began to sink, he cried out and said, “Lord, save me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:6–8; Psalms 138:7; Genesis 22:14"> Jesus immediately stretched out his hand, took hold of Peter, and said to him, “You of little faith, why did you doubt?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 107:29–30; Mark 4:41; John 6:21"> Then when Jesus and Peter went into the boat, the wind ceased blowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:27; Psalms 2:7; Romans 1:4"> Then the disciples in the boat worshiped Jesus and said, “Truly you are the Son of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 6:53–56; Luke 5:1; John 6:24–25"> When they had crossed over, they came to land at Gennesaret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 4:24–25; Mark 6:55; Mark 1:28–34"> When the men in that place recognized Jesus, they sent messages everywhere into the surrounding area, and they brought to him everyone who was sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Mark 3:10; Exodus 28:33–43"> They begged him that they might just touch the edge of his garment, and as many as touched it were healed.</span>
