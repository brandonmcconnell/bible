<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:13; Luke 8:37; Matthew 8:18"> Jesus entered a boat, crossed over, and came into his own city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 2:1–12; Luke 5:18–26; Jeremiah 31:33–34"> Behold, they brought to him a paralyzed man lying on a mat. Seeing their faith, Jesus said to the paralyzed man, “Son, be of good cheer. Your sins have been forgiven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 26:65; Luke 5:21; Leviticus 24:16"> Behold, some of the scribes said among themselves, “This man is blaspheming.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:8; Matthew 12:25; Revelation 2:23"> Jesus knew their thoughts and said, “Why are you thinking evil in your hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 5:8–14; Mark 2:9–12; Acts 3:16"> For which is easier, to say, 'Your sins are forgiven,' or to say, 'Get up and walk'?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Matthew 9:5; John 20:21–23"> But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins, ... “ he said to the paralytic, “Get up, pick up your mat, and go to your house.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then the man got up and went away to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 7:16; Matthew 15:31; Acts 4:21"> When the crowds saw this, they were amazed and praised God, who had given such authority to people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 5:27–38; Mark 2:14–22; Luke 19:2–10"> As Jesus passed by from there, he saw a man named Matthew, who was sitting at the tax collection's tent. He said to him, “Follow me.” He got up and followed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13–16; Luke 5:29–32; Mark 2:15–17"> As Jesus sat down to eat in the house, behold, many tax collectors and sinful people came and dined with Jesus and his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:19; Luke 5:30; Mark 2:16"> When the Pharisees saw it, they said to his disciples, “Why does your teacher eat with tax collectors and sinful people?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 5:31; Revelation 3:17–18; Mark 2:17"> When Jesus heard this, he said, “People who are strong in body do not need a physician, only those who are sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Luke 19:10; Hosea 6:6"> You should go and learn what this means: 'I desire mercy and not sacrifice.' For I came not to call the righteous to repent, but sinners.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:33–39; Mark 2:18–22; Matthew 6:16"> Then the disciples of John came to him and said, “Why do we and the Pharisees often fast, but your disciples do not fast?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; John 3:29; Revelation 21:2"> Jesus said to them, “Can wedding attendants be sorrowful while the bridegroom is still with them? But the days will come when the bridegroom will be taken away from them, and then they will fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:1–2; John 16:12; Psalms 125:3"> No man puts a piece of new cloth on an old garment, for the patch will tear away from the garment, and a worse tear will be made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 9:4; Job 32:19; Psalms 119:83"> Neither do people put new wine into old wineskins. If they do, the skins will burst, the wine will be spilled, and the wineskins will be destroyed. Instead, they put new wine into fresh wineskins, and both will be preserved.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:41–56; Mark 5:22–43; Matthew 8:2"> While Jesus was saying these things to them, behold, an official came and bowed down to him. He said, “My daughter has just now died, but come and lay your hand on her, and she will live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 10:38; Galatians 6:9–10; Matthew 8:7"> Then Jesus got up and followed him, and so did his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 8:43–56; Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56"> Behold, a woman who suffered from a discharge of blood for twelve years came up behind Jesus and touched the edge of his garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 5:26–33; Luke 8:45–47; Acts 19:12"> For she had said to herself, “If only I touch his clothes, I will be made well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Matthew 15:28; Matthew 9:2"> But Jesus turned and saw her, and said, “Daughter, take courage; your faith has made you well.” And the woman was healed from that hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:25; Mark 5:35–40; Luke 8:49–51"> When Jesus came into the official's house, he saw the flute players and the crowds making much noise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 20:10; John 11:11–13; Matthew 27:39–43"> He said, “Go away, for the girl is not dead, but she is asleep.” But they laughed at him in mockery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 9:27; Mark 5:41; Luke 8:54"> When the crowd had been put outside, he entered the room and took her by the hand, and the girl got up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 1:45"> The news about this spread into all that region.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Matthew 20:30–31; Mark 10:46–48"> As Jesus passed by from there, two blind men followed him. They kept shouting and saying, “Have mercy on us, Son of David!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:23–24; Matthew 13:58; Matthew 8:2"> When Jesus had come into the house, the blind men came to him. Jesus said to them, “Do you believe that I can do this?” They said to him, “Yes, Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Mark 10:52; Matthew 8:13"> Then Jesus touched their eyes and said, “Let it be done to you according to your faith,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Luke 5:14; Matthew 17:9"> and their eyes were opened. Then Jesus strictly commanded them and said, “See that no one knows about this.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 7:36; Matthew 9:26; Mark 1:44–45"> But the two men went out and spread the news about this throughout that region.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Matthew 12:22–24; Luke 11:14"> As those two men were going away, behold, a mute man possessed by a demon was brought to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 35:6; Mark 7:32–37; Exodus 4:11–12"> When the demon had been driven out, the mute man spoke. The crowds were astonished and said, “This has never been seen before in Israel!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 11:15; Mark 3:22; Matthew 12:23–24"> But the Pharisees were saying, “By the ruler of the demons, he drives out demons.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 4:23–24; Acts 10:38; Mark 6:56"> Jesus went about all the cities and the villages. He continued teaching in their synagogues, preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing all kinds of disease and all kinds of sickness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 6:34; Numbers 27:17; Jeremiah 50:6"> When he saw the crowds, he had compassion for them, because they were troubled and discouraged. They were like sheep without a shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 10:2; Mark 16:15; 1 Corinthians 3:9"> He said to his disciples, “The harvest is plentiful, but the laborers are few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 10:1–2; Jeremiah 3:15; 2 Thessalonians 3:1"> Therefore urgently pray to the Lord of the harvest, so that he may send out laborers into his harvest.”</span>
</div>
