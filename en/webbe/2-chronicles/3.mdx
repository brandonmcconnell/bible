<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 6:1"> He began to build in the second day of the second month, in the fourth year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 6:2–3; 1 Chronicles 28:11–19"> Now these are the foundations which Solomon laid for the building of God’s house: the length by cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A cubit is the length from the tip of the middle finger to the elbow on a man’s arm, or about 18 inches or 46 centimetres.">a</a></sup>after the first measure was sixty cubits, and the width twenty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 10:23; Acts 5:12; Acts 3:11"> The porch that was in front, its length, across the width of the house, was twenty cubits, and the height one hundred and twenty; and he overlaid it within with pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:15–17; 1 Kings 6:21–22"> He made the larger room with a ceiling of cypress wood, which he overlaid with fine gold, and ornamented it with palm trees and chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 54:11–12; 1 Chronicles 29:8; Revelation 21:18–21"> He decorated the house with precious stones for beauty. The gold was gold from Parvaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 6:20–22; Exodus 26:29; Ezekiel 7:20"> He also overlaid the house, the beams, the thresholds, its walls, and its doors with gold, and engraved cherubim on the walls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 26:33; 1 Kings 6:19–20; 1 Kings 6:16"> He made the most holy place. Its length, according to the width of the house, was twenty cubits, and its width twenty cubits; and he overlaid it with fine gold, amounting to six hundred talents.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A talent is about 30 kilograms or 66 pounds or 965 Troy ounces, so 600 talents is about 18 metric tonnes">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:11"> The weight of the nails was fifty shekels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.32 Troy ounces, so 50 shekels was about 0.5 kilograms or about 16 Troy ounces.">c</a></sup>of gold. He overlaid the upper rooms with gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 6:23–28; Exodus 25:18"> In the most holy place he made two cherubim by carving, and they overlaid them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The wings of the cherubim were twenty cubits long: the wing of the one was five cubits, reaching to the wall of the house; and the other wing was five cubits, reaching to the wing of the other cherub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The wing of the other cherub was five cubits, reaching to the wall of the house; and the other wing was five cubits, joining to the wing of the other cherub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 25:20"> The wings of these cherubim spread themselves out twenty cubits. They stood on their feet, and their faces were towards the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:3; Hebrews 10:20; Matthew 27:51"> He made the veil of blue, purple, crimson, and fine linen, and ornamented it with cherubim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 7:15–24; Jeremiah 52:20–23"> Also he made before the house two pillars thirty-five cubits high, and the capital that was on the top of each of them was five cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 7:20; 1 Kings 6:21"> He made chains in the inner sanctuary, and put them on the tops of the pillars; and he made one hundred pomegranates, and put them on the chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:21"> He set up the pillars before the temple, one on the right hand and the other on the left; and called the name of that on the right hand Jachin, and the name of that on the left Boaz.</span>
