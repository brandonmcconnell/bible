<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 21:1; Leviticus 13:46; Numbers 12:14"> “Command the children of Israel that they put out of the camp every leper, everyone who has a discharge, and whoever is unclean by a corpse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:14; Leviticus 26:11–12; Psalms 68:18"> You shall put both male and female outside of the camp so that they don’t defile their camp, in the midst of which I dwell.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> The children of Israel did so, and put them outside of the camp; as the LORD spoke to Moses, so the children of Israel did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1–4; Leviticus 5:14–7"> “Speak to the children of Israel: ‘When a man or woman commits any sin that men commit, so as to trespass against the LORD, and that soul is guilty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 5:5; Leviticus 26:40; Joshua 7:19"> then he shall confess his sin which he has done; and he shall make restitution for his guilt in full, add to it the fifth part of it, and give it to him in respect of whom he has been guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 6:6–7; Leviticus 7:7; Leviticus 25:25–26"> But if the man has no kinsman to whom restitution may be made for the guilt, the restitution for guilt which is made to the LORD shall be the priest’s, in addition to the ram of the atonement, by which atonement shall be made for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:3–4; Leviticus 7:6–14; Leviticus 6:17–18"> Every heave offering of all the holy things of the children of Israel, which they present to the priest, shall be his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:13"> Every man’s holy things shall be his; whatever any man gives the priest, it shall be his.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 5:19–20; Proverbs 2:16–17"> “Speak to the children of Israel, and tell them: ‘If any man’s wife goes astray and is unfaithful to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 18:20; Leviticus 20:10; Proverbs 7:18–19"> and a man lies with her carnally, and it is hidden from the eyes of her husband and this is kept concealed, and she is defiled, there is no witness against her, and she isn’t taken in the act;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:6; Proverbs 6:34; Zephaniah 3:8"> and the spirit of jealousy comes on him, and he is jealous of his wife and she is defiled; or if the spirit of jealousy comes on him, and he is jealous of his wife and she isn’t defiled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:16; 1 Kings 17:18; Leviticus 5:11"> then the man shall bring his wife to the priest, and shall bring her offering for her: one tenth of an ephah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1 ephah is about 22 litres or about 2/3 of a bushel">a</a></sup>of barley meal. He shall pour no oil on it, nor put frankincense on it, for it is a meal offering of jealousy, a meal offering of memorial, bringing iniquity to memory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 2:22–23; Jeremiah 17:10; Hebrews 13:4"> The priest shall bring her near, and set her before the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 30:18; John 8:6; Numbers 19:2–9"> The priest shall take holy water in an earthen vessel; and the priest shall take some of the dust that is on the floor of the tabernacle and put it into the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 5:4; Numbers 5:22; Revelation 10:9–10"> The priest shall set the woman before the LORD, and let the hair of the woman’s head go loose, and put the meal offering of memorial in her hands, which is the meal offering of jealousy. The priest shall have in his hand the water of bitterness that brings a curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 7:2; Numbers 5:12; Matthew 26:63"> The priest shall cause her to take an oath and shall tell the woman, “If no man has lain with you, and if you haven’t gone aside to uncleanness, being under your husband’s authority, be free from this water of bitterness that brings a curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 5:12"> But if you have gone astray, being under your husband’s authority, and if you are defiled, and some man has lain with you besides your husband—”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 6:26; Nehemiah 10:29; 1 Samuel 14:24"> then the priest shall cause the woman to swear with the oath of cursing, and the priest shall tell the woman, “May the LORD make you a curse and an oath amongst your people, when the LORD allows your thigh to fall away, and your body to swell;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 109:18; Psalms 89:52; Job 31:39–40"> and this water that brings a curse will go into your bowels, and make your body swell, and your thigh fall away.” The woman shall say, “Amen, Amen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Psalms 51:1; Psalms 51:9"> “‘The priest shall write these curses in a book, and he shall wipe them into the water of bitterness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 5:3–4; Malachi 3:5"> He shall make the woman drink the water of bitterness that causes the curse; and the water that causes the curse shall enter into her and become bitter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 8:27; Numbers 5:18; Numbers 5:15"> The priest shall take the meal offering of jealousy out of the woman’s hand, and shall wave the meal offering before the LORD, and bring it to the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Leviticus 2:9; Leviticus 5:12"> The priest shall take a handful of the meal offering, as its memorial portion, and burn it on the altar, and afterward shall make the woman drink the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:18; Zechariah 8:13; Jeremiah 29:18"> When he has made her drink the water, then it shall happen, if she is defiled and has committed a trespass against her husband, that the water that causes the curse will enter into her and become bitter, and her body will swell, and her thigh will fall away; and the woman will be a curse amongst her people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Micah 7:7–10; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Psalms 113:9"> If the woman isn’t defiled, but is clean; then she shall be free, and shall conceive offspring.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or, seed">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 5:19; Numbers 5:12; Numbers 5:15"> “‘This is the law of jealousy, when a wife, being under her husband, goes astray, and is defiled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> or when the spirit of jealousy comes on a man, and he is jealous of his wife; then he shall set the woman before the LORD, and the priest shall execute on her all this law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Romans 2:8–9; Ezekiel 18:4"> The man shall be free from iniquity, and that woman shall bear her iniquity.’”</span>
