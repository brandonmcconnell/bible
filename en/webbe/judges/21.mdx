<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Mark 6:23; Acts 23:12"> Now the men of Israel had sworn in Mizpah, saying, “None of us will give his daughter to Benjamin as a wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 20:26; Judges 20:18; 1 Samuel 30:4"> The people came to Bethel and sat there until evening before God, and lifted up their voices, and wept severely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; Joshua 7:7–9; Isaiah 63:17"> They said, “The LORD, the God of Israel, why has this happened in Israel, that there should be one tribe lacking in Israel today?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:25; 1 Kings 8:64; Psalms 78:34–35"> On the next day, the people rose early and built an altar there, and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 5:23; Judges 21:18; Judges 21:1"> The children of Israel said, “Who is there amongst all the tribes of Israel who didn’t come up in the assembly to the LORD?” For they had made a great oath concerning him who didn’t come up to the LORD to Mizpah, saying, “He shall surely be put to death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 21:15; Judges 20:23; Luke 19:41–42"> The children of Israel grieved for Benjamin their brother, and said, “There is one tribe cut off from Israel today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 21:18; 1 Samuel 14:28–29"> How shall we provide wives for those who remain, since we have sworn by the LORD that we will not give them of our daughters to wives?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:11–13; 1 Samuel 11:1–3; 2 Samuel 2:5–6"> They said, “What one is there of the tribes of Israel who didn’t come up to the LORD to Mizpah?” Behold, no one came from Jabesh Gilead to the camp to the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For when the people were counted, behold, there were none of the inhabitants of Jabesh Gilead there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 5:23; Judges 21:5; 1 Samuel 11:7"> The congregation sent twelve thousand of the most valiant men there, and commanded them, saying, “Go and strike the inhabitants of Jabesh Gilead with the edge of the sword, with the women and the little ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 31:17–18; Deuteronomy 2:34"> This is the thing that you shall do: you shall utterly destroy every male, and every woman who has lain with a man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Jeremiah 7:12; Psalms 78:60"> They found amongst the inhabitants of Jabesh Gilead four hundred young virgins who had not known man by lying with him; and they brought them to the camp to Shiloh, which is in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 20:47; Deuteronomy 20:10; Ephesians 2:17"> The whole congregation sent and spoke to the children of Benjamin who were in the rock of Rimmon, and proclaimed peace to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 21:12; 1 Corinthians 7:2; Judges 20:47"> Benjamin returned at that time; and they gave them the women whom they had saved alive of the women of Jabesh Gilead. There still weren’t enough for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 21:6; 1 Chronicles 15:13; Isaiah 58:12"> The people grieved for Benjamin, because the LORD had made a breach in the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then the elders of the congregation said, “How shall we provide wives for those who remain, since the women are destroyed out of Benjamin?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 26:55; Numbers 36:7"> They said, “There must be an inheritance for those who are escaped of Benjamin, that a tribe not be blotted out from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 11:35"> However, we may not give them wives of our daughters, for the children of Israel had sworn, saying, ‘Cursed is he who gives a wife to Benjamin.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:10; Deuteronomy 16:1; Deuteronomy 16:13"> They said, “Behold, there is a feast of the LORD from year to year in Shiloh, which is on the north of Bethel, on the east side of the highway that goes up from Bethel to Shechem, and on the south of Lebonah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> They commanded the children of Benjamin, saying, “Go and lie in wait in the vineyards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:34; Exodus 15:20; Ecclesiastes 3:4"> and see, and behold, if the daughters of Shiloh come out to dance in the dances, then come out of the vineyards, and each man catch his wife of the daughters of Shiloh, and go to the land of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 21:18; Judges 21:14"> It shall be, when their fathers or their brothers come to complain to us, that we will say to them, ‘Grant them graciously to us, because we didn’t take for each man his wife in battle, neither did you give them to them; otherwise you would now be guilty.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:48"> The children of Benjamin did so, and took wives for themselves according to their number, of those who danced, whom they carried off. They went and returned to their inheritance, built the cities, and lived in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The children of Israel departed from there at that time, every man to his tribe and to his family, and they each went out from there to his own inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:8; Judges 17:6; Proverbs 3:5"> In those days there was no king in Israel. Everyone did that which was right in his own eyes.</span>
