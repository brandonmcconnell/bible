</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:7; 1 Kings 8:17–18; 2 Kings 4:27"> Nathan said to the king, “Go, do all that is in your heart, for the LORD is with you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:3; Numbers 12:6; Amos 3:7"> That same night, the LORD’s word came to Nathan, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:3–32; 1 Kings 5:3–4; 1 Kings 8:16–19"> “Go and tell my servant David, ‘The LORD says, “Should you build me a house for me to dwell in?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 8:16; Joshua 18:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> For I have not lived in a house since the day that I brought the children of Israel up out of Egypt, even to this day, but have moved around in a tent and in a tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:6; 2 Samuel 5:2; Leviticus 26:11–12"> In all places in which I have walked with all the children of Israel, did I say a word to anyone from the tribes of Israel whom I commanded to be shepherd of my people Israel, saying, ‘Why have you not built me a house of cedar?’”’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 78:70–71; 2 Samuel 6:21; 2 Samuel 12:7"> Now therefore tell my servant David this: ‘The LORD of Armies says, “I took you from the sheep pen, from following the sheep, to be prince over my people, over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:10; 1 Chronicles 17:8; 1 Samuel 18:14"> I have been with you wherever you went, and have cut off all your enemies from before you. I will make you a great name, like the name of the great ones who are on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 9:15; 1 Chronicles 17:9; Jeremiah 24:6"> I will appoint a place for my people Israel, and will plant them, that they may dwell in their own place and be moved no more. The children of wickedness will not afflict them any more, as at the first,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:27; 2 Samuel 7:1; 1 Chronicles 17:10"> and as from the day that I commanded judges to be over my people Israel. I will cause you to rest from all your enemies. Moreover the LORD tells you that the LORD will make you a house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 8:20; Acts 2:30; Acts 13:36"> When your days are fulfilled and you sleep with your fathers, I will set up your offspring after you, who will proceed out of your body, and I will establish his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:31–33; 1 Kings 8:19; 2 Samuel 7:16"> He will build a house for my name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 1:5; Revelation 3:19; 1 Chronicles 17:13"> I will be his father, and he will be my son. If he commits iniquity, I will chasten him with the rod of men and with the stripes of the children of men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:23; 1 Samuel 15:28; Psalms 89:28"> but my loving kindness will not depart from him, as I took it from Saul, whom I put away before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Psalms 89:36–37; 2 Samuel 7:13"> Your house and your kingdom will be made sure forever before you. Your throne will be established forever.”’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:15; Acts 20:27; 1 Corinthians 15:3"> Nathan spoke to David all these words, and according to all this vision.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 8:4; 1 Chronicles 17:16; 1 Samuel 18:18"> Then David the king went in and sat before the LORD; and he said, “Who am I, Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word translated “Lord” is “Adonai.”">a</a></sup>GOD, and what is my house, that you have brought me this far?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 55:8–9; 1 Chronicles 17:17; Psalms 36:7"> This was yet a small thing in your eyes, Lord GOD, but you have spoken also of your servant’s house for a great while to come; and this amongst men, Lord GOD!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 21:17; 1 Samuel 16:7; Psalms 139:1"> What more can David say to you? For you know your servant, Lord GOD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 115:1; Psalms 138:2; Ephesians 1:9"> For your word’s sake, and according to your own heart, you have worked all this greatness, to make your servant know it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:10; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 86:8"> Therefore you are great, LORD God. For there is no one like you, neither is there any God besides you, according to all that we have heard with our ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:26; Deuteronomy 10:21; Deuteronomy 15:15"> What one nation on the earth is like your people, even like Israel, whom God went to redeem to himself for a people, and to make himself a name, and to do great things for you, and awesome things for your land, before your people, whom you redeemed to yourself out of Egypt, from the nations and their gods?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:18; 1 Peter 2:10; Genesis 17:7"> You established for yourself your people Israel to be your people forever; and you, LORD, became their God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 119:49; Ezekiel 36:37; Genesis 32:12"> “Now, LORD God, the word that you have spoken concerning your servant, and concerning his house, confirm it forever, and do as you have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:23–24; Psalms 72:18–19; Matthew 6:9"> Let your name be magnified forever, saying, ‘The LORD of Armies is God over Israel; and the house of your servant David will be established before you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:25–26; Psalms 10:17; Ruth 4:4"> For you, LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, have revealed to your servant, saying, ‘I will build you a house.’ Therefore your servant has found in his heart to pray this prayer to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 17:17; Exodus 34:6; Numbers 23:19"> “Now, O Lord GOD, you are God, and your words are truth, and you have promised this good thing to your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 6:24–26; 1 Chronicles 17:27; Psalms 115:12–15"> Now therefore, let it please you to bless the house of your servant, that it may continue forever before you; for you, Lord GOD, have spoken it. Let the house of your servant be blessed forever with your blessing.”</span>
</div>
