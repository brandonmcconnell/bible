<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Galatians 5:17; Romans 7:23"> Where do wars and fightings amongst you come from? Don’t they come from your pleasures that war in your members?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 16:24; Matthew 7:7–8; James 1:5"> You lust, and don’t have. You murder and covet, and can’t obtain. You fight and make war. You don’t have, because you don’t ask.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 5:14; 1 John 3:22; James 1:6–7"> You ask, and don’t receive, because you ask with wrong motives, so that you may spend it on your pleasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Romans 8:7; John 15:19"> You adulterers and adulteresses, don’t you know that friendship with the world is hostility towards God? Whoever therefore wants to be a friend of the world makes himself an enemy of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 6:19; Numbers 11:29"> Or do you think that the Scripture says in vain, “The Spirit who lives in us yearns jealously”?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 3:34; 1 Peter 5:5"> But he gives more grace. Therefore it says, “God resists the proud, but gives grace to the humble.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–9; Ephesians 4:27; Ephesians 6:11–12"> Be subject therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; Hebrews 10:22; Malachi 3:7"> Draw near to God, and he will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners. Purify your hearts, you double-minded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10–11; Matthew 5:4; Luke 6:25"> Lament, mourn, and weep. Let your laughter be turned to mourning and your joy to gloom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 147:6; Matthew 23:12; 1 Peter 5:6"> Humble yourselves in the sight of the Lord, and he will exalt you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 2:1; Romans 2:1; James 5:9"> Don’t speak against one another, brothers. He who speaks against a brother and judges his brother, speaks against the law and judges the law. But if you judge the law, you are not a doer of the law but a judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 14:4; Romans 2:1; Matthew 10:28"> Only one is the lawgiver, who is able to save and to destroy. But who are you to judge another?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Luke 12:17–20; Genesis 11:3–4"> Come now, you who say, “Today or tomorrow let’s go into this city and spend a year there, trade, and make a profit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; Psalms 39:5; 1 John 2:17"> Yet you don’t know what your life will be like tomorrow. For what is your life? For you are a vapour that appears for a little time and then vanishes away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Hebrews 6:3; Lamentations 3:37"> For you ought to say, “If the Lord wills, we will both live, and do this or that.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; 1 Corinthians 5:6; Psalms 52:7"> But now you glory in your boasting. All such boasting is evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:41; John 13:17; John 15:22"> To him therefore who knows to do good and doesn’t do it, to him it is sin.</span>
