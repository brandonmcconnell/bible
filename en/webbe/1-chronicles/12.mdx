<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:2–6; 1 Chronicles 11:24–25; 1 Chronicles 8:33"> Now these are those who came to David to Ziklag while he was a fugitive from Saul the son of Kish. They were amongst the mighty men, his helpers in war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 3:15; Judges 20:16; 1 Samuel 17:49"> They were armed with bows, and could use both the right hand and the left in slinging stones and in shooting arrows from the bow. They were of Saul’s relatives of the tribe of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:28; 2 Samuel 21:6; 1 Chronicles 11:33"> The chief was Ahiezer, then Joash, the sons of Shemaah the Gibeathite; Jeziel and Pelet, the sons of Azmaveth; Beracah; Jehu the Anathothite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 9:3; Joshua 15:36; 1 Chronicles 11:15"> Ishmaiah the Gibeonite, a mighty man amongst the thirty and a leader of the thirty; Jeremiah; Jahaziel; Johanan; Jozabad the Gederathite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Eluzai; Jerimoth; Bealiah; Shemariah; Shephatiah the Haruphite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Elkanah, Isshiah, Azarel, Joezer, and Jashobeam, the Korahites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 15:58; 1 Chronicles 4:18; 1 Chronicles 4:39"> and Joelah and Zebadiah, the sons of Jeroham of Gedor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:18; 2 Samuel 17:10; 2 Samuel 23:20"> Some Gadites joined David in the stronghold in the wilderness, mighty men of valour, men trained for war, who could handle shield and spear; whose faces were like the faces of lions, and they were as swift as the gazelles on the mountains:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Ezer the chief, Obadiah the second, Eliab the third,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Mishmannah the fourth, Jeremiah the fifth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Attai the sixth, Eliel the seventh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Johanan the eighth, Elzabad the ninth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Jeremiah the tenth, and Machbannai the eleventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:30; Leviticus 26:8"> These of the sons of Gad were captains of the army. He who was least was equal to one hundred, and the greatest to one thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 3:15; Joshua 4:18; Jeremiah 49:19"> These are those who went over the Jordan in the first month, when it had overflowed all its banks; and they put to flight all who lived in the valleys, both towards the east and towards the west.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Some of the children of Benjamin and Judah came to the stronghold to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 2:23; Jude 1:9; 2 Corinthians 13:11"> David went out to meet them, and answered them, “If you have come peaceably to me to help me, my heart will be united with you; but if you have come to betray me to my adversaries, since there is no wrong in my hands, may the God of our fathers see this and rebuke it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 6:34; Judges 3:10; 2 Samuel 17:25"> Then the Spirit came on Amasai, who was chief of the thirty, and he said, “We are yours, David, and on your side, you son of Jesse. Peace, peace be to you, and peace be to your helpers; for your God helps you.” Then David received them and made them captains of the band.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:2–9"> Some of Manasseh also joined David when he came with the Philistines against Saul to battle, but they didn’t help them, for the lords of the Philistines sent him away after consultation, saying, “He will desert to his master Saul to the jeopardy of our heads.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:17; Deuteronomy 1:15; 1 Samuel 29:11"> As he went to Ziklag, some from Manasseh joined him: Adnah, Jozabad, Jediael, Michael, Jozabad, Elihu, and Zillethai, captains of thousands who were of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:1–17; 1 Chronicles 12:20; 1 Chronicles 5:24"> They helped David against the band of raiders, for they were all mighty men of valour and were captains in the army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1; Job 17:9; Genesis 32:2"> For from day to day men came to David to help him, until there was a great army, like God’s army.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:3–4; 1 Chronicles 11:10; 1 Samuel 16:1"> These are the numbers of the heads of those who were armed for war, who came to David to Hebron to turn the kingdom of Saul to him, according to the LORD’s word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The children of Judah who bore shield and spear were six thousand and eight hundred, armed for war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Of the children of Simeon, mighty men of valour for the war: seven thousand and one hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Of the children of Levi: four thousand and six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 11:9; 2 Kings 25:18; 1 Chronicles 6:49–57"> Jehoiada was the leader of the household of Aaron; and with him were three thousand and seven hundred,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; 1 Chronicles 6:8; 1 Chronicles 6:53"> and Zadok, a young man mighty of valour, and of his father’s house twenty-two captains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8–9; 1 Chronicles 12:2; Genesis 31:23"> Of the children of Benjamin, Saul’s relatives: three thousand, for until then, the greatest part of them had kept their allegiance to Saul’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 6:4"> Of the children of Ephraim: twenty thousand and eight hundred, mighty men of valour, famous men in their fathers’ houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 17:1–18"> Of the half-tribe of Manasseh: eighteen thousand, who were mentioned by name, to come and make David king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ephesians 5:17; Luke 12:56–57; Esther 1:13"> Of the children of Issachar, men who had understanding of the times, to know what Israel ought to do, their heads were two hundred; and all their brothers were at their command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; John 1:47"> Of Zebulun, such as were able to go out in the army, who could set the battle in array with all kinds of instruments of war: fifty thousand who could command and were not of double heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Of Naphtali: one thousand captains, and with them with shield and spear thirty-seven thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Of the Danites who could set the battle in array: twenty-eight thousand and six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:33; Joel 2:7"> Of Asher, such as were able to go out in the army, who could set the battle in array: forty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 14:3; Deuteronomy 3:12–16; Joshua 13:7–32"> On the other side of the Jordan, of the Reubenites, the Gadites, and of the half-tribe of Manasseh, with all kinds of instruments of war for the battle: one hundred and twenty thousand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 49:8–10; 1 Kings 8:61; Psalms 101:2"> All these were men of war who could order the battle array, and came with a perfect heart to Hebron to make David king over all Israel; and all the rest also of Israel were of one heart to make David king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; 2 Samuel 19:42; Genesis 26:30"> They were there with David three days, eating and drinking; for their brothers had supplied provisions for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Samuel 16:1; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> Moreover those who were near to them, as far as Issachar, Zebulun, and Naphtali, brought bread on donkeys, on camels, on mules, and on oxen: supplies of flour, cakes of figs, clusters of raisins, wine, oil, cattle, and sheep in abundance; for there was joy in Israel.</span>
</div>
