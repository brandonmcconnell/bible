<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 2:13; Ephesians 2:5–6; Colossians 3:1–4"> You were made alive when you were dead in transgressions and sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 6:12; 1 John 5:19; Ephesians 2:3"> in which you once walked according to the course of this world, according to the prince of the power of the air, the spirit who now works in the children of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Ephesians 4:22; Galatians 5:16–24"> We also all once lived amongst them in the lusts of our flesh, doing the desires of the flesh and of the mind, and were by nature children of wrath, even as the rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; Ephesians 1:7; Psalms 86:15"> But God, being rich in mercy, for his great love with which he loved us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 3:24; Ephesians 2:8; Romans 5:10"> even when we were dead through our trespasses, made us alive together with Christ—by grace you have been saved—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–3; Ephesians 1:3; Romans 6:4–5"> and raised us up with him, and made us to sit with him in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:16–17; Titus 3:4; Ephesians 2:4"> that in the ages to come he might show the exceeding riches of his grace in kindness towards us in Christ Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:22–26; Ephesians 2:5; Romans 4:16"> for by grace you have been saved through faith, and that not of yourselves; it is the gift of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 11:6; Titus 3:3–5"> not of works, that no one would boast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Colossians 1:10; Hebrews 13:21"> For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared before that we would walk in them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Colossians 2:11; Colossians 2:13; Colossians 3:11"> Therefore remember that once you, the Gentiles in the flesh, who are called “uncircumcision” by that which is called “circumcision” (in the flesh, made by hands),</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Colossians 1:21; Jeremiah 31:31–34"> that you were at that time separate from Christ, alienated from the commonwealth of Israel, and strangers from the covenants of the promise, having no hope and without God in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:39; Colossians 1:20–22; Colossians 1:13–14"> But now in Christ Jesus you who once were far off are made near in the blood of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11; Colossians 3:15"> For he is our peace, who made both one, and broke down the middle wall of separation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 2:14; Galatians 3:10; Colossians 1:21–22"> having abolished in his flesh the hostility, the law of commandments contained in ordinances, that he might create in himself one new man of the two, making peace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:20–22; Romans 5:10; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> and might reconcile them both in one body to God through the cross, having killed the hostility through it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 57:19–21; Acts 10:36; Romans 5:1"> He came and preached peace to you who were far off and to those who were near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:13; Ephesians 3:12; Romans 5:2"> For through him we both have our access in one Spirit to the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; 1 John 3:1; Galatians 6:10"> So then you are no longer strangers and foreigners, but you are fellow citizens with the saints and of the household of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Isaiah 28:16; 1 Peter 2:7–8"> being built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Christ Jesus himself being the chief cornerstone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16–17; 1 Corinthians 3:9; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> in whom the whole building, fitted together, grows into a holy temple in the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; 1 Corinthians 3:16; 1 John 4:16"> in whom you also are built together for a habitation of God in the Spirit.</span>
