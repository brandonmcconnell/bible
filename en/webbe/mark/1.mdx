<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 5:11–12; Matthew 4:3; Matthew 3:17"> The beginning of the Good News of Jesus Christ, the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Matthew 11:10; Luke 1:76"> As it is written in the prophets,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Behold”, from “ἰδοὺ”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. It is often used as an interjection.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I send my messenger before your face,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who will prepare your way before you:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Matthew 3:3; Luke 3:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">the voice of one crying in the wilderness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Make ready the way of the Lord!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Make his paths straight!’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 19:3–4; Matthew 3:6; Acts 13:24–25"> John came baptising<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or, immersing">b</a></sup>in the wilderness and preaching the baptism of repentance for forgiveness of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–42; Acts 19:18; Matthew 3:5–6"> All the country of Judea and all those of Jerusalem went out to him. They were baptised by him in the Jordan river, confessing their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 1:8; Leviticus 11:22; Matthew 3:4"> John was clothed with camel’s hair and a leather belt around his waist. He ate locusts and wild honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 13:25; Luke 3:16; John 1:27"> He preached, saying, “After me comes he who is mightier than I, the strap of whose sandals I am not worthy to stoop down and loosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:5; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Acts 19:4–6"> I baptised you in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Greek word (en) translated here as “in” could also be translated as “with” in some contexts.">c</a></sup>water, but he will baptise you in the Holy Spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 3:13–17; Matthew 2:23; Luke 3:21–22"> In those days, Jesus came from Nazareth of Galilee, and was baptised by John in the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:31–34; Luke 3:22; Isaiah 42:1"> Immediately coming up from the water, he saw the heavens parting and the Spirit descending on him like a dove.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Mark 9:7; Matthew 3:17"> A voice came out of the sky, “You are my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 4:1–11; Luke 4:1–13"> Immediately the Spirit drove him out into the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17–18; Hebrews 4:15; Exodus 34:28"> He was there in the wilderness forty days, tempted by Satan. He was with the wild animals; and the angels were serving him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Matthew 4:12; Luke 8:1"> Now after John was taken into custody, Jesus came into Galilee, preaching the Good News of God’s Kingdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:36–38; Matthew 4:17"> and saying,<span className="woj">“The time is fulfilled, and God’s Kingdom is at hand! Repent, and believe in the Good News.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 4:18–22; John 1:40–42; John 6:8"> Passing along by the sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew, the brother of Simon, casting a net into the sea, for they were fishermen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 5:10; Matthew 4:19–20; Acts 2:38–41"> Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Come after me, and I will make you into fishers for men.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 18:28–30; Luke 5:11; Mark 10:28–31"> Immediately they left their nets, and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 5:37; Acts 1:13; Mark 10:35"> Going on a little further from there, he saw James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, who were also in the boat mending the nets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Luke 14:26; Mark 10:29"> Immediately he called them, and they left their father, Zebedee, in the boat with the hired servants, and went after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 6:2; Mark 1:39; Matthew 4:23"> They went into Capernaum, and immediately on the Sabbath day he entered into the synagogue and taught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54; Luke 4:32"> They were astonished at his teaching, for he taught them as having authority, and not as the scribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 9:25; Mark 7:25; Mark 1:34"> Immediately there was in their synagogue a man with an unclean spirit, and he cried out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; James 2:19; Acts 3:14"> saying, “Ha! What do we have to do with you, Jesus, you Nazarene? Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are: the Holy One of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 1:34; Luke 4:41; Acts 16:17"> Jesus rebuked him, saying,<span className="woj">“Be quiet, and come out of him!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 9:26; Mark 9:20; Luke 11:22"> The unclean spirit, convulsing him and crying with a loud voice, came out of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:31; Mark 7:37; Luke 9:1"> They were all amazed, so that they questioned amongst themselves, saying, “What is this? A new teaching? For with authority he commands even the unclean spirits, and they obey him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 9:31; Luke 4:37; Mark 1:45"> The report of him went out immediately everywhere into all the region of Galilee and its surrounding area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 4:38–39; Matthew 8:14–15; Mark 1:21"> Immediately, when they had come out of the synagogue, they came into the house of Simon and Andrew, with James and John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:5; Mark 5:23; John 11:3"> Now Simon’s wife’s mother lay sick with a fever, and immediately they told him about her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 9:41; Psalms 116:12; Mark 5:41"> He came and took her by the hand and raised her up. The fever left her immediately,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="NU omits “immediately”.">d</a></sup>and she served them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 8:16; Mark 3:2; Luke 4:40"> At evening, when the sun had set, they brought to him all who were sick and those who were possessed by demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 1:5; Acts 13:44"> All the city was gathered together at the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 3:12; Matthew 4:23"> He healed many who were sick with various diseases and cast out many demons. He didn’t allow the demons to speak, because they knew him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Luke 5:16; Luke 22:39–46"> Early in the morning, while it was still dark, he rose up and went out, and departed into a deserted place, and prayed there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Simon and those who were with him searched for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 12:19; Mark 1:5; Zechariah 11:11"> They found him and told him, “Everyone is looking for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 4:43; John 17:8; Isaiah 61:1–3"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Let’s go elsewhere into the next towns, that I may preach there also, because I came out for this reason.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Mark 1:21; Luke 4:43–44"> He went into their synagogues throughout all Galilee, preaching and casting out demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 5:12–14; Matthew 8:2–4; Leviticus 13:1–14"> A leper came to him, begging him, kneeling down to him, and saying to him, “If you want to, you can make me clean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 4:15"> Being moved with compassion, he stretched out his hand, and touched him, and said to him,<span className="woj">“I want to. Be made clean.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 15:3; Mark 5:29; John 4:50–53"> When he had said this, immediately the leprosy departed from him and he was made clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 9:30; Mark 7:36; Luke 8:56"> He strictly warned him and immediately sent him out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Leviticus 14:1–32; Luke 17:14; Matthew 8:4"> and said to him,<span className="woj">“See that you say nothing to anybody, but go show yourself to the priest and offer for your cleansing the things which Moses commanded, for a testimony to them.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 2:13; Matthew 9:31; John 6:2"> But he went out, and began to proclaim it much, and to spread about the matter, so that Jesus could no more openly enter into a city, but was outside in desert places. People came to him from everywhere.</span>
