<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 27:1–8; Ezekiel 43:13–17; Exodus 40:6"> He made the altar of burnt offering of acacia wood. It was square. Its length was five cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A cubit is the length from the tip of the middle finger to the elbow on a man’s arm, or about 18 inches or 46 centimetres.">a</a></sup>its width was five cubits, and its height was three cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 27:2; Job 6:12"> He made its horns on its four corners. Its horns were of one piece with it, and he overlaid it with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 27:3; 1 Samuel 2:13"> He made all the vessels of the altar: the pots, the shovels, the basins, the forks, and the fire pans. He made all its vessels of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He made for the altar a grating of a network of bronze, under the ledge around it beneath, reaching halfway up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 27:4"> He cast four rings for the four corners of bronze grating, to be places for the poles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:3; Exodus 25:6"> He made the poles of acacia wood, and overlaid them with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Corinthians 2:2; 1 Corinthians 1:24"> He put the poles into the rings on the sides of the altar, with which to carry it. He made it hollow with planks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:22; Luke 2:37; Exodus 40:7"> He made the basin of bronze, and its base of bronze, out of the mirrors of the ministering women who ministered at the door of the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 27:9–19; Psalms 89:7; 1 Kings 6:36"> He made the court: for the south side southward the hangings of the court were of fine twined linen, one hundred cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> their pillars were twenty, and their sockets twenty, of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their fillets were of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For the north side one hundred cubits, their pillars twenty, and their sockets twenty, of bronze; the hooks of the pillars, and their fillets, of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For the west side were hangings of fifty cubits, their pillars ten, and their sockets ten; the hooks of the pillars, and their fillets, of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For the east side eastward fifty cubits,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:14"> the hangings for the one side were fifteen cubits; their pillars three, and their sockets three;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and so for the other side: on this hand and that hand by the gate of the court were hangings of fifteen cubits; their pillars three, and their sockets three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> All the hangings around the court were of fine twined linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The sockets for the pillars were of bronze. The hooks of the pillars and their fillets were of silver. Their capitals were overlaid with silver. All the pillars of the court had silver bands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:14"> The screen for the gate of the court was the work of the embroiderer, of blue, purple, scarlet, and fine twined linen. Twenty cubits was the length, and the height along the width was five cubits, like the hangings of the court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Their pillars were four, and their sockets four, of bronze; their hooks of silver, and the overlaying of their capitals, and their fillets, of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:9; Exodus 27:19; Ephesians 2:21–22"> All the pins of the tabernacle, and around the court, were of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 7:44; Numbers 9:15; 2 Chronicles 24:6"> These are the amounts of materials used for the tabernacle, even the Tabernacle of the Testimony, as they were counted, according to the commandment of Moses, for the service of the Levites, by the hand of Ithamar, the son of Aaron the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 35:30–3; Exodus 31:1–5; Psalms 119:6"> Bezalel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah, made all that the LORD commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 35:34; Exodus 31:6"> With him was Oholiab, the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, an engraver, and a skilful workman, and an embroiderer in blue, in purple, in scarlet, and in fine linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 18:16; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:25"> All the gold that was used for the work in all the work of the sanctuary, even the gold of the offering, was twenty-nine talents<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A talent is about 30 kilograms or 66 pounds or 965 Troy ounces.">b</a></sup>and seven hundred and thirty shekels, according to the shekel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.32 Troy ounces.">c</a></sup>of the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:12"> The silver of those who were counted of the congregation was one hundred talents<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A talent is about 30 kilograms or 66 pounds">d</a></sup>and one thousand and seven hundred and seventy-five shekels,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.35 ounces.">e</a></sup>according to the shekel of the sanctuary:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Exodus 30:13; Exodus 12:37"> a beka<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="a beka is about 5 grams or about 0.175 ounces">f</a></sup>a head, that is, half a shekel, according to the shekel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.35 ounces.">g</a></sup>of the sanctuary, for everyone who passed over to those who were counted, from twenty years old and upward, for six hundred and three thousand and five hundred and fifty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:19; Exodus 26:25; Exodus 26:21"> The one hundred talents<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="A talent is about 30 kilograms or 66 pounds.">h</a></sup>of silver were for casting the sockets of the sanctuary and the sockets of the veil: one hundred sockets for the one hundred talents, one talent per socket.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 27:17"> From the one thousand and seven hundred and seventy-five shekels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.35 ounces, so 1775 shekels is about 17.75 kilograms or about 39 pounds.">i</a></sup>he made hooks for the pillars, overlaid their capitals, and made fillets for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The bronze of the offering was seventy talents<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="A talent is about 30 kilograms or 66 pounds">j</a></sup>and two thousand and four hundred shekels.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="70 talents + 2400 shekels is about 2124 kilograms, or 2.124 metric tonnes.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:37; Exodus 27:17; Exodus 27:10"> With this he made the sockets to the door of the Tent of Meeting, the bronze altar, the bronze grating for it, all the vessels of the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> the sockets around the court, the sockets of the gate of the court, all the pins of the tabernacle, and all the pins around the court.</span>
