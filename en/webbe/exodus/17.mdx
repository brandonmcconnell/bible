<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 78:18; Deuteronomy 6:16; 1 Corinthians 10:9"> Therefore the people quarrelled with Moses, and said, “Give us water to drink.” Moses said to them, “Why do you quarrel with me? Why do you test the LORD?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 16:2–3; Exodus 15:24"> The people were thirsty for water there; so the people murmured against Moses, and said, “Why have you brought us up out of Egypt, to kill us, our children, and our livestock with thirst?”</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 7:19–20; Numbers 20:8–11; Acts 20:23–24"> The LORD said to Moses, “Walk on before the people, and take the elders of Israel with you, and take the rod in your hand with which you struck the Nile, and go.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 81:7; Psalms 95:8; Numbers 20:13"> He called the name of the place Massah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Massah means testing.">a</a></sup>and Meribah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Meribah means quarrelling.">b</a></sup>because the children of Israel quarrelled, and because they tested the LORD, saying, “Is the LORD amongst us, or not?”</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 24:13; Deuteronomy 32:44; Exodus 4:20"> Moses said to Joshua, “Choose men for us, and go out to fight with Amalek. Tomorrow I will stand on the top of the hill with God’s rod in my hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 24:14; Exodus 31:2; Exodus 17:12"> So Joshua did as Moses had told him, and fought with Amalek; and Moses, Aaron, and Hur went up to the top of the hill.</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 10:42; Joshua 10:37; Joshua 10:32"> Joshua defeated Amalek and his people with the edge of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 34:27; Numbers 24:20; 1 Samuel 30:1"> The LORD said to Moses, “Write this for a memorial in a book, and rehearse it in the ears of Joshua: that I will utterly blot out the memory of Amalek from under the sky.”</span>
