<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:25; 2 Kings 2:3; Nehemiah 5:2–5"> Now a certain woman of the wives of the sons of the prophets cried out to Elisha, saying, “Your servant my husband is dead. You know that your servant feared the LORD. Now the creditor has come to take for himself my two children to be slaves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 17:12; James 2:5; 2 Kings 6:26–27"> Elisha said to her, “What should I do for you? Tell me, what do you have in the house?” She said, “Your servant has nothing in the house, except a pot of oil.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 2:7; Psalms 81:10; John 16:24"> Then he said, “Go, borrow empty containers from all your neighbours. Don’t borrow just a few containers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 8:5–9; Matthew 6:6; Ephesians 3:20"> Go in and shut the door on you and on your sons, and pour oil into all those containers; and set aside those which are full.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 5:11; Hebrews 11:7–8; 1 Kings 17:15–16"> So she went from him, and shut the door on herself and on her sons. They brought the containers to her, and she poured oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 14:20; 2 Kings 13:19; Luke 6:19"> When the containers were full, she said to her son, “Bring me another container.” He said to her, “There isn’t another container.” Then the oil stopped flowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–12; 1 Kings 12:22; Romans 12:17"> Then she came and told the man of God. He said, “Go, sell the oil, and pay your debt; and you and your sons live on the rest.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 19:18; Genesis 19:3; 2 Kings 4:11–12"> One day Elisha went to Shunem, where there was a prominent woman; and she persuaded him to eat bread. So it was, that as often as he passed by, he turned in there to eat bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 17:24; Proverbs 31:10–11; 2 Kings 4:7"> She said to her husband, “See now, I perceive that this is a holy man of God who passes by us continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:13; Matthew 10:41–42; Matthew 25:40"> Please, let’s make a little room on the roof. Let’s set a bed, a table, a chair, and a lamp stand for him there. When he comes to us, he can stay there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> One day he came there, and he went to the room and lay there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 8:4–5; 2 Kings 3:11; 2 Kings 4:29–31"> He said to Gehazi his servant, “Call this Shunammite.” When he had called her, she stood before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:16–18; Philippians 4:18–19; Hebrews 6:10"> He said to him, “Say now to her, ‘Behold, you have cared for us with all this care. What is to be done for you? Would you like to be spoken for to the king, or to the captain of the army?’” She answered, “I dwell amongst my own people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:7; Genesis 15:2–3; 1 Samuel 1:8"> He said, “What then is to be done for her?” Gehazi answered, “Most certainly she has no son, and her husband is old.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He said, “Call her.” When he had called her, she stood in the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 18:10; Luke 1:13; 2 Kings 4:28"> He said, “At this season next year, you will embrace a son.” She said, “No, my lord, you man of God, do not lie to your servant.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 113:9; Luke 1:36; Genesis 21:1"> The woman conceived, and bore a son at that season when the time came around, as Elisha had said to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 2:4"> When the child was grown, one day he went out to his father to the reapers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 14:1–2; Jeremiah 4:19"> He said to his father, “My head! My head!” He said to his servant, “Carry him to his mother.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 7:12; John 11:3; John 11:14"> When he had taken him and brought him to his mother, he sat on her knees until noon, and then died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 4:32; 2 Kings 4:10; 2 Kings 4:7"> She went up and laid him on the man of God’s bed, and shut the door on him, and went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 9:38; 2 Kings 4:26; John 11:3"> She called to her husband and said, “Please send me one of the servants, and one of the donkeys, that I may run to the man of God and come again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; Numbers 28:11; 1 Chronicles 23:31"> He said, “Why would you want to go to him today? It is not a new moon or a Sabbath.” She said, “It’s all right.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 4:20; 1 Kings 13:13; 1 Samuel 25:19–20"> Then she saddled a donkey, and said to her servant, “Drive, and go forward! Don’t slow down for me, unless I ask you to.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 2:25; Isaiah 35:2; 1 Kings 18:19"> So she went, and came to the man of God to Mount Carmel. When the man of God saw her afar off, he said to Gehazi his servant, “Behold, there is the Shunammite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 15:36; 2 Kings 4:23; 1 Samuel 17:18"> Please run now to meet her, and ask her, ‘Is it well with you? Is it well with your husband? Is it well with your child?’” She answered, “It is well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:3; Genesis 18:17; Amos 3:7"> When she came to the man of God to the hill, she caught hold of his feet. Gehazi came near to thrust her away; but the man of God said, “Leave her alone, for her soul is troubled within her; and the LORD has hidden it from me, and has not told me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 30:1; 2 Kings 4:16"> Then she said, “Did I ask you for a son, my lord? Didn’t I say, ‘Do not deceive me’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 2:14; 1 Kings 18:46; Exodus 14:16"> Then he said to Gehazi, “Tuck your cloak into your belt, take my staff in your hand, and go your way. If you meet any man, don’t greet him; and if anyone greets you, don’t answer him again. Then lay my staff on the child’s face.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ruth 1:16–18; 2 Kings 2:2; Exodus 33:12–16"> The child’s mother said, “As the LORD lives, and as your soul lives, I will not leave you.” So he arose, and followed her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:11; Mark 5:39; Mark 9:19–29"> Gehazi went ahead of them, and laid the staff on the child’s face; but there was no voice and no hearing. Therefore he returned to meet him, and told him, “The child has not awakened.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 8:52–53; John 11:17; 1 Kings 17:17"> When Elisha had come into the house, behold, the child was dead, and lying on his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; 2 Kings 4:4; Acts 9:40"> He went in therefore, and shut the door on them both, and prayed to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 17:21; Acts 20:10"> He went up and lay on the child, and put his mouth on his mouth, and his eyes on his eyes, and his hands on his hands. He stretched himself on him; and the child’s flesh grew warm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 8:5; 2 Kings 13:21; 1 Kings 17:22"> Then he returned, and walked in the house once back and forth, then went up and stretched himself out on him. Then the child sneezed seven times, and the child opened his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 11:35; 1 Kings 17:23; Luke 7:15"> He called Gehazi, and said, “Call this Shunammite!” So he called her. When she had come in to him, he said, “Take up your son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Hebrews 11:35; 2 Kings 2:15; 1 Kings 17:24"> Then she went in, fell at his feet, and bowed herself to the ground; then she picked up her son, and went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Kings 8:1; 2 Kings 2:3; 2 Kings 2:1"> Elisha came again to Gilgal. There was a famine in the land; and the sons of the prophets were sitting before him; and he said to his servant, “Get the large pot, and boil stew for the sons of the prophets.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Isaiah 5:4; Matthew 15:13"> One went out into the field to gather herbs, and found a wild vine, and gathered a lap full of wild gourds from it, and came and cut them up into the pot of stew; for they didn’t recognise them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 16:18; Exodus 15:23; Exodus 10:17"> So they poured out for the men to eat. As they were eating some of the stew, they cried out and said, “Man of God, there is death in the pot!” And they could not eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Kings 2:21; Exodus 15:25; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> But he said, “Then bring meal.” He threw it into the pot; and he said, “Serve it to the people, that they may eat.” And there was nothing harmful in the pot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:7; John 6:9; Matthew 14:16–21"> A man from Baal Shalishah came, and brought the man of God some bread of the first fruits: twenty loaves of barley and fresh ears of grain in his sack. Elisha said, “Give to the people, that they may eat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:13; Matthew 14:20; John 6:9"> His servant said, “What, should I set this before a hundred men?” But he said, “Give it to the people, that they may eat; for the LORD says, ‘They will eat, and will have some left over.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> So he set it before them and they ate and had some left over, according to the LORD’s word.</span>
