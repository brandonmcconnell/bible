<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:11–6; 1 Kings 9:19; 1 Kings 7:51"> When Solomon had finished the building of the LORD’s house, the king’s house, and all Solomon’s desire which he was pleased to do,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 3:5; 2 Chronicles 7:12; 1 Kings 11:9"> The LORD appeared to Solomon the second time, as he had appeared to him at Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 10:17; 1 John 5:14; 2 Kings 20:5"> The LORD said to him, “I have heard your prayer and your supplication that you have made before me. I have made this house holy, which you have built, to put my name there forever; and my eyes and my heart shall be there perpetually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 15:5; 1 Kings 3:14; 1 Kings 11:6"> As for you, if you will walk before me as David your father walked, in integrity of heart and in uprightness, to do according to all that I have commanded you, and will keep my statutes and my ordinances,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 2:4; 1 Kings 6:12; 2 Samuel 7:16"> then I will establish the throne of your kingdom over Israel forever, as I promised to David your father, saying, ‘There shall not fail from you a man on the throne of Israel.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14–16; 1 Chronicles 28:9; 2 Chronicles 15:2"> But if you turn away from following me, you or your children, and not keep my commandments and my statutes which I have set before you, but go and serve other gods and worship them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 44:14; Jeremiah 24:9; Deuteronomy 4:26"> then I will cut off Israel out of the land which I have given them; and I will cast this house, which I have made holy for my name, out of my sight; and Israel will be a proverb and a byword amongst all peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:24–26; Jeremiah 22:8–9; 2 Chronicles 7:21"> Though this house is so high, yet everyone who passes by it will be astonished and hiss; and they will say, ‘Why has the LORD done this to this land and to this house?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:19; Jeremiah 5:19; Deuteronomy 29:25–28"> and they will answer, ‘Because they abandoned the LORD their God, who brought their fathers out of the land of Egypt, and embraced other gods, and worshipped them, and served them. Therefore the LORD has brought all this evil on them.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 6:37–1; 2 Chronicles 8:1–18; 1 Kings 9:1"> At the end of twenty years, in which Solomon had built the two houses, the LORD’s house and the king’s house</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:2; 2 Chronicles 2:16; 2 Chronicles 2:8–10"> (now Hiram the king of Tyre had furnished Solomon with cedar trees and cypress trees, and with gold, according to all his desire), King Solomon gave Hiram twenty cities in the land of Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:34; Judges 14:3"> Hiram came out of Tyre to see the cities which Solomon had given him; and they didn’t please him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 19:27; Amos 1:9; 1 Kings 5:1–2"> He said, “What cities are these which you have given me, my brother?” He called them the land of Cabul<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Cabul” sounds like Hebrew for “good-for-nothing”.">a</a></sup>to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 9:11; 1 Kings 10:10; 1 Kings 9:28"> Hiram sent to the king one hundred and twenty talents<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A talent is about 30 kilograms or 66 pounds or 965 Troy ounces, so 120 talents is about 3.6 metric tonnes">b</a></sup>of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 17:11; 1 Kings 9:24; 2 Samuel 5:9"> This is the reason of the forced labour which King Solomon conscripted: to build the LORD’s house, his own house, Millo, Jerusalem’s wall, Hazor, Megiddo, and Gezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 3:1; 1 Kings 9:24; 1 Kings 7:8"> Pharaoh king of Egypt had gone up, taken Gezer, burnt it with fire, killed the Canaanites who lived in the city, and given it for a wedding gift to his daughter, Solomon’s wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 16:3; Joshua 21:22; 2 Chronicles 8:4–18"> Solomon built in the land Gezer, Beth Horon the lower,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 19:44; 2 Chronicles 8:4"> Baalath, Tamar in the wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 9:1; Ecclesiastes 6:9; Exodus 1:11"> all the storage cities that Solomon had, the cities for his chariots, the cities for his horsemen, and that which Solomon desired to build for his pleasure in Jerusalem, and in Lebanon, and in all the land of his dominion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 23:23; Genesis 15:19–21; Deuteronomy 7:1–3"> As for all the people who were left of the Amorites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, who were not of the children of Israel—</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 17:12; Judges 1:21; Joshua 15:63"> their children who were left after them in the land, whom the children of Israel were not able utterly to destroy—of them Solomon raised a levy of bondservants to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 25:39; 1 Kings 4:1–27; 1 Samuel 8:11–12"> But of the children of Israel Solomon made no bondservants; but they were the men of war, his servants, his princes, his captains, and rulers of his chariots and of his horsemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 5:16; 2 Chronicles 8:10; 2 Chronicles 2:18"> These were the five hundred and fifty chief officers who were over Solomon’s work, who ruled over the people who laboured in the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 7:8; 2 Samuel 5:9; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> But Pharaoh’s daughter came up out of David’s city to her house which Solomon had built for her. Then he built Millo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:12–13; 2 Chronicles 8:16; Exodus 23:14–17"> Solomon offered burnt offerings and peace offerings on the altar which he built to the LORD three times per year, burning incense with them on the altar that was before the LORD. So he finished the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 22:48; Numbers 33:35; Deuteronomy 2:8"> King Solomon made a fleet of ships in Ezion Geber, which is beside Eloth, on the shore of the Red Sea, in the land of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 10:11; 1 Kings 5:6; 1 Kings 5:9"> Hiram sent in the fleet his servants, sailors who had knowledge of the sea, with the servants of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:18; 1 Chronicles 29:4; 1 Kings 10:11"> They came to Ophir, and fetched from there gold, four hundred and twenty talents,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A talent is about 30 kilograms or 66 pounds or 965 Troy ounces, so 420 talents is about 12.6 metric tonnes">c</a></sup>and brought it to King Solomon.</span>
