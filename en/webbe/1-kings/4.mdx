<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:12; 2 Chronicles 9:30; 1 Kings 12:19–20"> King Solomon was king over all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; 1 Corinthians 12:28; 2 Samuel 8:15–18"> These were the princes whom he had: Azariah the son of Zadok, the priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16; Isaiah 62:6; 1 Chronicles 18:15"> Elihoreph and Ahijah, the sons of Shisha, scribes; Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud, the recorder;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 2:35; 1 Kings 2:26–27"> Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the army; Zadok and Abiathar were priests;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:33; 2 Samuel 15:37; 2 Samuel 16:16"> Azariah the son of Nathan was over the officers; Zabud the son of Nathan was chief minister, the king’s friend;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 9:15; 2 Samuel 20:24; 1 Kings 12:18"> Ahishar was over the household; and Adoniram the son of Abda was over the men subject to forced labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:1–15"> Solomon had twelve officers over all Israel, who provided food for the king and his household. Each man had to make provision for a month in the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 24:33; Judges 19:1; Judges 17:1"> These are their names: Ben Hur, in the hill country of Ephraim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 21:16; 1 Samuel 6:20; 1 Samuel 6:12"> Ben Deker, in Makaz, in Shaalbim, Beth Shemesh, and Elon Beth Hanan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; Joshua 12:17; Joshua 17:2"> Ben Hesed, in Arubboth (Socoh and all the land of Hepher belonged to him);</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 11:2; Joshua 12:23; Joshua 17:11"> Ben Abinadab, in all the height of Dor (he had Taphath, Solomon’s daughter, as wife);</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 17:11; Judges 5:19; Joshua 3:16"> Baana the son of Ahilud, in Taanach and Megiddo, and all Beth Shean which is beside Zarethan, beneath Jezreel, from Beth Shean to Abel Meholah, as far as beyond Jokmeam;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:4; Numbers 32:41; 1 Kings 22:3"> Ben Geber, in Ramoth Gilead (the towns of Jair the son of Manasseh, which are in Gilead, belonged to him; and the region of Argob, which is in Bashan, sixty great cities with walls and bronze bars, belonged to him);</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 13:26; 2 Samuel 17:24; 2 Samuel 2:8"> Ahinadab the son of Iddo, in Mahanaim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 19:32–39; 2 Samuel 15:27; 1 Samuel 18:18"> Ahimaaz, in Naphtali (he also took Basemath the daughter of Solomon as wife);</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 19:24–31; 2 Samuel 15:32"> Baana the son of Hushai, in Asher and Bealoth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 19:17–23"> Jehoshaphat the son of Paruah, in Issachar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 1:8; Joshua 18:20–28; Zechariah 12:13"> Shimei the son of Ela, in Benjamin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26–17; Joshua 13:9–12; Numbers 21:21–35"> Geber the son of Uri, in the land of Gilead, the country of Sihon king of the Amorites and of Og king of Bashan; and he was the only officer who was in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; 1 Kings 3:8; Genesis 32:12"> Judah and Israel were numerous as the sand which is by the sea in multitude, eating and drinking and making merry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Joshua 1:4; Psalms 68:29"> Solomon ruled over all the kingdoms from the River to the land of the Philistines, and to the border of Egypt. They brought tribute and served Solomon all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Solomon’s provision for one day was thirty cors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1 cor is the same as a homer, or about 55.9 U. S. gallons (liquid) or 211 litres or 6 bushels">a</a></sup>of fine flour, sixty measures of meal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:17–18"> ten head of fat cattle, twenty head of cattle out of the pastures, and one hundred sheep, in addition to deer, gazelles, roebucks, and fattened fowl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9; Psalms 72:11; Psalms 72:3"> For he had dominion over all on this side of the River, from Tiphsah even to Gaza, over all the kings on this side of the River; and he had peace on all sides around him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Micah 4:4; Zechariah 3:10; Judges 20:1"> Judah and Israel lived safely, every man under his vine and under his fig tree, from Dan even to Beersheba, all the days of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:14; 2 Chronicles 9:25; 2 Samuel 8:4"> Solomon had forty thousand stalls of horses for his chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Those officers provided food for King Solomon, and for all who came to King Solomon’s table, every man in his month. They let nothing be lacking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Esther 8:10; Esther 8:14; Micah 1:13"> They also brought barley and straw for the horses and swift steeds to the place where the officers were, each man according to his duty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12; James 3:17; 1 Kings 4:20"> God gave Solomon abundant wisdom, understanding, and breadth of mind like the sand that is on the seashore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 5:11–12; Genesis 25:6; Daniel 1:20"> Solomon’s wisdom excelled the wisdom of all the children of the east and all the wisdom of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:6; 1 Chronicles 15:19; 1 Kings 3:12"> For he was wiser than all men—wiser than Ethan the Ezrahite, Heman, Calcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol; and his fame was in all the nations all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:1–17; Matthew 13:35; Proverbs 1:1–31"> He spoke three thousand proverbs, and his songs numbered one thousand and five.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 19:23; Psalms 51:7; Psalms 92:12"> He spoke of trees, from the cedar that is in Lebanon even to the hyssop that grows out of the wall; he also spoke of animals, of birds, of creeping things, and of fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:23; 1 Kings 10:1; 2 Chronicles 9:1"> People of all nations came to hear the wisdom of Solomon, sent by all kings of the earth who had heard of his wisdom.</span>
