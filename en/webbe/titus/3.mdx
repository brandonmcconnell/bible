<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 13:1–7; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> Remind them to be in subjection to rulers and to authorities, to be obedient, to be ready for every good work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:31; 2 Timothy 2:24–25; Ephesians 4:2"> to speak evil of no one, not to be contentious, to be gentle, showing all humility towards all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–3; Romans 6:17; Colossians 1:21"> For we were also once foolish, disobedient, deceived, serving various lusts and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hateful, and hating one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–10; Titus 2:10–11; 1 Timothy 4:10"> But when the kindness of God our Saviour and his love towards mankind appeared,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8–9; 2 Timothy 1:9; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> not by works of righteousness which we did ourselves, but according to his mercy, he saved us through the washing of regeneration and renewing by the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Romans 5:5; Acts 10:45"> whom he poured out on us richly through Jesus Christ our Saviour;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Titus 2:11; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> that being justified by his grace, we might be made heirs according to the hope of eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Titus 3:14; Titus 1:9; Psalms 16:2–3"> This saying is faithful, and concerning these things I desire that you insist confidently, so that those who have believed God may be careful to maintain good works. These things are good and profitable to men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:14; 2 Timothy 2:23; 1 Timothy 4:7"> but shun foolish questionings, genealogies, strife, and disputes about the law; for they are unprofitable and vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 John 1:10; Matthew 18:15–17"> Avoid a factious man after a first and second warning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26; Luke 19:22; Matthew 25:26–28"> knowing that such a one is perverted and sinful, being self-condemned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:9; 2 Timothy 4:12; 1 Corinthians 16:8–9"> When I send Artemas to you, or Tychicus, be diligent to come to me to Nicopolis, for I have determined to winter there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 18:24; Matthew 22:35; 3 John 1:6–8"> Send Zenas the lawyer and Apollos on their journey speedily, that nothing may be lacking for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Titus 3:8; Matthew 7:19"> Let our people also learn to maintain good works to meet necessary needs, that they may not be unfruitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Ephesians 6:23–24; 2 John 1:1–2"> All who are with me greet you. Greet those who love us in faith. Grace be with you all. Amen.</span>
