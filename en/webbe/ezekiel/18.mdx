<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The LORD’s word came to me again, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:29–30; Lamentations 5:7; Ezekiel 37:11"> “What do you mean, that you use this proverb concerning the land of Israel, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The fathers have eaten sour grapes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the children’s teeth are set on edge’?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:19–20; Ezekiel 33:11–20; Ezekiel 18:30"> “As I live,” says the Lord GOD, “you shall not use this proverb any more in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:20; Romans 6:23; Numbers 16:22"> Behold, all souls are mine; as the soul of the father, so also the soul of the son is mine. The soul who sins, he shall die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:14; 1 John 3:7; Proverbs 21:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">“But if a man is just,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and does that which is lawful and right,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:15; Deuteronomy 4:19; Ezekiel 6:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">and has not eaten on the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">hasn’t lifted up his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">hasn’t defiled his neighbour’s wife,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">hasn’t come near a woman in her impurity,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:16; Exodus 22:26; Ezekiel 18:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">and has not wronged any,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but has restored to the debtor his pledge,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">has taken nothing by robbery,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">has given his bread to the hungry,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and has covered the naked with a garment;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Zechariah 8:16; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">he who hasn’t lent to them with interest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">hasn’t taken any increase from them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who has withdrawn his hand from iniquity,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">has executed true justice between man and man,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:11; Habakkuk 2:4; Amos 5:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">has walked in my statutes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and has kept my ordinances,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to deal truly;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he is just,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he shall surely live,” says the Lord GOD.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 21:12; 1 John 3:12; John 18:40"> “If he fathers a son who is a robber who sheds blood, and who does any one of these things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 22:14; Matthew 7:21–27; 1 Kings 13:8"> or who does not do any of those things<br /><span className="pl pl-1">but has eaten at the mountain shrines</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and defiled his neighbour’s wife,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:17; Ezekiel 8:6; 2 Kings 21:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">has wronged the poor and needy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">has taken by robbery,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">has not restored the pledge,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and has lifted up his eyes to the idols,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">has committed abomination,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Leviticus 20:9; Ezekiel 3:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">has lent with interest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and has taken increase from the poor,</span><br />shall he then live? He shall not live. He has done all these abominations. He shall surely die. His blood will be on him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:21; Proverbs 23:24; Jeremiah 8:6"> “Now, behold, if he fathers a son who sees all his father’s sins which he has done, and fears, and doesn’t do likewise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:6–7; Ezekiel 18:11–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">who hasn’t eaten on the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">hasn’t lifted up his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">hasn’t defiled his neighbour’s wife,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:7; Isaiah 58:7–10; Job 22:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">hasn’t wronged any,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">hasn’t taken anything to pledge,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">hasn’t taken by robbery,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but has given his bread to the hungry,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and has covered the naked with a garment;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:8–9; Matthew 18:27–35; Ezekiel 18:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">who has withdrawn his hand from the poor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who hasn’t received interest or increase,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">has executed my ordinances,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">has walked in my statutes;</span><br />he shall not die for the iniquity of his father. He shall surely live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:18; Ezekiel 18:26; Ezekiel 18:24"> As for his father, because he cruelly oppressed, robbed his brother, and did that which is not good amongst his people, behold, he will die in his iniquity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Zechariah 1:3–6; Deuteronomy 5:9"> “Yet you say, ‘Why doesn’t the son bear the iniquity of the father?’ When the son has done that which is lawful and right, and has kept all my statutes, and has done them, he will surely live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:16; 2 Kings 14:6; Ezekiel 18:4"> The soul who sins, he shall die. The son shall not bear the iniquity of the father, neither shall the father bear the iniquity of the son. The righteousness of the righteous shall be on him, and the wickedness of the wicked shall be on him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:27–28; Titus 2:11–14; Ezekiel 33:19"> “But if the wicked turns from all his sins that he has committed, and keeps all my statutes, and does that which is lawful and right, he shall surely live. He shall not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 7:19; Ezekiel 33:16; Isaiah 43:25"> None of his transgressions that he has committed will be remembered against him. In his righteousness that he has done, he shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:11; 1 Timothy 2:4; 2 Peter 3:9"> Have I any pleasure in the death of the wicked?” says the Lord GOD, “and not rather that he should return from his way, and live?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:18; Proverbs 21:16; 2 John 1:8"> “But when the righteous turns away from his righteousness, and commits iniquity, and does according to all the abominations that the wicked man does, should he live? None of his righteous deeds that he has done will be remembered. In his trespass that he has trespassed, and in his sin that he has sinned, in them he shall die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:17; Malachi 3:13–15; Malachi 2:17"> “Yet you say, ‘The way of the Lord is not equal.’ Hear now, house of Israel: Is my way not equal? Aren’t your ways unequal?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> When the righteous man turns away from his righteousness, and commits iniquity, and dies in it, then he dies in his iniquity that he has done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:21; Isaiah 1:18; Isaiah 55:7"> Again, when the wicked man turns away from his wickedness that he has committed, and does that which is lawful and right, he will save his soul alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Luke 15:17–18; James 2:10–12"> Because he considers, and turns away from all his transgressions that he has committed, he shall surely live. He shall not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:25; Proverbs 19:3; Ezekiel 18:2"> Yet the house of Israel says, ‘The way of the Lord is not fair.’ House of Israel, aren’t my ways fair? Aren’t your ways unfair?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:21; Matthew 16:27; Matthew 3:2"> “Therefore I will judge you, house of Israel, everyone according to his ways,” says the Lord GOD. “Return, and turn yourselves from all your transgressions, so iniquity will not be your ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 51:10; Ezekiel 36:26; Ezekiel 11:19"> Cast away from you all your transgressions in which you have transgressed; and make yourself a new heart and a new spirit. For why will you die, house of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Ezekiel 18:23; Ezekiel 33:11"> For I have no pleasure in the death of him who dies,” says the Lord GOD. “Therefore turn yourselves, and live!</span>
</div>
</div>
