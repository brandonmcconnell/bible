<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:1; Ezekiel 1:2; Jeremiah 39:2"> In the eleventh year, in the first of the month, the LORD’s word came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 23:1–18; Ezekiel 36:2; Joel 3:4"> “Son of man, because Tyre has said against Jerusalem, ‘Aha! She is broken! She who was the gateway of the peoples has been returned to me. I will be replenished, now that she is laid waste;’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:42; Isaiah 5:30; Micah 4:11"> therefore the Lord GOD says, ‘Behold, I am against you, Tyre, and will cause many nations to come up against you, as the sea causes its waves to come up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 23:11; Amos 1:10; Ezekiel 26:9"> They will destroy the walls of Tyre, and break down her towers. I will also scrape her dust from her, and make her a bare rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 27:32; Ezekiel 25:7"> She will be a place for the spreading of nets in the middle of the sea; for I have spoken it,’ says the Lord GOD. ‘She will become plunder for the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:46; Ezekiel 26:8; Jeremiah 49:2"> Her daughters who are in the field will be slain with the sword. Then they will know that I am the LORD.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 7:12; Daniel 2:37; Ezekiel 23:24"> “For the Lord GOD says: ‘Behold, I will bring on Tyre Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, king of kings, from the north, with horses, with chariots, with horsemen, and an army with many people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:22; Jeremiah 52:4; Jeremiah 32:24"> He will kill your daughters in the field with the sword. He will make forts against you, cast up a mound against you, and raise up the buckler against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:15"> He will set his battering engines against your walls, and with his axes he will break down your towers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:15; Ezekiel 27:28; Jeremiah 47:3"> By reason of the abundance of his horses, their dust will cover you. Your walls will shake at the noise of the horsemen, of the wagons, and of the chariots, when he enters into your gates, as men enter into a city which is broken open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:28; Isaiah 26:5; Habakkuk 1:8"> He will tread down all your streets with the hoofs of his horses. He will kill your people with the sword. The pillars of your strength will go down to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 23:8; 2 Chronicles 32:27; Zechariah 9:3–4"> They will make a plunder of your riches and make a prey of your merchandise. They will break down your walls and destroy your pleasant houses. They will lay your stones, your timber, and your dust in the middle of the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Isaiah 23:16; Isaiah 14:11"> I will cause the noise of your songs to cease. The sound of your harps won’t be heard any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 1:4; Job 12:14; Numbers 23:19"> I will make you a bare rock. You will be a place for the spreading of nets. You will be built no more; for I the LORD have spoken it,’ says the Lord GOD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:35; Jeremiah 49:21; Ezekiel 26:18"> “The Lord GOD says to Tyre: ‘Won’t the islands shake at the sound of your fall, when the wounded groan, when the slaughter is made within you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:10; Jonah 3:6; Job 8:22"> Then all the princes of the sea will come down from their thrones, and lay aside their robes, and strip off their embroidered garments. They will clothe themselves with trembling. They will sit on the ground, and will tremble every moment, and be astonished at you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 14:12; Ezekiel 19:1; 2 Samuel 1:25–27"> They will take up a lamentation over you, and tell you,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“How you are destroyed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who were inhabited by seafaring men,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the renowned city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who was strong in the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">she and her inhabitants,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who caused their terror to be on all who lived there!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:15; Isaiah 23:5–7; Ezekiel 27:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now the islands will tremble in the day of your fall.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Yes, the islands that are in the sea will be dismayed at your departure.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Ezekiel 26:3; Daniel 11:40"> “For the Lord GOD says: ‘When I make you a desolate city, like the cities that are not inhabited, when I bring up the deep on you, and the great waters cover you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 27:13; Luke 10:15; Zechariah 2:8"> then I will bring you down with those who descend into the pit, to the people of old time, and will make you dwell in the lower parts of the earth, in the places that are desolate of old, with those who go down to the pit, that you be not inhabited; and I will set glory in the land of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:36; Ezekiel 28:19; Revelation 18:21"> I will make you a terror, and you will no more have any being. Though you are sought for, yet you will never be found again,’ says the Lord GOD.”</span>
