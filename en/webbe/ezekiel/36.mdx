<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:4; Ezekiel 34:14; Ezekiel 37:22"> You, son of man, prophesy to the mountains of Israel, and say, “You mountains of Israel, hear the LORD’s word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Ezekiel 25:3; Ezekiel 35:10"> The Lord GOD says: ‘Because the enemy has said against you, “Aha!” and, “The ancient high places are ours in possession!”’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 44:13–14; Lamentations 2:15–16; Jeremiah 18:16"> therefore prophesy, and say, ‘The Lord GOD says: “Because, even because they have made you desolate, and swallowed you up on every side, that you might be a possession to the residue of the nations, and you are taken up in the lips of talkers, and the evil report of the people;”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:28; Deuteronomy 11:11; Ezekiel 36:6"> therefore, you mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord GOD: The Lord GOD says to the mountains and to the hills, to the watercourses and to the valleys, to the desolate wastes and to the cities that are forsaken, which have become a prey and derision to the residue of the nations that are all around;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 7:8; Ezekiel 36:3; Psalms 137:7"> therefore the Lord GOD says: “Surely in the fire of my jealousy I have spoken against the residue of the nations, and against all Edom, that have appointed my land to themselves for a possession with the joy of all their heart, with despite of soul, to cast it out for a prey.”’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 123:3–4; Ezekiel 34:29; Psalms 74:23"> Therefore prophesy concerning the land of Israel, and tell the mountains, the hills, the watercourses and the valleys, ‘The Lord GOD says: “Behold, I have spoken in my jealousy and in my wrath, because you have borne the shame of the nations.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:5; Amos 1:1–15; Ezekiel 20:15"> Therefore the Lord GOD says: “I have sworn, ‘Surely the nations that are around you will bear their shame.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; Hebrews 10:37; Ezekiel 34:26–29"> “‘“But you, mountains of Israel, you shall shoot out your branches and yield your fruit to my people Israel; for they are at hand to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 46:11; Hosea 2:21–23; Ezekiel 36:34"> For, behold, I am for you, and I will come to you, and you will be tilled and sown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:33; Jeremiah 33:12; Ezekiel 36:37"> I will multiply men on you, all the house of Israel, even all of it. The cities will be inhabited and the waste places will be built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:12; Jeremiah 23:5–8; Jeremiah 31:27"> I will multiply man and animal on you. They will increase and be fruitful. I will cause you to be inhabited as you were before, and you will do better than at your beginnings. Then you will know that I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:7; Ezekiel 36:13; Jeremiah 32:44"> Yes, I will cause men to walk on you, even my people Israel. They will possess you, and you will be their inheritance, and you will never again bereave them of their children.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 13:32"> “‘The Lord GOD says: “Because they say to you, ‘You are a devourer of men, and have been a bereaver of your nation;’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:25–28; Amos 9:15; Isaiah 60:21"> therefore you shall devour men no more, and not bereave your nation any more,” says the Lord GOD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:29; Psalms 89:50; Ezekiel 36:6"> “I won’t let you hear the shame of the nations any more. You won’t bear the reproach of the peoples any more, and you won’t cause your nation to stumble any more,” says the Lord GOD.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Moreover the LORD’s word came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:7; Numbers 35:33–34; Micah 2:10"> “Son of man, when the house of Israel lived in their own land, they defiled it by their ways and by their deeds. Their way before me was as the uncleanness of a woman in her impurity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:21; Ezekiel 7:8; Lamentations 2:4"> Therefore I poured out my wrath on them for the blood which they had poured out on the land, and because they had defiled it with their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:24; Deuteronomy 28:64; Ezekiel 5:12"> I scattered them amongst the nations, and they were dispersed through the countries. I judged them according to their way and according to their deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Isaiah 52:5; Jeremiah 33:24"> When they came to the nations where they went, they profaned my holy name, in that men said of them, ‘These are the LORD’s people, and have left his land.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 48:9; Psalms 74:18; Ezekiel 20:9"> But I had respect for my holy name, which the house of Israel had profaned amongst the nations where they went.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 106:8; Ezekiel 36:32; Psalms 115:1–2"> “Therefore tell the house of Israel, ‘The Lord GOD says: “I don’t do this for your sake, house of Israel, but for my holy name, which you have profaned amongst the nations where you went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:41; Isaiah 5:16; Ezekiel 39:7"> I will sanctify my great name, which has been profaned amongst the nations, which you have profaned amongst them. Then the nations will know that I am the LORD,” says the Lord GOD, “when I am proven holy in you before their eyes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:21; Ezekiel 34:13; Amos 9:14–15"> “‘“For I will take you from amongst the nations and gather you out of all the countries, and will bring you into your own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; John 3:5; Jeremiah 33:8"> I will sprinkle clean water on you, and you will be clean. I will cleanse you from all your filthiness and from all your idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19–20; Psalms 51:10; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> I will also give you a new heart, and I will put a new spirit within you. I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you a heart of flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Titus 3:3–6; Hebrews 13:21; 1 Corinthians 3:16"> I will put my Spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes. You will keep my ordinances and do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33; Jeremiah 32:38; Ezekiel 37:27"> You will dwell in the land that I gave to your fathers. You will be my people, and I will be your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 1:7–9; Matthew 1:21; Hosea 2:21–23"> I will save you from all your uncleanness. I will call for the grain and will multiply it, and lay no famine on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:27; Joel 2:26; Deuteronomy 29:23–28"> I will multiply the fruit of the tree and the increase of the field, that you may receive no more the reproach of famine amongst the nations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Ezekiel 20:43; Nehemiah 9:26–35"> “‘“Then you will remember your evil ways, and your deeds that were not good; and you will loathe yourselves in your own sight for your iniquities and for your abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:5; Ezekiel 36:22; 2 Timothy 1:9"> I don’t do this for your sake,” says the Lord GOD. “Let it be known to you. Be ashamed and confounded for your ways, house of Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 58:12; Ezekiel 36:10; Jeremiah 33:10"> “‘The Lord GOD says: “In the day that I cleanse you from all your iniquities, I will cause the cities to be inhabited and the waste places will be built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:14; Jeremiah 25:9–11; 2 Chronicles 36:21"> The land that was desolate will be tilled instead of being a desolation in the sight of all who passed by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Joel 2:3; Jeremiah 33:9"> They will say, ‘This land that was desolate has become like the garden of Eden. The waste, desolate, and ruined cities are fortified and inhabited.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Numbers 23:19; Ezekiel 17:24"> Then the nations that are left around you will know that I, the LORD, have built the ruined places and planted that which was desolate. I, the LORD, have spoken it, and I will do it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 5:14; Hebrews 10:21–22; Ezekiel 14:3"> “‘The Lord GOD says: “For this, moreover, I will be enquired of by the house of Israel, to do it for them: I will increase them with men like a flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 8:63; Acts 2:5–11; John 10:16"> As the flock for sacrifice, as the flock of Jerusalem in her appointed feasts, so the waste cities will be filled with flocks of men. Then they will know that I am the LORD.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
