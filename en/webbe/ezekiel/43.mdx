<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:1; Ezekiel 42:15; Ezekiel 46:1"> Afterward he brought me to the gate, even the gate that looks towards the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 18:1; Ezekiel 11:23; Isaiah 6:3"> Behold, the glory of the God of Israel came from the way of the east. His voice was like the sound of many waters; and the earth was illuminated with his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:5; Ezekiel 9:1; Jeremiah 1:10"> It was like the appearance of the vision which I saw, even according to the vision that I saw when I came to destroy the city; and the visions were like the vision that I saw by the river Chebar; and I fell on my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:18–19; Ezekiel 44:2; Ezekiel 43:2"> The LORD’s glory came into the house by the way of the gate which faces towards the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:2–4; 1 Kings 8:10–11; Ezekiel 8:3"> The Spirit took me up and brought me into the inner court; and behold, the LORD’s glory filled the house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Isaiah 66:6; Revelation 16:1"> I heard one speaking to me out of the house, and a man stood by me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:9; Psalms 47:8; Leviticus 26:30"> He said to me, “Son of man, this is the place of my throne and the place of the soles of my feet, where I will dwell amongst the children of Israel forever. The house of Israel will no more defile my holy name, neither they nor their kings, by their prostitution and by the dead bodies of their kings in their high places;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 8:3–16; 2 Chronicles 33:7"> in their setting of their threshold by my threshold and their door post beside my door post. There was a wall between me and them; and they have defiled my holy name by their abominations which they have committed. Therefore I have consumed them in my anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:7; Ezekiel 37:26–28; Ezekiel 18:30–31"> Now let them put away their prostitution, and the dead bodies of their kings far from me. Then I will dwell amongst them forever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:4; Ezekiel 16:61; Ezekiel 16:63"> “You, son of man, show the house to the house of Israel, that they may be ashamed of their iniquities; and let them measure the pattern.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Ezekiel 11:20; Matthew 28:20"> If they are ashamed of all that they have done, make known to them the form of the house, its fashion, its exits, its entrances, its structure, all its ordinances, all its forms, and all its laws; and write it in their sight, that they may keep the whole form of it, and all its ordinances, and do them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:2; Revelation 21:27; Psalms 93:5"> “This is the law of the house. On the top of the mountain the whole limit around it shall be most holy. Behold, this is the law of the house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:5; Exodus 27:1–8; Ezekiel 41:8"> “These are the measurements of the altar by cubits (the cubit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A normal cubit is the length from the tip of the middle finger to the elbow on a man’s arm, or about 18 inches or 46 centimetres. A hand width is about 4.3 inches or 11 cm.">a</a></sup>is a cubit and a hand width): the bottom shall be a cubit, and the width a cubit, and its border around its edge a span;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A span is the length from the tip of a man’s thumb to the tip of his little finger when his hand is stretched out (about half a cubit, or 9 inches, or 22.8 cm.)">b</a></sup>and this shall be the base of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> From the bottom on the ground to the lower ledge shall be two cubits, and the width one cubit; and from the lesser ledge to the greater ledge shall be four cubits, and the width a cubit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 27:2; Psalms 118:27; Leviticus 9:9"> The upper altar shall be four cubits; and from the altar hearth and upward there shall be four horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 27:1; 2 Chronicles 4:1; Exodus 38:1–2"> The altar hearth shall be twelve cubits long by twelve wide, square in its four sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 20:26; Ezekiel 40:6; Exodus 25:25"> The ledge shall be fourteen cubits long by fourteen wide in its four sides; and the border about it shall be half a cubit; and its bottom shall be a cubit around; and its steps shall look towards the east.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 40:29; Hebrews 10:4–12; Ezekiel 2:1"> He said to me, “Son of man, the Lord GOD says: ‘These are the ordinances of the altar in the day when they make it, to offer burnt offerings on it, and to sprinkle blood on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:46; Ezekiel 44:15; Numbers 16:40"> You shall give to the Levitical priests who are of the offspring of Zadok, who are near to me, to minister to me,’ says the Lord GOD, ‘a young bull for a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 16:19; Leviticus 8:15; Exodus 29:12"> You shall take of its blood and put it on its four horns, and on the four corners of the ledge, and on the border all around. You shall cleanse it and make atonement for it that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 4:12; Leviticus 8:17"> You shall also take the bull of the sin offering, and it shall be burnt in the appointed place of the house, outside of the sanctuary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:25–26; Ezekiel 43:20; 1 Peter 1:19"> “On the second day you shall offer a male goat without defect for a sin offering; and they shall cleanse the altar, as they cleansed it with the bull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 29:1"> When you have finished cleansing it, you shall offer a young bull without defect and a ram out of the flock without defect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 2:13; Mark 9:49–50; Numbers 18:19"> You shall bring them near to the LORD, and the priests shall cast salt on them, and they shall offer them up for a burnt offering to the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 8:33; Exodus 29:35–37; Leviticus 8:35"> “Seven days you shall prepare every day a goat for a sin offering. They shall also prepare a young bull and a ram out of the flock, without defect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 29:24; Leviticus 8:34; Exodus 32:29"> Seven days shall they make atonement for the altar and purify it. So shall they consecrate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 9:1; Ezekiel 20:40–41; 1 Peter 2:5"> When they have accomplished the days, it shall be that on the eighth day and onward, the priests shall make your burnt offerings on the altar and your peace offerings. Then I will accept you,’ says the Lord GOD.”</span>
</div>
