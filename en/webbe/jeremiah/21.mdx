<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:3; Jeremiah 42:4–6; Jeremiah 37:7"> “Please enquire of the LORD for us; for Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon makes war against us. Perhaps the LORD will deal with us according to all his wondrous works, that he may withdraw from us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then Jeremiah said to them, “Tell Zedekiah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:5; Jeremiah 37:8–10; Isaiah 5:5"> ‘The LORD, the God of Israel says, “Behold, I will turn back the weapons of war that are in your hands, with which you fight against the king of Babylon, and against the Chaldeans who besiege you outside the walls; and I will gather them into the middle of this city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; Exodus 6:6; Isaiah 5:25"> I myself will fight against you with an outstretched hand and with a strong arm, even in anger, in wrath, and in great indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Ezekiel 14:13; Jeremiah 12:3–4"> I will strike the inhabitants of this city, both man and animal. They will die of a great pestilence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:17; 2 Kings 25:5–7; Habakkuk 1:6–10"> Afterward,” says the LORD, “I will deliver Zedekiah king of Judah, his servants, and the people, even those who are left in this city from the pestilence, from the sword, and from the famine, into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of those who seek their life. He will strike them with the edge of the sword. He will not spare them, have pity, or have mercy.”’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:19; Deuteronomy 30:15; Deuteronomy 11:26"> “You shall say to this people, ‘The LORD says: “Behold, I set before you the way of life and the way of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:2; Jeremiah 45:5; Jeremiah 39:18"> He who remains in this city will die by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence, but he who goes out and passes over to the Chaldeans who besiege you, he will live, and he will escape with his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:11; Amos 9:4; Jeremiah 44:27"> For I have set my face on this city for evil, and not for good,” says the LORD. “It will be given into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he will burn it with fire.”’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:20; Micah 3:1; Jeremiah 13:18"> “Concerning the house of the king of Judah, hear the LORD’s word:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Proverbs 31:8–9; Jeremiah 4:4"> House of David, the LORD says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Execute justice in the morning,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and deliver him who is robbed out of the hand of the oppressor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">lest my wrath go out like fire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and burn so that no one can quench it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of the evil of your doings.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:8; Lamentations 4:12; Obadiah 1:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, I am against you, O inhabitant of the valley,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and of the rock of the plain,’ says the LORD.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘You that say, “Who would come down against us?”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or, “Who would enter into our homes?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10–11; Jeremiah 32:19; Proverbs 1:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will punish you according to the fruit of your doings,’ says the LORD;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘and I will kindle a fire in her forest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and it will devour all that is around her.’”</span></span>
