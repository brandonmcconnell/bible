<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 12:31–32; 1 John 5:16; 1 Timothy 1:13"> <span className="woj">Everyone who speaks a word against the Son of Man will be forgiven, but those who blaspheme against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 10:17–20; Luke 21:12–14; Acts 4:5–7"> <span className="woj">When they bring you before the synagogues, the rulers, and the authorities, don’t be anxious how or what you will answer or what you will say;</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 6:45; 1 Timothy 6:5; Ezekiel 33:31"> One of the multitude said to him, “Teacher, tell my brother to divide the inheritance with me.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; James 4:13–15; 1 Corinthians 15:32"> <span className="woj">I will tell my soul, “Soul, you have many goods laid up for many years. Take your ease, eat, drink, and be merry.”’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 27:8; Jeremiah 17:11; 1 Timothy 6:7"> <span className="woj">“But God said to him, ‘You foolish one, tonight your soul is required of you. The things which you have prepared—whose will they be?’</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 6:25–34; Philippians 4:6; Hebrews 13:5"> He said to his disciples,<span className="woj">“Therefore I tell you, don’t be anxious for your life, what you will eat, nor yet for your body, what you will wear.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 2:6; Proverbs 13:8; Job 2:4"> <span className="woj">Life is more than food, and the body is more than clothing.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:31; Psalms 147:9; Luke 12:7"> <span className="woj">Consider the ravens: they don’t sow, they don’t reap, they have no warehouse or barn, and God feeds them. How much more valuable are you than birds!</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Peter 3:14; Matthew 24:42; John 13:4–5"> <span className="woj">Blessed are those servants whom the lord will find watching when he comes. <span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:4–5; Matthew 25:6"> <span className="woj">They will be blessed if he comes in the second or third watch and finds them so.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Revelation 3:3; Revelation 16:15"> <span className="woj">But know this, that if the master of the house had known in what hour the thief was coming, he would have watched and not allowed his house to be broken into.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6; Matthew 25:13; Romans 13:11"> <span className="woj">Therefore be ready also, for the Son of Man is coming in an hour that you don’t expect him.”</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> <span className="woj">Blessed is that servant whom his lord will find doing so when he comes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 22:29–30; Matthew 24:47; Luke 19:17–19"> <span className="woj">Truly I tell you that he will set him over all that he has.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 12:27–28; John 19:30; John 7:6–8"> <span className="woj">But I have a baptism to be baptised with, and how distressed I am until it is accomplished!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 10:34–36; Luke 12:49; Matthew 24:7–10"> <span className="woj">Do you think that I have come to give peace in the earth? I tell you, no, but rather division.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 16:2–4; 1 Kings 18:44–45"> He said to the multitudes also,<span className="woj">“When you see a cloud rising from the west, immediately you say, ‘A shower is coming,’ and so it happens.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 16:3; Matthew 24:32–33; Matthew 11:25"> <span className="woj">You hypocrites! You know how to interpret the appearance of the earth and the sky, but how is it that you don’t interpret this time?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; John 7:24; Acts 13:26–38"> <span className="woj">“Why don’t you judge for yourselves what is right?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Proverbs 25:8–9; Matthew 5:23–26; Luke 13:24–28"> <span className="woj">For when you are going with your adversary before the magistrate, try diligently on the way to be released from him, lest perhaps he drag you to the judge, and the judge deliver you to the officer, and the officer throw you into prison.</span></span>
