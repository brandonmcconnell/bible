<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 13:1–37; Luke 21:5–36; Ezekiel 8:6"> Jesus went out from the temple, and was going on his way. His disciples came to him to show him the buildings of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 19:44; Jeremiah 26:18; Micah 3:12"> But he answered them,<span className="woj">“You see all of these things, don’t you? Most certainly I tell you, there will not be left here one stone on another, that will not be thrown down.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 13:3–4; Acts 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–11"> As he sat on the Mount of Olives, the disciples came to him privately, saying, “Tell us, when will these things be? What is the sign of your coming, and of the end of the age?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Jeremiah 29:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:3"> Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“Be careful that no one leads you astray.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 24:11; Matthew 24:24; Jeremiah 14:14"> <span className="woj">For many will come in my name, saying, ‘I am the Christ,’ and will lead many astray.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:7–8; Luke 21:9; 1 Peter 3:14–15"> <span className="woj">You will hear of wars and rumours of wars. See that you aren’t troubled, for all this must happen, but the end is not yet.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 21:11; Revelation 6:8; Isaiah 19:2"> <span className="woj">For nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom; and there will be famines, plagues, and earthquakes in various places.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Deuteronomy 28:59"> <span className="woj">But all these things are the beginning of birth pains.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 16:2; John 15:19–20; Luke 21:16–17"> <span className="woj">“Then they will deliver you up to oppression and will kill you. You will be hated by all of the nations for my name’s sake.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Luke 21:16; Matthew 11:6"> <span className="woj">Then many will stumble, and will deliver up one another, and will hate one another.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; Matthew 7:15; Matthew 24:5"> <span className="woj">Many false prophets will arise and will lead many astray.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 2:4–5; James 4:1–4; Revelation 3:15"> <span className="woj">Because iniquity will be multiplied, the love of many will grow cold.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 10:22; Mark 13:13; Revelation 2:10"> <span className="woj">But he who endures to the end will be saved.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 14:6; Romans 10:18; Romans 16:25–26"> <span className="woj">This Good News of the Kingdom will be preached in the whole world for a testimony to all the nations, and then the end will come.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 9:27; Daniel 12:11; Mark 13:14"> <span className="woj">“When, therefore, you see the abomination of desolation,</span><span className="woj">which was spoken of through Daniel the prophet, standing in the holy place (let the reader understand),</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 19:15–17; Luke 21:21–22; Hebrews 11:7"> <span className="woj">then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 17:31–33; Mark 13:15–16; Matthew 10:27"> <span className="woj">Let him who is on the housetop not go down to take out the things that are in his house.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="woj">Let him who is in the field not return back to get his clothes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 23:29–30; Deuteronomy 28:53–56; Luke 21:23"> <span className="woj">But woe to those who are with child and to nursing mothers in those days!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:29; Acts 1:12"> <span className="woj">Pray that your flight will not be in the winter nor on a Sabbath,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Joel 2:2; Malachi 4:1"> <span className="woj">for then there will be great suffering,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, oppression">a</a></sup><span className="woj">such as has not been from the beginning of the world until now, no, nor ever will be.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 13:20; 2 Timothy 2:10; Matthew 24:31"> <span className="woj">Unless those days had been shortened, no flesh would have been saved. But for the sake of the chosen ones, those days will be shortened.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Luke 17:23–24; Deuteronomy 13:1–3"> <span className="woj">“Then if any man tells you, ‘Behold, here is the Christ!’ or, ‘There!’ don’t believe it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Mark 13:22; Revelation 13:13–14"> <span className="woj">For false christs and false prophets will arise, and they will show great signs and wonders, so as to lead astray, if possible, even the chosen ones.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:1; Luke 21:13; Isaiah 48:5–6"> <span className="woj">“Behold, I have told you beforehand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 3:2–3; Isaiah 40:3; Matthew 3:1"> <span className="woj">“If therefore they tell you, ‘Behold, he is in the wilderness,’ don’t go out; or ‘Behold, he is in the inner rooms,’ don’t believe it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 17:24–37; Zechariah 9:14; Job 37:3"> <span className="woj">For as the lightning flashes from the east, and is seen even to the west, so will the coming of the Son of Man be.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 17:37; Job 39:27–30; Deuteronomy 28:49"> <span className="woj">For wherever the carcass is, that is where the vultures</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or, eagles">b</a></sup><span className="woj">gather together.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Amos 8:9; Joel 3:15; Isaiah 13:10"> <span className="woj">“But immediately after the suffering</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, oppression">c</a></sup><span className="woj">of those days, the sun will be darkened, the moon will not give its light, the stars will fall from the sky, and the powers of the heavens will be shaken;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Daniel 7:13; Luke 21:27"> <span className="woj">and then the sign of the Son of Man will appear in the sky. Then all the tribes of the earth will mourn, and they will see the Son of Man coming on the clouds of the sky with power and great glory.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16; Mark 13:27; Isaiah 27:13"> <span className="woj">He will send out his angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they will gather together his chosen ones from the four winds, from one end of the sky to the other.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 13:28–29; Luke 21:29–30"> <span className="woj">“Now from the fig tree learn this parable: When its branch has now become tender and produces its leaves, you know that the summer is near.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 5:9; Hebrews 10:37; Ezekiel 7:2–14"> <span className="woj">Even so you also, when you see all these things, know that he is near, even at the doors.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 13:30–31; Luke 21:32–33; Matthew 16:28"> <span className="woj">Most certainly I tell you, this generation</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The word for “generation” (genea) can also be translated as “race.”">d</a></sup><span className="woj">will not pass away until all these things are accomplished.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; 1 Peter 1:25; Numbers 23:19"> <span className="woj">Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not pass away.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Acts 1:7; Matthew 24:44"> <span className="woj">“But no one knows of that day and hour, not even the angels of heaven,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="NU adds “nor the son”">e</a></sup><span className="woj">but my Father only.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:1–7; 2 Peter 2:5"> <span className="woj">As the days of Noah were, so will the coming of the Son of Man be.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 17:26–28; Romans 13:13–14; Luke 14:18–20"> <span className="woj">For as in those days which were before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day that Noah entered into the ship,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:20; Matthew 24:37; Acts 13:41"> <span className="woj">and they didn’t know until the flood came and took them all away, so will the coming of the Son of Man be.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 17:34–37; 2 Peter 2:7–9; 2 Peter 2:5"> <span className="woj">Then two men will be in the field: one will be taken and one will be left.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 11:5; Isaiah 47:2; Luke 17:35"> <span className="woj">Two women will be grinding at the mill: one will be taken and one will be left.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 21:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:6; Matthew 25:13"> <span className="woj">Watch therefore, for you don’t know in what hour your Lord comes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 12:39; Matthew 24:44; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–6"> <span className="woj">But know this, that if the master of the house had known in what watch of the night the thief was coming, he would have watched, and would not have allowed his house to be broken into.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 25:10; Matthew 24:42; Matthew 25:13"> <span className="woj">Therefore also be ready, for in an hour that you don’t expect, the Son of Man will come.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Matthew 25:23; 1 Corinthians 4:1–2"> <span className="woj">“Who then is the faithful and wise servant, whom his lord has set over his household, to give them their food in due season?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; Luke 12:43; Luke 12:37"> <span className="woj">Blessed is that servant whom his lord finds doing so when he comes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 25:23; Matthew 25:21; Luke 12:37"> <span className="woj">Most certainly I tell you that he will set him over all that he has.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 18:32; Isaiah 32:6; Luke 12:45"> <span className="woj">But if that evil servant should say in his heart, ‘My lord is delaying his coming,’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Revelation 17:6; Revelation 16:6; Micah 3:5"> <span className="woj">and begins to beat his fellow servants, and eat and drink with the drunkards,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Revelation 3:3; Matthew 24:42–44"> <span className="woj">the lord of that servant will come in a day when he doesn’t expect it and in an hour when he doesn’t know it,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 22:13; Luke 12:46"> <span className="woj">and will cut him in pieces and appoint his portion with the hypocrites. That is where the weeping and grinding of teeth will be.</span></span>
