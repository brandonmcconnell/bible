<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:4–10; Matthew 13:1–15; Mark 4:1–12"> On that day Jesus went out of the house and sat by the seaside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 5:3; Matthew 4:25; Mark 4:1"> Great multitudes gathered to him, so that he entered into a boat and sat; and all the multitude stood on the beach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 4:2–9; Mark 4:13; Psalms 78:2"> He spoke to them many things in parables, saying,<span className="woj">“Behold, a farmer went out to sow.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:18–19"> <span className="woj">As he sowed, some seeds fell by the roadside, and the birds came and devoured them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Ezekiel 36:26; Zechariah 7:12"> <span className="woj">Others fell on rocky ground, where they didn’t have much soil, and immediately they sprang up, because they had no depth of earth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 13:21; James 1:11–12; Colossians 1:23"> <span className="woj">When the sun had risen, they were scorched. Because they had no root, they withered away.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 4:18–19; Jeremiah 4:3–4; Genesis 3:18"> <span className="woj">Others fell amongst thorns. The thorns grew up and choked them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 13:23; Luke 8:15; Genesis 26:12"> <span className="woj">Others fell on good soil and yielded fruit: some one hundred times as much, some sixty, and some thirty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 2:11; Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:7"> <span className="woj">He who has ears to hear, let him hear.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 4:33–34; Mark 4:10"> The disciples came, and said to him, “Why do you speak to them in parables?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9–10; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 19:11"> He answered them,<span className="woj">“To you it is given to know the mysteries of the Kingdom of Heaven, but it is not given to them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:29; Mark 4:24–25; Luke 8:18"> <span className="woj">For whoever has, to him will be given, and he will have abundance; but whoever doesn’t have, from him will be taken away even that which he has.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:2; Jeremiah 5:21; Isaiah 42:18–20"> <span className="woj">Therefore I speak to them in parables, because seeing they don’t see, and hearing, they don’t hear, neither do they understand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Ezekiel 12:2; 2 Corinthians 3:14"> <span className="woj">In them the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled, which says,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1 woj">‘By hearing you will hear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">and will in no way understand;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1 woj">Seeing you will see,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">and will in no way perceive;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Mark 4:12; Zechariah 7:11"> <span className="pl pl-1 woj">for this people’s heart has grown callous,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">their ears are dull of hearing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">and they have closed their eyes;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1 woj">or else perhaps they might perceive with their eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">hear with their ears,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">understand with their heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1 woj">and would turn again,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">and I would heal them.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:23–24; Ephesians 1:17–18; Matthew 16:17"> <span className="woj">“But blessed are your eyes, for they see; and your ears, for they hear.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:56; Hebrews 11:13; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> <span className="woj">For most certainly I tell you that many prophets and righteous men desired to see the things which you see, and didn’t see them; and to hear the things which you hear, and didn’t hear them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:11–12; Luke 8:11–15; Mark 4:13–20"> <span className="woj">“Hear, then, the parable of the farmer.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 8:11–15; Matthew 13:38; Hebrews 2:1"> <span className="woj">When anyone hears the word of the Kingdom and doesn’t understand it, the evil one comes and snatches away that which has been sown in his heart. This is what was sown by the roadside.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31–32; Psalms 106:12–13; John 5:35"> <span className="woj">What was sown on the rocky places, this is he who hears the word and immediately with joy receives it;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 2 Peter 1:8–9; Ephesians 3:17"> <span className="woj">yet he has no root in himself, but endures for a while. When oppression or persecution arises because of the word, immediately he stumbles.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Luke 8:14; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> <span className="woj">What was sown amongst the thorns, this is he who hears the word, but the cares of this age and the deceitfulness of riches choke the word, and he becomes unfruitful.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 1:11; Matthew 12:33; John 15:16"> <span className="woj">What was sown on the good ground, this is he who hears the word and understands it, who most certainly bears fruit and produces, some one hundred times as much, some sixty, and some thirty.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 25:1; Matthew 13:47; Matthew 20:1"> He set another parable before them, saying,<span className="woj">“The Kingdom of Heaven is like a man who sowed good seed in his field,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; 2 Peter 2:1; Acts 20:30–31"> <span className="woj">but while people slept, his enemy came and sowed darnel weeds</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="darnel is a weed grass (probably bearded darnel or lolium temulentum) that looks very much like wheat until it is mature, when the difference becomes very apparent.">a</a></sup><span className="woj">also amongst the wheat, and went away.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 4:26–29"> <span className="woj">But when the blade sprang up and produced grain, then the darnel weeds appeared also.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 Corinthians 6:1; 2 Corinthians 5:18–20"> <span className="woj">The servants of the householder came and said to him, ‘Sir, didn’t you sow good seed in your field? Where did these darnel weeds come from?’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:3–7; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; Luke 9:49–54"> <span className="woj">“He said to them, ‘An enemy has done this.’</span><span className="woj">“The servants asked him, ‘Do you want us to go and gather them up?’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> <span className="woj">“But he said, ‘No, lest perhaps while you gather up the darnel weeds, you root up the wheat with them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 3:12; 1 Timothy 5:24; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> <span className="woj">Let both grow together until the harvest, and in the harvest time I will tell the reapers, “First, gather up the darnel weeds, and bind them in bundles to burn them; but gather the wheat into my barn.”’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 13:18–19; Matthew 17:20; Mark 4:30–32"> He set another parable before them, saying,<span className="woj">“The Kingdom of Heaven is like a grain of mustard seed which a man took, and sowed in his field,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:6; Daniel 4:12; Ezekiel 17:23–24"> <span className="woj">which indeed is smaller than all seeds. But when it is grown, it is greater than the herbs and becomes a tree, so that the birds of the air come and lodge in its branches.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Galatians 5:9; 1 Corinthians 5:6–7; Luke 13:21"> He spoke another parable to them.<span className="woj">“The Kingdom of Heaven is like yeast which a woman took and hid in three measures</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="literally, three sata. Three sata is about 39 litres or a bit more than a bushel">b</a></sup><span className="woj">of meal, until it was all leavened.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 4:33–34; John 16:25; Matthew 13:13"> Jesus spoke all these things in parables to the multitudes; and without a parable, he didn’t speak to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Ephesians 3:9; 1 Peter 1:20–21"> that it might be fulfilled which was spoken through the prophet, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will open my mouth in parables;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will utter things hidden from the foundation of the world.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 16:17–20; Matthew 13:11; Matthew 9:28"> Then Jesus sent the multitudes away, and went into the house. His disciples came to him, saying, “Explain to us the parable of the darnel weeds of the field.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–7; Matthew 13:41; Luke 10:16"> He answered them,<span className="woj">“He who sows the good seed is the Son of Man,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 8:44; Philippians 3:18–19; Acts 13:10"> <span className="woj">the field is the world, the good seeds are the children of the Kingdom, and the darnel weeds are the children of the evil one.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 13:49; Revelation 14:15–19; Matthew 25:31"> <span className="woj">The enemy who sowed them is the devil. The harvest is the end of the age, and the reapers are angels.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 13:39"> <span className="woj">As therefore the darnel weeds are gathered up and burnt with fire; so will it be at the end of this age.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Matthew 18:7; 2 Peter 2:1–2"> <span className="woj">The Son of Man will send out his angels, and they will gather out of his Kingdom all things that cause stumbling and those who do iniquity,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 13:50; Luke 16:23–24; Matthew 8:12"> <span className="woj">and will cast them into the furnace of fire. There will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; Matthew 11:15; Luke 12:32"> <span className="woj">Then the righteous will shine like the sun in the Kingdom of their Father. He who has ears to hear, let him hear.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Proverbs 2:2–5; Colossians 2:3; Philippians 3:7–9"> <span className="woj">“Again, the Kingdom of Heaven is like treasure hidden in the field, which a man found and hid. In his joy, he goes and sells all that he has and buys that field.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 3:13–18; Matthew 16:26; Matthew 13:24"> <span className="woj">“Again, the Kingdom of Heaven is like a man who is a merchant seeking fine pearls,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 18:28–30; Proverbs 2:4"> <span className="woj">who having found one pearl of great price, he went and sold all that he had and bought it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 15:6; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Matthew 22:9–10"> <span className="woj">“Again, the Kingdom of Heaven is like a dragnet that was cast into the sea and gathered some fish of every kind,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Matthew 13:40–43; Matthew 3:12"> <span className="woj">which, when it was filled, fishermen drew up on the beach. They sat down and gathered the good into containers, but the bad they threw away.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Revelation 20:12–15; Matthew 24:31; Matthew 13:39"> <span className="woj">So it will be in the end of the world.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, end of the age.">c</a></sup><span className="woj">The angels will come and separate the wicked from amongst the righteous,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 13:42; Matthew 8:12; Revelation 14:10–11"> <span className="woj">and will cast them into the furnace of fire. There will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Matthew 13:11; Mark 4:34"> Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Have you understood all these things?”</span>They answered him, “Yes, Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 John 2:7–8; 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Titus 1:9"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Therefore every scribe who has been made a disciple in the Kingdom of Heaven is like a man who is a householder, who brings out of his treasure new and old things.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Mark 4:33–35"> When Jesus had finished these parables, he departed from there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; John 7:15–16; Mark 6:1–6"> Coming into his own country, he taught them in their synagogue, so that they were astonished and said, “Where did this man get this wisdom and these mighty works?</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 6:42; Mark 6:3; Luke 3:23"> Isn’t this the carpenter’s son? Isn’t his mother called Mary, and his brothers James, Joses, Simon, and Judas?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="or, Judah">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Aren’t all of his sisters with us? Where then did this man get all of these things?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 4:44; Matthew 11:6; Luke 4:24"> They were offended by him. But Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“A prophet is not without honour, except in his own country and in his own house.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Mark 6:5–6; Romans 11:20; Hebrews 3:12–19"> He didn’t do many mighty works there because of their unbelief.</span>
