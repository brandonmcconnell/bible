<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 18:3; James 2:2–3; Luke 15:30"> He may sentence him to no more than forty stripes. He shall not give more, lest if he should give more and beat him more than that many stripes, then your brother will be degraded in your sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:9–10; Proverbs 12:10"> You shall not muzzle the ox when he treads out the grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:24; Luke 20:28; Mark 12:19"> If brothers dwell together, and one of them dies and has no son, the wife of the dead shall not be married outside to a stranger. Her husband’s brother shall go in to her, and take her as his wife, and perform the duty of a husband’s brother to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:19; Psalms 9:5; Psalms 109:13"> It shall be that the firstborn whom she bears shall succeed in the name of his brother who is dead, that his name not be blotted out of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ruth 4:5–6; Ruth 4:1–2"> If the man doesn’t want to take his brother’s wife, then his brother’s wife shall go up to the gate to the elders, and say, “My husband’s brother refuses to raise up to his brother a name in Israel. He will not perform the duty of a husband’s brother to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 4:6"> Then the elders of his city shall call him, and speak to him. If he stands and says, “I don’t want to take her,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ruth 4:7–8; Numbers 12:14; Isaiah 50:6"> then his brother’s wife shall come to him in the presence of the elders, and loose his sandal from off his foot, and spit in his face. She shall answer and say, “So shall it be done to the man who does not build up his brother’s house.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> His name shall be called in Israel, “The house of him who had his sandal removed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9; Romans 3:8"> When men strive against each other, and the wife of one draws near to deliver her husband out of the hand of him who strikes him, and puts out her hand, and grabs him by his private parts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:13; Deuteronomy 19:21; Deuteronomy 7:2"> then you shall cut off her hand. Your eye shall have no pity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Proverbs 16:11; Leviticus 19:35–37"> You shall not have in your bag diverse weights, one heavy and one light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> You shall not have in your house diverse measures, one large and one small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 4:40; Ephesians 6:3"> You shall have a perfect and just weight. You shall have a perfect and just measure, that your days may be long in the land which the LORD your God gives you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Revelation 21:27; Deuteronomy 22:5"> For all who do such things, all who do unrighteously, are an abomination to the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 17:8–16; Numbers 25:17–18; Numbers 24:20"> Remember what Amalek did to you by the way as you came out of Egypt,</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 17:14; 1 Samuel 14:48; Exodus 17:16"> Therefore it shall be, when the LORD your God has given you rest from all your enemies all around, in the land which the LORD your God gives you for an inheritance to possess it, that you shall blot out the memory of Amalek from under the sky. You shall not forget.</span>
