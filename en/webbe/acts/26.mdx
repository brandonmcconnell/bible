<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Proverbs 1:24; Acts 26:2"> Agrippa said to Paul, “You may speak for yourself.”<br />Then Paul stretched out his hand, and made his defence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> “I think myself happy, King Agrippa, that I am to make my defence before you today concerning all the things the Jews accuse me of,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 6:14; Acts 26:26; Acts 26:7"> especially because you are expert in all customs and questions which are amongst the Jews. Therefore I beg you to hear me patiently.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 1:13; 2 Timothy 3:10; Philippians 3:5"> “Indeed, all the Jews know my way of life from my youth up, which was from the beginning amongst my own nation and at Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 23:6; Acts 22:3; Acts 24:5"> having known me from the first, if they are willing to testify, that after the strictest sect of our religion I lived a Pharisee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:13; Acts 24:15; Acts 28:20"> Now I stand here to be judged for the hope of the promise made by God to our fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:1; Philippians 3:11; 1 Timothy 5:5"> which our twelve tribes, earnestly serving night and day, hope to attain. Concerning this hope I am accused by the Jews, King Agrippa!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:12–20; Acts 4:2; Acts 17:31–32"> Why is it judged incredible with you if God does raise the dead?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Acts 22:8; Philippians 3:6"> “I myself most certainly thought that I ought to do many things contrary to the name of Jesus of Nazareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 8:3; Acts 9:13–14; Acts 9:21"> I also did this in Jerusalem. I both shut up many of the saints in prisons, having received authority from the chief priests; and when they were put to death I gave my vote against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 22:19; Matthew 10:17; Acts 13:45"> Punishing them often in all the synagogues, I tried to make them blaspheme. Being exceedingly enraged against them, I persecuted them even to foreign cities.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 26:10; Acts 22:5–11; Acts 9:1–8"> “Whereupon as I travelled to Damascus with the authority and commission from the chief priests,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 17:2; Revelation 21:23; Revelation 1:16"> at noon, O king, I saw on the way a light from the sky, brighter than the sun, shining around me and those who travelled with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 21:40; Acts 22:2; Acts 9:7"> When we had all fallen to the earth, I heard a voice saying to me in the Hebrew language,<span className="woj">‘Saul, Saul, why are you persecuting me? It is hard for you to kick against the goads.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 15:20–21; Matthew 25:45; Exodus 16:8"> “I said, ‘Who are you, Lord?’<br />“He said,<span className="woj">‘I am Jesus, whom you are persecuting.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 22:14–15; Ezekiel 2:1; Daniel 10:11"> <span className="woj">But arise, and stand on your feet, for I have appeared to you for this purpose: to appoint you a servant and a witness both of the things which you have seen and of the things which I will reveal to you;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Timothy 2:7; Jeremiah 1:19"> <span className="woj">delivering you from the people and from the Gentiles, to whom I send you,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Ephesians 1:18; Ephesians 5:8"> <span className="woj">to open their eyes, that they may turn from darkness to light and from the power of Satan to God, that they may receive remission of sins and an inheritance amongst those who are sanctified by faith in me.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 50:5; Galatians 1:16; Ezekiel 2:7–8"> “Therefore, King Agrippa, I was not disobedient to the heavenly vision,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 3:8; Acts 3:19; Acts 2:38"> but declared first to them of Damascus, at Jerusalem, and throughout all the country of Judea, and also to the Gentiles, that they should repent and turn to God, doing works worthy of repentance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 21:30–31; Acts 21:27; Acts 25:3"> For this reason the Jews seized me in the temple and tried to kill me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 24:14; John 5:46; Romans 3:21"> Having therefore obtained the help that is from God, I stand to this day testifying both to small and great, saying nothing but what the prophets and Moses said would happen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Luke 24:26; Colossians 1:18"> how the Christ must suffer, and how, by the resurrection of the dead, he would be first to proclaim light both to these people and to the Gentiles.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:13–14; 1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Corinthians 4:10"> As he thus made his defence, Festus said with a loud voice, “Paul, you are crazy! Your great learning is driving you insane!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 23:26; Titus 1:9; Titus 2:7–8"> But he said, “I am not crazy, most excellent Festus, but boldly declare words of truth and reasonableness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 26:2–3; Acts 2:1–12; Isaiah 30:20"> For the king knows of these things, to whom also I speak freely. For I am persuaded that none of these things is hidden from him, for this has not been done in a corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 26:22–23"> King Agrippa, do you believe the prophets? I know that you believe.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 11:26; Matthew 10:18; Acts 26:29"> Agrippa said to Paul, “With a little persuasion are you trying to make me a Christian?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; Colossians 1:28; Romans 10:1"> Paul said, “I pray to God, that whether with little or with much, not only you, but also all that hear me today, might become such as I am, except for these bonds.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 18:15; Acts 28:22; Acts 25:23"> The king rose up with the governor and Bernice, and those who sat with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 23:29; Acts 23:9; Acts 28:18"> When they had withdrawn, they spoke to one another, saying, “This man does nothing worthy of death or of bonds.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 28:18; Acts 25:11–12; Acts 25:25"> Agrippa said to Festus, “This man might have been set free if he had not appealed to Caesar.”</span>
</div>
</div>
