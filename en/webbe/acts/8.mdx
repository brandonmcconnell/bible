<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 22:20; Daniel 3:16–18; Acts 5:40"> Saul was consenting to his death. A great persecution arose against the assembly which was in Jerusalem in that day. They were all scattered abroad throughout the regions of Judea and Samaria, except for the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 50:10–11; Genesis 23:2; Jeremiah 22:18"> Devout men buried Stephen and lamented greatly over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Galatians 1:13; 1 Corinthians 15:9"> But Saul ravaged the assembly, entering into every house and dragged both men and women off to prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Acts 11:19; Acts 8:1"> Therefore those who were scattered abroad went around preaching the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 6:5; Acts 21:8; Acts 8:35–36"> Philip went down to the city of Samaria and proclaimed to them the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 4:41–42; 2 Chronicles 30:12; Matthew 20:15–16"> The multitudes listened with one accord to the things that were spoken by Philip when they heard and saw the signs which he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:1; Acts 14:8–10; Mark 16:17–18"> For unclean spirits came out of many of those who had them. They came out, crying with a loud voice. Many who had been paralysed and lame were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:52; Acts 13:48; Acts 8:39"> There was great joy in that city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 13:6; Acts 5:36; John 7:18"> But there was a certain man, Simon by name, who used to practise sorcery in the city and amazed the people of Samaria, making himself out to be some great one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; 2 Peter 2:2; Revelation 13:3"> to whom they all listened, from the least to the greatest, saying, “This man is that great power of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 8:9; Galatians 3:1; Isaiah 44:25"> They listened to him because for a long time he had amazed them with his sorceries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 1:3; Matthew 28:19"> But when they believed Philip preaching good news concerning God’s Kingdom and the name of Jesus Christ, they were baptised, both men and women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:6–7; John 5:20; Acts 19:11"> Simon himself also believed. Being baptised, he continued with Philip. Seeing signs and great miracles occurring, he was amazed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; Galatians 2:9"> Now when the apostles who were at Jerusalem heard that Samaria had received the word of God, they sent Peter and John to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:38; John 16:23–24; John 14:13–14"> who, when they had come down, prayed for them, that they might receive the Holy Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 19:2; Matthew 28:19"> for as yet he had fallen on none of them. They had only been baptised in the name of Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 6:6; 2 Timothy 1:6"> Then they laid their hands on them, and they received the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:5; Matthew 10:8; 2 Kings 8:9"> Now when Simon saw that the Holy Spirit was given through the laying on of the apostles’ hands, he offered them money,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; 3 John 1:9; John 5:44"> saying, “Give me also this power, that whomever I lay my hands on may receive the Holy Spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 5:17; Matthew 10:8; Acts 2:38"> But Peter said to him, “May your silver perish with you, because you thought you could obtain the gift of God with money!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Psalms 36:1; Matthew 6:22–24"> You have neither part nor lot in this matter, for your heart isn’t right before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:25–26; Daniel 4:27; Hebrews 4:12"> Repent therefore of this, your wickedness, and ask God if perhaps the thought of your heart may be forgiven you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 58:6; Proverbs 5:22; John 8:34"> For I see that you are in the poison of bitterness and in the bondage of iniquity.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Numbers 21:7; Job 42:8"> Simon answered, “Pray for me to the Lord, that none of the things which you have spoken happen to me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Acts 26:22–23; Acts 1:8"> They therefore, when they had testified and spoken the word of the Lord, returned to Jerusalem, and preached the Good News to many villages of the Samaritans.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 1:3"> Then an angel of the Lord spoke to Philip, saying, “Arise, and go towards the south to the way that goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza. This is a desert.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; Psalms 68:31; Isaiah 56:3–8"> He arose and went; and behold, there was a man of Ethiopia, a eunuch of great authority under Candace, queen of the Ethiopians, who was over all her treasure, who had come to Jerusalem to worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Isaiah 1:1; Acts 28:25"> He was returning and sitting in his chariot, and was reading the prophet Isaiah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:19; Acts 11:12; Acts 20:22–23"> The Spirit said to Philip, “Go near, and join yourself to this chariot.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 24:44–45; John 5:39; Ephesians 5:17"> Philip ran to him, and heard him reading Isaiah the prophet, and said, “Do you understand what you are reading?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Psalms 73:16–17; Isaiah 29:18–19"> He said, “How can I, unless someone explains it to me?” He begged Philip to come up and sit with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7–8; 1 Peter 2:21–24; Jeremiah 51:40"> Now the passage of the Scripture which he was reading was this,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He was led as a sheep to the slaughter.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">As a lamb before his shearer is silent,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so he doesn’t open his mouth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Isaiah 53:8; Zechariah 13:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">In his humiliation, his judgement was taken away.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Who will declare His generation?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">For his life is taken from the earth.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Matthew 2:2–4"> The eunuch answered Philip, “Who is the prophet talking about? About himself, or about someone else?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Acts 18:28; Acts 28:23"> Philip opened his mouth, and beginning from this Scripture, preached to him about Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 10:47; John 3:5; Titus 3:5–6"> As they went on the way, they came to some water; and the eunuch said, “Behold, here is water. What is keeping me from being baptised?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR adds Philip said, “If you believe with all your heart, you may.” He answered, “I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:1; John 3:22–23"> He commanded the chariot to stand still, and they both went down into the water, both Philip and the eunuch, and he baptised him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 18:12; 2 Kings 2:16; Ezekiel 8:3"> When they came up out of the water, the Spirit of the Lord caught Philip away, and the eunuch didn’t see him any more, for he went on his way rejoicing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 21:8; Acts 23:23; Acts 23:33"> But Philip was found at Azotus. Passing through, he preached the Good News to all the cities until he came to Caesarea.</span>
</div>
