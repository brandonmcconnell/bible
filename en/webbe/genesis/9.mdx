<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Colossians 2:21–22; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> Every moving thing that lives will be food for you. As I gave you the green herb, I have given everything to you.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 6:18; Isaiah 54:9–10; Romans 1:3"> “As for me, behold, I establish my covenant with you, and with your offspring after you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 8:1; Genesis 9:15–16; Psalms 36:5–6"> and with every living creature that is with you: the birds, the livestock, and every animal of the earth with you, of all that go out of the ship, even every animal of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 54:9; Genesis 8:21–22; Genesis 7:21–23"> I will establish my covenant with you: All flesh will not be cut off any more by the waters of the flood. There will never again be a flood to destroy the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 17:11; Matthew 26:26–28; Exodus 12:13"> God said, “This is the token of the covenant which I make between me and you and every living creature that is with you, for perpetual generations:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Revelation 4:3; Revelation 10:1"> I set my rainbow in the cloud, and it will be a sign of a covenant between me and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When I bring a cloud over the earth, that the rainbow will be seen in the cloud,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Ezekiel 16:60; Isaiah 54:8–10"> I will remember my covenant, which is between me and you and every living creature of all flesh, and the waters will no more become a flood to destroy all flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 54:8–10; Jeremiah 32:40; 2 Samuel 23:5"> The rainbow will be in the cloud. I will look at it, that I may remember the everlasting covenant between God and every living creature of all flesh that is on the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> God said to Noah, “This is the token of the covenant which I have established between me and all flesh that is on the earth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:4–28; Genesis 8:17; Genesis 5:32"> These three were the sons of Noah, and from these the whole earth was populated.</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 30:17; Galatians 6:1; Matthew 18:15"> Ham, the father of Canaan, saw the nakedness of his father, and told his two brothers outside.</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Noah awoke from his wine, and knew what his youngest son had done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 9:23; Deuteronomy 27:16; Judges 1:28–30"> He said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Canaan is cursed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He will be a servant of servants to his brothers.”</span></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 11:12; Romans 15:12; Hebrews 11:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">May God enlarge Japheth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Let him dwell in the tents of Shem.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Let Canaan be his servant.”</span></span>
