<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:32–33; Genesis 23:1–2; Genesis 28:1"> Abraham took another wife, and her name was Keturah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:32–33; Numbers 25:17–18; Job 2:11"> She bore him Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Jeremiah 49:8; Ezekiel 27:20"> Jokshan became the father of Sheba, and Dedan. The sons of Dedan were Asshurim, Letushim, and Leummim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6"> The sons of Midian were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah. All these were the children of Keturah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 24:36; Hebrews 1:2; Galatians 3:29"> Abraham gave all that he had to Isaac,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 21:14; Judges 6:3; Acts 14:17"> but Abraham gave gifts to the sons of Abraham’s concubines. While he still lived, he sent them away from Isaac his son, eastward, to the east country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 12:4"> These are the days of the years of Abraham’s life which he lived: one hundred and seventy-five years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 35:28–29; Genesis 15:15; 1 Chronicles 29:28"> Abraham gave up his spirit, and died at a good old age, an old man, and full of years, and was gathered to his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 50:13; Genesis 35:29; Genesis 49:29–30"> Isaac and Ishmael, his sons, buried him in the cave of Machpelah, in the field of Ephron, the son of Zohar the Hittite, which is near Mamre,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 23:16; Genesis 49:31"> the field which Abraham purchased from the children of Heth. Abraham was buried there with Sarah, his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 16:14; Genesis 24:62"> After the death of Abraham, God blessed Isaac, his son. Isaac lived by Beer Lahai Roi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 83:6; Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:13"> Now this is the history of the generations of Ishmael, Abraham’s son, whom Hagar the Egyptian, Sarah’s servant, bore to Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:29–31; Isaiah 60:7; Song of Solomon 1:5"> These are the names of the sons of Ishmael, by their names, according to the order of their birth: the firstborn of Ishmael, Nebaioth, then Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 21:11; Isaiah 21:16"> Mishma, Dumah, Massa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:19; Job 2:11"> Hadad, Tema, Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 17:20; Genesis 17:23"> These are the sons of Ishmael, and these are their names, by their villages, and by their encampments: twelve princes, according to their nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Genesis 25:7–8"> These are the years of the life of Ishmael: one hundred and thirty-seven years. He gave up his spirit and died, and was gathered to his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 16:12; Genesis 20:1; 2 Kings 23:29"> They lived from Havilah to Shur that is before Egypt, as you go towards Assyria. He lived opposite all his relatives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 1:2; Luke 3:34; Acts 7:8"> This is the history of the generations of Isaac, Abraham’s son. Abraham became the father of Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 24:29; Genesis 24:67; Genesis 22:23"> Isaac was forty years old when he took Rebekah, the daughter of Bethuel the Syrian of Paddan Aram, the sister of Laban the Syrian, to be his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Isaiah 58:9; Ezra 8:23"> Isaac entreated the LORD for his wife, because she was barren. The LORD was entreated by him, and Rebekah his wife conceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Samuel 28:6; 1 Samuel 30:8"> The children struggled together within her. She said, “If it is like this, why do I live?” She went to enquire of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 9:10–13; Genesis 27:40; Genesis 27:29"> The LORD said to her,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Two nations are in your womb.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Two peoples will be separated from your body.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The one people will be stronger than the other people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The elder will serve the younger.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> When her days to be delivered were fulfilled, behold, there were twins in her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 27:11; Genesis 27:16; Genesis 27:23"> The first came out red all over, like a hairy garment. They named him Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 27:36; Hosea 12:3; Genesis 38:28–30"> After that, his brother came out, and his hand had hold on Esau’s heel. He was named Jacob. Isaac was sixty years old when she bore them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 11:9; Genesis 21:20; Genesis 10:9"> The boys grew. Esau was a skilful hunter, a man of the field. Jacob was a quiet man, living in tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 27:19; Genesis 27:31; Genesis 27:4"> Now Isaac loved Esau, because he ate his venison. Rebekah loved Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 13:25; Isaiah 40:30–31; 1 Samuel 14:28"> Jacob boiled stew. Esau came in from the field, and he was famished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 36:1; Exodus 15:15; Genesis 36:9"> Esau said to Jacob, “Please feed me with some of that red stew, for I am famished.” Therefore his name was called Edom.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Edom” means “red”.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Jacob said, “First, sell me your birthright.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachi 3:14; Job 21:15; Job 22:17"> Esau said, “Behold, I am about to die. What good is the birthright to me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 12:16; Genesis 27:36; Hebrews 6:16"> Jacob said, “Swear to me first.” He swore to him. He sold his birthright to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Philippians 3:18–19; Hebrews 12:16–17; 1 Corinthians 15:32"> Jacob gave Esau bread and lentil stew. He ate and drank, rose up, and went his way. So Esau despised his birthright.</span>
