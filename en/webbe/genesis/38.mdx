<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1; 2 Kings 4:8; Proverbs 13:20"> At that time, Judah went down from his brothers, and visited a certain Adullamite, whose name was Hirah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3; Genesis 24:3; Genesis 6:2"> There, Judah saw the daughter of a certain Canaanite man named Shua. He took her, and went in to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> She conceived, and bore a son; and he named him Er.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> She conceived again, and bore a son; and she named him Onan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; Genesis 38:26; Genesis 46:12"> She yet again bore a son, and named him Shelah. He was at Chezib when she bore him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:3; Genesis 21:21; Matthew 1:3"> Judah took a wife for Er, his firstborn, and her name was Tamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3; Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> Er, Judah’s firstborn, was wicked in the LORD’s sight. So the LORD killed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Matthew 22:23–27; Ruth 4:5–11"> Judah said to Onan, “Go in to your brother’s wife, and perform the duty of a husband’s brother to her, and raise up offspring for your brother.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Deuteronomy 25:6; James 3:16"> Onan knew that the offspring wouldn’t be his; and when he went in to his brother’s wife, he spilled his semen on the ground, lest he should give offspring to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 11:1; 1 Chronicles 21:7"> The thing which he did was evil in the LORD’s sight, and he killed him also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 22:13; Ruth 1:11–13"> Then Judah said to Tamar, his daughter-in-law, “Remain a widow in your father’s house, until Shelah, my son, is grown up;” for he said, “Lest he also die, like his brothers.” Tamar went and lived in her father’s house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; 2 Samuel 13:39; Judges 14:1"> After many days, Shua’s daughter, the wife of Judah, died. Judah was comforted, and went up to his sheep shearers to Timnah, he and his friend Hirah, the Adullamite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Tamar was told, “Behold, your father-in-law is going up to Timnah to shear his sheep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 38:26; Genesis 38:11–13; Jeremiah 3:2"> She took off the garments of her widowhood, and covered herself with her veil, and wrapped herself, and sat in the gate of Enaim, which is on the way to Timnah; for she saw that Shelah was grown up, and she wasn’t given to him as a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> When Judah saw her, he thought that she was a prostitute, for she had covered her face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 26:15; 2 Samuel 13:11"> He turned to her by the way, and said, “Please come, let me come in to you,” for he didn’t know that she was his daughter-in-law.<br />She said, “What will you give me, that you may come in to me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:33; Genesis 38:20; Proverbs 20:16"> He said, “I will send you a young goat from the flock.”<br />She said, “Will you give me a pledge, until you send it?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 38:25–26; Hosea 4:11; Jeremiah 22:24"> He said, “What pledge will I give you?”<br />She said, “Your signet and your cord, and your staff that is in your hand.”<br />He gave them to her, and came in to her, and she conceived by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 38:14; 2 Samuel 14:5; 2 Samuel 14:2"> She arose, and went away, and put off her veil from her, and put on the garments of her widowhood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 20:9; Leviticus 19:17; 2 Samuel 13:3"> Judah sent the young goat by the hand of his friend, the Adullamite, to receive the pledge from the woman’s hand, but he didn’t find her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then he asked the men of her place, saying, “Where is the prostitute, that was at Enaim by the road?”<br />They said, “There has been no prostitute here.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> He returned to Judah, and said, “I haven’t found her; and also the men of the place said, ‘There has been no prostitute here.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; Proverbs 6:33; Revelation 16:15"> Judah said, “Let her keep it, lest we be shamed. Behold, I sent this young goat, and you haven’t found her.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 21:9; Ezekiel 23:19; Judges 19:2"> About three months later, Judah was told, “Tamar, your daughter-in-law, has played the prostitute. Moreover, behold, she is with child by prostitution.”<br />Judah said, “Bring her out, and let her be burnt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 37:32; Genesis 38:18; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> When she was brought out, she sent to her father-in-law, saying, “I am with child by the man who owns these.” She also said, “Please discern whose these are—the signet, and the cords, and the staff.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:17; John 8:9; Titus 2:11–12"> Judah acknowledged them, and said, “She is more righteous than I, because I didn’t give her to Shelah, my son.”<br />He knew her again no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 25:24"> In the time of her travail, behold, twins were in her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When she travailed, one put out a hand, and the midwife took and tied a scarlet thread on his hand, saying, “This came out first.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:4; Matthew 1:3; Genesis 46:12"> As he drew back his hand, behold, his brother came out, and she said, “Why have you made a breach for yourself?” Therefore his name was called Perez.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Perez means “breaking out”.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:6; 1 Chronicles 2:4; Matthew 1:3"> Afterward his brother came out, who had the scarlet thread on his hand, and his name was called Zerah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Zerah means “scarlet” or “brightness”.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
