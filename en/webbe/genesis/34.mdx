<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 11:2; Psalms 119:20; Psalms 84:2"> Hamor talked with them, saying, “The soul of my son, Shechem, longs for your daughter. Please give her to him as a wife.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 33:15; Genesis 18:3"> Shechem said to her father and to her brothers, “Let me find favour in your eyes, and whatever you will tell me I will give.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–39; 2 Samuel 15:7; Genesis 17:11"> and said to them, “We can’t do this thing, to give our sister to one who is uncircumcised; for that is a reproach to us.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But if you will not listen to us and be circumcised, then we will take our sister,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew has, literally, “daughter”">a</a></sup>and we will be gone.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:12; Acts 13:50; 1 Samuel 22:14"> The young man didn’t wait to do this thing, because he had delight in Jacob’s daughter, and he was honoured above all the house of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; Proverbs 31:23; Amos 5:12"> Hamor and Shechem, his son, came to the gate of their city, and talked with the men of their city, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> “These men are peaceful with us. Therefore let them live in the land and trade in it. For behold, the land is large enough for them. Let’s take their daughters to us for wives, and let’s give them our daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Only on this condition will the men consent to us to live with us, to become one people, if every male amongst us is circumcised, as they are circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 1:12–13; John 6:26–27; John 2:16"> Won’t their livestock and their possessions and all their animals be ours? Only let’s give our consent to them, and they will dwell with us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 23:10; 1 Corinthians 7:19; Isaiah 1:10–16"> All who went out of the gate of his city listened to Hamor, and to Shechem his son; and every male was circumcised, all who went out of the gate of his city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 49:5–7; Genesis 29:33–34; Numbers 31:7"> On the third day, when they were sore, two of Jacob’s sons, Simeon and Levi, Dinah’s brothers, each took his sword, came upon the unsuspecting city, and killed all the males.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:26; Deuteronomy 32:42; Isaiah 31:8"> They killed Hamor and Shechem, his son, with the edge of the sword, and took Dinah out of Shechem’s house, and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 7:13; Joshua 7:21; Exodus 2:14"> Jacob’s sons came on the dead, and plundered the city, because they had defiled their sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 1:15–16; Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Job 20:5"> They took their flocks, their herds, their donkeys, that which was in the city, that which was in the field,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> and all their wealth. They took captive all their little ones and their wives, and took as plunder everything that was in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 105:12; Joshua 7:25; Exodus 5:21"> Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, “You have troubled me, to make me odious to the inhabitants of the land, amongst the Canaanites and the Perizzites. I am few in number. They will gather themselves together against me and strike me, and I will be destroyed, I and my house.”</span>
</div>
