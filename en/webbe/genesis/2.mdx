<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 31:17; Exodus 20:11; Psalms 33:6"> The heavens, the earth, and all their vast array were finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:4; Exodus 31:17; Deuteronomy 5:14"> On the seventh day God finished his work which he had done; and he rested on the seventh day from all his work which he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:12; Mark 2:27; Leviticus 23:3"> God blessed the seventh day, and made it holy, because he rested in it from all his work of creation which he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 5:1; Revelation 1:8; Genesis 1:4"> This is the history of the generations of the heavens and of the earth when they were created, in the day that the LORD<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="When rendered in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS, “LORD” or “GOD” is the translation of God’s Proper Name (Hebrew “יהוה”, usually pronounced Yahweh).">a</a></sup>God made the earth and the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 3:23; Hebrews 6:7; Job 5:10"> No plant of the field was yet in the earth, and no herb of the field had yet sprung up; for the LORD God had not caused it to rain on the earth. There was not a man to till the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> but a mist went up from the earth, and watered the whole surface of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 33:4; 1 Corinthians 15:45"> The LORD God formed man from the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Genesis 3:24; Genesis 13:10"> The LORD God planted a garden eastward, in Eden, and there he put the man whom he had formed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 3:22; Revelation 2:7; Revelation 22:14"> Out of the ground the LORD God made every tree to grow that is pleasant to the sight, and good for food, including the tree of life in the middle of the garden and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Psalms 46:4"> A river went out of Eden to water the garden; and from there it was parted, and became the source of four rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Genesis 10:29; 1 Samuel 15:7"> The name of the first is Pishon: it flows through the whole land of Havilah, where there is gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 28:20; Numbers 11:7; Ezekiel 28:13"> and the gold of that land is good. Bdellium<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or, aromatic resin">b</a></sup>and onyx stone are also there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:6; Isaiah 11:11"> The name of the second river is Gihon. It is the same river that flows through the whole land of Cush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 10:4; Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 1:7"> The name of the third river is Hiddekel. This is the one which flows in front of Assyria. The fourth river is the Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Genesis 2:8; Ephesians 4:28"> The LORD God took the man, and put him into the garden of Eden to cultivate and keep it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; 1 Timothy 4:4; 1 Timothy 6:17"> The LORD God commanded the man, saying, “You may freely eat of every tree of the garden;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:23; James 1:15; Genesis 3:19"> but you shall not eat of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil; for in the day that you eat of it, you will surely die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:9–12; 1 Corinthians 11:7–12; Proverbs 18:22"> The LORD God said, “It is not good for the man to be alone. I will make him a helper comparable to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, suitable for, or appropriate for.">c</a></sup>him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Genesis 1:28; Genesis 9:2"> Out of the ground the LORD God formed every animal of the field, and every bird of the sky, and brought them to the man to see what he would call them. Whatever the man called every living creature became its name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:18"> The man gave names to all livestock, and to the birds of the sky, and to every animal of the field; but for man there was not found a helper comparable to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:12; Job 4:13; 1 Samuel 26:12"> The LORD God caused the man to fall into a deep sleep. As the man slept, he took one of his ribs, and closed up the flesh in its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:8–9; 1 Timothy 2:13; Hebrews 13:4"> The LORD God made a woman from the rib which he had taken from the man, and brought her to the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 5:28–30; 1 Corinthians 11:8–9; 2 Samuel 5:1"> The man said, “This is now bone of my bones, and flesh of my flesh. She will be called ‘woman,’ because she was taken out of Man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 19:3–9; Ephesians 5:28–31; Mark 10:6–12"> Therefore a man will leave his father and his mother, and will join with his wife, and they will be one flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 3:7; Genesis 3:10–11; Romans 10:11"> The man and his wife were both naked, and they were not ashamed.</span>
