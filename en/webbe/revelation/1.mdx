<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Revelation 22:6; Galatians 1:12"> This is the Revelation of Jesus Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Christ” means “Anointed One”.">a</a></sup>which God gave him to show to his servants the things which must happen soon, which he sent and made known by his angel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or, messenger (here and wherever angel is mentioned)">b</a></sup>to his servant, John,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:6; John 19:35; 1 John 1:1"> who testified to God’s word and of the testimony of Jesus Christ, about everything that he saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Revelation 22:10; Romans 13:11"> Blessed is he who reads and those who hear the words of the prophecy, and keep the things that are written in it, for the time is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; John 1:1; Hebrews 13:8"> John, to the seven assemblies that are in Asia: Grace to you and peace from God, who is and who was and who is to come; and from the seven Spirits who are before his throne;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:27; Hebrews 9:14; 1 John 1:7"> and from Jesus Christ, the faithful witness, the firstborn of the dead, and the ruler of the kings of the earth. To him who loves us, and washed us from our sins by his blood—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Revelation 20:6; Exodus 19:6"> and he made us to be a Kingdom, priests to his God and Father—to him be the glory and the dominion forever and ever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:30; Zechariah 12:10; Daniel 7:13"> Behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Behold”, from “ἰδοὺ”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. It is often used as an interjection.">c</a></sup>he is coming with the clouds, and every eye will see him, including those who pierced him. All the tribes of the earth will mourn over him. Even so, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Revelation 22:13; Isaiah 43:10"> <span className="woj">“I am the Alpha and the Omega,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="TR adds “the Beginning and the End”">d</a></sup><span className="woj">”</span>says the Lord God,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="TR omits “God”">e</a></sup><span className="woj">“who is and who was and who is to come, the Almighty.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 14:22; Revelation 3:10; John 16:33"> I John, your brother and partner with you in the oppression, Kingdom, and perseverance in Christ Jesus, was on the isle that is called Patmos because of God’s Word and the testimony of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:10; 2 Corinthians 12:2–4; Revelation 4:1–2"> I was in the Spirit on the Lord’s day, and I heard behind me a loud voice, like a trumpet</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:1; Jeremiah 30:2; Habakkuk 2:2"> saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="TR adds “I am the Alpha and the Omega, the First and the Last.”">f</a></sup><span className="woj">“What you see, write in a book and send to the seven assemblies:</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="TR adds “which are in Asia”">g</a></sup><span className="woj">to Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, and to Laodicea.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 4:2; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 1:20–1"> I turned to see the voice that spoke with me. Having turned, I saw seven golden lamp stands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Daniel 10:5–6; Daniel 10:16"> And amongst the lamp stands was one like a son of man, clothed with a robe reaching down to his feet, and with a golden sash around his chest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 10:6; Daniel 7:9; Revelation 19:12"> His head and his hair were white as white wool, like snow. His eyes were like a flame of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2; Daniel 10:6; Revelation 14:2"> His feet were like burnished brass, as if it had been refined in a furnace. His voice was like the voice of many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 19:15; Revelation 2:12"> He had seven stars in his right hand. Out of his mouth proceeded a sharp two-edged sword. His face was like the sun shining at its brightest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Revelation 22:13; Daniel 10:8–10"> When I saw him, I fell at his feet like a dead man. He laid his right hand on me, saying,<span className="woj">“Don’t be afraid. I am the first and the last,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 6:9; Hebrews 7:25; Romans 14:8–9"> <span className="woj">and the Living one. I was dead, and behold, I am alive forever and ever. Amen. I have the keys of Death and of Hades.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="or, Hell">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 4:1–11; Revelation 1:11–3"> <span className="woj">Write therefore the things which you have seen, and the things which are, and the things which will happen hereafter.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 2:1; Revelation 3:1; Revelation 1:16"> <span className="woj">The mystery of the seven stars which you saw in my right hand, and the seven golden lamp stands is this: The seven stars are the angels</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="or, messengers (here and wherever angels are mentioned)">i</a></sup><span className="woj">of the seven assemblies. The seven lamp stands are seven assemblies.</span></span>
