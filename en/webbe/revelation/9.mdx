<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 8:10; Romans 10:7; Revelation 9:2"> The fifth angel sounded, and I saw a star from the sky which had fallen to the earth. The key to the pit of the abyss was given to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:10; Genesis 19:28; Joel 2:2"> He opened the pit of the abyss, and smoke went up out of the pit, like the smoke from a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR adds “great”">a</a></sup>burning furnace. The sun and the air were darkened because of the smoke from the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Revelation 9:5; Joel 2:25"> Then out of the smoke came locusts on the earth, and power was given to them, as the scorpions of the earth have power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 7:2–4; Ezekiel 9:4; Revelation 14:1"> They were told that they should not hurt the grass of the earth, neither any green thing, neither any tree, but only those people who don’t have God’s seal on their foreheads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 9:10; Job 2:6; Revelation 9:3"> They were given power, not to kill them, but to torment them for five months. Their torment was like the torment of a scorpion when it strikes a person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 23:30; Jeremiah 8:3; Revelation 6:16"> In those days people will seek death, and will in no way find it. They will desire to die, and death will flee from them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 7:8; Nahum 3:17; Joel 2:4–5"> The shapes of the locusts were like horses prepared for war. On their heads were something like golden crowns, and their faces were like people’s faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joel 1:6; 1 Corinthians 11:14–15; 1 Peter 3:3"> They had hair like women’s hair, and their teeth were like those of lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joel 2:5–8; Nahum 2:4–5; Job 39:25"> They had breastplates like breastplates of iron. The sound of their wings was like the sound of many chariots and horses rushing to war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 9:5; Revelation 9:3; Revelation 9:19"> They have tails like those of scorpions, with stingers. In their tails they have power to harm men for five months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Revelation 9:1–2; Ephesians 2:2"> They have over them as king the angel of the abyss. His name in Hebrew is “Abaddon”,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Abaddon” is a Hebrew word that means “ruin”, “destruction”, or “the place of destruction”">b</a></sup>but in Greek, he has the name “Apollyon”.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Apollyon” means “Destroyer”.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 11:14; Revelation 8:13–2; Revelation 9:13–21"> The first woe is past. Behold, there are still two woes coming after this.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 8:3–5; Exodus 30:2–3; Revelation 9:1"> The sixth angel sounded. I heard a voice from the horns of the golden altar which is before God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 16:12; Revelation 9:15; 2 Samuel 8:3"> saying to the sixth angel who had the trumpet, “Free the four angels who are bound at the great river Euphrates!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 9:18; Revelation 8:7; Revelation 8:9"> The four angels were freed who had been prepared for that hour and day and month and year, so that they might kill one third of mankind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Psalms 68:17; Daniel 7:10"> The number of the armies of the horsemen was two hundred million.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="literally, “ten thousands of ten thousands”">d</a></sup>I heard the number of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 9:18; Revelation 14:10; 1 Chronicles 12:8"> Thus I saw the horses in the vision and those who sat on them, having breastplates of fiery red, hyacinth blue, and sulphur yellow; and the horses’ heads resembled lions’ heads. Out of their mouths proceed fire, smoke, and sulphur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 9:17; Revelation 9:15"> By these three plagues, one third of mankind was killed: by the fire, the smoke, and the sulphur, which proceeded out of their mouths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 9:15; Revelation 9:10; Ephesians 4:14"> For the power of the horses is in their mouths and in their tails. For their tails are like serpents, and have heads; and with them they harm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Deuteronomy 31:29; 1 Timothy 4:1"> The rest of mankind, who were not killed with these plagues, didn’t repent of the works of their hands, that they wouldn’t worship demons, and the idols of gold, and of silver, and of brass, and of stone, and of wood, which can’t see, hear, or walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; 2 Corinthians 12:21; Revelation 17:5"> They didn’t repent of their murders, their sorceries,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The word for “sorceries” (pharmakeia) also implies the use of potions, poisons, and drugs">e</a></sup>their sexual immorality, or their thefts.</span>
</div>
</div>
