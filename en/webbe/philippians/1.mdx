<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:1; Acts 16:12–15; Romans 1:1"> Paul and Timothy, servants of Jesus Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Christ” means “Anointed One”.">a</a></sup>to all the saints in Christ Jesus who are at Philippi, with the overseers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or, superintendents, or bishops">b</a></sup>and servants:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, deacons">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 2 Corinthians 1:2; 1 Peter 1:2"> Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:4; 1 Thessalonians 1:2–3; Philemon 1:4–5"> I thank my God whenever I remember you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:2; Romans 1:9; 2 John 1:4"> always in every request of mine on behalf of you all, making my requests with joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:14–15; Philippians 1:7; Hebrews 3:14"> for your partnership<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The word translated “partnership” (κοινωνίᾳ) also means “fellowship” and “sharing”.">d</a></sup>in furtherance of the Good News from the first day until now;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23–24; Philippians 2:13; Ephesians 4:12"> being confident of this very thing, that he who began a good work in you will complete it until the day of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:3; Philippians 1:5; Colossians 4:18"> It is even right for me to think this way on behalf of all of you, because I have you in my heart, because both in my bonds and in the defence and confirmation of the Good News, you all are partakers with me of grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:1; Romans 1:9; 2 Timothy 1:4"> For God is my witness, how I long after all of you in the tender mercies of Christ Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:12; Colossians 1:9; 2 Peter 1:5–6"> This I pray, that your love may abound yet more and more in knowledge and all discernment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> so that you may approve the things that are excellent, that you may be sincere and without offence to the day of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 12:11; John 15:8; John 15:4–5"> being filled with the fruits of righteousness which are through Jesus Christ, to the glory and praise of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:9; Romans 8:28; Acts 11:19–21"> Now I desire to have you know, brothers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here and where context allows may also be correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">e</a></sup>that the things which happened to me have turned out rather to the progress of the Good News,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:9; Ephesians 3:1; Philippians 1:7"> so that it became evident to the whole palace<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="or, praetorian">f</a></sup>guard, and to all the rest, that my bonds are in Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 6:19–20; 1 Thessalonians 2:2; Luke 1:74"> and that most of the brothers in the Lord, being confident through my bonds, are more abundantly bold to speak the word of God without fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:17–18; Philippians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 11:13"> Some indeed preach Christ even out of envy and strife, and some also out of good will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 2 Corinthians 4:1–2; Psalms 69:26"> The former insincerely preach Christ from selfish ambition, thinking that they add affliction to my chains;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:11–12; Philippians 2:3; Philippians 1:7"> but the latter out of love, knowing that I am appointed for the defence of the Good News.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 9:50; Mark 9:38–40; 1 Corinthians 15:11"> What does it matter? Only that in every way, whether in pretence or in truth, Christ is proclaimed. I rejoice in this, yes, and will rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Acts 16:7; 2 Corinthians 1:11"> For I know that this will turn out to my salvation through your prayers and the supply of the Spirit of Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 4:16; 1 Corinthians 6:20; Isaiah 54:4"> according to my earnest expectation and hope, that I will in no way be disappointed, but with all boldness, as always, now also Christ will be magnified in my body, whether by life or by death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; 2 Corinthians 5:8; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> For to me to live is Christ, and to die is gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; Galatians 2:20; Philippians 1:24"> But if I live on in the flesh, this will bring fruit from my work; yet I don’t know what I will choose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:8; Psalms 17:15; Revelation 14:13"> But I am hard pressed between the two, having the desire to depart and be with Christ, which is far better.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 16:7; Philippians 1:22; Philippians 1:25–26"> Yet to remain in the flesh is more needful for your sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 2:24; 2 Corinthians 1:24; Ephesians 4:11–13"> Having this confidence, I know that I will remain, yes, and remain with you all for your progress and joy in the faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:14; 2 Corinthians 5:12; Philippians 3:3"> that your boasting<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="or, rejoicing">g</a></sup>may abound in Christ Jesus in me through my presence with you again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 4:1; 1 Corinthians 16:13–14; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> Only let your way of life be worthy of the Good News of Christ, that whether I come and see you or am absent, I may hear of your state, that you stand firm in one spirit, with one soul striving for the faith of the Good News;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7–8; 2 Thessalonians 1:5–6; Matthew 10:28"> and in nothing frightened by the adversaries, which is for them a proof of destruction, but to you of salvation, and that from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 4:13; Acts 5:41"> Because it has been granted to you on behalf of Christ, not only to believe in him, but also to suffer on his behalf,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:2; Acts 16:19–40; 2 Timothy 4:7"> having the same conflict which you saw in me and now hear is in me.</span>
</div>
</div>
