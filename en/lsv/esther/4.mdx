<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 9:3; Ezekiel 27:30–31; 2 Samuel 13:19"> And Mordecai has known all that has been done, and Mordecai tears his garments, and puts on sackcloth and ashes, and goes forth into the midst of the city and cries—a cry loud and bitter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and he comes to the front of the gate of the king, but none is to come to the gate of the king with a sackcloth-garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 22:12; Daniel 9:3; Isaiah 58:5"> And in each and every province, the place where the word of the king, even his law, is coming, the Jews have a great mourning, and fasting, and weeping, and lamenting: sackcloth and ashes are spread for many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 77:2; Jeremiah 31:15; Esther 1:12"> And young women of Esther come in and her eunuchs, and declare &#91;it&#93; to her, and the queen is exceedingly pained, and sends garments to clothe Mordecai, and to turn aside his sackcloth from off him, and he has not received &#91;them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Romans 12:15; Esther 1:10"> And Esther calls to Hatach, of the eunuchs of the king, whom he has stationed before her, and gives him a charge for Mordecai, to know what this &#91;is&#93;, and why this &#91;is&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esther 4:3; Esther 7:2; Esther 9:12"> And Hatach goes out to Mordecai, to a broad place of the city, that &#91;is&#93; before the gate of the king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 3:2–15"> and Mordecai declares to him all that has met him, and the explanation of the money that Haman said to weigh to the treasuries of the king for the Jews, to destroy them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:4; Job 9:15; 1 Timothy 6:13"> and the copy of the writing of the law that had been given in Shushan to destroy them he has given to him, to show Esther, and to declare &#91;it&#93; to her, and to lay a charge on her to go in to the king, to make supplication to him, and to seek from before him, for her people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Hatach comes in and declares to Esther the words of Mordecai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and Esther speaks to Hatach, and charges him for Mordecai:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esther 5:1–2; Esther 8:4; Daniel 2:9"> “All servants of the king, and people of the provinces of the king, know that any man and woman who comes to the king, into the inner court, who is not called—one law of his &#91;is&#93; to put &#91;them&#93; to death, apart from him to whom the king holds out the golden scepter, then he has lived; and I have not been called to come in to the king these thirty days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And they declare to Mordecai the words of Esther,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 24:10–12; Philippians 2:30; John 12:25"> and Mordecai commands to send back to Esther: “Do not think in your soul to be delivered &#91;in&#93; the house of the king, more than all the Jews,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 45:4–8; 1 Samuel 12:22; Deuteronomy 32:36"> but if you keep entirely silent at this time, respite and deliverance remains to the Jews from another place, and you and the house of your fathers are destroyed; and who knows whether for a time like this you have come to the kingdom?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Esther commands to send back to Mordecai:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 20:24; 2 Chronicles 20:3; Joel 2:12–17"> “Go, gather all the Jews who are found in Shushan, and fast for me, and do not eat nor drink three days, by night and by day; also I and my young women fast likewise, and so I go in to the king, that &#91;is&#93; not according to law, and when I have perished—I have perished.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Mordecai passes on, and does according to all that Esther has charged on him.</span>
