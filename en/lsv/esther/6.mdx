<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 6:18; Daniel 2:1; Esther 2:23"> On that night the sleep of the king has fled away, and he commands to bring in the scroll of memorials of the chronicles, and they are read before the king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esther 2:21–22"> and it is found written that Mordecai had declared concerning Bigthana and Teresh, two of the eunuchs of the king, of the keepers of the threshold, who sought to put forth a hand on King Ahasuerus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:15; Genesis 40:23; Acts 28:8–10"> And the king says, “What honor and greatness has been done to Mordecai for this?” And the servants of the king, his ministers, say, “Nothing has been done with him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 5:14; Esther 4:11; Esther 5:1"> And the king says, “Who &#91;is&#93; in the court?” And Haman has come into the outer court of the house of the king, to say to the king to hang Mordecai on the tree that he had prepared for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the servants of the king say to him, “Behold, Haman is standing in the court”; and the king says, “Let him come in.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Obadiah 1:3; Isaiah 62:4–5"> And Haman comes in, and the king says to him, “What should &#91;I&#93; do with the man in whose honor the king has delighted?” And Haman says in his heart, “To whom does the king delight to do honor more than myself?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 6:11; Esther 6:9"> And Haman says to the king, “The man in whose honor the king has delighted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 1:33; Luke 15:22; Esther 1:11"> let them bring in royal clothing that the king has put on himself, and a horse on which the king has ridden, and that the royal crown be put on his head,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 41:43; 1 Kings 1:33–34; Zechariah 9:9"> and to give the clothing and the horse into the hand of a man of the heads of the king, the chiefs, and they have clothed the man in whose honor the king has delighted, and caused him to ride on the horse in a broad place of the city, and called before him: Thus it is done to the man in whose honor the king has delighted.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:7; Daniel 4:37; 2 Kings 10:10"> And the king says to Haman, “Hurry, take the clothing and the horse, as you have spoken, and do so to Mordecai the Jew, who is sitting in the gate of the king; there does not fall a thing of all that you have spoken.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 1:52; Esther 9:3; Revelation 3:9"> And Haman takes the clothing, and the horse, and clothed Mordecai, and causes him to ride in a broad place of the city, and calls before him, “Thus it is done to the man in whose glory the king has delighted.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:30; Jeremiah 14:3–4; Job 20:5"> And Mordecai turns back to the gate of the king, and Haman has been hurried to his house mourning, and with covered head,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Hosea 14:9; Genesis 40:19"> and Haman recounts to his wife Zeresh, and to all his friends, all that has met him, and his wise men and his wife Zeresh say to him, “If Mordecai &#91;is&#93; of the seed of the Jews, before whom you have begun to fall, you are not able for him, but certainly fall before him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 5:8; Esther 5:14; Deuteronomy 32:35–36"> They are yet speaking with him, and eunuchs of the king have come, and hurry to bring in Haman to the banquet that Esther has made.</span>
