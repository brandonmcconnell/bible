<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 24:7; Esther 5:11; Daniel 6:2"> After these things has King Ahasuerus exalted Haman son of Hammedatha the Agagite, and lifts him up, and sets his throne above all the heads who &#91;are&#93; with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esther 3:5; Psalms 15:4; Esther 5:9"> and all servants of the king, who &#91;are&#93; in the gate of the king, are bowing and doing homage to Haman, for so the king has commanded for him; and Mordecai does not bow nor pay respect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esther 3:2; Exodus 1:17; Esther 2:19"> And the servants of the king, who &#91;are&#93; in the gate of the king, say to Mordecai, “Why &#91;are&#93; you transgressing the command of the king?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 39:10; Jonah 1:9; Daniel 6:20–28"> And it comes to pass, in their speaking to him, day by day, and he has not listened to them, that they declare &#91;it&#93; to Haman, to see whether the words of Mordecai stand, for he has declared to them that he &#91;is&#93; a Jew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 3:19; Esther 5:9; Proverbs 21:24"> And Haman sees that Mordecai is not bowing and doing homage to him, and Haman is full of fury,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 83:4; Revelation 12:12"> and it is contemptible in his eyes to put forth a hand on Mordecai by himself, for they have declared to him the people of Mordecai, and Haman seeks to destroy all the Jews who &#91;are&#93; in all the kingdom of Ahasuerus—the people of Mordecai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 6:15; Ezekiel 21:21–22; Esther 9:24–26"> In the first month—it &#91;is&#93; the month of Nisan—in the twelfth year of King Ahasuerus, has one caused to fall Pur (that &#91;is&#93; the lot) before Haman, from day to day, and from month to month, &#91;to&#93; the twelfth, it &#91;is&#93; the month of Adar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 4:12–15; Acts 16:20–21; Deuteronomy 4:27"> And Haman says to King Ahasuerus, “There is one people scattered and separated among the peoples, in all provinces of your kingdom, and their laws &#91;are&#93; diverse from all people, and the laws of the king they are not doing, and for the king it is not profitable to permit them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 23:16; Matthew 18:24"> if to the king &#91;it be&#93; good, let it be written to destroy them, and ten thousand talents of silver I weigh into the hands of those doing the work, to bring &#91;it&#93; into the treasuries of the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 41:42; Esther 8:2; Esther 7:6"> And the king turns aside his signet from off his hand, and gives it to Haman son of Hammedatha the Agagite, adversary of the Jews;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 23:25; Jeremiah 40:4; Jeremiah 26:14"> and the king says to Haman, “The silver is given to you, and the people, to do with it as &#91;it is&#93; good in your eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 21:8; Esther 1:22; Daniel 6:12"> And scribes of the king are called, on the first month, on the thirteenth day of it, and it is written according to all that Haman has commanded, to lieutenants of the king, and to the governors who &#91;are&#93; over province and province, and to the heads of people and people, province and province, according to its writing, and people and people according to its tongue, in the name of King Ahasuerus it has been written and sealed with the signet of the king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:6; Esther 9:10; Esther 8:10–14"> and letters to be sent by the hand of the runners to all provinces of the king, to cut off, to slay, and to destroy all the Jews, from young even to old, infant and women, on one day, on the thirteenth of the twelfth month—it &#91;is&#93; the month of Adar—and to seize their spoil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 8:13–14"> a copy of the writing to be made law in each and every province is revealed to all the peoples, to be ready for this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esther 8:15; John 16:20; Proverbs 29:2"> The runners have gone forth, hurried by the word of the king, and the law has been given in Shushan the palace, and the king and Haman have sat down to drink, and the city Shushan is perplexed.</span>
