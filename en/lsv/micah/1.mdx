<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:18; Isaiah 1:1; Hosea 1:1"> A word of YHWH that has been to Micah the Morashite in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, Hezekiah, kings of Judah, that he has seen concerning Samaria and Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Psalms 11:4; Revelation 3:13"> Hear, O peoples, all of them! Attend, O earth, and its fullness, &#124;&#124; And Lord YHWH is against you for a witness, &#124;&#124; The Lord from His holy temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 4:13; Isaiah 26:21; Deuteronomy 32:13"> For behold, YHWH is going out from His place, &#124;&#124; And He has come down, &#124;&#124; And has trodden on high places of earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nahum 1:5; Psalms 97:5; Judges 5:4"> The mountains have been melted under Him, &#124;&#124; And the valleys split themselves, &#124;&#124; As wax from the presence of fire, &#124;&#124; As waters cast down by a slope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 8:14; Jeremiah 2:19; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16"> For the transgression of Jacob &#91;is&#93; all this, &#124;&#124; And for the sins of the house of Israel. What &#91;is&#93; the transgression of Jacob? Is it not Samaria? And what the high places of Judah? Is it not Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:14; Micah 3:12; Lamentations 4:1"> “And I have set Samaria for a heap of the field, &#124;&#124; For plantations of a vineyard, &#124;&#124; And poured out into a valley her stones, &#124;&#124; And her foundations I uncover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:18; Hosea 2:12; Hosea 8:6"> And all her carved images are beaten down, &#124;&#124; And all her wages are burned with fire, &#124;&#124; And all her idols I make a desolation, &#124;&#124; For from the wage of a harlot she gathered, and to the wage of a harlot they return.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 22:4; Isaiah 20:2–4; Jeremiah 4:19"> For this I lament and howl, &#124;&#124; I go spoiled and naked, &#124;&#124; I make a lamentation like dragons, &#124;&#124; And a mourning like daughters of an ostrich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:11–15; Isaiah 8:7–8; 2 Chronicles 32:1–23"> For mortal &#91;are&#93; her wounds, &#124;&#124; For it has come to Judah, &#124;&#124; It has come to a gate of My people—to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:20; Jeremiah 6:26; Joshua 18:23"> In Gath do not tell—in Acco do not weep, &#124;&#124; In Beth-Aphrah, roll yourself in dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 48:9; Micah 1:8"> Pass over for you, O inhabitant of Shaphir, &#124;&#124; Naked one of shame. The inhabitant of Zaanan has not gone out, &#124;&#124; The lamentation of Beth-Ezel takes from you its standing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:19; Micah 1:9; Amos 3:6"> For the inhabitant of Maroth has stayed for good, &#124;&#124; For calamity has come down from YHWH to the gate of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; 2 Chronicles 32:9; Joshua 10:3"> Bind the chariot to a swift beast, O inhabitant of Lachish, &#124;&#124; The beginning of sin &#91;is&#93; she to the daughter of Zion, &#124;&#124; For in you have been found the transgressions of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 15:44; 2 Kings 16:8; 2 Samuel 8:2"> Therefore you give presents to Moresheth-Gath, &#124;&#124; The houses of Achzib become a lying thing to the kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 15:44; 1 Samuel 22:1; Joshua 15:35"> Yet I bring the possessor to you, O inhabitant of Mareshah, &#124;&#124; The glory of Israel comes to Adullam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 22:12; Job 1:20; Jeremiah 7:29"> Make bald and shave, for your delightful sons, &#124;&#124; Enlarge your baldness as an eagle, &#124;&#124; For they have removed from you!</span>
