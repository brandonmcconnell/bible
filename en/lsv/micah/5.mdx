<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Lamentations 3:30; Job 16:10; Matthew 26:67"> Now gather yourself together, O daughter of troops, &#124;&#124; He has laid a siege against us, &#124;&#124; They strike &#91;the&#93; judge of Israel on the cheek with a rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:6; John 7:42; Luke 1:31–33"> “And you, Beth-Lehem Ephratah, &#124;&#124; Little to be among the chiefs of Judah! From you He comes forth to Me—to be ruler in Israel, &#124;&#124; And His comings forth &#91;are&#93; of old, &#124;&#124; From the days of antiquity.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:1–2; Romans 9:27–28; Micah 4:7"> Therefore He gives them up until the time she who brings forth has brought forth, &#124;&#124; And the remnant of His brothers return to the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 40:10–11; Micah 7:14; Luke 1:32"> And He has stood and delighted in the strength of YHWH, &#124;&#124; In the excellence of the Name of His God YHWH, &#124;&#124; And they have remained, &#124;&#124; For now He is great to the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 2:14; Zechariah 10:3; Ephesians 2:14–17"> And this &#91;One&#93; has been peace. Asshur, when he comes into our land, &#124;&#124; And when he treads in our palaces, &#124;&#124; We have raised against him seven shepherds, &#124;&#124; And eight anointed of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 10:8–11; Isaiah 14:25; Isaiah 14:2"> And they have afflicted the land of Asshur with the sword, &#124;&#124; And the land of Nimrod at its openings, &#124;&#124; And He has delivered from Asshur when he comes into our land, &#124;&#124; And when he treads in our borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 5:3; Isaiah 44:3; Psalms 72:6"> And the remnant of Jacob has been in the midst of many peoples, &#124;&#124; As dew from YHWH—as showers on the herb, &#124;&#124; That does not wait for man, nor stays for the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 10:5; Micah 4:13; Zechariah 9:15"> Indeed, the remnant of Jacob has been among nations, &#124;&#124; In the midst of many peoples, &#124;&#124; As a lion among beasts of a forest, &#124;&#124; As a young lion among ranks of a flock, &#124;&#124; Which if it has passed through, &#124;&#124; Has both trodden down and has torn, &#124;&#124; And there is no deliverer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Psalms 21:8; Isaiah 14:2–4"> High is your hand above your adversaries, &#124;&#124; And all your enemies are cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 9:10; Hosea 14:3; Psalms 20:7–8"> “And it has come to pass in that day,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “I have cut off your horses from your midst, &#124;&#124; And I have destroyed your chariots,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 2:12–17; Ezekiel 38:11; Hosea 10:14"> And I have cut off the cities of your land, &#124;&#124; And I have thrown down all your fortresses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:10–12; Isaiah 8:19–20; Isaiah 2:20"> And have cut off sorcerers out of your hand, &#124;&#124; And observers of clouds—you have none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Ezekiel 36:25; Hosea 14:8"> And I have cut off your carved images, &#124;&#124; And your standing-pillars out of your midst, &#124;&#124; And you do not bow yourself anymore &#124;&#124; To the work of your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 34:13"> And I have plucked up your Asherim out of your midst, &#124;&#124; And I have destroyed your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 149:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Micah 5:8"> And I have done vengeance in anger and in fury, &#124;&#124; With the nations who have not listened!”</span>
