<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:29; Deuteronomy 32:1; Micah 1:2"> Now hear that which YHWH is saying: “Rise—strive with the mountains, &#124;&#124; And cause the hills to hear your voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Hosea 12:2; Isaiah 1:18"> Hear, O mountains, the strife of YHWH, &#124;&#124; You strong ones—foundations of earth! For a strife &#91;is&#93; to YHWH, with His people, &#124;&#124; And with Israel He reasons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:5; Isaiah 43:22–23; Psalms 81:13"> O My people, what have I done to you? And how have I wearied you? Testify against Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 12:51; Deuteronomy 7:8; Amos 2:10"> For I brought you up from the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And I have ransomed you from the house of servants, &#124;&#124; And I send Moses, Aaron, and Miriam before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:9–10; Numbers 25:1; Judges 5:11"> O My people, please remember &#124;&#124; What Balak king of Moab counseled, &#124;&#124; What Balaam son of Beor answered him &#124;&#124; (From Shittim to Gilgal), &#124;&#124; In order to know the righteous acts of YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 51:16–17; Romans 10:2–3; Psalms 40:6–8"> With what do I come before YHWH? Do I bow to God Most High? Do I come before Him with burnt-offerings? With calves—sons of a year?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 16:3; 1 Samuel 15:22; Psalms 51:16"> Is YHWH pleased with thousands of rams? With myriads of streams of oil? Do I give my firstborn &#91;for&#93; my transgression? The fruit of my body &#91;for&#93; the sin of my soul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:12–13; Proverbs 21:3; Colossians 3:12"> He has declared to you, O man, what &#91;is&#93; good; Indeed, what is YHWH requiring of you, &#124;&#124; Except—to do judgment, and love kindness, &#124;&#124; And to walk lowly with your God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Hosea 14:9; Isaiah 30:27"> A voice of YHWH calls to the city, &#124;&#124; And wisdom fears Your Name, &#124;&#124; “Hear the rod, and Him who appointed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 3:10; Amos 8:5–6; Jeremiah 5:26–27"> Are there yet &#91;in&#93; the house of the wicked &#124;&#124; Treasures of wickedness, &#124;&#124; And the abhorred meager ephah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 12:7; Leviticus 19:36; Proverbs 16:11"> Do I reckon &#91;it&#93; pure with balances of wickedness? And with a bag of deceitful stones?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 1:23; Jeremiah 9:8; Hosea 7:13"> Whose rich ones have been full of violence, &#124;&#124; And its inhabitants have spoken falsehood, &#124;&#124; And their tongue &#91;is&#93; deceitful in their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:23; Deuteronomy 28:21–22; Hosea 13:16"> And I also, I have begun to strike you, &#124;&#124; To make desolate, because of your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 4:10; Leviticus 26:26; Isaiah 65:13"> You eat, and you are not satisfied, &#124;&#124; And your pit &#91;is&#93; in your midst, &#124;&#124; And you remove, and do not deliver, &#124;&#124; And that which you deliver, I give to a sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:13; Amos 5:11; Jeremiah 12:13"> You sow, and you do not reap, &#124;&#124; You tread the olive, &#124;&#124; And you do not pour out oil, &#124;&#124; And new wine—and you do not drink wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:51; 1 Kings 16:25–33; Jeremiah 7:24"> And the statutes of Omri are kept habitually, &#124;&#124; And all the work of the house of Ahab, &#124;&#124; And you walk in their counsels, &#124;&#124; For My giving you for a desolation, &#124;&#124; And its inhabitants for a hissing, &#124;&#124; And you bear the reproach of My people!”</span>
