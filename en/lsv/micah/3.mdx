<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:27; Proverbs 28:4; Psalms 53:4"> You who are hating good, and loving evil, &#124;&#124; Taking violently their skin from off them, &#124;&#124; And their flesh from off their bones,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 14:4; Ezekiel 11:6–7; Ezekiel 11:3"> And who have eaten the flesh of My people, &#124;&#124; And their skin from off them have stripped, &#124;&#124; And their bones they have broken, &#124;&#124; And they have spread &#91;them&#93; out as in a pot, &#124;&#124; And as flesh in the midst of a cauldron.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; Isaiah 1:15; Ezekiel 8:18"> Then they cry to YHWH, &#124;&#124; And He does not answer them, &#124;&#124; And hides His face from them at that time, &#124;&#124; As they have made evil their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 14:14–15; Ezekiel 22:25–29"> Thus said YHWH concerning the prophets &#124;&#124; Who are causing My people to err, &#124;&#124; Who are biting with their teeth, &#124;&#124; And have cried “Peace,” &#124;&#124; And he who does not give to their mouth, &#124;&#124; They have sanctified against him war:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 8:20–22; Amos 8:9–10; Isaiah 29:10"> “Therefore you have a night without vision, &#124;&#124; And you have darkness without divination, &#124;&#124; And the sun has gone in on the prophets, &#124;&#124; And the day has been black over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 13:4; Isaiah 44:25; 1 Samuel 28:6"> And the seers have been ashamed, &#124;&#124; And the diviners have been confounded, &#124;&#124; And all of them have covered their lip, &#124;&#124; For there is no answer, O God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 58:1; 1 Corinthians 2:12–13; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> And yet I have been full of power by the Spirit of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And of judgment, and of might, &#124;&#124; To declare to Jacob his transgression, &#124;&#124; And to Israel his sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:23; Psalms 58:1–2; Proverbs 17:15"> Now hear this, O heads of the house of Jacob, &#124;&#124; And you judges of the house of Israel, &#124;&#124; Who are making judgment abominable, &#124;&#124; And pervert all uprightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:9–12; Jeremiah 22:13–17; Matthew 27:25"> Building up Zion with blood, &#124;&#124; And Jerusalem with iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:13; Isaiah 1:23; Micah 3:5"> Her heads judge for a bribe, &#124;&#124; And her priests teach for hire, &#124;&#124; And her prophets divine for silver, &#124;&#124; And on YHWH they lean, saying, &#124;&#124; “Is not YHWH in our midst? Evil does not come in on us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:18; Psalms 79:1; Micah 4:1–2"> Therefore, for your sake, Zion is plowed a field, and Jerusalem is heaps, &#124;&#124; And the mountain of the house &#91;is&#93; for high places of a forest!</span>
