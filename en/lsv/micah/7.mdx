<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 28:4; Isaiah 24:13; Hosea 9:10"> My woe &#91;is&#93; to me, &#124;&#124; For I have been as gatherings of summer-fruit, &#124;&#124; As gleanings of harvest, &#124;&#124; There is no cluster to eat, &#124;&#124; The first-ripe fruit has my soul desired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 12:1; Isaiah 57:1; Isaiah 59:7"> The kind have perished out of the land, &#124;&#124; And upright among men—there are none, &#124;&#124; All of them lie in wait for blood, &#124;&#124; They each hunt his brother &#91;with&#93; a net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Proverbs 4:16–17; Amos 5:12"> On the evil &#91;are&#93; both hands to do &#91;it&#93; well, &#124;&#124; The prince is asking—also the judge—for repayment, &#124;&#124; And the great—he is speaking the mischief of his soul, &#124;&#124; And they wrap it up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 22:5; Ezekiel 2:6; Isaiah 10:3"> Their best one &#91;is&#93; as a brier, &#124;&#124; The upright one—than a thorn-hedge, &#124;&#124; The day of your watchmen—Your visitation—has come. Now is their perplexity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:4; Psalms 118:8–9; Matthew 10:16"> Do not believe in a friend, &#124;&#124; Do not trust in a leader, &#124;&#124; From her who is lying in your bosom keep the openings of your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 10:35–36; Luke 12:53; Matthew 10:21"> For a son is dishonoring a father, &#124;&#124; A daughter has stood against her mother, &#124;&#124; A daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law, &#124;&#124; The enemies of each &#91;are&#93; the men of his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; Psalms 37:7; 1 John 5:14–15"> And I—in YHWH I watch, &#124;&#124; I wait for the God of my salvation, &#124;&#124; My God hears me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:12; Isaiah 9:2; Psalms 27:1"> You do not rejoice over me, O my enemy, &#124;&#124; When I have fallen, I have risen, &#124;&#124; When I sit in darkness YHWH is a light to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 37:6; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Samuel 24:15"> I bear the indignation of YHWH, &#124;&#124; For I have sinned against Him, &#124;&#124; Until He pleads my cause, &#124;&#124; And has executed my judgment, &#124;&#124; He brings me forth to the light, &#124;&#124; I look on His righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 10:5; Jeremiah 50:33–34; Psalms 42:10"> And my enemy sees, &#124;&#124; And shame covers her, &#124;&#124; Who says to me, “Where &#91;is&#93; your God YHWH?” My eyes look on her, &#124;&#124; Now she is for a treading-place, &#124;&#124; As mire of the out-places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 9:11–15; Nehemiah 2:8; Nehemiah 2:17"> The day to build your walls! That day—removed is the limit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 19:23–25; Isaiah 11:16; Hosea 11:11"> That day—even to you it comes in, &#124;&#124; From Asshur and the cities of the fortress, &#124;&#124; And from the fortress even to the river, &#124;&#124; And from sea to sea, and mountain to mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10–11; Jeremiah 25:11; Micah 6:13"> And the land has been for a desolation, &#124;&#124; Because of its inhabitants, &#124;&#124; Because of the fruit of their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 23:1–4; Micah 5:4; Psalms 100:3"> Rule Your people with Your rod, &#124;&#124; The flock of Your inheritance, &#124;&#124; Dwelling alone &#91;in&#93; a forest in the midst of Carmel, &#124;&#124; They enjoy Bashan and Gilead as in days of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 78:12–72; Exodus 3:20; Psalms 68:22"> “According to the days of your coming forth out of the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; I show it wonderful things.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Isaiah 52:15; Job 29:9–10"> Nations see, and they are ashamed of all their might, &#124;&#124; They lay a hand on the mouth, their ears are deaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Psalms 72:9; Psalms 18:45"> They lick dust as a serpent, as fearful things of earth, &#124;&#124; They tremble from their enclosures, &#124;&#124; They are afraid of our God YHWH, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they are afraid of You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 44:22; Psalms 103:8–9"> Who &#91;is&#93; a God like You? Taking away iniquity, &#124;&#124; And passing by the transgression of the remnant of His inheritance, &#124;&#124; He has not retained His anger forever, &#124;&#124; Because He delights &#91;in&#93; kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 38:17; Romans 6:14; Isaiah 43:25"> He turns back, He pities us, &#124;&#124; He subdues our iniquities, &#124;&#124; And You cast all their sins into the depths of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–14; Luke 1:72–74; Deuteronomy 7:8"> You give truth to Jacob, kindness to Abraham, &#124;&#124; That You have sworn to our fathers, from the days of antiquity!</span>
