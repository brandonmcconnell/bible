<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 36:4; Isaiah 32:7; Psalms 7:14–16"> Woe &#91;to&#93; those devising iniquity, &#124;&#124; And working evil on their beds, &#124;&#124; In the morning light they do it, &#124;&#124; For their hand is—to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:8; Jeremiah 22:17; Amos 8:4"> And they have desired fields, &#124;&#124; And they have taken violently, &#124;&#124; And houses, and they have taken away, &#124;&#124; And have oppressed a man and his house, &#124;&#124; Even a man and his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:3; Amos 5:13; Isaiah 2:11–12"> Therefore, thus said YHWH: “Behold, I am devising against this family calamity, &#124;&#124; From which you do not remove your necks, &#124;&#124; Nor walk loftily, for it &#91;is&#93; a time of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:6; Jeremiah 9:10; Jeremiah 9:17–21"> In that day &#91;one&#93; takes up for you an allegory, &#124;&#124; And he has wailed a wailing of woe, &#124;&#124; He has said, We have been utterly spoiled, &#124;&#124; The portion of my people He changes, &#124;&#124; How He moves toward me! To the backslider our fields He apportions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:8; Joshua 18:4; Joshua 18:10"> Therefore, you have no caster of a line by lot &#124;&#124; In the assembly of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 2:12; Isaiah 30:10; Acts 5:40"> You do not prophesy—they prophesy, &#124;&#124; They do not prophesy to these, &#124;&#124; It does not remove shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 84:11; Psalms 15:2; Jeremiah 15:16"> &#91;Those&#93; named the house of Jacob: Has the Spirit of YHWH been shortened? Are these His doings? Do My words not benefit the people that are walking uprightly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 120:6–7; 2 Samuel 20:19; Isaiah 9:21"> And recently My people raise up as an enemy, &#124;&#124; You strip off the honorable ornament from the outer garment, &#124;&#124; From the confident passers by, &#124;&#124; You who are turning back from war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Ezekiel 39:21; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> The women of My people you cast out from its delightful house, &#124;&#124; From its sucklings you take away My honor for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:9; Psalms 106:38; 2 Kings 17:6"> Rise and go, for this &#91;is&#93; not the rest, &#124;&#124; Because of uncleanness it corrupts, &#124;&#124; And corruption is powerful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Isaiah 30:10–11; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> If one is going &#91;with&#93; the wind, &#124;&#124; And &#91;with&#93; falsehood has lied: I prophesy to you of wine, and of strong drink, &#124;&#124; He has been the prophet of this people!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 4:6–7; Micah 7:18; Hosea 1:11"> I surely gather you, O Jacob, all of you, &#124;&#124; I surely bring together the remnant of Israel, &#124;&#124; Together I set it as the flock of Bozrah, &#124;&#124; As a drove in the midst of its pasture, &#124;&#124; It makes a noise because of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 52:12; Zechariah 9:14–15; Isaiah 45:1–2"> The breaker has gone up before them, &#124;&#124; They have broken through, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they pass through the gate, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they go out through it, &#124;&#124; And their king passes on before them, &#124;&#124; And YHWH at their head!”</span>
