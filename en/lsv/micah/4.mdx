<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 2:1–4; Jeremiah 3:17; Acts 2:17"> And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; In the latter end of the days, &#124;&#124; The mountain of the house of YHWH &#124;&#124; Is established above the top of the mountains, &#124;&#124; And it has been lifted up above the hills, &#124;&#124; And flowed to it have peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 2:3; Jeremiah 31:6; Acts 1:8"> And gone have many nations and said, &#124;&#124; “Come and we go up to the mountain of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And to the house of the God of Jacob, &#124;&#124; And He teaches us of His ways, &#124;&#124; And we walk in His paths,” &#124;&#124; For a law goes forth from Zion, &#124;&#124; And a word of YHWH from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 2:4; Joel 3:9–16; Matthew 25:31–32"> And He has judged between many peoples, &#124;&#124; And given a decision to mighty nations far off, &#124;&#124; They have beaten their swords to plowshares, &#124;&#124; And their spears to pruning-hooks, &#124;&#124; Nation does not lift up sword to nation, &#124;&#124; Nor do they learn war anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 3:10; 1 Kings 4:25; Ezekiel 39:26"> And they have sat each under his vine, &#124;&#124; And under his fig tree, &#124;&#124; And there is none troubling, &#124;&#124; For the mouth of YHWH of Hosts has spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 10:12; Joshua 24:15; Colossians 3:17"> For all the peoples walk, &#124;&#124; Each in the name of its god—and we, &#124;&#124; We walk in the Name of our God YHWH, &#124;&#124; For all time and forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:19; Ezekiel 34:12–17; Isaiah 35:3–6"> “In that day,” a declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “I gather the halting one, &#124;&#124; And the driven away one I bring together, &#124;&#124; And she whom I have afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 7:14; Isaiah 24:23; Micah 2:12"> And I have set the halting for a remnant, &#124;&#124; And the far-off for a mighty nation, &#124;&#124; And YHWH has reigned over them in Mount Zion, &#124;&#124; From now on, and for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 1:26; Revelation 22:5; Zechariah 9:10"> And you, O Tower of Eder, &#124;&#124; Fort of the daughter of Zion, to you it comes, &#124;&#124; Indeed, the former rule has come in, &#124;&#124; The kingdom to the daughter of Jerusalem.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:19; Isaiah 3:1–7; Lamentations 4:20"> Now why do you shout aloud? Is there no king in you? Has your counselor perished, &#124;&#124; That pain as of a travailing woman has taken hold of you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Isaiah 52:9–12; 2 Kings 20:18"> Be pained, and bring forth, O daughter of Zion, &#124;&#124; As a travailing woman, &#124;&#124; For now you go forth from the city, &#124;&#124; And you have dwelt in the field, &#124;&#124; And you have gone to Babylon, &#124;&#124; There you are delivered, &#124;&#124; There YHWH redeems you from the hand of your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Obadiah 1:12; Isaiah 5:25–30; Micah 7:10"> And now, many nations have gathered against you, who are saying: “Let her be defiled, and our eyes look on Zion.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 55:8; Jeremiah 29:11; Romans 11:33–34"> They have not known the thoughts of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Nor have they understood His counsel, &#124;&#124; For He has gathered them as a sheaf &#91;into&#93; a threshing-floor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15–16; Daniel 2:44; Zechariah 9:13–15"> Arise, and thresh, O daughter of Zion, &#124;&#124; For I make your horn iron, &#124;&#124; And I make your hooves bronze, &#124;&#124; And you have beaten small many peoples, &#124;&#124; And I have devoted to YHWH their gain, &#124;&#124; And their wealth to the Lord of the whole earth!</span>
