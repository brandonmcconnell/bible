<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 23:4–5; Ezekiel 34:16; Isaiah 58:7"> “You do not see the ox of your brother or his sheep driven away, and have hidden yourself from them, you certainly turn them back to your brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; 1 Thessalonians 4:6"> and if your brother &#91;is&#93; not near to you, and you have not known him, then you have removed it to the midst of your house, and it has been with you until your brother seeks it, and you have given it back to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and so you do to his donkey, and so you do to his garment, and so you do to any lost thing of your brother’s, which is lost by him, and you have found it; you are not able to hide yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 6:1–2; Hebrews 12:12–13; Matthew 5:44"> You do not see the donkey of your brother, or his ox, falling in the way, and have hid yourself from them; you certainly raise &#91;them&#93; up for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:4–15; Deuteronomy 18:12"> Anything of a man is not on a woman, nor does a man put on the garment of a woman, for anyone doing these &#91;is&#93; an abomination &#91;to&#93; your God YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 22:28; Proverbs 12:10; Genesis 8:17"> When a bird’s nest comes before you in the way, in any tree, or on the earth, &#91;with&#93; brood or eggs, and the mother is sitting on the brood or on the eggs, you do not take the mother with the young ones;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Proverbs 22:4"> you certainly send the mother away, and take the young ones for yourself, so that it is well with you and you have prolonged days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:2; Isaiah 22:1; Exodus 21:28–36"> When you build a new house, then you have made a parapet for your roof, and you do not put blood on your house when one falls from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 19:19; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Matthew 9:16"> You do not sow your vineyard &#91;with&#93; two kinds &#91;of seed&#93;, lest the fullness of the seed which you sow and the increase of the vineyard be separated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–16"> You do not plow with an ox and with a donkey together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 19:19"> You do not put on mixed material, wool and linens together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; Numbers 15:37–41"> You make fringes for yourself on the four skirts of your covering with which you cover &#91;yourself&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 29:21; Genesis 29:31; Ephesians 5:28–29"> When a man takes a wife, and has gone in to her, and hated her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:19; Proverbs 18:21; Proverbs 18:8"> and laid evil deeds of words against her, and brought out an evil name against her, and said, I have taken this woman, and I draw near to her, and I have not found proofs of virginity in her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> then the father of the girl, and her mother, have taken and brought out the girl’s proofs of virginity to &#91;the&#93; elderly of the city in the gate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and the father of the girl has said to the elderly, I have given my daughter to this man for a wife, and he hates her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and behold, he has laid evil deeds of words, saying, I have not found proofs of virginity for your daughter—and these &#91;are&#93; the proofs of virginity of my daughter! And they have spread out the garment before &#91;the&#93; elderly of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 18:21"> And &#91;the&#93; elderly of that city have taken the man, and discipline him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:29; Matthew 19:8–9; Deuteronomy 24:1–4"> and fined him one hundred pieces of silver, and given &#91;them&#93; to the father of the girl, because he has brought out an evil name on a virgin of Israel; and she is to him for a wife—he is not able to send her away &#91;for&#93; all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And if this thing has been truth—proofs of virginity have not been found for the girl—</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:5; Genesis 34:7; Judges 20:6"> then they have brought out the girl to the opening of her father’s house, and the men of her city have stoned her with stones and she has died, for she has done folly in Israel, to go whoring &#91;in&#93; her father’s house; and you have put away evil out of your midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Ezekiel 23:45–47; John 8:4–5"> When a man is found lying with a woman married to a husband, then both of them have died—the man who is lying with the woman, and the woman; and you have put away evil out of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 1:18–19; Deuteronomy 20:7"> When there is a girl, a virgin, betrothed to a man, and a man has found her in a city and lain with her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:21–22; Deuteronomy 21:14; 1 Corinthians 5:13"> then you have brought them both out to the gate of that city, and stoned them with stones, and they have died: the girl, because that she has not cried in a city; and the man, because that he has humbled his neighbor’s wife; and you have put away evil out of your midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:14"> And if the man finds the girl who is betrothed in a field, and the man has laid hold on her, and lain with her, then the man who has lain with her has died alone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:22"> and you do not do anything to the girl—the girl has no deadly sin; for as a man rises against his neighbor and has murdered him, &#91;taking&#93; a life, so &#91;is&#93; this thing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:7"> for he found her in a field, &#91;and when&#93; she has cried—the girl who is betrothed—then she has no savior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 22:16–17"> When a man finds a girl, a virgin who is not betrothed, and has caught her, and lain with her, and they have been found,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:19; Deuteronomy 22:24; Deuteronomy 21:14"> then the man who is lying with her has given fifty pieces of silver to the father of the girl, and she is to him for a wife; because that he has humbled her, he is not able to send her away &#91;for&#93; all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:20; 1 Corinthians 5:1; Leviticus 18:8"> A man does not take his father’s wife, nor uncover his father’s skirt.”</span>
