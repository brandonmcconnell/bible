<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5; Deuteronomy 10:12; Luke 1:74–75"> “And you have loved your God YHWH, and kept His charge, and His statutes, and His judgments, and His commands, &#91;for&#93; all the days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2–5; Deuteronomy 29:10; Acts 26:22"> and you have known today—for it is not your sons who have not known and who have not seen the discipline of your God YHWH, His greatness, His strong hand, and His outstretched arm;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:19; Deuteronomy 4:34; Psalms 105:27–45"> and His signs and His doings which He has done in the midst of Egypt, to Pharaoh king of Egypt and to all his land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 106:11; Exodus 14:23–31; Exodus 15:19"> and that which He has done to the force of Egypt, to its horses, and to its chariot, when He has caused the waters of the Red Sea to flow against their faces in their pursuing after them, and YHWH destroys them, to this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 77:20; Psalms 106:12–48; Psalms 78:14–72"> and that which He has done to you in the wilderness until your coming to this place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 26:9–10; Psalms 106:17; Numbers 16:1–35"> and that which He has done to Dathan and to Abiram, sons of Eliab, sons of Reuben, when the earth has opened her mouth and swallows them, and their houses, and their tents, and all that lives which is at their feet, in the midst of all Israel—</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 145:4–6; Psalms 145:12; Psalms 150:2"> but &#91;it is&#93; your eyes which are seeing all the great work of YHWH which He has done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Joshua 1:6–7; Ephesians 3:16"> And you have kept all the command which I am commanding you today, so that you are strong, and have gone in, and possessed the land to where you are passing over to possess it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 6:2"> and so that you prolong days on the ground which YHWH has sworn to your fathers to give to them and to their seed—a land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 14:18"> For the land to where you are going in to possess it is not as the land of Egypt from where you have come out, where you sow your seed and have watered with your foot, as a garden of the green herb;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:7; Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Psalms 65:12–13"> but the land to where you are passing over to possess it &#91;is&#93; a land of hills and valleys—it drinks water of the rain of the heavens—</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Psalms 33:18; 1 Kings 9:3"> a land which your God YHWH is searching; the eyes of your God YHWH &#91;are&#93; continually on it, from the beginning of the year even to the latter end of the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:12; Deuteronomy 4:29; Deuteronomy 6:17"> And it has been, if you listen diligently to My commands which I am commanding you today, to love your God YHWH, and to serve Him with all your heart and with all your soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 5:7; Leviticus 26:4; Ezekiel 34:26"> then I have given the rain of your land in its season—autumn rain and spring rain—and you have gathered your grain, and your new wine, and your oil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 104:14; Deuteronomy 6:11; Joel 2:19"> and I have given herbs in your field for your livestock, and you have eaten and been satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 31:27; Hebrews 3:12; Hebrews 2:1"> Take heed to yourselves lest your heart be enticed, and you have turned aside, and served other gods, and bowed yourselves to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 8:35; Deuteronomy 6:15; 2 Chronicles 6:26"> and the anger of YHWH has burned against you, and He has restrained the heavens and there is no rain, and the ground does not give her increase, and you have perished quickly from off the good land which YHWH is giving to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Deuteronomy 6:6–9; Proverbs 3:1"> And you have placed these words of mine on your heart and on your soul, and have bound them for a sign on your hand, and they have been for frontlets between your eyes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Isaiah 38:19; Deuteronomy 4:9–10"> and you have taught them to your sons by speaking of them in your sitting in your house, and in your going in the way, and in your lying down, and in your rising up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:9"> and have written them on the doorposts of your house and on your gates,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 4:10; Proverbs 3:2; Proverbs 9:11"> so that your days are multiplied, and the days of your sons, on the ground which YHWH has sworn to your fathers to give to them, as the days of the heavens above the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:17; Deuteronomy 11:13; Deuteronomy 10:20"> For if you diligently keep all this command which I am commanding you to do—to love your God YHWH, to walk in all His ways, and to cleave to Him—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:38; Deuteronomy 9:1; Deuteronomy 7:1–2"> then YHWH has dispossessed all these nations from before you, and you have possessed nations greater and mightier than you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 14:9; Exodus 23:31; Genesis 15:18–21"> Every place on which the sole of your foot treads is yours: from the wilderness and Lebanon, from the river, the Euphrates River, even to the Western Sea, is your border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:24; Exodus 23:27; Joshua 1:5"> No man stations himself in your presence; your God YHWH puts your dread and your fear on the face of all the land on which you tread, as He has spoken to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:1; Deuteronomy 30:15–20; Galatians 3:10"> See, today I am setting before you a blessing and a reviling:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; Deuteronomy 28:1–14; Isaiah 3:10"> the blessing when you listen to the commands of your God YHWH which I am commanding you today;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Romans 2:8–9; Galatians 3:10"> and the reviling if you do not listen to the commands of your God YHWH, and have turned aside from the way which I am commanding you today, to go after other gods which you have not known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:12–26; Joshua 8:30–35"> And it has been, when your God YHWH brings you into the land to where you are going in to possess it, that you have given the blessing on Mount Gerizim and the reviling on Mount Ebal;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 12:6; Judges 7:1; Joshua 4:19"> are they not beyond the Jordan, behind the way of the going in of the sun, in the land of the Canaanite who is dwelling in the plain opposite Gilgal, near the oaks of Moreh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 1:11; Deuteronomy 9:1; Joshua 3:13–17"> For you are passing over the Jordan to go in to possess the land which your God YHWH is giving to you; and you have possessed it, and dwelt in it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; Deuteronomy 12:32; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> and observed to do all the statutes and the judgments which I am setting before you today.”</span>
