<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 21:4; Deuteronomy 1:40; Numbers 14:25"> “And we turn and journey into the wilderness, the way of the Red Sea, as YHWH has spoken to me, and we go around Mount Seir &#91;for&#93; many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And YHWH speaks to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:6; Deuteronomy 2:14; Deuteronomy 2:7"> For you go around this mountain long enough; turn for yourselves northward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 20:14–21; Exodus 15:15; Deuteronomy 23:7"> And the people command you, saying, You are passing over into the border of your brothers, sons of Esau, who are dwelling in Seir, and they are afraid of you; and you have been very watchful;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Joshua 24:4; Jeremiah 27:5"> you do not strive with them, for I do not give &#91;any&#93; of their land to you—even the treading of the sole of a foot; I have given Mount Seir to Esau for a possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Numbers 20:19; Romans 12:17"> You buy food from them with money, and have eaten; and you also buy water from them with money, and have drunk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Nehemiah 9:21; Deuteronomy 8:2–4"> For your God YHWH has blessed you in all the work of your hands; He has known your walking in this great wilderness these forty years; your God YHWH &#91;is&#93; with you; you have not lacked anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 9:26; Judges 11:18; 2 Kings 16:6"> And we pass by from our brothers, sons of Esau, who are dwelling in Seir, by the way of the plain, by Elath and by Ezion-Gaber, and we turn and pass over the way of the wilderness of Moab;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 19:36–37; Numbers 21:15; Numbers 21:28"> and YHWH says to me, Do not distress Moab, nor stir yourself up against them &#91;in&#93; battle, for I do not give &#91;any&#93; of their land to you &#91;for&#93; a possession; for I have given Ar to the sons of Lot &#91;for&#93; a possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 14:5; Deuteronomy 2:11; Numbers 13:22"> The Emim have formerly dwelt in it, a people great, and numerous, and tall as the Anakim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Numbers 13:28; Genesis 14:5"> they are reckoned &#91;as&#93; Rephaim, they also, as the Anakim; and the Moabites call them Emim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:22; Genesis 14:6; Deuteronomy 2:32–11"> And the Horim have formerly dwelt in Seir, but the sons of Esau dispossess them, and destroy them from before them, and dwell in their stead, as Israel has done to the land of his possession which YHWH has given to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 21:12; Numbers 13:23"> Now rise and pass over the Brook of Zered for yourselves; and we pass over the Brook of Zered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; Numbers 14:28–35; Deuteronomy 1:34–35"> And the days which we have walked from Kadesh-Barnea until we have passed over the Brook of Zered &#91;are&#93; thirty-eight years, until the consumption of all the generation of the men of battle from the midst of the camp, as YHWH has sworn to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 106:26; Isaiah 66:14; Psalms 32:4"> and indeed the hand of YHWH has been against them, to destroy them from the midst of the camp until they are consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And it comes to pass, when all the men of battle have finished dying from the midst of the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> that YHWH speaks to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 21:23; Isaiah 15:1; Numbers 21:15"> Today you are passing over the border of Moab, even Ar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:9; Judges 11:13–27; 2 Chronicles 20:10"> and &#91;when&#93; you have come near the sons of Ammon, you do not distress them, nor stir yourself up against them, for I do not give &#91;any&#93; of the land of the sons of Ammon to you &#91;for&#93; a possession; for I have given it to the sons of Lot &#91;for&#93; a possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 14:5"> It is reckoned &#91;as&#93; a land of Rephaim, even it; Rephaim formerly dwelt in it, and the Ammonites call them Zamzummim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:7–8; Deuteronomy 1:28; Deuteronomy 2:22"> a people great, and numerous, and tall as the Anakim, and YHWH destroys them before them, and they dispossess them, and dwell in their stead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Genesis 36:20–30; Deuteronomy 2:12"> as He has done for the sons of Esau who are dwelling in Seir, when He destroyed the Horim from before them, and they dispossess them, and dwell in their stead to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 10:14; Joshua 13:3; Amos 9:7"> As for the Avim who are dwelling in villages as far as Gaza, the Caphtorim—who are coming out from Caphtor—have destroyed them, and dwell in their stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5; Daniel 4:17; Judges 11:18–21"> Rise, journey, and pass over the Brook of Arnon; see, I have given into your hand Sihon king of Heshbon, the Amorite, and his land; begin to possess &#91;it&#93;, and stir yourself up against him &#91;in&#93; battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:25; Exodus 15:14–16; Deuteronomy 28:10"> This &#91;is&#93; the day I begin to put your dread and your fear on the face of the peoples under the whole heavens, who hear your fame, and have trembled and been pained because of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 10:5–6; Luke 10:10–12; Joshua 13:18"> And I send messengers from the wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon king of Heshbon, &#91;with&#93; words of peace, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 11:19; Deuteronomy 2:6; Numbers 21:21–23"> Let me pass over through your land; in the way—in the way I go, I do not turn aside &#91;to the&#93; right or left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 20:19"> You sell me food for money, and I have eaten; and you give to me water for money, and I have drunk; only, let me pass over on my feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 20:18; Judges 11:17–18; Deuteronomy 23:3–4"> as the sons of Esau who are dwelling in Seir and the Moabites who are dwelling in Ar have done for me, until I pass over the Jordan to the land which our God YHWH is giving to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 21:23; Exodus 4:21; Joshua 11:19–20"> And Sihon king of Heshbon has not been willing to let us pass over by him, for your God YHWH has hardened his spirit and strengthened his heart, so as to give him into your hand as at this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:8; Deuteronomy 2:24"> And YHWH says to me, See, I have begun to give Sihon and his land before your faces; begin to possess—to possess his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 21:23–30; Judges 11:20–23; Psalms 136:19"> And Sihon comes out to meet us, he and all his people, to the battle at Jahaz;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Joshua 21:44; Deuteronomy 3:2–3"> and our God YHWH gives him before our faces, and we strike him, and his sons, and all his people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; 1 Samuel 15:3; Deuteronomy 3:6"> and we capture all his cities at that time, and devote the whole city—men, and the women, and the infants; we have not left a remnant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Joshua 8:27; Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:9–11"> only, we have spoiled the livestock for ourselves, and the spoil of the cities which we have captured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 13:9; Deuteronomy 4:48; Deuteronomy 3:12"> From Aroer, which &#91;is&#93; by the edge of the Brook of Arnon, and the city which &#91;is&#93; by the brook, even to Gilead, there has not been a city which &#91;is&#93; too high for us; our God YHWH has given the whole before our faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 21:24; Deuteronomy 3:16; Genesis 32:22"> Only, you have not drawn near to the land of the sons of Ammon—any part of the Brook of Jabbok, and cities of the hill-country, and anything which our God YHWH has &#91;not&#93; commanded.”</span>
