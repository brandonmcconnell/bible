<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 21:33–35; Deuteronomy 1:4; Deuteronomy 29:7"> “And we turn and go up the way to Bashan, and Og king of Bashan comes out to meet us, he and all his people, to the battle &#91;at&#93; Edrei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 2:10; 2 Chronicles 20:17; Numbers 21:23–25"> And YHWH says to me, Do not fear him, for I have given him, and all his people, and his land into your hand, and you have done to him as you have done to Sihon king of the Amorite who is dwelling in Heshbon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 21:35; Joshua 13:12; Joshua 13:30"> And our God YHWH indeed gives Og king of Bashan and all his people into our hands, and we strike him until there has been no remnant left to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 4:13; Joshua 12:4; Joshua 13:30–31"> and we capture all his cities at that time; there has not been a city which we have not taken from them—sixty cities, all the region of Argob, the kingdom of Og in Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Numbers 13:28; Hebrews 11:30"> All these &#91;are&#93; cities fortified with high walls, double gates, and bar, apart from very many cities of the open place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:34; Psalms 135:10–12; Deuteronomy 2:24"> and we devote them, as we have done to Sihon king of Heshbon, devoting &#91;the&#93; men, the women, and the infants of every city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:35; Joshua 8:27; Joshua 11:11–14"> and all the livestock, and the spoil of the cities, we have spoiled for ourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:9–12; Joshua 12:2–6; Numbers 32:33–42"> And at that time we take the land out of the hand of the two kings of the Amorite, which is beyond the Jordan, from the Brook of Arnon to Mount Hermon</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:23; Psalms 29:6; Ezekiel 27:5"> (Sidonians call Hermon Sirion, and the Amorites call it Senir),</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 13:31; Joshua 13:11–12; Deuteronomy 4:49"> all the cities of the plain, and all Gilead, and all Bashan, as far as Salchah and Edrei, cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 14:5; Jeremiah 49:2; 2 Samuel 12:26"> For only Og king of Bashan had been left of the remnant of the Rephaim; behold, his bedstead &#91;is&#93; a bedstead of iron. Is it not in Rabbath of the sons of Ammon? Its length &#91;is&#93; nine cubits and its breadth &#91;is&#93; four cubits, by the cubit of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36; Numbers 32:32–38; Joshua 13:8–28"> And this land, &#91;which&#93; we had possessed at that time, from Aroer, which &#91;is&#93; by the Brook of Arnon, and the half of Mount Gilead and its cities, I have given to the Reubenite and to the Gadite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 32:39–42; Joshua 13:29–32; 1 Chronicles 5:23–26"> and the rest of Gilead and all Bashan, the kingdom of Og, I have given to the half tribe of Manasseh; all the region of Argob, with all Bashan—it is called the land of &#91;the&#93; Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 32:41; Joshua 13:13; 2 Samuel 3:3"> Jair son of Manasseh has taken all the region of Argob, as far as the border of Geshuri and Maachathi (&#91;namely&#93; Bashan), and calls them by his own name, Havoth-Jair, to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 32:39–40; Joshua 17:1–3; Joshua 22:7"> And I have given Gilead to Machir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 21:24; Deuteronomy 2:37; 2 Samuel 24:5"> And to the Reubenite and to the Gadite I have given from Gilead even to the Brook of Arnon, the middle of the valley and the border, even to the Brook of Jabbok, the border of the sons of Ammon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 12:3; Genesis 14:3; Joshua 3:16"> and the plain, and the Jordan, and the border, from Chinnereth even to the Sea of the Plain (the Salt Sea), under the Springs of Pisgah, at the &#91;sun&#93;-rising.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–24; Joshua 4:12–13; Joshua 1:12–15"> And at that time I command you, saying, Your God YHWH has given this land to you to possess it; you pass over armed before your brothers the sons of Israel, all the sons of might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Only your wives, and your infants, and your livestock—I have known that you have much livestock—dwell in your cities which I have given to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 22:4; Joshua 22:8"> until YHWH gives rest to your brothers like yourselves, and they also have possessed the land which your God YHWH is giving to them beyond the Jordan; then you have each turned back to his possession which I have given to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 27:18–23; 2 Corinthians 12:10; Psalms 9:10"> And I have commanded Joshua at that time, saying, Your eyes are seeing all that which your God YHWH has done to these two kings—so YHWH does to all the kingdoms to where you are passing over;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Deuteronomy 20:4; Deuteronomy 1:30"> do not fear them, for your God YHWH—He is fighting for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:8–9"> And to YHWH I beg for grace at that time, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Deuteronomy 11:2; Psalms 71:19"> Lord YHWH, You have begun to show Your servant Your greatness and Your strong hand; for who &#91;is&#93; a god in the heavens or in earth who does according to Your works and according to Your might?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:21–22; Ezekiel 20:6; Exodus 3:8"> Please let me pass over and see the good land which &#91;is&#93; beyond the Jordan, this good hill-country, and Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Deuteronomy 31:2; 1 Chronicles 22:7–9"> And YHWH shows Himself angry with me for your sake, and has not listened to me, and YHWH says to me, Enough of you; do not add to speak to Me about this thing anymore;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 27:12; Deuteronomy 34:1–4; Genesis 13:14–15"> go up &#91;to&#93; the top of Pisgah, and lift up your eyes westward, and northward, and southward, and eastward, and see with your eyes, for you do not pass over this Jordan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:3; Deuteronomy 31:7; Deuteronomy 1:38"> and charge Joshua, and strengthen him, and harden him, for he passes over before this people, and he causes them to inherit the land which you see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:6; Deuteronomy 4:46; Numbers 25:3"> And we dwell in a valley opposite Beth-Peor.”</span>
