<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:10; Exodus 23:10–11; Leviticus 25:2–4"> “At the end of seven years you make a release,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 7:42; James 2:13; Luke 6:34–38"> and this &#91;is&#93; the matter of the release: every owner of a loan &#91;is&#93; to release his hand which he lifts up against his neighbor, he does not exact of his neighbor and of his brother, but has proclaimed a release to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:20; Matthew 17:25–26; 1 Corinthians 6:6–7"> of the stranger you may exact, but that which is yours with your brother your hand releases,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 58:10–11; Proverbs 28:27; Proverbs 11:24–25"> except there is an end &#91;when&#93; no poor &#91;are&#93; with you; for YHWH greatly blesses you in the land which your God YHWH is giving to you &#91;for&#93; an inheritance to possess it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:1–15; Isaiah 1:19–20; Joshua 1:7"> only if you listen diligently to the voice of your God YHWH, to observe to do all this command which I am commanding you today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:12–13; Luke 6:35; Nehemiah 9:27"> For your God YHWH has blessed you as He has spoken to you; and you have lent &#91;to&#93; many nations, but you have not borrowed; and you have ruled over many nations, but they do not rule over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 3:16–17; Deuteronomy 15:9; Proverbs 21:13"> When there is with you any poor of one of your brothers, in one of your cities, in your land which your God YHWH is giving to you, you do not harden your heart, nor shut your hand from your needy brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 6:34; Matthew 5:42"> for you certainly open your hand to him, and certainly lend him sufficiency for his lack which he lacks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:15; Matthew 20:15; Jeremiah 17:10"> Take heed to yourself lest there be a worthless word in your heart, saying, The seventh year &#91;is&#93; near, the year of release; and your eye is evil against your needy brother, and you do not give to him, and he has called to YHWH concerning you, and it has been sin in you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 22:9; Hebrews 13:16; Isaiah 58:10"> you certainly give to him, and your heart is not sad in your giving to him, for because of this thing your God YHWH blesses you in all your works and in every putting forth of your hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 14:7; Matthew 26:11; John 12:8"> because the poor does not cease out of the land, therefore I am commanding you, saying, You certainly open your hand to your brother, to your poor and to your needy one in your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:14; Leviticus 25:39–41; Exodus 21:2–6"> When your brother is sold to you, a Hebrew or a Hebrewess, and he has served you &#91;for&#93; six years, then in the seventh year you send him away free from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 22:13; Proverbs 3:27–28"> And when you send him away free from you, you do not send him away empty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:2; Proverbs 10:22; Psalms 68:10"> you certainly adorn him from your flock, and from your threshing-floor, and from your wine-vat; you give to him &#91;from&#93; that which your God YHWH has blessed you &#91;with&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:12; Matthew 18:32–33; Deuteronomy 5:14–15"> and you have remembered that you have been a servant in the land of Egypt, and your God YHWH ransoms you; therefore I am commanding you this thing today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 21:5–6; Psalms 40:8; Psalms 40:6"> And it has been, when he says to you, I do not go out from you, because he has loved you and your house, because &#91;it is&#93; good for him with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:22; Leviticus 25:39–42"> then you have taken the awl, and have put &#91;it&#93; through his ear, and through the door, and he has been a perpetual servant to you; and you also do so to your handmaid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 17:7–8; Isaiah 16:14; Isaiah 21:16"> It is not hard in your eyes in your sending him away free from you; for &#91;his worth has been&#93; double the hire of a hired worker &#91;when&#93; he has served you six years; and your God YHWH has blessed you in all that you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12; Leviticus 27:26"> Every firstling that is born in your herd and in your flock—you sanctify the male to your God YHWH; you do not work with the firstling of your ox, nor shear the firstling of your flock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:17; Deuteronomy 14:23; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> you eat it before your God YHWH year by year, in the place which YHWH chooses, you and your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:1; Malachi 1:7–8; Leviticus 22:19–25"> And when there is a blemish in it, &#91;or it is&#93; lame or blind, &#91;or has&#93; any evil blemish, you do not sacrifice it to your God YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:21–22; Deuteronomy 12:15–16"> you eat it within your gates, the unclean and the clean alike, as the roe and as the deer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:16; Leviticus 7:26; Deuteronomy 12:23"> Only, you do not eat its blood—you pour it on the earth as water.”</span>
