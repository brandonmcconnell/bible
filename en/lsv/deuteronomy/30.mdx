<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:15; 1 Kings 8:47–48; Deuteronomy 30:19"> “And it has been, when all these things come on you, the blessing and the reviling, which I have set before you, and you have brought &#91;them&#93; back to your heart among all the nations to where your God YHWH has driven you away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:12–13; Nehemiah 1:9; Deuteronomy 4:28–31"> and have turned back to your God YHWH and listened to His voice, according to all that I am commanding you today, you and your sons, with all your heart and with all your soul—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14; Ezekiel 36:24; Psalms 147:2"> then your God YHWH has turned back &#91;to&#93; your captivity and pitied you, indeed, He has turned back and gathered you out of all the peoples to where your God YHWH has scattered you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:9; Ezekiel 39:25–29; Deuteronomy 28:64"> If your outcast is in the extremity of the heavens, your God YHWH gathers you from there and He takes you from there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14"> and your God YHWH has brought you into the land which your fathers have possessed, and you have inherited it, and He has done you good, and multiplied you above your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:16; Ezekiel 36:26–27; Jeremiah 32:39"> And your God YHWH has circumcised your heart and the heart of your seed, to love your God YHWH with all your heart and with all your soul, for the sake of your life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:15; Lamentations 4:21–22; Jeremiah 51:24–26"> and your God YHWH has put all this oath on your enemies and on those hating you, who have pursued you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 2:16; Ezekiel 36:27; Ezekiel 11:19–20"> And you turn back, and have listened to the voice of YHWH, and have done all His commands which I am commanding you today;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:11–14; Jeremiah 32:41; Jeremiah 33:9"> and your God YHWH has made you abundant in every work of your hand, in the fruit of your body, and in the fruit of your livestock, and in the fruit of your ground, for good; for YHWH turns back to rejoice over you for good, as He rejoiced over your fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40–41; Deuteronomy 4:29; Nehemiah 1:9"> for you listen to the voice of your God YHWH, to keep His commands and His statutes which are written in the scroll of this law, for you turn back to your God YHWH with all your heart and with all your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:19; Psalms 147:19–20; Romans 16:25–26"> For this command which I am commanding you today, it is not too wonderful for you, nor &#91;is&#93; it far off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 10:6–8; Proverbs 30:4; John 3:13"> It is not in the heavens, saying, Who goes up into the heavens for us, and takes it for us, and causes us to hear it, that we may do it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 10:33; Matthew 12:42; Proverbs 2:1–5"> And it &#91;is&#93; not beyond the sea, saying, Who passes over beyond the sea for us, and takes it for us, and causes us to hear it, that we may do it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 10:8–10; Jeremiah 12:2; Matthew 7:21"> For the word is very near to you, in your mouth, and in your heart—to do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:26; Deuteronomy 30:19; 1 John 5:11–12"> See, today I have set before you life and good, and death and evil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:6; Matthew 22:37–38; 1 John 5:2–3"> in that today I am commanding you to love your God YHWH, to walk in His ways, and to keep His commands, and His statutes, and His judgments; and you have lived and multiplied, and your God YHWH has blessed you in the land to where you are going in to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:4; Hebrews 3:12; Proverbs 1:32"> And if your heart turns, and you do not listen, and have been drawn away, and have bowed yourself to other gods and served them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:19–20; Isaiah 63:17–18; Deuteronomy 4:26"> I have declared to you this day that you certainly perish, you do not prolong days on the ground which you are passing over the Jordan to go in there to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:15; Deuteronomy 11:26; Psalms 119:30"> I have caused the heavens and the earth to testify against you today—I have set before you life and death, the blessing and the reviling, and you have fixed on life, so that you live, you and your seed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Deuteronomy 10:20; John 17:3"> to love your God YHWH, to listen to His voice, and to cleave to Him (for He &#91;is&#93; your life and the length of your days), to dwell on the ground which YHWH has sworn to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give to them.”</span>
