<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Romans 9:8; Galatians 3:26"> “You &#91;are&#93; sons of your God YHWH; you do not cut yourselves, nor make baldness between your eyes for the dead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Leviticus 20:26; Exodus 19:5–6"> for you &#91;are&#93; a holy people to your God YHWH, and YHWH has fixed on you to be to Him for a people, a peculiar treasure, out of all the peoples who &#91;are&#93; on the face of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:14; Romans 14:14; Leviticus 20:25"> You do not eat any abomination;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 11:2–45; Acts 10:14; 1 Kings 4:23"> this &#91;is&#93; the beast which you eat: ox, lamb of the sheep, or kid of the goats,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> deer, and roe, and fallow deer, and wild goat, and ibex, and antelope, and zemer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 18:1; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Psalms 1:1–2"> and every beast dividing the hoof, and cleaving the cleft into two hooves, bringing up the cud, among the beasts—you eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:22–23; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 3:5"> Only, this you do not eat, of those bringing up the cud, and of those dividing the cloven hoof: the camel, and the hare, and the hyrax, for they are bringing up the cud, but the hoof has not divided—they &#91;are&#93; unclean to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 11:26–27; Isaiah 66:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> and the sow, for it is dividing the hoof, and not &#91;bringing&#93; up the cud—it &#91;is&#93; unclean to you; you do not eat of their flesh, and you do not come against their carcass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 11:9–12"> This you eat of all which &#91;are&#93; in the waters: anything that has fins and scales you eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and anything which does not have fins and scales you do not eat—it &#91;is&#93; unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Any clean bird you eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 11:13–19"> and these &#91;are&#93; they of which you do not eat: the eagle, and the bearded vulture, and the osprey,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and the kite, and the falcon, and the vulture after its kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and every raven after its kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 30:29"> and the ostrich, and the nightjar &#91;&#91;or male ostrich&#93;&#93;, and the seagull, and the hawk after its kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> the little owl, and the great owl, and the waterhen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and the pelican, and the Egyptian vulture, and the cormorant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and the stork, and the heron after its kind, and the hoopoe, and the bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Leviticus 11:20–23"> And every teeming thing which is flying—it &#91;is&#93; unclean to you; they are not eaten;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> any clean bird you eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Exodus 34:26; Leviticus 22:8"> You do not eat of any carcass; you give it to the sojourner who &#91;is&#93; within your gates, and he has eaten it; or sell &#91;it&#93; to a stranger; for you &#91;are&#93; a holy people to your God YHWH. You do not boil a kid in its mother’s milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:37; Deuteronomy 12:17; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> You certainly tithe all the increase of your seed which the field is bringing forth year by year;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5–7; Deuteronomy 4:10; Deuteronomy 12:17–18"> and you have eaten before your God YHWH, in the place where He chooses to cause His Name to dwell, the tithe of your grain, of your new wine, and of your oil, and the firstlings of your herd, and of your flock, so that you learn to fear your God YHWH &#91;for&#93; all the days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:21; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 11:24"> And when the way is too much for you, that you are not able to carry it—when the place is too far off from you which your God YHWH chooses to put His Name there, when your God YHWH blesses you—</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> then you have given &#91;it&#93; in money, and have bound up the money in your hand, and gone to the place on which your God YHWH fixes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Deuteronomy 12:7; Matthew 21:12"> and you have given the money for anything which your soul desires, for oxen, and for sheep, and for wine, and for strong drink, and for anything which your soul asks for, and you have eaten before your God YHWH there, and you have rejoiced, you and your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 18:20; Deuteronomy 14:29; Deuteronomy 12:12"> As for the Levite who &#91;is&#93; within your gates, you do not forsake him, for he has no portion and inheritance with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:22; Amos 4:4; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> At the end of three years you bring out all the tithe of your increase in that year, and have placed &#91;it&#93; within your gates;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 14:27; Proverbs 19:17"> and the Levite has come in—for he has no part and inheritance with you—and the sojourner, and the fatherless, and the widow, who &#91;are&#93; within your gates, and they have eaten, and been satisfied, so that your God YHWH blesses you in all the work of your hand which you do.”</span>
