<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 18:8–9; Deuteronomy 10:9; 1 Corinthians 9:13–14"> “There is not for the priests, the Levites—all the tribe of Levi—a portion and inheritance with Israel; they eat fire-offerings of YHWH, even His inheritance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 1:5–6; Psalms 84:11; 1 Peter 2:5"> and he has no inheritance in the midst of his brothers; YHWH Himself &#91;is&#93; his inheritance, as He has spoken to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30–34; Deuteronomy 12:27"> And this is the priest’s right from the people, from those sacrificing a sacrifice, whether ox or sheep, he has even given to the priest the leg, and the two cheeks, and the stomach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44–47; Numbers 18:12–24; 2 Chronicles 31:4–10"> you give to him the first of your grain, of your new wine, and of your oil, and the first of the fleece of your flock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Numbers 3:10; Exodus 28:1–14"> for your God YHWH has fixed on him, out of all your tribes, to stand to serve in the Name of YHWH—him and his sons continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 35:2–3; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 16:2"> And when the Levite comes from one of your cities, out of all Israel where he has sojourned, and has come with all the desire of his soul to the place which YHWH chooses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:2–4"> then he has ministered in the Name of his God YHWH like all his brothers, the Levites, who are standing there before YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44; Nehemiah 12:47; 2 Chronicles 31:4"> They eat portion like portion, apart from his sold things from the fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:29–31; Leviticus 18:26–27; Leviticus 18:30"> When you are coming into the land which your God YHWH is giving to you, you do not learn to do according to the abominations of those nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Exodus 22:18; Leviticus 19:26"> There is not found in you one causing his son and his daughter to pass over into fire, a user of divinations, an observer of clouds, and an enchanter, and a sorcerer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; 1 Samuel 28:11–14"> and a charmer, and one inquiring from a familiar spirit, and a wizard, and one seeking to the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:24; Deuteronomy 9:4; Leviticus 18:27"> For everyone doing these &#91;is&#93; an abomination &#91;to&#93; YHWH, and because of these abominations your God YHWH is dispossessing them from your presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:48; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 6:9"> You must be perfect with your God YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 14:16; Deuteronomy 18:10; Genesis 20:6"> for these nations whom you are possessing listen to observers of clouds and to diviners; but you—your God YHWH has not permitted you so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Acts 7:37; John 1:45"> Your God YHWH raises up for you a Prophet like me out of your midst, out of your brothers—you must listen to Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Deuteronomy 5:23–28; Deuteronomy 9:10"> according to all that you asked from your God YHWH in Horeb, in the day of the assembly, saying, Do not let me add to hear the voice of my God YHWH, and do not let me see this great fire anymore, and I do not die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:28"> and YHWH says to me, They have done well &#91;in&#93; what they have spoken;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15; John 8:28; John 12:49–50"> I raise up for them a Prophet like you out of the midst of their brothers, and I have given My words in His mouth, and He has spoken to them all that which I command Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Hebrews 2:3; Hebrews 10:26"> and it has been &#91;that&#93; the man who does not listen to My words which He speaks in My Name, I require &#91;it&#93; of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:1–5; Jeremiah 27:15; Jeremiah 23:13–15"> Only, the prophet who presumes to speak a word in My Name, which I have not commanded him to speak, and who speaks in the name of other gods, indeed, that prophet has died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:1–3; 1 Thessalonians 5:24; Revelation 2:2"> And when you say in your heart, How do we know the word which YHWH has not spoken?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; 2 Kings 20:1; Jeremiah 28:1–17"> That which the prophet speaks in the Name of YHWH, and the thing is not, and does not come—it &#91;is&#93; the word which YHWH has not spoken; the prophet has spoken it in presumption; you are not afraid of him.”</span>
