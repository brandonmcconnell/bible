<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 14:6; Psalms 90:1; Genesis 27:4"> And this &#91;is&#93; the blessing &#91;with&#93; which Moses the man of God blessed the sons of Israel before his death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jude 1:14; Habakkuk 3:3; Revelation 5:11"> and he says: “YHWH has come from Sinai, &#124;&#124; And has risen from Seir for them; He has shone from Mount Paran, &#124;&#124; And has come &#91;with&#93; myriads of holy ones; At His right hand &#91;came&#93; a fiery law &#91;&#91;or a flaming fire&#93;&#93; for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 10:39; Malachi 1:2; Deuteronomy 7:6–8"> Indeed, He &#91;is&#93; loving the peoples; All His holy ones &#91;are&#93; in Your hand, &#124;&#124; And they sat down at Your foot, &#124;&#124; &#91;Each&#93; lifts up Your words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 1:17; Psalms 119:111; John 7:19"> Moses has commanded a law for us, &#124;&#124; A possession of the assembly of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Judges 17:6; Numbers 23:21"> And He is King in Yeshurun, &#124;&#124; In the heads of the people gathering together, &#124;&#124; The tribes of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:8; Numbers 32:31–32; Joshua 22:1–9"> Let Reuben live, and not die, &#124;&#124; And let his men be an &#91;incalculable&#93; number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 49:8–12; Psalms 20:2; 2 Samuel 7:9–12"> And this &#91;is&#93; for Judah, and he says: Hear, O YHWH, the voice of Judah, &#124;&#124; And You bring him in to his people; His hand has striven for him, &#124;&#124; And You are a help from his adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Exodus 28:30; Numbers 20:13"> And of Levi he said: Your Perfections and your Lights &#91;are&#93; for your pious one, &#124;&#124; Whom You have tried in Massah, &#124;&#124; You strive with him at the waters of Meribah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Exodus 32:25–29; Matthew 12:48"> Who is saying of his father and his mother, &#124;&#124; I have not seen him; And he has not discerned his brothers, &#124;&#124; And he has not known his sons; For they have observed Your saying, &#124;&#124; And they keep Your covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:11; Psalms 51:19; Deuteronomy 17:9–11"> They teach Your judgments to Jacob, &#124;&#124; And Your law to Israel; They put incense in Your nose, &#124;&#124; And whole burnt-offering on Your altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:23; Ezekiel 43:27; Ezekiel 20:40–41"> Bless, O YHWH, his strength, &#124;&#124; And accept the work of his hands, &#124;&#124; Strike the loins of his withstanders, &#124;&#124; And of those hating him—that they do not rise!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Psalms 91:4; Matthew 23:37"> Of Benjamin he said: The beloved of YHWH dwells confidently by Him, &#124;&#124; Covering him over every day; Indeed, he dwells between His shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 49:22–26; Micah 5:7; Isaiah 18:4"> And of Joseph he said: His land &#91;is&#93; blessed &#91;by&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; By a precious thing of the heavens, &#124;&#124; By dew, and by the deep crouching beneath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:19; Matthew 5:45; Psalms 8:3"> And by a precious thing—fruits of the sun, &#124;&#124; And by a precious thing—cast forth by the months,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:6; Genesis 49:26; James 5:7"> And by a chief thing—of the ancient mountains, &#124;&#124; And by a precious thing—of the continuous heights,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Psalms 24:1; Exodus 3:2–4"> And by a precious thing—of earth and its fullness, &#124;&#124; And the good pleasure of Him who is dwelling in the bush; Let it come for the head of Joseph, &#124;&#124; And for the crown of him &#91;who is&#93; separate from his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 22:11; Numbers 23:22; Psalms 44:5"> His splendor &#91;is&#93; a firstling of his ox, &#124;&#124; And his horns &#91;are&#93; horns of a wild ox; With them he pushes the peoples &#124;&#124; Altogether to the ends of the earth; And they &#91;are&#93; the myriads of Ephraim, &#124;&#124; And they &#91;are&#93; the thousands of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:13–15; Joshua 19:11; Judges 5:14"> And of Zebulun he said: Rejoice, O Zebulun, in your going out, &#124;&#124; And, O Issachar, in your tents;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 60:5; Isaiah 2:3; Psalms 4:5"> They call peoples &#91;to&#93; the mountain, &#124;&#124; There they sacrifice righteous sacrifices; For they suck up the abundance of the seas, &#124;&#124; And hidden things hidden in the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Genesis 9:26–27; 1 Chronicles 5:18–21"> And of Gad he said: Blessed is he who is enlarging Gad, &#124;&#124; He dwells as a lioness, &#124;&#124; And has torn the arm—also the crown!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 32:16–42; Judges 5:11; Judges 5:2"> And he provides the first part for himself, &#124;&#124; For there the portion of the lawgiver is covered, &#124;&#124; And he comes &#91;with&#93; the heads of the people; He has done the righteousness of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And His judgments with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 18:27; Joshua 19:47; Judges 15:8"> And of Dan he said: Dan &#91;is&#93; a lion’s whelp; He leaps from Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 49:21; Psalms 36:8; Matthew 4:16"> And of Naphtali he said: O Naphtali, satisfied with pleasure, &#124;&#124; And full of the blessing of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Possess &#91;the&#93; west and &#91;the&#93; south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:20; Job 29:6; Romans 14:18"> And of Asher he said: Asher &#91;is&#93; blessed with sons, &#124;&#124; Let him be accepted by his brothers, &#124;&#124; And dipping his foot in oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 138:3; Philippians 4:13"> Iron and bronze &#91;are&#93; your shoes, &#124;&#124; And as your days—your strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Isaiah 43:11–13; Jeremiah 10:6"> &#91;There is&#93; none like the God of Yeshurun, &#124;&#124; Riding the heavens to your help, &#124;&#124; And in His excellence the skies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Psalms 90:1–2; Psalms 46:1"> The eternal God &#91;is&#93; a habitation, &#124;&#124; And beneath &#91;are&#93; continuous arms. And He casts out the enemy from your presence and says, Destroy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:9; Genesis 27:28; Exodus 33:16"> And Israel dwells &#91;in&#93; confidence alone; The eye of Jacob &#91;is&#93; to a land of grain and wine; Also His heavens drop down dew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 45:17; Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 115:9–11"> O your blessedness, O Israel! Who is like you? A people saved by YHWH, &#124;&#124; The shield of your help, &#124;&#124; And He who &#91;is&#93; the sword of your excellence! And your enemies are subdued for you, &#124;&#124; And you tread on their high places.”</span>
