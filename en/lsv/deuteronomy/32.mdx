<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Deuteronomy 4:26; Jeremiah 22:29"> “Give ear, O heavens, and I speak; And you hear, O earth, sayings of my mouth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 55:10–11; Psalms 72:6; Micah 5:7"> My doctrine drops as rain; My sayings flow as dew; As storms on the tender grass, &#124;&#124; And as showers on the herb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:6; Psalms 150:2; Ephesians 1:19"> For I proclaim the Name of YHWH; Ascribe greatness to our God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 18:30–31; Psalms 92:15; Isaiah 26:4"> The Rock—His work &#91;is&#93; perfect, &#124;&#124; For all His ways &#91;are&#93; just; God of steadfastness, and without iniquity; Righteous and upright &#91;is&#93; He.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; Philippians 2:15; Matthew 17:17"> It has done corruptly to Him; Their blemish is not of His sons, &#124;&#124; A generation perverse and crooked!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:16; 1 John 3:1; Isaiah 44:2"> Do you act thus to YHWH, &#124;&#124; O people foolish and not wise? Is He not your Father—He who bought you? He made you, and establishes you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 78:3–4; Job 8:8–10; Psalms 44:1"> Remember days of old—Understand the years of many generations—Ask your father, and he tells you; Your elderly, and they say to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 17:26; Genesis 11:8–9; Daniel 4:17"> In the Most High causing nations to inherit, &#124;&#124; In His separating sons of Adam—He sets up the borders of the peoples &#124;&#124; By the number of the sons of God &#91;&#91;or sons of Israel&#93;&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; 1 Peter 2:9–10; Exodus 19:5–6"> For YHWH’s portion &#91;is&#93; His people, &#124;&#124; Jacob &#91;is&#93; the line of His inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; Psalms 17:8; Hosea 13:5"> He finds him in a land—a desert, &#124;&#124; And in a void—a howling wilderness, &#124;&#124; He turns him around—He causes him to understand—He keeps him as the apple of His eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Isaiah 46:4; Isaiah 63:9"> As an eagle wakes up its nest, &#124;&#124; Flutters over its young ones, &#124;&#124; Spreads its wings &#91;and&#93; takes them, &#124;&#124; Carries them on its pinions—</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 78:52–53; Isaiah 46:4; Isaiah 44:7–8"> YHWH alone leads him, &#124;&#124; And there is no strange god with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 58:14; Job 29:6; Psalms 81:16"> He makes him ride on high places of earth, &#124;&#124; And he eats increase of the fields, &#124;&#124; And He makes him suck honey from a rock, &#124;&#124; And oil out of the flint of a rock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 49:11; Psalms 81:16; Psalms 147:14"> Butter of the herd, and milk of the flock, &#124;&#124; With fat of lambs, and rams of sons of Bashan, &#124;&#124; And male goats, with fat of kidneys of wheat; And of the blood of the grape you drink wine!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:20; Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 33:5"> And Yeshurun waxes fat, and kicks: You have been fat, you have been thick, &#124;&#124; You have been covered. And he leaves God who made him, &#124;&#124; And dishonors the Rock of his salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 78:58; 1 Corinthians 10:22; Nahum 1:1–2"> They make Him zealous with strangers, &#124;&#124; They make Him angry with abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 5:8; Deuteronomy 28:64; Leviticus 17:7"> They sacrifice to the demons, not God, &#124;&#124; To gods they have not known, &#124;&#124; New ones—they came from the vicinity; Your fathers have not feared them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 17:10; Jeremiah 2:32; Psalms 106:21"> You forget the Rock that begot you, &#124;&#124; And neglect God who forms you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 106:40; Psalms 5:4; Isaiah 1:2"> And YHWH sees and despises—For the provocation of His sons and His daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:17–18; Luke 7:31–32; Deuteronomy 32:5"> And He says: I hide My face from them, &#124;&#124; I see what their latter end &#91;is&#93;; For they &#91;are&#93; a contrary generation, &#124;&#124; Sons in whom is no steadfastness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 10:19; Romans 9:25; Romans 11:11–14"> They have made Me jealous by &#91;what is&#93; not God, &#124;&#124; They made Me angry by their vanities; And I make them jealous by &#91;what is&#93; not a people, &#124;&#124; I make them angry by a foolish nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Lamentations 4:11; Jeremiah 15:14; Micah 1:4"> For a fire has been kindled in My anger, &#124;&#124; And it burns to the lowest &#91;part&#93; of Sheol, &#124;&#124; And consumes earth and its increase, &#124;&#124; And sets on fire &#91;the&#93; foundations of mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:16; Psalms 7:12–13; Isaiah 24:17–18"> I heap calamities on them, &#124;&#124; I consume My arrows on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 26:22; Ezekiel 5:17; Isaiah 65:25"> Exhausted by famine, &#124;&#124; And consumed by heat and bitter destruction—I also send the teeth of beasts on them, &#124;&#124; With poison of fearful things of the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:15; Lamentations 1:20; Isaiah 30:16"> The sword destroys from outside, &#124;&#124; And out of the inner-chambers—terror, &#124;&#124; Both youth and virgin, &#124;&#124; Suckling with man of grey hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:37"> I have said: I blow them away, &#124;&#124; I cause their remembrance to cease from man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 14:15–16; Exodus 32:12; Zechariah 1:14–15"> If I do not fear the anger of an enemy, &#124;&#124; Lest their adversaries know—Lest they say, Our hand is high, &#124;&#124; And YHWH has not worked all this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Isaiah 27:11; Job 28:28"> For they &#91;are&#93; a nation lost to counsels, &#124;&#124; And there is no understanding in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 81:13; Deuteronomy 5:29; Hosea 14:9"> If only they were wise, &#124;&#124; They would deal wisely &#91;with&#93; this, &#124;&#124; They would attend to their latter end!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 26:8; Joshua 23:10; Isaiah 30:17"> How does one pursue a thousand, &#124;&#124; And two cause a myriad to flee, &#124;&#124; If not that their Rock has sold them, &#124;&#124; And YHWH has shut them up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 14:25; 1 Samuel 2:2; Daniel 2:47"> For their rock &#91;is&#93; not as our Rock &#124;&#124; (And our enemies &#91;are&#93; judges!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:18; Ezekiel 16:45–51; Isaiah 1:10"> For their vine &#91;is&#93; of the vine of Sodom, &#124;&#124; And of the fields of Gomorrah; Their grapes &#91;are&#93; grapes of gall—They have bitter clusters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 58:4; Romans 3:13; Psalms 140:3"> The poison of dragons &#91;is&#93; their wine &#124;&#124; And the fierce venom of cobras.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hosea 13:12; Job 14:17; Jeremiah 2:22"> Is it not laid up with Me? Sealed among My treasures?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Hebrews 10:30; Nahum 1:2"> Vengeance and retribution &#91;are&#93; Mine, &#124;&#124; At the due time—their foot slides; For near is a day of their calamity, &#124;&#124; And things prepared for them have hastened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 135:14; Psalms 106:45; Judges 2:18"> For YHWH judges His people, &#124;&#124; And gives comfort over His servants. For He sees the going away of power, &#124;&#124; And none is restrained and left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Judges 10:14; 2 Kings 3:13"> And He has said, Where &#91;are&#93; their gods—The rock in which they trusted?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:18–19; Psalms 50:13; Judges 10:14"> Who eat the fat of their sacrifices, &#124;&#124; &#91;And&#93; drink the wine of their drink-offering? Let them arise and help you, &#124;&#124; Let it be a hiding place for you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:6; Hosea 6:1; Job 5:18"> See, now, that I &#91;am&#93; He, &#124;&#124; And there is no god besides Me; I put to death and I keep alive; I have struck and I heal; And there is not a deliverer from My hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 6:17–18; Genesis 14:22; Revelation 10:5–6"> For I lift up My hand to the heavens, &#124;&#124; And have said, I live for all time!</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 66:16; Isaiah 34:5–6; Isaiah 27:1"> If I have sharpened the brightness of My sword, &#124;&#124; And My hand lays hold on judgment, &#124;&#124; I return vengeance to My adversaries, &#124;&#124; And to those hating Me—I repay!</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Jeremiah 46:10; Ezekiel 38:21–22"> I make My arrows drunk with blood, &#124;&#124; And My sword devours flesh, &#124;&#124; From the blood of the pierced and captive, &#124;&#124; From the head of the leaders of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Revelation 19:2; Romans 12:19; Revelation 6:10"> Sing, O nations, &#91;with&#93; His people, &#124;&#124; For He avenges the blood of His servants, &#124;&#124; And He turns back vengeance on His adversaries, &#124;&#124; And has pardoned His land &#91;and&#93; His people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Numbers 13:8; Numbers 13:16; Deuteronomy 31:30"> And Moses comes and speaks all the words of this song in the ears of the people, he and Hoshea son of Nun;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> and Moses finishes to speak all these words to all Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:19; Ezekiel 40:4; Proverbs 3:1–4"> and says to them, “Set your heart to all the words which I am testifying against you today, that you command your sons to observe to do all the words of this law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Proverbs 4:22; Leviticus 18:5; 2 Peter 1:16"> for it &#91;is&#93; not a vain thing for you, for it &#91;is&#93; your life, and by this thing you prolong days on the ground to where you are passing over the Jordan to possess it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 27:12–13"> And YHWH speaks to Moses on this very same day, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 27:12; Isaiah 33:17; Deuteronomy 34:1–5"> “Go up to this Mount Abarim, Mount Nebo, which &#91;is&#93; in the land of Moab, which &#91;is&#93; on the front of Jericho, and see the land of Canaan which I am giving to the sons of Israel for a possession;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Numbers 33:38; Numbers 20:24–29"> and die on the mountain to where you are going up, and be gathered to your people, as your brother Aaron has died on Mount Hor and is gathered to his people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 27:14; Numbers 20:11–14; Deuteronomy 3:23–27"> because you trespassed against Me in the midst of the sons of Israel at the waters of Meribath-Kadesh, the wilderness of Zin, because you did not sanctify Me in the midst of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:1–4; Deuteronomy 3:27; Hebrews 11:39"> Indeed, you see the land before &#91;you&#93;, but you do not go in there to the land which I am giving to the sons of Israel.”</span>
