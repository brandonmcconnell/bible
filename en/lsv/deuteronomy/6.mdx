<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Deuteronomy 12:1; Deuteronomy 4:45"> “And this &#91;is&#93; the command, the statutes and the judgments which your God YHWH has commanded to teach you to do in the land which you are passing over there to possess it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 13:4; Exodus 20:20"> so that you fear your God YHWH, to keep all His statutes and His commands which I am commanding you, you, and your son, and your son’s son, all &#91;the&#93; days of your life, and so that your days are prolonged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Exodus 3:8; Genesis 26:4"> And you have heard, O Israel, and observed to do &#91;them&#93;, that it may be well with you, and that you may multiply exceedingly, as YHWH, God of your fathers, has spoken to you, &#91;in&#93; the land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 12:29–32; Isaiah 44:6; 1 Timothy 2:5"> Hear, O Israel: Our God YHWH—YHWH &#91;is&#93; one!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 12:30; Matthew 22:37; Luke 10:27"> And you have loved your God YHWH with all your heart, and with all your soul, and with all your might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Proverbs 7:3; Jeremiah 31:33"> And these words which I am commanding you today have been on your heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:19; Ephesians 6:4; Psalms 78:4–6"> and you have repeated them to your sons, and spoken of them in your sitting in your house, and in your walking in the way, and in your lying down, and in your rising up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:21; Proverbs 3:3; Deuteronomy 11:18"> and have bound them for a sign on your hand, and they have been for frontlets between your eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:20; Isaiah 30:8; Isaiah 57:8"> and you have written them on doorposts of your house and on your gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 24:13; Psalms 105:44; Nehemiah 9:25"> And it has been, when your God YHWH brings you into the land which He has sworn to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give to you great and good cities which you have not built,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:12–18; Deuteronomy 8:10–20; Ezekiel 16:10–20"> and houses full of all good things which you have not filled, and wells dug which you have not dug, vineyards and olive-yards which you have not planted, that you have eaten and been satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Take heed to yourself lest you forget YHWH who has brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of a house of servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:8; Deuteronomy 10:20; Matthew 4:10"> You fear your God YHWH, and you serve Him, and you swear by His Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:6; 1 John 5:21; Deuteronomy 13:7"> You do not go after other gods, of the gods of the peoples who &#91;are&#93; around you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 7:4"> for your God YHWH &#91;is&#93; a zealous God in your midst—lest the anger of your God YHWH burns against you, and He has destroyed you from off the face of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Luke 4:12; Matthew 4:7"> You do not try your God YHWH as you tried &#91;Him&#93; in Massah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:22; Psalms 119:4; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> You diligently keep the commands of your God YHWH, and His testimonies and His statutes which He has commanded you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 12:25; Romans 12:2"> and you have done that which is right and good in the eyes of YHWH, so that it is well with you, and you have gone in and possessed the good land which YHWH has sworn to your fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 33:52–53; Exodus 23:28–30; Judges 3:1–4"> to drive away all your enemies from your presence, as YHWH has spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Exodus 12:26; Joshua 4:6–7"> When your son asks you hereafter, saying, What &#91;are&#93; the testimonies, and the statutes, and the judgments which our God YHWH has commanded you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 6:17–18; Ephesians 2:11–12; Deuteronomy 15:15"> Then you have said to your son, We have been servants of Pharaoh in Egypt, and YHWH brings us out of Egypt by a high hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Psalms 58:10–11; Deuteronomy 7:19"> and YHWH gives signs and wonders, great and severe, on Egypt, on Pharaoh, and on all his house, before our eyes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:18; Deuteronomy 6:10; Exodus 13:5"> and He has brought us out from there, in order to bring us in, to give to us the land which He had sworn to our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Psalms 41:2; Deuteronomy 10:12–13"> And YHWH commands us to do all these statutes, to fear our God YHWH, for good to ourselves &#91;for&#93; all the days, to keep us alive, as &#91;at&#93; this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:13; Romans 10:3; Leviticus 18:5"> and it is righteousness for us when we observe to do all this command before our God YHWH, as He has commanded us.”</span>
