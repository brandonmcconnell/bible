<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Moses goes and speaks these words to all Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:7; Numbers 27:17; 1 Kings 3:7"> and he says to them, “I &#91;am&#93; a son of one hundred and twenty years today; I am not able to go out and to come in anymore, and YHWH has said to me, You do not pass over this Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:3; Deuteronomy 31:23; Deuteronomy 3:28"> Your God YHWH—He is passing over before you; He destroys these nations from before you, and you have possessed them; Joshua—he is passing over before you, as YHWH has spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 23:28–31; Deuteronomy 2:33; Deuteronomy 3:3–11"> And YHWH has done to them as he has done to Sihon and to Og, kings of the Amorite, and to their land, whom He destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Deuteronomy 20:16–17; Exodus 23:32–33"> And YHWH has given them before your face, and you have done to them according to all the command which I have commanded you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:10; Psalms 27:14"> be strong and courageous, do not fear, nor be terrified because of them, for your God YHWH &#91;is&#93; He who is going with you; He does not fail you nor forsake you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Deuteronomy 31:6; Deuteronomy 1:38"> And Moses calls for Joshua and says to him before the eyes of all Israel, “Be strong and courageous, for you go in with this people to the land which YHWH has sworn to their fathers to give to them, and you cause them to inherit it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Deuteronomy 31:6; Joshua 1:9"> and YHWH &#91;is&#93; He who is going before you, He Himself is with you; He does not fail you nor forsake you; do not fear, nor be frightened.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Joshua 3:3; Deuteronomy 17:18"> And Moses writes this law and gives it to the priests (sons of Levi, those carrying the Ark of the Covenant of YHWH), and to all &#91;the&#93; elderly of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 23:34–43; Deuteronomy 15:1–2"> and Moses commands them, saying, “At the end of seven years, in the appointed time, the year of release, in the Celebration of Shelters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 23:2; Joshua 8:34–35; Deuteronomy 16:16–17"> in the coming in of all Israel to see the face of YHWH in the place which He chooses, you proclaim this law before all Israel in their ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:10; Psalms 34:11–14; Deuteronomy 6:6–7"> Assemble the people, the men, and the women, and the infants, and your sojourner who &#91;is&#93; within your gates, so that they hear, and so that they learn, and have feared your God YHWH, and observed to do all the words of this law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:2; Deuteronomy 6:7; Proverbs 22:6"> and their sons, who have not known, hear and have learned to fear your God YHWH &#91;for&#93; all the days which you are living on the ground to where you are passing over the Jordan to possess it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 27:13; Deuteronomy 31:23; Job 2:1"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Behold, your days have drawn near to die; call Joshua, and station yourselves in the Tent of Meeting, and I charge him”; and Moses goes—Joshua also—and they station themselves in the Tent of Meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 40:38; Psalms 99:7; Exodus 33:9–10"> and YHWH is seen in the tent in a pillar of a cloud; and the pillar of the cloud stands at the opening of the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 10:6; Judges 10:13; Judges 2:12"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Behold, you are lying down with your fathers, and this people has risen and gone whoring after the gods of the stranger of the land, into the midst of which it has entered, and it has forsaken Me and broken My covenant which I made with it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:42; Isaiah 8:17; Psalms 104:29"> and My anger has burned against it in that day, and I have forsaken them, and hidden My face from them, and it has been for consumption, and many evils and distresses have found it, and it has said in that day, &#91;Is&#93; it not because my God is not in my midst that these evils have found me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:16–17"> And I certainly hide My face in that day for all the evil which it has done, for it has turned to other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Deuteronomy 31:21–22; Deuteronomy 31:26"> And now, write this song for yourselves, and teach it &#91;to&#93; the sons of Israel; put it in their mouths, so that this song is to Me for a witness against the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 6:10–12; Nehemiah 9:25–26"> When I bring them to the ground flowing with milk and honey, which I have sworn to their fathers, and they have eaten, and been satisfied, and been fat, and have turned to other gods, and they have served them, and despised Me, and broken My covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 5:3; Psalms 139:2; John 2:24–25"> then it has been, when many evils and distresses meet it, that this song has testified to its face for a witness; for it is not forgotten out of the mouth of its seed, for I have known its imagining which it is doing today, before I bring them into the land of which I have sworn.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19; Deuteronomy 31:9"> And Moses writes this song on that day, and teaches it &#91;to&#93; the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:14; Acts 7:45"> Then He commands Joshua son of Nun, and says, “Be strong and courageous, for you bring in the sons of Israel to the land which I have sworn to them, and I am with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:9; Deuteronomy 17:18"> And it comes to pass, when Moses finishes to write the words of this law on a scroll, until their completion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:9"> that Moses commands the Levites carrying the Ark of the Covenant of YHWH, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19; 2 Kings 22:8–11; Galatians 2:19"> “Take this Scroll of the Law, and you have set it on the side of the Ark of the Covenant of your God YHWH, and it has been against you for a witness there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:20; Deuteronomy 9:24; Acts 7:51"> for I have known your rebellion and your stiff neck; behold, in my being yet alive with you today, you have been rebelling against YHWH, and surely also after my death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Deuteronomy 4:26; Deuteronomy 30:19"> Assemble to me all the elderly of your tribes, and your authorities, and I speak these words in their ears, and cause the heavens and the earth to testify against them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 2:19; Deuteronomy 32:5; Luke 21:24"> for I have known that you act very corruptly after my death, and have turned aside from the way which I commanded you, and evil has met you in the latter end of the days, because you do evil in the eyes of YHWH, to make Him angry with the work of your hands.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 3:2; Deuteronomy 4:5; John 12:49"> And Moses speaks the words of this song in the ears of all the assembly of Israel, until their completion:</span>
