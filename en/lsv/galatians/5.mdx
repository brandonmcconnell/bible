<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Galatians 5:13; 1 Peter 2:16"> In the freedom, then, with which Christ made you free—stand, and do not be held fast again by a yoke of servitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; Acts 15:1; Romans 9:31–32"> Behold! I, Paul, say to you, that if you are &#91;to be&#93; circumcised, Christ will profit you nothing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Romans 2:25; James 2:10–11"> and I testify again to every man circumcised, that he is a debtor to do the whole law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:31–32; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Romans 11:6"> you were voided from the Christ, you who are declared righteous in law; you fell away from grace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 130:5; 1 Thessalonians 1:10; Psalms 62:5"> for we by the Spirit, by faith, wait for a hope of righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 7:19; James 2:14–26"> for in Christ Jesus neither circumcision avails anything, nor uncircumcision, but faith working through love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24; Galatians 3:1; Hebrews 12:1"> You were running well; who hindered you &#91;so as&#93; not to obey the truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 1:6; Romans 8:28"> The persuasion &#91;is&#93; not of Him who is calling you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 5:6–7; Mark 8:15"> A little leaven leavens the whole lump;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 5:12; 2 Corinthians 2:3; Galatians 1:7"> I have confidence in regard to you in the LORD that you will not be otherwise minded; and he who is troubling you will bear the judgment, whoever he may be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; Galatians 6:12; Galatians 4:29"> And I, brothers, if I still preach circumcision, why am I still persecuted? Then the stumbling-block of the Cross has been done away &#91;with&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 5:13; Titus 3:10"> O that even they would cut themselves off who are unsettling you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 9:19; 1 Corinthians 8:9"> For you were called to freedom, brothers, only the freedom &#91;is&#93; not for an occasion to the flesh, but serve one another through the love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Leviticus 19:18; Galatians 6:2"> for all the Law is fulfilled in one word—in this: “You will love your neighbor as yourself”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:3; James 3:14–3; Galatians 5:26"> and if you bite and devour one another, see that you may not be consumed by one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 5:24–25; Romans 8:12–14; Galatians 6:8"> And I say, walk in the Spirit, and you may not fulfill the desire of the flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:13; John 3:6; Romans 8:5–8"> for the flesh desires contrary to the Spirit, and the Spirit contrary to the flesh, and these are opposed to one another, that the things that you may will—these you may not do;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:14; Romans 6:14–15; Galatians 5:25"> and if you are led by the Spirit, you are not under law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Colossians 3:5–8; Matthew 15:18–19"> And the works of the flesh are also evident, which are: adultery, whoredom, uncleanness, licentiousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Revelation 21:8"> idolatry, witchcraft, enmities, strife, jealousy, wraths, rivalries, dissensions, sects,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Romans 8:13; Romans 13:13"> envyings, murders, drunkenness, reveling, and such like, of which I tell you before, as I also said before, that those doing such things will not inherit the Kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–17; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7; John 15:5"> And the fruit of the Spirit is: love, joy, peace, long-suffering, kindness, goodness, faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:9; Acts 24:25; Titus 2:2"> meekness, &#91;and&#93; self-control. Against such there is no law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Romans 13:14; Romans 8:13"> and those who are of Christ Jesus have crucified the flesh with the affections and the desires;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Galatians 5:16; Romans 8:4–5; Romans 8:2"> if we may live in the Spirit, we may also walk in the Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 3:14–16; Philippians 2:1–3; James 4:16"> let us not become vainglorious—provoking one another, envying one another!</span>
