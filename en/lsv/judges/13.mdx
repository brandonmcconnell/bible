<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:9; Judges 6:1; Romans 2:6"> And the sons of Israel add to do evil in the eyes of YHWH, and YHWH gives them into the hand of the Philistines &#91;for&#93; forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 19:41; Luke 1:7; Joshua 15:33"> And there is a certain man of Zorah, of the family of the Danite, and his name &#91;is&#93; Manoah, his wife &#91;is&#93; barren, and has not borne;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Luke 1:11; Judges 6:11–12"> and the Messenger of YHWH appears to the woman and says to her, “Now behold, you &#91;are&#93; barren and have not borne; when you have conceived, then you have borne a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Judges 13:14; Numbers 6:2–3"> And now, please take heed and do not drink wine and strong drink, and do not eat any unclean thing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 6:5; 1 Samuel 1:11; 1 Chronicles 18:1"> for behold, you are conceiving and bearing a son, and a razor does not go up on his head, for the youth is a Nazarite to God from the womb, and he begins to save Israel out of the hand of the Philistines.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 13:17–18; 1 Samuel 9:6; Matthew 28:3–4"> And the woman comes and speaks to her husband, saying, “A Man of God has come to me, and His appearance &#91;is&#93; as the appearance of the Messenger of God, very fearful, and I have not asked Him where He &#91;is&#93; from, and he has not declared His Name to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 13:4"> and He says to me, Behold, you are pregnant, and bearing a son, and now do not drink wine and strong drink, and do not eat any unclean thing, for the youth is a Nazarite to God from the womb until the day of his death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:6; Job 34:32; Proverbs 3:5–6"> And Manoah makes plea to YHWH and says, “O my Lord, the Man of God whom You sent, please let Him come in again to us, and direct us what we do to the youth who is born.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 65:2; Matthew 7:7–11"> And God listens to the voice of Manoah, and the Messenger of God comes again to the woman, and she &#91;is&#93; sitting in a field, and her husband Manoah is not with her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:28–29; John 1:41–42"> and the woman hurries, and runs, and declares &#91;it&#93; to her husband, and says to him, “Behold, He has appeared to me—the Man who came on &#91;that&#93; day to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Manoah rises, and goes after his wife, and comes to the Man, and says to Him, “Are you the Man who spoke to the woman?” And He says, “I &#91;am&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 22:6; Ephesians 6:4; Genesis 18:19"> And Manoah says, “Now let Your words come to pass; what is the custom of the youth—and his work?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the Messenger of YHWH says to Manoah, “Of all that I said to the woman let her take heed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 2:5; Judges 13:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:4"> she does not eat of anything which comes out from the wine-vine, and she does not drink wine and strong drink, and she does not eat any unclean thing; she observes all that I have commanded her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 6:18–19; Genesis 18:3–5; Judges 13:3"> And Manoah says to the Messenger of YHWH, “Please let us detain You, and we prepare a kid of the goats before You.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 13:23; Judges 6:20; Judges 6:26"> And the Messenger of YHWH says to Manoah, “If you detain Me—I do not eat of your bread; and if you prepare a burnt-offering—offer it to YHWH”; for Manoah has not known that He &#91;is&#93; the Messenger of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 32:29"> And Manoah says to the Messenger of YHWH, “What &#91;is&#93; Your Name? When Your words come to pass, then we have honored You.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 32:29; Isaiah 9:6; Judges 13:6"> And the Messenger of YHWH says to him, “Why do you ask this, My Name, since it &#91;is&#93; incomprehensible?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 6:19–21; 1 Kings 18:30–38"> And Manoah takes the kid of the goats, and the present, and offers on the rock to YHWH, and He is doing wonderfully, and Manoah and his wife are looking on,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 9:24; 1 Chronicles 21:16; 2 Kings 2:11"> and it comes to pass, in the going up of the flame from off the altar toward the heavens, that the Messenger of YHWH goes up in the flame of the altar, and Manoah and his wife are looking on, and they fall on their faces to the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 6:22; Hosea 12:4–5; Judges 13:16"> and the Messenger of YHWH has not added again to appear to Manoah, and to his wife, then Manoah has known that He &#91;is&#93; the Messenger of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 32:30; Deuteronomy 5:26; John 1:18"> And Manoah says to his wife, “We certainly die, for we have seen God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; 1 Corinthians 12:21; John 15:15"> And his wife says to him, “If YHWH were desirous to put us to death, He had not received burnt-offering and present from our hands, nor showed us all these things, nor caused us to hear &#91;anything&#93; like this as &#91;at this&#93; time.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 1:80; 1 Samuel 3:19; Hebrews 11:32"> And the woman bears a son, and calls his name Samson, and the youth grows, and YHWH blesses him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 3:10; Joshua 15:33; John 3:34"> and the Spirit of YHWH begins to move him in the camp of Dan, between Zorah and Eshtaol.</span>
