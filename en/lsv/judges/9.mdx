<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 8:31; Psalms 83:2–4; 2 Samuel 15:6"> And Abimelech son of Jerubba‘al goes to Shechem, to his mother’s brothers, and speaks to them, and to all the family of the house of his mother’s father, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 29:14; Judges 8:30; Hebrews 2:14"> “Now speak in the ears of all the masters of Shechem, Which &#91;is&#93; good for you—seventy men ruling over you (all the sons of Jerubba‘al), or one man ruling over you? And you have remembered that I &#91;am&#93; your bone and your flesh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 29:15; Psalms 10:3; Proverbs 1:11–14"> And his mother’s brothers speak concerning him, in the ears of all the masters of Shechem, all these words, and their heart inclines after Abimelech, for they said, “He &#91;is&#93; our brother”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 8:33; Acts 17:5; 2 Chronicles 13:7"> and they give seventy &#91;pieces&#93; of silver out of the house of Ba‘al-Berith to him, and Abimelech hires vain and unstable men with them, and they go after him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 11:1–2; 2 Chronicles 21:4; Judges 6:24"> and he goes into the house of his father at Ophrah, and slays his brothers, sons of Jerubba‘al, seventy men, on one stone; and Jotham, youngest son of Jerubba‘al, is left, for he was hidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:9; 1 Kings 12:20; 2 Kings 12:20"> And all the masters of Shechem are gathered together, and all the house of Millo, and come and cause Abimelech to reign for king at the oak of the camp which &#91;is&#93; in Shechem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:29; John 4:20; Deuteronomy 27:12"> and they declare &#91;it&#93; to Jotham, and he goes and stands on the top of Mount Gerizim, and lifts up his voice, and calls, and says to them, “Listen to me, O masters of Shechem, and God listens to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 8:22–23; Daniel 4:10–18; 2 Kings 14:9"> The trees have diligently gone to anoint a king over them, and they say to the olive, Reign over us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 29:7; Acts 4:27"> And the olive says to them, Have I ceased from my fatness, by which they honor gods and men, that I have gone to stagger over the trees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the trees say to the fig, Come, reign over us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 13:6–7"> And the fig says to them, Have I ceased from my sweetness, and my good increase, that I have gone to stagger over the trees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the trees say to the vine, Come, reign over us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Ecclesiastes 10:19; Numbers 15:5"> And the vine says to them, Have I ceased from my new wine, which is making gods and men glad, that I have gone to stagger over the trees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 14:9"> And all the trees say to the bramble, Come, reign over us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 30:2; Judges 9:20; Hosea 14:7"> And the bramble says to the trees, If in truth you are anointing me for king over you, come, take refuge in my shadow; and if not—fire comes out from the bramble, and devours the cedars of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 8:35"> And now, if you have acted in truth and in sincerity when you make Abimelech king; and if you have done good with Jerubba‘al, and with his house; and if you have done to him according to the deed of his hands—</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 16:4; Romans 5:8; Revelation 12:11"> because my father has fought for you, and casts his life away from &#91;him&#93;, and delivers you from the hand of Midian;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 9:5–6; Judges 8:30–31; Judges 9:14"> and you have risen against the house of my father today, and slay his sons, seventy men, on one stone, and cause Abimelech son of his handmaid to reign over the masters of Shechem, because he &#91;is&#93; your brother—</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 8:6; James 4:16; Philippians 3:3"> indeed, if in truth and in sincerity you have acted with Jerubba‘al and with his house this day, rejoice in Abimelech, and he rejoices—even he—in you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 9:15; Judges 9:56–57; 2 Chronicles 20:22–23"> and if not—fire comes out from Abimelech and devours the masters of Shechem and the house of Millo, and fire comes out from the masters of Shechem and from the house of Millo, and devours Abimelech.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 21:16; 2 Samuel 20:14; Joshua 19:8"> And Jotham hurries, and flees, and goes to Beer, and dwells there, from the face of his brother Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Abimelech is prince over Israel &#91;for&#93; three years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Isaiah 19:14; Isaiah 19:2"> and God sends an evil spirit between Abimelech and the masters of Shechem, and the masters of Shechem deal treacherously with Abimelech,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 2:32; Psalms 7:16; Esther 9:25"> for &#91;the&#93; coming in of &#91;the&#93; violence &#91;against&#93; seventy sons of Jerubba‘al, and to place their blood on their brother Abimelech, who slew them, and on the masters of Shechem, who strengthened his hands to slay his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 8:12–13; Proverbs 1:11–12; Joshua 8:4"> And the masters of Shechem set ambushes for him on the top of the hills, and rob everyone who passes over by them in the way, and it is declared to Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 13:8; Genesis 19:7"> And Gaal son of Ebed comes—also his brothers—and they pass over into Shechem, and the masters of Shechem trust in him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 9:4; Judges 9:46; Psalms 109:17"> and go out into the field, and gather their vineyards, and tread, and make praises, and go into the house of their god, and eat and drink, and revile Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 34:2; Genesis 34:6; 1 Samuel 25:10"> And Gaal son of Ebed says, “Who &#91;is&#93; Abimelech, and who &#91;is&#93; Shechem, that we serve him? Is &#91;he&#93; not son of Jerubba‘al? And his commander Zebul? The men of Hamor father of Shechem serve you, and why do we serve him—we?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:4; 2 Samuel 2:14–17; Isaiah 36:8–9"> And oh that this people were in my hand—then I turn Abimelech aside”; and he says to Abimelech, “Increase your host, and come out.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Zebul, prince of the city, hears the words of Gaal son of Ebed, and his anger burns,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> and he sends messengers to Abimelech deceitfully, saying, “Behold, Gaal son of Ebed and his brothers are coming into Shechem, and behold, they are fortifying the city against you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 3:15; Psalms 36:4; Proverbs 1:11–16"> and now, rise by night, you and the people who &#91;are&#93; with you, and lay wait in the field,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:7; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Leviticus 25:26"> and it has been, in the morning, about the rising of the sun, you rise early, and have pushed against the city; and behold, he and the people who &#91;are&#93; with him are going out to you—and you have done to him as your hand finds.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And Abimelech rises, and all the people who &#91;are&#93; with him, by night, and they lay wait against Shechem—four detachments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and Gaal son of Ebed goes out, and stands at the opening of the gate of the city, and Abimelech rises—also the people who &#91;are&#93; with him—from the ambush,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 8:24; Ezekiel 7:7"> and Gaal sees the people and says to Zebul, “Behold, people are coming down from the top of the hills”; and Zebul says to him, “You are seeing the shadow of the hills like men.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:14"> And Gaal adds yet to speak and says, “Behold, people are coming down from the high part of the land, and another detachment is coming by the way of the oak of Meonenim.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Judges 9:28–29; 2 Kings 14:8–14; 2 Samuel 2:26–27"> And Zebul says to him, “Where &#91;is&#93; your mouth now, in that you say, Who &#91;is&#93; Abimelech that we serve him? Is this not the people against which you have kicked? Please go out now and fight against it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And Gaal goes out before the masters of Shechem, and fights against Abimelech,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Kings 20:18–21; 1 Kings 20:30"> and Abimelech pursues him, and he flees from his presence, and many fall wounded—to the opening of the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Judges 9:28; Judges 9:30"> And Abimelech abides in Arumah, and Zebul casts out Gaal and his brothers from dwelling in Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And it comes to pass, on the next day, that the people go out to the field, and they declare &#91;it&#93; to Abimelech,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> and he takes the people, and divides them into three detachments, and lays wait in a field, and looks, and behold, the people are coming out from the city, and he rises against them, and strikes them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Galatians 5:15; Judges 9:15; Judges 9:20"> And Abimelech and the detachments who &#91;are&#93; with him have pushed on, and stand at the opening of the gate of the city, and the two detachments have pushed against all who are in the field, and strike them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Kings 3:25; Deuteronomy 29:23; James 2:13"> and Abimelech has fought against the city all that day, and captures the city, and has slain the people who &#91;are&#93; in it, and he breaks down the city, and sows it &#91;with&#93; salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Judges 8:33; Judges 9:4; 1 Kings 8:26"> And all the masters of the Tower of Shechem hear, and go into the high place of the house of the god Berith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> and it is declared to Abimelech that all the masters of the Tower of Shechem have gathered themselves together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 68:14; Proverbs 1:11–12; Judges 7:17–18"> and Abimelech goes up to Mount Zalmon, he and all the people who &#91;are&#93; with him, and Abimelech takes the great axe in his hand, and cuts off a bough of the trees, and lifts it up, and sets &#91;it&#93; on his shoulder, and says to the people who &#91;are&#93; with him, “What you have seen me do—hurry, do as I &#91;have done&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Galatians 5:15; James 3:16; Judges 9:20"> And every one of the people cuts down his bough and goes after Abimelech, and sets &#91;them&#93; at the high place, and burns the high place with fire by these, and also all the men of the Tower of Shechem die—about one thousand men and women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> And Abimelech goes to Thebez, and encamps against Thebez, and captures it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> and a strong tower has been in the midst of the city, and all the men and the women flee there, and all the masters of the city, and they shut &#91;it&#93; behind them, and go up on the roof of the tower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Judges 9:48–49; 2 Kings 14:10; 2 Kings 15:16"> And Abimelech comes to the tower, and fights against it, and draws near to the opening of the tower to burn it with fire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:21; Job 31:3; Jeremiah 49:20"> and a certain woman casts a piece of a millstone on the head of Abimelech, and breaks his skull,</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:4–5"> and he calls quickly to the young man carrying his weapons and says to him, “Draw your sword, and you have put me to death, lest they say of me—A woman slew him”; and his young man pierced him through, and he dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:16; 2 Samuel 20:21–22; 1 Kings 22:35–36"> And the men of Israel see that Abimelech &#91;is&#93; dead, and each one goes to his place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Psalms 94:23; Judges 9:24; Proverbs 5:22"> and God turns back the evil of Abimelech which he did to his father to slay his seventy brothers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Judges 9:20; Joshua 6:26; 1 Kings 16:34"> and God has returned all the evil of the men of Shechem on their &#91;own&#93; head, and the cursing of Jotham son of Jerubba‘al comes to them.</span>
