<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Revelation 19:1–3; Psalms 18:1"> And Deborah sings—also Barak son of Abinoam—on that day, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 5:9; 2 Chronicles 17:16; Psalms 110:3"> “For freeing leaders in Israel, &#124;&#124; For a people willingly offering themselves, &#124;&#124; Bless YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 138:4–5; Psalms 119:46; Psalms 49:1–2"> Hear, you kings; give ear, you princes, &#124;&#124; I sing to YHWH, &#124;&#124; I sing praise to YHWH, God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Psalms 68:7–8; Psalms 77:17"> YHWH, in Your going forth out of Seir, &#124;&#124; In Your stepping out of the field of Edom, &#124;&#124; The earth trembled and the heavens dropped, &#124;&#124; And thick clouds dropped water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 19:18; Psalms 97:5; Isaiah 64:1–3"> Hills flowed from the face of YHWH, &#124;&#124; This one—Sinai—From the face of YHWH, God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 33:8; Judges 3:31; Lamentations 1:4"> In the days of Shamgar son of Anath—In the days of Jael—The ways have ceased, &#124;&#124; And those going in the paths go &#91;in&#93; crooked ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; 2 Samuel 20:19; Esther 9:19"> Villages ceased in Israel—they ceased, &#124;&#124; Until I arose—Deborah, &#124;&#124; That I arose, a mother in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16–17; Judges 2:12; Judges 2:17"> He chooses new gods, &#124;&#124; Then war &#91;is&#93; at the gates! A shield is not seen—and a spear &#124;&#124; Among forty thousand in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 5:2; 2 Corinthians 8:3–4; 2 Corinthians 9:5"> My heart &#91;is&#93; to the lawgivers of Israel, &#124;&#124; Who are offering themselves willingly among the people, &#124;&#124; Bless YHWH!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 12:14; Judges 10:4; Psalms 105:2"> Riders on white donkeys—Sitters on a long robe—And walkers by the way—meditate!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 6:5; Judges 5:8; 1 Samuel 12:7"> By the voice of shouters &#124;&#124; Between the places of drawing water, &#124;&#124; There they give out righteous acts of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Righteous acts of His villages in Israel, &#124;&#124; Then the people of YHWH have gone down to the gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:8; Psalms 68:18; Psalms 57:8"> Awake, awake, Deborah; Awake, awake, utter a song; Rise, Barak, and take your captivity captive, &#124;&#124; Son of Abinoam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 2:26–27; Romans 8:37; Psalms 75:7"> Then him who is left of the majestic ones He caused to rule the people of YHWH, &#124;&#124; He caused me to rule among the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 12:15; Numbers 32:39–40; Judges 3:27"> From Ephraim &#91;are&#93; those whose root &#91;is&#93; in Amalek, &#124;&#124; After you, Benjamin, among your peoples, &#124;&#124; From Machir lawgivers came down, &#124;&#124; And from Zebulun those drawing with the reed of a writer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 4:14; Proverbs 22:13; Acts 20:13"> And princes in Issachar &#91;are&#93; with Deborah, &#124;&#124; Indeed, Issachar &#91;is&#93; right with Barak, &#124;&#124; Into the valley he was sent on his feet. In the divisions of Reuben, &#124;&#124; The decrees of heart &#91;are&#93; great!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 32:24; Numbers 32:1–5; Judges 5:15"> Why have you abided between the boundaries, &#124;&#124; To hear lowings of herds? For the divisions of Reuben, &#124;&#124; The searchings of heart &#91;are&#93; great!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 13:25; Joshua 19:24–31; Joshua 13:31"> Gilead dwelt beyond the Jordan, &#124;&#124; And why does Dan sojourn &#91;in&#93; ships? Asher has abided at the haven of the seas, &#124;&#124; And dwells by his creeks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 4:10; Judges 4:6; Esther 4:16"> Zebulun &#91;is&#93; a people who exposed its soul to death, &#124;&#124; Naphtali also—on high places of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Judges 1:27; 1 Kings 4:12"> Kings came—they fought; Then kings of Canaan fought, &#124;&#124; In Taanach, by the waters of Megiddo; They did not take gain of money!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; 1 Samuel 7:10; Psalms 77:17–18"> They fought from the heavens: The stars fought with Sisera from their highways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 4:7; Micah 7:10; Psalms 44:5"> The Brook of Kishon swept them away, &#124;&#124; The most ancient brook—the Brook of Kishon. You tread down strength, O my soul!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 20:7; Psalms 147:10–11; Jeremiah 47:4"> Then the horse-heels were broken, &#124;&#124; By gallopings—gallopings of its mighty ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:10; Judges 21:9–10; 1 Corinthians 16:22"> Curse Meroz, said a messenger of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Cursing, curse its inhabitants! For they did not come to the help of YHWH, &#124;&#124; To the help of YHWH among the mighty!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 1:42; Luke 1:28; Judges 4:17"> Above women is Jael, &#124;&#124; The wife of Heber the Kenite, &#124;&#124; She is blessed above women in the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 4:19–21"> He asked for water—she gave milk; She brought butter near in a lordly dish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:49–51; Judges 4:21; 2 Samuel 20:22"> She sends forth her hand to the pin, &#124;&#124; And her right hand to the laborers’ hammer, &#124;&#124; And she hammered Sisera—she struck his head, &#124;&#124; Indeed, she struck, and it passed through his temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 52:7; James 2:13; Matthew 7:2"> He bowed between her feet—He fell, he lay down; He bowed between her feet, he fell; Where he bowed, there he fell—destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 4:15; James 5:7; Song of Songs 8:14"> She has looked out through the window—Indeed, she cries out—the mother of Sisera, &#124;&#124; Through the lattice: Why is his chariot delaying to come? Why have the steps of his chariot tarried?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The wise ones, her princesses, answer her, &#124;&#124; Indeed, she returns her sayings to herself:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 15:9; 2 Samuel 13:18; Genesis 37:3"> Do they not find? They apportion spoil, &#124;&#124; A female—two females—for every head, &#124;&#124; Spoil of finger-work for Sisera, &#124;&#124; Spoil of embroidered finger-work, &#124;&#124; Finger-work—a pair of embroidered things, &#124;&#124; For the necks of the spoil!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 37:6; 2 Samuel 23:4; Psalms 19:4–5"> So do all Your enemies perish, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; And those loving Him &#91;are&#93; &#124;&#124; As the going out of the sun in its might!” And the land rests &#91;for&#93; forty years.</span>
