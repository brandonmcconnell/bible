<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:41; Judges 12:1–6; Job 5:2"> And the men of Ephraim say to him, “What &#91;is&#93; this thing you have done to us—not to call for us when you went to fight with Midian?” And they strive with him severely;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:4–7; Philippians 2:2–3; James 3:13–18"> and he says to them, “What have I now done like you? Are the gleanings of Ephraim not better than the harvest of Abi-Ezer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Judges 7:24–25; John 4:37"> God has given the heads of Midian, Oreb and Zeeb, into your hand; and what have I been able to do like you?” Then their temper desisted from off him in his speaking this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Corinthians 4:16; 1 Samuel 14:28–29"> And Gideon comes to the Jordan, passing over, he and the three hundred men who &#91;are&#93; with him—wearied, yet pursuing—</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:17; Psalms 60:6; 1 Samuel 25:18"> and he says to the men of Succoth, “Please give cakes of bread to the people who &#91;are&#93; at my feet, for they &#91;are&#93; wearied, and I am pursuing after Zebah and Zalmunna, kings of Midian.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 20:11; Philippians 2:21; Proverbs 18:23"> And the heads of Succoth say, “Is the hand of Zebah and Zalmunna now in your hand, that we give bread to your host?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 7:15"> And Gideon says, “Therefore—in YHWH’s giving Zebah and Zalmunna into my hand—I have threshed your flesh with the thorns of the wilderness, and with the threshing instruments.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:30–31; 1 Kings 12:25"> And he goes up there &#91;to&#93; Penuel, and speaks to them thus; and the men of Penuel answer him as the men of Succoth answered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 8:17; 1 Kings 22:27–28"> And he also speaks to the men of Penuel, saying, “In my turning back in peace, I break down this tower.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 20:25; Judges 20:2"> And Zebah and Zalmunna &#91;are&#93; in Karkor, and their camps with them, about fifteen thousand, all who are left of all the camp of the sons of the east; and those falling &#91;are&#93; one hundred and twenty thousand men, drawing sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 32:35; Numbers 32:42; Judges 18:27"> And Gideon goes up the way of those who dwell in tents, on the east of Nobah and Jogbehah, and strikes the camp, and the camp was confident;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 83:11; Amos 2:14; Job 12:16–21"> and Zebab and Zalmunna flee, and he pursues after them, and captures the two kings of Midian, Zebah and Zalmunna, and he has caused all the camp to tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Gideon son of Joash turns back from the battle, at the going up of the sun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 1:24–25; 1 Samuel 30:11–15"> and captures a young man of the men of Succoth, and asks him, and he describes to him the heads of Succoth, and its elderly—seventy-seven men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 8:6–7"> And he comes to the men of Succoth and says, “Behold Zebah and Zalmunna, with whom you reproached me, saying, Is the hand of Zebah and Zalmunna now in your hand that we give bread to your men who &#91;are&#93; wearied?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 8:7; Ezra 2:6; Proverbs 10:13"> And he takes the elderly of the city, and &#91;the&#93; thorns of the wilderness, and the threshing instruments, and teaches the men of Succoth by them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 8:9; 1 Kings 12:25"> and he has broken down the Tower of Penuel, and slays the men of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 4:6; Jude 1:16; Psalms 89:12"> And he says to Zebah and to Zalmunna, “What manner of men &#91;were they&#93; whom you slew in Tabor?” And they say, “As you—so they, &#91;each&#93; one as the form of the king’s sons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And he says, “They &#91;were&#93; my brothers—sons of my mother; YHWH lives, if you had kept them alive—I would not kill you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 149:9; 1 Samuel 15:33; Joshua 10:24"> And he says to his firstborn Jether, “Rise, slay them”; and the young man has not drawn his sword, for he has been afraid, for he &#91;is&#93; yet a youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 83:11; Judges 8:26; Isaiah 3:18"> And Zebah and Zalmunna say, “Rise and fall on us; for as the man—his might”; and Gideon rises, and slays Zebah and Zalmunna, and takes the crescents which &#91;are&#93; on the necks of their camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 6:15; Judges 9:8–15; 1 Samuel 12:12"> And the men of Israel say to Gideon, “Rule over us, both you, and your son, and your son’s son, for you have saved us from the hand of Midian.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 33:22; 1 Samuel 12:12; 1 Samuel 10:19"> And Gideon says to them, “I do not rule over you, nor does my son rule over you; YHWH rules over you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 25:13; Genesis 37:25; Genesis 37:28"> And Gideon says to them, “Let me ask a petition of you, and each give to me the ring of his prey, for they have rings of gold, for they &#91;are&#93; Ishmaelites.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And they say, “We certainly give”; and they spread out the garment, and each casts the ring of his prey there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 18:16; John 19:2; Revelation 18:12"> and the weight of the rings of gold which he asked for is one thousand and seven hundred &#91;shekels&#93; of gold, apart from the crescents, and the pendants, and the purple garments, which &#91;are&#93; on the kings of Midian, and apart from the chains which &#91;are&#93; on the necks of their camels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 17:5; Judges 18:14; Psalms 106:39"> and Gideon makes it into an ephod, and sets it up in his city, in Ophrah, and all Israel go whoring after it there, and it is for a snare to Gideon and to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Judges 3:11; Judges 3:30"> And Midian is humbled before the sons of Israel, and have not added to lift up their head; and the land rests &#91;for&#93; forty years in the days of Gideon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 6:32; Judges 7:1; 1 Samuel 12:11"> And Jerubba‘al son of Joash goes and dwells in his own house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 9:5; Judges 9:2; 1 Kings 11:3"> and there have been seventy sons of Gideon, coming out of his loin, for he had many wives;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 9:18; Genesis 22:24; Genesis 16:15"> and his concubine, who &#91;is&#93; in Shechem, has borne to him—even she—a son, and he appoints his name Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Judges 6:24; Job 5:26"> And Gideon son of Joash dies, in a good old age, and is buried in the burying-place of his father Joash, in Ophrah of the Abi-Ezrite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Judges 9:46; Judges 2:19; Judges 9:4"> And it comes to pass, when Gideon &#91;is&#93; dead, that the sons of Israel turn back and go whoring after the Ba‘alim, and set Ba‘al-Berith over them for a god;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 78:42; Psalms 106:21; Psalms 78:11"> and the sons of Israel have not remembered their God YHWH, who is delivering them out of the hand of all their surrounding enemies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:14–15; Judges 9:5; Judges 9:16–19"> neither have they done kindness with the house of Jerubba‘al—Gideon—according to all the good which he did with Israel.</span>
