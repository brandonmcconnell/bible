<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 17:6; Judges 19:1; Judges 1:34"> In those days there is no king in Israel, and in those days the tribe of the Danite is seeking an inheritance to inhabit for itself, for &#91;that&#93; has not fallen to it to that day in the midst of the tribes of Israel by inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 13:25; Joshua 2:1; Judges 18:11"> And the sons of Dan send, out of their family, five of their men—men, sons of valor—from Zorah, and from Eshtaol, to traverse the land, and to search it, and they say to them, “Go, search the land”; and they come into the hill-country of Ephraim, to the house of Micah, and lodge there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 12:6; Genesis 27:22; Matthew 26:73"> They &#91;are&#93; with the household of Micah, and they have discerned the voice of the young man, the Levite, and turn aside there, and say to him, “Who has brought you here? And what are you doing in this &#91;place&#93;? And why are you here?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 17:10; Ezekiel 13:19; Proverbs 28:21"> And he says to them, “Thus and thus has Micah done to me; and he hires me, and I am for a priest to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:10; Isaiah 30:1; 1 Kings 22:5"> And they say to him, “Please ask of God, and we know whether our way on which we are going is prosperous.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 22:6; 1 Kings 22:15; Psalms 33:18"> And the priest says to them, “Go in peace; before YHWH &#91;is&#93; your way in which you go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:27–28; 1 Samuel 3:13"> And the five men go, and come to Laish, and see the people which &#91;is&#93; in its midst, dwelling confidently, according to the custom of Zidonians, quiet and confident; and there is none putting to shame in the land in &#91;any&#93; thing, possessing restraint, and they &#91;are&#93; far off from the Zidonians, and have no word with &#91;any&#93; man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 18:11; Judges 18:2; Judges 13:2"> And they come to their brothers at Zorah and Eshtaol, and their brothers say to them, “What &#91;did&#93; you &#91;find&#93;?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 2:23–24; Numbers 13:30; Joshua 18:3"> And they say, “Rise, and we go up against them, for we have seen the land, and behold—very good; and you are keeping silent! Do not be slothful to go—to enter to possess the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 18:27; Judges 18:7; Deuteronomy 2:29"> When you go, you come to a confident people, and the land &#91;is&#93; large on both hands, for God has given it into your hand, a place where there is no lack of anything which &#91;is&#93; in the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 18:16"> And there journey there, of the family of the Danite, from Zorah, and from Eshtaol, six hundred men girded with weapons of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:25; Joshua 15:60; 2 Chronicles 1:4"> And they go up and encamp in Kirjath-Jearim, in Judah, therefore they have called that place, “Camp of Dan,” until this day; behold, behind Kirjath-Jearim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 24:30; Judges 19:1; Judges 18:2–3"> And they pass over there &#91;to&#93; the hill-country of Ephraim, and come to the house of Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 17:5; Judges 18:3–4; 1 Samuel 14:28"> And the five men, those going to traverse the land of Laish, answer and say to their brothers, “Have you known that in these houses there is an ephod, and teraphim, and carved image, and molten image? And now, know what you do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 43:27; 1 Samuel 17:22; John 14:27"> And they turn aside there, and come to the house of the young man, the Levite, the house of Micah, and ask of him of welfare—</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 18:11"> (and the six hundred men girded with their weapons of war, who &#91;are&#93; of the sons of Dan, are standing at the opening of the gate)—</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 18:14; Judges 17:4–5; Judges 18:2"> indeed, the five men, those going to traverse the land, go up—they have come in there—they have taken the carved image, and the ephod, and the teraphim, and the molten image—and the priest is standing at the opening of the gate, and the six hundred men who are girded with weapons of war—</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> indeed, these have entered the house of Micah, and take the carved image, the ephod, and the teraphim, and the molten image; and the priest says to them, “What are you doing?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 29:9; Job 21:5; Judges 17:10"> And they say to him, “Keep silent, lay your hand on your mouth, and go with us, and be for a father and for a priest to us: is it better your being a priest for the house of one man, or your being priest for a tribe and for a family in Israel?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Ezekiel 13:19; Proverbs 30:15"> And the heart of the priest is glad, and he takes the ephod, and the teraphim, and the carved image, and goes into the midst of the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and they turn and go, and put the infants, and the livestock, and the baggage, before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> They have been far off from the house of Micah—and the men who &#91;are&#93; in the houses which &#91;are&#93; near the house of Micah have been called together, and overtake the sons of Dan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 114:5; 2 Samuel 14:5; 1 Samuel 11:5"> and call to the sons of Dan, and they turn their faces around, and say to Micah, “What &#91;is this&#93; to you that you have been called together?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18–19; Isaiah 44:18–20; Psalms 115:8"> And he says, “You have taken my gods which I made, and the priest, and you go; and what more do I &#91;have&#93;? And what &#91;is&#93; this you say to me, What &#91;is this&#93; to you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 27:2; Job 3:5; 1 Samuel 30:6"> And the sons of Dan say to him, “Do not let your voice be heard with us, lest men bitter in soul fall on you, and you have gathered your life, and the life of your household”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and the sons of Dan go on their way, and Micah sees that they are stronger than he, and turns, and goes back to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:7; Judges 18:10"> And they have taken that which Micah had made, and the priest whom he had, and come in against Laish, against a people quiet and confident, and strike them by the mouth of the sword, and have burned the city with fire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:6; Judges 18:7; Numbers 13:21"> and there is no deliverer, for it &#91;is&#93; far off from Sidon, and they have no word with &#91;any&#93; man, and it &#91;is&#93; in the valley which &#91;is&#93; by Beth-Rehob; and they build the city, and dwell in it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Genesis 14:14; 1 Kings 15:20"> and call the name of the city Dan, by the name of their father Dan, who was born to Israel; and yet Laish &#91;is&#93; the name of the city at the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:10–11; Judges 13:1; Judges 17:5"> And the sons of Dan raise up the carved image for themselves, and Jonathan son of Gershom, son of Manasseh, he and his sons have been priests for the tribe of the Danite, until the day of the expulsion of &#91;the people&#93; of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; 1 Samuel 1:3; Judges 19:18"> And they appoint for themselves the carved image of Micah, which he had made, all the days of the house of God being in Shiloh.</span>
