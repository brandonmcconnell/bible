<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:32; Judges 6:12; 2 Kings 5:1"> And Jephthah the Gileadite has been a mighty man of valor, and he &#91;is the&#93; son of a harlot woman; and Gilead begets Jephthah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Proverbs 2:16; Deuteronomy 23:2"> and the wife of Gilead bears sons to him, and the wife’s sons grow up and cast Jephthah out, and say to him, “You do not inherit in the house of our father; for you &#91;are the&#93; son of another woman.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 9:4; 1 Samuel 22:2; 2 Samuel 10:8"> And Jephthah flees from the face of his brothers, and dwells in the land of Tob; and vain men gather themselves together to Jephthah, and they go out with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 10:9"> And it comes to pass, after a time, that the sons of Ammon fight with Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Acts 7:35–39; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29"> and it comes to pass, when the sons of Ammon have fought with Israel, that &#91;the&#93; elderly of Gilead go to take Jephthah from the land of Tob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and they say to Jephthah, “Come, and you have been for a captain to us, and we fight against the sons of Ammon.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Genesis 45:4–5; Isaiah 60:14"> And Jephthah says to &#91;the&#93; elderly of Gilead, “Have you not hated me? And you cast me out from the house of my father, and why have you come to me now when you are in distress?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 10:18; 1 Kings 13:6; Exodus 10:17"> And &#91;the&#93; elderly of Gilead say to Jephthah, “Therefore, now, we have turned back to you; and you have gone with us, and have fought against the sons of Ammon, and you have been for head to us—to all the inhabitants of Gilead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–29"> And Jephthah says to &#91;the&#93; elderly of Gilead, “If you are taking me back to fight against the sons of Ammon, and YHWH has given them before me—am I for a head to you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:5; Genesis 31:50; Jeremiah 29:23"> And &#91;the&#93; elderly of Gilead say to Jephthah, “YHWH is listening between us—if we do not do so according to your word.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 10:17; 1 Samuel 10:17; Judges 20:1"> And Jephthah goes with &#91;the&#93; elderly of Gilead, and the people set him over them for head and for captain, and Jephthah speaks all his words before YHWH in Mizpeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26; Matthew 18:15–16; Proverbs 25:8–9"> And Jephthah sends messengers to the king of the sons of Ammon, saying, “What &#91;is this&#93; to me and to you, that you have come to me, to fight in my land?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 32:22; Numbers 21:24–26; Deuteronomy 2:37"> And the king of the sons of Ammon says to the messengers of Jephthah, “Because Israel took my land in his coming up out of Egypt, from Arnon, and to the Jabbok, and to the Jordan; and now, restore them in peace.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:11; Romans 12:18; Hebrews 12:14"> And Jephthah adds yet and sends messengers to the king of the sons of Ammon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Deuteronomy 2:9; Numbers 21:27–30"> and says to him, “Thus said Jephthah: Israel did not take the land of Moab, and the land of the sons of Ammon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 14:25; Deuteronomy 1:40; Numbers 20:1"> for in their coming up out of Egypt, Israel goes in the wilderness to the Red Sea, and comes to Kadesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 20:14–21; Deuteronomy 2:4–9; Numbers 20:1"> and Israel sends messengers to the king of Edom, saying, Please let me pass over through your land, and the king of Edom did not listen; and &#91;Israel&#93; has also sent to the king of Moab, and he has not been willing; and Israel abides in Kadesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:1–8; Numbers 22:36; Numbers 21:4–13"> and he goes through the wilderness, and goes around the land of Edom and the land of Moab, and comes in at the rising of the sun of the land of Moab, and they encamp beyond Arnon, and have not come into the border of Moab, for Arnon &#91;is&#93; the border of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Deuteronomy 2:26–36; Deuteronomy 3:1–17"> And Israel sends messengers to Sihon, king of the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and Israel says to him, Please let us pass over through your land, to my place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 13:15–32; Numbers 21:23–25; Psalms 136:17–21"> and Sihon has not trusted Israel to pass over through his border, and Sihon gathers all his people, and they encamp in Jahaz, and fight with Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and YHWH, God of Israel, gives Sihon and all his people into the hand of Israel, and they strike them, and Israel possesses all the land of the Amorite, the inhabitant of that land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36"> and they possess all the border of the Amorite from Arnon, and to the Jabbok, and from the wilderness, and to the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And now, YHWH, God of Israel, has dispossessed the Amorite from the presence of His people Israel, and you would possess it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 21:29; Psalms 78:55; Micah 4:5"> That which your god Chemosh causes you to possess—do you not possess it? And all that which our God YHWH has dispossessed from our presence—we possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Micah 6:5; Joshua 24:9–10; Deuteronomy 23:3–4"> And now, &#91;are&#93; you at all better than Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab? Did he strive with Israel at all? Did he fight against them at all?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36; Joshua 12:5; Judges 10:8"> In Israel’s dwelling in Heshbon and in its towns, and in Aroer and in its towns, and in all the cities which &#91;are&#93; by the sides of Arnon &#91;for&#93; three hundred years—and why have you not delivered them in that time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:15; Genesis 16:5; 1 Samuel 24:12"> And I have not sinned against you, and you are doing evil with me—to fight against me. YHWH, the Judge, judges between the sons of Israel and the sons of Ammon today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 14:11; Proverbs 16:18"> And the king of the sons of Ammon has not listened to the words of Jephthah which he sent to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 11:25; Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25"> and the Spirit of YHWH is on Jephthah, and he passes over Gilead and Manasseh, and passes over Mizpeh of Gilead, and he has passed over from Mizpeh of Gilead to the sons of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 28:20; 1 Samuel 1:11; Ecclesiastes 5:1–2"> And Jephthah vows a vow to YHWH and says, “If You give the sons of Ammon into my hand at all—</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 66:13–14; Leviticus 27:28–29; 1 Samuel 1:28"> then it has been, that which comes out from the doors of my house at all to meet me in my turning back in peace from the sons of Ammon—it has been for YHWH, or I have offered up a burnt-offering for it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 1:4; Judges 2:18; Judges 3:10"> And Jephthah passes over to the sons of Ammon to fight against them, and YHWH gives them into his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:17; Deuteronomy 2:36"> and he strikes them from Aroer, and to &#91;where&#93; you are going in to Minnith—twenty cities—and to the meadow of the vineyards, &#91;with&#93; a very great striking; and the sons of Ammon are humbled at the presence of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:4; Exodus 15:20; Judges 10:17"> And Jephthah comes into Mizpeh, to his house, and behold, his daughter is coming out to meet him with timbrels, and with choruses, and except her alone, he has no son or daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 14:7–9; Genesis 37:29–30; Genesis 42:36–38"> And it comes to pass, when he sees her, that he tears his garments and says, “Aah! My daughter, you have caused me to bend greatly, and you have been among those troubling me; and I have opened my mouth to YHWH, and I am not able to turn back.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:19; Philippians 2:30; Romans 16:4"> And she says to him, “My father, you have opened your mouth to YHWH, do to me as it has gone out from your mouth, after that YHWH has done vengeance for you on your enemies, on the sons of Ammon.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 1:25; 1 Samuel 1:6"> And she says to her father, “Let this thing be done to me; desist from me &#91;for&#93; two months, and I go on, and have gone down on the hills, and I weep for my virginity—I and my friends.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And he says, “Go”; and he sends her away &#91;for&#93; two months, and she goes, she and her friends, and she weeps for her virginity on the hills;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 66:3; Judges 11:31; Leviticus 27:28–29"> and it comes to pass at the end of two months that she turns back to her father, and he does to her his vow which he has vowed, and she did not know a man; and it is a statute in Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Kings 9:25; Judges 5:11"> from time to time the daughters of Israel go to talk to the daughter of Jephthah the Gileadite, four days in a year.</span>
