<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 2:21–22; Deuteronomy 7:22; Proverbs 17:3"> And these &#91;are&#93; the nations which YHWH left, to try Israel by them, all who have not known all the wars of Canaan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 3:5; Genesis 3:7; 1 Corinthians 9:26–27"> (only for the sake of the generations of the sons of Israel knowing, to teach them war, only those who formerly have not known them)—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 13:3; Joshua 13:5; Judges 10:7"> five princes of the Philistines, and all the Canaanite, and the Zidonian, and the Hivite inhabiting Mount Lebanon, from Mount Ba‘al-Hermon to the entering in of Hamath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 3:1; Judges 2:22; Exodus 15:25"> and they are to prove Israel by them, to know whether they obey the commands of YHWH that He commanded their fathers by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 106:34–38; Genesis 10:15–18; Exodus 3:8"> And the sons of Israel have dwelt in the midst of the Canaanite, the Hittite, and the Amorite, and the Perizzite, and the Hivite, and the Jebusite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3–4; Exodus 34:16; 1 Kings 11:1–5"> and take their daughters to them for wives, and have given their daughters to their sons, and they serve their gods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 34:13; 2 Kings 23:6; 1 Kings 18:19"> and the sons of Israel do evil in the eyes of YHWH, and forget their God YHWH, and serve the Ba‘alim and the Asheroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Habakkuk 3:7; Judges 4:9"> And the anger of YHWH burns against Israel, and He sells them into the hand of Chushan-Rishathaim king of Aram-Naharaim, and the sons of Israel serve Chushan-Rishathaim eight years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 3:15; Judges 1:13; Psalms 22:5"> and the sons of Israel cry to YHWH, and YHWH raises a savior to the sons of Israel, and he saves them—Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb’s younger brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 13:25; 1 Samuel 11:6; Judges 14:19"> and the Spirit of YHWH is on him, and he judges Israel, and goes out to battle, and YHWH gives Chushan-Rishathaim king of Aram into his hand, and his hand is strong against Chushan-Rishathaim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Judges 8:28; Joshua 11:23"> and the land rests forty years. And Othniel son of Kenaz dies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:9; Judges 2:19; Judges 2:14"> and the sons of Israel add to do evil in the eyes of YHWH; and YHWH strengthens Eglon king of Moab against Israel, because that they have done evil in the eyes of YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Deuteronomy 34:3; Judges 5:14"> and he gathers the sons of Ammon and Amalek to himself, and goes and strikes Israel, and they possess the city of palms;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:47–48; Deuteronomy 28:40; Leviticus 26:23–25"> and the sons of Israel serve Eglon king of Moab eighteen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 3:9; Psalms 78:34; Isaiah 36:16"> And the sons of Israel cry to YHWH, and YHWH raises a savior to them, Ehud son of Gera, a Benjamite (a man &#91;with&#93; his right hand bound), and the sons of Israel send a present by his hand to Eglon king of Moab;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Song of Songs 3:8; Revelation 2:12; Psalms 149:6"> and Ehud makes a sword for himself, and it has two mouths (its length &#91;is&#93; a cubit), and he girds it under his long robe on his right thigh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:20; Job 15:27; Jeremiah 50:11"> and he brings the present near to Eglon king of Moab, and Eglon &#91;is&#93; a very fat man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And it comes to pass, when he has finished to bring the present near, that he sends the people carrying the present away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 4:20; Judges 3:20; Acts 23:18–19"> and he himself has turned back from the carved images which &#91;are&#93; at Gilgal and says, “I have a secret word for you, O king”; and he says, “Hush!” And all those standing by him go out from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:7; Amos 3:15; Psalms 29:1"> And Ehud has come to him, and he is sitting in the cool upper chamber which he has for himself, and Ehud says, “I have a word of God for you”; and he rises from off the throne;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 13:3; Job 20:25; 1 Samuel 15:33"> and Ehud puts forth his left hand, and takes the sword from off his right thigh, and thrusts it into his belly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and the hilt also goes in after the blade, and the fat shuts on the blade, that he has not drawn the sword out of his belly, and he goes out &#91;through&#93; the antechamber &#91;&#91;or and the dung came out&#93;&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Ehud goes out at the porch, and shuts the doors of the upper chamber on him, and has bolted &#91;it&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:3"> and he has gone out, and his servants have come in, and look, and behold, the doors of the upper chamber are bolted, and they say, “He is surely covering his feet, &#91;relieving himself,&#93; in the cool inner chamber.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And they stay until confounded, and behold, he is not opening the doors of the upper chamber, and they take the key, and open, and behold, their lord is fallen to the earth—dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Ehud escaped during their lingering, and has passed by the images, and escapes to Seirath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 6:34; 1 Samuel 13:3; Judges 5:14"> And it comes to pass, in his coming in, that he blows with a horn in the hill-country of Ephraim, and the sons of Israel go down with him from the hill-country, and he before them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 12:5; Judges 7:9; Judges 7:15"> and he says to them, “Pursue after me, for YHWH has given your enemies, the Moabites, into your hand”; and they go down after him, and capture the passages of the Jordan toward Moab, and have not permitted a man to pass over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Judges 3:17; Psalms 17:10"> And they strike Moab at that time, about ten thousand men, all robust, and everyone a man of valor, and no man has escaped,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 3:11; Judges 5:31"> and Moab is humbled in that day under the hand of Israel; and the land rests &#91;for&#93; eighty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 5:6; 1 Corinthians 1:17; 1 Samuel 17:50"> And after him has been Shamgar son of Anath, and he strikes the Philistines—six hundred men—with an ox-goad, and he also saves Israel.</span>
