<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; Joshua 19:43; 1 John 2:16"> And Samson goes down to Timnath, and sees a woman in Timnath of the daughters of the Philistines,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 34:4; Genesis 21:21; Genesis 24:2–3"> and comes up and declares &#91;it&#93; to his father and to his mother, and says, “I have seen a woman in Timnath, of the daughters of the Philistines; and now, take her for me for a wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 15:18; 1 Samuel 17:36; 1 Samuel 14:6"> And his father says to him—also his mother, “Is there no woman among the daughters of your brothers, and among all my people, that you are going to take a woman from the uncircumcised Philistines?” And Samson says to his father, “Take her for me, for she is right in my eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:20; Psalms 115:3; Judges 13:1"> And his father and his mother have not known that it &#91;is&#93; from YHWH, that He is seeking a meeting of the Philistines; and the Philistines are ruling over Israel at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Samson goes down—also his father and his mother, to Timnath, and they come to the vineyards of Timnath, and behold, a lion’s whelp roars at meeting him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25; 1 Samuel 11:6"> and the Spirit of YHWH prospers over him, and he tears it as the tearing of a kid, and there is nothing in his hand, and he has not declared to his father and to his mother that which he has done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And he goes down and speaks to the woman, and she is right in the eyes of Samson;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 1:20; Genesis 29:21"> and he turns back after &#91;some&#93; days to take her, and turns aside to see the carcass of the lion, and behold, a swarm of bees &#91;are&#93; in the body of the lion—and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:25–30; Proverbs 25:15"> And he takes it down on to his hands, and goes on, going and eating; and he goes to his father, and to his mother, and gives to them, and they eat, and he has not declared to them that he took down the honey from the body of the lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 29:22; Revelation 19:9; Esther 1:7–22"> And his father goes down to the woman, and Samson makes a banquet there, for so the young men does;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 9:15; 1 Samuel 16:6; 1 Samuel 10:23"> and it comes to pass, when they see him, that they take thirty companions, and they are with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 10:1; Ezekiel 17:2; Genesis 45:22"> And Samson says to them, “Now let me put forth a riddle to you; if you certainly declare it to me &#91;in&#93; the seven days of the banquet, and have found &#91;it&#93; out, then I have given thirty linen shirts and thirty changes of garments to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and if you are not able to declare &#91;it&#93; to me, then you have given thirty linen shirts and thirty changes of garments to me.” And they say to him, “Put forth your riddle, and we hear it!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:2–4; 1 Kings 17:6; Romans 8:37"> And he says to them: “Out of the eater came forth something to eat, &#124;&#124; And out of the strong came forth &#91;something&#93; sweet”; and they were not able to declare the riddle &#91;in&#93; three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 16:5; Judges 15:6; Proverbs 6:26"> And it comes to pass, on the seventh day, that they say to Samson’s wife, “Entice your husband, that he declare the riddle to us, lest we burn you and the house of your father with fire; have you not called for us &#91;here&#93; to rob us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 16:15; Genesis 2:24"> And Samson’s wife weeps for it and says, “You have only hated me, and have not loved me; you have put forth the riddle to the sons of my people, but have not declared it to me”; and he says to her, “Behold, I have not declared &#91;it&#93; to my father and to my mother—and I declare &#91;it&#93; to you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 16:16; Job 2:9; Judges 16:13"> And she weeps for it the seven days &#91;in&#93; which their banquet has been, and it comes to pass on the seventh day that he declares &#91;it&#93; to her, for she has distressed him; and she declares the riddle to the sons of her people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the men of the city say to him on the seventh day, before the sun goes in: “What &#91;is&#93; sweeter than honey? And what &#91;is&#93; stronger than a lion?” And he says to them: “Unless you had plowed with my heifer, &#124;&#124; You had not found out my riddle.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:6; Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25"> And the Spirit of YHWH prospers over him, and he goes down to Ashkelon, and strikes down thirty of their men, and takes their armor, and gives the changes to those declaring the riddle; and his anger burns, and he goes up to the house of his father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 15:2; John 3:29; Psalms 55:12–13"> and Samson’s wife becomes his companion’s, who had attended to him.</span>
