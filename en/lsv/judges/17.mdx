<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 18:2; Joshua 17:14–18; Judges 10:1"> And there is a man of the hill-country of Ephraim, and his name &#91;is&#93; Micah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:25; Ruth 3:10; 1 Samuel 23:21"> and he says to his mother, “The eleven hundred pieces of silver which have been taken of yours, and &#91;of which&#93; you have sworn, and also spoken in my ears; behold, the silver &#91;is&#93; with me, I have taken it”; and his mother says, “Blessed &#91;is&#93; my son of YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:4; Exodus 20:23; Leviticus 19:4"> And he gives back the eleven hundred pieces of silver to his mother, and his mother says, “I had certainly sanctified the silver to YHWH, from my hand, for my son, to make a carved image, and a molten image; and now, I give it back to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6–7; Jeremiah 10:9–10"> And he gives back the money to his mother, and his mother takes two hundred pieces of silver, and gives them to a refiner, and he makes them a carved image, and a molten image, and it is in the house of Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 18:14; Judges 8:27; Genesis 31:19"> As for the man Micah, he has a house of gods, and he makes an ephod, and teraphim, and consecrates the hand of one of his sons, and he is for a priest to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:8; Proverbs 14:12; Judges 21:25"> in those days there is no king in Israel, each does that which is right in his own eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:1; Ruth 1:1–2"> And there is a young man of Beth-Lehem-Judah, of the family of Judah, and he &#91;is&#93; a Levite, and he &#91;is&#93; a sojourner there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:10–11; Judges 17:11; Joshua 24:33"> And the man goes out of the city, out of Beth-Lehem-Judah, to sojourn where he finds, and comes to the hill-country of Ephraim, to the house of Micah, to work his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Micah says to him, “Where do you come from?” And he says to him, “I &#91;am&#93; a Levite of Beth-Lehem-Judah, and I am going to sojourn where I find.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:19; Judges 18:19–20; 1 Timothy 6:10"> And Micah says to him, “Dwell with me, and be for a father and for a priest to me, and I give ten pieces of silver to you for the days, and a suit of garments, and your sustenance”; and the Levite goes &#91;in&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the Levite is willing to dwell with the man, and the young man is to him as one of his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 18:30; Numbers 16:8–10; Numbers 16:5"> And Micah consecrates the hand of the Levite, and the young man is for a priest to him, and he is in the house of Micah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 10:2–3; Proverbs 14:12; John 16:2"> and Micah says, “Now I have known that YHWH does good to me, for the Levite has been for a priest to me.”</span>
