<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 4:32; Leviticus 23:24; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> And the seventh month comes, and the sons of Israel &#91;are&#93; in the cities, and the people are gathered, as one man, to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:17; Ezra 2:2; Haggai 1:1"> And Jeshua son of Jozadak rises, and his brothers the priests, and Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, and his brothers, and they build the altar of the God of Israel, to cause to ascend on it burnt-offerings, as it is written in the Law of Moses, the man of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 4:4; Numbers 28:2–8; Ezra 8:21–22"> And they establish the altar on its bases, because of the fear on them of the peoples of the lands, and he causes burnt-offerings to ascend on it to YHWH, burnt-offerings for the morning and for the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:14–17; Numbers 29:12–38; Exodus 23:16"> And they make the Celebration of the Shelters as it is written, and the burnt-offering of the day daily in number according to the ordinance, the matter of a day in its day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 29:39; Numbers 28:19; Exodus 29:38–42"> and after this a continual burnt-offering, and for new moons, and for all appointed times of YHWH that are sanctified; and for everyone who is willingly offering a willing-offering to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> From the first day of the seventh month they have begun to cause burnt-offerings to ascend to YHWH, and the temple of YHWH has not been founded,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 5:6; Acts 9:36; Acts 12:20"> and they give money to hewers and to craftsmen, and food, and drink, and oil to Zidonians and to Tyrians, to bring in cedar-trees from Lebanon to the Sea of Joppa, according to the permission of Cyrus king of Persia concerning them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 4:3; 1 Chronicles 23:4; 1 Chronicles 23:24–32"> And in the second year of their coming to the house of God, to Jerusalem, in the second month, began Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, and Jeshua son of Jozadak, and the remnant of their brothers the priests and the Levites, and all those coming from the captivity to Jerusalem, and they appoint the Levites from a son of twenty years and upward, to overlook the work of the house of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezra 2:40; Ezra 3:8; Nehemiah 7:43"> And Jeshua stands, &#91;and&#93; his sons, and his brothers, Kadmiel and his sons, sons of Judah together, to overlook those doing the work in the house of God; the sons of Henadad, &#91;and&#93; their sons and their brothers the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:1–7; 1 Chronicles 15:27; 1 Chronicles 16:37"> And those building have founded the temple of YHWH, and they appoint the priests, clothed, with trumpets, and the Levites, sons of Asaph, with cymbals, to praise YHWH, by means of &#91;the instruments of&#93; David king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:34; Psalms 106:1; Psalms 107:1"> And they respond in praising and in giving thanks to YHWH, “For &#91;He is&#93; good, for His kindness &#91;is&#93; for all time over Israel!” And all the people have shouted &#91;with&#93; a great shout in giving praise to YHWH, because the house of YHWH has been founded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Haggai 2:3; Matthew 13:31–32; Isaiah 60:22"> And many of the priests, and the Levites, and the heads of the fathers, the aged men who had seen the first house—in this house being founded before their eyes—are weeping with a loud voice, and many with a shout, in joy, lifting up the voice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 19:37–40; 1 Samuel 4:5; Nehemiah 12:43"> and the people are not discerning the noise of the shout of joy from the noise of the weeping of the people, for the people are shouting &#91;with&#93; a great shout, and the noise has been heard to a distance.</span>
