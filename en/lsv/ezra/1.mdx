<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; 2 Chronicles 36:22–23; Jeremiah 25:12–14"> And in the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, at the completion of the word of YHWH from the mouth of Jeremiah, has YHWH awoken the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia, and he causes an intimation to pass over into all his kingdom, and also in writing, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 45:12–13; Isaiah 44:26–1; Daniel 5:23"> “Thus said Cyrus king of Persia: YHWH, God of the heavens, has given all kingdoms of the earth to me, and He has laid a charge on me to build a house for Him in Jerusalem, that &#91;is&#93; in Judah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 6:26; Jeremiah 10:10; Isaiah 45:5"> who &#91;is&#93; among you of all His people? His God is with him, and he goes up to Jerusalem, that &#91;is&#93; in Judah, and builds the house of YHWH, God of Israel (He &#91;alone is&#93; God), that &#91;is&#93; in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:3; Ezra 7:16–18; Ezra 2:68–70"> And everyone who is left, of any of the places where he &#91;is&#93; a sojourner, the men of his place assist him with silver, and with gold, and with goods, and with beasts, along with a free-will offering for the house of God, that &#91;is&#93; in Jerusalem.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezra 1:1; 2 Chronicles 36:22; Philippians 2:13"> And heads of the fathers of Judah and Benjamin rise, and the priests and the Levites, even everyone whose spirit God has awoken, to go up to build the house of YHWH, that &#91;is&#93; in Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Ezra 1:4; Ezra 8:33"> and all those around them have strengthened &#91;them&#93; with their hands, with vessels of silver, with gold, with goods, and with beasts, and with precious things, apart from all that has been offered willingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:7; Ezra 5:14; 2 Kings 24:13"> And King Cyrus has brought out the vessels of the house of YHWH that Nebuchadnezzar has brought out of Jerusalem, and puts them in the house of his gods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 5:14; Zechariah 4:6–10; Ezra 1:11"> indeed, Cyrus king of Persia brings them out by the hand of Mithredath the treasurer, and numbers them to Sheshbazzar the prince of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 7:50; Numbers 7:19–89; 2 Chronicles 4:8"> And this &#91;is&#93; their number: dishes of gold thirty, dishes of silver one thousand, knives twenty-nine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> basins of gold thirty, basins of silver (seconds) four hundred and ten, other vessels one thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:19–21; Romans 9:23; Matthew 1:11–12"> All the vessels of gold and of silver &#91;are&#93; five thousand and four hundred; the whole has Sheshbazzar brought up with the going up of the expulsion from Babylon to Jerusalem.</span>
