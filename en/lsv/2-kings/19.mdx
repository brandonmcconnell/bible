<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 21:27; 2 Kings 18:37; 2 Chronicles 32:20–22"> And it comes to pass, at King Hezekiah’s hearing, that he tears his garments, and covers himself with sackcloth, and enters the house of YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; Isaiah 2:1; Matthew 4:14"> and sends Eliakim, who &#91;is&#93; over the house, and Shebna the scribe, and the elderly of the priests, covering themselves with sackcloth, to Isaiah the prophet, son of Amoz,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:5–7; Isaiah 66:9; Hosea 13:13"> and they say to him, “Thus said Hezekiah: This day &#91;is&#93; a day of distress, and rebuke, and despising; for sons have come to the birth, and there is not power to bring forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Romans 9:27; Joshua 14:12"> It may be your God YHWH hears all the words of the chief of the butlers with which the king of Asshur his lord has sent him to reproach the living God, and has decided concerning the words that your God YHWH has heard, and you have lifted up prayer for the remnant that is found.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the servants of King Hezekiah come to Isaiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 18:35; 2 Kings 18:17; Isaiah 37:6–38"> and Isaiah says to them, “Thus you say to your lord, Thus said YHWH: Do not be afraid because of the words that you have heard, with which the servants of the king of Asshur have reviled Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Obadiah 1:1; 2 Kings 7:6; Psalms 18:14–15"> Behold, I am giving a spirit in him, and he has heard a report, and has turned back to his land, and I have caused him to fall by the sword in his land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 18:14; Joshua 10:29; Joshua 12:11"> And the chief of the butlers turns back and finds the king of Asshur fighting against Libnah, for he has heard that he has journeyed from Lachish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:27; Isaiah 37:9; 2 Kings 18:17"> And he hears concerning Tirhakah king of Cush, saying, “Behold, he has come out to fight with you”; and he turns and sends messengers to Hezekiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 18:5; 2 Kings 18:29–30; 2 Chronicles 32:15–19"> “Thus you speak to Hezekiah king of Judah, saying, Do not let your God in whom you are trusting lift you up, saying, Jerusalem is not given into the hand of the king of Asshur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 10:8–11; 2 Chronicles 32:13–14; 2 Kings 19:17–18"> Behold, you have heard that which the kings of Asshur have done to all the lands—to devote them; and are you delivered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 17:6; Genesis 11:31; Ezekiel 27:23"> Did the gods of the nations deliver them whom my fathers destroyed—Gozan, and Haran, and Rezeph, and the sons of Eden, who &#91;are&#93; in Thelassar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 18:34; 2 Kings 17:24; Isaiah 11:11"> Where &#91;is&#93; the king of Hamath, and the king of Arpad, and the king of the city of Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 37:14; Psalms 91:1–2; Psalms 123:1–4"> And Hezekiah takes the letters out of the hand of the messengers and reads them, and goes up to the house of YHWH, and Hezekiah spreads it before YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; 2 Kings 5:15; Daniel 4:34–35"> And Hezekiah prays before YHWH and says, “O YHWH, God of Israel, inhabiting the cherubim, You &#91;are&#93; God Himself—You alone—to all the kingdoms of the earth: You have made the heavens and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:40; Psalms 31:2; 2 Kings 19:4"> Incline, O YHWH, Your ear, and hear; open, O YHWH, Your eyes, and see; and hear the words of Sennacherib with which he has sent him to reproach the living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; Job 9:2; 1 Corinthians 14:25"> Truly, O YHWH, kings of Asshur have laid waste the nations and their land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 17:29; Isaiah 44:9–20; Jeremiah 10:3–9"> and have put their gods into fire, for they &#91;are&#93; no gods, but work of the hands of man, wood and stone, and destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; 1 Kings 8:43; Psalms 67:1–2"> And now, O our God YHWH, please save us out of his hand, and all kingdoms of the earth know that You &#91;are&#93; YHWH God—You alone.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 20:5; 2 Chronicles 32:20–21; Jeremiah 33:3"> And Isaiah son of Amoz sends to Hezekiah, saying, “Thus said YHWH, God of Israel: That which you have prayed to Me concerning Sennacherib king of Asshur I have heard;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Lamentations 2:13; Job 16:4; Jeremiah 14:17"> this &#91;is&#93; the word that YHWH spoke concerning him: Trampled on you—laughed at you &#124;&#124; Has the virgin daughter of Zion; Shaken the head behind you &#124;&#124; Has the daughter of Jerusalem!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Psalms 71:22; Isaiah 30:11–12"> Whom have you reproached and reviled? And against whom lifted up a voice? Indeed, you lift up your eyes on high—Against the Holy One of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Psalms 20:7; Isaiah 10:18"> By the hand of your messengers &#124;&#124; You have reproached the Lord, and say, &#124;&#124; In the multitude of my chariots I have come up to a high place of mountains—The sides of Lebanon, &#124;&#124; And I cut down the height of its cedars, &#124;&#124; The choice of its firs, &#124;&#124; And I enter the lodging of its extremity, &#124;&#124; The forest of its Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 19:6; Exodus 15:9; 2 Samuel 17:13"> I have dug, and drunk strange waters, &#124;&#124; And I dry up with the sole of my steps &#124;&#124; All floods of a bulwark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Isaiah 10:5–6; Isaiah 37:26–27"> Have you not heard from afar &#91;that&#93; I made it, &#124;&#124; From days of old that I formed it? Now I have brought it in, &#124;&#124; And it becomes a desolation, &#124;&#124; Ruinous heaps &#91;are&#93; fortified cities,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Psalms 102:11; Jeremiah 51:32"> And their inhabitants &#91;are&#93; feeble-handed, &#124;&#124; They were broken down, and are dried up, &#124;&#124; They have been the herb of the field, &#124;&#124; And the greenness of the tender grass, &#124;&#124; Grass of the roofs, &#124;&#124; And blasted grain—before it has risen up!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 37:28–29; Jeremiah 23:23–24; Psalms 139:1–11"> And your sitting down, and your going out, &#124;&#124; And your coming in, I have known, &#124;&#124; And your anger toward Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:4; Ezekiel 38:4; 2 Kings 19:33"> Because of your anger toward Me, &#124;&#124; And your noise—it came up into My ears, &#124;&#124; I have put My hook in your nose, &#124;&#124; And My bridle in your lips, &#124;&#124; And have caused you to turn back &#124;&#124; In the way in which you came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 25:20–22; 2 Kings 20:8–9; Exodus 3:12"> And this &#91;is&#93; the sign to you, &#124;&#124; Food of the year &#91;is&#93; the spontaneous growth, &#124;&#124; And in the second year the self-produced, &#124;&#124; And in the third year you sow, and reap, &#124;&#124; And plant vineyards, and eat their fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 19:4; Isaiah 27:6; 2 Chronicles 32:22–23"> And it has continued—The escaped of the house of Judah &#124;&#124; That has been left—to take root beneath, &#124;&#124; And has made fruit upward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 9:7; Romans 11:5; Romans 9:27"> For a remnant goes out from Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And an escape from Mount Zion; The zeal of YHWH &#91;of Hosts&#93; does this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–10; 2 Samuel 20:15; Isaiah 37:33–35"> Therefore, thus said YHWH, &#124;&#124; Concerning the king of Asshur: He does not come into this city, &#124;&#124; Nor does he shoot an arrow there, &#124;&#124; Nor does he come before it with shield, &#124;&#124; Nor does he pour out a mound against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; 2 Kings 19:36"> In the way that he comes in—In it he turns back, &#124;&#124; And to this city he does not come in, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH—</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 11:12–13; 2 Kings 20:6; 1 Kings 15:4"> And I have covered over this city, &#124;&#124; To save it for My own sake, &#124;&#124; And for the sake of My servant David.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:21–22; 2 Samuel 24:16; Exodus 12:29–30"> And it comes to pass, in that night, that a messenger of YHWH goes out, and strikes one hundred eighty-five thousand in the camp of Asshur, and they rise early in the morning, and behold, all of them &#91;are&#93; dead corpses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jonah 1:2; 2 Kings 19:7; 2 Kings 19:33"> And Sennacherib king of Asshur journeys, and goes, and turns back, and dwells in Nineveh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Ezra 4:2; Genesis 8:4; 2 Kings 19:7"> and it comes to pass, he is bowing himself in the house of his god Nisroch, and &#91;his sons&#93; Adramelech and Sharezar have struck him with the sword, and they have escaped to the land of Ararat, and his son Esar-Haddon reigns in his stead.</span>
