<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 1:17; 1 Kings 22:51; 2 Kings 8:16"> And Jehoram son of Ahab has reigned over Israel, in Samaria, in the eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and he reigns twelve years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 10:18; 2 Kings 10:26–28; 1 Kings 21:25"> and does evil in the eyes of YHWH, only not like his father, and like his mother, and he turns aside the standing-pillar of Ba‘al that his father made;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 14:16; 1 Kings 15:34; 2 Kings 15:18"> surely he has cleaved to the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat that he caused Israel to sin; he has not turned aside from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:2; Genesis 26:13–14; Job 1:3"> And Mesha king of Moab was a sheep-master, and he rendered to the king of Israel one hundred thousand lambs and one hundred thousand rams, &#91;with&#93; wool,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 1:1; 2 Kings 8:20; 2 Chronicles 21:8–10"> and it comes to pass at the death of Ahab, that the king of Moab transgresses against the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:1–25; 1 Samuel 11:8; 1 Samuel 15:4"> And King Jehoram goes out from Samaria in that day, and inspects all Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 22:4; 2 Chronicles 22:10–12; 2 Chronicles 18:3"> and goes and sends to Jehoshaphat king of Judah, saying, “The king of Moab has transgressed against me; do you go with me to Moab for battle?” And he says, “I go up, as I, so you; as my people, so your people; as my horses, so your horses.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Malachi 1:2–3; Numbers 21:4"> And he says, “Where &#91;is&#93; this—the way we go up?” And he says, “The way of the wilderness of Edom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 22:47; Numbers 20:2; Exodus 17:1"> And the king of Israel goes, and the king of Judah, and the king of Edom, and they turn around the way seven days, and there has been no water for the camp, and for the livestock that &#91;are&#93; at their feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 78:34–36; Isaiah 51:20; Isaiah 8:21"> and the king of Israel says, “Aah! For YHWH has called for these three kings to give them into the hand of Moab.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 22:7; 1 Kings 19:21; 2 Kings 3:3"> And Jehoshaphat says, “Is there not a prophet of YHWH here, and we seek YHWH by him?” And one of the servants of the king of Israel answers and says, “Here &#91;is&#93; Elisha son of Shaphat, who poured water on the hands of Elijah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 5:15; 2 Kings 2:21; Isaiah 60:14"> And Jehoshaphat says, “The word of YHWH is with him”; and the king of Israel goes down to him, and Jehoshaphat, and the king of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 22:6; 1 Kings 18:19; 1 Kings 22:10–11"> And Elisha says to the king of Israel, “And what do I &#91;have to do&#93; with you? Go to the prophets of your father, and to the prophets of your mother”; and the king of Israel says to him, “No, for YHWH has called for these three kings to give them into the hand of Moab.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 5:16; 1 Kings 17:1; 2 Chronicles 19:3–4"> And Elisha says, “YHWH of Hosts lives, before whom I have stood; for unless I am lifting up the face of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, I do not look to you, nor see you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:23; Ephesians 5:18–19; Ezekiel 1:3"> and now, bring a musician to me”; and it has been, at the playing of the musician, that the hand of YHWH is on him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 2:16–18; 2 Kings 4:3"> and he says, “Thus said YHWH: Make this valley ditches—ditches;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 107:35; Isaiah 41:17–18; Isaiah 48:21"> for thus said YHWH: You do not see wind, nor do you see rain, and that valley is full of water, and you have drunk—you, and your livestock, and your beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:27; Luke 1:37; Jeremiah 32:17"> And this has been light in the eyes of YHWH, and he has given Moab into your hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 13:17; Deuteronomy 20:19–20; Judges 6:16"> and you have struck every fortified city, and every choice city, and you cause every good tree to fall, and you stop all fountains of waters, and you mar every good portion with stones.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 29:39–40; Daniel 9:21; 1 Kings 18:36"> And it comes to pass in the morning, at the ascending of the &#91;morning&#93; present, that behold, waters are coming in from the way of Edom, and the land is filled with the waters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 6:14; 1 Kings 20:11"> and all of Moab has heard that the kings have come up to fight against them, and they are called together, from everyone girding on a girdle and upward, and they stand by the border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And they rise early in the morning, and the sun has shone on the waters, and the Moabites see, from opposite &#91;them&#93;, &#91;that&#93; the waters &#91;are&#93; red as blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Exodus 15:9; Isaiah 10:14"> and say, “This &#91;is&#93; blood; the kings have been surely destroyed, and they each strike his neighbor; and now for spoil, Moab!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3–4; Joshua 8:20–22; Judges 20:40–46"> And they come to the camp of Israel, and Israel rises, and strikes the Moabites, and they flee from their face; and they enter into Moab, so as to strike Moab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 16:7; Jeremiah 48:36; Jeremiah 48:31"> and they break down the cities, and they each cast his stone &#91;on&#93; every good portion, and have filled it, and they stop every fountain of water, and they cause every good tree to fall—until one had left its stones in Kir-Haraseth, and the slingers go around and strike it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 3:9; Amos 2:1"> And the king of Moab sees that the battle has been too strong for him, and he takes with him seven hundred men drawing sword, to cleave through to the king of Edom, and they have not been able,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 6:7; Deuteronomy 12:31; Judges 11:31"> and he takes his son, the firstborn who reigns in his stead, and causes him to ascend &#91;as&#93; a burnt-offering on the wall, and there is great wrath against Israel, and they journey from off him, and return to the land.</span>
