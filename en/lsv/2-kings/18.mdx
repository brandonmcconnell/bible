<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:27–1; Matthew 1:9–10; 1 Chronicles 3:13"> And it comes to pass, in the third year of Hoshea son of Elah, king of Israel, Hezekiah son of Ahaz, king of Judah, has reigned;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:1–2"> he was a son of twenty-five years in his reigning, and he has reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother &#91;is&#93; Abi daughter of Zechariah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 20:3; 1 Kings 11:4; 2 Chronicles 31:20–21"> And he does that which &#91;is&#93; right in the eyes of YHWH, according to all that his father David did,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:1; Numbers 21:8–9; 2 Kings 18:22"> he has turned aside the high places, and broken in pieces the standing-pillars, and cut down the Asherah, and beaten down the bronze serpent that Moses made, for up to these days the sons of Israel were making incense to it, and he calls it “a piece of bronze.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 23:25; 2 Kings 19:10; Matthew 27:43"> In YHWH, God of Israel, he has trusted, and after him there has not been like him among all the kings of Judah, nor &#91;among any&#93; who were before him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 23:8; Deuteronomy 10:20; John 14:15"> and he cleaves to YHWH, he has not turned aside from after Him, and keeps His commands that YHWH commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Genesis 39:2–3; 2 Kings 16:7"> And YHWH has been with him; in every place where he goes out he acts wisely, and he rebels against the king of Asshur, and has not served him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 14:29; 2 Kings 17:9; 2 Chronicles 28:18"> he has struck the Philistines, even to Gaza and its borders, from a tower of watchers to the fortified city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 10:14; 2 Kings 17:3–23; 2 Kings 18:1"> And it comes to pass, in the fourth year of King Hezekiah—it &#91;is&#93; the seventh year of Hoshea son of Elah, king of Israel—Shalmaneser king of Asshur has come up against Samaria and lays siege to it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 6:7; Amos 9:1–4; Micah 1:6–9"> and they capture it at the end of three years; in the sixth year of Hezekiah—it &#91;is&#93; the ninth year of Hoshea king of Israel—Samaria has been captured,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; 2 Kings 17:6; Isaiah 37:12"> and the king of Asshur removes Israel to Asshur, and placed them in Halah, and in Habor &#91;by&#93; the river Gozan, and &#91;in&#93; cities of the Medes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17; 1 Peter 2:8; Micah 3:4"> because that they have not listened to the voice of their God YHWH, and transgress His covenant—all that He commanded Moses, servant of YHWH—indeed, they have not listened nor done &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 36:1–22; 2 Chronicles 32:1–23; Isaiah 7:17–25"> And in the fourteenth year of King Hezekiah, Sennacherib king of Asshur has come up against all the fortified cities of Judah, and seizes them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 18:7; Luke 14:32; 1 Kings 20:4"> and Hezekiah king of Judah sends to the king of Asshur at Lachish, saying, “I have sinned, turn back from off me; that which you put on me I bear”; and the king of Asshur lays on Hezekiah king of Judah three hundred talents of silver and thirty talents of gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 16:8; 1 Kings 15:18–19; 2 Kings 12:18"> and Hezekiah gives all the silver that is found in the house of YHWH and in the treasures of the house of the king;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:3; 1 Kings 6:31–35"> at that time Hezekiah has cut off the doors of the temple of YHWH, and the pillars that Hezekiah king of Judah had overlaid, and gives them to the king of Asshur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 20:1; 2 Kings 20:20; Isaiah 7:3"> And the king of Asshur sends Tartan, and the chief of the eunuchs, and the chief of the butlers, from Lachish, to King Hezekiah, with a heavy force, to Jerusalem, and they go up and come to Jerusalem, and they go up, and come in and stand by the conduit of the upper pool that &#91;is&#93; in the highway of the fuller’s field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 19:2; Isaiah 36:3; 2 Samuel 8:16–17"> And they call to the king, and Eliakim son of Hilkiah, who &#91;is&#93; over the house, and Shebna the scribe, and Joah son of Asaph, the remembrancer, go out to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 36:4; 2 Kings 19:10; Isaiah 10:8–14"> And the chief of the butlers says to them, “Now say to Hezekiah, Thus said the great king, the king of Asshur: What &#91;is&#93; this confidence in which you have confided?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30–31"> You have said: Only a word of the lips! Counsel and might &#91;are&#93; for battle; now, on whom have you trusted that you have rebelled against me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 30:7; Ezekiel 29:6–7; Isaiah 30:2–3"> Now behold, you have trusted for yourself on the staff of this broken reed, on Egypt; which a man leans on, and it has gone into his hand and pierced it! So &#91;is&#93; Pharaoh king of Egypt to all those trusting on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:1; 2 Kings 18:4–5; Matthew 27:43"> And when you say to me, We have trusted in our God YHWH, is it not He whose high places and whose altars Hezekiah has turned aside, and says to Judah and to Jerusalem, You bow yourselves before this altar in Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:42; 1 Kings 20:10; Nehemiah 4:2–5"> And now, please give a pledge for yourself to my lord the king of Asshur, and I give two thousand horses to you, if you are able to give riders for yourself on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 10:8; Deuteronomy 17:16; Isaiah 36:6"> And how do you turn back the face of one captain of the least of the servants of my lord, that you trust for yourself on Egypt for chariot and for horsemen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 19:6; Isaiah 10:5–6; 2 Kings 19:22–37"> Have I now come up without YHWH against this place to destroy it? YHWH said to me, Go up against this land, and you have destroyed it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezra 4:7; Daniel 2:4; Isaiah 36:11–12"> And Eliakim son of Hilkiah says—and Shebna and Joah—to the chief of the butlers, “Please speak to your servants &#91;in&#93; Aramaic, for we are understanding, but do not speak with us &#91;in&#93; Jewish, in the ears of the people who &#91;are&#93; on the wall.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 73:8; Lamentations 4:5; Ezekiel 4:15"> And the chief of the butlers says to them, “For your lord, and to you, has my lord sent me to speak these words? Is it not for the men—those sitting on the wall—to eat their own dung and to drink their own water, with you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 36:13–18; Isaiah 10:8–13; Ezekiel 29:3"> And the chief of the butlers stands and calls with a great voice &#91;in&#93; Jewish, and speaks and says, “Hear a word of the great king, the king of Asshur—</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:15; 2 Thessalonians 2:8; 2 Chronicles 32:11"> thus said the king: Do not let Hezekiah lift you up, for he is not able to deliver you out of his hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 125:1–2; Luke 23:35; 2 Kings 19:32–34"> and do not let Hezekiah make you trust in YHWH, saying, YHWH certainly delivers us, and this city is not given into the hand of the king of Asshur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 4:25; 1 Kings 4:20; Zechariah 3:10"> Do not listen to Hezekiah, for thus said the king of Asshur: Make a blessing with me, and come out to me, and each eat of his vine, and each &#91;eat&#93; of his fig tree, and each drink the waters of his own well,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Exodus 3:8; Deuteronomy 11:12"> until my coming in, and I have taken you to a land like your own land, a land of grain and new wine, a land of bread and vineyards, a land of olive oil, and honey; and live, and do not die; and do not listen to Hezekiah when he persuades you, saying, YHWH delivers us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 10:10–11; 2 Kings 19:12–13; 2 Chronicles 32:19"> Have each of the gods of the nations ever delivered his land out of the hand of the king of Asshur?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 10:9; 2 Kings 17:23–33; Jeremiah 49:23"> Where &#91;are&#93; the gods of Hamath and Arpad? Where &#91;are&#93; the gods of Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah, that they have delivered Samaria out of my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Daniel 3:15; Isaiah 10:15; Exodus 5:2"> Who &#91;are they&#93; among all the gods of the lands that have delivered their land out of my hand, that YHWH delivers Jerusalem out of my hand?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Proverbs 9:7; Matthew 7:6"> And the people have kept silent, and have not answered him a word, for the command of the king is, saying, “Do not answer him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Kings 22:11; Genesis 37:29; 2 Kings 5:7"> And Eliakim son of Hilkiah, who &#91;is&#93; over the house, comes in, and Shebna the scribe, and Joah son of Asaph, the remembrancer, to Hezekiah, with torn garments, and they declare to him the words of the chief of the butlers.</span>
