<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 8:30; 1 Kings 13:32; 1 Kings 21:8–14"> And Ahab has seventy sons in Samaria, and Jehu writes letters, and sends &#91;them&#93; to Samaria, to the heads of Jezreel, the elderly, and to those supporting Ahab, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 5:6"> “And now, at the coming in of this letter to you, and sons of your lord &#91;are&#93; with you, and the chariots and the horses, and a fortified city, and the armor, &#91;are&#93; with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8–9; 1 Kings 12:20–21; 2 Samuel 2:12–17"> that you have seen the best and the most upright of the sons of your lord, and have set &#91;him&#93; on the throne of his father, and fight for the house of your lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 9:24; 2 Kings 9:27; Isaiah 27:4"> And they fear very greatly, and say, “Behold, the two kings have not stood before him, and how do we stand—we?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 20:32; 1 Kings 20:4; Joshua 9:11"> And he who &#91;is&#93; over the house, and he who &#91;is&#93; over the city, and the elderly, and those supporting, send to Jehu, saying, “We &#91;are&#93; your servants, and all that you say to us we do; we do not make anyone king—do that which &#91;is&#93; good in your eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 2:20–23; Deuteronomy 5:9; Numbers 25:4"> And he writes a letter to them a second time, saying, “If you &#91;are&#93; for me, and are listening to my voice, take the heads of the men—the sons of your lord, and come to me about this time tomorrow, to Jezreel”; and the sons of the king &#91;are&#93; seventy men, with the great ones of the city—those bringing them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 21:21; 2 Kings 11:1; 2 Kings 10:9"> And it comes to pass, at the coming in of the letter to them, that they take the sons of the king, and slaughter seventy men, and put their heads in baskets, and send &#91;them&#93; to him at Jezreel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:23; 2 Samuel 11:18–21; Mark 6:28"> and the messenger comes in, and declares &#91;it&#93; to him, saying, “They have brought in the heads of the sons of the king,” and he says, “Make them two heaps at the opening of the gate until the morning.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 9:14–24; Isaiah 5:3; Hosea 1:4"> And it comes to pass in the morning, that he goes out, and stands, and says to all the people, “You are righteous; behold, I have conspired against my lord, and slay him—and who struck all these?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 21:29; 1 Kings 21:19; 2 Kings 9:7–10"> Now you know that nothing falls of the word of YHWH to the earth that YHWH spoke against the house of Ahab, and YHWH has done that which He spoke by the hand of His servant Elijah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 18:19; 1 Kings 18:19; 1 Kings 22:6"> And Jehu strikes all those left to the house of Ahab in Jezreel, and all his great men, and his acquaintances, and his priests, until he has not left a remnant to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he rises, and comes in and goes to Samaria; he &#91;is&#93; at the shepherds’ shearing-house in the way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 8:29; 2 Kings 8:24; 2 Chronicles 21:17"> and Jehu has found the brothers of Ahaziah king of Judah and says, “Who &#91;are&#93; you?” And they say, “We &#91;are&#93; brothers of Ahaziah, and we go down to greet the sons of the king and the sons of the mistress.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:10; 2 Kings 10:6; 2 Kings 10:10–11"> And he says, “Catch them alive”; and they catch them alive, and slaughter them at the pit of the shearing-house, forty-two men, and he has not left &#91;even one&#93; man of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:14–19; Ezra 10:19; Ezekiel 17:18"> And he goes there, and finds Jehonadab son of Rechab to meet him, and blesses him, and says to him, “Is your heart right, as my heart &#91;is&#93; with your heart?” And Jehonadab says, “It is.” “Then it is; give your hand”; and he gives his hand, and he causes him to come up into the chariot to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10; Matthew 6:5; 2 Kings 10:31"> and says, “Come with me, and look on my zeal for YHWH”; and they cause him to ride in his chariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 9:8; 2 Chronicles 22:8; Malachi 4:1"> And he comes to Samaria, and strikes all those left to Ahab in Samaria, until his destroying him, according to the word of YHWH that He spoke to Elisha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 16:31–32; 2 Kings 3:2; Romans 3:8"> And Jehu gathers the whole of the people and says to them, “Ahab served Ba‘al a little—Jehu serves him much:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 22:6; 1 Kings 18:19; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> and now, all the prophets of Ba‘al, all his servants, and all his priests, call to me; do not let a man be lacking, for I have a great sacrifice for Ba‘al; everyone who is lacking—he does not live”; and Jehu has done &#91;it&#93; in subtlety, in order to destroy the servants of Ba‘al.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joel 1:14; Exodus 32:5; 1 Kings 18:19–20"> And Jehu says, “Sanctify a restraint for Ba‘al”; and they proclaim &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 16:32; 2 Kings 11:18; Joel 3:2"> And Jehu sends into all Israel, and all the servants of Ba‘al come in, and there has not been left a man who has not come in; and they come into the house of Ba‘al, and the house of Ba‘al is full—mouth to mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 28:2; Matthew 22:11–12"> And he says to him who &#91;is&#93; over the wardrobe, “Bring out clothing for all those serving Ba‘al”; and he brings out the clothing to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Matthew 25:32–33; Matthew 13:41"> And Jehu goes in—and Jehonadab son of Rechab—to the house of Ba‘al, and says to the servants of Ba‘al, “Search and see, lest there be &#91;any&#93; from the servants of YHWH here with you—but &#91;only&#93; the servants of Ba‘al by themselves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 20:30–42"> And they come in to make sacrifices and burnt-offerings, and Jehu has set eighty men for himself in an out-place, and says, “The man who lets &#91;any&#93; of the men whom I am bringing into your hand escape—his soul for his soul.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 16:6–7; Ezekiel 22:21–22; 1 Kings 18:40"> And it comes to pass, at his finishing to make the burnt-offering, that Jehu says to the runners and to the captains, “Go in, strike them, let none come out”; and they strike them by the mouth of the sword, and the runners and the captains cast &#91;them&#93; out; and they go to the city, to the house of Ba‘al,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 14:23; 2 Kings 3:2; 2 Kings 19:18"> and bring out the standing-pillars of the house of Ba‘al, and burn them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Daniel 3:29; Ezra 6:11; Daniel 2:5"> and break down the standing-pillar of Ba‘al, and break down the house of Ba‘al, and appoint it for a latrine to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Jehu destroys Ba‘al out of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28–30; 1 Kings 14:16; 1 Kings 13:33–34"> only &#91;not&#93;—the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, that he caused Israel to sin, Jehu has not turned aside from after them—the calves of gold that &#91;are&#93; at Beth-El and in Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 10:35; 2 Kings 13:1; 2 Kings 13:10"> And YHWH says to Jehu, “Because that you have done well, to do that which &#91;is&#93; right in My eyes—according to all that &#91;is&#93; in My heart you have done to the house of Ahab—the sons of the fourth &#91;generation&#93; sit on the throne of Israel for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 10:29; Proverbs 4:23; Hebrews 2:1"> And Jehu has not taken heed to walk in the Law of YHWH, God of Israel, with all his heart; he has not turned aside from the sins of Jeroboam that he caused Israel to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 1 Kings 19:17; 2 Kings 13:22"> In those days YHWH has begun to cut off &#91;some&#93; in Israel, and Hazael strikes them in all the border of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Amos 1:3–4; Deuteronomy 2:36; Joshua 13:9–12"> from the Jordan, at the sun-rising: the whole land of Gilead, of the Gadite, and the Reubenite, and the Manassite (from Aroer, that &#91;is&#93; by the Brook of Arnon), even Gilead and Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 11:41; 1 Kings 14:29; 1 Kings 14:19"> And the rest of the matters of Jehu, and all that he did, and all his might, are they not written on the scroll of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 14:20; 1 Kings 14:31; 1 Kings 1:21"> And Jehu lies with his fathers, and they bury him in Samaria, and his son Jehoahaz reigns in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And the days that Jehu has reigned over Israel in Samaria &#91;are&#93; twenty-eight years.</span>
