<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 3:5; Exodus 16:12; Ezekiel 37:4"> And Elisha says, “Hear a word of YHWH! Thus said YHWH: About this time tomorrow, a measure of fine flour &#91;is&#93; at a shekel, and two measures of barley at a shekel, in the Gate of Samaria.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 18:12–14; Genesis 7:11; Malachi 3:10"> And the captain whom the king has, by whose hand he has been supported, answers the man of God and says, “Behold, YHWH is making windows in the heavens—will this thing be?” And he says, “Behold, you are seeing it with your eyes, and you do not eat thereof.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46; Numbers 5:2–4; 2 Kings 7:4"> And four men have been leprous at the opening of the gate, and they say to one another, “Why are we sitting here until we have died?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:18; Luke 15:17–19; 2 Samuel 14:14"> If we have said, We go into the city, then the famine &#91;is&#93; in the city and we have died there; and if we have sat here, then we have died; and now, come and we go down to the camp of Aram; if they keep us alive, we live, and if they put us to death—we have died.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:7; Deuteronomy 32:25; 1 Samuel 30:17"> And they rise in the twilight, to go to the camp of Aram, and they come to the extremity of the camp of Aram, and behold, there is not a man there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:24; Isaiah 31:1; 1 Kings 10:29"> seeing YHWH has caused the camp of Aram to hear a noise of chariot and a noise of horse—a noise of great force, and they say to one another, “Behold, the king of Israel has hired against us the kings of the Hittites, and the kings of Egypt, to come against us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Psalms 48:4–6; Hebrews 6:18"> And they rise and flee in the twilight, and forsake their tents, and their horses, and their donkeys—the camp as it &#91;is&#93;—and flee for their life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Joshua 7:21; Matthew 25:18"> And these lepers come to the extremity of the camp, and come into one tent, and eat, and drink, and lift up silver, and gold, and garments from there, and go and hide; and they turn back and go into another tent, and lift up from there, and go and hide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Philippians 2:4; Nahum 1:15"> And they say to one another, “We are not doing right this day; it &#91;is&#93; a day of tidings, and we are keeping silent; and we have waited until the morning light, then punishment has found us; and now, come and we go in and declare &#91;it&#93; to the house of the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 7:11; Mark 13:34–35; Psalms 127:1"> And they come in, and call to the gatekeeper of the city, and declare for themselves, saying, “We have come to the camp of Aram, and behold, there is no man or sound of man there, but the bound horse, and the bound donkey, and tents as they &#91;are&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he calls the gatekeepers, and they declare &#91;it&#93; to the house of the king within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 6:25–29; Joshua 8:4"> And the king rises by night and says to his servants, “Now let me declare to you that which the Arameans have done to us; they have known that we are famished, and they go out from the camp to be hidden in the field, saying, When they come out from the city, then we catch them alive, and we enter into the city.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 7:4; Lamentations 4:9; 2 Kings 5:13"> And one of his servants answers and says, “Then please let them take five of the horses that are left, that have been left in it—behold, they &#91;are&#93; as all the multitude of Israel who have been left in it; behold, they are as all the multitude of Israel who have been consumed—and we send and see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And they take two chariot-horses, and the king sends &#91;them&#93; after the camp of Aram, saying, “Go, and see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 22:24; Matthew 24:16–18; Hebrews 12:1"> And they go after them to the Jordan, and behold, all the way is full of garments and vessels that the Arameans have cast away in their haste, and the messengers return and declare &#91;it&#93; to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 7:1; Isaiah 33:4; Isaiah 33:23"> And the people go out and spoil the camp of Aram, and there is a measure of fine flour at a shekel, and two measures of barley at a shekel, according to the word of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 7:2; 2 Kings 6:32; Judges 20:43"> And the king has appointed the captain, by whose hand he is supported, over the gate, and the people tread him down in the gate, and he dies, as the man of God spoke, which he spoke in the coming down of the king to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 18:14; 2 Kings 7:1–2; 2 Kings 6:32"> indeed, it comes to pass, according to the speaking of the man of God to the king, saying, “Two measures of barley at a shekel, and a measure of fine flour at a shekel are, at this time tomorrow, in the Gate of Samaria”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 7:2"> and the captain answers the man of God and says, “And behold, YHWH is making windows in the heavens—will this thing be?” And he says, “Behold, you are seeing it with your eyes, and you do not eat thereof”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5–6; 2 Chronicles 20:20; Job 20:23"> and it comes to him so, and the people tread him down in the gate, and he dies.</span>
