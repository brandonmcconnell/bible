<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 1:1; Jeremiah 25:1; Jeremiah 25:9"> In his days Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has come up, and Jehoiakim is a servant to him &#91;for&#93; three years; and he turns and rebels against him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:11; 2 Kings 23:27; 2 Kings 21:12–14"> and YHWH sends against him the troops of the Chaldeans, and the troops of Aram, and the troops of Moab, and the troops of the sons of Ammon, and He sends them against Judah to destroy it, according to the word of YHWH that He spoke by the hand of His servants the prophets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 18:25; 2 Kings 21:2–11; 2 Kings 23:26–27"> only, by the command of YHWH it has been against Judah to turn &#91;them&#93; aside from His presence, for the sins of Manasseh, according to all that he did,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 21:16; Psalms 106:38; Numbers 35:33"> and also the innocent blood that he has shed, and he fills Jerusalem with innocent blood, and YHWH was not willing to forgive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:8; Jeremiah 26:1–24; Jeremiah 22:13–17"> And the rest of the matters of Jehoiakim and all that he did, are they not written on the scroll of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18–19; 2 Chronicles 36:6; Jeremiah 36:30"> And Jehoiakim lies with his fathers, and his son Jehoiachin reigns in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:5–7; Jeremiah 46:2; Genesis 15:18"> And the king of Egypt has not added anymore to go out from his own land, for the king of Babylon has taken, from the Brook of Egypt to the Euphrates River, all that had been to the king of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:9; 1 Chronicles 3:16; Jeremiah 37:1"> Jehoiachin &#91;is&#93; a son of eighteen years in his reigning, and he has reigned three months in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother &#91;is&#93; Nehushta daughter of Elnathan of Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and he does evil in the eyes of YHWH, according to all that his fathers did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 25:2; Daniel 1:1–2"> At that time servants of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon have come up to Jerusalem, and the city goes into siege,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon comes against the city, and his servants are laying siege to it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:1; Jeremiah 52:28; 2 Kings 25:27"> and Jehoiachin king of Judah goes out to the king of Babylon, he, and his mother, and his servants, and his chiefs, and his eunuchs, and the king of Babylon takes him in the eighth year of his reign,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 39:6; 2 Kings 20:17; Jeremiah 20:5"> and brings out from there all the treasures of the house of YHWH, and the treasures of the house of the king, and cuts in pieces all the vessels of gold that Solomon king of Israel made in the temple of YHWH, as YHWH had spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:28; 2 Kings 25:12; 2 Kings 24:16"> And he has removed all Jerusalem, and all the chiefs, and all the mighty men of valor—ten thousand &#91;is&#93; the expulsion—and every craftsman and smith; none has been left except the poor of the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:10; Jeremiah 22:24–28; Esther 2:6"> And he removes Jehoiachin to Babylon, and the mother of the king, and the wives of the king, and his eunuchs, and the mighty ones of the land—he has caused a removal to go from Jerusalem to Babylon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:28; 2 Kings 24:14; Jeremiah 29:2"> and all the men of valor, seven thousand, and the craftsmen and the smiths, one thousand, the whole &#91;are&#93; mighty men, warriors; and the king of Babylon brings them in a captivity to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:1; 2 Chronicles 36:4; 2 Kings 23:34"> And the king of Babylon causes his father’s brother Mattaniah to reign in his stead, and turns his name to Zedekiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 23:31; Jeremiah 52:1–11; Jeremiah 37:1"> Zedekiah &#91;is&#93; a son of twenty-one years in his reigning, and he has reigned eleven years in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother &#91;is&#93; Hamutal daughter of Jeremiah of Libnah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 23:37; Jeremiah 24:8; Ezekiel 21:25"> and he does evil in the eyes of YHWH according to all that Jehoiakim did,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:13; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Jeremiah 38:17–21"> for by the anger of YHWH it has been against Jerusalem and against Judah, until He &#91;finally&#93; cast them out from His presence. And Zedekiah rebels against the king of Babylon.</span>
