<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 10:42; 2 Timothy 4:8; 1 Timothy 5:21"> I fully testify, then, before God, and the Lord Jesus Christ, who is about to judge &#91;the&#93; living and dead at His appearing and His Kingdom—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Timothy 4:15–16; Titus 2:15"> preach the word; be earnest in season, out of season; convict, rebuke, exhort, in all long-suffering and teaching,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; 2 Timothy 3:1–6; 1 Timothy 4:1–3"> for there will be a season when they will not endure the sound teaching, but they will heap up teachers according to their own desires—having an itching ear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 7:11; 1 Timothy 1:4; Acts 7:57"> and, indeed, they will turn away from hearing the truth, and they will be turned aside to the fables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Peter 1:13; 2 Timothy 2:3"> But you—watch in all things; suffer evil; do the work of one proclaiming good news; make full assurance of your ministry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 1:23; Philippians 2:17; 2 Peter 1:14–15"> for I am already being poured out, and the time of my release has arrived;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:12; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Hebrews 12:1–2"> I have striven the good strife, I have finished the course, I have kept the faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 1:12; Revelation 2:10; 1 Peter 5:4"> from now on there is laid up for me the garland of righteousness that the LORD—the Righteous Judge—will give to me in that day, and not only to me, but also to all those loving His appearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:4; 2 Timothy 4:21"> Be diligent to come to me quickly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; 2 Timothy 1:15; Colossians 4:14–15"> for Demas forsook me, having loved the present age, and went on to Thessalonica, Crescens to Galatia, Titus to Dalmatia;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Philemon 1:24; Acts 12:25"> only Lucas is with me; having taken Mark, bring &#91;him&#93; with you, for he is profitable to me for ministry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 6:21; Acts 20:4; Titus 3:12"> and I sent Tychicus to Ephesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:11; 2 Corinthians 11:27; Acts 16:11"> coming, bring the cloak that I left in Troas with Carpus and the scrolls—especially the parchments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; 2 Thessalonians 1:6; Psalms 28:4"> Alexander the coppersmith did me much evil; may the LORD repay to him according to his works,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:8; Philippians 3:2; Matthew 10:16–17"> of whom you also beware, for he has greatly stood against our words;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 7:60; John 16:32; Mark 14:50"> no one stood with me in my first defense, but all forsook me (may it not be reckoned to them),</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Philippians 1:12–14; 1 Samuel 17:37"> but the LORD stood by me, and strengthened me, that the preaching might be fully assured through me, and all the nations might hear, and I was freed out of the mouth of a lion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 121:7; 2 Thessalonians 3:3; Jude 1:24–25"> and the LORD will free me from every evil work, and will save &#91;me&#93;—to His heavenly kingdom; to whom &#91;is&#93; the glory through the ages of the ages! Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 18:2; 1 Corinthians 16:19; Romans 16:3–4"> Greet Prisca and Aquilas, and Onesiphorus’ household;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Acts 19:22; Philippians 2:26–27"> Erastus remained in Corinth, and I left Trophimus ailing in Miletus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:9; Philippians 4:22; 3 John 1:14"> be diligent to come before winter. Eubulus greets you, and Pudens, and Linus, and Claudia, and all the brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philemon 1:25; Galatians 6:18; Colossians 4:18"> The Lord Jesus Christ &#91;is&#93; with your spirit. The grace &#91;is&#93; with you! Amen.</span>
