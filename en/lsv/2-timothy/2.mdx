<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Ephesians 6:10; 2 Timothy 1:7"> You, therefore, my child, be strong in the grace that &#91;is&#93; in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13–14; 1 Timothy 4:6; 1 Timothy 6:12"> and the things that you heard from me through many witnesses, be committing these things to steadfast men, who will also be sufficient to teach others;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2–3; James 1:12; 2 Timothy 4:5"> you, therefore, suffer evil as a good soldier of Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 8:14; 2 Peter 2:20"> no one serving as a soldier entangles himself with the affairs of life, that he may please him who enlisted him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Revelation 3:11; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> and if anyone also may strive, he is not crowned, except he may strive lawfully;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; 1 Corinthians 9:7–11; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9"> it is first necessary &#91;for&#93; the laboring farmer to partake of the fruits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:5; 1 John 5:20; Luke 24:45"> be considering what things I say, for the LORD gives to you understanding in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Romans 2:16; 1 Corinthians 15:11–20"> Remember Jesus Christ, raised out of the dead, of the seed of David, according to my good news,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Acts 28:31; 2 Timothy 1:8"> in which I suffer evil—to bonds, as an evildoer, but the word of God has not been bound;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; Colossians 1:24; 2 Timothy 2:3"> because of this I endure all things, because of the chosen ones, that they also may obtain salvation that &#91;is&#93; in Christ Jesus, with perpetual glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:8; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:5"> The word &#91;is&#93; steadfast: For if we died together—we will also live together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Matthew 10:33; Revelation 20:4"> if we endure together—we will also reign together; if we deny &#91;Him&#93;, He will also deny us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 3:3; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> if we are not steadfast, He remains steadfast; He is not able to deny Himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 3:9–11; 2 Timothy 2:23; 2 Timothy 4:1"> Remind &#91;them&#93; of these things, testifying fully before the LORD—not to strive about words to nothing profitable, but to the subversion of those hearing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; 2 Peter 1:10; 1 Timothy 4:6"> be diligent to present yourself approved to God—a workman not ashamed, straightly cutting the word of truth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:14"> and stand aloof from the profane vain talkings, for they will advance to more impiety,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; Nahum 3:15; James 5:3"> and their word will have pasture as a gangrene, of whom is Hymenaeus and Philetus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:12; 1 Timothy 1:19; Hebrews 3:10"> who swerved concerning the truth, saying the resurrection to have already been, and overthrows the faith of some;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 10:14; 1 Corinthians 8:3; Nahum 1:7"> sure, nevertheless, the foundation of God has stood, having this seal: “The LORD has known those who are His,” and, “Let him depart from unrighteousness—everyone who is naming the Name of Christ.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:21–23; 1 Peter 2:5; 1 Timothy 3:15"> And in a great house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver, but also of wood and of earth, and some to honor, and some to dishonor:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:17; Ephesians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> if, then, anyone may cleanse himself from these, he will be a vessel to honor, sanctified and profitable to the master—having been prepared to every good work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; 1 Peter 3:11; 1 Corinthians 6:18"> and flee the youthful lusts, and pursue righteousness, faith, love, peace, with those calling on the LORD out of a pure heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:14; Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 4:7"> and be avoiding the foolish and uninstructed questions, having known that they beget strife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Titus 3:2; James 1:19–20; 1 Timothy 3:2–3"> and a servant of the LORD must not quarrel, but to be gentle to all, apt to teach, patient under evil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 11:18; Galatians 6:1; 1 Timothy 2:4"> instructing those opposing in meekness—if perhaps God may give to them conversion to an acknowledging of the truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; Isaiah 49:25–26; Psalms 124:7"> and they may awake out of the Devil’s snare, having been caught by him at his will.</span>
