<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3–4; Romans 15:31; Deuteronomy 32:20"> and that we may be delivered from the unreasonable and evil men, for not all &#91;are&#93; of the faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:18; 1 Thessalonians 5:24; 1 Corinthians 10:13"> but faithful is the LORD who will establish you, and will guard &#91;you&#93; from the evil &#91;one&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Philemon 1:21; 1 Thessalonians 4:10"> and we now have confidence in the LORD, that which we command you both do and will do;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 119:36; 1 Chronicles 29:18; Deuteronomy 30:6"> and the LORD direct your hearts to the love of God, and to the endurance of the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:10–11; 2 Thessalonians 3:7; Romans 16:17"> And we command you, brothers, in the Name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to withdraw yourselves from every brother walking disorderly, and not after the tradition that you received from us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:6; 2 Thessalonians 3:9; 1 Corinthians 4:16"> for you have known how it is necessary to imitate us, because we did not act disorderly among you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:11; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; Ephesians 4:28"> nor did we eat bread of anyone for nothing, but in labor and in travail, working night and day, not to be chargeable to any of you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7; 1 Thessalonians 2:6; 1 Corinthians 9:4–14"> not because we have no authority, but that we might give ourselves to you &#91;as&#93; a pattern, to imitate us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 20:4"> for even when we were with you, this we commanded you, that if anyone is not willing to work, neither let him eat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:13; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Thessalonians 4:11"> for we hear of some walking disorderly among you, working nothing, but being busybodies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:28; 1 Thessalonians 4:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:8"> and such we command and exhort through our Lord Jesus Christ, that working with quietness, they may eat their own bread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 6:9–10; Hebrews 12:3; 2 Corinthians 4:1"> and you, brothers, may you not be weary doing well,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Corinthians 5:11"> and if anyone does not obey our word through the letter, note this one, and have no company with him, that he may be ashamed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Corinthians 5:5"> and do not count as an enemy, but admonish as a brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 29:11; John 16:33; Matthew 1:23"> and may the LORD of peace Himself always give to you peace in every way; the LORD &#91;is&#93; with you all!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:21; 2 Thessalonians 1:5; Colossians 4:18"> The salutation by the hand of me, Paul, which is a sign in every letter; thus I write.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Romans 16:23"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ &#91;is&#93; with you all! Amen.</span>
