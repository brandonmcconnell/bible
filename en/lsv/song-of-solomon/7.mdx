<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3; Psalms 45:16; Romans 7:4"> Your waist &#91;is&#93; a basin of roundness, &#124;&#124; It does not lack the mixture, &#124;&#124; Your body a heap of wheat, fenced with lilies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Song of Songs 4:5; Song of Songs 6:6"> Your two breasts as two young ones, twins of a roe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song of Songs 4:4; Song of Songs 4:8–9; Psalms 144:12"> Your neck as a tower of the ivory, &#124;&#124; Your eyes pools in Heshbon, near the Gate of Bath-Rabbim, &#124;&#124; Your face as a tower of Lebanon looking to Damascus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Song of Songs 4:1; Micah 7:14"> Your head on you as Carmel, &#124;&#124; And the locks of your head as purple, &#124;&#124; The king is bound with the flowings!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Song of Songs 1:15–16; Psalms 45:11; Song of Songs 4:10"> How beautiful and how pleasant you have been, &#124;&#124; O love, in delights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Songs 4:5; Song of Songs 1:13; Ephesians 4:13"> This your stature has been like to a palm, &#124;&#124; And your breasts to clusters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; John 14:21–23; Song of Songs 2:5"> I said, “Let me go up on the palm, &#124;&#124; Let me lay hold on its boughs,” &#124;&#124; Indeed, let your breasts now be as clusters of the vine, &#124;&#124; And the fragrance of your face as citrons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Songs 5:16; Isaiah 62:8–9; Acts 4:31–32"> And your palate as the good wine—Flowing to my beloved in uprightness, &#124;&#124; Strengthening the lips of the aged!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 45:11; Song of Songs 2:16; Song of Songs 6:3"> I &#91;am&#93; my beloved’s, and on me &#91;is&#93; his desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Song of Songs 4:8; Song of Songs 2:10–13; Song of Songs 1:4"> Come, my beloved, we go forth to the field,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Song of Songs 6:11; Ephesians 6:24; Song of Songs 7:6"> We lodge in the villages, we go early to the vineyards, &#124;&#124; We see if the vine has flourished, &#124;&#124; The sweet smelling-flower has opened. The pomegranates have blossomed, &#124;&#124; There I give to you my loves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 30:14; Matthew 13:52; 1 Peter 4:11"> The mandrakes have given fragrance, &#124;&#124; And at our openings all pleasant things, &#124;&#124; New, indeed, old, my beloved, I laid up for you!</span>
