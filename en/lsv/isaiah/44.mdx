<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 41:8; Romans 11:5–6; Jeremiah 30:10"> “And now, hear, O Jacob, My servant, &#124;&#124; And Israel, whom I have fixed on—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 43:1; Isaiah 46:3–4"> Thus said YHWH, your Maker, and your Former, &#124;&#124; From the womb He helps you: Do not fear, My servant Jacob, &#124;&#124; And Yeshurun, whom I have fixed on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Acts 2:17; Isaiah 59:21"> For I pour waters on a thirsty one, &#124;&#124; And floods on a dry land, &#124;&#124; I pour My Spirit on your seed, &#124;&#124; And My blessing on your offspring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; Psalms 1:3; Psalms 92:13–15"> And they have sprung up as among grass, &#124;&#124; As willows by conduits of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:5; Zechariah 13:9; 1 Peter 2:9"> This &#91;one&#93; says, For I &#91;am&#93; YHWH’s, &#124;&#124; And this calls &#91;himself&#93; by the name of Jacob, &#124;&#124; And this &#91;one&#93; writes &#91;with&#93; his hand, For YHWH, &#124;&#124; And surnames himself by the name of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 41:4; Isaiah 43:10–11; Revelation 1:8"> Thus said YHWH, King of Israel, &#124;&#124; And his Redeemer, YHWH of Hosts: “I &#91;am&#93; the first, and I &#91;am&#93; the last, &#124;&#124; And besides Me there is no God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 41:26; Isaiah 41:22; Isaiah 46:9–10"> And who &#91;is&#93; as I, call and declare it, &#124;&#124; And arrange it for Me, &#124;&#124; Since My placing the people of antiquity, &#124;&#124; And things that are coming, &#124;&#124; And those that come, they declare to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; 1 Samuel 2:2; Deuteronomy 4:35"> Do not fear, nor be afraid, &#124;&#124; Have I not caused you to hear from that time, and declared? And you &#91;are&#93; My witnesses, &#124;&#124; Is there a God besides Me? Indeed, there is none, &#124;&#124; I have not known another Rock.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 10:14–15; Psalms 97:7"> Framers of a carved image, all of them &#91;are&#93; emptiness, &#124;&#124; And their desirable things do not profit, &#124;&#124; And they &#91;are&#93; their own witnesses, &#124;&#124; They do not see, nor know, that they may be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:5; Habakkuk 2:18; Acts 19:26"> Who has formed a god, &#124;&#124; And poured out a molten image—not profitable?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:17; Isaiah 45:16; Psalms 97:7"> Behold, all his companions are ashamed, &#124;&#124; As for artisans—they &#91;are&#93; of men, &#124;&#124; All of them gather together, they stand up, &#124;&#124; They fear, they are ashamed together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:6–7; Isaiah 40:19; Isaiah 46:6–7"> He has worked iron &#91;with&#93; an axe, &#124;&#124; And has worked with coals, &#124;&#124; And forms it with hammers, &#124;&#124; And works it by his powerful arm, &#124;&#124; Indeed, he is hungry, and there is no power, &#124;&#124; He does not drink water, and he is wearied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 17:4–5; Psalms 115:5–7; Acts 17:29"> He has worked &#91;with&#93; wood, &#124;&#124; He has stretched out a rule, &#124;&#124; He marks it out with a line, &#124;&#124; He makes it with carving tools, &#124;&#124; And he marks it out with a compass, &#124;&#124; And makes it according to the form of a man, &#124;&#124; According to the beauty of a man, &#124;&#124; To remain in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:20; Jeremiah 10:3–8; Hosea 4:12"> Cutting down cedars for himself, &#124;&#124; He also takes a cypress, and an oak, &#124;&#124; And he strengthens &#91;it&#93; for himself &#124;&#124; Among the trees of a forest, &#124;&#124; He has planted an ash, and the shower nourishes &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:14; Revelation 9:20; Judges 2:19"> And it has been for man to burn, &#124;&#124; And he takes of them, and becomes warm, &#124;&#124; Indeed, he kindles &#91;it&#93;, and has baked bread, &#124;&#124; Indeed, he makes a god, and bows himself, &#124;&#124; He has made it a carved image, &#124;&#124; And he falls down to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Half of it he has burned in the fire, &#124;&#124; By &#91;this&#93; half of it he eats flesh, &#124;&#124; He roasts a roasting and is satisfied, &#124;&#124; Indeed, he is warm and says: “Aha, I have become warm, I have enjoyed the light.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 45:20; 1 Kings 18:26; Daniel 6:27"> And he has made its remnant for a god—For his carved image, &#124;&#124; He falls down to it, and worships, &#124;&#124; And prays to it, and he says, “Deliver me, for you &#91;are&#93; my god.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; John 12:39–40"> They have not known, nor do they understand, &#124;&#124; For He has coated their eyes from seeing, &#124;&#124; Their heart from acting wisely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:15; 1 Kings 11:7; 1 Kings 11:5"> And none turn &#91;it&#93; back to his heart, &#124;&#124; Nor has knowledge nor understanding to say, “I have burned half of it in the fire, &#124;&#124; Indeed, I have also baked bread over its coals, &#124;&#124; I roast flesh and I eat, &#124;&#124; And I make its remnant for an abomination, &#124;&#124; I fall down to the stock of a tree.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:28; Job 15:31; Hosea 12:1"> Feeding on ashes, the heart is deceived, &#124;&#124; It has turned him aside, &#124;&#124; And he does not deliver his soul, nor says: “Is there not a lie in my right hand?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 44:1–2; Isaiah 49:15–16; Isaiah 43:15"> “Remember these, O Jacob, and Israel, &#124;&#124; For you &#91;are&#93; My servant, &#124;&#124; I formed you, you &#91;are&#93; a servant to Me, &#124;&#124; O Israel, you do not forget Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; 1 Corinthians 6:20; Acts 3:18–19"> I have blotted out, as &#91;by&#93; a thick cloud, &#124;&#124; Your transgressions, &#124;&#124; And as &#91;by&#93; a cloud, your sins, &#124;&#124; Return to Me, for I have redeemed you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 1:6–7; Revelation 5:8–14; Psalms 69:34"> Sing, O heavens, for YHWH has worked, &#124;&#124; Shout, O lower parts of earth, &#124;&#124; Break forth, O mountains, with singing, &#124;&#124; Forest, and every tree in it, &#124;&#124; For YHWH has redeemed Jacob, &#124;&#124; And He beautifies Himself in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 45:12; Isaiah 42:5; Isaiah 54:5"> Thus said YHWH, your Redeemer, &#124;&#124; And your Framer from the womb: “I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, doing all things, &#124;&#124; Stretching out the heavens by Myself, &#124;&#124; Spreading out the earth—who &#91;is&#93; with Me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Job 5:12–14; Jeremiah 50:36"> Making void the tokens of devisers, &#124;&#124; And makes diviners mad, &#124;&#124; Turning the wise backward, &#124;&#124; And makes their knowledge foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 18:36–38; Isaiah 42:9; Nehemiah 1:3"> Confirming the word of His servant, &#124;&#124; He perfects the counsel of His messengers, &#124;&#124; Who is saying of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; She is inhabited, &#124;&#124; And of cities of Judah, &#124;&#124; They will be built, and her ruins I raise up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 42:15; Jeremiah 51:36; Jeremiah 50:38"> Who is saying to the deep, &#124;&#124; Be dry, and your rivers I cause to dry up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:22–3; Isaiah 45:1; Isaiah 45:13"> Who is saying of Cyrus, My shepherd, &#124;&#124; And he perfects all My delight, &#124;&#124; So as to say of Jerusalem, You are built, &#124;&#124; And of the temple, You are founded.”</span>
