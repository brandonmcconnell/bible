<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Psalms 125:2; Joel 3:12"> The burden of the Valley of Vision. What is &#91;troubling&#93; you now, that you have gone up, &#124;&#124; All of you—to the roofs?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 32:13; Isaiah 23:7; Lamentations 2:20"> Full of stirs—a noisy city—an exulting city, &#124;&#124; Your pierced are not pierced of the sword, &#124;&#124; Nor dead in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 25:4–7; Isaiah 3:1–8; Jeremiah 39:4–7"> All your rulers fled from the bow together, &#124;&#124; All found of you have been bound, &#124;&#124; They have been kept bound together, &#124;&#124; They have fled far off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:1; Matthew 2:18; Psalms 77:2"> Therefore I said, “Look &#91;away&#93; from me, &#124;&#124; I am bitter in my weeping, do not hurry to comfort me, &#124;&#124; For the destruction of the daughter of my people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 7:4; Isaiah 37:3; Luke 23:30"> For a day of noise, and of treading down, &#124;&#124; And of perplexity, &#91;is&#93; to the Lord, YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; In the Valley of Vision, digging down a wall, &#124;&#124; And crying to the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 16:9; Isaiah 21:2; Amos 1:5"> And Elam has carried a quiver, &#124;&#124; In a chariot of men—horsemen, &#124;&#124; And Kir has exposed a shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Isaiah 10:28–32; Jeremiah 39:1–3"> And it comes to pass, &#124;&#124; The choice of your valleys have been full of chariots, &#124;&#124; And the horsemen diligently place themselves at the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2; 1 Kings 10:17; Isaiah 36:1–3"> And one removes the covering of Judah, &#124;&#124; And in that day you look &#124;&#124; To the armor of the house of the forest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 20:20; Nehemiah 3:16; 2 Chronicles 32:1–6"> And you have seen the breaches of the City of David, &#124;&#124; For they have become many, &#124;&#124; And you gather the waters of the lower pool,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And you numbered the houses of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And you break down the houses to fortify the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 7:7; Jeremiah 33:2–3; Isaiah 31:1"> And you made a ditch between the two walls, &#124;&#124; For the waters of the old pool, &#124;&#124; And you have not looked to its Maker, &#124;&#124; And you have not seen its Framer of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Micah 1:16; Joel 1:13"> And the Lord calls, YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; In that day, to weeping and to lamentation, &#124;&#124; And to baldness and to girding on of sackcloth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 56:12; 1 Corinthians 15:32; Luke 17:26–29"> And behold, joy and gladness, slaying of oxen, &#124;&#124; And slaughtering of sheep, &#124;&#124; Eating of flesh, and drinking of wine, &#124;&#124; Eat and drink, for tomorrow we die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:13; 1 Samuel 3:14; Isaiah 5:9"> And it has been revealed in my ears, &#124;&#124; &#91;By&#93; YHWH of Hosts: This iniquity is not pardoned to you, &#124;&#124; Until you die, said the Lord, YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; 2 Kings 18:18; 2 Kings 19:2"> Thus said the Lord, YHWH of Hosts: “Go forth, go to this steward, &#124;&#124; To Shebna, who &#91;is&#93; over the house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:60; 2 Chronicles 16:14; 2 Samuel 18:18"> What are you &#91;doing&#93; here? And who has &#91;allowed&#93; you here? That you have hewn out a tomb for yourself here? Hewing his tomb on high, &#124;&#124; Carving a dwelling for himself in a rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3; Job 9:24; Esther 7:8"> Behold, YHWH is casting you up and down, &#124;&#124; A casting up and down, O mighty one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 17:13; Amos 7:17"> And your coverer covering, wrapping around, &#124;&#124; Wraps you around, O babbler, &#124;&#124; On a land of broad sides—there you die, &#124;&#124; And there the chariots of your glory &#91;are&#93; the shame of the house of your lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; Psalms 75:6–7; Job 40:11–12"> And I have thrust you from your station, &#124;&#124; And he throws you down from your office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; 2 Kings 18:18; Isaiah 37:2"> And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; That I have called to My servant, &#124;&#124; To Eliakim son of Hilkiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:8; Isaiah 9:6–7; Genesis 41:42–43"> And I have clothed him with your coat, &#124;&#124; And I strengthen him with your girdle, &#124;&#124; And I give your garment into his hand, &#124;&#124; And he has been for a father to the inhabitant of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And to the house of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:18–19; Revelation 3:7; Job 12:14"> And I have placed the key &#124;&#124; Of the house of David on his shoulder, &#124;&#124; And he has opened, and none is shutting, &#124;&#124; And has shut, and none is opening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:8; Job 36:7; Zechariah 10:4"> And I have fixed him &#91;as&#93; a nail in a steadfast place, &#124;&#124; And he has been for a throne of glory &#124;&#124; To the house of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 6:1–3; John 20:21–23; Matthew 28:18"> And they have hanged on him &#124;&#124; All the glory of the house of his father, &#124;&#124; The offspring and the issue, &#124;&#124; All vessels of small quality, &#124;&#124; From vessels of basins to all vessels of flagons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 46:11; Esther 9:24–25; Psalms 146:3"> In that day—a declaration of YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; The nail that is fixed is moved &#124;&#124; In a steadfast place, &#124;&#124; Indeed, it has been cut down, and has fallen, &#124;&#124; And the burden that &#91;is&#93; on it has been cut off, &#124;&#124; For YHWH has spoken!”</span>
