<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 12:41; Revelation 15:8; Revelation 7:15–17"> In the year of the death of King Uzziah—I see the Lord, sitting on a throne, high and lifted up, and His train is filling the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 4:8; Ezekiel 1:11; Psalms 104:4"> Seraphim are standing above it: each one has six wings; with two &#91;each&#93; covers its face, and with two &#91;each&#93; covers its feet, and with two &#91;each&#93; flies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 4:8–9; Psalms 72:19; Numbers 14:21"> And this one has called to that, and has said: “HOLY, HOLY, HOLY, &#91;is&#93; YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; The fullness of all the earth &#91;is&#93; His glory!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 15:8; Exodus 40:34; 2 Chronicles 5:13–1"> And the posts of the thresholds are moved by the voice of him who is calling, and the house is full of smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 5:8–9; James 3:6–10; Jeremiah 9:3–8"> And I say, “Woe to me, for I have been silent, &#124;&#124; For I &#91;am&#93; a man of unclean lips, &#124;&#124; And I am dwelling in &#91;the&#93; midst of a people of unclean lips, &#124;&#124; Because the King, YHWH of Hosts, my eyes have seen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 8:3–5; Hebrews 9:22–26; Ezekiel 10:2"> And one of the seraphim flees to me, and in his hand—a burning coal (with tongs he has taken &#91;it&#93; from off the altar),</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; 1 John 1:7; Isaiah 43:25"> and he strikes against my mouth and says: “Behold, this has struck against your lips, &#124;&#124; And your iniquity is turned aside, &#124;&#124; And your sin is covered.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:10–13; Acts 26:16–17; Ephesians 3:8"> And I hear the voice of the Lord, saying, “Whom do I send? And who goes for Us?” And I say, “Here I &#91;am&#93;, send me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 13:14–15; Luke 8:10; Mark 4:12"> And He says, “Go, and you have said to this people, Hearing you hear, and you do not understand, &#124;&#124; And seeing you see, and you do not know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Acts 28:27; Jeremiah 5:21"> Declare the heart of this people fat, &#124;&#124; And declare its ears heavy, &#124;&#124; And declare its eyes dazzled, &#124;&#124; Lest it see with its eyes, &#124;&#124; And hear with its ears, and consider with its heart, &#124;&#124; And it has turned back, and has health.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 24:1–12; Psalms 94:3; Leviticus 26:31"> And I say, “Until when, O Lord?” And He says, “Surely until cities have been ruined without inhabitant, &#124;&#124; And houses without man, &#124;&#124; And the ground is ruined—a desolation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:29; Deuteronomy 28:64; 2 Kings 25:11"> And YHWH has put man far off, &#124;&#124; And the forsaken part &#91;is&#93; great in the heart of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 14:7–9; Romans 11:5–6; Genesis 22:18"> And yet a tenth in it, and it has turned, &#124;&#124; And has been for a burning, &#124;&#124; As a teil-tree, and as an oak, that in falling, &#124;&#124; Has substance in them, &#124;&#124; The holy seed &#91;is&#93; its substance!”</span>
