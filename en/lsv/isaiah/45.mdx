<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:20–24; Isaiah 44:28; Isaiah 42:6"> Thus said YHWH, &#124;&#124; To His anointed, to Cyrus, &#124;&#124; Whose right hand I have laid hold on, &#124;&#124; To subdue nations before him, &#124;&#124; Indeed, I loose loins of kings, &#124;&#124; To open double doors before him, &#124;&#124; Indeed, gates are not shut:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:4; Luke 3:5; Isaiah 42:16"> “I go before you, and make crooked places straight, &#124;&#124; I shatter doors of bronze, &#124;&#124; And I cut bars of iron apart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 48:15; Isaiah 43:1; Exodus 33:17"> And have given to you treasures of darkness, &#124;&#124; Even treasures of secret places, &#124;&#124; So that you know that I, YHWH, &#124;&#124; Who am calling on your name—&#91;am&#93; the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 41:8–9; Acts 17:23; Isaiah 44:1"> For the sake of My servant Jacob, &#124;&#124; And of Israel My chosen, &#124;&#124; I also call you by your name, I surname you, &#124;&#124; And you have not known Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 44:8; Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 46:9"> I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, and there is none else, &#124;&#124; There is no God except Me, &#124;&#124; I gird you, and you have not known Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 1:11; Isaiah 45:5; Psalms 46:10"> So that they know from the rising of the sun, &#124;&#124; And from the west, that there is none besides Me, &#124;&#124; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, and there is none else,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:13–14; Amos 3:6; Psalms 75:7"> Forming light, and creating darkness, &#124;&#124; Making peace, and creating calamity, &#124;&#124; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, doing all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Isaiah 61:11; Psalms 72:6"> Drop, you heavens, from above, &#124;&#124; And clouds cause righteousness to flow, &#124;&#124; Earth opens, and they are fruitful, &#124;&#124; Salvation and righteousness spring up together, &#124;&#124; I, YHWH, have created it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Romans 9:20–21; Isaiah 10:15"> Woe &#91;to&#93; him who is striving with his Former &#124;&#124; (A potsherd with potsherds of the ground!) Does clay say to its Framer, What &#91;are&#93; you doing? And your work, He has no hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:16; Hebrews 12:9; Malachi 1:6"> Woe &#91;to&#93; him who is saying to a father, What do you beget? Or to a wife, What do you bring forth?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:3; Isaiah 48:17; Mark 11:24"> Thus said YHWH, The Holy One of Israel, and his Former: “Ask Me of the things coming concerning My sons, &#124;&#124; Indeed, concerning the work of My hands, you command Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 42:5; Isaiah 44:24; Jeremiah 27:5"> I made earth, and created man on it, &#124;&#124; My hands stretched out the heavens, &#124;&#124; And I have commanded all their host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:2; Isaiah 13:17; Isaiah 41:25"> I have stirred him up in righteousness, &#124;&#124; And I make all his ways straight, &#124;&#124; He builds My city, and sends out My captivity, &#124;&#124; Not for price, nor for bribe,” said YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; 1 Corinthians 14:25; Jeremiah 16:19"> Thus said YHWH: “The labor of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And the merchandise of Cush, &#124;&#124; And of the Sebaim—men of measure, &#124;&#124; Pass over to you, and they are yours, &#124;&#124; They go after you, they pass over in chains, &#124;&#124; And they bow themselves to you, &#124;&#124; They pray to you: Surely God &#91;is&#93; in you, &#124;&#124; And there is none else, no &#91;other&#93; God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17; Psalms 44:24; Isaiah 57:17"> Surely You &#91;are&#93; a God hiding Yourself, God of Israel—Savior!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 44:9; Isaiah 44:11; Isaiah 42:17"> They have been ashamed, &#124;&#124; And they have even blushed—all of them, &#124;&#124; Those carving images have gone together in confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalms 25:3; Isaiah 54:4"> Israel has been saved in YHWH, &#124;&#124; A perpetual salvation! You are not ashamed nor confounded &#124;&#124; For all ages of eternity!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 115:16; Jeremiah 10:12; Isaiah 45:12"> For thus said YHWH, Creator of the heavens, &#124;&#124; He is God, &#124;&#124; Former of earth, and its Maker, &#124;&#124; He established it—He did not create it empty, &#124;&#124; For He formed it to be inhabited: “I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, and there is none else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 48:16; Amos 5:4; Numbers 23:19–20"> I have not spoken in secret, in a dark place of the earth, &#124;&#124; I have not said to the seed of Jacob, &#124;&#124; Seek Me in vain, &#124;&#124; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, speaking righteousness, &#124;&#124; Declaring uprightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:5; Isaiah 44:17–20; Isaiah 43:9"> Be gathered, and come in, &#124;&#124; Come near together, you escaped of the nations, &#124;&#124; They have not known, &#124;&#124; Who are lifting up the wood of their carved image, &#124;&#124; And praying to a god &#91;that&#93; does not save.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 45:5; Isaiah 43:11; Isaiah 44:7–8"> Declare, and bring near, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they take counsel together, &#124;&#124; Who has proclaimed this from of old? &#91;Who&#93; has declared it from that time? Is it not I—YHWH? And there is no other god besides Me, &#124;&#124; A God righteous and saving, there is none except Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 6:40; Micah 7:7; Hebrews 12:2"> Turn to Me, and be saved, all the ends of the earth, &#124;&#124; For I &#91;am&#93; God, and there is none else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; Romans 14:10–12; Isaiah 55:11"> I have sworn by Myself, &#124;&#124; A word has gone out from My mouth in righteousness, &#124;&#124; And it does not return, &#124;&#124; That to Me, every knee bows, every tongue swears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 41:11; Isaiah 54:17; Ephesians 6:10"> Only in YHWH, one has said, &#124;&#124; Do I have righteousness and strength, &#124;&#124; He comes to Him, &#124;&#124; And all those displeased with Him are ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:16; Galatians 3:27–29; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> In YHWH are all the seed of Israel justified, &#124;&#124; And they boast themselves.”</span>
