<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:12; Revelation 1:7; Joshua 2:11"> The burden of Egypt. Behold, YHWH is riding on a swift thick cloud, &#124;&#124; And He has entered Egypt, &#124;&#124; And the idols of Egypt have been moved at His presence, &#124;&#124; And the heart of Egypt melts in its midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 1 Samuel 14:20; Matthew 10:36"> And I armed Egyptians against Egyptians, &#124;&#124; And they fought, each against his brother, &#124;&#124; And each against his neighbor, &#124;&#124; City against city, kingdom against kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; Daniel 2:2; 1 Chronicles 10:13"> And the spirit of Egypt has been emptied out in its midst. And I swallow up its counsel, &#124;&#124; And they have sought to the idols, &#124;&#124; And to the charmers, &#124;&#124; And to those having familiar spirits, &#124;&#124; And to the wizards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 46:26; Ezekiel 29:19"> And I have delivered the Egyptians &#124;&#124; Into the hand of a hard lord, &#124;&#124; And a strong king rules over them, &#124;&#124; A declaration of the Lord, YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:36; Ezekiel 30:12; Zechariah 14:18"> And waters have failed from the sea, &#124;&#124; And a river is desolated and dried up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:25; Exodus 7:18; 2 Kings 19:24"> And they have turned away the flowings, &#124;&#124; Brooks of the bulwark have been weak and dried up, &#124;&#124; Reed and flag have withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 32:20; Joel 1:17–18; Isaiah 23:3"> Exposed things by the brook, by the edge of the brook, &#124;&#124; And every sown thing of the brook, has withered, &#124;&#124; It has been driven away, and is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:10; Habakkuk 1:15; Numbers 11:5"> And the fishers have lamented, &#124;&#124; And all casting a hook into the brook have mourned, &#124;&#124; And those spreading dragnets on the face of the waters have languished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:7; Proverbs 7:16; 1 Kings 10:28"> And makers of fine flax have been ashamed, &#124;&#124; And weavers of networks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:10; Exodus 7:19; Exodus 8:5"> And its foundations have been struck, &#124;&#124; All making wages &#91;are&#93; afflicted in soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Acts 7:22; 1 Kings 4:30"> Only, the princes of Zoan &#91;are&#93; fools, &#124;&#124; The counsel of the wise ones of the counselors of Pharaoh has become brutish. How do you say to Pharaoh, “I am a son of the wise, a son of kings of antiquity?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20; Isaiah 14:24; Judges 9:38"> Where &#91;are&#93; they now, your wise ones? Indeed, let them now tell &#91;it&#93; to you, &#124;&#124; And they know what YHWH of Hosts has counseled against Egypt!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:13; Jeremiah 2:16; Jeremiah 46:14"> Princes of Zoan have been foolish, &#124;&#124; Princes of Noph have been lifted up, &#124;&#124; And they have caused Egypt to err, &#124;&#124; The chief of her tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Kings 22:20–23; Ezekiel 14:7–9"> YHWH has mingled in her midst &#124;&#124; A spirit of perverseness, &#124;&#124; And they have caused Egypt to err in all its work, &#124;&#124; As a drunkard errs in his vomit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:14–15; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12; Proverbs 14:23"> And there is no work to Egypt, &#124;&#124; That head or tail, branch or reed, may do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Nahum 3:13; Isaiah 11:15"> In that day Egypt is like women, &#124;&#124; And it has mourned, and been afraid, &#124;&#124; Because of the waving of the hand of YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; That He is waving over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 14:24; Daniel 4:35; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> And the land of Judah has been to Egypt for a cause of staggering, &#124;&#124; Everyone who mentions it, fears for himself, &#124;&#124; Because of the counsel of YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; That He is counseling against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:9; Zechariah 2:11; Isaiah 19:21"> In that day there are five cities in the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; Speaking the lip of Canaan, &#124;&#124; And swearing to YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; “The city of destruction,” is said of one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 28:18; Joshua 22:10; Joshua 22:26"> In that day there is an altar to YHWH &#124;&#124; In the midst of the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And a standing pillar to YHWH near its border,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Titus 2:13; Isaiah 45:21–22"> And it has been for a sign and for a testimony, &#124;&#124; To YHWH of Hosts in the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; For they cry to YHWH from the face of oppressors, &#124;&#124; And He sends a Savior to them, &#124;&#124; Even a great one, and has delivered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; Malachi 1:11; John 17:3"> And YHWH has been known to Egypt, &#124;&#124; And the Egyptians have known YHWH in that day, &#124;&#124; And done sacrifice and present, &#124;&#124; And vowed a vow to YHWH, and completed &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Hebrews 12:11; Hosea 14:1"> And YHWH has struck Egypt, striking and healing, &#124;&#124; And they have turned back to YHWH, &#124;&#124; And He has accepted their plea, &#124;&#124; And has healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 11:16; Ephesians 2:18–22; Isaiah 35:8–10"> In that day a highway is out of Egypt to Asshur, &#124;&#124; And the Assyrians have come into Egypt, &#124;&#124; And the Egyptians into Asshur, &#124;&#124; And the Egyptians have served with the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 65:8; Genesis 12:2; Galatians 3:14"> In that day Israel is third, &#124;&#124; With Egypt, and with Asshur, &#124;&#124; A blessing in the heart of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 29:23; Hosea 2:23; Romans 9:24–25"> In that YHWH of Hosts blessed it, saying, “Blessed &#91;is&#93; My people—Egypt, &#124;&#124; And the work of My hands—Asshur, &#124;&#124; And My inheritance—Israel!”</span>
