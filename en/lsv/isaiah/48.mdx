<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:2; Psalms 68:26; Isaiah 65:16"> “Hear this, O house of Jacob, &#124;&#124; Who are called by the name of Israel, &#124;&#124; And from the waters of Judah came out, &#124;&#124; Who are swearing by the Name of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And make mention of the God of Israel, &#124;&#124; Not in truth nor in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Romans 2:17; Isaiah 52:1"> For they have been called from the Holy City, &#124;&#124; And been supported on the God of Israel, &#124;&#124; YHWH of Hosts &#91;is&#93; His Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 42:9; Joshua 21:45; Isaiah 41:22"> I declared the former things from that time, &#124;&#124; And they have gone forth from My mouth, &#124;&#124; And I proclaim them, &#124;&#124; Suddenly I have done, and it comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Exodus 32:9; Zechariah 7:11–12"> From My knowing that you are obstinate, &#124;&#124; And your neck—a sinew of iron, &#124;&#124; And your forehead—bronze,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 48:3; Luke 1:70; Acts 15:18"> And from that time I declare &#91;it&#93; to you, &#124;&#124; Before it comes I have caused you to hear, &#124;&#124; Lest you say, My idol has done them, &#124;&#124; And my carved image, &#124;&#124; And my molten image commanded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 4:1; Isaiah 42:9; Romans 16:25–26"> You have heard, see the whole of it, &#124;&#124; And you, do you not declare? I have caused you to hear new things from this time, &#124;&#124; And things reserved that you did not know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Now they have been produced and not from that time, &#124;&#124; Indeed, before the day, and you have not heard them, &#124;&#124; Lest you say, Behold, I have known them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 58:3; Hosea 5:7; Jeremiah 6:10"> Indeed, you have not heard, &#124;&#124; Indeed, you have not known, &#124;&#124; Indeed, your ear has not opened from that time, &#124;&#124; For I have known you deal treacherously, &#124;&#124; And &#91;are&#93; a transgressor from the belly, &#124;&#124; One is crying to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 79:9; Psalms 106:8; Psalms 143:11"> I defer My anger for My Name’s sake, &#124;&#124; And &#91;for&#93; My praise I restrain for you, &#124;&#124; So as not to cut you off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12; Proverbs 17:3; Job 23:10"> Behold, I have refined you, and not with silver, &#124;&#124; I have chosen you in a furnace of affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:8; Ezekiel 20:9; Isaiah 37:35"> For My sake, for My own sake, I do &#91;it&#93;, &#124;&#124; For how is it defiled? And I do not give My glory to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:4; Revelation 22:13; Revelation 1:8"> Listen to me, O Jacob, and Israel, My called one, &#124;&#124; I &#91;am&#93; He, &#124;&#124; I &#91;am&#93; first, and I &#91;am&#93; last;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:10–12; Psalms 102:25; Isaiah 40:26"> Also, My hand has founded earth, &#124;&#124; And My right hand stretched out the heavens, &#124;&#124; I am calling to them, they stand together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 43:9; Jeremiah 50:21–29; Isaiah 13:17–18"> Be gathered, all of you, and hear, &#124;&#124; Who among them declared these things? YHWH has loved him, &#124;&#124; He does His pleasure on Babylon, &#124;&#124; And His arm &#91;is on&#93; the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 1:8; Isaiah 45:1–2; Psalms 45:4"> I have spoken, indeed, I have called him, I have brought him in, &#124;&#124; And he has made his way prosperous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 18:20; John 20:21–22; John 3:34"> Come near to Me, hear this, &#124;&#124; I have not spoken in secret from the beginning, &#124;&#124; From the time of its being, I &#91;am&#93; there, &#124;&#124; And now Lord YHWH has sent Me, and His Spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Psalms 25:12; Job 22:21–22"> Thus said YHWH, your Redeemer, &#124;&#124; The Holy One of Israel: “I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH, teaching you to profit, &#124;&#124; Causing you to tread in the way you go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:29; Isaiah 66:12; Psalms 119:165"> O that you had attended to My commands, &#124;&#124; Then your peace is as a river, &#124;&#124; And your righteousness as billows of the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22; Genesis 22:17; Romans 9:27"> And your seed is as sand, &#124;&#124; And the offspring of your bowels as gravel, &#124;&#124; His name would not be cut off nor destroyed before Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:45; Jeremiah 50:8; Isaiah 52:9"> Go out from Babylon, flee from the Chaldeans, &#124;&#124; Declare with a voice of singing, &#124;&#124; Cause this to be heard, &#124;&#124; Bring it forth to the end of the earth, &#124;&#124; Say, “YHWH has redeemed His servant Jacob.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 17:6; Isaiah 43:19–20; Psalms 105:41"> And they have not thirsted in wastelands, &#124;&#124; He has caused them to go on, &#124;&#124; He has caused waters from a rock to flow to them, &#124;&#124; Indeed, he cleaves a rock, and waters flow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 57:21; Job 15:20–24; Luke 19:42"> “There is no peace,” said YHWH, “for the wicked!”</span>
