<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:26; Isaiah 41:1; Isaiah 1:2"> Come near, you nations, to hear, &#124;&#124; And you peoples, give attention, &#124;&#124; The earth and its fullness hear, &#124;&#124; The world, and all its productions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:15–29; Zephaniah 3:8; Zechariah 14:12–16"> For to YHWH &#91;is&#93; wrath against all the nations, &#124;&#124; And fury against all their host, &#124;&#124; He has devoted them to destruction, &#124;&#124; He has given them to slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 2:20; Ezekiel 39:4; Isaiah 34:7"> And their wounded are cast out, &#124;&#124; And their carcasses cause their stench to ascend, &#124;&#124; And mountains have been melted from their blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 6:13–14; Matthew 24:29; Ezekiel 32:7–8"> And all the host of the heavens have been consumed, &#124;&#124; And the heavens have been rolled together as a scroll, &#124;&#124; And all their hosts fade, &#124;&#124; As the fading of a leaf of a vine, &#124;&#124; And as the fading one of a fig tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:10; Malachi 1:4; Isaiah 63:1"> For My sword was soaked in the heavens, &#124;&#124; Behold, it comes down on Edom, &#124;&#124; On the people of My curse for judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:1; Jeremiah 49:13; Isaiah 34:5"> A sword &#91;is&#93; to YHWH—it has been full of blood, &#124;&#124; It has been made fat with fatness, &#124;&#124; With blood of lambs and male goats. With fat of kidneys of rams, &#124;&#124; For to YHWH &#91;is&#93; a sacrifice in Bozrah, &#124;&#124; And a great slaughter in the land of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 68:30; Numbers 23:22; Jeremiah 50:27"> And reems have come down with them, &#124;&#124; And bullocks with bulls, &#124;&#124; And their land has been soaked from blood, &#124;&#124; And their dust is made fat from fatness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 63:4; Isaiah 61:2; Jeremiah 46:10"> (For a day of vengeance &#91;is&#93; to YHWH, &#124;&#124; A year of recompenses for Zion’s strife),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; Psalms 11:6; Genesis 19:28"> And her streams have been turned to pitch, &#124;&#124; And her dust to brimstone, &#124;&#124; And her land has become burning pitch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 19:3; Isaiah 13:20; Malachi 1:3–4"> She is not quenched by night and by day, &#124;&#124; Her smoke goes up for all time, &#124;&#124; She is desolate from generation to generation, &#124;&#124; Forever and ever, none is passing into her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 2:8; 2 Kings 21:13; Zephaniah 2:14"> And pelican and hedgehog possess her, &#124;&#124; And owl and raven dwell in her, &#124;&#124; And He has stretched out over her &#124;&#124; A line of vacancy, and stones of emptiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 3:6–8; 1 Corinthians 13:2; Jeremiah 39:6"> They call her nobles &#91;to&#93; the kingdom, &#124;&#124; But there are none there, &#124;&#124; And all her princes are at an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 10:22; Malachi 1:3"> And thorns have gone up her palaces, &#124;&#124; Nettle and bramble &#91;are&#93; in her fortresses, &#124;&#124; And it has been a habitation of dragons, &#124;&#124; A court for daughters of an ostrich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 13:21–22"> And desert-dwellers have met with howlers, &#124;&#124; And the goat calls for its companion, &#124;&#124; Surely the night-owl has rested there, &#124;&#124; And has found a place of rest for herself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:13"> The owl has made her nest there, &#124;&#124; Indeed, she lays, and has hatched, &#124;&#124; And has gathered under her shadow, &#124;&#124; Surely vultures have been gathered there, &#124;&#124; Each with its companion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 33:9; Joshua 1:8; Matthew 5:18"> Seek out of the scroll of YHWH, and read, &#124;&#124; One of these has not been lacking, &#124;&#124; None has missed its companion, &#124;&#124; For My mouth—it has commanded, &#124;&#124; And His Spirit—He has gathered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 34:10; Acts 17:26; Isaiah 17:14"> And He has cast a lot for them, &#124;&#124; And His hand has apportioned &#91;it&#93; to them by line, &#124;&#124; They possess it for all time, &#124;&#124; They dwell in it from generation to generation!</span>
