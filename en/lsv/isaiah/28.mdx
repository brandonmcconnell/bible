<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 7:5; Isaiah 28:7; 2 Kings 14:25–27"> Woe &#91;to&#93; the proud crown of the drunkards of Ephraim. And the fading flower of the beauty of his glory, &#124;&#124; That &#91;is&#93; on the head of the fat valley of the broken down of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:30; Nahum 1:8; Ezekiel 13:11"> Behold, a mighty and strong one &#91;is&#93; to the Lord, &#124;&#124; As a storm of hail—a destructive shower, &#124;&#124; As an inundation of mighty waters overflowing, &#124;&#124; He cast down to the earth with the hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 26:6; Hebrews 10:29; Revelation 11:2"> The proud crown of the drunkards of Ephraim is trodden down by feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nahum 3:12; Micah 7:1; Isaiah 28:1"> And the fading flower of the beauty of his glory &#124;&#124; That &#91;is&#93; on the head of the fat valley, &#124;&#124; Has been as its first-fruit before summer, &#124;&#124; That its beholder sees; He swallows it while it &#91;is&#93; yet in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 5:4; Isaiah 62:3; Isaiah 60:19"> In that day YHWH of Hosts is &#124;&#124; For a crown of beauty, and for a circlet of glory, &#124;&#124; To the remnant of His people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 18:32–34; John 5:30; Joshua 1:9"> And for a spirit of judgment &#124;&#124; To him who is sitting in the judgment, &#124;&#124; And for might &#91;to&#93; those turning back the battle to the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 56:10–12; Luke 21:34; Isaiah 19:14"> And even these have erred through wine, &#124;&#124; And have wandered through strong drink, &#124;&#124; Priest and prophet erred through strong drink, &#124;&#124; They have been swallowed up of the wine, &#124;&#124; They wandered because of the strong drink, &#124;&#124; They have erred in seeing, &#124;&#124; They have stumbled judicially.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:26; Proverbs 26:11; Habakkuk 2:15–16"> For all tables have been full of vomit, &#124;&#124; Filth—without place!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 131:2; Jeremiah 6:10; John 12:38"> By whom does He teach knowledge? And by whom does He cause to understand the report? The weaned from milk, the removed from breasts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 28:13; 2 Timothy 3:7; Jeremiah 11:7"> For rule on rule, rule on rule, &#124;&#124; Line on line, line on line, &#124;&#124; A little here, a little there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:21; Deuteronomy 28:49; Isaiah 33:19"> For by scorned lip, and by another tongue, &#124;&#124; Does He speak to this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 11:28–29; Isaiah 30:15; Jeremiah 6:16"> To whom He has said, “This &#91;is&#93; the rest, give rest to the weary, &#124;&#124; And this—the refreshing”: And they have not been willing to hear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 13:14; Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Peter 3:16"> And to whom a word of YHWH has been, &#124;&#124; Rule on rule, rule on rule, &#124;&#124; Line on line, line on line, &#124;&#124; A little here, a little there, &#124;&#124; So that they go and have stumbled backward, &#124;&#124; And been broken, and snared, and captured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 28:22; Isaiah 29:20; Isaiah 1:10"> Therefore, hear a word of YHWH, you men of scorning, &#124;&#124; Ruling this people that &#91;is&#93; in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Ezekiel 13:22; Amos 2:4"> Because you have said: “We have made a covenant with death, &#124;&#124; And we have made a provision with Sheol, &#124;&#124; An overflowing scourge, when it passes over, &#124;&#124; Does not meet us, &#124;&#124; Though we have made a lie our refuge, &#124;&#124; And have been hidden in falsehood.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 118:22; Acts 4:11–12; 1 Peter 2:6–8"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: “Behold, I am laying a foundation in Zion, &#124;&#124; A stone—a tried stone, a precious corner stone, a settled foundation, &#124;&#124; He who is believing does not make haste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Amos 7:7–9; Revelation 16:21"> And I have put judgment for a line, &#124;&#124; And righteousness for a plummet, &#124;&#124; And hail sweeps away the refuge of lies, &#124;&#124; And waters overflow the secret hiding place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 7:7; Isaiah 8:10; Isaiah 28:15"> And your covenant with death has been annulled, &#124;&#124; And your provision with Sheol does not stand, &#124;&#124; An overflowing scourge, when it passes over, &#124;&#124; Then you have been to it for a treading-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Isaiah 36:22; 2 Kings 24:2"> From the fullness of its passing over it takes you, &#124;&#124; For it passes over morning by morning, &#124;&#124; By day and by night, &#124;&#124; And it has been only a trembling to consider the report.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; 1 Corinthians 1:18–31; Romans 9:30–32"> For the bed has been shorter &#124;&#124; Than to stretch one’s self out in, &#124;&#124; And the covering has been narrower &#124;&#124; Than to wrap one’s self up in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:11; Joshua 10:12; 2 Samuel 5:20"> For as YHWH rises &#91;at&#93; Mount Perazim, &#124;&#124; As He is troubled &#91;at&#93; the valley in Gibeon, &#124;&#124; To do His work—strange &#91;is&#93; His work, &#124;&#124; And to do His deed—strange &#91;is&#93; His deed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22–23; Isaiah 24:1–23; Matthew 27:44"> And now, do not show yourselves scorners, &#124;&#124; Lest your bands are strong, &#124;&#124; For a consumption, that is determined, &#124;&#124; I have heard, by the Lord, YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; &#91;Is&#93; for all the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Jeremiah 22:29; Revelation 2:11"> Give ear, and hear my voice, &#124;&#124; Attend, and hear my saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Hosea 10:11–12"> Does the plowman plow the whole day to sow? He opens and harrows his ground!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:9; Matthew 23:23; Exodus 9:31–32"> Has he not, if he has made its face level, &#124;&#124; Then scattered fitches, and sprinkles cumin, &#124;&#124; And has placed the principal wheat, &#124;&#124; And the appointed barley, &#124;&#124; And the rye &#91;in&#93; its own border?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 31:3–6; Exodus 36:2; Job 39:17"> And his God instructs him for judgment, &#124;&#124; He directs him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15; Isaiah 27:7–8; Jeremiah 46:28"> For fitches are not threshed with a sharp-pointed thing, &#124;&#124; And the wheel of a cart turned around on cumin, &#124;&#124; For fitches are beaten out with a staff, &#124;&#124; And cumin with a rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:9–10; Matthew 3:12; John 12:24"> Bread-&#91;grain&#93; is beaten small, &#124;&#124; For he does not severely thresh it forever, &#124;&#124; Nor has a wheel of his cart crushed &#91;it&#93;, &#124;&#124; Nor do his hooves beat it small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Jeremiah 32:19; Job 5:9"> Even this from YHWH of Hosts has gone out, &#124;&#124; He has made counsel wonderful, &#124;&#124; He has made wisdom great!</span>
