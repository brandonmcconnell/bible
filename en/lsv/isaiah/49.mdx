<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Galatians 1:15; Isaiah 44:2"> Listen, O islands, to Me, &#124;&#124; And attend, O peoples, from afar, &#124;&#124; YHWH has called Me from the womb, &#124;&#124; From the bowels of My mother &#124;&#124; He has made mention of My Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 1:16; Isaiah 50:4"> And He makes My mouth as a sharp sword, &#124;&#124; He has hid Me in the shadow of His hand, &#124;&#124; And He makes Me for a clear arrow, &#124;&#124; He has hid Me in His quiver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; 1 Peter 2:9; Isaiah 44:23"> And He says to me, “You are My servant, O Israel, &#124;&#124; In whom I beautify Myself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; Galatians 4:11; Isaiah 53:10–12"> And I said, “I labored in vain, &#124;&#124; I consumed my power for emptiness and vanity, &#124;&#124; But my judgment &#91;is&#93; with YHWH, &#124;&#124; And my wage with my God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Matthew 17:5; John 3:35"> “And now,” said YHWH, who is forming Me from the belly—His Servant, &#124;&#124; To bring Jacob back to Him &#124;&#124; (Though Israel is not gathered, &#124;&#124; Yet I am honored in the eyes of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And My God has been My strength).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 13:47; Isaiah 42:6; Luke 2:32"> And He says, “It has been a light thing &#124;&#124; That You are My Servant &#124;&#124; To raise up the tribes of Jacob, &#124;&#124; And to bring back the preserved of Israel, &#124;&#124; And I have given You for a light to the nations, &#124;&#124; To be My salvation to the end of the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; 1 Peter 2:4; Isaiah 53:3"> Thus said YHWH, Redeemer of Israel, His Holy One, &#124;&#124; To the despised in soul, &#124;&#124; To the detested of a nation, &#124;&#124; To the Servant of rulers: “Kings see, and have risen, princes, and worship, &#124;&#124; For the sake of YHWH, who is faithful, &#124;&#124; The Holy of Israel, and He chooses You.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:2; Isaiah 42:6; Psalms 69:13"> Thus said YHWH: “In a time of good pleasure I answered You, &#124;&#124; And in a day of salvation I helped You, &#124;&#124; And I keep You, and give You, &#124;&#124; For a covenant of the people, &#124;&#124; To establish the earth, &#124;&#124; To cause to inherit desolate inheritances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 42:7; Isaiah 42:16; 1 Peter 2:9"> To say to the bound, Go out, &#124;&#124; To those in darkness, Be uncovered. They feed on the ways, &#124;&#124; And their pasture is in all high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 7:16–17; Matthew 5:6; Ezekiel 34:23"> They do not hunger, nor thirst, &#124;&#124; Nor do mirage and sun strike them, &#124;&#124; For He who is pitying them leads them, &#124;&#124; And tends them by fountains of waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 11:16; Isaiah 62:10; Isaiah 35:8–10"> And I have made all My mountains for a way, &#124;&#124; And My highways are lifted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5–6; Matthew 8:11; Revelation 11:15"> Behold, these come in from afar, &#124;&#124; And behold, these from the north, and from the sea, &#124;&#124; And these from the land of Sinim.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 44:23; Isaiah 51:3; Psalms 96:11–13"> Sing, O heavens, and rejoice, O earth, &#124;&#124; And break forth, O mountains, with singing, &#124;&#124; For YHWH has comforted His people, &#124;&#124; And He pities His afflicted ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:27; Romans 11:1–5; Psalms 22:1"> And Zion says, “YHWH has forsaken me, &#124;&#124; And my Lord has forgotten me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 44:21; Psalms 103:13; Matthew 7:11"> “Does a woman forget her suckling, &#124;&#124; The loved one—the son of her womb? Indeed, these forget—but I do not forget you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Haggai 2:23; Isaiah 62:6; Isaiah 60:18"> Behold, I have carved you on the palms of the hand, &#124;&#124; Your walls &#91;are&#93; continually before Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 51:13; Isaiah 62:5; Ezekiel 28:24"> Those building you have hurried, &#124;&#124; Those destroying you, and laying you waste, go out from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 54:1–3; Isaiah 66:12–13; Isaiah 61:10"> Lift up your eyes, &#91;look&#93; around and see, &#124;&#124; All of them have been gathered, &#124;&#124; They have come to you. &#91;As&#93; I live,” a declaration of YHWH! “Surely you put on all of them as an ornament, &#124;&#124; And you bind them on like a bride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 10:10; Isaiah 54:1–2; Jeremiah 30:18–19"> Because your ruins, and your desolate places, &#124;&#124; And the land of your ruins, &#124;&#124; Are now surely restricted because of inhabitants, &#124;&#124; And those consuming you have been far off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hosea 1:10; Joshua 17:14–16; Galatians 4:26–28"> Again the sons of your bereavement say in your ears: The place is too narrow for me, &#124;&#124; Come near to me—and I dwell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13; Isaiah 3:26; Lamentations 1:1–3"> And you have said in your heart: Who has begotten these for me? And I &#91;am&#93; bereaved and barren, &#124;&#124; A captive, and turned aside, &#124;&#124; And who has nourished these? Behold, I was left by myself, these—where &#91;are&#93; they from?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Luke 13:29; Isaiah 14:2"> Thus said Lord YHWH: “Behold, I lift up My hand to nations, &#124;&#124; And I raise up My ensign to peoples, &#124;&#124; And they have brought your sons in the bosom, &#124;&#124; And your daughters are carried on the shoulder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:14; Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 60:3"> And kings have been your nursing fathers, &#124;&#124; And their princesses—your nursing mothers; Face to the earth—they bow down to you, &#124;&#124; And they lick up the dust of your feet, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; That those expecting Me are not ashamed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 11:21–22; Psalms 124:6–7; Matthew 12:29"> Is prey taken from the mighty? And the captive of the righteous delivered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 54:15–17; Isaiah 35:4; Isaiah 41:11–12"> For thus said YHWH: “Even the captive of the mighty is taken, &#124;&#124; And the prey of the terrible is delivered, &#124;&#124; And I strive with your striver, and I save your sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Isaiah 41:14–20; Revelation 16:6"> And I have caused your oppressors to eat their own flesh, &#124;&#124; And they drink their own blood as new wine, &#124;&#124; And all flesh has known that I, YHWH, &#124;&#124; Your Savior, and your Redeemer, &#124;&#124; &#91;Am&#93; the Mighty One of Jacob!”</span>
