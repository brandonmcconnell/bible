<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 7:13–14; Isaiah 42:16; John 14:6"> And a highway has been there, and a way, &#124;&#124; And it is called the “Way of Holiness.” The unclean do not pass over it, &#124;&#124; And He Himself &#91;is&#93; by them, &#124;&#124; Whoever is going in the way—even fools do not err.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 62:12; Hosea 2:18; Leviticus 26:6"> No lion is there, &#124;&#124; Indeed, a destructive beast does not ascend it, &#124;&#124; It is not found there, &#124;&#124; And the redeemed have walked,</span>
