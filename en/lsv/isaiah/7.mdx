<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 15:37; 2 Kings 16:1; Isaiah 1:1"> And it comes to pass in the days of Ahaz, son of Jotham, son of Uzziah, king of Judah, &#91;that&#93; Rezin king of Aram, and Pekah, son of Remaliah, king of Israel, have gone up to Jerusalem, to battle against it, and he is not able to fight against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 8:12; Isaiah 7:13; Isaiah 9:9"> And it is declared to the house of David, saying, “Aram has been led toward Ephraim,” and his heart and the heart of his people is moved, like the moving of trees of a forest by the presence of wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Isaiah 36:2; Romans 9:27"> And YHWH says to Isaiah, “Now go forth to meet Ahaz, you and your son Shear-Jashub, to the end of the conduit of the upper pool, to the highway of the fuller’s field,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; Lamentations 3:26; Deuteronomy 20:3"> and you have said to him: Take heed, and be quiet, do not fear, &#124;&#124; And do not let your heart be timid, &#124;&#124; Because of these two tails of smoking brands, &#124;&#124; For the fierceness of the anger of Rezin and Aram, &#124;&#124; And the son of Remaliah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 83:3–4; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 2:2"> Because that Aram counseled evil against you, Ephraim and the son of Remaliah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> We go up into Judah, and we distress it, &#124;&#124; And we divide it to ourselves, &#124;&#124; And we cause a king to reign in its midst—The son of Tabeal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:10; Lamentations 3:37; Isaiah 46:10–11"> Thus said Lord YHWH: It does not stand, nor will it be!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 17:1–3; Genesis 14:15; 2 Samuel 8:6"> For the head of Aram &#91;is&#93; Damascus, &#124;&#124; And the head of Damascus &#91;is&#93; Rezin, &#124;&#124; And within sixty-five years &#124;&#124; Is Ephraim broken from &#91;being&#93; a people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 11:6; Romans 11:20; 2 Chronicles 20:20"> And the head of Ephraim &#91;is&#93; Samaria, &#124;&#124; And the head of Samaria &#91;is&#93; the son of Remaliah. If you do not give credence, &#124;&#124; Surely you are not steadfast.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 8:5; Isaiah 1:5; Isaiah 10:20"> And YHWH adds to speak to Ahaz, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 37:30; Isaiah 38:7–8; Isaiah 38:22"> “Ask for a sign from your God YHWH, &#124;&#124; Make the request deep, or make &#91;it&#93; high upwards.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; 2 Chronicles 28:22; Malachi 3:15"> And Ahaz says, “I do not ask nor try YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 43:24; Isaiah 63:10; Ezekiel 34:18"> And he says, “Now hear, O house of David, &#124;&#124; Is it a little thing for you to weary men, &#124;&#124; That you also weary my God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 1:23; Isaiah 9:6; Luke 1:31"> Therefore the Lord Himself gives a sign to you, &#124;&#124; Behold, the virgin is conceiving, &#124;&#124; And is bringing forth a Son, &#124;&#124; And has called His Name Immanuel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 7:22; Amos 5:15; Matthew 3:4"> He eats butter and honey, &#124;&#124; When He knows to refuse evil, and to fix on good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 8:4; Deuteronomy 1:39; Isaiah 9:11"> For before the youth knows &#124;&#124; To refuse evil, and to fix on good, &#124;&#124; The land you are distressed with is forsaken, because of her two kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Isaiah 10:5–6; 2 Chronicles 28:19–21"> YHWH brings on you, and on your people, &#124;&#124; And on the house of your father, &#124;&#124; Days that have not come, &#124;&#124; Even from the day of the turning aside of Ephraim from Judah, &#124;&#124; By the king of Asshur.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 5:26; Joshua 24:12; Deuteronomy 1:44"> And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; YHWH hisses for a fly that &#91;is&#93; in the extremity of the brooks of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And for a bee that &#91;is&#93; in the land of Asshur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19; Jeremiah 16:16; Isaiah 2:21"> And they have come, and all of them rested in the desolate valleys, &#124;&#124; And in holes of the rocks, and on all the thorns, &#124;&#124; And on all the commendable things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7; Ezekiel 29:18; Isaiah 10:15"> In that day the Lord shaves, &#124;&#124; By a razor that is hired beyond the river, &#124;&#124; By the king of Asshur, &#124;&#124; The head, and the hair of the feet, &#124;&#124; Indeed, it also consumes the beard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:17; Jeremiah 39:10; Isaiah 37:30"> And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; A man keeps alive a heifer of the herd, &#124;&#124; And two of the flock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15; Matthew 3:4; 2 Samuel 17:29"> And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; He eats butter from the abundance of the yielding of milk, &#124;&#124; For everyone who is left in the heart of the land eats butter and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 5:6; Song of Songs 8:11–12; Matthew 21:33"> And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; Every place where there are one thousand vines, &#124;&#124; At one thousand pieces of silver, &#124;&#124; Is for briers and for thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 27:3"> He comes there with arrows and with bow, &#124;&#124; Because all the land is brier and thorn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 7:21–22; Isaiah 13:20–22; Isaiah 5:17"> And all the hills that are kept in order with a mattock, &#124;&#124; There does not come &#91;for&#93; fear of brier and thorn, &#124;&#124; And it has been for the sending forth of ox, &#124;&#124; And for the treading of sheep!</span>
