<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Isaiah 51:17; Zechariah 3:4"> Awake, awake, put on your strength, O Zion, &#124;&#124; Put on the garments of your beauty, Jerusalem—the Holy City; For the uncircumcised and unclean no longer enter into you again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 51:14; Jeremiah 51:50; Luke 4:18"> Shake yourself from dust, arise, sit, O Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; Bands of your neck have loosed themselves, &#124;&#124; O captive daughter of Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18; Isaiah 45:13; Psalms 44:12"> For thus said YHWH: “You have been sold for nothing, &#124;&#124; And you are redeemed without money.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:6; Psalms 69:4; Acts 7:14–15"> For thus said Lord YHWH: “My people went down at first &#124;&#124; To Egypt to sojourn there, &#124;&#124; And Asshur—he has oppressed it for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Psalms 44:16; Psalms 74:10"> And now, what &#91;have&#93; I here,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “That My people are taken for nothing? Its rulers cause howling,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And My Name is continually despised all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 8:10–11; Ezekiel 37:13–14; Ezekiel 20:44"> Therefore My people know My Name, &#124;&#124; Therefore, in that day, &#124;&#124; Surely I &#91;am&#93; He who is speaking, behold Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 10:12–15; Nahum 1:15; Isaiah 61:1–3"> How lovely on the mountains, &#124;&#124; Have been the feet of one proclaiming tidings, &#124;&#124; Sounding peace, proclaiming good tidings, &#124;&#124; Sounding salvation, &#124;&#124; Saying to Zion, “Your God has reigned.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 62:6; Ezekiel 33:7; Jeremiah 32:39"> The voice of your watchmen! They have lifted up the voice, together they cry aloud, &#124;&#124; Because they see eye to eye, &#124;&#124; In YHWH’s turning back &#91;to&#93; Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Psalms 98:4; Isaiah 48:20"> Break forth, sing together, &#124;&#124; O ruins of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; For YHWH has comforted His people, &#124;&#124; He has redeemed Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 3:6; Isaiah 51:9; Psalms 98:1–3"> YHWH has made His holy arm bare &#124;&#124; Before the eyes of all the nations, &#124;&#124; And all the ends of the earth have seen &#124;&#124; The salvation of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:17; Zechariah 2:6–7; Isaiah 48:20"> Turn aside, turn aside, go out from there, &#124;&#124; Do not touch the unclean, &#124;&#124; Go out from her midst, &#124;&#124; Be pure, who are carrying the weapons of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 58:8; Deuteronomy 20:4; Exodus 14:19–20"> For you do not go out in haste, &#124;&#124; Indeed, you do not go on with flight, &#124;&#124; For YHWH &#91;is&#93; going before you, &#124;&#124; And the God of Israel &#91;is&#93; gathering you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:7–11; Matthew 28:18; John 3:31"> “Behold, My Servant acts wisely, &#124;&#124; He is high, and has been lifted up, &#124;&#124; And has been very high.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:2–5; Matthew 26:67"> As many have been astonished at You &#124;&#124; (His appearance so marred by man, &#124;&#124; And His form by sons of men),</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:20–21; Isaiah 49:7; Isaiah 55:5"> So He sprinkles many nations. Kings shut their mouth concerning Him, &#124;&#124; For that which was not recounted to them they have seen, &#124;&#124; And that which they had not heard they have understood!</span>
