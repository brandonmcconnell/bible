<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Micah 3:1–4; Matthew 23:23"> “Woe &#91;to&#93; those decreeing decrees of iniquity, &#124;&#124; And writers who have prescribed perverseness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Isaiah 3:14; Isaiah 29:21"> Of My people, &#124;&#124; That widows may be their prey, &#124;&#124; That they may spoil the fatherless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 9:7; Luke 19:44; Job 31:14"> And what do you do at a day of inspection? And at desolation that comes from afar? Near whom do you flee for help? And where do you leave your glory?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25; Isaiah 9:12; Jeremiah 37:10"> It has bowed down without Me &#124;&#124; In the place of a bound one, &#124;&#124; And they fall in the place of the slain.” With all this His anger has not turned back, &#124;&#124; And His hand is still stretched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:20–24; Isaiah 14:25; Isaiah 30:30"> “Woe &#91;to&#93; Asshur, a rod of My anger, &#124;&#124; And My indignation &#91;is&#93; a staff in their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 9:17; Jeremiah 25:9; Zechariah 10:5"> I send him against a profane nation, &#124;&#124; And concerning a people of My wrath &#124;&#124; I charge him, &#124;&#124; To spoil spoil, and to seize prey, &#124;&#124; And to make it a treading-place as the clay of out places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Micah 4:11–12; Acts 2:23"> And he does not think &#91;it&#93; so, &#124;&#124; And his heart does not reckon &#91;it&#93; so, &#124;&#124; For—to destroy &#91;is&#93; in his heart, &#124;&#124; And to cut off nations—not a few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 18:24; 2 Kings 19:10; Ezekiel 26:7"> For he says, Are my princes not altogether kings?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:20; 2 Kings 16:9; Genesis 10:10"> Is not Calno as Carchemish? Is not Hamath as Arpad? Is not Samaria as Damascus?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 10:14; 2 Kings 19:17–19; 2 Kings 18:33–35"> As my hand has gotten to the kingdoms of a worthless thing, and their carved images, &#124;&#124; &#91;Greater&#93; than Jerusalem and than Samaria,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 37:10–13; Isaiah 2:8; Isaiah 36:19–20"> Do I not—as I have done to Samaria, &#124;&#124; And to her worthless things, &#124;&#124; So do to Jerusalem and to her grievous things?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 19:31; Isaiah 65:7; Jeremiah 50:18"> And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; When the Lord fulfills all His work &#124;&#124; In Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; I see concerning the fruit of the greatness &#124;&#124; Of the heart of the king of Asshur, &#124;&#124; And concerning the glory of the height of his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:17; Daniel 4:30; Ezekiel 26:2"> For he has said, “I have worked by the power of my hand, &#124;&#124; And by my wisdom, for I have been intelligent, &#124;&#124; And I remove borders of the peoples, &#124;&#124; And I have spoiled their chief ones, &#124;&#124; And I put down the inhabitants as a mighty one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 31:25; Proverbs 21:6–7; Nahum 2:9–1"> And my hand gets to the wealth of the peoples as to a nest, &#124;&#124; And as a gathering of forsaken eggs &#124;&#124; I have gathered all the earth, &#124;&#124; And there has not been one moving wing, &#124;&#124; Or opening mouth, or whispering.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 9:20–21; Isaiah 45:9; Isaiah 10:5"> Does the axe glorify itself &#124;&#124; Against him who is hewing with it? Does the saw magnify itself &#124;&#124; Against him who is shaking it? As a rod waving those lifting it up! As a staff lifting up that which is not wood!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:21; Acts 12:23; Psalms 106:15"> Therefore the Lord, the Lord of Hosts, &#124;&#124; Sends leanness among his fat ones, &#124;&#124; And under his glory &#124;&#124; He kindles a burning &#124;&#124; As the burning of a fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 27:4; Numbers 11:1–3; Isaiah 37:23"> And the light of Israel has been for a fire, &#124;&#124; And his Holy One for a flame, &#124;&#124; And it has burned, and devoured his thorn &#124;&#124; And his brier in one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 19:23; Isaiah 10:33–34; Isaiah 9:18"> And the glory of his forest, and his fruitful field, &#124;&#124; He consumes from soul even to flesh, &#124;&#124; And it has been as the fainting of a standard-bearer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 21:17; Isaiah 37:36"> And the rest of the trees of his forest &#91;are&#93; few, &#124;&#124; And a youth writes them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 37:31–32; Isaiah 50:10; Isaiah 1:9"> And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; The remnant of Israel, &#124;&#124; And the escaped of the house of Jacob, &#124;&#124; Do not add anymore to lean on its striker, &#124;&#124; And have leaned on YHWH, &#124;&#124; The Holy One of Israel, in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; 2 Corinthians 3:14–16; Hosea 7:10"> A remnant returns—a remnant of Jacob, &#124;&#124; To the Mighty God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 9:27–28; Isaiah 6:13; Revelation 20:8"> For though your people Israel be as the sand of the sea, &#124;&#124; A remnant of it returns, &#124;&#124; A consumption determined, &#124;&#124; Overflowing &#91;with&#93; righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 28:22; Isaiah 24:1–23; Romans 9:28"> For a consumption that is determined, &#124;&#124; The Lord, YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; Is making in the midst of all the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 12:22–24; Isaiah 37:6; Isaiah 61:3"> Therefore, thus said the Lord, YHWH of Hosts: “Do not be afraid, My people, inhabiting Zion, because of Asshur, &#124;&#124; He strikes you with a rod, &#124;&#124; And his staff lifts up against you, in the way of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 11:36; 2 Kings 19:35; Hebrews 10:37"> For yet a very little &#91;while&#93;, &#124;&#124; And the indignation has been completed, &#124;&#124; And My anger by their wearing out.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Judges 7:25; 2 Kings 19:35"> And YHWH of Hosts is awaking for him, &#124;&#124; A scourge like the striking of Midian at the rock Oreb, &#124;&#124; And his rod &#91;is&#93; over the sea, &#124;&#124; And he has lifted it in the way of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Luke 4:18; Isaiah 14:25"> And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; His burden is turned from off your shoulder, &#124;&#124; And his yoke from off your neck, &#124;&#124; And the yoke has been destroyed, because of prosperity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:2; 1 Samuel 13:2; 1 Samuel 13:5"> He has come in against Aiath, &#124;&#124; He has passed over into Migron, &#124;&#124; At Michmash he looks after his vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:23; 1 Samuel 7:17; Joshua 21:17"> They have gone over the passage, &#124;&#124; They have made Geba a lodging place, &#124;&#124; Rama has trembled, &#124;&#124; Gibeah of Saul fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:44; Jeremiah 1:1; Joshua 21:18"> Cry aloud &#91;with&#93; your voice, daughter of Gallim, &#124;&#124; Give attention, Laish! Answer her, Anathoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 15:31"> Madmenah has fled away, &#124;&#124; The inhabitants of the high places have hardened themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:1; Isaiah 37:22; Isaiah 19:16"> Yet today to remain in Nob, &#124;&#124; The mountain of the daughter of Zion waves its hand, &#124;&#124; The hill of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Daniel 4:37; Isaiah 37:38"> Behold, the Lord, YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; Is lopping a branch with violence, &#124;&#124; And the high of stature are cut down, &#124;&#124; And the lofty have become low,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 37:24; Daniel 4:23; Jeremiah 48:2"> And He has gone around the thickets of the forest with iron, &#124;&#124; And Lebanon falls by a mighty one!</span>
