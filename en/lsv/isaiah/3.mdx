<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Ezekiel 14:13; Isaiah 1:24"> For behold, the Lord, YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; Is turning aside from Jerusalem and from Judah &#124;&#124; &#91;Both&#93; stay and staff: Every stay of bread and every stay of water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 9:14–15; 2 Kings 24:14–16; Lamentations 5:12–14"> Mighty and man of war, judge and prophet, &#124;&#124; And diviner and elderly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 8:18; Deuteronomy 1:15; Exodus 18:21"> Head of fifty, and accepted of faces, &#124;&#124; And counselor, and wise craftsmen, &#124;&#124; And discerning charmer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:16; 2 Chronicles 33:1; 1 Kings 3:7–9"> And I have made youths their heads, &#124;&#124; And sucklings rule over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:3–8; Malachi 3:5; Matthew 27:28–30"> And the people has exacted—man on man, &#124;&#124; Even a man on his neighbor, &#124;&#124; The youths enlarge themselves against the aged, &#124;&#124; And the lightly esteemed against the honored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 4:1; John 6:15; Judges 11:6–8"> When one lays hold on his brother, &#124;&#124; Of the house of his father, &#91;by&#93; the garment, “Come, you are a ruler to us, &#124;&#124; And this ruin &#91;is&#93; under your hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 5:13; Lamentations 2:13; Ezekiel 34:4"> He lifts up, in that day, saying, “I am not a binder up, &#124;&#124; And in my house is neither bread nor garment, &#124;&#124; You do not make me a ruler of the people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:22; Ezekiel 9:9; Malachi 3:13–15"> For Jerusalem has stumbled, and Judah has fallen, &#124;&#124; For their tongue and their doings &#91;are&#93; against YHWH, &#124;&#124; To provoke the eyes of His glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 13:13; Genesis 18:20–21; 2 Kings 9:30"> The appearance of their faces witnessed against them, &#124;&#124; And their sin, as Sodom, they declared, &#124;&#124; They have not hidden! Woe to their soul, &#124;&#124; For they have done evil to themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 128:1–2; Hebrews 6:10; Ecclesiastes 8:12"> Say to the righteous that &#91;it is&#93; good, &#124;&#124; Because they eat the fruit of their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:13; James 2:13; Psalms 62:12"> Woe to the wicked—evil, &#124;&#124; Because the deed of his hand is done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 3:4; Nahum 3:13; 2 Kings 11:1"> My people—its exactors &#91;are&#93; sucklings, &#124;&#124; And women have ruled over it. My people—your eulogists are causing to err, &#124;&#124; And the way of your paths swallowed up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 6:2; Hosea 4:1–2; Proverbs 22:22–23"> YHWH has stood up to plead, &#124;&#124; And He is standing to judge the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:4; Psalms 143:2; Isaiah 3:2–3"> YHWH enters into judgment &#124;&#124; With &#91;the&#93; elderly of His people, and its heads: “And you, you have consumed the vineyard, &#124;&#124; Plunder of the poor &#91;is&#93; in your houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 3:2–3; Psalms 94:5; Amos 2:6–7"> Why? Why do you &#91;do this&#93;? You bruise My people, &#124;&#124; And you grind the faces of the poor.” A declaration of the Lord, YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; And YHWH says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 32:9–11; Ezekiel 16:49–50; Isaiah 4:4"> “Because that daughters of Zion have been haughty, &#124;&#124; And they walk stretching out the neck, &#124;&#124; And deceiving &#91;with&#93; the eyes, &#124;&#124; They go walking and mincing, &#124;&#124; And they make a jingling with their feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:22; Micah 1:11; Revelation 16:2"> The Lord has also scabbed &#124;&#124; The crown of the head of daughters of Zion, &#124;&#124; And YHWH exposes their simplicity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 8:21; Judges 8:26"> In that day the Lord turns aside &#124;&#124; The beauty of the jingling ornaments, &#124;&#124; And of the embroidered works, &#124;&#124; And of the crescents,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:11; Numbers 31:50; Exodus 35:22"> Of the pendants, and the bracelets, and the veils,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 39:28; Genesis 35:4; Ezekiel 16:12"> Of the headdresses, and the ornaments of the legs, &#124;&#124; And of the bands, &#124;&#124; And of the perfume boxes, and the amulets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 24:47; 1 Peter 3:3–4; Luke 15:22"> Of the seals, and of the nose-rings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Of the costly apparel, and of the mantles, &#124;&#124; And of the coverings, and of the purses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 38:8; Ruth 3:15; Ezekiel 16:10"> Of the mirrors, and of the linen garments, &#124;&#124; And of the hoods, and of the veils,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Lamentations 2:10; Ezekiel 27:31; Isaiah 22:12"> And it has been, instead of spice is muck, &#124;&#124; And instead of a girdle, a rope, &#124;&#124; And instead of curled work, baldness, &#124;&#124; And instead of a stomacher a girdle of sackcloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:9; Isaiah 1:20; Amos 9:10"> For instead of glory, your men fall by sword, &#124;&#124; And your might in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Lamentations 2:10; Jeremiah 14:2; Lamentations 1:4"> And her openings have lamented and mourned, &#124;&#124; Indeed, she has been emptied, she sits on the earth!”</span>
