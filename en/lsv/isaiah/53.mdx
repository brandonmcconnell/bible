<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 12:38; Romans 10:16–17; 1 Corinthians 1:18"> Who has given credence to that which we heard? And the arm of YHWH, &#124;&#124; On whom has it been revealed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:6–7; Isaiah 52:14; John 1:10–14"> Indeed, He comes up as a tender plant before Him, &#124;&#124; And as a root out of dry land, &#124;&#124; He has no form or splendor when we observe Him, &#124;&#124; Nor appearance, that we desire Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; John 1:10–11; Isaiah 53:10"> He is despised, and left of men, &#124;&#124; A Man of pains, and acquainted with sickness, &#124;&#124; And as one hiding the face from us, &#124;&#124; He is despised, and we did not esteem Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Matthew 8:17; Galatians 3:13"> Surely He has borne our sicknesses, &#124;&#124; And our pains—He has carried them, &#124;&#124; And we have esteemed Him &#91;as&#93; plagued, struck of God, and afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24–25; 1 Peter 3:18; Matthew 20:28"> And He is pierced for our transgressions, bruised for our iniquities, &#124;&#124; The discipline of our peace &#91;is&#93; on Him, &#124;&#124; And by His scourging we are healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:25; 1 Peter 3:18; Psalms 119:176"> All of us, like sheep, have wandered, &#124;&#124; Man has turned to his own way, &#124;&#124; And YHWH has laid on Him the punishment of us all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 8:32–33; Matthew 26:63; 1 Peter 2:23"> It has been exacted, and He has answered, &#124;&#124; And He does not open His mouth, &#124;&#124; He is brought as a lamb to the slaughter, &#124;&#124; And as a sheep before its shearers is silent, &#124;&#124; So He does not open His mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 3:18; Acts 8:33; Matthew 26:65–66"> By restraint and by judgment He has been taken, &#124;&#124; And of His generation who meditates, &#124;&#124; That He has been cut off from the land of the living? He is plagued by the transgression of My people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 27:57–60; 1 Peter 2:22; 1 John 3:5"> And He appoints His grave with the wicked, &#124;&#124; And with the rich at His death, &#124;&#124; Because He has done no violence, &#124;&#124; Nor &#91;is&#93; deceit in His mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:32; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Galatians 3:13"> And YHWH has delighted to crush Him, &#124;&#124; He has made Him sick; If His soul makes an offering for guilt, &#124;&#124; He sees &#91;His&#93; seed—He prolongs &#91;His&#93; days, &#124;&#124; And the pleasure of YHWH prospers in His hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 5:18–19; 1 Peter 2:24; Isaiah 53:4–6"> Of the labor of His soul He sees—He is satisfied, &#124;&#124; Through His knowledge My Righteous Servant gives righteousness to many, &#124;&#124; And He bears their iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 22:37; Hebrews 9:28; Luke 23:32–34"> Therefore I give a portion to Him among the many, &#124;&#124; And He apportions spoil with the mighty, &#124;&#124; Because that He exposed His soul to death, &#124;&#124; And He was numbered with transgressors, &#124;&#124; And He has borne the sin of many, &#124;&#124; And He intercedes for transgressors.</span>
