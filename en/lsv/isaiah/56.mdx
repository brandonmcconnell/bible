<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 13:11–14; Psalms 85:9; Matthew 3:2"> Thus said YHWH: “Keep judgment, and do righteousness, &#124;&#124; For My salvation &#91;is&#93; near to come, &#124;&#124; And My righteousness to be revealed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 31:13–16; Isaiah 58:13; Psalms 112:1"> O the blessedness of a man who does this, &#124;&#124; And of a son of man who keeps hold on it, &#124;&#124; Keeping the Sabbath from defiling it, &#124;&#124; And keeping his hand from doing any evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:17; Acts 10:34; Matthew 19:12–30"> Do not let a son of the stranger speak, &#124;&#124; Who is joined to YHWH, saying, “YHWH certainly separates me from His people.” Nor let the eunuch say, “Behold, I am a dried up tree,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:111; Luke 10:42; Isaiah 27:5"> For thus said YHWH: “To the eunuchs who keep My Sabbaths, &#124;&#124; And have fixed on that which I desired, &#124;&#124; And are keeping hold on My covenant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:12; 1 John 3:1; Revelation 3:5"> I have given to them in My house, &#124;&#124; And within My walls a station and a name, &#124;&#124; Better than sons and than daughters, &#124;&#124; I give a continuous name to him &#124;&#124; That is not cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; Isaiah 56:2–4; Isaiah 58:13"> And sons of the stranger, who are joined to YHWH, &#124;&#124; To serve Him, and to love the Name of YHWH, &#124;&#124; To be for servants to Him, &#124;&#124; All keeping from defiling the Sabbath, &#124;&#124; And those keeping hold on My covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 11:17; Isaiah 2:2–3; Malachi 1:11"> I have brought them to My holy mountain, &#124;&#124; And caused them to rejoice in My house of prayer, &#124;&#124; Their burnt-offerings and their sacrifices &#124;&#124; &#91;Are&#93; for a pleasing thing on My altar, &#124;&#124; For My house is called a house of prayer for all the peoples.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:16; Isaiah 54:7; Isaiah 60:3–11"> A declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; Who is gathering the outcasts of Israel: “Again I gather to him—to his gathered ones.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:9; Revelation 19:17–18; Isaiah 18:6"> Every beast of the field, &#124;&#124; Come to devour, every beast in the forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 29:10; Philippians 3:2; Isaiah 58:1"> His watchmen &#91;are&#93; blind—all of them, &#124;&#124; They have not known, &#124;&#124; All of them &#91;are&#93; mute dogs, they are not able to bark, &#124;&#124; Dozing, lying down, loving to slumber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2–3; Ezekiel 13:19; Micah 3:11"> And the dogs &#91;are&#93; strong of desire, &#124;&#124; They have not known sufficiency, &#124;&#124; And they &#91;are&#93; shepherds! They have not known understanding, &#124;&#124; All of them turned to their own way, &#124;&#124; Each to his dishonest gain from his quarter:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 10:6; Proverbs 27:1; Luke 12:19–20"> “Come, I take wine, &#124;&#124; And we drink, gulp strong drink, &#124;&#124; And tomorrow has been as this day, &#124;&#124; Great—exceedingly abundant!”</span>
