<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Isaiah 24:16; Isaiah 10:12"> Woe, spoiler! And you not spoiled, &#124;&#124; And treacherous! And they did not deal treacherously with you, &#124;&#124; When you finish, O spoiler, you are spoiled, &#124;&#124; When you finish dealing treacherously, &#124;&#124; They deal treacherously with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; Isaiah 59:16; Isaiah 30:18–19"> O YHWH, favor us, &#124;&#124; We have waited for You, &#124;&#124; Be their arm, in the mornings, &#124;&#124; Indeed, our salvation in time of adversity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:13–14; Isaiah 10:32–34; Isaiah 37:11–18"> From the voice of a multitude peoples have fled, &#124;&#124; From Your exaltation nations have been scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 33:23; Joel 2:25; 2 Chronicles 20:25"> And Your spoil has been gathered, &#124;&#124; A gathering of the caterpillar, &#124;&#124; As a running to and fro of locusts &#124;&#124; He is running on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 2:17; Ephesians 1:20–21; Isaiah 32:15–18"> YHWH is set on high, for He is dwelling on high, &#124;&#124; He filled Zion &#91;with&#93; judgment and righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 112:1–3; Proverbs 19:23; 1 Timothy 4:8"> And has been the steadfastness of your times, &#124;&#124; The strength of salvation, wisdom, and knowledge, &#124;&#124; Fear of YHWH—it &#91;is&#93; His treasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 18:18; 2 Kings 18:37–3; Isaiah 36:22"> Behold, “Their Ariel,” they have cried outside, &#124;&#124; Messengers of peace weep bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 5:6; Isaiah 35:8; Lamentations 1:4"> Highways have been desolated, &#124;&#124; He who passes along the path has ceased, &#124;&#124; He has broken covenant, &#124;&#124; He has despised enemies, &#124;&#124; He has not esteemed a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Nahum 1:4; Isaiah 65:10"> The land has mourned, languished, &#124;&#124; Lebanon has been confounded, &#124;&#124; Sharon has been withered as a wilderness, &#124;&#124; And Bashan and Carmel are shaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 102:13–18; Amos 6:1"> Now I arise, says YHWH, &#124;&#124; Now I am exalted, now I am lifted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Isaiah 59:4; James 1:15"> You conceive chaff, you bear stubble; Your spirit—a fire &#91;that&#93; devours you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 27:4; 2 Samuel 23:6–7; Isaiah 10:17"> And peoples have been &#91;as&#93; burnings of lime, &#124;&#124; Thorns, as sweepings, they burn with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 48:10; Ephesians 2:11–18; Daniel 3:27–3"> Hear, you far off, that which I have done, &#124;&#124; And know, you near ones, My might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Matthew 25:46; Isaiah 66:24"> Sinners have been afraid in Zion, &#124;&#124; Trembling has seized the profane: Who dwells for us—consuming fire, &#124;&#124; Who dwells for us—burnings of the age?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:37; 1 John 3:7; Ephesians 5:11–13"> Whoever is walking righteously, &#124;&#124; And is speaking uprightly, &#124;&#124; Kicking against gain of oppressions, &#124;&#124; Shaking his hands from taking hold on a bribe, &#124;&#124; Stopping his ear from hearing of blood, &#124;&#124; And shutting his eyes from looking on evil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 12:29–31; Isaiah 32:18; Proverbs 1:33"> He inhabits high places, &#124;&#124; Strongholds of rock &#91;are&#93; his high tower, &#124;&#124; His bread has been given, his waters steadfast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 9:17; John 14:21; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> Your eyes see a king in his beauty, &#124;&#124; They see a far-off land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20; Psalms 31:7–8; Isaiah 38:9–22"> Your heart meditates &#91;on&#93; terror, &#124;&#124; Where &#91;is&#93; he who is counting? Where &#91;is&#93; he who is weighing? Where &#91;is&#93; he who is counting the towers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:11; Deuteronomy 28:49–50; Jeremiah 5:15"> You do not see the strong people, &#124;&#124; A people deeper of lip than to be understood, &#124;&#124; Of a scorned tongue, there is no understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 46:5; Isaiah 32:18; Isaiah 54:2"> See Zion, the city of our meetings, &#124;&#124; Your eyes see Jerusalem—a quiet habitation, &#124;&#124; A tent not taken down, its pins are not removed forever, &#124;&#124; And none of its cords are broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 46:4–5; Isaiah 48:18; Isaiah 66:12"> But YHWH &#91;is&#93; mighty for us there, &#124;&#124; A place of rivers—streams of broad sides, &#124;&#124; No ship with oars goes into it, &#124;&#124; And a mighty ship does not pass over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 5:31; James 4:12; Zephaniah 3:15–17"> For YHWH, ours who is judging, &#124;&#124; YHWH our lawgiver, &#124;&#124; YHWH our King—He saves us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 7:8; Psalms 68:12; 1 Corinthians 1:27"> Your ropes have been left, &#124;&#124; They do not correctly strengthen their mast, &#124;&#124; They have not spread out a sail, &#124;&#124; Then a prey of much spoil has been apportioned, &#124;&#124; The lame have taken spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:20; 1 John 1:7–9; Revelation 21:4"> Nor does an inhabitant say, “I was sick”; The people dwelling in it &#91;are&#93; forgiven of &#91;their&#93; iniquity!</span>
