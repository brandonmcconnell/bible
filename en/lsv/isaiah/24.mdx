<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nahum 2:10; Isaiah 2:19; Isaiah 42:15"> Behold, YHWH is emptying the earth, &#124;&#124; And is making it desolate, &#124;&#124; And has overturned &#91;it on&#93; its face, &#124;&#124; And has scattered its inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 4:9; Lamentations 5:12–14; Ezekiel 7:12–13"> And it has been—as a people so a priest, &#124;&#124; As the servant so his master, &#124;&#124; As the maidservant so her mistress, &#124;&#124; As the buyer so the seller, &#124;&#124; As the lender so the borrower, &#124;&#124; As the usurer so he who is lifting &#91;it&#93; on himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 24:1; Isaiah 6:11; Deuteronomy 29:23"> The earth is utterly emptied, and utterly spoiled, &#124;&#124; For YHWH has spoken this word:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Isaiah 33:9; Isaiah 3:26"> The earth has mourned, faded, &#124;&#124; The world has languished, faded, &#124;&#124; They have languished—the high place of the people of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Romans 8:20–21; Daniel 7:25"> And the earth has been defiled under its inhabitants, &#124;&#124; Because they have transgressed laws, &#124;&#124; They have changed a statute, &#124;&#124; They have made void a perpetual covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Matthew 7:14; Zechariah 5:3–4"> Therefore a curse has consumed the earth, &#124;&#124; And the inhabitants in it become desolate, &#124;&#124; Therefore inhabitants of the earth have been consumed, &#124;&#124; And few men have been left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 16:10; Joel 1:10–12; Isaiah 16:8"> The new wine has mourned, the vine languished, &#124;&#124; All the joyful of heart have sighed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 2:11; Revelation 18:22; Ezekiel 26:13"> The joy of tambourines has ceased, &#124;&#124; The noise of exulting ones has ceased, &#124;&#124; The joy of a harp has ceased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 5:22; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Isaiah 5:20"> They do not drink wine with a song, &#124;&#124; Strong drink is bitter to those drinking it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 17:5–6; Isaiah 24:12"> It was broken down—a city of emptiness, &#124;&#124; Every house has been shut from &#91;its&#93; entrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 7:14; Isaiah 24:7–9; Jeremiah 48:33"> In out-places &#91;is&#93; a cry over the wine, &#124;&#124; All joy has been darkened, &#124;&#124; The joy of the land has been removed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Micah 1:12; Lamentations 5:18"> Desolation &#91;is&#93; left in the city, &#124;&#124; And the gate is struck &#91;with&#93; ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Ezekiel 6:8–11; Ezekiel 11:16–20"> When thus it is in the heart of the land, &#124;&#124; In the midst of the peoples, &#124;&#124; As the surrounding of the olive, &#124;&#124; As gleanings when harvest has been finished,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Jeremiah 31:12; Isaiah 54:1"> They lift up their voice, &#124;&#124; They sing of the excellence of YHWH, &#124;&#124; They have cried aloud from the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 1:11; Isaiah 42:4; Isaiah 60:9"> Therefore honor YHWH in prosperity, &#124;&#124; In islands of the sea, the Name of YHWH, God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 21:2; Jeremiah 5:11; Isaiah 33:1"> From the skirt of the earth we heard songs, &#124;&#124; The desire of the righteous. And I say, “Leanness &#91;is&#93; to me, &#124;&#124; Leanness &#91;is&#93; to me, woe &#91;is&#93; to me.” Treacherous dealers dealt treacherously, &#124;&#124; Indeed, treachery, treacherous dealers dealt treacherously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:43–44; 1 Kings 19:17; Leviticus 26:21–22"> Fear, and a snare, and a trap, &#124;&#124; &#91;Are&#93; on you, O inhabitant of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Psalms 18:7; Joshua 10:10–11"> And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; He who is fleeing from the noise of the fear falls into the snare, &#124;&#124; And he who is coming up from the midst of the snare, &#124;&#124; Is captured by the trap, &#124;&#124; For windows have been opened on high, &#124;&#124; And foundations of the earth are shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4–10; Deuteronomy 11:6; Revelation 20:11"> The earth has been utterly broken down, &#124;&#124; The earth has been utterly broken, &#124;&#124; The earth has been utterly moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 19:14; Hosea 4:1–5; Daniel 11:19"> The earth staggers greatly as a drunkard, &#124;&#124; And it has been moved as a lodge, &#124;&#124; And its transgression has been heavy on it, &#124;&#124; And it has fallen, and does not add to rise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 76:12; Isaiah 34:2–17; Zechariah 14:12–19"> And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; YHWH lays a charge on the host of the high place in the high place, &#124;&#124; And on the kings of the earth on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11; Isaiah 42:22; Jeremiah 38:6–13"> And they have been gathered—A gathering of bound ones in a pit, &#124;&#124; And they have been shut up in a prison, &#124;&#124; And after a multitude of days are inspected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19; Revelation 21:23; Isaiah 13:10"> And the moon has been confounded, &#124;&#124; And the sun has been ashamed, &#124;&#124; For YHWH of Hosts has reigned &#124;&#124; In Mount Zion, and in Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And before His elderly—glory!</span>
