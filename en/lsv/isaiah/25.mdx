<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:2; Psalms 118:28; Numbers 23:19"> O YHWH, You &#91;are&#93; my God, I exalt You, I confess Your Name, &#124;&#124; For You have done a wonderful thing, &#124;&#124; Counsels of old, steadfastness, O steadfast One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 17:1; Isaiah 13:22; Isaiah 25:12"> For You made of a city a heap, &#124;&#124; Of a fortified city a ruin, &#124;&#124; A high place of strangers from &#91;being&#93; a city, &#124;&#124; It is not built for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; Isaiah 49:23–26; Psalms 46:10–11"> Therefore a strong people honors You, &#124;&#124; A city of the terrible nations fears You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:12; Isaiah 32:2; James 2:5"> For You have been a stronghold for the poor, &#124;&#124; A stronghold for the needy in his distress, &#124;&#124; A refuge from storm, a shadow from heat, &#124;&#124; When the spirit of the terrible &#91;is&#93; as a storm—a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 20:8–9; Isaiah 49:10; Psalms 105:39"> As heat in a dry place, &#124;&#124; You humble the noise of strangers, &#124;&#124; &#91;As&#93; heat with the shadow of a thick cloud, &#124;&#124; The singing of the terrible is humbled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 8:11; Psalms 63:5; Zechariah 9:16–17"> And YHWH of Hosts has made, &#124;&#124; For all the peoples in this mountain, &#124;&#124; A banquet of fat things, &#124;&#124; A banquet of preserved things, &#124;&#124; Fat things full of marrow, &#124;&#124; Preserved things—refined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:18; Isaiah 60:1–3; Matthew 27:51"> And He has swallowed up in this mountain &#124;&#124; The face of the wrapping that is wrapped over all the peoples, &#124;&#124; And of the covering that is spread over all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17; 1 Corinthians 15:54"> He has swallowed up death in victory, &#124;&#124; And Lord YHWH has wiped &#91;the&#93; tear from off all faces, &#124;&#124; And He turns aside the reproach of His people from off all the earth, &#124;&#124; For YHWH has spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Genesis 49:18; Micah 7:7"> And &#91;one&#93; has said in that day, “Behold, this &#91;is&#93; our God, &#124;&#124; We waited for Him, and He saves us, &#124;&#124; This &#91;is&#93; YHWH, we have waited for Him, &#124;&#124; We are glad and rejoice in His salvation!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; Isaiah 18:4; Zephaniah 2:9"> For the hand of YHWH rests on this mountain, &#124;&#124; And Moab is trodden down under Him, &#124;&#124; As straw is trodden down on a dunghill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 16:14; Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 16:6"> And He spread out His hands in its midst, &#124;&#124; As the swimmer spreads out to swim; And He has humbled his excellence &#124;&#124; With the machinations of his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 26:5; Revelation 18:21; Isaiah 14:23"> And the fortress of the high place of your walls &#124;&#124; He has bowed down—He has made low, &#124;&#124; He has caused &#91;it&#93; to come to the earth—to dust.</span>
