<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; 2 Samuel 22:43; Ezra 1:2"> Who stirred up a righteous one from the east? He calls him to His foot, &#124;&#124; He gives nations before him, &#124;&#124; And He causes him to rule kings, &#124;&#124; He gives &#91;them&#93; as dust &#91;to&#93; his sword, &#124;&#124; As driven stubble &#91;to&#93; his bow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 5:24; Isaiah 57:2"> He pursues them, he passes over in safety &#124;&#124; A path he does not enter with his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 48:12; Revelation 22:13; Revelation 1:17"> Who has worked and done, &#124;&#124; Calling the generations from the first? I, YHWH, the first, and with the last I &#91;am&#93; He.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:15–16; Psalms 65:8; Psalms 66:3"> Islands have seen and fear, &#124;&#124; The ends of the earth tremble, &#124;&#124; They have drawn near, indeed, they come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 44:12; Isaiah 40:19; Daniel 3:1–7"> They each help his neighbor, &#124;&#124; And to his brother he says, “Be strong.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 40:19–20; Daniel 3:1–7; Judges 18:17–18"> And an artisan strengthens the refiner, &#124;&#124; A smoother &#91;with&#93; a hammer, &#124;&#124; Him who is beating &#91;on&#93; an anvil, saying, “For joining it &#91;is&#93; good,” &#124;&#124; And he strengthens it with nails, it is not moved!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 2:23; 2 Chronicles 20:7; Deuteronomy 14:2"> And you, O Israel, My servant, &#124;&#124; Jacob, whom I have chosen, &#124;&#124; Seed of Abraham, My lover,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:22; Psalms 94:14; Deuteronomy 7:6–7"> Whom I have taken hold of, from the ends of the earth, &#124;&#124; And from its near places I have called you, &#124;&#124; And I say to you, You &#91;are&#93; My servant, &#124;&#124; I have chosen you, and not rejected you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 43:1–2; Romans 8:31"> Do not be afraid, for I &#91;am&#93; with you, do not look around, for I &#91;am&#93; your God, &#124;&#124; I have strengthened you, &#124;&#124; Indeed, I have helped you, indeed, I upheld you, &#124;&#124; With the right hand of My righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 23:22; Isaiah 45:24; Revelation 3:9"> Behold, all those displeased with you, &#124;&#124; They are ashamed and blush, &#124;&#124; They are as nothing, indeed, &#124;&#124; The men who strive with you perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:35–36; Job 20:7–9; Isaiah 17:14"> You seek them, and do not find them, &#124;&#124; The men who debate with you, &#124;&#124; They are as nothing, indeed, as nothing, &#124;&#124; The men who war with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 73:23; Psalms 63:8"> For I, your God YHWH, &#124;&#124; Am strengthening your right hand, &#124;&#124; He who is saying to you, “Do not fear, I have helped you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 43:14; Psalms 22:6; Isaiah 54:5"> Do not fear, O worm Jacob, &#124;&#124; You men of Israel, &#124;&#124; I helped you, a declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Even your redeemer, the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 4:13; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Zechariah 4:7"> Behold, I have set you for a new sharp threshing instrument, &#124;&#124; Possessing teeth, you thresh mountains, &#124;&#124; And beat small, and make hills as chaff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:2; Isaiah 61:10–11; Isaiah 45:24–25"> You winnow them, and a wind lifts them up, &#124;&#124; And a whirlwind scatters them, &#124;&#124; And you rejoice in YHWH, &#124;&#124; &#91;And&#93; boast yourself in the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 55:1; Isaiah 30:19; Isaiah 44:3"> The poor and the needy are seeking water, &#124;&#124; And there is none, &#124;&#124; Their tongue has failed with thirst, &#124;&#124; I, YHWH, answer them, &#124;&#124; The God of Israel—I do not forsake them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 107:35; Isaiah 35:6–7; Isaiah 58:11"> I open rivers on high places, &#124;&#124; And fountains in midst of valleys, &#124;&#124; I make a wilderness become a pond of water, &#124;&#124; And a dry land becomes springs of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 55:13; Isaiah 37:31–32; Isaiah 35:1"> I give in a wilderness the cedar, &#124;&#124; Shittah, and myrtle, and oil-tree, &#124;&#124; I set in a desert the fir-pine and box-wood together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 12:9; Isaiah 44:23; Isaiah 43:21"> So that they see, and know, &#124;&#124; And regard, and act wisely together, &#124;&#124; For the hand of YHWH has done this, &#124;&#124; And the Holy One of Israel has created it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 23:3–4; Job 38:3; Micah 6:1–2"> Bring your cause near, says YHWH, &#124;&#124; Bring your mighty ones near, says the King of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 45:21; Isaiah 48:14; Isaiah 44:7"> They bring &#91;them&#93; near, and declare to us that which happens, &#124;&#124; Declare the first things—what they &#91;are&#93;, &#124;&#124; And we set our heart, and know their latter end, &#124;&#124; Or cause us to hear the coming things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:19; Isaiah 42:9; Jeremiah 10:5"> Declare the things that are coming hereafter, &#124;&#124; And we know that you &#91;are&#93; gods, &#124;&#124; Indeed, you may do good or do evil, &#124;&#124; And we look around and see &#91;it&#93; together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 115:8; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 41:29"> Behold, you &#91;are&#93; of nothing, and your work of nothing, &#124;&#124; An abomination—it fixes on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:2; 2 Samuel 22:43; Isaiah 10:6"> I have stirred up &#91;one&#93; from the north, &#124;&#124; And he comes, &#124;&#124; From the rising of the sun he calls in My Name, &#124;&#124; And he comes in &#91;on&#93; prefects as &#91;on&#93; clay, &#124;&#124; And as a potter treads down mire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 44:7; Isaiah 41:22; Isaiah 45:21"> Who has declared from the first, and we know? And formerly, and we say, “Righteous?” Indeed, there is none declaring, &#124;&#124; Indeed, there is none proclaiming, &#124;&#124; Indeed, there is none hearing your sayings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Isaiah 52:7; Isaiah 44:28"> First to Zion, &#124;&#124; Behold, behold them, &#124;&#124; And to Jerusalem I give one proclaiming tidings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 63:5; Daniel 2:10–11; Isaiah 40:13–14"> And I see that there is no man, &#124;&#124; Indeed, of these that there is no counselor, &#124;&#124; And I ask them, and they return word:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:13; Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 10:2–16"> “Behold, all of them &#91;are&#93; vanity, &#124;&#124; Their works &#91;are&#93; nothing, &#124;&#124; Their molten images &#91;are&#93; wind and emptiness!”</span>
