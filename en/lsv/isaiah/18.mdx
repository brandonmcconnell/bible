<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Ezekiel 39:17–20; Ezekiel 32:4–6"> They are left together to the ravenous bird of the mountains, &#124;&#124; And to the beast of the earth, &#124;&#124; And the ravenous bird has summered on them, &#124;&#124; And every beast of the earth winters on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; Acts 8:27–28; Zechariah 14:16–17"> At that time a present is brought to YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; A nation drawn out and peeled.
