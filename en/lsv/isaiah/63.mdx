<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 19:13; Isaiah 34:5–6; Psalms 137:7"> Who &#91;is&#93; this coming from Edom? With dyed garments from Bozrah? This that is honorable in His clothing, &#124;&#124; Traveling in the abundance of His power? “I, speaking in righteousness, mighty to save.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:13; Revelation 19:15"> Why &#91;is&#93; Your clothing red? And Your garments as treading in a winepress?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 19:13–15; Revelation 14:19–20; Malachi 4:3"> “I have trodden a wine vat by Myself, &#124;&#124; And of the peoples there is no one with Me, &#124;&#124; And I tread them in My anger, &#124;&#124; And I trample them in My fury, &#124;&#124; Their strength is sprinkled on My garments, &#124;&#124; And I have defiled all My clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 34:8; Jeremiah 51:6; Isaiah 35:4"> For the day of vengeance &#91;is&#93; in My heart, &#124;&#124; And the year of My redeemed has come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Isaiah 40:10; Psalms 98:1"> And I look attentively, and there is none helping, &#124;&#124; And I am astonished that there is none supporting, &#124;&#124; And My own arm gives salvation to Me. And My wrath—it has supported Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 14:10; Revelation 16:19; Isaiah 49:26"> And I tread down peoples in My anger, &#124;&#124; And I make them drunk in My fury, &#124;&#124; And I bring down their strength to earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4; Psalms 86:5; Psalms 86:15"> I make mention of the kind acts of YHWH, &#124;&#124; The praises of YHWH, &#124;&#124; According to all that YHWH has done for us, &#124;&#124; And the abundance of the goodness to the house of Israel, &#124;&#124; That He has done for them, &#124;&#124; According to His mercies, &#124;&#124; And according to the abundance of His kind acts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 17:7; Isaiah 12:2; Exodus 6:7"> And He says, “Surely they &#91;are&#93; My people, &#124;&#124; Sons—they do not lie,” and He is to them for a Savior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Exodus 23:20–21; Malachi 3:1"> In all their distress &#91;He is&#93; no adversary, &#124;&#124; And the Messenger of His Presence saved them, &#124;&#124; In His love and in His pity He redeemed them, &#124;&#124; And He lifts them up, &#124;&#124; And carries them all the days of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Ephesians 4:30; Psalms 78:56"> And they have been rebellious and have grieved His Holy Spirit, &#124;&#124; And He turns to them for an enemy, &#124;&#124; He Himself has fought against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Exodus 14:22; Psalms 77:20"> And He remembers the days of old, &#124;&#124; Moses—his people. Where &#91;is&#93; He who is bringing them up from the sea, &#124;&#124; The shepherd of His flock? Where &#91;is&#93; He who is putting in its midst His Holy Spirit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:16; Psalms 78:13; Joshua 3:16"> Leading by the right hand of Moses, the arm of His glory, &#124;&#124; Cleaving waters from before them, &#124;&#124; To make to Himself a continuous Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Psalms 106:9; Habakkuk 3:15"> Leading them through the depths, &#124;&#124; They do not stumble as a horse in a plain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 63:12; Hebrews 4:8–11; Joshua 23:1"> As a beast goes down into a valley, &#124;&#124; The Spirit of YHWH causes him to rest, &#124;&#124; So You have led Your people, &#124;&#124; To make to Yourself a glorious Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:20; Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalms 80:14"> Look attentively from the heavens, &#124;&#124; And see from Your holy and beautiful habitation, &#124;&#124; Where &#91;is&#93; Your zeal and Your might? The multitude of Your bowels and Your mercies—Are they restrained?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Malachi 2:10; Jeremiah 3:19"> For You &#91;are&#93; our Father, &#124;&#124; For Abraham has not known us, &#124;&#124; And Israel does not acknowledge us, &#124;&#124; You, O YHWH, &#91;are&#93; our Father, &#124;&#124; Our Redeemer from the Age, &#91;is&#93; Your Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Joshua 11:20; Ezekiel 14:7–9"> Why cause us to wander, O YHWH, from Your ways? You harden our heart from Your fear, &#124;&#124; Turn back for Your servants’ sake, &#124;&#124; The tribes of Your inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 74:3–7; Revelation 11:2; Deuteronomy 7:6"> For a little while Your holy people possessed, &#124;&#124; Our adversaries have trodden down Your sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 135:4; Acts 14:16; Romans 9:4"> We have been from of old, &#124;&#124; You have not ruled over them, &#124;&#124; Your Name is not called on them!</span>
