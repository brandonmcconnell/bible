The land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali, &#124;&#124; So the latter has honored the way of the sea, &#124;&#124; Beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:16; Ephesians 5:8; John 8:12"> The people who are walking in darkness &#124;&#124; Have seen a great light, &#124;&#124; Dwellers in a land of death-shade, &#124;&#124; Light has shone on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 35:10; Psalms 119:162; Isaiah 26:15"> You have multiplied the nation, &#124;&#124; You have made its joy great, &#124;&#124; They have rejoiced before You as the joy in harvest, &#124;&#124; As &#91;men&#93; rejoice in their apportioning spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:8; Isaiah 14:25; Isaiah 10:26–27"> Because the yoke of its burden, &#124;&#124; And the staff of its shoulder, the rod of its exactor, &#124;&#124; You have broken as &#91;in&#93; the day of Midian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:8; Psalms 46:9; Isaiah 30:33"> For every battle of a warrior &#91;is&#93; with rushing, and raiment rolled in blood, &#124;&#124; And it has been for burning—fuel of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 2:11; Matthew 28:18; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> For a Child has been born to us, &#124;&#124; A Son has been given to us, &#124;&#124; And the dominion is on His shoulder, &#124;&#124; And He calls His Name &#124;&#124; Wonderful, Counselor, Mighty God, &#124;&#124; Father of Eternity, Prince of Peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Daniel 2:44; Daniel 7:14"> Of the increase of &#91;His&#93; dominion, &#124;&#124; And of peace, there is no end, &#124;&#124; On the throne of David, and on His kingdom, &#124;&#124; To establish it, and to support it, &#124;&#124; In judgment and in righteousness, &#124;&#124; From now on, even for all time, &#124;&#124; The zeal of YHWH of Hosts does this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:4–8; Zechariah 1:6; Zechariah 5:1–4"> The Lord has sent a word into Jacob, &#124;&#124; And it has fallen in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 46:12; 1 Peter 5:5; Ezekiel 7:27"> And the people have known—all of it, Ephraim, and the inhabitant of Samaria, &#124;&#124; In pride and in greatness of heart, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 1:4; 1 Kings 10:27; 1 Kings 7:9–12"> “Bricks have fallen, and we build hewn work, &#124;&#124; Sycamores have been cut down, and we renew cedars.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 8:4–7; Isaiah 17:1–5; Isaiah 7:8"> And YHWH sets the adversaries of Rezin on high above him, &#124;&#124; And he joins his enemies together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25; 2 Chronicles 28:18; Psalms 79:7"> Aram from before, and Philistia from behind, &#124;&#124; And they devour Israel with the whole mouth. With all this His anger has not turned back, &#124;&#124; And His hand is still stretched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 7:10; Isaiah 31:1; Jeremiah 5:3"> And the people has not turned back to Him who is striking it, &#124;&#124; And they have not sought YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 19:15; Revelation 18:8; Hosea 1:6"> And YHWH cuts off head and tail from Israel, &#124;&#124; Branch and reed—the same day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 29:10; Matthew 24:24; 1 Samuel 9:6"> Elderly and accepted of faces—he &#91;is&#93; the head, &#124;&#124; And prophet teaching falsehood—he &#91;is&#93; the tail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:14; Isaiah 3:12; Matthew 23:16–36"> And the eulogists of this people are causing to err, &#124;&#124; And its eulogized ones are consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; Isaiah 10:6; Isaiah 5:25"> Therefore, the Lord does not rejoice over its young men, &#124;&#124; And He does not pity its orphans and its widows, &#124;&#124; For everyone &#91;is&#93; profane, and an evildoer, &#124;&#124; And every mouth is speaking folly. With all this His anger has not turned back, &#124;&#124; And His hand is still stretched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 83:14; Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 5:24"> For wickedness has burned as a fire, &#124;&#124; It devours brier and thorn, &#124;&#124; And it kindles in thickets of the forest, &#124;&#124; And they lift themselves up, an exaltation of smoke!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 7:2; Micah 7:6; Joel 2:2"> In the wrath of YHWH of Hosts &#124;&#124; The land has been consumed, &#124;&#124; And the people is as fuel of fire; A man has no pity on his brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Jeremiah 19:9; Lamentations 4:10"> And cuts down on the right, and has been hungry, &#124;&#124; And he devours on the left, &#124;&#124; And they have not been satisfied, &#124;&#124; They each devour the flesh of his own arm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25; Galatians 5:15; Isaiah 9:12"> Manasseh—Ephraim, and Ephraim—Manasseh, &#124;&#124; Together they &#91;are&#93; against Judah, &#124;&#124; With all this His anger has not turned back. And His hand is still stretched out!</span>
