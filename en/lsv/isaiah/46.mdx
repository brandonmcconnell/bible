<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Psalms 33:11; Ephesians 1:9–11"> Declaring the latter end from the beginning, &#124;&#124; And from of old that which has not been done, &#124;&#124; Saying, My counsel stands, &#124;&#124; And all My delight I do.</span>
