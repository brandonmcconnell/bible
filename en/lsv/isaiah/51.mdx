<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:7; Psalms 94:15; Hebrews 12:14"> “Listen to Me, you pursuing righteousness, &#124;&#124; Seeking YHWH, &#124;&#124; Look attentively to the rock—you have been hewn, &#124;&#124; And to the hole of the pit—you have been dug.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:24; Galatians 3:9–14; Romans 4:1–5"> Look attentively to your father Abraham, &#124;&#124; And to Sarah—she brings you forth, &#124;&#124; For—one—I have called him, &#124;&#124; And I bless him, and multiply him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Joel 2:3; Isaiah 52:9"> For YHWH has comforted Zion, &#124;&#124; He has comforted all her ruins, &#124;&#124; And He sets her wilderness as Eden, &#124;&#124; And her desert as a garden of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Joy, indeed, gladness is found in her, &#124;&#124; Confession, and the voice of song.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Isaiah 2:3; 1 Corinthians 9:21"> “Attend to Me, O My people, &#124;&#124; And, O My nation, give ear to Me. For a law goes out from Me, &#124;&#124; And My judgment to the light, &#124;&#124; I cause peoples to rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:17–18; Isaiah 46:13; Isaiah 42:4"> My righteousness &#91;is&#93; near, &#124;&#124; My salvation has gone out, &#124;&#124; And My arms judge peoples, &#124;&#124; Islands wait on Me, &#124;&#124; Indeed, on My arm they wait with hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Psalms 102:25–26; Isaiah 34:4"> Lift up your eyes to the heavens, &#124;&#124; And look attentively to the earth beneath, &#124;&#124; For the heavens have vanished as smoke, &#124;&#124; And the earth wears out as a garment, &#124;&#124; And its inhabitants die as gnats, &#124;&#124; And My salvation is for all time, &#124;&#124; And My righteousness is not broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 5:11; Psalms 37:31; Ezekiel 2:6"> Listen to Me, you who know righteousness, &#124;&#124; A people in whose heart &#91;is&#93; My law, &#124;&#124; Do not fear the reproach of men, &#124;&#124; And do not be frightened of their reviling,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Isaiah 50:9; Hosea 5:12"> For a moth eats them as a garment, &#124;&#124; And a worm eats them as wool, &#124;&#124; And My righteousness is for all time, &#124;&#124; And My salvation from generation to generation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 74:13–14; Isaiah 52:1; Psalms 89:10"> Awake, awake, put on strength, O arm of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Awake, as &#91;in&#93; days of old, generations of the ages, &#124;&#124; Are You not it that is hewing down Rahab, &#124;&#124; Piercing a dragon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 43:16; Isaiah 63:11–12; Isaiah 50:2"> Are You not it that is drying up a sea, &#124;&#124; Waters of a great deep? That has made deep places of a sea &#124;&#124; A way for the passing of the redeemed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17"> And the ransomed of YHWH return, &#124;&#124; And they have come to Zion with singing, &#124;&#124; And continuous joy &#91;is&#93; on their head, &#124;&#124; They attain gladness and joy, &#124;&#124; Sorrow and sighing have fled away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 118:6; 1 Peter 1:24; Isaiah 2:22"> “I &#91;am&#93; He who comforts you, &#124;&#124; Who &#91;are&#93; you—and you are afraid of man? He dies! And of the son of man—he is made &#91;like&#93; grass!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 45:12; Isaiah 40:22; Psalms 104:2"> And you forget YHWH your Maker, &#124;&#124; Who is stretching out the heavens, and founding earth, &#124;&#124; And you continually fear all the day, &#124;&#124; Because of the fury of the oppressor, &#124;&#124; As he has prepared to destroy. And where &#91;is&#93; the fury of the oppressor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Zechariah 9:11; Isaiah 52:2"> A wanderer has hurried to be loosed, &#124;&#124; And he does not die in the pit, &#124;&#124; And his bread is not lacking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35; Isaiah 54:5; Amos 9:5–6"> And I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH, &#124;&#124; Quieting the sea when its billows roar, &#124;&#124; YHWH of Hosts &#91;is&#93; His Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:21; Isaiah 49:2; John 17:8"> And I put My words in your mouth, &#124;&#124; And have covered you with the shadow of My hand, &#124;&#124; To plant the heavens, and to found earth, &#124;&#124; And to say to Zion, You &#91;are&#93; My people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:1; Revelation 14:10; Psalms 60:3"> Stir yourself, stir yourself, rise, Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; You who have drunk from the hand of YHWH &#124;&#124; The cup of His fury, &#124;&#124; The goblet, the cup of trembling, you have drunk, &#124;&#124; You have wrung out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 49:21; Psalms 88:18; Psalms 142:4"> There is not a leader to her &#124;&#124; Out of all the sons she has borne, &#124;&#124; And there is none laying hold on her hand &#124;&#124; Out of all the sons she has nourished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Amos 7:2; Isaiah 47:9; Ezekiel 14:21"> These two are meeting you, &#124;&#124; Who is moved for you? Spoiling and destruction, famine and sword! By whom do I comfort you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11–12; Revelation 16:9–11; Ezekiel 39:19"> Your sons have been wrapped up, they have lain down, &#124;&#124; At the head of all out places, as an antelope &#91;in&#93; a dragnet, &#124;&#124; They are full of the fury of YHWH, &#124;&#124; The rebuke of Your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 29:9; Isaiah 54:11; Isaiah 51:17"> Therefore, please hear this, &#124;&#124; O afflicted and drunken one, and not with wine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 22:23; Isaiah 51:17; Jeremiah 50:34"> Thus said your Lord YHWH, and your God, &#124;&#124; He pleads &#91;for&#93; His people: “Behold, I have taken the cup of trembling out of your hand, &#124;&#124; The goblet, the cup of My fury, &#124;&#124; You do not add to drink it anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:24; Zechariah 12:2; Isaiah 49:25–26"> And I have put it into the hand of those afflicting you, &#124;&#124; Who have said to your soul, &#124;&#124; Bow down, and we pass over, &#124;&#124; And you make your body as the earth, &#124;&#124; And as the street to those passing by!”</span>
