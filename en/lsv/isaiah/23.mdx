<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 10:8; Jeremiah 50:44–45; Deuteronomy 29:24–28"> Who has counseled this against Tyre, &#124;&#124; The crowning one, whose traders &#91;are&#93; princes, &#124;&#124; Her merchants—the honored of earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 4:6; Isaiah 13:11; Job 40:11–12"> YHWH of Hosts has counseled it, &#124;&#124; To defile the excellence of all beauty, &#124;&#124; To make light all the honored of earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 12:21; Isaiah 23:14; Psalms 18:32"> Pass through your land as a brook, &#124;&#124; Daughter of Tarshish, &#124;&#124; There is no longer a girdle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 9:3–4; Exodus 14:21; Jeremiah 47:7"> He has stretched out His hand over the sea, &#124;&#124; He has caused kingdoms to tremble, &#124;&#124; YHWH has charged concerning the merchant one, &#124;&#124; To destroy her strong places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 18:22; Ezekiel 26:13–14; Isaiah 23:1–2"> And He says, “You do not add to exult anymore, &#124;&#124; O oppressed one, virgin daughter of Sidon, &#124;&#124; To Chittim arise, pass over, &#124;&#124; Even there—there is no rest for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:6; Isaiah 10:5; 2 Kings 17:24"> Behold, the land of the Chaldeans—this people was not, &#124;&#124; Asshur founded it for desert-dwellers, &#124;&#124; They raised its watchtowers, &#124;&#124; They lifted up her palaces—He has appointed her for a ruin!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 23:1; Isaiah 23:6; Ezekiel 27:25–30"> Howl, you ships of Tarshish, &#124;&#124; For your strength has been destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:22; Jeremiah 25:9–11; Daniel 7:14"> And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; That Tyre is forgotten seventy years, &#124;&#124; According to the days of one king. At the end of seventy years there is to Tyre as the song of the harlot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:14; Proverbs 7:10–12"> Take a harp, go around the city, O forgotten harlot, play well, &#124;&#124; Multiply song that you may be remembered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nahum 3:4; Ezekiel 27:6–36; Revelation 19:2"> And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; At the end of seventy years YHWH inspects Tyre, &#124;&#124; And she has returned to her wage, &#124;&#124; And she committed fornication &#124;&#124; With all kingdoms of the earth on the face of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 72:10; Deuteronomy 26:12–14; Philippians 4:17–18"> And her merchandise and her wage have been holy to YHWH, &#124;&#124; Not treasured up nor stored, &#124;&#124; For her merchandise is to those sitting before YHWH, &#124;&#124; To eat to satiety, and for a lasting covering!</span>
