<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 47:1–15; Jeremiah 50:1–64; Isaiah 1:1"> The burden of Babylon that Isaiah son of Amoz has seen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Isaiah 5:26; Jeremiah 50:2"> “Lift up an ensign on a high mountain, &#124;&#124; Raise the voice to them, wave the hand, &#124;&#124; And they go into the openings of nobles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 3:11; Jeremiah 51:20–24; Revelation 17:12–18"> I have given charge to My sanctified ones, &#124;&#124; Also I have called My mighty ones for My anger, &#124;&#124; Those rejoicing at My excellence.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joel 3:14; Revelation 19:11–21; Joel 2:25"> A voice of a multitude in the mountains, &#124;&#124; A likeness of a numerous people, &#124;&#124; A voice of noise from the kingdoms of nations who are gathered, &#124;&#124; YHWH of Hosts inspecting a host of battle!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:20–46; Matthew 24:31; Jeremiah 50:9"> They are coming in from a far-off land, &#124;&#124; From the end of the heavens, &#124;&#124; YHWH and the instruments of His indignation, &#124;&#124; To destroy all the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 1:15; Isaiah 13:9; Amos 5:18"> Howl, for the Day of YHWH &#91;is&#93; near, &#124;&#124; It comes as destruction from the Mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Nahum 2:10; Nahum 1:6"> Therefore, all hands fail, &#124;&#124; And every heart of man melts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 26:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Nahum 2:10"> And they have been troubled, &#124;&#124; Pains and pangs take them, &#124;&#124; They are pained as a travailing woman, &#124;&#124; A man marvels at his friend, &#124;&#124; The appearance of flames—their faces!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 13:15–18"> Behold, the Day of YHWH comes, &#124;&#124; Fierce, with wrath, and heat of anger, &#124;&#124; To make the land become a desolation, &#124;&#124; Indeed, He destroys its sinning ones from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:31; Matthew 24:29; Mark 13:24"> For the stars of the heavens, and their constellations, &#124;&#124; Do not cause their light to shine, &#124;&#124; The sun has been darkened in its going out, &#124;&#124; And the moon does not cause its light to come forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 5:15; Daniel 5:22–23"> “And I have appointed evil on the world, &#124;&#124; And on the wicked their iniquity, &#124;&#124; And have caused the excellence of the proud to cease, &#124;&#124; And I make the excellence of the terrible low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 4:1; Isaiah 24:6; Job 28:16"> I make man more rare than fine gold, &#124;&#124; And a common man than pure gold of Ophir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 12:26–27; Joel 3:16; Matthew 24:29"> Therefore I cause the heavens to tremble, &#124;&#124; And the earth shakes from its place, &#124;&#124; In the wrath of YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; And in a day of the heat of His anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 22:17; Jeremiah 50:16; 1 Kings 22:36"> And it has been, as a roe driven away, &#124;&#124; And as a flock that has no gatherer, &#124;&#124; Each to his people—they turn, &#124;&#124; And each to his land—they flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 14:19–22; Jeremiah 50:27; Isaiah 47:9–14"> Everyone who is found is thrust through, &#124;&#124; And everyone who is added falls by sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nahum 3:10; Hosea 10:14; Psalms 137:8–9"> And their sucklings are dashed to pieces before their eyes, &#124;&#124; Their houses are spoiled, and their wives lain with.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 5:28–31; Jeremiah 51:11; Isaiah 41:25"> Behold, I am stirring up the Medes against them, &#124;&#124; Who do not esteem silver, &#124;&#124; And gold—they do not delight in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 2 Chronicles 36:17; Isaiah 13:16"> And bows dash young men to pieces, &#124;&#124; And they do not pity the fruit of the womb, &#124;&#124; Their eye has no pity on sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:30; Zephaniah 2:9; Jeremiah 51:41"> And Babylon, the beauty of kingdoms, &#124;&#124; The glory, the excellence of the Chaldeans, &#124;&#124; Has been as overthrown by God, &#124;&#124; With Sodom and with Gomorrah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:43; Isaiah 14:23; Jeremiah 50:3"> She does not sit forever, &#124;&#124; Nor continue to many generations, &#124;&#124; Nor does Arab pitch tent there, &#124;&#124; And shepherds do not lie down there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Isaiah 34:11–15"> And desert-dwellers have lain down there, &#124;&#124; And their houses have been full of howlers, &#124;&#124; And daughters of an ostrich have dwelt there, &#124;&#124; And goats skip there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Isaiah 25:2; Isaiah 35:7"> And howlers—he has responded in his forsaken habitations, &#124;&#124; And dragons in palaces of delight, &#124;&#124; And her time &#91;is&#93; near to come, &#124;&#124; And her days are not drawn out!”</span>
