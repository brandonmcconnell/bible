<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Luke 20:9–18; Isaiah 5:1–7"> In that day respond to her, “A desirable vineyard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; John 15:1–2; 1 Samuel 2:9"> I, YHWH, am its keeper, &#124;&#124; I water it every moment, &#124;&#124; Lest any lay a charge against it, &#124;&#124; Night and day I keep it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:17; Hebrews 6:8; Matthew 3:12"> Fury is not in Me; Who gives Me a brier—a thorn in battle? I step into it, &#124;&#124; I burn it at once.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; Job 22:21; Ephesians 2:16–17"> Or—he takes hold on My strength, &#124;&#124; &#91;That&#93; he makes peace with Me, &#124;&#124; &#91;And&#93; he makes peace with Me.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 37:36–38; Jeremiah 51:24; Isaiah 10:20–25"> Has He struck him as the striking of his striker? Does He slay as the slaying of his slain?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:24; Jeremiah 4:11; Ezekiel 19:12"> In measure, in sending it forth, you strive with it, &#124;&#124; He has taken away by His sharp wind, &#124;&#124; In the day of an east wind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:27; Isaiah 48:10; Isaiah 17:8"> Therefore the iniquity of Jacob is covered by this, &#124;&#124; And this &#91;is&#93; all the fruit—To take away his sin, &#124;&#124; In his setting all the stones of an altar, &#124;&#124; As chalkstones beaten in pieces, &#124;&#124; They do not rise—Asherim and images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:2; Jeremiah 26:18; Isaiah 32:13–14"> For the fortified city &#91;is&#93; alone, &#124;&#124; A habitation cast out and forsaken as a wilderness, &#124;&#124; There the calf delights, &#124;&#124; And there it lies down, &#124;&#124; And has consumed its branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Isaiah 1:3; Isaiah 43:1"> In the withering of its branch it is broken off, &#124;&#124; Women are coming in &#91;and&#93; setting it on fire, &#124;&#124; For it &#91;is&#93; not a people of understanding, &#124;&#124; Therefore its Maker does not pity it, &#124;&#124; And its Former does not favor it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 30:3–4; Isaiah 56:8"> And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; YHWH beats out from the branch of the river, &#124;&#124; To the stream of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And you are gathered one by one, O sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Leviticus 25:9; Hebrews 12:22"> And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; It is blown with a great horn, &#124;&#124; And those perishing in the land of Asshur have come in, &#124;&#124; And those cast out in the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And have bowed themselves to YHWH, &#124;&#124; In the holy mountain—in Jerusalem!</span>
