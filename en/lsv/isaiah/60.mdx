<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ephesians 5:14; Ephesians 5:8; John 8:12"> Arise, shine, for your light has come, &#124;&#124; And the glory of YHWH has risen on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 1 Peter 2:9; Acts 17:30–31"> For behold, the darkness covers the earth, &#124;&#124; And thick darkness the peoples, &#124;&#124; And YHWH rises on you, &#124;&#124; And His glory is seen on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 13:47; Isaiah 49:23; Revelation 21:24"> And nations have come to your light, &#124;&#124; And kings to the brightness of your rising.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 42:6; Isaiah 49:20–22; Isaiah 49:18"> “Lift up your eyes around and see, &#124;&#124; All of them have been gathered, they have come to you, &#124;&#124; Your sons come from afar, &#124;&#124; And your daughters are supported on the side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 61:6; Isaiah 23:18; Isaiah 60:11"> Then you see, and have become bright, &#124;&#124; And your heart has been afraid and enlarged, &#124;&#124; For the multitude of the sea turn to you, &#124;&#124; The forces of nations come to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 2:11; Psalms 72:10; Psalms 72:15"> A multitude of camels covers you, &#124;&#124; Dromedaries of Midian and Ephah, &#124;&#124; All of them from Sheba come, &#124;&#124; They carry gold and frankincense, &#124;&#124; And they proclaim the praises of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Genesis 25:13; Hebrews 13:15–16"> All the flock of Kedar are gathered to you, &#124;&#124; The rams of Nebaioth serve you, &#124;&#124; They ascend My altar for acceptance, &#124;&#124; And I beautify the house of My beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Luke 13:29; Isaiah 60:4"> Who &#91;are&#93; these—they fly as a thick cloud, &#124;&#124; And as doves to their windows?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 55:5; Isaiah 2:16; Isaiah 51:5"> Surely islands wait for Me, &#124;&#124; And ships of Tarshish first, &#124;&#124; To bring your sons from afar, &#124;&#124; Their silver and their gold with them, &#124;&#124; To the Name of your God YHWH, &#124;&#124; And to the Holy One of Israel, &#124;&#124; Because He has beautified you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 61:5; Isaiah 49:23; Revelation 21:26"> And sons of a stranger have built your walls, &#124;&#124; And their kings serve you, &#124;&#124; For in My wrath I have struck you, &#124;&#124; And in My good pleasure I have pitied you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 21:25–26; Isaiah 60:5; Isaiah 60:18"> And your gates have continually opened, &#124;&#124; They are not shut by day and by night, &#124;&#124; To bring the force of nations to you, &#124;&#124; Even their kings are led.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 14:12–19; Isaiah 54:15; Psalms 2:12"> For the nation and the kingdom that does not serve you perishes, &#124;&#124; Indeed, the nations are utterly desolated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2; Isaiah 35:2; Psalms 132:7"> The glory of Lebanon comes to you, &#124;&#124; Fir, pine, and box together, &#124;&#124; To beautify the place of My sanctuary, &#124;&#124; And I make the place of My feet honorable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Isaiah 49:23; Hebrews 12:22"> And the sons of those afflicting you &#124;&#124; Have come to you, bowing down, &#124;&#124; And all despising you &#124;&#124; Have bowed themselves to the soles of your feet, &#124;&#124; And they have cried to you: The City of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Zion of the Holy One of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 61:7; Jeremiah 30:17; Isaiah 49:14–23"> Instead of your being forsaken and hated, &#124;&#124; And none passing through, &#124;&#124; I have made you for a continuous excellence, &#124;&#124; A joy of generation and generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Isaiah 66:11–12; Isaiah 66:14"> And you have sucked the milk of nations, &#124;&#124; Indeed, you suckle the breast of kings, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I, YHWH, &#124;&#124; Your Savior, and Your Redeemer, &#124;&#124; &#91;Am&#93; the Mighty One of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 11:40; Isaiah 30:26; Isaiah 1:26"> Instead of the bronze I bring in gold, &#124;&#124; And instead of the iron I bring in silver, &#124;&#124; And instead of the wood bronze, &#124;&#124; And instead of the stone iron, &#124;&#124; And I have made your inspection peace, &#124;&#124; And your exactors righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 26:1; Isaiah 11:9; Zechariah 9:8"> Violence is not heard in your land anymore, &#124;&#124; Spoiling and destruction in your borders, &#124;&#124; And you have called your walls Salvation, &#124;&#124; And your gates Praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 21:23; Revelation 22:5; Zechariah 2:5"> The sun is no longer your light by day, &#124;&#124; And for brightness the moon does not give light to you, &#124;&#124; And YHWH has become to you &#124;&#124; A continuous light, and your God your beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Psalms 84:11; Isaiah 35:10"> Your sun goes in no more, &#124;&#124; And your moon is not removed, &#124;&#124; For YHWH becomes a continuous light to you. And the days of your mourning have been completed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Psalms 37:22; Psalms 37:11"> And all your people &#91;are&#93; righteous, &#124;&#124; They possess the earth for all time, &#124;&#124; A branch of My planting, &#124;&#124; A work of My hands, to be beautified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; Hebrews 10:36; Luke 18:7"> The little one becomes a chief, &#124;&#124; And the small one a mighty nation, &#124;&#124; I, YHWH, hurry it in its own time!”</span>
