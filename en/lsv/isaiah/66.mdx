<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:48–50; Acts 17:24; Matthew 5:34–35"> Thus said YHWH: “The heavens &#91;are&#93; My throne, &#124;&#124; And the earth My footstool, &#124;&#124; Where &#91;is&#93; this—the house that you build for Me? And where &#91;is&#93; this—the place—My rest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 57:15; Psalms 34:18; Psalms 51:17"> And My hand has made all these, &#124;&#124; And all these things are,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH! “And to this one I look attentively, &#124;&#124; To the humble and bruised in spirit, &#124;&#124; And who is trembling at My word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; Amos 5:21–22; Isaiah 1:11–15"> Whoever slaughters the ox strikes a man, &#124;&#124; Whoever sacrifices the lamb beheads a dog, &#124;&#124; Whoever is bringing up a present—The blood of a sow, &#124;&#124; Whoever is making mention of frankincense, &#124;&#124; Is blessing iniquity. Indeed, they have fixed on their own ways, &#124;&#124; And their soul has delighted in their abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; Jeremiah 7:13; Proverbs 10:24"> I also—I fix on their distress, &#124;&#124; And I bring in their fears to them, &#124;&#124; Because I have called, and there is none answering, &#124;&#124; I spoke, and they have not listened, &#124;&#124; And they do evil in My eyes, &#124;&#124; And on that which I did not desire—fixed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:18–20; Isaiah 66:2; Matthew 5:10–12"> Hear a word of YHWH, &#124;&#124; You who are trembling at His word: “Your brothers who are hating you, &#124;&#124; Who are driving you out for My Name’s sake, have said, &#124;&#124; YHWH is honored, and we look on your joy, &#124;&#124; But they are ashamed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 3:7–16; Amos 1:2; Isaiah 34:8"> A voice of noise from the city, &#124;&#124; A voice from the temple, &#124;&#124; The voice of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Giving repayment to His enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 12:1–5; Isaiah 54:1; Galatians 4:26"> “Before she is pained she has brought forth, &#124;&#124; Before a pang comes to her, &#124;&#124; She has delivered a male.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Isaiah 49:20–22; Romans 15:18–21"> Who has heard anything like this? Who has seen anything like these? Is earth caused to bring forth in one day? Is a nation born at once? For she has been pained, &#124;&#124; Zion has also borne her sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 37:3; Genesis 18:14"> Do I bring to the birth, &#124;&#124; And not cause to bring forth?” says YHWH, &#124;&#124; “Am I not He who is causing to beget? I have also restrained,” said your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; Deuteronomy 32:43; Psalms 137:6"> “Rejoice with Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And be glad in her, all you loving her, &#124;&#124; Rejoice with her for joy, &#124;&#124; All you who are mourning for her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 60:16; Joel 3:18; Isaiah 60:5"> So that you suckle, and have been satisfied, &#124;&#124; From the breast of her consolations, &#124;&#124; So that you wring out, and have delighted yourselves &#124;&#124; From the abundance of her glory.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 48:18; Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 45:14"> For thus said YHWH: “Behold, I am stretching out to her peace as a river, &#124;&#124; And the glory of nations as an overflowing stream, &#124;&#124; And you have sucked, &#124;&#124; You are carried on the side, &#124;&#124; And you are dandled on the knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; Isaiah 51:3; Isaiah 40:1"> As one whom his mother comforts, so do I comfort you, &#124;&#124; Indeed, you are comforted in Jerusalem.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 10:7; Ezra 8:31; Ezra 7:9"> And you have seen, and your heart has rejoiced, &#124;&#124; And your bones flourish as tender grass, &#124;&#124; And the hand of YHWH has been known to His servants, &#124;&#124; And He has been indignant with His enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 30:33; 2 Thessalonians 1:6–9; 2 Peter 3:10–12"> For behold, YHWH comes in fire, &#124;&#124; And His chariots as a windstorm, &#124;&#124; To refresh His anger in fury, &#124;&#124; And His rebuke in flames of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:21–22; Isaiah 30:30; Ezekiel 39:2–10"> For by fire and by His sword, &#124;&#124; YHWH does judgment with all flesh. And many have been YHWH’s pierced ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 65:3–4; Deuteronomy 14:3–8; Isaiah 1:28–29"> “Those sanctifying and cleansing themselves &#91;to go&#93; to the gardens, &#124;&#124; One after another in the midst, &#124;&#124; Eating flesh of the sow, &#124;&#124; And of the abomination, and of the muroid, &#124;&#124; Are consumed together,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Revelation 11:15; 2 Corinthians 4:4–6"> “And I—their works and their thoughts, &#124;&#124; I come to gather all the nations and tongues, &#124;&#124; And they have come and seen My glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 52:15; Romans 15:21; Isaiah 55:5"> And I have set a sign among them, &#124;&#124; And have sent out of them those escaping to the nations &#124;&#124; (Tarshish, Pul, and Lud, drawing bow, Tubal and Javan, the islands that are far off), &#124;&#124; Who have not heard My fame, nor seen My glory, &#124;&#124; And they have declared My glory among nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 60:3–14; Isaiah 65:11; Isaiah 11:9"> And they have brought all your brothers out of all the nations, &#124;&#124; A present to YHWH, &#124;&#124; On horses, and on chariot, and on litters, &#124;&#124; And on mules, and on dromedaries, &#124;&#124; To My holy mountain Jerusalem,” said YHWH, “As the sons of Israel bring the present in a clean vessel, &#124;&#124; Into the house of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Exodus 19:6; Revelation 1:6"> And I also take of them for priests, &#124;&#124; For Levites,” said YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; 2 Peter 3:13; Revelation 21:1"> “For as the new heavens and the new earth that I am making, &#124;&#124; Are standing before Me,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “So your seed and your name remain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:1; Psalms 86:9; Colossians 2:16–17"> And it has been from month to month, &#124;&#124; And from Sabbath to Sabbath, &#124;&#124; All flesh come to bow themselves before Me,” said YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 14:10–11; Isaiah 14:11; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16"> “And they have gone forth, &#124;&#124; And looked on the carcasses of the men &#124;&#124; Who are transgressing against Me, &#124;&#124; For their worm does not die, &#124;&#124; And their fire is not quenched, &#124;&#124; And they have been an abhorrence to all flesh!”</span>
