<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 34:10; Psalms 65:5–6; Psalms 66:3"> In Your doing fearful things &#91;that&#93; we do not expect, &#124;&#124; You came down, &#124;&#124; Mountains flowed from Your presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9–10; Psalms 31:19; James 5:7"> Even from antiquity &#91;men&#93; have not heard, &#124;&#124; They have not given ear, &#124;&#124; Eye has not seen a God except You, &#124;&#124; He works for those waiting for Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:13–15; Hebrews 4:16; Acts 10:35"> You have met with the rejoicer &#124;&#124; And the doer of righteousness, &#124;&#124; In Your ways they remember You, &#124;&#124; Behold, You have been angry when we sin, &#124;&#124; By them &#91;is&#93; continuance, and we are saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 7:18; Romans 7:24; Ephesians 2:1–2"> And we are as unclean—all of us, &#124;&#124; And all our righteous acts &#91;are&#93; as garments of menstruation; And we fade as a leaf—all of us. And our iniquities take us away as wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Hosea 7:14; Isaiah 59:16"> And there is none calling on Your Name, &#124;&#124; Stirring himself up to lay hold on You, &#124;&#124; For You have hid Your face from us, &#124;&#124; And You melt us away by our iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Psalms 119:73; Jeremiah 18:2–6"> And now, O YHWH, You &#91;are&#93; our Father, &#124;&#124; We &#91;are&#93; the clay, and You &#91;are&#93; our Framer, &#124;&#124; And the work of Your hand—all of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 7:18–20; Psalms 74:1–2; Psalms 79:13"> Do not be angry, O YHWH, very severely, &#124;&#124; Nor remember iniquity forever, &#124;&#124; Behold, look attentively, we implore You, &#124;&#124; We &#91;are&#93; all Your people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; Luke 21:24; 2 Chronicles 36:19–21"> Your holy cities have been a wilderness, &#124;&#124; Zion has been a wilderness, &#124;&#124; Jerusalem a desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 25:9; Psalms 74:5–7; Lamentations 1:7"> Our holy and our beautiful house, &#124;&#124; Where our fathers praised You, &#124;&#124; Has become burned with fire, &#124;&#124; And all our desirable things have become a ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 42:14; Psalms 74:10–11; Psalms 83:1"> Do You refrain Yourself for these, YHWH? You are silent, and afflict us very severely!</span>
