<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 10:20; Romans 9:24–26; 1 Peter 2:10"> “I have been inquired of by those who did not ask, &#124;&#124; I have been found by those who did not seek Me, &#124;&#124; I have said, Behold Me, behold Me, &#124;&#124; To a nation not calling on My Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:21; Proverbs 1:24; Jeremiah 4:14"> I have spread out My hands all the day &#124;&#124; To an apostate people, &#124;&#124; Who are going in the way &#91;that is&#93; not good, &#124;&#124; After their own thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; Deuteronomy 32:21; Isaiah 66:17"> The people who are continually provoking Me to anger, &#124;&#124; To My face, &#124;&#124; Sacrificing in gardens, and making incense on the bricks:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 66:17; Leviticus 11:7; Isaiah 66:3"> Who are dwelling among tombs, &#124;&#124; And lodge in reserved places, &#124;&#124; Who are eating flesh of the sow, &#124;&#124; And a piece of abominations—their vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:9–12; Luke 7:39; Proverbs 16:5"> Who are saying, Keep to yourself, do not come near to me, &#124;&#124; For I have declared you unholy. These &#91;are&#93; smoke in My anger, &#124;&#124; A fire burning all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 50:3; Jeremiah 16:18; Psalms 79:12"> Behold, it is written before Me: I am not silent, but have repaid; And I have repaid into their bosom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:27–28; Isaiah 57:7; Exodus 20:5"> Your iniquities, and the iniquities of your fathers together,” said YHWH, “Who have made incense on the mountains, &#124;&#124; And have reproached Me on the heights, &#124;&#124; And I have measured their former work into their bosom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 9:27–29; Amos 9:8–9; Romans 11:24–26"> Thus said YHWH: “As the new wine is found in the cluster, &#124;&#124; And one has said, Do not destroy it for a blessing &#91;is&#93; in it, &#124;&#124; So I do for My servants’ sake, &#124;&#124; Not to destroy the whole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 9:11–15; Isaiah 27:6; Romans 11:5–7"> And I have brought out a Seed from Jacob, &#124;&#124; And from Judah a Possessor of My mountain, &#124;&#124; And My chosen ones possess it, &#124;&#124; And My servants dwell there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Isaiah 33:9; Hosea 2:15"> And Sharon has been for the habitation of a flock, &#124;&#124; And the Valley of Achor for the lying down of a herd, &#124;&#124; For My people who have sought Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 2:2; Isaiah 1:28; Deuteronomy 32:17"> And you &#91;are&#93; those forsaking YHWH, &#124;&#124; Who are forgetting My holy mountain, &#124;&#124; Who are setting a table in array for Gad, &#124;&#124; And who are filling a mixture for Meni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:13; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Zechariah 7:11–13"> And I have numbered you for the sword, &#124;&#124; And all of you bow down for slaughter, &#124;&#124; Because I called, and you have not answered, &#124;&#124; I have spoken, and you have not listened, &#124;&#124; And you do evil in My eyes, &#124;&#124; And on that which I did not desire—fixed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Isaiah 66:14; Isaiah 61:7"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: “Behold, My servants eat, and you hunger, &#124;&#124; Behold, My servants drink, and you thirst, &#124;&#124; Behold, My servants rejoice, and you are ashamed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Psalms 66:4; Matthew 22:13"> Behold, My servants sing from joy of heart, &#124;&#124; And you cry from pain of heart, &#124;&#124; And you howl from breaking of spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 62:2; Romans 9:26; Acts 11:26"> And you have left your name &#124;&#124; For an oath for My chosen ones, &#124;&#124; And Lord YHWH has put you to death, &#124;&#124; And He gives another name to His servants;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 19:18; Jeremiah 4:2; Psalms 31:5"> So that he who is blessing himself in the earth, &#124;&#124; Blesses himself in the God of faithfulness, &#124;&#124; And he who is swearing in the earth, &#124;&#124; Swears by the God of faithfulness, &#124;&#124; Because the former distresses have been forgotten, &#124;&#124; And because they have been hid from My eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 3:13; Revelation 21:1–5; Isaiah 66:22"> For behold, I am creating new heavens and a new earth, &#124;&#124; And the former things are not remembered, &#124;&#124; Nor do they ascend on the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 98:1–9; Revelation 19:1–6; Isaiah 66:10–14"> But rejoice, and rejoice forever, that I &#91;am&#93; Creator, &#124;&#124; For behold, I am creating Jerusalem &#91;to be&#93; a rejoicing, &#124;&#124; And her people a joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17; Isaiah 25:8"> And I have rejoiced in Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And have rejoiced in My people, &#124;&#124; And the voice of weeping is not heard in her anymore, and the voice of crying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 5:26; Ecclesiastes 8:12–13; Deuteronomy 4:40"> There is not a suckling of &#91;mere&#93; days there anymore, &#124;&#124; And an aged man who does not complete his days, &#124;&#124; For the youth dies one hundred years old, &#124;&#124; And the sinner, one hundred years old, is lightly esteemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Amos 9:14; Isaiah 62:8–9; Isaiah 32:18"> And they have built houses, and inhabited, &#124;&#124; And planted vineyards, and eaten their fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 92:12–14; Psalms 91:16; Psalms 21:4"> They do not build, and another inhabit, &#124;&#124; They do not plant, and another eat, &#124;&#124; For as the days of a tree &#91;are&#93; the days of My people, &#124;&#124; And My chosen wear out the work of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 61:9; Jeremiah 32:38–39; Deuteronomy 28:3–12"> They do not labor for a vain thing, &#124;&#124; Nor do they bring forth for trouble, &#124;&#124; For they &#91;are&#93; the seed of the blessed of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And their offspring with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 10:12; 1 John 5:14–15; Isaiah 58:9"> And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; They do not yet call, and I answer, &#124;&#124; They are yet speaking, and I hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 11:6–9; Micah 4:3; Acts 9:19–21"> Wolf and lamb feed as one, &#124;&#124; And a lion eats straw as an ox, &#124;&#124; As for the serpent—its food &#91;is&#93; dust, &#124;&#124; They do no evil, nor destroy, &#124;&#124; In all My holy mountain,” said YHWH!</span>
