<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 2:15; Hosea 8:1; Acts 20:26–27"> “Call with the throat, do not restrain, lift up your voice as a horn, &#124;&#124; And declare to My people their transgression, &#124;&#124; And to the house of Jacob their sins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Isaiah 29:13; Matthew 15:7–9"> They seek Me day by day, &#124;&#124; And they desire the knowledge of My ways, &#124;&#124; As a nation that has done righteousness, &#124;&#124; And has not forsaken the judgment of its God, &#124;&#124; They ask of Me judgments of righteousness, &#124;&#124; They desire the drawing near of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 18:9–12; Malachi 3:14; Leviticus 16:29"> Why have we fasted, and You have not seen? We have afflicted our soul, and You do not know. Behold, you find pleasure in the day of your fast, &#124;&#124; And exact all your laborers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 21:9–13; Isaiah 59:2; Proverbs 21:27"> Behold, you fast for strife and debate, &#124;&#124; And to strike with the fist of wickedness, &#124;&#124; You do not fast as &#91;this&#93; day, &#124;&#124; To sound your voice in the high place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 9:3–19; Zechariah 7:5; Isaiah 49:8"> Is this like the fast that I choose? The day of a man’s afflicting his soul? To bow his head as a reed, &#124;&#124; And spread out sackcloth and ashes? Do you call this a fast, &#124;&#124; And a desirable day—to YHWH?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:10–12; Isaiah 58:9; Jeremiah 34:8–11"> Is this not the fast that I chose—To loose the bands of wickedness, &#124;&#124; To shake off the burdens of the yoke, &#124;&#124; And to send out the oppressed free, &#124;&#124; And draw off every yoke?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 25:35–45; Luke 3:11; 1 John 3:17–18"> Is it not to deal your bread to the hungry, &#124;&#124; And bring home the wandering poor, &#124;&#124; That you see the naked and cover him, &#124;&#124; And do not hide yourself from your own flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 52:12; Jeremiah 33:6; Psalms 37:6"> Then your light breaks forth as the dawn, &#124;&#124; And your health springs up in haste, &#124;&#124; Your righteousness has gone before you, &#124;&#124; The glory of YHWH gathers you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12–13; Psalms 50:15; Isaiah 65:24"> Then you call, and YHWH answers, &#124;&#124; You cry, and He says, Behold Me. If you turn aside the yoke from your midst, &#124;&#124; The sending forth of the finger, &#124;&#124; And the speaking of vanity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; Isaiah 58:7–8; Isaiah 42:16"> And bring out your soul to the hungry, &#124;&#124; And satisfy the afflicted soul, &#124;&#124; Then your light has risen in the darkness, &#124;&#124; And your thick darkness &#91;is&#93; as noon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 4:14; Jeremiah 31:12; Psalms 32:8"> And YHWH continually leads you, &#124;&#124; And has satisfied your soul in drought, &#124;&#124; And He arms your bones, &#124;&#124; And you have been as a watered garden, &#124;&#124; And as an outlet of waters, whose waters do not lie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 61:4; Ezekiel 36:33; Amos 9:14"> And they have built the ancient ruins from you, &#124;&#124; You raise up the foundations of many generations, &#124;&#124; And one calls you, Repairer of the breach, &#124;&#124; Restorer of paths to rest in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:21–27; Psalms 92:1–2; Nehemiah 13:15–22"> If you turn your foot from the Sabbath, &#124;&#124; &#91;From&#93; doing your own pleasure on My holy day, &#124;&#124; And have cried to the Sabbath, A delight, &#124;&#124; To the holy of YHWH, Honored, &#124;&#124; And have honored it, without doing your own ways, &#124;&#124; Without finding your own pleasure, &#124;&#124; And speaking a word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Job 22:26; Isaiah 33:16"> Then you delight yourself on YHWH, &#124;&#124; And I have caused you to ride on high places of earth, &#124;&#124; And have caused you to eat the inheritance of your father Jacob, &#124;&#124; For the mouth of YHWH has spoken!”</span>
