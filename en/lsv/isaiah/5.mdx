<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 12:1; Luke 20:9; John 15:1"> Now let me sing for my Beloved, &#124;&#124; A song of my Beloved as to His vineyard: My beloved has a vineyard in a fruitful hill,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Deuteronomy 32:32–33; Mark 11:13"> And He fences it, and casts out its stones, &#124;&#124; And plants it &#91;with&#93; a choice vine, &#124;&#124; And builds a tower in its midst, &#124;&#124; And has also hewn out a winepress in it, &#124;&#124; And He waits for the yielding of grapes, &#124;&#124; And it yields bad ones!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 2:5; Psalms 51:4; Micah 6:2–3"> And now, O inhabitant of Jerusalem and man of Judah, &#124;&#124; Please judge between Me and My vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 23:37; Micah 6:3–4; Jeremiah 2:5"> What &#91;is there&#93; to do still to My vineyard, &#124;&#124; That I have not done in it? For what reason have I waited for the yielding of grapes, &#124;&#124; And it yields &#91;only&#93; bad ones?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 1:15; Luke 21:24; Isaiah 28:18"> And now, pray, let Me cause you to know, &#124;&#124; That which I am doing to My vineyard, &#124;&#124; To turn its hedge aside, &#124;&#124; And it has been for consumption, &#124;&#124; To break down its wall, &#124;&#124; And it has been for a treading-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 7:23–25; Jeremiah 25:11; Isaiah 24:1–3"> And I make it a waste, &#124;&#124; It is not pruned, nor arranged, &#124;&#124; And brier and thorn have gone up, &#124;&#124; And I lay a charge on the thick clouds, &#124;&#124; From raining on it rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 80:8–11; Jeremiah 12:10; James 5:4"> Because the vineyard of YHWH of Hosts &#124;&#124; &#91;Is&#93; the house of Israel, &#124;&#124; And the man of Judah His pleasant plant, &#124;&#124; And He waits for judgment, and behold, oppression, &#124;&#124; For righteousness, and behold, a cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 2:2; Habakkuk 2:9–12; Jeremiah 22:13–17"> Woe &#91;to&#93; those joining house to house, &#124;&#124; They bring field near to field, &#124;&#124; Until there is no place, &#124;&#124; And you have been settled by yourselves &#124;&#124; In the midst of the land!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 23:38; Isaiah 22:14; Amos 5:11"> Do many houses not become a desolation by the weapons of YHWH of Hosts? Great and good without inhabitant!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Haggai 2:16; Haggai 1:6"> For ten acres of vineyard yield one bath, &#124;&#124; And a homer of seed yields an ephah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29–30; Romans 13:13; Proverbs 20:1"> Woe &#91;to&#93; those rising early in the morning, &#124;&#124; They pursue strong drink! Lingering in twilight, wine inflames them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 21:11–14; Psalms 28:5; Amos 6:4–6"> And harp, and stringed instrument, tambourine, and pipe, &#124;&#124; And wine, have been their banquets, &#124;&#124; And they do not behold the work of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they have not seen the work of His hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; Isaiah 1:3; Isaiah 27:11"> Therefore my people removed without knowledge, &#124;&#124; And its honorable ones are famished, &#124;&#124; And its multitude dried up of thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 30:16; Luke 21:34; Habakkuk 2:5"> Therefore Sheol has enlarged herself, &#124;&#124; And has opened her mouth without limit. And its honor has gone down, and its multitude, &#124;&#124; And its noise, and its exulting one—into her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 2:9; Jeremiah 5:4–5"> And the low is bowed down, and the high humbled, &#124;&#124; And the eyes of the haughty become low,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 46:10; Isaiah 12:4; 1 Chronicles 29:11"> And YHWH of Hosts is high in judgment, &#124;&#124; And the Holy God sanctified in righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:6; Zephaniah 2:14; Isaiah 7:25"> And lambs have fed according to their leading, &#124;&#124; And sojourners consume wastelands of the fat ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Ezekiel 13:22; Jeremiah 5:31"> Woe &#91;to&#93; those drawing out iniquity with cords of vanity, &#124;&#124; And as &#91;with&#93; thick ropes of the cart—sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:22; Jeremiah 17:15; Jeremiah 23:36"> Who are saying, “Let Him hurry, &#124;&#124; Let Him hurry His work, that we may see, &#124;&#124; And let the counsel of the Holy One of Israel &#124;&#124; Draw near and come, and we know.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; 2 Timothy 3:1–5; Luke 16:15"> Woe &#91;to&#93; those saying to evil “good,” and to good “evil,” &#124;&#124; Putting darkness for light, and light for darkness, &#124;&#124; Putting bitter for sweet, and sweet for bitter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:18–20; Proverbs 3:7; Romans 12:16"> Woe &#91;to&#93; the wise in their own eyes, &#124;&#124; And—before their own faces—intelligent!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 5:11; Habakkuk 2:15; Isaiah 28:7"> Woe &#91;to&#93; the mighty to drink wine, &#124;&#124; And men of strength to mingle strong drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 10:2; James 5:6; Psalms 94:21"> Declaring righteous the wicked for a bribe, &#124;&#124; And the righteousness of the righteous &#124;&#124; They turn aside from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 30:12; Job 18:16; Joel 2:5"> Therefore, as a tongue of fire devours stubble, &#124;&#124; And flaming hay falls, &#124;&#124; Their root is as muck, &#124;&#124; And their flower goes up as dust. Because they have rejected the Law of YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; And despised the saying of the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 10:4; Isaiah 9:21; Isaiah 9:17"> Therefore the anger of YHWH has burned among His people, &#124;&#124; And He stretches out His hand against it, &#124;&#124; And strikes it, and the mountains tremble, &#124;&#124; And their carcass is as filth in the midst of the out-places. With all this His anger did not turn back, &#124;&#124; And still His hand is stretched out!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Zechariah 10:8; Deuteronomy 28:49; Isaiah 7:18"> And He lifted up an ensign to the far-off nations, &#124;&#124; And hissed to it from the end of the earth, &#124;&#124; And behold, with haste, it comes swiftly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joel 2:7–8; Job 12:18; Job 12:21"> There is none weary, nor stumbling in it, &#124;&#124; It does not slumber, nor sleep, &#124;&#124; Nor has the girdle of its loins been opened, &#124;&#124; Nor the strap of its sandals drawn away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 45:5; Nahum 2:3–4; Ezekiel 21:9–11"> Whose arrows &#91;are&#93; sharp, and all its bows bent, &#124;&#124; Hooves of its horses have been reckoned as flint, &#124;&#124; And its wheels as a windstorm!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 42:22; Zechariah 11:3; Micah 5:8"> Its roaring &#91;is&#93; like a lioness, &#124;&#124; It roars like young lions, &#124;&#124; And it howls, and seizes prey, &#124;&#124; And carries away safely, and there is none delivering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 8:22; Joel 2:10; Jeremiah 4:23–28"> And it howls against it in that day as the howling of a sea, &#124;&#124; And it has looked attentively to the land, &#124;&#124; And behold, darkness—distress, &#124;&#124; And light has been darkened by its abundance!</span>
