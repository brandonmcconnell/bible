<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Luke 2:29"> He enters into peace, they rest on their beds, &#124;&#124; &#91;Each&#93; is going straightforward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 3:10; Matthew 16:4; Hosea 1:2"> “And you, come near here, &#124;&#124; O sons of a sorceress, seed of an adulterer, &#124;&#124; Even you commit whoredom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 2:13; Psalms 35:21; Isaiah 1:4"> Against whom do you sport yourselves? Against whom do you enlarge the mouth &#124;&#124; &#91;And&#93; prolong the tongue? Are you not children of transgression? A false seed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; 2 Kings 16:3–4; Ezekiel 16:20"> Who are inflamed among oaks, under every green tree, &#124;&#124; Slaughtering the children in valleys, &#124;&#124; Under clefts of the rocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:9; Jeremiah 7:18; Habakkuk 2:19"> Your portion &#91;is&#93; among the smooth things of a brook, &#124;&#124; They &#91;are&#93; your lot, &#124;&#124; You have also poured out an oblation to them, &#124;&#124; You have caused a present to ascend, &#124;&#124; Am I comforted in these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:16; Ezekiel 23:41; Jeremiah 2:20"> On a mountain, high and exalted, &#124;&#124; You have set your bed, &#124;&#124; You have also gone up there to make a sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:32; Ezekiel 23:2–20; Ezekiel 16:25–28"> And behind the door, and the post, &#124;&#124; You have set up your memorial, &#124;&#124; For you have removed from Me, and go up, &#124;&#124; You have enlarged your bed, &#124;&#124; And cut &#91;a covenant&#93; with them, &#124;&#124; You have loved their bed, the station you saw,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:16; Colossians 2:18; Ezekiel 23:40"> And go joyfully to the king in ointment, &#124;&#124; And multiply your perfumes, &#124;&#124; And send your ambassadors far off, &#124;&#124; And humble yourself to Sheol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:25; Jeremiah 18:12; Jeremiah 5:3"> You have labored in the greatness of your way, &#124;&#124; You have not said, It is desperate. You have found the life of your hand, &#124;&#124; Therefore you have not been sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:12–13; Proverbs 29:25; Psalms 50:21"> And of whom have you been afraid, and fear, &#124;&#124; That you lie, and have not remembered Me? You have not laid &#91;it&#93; to your heart, &#124;&#124; Am I not silent, even from of old? And you do not fear Me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Micah 3:2–4; Isaiah 1:11–15"> I declare your righteousness, and your works, &#124;&#124; And they do not profit you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 37:9; Psalms 37:3; Isaiah 56:7"> When you cry, let your gatherings deliver you, &#124;&#124; And wind carries all of them away, &#124;&#124; Vanity takes away, &#124;&#124; And whoever is trusting in Me inherits the land, &#124;&#124; And possesses My holy mountain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 62:10; Isaiah 35:8; Isaiah 40:3"> And He has said, “Raise up, raise up, prepare a way, &#124;&#124; Lift a stumbling-block out of the way of My people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 34:18; Isaiah 66:1–2; Psalms 138:6"> For thus said the high and exalted One, &#124;&#124; Inhabiting eternity, and His Name &#91;is&#93; holy: “I dwell in the high and holy place, &#124;&#124; And with the bruised and humble of spirit, &#124;&#124; To revive the spirit of the humble, &#124;&#124; And to revive the heart of bruised ones,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Micah 7:18; Jeremiah 10:24; Psalms 103:9–16"> For I do not strive for all time, nor am I angry forever, &#124;&#124; For the spirit is feeble before Me, &#124;&#124; And the souls I have made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Jeremiah 6:13; Colossians 3:5"> For the iniquity of his dishonest gain, &#124;&#124; I have been angry, and I strike him, &#124;&#124; Hiding—and am angry, &#124;&#124; And he goes on turning back in the way of his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:6; Revelation 7:17; Jeremiah 3:22"> I have seen his ways, and I heal him, indeed, I lead him, &#124;&#124; And repay comforts to him and to his mourning ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Acts 2:39; Luke 2:14"> Producing the fruit of the lips, &#124;&#124; Peace, peace, to the far off, and to the near, &#124;&#124; And I have healed him,” said YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 3:11; Proverbs 4:16–17; Job 18:5–14"> And the wicked &#91;are&#93; as the driven out sea, &#124;&#124; For it is not able to rest, &#124;&#124; And its waters cast out filth and mire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 48:22; Isaiah 3:11; 2 Kings 9:22"> “There is no peace,” said my God, “for the wicked!”</span>
