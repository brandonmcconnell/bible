<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 18:1–21; 2 Chronicles 26:1–33; Isaiah 6:1"> The visions of Isaiah son of Amoz, that he has seen concerning Judah and Jerusalem, in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, Hezekiah, kings of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Deuteronomy 30:19; Micah 1:2"> Hear, O heavens, and give ear, O earth, &#124;&#124; For YHWH has spoken: “I have nourished and brought up sons, &#124;&#124; And they transgressed against Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Jeremiah 4:22; Isaiah 44:18"> An ox has known its owner, &#124;&#124; And a donkey the crib of its master, &#124;&#124; Israel has not known, &#124;&#124; My people have not understood.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Jeremiah 2:17; Jeremiah 2:13"> Oh, sinning nation, a people heavy &#91;with&#93; iniquity, &#124;&#124; A seed of evildoers, sons—corrupters! They have forsaken YHWH, &#124;&#124; They have despised the Holy One of Israel, &#124;&#124; They have gone away backward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 9:8–11; Ezekiel 24:13; Jeremiah 5:3"> Why are you struck anymore? You add apostasy! Every head has become diseased, and every heart &#91;is&#93; sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 33:6; Matthew 9:12"> From the sole of the foot—to the head, &#124;&#124; There is no soundness in it, &#124;&#124; Wound, and bruise, and fresh striking! They have not been closed nor bound, &#124;&#124; Nor have they softened with ointment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Lamentations 5:2; Deuteronomy 28:43; Deuteronomy 28:33"> Your land &#91;is&#93; a desolation, your cities burned with fire, &#124;&#124; Your ground—strangers are consuming it before you, &#124;&#124; And a desolation as overthrown by strangers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; John 12:15; Zechariah 2:10"> And the daughter of Zion has been left, &#124;&#124; As a shelter in a vineyard, &#124;&#124; As a lodge in a place of cucumbers—as a city besieged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 9:29; Isaiah 37:31–32; Genesis 19:24"> Unless YHWH of Hosts had left a remnant to us, &#124;&#124; Shortly—we had been as Sodom, &#124;&#124; We had been like to Gomorrah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 11:8; Ezekiel 16:49; Amos 9:7"> Hear the word of YHWH, you rulers of Sodom, &#124;&#124; Give ear to the Law of our God, you people of Gomorrah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; Amos 5:21; Jeremiah 6:20"> “Why the abundance of your sacrifices to Me?” says YHWH, &#124;&#124; “I have been satiated &#91;with&#93; burnt-offerings of rams, &#124;&#124; And fat of fatlings; And blood of bullocks, and lambs, &#124;&#124; And I have not desired male goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 34:23; Exodus 23:17; Micah 6:8"> When you come to appear before Me, &#124;&#124; Who has required this of your hand, &#124;&#124; To trample My courts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 66:3; Malachi 1:10; Matthew 15:9"> Do not add to bring in a vain present, &#124;&#124; Incense—it &#91;is&#93; an abomination to Me, &#124;&#124; New moon, and Sabbath, calling of convocation! Do not render iniquity—and a restraint!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:21; Isaiah 43:24; Isaiah 7:13"> My soul has hated your new moons and your set seasons, &#124;&#124; They have been on Me for a burden, &#124;&#124; I have been weary of bearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 3:4; Isaiah 59:2–3; Proverbs 1:28"> And in your spreading forth your hands, I hide My eyes from you, &#124;&#124; Also when you increase prayer, I do not hear, &#124;&#124; Your hands have been full of blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:11; Isaiah 55:6–7; 1 Peter 2:1"> Wash yourselves, make yourselves pure, &#124;&#124; Turn aside the evil of your doings, from before My eyes, &#124;&#124; Cease to do evil, learn to do good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Jeremiah 22:3; Psalms 82:3–4"> Seek judgment, make the oppressed blessed, &#124;&#124; Judge the fatherless, strive &#91;for&#93; the widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 44:22; Revelation 7:14; Psalms 51:7"> Now come, and we settle this,” says YHWH, “If your sins are as scarlet, they will be white as snow, &#124;&#124; If they are red as crimson, they will be as wool!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:15–16; Isaiah 3:10; Joel 2:26"> If you are willing, and have listened, &#124;&#124; The good of the land you consume,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; Isaiah 58:14; Numbers 23:19"> And if you refuse, and have rebelled, &#124;&#124; You are consumed &#91;by&#93; the sword,” &#124;&#124; For the mouth of YHWH has spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:1–23; Zechariah 8:3; Zephaniah 3:1–3"> How has a faithful city become a harlot? I have filled it &#91;with&#93; judgment, &#124;&#124; Righteousness lodges in it—now murderers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:28–30; Lamentations 4:1–2; Hosea 4:18"> Your silver has become dross, &#124;&#124; Your drink defiled with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Micah 7:3; Exodus 23:8; Zechariah 7:10"> Your princes &#91;are&#93; apostates, and companions of thieves, &#124;&#124; Everyone loving a bribe, and pursuing rewards, &#124;&#124; They do not judge the fatherless, &#124;&#124; And the plea of the widow does not come to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 35:4; Deuteronomy 28:63"> Therefore—a declaration of the Lord—YHWH of Hosts, the Mighty One of Israel: “Oh, I relieve Myself from My adversaries, &#124;&#124; And avenge Myself against My enemies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Malachi 3:3; Jeremiah 9:7; Isaiah 4:4"> And I turn back My hand on you, &#124;&#124; And I refine your dross as purity, &#124;&#124; And I turn aside all your tin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:7; Zechariah 8:3; Isaiah 62:1–2"> And I give back your judges as at the first, &#124;&#124; And your counselors as in the beginning, &#124;&#124; After this you are called, A city of righteousness—a faithful city.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 62:12; Isaiah 45:21–25; Isaiah 5:16"> Zion is redeemed in judgment, &#124;&#124; And her captivity in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:8–9; Psalms 1:6; Isaiah 30:13"> And the destruction of transgressors and sinners &#91;is&#93; together, &#124;&#124; And those forsaking YHWH are consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 65:3; Isaiah 57:5; Isaiah 66:17"> For &#91;men&#93; are ashamed because of the oaks &#124;&#124; That you have desired, &#124;&#124; And you are confounded because of the gardens &#124;&#124; That you have chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5–6; Ezekiel 17:9–10; Matthew 21:19"> For you are as an oak whose leaf is fading, &#124;&#124; And as a garden that has no water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 66:24; Ezekiel 32:21; Malachi 4:1"> And the strong has been for tow, &#124;&#124; And his work for a spark, &#124;&#124; And both of them have burned together, &#124;&#124; And there is none quenching!</span>
