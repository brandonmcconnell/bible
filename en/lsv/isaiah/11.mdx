<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 15:9–12; Luke 2:32; Isaiah 11:1"> And there has been, in that day, &#124;&#124; A Root of Jesse that is standing for an ensign of peoples, &#124;&#124; Nations seek Him, &#124;&#124; And His rest has been glorious!</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:18; Hosea 1:11; Ezekiel 37:16–24"> And has turned aside the envy of Ephraim, &#124;&#124; And the adversaries of Judah are cut off, &#124;&#124; Ephraim does not envy Judah, &#124;&#124; And Judah does not distress Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 3:19; Isaiah 25:10; Jeremiah 49:28"> And they have flown on the shoulder of the Philistines westward, &#124;&#124; Together they spoil the sons of the east, &#124;&#124; Edom and Moab sending forth their hand, &#124;&#124; And sons of Ammon obeying them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 10:11; Revelation 16:12; Isaiah 50:2"> And YHWH has devoted to destruction &#124;&#124; The tongue of the Sea of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And has waved His hand over the river, &#124;&#124; In the terror of His wind, &#124;&#124; And has struck it at the seven streams, &#124;&#124; And has caused &#91;men&#93; to tread &#91;it&#93; with shoes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 19:23; Isaiah 35:8–10; Isaiah 11:11"> And there has been a highway, &#124;&#124; For the remnant of His people that is left, from Asshur, &#124;&#124; As there was for Israel in the day of his coming up out of the land of Egypt!</span>
