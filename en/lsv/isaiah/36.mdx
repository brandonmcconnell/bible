<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:1; 2 Kings 18:13; 2 Kings 18:17"> And it comes to pass, in the fourteenth year of King Hezekiah, Sennacherib king of Asshur has come up against all the fortified cities of Judah, and seizes them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:9–23; Isaiah 7:3; 2 Kings 18:17–37"> And the king of Asshur sends Rabshakeh from Lachish to Jerusalem, to King Hezekiah, with a heavy force, and he stands by the conduit of the upper pool, in the highway of the fuller’s field,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16–17; Isaiah 22:15–21; 2 Samuel 20:24–25"> and Eliakim son of Hilkiah goes forth to him, who &#91;is&#93; over the house, and Shebna the scribe, and Joah son of Asaph, the remembrancer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 18:19–37; Jude 1:16; Proverbs 16:18"> And Rabshakeh says to them, “Now say to Hezekiah, Thus said the great king, the king of Asshur: What &#91;is&#93; this confidence in which you have confided?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 18:7; Jeremiah 52:3; Proverbs 21:30–31"> I have said, Only a word of the lips! Counsel and might &#91;are&#93; for battle. Now, on whom have you trusted, that you have rebelled against me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:6–7; Jeremiah 37:5–8; 2 Kings 18:21"> Behold, you have trusted on the staff of this broken reed—on Egypt—which a man leans on, and it has gone into his hand, and pierced it—so &#91;is&#93; Pharaoh king of Egypt to all those trusting on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 18:4–5; Psalms 42:10–11; Psalms 42:5"> And do you say to me, We have trusted in our God YHWH? Is it not He whose high places and whose altars Hezekiah has turned aside, and says to Judah and to Jerusalem, Bow yourselves before this altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:40–43; 1 Kings 20:10; Nehemiah 4:2–5"> And now, please negotiate with my lord the king of Asshur, and I give two thousand horses to you, if you are able to put riders on them for yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 10:8; Isaiah 30:16–17; Isaiah 36:6"> And how do you turn back the face of one captain of the least of the servants of my lord, and trust on Egypt for yourself, for chariot and for horsemen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 18:25; Isaiah 10:5–7; 1 Kings 13:18"> And now, without YHWH have I come up against this land to destroy it? YHWH said to me, Go up to this land, and you have destroyed it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezra 4:7; Daniel 2:4; 2 Kings 18:26–27"> And Eliakim says—and Shebna and Joah—to Rabshakeh, “Please speak to your servants &#91;in&#93; Aramaic, for we are understanding; and do not speak to us &#91;in&#93; Jewish, in the ears of the people who &#91;are&#93; on the wall.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 18:27; Leviticus 26:29; Lamentations 4:9–10"> And Rabshakeh says, “To your lord, and to you, has my lord sent me to speak these words? Is it not for the men—those sitting on the wall to eat their own dung and to drink their own water with you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:18; Psalms 73:8–9; 2 Kings 18:28–32"> And Rabshakeh stands and calls with a great voice &#91;in&#93; Jewish, and says, “Hear the words of the great king, the king of Asshur—</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:4; Revelation 13:5–6; Daniel 6:20"> thus said the king: Do not let Hezekiah lift you up, for he is not able to deliver you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 37:23–24; Matthew 27:43; Psalms 71:9–11"> and do not let Hezekiah make you trust to YHWH, saying, YHWH certainly delivers us, this city is not given into the hand of the king of Asshur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 4:25; Zechariah 3:10; Micah 4:4"> Do not listen to Hezekiah, for thus said the king of Asshur: Make a blessing with me, and come out to me, and each of you eat of his vine, and each of his fig tree, and each drink the waters of his own well,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 18:32; Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Job 20:17"> until my coming in, and I have taken you to a land like your own land, a land of grain and wine, a land of bread and vineyards;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 37:17–18; Isaiah 36:15; Isaiah 37:10"> lest Hezekiah persuades you, saying, YHWH delivers us. Have the gods of the nations each delivered his land out of the hand of the king of Asshur?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 10:9–11; Jeremiah 49:23; 2 Samuel 8:9"> Where &#91;are&#93; the gods of Hamath and Arpad? Where &#91;are&#93; the gods of Sepharvaim, that they have delivered Samaria out of my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 50:21; 2 Chronicles 32:19; Isaiah 37:23–29"> Who among all the gods of these lands &#91;are&#93; they who have delivered their land out of my hand, that YHWH delivers Jerusalem out of my hand?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 26:4; Psalms 38:13–15; Matthew 7:6"> And they keep silent, and have not answered him a word, for a command of the king is, saying, “Do not answer him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; Isaiah 33:7; Matthew 26:65"> And Eliakim son of Hilkiah, who &#91;is&#93; over the house, comes in, and Shebna the scribe, and Joah son of Asaph, the remembrancer, to Hezekiah, with torn garments, and they declare to him the words of Rabshakeh.</span>
