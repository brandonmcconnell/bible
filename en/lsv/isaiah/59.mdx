<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Isaiah 50:2; Numbers 11:23"> Behold, the hand of YHWH &#124;&#124; Has not been shortened from saving, &#124;&#124; Nor His ear heavy from hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:25; Proverbs 15:29; Micah 3:4"> But your iniquities have been separating &#124;&#124; Between you and your God, &#124;&#124; And your sins have hidden &#124;&#124; The Presence from you—from hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; Ezekiel 13:8; Jeremiah 2:34"> For your hands have been defiled with blood, &#124;&#124; And your fingers with iniquity, &#124;&#124; Your lips have spoken falsehood, &#124;&#124; Your tongue mutters perverseness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 15:35; James 1:15; Micah 7:2–5"> There is none calling in righteousness, &#124;&#124; And there is none pleading in faithfulness, &#124;&#124; Trusting on emptiness, and speaking falsehood, &#124;&#124; Conceiving perverseness, and bearing iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:7; Isaiah 14:29; Proverbs 23:32"> They have hatched eggs of a viper, &#124;&#124; And weave webs of a spider, &#124;&#124; Whoever is eating their eggs dies, and the crushed hatches a viper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 57:12; Revelation 3:17–18; Jeremiah 6:7"> Their webs do not become a garment, &#124;&#124; Nor do they cover themselves with their works, &#124;&#124; Their works &#91;are&#93; works of iniquity, &#124;&#124; And a deed of violence &#91;is&#93; in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:15–17; Proverbs 6:17; Mark 7:21–22"> Their feet run to evil, &#124;&#124; And they hurry to shed innocent blood, &#124;&#124; Their thoughts &#91;are&#93; thoughts of iniquity, &#124;&#124; Spoiling and destruction &#91;are&#93; in their highways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 57:20–21; Romans 3:17; Luke 1:79"> They have not known a way of peace, &#124;&#124; And there is no judgment in their paths, &#124;&#124; They have made their paths perverse for themselves, &#124;&#124; None treading in it has known peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 5:30; Job 30:26; Lamentations 5:16–17"> Therefore judgment has been far from us, &#124;&#124; And righteousness does not reach us, &#124;&#124; We wait for light, and behold, darkness, &#124;&#124; For brightness—in thick darkness we go,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:29; Job 5:14; Lamentations 3:6"> We feel &#91;for&#93; the wall like the blind, &#124;&#124; Indeed, we feel as without eyes, &#124;&#124; We have stumbled at noon as at twilight, &#124;&#124; In desolate places as the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Ezekiel 7:16; Jeremiah 9:1"> We make a noise as bears—all of us, &#124;&#124; And we coo severely as doves; We wait for judgment, and there is none, &#124;&#124; For salvation—it has been far from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; Hosea 5:5; Jeremiah 14:7"> For our transgressions have been multiplied before You, &#124;&#124; And our sins have testified against us, &#124;&#124; For our transgressions &#91;are&#93; with us, &#124;&#124; And our iniquities—we have known them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 7:21–22; James 3:6; Hebrews 3:12"> Transgressing, and lying against YHWH, &#124;&#124; And removing from after our God, &#124;&#124; Speaking oppression and apostasy, &#124;&#124; Conceiving and uttering from the heart &#124;&#124; Words of falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:4; Ecclesiastes 3:16; Jeremiah 5:31"> And judgment is removed backward, &#124;&#124; And righteousness stands far off, &#124;&#124; For truth has been feeble in the street, &#124;&#124; And straightforwardness is not able to enter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 7:2; Jeremiah 7:28; 1 John 3:11–12"> And the truth is lacking, &#124;&#124; And whoever is turning aside from evil, &#124;&#124; Is making himself a spoil. And YHWH sees, and it is evil in His eyes, &#124;&#124; That there is no judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Psalms 106:23; Isaiah 64:7"> And He sees that there is no man, &#124;&#124; And is astonished that there is no intercessor, &#124;&#124; And His own arm gives salvation to Him, &#124;&#124; And His righteousness—it sustained Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 6:17; Ephesians 6:14; 1 Thessalonians 5:8"> And He puts on righteousness as a breastplate, &#124;&#124; And a helmet of salvation on His head, &#124;&#124; And He puts on garments of vengeance &#91;for&#93; clothing, &#124;&#124; And is covered, as &#91;with&#93; an upper-robe, &#91;with&#93; zeal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Isaiah 63:6; Romans 2:6"> According to deeds—so He repays. Fury to His adversaries, &#91;their&#93; deed to His enemies, &#124;&#124; To the islands He repays &#91;their&#93; deed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Psalms 113:3; Zechariah 4:6"> And from the west they fear the Name of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And from the rising of the sun—His glory, &#124;&#124; When an adversary comes in as a flood, &#124;&#124; The Spirit of YHWH has raised an ensign against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 11:26–27; Titus 2:11–14; Acts 3:19"> “And the Redeemer has come to Zion, &#124;&#124; Even to captives of transgression in Jacob,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Hebrews 10:16; Ezekiel 36:25–27"> “And I—this &#91;is&#93; My covenant with them,” said YHWH, “My Spirit that &#91;is&#93; on you, &#124;&#124; And My words that I have put in your mouth, &#124;&#124; Do not depart from your mouth, &#124;&#124; And from the mouth of your seed, &#124;&#124; And from the mouth of your seed’s seed,” said YHWH, “From now on, even for all time!”</span>
