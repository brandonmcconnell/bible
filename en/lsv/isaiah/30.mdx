<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 65:2; Isaiah 1:2; Hosea 7:13"> Woe &#91;to&#93; apostate sons, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH! To do counsel, and not from Me, &#124;&#124; And to spread out a covering, and not of My Spirit, &#124;&#124; So as to add sin to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Numbers 27:21; Deuteronomy 28:68"> Who are walking to go down to Egypt, &#124;&#124; And have not asked My mouth, &#124;&#124; To be strong in the strength of Pharaoh, &#124;&#124; And to trust in the shadow of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 20:5; Romans 10:11; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> And the strength of Pharaoh &#124;&#124; Has been to you for shame, &#124;&#124; And the trust in the shadow of Egypt confusion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Isaiah 57:9; Jeremiah 43:7"> For his princes were in Zoan, &#124;&#124; And his messengers reach Hanes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:36; Isaiah 30:7; Isaiah 30:16"> He made all ashamed of a people that do not profit, &#124;&#124; Not for help, and not for profit, &#124;&#124; But for shame, and also for reproach!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:15; Isaiah 46:1–2; Jeremiah 11:4"> The burden of the beasts of the south. Into a land of adversity and distress, &#124;&#124; Of young lion and of old lion, &#124;&#124; From where &#91;are&#93; viper and flying, burning serpent, &#124;&#124; They carry their wealth on the shoulder of donkeys, &#124;&#124; And their treasures on the hump of camels, &#124;&#124; To a people not profitable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:22; Isaiah 7:4; Psalms 118:8–9"> Indeed, Egyptians &#91;are&#93; vanity, and help in vain, &#124;&#124; Therefore I have cried concerning this: “Their strength &#91;is&#93; to sit still.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:22; Habakkuk 2:2; Isaiah 8:1"> No, go in, write it on a tablet with them, &#124;&#124; And inscribe it on a scroll, &#124;&#124; And it is for a latter day, for a witness for all time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 30:1; Acts 7:51; Zechariah 1:4–6"> That this &#91;is&#93; a people of rebellion, sons—liars, &#124;&#124; Sons not willing to hear the Law of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:7–10; Romans 16:18; Amos 2:12"> Who have said to seers, “Do not see,” &#124;&#124; And to prophets, “Do not prophesy to us straightforward things, &#124;&#124; Speak to us smooth things, prophesy deceits,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:7; Romans 1:30; John 15:23–24"> Turn aside from the way, &#124;&#124; Decline from the path, &#124;&#124; Cause the Holy One of Israel &#124;&#124; To cease from before us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 31:1–3; Isaiah 5:24; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> Therefore, thus said the Holy One of Israel: “Because of your kicking against this word, &#124;&#124; And you trust in oppression, &#124;&#124; And perverseness, and rely on it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 29:5; Ezekiel 13:10–15; 1 Kings 20:30"> Therefore this iniquity is to you as a breach falling, &#124;&#124; Swelled out in a wall set on high, &#124;&#124; Whose destruction comes suddenly, in an instant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 2:9; Jeremiah 19:10–11; 2 Peter 2:4–5"> And He has broken it &#124;&#124; As the breaking of the potters’ bottle, &#124;&#124; Beaten down—He does not spare, &#124;&#124; Nor is there found, in its beating down, &#124;&#124; A potsherd to take fire from the burning, &#124;&#124; And to draw out waters from a ditch.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 26:3–4; Isaiah 32:17; Jeremiah 3:22–23"> For thus said Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; The Holy One of Israel: “In returning and rest you are saved, &#124;&#124; In keeping quiet and in confidence is your might,” &#124;&#124; And you have not been willing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 31:1; Isaiah 31:3; Deuteronomy 28:25"> And you say, “No, for we flee on a horse,” &#124;&#124; Therefore you flee, &#124;&#124; And, “We ride on the swift!” Therefore your pursuers are swift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:25; Joshua 23:10; Deuteronomy 32:30"> One thousand &#91;flee&#93; because of the rebuke of one, &#124;&#124; Because of the rebuke of five you flee, &#124;&#124; Until you have surely been left as a pole &#124;&#124; On the top of the mountain, &#124;&#124; And as an ensign on the height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; 2 Peter 3:9; Proverbs 16:20"> And therefore YHWH waits to favor you, &#124;&#124; And therefore He is exalted to pity you, &#124;&#124; For YHWH &#91;is&#93; a God of judgment, &#124;&#124; O the blessedness of all waiting for Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 60:20; Psalms 50:15; Isaiah 65:24"> For the people in Zion dwell in Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; Do not weep—weeping, &#124;&#124; Pitying, He pities you at the voice of your cry, &#124;&#124; When He hears He answers you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 22:27; Psalms 80:5; Amos 8:11–12"> And the Lord has given to you bread of adversity, &#124;&#124; And water of oppression. And your teachers remove no longer, &#124;&#124; And your eyes have seen your teachers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Proverbs 3:5–6; Isaiah 58:11"> And your ear hears a word behind you, saying, “This &#91;is&#93; the way, go in it,” &#124;&#124; When you turn to the right, &#124;&#124; And when you turn to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 17:3–4; Isaiah 31:7; Zechariah 13:2"> And you have defiled the covering of Your carved images of silver, &#124;&#124; And the ephod of your molten image of gold, &#124;&#124; You scatter them as a sickening thing, “Go out,” you say to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 8:11–12; Isaiah 32:20; Psalms 65:9–13"> And He has given rain &#91;for&#93; your seed, &#124;&#124; With which you sow the ground, &#124;&#124; And bread, the increase of the ground, &#124;&#124; And it has been fat and plentiful, &#124;&#124; Your livestock enjoy an enlarged pasture in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 45:6; Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Samuel 8:12"> And the oxen and the young donkeys serving the ground, &#124;&#124; Eat fermented provender, &#124;&#124; That one is winnowing with shovel and fan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 43:19–20; Isaiah 35:6–7; Isaiah 2:14–15"> And there has been on every high mountain, &#124;&#124; And on every exalted hill, &#124;&#124; Streams—conduits of waters, &#124;&#124; In a day of much slaughter, in the falling of towers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Deuteronomy 32:39; Revelation 22:5"> And the light of the moon has been as the light of the sun, &#124;&#124; And the light of the sun is sevenfold, &#124;&#124; As the light of seven days, &#124;&#124; In the day of YHWH’s binding up the breach of His people, &#124;&#124; When He heals the stroke of its wound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:8; Nahum 1:5–6; Psalms 18:7–9"> Behold, the Name of YHWH is coming from far, &#124;&#124; His anger is burning, and the flame &#91;is&#93; great, &#124;&#124; His lips have been full of indignation, &#124;&#124; And His tongue &#91;is&#93; as a devouring fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Isaiah 11:4; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> And His breath &#91;is&#93; as an overflowing stream, &#124;&#124; It divides to the neck, &#124;&#124; To sift nations with a sieve of vanity, &#124;&#124; And a bridle causing to err, &#124;&#124; &#91;Is&#93; on the jaws of the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Psalms 95:1–2; Exodus 15:1–21"> Singing is to you as in a night sanctified for a festival, &#124;&#124; And joy of heart as he who is going with a pipe, &#124;&#124; To go to the mountain of YHWH, &#124;&#124; To the rock of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 18:13–14; Joshua 10:11; Isaiah 29:6"> And YHWH has caused &#124;&#124; The splendor of His voice to be heard, &#124;&#124; And the coming down of His arm &#124;&#124; He shows with the raging of anger, &#124;&#124; And the flame of a consuming fire, &#124;&#124; Scattering, and inundation, and hailstone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 11:4; Isaiah 9:4; Micah 5:5–6"> For from the voice of YHWH Asshur &#91;is&#93; broken down, &#124;&#124; He strikes with a rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 11:15; Isaiah 19:16; Isaiah 2:19"> And every passage of the settled staff, &#124;&#124; That YHWH causes to rest on him, &#124;&#124; Has been with tambourines and with harps, &#124;&#124; And in battles of shaking He has fought with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 23:10; Genesis 19:24; Jeremiah 19:6"> For Tophet is arranged from former time, &#124;&#124; Even it is prepared for the king, &#124;&#124; He has made deep, &#124;&#124; He has made large, &#124;&#124; Its pile &#91;is&#93; fire and much wood, &#124;&#124; The breath of YHWH, &#124;&#124; As a stream of brimstone, is burning in it!</span>
