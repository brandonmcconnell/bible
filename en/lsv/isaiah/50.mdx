<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:30; Jeremiah 3:8; Matthew 18:25"> Thus said YHWH: “Where &#91;is&#93; this—the bill of your mother’s divorce, &#124;&#124; Whom I sent away? Or to which of My creditors have I sold you? Behold, you have been sold for your iniquities, &#124;&#124; And for your transgressions &#124;&#124; Your mother has been sent away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 59:1; Isaiah 66:4; Numbers 11:23"> Why have I come, and there is no one? I called, and there is none answering, &#124;&#124; Has My hand been at all short of redemption? And is there not power in Me to deliver? Behold, by My rebuke I dry up a sea, &#124;&#124; I make rivers a wilderness, &#124;&#124; Their fish stink, for there is no water, &#124;&#124; And die with thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 6:12; Psalms 18:11–12; Exodus 10:21"> I clothe the heavens &#91;with&#93; blackness, &#124;&#124; And I make their covering sackcloth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Jeremiah 1:9; John 7:15–17"> Lord YHWH has given to Me &#124;&#124; The tongue of taught ones, &#124;&#124; To know to aid the weary &#91;by&#93; a word, &#124;&#124; He awakens &#91;Me&#93; morning by morning, &#124;&#124; He awakens &#91;My&#93; ear to hear as taught ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 14:31; Hebrews 5:8; Isaiah 35:5"> Lord YHWH opened My ear, &#124;&#124; And I did not rebel—I did not move backward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Mark 14:65; Mark 15:19"> I have given My back to those striking, &#124;&#124; And My cheeks to those plucking out, &#124;&#124; I did not hide My face from shame and spitting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Ezekiel 3:8–9; John 16:33"> And Lord YHWH gives help to Me, &#124;&#124; Therefore I have not been ashamed, &#124;&#124; Therefore I have set My face as a flint, &#124;&#124; And I know that I am not ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:32–34; Revelation 12:10; Isaiah 41:1"> Near &#91;is&#93; He who is justifying Me, &#124;&#124; Who contends with Me? We stand together, who &#91;is&#93; My opponent? Let him come near to Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 13:28; Isaiah 41:10; Hebrews 1:11–12"> Behold, Lord YHWH gives help to Me, &#124;&#124; Who &#91;is&#93; he that declares Me wicked? Behold, all of them wear out as a garment, &#124;&#124; A moth eats them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 12:46; Micah 7:7–9; Isaiah 26:3–4"> “Who &#91;is&#93; among you, fearing YHWH, &#124;&#124; Listening to the voice of His Servant, &#124;&#124; That has walked in dark places, &#124;&#124; And there is no brightness for him? Let him trust in the Name of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And lean on his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5–7; Romans 1:21–22; Romans 10:3"> Behold, all you kindling a fire, girding on sparks, &#124;&#124; Walk in the light of your fire, &#124;&#124; And in the sparks you have caused to burn, &#124;&#124; This has been to you from My hand, &#124;&#124; You lie down in grief!”</span>
