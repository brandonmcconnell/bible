<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 45:7; Amos 3:6"> And He &#91;is&#93; also wise, and brings in evil, &#124;&#124; And He has not turned aside His words, &#124;&#124; And He has risen against a house of evildoers, &#124;&#124; And against the help of workers of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 9:17; Ezekiel 20:33–34; Ezekiel 28:9"> And the Egyptians &#91;are men&#93;, and not God, &#124;&#124; And their horses &#91;are&#93; flesh, and not spirit, &#124;&#124; And YHWH stretches out His hand, &#124;&#124; And the helper has stumbled, &#124;&#124; And the helped one has fallen, &#124;&#124; And together all of them are consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 11:10; Amos 3:8; Isaiah 42:13"> For thus said YHWH to me: “As the lion and the young lion growl over his prey, &#124;&#124; Against whom a multitude of shepherds is called, &#124;&#124; He is not frightened from their voice, &#124;&#124; And he is not humbled from their noise; So YHWH of Hosts comes down &#124;&#124; To war on Mount Zion, and on her height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 91:4; Psalms 37:40; Isaiah 37:35"> As birds flying, so does YHWH of Hosts &#124;&#124; Cover over Jerusalem, covering and delivering, &#124;&#124; Passing over, and causing to escape.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:22; Jeremiah 3:14; Isaiah 55:7"> Turn back to Him from whom sons of Israel &#124;&#124; Have deepened apostasy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 30:22; Isaiah 2:20; Ezekiel 36:25"> For in that day each despises His idols of silver, and his idols of gold, &#124;&#124; That your hands made to you—a sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 14:25; Genesis 49:15; Isaiah 37:7"> And Asshur has fallen by sword, not of the high, &#124;&#124; Indeed, a sword—not of the low, consumes him, &#124;&#124; And he has fled for himself from the face of a sword, &#124;&#124; And his young men become tributary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 29:6; Leviticus 6:13"> And he passes on &#91;to&#93; his rock from fear, &#124;&#124; And his princes have been frightened by the ensign—a declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Who has a light in Zion, &#124;&#124; And who has a furnace in Jerusalem!</span>
