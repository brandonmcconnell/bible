<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 38:1–8; 2 Chronicles 32:24; 2 Kings 20:1–11"> In those days has Hezekiah been sick to death, and Isaiah son of Amoz, the prophet, comes to him and says to him, “Thus said YHWH: Give a charge to your house, for you &#91;are&#93; dying, and do not live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; Psalms 91:15; 1 Kings 8:30"> And Hezekiah turns around his face to the wall, and prays to YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 18:20–27; Hebrews 6:10"> and says, “Ah, now, O YHWH, please remember how I have habitually walked before You in truth, and with a perfect heart, and I have done that which &#91;is&#93; good in your eyes”; and Hezekiah weeps &#91;with&#93; a great weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And a word of YHWH is to Isaiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 34:5–6; 1 John 5:14–15; Revelation 7:17"> “Go, and you have said to Hezekiah, Thus said YHWH, God of your father David: I have heard your prayer, I have seen your tear, behold, I am adding fifteen years to your days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:35; Isaiah 31:4–5; Isaiah 12:6"> and out of the hand of the king of Asshur I deliver you and this city, and have covered over this city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 6:37–39; Isaiah 37:30; Isaiah 7:11–14"> And this &#91;is&#93; to you the sign from YHWH, that YHWH does this thing that He has spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 20:9–11; Joshua 10:12–14; Matthew 16:1"> Behold, I am bringing back the shadow of the degrees that it has gone down on the degrees of Ahaz, by the sun, backward ten degrees”; and the sun turns back ten degrees in the degrees that it had gone down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 18:1; Exodus 15:1–21; Job 5:18"> A writing of Hezekiah king of Judah concerning his being sick when he revives from his sickness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 102:24; 2 Corinthians 1:9; Psalms 107:18"> “I said in the cutting off of my days, &#124;&#124; I go to the gates of Sheol, &#124;&#124; I have numbered the remnant of my years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:13; Psalms 116:8–9; Psalms 31:22"> I said, I do not see YAH—YAH! In the land of the living, &#124;&#124; I do not behold man anymore, &#124;&#124; With the inhabitants of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 73:14; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Hebrews 1:12"> My sojourning has departed, &#124;&#124; And been removed from me as a shepherd’s tent, &#124;&#124; I have drawn together, as a weaver, my life, &#124;&#124; By weakness it cuts me off, &#124;&#124; From day to night You end me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 51:8; Daniel 6:24; Psalms 50:22"> I have set &#91;Him&#93; as a lion until morning, &#124;&#124; So He breaks all my bones, &#124;&#124; From day to night You end me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:11; Psalms 69:3; Psalms 123:1–4"> As a crane—a swallow—so I chatter, &#124;&#124; I mourn as a dove, &#124;&#124; My eyes have been drawn up on high, &#124;&#124; O YHWH, oppression &#91;is&#93; on me, be my guarantor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 21:27; Job 7:11; Job 10:1"> What do I say? Seeing He spoke to me, &#124;&#124; And He Himself has worked, &#124;&#124; I go softly all my years for the bitterness of my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:10–11; 1 Corinthians 11:32; Psalms 119:25"> Lord, &#91;men&#93; live by these, &#124;&#124; And by all in them &#91;is&#93; the life of my spirit, &#124;&#124; And You save me, make me to also live,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Micah 7:18–19; Jonah 2:6"> Behold, He changed bitterness to peace for me, &#124;&#124; And You have delighted in my soul without corruption, &#124;&#124; For You have cast all my sins behind Your back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Psalms 30:9; Psalms 6:5"> For Sheol does not confess You, &#124;&#124; Death does not praise You, &#124;&#124; Those going down to the pit do not hope for Your truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Psalms 119:175; Psalms 78:3–6"> The living, the living, he confesses You,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 116:2; Psalms 150:4; Psalms 145:2"> Like myself today; A father makes known to &#91;his&#93; sons of Your faithfulness, &#124;&#124; O YHWH—to save me: And we sing my songs all &#91;the&#93; days of our lives &#124;&#124; In the house of YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 20:7–8; Mark 7:33; John 9:6"> And Isaiah says, “Let them take a bunch of figs, and plaster over the ulcer, and he lives.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 118:18–19; 2 Kings 20:8; John 5:14"> And Hezekiah says, “What &#91;is&#93; the sign that I go up to the house of YHWH?”</span>
