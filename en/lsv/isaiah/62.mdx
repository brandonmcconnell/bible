<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 62:6–7; Proverbs 4:18; Isaiah 32:15–17"> For Zion’s sake I am not silent, &#124;&#124; And for Jerusalem’s sake I do not rest, &#124;&#124; Until her righteousness goes out as brightness, &#124;&#124; And her salvation burns as a torch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 62:12; Genesis 17:5; Isaiah 66:12"> And nations have seen your righteousness, &#124;&#124; And all kings your glory, &#124;&#124; And He is giving a new name to you, &#124;&#124; That the mouth of YHWH defines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 28:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:19; Zechariah 9:16"> And you have been a crown of beauty in the hand of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And a turban of royalty in the hand of your God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:17; Hosea 2:19–20; Isaiah 62:12"> It is not said of you anymore, “Forsaken!” And of your land it is not said anymore, “Desolate,” &#124;&#124; For to you is cried, “My delight &#91;is&#93; in her,” &#124;&#124; And to your land, “Married,” &#124;&#124; For YHWH has delighted in you, &#124;&#124; And your land is married.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 62:4; Isaiah 65:19; Hebrews 12:2"> For a young man marries a virgin, &#124;&#124; Your builders marry you, &#124;&#124; With the joy of a bridegroom over a bride, &#124;&#124; Your God rejoices over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 52:8; Ezekiel 3:17–21; Psalms 134:1–2"> “On your walls, O Jerusalem, I have appointed watchmen, &#124;&#124; All the day and all the night, &#124;&#124; Continually, they are not silent.” O you remembering YHWH, do not keep silence for yourselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:9; Zephaniah 3:19–20; Isaiah 60:18"> And do not give silence to Him, &#124;&#124; Until He establishes, and until He makes Jerusalem a praise in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 65:21–23; Judges 6:3–6; Deuteronomy 28:33"> YHWH has sworn by His right hand, &#124;&#124; Even by the arm of His strength: “I do not give your grain anymore &#91;as&#93; food for your enemies, &#124;&#124; Nor do sons of a stranger drink your new wine, &#124;&#124; For which you have labored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:12; Deuteronomy 16:11; Deuteronomy 12:7"> For those gathering it eat it, and have praised YHWH, &#124;&#124; And those collecting it drink it in My holy courts.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 57:14; Isaiah 40:3; Hebrews 12:13"> Pass on, pass on through the gates, &#124;&#124; Prepare the way of the people, &#124;&#124; Raise up, raise up the highway, clear it from stones, &#124;&#124; Lift up an ensign over the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Matthew 21:5; John 12:15"> Behold, YHWH has proclaimed to the end of the earth: “Say to the daughter of Zion, &#124;&#124; Behold, your salvation has come, &#124;&#124; Behold, His hire &#91;is&#93; with Him, &#124;&#124; And His wage before Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Deuteronomy 26:19; Isaiah 62:4"> And they have cried to them, “The People of the Holy One! The Redeemed of YHWH!” Indeed, to you is called, “The sought out one, a city not forsaken!”</span>
