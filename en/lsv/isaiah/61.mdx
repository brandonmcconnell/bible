<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 5:4; Isaiah 34:8; Luke 4:19"> To proclaim the year of the favor of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And the day of vengeance of our God, &#124;&#124; To comfort all mourners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 30:11; Jeremiah 17:7–8; John 16:20"> To appoint to mourners in Zion, &#124;&#124; To give to them beauty instead of ashes, &#124;&#124; The oil of joy instead of mourning, &#124;&#124; A covering of praise for a faded spirit, &#124;&#124; And He is calling to them, Trees of righteousness, &#124;&#124; The planting of YHWH—to be beautified.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 58:12; Ezekiel 36:33–36; Amos 9:14–15"> And they have built the ancient ruins, &#124;&#124; They raise up the desolations of the ancients, &#124;&#124; And they have renewed ruined cities, &#124;&#124; The desolations of generation and generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 60:10–14; Isaiah 14:1–2; Ephesians 2:12–20"> And strangers have stood and fed your flock, &#124;&#124; And the sons of a foreigner &#124;&#124; &#91;Are&#93; your farmers and your vinedressers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 19:6; 1 Peter 2:5; 1 Peter 2:9"> And you are called priests of YHWH, &#124;&#124; It is said of you &#91;that you are&#93; ministers of our God, &#124;&#124; You consume the strength of nations, &#124;&#124; And you boast yourselves in their glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 42:10; Zechariah 9:12; Isaiah 40:2"> Instead of your shame—double &#91;honor&#93;, &#124;&#124; And &#91;instead of&#93; confusion, &#124;&#124; They rejoice in their portion, &#124;&#124; Therefore they take possession in their land a second time, &#124;&#124; Continuous joy &#91;is&#93; for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 11:7; Jeremiah 9:24; Zechariah 8:16–17"> “For I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, loving judgment, &#124;&#124; Hating plunder for a burnt-offering, &#124;&#124; And I have given their wage in truth, &#124;&#124; And I make a perpetual covenant for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Psalms 115:14; Genesis 22:18"> And their seed has been known among the nations, &#124;&#124; And their offspring in the midst of the peoples, &#124;&#124; All their beholders acknowledge them, &#124;&#124; For they &#91;are&#93; a seed YHWH has blessed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–8; Psalms 132:9; Psalms 132:16"> I greatly rejoice in YHWH, &#124;&#124; My soul rejoices in my God, &#124;&#124; For He clothed me with garments of salvation, &#124;&#124; &#91;And&#93; covered me with a robe of righteousness, &#124;&#124; As a bridegroom prepares ornaments, &#124;&#124; And as a bride puts on her jewels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 4:26–32; Isaiah 58:11; 1 Peter 2:9"> For as the earth brings forth her shoots, &#124;&#124; And as a garden causes its sown things to shoot up, &#124;&#124; So Lord YHWH causes righteousness and praise &#124;&#124; To shoot up before all the nations!</span>
