<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; Isaiah 51:12; Isaiah 51:3"> “Comfort, comfort My people,” says your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 9:12; Isaiah 61:7; Hosea 2:14"> Speak to the heart of Jerusalem, and call to her, &#124;&#124; That her warfare has been completed, &#124;&#124; That her punishment has been accepted, &#124;&#124; That she has received from the hand of YHWH &#124;&#124; Double for all her sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Mark 1:2–5; John 1:23"> A voice is crying in a wilderness: “Prepare the way of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Make straight in a desert a highway for our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 3:5; Ezekiel 21:26; Luke 18:14"> Every valley is raised up, &#124;&#124; And every mountain and hill become low, &#124;&#124; And the crooked place has become a plain, &#124;&#124; And the entangled places a valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Joel 2:28; Isaiah 52:10"> And the glory of YHWH has been revealed, &#124;&#124; And all flesh have seen &#91;it&#93; together, &#124;&#124; For the mouth of YHWH has spoken.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 103:15–16; 1 Peter 1:24–25; James 1:10–11"> A voice is saying, “Call,” &#124;&#124; And he said, “What do I call?” All flesh &#91;is&#93; grass, and all its goodness &#124;&#124; &#91;Is&#93; as a flower of the field:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 41:21"> Grass has withered, the flower faded, &#124;&#124; For the Spirit of YHWH blew on it, &#124;&#124; Surely the people &#91;is&#93; grass;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 13:31; 1 Peter 1:25; Matthew 24:35"> Grass has withered, the flower faded, &#124;&#124; But a word of our God rises forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7–8; Isaiah 12:2; Isaiah 25:9"> Get up on a high mountain, O Zion, &#124;&#124; Proclaiming tidings, &#124;&#124; Lift up your voice with power, O Jerusalem, proclaiming tidings, &#124;&#124; Lift up, do not fear, say to cities of Judah, “Behold, your God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:12; Isaiah 62:11; Revelation 17:14"> Behold, Lord YHWH comes with strength, &#124;&#124; And His arm is ruling for Him, &#124;&#124; Behold, His hire &#91;is&#93; with Him, and His wage before Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 7:17; Micah 5:4; Psalms 23:1–6"> He feeds His flock as a shepherd, &#124;&#124; He gathers lambs with His arm, &#124;&#124; And He carries &#91;them&#93; in His bosom: He leads suckling ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Hebrews 1:10–12; Psalms 102:25–26"> Who has measured the waters in the hollow of His hand, &#124;&#124; And has meted out the heavens by a span, &#124;&#124; And comprehended the dust of the earth in a measure, &#124;&#124; And has weighed the mountains in scales, &#124;&#124; And the hills in a balance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Romans 11:34; Job 21:22"> Who has meted out the Spirit of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And &#91;being&#93; His counselor, teaches Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 21:22; Colossians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 12:4–6"> With whom &#91;did&#93; He consult, &#124;&#124; That he causes Him to understand? And teaches Him in the path of judgment, &#124;&#124; And teaches Him knowledge? And causes Him to know the way of understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Job 34:14–15; Jeremiah 10:10"> Behold, nations &#91;are&#93; as a drop from a bucket, &#124;&#124; And have been reckoned as small dust of the balance, &#124;&#124; Behold, He takes up islands as a small thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; Psalms 40:6; Psalms 50:9–12"> And Lebanon is not sufficient to burn, &#124;&#124; Nor its beasts sufficient for a burnt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 62:9; Daniel 4:34–35; Isaiah 29:7"> All the nations &#91;are&#93; as nothing before Him, &#124;&#124; Less than nothing and emptiness, &#124;&#124; They have been reckoned to Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 46:5; 1 Samuel 2:2; Isaiah 40:25"> And to whom do you liken God, &#124;&#124; And what likeness do you compare to Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:9; Habakkuk 2:18–19; Isaiah 46:6–7"> An artisan has poured out the carved image, &#124;&#124; And a refiner spreads it over with gold, &#124;&#124; And he is refining chains of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:3–4; Isaiah 41:7; Isaiah 46:7"> He who is poor &#91;by&#93; raised-offerings, &#124;&#124; Chooses a tree &#91;that is&#93; not rotten, &#124;&#124; He seeks a skillful artisan for it, &#124;&#124; To establish a carved image—not moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 48:13; Psalms 50:6; Acts 14:17"> Do you not know—do you not hear? Has it not been declared from the first to you? Have you not understood &#124;&#124; &#91;From&#93; the foundations of the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 8:27; Psalms 104:2; Isaiah 42:5"> He who is sitting on the circle of the earth, &#124;&#124; And its inhabitants &#91;are&#93; as grasshoppers, &#124;&#124; He who is stretching out the heavens as a thin thing, &#124;&#124; And spreads them as a tent to dwell in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 12:21; Psalms 107:40; Isaiah 34:12"> He who is making princes become nothing, &#124;&#124; Has made judges of earth formless;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 41:16; Isaiah 17:13; Haggai 1:9"> Indeed, they have not been planted, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they have not been sown, &#124;&#124; Indeed, their stock is not taking root in the earth, &#124;&#124; And He has also blown on them, and they wither, &#124;&#124; And a whirlwind takes them away as stubble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 40:18; Deuteronomy 4:33; Deuteronomy 4:15–18"> And to whom do you liken Me, &#124;&#124; And &#91;to whom&#93; I equal? Says the Holy One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 1:16–17; Jeremiah 32:17–19; Isaiah 48:13"> Lift up your eyes on high, &#124;&#124; And see—who has created these? He who is bringing out their host by number, &#124;&#124; He calls to all of them by name, &#124;&#124; By the abundance of His strength &#124;&#124; And mighty power, &#124;&#124; Not one is lacking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 18:7–8; Isaiah 49:4; Isaiah 49:14"> Why say, O Jacob, and speak, O Israel, “My way has been hid from YHWH, &#124;&#124; And from my God my judgment passes over?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 11:33–34; Psalms 147:5; Isaiah 55:8–9"> Have you not known? Have you not heard? The God of the age—YHWH, &#124;&#124; Preparer of the ends of the earth, &#124;&#124; Is not wearied nor fatigued, &#124;&#124; There is no searching of His understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> He is giving power to the weary, &#124;&#124; And to those not strong He increases might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Amos 2:14; Psalms 34:10; Psalms 33:16"> Even youths are wearied and fatigued, &#124;&#124; And young men utterly stumble,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Psalms 27:13–14"> But those expecting YHWH pass &#91;to&#93; power, &#124;&#124; They raise up the pinion as eagles, &#124;&#124; They run and are not fatigued, &#124;&#124; They go on and do not faint!</span>
