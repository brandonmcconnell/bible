<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:18–21; Luke 3:22; Isaiah 61:1"> Behold, My servant, I take hold on Him, &#124;&#124; My Chosen One—My soul has accepted, I have put My Spirit on Him, &#124;&#124; He brings forth judgment to nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:16–20; 2 Timothy 2:24; Matthew 11:29"> He does not cry, nor lift up, &#124;&#124; Nor cause His voice to be heard in the street.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Jeremiah 31:25; Psalms 147:3"> A bruised reed He does not break, &#124;&#124; And faded flax He does not quench, &#124;&#124; He brings forth judgment to truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–12; Genesis 49:10; Isaiah 52:13–15"> He does not become weak nor bruised, &#124;&#124; Until He sets judgment in the earth, &#124;&#124; And islands wait with hope for His law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Isaiah 45:12; Jeremiah 10:12"> Thus said God, YHWH, &#124;&#124; Creating the heavens, and stretching them out, &#124;&#124; Spreading out the earth and its productions, &#124;&#124; Giving breath to the people on it, &#124;&#124; And spirit to those walking in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 2:32; John 8:12; Acts 13:47"> I, YHWH, called you in righteousness, &#124;&#124; And I lay hold on your hand, and keep you, &#124;&#124; And I give you for a covenant of a people, &#124;&#124; And a light of nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1; Ephesians 1:17–18; Isaiah 35:5"> To open the eyes of the blind, &#124;&#124; To bring forth the bound one from prison, &#124;&#124; Those sitting in darkness from the house of restraint.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 48:11; Isaiah 43:11; Exodus 34:14"> I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, this &#91;is&#93; My Name, &#124;&#124; And I do not give My glory to another, &#124;&#124; Nor My praise to carved images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 43:19; Isaiah 46:9–10; John 13:19"> The former things, behold, have come, &#124;&#124; And I am declaring new things, &#124;&#124; Before they spring up I cause you to hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 33:3; Psalms 40:3; Romans 15:9–11"> Sing a new song to YHWH, &#124;&#124; His praise from the end of the earth, &#124;&#124; You who are going down to the sea, and its fullness, &#124;&#124; Islands, and their inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 32:16; Isaiah 60:7; Isaiah 41:18–19"> The wilderness and its cities lift up &#91;their voice&#93;, &#124;&#124; Kedar inhabits the villages, &#124;&#124; The inhabitants of Sela sing, &#124;&#124; They cry from the top of mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:9–11; Psalms 117:1–2; Revelation 7:9–12"> They ascribe to YHWH glory, &#124;&#124; And they declare His praise in the islands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 15:1–3; Psalms 78:65; Isaiah 59:16–19"> YHWH goes forth as a mighty one. He stirs up zeal as a man of war, &#124;&#124; He cries, indeed, He shrieks, &#124;&#124; He shows Himself mighty against His enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 83:1–2; Ecclesiastes 8:11–12; 2 Peter 3:15"> I have kept silent from of old, &#124;&#124; I keep silent, I refrain Myself, &#124;&#124; I cry out as a travailing woman, &#124;&#124; I desolate and swallow up together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 50:2; Nahum 1:4–6; Isaiah 44:27"> I make desolate mountains and hills, &#124;&#124; And I dry up all their herbs, &#124;&#124; And I have made rivers become islands, &#124;&#124; And I dry up ponds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 30:21; Luke 3:5; Isaiah 60:19–20"> And I have caused the blind to go, &#124;&#124; In a way they have not known, &#124;&#124; I cause them to tread in paths they have not known, &#124;&#124; I make a dark place become light before them, &#124;&#124; And unleveled places become a plain, &#124;&#124; These &#91;are&#93; the things I have done to them, &#124;&#124; And I have not forsaken them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 97:7; Isaiah 1:29; Isaiah 44:11"> Removed backward—utterly ashamed, &#124;&#124; Are those trusting in a carved image, &#124;&#124; Those saying to a molten image, “You &#91;are&#93; our gods.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 43:8; Proverbs 20:12; Mark 7:34–37"> You deaf, hear; and you blind, look to see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; Ezekiel 12:2; Jeremiah 5:21"> Who &#91;is&#93; blind but My servant? And deaf as My messenger I send? Who &#91;is&#93; blind as he who is at peace, &#124;&#124; Indeed, blind, as the servant of YHWH?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:2–4; Romans 2:21; Acts 28:22–27"> Seeing many things, and you do not observe, &#124;&#124; Opening ears, and he does not hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 8:10; Romans 3:31; Isaiah 46:12–13"> YHWH has delight for the sake of His righteousness, &#124;&#124; He magnifies law, and makes honorable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 102:20; Jeremiah 50:17; Isaiah 51:23"> And this &#91;is&#93; a people seized and spoiled, &#124;&#124; Snared in holes—all of them, &#124;&#124; And they were hidden in houses of restraint, &#124;&#124; They have been for a prey, &#124;&#124; And there is no deliverer, &#124;&#124; A spoil, and none is saying, “Restore.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Isaiah 48:18; Deuteronomy 32:29"> Who among you gives ear &#91;to&#93; this? Attends, and hears afterward?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 47:6; Isaiah 10:5–6; Matthew 22:7"> Who has given Jacob for a spoil, &#124;&#124; And Israel to the spoilers? Is it not YHWH—He against whom we sinned? Indeed, they have not been willing to walk in His ways, &#124;&#124; Nor have they listened to His law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 7:9; Isaiah 57:11; Malachi 2:2"> And He pours fury on him, &#124;&#124; His anger, and the strength of battle, &#124;&#124; And it sets him on fire all around, &#124;&#124; And he has not known, &#124;&#124; And it burns against him, and he does not lay it to heart!</span>
