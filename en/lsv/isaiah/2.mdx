<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; Isaiah 13:1; Habakkuk 1:1"> The thing that Isaiah son of Amoz has seen concerning Judah and Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 4:1–3; Isaiah 56:7; Zechariah 8:3"> And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; In the latter end of the days, &#124;&#124; The mountain of YHWH’s house is established, &#124;&#124; Above the top of the mountains, &#124;&#124; And it has been lifted up above the heights, &#124;&#124; And all the nations have flowed to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:4–5; Luke 24:47; Zechariah 8:20–23"> And many peoples have gone and said, “Come, and we go up to the mountain of YHWH, &#124;&#124; To the house of the God of Jacob, &#124;&#124; And He teaches us of His ways, &#124;&#124; And we walk in His paths, &#124;&#124; For a law goes forth from Zion, &#124;&#124; And a word of YHWH from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 2:18; Micah 4:3; Isaiah 11:6–9"> And He has judged between the nations, &#124;&#124; And has given a decision to many peoples, &#124;&#124; And they have beat their swords to plowshares, &#124;&#124; And their spears to pruning-hooks, &#124;&#124; Nation does not lift up sword to nation, &#124;&#124; Nor do they learn anymore—war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Luke 1:79; Isaiah 60:19–20"> O house of Jacob, come, &#124;&#124; And we walk in the light of YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:20; 2 Kings 1:2; Psalms 106:35"> For You have left Your people, the house of Jacob. For they have been filled from the east, &#124;&#124; And &#91;are&#93; sorcerers like the Philistines, &#124;&#124; And strike hands with the children of strangers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16–17; Isaiah 30:16; Isaiah 31:1"> And its land is full of silver and gold, &#124;&#124; And there is no end to its treasures, &#124;&#124; And its land is full of horses, &#124;&#124; And there is no end to its chariots,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 115:4–8; Revelation 9:20; Jeremiah 2:28"> And its land is full of idols, &#124;&#124; It bows itself to the work of its hands, &#124;&#124; To that which its fingers have made,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 5:15; Joshua 24:19; Romans 3:23"> And the low bows down, and the high is humbled, &#124;&#124; And You do not accept them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 6:15–16; Isaiah 2:19–21; Luke 23:30"> Enter into a rock, and be hidden in dust, &#124;&#124; Because of the fear of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And because of the honor of His excellence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:15–16; Psalms 18:27; Isaiah 2:17"> The haughty eyes of man have been humbled, &#124;&#124; And the loftiness of men has been bowed down, &#124;&#124; And YHWH alone has been set on high in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 23:9; Proverbs 16:5"> For a day &#91;is&#93; to YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; For every proud and high one, &#124;&#124; And for every lifted up and low one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 14:8; Zechariah 11:1–2; Ezekiel 31:3–12"> And for all cedars of Lebanon, &#124;&#124; The high and the exalted ones, &#124;&#124; And for all oaks of Bashan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 30:25; Isaiah 40:4; 2 Corinthians 10:5"> And for all the high mountains, &#124;&#124; And for all the exalted heights,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 25:12"> And for every high tower, &#124;&#124; And for every fortified wall,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 10:22; Isaiah 23:1; Numbers 33:52"> And for all ships of Tarshish, &#124;&#124; And for all desirable pictures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:29–30; Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 13:11"> And the haughtiness of man has been bowed down, &#124;&#124; And the loftiness of men humbled, &#124;&#124; And YHWH alone has been set on high in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 27:9; Ezekiel 36:25; Isaiah 21:9"> And the idols—they completely pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 2:10; Luke 23:30; Hosea 10:8"> And &#91;men&#93; have entered into caverns of rocks, &#124;&#124; And into caves of dust, &#124;&#124; Because of the fear of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And because of the honor of His excellence, &#124;&#124; In His rising to terrify the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 30:22; Isaiah 31:7; Isaiah 46:6"> In that day man casts his idols of silver, &#124;&#124; And his idols of gold, &#124;&#124; That they have made for him to worship, &#124;&#124; To moles, and to bats,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19; Song of Songs 2:14; Isaiah 2:10"> To enter into cavities of the rocks, &#124;&#124; And into clefts of the high places, &#124;&#124; Because of the fear of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And because of the honor of His excellence, &#124;&#124; In His rising to terrify the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Psalms 146:3; James 4:14"> Cease yourselves from man, &#124;&#124; Whose breath &#91;is&#93; in his nostrils, &#124;&#124; For—in what is he esteemed?</span>
