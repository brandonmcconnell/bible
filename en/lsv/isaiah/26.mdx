<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 60:18; Isaiah 62:11; Ephesians 5:19–20"> In that day this song is sung in the land of Judah: “We have a strong city, &#124;&#124; He makes salvation &#91;for&#93; walls and bulwark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Isaiah 60:11; 1 Peter 2:9"> Open the gates, &#124;&#124; That a righteous nation may enter, &#124;&#124; Preserving steadfastness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 4:7; John 16:33; John 14:27"> You keep &#91;him&#93; in peace &#91;whose&#93; imagination &#91;is&#93; stayed—peace! For he is confident in You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 62:8; Psalms 55:22"> Trust in YHWH forever, &#124;&#124; For in YAH—YHWH &#91;is&#93; a rock of ages,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:37; Revelation 18:2; Job 40:11–13"> For He bowed down the dwellers on high, &#124;&#124; A city set on high He makes low, &#124;&#124; He makes it low to the earth, &#124;&#124; He causes it to come to the dust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Luke 10:19; Revelation 2:26"> A foot treads it down, &#124;&#124; Feet of the poor—steps of the weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Isaiah 42:16; 1 John 3:7"> The path for the righteous &#91;is&#93; uprightness, &#124;&#124; O upright One, &#124;&#124; You ponder the path of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 37:3–7; James 5:7–11; Isaiah 33:2"> Also, &#91;in&#93; the path of Your judgments, &#124;&#124; O YHWH, we have waited &#91;for&#93; You, &#124;&#124; To Your Name and to Your remembrance &#124;&#124; &#91;Is&#93; the desire of the soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Psalms 63:1; Psalms 119:62"> I desired You in the night &#91;with&#93; my soul, &#124;&#124; Also, I seek You earnestly &#91;with&#93; my spirit within me, &#124;&#124; For when Your judgments &#91;are&#93; on the earth, &#124;&#124; The inhabitants of the world have learned righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 11:7; John 5:37–38; Romans 2:4–5"> The wicked finds favor, &#124;&#124; He has not learned righteousness, &#124;&#124; He deals perversely in a land of straightforwardness, &#124;&#124; And does not see the excellence of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:27; Micah 5:9; Isaiah 5:24"> O YHWH, Your hand &#91;is&#93; high—they do not see, &#124;&#124; They see the zeal of the people, and are ashamed, &#124;&#124; Also, the fire consumes Your adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Psalms 57:2; Psalms 29:11"> O YHWH, You appoint peace to us, &#124;&#124; For You have also worked all our works for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 63:7; Isaiah 2:8; Isaiah 10:11"> O our God YHWH, lords have ruled us besides You, &#124;&#124; Only, by You we make mention of Your Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 18:2–3; Proverbs 10:7; Isaiah 51:12–13"> Dead—they do not live, &#124;&#124; Rephaim, they do not rise, &#124;&#124; Therefore You have inspected and destroy them, &#124;&#124; Indeed, you destroy all their memory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:3; Isaiah 33:17; Nehemiah 9:23"> You have added to the nation, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; You have added to the nation, &#124;&#124; You have been honored, &#124;&#124; You have put all the ends of the earth far off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Psalms 50:15; Psalms 77:1–2"> O YHWH, in distress they missed You, &#124;&#124; They have poured out a whisper, &#124;&#124; Your discipline &#91;is&#93; on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 21:3; Isaiah 13:8; John 16:21"> When a pregnant woman comes near to the birth, &#124;&#124; She is pained—she cries in her pangs, &#124;&#124; So we have been from Your face, O YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 13:13; Psalms 17:14; 2 Kings 19:3"> We have conceived, we have been pained. We have brought forth, as it were, wind, &#124;&#124; We do not work salvation in the earth, &#124;&#124; Nor do the inhabitants of the world fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Ezekiel 37:1–14; Matthew 27:52"> Your dead live—My dead body, they rise. Awake and sing, you dwellers in the dust, &#124;&#124; For the dew of herbs &#91;is&#93; your dew, &#124;&#124; And you cause the land of Rephaim to fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; Psalms 27:5; Isaiah 54:7–8"> Come, My people, enter into your inner chambers, &#124;&#124; And shut your doors behind you, &#124;&#124; Hide yourself shortly &#91;for&#93; a moment until the indignation passes over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 6:9–11; Isaiah 13:11; Jude 1:14–15"> For behold, YHWH is coming out of His place, &#124;&#124; To charge the iniquity of the inhabitant of the earth on him, &#124;&#124; And the earth has revealed her blood, &#124;&#124; Nor does she cover her slain anymore!”</span>
