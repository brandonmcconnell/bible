<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; Isaiah 43:7; Zechariah 13:9"> And now, thus said YHWH, Your Creator, O Jacob, and your Fashioner, O Israel: Do not be afraid, for I have redeemed you, &#124;&#124; I have called on your name—you &#91;are&#93; Mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Joshua 1:9; Psalms 66:12"> When you pass into waters, I &#91;am&#93; with you, &#124;&#124; And into floods, they do not overflow you, &#124;&#124; When you go into fire, you are not burned, &#124;&#124; And a flame does not burn against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 45:15; 2 Chronicles 14:9–14"> For I—your God YHWH, &#124;&#124; The Holy One of Israel, your Savior, &#124;&#124; I have appointed Egypt your atonement, &#124;&#124; Cush and Seba in your stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6–8; 1 Peter 2:9; Jeremiah 31:3"> Since you were precious in My eyes, &#124;&#124; You were honored, and I have loved you, &#124;&#124; And I appoint men in your stead, &#124;&#124; And peoples instead of your life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:3; Isaiah 49:12; Luke 13:29"> Do not be afraid, for I &#91;am&#93; with you, &#124;&#124; I bring in your seed from the east, &#124;&#124; And I gather you from the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:17–18; Jeremiah 3:14; Jeremiah 3:18–19"> I am saying to the north, “Give up,” &#124;&#124; And to the south, “Do not restrain.” Bring in My sons from afar, &#124;&#124; And My daughters from the end of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Isaiah 43:21; Ephesians 1:12"> Everyone who is called by My Name, &#124;&#124; Even for My glory I have created him, &#124;&#124; I have formed him, indeed, I have made him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; Ezekiel 12:2; Isaiah 42:18–20"> He brought out a blind people who have eyes, &#124;&#124; And deaf ones who have ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 46:10; Isaiah 44:7–9; Isaiah 41:21–26"> All the nations have been gathered together, &#124;&#124; And the peoples are assembled, &#124;&#124; Who among them declares this, &#124;&#124; And causes us to hear former things? They give their witnesses, &#124;&#124; And they are declared righteous, &#124;&#124; And they hear and say, “Truth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:4; Isaiah 45:6; Isaiah 46:8–9"> You &#91;are&#93; My witnesses, a declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And My servant whom I have chosen, &#124;&#124; So that you know and give credence to Me, &#124;&#124; And understand that I &#91;am&#93; He, &#124;&#124; Before Me there was no God formed, &#124;&#124; And after Me there is none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 13:4; Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 45:21–22"> I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; And besides Me there is no savior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:12; Psalms 81:9–10; Isaiah 43:10"> I declared, and saved, and proclaimed, &#124;&#124; And there is no stranger with you, &#124;&#124; And you &#91;are&#93; My witnesses, a declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And I &#91;am&#93; God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 14:27; Isaiah 46:10; Psalms 90:2"> Even from the day I &#91;am&#93; He, &#124;&#124; And there is no deliverer from My hand, &#124;&#124; I work, and who turns it back?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 23:13; Jeremiah 51:24"> Thus said YHWH, your Redeemer, &#124;&#124; The Holy One of Israel: “I have sent to Babylon for your sake, &#124;&#124; And caused bars to descend—all of them, &#124;&#124; And the Chaldeans, whose song &#91;is&#93; in the ships.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 43:3; Matthew 25:34; Isaiah 48:17"> I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, your Holy One, &#124;&#124; Creator of Israel, your King.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 51:10; Isaiah 43:2; Psalms 77:19"> Thus said YHWH, &#124;&#124; Who is giving a way in the sea, &#124;&#124; And a path in the strong waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:4–9; Isaiah 1:31; Ezekiel 38:8–18"> Who is bringing forth chariot and horse, &#124;&#124; A force, even a strong one: “They lie down together—they do not rise, &#124;&#124; They have been extinguished, &#124;&#124; They have been quenched as flax.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; 2 Corinthians 3:10; 1 Chronicles 16:12"> Do not remember former things, &#124;&#124; And do not consider ancient things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 48:6; Isaiah 42:9; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> Behold, I am doing a new thing, now it springs up, &#124;&#124; Do you not know it? Indeed, I put a way in a wilderness, &#124;&#124; In a desolate place—floods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:17; Isaiah 48:21; Isaiah 49:10"> The beast of the field honors Me, &#124;&#124; Dragons and daughters of an ostrich, &#124;&#124; For I have given waters in a wilderness, &#124;&#124; Floods in a desolate place, &#124;&#124; To give drink to My people—My chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 102:18"> I have formed this people for Myself, &#124;&#124; They recount My praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 6:3; Malachi 3:14; Malachi 1:13"> And you have not called Me, O Jacob, &#124;&#124; For you have been wearied of Me, O Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Amos 5:25; Zechariah 7:5–6; Malachi 3:8"> You have not brought to Me, &#124;&#124; The lamb of your burnt-offerings, &#124;&#124; And &#91;with&#93; your sacrifices you have not honored Me, &#124;&#124; I have not caused you to serve with a present, &#124;&#124; Nor wearied you with frankincense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 7:13; Jeremiah 6:20; Isaiah 1:14"> You have not bought Me sweet cane with money, &#124;&#124; And have not filled Me &#91;with&#93; the fat of your sacrifices, &#124;&#124; Only—you have caused Me to serve with your sins, &#124;&#124; You have wearied Me with your iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:12; Micah 7:18–19; Hebrews 10:17"> I &#91;am&#93; He who is blotting out Your transgressions for My own sake, &#124;&#124; And I do not remember your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Isaiah 43:9; Romans 8:33"> Cause Me to remember—we are judged together, &#124;&#124; Declare that you may be justified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 28:7; Jeremiah 5:31; Ezekiel 22:25–28"> Your first father sinned, &#124;&#124; And your interpreters transgressed against Me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; Isaiah 65:15; Zechariah 8:13"> And I defile princes of the sanctuary, &#124;&#124; And I give Jacob to destruction, and Israel to revilings!</span>
