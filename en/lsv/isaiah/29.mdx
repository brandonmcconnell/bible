<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 4:4–5; 2 Samuel 5:9; Micah 6:6–7"> Woe &#91;to&#93; Ariel, Ariel, &#124;&#124; The city of the encampment of David! Add year to year, let festivals go around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25–30; Isaiah 3:26; Isaiah 10:32"> And I have sent distress to Ariel, &#124;&#124; And it has been lamentation and mourning, &#124;&#124; And it has been to me as Ariel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 19:43–44; Ezekiel 21:22; 2 Kings 25:1–4"> And I encamped, O babbler, against you, &#124;&#124; And I laid siege against you—a camp. And I raised up bulwarks against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; Psalms 44:25; Isaiah 3:8"> And you have been low, &#124;&#124; You speak from the earth, &#124;&#124; And make your saying low from the dust, &#124;&#124; And your voice has been from the earth, &#124;&#124; As one having a familiar spirit, &#124;&#124; And your saying whispers from the dust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Isaiah 17:13–14; Isaiah 37:36"> And as small dust has been &#124;&#124; The multitude of those scattering you, &#124;&#124; And as chaff passing on the multitude of the terrible, &#124;&#124; And it has been in an instant—suddenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:8; 1 Samuel 2:10; Matthew 24:7"> You are inspected by YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; With thunder, and with an earthquake, &#124;&#124; And great noise, windstorm, and whirlwind, &#124;&#124; And flame of devouring fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 20:8; Micah 4:11–12; Zechariah 12:9"> And as a dream, a vision of night, &#124;&#124; Has been the multitude of all the nations &#124;&#124; Who are warring against Ariel, &#124;&#124; And all its warriors, and its bulwark, &#124;&#124; Even of those distressing her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:12; Psalms 73:20; Isaiah 10:7–16"> And it has been, as when the hungry dreams, &#124;&#124; And behold, he is eating, &#124;&#124; And he has awoken, &#124;&#124; And his soul &#91;is&#93; empty, &#124;&#124; And as when the thirsty dreams, &#124;&#124; And behold, he is drinking, &#124;&#124; And he has awoken, &#124;&#124; And behold, he is weary, &#124;&#124; And his soul is longing, &#124;&#124; So is the multitude of all the nations &#124;&#124; Who are warring against Mount Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 51:21–22; Isaiah 19:14; Revelation 17:6"> Linger and wonder, look, indeed, look, &#124;&#124; Be drunk, and not with wine, &#124;&#124; Stagger, and not with strong drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 11:8; Isaiah 44:18; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–12"> For YHWH has poured out a spirit of deep sleep on you, &#124;&#124; And He closes your eyes—the prophets, &#124;&#124; And your heads—the seers—He covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:11; Daniel 12:4; Isaiah 8:16"> And the vision of the whole is to you, &#124;&#124; As words of the sealed scroll, &#124;&#124; That they give to one knowing scrolls, &#124;&#124; Saying, “Please read this,” &#124;&#124; And he has said, “I am not able, for it &#91;is&#93; sealed”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 7:15–16; Isaiah 29:18; Jeremiah 5:4"> And the scroll is given to him who has not known scrolls, &#124;&#124; Saying, “Please read this,” &#124;&#124; And he has said, “I have not known scrolls.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:2; Ezekiel 33:31–33; Mark 7:2–13"> And the Lord says: “Because this people has drawn near with its mouth, &#124;&#124; And they have honored Me with its lips, &#124;&#124; And it has put its heart far off from Me, &#124;&#124; And their fear of Me is a precept taught by men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; 1 Corinthians 1:19–24; Isaiah 28:21"> Therefore, behold, I am adding to do wonderfully with this people, &#124;&#124; A wonder, and a marvel, &#124;&#124; And the wisdom of its wise ones has perished, &#124;&#124; And the understanding of its intelligent ones hides itself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Isaiah 47:10; Isaiah 30:1"> Woe &#91;to&#93; those going deep from YHWH to hide counsel, &#124;&#124; And whose works have been in darkness. And they say, “Who is seeing us? And who is knowing us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 9:19–21; Isaiah 64:8; Isaiah 45:9–11"> Your perversion! Is the potter esteemed as clay, &#124;&#124; That the work says of its maker, “He has not made me?” And the framed thing said of its framer, “He did not understand?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 32:15; Matthew 19:30; Hebrews 10:37"> Is it not yet a very little &#91;while&#93;, &#124;&#124; And Lebanon has turned into a fruitful field, &#124;&#124; And the fruitful field is reckoned for a forest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5; Matthew 11:5; Mark 7:37"> And the deaf have heard the words of a scroll in that day, &#124;&#124; And out of thick darkness, and out of darkness, &#124;&#124; The eyes of the blind see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 2:5; Isaiah 61:1; Matthew 11:29"> And the humble have added joy in YHWH, &#124;&#124; And the poor among men &#124;&#124; Rejoice in the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Isaiah 28:14–22; Luke 16:14"> For the terrible one has ceased, &#124;&#124; And the scorner has been consumed, &#124;&#124; And all watching for iniquity have been cut off,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Amos 5:10–12; James 5:6; Acts 3:14"> Who cause men to sin in word, &#124;&#124; And lay a snare for a reprover in the gate, &#124;&#124; And turn aside the righteous into emptiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 45:17; Isaiah 51:2; Jeremiah 30:10"> Therefore, thus said YHWH, &#124;&#124; Who ransomed Abraham, &#124;&#124; Concerning the house of Jacob: “Jacob is not ashamed now, &#124;&#124; Nor does his face now become pale,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Isaiah 19:25; Isaiah 45:11"> For in his seeing his children, &#124;&#124; The work of My hand, in his midst, &#124;&#124; They sanctify My Name, &#124;&#124; And have sanctified the Holy One of Jacob, &#124;&#124; And they declare the God of Israel fearful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 15:17–19; Hebrews 5:2; Isaiah 28:7"> And the erring in spirit have known understanding, &#124;&#124; And murmurers learn doctrine!”</span>
