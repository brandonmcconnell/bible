<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 2:1–3; Jeremiah 48:1–47; Isaiah 11:14"> The burden of Moab. Because in a night Ar of Moab was destroyed—It has been cut off, &#124;&#124; Because in a night Kir of Moab was destroyed—It has been cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:1; Leviticus 21:5; Isaiah 3:24"> He has gone up to Bajith and Dibon, &#124;&#124; The high places—to weep, &#124;&#124; Moab howls on Nebo and on Medeba, &#124;&#124; Baldness &#91;is&#93; on all its heads, every beard cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jonah 3:6–8; Jeremiah 19:13; Deuteronomy 22:8"> In its out-places they girded on sackcloth, &#124;&#124; On its pinnacles, and in its broad places, &#124;&#124; Everyone howls—going down with weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:34; 1 Kings 19:4; Jonah 4:8"> And Heshbon and Elealeh cry, &#124;&#124; Their voice has been heard to Jahaz, &#124;&#124; Therefore the armed ones of Moab shout, &#124;&#124; His life has been grievous to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:5; Jeremiah 4:20; Luke 19:41–44"> My heart &#91;is&#93; toward Moab, &#124;&#124; Her fugitives cry to Zoar, a heifer of the third &#91;year&#93;, &#124;&#124; For—the ascent of Luhith—He goes up in it with weeping, &#124;&#124; For in the way of Horonaim, &#124;&#124; They wake up a cry of destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 1:10–12; Isaiah 19:5–7; Numbers 32:36"> For the waters of Nimrim are desolations, &#124;&#124; For the hay has been withered, &#124;&#124; The tender grass has been finished, &#124;&#124; There has not been a green thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:36; Nahum 2:12–13; Isaiah 10:6"> Therefore the abundance he made, and their store, &#124;&#124; They carry to the Brook of the Willows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:10; Isaiah 15:2–5; Jeremiah 48:31–34"> For the cry has gone around the border of Moab, &#124;&#124; Its howling &#91;is&#93; to Eglaim, &#124;&#124; And its howling &#91;is&#93; to Beer-Elim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 17:25; Leviticus 26:28; Leviticus 26:24"> For the waters of Dimon have been full of blood, &#124;&#124; For I set additions on Dimon, &#124;&#124; A lion for the escaped of Moab, &#124;&#124; And for the remnant of Adamah!</span>
