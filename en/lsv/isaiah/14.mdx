<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 102:13; Zechariah 1:17; Zechariah 8:22–23"> For YHWH loves Jacob, &#124;&#124; And has fixed again on Israel, &#124;&#124; And given them rest on their own land, &#124;&#124; And the sojourner has been joined to them, &#124;&#124; And they have been admitted to the house of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 61:5; Isaiah 60:14; Jeremiah 30:16"> And peoples have taken them, &#124;&#124; And have brought them to their place, &#124;&#124; And the house of Israel has inherited them, &#124;&#124; On the land of YHWH, &#124;&#124; For menservants and for maidservants, &#124;&#124; And they have been captors of their captors, &#124;&#124; And have ruled over their exactors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:10; Isaiah 32:18; Ezra 9:8–9"> And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; In the day of YHWH’s giving rest to you, &#124;&#124; From your grief, and from your trouble, &#124;&#124; And from the sharp bondage, &#124;&#124; That has been served on you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Habakkuk 1:2–10; Jeremiah 51:34–35"> That you have taken up this allegory &#124;&#124; Concerning the king of Babylon, and said, “How the exactor has ceased,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 125:3; Isaiah 9:4; Jeremiah 48:15–17"> The golden one has ceased. YHWH has broken the staff of the wicked, &#124;&#124; The scepter of rulers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 7:19–21; James 2:13; Revelation 17:16–17"> He who is striking peoples in wrath, &#124;&#124; A striking without intermission, &#124;&#124; He who is ruling nations in anger, &#124;&#124; Pursuing without restraint!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 126:1–3; Psalms 98:7–9; Psalms 96:11–13"> At rest—all the earth has been quiet, &#124;&#124; They have broken forth &#91;into&#93; singing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:16; Isaiah 55:12–13; Zechariah 11:2"> Even firs have rejoiced over you, &#124;&#124; Cedars of Lebanon, &#91;saying&#93;, Since you have lain down, &#124;&#124; The hewer does not come up against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:21–32; Jeremiah 50:8; Proverbs 15:24"> Sheol beneath has been troubled at you, &#124;&#124; To meet your coming in, &#124;&#124; It is waking up Rephaim for you, &#124;&#124; All chief ones of earth, &#124;&#124; It has raised up from their thrones &#124;&#124; All kings of nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 49:6–14; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Psalms 82:6–7"> All of them answer and say to you, Even you have become weak like us! You have become like to us!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 5:25–30; Job 21:11–15; Isaiah 66:24"> Your excellence has been brought down to Sheol, &#124;&#124; The noise of your stringed instruments, &#124;&#124; The worm has been spread out under you, &#124;&#124; Indeed, the worm is covering you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13–17; Revelation 12:7–10; Luke 10:18"> How you have fallen from the heavens, &#124;&#124; O shining one, son of the dawn! You have been cut down to earth, &#124;&#124; O weakener of nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 8:10–12; Ezekiel 28:2; Ezekiel 28:12–16"> And you said in your heart: I go up into the heavens, &#124;&#124; I raise my throne above the stars of God, &#124;&#124; And I sit on the mountain of meeting in the sides of the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 47:8; Genesis 3:5; 2 Thessalonians 2:4"> I go up above the heights of a thick cloud, I am like to the Most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 11:23; Ezekiel 32:23; Acts 12:22–23"> Only—you are brought down to Sheol, &#124;&#124; To the sides of the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:23; Psalms 58:10–11; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> Your beholders look to you, they attend to you, &#124;&#124; Is this the man causing the earth to tremble, &#124;&#124; Shaking kingdoms?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:3; Isaiah 13:19–22; Isaiah 45:13"> He has made the world as a wilderness, &#124;&#124; And he has broken down his cities, &#124;&#124; He did not open the house of his bound ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:25; Isaiah 22:16; Ezekiel 32:18–32"> All kings of nations—all of them, &#124;&#124; Have lain down in glory, each in his house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:9; Jeremiah 41:7; 1 Kings 21:24"> And you have been cast out of your grave, &#124;&#124; As an abominable branch, raiment of the slain, &#124;&#124; Thrust through ones of the sword, &#124;&#124; Going down to the sons of the pit, &#124;&#124; As a carcass trodden down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 21:10; Job 18:19; Psalms 37:28"> You are not united with them in burial, &#124;&#124; For you have destroyed your land, &#124;&#124; You have slain your people, &#124;&#124; The seed of evildoers is not named for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 23:35; Exodus 20:5; Habakkuk 2:8–12"> Prepare slaughter for his sons; Because of the iniquity of their fathers, &#124;&#124; They do not rise, nor have possessed the land, &#124;&#124; Nor filled the face of the world &#91;with&#93; cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:29–35; Proverbs 10:7; 1 Kings 14:10"> And I have risen up against them,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; “And have cut off, in reference to Babylon, &#124;&#124; Name and remnant, and continuator and successor,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:14; Isaiah 13:21–22; 1 Kings 14:10"> “And have made it for a possession of a hedgehog, &#124;&#124; And ponds of waters, &#124;&#124; And swept it with the broom of destruction,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH of Hosts!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Job 23:13; Isaiah 46:10–11"> YHWH of Hosts has sworn, saying, “As I thought—so has it not been? And as I counseled—it stands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Nahum 1:13; Isaiah 37:36–38"> To break Asshur in My land, &#124;&#124; And I tread him down on My mountain, &#124;&#124; And his yoke has turned from off them, &#124;&#124; Indeed, his burden turns aside from off their shoulder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 23:9; Zephaniah 3:6–8; Exodus 15:12"> This &#91;is&#93; the counsel that is counseled for all the earth, &#124;&#124; And this &#91;is&#93; the hand that is stretched out for all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 43:13; Job 23:13; 2 Chronicles 20:6"> For YHWH of Hosts has purposed, &#124;&#124; And who makes void? And His hand that is stretched out, &#124;&#124; Who turns it back?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 16:20; Isaiah 13:1; 2 Chronicles 28:27"> In the year of the death of King Ahaz was this burden:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 30:6; Isaiah 11:8; 2 Chronicles 26:6"> “Do not rejoice, Philistia, all of you, &#124;&#124; That the rod of your striker has been broken, &#124;&#124; For from the root of a serpent comes out a viper, &#124;&#124; And its fruit &#91;is&#93; a flying, burning serpent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 7:21–22; Zephaniah 2:4–7; Isaiah 5:17"> And the firstborn of the poor have delighted, &#124;&#124; And the needy lie down in confidence, &#124;&#124; And I have put to death your root with famine, &#124;&#124; And it slays your remnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:14; Isaiah 20:1; Isaiah 14:29"> Howl, O gate; cry, O city, &#124;&#124; You are melted, Philistia, all of you, &#124;&#124; For smoke has come from the north, &#124;&#124; And there is none alone in his set places.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 102:16; Psalms 87:5; James 2:5"> And what does one answer the messengers of a nation? That YHWH has founded Zion, &#124;&#124; And the poor of His people take refuge in it!</span>
