<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 25:2; Zechariah 9:1; Amos 1:3–5"> The burden of Damascus. Behold, Damascus is taken away from &#91;being&#93; a city, &#124;&#124; And it has been a heap—a ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:5; Jeremiah 7:33; Numbers 32:34"> The cities of Aroer are forsaken, &#124;&#124; They are for droves, and they have lain down, &#124;&#124; And there is none troubling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 9:11; Isaiah 8:4; Isaiah 7:16"> And the fortress has ceased from Ephraim, &#124;&#124; And the kingdom from Damascus, &#124;&#124; And the remnant of Aram are as the glory of the sons of Israel, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH of Hosts!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16; Deuteronomy 32:15–27; Isaiah 24:13"> And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; The glory of Jacob waxes poor, &#124;&#124; And the fatness of his flesh waxes lean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Joel 3:13; Jeremiah 51:33"> And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; As the gathering of the standing grain by the reaper, &#124;&#124; And his arm reaps the ears, &#124;&#124; And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; As the gathering of the ears in the Valley of Rephaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 24:13; Deuteronomy 4:27; Romans 9:27"> And gleanings have been left in him, &#124;&#124; As the surrounding of an olive, &#124;&#124; Two—three berries on the top of a branch, &#124;&#124; Four—five on the fruitful boughs, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, God of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 7:7; Hosea 6:1; Hosea 3:5"> In that day man looks to His Maker, &#124;&#124; Indeed, his eyes look to the Holy One of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 30:22; Isaiah 27:9; Isaiah 2:8"> And he does not look to the altars. The work of his own hands, &#124;&#124; And that which his own fingers made, He does not see—the Asherim and the images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 3:11–15; Isaiah 27:10; Isaiah 24:1–12"> In that day the cities of his strength are &#124;&#124; As the forsaken thing of the forest, &#124;&#124; And the branch that they have left, &#124;&#124; Because of the sons of Israel, &#124;&#124; It has also been a desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 106:21; Deuteronomy 32:18; Isaiah 26:4"> Because you have forgotten the God of your salvation, &#124;&#124; And have not remembered the Rock of your strength, &#124;&#124; Therefore you plant plants of pleasantness, &#124;&#124; And sow it with a strange shoot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 4:8; Hosea 8:7; Galatians 6:7–8"> You cause your plant to become great in the day, &#124;&#124; And make your seed to flourish in the morning, &#124;&#124; The harvest &#91;is&#93; a heap in a day of overflowing, &#124;&#124; And of mortal pain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:23; Luke 21:25; Psalms 18:4"> Woe &#91;to&#93; the multitude of many peoples, &#124;&#124; They sound as the sounding of seas; And the roaring of nations, &#124;&#124; As the roaring of mighty waters &#91;that&#93; make a crashing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 9:5; Psalms 1:4; Isaiah 41:15–16"> Nations crash as the roaring of many waters, &#124;&#124; And He rebuked them, &#124;&#124; And they fled far off, &#124;&#124; And were pursued as chaff of hills before wind, &#124;&#124; And as a rolling thing before a windstorm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 19:35; Proverbs 22:23; Jeremiah 13:25"> At evening, behold, terror, before morning it is not, &#124;&#124; This &#91;is&#93; the portion of our spoilers, &#124;&#124; And the lot of our plunderers!</span>
