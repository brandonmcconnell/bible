<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 27:8; Judges 11:18; Jeremiah 48:20"> And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; As a wandering bird, cast out of a nest, &#124;&#124; Are daughters of Moab, &#91;at&#93; fords of Arnon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 32:2; 1 Kings 18:4; Isaiah 25:4"> Bring in counsel, do judgment, &#124;&#124; Make your shadow as night in the midst of noon, &#124;&#124; Hide outcasts, do not reveal the wanderer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 14:4; Isaiah 51:13; Isaiah 9:4"> My outcasts sojourn in you, O Moab, &#124;&#124; Be a secret hiding place for them, &#124;&#124; From the face of a destroyer, &#124;&#124; For the extortioner has ceased, &#124;&#124; Devastation has been finished, &#124;&#124; The tramplers are consumed out of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 7:14; Micah 4:7; Isaiah 9:6–7"> And the throne is established in kindness, &#124;&#124; And &#91;One&#93; has sat on it in truth, in the tent of David, &#124;&#124; Judging and seeking judgment, and hastening righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 2:1; Obadiah 1:3–4; Jeremiah 48:29–30"> We have heard of the pride of Moab—very proud, &#124;&#124; His pride, and his arrogance, and his wrath—his boastings &#91;are&#93; not right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 3:25; 1 Chronicles 16:3; Jeremiah 48:20"> Therefore Moab howls for Moab, all of it howls, &#124;&#124; It meditates for the grape-cakes of Kir-Hareseth, &#124;&#124; They are surely struck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 15:4; Numbers 32:38; Jeremiah 48:32"> Because fields of Heshbon languish, &#124;&#124; The vine of Sibmah, &#124;&#124; Lords of nations beat her choice vines, &#124;&#124; They have come to Jazer, &#124;&#124; They have wandered in a wilderness, &#124;&#124; Her plants have spread themselves, &#124;&#124; They have passed over a sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:12; Isaiah 15:4–5; Jeremiah 40:10"> Therefore I weep with the weeping of Jazer, &#124;&#124; The vine of Sibmah, &#124;&#124; I water you &#91;with&#93; my tear, &#124;&#124; O Heshbon and Elealeh, &#124;&#124; For—for your summer fruits, and for your harvest, &#124;&#124; The shouting has fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:33; Judges 9:27; Amos 5:17"> And gladness and joy have been removed from the fruitful field, &#124;&#124; And they do not sing in vineyards, nor shout, &#124;&#124; The treader does not tread wine in the presses, &#124;&#124; I have caused shouting to cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 15:5; Jeremiah 48:36; Hosea 11:8"> Therefore my bowels sound as a harp for Moab, &#124;&#124; And my inward parts for Kir-Haresh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 15:2; 1 Kings 18:29; Jeremiah 48:35"> And it has come to pass, when it has been seen, &#124;&#124; That Moab has been weary on the high place, &#124;&#124; And he has come to his sanctuary to pray, &#124;&#124; And is not able.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 44:8"> This &#91;is&#93; the word that YHWH has spoken to Moab from that time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 21:16; Jeremiah 48:46–47; Isaiah 23:9"> And now YHWH has spoken, saying, “In three years, as years of a hired worker, &#124;&#124; The glory of Moab is lightly esteemed, &#124;&#124; With all the great multitude, &#124;&#124; And the remnant &#91;is&#93; little, small, not mighty!”</span>
