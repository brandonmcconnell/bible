<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; Jeremiah 33:15; Hebrews 1:8–9"> Behold, a king reigns for righteousness, &#124;&#124; As for princes, they rule for judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; Isaiah 44:3; Isaiah 9:6"> And each has been as a hiding place &#91;from&#93; wind, &#124;&#124; And as a secret hiding place &#91;from&#93; inundation, &#124;&#124; As streams of waters in a dry place, &#124;&#124; As a shadow of a heavy rock in a weary land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 29:18; Acts 26:18; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> And the eyes of beholders are not dazzled, &#124;&#124; And the ears of hearers attend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 29:24; Luke 21:14–15; Matthew 16:17"> And the heart of those hurried understands to know, &#124;&#124; And the tongue of stammerers hurries to speak clearly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:20; Psalms 15:4; Malachi 3:18"> A fool is no more called “noble,” &#124;&#124; And to a miser it is not said, “rich”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; Matthew 23:13; Isaiah 3:15"> For a fool speaks folly, &#124;&#124; And his heart does iniquity, to do profanity, &#124;&#124; And to speak error concerning YHWH, &#124;&#124; To empty the soul of the hungry, &#124;&#124; Indeed, he causes the thirsty to lack &#91;their&#93; drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26–28; Matthew 26:59–60; Matthew 26:14–16"> And the miser—his instruments &#91;are&#93; evil, &#124;&#124; He has counseled wicked schemes, &#124;&#124; To corrupt the poor with lying sayings, &#124;&#124; Even when the needy speaks justly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:2; Acts 11:29–30; Luke 6:33–35"> And the noble counseled noble things, &#124;&#124; And he rises up for noble things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 13:9; Isaiah 3:16; Isaiah 28:23"> Women, easy ones, rise, hear my voice, &#124;&#124; Daughters, confident ones, give ear &#91;to&#93; my saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 7:23; Jeremiah 8:13; Zephaniah 1:13"> In days and a year &#124;&#124; You are troubled, O confident ones, &#124;&#124; For harvest has been consumed, &#124;&#124; The gathering does not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 3:24; Isaiah 33:14; Isaiah 20:4"> Tremble, you women, you easy ones, &#124;&#124; Be troubled, you confident ones, &#124;&#124; Strip and make bare, with a girdle on the loins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:15; Ezekiel 20:6; Nahum 2:7"> They are lamenting for breasts, &#124;&#124; For fields of desire, for the fruitful vine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 22:2; Hosea 9:6; Isaiah 7:23"> Over the ground of my people thorn &#91;and&#93; brier go up, &#124;&#124; Surely over all houses of joy of the exulting city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 24:12; Isaiah 24:10; Isaiah 25:2"> The palace has been left, &#124;&#124; The multitude of the city forsaken, &#124;&#124; Fort and watchtower have been for dens for all time, &#124;&#124; A joy of wild donkeys—a pasture of herds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 29:17; Ezekiel 39:29; Isaiah 44:3"> Until the Spirit is emptied out on us from on high, &#124;&#124; And a wilderness has become a fruitful field, &#124;&#124; And the fruitful field is reckoned for a forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 94:14–15; 1 Peter 2:9–12; Isaiah 60:21"> And judgment has dwelt in the wilderness, &#124;&#124; And righteousness remains in the fruitful field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 26:3; Psalms 119:165; Philippians 4:6–9"> And a work of the righteousness has been peace, &#124;&#124; And a service of the righteousness—Keeping quiet and confidence for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 2:5; Hosea 2:18–23; Jeremiah 33:16"> And My people have dwelt in a peaceful habitation, &#124;&#124; And in steadfast dwelling places, &#124;&#124; And in quiet resting places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:17; Isaiah 30:30; Isaiah 28:2"> And it has hailed in the going down of the forest, &#124;&#124; And the city is low in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:1; Isaiah 30:23–24; Isaiah 55:10–11"> Blessed &#91;are&#93; you sowing by all waters, &#124;&#124; Sending forth the foot of the ox and the donkey!</span>
