<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 2:10; Jeremiah 8:3; Micah 3:1"> Hear this word that YHWH has spoken concerning you, O sons of Israel, concerning all the family that I brought up from the land of Egypt, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Exodus 19:5–6; Deuteronomy 10:15"> “Only you have I known of all families of the earth, &#124;&#124; Therefore I charge on you all your iniquities.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–16; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 5:22"> Do two walk together if they have not met?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 11:10; Psalms 104:21; Amos 1:2"> Does a lion roar in a forest and he has no prey? Does a young lion give out his voice from his habitation, &#124;&#124; If he has not caught?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:12; Jeremiah 31:28; Daniel 9:14"> Does a bird fall into a snare of the earth, &#124;&#124; And there is no trap for it? Does a snare go up from the ground, &#124;&#124; And it does not capture prey?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Zephaniah 1:16; Isaiah 14:24–27"> Is a horn blown in a city, &#124;&#124; And do people not tremble? Is there affliction in a city, &#124;&#124; And YHWH has not done &#91;it&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:15; Genesis 18:17; Psalms 25:14"> For Lord YHWH does nothing, &#124;&#124; Except He has revealed His counsel to His servants the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 4:20; Jeremiah 20:9; Amos 3:4"> A lion has roared—who does not fear? Lord YHWH has spoken—who does not prophesy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 4:1; Amos 6:1; Amos 1:8"> “Sound to palaces in Ashdod, &#124;&#124; And to palaces in the land of Egypt, and say: Be gathered on mountains of Samaria, &#124;&#124; And see many troubles within her, &#124;&#124; And oppressed ones in her midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Zephaniah 1:9; Zechariah 5:3–4"> And they have not known to act straightforwardly,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “Who are treasuring up violence and spoil in their palaces.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 18:9–11; 2 Kings 17:3–6; 2 Kings 15:19"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: “An adversary—and surrounding the land, &#124;&#124; And he has brought down your strength from you, &#124;&#124; And your palaces have been spoiled.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:34–37; Romans 11:4–5; Isaiah 31:4"> Thus said YHWH: “As the shepherd delivers from the lion’s mouth &#124;&#124; Two legs, or a piece of an ear, &#124;&#124; So the sons of Israel are delivered, &#124;&#124; Who are sitting in Samaria on the corner of a bed, &#124;&#124; And in Damascus &#91;on that of&#93; a couch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:18–19; 2 Chronicles 24:19; Acts 20:21"> Hear and testify to the house of Jacob,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH, God of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 23:15; Hosea 10:5–8; Hosea 10:14–15"> “For in the day of My charging the transgressions of Israel on him, I have laid a charge on the altars of Beth-El, &#124;&#124; And the horns of the altar have been cut off, &#124;&#124; And they have fallen to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 22:39; Jeremiah 36:22; Amos 6:11"> And I have struck the winter-house with the summer-house, &#124;&#124; And houses of ivory have perished, &#124;&#124; And many houses have been consumed,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH!</span>
