<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 7:1; Amos 7:7; Amos 7:4"> Thus has Lord YHWH showed me, and behold, a basket of summer-fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 7:8; Lamentations 4:18; Jeremiah 24:1–3"> And He says, “What are you seeing, Amos?” And I say, “A basket of summer-fruit.” And YHWH says to me: “The end has come to My people Israel, &#124;&#124; I no longer pass over to it anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 5:23; Amos 6:9–10; Jeremiah 9:21–22"> And female singers have howled of a palace in that day,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; “Many &#91;are&#93; the carcasses, into any place throw—hush!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:14; Psalms 14:4; Amos 5:11"> Hear this, you who are swallowing up the needy, &#124;&#124; To cause the poor of the land to cease,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15–21; Micah 6:10–11; Hosea 12:7"> Saying, “When does the new moon pass, &#124;&#124; And we sell ground grain? And the Sabbath, and we open out pure grain? To make little the ephah, &#124;&#124; And to make great the shekel, &#124;&#124; And to use balances of deceit perversely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 2:6; Leviticus 25:39–42; Nehemiah 5:8"> To purchase the poor with money, &#124;&#124; And the needy for a pair of sandals, &#124;&#124; Indeed, the refuse of the pure grain we sell.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Hosea 9:9; Hosea 8:13"> YHWH has sworn by the excellence of Jacob: “I do not forget any of their works forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 9:5; Hosea 4:3; Jeremiah 46:8"> Does the land not tremble for this, &#124;&#124; And every dweller in it has mourned? And all of it has come up as a flood. And it has been cast out, and has sunk, &#124;&#124; Like the flood of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 15:33; Matthew 27:45; Jeremiah 15:9"> And it has come to pass in that day,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; “I have caused the sun to go in at noon, &#124;&#124; And caused darkness on the land in a day of light,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Zechariah 12:10; Job 20:23"> And have turned your festivals to mourning, &#124;&#124; And all your songs to lamentation, &#124;&#124; And caused sackcloth to come up on all loins, &#124;&#124; And on every head—baldness, &#124;&#124; And made it as a mourning of an only one, &#124;&#124; And its latter end as a day of bitterness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 74:9; 1 Samuel 3:1; 2 Chronicles 15:3"> Behold, days are coming,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And I have sent a famine into the land, &#124;&#124; Not a famine of bread, nor a thirst of water, &#124;&#124; But of hearing the words of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:7–10; Matthew 24:23–26; Daniel 12:4"> And they have wandered from sea to sea, &#124;&#124; And from north even to east, &#124;&#124; They go to and fro to seek the word of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And they do not find &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Lamentations 1:18; Lamentations 2:21"> In that day the beautiful virgins, &#124;&#124; And the young men, faint with thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:5; 1 Kings 12:28–29; Deuteronomy 9:21"> Those swearing by the guilt of Samaria, &#124;&#124; And who have said, Your god lives, O Dan, &#124;&#124; And, The way of Beer-Sheba lives, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they have fallen and do not rise again!”</span>
