<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:13; Isaiah 37:4; Jeremiah 42:2"> and it has come to pass, when it has finished to consume the herb of the land, that I say, “Lord YHWH, please forgive, &#124;&#124; How does Jacob arise—for he &#91;is&#93; small?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; Jonah 3:10; Hosea 11:8"> YHWH has relented of this, “It will not be,” said YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 7:1; Isaiah 66:15–16; Hebrews 1:7"> Thus has Lord YHWH showed me, and behold, Lord YHWH is calling to contend by the fire, and it consumes the great deep, indeed, it has consumed the portion, and I say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 85:4; Isaiah 1:9; Jeremiah 30:19"> “Lord YHWH, please cease, &#124;&#124; How does Jacob arise—for he &#91;is&#93; small?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 7:3; Judges 2:18; Psalms 90:13"> YHWH has relented of this, “It also will not be,” said Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Revelation 21:15; Isaiah 28:17"> Thus has He showed me, and behold, the Lord is standing by a wall &#91;made according to&#93; a plumb-line, and in His hand a plumb-line;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 8:2; Lamentations 2:8; Ezekiel 7:2–9"> and YHWH says to me, “What are you seeing, Amos?” And I say, “A plumb-line”; and the Lord says: “Behold, I am setting a plumb-line in the midst of My people Israel, I no longer pass over to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 15:8–10; Genesis 46:1; Amos 8:14"> And high places of Isaac have been desolated, &#124;&#124; And sanctuaries of Israel are dried up, &#124;&#124; And I have risen against the house of Jeroboam with a sword.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:4; 1 Kings 12:31–32; Jeremiah 26:8–11"> And Amaziah priest of Beth-El sends to Jeroboam king of Israel, saying, “Amos has conspired against you in the midst of the house of Israel; the land is not able to bear all his words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 17:6; Psalms 56:5; Amos 6:7–8"> for thus said Amos: Jeroboam dies by sword, &#124;&#124; And Israel certainly removes from off its land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 8:34; 1 Samuel 9:9"> And Amaziah says to Amos, “Seer, go, flee to the land of Judah for yourself, and eat bread there, and there you prophesy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 12:32; Amos 2:12; 1 Kings 12:29"> and no longer prophesy &#91;at&#93; Beth-El anymore, for it &#91;is&#93; the king’s sanctuary, and it &#91;is&#93; the royal house.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 1:1; 2 Kings 4:38; 2 Kings 2:5"> And Amos answers and says to Amaziah, “I &#91;am&#93; no prophet, nor &#91;am&#93; I a prophet’s son, but I &#91;am&#93; a herdsman, and a cultivator of sycamores,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:8; Ezekiel 2:3–4; Jeremiah 1:7"> and YHWH takes me from after the flock, and YHWH says to me, Go, prophesy to My people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46; Micah 2:6; Ezekiel 21:2"> And now, hear a word of YHWH: You are saying, Do not prophesy against Israel, nor drop &#91;anything&#93; against the house of Isaac,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 9:3; 2 Kings 17:6; Hosea 4:13–14"> therefore, thus said YHWH: Your wife goes whoring in the city, &#124;&#124; And your sons and your daughters fall by sword, &#124;&#124; And your land is apportioned by line, &#124;&#124; And you die on an unclean land, &#124;&#124; And Israel certainly removes from off its land.”</span>
