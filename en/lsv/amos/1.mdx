<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zechariah 14:5; 2 Chronicles 26:1–23; 1 Kings 19:19"> Words of Amos—who has been among herdsmen of Tekoa—that he has seen concerning Israel, in the days of Uzziah king of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash king of Israel, two years before the shaking;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 3:16; Jeremiah 25:30; Amos 9:3"> and he says, “YHWH roars from Zion, &#124;&#124; And gives forth His voice from Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And pastures of the shepherds have mourned, &#124;&#124; And the top of Carmel has withered!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; Amos 1:9; Isaiah 8:4"> And thus said YHWH: “For three transgressions of Damascus, &#124;&#124; And for four, I do not reverse it, &#124;&#124; Because of their threshing Gilead with sharp-pointed irons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:27; 2 Kings 6:24; 2 Kings 13:25"> And I have sent a fire against the house of Hazael, &#124;&#124; And it has consumed the palaces of Ben-Hadad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 16:9; Amos 9:7; Jeremiah 51:30"> And I have broken the bar of Damascus, &#124;&#124; And cut off the inhabitant from Bikat-Aven, &#124;&#124; And a holder of a scepter from Beth-Eden, &#124;&#124; And the people of Aram have been removed to Kir,” said YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 1:11; 2 Chronicles 28:18; Obadiah 1:11"> Thus said YHWH: “For three transgressions of Gaza, &#124;&#124; And for four, I do not reverse it, &#124;&#124; Because of their removing a complete captivity, &#124;&#124; To deliver up to Edom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 18:8; Jeremiah 47:1; Zephaniah 2:4"> And I have sent a fire against the wall of Gaza, &#124;&#124; And it has consumed her palaces;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:16; Isaiah 14:29–31; Jeremiah 47:4–5"> And I have cut off the inhabitant from Ashdod, &#124;&#124; And a holder of a scepter from Ashkelon, &#124;&#124; And have turned back My hand against Ekron, &#124;&#124; And the remnant of the Philistines have perished,” said Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 9:11–14; Isaiah 23:1–18; Joel 3:4–8"> Thus said YHWH: “For three transgressions of Tyre, &#124;&#124; And for four, I do not reverse it, &#124;&#124; Because of their delivering up a complete captivity to Edom, &#124;&#124; And they did not remember the brotherly covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 9:4; Amos 1:7; Amos 1:4"> And I have sent a fire against the wall of Tyre, &#124;&#124; And it has consumed her palaces.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:12–14; 2 Chronicles 28:17; Malachi 1:4"> Thus said YHWH: “For three transgressions of Edom, &#124;&#124; And for four, I do not reverse it, &#124;&#124; Because of his pursuing his brother with a sword, &#124;&#124; And he has destroyed his mercies, &#124;&#124; And his anger tears continuously, &#124;&#124; And his wrath—he has kept it forever,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:7; Obadiah 1:9–10; Jeremiah 49:20"> And I have sent a fire against Teman, &#124;&#124; And it has consumed palaces of Bozrah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; Ezekiel 25:2–7; Jeremiah 49:1–6"> Thus said YHWH: “For three transgressions of the sons of Ammon, &#124;&#124; And for four, I do not reverse it, &#124;&#124; Because of their ripping up the pregnant ones of Gilead, &#124;&#124; To enlarge their border,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:2; Amos 2:2; Deuteronomy 3:11"> And I have kindled a fire against the wall of Rabbah, &#124;&#124; And it has consumed her palaces, &#124;&#124; With a shout in a day of battle, &#124;&#124; With a whirlwind in a day of windstorm,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:3"> And their king has gone in a removal, &#124;&#124; He and his heads together,” said YHWH!</span>
