<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 68:21; Revelation 1:17; Isaiah 6:1"> I have seen the Lord standing by the altar, and He says, &#124;&#124; “Strike the knob, and the thresholds shake, &#124;&#124; And cut them off by the head—all of them, &#124;&#124; And I slay their posterity with a sword, &#124;&#124; None that flee of them flee away, &#124;&#124; Nor a fugitive of them escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:53; Psalms 139:7–10; Obadiah 1:4"> If they dig through into Sheol, &#124;&#124; From there My hand takes them, &#124;&#124; And if they go up &#91;to&#93; the heavens, &#124;&#124; From there I cause them to come down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:16; Jeremiah 23:23–24; Isaiah 27:1"> And if they are hid in the top of Carmel, &#124;&#124; From there I search out, and have taken them, &#124;&#124; And if they are hid from My eyes in the bottom of the sea, &#124;&#124; From there I command the serpent, &#124;&#124; And it has bitten them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:33; Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 44:11"> And if they go into captivity before their enemies, &#124;&#124; From there I command the sword, &#124;&#124; And it has slain them, &#124;&#124; And I have set My eye on them for evil, &#124;&#124; And not for good.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 8:8; Psalms 46:6; Isaiah 64:1"> And &#91;it is&#93; the Lord, YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; Who is striking against the land, and it melts, &#124;&#124; And mourned have all the inhabitants in it, &#124;&#124; And all of it has come up as a flood, &#124;&#124; And it has sunk—like the flood of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Amos 4:13; Psalms 104:3"> Who is building His upper chambers in the heavens; As for His troop, &#124;&#124; On earth He has founded it, &#124;&#124; Who is calling for the waters of the sea, &#124;&#124; And pours them out on the face of the land, &#124;&#124; YHWH &#91;is&#93; His Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:4; Deuteronomy 2:23; 2 Kings 16:9"> “Are you not as sons of Cushim to Me, O sons of Israel?” A declaration of YHWH. “Did I not bring Israel up out of the land of Egypt? And the Philistines from Caphtor, and Aram from Kir?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:11; Amos 9:4; Jeremiah 44:27"> Behold, &#91;the&#93; eyes of Lord YHWH &#91;are&#93; on the sinful kingdom, &#124;&#124; And I have destroyed it from off the face of the ground, &#124;&#124; Only, I do not utterly destroy the house of Jacob,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:31; Deuteronomy 28:64; Isaiah 30:28"> “For behold, I am commanding, &#124;&#124; And I have shaken among all the nations the house of Israel, &#124;&#124; As &#91;one&#93; shakes with a sieve, &#124;&#124; And there does not fall a grain &#91;to&#93; the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 6:3; Isaiah 33:14; Amos 6:1"> By sword all sinners of My people die, &#124;&#124; Who are saying, &#124;&#124; Evil does not overtake, or go before, &#124;&#124; For our sakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 15:15–17; Jeremiah 33:14–16; Isaiah 16:5"> In that day I raise the dwelling place of David that is fallen, &#124;&#124; And I have repaired their breaches, &#124;&#124; And I raise up its ruins, &#124;&#124; And I have built it up as in days of old,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:7; Isaiah 65:1; Acts 15:17"> So that they possess the remnant of Edom, &#124;&#124; And all the nations on whom My Name is called,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH—the doer of this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joel 3:18; John 4:35; Leviticus 26:5"> “Behold, days are coming,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And the plowman has come near to the reaper, &#124;&#124; And the treader of grapes to the scatterer of seed, &#124;&#124; And the mountains have dropped juice, &#124;&#124; And all the hills melt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:4; Jeremiah 30:3; Jeremiah 30:18"> And I have turned back &#91;to&#93; the captivity of My people Israel, &#124;&#124; And they have built desolate cities, and inhabited, &#124;&#124; And have planted vineyards, and drunk their wine, &#124;&#124; And made gardens, and eaten their fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Ezekiel 37:25; Isaiah 60:21"> And I have planted them on their own ground, &#124;&#124; And they are not plucked up anymore from off their own ground, &#124;&#124; That I have given to them,” said your God YHWH!</span>
