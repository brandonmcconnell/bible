<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:14; Amos 4:2; Amos 8:7"> Lord YHWH has sworn by Himself, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, God of Hosts: “I am abominating the excellence of Jacob, &#124;&#124; And his high places I have hated, &#124;&#124; And I have delivered up the city and its fullness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:3; Job 1:2; Psalms 109:13"> And if there are left ten persons in one house, &#124;&#124; It has come to pass—that they have died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 8:3; 1 Samuel 31:12; Amos 5:13"> And his loved one has lifted him up, even his burner, &#124;&#124; To bring forth the bones from the house, &#124;&#124; And he said to him who &#91;is&#93; in the sides of the house, &#124;&#124; “Is there &#91;any&#93; yet with you?” And he said, “None,” &#124;&#124; Then he said, “Hush! We must not make mention of the Name of YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 3:15; Isaiah 55:11; 2 Kings 25:9"> For behold, YHWH is commanding, &#124;&#124; And He has struck the great house &#91;with&#93; breaches, &#124;&#124; And the little house &#91;with&#93; clefts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 5:7; Hosea 10:4; Amos 5:11–12"> Do horses run on a rock? Does one plow &#91;it&#93; with oxen? For you have turned judgment to gall, &#124;&#124; And the fruit of righteousness to wormwood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 28:14–15; Luke 12:19–20; 2 Kings 14:25"> O you who are rejoicing at nothing, &#124;&#124; Who are saying, “Have we not taken to ourselves horns by our strength?”</span>
