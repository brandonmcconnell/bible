<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:24; Jeremiah 48:41; Amos 1:14"> And I have sent a fire against Moab, &#124;&#124; And it has consumed the palaces of Kerioth, &#124;&#124; And Moab is dying with noise, &#124;&#124; With shouting, with voice of a horn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; Jeremiah 48:7; Jeremiah 48:25"> And I have cut off judge from her midst, &#124;&#124; And I slay all its heads with him,” said YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:24; Ezekiel 20:16; Ezekiel 20:13"> Thus said YHWH: “For three transgressions of Judah, &#124;&#124; And for four, I do not reverse it, &#124;&#124; Because of their loathing the Law of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And His statutes they have not kept, &#124;&#124; And their lies cause them to err, &#124;&#124; After which their fathers walked,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 8:14; Jeremiah 17:27; Jeremiah 21:10"> And I have sent a fire against Judah, &#124;&#124; And it has consumed palaces of Jerusalem.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 3:3; Micah 3:2–3; Amos 5:11–12"> Thus said YHWH: “For three transgressions of Israel, &#124;&#124; And for four, I do not reverse it, &#124;&#124; Because of their selling the righteous for silver, &#124;&#124; And the needy for a pair of sandals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:12; Isaiah 10:2; Ezekiel 22:11"> Who are panting for the dust of the earth on the head of the poor, &#124;&#124; And the way of the humble they turn aside, &#124;&#124; And a man and his father go to the girl, &#124;&#124; So as to defile My holy Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:12–17; Exodus 22:26–27; Amos 6:6"> And they stretch themselves on pledged garments near every altar, &#124;&#124; And the wine of fined ones they drink &#91;in&#93; the house of their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 18:16; Malachi 4:1; Deuteronomy 3:11"> And I have destroyed the Amorite from before them, &#124;&#124; Whose height &#91;is&#93; as the height of cedars, &#124;&#124; And he &#91;is&#93; strong as the oaks, &#124;&#124; And I destroy his fruit from above, &#124;&#124; And his roots from beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:7; Exodus 12:51; Amos 3:1"> And I have brought you up from the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And cause you to go in a wilderness forty years, &#124;&#124; To possess the land of the Amorite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:25; Numbers 6:2–3; 1 Kings 20:41"> And I raise of your sons for prophets, &#124;&#124; And of your choice ones for Nazarites, &#124;&#124; Is this not true, O sons of Israel?” A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 7:13; Isaiah 30:10; Jeremiah 11:21"> “And you cause the Nazarites to drink wine, &#124;&#124; And on the prophets you have laid a charge, &#124;&#124; Saying, Do not prophesy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:14; Isaiah 43:24; Ezekiel 16:43"> Behold, I am pressing you under, &#124;&#124; As the full cart presses a sheaf for itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; Psalms 33:16; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> And refuge has perished from the swift, &#124;&#124; And the strong does not strengthens his power, &#124;&#124; And the mighty does not deliver his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 33:16–17; Ezekiel 39:3"> And the handler of the bow does not stand, &#124;&#124; And the swift does not deliver &#91;himself&#93; with his feet, &#124;&#124; And the rider of the horse does not deliver his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:52; Jeremiah 48:41; 2 Kings 7:8–20"> And the courageous of heart among the mighty, &#124;&#124; Flees naked in that day,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH!</span>
