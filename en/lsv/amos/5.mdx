<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1; Jeremiah 9:10; Jeremiah 7:29"> Hear this word that I am bearing to you, &#124;&#124; A lamentation, O house of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:17; Lamentations 2:13; Jeremiah 2:27"> “Fallen, not to rise again, has the virgin of Israel, &#124;&#124; Left on her land—she has none &#91;to&#93; raise &#91;her&#93; up.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22; Ezekiel 12:16; Isaiah 6:13"> For thus said Lord YHWH: “The city that is going out one thousand, &#124;&#124; Leaves one hundred, &#124;&#124; And that which is going out one hundred, &#124;&#124; Leaves ten to the house of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Zephaniah 2:3; Jeremiah 29:12–13"> For thus said YHWH to the house of Israel: “Seek Me, and live,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Amos 4:4; Amos 8:14"> And do not seek Beth-El, and do not enter Gilgal, &#124;&#124; And do not pass through Beer-Sheba, &#124;&#124; For Gilgal utterly removes, &#124;&#124; And Beth-El becomes vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:11; Isaiah 55:6; Ezekiel 20:47–48"> Seek YHWH, and live, &#124;&#124; Lest He prosper as fire &#91;against&#93; the house of Joseph, &#124;&#124; And it has consumed, &#124;&#124; And there is no quencher for Beth-El—</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 6:12; Amos 5:11–12; Zephaniah 1:6"> You who are turning judgment to wormwood, &#124;&#124; And have put down righteousness to the earth!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 4:13; Job 9:9; Amos 9:6"> The Maker of the Pleiades and Orion, &#124;&#124; And He who is turning death-shade to the morning—And He has made day &#91;as&#93; dark &#91;as&#93; night—Who is calling to the waters of the sea, &#124;&#124; And pours them on the face of the earth, &#124;&#124; YHWH &#91;is&#93; His Name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 13:17; Jeremiah 37:10; Micah 5:11"> Who is brightening up the spoiled against the strong, &#124;&#124; And the spoiled comes against a fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 29:21; 1 Kings 22:8; John 8:45–47"> They have hated a reprover in the gate, &#124;&#124; And they detest a plain speaker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:30; Micah 6:15; Zephaniah 1:13"> Therefore, because of your trampling on the poor, &#124;&#124; And the tribute of grain you take from him, &#124;&#124; Houses of hewn work you have built, &#124;&#124; And you do not dwell in them, &#124;&#124; Desirable vineyards you have planted, &#124;&#124; And you do not drink their wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 2:6–7; Amos 5:10; Isaiah 1:23"> For I have known—many &#91;are&#93; your transgressions, &#124;&#124; And mighty your sins, &#124;&#124; Adversaries of the righteous, taking ransoms, &#124;&#124; And you turned aside the needy in the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:4; 2 Timothy 3:1; Ecclesiastes 3:7"> Therefore the wise is silent at that time, &#124;&#124; For it &#91;is&#93; an evil time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Matthew 6:33; Micah 6:8"> Seek good, and not evil, that you may live, and it is so; YHWH, God of Hosts, &#91;is&#93; with you, as you said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:9; 3 John 1:11; Psalms 97:10"> Hate evil, and love good, &#124;&#124; And set up judgment in the gate, &#124;&#124; It may be YHWH, God of Hosts, pities the remnant of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 1:11; Jeremiah 9:17–20; Jeremiah 9:10"> Therefore, thus said YHWH, God of Hosts, the Lord: “Lamentation is in all broad places, &#124;&#124; And in all out-places they say, Oh! Oh! And called the farmer to mourning, &#124;&#124; And the skillful of wailing to lamentation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:12; Jeremiah 48:33; Isaiah 16:10"> And in all vineyards &#91;is&#93; lamentation, &#124;&#124; For I pass into your midst,” said YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joel 1:15; Joel 2:1–2; Joel 2:31"> Behold, you who are desiring the Day of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Why &#91;is&#93; this to you—the Day of YHWH? It is darkness, and not light,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 24:17–18; Jeremiah 15:2–3; Acts 28:4"> As &#91;when&#93; one flees from the face of the lion, &#124;&#124; And the bear has met him, &#124;&#124; And he has come into the house, &#124;&#124; And has leaned his hand on the wall, &#124;&#124; And the serpent has bitten him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Ezekiel 34:12; Zephaniah 1:15"> Is not the Day of YHWH darkness and not light, &#124;&#124; Even thick darkness that has no brightness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–16; Proverbs 28:9; Leviticus 26:31"> “I have hated—I have loathed your festivals, &#124;&#124; And I am not refreshed by your restraints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; Amos 4:4–5; Isaiah 66:3"> For though you cause burnt-offerings and your presents to ascend to Me, &#124;&#124; I am not pleased, &#124;&#124; And I do not behold the peace-offering of your fatlings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Amos 6:5; Amos 8:10; Amos 8:3"> Turn the noise of your songs aside from Me, &#124;&#124; Indeed, I do not hear the praise of your stringed instruments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Jeremiah 22:3; Hosea 6:6"> And judgment rolls on as waters, &#124;&#124; And righteousness as a perennial stream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 7:42–43; Ezekiel 20:8; Nehemiah 9:18"> Did you bring sacrifices and offerings near to Me, &#124;&#124; In a wilderness forty years, O house of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 23:12–13; 1 Kings 11:33; Leviticus 20:2–5"> And you bore Succoth your king, and Chiun your images, &#124;&#124; The star of your god, that you made for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 7:43; 2 Kings 17:6; Amos 4:13"> And I removed you beyond Damascus,” said YHWH—God of Hosts &#91;is&#93; His Name.</span>
