<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 22:12; Amos 6:1; Amos 5:11"> Hear this word, you cows of Bashan, &#124;&#124; Who &#91;are&#93; on the mountain of Samaria, &#124;&#124; Who are oppressing the poor, &#124;&#124; Who are bruising the needy, &#124;&#124; Who are saying to their lords: “Bring in, and we drink.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:16; Psalms 89:35; Habakkuk 1:15–16"> Lord YHWH has sworn by His holiness, &#124;&#124; “Behold, days are coming on you, &#124;&#124; And He has taken you away with hooks, &#124;&#124; And your posterity with fish-hooks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:5; Ezekiel 12:12; Matthew 16:26"> And &#91;by&#93; breaches you go forth, &#124;&#124; A woman before her, &#124;&#124; And you have cast down the high place,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:39; Hosea 4:15; Numbers 28:3–4"> “Enter Beth-El, and transgress, &#124;&#124; At Gilgal multiply transgression, &#124;&#124; And bring in your sacrifices every morning, &#124;&#124; Your tithes every third year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 22:18–21; Psalms 81:12; Hosea 9:10"> And burn a thank-offering with leaven as incense, &#124;&#124; And proclaim willing gifts, sound! For so you have loved, O sons of Israel,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 2:17; Jeremiah 5:3; Isaiah 9:13"> “And I also—I have given to you cleanness of teeth in all your cities, &#124;&#124; And lack of bread in all your places, &#124;&#124; And you have not turned back to Me,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 9:26; Jeremiah 3:3; 2 Chronicles 7:13–14"> “And I also—I have withheld from you the rain, &#124;&#124; While yet three months to harvest, &#124;&#124; And I have sent rain on one city, &#124;&#124; And on another city I do not send rain, &#124;&#124; One portion is rained on, &#124;&#124; And the portion on which it does not rain withers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 4:6; 1 Kings 18:5; Haggai 1:6"> And two or three cities have wandered, &#124;&#124; To the same city to drink water, &#124;&#124; And they are not satisfied, &#124;&#124; And you have not turned back to Me,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:22; Haggai 2:17; Joel 1:4"> “I have struck you with blasting and with mildew, &#124;&#124; The abundance of your gardens and of your vineyards, &#124;&#124; And of your figs, and of your olives, &#124;&#124; The palmer-worm eats, &#124;&#124; And you have not turned back to Me,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:60; Leviticus 26:25; Joel 2:20"> “I have sent among you pestilence by the way of Egypt, &#124;&#124; I have slain your choice ones by sword, &#124;&#124; With your captive horses, &#124;&#124; And I cause the stink of your camps to come up—Even into your nostrils, &#124;&#124; And you have not turned back to Me,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 13:19; Zechariah 3:2; Genesis 19:24–25"> “I have overturned among you, &#124;&#124; Like the overturn by God of Sodom and Gomorrah, &#124;&#124; And you are as a brand delivered from a burning, &#124;&#124; And you have not turned back to Me,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–4; James 4:1–10; Amos 9:1–4"> “Therefore, thus I do to you, O Israel, at last, &#124;&#124; Because this I do to you, &#124;&#124; Prepare to meet your God, O Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Micah 1:3; Amos 9:6"> For behold, the Former of mountains, and Creator of wind, &#124;&#124; And the Declarer to man what &#91;is&#93; His thought, &#124;&#124; He is making dawn obscurity, &#124;&#124; And is treading on high places of earth, &#124;&#124; YHWH, God of Hosts, &#91;is&#93; His Name!</span>
