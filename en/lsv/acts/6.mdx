<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:9–14; Exodus 18:17–26; 2 Timothy 2:4"> and the Twelve, having called near the multitude of the disciples, said, “It is not pleasing that we, having left the word of God, minister at tables;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7–15; James 3:17–18; Deuteronomy 1:13"> look out, therefore, brothers, seven men of you who are testified well of, full of the Holy Spirit and wisdom, whom we may set over this necessity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 1:14; 1 Timothy 4:13–16; Philippians 1:9–11"> and we to prayer, and to the ministry of the word, will give ourselves continually.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 11:24; Acts 21:8; Acts 8:5–40"> And the thing was pleasing before all the multitude, and they chose Stephen, a man full of faith and the Holy Spirit, and Philip, and Prochorus, and Nicanor, and Timon, and Parmenas, and Nicolaus, a proselyte of Antioch,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; Numbers 8:10; Acts 1:24"> whom they set before the apostles, and they, having prayed, laid &#91;their&#93; hands on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:20; Acts 12:24; Colossians 1:6"> And the word of God increased, and the number of the disciples multiplied in Jerusalem exceedingly; a great multitude of the priests were also obedient to the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 4:29–30; Acts 7:55; Acts 6:5"> And Stephen, full of faith and power, was doing great wonders and signs among the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 15:23; Acts 22:3; Acts 15:41"> and there arose certain of those of the synagogue, the &#91;one&#93; called Libertines (and Cyrenians, and Alexandrians, and of those from Cilicia, and Asia), disputing with Stephen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Matthew 10:19–20; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> and they were not able to resist the wisdom and the Spirit with which he was speaking;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 26:59–60; 1 Kings 21:10–13; 1 Timothy 1:13"> then they suborned men, saying, “We have heard him speaking slanderous sayings in regard to Moses and God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Acts 14:2; Matthew 26:57"> They also stirred up the people, and the elders, and the scribes, and having come on &#91;him&#93;, they caught him, and brought &#91;him&#93; to the Sanhedrin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 6:11; Acts 21:28; Acts 25:8"> they also set up false witnesses, saying, “This one does not cease to speak evil sayings against this holy place and the Law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; Hebrews 12:26–28; Acts 21:21"> for we have heard him saying that this Jesus the Nazarean will overthrow this place, and will change the customs that Moses delivered to us”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Ecclesiastes 8:1; Matthew 17:2"> and gazing at him, all those sitting in the Sanhedrin saw his face as it were the face of a messenger.</span>
