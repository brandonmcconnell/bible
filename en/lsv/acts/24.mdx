<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 24:11; Acts 23:2; Acts 25:2"> And after five days the chief priest Ananias came down, with the elders, and a certain orator—Tertullus, and they disclosed to the governor &#91;the things&#93; against Paul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:5; Proverbs 26:28; Acts 24:26–27"> and he having been called, Tertullus began to accuse &#91;him&#93;, saying, “Enjoying much peace through you, and worthy deeds being done to this nation through your forethought,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 23:26; Acts 26:25; Luke 1:3"> always, also, and everywhere we receive it, most noble Felix, with all thankfulness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:32"> and that I may not be further tedious to you, I exhort you to hear us concisely in your gentleness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 28:22; Acts 24:14; Acts 26:5"> for having found this man a pestilence, and moving a dissension to all the Jews through the world—also a ringleader of the sect of the Nazarenes—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 24:12; Acts 21:27–32; John 18:31"> who also tried to profane the temple, whom we also took, &#91;&#91;and wished to judge according to our law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and Lysias the chief captain having come near, took away out of our hands with much violence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 23:35; Acts 25:15–16; Acts 25:5"> having commanded his accusers to come to you,&#93;&#93; from whom you may be able, yourself having examined, to know concerning all these things of which we accuse him”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:16; Micah 7:2–3; Psalms 4:2"> and the Jews also agreed, professing these things to be so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15; 1 Samuel 2:25; Luke 18:2"> And Paul—the governor having beckoned to him to speak—answered, “Knowing &#91;that&#93; for many years you have been a judge to this nation, I answer more cheerfully the things concerning myself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 24:1; Acts 21:18; Acts 23:11"> you being able to know that it is not more than twelve days to me since I went up to worship in Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 25:8; Acts 28:17; Acts 24:18"> and neither did they find me reasoning with anyone in the temple, or making a dissension of the multitude, nor in the synagogues, nor in the city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 25:7; 1 Peter 3:16"> nor are they able to prove against me the things concerning which they now accuse me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 9:2; Romans 3:21; Acts 3:13"> And I confess this to you, that, according to The Way that they call a sect, so I serve the God of the fathers, believing all things that have been written in the Law and the Prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 5:28–29; 1 Thessalonians 4:14–16"> having hope toward God, which they themselves also wait for, &#91;that&#93; there is about to be a resurrection of the dead, both of righteous and unrighteous;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 23:1; 1 Timothy 1:5; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> and in this I exercise myself, to always have a conscience void of offense toward God and men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 2:10; Romans 15:25–28; 1 Corinthians 16:1–4"> And after many years I came, about to do kind acts to my nation, and offerings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:21; Acts 21:26–30; Acts 24:12"> in which certain Jews from Asia found me purified in the temple, not with multitude, nor with tumult,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 23:30; Acts 25:16"> whom it is necessary to be present before you, and to accuse, if they had anything against me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> or let these same say if they found any unrighteousness in me in my standing before the Sanhedrin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 23:6; Acts 4:2; Acts 26:6–8"> except concerning this one voice, in which I cried, standing among them—Concerning a resurrection of the dead I am judged by you today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 24:6; Acts 24:24; Acts 25:26"> And having heard these things, Felix delayed them—having known more exactly of the things concerning The Way—saying, “When Lysias the chief captain may come down, I will know fully the things concerning you”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 28:16; Acts 27:3; Proverbs 16:7"> having also given a direction to the centurion to keep Paul, to let &#91;him&#93; also have liberty, and to forbid none of his own friends to minister or to come near to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Galatians 2:16; Mark 6:20"> And after certain days, Felix having come with his wife Drusilla, being a Jewess, he sent for Paul, and heard him concerning faith toward Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:8; 1 Peter 3:15; Acts 10:42"> and he reasoning concerning righteousness, and self-control, and the judgment that is about to be, Felix, having become afraid, answered, “For the present be going, and having time, I will call for you”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Proverbs 17:23; Isaiah 56:11"> and at the same time also hoping that money will be given to him by Paul, that he may release him, therefore, also sending for him often, he was conversing with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 25:9; Acts 25:14; Acts 12:3"> and two years having been fulfilled, Felix received a successor, Porcius Festus; Felix also willing to lay a favor on the Jews, left Paul bound.</span>
