<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 27:26; Acts 27:44; Acts 27:39"> And having been saved, then they knew that the island is called Malta,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:14; Hebrews 13:2; Colossians 3:11"> and the foreigners were showing us no ordinary kindness, for having kindled a fire, they received us all, because of the pressing rain, and because of the cold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 23:33; Matthew 12:34; Acts 28:4"> but Paul having gathered together a quantity of sticks, and having laid &#91;them&#93; on the fire, a viper—having come out of the heat—fastened on his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 13:2; Luke 13:4; John 7:24"> And when the foreigners saw the beast hanging from his hand, they said to one another, “Certainly this man is a murderer, whom, having been saved out of the sea, the justice did not permit to live”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 10:19; Mark 16:18; Psalms 91:13"> he then, indeed, having shaken off the beast into the fire, suffered no evil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 12:22; Acts 14:11–13; Matthew 21:9"> and they were expecting him to be about to be inflamed, or to suddenly fall down dead, and they, expecting &#91;it&#93; a long time, and seeing nothing uncommon happening to him, changing &#91;their&#93; minds, said he was a god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:40–41; Luke 19:6–9; Acts 28:2"> And in the neighborhood of that place were lands of the principal man of the island, by name Publius, who, having received us, courteously lodged &#91;us for&#93; three days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:40; Mark 6:5; James 5:14–16"> and it came to pass, the father of Publius was lying, oppressed with fevers and dysentery, to whom Paul, having entered and having prayed, having laid &#91;his&#93; hands on him, healed him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 5:12; Mark 6:54–56; Acts 5:15"> this, therefore, being done, also the others in the island having sicknesses were coming and were healed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; Philippians 4:11–12; Matthew 6:31–34"> who also honored us with many honors, and we setting sail—they were loading &#91;us&#93; with the things that were necessary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 27:6; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 45:20"> And after three months, we set sail in a ship (that had wintered in the island) of Alexandria, with the sign Dioscuri,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and having landed at Syracuse, we remained three days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 27:13"> there having gone around, we came to Rhegium, and after one day, a south wind having sprung up, the second &#91;day&#93; we came to Puteoli,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 21:4; Acts 20:6; Acts 19:1"> where, having found brothers, we were called on to remain with them seven days, and thus we came to Rome;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:3; Romans 15:24; Exodus 4:14"> and there, the brothers having heard the things concerning us, came forth to meet us, as far as &#91;the&#93; Forum of Appius, and Three Taverns—whom Paul having seen, having given thanks to God, took courage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 27:3; Acts 24:23; Acts 28:30–31"> And when we came to Rome, the centurion delivered up the prisoners to the captain of the barracks, but Paul was permitted to remain by himself, with the soldier guarding him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 25:8; Acts 6:14; Acts 25:10"> And it came to pass after three days, Paul called together those who are the principal men of the Jews, and they having come together, he said to them: “Men, brothers, I—having done nothing contrary to the people, or to the customs of the fathers—a prisoner from Jerusalem, was delivered up into the hands of the Romans;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:31–32; Acts 23:29; Acts 22:24–25"> who having examined me, were willing to release &#91;me&#93;, because of there being no cause of death in me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 26:32; Acts 25:21; Acts 25:10–12"> and the Jews having spoken against &#91;it&#93;, I was constrained to appeal to Caesar—not as having anything to accuse my nation of;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:9; Acts 26:6–7"> for this cause, therefore, I called for you to see and to speak with &#91;you&#93;, for because of the hope of Israel I am bound with this chain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 54:17; Isaiah 41:11; Isaiah 50:8"> And they said to him, “We neither received letters concerning you from Judea, nor did anyone who came of the brothers declare or speak any evil concerning you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Acts 24:14; 1 Peter 3:16"> and we think it good from you to hear what you think, for indeed, concerning this sect it is known to us that it is spoken against everywhere”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 17:2–3; Acts 19:8; Acts 18:28"> and having appointed him a day, more of them came to him, to the lodging, to whom he was setting &#91;it&#93; forth, testifying fully the Kingdom of God, persuading them also of the things concerning Jesus, both from the Law of Moses, and the Prophets, from morning until evening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 14:4; Acts 17:4–5; Acts 19:8–9"> and some, indeed, were believing the things spoken, and some were not believing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 15:7; 2 Peter 1:21; Mark 7:6"> And not being agreed with one another, they were going away, Paul having spoken one word, “The Holy Spirit spoke well through Isaiah the prophet to our fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Ezekiel 12:2; Isaiah 42:19–20"> saying, Go on to this people and say, With hearing you will hear, and you will not understand, and seeing you will see, and you will not perceive,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10"> for the heart of this people was made obtuse, and with the ears they barely heard, and they closed their eyes, lest they may see with the eyes, and may understand with the heart, and should turn, and I may heal them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:30–32; Acts 26:17–18; Romans 3:29–30"> Be it known, therefore, to you, that the salvation of God was sent to the nations, these also will hear it”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> &#91;&#91;and he having said these things, the Jews went away, having much debate among themselves;&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and Paul remained an entire two years in his own hired &#91;house&#93;, and was receiving all those coming in to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 4:29; Ephesians 6:19–20; Matthew 4:23"> preaching the Kingdom of God, and teaching the things concerning the Lord Jesus Christ with all boldness—unforbidden.</span>
