<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 24:9; John 15:20; Matthew 10:17–18"> And about that time, Herod the king put forth his hands to do evil to certain of those of the Assembly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:37; Matthew 20:23; Matthew 4:21–22"> and he killed James, the brother of John, with the sword,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 23:15; John 12:43; Acts 24:27"> and having seen that it is pleasing to the Jews, he added to lay hold of Peter also—and they were the days of the Unleavened &#91;Bread&#93;—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Luke 22:33; Luke 21:12"> whom also having seized, he put in prison, having delivered &#91;him&#93; to four squads of four soldiers to guard him, intending to bring him forth to the people after the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; James 5:16"> Peter, therefore, indeed, was kept in the prison, and fervent prayer was being made by the Assembly to God for him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 21:33; Hebrews 13:6; Philippians 4:6–7"> and when Herod was about to bring him forth, the same night was Peter sleeping between two soldiers, having been bound with two chains, guards were also keeping the prison before the door,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Acts 16:26; Psalms 146:7"> and behold, a messenger of the LORD stood by, and a light shone in the buildings, and having struck Peter on the side, he raised him up, saying, “Rise in haste,” and his chains fell from off &#91;his&#93; hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The messenger also said to him, “Gird yourself, and bind on your sandals”; and he did so; and he says to him, “Cast your garment around and follow me”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 126:1; Hebrews 11:8; Genesis 45:26"> and having gone forth, he was following him, and he did not know that it is true that which is done through the messenger, and was thinking he saw a vision,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Acts 16:26; Isaiah 45:1–2"> and having passed through a first ward, and a second, they came to the iron gate that is leading into the city, which opened to them of its own accord, and having gone forth, they went on through one street, and immediately the messenger departed from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 6:22; Daniel 3:28; 2 Peter 2:9"> And Peter having come to himself, said, “Now I have known of a truth that the LORD sent forth His messenger, and delivered me out of the hand of Herod, and all the expectation of the people of the Jews”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 5:14–15; Acts 12:5; Matthew 18:19–20"> also, having considered, he came to the house of Mary, the mother of John, who is surnamed Mark, where there were many thronged together and praying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:16; Luke 13:25; John 18:16–17"> And Peter having knocked at the door of the porch, there came a girl to listen, by name Rhoda,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 24:41; Matthew 28:8"> and having known the voice of Peter, from the joy she did not open the porch, but having run in, told of the standing of Peter before the porch,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:10; Luke 24:11; Mark 16:14"> and they said to her, “You are mad”; and she was confidently affirming &#91;it&#93; to be so, and they said, “It is his messenger”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and Peter was continuing knocking, and having opened, they saw him, and were astonished,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 21:40; Acts 13:16; Acts 19:33"> and having beckoned to them with the hand to be silent, he declared to them how the LORD brought him out of the prison, and he said, “Declare these things to James and to the brothers”; and having gone forth, he went on to another place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:22–25; Acts 16:27; Acts 19:23"> And day having come, there was not a little stir among the soldiers what then was become of Peter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 16:27; Psalms 37:32–33; John 12:10–11"> and Herod having sought for him, and having not found, having examined the guards, commanded &#91;them&#93; to be led away to punishment, and having gone down from Judea to Caesarea, he was abiding &#91;there&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezra 3:7; Matthew 11:21–22; Ezekiel 27:17"> And Herod was highly displeased with the Tyrians and Sidonians, and with one accord they came to him, and having made a friend of Blastus, who &#91;is&#93; over the bed-chambers of the king, they were asking peace, because of their country being nourished from the king’s;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and on a set day, Herod having clothed himself in kingly clothing, and having sat down on the judgment seat, was making an oration to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 14:10–13; Revelation 13:4; Jude 1:16"> and the populace were shouting, “The voice of a god, and not of a man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 115:1; Acts 10:25–26; 2 Thessalonians 2:4"> And immediately a messenger of the LORD struck him in return for that he did not give the glory to God, and having been eaten of worms, he expired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 6:7; Acts 19:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:1"> And the word of God grew and multiplied,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 12:12; 1 Peter 5:13; Acts 15:37"> and Barnabas and Saul turned back out of Jerusalem, having fulfilled the ministry, having also taken John with &#91;them&#93;, who was surnamed Mark.</span>
