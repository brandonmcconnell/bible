<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:6; Philippians 2:2; Acts 1:13–15"> And in the day of the Pentecost being fulfilled, they were all with one accord at the same place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:31; 1 Kings 19:11; Psalms 18:10"> and there came suddenly out of the sky a sound as of a violent rushing wind, and it filled all the house where they were sitting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; John 1:32–33; Jeremiah 23:29"> and there appeared to them divided tongues, as it were of fire; it also sat on each one of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Acts 13:52; Acts 4:31"> and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit, and began to speak with other tongues, according as the Spirit was giving them to declare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Acts 8:2; Colossians 1:23"> And there were Jews dwelling in Jerusalem, devout men from every nation of those under the heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 2:3; 1 Corinthians 16:9; Acts 2:2"> and the rumor of this having come, the multitude came together, and was confounded, because they were, each one, hearing them speaking in his proper dialect,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 2:12; Acts 1:11; John 7:52"> and they were all amazed, and wondered, saying to one another, “Behold, are not all these who are speaking Galileans?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And how do we hear, each in our proper dialect, in which we were born?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 1:1; Acts 18:2; Revelation 1:4"> Parthians, and Medes, and Elamites, and those dwelling in Mesopotamia, in Judea also, and Cappadocia, Pontus, and Asia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 16:6; Zechariah 8:23; Zechariah 8:20"> Phrygia also, and Pamphylia, Egypt, and the parts of Libya that &#91;are&#93; along Cyrene, and the strangers of Rome, both Jews and proselytes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 78:4"> Cretes and Arabians, we heard them speaking the great things of God in our tongues.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 15:26; Acts 17:20; Luke 18:36"> And they were all amazed, and were in doubt, saying to one another, “What would this wish to be?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:23; Acts 2:15; 1 Samuel 1:14"> And others mocking said, “They are full of sweet wine”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 51:7; Isaiah 51:1; Deuteronomy 27:9"> and Peter having stood up with the Eleven, lifted up his voice and declared to them: “Men—Jews, and all those dwelling in Jerusalem! Let this be known to you, and listen to my sayings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Samuel 1:15; Matthew 20:3"> for these are not drunken, as you take it up, for it is the third hour of the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32"> But this is that which has been spoken through the prophet Joel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32; Isaiah 44:3; Titus 3:4–6"> And it will be in the last days, says God, &#124;&#124; I will pour out of My Spirit on all flesh, &#124;&#124; And your sons and your daughters will prophesy, &#124;&#124; And your young men will see visions, &#124;&#124; And your old men will dream dreams;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11; 1 Corinthians 7:21–22"> And also on My menservants, and on My maidservants, &#124;&#124; In those days, I will pour out of My Spirit, &#124;&#124; And they will prophesy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:30–31; Zephaniah 1:14–18; Malachi 4:1–6"> And I will give wonders in the sky above, &#124;&#124; And signs on the earth beneath—Blood, and fire, and vapor of smoke,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Amos 8:9; Matthew 24:29"> The sun will be turned to darkness, &#124;&#124; And the moon to blood, &#124;&#124; Before the coming of the Day of the LORD—the great and conspicuous;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 10:12–13; Joel 2:32; Psalms 86:5"> And it will be, everyone who, if he may have called on the Name of the LORD, will be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 3:2; John 14:10–11; Matthew 12:28"> Men, Israelites! Hear these words: Jesus the Nazarene, a man approved of God among you by mighty works, and wonders, and signs, that God did through Him in the midst of you, according as also you yourselves have known,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:22; Acts 4:28; Acts 3:18"> this One, by the determinate counsel and foreknowledge of God, being given out, having been taken by lawless hands, having been crucified—you slew,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:14; 1 Peter 1:21; Acts 2:32"> whom God raised up, having loosed the travails of death, because it was not possible for Him to be held by it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 16:8–11; Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 62:6"> for David says in regard to Him: I foresaw the LORD always before me—Because He is on my right hand—That I may not be moved;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 16:9; Psalms 63:5; Psalms 71:23"> Because of this was my heart cheered, &#124;&#124; And my tongue was glad, &#124;&#124; And yet—my flesh will also rest on hope,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Acts 2:31; Revelation 1:18"> Because You will not leave my soul to Hades, &#124;&#124; Nor will You give Your Holy One to see corruption;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 25:4; Psalms 21:6"> You made known to me ways of life, &#124;&#124; You will fill me with joy with Your countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 13:36; 1 Kings 2:10; Acts 7:8–9"> Men, brothers! It is permitted to speak with freedom to you concerning the patriarch David, that he both died and was buried, and his tomb is among us to this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 132:11–18; Psalms 89:3–4; Jeremiah 33:14–15"> therefore, being a prophet, and knowing that God swore to him with an oath, out of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh, to raise up the Christ, to sit on his throne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 2:27; Psalms 16:10; Acts 13:35"> having foreseen, he spoke concerning the resurrection of the Christ, that His soul was not left to Hades, nor did His flesh see corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 3:15; Acts 1:8; Acts 1:22"> God raised up this Jesus, of which we are all witnesses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:4; Acts 5:31; Acts 2:17"> then having been exalted at the right hand of God—also having received the promise of the Holy Spirit from the Father—He poured forth this which you now see and hear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Matthew 22:42–45; Hebrews 1:13"> for David did not go up into the heavens, and he says himself: The LORD says to my Lord, &#124;&#124; Sit at My right hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 19:19–3; Romans 16:20; Isaiah 60:14"> Until I make Your enemies Your footstool;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 5:30–31; Romans 14:8–12; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> assuredly, therefore, let all the house of Israel know that God made Him both Lord and Christ—this Jesus whom you crucified.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 3:10; Acts 22:10; Hebrews 4:12–13"> And having heard, they were pricked to the heart; they also say to Peter and to the rest of the apostles, “What will we do, men, brothers?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 22:16; Mark 16:16; Acts 8:12"> And Peter said to them, “Convert, and each of you be immersed on the Name of Jesus Christ, for forgiveness of sins, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 8:30"> for the promise is to you and to your children, and to all those far off, as many as the LORD our God will call.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Deuteronomy 32:5; Matthew 17:17"> Also with many more other words he was testifying and exhorting, saying, “Be saved from this perverse generation”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 4:4; Acts 2:47; Acts 16:31–34"> then those, indeed, who gladly received his word were immersed, and there were added on that day, as it were, three thousand souls,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; Acts 1:14; 1 John 1:3"> and they were continuing steadfastly in the teaching of the apostles, and the fellowship, and the breaking of the bread, and the prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 7:16; John 14:12; Luke 8:37"> And fear came on every soul, also many wonders and signs were being done through the apostles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 4:32; 1 John 3:16–18; 2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> and all those believing were at the same place, and had all things common,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 John 3:17; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; James 2:14–16"> and they were selling the possessions and the goods, and were parting them to all, according as anyone had need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 5:42; Acts 20:7; Acts 2:42"> Also continuing daily with one accord in the temple, also breaking bread at every house, they were partaking of food in gladness and simplicity of heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 16:5; Acts 5:13–14; Acts 2:41"> praising God, and having favor with all the people, and the LORD was adding those being saved every day to the Assembly.</span>
