<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:5; Acts 19:21; 1 Timothy 1:3"> And after the ceasing of the tumult, Paul having called near the disciples, and having embraced &#91;them&#93;, went forth to go on to Macedonia;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 4:1; Colossians 1:28"> and having gone through those parts, and having exhorted them with many words, he came to Greece;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 20:19; 2 Corinthians 11:26; Acts 9:23–24"> and having continued three months—a counsel of the Jews having been against him—being about to set forth to Syria, there came &#91;to him&#93; a resolution of returning through Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 19:29; Ephesians 6:21; 2 Timothy 4:20"> And there were accompanying him to Asia, Sopater of Pyrrhus from Berea, and of Thessalonians Aristarchus and Secundus, and Gaius of Derbe, and Timotheus, and of Asians Tychicus and Trophimus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 16:8; Acts 16:10–11; 2 Timothy 4:13"> these, having gone before, remained for us in Troas,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 12:3; Acts 16:12; Exodus 23:15"> and we sailed, after the days of the Unleavened &#91;Bread&#93;, from Philippi, and came to them to Troas in five days, where we abided seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:2; Acts 2:42; John 20:19"> And on the first &#91;day&#93; of the weeks, the disciples having been gathered together to break bread, Paul was discoursing to them, about to depart on the next day, he was also continuing the discourse until midnight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Luke 22:12"> and there were many lamps in the upper chamber where they were gathered together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 13:36; Matthew 26:40–41; Jonah 1:5–6"> and there a certain youth was sitting, by name Eutychus, on the window—being borne down by a deep sleep, Paul discoursing long—he having sunk down from the sleep, fell down from the third story, and was lifted up dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 5:39; Matthew 9:23–24; 1 Kings 17:21–22"> And Paul, having gone down, fell on him, and having embraced &#91;him&#93;, said, “Make no tumult, for his life is in him”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 20:7; Acts 20:9"> and having come up, and having broken bread, and having tasted, for a long time also having talked—until daylight, so he went forth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:16; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; 2 Corinthians 1:4"> and they brought up the boy alive, and were comforted in no ordinary measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 6:46; Mark 1:35; Mark 6:31–33"> And we having gone before to the ship, sailed to Assos, there intending to take in Paul, for so he had arranged, intending himself to go on foot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and when he met with us at Assos, having taken him up, we came to Mitylene,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:20; Acts 20:17"> and there having sailed, on the next day we came opposite Chios, and the next day we arrived at Samos, and having remained in Trogyllium, on the following day we came to Miletus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:8; Acts 2:1; Acts 19:21"> for Paul decided to sail past Ephesus, that there may not be to him a loss of time in Asia, for he was hurrying, if it were possible for him, to be at Jerusalem on the day of the Pentecost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Titus 1:5; Acts 11:30; 1 Timothy 5:17"> And from Miletus, having sent to Ephesus, he called for the elders of the assembly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:5–6; Acts 18:19; Acts 19:1"> and when they were come to him, he said to them, “You know from the first day in which I came to Asia, how I was with you at all times;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; 2 Corinthians 2:4; Romans 12:11"> serving the LORD with all humility, and many tears, and temptations, that befell me in the counsels of the Jews against &#91;me&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:27; 2 Timothy 4:2; Acts 5:42"> how I kept back nothing of what things are profitable, not to declare to you, and to teach you publicly, and in every house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 11:18; 1 John 5:11–13"> testifying fully both to Jews and Greeks, conversion toward God, and faith toward our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 4:14; 2 Corinthians 5:14; 2 Peter 1:14"> And now, behold, I—bound in the Spirit—go on to Jerusalem, not knowing the things that will befall me in it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 9:16; 1 Thessalonians 3:3; Acts 21:4"> except that the Holy Spirit fully testifies in every city, saying that bonds and tribulations remain for me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6–8; 2 Corinthians 4:1; 2 Corinthians 12:10"> but I make account of none of these, neither do I count my life precious to myself, so that I finish my course with joy, and the ministry that I received from the Lord Jesus, to fully testify &#91;to&#93; the good news of the grace of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 28:31; Acts 20:38; Matthew 4:23"> And now, behold, I have known that you will no longer see my face, among all you &#91;to&#93; whom I went preaching the Kingdom of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Ezekiel 3:18–21; Ezekiel 33:2–9"> for this reason I take you to witness this day, that I &#91;am&#93; clear from the blood of all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 20:20; 1 Thessalonians 2:4; Galatians 1:7–10"> for I did not keep back from declaring to you all the counsel of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 5:2–3; 1 Timothy 4:16; Isaiah 40:11"> Take heed, therefore, to yourselves, and to all the flock, among which the Holy Spirit made you overseers, to feed the Assembly of God that He acquired through His own blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; 2 Peter 2:1; John 10:12"> for I have known this, that there will enter in, after my departing, grievous wolves to you, not sparing the flock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19–20; 2 Timothy 4:3–4; Proverbs 23:33"> and there will arise men of your own selves, speaking perverse things, to draw away the disciples after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:8; Acts 20:19; Acts 19:10"> Therefore, watch, remembering that three years, night and day, I did not cease with tears warning each one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 1:12; Ephesians 1:14; Colossians 3:24"> and now, I commend you, brothers, to God, and to the word of His grace, that is able to build up, and to give you an inheritance among all those sanctified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; 1 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Corinthians 9:18"> I coveted the silver or gold or clothing of no one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 18:3; 1 Corinthians 4:12; 2 Thessalonians 3:8–9"> and you yourselves know that to my necessities, and to those who were with me, these hands ministered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 19:17; 2 Corinthians 9:6–12"> I showed you all things, that, thus laboring, it is necessary to partake with the ailing, to also be mindful of the words of the Lord Jesus, that He Himself said: It is more blessed to give than to receive.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 21:5; Luke 22:41; Philippians 4:6"> And having said these things, having bowed his knees, with them all, he prayed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:4; 1 Corinthians 16:20; 1 Thessalonians 5:26"> and there came a great weeping to all, and having fallen on the neck of Paul, they were kissing him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 15:3; 1 Corinthians 16:11; Acts 20:25"> sorrowing most of all for the word that he had said—that they are about to see his face no longer; and they were accompanying him to the ship.</span>
