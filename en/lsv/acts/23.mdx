<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 24:16; 2 Timothy 1:3; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> And Paul having earnestly beheld the Sanhedrin, said, “Men, brothers, I have lived to God in all good conscience to this day”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 18:22; Acts 24:1; Micah 5:1"> and the chief priest Ananias commanded those standing by him to strike him on the mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:1–2; John 7:51; Matthew 23:27–28"> then Paul said to him, “God is about to strike you, you whitewashed wall, and you sit judging me according to the Law, and violating law, order me to be struck!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And those who stood by said, “Do you revile the chief priest of God?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Ecclesiastes 10:20; Jude 1:8–9"> And Paul said, “I did not know, brothers, that he is chief priest, for it has been written: You will not speak evil of the ruler of your people”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 24:15; Acts 24:21; Acts 26:5–8"> and Paul having known that one part are Sadducees, and the other Pharisees, cried out in the Sanhedrin, “Men, brothers, I am a Pharisee—son of a Pharisee—concerning &#91;the&#93; hope and resurrection of &#91;the&#93; dead I am judged.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 55:9; John 7:40–43; Matthew 10:34"> And he having spoken this, there came a dissension of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees, and the crowd was divided,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 20:27; Matthew 22:23; Mark 12:18"> for Sadducees, indeed, say there is no resurrection, nor messenger, nor spirit, but Pharisees confess both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 22:7; Acts 22:17–18; John 12:29"> And there came a great cry, and the scribes of the Pharisees’ part having arisen, were striving, saying, “We find no evil in this man; and if a spirit spoke to him, or a messenger, we may not fight against God”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:19; James 3:14–2; Acts 22:24"> and a great dissension having come, the chief captain having been afraid lest Paul may be pulled to pieces by them, commanded the army, having gone down, to seize him out of their midst, and to bring &#91;him&#93; into the stronghold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 18:9; Acts 19:21; 2 Timothy 4:17"> And on the following night, the LORD having stood by him, said, “Take courage, Paul, for as you fully testified &#91;to&#93; the things concerning Me at Jerusalem, so you must also testify at Rome.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 23:30; Isaiah 8:9–10; Acts 23:21"> And day having come, certain of the Jews having made a concourse, cursed themselves, saying neither to eat nor to drink until they may kill Paul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 16:2; 2 Samuel 15:31; 2 Samuel 15:12"> and they were more than forty who made this conspiracy by oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 23:12; Jeremiah 8:12; Isaiah 3:9"> who having come near to the chief priests and to the elders said, “With a curse we accursed ourselves—to taste nothing until we have killed Paul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Psalms 37:32–33; Proverbs 1:16"> now, therefore, you, signify to the chief captain, with the Sanhedrin, that tomorrow he may bring him down to you, as being about to know more exactly the things concerning him; and we, before his coming near, are ready to put him to death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 23:10; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Acts 23:32"> And the son of Paul’s sister having heard of the lying in wait, having gone and entered into the stronghold, told Paul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 22:3; Matthew 8:8–9; Matthew 10:16"> and Paul having called near one of the centurions, said, “Lead this young man to the chief captain, for he has something to tell him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 3:1; Ephesians 4:1; Acts 28:17"> He indeed, then, having taken him, brought him to the chief captain and says, “The prisoner Paul, having called me near, asked &#91;me&#93; to bring to you this young man, having something to say to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; Esther 9:12; Esther 7:2"> And the chief captain having taken him by the hand, and having withdrawn by themselves, inquired, “What is that which you have to tell me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 23:14–15; Acts 23:1; Psalms 12:2"> And he said, “The Jews agreed to request you, that tomorrow you may bring down Paul to the Sanhedrin, as being about to inquire something more exactly concerning him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 23:12–14; Acts 14:5–6; 2 Corinthians 11:26"> you, therefore, may you not yield to them, for there more than forty men of them lie in wait for him, who cursed themselves—not to eat nor to drink until they kill him, and now they are ready, waiting for the promise from you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 1:44; Joshua 2:14"> The chief captain, then, indeed, let the young man go, having charged &#91;him&#93;, “Tell no one that you have shown these things to me”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 14:25; Acts 23:33; Luke 12:38"> and having called a certain two of the centurions near, he said, “Make ready two hundred soldiers, that they may go on to Caesarea, and seventy horsemen, and two hundred spearmen, from the third hour of the night;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 25:14; Acts 23:26; Acts 24:10"> also provide beasts, that, having set Paul on, they may bring him safe to Felix the governor”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> he having written a letter after this description:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 15:23; Acts 26:25; Luke 1:3"> “Claudius Lysias, to the most noble governor Felix, greetings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 22:25–29; Acts 21:31–33; Acts 24:6"> This man having been taken by the Jews, and being about to be killed by them—having come with the army, I rescued him, having learned that he is a Roman;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 22:30"> and intending to know the cause for which they were accusing him, I brought him down to their Sanhedrin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 26:31; Acts 25:25; Acts 18:15"> whom I found accused concerning questions of their law, and having no accusation worthy of death or bonds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 23:35; Acts 25:16; Acts 25:5–6"> and a plot having been intimated to me against this man—about to be of the Jews—I sent to you at once, having also given command to the accusers to say the things against him before you; be strong.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:3–4; Luke 7:8; Acts 23:23–24"> Then, indeed, the soldiers according to that directed them, having taken up Paul, brought him through the night to Antipatris,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 23:23; Acts 23:10"> and on the next day, having permitted the horsemen to go on with him, they returned to the stronghold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 23:26; Acts 23:23–24; Acts 28:16"> those having entered into Caesarea, and delivered the letter to the governor, also presented Paul to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 21:39; Acts 25:1; Daniel 2:49"> And the governor having read &#91;it&#93;, and inquired of what province he is, and understood that &#91;he is&#93; from Cilicia;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 23:30; Acts 25:16; Matthew 27:27"> “I will hear you,” he said, “when your accusers may also have come”; he also commanded him to be kept in the Praetorium of Herod.</span>
