<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Proverbs 1:24; Acts 26:2"> And Agrippa said to Paul, “It is permitted to you to speak for yourself”; then Paul having stretched forth the hand, was making a defense:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> “Concerning all things of which I am accused by Jews, King Agrippa, I have thought myself blessed, being about to make a defense before you today,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 6:14; Acts 26:26; Acts 26:7"> especially knowing you to be acquainted with all things—both customs and questions—among Jews; for this reason, I implore you to hear me patiently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 1:13; 2 Timothy 3:10; Philippians 3:5"> The manner of my life then, indeed, from youth—which from the beginning was among my nation, in Jerusalem—all the Jews know,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 23:6; Acts 22:3; Acts 24:5"> knowing me before from the first (if they may be willing to testify), that after the most exact sect of our worship, I lived a Pharisee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:13; Acts 24:15; Acts 28:20"> and now for the hope of the promise made to the fathers by God, I have stood judged,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:1; Philippians 3:11; 1 Timothy 5:5"> to which our twelve tribes, intently serving night and day, hope to come, concerning which hope I am accused, King Agrippa, by the Jews;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:12–20; Acts 4:2; Acts 17:31–32"> why is it judged incredible with you if God raises the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Acts 22:8; Philippians 3:6"> I indeed, therefore, thought with myself that it was necessary &#91;for me&#93; to do many things against the Name of Jesus of Nazareth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 8:3; Acts 9:13–14; Acts 9:21"> which I also did in Jerusalem, and I shut up many of the holy ones in prison, having received the authority from the chief priests; they also being put to death, I gave my vote against them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 22:19; Matthew 10:17; Acts 13:45"> and in every synagogue, often punishing them, I was constraining &#91;them&#93; to speak evil, being also exceedingly mad against them, I was also persecuting &#91;them&#93; even to strange cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 26:10; Acts 22:5–11; Acts 9:1–8"> In which things, also, going on to Damascus—with authority and commission from the chief priests—</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 17:2; Revelation 21:23; Revelation 1:16"> at midday, I saw in the way, O king, out of Heaven, above the brightness of the sun, shining around me a light—and those going on with me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 21:40; Acts 22:2; Acts 9:7"> and we all having fallen to the earth, I heard a voice speaking to me, and saying in the Hebrew dialect, Saul, Saul, why do you persecute Me? &#91;It is&#93; hard for you to kick against goads!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 15:20–21; Matthew 25:45; Exodus 16:8"> And I said, Who are You, Lord? And He said, I am Jesus whom you persecute;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 22:14–15; Ezekiel 2:1; Daniel 10:11"> but rise, and stand on your feet, for this I appeared to you, to appoint you an officer and a witness both of the things you saw, and of the things &#91;in which&#93; I will appear to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Timothy 2:7; Jeremiah 1:19"> delivering you from the people, and the nations, to whom I now send you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Ephesians 1:18; Ephesians 5:8"> to open their eyes, to turn &#91;them&#93; from darkness to light, and &#91;from&#93; the authority of Satan to God, for their receiving forgiveness of sins, and a lot among those having been sanctified by faith that &#91;is&#93; toward Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 50:5; Galatians 1:16; Ezekiel 2:7–8"> After which, King Agrippa, I was not disobedient to the heavenly vision,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 3:8; Acts 3:19; Acts 2:38"> but to those in Damascus first, and to those in Jerusalem, also to all the region of Judea, and to the nations, I was preaching to convert, and to turn back to God, doing works worthy of conversion;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 21:30–31; Acts 21:27; Acts 25:3"> because of these things the Jews—having caught me in the temple—were endeavoring to kill &#91;me&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 24:14; John 5:46; Romans 3:21"> Having obtained, therefore, help from God, until this day, I have stood witnessing both to small and to great, saying nothing besides the things that both the prophets and Moses spoke of as about to come,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Luke 24:26; Colossians 1:18"> that the Christ is to suffer, whether first by a resurrection from the dead, He is about to proclaim light to the people and to the nations.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:13–14; 1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Corinthians 4:10"> And he thus making a defense, Festus said with a loud voice, “You are mad, Paul; much learning turns you mad!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 23:26; Titus 1:9; Titus 2:7–8"> And he says, “I am not mad, most noble Festus, but of truth and soberness I speak forth the sayings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 26:2–3; Acts 2:1–12; Isaiah 30:20"> for the king knows concerning these things, before whom I also speak boldly, for none of these things, I am persuaded, are hidden from him; for this thing has not been done in a corner;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 26:22–23"> do you believe, King Agrippa, the prophets? I have known that you believe!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 11:26; Matthew 10:18; Acts 26:29"> And Agrippa said to Paul, “In &#91;so&#93; little you persuade me to become a Christian?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; Colossians 1:28; Romans 10:1"> And Paul said, “I would have wished to God, both in a little, and in much, not only you, but also all those hearing me today, to become such as I also am—except these bonds.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 18:15; Acts 28:22; Acts 25:23"> And he having spoken these things, the king rose up, and the governor, Bernice also, and those sitting with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 23:29; Acts 23:9; Acts 28:18"> and having withdrawn, they were speaking to one another, saying, “This man does nothing worthy of death or of bonds”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 28:18; Acts 25:11–12; Acts 25:25"> and Agrippa said to Festus, “This man might have been released if he had not appealed to Caesar.”</span>
