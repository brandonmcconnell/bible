<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 1:7; Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:2"> The word &#91;is&#93; steadfast: If anyone longs for overseership, he desires a right work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:6–9; 2 Timothy 2:24; Romans 12:13"> it is required, therefore, the overseer to be blameless, a husband of one wife, vigilant, sober, respectable, a friend of strangers, apt to teach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 1:7; Ephesians 5:18; Titus 3:2"> not given to wine, not a striker, but gentle, not contentious, not a lover of money,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Titus 1:6; Genesis 18:19; 1 Timothy 3:12"> leading his own house well, having children in subjection with all dignity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:13; Ephesians 5:24; Acts 20:28"> (and if anyone has not known &#91;how&#93; to lead his own house, how will he take care of an assembly of God?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:4; Hebrews 5:12–13; 1 Peter 2:2"> not a new convert, lest having been puffed up he may fall to a judgment of the Devil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Timothy 2:26; Colossians 4:5"> and it is required of him also to have a good testimony from those outside, that he may not fall into reproach and a snare of the Devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:1; 1 Timothy 5:23; Acts 6:3–6"> Servants, in like manner, dignified, not double-tongued, not given to much wine, not given to shameful gain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19; 2 John 1:9–10; 1 Timothy 1:5"> having the secret of the faith in a pure conscience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:22; 1 Timothy 3:13; Titus 1:6–7"> and let these also first be proved, then let them minister, being unblameable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Titus 2:3; Titus 3:2; 1 Timothy 3:2"> Women, in like manner, dignified, not false accusers, vigilant, faithful in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; 1 Timothy 3:4–5; Philippians 1:1"> Servants—let them be husbands of one wife, leading the children well, and their own houses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Hebrews 6:10; Acts 21:35"> for those who ministered well acquire a good step to themselves, and much boldness in faith that &#91;is&#93; in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:34; Philemon 1:22; 2 Corinthians 1:15–17"> I write to you these things, hoping to come to you soon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Ephesians 2:21–22; 1 Thessalonians 1:9"> and if I delay, that you may know how it is required to conduct yourself in the house of God, which is an assembly of the living God—a pillar and foundation of the truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:14; Hebrews 1:3; Mark 16:19"> and confessedly, great is the secret of piety: who was revealed in flesh, declared righteous in &#91;the&#93; Spirit, seen by messengers, preached among nations, believed on in the world, taken up in glory!</span>
