<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 1:3; Colossians 1:27; Acts 9:15"> Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ, according to a command of God our Savior, and of the Lord Jesus Christ our hope,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:2; 1 Corinthians 4:14–17"> to Timotheus—genuine child in faith: Grace, kindness, peace, from God our Father and Christ Jesus our Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:3; Galatians 1:6–7; 2 John 1:9–10"> According as I exhorted you to remain in Ephesus—I going on to Macedonia—that you might charge certain &#91;ones&#93; not to teach any other thing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:7; Titus 1:14; Titus 3:9"> nor to give heed to fables and endless genealogies, that cause questions rather than &#91;the&#93; stewardship of God which &#91;is&#93; in faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19; Galatians 5:22; 2 Timothy 2:22"> And the end of the charge is love out of a pure heart, and of a good conscience, and of unhypocritical faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 1:10; 2 Timothy 2:23–24; Titus 3:9"> from which certain &#91;men&#93;, having swerved, turned aside to vain discourse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 1:22; 1 Timothy 6:4; 2 Peter 2:12"> willing to be teachers of law, not understanding either the things they say, nor concerning what they confidently assert,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:12–13; Psalms 19:7–10; Romans 7:16"> and we have known that the Law &#91;is&#93; good, if anyone may use it lawfully;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:19; 1 Peter 4:18; Romans 5:20"> having known this, that law is not set for a righteous man, but for lawless and insubordinate persons, ungodly and sinners, impious and profane, murderers of fathers and murderers of mothers, manslayers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Leviticus 18:22; 2 Timothy 4:3"> whoremongers, homosexuals, enslavers, liars, perjured persons, and if there be any other thing that is adverse to sound doctrine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 2:7; Titus 1:3; 1 Timothy 6:15"> according to the good news of the glory of the blessed God, with which I was entrusted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Timothy 4:17; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> And I give thanks to Him who enabled me—Christ Jesus our Lord—that He reckoned me steadfast, having put &#91;me&#93; to the ministry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Peter 2:10; Hebrews 6:4–8"> who before was speaking slander, and persecuting, and insulting, but I found kindness, because, being ignorant, I did &#91;it&#93; in unbelief,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13; Ephesians 1:7–8; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> and the grace of our Lord exceedingly abounded, with faith and love that &#91;is&#93; in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Luke 19:10; John 3:16–17"> The word &#91;is&#93; steadfast, and worthy of all acceptance, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners—of whom I am first;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Ephesians 2:7; Luke 7:47"> but because of this I found kindness, that Jesus Christ might first show forth all long-suffering in me, for a pattern of those about to believe on Him to continuous life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20–21; Jude 1:25; 1 Timothy 6:15–16"> And to the King of the ages, the incorruptible, invisible, only wise God, &#91;is&#93; honor and glory through the ages of the ages! Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 4:1–3"> I commit to you this charge, child Timotheus, according to the prophecies that went before on you, that you may war in them the good warfare,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:9; 1 Peter 3:15–16; Revelation 3:10"> having faith and a good conscience, which some having thrust away, made shipwreck concerning the faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:4–5; 2 Timothy 2:17; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> of whom are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I delivered to Satan, that they might be instructed not to speak evil.</span>
