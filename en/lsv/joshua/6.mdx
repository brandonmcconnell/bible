<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 2:24; Joshua 2:9–14; Psalms 127:1"> And Jericho shuts itself up, and is shut up, because of the presence of the sons of Israel—none going out, and none coming in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:24; Joshua 8:1; Joshua 2:24"> And YHWH says to Joshua, “See, I have given Jericho and its king into your hand—mighty men of valor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:7; Numbers 14:9; Joshua 6:7"> and you have surrounded the city—all the men of battle—going around the city once; thus you do &#91;for&#93; six days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:17; Revelation 16:1; 1 Kings 18:43"> and seven priests carry seven horns of the rams before the Ark, and on the seventh day you go around the city seven times, and the priests blow with the horns,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Hebrews 11:30; Exodus 19:19"> and it has been, in the prolonging of the horn of the ram, in your hearing the voice of the horn, all the people shout &#91;with&#93; a great shout, and the wall of the city has fallen under it, and the people have gone up, each straight before him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 9:1; Deuteronomy 20:2–4; Joshua 3:3"> And Joshua son of Nun calls to the priests and says to them, “Carry the Ark of the Covenant, and seven priests carry seven horns of the rams before the Ark of YHWH”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 4:13; Joshua 6:3; Joshua 1:14"> and He said to the people, “Pass over, and go around the city, and he who is armed passes over before the Ark of YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 32:20; Joshua 6:3–4"> And it comes to pass, when Joshua speaks to the people, that the seven priests carrying seven horns of the rams before YHWH have passed over and blown with the horns, and the Ark of the Covenant of YHWH is going after them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 6:13; Numbers 10:25; Isaiah 58:8"> and he who is armed is going before the priests blowing the horns, and he who is gathering up is going after the Ark, going on and blowing with the horns;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 24:49; Matthew 12:19; Isaiah 42:2"> and Joshua has commanded the people, saying, “Do not shout, nor cause your voice to be heard, nor does a word go out from your mouth, until the day of my saying to you, Shout! Then you have shouted.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the Ark of YHWH goes around the city, going around once, and they come into the camp, and lodge in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 3:1; Hebrews 11:7–8; Genesis 22:3"> And Joshua rises early in the morning, and the priests carry the Ark of YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; Matthew 24:13; 1 Chronicles 15:26"> and seven priests carrying seven horns of the rams before the Ark of YHWH are walking, going on, and they have blown with the horns—and he who is armed is going before them, and he who is gathering up is going behind the Ark of YHWH—going on and blowing with the horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 6:15; Joshua 6:11; Joshua 6:3"> And they go around the city once on the second day, and return to the camp; thus they have done &#91;for&#93; six days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:147; 2 Peter 1:19; Matthew 28:1"> And it comes to pass, on the seventh day, that they rise early, at the ascending of the dawn, and go around the city, according to this manner, seven times; (only on that day have they gone around the city seven times);</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:15; Joshua 6:5; 2 Chronicles 20:22–23"> and it comes to pass, at the seventh time, the priests have blown with the horns, and Joshua says to the people, “Shout! For YHWH has given the city to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:17; James 2:25; Micah 4:13"> and the city has been devoted, it and all that &#91;is&#93; in it, to YHWH; only Rahab the harlot lives, she and all who &#91;are&#93; with her in the house, for she hid the messengers whom we sent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 5:21; Ephesians 5:11; Joshua 7:1"> and surely you have kept from the devoted thing, lest you devote &#91;yourselves&#93;, and have taken from the devoted thing, and have made the camp of Israel become a devoted thing, and have troubled it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:20; Matthew 27:6; Mark 12:41"> and all the silver and gold, and vessels of bronze and iron—it &#91;is&#93; holy to YHWH; it comes into the treasury of YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 11:30; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Joshua 6:5"> And the people shout, and blow with the horns, and it comes to pass, when the people hear the voice of the horn, that the people shout &#91;with&#93; a great shout, and the wall falls under it, and the people goes up into the city, each straight before him, and they capture the city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 137:8–9; Jeremiah 48:18; Joshua 11:14"> and they devote all that &#91;is&#93; in the city, from man even to woman, from young even to aged, even to ox, and sheep, and donkey, by the mouth of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Joshua 6:17; Ezekiel 17:18–19"> And Joshua said to the two men who are spying out the land, “Go into the house of the woman, the harlot, and bring out the woman from there, and all whom she has, as you have sworn to her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; Genesis 18:24; Acts 27:24"> And the young men, those spying, go in and bring out Rahab, and her father, and her mother, and her brothers, and all whom she has; indeed, they have brought out all her relatives, and place them at the outside of the camp of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 8:28; Revelation 17:16; 2 Kings 25:9"> And they have burned the city with fire, and all that &#91;is&#93; in it; only, the silver and the gold, and the vessels of bronze, and of iron, they have given &#91;to&#93; the treasury of the house of YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Matthew 1:5; Judges 1:24–25"> and Rahab the harlot, and the house of her father, and all whom she has, Joshua has kept alive; and she dwells in the midst of Israel to this day, for she hid the messengers whom Joshua sent to spy out Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 16:34; Acts 19:13; Numbers 5:19–21"> And Joshua adjures &#91;them&#93; at that time, saying, “Cursed &#91;is&#93; the man before YHWH who raises up and has built this city, &#91;even&#93; Jericho; he lays its foundation in his firstborn, and he sets up its doors in his youngest.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 9:3; Joshua 1:5; Matthew 28:20"> And YHWH is with Joshua, and his fame is in all the land.</span>
