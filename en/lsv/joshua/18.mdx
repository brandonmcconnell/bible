<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 19:51; 1 Samuel 1:3; Joshua 21:2"> And all the congregation of the sons of Israel is assembled &#91;at&#93; Shiloh, and they cause the Tent of Meeting to dwell there, and the land has been subdued before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And there are seven tribes left among the sons of Israel who have not shared their inheritance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 18:9; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 10:4"> and Joshua says to the sons of Israel, “Until when are you remiss to go in to possess the land which He, YHWH, God of your fathers, has given to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 18:6; Joshua 4:2; Joshua 18:3"> Give three men from you for &#91;each&#93; tribe, and I send them, and they rise and go up and down through the land, and describe it according to their inheritance, and come to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 16:1–10; Joshua 19:1–9; Joshua 15:1–12"> and they have divided it into seven portions—Judah stays by its border on the south, and the house of Joseph stays by their border on the north—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 14:2; Joshua 18:10; Acts 13:19"> and you describe the land &#91;in&#93; seven portions, and have brought &#91;it&#93; to me here, and I have cast a lot for you here before our God YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 13:33; Joshua 13:8–31; Numbers 18:20"> for there is no portion for the Levites in your midst, for the priesthood of YHWH &#91;is&#93; their inheritance, and Gad, and Reuben, and half of the tribe of Manasseh received their inheritance beyond the Jordan eastward, which Moses, servant of YHWH, gave to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 18:10; Romans 14:19; 1 Samuel 14:41"> And the men rise and go; and Joshua commands those who are going to describe the land, saying, “Go, and walk up and down through the land, and describe it, and return to me, and here I cast a lot for you before YHWH in Shiloh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 13:19"> And the men go, and pass over through the land, and describe it by cities, in seven portions, on a scroll, and they come to Joshua, to the camp &#91;at&#93; Shiloh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 1:12; Acts 13:19; Joshua 18:6"> And Joshua casts a lot for them in Shiloh before YHWH, and there Joshua apportions the land to the sons of Israel, according to their divisions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 16:1–10; Joshua 15:1–8; Deuteronomy 10:1–22"> And a lot goes up &#91;for&#93; the tribe of the sons of Benjamin, for their families; and the border of their lot goes out between the sons of Judah and the sons of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 16:1; Joshua 7:2; Joshua 2:1"> And the border is for them at the north side from the Jordan, and the border has gone up to the side of Jericho on the north, and gone up through the hill-country westward, and its outgoings have been at the wilderness of Beth-Aven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Joshua 16:2–3; Joshua 10:11"> and the border has gone over there to Luz, to the side of Luz (it &#91;is&#93; Beth-El) southward, and the border has gone down &#91;to&#93; Atroth-Addar, by the hill that &#91;is&#93; on the south of the lower Beth-Horon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 15:9; 1 Chronicles 13:5–6; 1 Samuel 7:1–2"> and the border has been marked out, and has gone around to the corner of the sea southward, from the hill which &#91;is&#93; at the front of Beth-Horon southward, and its outgoings have been to Kirjath-Ba‘al (it &#91;is&#93; Kirjath-Jearim), a city of the sons of Judah: this &#91;is&#93; the west quarter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 15:9"> And the south quarter &#91;is&#93; from the end of Kirjath-Jearim, and the border has gone out westward, and has gone out to the fountain of the waters of Nephtoah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 23:10; Joshua 15:7–8; Isaiah 30:33"> and the border has come down to the extremity of the hill which &#91;is&#93; on the front of the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, which &#91;is&#93; in the Valley of the Rephaim northward, and has gone down the Valley of Hinnom to the side of Jebusi southward, and gone down &#91;to&#93; En-Rogel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 15:6"> and has been marked out on the north, and gone out to En-Shemesh, and gone out to Geliloth, which &#91;is&#93; opposite the ascent of Adummim, and gone down &#91;to&#93; the stone of Bohan son of Reuben,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 15:6; Joshua 15:61"> and passed over to the side in front of the Arabah northward, and gone down to Arabah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Joshua 15:2; Numbers 34:3"> and the border has passed over to the side of Beth-Hoglah northward, and the outgoings of the border have been to the north bay of the Salt Sea, to the south extremity of the Jordan; this &#91;is&#93; the south border;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> and the Jordan borders it at the east quarter; this &#91;is&#93; the inheritance of the sons of Benjamin, by its borders all around, for their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 18:12; Joshua 6:1; Joshua 18:19"> And the cities for the tribe of the sons of Benjamin, for their families, have been Jericho, and Beth-Hoglah, and the Valley of Keziz,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 15:6; 1 Kings 12:29–32; Joshua 18:18"> and Beth-Arabah, Zemaraim, and Beth-El,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:17"> and Avim, and Parah, and Ophrah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra 2:26; Joshua 21:17; Isaiah 10:29"> and Chephar-Haammonai, and Ophni, and Gaba; twelve cities and their villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 9:17; 1 Kings 3:4–5; Joshua 10:2"> Gibeon, and Ramah, and Beeroth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Judges 10:17; Ezra 2:25; Joshua 9:17"> and Mizpeh, and Chephirah, and Mozah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and Rekem, and Irpeel, and Taralah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:14; Joshua 15:8; Judges 20:4–5"> and Zelah, Eleph, and Jebusi (it &#91;is&#93; Jerusalem), Gibeath, Kirjath; fourteen cities and their villages. This &#91;is&#93; the inheritance of the sons of Benjamin, for their families.</span>
