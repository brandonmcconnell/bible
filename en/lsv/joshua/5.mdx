<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 2:9–11; Numbers 13:29; 1 Kings 10:5"> And it comes to pass, when all the kings of the Amorite which &#91;are&#93; beyond the Jordan, toward the sea, and all the kings of the Canaanite which &#91;are&#93; by the sea, hear how that YHWH has dried up the waters of the Jordan at the presence of the sons of Israel until their passing over, that their heart is melted, and there has not been anymore spirit in them because of the presence of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:6; Deuteronomy 10:16; Exodus 4:25"> At that time YHWH said to Joshua, “Make knives of flint for yourself, and return, circumcise the sons of Israel a second time”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; Genesis 17:23–27"> and Joshua makes knives of flint for himself, and circumcises the sons of Israel at the height of the foreskins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; 1 Corinthians 10:5; Deuteronomy 2:16"> And this &#91;is&#93; the thing &#91;for&#93; which Joshua circumcises &#91;them&#93;: all the people who are coming out of Egypt, who are males, all the men of war have died in the wilderness, in the way, in their coming out of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:8–9; Hosea 6:6–7; Matthew 12:7"> for all the people who are coming out were circumcised, and all the people who &#91;are&#93; born in the wilderness, in the way, in their coming out from Egypt, they have not circumcised;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:23; Deuteronomy 2:14; Hebrews 3:11"> for forty years the sons of Israel have gone in the wilderness, until all the nation of the men of war who are coming out of Egypt, who did not listen to the voice of YHWH, to whom YHWH has sworn not to show them the land which YHWH swore to their fathers to give to us, a land flowing with milk and honey, are consumed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 14:31; Deuteronomy 1:39"> and He raised up their sons in their stead, Joshua has circumcised them, for they have been uncircumcised, for they have not circumcised them in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 34:25"> And it comes to pass, when all the nation has completed to be circumcised, that they abide in their places in the camp until their recovering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 4:19; 1 Samuel 17:26; Genesis 34:14"> and YHWH says to Joshua, “Today I have rolled the reproach of Egypt from off you”; and &#91;one&#93; calls the name of that place Gilgal to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 12:6; Numbers 9:1–5; Ezekiel 12:3"> And the sons of Israel encamp in Gilgal, and make the Passover on the fourteenth day of the month, at evening, in the plains of Jericho;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 23:14; Exodus 13:6–7; Leviticus 23:6"> and they eat of the old grain of the land on the next day of the Passover, unleavened things and roasted &#91;grain&#93;, on this very same day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 16:35; Isaiah 65:13–14; Proverbs 13:22"> and the manna ceases on the next day in their eating of the old grain of the land, and there has been no more manna for &#91;the&#93; sons of Israel, and they eat of the increase of the land of Canaan in that year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 18:2; Numbers 22:23; Numbers 22:31"> And it comes to pass in Joshua’s being by Jericho, that he lifts up his eyes, and looks, and behold, &#91;there is&#93; one standing in front of him, and his drawn sword &#91;is&#93; in his hand, and Joshua goes to him, and says to him, “Are you for us or for our adversaries?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 55:4; Genesis 17:3; Revelation 19:10–14"> And He says, “No, for I &#91;am&#93; Prince of YHWH’s host; now I have come”; and Joshua falls on his face to the earth, and pays respect, and says to Him, “What is my Lord speaking to His servant?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 3:5; Acts 7:32–33; 2 Peter 1:18"> And the Prince of YHWH’s host says to Joshua, “Cast off your shoe from off your foot, for the place on which you are standing is holy”; and Joshua does so.</span>
