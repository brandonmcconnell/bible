<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 21:20–26; Exodus 6:16–25; 1 Chronicles 6:66–70"> and for the sons of Kohath who are left, out of the families of the tribe of Ephraim, and out of the tribe of Dan, and out of the half-tribe of Manasseh, ten cities by lot.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:65; Joshua 21:13–18"> And they give out of the tribe of the sons of Judah, and out of the tribe of the sons of Simeon, these cities which are called by name;</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:55; Luke 1:39; 2 Samuel 5:1–5"> and they give to them the city of Arba father of Anak (it &#91;is&#93; Hebron), in the hill-country of Judah, and its outskirts around it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 14:13–15; 1 Chronicles 6:55–57"> and they have given the field of the city and its villages to Caleb son of Jephunneh for his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 15:42; Joshua 10:29; Joshua 15:54"> And to the sons of Aaron the priest they have given the city of refuge &#91;for&#93; the manslayer, Hebron and its outskirts, and Libnah and its outskirts,</span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> all the cities &#91;are&#93; ten and their outskirts, for the families of the sons of Kohath who are left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:71; Deuteronomy 1:4; Joshua 20:8"> And for the sons of Gershon, of the families of the Levites, out of the half-tribe of Manasseh, the city of refuge &#91;for&#93; the manslayer, Golan in Bashan and its outskirts, and Beeshterah and its outskirts—two cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 19:12; 1 Chronicles 6:72–73"> And out of the tribe of Issachar, Kishon and its outskirts, Dabarath and its outskirts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 10:23; Joshua 12:11; Joshua 10:3"> Jarmuth and its outskirts, En-Gannim and its outskirts—four cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:74–75; Joshua 19:25–28"> And out of the tribe of Asher, Mishal and its outskirts, Abdon and its outskirts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 18:21; 1 Chronicles 6:75; Judges 1:31"> Helkath and its outskirts, and Rehob and its outskirts—four cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:76; Joshua 20:7; Joshua 19:37"> And out of the tribe of Naphtali, the city of refuge &#91;for&#93; the manslayer, Kedesh in Galilee and its outskirts, and Hammoth-Dor and its outskirts, and Kartan and its outskirts—three cities;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> all the cities of the Gershonite, for their families, &#91;are&#93; thirteen cities and their outskirts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:77; Joshua 19:11; Joshua 21:7"> And for the families of the sons of Merari, the Levites, who are left, &#91;are,&#93; out of the tribe of Zebulun, Jokneam and its outskirts, Kartah and its outskirts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Dimnah and its outskirts, Nahalal and its outskirts—four cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 20:8; Deuteronomy 4:43; Numbers 21:23"> And out of the tribe of Reuben, Bezer and its outskirts, and Jahazah and its outskirts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Kedemoth and its outskirts, and Mephaath and its outskirts—four cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 32:2; 1 Kings 22:3; 1 Chronicles 6:80"> And out of the tribe of Gad, the city of refuge &#91;for&#93; the manslayer, Ramoth in Gilead and its outskirts, and Mahanaim and its outskirts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 16:8–9; Numbers 32:35; Numbers 32:1"> Heshbon and its outskirts, Jazer and its outskirts—four cities &#91;in&#93; all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> All the cities for the sons of Merari, for their families, who are left of the families of the Levites—their lot is twelve cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 35:1–8; Deuteronomy 33:10; Genesis 49:7"> All the cities of the Levites in the midst of the possession of the sons of Israel &#91;are&#93; forty-eight cities, and their outskirts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> These cities are each city and its outskirts around it; so to all these cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Psalms 106:42–45; Exodus 23:27–31"> And YHWH gives to Israel the whole of the land which He has sworn to give to their fathers, and they possess it, and dwell in it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Joshua 11:23; Hebrews 4:9; Deuteronomy 31:3–5"> and YHWH gives rest to them all around, according to all that which He has sworn to their fathers, and there has not stood a man in their presence of all their enemies, YHWH has given the whole of their enemies into their hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 8:56; Joshua 23:14–15; Numbers 23:19"> there has not fallen a thing of all the good thing which YHWH spoke to the house of Israel—the whole has come.</span>
