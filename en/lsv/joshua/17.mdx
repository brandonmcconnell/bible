<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:18; Numbers 26:29–32; Judges 6:11"> And there is &#91;a lot&#93; for the sons of Manasseh who are left, for their families; for the sons of Abiezer, and for the sons of Helek, and for the sons of Asriel, and for the sons of Shechem, and for the sons of Hepher, and for the sons of Shemida; these &#91;are&#93; the children of Manasseh son of Joseph—the males—by their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 27:1; Numbers 26:33; Numbers 36:2–11"> As for Zelophehad, son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, he has no children except daughters, and these &#91;are&#93; the names of his daughters: Mahlah, and Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Numbers 27:5–7; Galatians 3:28"> and they draw near before Eleazar the priest, and before Joshua son of Nun, and before the princes, saying, “YHWH commanded Moses to give an inheritance to us in the midst of our brothers”; and he gives to them, at the command of YHWH, an inheritance in the midst of the brothers of their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 17:2–3; Numbers 32:30–42; Joshua 13:29–31"> And ten portions fall &#91;to&#93; Manasseh, apart from the land of Gilead and Bashan, which &#91;are&#93; beyond the Jordan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> for the daughters of Manasseh have inherited an inheritance in the midst of his sons, and the land of Gilead has been for the sons of Manasseh who are left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:21; Joshua 20:7; Joshua 24:32"> And the border of Manasseh is from Asher to Michmethah, which &#91;is&#93; on the front of Shechem, and the border has gone on to the right, to the inhabitants of En-Tappuah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 16:8; Joshua 15:34; Joshua 12:17"> The land of Tappuah has been for Manasseh, and Tappuah to the border of Manasseh for the sons of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 16:3; Joshua 19:29; Joshua 16:8–9"> And the border has come down &#91;to&#93; the Brook of Kanah, southward of the brook; these cities of Ephraim &#91;are&#93; in the midst of the cities of Manasseh, and the border of Manasseh &#91;is&#93; on the north of the brook, and its outgoings are at the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Southward &#91;is&#93; for Ephraim and northward for Manasseh, and the sea is his border, and in Asher they meet on the north, and in Issachar on the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:29; 2 Kings 9:27; Judges 5:19"> And in Issachar and in Asher, Manasseh has Beth-Shean and its towns, and Ibleam and its towns, and the inhabitants of Dor and its towns, and the inhabitants of En-Dor and its towns, and the inhabitants of Taanach and its towns, and the inhabitants of Megiddo and its towns—three counties.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 1:27–28; Exodus 23:29–33; Romans 6:12–14"> And the sons of Manasseh have not been able to occupy these cities, and the Canaanite is desirous to dwell in this land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; Philippians 4:13; Judges 1:35"> and it comes to pass, when the sons of Israel have been strong, that they put the Canaanite to forced labor, and have not utterly dispossessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 26:34–37; Genesis 48:19; Genesis 48:22"> And the sons of Joseph speak with Joshua, saying, “Why have you given an inheritance to me—one lot and one portion, and I &#91;am&#93; a numerous people? YHWH has blessed me until now.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 13:7; Genesis 14:5; Luke 12:48"> And Joshua says to them, “If you &#91;are&#93; a numerous people, go up to the forest for yourself, then you have created &#91;a place&#93; for yourself there in the land of the Perizzite and of the Rephaim, when Mount Ephraim has been narrow for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 4:12; Judges 4:3; Judges 1:19"> And the sons of Joseph say, “The mountain is not enough for us, and a chariot of iron &#91;is&#93; with every Canaanite who is dwelling in the land of the valley—to him who &#91;is&#93; in Beth-Shean and its towns, and to him who &#91;is&#93; in the Valley of Jezreel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Joshua speaks to the house of Joseph, to Ephraim and to Manasseh, saying, “You &#91;are&#93; a numerous people, and have great power; you do not have &#91;only&#93; one lot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Romans 8:31; Numbers 14:6–9"> because the mountain is yours; because it &#91;is&#93; a forest—you have created it, and its outgoings have been yours; because you dispossess the Canaanite, though it has chariots of iron—though it &#91;is&#93; strong.”</span>
