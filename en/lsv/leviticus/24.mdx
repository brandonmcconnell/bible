<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:6; Ephesians 5:8–14; Exodus 27:20–21"> “Command the sons of Israel, and they bring pure oil of beaten olive to you for the lamp, to cause a light to go up continually;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Aaron arranges it at the outside of the veil of the Testimony, in the Tent of Meeting, from evening until morning before YHWH continually—a continuous statute throughout your generations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 31:8; Exodus 37:17–24; Exodus 39:37"> he arranges the lights on the pure lampstand before YHWH continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; 1 Kings 18:31; Exodus 40:23"> And you have taken flour, and have baked twelve cakes with it, two-tenth parts are in one cake,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 7:48; Exodus 25:23–24; 2 Chronicles 4:19"> and you have set them &#91;in&#93; two rows (six in the row) on the pure table before YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Revelation 8:3–4; Genesis 9:16"> and you have put pure frankincense on the row, and it has been with the bread for a memorial, a fire-offering to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4; Numbers 4:7; 1 Chronicles 9:32"> On each Sabbath day he arranges it before YHWH continually, from the sons of Israel—a perpetual covenant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 8:31; Luke 6:4; Mark 2:26"> and it has been for Aaron and for his sons, and they have eaten it in the holy place, for it &#91;is&#93; most holy to him, from the fire-offerings of YHWH—a continuous statute.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 11:4; Exodus 12:38"> And a son of an Israeli woman goes out (and he &#91;is&#93; son of an Egyptian man) in the midst of the sons of Israel, and the son of the Israeli woman and a man of Israel strive in the camp,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Leviticus 24:15–16; Exodus 3:15"> and the son of the Israeli woman execrates the Name and reviles; and they bring him to Moses; and his mother’s name &#91;is&#93; Shelomith daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 18:15–16; Numbers 15:34; Numbers 27:5"> and he causes him to rest in confinement —to explain to them by the mouth of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:7; Leviticus 20:27; Deuteronomy 21:21"> “Bring out the reviler to the outside of the camp; and all those hearing have laid their hands on his head, and all the congregation has stoned him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 9:13; Exodus 22:28; Leviticus 5:1"> And you speak to the sons of Israel, saying, When any man reviles his God—then he has borne his sin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 12:31; James 2:7; John 10:33–36"> and he who is execrating the Name of YHWH is certainly put to death; all the congregation certainly casts stones at him; as a sojourner so a native, in his execrating the Name, he is put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:5–6; Deuteronomy 19:11–12; Deuteronomy 27:24"> And when a man strikes any soul of man, he is certainly put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 24:21; Exodus 21:34–36"> And he who strikes a beast repays it, body for body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:38; Matthew 7:2; Deuteronomy 19:21"> And when a man puts a blemish in his fellow, as he has done so it is done to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Matthew 5:38; Exodus 21:23–25"> breach for breach, eye for eye, tooth for tooth; as he puts a blemish in a man so it is done in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17–18; Exodus 21:33"> And he who strikes a beast repays it, and he who strikes &#91;the life of&#93; man is put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 12:49; Numbers 15:15–16; Numbers 9:14"> One judgment is for you; as a sojourner so is a native; for I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28–29; Hebrews 2:2–3; Numbers 15:35–36"> And Moses speaks to the sons of Israel, and they bring out the reviler to the outside of the camp, and stone him &#91;with&#93; stone; and the sons of Israel have done as YHWH has commanded Moses.</span>
