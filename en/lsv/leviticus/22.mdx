<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Deuteronomy 15:19; Leviticus 22:32"> “Speak to Aaron and to his sons, and they are separated from the holy things of the sons of Israel, and they do not defile My holy Name in what they are hallowing to Me; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 7:20–21; Numbers 19:13; Psalms 16:11"> Say to them: Throughout your generations, any man who draws near, out of all your seed, to the holy things which the sons of Israel sanctify to YHWH, and his uncleanness &#91;is&#93; on him—indeed, that person has been cut off from before My face; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 11:39; Leviticus 15:2–3; Leviticus 15:13–16"> Any man of the seed of Aaron, and he is leprous or has discharging—he does not eat of the holy things until he is clean; and he who is coming against any uncleanness of a person, or a man whose seed &#91;from&#93; intercourse goes out from him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 11:43–44; Leviticus 15:7; Leviticus 15:19"> or a man who comes against any teeming thing which is unclean to him, or against a man who is unclean to him, even any of his uncleanness—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Hebrews 10:22; Haggai 2:13"> the person who comes against it has even been unclean until the evening, and does not eat of the holy things, but has bathed his flesh with water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 21:22; Deuteronomy 18:3–4; 1 Corinthians 9:13–14"> and the sun has gone in, and he has been clean, and afterward he eats of the holy things, for it &#91;is&#93; his food;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 17:15; Exodus 22:31; Ezekiel 44:31"> he does not eat a carcass or torn thing, to become unclean with it; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 28:43; Numbers 18:22; Numbers 18:32"> And they have kept My charge, and bear no sin for it, that they have died for it when they defile it; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH sanctifying them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 12:4; 1 Samuel 21:6; Exodus 29:33"> And no stranger eats of the holy thing; a settler &#91;with&#93; a priest and a hired worker does not eat of the holy thing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:13; Exodus 12:44; Numbers 18:11–13"> but when a priest buys a person, the purchase of his money, he eats of it, also one born in his house; they eat of his bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13; Leviticus 21:3"> And a priest’s daughter, when she is a strange man’s, she does not eat of the raised-offering of the holy things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 10:14; Genesis 38:11; Numbers 18:11–19"> but a priest’s daughter, when she is a widow, or cast out, and has no seed, and has turned back to the house of her father, as &#91;in&#93; her youth, she eats of her father’s bread; but no stranger eats of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 27:15; Leviticus 27:13; Leviticus 5:15–19"> And when a man eats of a holy thing through ignorance, then he has added its fifth part to it, and has given &#91;it&#93; to the priest, with the holy thing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 18:32; Leviticus 19:8; Ezekiel 22:26"> and they do not defile the holy things of the sons of Israel—that which they lift up to YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 22:9; 1 Peter 2:24; Isaiah 53:11–12"> or have caused them to bear the iniquity of the guilt-offering in their eating their holy things; for I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, sanctifying them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 1:2; Deuteronomy 12:6; Numbers 15:3"> “Speak to Aaron, and to his sons, and to all the sons of Israel, and you have said to them: Any man of the house of Israel, or of the sojourners in Israel, who brings his offering near, of all his vows, or of all his willing offerings which they bring near to YHWH for a burnt-offering—</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 1:10; Leviticus 1:3; Ephesians 5:27"> &#91;you bring near&#93; at your pleasure a perfect one, a male of the herd, of the sheep or of the goats;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:1; Malachi 1:8; Deuteronomy 15:21"> nothing in which &#91;is&#93; blemish do you bring near, for it is not for a pleasing thing for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 15:8; Numbers 15:3; Leviticus 3:6"> And when a man brings a sacrifice of peace-offerings near to YHWH, to complete a special vow, or for a willing-offering, of the herd or of the flock, it is perfect for a pleasing thing: no blemish is in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 22:20; Malachi 1:8; Leviticus 1:9"> Blind, or broken, or maimed, or &#91;having&#93; an oozing sore &#91;&#91;or a defect of the eye&#93;&#93;, or itch, or scab—you do not bring these near to YHWH, and you do not make a fire-offering from them on the altar to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 21:18"> As for an ox or sheep &#91;that&#93; is deformed or stunted—you make it a willing-offering, but it is not pleasing for a vow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 22:20; Deuteronomy 23:1; Leviticus 21:20"> As for bruised, or beaten, or torn, or cut—you do not bring &#91;it&#93; near to YHWH; and in your land you do not do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 21:8; Ezra 6:8–10; Numbers 15:14–16"> And you do not bring the bread of your God near from the hand of a son of a stranger, from any of these, for their corruption &#91;is&#93; in them; blemish &#91;is&#93; in them; they are not pleasing for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 22:30; Leviticus 19:23–24; Leviticus 22:25"> “When ox, or lamb, or goat is born, and it has been under its mother &#91;for&#93; seven days, then from the eighth day and from now on, it is pleasing for an offering, a fire-offering to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:6–7; Deuteronomy 14:21; Exodus 34:26"> but an ox or sheep—you do not slaughter it and its young one in one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 116:17; Psalms 107:22; Amos 4:5"> And when you sacrifice a sacrifice of thanksgiving to YHWH, you sacrifice at your pleasure;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 7:15–18; Exodus 16:19–20; Leviticus 19:7"> it is eaten on that day—you do not leave of it until morning; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Leviticus 19:37; Numbers 15:40"> And you have kept my commands and have done them; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Luke 11:2; Matthew 6:9"> and you do not defile My holy Name, and I have been hallowed in the midst of the sons of Israel; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, sanctifying you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 11:45; Exodus 6:7; Leviticus 25:38"> who am bringing you up out of the land of Egypt, to become your God; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.”</span>
