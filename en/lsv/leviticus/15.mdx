<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Leviticus 13:1; Leviticus 11:1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses and to Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; 2 Samuel 3:29; Leviticus 22:4"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, and you have said to them: When there is discharging out of the flesh of any man, he &#91;is&#93; unclean &#91;from&#93; his discharge;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:20; Ezekiel 16:26; Leviticus 12:3"> and this is his uncleanness in his discharge—his flesh has run with his discharge, or his flesh has stopped from his discharge; it &#91;is&#93; his uncleanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Ephesians 5:11; 1 Corinthians 15:33"> Every bed on which he who has the discharging lies is unclean, and every vessel on which he sits is unclean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 16:26; Leviticus 17:15"> and anyone who comes against his bed washes his garments, and has bathed with water, and been unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 1:16"> And he who is sitting on the vessel on which he sits who has the discharging, washes his garments, and has bathed with water, and been unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And he who is coming against the flesh of him who has the discharging, washes his garments, and has bathed with water, and been unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:4; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Isaiah 1:16"> And when he who has the discharging spits on him who is clean, then he has washed his garments, and has bathed with water, and been unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:34"> And all the saddle on which he who has the discharging rides is unclean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:8; Psalms 26:6; Leviticus 15:8"> and anyone who is coming against anything which is under him is unclean until the evening, and he who is carrying them washes his garments, and has bathed with water, and been unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And anyone against whom he who has the discharging comes (and has not rinsed his hands with water) has even washed his garments, and bathed with water, and been unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 11:32–33; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> And the earthen vessel which he who has the discharging comes against is broken; and every wooden vessel is rinsed with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 14:8; Leviticus 8:33; Leviticus 15:28"> And when he who has the discharging is clean from his discharge, then he has numbered seven days for himself for his cleansing, and has washed his garments, and has bathed his flesh with running water, and been clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 12:8; Leviticus 12:6; Hebrews 10:10"> And on the eighth day he takes two turtle-doves or two young pigeons for himself, and has come in before YHWH to the opening of the Tent of Meeting, and has given them to the priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 14:30–31; Leviticus 4:26; Matthew 3:17"> and the priest has made them, one a sin-offering and one a burnt-offering; and the priest has made atonement for him before YHWH, because of his discharge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:10–11; Leviticus 22:4; Leviticus 15:5"> And when a man’s seed &#91;from&#93; intercourse goes out from him, then he has bathed all his flesh with water, and been unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And any garment, or any skin on which there is seed &#91;from&#93; intercourse, has also been washed with water, and been unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–5; 1 Corinthians 6:12"> And a woman with whom a man lies with seed &#91;from&#93; intercourse, they also have bathed with water, and been unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 12:2; Matthew 15:19; Mark 5:25"> And when a woman has discharging—blood is her discharge in her flesh—she is in her separation &#91;for&#93; seven days, and anyone who is coming against her is unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 15:4–9; Proverbs 2:16–19; Proverbs 6:35"> And anything on which she lies in her separation is unclean, and anything on which she sits is unclean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 7:14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Isaiah 22:14"> and anyone who is coming against her bed washes his garments, and has bathed with water, and been unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And anyone who is coming against any vessel on which she sits washes his garments, and has washed with water, and been unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And if it &#91;is&#93; on the bed, or on the vessel on which she is sitting, in his coming against it, he is unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 20:18; 1 Peter 2:11; Leviticus 18:19"> And if a man really lies with her, and her separation is on him, then he has been unclean &#91;for&#93; seven days, and all the bed on which he lies is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 5:25; Matthew 9:20; Luke 8:43"> And when a woman’s discharge of blood flows many days within the time of her separation, or when it flows over her separation—all the days of the discharge of her uncleanness are as the days of her separation; she &#91;is&#93; unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Every bed on which she lies all the days of her discharge is as the bed of her separation to her, and every vessel on which she sits is unclean as the uncleanness of her separation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 15:21; Hebrews 10:22; Leviticus 17:15–16"> and anyone who is coming against them is unclean, and has washed his garments, and has bathed with water, and been unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 15:13–15; Galatians 3:13; Ephesians 1:6–7"> And if she has been clean from her discharge, then she has numbered seven days for herself, and afterward she is clean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> and on the eighth day she takes two turtle-doves or two young pigeons for herself, and has brought them to the priest, to the opening of the Tent of Meeting;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and the priest has made one a sin-offering and one a burnt-offering, and the priest has made atonement for her before YHWH, because of the discharge of her uncleanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 19:20; Numbers 19:13; Ezekiel 5:11"> And you have separated the sons of Israel from their uncleanness, and they do not die in their uncleanness, in their defiling My Dwelling Place which &#91;is&#93; in their midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 11:46; Leviticus 13:59; Numbers 6:13"> This &#91;is&#93; the law of him who has the discharging, and of him whose seed &#91;from&#93; intercourse goes out from him, to become unclean with it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 15:19; Leviticus 20:18; Leviticus 15:24–25"> and of her who is sick in her separation, and of him who has the discharging (his discharge, of the male or of the female), and of a man who lies with an unclean woman.”</span>
