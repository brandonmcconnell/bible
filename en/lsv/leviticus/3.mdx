<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:13; Leviticus 4:8–9; Exodus 29:22"> And he has brought near from the sacrifice of the peace-offerings a fire-offering to YHWH, the fat which is covering the innards, and all the fat which &#91;is&#93; on the innards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 3:10; Leviticus 8:25; Exodus 29:22"> and the two kidneys, and the fat which &#91;is&#93; on them, which &#91;is&#93; on the flanks, and the redundance on the liver above the kidneys—he turns it aside,</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:11–12; Galatians 3:28; Leviticus 1:2"> And if his offering &#91;is&#93; out of the flock for a sacrifice of peace-offerings to YHWH, male or female, he brings a perfect one near;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 8:62; Ephesians 5:12; Leviticus 17:8–9"> if he is bringing a sheep near &#91;for&#93; his offering, then he has brought it near before YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 1:5; Ephesians 3:12; Leviticus 3:13"> and has laid his hand on the head of his offering, and has slaughtered it before the Tent of Meeting, and sons of Aaron have sprinkled its blood around the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 29:22; Leviticus 9:19; Leviticus 8:25"> And he has brought near from the sacrifice of the peace-offerings a fire-offering to YHWH, its fat, the whole fat tail close by the bone—he turns it aside, and the fat which is covering the innards, and all the fat which &#91;is&#93; on the innards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 3:4"> and the two kidneys, and the fat which &#91;is&#93; on them, which &#91;is&#93; on the flanks, and the redundance on the liver above the kidneys—he turns it aside,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 21:17; Leviticus 21:6; Leviticus 3:16"> and the priest has made it an incense on the altar &#91;as&#93; bread of a fire-offering to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 9:15; Isaiah 53:2; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> And if his offering &#91;is&#93; a goat, then he has brought it near before YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 3:8; Romans 5:15–21; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> and has laid his hand on its head, and has slaughtered it before the Tent of Meeting, and sons of Aaron have sprinkled its blood around the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 26:38; Matthew 22:37; Leviticus 3:9–11"> and he has brought his offering near from it, a fire-offering to YHWH, the fat which is covering the innards, and all the fat which &#91;is&#93; on the innards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and the two kidneys, and the fat which &#91;is&#93; on them, which &#91;is&#93; on the flanks, and the redundance on the liver above the kidneys—he turns it aside,</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:4; Matthew 26:28; Deuteronomy 15:23"> &#91;This is&#93; a continuous statute throughout your generations in all your dwellings: you do not eat any fat or any blood.”</span>
