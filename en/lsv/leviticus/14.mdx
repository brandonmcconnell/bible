<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:14; Luke 5:12–14; Mark 1:40–44"> “This is a law of the leper, in the day of his cleansing, that he has been brought to the priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 13:46; Luke 7:22; Luke 17:15–19"> and the priest has gone out to the outside of the camp, and the priest has seen, and behold, the plague of leprosy has ceased from the leper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 19:6; Leviticus 14:6; Hebrews 9:19"> and the priest has commanded, and he has taken for him who is to be cleansed, two clean living birds, and cedar wood, and scarlet, and hyssop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 14:50; Hebrews 2:14; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> And the priest has commanded, and he has slaughtered one bird on an earthen vessel, over running water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:5; Zechariah 13:1; Philippians 2:9–11"> &#91;as for&#93; the living bird, he takes it, and the cedar wood, and the scarlet, and the hyssop, and has dipped them and the living bird in the blood of the slaughtered bird, over the running water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 5:14; Ezekiel 36:25; Isaiah 52:15"> and he has sprinkled on him who is to be cleansed from the leprosy seven times, and has pronounced him clean, and has sent out the living bird over the face of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Numbers 8:7; 1 Peter 3:21"> And he who is to be cleansed has washed his garments, and has shaved all his hair, and has bathed with water, and has been clean, and afterward he comes into the camp, and has dwelt at the outside of his tent &#91;for&#93; seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 6:9; Leviticus 14:8; Numbers 8:7"> And it has been, on the seventh day—he shaves all his hair, his head, and his beard, and his eyebrows, even all his hair he shaves, and he has washed his garments, and has bathed his flesh with water, and has been clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Matthew 8:4; Luke 5:14"> And on the eighth day he takes two lambs, perfect ones, and one ewe-lamb, daughter of a year, a perfect one, and three-tenth parts of flour &#91;for&#93; a present, mixed with oil, and one log of oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 5:26–27; Exodus 29:1–4; Numbers 8:21"> And the priest who is cleansing has caused the man who is to be cleansed to stand with them before YHWH, at the opening of the Tent of Meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 29:24; Leviticus 6:6–7; Leviticus 8:27–29"> and the priest has taken one male lamb, and has brought it near for a guilt-offering, also the log of oil, and has waved them &#91;as&#93; a wave-offering before YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 29:11; Leviticus 1:11; Leviticus 4:24"> And he has slaughtered the lamb in the place where he slaughters the sin-offering and the burnt-offering, in the holy place; for like the sin-offering, the guilt-offering is for the priest; it &#91;is&#93; most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 29:20; Leviticus 8:23–24; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> And the priest has taken of the blood of the guilt-offering, and the priest has put &#91;it&#93; on the tip of the right ear of him who is to be cleansed, and on the thumb of his right hand, and on the great toe of his right foot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 45:7; John 3:34; 1 John 2:20"> and the priest has taken of the log of oil, and has poured &#91;it&#93; on the left palm of the priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 4:17; 1 Corinthians 10:31; Leviticus 4:6"> and the priest has dipped his right finger in the oil which &#91;is&#93; on his left palm, and has sprinkled of the oil with his finger seven times before YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 1:2; Ezekiel 36:27; Leviticus 14:14"> And of the remainder of the oil which &#91;is&#93; on his palm, the priest puts &#91;it&#93; on the tip of the right ear of him who is to be cleansed, and on the thumb of his right hand, and on the great toe of his right foot, on the blood of the guilt-offering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 4:26; 2 Corinthians 1:21–22; Ephesians 1:17–18"> and the remnant of the oil which &#91;is&#93; on the palm of the priest, he puts &#91;it&#93; on the head of him who is to be cleansed, and the priest has made atonement for him before YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 14:12; Leviticus 5:1; Leviticus 12:6–8"> And the priest has made the sin-offering, and has made atonement for him who is to be cleansed from his uncleanness, and afterward he slaughters the burnt-offering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 14:10; Ephesians 5:2; Leviticus 14:8"> and the priest has caused the burnt-offering to ascend, also the present, on the altar, and the priest has made atonement for him, and he has been clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 5:7; Leviticus 12:8; Proverbs 17:5"> And if he &#91;is&#93; poor, and his hand is not reaching &#91;these things&#93;, then he has taken one lamb &#91;for&#93; a guilt-offering, for a wave-offering, to make atonement for him, and one-tenth part of flour mixed with oil for a present, and a log of oil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:16; Isaiah 59:11; Jeremiah 48:28"> and two turtle-doves, or two young pigeons, which his hand reaches to, and one has been a sin-offering and one a burnt-offering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 14:10–11"> and he has brought them in on the eighth day for his cleansing to the priest, to the opening of the Tent of Meeting, before YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 14:12; Leviticus 14:10"> And the priest has taken the lamb of the guilt-offering, and the log of oil, and the priest has waved them &#91;as&#93; a wave-offering before YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:1; Psalms 40:6; Leviticus 14:14–20"> and he has slaughtered the lamb of the guilt-offering, and the priest has taken of the blood of the guilt-offering, and has put &#91;it&#93; on the tip of the right ear of him who is to be cleansed, and on the thumb of his right hand, and on the great toe of his right foot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and the priest pours of the oil on the left palm of the priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and the priest has sprinkled with his right finger of the oil which &#91;is&#93; on his left palm, seven times before YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the priest has put of the oil which &#91;is&#93; on his palm, on the tip of the right ear of him who is to be cleansed, and on the thumb of his right hand, and on the great toe of his right foot, on the place of the blood of the guilt-offering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 14:18; 1 John 2:1–2; Exodus 30:15–16"> and he puts the remnant of the oil which &#91;is&#93; on the palm of the priest on the head of him who is to be cleansed, to make atonement for him, before YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 14:22; Romans 8:3; Leviticus 12:8"> And he has made one of the turtle-doves or of the young pigeons (from that which his hand reaches to,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 14:18; Leviticus 5:7"> &#91;even&#93; that which his hand reaches to), one a sin-offering and one a burnt-offering, besides the present, and the priest has made atonement for him who is to be cleansed before YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 14:10; Leviticus 14:21; Matthew 11:5"> This &#91;is&#93; a law of him in whom &#91;is&#93; a plague of leprosy, whose hand does not reach to his cleansing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And YHWH speaks to Moses and to Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:49; Genesis 17:8; Numbers 32:22"> “When you come into the land of Canaan, which I am giving to you for a possession, and I have put a plague of leprosy in a house &#91;in&#93; the land of your possession,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 91:10; Deuteronomy 7:26; Zechariah 5:4"> then he whose the house &#91;is&#93; has come in and declared &#91;it&#93; to the priest, saying, Some plague has appeared to me in the house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Hebrews 12:15; 2 Timothy 2:17–18"> and the priest has commanded, and they have prepared the house before the priest comes in to see the plague (that all which &#91;is&#93; in the house is not unclean), and afterward the priest comes in to see the house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 13:42; Leviticus 13:3; Leviticus 13:19–20"> and he has seen the plague, and behold, the plague &#91;is&#93; in the walls of the house, hollow streaks, greenish or reddish, and their appearance &#91;is&#93; lower than the wall,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 13:50"> and the priest has gone out of the house to the opening of the house, and has shut up the house &#91;for&#93; seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Leviticus 13:36; Leviticus 13:27; Leviticus 13:22"> And the priest has turned back on the seventh day, and has seen, and behold, the plague has spread in the walls of the house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 2:20; Titus 3:10; 1 Corinthians 5:5–6"> and the priest has commanded, and they have drawn out the stones in which the plague &#91;is&#93;, and have cast them to the outside of the city, to an unclean place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 65:4; Revelation 22:15; Matthew 24:51"> and he causes the house to be scraped all around inside, and they have poured out the clay which they have scraped off, at the outside of the city, at an unclean place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:2; 1 Timothy 5:21–22; 2 Chronicles 17:7–9"> and they have taken other stones, and brought &#91;them&#93; to the place of the stones, and he takes other clay and has coated the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:28–30; Jude 1:12; 2 Peter 2:20"> And if the plague returns, and has broken out in the house, after he has drawn out the stones, and after the scraping of the house, and after the coating,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Leviticus 13:51–52; Zechariah 5:4"> then the priest has come in and seen, and behold, the plague has spread in the house; it &#91;is&#93; a fretting leprosy in the house; it &#91;is&#93; unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Kings 10:27; Matthew 24:2; 1 Kings 9:6–9"> And he has broken down the house, its stones, and its wood, and all the clay of the house, and he has brought &#91;them&#93; forth to the outside of the city, to an unclean place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 19:21–22; Leviticus 22:6; Leviticus 11:28"> And he who is going into the house all the days he has shut it up, is unclean until the evening;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Leviticus 14:8–9; Leviticus 11:25"> and he who is lying in the house washes his garments; and he who is eating in the house washes his garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; Job 5:18; Mark 5:29"> And if the priest certainly comes in, and has seen, and behold, the plague has not spread in the house after the coating of the house, then the priest has pronounced the house clean, for the plague has been healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Leviticus 14:4"> And he has taken two birds, and cedar wood, and scarlet, and hyssop for the cleansing of the house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> and he has slaughtered one bird on an earthen vessel, over running water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 51:7"> and he has taken the cedar wood, and the hyssop, and the scarlet, and the living bird, and has dipped them in the blood of the slaughtered bird, and in the running water, and has sprinkled against the house seven times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And he has cleansed the house with the blood of the bird, and with the running water, and with the living bird, and with the cedar wood, and with the hyssop, and with the scarlet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Leviticus 14:20; Leviticus 14:7"> and he has sent the living bird away to the outside of the city to the face of the field, and has made atonement for the house, and it has been clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Leviticus 15:32; Leviticus 14:32; Leviticus 6:9"> This &#91;is&#93; the law for every plague of the leprosy and for scale,</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Leviticus 13:47–59"> and for leprosy of a garment, and of a house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Leviticus 13:2"> and for a rising, and for a scab, and for a bright spot—</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Ezekiel 44:23; Jeremiah 15:19"> to direct in the day of being unclean, and in the day of being clean; this &#91;is&#93; the law of the leprosy.”</span>
