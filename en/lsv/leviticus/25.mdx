<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 1:1; Galatians 4:24–25; Exodus 19:1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, in Mount Sinai, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:21; Leviticus 26:34–35; Exodus 23:10"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, and you have said to them: When you come into the land which I am giving to you, then the land has kept a Sabbath to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 23:10"> &#91;For&#93; six years you sow your field, and &#91;for&#93; six years you prune your vineyard, and have gathered its increase,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:21; Leviticus 25:20–23; Exodus 23:10–11"> and in the seventh year is a Sabbath of rest for the land, a Sabbath to YHWH; you do not sow your field, and you do not prune your vineyard;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 19:29; Isaiah 37:30"> you do not reap the spontaneous growth of your harvest, and you do not gather the grapes of your separated thing; it is a year of rest for the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 2:44; Exodus 23:11; Acts 4:34–35"> And the Sabbath &#91;increase&#93; of the land has been for you for food, to you, and to your manservant, and to your handmaid, and to your hired worker, and to your settler, who are sojourning with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and for your livestock, and for the beast which &#91;is&#93; in your land, is all its increase for &#91;them&#93; to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 23:15; Genesis 2:2"> And you have numbered seven Sabbaths of years for yourself, seven times seven years, and the days of the seven Sabbaths of years have been forty-nine years for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:24; Leviticus 23:27; 1 Thessalonians 1:8"> and you have caused a horn of shouting to pass over in the seventh month, on the tenth of the month; in the Day of Atonements you cause a horn to pass over through all your land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:8; Isaiah 63:4; 1 Peter 2:16"> and you have hallowed the year, the fiftieth year; and you have proclaimed liberty in the land to all its inhabitants; it is a Jubilee for you; and you have turned back each to his possession; indeed, you return each to his family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 27:17; Leviticus 25:4–5"> It &#91;is&#93; a Jubilee, the fiftieth year, it is a year for you; you do not sow, nor reap its spontaneous growth, nor gather its separated things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 25:6–7"> for it &#91;is&#93; a Jubilee—it is holy to you; you eat its increase from the field;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Leviticus 27:17–24; Numbers 36:4"> you return each to his possession in this Year of the Jubilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Leviticus 25:17; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> And when you sell anything to your fellow, or buy from the hand of your fellow, you do not oppress one another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 27:18–23; Philippians 4:5"> you buy from your fellow by the number of years after the Jubilee; he sells to you by the number of the years of increase;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 25:27; Leviticus 25:51"> you multiply its price according to the multitude of the years, and you diminish its price according to the fewness of the years; for &#91;it is&#93; a number of increases &#91;that&#93; he is selling to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 19:14; Leviticus 19:32; Leviticus 25:43"> and you do not oppress one another, and you have been afraid of your God; for I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:6; Deuteronomy 12:10; Proverbs 1:33"> And you have done My statutes, and you keep My judgments, and have done them, and you have dwelt on the land confidently,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:26; Psalms 85:12; Leviticus 26:5"> and the land has given its fruit, and you have eaten to satiety, and have dwelt confidently on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 25:4; Luke 12:29; Numbers 11:4"> And when you say, What do we eat in the seventh year, behold, do we not sow, nor gather our increase?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:8; Proverbs 10:22; 2 Corinthians 9:10"> Then I have commanded My blessing on you in the sixth year, and it has made the increase for three years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 19:29; Leviticus 26:10; Isaiah 37:30"> and you have sown &#91;in&#93; the eighth year, and have eaten of the old increase; you eat the old until the ninth year, until the coming in of its increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Psalms 39:12; Ezekiel 48:14"> And the land is not sold—to extinction, for the land &#91;is&#93; Mine, for you &#91;are&#93; sojourners and settlers with Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Leviticus 25:51–53; Leviticus 25:27"> and in all the land of your possession you give a redemption for the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ruth 2:20; Jeremiah 32:7–8; Ruth 3:12"> When your brother becomes poor, and has sold his possession, then his redeemer who is near to him has come, and he has redeemed the sold thing of his brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 5:7"> and when a man has no redeemer, and his own hand has attained &#91;means&#93;, and he has found &#91;it&#93; as sufficient &#91;for&#93; its redemption,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> then he has reckoned the years of its sale, and has given back that which is over to the man to whom he sold &#91;it&#93;, and he has returned to his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 35:9–10; Leviticus 25:10; 1 Peter 1:4–5"> And if his hand has not found sufficiency to give back to him, then his sold thing has been in the hand of him who buys it until the Year of Jubilee; and it has gone out in the Jubilee, and he has returned to his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And when a man sells a dwelling-house &#91;in&#93; a walled city, then his right of redemption has been until the completion of a year from its selling; his right of redemption is &#91;during these&#93; days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and if it is not redeemed to him until the fullness of a perfect year, then the house which &#91;is&#93; in a walled city has been established to extinction to him buying it, throughout his generations; it does not go out in the Jubilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–8"> And a house of the villages which have no surrounding wall is reckoned on the field of the country; there is redemption for it, and it goes out in the Jubilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 21:1–45; Numbers 35:1–8"> As for cities of the Levites—houses of the cities of their possession—continuous redemption is for the Levites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 18:20–24; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> as for him who redeems from the Levites, then the sale of a house (and &#91;in&#93; the city of his possession) has gone out in the Jubilee, for the houses of the cities of the Levites are their possession in the midst of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 25:23; Acts 4:36–37; Numbers 35:2–5"> And a field, a outskirt of their cities, is not sold; for it &#91;is&#93; a continuous possession for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:7–8; Acts 11:29; Leviticus 25:25"> And when your brother has become poor, and his hand has failed with you, then you have kept hold on him, sojourner and settler, and he has lived with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Ezekiel 18:8"> you take no usury or increase from him; and you have been afraid of your God; and your brother has lived with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> you do not give your money to him in usury, and you do not give your food for increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 11:45; Numbers 15:41; Exodus 20:2"> I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH, who has brought you out of the land of Egypt to give the land of Canaan to you, to become your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 21:2; 1 Kings 9:22; 2 Kings 4:1"> And when your brother becomes poor with you, and he has been sold to you, you do not lay servile service on him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 21:2–3"> he is as a hired worker &#91;and&#93; as a settler with you; he serves with you until the Year of the Jubilee—</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 25:28; Exodus 21:3; John 8:32"> then he has gone out from you, he and his sons with him, and has turned back to his family; and he turns back to the possession of his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Leviticus 25:55; Romans 6:22; 1 Corinthians 7:21–23"> For they &#91;are&#93; My servants, whom I have brought out from the land of Egypt: they are not sold &#91;with&#93; the sale of a servant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Colossians 4:1; Leviticus 25:46; Exodus 1:13–14"> you do not rule over him with rigor, and you have been afraid of your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 12:44; Psalms 2:8–9; Isaiah 14:1–2"> And your manservant and your handmaid whom you have &#91;are&#93; from the nations who &#91;are&#93; around you; you buy manservant and handmaid from them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Isaiah 56:3–6"> and also from the sons of the settlers who are sojourning with you, you buy from them, and from their families who &#91;are&#93; with you, which they have begotten in your land, and they have been to you for a possession;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 14:2; Leviticus 25:43"> and you have taken them for an inheritance for your sons after you, to inherit &#91;for&#93; a possession; you lay service on them for all time, but on your brothers, the sons of Israel, each with his brother—you do not rule over him with rigor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="James 2:5; Leviticus 25:26; 1 Samuel 2:7–8"> And when the hand of a sojourner or settler with you attains &#91;riches&#93;, and your brother with him has become poor, and he has been sold to a sojourner, a settler with you, or to the root of the family of a sojourner,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; Hebrews 2:11–13; Leviticus 25:35"> after he has been sold, there is a right of redemption for him; one of his brothers redeems him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Leviticus 25:26"> or his uncle or a son of his uncle redeems him, or any of the relations of his flesh, of his family, redeems him, or &#91;if&#93; his own hand has attained &#91;means&#93; then he has been redeemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Isaiah 16:14; Isaiah 21:16; Leviticus 25:53"> And he has reckoned with his buyer from the year of his being sold to him until the Year of Jubilee, and the money of his sale has been by the number of years; it is with him as the days of a hired worker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> If many years still &#91;remain&#93;, he gives back his redemption &#91;money&#93; according to them, from the money of his purchase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And if few are left of the years until the Year of Jubilee, then he has reckoned with him &#91;and&#93; he gives back his redemption &#91;money&#93; according to his years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Leviticus 25:46; Leviticus 25:43"> as a hired worker, year by year, he is with him, and he does not rule him with rigor before your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 21:2–3; Leviticus 25:40–41; Isaiah 49:9"> And if he is not redeemed in these &#91;years&#93;, then he has gone out in the Year of Jubilee, he and his sons with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Leviticus 25:42; Isaiah 43:3; Exodus 13:3"> For the sons of Israel &#91;are&#93; servants to Me; they &#91;are&#93; My servants whom I have brought out of the land of Egypt; I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH.”</span>
