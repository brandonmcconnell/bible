<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:4; Leviticus 23:37; Numbers 29:39"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, and you have said to them: Appointed times of YHWH, which you proclaim &#91;as&#93; holy convocations, these &#91;are&#93; My appointed times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 23:12; Exodus 35:2–3"> &#91;For&#93; six days work is done, and in the seventh day &#91;is&#93; a Sabbath of rest, a holy convocation; you do no work; it &#91;is&#93; a Sabbath to YHWH in all your dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 23:2; Exodus 23:14; Leviticus 23:37"> These &#91;are&#93; appointed times of YHWH, holy convocations, which you proclaim in their appointed times:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:10; Deuteronomy 16:1–8; Numbers 28:16–17"> in the first month, on the fourteenth of the month, between the evenings, &#91;is&#93; the Passover to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 34:18; Deuteronomy 16:8; Exodus 12:15–16"> and on the fifteenth day of this month &#91;is&#93; the Celebration of Unleavened Things to YHWH; &#91;for&#93; seven days you eat unleavened things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 28:18–25; Leviticus 23:8; Leviticus 23:21"> on the first day you have a holy convocation, you do no servile work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and you have brought a fire-offering near to YHWH &#91;for&#93; seven days; in the seventh day &#91;is&#93; a holy convocation; you do no servile work.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 34:26; Exodus 23:19; Deuteronomy 16:9"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, and you have said to them: When you come into the land which I am giving to you, and have reaped its harvest, and have brought in the sheaf, the beginning of your harvest to the priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 29:24; Leviticus 10:14; Leviticus 9:21"> then he has waved the sheaf before YHWH for your acceptance; on the next day of the Sabbath the priest waves it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:10–12; 1 Peter 1:19; Leviticus 1:10"> And you have prepared a lamb, a perfect one, a son of a year, in the day of your waving the sheaf for a burnt-offering to YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 2:14–16; Joel 1:9; Numbers 28:10"> and its present &#91;is&#93; two-tenth parts of flour mixed with oil, a fire-offering to YHWH, a refreshing fragrance, and its drink-offering &#91;is&#93; a fourth of the hin of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 34:26; Genesis 4:4–5; Leviticus 3:17"> And you do not eat bread and roasted grain and full ears until this very day, until your bringing in the offering of your God—a continuous statute throughout your generations, in all your dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10–11; Leviticus 25:8; Deuteronomy 16:9–10"> And you have numbered for yourselves from the day after the Sabbath, from the day of your bringing in the sheaf of the wave-offering: they are seven perfect Sabbaths;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:1; Numbers 28:26"> you number fifty days to the day after the seventh Sabbath, and you have brought a new present near to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10; Exodus 34:26; Exodus 34:22"> you bring in two &#91;loaves&#93; of bread out of your dwellings &#91;for&#93; a wave-offering; they are of two-tenth parts of flour; they are baked &#91;with&#93; yeast—first-&#91;fruits&#93; to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 28:27–31; Leviticus 23:12–13; Malachi 1:13–14"> And you have brought near, besides the bread, seven lambs, perfect ones, sons of a year, and one bullock, a son of the herd, and two rams; they are a burnt-offering to YHWH, with their present and their drink-offerings, a fire-offering of refreshing fragrance to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 28:30; Leviticus 4:23–28; Leviticus 7:11–18"> And you have prepared one kid of the goats for a sin-offering, and two lambs, sons of a year, for a sacrifice of peace-offerings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:4; Ephesians 2:14; Numbers 18:8–12"> and the priest has waved them, besides the bread of the first-&#91;fruits&#93;, &#91;as&#93; a wave-offering before YHWH, besides the two lambs; they are holy to YHWH for the priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 23:2; Leviticus 23:4; Numbers 18:23"> and you have proclaimed in this very day &#91;that&#93; it is a holy convocation for yourselves, you do no servile work—a continuous statute in all your dwellings, throughout your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:19–21; Leviticus 19:9–10; Isaiah 58:10"> And in your reaping the harvest of your land you do not complete the corner of your field in your reaping, and you do not gather the gleaning of your harvest, you leave them for the poor and for the sojourner; I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:52; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; Leviticus 25:9"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, In the seventh month, on the first of the month, you have a Sabbath, a memorial of shouting, a holy convocation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:21"> you do no servile work, and you have brought a fire-offering near to YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 16:29–31; Exodus 30:10; Leviticus 16:24"> “Only—on the tenth of this seventh month is the Day of Atonements; you have a holy convocation, and you have humbled yourselves, and have brought a fire-offering near to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Zechariah 3:9; Leviticus 16:34; Hebrews 10:10"> and you do no work in this very day, for it is a day of atonements, to make atonement for you, before your God YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 17:14; Numbers 5:2; Leviticus 23:27"> For any person who is not humbled in this very day has even been cut off from his people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 20:3; Zephaniah 2:5; Jeremiah 15:7"> and any person who does any work in this very day, I have even destroyed that person from the midst of his people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> you do no work—a continuous statute throughout your generations in all your dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 16:31; Matthew 11:28–30; Psalms 69:10–11"> It &#91;is&#93; a Sabbath of rest for yourselves, and you have humbled yourselves in the ninth of the month at evening; you keep your Sabbath from evening until evening.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 7:2; Nehemiah 8:14; Ezra 3:4"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, In the fifteenth day of this seventh month &#91;is the&#93; Celebration of Shelters &#91;for&#93; seven days to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 23:24–25; Leviticus 23:7–8"> the first day &#91;is&#93; a holy convocation, you do no servile work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:18; John 7:37; 2 Chronicles 7:8–11"> &#91;for&#93; seven days you bring a fire-offering near to YHWH, on the eighth day you have a holy convocation, and you have brought a fire-offering near to YHWH; it &#91;is&#93; a restraint, you do no servile work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 23:2; Leviticus 23:4; Ecclesiastes 3:1"> These &#91;are&#93; appointed times of YHWH, which you proclaim &#91;as&#93; holy convocations, to bring a fire-offering near to YHWH, a burnt-offering, and a present, a sacrifice, and drink-offerings, a thing of a day in its day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 29:39; Leviticus 19:3; Ezra 2:68–69"> apart from the Sabbaths of YHWH, and apart from your gifts, and apart from all your vows, and apart from all your willing-offerings, which you give to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Deuteronomy 16:13; Leviticus 23:34"> Only—on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, in your gathering the increase of the land, you keep the celebration of YHWH &#91;for&#93; seven days; on the first day &#91;is&#93; a Sabbath, and on the eighth day a Sabbath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 4:4; Revelation 7:9; Deuteronomy 16:14–15"> and you have taken for yourselves on the first day the fruit of beautiful trees, branches of palms, and boughs of thick trees, and willows of a brook, and have rejoiced before your God YHWH &#91;for&#93; seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 29:12; Nehemiah 8:18"> And you have kept it &#91;as&#93; a celebration to YHWH, seven days in a year—a continuous statute throughout your generations; in the seventh month you keep it &#91;as&#93; a celebration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:14–17; Genesis 33:17; Numbers 24:5"> You dwell in shelters &#91;for&#93; seven days; all who are natives in Israel dwell in shelters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:10–13; Psalms 78:5–6; Exodus 13:14"> so that your generations know that I caused the sons of Israel to dwell in shelters in My bringing them out of the land of Egypt; I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Leviticus 23:1–2; Matthew 18:20; Leviticus 21:24"> And Moses speaks to the sons of Israel &#91;concerning&#93; the appointed times of YHWH.</span>
