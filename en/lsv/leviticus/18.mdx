<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Leviticus 11:44; Ezekiel 20:5"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, and you have said to them: I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Exodus 23:24; 1 Peter 4:2–4"> you do not do according to the work of the land of Egypt in which you have dwelt, and you do not do according to the work of the land of Canaan to where I am bringing you in, and you do not walk in their statutes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 18:2; Leviticus 18:26; Ezekiel 20:19"> You do My judgments and you keep My statutes, to walk in them; I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Galatians 3:12"> And you have kept My statutes and My judgments which man does and lives in them; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17–21; Leviticus 18:7–19; Leviticus 20:11–12"> None of you draws near to any relation of his flesh to uncover nakedness; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; Ezekiel 22:10; Leviticus 18:8–16"> You do not uncover the nakedness of your father and the nakedness of your mother, she &#91;is&#93; your mother; you do not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; 1 Corinthians 5:1; Deuteronomy 27:20"> You do not uncover the nakedness of the wife of your father; it &#91;is&#93; the nakedness of your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Deuteronomy 27:22; Ezekiel 22:11"> The nakedness of your sister, daughter of your father, or daughter of your mother, born at home or born outside—you do not uncover their nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The nakedness of your son’s daughter, or of your daughter’s daughter—you do not uncover their nakedness; for theirs &#91;is&#93; your nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The nakedness of a daughter of your father’s wife, begotten of your father, she &#91;is&#93; your sister—you do not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 20:19; Exodus 6:20"> You do not uncover the nakedness of a sister of your father; she &#91;is&#93; a relation of your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> You do not uncover the nakedness of your mother’s sister, for she &#91;is&#93; your mother’s relation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20"> You do not uncover the nakedness of your father’s brother; you do not draw near to his wife; she &#91;is&#93; your aunt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 20:12; Genesis 38:26; Ezekiel 22:11"> You do not uncover the nakedness of your daughter-in-law; she &#91;is&#93; your son’s wife; you do not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Leviticus 20:21; Matthew 22:24"> You do not uncover the nakedness of your brother’s wife; it &#91;is&#93; your brother’s nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Amos 2:7; Deuteronomy 27:23"> You do not uncover the nakedness of a woman and her daughter, nor do you take her son’s daughter and her daughter’s daughter, to uncover her nakedness; they &#91;are&#93; her relations; it &#91;is&#93; wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachi 2:15; Genesis 30:15; Genesis 29:28"> And you do not take a woman &#91;in addition&#93; to her sister, to be an adversary, to uncover her nakedness beside her, in her life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 20:18; Leviticus 15:24; Ezekiel 22:10"> And you do not draw near to a woman in the separation of her uncleanness to uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; 1 Corinthians 6:9; Hebrews 13:4"> And you do not give your seed &#91;from&#93; intercourse to the wife of your fellow, to become unclean with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:31; Leviticus 20:2–5; Deuteronomy 18:10"> And you do not give from your seed to pass over to the Molech, nor do you defile the Name of your God; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:13; Romans 1:26–27; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> And you do not lie with a male as one lies with a woman; it &#91;is&#93; an abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15–16; Leviticus 20:12"> And you do not commit your intercourse with any beast, to become unclean with it; and a woman does not stand before a beast to mate with it; it &#91;is&#93; perversion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:12; Leviticus 18:30; Leviticus 20:22–23"> Do not defile yourselves with all these, for with all these the nations have been defiled which I am sending away from before you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 18:28; Leviticus 20:22–23; Jeremiah 2:7"> and the land is defiled, and I charge its iniquity on it, and the land vomits out its inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Leviticus 17:10; Leviticus 18:30"> And you have kept My statutes and My judgments, and do not do &#91;any&#93; of all these abominations, the native and the sojourner who is sojourning in your midst,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:14; Leviticus 18:24; Deuteronomy 23:18"> for the men of the land who &#91;are&#93; before you have done all these abominations and the land is defiled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 3:16; Leviticus 20:22; Leviticus 18:25"> and the land does not vomit you out in your defiling it, as it has vomited out the nation which &#91;is&#93; before you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:6; Exodus 12:15; Leviticus 17:10"> for anyone who does &#91;any&#93; of all these abominations—even the persons who are doing &#91;so&#93;—have been cut off from the midst of their people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Deuteronomy 18:9–12; Leviticus 22:9"> and you have kept My charge, so as not to do &#91;any&#93; of the abominable statutes which have been done before you, and you do not defile yourselves with them; I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH.”</span>
