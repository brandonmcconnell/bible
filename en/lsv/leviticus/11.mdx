<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses and to Aaron, saying to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 15:11; Leviticus 11:11; Hebrews 9:10"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, This &#91;is&#93; the beast which you eat out of all the beasts which &#91;are&#93; on the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:10; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Deuteronomy 6:6–7"> any dividing a hoof, and cleaving the cleft of the hooves, bringing up the cud, among the beasts—you eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Only, this you do not eat, of those bringing up the cud, and of those dividing the hoof: the camel, though it is bringing up the cud, yet the hoof is not dividing—it &#91;is&#93; unclean to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 104:18; Proverbs 30:26; Matthew 7:26"> and the hyrax, though it is bringing up the cud, yet the hoof is not divided—it &#91;is&#93; unclean to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:7"> and the hare, though it is bringing up the cud, yet the hoof has not divided—it &#91;is&#93; unclean to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 66:17; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> and the sow, though it is dividing the hoof, and cleaving the cleft of the hoof, yet it does not bring up the cud—it &#91;is&#93; unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Colossians 2:16; Hebrews 9:10"> You do not eat of their flesh, and you do not come against their carcass—they &#91;are&#93; unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; James 2:18; Acts 20:21"> This you eat of all which &#91;are&#93; in the waters: anything that has fins and scales in the waters, in the seas, and in the brooks, them you eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 29:27; Leviticus 7:18; Psalms 139:21–22"> and anything that does not have fins and scales in the seas, and in the brooks, of any teeming creature of the waters, and of any creature which lives, which &#91;is&#93; in the waters—they &#91;are&#93; an abomination to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> indeed, they are an abomination to you; you do not eat of their flesh, and you detest their carcass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Anything that does not have fins and scales in the waters—it &#91;is&#93; an abomination to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 39:27–30; Jeremiah 4:22; Titus 3:3"> And these you detest of the bird—they are not eaten, they &#91;are&#93; an abomination: the eagle, and the bearded vulture, and the osprey,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and the kite, and the falcon after its kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 12:24; 1 Kings 17:6; Proverbs 30:17"> every raven after its kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 34:11–15; Ephesians 5:7–11; Ephesians 2:2–3"> and the ostrich, and the nightjar &#91;&#91;or male ostrich&#93;&#93;, and the seagull, and the hawk after its kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and the little owl, and the cormorant, and the great owl,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and the waterhen, and the pelican, and the Egyptian vulture,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and the stork, the heron after its kind, and the hoopoe, and the bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Philippians 3:18–19; Leviticus 11:23"> Every teeming creature which is flying, which is going on four &#91;legs&#93;—it &#91;is&#93; an abomination to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Only, this you eat of any teeming thing which is flying, which is going on four, which has legs above its feet, to move with them on the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; Mark 1:6; Exodus 10:4–5"> these of them you eat: the locust after its kind, and the bald locust after its kind, and the cricket after its kind, and the grasshopper after its kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and every teeming thing which is flying, which has four feet—it &#91;is&#93; an abomination to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Ephesians 2:1–3; 2 Corinthians 6:17"> And you are made unclean by these; anyone who is coming against their carcass is unclean until the evening;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Leviticus 14:8; Numbers 31:24"> and anyone who is lifting up of their carcass washes his garments, and has been unclean until the evening—</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> even every beast which is dividing the hoof, and is not cloven-footed, and is not bringing up the cud—they &#91;are&#93; unclean to you; anyone who is coming against them is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 11:23; Leviticus 11:20"> And anything going on its paws, among all the beasts which are going on four—they &#91;are&#93; unclean to you; anyone who is coming against their carcass is unclean until the evening;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25"> and he who is lifting up their carcass washes his garments, and has been unclean until the evening—they &#91;are&#93; unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 11:41–42; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Psalms 10:3"> And this &#91;is&#93; the unclean to you among the teeming things which are teeming on the earth: the weasel, and the muroid, and the tortoise &#91;&#91;or large lizard&#93;&#93; after its kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and the gecko &#91;&#91;or ferret&#93;&#93;, and the chameleon, and the lizard, and the snail, and the mole;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25; Leviticus 11:8"> these &#91;are&#93; the unclean to you among all which are teeming; anyone who is coming against them in their death is unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Titus 3:5; Titus 2:14"> And anything on which anyone of them falls, in their death, is unclean, of any vessel of wood or garment or skin or sack, any vessel in which work is done is brought into water, and has been unclean until the evening, then it has been clean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 14:45"> and any earthen vessel, into the midst of which &#91;any&#93; one of them falls, all that &#91;is&#93; in its midst is unclean, and you break it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Proverbs 21:4; Proverbs 21:27"> Of all the food which is eaten, that on which comes &#91;such&#93; water, is unclean, and all drink which is drunk in any &#91;such&#93; vessel is unclean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 11:33"> and anything on which &#91;any&#93; of their carcass falls is unclean; oven or double pots, it is broken down, they &#91;are&#93; unclean, indeed, they are unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; John 4:14"> Only, a fountain or pit, a collection of water, is clean, but that which is coming against their carcass is unclean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:37; 1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:18"> and when &#91;any&#93; of their carcass falls on any sown seed which is sown—it &#91;is&#93; clean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and when water is put on the seed, and &#91;any&#93; of its carcass has fallen on it—it &#91;is&#93; unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Leviticus 11:40; Leviticus 11:24; Leviticus 15:7"> And when any of the beasts which are for food for you dies, he who is coming against its carcass is unclean until the evening;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Leviticus 22:8; Ezekiel 44:31; Deuteronomy 14:21"> and he who is eating of its carcass washes his garments, and has been unclean until the evening; and he who is lifting up its carcass washes his garments, and has been unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 11:29; Leviticus 11:20; Leviticus 11:23"> And every teeming thing which is teeming on the earth is an abomination, it is not eaten;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Titus 1:12; Isaiah 65:25; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> anything going on the belly, and any going on four, to every multiplier of feet, to every teeming thing which is teeming on the earth—you do not eat them, for they &#91;are&#93; an abomination;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 20:25; Leviticus 11:41–42"> you do not make yourselves abominable with any teeming thing which is teeming, nor do you make yourselves unclean with them, so that you have been unclean with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Leviticus 19:2"> For I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH, and you have sanctified yourselves, and you have been holy, for I &#91;am&#93; holy; and you do not defile your persons with any teeming thing which is creeping on the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Exodus 20:2; Exodus 6:7"> for I &#91;am&#93; YHWH who am bringing you up out of the land of Egypt to become your God; and you have been holy, for I &#91;am&#93; holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Leviticus 14:54; Leviticus 15:32; Ezekiel 43:12"> This &#91;is&#93; a law of the beasts, and of the bird, and of every living creature which is moving in the waters, and of every creature which is teeming on the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Ezekiel 44:23; Romans 14:13–23"> to make separation between the unclean and the pure, and between the beast that is eaten, and the beast that is not eaten.”</span>
