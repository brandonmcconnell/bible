<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 19:12; Hebrews 9:7; Leviticus 4:27"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, When a person sins through ignorance against any of the commands of YHWH &#91;regarding things&#93; which are not to be done, and has done &#91;something&#93; against one of these—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 7:27–28; Leviticus 4:14; Leviticus 9:2"> if the priest who is anointed sins according to the guilt of the people, then he has brought near for his sin which he has sinned a bullock, a son of the herd, a perfect one, to YHWH, for a sin-offering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 1:3–4; Daniel 9:26; Isaiah 53:6"> and he has brought in the bullock to the opening of the Tent of Meeting before YHWH, and has laid his hand on the head of the bullock, and has slaughtered the bullock before YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 16:14; Numbers 19:4; Leviticus 4:16–17"> And the priest who is anointed has taken of the blood of the bullock, and has brought it into the Tent of Meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 16:14; Leviticus 14:18; Leviticus 26:28"> and the priest has dipped his finger in the blood, and sprinkled of the blood seven times before YHWH, at the front of the veil of the holy place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 8:15; Leviticus 9:9; Leviticus 16:18"> and the priest has put of the blood on the horns of the altar of spice-incense before YHWH, which &#91;is&#93; in the Tent of Meeting, and he pours out all the blood of the bullock at the foundation of the altar of the burnt-offering, which &#91;is&#93; at the opening of the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 3:3–5; Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:31"> And he lifts up from it all the fat of the bullock of the sin-offering, the fat which is covering over the innards, and all the fat which &#91;is&#93; on the innards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 3:4"> and the two kidneys, and the fat which &#91;is&#93; on them, which &#91;is&#93; on the flanks, and the redundance on the liver above the kidneys—he turns it aside,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> as it is lifted up from the ox of the sacrifice of the peace-offerings; and the priest has made them an incense on the altar of the burnt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 19:5; Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 9:8–11"> And the skin of the bullock, and all its flesh, with its head, and with its legs, and its innards, and its dung—</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:11; Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 16:27"> he has even brought out the whole bullock to the outside of the camp, to a clean place, to the place of the pouring out of the ashes, and he has burned it on the wood with fire; it is burned beside the place of the pouring out of the ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 5:17; Leviticus 5:2–5; Hosea 5:15"> And if all the congregation of Israel errs ignorantly, and the thing has been hidden from the eyes of the assembly, and they have done &#91;something against&#93; one of all the commands of YHWH &#91;regarding things&#93; which are not to be done, and have been guilty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 4:23; Leviticus 4:3; Leviticus 4:28"> when the sin which they have sinned concerning it has been known, then the assembly has brought a bullock near, a son of the herd, for a sin-offering, and they have brought it in before the Tent of Meeting;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 1:4; Leviticus 16:21; Leviticus 8:14"> and the elderly of the congregation have laid their hands on the head of the bullock, before YHWH, and &#91;one&#93; has slaughtered the bullock before YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 4:5–12; Hebrews 9:12–14"> And the priest who is anointed has brought in of the blood of the bullock to the Tent of Meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 4:6–7"> and the priest has dipped his finger in the blood, and has sprinkled seven times before YHWH at the front of the veil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 4:7"> and he puts &#91;some&#93; of the blood on the horns of the altar which &#91;is&#93; before YHWH, which &#91;is&#93; in the Tent of Meeting; and he pours out all the blood at the foundation of the altar of the burnt-offering, which &#91;is&#93; at the opening of the Tent of Meeting;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 5:6; Leviticus 4:26; Numbers 15:25"> and he lifts up all its fat from it, and has made incense on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 15:25; Hebrews 10:10–12; Leviticus 12:8"> And he has done to the bullock as he has done to the bullock of the sin-offering, so he does to it; and the priest has made atonement for them, and it has been forgiven them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 16:15; Matthew 20:28; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> and he has brought out the bullock to the outside of the camp, and has burned it as he has burned the first bullock; it &#91;is&#93; a sin-offering of the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:2; Leviticus 4:13; Numbers 31:13"> When a prince sins, and has done &#91;something against&#93; one of all the commands of his God YHWH &#91;regarding things&#93; which are not to be done, through ignorance, and has been guilty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 4:14; Romans 8:3; Numbers 7:22"> or his sin wherein he has sinned has been made known to him, then he has brought in his offering, a kid of the goats, a male, a perfect one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 3:13; Leviticus 7:2; Leviticus 4:3–35"> and he has laid his hand on the head of the goat, and has slaughtered it in the place where he slaughters the burnt-offering before YHWH; it &#91;is&#93; a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 4:7; Leviticus 4:18; Leviticus 4:30"> And the priest has taken of the blood of the sin-offering with his finger, and has put &#91;it&#93; on the horns of the altar of the burnt-offering, and he pours out its blood at the foundation of the altar of the burnt-offering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 4:20; Leviticus 4:31; Leviticus 4:35"> and he makes incense on the altar with all its fat, as the fat of the sacrifice of the peace-offerings; and the priest has made atonement for him because of his sin, and it has been forgiven him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 4:2; Numbers 15:27; Leviticus 4:13"> And if any person of the people of the land sins through ignorance, by his doing &#91;something against&#93; one of the commands of YHWH &#91;regarding things&#93; which are not to be done, and has been guilty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 4:23; Leviticus 4:3; Leviticus 4:32"> or his sin which he has sinned has been made known to him, then he has brought in his offering, a kid of the goats, a perfect one, a female, for his sin which he has sinned,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 4:4; Leviticus 4:24; Leviticus 1:4"> and he has laid his hand on the head of the sin-offering, and has slaughtered the sin-offering in the place of the burnt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 4:7; Leviticus 4:25; Leviticus 4:34"> And the priest has taken of its blood with his finger, and has put &#91;it&#93; on the horns of the altar of the burnt-offering, and he pours out all its blood at the foundation of the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:35; Leviticus 1:9"> and he turns aside all its fat, as the fat has been turned aside from off the sacrifice of the peace-offerings, and the priest has made incense on the altar for refreshing fragrance to YHWH; and the priest has made atonement for him, and it has been forgiven him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 4:28; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 Peter 2:22"> And if he brings in a sheep &#91;for&#93; his offering, a female for a sin-offering, he brings in a perfect one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 4:29–31; Leviticus 4:4"> and he has laid his hand on the head of the sin-offering, and has slaughtered it for a sin-offering in the place where he slaughters the burnt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 4:30; Leviticus 4:25; Leviticus 4:7"> And the priest has taken of the blood of the sin-offering with his finger, and has put &#91;it&#93; on the horns of the altar of the burnt-offering, and he pours out all its blood at the foundation of the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:20; Leviticus 14:18"> and he turns aside all its fat, as the fat of the sheep is turned aside from the sacrifice of the peace-offerings, and the priest has made them an incense on the altar, according to the fire-offerings of YHWH, and the priest has made atonement for him, for his sin which he has sinned, and it has been forgiven him.”</span>
