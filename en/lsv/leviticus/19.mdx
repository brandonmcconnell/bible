<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44–45; Leviticus 20:7; 1 Peter 1:15–16"> “Speak to all the congregation of the sons of Israel, and you have said to them: You are holy, because I, your God YHWH, &#91;am&#93; holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Exodus 20:8; Hebrews 12:9"> You each fear his mother and his father, and you keep My Sabbaths; I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:1; Exodus 20:23; Psalms 115:4–7"> You do not turn to the idols, and you do not make a molten god for yourselves; I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 22:19; Leviticus 22:29; Leviticus 7:16"> And when you sacrifice a sacrifice of peace-offerings to YHWH, you sacrifice it at your pleasure;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 7:11–17"> it is eaten in the day of your sacrificing &#91;it&#93;, and on the next day, and that which is left to the third day is burned with fire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 7:18–21; Leviticus 22:25; Jeremiah 16:18"> and if it is really eaten on the third day, it &#91;is&#93; an abomination, it is not pleasing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 22:15; Leviticus 5:1"> and he who is eating it bears his iniquity, for he has defiled the holy thing of YHWH, and that person has been cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:22; Deuteronomy 24:19–22; Ruth 2:2"> And in your reaping the harvest of your land you do not completely reap the corner of your field, and you do not gather the gleaning of your harvest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:6; Leviticus 25:6; Isaiah 24:13"> and you do not glean your vineyard, even the omitted part of your vineyard you do not gather, you leave them for the poor and for the sojourner; I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 101:7; Ephesians 4:25; Colossians 3:9"> You do not steal, nor feign, nor lie—each against his fellow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Deuteronomy 5:11; Leviticus 18:21"> And you do not swear by My Name for falsehood, or you have defiled the Name of your God; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 5:4; Malachi 3:5; Deuteronomy 24:14–15"> You do not oppress your neighbor, nor take plunder; the wages of the hired worker do not remain with you until morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:18; Leviticus 19:32; Leviticus 25:17"> You do not revile the deaf; and you do not put a stumbling block before the blind; and you have been afraid of your God; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:17; Proverbs 24:23; Deuteronomy 27:19"> You do not do perversity in judgment; you do not lift up the face of the poor, nor honor the face of the great; you judge your fellow in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 23:1; Exodus 23:7; Proverbs 20:19"> You do not go slandering among your people; you do not stand against the blood of your neighbor; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 2:9; 1 John 2:11; Luke 17:3"> You do not hate your brother in your heart; you certainly reprove your fellow, and do not permit sin on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 22:39–40; Mark 12:31–34"> You do not take vengeance, nor watch the sons of your people; and you have had love for your neighbor as yourself; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Deuteronomy 22:9–11; Matthew 9:16–17"> You keep My statutes. You do not cause your livestock to mate &#91;with&#93; two kinds; you do not sow your field with two kinds; and a garment of two kinds, mixed material, does not go up on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:23–24; Exodus 21:20–21"> And when a man lies with a woman with seed &#91;from&#93; intercourse, and she &#91;is&#93; a maidservant, betrothed to a man, and not really ransomed, or freedom has not been given to her, there is an investigation; they are not put to death, for she &#91;is&#93; not free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 6:6–7; Leviticus 5:1–6; Leviticus 5:15"> And he has brought in his guilt-offering to YHWH, to the opening of the Tent of Meeting, a ram &#91;for&#93; a guilt-offering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:20"> and the priest has made atonement for him with the ram of the guilt-offering before YHWH, for his sin which he has sinned, and it has been forgiven him because of his sin which he has sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 22:27; Acts 7:51; Jeremiah 9:25–26"> And when you come into the land and have planted all &#91;kinds&#93; of trees &#91;for&#93; food, then you have reckoned its fruit as uncircumcised, it is uncircumcised to you &#91;for&#93; three years, it is not eaten,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9; Deuteronomy 18:4; Numbers 18:12–13"> and in the fourth year all its fruit is holy—praises for YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Haggai 1:9–11; Malachi 3:8–10; Leviticus 26:3–4"> And in the fifth year you eat its fruit—to add to you its increase; I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:10–14; 2 Kings 17:17; 2 Chronicles 33:6"> You do not eat with the blood. You do not enchant, nor observe clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Deuteronomy 14:1; Ezekiel 44:20"> You do not round the corner of your head, nor destroy the corner of your beard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Leviticus 21:5; 1 Kings 18:28"> And you do not put a cutting for the soul in your flesh; and a writing, a cross-mark, you do not put on yourself; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:17–18; Leviticus 21:7; Hosea 4:12–14"> You do not defile your daughter to cause her to go whoring, that the land does not go whoring, and the land has been full of wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 26:2; Leviticus 19:3; Ecclesiastes 5:1"> You keep My Sabbaths and you revere My sanctuary; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; Leviticus 20:27; Leviticus 20:6–7"> You do not turn to those having familiar spirits, and you do not seek for wizards, to become unclean by them; I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:1; Lamentations 5:12; Proverbs 16:31"> You rise up at the presence of grey hairs, and you have honored the presence of an old man, and have been afraid of your God; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 23:9; Exodus 22:21; Malachi 3:5"> And when a sojourner sojourns with you in your land, you do not oppress him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:19; Leviticus 19:18; Exodus 12:48–49"> the sojourner who is sojourning with you is to you as a native among you, and you have had love for him as for yourself, for you have been sojourners in the land of Egypt; I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:15; Deuteronomy 25:13; Proverbs 16:11"> You do not do perversity in the judgment, in the measure, in the weight, and in the liquid measure;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Exodus 20:2; Deuteronomy 25:13–15"> you have righteous balances, righteous weights, a righteous ephah, and a righteous hin; I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH, who has brought you out from the land of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:1; Leviticus 18:4–5; Deuteronomy 5:1"> and you have observed all my statutes, and all my judgments, and have done them; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.”</span>
