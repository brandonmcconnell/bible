<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 28:2–4; Exodus 39:41; Hebrews 7:27"> “Take Aaron and his sons with him, and the garments, and the anointing oil, and the bullock of the sin-offering, and the two rams, and the basket of unleavened things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:13; Acts 2:1; Nehemiah 8:1"> and assemble all the congregation to the opening of the Tent of Meeting.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 39:26; Exodus 39:7; Exodus 39:5"> And Moses does as YHWH has commanded him, and the congregation is assembled to the opening of the Tent of Meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 29:4–37"> and Moses says to the congregation, “This &#91;is&#93; the thing which YHWH has commanded to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; Ephesians 5:26; Exodus 29:4"> And Moses brings Aaron and his sons near, and bathes them with water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Isaiah 61:10; Galatians 3:27"> and puts the coat on him, and girds him with the girdle, and clothes him with the upper robe, and puts the ephod on him, and girds him with the girdle of the ephod, and binds &#91;it&#93; to him with it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 2:63; 1 Thessalonians 5:8; Isaiah 59:17"> and puts the breastplate on him, and puts the Lights and the Perfections into the breastplate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 2:9–11; Zechariah 6:11–14; Exodus 28:36–38"> and puts the turban on his head, and puts the golden flower of the holy crown on the turban, toward the front of its face, as YHWH has commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 40:9–11; Leviticus 8:2; Exodus 30:23–29"> And Moses takes the anointing oil, and anoints the Dwelling Place, and all that &#91;is&#93; in it, and sanctifies them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Titus 3:6; Exodus 30:29; Exodus 16:19"> and he sprinkles of it on the altar seven times, and anoints the altar, and all its vessels, and the laver, and its base, to sanctify them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 30:30; Psalms 133:2; Exodus 29:7"> and he pours of the anointing oil on the head of Aaron, and anoints him to sanctify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 29:8–9; Revelation 5:10; Revelation 1:6"> And Moses brings the sons of Aaron near, and clothes them &#91;with&#93; coats, and girds them &#91;with&#93; girdles, and binds caps to them, as YHWH has commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:19; Psalms 66:15; 1 Peter 3:18"> And he brings the bullock of the sin-offering near, and Aaron lays—his sons also—their hands on the head of the bullock of the sin-offering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 4:7; Hebrews 9:18–23; Ezekiel 43:19–27"> and &#91;one&#93; slaughters, and Moses takes the blood, and puts &#91;it&#93; around the horns of the altar with his finger, and cleanses the altar, and he has poured out the blood at the foundation of the altar, and sanctifies it, to make atonement on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 29:13; Leviticus 3:3–5; Leviticus 4:8–9"> And he takes all the fat that &#91;is&#93; on the innards, and the redundance on the liver, and the two kidneys, and their fat, and Moses makes incense on the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 4:11–12; Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 6:30"> and the bullock, and its skin, and its flesh, and its dung, he has burned with fire, at the outside of the camp, as YHWH has commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 8:2; Leviticus 1:4–13; Exodus 29:15–18"> And he brings the ram of the burnt-offering near, and Aaron and his sons lay their hands on the head of the ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and &#91;one&#93; slaughters, and Moses sprinkles the blood around the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 1:8"> and he has cut the ram into its pieces, and Moses makes incense with the head, and the pieces, and the fat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Exodus 29:18; Leviticus 1:17"> and he has washed the innards and the legs with water, and Moses makes incense with the whole ram on the altar; it &#91;is&#93; a burnt-offering for refreshing fragrance; it &#91;is&#93; a fire-offering to YHWH, as YHWH has commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 8:2; Leviticus 8:29; Exodus 29:19–31"> And he brings the second ram near, a ram of the consecrations, and Aaron and his sons lay their hands on the head of the ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 29:20; Leviticus 14:14; Romans 12:1"> and &#91;one&#93; slaughters, and Moses takes of its blood, and puts &#91;it&#93; on the tip of the right ear of Aaron, and on the thumb of his right hand, and on the great toe of his right foot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 9:18–22"> and he brings the sons of Aaron near, and Moses puts of the blood on the tip of their right ear, and on the thumb of their right hand, and on the great toe of their right foot. And Moses sprinkles the blood around the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 3:9; Leviticus 3:3–5; Isaiah 53:10"> and takes the fat, and the fat tail, and all the fat that &#91;is&#93; on the innards, and the redundance on the liver, and the two kidneys, and their fat, and the right leg;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 29:23; 1 Timothy 2:5; Acts 5:12"> and out of the basket of unleavened things, which &#91;is&#93; before YHWH, he has taken one unleavened cake, and one cake of oiled bread, and one thin cake, and puts &#91;them&#93; on the fat, and on the right leg;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:21; Hebrews 9:14; Exodus 29:24–37"> and he puts the whole on the hands of Aaron, and on the hands of his sons, and waves them &#91;as&#93; a wave-offering before YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 29:25; Psalms 22:13–14; Hebrews 10:14–22"> And Moses takes them from off their hands, and makes incense on the altar, on the burnt-offering—they &#91;are&#93; consecrations for refreshing fragrance; it &#91;is&#93; a fire-offering to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:31; 1 Peter 4:11; Leviticus 7:30–34"> and Moses takes the breast, and waves it &#91;as&#93; a wave-offering before YHWH; it has been for Moses for a portion of the ram of the consecrations, as YHWH has commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 29:21; Numbers 3:3; Exodus 30:30"> And Moses takes of the anointing oil, and of the blood which &#91;is&#93; on the altar, and sprinkles &#91;them&#93; on Aaron, on his garments, and on his sons, and on the garments of his sons with him, and he sanctifies Aaron, his garments, and his sons, and the garments of his sons with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 29:31–32; Galatians 2:20; 1 Samuel 2:13–17"> And Moses says to Aaron and to his sons, “Boil the flesh at the opening of the Tent of Meeting, and there you eat it and the bread which &#91;is&#93; in the basket of the consecrations, as I have commanded, saying, Aaron and his sons eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 29:34; Exodus 12:10; Hebrews 3:13–14"> And the remnant of the flesh and of the bread you burn with fire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 29:35; Numbers 19:12; Ezekiel 43:25–27"> and you do not go out from the opening of the Tent of Meeting &#91;for&#93; seven days, until the day of the fullness, the days of your consecration—for seven days he consecrates your hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 7:27; Hebrews 7:16; Hebrews 10:11–12"> as he has done on this day, YHWH has commanded to do, to make atonement for you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 3:7; Deuteronomy 11:1; 1 Kings 2:3"> and you abide at the opening of the Tent of Meeting by day and by night &#91;for&#93; seven days, and you have kept the charge of YHWH, and do not die, for so I have been commanded.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:2; Exodus 40:16"> And Aaron does—his sons also—all the things which YHWH has commanded by the hand of Moses.</span>
