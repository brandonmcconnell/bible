<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Acts 5:4; Leviticus 5:15"> “When any person sins, and has committed a trespass against YHWH, and has lied to his fellow concerning a deposit, or concerning fellowship, or concerning violent robbery, or has oppressed his fellow;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 23:4; Leviticus 19:12; Exodus 22:9–11"> or has found a lost thing, and has lied concerning it, and has sworn to a falsehood, concerning one of all &#91;these&#93; which man does, sinning in them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:18; Amos 3:10; Leviticus 5:3–4"> then it has been, when he sins and has been guilty, that he has returned the plunder which he has violently taken away, or the thing which he has gotten by oppression, or the deposit which has been deposited with him, or the lost thing which he has found;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 5:16; Luke 19:8; Numbers 5:7–8"> or all that concerning which he swears falsely, he has even repaid it in its principal, and he is adding to it its fifth; he gives it to him whose it &#91;is&#93; in the day of his guilt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 5:15; Leviticus 5:18; Isaiah 53:10–11"> And he brings his guilt-offering to YHWH, a ram, a perfect one, out of the flock, at your valuation, for a guilt-offering, to the priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 4:26; 1 John 1:9; 1 John 2:1–2"> and the priest has made atonement for him before YHWH, and it has been forgiven him, concerning one thing of all that he does, by being guilty therein.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 6:12–13; Numbers 28:3; Exodus 29:38–42"> “Command Aaron and his sons, saying, This &#91;is&#93; a law of the burnt-offering: the burnt-offering on &#91;the&#93; burning pile &#91;remains&#93; on the altar all the night until the morning, and the fire of the altar is burning on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 28:39–43; Leviticus 16:4; Leviticus 1:13"> And the priest has put on his long robe of fine linen, and he puts his fine linen trousers on his flesh, and has lifted up the ashes which the fire consumes with the burnt-offering on the altar, and has put them near the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 4:12; Ezekiel 44:19; Hebrews 13:11–13"> and he has stripped off his garments, and has put on other garments, and has brought out the ashes to the outside of the camp, to a clean place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 9:24; Exodus 29:38–42; Nehemiah 13:31"> And the fire on the altar is burning on it, it is not quenched, and the priest has burned wood on it morning by morning, and has arranged the burnt-offering on it, and has made incense on it &#91;with&#93; the fat of the peace-offerings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> fire is continually burning on the altar, it is not quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 15:4; Leviticus 2:1–2; John 6:32"> And this &#91;is&#93; a law of the present: sons of Aaron have brought it near before YHWH to the front of the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Leviticus 2:9"> and &#91;one&#93; has lifted up of it with his hand from the flour of the present, and from its oil, and all the frankincense which &#91;is&#93; on the present, and has made incense on the altar of refreshing fragrance—its memorial to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:29; Leviticus 2:3; Leviticus 10:12–13"> And Aaron and his sons eat the remnant of it; it is eaten &#91;with&#93; unleavened things in a holy place—they eat it in the court of the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Leviticus 2:11; Numbers 18:9–10"> It is not baked &#91;with&#93; anything fermented; I have given it &#91;for&#93; their portion out of My fire-offerings; it &#91;is&#93; most holy, like the sin-offering, and like the guilt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 6:29; Numbers 18:10; Haggai 2:12–14"> Every male among the sons of Aaron eats it—a continuous statute throughout your generations, out of the fire-offerings of YHWH. All that comes against them is holy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 29:2; Exodus 16:36; Leviticus 2:1–16"> “This &#91;is&#93; an offering of Aaron and of his sons, which they bring near to YHWH in the day of his being anointed: a tenth of the ephah of flour &#91;for&#93; a continual present, half of it in the morning, and half of it in the evening;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 2:5; Leviticus 7:9; 1 Chronicles 9:31"> it is made on a griddle with oil—you bring it in stirred; you bring baked pieces of the present near &#91;for&#93; a refreshing fragrance to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:22–25; Isaiah 53:10; Leviticus 8:21"> And the priest who is anointed in his stead, from among his sons, makes it; &#91;it is&#93; a continuous statute of YHWH; it is completely burned as incense;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 2:10; Leviticus 6:16–17"> and every present of a priest is a whole burnt-offering; it is not eaten.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 4:24; Leviticus 1:11; Leviticus 1:3"> “Speak to Aaron and to his sons, saying, This &#91;is&#93; a law of the sin-offering: in the place where the burnt-offering is slaughtered, the sin-offering is slaughtered before YHWH; it &#91;is&#93; most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 10:17–18; Ezekiel 44:28–29; Hosea 4:8"> The priest who is making atonement with it eats it; it is eaten in a holy place, in the court of the Tent of Meeting;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 29:37; Haggai 2:12; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> all that comes against its flesh is holy, and when &#91;any&#93; of its blood is sprinkled on the garment, that on which it is sprinkled you wash in the holy place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 11:33; Leviticus 15:12; Hebrews 9:9–10"> and an earthen vessel in which it is boiled is broken, and if it is boiled in a bronze vessel, then it is scoured and rinsed with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 6:25; Leviticus 6:17–18; Numbers 18:10"> Every male among the priests eats it—it &#91;is&#93; most holy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 10:18; Leviticus 16:27–28; Hebrews 13:11"> and no sin-offering, &#91;any&#93; of whose blood is brought into the Tent of Meeting to make atonement in the holy place, is eaten; it is burned with fire.”</span>
