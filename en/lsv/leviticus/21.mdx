<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:25; Leviticus 21:11; Leviticus 10:6–7"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Speak to the priests, sons of Aaron, and you have said to them: No one defiles himself for a &#91;dead&#93; person among his people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:13; Leviticus 18:6"> except for his relation who &#91;is&#93; near to him: for his mother, and for his father, and for his son, and for his daughter, and for his brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and for his sister, the virgin, who is near to him, who has not been to a man; he is defiled for her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:16–17"> A master &#91;priest&#93; does not defile himself among his people—to defile himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Ezekiel 44:20; Leviticus 19:27–28"> they do not make baldness on their head, and they do not shave the corner of their beard, and they do not make a cutting in their flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 3:11; Leviticus 18:21; 1 Peter 2:9"> they are holy to their God, and they do not defile the Name of their God, for the fire-offerings of YHWH, bread of their God, they are bringing near, and have been holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 21:13–14; Ezekiel 44:22; Leviticus 21:8"> They do not take a woman of harlotry, or defiled, and they do not take a woman cast out from her husband, for he &#91;is&#93; holy to his God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:7–8; Leviticus 19:2; Leviticus 11:44–45"> and you have sanctified him, for he is bringing the bread of your God near. He is holy to you, for I, YHWH, sanctifying you, &#91;am&#93; holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 38:24; Leviticus 19:29; Ezekiel 9:6"> And when a daughter of any priest defiles herself by going whoring—she is defiling her father; she is burned with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:6–7; Leviticus 16:32; Matthew 26:65"> And the high priest of his brothers, on whose head the anointing oil is poured, and who has consecrated his hand to put on the garments, does not uncover his head, nor tear his garments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 19:14; Leviticus 21:1–2; Numbers 6:7"> nor does he come beside any dead person; he does not defile himself for his father and for his mother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 10:7; Exodus 29:6–7; Leviticus 8:9–12"> nor does he go out from the sanctuary, nor does he defile the sanctuary of his God, for the separation of the anointing oil of his God &#91;is&#93; on him; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Revelation 14:4"> And he takes a wife in her virginity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; Ezekiel 44:22"> a widow, or cast out, or defiled, &#91;or&#93; a harlot—these he does not take, but he takes a virgin of his own people &#91;for&#93; a wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 11:16; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Nehemiah 13:23–29"> and he does not defile his seed among his people; for I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, sanctifying him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 65:4; Numbers 16:5; Leviticus 10:3"> “Speak to Aaron, saying, No man of your seed throughout their generations in whom there is blemish draws near to bring the bread of his God near,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2–3; Leviticus 22:19–25; Titus 1:7"> for no man in whom &#91;is&#93; a blemish draws near: a man blind, or lame, or disfigured, or deformed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> or a man in whom there is a breach in the foot, or a breach in the hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:1; Isaiah 56:3"> or hunchbacked, or a dwarf, or &#91;with&#93; a defect in his eye, or &#91;with&#93; an itch, or &#91;with&#93; a scab, or &#91;with&#93; a broken testicle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 21:6; Leviticus 21:8; Leviticus 21:17"> No man in whom is blemish (of the seed of Aaron the priest) comes near to bring the fire-offerings of YHWH near; blemish &#91;is&#93; in him; he does not come near to bring the bread of his God near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Numbers 18:19; Leviticus 24:8–9"> Bread of his God—from the most holy things and from the holy things—he eats;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 21:12; Ezekiel 44:9–14; Exodus 30:6–8"> only, he does not come toward the veil, and he does not draw near to the altar; for blemish &#91;is&#93; in him; and he does not defile My sanctuaries; for I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, sanctifying them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:18; Malachi 2:1–7; 2 Timothy 2:2"> And Moses speaks to Aaron, and to his sons, and to all the sons of Israel.</span>
