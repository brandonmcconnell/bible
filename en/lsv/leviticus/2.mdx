<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 6:14–18; Leviticus 9:17; Isaiah 66:20"> “And when a person brings an offering near, a present to YHWH, &#91;and&#93; his offering is of flour, then he has poured oil on it, and has put frankincense on it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 5:12; Leviticus 6:15; Acts 10:4"> and he has brought it to the sons of Aaron, the priests, and one &#91;of the priests&#93; has taken from there the fullness of his hand of its flour and of its oil, besides all its frankincense, and the priest has made incense with its memorial on the altar, a fire-offering of refreshing fragrance to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 10:12–13; Numbers 18:9; Leviticus 21:22"> and the remnant of the present &#91;is&#93; for Aaron and for his sons, most holy, of the fire-offerings of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:2; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Psalms 22:14"> And when you bring an offering near, a present baked in an oven, &#91;it is of&#93; unleavened cakes of flour mixed with oil, or thin unleavened cakes anointed with oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 6:21; Leviticus 7:9"> And if your offering &#91;is&#93; a present &#91;made&#93; on the griddle, it is of flour, mixed with oil, unleavened;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:1–19; Psalms 22:1–21; Mark 14:1–15"> divide it into parts, and you have poured oil on it; it &#91;is&#93; a present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1–2; Leviticus 7:9"> And if your offering &#91;is&#93; a present &#91;made&#93; on the frying-pan, it is made of flour with oil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and you have brought in the present which is made of these to YHWH, and &#91;one&#93; has brought it near to the priest, and he has brought it near to the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Philippians 4:18; Leviticus 6:15"> and the priest has lifted up from the present its memorial, and has made incense on the altar, a fire-offering of refreshing fragrance to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3"> and the remnant of the present &#91;is&#93; for Aaron and for his sons, most holy, of the fire-offerings of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 5:9; Luke 12:1; Exodus 23:18"> No present which you bring near to YHWH is made fermented, for you do not make incense &#91;as&#93; a fire-offering to YHWH with any leaven or any honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Genesis 23:10–11; Exodus 22:29"> An offering of first-&#91;fruits&#93;—you bring them near to YHWH, but they do not go up on the altar for refreshing fragrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:24; Matthew 5:13; Colossians 4:6"> And every offering—your present—you season with salt, and you do not let the salt of the covenant of your God cease from your present; you bring salt near with all your offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10; Malachi 1:11; Numbers 28:2"> And if you bring a present of first-fruits near to YHWH, you bring the present of your first-fruits near of green ears, roasted with fire, beaten out &#91;grain&#93; of a fruitful field,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and you have put oil on it, and laid frankincense on it, &#91;for&#93; it &#91;is&#93; a present;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Romans 8:26–27; Isaiah 61:1"> and the priest has made incense with its memorial from its beaten out &#91;grain&#93;, and from its oil, besides all its frankincense, &#91;as&#93; a fire-offering to YHWH.”</span>
