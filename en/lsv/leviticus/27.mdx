<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 6:2; 1 Samuel 1:28; Judges 11:30–31"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, and you have said to them: When a man makes a special vow in your valuation of persons to YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 27:25; Numbers 18:16"> then your valuation has been of the male from a son of twenty years even to a son of sixty years, and your valuation has been fifty shekels of silver by the shekel of the holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 27:9–10; Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 26:15"> And if it &#91;is&#93; a female, then your valuation has been thirty shekels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and if from a son of five years even to a son of twenty years, then your valuation of the male has been twenty shekels, and for the female, ten shekels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 3:40–43; Numbers 18:14–16"> and if from a son of a month even to a son of five years, then your valuation of the male has been five shekels of silver, and for the female your valuation &#91;is&#93; three shekels of silver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 90:10"> and if from a son of sixty years and above, if a male, then your valuation has been fifteen shekels, and for a female, ten shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 14:7; Leviticus 14:21–22; Luke 21:1–4"> And if he is poorer than your valuation, then he has presented himself before the priest, and the priest has valued him; according to that which the hand of him who is vowing reaches, the priest values him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And if &#91;it is&#93; a beast of which they bring near &#91;as&#93; an offering to YHWH, all that &#91;one&#93; gives of it to YHWH is holy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 27:15–33; James 1:8"> he does not change it nor exchange it, a good for a bad, or a bad for a good; and if he really exchanges beast for beast, then it has been &#91;holy&#93; and its exchange is holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:18; Malachi 1:14"> And if &#91;it is&#93; any unclean beast of which they do not bring near &#91;as&#93; an offering to YHWH, then he has presented the beast before the priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and the priest has valued it, whether good or bad; according to your valuation, O priest, so it is;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:19; Leviticus 27:15; Leviticus 22:14"> and if he really redeems it, then he has added its fifth to your valuation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 25:29–31; Leviticus 27:21"> And when a man sanctifies his house &#91;as&#93; a holy thing to YHWH, then the priest has valued it, whether good or bad; as the priest values it so it stands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 27:13"> and if he who is sanctifying redeems his house, then he has added a fifth of the money of your valuation to it, and it has become his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:11–14; Hosea 3:2; Acts 4:34–37"> And if a man sanctifies to YHWH from a field of his possession, then your valuation has been according to its seed—a homer of barley-seed at fifty shekels of silver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> if he sanctifies his field from the Year of the Jubilee, according to your valuation it stands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 25:15–16; Leviticus 25:51–52; Leviticus 25:27"> and if he sanctifies his field after the Jubilee, then the priest has reckoned the money to him according to the years which are left, to the Year of the Jubilee, and it has been diminished from your valuation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 27:13"> And if he really redeems the field—he who is sanctifying it—then he has added a fifth of the money of your valuation to it, and it has been established to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> and if he does not redeem the field, or if he has sold the field to another man, it is not redeemed anymore;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:29; Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 25:31"> and the field has been, in its going out in the Jubilee, holy to YHWH as a field which is devoted; his possession is for the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Leviticus 25:25"> And if he sanctifies a field of his purchase to YHWH, which &#91;is&#93; not of the fields of his possession,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 27:18; Leviticus 27:12"> then the priest has reckoned to him the amount of your valuation up to the Year of Jubilee, and he has given your valuation in that day &#91;as&#93; a holy thing to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 25:28; Leviticus 27:20"> in the Year of the Jubilee the field returns to him from whom he bought it, to him who &#91;has&#93; the possession of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Numbers 18:16; Numbers 3:47"> And all your valuation is by the shekel of the holy place: the shekel is twenty gerahs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12–13; Numbers 18:17"> Only, a firstling which is YHWH’s firstling among beasts—no man sanctifies it, whether ox or sheep; it &#91;is&#93; YHWH’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 27:11"> And if &#91;from&#93; among the unclean beast, then he has ransomed &#91;it&#93; at your valuation, and he has added its fifth to it; and if it is not redeemed, then it has been sold at your valuation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 6:17–19; Leviticus 27:21; Exodus 22:20"> Only, no devoted thing which a man devotes to YHWH of all that he has, of man, and beast, and of the field of his possession, is sold or redeemed; every devoted thing is most holy to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:18–23; Numbers 21:2–3"> No devoted thing, which is devoted of man, is ransomed, it is surely put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 23:23; Nehemiah 13:12; Genesis 28:22"> And all tithe of the land, of the seed of the land, of the fruit of the tree, is YHWH’s; &#91;it is&#93; holy to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And if a man really redeems &#91;any&#93; of his tithe, he adds its fifth to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:13; Ezekiel 20:37; Micah 7:14"> And all the tithe of the herd and of the flock—all that passes by under the rod—the tenth is holy to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 27:10"> he does not inquire between good and bad, nor does he exchange it; and if he really exchanges it, then it has been &#91;holy&#93; and its exchange is holy; it is not redeemed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 26:46; Numbers 1:1; Hebrews 12:18–25"> These &#91;are&#93; the commands which YHWH has commanded Moses for the sons of Israel in Mount Sinai.</span>
