<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 19:3; Exodus 25:22; Exodus 29:42"> And YHWH calls to Moses, and speaks to him out of the Tent of Meeting, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 22:18–19; Romans 12:6; 1 Chronicles 16:29"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, and you have said to them: Any man of you, when he brings an offering near to YHWH out of the livestock—out of the herd or out of the flock—you bring your offering near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Hebrews 9:14; Leviticus 22:19–24"> If his offering &#91;is&#93; a burnt-offering out of the herd, he brings a male near, a perfect one, to the opening of the Tent of Meeting; he brings it near at his pleasure before YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:10; Exodus 29:15; Leviticus 4:20"> and he has laid his hand on the head of the burnt-offering, and it has been accepted for him to make atonement for him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 1:11; Leviticus 3:8; Leviticus 3:2"> and he has slaughtered the son of the herd before YHWH; and sons of Aaron, the priests, have brought the blood near, and sprinkled the blood around the altar, which &#91;is&#93; at the opening of the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 7:8; Genesis 3:21"> And he has stripped the burnt-offering, and has cut it into its pieces;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 6:12–13; Genesis 22:9; 1 Chronicles 21:26"> and the sons of Aaron the priest have put fire on the altar, and arranged wood on the fire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 9:13–14; Leviticus 1:12; 1 Kings 18:33"> and sons of Aaron, the priests, have arranged the pieces, with the head and the fat, on the wood, which &#91;is&#93; on the fire, which &#91;is&#93; on the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 8:21; Leviticus 1:13; Ephesians 5:2"> and he washes its innards and its legs with water; and the priest has made incense with the whole on the altar, a burnt-offering, a fire-offering of refreshing fragrance to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6–7; Leviticus 1:2–3; Genesis 8:20"> And if his offering &#91;is&#93; out of the flock—out of the sheep or out of the goats—he brings a male near, a perfect one, for a burnt-offering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 1:5; Exodus 40:22; Leviticus 7:2"> and he has slaughtered it by the side of the altar northward, before YHWH; and sons of Aaron, the priests, have sprinkled its blood around the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and he has cut it into its pieces, and its head and its fat, and the priest has arranged them on the wood, which &#91;is&#93; on the fire, which &#91;is&#93; on the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 1:9"> and he washes the innards and the legs with water, and the priest has brought the whole near, and has made incense on the altar; it &#91;is&#93; a burnt-offering, a fire-offering of refreshing fragrance to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 5:7; Luke 2:24; Leviticus 12:8"> And if his offering &#91;is&#93; a burnt-offering out of the bird to YHWH, then he has brought his offering near out of the turtle-doves or out of the young pigeons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 5:8–9; Psalms 22:21; 1 John 2:27"> and the priest has brought it near to the altar, and has wrung off its head, and has made incense on the altar, and its blood has been wrung out by the side of the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 1:2; Luke 1:35; Leviticus 16:27"> and he has turned aside its crop with its feathers, and has cast it near the altar, eastward, to the place of ashes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:10; John 19:30; Leviticus 1:9–10"> and he has cleaved it with its wings (he does not separate &#91;it&#93;), and the priest has made it an incense on the altar, on the wood, which &#91;is&#93; on the fire; it &#91;is&#93; a burnt-offering, a fire-offering of refreshing fragrance to YHWH.”</span>
