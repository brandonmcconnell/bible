<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 6:17; Leviticus 5:14–7; Leviticus 6:25"> “And this &#91;is&#93; a law of the guilt-offering: it &#91;is&#93; most holy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 1:5; Leviticus 6:25; Leviticus 1:11"> in the place where they slaughter the burnt-offering they slaughter the guilt-offering, and he sprinkles its blood on the altar all around,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:13; Leviticus 3:15–16; Psalms 51:6"> and he brings near all its fat from it, the fat tail, and the fat which is covering the innards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 3:4"> and the two kidneys, and the fat which &#91;is&#93; on them, which &#91;is&#93; on the flanks, and the redundance on the liver above the kidneys—he turns it aside;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 1:13; Galatians 5:24; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> and the priest has made them an incense on the altar, a fire-offering to YHWH; it &#91;is&#93; a guilt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 6:29; Numbers 18:9–10; Leviticus 2:3"> Every male among the priests eats it; it is eaten in a holy place—it &#91;is&#93; most holy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 6:25–26; Leviticus 14:13; 1 Corinthians 9:13"> as &#91;is&#93; a sin-offering, so &#91;is&#93; a guilt-offering; one law &#91;is&#93; for them; the priest who makes atonement by it—it is his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 4:11; Exodus 29:14; Romans 13:14"> And the priest who is bringing any man’s burnt-offering near, the skin of the burnt-offering which he has brought near, it is the priest’s, his own;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 18:9; Ezekiel 44:29; Leviticus 2:10"> and every present which is baked in an oven, and all done in a frying-pan, and on a griddle, &#91;is&#93; the priest’s who is bringing it near; it is his;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14; Exodus 16:18"> and every present, mixed with oil or dry, is for all the sons of Aaron—one as another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 22:18–21; Ezekiel 45:15; Leviticus 3:1–17"> And this &#91;is&#93; a law of the sacrifice of the peace-offerings which &#91;one&#93; brings near to YHWH:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 2:4; Numbers 6:15; 1 Peter 2:5"> if he brings it near for a thank-offering, then he has brought near with the sacrifice of thank-offering unleavened cakes mixed with oil, and thin unleavened cakes anointed with oil, and of stirred flour cakes mixed with oil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 4:5; Leviticus 23:17; Matthew 13:33"> besides the cakes, he brings fermented bread near &#91;with&#93; his offering, besides the sacrifice of thank-offering of his peace-offerings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:19; Exodus 29:27–28; Numbers 18:24–32"> and he has brought near from it one &#91;cake&#93; from every offering &#91;as&#93; a raised-offering to YHWH for the priest who is sprinkling the blood of the peace-offerings; it is for him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 22:29–30"> as for the flesh of the sacrifice of the thank-offering of his peace-offerings, it is eaten in the day of his offering; he does not leave of it until morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:5–8; Deuteronomy 12:6; Psalms 116:14"> And if the sacrifice of his offering &#91;is&#93; a vow or free-will offering, it is eaten in the day of his bringing his sacrifice near; and on the next day the remnant of it is also eaten;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:10; Hosea 6:2; 1 Corinthians 15:4"> and the remnant of the flesh of the sacrifice is burned with fire on the third day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:7–8; Leviticus 11:41; Numbers 18:27"> and if any of the flesh of the sacrifice of his peace-offerings is eaten at all on the third day, it is not pleasing; it is not reckoned for him who is bringing it near; it is an abomination, and the person who is eating of it bears his iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 11:41; Titus 1:15; Acts 10:15–16"> And the flesh which comes against any unclean thing is not eaten; it is burned with fire; as for the flesh, every clean one eats of the flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 15:2–33; Genesis 17:14; Leviticus 22:3–7"> and the person who eats of the flesh of the sacrifice of the peace-offerings which &#91;are&#93; YHWH’s, and his uncleanness &#91;is&#93; on him, indeed, that person has been cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 12:1–59; Leviticus 11:20; Ezekiel 4:14"> And when a person comes against anything unclean, of the uncleanness of man, or of the uncleanness of beasts, or of any unclean teeming creature, and has eaten of the flesh of the sacrifice of the peace-offerings which &#91;are&#93; YHWH’s, indeed, that person has been cut off from his people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 3:16–17; Leviticus 17:6; 1 Samuel 2:15–17"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, You do not eat any fat of ox, and sheep, and goat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 22:31; Leviticus 17:15; Leviticus 22:8"> and the fat of a carcass, and the fat of a torn thing, is prepared for any work, but you certainly do not eat it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> for whoever eats the fat of the beast, of which &#91;one&#93; brings near &#91;as&#93; a fire-offering to YHWH, indeed, the person who eats &#91;it&#93; has been cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 9:4; Leviticus 3:17; Acts 15:20"> And you do not eat any blood in all your dwellings, of bird, or of beast;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 10:29; Leviticus 7:20–21; Leviticus 7:25"> any person who eats any blood, indeed, that person has been cut off from his people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 1:20; 1 John 1:7; Leviticus 3:1–17"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, He who is bringing the sacrifice of his peace-offerings near to YHWH brings in his offering to YHWH from the sacrifice of his peace-offerings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 6:20; Leviticus 3:9; Leviticus 3:14"> his own hands bring in the fire-offerings of YHWH; the fat beside the breast—he brings it in with the breast to wave it &#91;as&#93; a wave-offering before YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 3:5; Leviticus 3:11; Leviticus 3:16"> And the priest has made incense with the fat on the altar, and the breast has been Aaron’s and his sons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 6:20; Leviticus 7:34; Leviticus 9:21"> and you give the right leg to the priest &#91;as&#93; a raised-offering of the sacrifices of your peace-offerings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 26:1–46; Leviticus 6:1–30; Leviticus 7:3"> he from the sons of Aaron who is bringing the blood of the peace-offerings and the fat near—the right leg is for a portion for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 18:18–19; Leviticus 10:14–15; Exodus 29:9"> For I have taken the breast of the wave-offering and the leg of the raised-offering from the sons of Israel, from the sacrifices of their peace-offerings, and I give them to Aaron the priest, and to his sons, by a continuous statute, from the sons of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:20; 1 John 2:27; Numbers 18:7–19"> This &#91;is&#93; an anointed portion &#91;for&#93; Aaron, and an anointed portion &#91;for&#93; his sons out of the fire-offerings of YHWH, in the day he has brought them near to act as priest of YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Leviticus 8:12; Leviticus 8:30; Exodus 40:13–15"> which YHWH has commanded to give to them from the sons of Israel, in the day of His anointing them—a continuous statute throughout their generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 29:1; Leviticus 1:1–6; Leviticus 6:9–18"> This &#91;is&#93; the law for burnt-offering, for present, and for sin-offering, and for guilt-offering, and for consecrations, and for a sacrifice of the peace-offerings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 1:1–2"> which YHWH has commanded Moses in Mount Sinai, in the day of His commanding the sons of Israel to bring their offerings near to YHWH, in the wilderness of Sinai.</span>
