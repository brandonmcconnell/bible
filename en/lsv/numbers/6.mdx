<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 13:5; Amos 2:11–12; Romans 1:1"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, and you have said to them: When a man or woman does extraordinarily by vowing a vow of a Nazarite, to be separate to YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 1:15; 1 Timothy 5:23; Amos 2:12"> he keeps separate from wine and strong drink; he does not drink vinegar of wine and vinegar of strong drink, and he does not drink any juice of grapes, and he does not eat moist or dry grapes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 6:5; Numbers 6:21; Numbers 6:8–9"> all &#91;the&#93; days of his separation he does not eat of anything which is made of the wine-vine, from kernels even to husk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:11; Judges 16:17; Judges 13:5"> All &#91;the&#93; days of the vow of his separation a razor does not pass over his head; he is holy until the fullness of the days &#91;in&#93; which he separates himself to YHWH; the upper part of the hair of his head has grown up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 21:11; Leviticus 19:28; Leviticus 21:1–3"> All &#91;the&#93; days of his keeping separate to YHWH, he does not go near a dead person;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 9:6; Leviticus 21:1–2; Leviticus 21:10–12"> for his father, or for his mother, for his brother, or for his sister—he does not become unclean for them at their death, for the separation of his God &#91;is&#93; on his head;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:17–18"> all &#91;the&#93; days of his separation he &#91;is&#93; holy to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 6:18; Acts 18:18; Leviticus 14:9"> And when the dead dies beside him in an instant, suddenly, and he has defiled the head of his separation, then he has shaved his head in the day of his cleansing; on the seventh day he shaves it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 15:29; Leviticus 1:14; Leviticus 15:14"> and on the eighth day he brings in two turtle-doves or two young pigeons to the priest, to the opening of the Tent of Meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 14:30–31; Leviticus 5:8–10"> and the priest has prepared one for a sin-offering and one for a burnt-offering, and has made atonement for him because of that which he has sinned by the body, and he has hallowed his head on that day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 5:6; Matthew 24:13; Leviticus 14:24"> and he has separated to YHWH the days of his separation, and he has brought in a lamb, a son of a year, for a guilt-offering, and the former days are fallen, for his separation has been defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 21:26"> And this &#91;is&#93; the law of the Nazarite: in the day of the fullness of the days of his separation, he is brought to the opening of the Tent of Meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 3:6; Leviticus 4:32; Leviticus 4:2–3"> and he has brought his offering near to YHWH—one male lamb, a son of a year, a perfect one, for a burnt-offering, and one female lamb, a daughter of a year, a perfect one, for a sin-offering, and one ram, a perfect one, for peace-offerings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 2:4; Exodus 29:2; Numbers 15:10"> and a basket of unleavened things of flour, cakes mixed with oil, and thin cakes of unleavened things anointed with oil, and their present, and their drink-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the priest has brought &#91;them&#93; near before YHWH, and has made his sin-offering and his burnt-offering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and he makes the ram &#91;as&#93; a sacrifice of peace-offerings to YHWH, besides the basket of unleavened things; and the priest has made its present and its drink-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:24; Numbers 6:9; Acts 18:18"> And the Nazarite has shaved (at the opening of the Tent of Meeting) the head of his separation, and has taken the hair of the head of his separation, and has put &#91;it&#93; on the fire which &#91;is&#93; under the sacrifice of the peace-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:15; Leviticus 7:30; Exodus 29:23–28"> And the priest has taken the boiled shoulder from the ram, and one unleavened cake out of the basket, and one thin unleavened cake, and has put &#91;them&#93; on the palms of the Nazarite after his shaving &#91;the hair of&#93; his separation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Exodus 29:27–28; Numbers 5:25"> and the priest has waved them &#91;as&#93; a wave-offering before YHWH; it &#91;is&#93; holy to the priest, besides the breast of the wave-offering, and besides the leg of the raised-offering; and afterward the Nazarite drinks wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Ezra 2:69; Numbers 5:29"> This &#91;is&#93; the law of the Nazarite who vows his offering to YHWH for his separation, apart from that which his hand attains; according to his vow which he vows so he does by the law of his separation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; 1 Chronicles 23:13; Genesis 48:20"> “Speak to Aaron and to his sons, saying, Thus you bless the sons of Israel, saying to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23; Psalms 121:4–7; Philippians 4:7"> YHWH bless you and keep you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 80:19; Psalms 119:135; Psalms 31:16"> YHWH cause His face to shine on you, &#124;&#124; And favor you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 4:6; Psalms 29:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> YHWH lift up His countenance on you, &#124;&#124; And appoint for you—peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; Daniel 9:18–19; Deuteronomy 28:10"> And they have put My Name on the sons of Israel, and I bless them.”</span>
</div>
