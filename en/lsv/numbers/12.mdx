<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:12–13; Numbers 16:3; Micah 6:4"> and they say, “Has YHWH only spoken by Moses? Has he not also spoken by us?” And YHWH hears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; James 3:13; 2 Corinthians 11:5"> And the man Moses &#91;is&#93; very humble, more than any of the men who &#91;are&#93; on the face of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 76:9; Numbers 16:16–21"> And YHWH suddenly says to Moses, and to Aaron, and to Miriam, “Come out, you three, to the Tent of Meeting”; and those three come out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 11:25; Exodus 34:5; Psalms 99:7"> And YHWH comes down in the pillar of the cloud, and stands at the opening of the tent, and calls Aaron and Miriam, and both of them come out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 3:5; Genesis 31:10–11; Genesis 46:2"> And He says, “Now hear My words: If your prophet is of YHWH, &#124;&#124; I make Myself known to him in a vision; I speak with him in a dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 3:2–6; Joshua 1:1; Psalms 105:26"> Not so My servant Moses; He &#91;is&#93; steadfast in all My house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:10; 1 Corinthians 13:12; Exodus 33:11"> I speak with him mouth to mouth, &#124;&#124; Even &#91;by&#93; an appearance, and not in riddles; And he beholds the form of YHWH attentively. Now why have you not been afraid &#124;&#124; To speak against My servant—against Moses?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 17:22; Hosea 5:15; Numbers 11:1"> And the anger of YHWH burns against them, and He goes on,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:9; 2 Kings 5:27; Exodus 4:6"> and the cloud has turned aside from off the tent, and behold, Miriam &#91;is&#93; leprous as snow; and Aaron turns to Miriam, and behold, leprous!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:19; 2 Samuel 24:10; Proverbs 30:32"> And Aaron says to Moses, “O my lord, please do not lay &#91;this&#93; sin on us &#91;in&#93; which we have been foolish, and &#91;in&#93; which we have sinned;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 88:4–5; 1 Corinthians 15:8; 1 Timothy 5:6"> please do not let her be as &#91;one&#93; dead, when in his coming out from the womb of his mother—the half of his flesh is consumed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 12:21; Luke 6:28; James 5:15"> And Moses cries to YHWH, saying, “O God, please give healing to her! Please!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:9; Job 30:10; Isaiah 50:6"> And YHWH says to Moses, “But &#91;if&#93; her father had but spat in her face—is she not ashamed &#91;for&#93; seven days? She is shut out &#91;for&#93; seven days at the outside of the camp, and afterward she is gathered.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:8–9; Micah 7:8–9; Micah 6:4"> And Miriam is shut out at the outside of the camp &#91;for&#93; seven days, and the people have not journeyed until Miriam is gathered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 11:35; Numbers 33:18; Numbers 10:12"> and afterward the people have journeyed from Hazeroth, and they encamp in the wilderness of Paran.</span>
