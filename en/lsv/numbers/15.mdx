<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10; Numbers 15:18; Leviticus 25:2"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, and you have said to them: When you come into the land of your dwellings, which I am giving to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:18; Genesis 8:21; Philippians 4:18"> then you have prepared a fire-offering to YHWH, a burnt-offering or a sacrifice, for separating a vow or free-will offering, or in your appointed things, to make a refreshing fragrance to YHWH, out of the herd or out of the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:40; Leviticus 23:13; Leviticus 6:14"> And he who is bringing his offering near to YHWH has brought a present near of a tenth part of flour mixed with a fourth of the hin of oil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 28:7; Numbers 28:14; Judges 9:13"> and you prepare a fourth of the hin of wine for a drink-offering, with the burnt-offering or for the sacrifice, for one lamb;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 28:12–14; Numbers 15:4"> or for a ram you prepare a present of two-tenth parts of flour mixed with a third of the hin of oil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and you bring a third part of the hin of wine near for a drink-offering—a refreshing fragrance to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 3:1; Leviticus 7:11–18; Leviticus 1:3"> And when you make a son of the herd a burnt-offering, or a sacrifice for separating a vow, or peace-offerings to YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 28:14; Numbers 28:12; Leviticus 6:14"> then he has brought near for the son of the herd a present of three-tenth parts of flour mixed with a half of the hin of oil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 15:5; Numbers 6:15"> and you bring a half of the hin of wine near for a drink-offering, &#91;for&#93; a fire-offering of refreshing fragrance to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> thus it is done for one ox, or for one ram, or for a lamb of the sheep or of the goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> According to the number that you prepare, so you do to each, according to their number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> every native does thus with these, for bringing a fire-offering near of refreshing fragrance to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and when a sojourner sojourns with you, or whoever &#91;is&#93; in your midst throughout your generations, and he has made a fire-offering of refreshing fragrance to YHWH, as you do so he does.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 9:14; Numbers 15:29; Exodus 12:49"> One statute is for you of the congregation and for the sojourner who is sojourning, a continuous statute throughout your generations: as you &#91;are&#93; so is the sojourner before YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> one law and one ordinance is for you and for the sojourner who is sojourning with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 15:2; Deuteronomy 26:1–15"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, and you have said to them: In your coming into the land to where I am bringing you in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 5:11–12"> then it has been, in your eating of the bread of the land, you raise up a raised-offering to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 2:14; Exodus 34:26; Deuteronomy 26:2–10"> you raise up a cake of the first of your dough &#91;as&#93; a raised-offering; as the raised-offering of a threshing-floor, so you raise it up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 29:28; Numbers 18:26"> You give to YHWH from the first of your dough &#91;as&#93; a raised-offering throughout your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:2; Leviticus 4:27; Leviticus 5:15–17"> And when you err, and do not do all these commands which YHWH has spoken to Moses—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> the whole that YHWH has charged on you by the hand of Moses, from the day that YHWH has commanded and from now on, throughout your generations—</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 4:23; Leviticus 4:13–21; Leviticus 5:15"> then it has been, if from the eyes of the congregation it has been done through ignorance, that all the congregation has prepared one bullock, a son of the herd, for a burnt-offering, for refreshing fragrance to YHWH, and its present, and its drink-offering, according to the ordinance, and one kid of the goats for a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 4:20; Romans 3:25; Hebrews 2:17"> And the priest has made atonement for all the congregation of the sons of Israel, and it has been forgiven them, for it &#91;is&#93; ignorance, and they have brought in their offering, a fire-offering to YHWH, and their sin-offering before YHWH for their ignorance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 15:24"> and it has been forgiven to all the congregation of the sons of Israel and to the sojourner who is sojourning in their midst, for that all the people &#91;did it&#93; through ignorance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27–28; Acts 17:30; Acts 3:17"> And if one person sins through ignorance, then he has brought a female goat near, daughter of a year, for a sin-offering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 4:35"> and the priest has made atonement for the person who is erring, in his sinning through ignorance before YHWH, by making atonement for him, and it has been forgiven him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 15:15; Leviticus 16:29; Leviticus 17:15"> one law is for yourselves—for the native among the sons of Israel and for the sojourner who is sojourning in their midst—for him who is doing &#91;anything&#93; through ignorance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26; Psalms 19:13; Deuteronomy 1:43"> And the person who does &#91;evil&#93; with a high hand—of the native or of the sojourner—he is reviling YHWH, and that person has been cut off from the midst of his people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:9; Proverbs 13:13; Ezekiel 18:20"> because he despised the word of YHWH and has broken His command, that person is certainly cut off; his iniquity &#91;is&#93; on him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 35:2–3; Exodus 31:14–15; Exodus 20:8–10"> And the sons of Israel are in the wilderness, and they find a man gathering wood on the Sabbath day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 8:3–20"> and those finding him gathering wood bring him near to Moses, and to Aaron, and to all the congregation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 24:12; Numbers 9:8"> and they place him in confinement, for it &#91;is&#93; not explained what is &#91;to be&#93; done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 7:58; Exodus 31:14–15; 1 Kings 21:13"> And YHWH says to Moses, “The man is certainly put to death &#91;by&#93; all the congregation stoning him with stones at the outside of the camp.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 7:25"> And all the congregation brings him out to the outside of the camp and stone him with stones, and he dies, as YHWH has commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; Deuteronomy 22:12; Matthew 9:20"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, and you have commanded to them, and they have made fringes on the skirts of their garments for themselves, throughout their generations, and they have put a ribbon of blue on the fringe of the skirt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 106:39; Proverbs 28:26; Psalms 73:27"> and it has been to you for a fringe, and you have seen it, and have remembered all the commands of YHWH, and have done them, and you do not search after your heart and after your eyes, after which you are going whoring,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 12:1; 1 Thessalonians 4:7; Leviticus 11:44–45"> so that you remember and have done all My commands, and you have been holy to your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9–10; Ezekiel 36:25–27; Hebrews 11:16"> I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH, who has brought you out from the land of Egypt to become your God; I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH.”</span>
