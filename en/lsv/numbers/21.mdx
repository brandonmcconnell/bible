<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Numbers 33:40; Joshua 12:14"> And the Canaanite, king of Arad, dwelling in the south, hears that Israel has come the way of the Atharim, and he fights against Israel, and takes &#91;some&#93; of them captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:30; Genesis 28:20; 1 Samuel 1:11"> And Israel vows a vow to YHWH and says, “If You certainly give this people into my hand, then I have devoted their cities”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 14:45; Deuteronomy 1:44; Psalms 10:17"> and YHWH listens to the voice of Israel and gives up the Canaanite, and he devotes them and their cities, and calls the name of the place Hormah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 6:9; Judges 11:18; Numbers 32:7"> And they journey from Mount Hor, the way of the Red Sea, to go around the land of Edom, and the soul of the people is short in the way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 78:19; Numbers 16:13–14; Exodus 17:2–3"> and the people speak against God and against Moses, “Why have you brought us up out of Egypt to die in a wilderness? For there is no bread, and there is no water, and our soul has been weary of this light bread.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Jeremiah 8:17; Deuteronomy 8:15"> And YHWH sends the burning serpents among the people, and they bite the people, and many people of Israel die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 8:24; Numbers 11:2; Psalms 78:34"> and the people come to Moses and say, “We have sinned, for we have spoken against YHWH and against you; pray to YHWH that He turns the serpent aside from us”; and Moses prays in behalf of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:14; Psalms 106:43–45; Psalms 145:8"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Make a burning &#91;serpent&#93; for yourself, and set it on an ensign; and it has been, everyone who is bitten and has seen it—he has lived.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:14–15; Hebrews 12:2; John 6:40"> And Moses makes a serpent of bronze, and sets it on the ensign, and it has been, if the serpent has bitten any man, and he has looked expectingly to the serpent of bronze—he has lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 33:43–45"> And the sons of Israel journey and encamp in Oboth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And they journey from Oboth and encamp in Ije-Abarim, in the wilderness that &#91;is&#93; on the front of Moab, at the rising of the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:13–14"> From there they have journeyed and encamp in the Valley of Zared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 11:18; Numbers 22:36; Isaiah 16:2"> From there they have journeyed and encamp beyond Arnon, which &#91;is&#93; in the wilderness which is coming out of the border of the Amorite, for Arnon &#91;is&#93; the border of Moab, between Moab and the Amorite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 10:13; 2 Samuel 1:18"> therefore it is said in &#91;the&#93; scroll of the Wars of YHWH: “Waheb in Suphah, &#124;&#124; And the brooks of Arnon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 21:28; Deuteronomy 2:18; Deuteronomy 2:9"> And the spring of the brooks, &#124;&#124; Which turned aside to the dwelling of Ar, &#124;&#124; And has leaned to the border of Moab.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 20:8; Isaiah 12:3; Isaiah 41:17–18"> And from there &#91;they journeyed&#93; to Beer; it &#91;is&#93; the well &#91;concerning&#93; which YHWH said to Moses, “Gather the people, and I give water to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 5:13; Exodus 15:1–2; Psalms 106:12"> Then Israel sings this song: “Spring up, O well, &#124;&#124; Let all answer to it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 33:22; 1 Timothy 6:17–18; James 4:12"> A well—princes have dug it, &#124;&#124; Nobles of the people have prepared it, &#124;&#124; With the lawgiver, with their staffs.” And from the wilderness &#91;they journeyed&#93; to Mattanah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and from Mattanah to Nahaliel, and from Nahaliel to Bamoth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 23:28; Numbers 23:14; Deuteronomy 1:5"> and from Bamoth, in the valley which &#91;is&#93; in the field of Moab, &#91;to&#93; the top of Pisgah, which has looked on the front of the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26–28; Numbers 20:14–19; Judges 11:19–21"> And Israel sends messengers to Sihon king of the Amorite, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 20:17"> “Let me pass through your land, we do not turn aside into a field or into a vineyard; we do not drink waters of a well; we go in the king’s way until we pass over your border.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 11:20; Deuteronomy 29:7–8; Isaiah 15:4"> And Sihon has not permitted Israel to pass through his border, and Sihon gathers all his people, and comes out to meet Israel in the wilderness, and comes to Jahaz, and fights against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Joshua 24:8; Genesis 32:22"> And Israel strikes him by the mouth of the sword, and possesses his land from Arnon to Jabbok—to the sons of Ammon; for the border of the sons of Ammon &#91;is&#93; strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 32:33–42; Isaiah 16:8–9; Jeremiah 48:2"> And Israel takes all these cities, and Israel dwells in all the cities of the Amorite, in Heshbon and in all its villages;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> for Heshbon is a city of Sihon king of the Amorite, and he has fought against the former king of Moab, and takes all his land out of his hand as far as Arnon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 14:4; Numbers 21:14; Habakkuk 2:6"> therefore those using allegories say: “Enter Heshbon, &#124;&#124; Let the city of Sihon be built and ready,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 21:15; Jeremiah 48:45–46; Isaiah 15:1–2"> For fire has gone out from Heshbon, &#124;&#124; A flame from the city of Sihon, &#124;&#124; It has consumed Ar of Moab, &#124;&#124; Owners of the high places of Arnon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 11:24; 2 Kings 23:13; Jeremiah 48:46"> Woe to you, O Moab, &#124;&#124; You have perished, O people of Chemosh! He has given his sons &#91;as&#93; fugitives, &#124;&#124; And his daughters into captivity, &#124;&#124; To Sihon king of the Amorite!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:18; Isaiah 15:2; Jeremiah 48:22"> And we shoot them; Heshbon has perished as far as Dibon, &#124;&#124; And we make desolate as far as Nophah, &#124;&#124; Which &#91;is&#93; as far as Medeba.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 13:8–12; Numbers 32:33–42; Deuteronomy 3:16–17"> And Israel dwells in the land of the Amorite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:32; Numbers 32:1; Numbers 32:35"> and Moses sends to spy out Jaazer, and they capture its villages, and dispossess the Amorite who &#91;is&#93; there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 13:12; Deuteronomy 1:4; Deuteronomy 29:7"> and turn and go up the way of Bashan, and Og king of Bashan comes out to meet them, he and all his people, to battle &#91;at&#93; Edrei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 135:10–11; Joshua 8:7; Deuteronomy 3:2–3"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Do not fear him, for into your hand I have given him, and all his people, and his land, and you have done to him as you have done to Sihon king of the Amorite, who is dwelling in Heshbon.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:7–8; Joshua 13:12; Deuteronomy 3:3–17"> And they strike him, and his sons, and all his people, until he has no remnant left to him, and they possess his land.</span>
