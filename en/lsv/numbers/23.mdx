<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 23:29; Jude 1:11; Exodus 27:1–8"> And Balaam says to Balak, “Build seven altars for me in this &#91;place&#93;, and prepare seven bullocks and seven rams for me in this &#91;place&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:14; Numbers 23:30"> And Balak does as Balaam has spoken, and Balak—Balaam also—offers a bullock and a ram on the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 23:15; Genesis 8:20; Genesis 22:2"> and Balaam says to Balak, “Station yourself by your burnt-offering and I go on, it may be YHWH comes to meet me, and the thing which He shows me I have declared to you”; and he goes &#91;to&#93; a high place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 23:16; Ephesians 2:9; Luke 18:12"> And God comes to Balaam, and he says to Him, “I have arranged the seven altars, and I offer a bullock and a ram on the altar”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18; Jeremiah 1:9; Isaiah 59:21"> and YHWH puts a word in the mouth of Balaam and says, “Return to Balak, and thus you speak.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he returns to him, and behold, he is standing by his burnt-offering, he and all the princes of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:4; Numbers 24:3; Numbers 22:5–6"> And he takes up his allegory and says: “Balak king of Moab leads me from Aram; From mountains of the east. Come—curse Jacob for me, &#124;&#124; And come—be indignant &#91;with&#93; Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:23; Isaiah 44:25; Numbers 23:20"> How do I pierce &#91;when&#93; God has not pierced? And how am I indignant &#91;when&#93; YHWH has not been indignant?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:28; Deuteronomy 32:8; Exodus 33:16"> For from the top of rocks I see him, &#124;&#124; And from heights I behold him; Behold a people! He dwells alone, &#124;&#124; And does not reckon himself among nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:37; Genesis 13:16; Psalms 116:15"> Who has counted the dust of Jacob, &#124;&#124; And the number of the fourth of Israel? Let me die the death of upright ones, &#124;&#124; And let my last end be like his!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:2; Numbers 24:10; Numbers 22:17"> And Balak says to Balaam, “What have you done to me? I have taken you to pierce my enemies, and behold, you have certainly blessed”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:38; Numbers 23:20; Numbers 23:26"> and he answers and says, “That which YHWH puts in my mouth—do I not take heed to speak it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 109:17; James 3:9–10; Numbers 22:41"> And Balak says to him, “Come! Come to another place with me, from where you see it—you see only its extremity, and you do not see all of it, and pierce it for me there”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6; Numbers 23:29; Isaiah 1:10–11"> and he takes him &#91;to&#93; the field of Zophim, to the top of Pisgah, and builds seven altars, and offers a bullock and a ram on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 23:3; Numbers 22:8"> And he says to Balak, “Station yourself here by your burnt-offering, and I meet &#91;Him&#93; there”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 23:5; Numbers 24:1; Numbers 22:35"> and YHWH comes to Balaam, and sets a word in his mouth, and says, “Return to Balak, and thus you speak.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 23:26; 1 Samuel 3:17; Jeremiah 37:17"> And he comes to him, and behold, he is standing by his burnt-offering, and the princes of Moab &#91;are&#93; with him, and Balak says to him: “What has YHWH spoken?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 3:20"> And he takes up his allegory and says: “Rise, Balak, and hear; Give ear to me, son of Zippor!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:29; James 1:17; Malachi 3:6"> God &#91;is&#93; not a man—and lies, &#124;&#124; And a son of man—and relents! Has He said—and He does not do &#91;it&#93;? And spoken—and He does not confirm it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:27–29; Numbers 22:12; Genesis 12:2"> Behold, I have received &#91;only&#93; to bless; Indeed, He blesses, and I cannot reverse it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 23:4; Matthew 1:23; Jeremiah 50:20"> He has not beheld iniquity in Jacob, &#124;&#124; Nor has He seen perverseness in Israel; His God YHWH &#91;is&#93; with him, &#124;&#124; And a shout of a king &#91;is&#93; in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 24:8; Deuteronomy 33:17; Job 39:9–11"> God is bringing them out from Egypt, &#124;&#124; As the swiftness of a wild ox for him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 10:18–19; Joshua 13:22; Galatians 1:23–24"> For no enchantment &#91;is&#93; against Jacob, &#124;&#124; Nor divination against Israel; At the time it is said of Jacob and Israel, &#124;&#124; O what God has worked!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 24:17"> Behold, the people rises as a lioness, &#124;&#124; And he lifts himself up as a lion; He does not lie down until he eats prey, &#124;&#124; And drinks blood of pierced ones.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–3"> And Balak says to Balaam, “Neither pierce it at all, nor bless it at all”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Numbers 22:18; Acts 4:19–20"> and Balaam answers and says to Balak, “Have I not spoken to you, saying, All that YHWH speaks—I do it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 11:29; Proverbs 21:30; Numbers 23:19–20"> And Balak says to Balaam, “Please come, I take you to another place; it may be it is right in the eyes of God to pierce it for me from there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 21:20; Psalms 106:28"> And Balak takes Balaam to the top of Peor, which is looking on the front of the wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 23:1–2"> and Balaam says to Balak, “Build seven altars for me in this &#91;place&#93;, and prepare seven bullocks and seven rams for me in this &#91;place&#93;”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and Balak does as Balaam said, and he offers a bullock and a ram on an altar.</span>
