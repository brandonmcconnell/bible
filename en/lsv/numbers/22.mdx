<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:1; Deuteronomy 3:8; Numbers 21:20"> And the sons of Israel journey and encamp in the plains of Moab beyond the Jordan, &#91;by&#93; Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:25; Numbers 21:20–35; Numbers 21:3"> And Balak son of Zippor sees all that Israel has done to the Amorite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 15:15; Isaiah 23:5; Joshua 9:24"> and Moab is exceedingly afraid of the presence of the people, for it &#91;is&#93; numerous; and Moab is distressed by the presence of the sons of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 25:15–18; Numbers 31:8; Numbers 24:17"> and Moab says to &#91;the&#93; elderly of Midian, “Now the assembly licks up all that is around us, as the ox licks up the green thing of the field.” And Balak son of Zippor &#91;is&#93; king of Moab at that time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:4; Joshua 24:9; Micah 6:5"> and he sends messengers to Balaam son of Beor, to Pethor, which &#91;is&#93; by the River of the land of the sons of his people, to call for him, saying, “Behold, a people has come out of Egypt; behold, it has covered the eye of the land, and it is abiding in front of me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Numbers 23:7–8; Acts 16:16"> and now, come, please curse this people for me, for it &#91;is&#93; mightier than I. It may be I prevail, &#91;and&#93; we strike it, and I cast it out from the land; for I have known that which you bless is blessed, and that which you curse is cursed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:7–8; Micah 3:11; Romans 16:18"> And &#91;the&#93; elderly of Moab and &#91;the&#93; elderly of Midian go with divinations in their hand, and they come to Balaam and speak the words of Balak to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:12; Numbers 22:19–20; Jeremiah 12:2"> and he says to them, “Lodge here tonight, and I have brought you back word, as YHWH speaks to me”; and the princes of Moab abide with Balaam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 20:3; Numbers 22:20; Genesis 16:8"> And God comes to Balaam and says, “Who &#91;are&#93; these men with you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Balaam says to God, “Balak, son of Zippor, king of Moab, has sent to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Behold, the people that is coming out from Egypt and covers the eye of the land—now come, pierce it for me; it may be I am able to fight against it and have cast it out”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Ephesians 1:3; Genesis 12:2"> and God says to Balaam, “You do not go with them; you do not curse the people, for it &#91;is&#93; blessed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Numbers 22:14"> And Balaam rises in the morning and says to the princes of Balak, “Go to your land, for YHWH is refusing to permit me to go with you”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 22:37; Numbers 22:13"> and the princes of Moab rise, and come to Balak, and say, “Balaam is refusing to come with us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 10:7–8; Numbers 22:7–8"> And Balak adds yet to send princes, more numerous and honored than these,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and they come to Balaam and say to him, “Thus said Balak son of Zippor: Please do not be withheld from coming to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 24:11; Esther 5:11; Matthew 4:8–9"> for I honor you very greatly, and all that you say to me I do; and come, please pierce this people for me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 24:13; 1 Kings 22:14; 2 Chronicles 18:13"> And Balaam answers and says to the servants of Balak, “If Balak gives to me the fullness of his house of silver and gold, I am not able to pass over the command of my God YHWH, to do a little or a great thing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jude 1:11; 2 Peter 2:3; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> and now, please abide in this &#91;place&#93;, you also, tonight, then I know what YHWH is adding to speak with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 23:12; Numbers 24:13; Numbers 23:26"> And God comes to Balaam by night and says to him, “If the men have come to call for you, rise, go with them, and only the thing which I speak to you—do it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 1:15–16"> And Balaam rises in the morning, and saddles his donkey, and goes with the princes of Moab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 4:24; Lamentations 2:4; Numbers 22:32"> and the anger of God burns because he is going, and the Messenger of YHWH stations Himself in the way for an adversary to him, and he is riding on his donkey, and two of his servants &#91;are&#93; with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jude 1:11; 2 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29"> and the donkey sees the Messenger of YHWH standing in the way with His drawn sword in His hand, and the donkey turns aside out of the way and goes into a field, and Balaam strikes the donkey to turn it aside into the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And the Messenger of YHWH stands in a narrow path of the vineyards, &#91;with&#93; a wall on this &#91;side&#93; and a wall on that &#91;side&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 47:12; Job 5:13–15"> and the donkey sees the Messenger of YHWH, and is pressed to the wall, and presses Balaam’s foot to the wall, and he adds to strike her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 2:6; Isaiah 26:11"> and the Messenger of YHWH adds to pass over, and stands in a narrow place where there is no way to turn aside &#91;to&#93; the right or left,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 14:16; James 1:19; Proverbs 27:3–4"> and the donkey sees the Messenger of YHWH, and crouches under Balaam, and the anger of Balaam burns, and he strikes the donkey with a staff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Peter 2:16; Luke 1:37; Exodus 4:11"> And YHWH opens the mouth of the donkey, and she says to Balaam, “What have I done to you that you have struck me these three times?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 12:10; Matthew 15:19; Proverbs 12:16"> And Balaam says to the donkey, “Because you have rolled yourself against me; oh that there were a sword in my hand, for now I had slain you”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 1:27–28"> and the donkey says to Balaam, “Am I not your donkey on which you have ridden since &#91;I became&#93; yours, even to this day? Have I at all been accustomed to do thus to you?” And he says, “No.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 21:19; Luke 24:31; Acts 26:18"> And YHWH uncovers the eyes of Balaam, and he sees the Messenger of YHWH standing in the way with His drawn sword in His hand, and he bows and pays respect, to his face;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Acts 13:10; Numbers 22:22"> and the Messenger of YHWH says to him, “Why have you struck your donkey these three times? Behold, I have come out for an adversary, for &#91;your&#93; way has been perverse before Me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 16:33–35; Numbers 14:37; 1 Kings 13:24–28"> and the donkey sees Me and turns aside at My presence these three times; unless she had turned aside from My presence, surely I had also slain you now, and kept her alive.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 34:31–32; 2 Samuel 12:13; 1 Samuel 15:24"> And Balaam says to the Messenger of YHWH, “I have sinned, for I did not know that You &#91;are&#93; standing to meet me in the way; and now, if &#91;it is&#93; evil in Your eyes, I turn back by myself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–12; Isaiah 37:26–29; Numbers 22:20–21"> And the Messenger of YHWH says to Balaam, “Go with the men; and only the word which I speak to you—it you speak”; and Balaam goes with the princes of Balak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 16:2; Acts 28:15; Jeremiah 48:20"> And Balak hears that Balaam has come, and goes out to meet him, to a city of Moab, which &#91;is&#93; on the border of Arnon, which &#91;is&#93; in the extremity of the border;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 24:11; John 5:44; Matthew 4:8–9"> and Balak says to Balaam, “Did I not diligently send to you to call for you? Why did you not come to me? Am I not truly able to honor you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 22:18; Proverbs 19:21; Numbers 23:16"> And Balaam says to Balak, “Behold, I have come to you; now, am I able to speak anything at all? The word which God sets in my mouth—it I speak.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And Balaam goes with Balak, and they come to Kirjath-Huzoth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 23:14; Proverbs 1:16; Numbers 23:2"> and Balak sacrifices oxen and sheep, and sends &#91;some&#93; to Balaam and to the princes who &#91;are&#93; with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 23:13; Numbers 25:2–3; 2 Chronicles 11:15"> and it comes to pass in the morning, that Balak takes Balaam and causes him to go up &#91;to&#93; the high places of Ba‘al, and he sees the extremity of the people from there.</span>
