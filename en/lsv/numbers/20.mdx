<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; Exodus 15:20; Numbers 33:36"> And the sons of Israel, all the congregation, come &#91;into&#93; the wilderness of Zin in the first month, and the people abide in Kadesh; and Miriam dies there and is buried there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 16:19; Numbers 16:42; Exodus 15:23–24"> And there has been no water for the congregation, and they are assembled against Moses and against Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 17:2; Numbers 14:1–2; Numbers 16:31–35"> and the people strive with Moses and speak, saying, “And oh that we had expired when our brothers expired before YHWH!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 17:3; Psalms 106:21; Numbers 16:13–14"> And why have you brought in the assembly of YHWH to this wilderness to die there, we and our beasts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:14; Jeremiah 2:2; Jeremiah 2:6"> And why have you brought us up out of Egypt to bring us to this evil place? &#91;It is&#93; not a place of seed, and fig, and vine, and pomegranate; and there is no water to drink.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:5; Numbers 16:4; Numbers 16:19"> And Moses and Aaron go in from the presence of the assembly to the opening of the Tent of Meeting and fall on their faces, and the glory of YHWH is seen by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:17; Acts 2:1–4; Revelation 22:17"> “Take the rod and assemble the congregation—you and your brother Aaron—and you have spoken to the rock before their eyes, and it has given its water, and you have brought out water to them from the rock, and have watered the congregation and their beasts.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 17:10"> And Moses takes the rod from before YHWH as He has commanded him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 106:32–33; Acts 14:9–15; Luke 9:54–55"> and Moses and Aaron assemble the assembly to the front of the rock, and he says to them, “Now hear, O rebels, do we bring out water to you from this rock?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:4; Exodus 17:6; Deuteronomy 8:15"> And Moses lifts up his hand and strikes the rock with his rod twice; and much water comes out, and the congregation drinks, and their beasts &#91;drink&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Numbers 27:14; Ezekiel 36:23"> And YHWH says to Moses and to Aaron, “Because you have not believed in Me to sanctify Me before the eyes of the sons of Israel, therefore you do not bring in this assembly to the land which I have given to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Psalms 95:8; Deuteronomy 33:8"> These &#91;are&#93; the waters of Meribah, because the sons of Israel have “striven” with YHWH, and He is sanctified among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 11:16–17; Deuteronomy 23:7; Obadiah 1:10–12"> And Moses sends messengers from Kadesh to the king of Edom, “Thus said your brother Israel: You have known all the travail which has found us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 12:40; Deuteronomy 26:6; Genesis 46:6"> that our fathers go down to Egypt, and we dwell in Egypt &#91;for&#93; many days, and the Egyptians do evil to us and to our fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:19; Exodus 23:20; Exodus 33:2"> then we cry to YHWH, and He hears our voice, and sends a Messenger, and is bringing us out of Egypt; and behold, we &#91;are&#93; in Kadesh, a city &#91;in&#93; the extremity of your border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:27; Deuteronomy 2:29; Numbers 21:22–24"> Please let us pass over through your land; we do not pass over through a field or through a vineyard, nor do we drink waters of a well; we go the way of the king, we do not turn aside &#91;to&#93; the right or left until we pass over your border.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And Edom says to him, “You do not pass over through me, lest I come out to meet you with the sword.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:28; Deuteronomy 2:6; Exodus 12:38"> And the sons of Israel say to him, “We go in the highway, and if we drink of your waters—I and my livestock—then I have given their price; only, let me pass over on my feet, nothing &#91;more&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 11:17; Amos 1:11; Genesis 32:6"> And he says, “You do not pass over”; and Edom comes out to meet him with many people and with a strong hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:18; Deuteronomy 2:29; Deuteronomy 2:4–8"> and Edom refuses to permit Israel to pass over through his border, and Israel turns aside from off him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 20:14; Numbers 21:4"> And the sons of Israel, all the congregation, journey from Kadesh and come to Mount Hor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 33:37"> and YHWH speaks to Moses and to Aaron in Mount Hor, on the border of the land of Edom, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Deuteronomy 32:50; Numbers 31:2"> “Aaron is gathered to his people, for he does not go into the land which I have given to the sons of Israel, because that you provoked My mouth at the waters of Meribah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 33:38–39"> Take Aaron and his son Eleazar, and cause them to go up Mount Hor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 20:24; Isaiah 22:21–22; Hebrews 7:11"> and strip Aaron of his garments, and you have clothed his son Eleazar &#91;with&#93; them, and Aaron is gathered, and dies there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And Moses does as YHWH has commanded, and they go up to Mount Hor before the eyes of all the congregation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:6; Exodus 29:29–30; Deuteronomy 34:9"> and Moses strips Aaron of his garments, and clothes his son Eleazar with them, and Aaron dies there on the top of the mountain; and Moses comes down—Eleazar also—from the mountain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:8; 2 Chronicles 35:24–25; Genesis 1:10"> and all the congregation sees that Aaron has expired, and all the house of Israel laments Aaron &#91;for&#93; thirty days.</span>
