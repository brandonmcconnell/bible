<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 7:2; Deuteronomy 1:13–17; Exodus 18:25"> And Moses speaks to the heads of the tribes of the sons of Israel, saying, “This &#91;is&#93; the thing which YHWH has commanded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:14; Psalms 116:14; Ecclesiastes 5:4–5"> When a man vows a vow to YHWH, or has sworn an oath to bind a bond on his soul, he does not defile his word; he does according to all that is going out from his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And when a woman vows a vow to YHWH, and has bound a bond in the house of her father in her youth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and her father has heard her vow and her bond which she has bound on her soul, and her father has kept silent to her, then all her vows have been established, and every bond which she has bound on her soul is established.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 7:10–13; Matthew 15:4–6; Ephesians 6:1"> And if her father has disallowed her in the day of his hearing, none of her vows and her bonds which she has bound on her soul are established, and YHWH is propitious to her, for her father has disallowed her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 56:12"> And if she is having a husband, and her vows &#91;are&#93; on her, or a wrongful utterance &#91;on&#93; her lips which she has bound on her soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and her husband has heard, and in the day of his hearing he has kept silent to her, then her vows have been established, and her bonds which she has bound on her soul are established.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:16; Ephesians 5:22–24; 1 Corinthians 14:34"> And if in the day of her husband’s hearing he disallows her, then he has broken her vow which &#91;is&#93; on her, and the wrongful utterance of her lips which she has bound on her soul, and YHWH is propitious to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 2:37; Romans 7:2; Leviticus 21:7"> As for the vow of a widow or cast-out woman, all that she has bound on her soul is established on her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And if she has vowed &#91;in&#93; the house of her husband, or has bound a bond on her soul with an oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and her husband has heard, and has kept silent to her—he has not disallowed her—then all her vows have been established, and every bond which she has bound on her soul is established.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 30:8; 1 Corinthians 11:3; Numbers 15:25"> And if her husband certainly breaks them in the day of his hearing, none of the outgoing of her lips concerning her vows, or concerning the bond of her soul, is established—her husband has broken them—and YHWH is propitious to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3; 1 Peter 3:1–6; Isaiah 58:5"> Every vow and every oath—a bond to humble a soul—her husband establishes it, or her husband breaks it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and if her husband certainly keeps silent to her from day to day, then he has established all her vows or all her bonds which &#91;are&#93; on her; he has established them, for he has kept silent to her in the day of his hearing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Numbers 30:5; Numbers 30:8"> and if he indeed breaks them after his hearing, then he has borne her iniquity.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 14:54–57; Leviticus 11:46–47; Numbers 5:29–30"> These &#91;are&#93; the statutes which YHWH has commanded Moses between a man and his wife, between a father and his daughter, in her youth, &#91;in&#93; the house of her father.</span>
