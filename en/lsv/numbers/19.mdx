<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses and to Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 22:20–25; Deuteronomy 21:3; 1 Samuel 6:7"> “This &#91;is&#93; a statute of the law which YHWH has commanded, saying, Speak to the sons of Israel, and they bring a red cow to you, a perfect one, in which there is no blemish, on which no yoke has gone up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 4:12; Leviticus 4:21; Leviticus 16:27"> and you have given it to Eleazar the priest, and he has brought it out to the outside of the camp, and has slaughtered it before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 4:17; Leviticus 4:6; Leviticus 16:14"> And Eleazar the priest has taken of its blood with his finger, and has sprinkled &#91;it&#93; toward the front of the face of the Tent of Meeting from its blood seven times;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 4:11–12; Psalms 22:14"> and &#91;one&#93; has burned the cow before his eyes; her skin, and her flesh, and her blood, besides her dung, he burns;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 14:4; Leviticus 14:49; Psalms 51:7"> and the priest has taken cedar wood, and hyssop, and scarlet, and has cast &#91;them&#93; into the midst of the burning of the cow;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 11:40; Numbers 19:8"> and the priest has washed his garments, and has bathed his flesh with water, and afterward comes into the camp, and the priest is unclean until the evening;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and he who is burning it washes his garments with water, and has bathed his flesh with water, and is unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 9:13; Numbers 19:13; Numbers 19:20–21"> And a clean man has gathered the ashes of the cow, and has placed &#91;them&#93; at the outside of the camp in a clean place, and it has become a charge for the congregation of the sons of Israel for waters of separation—it &#91;is for&#93; sin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 12:49; Numbers 19:7–8; Colossians 3:11"> and he who is gathering the ashes of the heifer has washed his garments and is unclean until the evening; and it has been to the sons of Israel and to the sojourner who is sojourning in their midst for a continuous statute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; Numbers 31:19; Leviticus 21:1"> He who is coming against the dead body of any man is unclean &#91;for&#93; seven days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 31:19; Revelation 7:14; Leviticus 7:17"> he cleanses himself for it on the third day and on the seventh day, &#91;and&#93; he is clean; and if he does not cleanse himself on the third day and on the seventh day, he is not clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 15:31; Leviticus 22:3; Leviticus 7:20"> Anyone who is coming against the dead, against the body of man who dies, and does not cleanse himself, he has defiled the Dwelling Place of YHWH, and that person has been cut off from Israel, for water of separation is not sprinkled on him; he is unclean; his uncleanness &#91;is&#93; still on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> This &#91;is&#93; the law when a man dies in a tent: everyone who is coming into the tent and all that &#91;is&#93; in the tent are unclean &#91;for&#93; seven days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 14:36; Leviticus 11:32; Numbers 31:20"> and every open vessel which has no covering of thread on it is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 31:19; Matthew 23:27; Luke 11:44"> And everyone on the face of the field who comes against the pierced of a sword, or against the dead, or against a bone of man, or against a grave, is unclean &#91;for&#93; seven days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 7:38; Genesis 26:19; Revelation 7:17"> and for the unclean person they have taken from the ashes of the burning of the &#91;cow&#93;, and he has put running water over it into a vessel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25–27; Hebrews 9:14; Numbers 19:9"> and a clean person has taken hyssop, and has dipped &#91;it&#93; in water, and has sprinkled &#91;it&#93; on the tent, and on all the vessels, and on the persons who have been there, and on him who is coming against a bone, or against one pierced, or against the dead, or against a grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 1:7; Hebrews 10:22; Ezekiel 36:25"> And the clean has sprinkled &#91;it&#93; on the unclean on the third day and on the seventh day, and has cleansed him on the seventh day, and he has washed his garments, and has bathed with water, and has been clean in the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 16:16; Genesis 17:14; Numbers 19:13"> And the man who is unclean and does not cleanse himself, indeed, that person has been cut off from the midst of the assembly; for he has defiled the sanctuary of YHWH; water of separation is not sprinkled on him; he &#91;is&#93; unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 9:13–14; Hebrews 10:4; Hebrews 9:10"> And it has been for a continuous statute to them, that he who is sprinkling the water of separation washes his garments, and he who is coming against the water of separation is unclean until the evening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Haggai 2:13; Leviticus 7:19; Matthew 15:19–20"> and all against which the unclean person comes is unclean, and the person who is coming against &#91;it&#93; is unclean until the evening.”</span>
