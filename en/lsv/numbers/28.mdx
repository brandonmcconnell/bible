<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 3:11; Genesis 8:21; Numbers 9:2–3"> “Command the sons of Israel, and you have said to them: My offering, My bread for My fire-offerings, My refreshing fragrance, you take heed to bring near to Me in its appointed time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 6:9; Daniel 11:31; John 1:29"> And you have said to them: This &#91;is&#93; the fire-offering which you bring near to YHWH: two lambs, sons of a year, perfect ones, daily, &#91;for&#93; a continual burnt-offering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 9:21; 1 Kings 18:36; Exodus 12:6"> you prepare the first lamb in the morning, and you prepare the second lamb between the evenings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 16:36; Exodus 29:38–42"> and a tenth of the ephah of flour for a present mixed with a fourth of the hin of beaten oil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 5:25; Leviticus 6:9; Psalms 50:8"> &#91;it is&#93; a continual burnt-offering, which was made in Mount Sinai for refreshing fragrance, &#91;for&#93; a fire-offering to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 29:42; Numbers 28:14; Isaiah 57:6"> And its drink-offering &#91;is&#93; a fourth of the hin for one lamb; pour out a drink-offering of strong drink to YHWH in the holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And you prepare the second lamb between the evenings; as the present of the morning and as its drink-offering, you prepare &#91;it as&#93; a fire-offering of refreshing fragrance to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 58:13; Psalms 92:1–4; Revelation 1:10"> And on the Sabbath day, two lambs, sons of a year, perfect ones, and two-tenth parts of flour, a present mixed with oil, and its drink-offering—</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:4–5; Numbers 29:22; Numbers 29:31"> the burnt-offering of the Sabbath in its Sabbath, besides the continual burnt-offering and its drink-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; Ezekiel 46:6; Ezra 3:5"> And in the beginnings of your months you bring a burnt-offering near to YHWH: two bullocks, sons of the herd, and one ram, seven lambs, sons of a year, perfect ones;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 15:4–12; Ezekiel 46:5–7; Numbers 29:10"> and three-tenth parts of flour, a present mixed with oil, for one bullock, and two-tenth parts of flour, a present mixed with oil, for one ram;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and a tenth—a tenth part of flour, a present mixed with oil, for one lamb, &#91;for&#93; a burnt-offering, a refreshing fragrance, a fire-offering to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and their drink-offerings are a half of the hin for a bullock, and a third of the hin for a ram, and a fourth of the hin for a lamb, of wine; this &#91;is&#93; the burnt-offering of every month for the months of the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Numbers 28:22; Numbers 15:24"> And one kid of the goats is prepared for a sin-offering to YHWH, besides the continual burnt-offering and its drink-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–8; Exodus 12:18; Leviticus 23:5–8"> And in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, &#91;is&#93; the Passover to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 23:6; Exodus 23:15; Exodus 13:6"> and in the fifteenth day of this month &#91;is&#93; a festival; &#91;for&#93; seven days unleavened bread is eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 12:16; Leviticus 23:7–8"> In the first day &#91;is&#93; a holy convocation; you do no servile work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Ezekiel 45:21–25; Numbers 29:8"> and you have brought a fire-offering near, a burnt-offering to YHWH: two bullocks, sons of the herd, and one ram, and seven lambs, sons of a year, they are perfect ones for you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> and their present of flour mixed with oil—you prepare three-tenth parts for a bullock and two-tenth parts for a ram;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> you prepare a tenth—a tenth part for one lamb, for the seven lambs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 8:3"> and one goat &#91;for&#93; a sin-offering, to make atonement for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Numbers 28:10"> Apart from the burnt-offering of the morning, which &#91;is&#93; for the continual burnt-offering, you prepare these;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> like these you prepare bread of a fire-offering daily &#91;for&#93; seven days, a refreshing fragrance to YHWH; it is prepared besides the continual burnt-offering and its drink-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:8; Numbers 28:18; Exodus 13:6"> And on the seventh day you have a holy convocation; you do no servile work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Exodus 34:22; Leviticus 23:10"> And on the day of the first-fruits, in your bringing a new present near to YHWH, in your weeks, you have a holy convocation; you do no servile work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 23:18–19; Numbers 28:11; Numbers 28:19"> And you have brought a burnt-offering near for refreshing fragrance to YHWH: two bullocks, sons of the herd, one ram, seven lambs, sons of a year,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and their present, flour mixed with oil, three-tenth parts for one bullock, two-tenth parts for one ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> a tenth—a tenth part for one lamb, for the seven lambs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 28:15; Numbers 28:22; 1 Peter 2:24"> one kid of the goats to make atonement for you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 28:19; Numbers 28:3; Malachi 1:13–14"> apart from the continual burnt-offering and its present, you prepare &#91;them&#93; and their drink-offerings; they are perfect ones for you.”</span>
</div>
