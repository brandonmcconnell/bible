<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 23:3; Numbers 23:23; Numbers 23:28"> And Balaam sees that &#91;it is&#93; good in the eyes of YHWH to bless Israel, and he has not gone as time by time to seek enchantments, and he sets his face toward the wilderness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:20; 2 Chronicles 15:1; 1 Samuel 19:23"> and Balaam lifts up his eyes, and sees Israel dwelling by its tribes, and the Spirit of God is on him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 22:31; Numbers 24:16; Numbers 23:7"> and he takes up his allegory and says: “An affirmation of Balaam son of Beor, &#124;&#124; And an affirmation of the man whose eyes are shut,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Revelation 1:10; Numbers 12:6"> An affirmation of him who is hearing sayings of God, &#124;&#124; Who sees a vision of the Almighty, &#124;&#124; Falling—and eyes uncovered:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> How good have been your tents, O Jacob, &#124;&#124; Your dwelling places, O Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Isaiah 58:11; Psalms 104:16"> They have been stretched out as valleys, &#124;&#124; As gardens by a river; As aloes YHWH has planted, &#124;&#124; As cedars by waters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:12; 1 Chronicles 14:2; Revelation 17:1"> He makes water flow from his buckets, &#124;&#124; And his seed &#91;is&#93; in many waters; And his King &#91;is&#93; higher than Gog &#91;&#91;or Agag&#93;&#93;, &#124;&#124; And his kingdom is exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 45:5; Psalms 2:9; Numbers 14:9"> God is bringing him out of Egypt, &#124;&#124; As the swiftness of a wild ox for him, &#124;&#124; He eats up nations—his adversaries, &#124;&#124; And he breaks their bones, &#124;&#124; And he strikes &#91;with&#93; his arrows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Genesis 27:29; Genesis 49:9"> He has bent, he has lain down as a lion, &#124;&#124; And as a lioness, who raises him up? He who is blessing you &#91;is&#93; blessed, &#124;&#124; And he who is cursing you &#91;is&#93; cursed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 23:11; Ezekiel 21:14; Ezekiel 21:17"> And the anger of Balak burns against Balaam, and he strikes his hands; and Balak says to Balaam, “I called you to pierce my enemies, and behold, you have certainly blessed these three times;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 22:17; Numbers 22:37; Hebrews 11:24–26"> and now, flee for yourself to your place; I have said, I greatly honor you, and behold, YHWH has kept you back from honor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:18; Numbers 22:38"> And Balaam says to Balak, “Did I not also speak to your messengers whom you have sent to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 16:28; Numbers 22:18; Numbers 22:20"> If Balak gives to me the fullness of his house of silver and gold, I am not able to pass over the command of YHWH, to do good or evil of my own heart; that which YHWH speaks—it I speak?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 6:5; Revelation 2:14; Genesis 49:1"> And now, behold, I am going to my people; come, I counsel you &#91;concerning&#93; that which this people does to your people in the latter end of the days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 24:3–4; Numbers 23:7; Matthew 13:35"> And he takes up his allegory and says: “An affirmation of Balaam son of Beor, &#124;&#124; And an affirmation of the man whose eyes &#91;are&#93; shut;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 24:4; 1 Corinthians 8:1; 2 Samuel 23:1–2"> An affirmation of him who is hearing sayings of God, &#124;&#124; And knowing knowledge of the Most High; He sees a vision of the Almighty, &#124;&#124; Falling—and eyes uncovered:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Luke 1:32–33; Genesis 49:10"> I see Him, but not now; I behold Him, but not near; A star has proceeded from Jacob, &#124;&#124; And a scepter has risen from Israel, &#124;&#124; And has struck corners of Moab, &#124;&#124; And has destroyed all sons of Seth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 9:12; Genesis 27:29; 2 Samuel 8:14"> And Edom has been a possession, &#124;&#124; And Seir has been a possession &#91;for&#93; his enemies, &#124;&#124; And Israel is doing valiantly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 49:10; Micah 5:2; Psalms 2:1–12"> And &#91;One&#93; rules out of Jacob, &#124;&#124; And has destroyed a remnant from the city.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 17:14; Judges 6:3; 1 Chronicles 4:43"> And he sees Amalek, and takes up his allegory, and says: “Amalek &#91;is&#93; first &#91;among&#93; nations, &#124;&#124; But his latter end—destruction forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:19; Judges 1:16; Job 29:18"> And he sees the Kenite, and takes up his allegory, and says: “Your dwelling &#91;is&#93; enduring, &#124;&#124; And your nest is being set in a rock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 83:8; Genesis 10:22; Ezra 4:2"> But the Kenite is for a burning; Until when does Asshur keep you captive?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 23:23; Malachi 3:2; 2 Kings 5:1"> And he takes up his allegory and says: “Woe! Who lives when God does this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Numbers 24:20; Daniel 10:20"> But ships &#91;come&#93; from the side of Chittim, &#124;&#124; And they have humbled Asshur, &#124;&#124; And they have humbled Eber, &#124;&#124; But it is also perishing forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Joshua 13:22; Numbers 24:11"> And Balaam rises, and goes, and turns back to his place, and Balak has also gone on his way.</span>
