<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Psalms 105:11; Deuteronomy 1:7–8"> “Command the sons of Israel, and you have said to them: When you are coming into the land of Canaan—this &#91;is&#93; the land which falls to you by inheritance, the land of Canaan, by its borders—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Ezekiel 47:13; Joshua 3:16"> then the south quarter has been to you from the wilderness of Zin, by the sides of Edom, indeed, the south border has been to you from the extremity of the Salt Sea eastward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Joshua 15:3–4; Numbers 13:26"> and the border has turned around to you from the south to the ascent of Akrabbim, and has passed on to Zin, and its outgoings have been from the south to Kadesh-Barnea, and it has gone out at Hazar-Addar, and has passed on to Azmon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 15:4; Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 8:65"> and the border has turned around from Azmon to the Brook of Egypt, and its outgoings have been at the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 1:4; Ezekiel 47:20; Ezekiel 47:15"> As for the west border, even the Great Sea has been a border to you; this is the west border to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:37; Numbers 34:6; Numbers 34:3"> And this is the north border to you: from the Great Sea you mark out for yourselves Mount Hor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; 2 Kings 14:25; Joshua 13:5–6"> from Mount Hor you mark out to go to Hamath, and the outgoings of the border have been to Zedad;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:17"> and the border has gone out to Ziphron, and its outgoings have been at Hazar-Enan; this is the north border to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And you have marked out for yourselves for the border eastward, from Hazar-Enan to Shepham;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33; Deuteronomy 3:17; Joshua 11:2"> and the border has gone down from Shepham to Riblah, on the east of Ain, and the border has gone down, and has struck against the shoulder of the Sea of Chinnereth eastward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 34:3; Genesis 13:10; Genesis 19:24–26"> and the border has gone down to the Jordan, and its outgoings have been at the Salt Sea; this is the land for you by its borders all around.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 34:1; Joshua 14:1–5"> And Moses commands the sons of Israel, saying, “This &#91;is&#93; the land which you inherit by lot, which YHWH has commanded to give to the nine tribes and the half of the tribe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 32:33; Numbers 32:23; Joshua 14:2–3"> for the tribe of the sons of Reuben, by the house of their fathers, and the tribe of the children of Gad, by the house of their fathers, have received &#91;their inheritance&#93;; and &#91;those of&#93; the half of the tribe of Manasseh have received their inheritance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 32:32"> the two tribes and the half of the tribe have received their inheritance beyond the Jordan, &#91;near&#93; Jericho, eastward, at the &#91;sun&#93;-rising.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Joshua 19:51; Numbers 13:8"> “These &#91;are&#93; the names of the men who give the inheritance of the land to you: Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:4–16"> And one prince—you take one prince from a tribe to give the land by inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 26:65; Numbers 13:30; Numbers 14:24"> And these &#91;are&#93; the names of the men: of the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:5"> and of the tribe of the sons of Simeon, Shemuel son of Aminihud;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Psalms 68:27"> of the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad son of Chislon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and a prince of the tribe of the sons of Dan, Bukki son of Jogli;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> of the sons of Joseph, a prince of the tribe of the sons of Manasseh, Hanniel son of Ephod;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and a prince of the tribe of the sons of Ephraim, Kemuel son of Shiphtan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and a prince of the tribe of the sons of Zebulun, Elizaphan son of Parnach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and a prince of the tribe of the sons of Issachar, Paltiel son of Azzan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and a prince of the tribe of the sons of Asher, Ahihud son of Shelomi;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and a prince of the tribe of the sons of Naphtali, Pedahel son of Ammihud.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:51; Numbers 34:18"> These &#91;are&#93; those whom YHWH has commanded to give the sons of Israel inheritance in the land of Canaan.</span>
</div>
