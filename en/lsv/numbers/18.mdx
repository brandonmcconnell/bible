<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 28:38; Hebrews 13:17; 1 Peter 2:24"> And YHWH says to Aaron, “You, and your sons, and the house of your father with you, bear the iniquity of the sanctuary; and you and your sons with you bear the iniquity of your priesthood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 29:34; Numbers 18:4; Ezekiel 44:15"> and also your brothers, the tribe of Levi, the tribe of your father, bring near with you, and they are joined to you, and serve you, even you and your sons with you, before the Tent of the Testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Numbers 16:40; Numbers 4:19–20"> And they have kept your charge, and the charge of all the tent; only, they do not come near to the vessels of the holy place and to the altar, and they do not die, either they or you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19; Numbers 1:51; Numbers 3:10"> and they have been joined to you and have kept the charge of the Tent of Meeting for all the service of the tent; and a stranger does not come near to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 27:21; Leviticus 24:3; Numbers 16:46"> and you have kept the charge of the holy place and the charge of the altar, and there is no more wrath against the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 3:9; Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:45"> And I, behold, have taken your brothers the Levites from the midst of the sons of Israel; they are a gift given to you by YHWH to do the service of the Tent of Meeting;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 15:15–16; John 3:27; Numbers 3:10"> and you and your sons with you keep your priesthood for everything of the altar and within the veil, and you have served; I make your priesthood a gift &#91;for&#93; service; and the stranger who is coming near is put to death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 6:16; Leviticus 7:6; Exodus 29:29"> And YHWH speaks to Aaron: “And I, behold, have given to you the charge of My raised-offerings, of all the hallowed things of the sons of Israel—I have given them to you and to your sons for the anointing, by a continuous statute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 7:7; Leviticus 14:13; Leviticus 6:25–26"> This is yours from the most holy things, from the fire: all their offering, of all their present, and of all their sin-offering, and of all their guilt-offering, which they give back to Me, is most holy to you and to your sons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 7:6; Leviticus 6:29; Leviticus 6:16"> you eat it among the most holy things; every male eats it; it is holy to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:3; Exodus 29:27–28; Leviticus 10:14"> And this &#91;is&#93; yours: the raised-offering of their gift, with all the wave-offerings of the sons of Israel; I have given them to you, and to your sons, and to your daughters with you, by a continuous statute; every clean one in your house eats it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:4; Exodus 23:19; Exodus 34:26"> all the best of the oil, and all the best of the new wine, and wheat—their first-&#91;fruits&#93; which they give to YHWH—I have given them to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:29; Exodus 23:19; Micah 7:1"> The first-fruits of all that &#91;is&#93; in their land, which they bring to YHWH, are yours; every clean one in your house eats it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 27:28; Ezekiel 44:29"> every devoted thing in Israel is yours;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:20; Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12–13"> everyone opening a womb of all flesh which they bring near to YHWH, among man and among beast, is yours; only, you certainly ransom the firstborn of man, and you ransom the firstling of the unclean beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:2–7"> And their ransomed ones from a son of a month, you ransom with your valuation &#91;for&#93; five shekels of silver, by the shekel of the holy place; it &#91;is&#93; twenty gerahs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 29:16; Deuteronomy 15:19–22; Leviticus 3:2–5"> Only, the firstling of a cow, or the firstling of a sheep, or the firstling of a goat, you do not ransom, they &#91;are&#93; holy: you sprinkle their blood on the altar, and you make incense of their fat &#91;as&#93; a fire-offering of refreshing fragrance to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 7:31–34; Exodus 29:26–28"> and their flesh is yours, as the breast of the wave-offering and as the right leg are yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:5; Leviticus 2:13; Numbers 18:11"> All the raised-offerings of the holy things, which the sons of Israel lift up to YHWH, I have given to you, and to your sons, and to your daughters with you, by a continuous statute—a covenant of salt; it &#91;is&#93; continuous before YHWH to you and to your seed with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:28; Deuteronomy 10:9; Joshua 13:33"> And YHWH says to Aaron, “You do not inherit in their land, and you do not have a portion in their midst: I &#91;am&#93; your portion and your inheritance in the midst of the sons of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44; Deuteronomy 14:22–29; Nehemiah 10:37"> and to the sons of Levi, behold, have given all the tenth in Israel for inheritance in exchange for their service which they are serving—the service of the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 22:9; Numbers 1:51–52; Numbers 3:38"> And the sons of Israel no longer come near to the Tent of Meeting, to bear sin, to die,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 3:7; Numbers 18:20; Numbers 18:1"> and the Levites have done the service of the Tent of Meeting, and they bear their iniquity; &#91;it is&#93; a continuous statute throughout your generations that they have no inheritance in the midst of the sons of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 18:26; Malachi 3:8–10"> but the tithe of the sons of Israel which they lift up to YHWH, a raised-offering, I have given to the Levites for inheritance; therefore I have said of them, They have no inheritance in the midst of the sons of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:38; Numbers 18:21"> “And you speak to the Levites, and you have said to them: When you take the tithe from the sons of Israel which I have given to you from them for your inheritance, then you have lifted up the raised-offering of YHWH from it, a tithe of the tithe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 18:30; Deuteronomy 15:14; 2 Kings 6:27"> and your raised-offering has been reckoned to you as grain from the threshing-floor and as the fullness from the wine-vat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 6:20–10; Genesis 14:18"> so you lift up—you also—the raised-offering of YHWH from all your tithes which you receive from the sons of Israel; and you have given the raised-offering of YHWH from it to Aaron the priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> out of all your gifts you lift up every raised-offering of YHWH, out of all its fat—its hallowed part—out of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Genesis 43:11; Matthew 6:33"> And you have said to them: In your lifting up its fat out of it, then it has been reckoned to the Levites as increase of a threshing-floor and as increase of a wine-vat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:10–14; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; Luke 10:7"> and you have eaten it in every place, you and your households, for it &#91;is&#93; your hire in exchange for your service in the Tent of Meeting;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 19:8; Leviticus 22:2; Malachi 1:7"> and you bear no sin for it in your lifting up its fat out of it, but you do not defile the holy things of the sons of Israel, and you do not die.”</span>
