<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 4:17; Ezekiel 19:14; Genesis 49:10"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, and take a rod from each of them, for a father’s house, from all their princes, for the house of their fathers—twelve rods; you write the name of each on his rod,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 6:20; Numbers 18:1; Numbers 3:2–3"> and you write Aaron’s name on the tribe of Levi; for one rod &#91;is&#93; for the head of their fathers’ house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 30:36; Exodus 29:42–43; Exodus 25:16–22"> And you have placed them in the Tent of Meeting before the Testimony, where I meet with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:5; Numbers 17:8; Numbers 16:11"> And it has come to pass, the man’s rod on whom I fix flourishes, and I have caused the murmurings of the sons of Israel, which they are murmuring against you, to cease from off Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Moses speaks to the sons of Israel, and all their princes give to him one rod for a prince, one rod for a prince, for their fathers’ house—twelve rods; and the rod of Aaron &#91;is&#93; in the midst of their rods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 7:44; Exodus 38:21; Numbers 18:2"> and Moses places the rods before YHWH in the Tent of the Testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; John 15:1–6; Psalms 132:17–18"> And it comes to pass, on the next day, that Moses goes into the Tent of the Testimony, and behold, the rod of Aaron has flourished for the house of Levi, and brings out a bud, and blossoms &#91;with&#93; a blossom, and produces almonds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and Moses brings out all the rods from before YHWH to all the sons of Israel, and they look, and each takes his rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Ephesians 5:6; Isaiah 1:2"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Put back the rod of Aaron before the Testimony, for a charge, for a sign to the sons of rebellion, and you remove their murmurings from off Me, and they do not die”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and Moses does as YHWH has commanded him; so he has done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 26:11; Isaiah 57:16; Isaiah 6:5"> And the sons of Israel speak to Moses, saying, “Behold, we have expired, we have perished, all of us have perished!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; 2 Samuel 6:6–12; Genesis 3:3"> Any who is at all drawing near to the Dwelling Place of YHWH dies; have we not been consumed—to expire?”</span>
