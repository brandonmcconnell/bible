<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 22:1; Numbers 31:12; Numbers 33:50"> And YHWH speaks to Moses in the plains of Moab by the Jordan, &#91;near&#93; Jericho, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 14:3–4; Ezekiel 48:8; Joshua 21:2–42"> “Command the sons of Israel, and they have given to the Levites cities to inhabit from the inheritance of their possession; you also give &#91;the&#93; outskirt around them, of the cities, to the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:14; Joshua 21:11; Ezekiel 45:2"> And the cities have been for them to inhabit, and their outskirts are for their livestock, and for their goods, and for all their beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And the outskirts of the cities which you give to the Levites &#91;are&#93;, from the wall of the city and outside, one thousand cubits around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And you have measured from the outside of the city: the east quarter—two thousand by the cubit, and the south quarter—two thousand by the cubit, and the west quarter—two thousand by the cubit, and the north quarter—two thousand by the cubit; and the city &#91;is&#93; in the midst; this is the outskirts of the cities to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 21:13; Joshua 21:3; Psalms 9:9"> And the cities which you give to the Levites &#91;are&#93; the six cities of refuge, which you give for the fleeing there of the manslayer, and besides them you give forty-two cities;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:3–42; 1 Chronicles 6:54–81"> all the cities which you give to the Levites &#91;are&#93; forty-eight cities, them and their outskirts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 26:54; Numbers 33:54; Joshua 21:1–42"> And the cities which you give &#91;are&#93; from the possession of the sons of Israel; from the many you multiply, and from the few you diminish; each, according to his inheritance which they inherit, gives to the Levites from his cities.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:9; Deuteronomy 19:1–2; Leviticus 14:34"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, and you have said to them: When you are passing over the Jordan to the land of Canaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:2; Numbers 35:6"> and have prepared cities for yourselves—they are cities of refuge to you—then a manslayer, striking a person through ignorance, has fled there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 20:3–6; Deuteronomy 19:6; Joshua 20:9"> and the cities have been for a refuge to you from the redeemer, and the manslayer does not die until his standing before the congregation for judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> As for the cities which you give, six &#91;are&#93; cities of refuge to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:8–10; Deuteronomy 4:41–43; Joshua 20:7–9"> three of the cities you give from beyond the Jordan, and three of the cities you give in the land of Canaan; they are cities of refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 15:16; Galatians 3:28; Romans 3:29"> For sons of Israel, and for a sojourner, and for a settler in their midst, are these six cities for a refuge, for the fleeing there of anyone striking a person through ignorance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17; Genesis 9:5–6; Deuteronomy 19:11–13"> And if he has struck him with an instrument of iron, and he dies, he &#91;is&#93; a murderer: the murderer is certainly put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 21:18"> And if he has struck him with a stone &#91;in&#93; the hand, with which he could die, and he dies, he &#91;is&#93; a murderer: the murderer is certainly put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Or &#91;if&#93; he has struck him with a wooden instrument &#91;in&#93; the hand, with which he could die, and he dies, he &#91;is&#93; a murderer: the murderer is certainly put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 20:3; Deuteronomy 19:6; Deuteronomy 19:12"> The redeemer of blood puts the murderer to death himself; in his coming against him he puts him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 21:14; Deuteronomy 19:11; 2 Samuel 3:27"> And if he pushes him in hatred, or has cast &#91;anything&#93; at him by lying in wait, and he dies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> or he has struck him with his hand in enmity, and he dies, the striker is certainly put to death; he &#91;is&#93; a murderer; the redeemer of blood puts the murderer to death in his coming against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 35:11; Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:3"> And if, in an instant, without enmity, he has pushed him, or has cast any instrument at him without lying in wait,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> or with any stone with which he could die, without seeing, indeed, causes &#91;it&#93; to fall on him, and he dies, and he &#91;is&#93; not his enemy, nor seeking his evil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 20:6; Numbers 35:12"> then the congregation has judged between the striker and the redeemer of blood by these judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 29:7; Leviticus 4:3; Romans 3:24–26"> And the congregation has delivered the manslayer out of the hand of the redeemer of blood, and the congregation has caused him to return to the city of his refuge, to where he has fled, and he has dwelt in it until the death of the chief priest who has been anointed with the holy oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And if the manslayer indeed goes out &#91;from&#93; the border of the city of his refuge to where he flees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 22:2; Deuteronomy 19:10; Deuteronomy 19:6"> and the redeemer of blood has found him at the outside of the border of the city of his refuge, and the redeemer of blood has slain the manslayer, blood &#91;is&#93; not &#91;reckoned&#93; to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 10:26–30; Hebrews 6:4–8"> for he dwells in the city of his refuge until the death of the chief priest; and after the death of the chief priest the manslayer turns back to the city of his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 27:11; Numbers 27:1"> And these things have been for a statute of judgment to you throughout your generations, in all your dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28; Deuteronomy 19:15; Matthew 18:16"> Whoever strikes a person, by the mouth of witnesses &#91;one&#93; slays the murderer; and one witness does not testify against a person to cause &#91;him&#93; to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:11–13; Genesis 9:5–6; Psalms 51:14"> And you take no atonement for the life of a murderer who &#91;is&#93; condemned to die, for he is certainly put to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 4:12; Galatians 3:10–13; Galatians 2:21"> and you take no atonement for him &#91;who had&#93; to flee to the city of his refuge, to return to dwell in the land, until the death of the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 11:50–51; Psalms 106:38; Hosea 4:2–3"> And you do not profane the land which you &#91;are&#93; in, for blood profanes the land; as for the land, it is not pardoned for blood which is shed in it except by the blood of him who sheds it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 5:3; Psalms 135:21; Revelation 21:27"> and you do not defile the land in which you are dwelling, in the midst of which I dwell, for I, YHWH, dwell in the midst of the sons of Israel.”</span>
