<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 27:13; Numbers 25:14–18; Revelation 6:10"> “Execute the vengeance of the sons of Israel against the Midianites—afterward you are gathered to your people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Numbers 25:11; 2 Kings 10:30"> And Moses speaks to the people, saying, “Arm men from &#91;among&#93; you for the war, and they are against Midian, to put the vengeance of YHWH on Midian;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:8; 1 Samuel 14:6; Judges 7:2"> one thousand for a tribe—you send one thousand for a tribe, of all the tribes of Israel, to the war.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And there are given out of the thousands of Israel one thousand for a tribe—twelve thousand armed ones &#91;for&#93; war;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:44; Numbers 10:8–9; 2 Chronicles 13:12–15"> and Moses sends them, one thousand for a tribe, to the war; &#91;he sent&#93; them and Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest to the war, with the holy vessels and the trumpets of shouting in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 21:11; 1 Kings 11:15–16; 1 Samuel 27:9"> And they war against Midian, as YHWH has commanded Moses, and slay every male;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:21–22; Numbers 25:15; Revelation 2:14"> and they have slain the kings of Midian besides their pierced ones: Evi, and Rekem, and Zur, and Hur, and Reba—five kings of Midian; and they have slain Balaam son of Beor with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; 2 Chronicles 28:5"> And the sons of Israel take the women of Midian and their infants captive; and they have plundered all their livestock, and all their substance, and all their wealth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; 1 Samuel 30:1; Joshua 6:24"> and they have burned all their cities, with their habitations, and all their towers, with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 8:2"> And they take all the spoil and all the prey, among man and among beast;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:1"> and they bring in the captives, and the prey, and the spoil to Moses, and to Eleazar the priest, and to the congregation of the sons of Israel, to the camp, to the plains of Moab, which &#91;are&#93; by the Jordan, &#91;near&#93; Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; 1 Samuel 30:21; Numbers 31:12"> And Moses, and Eleazar the priest, and all the princes of the congregation, go out to meet them, to the outside of the camp,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 13:19; 1 Samuel 15:13–14; Numbers 31:48"> and Moses is angry against the inspectors of the force, chiefs of the thousands, and chiefs of the hundreds, who are coming in from the warfare of battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Jeremiah 48:10; Deuteronomy 2:34"> And Moses says to them, “Have you kept every female alive?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 2 Peter 2:15; Revelation 2:14"> Behold, they have been to the sons of Israel, through the word of Balaam, to cause a trespass against YHWH in the matter of Peor, and the plague is in the congregation of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 21:11–12; Deuteronomy 20:16–18; Deuteronomy 7:2"> And now, slay every male among the infants, indeed, slay every woman knowing a man by the lying of a male;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:10–14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; Deuteronomy 20:14"> and all the infants among the women who have not known the lying of a male you have kept alive for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 19:11–22; Numbers 5:2; 1 Chronicles 22:8"> And you, encamp at the outside of the camp &#91;for&#93; seven days; any who has slain a person, and any who has come against a pierced one, cleanse yourselves on the third day and on the seventh day—you and your captives;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 19:22; Genesis 35:2; Exodus 19:10"> and every garment, and every skin vessel, and every work of goats’ &#91;hair&#93;, and every wooden vessel, you yourselves cleanse.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 30:16"> And Eleazar the priest says to the men of war who go to battle, “This &#91;is&#93; the statute of the law which YHWH has commanded Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> only the gold, and the silver, the bronze, the iron, the tin, and the lead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 19:9; Numbers 19:17; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> everything which may go into fire, you cause to pass over through fire, and it has been clean; it is surely cleansed with the water of separation; and all that may not go into fire, you cause to pass over through water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 15:13; Leviticus 14:9"> and you have washed your garments on the seventh day and have been clean, and afterward you come into the camp.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> “Take up the sum of the prey of the captives, among man and among beast, you, and Eleazar the priest, and the heads of the fathers of the congregation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 22:8; Psalms 68:12; 1 Samuel 30:24–25"> and you have halved the prey between those handling the battle, who go out to the war, and all the congregation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 18:26; Numbers 31:30; Numbers 31:47"> and you have raised a tribute to YHWH from the men of battle, who go out to the war, one body out of five hundred, of man, and of the herd, and of the donkeys, and of the flock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 29:27; Deuteronomy 12:19; Deuteronomy 12:12"> you take from their half and have given &#91;it&#93; to Eleazar the priest &#91;as&#93; a raised-offering of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 3:7–8; Numbers 3:25"> And from the sons of Israel’s half you take one possession out of fifty, of man, of the herd, of the donkeys, and of the flock, of all the livestock, and you have given them to the Levites keeping the charge of the Dwelling Place of YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Moses does—Eleazar the priest also—as YHWH has commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the prey, the remainder of the spoil which the people of the host have spoiled, is six hundred thousand and seventy thousand and five thousand of the flock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and seventy-two thousand of the herd;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> and sixty-one thousand donkeys;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and of mankind—of the women who have not known the lying of a male—all the persons &#91;are&#93; thirty-two thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And the half, the portion of those who go out into the war, is &#91;in&#93; number three hundred thousand and thirty thousand and seven thousand and five hundred of the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And the tribute to YHWH of the sheep is six hundred seventy-five;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and the herd &#91;is&#93; thirty-six thousand, and their tribute to YHWH &#91;is&#93; seventy-two;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> and the donkeys &#91;are&#93; thirty thousand and five hundred, and their tribute to YHWH &#91;is&#93; sixty-one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and of mankind &#91;are&#93; sixteen thousand, and their tribute to YHWH &#91;is&#93; thirty-two persons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 18:8; Numbers 18:19; Hebrews 7:9–12"> And Moses gives the tribute—YHWH’s raised-offering—to Eleazar the priest, as YHWH has commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And of the sons of Israel’s half, which Moses halved from the men who war—</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> and the congregation’s half is three hundred thousand and thirty thousand &#91;and&#93; seven thousand and five hundred of the flock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and thirty-six thousand of the herd;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> and thirty thousand and five hundred donkeys;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> and sixteen thousand of mankind—</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 31:30; Luke 10:1–8; Isaiah 56:10–11"> Moses takes from the sons of Israel’s half one possession from the fifty, of man and of beast, and gives them to the Levites keeping the charge of the Dwelling Place of YHWH, as YHWH has commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And the inspectors whom the thousands of the host have (heads of the thousands and heads of the hundreds), draw near to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 18:9; 1 Samuel 30:18–19; Psalms 72:14"> and they say to Moses, “Your servants have taken up the sum of the men of war who &#91;are&#93; with us, and not a man of us has been missed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 30:12; Exodus 30:15–16; Psalms 116:12"> and we bring YHWH’s offering near, each of that which he has found of vessels of gold—chain, and bracelet, seal-ring, &#91;ear&#93;-ring, and bead—to make atonement for ourselves before YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 7:2–6"> And Moses receives—Eleazar the priest also—the gold from them, every vessel of workmanship;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> and all the gold of the raised-offering which they have lifted up to YHWH is sixteen thousand seven hundred and fifty shekels, from heads of the thousands and from heads of the hundreds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:32"> (the men of the host have each taken spoil for himself);</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 30:16; Zechariah 6:14; Acts 10:4"> and Moses takes—Eleazar the priest also—the gold from the heads of the thousands and of the hundreds, and they bring it into the Tent of Meeting &#91;as&#93; a memorial for the sons of Israel before YHWH.</span>
</div>
