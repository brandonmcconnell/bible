<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Exodus 19:1; Numbers 10:11–12"> And YHWH speaks to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the Tent of Meeting, on the first of the second month, in the second year of their going out of the land of Egypt, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 38:26; Exodus 30:12; Numbers 1:18"> “Take a census of all the congregation of the sons of Israel by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names—every male by their counted heads;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 30:14; Numbers 14:29; Numbers 33:1"> from a son of twenty years and upward, everyone going out to the host in Israel, you number them by their hosts, you and Aaron;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 1:16; Exodus 18:25; Numbers 13:2–15"> and with you there is a man for a tribe, each is a head of the house of his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 7:30; Genesis 30:5–20; Genesis 49:1–33"> And these &#91;are&#93; the names of the men who stand with you: for Reuben, Elizur son of Shedeur;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 7:36; Numbers 2:12"> for Simeon, Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai;</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 7:54; Numbers 7:48; 1 Chronicles 7:26–27"> for the sons of Joseph: for Ephraim, Elishama son of Ammihud; for Manasseh, Gamaliel son of Pedahzur;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 7:60; Numbers 10:24; Numbers 2:22"> for Benjamin, Abidan son of Gideoni;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 7:66; Numbers 2:25; Numbers 10:25"> for Dan, Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 7:72; Numbers 10:26; Numbers 2:27"> for Asher, Pagiel son of Ocran;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 2:14; Numbers 7:42; Numbers 10:20"> for Gad, Eliasaph son of Deuel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 7:78; Numbers 10:27; Numbers 2:29"> for Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 7:2; Exodus 18:21; Exodus 18:25"> These &#91;are&#93; those called of the congregation, princes of the tribes of their fathers; they &#91;are&#93; heads of the thousands of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 10:3; Revelation 7:4–17; Numbers 1:5–15"> And Moses takes—Aaron also—these men, who were defined by name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezra 2:59; Nehemiah 7:61; Hebrews 7:3"> and they assembled all the congregation on the first of the second month, and they declare their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names from a son of twenty years and upward, by their counted heads,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 1:2; Numbers 26:1–2; 2 Samuel 24:1–10"> as YHWH has commanded Moses; and he numbers them in the wilderness of Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 26:5–7; Numbers 2:10–11; Genesis 46:9"> And the sons of Reuben, Israel’s firstborn—their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, by their counted heads, every male from a son of twenty years and upward, everyone going out to the host—</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 26:7; Numbers 2:10–11"> their numbered ones, for the tribe of Reuben, are forty-six thousand and five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 26:12–14; Genesis 34:25–30; Numbers 2:12–13"> Of the sons of Simeon—their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, its numbered ones in the number of names, by their counted heads, every male from a son of twenty years and upward, everyone going out to the host—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 26:14; Numbers 2:13; Numbers 25:8–9"> their numbered ones, for the tribe of Simeon, &#91;are&#93; fifty-nine thousand and three hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 26:15–18; Genesis 46:16; Numbers 2:14–15"> Of the sons of Gad—their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, everyone going out to the host—</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 26:18; Numbers 2:15"> their numbered ones, for the tribe of Gad, &#91;are&#93; forty-five thousand and six hundred and fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 29:35; Numbers 26:19–22; 2 Samuel 24:9"> Of the sons of Judah—their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, everyone going out to the host—</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:9; Numbers 26:22; 2 Chronicles 17:14–16"> their numbered ones, for the tribe of Judah, &#91;are&#93; seventy-four thousand and six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 46:13; Genesis 49:14–15; Genesis 30:18"> Of the sons of Issachar—their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, everyone going out to the host—</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 26:25; Numbers 2:6"> their numbered ones, for the tribe of Issachar, &#91;are&#93; fifty-four thousand and four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 26:26–27; Genesis 49:13; Genesis 30:20"> Of the sons of Zebulun—their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, everyone going out to the host—</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 26:27; Numbers 2:8"> their numbered ones, for the tribe of Zebulun, &#91;are&#93; fifty-seven thousand and four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 26:35–37; Deuteronomy 33:17; Genesis 46:20"> Of the sons of Joseph: of the sons of Ephraim—their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, everyone going out to the host—</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 26:37; Deuteronomy 33:17; Numbers 2:19"> their numbered ones, for the tribe of Ephraim, &#91;are&#93; forty thousand and five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 26:28–34"> Of the sons of Manasseh—their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, everyone going out to the host—</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 26:34; Genesis 48:19–20; Numbers 2:21"> their numbered ones, for the tribe of Manasseh, &#91;are&#93; thirty-two thousand and two hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Genesis 44:20; Revelation 7:8"> Of the sons of Benjamin—their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, everyone going out to the host—</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 26:41; Judges 20:44–46; 2 Chronicles 17:17"> their numbered ones, for the tribe of Benjamin, &#91;are&#93; thirty-five thousand and four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 46:23; Genesis 30:5–6; Genesis 49:16–17"> Of the sons of Dan—their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, everyone going out to the host—</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 26:43; Numbers 2:26"> their numbered ones, for the tribe of Dan, &#91;are&#93; sixty-two thousand and seven hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 30:12–13; Genesis 46:27; Genesis 49:20"> Of the sons of Asher—their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, everyone going out to the host—</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 26:47; Numbers 2:28"> their numbered ones, for the tribe of Asher, &#91;are&#93; forty-one thousand and five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 49:21; Numbers 26:48–50; Genesis 46:24"> Of the sons of Naphtali—their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, everyone going out to the host—</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 26:50; Numbers 2:30"> their numbered ones, for the tribe of Naphtali, &#91;are&#93; fifty-three thousand and four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Numbers 26:64; Numbers 1:2–16"> These &#91;are&#93; those numbered, whom Moses numbered—Aaron also—and the princes of Israel, twelve men, each one has been &#91;the representative&#93; for the house of his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And they are, all those numbered of the sons of Israel, by the house of their fathers, from a son of twenty years and upward, everyone going out to the host in Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 12:37; Numbers 26:51; Exodus 38:26"> indeed, all those numbered are six hundred thousand and three thousand and five hundred and fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 2:33; Numbers 3:1–49; Numbers 26:57–62"> And the Levites, for the tribe of their fathers, have not numbered themselves in their midst,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> seeing YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Numbers 2:33"> “Only the tribe of Levi you do not number, and you do not take up their census in the midst of the sons of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 38:21; Exodus 31:18; Numbers 4:15"> and you, appoint the Levites over the Dwelling Place of the Testimony, and over all its vessels, and over all that it has; they carry the Dwelling Place, and all its vessels, and they serve it; and they encamp around the Dwelling Place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 3:38; Numbers 3:10; 1 Samuel 6:19"> And in the journeying of the Dwelling Place, the Levites take it down, and in the encamping of the Dwelling Place, the Levites raise it up; and the stranger who is coming near is put to death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Numbers 2:2; Numbers 2:34; Numbers 10:1–36"> And the sons of Israel have encamped, each by his camp, and each by his standard, by their hosts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Numbers 16:46; 1 Chronicles 23:32; Leviticus 10:6"> and the Levites encamp around the Dwelling Place of the Testimony; and there is no wrath on the congregation of the sons of Israel, and the Levites have kept the charge of the Dwelling Place of the Testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Exodus 40:16; Exodus 39:32"> And the sons of Israel do according to all that YHWH has commanded Moses; so they have done.</span>
