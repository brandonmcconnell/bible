<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 5:4; Leviticus 16:29–31; Zechariah 12:10"> And on the tenth of this seventh month you have a holy convocation, and you have humbled your souls; you do no work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 28:19; Numbers 29:13; Numbers 29:2"> and you have brought a burnt-offering near to YHWH, a refreshing fragrance: one bullock, a son of the herd, one ram, seven lambs, sons of a year; they are perfect ones for you;</span>

<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And on the seventh day: seven bullocks, two rams, fourteen lambs, sons of a year, perfect ones;</span>
