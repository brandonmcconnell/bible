<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 21:1; Leviticus 13:46; Numbers 12:14"> “Command the sons of Israel, and they send out of the camp every leper, and everyone with discharging, and everyone defiled by a body;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:14; Leviticus 26:11–12; Psalms 68:18"> you send out from male even to female; you send them to the outside of the camp and they do not defile their camps in the midst of which I dwell.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And the sons of Israel do so, and they send them out to the outside of the camp; as YHWH has spoken to Moses so the sons of Israel have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1–4; Leviticus 5:14–7"> “Speak to the sons of Israel: Man or woman, when they do any of the sins of man, by committing a trespass against YHWH, and that person &#91;is&#93; guilty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 5:5; Leviticus 26:40; Joshua 7:19"> and they have confessed their sin which they have done, then he has restored his guilt in its principal, and is adding its fifth to it, and has given &#91;it&#93; to him in reference to whom he has been guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 6:6–7; Leviticus 7:7; Leviticus 25:25–26"> And if the man has no redeemer to restore the guilt to, the guilt which is restored &#91;is&#93; to YHWH for the priest, apart from the ram of the atonements, whereby he makes atonement for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:3–4; Leviticus 7:6–14; Leviticus 6:17–18"> And every raised-offering of all the holy things of the sons of Israel, which they bring near to the priest, becomes his;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:13"> and any man’s hallowed things become his; that which any man gives to the priest becomes his.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 5:19–20; Proverbs 2:16–17"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, and you have said to them: When any man’s wife turns aside, and has committed a trespass against him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 18:20; Leviticus 20:10; Proverbs 7:18–19"> and a man has lain with her &#91;with&#93; the seed &#91;from&#93; intercourse, and it has been hid from the eyes of her husband, and concealed, and she has been defiled, and there is no witness against her, and she has not been caught,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Song of Songs 8:6; Proverbs 6:34; Zephaniah 3:8"> and a spirit of jealousy has passed over him, and he has been jealous &#91;for&#93; his wife, and she has been defiled—or a spirit of jealousy has passed over him, and he has been jealous &#91;for&#93; his wife, and she has not been defiled—</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:16; 1 Kings 17:18; Leviticus 5:11"> then the man has brought in his wife to the priest, and he has brought in her offering for her—a tenth of the ephah of barley meal; he does not pour oil on it, nor does he put frankincense on it, for it &#91;is&#93; a present of jealousy, a present of memorial, causing remembrance of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 2:22–23; Jeremiah 17:10; Hebrews 13:4"> And the priest has brought her near, and has caused her to stand before YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 30:18; John 8:6; Numbers 19:2–9"> and the priest has taken holy water in an earthen vessel, and the priest takes of the dust which is on the floor of the Dwelling Place, and has put &#91;it&#93; into the water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 5:4; Numbers 5:22; Revelation 10:9–10"> and the priest has caused the woman to stand before YHWH, and has uncovered the woman’s head, and has given the present of the memorial into her hands (it &#91;is&#93; a present of jealousy), and the bitter waters which cause the curse are in the hand of the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 7:2; Numbers 5:12; Matthew 26:63"> And the priest has caused her to swear, and has said to the woman, If no man has lain with you, and if you have not turned aside &#91;to&#93; uncleanness under your husband, be free from these bitter waters which cause the curse;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 5:12"> and you, if you have turned aside under your husband, and if you have been defiled, and a man commits his intercourse with you besides your husband—</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 6:26; Nehemiah 10:29; 1 Samuel 14:24"> then the priest has caused the woman to swear with an oath of execration, and the priest has said to the woman—YHWH gives you for an execration and for a curse in the midst of your people, in YHWH’s giving your thigh to fall and your belly to swell,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 109:18; Psalms 89:52; Job 31:39–40"> and these waters which cause the curse have gone into your bowels to cause the belly to swell and the thigh to fall; and the woman has said, Amen, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Psalms 51:1; Psalms 51:9"> And the priest has written these execrations in a scroll, and has blotted &#91;them&#93; out with the bitter waters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 5:3–4; Malachi 3:5"> and has caused the woman to drink the bitter waters which cause the curse, and the waters which cause the curse have entered into her for bitter things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 8:27; Numbers 5:18; Numbers 5:15"> And the priest has taken the present of jealousy out of the hand of the woman, and has waved the present before YHWH, and has brought it near to the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Leviticus 2:9; Leviticus 5:12"> and the priest has taken a handful of the present, its memorial, and has made incense on the altar, and afterward causes the woman to drink the water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:18; Zechariah 8:13; Jeremiah 29:18"> indeed, he has caused her to drink the water, and it has come to pass, if she has been defiled and commits a trespass against her husband, that the waters which cause the curse have gone into her for bitter things, and her belly has swelled, and her thigh has fallen, and the woman has become an execration in the midst of her people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Micah 7:7–10; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Psalms 113:9"> And if the woman has not been defiled, and is clean, then she has been acquitted, and has been sown &#91;with&#93; seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 5:19; Numbers 5:12; Numbers 5:15"> This &#91;is&#93; the law of jealousies when a wife turns aside under her husband and has been defiled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> or when a spirit of jealousy passes over a man and he has been jealous of his wife, then he has caused the woman to stand before YHWH, and the priest has done to her all this law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Romans 2:8–9; Ezekiel 18:4"> and the man has been acquitted from iniquity, and that woman bears her iniquity.”</span>
