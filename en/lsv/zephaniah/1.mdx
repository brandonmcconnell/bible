<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:2; 2 Chronicles 34:1–33; 2 Peter 1:19"> A word of YHWH that has been to Zephaniah son of Cushi, son of Gedaliah, son of Amariah, son of Hezikiah, in the days of Josiah son of Amoz, king of Judah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 7:13; 2 Kings 22:16–17; Ezekiel 33:27–29"> “I utterly consume all from off the face of the ground,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Ezekiel 7:19; Jeremiah 12:4"> “I consume man and beast, &#124;&#124; I consume bird of the heavens, and fishes of the sea, &#124;&#124; And the stumbling-blocks—the wicked, &#124;&#124; And I have cut off man from the face of the ground,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Micah 5:13; 2 Kings 23:4–5"> “And I have stretched out My hand against Judah, &#124;&#124; And against all inhabiting Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And cut off from this place the remnant of Ba‘al, &#124;&#124; The name of the idolatrous priests, with the priests,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:13; 1 Kings 11:33; 2 Kings 17:33"> And those bowing themselves &#124;&#124; On the roofs to the host of the heavens, &#124;&#124; And those bowing themselves, &#124;&#124; Swearing to YHWH, and swearing by Malcham,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; Jeremiah 2:13; Jeremiah 15:6"> And those removing from after YHWH, &#124;&#124; And who have not sought YHWH, nor implored Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:20; Zechariah 2:13; Zephaniah 1:14"> Hush! Because of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; For near &#91;is the&#93; Day of YHWH, &#124;&#124; For YHWH has prepared a sacrifice, &#124;&#124; He has sanctified His invited ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 24:21; Isaiah 3:18–24; Jeremiah 22:24–30"> “And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; In the day of the sacrifice of YHWH, &#124;&#124; That I have laid a charge on the heads, &#124;&#124; And on sons of the king, &#124;&#124; And on all putting on strange clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:5; Amos 3:10; 1 Samuel 2:15–16"> And I have laid a charge on everyone &#124;&#124; Who is leaping over the threshold in that day, &#124;&#124; Who are filling the house of their masters &#124;&#124; &#91;With&#93; violence and deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:14; Amos 8:3; 2 Kings 22:14"> And there has been in that day,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “The noise of a cry from the Fish Gate, &#124;&#124; And of a howling from the Second &#91;Quarter&#93;, &#124;&#124; And of great destruction from the hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 5:1; Jeremiah 25:34; Hosea 12:7–8"> Howl, you inhabitants of the hollow place, &#124;&#124; For all the merchant people have been cut off, &#124;&#124; All carrying silver have been cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 6:1; Jeremiah 48:11; Ezekiel 8:12"> And it has come to pass at that time, &#124;&#124; I search Jerusalem with lights, &#124;&#124; And I have laid a charge on the men &#124;&#124; Who are hardened on their preserved things, &#124;&#124; Who are saying in their heart: YHWH does no good, nor does He do evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 5:11; Micah 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:30"> And their wealth has been for a spoil, &#124;&#124; And their houses for desolation, &#124;&#124; And they have built houses, and do not inhabit, &#124;&#124; And they have planted vineyards, &#124;&#124; And they do not drink their wine.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Ezekiel 30:3; Zephaniah 1:7"> Near &#91;is&#93; the Great Day of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Near, and hastening exceedingly, &#124;&#124; The noise of the Day of YHWH, &#124;&#124; There a mighty one bitterly shrieks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 2:2; Isaiah 22:5; Revelation 6:17"> A day of wrath &#91;is&#93; that day, &#124;&#124; A day of adversity and distress, &#124;&#124; A day of devastation and desolation, &#124;&#124; A day of darkness and gloominess, &#124;&#124; A day of cloud and thick darkness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Amos 3:6; Isaiah 32:14"> A day of horn and shouting against the fortified cities, &#124;&#124; And against the high corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Psalms 83:10; Jeremiah 2:17"> “And I have sent distress to men, &#124;&#124; And they have walked as the blind, &#124;&#124; For against YHWH they have sinned, &#124;&#124; And poured out is their blood as dust, &#124;&#124; And their flesh &#91;is&#93; as dung.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:8; Ezekiel 7:19; Proverbs 11:4"> Even their silver, even their gold, is not able to deliver them in a day of the wrath of YHWH, and in the fire of His jealousy is the whole land consumed, for He makes only a hurried end of all the inhabitants of the land!</span>
