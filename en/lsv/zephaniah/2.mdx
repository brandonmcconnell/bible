<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 17:13; Hosea 13:3; Nahum 1:6"> Before the bringing forth of a statute, &#124;&#124; The day has passed on as chaff, &#124;&#124; While the heat of the anger of YHWH has not yet come in on you, &#124;&#124; While &#91;the&#93; Day of the anger of YHWH has not yet come in on you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 5:14–15; Isaiah 26:20–21; Psalms 76:9"> Seek YHWH, all you humble of the land, &#124;&#124; Who have done His judgment, &#124;&#124; Seek righteousness, seek humility, &#124;&#124; It may be you are hidden in the Day of the anger of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 9:5–7; Amos 1:6–8; Jeremiah 47:1–7"> For Gaza is forsaken, &#124;&#124; And Ashkelon &#91;is&#93; for a desolation, &#124;&#124; Ashdod! At noon they cast her forth, &#124;&#124; And Ekron is rooted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:16; Jeremiah 47:7; Joshua 13:3"> Behold! O inhabitants of the seacoast, &#124;&#124; Nation of the Cherethites, &#124;&#124; A word of YHWH &#91;is&#93; against you, &#124;&#124; Canaan, land of the Philistines, &#124;&#124; “And I have destroyed you without an inhabitant.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 17:2; Ezekiel 25:5; Isaiah 5:17"> And the seacoast has been habitations, &#124;&#124; Cottages &#91;for&#93; shepherds, and folds &#91;for&#93; a flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; Ezekiel 39:25; Zephaniah 3:20"> And the coast has been for the remnant of the house of Judah, &#124;&#124; By them they have pleasure, &#124;&#124; In houses of Ashkelon they lie down at evening, &#124;&#124; For their God YHWH inspects them, &#124;&#124; And He has turned back &#91;to&#93; their captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 1:13; Psalms 83:4–7; Jeremiah 48:27–29"> “I have heard the reproach of Moab, &#124;&#124; And the revilings of the sons of Ammon, &#124;&#124; With which they reproached My people, &#124;&#124; And magnify &#91;themselves&#93; against their border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 15:1–9; Isaiah 11:14; Deuteronomy 29:23"> Therefore, &#91;as&#93; I live,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH of Hosts, God of Israel, &#124;&#124; “Surely Moab is as Sodom, &#124;&#124; And the sons of Ammon as Gomorrah, &#124;&#124; An overrunning of nettles and salt-pits, &#124;&#124; And a desolation for all time. A remnant of My people seizes them, &#124;&#124; And a remnant of My nation inherits them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 16:6; Jeremiah 48:29; Zephaniah 2:8"> This &#91;is&#93; to them for their arrogancy, &#124;&#124; Because they have reproached, &#124;&#124; And they magnify &#91;themselves&#93; against the people of YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 4:1–3; Zechariah 13:2; Psalms 138:4"> Fearful &#91;is&#93; YHWH against them, &#124;&#124; For He made all gods of the land bare, &#124;&#124; And all islanders of the nations bow themselves to Him, &#124;&#124; Each from his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:4–9; Isaiah 43:3; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> “Also you, O Cushim, they &#91;are&#93; pierced of My sword.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nahum 3:7; Nahum 1:1; Isaiah 10:12"> And He stretches His hand against the north, &#124;&#124; And destroys Asshur, &#124;&#124; And He sets Nineveh for a desolation, &#124;&#124; A dry land like a wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 13:19–22; Isaiah 34:11–17; Amos 9:1"> And droves have crouched in her midst, &#124;&#124; Every beast of the nation, &#124;&#124; Both pelican and hedgehog lodge in her knobs, &#124;&#124; A voice sings at the window, &#124;&#124; “Destruction &#91;is&#93; at the threshold, &#124;&#124; For the cedar-work is exposed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Isaiah 22:2; Isaiah 47:7–8"> This &#91;is&#93; the exulting city that is dwelling confidently, &#124;&#124; That is saying in her heart, &#124;&#124; “I &#91;am&#93;, and there is none beside me,” &#124;&#124; How she has been for a desolation, &#124;&#124; A crouching-place for beasts, &#124;&#124; Everyone passing by her hisses, &#124;&#124; He shakes his hand!</span>
