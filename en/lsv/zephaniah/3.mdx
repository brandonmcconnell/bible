<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:6; Isaiah 30:12; Isaiah 59:13"> Woe &#91;to&#93; the rebelling and defiling, &#124;&#124; The oppressing city!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Jeremiah 22:21; Jeremiah 7:23–28"> She has not listened to the voice, &#124;&#124; She has not accepted instruction, &#124;&#124; In YHWH she has not trusted, &#124;&#124; She has not drawn near to her God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Ezekiel 22:25–27; Jeremiah 5:6"> Her heads in her midst &#91;are&#93; roaring lions, &#124;&#124; Her judges &#91;are&#93; wolves &#91;as in&#93; evening, &#124;&#124; They have not gnawn the bone in the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:26; Hosea 9:7; Malachi 2:8"> Her prophets unstable—men of treachery, &#124;&#124; Her priests have defiled the sanctuary, &#124;&#124; They have violated the Law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Psalms 145:17; Lamentations 3:23"> YHWH &#91;is&#93; righteous in her midst, &#124;&#124; He does not do perverseness, &#124;&#124; Morning by morning His judgment He gives to the light, &#124;&#124; It has not been lacking, &#124;&#124; And the perverse do not know shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Zephaniah 2:5; 1 Corinthians 10:11"> “I have cut off nations, &#124;&#124; Desolated have been their chief ones, &#124;&#124; I have laid waste their out-places without any passing by, &#124;&#124; Destroyed have been their cities, &#124;&#124; Without man, without inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Jeremiah 36:3; Micah 2:1–2"> I have said: Only, you fear Me, &#124;&#124; You accept instruction, &#124;&#124; And her habitation is not cut off, &#124;&#124; All that I have appointed for her, &#124;&#124; But they have risen early, &#124;&#124; They have corrupted all their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Zephaniah 1:18; Micah 7:7"> Therefore, wait for Me,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “For the day of My rising for prey, &#124;&#124; For My judgment &#91;is&#93; to gather nations, &#124;&#124; To assemble kingdoms, &#124;&#124; To pour out on them My indignation, &#124;&#124; All the heat of My anger, &#124;&#124; For by the fire of My jealousy all the earth is consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 11:1; Psalms 22:27; Isaiah 19:18"> For then I turn a pure lip to peoples, &#124;&#124; To call all of them by the Name of YHWH, &#124;&#124; To serve Him &#91;with&#93; one shoulder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 11:11; Psalms 68:31; Isaiah 18:1"> From beyond the rivers of Cush, My supplicants, &#124;&#124; The daughter of My scattered ones, &#124;&#124; Bring My present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 54:4; Joel 2:26–27; Isaiah 11:9"> In that day you are not ashamed because of any of your actions, &#124;&#124; With which you have transgressed against Me, &#124;&#124; For then I turn aside from your midst &#124;&#124; The exulting ones of your excellence, &#124;&#124; And you are to be haughty no longer, &#124;&#124; In My holy mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 14:32; Nahum 1:7; Zechariah 13:8–9"> And I have left in your midst a people humble and poor, &#124;&#124; And they have trusted in the Name of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:5; Zephaniah 2:7; Micah 4:7"> The remnant of Israel does no perversity, nor speaks lies, &#124;&#124; Nor is a deceitful tongue found in their mouth, &#124;&#124; For they have delight, and have lain down, &#124;&#124; And there is none troubling.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 12:6; Zechariah 2:10–11; Micah 4:8"> Cry aloud, O daughter of Zion, shout, O Israel, &#124;&#124; Rejoice and exult with the whole heart, O daughter of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 51:22; Zephaniah 3:5; Isaiah 33:22"> YHWH has turned aside your judgments, &#124;&#124; He has faced your enemy, &#124;&#124; The King of Israel, YHWH, &#91;is&#93; in your midst, &#124;&#124; You see evil no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:12; Isaiah 35:3–4; Isaiah 41:10"> In that day it is said to Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; “Do not fear, O Zion, &#124;&#124; Do not let your hands be feeble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 149:4; Psalms 147:11; Jeremiah 32:41"> Your God YHWH &#91;is&#93; in your midst, &#124;&#124; A mighty one &#91;to&#93; save, &#124;&#124; He rejoices over you with joy, &#124;&#124; He works in His love, &#124;&#124; He delights over you with singing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 42:2–4; Ezekiel 36:24; Lamentations 2:6–7"> “I have gathered My afflicted from the appointed place, &#124;&#124; They have been bearing reproach from you for her sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 49:25–26; Isaiah 60:14; Jeremiah 30:16"> Behold, I am dealing with all afflicting you at that time, &#124;&#124; And I have saved the halting one, &#124;&#124; And I gather the ones driven out, &#124;&#124; And have set them for a praise and for a name, &#124;&#124; In all the land of their shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14; Malachi 3:12; Isaiah 56:5"> At that time I bring you in, &#124;&#124; Even at the time of My assembling you, &#124;&#124; For I give you for a name, and for a praise, &#124;&#124; Among all peoples of the land, &#124;&#124; In My turning back &#91;to&#93; your captivity before your eyes,” said YHWH!</span>
