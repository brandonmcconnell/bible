<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esther 1:3; Mark 6:21–22; Isaiah 21:4–5"> Belshazzar the king has made a great feast to one thousand of his great men, and before the one thousand he is drinking wine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 1:2; 2 Kings 24:13; Jeremiah 27:16–22"> Belshazzar has said—while tasting the wine—to bring in the vessels of gold and of silver that his father Nebuchadnezzar had taken from the temple that &#91;is&#93; in Jerusalem, that the king may drink with them, and his great men, his wives, and his concubines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then they have brought in the vessels of gold that had been taken out of the temple of the house of God that &#91;is&#93; in Jerusalem, and the king and his great men, his wives and his concubines, have drunk with them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Habakkuk 2:19; Psalms 135:15–18"> they have drunk wine, and have praised the gods of gold, and of silver, of bronze, of iron, of wood, and of stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:31; Daniel 5:24–28; Job 20:5"> In that hour fingers of a man’s hand have come forth, and they are writing in front of the lampstand, on the plaster of the wall of the king’s palace: and the king is seeing the extremity of the hand that is writing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:17; Daniel 7:28; Nahum 2:10"> then the king’s countenance has changed, and his thoughts trouble him, and the joints of his loins are loosed, and his knees are striking against one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Daniel 5:16; Daniel 2:48"> The king calls mightily, to bring up the enchanters, the Chaldeans, and the soothsayers. The king has answered and said to the wise men of Babylon, that, “Any man who reads this writing, and shows me its interpretation, he puts on purple, and a bracelet of gold &#91;is&#93; on his neck, and he rules third in the kingdom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 2:27; Daniel 4:7; Genesis 41:8"> Then all the wise men of the king are coming up, and they are not able to read the writing, and to make known the interpretation to the king;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:6; Daniel 2:1; Jeremiah 6:24"> then King Belshazzar is greatly troubled, and his countenance is changing in him, and his great men are perplexed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 3:9; Daniel 6:6; Daniel 2:4"> The queen, on account of the words of the king and his great men, has come up to the banquet-house. The queen has answered and said, “O king, live for all ages; do not let your thoughts trouble you, nor your countenance be changed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 4:8–9; Daniel 2:47–48; Daniel 4:18"> there is a man in your kingdom in whom &#91;is&#93; the spirit of the holy gods: and in the days of your father, light, and understanding, and wisdom—as the wisdom of the gods—was found in him; and your father King Nebuchadnezzar, chief of the scribes, enchanters, Chaldeans, soothsayers, established him—your father, O king—</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 5:14; Daniel 6:3; Daniel 1:7"> because that an excellent spirit, and knowledge, and understanding, interpreting of dreams, and showing of enigmas, and loosing of knots was found in him, in Daniel, whose name the king made Belteshazzar: now let Daniel be called, and the interpretation he shows.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 6:13; Daniel 5:11; Daniel 2:25"> Then Daniel has been caused to come up before the king; the king has answered and said to Daniel, “You are that Daniel who &#91;is&#93; of the sons of the expulsion of Judah, whom my father the king brought in out of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 5:11–12"> And I have heard of you, that the spirit of the gods &#91;is&#93; in you, and light, and understanding, and excellent wisdom have been found in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 5:7–8; Isaiah 47:12; Isaiah 29:10–12"> And now, caused to come up before me have been the wise men, the enchanters, that they may read this writing, and its interpretation to cause me to know: and they are not able to show the interpretation of the thing:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 5:7; Genesis 40:8; Daniel 5:29"> and I have heard of you, that you are able to give interpretations, and to loose knots: now, behold—you are able to read the writing, and its interpretation to cause me to know—purple you put on, and a bracelet of gold &#91;is&#93; on your neck, and you rule third in the kingdom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 5:16; Genesis 14:23; 2 Kings 5:26"> Then Daniel has answered and said before the king, “Your gifts be to yourself, and give your fee to another; nevertheless, the writing I read to the king, and the interpretation I cause him to know;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 2:37–38; Daniel 4:17; Daniel 4:22–25"> you, O king, God Most High, a kingdom, and greatness, and glory, and honor, gave to your father Nebuchadnezzar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 3:6; Daniel 2:12–13; Daniel 3:4"> and because of the greatness that He gave to him, all peoples, nations, and languages were trembling and fearing before him: whom he willed he was slaying, and whom he willed he was keeping alive, and whom he willed he was raising up, and whom he willed he was making low;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 9:17; Jeremiah 13:18; Hebrews 3:13"> and when his heart was high, and his spirit was strong to act proudly, he has been caused to come down from the throne of his kingdom, and his glory they have caused to pass away from him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Ezekiel 17:24; Daniel 4:35"> and he is driven from the sons of men, and his heart has been like with the beasts, and with the wild donkeys &#91;is&#93; his dwelling; they cause him to eat the herb like oxen, and by the dew of the heavens is his body wet, until he has known that God Most High is ruler in the kingdom of men, and whom He wills He raises up over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:23; Exodus 10:3; James 4:17"> And you, his son, Belshazzar, have not humbled your heart, though all this you have known;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 12:10; Daniel 4:37; Jeremiah 10:23"> and against the Lord of the heavens you have lifted up yourself; and the vessels of His house they have brought in before you, and you, and your great men, your wives, and your concubines, are drinking wine with them, and gods of silver, and of gold, of bronze, of iron, of wood, and of stone, that are not seeing, nor hearing, nor knowing, you have praised: and the God in whose hand &#91;is&#93; your breath, and all your ways, Him you have not honored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 5:5"> Then from before Him is the extremity of the hand sent, and the writing is noted down;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and this &#91;is&#93; the writing that is noted down: Numbered, Numbered, Weighed, and Divided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:11–12; Isaiah 47:1–15; Jeremiah 27:7"> This &#91;is&#93; the interpretation of the thing: Numbered—God has numbered your kingdom, and has finished it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 31:6; 1 Corinthians 3:13; Psalms 62:9"> Weighed—You are weighed in the balances, and have been found lacking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 13:17; Daniel 6:28; Daniel 5:31"> Divided—Your kingdom is divided, and it has been given to the Medes and Persians.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 5:16; Daniel 5:7"> Then Belshazzar has spoken, and they have clothed Daniel with purple, and a bracelet of gold &#91;is&#93; on his neck, and they have proclaimed concerning him that he is the third ruler in the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:31; Jeremiah 51:57; Jeremiah 51:39"> In that night Belshazzar king of the Chaldeans is slain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 9:1; Daniel 6:1"> and Darius the Mede has received the kingdom when a son of sixty-two years.</span>
