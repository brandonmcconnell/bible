<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:7; Matthew 24:21; Mark 13:19"> “And at that time Michael stands up, the great head, who is standing up for the sons of your people, and there has been a time of distress, such as has not been since there has been a nation until that time, and at that time your people escape, everyone who is found written in the scroll.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 5:28–29; Isaiah 26:19; Matthew 25:46"> And the multitude of those sleeping in the dust of the ground awake, some to continuous life, and some to reproaches—to continuous abhorrence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Proverbs 4:18; James 5:19–20"> And those teaching shine as the brightness of the expanse, and those justifying the multitude as stars for all time and forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 12:9; Revelation 22:10; Daniel 8:26"> “And you, O Daniel, hide the things, and seal the scroll until the time of the end, many go to and fro, and knowledge is multiplied.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 10:4–6; Daniel 10:16; Daniel 10:10"> “And I have looked—I, Daniel—and behold, two others are standing, one here at the edge of the flood and one there at the edge of the flood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 8:13; Ezekiel 9:2; Zechariah 1:12–13"> and he says to the one clothed in linen, who &#91;is&#93; on the waters of the flood, Until when &#91;is&#93; the end of these wonders?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 7:25; Revelation 12:14; Daniel 8:24"> And I hear the one clothed in linen, who &#91;is&#93; on the waters of the flood, and he lifts up his right hand and his left to the heavens, and swears by Him who is living for all time, that, After a time, times, and a half, and at the completion of the scattering of the power of the holy people, all these are finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:7; 1 Peter 1:11; Daniel 10:14"> And I have heard, and I do not understand, and I say, O my lord, what &#91;is&#93; the latter end of these?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 12:4; Daniel 8:26; Revelation 10:4"> And he says, Go, Daniel; for hidden and sealed &#91;are&#93; the things until the time of the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:11; Daniel 11:35; Hosea 14:9"> Purify themselves, indeed, make themselves white, indeed, many are refined: and the wicked have done wickedly, and none of the wicked understand, and those acting wisely understand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 11:31; Matthew 24:15; Mark 13:14"> and from the time of the turning aside of the continuous &#91;sacrifice&#93;, and to the giving out of the desolating abomination, &#91;are&#93; one thousand, two hundred, and ninety days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 30:18; Daniel 8:14; Romans 11:15"> O the blessedness of him who is waiting earnestly, and comes to the one thousand, three hundred, thirty-five days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; Zechariah 3:7"> And you, go on to the end, then you rest, and stand in your lot at the end of the days.”</span>
