<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:7; Ruth 3:12; Deuteronomy 16:18"> And Boaz has gone up to the gate, and sits there, and behold, the redeemer is passing by of whom Boaz had spoken, and he says, “Turn aside, sit down here, such a one, such a one”; and he turns aside and sits down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 31:23; 1 Kings 21:8; Acts 6:12"> And he takes ten men from &#91;the&#93; elderly of the city, and says, “Sit down here”; and they sit down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 13:10; Psalms 112:5"> And he says to the redeemer, “A portion of the field which &#91;is&#93; to our brother, to Elimelech, has Naomi sold, who has come back from the fields of Moab;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 25:25–29; Jeremiah 32:7–12; Romans 12:17"> and I said, I uncover your ear, saying, Buy before the inhabitants and before &#91;the&#93; elderly of my people; if you redeem—redeem, and if none redeems—declare &#91;it&#93; to me and I know, for there is none except you to redeem, and I after you.” And he says, “I redeem &#91;it&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 38:8; Deuteronomy 25:5–6; Matthew 22:24"> And Boaz says, “In the day of your buying the field from the hand of Naomi, then from Ruth the Moabitess, wife of the dead, you have bought &#91;it&#93;, to raise up the name of the dead over his inheritance.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 25:25; Ruth 3:13"> And the redeemer says, “I am not able to redeem &#91;it&#93; for myself, lest I destroy my inheritance; redeem for yourself—you—my right of redemption, for I am not able to redeem.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:7–10"> And this &#91;is&#93; formerly in Israel for redemption and for exchanging, to establish anything: a man has drawn off his sandal, and given &#91;it&#93; to his neighbor, and this &#91;is&#93; the Testimony in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the redeemer says to Boaz, “Buy &#91;it&#93; for yourself,” and draws off his sandal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:10–12; Genesis 23:16–18"> And Boaz says to the elderly, and &#91;to&#93; all the people, “You &#91;are&#93; witnesses today that I have bought all that &#91;belonged&#93; to Elimelech, and all that &#91;belonged&#93; to Chilion and Mahlon, from the hand of Naomi;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:6; Ephesians 5:25; Genesis 29:27"> and also Ruth the Moabitess, wife of Mahlon, I have bought to myself for a wife, to raise up the name of the dead over his inheritance; and the name of the dead is not cut off from among his brothers, and from the gate of his place; witnesses you &#91;are&#93; today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 29:32–24; Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:6"> And all the people who &#91;are&#93; in the gate say—also the elderly, “Witnesses! YHWH makes the woman who is coming to your house as Rachel and as Leah, both of whom built the house of Israel; and you do virtuously in Ephrathah, and proclaim the name in Beth-Lehem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 38:29; 1 Samuel 2:20; 1 Chronicles 2:4"> and let your house be as the house of Perez (whom Tamar bore to Judah), of the seed which YHWH gives to you of this young woman.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 33:5; Genesis 29:31; Ruth 3:11"> And Boaz takes Ruth, and she becomes his wife, and he goes in to her, and YHWH gives conception to her, and she bears a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 1:5–20; Luke 1:58; Genesis 12:2"> And the women say to Naomi, “Blessed &#91;is&#93; YHWH who has not let a redeemer cease to you today, and his name is proclaimed in Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 55:22; Isaiah 46:4; Genesis 45:11"> and he has been to you for a restorer of life, and for a nourisher of your old age, for your daughter-in-law who has loved you—who is better to you than seven sons—has borne him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Naomi takes the boy, and lays him in her bosom, and is to him for a nurse;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 1:58–63"> and the neighboring women give a name to him, saying, “There has been a son born to Naomi,” and they call his name Obed; he &#91;is&#93; father of Jesse, father of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 1:3–6; 1 Chronicles 2:4–8; Luke 3:33"> And these are generations of Perez: Perez begot Hezron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 3:33; 1 Chronicles 2:9–10; Matthew 1:4"> and Hezron begot Ram, and Ram begot Amminidab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 1:7; Luke 3:32; Matthew 1:4"> and Amminidab begot Nahshon, and Nahshon begot Salmon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 3:32; Matthew 1:5; 1 Chronicles 2:11–12"> and Salmon begot Boaz, and Boaz begot Obed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:15; Isaiah 11:1; Matthew 1:6"> and Obed begot Jesse, and Jesse begot David.</span>
