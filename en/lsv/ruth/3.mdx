<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Ruth 2:20–23; Ruth 2:8"> And now, is not Boaz of our acquaintance, with whose young women you have been? Behold, he is winnowing the threshing-floor of barley tonight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:2; 1 Timothy 2:9–10; Esther 5:1"> and you have bathed, and anointed yourself, and put your garments on you, and gone down to the threshing-floor; do not let yourself be known to the man until he completes to eat and to drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:22"> and it comes to pass, when he lies down, that you have known the place where he lies down, and have gone in, and uncovered his feet, and lain down—and he declares to you that which you do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 3:20; Ephesians 6:1"> And she says to her, “All that you say—I do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 1:8; John 2:5; Exodus 20:12"> And she goes down &#91;to&#93; the threshing-floor, and does according to all that her mother-in-law commanded her</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:6; Esther 1:10; 2 Samuel 13:28"> And Boaz eats and drinks, and his heart is glad; and he goes in to lie down at the end of the heap; and she comes in gently, and uncovers his feet, and lies down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And it comes to pass, at the middle of the night, that the man trembles, and turns himself, and behold, a woman is lying at his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:8; Ruth 2:20; Ruth 3:12"> And he says, “Who &#91;are&#93; you?” And she says, “I &#91;am&#93; Ruth your handmaid, and you have spread your skirt over your handmaid, for you &#91;are&#93; a redeemer.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ruth 2:20; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5; Ruth 1:8"> And he says, “Blessed &#91;are&#93; you of YHWH, my daughter; you have dealt more kindly at the latter end than at the beginning—not to go after the young men, either poor or rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 31:29–31; Proverbs 31:10; Proverbs 12:4"> And now, my daughter, do not fear, all that you say I do to you, for all the gate of my people knows that you &#91;are&#93; a virtuous woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; 1 Thessalonians 4:6; Matthew 7:12"> And now, surely, true, that I &#91;am&#93; a redeemer, but also there is a redeemer nearer than I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ruth 4:5; Jeremiah 4:2; Ruth 2:20"> Lodge tonight, and it has been in the morning, if he redeems you, well: he redeems; and if he does not delight to redeem you, then I have redeemed you—I; YHWH lives! Lie down until the morning.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; Romans 14:16; 1 Corinthians 10:32"> And she lies down at his feet until the morning, and rises before one discerns another; and he says, “Let it not be known that the woman has come into the floor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 32:8; Galatians 6:10"> And he says, “Give the covering which &#91;is&#93; on you, and keep hold on it”; and she keeps hold on it, and he measures six &#91;measures&#93; of barley, and lays &#91;it&#93; on her; and he goes into the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And she comes to her mother-in-law, and she says, “Who &#91;are&#93; you, my daughter?” And she declares to her all that the man has done to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And she says, “These six &#91;measures&#93; of barley he has given to me, for he said, You do not go in empty to your mother-in-law.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 37:3–5; Isaiah 28:16; Isaiah 30:7"> And she says, “Sit still, my daughter, until you know how the matter falls, for the man does not rest except he has completed the matter today.”</span>
