<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:17; 1 Corinthians 2:13; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> And I, having come to you, brothers, came—not in superiority of discourse or wisdom—declaring to you the testimony of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 6:14; John 17:3; 1 Corinthians 1:22–25"> for I decided not to know anything among you, except Jesus Christ, and Him crucified;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:4; 1 Corinthians 4:10–13; 2 Corinthians 12:5–10"> and I, in weakness, and in fear, and in much trembling, was with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:20; 1 Thessalonians 1:5; 1 Corinthians 1:17"> and my word and my preaching was not in persuasive words of wisdom, but in demonstration of the Spirit and of power—</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:7; 2 Corinthians 12:9; 2 Corinthians 6:7"> that your faith may not be in the wisdom of men, but in the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 5:14; James 3:15; 2 Corinthians 4:4"> And wisdom we speak among the perfect, and wisdom not of this age, nor of the rulers of this age—of those becoming useless,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 1:26–27; 2 Peter 1:3; Matthew 11:25"> but we speak the hidden wisdom of God in a secret, that God foreordained before the ages to our glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:27; James 2:1; Acts 7:2"> which no one of the rulers of this age knew, for if they had known, they would not have crucified the Lord of Glory;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Psalms 31:19; James 1:12"> but according as it has been written: “What eye did not see, and ear did not hear, and on the heart of man did not come up, what God prepared for those loving Him”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 14:26; Ephesians 3:5; Luke 10:21"> but God revealed &#91;them&#93; to us through His Spirit, for the Spirit searches all things, even the depths of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:27; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Romans 11:33–34"> for who of men has known the things of the man, except the spirit of the man that &#91;is&#93; in him? So also the things of God no one has known, except the Spirit of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 2:27; Romans 8:5–6; 1 John 2:20"> And we did not receive the spirit of the world, but the Spirit that &#91;is&#93; of God, that we may know the things conferred by God on us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4; 1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 1:17"> which things we also speak, not in words taught by human wisdom, but in those taught by the Holy Spirit, comparing spiritual things with spiritual things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:18; Romans 8:5–8; Matthew 13:11–17"> and the natural man does not receive the things of the Spirit of God, for they are foolishness to him, and he is not able to know &#91;them&#93;, because they are discerned spiritually;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Proverbs 28:5; Hebrews 5:14"> and he who is spiritual, indeed discerns all things, and he himself is discerned by no one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34; John 16:13–16"> for who knew the mind of the LORD that he will instruct Him? And we have the mind of Christ.</span>
