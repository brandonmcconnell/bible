<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:6–12; 1 Peter 5:12; 1 Corinthians 15:3–11"> And I make known to you, brothers, the good news that I proclaimed to you, which you also received, in which you also have stood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 3:6; Hebrews 2:1; Hebrews 3:14"> through which you are also being saved, if you hold fast &#91;to&#93; the word—what I proclaimed as good news to you—unless you believed in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Galatians 1:4; Isaiah 53:1–12"> For I delivered to you as most important what I also received: that Christ died for our sins according to the Writings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 6:2; Matthew 12:40; Psalms 16:10–11"> and that He was buried, and that He has risen on the third day according to the Writings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 16:14; 1 Corinthians 1:12; John 1:42"> and that He appeared to Cephas, then to the Twelve,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 28:10; Mark 16:7; Acts 13:36"> afterward He appeared to above five hundred brothers at once, of whom the greater part remain until now, and some also fell asleep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 24:50; Acts 1:2–12; Luke 24:33"> afterward He appeared to James, then to all the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:1; Acts 26:16; Acts 22:18"> And last of all—as to the untimely birth—He also appeared to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 3:7–8; 1 Timothy 1:13–15; Acts 8:3"> for I am the least of the apostles, who am not worthy to be called an apostle, because I persecuted the Assembly of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 3:5; Philippians 2:13"> and by the grace of God I am what I am, and His grace that &#91;is&#93; toward me did not come in vain, but I labored more abundantly than they all, yet not I, but the grace of God that &#91;is&#93; with me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:3–4; 1 Corinthians 2:2"> whether, then, I or they, so we preach, and so you believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 26:8; Acts 17:32; 2 Timothy 2:18"> And if Christ is preached, that He has risen out of the dead, how &#91;do&#93; certain among you say that there is no resurrection of &#91;the&#93; dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Romans 8:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:14"> And if there is no resurrection of &#91;the&#93; dead, neither has Christ risen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:14; Galatians 2:2"> and if Christ has not risen, then our preaching &#91;is&#93; void, and your faith &#91;is&#93; also void,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Job 13:7–10; Acts 2:32"> and we are also found &#91;to be&#93; false witnesses of God, because we testified of God that He raised up the Christ, whom He did not raise if then dead persons do not rise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> for if dead persons do not rise, neither has Christ risen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 4:25; 1 Peter 1:3; 1 Corinthians 15:14"> and if Christ has not risen, your faith is vain, you are yet in your sins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:13–14; Revelation 14:13; 1 Thessalonians 4:16"> then, also, those having fallen asleep in Christ perished;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 1:21; Revelation 14:13; 2 Timothy 1:12"> if we only have hope in Christ in this life, we are to be most pitied of all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:23; Acts 26:23; Revelation 1:5"> And now, Christ has risen out of the dead—He became the first-fruits of those sleeping,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:25; Romans 5:12–17; 1 Corinthians 15:22"> for since through man &#91;is&#93; death, also through Man &#91;is&#93; a resurrection of the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 5:12–21; 1 Corinthians 15:45–49; John 5:21–29"> for even as in Adam all die, so also in the Christ all will be made alive,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; Galatians 3:29; 1 Corinthians 15:52"> and each in his proper order: Christ, a first-fruit, afterward those who are the Christ’s in His coming,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Daniel 7:14; Matthew 28:18"> then—the end, when He may deliver up the kingdom to God, even the Father, when He may have made all rule useless, and all authority and power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 10:12–13; Psalms 110:1; Matthew 22:44"> For it is necessary for Him to reign until He may have put all the enemies under His feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:10; 1 Corinthians 15:55; Revelation 21:4"> The last enemy is done away with—death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 8:6; Hebrews 2:8; Matthew 28:18"> For He put all things under His feet, and when one may say that all things have been subjected, &#91;it is&#93; evident that He is excepted who subjected all things to Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; 1 Corinthians 3:23; Ephesians 1:23"> and when all things may be subjected to Him, then also the Son Himself will be subject to Him, who subjected to Him all things, that God may be the all in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 6:3–4; 1 Corinthians 15:16; Matthew 20:22"> Seeing what will they do who are immersed for the dead, if the dead do not rise at all? Why are they also immersed for the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:9; Galatians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 4:7–12"> Why do we also stand in peril every hour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:36; 1 Corinthians 4:9–13"> I die every day, by the glorying of you that I have in Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 22:13; Isaiah 56:12; Luke 12:19"> If I fought with wild beasts in Ephesus after the manner of a man, what &#91;is&#93; the advantage to me if the dead do not rise? Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Matthew 24:4; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> Do not be led astray; evil communications corrupt good manners;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:5; Romans 13:11; Hebrews 5:11–12"> wake up, as is right, and do not sin; for some have an ignorance of God; I say &#91;it&#93; to you for shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:3; 1 Corinthians 15:38–53; Romans 9:19"> But someone will say, “How do the dead rise?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 12:24; Luke 11:40; Ephesians 5:15"> Unwise! You—what you sow is not quickened except it may die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and that which you sow, you do not sow the body that will be, but a bare grain, it may be of wheat, or of someone of the others,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:7; Isaiah 61:11; Mark 4:26–29"> and God gives a body to it according as He willed, and its proper body to each of the seeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–26"> All flesh &#91;is&#93; not the same flesh, but there is one flesh of men, and another flesh of beasts, and another of fishes, and another of birds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and &#91;there are&#93; heavenly bodies and earthly bodies; but one &#91;is&#93; the glory of the heavenly, and another that of the earthly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 24:23; Psalms 148:3–5; Deuteronomy 4:19"> one glory of sun, and another glory of moon, and another glory of stars, for star differs from star in glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Daniel 12:3; Romans 8:21"> So also &#91;is&#93; the resurrection of the dead: it is sown in corruption, it is raised in incorruption;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:14; Colossians 3:4; Philippians 3:20–21"> it is sown in dishonor, it is raised in glory; it is sown in weakness, it is raised in power;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:50; Luke 24:31; John 20:19"> it is sown a natural body, it is raised a spiritual body; there is a natural body, and there is a spiritual body;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 5:21; Genesis 2:7; Romans 8:2"> so also it has been written: “The first man Adam became a living creature,” the last Adam &#91;is&#93; for a life-giving spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Colossians 3:9–10; Romans 6:6; Ephesians 4:22–24"> but that which is spiritual &#91;is&#93; not first, but that which &#91;was&#93; natural, afterward that which &#91;is&#93; spiritual.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:31; Genesis 2:7; Genesis 3:19"> The first man &#91;is&#93; out of the earth—earthly; the second Man &#91;is&#93; the LORD out of Heaven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Genesis 5:3; Romans 5:12–21; John 3:6"> as &#91;is&#93; the earthly, such &#91;are&#93; also the earthly; and as &#91;is&#93; the heavenly, such &#91;are&#93; also the heavenly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Romans 8:29; 2 Corinthians 3:18; Genesis 5:3"> and according as we bore the image of the earthly, we will also bear the image of the heavenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 3:3–6; Matthew 16:17; Ephesians 4:17"> And this I say, brothers, that flesh and blood are not able to inherit the Kingdom of God, nor does the corruption inherit the incorruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Philippians 3:21; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> Behold, I tell you a secret: we indeed will not all sleep, but we will all be changed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; Matthew 24:31; John 5:28"> in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, in the last trumpet, for it will sound, and the dead will be raised incorruptible, and we will be changed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:2–4; 1 John 3:2; Romans 2:7"> for it is necessary for this corruptible to put on incorruption, and this mortal to put on immortality;</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 20:14; Hebrews 2:14–15"> and when this corruptible may have put on incorruption, and this mortal may have put on immortality, then will be brought to pass the word that has been written: “Death was swallowed up—to victory;</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; Psalms 89:48; Acts 2:27"> Where, O Death, your sting? Where, O Death &#91;&#91;or Hades&#93;&#93;, your victory?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Romans 4:15; Galatians 3:10–13; Romans 7:5–13"> And the sting of death &#91;is&#93; sin, and the power of sin the Law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; Romans 8:37; 1 John 5:4–5"> and to God—thanks, to Him who is giving us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Chronicles 15:7; Hebrews 6:10"> so that, my beloved brothers, become steadfast, unmovable, abounding in the work of the LORD at all times, knowing that your labor in the LORD is not vain.</span>
