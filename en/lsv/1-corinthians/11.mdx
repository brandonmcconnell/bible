<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Philippians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 1:6; 1 Corinthians 4:16"> Become followers of me, as I also &#91;am&#93; of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:15; 1 Corinthians 4:17; Proverbs 31:28–31"> And I praise you, brothers, that in all things you remember me, and according as I delivered to you, you keep the deliverances,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:16; Colossians 1:18; Colossians 3:18"> and I wish you to know that the Christ is the head of every man, and the head of a woman is the husband, and the head of Christ is God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:30; 1 Corinthians 12:28; 1 Corinthians 12:10"> Every man praying or prophesying, having the head covered, dishonors his head,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:12; Luke 2:36; Acts 2:17"> and every woman praying or prophesying with the head uncovered, dishonors her own head, for it is one and the same thing with her being shaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:5; Numbers 5:18"> for if a woman is not covered—then let her be shorn, and if &#91;it is&#93; a shame for a woman to be shorn or shaven—let her be covered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3; Genesis 5:1; Psalms 8:6"> for a man, indeed, ought not to cover the head, being the image and glory of God, and a woman is the glory of a man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:13; Genesis 2:21–23"> for a man is not of a woman, but a woman &#91;is&#93; of a man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 2:18; Genesis 2:23–24; Genesis 2:20"> for also was a man not created because of the woman, but a woman because of the man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 24:64–65; Hebrews 1:14; Matthew 18:10"> because of this the woman ought to have &#91;a token of&#93; authority on the head, because of the messengers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; 1 Corinthians 7:10–14; 1 Corinthians 12:12–22"> but neither &#91;is&#93; a man apart from a woman, nor a woman apart from a man, in the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:36; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 1:2–3"> for as the woman &#91;is&#93; of the man, so also the man &#91;is&#93; through the woman, and all the things &#91;are&#93; of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:57; John 7:24; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> Judge in your own selves: is it seemly for a woman to pray to God uncovered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:26; 1 Corinthians 14:35"> Does not even nature itself teach you, that if a man indeed has long hair, it is a dishonor to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And a woman, if she has long hair, it is a glory to her, because the hair has been given to her instead of a covering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Timothy 6:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 2:14"> and if anyone thinks to be contentious, we have no such custom, neither the assemblies of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; 1 Corinthians 14:23; 1 Corinthians 11:22"> And declaring this, I give no praise, because you do not come together for the better, but for the worse;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 1:10–12; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> for first, indeed, coming together in an assembly, I hear of divisions being among you, and I partly believe &#91;it&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 2:19; Titus 3:10; Acts 20:30"> for it is also necessary for sects to be among you, that those approved may become visible among you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> you, then, coming together at the same place—it is not to eat the LORD’s Dinner;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:13; Jude 1:12; 1 Corinthians 11:23–25"> for each takes his own dinner before in the eating, and one is hungry, and another is drunk;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 20:28; Proverbs 17:5; James 2:5–6"> why, do you not have houses to eat and to drink in? Or do you despise the Assembly of God, and shame those not having? What may I say to you? Will I praise you in this? I do not praise!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:19–20; Matthew 26:26–28; 1 Corinthians 11:23–25"> For I received from the LORD that which I also delivered to you, that the Lord Jesus in the night in which He was delivered up, took bread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:53–58; 1 Corinthians 11:27–28; 1 Corinthians 10:3–4"> and having given thanks, He broke, and said, “Take, eat, this is My body that is being broken for you; do this—to the remembrance of Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 22:20; Hebrews 9:15–20; Hebrews 13:20"> In like manner also the cup after the supping, saying, “This cup is the New Covenant in My blood; do this, as often as you may drink &#91;it&#93;—to the remembrance of Me”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Revelation 1:7; John 14:3"> for as often as you may eat this bread, and may drink this cup, you show forth the death of the LORD—until He may come;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 6:51; 1 Corinthians 10:21; Hebrews 10:29"> so that whoever may eat this bread or may drink the cup of the LORD unworthily, he will be guilty of the body and blood of the LORD:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 6:4; 2 Corinthians 13:5; Lamentations 3:40"> and let a man be proving himself, and so let him eat of the bread, and let him drink of the cup;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:27; 1 Corinthians 11:30; 1 Corinthians 11:32–34"> for he who is eating and drinking unworthily, he eats and drinks judgment to himself—not discerning the body of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Numbers 20:12; Amos 3:2"> Because of this many &#91;are&#93; weak and sickly among you, and many sleep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 1:9; Psalms 32:3–5; Revelation 2:5"> for if we were discerning ourselves, we would not be being judged,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5–11; Psalms 118:18; Proverbs 3:11–12"> and being judged by the LORD, we are disciplined, that we may not be condemned with the world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> so then, my brothers, coming together to eat, wait for one another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:21–22; 1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Corinthians 4:19"> and if anyone is hungry, let him eat at home, that you may not come together to judgment; and the rest, whenever I may come, I will arrange.</span>
