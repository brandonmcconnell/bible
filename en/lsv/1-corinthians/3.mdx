<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 5:13; 1 Corinthians 2:14–15; Ephesians 4:13–14"> And I, brothers, was not able to speak to you as to spiritual, but as to fleshly—as to babies in Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 5:11–14; John 16:12; 1 Peter 2:2"> with milk I fed you, and not with meat, for you were not yet able, but not even yet are you now able,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:16; Galatians 5:15; Titus 3:3"> for yet you are fleshly, for where &#91;there is&#93; among you envying, and strife, and divisions, are you not fleshly, and walk in the manner of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 4:6"> For when one may say, “I, indeed, am of Paul,” and another, “I—of Apollos,” are you not fleshly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:4; 2 Corinthians 6:1; 1 Peter 4:10"> Who, then, is Paul, and who Apollos, but servants through whom you believed, and to each as the LORD gave?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9–10; Proverbs 11:25; 2 Corinthians 10:14–15"> I planted, Apollos watered, but God was giving growth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:5; Galatians 6:3; Psalms 115:1"> so that neither is he who is planting anything, nor he who is watering, but He who is giving growth—God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 2:6; Matthew 16:27; 2 John 1:8"> and he who is planting and he who is watering are one, and each will receive his own reward according to his own labor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> for we are fellow-workmen of God; you are God’s tillage, God’s building.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:11; Ephesians 2:20; Romans 15:20"> According to the grace of God that was given to me, as a wise master-builder, I have laid a foundation, and another builds on &#91;it&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20; Isaiah 28:16; 2 Timothy 2:19"> for no one is able to lay another foundation except that which is laid, which is Jesus the Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3; 2 Timothy 3:7; 2 Timothy 2:20"> and if anyone builds on this foundation gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, straw—</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 1:7; 1 Corinthians 3:14–15; Revelation 20:12"> the work will become visible of each, for the day will declare &#91;it&#93;, because it is revealed in fire, and the work of each, what kind it is, the fire will prove;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:8; Matthew 25:21–23; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> if the work of anyone remains that he built on &#91;it&#93;, he will receive a wage;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:12–13; Jude 1:23"> if the work of any is burned up, he will suffer loss, but himself will be saved, but so as through fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; Ezekiel 36:27; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> Have you not known that you are a temple of God, and the Spirit of God dwells in you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:18–20; Ezekiel 43:12; Numbers 19:20"> If anyone ruins the temple of God, God will ruin him; for the temple of God is holy, which you are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Isaiah 5:21; Galatians 6:3"> Let no one deceive himself; if anyone seems to be wise among you in this age—let him become a fool, that he may become wise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 5:13; Psalms 141:10; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> for the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God, for it has been written: “Who is taking the wise in their craftiness”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 94:11; Colossians 2:8; Romans 1:21"> and again, “The LORD knows the reasonings of the wise, that they are vain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Romans 8:28; Jeremiah 9:23–24"> So then, let no one glory in men, for all things are yours,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–12; Philippians 1:21; 1 Corinthians 9:19–22"> whether Paul, or Apollos, or Cephas, or the world, or life, or death, or things present, or things about to be—all are yours,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; 1 Corinthians 11:3; John 17:9–10"> and you &#91;are&#93; Christ’s, and Christ &#91;is&#93; God’s.</span>
