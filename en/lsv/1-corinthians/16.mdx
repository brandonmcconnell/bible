<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:9–10; Deuteronomy 15:11–14; Deuteronomy 8:18"> on every first &#91;day&#93; of the week, let each one of you lay by him, treasuring up whatever he may have prospered, that when I may come then collections may not be made;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; 1 Corinthians 11:34; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21"> and whenever I may come, whomsoever you may approve, through letters, these I will send to carry your favor to Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:4; 2 Corinthians 8:19; Romans 15:25"> and if it be worthy for me also to go, with me they will go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 19:21; 1 Corinthians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 1:15–17"> And I will come to you when I pass through Macedonia—for I pass through Macedonia—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 15:3; Romans 15:24; Acts 20:38"> and with you, it may be, I will abide, or even winter, that you may send me forward wherever I go,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 18:21; 1 Corinthians 4:19; James 4:15"> for I do not wish to see you now in the passing, but I hope to remain a certain time with you, if the LORD may permit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:1; Exodus 23:16; Acts 18:19"> and I will remain in Ephesus until the Pentecost,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12; Acts 14:27; Colossians 4:3"> for a door has been opened to me—great and effectual—and withstanders &#91;are&#93; many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; Romans 16:21"> And if Timotheus may come, see that he may become without fear with you, for he works the work of the LORD, even as I,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:12; Acts 15:33; Titus 2:15"> no one, then, may despise him; and send him forward in peace, that he may come to me, for I expect him with the brothers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:5; Titus 3:4"> and concerning Apollos our brother, I begged him much that he may come to you with the brothers, and it was not at all &#91;his&#93; will that he may come now, and he will come when he may find convenient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:13–18; Ephesians 6:10"> Watch, stand in the faith; be men, be strong;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 13:34–35; 1 Peter 4:8; 1 Timothy 1:5"> let all your things be done in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:16; Romans 16:5; Romans 16:2"> And I beg you, brothers, you have known the household of Stephanas, that it is the first-fruit of Achaia, and they set themselves to the ministry to the holy ones—</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:12; 3 John 1:8"> that you also be subject to such, and to everyone who is working with &#91;us&#93; and laboring;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; Philippians 2:30; Philemon 1:13"> and I rejoice over the coming of Stephanas, and Fortunatus, and Achaicus, because these filled up the lack of you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philemon 1:7; 2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:12"> for they refreshed my spirit and yours; acknowledge, therefore, those who &#91;are&#93; such.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 18:2; Philemon 1:2; Romans 16:3–5"> The assemblies of Asia greet you; Aquilas and Priscilla greet you much in the LORD, with the assembly in their house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 16:16; 1 Thessalonians 5:26; 1 Peter 5:14"> all the brothers greet you; greet one another in a holy kiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Colossians 4:18; Galatians 6:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:17"> The salutation of &#91;me&#93;, Paul, with my hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 6:24; John 14:21; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> if anyone does not cherish the Lord Jesus Christ—let him be accursed! The LORD has come!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Romans 16:23"> The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ &#91;is&#93; with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:16; Revelation 3:19; 2 Corinthians 12:15"> my love &#91;is&#93; with you all in Christ Jesus! Amen.</span>
