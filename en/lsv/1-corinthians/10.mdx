<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 14:29; Nehemiah 9:11–12; Psalms 78:13–14"> And I do not wish you to be ignorant, brothers, that all our fathers were under the cloud, and all passed through the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 3:2–3; 1 Corinthians 1:13–16; Exodus 14:31"> and all were immersed into Moses in the cloud, and in the sea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Nehemiah 9:15; Exodus 16:35"> and all ate the same spiritual food,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 17:6; John 7:37; Isaiah 48:21"> and all drank the same spiritual drink, for they were drinking of a spiritual rock following them, and the rock was the Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 3:17; Jude 1:5; Numbers 14:37"> but in the most of them God was not well pleased, for they were strewn in the wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:7; 1 Corinthians 10:11; Hebrews 4:11"> and those things became types of us, for our not passionately desiring evil things, as also these desired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 32:6–8; Psalms 106:19–20; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> Neither become idolaters, as certain of them, as it has been written: “The people sat down to eat and to drink, and stood up to play”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 1 Corinthians 6:18; Psalms 106:29"> neither may we commit whoredom, as certain of them committed whoredom, and there fell in one day twenty-three thousand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 21:5–6; Psalms 78:18; Hebrews 3:8–11"> neither may we tempt the Christ, as also certain of them tempted, and perished by the serpents;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 16:41; Numbers 14:37"> neither murmur, as also some of them murmured, and perished by the destroyer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 15:4; Romans 13:11; 1 Corinthians 9:10"> And all these things happened to those persons as types, and they were written for our admonition, to whom the end of the ages came,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:20; 2 Peter 3:17; Proverbs 16:18"> so that he who is thinking to stand—let him observe, lest he fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 2:9; 2 Timothy 4:18; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> No temptation has taken you—except that of man; and God is faithful, who will not permit you to be tempted above what you are able, but He will make, with the temptation, also the outlet, for your being able to bear &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; 1 John 5:21; 1 Peter 2:11"> For this reason, my beloved, flee from the idolatry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 11:13"> as to wise men I speak—judge what I say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:26–28; 1 Corinthians 11:23–29; Acts 2:42"> The cup of the blessing that we bless—is it not the fellowship of the blood of the Christ? The bread that we break—is it not the fellowship of the body of the Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:5; Colossians 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> Because one bread, one body, are we the many—for we all partake of the one bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 3:3–5; Galatians 6:16; Leviticus 7:6"> See Israel according to the flesh! Are those not eating the sacrifices in the fellowship of the altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:4; 1 Corinthians 3:7; 1 Corinthians 13:2"> What do I say then? That an idol is anything? Or that a sacrifice offered to an idol is anything?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 9:20; Psalms 106:37–39; Deuteronomy 32:16–17"> &#91;No,&#93; but that the things that the nations sacrifice—they sacrifice to demons and not to God; and I do not wish you to come into the fellowship of the demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; 1 Kings 18:21; 2 Corinthians 6:15–17"> You are not able to drink the cup of the LORD and the cup of demons; you are not able to partake of the table of the LORD and of the table of demons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Deuteronomy 32:16; Hebrews 10:31"> do we arouse the LORD to jealousy? Are we stronger than He?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:12; 1 Corinthians 8:9; Ephesians 4:29"> All things are lawful to me, but not all things are profitable; all things are lawful to me, but not all things build up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:1–2; 1 Corinthians 10:33; Philippians 2:4–5"> let no one seek his own—but each another’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; 1 Corinthians 8:7; Titus 1:15"> Eat whatever is sold in the meat-market, not inquiring, because of the conscience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 24:1; Exodus 19:5; Psalms 50:12"> for the earth and its fullness &#91;are&#93; the LORD’s;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:25; Luke 10:7–8; 1 Corinthians 5:9–11"> and if anyone of the unbelieving calls you, and you wish to go, eat all that is set before you, inquiring nothing, because of the conscience;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:10–13; 1 Corinthians 8:7; 1 Corinthians 10:26"> and if anyone may say to you, “This is a thing sacrificed to an idol,” do not eat, because of that one who showed &#91;it&#93;, and of the conscience, for the LORD’s &#91;is&#93; the earth and its fullness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:19; Romans 14:15–21; 1 Corinthians 8:9–13"> and conscience, I say, not of yourself, but of the other, for why &#91;is it&#93; that my liberty is judged by another’s conscience?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 14:6; 1 Timothy 4:3–4"> And if I partake thankfully, why am I spoken of &#91;as&#93; evil, for that for which I give thanks?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Colossians 3:23; 1 Peter 4:11"> Whether, then, you eat, or drink, or do anything, do all to the glory of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 20:28; 1 Corinthians 10:33; Romans 14:13"> become offenseless, both to Jews and Greeks, and to the Assembly of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23"> as I also please all in all things, not seeking my own profit, but that of many—that they may be saved.</span>
