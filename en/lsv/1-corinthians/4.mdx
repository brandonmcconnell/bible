<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Titus 1:7; 1 Timothy 3:9"> Let a man so reckon us as officers of Christ, and stewards of the secrets of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Luke 12:42; Luke 16:10–12"> and as to the rest, it is required in the stewards that one may be found faithful,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 7:24; 1 Corinthians 2:15; 1 Samuel 16:7"> and to me it is for a very little thing that by you I may be judged, or by man’s Day, but I do not even judge myself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 143:2; 1 John 3:20–21; Psalms 130:3"> for I have been conscious of nothing for myself, but I have not been declared right in this—and He who is discerning me is the LORD:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:7; 2 Corinthians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> so, then, judge nothing before the time, until the LORD may come, who will both bring to light the hidden things of the darkness, and will reveal the counsels of the hearts, and then the praise will come to each from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:6; 2 Corinthians 11:4; 2 Corinthians 12:19"> And these things, brothers, I transferred to myself and to Apollos because of you, that in us you may learn not to think above that which has been written, that you may not be puffed up one for one against the other,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 3:27; 1 Peter 4:10; James 1:17"> for who makes you to differ? And what do you have, that you did not receive? And if you also received, why do you glory as not having received?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 6:25; Proverbs 13:7; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> Already you are having been filled, already you were rich, apart from us you reigned, and I also wish you reigned, that we also may reign together with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:33; Romans 8:36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–12"> for I think that God set forth us the apostles last—as appointed to death, because we became a spectacle to the world, and messengers, and men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:3; Acts 26:24; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> we &#91;are&#93; fools because of Christ, and you wise in Christ; we &#91;are&#93; ailing, and you strong; you glorious, and we dishonored;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:35; 2 Corinthians 11:23–27; Philippians 4:12"> to the present hour we both hunger, and thirst, and are naked, and are battered, and wander,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Matthew 5:11; 1 Peter 4:12–14"> and labor, working with &#91;our&#93; own hands; being reviled, we bless; being persecuted, we endure;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Lamentations 3:45; Acts 22:22"> being spoken evil of, we plead; we became as filth of the world—of all things an outcast—until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Corinthians 4:15; 3 John 1:4"> Not &#91;as&#93; putting you to shame do I write these things, but I admonish as my beloved children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:19; 1 Corinthians 3:10; 1 Corinthians 9:14"> for if a myriad of child-conductors you may have in Christ, yet not many fathers; for in Christ Jesus, through the good news, I begot you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Thessalonians 3:9; Philippians 3:17"> I call on you, therefore, become followers of me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:10; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> because of this I sent to you Timotheus, who is my child, beloved and faithful in the LORD, who will remind you of my ways in Christ, according as everywhere in every assembly I teach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:6–8; 1 Corinthians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 4:21"> And some were puffed up as if I were not coming to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:1–4; Acts 18:21; Acts 19:21"> but I will come quickly to you, if the LORD may will, and I will not know the word of those puffed up, but the power;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4; 1 Thessalonians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5"> for not in word is the Kingdom of God, but in power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:2; 2 Corinthians 13:10; 1 Thessalonians 2:7"> What do you wish? Will I come to you with a rod, or in love, also with a spirit of meekness?</span>
