<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:39; 1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Corinthians 12:1"> Pursue love, and earnestly seek the spiritual things, and rather that you may prophesy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Acts 19:6; Acts 10:46"> for he who is speaking in an &#91;unknown&#93; tongue—he does not speak to men, but to God, for no one listens, and he speaks secrets in spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:12; 1 Timothy 4:13; Jude 1:20"> and he who is prophesying to men speaks edification, and exhortation, and comfort;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:22; 1 Corinthians 14:26; Mark 16:17"> he who is speaking in an &#91;unknown&#93; tongue, edifies himself, and he who is prophesying, edifies the Assembly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:28–30; 1 Corinthians 12:10; Mark 16:17"> and I wish you all to speak with tongues, and more that you may prophesy, for greater is he who is prophesying than he who is speaking with tongues, except one may interpret, that the Assembly may receive edification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17; Romans 6:17; 2 Timothy 3:16"> And now, brothers, if I may come to you speaking tongues, what will I profit you, except I will speak to you either in revelation, or in knowledge, or in prophesying, or in teaching?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:8; Luke 7:32; 1 Corinthians 13:1"> Yet the things without life giving sound—whether pipe or harp—if they may not give a difference in the sounds, how will be known that which is piped or that which is harped?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 10:9; Ezekiel 33:3–6; Jeremiah 4:19"> For also, if a trumpet may give an uncertain sound, who will prepare himself for battle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:26"> So also you, if you may not give speech easily understood through the tongue—how will that which is spoken be known? For you will be speaking to air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> There are, it may be, so many kinds of voices in the world, and none of them is unmeaning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 28:2; Romans 1:14; Colossians 3:11"> If, then, I do not know the power of the voice, I will be a foreigner to him who is speaking, and he who is speaking is a foreigner to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:26; 1 Corinthians 14:3–4; 1 Corinthians 14:1"> so you also, since you are earnestly desirous of spiritual gifts, seek for the building up of the Assembly that you may abound;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 11:24; 1 Corinthians 12:30; John 14:13–14"> for this reason he who is speaking in an &#91;unknown&#93; tongue—let him pray that he may interpret;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:2; 1 Corinthians 14:15–16; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> for if I pray in an &#91;unknown&#93; tongue, my spirit prays, and my understanding is unfruitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; John 4:23–24; Ephesians 6:18"> What then is it? I will pray &#91;in&#93; the spirit, and I will also pray &#91;with&#93; understanding; I will sing psalms &#91;in&#93; the spirit, and I will also sing psalms &#91;with&#93; understanding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:36; Psalms 106:48; Revelation 5:14"> since, if you may bless in spirit, he who is filling the place of the commoner, how will he say the Amen at your giving of thanks, since he has not known what you say?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:4; 1 Corinthians 14:6"> For you, indeed, give thanks well, but the other is not built up!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:4–6; 1 Corinthians 4:7"> I give thanks to my God—more than you all with tongues speaking—</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:21–22; 1 Corinthians 14:4"> but I wish to speak five words in an assembly through my understanding, that I also may instruct others, rather than myriads of words in an &#91;unknown&#93; tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:2; 1 Corinthians 13:11; Romans 16:19"> Brothers, do not become children in the understanding, but in the evil be children, and in the understanding become perfect;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 28:11–12; Jeremiah 5:15; Deuteronomy 28:49"> in the Law it has been written, that, “With other tongues and with other lips I will speak to this people, and even so they will not hear Me, says the LORD”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 2:6–12; Mark 16:17; 1 Corinthians 14:1"> so that the tongues are for a sign, not to the believing, but to the unbelieving; and the prophecy &#91;is&#93; not for the unbelieving, but for the believing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 2:13; Acts 26:24; 1 Corinthians 11:18"> If, therefore, the whole assembly may come together to the same place, and all may speak with tongues, and there may come in commoners or unbelievers, will they not say that you are mad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 2:37; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Hebrews 4:12–13"> And if all may prophesy, and anyone may come in, an unbeliever or commoner, he is convicted by all, he is discerned by all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 45:14; Zechariah 8:23; Psalms 72:11"> and so the secrets of his heart become visible, and so having fallen on &#91;his&#93; face, he will worship God, declaring that God really is among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 5:19; Romans 14:19; 1 Corinthians 12:7–10"> What then is it, brothers? Whenever you may come together, each of you has a psalm, has a teaching, has a tongue, has a revelation, has an interpretation. Let all things be for building up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:26"> if anyone speaks an &#91;unknown&#93; tongue, by two, or at the most, by three, and in turn, and let one interpret;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and if there may be no interpreter, let him be silent in an assembly, and let him speak to himself, and to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:19–21; 1 John 4:1–3"> And prophets—let two or three speak, and let the others discern,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:19–20; Job 33:31–33; Job 32:11"> and if &#91;anything&#93; may be revealed to another &#91;who is&#93; sitting, let the first be silent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Corinthians 14:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> for you are able, one by one, all to prophesy, that all may learn, and all may be exhorted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 4:19–20; 1 John 4:1; 1 Samuel 10:10–13"> and the spiritual gift of prophets are subject to prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 3:17–18; 1 Corinthians 14:40; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> for God is not &#91;a God&#93; of tumult, but of peace, as in all the assemblies of the holy ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:11–12; Genesis 3:16; Numbers 30:3–13"> Your women, let them be silent in the assemblies, for it has not been permitted to them to speak, but to be subject, as the Law also says;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; 1 Peter 3:7; Ephesians 5:25–27"> and if they wish to learn anything, let them question their own husbands at home, for it is a shame to women to speak in an assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 18:1–17; Zechariah 14:8; Acts 17:15"> Did the word of God come forth from you? Or did it come to you alone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:7; 2 Peter 3:2"> If anyone thinks to be a prophet, or spiritual, let him acknowledge the things that I write to you—that they are commands of the LORD;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Hosea 4:17; Revelation 22:11–12"> and if anyone is ignorant—let him be ignorant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:31; 1 Corinthians 14:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:20"> so that, brothers, earnestly desire to prophesy, and do not forbid to speak with tongues;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Colossians 2:5; Titus 1:5; 1 Corinthians 14:26–33"> let all things be done decently and in order.</span>
