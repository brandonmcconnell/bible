<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 16:1; Exodus 12:6; Leviticus 23:16–18"> In the third month of the going out of the sons of Israel from the land of Egypt, in this day they have come into the wilderness of Sinai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Exodus 17:1; Exodus 3:12"> and they journey from Rephidim, and enter the wilderness of Sinai, and encamp in the wilderness; and Israel encamps there before the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 3:4; Acts 7:38; Exodus 20:21"> And Moses has gone up to God, and YHWH calls to him out of the mountain, saying, “Thus you say to the house of Jacob, and declare to the sons of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:11–12; Isaiah 63:9; Isaiah 40:31"> You have seen that which I have done to the Egyptians, and &#91;how&#93; I carry you on eagles’ wings and bring you to Myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:2; Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 26:18"> And now, if you really listen to My voice, then you have kept My covenant, and have been a special treasure to Me more than all the peoples, for all the earth &#91;is&#93; Mine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; 1 Peter 2:9; 1 Peter 2:5"> and you are to Me a kingdom of priests and a holy nation: these &#91;are&#93; the words which you speak to the sons of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 4:29–30; Exodus 3:16; 1 Corinthians 15:1"> And Moses comes, and calls for &#91;the&#93; elderly of the people, and sets before them all these words which YHWH has commanded him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 24:7; Exodus 24:3; Joshua 24:24"> and all the people answer together and say, “All that YHWH has spoken we do”; and Moses returns the words of the people to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:36; Exodus 19:16; Exodus 24:15–16"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Behold, I am coming to you in the thickness of the cloud, so that the people hear in My speaking with you, and also believe in you for all time”; and Moses declares the words of the people to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; Genesis 35:2; Leviticus 11:44–45"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Go to the people and you have sanctified them today and tomorrow, and they have washed their garments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:16; Exodus 34:5; Deuteronomy 33:2"> and have been prepared for the third day; for on the third day YHWH comes down on Mount Sinai before the eyes of all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 12:20–21; Joshua 3:4; Exodus 19:21"> And you have made a border &#91;for&#93; the people all around, saying, Take heed to yourselves, going up into the mountain or coming against its extremity; whoever is coming against the mountain is certainly put to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16; Hebrews 12:20; Exodus 19:16"> a hand does not come against him, for he is certainly stoned or shot through, whether beast or man—it does not live; in the prolonging of the ram’s horn they go up into the mountain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:10"> And Moses comes down from the mountain to the people, and sanctifies the people, and they wash their garments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:5; 1 Samuel 21:4–5; Exodus 19:11"> and he says to the people, “Be prepared for the third day, do not come near to a woman.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:18–19; Psalms 18:11–14; Hebrews 12:21"> And it comes to pass, on the third day, while it is morning, that there are voices, and lightnings, and a heavy cloud on the mountain, and the sound of a very strong horn; and all the people who &#91;are&#93; in the camp tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:10; Deuteronomy 5:5"> And Moses brings out the people from the camp to meet God, and they station themselves at the lower part of the mountain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 5:5; Exodus 3:2; Psalms 144:5"> and Mount Sinai &#91;is&#93; wholly &#91;in&#93; smoke from the presence of YHWH, who has come down on it in fire, and its smoke goes up as smoke of the furnace, and the whole mountain trembles exceedingly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 81:7; Exodus 19:16; Nehemiah 9:13"> and the sound of the horn is going on, and &#91;is&#93; very strong; Moses speaks, and God answers him with a voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:13; Exodus 34:2; Exodus 19:11"> And YHWH comes down on Mount Sinai, to the top of the mountain, and YHWH calls for Moses to the top of the mountain, and Moses goes up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19; Exodus 3:5; Hebrews 12:28–29"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Go down, protest to the people, lest they break through to YHWH to see, and many of them have fallen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 24:5; Acts 5:10; 1 Chronicles 13:9–11"> and also the priests who are coming near to YHWH sanctify themselves, lest YHWH break forth on them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 19:12; Joshua 3:4–5"> And Moses says to YHWH, “The people &#91;are&#93; unable to come up to Mount Sinai, for You have protested to us, saying, Make a border &#91;for&#93; the mountain, then you have sanctified it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 4:16; Hebrews 12:18–25; Hebrews 12:29"> And YHWH says to him, “Go, descend, then you have come up, you and Aaron with you; and the priests and the people do not break through to come up to YHWH, lest He break forth on them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Moses goes down to the people and speaks to them.</span>
