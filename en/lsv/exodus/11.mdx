<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 3:22; Exodus 12:35–36; Haggai 2:8"> now speak in the ears of the people, and they ask—each man from his neighbor, and each woman from her neighbor—&#91;for&#93; vessels of silver and vessels of gold.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 12:29; Psalms 60:10; Job 34:20"> And Moses says, “Thus said YHWH: About midnight I am going out into the midst of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 78:51; Exodus 12:12; Psalms 105:36"> and every firstborn in the land of Egypt has died, from the firstborn of Pharaoh who is sitting on his throne, to the firstborn of the maidservant who &#91;is&#93; behind the millstones, and all the firstborn of beasts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 12:30; Amos 5:17; Zephaniah 1:10"> and there has been a great cry in all the land of Egypt, such as there has not been, and such as there is not again;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 10:21; Exodus 10:23; Exodus 8:22"> and against all the sons of Israel a dog does not sharpen its tongue, from man even to beast, so that you know that YHWH makes a separation between the Egyptians and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 12:31–33; Ezekiel 3:14; Daniel 3:19"> And all these servants of yours have come down to me, and bowed themselves to me, saying, Go out, you and all the people who &#91;are&#93; at your feet; and afterward I go out”; and he goes out from Pharaoh in the heat of anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 7:3–4; Exodus 3:19; Exodus 10:1"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Pharaoh does not listen to you, so as to multiply My wonders in the land of Egypt”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 10:20; Exodus 10:27"> and Moses and Aaron have done all these wonders before Pharaoh, and YHWH strengthens Pharaoh’s heart, and he has not sent the sons of Israel out of his land.</span>
