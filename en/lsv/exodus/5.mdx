<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 10:9; Acts 4:29; Matthew 10:28"> And afterward Moses and Aaron have entered, and they say to Pharaoh, “Thus said YHWH, God of Israel: Send My people away, and they keep a celebration for Me in the wilderness”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 3:19; Job 21:15; 2 Kings 18:35"> and Pharaoh says, “Who &#91;is&#93; YHWH, that I listen to His voice to send Israel away? I have not known YHWH, and I also do not send Israel away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Zechariah 14:16–19; Ezekiel 6:11"> And they say, “The God of the Hebrews has met with us; please let us go a journey of three days into the wilderness, and we sacrifice to our God YHWH, lest He meet us with pestilence or with sword.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Acts 24:5; Amos 7:10"> And the king of Egypt says to them, “Why, Moses and Aaron, do you free the people from its works? Go to your burdens.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 1:7–11; Proverbs 14:28"> Pharaoh also says, “Behold, the people of the land &#91;are&#93; numerous now, and you have caused them to cease from their burdens!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 5:10; Exodus 5:19; Deuteronomy 16:18"> And on that day Pharaoh commands the exactors among the people and its authorities, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:19; Genesis 24:25"> “You do not add to give straw to the people for the making of the bricks as before—they go and have gathered straw for themselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:41"> and you put on them the proper quantity of the bricks which they are making before, you do not diminish from it, for they are remiss, therefore they are crying, saying, Let us go, let us sacrifice to our God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 18:20; Jeremiah 43:2; Job 16:3"> let the service be heavy on the men, and let them work at it, and not be dazzled by lying words.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Exodus 3:7; Proverbs 29:12"> And the exactors of the people, and its authorities, go out, and speak to the people, saying, “Thus said Pharaoh: I do not give you straw,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 5:13–14"> you—go, take straw for yourselves where you can find &#91;it&#93;, for there is nothing diminished of your service.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:7; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 47:14"> And the people are scattered over all the land of Egypt, to gather stubble for straw,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and the exactors are making haste, saying, “Complete your works, the matter of a day in its day, as when there is straw.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 10:24"> And the authorities of the sons of Israel, whom the exactors of Pharaoh have placed over them, are beaten, saying, “Why have you not completed your portion in making brick as before, both yesterday and today?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the authorities of the sons of Israel come in and cry to Pharaoh, saying, “Why do you do thus to your servants?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Straw is not given to your servants, and they are saying to us, Make bricks, and behold, your servants are struck—and your people have sinned.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 26:8; John 6:27; Exodus 5:8"> And he says, “Remiss—you are remiss, therefore you are saying, Let us go, let us sacrifice to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:18; Daniel 2:9–13"> and now, go, serve; and straw is not given to you, and you give the &#91;required&#93; measure of bricks.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:8; Ecclesiastes 4:1; Deuteronomy 32:36"> And the authorities of the sons of Israel see them in affliction, saying, “You do not diminish from your bricks; the matter of a day in its day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And they meet Moses and Aaron standing to meet them in their coming out from Pharaoh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Chronicles 19:6"> and say to them, “YHWH look on you, and judge, because you have caused our fragrance to stink in the eyes of Pharaoh, and in the eyes of his servants—to give a sword into their hand to slay us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:7; 1 Kings 19:10; Numbers 11:14–15"> And Moses turns back to YHWH and says, “Lord, why have You done evil to this people? Why &#91;is&#93; this &#91;that&#93; You have sent me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:21; Hebrews 10:36–37; Isaiah 28:16"> And since I have come to Pharaoh to speak in Your Name, he has done evil to this people, and You have not delivered Your people at all.”</span>
