<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 24:15; Exodus 24:13; Hebrews 10:21–22"> and Moses has drawn near to YHWH by himself; and they do not draw near, and the people do not go up with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 19:8; Exodus 24:7; Joshua 24:22"> And Moses comes in, and recounts to the people all the words of YHWH, and all the judgments, and all the people answer &#91;with&#93; one voice, and say, “All the words which YHWH has spoken we do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:18; Deuteronomy 31:9; Joshua 4:8–9"> And Moses writes all the words of YHWH, and rises early in the morning, and builds an altar under the hill, and twelve standing pillars for the twelve tribes of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:12; Leviticus 3:1–17; Exodus 19:22"> and he sends the youths of the sons of Israel, and they cause burnt-offerings to ascend, and sacrifice sacrifices of peace-offerings to YHWH—calves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19; Exodus 24:8; Hebrews 9:18"> And Moses takes half of the blood and puts &#91;it&#93; in basins, and he has sprinkled half of the blood on the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:11–13; 1 Thessalonians 5:27; Exodus 24:3–4"> and he takes the Scroll of the Covenant, and proclaims &#91;it&#93; in the ears of the people, and they say, “All that which YHWH has spoken we do, and we obey.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–5; Exodus 24:6; Ezekiel 36:25"> And Moses takes the blood and sprinkles &#91;it&#93; on the people, and says, “Behold, the blood of the covenant which YHWH has made with you, concerning all these things.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 24:1"> And Moses goes up, Aaron also, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy from &#91;the&#93; elderly of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:18; Ezekiel 10:1; Genesis 32:30"> and they see the God of Israel, and under His feet &#91;is&#93; as the work of a pavement of sapphire, and as the substance of the heavens for purity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 15:23–24; Exodus 18:12; Genesis 31:54"> and He has not put forth His hand to those of the sons of Israel who are near, and they see God, and eat and drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:22; Exodus 31:18; Exodus 32:15–16"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Come up to Me &#91;on&#93; the mountain and be there, and I give to you the tablets of stone, and the Law, and the command, which I have written to direct them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 33:11; Numbers 11:28; Exodus 3:1"> And Moses rises—his minister Joshua also—and Moses goes up to the mountain of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 17:10; Exodus 17:12; Genesis 22:5"> and he has said to the elderly, “Abide for us in this &#91;place&#93; until we return to you, and behold, Aaron and Hur &#91;are&#93; with you—he who has matters comes near to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Matthew 17:5; Exodus 19:16"> And Moses goes up to the mountain, and the cloud covers the mountain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 16:10; Numbers 16:42; Ezekiel 1:28"> and the glory of YHWH dwells on Mount Sinai, and the cloud covers it &#91;for&#93; six days, and He calls to Moses on the seventh day from the midst of the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Deuteronomy 4:24; Exodus 3:2"> And the appearance of the glory of YHWH &#91;is&#93; as a consuming fire on the top of the mountain, before the eyes of the sons of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:9; Exodus 34:28; Deuteronomy 10:10"> and Moses goes into the midst of the cloud, and goes up to the mountain, and Moses is on the mountain forty days and forty nights.</span>
