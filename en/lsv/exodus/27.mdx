<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–7; Exodus 20:24–26; Exodus 40:10"> “And you have made the altar of shittim wood, five cubits the length, and five cubits the breadth—the altar is square—and three cubits &#91;is&#93; its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:27; Exodus 29:12; Leviticus 4:7"> And you have made its horns on its four corners, its horns are of the same, and you have overlaid it &#91;with&#93; bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 7:45; Numbers 4:14; 1 Chronicles 28:17"> And you have made its pots to remove its ashes, and its shovels, and its bowls, and its forks, and its fire-pans, even all its vessels you make of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 35:16; Exodus 25:12; Exodus 38:4–5"> And you have made a grate of network of bronze for it, and have made four rings of bronze on the net on its four extremities,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 38:4"> and have put it under the rim of the altar beneath, and the net has been up to the middle of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:13–15; Numbers 4:44; Exodus 30:4"> And you have made poles for the altar, poles of shittim wood, and have overlaid them &#91;with&#93; bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:4; Numbers 4:13–14"> And the poles have been brought into the rings, and the poles have been on the two sides of the altar in carrying it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 25:9; Hebrews 8:5"> You make it hollow &#91;with&#93; boards; as it has been showed &#91;to&#93; you on the mountain, so do they make &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:9–20; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 42:3"> And you have made the court of the Dwelling Place: for the south side southward, hangings for the court of twined linen, the length of the first side &#91;is&#93; one hundred by the cubit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 26:19–21; Jeremiah 52:21; Exodus 36:38"> and its twenty pillars and their twenty sockets &#91;are&#93; of bronze, the pegs of the pillars and their fillets &#91;are&#93; of silver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and so for the north side in length, hangings of one hundred &#91;cubits&#93; in length, and its twenty pillars and their twenty sockets &#91;are&#93; of bronze, the pegs of the pillars and their fillets &#91;are&#93; of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And &#91;for&#93; the breadth of the court at the west side &#91;are&#93; hangings of fifty cubits, their pillars ten, and their sockets ten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the breadth of the court at the east side, eastward, &#91;is&#93; fifty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Exodus 26:36"> And the hangings at the side &#91;are&#93; fifteen cubits, their pillars three, and their sockets three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And at the second side &#91;are&#93; hangings of fifteen &#91;cubits&#93;, their pillars three, and their sockets three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 26:36; Judges 5:30; Exodus 28:39"> And for the gate of the court &#91;is&#93; a covering of twenty cubits of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined linen, the work of an embroiderer; their pillars four, their sockets four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> All the pillars of the court all around &#91;are&#93; filleted &#91;with&#93; silver, their pegs &#91;are&#93; silver, and their sockets bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The length of the court &#91;is&#93; one hundred by the cubit, and the breadth fifty by fifty, and the height five cubits, of twined linen, and their sockets &#91;are&#93; bronze;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Isaiah 22:23–25; Numbers 4:32"> for all the vessels of the Dwelling Place, in all its service, and all its pins, and all the pins of the court, &#91;are&#93; bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 24:1–4; Zechariah 4:11–14; Psalms 23:5"> And you command the sons of Israel, and they bring pure oil of beaten olive to you for the light, to cause the lamp to go up continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 19:21; Numbers 18:23; 1 Samuel 3:3"> In the Tent of Meeting, at the outside of the veil, which &#91;is&#93; over the Testimony, Aaron—his sons also—arrange it from evening until morning before YHWH. &#91;It is&#93; a continuous statute for their generations, from the sons of Israel.”</span>
