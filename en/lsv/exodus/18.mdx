<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 2:16; Psalms 77:14–15; Exodus 3:1"> And Jethro priest of Midian, father-in-law of Moses, hears all that God has done for Moses and for His people Israel, that YHWH has brought out Israel from Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 2:21; Exodus 4:25–26"> and Jethro, father-in-law of Moses, takes Zipporah, wife of Moses, after her parting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 2:22; Acts 7:29; Psalms 39:12"> and her two sons, of whom the name of one &#91;is&#93; Gershom, for he said, “I have been a sojourner in a strange land”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; 2 Timothy 4:17; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> and the name of the other &#91;is&#93; Eliezer, for, “The God of my father &#91;is&#93; for my help, and He delivers me from the sword of Pharaoh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Exodus 3:12; Exodus 24:16–17"> And Jethro, father-in-law of Moses, comes, and his sons, and his wife, to Moses, to the wilderness where he is encamping—the mountain of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and he says to Moses, “I, your father-in-law Jethro, am coming to you, and your wife, and her two sons with her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 29:13; Genesis 14:17; 2 Samuel 11:7"> And Moses goes out to meet his father-in-law, and bows himself, and kisses him, and they ask of one another of welfare, and come into the tent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 145:4–12; Nehemiah 9:9–15; Psalms 107:2"> and Moses recounts to his father-in-law all that YHWH has done to Pharaoh, and to the Egyptians, on account of Israel, all the travail which has found them in the way, and &#91;how&#93; YHWH delivers them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Romans 12:15; Isaiah 66:10"> And Jethro rejoices for all the good which YHWH has done to Israel, whom He has delivered from the hand of the Egyptians;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 14:20; 2 Samuel 18:28; Luke 1:68"> and Jethro says, “Blessed &#91;is&#93; YHWH, who has delivered you from the hand of the Egyptians, and from the hand of Pharaoh—who has delivered this people from under the hand of the Egyptians;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 95:3; Exodus 15:11; 2 Chronicles 2:5"> now I have known that YHWH &#91;is&#93; greater than all the gods, for in the thing they have acted proudly—&#91;He is&#93; above them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Deuteronomy 12:7; Exodus 24:11"> And Jethro, father-in-law of Moses, takes a burnt-offering and sacrifices &#91;it&#93; for God; and Aaron comes in, and all &#91;the&#93; elderly of Israel, to eat bread with the father-in-law of Moses, before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 16:5; Job 29:7; Judges 5:10"> And it comes to pass on the next day, that Moses sits to judge the people, and the people stand before Moses from the morning to the evening;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and the father-in-law of Moses sees all that he is doing for the people and says, “What &#91;is&#93; this thing which you are doing for the people? Why are you sitting by yourself, and all the people standing by you from morning until evening?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 15:34; Numbers 27:5; Numbers 9:8"> And Moses says to his father-in-law, “Because the people come to me to seek God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 24:14; 1 Corinthians 6:1; 2 Samuel 15:3"> when they have a matter, it has come to me, and I have judged between a man and his neighbor, and made known the statutes of God, and His laws.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the father-in-law of Moses says to him, “The thing which you are doing &#91;is&#93; not good;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 11:14–17; Acts 6:1–4; 2 Corinthians 12:15"> you surely wear away, both you, and this people which &#91;is&#93; with you, for the thing is too heavy for you, you are not able to do it by yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 27:5; Deuteronomy 5:5; Exodus 20:19"> Now listen to my voice, I counsel you, and God is with you: be for the people before God, and you have brought in the things to God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:1; Psalms 143:8; Deuteronomy 1:18"> and you have warned them &#91;concerning&#93; the statutes and the laws, and have made known to them the way in which they go, and the work which they do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 6:3; Deuteronomy 1:13–17; 2 Chronicles 19:5–10"> And you provide out of all the people men of ability, fearing God, men of truth, hating dishonest gain, and have placed &#91;these&#93; over them &#91;for&#93; heads of thousands, heads of hundreds, heads of fifties, and heads of tens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Numbers 27:2; Numbers 15:33"> and they have judged the people at all times; and it has come to pass, they bring every great matter to you, and they judge every small matter themselves; and lighten it from off yourself, and they have borne with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:6–7; Exodus 18:18; 1 Samuel 8:22"> If you do this thing, and God has commanded you, then you have been able to stand, and all this people also goes to its place in peace.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra 10:2; Exodus 18:19; Exodus 18:2–5"> And Moses listens to the voice of his father-in-law, and does all that he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:15; Exodus 18:21; Acts 6:5"> and Moses chooses men of ability out of all Israel, and makes them chiefs over the people &#91;for&#93; heads of thousands, heads of hundreds, heads of fifties, and heads of tens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 18:22; Exodus 18:14–15; Deuteronomy 17:8"> and they have judged the people at all times; they bring the hard matter to Moses, and they judge every small matter themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 10:29–30; Judges 19:9; Genesis 31:55"> And Moses sends his father-in-law away, and he goes away to his own land.</span>
