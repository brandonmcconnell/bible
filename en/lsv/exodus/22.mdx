<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 19:8; 2 Samuel 12:6; Proverbs 6:31"> When a man steals an ox or sheep, and has slaughtered it or sold it, he repays &#91;with&#93; five of the herd for the ox, and four of the flock for the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 24:43; Matthew 6:19–20; Numbers 35:27"> If in the breaking through, the thief is found, and he has been struck and has died, there is no blood for him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 21:2; Isaiah 50:1; Matthew 18:25"> if the sun has risen on him, blood &#91;is&#93; for him, he certainly repays; if he has nothing, then he has been sold for his theft;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 22:7; Exodus 22:1; Exodus 22:9"> if the theft is certainly found alive in his hand, whether ox, or donkey, or sheep—he repays double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 22:3; Exodus 22:12; Job 20:18"> When a man depastures a field or vineyard, and has sent out his beast, and it has pastured in the field of another, he repays &#91;with&#93; the best of his field, and the best of his vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:9; Exodus 21:33–34; 2 Samuel 14:30–31"> When fire goes forth and has found thorns, and a stack, or the standing grain, or the field has been consumed, he who causes the burning certainly repays.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 22:4; Proverbs 6:30–31; Jeremiah 2:26"> When a man gives silver or vessels to his neighbor to keep, and it has been stolen out of the man’s house; if the thief is found, he repays double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Deuteronomy 16:18; Exodus 21:6"> If the thief is not found, then the master of the house has been brought near to God &#91;to see&#93; whether he has not put forth his hand against the work of his neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:1; Exodus 22:4; Numbers 5:6–7"> For every matter of transgression, for ox, for donkey, for sheep, for raiment, for any lost thing of which it is said that it is his, the matter of them both comes to God; he whom God condemns repays double to his neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:48; 2 Timothy 1:12; Luke 16:11"> When a man gives to his neighbor a donkey, or ox, or sheep, or any beast to keep, and it has died, or has been hurt, or taken captive, &#91;with&#93; none seeing—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:16; Leviticus 5:1; 1 Kings 2:42–43"> an oath of YHWH is between them both, that he has not put forth his hand against the work of his neighbor, and its owner has accepted, and he does not repay;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:39; Exodus 22:7"> but if it is certainly stolen from him, he repays to its owner;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nahum 2:12; Micah 5:8; Ezekiel 4:14"> if it is certainly torn, he brings it in &#91;as&#93; a witness; he does not repay the torn thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Exodus 21:34; Deuteronomy 15:2"> And when a man asks for &#91;anything&#93; from his neighbor, and it has been hurt or has died—its owner not being with it—he certainly repays;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 8:10"> if its owner &#91;is&#93; with it, he does not repay—if it &#91;is&#93; a hired thing, it has come for its hire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:28–29; Genesis 34:2–4"> And when a man entices a virgin who &#91;is&#93; not betrothed, and has lain with her, he certainly endows her to himself for a wife;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 34:12; 1 Samuel 18:25; Deuteronomy 22:29"> if her father utterly refuses to give her to him, he weighs out money according to the dowry of virgins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 20:27; Deuteronomy 18:10–11; 1 Samuel 28:3"> You do not keep a witch alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Deuteronomy 27:21; Leviticus 20:15–16"> Whoever lies with a beast is certainly put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–5; Numbers 25:7–8; Deuteronomy 18:20"> He who is sacrificing to a god, except to YHWH alone, is devoted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:9; Deuteronomy 10:19; Leviticus 19:33"> And you do not oppress a sojourner, nor crush him, for you have been sojourners in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:17; Isaiah 1:17; Isaiah 1:23"> You do not afflict any widow or orphan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Psalms 10:17–18; Luke 18:7"> if you really afflict him, surely if he cries to Me at all, I certainly hear his cry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 109:9; Jeremiah 15:8; Psalms 76:7"> and My anger has burned, and I have slain you by the sword, and your wives have been widows, and your sons orphans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 15:5; Leviticus 25:35–37; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> If you lend money &#91;to&#93; My poor people &#91;who are&#93; with you, you are not as a usurer to him; you do not lay usury on him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 20:16; Amos 2:8; Deuteronomy 24:6"> if you take the garment of your neighbor in pledge at all, you return it to him during the going in of the sun:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 34:6; 2 Chronicles 30:9; Psalms 136:10–11"> for it is his only covering, it &#91;is&#93; his garment for his skin; wherein does he lie down? And it has come to pass, when he cries to Me, that I have heard, for I &#91;am&#93; gracious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 23:5; Ecclesiastes 10:20; Exodus 22:8–9"> You do not revile God, and you do not curse a prince among your people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Exodus 13:2; James 1:18"> You do not delay your fullness and your liquids; you give the firstborn of your sons to Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 22:27; Deuteronomy 15:19; Exodus 13:12"> so you do to your ox &#91;and&#93; to your sheep; it is with its mother &#91;for&#93; seven days, on the eighth day you give it to Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:14; Leviticus 19:2; Deuteronomy 14:21"> And you are holy men to Me, and you do not eat flesh torn in the field, you cast it to a dog.</span>
