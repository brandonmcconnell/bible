<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses and to Aaron in the land of Egypt, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 13:4; Deuteronomy 16:1; Exodus 34:18"> “This month &#91;is&#93; the chief of months to you—it &#91;is&#93; the first of the months of the year to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 1:2; 1 Corinthians 5:7; John 1:29"> speak to all the congregation of Israel, saying, In the tenth of this month they take to themselves, each man, a lamb for the house of the fathers, a lamb for a house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And if the household is too few for a lamb, then he has taken, he and his neighbor who is near to his house, for the number of persons, each according to his eating you count for the lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; Hebrews 9:13–14; Malachi 1:14"> A lamb, a perfect one, a male, a son of a year, let &#91;it&#93; be to you; you take &#91;it&#93; from the sheep or from the goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 23:5; 2 Chronicles 30:15–18; Numbers 28:16"> And it has become a charge to you, until the fourteenth day of this month, and the whole assembly of the congregation of Israel has slaughtered it between the evenings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:28; Exodus 12:22–23; Hebrews 9:22"> and they have taken of the blood, and have put &#91;it&#93; on the two doorposts, and on the lintel over the houses in which they eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 34:25; Deuteronomy 16:7; Exodus 13:3"> And they have eaten the flesh in this night, a roast with fire; they eat it with unleavened things and bitters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 12:8; Deuteronomy 16:7; Lamentations 1:13"> you do not eat of it raw, or boiled in water at all, but a roast with fire, its head with its legs, and with its innards;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 23:18; Exodus 34:25; Exodus 29:34"> and you do not leave of it until morning, and that which is remaining of it until morning you burn with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 12:27; Ephesians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 5:7"> And thus you eat it: your loins girded, your sandals on your feet, and your staff in your hand, and you have eaten it in haste; it is YHWH’s Passover,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 33:4; Exodus 12:23; Psalms 82:6"> and I have passed over through the land of Egypt during this night, and have struck every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from man even to beast, and I do judgments on all the gods of Egypt; I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 11:28; Exodus 12:23; 1 John 1:7"> And the blood has become a sign for you on the houses where you &#91;are&#93;, and I have seen the blood, and have passed over you, and a plague is not on you for destruction in My striking in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 23:21; Exodus 12:24; Exodus 13:9–10"> And this day has become a memorial to you, and you have kept it &#91;for&#93; a celebration to YHWH throughout your generations—a continuous statute; you keep it &#91;for&#93; a celebration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 17:14; Deuteronomy 16:3; Exodus 23:15"> Seven days you eat unleavened things; only—in the first day you cause leaven to cease out of your houses; for anyone eating anything fermented from the first day until the seventh day, indeed, that person has been cut off from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 23:7–8; Numbers 28:18; Exodus 16:23"> And in the first day &#91;is&#93; a holy convocation, and in the seventh day you have a holy convocation; any work is not done in them, only that which is eaten by any person—it alone is done by you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 7:5; Exodus 13:3; Exodus 12:41"> and you have observed the Unleavened Things, for in this very day I have brought out your hosts from the land of Egypt, and you have observed this day throughout your generations—a continuous statute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 23:5–8; Exodus 12:15; Exodus 12:1–2"> In the first &#91;month&#93;, on the fourteenth day of the month, in the evening, you eat unleavened things until the twenty-first day of the month, at evening;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 12:15; Exodus 23:15; Deuteronomy 16:3"> seven days leaven is not found in your houses, for anyone eating anything fermented—that person has been cut off from the congregation of Israel, among the sojourners or among the natives of the land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> you do not eat anything fermented—in all your dwellings you eat &#91;only&#93; unleavened things.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 12:3; Luke 22:7–13; 2 Chronicles 35:5–6"> And Moses calls for all &#91;the&#93; elderly of Israel and says to them, “Draw out and take for yourselves &#91;from&#93; the flock, for your families, and slaughter the Passover-sacrifice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:28; Numbers 19:18; Psalms 51:7"> and you have taken a bunch of hyssop, and have dipped &#91;it&#93; in the blood which &#91;is&#93; in the basin, and have struck &#91;it&#93; on the lintel, and on the two doorposts, from the blood which &#91;is&#93; in the basin, and you, you do not go out—each from the opening of his house—until morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 7:3; Revelation 9:4; Hebrews 11:28"> And YHWH has passed on to strike the Egyptians, and has seen the blood on the lintel, and on the two doorposts, and YHWH has passed over the opening, and does not permit the destruction to come into your houses to strike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Genesis 17:8–10"> And you have observed this thing for a statute to you and to your sons for all time;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 3:17; Exodus 3:8; Deuteronomy 12:8–9"> and it has been, when you come to the land which YHWH gives to you as He has spoken, that you have kept this service;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 13:14–15; Deuteronomy 32:7; Psalms 145:4"> and it has come to pass, when your sons say to you, What &#91;is&#93; this service you have?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; Exodus 12:11; 1 Corinthians 5:7"> That you have said, It &#91;is&#93; a sacrifice of Passover to YHWH, who passed over the houses of the sons of Israel in Egypt, in His striking the Egyptians, and our houses He delivered.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 11:28"> And the people bow and pay respect, and the sons of Israel go and do as YHWH commanded Moses and Aaron; so they have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 78:51; Exodus 4:23; Psalms 105:36"> And it comes to pass, at midnight, that YHWH has struck every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from the firstborn of Pharaoh who is sitting on his throne, to the firstborn of the captive who &#91;is&#93; in the prison-house, and every firstborn of beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 11:6; Amos 5:17; Proverbs 21:13"> And Pharaoh rises by night, he and all his servants, and all the Egyptians, and there is a great cry in Egypt, for there is not a house where there is not &#91;one&#93; dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 6:1; Exodus 10:9; Exodus 3:19–20"> and he calls for Moses and for Aaron by night and says, “Rise, go out from the midst of my people, both you and the sons of Israel, and go, serve YHWH according to your word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 10:26; Genesis 27:34; Genesis 27:38"> take both your flock and your herd as you have spoken, and go; then you have also blessed me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 105:38; Exodus 11:1; Genesis 20:3"> And the Egyptians are urgent on the people, hastening to send them away out of the land, for they said, “We are all dead”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 8:3"> and the people take up their dough before it is fermented, their kneading-troughs &#91;are&#93; bound up in their garments on their shoulder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 3:21–22; Exodus 11:2–3; Genesis 15:14"> And the sons of Israel have done according to the word of Moses, and they ask for vessels of silver and vessels of gold, and garments, from the Egyptians;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 15:14; Acts 7:10; Exodus 3:21–22"> and YHWH has given the grace of the people in the eyes of the Egyptians, and they cause them to ask, and they spoil the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Exodus 38:26; Numbers 33:3"> And the sons of Israel journey from Rameses to Succoth, about six hundred thousand men on foot, apart from infants;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 11:4; Zechariah 8:23"> and a great rabble has also gone up with them, and flock and herd—very much livestock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 11:1; Exodus 6:1; Exodus 12:31–33"> And they bake unleavened cakes with the dough which they have brought out from Egypt, for it has not fermented; for they have been cast out of Egypt, and have not been able to delay, and also they have not made provision for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 15:13; Acts 7:6; Acts 13:17"> And the dwelling of the sons of Israel &#91;in&#93; which they have dwelt in Egypt &#91;is&#93; four hundred and thirty years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; John 7:8; Acts 1:7"> and it comes to pass, at the end of four hundred and thirty years—indeed, it comes to pass on this very same day—all the hosts of YHWH have gone out from the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–6; Exodus 12:14; Exodus 13:10"> It &#91;is&#93; a night of watchings to YHWH, to bring them out from the land of Egypt; it &#91;is&#93; this night of watchings to YHWH to all the sons of Israel throughout their generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Exodus 12:48; Numbers 9:14; Exodus 12:11"> And YHWH says to Moses and Aaron, “This &#91;is&#93; a statute of the Passover; any son of a stranger does not eat of it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 17:12–13; Genesis 17:23"> and any man’s servant, the purchase of money, when you have circumcised him—then he eats of it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 22:10; Ephesians 2:12"> a settler or hired servant does not eat of it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 9:12; John 19:36; John 19:33"> it is eaten in one house, you do not carry out of the house &#91;any&#93; of the flesh outside, and you do not break a bone of it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Exodus 12:6; Numbers 9:13; Exodus 12:3"> all the congregation of Israel keeps it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 9:14; Colossians 3:11; Galatians 3:28"> And when a sojourner sojourns with you, and has made a Passover to YHWH, every male of his &#91;is&#93; to be circumcised, and then he comes near to keep it, and he has been as a native of the land, but any uncircumcised one does not eat of it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 15:15–16; Numbers 15:29; Leviticus 24:22"> one law is to a native, and to a sojourner who is sojourning in your midst.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 7:24–25; Exodus 6:26; Exodus 7:4"> And all the sons of Israel do as YHWH commanded Moses and Aaron; so they have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Exodus 12:41; Exodus 6:26"> And it comes to pass on this very same day, YHWH has brought out the sons of Israel from the land of Egypt by their hosts.</span>
