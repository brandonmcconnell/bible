<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 8:1; Exodus 10:3; Exodus 8:20"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Go to Pharaoh, and you have spoken to him, Thus said YHWH, God of the Hebrews: Send My people away, and they serve Me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 8:2; Exodus 4:23; Leviticus 26:27–28"> for if you are refusing to send &#91;them&#93; away, and are still keeping hold on them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 7:4; Acts 13:11; 1 Samuel 6:9"> behold, the hand of YHWH is on your livestock which &#91;are&#93; in the field, on horses, on donkeys, on camels, on herd, and on flock—a very grievous pestilence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 8:22; Exodus 10:23; Malachi 3:18"> And YHWH has separated between the livestock of Israel and the livestock of Egypt, and there does not die a thing of all the sons of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 8:23; Exodus 10:4; Jeremiah 28:16–17"> and YHWH sets an appointed time, saying, Tomorrow YHWH does this thing in the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 78:48; Exodus 9:25; Psalms 78:50"> And YHWH does this thing on the next day, and all the livestock of Egypt die, and of the livestock of the sons of Israel not one has died;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 7:14; Exodus 8:32; Isaiah 48:4"> and Pharaoh sends, and behold, not even one of the livestock of Israel has died, and the heart of Pharaoh is hard, and he has not sent the people away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 8:16"> And YHWH says to Moses and to Aaron, “Take for yourselves the fullness of your handfuls—soot of a furnace, and Moses has sprinkled it toward the heavens, before the eyes of Pharaoh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 16:2; Deuteronomy 28:27; Job 2:7"> and it has become small dust over all the land of Egypt, and it has become on man and on livestock a boil breaking forth &#91;with&#93; blisters in all the land of Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:27"> And they take the soot of the furnace, and stand before Pharaoh, and Moses sprinkles it toward the heavens, and it is a boil &#91;with&#93; blisters breaking forth on man and on beast;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 7:11–12; Exodus 8:18–19; Revelation 16:2"> and the enchanters have not been able to stand before Moses because of the boil, for the boil has been on the enchanters, and on all the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 7:13–14; Psalms 81:11–12"> And YHWH strengthens the heart of Pharaoh, and he has not listened to them, as YHWH has spoken to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 8:20; Exodus 7:15; Exodus 9:1"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Rise early in the morning, and station yourself before Pharaoh, and you have said to him, Thus said YHWH, God of the Hebrews: Send My people away, and they serve Me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 8:10; Leviticus 26:28; 1 Chronicles 17:20"> for at this time I am sending all My plagues to your heart, and on your servants, and on your people, so that you know that there is none like Me in all the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 3:20; Exodus 9:3; Exodus 9:16"> for now I have put forth My hand, and I strike you, and your people, with pestilence, and you are hidden from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 9:17; Proverbs 16:4; Psalms 83:17–18"> And yet for this I have caused you to stand, so as to show you My power, and for the sake of declaring My Name in all the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Isaiah 10:15; Isaiah 37:23–24"> still you are exalting yourself against My people—so as not to send them away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 20:6; Psalms 83:15; Exodus 9:22–25"> behold, I am raining very grievous hail about &#91;this&#93; time tomorrow, such as has not been in Egypt from the day of its founding and until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:2"> And now, send, strengthen your livestock and all that you have in the field; every man and beast which is found in the field, and is not gathered into the house—the hail has come down on them, and they have died.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 13:13; Proverbs 22:3; Hebrews 11:7"> He who is fearing the word of YHWH among the servants of Pharaoh has caused his servants and his livestock to flee to the houses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:4; Job 34:14; 1 Chronicles 22:19"> and he who has not set his heart to the word of YHWH leaves his servants and his livestock in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 16:21; Exodus 7:19; Exodus 8:5"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Stretch forth your hand toward the heavens, and there is hail in all the land of Egypt, on man, and on beast, and on every herb of the field in the land of Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 18:13; Joshua 10:11; Isaiah 30:30"> And Moses stretches out his rod toward the heavens, and YHWH has given voices and hail, and fire goes toward the earth, and YHWH rains hail on the land of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 9:23; Matthew 24:21; Exodus 10:6"> and there is hail, and fire catching itself in the midst of the hail, very grievous, such as has not been in all the land of Egypt since it has become a nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 78:47; Psalms 105:32–33"> And the hail strikes all that &#91;is&#93; in the field in all the land of Egypt, from man even to beast, and the hail has struck every herb of the field, and it has broken every tree of the field;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 11:7; Exodus 9:4; Exodus 10:23"> only in the land of Goshen, where the sons of Israel &#91;are&#93;, there has been no hail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Lamentations 1:18; Psalms 129:4; Exodus 10:16"> And Pharaoh sends, and calls for Moses and for Aaron, and says to them, “I have sinned this time—YHWH &#91;is&#93; the righteous, and I and my people &#91;are&#93; the wicked,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Exodus 10:17; Exodus 8:28"> make supplication to YHWH, and plead that there be no voices of God and hail, and I send you away, and you do not add to remain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:26; 1 Kings 8:38; Isaiah 1:15"> And Moses says to him, “At my going out of the city, I spread my palms to YHWH—the voices cease, and there is no more hail, so that you know that the earth &#91;is&#93; YHWH’s;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; Proverbs 16:6; Isaiah 63:17"> but you and your servants—I have known that you are not yet afraid of the face of YHWH God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ruth 1:22; Ruth 2:23; Habakkuk 3:17"> And the flax and the barley have been struck, for the barley &#91;is&#93; budding, and the flax forming flowers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 10:22"> and the wheat and the rye have not been struck, for they are late.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 9:29; Exodus 8:12; James 5:17–18"> And Moses goes out from Pharaoh, &#91;from&#93; the city, and spreads his hands to YHWH, and the voices and the hail cease, and rain has not been poured out to the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 7:14; 2 Chronicles 36:13; Exodus 4:21"> and Pharaoh sees that the rain has ceased, and the hail and the voices, and he continues to sin, and hardens his heart, he and his servants;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 4:21"> and the heart of Pharaoh is strong, and he has not sent the sons of Israel away, as YHWH has spoken by the hand of Moses.</span>
