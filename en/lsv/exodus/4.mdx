<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Exodus 2:14; Jeremiah 1:6"> And Moses answers and says, “And if they do not give credence to me, nor listen to my voice, and say, YHWH has not appeared to you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 4:17; Exodus 4:20; Isaiah 11:4"> And YHWH says to him, “What &#91;is&#93; this in your hand?” And he says, “A rod”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 7:10–15; Amos 5:19; Exodus 4:17"> and He says, “Cast it to the earth”; and he casts it to the earth, and it becomes a serpent—and Moses flees from its presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 28:3–6; Genesis 22:1–2; Psalms 91:13"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Put forth your hand, and lay hold on the tail of it”; and he puts forth his hand, and lays hold on it, and it becomes a rod in his hand—</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Genesis 48:3; Exodus 3:15"> “so that they believe that YHWH, God of their fathers, has appeared to you, God of Abraham, God of Isaac, and God of Jacob.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 5:27; Numbers 12:10"> And YHWH says to him again, “Now put your hand into your bosom”; and he puts his hand into his bosom, and he brings it out, and behold, his hand &#91;is&#93; leprous as snow;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 5:14; Deuteronomy 32:39; Matthew 8:3"> and He says, “Put your hand back into your bosom”; and he puts his hand back into his bosom, and he brings it out from his bosom, and behold, it has turned back as his flesh—</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 28:10; Deuteronomy 32:39; Exodus 4:30–31"> “and it has come to pass, if they do not give credence to you, and do not listen to the voice of the first sign, that they have given credence to the voice of the latter sign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 7:19–25; Exodus 1:22; Matthew 7:2"> And it has come to pass, if they do not give credence even to these two signs, nor listen to your voice, that you have taken of the waters of the River, and have poured &#91;it&#93; on the dry land, and the waters which you take from the River have been, indeed, they have become blood on the dry land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:6; Exodus 6:12; Acts 7:22"> And Moses says to YHWH, “O my Lord, I &#91;am&#93; not a man of words, either yesterday, or before, or since Your speaking to Your servant, for I &#91;am&#93; slow of mouth, and slow of tongue.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 3:6; Psalms 146:8; Ezekiel 3:26–27"> And YHWH says to him, “Who appointed a mouth for man? Or who appoints the mute, or deaf, or open, or blind? Is it not I, YHWH?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–20"> And now, go, and I am with your mouth, and have directed you that which you speak”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 1:3; Exodus 4:1; Matthew 13:41"> and he says, “O my Lord, please send by the hand &#91;of another that&#93; You send.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 14:13–15; Exodus 4:27; 1 Chronicles 21:7"> And the anger of YHWH burns against Moses, and He says, “Is Aaron the Levite not your brother? I have known that he speaks well, and also, behold, he is coming out to meet you; when he has seen you, then he has rejoiced in his heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 51:16; Deuteronomy 18:18; Luke 21:15"> and you have spoken to him, and have set the words in his mouth, and I am with your mouth, and with his mouth, and have directed you that which you do;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 7:1–2; Psalms 82:6; John 10:34–35"> and he, he has spoken to the people for you, and it has come to pass, he is to you for a mouth, and you are to him for God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 4:2; 1 Corinthians 1:27; Exodus 7:9–20"> and you take this rod in your hand, with which you do the signs.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; 1 Samuel 1:17; 1 Timothy 6:1"> And Moses goes and turns back to his father-in-law Jethro, and says to him, “Please let me go, and I return to my brothers who &#91;are&#93; in Egypt, and I see whether they are yet alive.” And Jethro says to Moses, “Go in peace.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 2:20; Exodus 2:23; Exodus 2:15"> And YHWH says to Moses in Midian, “Go, return to Egypt, for all the men who seek your life have died”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 17:9; Numbers 20:8–9; Exodus 18:3–4"> and Moses takes his wife, and his sons, and causes them to ride on the donkey, and turns back to the land of Egypt, and Moses takes the rod of God in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Isaiah 63:17; Exodus 14:8"> And YHWH says to Moses, “In your going to return to Egypt, see—all the wonders which I have put in your hand—that you have done them before Pharaoh, and I strengthen his heart, and he does not send the people away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 11:1; Romans 9:4; Jeremiah 31:9"> and you have said to Pharaoh, Thus said YHWH: My son, My firstborn &#91;is&#93; Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 11:5; Exodus 12:29; Psalms 135:8"> and I say to you, send My son away, and he serves Me; and &#91;if&#93; you refuse to send him away, behold, I am slaying your son, your firstborn.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 17:14; Numbers 22:22–23; Hosea 13:8"> And it comes to pass in the way, in a lodging place, that YHWH meets him, and seeks to put him to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 5:2–3; Genesis 17:14; 2 Samuel 16:7"> and Zipporah takes a flint, and cuts off the foreskin of her son, and causes &#91;it&#93; to touch his feet, and says, “You &#91;are&#93; surely a bridegroom of blood to me”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and He desists from him. Then she said, “A bridegroom of blood,” in reference to the circumcision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Exodus 4:14–16; 1 Kings 19:8"> And YHWH says to Aaron, “Go into the wilderness to meet Moses”; and he goes, and meets him on the mountain of God, and kisses him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 4:8–9; Exodus 4:15–16; Matthew 21:29"> and Moses declares to Aaron all the words of YHWH with which He has sent him, and all the signs with which He has charged him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 3:16; Exodus 24:11; Exodus 24:1"> And Moses goes—Aaron also—and they gather all the elderly of the sons of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 4:16"> and Aaron speaks all the words which YHWH has spoken to Moses, and does the signs before the eyes of the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Exodus 2:25; Psalms 106:12–13"> and the people believe when they hear that YHWH has looked after the sons of Israel, and that He has seen their affliction; and they bow and pay respect.</span>
