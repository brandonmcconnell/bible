<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; 1 Chronicles 23:12–14; Exodus 6:16–20"> And there goes a man of the house of Levi, and he takes the daughter of Levi,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:23; Acts 7:20; Psalms 112:5"> and the woman conceives, and bears a son, and she sees him, that he &#91;is&#93; beautiful, and she hides him &#91;for&#93; three months,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Acts 7:19; Genesis 6:14"> and she has not been able to hide him anymore, and she takes an ark of rushes for him, and covers it with bitumen and with pitch, and puts the boy in it, and puts &#91;it&#93; in the weeds by the edge of the River;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; Exodus 15:20; Micah 6:4"> and his sister stations herself far off, to know what is done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 7:21; Psalms 12:5; Psalms 9:9"> And a daughter of Pharaoh comes down to bathe at the River, and her girls are walking by the side of the River, and she sees the ark in the midst of the weeds, and sends her handmaid, and she takes it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 8:50; 1 Peter 3:8; Acts 7:21"> and opens, and sees him—the boy, and behold, a child weeping! And she has pity on him and says, “This is &#91;one&#93; of the Hebrews’ children.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 2:4; Numbers 26:59; Numbers 12:1"> And his sister says to the daughter of Pharaoh, “Do I go? When I have called a suckling woman of the Hebrews for you, then she suckles the boy for you”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3–4; Psalms 27:10; Exodus 6:20"> and the daughter of Pharaoh says to her, “Go”; and the virgin goes, and calls the mother of the boy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and the daughter of Pharaoh says to her, “Take this boy away, and suckle him for me, and I give your hire”; and the woman takes the boy, and suckles him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24; Genesis 16:11; 1 Samuel 1:20"> And the boy grows, and she brings him to the daughter of Pharaoh, and he is to her for a son, and she calls his name Moses, and says, “Because I have drawn him from the water.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24–26; Acts 7:22–24; Exodus 1:11"> And it comes to pass, in those days, that Moses is grown, and he goes out to his brothers, and looks on their burdens, and sees a man, an Egyptian, striking a man, a Hebrew, &#91;one&#93; of his brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 7:24–26"> and he turns here and there, and sees that there is no man, and strikes the Egyptian, and hides him in the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:26–28; 1 Corinthians 6:7–8"> And he goes out on the second day, and behold, two men, Hebrews, are striving, and he says to the wrongdoer, “Why do you strike your neighbor?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 12:14; Matthew 21:23; Genesis 19:9"> And he says, “Who set you for a head and judge over us? Are you saying &#91;it&#93; to slay me as you have slain the Egyptian?” And Moses fears and says, “Surely the thing has been known.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 7:29; Hebrews 11:27; Genesis 24:11"> And Pharaoh hears of this thing, and seeks to slay Moses, and Moses flees from the face of Pharaoh, and dwells in the land of Midian, and dwells by the well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 24:11; Exodus 3:1; 1 Samuel 9:11"> And to a priest of Midian &#91;are&#93; seven daughters, and they come and draw, and fill the troughs to water the flock of their father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 29:10; Genesis 21:25; Genesis 26:15–22"> and the shepherds come and drive them away, and Moses arises, and saves them, and waters their flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Numbers 10:29; Exodus 4:18"> And they come to their father Reuel, and he says, “Why have you hurried to come in today?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:11; Genesis 29:10"> And they say, “A man, an Egyptian, has delivered us out of the hand of the shepherds, and has also diligently drawn for us, and waters the flock”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Genesis 43:25; Hebrews 13:2"> and he says to his daughters, “And where &#91;is&#93; he? Why &#91;is&#93; this &#91;that&#93; you left the man? Call for him, and he eats bread.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 31:38–40; Hebrews 13:5; Hebrews 11:25"> And Moses is willing to dwell with the man, and he gives his daughter Zipporah to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13–14; Acts 7:29; Exodus 22:21"> and she bears a son, and he calls his name Gershom, for he said, “I have been a sojourner in a strange land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 18:20–21; James 5:4; Exodus 3:7–9"> And it comes to pass during these many days, that the king of Egypt dies, and the sons of Israel sigh because of the service, and cry, and their cry goes up to God, because of the service;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 26:3; Psalms 105:42; Exodus 6:5"> and God hears their groaning, and God remembers His covenant with Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; Psalms 55:22; Exodus 3:7–8"> and God sees the sons of Israel, and God knows.</span>
