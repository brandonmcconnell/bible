<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–20; Exodus 26:33; Numbers 10:33–36"> And Bezaleel makes the Ark of shittim wood; two cubits and a half its length, and a cubit and a half its breadth, and a cubit and a half its height;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 30:3"> and he overlays it with pure gold inside and outside, and makes a wreath of gold for it all around;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and he casts four rings of gold for it on its four feet, even two rings on its one side, and two rings on its second side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Peter 1:7; Numbers 4:14–15"> and he makes poles of shittim wood, and overlays them with gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; 2 Samuel 6:3–7; Numbers 1:50"> and he brings in the poles into the rings, by the sides of the Ark, to carry the Ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Titus 2:14; Romans 3:25"> And he makes a propitiatory covering of pure gold, two cubits and a half its length, and a cubit and a half its breadth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:2; Psalms 104:4; Psalms 80:1"> and he makes two cherubim of gold; he has made them of beaten work at the two ends of the propitiatory covering—</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> one cherub at the end on this &#91;side&#93; and one cherub at the end on that &#91;side&#93;; he has made the cherubim from &#91;above&#93; the propitiatory covering, at its two ends;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 1:51; Hebrews 1:14; Genesis 28:12"> and the cherubim are spreading out wings on high, covering over the propitiatory covering with their wings, and their faces &#91;are&#93; toward one another; the faces of the cherubim have been toward the propitiatory covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:14; Ezekiel 40:39–42; Malachi 1:12"> And he makes the table of shittim wood; two cubits its length, and a cubit its breadth, and a cubit and a half its height,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and overlays it with pure gold, and makes a wreath of gold for it all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he makes a border for it of a handbreadth around, and makes a wreath of gold around for its border;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and he casts four rings of gold for it, and puts the rings on the four corners which &#91;are&#93; at its four feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> the rings have been close by the border, places for poles to carry the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he makes the poles of shittim wood, and overlays them with gold, to carry the table;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:29; 1 Kings 7:50; 2 Kings 12:13"> and he makes the vessels which &#91;are&#93; on the table, its dishes, and its bowls, and its cups, and the cups by which they pour out, of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Hebrews 9:2; 2 Chronicles 13:11"> And he makes the lampstand of pure gold; he has made the lampstand of beaten work, its base, and its branch, its calyxes, its knobs, and its flowers, have been of the same;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and six branches are coming out of its sides, three branches of the lampstand out of its one side, and three branches of the lampstand out of its second side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> three calyxes, made like almonds, in one branch, a knob and a flower; and three calyxes, made like almonds, in another branch, a knob and a flower; so for the six branches which are coming out of the lampstand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5; Jeremiah 1:11; Exodus 25:33"> And in the lampstand &#91;are&#93; four calyxes, made like almonds, its knobs, and its flowers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:35"> and a knob under the two branches of the same, and a knob under the two branches of the same, and a knob under the two branches of the same, &#91;are&#93; for the six branches which are coming out of it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Exodus 25:31; Psalms 51:17"> their knobs and their branches have been of the same; all of it one beaten work of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 1:12; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 1:20–1"> And he makes its seven lamps, and its snuffers, and its snuff-dishes, of pure gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> he has made it of a talent of pure gold, and with all its vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:1–5; Hebrews 7:25; Revelation 8:3–4"> And he makes the incense-altar of shittim wood; a cubit its length, and a cubit its breadth (square), and two cubits its height; its horns have been of the same;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and he overlays it with pure gold, around its top and its sides, and its horns; and he makes a wreath of gold for it all around;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and he has made two rings of gold for it under its wreath, at its two corners, at its two sides, for places for poles to carry it with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he makes the poles of shittim wood, and overlays them with gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Hebrews 7:25; 1 John 2:20"> and he makes the holy anointing oil, and the pure spice-incense—work of a compounder.</span>
