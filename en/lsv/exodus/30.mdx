<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 8:3; Exodus 37:25–28; Exodus 40:5"> “And you have made an altar &#91;for&#93; making incense; you make it of shittim wood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 27:2"> a cubit its length and a cubit its breadth (it is square), and two cubits its height; its horns &#91;are&#93; of the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; Exodus 25:24"> And you have overlaid it with pure gold—its top, and around its sides, and its horns; and you have made a crown of gold for it all around;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:27; Exodus 26:29; Exodus 27:7"> and you make two rings of gold for it, under its crown on its two ribs; you make &#91;them&#93; on its two sides, and they have become places for poles to carry it with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:13; Exodus 25:27"> And you have made the poles of shittim wood, and have overlaid them with gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:21–22; Exodus 30:36; Leviticus 16:13"> and you have put it before the veil, which &#91;is&#93; by the Ark of the Testimony, before the propitiatory covering which &#91;is&#93; over the Testimony, where I meet with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:28; 1 Chronicles 23:13; Exodus 27:20–21"> And Aaron has made incense on it, incense of spices, morning by morning; in his making the lamps right, he makes incense &#91;on&#93; it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; Hebrews 7:25"> and in Aaron’s causing the lamps to go up between the evenings, he makes incense &#91;on&#93; it; &#91;it is&#93; a continual incense before YHWH throughout your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1"> You do not cause strange incense to go up on it—and burnt-offering and present; and you do not pour out a drink-offering on it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:18; Hebrews 9:22–23; Leviticus 16:29–30"> and Aaron has made atonement on its horns once in a year, by the blood of the sin-offering of atonements; once in a year he makes atonement for it, throughout your generations; it &#91;is&#93; most holy to YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; 1 Chronicles 27:24; Exodus 38:25–26"> “When you take up the census of the sons of Israel for their numbers, then they have each given an atonement &#91;for&#93; his soul to YHWH in their being numbered, and there is no plague among them in their being numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:25; Numbers 3:47; Ezekiel 45:12"> This they give, everyone passing over to those numbered, half a shekel, by the shekel of the holy place (the shekel &#91;is&#93; twenty gerahs); half a shekel &#91;is&#93; the raised-offering to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:20; Numbers 1:18; Numbers 1:3"> everyone passing over to those numbered, from a son of twenty years and upwards, gives the raised-offering of YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; Leviticus 17:11; Exodus 30:12"> the rich do not multiply, and the poor do not diminish from the half-shekel, to give the raised-offering of YHWH, to make atonement for your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:32–33; Numbers 16:40; Exodus 38:25–31"> And you have taken the atonement-money from the sons of Israel, and have given it for the service of the Tent of Meeting; and it has been to the sons of Israel for a memorial before YHWH, to make atonement for your souls.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 40:7; Exodus 38:8; 1 Kings 7:23"> “And you have made a laver of bronze (and its base of bronze), for washing; and you have put it between the Tent of Meeting and the altar, and have put water &#91;in&#93; there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Exodus 40:31–32; Psalms 26:6"> and Aaron and his sons have washed their hands and their feet from it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–3; Hebrews 12:28–29; 1 Chronicles 13:10"> they wash &#91;with&#93; water in their going into the Tent of Meeting, and do not die; or in their drawing near to the altar to minister, to make incense &#91;as&#93; a fire-offering to YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 28:43; Exodus 27:21"> then they have washed their hands and their feet, and they do not die, and it has been a continuous statute to them, to him and to his seed, throughout their generations.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:19; Song of Songs 4:14; Ezekiel 27:22"> “And you, take &#91;these&#93; principal spices for yourself: five hundred &#91;shekels&#93; of liquid myrrh, and the half of that—two hundred and fifty &#91;shekels&#93;—of spice-cinnamon, and two hundred and fifty &#91;shekels&#93; of spice-cane,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 45:8; Exodus 29:40; Numbers 3:47"> and five hundred &#91;shekels&#93; of cassia, by the shekel of the holy place, and a hin of olive oil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; 1 Chronicles 9:30; Exodus 40:9"> and you have made it a holy anointing oil, a compound mixture, work of a compounder; it is a holy anointing oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 7:1; 1 John 2:20; Isaiah 61:1"> And with it you have anointed the Tent of Meeting, and the Ark of the Testimony,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and the table and all its vessels, and the lampstand and its vessels, and the altar of incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and the altar of burnt-offering and all its vessels, and the laver and its base;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Exodus 29:37; Leviticus 6:18"> and you have sanctified them, and they have been most holy; all that is coming against them is holy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 8:12; Exodus 28:3; Leviticus 8:30"> and you anoint Aaron and his sons, and have sanctified them for being priests to Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Leviticus 21:10; Psalms 89:20"> And you speak to the sons of Israel, saying, This is a holy anointing oil to Me, throughout your generations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 30:25; Exodus 30:37–38; Leviticus 21:10"> it is not poured on &#91;the&#93; flesh of man, and you make nothing &#91;else&#93; like it in its proportion; it &#91;is&#93; holy—it is holy to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 30:38; Genesis 17:14; Exodus 12:15"> a man who compounds &#91;any&#93; like it, or who puts of it on a stranger, has even been cut off from his people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Exodus 25:6; Exodus 30:23"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Take to yourself spices—stacte, and onycha, and galbanum—spices and pure frankincense; they are part for part;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 2:13; Exodus 30:25; John 12:3"> and you have made it an incense, a compound, work of a compounder, salted, pure, holy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Exodus 30:6; Exodus 16:34"> and you have beaten &#91;some&#93; of it small, and have put of it before the Testimony in the Tent of Meeting, to where I meet with you; it is most holy to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:32–33"> As for the incense which you make, you do not make &#91;any&#93; for yourselves in its proportion; it is holy to you for YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:33"> a man who makes &#91;any&#93; like it—to be refreshed by it—has even been cut off from his people.”</span>
