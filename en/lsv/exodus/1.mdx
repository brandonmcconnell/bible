<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Dan, and Naphtali, Gad, and Asher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Genesis 46:26–27; Judges 8:30"> And all the persons coming out of the thigh of Jacob are seventy persons; as for Joseph, he was in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 50:26; Genesis 50:24; Acts 7:14–16"> And Joseph dies, and all his brothers, and all that generation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 46:3; Genesis 1:28; Deuteronomy 26:5"> and the sons of Israel have been fruitful, and they teem, and multiply, and are very, very mighty, and the land is filled with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 7:18; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Ecclesiastes 9:15"> And there rises a new king over Egypt, who has not known Joseph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 105:24–25; Ecclesiastes 4:4; James 3:14–16"> and he says to his people, “Behold, the people of the sons of Israel &#91;are&#93; more numerous and mighty than we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Psalms 83:3–4; Proverbs 1:11"> Give help! Let us act wisely concerning it, lest it multiply, and it has come to pass, when war happens, that it has been joined, even it, to those hating us, and has fought against us, and has gone up out of the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 15:13; Genesis 47:11; Exodus 3:7"> And they set princes of tribute over it, so as to afflict it with their burdens, and it builds the store-cities of Pithom and Rameses for Pharaoh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 105:24; Exodus 1:9; Romans 8:28"> and as they afflict it, so it multiplies, and so it breaks forth, and they are distressed because of the sons of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:20"> and the Egyptians cause the sons of Israel to serve with rigor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Numbers 20:15; Exodus 2:23"> and make their lives bitter in hard service, in clay, and in brick, and in every &#91;kind&#93; of service in the field; all their service in which they have served &#91;is&#93; with rigor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the king of Egypt speaks to the midwives, the Hebrewesses (of whom the name of the first &#91;is&#93; Shiphrah, and the name of the second Puah),</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Matthew 21:38; Revelation 12:4"> and says, “When you cause the Hebrew women to bear, and have looked on the children, if it &#91;is&#93; a son, then you have put him to death; and if it &#91;is&#93; a daughter, then she has lived.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Exodus 1:21; Matthew 10:28"> And the midwives fear God, and have not done as the king of Egypt has spoken to them, and they keep the boys alive;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:4; 2 Samuel 13:28"> and the king of Egypt calls for the midwives and says to them, “Why have you done this thing, and keep the boys alive?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 2:4–24; 2 Samuel 17:19–20; 1 Samuel 21:2"> And the midwives say to Pharaoh, “Because the Hebrew women &#91;are&#93; not as the Egyptian women, for they &#91;are&#93; lively; before the midwife comes to them—they have borne!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10; Ecclesiastes 8:12; Proverbs 11:18"> And God does good to the midwives, and the people multiply, and are very mighty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 11:38; 1 Samuel 2:35; Psalms 37:3"> and it comes to pass, because the midwives have feared God, that He makes households for them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Proverbs 1:16; Exodus 1:16"> and Pharaoh lays a charge on all his people, saying, “Every son who is born—you cast him into the River, and every daughter you keep alive.”</span>
