<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Nehemiah 11:2; Ezra 2:68"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, and they take a raised-offering for Me; you take My raised-offering from every man whose heart impels him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:9; Job 28:2"> And this &#91;is&#93; the raised-offering which you take from them: gold, and silver, and bronze,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Genesis 41:42; Ezekiel 16:10"> and blue, and purple, and scarlet, and linen, and goats’ &#91;hair&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 26:37–1; Exodus 26:14–15; Exodus 36:20"> and rams’ skins made red, and tachashim skins, and shittim wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:37; Exodus 40:24–25"> oil for the light, spices for the anointing oil, and for the incense of the spices,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Exodus 28:6–30"> shoham stones, and stones for setting for an ephod, and for a breastplate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Kings 6:13; Revelation 21:3"> And they have made a sanctuary for Me, and I have dwelt in their midst;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Hebrews 8:5; Acts 7:44"> according to all that which I am showing you, the pattern of the Dwelling Place, and the pattern of all its vessels, even so you make &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Exodus 37:1–3; Deuteronomy 10:1–3"> And they have made an ark of shittim wood; two cubits and a half its length, and a cubit and a half its breadth, and a cubit and a half its height;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 25:24; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:4"> and you have overlaid it &#91;with&#93; pure gold, you overlay it inside and outside, and you have made a ring of gold on it all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 27:7; Exodus 25:26; Exodus 25:15"> And you have cast four rings of gold for it, and have put &#91;them&#93; on its four feet, even two rings on its one side, and two rings on its second side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:15; Numbers 4:11; Exodus 27:6"> and you have made poles of shittim wood, and have overlaid them &#91;with&#93; gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and have brought the poles into the rings on the sides of the Ark, to carry the Ark by them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:8; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> the poles are in the rings of the Ark, they are not turned aside from it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:26; 1 Kings 8:9; Hebrews 9:4"> and you have put the Testimony which I give to you into the Ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 37:6; Hebrews 4:16"> And you have made a propitiatory covering of pure gold, two cubits and a half its length, and a cubit and a half its breadth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:20; Hebrews 9:5; 1 Kings 8:6–7"> and you have made two cherubim of gold; you make them beaten work at the two ends of the propitiatory covering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and make one cherub at the end on this &#91;side&#93; and one cherub at the end on that &#91;side&#93;; at the propitiatory covering you make the cherubim on its two ends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:18; 1 Kings 8:7; Hebrews 9:5"> And the cherubim have been spreading out wings on high, covering over the propitiatory covering with their wings, and their faces &#91;are&#93; toward one another—the faces of the cherubim are toward the propitiatory covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:34; Romans 10:4; Exodus 25:16–17"> And you have put the propitiatory covering on the Ark above, and you put the Testimony which I give to you into the Ark;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:42–43; Numbers 7:89; Isaiah 37:16"> and I have met with you there, and have spoken with you from off the propitiatory covering (from between the two cherubim, which &#91;are&#93; on the Ark of the Testimony) all that which I command you concerning the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 9:2; Exodus 37:10–16; 1 Kings 7:48"> And you have made a table of shittim wood, two cubits its length, and a cubit its breadth, and a cubit and a half its height,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; 1 Kings 6:20–22"> and have overlaid it &#91;with&#93; pure gold, and have made a crown of gold for it all around,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:2; Exodus 30:3"> and have made a border of a handbreadth for it all around, and have made a crown of gold for its border all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And you have made four rings of gold for it, and have put the rings on the four corners, which &#91;are&#93; at its four feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Exodus 25:28"> close by the border are the rings for places for poles to carry the table;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Acts 9:15; Numbers 10:17"> and you have made the poles of shittim wood, and have overlaid them with gold, and the table has been carried with them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 4:7; Exodus 37:16; Revelation 3:20"> and you have made its dishes, and its bowls, and its covers, and its cups, with which they pour out; you make them of pure gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–9; Matthew 12:4; Exodus 39:36"> and you have put Bread of the Presentation on the table before Me continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 7:49; Zechariah 4:2; Exodus 37:17–24"> And you have made a lampstand of pure gold; the lampstand is made of beaten work; its base, and its branch, its calyxes, its knobs, and its flowers are of the same;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> and six branches are coming out of its sides, three branches of the lampstand out of the first side, and three branches of the lampstand out of the second side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 17:4–8; Jeremiah 1:11–12; Zechariah 4:3"> three calyxes made like almonds in one branch, a knob and a flower, and three calyxes made like almonds in one branch, a knob and a flower—so for the six branches which are coming out from the lampstand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And in the lampstand &#91;are&#93; four calyxes made like almonds, its knobs and its flowers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and a knob under two branches of the same, and a knob under two branches of the same, and a knob under two branches of the same, for the six branches which are coming out of the lampstand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:18; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 9:15"> their knobs and their branches are of the same, all of it one beaten work of pure gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 8:2; Revelation 1:20–1; Leviticus 24:2–4"> and you have made its seven lamps, and &#91;one&#93; has caused its lamps to go up, and it has given light over &#91;and&#93; beyond its front.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Kings 12:13; Jeremiah 52:18; 2 Kings 25:14"> And its snuffers and its snuff dishes &#91;are&#93; of pure gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> he makes it of a talent of pure gold, with all these vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Exodus 26:30; Acts 7:44"> Now see and make &#91;them&#93; by their pattern which you are shown on the mountain.”</span>
