<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:1; Exodus 13:1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 33:7–8; Jeremiah 44:1; Exodus 13:17–18"> “Speak to the sons of Israel, and they turn back and encamp before Pi-Hahiroth, between Migdol and the sea, before Ba‘al-Zephon; you encamp in front of it by the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 4:28; 1 Samuel 23:23; Psalms 71:11"> and Pharaoh has said of the sons of Israel, They are entangled in the land, the wilderness has shut on them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:22–23; Exodus 7:5; Romans 9:17"> and I have strengthened the heart of Pharaoh, and he has pursued after them, and I am honored by Pharaoh, and by all his force, and the Egyptians have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH”; and they do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 105:25; Exodus 12:33; Luke 11:24–26"> And it is declared to the king of Egypt that the people have fled, and the heart of Pharaoh and of his servants is turned against the people, and they say, “What &#91;is&#93; this we have done, that we have sent Israel away from our service?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he harnesses his chariot, and he has taken his people with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 15:4; Judges 4:3; Psalms 68:17"> and he takes six hundred chosen chariots, even all the chariots of Egypt, and captains over them all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:17; Numbers 33:3; Deuteronomy 26:8"> and YHWH strengthens the heart of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and he pursues after the sons of Israel, and the sons of Israel are going out with a high hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 15:9; Joshua 24:6; Exodus 14:2"> and the Egyptians pursue after them, and all the chariot horses of Pharaoh, and his horsemen, and his force, overtake them, encamping by the sea, by Pi-Hahiroth, before Ba‘al-Zephon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 24:7; Psalms 34:17; Nehemiah 9:9"> And Pharaoh has drawn near, and the sons of Israel lift up their eyes, and behold, the Egyptians are journeying after them, and they fear exceedingly, and the sons of Israel cry to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 106:7–8; Numbers 14:1–4; Exodus 15:23–24"> And they say to Moses, “Because there are no graves in Egypt, have you taken us away to die in a wilderness? What is this you have done to us—to bring us out from Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jonah 4:3; Exodus 6:9; Jonah 4:8"> Is this not the word which we spoke to you in Egypt, saying, Cease from us, and we serve the Egyptians; for &#91;it is&#93; better for us to serve the Egyptians than to die in a wilderness?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:17; 2 Chronicles 20:15; Exodus 14:30"> And Moses says to the people, “Do not fear, station yourselves, and see the salvation of YHWH, which He does for you today; for as you have seen the Egyptians today, you add no more to see them for all time;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:22; Deuteronomy 20:4; 2 Chronicles 20:17"> YHWH fights for you, and you keep silent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 7:10; Ezra 10:4–5; Nehemiah 9:9"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Why do you cry to Me? Speak to the sons of Israel, and they journey;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 7:19; Exodus 4:20; Exodus 4:2"> and you, lift up your rod, and stretch out your hand toward the sea, and cleave it, and the sons of Israel go into the midst of the sea on dry land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:8; Exodus 14:4; Ezekiel 34:11"> And I—behold, I am strengthening the heart of the Egyptians, and they go in after them, and I am honored by Pharaoh, and by all his force, by his chariots, and by his horsemen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 7:5; Exodus 14:4; Exodus 7:17"> and the Egyptians have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, in My being honored by Pharaoh, by his chariots, and by his horsemen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 63:9; Exodus 32:34; Exodus 23:20–21"> And the Messenger of God, who is going before the camp of Israel, journeys and goes at their rear; and the pillar of the cloud journeys from their front, and stands at their rear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; 2 Corinthians 2:15–16; Proverbs 4:18–19"> and comes in between the camp of the Egyptians and the camp of Israel, and the cloud and the darkness are, and he enlightens the night, and the one has not drawn near to the other all the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 63:12; Exodus 15:8; Psalms 66:6"> And Moses stretches out his hand toward the sea, and YHWH causes the sea to go on by a strong east wind all the night, and makes the sea become dry ground, and the waters are cleaved,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:29; Psalms 66:6; Exodus 15:19"> and the sons of Israel go into the midst of the sea on dry land, and the waters &#91;are&#93; a wall to them, on their right and on their left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 15:19; Exodus 14:17; Exodus 15:9"> And the Egyptians pursue, and go in after them (all the horses of Pharaoh, his chariots, and his horsemen) into the midst of the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Psalms 77:16–19; Exodus 14:25"> and it comes to pass, in the morning watch, that YHWH looks to the camp of the Egyptians through the pillar of fire and of the cloud, and troubles the camp of the Egyptians,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Deuteronomy 3:22; Job 20:24"> and turns aside the wheels of their chariots, and they lead them with difficulty, and the Egyptians say, “Let us flee from the face of Israel, for YHWH is fighting for them against the Egyptians.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 14:16; Exodus 8:5; James 2:13"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Stretch out your hand toward the sea, and the waters turn back on the Egyptians, on their chariots, and on their horsemen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 4:18; Psalms 78:53; Hebrews 11:29"> And Moses stretches out his hand toward the sea, and the sea turns back, at the turning of the morning, to its perennial flow, and the Egyptians are fleeing at its coming, and YHWH shakes off the Egyptians in the midst of the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 78:53; Psalms 106:9–11; Habakkuk 3:13"> and the waters turn back, and cover the chariots and the horsemen, even all the force of Pharaoh, who are coming in after them into the sea—there has not been left even one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 14:22; Psalms 77:19–20; Isaiah 43:2"> And the sons of Israel have gone on dry land in the midst of the sea, and the waters &#91;are&#93; a wall to them, on their right and on their left;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 106:8; Psalms 106:10; Psalms 92:9–11"> and YHWH saves Israel out of the hand of the Egyptians in that day, and Israel sees the Egyptians dead on the seashore,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 2:11; John 11:45; John 2:23–25"> and Israel sees the great hand with which YHWH has worked against the Egyptians, and the people fear YHWH, and remain steadfast in YHWH, and in His servant Moses.</span>
