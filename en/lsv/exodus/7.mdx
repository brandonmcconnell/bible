<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 4:15–16; Psalms 82:6; John 10:35–36"> And YHWH says to Moses, “See, I have given you &#91;as&#93; a god to Pharaoh, and your brother Aaron is your prophet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 4:15; Exodus 6:29; Deuteronomy 4:2"> you speak all that I command you, and your brother Aaron speaks to Pharaoh, and he has sent the sons of Israel out of his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 11:9; Psalms 105:27–36"> And I harden the heart of Pharaoh, and have multiplied My signs and My wonders in the land of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 11:9; Exodus 6:6; Exodus 12:51"> and Pharaoh does not listen, and I have put My hand on Egypt, and have brought out My hosts, My people, the sons of Israel, from the land of Egypt by great judgments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 14:18; Exodus 7:17; Exodus 8:22"> and the Egyptians have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, in My stretching out My hand against Egypt; and I have brought out the sons of Israel from their midst.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 7:2; Genesis 6:22; Exodus 7:10"> And Moses does—Aaron also—as YHWH commanded them; so have they done;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:7; Acts 7:30; Deuteronomy 31:2"> and Moses &#91;is&#93; a son of eighty years, and Aaron &#91;is&#93; a son of eighty-three years, in their speaking to Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And YHWH speaks to Moses and to Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 4:2; John 2:18; Isaiah 7:11"> “When Pharaoh speaks to you, saying, Give a wonder for yourselves; then you have said to Aaron, Take your rod and cast &#91;it&#93; before Pharaoh—it becomes a dragon.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 4:3; Luke 10:19; Amos 9:3"> And Moses goes in—Aaron also—to Pharaoh, and they do so as YHWH has commanded; and Aaron casts his rod before Pharaoh and before his servants, and it becomes a dragon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 7:22; 2 Timothy 3:8; Exodus 8:18"> And Pharaoh also calls for wise men and for sorcerers; and the enchanters of Egypt, they also, with their &#91;enchanting&#93; flames, do so,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 4:4; Exodus 9:11; Exodus 8:18–19"> and they each cast down his rod, and they become dragons, and the rod of Aaron swallows their rods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 8:15; Zechariah 7:11–12"> and the heart of Pharaoh is strong, and he has not listened to them, as YHWH has spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 10:20; Exodus 8:15; Exodus 10:1"> And YHWH says to Moses, “The heart of Pharaoh has been hard, he has refused to send the people away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 8:20; Exodus 2:5; Exodus 7:10"> go to Pharaoh in the morning, behold, he is going out to the water, and you have stood to meet him by the edge of the River, and the rod which was turned to a serpent you take in your hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Exodus 9:1; Exodus 9:13"> and you have said to him: YHWH, God of the Hebrews, has sent me to you, saying, Send My people away, and they serve Me in the wilderness; and behold, you have not listened until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 4:9; Exodus 5:2; Exodus 7:5"> Thus said YHWH: By this you know that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH; behold, I am striking with the rod which &#91;is&#93; in my hand, on the waters which &#91;are&#93; in the River, and they have been turned to blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 7:24; Exodus 7:21; Numbers 11:20"> and the fish that &#91;are&#93; in the River die, and the River has stunk, and the Egyptians have been wearied of drinking waters from the River.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Exodus 10:12; Exodus 8:5–6"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Say to Aaron, Take your rod, and stretch out your hand against the waters of Egypt, against their streams, against their rivers, and against their ponds, and against all their collections of waters; and they are blood—and there has been blood in all the land of Egypt, both in &#91;vessels of&#93; wood, and in &#91;those of&#93; stone.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 78:44; Psalms 105:29; Exodus 7:17–18"> And Moses and Aaron do so, as YHWH has commanded, and he lifts up &#91;his hand&#93; with the rod, and strikes the waters which &#91;are&#93; in the River, before the eyes of Pharaoh and before the eyes of his servants, and all the waters which &#91;are&#93; in the River are turned to blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 7:18; Revelation 8:9"> and the fish which &#91;is&#93; in the River has died, and the River stinks, and the Egyptians have not been able to drink water from the River; and the blood is in all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 7:11; 2 Timothy 3:8; Jeremiah 27:18"> And the enchanters of Egypt do so with their secrets, and the heart of Pharaoh is strong, and he has not listened to them, as YHWH has spoken,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:24; Ezekiel 40:4; Malachi 2:2"> and Pharaoh turns and goes into his house, and has not set his heart even to this;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and all the Egyptians seek water all around the River to drink, for they have not been able to drink of the waters of the River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 10:23; Exodus 8:9–10; 2 Samuel 24:13"> And seven days are completed after YHWH’s striking the River.</span>
