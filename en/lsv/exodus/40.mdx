<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 26:30; Exodus 13:4; Numbers 7:1"> “On the first day of the month, in the first month, you raise up the Dwelling Place of the Tent of Meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:12; Numbers 4:5; Revelation 11:19"> and have set the Ark of the Testimony &#91;in&#93; there, and have covered over the Ark with the veil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 24:8; Exodus 40:22–25; Exodus 37:10–24"> and have brought in the table, and set its arrangement in order, and have brought in the lampstand, and caused its lamps to go up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Hebrews 9:24; 1 John 2:1–2"> And you have put the golden altar for incense before the Ark of the Testimony, and have put the covering of the opening to the Dwelling Place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 40:29; 1 John 4:9–10; Hebrews 13:10"> and have put the altar of the burnt-offering before the opening of the Dwelling Place of the Tent of Meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; 1 John 1:7; Psalms 26:6"> and have put the laver between the Tent of Meeting and the altar, and have put water &#91;in&#93; there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 40:33; Ephesians 4:11–12; Matthew 16:18"> And you have set the court all around, and have placed the covering of the gate of the court,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 8:10; Numbers 7:1; Matthew 3:16"> and have taken the anointing oil, and anointed the Dwelling Place, and all that &#91;is&#93; in it, and hallowed it, and all its vessels, and it has been holy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 29:36–37; Luke 1:35; Isaiah 61:1"> and you have anointed the altar of the burnt-offering, and all its vessels, and sanctified the altar, and the altar has been most holy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and you have anointed the laver and its base, and sanctified it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 8:1–13; Leviticus 9:1–24; Exodus 29:1–35"> And you have brought Aaron and his sons near to the opening of the Tent of Meeting, and have bathed them with water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 28:41; 1 John 2:20; 1 John 2:27"> and you have clothed Aaron with the holy garments, and anointed him, and sanctified him, and he has acted as priest to Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Romans 8:30; 1 Corinthians 1:30"> And you bring his sons near, and have clothed them with coats,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 25:13; Hebrews 5:1–14; Hebrews 8:1–10"> and anointed them as you have anointed their father, and they have acted as priests to Me, and their anointing has been to them for a continuous priesthood throughout their generations.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Exodus 40:17–32; Deuteronomy 12:32"> And Moses does according to all that YHWH has commanded him; so he has done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 7:1; Exodus 40:1–2; Numbers 9:1"> And it comes to pass, in the first month, in the second year, on the first of the month, the Dwelling Place has been raised up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27–28; 1 Peter 1:5; Leviticus 26:11"> and Moses raises up the Dwelling Place, and sets its sockets, and places its boards, and places its bars, and raises its pillars,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:1–14; Exodus 36:8–19"> and spreads the tent over the Dwelling Place, and puts the covering of the tent on it above, as YHWH has commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:34; Deuteronomy 10:5; Hebrews 10:19–21"> And he takes and puts the Testimony into the Ark, and sets the poles on the Ark, and puts the propitiatory covering on the Ark above;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:33; Exodus 40:3; Exodus 35:12"> and brings the Ark into the Dwelling Place, and places the veil of the covering, and covers over the Ark of the Testimony, as YHWH has commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 26:35; John 6:53–57; Ephesians 3:8"> And he puts the table in the Tent of Meeting, on the side of the Dwelling Place northward, at the outside of the veil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; Exodus 40:4; Hebrews 9:2"> and sets in order the arrangement of bread on it, before YHWH, as YHWH has commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 26:35; Revelation 2:5; John 1:1"> And he puts the lampstand in the Tent of Meeting, opposite the table, on the side of the Dwelling Place southward,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 40:4; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 4:5"> and causes the lamps to go up before YHWH, as YHWH has commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Hebrews 7:25; John 11:42"> And he sets the golden altar in the Tent of Meeting, before the veil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 30:7"> and makes incense on it—spice-incense—as YHWH has commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Hebrews 10:19–20; Ephesians 2:18"> And he sets the covering &#91;at&#93; the opening of the Dwelling Place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 40:6; Hebrews 13:5–6; Hebrews 13:10"> and he has set the altar of the burnt-offering &#91;at&#93; the opening of the Dwelling Place of the Tent of Meeting, and causes the burnt-offering to go up on it, and the present, as YHWH has commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 40:7; Exodus 38:8; Ezekiel 36:25"> And he puts the laver between the Tent of Meeting and the altar, and puts water &#91;in&#93; there for washing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 30:19–20; Psalms 26:6; John 13:10"> and Moses and Aaron and his sons have washed their hands and their feet at the same;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 73:19; Exodus 30:19–20; Exodus 40:19"> in their going into the Tent of Meeting, and in their drawing near to the altar, they wash, as YHWH has commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 40:8; Exodus 27:9–16; John 17:4"> And he raises up the court all around the Dwelling Place, and around the altar, and places the covering of the gate of the court; and Moses completes the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 15:8; Numbers 9:15–23; 2 Chronicles 7:2"> And the cloud covers the Tent of Meeting, and the glory of YHWH has filled the Dwelling Place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 8:11; Leviticus 16:2; 2 Chronicles 7:2"> and Moses has not been able to go into the Tent of Meeting, for the cloud has dwelt on it, and the glory of YHWH has filled the Dwelling Place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:19; Numbers 9:17; Exodus 13:21–22"> And in the going up of the cloud from off the Dwelling Place the sons of Israel journey in all their journeys;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 9:19–22; Psalms 31:15"> and if the cloud does not go up then they do not journey, until the day of its going up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 78:14; Numbers 9:15; Exodus 13:21"> For the cloud of YHWH &#91;is&#93; on the Dwelling Place by day, and fire is in it by night, before the eyes of all the house of Israel in all their journeys.</span>
