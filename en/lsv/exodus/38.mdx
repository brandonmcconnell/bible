<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 27:1–8; Ezekiel 43:13–17; Exodus 40:6"> And he makes the altar of burnt-offering of shittim wood; five cubits its length, and five cubits its breadth (square), and three cubits its height;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 27:2; Job 6:12"> and he makes its horns on its four corners; its horns have been of the same; and he overlays it with bronze;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 27:3; 1 Samuel 2:13"> and he makes all the vessels of the altar, the pots, and the shovels, and the sprinkling-pans, the forks, and the fire-pans; he has made all its vessels of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And he makes a bronze grate of network for the altar, under its rim beneath, to its midst;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 27:4"> and he casts four rings for the four ends of the bronze grate—places for bars;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:3; Exodus 25:6"> and he makes the poles of shittim wood, and overlays them with bronze;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Corinthians 2:2; 1 Corinthians 1:24"> and he brings in the poles into the rings on the sides of the altar, to carry it with them; he made it hollow &#91;with&#93; boards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:22; Luke 2:37; Exodus 40:7"> And he makes the laver of bronze, and its base of bronze, with the mirrors of the women assembling, who have assembled at the opening of the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 27:9–19; Psalms 89:7; 1 Kings 6:36"> And he makes the court: at the south side southward, the hangings of the court of twined linen, one hundred by the cubit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> their pillars &#91;are&#93; twenty, and their bronze sockets twenty, the pegs of the pillars and their fillets &#91;are&#93; silver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and at the north side, one hundred by the cubit, their pillars &#91;are&#93; twenty, and their sockets of bronze twenty; the pegs of the pillars and their fillets &#91;are&#93; silver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and at the west side &#91;are&#93; hangings, fifty by the cubit; their pillars &#91;are&#93; ten, and their sockets ten; the pegs of the pillars and their fillets &#91;are&#93; silver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and at the east side eastward, fifty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:14"> The hangings on the side &#91;are&#93; fifteen cubits, their pillars three, and their sockets three,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and at the second side at the gate of the court, on this &#91;side&#93; and on that &#91;side&#93;—hangings of fifteen cubits, their pillars three, and their sockets three;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> all the hangings of the court around &#91;are&#93; of twined linen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and the sockets for the pillars of bronze, the pegs of the pillars and their fillets of silver, and the overlaying of their tops of silver, and all the pillars of the court are filleted with silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:14"> And the covering of the gate of the court &#91;is&#93; the work of an embroiderer, of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined linen; and twenty cubits &#91;is&#93; the length, and the height with the breadth &#91;is&#93; five cubits, corresponding to the hangings of the court;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and their pillars &#91;are&#93; four, and their sockets of bronze four, their pegs &#91;are&#93; of silver, and the overlaying of their tops and their fillets &#91;are&#93; of silver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:9; Exodus 27:19; Ephesians 2:21–22"> and all the pins for the Dwelling Place, and for the court all around, &#91;are&#93; of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 7:44; Numbers 9:15; 2 Chronicles 24:6"> These are the numberings of the Dwelling Place (the Dwelling Place of Testimony), which has been numbered by the command of Moses, the service of the Levites, by the hand of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 35:30–3; Exodus 31:1–5; Psalms 119:6"> And Bezaleel son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah, has made all that YHWH commanded Moses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 35:34; Exodus 31:6"> and with him &#91;is&#93; Aholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, an engraver, and designer and embroiderer in blue, and in purple, and in scarlet, and in linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 18:16; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:25"> All the gold which is prepared for the work in all the work of the holy place (and it is the gold of the wave-offering) &#91;is&#93; twenty-nine talents, and seven hundred and thirty shekels, by the shekel of the holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:12"> And the silver of those numbered of the congregation &#91;is&#93; one hundred talents, and one thousand and seven hundred and seventy-five shekels, by the shekel of the holy place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Exodus 30:13; Exodus 12:37"> a bekah for the counted head (half a shekel, by the shekel of the holy place), for everyone who is passing over to those numbered, from a son of twenty years and upwards, for six hundred thousand and three thousand and five hundred and fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:19; Exodus 26:25; Exodus 26:21"> And one hundred talents of silver are for casting the sockets of the holy place, and the sockets of the veil; one hundred sockets for the hundred talents, a talent for a socket;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 27:17"> and &#91;from&#93; the one thousand and seven hundred and seventy-five &#91;shekels&#93; he has made pegs for the pillars, and overlaid their tops, and filleted them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And the bronze of the wave-offering &#91;is&#93; seventy talents, and two thousand and four hundred shekels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:37; Exodus 27:17; Exodus 27:10"> and he makes with it the sockets of the opening of the Tent of Meeting, and the bronze altar, and the bronze grate which it has, and all the vessels of the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> and the sockets of the court all around, and the sockets of the gate of the court, and all the pins of the Dwelling Place, and all the pins of the court all around.</span>
