<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 36:1; Exodus 35:30; John 3:27"> “See, I have called by name Bezaleel, son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:31; Isaiah 28:26; 1 Kings 7:14"> and I fill him &#91;with&#93; the Spirit of God, in wisdom, and in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 26:1; Exodus 25:32–35; 2 Chronicles 2:13–14"> to devise inventions to work in gold, and in silver, and in bronze,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 28:9–21"> and in a carving of stone for settings, and in a carving of wood to work in all work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:10; Exodus 35:34–1; Exodus 28:3"> And I, behold, have given with him Aholiab, son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, and I have given wisdom in the heart of every wise-hearted one, and they have made all that which I have commanded you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 36:8–9; Exodus 25:10–22; Exodus 26:1–37"> The Tent of Meeting, and the Ark of Testimony, and the propitiatory covering which &#91;is&#93; on it, and all the vessels of the tent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 37:10–28; Exodus 30:1–10; Exodus 25:23–40"> and the table and its vessels, and the pure lampstand and all its vessels, and the altar of the incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–8; Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 40:11"> and the altar of the burnt-offering and all its vessels, and the laver and its base,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 4:5–14; Exodus 28:1–43; Exodus 39:1–43"> and the colored garments, and the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons, for acting as priests in;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Exodus 30:23–38"> and the anointing oil, and the incense of the spices for the holy place; according to all that I have commanded you—they do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:12; Ezekiel 20:20; Leviticus 19:30"> “And you, speak to the sons of Israel, saying, Surely you keep My Sabbaths, for it &#91;is&#93; a sign between Me and you throughout your generations, to know that I, YHWH, am sanctifying you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 35:2–3; Numbers 15:32–36; Ezekiel 20:21"> and you have kept the Sabbath, for it &#91;is&#93; holy to you, he who is defiling it is certainly put to death—for any who does work in it—that person has even been cut off from the midst of his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 2:2; Exodus 16:23; Leviticus 23:3"> &#91;For&#93; six days work is done, and in the seventh day &#91;is&#93; a Sabbath of holy rest to YHWH; any who does work in the Sabbath day is certainly put to death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; Genesis 9:13; Genesis 17:11"> and the sons of Israel have observed the Sabbath; to keep the Sabbath throughout their generations &#91;is&#93; a perpetual covenant—</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:31; Exodus 31:13; Genesis 2:2–3"> it &#91;is&#93; a sign between Me and the sons of Israel for all time; for &#91;in&#93; six days YHWH made the heavens and the earth, and in the seventh day He has ceased and is refreshed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:13; Exodus 32:15–16; Deuteronomy 5:22"> And He gives to Moses, when He finishes speaking with him in Mount Sinai, two tablets of the Testimony, tablets of stone, written by the finger of God.</span>
