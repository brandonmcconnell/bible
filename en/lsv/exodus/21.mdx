<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:14; Deuteronomy 6:20; Exodus 24:3–4"> And these &#91;are&#93; the judgments which you set before them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12–15; Exodus 12:44; Deuteronomy 15:18"> When you buy a Hebrew servant, he serves &#91;for&#93; six years, and in the seventh he goes out as a freeman for nothing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12–14"> if he comes in by himself, he goes out by himself; if he &#91;is&#93; owner of a wife, then his wife has gone out with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> if his lord gives a wife to him, and she has borne sons or daughters to him—the wife and her children are her lord’s, and he goes out by himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:14–15; Deuteronomy 15:16–17; Isaiah 26:13"> And if the servant really says: I have loved my lord, my wife, and my sons—I do not go out free,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:17; Leviticus 25:40; Deuteronomy 19:17–18"> then his lord has brought him near to God, and has brought him near to the door, or to the doorpost, and his lord has bored his ear with an awl, and he has served him for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; Exodus 21:2–3"> And when a man sells his daughter for a handmaid, she does not go out according to the going out of the menservants;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 28:8; Deuteronomy 20:7; Malachi 2:11–15"> if &#91;it is&#93; evil in the eyes of her lord, so that he has not betrothed her, then he has let her be ransomed; he has no power to sell her to a strange people, in his dealing treacherously with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And if he betroths her to his son, he does to her according to the right of daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1–6"> If he takes another &#91;woman&#93; for him, he does not withdraw her food, her covering, and her habitation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and if he does not do these three for her, then she has gone out for nothing, without money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; Matthew 26:52; Leviticus 24:17"> He who strikes a man so that he has died is certainly put to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 20:2–9; 1 Samuel 24:18; Deuteronomy 4:41–43"> as for him who has not laid wait, but God has brought &#91;him&#93; to his hand, I have even set a place for you to where he flees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28–34; Hebrews 10:26; Numbers 15:30–31"> And when a man presumes against his neighbor to slay him with subtlety, you take him from My altar to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:18–21; Proverbs 30:17; 1 Timothy 1:9"> And he who strikes his father or his mother is certainly put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:7; 1 Timothy 1:10; Exodus 22:4"> And he who steals a man, and has sold him, and he has been found in his hand, is certainly put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:20; Mark 7:10–11; Deuteronomy 27:16"> And he who is reviling his father or his mother is certainly put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Deuteronomy 25:11; 2 Samuel 14:6"> And when men contend, and a man has struck his neighbor with a stone, or with the fist, and he does not die, but has fallen on the bed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:29; Zechariah 8:4"> if he rises, and has gone up and down outside on his staff, then the striker has been acquitted; he only gives &#91;for&#93; his cessation, and he is thoroughly healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 29:19; Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:19"> And when a man strikes his manservant or his handmaid with a rod, and he has died under his hand—he is certainly avenged;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 25:44–46"> only if he remains a day, or two days, he is not avenged, for he &#91;is&#93; his money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:18–19; Exodus 21:30; Deuteronomy 16:18"> And when men strive, and have struck a pregnant woman, and her children have come out, and there is no harm &#91;to them&#93;, he is certainly fined as the husband of the woman lays on him, and he has given through the judges;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:31; Leviticus 24:19"> and if there is harm &#91;to them&#93;, then you have given life for life,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Matthew 5:38–40; Matthew 7:2"> eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> burning for burning, wound for wound, stripe for stripe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Job 31:13–15; Psalms 72:12–14"> And when a man strikes the eye of his manservant, or the eye of his handmaid, and has destroyed it, he sends him away as a freeman for his eye;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and if he knocks out a tooth of his manservant or a tooth of his handmaid, he sends him away as a freeman for his tooth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 21:32; Leviticus 20:15–16; Genesis 9:5–6"> And when an ox gores man or woman, and they have died, the ox is certainly stoned, and his flesh is not eaten, and the owner of the ox &#91;is&#93; acquitted;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:1–9"> and if the ox is &#91;one&#93; accustomed to gore before, and it has been testified to its owner, and he does not watch it, and it has put to death a man or woman, the ox is stoned, and its owner is also put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 21:22; Exodus 30:12; Numbers 35:31–33"> If atonement is laid on him, then he has given the ransom of his life, according to all that is laid on him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> whether it gores a son or gores a daughter, according to this judgment it is done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 27:3–9"> If the ox gores a manservant or a handmaid, he gives thirty silver shekels to their lord, and the ox is stoned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 28:10; Jeremiah 18:20; Ecclesiastes 10:8"> And when a man opens a pit, or when a man digs a pit, and does not cover it, and an ox or donkey has fallen &#91;in&#93; there—</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 22:6; Exodus 21:29–30; Exodus 22:14"> the owner of the pit repays, he gives back money to its owner, and the dead is his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And when a man’s ox strikes the ox of his neighbor and it has died, then they have sold the living ox, and halved its money, and they also halve the dead one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> or, &#91;if&#93; it has been known that the ox is &#91;one&#93; accustomed to gore before, and its owner does not watch it, he certainly repays ox for ox, and the dead is his.</span>
