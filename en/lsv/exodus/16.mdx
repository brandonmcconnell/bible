<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 33:10–12; Exodus 15:27; Exodus 17:1"> And they journey from Elim, and all the congregation of the sons of Israel come to the wilderness of Sin, which &#91;is&#93; between Elim and Sinai, on the fifteenth day of the second month of their going out from the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:10; Exodus 15:24; Psalms 106:25"> And all the congregation of the sons of Israel murmur against Moses and against Aaron in the wilderness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 11:4–5; Numbers 20:3–5; Exodus 17:3"> and the sons of Israel say to them, “Oh that we had died by the hand of YHWH in the land of Egypt, in our sitting by the flesh-pot, in our eating bread to satiety—for you have brought us out to this wilderness to put all this assembly to death with hunger.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3; Psalms 78:24–25; Deuteronomy 8:2"> And YHWH says to Moses, “Behold, I am raining bread from the heavens for you—and the people have gone out and gathered the matter of a day in its day—so that I try them whether they walk in My law or not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 16:22–23; Exodus 35:2–3; Leviticus 25:21–22"> and it has been on the sixth day, that they have prepared that which they bring in, and it has been double above that which they gather day &#91;by&#93; day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Numbers 16:28; Exodus 16:12–13"> And Moses says—Aaron also—to all the sons of Israel, “Evening—and you have known that YHWH has brought you out from the land of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 40:5; Numbers 16:11; John 11:40"> and morning—and you have seen the glory of YHWH, in His hearing your murmurings against YHWH, and what &#91;are&#93; we, that you murmur against us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:2; 1 Samuel 8:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> And Moses says, “In YHWH’s giving to you flesh to eat in the evening, and bread in the morning to satiety—in YHWH’s hearing your murmurings, which you are murmuring against Him, and what &#91;are&#93; we? Your murmurings &#91;are&#93; not against us, but against YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 16:16; Exodus 16:2; Exodus 16:8"> And Moses says to Aaron, “Say to all the congregation of the sons of Israel, Come near before YHWH, for He has heard your murmurings”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 16:7; Numbers 16:19; Numbers 16:42"> and it comes to pass, when Aaron is speaking to all the congregation of the sons of Israel, that they turn toward the wilderness, and behold, the glory of YHWH is seen in the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And YHWH speaks to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Ezekiel 39:22; Exodus 6:7"> “I have heard the murmurings of the sons of Israel; speak to them, saying, Between the evenings you eat flesh, and in the morning you are satisfied &#91;with&#93; bread, and you have known that I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 11:9; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:27–28"> And it comes to pass in the evening, that the quail comes up, and covers the camp, and in the morning there has been the lying of dew around the camp,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 11:7–9; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:24"> and the lying of the dew goes up, and behold, on the face of the wilderness &#91;is&#93; a thin, bare thing, thin as hoarfrost on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:4; John 6:58; Exodus 16:31"> And the sons of Israel see, and say to one another, “What &#91;is&#93; it?” For they have not known what it &#91;is&#93;; and Moses says to them, “It &#91;is&#93; the bread which YHWH has given to you for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 16:36; Exodus 16:18; Exodus 16:32–33"> This &#91;is&#93; the thing which YHWH has commanded: Gather of it, each according to his eating, an omer for the counted head; and the number of your persons, take, each, for those in his tent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the sons of Israel do so, and they gather, he who is &#91;gathering&#93; much, and he who is &#91;gathering&#93; little;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> and they measure with an omer, and he who is &#91;gathering&#93; much has nothing over, and he who is &#91;gathering&#93; little has no lack, each according to his eating they have gathered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 12:10; Exodus 23:18; Matthew 6:34"> And Moses says to them, “Let no man leave of it until morning”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 10:14; Numbers 16:15; Ephesians 4:26"> and they have not listened to Moses, and some of them leave of it until morning, and it brings up worms and stinks; and Moses is angry with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–11; Ecclesiastes 12:1; 2 Corinthians 6:2"> And they gather it morning by morning, each according to his eating; when the sun has been warm, then it has melted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 16:5; Leviticus 25:22; Exodus 16:16"> And it comes to pass on the sixth day, they have gathered a second bread, two omers for one, and all the princes of the congregation come in, and declare &#91;it&#93; to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 23:3; Exodus 31:15; Exodus 20:8–11"> And he says to them, “It &#91;is&#93; that which YHWH has spoken: A rest—a holy Sabbath to YHWH—&#91;is&#93; tomorrow; that which you bake, bake; and that which you boil, boil; and all that is remaining, let &#91;it&#93; rest for yourselves for preservation until the morning.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:20; Exodus 16:33"> And they let it rest until the morning, as Moses has commanded, and it has not stunk, and a worm has not been in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 16:23; Exodus 16:29; Nehemiah 9:14"> And Moses says, “Eat it today, for today &#91;is&#93; a Sabbath to YHWH; today you do not find it in the field:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:13; Exodus 20:9–11; Ezekiel 46:1"> six days you gather it, and in the seventh day—the Sabbath—there is none in it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 20:4"> And it comes to pass on the seventh day, some of the people have gone out to gather, and have not found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 78:10; Psalms 106:13; Numbers 14:11"> And YHWH says to Moses, “How long have you refused to keep My commands and My laws?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 31:13; Isaiah 58:13–14; Ezekiel 20:12"> See, because YHWH has given the Sabbath to you, therefore He is giving to you on the sixth day bread of two days; each abide &#91;in&#93; his place, no one goes out from his place on the seventh day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:12–14; Leviticus 23:3; Hebrews 4:9"> And the people rest on the seventh day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Song of Songs 2:3; Numbers 11:6–9; Exodus 16:15"> and the house of Israel calls its name Manna, and it &#91;is&#93; as white coriander seed; and its taste &#91;is&#93; as a cake with honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 105:5; Luke 22:19; Psalms 111:4–5"> And Moses says, “This &#91;is&#93; the thing which YHWH has commanded: Fill the omer with it, for a charge for your generations, so that they see the bread which I have caused you to eat in the wilderness, in My bringing you out from the land of Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4"> And Moses says to Aaron, “Take one pot, and put the fullness of the omer of manna &#91;in&#93; there, and let it rest before YHWH, for a charge for your generations”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 27:21; Exodus 25:21; Exodus 25:16"> as YHWH has given command to Moses, so Aaron lets it rest before the Testimony, for a charge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:20–21; Joshua 5:12; Deuteronomy 8:2–3"> And the sons of Israel have eaten the manna &#91;for&#93; forty years, until their coming to the land to be inhabited; they have eaten the manna until their coming to the extremity of the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 16:16; Exodus 16:32–33"> And the omer is a tenth of the ephah.</span>
