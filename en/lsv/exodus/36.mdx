<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 28:3; Exodus 35:30–35; Exodus 31:1–6"> And Bezaleel, and Aholiab, and every wise-hearted man, in whom YHWH has given wisdom and understanding to know to do every work of the service of the holy place, have done according to all that YHWH commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 4:17; Hebrews 5:4; Exodus 31:6"> And Moses calls to Bezaleel, and to Aholiab, and to every wise-hearted man in whose heart YHWH has given wisdom, everyone whom his heart lifted up, to come near to the work to do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:27; Exodus 35:5–21"> And they take from before Moses all the raised-offering which the sons of Israel have brought in for the work of the service of the holy place to do it; and still they have brought to him a willing-offering morning by morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:10; Luke 12:42; Matthew 24:45"> And all the wise men, who are doing all the work of the holy place, each come from his work which they are doing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:6–10; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 2 Chronicles 24:14"> and speak to Moses, saying, “The people are multiplying to bring in more than sufficient for the service of the work which YHWH commanded &#91;us&#93; to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Moses commands, and they cause a voice to pass over through the camp, saying, “Do not let man or woman make anymore work for the raised-offering of the holy place”; and the people are restrained from bringing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:10"> and the work has been sufficient for them, for all the work, to do it, and to leave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 6:23; Exodus 26:1–37; Ezekiel 1:5–28"> And all the wise-hearted ones among the doers of the work make the Dwelling Place; he has made them &#91;with&#93; ten curtains of twined linen, and blue, and purple, and scarlet, &#91;with&#93; cherubim, the work of a designer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The length of one curtain &#91;is&#93; twenty-eight by the cubit, and the breadth of one curtain &#91;is&#93; four by the cubit; one measure &#91;is&#93; for all the curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; Exodus 26:3; Psalms 122:3"> And he joins the five curtains to one another, and the &#91;other&#93; five curtains he has joined to one another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:4"> and he makes loops of blue on the edge of one curtain, at the end, in the joining; so he has made in the edge of the outmost curtain, in the joining of the second;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 26:5; Exodus 26:10"> he has made fifty loops in the first curtain, and he has made fifty loops in the end of the curtain which &#91;is&#93; in the joining of the second; the loops are taking hold on one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 12:20"> And he makes fifty hooks of gold, and joins the curtains to one another by the hooks, and the Dwelling Place is one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–13"> And he makes curtains of goats’ &#91;hair&#93; for a tent over the Dwelling Place; eleven curtains he has made them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> the length of one curtain &#91;is&#93; thirty by the cubit, and the breadth of one curtain &#91;is&#93; four cubits; one measure &#91;is&#93; for the eleven curtains;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and he joins the five curtains apart, and the six curtains apart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he makes fifty loops on the outer edge of the curtain, in the joining; and he has made fifty loops on the edge of the curtain which is joining the second;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and he makes fifty hooks of bronze to join the tent—to be one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:14"> and he makes a covering for the tent of rams’ skins made red, and a covering of tachashim skins above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 25:5; Numbers 25:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> And he makes the boards for the Dwelling Place of shittim wood, standing up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> ten cubits &#91;is&#93; the length of the &#91;one&#93; board, and a cubit and a half the breadth of the &#91;one&#93; board;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> two handles &#91;are&#93; to one board, joined to one another; so he has made for all the boards of the Dwelling Place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And he makes the boards for the Dwelling Place; twenty boards for the south side southward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and he has made forty sockets of silver under the twenty boards: two sockets under one board for its two handles, and two sockets under the other board for its two handles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And for the second side of the Dwelling Place, for the north side, he has made twenty boards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and their forty sockets of silver: two sockets under one board, and two sockets under the other board;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:22; Exodus 26:27"> and for the sides of the Dwelling Place westward, he has made six boards;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and he has made two boards for the corners of the Dwelling Place, in the two sides;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:32; Ephesians 3:18–19; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> and they have been twins below, and together they are twins at its head, at one ring; so he has done to both of them at the two corners;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:25"> and there have been eight boards; and their sockets of silver &#91;are&#93; sixteen sockets, two sockets under one board.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 26:26–29; Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:5"> And he makes bars of shittim wood: five for the boards of the first side of the Dwelling Place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 26:26"> and five bars for the boards of the second side of the Dwelling Place, and five bars for the boards of the Dwelling Place, for the sides westward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and he makes the middle bar to enter into the midst of the boards from end to end;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> and he has overlaid the boards with gold, and he has made their rings of gold, places for bars, and he overlays the bars with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 10:20; Exodus 40:21"> And he makes the veil of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined linen; he has made it &#91;with&#93; cherubim—the work of a designer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18"> and he makes four pillars of shittim &#91;wood&#93; for it, and overlays them with gold; their pegs &#91;are&#93; of gold; and he casts four sockets of silver for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 26:36–37; Exodus 40:28"> And he makes a covering for the opening of the tent, of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined linen, the work of an embroiderer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 27:10"> also its five pillars, and their pegs; and he overlaid their tops and their fillets &#91;with&#93; gold, and their five sockets &#91;are&#93; bronze.</span>
