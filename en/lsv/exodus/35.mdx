<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 34:32; Exodus 31:1–11; Romans 2:13"> And Moses assembles all the congregation of the sons of Israel and says to them, “These &#91;are&#93; the things which YHWH has commanded—to do them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:3; Luke 13:14–15; Exodus 20:9–10"> &#91;For&#93; six days work is done, and on the seventh day there is a holy &#91;day&#93; for you, a Sabbath of rest to YHWH; any who does work in it is put to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 16:23; Numbers 15:32–36; Exodus 12:16"> you do not burn a fire in any of your dwellings on the Sabbath day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:1–2"> And Moses speaks to all the congregation of the sons of Israel, saying, “This &#91;is&#93; the thing which YHWH has commanded, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Psalms 110:3; Exodus 25:2–7"> Take a raised-offering for YHWH from among you; everyone whose heart &#91;is&#93; willing brings it &#91;as&#93; the raised-offering of YHWH: gold, and silver, and bronze,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–14; Exodus 26:31; Exodus 28:15"> and blue, and purple, and scarlet, and linen, and goats’ &#91;hair&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and rams’ skins made red, and tachashim skins, and shittim wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:1–40; Exodus 30:23"> and oil for the light, and spices for the anointing oil, and for the spice-incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 39:6–14; Exodus 28:9; Exodus 25:5"> and shoham stones, and stones for settings, for an ephod, and for a breastplate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 31:1–6; Exodus 36:1–4"> And all the wise-hearted among you come in, and make all that YHWH has commanded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:1–37; Exodus 36:8–34; Exodus 31:7–9"> the Dwelling Place, its tent, and its covering, its hooks, and its boards, its bars, its pillars, and its sockets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–22; Exodus 26:31–33; Exodus 37:1–9"> the Ark and its poles, the propitiatory covering, and the veil of the covering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 25:23–30; Leviticus 24:5–6; Exodus 37:10–16"> the table and its poles, and all its vessels, and the Bread of the Presentation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Matthew 5:14–15; Exodus 37:17–24"> and the lampstand for the light, and its vessels, and its lamps, and the oil for the light,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 30:1–10; Exodus 30:22–38; Exodus 37:25–28"> and the altar of incense, and its poles, and the anointing oil, and the spice-incense, and the covering of the opening at the opening of the Dwelling Place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 30:18–21; Exodus 38:1–8"> the altar of burnt-offering and the bronze grate which it has, its poles, and all its vessels, the laver and its base,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Exodus 38:9–20; Exodus 27:9–19"> the hangings of the court, its pillars, and their sockets, and the covering of the gate of the court,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 27:19"> the pins of the Dwelling Place, and the pins of the court, and their cords,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 31:10; Numbers 4:5–15; Exodus 28:1–43"> the colored garments, to do service in the holy place, the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons to act as priest in.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And all the congregation of the sons of Israel go out from the presence of Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:2; Exodus 35:26; 1 Chronicles 29:14"> and they come in—every man whom his heart has lifted up, and everyone whom his spirit has made willing—they have brought in the raised-offering of YHWH for the work of the Tent of Meeting, and for all its service, and for the holy garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:70–72; Matthew 2:11; Ezra 2:68–69"> And they come in—the men with the women—every willing-hearted one—they have brought in nose-ring, and earring, and seal-ring, and necklace, all golden goods, even everyone who has waved a wave-offering of gold to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:8; Exodus 25:2–7; Exodus 35:6–10"> And every man with whom has been found blue, and purple, and scarlet, and linen, and goats’ &#91;hair&#93;, and rams’ skins made red, and tachashim skins, have brought &#91;them&#93; in;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12"> everyone lifting up a raised-offering of silver and bronze has brought in the raised-offering of YHWH; and everyone with whom has been found shittim wood for any work of the service brought &#91;it&#93; in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 16:12; Luke 8:2–3; Proverbs 14:1"> And every wise-hearted woman has spun with her hands, and they bring in yarn—of the blue, and the purple, &#91;and&#93; the scarlet—and the linen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:21; Exodus 36:8; Exodus 35:29"> and all the women whose heart has lifted them up in wisdom, have spun the goats’ &#91;hair&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezra 2:68; 1 Chronicles 29:6; Exodus 35:9"> And the princes have brought in the shoham stones, and the stones for settings, for the ephod, and for the breastplate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 30:23–38; Exodus 35:8"> and the spices, and the oil for the light, and for the anointing oil, and for the spice-incense;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Exodus 35:21–22; Exodus 35:4"> every man and woman of the sons of Israel (whom their heart has made willing to bring in for all the work which YHWH commanded to be done by the hand of Moses) brought in a willing-offering to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 31:1–6; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> And Moses says to the sons of Israel, “See, YHWH has called Bezaleel, son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah, by name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:17; Isaiah 11:2–5; Isaiah 61:1–3"> and He fills him &#91;with&#93; the Spirit of God, in wisdom, in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> even to devise inventions to work in gold, and in silver, and in bronze,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and in a carving of stones for settings, and in a carving of wood to work in any work of design.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 31:6; 2 Chronicles 2:14; Ezra 7:27"> And He has put &#91;it&#93; in his heart to direct, he and Aholiab, son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 35:31; 1 Kings 7:14; Isaiah 28:26"> He has filled them with wisdom of heart to do every work, of engraver, and designer, and embroiderer (in blue, and in purple, in scarlet, and in linen), and weaver, who does any work, and of designers of designs.”</span>
