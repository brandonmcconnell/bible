<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 11:2; Joshua 3:7; Numbers 13:16"> “When Ephraim speaks tremblingly, &#124;&#124; He has been lifted up in Israel, &#124;&#124; When he becomes guilty in Ba‘al he dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 44:17–20; 1 Kings 19:18; Isaiah 46:6"> And now they sin, &#124;&#124; And make for themselves a molten image of their silver, &#124;&#124; By their own understanding—idols, &#124;&#124; A work of artisans—all of it, &#124;&#124; Of them they say, who &#91;are&#93; sacrificers among men, The calves let them kiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 6:4; Psalms 1:4; Psalms 68:2"> Therefore they are as a cloud of the morning, &#124;&#124; And as dew, rising early, going away, &#124;&#124; As chaff tossed about out of a floor, &#124;&#124; And as smoke out of a window.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 45:21–22; Hosea 12:9; Isaiah 43:10–13"> And I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH from the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And you do not know a God besides Me, &#124;&#124; And a Savior—there is none except Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:15; Deuteronomy 2:7; Deuteronomy 32:10"> I have known you in a wilderness, &#124;&#124; In a land of droughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–15; Deuteronomy 8:12–14; Nehemiah 9:35"> They are satiated according to their feedings, &#124;&#124; They have been satiated, &#124;&#124; And their heart is lifted up, &#124;&#124; Therefore they have forgotten Me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 5:14; Jeremiah 5:6; Lamentations 3:10"> And I am to them as a lion, &#124;&#124; I look out as a leopard by the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:8; Isaiah 5:29; Psalms 50:22"> I meet them as a bereaved bear, &#124;&#124; And I tear the enclosure of their heart, &#124;&#124; And I consume them there as a lioness, &#124;&#124; A beast of the field tears them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:17; Jeremiah 2:19; Deuteronomy 33:26"> You have destroyed yourself, O Israel, &#124;&#124; But in Me &#91;is&#93; your help;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 8:4; 1 Samuel 8:5–6; Hosea 10:3"> Where &#91;is&#93; your king now—And he saves you in all your cities? And your judges of whom you said, &#124;&#124; Give a king and heads to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:19; 1 Samuel 15:22–23; 1 Kings 14:7–16"> I give to you a king in My anger, &#124;&#124; And I take away in My wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:34–35; Job 14:17; Romans 2:5"> Bound up &#91;is&#93; the iniquity of Ephraim, &#124;&#124; Hidden &#91;is&#93; his sin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 37:3; Isaiah 13:8; Psalms 48:6"> Pangs of a travailing woman come to him, &#124;&#124; He &#91;is&#93; not a wise son, &#124;&#124; For he does not remain &#91;at&#93; the time for the breaking forth of sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 21:4; Isaiah 26:19"> Will I ransom them from the hand of Sheol? Will I redeem them from death? Where &#91;is&#93; your plague, O death? Where your destruction, O Sheol? Comfort is hid from My eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:12; Ezekiel 17:10; Genesis 41:52"> Though he produces fruit among brothers, &#124;&#124; An east wind comes in, a wind of YHWH, &#124;&#124; From a wilderness it is coming up, &#124;&#124; And it dries up his fountain, &#124;&#124; And his spring becomes dry, &#124;&#124; It—it spoils a treasure—every desirable vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 2 Kings 15:16; Isaiah 13:16"> Samaria becomes desolate, &#124;&#124; Because she has been rebellious against her God, &#124;&#124; They fall by sword, &#124;&#124; Their sucklings are dashed in pieces, &#124;&#124; And its pregnant ones are ripped up!”</span>
