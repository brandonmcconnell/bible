<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Micah 6:2; Isaiah 3:13–14"> “Hear a word of YHWH, sons of Israel, &#124;&#124; For a strife &#91;is&#93; to YHWH with inhabitants of the land, &#124;&#124; For there is no truth, nor kindness, &#124;&#124; Nor knowledge of God in the land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 6:9; Jeremiah 6:7; Hosea 7:1"> Swearing, and lying, and murdering, &#124;&#124; And stealing, and committing adultery—have increased, &#124;&#124; And blood has touched against blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Isaiah 24:4–12; Nahum 1:4"> Therefore the land mourns, &#124;&#124; And every dweller is weak in it, &#124;&#124; With the beast of the field, &#124;&#124; And with the bird of the heavens, &#124;&#124; And the fishes of the sea—they are removed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:12; Hosea 4:17; Amos 5:13"> Only, let no one strive, nor reprove a man, &#124;&#124; And your people &#91;are&#93; as those striving with a priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:8; Hosea 2:2; Galatians 4:26"> And you have stumbled in the day, &#124;&#124; And a prophet has also stumbled with you in the night, &#124;&#124; And I have cut off your mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13; Proverbs 19:2; Hosea 6:6"> My people have been cut off for lack of knowledge, &#124;&#124; Because you have rejected knowledge, &#124;&#124; I reject you from being priest to Me, &#124;&#124; And you forget the Law of your God—I forget your sons, I also!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Philippians 3:19; 1 Samuel 2:30"> According to their abundance so they sinned against Me, &#124;&#124; I change their glory into shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Isaiah 56:11; Titus 1:11"> The sin of My people they eat, &#124;&#124; And to their iniquity lift up their soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 24:2; Jeremiah 5:31; Matthew 15:14"> And it has been, like people, like priest, &#124;&#124; And I have charged on it its ways, &#124;&#124; And its habitual doings I return to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 6:14; Leviticus 26:26; Haggai 1:6"> And they have eaten, and are not satisfied, &#124;&#124; They have gone whoring, and do not increase, &#124;&#124; For they have left off taking heed to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Luke 21:34; Isaiah 28:7"> Whoredom, and wine, and new wine, take the heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:27; Hosea 5:4; Habakkuk 2:19"> My people at its staff asks and its rod declares to it, &#124;&#124; For a spirit of whoredoms has caused to err, &#124;&#124; And they go whoring from under their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; Jeremiah 3:6; Ezekiel 6:13"> On tops of the mountains they sacrifice, &#124;&#124; And on the hills they make incense, &#124;&#124; Under oak, and poplar, and terebinth, &#124;&#124; For good &#91;is&#93; its shade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; John 8:43; Ephesians 4:18"> Therefore your daughters commit whoredom, &#124;&#124; And your spouses commit adultery, &#124;&#124; I do not see after your daughters when they commit whoredom, &#124;&#124; And after your spouses when they commit adultery, &#124;&#124; For they with the harlots are separated, &#124;&#124; And with the whores they sacrifice, &#124;&#124; A people that does not understand kicks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 12:11; Hosea 9:15; Amos 4:4"> Though you &#91;are&#93; a harlot, O Israel, &#124;&#124; Do not let Judah become guilty, &#124;&#124; And do not come to Gilgal, nor go up to Beth-Aven, &#124;&#124; Nor swear, YHWH lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 8:5; Jeremiah 3:11"> For Israel has turned aside as a stubborn heifer, &#124;&#124; Now YHWH feeds them as a lamb in a large place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 81:12; Revelation 22:11; Hosea 13:2"> Ephraim is joined to idols, leave him alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Amos 5:12; 2 Kings 17:7–17"> Sour &#91;is&#93; their drink, &#124;&#124; They have gone whoring diligently, &#124;&#124; Her protectors have thoroughly loved shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; Jeremiah 4:11–12; Hosea 13:15"> Wind has distressed her with its wings, &#124;&#124; And they are ashamed of their sacrifices!”</span>
