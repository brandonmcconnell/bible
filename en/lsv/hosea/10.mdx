<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 8:11; Leviticus 26:1; Philippians 2:21"> “Israel &#91;is&#93; an empty vine, &#124;&#124; He makes fruit like to himself, &#124;&#124; According to the abundance of his fruit, &#124;&#124; He has multiplied for the altars, &#124;&#124; According to the goodness of his land, &#124;&#124; They have made good standing-pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 5:13; 1 Kings 18:21; Zephaniah 1:5"> Their heart has been divided, now they are guilty, &#124;&#124; He breaks down their altars, &#124;&#124; He destroys their standing-pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 10:15; Hosea 10:7; Hosea 13:11"> For now they say: We have no king, &#124;&#124; Because we have not feared YHWH, &#124;&#124; And the king—what does he do for us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 5:7; Amos 6:12; Hosea 4:2"> They have spoken words, &#124;&#124; To swear falsehood in making a covenant, &#124;&#124; And flourished as a poisonous herb has judgment, on the furrows of a field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 9:11; Hosea 5:8; 2 Kings 23:5"> For the inhabitants of Samaria fear the calves of Beth-Aven, &#124;&#124; Surely its people have mourned on account of it, &#124;&#124; And its priests leap about on account of it, &#124;&#124; Because of its glory, for it has removed from it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 5:13; Isaiah 30:3; Daniel 11:8"> Also, it is carried to Asshur, a present to a warlike king, &#124;&#124; Ephraim receives shame, &#124;&#124; And Israel is ashamed of its own counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 10:3; Jude 1:13; 2 Kings 1:3"> Samaria is cut off! Its king &#91;is&#93; as wrath on the face of the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 23:30; Revelation 6:16; Isaiah 2:19"> And high places of Aven have been destroyed, the sin of Israel. Thorn and bramble go up on their altars, &#124;&#124; And they have said to hills, Cover us, &#124;&#124; And to heights, Fall on us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Judges 20:5; Judges 19:22–30"> From the days of Gibeah you have sinned, O Israel, &#124;&#124; There they have stood, &#124;&#124; Battle does not overtake them in Gibeah, &#124;&#124; Because of sons of perverseness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Hosea 4:9; Jeremiah 16:16"> When I desire, then I bind them, &#124;&#124; And peoples have gathered against them, &#124;&#124; When they bind themselves to their two iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 4:16; Jeremiah 50:11; Deuteronomy 25:4"> And Ephraim &#91;is&#93; a trained heifer—loving to thresh, &#124;&#124; And I have passed over on the goodness of its neck, &#124;&#124; I cause &#91;one&#93; to ride Ephraim, &#124;&#124; Judah plows, Jacob harrows for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3–4; James 3:18; Isaiah 45:8"> Sow for yourselves in righteousness, &#124;&#124; Reap according to loving-kindness, &#124;&#124; Till for yourselves tillage of knowledge, &#124;&#124; To seek YHWH, &#124;&#124; Until He comes and shows righteousness to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 4:8; Galatians 6:7–8; Proverbs 1:31"> You have plowed wickedness, &#124;&#124; Perversity you have reaped, &#124;&#124; You have eaten the fruit of lying, &#124;&#124; For you have trusted in your way, &#124;&#124; In the abundance of your might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; 2 Kings 18:9–10; Amos 3:8"> And a tumult rises among your people, &#124;&#124; And all your fortresses are spoiled, &#124;&#124; As the spoiling of Shalman of Beth-Arbel, &#124;&#124; In a day of battle, &#124;&#124; Mother against sons dashed in pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Hosea 10:7; Romans 7:13"> Thus has Beth-El done to you, &#124;&#124; Because of the evil of your wickedness, &#124;&#124; In the dawn a king of Israel is utterly cut off!”</span>
