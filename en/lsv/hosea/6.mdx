<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 5:1; Hosea 7:1; Zephaniah 3:3"> And as bands wait for a man, &#124;&#124; A company of priests murder—the way to Shechem, &#124;&#124; For they have done wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 5:3; Jeremiah 23:14; Jeremiah 5:30–31"> In the house of Israel I have seen a horrible thing, &#124;&#124; There &#91;is&#93; the whoredom of Ephraim—Israel is defiled.</span>
