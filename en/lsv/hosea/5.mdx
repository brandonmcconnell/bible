<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 9:8; Micah 7:2; Micah 3:9"> “Hear this, O priests, and attend, O house of Israel, &#124;&#124; And, O house of the king, give ear, &#124;&#124; For the judgment &#91;is&#93; for you, &#124;&#124; For you have been a snare on Mizpah, &#124;&#124; And a net spread out on Tabor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 9:15; Isaiah 29:15; Hosea 6:9"> And to slaughter sinners have gone deep, &#124;&#124; And I &#91;am&#93; a chain to them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 3:2; Hebrews 4:13; Hosea 13:1"> I have known Ephraim, &#124;&#124; And Israel has not been hid from Me, &#124;&#124; For now you have gone whoring, Ephraim, &#124;&#124; Israel is defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:12; 1 John 2:3–4; Hosea 4:1"> They do not give up their habitual doings, &#124;&#124; To turn back to their God, &#124;&#124; For a spirit of whoredoms &#91;is&#93; in their midst, &#124;&#124; And YHWH they have not known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 7:10; Amos 5:2; Ezekiel 23:31–35"> And humbled has been the excellence of Israel to his face, &#124;&#124; And Israel and Ephraim stumble by their iniquity, &#124;&#124; Judah has also stumbled with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; John 7:34; Proverbs 1:28"> With their flock and with their herd, &#124;&#124; They go to seek YHWH, and do not find, &#124;&#124; He has withdrawn from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 6:7; Isaiah 48:8; Jeremiah 3:20"> Against YHWH they dealt treacherously, &#124;&#124; For they have begotten strange sons, &#124;&#124; Now a month consumes them &#91;with&#93; their portions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Hosea 9:9; 1 Samuel 15:34"> Blow a horn in Gibeah, a trumpet in Ramah, &#124;&#124; Shout, O Beth-Aven, after you, O Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 1:6; Isaiah 46:10; Hosea 9:11–17"> Ephraim is for a desolation in a day of reproof, &#124;&#124; I have made known a sure thing among the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Deuteronomy 27:17; Psalms 93:3–4"> Princes of Judah have been as those removing a border, &#124;&#124; I pour out My wrath as water on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Micah 6:16; 1 Kings 12:26–33"> Ephraim is oppressed, broken in judgment, &#124;&#124; When he pleased he went after the command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 51:8; Job 13:28; Isaiah 50:9"> And I &#91;am&#93; as a moth to Ephraim, &#124;&#124; And as a rotten thing to the house of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 7:11; Hosea 12:1; Hosea 10:6"> And Ephraim sees his sickness, and Judah his wound, &#124;&#124; And Ephraim goes to Asshur, &#124;&#124; And sends to a warlike king, &#124;&#124; And he is not able to give healing to you, &#124;&#124; Nor does he remove a scar from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 7:2; Micah 5:8; Hosea 13:7–8"> For I &#91;am&#93; as a lion to Ephraim, &#124;&#124; And as a young lion to the house of Judah, I tear and go, &#124;&#124; I carry away, and there is no deliverer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 26:16; Jeremiah 29:12–14; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> I go—I return to My place, &#124;&#124; Until they are desolate, and have sought My face. In their distress they seek Me speedily!”</span>
