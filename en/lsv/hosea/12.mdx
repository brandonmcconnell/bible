<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 11:12; Hosea 8:7; Ezekiel 17:10"> “Ephraim is enjoying wind, &#124;&#124; And is pursuing an east wind, &#124;&#124; All the day he multiplies lying and spoiling, &#124;&#124; And they make a covenant with Asshur, &#124;&#124; And oil is carried to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 6:2; Isaiah 10:6; Hosea 4:1"> And YHWH has a controversy with Judah, &#124;&#124; To lay a charge on Jacob according to his ways, &#124;&#124; He returns to him according to his doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:26; Genesis 32:24–28; Romans 9:11"> In the womb he took his brother by the heel, &#124;&#124; And by his strength he was a prince with God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:15; Malachi 3:1; Genesis 35:9–10"> Indeed, he is a prince to the Messenger, &#124;&#124; And he overcomes &#91;by&#93; weeping, &#124;&#124; And he makes supplication to Him, &#124;&#124; At Bethel He finds him, &#124;&#124; And there He speaks with us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:15; Isaiah 42:8; Psalms 135:13"> Even YHWH, God of the Hosts, YHWH &#91;is&#93; His memorial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Hosea 14:1; Micah 7:7"> And you, through your God, turn, &#124;&#124; Keep kindness and judgment, &#124;&#124; And wait on your God continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 6:10–11; Amos 8:5–6; Proverbs 11:1"> Canaan! In his hand &#91;are&#93; balances of deceit! He has loved to oppress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Psalms 62:10; Psalms 52:7"> And Ephraim says, &#124;&#124; Surely I have become rich, I have found wealth for myself, &#124;&#124; All my labors—they do not find against me iniquity that &#91;is&#93; sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40–43; Nehemiah 8:15–17; Hosea 13:4"> And I—your God YHWH from the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; Again I turn you back into tents, &#124;&#124; As in the days of the appointed time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 17:13; Ezekiel 20:49; Jeremiah 19:1"> And I have spoken to the prophets, &#124;&#124; And I have multiplied vision, &#124;&#124; And by the hand of the prophets I use allegories.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Hosea 6:8; Hosea 8:11"> Surely Gilead &#91;is&#93; iniquity, &#124;&#124; They have been only vanity, &#124;&#124; In Gilead they have sacrificed bullocks, &#124;&#124; Also their altars &#91;are&#93; as heaps, on the furrows of a field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:41; Genesis 27:43; Genesis 28:1–22"> And Jacob flees to the country of Aram, &#124;&#124; And Israel serves for a wife, &#124;&#124; Indeed, he has kept watch for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:50–51; Acts 3:22–23; Psalms 77:20"> And by a prophet has YHWH brought up Israel out of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And by a prophet it has been watched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:13; Daniel 11:18; 2 Kings 17:7–18"> Ephraim has most bitterly provoked, &#124;&#124; And he leaves his blood on himself, &#124;&#124; And his Lord turns his reproach back to him!”</span>
