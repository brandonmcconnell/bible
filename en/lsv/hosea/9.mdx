<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Hosea 4:12; Jeremiah 44:17"> “Do not rejoice, O Israel, do not be joyful like the peoples, &#124;&#124; For you have gone whoring from your God, &#124;&#124; You have loved a wage near all floors of grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 2:9; Micah 6:13–16; Joel 1:3–7"> Floor and winepress do not feed them, &#124;&#124; And new wine fails in her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:13; Hosea 8:13; Leviticus 25:23"> They do not abide in the land of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And Ephraim has turned back &#91;to&#93; Egypt, &#124;&#124; And they eat an unclean thing in Asshur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Deuteronomy 26:14; Jeremiah 6:20"> They do not pour out wine to YHWH, &#124;&#124; Nor are they sweet to Him, &#124;&#124; Their sacrifices &#91;are&#93; as bread of mourners to them, &#124;&#124; All eating it are unclean: For their bread &#91;is&#93; for themselves, &#124;&#124; It does not come into the house of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 10:3; Jeremiah 5:31; Hosea 2:11"> What do you do at the day appointed? And at the day of YHWH’s festival?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 10:8; Isaiah 5:6; Isaiah 7:23"> For behold, they have gone because of destruction, &#124;&#124; Egypt gathers them, Moph buries them, &#124;&#124; The desirable things of their silver, &#124;&#124; Nettles possess them—a thorn &#91;is&#93; in their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 26:24–25; Micah 7:4; 2 Kings 9:11"> The days of inspection have come in, &#124;&#124; The days of repayment have come in, Israel knows! The prophet &#91;is&#93; a fool, &#124;&#124; Mad &#91;is&#93; the man of the spirit, &#124;&#124; Because of the abundance of your iniquity, &#124;&#124; And great &#91;is&#93; the hatred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 5:1; Ezekiel 3:17; Isaiah 62:6"> Ephraim is looking &#91;away&#93; from My God, &#124;&#124; The prophet! A snare of a fowler &#91;is&#93; over all his ways, &#124;&#124; Hatred &#91;is&#93; in the house of his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 10:9; Judges 19:16–21; Hosea 8:13"> They have gone deep—have done corruptly, &#124;&#124; As &#91;in&#93; the days of Gibeah, &#124;&#124; He remembers their iniquity, He inspects their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:13; Hosea 4:14; Numbers 25:1–18"> As grapes in a wilderness I found Israel, &#124;&#124; As the first-fruit in a fig tree, at its beginning, I have seen your fathers, &#124;&#124; They have gone in &#91;to&#93; Ba‘al-Peor, &#124;&#124; And are separated to a shameful thing, &#124;&#124; And have become abominable like their love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:18; Hosea 9:14; Hosea 4:7"> Ephraim &#91;is&#93; as a bird, their glory flies away, without birth, and without womb, and without conception.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 7:13; Deuteronomy 32:25; Deuteronomy 31:17"> For though they nourish their sons, I have made them childless—without man, &#124;&#124; Surely also, woe to them when I turn aside from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:1–21; Ezekiel 27:3; Hosea 13:8"> Ephraim! When I have looked to the rock, &#124;&#124; Is planted in comeliness, &#124;&#124; And Ephraim &#91;is&#93; to bring out his sons to a slayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:29; 1 Corinthians 7:26; Mark 13:17"> Give to them, YHWH—what do You give? Give to them miscarrying womb, and dry breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Isaiah 1:23; Amos 5:5"> All their evil &#91;is&#93; in Gilgal, &#124;&#124; Surely there I have hated them, &#124;&#124; Because of the evil of their doings, &#124;&#124; Out of My house I drive them, &#124;&#124; I no longer love them, &#124;&#124; All their heads &#91;are&#93; apostates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 9:11–13; Ezekiel 24:21; Isaiah 5:24"> Ephraim has been struck, &#124;&#124; Their root has dried up, they do not yield fruit, &#124;&#124; Indeed, though they bring forth, &#124;&#124; I have put to death the desired of their womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 7:13; Deuteronomy 28:64–65; Acts 3:23"> My God rejects them, &#124;&#124; Because they have not listened to Him, &#124;&#124; And they are wanderers among nations!”</span>
