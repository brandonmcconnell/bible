<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10; Hosea 2:23; Zechariah 13:9"> “Say to your brothers—Ammi, &#124;&#124; And to your sisters—Ruhamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 50:1; Ezekiel 16:25; Ezekiel 23:45"> Plead with your mother—plead &#124;&#124; (For she &#91;is&#93; not My wife, and I &#91;am&#93; not her husband), &#124;&#124; And she turns her whoredoms from before her, &#124;&#124; And her adulteries from between her breasts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:22; Isaiah 32:13–14; Ezekiel 19:13"> Lest I strip her naked. And have set her up as &#91;in&#93; the day of her birth, &#124;&#124; And have made her as a wilderness, &#124;&#124; And have set her as a dry land, &#124;&#124; And have put her to death with thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:14; Hosea 1:6; Ezekiel 8:18"> And her sons I do not pity, &#124;&#124; For they &#91;are&#93; sons of whoredoms,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:21; Hosea 3:1; Jeremiah 44:17–18"> For their mother has gone whoring, &#124;&#124; Their conceiver has acted shamefully, &#124;&#124; For she has said, I go after my lovers, &#124;&#124; Those giving my bread and my water, &#124;&#124; My wool and my flax, my oil and my drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 19:8; Job 3:23; Lamentations 3:7–9"> Therefore, behold, I am hedging up your way with thorns, &#124;&#124; And I have made a wall for her, &#124;&#124; And her paths she does not find.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:2; Hosea 5:13; Ezekiel 23:4"> And she has pursued her lovers, &#124;&#124; And she does not overtake them, &#124;&#124; And has sought them, and does not find &#91;them&#93;, &#124;&#124; And she has said: I go, and I return to my first husband, &#124;&#124; For—better to me then than now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 1:3; Hosea 4:11; Hosea 13:2"> And she did not know that I had given to her &#124;&#124; The grain, and the new wine, and the oil. Indeed, I multiplied silver to her, &#124;&#124; And the gold they prepared for Ba‘al.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Haggai 1:6–11; Malachi 1:4"> Therefore I return, &#124;&#124; And I have taken My grain in its season, &#124;&#124; And My new wine in its appointed time, &#124;&#124; And I have taken away My wool and My flax, covering her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:29; Jeremiah 13:26; Hosea 13:7–8"> And now I reveal her dishonor before the eyes of her lovers, &#124;&#124; And none deliver her out of My hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13–14; Amos 5:21; Jeremiah 7:34"> And I have caused all her joy to cease, &#124;&#124; Her festival, her new moon, and her Sabbath, &#124;&#124; Even all her appointed times,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 7:23; Isaiah 5:5; Hosea 2:5"> And made desolate her vine and her fig tree, &#124;&#124; Of which she said, They &#91;are&#93; a wage to me, &#124;&#124; That my lovers have given to me, &#124;&#124; And I have made them for a forest, &#124;&#124; And a beast of the field has consumed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Judges 3:7; Hosea 13:1"> And I have charged on her the days of the Ba‘alim, &#124;&#124; To whom she makes incense, &#124;&#124; And puts on her ring and her ornament, &#124;&#124; And goes after her lovers, &#124;&#124; And forgot Me,” a declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:1–2; Revelation 12:6; Ezekiel 20:35–36"> “Therefore, behold, I am enticing her, &#124;&#124; And have caused her to go to the wilderness, &#124;&#124; And I have spoken to her heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:2; Ezekiel 28:26; Hosea 11:1"> And given to her her vineyards from there, &#124;&#124; And the Valley of Achor for an opening of hope, &#124;&#124; And she has responded there as in the days of her youth, &#124;&#124; And as in the day of her coming up out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 54:5; Hosea 2:7; Jeremiah 3:14"> And it has come to pass in that day,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “You call Me: My husband, &#124;&#124; And do not call Me anymore: My lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 23:13; Joshua 23:7; Psalms 16:4"> And I have turned aside the names of the lords from her mouth, &#124;&#124; And they are not remembered anymore by their name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:25; Ezekiel 39:9–10; Psalms 46:9"> And I have made a covenant for them in that day, &#124;&#124; With the beast of the field, &#124;&#124; And with the bird of the heavens, &#124;&#124; And the creeping thing of the ground, &#124;&#124; And bow, and sword, and war I break from off the land, &#124;&#124; And have caused them to lie down confidently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:2; Revelation 21:2; Isaiah 54:8–10"> And I have betrothed you to Me for all time, &#124;&#124; And betrothed you to Me in righteousness, &#124;&#124; And in judgment, and kindness, and mercies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 John 5:20; John 17:3; Jeremiah 24:7"> And betrothed you to Me in faithfulness, &#124;&#124; And you have known YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 8:12; Isaiah 65:24; Romans 8:32"> And it has come to pass in that day, I answer,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “I answer the heavens, and they answer the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joel 2:19; Hosea 1:4; Hosea 1:11"> And the earth answers the grain, &#124;&#124; And the new wine, and the oil, &#124;&#124; And they answer Jezreel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 9:25–26; 1 Peter 2:9–10; Zechariah 13:9"> And I have sowed her to Me in the land, &#124;&#124; And I have pitied Lo-Ruhamah, &#124;&#124; And I have said to Lo-Ammi, You &#91;are&#93; My people, &#124;&#124; And he says, My God!”</span>
