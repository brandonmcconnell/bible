<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 7:16; Hosea 8:13; 2 Kings 17:13–14"> He does not return to the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And Asshur—he &#91;is&#93; his king, &#124;&#124; For they have refused to return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; Hosea 10:6; Hosea 10:14"> The sword has been grievous in his cities, &#124;&#124; And it has ended his bars, and consumed—from their own counsels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:5; Amos 5:14–15; Hosea 7:16"> And My people are hanging in suspense about My returning, &#124;&#124; And to the Most High they call, &#124;&#124; Together they do not exalt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:20; Matthew 23:37; Hosea 6:4"> How do I give you up, O Ephraim? Do I deliver you up, O Israel? How do I make you as Admah? Do I set you as Zeboim? My heart is turned in Me, &#124;&#124; My sympathy has been kindled together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 55:8–9; Micah 7:18–20"> I do not do the fierceness of My anger, &#124;&#124; I do not turn back to destroy Ephraim, &#124;&#124; For I &#91;am&#93; God, and not a man—The Holy One in your midst, and I do not enter in enmity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 31:4; Joel 3:16; Amos 1:2"> They go after YHWH—He roars as a lion, &#124;&#124; When He roars, then the sons from the west tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 11:11; Isaiah 60:8; Zechariah 10:10"> They tremble as a sparrow out of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And as a dove out of the land of Asshur, &#124;&#124; And I have caused them to dwell in their own houses,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
