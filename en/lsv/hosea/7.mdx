<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 7:13; Micah 7:3–7; Hosea 6:4"> “When I give healing to Israel, &#124;&#124; Then the iniquity of Ephraim is revealed, &#124;&#124; And the wickedness of Samaria, &#124;&#124; For they have worked falsehood, &#124;&#124; And a thief comes in, &#124;&#124; A troop has stripped off in the street,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:2; Amos 8:7; Jeremiah 14:10"> And they do not say to their heart, &#124;&#124; &#91;That&#93; I have remembered all their evil, &#124;&#124; Now their doings have surrounded them, &#124;&#124; They have been before My face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Micah 7:3; Romans 1:32; Jeremiah 28:1–4"> With their wickedness they make a king glad, &#124;&#124; And with their lies—princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:2; James 4:4; Jeremiah 5:7–8"> All of them &#91;are&#93; adulterers, &#124;&#124; Like a burning oven of a baker, &#124;&#124; He ceases from stirring up after kneading the dough, until its leavening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 28:1; Matthew 14:6; Genesis 40:20"> A day of our king! Princes have defiled themselves &#91;with&#93; the poison of wine, &#124;&#124; He has drawn out his hand with scorners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 7:7; Hosea 7:4; 1 Samuel 19:11–15"> For they have drawn near, &#124;&#124; Their heart &#91;is&#93; as an oven &#124;&#124; In their lying in wait—their baker sleeps all night, &#124;&#124; Morning! He is burning as a flaming fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 15:25; 2 Kings 15:30; 2 Kings 15:10"> All of them are warm as an oven, &#124;&#124; And they have devoured their judges, &#124;&#124; All their kings have fallen, &#124;&#124; There is none calling to Me among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:35; Ezra 9:12; Nehemiah 13:23–25"> Ephraim! He mixes himself among peoples, &#124;&#124; Ephraim has been an unturned cake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 8:7; Isaiah 42:22–25; 2 Kings 13:3–7"> Strangers have devoured his power, &#124;&#124; And he has not known, &#124;&#124; Also old age has sprinkled &#91;itself&#93; on him, &#124;&#124; And he has not known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 5:5; Isaiah 9:13; Amos 4:6–13"> And the excellence of Israel has been humbled to his face, &#124;&#124; And they have not turned back to their God YHWH, &#124;&#124; Nor have they sought Him for all this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 12:1; Hosea 5:13; Hosea 11:11"> And Ephraim is as a simple dove without heart, &#124;&#124; Egypt they called on—&#91;to&#93; Asshur they have gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 31:16–29"> When they go I spread over them My net, &#124;&#124; As the bird of the heavens I bring them down, &#124;&#124; I discipline them as their congregation has heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 9:12; Hosea 11:12; Isaiah 63:8"> Woe to them, for they wandered from Me, &#124;&#124; Destruction to them, for they transgressed against Me, &#124;&#124; And I ransom them, and they have spoken lies against Me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 4:3; Job 35:9–10; Jeremiah 3:10"> And have not cried to Me with their heart, but howl on their beds; They assemble themselves for grain and new wine, &#124;&#124; They turn aside against Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; Psalms 62:3; Jeremiah 17:9"> And I instructed—I strengthened their arms, &#124;&#124; And concerning Me they think evil!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 78:57; Hosea 9:3; Ezekiel 23:32"> They turn back—not to the Most High, &#124;&#124; They have been as a deceitful bow, &#124;&#124; Their princes fall by sword, &#124;&#124; From the insolence of their tongue, &#124;&#124; This &#91;is&#93; their derision in the land of Egypt!”</span>
