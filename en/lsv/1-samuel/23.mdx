<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:44; Micah 6:15; Nehemiah 3:17–18"> And they declare to David, saying, “Behold, the Philistines are fighting against Keilah, and they are spoiling the threshing-floors.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:4; 2 Samuel 5:19; 2 Samuel 5:23"> And David inquires of YHWH, saying, “Do I go? And have I struck among these Philistines?” And YHWH says to David, “Go, and you have struck among the Philistines, and saved Keilah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:5; Psalms 11:1; 1 Samuel 23:15"> And David’s men say to him, “Behold, we here in Judah are afraid; and how much more when we go to Keilah, to the ranks of the Philistines?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 7:7; Joshua 8:7; 2 Kings 3:18"> And David adds again to inquire of YHWH, and YHWH answers him and says, “Rise, go down to Keilah, for I am giving the Philistines into your hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And David goes, and his men, to Keilah, and fights with the Philistines, and leads away their livestock, and strikes among them &#91;with&#93; a great striking, and David saves the inhabitants of Keilah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:20; 1 Samuel 14:3; 1 Samuel 14:18"> And it comes to pass, in the fleeing of Abiathar son of Ahimelech to David, to Keilah, an ephod came down in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:4–6; Job 20:5; 1 Samuel 26:8–9"> And it is declared to Saul that David has come to Keilah, and Saul says, “God has made him known for my hand, for he has been shut in, to enter into a city of doors and bar.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Saul summons all the people to the battle, to go down to Keilah, to lay siege to David and to his men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:7; 1 Samuel 23:6; Numbers 27:21"> And David knows that Saul is devising the evil against him and says to Abiathar the priest, “Bring the ephod near.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 3:15–16; 1 Samuel 23:8; Genesis 18:24"> And David says, “YHWH, God of Israel, Your servant has certainly heard that Saul is seeking to come to Keilah, to destroy the city on my account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:3; Psalms 50:15; Matthew 7:7–8"> Do the possessors of Keilah shut me up into his hand? Does Saul come down as Your servant has heard? YHWH, God of Israel, please declare &#91;it&#93; to Your servant.” And YHWH says, “He comes down.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:20; Psalms 31:8; Psalms 62:1"> And David says, “Do the possessors of Keilah shut up me and my men into the hand of Saul?” And YHWH says, “They shut &#91;you&#93; up.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:2; 1 Samuel 25:13; 1 Samuel 30:9–10"> And David rises—and his men—about six hundred men, and they go out from Keilah, and go up and down where they go up and down; and it has been declared to Saul that David has escaped from Keilah, and he ceases to go out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 54:3–4; Psalms 32:7; Joshua 15:24"> And David abides in the wilderness, in fortresses, and abides in the hill-country, in the wilderness of Ziph; and Saul seeks him &#91;for&#93; all the days, and God has not given him into his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And David sees that Saul has come out to seek his life, and David &#91;is&#93; in the wilderness of Ziph, in a forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3–4; Hebrews 12:12–13; Job 4:3–4"> And Jonathan son of Saul rises, and goes to David &#91;in the&#93; forest, and strengthens his hand in God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:31; 1 Samuel 24:20; Acts 28:16"> and says to him, “Do not fear, for the hand of my father Saul does not find you, and you reign over Israel, and I am to you for second, and also so knows my father Saul.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:7; 1 Samuel 20:42; 1 Samuel 18:3"> And both of them make a covenant before YHWH; and David abides in the forest, and Jonathan has gone to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:1; 1 Samuel 26:3; Psalms 54:1–7"> And the Ziphites go up to Saul at Gibeah, saying, “Is David not hiding himself with us in fortresses, in the forest, in the height of Hachilah, which &#91;is&#93; on the south of the desolate place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:12; Proverbs 29:26; Proverbs 11:23"> And now, by all the desire of your soul, O king, to come down, come down, and ours &#91;is&#93; to shut him up into the hand of the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:8; Isaiah 66:5; Micah 3:11"> And Saul says, “Blessed &#91;are&#93; you of YHWH, for you have pity on me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 5:13"> now go prepare yet, and know and see his place where his foot is; who has seen him there? For &#91;one&#93; has said to me, He is very cunning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 3:15–16; Mark 14:1; Numbers 10:36"> And see and know of all the hiding places where he hides himself, and you have turned back prepared to me, and I have gone with you, and it has been, if he is in the land, that I have searched him out through all the thousands of Judah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:2; Joshua 15:55"> And they rise and go to Ziph before Saul, and David and his men &#91;are&#93; in the wilderness of Maon, in the plain, at the south of the desolate place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 15:8; 1 Samuel 23:28"> And Saul and his men go to seek, and they declare &#91;it&#93; to David, and he goes down the rock, and abides in the wilderness of Maon; and Saul hears, and pursues after David &#91;to&#93; the wilderness of Maon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 17:9; 2 Corinthians 1:8; Psalms 140:1–9"> And Saul goes on this side of the mountain, and David and his men on that side of the mountain, and David is hurried to go from the face of Saul, and Saul and his men are surrounding David and his men, to catch them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 19:9; Psalms 116:3; Deuteronomy 32:36"> And a messenger has come to Saul, saying, “Hurry, and come, for the Philistines have pushed against the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Saul turns back from pursuing after David, and goes to meet the Philistines, therefore they have called that place “The Rock of Divisions.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:2; Joshua 15:62; Ezekiel 47:10"> And David goes up there, and abides in fortresses &#91;at&#93; En-gedi.</span>
