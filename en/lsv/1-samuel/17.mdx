<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:9; 1 Samuel 17:19"> and Saul and the men of Israel have been gathered, and encamp by the Valley of Elah, and set in array &#91;for&#93; battle to meet the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And the Philistines are standing on the mountain on this &#91;side&#93;, and Israel is standing on the mountain on that &#91;side&#93;, and the valley &#91;is&#93; between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:22; 2 Samuel 21:16–22; 1 Chronicles 20:4–8"> And a man goes out, the champion from the camps of the Philistines, his name &#91;is&#93; Goliath, from Gath; his height &#91;is&#93; six cubits and a span &#91;&#91;or four cubits and a span&#93;&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and a helmet of bronze &#91;is&#93; on his head, and he is clothed &#91;with&#93; a scaled coat of mail, and the weight of the coat of mail &#91;is&#93; five thousand shekels of bronze,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:45; 2 Chronicles 9:15; 1 Kings 10:16"> and a frontlet of bronze &#91;is&#93; on his feet, and a javelin of bronze between his shoulders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:19; 1 Samuel 17:41; 1 Chronicles 11:23"> and the wood of his spear &#91;is&#93; like a weavers’ beam, and the flame of his spear &#91;is&#93; six hundred shekels of iron, and the bearer of the buckler is going before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:17; 1 Chronicles 21:3; 1 Samuel 17:26"> And he stands and calls to the ranks of Israel, and says to them, “Why do you come out to set in array for battle? &#91;Am&#93; I not the Philistine, and you the servants of Saul? Choose a man for yourselves, and let him come down to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1"> if he is able to fight with me, and has struck me, then we have been for servants to you; and if I prevail against him, and have struck him, then you have been for servants to us, and have served us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:25–26; 1 Samuel 17:45; 1 Samuel 17:36"> And the Philistine says, “I have reproached the ranks of Israel this day; give a man to me and we fight together!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Deuteronomy 31:8; Proverbs 28:1"> And Saul hears—and all Israel—these words of the Philistine, and they are broken down and greatly afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:10–11; Genesis 35:19; 1 Samuel 16:18"> And David &#91;is&#93; son of this Ephraimite of Beth-Lehem-Judah, whose name &#91;is&#93; Jesse, and he has eight sons, and the man has become aged among men in the days of Saul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13; 2 Samuel 21:21; 1 Samuel 17:28"> and the three eldest sons of Jesse go, they have gone after Saul to battle; and the name of his three sons who have gone into battle &#91;are&#93; Eliab the firstborn, and his second Abinadab, and the third Shammah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:11; Genesis 25:23"> And David is the youngest, and the three eldest have gone after Saul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:11; 1 Samuel 16:19–23"> and David is going and returning from Saul, to feed the flock of his father at Beth-Lehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 4:2; Luke 4:2"> And the Philistine draws near, morning and evening, and stations himself &#91;for&#93; forty days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Samuel 17:28; Ruth 2:14"> And Jesse says to his son David, “Now take an ephah of this roasted &#91;grain&#93; and these ten loaves for your brothers, and run to the camp to your brothers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 37:14; 1 Samuel 16:20; 2 Samuel 17:29"> and take these ten cuttings of the cheese to the head of the one thousand, and inspect your brothers for welfare, and receive their pledge.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Saul, and they, and all the men of Israel, &#91;are&#93; in the Valley of Elah, fighting with the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:5; Luke 19:43; Ephesians 6:1–2"> And David rises early in the morning, and leaves the flock to a keeper, and lifts up, and goes, as Jesse commanded him, and he comes to the path, and to the force which is going out to the rank, and they have shouted for battle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and Israel and the Philistines set in array rank to meet rank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 10:5–6; Judges 18:15; Isaiah 10:28"> And David lets down the goods from off him on the hand of a keeper of the goods, and runs into the rank, and comes and asks of his brothers of &#91;their&#93; welfare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:8–10"> And he is speaking with them, and behold, a man, the champion, is coming up, his name &#91;is&#93; Goliath the Philistine, from Gath, out of the ranks of the Philistines, and he speaks according to those words, and David hears;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:6–7; Leviticus 26:36; Isaiah 7:2"> and all the men of Israel, when they see the man, flee from his presence, and are greatly afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 15:16; Revelation 2:7; Revelation 3:12"> And the men of Israel say, “Have you seen this man who is coming up? For he is coming up to reproach Israel, and it has been—the man who strikes him, the king enriches him with great riches, and he gives his daughter to him, and makes his father’s house free in Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:26; 1 Samuel 11:2; 1 Samuel 14:6"> And David speaks to the men who are standing by him, saying, “What is done to the man who strikes this Philistine, and has turned aside reproach from Israel? For who &#91;is&#93; this uncircumcised Philistine that he has reproached the ranks of the living God?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:25"> And the people speak to him according to this word, saying, “Thus it is done to the man who strikes him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 10:36; Genesis 37:11; Proverbs 18:19"> And Eliab, his eldest brother, hears when he speaks to the men, and the anger of Eliab burns against David, and he says, “Why &#91;is&#93; this—&#91;that&#93; you have come down? And to whom have you left those few sheep in the wilderness? I have known your pride, and the evil of your heart—for you have come down to see the battle.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Proverbs 15:1"> And David says, “What have I done now? Is it not a word?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:26–27"> And he turns around from him to another, and says according to this word, and the people return him word as the first word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29"> And the words which David has spoken are heard, and they declare &#91;them&#93; before Saul, and he receives him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:18; Deuteronomy 20:1–3; Psalms 3:6"> and David says to Saul, “Let no man’s heart fall because of him, your servant goes, and has fought with this Philistine.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 13:31; Deuteronomy 9:2; Revelation 13:4"> And Saul says to David, “You are not able to go to this Philistine, to fight with him, for you &#91;are&#93; a youth, and he &#91;has been&#93; a man of war from his youth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And David says to Saul, “Your servant has been a shepherd among the sheep for his father, and the lion has come—and the bear—and has taken away a sheep out of the drove,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Amos 3:12; 2 Timothy 4:17–18; Daniel 6:22"> and I have gone out after him, and struck him, and delivered &#91;it&#93; out of his mouth, and he rises against me, and I have taken hold on his beard, and struck him, and put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:10; Acts 5:38–39; Romans 2:28–29"> Your servant has struck both the lion and the bear, and this uncircumcised Philistine has been as one of them, for he has reproached the ranks of the living God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17–18; Psalms 138:7–8; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> And David says, “YHWH, who delivered me out of the paw of the lion, and out of the paw of the bear, He delivers me from the hand of this Philistine.” And Saul says to David, “Go, and YHWH is with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And Saul clothes David with his long robe, and has put a helmet of bronze on his head, and clothes him with a coat of mail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Hosea 1:7"> And David girded his sword above his long robe, and begins to go, for he has not tried &#91;it&#93;; and David says to Saul, “I am not able to go with these, for I had not tried”; and David turns them aside from off him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27–29; Matthew 10:10; Judges 15:15–16"> And he takes his staff in his hand, and chooses five smooth stones for himself from the brook, and puts them in the shepherds’ vessel that he has, even in the leather pouch, and his sling &#91;is&#93; in his hand, and he draws near to the Philistine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And the Philistine goes on, going and drawing near to David, and the man carrying the buckler &#91;is&#93; before him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12; 1 Samuel 17:33; Psalms 123:3–4"> and the Philistine looks attentively, and sees David, and despises him, for he was a youth, and ruddy, with a handsome appearance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:8; 2 Samuel 3:8; 1 Samuel 24:14"> And the Philistine says to David, “Am I a dog that you are coming to me with sticks?” And the Philistine reviles David by his gods,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:9–10; Proverbs 18:12; 1 Kings 20:10–11"> and the Philistine says to David, “Come to me, and I give your flesh to the bird of the heavens, and to the beast of the field.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; 2 Chronicles 32:8; 2 Corinthians 3:5"> And David says to the Philistine, “You are coming to me with sword, and with spear, and with buckler, and I am coming to you in the Name of YHWH of Hosts, God of the ranks of Israel, which you have reproached!</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 52:10; Joshua 4:24; 2 Kings 19:19"> This day YHWH shuts you up into my hand—and I have struck you, and turned aside your head from off you, and given the carcass of the camp of the Philistines this day to the bird of the heavens, and to the beast of the earth, and all the earth knows that God is for Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 44:6–7; Zechariah 4:6; Hosea 1:7"> And all this assembly knows that YHWH does not save by sword and by spear, for the battle &#91;is&#93; YHWH’s, and He has given you into our hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Psalms 27:1"> And it has come to pass, that the Philistine has risen, and goes, and draws near to meet David, and David hurries and runs to the rank to meet the Philistine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27–28; 2 Kings 9:24; 1 Kings 22:34"> and David puts forth his hand into the vessel, and takes a stone from there, and slings, and strikes the Philistine on his forehead, and the stone sinks into his forehead, and he falls on his face to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Judges 15:15; 1 Samuel 17:39; 1 Samuel 23:21"> And David is stronger than the Philistine with a sling and with a stone, and strikes the Philistine, and puts him to death, and there is no sword in the hand of David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Hebrews 11:34; 1 Samuel 21:9; 2 Samuel 23:21"> and David runs and stands over the Philistine, and takes his sword, and draws it out of its sheath, and puts him to death, and cuts off his head with it; and the Philistines see that their hero &#91;is&#93; dead, and flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Joshua 15:11; Joshua 15:33–36; Joshua 15:45–46"> And the men of Israel rise—also Judah—and shout, and pursue the Philistines until you enter the valley, and to the gates of Ekron, and the wounded of the Philistines fall in the way of Shaaraim, even to Gath, and to Ekron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; 2 Kings 7:7–16; Jeremiah 30:16"> and the sons of Israel return from burning after the Philistines, and spoil their camps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 16:33; Joshua 4:7–8; 1 Samuel 21:9"> And David takes the head of the Philistine, and brings it to Jerusalem, and he has put his weapons in his own tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:21–22; 1 Samuel 17:58"> And when Saul sees David going out to meet the Philistine, he has said to Abner, head of the host, “Whose son &#91;is&#93; this—the youth, Abner?” And Abner says, “Your soul lives, O king, I have not known.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And the king says, “Ask whose son this &#91;is&#93;—the young man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:54"> And when David turns back from striking the Philistine, then Abner takes him and brings him in before Saul, and the head of the Philistine &#91;is&#93; in his hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:12; 1 Samuel 16:18–19"> and Saul says to him, “Whose son &#91;are&#93; you, O youth?” And David says, “Son of your servant Jesse, the Beth-Lehemite.”</span>
