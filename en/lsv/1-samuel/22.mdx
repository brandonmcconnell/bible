<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 57:1–11; Psalms 142:1–7; 1 Chronicles 11:15"> And David goes from there, and escapes to the cave of Adullam, and his brothers hear, and all the house of his father, and they go down to him there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; 1 Samuel 30:22–24; Psalms 72:12–14"> and every man in distress gathers themselves to him, and every man who has an exactor, and every man bitter in soul, and he is for head over them, and there are about four hundred men with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:18; 2 Samuel 15:25–26; Judges 11:29"> And David goes from there to Mizpeh of Moab and says to the king of Moab, “Please let my father and my mother come out with you, until I know what God does for me”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 11:1; 1 Chronicles 12:16; 1 Chronicles 29:29"> and he leads them before the king of Moab, and they dwell with him all the days of David’s being in the fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:25; 1 Chronicles 21:9; 1 Chronicles 29:29"> And the prophet Gad says to David, “You do not abide in a fortress, go, and you have entered the land of Judah for yourself”; and David goes and enters the forest of Hareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:9; 1 Samuel 18:10; 1 Samuel 20:33"> And Saul hears that David has become known, and the men who &#91;are&#93; with him, and Saul is abiding in Gibeah, under the grove in Ramah, and his spear &#91;is&#93; in his hand, and all his servants &#91;are&#93; standing by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 12:16; 1 Samuel 8:14–15; 1 Samuel 22:13"> And Saul says to his servants who are standing by him, “Now hear, sons of Benjamin; does the son of Jesse also give fields and vineyards to all of you? Does he appoint all of you &#91;to be&#93; heads of thousands and heads of hundreds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:3; 1 Samuel 23:21; 1 Samuel 20:13–17"> For all of you have conspired against me, and there is none uncovering my ear about my son’s cutting &#91;a covenant&#93; with the son of Jesse, and there is none of you grieving for me, and uncovering my ear, that my son has raised up my servant against me, to lie in wait as &#91;at&#93; this day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 52:1–9; 1 Samuel 14:3; Ezekiel 22:9"> And Doeg the Edomite answers, who is set over the servants of Saul, and says, “I have seen the son of Jesse coming to Nob, to Ahimelech son of Ahitub,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 27:21; 1 Samuel 30:8; 1 Samuel 23:4"> and he inquires of YHWH for him, and has given provision to him, and has given the sword of Goliath the Philistine to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:15"> And the king sends to call Ahimelech son of Ahitub, the priest, and all the house of his father, the priests, who &#91;are&#93; in Nob, and all of them come to the king;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:6; 1 Samuel 22:7; Isaiah 65:1"> and Saul says, “Now hear, son of Ahitub”; and he says, “Here I &#91;am&#93;, my lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:8; Luke 23:2–5; Amos 7:10"> And Saul says to him, “Why have you conspired against me, you and the son of Jesse, by your giving bread and a sword to him, and to inquire of God for him, to rise against me, to lie in wait, as &#91;at&#93; this day?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:32; 1 Samuel 19:4–5; 1 Samuel 22:13"> And Ahimelech answers the king and says, “And who among all your servants &#91;is&#93; as David—faithful, and son-in-law of the king, and has turned aside to your council, and is honored in your house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:36; 1 Peter 3:16–17; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> Today have I begun to inquire of God for him? Far be it from me! Do not let the king lay anything against his servant, against any of the house of my father, for your servant has known nothing of all this, little or much.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 28:15; Daniel 3:19–20; 1 Kings 19:2"> And the king says, “You surely die, Ahimelech, you and all the house of your father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 1:17; 1 Samuel 14:45; 2 Kings 1:13–14"> And the king says to the runners, those standing by him, “Turn around, and put the priests of YHWH to death, because their hand &#91;is&#93; also with David, and because they have known that he is fleeing, and have not uncovered my ear”; and the servants of the king have not been willing to put forth their hand to come against the priests of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:10–11; Hosea 7:3; 1 Samuel 2:18"> And the king says to Doeg, “Turn around, and come against the priests”; and Doeg the Edomite turns around, and comes against the priests himself, and in that day puts to death eighty-five men bearing a linen ephod,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Hosea 10:14; 1 Samuel 21:1"> and Nob, the city of the priests, he has struck by the mouth of the sword, from man and to woman, from infant and to suckling, and ox, and donkey, and sheep, by the mouth of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:6; 1 Samuel 30:7; 1 Kings 2:26–27"> And one son of Ahimelech, son of Ahitub, escapes, and his name &#91;is&#93; Abiathar, and he flees after David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and Abiathar declares to David that Saul has slain the priests of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:1–9; Psalms 44:22"> And David says to Abiathar, “I have known on that day when Doeg the Edomite &#91;is&#93; there, that he certainly declares &#91;it&#93; to Saul; I have brought &#91;it&#93; around to every person of the house of your father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 2:26; Hebrews 12:1–3; John 10:28–30"> dwell with me; do not fear; for he who seeks my life seeks your life; for you &#91;are my&#93; charge with me.”</span>
