<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 40:27–31; 1 Samuel 21:10–15; Isaiah 51:12"> And David says to his heart, “Now I am consumed by the hand of Saul one day; there is nothing better for me than that I diligently escape to the land of the Philistines, and Saul has been despairing of me—of seeking me anymore in all the border of Israel, and I have escaped out of his hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:10; 1 Samuel 25:13; 1 Kings 2:39–40"> And David rises, and passes over, he and six hundred men who &#91;are&#93; with him, to Achish son of Maoch king of Gath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:42–43; 1 Samuel 30:5; 1 Samuel 25:18–35"> and David dwells with Achish in Gath, he and his men, each one with his household, &#91;even&#93; David and his two wives, Ahinoam the Jezreelitess, and Abigail wife of Nabal the Carmelitess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:21"> And it is declared to Saul that David has fled to Gath, and he has not added to seek him anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 46:34; 2 Corinthians 6:17"> And David says to Achish, “Now if I have found grace in your eyes, they give a place to me in one of the cities of the field, and I dwell there, indeed, why does your servant dwell in the royal city with you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:28; Joshua 15:31; 1 Chronicles 12:20"> And Achish gives Ziklag to him in that day, therefore Ziklag has been for the kings of Judah until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:3"> And the number of the days which David has dwelt in the field of the Philistines &#91;is one year&#93; of days and four months;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:7–8; Joshua 13:2; Joshua 13:13"> and David and his men go up, and they push toward the Geshurite, and the Gerizite, and the Amalekite (for they are inhabitants of the land from of old), as you come to Shur and to the land of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Joshua 6:21; Deuteronomy 25:17–19"> and David has struck the land, and does not keep alive man and woman, and has taken sheep, and oxen, and donkeys, and camels, and garments, and turns back, and comes to Achish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 1:16; 1 Chronicles 2:9; 1 Chronicles 2:25"> And Achish says, “To where have you pushed today?” And David says, “Against the south of Judah, and against the south of the Jerahmeelite, and to the south of the Kenite.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Proverbs 12:19; 1 Samuel 22:22"> David keeps alive neither man nor woman, to bring in &#91;word&#93; to Gath, saying, “Lest they declare &#91;it&#93; against us, saying, Thus David has done, and thus &#91;is&#93; his custom all the days that he has dwelt in the fields of the Philistines.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; 1 Samuel 13:4"> And Achish believes in David, saying, “He has made himself utterly abhorred among his people in Israel, and has been for a perpetual servant to me.”</span>
