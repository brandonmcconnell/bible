<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 10:1–12; 1 Samuel 28:4; 2 Samuel 1:21"> And the Philistines are fighting against Israel, and the men of Israel flee from the face of the Philistines, and fall wounded in Mount Gilboa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:33; 1 Samuel 14:49; 1 Samuel 23:17"> and the Philistines follow Saul and his sons, and the Philistines strike Jonathan, and Abinadab, and Malchishua, the sons of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:6; Genesis 49:23; 1 Kings 22:34"> And the battle is hard against Saul, and the archers find him—men with bow—and he is greatly pained by the archers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 9:54; 2 Samuel 1:6; 2 Samuel 1:9–10"> and Saul says to the bearer of his weapons, “Draw your sword, and pierce me with it, lest they come—these uncircumcised—and have pierced me, and rolled themselves on me”; and the bearer of his weapons has not been willing, for he is greatly afraid, and Saul takes the sword, and falls on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 10:5"> And the bearer of his weapons sees that Saul &#91;is&#93; dead, and he falls—he also—on his sword, and dies with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:17; Hosea 13:10–11; 1 Chronicles 10:6"> and Saul dies, and three of his sons, and the bearer of his weapons, also all his men, together on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Leviticus 26:32; Leviticus 26:36"> And they see—the men of Israel, who &#91;are&#93; beyond the valley, and who &#91;are&#93; beyond the Jordan—that the men of Israel have fled, and that Saul and his sons have died, and they forsake the cities and flee, and Philistines come in, and dwell in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:25; 1 Chronicles 10:8"> And it comes to pass on the next day, that the Philistines come to strip the wounded, and they find Saul and his three sons fallen on Mount Gilboa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:20; Judges 16:23–24; 1 Samuel 17:54"> and they cut off his head, and strip off his weapons, and send &#91;them&#93; into the land of the surrounding Philistines, to proclaim tidings &#91;in&#93; the house of their idols, and &#91;among&#93; the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 2:13; Joshua 17:11; 1 Samuel 21:9"> and they place his weapons &#91;in&#93; the house of Ashtaroth, and they have fixed his body on the wall of Beth-Shan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1–11; 2 Samuel 2:4"> And they hear regarding it—the inhabitants of Jabesh-Gilead—that which the Philistines have done to Saul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:14; Jeremiah 34:5; 2 Samuel 2:4–7"> and all the men of valor arise, and go all the night, and take the body of Saul, and the bodies of his sons, from the wall of Beth-Shan, and come to Jabesh, and burn them there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:12–14; Genesis 50:10; 1 Samuel 22:6"> and they take their bones, and bury &#91;them&#93; under the tamarisk in Jabesh, and fast seven days.</span>
