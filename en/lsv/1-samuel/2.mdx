<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 1:46–56; Psalms 13:5; Isaiah 12:2–3"> And Hannah prays and says: “My heart has exulted in YHWH, &#124;&#124; My horn has been high in YHWH, &#124;&#124; My mouth has been large over my enemies, &#124;&#124; For I have rejoiced in Your salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:32; Isaiah 44:6; Psalms 86:8"> There is none holy like YHWH, &#124;&#124; For there is none except You, &#124;&#124; And there is no rock like our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 8:13; 1 Kings 8:39; Proverbs 24:12"> You do not multiply—you speak haughtily—The old saying goes out from your mouth, &#124;&#124; For YHWH &#91;is&#93; a God of knowledge, &#124;&#124; And actions are weighed by Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 76:3; Psalms 37:15; Psalms 46:9"> Bows of the mighty are broken, &#124;&#124; And the stumbling have girded on strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:9; Luke 1:53; Psalms 113:9"> The satiated hired themselves for bread, &#124;&#124; And the hungry have ceased. While the barren has borne seven, &#124;&#124; And she abounding with sons has languished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Isaiah 26:19; Revelation 1:18"> YHWH puts to death, and keeps alive, &#124;&#124; He brings down to Sheol, and brings up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 75:7; Job 5:11; Deuteronomy 8:17–18"> YHWH dispossesses, and He makes rich, &#124;&#124; He makes low, indeed, He makes high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Daniel 2:48; James 2:5"> He raises the poor from the dust, &#124;&#124; He lifts up the needy from a dunghill, &#124;&#124; To cause &#91;them&#93; to sit with nobles, &#124;&#124; Indeed, He causes them to inherit a throne of glory, &#124;&#124; For the fixtures of earth &#91;are&#93; of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And He sets the habitable world on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 121:3; Psalms 91:11–12; Psalms 33:16–17"> He keeps the feet of His saints, &#124;&#124; And the wicked are silent in darkness, &#124;&#124; For man does not become mighty by power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 2:9; Psalms 89:24; Psalms 18:13–14"> YHWH—His adversaries are broken down, &#124;&#124; He thunders against them in the heavens: YHWH judges the ends of the earth, &#124;&#124; And gives strength to His king, &#124;&#124; And exalts the horn of His anointed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:1; 1 Samuel 2:18; 1 Samuel 1:28"> And Elkanah goes to Ramath, to his house, and the youth has been serving YHWH &#91;in&#93; the presence of Eli the priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 1:28–30; Jeremiah 2:8; Judges 2:10"> and the sons of Eli &#91;are&#93; sons of worthlessness, they have not known YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 7:29–34"> And the custom of the priests with the people &#91;is that when&#93; any man is sacrificing a sacrifice, then the servant of the priest has come in when the flesh is boiling, and &#91;with&#93; the hook of three teeth in his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 29:27–28; Isaiah 56:11; 1 Samuel 2:29"> and has struck &#91;it&#93; into the pan, or kettle, or cauldron, or pot; all that the hook brings up the priest takes for himself; thus they do to all Israel who are coming in there in Shiloh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 3:16; Leviticus 3:3–5; Romans 16:18"> Also, before they make incense with the fat, then the priest’s servant has come in and said to the man who is sacrificing, “Give flesh to roast for the priest, and he does not take boiled flesh from you, but raw”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 7:23–25; Leviticus 3:16; 1 Peter 5:2–3"> and the man says to him, “Let them surely make incense with the fat according to the &#91;custom&#93; today, then take to yourself as your soul desires”; and he has said to him, “Surely you give now; and if not—I have taken by strength.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 2:7–9; Genesis 13:13; Malachi 2:13"> And the sin of the young men is very great &#91;in&#93; the presence of YHWH, for the men have despised the offering of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:1; Exodus 28:4; 1 Samuel 2:11"> And Samuel is ministering &#91;in&#93; the presence of YHWH, a youth girt &#91;with&#93; an ephod of linen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:3; 1 Samuel 1:21; Exodus 23:14"> and his mother makes a small upper coat for him, and she has brought it up to him from time to time, in her coming up with her husband to sacrifice the sacrifice of the time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:27–28; Luke 2:34; Ruth 4:11"> And Eli blessed Elkanah and his wife, and said, “YHWH appoints seed of this woman for you, for the petition which she asked for YHWH”; and they have gone to their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 2:40; 1 Samuel 3:19; Genesis 21:1"> When YHWH has looked after Hannah, then she conceives and bears three sons and two daughters; and the youth Samuel grows up with YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 38:8; 1 Samuel 2:13–17; Jeremiah 7:9–10"> And Eli &#91;is&#93; very old, and has heard all that his sons do to all Israel, and how that they lie with the women who are assembling &#91;at&#93; the opening of the Tent of Meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:12; Philippians 3:19; Acts 9:4"> and he says to them, “Why do you do things like these? For I am hearing of your evil words from all the people—these!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Malachi 2:8; 1 Timothy 3:7; Acts 6:3"> No, my sons; for the report which I am hearing is not good, causing the people of YHWH to transgress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:14; Joshua 11:20; Hebrews 10:26"> If a man sins against a man, then God has judged him; but if a man sins against YHWH, who prays for him?” And they do not listen to the voice of their father, though YHWH has delighted to put them to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 2:52; Luke 2:40; Acts 2:47"> And the youth Samuel is going on and growing up, and &#91;is&#93; good with both YHWH, and also with men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; Exodus 4:27; Exodus 4:14–16"> And a man of God comes to Eli and says to him, “Thus said YHWH: Was I really revealed to the house of your father in their being in Egypt, before Pharaoh’s house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 28:1; Leviticus 8:7–8; Numbers 18:19"> even to choose him out of all the tribes of Israel for a priest for Myself, to go up on My altar, to make incense, to bear an ephod before Me, and I give to the house of your father all the fire-offerings of the sons of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Deuteronomy 32:15; 1 Samuel 2:13–17"> Why do you kick at My sacrifice and at My offering, which I commanded &#91;in&#93; My habitation, and honor your sons above Me, to make yourselves fat from the first part of every offering of Israel, of My people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:26; Jeremiah 18:9–10; Proverbs 3:9–10"> Therefore—a declaration of YHWH, God of Israel—I certainly said, Your house and the house of your father walk up and down before Me for all time; and now—a declaration of YHWH—Far be it from Me! For he who is honoring Me, I honor, and those despising Me, are lightly esteemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:17–20; 1 Kings 2:26–27; Psalms 37:17"> Behold, days &#91;are&#93; coming, and I have cut off your arm, and the arm of the house of your father, that an old man is not in your house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Zechariah 8:4; 1 Samuel 4:11; Psalms 78:59–64"> and you have beheld an adversary &#91;in My&#93; habitation, in all that He does good with Israel, and there is not an old man in your house all the days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 2:26–27; 1 Kings 1:7; 1 Samuel 22:21–23"> And the man of yours I do not cut off from My altar—&#91;My purpose is&#93; to consume your eyes, and to grieve your soul; and all the increase of men &#91;in&#93; your house die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:11; 1 Kings 13:3; 1 Samuel 4:17"> and this &#91;is&#93; the sign to you that comes to your two sons, to Hophni and Phinehas—in one day both of them die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:27; 2 Samuel 7:11; 1 Kings 11:38"> and I have raised up a steadfast priest for Myself; he does as in My heart and in My soul; and I have built a steadfast house for him, and he has walked up and down before My anointed all the days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 2:27; Malachi 1:13; Ezekiel 44:10–12"> and it has been, everyone who is left in your house comes to bow himself to him for a wage of silver and a cake of bread, and has said, Please admit me to one of the priest’s offices, to eat a morsel of bread.”</span>
