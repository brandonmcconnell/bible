<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:12; 1 Kings 20:34; Ezekiel 17:13"> And Nahash the Ammonite comes up, and encamps against Jabesh-Gilead, and all the men of Jabesh say to Nahash, “Make a covenant with us, and we serve you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:26; Genesis 34:14; Numbers 16:14"> And Nahash the Ammonite says to them, “For this I cut &#91;a covenant&#93; with you, in picking out every right eye of yours—and I have set it &#91;for&#93; a reproach on all Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And &#91;the&#93; elderly of Jabesh say to him, “Leave us alone &#91;for&#93; seven days, and we send messengers into all the border of Israel: and if there is none saving us—then we have come out to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 2:4; 1 Samuel 10:26; 1 Samuel 15:34"> And the messengers come to Gibeah of Saul, and speak the words in the ears of the people, and all the people lift up their voice and weep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 19:19; Judges 18:23; Genesis 21:17"> and behold, Saul has come out of the field after the herd, and Saul says, “What &#91;is that&#93; to the people, that they weep?” And they recount the words of the men of Jabesh to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:10; 1 Samuel 16:13; Judges 6:34"> And the Spirit of God prospers over Saul, in his hearing these words, and his anger burns greatly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:29; Judges 20:1; Judges 21:5–11"> and he takes a couple of oxen, and cuts them in pieces, and sends &#91;them&#93; through all the border of Israel, by the hand of the messengers, saying, “He who is not coming out after Saul and after Samuel—thus it is done to his oxen”; and the fear of YHWH falls on the people, and they come out as one man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 1:4–5; 2 Samuel 24:9; 2 Chronicles 17:12–19"> And he inspects them in Bezek, and the sons of Israel are three hundred thousand, and the men of Judah thirty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 18:17"> And they say to the messengers who are coming, “Thus you say to the men of Jabesh-Gilead: Tomorrow you have safety—by the heat of the sun”; and the messengers come and declare &#91;it&#93; to the men of Jabesh, and they rejoice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:2–3"> and the men of Jabesh say &#91;to the Ammonites&#93;, “Tomorrow we come out to you, and you have done to us according to all that &#91;is&#93; good in your eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 7:16; James 2:13; Exodus 14:24"> And it comes to pass, on the next day, that Saul puts the people in three detachments, and they come into the midst of the camp in the morning-watch, and strike Ammon until the heat of the day; and it comes to pass that those left are scattered, and there have not been left two of them together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:27; Luke 19:27; Psalms 21:8"> And the people say to Samuel, “Who is he that says, Saul reigns over us? Give up the men, and we put them to death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:22; 1 Samuel 19:5; Exodus 14:13"> And Saul says, “There is no man put to death on this day, for today YHWH has worked salvation in Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:8; 1 Samuel 7:16; 1 Chronicles 12:38–39"> And Samuel says to the people, “Come and we go to Gilgal, and renew the kingdom there”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:8; 1 Samuel 10:17; 1 Chronicles 29:21–24"> and all the people go to Gilgal, and cause Saul to reign there before YHWH in Gilgal, and sacrifice sacrifices of peace-offerings there before YHWH, and Saul rejoices there—and all the men of Israel—very greatly.</span>
