<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 8:11–12; Psalms 74:9; 1 Samuel 3:21"> And the youth Samuel is serving YHWH before Eli, and the word of YHWH has been precious in those days—there is no vision breaking forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:15; Psalms 90:10; Genesis 27:1"> And it comes to pass, at that time, that Eli is lying down in his place, and his eyes have begun &#91;to be&#93; faded—he is not able to see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Leviticus 24:2–4; Exodus 27:20–21"> And the lamp of God is not yet extinguished, and Samuel is lying down in the temple of YHWH, where the Ark of God &#91;is&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 6:8; 1 Corinthians 12:6–11; Exodus 3:4"> and YHWH calls to Samuel, and he says, “Here I &#91;am&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he runs to Eli and says, “Here I &#91;am&#93;, for you have called for me”; and he says, “I did not call; turn back, lie down”; and he goes and lies down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; 1 Samuel 4:16; 2 Samuel 18:22"> And YHWH adds to call Samuel again, and Samuel rises and goes to Eli, and says, “Here I &#91;am&#93;, for you have called for me”; and he says, “I have not called, my son, turn back, lie down.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:2; Jeremiah 9:24"> And Samuel has not yet known YHWH, and the word of YHWH is not yet revealed to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 33:14–15; 1 Corinthians 13:11–12"> And YHWH adds to call Samuel the third time, and he rises and goes to Eli, and says, “Here I &#91;am&#93;, for you have called for me”; and Eli understands that YHWH is calling to the youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Isaiah 6:8; Psalms 85:8"> And Eli says to Samuel, “Go, lie down, and it has been, if He calls to you, that you have said, Speak, YHWH, for Your servant is hearing”; and Samuel goes and lies down in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:8; 1 Samuel 3:4–6"> And YHWH comes, and stations Himself, and calls as time by time, “Samuel, Samuel”; and Samuel says, “Speak, for Your servant is hearing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; Jeremiah 19:3; 2 Kings 21:12"> And YHWH says to Samuel, “Behold, I am doing a thing in Israel, at which the two ears of everyone hearing it tingle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:27–36; Zechariah 1:6; Joshua 23:15"> In that day I establish to Eli all that I have spoken to his house, beginning and completing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 19:18; Matthew 10:37; 1 Samuel 2:12"> and I have declared to him that I am judging his house for all time, for the iniquity which he has known, for his sons are making themselves vile, and he has not restrained them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 22:14; 1 Samuel 2:25; Jeremiah 15:1"> and therefore I have sworn to the house of Eli: the iniquity of the house of Eli is not atoned for, by sacrifice, and by offering—for all time.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:6–8; Malachi 1:10; 1 Samuel 1:9"> And Samuel lies until the morning, and opens the doors of the house of YHWH, and Samuel is afraid of declaring the vision to Eli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Eli calls Samuel and says, “Samuel, my son”; and he says, “Here I &#91;am&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:35; Ruth 1:17; Psalms 141:5"> And he says, “What &#91;is&#93; the word which He has spoken to you? Please do not hide it from me; so God does to you, and so does He add, if you hide from me a word of all the words that He has spoken to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 2:10; Isaiah 39:8; Judges 10:15"> And Samuel declares to him the whole of the words, and has not hid from him; and he says, “It &#91;is&#93; YHWH; that which is good in His eyes He does.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 39:2; 1 Samuel 9:6; 1 Kings 8:56"> And Samuel grows up, and YHWH has been with him, and has not let any of his words fall to the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 2 Samuel 3:10; 1 Timothy 1:12"> and all Israel knows, from Dan even to Beer-Sheba, that Samuel is established for a prophet to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:1; Genesis 12:7; 1 Samuel 3:4"> And YHWH adds to appear in Shiloh, for YHWH has been revealed to Samuel, in Shiloh, by the word of YHWH.</span>
