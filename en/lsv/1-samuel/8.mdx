<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:18–19; 1 Timothy 5:21; Nehemiah 7:2"> And it comes to pass, when Samuel &#91;is&#93; aged, that he makes his sons judges over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:38; Genesis 22:19; 1 Chronicles 6:28"> And the name of his firstborn son is Joel, and the name of his second Abiah, judges in Beer-Sheba:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Psalms 15:5; 1 Timothy 6:10"> and his sons have not walked in his ways, and turn aside after the dishonest gain, and take a bribe, and turn aside judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:17; 2 Samuel 5:3; Exodus 3:16"> And all &#91;the&#93; elderly of Israel gather themselves together, and come to Samuel at Ramath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:14–15; Hosea 13:10–11; 1 Samuel 8:6–8"> and say to him, “Behold, you have become aged, and your sons have not walked in your ways; now, appoint a king to us, to judge us, like all the nations.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:11; James 1:5; 1 Samuel 12:17"> And the thing is evil in the eyes of Samuel when they have said, “Give a king to us, to judge us”; and Samuel prays to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:16; 1 Samuel 10:19; Exodus 16:8"> And YHWH says to Samuel, “Listen to the voice of the people, to all that they say to you, for they have not rejected you, but they have rejected Me from reigning over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:34–40; Exodus 32:1; Judges 13:1"> According to all the works that they have done from the day of My bringing them up out of Egypt, even to this day, when they forsake Me, and serve other gods—so they are also doing to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:25; 1 Samuel 8:11–18; Ezekiel 46:18"> And now, listen to their voice; only, surely you certainly protest to them, and have declared to them the custom of the king who reigns over them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Samuel speaks all the words of YHWH to the people who are asking &#91;for&#93; a king from him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:52; Deuteronomy 17:14–20; 1 Samuel 10:25"> and says, “This is the custom of the king who reigns over you: he takes your sons, and has appointed for himself among his chariots, and among his horsemen, and they have run before his chariots;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:7; 1 Kings 4:7; 1 Chronicles 27:1–22"> also to appoint for himself heads of thousands, and heads of fifties; also to plow his plowing, and to reap his reaping; and to make instruments of his war, and instruments of his charioteer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he takes your daughters for perfumers, and for cooks, and for bakers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:18; 1 Kings 21:7; 1 Kings 21:19"> and your fields, and your vineyards, and your olive-yards—he takes the best, and has given to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 1:3; Isaiah 39:7; Genesis 37:36"> And he tithes your seed and your vineyards, and has given to his eunuchs, and to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And your menservants and your maidservants, and the best of your young men and your donkeys, he takes, and has prepared for his own work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> he tithes your flock, and you are for servants to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:4; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 1:25–28"> And you have cried out in that day because of the king whom you have chosen for yourselves, and YHWH does not answer you in that day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16; Jeremiah 7:13; Isaiah 66:4"> And the people refuse to listen to the voice of Samuel, and say, “No, but a king is over us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:5; Psalms 106:35; Deuteronomy 7:6"> and we have been, even we, like all the nations; and our king has judged us, and gone out before us, and fought our battles.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:11"> And Samuel hears all the words of the people, and speaks them in the ears of YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Hosea 13:11"> and YHWH says to Samuel, “Listen to their voice, and you have caused a king to reign over them.” And Samuel says to the men of Israel, “Go, each man, to his city.”</span>
