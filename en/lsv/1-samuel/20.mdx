<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 2:9; 2 Corinthians 1:12; 1 John 3:21"> And David flees from Naioth in Ramah, and comes, and says before Jonathan, “What have I done? What &#91;is&#93; my iniquity? And what &#91;is&#93; my sin before your father, that he is seeking my life?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:15; John 17:8; John 15:15"> And he says to him, “Far be it! You do not die; behold, my father does not do anything great or small and does not uncover my ear; and why does my father hide this thing from me? This &#91;thing&#93; is not.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:26; Deuteronomy 6:13; 2 Kings 2:6"> And David swears again and says, “Your father has certainly known that I have found grace in your eyes, and he says, Do not let Jonathan know this, lest he is grieved; and yet, YHWH lives, and your soul lives, but—as a step between me and death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And Jonathan says to David, “What does your soul &#91;desire&#93;? Command and I do it for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; 1 Samuel 19:2; Numbers 28:11"> And David says to Jonathan, “Behold, the new moon &#91;is&#93; tomorrow; and I certainly sit with the king to eat; and you have sent me away, and I have been hidden in a field until the third evening;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:58; 1 Samuel 9:12; Deuteronomy 12:5"> if your father looks after me at all, then you have said, David earnestly asked of me to run to his city of Beth-Lehem, for there &#91;is&#93; a sacrifice of the days for all the family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:17; Esther 7:7; Deuteronomy 1:23"> If thus he says: Good; &#91;there is&#93; peace for your servant; and if it is very displeasing to him—know that the evil has been determined by him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:32; 1 Samuel 18:3; 1 Samuel 23:18"> and you have done kindness to your servant, for you have brought your servant into a covenant of YHWH with you—and if there is iniquity in me, put me to death yourself; for why &#91;is&#93; this &#91;that&#93; you bring me to your father?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:42; 1 Samuel 19:2; 1 Samuel 20:38"> And Jonathan says, “Far be it from you! For I certainly do not know that the evil has been determined by my father to come on you, and I do not declare it to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:14; 1 Samuel 25:17; Genesis 42:30"> And David says to Jonathan, “Who declares &#91;it&#93; to me? Or what &#91;if&#93; your father answers you sharply?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Jonathan says to David, “Come, and we go out into the field”; and both of them go out into the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 22:22; Job 31:4; Proverbs 25:2–3"> And Jonathan says to David, “YHWH, God of Israel, &#91;is my witness&#93;—when I search out my father about &#91;this&#93; time tomorrow &#91;or&#93; the third &#91;day&#93;, and behold, &#91;there is&#93; good toward David, and I do not then send to you, and have uncovered your ear—</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ruth 1:17; 1 Chronicles 22:11; Joshua 1:5"> thus YHWH does to Jonathan, and thus He adds; when the evil concerning you is good to my father, then I have uncovered your ear, and sent you away, and you have gone in peace, and YHWH is with you, as he was with my father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:3; Ephesians 5:1–2"> and not only while I am alive do you do the kindness of YHWH with me, and I do not die,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:7; 1 Samuel 24:21; 2 Samuel 9:1–7"> but you do not cut off your kindness from my house for all time, nor in YHWH’s cutting off the enemies of David, each one from off the face of the ground.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:22; 1 Samuel 18:3; 2 Samuel 21:8"> So Jonathan cuts &#91;a covenant&#93; with the house of David, and YHWH has sought &#91;it&#93; from the hand of the enemies of David;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 18:24; 1 Samuel 18:1; 1 Samuel 18:3"> and Jonathan adds to cause David to swear, because he loves him, for he has loved him &#91;as&#93; the love &#91;for&#93; his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:25; 1 Samuel 20:5"> And Jonathan says to him, “Tomorrow &#91;is&#93; the new moon, and you have been looked after, for your seat is looked after;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:2; 1 Samuel 20:5"> and on the third day you certainly come down, and have come to the place where you were hidden in the day of the work, and have remained near the stone of Ezel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And I shoot three of the arrows at the side, sending out at a mark for myself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:2; Amos 8:14; Jeremiah 12:16"> and behold, I send the youth, &#91;saying&#93;, Go, find the arrows. If I say to the youth at all, Behold, the arrows &#91;are&#93; on this side of you—take them and come, for &#91;there is&#93; peace for you, and there is no &#91;adverse&#93; word—&#91;as&#93; YHWH lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:37"> And if thus I say to the young man, Behold, the arrows &#91;are&#93; beyond you—go, for YHWH has sent you away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:14–15; 1 Samuel 20:42; Genesis 31:50"> as for the thing which you and I have spoken, behold, YHWH &#91;is&#93; between me and you for all time.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 4:17; Isaiah 1:11–15; Proverbs 21:3"> And David is hidden in the field, and it is the new moon, and the king sits down by the food to eat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:18; Judges 16:20"> and the king sits on his seat, as time by time, on a seat by the wall, and Jonathan rises, and Abner sits at the side of Saul, and David’s place is looked after.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Leviticus 11:24; Leviticus 11:27"> And Saul has not spoken anything on that day, for he said, “It &#91;is&#93; an accident; he is not clean—surely not clean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4; 1 Samuel 19:15; 1 Samuel 18:11"> And it comes to pass on the second day of the new moon, that David’s place is looked after, and Saul says to his son Jonathan, “Why has the son of Jesse not come in, either yesterday or today, to the food?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:6"> And Jonathan answers Saul, “David has earnestly asked &#91;permission&#93; from me &#91;to go&#93; to Beth-Lehem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:28"> and he says, Please send me away, for we have a family sacrifice in the city, and my brother has given command to me himself, and now, if I have found grace in your eyes, please let me go away and see my brothers; therefore he has not come to the table of the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 21:24; James 1:19–20; Proverbs 25:28"> And the anger of Saul burns against Jonathan, and he says to him, “Son of a perverse &#91;woman&#93; of rebellion! Have I not known that you are fixing on the son of Jesse to your own shame, and to the shame of the nakedness of your mother?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:6; 1 Samuel 20:8; 1 Samuel 19:11–15"> For all the days that the son of Jesse lives on the ground you are not established, you and your kingdom; and now, send and bring him to me, for he &#91;is&#93; a son of death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 27:23; 1 Samuel 19:5; Luke 23:22"> And Jonathan answers his father Saul and says to him, “Why is he put to death? What has he done?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:7; 1 Samuel 18:11; 1 Samuel 19:10"> And Saul casts the javelin at him to strike him, and Jonathan knows that it has been determined by his father to put David to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ephesians 4:26; Mark 3:5; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> And Jonathan rises from the table in the heat of anger, and has not eaten food on the second day of the new moon, for he has been grieved for David, for his father put him to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:5; 1 Samuel 20:19"> And it comes to pass in the morning, that Jonathan goes out into the field for the appointment with David, and a little youth &#91;is&#93; with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:20–22"> And he says to his youth, “Now run, find the arrows which I am shooting”; the youth is running, and he has shot the arrow, causing &#91;it&#93; to pass over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:22"> And the youth comes to the place of the arrow which Jonathan has shot, and Jonathan calls after the youth, and says, “Is the arrow not beyond you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 55:6–9; Proverbs 6:4–5; Mark 13:14–16"> And Jonathan calls after the youth, “Speed, hurry, do not stand”; and Jonathan’s youth gathers the arrows, and comes to his lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And the youth has not known anything, only Jonathan and David knew the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And Jonathan gives his weapons to the youth whom he has and says to him, “Go, carry into the city.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Genesis 29:11; 1 Samuel 18:3; 2 Samuel 9:6"> The youth has gone, and David has risen from Ezel, at the south, and falls on his face to the earth, and bows himself three times, and they kiss one another, and they weep with one another, until David exerted himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:17; 1 Samuel 23:18; 1 Samuel 20:22–23"> and Jonathan says to David, “Go in peace, in that we have sworn—the two of us—in the Name of YHWH, saying, YHWH is between me and you, and between my seed and your seed—for all time”; and he rises and goes; and Jonathan has gone into the city.</span>
