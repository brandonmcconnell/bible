<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:3–5; Genesis 35:19–20; Joshua 18:28"> In your going from me today—then you have found two men by the grave of Rachel, in the border of Benjamin, at Zelzah, and they have said to you, The donkeys have been found which you have gone to seek; and behold, your father has left the matter of the donkeys, and has sorrowed for you, saying, What do I do for my son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 35:3; Genesis 35:1; Genesis 28:22"> And you have passed on there, and beyond, and have come to the oak of Tabor, and three men going up to God to Beth-El have found you there, one carrying three kids, and one carrying three cakes of bread, and one carrying a bottle of wine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 18:15"> and they have asked of your welfare, and given two loaves to you, and you have received from their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 3:15; 1 Samuel 13:3; 1 Samuel 19:20"> Afterward you come to the hill of God, where the garrison of the Philistines &#91;is&#93;, and it comes to pass, at your coming in there to the city, that you have met a band of prophets coming down from the high place, and before them stringed instrument, and tambourine, and pipe, and harp, and they are prophesying;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:10; 1 Samuel 19:23–24; Numbers 11:25"> and the Spirit of YHWH has prospered over you, and you have prophesied with them, and have turned into a &#91;new&#93; man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 6:12; Joshua 1:5; Isaiah 7:14"> and it has been, when these signs come to you—do for yourself as your hand finds, for God &#91;is&#93; with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:14–15; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Samuel 13:8–15"> And you have gone down before me to Gilgal, and behold, I am going down to you, to cause burnt-offerings to ascend, to sacrifice sacrifices of peace-offerings; you wait &#91;for&#93; seven days until my coming to you, and I have made known to you that which you do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 38:7–8; 1 Samuel 10:2–6; Judges 7:11"> And it has been, at his turning his shoulder to go from Samuel, that God turns to him another heart, and all these signs come on that day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5–6; 1 Samuel 19:20–24"> and they come in there to the height, and behold, a band of prophets &#91;is there&#93; to meet him, and the Spirit of God prospers over him, and he prophesies in their midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:24; John 7:15; Matthew 13:54–55"> And it comes to pass, all knowing him before, see, and behold, he has prophesied with prophets, and the people say to one another, “What &#91;is&#93; this &#91;that&#93; has happened to the son of Kish? Is Saul also among the prophets?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 54:13; John 6:45; James 1:17"> And a man there answers and says, “And who &#91;is&#93; their father?” Therefore it has been for an allegory, “Is Saul also among the prophets?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he ceases from prophesying, and comes to the high place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:50; 2 Kings 5:25; 1 Samuel 9:3–10"> and the uncle of Saul says to him, and to his young man, “To where did you go?” And he says, “To seek the donkeys; and we see that they are not, and we come to Samuel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the uncle of Saul says, “Please declare to me what Samuel said to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:11; Judges 14:6; 1 Samuel 9:20"> And Saul says to his uncle, “He certainly declared to us that the donkeys were found”; and of the matter of the kingdom he has not declared to him that which Samuel said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 1 Samuel 7:5–6"> And Samuel calls the people to YHWH to Mizpeh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 6:8–9; Nehemiah 9:27–28; Judges 2:1"> and says to the sons of Israel, “Thus said YHWH, God of Israel: I have brought up Israel out of Egypt, and I deliver you out of the hand of the Egyptians, and out of the hand of all the kingdoms who are oppressing you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:12; 1 Samuel 8:19; Joshua 24:1"> and you have rejected your God today, who &#91;is&#93; Himself your Savior out of all your evils and your distresses, and you say, No, but you set a king over us; and now, station yourselves before YHWH, by your tribes, and by your thousands.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 7:16–18; Acts 1:24–26; 1 Samuel 14:41"> And Samuel brings all the tribes of Israel near, and the tribe of Benjamin is captured,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and he brings the tribe of Benjamin near by its families, and the family of Matri is captured, and Saul son of Kish is captured, and they seek him, and he has not been found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 27:21; Judges 20:18; 1 Samuel 23:2–4"> And they ask again of YHWH, “Has the man come here yet?” And YHWH says, “Behold, he has been hidden near the vessels.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:2; 1 Samuel 16:7; 1 Samuel 17:4"> And they run and bring him there, and he stationed himself in the midst of the people, and he is higher than any of the people from his shoulder and upward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 1:25; 1 Kings 1:39; 2 Samuel 21:6"> And Samuel says to all the people, “Have you seen him on whom YHWH has fixed, for there is none like him among all the people?” And all the people shout, and say, “Let the king live!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11–18; Deuteronomy 17:14–20; Romans 13:1–7"> And Samuel speaks to the people the right of the kingdom, and writes in a scroll, and places &#91;it&#93; before YHWH; and Samuel sends all the people away, each to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:4; Joshua 18:28; 1 Samuel 15:34"> And Saul has also gone to his house, to Gibeah, and the force goes with him whose heart God has touched;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:13; 2 Chronicles 17:5; 1 Kings 10:25"> and the sons of worthlessness have said, “How can this one save us?” And they despise him, and have not brought a present to him; and he is as one being deaf.</span>
