<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:19; Psalms 54:1–7; Joshua 15:24"> And the Ziphites come to Saul at Gibeah, saying, “Is David not hiding himself in the height of Hachilah, on the front of the desert?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:2; 1 Samuel 13:2; Psalms 140:4–9"> And Saul rises, and goes down to the wilderness of Ziph, and with him &#91;are&#93; three thousand men, chosen ones of Israel, to seek David in the wilderness of Ziph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:19; 1 Samuel 26:1"> And Saul encamps in the height of Hachilah, which &#91;is&#93; on the front of the desert, by the way, and David is abiding in the wilderness, and he sees that Saul has come into the wilderness after him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; Joshua 2:1"> and David sends spies, and knows that Saul has come to Nachon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:55; 1 Samuel 17:20; 1 Samuel 14:50–51"> and David rises, and comes to the place where Saul has encamped, and David sees the place where Saul has lain, and Abner son of Ner, head of his host, and Saul is lying in the path, and the people are encamping around him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 7:10–11; 2 Samuel 2:18; Genesis 15:20"> And David answers and says to Ahimelech the Hittite, and to Abishai son of Zeruiah, brother of Joab, saying, “Who goes down with me to Saul, to the camp?” And Abishai says, “I go down with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–3"> And David comes—and Abishai—by night to the people, and behold, Saul is lying sleeping in the path, and his spear is pressed into the earth by his pillow, and Abner and the people are lying around him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:4; 1 Samuel 26:23; Deuteronomy 32:30"> And Abishai says to David, “God has shut up your enemy into your hand today; and now, please let me strike him with &#91;the&#93; spear, even into the earth, at once—and not repeat &#91;it&#93; to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:14; Psalms 105:15; 2 Samuel 1:16"> And David says to Abishai, “Do not destroy him; for who has put forth his hand against the anointed of YHWH and been acquitted?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 47:29; Deuteronomy 31:14; Psalms 37:13"> And David says, “YHWH lives; except YHWH strikes him, or his day comes that he has died, or he goes down into battle and has been consumed—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:6; 1 Samuel 24:12; 2 Samuel 1:14"> far be it from me, by YHWH, from putting forth my hand against the anointed of YHWH; and now, please take the spear which &#91;is&#93; by his pillow, and the jug of water, and we go away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 2:21; Genesis 15:12; Isaiah 29:10"> And David takes the spear, and the jug of water by the pillow of Saul, and they go away, and there is none seeing, and there is none knowing, and there is none awaking, for all of them are sleeping, for a deep sleep &#91;from&#93; YHWH has fallen on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:8; Judges 9:7"> And David passes over to the other side, and stands on the top of the hill far off—great &#91;is&#93; the place between them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and David calls to the people, and to Abner son of Ner, saying, “Do you not answer, Abner?” And Abner answers and says, “Who &#91;are&#93; you &#91;who&#93; have called to the king?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And David says to Abner, “Are you not a man? And who &#91;is&#93; like you in Israel? But why have you not watched over your lord the king? For one of the people had come in to destroy the king, your lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 79:11; 2 Samuel 12:5; 1 Samuel 26:9"> This thing which you have done is not good; YHWH lives, but you &#91;are&#93; sons of death, in that you have not watched over your lord, over the anointed of YHWH; and now, see where the king’s spear &#91;is&#93;, and the jug of water which &#91;is&#93; by his pillow.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:16; 1 Samuel 24:8"> And Saul discerns the voice of David and says, “Is this your voice, my son David?” And David says, “My voice, my lord, O king!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:9; 1 Samuel 24:11–14; John 10:32"> And he says, “Why &#91;is&#93; this—my lord is pursuing after his servant? For what have I done, and what evil &#91;is&#93; in my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:16; 2 Samuel 16:11; 2 Samuel 20:19"> And now, please let my lord the king hear the words of his servant: if YHWH has moved you against me, let Him accept a present; but if &#91;merely&#93; the sons of men, they &#91;are&#93; cursed before YHWH, for today they have cast me out from being admitted into the inheritance of YHWH, saying, Go, serve other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:14; Matthew 26:55; 1 Samuel 25:29"> And now, do not let my blood fall to the earth before the face of YHWH, for the king of Israel has come out to seek one flea, as &#91;one&#93; pursues the partridge in mountains.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:24; 1 Samuel 24:17; Exodus 9:27"> And Saul says, “I have sinned; return, my son David, for I do evil to you no more, because that my soul has been precious in your eyes this day; behold, I have acted foolishly, and err very greatly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And David answers and says, “Behold, the king’s spear; and let one of the young men pass over, and receive it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 62:12; 1 Samuel 24:6–7; Psalms 18:20–26"> and YHWH turns each back to his righteousness and his faithfulness, in that YHWH has given you into &#91;my&#93; hand today, and I have not been willing to put forth my hand against the anointed of YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10; Psalms 54:7"> and behold, as your soul has been great in my eyes this day, so is my soul great in the eyes of YHWH, and He delivers me out of all distress.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 24:9–10; Proverbs 26:25; 1 Samuel 24:19"> And Saul says to David, “Blessed &#91;are&#93; you, my son David, also working you work, and also prevailing you prevail.” And David goes on his way, and Saul has turned back to his place.</span>
