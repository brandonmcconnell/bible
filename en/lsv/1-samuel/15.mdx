<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:16; 1 Samuel 12:14; Psalms 2:10–11"> And Samuel says to Saul, “YHWH sent me to anoint you for king over His people, over Israel; and now, listen to the voice of the words of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:17–19; Exodus 17:8–16; Numbers 24:20"> Thus said YHWH of Hosts: I have looked after that which Amalek did to Israel, that which he laid for him in the way in his going up out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:16–18; Numbers 24:20; Numbers 31:17"> Now go, and you have struck Amalek, and devoted all that he has, and you have no pity on him, and have put to death from man to woman, from infant to suckling, from ox to sheep, from camel to donkey.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 15:24; 1 Samuel 11:8; 1 Samuel 13:15"> And Saul summons the people, and inspects them in Telaim, two hundred thousand footmen, and ten thousand &#91;are&#93; men of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Saul comes to a city of Amalek, and lays wait in a valley;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Numbers 10:29–32; Exodus 18:19"> and Saul says to the Kenite, “Go, turn aside, go down from the midst of Amalek, lest I consume you with it, and you did kindness with all the sons of Israel, in their going up out of Egypt”; and the Kenite turns aside from the midst of Amalek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 16:7; Genesis 25:18; 1 Samuel 14:48"> And Saul strikes Amalek from Havilah &#91;to&#93; your going to Shur, which &#91;is&#93; on the front of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:1; Esther 3:1; Numbers 24:7"> and he catches Agag king of Amalek alive, and he has devoted all the people by the mouth of the sword;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:15; 1 Samuel 15:19; 1 Samuel 15:3"> and Saul has pity—also the people—on Agag, and on the best of the flock, and of the herd, and of the seconds, and on the lambs, and on all that &#91;is&#93; good, and have not been willing to devote them; and all the work, despised and wasted—it they devoted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the word of YHWH is to Samuel, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:13; Genesis 6:6; 2 Samuel 24:16"> “I have regretted that I caused Saul to reign for king, for he has turned back from after Me, and he has not performed My words”; and it is displeasing to Samuel, and he cries to YHWH all the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:55; 2 Samuel 18:18; 1 Samuel 25:2"> And Samuel rises early to meet Saul in the morning, and it is declared to Samuel, saying, “Saul has come to Carmel, and behold, he is setting up a monument to himself, and goes around, and passes over, and goes down to Gilgal.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 17:2; Ruth 3:10; Proverbs 31:31"> And Samuel comes to Saul, and Saul says to him, “Blessed &#91;are&#93; you of YHWH; I have performed the word of YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 3:13–15; Jeremiah 2:18–19; Jeremiah 2:34–37"> And Samuel says, “And what &#91;is&#93; the noise of this flock in my ears—and the noise of the herd which I am hearing?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:21; 1 Samuel 15:9; Exodus 32:22–23"> And Saul says, “They have brought them from Amalek, because the people had pity on the best of the flock, and of the herd, in order to sacrifice to your God YHWH, and we have devoted the remnant.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:27; 1 Kings 22:16; 1 Samuel 12:7"> And Samuel says to Saul, “Desist, and I declare to you that which YHWH has spoken to me tonight”; and he says to him, “Speak.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:22; 1 Samuel 9:21; 1 Samuel 15:1–3"> And Samuel says, “Are you not, if you &#91;are&#93; little in your own eyes, head of the tribes of Israel? And YHWH anoints you for king over Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:29; Genesis 13:13; Genesis 15:16"> and YHWH sends you in the way, and says, Go, and you have devoted the sinners, the Amalekite, and fought against them until they are consumed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:32; 2 Chronicles 33:6; 2 Chronicles 36:12"> and why have you not listened to the voice of YHWH—and fly to the spoil, and do evil in the eyes of YHWH?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:13; Matthew 19:20; Romans 10:3"> And Saul says to Samuel, “Because—I have listened to the voice of YHWH, and I go in the way which YHWH has sent me, and bring in Agag king of Amalek, and I have devoted Amalek;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:15; Exodus 32:22–23; Genesis 3:13"> and the people take of the spoil of the flock and herd, the first part of the devoted thing, for sacrifice to your God YHWH in Gilgal.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Psalms 51:16–17; Proverbs 21:3"> And Samuel says, “Has YHWH had delight in burnt-offerings and sacrifices as &#91;in&#93; listening to the voice of YHWH? Behold, listening is better than sacrifice; to give attention than fat of rams;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:16; 1 Samuel 13:14; Leviticus 20:6"> for rebellion &#91;is as&#93; a sin of divination, and stubbornness &#91;is as&#93; iniquity and teraphim; because you have rejected the word of YHWH, He also rejects you from &#91;being&#93; king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13; Isaiah 51:12–13; Numbers 22:34"> And Saul says to Samuel, “I have sinned, for I passed over the command of YHWH, and your words; because I have feared the people, I also listen to their voice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 10:17"> and now, please bear with my sin, and return with me, and I bow myself to YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; 1 Samuel 13:14; 1 Samuel 16:1"> And Samuel says to Saul, “I do not return with you; for you have rejected the word of YHWH, and YHWH rejects you from being king over Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 11:30–31"> And Samuel turns around to go, and he lays hold on the skirt of his upper robe—and it is torn!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:17–18; Acts 13:22; Daniel 4:32"> And Samuel says to him, “YHWH has torn the kingdom of Israel from you today, and given it to your neighbor who is better than you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:14; Numbers 23:19; Titus 1:2"> and also, the Preeminence of Israel does not lie nor relent, for He &#91;is&#93; not a man that He should relent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 5:44; John 12:43; Isaiah 29:13"> And he says, “I have sinned; now please honor me before &#91;the&#93; elderly of my people and before Israel, and return with me; and I have bowed myself to your God YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Samuel turns back after Saul, and Saul bows himself to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 18:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Jeremiah 48:44"> and Samuel says, “Bring Agag king of Amalek to me,” and Agag comes to him daintily, and Agag says, “Surely the bitterness of death has turned aside.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Judges 1:7; Genesis 9:6; Matthew 7:2"> And Samuel says, “As your sword bereaved women—so is your mother bereaved above women”; and Samuel hews Agag in pieces before YHWH in Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:4; 1 Samuel 7:17"> And Samuel goes to Ramath, and Saul has gone to his house—to Gibeah of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:11; 1 Samuel 19:24; 1 Samuel 16:1"> And Samuel has not added to see Saul until the day of his death, for Samuel mourned for Saul, and YHWH regretted that He had caused Saul to reign over Israel.</span>
