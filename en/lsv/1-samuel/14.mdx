<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 6:27; Micah 7:5; 2 Samuel 1:25–26"> And the day comes that Jonathan son of Saul says to the young man carrying his weapons, “Come, and we pass over to the station of the Philistines, which &#91;is&#93; on the other side of this”; and he has not declared &#91;it&#93; to his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:15–16; Isaiah 10:28–29"> And Saul is abiding at the extremity of Gibeah, under the pomegranate which &#91;is&#93; in Migron, and the people who &#91;are&#93; with him &#91;are&#93; about six hundred men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:20; 1 Samuel 2:28; 1 Samuel 4:21"> and Ahiah, son of Ahitub, brother of Ichabod, son of Phinehas son of Eli priest of YHWH in Shiloh, &#91;was&#93; bearing an ephod; and the people did not know that Jonathan has gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:23"> And between the passages where Jonathan sought to pass over to the station of the Philistines &#91;is&#93; the edge of a rock on one side, and the edge of a rock on the other side, and the name of one is Bozez, and the name of the other Seneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> One edge &#91;is&#93; fixed on the north in front of Michmash, and the one on the south in front of Gibeah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Romans 8:31; Zechariah 4:6"> And Jonathan says to the young man carrying his weapons, “Come, and we pass over to the station of these uncircumcised; it may be YHWH works for us, for there is no restraint to YHWH to save by many or by few.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:3; Zechariah 8:23; 1 Samuel 10:7"> And the bearer of his weapons says to him, “Do all that &#91;is&#93; in your heart; turn for yourself; behold, I &#91;am&#93; with you, as your own heart.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 7:9–14"> And Jonathan says, “Behold, we are passing over to the men, and are revealed to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 6:36–40; Genesis 24:13–14"> if they thus say to us, Stand still until we have come to you, then we have stood in our place, and do not go up to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 24:14; 1 Samuel 10:7; Judges 7:11"> and if they thus say, Come up against us, then we have gone up, for YHWH has given them into our hand, and this &#91;is&#93; the sign to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:6; 1 Samuel 14:22; Judges 6:2"> And both of them are revealed to the station of the Philistines, and the Philistines say, “Behold, Hebrews are coming out of the holes where they have hid themselves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:43–44; 2 Samuel 5:24; Judges 7:15"> And the men of the station answer Jonathan, and the bearer of his weapons, and say, “Come up to us, and we cause you to know something.” And Jonathan says to the bearer of his weapons, “Come up after me, for YHWH has given them into the hand of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Joshua 23:10; Leviticus 26:7–8"> And Jonathan goes up on his hands, and on his feet, and the bearer of his weapons after him; and they fall before Jonathan, and the bearer of his weapons is putting &#91;them&#93; to death after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the first striking which Jonathan and the bearer of his weapons have struck is of about twenty men, in about half a furrow of a yoke of a field,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:17; Genesis 35:5; 2 Samuel 5:24"> and there is a trembling in the camp, in the field, and among all the people, the station and the destroyers have trembled—even they, and the earth shakes, and it becomes a trembling of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:22–25; Isaiah 19:2; Judges 7:22"> And the watchmen of Saul in Gibeah of Benjamin see, and behold, the multitude has melted away, and it goes on, and is beaten down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Saul says to the people who &#91;are&#93; with him, “Now inspect and see; who has gone from us?” And they inspect, and behold, Jonathan and the bearer of his weapons are not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:7–8; 1 Samuel 5:2; Judges 20:23"> And Saul says to Ahiah, “Bring the Ark of God near”; for the Ark of God has been with the sons of Israel on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:24; Numbers 27:21; Joshua 9:14"> And it comes to pass, while Saul spoke to the priest, that the noise which &#91;is&#93; in the camp of the Philistines goes on, going on and becoming great, and Saul says to the priest, “Remove your hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 2 Chronicles 20:23; 1 Samuel 14:16"> And Saul is called, and all the people who &#91;are&#93; with him, and they come to the battle, and behold, the sword of each has been against his neighbor—a very great destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:4; Judges 7:23"> And the Hebrews &#91;who&#93; have been with the Philistines before the day prior, who had gone up with them into the camp, have turned around, even they, to be with Israel who &#91;are&#93; with Saul and Jonathan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:6; 1 Samuel 31:7"> and all the men of Israel, who are hiding themselves in the hill-country of Ephraim, have heard that the Philistines have fled, and they pursue—even they—after them in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 14:30; 1 Samuel 13:5; Psalms 44:6–8"> And YHWH saves Israel on that day, and the battle has passed over to Beth-Aven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 6:26; Romans 10:2; Judges 1:28"> And the men of Israel have been distressed on that day, and Saul adjures the people, saying, “Cursed &#91;is&#93; the man who eats food until the evening, and I have been avenged of my enemies”; and none of the people have tasted food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Numbers 13:27; Deuteronomy 9:28"> And all &#91;those of&#93; the land have come into a forest, and there is honey on the face of the field;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:2"> and the people come into the forest, and behold, the honey dropped, and none is moving his hand to his mouth, for the people feared the oath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:29; 1 Samuel 14:43; 1 Samuel 30:12"> And Jonathan has not heard of his father’s adjuring the people, and puts forth the end of the rod, which &#91;is&#93; in his hand, and dips it in the honeycomb, and brings back his hand to his mouth—and his eyes see!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:43; 1 Samuel 14:24"> And a man of the people answers and says, “Your father certainly adjured the people, saying, Cursed &#91;is&#93; the man who eats food today; and the people are weary.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 18:18; Joshua 7:25"> And Jonathan says, “My father has troubled the land; now see that my eyes have become bright because I tasted a little of this honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:18"> How much more if the people had well eaten today of the spoil of its enemies which it has found, for now the striking has not been great among the Philistines.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 10:12; Joshua 19:42"> And they strike on that day among the Philistines from Michmash to Aijalon, and the people are very weary,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:19; Leviticus 3:17; Leviticus 17:10–14"> and the people make toward the spoil, and take sheep, and oxen, and sons of the herd, and slaughter on the earth, and the people eat with the blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Romans 2:1; Matthew 7:5"> And they declare to Saul, saying, “Behold, the people are sinning against YHWH, to eat with the blood.” And he says, “You have dealt treacherously, roll a great stone to me today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And Saul says, “Be scattered among the people, and you have said to them: Each bring his ox to me, and each his sheep; and you have slain &#91;them&#93; in this place, and eaten, and you do not sin against YHWH to eat with the blood.” And all the people bring &#91;them&#93;—each one &#91;with&#93; his ox in his hand, that night—and slaughter &#91;them&#93; there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:17; 1 Samuel 7:12; 2 Timothy 3:5"> And Saul builds an altar to YHWH; with it he has begun to build altars to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 48:1–2; Psalms 73:28; Malachi 2:7"> And Saul says, “Let us go down after the Philistines by night, and we prey on them until the morning light, and do not leave a man of them.” And they say, “Do all that is good in your eyes.” And the priest says, “Let us draw near to God here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:6; 1 Samuel 30:7–8; 1 Samuel 23:9–12"> And Saul asks of God, “Do I go down after the Philistines? Do You give them into the hand of Israel?” And He has not answered him on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Judges 20:2; Joshua 7:14–26; Numbers 24:17"> And Saul says, “Everyone draw near here, the chiefs of the people, and know and see in what this sin has been today;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:44; 2 Samuel 12:5; 1 Samuel 14:24"> for YHWH lives, who is saving Israel: surely if it is in my son Jonathan, surely he certainly dies”; and none is answering him out of all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:15; 1 Samuel 14:7; 1 Samuel 14:36"> And he says to all Israel, “You are on one side, and I and my son Jonathan are on another side”; and the people say to Saul, “Do that which is good in your eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 1:24; Joshua 7:16–18; 1 Samuel 10:20–21"> And Saul says to YHWH, God of Israel, “Give perfection”; and Jonathan and Saul are captured, and the people went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And Saul says, “Cast between me and my son Jonathan”; and Jonathan is captured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 7:19; 1 Samuel 14:27; Jonah 1:7–10"> And Saul says to Jonathan, “Declare to me, what have you done?” And Jonathan declares to him and says, “I certainly tasted a little honey with the end of the rod that &#91;is&#93; in my hand; behold, I die!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:39; Ruth 1:17; 1 Samuel 25:22"> And Saul says, “Thus God does, and thus does He add, for you certainly die, Jonathan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 21:18; Acts 27:34; 2 Samuel 14:11"> And the people say to Saul, “Does Jonathan die who worked this great salvation in Israel? Certainly not! YHWH lives, if there falls to the earth &#91;even one&#93; hair from his head, for with God he has worked this day”; and the people rescue Jonathan, and he has not died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And Saul goes up from after the Philistines, and the Philistines have gone to their place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:6; 1 Samuel 14:52; 2 Samuel 8:3"> and Saul captured the kingdom over Israel, and he fights all around against all his enemies, against Moab, and against the sons of Ammon, and against Edom, and against the kings of Zobah, and against the Philistines, and wherever he turns he distresses &#91;them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Exodus 17:14; 1 Samuel 15:3–7; Deuteronomy 25:19"> And he makes a force, and strikes Amalek, and delivers Israel out of the hand of its spoiler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:33; 1 Samuel 31:2; 2 Samuel 6:20–23"> And the sons of Saul are Jonathan, and Ishui, and Melchi-Shua; as for the name of his two daughters, the name of the firstborn &#91;is&#93; Merab, and the name of the younger Michal;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8; 1 Samuel 17:55; 2 Samuel 3:27"> and the name of the wife of Saul &#91;is&#93; Ahinoam, daughter of Ahimaaz; and the name of the head of his host &#91;is&#93; Abner son of Ner, uncle of Saul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:1; 1 Samuel 9:21"> and Kish &#91;is&#93; the father of Saul, and Ner, the father of Abner, &#91;is&#93; the son of Ahiel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11; 1 Samuel 8:1"> And the war is severe against the Philistines all the days of Saul; when Saul has seen any mighty man, and any son of valor, then he gathers him to himself.</span>
