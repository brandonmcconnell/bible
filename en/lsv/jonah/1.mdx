<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 11:32; Luke 11:29–30; 2 Kings 14:25"> And there is a word of YHWH to Jonah son of Amittai, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 18:20–21; Genesis 10:11; Isaiah 58:1"> “Rise, go to Nineveh, the great city, and proclaim against it that their wickedness has come up before Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 139:7–12; Genesis 4:16; Joshua 19:46"> And Jonah rises to flee to Tarshish from the face of YHWH, and goes down &#91;to&#93; Joppa, and finds a ship going &#91;to&#93; Tarshish, and he gives its fare, and goes down into it, to go with them to Tarshish from the face of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 107:23–31; Matthew 8:24–27; Exodus 10:19"> And YHWH has cast a great wind on the sea, and there is a great storm in the sea, and the ship has reckoned to be broken;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Acts 27:18–19; 1 Kings 18:26"> and the mariners are afraid, and they each cry to his god, and cast the goods that &#91;are&#93; in the ship into the sea, to make &#91;it&#93; light of them; and Jonah has gone down to the sides of the vessel, and he lies down, and is fast asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jonah 3:9; Psalms 107:28–29; Ephesians 5:14"> And the chief of the company draws near to him and says to him, “What are you &#91;doing&#93;, O sleeper? Rise, call to your God, it may be God considers Himself of us, and we do not perish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:33; Joshua 7:13–18; 1 Samuel 10:20–21"> And they each say to his neighbor, “Come, and we cast lots, and we know on whose account this evil &#91;is&#93; on us.” And they cast lots, and the lot falls on Jonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 47:3; 1 Samuel 30:13; Joshua 7:19"> And they say to him, “Now declare to us, on whose account &#91;is&#93; this evil on us? What &#91;is&#93; your occupation, and where do you come from? What &#91;is&#93; your country, seeing you are not of this people?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 146:5–6; Psalms 95:5–6; Nehemiah 9:6"> And he says to them, “I—a Hebrew, and I fear YHWH, God of the heavens, who made the sea and the dry land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jonah 1:3; Job 27:22; Joshua 7:25"> And the men fear a great fear, and say to him, “What &#91;is&#93; this you have done!” For the men have known that he is fleeing from the face of YHWH, for he has told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:1–6; Micah 6:6–7; 1 Samuel 6:2–3"> And they say to him, “What do we do to you that the sea may cease from us, for the sea is more and more turbulent?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:17; 1 Chronicles 21:17; Joshua 7:12"> And he says to them, “Lift me up, and cast me into the sea, and the sea ceases from you; for I know that on my account this great storm &#91;is&#93; on you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; Job 34:29"> And the men row to turn back to the dry land, and are not able, for the sea is more and more turbulent against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 115:3; Psalms 135:6; Ephesians 1:11"> And they cry to YHWH, and say, “Ah, now, O YHWH, please do not let us perish for this man’s life, and do not lay innocent blood on us! For You, YHWH, as You have pleased, You have done.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 8:24; Psalms 93:3–4; Psalms 107:29"> And they lift up Jonah, and cast him into the sea, and the sea ceases from its raging;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 50:14; Genesis 8:20; Daniel 6:26"> and the men fear YHWH &#91;with&#93; a great fear, and sacrifice a sacrifice to YHWH, and vow vows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 12:40; Luke 11:30; Matthew 16:4"> And YHWH appoints a great fish to swallow up Jonah, and Jonah is in the bowels of the fish three days and three nights.</span>
