<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 7:20; Ezekiel 44:20; Leviticus 21:5"> “And you, son of man, take a sharp weapon for yourself, take the barber’s razor for yourself, and you have caused &#91;it&#93; to pass over your head, and over your beard, and you have taken weighing scales for yourself, and apportioned them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:12; Leviticus 26:33; Ezekiel 4:1–8"> You burn a third part with fire in the midst of the city, at the fullness of the days of the siege; and you have taken the third part, you strike with a weapon around it; and the third part you scatter to the wind, and I draw out a weapon after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:16; Jeremiah 40:6; Jeremiah 39:10"> And you have taken there a few in number—and have bound them in your skirts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Jeremiah 41:1–18; 2 Kings 25:25"> and you take of them again, and have cast them into the midst of the fire, and have burned them in the fire—out of it comes forth a fire to all the house of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:1; Deuteronomy 4:6; Jeremiah 6:6"> Thus said Lord YHWH: “This &#91;is&#93; Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; I have set her in the midst of the nations, &#124;&#124; And the lands &#91;are&#93; around her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:10; Ezekiel 16:47–48; Nehemiah 9:16–17"> And she changes My judgments into wickedness more than the nations, &#124;&#124; And My statutes more than the lands that &#91;are&#93; around her, &#124;&#124; For they have kicked against My judgments, &#124;&#124; And My statutes—they have not walked in them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:10–11; 2 Chronicles 33:9; 2 Kings 21:9–11"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: “Because of your multiplying above the nations that &#91;are&#93; around you, &#124;&#124; You have not walked in My statutes, &#124;&#124; And you have not done My judgments, &#124;&#124; According to the judgments of the nations that &#91;are&#93; around you, you have not done.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 15:7; Jeremiah 24:9; Ezekiel 21:3"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: “Behold, I &#91;am&#93; against you, even I, &#124;&#124; And I have done judgments in your midst, &#124;&#124; Before the eyes of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 24:21; Daniel 9:12; Amos 3:2"> And I have done in you that which I have not done, &#124;&#124; And the like of which I do not do again, &#124;&#124; Because of all your abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 2:6; Deuteronomy 28:64; Ezekiel 12:14"> Therefore fathers eat sons in your midst, &#124;&#124; And sons eat their fathers, &#124;&#124; And I have done judgments in you, &#124;&#124; And have scattered all your remnant to every wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:20; Ezekiel 7:9; Ezekiel 11:18"> Therefore, &#91;as&#93; I live,” a declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; “Because you have defiled My sanctuary &#124;&#124; With all your detestable things, &#124;&#124; And with all your abominations, &#124;&#124; Therefore I also diminish you, &#124;&#124; And My eye does not pity, and I do not spare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:2; Jeremiah 15:2; Jeremiah 43:10–11"> Your third part dies by pestilence, &#124;&#124; And are consumed by famine in your midst, &#124;&#124; And the third part fall by sword around you, &#124;&#124; And the third part I scatter to every wind, &#124;&#124; And I draw out a sword after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:24; Ezekiel 36:5–6; Ezekiel 21:17"> And My anger has been completed, &#124;&#124; And I have caused My fury to rest on them, &#124;&#124; And I have been comforted, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I, YHWH, have spoken in My zeal, &#124;&#124; In My completing My fury on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:4; Nehemiah 2:17; Psalms 79:1–4"> And I give you for a ruin, &#124;&#124; And for a reproach among nations that &#91;are&#93; around you, &#124;&#124; Before the eyes of everyone passing by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:17; Jeremiah 22:8–9; 1 Kings 9:7"> And it has been a reproach and a reviling, &#124;&#124; An instruction and an astonishment, &#124;&#124; To nations that &#91;are&#93; around you, &#124;&#124; In My doing judgments in you, &#124;&#124; In anger and fury, and in furious reproofs, &#124;&#124; I, YHWH, have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16; Deuteronomy 32:23–24; Ezekiel 14:13"> In My sending the evil arrows of famine among them, &#124;&#124; That have been for destruction, &#124;&#124; That I send to destroy you, &#124;&#124; And I am adding famine on you, &#124;&#124; And I have broken your staff of bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Ezekiel 14:21; Leviticus 26:22"> And I have sent famine and evil beasts on you, &#124;&#124; And they have bereaved you, &#124;&#124; And pestilence and blood pass over on you, &#124;&#124; And I bring a sword in against you, &#124;&#124; I, YHWH, have spoken!”</span>
