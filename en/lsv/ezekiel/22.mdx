<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 58:1; Ezekiel 24:6; Ezekiel 24:9"> “And you, son of man, do you judge? Do you judge the city of blood? Then you have caused it to know all its abominations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:27; Romans 2:5; 2 Peter 2:3"> and you have said, Thus said Lord YHWH: The city is shedding blood in its midst, &#124;&#124; For the coming in of its time, &#124;&#124; And it has made idols on it for defilement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 21:16; Psalms 44:13–14; Ezekiel 22:2"> You have been guilty by your blood that you have shed, &#124;&#124; And you have been defiled by your idols that you have made, &#124;&#124; And you cause your days to draw near, &#124;&#124; And have come to your years, &#124;&#124; Therefore I have given you &#91;for&#93; a reproach to nations, &#124;&#124; And a derision to all the lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 22:2; Jeremiah 15:2–3"> The near and the far-off from you scoff at you, &#124;&#124; O defiled of name—abounding in trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:27; Isaiah 1:23; Zechariah 3:3"> Behold, princes of Israel—each according to his arm &#124;&#124; Have been in you to shed blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:16; Exodus 22:21–22; Leviticus 20:9"> They have made light of father and mother in you, &#124;&#124; They dealt oppressively to a sojourner in your midst, &#124;&#124; They oppressed fatherless and widow in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:38–39; Ezekiel 22:26; Ezekiel 20:13"> You have despised My holy things, &#124;&#124; And you have defiled My Sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 4:14; Leviticus 19:16; Hosea 4:10"> Men of slander have been in you to shed blood, &#124;&#124; And they have eaten on the mountains in you, &#124;&#124; They have done wickedness in your midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 18:19; Leviticus 18:7–8; Ezekiel 18:6"> One has uncovered the nakedness of a father in you, &#124;&#124; They humbled the menstruous woman in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 18:15; 2 Samuel 13:14; Leviticus 20:17"> And each has done abomination with the wife of his neighbor, &#124;&#124; And each has defiled his daughter-in-law through wickedness, &#124;&#124; And each has humbled his sister, his father’s daughter, in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:25; Ezekiel 23:35; Psalms 106:21"> They have taken a bribe to shed blood in you, &#124;&#124; You have taken usury and increase, &#124;&#124; And cut off your neighbor by oppression, &#124;&#124; And you have forgotten Me, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:17; Isaiah 33:15; Amos 2:6–8"> And behold, I have struck My hand, &#124;&#124; Because of your dishonest gain that you have gained, &#124;&#124; And for your blood that has been in your midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Ezekiel 17:24; Ezekiel 24:14"> Does your heart stand—are your hands strong, &#124;&#124; For the days that I am dealing with you? I, YHWH, have spoken and have done &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:8; Ezekiel 22:22; Deuteronomy 4:27"> And I have scattered you among nations, &#124;&#124; And have spread you out among lands, &#124;&#124; And consumed your uncleanness out of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Psalms 83:18; Ezekiel 7:24"> And you have been defiled in yourself &#124;&#124; Before the eyes of nations, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:119; Isaiah 1:22; Jeremiah 6:28–30"> “Son of man, the house of Israel has been for dross to Me, &#124;&#124; All of them &#91;are&#93; bronze, and tin, and iron, and lead, &#124;&#124; In the midst of a furnace—silver has been dross,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Ezekiel 24:3–6; Micah 4:12"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: Because you have all become dross, &#124;&#124; Therefore, behold, I am gathering you into the midst of Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:11–12; Isaiah 54:16; Ezekiel 24:13"> A gathering of silver, and bronze, and iron, and lead, and tin, &#124;&#124; Into the midst of a furnace—to blow fire on it, to melt it, &#124;&#124; So I gather in My anger and in My fury, &#124;&#124; And I have let rest, and have melted you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 64:7; Nahum 1:6; Psalms 50:3"> And I have heaped you up, &#124;&#124; And blown on you in the fire of My wrath, &#124;&#124; And you have been melted in its midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:8; Ezekiel 20:33; Hosea 5:10"> As the melting of silver in the midst of a furnace, &#124;&#124; So you are melted in its midst, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I, YHWH, have poured out My fury on you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:14–16; Jeremiah 2:30; 2 Chronicles 28:22"> “Son of man, say to it, You &#91;are&#93; a land, &#124;&#124; It &#91;is&#93; not cleansed nor rained on in a day of indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 6:9; Jeremiah 2:34; Ezekiel 13:19"> A conspiracy of its prophets &#91;is&#93; in its midst, &#124;&#124; As a roaring lion tearing prey; They have devoured the soul, &#124;&#124; They have taken wealth and glory, &#124;&#124; Its widows have multiplied in its midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Ezekiel 44:23; Ezekiel 22:8"> Its priests have wronged My law, &#124;&#124; And they defile My holy things, &#124;&#124; They have not made separation between holy and common, &#124;&#124; And they have not made known &#91;the difference&#93; between the unclean and the clean, &#124;&#124; And they have hidden their eyes from My Sabbaths, &#124;&#124; And I am pierced in their midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:13; Isaiah 1:23; Ezekiel 22:6"> Its princes &#91;are&#93; as wolves in its midst, &#124;&#124; Tearing prey, to shed blood, to destroy souls, &#124;&#124; For the sake of gaining dishonest gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6–7; Ezekiel 13:10–16; Jeremiah 23:25–32"> And its prophets have coated with chalk for them, &#124;&#124; Seeing a vain thing, and divining a lie for them, &#124;&#124; Saying, Thus said Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; And YHWH has not spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:7; Exodus 23:9; Exodus 22:21"> The people of the land have used oppression, &#124;&#124; And have taken plunder away violently, &#124;&#124; And have oppressed humble and needy, &#124;&#124; And oppressed the sojourner without judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 59:16; Jeremiah 5:1; Psalms 106:23"> And I seek from them a man making a wall, &#124;&#124; And standing in the breach before Me, &#124;&#124; In behalf of the land—not to destroy it, &#124;&#124; And I have found none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:43; Ezekiel 7:8–9; Ezekiel 9:10"> And I pour out My indignation on them, &#124;&#124; I have consumed them by &#91;the&#93; fire of My wrath, &#124;&#124; I have put their way on their own head, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH!”</span>
