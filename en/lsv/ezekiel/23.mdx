<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying, “Son of man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:44–46; Jeremiah 3:7–10"> Two women were daughters of one mother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 17:7; Ezekiel 16:22; Joshua 24:14"> And they go whoring in Egypt, &#124;&#124; They have gone whoring in their youth, &#124;&#124; There they have bruised their breasts, &#124;&#124; And there they have dealt with the loves of their virginity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:8; Psalms 76:2; Jeremiah 2:2–3"> And their names &#91;are&#93; Aholah the older, &#124;&#124; And Aholibah her sister, &#124;&#124; And they are Mine, and bear sons and daughters. As for their names—Samaria &#91;is&#93; Aholah, &#124;&#124; And Jerusalem &#91;is&#93; Aholibah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 5:13; 2 Kings 17:3; 2 Kings 16:7"> And Aholah goes whoring under Me, and she lusts on her lovers, on the neighboring Assyrians,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:23"> Clothed with blue—governors and prefects, &#124;&#124; All of them desirable young men, &#124;&#124; Horsemen, riding on horses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 5:3; Hosea 6:10; Ezekiel 22:3–4"> And she gives her whoredoms on them, &#124;&#124; The choice of the sons of Asshur, &#124;&#124; All of them—even all on whom she lusted, &#124;&#124; She has been defiled by all their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:19; Ezekiel 23:3; Exodus 32:4"> And she has not forsaken her whoredoms out of Egypt, &#124;&#124; For with her they lay in her youth, &#124;&#124; And they dealt with the loves of her virginity, &#124;&#124; And they pour out their whoredoms on her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 11:5; 2 Kings 15:29; 2 Kings 17:23"> Therefore I have given her into the hand of her lovers, &#124;&#124; Into the hand of sons of Asshur on whom she lusted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:29; Hosea 2:10; Jeremiah 22:8–9"> They have uncovered her nakedness, &#124;&#124; They have taken her sons and her daughters, &#124;&#124; And they have slain her by sword, &#124;&#124; And she is a name for women, &#124;&#124; And they have done judgments with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:8–11; Ezekiel 16:47–51; Ezekiel 23:4"> And her sister Aholibah sees, &#124;&#124; And she makes her unhealthy love more corrupt than she, &#124;&#124; And her whoredoms than the whoredoms of her sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 16:7–15; Ezekiel 23:23; 2 Chronicles 28:16–23"> She has lusted on sons of Asshur, &#124;&#124; Governors and prefects, &#124;&#124; Neighboring ones—clothed in perfection, &#124;&#124; Horsemen, riding on horses, &#124;&#124; All of them desirable young men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 12:1–2; 2 Kings 17:18–19; Ezekiel 23:31"> And I see that she has been defiled, &#124;&#124; One way &#91;is&#93; to them both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:10; Jeremiah 22:14; Jeremiah 50:2"> And she adds to her whoredoms, &#124;&#124; And she sees men carved on the wall, &#124;&#124; Pictures of Chaldeans, carved with vermillion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 8:18; 1 Samuel 18:4; Isaiah 22:21"> Girded with a girdle on their loins, &#124;&#124; Dyed attire spread out on their heads, &#124;&#124; The appearance of rulers—all of them, &#124;&#124; The likeness of sons of Babylon, &#124;&#124; Chaldea is the land of their birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:29; Matthew 5:28; Job 31:1"> And she lusts on them at the sight of her eyes, &#124;&#124; And sends messengers to them, to Chaldea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:28; Ezekiel 23:22; Genesis 10:10"> And sons of Babylon come in to her, &#124;&#124; To the bed of loves, &#124;&#124; And they defile her with their whoredoms, &#124;&#124; And she is defiled with them, &#124;&#124; And her soul is alienated from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 78:59; Psalms 106:40; Jeremiah 12:8"> And she reveals her whoredoms, &#124;&#124; And she reveals her nakedness, &#124;&#124; And My soul is alienated from off her, &#124;&#124; As My soul was alienated from off her sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:3; Ezekiel 20:7; Ezekiel 16:51"> And she multiplies her whoredoms, &#124;&#124; To remember the days of her youth, &#124;&#124; When she went whoring in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:15; Ezekiel 16:26; Jeremiah 5:8"> And she lusts on their lovers, &#124;&#124; Whose flesh &#91;is&#93; the flesh of donkeys, &#124;&#124; And the emission of horses—their emission.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> You look after the wickedness of your youth, &#124;&#124; In dealing out of Egypt your loves, &#124;&#124; For the sake of the breasts of your youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:37; Revelation 17:16; Jeremiah 6:22–23"> Therefore, O Aholibah, thus said Lord YHWH: Behold, I am stirring up your lovers against you, &#124;&#124; From whom your soul has been alienated, &#124;&#124; And have brought them in against you from all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:21; 2 Kings 24:2; 2 Kings 20:14–17"> Sons of Babylon, and of all Chaldea, Pekod, and Shoa, and Koa, &#124;&#124; All the sons of Asshur with them, &#124;&#124; Desirable young men, governors and prefects, &#124;&#124; All of them—rulers and proclaimed ones, &#124;&#124; All of them riding on horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:14; Ezekiel 26:10; Ezekiel 23:45"> And they have come in against you, &#124;&#124; With arms, rider, and wheel, &#124;&#124; And with an assembly of peoples; Buckler, and shield, and helmet, &#124;&#124; They set against you all around, &#124;&#124; And I have set judgment before them, &#124;&#124; They have judged you in their judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:47; Ezekiel 8:1–18; Ezekiel 15:6–7"> And I have set My jealousy against you, &#124;&#124; And they have dealt with you in fury, &#124;&#124; They turn aside your nose and your ears, &#124;&#124; And your posterity falls by sword, &#124;&#124; They take away your sons and your daughters, &#124;&#124; And your posterity is devoured by fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:39; Jeremiah 13:22; Ezekiel 23:29"> And they have stripped you of your garments, &#124;&#124; And have taken your beautiful jewels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:41; Ezekiel 23:3; Ezekiel 23:19"> And I have caused your wickedness to cease from you, &#124;&#124; And your whoredoms out of the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And you do not lift up your eyes to them, &#124;&#124; And you do not remember Egypt again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:22; Ezekiel 23:17; Jeremiah 34:20"> For thus said Lord YHWH: Behold, I am giving you into a hand that you have hated, &#124;&#124; Into a hand from which you were alienated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:39; Ezekiel 23:25–26; Ezekiel 23:45–47"> And they have dealt with you in hatred, &#124;&#124; And they have taken all your labor, &#124;&#124; And they have left you naked and bare, &#124;&#124; And the nakedness of your whoredoms has been revealed, &#124;&#124; And the wickedness of your whoredoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Psalms 106:35–38; Jeremiah 2:18–20"> To do these things to you, &#124;&#124; In your going whoring after nations, &#124;&#124; Because you have been defiled with their idols,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Jeremiah 7:14–15; Ezekiel 16:47–51"> You have walked in the way of your sister, &#124;&#124; And I have given her cup into your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 51:17; Psalms 60:3; Ezekiel 22:4–5"> Thus said Lord YHWH: You drink the cup of your sister, &#124;&#124; The deep and the wide one &#124;&#124; (You are for laughter and for scorn), &#124;&#124; Abundant to contain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 51:17; Jeremiah 25:27; Jeremiah 25:15–16"> You are filled with drunkenness and sorrow, &#124;&#124; A cup of astonishment and desolation, &#124;&#124; The cup of your sister Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 75:8; Isaiah 51:17; Revelation 18:7"> And you have drunk it, and have drained &#91;it&#93;, &#124;&#124; And you gnaw its earthen ware, &#124;&#124; And you pluck off your own breasts, &#124;&#124; For I have spoken, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 14:9; Isaiah 17:10; Jeremiah 32:33"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: Because you have forgotten Me, &#124;&#124; And you cast Me behind your back, &#124;&#124; Even you also bear your wickedness and your whoredoms.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:2; Isaiah 58:1; Ezekiel 16:2"> And YHWH says to me, “Son of man, &#124;&#124; Do you judge Aholah and Aholibah? Then declare their abominations to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:36; Jeremiah 32:35; Jeremiah 7:31"> For they have committed adultery, &#124;&#124; And blood &#91;is&#93; in their hands, &#124;&#124; They committed adultery with their idols, &#124;&#124; And also their sons whom they bore to Me, &#124;&#124; They caused to pass over to them for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:13; 2 Kings 21:7; Ezekiel 7:20"> Again, they have done this to Me, &#124;&#124; They defiled My sanctuary in that day, &#124;&#124; And they have defiled My Sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Kings 21:4; Ezekiel 44:7; Jeremiah 23:11"> And in their slaughtering their sons to their idols &#124;&#124; They also come into My sanctuary in that day to defile it, &#124;&#124; And behold, thus they have done in the midst of My house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Kings 9:30; Jeremiah 4:30; Ezekiel 16:13–16"> And also that they send to men coming from afar, &#124;&#124; To whom a messenger is sent, &#124;&#124; And behold, they have come in for whom you have washed, &#124;&#124; Painted your eyes, and put on adornment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Esther 1:6; Jeremiah 44:17; Isaiah 65:11"> And you have sat on a couch of honor, &#124;&#124; And a table arrayed before it, &#124;&#124; And placed My incense and My oil on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:11–12; Revelation 12:3; Exodus 32:18–19"> And the voice of a multitude at ease &#91;is&#93; with her, &#124;&#124; And Sabeans from the wilderness are brought in to men of the common people, &#124;&#124; And they put bracelets on their hands, &#124;&#124; And a beautiful crown on their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:3; Jeremiah 13:23; Ezra 9:7"> And I say of the worn-out one in adulteries, &#124;&#124; Now they commit her whoredoms—she also!</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:9–13; Ezekiel 23:3"> And they come in to her, &#124;&#124; As the coming in to a whorish woman, &#124;&#124; So they have come in to Aholah, &#124;&#124; And to Aholibah—the wicked women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hosea 6:5; Leviticus 20:10; Ezekiel 23:36–39"> As for righteous men, they judge them with the judgment of adulteresses, &#124;&#124; And the judgment of women shedding blood, &#124;&#124; For they &#91;are&#93; adulteresses, &#124;&#124; And blood &#91;is&#93; in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:40; Jeremiah 15:4; Jeremiah 24:9"> For thus said Lord YHWH: Bring up an assembly against them, &#124;&#124; And give them to trembling and to spoiling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:40–41; Jeremiah 39:8; Ezekiel 24:21"> And they have cast the stone of the assembly at them, &#124;&#124; And cut them with their swords, &#124;&#124; They slay their sons and their daughters, &#124;&#124; And they burn their houses with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:6; Ezekiel 23:27; Ezekiel 16:41"> And I have caused wickedness to cease from the land, &#124;&#124; And all the women have been instructed, &#124;&#124; And they do not do according to your wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:10; Ezekiel 7:4; Ezekiel 6:7"> And they have put your wickedness on you, &#124;&#124; And you bear the sins of your idols, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; Lord YHWH!”</span>
