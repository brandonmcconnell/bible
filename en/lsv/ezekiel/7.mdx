<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying, “And you, son of man, &#124;&#124; Thus said Lord YHWH to the ground of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 8:2; 1 Peter 4:7; Ezekiel 7:3"> An end, the end has come on the four corners of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Ezekiel 5:13; Ezekiel 33:20"> Now &#91;is&#93; the end for you, &#124;&#124; And I have sent My anger on you, &#124;&#124; And judged you according to your ways, &#124;&#124; And set all your abominations against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Ezekiel 11:21; Ezekiel 5:11"> And My eye has no pity on you, nor do I spare, &#124;&#124; For I set your ways against you, &#124;&#124; And your abominations are in your midst, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; 2 Kings 21:12–13; Ezekiel 5:9"> Thus said Lord YHWH: Calamity, a single calamity, behold, it has come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Ezekiel 7:3; 2 Peter 2:5"> An end has come, the end has come, &#124;&#124; It has awoken for you, behold, it has come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:28; Ezekiel 7:12; Isaiah 22:5"> The circlet has come to you, O inhabitant of the land! The time has come, a day of trouble &#91;is&#93; near, &#124;&#124; And not the shouting of mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:8; Ezekiel 14:19; Ezekiel 20:8"> Now shortly I pour out My fury on you, &#124;&#124; And have completed My anger against you, &#124;&#124; And judged you according to your ways, &#124;&#124; And set all your abominations against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 20:13; Micah 6:9; Isaiah 9:13"> And My eye does not pity, nor do I spare, &#124;&#124; I give to you according to your ways, &#124;&#124; And your abominations are in your midst, &#124;&#124; And you have known that &#91;it is&#93; I, YHWH, striking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 10:5; James 4:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> Behold, the day, behold, it has come, &#124;&#124; The circlet has gone forth, &#124;&#124; The rod has blossomed, the pride has flourished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:18; Ezekiel 7:23; Isaiah 59:6–8"> The violence has risen to a rod of wickedness, &#124;&#124; There is none of them, nor of their multitude, &#124;&#124; Nor of their noise, nor is there wailing for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13–14; 1 Corinthians 7:29–31; Ezekiel 7:5–7"> The time has come, the day has arrived, &#124;&#124; The buyer does not rejoice, &#124;&#124; And the seller does not become a mourner, &#124;&#124; For wrath &#91;is&#93; to all its multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 25:24–28; Leviticus 25:31; Job 15:25"> For the seller does not turn to the sold thing, &#124;&#124; And yet their life &#91;is&#93; among the living, &#124;&#124; For the vision &#91;is&#93; to all its multitude, &#124;&#124; It does not turn back, &#124;&#124; And none strengthens his life by his iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:5; Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 51:27"> They have blown with a horn to prepare the whole, &#124;&#124; And none is going to battle, &#124;&#124; For My wrath &#91;is&#93; to all its multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:18; Ezekiel 5:12; Lamentations 1:20"> The sword &#91;is&#93; outside, &#124;&#124; And the pestilence and the famine within, &#124;&#124; He who is in a field dies by sword, &#124;&#124; And he who is in a city—Famine and pestilence devour him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:11; Isaiah 38:14; Ezra 9:15"> And their fugitives have escaped away, &#124;&#124; And they have been on the mountains &#124;&#124; As doves of the valleys, &#124;&#124; All of them making a noise—each for his iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Hebrews 12:12; Jeremiah 6:24"> All the hands are feeble, and all knees go &#91;as&#93; waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 8:10; Isaiah 15:2–3; Isaiah 3:24"> And they have girded on sackcloth, &#124;&#124; And trembling has covered them, &#124;&#124; And shame &#91;is&#93; on all faces, &#124;&#124; And baldness on all their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Zephaniah 1:18; Luke 12:19–20"> They cast their silver into out-places, &#124;&#124; And their gold becomes impurity. Their silver and gold are not able to deliver them, &#124;&#124; In a day of the wrath of YHWH, &#124;&#124; They do not satisfy their soul, &#124;&#124; And they do not fill their bowels, &#124;&#124; For it has been the stumbling-block of their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:21; Jeremiah 7:30; Ezekiel 8:15–16"> As for the beauty of his ornament, &#124;&#124; He set it for excellence, &#124;&#124; And the images of their abominations, &#124;&#124; Their detestable things—they made in it, &#124;&#124; Therefore I have given it to them for impurity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 24:13; Psalms 74:2–8; 2 Chronicles 36:18–19"> And I have given it into the hand of the strangers for a prey, &#124;&#124; And to the wicked of the land for a spoil, &#124;&#124; And they have defiled it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:17; Psalms 74:18–23; Psalms 74:10–11"> And I have turned My face from them, &#124;&#124; And they have defiled My hidden place, &#124;&#124; Indeed, destroyers have come into it, and defiled it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:9; Jeremiah 27:2; Ezekiel 11:6"> Make the chain; for the land &#124;&#124; Has been full of bloody judgments, &#124;&#124; And the city has been full of violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Psalms 106:41; Ezekiel 21:31"> And I have brought in the wicked of the nations, &#124;&#124; And they have possessed their houses, &#124;&#124; And I have caused the excellence of the strong to cease, &#124;&#124; And those sanctifying them have been defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:15–16; Isaiah 57:21; Lamentations 4:17–18"> Destruction has come, &#124;&#124; And they have sought peace, and there is none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; Ezekiel 14:1; Micah 3:6"> Disaster comes on disaster, and report is on report, &#124;&#124; And they have sought a vision from a prophet, &#124;&#124; And law perishes from &#91;the&#93; priest, &#124;&#124; And counsel from &#91;the&#93; elderly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Ezekiel 7:4–8; Ezekiel 26:16"> The king becomes a mourner, &#124;&#124; And a prince puts on desolation, &#124;&#124; And the hands of the people of the land are troubled, &#124;&#124; I deal with them from their own way, &#124;&#124; And I judge them with their own judgments, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH!”</span>
