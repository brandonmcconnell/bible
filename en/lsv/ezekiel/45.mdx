<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:29; Zechariah 14:20–21; Numbers 34:13"> “And in your causing the land to fall into inheritance, you lift up a raised-offering to YHWH, a holy &#91;portion&#93; of the land: the length—twenty-five thousand &#91;is&#93; the length, and the breadth—ten thousand; it &#91;is&#93; holy in all its surrounding border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:16–20; Ezekiel 27:28"> There is of this for the sanctuary five hundred by five hundred, square, around; and fifty cubits of outskirt &#91;is&#93; around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:10"> And by this measure you measure: the length—twenty-five thousand, and the breadth—ten thousand: and the sanctuary is in it, the Holy of Holies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:19; Numbers 16:5; Ezekiel 40:45"> It &#91;is&#93; the holy &#91;portion&#93; of the land; it is for priests, servants of the sanctuary, who are drawing near to serve YHWH; and it has been a place for their houses, and a holy place for the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:13; Nehemiah 10:38–39; Ezekiel 48:20"> And of the twenty-five thousand of length, and of the ten thousand of breadth, there is to the Levites, servants of the house, for them—for a possession—twenty chambers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:15–18; Ezekiel 48:30–35"> And of the possession of the city you set &#91;an area&#93; of five thousand breadth, and twenty-five thousand length, alongside the raised-offering of the holy &#91;portion&#93;; it is for all the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:21; Ezekiel 34:24; Ezekiel 46:16–18"> And for the prince: on this side and on that side of the raised-offering of the holy place, and of the possession of the city, at the front of the raised-offering of the holy place, and at the front of the possession of the city, from the west corner westward, and from the east corner eastward—and the length &#91;is&#93; corresponding to one of the portions from the west border to the east border—</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–5; Ezekiel 46:18; Ezekiel 22:27"> of the land there is for a possession to him in Israel, and My princes do not oppress My people anymore, and they give the land to the house of Israel according to their tribes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 8:16; Jeremiah 22:3; Ezekiel 44:6"> Thus said Lord YHWH: “Enough from you—princes of Israel; turn violence and spoil aside, and do judgment and righteousness; lift up your exactions from off My people—a declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 6:10–11; Amos 8:4–6; Proverbs 16:11"> You must have just balances, and a just ephah, and a just bath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:10"> The ephah and the bath is of one measure, for the bath to carry a tenth of the homer, and the ephah a tenth of the homer: its measurement is according to the homer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:25"> And the shekel &#91;is&#93; twenty gerah: twenty shekels, twenty-five shekels, fifteen shekels—is your maneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> This &#91;is&#93; the raised-offering that you lift up; a sixth part of the ephah of a homer of wheat, also you have given a sixth part of the ephah of a homer of barley,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and the portion of oil, the bath of oil, a tenth part of the bath out of the cor, a homer of ten baths—for ten baths &#91;are&#93; a homer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 1:4; Leviticus 6:30; Colossians 1:21"> and one lamb out of the flock, out of two hundred, out of the watered country of Israel, for a present, and for a burnt-offering, and for peace-offerings, to make atonement by them—a declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 30:14–15; Isaiah 16:1"> All the people of the land are at this raised-offering for the prince in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:4–12; 2 Chronicles 31:3; Isaiah 66:23"> And on the prince are the burnt-offerings, and the present, and the drink-offering, in celebrations, and in new moons, and in Sabbaths, in all appointed times of the house of Israel: he makes the sin-offering, and the present, and the burnt-offering, and the peace-offerings, to make atonement for the house of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 16:16; Exodus 12:2; Ezekiel 43:22"> Thus said Lord YHWH: “In the first &#91;month&#93;, on the first of the month, you take a bullock, a son of the herd, a perfect one, and have cleansed the sanctuary:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:20; Ezekiel 43:14; Leviticus 16:18–20"> and the priest has taken from the blood of the sin offering, and has put &#91;it&#93; on the doorpost of the house, and on the four corners of the border of the altar, and on the post of the gate of the inner court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 19:12; Leviticus 4:27–35; Ezekiel 45:15"> And so you do on the seventh of the month, because of each erring one, and because of the simple one—and you have purified the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 23:5–8; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Numbers 9:2–14"> In the first &#91;month&#93;, on the fourteenth day of the month, you have the Passover, a celebration of seven days, unleavened bread is eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:14; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Matthew 20:28"> And the prince has prepared on that day, for himself, and for all the people of the land, a bullock, a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 42:8; Leviticus 23:8; Numbers 23:1–2"> And &#91;on&#93; the seven days of the celebration he prepares a burnt-offering to YHWH, seven bullocks, and seven rams, perfect ones, daily &#91;for&#93; seven days, and a sin-offering, a kid of the goats, daily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:5–7; Numbers 28:12–15"> And he prepares a present of an ephah for a bullock, and an ephah for a ram, and a hin of oil for an ephah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:33–43; Numbers 29:12–38; 2 Chronicles 5:3"> In the seventh &#91;month&#93;, on the fifteenth day of the month, in the celebration, he does according to these things &#91;for&#93; seven days; as the sin-offering so the burnt-offering, and as the present so also the oil.”</span>
