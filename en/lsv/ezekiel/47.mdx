<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Zechariah 14:8; Joel 3:18"> And he causes me to turn back to the opening of the house; and behold, water is coming forth from under the threshold of the house eastward, for the front of the house &#91;is&#93; eastward, and the water is coming down from beneath, from the right side of the house, from south of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:4; Ezekiel 44:2"> And he causes me to go out the way of the gate northward, and causes me to turn around the way outside, to the gate that &#91;is&#93; outside, the way that is looking eastward, and behold, water is coming forth from the right side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Acts 10:45–46; Acts 11:16–18"> In the going out of the man eastward—and a line in his hand—then he measures one thousand by the cubit, and he causes me to pass over into the waters—waters &#91;to my&#93; ankles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 1:6; Romans 15:19; Acts 19:10–20"> And he measures one thousand, and causes me to pass over into the waters—waters &#91;to my&#93; knees. And he measures one thousand, and causes me to pass over—waters &#91;to my&#93; loins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; Habakkuk 2:14; Matthew 13:31–32"> And he measures one thousand—a stream that I am not able to pass over; for the waters have risen—waters to swim in—a stream that is not passed over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:5; Ezekiel 40:4; Zechariah 4:2"> And he says to me, “Have you seen, son of man?” And he leads me, and brings me back to the edge of the stream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:12; Revelation 22:2; 2 Kings 2:13"> In my turning back, then, behold, at the edge of the stream &#91;are&#93; very many trees, on this side and on that side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; Deuteronomy 3:17; 2 Kings 2:19–22"> And he says to me, “These waters are going forth to the east circuit, and have gone down to the desert, and have entered the sea; they are brought forth to the sea, and the waters have been healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 7:37–38; John 6:63; John 4:14"> And it has come to pass, every living creature that teems, wherever the streams come, lives: and there has been great abundance of fish, for these waters have come there, and they are healed; and everything to where the stream comes has lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 4:19; Psalms 104:25; Numbers 34:6"> And it has come to pass, fishers stand by it, from En-Gedi even to En-Eglaim; they are a spreading place for the nets; their fish are of the same kind as the fish of the Great Sea—very many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; Revelation 22:11; Jeremiah 17:6"> Its miry and its marshy places—they are not healed; they have been given up to salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 22:2; Psalms 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8"> And there comes up by the stream, on its edge, on this side and on that side, every &#91;kind of&#93; fruit-tree whose leaf does not fade, and its fruit is not consumed, it yields first-fruits according to its months, because its waters are coming forth from the sanctuary; and its fruits have been for food, and its leaf for medicine.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:4–6; Jeremiah 3:18; Numbers 34:2–12"> Thus said Lord YHWH: “This &#91;is&#93; the border whereby you inherit the land, according to the twelve tribes of Israel; Joseph &#91;has two&#93; portions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:5–6; Genesis 12:7; Ezekiel 48:29"> And you have inherited it, one as well as another, in that I have lifted up My hand to give it to your fathers; and this land has fallen to you in inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 34:8; Ezekiel 48:1"> And this &#91;is&#93; the border of the land at the north quarter; from the Great Sea, the way of Hethlon, at the coming in to Zedad:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:1; Numbers 13:21; 2 Samuel 8:8"> Hamath, Berothah, Sibraim, that &#91;is&#93; between the border of Damascus and the border of Hamath; Hazar-Hatticon, that &#91;is&#93; at the coast of Havran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:1; Numbers 34:9"> And the border from the sea has been Hazar-Enan, the border of Damascus, and Zaphon at the north, and the border of Hamath: and &#91;this is&#93; the north quarter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Genesis 31:23; Genesis 31:47"> And the east quarter &#91;is&#93; from between Havran, and Damascus, and Gilead, and the land of Israel, &#91;to&#93; the Jordan; you measure from the border on the Eastern Sea: and &#91;this is&#93; the east quarter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:28; Deuteronomy 32:51; Numbers 34:5"> And the south quarter southward &#91;is&#93; from Tamar to the waters of Meriboth-Kadesh, the stream to the Great Sea: and &#91;this is&#93; the south quarter southward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 34:6; Ezekiel 48:1"> And the west quarter &#91;is&#93; the Great Sea, from the border until opposite the coming in to Hamath: this &#91;is&#93; the west quarter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And you have divided this land to yourselves, according to the tribes of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Ephesians 3:6; Acts 11:18"> and it has come to pass, you separate it for an inheritance to yourselves, and to the sojourners who are sojourning in your midst, who have begotten sons in your midst, and they have been to you as native, with the sons of Israel; they are separated with you for an inheritance in the midst of the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And it has come to pass, in the tribe with which the sojourner sojourns, there you give his inheritance,” a declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
