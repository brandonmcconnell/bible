<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 17:8–10; Joshua 17:14–18; Joshua 16:1–10"> and by the border of Manasseh, from the east side to the west side, Ephraim, one &#91;portion&#93;;</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The raised-offering that you lift up to YHWH &#91;is&#93; twenty-five thousand long and ten thousand broad.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:46; Ezekiel 44:10; Matthew 24:45–46"> For the priests who are sanctified of the sons of Zadok, who have kept My charge, who did not err in the erring of the sons of Israel, as the Levites erred,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 27:21; Ezekiel 45:4"> even the raised-offering has been for them, out of the raised-offering of the land, most holy, by the border of the Levites.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:20; Ezekiel 45:6; Ezekiel 44:23"> And the five thousand that is left in the breadth, on the front of the twenty-five thousand, is common—for the city, for dwelling, and for outskirt, and the city has been in its midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 21:16"> And these &#91;are&#93; its measurements: the north side—four thousand and five hundred, and the south side—four thousand and five hundred, and on the east side—four thousand and five hundred, and the west side—four thousand and five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the outskirt of the city has been northward—two hundred and fifty, and southward—two hundred and fifty, and eastward—two hundred and fifty, and westward—two hundred and fifty.</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:6; 1 Kings 4:7–23; Nehemiah 11:1–36"> even &#91;to&#93; him who is serving the city, they serve it out of all the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 21:16; Hebrews 12:17"> All the raised-offering &#91;is&#93; twenty-five thousand by twenty-five thousand square—you lift up the raised-offering of the holy &#91;portion&#93; with the possession of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:22; Ezekiel 37:24; Ezekiel 48:8"> And the remainder &#91;is&#93; for the prince, on this side and on that side of the raised-offering of the holy &#91;portion&#93;, and of the possession of the city, on the front of the twenty-five thousand of the raised-offering to the east border, and westward, on the front of the twenty-five thousand on the west border, alongside the portions of the prince; and the raised-offering of the holy &#91;portion&#93;, and the sanctuary of the house, has been in its midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And from the possession of the Levites, from the possession of the city, in the midst of that which is for the prince, between the border of Judah and the border of Benjamin, there is for the prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 18:21–28; Ezekiel 48:1–7; Genesis 35:16–19"> As for the rest of the tribes, from the east side to the west side, Benjamin, one &#91;portion&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 19:1–9; Genesis 29:33; Genesis 49:5–7"> and by the border of Benjamin, from the east side to the west side, Simeon, one &#91;portion&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 19:17–23; Genesis 30:14–18"> and by the border of Simeon, from the east side to the west side, Issachar, one &#91;portion&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 19:10–16; Genesis 30:19–20"> and by the border of Issachar, from the east side to the west side, Zebulun, one &#91;portion&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 13:24–28; Genesis 30:10–11"> and by the border of Zebulun, from the east side to the west side, Gad, one &#91;portion&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:15; 2 Chronicles 20:2; Ezekiel 47:19–20"> and by the border of Gad, at the south side southward, the border has been from Tamar &#91;to&#93; the waters of Meriboth-Kadesh, the stream by the Great Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 13:1–21; Numbers 34:2; Numbers 34:13"> This &#91;is&#93; the land that you separate by inheritance to the tribes of Israel, and these &#91;are&#93; their divisions,” a declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:16; Revelation 21:16; Ezekiel 48:32–35"> “And these &#91;are&#93; the outgoings of the city: on the north side, four thousand and five hundred measures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 21:12–13; Isaiah 26:1–2; Revelation 21:21"> and the gates of the city &#91;are&#93; according to the names of the tribes of Israel; three gates northward—one gate of Reuben, one gate of Judah, one gate of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And on the east side, four thousand and five hundred, and three gates—one gate of Joseph, one gate of Benjamin, one gate of Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And the south side, four thousand and five hundred, and three gates—one gate of Simeon, one gate of Issachar, one gate of Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> The west side, four thousand and five hundred, &#91;with&#93; their three gates—one gate of Gad, one gate of Asher, one gate of Naphtali.</span>
