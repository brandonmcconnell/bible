<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 24:10; Revelation 4:2–3; Revelation 1:13"> And I look, and behold, on the expanse that &#91;is&#93; above the head of the cherubim, as a sapphire stone, as the appearance of the likeness of a throne, He has been seen over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 8:5; Ezekiel 10:16; Ezekiel 1:13"> And He speaks to the man clothed with linen and says, “Go into the midst of the wheel, to the place of the cherub, and fill your hands with coals of fire from between the cherubim, and scatter over the city.” And he goes in before my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:4; Ezekiel 9:3; Ezekiel 8:16"> And the cherubim are standing on the right side of the house, at the going in of the man, and the cloud has filled the inner court,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Ezekiel 9:3; Exodus 40:35"> and the glory of YHWH becomes high above the cherub, over the threshold of the house, and the house is filled with the cloud, and the court has been filled with the brightness of the glory of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:24; Job 40:9; Exodus 19:16"> And a noise of the wings of the cherubim has been heard in the outer court, as the voice of God—the Mighty One—in His speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:2; Psalms 99:1; Psalms 80:1"> And it comes to pass, in His commanding the man clothed with linen, saying, “Take fire from between the wheel, from between the cherubim,” and he goes in and stands near the wheel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 13:41–42; Ezekiel 1:13; Ezekiel 41:23–26"> that the &#91;one&#93; cherub puts forth his hand from between the cherubim to the fire that &#91;is&#93; between the cherubim, and lifts up, and gives &#91;it&#93; into the hands of him who is clothed with linen, and he receives, and comes forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:8; Ezekiel 10:21; Isaiah 6:6"> And there appears in the cherubim the form of a hand of man under their wings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:15–17; Daniel 10:6; Revelation 21:20"> and I look, and behold, four wheels near the cherubim, one wheel near one cherub, and another wheel near the other cherub, and the appearance of the wheels &#91;is&#93; as the color of a beryl stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:16; Psalms 36:6; Psalms 104:24"> And &#91;as for&#93; their appearances, &#91;the&#93; four had one likeness, as it were the wheel in the midst of the wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:17; Ezekiel 10:22; Ezekiel 1:20"> In their going, they go on their four sides; they do not turn around in their going, for to the place to where the head turns, they go after it, they do not turn around in their going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:18; Revelation 4:8; Revelation 4:6"> And all their flesh, and their backs, and their hands, and their wings, and the wheels, are full of eyes all around—&#91;the&#93; wheels &#91;the&#93; four had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> As for the wheels—they were called “Whirling Wheel” in my ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 4:7; Ezekiel 10:21; 1 Kings 7:36"> And four faces &#91;are&#93; to each; the face of the first &#91;is&#93; the face of the cherub, and the face of the second &#91;is&#93; the face of man, and of the third the face of a lion, and of the fourth the face of an eagle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:5; Hosea 9:12; Ezekiel 11:22"> And the cherubim are lifted up, it &#91;is&#93; the living creature that I saw by the river Chebar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:19–21"> And in the going of the cherubim, the wheels go beside them; and in the cherubim lifting up their wings to be high above the earth, the wheels do not turn around, even they, from being beside them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:20–21; Romans 8:2; Ezekiel 1:12"> In their standing they stand, and in their exaltation they are exalted with them: for &#91;the&#93; spirit of the living creature &#91;is&#93; in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 18:10; Matthew 23:37–39; Psalms 78:60–61"> And the glory of YHWH goes forth from off the threshold of the house, and stands over the cherubim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Ezekiel 1:17–21; Ezekiel 1:26–28"> and the cherubim lift up their wings, and are lifted up from the earth before my eyes; in their going forth, the wheels &#91;are&#93; also alongside them, and he stands at the opening of the east gate of the house of YHWH, and the glory of the God of Israel &#91;is&#93; over them from above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:1; Ezekiel 10:15; 1 Kings 7:36"> It &#91;is&#93; the living creature that I saw under the God of Israel by the river Chebar, and I know that they are cherubim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:6; Ezekiel 41:18–19; Ezekiel 10:14"> &#91;The&#93; four—each had four faces, and each had four wings, and the likeness of the hands of man &#91;is&#93; under their wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:11; Ezekiel 1:10; Ezekiel 1:12"> And the likeness of their faces, they &#91;are&#93; the faces that I saw by the river Chebar, their appearances and themselves; they each go straight forward.</span>
