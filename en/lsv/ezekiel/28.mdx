<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:9; 2 Thessalonians 2:4; Isaiah 14:13–14"> “Son of man, say to the leader of Tyre, Thus said Lord YHWH: Because your heart has been high, &#124;&#124; And you say: I &#91;am&#93; a god, &#124;&#124; I have inhabited the habitation of God, &#124;&#124; In the heart of the seas, &#124;&#124; And you &#91;are&#93; man, and not God, &#124;&#124; And you give out your heart as the heart of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 1:20; Daniel 2:22; Daniel 2:27–28"> Behold, you &#91;are&#93; wiser than Daniel, &#124;&#124; No hidden thing have they concealed from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Proverbs 23:4–5; Proverbs 18:11"> By your wisdom and by your understanding &#124;&#124; You have made wealth for yourself, &#124;&#124; And make gold and silver in your treasuries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 62:10; Psalms 52:7; Ezekiel 28:2"> By the abundance of your wisdom, &#124;&#124; Through your merchandise, &#124;&#124; You have multiplied your wealth, &#124;&#124; And your heart is high through your wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Exodus 9:17; James 1:11"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: Because of your giving out your heart as the heart of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:12; Ezekiel 32:12; Ezekiel 30:11"> Therefore, behold, I am bringing in strangers against you, &#124;&#124; The terrible of the nations, &#124;&#124; And they have drawn out their swords &#124;&#124; Against the beauty of your wisdom, &#124;&#124; And they have pierced your brightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:26–27; Ezekiel 27:34; Proverbs 1:12"> They bring you down to destruction, &#124;&#124; You die by the deaths of the wounded, in the heart of the seas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Isaiah 31:3; Psalms 82:7"> Do you really say, I &#91;am&#93; God, &#124;&#124; Before him who is slaying you? And you &#91;are&#93; man, and not God, &#124;&#124; In the hand of him who is piercing you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:18; Ezekiel 32:19; Philippians 3:3"> You die the deaths of the uncircumcised, &#124;&#124; By the hand of strangers, for I have spoken,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1; Ezekiel 26:17; 2 Corinthians 1:22"> “Son of man, lift up a lamentation for the king of Tyre, &#124;&#124; And you have said to him, Thus said Lord YHWH: You are sealing up a measurement, &#124;&#124; Full of wisdom, and perfect in beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:15; Genesis 2:8; Ezekiel 27:16"> You have been in Eden, the garden of God, &#124;&#124; Every precious stone &#91;was&#93; your covering, &#124;&#124; Ruby, topaz, and diamond, beryl, onyx, and jasper, &#124;&#124; Sapphire, emerald, and carbuncle, and gold, &#124;&#124; The workmanship of your tambourines and of your pipes, &#124;&#124; In you in the day of your being produced, have been created;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 14:12–15; Exodus 30:26; Ezekiel 28:13"> And I established you as the anointed, covering cherub, &#124;&#124; You have been in &#91;the&#93; holy mountain of God, &#124;&#124; You have walked up and down in &#91;the&#93; midst of &#91;the&#93; stones of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Ezekiel 28:17–18; Romans 7:9"> You &#91;were&#93; perfect in your ways, &#124;&#124; From &#91;the&#93; day of your being created, &#124;&#124; Until perversity was found in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Habakkuk 2:8; Habakkuk 2:17"> By &#91;the&#93; abundance of your merchandise &#124;&#124; They have filled your midst with violence, &#124;&#124; And you sin, &#124;&#124; And I thrust you from &#91;the&#93; mountain of God, &#124;&#124; And I destroy you, O covering cherub, &#124;&#124; From &#91;the&#93; midst of &#91;the&#93; stones of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:5; Ezekiel 31:10; James 4:6"> Your heart has been high because of your beauty, &#124;&#124; You have corrupted your wisdom because of your brightness, &#124;&#124; I have cast you to earth, &#124;&#124; I have set you before kings, to look on you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Revelation 18:8; Amos 1:9–10"> From &#91;the&#93; abundance of your iniquity, &#124;&#124; By &#91;the&#93; perversity of your merchandise, &#124;&#124; You have defiled your sanctuaries, &#124;&#124; And I bring forth fire from your midst, &#124;&#124; It has devoured you, &#124;&#124; And I make you become ashes on the earth, &#124;&#124; In &#91;the&#93; eyes of all beholding you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:21; Revelation 18:21; Revelation 18:15–19"> All knowing you among the peoples &#124;&#124; Have been astonished at you, &#124;&#124; You have been terrors, and you are not—for all time.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Genesis 10:15; Jeremiah 25:22"> “Son of man, set your face toward Sidon, and prophesy concerning it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:13; Exodus 14:17; Ezekiel 20:41"> and you have said, Thus said Lord YHWH: Behold, I &#91;am&#93; against you, O Sidon, &#124;&#124; And I have been honored in your midst, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; In My doing judgments in her, &#124;&#124; And I have been sanctified in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Ezekiel 26:6; Ezekiel 25:11"> And I have sent pestilence into her, &#124;&#124; And blood into her out-places, &#124;&#124; The wounded has been judged in her midst, &#124;&#124; By the sword on her all around, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 33:55; Joshua 23:13; 2 Corinthians 12:7"> And there is no more pricking brier and paining thorn &#124;&#124; For the house of Israel, &#124;&#124; Of all around them—despising them, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:37; Ezekiel 20:41; Ezekiel 36:28"> Thus said Lord YHWH: In My gathering the house of Israel, &#124;&#124; Out of the peoples among whom they were scattered, &#124;&#124; I have been sanctified in them, &#124;&#124; Before the eyes of the nations, &#124;&#124; And they have dwelt on their ground, &#124;&#124; That I gave to My servant, to Jacob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:24; Ezekiel 28:22; Jeremiah 32:15"> And they have dwelt confidently on it, &#124;&#124; And built houses, and planted vineyards, &#124;&#124; And dwelt confidently—in My doing judgments, &#124;&#124; On all those despising them all around, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; their God YHWH!”</span>
