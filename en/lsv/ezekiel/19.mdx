<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 11:3; Isaiah 5:29; Job 4:11"> and you have said: What &#91;is&#93; your mother? A lioness, &#124;&#124; She has crouched down among lions, &#124;&#124; She has multiplied her whelps in the midst of young lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:25; 2 Kings 23:31–32; 2 Chronicles 36:1–2"> And she brings up one of her whelps, &#124;&#124; He has been a young lion, &#124;&#124; And he learns to tear prey, &#124;&#124; He has devoured man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:4; 2 Kings 23:33–34; 2 Chronicles 36:6"> And nations hear of him, &#124;&#124; He has been caught in their pit, &#124;&#124; And they bring him to the land of Egypt in chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:3; 2 Kings 23:34–37"> And as she waited she sees that her hope has perished, &#124;&#124; And she takes one of her whelps, &#124;&#124; She has made him a young lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:5; 2 Chronicles 36:9; Jeremiah 26:1–24"> And he goes up and down in the midst of lions, &#124;&#124; He has been a young lion, &#124;&#124; And he learns to tear prey, &#124;&#124; He has devoured man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:12; Ezekiel 12:19; Micah 1:2"> And he knows his forsaken habitations, &#124;&#124; And he has laid waste &#91;to&#93; their cities, &#124;&#124; And the land and its fullness is desolate, &#124;&#124; Because of the voice of his roaring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 4:20; Ezekiel 19:4; Ezekiel 12:13"> And surrounding nations set against him from the provinces. And they spread out their net for him, &#124;&#124; He has been caught in their pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:6; Ezekiel 6:2; Jeremiah 36:30–31"> And they put him in prison—in chains, &#124;&#124; And they bring him to the king of Babylon, &#124;&#124; They bring him into bulwarks, &#124;&#124; So that his voice is not heard &#124;&#124; On mountains of Israel anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 80:8–11; Deuteronomy 8:7; Numbers 24:6–7"> Your mother, like the vine in your blood, &#124;&#124; Is being planted by waters, &#124;&#124; She was bearing fruit and full of boughs, &#124;&#124; Because of many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:3; Psalms 80:15; Daniel 4:11"> And she has strong rods for scepters of rulers, &#124;&#124; And she is high in stature above—between thick branches, &#124;&#124; And it appears in its height &#124;&#124; In the multitude of its thin shoots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Hosea 13:15; Isaiah 27:11"> And she is plucked up in fury, &#124;&#124; She has been cast to the earth, &#124;&#124; And the east wind has dried up her fruit, &#124;&#124; &#91;The&#93; rod of her strength has been broken and withered, &#124;&#124; Fire has consumed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Ezekiel 19:10; Psalms 63:1"> And now she is planted in a wilderness, &#124;&#124; In a dry and thirsty land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 80:15–16; Ezekiel 19:11; 2 Kings 24:20"> And fire goes forth from a rod of her boughs, &#124;&#124; It has devoured her fruit, &#124;&#124; And she has no rod of strength—a scepter to rule, &#124;&#124; A lamentation—and she has become for a lamentation!”</span>
