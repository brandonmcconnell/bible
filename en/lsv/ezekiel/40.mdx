<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:21; Ezekiel 1:2–3; Ezekiel 3:14"> In the twenty-fifth year of our removal, in the beginning of the year, on the tenth of the month, in the fourteenth year after which the city was struck, on this very same day a hand of YHWH has been on me, and He brings me in there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:1; Daniel 7:1; Daniel 7:7"> in visions of God He has brought me into the land of Israel, and causes me to rest on a very high mountain, and on it &#91;is&#93; as the frame of a city from the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:3; Revelation 11:1; Daniel 10:5–6"> And He brings me in there, and behold, a man, his appearance as the appearance of bronze, and a thread of flax in his hand, and a measuring-reed, and he is standing at the gate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:5; Matthew 10:27; Ezekiel 2:7–8"> and the man speaks to me: “Son of man, see with your eyes, &#124;&#124; And hear with your ears, &#124;&#124; And set your heart to all that I am showing you, &#124;&#124; For in order to show &#91;it&#93; you, &#124;&#124; You have been brought in here, &#124;&#124; Declare all that you are seeing to the house of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:20; Isaiah 26:1; Revelation 21:12"> And behold, a wall all around on the outside of the house, and a measuring-reed in the hand of the man, six cubits by a cubit and a handbreadth, and he measures the breadth of the building—one reed, and the height—one reed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:1; Ezekiel 8:16; Ezekiel 11:1"> And he comes to the gate whose front &#91;is&#93; eastward, and he goes up by its steps, and he measures the threshold of the gate—one reed broad, even one threshold—one reed broad,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:36; Ezekiel 40:33; 1 Chronicles 23:28"> and the little chamber—one reed long and one reed broad, and between the little chambers—five cubits, and the threshold of the gate, from the side of the porch of the gate from within—one reed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And he measures the porch of the gate from within—one reed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:19"> and he measures the porch of the gate—eight cubits, and its posts—two cubits, and the porch of the gates from within,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and the little chambers of the gate eastward, three on this side and three on that side; the three of them—one measure, and the posts—one measure, on this side and on that side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he measures the breadth of the opening of the gate—ten cubits, the length of the gate—thirteen cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and a border before the little chambers—one cubit, and one cubit &#91;is&#93; the border on this side, and the little chamber—six cubits on this side, and six cubits on that side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he measures the gate from the roof of the &#91;one&#93; little chamber to the roof of another; the breadth—twenty-five cubits, opening opposite opening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Isaiah 62:9; Ezekiel 42:1"> And he makes the posts of sixty cubits, even to the post of the court, the gate all around;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and by the front of the gate of the entrance, by the front of the porch of the inner gate—fifty cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:5; 1 Kings 6:4; Ezekiel 41:26"> and narrow windows &#91;are&#93; in the little chambers, and in their posts on the inside of the gate all around—and so to the arches—and windows all around on the inside, and palm-trees &#91;are&#93; on the post.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Ezekiel 42:1; 1 Chronicles 23:28"> And he brings me into the outer court, and behold, chambers and a pavement made for the court all around—thirty chambers on the pavement—</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and the pavement to the side of the gates, corresponding to the length of the gates, &#91;is&#93; the lower pavement;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:23; Ezekiel 40:27; Ezekiel 46:1–2"> and he measures the breadth from before the lower gate, to the front of the inner court, on the outside—one hundred cubits, eastward and northward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:6"> As for the gate of the outer court whose front &#91;is&#93; northward, he has measured its length and its breadth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:29–30; Ezekiel 40:25–26; Ezekiel 40:10–16"> and its little chambers, three on this side and three on that side, and its posts and its arches have been according to the measure of the first gate, its length &#91;is&#93; fifty cubits, and breadth &#91;is&#93; twenty-five by the cubit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:31; Ezekiel 40:37; Ezekiel 40:6"> and its windows, and its arches, and its palm-trees &#91;are&#93; according to the measure of the gate whose face &#91;is&#93; eastward, and they go up on it by seven steps, and its arches &#91;are&#93; before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:19; Ezekiel 40:27–28; Exodus 38:9–12"> And the gate of the inner court &#91;is&#93; opposite the gate at the north and at the east; and he measures from gate to gate—one hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:9; Ezekiel 40:6; Ezekiel 40:20–21"> And he causes me to go southward, and behold, a gate southward, and he has measured its posts and its arches according to these measurements;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:33; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:21–22"> and windows &#91;are&#93; in it and in its arches all around, like these windows, the length—fifty cubits, and the breadth—twenty-five cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:22; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:6"> and seven steps &#91;are&#93; its ascent, and its arches &#91;are&#93; before them, and palm-trees &#91;are&#93; in it, one on this side and one on that side, on its posts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:23; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 40:19"> and the gate of the inner court &#91;is&#93; southward, and he measures from gate to gate southward, one hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:35; Ezekiel 40:32"> And he brings me into the inner court by the south gate, and he measures the south gate according to these measurements;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:25; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:7"> and its little chambers, and its posts, and its arches &#91;are&#93; according to these measurements, and windows &#91;are&#93; in it and in its arches all around; the length—fifty cubits, and the breadth—twenty-five cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:21; Ezekiel 40:36; Ezekiel 40:25"> As for the arches all around, the length—twenty-five cubits, and the breadth—five cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:26; Ezekiel 40:34; Ezekiel 40:22"> and its arches &#91;are&#93; toward the outer court, and palm-trees &#91;are&#93; on its posts, and eight steps &#91;are&#93; its ascent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:35; Ezekiel 40:28–31"> And he brings me into the inner court eastward, and he measures the gate according to these measurements;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:21; Ezekiel 40:36; Ezekiel 40:25"> and its little chambers, and its posts, and its arches &#91;are&#93; according to these measurements: and windows &#91;are&#93; in it and in its arches all around, the length—fifty cubits, and the breadth—twenty-five cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:22; Ezekiel 40:31; Ezekiel 40:37"> and its arches &#91;are&#93; toward the outer court, and palm-trees &#91;are&#93; toward its posts, on this side and on that side, and eight steps &#91;are&#93; its ascent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:2; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 44:4"> And he brings me into the north gate, and has measured according to these measurements;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:29; Ezekiel 40:21; Ezekiel 40:16"> its little chambers, its posts, and its arches; and windows &#91;are&#93; in it all around: the length—fifty cubits, and the breadth—twenty-five cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:34; Ezekiel 40:31"> and its posts &#91;are&#93; toward the outer court, and palm-trees &#91;are&#93; on its posts, on this side and on that side, and eight steps &#91;are&#93; its ascent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:6; Ezekiel 40:17; Leviticus 8:21"> And the chamber and its opening &#91;is&#93; by the posts of the gates, there they purge the burnt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:2; Leviticus 1:3–17; Leviticus 4:2–3"> And in the porch of the gate &#91;are&#93; two tables on this side and two tables on that side, to slaughter on them the burnt-offering, and the sin-offering, and the guilt-offering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and at the side without, at the going up to the opening of the north gate, &#91;are&#93; two tables; and at the other side that &#91;is&#93; at the porch of the gate, &#91;are&#93; two tables;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:39–40"> four tables &#91;are&#93; on this side and four tables on that side, at the side of the gate, eight tables on which they slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:39; Exodus 20:25"> And the four tables for burnt-offering &#91;are&#93; of hewn stone: the length—one cubit and a half, and the breadth—one cubit and a half, and the height—one cubit; they place on them the instruments with which they slaughter the burnt-offering and the sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 1:8; Leviticus 1:6; Leviticus 8:20"> And the boundaries &#91;are&#93; one handbreadth, prepared within all around: and the flesh of the offering &#91;is&#93; on the tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:31–32; 1 Chronicles 16:41–43; Ezekiel 40:27"> And on the outside of the inner gate &#91;are&#93; chambers of the singers, in the inner court, that &#91;are&#93; at the side of the north gate, and their fronts &#91;are&#93; southward, one at the side of the east gate &#91;with&#93; front northward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:23; Psalms 134:1; Leviticus 8:35"> And he speaks to me: “This chamber, whose front &#91;is&#93; southward, &#91;is&#93; for priests keeping charge of the house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:15; Ezekiel 43:19; 1 Kings 2:35"> and the chamber, whose front &#91;is&#93; northward, &#91;is&#93; for priests keeping charge of the altar: they &#91;are&#93; sons of Zadok, who are drawing near of the sons of Levi to YHWH, to serve Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:19; Ezekiel 40:23; Ezekiel 40:27"> And he measures the court: the length—one hundred cubits, and the breadth—one hundred cubits, square, and the altar &#91;is&#93; before the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:4; 1 Kings 6:3"> And he brings me to the porch of the house, and he measures the post of the porch—five cubits on this side and five cubits on that side, and the breadth of the gate—three cubits on this side and three cubits on that side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Kings 7:15–21; Ezekiel 40:34; 2 Chronicles 3:17"> the length of the porch—twenty cubits, and the breadth—eleven &#91;&#91;or twelve&#93;&#93; cubits; and they go up to it by the &#91;&#91;ten&#93;&#93; steps. And pillars &#91;are&#93; by the posts, one on this side and one on that side.</span>
