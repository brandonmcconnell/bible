<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:2–3; Ezekiel 40:17; Ezekiel 41:23"> And he brings me into the temple, and he measures the posts, six cubits the breadth on this side and six cubits the breadth on that side—the breadth of the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 6:17; 2 Chronicles 3:3; 1 Kings 6:2"> And the breadth of the opening &#91;is&#93; ten cubits; and the sides of the opening &#91;are&#93; five cubits on this side and five cubits on that side; and he measures its length—forty cubits, and the breadth—twenty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:16"> And he has gone inward, and measures the post of the opening—two cubits, and the opening—six cubits, and the breadth of the opening—seven cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 6:20; Hebrews 9:3–8; Exodus 26:33–34"> And he measures its length—twenty cubits, and the breadth—twenty cubits, to the front of the temple, and he says to me, “This &#91;is&#93; the Holy of Holies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:5–6; Ezekiel 42:3–14; Ezekiel 41:6–9"> And he measures the wall of the house—six cubits, and the breadth of the side-chamber—four cubits, all around the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 6:10; 1 Kings 6:5–6; Ezekiel 40:17"> And the side-chambers &#91;are&#93; side-chamber by side-chamber, thirty-three times; and they are entering into the wall—which the house has for the side-chambers all around—to be taken hold of, and they are not taken hold of by the wall of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 6:8; Hebrews 6:1; Matthew 13:32"> And it has become wider when one has turned even higher toward the side-chambers, for the encompassment of the house &#91;is&#93; even higher all around the house: therefore the breadth of the house &#91;goes&#93; upwards, and so the lower &#91;story&#93; goes up to the higher by the middle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:5; Revelation 21:16"> And I have looked at the height all around the house: the foundations of the side-chambers &#91;are&#93; the fullness of the reed, six cubits by the joining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:11; Ezekiel 42:4; Ezekiel 42:1"> The breadth of that wall, of the side-chamber, at the outside, &#91;is&#93; five cubits; and the space remaining of the side-chambers—that of the house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:17"> and between the chambers—&#91;is&#93; a breadth of twenty cubits around the house, all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:9; Ezekiel 42:4"> And the opening of the side-chamber &#91;is&#93; to the remaining &#91;space&#93;, one opening northward and one opening southward, and the breadth of the remaining space &#91;is&#93; five cubits all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:1; Ezekiel 42:13; Ezekiel 41:13–15"> As for the building that &#91;is&#93; at the front of the separate place &#91;at&#93; the corner westward, the breadth &#91;is&#93; seventy cubits, and the wall of the building &#91;is&#93; five cubits broad all around, and its length—ninety cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:47"> And he has measured the house, the length &#91;is&#93; one hundred cubits; and the separate place, and the building, and its walls, the length &#91;is&#93; one hundred cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and the breadth of the front of the house, and of the separate place eastward—one hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:3; Ezekiel 42:1; Ezekiel 41:12"> And he has measured the length of the building to the front of the separate place that &#91;is&#93; at its back part, and its galleries on this side and on that side—one hundred cubits. And the inner temple and the porches of the court,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 6:4; Ezekiel 40:25; Ezekiel 40:16"> the thresholds, and the narrow windows, and the galleries around the three of them, opposite the threshold, &#91;were&#93; paneled with wood all around—and the ground to the windows, and the windows were covered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:15"> over above the opening, and to the inner-house, and at the outside, and by all the wall all around inside and outside &#91;by&#93; measurements.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:36; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:22"> And it is made &#91;with&#93; cherubim and palm-trees, and a palm-tree &#91;is&#93; between cherub and cherub, and two faces &#91;are&#93; on the cherub;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Ezekiel 1:10"> and the face of a man toward the palm-tree on this side and the face of a young lion toward the palm-tree on that side; it is made to all the house all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:18"> From the earth to above the opening the cherubim and the palm-trees &#91;were&#93; made, and &#91;on&#93; the wall of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:1; 1 Kings 6:33; Ezekiel 40:14"> The doorpost of the temple &#91;is&#93; square, and of the front of the sanctuary, the appearance &#91;is&#93; as that appearance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Malachi 1:12; Ezekiel 23:41; Malachi 1:7"> Of the altar, the wood &#91;is&#93; three cubits in height, and its length—two cubits; and its corners &#91;are&#93; to it, and its length, and its walls &#91;are&#93; of wood, and he speaks to me, “This &#91;is&#93; the table that &#91;is&#93; before YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 6:31–35; 2 Chronicles 4:22; Ezekiel 41:1"> And the temple and the sanctuary had two doors;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 6:34; Ezekiel 40:48"> and the doors had two panels, two turning panels: two on one door, and two panels on the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:18"> And made on them, on the doors of the temple, &#91;are&#93; cherubim and palm-trees as are made on the walls, and a thickness of wood &#91;is&#93; at the front of the porch on the outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:16; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 41:5"> And narrow windows and palm-trees &#91;are&#93; on this side and on that side, at the sides of the porch, and the side-chambers of the house, and the thick places.</span>
