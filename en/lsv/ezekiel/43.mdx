<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:1; Ezekiel 42:15; Ezekiel 46:1"> And he causes me to go to the gate, the gate that is looking eastward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 18:1; Ezekiel 11:23; Isaiah 6:3"> And behold, the glory of the God of Israel has come from the way of the east, and His voice &#91;is&#93; as the noise of many waters, and the earth has shone from His glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:5; Ezekiel 9:1; Jeremiah 1:10"> And according to the appearance &#91;is&#93; the appearance that I saw, as the appearance that I saw in my coming in to destroy the city, and the appearances &#91;are&#93; as the appearance that I saw at the river Chebar, and I fall on my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:18–19; Ezekiel 44:2; Ezekiel 43:2"> And the glory of YHWH has come into the house, the way of the gate whose face &#91;is&#93; eastward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:2–4; 1 Kings 8:10–11; Ezekiel 8:3"> And &#91;the&#93; Spirit takes me up, and brings me into the inner court, and behold, the glory of YHWH has filled the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Isaiah 66:6; Revelation 16:1"> And I hear one speaking to me from the house, and a man has been standing near me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:9; Psalms 47:8; Leviticus 26:30"> and He says to me: “Son of man, the place of My throne, &#124;&#124; And the place of the soles of My feet, &#124;&#124; Where I dwell in the midst of the sons of Israel for all time, &#124;&#124; The house of Israel defiles My holy Name no longer, &#124;&#124; They, and their kings, by their whoredom, &#124;&#124; And by the carcasses of their kings—their high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 8:3–16; 2 Chronicles 33:7"> In their putting their threshold with My threshold, &#124;&#124; And their doorpost near My doorpost, &#124;&#124; And the wall between Me and them, &#124;&#124; And they have defiled My holy Name, &#124;&#124; By their abominations that they have done, &#124;&#124; And I consume them in My anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:7; Ezekiel 37:26–28; Ezekiel 18:30–31"> Now they put their whoredom far off, &#124;&#124; And the carcasses of their kings—from Me, &#124;&#124; And I have dwelt in their midst for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:4; Ezekiel 16:61; Ezekiel 16:63"> You, son of man, &#124;&#124; Show the house of Israel the house, &#124;&#124; And they are ashamed of their iniquities, &#124;&#124; And they have measured the measurement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Ezekiel 11:20; Matthew 28:20"> And since they have been ashamed of all that they have done, &#124;&#124; The form of the house, and its measurement, &#124;&#124; And its outlets, and its inlets, and all its forms, &#124;&#124; And all its statutes, even all its forms, &#124;&#124; And all its laws—cause them to know, &#124;&#124; And write &#91;it&#93; before their eyes, &#124;&#124; And they observe all its forms, &#124;&#124; And all its statutes, and have done them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:2; Revelation 21:27; Psalms 93:5"> This &#91;is&#93; a law of the house: on the top of the mountain, all its border all around &#91;is&#93; most holy; behold, this &#91;is&#93; a law of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:5; Exodus 27:1–8; Ezekiel 41:8"> And these &#91;are&#93; measurements of the altar by cubits: the cubit &#91;is&#93; a cubit and a handbreadth, and the center &#91;is&#93; a cubit, and the breadth a cubit; and its border on its edge around &#91;is&#93; one span, and this &#91;is&#93; the upper part of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And from the center of the ground to the lower border &#91;is&#93; two cubits, and the breadth—one cubit, and from the lesser border to the greater border—four cubits, and the breadth a cubit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 27:2; Psalms 118:27; Leviticus 9:9"> And the altar &#91;is&#93; four cubits, and from the altar and upward &#91;are&#93; four horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 27:1; 2 Chronicles 4:1; Exodus 38:1–2"> And the altar &#91;is&#93; twelve long by twelve broad, square in its four sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 20:26; Ezekiel 40:6; Exodus 25:25"> And the border &#91;is&#93; fourteen long by fourteen broad, at its four sides, and the border around it &#91;is&#93; half a cubit, and the center of it &#91;is&#93; a cubit around, and its steps are looking eastward.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 40:29; Hebrews 10:4–12; Ezekiel 2:1"> And He says to me, “Son of man, thus said Lord YHWH: These &#91;are&#93; statutes of the altar in the day of its being made to cause burnt-offering to go up on it, and to sprinkle blood on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:46; Ezekiel 44:15; Numbers 16:40"> And you have given to the priests, the Levites—who &#91;are&#93; of the seed of Zadok, who are near to Me, a declaration of Lord YHWH, to serve Me—a calf from the herd, for a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 16:19; Leviticus 8:15; Exodus 29:12"> And you have taken of its blood, and have put it on its four horns, and on the four corners of its border, and on the border all around, and have cleansed it, and purified it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 4:12; Leviticus 8:17"> And you have taken the bullock of the sin-offering, and have burned it in the appointed place of the house at the outside of the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:25–26; Ezekiel 43:20; 1 Peter 1:19"> And on the second day you bring a kid of the goats near, a perfect one, for a sin-offering, and they have cleansed the altar, as they cleansed &#91;it&#93; for the bullock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 29:1"> In your finishing cleansing, you bring a calf near, a son of the herd, a perfect one, and a ram out of the flock, a perfect one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 2:13; Mark 9:49–50; Numbers 18:19"> And you have brought them near before YHWH, and the priests have cast salt on them, and have caused them to go up, a burnt-offering to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 8:33; Exodus 29:35–37; Leviticus 8:35"> &#91;For&#93; seven days you prepare a goat daily for a sin-offering; and they also prepare a bullock, a son of the herd, and a ram out of the flock—perfect ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 29:24; Leviticus 8:34; Exodus 32:29"> &#91;For&#93; seven days they purify the altar, and have cleansed it, and filled their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 9:1; Ezekiel 20:40–41; 1 Peter 2:5"> And the days are completed, and it has come to pass on the eighth day, and from now on, the priests prepare your burnt-offerings and your peace-offerings on the altar, and I have accepted you—a declaration of Lord YHWH.”</span>
