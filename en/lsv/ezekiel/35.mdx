<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:1; Ezekiel 21:1; Ezekiel 34:1"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Genesis 32:3; Ezekiel 25:8"> “Son of man, set your face against Mount Seir, and prophesy against it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:12; Ezekiel 35:7; Ezekiel 6:14"> and you have said to it, Thus said Lord YHWH: Behold, I &#91;am&#93; against you, O Mount Seir, &#124;&#124; And have stretched out My hand against you, &#124;&#124; And made you a desolation and an astonishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:9; Malachi 1:3–4; Joel 3:19"> I make your cities a ruin, and you are a desolation, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 137:7; Obadiah 1:10–16; Amos 1:11"> Because of your having continuous enmity, &#124;&#124; And you saw the sons of Israel, &#124;&#124; By the hands of the sword, &#124;&#124; In the time of their calamity, &#124;&#124; In the time of the iniquity of the end:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:2–6; Revelation 18:24; Obadiah 1:15"> Therefore, &#91;as&#93; I live—a declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; Surely I appoint you for blood, &#124;&#124; And blood pursues you, &#124;&#124; If you have not hated blood, &#124;&#124; Blood also pursues you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:11; Ezekiel 35:3; Judges 5:6–7"> And I have given Mount Seir for a desolation and an astonishment, &#124;&#124; And have cut off from it him who is passing over and him who is returning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:12; Ezekiel 32:4–5; Ezekiel 39:4–5"> And filled his mountains with his wounded, &#124;&#124; Your hills, and your valleys, and all your streams, &#124;&#124; The pierced of the sword fall into them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Malachi 1:3–4; Ezekiel 6:7"> I make you continuous desolations, &#124;&#124; And your cities do not return, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:5; Psalms 83:4–12; Ezekiel 48:35"> Because of your saying: The two nations and the two lands are mine, and we have possessed it, &#124;&#124; And YHWH has been there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 9:16; Matthew 7:2; Psalms 73:17–18"> Therefore, &#91;as&#93; I live—a declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; And I have done according to your anger, &#124;&#124; And according to your envy, &#124;&#124; With which you have worked, &#124;&#124; Because of your hatred against them, &#124;&#124; And I have been known among them when I judge you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:2; Psalms 83:12; Ezekiel 6:7"> And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; I have heard all your despisings that you have spoken &#124;&#124; Against mountains of Israel, saying, &#124;&#124; A desolation—they were given to us for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 11:36; Isaiah 36:20; Jeremiah 29:23"> And you magnify yourselves against Me with your mouth, &#124;&#124; And have made your words abundant against Me; I have heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:48; Isaiah 14:7–8; Isaiah 65:13–15"> Thus said Lord YHWH: According to the rejoicing of the whole land, &#124;&#124; I make a desolation of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:3–4; Lamentations 4:21; Isaiah 34:5–6"> According to your joy at the inheritance of the house of Israel because of desolation, &#124;&#124; So I do to you—you are a desolation, &#124;&#124; O Mount Seir, and all Edom—all of it, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH!”</span>
