<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:1; Ezekiel 40:17; Ezekiel 42:14"> And he causes me to turn back the way of the gate of the outer sanctuary that is looking eastward, and it is shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2–4; Exodus 24:10; Isaiah 6:1–5"> And YHWH says to me, “This gate is shut, it is not opened, and none go in by it, for YHWH, God of Israel, has come in by it, and it has been shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:2; Ezekiel 34:24; Ezekiel 37:25"> The prince, who &#91;is&#93; prince, he sits by it to eat bread before YHWH, he comes in by the way of the porch of the gate, and by its way he goes out.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Revelation 1:17; Ezekiel 3:23"> And he brings me in the way of the north gate to the front of the house, and I look, and behold, the glory of YHWH has filled the house of YHWH, and I fall on my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:4; Ezekiel 43:10–11; Deuteronomy 12:32"> And YHWH says to me, “Son of man, set your heart, and see with your eyes, and hear with your ears, all that I am speaking with you, all of the statutes of the house of YHWH, and all of its laws; and you have set your heart to the entrance of the house, with all the outlets of the sanctuary,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:9; Ezekiel 45:9; 1 Peter 4:3"> and have said to &#91;the&#93; rebellion, to the house of Israel, Thus said Lord YHWH: Enough from you—of all your abominations, O house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 22:25; Jeremiah 9:26; Ezekiel 44:9"> In your bringing in sons of a stranger, uncircumcised of heart, and uncircumcised of flesh, to be in My sanctuary, to defile it, even My house, in your bringing near My bread, fat, and blood, and they break My covenant by all your abominations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:14; Numbers 18:3–5; Ezekiel 40:45–46"> and you have not kept &#91;the&#93; charge of My holy things, and you set &#91;others&#93; for keeping My charge in My sanctuary for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Ezekiel 44:7; Zechariah 14:21"> Thus said Lord YHWH: No son of a stranger, uncircumcised of heart, and uncircumcised of flesh, comes into My sanctuary, even any son of a stranger, who &#91;is&#93; in the midst of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:26; Ezekiel 48:11; 2 Kings 23:8–9"> And the Levites who have gone far off from Me, in the wandering of Israel when they went astray from Me after their idols, and they have borne their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 16:9; 2 Chronicles 29:34; 2 Chronicles 30:17"> And they have been servants in My sanctuary, overseers at the gates of the house, and servants at the house; they slay the burnt-offering and the sacrifice for the people, and they stand before them to serve them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 16:10–16; Psalms 106:26; Ezekiel 14:3–4"> Because that they serve them before their idols, and have been for a stumbling-block of iniquity to the house of Israel, therefore I have lifted up My hand against them—a declaration of Lord YHWH—and they have borne their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 18:3; 2 Kings 23:9; Ezekiel 16:61"> And they do not draw near to Me to act as My priest, and to draw near to any of My holy things, to the Holy of Holies, and they have borne their shame and their abominations that they have done,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:28–32; Numbers 18:4"> and I made them keepers of the charge of the house, for all its service and for all that is done in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Ezekiel 48:11; Ezekiel 44:7"> And the priests, the Levites, sons of Zadok, who have kept the charge of My sanctuary in the wandering of the sons of Israel from off Me, they draw near to Me to serve Me, and have stood before Me, to bring fat and blood near to Me—a declaration of Lord YHWH:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:22; Malachi 1:12; Numbers 18:7–8"> they come into My sanctuary, and they draw near to My table to serve Me, and they have kept My charge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Exodus 28:43; Exodus 39:27–29"> And it has come to pass, in their going into the gates of the inner court, they put on linen garments; and no wool comes up on them in their ministering in the gates of the inner court and within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 39:28; 1 Corinthians 11:4–10; Isaiah 3:20"> Linen headdresses are on their head, and linen trousers are on their loins, they are not restrained with sweat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:20; Leviticus 6:27; Leviticus 6:10–11"> And in their going forth to the outer court—to the outer court to the people—they strip off their garments, in which they are ministering, and have placed them in the holy chambers, and have put on other garments; and they do not sanctify the people in their own garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:14; Numbers 6:5; Leviticus 21:5–24"> And they do not shave their head, and they do not send forth the lock; they certainly trim their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 10:9; Titus 1:7–8; 1 Timothy 5:23"> And no priest drinks wine in their coming into the inner court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; Leviticus 21:13–14; 1 Timothy 3:2"> And they do not take a widow or divorced woman to themselves for wives, but they take virgins of the seed of the house of Israel, and the widow who is widow of a priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:26; Leviticus 10:10–11; 2 Timothy 2:24–25"> And they direct My people between holy and common, and they cause them to discern between unclean and clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:4; Ezekiel 22:26; 2 Chronicles 19:8–10"> And concerning controversy, they stand up for judgment; they judge it with My judgments; and they keep My law and My statutes in all My appointed places; and they sanctify My Sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:16; Leviticus 22:4; Matthew 8:21–22"> And he does not come near to any dead man to be defiled, but if for father, and for mother, and for son, and for daughter, for brother, for sister who has not been to a man, they may defile themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 9:13–14; Numbers 19:11–14; Numbers 6:10–21"> And after his cleansing, they number seven days to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 6:9–11; Ezekiel 44:17; Leviticus 4:3–35"> And in the day of his coming into the sanctuary, into the inner court, to minister in the sanctuary, he brings his sin-offering near—a declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 13:33; Numbers 18:20; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> And it has been for an inheritance to them; I &#91;am&#93; their inheritance, and you do not give a possession to them in Israel; I &#91;am&#93; their possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 27:21; Leviticus 27:28"> The present, and the sin-offering, and the guilt-offering, they eat, and every devoted thing in Israel is theirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:35–37; 2 Chronicles 31:10; Deuteronomy 18:4"> And the first of all the first-fruits of all, and every raised-offering of all, of all your raised-offerings, are the priests’; and you give the first of your dough to the priest, to cause a blessing to rest on your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 22:8; Exodus 22:31; Deuteronomy 14:21"> The priests do not eat any carcass or torn thing, from the bird or from the beast.”</span>
