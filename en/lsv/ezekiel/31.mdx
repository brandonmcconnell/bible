<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:20; Jeremiah 52:5–6; Ezekiel 1:2"> And it comes to pass, in the eleventh year, in the third &#91;month&#93;, on the first of the month, a word of YHWH has been to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 30:10; Ezekiel 31:18"> “Son of man, say to Pharaoh king of Egypt, and to his multitude: To whom have you been like in your greatness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:33–34; Daniel 4:10; Daniel 4:20–23"> Behold, Asshur, a cedar in Lebanon, &#124;&#124; Beautiful in branch, and shading bough, and high in stature, &#124;&#124; And its foliage has been between thickets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 17:15; Proverbs 14:28; Ezekiel 17:5"> Waters have made it great, &#124;&#124; The deep has exalted him with its flowings, &#124;&#124; Going around its planting, &#124;&#124; And it has sent forth its conduits to all trees of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:11; Ezekiel 17:5; Psalms 1:3"> Therefore his stature has been higher than all trees of the field, &#124;&#124; And his boughs are multiplied, and his branches are long, &#124;&#124; Because of many waters in his shooting forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:23; Matthew 13:32; Daniel 4:12"> Every bird of the heavens has made a nest in his boughs, &#124;&#124; And every beast of the field has brought forth under his branches, &#124;&#124; And all great nations dwell in his shade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And he is beautiful in his greatness, &#124;&#124; In the length of his thin shoots, &#124;&#124; For his root has been by great waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Genesis 13:10; Isaiah 51:3"> Cedars have not hid him in the garden of God, &#124;&#124; Firs have not been like to his boughs, &#124;&#124; And plane-trees have not been as his branches, &#124;&#124; No tree in the garden of God has been like to him in his beauty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Daniel 2:37–38; Genesis 13:10"> I have made him beautiful in the multitude of his thin shoots, &#124;&#124; And all trees of Eden that &#91;are&#93; in the garden of God envy him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:17; Daniel 5:20; 2 Chronicles 32:25"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: Because that you have been high in stature, &#124;&#124; And he yields his foliage between thickets, &#124;&#124; And his heart is high in his haughtiness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:11–12; Nahum 3:18; Daniel 5:18–19"> I give him into the hand of a god of nations, &#124;&#124; He deals severely with him, &#124;&#124; In his wickedness I have cast him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Ezekiel 35:8; Ezekiel 30:11"> And strangers cut him off, &#124;&#124; The terrible of nations, and they leave him, &#124;&#124; His thin shoots have fallen on the mountains and in all valleys, &#124;&#124; And his boughs are broken at all streams of the land, &#124;&#124; And all peoples of the land go down from his shade, and they leave him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:4; Ezekiel 29:5; Isaiah 18:6"> Every bird of the heavens dwells on his ruin, &#124;&#124; And all beasts of the field have been on his boughs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 63:9–10; Nehemiah 13:18; 2 Peter 2:6"> In order that none of the trees of the waters &#124;&#124; May become haughty because of their stature, &#124;&#124; Nor give their foliage between thickets, &#124;&#124; Nor any drinking waters stand up to them in their haughtiness, &#124;&#124; For all of them are given up to death, &#124;&#124; To the lower earth, &#124;&#124; In the midst of the sons of men, &#124;&#124; To those going down to the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 3:4; Nahum 2:8–10; Revelation 18:9–11"> Thus said Lord YHWH: I have caused mourning in the day of his going down to Sheol, &#124;&#124; I have covered the deep over him, and diminish its flowings, &#124;&#124; And many waters are restrained, &#124;&#124; And I make Lebanon black for him, &#124;&#124; And all trees of the field &#91;have been&#93; a covering for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 14:15; Isaiah 14:8; Ezekiel 26:15"> I have caused nations to shake at the sound of his fall, &#124;&#124; In My causing him to go down to Sheol, &#124;&#124; With those going down to the pit, &#124;&#124; And all trees of Eden are comforted in the lower earth, &#124;&#124; The choice and good of Lebanon—All drinking waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 9:17; Ezekiel 31:6; Ezekiel 31:3"> Those with him have also gone down to Sheol, &#124;&#124; To the pierced of the sword, &#124;&#124; And—his arm—they dwelt in his shade in the midst of nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:19; Ezekiel 32:21; Ezekiel 28:10"> To whom have you been thus like, &#124;&#124; In glory and in greatness among the trees of Eden? And you have been brought down with the trees of Eden, &#124;&#124; To the lower earth, &#124;&#124; You lie in the midst of the uncircumcised, &#124;&#124; With the pierced of the sword. This &#91;is&#93; Pharaoh, and all his multitude, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH!”</span>
