<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:4; Ezekiel 34:14; Ezekiel 37:22"> “And you, son of man, prophesy to mountains of Israel, and you have said, O mountains of Israel, hear a word of YHWH!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Ezekiel 25:3; Ezekiel 35:10"> Thus said Lord YHWH: Because the enemy said against you, Aha! The high places of old have also become our possession,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 44:13–14; Lamentations 2:15–16; Jeremiah 18:16"> therefore, prophesy, and you have said, Thus said Lord YHWH: Because, even because, of desolating, &#124;&#124; And of swallowing you up from all around, &#124;&#124; For your being a possession to the remnant of the nations, &#124;&#124; And you are taken up on the tip of the tongue, &#124;&#124; And &#91;are&#93; an evil report to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:28; Deuteronomy 11:11; Ezekiel 36:6"> Therefore, O mountains of Israel, &#124;&#124; Hear a word of Lord YHWH! Thus said Lord YHWH, to mountains, and to hills, &#124;&#124; To streams, and to valleys, &#124;&#124; And to ruins that &#91;are&#93; desolate, &#124;&#124; And to cities that are forsaken, &#124;&#124; That have been for a prey, &#124;&#124; And for a scorn, to the remnant of the surrounding nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 7:8; Ezekiel 36:3; Psalms 137:7"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: Have I not, in the fire of My jealousy, &#124;&#124; Spoken against the remnant of the nations, &#124;&#124; And against Edom—all of it, &#124;&#124; Who gave My land to themselves for a possession, &#124;&#124; With the joy of the whole heart—with despite of soul, &#124;&#124; For the sake of casting it out for a prey?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 123:3–4; Ezekiel 34:29; Psalms 74:23"> Therefore, prophesy concerning the ground of Israel, &#124;&#124; And you have said to mountains, and to hills, &#124;&#124; To streams, and to valleys, &#124;&#124; Thus said Lord YHWH: Behold, I, in My jealousy and in My fury, have spoken, &#124;&#124; Because you have borne the shame of nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:5; Amos 1:1–15; Ezekiel 20:15"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: I have lifted up My hand, &#124;&#124; Do the nations who &#91;are&#93; surrounding you not bear their own shame?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; Hebrews 10:37; Ezekiel 34:26–29"> And you, O mountains of Israel, &#124;&#124; You give out your branch, &#124;&#124; And you bear your fruits for My people Israel, &#124;&#124; For they have drawn near to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 46:11; Hosea 2:21–23; Ezekiel 36:34"> For behold, I &#91;am&#93; for you, and have turned to you, &#124;&#124; And you have been tilled and sown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:33; Jeremiah 33:12; Ezekiel 36:37"> And I have multiplied men on you, &#124;&#124; All the house of Israel—all of it, &#124;&#124; And the cities have been inhabited, &#124;&#124; And the ruins are built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:12; Jeremiah 23:5–8; Jeremiah 31:27"> And I have multiplied man and beast on you, &#124;&#124; And they have multiplied and been fruitful, &#124;&#124; And I have caused you to dwell according to your former states, &#124;&#124; And I have done better than at your beginnings, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:7; Ezekiel 36:13; Jeremiah 32:44"> And I have caused man to walk over you—My people Israel, &#124;&#124; And they possess you, and you have been their inheritance, &#124;&#124; And you no longer add to bereave them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 13:32"> Thus said Lord YHWH: Because they are saying to you, You &#91;are&#93; a devourer of men, &#124;&#124; And you have been a bereaver of your nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:25–28; Amos 9:15; Isaiah 60:21"> Therefore, you no longer devour man, &#124;&#124; And you do not cause your nations to stumble anymore, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:29; Psalms 89:50; Ezekiel 36:6"> And I do not proclaim the shame of the nations to you anymore, &#124;&#124; And you no longer bear the reproach of peoples, &#124;&#124; And your nations do not stumble anymore, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:7; Numbers 35:33–34; Micah 2:10"> “Son of man, &#124;&#124; The house of Israel are dwelling on their land, &#124;&#124; And they defile it by their way and by their doings, &#124;&#124; Their way has been as the uncleanness of a separated one before Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:21; Ezekiel 7:8; Lamentations 2:4"> And I pour out My fury on them &#124;&#124; For the blood that they shed on the land, &#124;&#124; And they have defiled it with their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:24; Deuteronomy 28:64; Ezekiel 5:12"> And I scatter them among nations, &#124;&#124; And they are spread through lands, &#124;&#124; According to their way, and according to their doings, &#124;&#124; I have judged them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Isaiah 52:5; Jeremiah 33:24"> And one goes to the nations to where they have gone, &#124;&#124; And they defile My holy Name by saying to them, &#124;&#124; These &#91;are&#93; the people of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And they have gone forth from His land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 48:9; Psalms 74:18; Ezekiel 20:9"> And I have pity on My holy Name, &#124;&#124; That the house of Israel has defiled &#124;&#124; Among the nations to where they have gone in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 106:8; Ezekiel 36:32; Psalms 115:1–2"> Therefore, say to the house of Israel, Thus said Lord YHWH: I am not working for your sake, &#124;&#124; O house of Israel, but for My holy Name &#124;&#124; That you have defiled &#124;&#124; Among the nations to where you have gone in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:41; Isaiah 5:16; Ezekiel 39:7"> And I have sanctified My great Name, &#124;&#124; That is profaned among the nations, &#124;&#124; That you have defiled in your midst, &#124;&#124; And the nations have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; In My being sanctified in you before your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:21; Ezekiel 34:13; Amos 9:14–15"> And I have taken you out of the nations, &#124;&#124; And have gathered you out of all the lands, &#124;&#124; And I have brought you into your land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; John 3:5; Jeremiah 33:8"> And I have sprinkled clean water over you, &#124;&#124; And you have been clean; I cleanse you from all your uncleannesses, &#124;&#124; And from all your idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19–20; Psalms 51:10; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> And I have given a new heart to you, &#124;&#124; And I give a new spirit in your midst, &#124;&#124; And I have turned aside the heart of stone out of your flesh, &#124;&#124; And I have given a heart of flesh to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Titus 3:3–6; Hebrews 13:21; 1 Corinthians 3:16"> And I give My Spirit in your midst, &#124;&#124; And I have done this, so that you walk in My statutes, &#124;&#124; And you keep My judgments, and have done them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33; Jeremiah 32:38; Ezekiel 37:27"> And you have dwelt in the land that I have given to your fathers, &#124;&#124; And you have been to Me for a people, &#124;&#124; And I am to you for God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 1:7–9; Matthew 1:21; Hosea 2:21–23"> And I have saved you from all your uncleannesses, &#124;&#124; And I have called to the grain, and multiplied it, &#124;&#124; And I have put no famine on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:27; Joel 2:26; Deuteronomy 29:23–28"> And I have multiplied the fruit of the tree, &#124;&#124; And the increase of the field, &#124;&#124; So that you do not receive a reproach of famine among nations anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Ezekiel 20:43; Nehemiah 9:26–35"> And you have remembered your ways that &#91;are&#93; evil, &#124;&#124; And your doings that &#91;are&#93; not good, &#124;&#124; And have been loathsome in your own faces, &#124;&#124; For your iniquities, and for your abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:5; Ezekiel 36:22; 2 Timothy 1:9"> I am not working for your sake, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; Be it known to you, &#124;&#124; Be ashamed and confounded, because of your ways, &#124;&#124; O house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 58:12; Ezekiel 36:10; Jeremiah 33:10"> Thus said Lord YHWH: In the day of My cleansing you from all your iniquities, &#124;&#124; I have caused the cities to be inhabited, &#124;&#124; And the ruins have been built,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:14; Jeremiah 25:9–11; 2 Chronicles 36:21"> And the desolate land is tilled, &#124;&#124; Instead of which it was a desolation before the eyes of everyone passing by,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Joel 2:3; Jeremiah 33:9"> And they have said: This land, that was desolated, &#124;&#124; Has been as the Garden of Eden, &#124;&#124; And the cities—the dried up, &#124;&#124; And the desolated, and the broken down, &#124;&#124; &#91;And the&#93; fortified have remained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Numbers 23:19; Ezekiel 17:24"> And the nations who are left around you have known &#124;&#124; That I, YHWH, have built the thrown down, &#124;&#124; I have planted the desolated: I, YHWH, have spoken, and I have done &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 5:14; Hebrews 10:21–22; Ezekiel 14:3"> Thus said Lord YHWH: Yet this I am inquired of by the house of Israel to do for them: I multiply them as a flock of men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 8:63; Acts 2:5–11; John 10:16"> As a flock of holy ones, as a flock of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; In her appointed times, &#124;&#124; So the dried up cities are full of flocks of man, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH!”</span>
