<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Ezekiel 11:24; Ezekiel 11:13"> And &#91;the&#93; Spirit lifts me up, and it brings me to the east gate of the house of YHWH, that is facing the east, and behold, at the opening of the gate &#91;are&#93; twenty-five men, and I see in their midst Jaazaniah son of Azzur, and Pelatiah son of Benaiah, heads of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:1; Jeremiah 5:5; Psalms 2:1–2"> And He says to me, “Son of man, these &#91;are&#93; the men who are devising iniquity, and who are giving evil counsel in this city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:27; Ezekiel 12:22; Ezekiel 11:7–11"> who are saying, It &#91;is&#93; not near—to build houses, it &#91;is&#93; the pot, and we &#91;are&#93; the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46–47; Ezekiel 21:2; Ezekiel 3:2–15"> Therefore prophesy concerning them, prophesy, son of man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Jeremiah 16:17; Revelation 2:23"> And &#91;the&#93; Spirit of YHWH falls on me, and He says to me, “Say, Thus said YHWH: You have said correctly, O house of Israel, &#124;&#124; And I have known the steps of your spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:23; Ezekiel 22:27; Ezekiel 22:12"> You multiplied your wounded in this city, &#124;&#124; And filled its out-places with the wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:3–13; Micah 3:2–3; 2 Kings 25:18–22"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: Your wounded whom you placed in its midst, &#124;&#124; They &#91;are&#93; the flesh, and it &#91;is&#93; the pot, &#124;&#124; And He has brought you out from its midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:24; Isaiah 24:17–18; Job 3:25"> You have feared a sword, &#124;&#124; And I bring in a sword against you, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:8; Psalms 106:41; Deuteronomy 28:36"> And I have brought you out of its midst, &#124;&#124; And given you into the hand of strangers, &#124;&#124; And I have done judgments among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 14:25; Jeremiah 52:9–10; 2 Kings 25:19–21"> You fall by the sword, &#124;&#124; I judge you on the border of Israel, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 11:7–10"> It is not for a pot for you, &#124;&#124; Nor are you for flesh in its midst, &#124;&#124; I judge you at the border of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:10; Ezekiel 8:16; Ezekiel 8:14"> And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; For you have not walked in My statutes, &#124;&#124; And you have not done My judgments, &#124;&#124; And according to the judgments of the nations &#124;&#124; Who are around you, you have done!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:1; Ezekiel 9:8; Acts 5:5"> And it comes to pass, at my prophesying, that Pelatiah son of Benaiah is dying, and I fall on my face, and cry—a loud voice—and say, “Aah! Lord YHWH, You are making an end of the remnant of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:24; Isaiah 65:5; John 16:2"> “Son of man, your brothers, your brothers, men of your family, and all the house of Israel—all of it—&#91;are&#93; they to whom inhabitants of Jerusalem have said, Keep far off from YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; Deuteronomy 30:3–4; Psalms 90:1"> it &#91;is&#93; ours, the land has been given for an inheritance; therefore say, Thus said Lord YHWH: Because I put them far off among nations, &#124;&#124; And because I scattered them through lands, I am also for a little sanctuary to them, &#124;&#124; In lands to where they have gone in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:25; Jeremiah 3:18; Ezekiel 34:13"> Therefore say, Thus said Lord YHWH: And I have assembled you from the peoples, &#124;&#124; And I have gathered you from the lands, &#124;&#124; Into which you have been scattered, &#124;&#124; And I have given the ground of Israel to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:23; Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 42:7–8"> And they have gone in there, &#124;&#124; And turned aside all its detestable things, &#124;&#124; And all its abominations—out of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26–27; Jeremiah 31:33; Psalms 51:10"> And I have given one heart to them, &#124;&#124; And I give a new spirit in your midst, &#124;&#124; And I have turned the heart of stone out of their flesh, &#124;&#124; And I have given a heart of flesh to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:11; Psalms 105:45; Jeremiah 30:22"> So that they walk in My statutes, &#124;&#124; And keep My judgments, and have done them, &#124;&#124; And they have been to Me for a people, &#124;&#124; And I am to them for God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:10; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Jeremiah 1:16"> As for those whose heart is going to the heart &#124;&#124; Of their detestable things and their abominations, &#124;&#124; I have put their way on their head, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:19; Ezekiel 1:19–20"> And the cherubim lift up their wings, and the wheels &#91;are&#93; alongside them, and the glory of the God of Israel &#91;is&#93; over them above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 14:4; Ezekiel 8:4; Ezekiel 10:4"> And the glory of YHWH goes up from off the midst of the city, and stands on the mountain, that &#91;is&#93; on the east of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:12; Ezekiel 8:3; Ezekiel 11:1"> And &#91;the&#93; Spirit has lifted me up, and brings me to Chaldea, to the expulsion, in a vision, by &#91;the&#93; Spirit of God, and the vision that I have seen goes up from off me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:4; Ezekiel 3:27; Ezekiel 3:17"> and I speak to the expulsion all the matters of YHWH that He has showed me.</span>
