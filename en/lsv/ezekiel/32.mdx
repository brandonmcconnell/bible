<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:20; Ezekiel 32:17; Ezekiel 31:1"> And it comes to pass, in the twelfth year, in the twelfth month, on the first of the month, a word of YHWH has been to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Nahum 2:11–13; Ezekiel 27:2"> “Son of man, lift up a lamentation for Pharaoh king of Egypt, and you have said to him: You have been like a young lion of nations, &#124;&#124; And you &#91;are&#93; as a dragon in the seas, &#124;&#124; And you come forth with your flowings, &#124;&#124; And trouble the waters with your feet, &#124;&#124; And you foul their flowings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 16:16; Ecclesiastes 9:12"> Thus said Lord YHWH: And I have spread out My net for you, &#124;&#124; With an assembly of many peoples, &#124;&#124; And they have brought you up in My net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 18:6; Ezekiel 29:5; Ezekiel 31:12–13"> And I have left you in the land, &#124;&#124; I cast you out on the face of the field, &#124;&#124; And I have caused every bird of the heavens to dwell on you, &#124;&#124; And have satisfied every beast of the earth with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:12"> And I have put your flesh on the mountains, &#124;&#124; And filled the valleys &#91;with&#93; your heap,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 34:3; Isaiah 34:7; Revelation 14:20"> And watered the land with your flowing, &#124;&#124; From your blood—to the mountains, &#124;&#124; And streams are filled with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Joel 2:31; Isaiah 13:10"> And in quenching you I have covered the heavens, &#124;&#124; And have made their stars black, &#124;&#124; I cover the sun with a cloud, &#124;&#124; And the moon does not cause its light to shine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:14; Proverbs 20:20"> I make all luminaries of light in the heavens black over you, &#124;&#124; And I have given darkness over your land, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 18:10–15; Ezekiel 30:26; Jeremiah 25:15–25"> And I have distressed the heart of many peoples, &#124;&#124; In My bringing in your destruction among nations, &#124;&#124; To lands that you have not known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:16; Ezekiel 27:35; 1 Kings 9:8"> And I have made many peoples astonished at you, &#124;&#124; And their kings are afraid because of you with trembling, &#124;&#124; In My brandishing My sword before their faces, &#124;&#124; And they have trembled every moment, &#124;&#124; Each for his life—in the day of your fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:10; Ezekiel 26:7; Ezekiel 30:22–25"> For thus said Lord YHWH: A sword of the king of Babylon enters you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 31:11"> I cause your multitude to fall by swords of the mighty, &#124;&#124; The terrible of nations—all of them, &#124;&#124; And they have spoiled the excellence of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And all her multitude has been destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:11; Ezekiel 29:8; Ezekiel 30:12"> And I have destroyed all her beasts, &#124;&#124; From beside many waters, &#124;&#124; And a foot of man does not trouble them anymore, &#124;&#124; Indeed, the hooves of beasts do not trouble them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Then I cause their waters to sink, &#124;&#124; And I cause their rivers to go as oil, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Exodus 7:5; Exodus 14:4"> In My making the land of Egypt a desolation, &#124;&#124; And the land has been desolated of its fullness, &#124;&#124; In My striking all the inhabitants in it, &#124;&#124; Then they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:17; 2 Chronicles 35:25; Ezekiel 26:17"> It &#91;is&#93; a lamentation, and they have lamented her, &#124;&#124; Daughters of the nations lament her, &#124;&#124; For Egypt, and for all her multitude, they lament her, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:1; Ezekiel 33:21; Ezekiel 1:2"> And it comes to pass, in the twelfth year, on the fifteenth of the month, a word of YHWH has been to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:14; Ezekiel 32:2; Jeremiah 1:10"> “Son of man, &#124;&#124; Wail for the multitude of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And cause it to go down, &#124;&#124; It, and the daughters of majestic nations, &#124;&#124; To the lower parts of the earth, &#124;&#124; With those going down to the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:18; Ezekiel 32:29–30; Jeremiah 9:25–26"> Whom have you surpassed in beauty? Go down, and be laid with &#91;the&#93; uncircumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 28:3; Ezekiel 32:23–26; Ezekiel 32:29–30"> They fall in the midst of the pierced of the sword, &#124;&#124; She has been given &#91;to&#93; the sword, &#124;&#124; They drew her out, and all her multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 14:9–10; Ezekiel 32:27; Ezekiel 32:19"> The gods of the mighty speak to him out of the midst of Sheol, &#124;&#124; With his helpers—they have gone down, &#124;&#124; They have lain with the uncircumcised, &#124;&#124; The pierced of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:26; Ezekiel 32:29–30; Ezekiel 32:24"> There &#91;is&#93; Asshur, and all her assembly, &#124;&#124; His graves &#91;are&#93; around him, &#124;&#124; All of them &#91;are&#93; wounded, who are falling by sword,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:20; Ezekiel 32:24–27; Psalms 27:13"> Whose graves are appointed in the sides of the pit, &#124;&#124; And her assembly is around her grave, &#124;&#124; All of them wounded, falling by sword, &#124;&#124; Because they gave terror in the land of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:18; Ezekiel 26:20; Jeremiah 49:34–39"> There &#91;is&#93; Elam, and all her multitude, &#124;&#124; Around her grave, &#124;&#124; All of them wounded, who are falling by sword, &#124;&#124; Who have gone down uncircumcised to the lower parts of the earth, &#124;&#124; Because they gave their terror in the land of the living, &#124;&#124; And they bear their shame with those going down to the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 139:8; 1 Chronicles 10:4; 2 Samuel 1:20"> They have appointed a bed for her in the midst of the wounded, &#124;&#124; With all her multitude, &#124;&#124; Her graves &#91;are&#93; around them, &#124;&#124; All of them uncircumcised, pierced of the sword, &#124;&#124; For their terror was given in the land of the living, &#124;&#124; And they bear their shame with those going down to the pit, &#124;&#124; He has been set in the midst of the pierced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:13; Genesis 10:2; Ezekiel 38:2–3"> There &#91;is&#93; Meshech, Tubal, and all her multitude, &#124;&#124; Her graves &#91;are&#93; around them, &#124;&#124; All of them uncircumcised, pierced by the sword, &#124;&#124; For they gave their terror in the land of the living,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 14:18–19; Job 3:13–15; Ezekiel 32:21"> And they do not lie with the mighty, &#124;&#124; Who are falling of the uncircumcised, &#124;&#124; Who have gone down to Sheol with their weapons of war, &#124;&#124; And they put their swords under their heads, &#124;&#124; And their iniquities are on their bones, &#124;&#124; For the terror of the mighty &#91;is&#93; in the land of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Daniel 2:34–35"> And you are broken in the midst of the uncircumcised, &#124;&#124; And lie with the pierced of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:7–22; Malachi 1:3–4; Obadiah 1:1–9"> There &#91;is&#93; Edom, her kings, and all her princes, &#124;&#124; Who have been given up in their might, &#124;&#124; With the pierced of the sword, &#124;&#124; They lie with the uncircumcised, &#124;&#124; And with those going down to the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:6; Ezekiel 39:2; Ezekiel 28:21"> There &#91;are&#93; princes of the north, &#124;&#124; All of them, and every Zidonian, &#124;&#124; Who have gone down with the pierced in their terror, &#124;&#124; They are ashamed of their might, &#124;&#124; And they lie uncircumcised with the pierced of the sword, &#124;&#124; And they bear their shame with those going down to the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:16; Ezekiel 14:22; Lamentations 2:13"> Then Pharaoh sees, &#124;&#124; And he has been comforted for all his multitude, &#124;&#124; The pierced of the sword—Pharaoh and all his force, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:27; Genesis 35:5; 2 Corinthians 5:11"> For I have given his terror in the land of the living, &#124;&#124; And he has been laid down in &#91;the&#93; midst of &#91;the&#93; uncircumcised, &#124;&#124; With &#91;the&#93; pierced of &#91;the&#93; sword—Pharaoh, and all his multitude, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH!”</span>
