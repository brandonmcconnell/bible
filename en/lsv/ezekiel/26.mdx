<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:1; Ezekiel 1:2; Jeremiah 39:2"> And it comes to pass, in the eleventh year, on the first of the month, there has been a word of YHWH to me, saying, “Son of man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 23:1–18; Ezekiel 36:2; Joel 3:4"> Because that Tyre has said of Jerusalem: Aha, she has been broken, &#124;&#124; The doors of the peoples, &#124;&#124; She has turned around to me, &#124;&#124; I am filled—she has been laid waste,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:42; Isaiah 5:30; Micah 4:11"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: Behold, I &#91;am&#93; against you, O Tyre, &#124;&#124; And have caused many nations to come up against you, &#124;&#124; As the sea causes its billows to come up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 23:11; Amos 1:10; Ezekiel 26:9"> And they have destroyed the walls of Tyre, &#124;&#124; And they have broken down her towers, &#124;&#124; And I have scraped her dust from her, &#124;&#124; And made her for a clear place of a rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 27:32; Ezekiel 25:7"> She is a spreading place of nets in the midst of the sea, &#124;&#124; For I have spoken,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And she has been for a spoil to nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:46; Ezekiel 26:8; Jeremiah 49:2"> And her daughters who &#91;are&#93; in the field, are slain by sword, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 7:12; Daniel 2:37; Ezekiel 23:24"> For thus said Lord YHWH: “Behold, I am bringing Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon to Tyre, &#124;&#124; From the north—a king of kings, &#124;&#124; With horse, and with chariot, and with horsemen, &#124;&#124; Even an assembly, and a numerous people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:22; Jeremiah 52:4; Jeremiah 32:24"> He slays your daughters in the field by sword, &#124;&#124; And he has made a fort against you, &#124;&#124; And has poured out a mound against you, &#124;&#124; And has raised a buckler against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:15"> And he places a battering-ram before him against your walls, &#124;&#124; And he breaks your towers with his weapons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:15; Ezekiel 27:28; Jeremiah 47:3"> From the abundance of his horses their dust cover you, &#124;&#124; From the noise of horseman, and wheel, and rider, &#124;&#124; Your walls shake, in his coming into your gates, &#124;&#124; As the coming into a city that has been broken-up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:28; Isaiah 26:5; Habakkuk 1:8"> He treads all your out-places with &#91;the&#93; hooves of his horses, &#124;&#124; He slays your people by sword, &#124;&#124; And the pillars of your strength come down to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 23:8; 2 Chronicles 32:27; Zechariah 9:3–4"> And they have spoiled your wealth, &#124;&#124; And they have plundered your merchandise, &#124;&#124; And they have thrown down your walls, &#124;&#124; And they break down your desirable houses, &#124;&#124; And your stones, and your wood, and your dust, &#124;&#124; They place in the midst of the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Isaiah 23:16; Isaiah 14:11"> And I have caused the noise of your songs to cease, &#124;&#124; And the voice of your harps is heard no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 1:4; Job 12:14; Numbers 23:19"> And I have given you up for a clear place of a rock, &#124;&#124; You are a spreading-place of nets, &#124;&#124; You are not built up anymore, &#124;&#124; For I, YHWH, have spoken,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:35; Jeremiah 49:21; Ezekiel 26:18"> Thus said Lord YHWH to Tyre: “Do not—from the noise of your fall, &#124;&#124; In the groaning of the wounded, &#124;&#124; In the slaying of the slaughter in your midst—The islands shake?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:10; Jonah 3:6; Job 8:22"> And all princes of the sea have come down from off their thrones, &#124;&#124; And they have turned their robes aside, &#124;&#124; And strip off their embroidered garments, &#124;&#124; They put on trembling, &#124;&#124; They sit on the earth, &#124;&#124; And they have trembled every moment, &#124;&#124; And they have been astonished at you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 14:12; Ezekiel 19:1; 2 Samuel 1:25–27"> And have lifted up a lamentation for you, &#124;&#124; And said to you: How you have perished, &#124;&#124; That are inhabited from the seas, &#124;&#124; The praised city, that was strong in the sea, &#124;&#124; She and her inhabitants, &#124;&#124; Who put their terror on all her inhabitants!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:15; Isaiah 23:5–7; Ezekiel 27:35"> Now the islands tremble &#91;on the&#93; day of your fall; The islands that &#91;are&#93; in the sea have been troubled at your outgoing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Ezekiel 26:3; Daniel 11:40"> For thus said Lord YHWH: “In My making you a city dried up, &#124;&#124; Like cities that have not been inhabited, &#124;&#124; In bringing up the deep against you, &#124;&#124; Then the great waters have covered you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 27:13; Luke 10:15; Zechariah 2:8"> And I have caused you to go down, &#124;&#124; With those going down to the pit, &#124;&#124; To the people of old, &#124;&#124; And I have caused you to dwell &#124;&#124; In the lower parts of the earth—in ancient ruins, &#124;&#124; With those going down to the pit, &#124;&#124; So that you are not inhabited, &#124;&#124; And I have given beauty in the land of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:36; Ezekiel 28:19; Revelation 18:21"> I make you a terror, and you are not, &#124;&#124; And you are sought, and are not found anymore—for all time,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH!</span>
