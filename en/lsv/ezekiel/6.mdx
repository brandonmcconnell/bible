<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:9; Ezekiel 34:14; Ezekiel 20:46"> “Son of man, set your face toward mountains of Israel, and prophesy concerning them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:30; Ezekiel 36:1–4; Jeremiah 3:23"> And you have said, Mountains of Israel, &#124;&#124; Hear a word of Lord YHWH! Thus said Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; To the mountains, and to the hills, &#124;&#124; To the streams, and to the valleys: Behold, I am bringing in a sword against you, &#124;&#124; And I have destroyed your high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:5; Leviticus 26:30; 2 Kings 23:14"> And your altars have been desolated, &#124;&#124; And your images have been broken, &#124;&#124; And I have caused your wounded to fall before your idols,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 23:16; Jeremiah 8:1–2; 2 Kings 23:14"> And I have put the carcasses of the sons of Israel before their idols, &#124;&#124; And I have scattered your bones around your altars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:14; Isaiah 6:11; Zechariah 13:2"> In all your dwellings the cities are laid waste, &#124;&#124; And the high places are desolate, &#124;&#124; So that your altars are dry and desolate, &#124;&#124; Your idols have broken and ceased, &#124;&#124; And your images have been cut down, &#124;&#124; And your works have been blotted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:33; Ezekiel 13:23; Ezekiel 35:15"> And the wounded has fallen in your midst, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:22; Jeremiah 44:28; Ezekiel 12:16"> And I have caused &#91;some&#93; to remain, &#124;&#124; In their being for you the escaped of the sword among nations, &#124;&#124; In your being scattered through lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:43; Isaiah 7:13; Job 42:6"> And your escaped have remembered Me among nations, &#124;&#124; To where they have been taken captive, &#124;&#124; Because I have been broken with their heart that is going whoring, &#124;&#124; That has turned aside from off Me, &#124;&#124; And with their eyes they are going whoring after their idols, &#124;&#124; And they have been loathsome in their own faces, &#124;&#124; For the evils that they have done—all their abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12–14; Daniel 9:12; Jeremiah 44:28"> And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; I have not spoken to do this evil to them for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:12; Ezekiel 9:4; Ezekiel 21:14–17"> Thus said Lord YHWH: Strike with your palm, and stamp with your foot, &#124;&#124; And say: Aah! For all the evil abominations of the house of Israel, &#124;&#124; Which falls by sword, by famine, and by pestilence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Daniel 9:7; Lamentations 4:22"> The far-off dies by pestilence, &#124;&#124; And the near falls by sword, &#124;&#124; And the left and the besieged dies by famine, &#124;&#124; And I have completed My fury on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:28; Hosea 4:13; 1 Kings 14:23"> And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; In their wounded being in the midst of their idols, &#124;&#124; Around their altars, &#124;&#124; On every high hill, on all tops of mountains, &#124;&#124; And under every green tree, and under every thick oak, &#124;&#124; The place where they gave refreshing fragrance to all their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25; Ezekiel 20:33–34; Isaiah 9:12"> And I have stretched out My hand against them, &#124;&#124; And have made the land a desolation, &#124;&#124; Even a desolation from the wilderness to Diblath, &#124;&#124; In all their dwellings, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH!”</span>
