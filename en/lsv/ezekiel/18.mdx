<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:29–30; Lamentations 5:7; Ezekiel 37:11"> “What &#91;is it&#93; to you &#91;that&#93; you are using this allegory &#124;&#124; Concerning the ground of Israel, saying, &#124;&#124; Fathers eat unripe fruit, &#124;&#124; And the sons’ teeth are blunted?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:19–20; Ezekiel 33:11–20; Ezekiel 18:30"> &#91;As&#93; I live,” a declaration of Lord YHWH, “You no longer have the use of this allegory in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:20; Romans 6:23; Numbers 16:22"> Behold, all the souls are Mine, &#124;&#124; As the soul of the father, &#124;&#124; So also the soul of the son—they are Mine, &#124;&#124; The soul that is sinning—it dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:14; 1 John 3:7; Proverbs 21:3"> And a man, when he is righteous, &#124;&#124; And has done judgment and righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:15; Deuteronomy 4:19; Ezekiel 6:13"> He has not eaten on the mountains, &#124;&#124; And has not lifted up his eyes &#124;&#124; To idols of the house of Israel, &#124;&#124; And did not defile his neighbor’s wife, &#124;&#124; And did not come near to a separated woman,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:16; Exodus 22:26; Ezekiel 18:12"> He does not oppress a man, &#124;&#124; He returns his pledge to the debtor, &#124;&#124; He does not take away plunder, &#124;&#124; He gives his bread to the hungry, &#124;&#124; And covers the naked with a garment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Zechariah 8:16; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> He does not give in usury, and does not take increase, &#124;&#124; He turns back his hand from perversity, &#124;&#124; He does true judgment between man and man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:11; Habakkuk 2:4; Amos 5:4"> He walks in My statutes, &#124;&#124; And he has kept My judgments—to deal truly, &#124;&#124; He &#91;is&#93; righteous—he surely lives,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 21:12; 1 John 3:12; John 18:40"> “And he has begotten a son, &#124;&#124; A burglar—a shedder of blood, &#124;&#124; And he has made a brother of one of these,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 22:14; Matthew 7:21–27; 1 Kings 13:8"> And he has not done all those, &#124;&#124; For he has even eaten on the mountains, &#124;&#124; And he has defiled his neighbor’s wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:17; Ezekiel 8:6; 2 Kings 21:11"> He has oppressed the afflicted and needy, &#124;&#124; He has taken away plunder violently, &#124;&#124; He does not return a pledge, &#124;&#124; And he has lifted up his eyes to the idols, &#124;&#124; He has done abomination!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Leviticus 20:9; Ezekiel 3:18"> He has given in usury, and taken increase, &#124;&#124; And he lives? He does not live, &#124;&#124; He has done all these abominations, &#124;&#124; He surely dies, his blood is on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:21; Proverbs 23:24; Jeremiah 8:6"> And—behold, he has begotten a son, &#124;&#124; And he sees all the sins of his father, &#124;&#124; That he has done, and he fears, &#124;&#124; And does not do like them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:6–7; Ezekiel 18:11–13"> He has not eaten on the mountains, &#124;&#124; And he has not lifted up his eyes &#124;&#124; To idols of the house of Israel, &#124;&#124; He has not defiled his neighbor’s wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:7; Isaiah 58:7–10; Job 22:7"> He has not oppressed a man, &#124;&#124; He has not bound a pledge, &#124;&#124; And he has not taken away plunder, &#124;&#124; He has given his bread to the hungry, &#124;&#124; And he covered the naked with a garment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:8–9; Matthew 18:27–35; Ezekiel 18:13"> He has turned back his hand from the afflicted, &#124;&#124; He has not taken usury and increase, &#124;&#124; He has done My judgments, &#124;&#124; He has walked in My statutes, &#124;&#124; He does not die for the iniquity of his father, &#124;&#124; He surely lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:18; Ezekiel 18:26; Ezekiel 18:24"> His father—because he used oppression, &#124;&#124; Violently plundered a brother, &#124;&#124; And did that which &#91;is&#93; not good in the midst of his people, &#124;&#124; And behold, he is dying in his iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Zechariah 1:3–6; Deuteronomy 5:9"> And you have said, Why has the son not borne of the iniquity of the father? And the son has done judgment and righteousness, &#124;&#124; He has kept all My statutes, &#124;&#124; And he does them, he surely lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:16; 2 Kings 14:6; Ezekiel 18:4"> The soul that sins—it dies. A son does not bear of the iniquity of the father, &#124;&#124; And a father does not bear of the iniquity of the son, &#124;&#124; The righteousness of the righteous is on him, &#124;&#124; And the wickedness of the wicked is on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:27–28; Titus 2:11–14; Ezekiel 33:19"> And the wicked—when he turns back &#124;&#124; From all his sins that he has done, &#124;&#124; And he has kept all My statutes, &#124;&#124; And has done judgment and righteousness, &#124;&#124; He surely lives, he does not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 7:19; Ezekiel 33:16; Isaiah 43:25"> All his transgressions that he has done &#124;&#124; Are not remembered to him, &#124;&#124; In his righteousness that he has done he lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:11; 1 Timothy 2:4; 2 Peter 3:9"> Do I take pleasure &#91;or&#93; delight in the death of the wicked?” A declaration of Lord YHWH, “Is it not in his turning back from his way—And he has lived?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:18; Proverbs 21:16; 2 John 1:8"> And in the turning back of the righteous from his righteousness, &#124;&#124; And he has done perversity, &#124;&#124; According to all the abominations &#124;&#124; That the wicked has done, &#124;&#124; Does he then live? All his righteous deeds that he has done are not remembered, &#124;&#124; For his trespass that he has trespassed, &#124;&#124; And for his sin that he has sinned, &#124;&#124; For them he dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:17; Malachi 3:13–15; Malachi 2:17"> And you have said, The way of the Lord is not pondered. Now hear, O house of Israel, &#124;&#124; My way—is it not pondered? Are not your ways unpondered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> In the turning back of the righteous from his righteousness, &#124;&#124; And he has done perversity, &#124;&#124; And he is dying by them, &#124;&#124; He dies for his perversity that he has done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:21; Isaiah 1:18; Isaiah 55:7"> And in the turning back of the wicked &#124;&#124; From his wickedness that he has done, &#124;&#124; And he does judgment and righteousness, &#124;&#124; He keeps his soul alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Luke 15:17–18; James 2:10–12"> And he sees and turns back, &#124;&#124; From all his transgressions that he has done, &#124;&#124; He surely lives, he does not die,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:25; Proverbs 19:3; Ezekiel 18:2"> And the house of Israel has said, The way of the Lord is not pondered, &#124;&#124; My ways—are they not pondered? O house of Israel—are not your ways unpondered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:21; Matthew 16:27; Matthew 3:2"> Therefore, I judge each according to his ways, &#124;&#124; O house of Israel,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; “Turn back, indeed, turn yourselves back, &#124;&#124; From all your transgressions, &#124;&#124; And iniquity is not for a stumbling-block to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 51:10; Ezekiel 36:26; Ezekiel 11:19"> Cast away all your transgressions from over you, &#124;&#124; By which you have transgressed, &#124;&#124; And make a new heart and a new spirit for yourselves, &#124;&#124; And why do you die, &#124;&#124; O house of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Ezekiel 18:23; Ezekiel 33:11"> For I have no pleasure in the death of the dying,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; “Therefore turn back and live!”</span>
