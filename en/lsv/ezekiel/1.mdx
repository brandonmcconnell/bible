<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 3:16; Ezekiel 11:24; Acts 7:56"> And it comes to pass, in the thirtieth year, in the fourth &#91;month&#93;, on the fifth of the month, and I &#91;am&#93; in the midst of the expulsion by the river Chebar, the heavens have been opened, and I see visions of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 24:12–15; Ezekiel 8:1; Ezekiel 20:1"> In the fifth of the month—it is the fifth year of the expulsion of King Jehoiachin—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Ezekiel 3:22; 2 Kings 3:15"> the word of YHWH has certainly been to Ezekiel son of Buzi the priest, in the land of the Chaldeans, by the river Chebar, and there is on him there a hand of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:27; Ezekiel 8:2; Jeremiah 23:19"> And I look, and behold, a turbulent wind is coming from the north, a great cloud, and fire catching itself, and brightness to it all around, and out of its midst as the color of electrum, out of the midst of the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 4:6–8; Ezekiel 1:26; Revelation 6:6"> And out of its midst &#91;is&#93; a likeness of four living creatures, and this &#91;is&#93; their appearance; a likeness of man &#91;is&#93; to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Revelation 4:7–8; Ezekiel 1:15"> and each had four faces and each of them had four wings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 1:15; Daniel 10:6; Psalms 104:4"> and their feet &#91;are&#93; straight feet, and the sole of their feet &#91;is&#93; as a sole of a calf’s foot, and they are sparkling as the color of bright bronze;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:21; Ezekiel 10:11; Isaiah 6:6"> and on their four sides &#91;each had&#93; hands of man under their wings; and &#91;each&#93; of the four had their faces and their wings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:22; Ezekiel 10:11; Ezekiel 1:11–12"> their wings &#91;are&#93; joining to one another, they do not turn around in their going, they each go straight forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Revelation 4:7; 1 Corinthians 9:9–10"> As for the likeness of their faces, &#91;each had&#93; the face of a man, and toward the right the four had the face of a lion, and on the left the four had the face of an ox, and the four had the face of an eagle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 6:2; Ezekiel 1:23; Ezekiel 10:19"> And their faces and their wings dividing from above, of each &#91;are&#93; two joining together, and two are covering their bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:9; Ezekiel 10:22; Hebrews 1:14"> And they each go straight forward, to where the Spirit is to go, they go, they do not turn around in their going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 4:5; Psalms 104:4; Matthew 28:3"> As for the likeness of the living creatures, their appearances &#91;are&#93; as coals of fire—burning as the appearance of lamps; it is going up and down between the living creatures, and brightness &#91;is&#93; to the fire, and lightning is going forth out of the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 24:27; Zechariah 4:10; Mark 13:27"> And the living creatures are running, and turning back, as the appearance of the flash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 7:9; Ezekiel 10:9; Ezekiel 1:19–21"> And I see the living creatures, and behold, one wheel &#91;is&#93; in the earth, near the living creatures, at its four faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 10:6; Ezekiel 10:9–11; Exodus 39:13"> The appearance of the wheels and their works &#91;is&#93; as the color of beryl, and the four of them had one likeness, and their appearances and their works &#91;are&#93; as it were the wheel in the midst of the wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:9; Ezekiel 1:12; Isaiah 55:11"> On their four sides, in their going they go, they do not turn around in their going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:12; Revelation 4:6; Revelation 4:8"> As for their rings, they are both high and fearful, and their rings, of the four of them, &#91;are&#93; full of eyes around them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:16; Ezekiel 10:19; Psalms 103:20"> And in the going of the living creatures, the wheels go beside them, and in the living creatures being lifted up from off the earth, the wheels are lifted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:12; Ezekiel 10:17; 1 Corinthians 14:32"> To where the Spirit is to go, they go, there the Spirit &#91;is&#93; to go, and the wheels are lifted up alongside them, for a living spirit &#91;is&#93; in the wheels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:17; Ezekiel 1:19–20; Romans 8:2"> In their going, they go; and in their standing, they stand; and in their being lifted up from off the earth, the wheels are lifted up alongside them; for a living spirit &#91;is&#93; in the wheels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:1; Ezekiel 1:26; Revelation 4:6"> And over the heads of the living creatures—a likeness of an expanse, as the color of the fearful ice, stretched out over their heads from above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 4:18; Ezekiel 1:11–12; Psalms 89:7"> And under the expanse their wings &#91;are&#93; straight, one toward &#91;its&#93; sister; two &#91;wings&#93; of each are covering them, and two &#91;wings&#93; of each are covering their bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2; Daniel 10:6; Revelation 1:15"> And I hear the noise of their wings, as the noise of many waters, as the noise of the Mighty One, in their going—the noise of tumult, as the noise of a camp, in their standing they let their wings fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:22"> And there is a voice from above the expanse, that &#91;is&#93; above their head: in their standing they let their wings fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:1; Exodus 24:10; Revelation 1:13"> And above the expanse that &#91;is&#93; over their head, as an appearance of a sapphire stone, &#91;is&#93; the likeness of a throne, and on the likeness of the throne a likeness, as the appearance of man on it from above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:4; Ezekiel 8:2; Hebrews 12:29"> And I see as the color of electrum, as the appearance of fire all around within it, from the appearance of His loins and upward; and from the appearance of His loins and downward, I have seen as the appearance of fire, and brightness &#91;is&#93; all around Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 10:1; Revelation 4:3; Genesis 17:3"> As the appearance of the bow that is in a cloud in a day of rain, so &#91;is&#93; the appearance of the brightness all around.</span>
