<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:1; Ezekiel 30:20; Ezekiel 24:1"> And it comes to pass, in the seventh year, in the fifth &#91;month&#93;, on the tenth of the month, men from &#91;the&#93; elderly of Israel have come in to seek YHWH, and they sit before me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 28:9; Matthew 15:8–9; Psalms 50:15–21"> “Son of man, speak to the elderly of Israel, and you have said to them, Thus said Lord YHWH: Are you coming in to seek Me? &#91;As&#93; I live—I am not sought by you—a declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:2; Ezekiel 23:36; Ezekiel 14:20"> Do you judge them? Do you judge, son of man? Cause them to know the abominations of their fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Ezekiel 20:15; Exodus 3:8"> and you have said to them, Thus said Lord YHWH: In the day of My fixing on Israel, &#124;&#124; I lift up My hand, &#124;&#124; To the seed of the house of Jacob, &#124;&#124; And am known to them in the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And I lift up My hand to them, &#124;&#124; Saying, I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Jeremiah 32:22; Ezekiel 20:15"> In that day I lifted up My hand to them, &#124;&#124; To bring them forth from the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; To a land that I spied out for them, &#124;&#124; Flowing with milk and honey, &#124;&#124; It &#91;is&#93; a beauty to all the lands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 18:3; Ezekiel 20:8; Leviticus 11:44"> And I say to them, Let each cast away the detestable things of his eyes, &#124;&#124; And do not be defiled with the idols of Egypt, &#124;&#124; I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:7; Isaiah 63:10; Ezekiel 20:21"> And they rebel against Me, &#124;&#124; And have not been willing to listen to Me, &#124;&#124; Each has not cast away the detestable things of their eyes, &#124;&#124; And have not forsaken the idols of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And I speak—to pour out My fury on them, &#124;&#124; To complete My anger against them, &#124;&#124; In the midst of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:7; Ezekiel 20:22; Exodus 32:12"> And I do &#91;it&#93; for My Name’s sake, &#124;&#124; Not to defile &#91;it&#93; before the eyes of the nations, &#124;&#124; In whose midst they &#91;are in&#93;, &#124;&#124; Before whose eyes I became known to them, &#124;&#124; To bring them out from the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 15:22; Exodus 13:17–18; Exodus 14:17–22"> And I bring them out of the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And I bring them into the wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Leviticus 18:5; Deuteronomy 4:8"> And I give My statutes to them, &#124;&#124; And I caused them to know My judgments, &#124;&#124; Which the man who does—lives by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:20; Ezekiel 37:28; Leviticus 21:23"> And I have also given My Sabbaths to them, &#124;&#124; To be for a sign between Me and them, &#124;&#124; To know that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH who is sanctifying them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:21; Ezekiel 20:8; Isaiah 56:6"> And the house of Israel rebels against Me in the wilderness, &#124;&#124; They have not walked in My statutes, &#124;&#124; And they have despised My judgments, &#124;&#124; Which the man who does—lives by them. And they have greatly defiled My Sabbaths, &#124;&#124; And I speak—to pour out My fury on them in the wilderness, to consume them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:9; Ezekiel 36:22–23; Ezekiel 20:22"> And I do &#91;it&#93; for My Name’s sake, &#124;&#124; Not to defile &#91;it&#93; before the eyes of the nations, &#124;&#124; Before whose eyes I brought them forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Psalms 106:26; Hebrews 3:18"> And also, I have lifted up My hand to them in the wilderness, &#124;&#124; Not to bring them into the land that I had given, &#124;&#124; Flowing with milk and honey, &#124;&#124; It &#91;is&#93; a beauty to all the lands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 15:39; Ezekiel 20:8; Ezekiel 14:3–4"> Because they kicked against My judgments, &#124;&#124; And they have not walked in My statutes, &#124;&#124; And they have defiled My Sabbaths, &#124;&#124; For their heart is going after their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:13; Jeremiah 4:27; Jeremiah 5:18"> And My eye has pity on them—against destroying them, &#124;&#124; And I have not made an end of them in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 1:2–4; Psalms 78:6–8; 1 Peter 1:18"> And I say to their sons in the wilderness: Do not walk in the statutes of your fathers, &#124;&#124; And do not observe their judgments, &#124;&#124; And do not defile yourselves with their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Deuteronomy 5:32–8; Ezekiel 37:24"> I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH, walk in My statutes, &#124;&#124; And observe My judgments, and do them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 58:13; Jeremiah 17:22; Exodus 31:13–17"> And sanctify My Sabbaths, &#124;&#124; And they have been for a sign between Me and you, &#124;&#124; To know that I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:13; Numbers 21:5; Ezekiel 20:8"> And the sons rebel against Me, &#124;&#124; They have not walked in My statutes, &#124;&#124; And they have not observed My judgments—to do them, &#124;&#124; Which the man who does—lives by them. They have defiled My Sabbaths, &#124;&#124; And I speak—to pour out My fury on them, &#124;&#124; To complete My anger against them in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:14; Ezekiel 20:9; Jeremiah 14:21"> And I have turned back My hand, &#124;&#124; And I do &#91;it&#93; for My Name’s sake, &#124;&#124; Not to defile &#91;it&#93; before the eyes of the nations, &#124;&#124; Before whose eyes I brought them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:64–68; Deuteronomy 32:26–27"> I also, I have lifted up My hand to them in the wilderness, &#124;&#124; To scatter them among nations, &#124;&#124; And to spread them through lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:16; Ezekiel 6:9; Amos 2:4"> Because they have not done My judgments, &#124;&#124; And they have despised My statutes, &#124;&#124; And they have defiled My Sabbaths, &#124;&#124; And their eyes have been after &#91;the&#93; idols of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 81:12; Isaiah 66:4; Ezekiel 20:39"> And I also, I have given statutes to them &#91;that are&#93; not good, &#124;&#124; And judgments by which they do not live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Leviticus 18:21; Ezekiel 20:31"> And I defile them by their own gifts, &#124;&#124; By causing every opener of a womb to pass away, &#124;&#124; So that I make them desolate, &#124;&#124; So that they know that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Ezekiel 2:7; Ezekiel 3:11"> Therefore, speak to the house of Israel, son of man, and you have said to them, Thus said Lord YHWH: Still in this your fathers have reviled Me, &#124;&#124; In their committing a trespass against Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:13; Nehemiah 9:22–26; Jeremiah 3:6"> And I bring them into the land, &#124;&#124; That I lifted up My hand to give to them, &#124;&#124; And they see every high hill, and every thick tree, &#124;&#124; And they sacrifice their sacrifices there, &#124;&#124; And give the provocation of their offering there, &#124;&#124; And make their refreshing fragrance there, &#124;&#124; And they pour out their drink-offerings there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:24–25; Ezekiel 16:31"> And I say to them: What &#91;is&#93; the high place to where you are going in?” And its name is called “High Place” to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; Jeremiah 7:26; Judges 2:19"> “Therefore, say to the house of Israel, Thus said Lord YHWH: Are you defiling yourselves in the way of your fathers? And do you go whoring after their detestable things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:26; Psalms 106:37–39; Jeremiah 7:31"> And in the offering of your gifts, &#124;&#124; In causing your sons to pass through fire, &#124;&#124; You are defiled by all your idols to this day, &#124;&#124; And I am sought by you, O house of Israel? &#91;As&#93; I live—a declaration of Lord YHWH, I am not sought by you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:5; Jeremiah 44:17; Psalms 139:2"> And that which is going up on your mind, &#124;&#124; It is not at all—in that you are saying: We will be as the nations, as the families of the lands, &#124;&#124; To serve wood and stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:5; Ezekiel 8:18; Jeremiah 44:6"> &#91;As&#93; I live—a declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; Do I not, with a strong hand, &#124;&#124; And with an outstretched arm, &#124;&#124; And with poured out fury—rule over you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:38; Amos 9:9–10; Ezekiel 34:16"> And I have brought you forth from the peoples, &#124;&#124; And assembled you from the lands &#124;&#124; In which you have been scattered, &#124;&#124; With a strong hand and with an outstretched arm, &#124;&#124; And with poured out fury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hosea 2:14; Jeremiah 2:35; Ezekiel 17:20"> And I have brought you into the wilderness of the peoples, &#124;&#124; And have been judged with you there face to face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5–10; Numbers 11:1–35; Ezekiel 20:13"> As I was judged with your fathers, &#124;&#124; In the wilderness of the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; So I am judged with you, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:13; Leviticus 27:32; Matthew 25:32–33"> And I have caused you to pass under the rod, &#124;&#124; And brought you into the bond of the covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:9; Amos 9:9–10; Psalms 95:11"> And cleared out the rebels from you, &#124;&#124; And those transgressing against Me, &#124;&#124; I bring them out from the land of their sojournings, &#124;&#124; And they do not come to the land of Israel, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Hosea 4:17; Isaiah 1:13–15; Ezekiel 43:7"> And you, O house of Israel, thus said Lord YHWH: Each go, serve his idols, &#124;&#124; And afterward, if you are not listening to Me &#91;anyway&#93;, &#124;&#124; But you do not defile My holy Name anymore by your gifts, and by your idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Malachi 3:4; Isaiah 60:7"> For on My holy mountain, &#124;&#124; On the mountain of the height of Israel, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; All the house of Israel serves Me there, &#124;&#124; All of it, in the land—I accept them there, &#124;&#124; And I seek your raised-offerings there, &#124;&#124; And with the first-fruit of your gifts, &#124;&#124; With all your holy things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:25; Ezekiel 11:17; Ezekiel 38:23"> With refreshing fragrance I accept you, &#124;&#124; In My bringing you out from the peoples, &#124;&#124; And I have assembled you from the lands &#124;&#124; In which you have been scattered, &#124;&#124; And I have been sanctified in you &#124;&#124; Before the eyes of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:13; Ezekiel 38:23; Ezekiel 36:23–24"> And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; In My bringing you to the ground of Israel, &#124;&#124; To the land that I lifted up My hand &#124;&#124; To give it to your fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Hosea 5:15; Jeremiah 31:18"> And you have remembered your ways there, &#124;&#124; And all your doings, &#124;&#124; In which you have been defiled, &#124;&#124; And you have been loathsome in your own faces, &#124;&#124; For all your evils that you have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:24; Psalms 115:1; Ezekiel 20:9"> And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; In My dealing with you for My Name’s sake, &#124;&#124; Not according to your evil ways, &#124;&#124; And according to your corrupt doings, O house of Israel, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Amos 7:16; Ezekiel 21:2; Jeremiah 13:19"> “Son of man, set your face the way of Teman, and prophesy to the south, and prophesy to the forest of the field—the south;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:14; Isaiah 24:1–6; Ezekiel 17:24"> and you have said to the forest of the south, Hear a word of YHWH! Thus said Lord YHWH: Behold, I am kindling a fire in you, &#124;&#124; And it has devoured every moist tree in you, and every dry tree, &#124;&#124; The glowing flames are not quenched, &#124;&#124; And all faces from south to north have been burned by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:2–3; Jeremiah 7:20; Deuteronomy 29:24–28"> And all flesh has seen, that I, YHWH, have kindled it—it is not quenched.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 16:25; Acts 17:18; Ezekiel 17:2"> And I say, “Aah! Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; They are saying of me, &#124;&#124; Is he not using allegories?”</span>
