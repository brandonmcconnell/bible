<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:17; Ezekiel 26:1; Ezekiel 20:1"> In the tenth year, in the tenth &#91;month&#93;, on the twelfth of the month, a word of YHWH has been to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Jeremiah 44:30; Jeremiah 46:2–16"> “Son of man, set your face against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and prophesy concerning him, and concerning Egypt—all of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:2; Isaiah 27:1; Jeremiah 44:30"> Speak, and you have said, Thus said Lord YHWH: Behold, I &#91;am&#93; against you, &#124;&#124; Pharaoh king of Egypt! The great dragon that is crouching in the midst of his floods, &#124;&#124; Who has said, My flood &#91;is&#93; my own, &#124;&#124; And I have made it &#91;for&#93; myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; Ezekiel 38:4; Amos 4:2"> And I have put hooks in your jaws, &#124;&#124; And I have caused the fish of your floods to cleave to your scales, &#124;&#124; And I have caused you to come up from the midst of your floods, &#124;&#124; And every fish of your floods cleaves to your scales.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:20; Ezekiel 32:4–6; Jeremiah 8:2"> And I have left you in the wilderness, &#124;&#124; You and every fish of your floods, &#124;&#124; You fall on the face of the field, &#124;&#124; You are not gathered nor assembled, &#124;&#124; I have given you for food to the beast of the earth and to the bird of the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 18:21; Isaiah 36:6; Exodus 9:14"> And all inhabitants of Egypt have known &#124;&#124; That I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; Because of their being a staff of reed to the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Ezekiel 17:15–17; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> In their taking hold of you by your hand, &#124;&#124; You crush and have torn every shoulder of theirs, &#124;&#124; And in their leaning on you you are broken, &#124;&#124; And have caused all their thighs to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:17; Ezekiel 30:10; Ezekiel 29:19–20"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: Behold, I am bringing in a sword against you, &#124;&#124; And have cut off man and beast from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Proverbs 16:18; Ezekiel 29:10–12"> And the land of Egypt has been for a desolation and a ruin, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; Because he said: The flood &#91;is&#93; mine, and I made &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:12; Ezekiel 30:6–9; Exodus 14:2"> Therefore, behold, I &#91;am&#93; against you, and against your floods, &#124;&#124; And have given the land of Egypt for ruins, &#124;&#124; A dry place, a desolation, from Migdol to Syene, &#124;&#124; And to the border of Cush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:13; Jeremiah 43:11–12; Ezekiel 30:10–13"> A foot of man does not pass over into it, &#124;&#124; Indeed, the foot of beast does not pass into it, &#124;&#124; Nor is it inhabited forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:19; Ezekiel 30:7; Ezekiel 30:23"> And I have made the land of Egypt a desolation, &#124;&#124; In the midst of desolate lands, &#124;&#124; And its cities, in the midst of cities &#91;that are&#93; laid waste, &#124;&#124; Are a desolation &#91;for&#93; forty years, &#124;&#124; And I have scattered the Egyptians among nations, &#124;&#124; And I have dispersed them through lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:26; Isaiah 19:22"> But thus said Lord YHWH: At the end of forty years I gather the Egyptians &#124;&#124; Out of the peoples to where they have been scattered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:14; Isaiah 11:11; Jeremiah 44:1"> And I have turned back &#91;to&#93; the captivity of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And I have brought them back &#124;&#124; &#91;To&#93; the land of Pathros, to the land of their birth, &#124;&#124; And they have been a low kingdom there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 10:11; Ezekiel 30:13; Ezekiel 17:14"> It is lowest of the kingdoms, &#124;&#124; And it does not lift itself up above the nations anymore, &#124;&#124; And I have made them few, &#124;&#124; So as not to rule among nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Isaiah 20:5; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> And it is no longer for confidence to the house of Israel, &#124;&#124; Bringing iniquity to remembrance, &#124;&#124; By their turning after them, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; Lord YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:1; Ezekiel 1:2; Ezekiel 24:1"> And it comes to pass, in the twenty-seventh year, in the first &#91;month&#93;, on the first of the month, a word of YHWH has been to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:6; Jeremiah 25:9; Ezekiel 26:7–12"> “Son of man, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, &#124;&#124; Has caused his force to serve a great service against Tyre, &#124;&#124; Every head &#91;is&#93; bald—every shoulder peeled, &#124;&#124; And neither he, nor his force, had reward out of Tyre, &#124;&#124; For the service that he served against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:24–25; Jeremiah 43:10–13; Ezekiel 32:11"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: Behold, I am giving the land of Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, &#124;&#124; And he has taken away its store, &#124;&#124; And has taken its spoil, and taken its prey, &#124;&#124; And it has been a reward to his force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Isaiah 10:6–7; Isaiah 45:1–3"> I have given to him his wage for which he labored—The land of Egypt, in that they worked for Me, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:10; Luke 21:15; Psalms 132:17"> In that day I cause a horn to shoot up for the house of Israel, &#124;&#124; And I give an opening to your mouth in their midst, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH!”</span>
