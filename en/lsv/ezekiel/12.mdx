<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 4:12; Jeremiah 5:21; Psalms 78:40"> “Son of man, you are dwelling in the midst of the house of rebellion, that have eyes to see, and they have not seen; they have ears to hear, and they have not heard; for they &#91;are&#93; a house of rebellion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:3; 2 Timothy 2:25; Jeremiah 26:3"> And you, son of man, make your vessels of removal, and remove by day before their eyes, and you have removed from your place to another place before their eyes, it may be they consider, for they &#91;are&#93; a house of rebellion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:4; Ezekiel 12:12; 2 Kings 25:4"> And you have brought forth your vessels as vessels of removal by day before their eyes, and you go forth in the evening before their eyes, as the goings forth of a removal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 25:4; Jeremiah 39:2–4"> You dig through the wall before their eyes, and you have brought forth by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:3; Isaiah 8:18; Ezekiel 24:24"> Carry on the shoulder before their eyes, bring forth in the darkness, cover your face, and you do not see the earth, for I have given you &#91;as&#93; a sign to the house of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:18; Ezekiel 37:10; Ezekiel 37:7"> And I do so, as I have been commanded; I have brought forth my vessels as vessels of removal by day, and I have dug through the wall with my hand in the evening; I have brought forth in the darkness, I have carried away on the shoulder, before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, in the morning, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19; Ezekiel 17:12; Ezekiel 20:49"> “Son of man, have they not said to you—the house of Israel—the house of rebellion—What are you doing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 9:25; Isaiah 13:1; Jeremiah 24:8"> Say to them, Thus said Lord YHWH: This burden &#91;concerns&#93; the prince in Jerusalem, and all the house of Israel who are in their midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:15; Jeremiah 15:2; Jeremiah 52:28–30"> Say: I &#91;am&#93; your sign; as I have done so it is done to them, into a removal, into a captivity, they go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 25:4; Jeremiah 39:4; Ezekiel 12:6"> As for the prince who &#91;is&#93; in their midst, he carries on the shoulder in the darkness, and he goes forth; they dig through the wall to bring forth by it; he covers his face that he may not look on the very surface of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:7; Hosea 7:12; Ezekiel 17:20"> And I have spread My net for him, and he has been caught in My snare, and I have brought him to Babylon, the land of the Chaldeans, and he does not see it—and he dies there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:2; 2 Kings 25:4–5; Ezekiel 17:21"> And all who are around him to help him, and all his bands, I scatter to every wind, and I draw out a sword after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Ezekiel 12:16; Ezekiel 12:20"> And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, in My scattering them among nations, and I have spread them through lands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; Ezekiel 14:22–23; Ezekiel 6:8–10"> and I have left of them, a few in number, from the sword, from the famine, and from the pestilence, so that they recount all their abominations among the nations to where they have come, and they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 5:9; Psalms 80:5; Job 3:24"> “Son of man, eat your bread in haste, and drink your water with trembling and with fear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 7:14; Ezekiel 6:6–7; Ezekiel 6:14"> and you have said to the people of the land, Thus said Lord YHWH concerning the inhabitants of Jerusalem, concerning the land of Israel: They eat their bread with fear, and drink their water with astonishment, because its land is desolate, because of its fullness, because of the violence of all who are dwelling in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Isaiah 7:23–24; Jeremiah 25:9"> And the cities that are inhabited are laid waste, and the land is a desolation, and you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:2–3; Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 12:27"> “Son of man, what &#91;is&#93; this allegory to you, concerning the land of Israel, saying, The days are prolonged, and every vision has perished?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joel 2:1; Ezekiel 7:5–7; Zephaniah 1:14"> Therefore say to them, Thus said Lord YHWH: I have caused this allegory to cease, &#124;&#124; And they do not use it as an allegory in Israel again, &#124;&#124; But speak to them: The days have drawn near, &#124;&#124; And every vision has spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:13–16; Ezekiel 13:23; Zechariah 13:2–4"> For there is no longer any vain vision, and flattering divination, &#124;&#124; In the midst of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; Ezekiel 12:28; Isaiah 55:11"> For I, YHWH, speak, &#124;&#124; The word that I speak—it is done, &#124;&#124; It is not prolonged anymore, &#124;&#124; For in your days, O house of rebellion, I speak a word, and I have done it, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Daniel 10:14; Ezekiel 12:22; 2 Peter 3:4"> “Son of man, behold, the house of Israel is saying, The vision that he is seeing &#91;is not&#93; for many days, and he is prophesying of far-off times,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:23–25; Mark 13:32–37; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–3"> therefore say to them, Thus said Lord YHWH: None of My words are prolonged anymore, &#124;&#124; When I speak a word—it is done, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH!”</span>
