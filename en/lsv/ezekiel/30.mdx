<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 13:6; Ezekiel 21:12; Joel 1:11"> “Son of man, prophesy, and you have said, Thus said Lord YHWH: Howl, Aah! For the day!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Obadiah 1:15; Joel 2:1–2; Joel 1:15"> For a day &#91;is&#93; near, a day &#91;is&#93; near to YHWH! It is a time of cloud &#91;over&#93; the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 29:8; Isaiah 16:7"> And a sword has come into Egypt, &#124;&#124; And there has been great pain in Cush, &#124;&#124; In the falling of the wounded in Egypt, &#124;&#124; And they have taken its store, &#124;&#124; And its foundations have been broken down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:20; Ezekiel 27:10; Jeremiah 25:24"> Cush, and Phut, and Lud, and all the mixture, and Chub, &#124;&#124; And the sons of the land of the covenant fall by sword with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:10; Isaiah 20:3–6; Isaiah 31:3"> Thus said YHWH: And those supporting Egypt have fallen, &#124;&#124; And the arrogance of her strength has come down, &#124;&#124; From Migdol to Syene, they fall by sword in her, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:12; Jeremiah 25:18–26; Ezekiel 32:18–32"> And they have been desolated in the midst of desolate lands, &#124;&#124; And its cities are in the midst of cities &#91;that are&#93; laid waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:6; Ezekiel 29:16; Amos 1:7"> And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; In My giving fire against Egypt, &#124;&#124; And all her helpers have been broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 18:1–2; Isaiah 23:5; Ezekiel 32:9–10"> In that day messengers go forth from before Me in ships, &#124;&#124; To trouble confident Cush, &#124;&#124; And there has been great pain among them, &#124;&#124; As the day of Egypt, for behold, it has come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 32:11–16; Ezekiel 29:4–5"> Thus said Lord YHWH: I have caused the multitude of Egypt to cease, &#124;&#124; By the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Ezekiel 32:12; Ezekiel 39:11–20"> He and his people with him—the terrible of nations, &#124;&#124; Are brought in to destroy the land, &#124;&#124; And they have drawn their swords against Egypt, &#124;&#124; And have filled the land &#91;with&#93; the wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Nahum 1:4; Revelation 16:12"> And I have made floods a dry place, &#124;&#124; And I have sold the land into the hand of evildoers, &#124;&#124; And I have made the land desolate, &#124;&#124; And its fullness, by the hand of strangers, &#124;&#124; I, YHWH, have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 10:11; Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 2:16"> Thus said Lord YHWH: And I have destroyed idols, &#124;&#124; And caused vain things to cease from Noph, &#124;&#124; And there is no longer a prince of the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And I give fear in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:14; Jeremiah 46:25; Psalms 78:43"> And I have made Pathros desolate, &#124;&#124; And I have given fire against Zoan, &#124;&#124; And I have done judgments in No,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 16:1; Nahum 1:6; Psalms 11:6"> And I have poured out My fury on Sin, the stronghold of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And I have cut off the multitude of No.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:8–9; Ezekiel 28:18"> And I have given fire against Egypt, &#124;&#124; Sin is greatly pained, and No is to be broken, &#124;&#124; And Noph has daily distresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 41:45"> The youths of Aven and Pi-Beseth fall by sword, &#124;&#124; And these go into captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:3; Ezekiel 29:15; Isaiah 10:27"> And the day has been dark in Tehaphnehes, &#124;&#124; In My breaking the yokes of Egypt there, &#124;&#124; And the excellence of her strength has ceased in her, &#124;&#124; Her! A cloud covers her, &#124;&#124; And her daughters go into captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:14; Revelation 17:1; Ezekiel 5:8"> And I have done judgments in Egypt, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:17; Ezekiel 26:1; Ezekiel 29:1"> And it comes to pass, in the eleventh year, in the first &#91;month&#93;, on the seventh of the month, a word of YHWH has been to me, saying, “Son of man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:13; Jeremiah 46:11; Psalms 37:17"> I have broken the arm of Pharaoh, king of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And behold, it has not been bound up to give healing, &#124;&#124; To put a bandage to bind it, &#124;&#124; To strengthen it—to lay hold on the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 37:17; Jeremiah 37:7; 2 Kings 24:7"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: Behold, I &#91;am&#93; against Pharaoh, king of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And I have broken his arms, &#124;&#124; The strong one and the broken one, &#124;&#124; And have caused the sword to fall out of his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:26; Ezekiel 30:17–18; Ezekiel 29:12–13"> And I have scattered the Egyptians among nations, &#124;&#124; And have spread them through lands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:25; Zephaniah 2:12; Isaiah 45:5"> And I have strengthened the arms of the king of Babylon, &#124;&#124; And have given My sword into his hand, &#124;&#124; And I have broken the arms of Pharaoh, &#124;&#124; And he has groaned the groans of a pierced one—before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:21; Ezekiel 32:15; Ezekiel 29:16"> And I have strengthened the arms of the king of Babylon, &#124;&#124; And the arms of Pharaoh fall down, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; In My giving My sword into the hand of the king of Babylon, &#124;&#124; And he has stretched it out toward the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 11:42; Ezekiel 29:12; Ezekiel 30:23"> And I have scattered the Egyptians among nations, &#124;&#124; And I have spread them through lands, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH!”</span>
