<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:1; Ezekiel 20:1; 2 Kings 6:32"> And men from &#91;the&#93; elderly of Israel come to me, and sit before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 3:7; 1 Kings 14:4"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:7; Ezekiel 7:19; Ezekiel 20:3"> “Son of man, these men have caused their idols to go up on their heart, and they have put the stumbling-block of their iniquity before their faces; am I inquired of at all by them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 66:4; Ezekiel 14:7; Isaiah 3:11"> Therefore, speak with them, and you have said to them, Thus said Lord YHWH: Everyone of the house of Israel who causes his idols to go up to his heart, and sets the stumbling-block of his iniquity before his face, and has gone to the prophet—I, YHWH, have given an answer to him for this, for the abundance of his idols,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; Zechariah 11:8; Romans 1:28"> in order to catch the house of Israel by their heart, in that they have become estranged from off Me by their idols—all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Isaiah 30:22; Ezekiel 36:31–32"> Therefore say to the house of Israel, Thus said Lord YHWH: Turn back, indeed, turn back from your idols, and turn back your faces from all your abominations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:48; Isaiah 58:1–2; Leviticus 24:22"> for everyone of the house of Israel, and of the sojourners who sojourn in Israel, who is separated from after Me, and causes his idols to go up to his heart, and sets the stumbling-block of his iniquity before his face, and has come to the prophet to inquire of him concerning Me, I, YHWH, have answered him for Myself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 15:7; Jeremiah 44:11; Ezekiel 5:15"> and I have set My face against that man, and made him for a sign, and for allegories, and I have cut him off from the midst of My people, and you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:25; Jeremiah 4:10; Psalms 81:11–12"> And the prophet, when he is enticed, and has spoken a word—I, YHWH, have enticed that prophet, and have stretched out My hand against him, and have destroyed him from the midst of My people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:49; Ezekiel 14:4; Jeremiah 8:11–12"> And they have borne their iniquity: as the iniquity of the inquirer, so is the iniquity of the prophet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:23; Ezekiel 44:10; Revelation 21:7"> so that the house of Israel does not wander from after Me anymore, nor are they defiled with all their transgressions anymore, and they have been to Me for a people, and I am to them for God—a declaration of Lord YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Ezekiel 4:16; Isaiah 24:20"> “Son of man, when the land sins against Me to commit a trespass, and I have stretched out My hand against it, and broken the staff of bread for it, and sent famine into it, and cut off man and beast from it—</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:20; Jeremiah 15:1; Ezekiel 28:3"> and &#91;despite&#93; these three men &#91;that&#93; have been in its midst: Noah, Daniel, and Job—they &#91;only&#93; deliver their own soul by their righteousness,” a declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:17; Leviticus 26:22; Jeremiah 15:3"> “If I cause an evil beast to pass through the land, and it has bereaved, and it has been a desolation, without any passing through because of the beast—</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 19:29; James 5:16; Ezekiel 33:11"> &#91;despite&#93; these three men in its midst—&#91;as&#93; I live,” a declaration of Lord YHWH, “they deliver neither sons nor daughters; they alone are delivered, and the land is a desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Ezekiel 21:3–4; Zephaniah 1:3"> Or &#91;if&#93; I bring a sword in against that land, and I have said: Sword, pass over through the land, and I have cut off man and beast from it—</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and &#91;despite&#93; these three men in its midst—&#91;as&#93; I live,” a declaration of Lord YHWH, “they do not deliver sons and daughters, for they alone are delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Ezekiel 5:12; Jeremiah 14:12"> Or &#91;if&#93; I send pestilence to that land, and I have poured out My fury against it in blood, to cut off man and beast from it—</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:14; 1 John 2:29; Isaiah 3:10"> and &#91;despite&#93; Noah, Daniel, and Job, in its midst—&#91;as&#93; I live,” a declaration of Lord YHWH, “they deliver neither son nor daughter; they, by their righteousness, &#91;only&#93; deliver their own soul.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:27; Ezekiel 5:17; Ezekiel 14:13"> For thus said Lord YHWH: “Although My four severe judgments—sword, and famine, and wild beast, and pestilence—I have sent to Jerusalem, to cut off man and beast from it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:16; Ezekiel 20:43; Hebrews 12:6–11"> yet, behold, there has been left an escape in it, who are brought forth, sons and daughters, behold, they are coming forth to you, and you have seen their way, and their doings, and have been comforted concerning the calamity that I have brought in against Jerusalem, all that which I have brought in against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; Revelation 15:4; Deuteronomy 8:2"> And they have comforted you, for you see their way and their doings, and you have known that I have not done all that which I have done in her for nothing,” a declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
