<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:17; Jeremiah 37:19; Ezekiel 22:28"> “Son of man, prophesy concerning the prophets of Israel who are prophesying, and you have said to those prophesying from their own heart, Hear a word of YHWH!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 2:14; Jeremiah 23:28–32; Hosea 9:7"> Thus said Lord YHWH: Woe to the prophets who are foolish, &#124;&#124; Who are going after their own spirit, &#124;&#124; And they have seen nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 19:20; Micah 2:11; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–10"> Your prophets have been &#124;&#124; As foxes in the wasteland, O Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Psalms 106:23; Isaiah 58:12"> You have not gone up into breaches, &#124;&#124; Nor do you make a wall for the house of Israel, &#124;&#124; To stand in battle in a day of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:8; Ezekiel 22:28; Jeremiah 28:15"> They have seen vanity, and lying divination, &#124;&#124; Who are saying: A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And YHWH has not sent them, &#124;&#124; And they have hoped to establish a word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6; Matthew 24:23–24; Ezekiel 13:2–3"> Have you not seen a vain vision, &#124;&#124; And spoken a lying divination, &#124;&#124; When you say, A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And I have not spoken?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:8; Ezekiel 21:3; Ezekiel 28:22"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: Because you have spoken vanity, and seen a lie, &#124;&#124; Therefore, behold, I &#91;am&#93; against you, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 87:6; Psalms 69:28; Ezekiel 20:38"> And My hand has been on the prophets, &#124;&#124; Who are seeing vanity, and who are divining a lie, &#124;&#124; They are not in the assembly of My people, &#124;&#124; And they are not written in the writing of the house of Israel, &#124;&#124; And they do not come to the ground of Israel, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:28; Ezekiel 13:16; Jeremiah 6:14"> Because, even because, they caused My people to err, &#124;&#124; Saying, Peace! And there is no peace, &#124;&#124; And that one is building a wall, &#124;&#124; And behold, they are coating it with chalk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Isaiah 28:2; Nahum 1:7–8"> Say to those coating with chalk—It falls, &#124;&#124; There has been an overflowing shower, &#124;&#124; And you, O hailstones, fall, &#124;&#124; And a turbulent wind breaks out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 3:13; Lamentations 2:14–15; Judges 9:38"> And behold, the wall has fallen! Does one not say to you, Where &#91;is&#93; the coating that you coated?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 30:30; Revelation 16:21; Psalms 148:8"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: I have broken with a turbulent wind in My fury, &#124;&#124; And an overflowing shower is in My anger, &#124;&#124; And hailstones in My fury—to consume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 1:6; Jeremiah 14:15; Jeremiah 6:15"> And I have broken down the wall that you coated with chalk, &#124;&#124; And have caused it to come to the earth, &#124;&#124; And its foundation has been revealed, &#124;&#124; And it has fallen, &#124;&#124; And you have been consumed in its midst, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 30:13; Psalms 62:3; Nehemiah 4:3"> And I have completed My wrath on the wall, &#124;&#124; And on those coating it with chalk, &#124;&#124; And I say to you: The wall is not, &#124;&#124; And those coating it are not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:10; Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 8:11"> &#91;These&#93;—the prophets of Israel, who are prophesying concerning Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And who are seeing a vision of peace for her, &#124;&#124; And there is no peace, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:2; 2 Kings 22:14; Judges 4:4"> And you, son of man, set your face against the daughters of your people, who are prophesying out of their own heart, and prophesy concerning them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 2:14; Ezekiel 13:20; 2 Timothy 4:3"> And you have said, Thus said Lord YHWH: Woe to those sowing &#91;magic&#93; bands for all joints of the arm, &#124;&#124; And to those making the veils &#124;&#124; For the head of every stature—to hunt souls, &#124;&#124; Do you hunt the souls of My people? And do your souls that live remain alive?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 3:5; Proverbs 28:21; Jeremiah 23:17"> Indeed, you pierce Me concerning My people, &#124;&#124; For handfuls of barley, &#124;&#124; And for pieces of bread, &#124;&#124; To put to death souls that should not die, &#124;&#124; And to keep alive souls that should not live, &#124;&#124; By your lying to My people—listening to lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:18; 2 Timothy 3:8–9; Ezekiel 13:8–9"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: Behold, I &#91;am&#93; against your &#91;magic&#93; bands, &#124;&#124; With which you are hunting there the souls of the flourishing, &#124;&#124; And I have torn them from off your arms, &#124;&#124; And have sent away the souls that you are hunting, &#124;&#124; The souls of the flourishing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 91:3"> And I have torn your veils, &#124;&#124; And delivered My people out of your hand, &#124;&#124; And they are no longer for a prey in your hand, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Jeremiah 14:13–17; 2 Peter 2:18–19"> Because of paining the heart of the righteous with falsehood, &#124;&#124; And I have not pained it, &#124;&#124; And strengthening the hands of the wicked, &#124;&#124; So as not to turn &#91;him&#93; back from his evil way, &#124;&#124; To keep him alive,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:21; Ezekiel 12:24; Micah 3:6"> Therefore, you do not see vanity, &#124;&#124; And you do not divine divination again, &#124;&#124; And I have delivered My people out of your hand, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH!”</span>
