<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:49; Matthew 13:35; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> “Son of man, put forth a riddle, and use an allegory to the house of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Jeremiah 48:40; Ezekiel 17:7"> and you have said, Thus said Lord YHWH: The great eagle, great-winged, long-pinioned, &#124;&#124; Full of feathers, that has diverse colors, &#124;&#124; Has come to Lebanon, &#124;&#124; And he takes the foliage of the cedar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 43:14; Isaiah 47:15; Revelation 18:11–19"> He has cropped the top of its tender twigs, &#124;&#124; And he brings it to the land of Canaan. He has placed it in a city of merchants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 44:4; Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Jeremiah 37:1"> And he takes of the seed of the land, &#124;&#124; And puts it in a field of seed, &#124;&#124; He took &#91;it&#93; by many waters, &#124;&#124; He has set it in a conspicuous place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:14; Proverbs 16:18–19"> And it springs up, and becomes a spreading vine, humble of stature, &#124;&#124; To turn its thin shoots toward itself, &#124;&#124; And its roots are under it, &#124;&#124; And it becomes a vine, and makes boughs, &#124;&#124; And sends forth beautiful branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:4; Ezekiel 17:15; 2 Kings 24:20"> And there is another great eagle, &#124;&#124; Great-winged, and abounding with feathers, &#124;&#124; And behold, this vine has bent its roots toward him, &#124;&#124; And it has sent out its thin shoots toward him, &#124;&#124; To water it from the furrows of its planting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:5–6"> On a good field, by many waters, it is planted, to make branches, and to bear fruit, to be for a good vine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Jeremiah 32:5; Isaiah 30:1–7"> Say, Thus said Lord YHWH: It prospers—does he not draw out its roots, &#124;&#124; And cut off its fruit, and it is withered? &#91;In&#93; all the leaves of its springing it withers, &#124;&#124; And not by great strength, or by numerous people, &#124;&#124; To lift it up by its roots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 13:15; John 15:6; Matthew 21:19"> And behold, the planted thing—does it prosper? When the east wind comes against it, does it not utterly wither? On the furrows of its springing it withers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19; Ezekiel 17:3; Ezekiel 1:2"> “Now say to the house of rebellion, &#124;&#124; Have you not known what these &#91;are&#93;? Say, Behold, the king of Babylon has come to Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And he takes its king, and its princes, &#124;&#124; And brings them to himself to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:13; 2 Kings 24:15–17; Ezekiel 17:5"> And he takes of the seed of the kingdom, &#124;&#124; And makes a covenant with him, &#124;&#124; And brings him into an oath, &#124;&#124; And he has taken the mighty of the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:14; Deuteronomy 28:43; 1 Samuel 2:30"> That the kingdom may be humble, &#124;&#124; That it may not lift itself up, &#124;&#124; To keep his covenant—that it may stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:3; Jeremiah 38:18; Ezekiel 17:7"> And he rebels against him, &#124;&#124; To send his messengers to Egypt, &#124;&#124; To give to him horses, and many people, &#124;&#124; Does he prosper? Does he who is doing these things escape? And has he broken covenant and escaped?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 52:11; Ezekiel 17:18–19"> &#91;As&#93; I live—a declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; Does he not—in the place of the king who is causing him to reign, &#124;&#124; Whose oath he has despised, &#124;&#124; And whose covenant he has broken—die with him in the midst of Babylon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:7; Ezekiel 4:2; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> And not with a great force, and with a numerous assembly, &#124;&#124; Does Pharaoh maintain him in battle, &#124;&#124; By pouring out a mound, and in building a fortification, &#124;&#124; To cut off many souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:24; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Lamentations 5:6"> And he despised the oath—to break covenant, &#124;&#124; And behold, he has given his hand, &#124;&#124; And all these he has done—he does not escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:11; Jeremiah 5:9; Jeremiah 5:2"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: &#91;As&#93; I live—My oath that he has despised, &#124;&#124; And My covenant that he has broken, &#124;&#124; Have I not put it on his head?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Ezekiel 32:3; Jeremiah 2:35"> And I have spread out My snare for him, &#124;&#124; And he has been caught in My net, &#124;&#124; And I have brought him to Babylon, &#124;&#124; And judged him there &#91;for the&#93; trespass &#124;&#124; That he has trespassed against Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 25:5; 2 Kings 25:11; Ezekiel 12:14"> And all his fugitives, with all his bands, &#124;&#124; Fall by sword, and those remaining, &#124;&#124; They are spread out to every wind, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I, YHWH, have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2; Zechariah 6:12–13; Isaiah 11:1–5"> Thus said Lord YHWH: I have taken of the foliage of the high cedar, &#124;&#124; And I have set &#91;it&#93;, &#124;&#124; I crop a tender one from the top of its tender shoots, &#124;&#124; And I have planted &#91;it&#93; on a high and lofty mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:32; Ezekiel 31:6; Isaiah 2:2"> In a mountain—the high place of Israel, I plant it, &#124;&#124; And it has borne boughs, and yielded fruit, &#124;&#124; And become a good cedar, &#124;&#124; And every bird of every &#91;kind of&#93; wing has dwelt under it, &#124;&#124; They dwell in the shade of its thin shoots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 9:11; 1 Corinthians 1:27–28; Ezekiel 24:14"> And all trees of the field have known &#124;&#124; That I, YHWH, have made the high tree low, &#124;&#124; I have set the low tree on high, &#124;&#124; I have dried up the moist tree, &#124;&#124; And I have caused the dry tree to flourish, &#124;&#124; I, YHWH, have spoken, and have done &#91;it&#93;!”</span>
