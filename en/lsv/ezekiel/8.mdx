<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:1; Ezekiel 1:2–3; Ezekiel 14:1"> And it comes to pass, in the sixth year, in the sixth &#91;month&#93;, on the fifth of the month, I am sitting in my house, and &#91;the&#93; elderly of Judah are sitting before me, and there a hand of Lord YHWH falls on me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:4; Ezekiel 1:26–27; Daniel 7:9–10"> and I look, and behold, a likeness as the appearance of fire, from the appearance of His loins and downward—fire, and from His loins and upward, as the appearance of brightness, as the color of electrum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16; Ezekiel 11:1; Ezekiel 5:11"> And He puts forth a form of a hand, and takes me by a lock of my head, and &#91;the&#93; Spirit lifts me up between the earth and the heavens, and brings me to Jerusalem in visions of God, to the opening of the inner gate that is facing the north, where the seat of the figure of jealousy &#91;is&#93; that is making jealous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:22–23; Exodus 40:34–35; Ezekiel 11:22–23"> and behold, there the glory of the God of Israel &#91;is&#93; as the appearance that I saw in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:3; Jeremiah 3:2; Jeremiah 32:34"> And He says to me, “Son of man, now lift up your eyes &#91;toward&#93; the way of the north.” And I lift up my eyes &#91;toward&#93; the way of the north, and behold, on the north of the gate of the altar &#91;is&#93; this figure of jealousy, at the entrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 10:19; Ezekiel 8:9"> And He says to me, “Son of man, are you seeing what they are doing? The great abominations that the house of Israel is doing here, to keep far off from My sanctuary; and turn again, &#91;and&#93; see great abominations.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 21:5; 1 Kings 7:12"> And He brings me to an opening of the court, and I look, and behold, a hole in the wall;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 29:15; Job 34:22; Amos 9:2–3"> and He says to me, “Son of man, now dig through the wall”; and I dig through the wall, and behold, an opening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:8"> And He says to me, “Go in, and see the evil abominations that they are doing here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 1:23; Exodus 20:4; Jeremiah 16:18"> And I go in, and look, and behold, every form of creeping thing, and detestable beast—and all the idols of the house of Israel—carved on the wall, all around,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 16:17; Numbers 16:35; Numbers 11:16"> and seventy men from &#91;the&#93; elderly of the house of Israel—and Jaazaniah son of Shaphan standing in their midst—are standing before them, and each &#91;with&#93; his censer in his hand, and &#91;the&#93; abundance of &#91;the&#93; cloud of the incense is going up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 29:15; Ezekiel 9:9; Ezekiel 8:15"> And He says to me, “Have you seen, son of man, that which elderly of the house of Israel are doing in the darkness, each in &#91;the&#93; inner chambers of his imagery, for they are saying, YHWH is not seeing us, YHWH has forsaken the land?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:6; Jeremiah 9:3; Ezekiel 8:15"> And He says to me, “Turn again, &#91;and&#93; see &#91;the&#93; great abominations that they are doing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:9; Ezekiel 44:4"> And He brings me to the opening of the gate of the house of YHWH that &#91;is&#93; at the north, and behold, there the women are sitting, weeping for Tammuz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12–13; Ezekiel 8:9; Ezekiel 8:6"> And He says to me, “Have you seen, son of man? Turn again, &#91;and&#93; see greater abominations than these.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:19; Deuteronomy 17:3; Jeremiah 32:33"> And He brings me into the inner court of the house of YHWH, and behold, at the opening of the temple of YHWH, between the porch and the altar, about twenty-five men, their backs toward the temple of YHWH, and their faces eastward, and they are bowing themselves eastward to the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:9; Ezekiel 7:23; Jeremiah 6:7"> And He says to me, “Have you seen, son of man? Has it been a light thing to the house of Judah to do the abomination that they have done here, that they have filled the land with violence, and turn back to provoke Me to anger? And behold, they are putting forth the branch to their nose!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:4; Jeremiah 11:11; Isaiah 1:15"> And I also deal in fury, My eye does not pity, nor do I spare, and they have cried &#91;with&#93; a loud voice in My ears and I do not hear them.”</span>
