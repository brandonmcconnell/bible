<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:1; Jeremiah 3:15; Isaiah 40:11"> “Son of man, prophesy concerning shepherds of Israel, prophesy, and you have said to them, Thus said Lord YHWH to the shepherds: Woe &#91;to&#93; the shepherds of Israel, &#124;&#124; Who have been feeding themselves! Do the shepherds not feed the flock?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 11:16; Jeremiah 2:30; Ezekiel 33:25–26"> You eat the fat, and you put on the wool, &#124;&#124; You slaughter the fed one, &#124;&#124; You do not feed the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 18:12–13; Matthew 9:36; Ezekiel 34:16"> You have not strengthened the weak, &#124;&#124; And you have not healed the sick one, &#124;&#124; And you have not bound up the broken, &#124;&#124; And have not brought back the driven away, &#124;&#124; And you have not sought the lost, &#124;&#124; And you have ruled them with might and with rigor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; 2 Chronicles 18:16; Jeremiah 23:2"> And they are scattered from want of a shepherd, &#124;&#124; And are for food to every beast of the field, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they are scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 142:4; 1 Peter 2:25; John 10:16"> My flock goes astray on all the mountains, &#124;&#124; And on every high hill, &#124;&#124; And My flock has been scattered on &#91;the&#93; whole face of the earth, &#124;&#124; And there is none inquiring, and none seeking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:2–3; Micah 3:8–9; Psalms 82:1–7"> Therefore, shepherds, hear a word of YHWH:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Peter 2:13; Acts 20:33; Ezekiel 34:5–6"> &#91;As&#93; I live—a declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; If not, because of My flock being for a prey, &#124;&#124; Indeed, My flock is for food to every beast of the field, &#124;&#124; Because there is no shepherd, &#124;&#124; And My shepherds have not sought My flock, &#124;&#124; And the shepherds feed themselves, &#124;&#124; And they have not fed My flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Therefore, O shepherds, hear a word of YHWH!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Zechariah 10:3; Ezekiel 13:8"> Thus said Lord YHWH: Behold, I &#91;am&#93; against the shepherds, &#124;&#124; And have required My flock from their hand, &#124;&#124; And caused them to cease from feeding the flock, &#124;&#124; And the shepherds feed themselves no more, &#124;&#124; And I have delivered My flock from their mouth, &#124;&#124; And they are not for food to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:16; Luke 19:10; Jeremiah 23:3"> For thus said Lord YHWH: Behold, I—even I, have required My flock, &#124;&#124; And I have sought it out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; John 10:11–12; Luke 19:10"> As a shepherd’s searching of his drove, &#124;&#124; In the day of his being in the midst of his scattered flock, so I seek My flock, &#124;&#124; And have delivered them out of all places, &#124;&#124; To where they have been scattered, &#124;&#124; In a day of cloud and thick darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:25–26; Ezekiel 11:17; Isaiah 11:11–16"> And I have brought them out from the peoples, &#124;&#124; And have gathered them from the lands, &#124;&#124; And brought them to their own ground, &#124;&#124; And have fed them on mountains of Israel, &#124;&#124; By streams, and by all dwellings of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 10:9; Jeremiah 31:25; Psalms 31:8–10"> I feed them with good pasture, &#124;&#124; And their habitation is on mountains of the high place of Israel, &#124;&#124; There they lie down in a good habitation, &#124;&#124; And they enjoy fat pastures on mountains of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; Psalms 23:1–2; John 21:15"> I feed My flock, and cause them to lie down, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Ezekiel 34:4; Ezekiel 34:11"> I seek the lost, and bring back the driven away, &#124;&#124; And I bind up the broken, and I strengthen the sick, &#124;&#124; And I destroy the fat and the strong, &#124;&#124; I feed it with judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:20–22; Matthew 25:32–33; Zechariah 10:3"> And you, My flock, thus said Lord YHWH: Behold, I am judging between sheep and sheep, &#124;&#124; Between rams and male goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Luke 11:52; Numbers 16:13"> Is it a little thing for you—&#91;that&#93; you enjoy the good pasture, &#124;&#124; And tread down the remnant of your pasture with your feet, &#124;&#124; And drink a depth of waters, &#124;&#124; And trample the remainder with your feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And My flock consumes the trodden thing of your feet, &#124;&#124; And drinks the trampled thing of your feet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:31–46; Ezekiel 34:10; Psalms 22:12–16"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH to them: Behold, I—even I, have judged between fat sheep and lean sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 11:5; Deuteronomy 33:17; Luke 13:14–16"> Because you thrust away with side and with shoulder, &#124;&#124; And push all the diseased with your horns, &#124;&#124; Until you have scattered them to the out-place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:2–3; Psalms 72:12–14; Ezekiel 34:10"> Therefore I have given safety to My flock, &#124;&#124; And they are not for prey anymore, &#124;&#124; And I have judged between sheep and sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Jeremiah 30:9; Isaiah 40:11"> And have raised up one shepherd over them, &#124;&#124; And he has fed them—My servant David, &#124;&#124; He feeds them, and he is their shepherd,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 36:28; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> And I, YHWH, I am their God, &#124;&#124; And My servant David &#91;is&#93; prince in their midst, &#124;&#124; I, YHWH, have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 11:6–9; Ezekiel 37:26; Leviticus 26:6"> And I have made a covenant of peace with them, &#124;&#124; And caused evil beasts to cease out of the land, &#124;&#124; And they have dwelt confidently in a wilderness, &#124;&#124; And they have slept in forests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:12; Isaiah 44:3; Leviticus 26:4"> And I have given them, and the outskirts of My hill, a blessing, &#124;&#124; And caused the shower to come down in its season, &#124;&#124; They are showers of blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:8; Leviticus 26:4; Leviticus 26:13"> And the tree of the field has given its fruit, &#124;&#124; And the land gives her increase, &#124;&#124; And they have been confident on their land, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; In My breaking the bands of their yoke, &#124;&#124; And I have delivered them from the hand of those laying service on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:27; Jeremiah 30:10; Ezekiel 39:26"> And they are no longer a prey to nations, &#124;&#124; And the beast of the earth does not devour them, &#124;&#124; And they have dwelt confidently, &#124;&#124; And there is none troubling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:29; Isaiah 4:2; Ezekiel 34:26–27"> And I have raised a plantation of renown for them, &#124;&#124; And they are no longer consumed by hunger in the land, &#124;&#124; And they no longer bear the shame of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27; Ezekiel 34:24; Psalms 46:11"> And they have known that I, YHWH, their God, &#91;am&#93; with them, &#124;&#124; And they—the house of Israel—My people, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; John 10:16; Psalms 78:52"> And you, My flock, the flock of My pasture, &#124;&#124; You &#91;are&#93; men &#91;and&#93; I &#91;am&#93; your God, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH!”</span>
