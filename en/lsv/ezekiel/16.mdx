<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:2; Ezekiel 20:4; Isaiah 58:1"> “Son of man, cause Jerusalem to know her abominations, and you have said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:45; Isaiah 51:1–2; Deuteronomy 20:17"> Thus said Lord YHWH to Jerusalem: Your birth and your nativity &#124;&#124; &#91;Are&#93; of the land of the Canaanite, &#124;&#124; Your father the Amorite, and your mother a Hittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Genesis 15:13; Exodus 5:16–21"> As for your nativity, in the day you were born, &#124;&#124; Your navel has not been cut, &#124;&#124; And you were not washed in water for ease, &#124;&#124; And you have not been salted at all, &#124;&#124; And you have not been swaddled at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:10; Genesis 21:10; Exodus 1:22"> No eye has had pity on you, to do any of these to you, &#124;&#124; To have compassion on you, &#124;&#124; And you are cast on the face of the field, &#124;&#124; With loathing of your soul &#124;&#124; In the day you were born!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–5; John 5:25; Titus 3:3–7"> And I pass over by you, &#124;&#124; And I see you trodden down in your blood, &#124;&#124; And I say to you in your blood, Live! Indeed, I say to you in your blood, Live!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:10; Exodus 1:7; Isaiah 62:3"> I have made you a myriad as the shoot of the field, &#124;&#124; And you are multiplied, and are great, &#124;&#124; And come in with an excellent adornment, &#124;&#124; Breasts have been formed, and your hair has grown—And you, naked and bare!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 3:9; Jeremiah 2:2–3; Romans 5:8"> And I pass over by you, and I see you, &#124;&#124; And behold, your time &#91;is&#93; a time of loves, &#124;&#124; And I spread My skirt over you, &#124;&#124; And I cover your nakedness, &#124;&#124; And I swear to you, and come into a covenant with you, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; And you become Mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Ruth 3:3; 1 John 2:27"> And I wash you with water, &#124;&#124; And I wash away your blood from off you, &#124;&#124; And I anoint you with oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:18; Ezekiel 16:13; Isaiah 61:10"> And I clothe you with embroidery, &#124;&#124; And I shoe you with tachash &#91;skin&#93;, &#124;&#124; And I gird you with fine linen, &#124;&#124; And I cover you with figured silk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 24:22; Isaiah 3:19; Genesis 41:42"> And I adorn you with adornments, &#124;&#124; And I give bracelets for your hands, &#124;&#124; And a chain for your neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 3:21; Isaiah 28:5; Jeremiah 13:18"> And I give a ring for your nose, &#124;&#124; And rings for your ears, &#124;&#124; And a crown of beauty on your head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–14; Psalms 50:2; 1 Kings 4:21"> And you put on gold and silver, &#124;&#124; And your clothing &#91;is&#93; fine linen, &#124;&#124; And figured silk and embroidery, &#124;&#124; You have eaten fine flour, and honey, and oil, &#124;&#124; And you are very, very beautiful, &#124;&#124; And go prosperously to the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 10:24; Lamentations 2:15; 1 Corinthians 4:7"> And your name goes forth among nations, &#124;&#124; Because of your beauty—for it &#91;is&#93; complete, &#124;&#124; In My honor that I have set on you, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 57:8; Jeremiah 2:20; Ezekiel 16:25"> And you trust in your beauty, &#124;&#124; And go whoring because of your renown, &#124;&#124; And pour out your whoredoms on everyone passing by—to him &#91;that would have&#93; it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 23:7; Ezekiel 7:20; 2 Chronicles 28:24"> And you take from your garments, &#124;&#124; And make spotted high-places for yourself, &#124;&#124; And go whoring on them, &#124;&#124; They are not coming in—nor will it be!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:14–21; Jeremiah 3:9; Ezekiel 16:11"> And you take your beautiful vessels &#124;&#124; Of My gold and My silver that I gave to you, &#124;&#124; And make images of a male for yourself, &#124;&#124; And go whoring with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And you take the garments of your embroidery, &#124;&#124; And cover them, &#124;&#124; And you have set My oil and My incense before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:13; Hosea 2:8–13; Genesis 8:21"> And My bread, that I gave to you, &#124;&#124; Fine flour, and oil, and honey, that I caused you to eat. You have even set it before them, &#124;&#124; For a refreshing fragrance—thus it is, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:37; Ezekiel 20:31; Jeremiah 7:31"> And you take your sons and your daughters whom you have born to Me, &#124;&#124; And sacrifice them to them for food. Is it a little thing because of your whoredoms,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 17:17; Leviticus 18:21; Deuteronomy 18:10"> that you slaughter My sons, &#124;&#124; And give them up in causing them to pass over to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:2; Hosea 11:1; Ezekiel 16:43"> And with all your abominations and your whoredoms, &#124;&#124; You have not remembered the days of your youth, &#124;&#124; When you were naked and bare, &#124;&#124; You were trodden down in your blood!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 12:12; Ezekiel 2:10; Ezekiel 24:6"> And it comes to pass, after all your wickedness &#124;&#124; (Woe, woe, to you—a declaration of Lord YHWH),</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 78:58; Ezekiel 16:39; Ezekiel 16:31"> That you build an arch for yourself, &#124;&#124; And make a high place for yourself in every broad place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:31; Jeremiah 3:2; Genesis 38:21"> At every head of the way you have built your high place, &#124;&#124; And you make your beauty abominable, &#124;&#124; And open wide your feet to everyone passing by, &#124;&#124; And multiply your whoredoms,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:19–21; Ezekiel 20:7–8; Ezekiel 8:10"> And go whoring to sons of Egypt, &#124;&#124; Your neighbors—great of appetite! And you multiply your whoredoms, &#124;&#124; To provoke Me to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 9:12; Ezekiel 16:57; Ezekiel 16:37"> And behold, I have stretched out My hand against you, &#124;&#124; And I diminish your portion, &#124;&#124; And give you to the desire of those hating you, &#124;&#124; The daughters of the Philistines, &#124;&#124; Who are ashamed of your wicked way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 16:7; Hosea 10:6; 2 Kings 16:10–18"> And you go whoring to sons of Asshur, &#124;&#124; Without your being satisfied, &#124;&#124; And you go whoring with them, &#124;&#124; And also—you have not been satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 21:9; Judges 2:12–19; Ezekiel 13:14–23"> And you multiply your whoredoms &#124;&#124; On the land of Canaan—toward Chaldea, &#124;&#124; And even with this you have not been satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:3; Isaiah 1:3; Isaiah 3:9"> How weak &#91;is&#93; your heart, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; In your doing all these, &#124;&#124; The work of a domineering, whorish woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:33–34; Isaiah 52:3; Ezekiel 16:39"> In your building your arch at the head of every way, &#124;&#124; You have made your high place in every broad place, &#124;&#124; And have not been as a whore deriding a wage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Ezekiel 23:37; Jeremiah 2:25"> The wife who commits adultery—Under her husband—receives strangers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 57:9; Hosea 8:9–10; Ezekiel 16:41"> They give a gift to all whores, &#124;&#124; And you have given your gifts to all your lovers, &#124;&#124; And bribe them to come in to you, &#124;&#124; From all around—in your whoredoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And you are opposite from &#91;other&#93; women in your whoredoms, &#124;&#124; That none go whoring after you; And in your giving a wage, &#124;&#124; And a wage has not been given to you; Therefore you are the opposite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 4:18; 1 Kings 22:19; John 4:10"> Therefore, O whore, hear a word of YHWH!</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:10; Ezekiel 24:13; Zephaniah 3:1"> Thus said Lord YHWH: Because of your bronze being poured forth, &#124;&#124; And your nakedness is revealed in your whoredoms near your lovers, &#124;&#124; And near all the idols of your abominations, &#124;&#124; And according to the blood of your sons, &#124;&#124; Whom you have given to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Hosea 2:10; Jeremiah 13:22; Hosea 2:3"> Therefore, behold, I am assembling all your lovers, &#124;&#124; To whom you have been sweet, &#124;&#124; And all whom you have loved, &#124;&#124; Besides all whom you have hated; And I have assembled them by you all around, &#124;&#124; And have revealed your nakedness to them, &#124;&#124; And they have seen all your nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Genesis 9:6; Zephaniah 1:17"> And I have judged you—judgments of adultresses, &#124;&#124; And of women shedding blood, &#124;&#124; And have given you blood, fury, and jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:26; Hosea 2:3; Ezekiel 16:24–25"> And I have given you into their hand, &#124;&#124; And they have thrown down your arch, &#124;&#124; And they have broken down your high places, &#124;&#124; And they have stripped you of your garments, &#124;&#124; And they have taken your beautiful vessels, &#124;&#124; And they have left you naked and bare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:47; Habakkuk 1:6–10; John 8:5–7"> And have caused an assembly to come up against you, &#124;&#124; And stoned you with stones, &#124;&#124; And thrust you through with their swords,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:48; Jeremiah 52:13; Jeremiah 39:8"> And burned your houses with fire, &#124;&#124; And done judgments in you before the eyes of many women, &#124;&#124; And I have caused you to cease from going whoring, &#124;&#124; And also, you no longer give a wage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Ezekiel 21:17; Isaiah 40:1–2"> And I have caused My fury against you to rest, &#124;&#124; And My jealousy has turned aside from you, &#124;&#124; And I have been quiet, and I am not angry anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:22; Ezekiel 11:21; Psalms 78:42"> Because you have not remembered the days of your youth, &#124;&#124; And give trouble to Me in all these, &#124;&#124; Therefore I also—behold—I put your way on &#91;your own&#93; head, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; And you will not have done—have done this wickedness above all your abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:2–3; Psalms 106:35–38; 1 Samuel 24:13"> Behold, everyone using the allegory against you, &#124;&#124; Uses &#91;this&#93; allegory concerning &#91;you&#93;, saying, &#124;&#124; As the mother—her daughter!</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Zechariah 11:8; Isaiah 1:4; Ezekiel 23:37–39"> You &#91;are&#93; your mother’s daughter, &#124;&#124; Loathing her husband and her sons, &#124;&#124; And you &#91;are&#93; your sisters’ sister, &#124;&#124; Who loathed their husbands and their sons, &#124;&#124; Your mother &#91;is&#93; a Hittite, and your father an Amorite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:4; Ezekiel 16:48–49; Ezekiel 16:53–56"> And your older sister &#91;is&#93; Samaria, she and her daughters, &#124;&#124; Who is dwelling at your left hand, &#124;&#124; And your younger sister, who is dwelling on your right hand, &#91;is&#93; Sodom and her daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="2 Kings 21:9; Ezekiel 16:51; Ezekiel 5:6–7"> And you have not walked in their ways, &#124;&#124; And done according to their abominations, &#124;&#124; It has been loathed as a little thing, &#124;&#124; Indeed, you do more corruptly than they in all your ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 10:15; Luke 10:12; Acts 7:52"> &#91;As&#93; I live—a declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; Your sister Sodom has not done—she and her daughters—As you have done—you and your daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 138:6; Ezekiel 28:2; Genesis 13:10"> Behold, this has been the iniquity of your sister Sodom, &#124;&#124; Arrogancy, fullness of bread, and quiet ease, &#124;&#124; Have been to her and to her daughters, &#124;&#124; And the hand of the afflicted and needy &#124;&#124; She has not strengthened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 19:24; Genesis 18:20; Genesis 19:5"> And they are haughty and do abomination before Me, &#124;&#124; And I turn them aside when I have seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:8–11; Luke 12:47–48; Romans 3:9–20"> And Samaria has not sinned even half of your sins, &#124;&#124; But you multiply your abominations more than they, &#124;&#124; And justify your sisters by all your abominations that you have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 7:1–5; Ezekiel 36:15; Ezekiel 16:56"> You also—bear your shame, &#124;&#124; That you have adjudged for your sisters, &#124;&#124; Because of your sins that you have done more abominably than they, &#124;&#124; They are more righteous than you, &#124;&#124; And you, also, be ashamed and bear your shame, &#124;&#124; In your justifying your sisters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:14; Jeremiah 12:16; Ezekiel 39:25"> And I have turned back &#91;to&#93; their captivity, &#124;&#124; The captivity of Sodom and her daughters, &#124;&#124; And the captivity of Samaria and her daughters, &#124;&#124; And the captivity of your captives in their midst,</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:22–23; Jeremiah 2:26; Ezekiel 36:31–32"> So that you bear your shame, &#124;&#124; And have been ashamed of all that you have done, &#124;&#124; In your comforting them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:11; Malachi 3:4; Ezekiel 16:53"> And your sisters, Sodom and her daughters, &#124;&#124; Return to their former state, &#124;&#124; And Samaria and her daughters return to their former state, &#124;&#124; And you and your daughters return to your former state.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Isaiah 65:5; Luke 15:28–30; Zephaniah 3:11"> And your sister Sodom has not been for a report in your mouth, &#124;&#124; In the day of your arrogancy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="2 Kings 16:5–7; Ezekiel 16:36–37; 2 Chronicles 28:5–6"> Before your wickedness is revealed, &#124;&#124; As &#91;at&#93; the time of the reproach of the daughters of Aram, &#124;&#124; And of all her neighbors, the daughters of the Philistines, &#124;&#124; Who are despising you all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:49; Lamentations 5:7; Genesis 4:13"> Your wicked plans and your abominations, &#124;&#124; You have borne them, a declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Isaiah 24:5; Ezekiel 17:19; Deuteronomy 29:25"> For thus said Lord YHWH: I have dealt with you as you have done, &#124;&#124; In that you have despised an oath—to break covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Hosea 2:19–20; Hebrews 12:24; Ezekiel 16:8"> And I have remembered My covenant with you, &#124;&#124; In the days of your youth, &#124;&#124; And I have established a perpetual covenant for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Hebrews 8:13; Galatians 4:26–31; Isaiah 66:7–12"> And you have remembered your ways, &#124;&#124; And you have been ashamed, &#124;&#124; In your receiving your sisters—Your older with your younger, &#124;&#124; And I have given them to you for daughters, &#124;&#124; And not by your covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Ezekiel 6:7; Ezekiel 39:22"> And I have established My covenant with you, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Psalms 39:9; Romans 3:19; Ezekiel 36:31–32"> So that you remember, &#124;&#124; And you have been ashamed, &#124;&#124; And there is not an opening of the mouth to you anymore because of your shame, &#124;&#124; In My receiving atonement for you, &#124;&#124; For all that you have done, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH!”</span>
