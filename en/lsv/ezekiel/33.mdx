<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:30; Ezekiel 33:17; Ezekiel 3:11"> “Son of man, speak to the sons of your people, and you have said to them: When I bring a sword on a land, &#124;&#124; And the people of the land have taken one man out of their borders, &#124;&#124; And made him their watchman,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Nehemiah 4:20; Nehemiah 4:18"> And he has seen the sword coming against the land, &#124;&#124; And has blown with a horn, and has warned the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:13; Acts 18:6; Jeremiah 6:17"> And the hearer has heard the voice of the horn, and he has not taken warning, &#124;&#124; And the sword comes in, and takes him away, &#124;&#124; His blood is on his &#91;own&#93; head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 9:19–21; Hebrews 11:7; John 8:39"> The voice of the horn he heard, &#124;&#124; And he has not taken warning, his blood is on him, &#124;&#124; And he who took warning has delivered his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:18–20; Ezekiel 33:8–9; Genesis 42:22"> And the watchman, when he sees the sword coming in, &#124;&#124; And he has not blown with a horn, &#124;&#124; And the people have not been warned, &#124;&#124; And a sword comes in, &#124;&#124; And takes away a soul from them, &#124;&#124; He is taken away in his iniquity, &#124;&#124; And I require his blood from the hand of the watchman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:17–21; Jeremiah 26:2; Ezekiel 2:7–8"> And you, son of man, &#124;&#124; I gave you &#91;as&#93; a watchman for the house of Israel, &#124;&#124; And you have heard a word from My mouth, &#124;&#124; And you have warned them from Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 20:26–27; Ezekiel 33:6; Ezekiel 33:14"> In My saying to the wicked, O wicked one—you surely die, &#124;&#124; And you have not spoken to warn the wicked from his way, &#124;&#124; He—the wicked—dies in his iniquity, &#124;&#124; And I require his blood from your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:19; Acts 20:26; Proverbs 15:10"> And you, when you have warned the wicked of his way, to turn back from it, &#124;&#124; And he has not turned back from his way, &#124;&#124; He dies in his iniquity, &#124;&#124; And you have delivered your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 49:14; Ezekiel 37:11; Ezekiel 24:23"> And you, son of man, say to the house of Israel: You have spoken correctly, saying, &#124;&#124; Surely our transgressions and our sins &#91;are&#93; on us, &#124;&#124; And we are wasting away in them, &#124;&#124; How, then, do we live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; Ezekiel 18:23; 2 Peter 3:9"> Say to them, &#91;As&#93; I live—a declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; I do not delight in the death of the wicked, &#124;&#124; But in the turning of the wicked from his way, and he has lived. Turn back, turn back, from your evil ways! Indeed, why do you die, O house of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; Ezekiel 33:18–19; Romans 3:25"> And you, son of man, say to the sons of your people: The righteousness of the righteous does not deliver him in the day of his transgression, &#124;&#124; And the wickedness of the wicked, &#124;&#124; He does not stumble for it in the day of his turning from his wickedness, &#124;&#124; And the righteous is not able to live in it in the day of his sinning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:24; Hebrews 10:38; Ezekiel 18:4"> In My saying of the righteous: He surely lives, &#124;&#124; And—he has trusted on his righteousness, &#124;&#124; And he has done perversity, &#124;&#124; All his righteous acts are not remembered, &#124;&#124; And for his perversity that he has done, &#124;&#124; He dies for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:27; Jeremiah 18:7–8; Hosea 14:1"> And in My saying to the wicked: You surely die, &#124;&#124; And—he has turned back from his sin, &#124;&#124; And has done judgment and righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:11; Luke 19:8; Exodus 22:1–4"> (The wicked restores a pledge, he repays &#91;for&#93; plunder), &#124;&#124; He has walked in the statutes of life, &#124;&#124; So as not to do perversity, &#124;&#124; He surely lives—he does not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Ezekiel 18:22; Micah 7:18–19"> All his sin—his sins that he has sinned—are not remembered against him, &#124;&#124; He has done judgment and righteousness, &#124;&#124; He surely lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 25:24–26; Ezekiel 18:25; Ezekiel 18:29"> And the sons of your people have said: The way of the Lord is not pondered, &#124;&#124; As for them—their way is not pondered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:12–13; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Hebrews 10:38"> In the turning back of the righteous from his righteousness, &#124;&#124; And he has done perversity—he dies for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:27–28; Ezekiel 33:14"> And in the turning back of the wicked from his wickedness, &#124;&#124; And he has done judgment and righteousness—he lives by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Ezekiel 18:25; Ezekiel 33:17"> And you have said, The way of the Lord is not pondered; I judge each of you according to his ways, O house of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:2; Jeremiah 39:1–8; 2 Kings 25:4"> And it comes to pass, in the twelfth year—in the tenth &#91;month&#93;, in the fifth of the month—of our removal, one who is escaped from Jerusalem comes to me, saying, “The city has been struck.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:3; Ezekiel 3:26–27; Ezekiel 24:26–27"> And the hand of YHWH has been to me in the evening, before the coming in of the escaped one, and He opens my mouth until &#91;his&#93; coming to me in the morning, and my mouth is opened, and I have not been silent again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:27; Isaiah 51:2; Acts 7:5"> “Son of man, the inhabitants of these ruins on the ground of Israel are speaking, saying, Abraham has been alone—and he possesses the land, and we &#91;are&#93; many—the land has been given to us for a possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:16; Jeremiah 7:9–10; Genesis 9:4"> Therefore say to them, Thus said Lord YHWH: You eat with the blood, &#124;&#124; And you lift up your eyes to your idols, &#124;&#124; And you shed blood, &#124;&#124; Should you then inherit the land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 27:40; Micah 2:1–2; Zephaniah 3:3"> You have stood on your sword, &#124;&#124; You have done abomination, &#124;&#124; You have each defiled the wife of his neighbor, &#124;&#124; Should you then possess the land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:6; Jeremiah 42:22; Ezekiel 39:4"> Thus you say to them, Thus said Lord YHWH: &#91;As&#93; I live—do they who &#91;are&#93; in the ruins not fall by the sword? And they who &#91;are&#93; on the face of the field, &#124;&#124; I have given for food to the beast, &#124;&#124; And they who are in strongholds and in caves die by pestilence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:24; Jeremiah 44:2; Jeremiah 44:6"> And I have made the land a desolation and an astonishment, &#124;&#124; And the excellence of its strength has ceased, &#124;&#124; And mountains of Israel have been desolated, &#124;&#124; Without anyone passing through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Ezekiel 36:17–18; Ezekiel 22:2–15"> And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; In My making the land a desolation and an astonishment, &#124;&#124; For all their abominations that they have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 22:16–17; Jeremiah 23:35; Isaiah 29:13"> And you, son of man, &#124;&#124; The sons of your people who are speaking about you, &#124;&#124; By the walls, and in openings of the houses, &#124;&#124; Have spoken with one another, each with his brother, &#124;&#124; Saying, Please come in, &#124;&#124; And hear what the word &#91;is&#93; that comes out from YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 13:22; Psalms 78:36–37; James 1:22–24"> And they come in to you as the coming in of a people, &#124;&#124; And they sit before you—My people, &#124;&#124; And have heard your words, and they do not do them, &#124;&#124; For they are making unhealthy loves with their mouth, &#124;&#124; Their heart is going after their dishonest gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 6:20; Mark 4:16–17; John 5:35"> And behold, you &#91;are&#93; as a singer of unhealthy loves to them, &#124;&#124; A beautiful voice, and playing well on an instrument, &#124;&#124; And they have heard your words, and they are not doing them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:9; 1 Samuel 3:19–20; Ezekiel 2:5"> And in its coming in—behold, it has come, &#124;&#124; And they have known that a prophet has been in their midst!”</span>
