<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46; Ezekiel 4:7; Ezekiel 25:2"> “Son of man, set your face toward Jerusalem, and prophesy to the holy places, and prophesy to the ground of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 9:22; Jeremiah 21:13; Ezekiel 5:8"> and you have said to the ground of Israel, Thus said YHWH: Behold, I &#91;am&#93; against you, &#124;&#124; And have brought out My sword from its scabbard, &#124;&#124; And have cut off righteous and wicked from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:47; Ezekiel 7:2; Ezekiel 6:11–14"> Because that I have cut off righteous and wicked from you, &#124;&#124; Therefore My sword goes out from its scabbard, &#124;&#124; To all flesh, from south to north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:30; Nahum 1:9; 1 Samuel 3:12"> And all flesh has known that I, YHWH, &#124;&#124; Have brought out My sword from its scabbard, &#124;&#124; It does not turn back anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 22:4; Ezekiel 6:11; Jeremiah 19:10"> And you, son of man, sigh with breaking of loins, indeed, sigh before their eyes with bitterness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:17; Ezekiel 7:26; Isaiah 13:7"> and it has come to pass, when they say to you, Why are you sighing? That you have said: Because of the report, for it is coming, &#124;&#124; And every heart has melted, &#124;&#124; And all hands have been feeble, &#124;&#124; And every spirit is weak, &#124;&#124; And all knees go &#91;as&#93; waters, &#124;&#124; Behold, it is coming, indeed, it has been, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 34:5–6; Ezekiel 21:28; Ezekiel 21:3"> “Son of man, prophesy, and you have said, Thus said YHWH: Say, A sword, a sword is sharpened, and also polished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 110:5–6; Ezekiel 20:47; Ezekiel 21:25–27"> It is sharpened so as to slaughter a slaughter. It is polished so as to have brightness, &#124;&#124; Or do we rejoice? It is despising the scepter of My son &#91;as&#93; every tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:19; Jeremiah 25:33; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> And He gives it for polishing, &#124;&#124; For laying hold of by the hand. It is sharpened—the sword—and polished, &#124;&#124; To give it into the hand of a slayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:6; Joel 1:13; Ezekiel 21:14"> Cry and howl, son of man, &#124;&#124; For it has been among My people, &#124;&#124; It &#91;is&#93; among all the princes of Israel, &#124;&#124; My people have been cast to the sword. Therefore strike on your thigh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:10; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Ezekiel 21:25"> Because &#91;it is&#93; a trier, &#124;&#124; And what if it is even despising the scepter? It will not be, a declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 24:1; Numbers 24:10; Leviticus 26:24"> And you, son of man, prophesy, &#124;&#124; And strike hand on hand, &#124;&#124; And the sword is bent a third time, &#124;&#124; The sword of the wounded! It &#91;is&#93; the sword of the wounded—the great one, &#124;&#124; That is entering the inner chamber to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:10; Jeremiah 17:27; Ezekiel 21:22"> To melt the heart, and to multiply the ruins, &#124;&#124; I have set the point of a sword by all their gates. Aah! It is made for brightness, &#124;&#124; Wrapped up for slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; Ezekiel 21:4; Ezekiel 14:17"> Take possession of the right, place yourself at the left, &#124;&#124; To where your face is appointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Ezekiel 21:14; Zechariah 6:8"> And I also, I strike My hand on My hand, &#124;&#124; And have caused My fury to rest; I, YHWH, have spoken.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:1–3; Jeremiah 1:10; Ezekiel 5:1–17"> “And you, son of man, appoint two ways for yourself, for the coming in of the sword of the king of Babylon; they come forth from one land, both of them. And create a station; create &#91;it&#93; at the top of the way of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Amos 1:14; Jeremiah 49:2; Ezekiel 25:5"> Appoint a way for the coming of the sword, &#124;&#124; To Rabbath of the sons of Ammon, &#124;&#124; And to Judah, in fortified Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 18:20; Proverbs 16:33; Judges 17:5"> For the king of Babylon has stood at the head of the way, &#124;&#124; At the top of the two ways, to use divination, &#124;&#124; He has moved lightly with the arrows, &#124;&#124; He has inquired of the teraphim, &#124;&#124; He has looked on the liver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:2; Ezekiel 26:9; Jeremiah 51:14"> The divination &#91;for&#93; Jerusalem has been at his right, &#124;&#124; To place battering-rams, &#124;&#124; To open the mouth with slaughter, &#124;&#124; To lift up a voice with shouting, &#124;&#124; To place battering-rams against the gates, &#124;&#124; To pour out a mound, to build a fortification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 5:15; Ezekiel 21:24; Ezekiel 29:16"> And it has been to them as a false divination in their eyes, &#124;&#124; Who have sworn oaths to them, &#124;&#124; But he is causing iniquity to be remembered &#91;so they&#93; are caught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 4:2; Micah 3:10–12; Jeremiah 3:2"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: Because of your causing your iniquity to be remembered, &#124;&#124; In your transgressions being revealed, &#124;&#124; For your sins being seen, in all your doings, &#124;&#124; Because of your being remembered, &#124;&#124; You are caught by the hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:5; Ezekiel 21:29; Ezekiel 17:19"> And you, wounded, wicked one, &#124;&#124; Prince of Israel, whose day has come, &#124;&#124; In the time of the iniquity of the end,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; Psalms 75:7; Jeremiah 13:18"> Thus said Lord YHWH: Turn aside the turban, and carry away the crown, &#124;&#124; This—not this—make high the low, &#124;&#124; And make low the high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Zechariah 9:9"> An overturn, overturn, overturn, I make it, &#124;&#124; Also this has not been until the coming of Him, &#124;&#124; Whose &#91;is&#93; the judgment, and I have given it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; Ezekiel 21:9–10; Zephaniah 2:8–10"> And you, son of man, prophesy, and you have said, Thus said Lord YHWH concerning the sons of Ammon, and concerning their reproach, and you have said: A sword, a sword, open for slaughter, &#124;&#124; Polished to the utmost for brightness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:25; Ezekiel 22:28; Psalms 37:13"> In seeing a vain thing for you, &#124;&#124; In divining a lie for you, &#124;&#124; To put you on the necks of the wounded of the wicked, whose day has come, &#124;&#124; In the time of the iniquity of the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:38; Genesis 15:14; Jeremiah 47:6–7"> Turn &#91;it&#93; back to its scabbard; I judge you &#124;&#124; In the place where you were produced, &#124;&#124; In the land of your birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Jeremiah 6:22–23; Ezekiel 14:19"> And I have poured My indignation on you, &#124;&#124; I blow against you with &#91;the&#93; fire of My wrath, &#124;&#124; And have given you into the hand of brutish men—craftsmen of destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:10; Malachi 4:1; Ezekiel 20:47–48"> You are fuel for the fire, &#124;&#124; Your blood is in the midst of the land, &#124;&#124; You are not remembered, &#124;&#124; For I, YHWH, have spoken!”</span>
