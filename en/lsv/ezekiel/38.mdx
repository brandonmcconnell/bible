<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:1; Ezekiel 27:13; Ezekiel 38:3"> “Son of man, set your face toward Gog, of the land of Magog, chief prince of &#91;&#91;or prince of Rosh,&#93;&#93; Meshech and Tubal, and prophesy concerning him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:8; Ezekiel 39:1–10; Ezekiel 29:3"> and you have said, Thus says Lord YHWH: Behold, I &#91;am&#93; against you, O Gog, &#124;&#124; Chief prince of &#91;&#91;or prince of Rosh,&#93;&#93; Meshech and Tubal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:4; Daniel 11:40; Ezekiel 38:15"> And I have turned you back, &#124;&#124; And I have put hooks in your jaws, &#124;&#124; And have brought you out, and all your force, &#124;&#124; Horses and horsemen, &#124;&#124; All of them clothed in perfection, &#124;&#124; A numerous assembly, &#91;with&#93; buckler and shield, &#124;&#124; All of them handling swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:10; Genesis 10:6; Ezekiel 30:5"> Persia, Cush, and Phut, with them, &#124;&#124; All of them &#91;with&#93; shield and helmet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:14; Genesis 10:2–3; 1 Chronicles 1:5–6"> Gomer and all its bands, &#124;&#124; The house of Togarmah &#91;from&#93; the sides of the north, &#124;&#124; And all its bands, many peoples with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:8; Joel 3:9–12; Isaiah 37:22"> Be prepared, indeed, prepare yourself, &#124;&#124; You and all your assemblies who are assembled to you, &#124;&#124; And you have been for a watch for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Isaiah 24:22; Ezekiel 38:11–12"> After many days you are appointed, &#124;&#124; In the latter end of the years you come into a land brought back from sword—Gathered out of many peoples, &#124;&#124; On mountains of Israel, &#124;&#124; That have been for a continuous ruin, &#124;&#124; And it has been brought out from the peoples, &#124;&#124; And all of them have dwelt safely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 28:2; Jeremiah 4:13; Joel 2:2"> And you have gone up—you come in as desolation, &#124;&#124; You are as a cloud to cover the land, &#124;&#124; You and all your bands, and many peoples with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 36:4; Micah 2:1; Psalms 83:3–4"> Thus said Lord YHWH: And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; Things come up on your heart, &#124;&#124; And you have thought an evil thought,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 2:4–5; Proverbs 3:29–30; Jeremiah 49:31–32"> And you have said: I go up over a land of open places, &#124;&#124; I go to those at rest, dwelling confidently, &#124;&#124; All of them are dwelling without walls, &#124;&#124; And they have no bar and doors—</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 10:6; Ezekiel 38:8; Ezekiel 29:19"> To take a spoil, and to take a prey, &#124;&#124; To turn back your hand on inhabited ruins, &#124;&#124; And on a people gathered out of nations, &#124;&#124; Making livestock and substance, &#124;&#124; Dwelling on a high part of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:15; Ezekiel 27:22–23; Ezekiel 32:2"> Sheba, and Dedan, and merchants of Tarshish, &#124;&#124; And all its young lions say to you, &#124;&#124; Are you coming to take spoil? Have you assembled your assembly to take prey? To carry away silver and gold? To take away livestock and substance? To take a great spoil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:8; Zechariah 2:5; Jeremiah 23:6"> Therefore, prophesy, son of man, and you have said to Gog, Thus said Lord YHWH: In that day, in the dwelling of My people Israel safely, &#124;&#124; Do you not know?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:2; Ezekiel 38:6; Revelation 20:8"> And you have come in out of your place, &#124;&#124; From the sides of the north, &#124;&#124; You and many peoples with you, &#124;&#124; Riding on horses—all of them, &#124;&#124; A great assembly, and a numerous force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:21; Ezekiel 38:23; Hosea 3:5"> And you have come up against My people Israel, &#124;&#124; As a cloud to cover the land, &#124;&#124; It is in the latter end of the days, &#124;&#124; And I have brought you in against My land, &#124;&#124; In order that the nations may know Me, &#124;&#124; In My being sanctified in you before their eyes, O Gog.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 11:40–45; Isaiah 63:1–6; Joel 3:9–14"> Thus said Lord YHWH: Are you he of whom I spoke in former days, &#124;&#124; By the hand of My servants, prophets of Israel, &#124;&#124; Who were prophesying &#91;for&#93; years in those days, &#124;&#124; To bring you in against them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:5–6; Hebrews 12:29; Psalms 89:46"> And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; In the day of the coming in of Gog against the land of Israel, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; My fury comes up in My face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Haggai 2:6–7; Revelation 11:13; Revelation 16:20"> And in My zeal, in the fire of My wrath, &#124;&#124; I have spoken: Is there not a great rushing on the land of Israel in that day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Nahum 1:4–6; Revelation 6:12–13"> And rushed from My presence have fishes of the sea, &#124;&#124; And the bird of the heavens, &#124;&#124; And the beast of the field, &#124;&#124; And every creeping thing that is creeping on the ground, &#124;&#124; And all men who &#91;are&#93; on the face of the ground, &#124;&#124; And the mountains have been thrown down, &#124;&#124; And the ascents have fallen, &#124;&#124; And every wall falls to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Haggai 2:22; Judges 7:22; Ezekiel 14:17"> And I have called for a sword against him throughout all My mountains, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; The sword of each is against his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 66:16; Psalms 11:6; Revelation 16:21"> And I have judged him with pestilence and with blood, &#124;&#124; And an overflowing rain and hailstones, &#124;&#124; I rain fire and brimstone on him, and on his bands, &#124;&#124; And on many peoples who &#91;are&#93; with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 9:16; Revelation 19:1–6; Ezekiel 38:16"> And I have magnified Myself, and sanctified Myself, &#124;&#124; And I have been known before the eyes of many nations, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH!”</span>
