<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 66:23; Ezekiel 44:1–2; Exodus 20:9"> Thus said Lord YHWH: “The gate of the inner court that is looking eastward is shut &#91;during&#93; the six days of work, and on the day of rest it is opened, and in the day of the new moon it is opened;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:3; Ezekiel 46:8; 1 Chronicles 29:10–12"> and the prince has come in the way of the porch of the gate from the outside, and he has stood by the post of the gate, and the priests have made his burnt-offering, and his peace-offerings, and he has bowed himself by the opening of the gate, and has gone forth, and the gate is not shut until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 1:10; John 10:9; Hebrews 10:19–22"> And the people of the land have bowed themselves at the opening of that gate, on Sabbaths, and on new moons, before YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:17; Numbers 28:9–10"> And the burnt-offering that the prince brings near to YHWH on the day of rest &#91;is&#93; six lambs, perfect ones, and a ram, a perfect one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:24; Ezekiel 46:7; Deuteronomy 16:17"> And the present &#91;is&#93; an ephah for a ram, and for the lambs a present, the gift of his hand, and a hin of oil for an ephah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:1"> And on the day of the new moon—a bullock, a son of the herd, a perfect one, and six lambs and a ram; they are perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:5"> And with an ephah for a bullock, and an ephah for a ram, he prepares a present, and for the lambs as his hand attains, and a hin of oil for an ephah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:2; Ezekiel 44:1–3; Colossians 1:18"> And in the coming in of the prince, he comes in the way of the porch of the gate, and by its way he goes out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 23:14–17; 2 Peter 2:20–21"> And in the coming in of the people of the land before YHWH at appointed times, he who has come in the way of the north gate to bow himself, goes out the way of the south gate, and he who has come in the way of the south gate, goes out by the way of the north gate: he does not turn back the way of the gate by which he came in, but he goes out opposite it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; 1 Chronicles 29:20; 1 Chronicles 29:22"> And in their coming in the prince in their midst comes in, and in their going out he goes out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:5; Ezekiel 46:7; Numbers 15:1–41"> And in celebrations, and in appointed times, the present is an ephah for a bullock, and an ephah for a ram, and for lambs the gift of his hand, and a hin of oil for an ephah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:1–2; Ezekiel 45:17; Ezekiel 44:3"> And when the prince makes a free-will burnt-offering, or free-will peace-offerings, to YHWH, then he has opened the gate that is looking eastward for himself, and has made his burnt-offering and his peace-offerings as he does in the day of rest, and he has gone out, and has shut the gate after his going out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 50:4; John 1:29; Exodus 12:5"> And you make a daily burnt-offering of a lamb, son of a year, a perfect one, to YHWH; you make it morning by morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 28:5"> And you make a present with it morning by morning, a sixth part of the ephah, and a third part of the hin of oil, to temper with the fine flour, a present to YHWH, by a continuous statute—continually;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 10:1–10; Numbers 28:6; Exodus 29:42"> and they prepare the lamb, and the present, and the oil, morning by morning—a continual burnt-offering.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:3; Galatians 4:7; Genesis 25:5–6"> Thus said Lord YHWH: “When the prince gives a gift to any of his sons, it &#91;is&#93; his inheritance, it &#91;is&#93; for his sons; it &#91;is&#93; their possession by inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Luke 19:25–26; Galatians 4:30–31"> And when he gives a gift out of his inheritance to one of his servants, then it has been his until the year of freedom, and it has turned back to the prince, only the inheritance of his sons is theirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:8; 1 Kings 21:19; Ezekiel 22:27"> And the prince does not take from the people’s inheritance to oppress them—from their possession; he causes his sons to inherit out of his own possession, so that My people are not each scattered from his possession.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:9; Ezekiel 44:4–5; Ezekiel 40:44–46"> And he brings me in through the entrance that &#91;is&#93; by the side of the gate, to the holy chambers, to the priests, that are looking northward, and behold, there &#91;is&#93; a place in their two sides westward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:13; Ezekiel 44:29; Ezekiel 44:19"> And he says to me, “This &#91;is&#93; the place where the priests boil the guilt-offering and the sin-offering, where they bake the present, so as not to bring &#91;it&#93; out to the outer court, to sanctify the people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And he brings me out to the outer court, and causes me to pass over to the four corners of the court, and behold, a court in a corner of the court, &#91;moreover&#93; a court in &#91;every&#93; corner of the corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> In the four corners of the court &#91;are&#93; enclosed courts, forty long and thirty broad, &#91;with&#93; one measure for the four corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And a row &#91;is&#93; all around in them—around the four of them—and made with boilers under the rows all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:20; John 21:15–17; 1 Peter 5:2"> And he says to me, “These &#91;are&#93; the houses of those boiling where the servants of the house boil the sacrifice of the people.”</span>
