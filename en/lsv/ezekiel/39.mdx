<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:2–3; Ezekiel 35:3; Nahum 3:5"> “And you, son of man, prophesy concerning Gog, and you have said, Thus said Lord YHWH: Behold, I &#91;am&#93; against you, O Gog, &#124;&#124; Chief prince of &#91;&#91;or prince of Rosh,&#93;&#93; Meshech and Tubal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 40:14; Ezekiel 38:15; Isaiah 37:29"> And have turned you back, and enticed you, &#124;&#124; And caused you to come up from the sides of the north, &#124;&#124; And brought you in against mountains of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 76:3; Hosea 1:5; Jeremiah 21:4–5"> And have struck your bow out of your left hand, &#124;&#124; Indeed, I cause your arrows to fall out of your right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:17–20; Ezekiel 33:27; Ezekiel 38:21"> You fall on mountains of Israel, &#124;&#124; You, and all your bands, and the peoples who &#91;are&#93; with you, &#124;&#124; To ravenous bird—a bird of every wing, &#124;&#124; And &#91;to&#93; a beast of the field, I have given you for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:19; Ezekiel 32:4; Jeremiah 8:2"> You fall on the face of the field, for I have spoken, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:16; Amos 1:4; Ezekiel 30:8"> And I have sent a fire against Magog, &#124;&#124; And against the confident inhabitants of the islands, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 38:23; Exodus 20:7"> And I make My holy Name known in the midst of My people Israel, &#124;&#124; And I do not defile My holy Name anymore, &#124;&#124; And the nations have known that I, YHWH, the Holy One, &#91;am&#93; in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:17; Revelation 16:17; Ezekiel 7:2–10"> Behold, it has come, and it has been done, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; It &#91;is&#93; the day of which I spoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 46:9; Isaiah 66:24; Malachi 1:5"> And those dwelling &#91;in the&#93; cities of Israel have gone out, &#124;&#124; And they have burned and kindled &#91;a fire&#93;, &#124;&#124; With armor, and shield, and buckler, &#124;&#124; With bow, and with arrows, &#124;&#124; And with hand weapon, and with javelin, &#124;&#124; And they have caused a fire to burn seven years with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Isaiah 14:2; Micah 5:8"> And they do not take wood out of the field, &#124;&#124; Nor do they hew out of the forests, &#124;&#124; For they cause the fire to burn with armor, &#124;&#124; And they have spoiled their spoilers, &#124;&#124; And they have plundered their plunderers, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:18; Numbers 34:11; Ezekiel 38:2"> And it has come to pass in that day, I give a place to Gog there—a grave in Israel, the valley of those passing by, east of the sea, and it is stopping those passing by, and they have buried there Gog, and all his multitude, and have cried, O valley of the multitude of Gog!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:23; Ezekiel 39:14; Ezekiel 39:16"> And the house of Israel has buried them—in order to cleanse the land—seven months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:22; Zephaniah 3:19–20; Jeremiah 33:9"> Indeed, all the people of the land have buried them, and it has been to them for a name—the day of My being honored—a declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:12; Numbers 19:11–19"> And they separate men for continual employment, with those passing through, passing on through the land, burying those who are left on the face of the earth, to cleanse it. At the end of seven months they search.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 11:44"> And those passing by have passed through the land, and seen a bone of man, and one has constructed a sign near it until those burying have buried it in the valley of the multitude of Gog.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And also the name of the city &#91;is&#93; The Multitude; and they have cleansed the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:7; Revelation 19:17–18; Isaiah 34:6"> And you, son of man, thus said Lord YHWH: Say to the bird—every wing, and to every beast of the field: Be assembled and come in, &#124;&#124; Be gathered from all around, &#124;&#124; For My sacrifice that I am sacrificing for you, &#124;&#124; A great sacrifice on mountains of Israel, &#124;&#124; And you have eaten flesh, and drunk blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 22:12; Jeremiah 51:40; Revelation 19:17–18"> You eat &#91;the&#93; flesh of &#91;the&#93; mighty, &#124;&#124; And you drink &#91;the&#93; blood of &#91;the&#93; princes of the earth, &#124;&#124; Of rams, of lambs, and of male goats, &#124;&#124; Of calves, fatlings of Bashan—all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And you have eaten fat to satiety, &#124;&#124; And you have drunk blood—to drunkenness, &#124;&#124; Of My sacrifice that I sacrificed for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 19:18; Haggai 2:22; Psalms 76:5–6"> And you have been satisfied at My table with horse and rider, &#124;&#124; Mighty man, and every man of war, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 38:23; Isaiah 37:20"> And I have given My glory among nations, &#124;&#124; And all the nations have seen My judgment that I have done, &#124;&#124; And My hand that I have laid on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Ezekiel 28:26; Ezekiel 39:28"> And the house of Israel has known that I &#91;am&#93; their God YHWH, &#124;&#124; From that day and from now on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; Isaiah 1:15; Isaiah 59:2"> And the nations have known &#124;&#124; That the house of Israel was removed for their iniquity, &#124;&#124; Because they have trespassed against Me, &#124;&#124; And I hide My face from them, &#124;&#124; And give them into the hand of their adversaries, &#124;&#124; And they fall by sword—all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:19; Jeremiah 2:17; Jeremiah 4:18"> According to their uncleanness, &#124;&#124; And according to their transgressions, I have done with them, &#124;&#124; And I hide My face from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:13; Isaiah 27:12–13; Hosea 1:11"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: Now I bring back the captivity of Jacob, &#124;&#124; And I have pitied all the house of Israel, &#124;&#124; And have been zealous for My holy Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Micah 4:4; 1 Kings 4:25; Ezekiel 16:63"> And they have forgotten their shame, &#124;&#124; And all their trespass that they trespassed against Me, &#124;&#124; In their dwelling on their land confidently and none troubling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 38:23; Ezekiel 36:23–24"> In My bringing them back from the peoples, &#124;&#124; I have assembled them from the lands of their enemies, &#124;&#124; And I have been sanctified in them before the eyes of the many nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:22; Amos 9:9; Isaiah 27:12"> And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; their God YHWH, &#124;&#124; In My removing them to the nations, &#124;&#124; And I have gathered them to their land, &#124;&#124; And I leave none of them there anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joel 2:28; 1 John 3:24; Isaiah 32:15"> And I do not hide My face from them anymore, &#124;&#124; In that I have poured out My Spirit on the house of Israel, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH!”</span>
