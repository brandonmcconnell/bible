<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:1–6; Amos 1:13–15; Ezekiel 6:2"> “Son of man, set your face toward the sons of Ammon, and prophesy against them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:2; Psalms 70:2–3; Proverbs 17:5"> and you have said to the sons of Ammon: Hear a word of Lord YHWH! Thus said Lord YHWH: Because of your saying, Aha, to My sanctuary, &#124;&#124; Because it has been defiled, &#124;&#124; And to the ground of Israel, &#124;&#124; Because it has been desolate, &#124;&#124; And to the house of Judah, &#124;&#124; Because they have gone into a removal:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Judges 6:33; 1 Kings 4:30"> Therefore, behold, I am giving you to sons of the east for a possession, &#124;&#124; And they set their towers in you, &#124;&#124; And have placed their dwelling places in you. They eat your fruit, and they drink your milk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; 2 Samuel 12:26; Isaiah 17:2"> And I have given Rabbah for a habitation of camels, &#124;&#124; And the sons of Ammon for the crouching of a flock, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:8; Obadiah 1:12; Zephaniah 2:10"> For thus said Lord YHWH: Because of your clapping the hand, &#124;&#124; And of your stamping with the foot, &#124;&#124; And you rejoice with all your despite in soul &#124;&#124; Against the ground of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:4; Ezekiel 25:16; Ezekiel 6:14"> Therefore, behold, I have stretched out My hand against you, &#124;&#124; And have given you for a portion to nations, &#124;&#124; And I have cut you off from the peoples, &#124;&#124; And caused you to perish from the lands; I destroy you, and you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 15:1–9; Jeremiah 25:21; Jeremiah 48:1–47"> Thus said Lord YHWH: Because of the saying of Moab and Seir: Behold, the house of Judah &#91;is&#93; as all the nations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:23; Joshua 13:17; Joshua 13:19–20"> Therefore, behold, I am opening the shoulder of Moab—From the cities—from his cities—from his frontier, &#124;&#124; The beauty of the land, Beth-Jeshimoth, Ba‘al-Meon, and Kiriathaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:32; Psalms 83:3–6; Ezekiel 25:4"> To the sons of the east, with the sons of Ammon, &#124;&#124; And I have given it for a possession, &#124;&#124; So that the sons of Ammon are not remembered among nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Ezekiel 30:19; Psalms 149:7"> And I do judgments in Moab, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:7–22; Ezekiel 25:8; Psalms 137:7"> Thus said Lord YHWH: Because of the doings of Edom, &#124;&#124; In taking vengeance on the house of Judah, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they are very guilty, &#124;&#124; And they have taken vengeance on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:8; Malachi 1:3–4; Jeremiah 25:23"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: I have stretched out My hand against Edom, &#124;&#124; And I have cut off man and beast from it, &#124;&#124; And given it up—a ruin, from Teman even to Dedan, &#124;&#124; They fall by sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 11:14; Genesis 27:29; Hebrews 10:30–31"> And I have given My vengeance on Edom, &#124;&#124; By the hand of My people Israel, &#124;&#124; And they have done in Edom, &#124;&#124; According to My anger, and according to My fury, &#124;&#124; And they have known My vengeance, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:6; Isaiah 14:29–31; Ezekiel 25:12"> Thus said Lord YHWH: Because of the doings of the Philistines in vengeance, &#124;&#124; And they take vengeance with despite in soul, &#124;&#124; Destruction &#91;with&#93; continuous enmity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:14; Zephaniah 2:4–15; Ezekiel 25:13"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: Behold, I am stretching out My hand against the Philistines, &#124;&#124; And I have cut off the Cherethim, &#124;&#124; And destroyed the remnant of the haven of the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:11; Ezekiel 25:14; Ezekiel 5:15"> And done great vengeance on them with furious reproofs, &#124;&#124; And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; In My giving out My vengeance on them!”</span>
