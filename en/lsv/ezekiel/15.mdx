<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 10:1; Psalms 80:8–16; Isaiah 5:1–7"> “Son of man, &#124;&#124; What is the vine-tree more than any tree? The vine-branch that has been, &#124;&#124; Among trees of the forest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 14:34–35; Jeremiah 24:8; Mark 9:50"> Is wood taken from it to use for work? Do they take a pin of it to hang any vessel on it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:6; Isaiah 27:11; Psalms 80:16"> Behold, it has been given to the fire for fuel, &#124;&#124; The fire has eaten its two ends, &#124;&#124; And its midst has been scorched! Is it profitable for work?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:16"> Behold, in its being perfect it is not used for work, &#124;&#124; How much less when fire has eaten of it, &#124;&#124; And it is scorched, &#124;&#124; Has it been used yet for work?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 15:2; Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 24:8–10"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: “As the vine-tree among trees of the forest, &#124;&#124; That I have given to the fire for fuel, &#124;&#124; So I have given the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:8; Psalms 34:16; Isaiah 24:18"> And I have set My face against them, &#124;&#124; They have gone forth from the fire, &#124;&#124; And the fire consumes them, &#124;&#124; And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; In My setting My face against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:14; Zephaniah 1:18; Isaiah 6:11"> And I have made the land a desolation, &#124;&#124; Because they have committed a trespass,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH!</span>
