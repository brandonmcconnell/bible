<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Galatians 5:17; Romans 7:23"> From where &#91;are&#93; wars and fightings among you? &#91;Is it&#93; not from here, out of your passions warring in your members?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 16:24; Matthew 7:7–8; James 1:5"> You desire, and do not have, &#91;so&#93; you murder; and you are zealous, and are not able to attain, &#91;so&#93; you fight and war; and you do not have, because of your not asking;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 5:14; 1 John 3:22; James 1:6–7"> you ask, and you do not receive, because you ask badly, that you may spend &#91;it&#93; in your pleasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Romans 8:7; John 15:19"> Adulterers and adulteresses! Have you not known that friendship of the world is enmity with God? Whoever, then, may intend to be a friend of the world, he is designated &#91;as&#93; an enemy of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 6:19; Numbers 11:29"> Or, do you think that the Writing says emptily, “The Spirit that has dwelt in us yearns with envy,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 3:34; 1 Peter 5:5"> but &#91;God&#93; gives greater grace, for this reason it says, “God sets Himself up against proud ones, and He gives grace to lowly ones.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–9; Ephesians 4:27; Ephesians 6:11–12"> Be subject, then, to God; stand up against the Devil, and he will flee from you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; Hebrews 10:22; Malachi 3:7"> draw near to God, and He will draw near to you; cleanse hands, you sinners! And purify hearts, you split-souled!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10–11; Matthew 5:4; Luke 6:25"> Be exceedingly afflicted, and mourn, and weep, let your laughter be turned to mourning, and the joy to heaviness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 147:6; Matthew 23:12; 1 Peter 5:6"> be made low before the LORD, and He will exalt you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 2:1; Romans 2:1; James 5:9"> Do not speak against one another, brothers; he who is speaking against a brother, and is judging his brother, speaks against law, and judges law, and if you judge law, you are not a doer of law but a judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 14:4; Romans 2:1; Matthew 10:28"> One is the lawgiver, who is able to save and to destroy; you—who are you that judges the other?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Luke 12:17–20; Genesis 11:3–4"> Go, now, you who are saying, “Today and tomorrow we will go on to such a city, and will pass there one year, and traffic, and make gain,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; Psalms 39:5; 1 John 2:17"> who does not know the thing of tomorrow; for what is your life? For it is a vapor that is appearing for a little &#91;while&#93;, and then is vanishing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Hebrews 6:3; Lamentations 3:37"> instead, you &#91;ought&#93; to say, “If the LORD may will, we will live, and do this or that”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; 1 Corinthians 5:6; Psalms 52:7"> but now you glory in your pride; all such glorying is evil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:41; John 13:17; John 15:22"> to him, then, knowing to do good, and not doing &#91;it&#93;, it is sin to him.</span>
