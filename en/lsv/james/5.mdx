<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 11:28; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; Proverbs 11:4"> Go, now, you rich! Weep, howling over your miseries that are coming on &#91;you&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–20; Job 13:28; Luke 12:33"> your riches have rotted, and your garments have become moth-eaten;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 2:5; James 5:7–8; Revelation 21:8"> your gold and silver have rotted, and the rust of them will be to you for a testimony, and will eat your flesh as fire. You have stored up treasure in the last days!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Jeremiah 22:13; Deuteronomy 24:14–15"> Behold, the reward of the workmen cries out, of those who in-gathered your fields, which has been fraudulently kept back by you, and the exclamations of those who reaped have entered into the ears of the LORD of Hosts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:6; Luke 16:25; Luke 16:19"> you lived in luxury on the earth, and were wanton; you nourished your hearts, as in a day of slaughter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:22–23; John 19:9–11; Isaiah 53:7"> you condemned—you murdered the righteous; he does not resist you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 12:1–3; James 5:8–9; Joel 2:23"> Be patient, then, brothers, until the coming of the LORD; behold, the farmer expects the precious fruit of the earth, being patient for it, until he may receive rain—early and latter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 5:9; Galatians 5:22; Psalms 37:7"> you also be patient; establish your hearts, because the coming of the LORD has drawn near;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; 1 Corinthians 4:5; James 4:11"> do not murmur against one another, brothers, that you may not be condemned; behold, the Judge has stood before the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 11:32–38; Matthew 5:11–12; Hebrews 13:7"> Brothers, &#91;as&#93; an example of the suffering of evil and of patience, take the prophets who spoke in the Name of the LORD;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 1:12; Exodus 34:6; Job 1:21–22"> behold, we call those who are enduring blessed; you heard of the endurance of Job, and you have seen the end from the LORD, that the LORD is very compassionate, and pitying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:33–37; Matthew 23:16–22; 2 Corinthians 1:17–20"> And before all things, my brothers, do not swear, neither by Heaven, neither by the earth, neither by any other oath, but let your “Yes” be yes, and the “No,” no, that you may not fall under judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Jonah 2:2; Acts 16:24–25"> Does anyone suffer evil among you? Let him pray; is anyone of good cheer? Let him sing psalms;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 6:13; Acts 28:8; Mark 16:18"> is anyone sick among you? Let him call for the elders of the assembly, and let them pray over him, having anointed him with oil, in the Name of the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 5:14–16; James 5:13; James 5:16"> and the prayer of faith will save the distressed one, and the LORD will raise him up, and if he may have committed sins, they will be forgiven to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12–13; Proverbs 15:29; Matthew 21:22"> Be confessing to one another the trespasses, and be praying for one another, that you may be healed; very strong is a working supplication of a righteous man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Luke 4:25; Revelation 11:6"> Elijah was a similar-feeling man as us, and with prayer he prayed—not to rain, and it did not rain on the land three years and six months;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 18:42–45; Jeremiah 14:22; 1 Kings 18:18"> and again he prayed, and the sky gave rain, and the land brought forth her fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:1; Matthew 18:15; James 5:20"> Brothers, if anyone among you may go astray from the truth, and anyone may turn him back,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; Proverbs 10:12; 1 Timothy 4:16"> let him know that he who turned back a sinner from the straying of his way will save a soul from death, and will cover a multitude of sins.</span>
