<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:47–48; 1 Timothy 1:7; 1 Peter 5:3"> Do not let many be teachers, my brothers, having known that we will receive greater judgment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:26; Proverbs 13:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> for we all make many stumbles; if anyone does not stumble in word, this one &#91;is&#93; a perfect man, able to also bridle the whole body;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 32:9; Psalms 39:1; James 1:26"> behold, the bits we put into the mouths of the horses for their obeying us, and we direct their whole body;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 107:25–27; Matthew 8:24; Acts 27:14–38"> behold, also the ships, being so great, and being driven by fierce winds, are directed by a very small rudder, wherever the impulse of the &#91;one&#93; steering wills,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:21; Jeremiah 9:3–8; Proverbs 12:18"> so also the tongue is a little member, and boasts greatly; behold, how much forest a little fire kindles!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 120:2–3; Proverbs 16:27; Mark 7:15"> And the tongue &#91;is&#93; a fire, the world of the unrighteousness, so the tongue is set in our members, which is spotting our whole body, and is setting on fire the course of nature, and is set on fire by Gehenna.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 5:4"> For every nature, both of beasts and of birds, both of creeping things and things of the sea, is subdued, and has been subdued, by the human nature,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalms 140:3; Psalms 55:21"> but no one of men is able to subdue the tongue—&#91;it is&#93; an unruly evil, full of deadly poison;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Genesis 1:26–27; Psalms 62:4"> with it we bless the God and Father, and with it we curse the men made according to &#91;the&#93; likeness of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:14; 1 Peter 3:9; 1 Corinthians 3:3"> out of the same mouth comes forth blessing and cursing; it does not need, my brothers, these things to so happen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> does the fountain out of the same opening pour forth the sweet and the bitter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:43–44; Matthew 12:33; Matthew 7:16–20"> Is a fig tree able, my brothers, to make olives? Or a vine figs? Neither is salty &#91;water able&#93; to have made sweet water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; James 3:17; Matthew 5:5"> Who &#91;is&#93; wise and intelligent among you? Let him show his works out of good behavior in meekness of wisdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 3:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4; Philippians 2:3"> yet, if you have bitter zeal, and rivalry in your heart, do not glory, nor lie against the truth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> this wisdom is not descending from above, but earthly, physical, demon-like,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 3:14; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> for where zeal and rivalry &#91;are&#93;, there is insurrection and every evil matter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 5:22–23; Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> but the wisdom from above, first, indeed, is pure, then peaceable, gentle, well-convinced, full of kindness and good fruits, uncontentious, and unhypocritical—</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Hosea 10:12; Philippians 1:11"> and the fruit of righteousness in peace is sown to those making peace.</span>
