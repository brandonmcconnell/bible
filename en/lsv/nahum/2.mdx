<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 80:12–13; Ezekiel 37:23; Zephaniah 3:11"> For YHWH has turned back to the excellence of Jacob, &#124;&#124; As &#91;to&#93; the excellence of Israel, &#124;&#124; For emptiers have emptied them out, &#124;&#124; And they have marred their branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:14–15; Isaiah 63:1–3; Zechariah 6:2"> The shield of his mighty ones has become red, &#124;&#124; Men of might &#91;are in&#93; scarlet, &#124;&#124; With fiery torches &#91;is&#93; the chariot in a day of his preparation, &#124;&#124; And the firs have been caused to tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Ezekiel 26:10; Nahum 3:2–3"> In out-places the chariots shine, &#124;&#124; They go to and fro in broad places, &#124;&#124; Their appearances &#91;are&#93; like torches, &#124;&#124; As lightnings they run.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:12; Jeremiah 51:27–28; Jeremiah 50:29"> He remembers his majestic ones, &#124;&#124; They stumble in their goings, &#124;&#124; They hurry &#91;to&#93; its wall, &#124;&#124; And the covering is prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1–2; 2 Peter 3:10–11"> Gates of the rivers have been opened, &#124;&#124; And the palace is dissolved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Isaiah 59:11; Luke 23:48"> And it is established—she has removed, &#124;&#124; She has been brought up, &#124;&#124; And her handmaids are leading as the voice of doves, &#124;&#124; Tabering on their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:13; Revelation 17:1; Jeremiah 46:5"> And Nineveh &#91;is&#93; as a pool of waters, &#124;&#124; From of old it &#91;is&#93;—and they are fleeing! “Stand, stand”; and none is turning!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:12; Daniel 11:8; Nahum 2:12–13"> Seize silver, seize gold, &#124;&#124; And there is no end to the prepared things, &#124;&#124; &#91;To&#93; the abundance of all desirable vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:6; Psalms 22:14; Daniel 5:6"> She is empty, indeed, emptiness and waste, &#124;&#124; And the heart has melted, &#124;&#124; And the knees have struck together, &#124;&#124; And great pain &#91;is&#93; in all loins, &#124;&#124; And the faces of all of them have gathered paleness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:29; Jeremiah 2:15; Isaiah 31:4"> Where &#91;is&#93; the habitation of lionesses? And a feeding-place it &#91;is&#93; for young lions &#124;&#124; Where a lion has walked, an old lion, &#124;&#124; A lion’s whelp, and there is none troubling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34; Psalms 17:12; Isaiah 10:6–14"> The lion is tearing parts &#91;for&#93; his whelps, &#124;&#124; And is strangling for his lionesses, &#124;&#124; And he fills his holes &#91;with&#93; prey, &#124;&#124; And his habitations &#91;with&#93; torn flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Psalms 46:9; Nahum 3:5"> “Behold, I &#91;am&#93; against you,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; “And I have burned its chariot in smoke, &#124;&#124; And a sword consumes your young lions, &#124;&#124; And I have cut off your prey from the land, &#124;&#124; And the voice of your messengers is not heard anymore!”</span>
