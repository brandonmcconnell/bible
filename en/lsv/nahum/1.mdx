<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:13; Jonah 1:2; Zechariah 9:1"> Burden of Nineveh. The Scroll of the Vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 8:2; Exodus 20:5; Psalms 94:1"> A God zealous and avenging &#91;is&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; An avenger &#91;is&#93; YHWH, and possessing fury. An avenger &#91;is&#93; YHWH on His adversaries, &#124;&#124; And He is watching for His enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 147:5; Exodus 34:6–7; Nehemiah 9:17"> YHWH &#91;is&#93; slow to anger, and great in power, &#124;&#124; And YHWH does not entirely acquit, &#124;&#124; In a windstorm and in a storm &#91;is&#93; His way, &#124;&#124; And a cloud &#91;is&#93; the dust of His feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 33:9; Psalms 106:9; Psalms 114:5"> He is pushing against a sea, and dries it up, &#124;&#124; Indeed, He has made all the floods dry, &#124;&#124; Bashan and Carmel &#91;are&#93; languishing, &#124;&#124; Indeed, the flower of Lebanon &#91;is&#93; languishing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 1:4; 2 Samuel 22:8; Jeremiah 4:24"> Mountains have shaken because of Him, &#124;&#124; And the hills have been melted; And the earth &#91;is&#93; lifted up at His presence, &#124;&#124; And the world and all dwelling in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:10; Malachi 3:2; Deuteronomy 32:22–23"> Who stands before His indignation? And who rises up in the heat of His anger? His fury has been poured out like fire, &#124;&#124; And the rocks have been broken by Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:27; 2 Timothy 2:19; Lamentations 3:25"> Good &#91;is&#93; YHWH for a strong place in a day of distress. And He knows those trusting in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 28:17; Isaiah 8:22; Ezekiel 13:13"> And with a flood passing over, &#124;&#124; An end He makes of its place, &#124;&#124; And darkness pursues His enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 21:11; Proverbs 21:30; Psalms 33:10"> What do we devise against YHWH? He is making an end, distress does not arise twice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 7:4; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 9:18"> For while princes &#91;are&#93; perplexed, &#124;&#124; And with their drink are drunken, &#124;&#124; They have been consumed as stubble fully dried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; 2 Chronicles 13:7; 2 Kings 18:13–14"> From you has come forth a deviser of evil &#124;&#124; Against YHWH—a worthless counselor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 30:19; Joel 2:19; Isaiah 17:14"> Thus said YHWH: “Though complete, and thus many, &#124;&#124; Yet thus they have been cut off, &#124;&#124; And he has passed away. Though I afflicted you, I afflict you no longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 10:27; Isaiah 9:4; Jeremiah 2:20"> And now I break his rod from off you, &#124;&#124; And your bands I draw away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 109:13; Isaiah 46:1–2; Micah 5:13"> And YHWH has commanded concerning you, &#124;&#124; “No more of your name spreads abroad, &#124;&#124; I cut off carved and molten image from the house of your gods, &#124;&#124; I appoint your grave, for you have been vile.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Romans 10:15; Isaiah 40:9–10"> Behold, on the mountains the feet of one proclaiming tidings, sounding peace! Celebrate, O Judah, your festivals, complete your vows, &#124;&#124; For the worthless no longer pass over into you, &#124;&#124; He has been completely cut off!</span>
