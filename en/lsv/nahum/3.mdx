<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 6:2; Ezekiel 30:14–16; Jeremiah 46:25–26"> Are you better than No-Ammon, &#124;&#124; That is dwelling among brooks? Waters she has around her, &#124;&#124; Whose bulwark &#91;is&#93; the sea, waters her wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:5; Ezekiel 27:10; Isaiah 20:5"> Cush her might, and Egypt, and there is no end. Put and Lubim have been for your help.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Isaiah 2:10; Psalms 75:8"> Even you are drunken, you are hidden, &#124;&#124; Even you seek a strong place, because of an enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 6:13; Isaiah 28:4; Habakkuk 1:10"> All your fortresses &#91;are&#93; fig trees with first-fruits, &#124;&#124; If they are shaken, &#124;&#124; They have fallen into the mouth of the eater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Isaiah 19:16; Jeremiah 50:37"> Behold, your people &#91;are&#93; women in your midst, &#124;&#124; To your enemies thoroughly opened &#124;&#124; Have been the gates of your land, &#124;&#124; Fire has consumed your bars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:3–4; Nahum 2:1; 2 Chronicles 32:11"> Waters of a siege draw for yourself, &#124;&#124; Strengthen your fortresses, &#124;&#124; Enter into mire, and tread on clay, &#124;&#124; Make strong a brick-kiln.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Nahum 2:13; Nahum 3:13"> There a fire consumes you, &#124;&#124; A sword cuts you off, &#124;&#124; It consumes you as a cankerworm! Make yourself heavy as the cankerworm, &#124;&#124; Make yourself heavy as the locust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Nehemiah 9:23; Jeremiah 33:22"> Multiply your merchants above the stars of the heavens, &#124;&#124; The cankerworm has stripped off, and flees away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:27; Revelation 9:7"> Your crowned ones &#91;are&#93; as a locust, &#124;&#124; And your princes as great grasshoppers, &#124;&#124; That encamp in hedges in a day of cold, &#124;&#124; The sun has risen, and it flees away, &#124;&#124; And its place where they are is not known.</span>
