<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 16:9; 2 Corinthians 9:2; 2 Corinthians 9:4"> And we make known to you, brothers, the grace of God, that has been given in the assemblies of Macedonia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 2:5; Proverbs 11:25; 2 Corinthians 6:10"> because in much trial of tribulation the abundance of their joy, and their deep poverty, abounded to the riches of their liberality;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:5; Exodus 35:21–22"> because, according to &#91;their&#93; power, I testify, and above &#91;their&#93; power, they were willing of themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> with much plea calling on us to receive the favor and the fellowship of the ministry to the holy ones,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 12:1; Romans 14:7–9; 2 Chronicles 30:12"> and not according as we expected, but they gave themselves first to the LORD, and to us, through the will of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 2 Corinthians 8:4"> so that we exhorted Titus, that, according as he began before, so also he may also finish this favor to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8; 2 Peter 1:5–8; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> but even as in everything you abound, in faith, and word, and knowledge, and all diligence, and in your love to us, that also in this grace you may abound;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:6; 1 John 3:17–19; James 2:14–16"> I do not speak according to command, but because of the diligence of others, and proving the genuineness of your love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Philippians 2:6–8; Matthew 20:28"> for you know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that because of you He became poor—being rich, that you may become rich by that poverty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18–19; Hebrews 13:16; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> And I give an opinion in this: for this &#91;is&#93; expedient to you, who not only to do, but also to will, began before—a year ago,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:2"> and now also finish doing &#91;it&#93;, that even as &#91;there is&#93; the readiness of the will, so also the finishing, out of that which you have,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 12:42–44; 2 Corinthians 9:7; Luke 21:1–4"> for if the willing mind is present, it is well-accepted according to that which anyone may have, not according to that which he does not have;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 4:34; Romans 15:26–27"> for I do not speak that for others &#91;to be&#93; released, and you pressured,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:12; Acts 4:34"> but by equality, at the present time your abundance—for their want, that also their abundance may be for your want, that there may be equality,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:18; Luke 22:35"> according as it has been written: “He who &#91;gathered&#93; much, had nothing over; and he who &#91;gathered&#93; little, had no lack.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Jeremiah 32:40; 2 Corinthians 2:13–14"> And thanks to God, who is putting the same diligence for you in the heart of Titus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 13:22; 2 Corinthians 8:10"> because he indeed accepted the exhortation, and being more diligent, he went forth to you of his own accord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> and we sent with him the brother, whose praise in the good news &#91;is&#93; through all the assemblies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:3–4; 2 Corinthians 4:15; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> and not only so, but who was also appointed by vote by the assemblies, our fellow-traveler, with this favor that is ministered by us, to the glory of the same Lord, and your willing mind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:22; Romans 14:16; Ephesians 5:15"> avoiding this, lest anyone may blame us in this abundance that is ministered by us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Peter 2:12; Philippians 4:8"> providing right things, not only before the LORD, but also before men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 2:20–22"> and we sent our brother with them, whom we proved being diligent many times in many things, and now much more diligent, by the great confidence that is toward you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Philemon 1:17; 2 Corinthians 8:6"> whether—about Titus—my partner and fellow-worker toward you, whether—our brothers, apostles of assemblies—glory of Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:14; 2 Corinthians 7:4; 2 Corinthians 9:2–4"> the showing therefore of your love, and of our boasting on your behalf, show to them, even in the face of the assemblies.</span>
