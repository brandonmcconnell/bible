<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:23; 2 Corinthians 12:20–21; 2 Corinthians 13:10"> And I decided this to myself, not to come again to you in sorrow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:8; 2 Corinthians 1:14; 1 Corinthians 12:26"> for if I make you sorry, then who is he who is making me glad, except he who is made sorry by me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 5:10; 2 Corinthians 8:22; Philemon 1:21"> And I wrote to you this same thing, that having come, I may not have sorrow from them of whom it was necessary &#91;for&#93; me to have joy, having confidence in you all, that my joy is of you all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:12; Proverbs 27:5–6; Leviticus 19:17–18"> for out of much tribulation and pressure of heart I wrote to you through many tears, not that you might be made sorry, but that you might know the love that I have more abundantly toward you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:12–13; Galatians 4:12; Proverbs 17:25"> And if anyone has caused sorrow, he has not caused sorrow to me, but in part, that I may not burden you all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:4–5; 1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Corinthians 7:11"> sufficient to such a one is this punishment, that &#91;is&#93; by the greater part,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Galatians 6:1–2; Colossians 3:13"> so that, on the contrary, &#91;it is&#93; rather for you to forgive and to comfort, lest by over abundant sorrow such a one may be swallowed up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:22–23; Galatians 6:1–2; Galatians 6:10"> for this reason, I call on you to confirm love to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:6; Philemon 1:21; Philippians 2:22"> for this also I wrote, that I might know your proof, whether you are obedient in regard to all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 20:23; Matthew 18:18; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> And to whom you forgive anything—I also; for I also, if I have forgiven anything, to whom I have forgiven &#91;it&#93;, because of you—in the person of Christ—&#91;I forgive it,&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Ephesians 6:11–12; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> that we may not be over-reached by Satan, for we are not ignorant of his schemes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 16:8; Acts 14:27; Colossians 4:3"> And having come to Troas for the good news of the Christ, and a door having been opened to me in the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:5–6; 2 Corinthians 12:18; Titus 1:4"> I have not had rest to my spirit, on my not finding my brother Titus, but having taken leave of them, I went forth to Macedonia;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Romans 8:37; 2 Corinthians 2:15–16"> and to God &#91;is&#93; thanks, who at all times is leading us in triumph in the Christ, and the fragrance of His knowledge He is revealing through us in every place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 1:18; Ezekiel 20:41"> we are a refreshing fragrance to God because of Christ, in those being saved, and in those being lost;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5–6; John 9:39; 1 Peter 2:7–8"> to one, indeed, a fragrance of death to death, and to the other, a fragrance of life to life; and who is sufficient for these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Corinthians 1:12; 2 Peter 2:1–3"> For we are not as the many, adulterating the word of God, but as of sincerity—but as of God; in the presence of God, in Christ we speak.</span>
