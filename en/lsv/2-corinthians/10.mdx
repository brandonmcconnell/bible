<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:10; Matthew 11:29; Galatians 5:2"> And I, Paul, myself, call on you—through the meekness and gentleness of the Christ—who in presence &#91;am&#93; indeed humble among you, and being absent, have courage toward you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:10; 2 Corinthians 13:2; Galatians 5:16–25"> and I implore &#91;you&#93;, that, being present, I may not have courage, with the confidence with which I reckon to be bold against certain reckoning us as walking according to the flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; Galatians 2:20; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> for walking in the flesh, we do not war according to the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:7; Ephesians 6:13–18; Jeremiah 1:10"> for the weapons of our warfare &#91;are&#93; not fleshly, but powerful to God for bringing down of strongholds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Isaiah 55:7; 1 Peter 1:14–15"> bringing down reasonings, and every high thing lifted up against the knowledge of God, and bringing into captivity every thought to the obedience of the Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:9; Numbers 16:26–30; 2 Corinthians 7:15"> and being in readiness to avenge every disobedience whenever your obedience may be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:12; John 7:24; 1 Corinthians 14:37"> You see the outward appearance. If anyone has trusted in himself to be Christ’s, let him reckon this again from himself, that according as he is Christ’s, so also we &#91;are&#93; Christ’s;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:10; 2 Corinthians 12:6; Galatians 1:1"> for even if I will also boast anything more abundantly concerning our authority, that the LORD gave us for building up, and not for casting you down, I will not be ashamed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:10; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> that I may not seem as if I would terrify you through the letters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:17; Exodus 4:10; Galatians 4:13–14"> “because the letters indeed,” says one, “&#91;are&#93; weighty and strong, and the bodily presence weak, and the speech despicable.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:20; 2 Corinthians 13:2–3; 2 Corinthians 13:10"> This one—let him reckon thus: that such as we are in word, through letters, being absent, such also, being present, &#91;we are&#93; in deed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; Proverbs 26:12; Luke 18:11"> For we do not make bold to rank or to compare ourselves with certain of those commending themselves, but they, measuring themselves among themselves, and comparing themselves with themselves, are not wise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 12:6; Romans 15:20; 1 Peter 4:10"> and we will not boast ourselves in regard to the unmeasured things, but after the measure of the line that the God of measure appointed to us—to reach even to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Timothy 1:11; Romans 1:16"> for we do not stretch ourselves too much, as not reaching to you, for even to you we came in the good news of the Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:20; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 2 Corinthians 10:13"> not boasting of the things not measured, in other men’s labors, and having hope—your faith increasing—in you to be enlarged, according to our line—into abundance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 15:24–28; Acts 19:21"> to proclaim good news in the &#91;places&#93; beyond you, not in another’s line in regard to the things made ready, to boast;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; 1 Corinthians 1:31; James 1:9–10"> and he who is boasting—let him boast in the LORD;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Proverbs 21:2; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> for he who is commending himself is not approved, but he whom the LORD commends.</span>
