<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:26–39; Matthew 8:28–34; Mark 4:35"> And they came to the other side of the sea, to the region of the Gadarenes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 1:23; Isaiah 65:4; Mark 5:8"> and He having come forth out of the boat, immediately there met Him out of the tombs a man with an unclean spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 65:4; Luke 8:29; Daniel 4:32–33"> who had his dwelling in the tombs, and not even with chains was anyone able to bind him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 3:7–8"> because that many times he had been bound with shackles and chains, and the chains had been pulled in pieces by him, and the shackles broken in pieces, and none was able to tame him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 2:7–8; 1 Kings 18:28; John 8:44"> and always, night and day, he was in the mountains, and in the tombs, crying and cutting himself with stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 2:19; Psalms 66:3; Luke 4:41"> And having seen Jesus from afar, he ran and prostrated to Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; Acts 16:17; Matthew 26:63"> and having called with a loud voice, he said, “What &#91;regards&#93; me and You, Jesus, Son of God the Most High? I adjure You by God, may You not afflict me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 1:25; Mark 9:25–26; Acts 16:18"> For He said to him, “Come forth, unclean spirit, out of the man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 8:30; Mark 5:15; Matthew 26:53"> And He was questioning him, “What &#91;is&#93; your name?” And he answered, saying, “Legion &#91;is&#93; my name, because we are many”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Mark 5:13"> and he was calling on Him much, that He may not send them out of the region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 11:7–8; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> And there was there, near the mountains, a great herd of pigs feeding,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:11; Job 1:10–12; Luke 22:31–32"> and all the demons called on Him, saying, “Send us to the pigs, that into them we may enter”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 22:22; 1 Peter 3:22; Job 2:6"> and immediately Jesus gave them leave, and having come forth, the unclean spirits entered into the pigs, and the herd rushed down the steep place to the sea—and they were about two thousand—and they were choked in the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 8:34; Matthew 8:33"> And those feeding the pigs fled, and told in the city, and in the fields, and they came forth to see what it is that has been done;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 2 Timothy 1:7; 1 Samuel 6:20–21"> and they come to Jesus, and see the demoniac, sitting, and clothed, and right-minded—him having had the legion—and they were afraid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and those having seen &#91;it&#93;, declared to them how it had come to pass to the demoniac, and about the pigs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 8:34; Mark 1:24; Luke 8:37"> and they began to call on Him to go away from their borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:38–39; Philippians 1:23–24; Mark 5:17"> And He having gone into the boat, the demoniac was calling on Him that he may be with Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 66:16; John 4:29; Isaiah 38:9–20"> and Jesus did not permit him, but says to him, “Go away to your house, to your own &#91;friends&#93;, and tell them how the LORD did great things to you, and dealt kindly with you”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:31; Matthew 4:25"> and he went away, and began to proclaim in the Decapolis how Jesus did great things to him, and all were wondering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 9:1; Mark 4:1; Luke 8:40"> And Jesus having passed over in the boat again to the other side, there was gathered a great multitude to Him, and He was near the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Acts 18:8; Mark 5:35–36"> and behold, there comes one of the chiefs of the synagogue, by name Jairus, and having seen Him, he falls at His feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 13:13; Luke 4:40; Mark 16:18"> and he was calling on Him much, saying, “My little daughter is at the last extremity—that having come, You may lay on her &#91;Your&#93; hands, so that she may be saved, and she will live”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 5:31; Mark 3:20; Luke 7:6"> and He went away with him. And there was following Him a great multitude, and they were thronging Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:25–27; Luke 8:43–44; Matthew 9:20–22"> and a certain woman, being with a flow of blood &#91;for&#93; twelve years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:12–13; Jeremiah 8:22; Psalms 108:12"> and having suffered many things under many physicians, and having spent all that she had, and having profited nothing, but rather having come to the worse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 19:12; Matthew 14:36; Acts 5:15"> having heard about Jesus, having come in the multitude behind, she touched His garment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> for she said, “If I may even touch His garments, I will be saved”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Psalms 30:2; Mark 3:10"> and immediately the fountain of her blood was dried up, and she knew in the body that she has been healed of the plague.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 8:46; Luke 5:17"> And immediately Jesus having known in Himself that power had gone forth out of Him, having turned in the multitude, said, “Who touched My garments?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:45; Luke 9:12"> And His disciples said to Him, “You see the multitude thronging You, and You say, Who touched Me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And He was looking around to see her who did this,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:47; Luke 1:29; Luke 1:12"> and the woman, having been afraid, and trembling, knowing what was done on her, came, and fell down before Him, and told Him all the truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 8:48; Matthew 9:22; Luke 7:50"> and He said to her, “Daughter, your faith has saved you; go away in peace, and be whole from your plague.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 8:49; John 11:39; Mark 5:22"> As He is yet speaking, there come from the chief of the synagogue’s &#91;house, certain&#93;, saying, “Your daughter died, why do you still harass the Teacher?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 8:50; Matthew 9:28–29; John 11:40"> And Jesus immediately, having heard the word that is spoken, says to the chief of the synagogue, “Do not be afraid, only believe.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 9:40; Mark 14:33; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> And He did not permit anyone to follow with Him, except Peter, and James, and John the brother of James;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:23–24; Luke 8:52–53; Mark 5:22"> and He comes into the house of the chief of the synagogue, and sees a tumult, much weeping and wailing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 20:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–14"> and having gone in He says to them, “Why do you make a tumult, and weep? The child did not die, but sleeps”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; 2 Kings 4:33; Matthew 9:24–25"> and they were laughing at Him. And He, having put all forth, takes the father of the child, and the mother, and those with Him, and goes in where the child is lying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Luke 7:14–15; Luke 8:54–55"> and having taken the hand of the child, He says to her, “Talitha cumi”; which is, being interpreted, “Girl (I say to you), arise.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Mark 4:41; Acts 3:10–13; Mark 7:37"> And immediately the girl arose, and was walking, for she was twelve years &#91;old&#93;; and they were amazed with a great amazement,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Mark 1:43"> and He charged them much, that no one may know this thing, and He said that there be given to her to eat.</span>
