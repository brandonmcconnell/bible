<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:16–30; Matthew 13:54–58; Matthew 2:23"> And He went forth from there, and came to His own country, and His disciples follow Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 7:15; John 6:42; Acts 4:13–14"> and Sabbath having come, He began to teach in the synagogue, and many hearing were astonished, saying, “From where &#91;did&#93; this One &#91;hear&#93; these things? And what &#91;is&#93; the wisdom that was given to Him, that also such mighty works are done through His hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; Matthew 13:55–57; John 6:42"> Is this not the carpenter, the Son of Mary, and brother of James, and Joses, and Judas, and Simon? And are His sisters not here with us?” And they were being stumbled at Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 4:44; Luke 4:24; Matthew 13:57"> And Jesus said to them, “A prophet is not without honor, except in his own country, and among his relatives, and in his own house”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 13:58; Mark 9:23; Mark 5:23"> and He was not able to do any mighty work there, except having put hands on a few sick, He healed &#91;them&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 9:35; Matthew 8:10; Luke 13:22"> and He wondered because of their unbelief. And He was going around the villages, in a circle, teaching,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:9–10; Luke 10:17–20; Matthew 10:1–5"> and He calls near the Twelve, and He began to send them forth two by two, and He was giving them power over the unclean spirits,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:3; Matthew 10:9–10; Luke 10:4"> and He commanded them that they may take nothing for the way, except a staff only—no leather pouch, no bread, no brass in the girdle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 12:8; Ephesians 6:15"> but having been shod with sandals, and you may not put on two coats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 10:7–8; Luke 9:4; Matthew 10:11–13"> And He said to them, “Whenever you may enter into a house, remain there until you may depart from there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 10:14–15; Acts 18:6; Luke 10:10–15"> and as many as may not receive you nor hear you, going out from there, shake off the dust that is under your feet for a testimony to them; &#91;&#91;truly I say to you, it will be more tolerable for Sodom or Gomorrah in &#91;the&#93; day of judgment than for that city.”&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 4:17; Luke 13:3; Acts 20:21"> And having gone forth they were preaching that &#91;men&#93; might convert,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 5:14–15; Mark 6:7; Luke 10:17"> and they were casting out many demons, and they were anointing many sick with oil, and they were healing &#91;them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 9:7–9; Mark 6:14–29; Luke 23:7–12"> And King Herod heard (for His Name became public), and he said, “John the Immerser was raised out of the dead, and because of this the mighty powers are working in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 16:14; Malachi 4:5; Matthew 21:11"> Others said, “It is Elijah,” and others said, “It is a prophet, or as one of the prophets.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 11:10–13; Matthew 27:4; Luke 9:9"> And Herod having heard, said, “He whom I beheaded—John—this is he; he was raised out of the dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 3:19–20; Matthew 11:2; Luke 3:1"> For Herod himself, having sent forth, laid hold on John, and bound him in the prison, because of Herodias, the wife of his brother Philip, because he married her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 20:21; Leviticus 18:16; 1 Kings 22:14"> for John said to Herod, “It is not lawful for you to have the wife of your brother”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:9; Ephesians 4:26–27; Genesis 39:17–20"> and Herodias was having a quarrel with him, and was willing to kill him, and was not able,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 14:5; 2 Chronicles 24:15–22"> for Herod was fearing John, knowing him &#91;to be&#93; a righteous and holy man, and was keeping watch over him, and having heard him, was doing many things, and hearing him gladly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esther 2:18; Genesis 40:20; 1 Peter 4:3"> And a seasonable day having come when Herod on his birthday was making a banquet to his great men, and to the chiefs of thousands, and to the first men of Galilee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esther 1:10–12; Matthew 14:6; Daniel 5:2"> and the daughter of that Herodias having come in, and having danced, and having pleased Herod and those dining with him, the king said to the girl, “Ask of me whatever you will, and I will give to you,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esther 7:2; Esther 5:3; Esther 5:6"> and he swore to her, “Whatever you may ask me, I will give to you—to the half of my kingdom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 31:31; Ezekiel 19:2–3; Genesis 27:8–11"> And she, having gone forth, said to her mother, “What will I ask for myself?” And she said, “The head of John the Immerser”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 3:15; Numbers 7:19–89; Numbers 7:13"> and having come in immediately with haste to the king, she asked, saying, “I will that you may immediately give me the head of John the Immerser on a plate.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:24–25; Matthew 14:9; Matthew 27:3–5"> And the king, made very sorrowful because of the oaths and of those reclining with him, would not put her away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 14:10–11"> and immediately the king having sent a guardsman, commanded his head to be brought,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and he having gone, beheaded him in the prison, and brought his head on a plate, and gave it to the girl, and the girl gave it to her mother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 14:12; 2 Chronicles 24:16; Matthew 27:57–60"> and having heard, his disciples came and took up his corpse, and laid it in the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 9:10; Luke 10:17; Matthew 10:2"> And the apostles are gathered together to Jesus, and they told Him all, and how many things they did, and how many things they taught,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 3:20; John 6:1; Mark 1:45"> and He said to them, “Come yourselves apart to a desolate place, and rest a little,” for those coming and those going were many, and not even to eat had they opportunity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 8:2–9; Mark 6:45; Mark 4:36"> and they went away to a desolate place, in the boat, by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 6:2; Matthew 15:29–31; Mark 6:54–55"> And the multitudes saw them going away, and many recognized Him, and they ran there by land from all the cities, and went before them, and came together to Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Luke 9:11; Numbers 27:17"> and having come forth, Jesus saw a great multitude, and was moved with compassion on them, that they were as sheep not having a shepherd, and He began to teach many things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 6:5–15; Luke 9:12–17; Matthew 14:15–21"> And now the hour being advanced, His disciples having come near to Him, say, “The place is desolate, and the hour is now advanced,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 15:23; Mark 3:21; Mark 5:31"> let them away, that having gone away into the surrounding fields and villages, they may buy loaves for themselves, for they do not have what they may eat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; Matthew 15:32–33; Mark 8:2–3"> And He answering said to them, “You give them to eat,” and they say to Him, “Having gone away, may we buy two hundred denarii worth of loaves, and give to them to eat?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 15:34; Mark 8:5; John 6:9"> And He says to them, “How many loaves do you have? Go and see”; and having known, they say, “Five, and two fishes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:40; 1 Corinthians 14:33; Matthew 15:35"> And He commanded them to make all recline in companies on the green grass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 9:14–15"> and they sat down in squares, by hundreds, and by fifties.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; Mark 7:34; Luke 9:16"> And having taken the five loaves and the two fishes, having looked up to the sky, He blessed, and broke the loaves, and was giving &#91;them&#93; to His disciples, that they may set &#91;them&#93; before them, and the two fishes He divided to all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 9:17; Mark 8:8–9; Matthew 15:37–38"> and they all ate, and were filled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 8:19–20"> and they took up of broken pieces twelve hand-baskets full, and of the fishes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and those eating of the loaves were about five thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 14:22–33; John 6:15–21; Mark 6:32"> And immediately He constrained His disciples to go into the boat, and to go before &#91;Him&#93; to the other side, to Bethsaida, until He may let the multitude away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Mark 1:35; Matthew 6:6; Luke 6:12"> and having taken leave of them, He went away to the mountain to pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 14:23; John 6:16–17"> And evening having come, the boat was in the midst of the sea, and He alone on the land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 24:28; Luke 12:38; Job 9:8"> and He saw them harassed in the rowing, for the wind was against them, and about the fourth watch of the night He comes to them walking on the sea, and wished to pass by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 14:25–26; Luke 24:37; Job 9:8"> And they having seen Him walking on the sea, thought &#91;it&#93; to be an apparition, and cried out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 14:27; John 6:19–20; Isaiah 43:2"> for they all saw Him, and were troubled, and immediately He spoke with them, and says to them, “Take courage! I AM; do not be afraid.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 93:3–4; Mark 4:39; John 6:21"> And He went up to them into the boat, and the wind stilled, and greatly out of measure they were amazed in themselves, and were wondering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Mark 8:17–21; Luke 24:25; Isaiah 63:17"> for they did not understand concerning the loaves, for their heart has been hard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 14:34–36; John 6:24–25; Luke 5:1"> And having passed over, they came on the land of Gennesaret, and drew to the shore,</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Psalms 9:10; Philippians 3:10"> and they having come forth out of the boat, immediately having recognized Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Mark 2:1–3; Mark 3:7–11"> they ran around through all that surrounding region, and they began to carry around on the pallets those being ill, where they were hearing that He is,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 9:20; Luke 6:19; Numbers 15:38–39"> and wherever He was going, to villages, or cities, or fields, in the marketplaces they were laying the ailing, and were calling on Him, that they may touch if it were but the fringe of His garment, and as many as were touching Him were saved.</span>
