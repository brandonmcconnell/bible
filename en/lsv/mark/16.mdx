<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; 1 Corinthians 15:3–7; Luke 24:46"> And he says to them, “Do not be amazed, you seek Jesus the Nazarene, the crucified &#91;One&#93;: He rose—He is not here; behold the place where they laid Him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:28; Matthew 26:32; Matthew 28:10"> And go, say to His disciples and Peter that He goes before you to Galilee; there you will see Him, as He said to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 24:22–24; Luke 24:9–11; Mark 16:5–6"> And having come forth quickly, they fled from the tomb, and trembling and amazement had seized them, and they said to no one anything, for they were afraid. &#91;&#91;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 20:14–19; Luke 8:2; John 20:12"> And He, having risen in the morning of the first of the week, appeared first to Mary the Magdalene, out of whom He had cast seven demons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 20:18; Luke 24:17; John 16:6"> she having gone, told those who had been with Him, mourning and weeping;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 24:11; Mark 16:13–14; Mark 9:19"> and they, having heard that He is alive, and was seen by her, did not believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:13–32; John 21:14; John 21:1"> And after these things, to two of them, as they are going into a field, walking, He appeared in another form,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:25; Luke 24:33–35; John 20:8"> and they having gone, told &#91;it&#93; to the rest; not even them did they believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5; Luke 24:36–43; Hebrews 3:7–8"> Afterward, as they are reclining, He appeared to the Eleven, and reproached their unbelief and stiffness of heart, because they did not believe those having seen Him being raised;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; Mark 13:10; Acts 1:8"> and He said to them, “Having gone into all the world, proclaim the good news to all the creation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 3:36; Romans 10:9–14"> he who has believed and has been immersed will be saved; and he who has not believed will be condemned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:16; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Acts 8:7"> And signs will accompany those believing these things: they will cast out demons in My Name; they will speak with new tongues;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 10:19; James 5:14–15; Acts 28:3–6"> they will take up serpents; and if they may drink any deadly thing, it will not hurt them; they will lay hands on the ailing, and they will be well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; Hebrews 1:3; Hebrews 12:2"> The LORD, then, indeed, after speaking to them, was received up to Heaven, and sat on the right hand of God;</span>
