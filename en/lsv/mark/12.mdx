<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–4; Mark 13:34; Song of Songs 8:11–12"> And He began to speak to them in allegories: “A man planted a vineyard, and put a hedge around, and dug a wine vat, and built a tower, and gave it out to farmers, and went abroad;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:1–8; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 44:4"> and he sent to the farmers at the due time a servant, that from the farmers he may receive from the fruit of the vineyard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15; Nehemiah 9:26; Jeremiah 29:26"> and they, having taken him, severely beat &#91;him&#93;, and sent him away empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And again he sent to them another servant, and having cast stones at that one, they wounded &#91;him&#93; in the head, and sent &#91;him&#93; away—dishonored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:6; Matthew 23:37; Nehemiah 9:30"> And again he sent another, and that one they killed; and many others, some beating, and some killing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 1:1–2; Matthew 3:17; John 1:34"> Having yet therefore one son—his beloved—he also sent him to them last, saying, They will respect my son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Matthew 2:16; Acts 2:23"> and those farmers said among themselves, This is the heir, come, we may kill him, and the inheritance will be ours;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 21:39; Luke 20:15; Matthew 21:33"> and having taken him, they killed, and cast &#91;him&#93; forth outside the vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 10:20–12; Matthew 21:43; Luke 19:27"> What therefore will the lord of the vineyard do? He will come and destroy the farmers, and will give the vineyard to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 28:16"> And this Writing you did not read: A stone that the builders rejected, it became the head of a corner;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 118:23; Acts 3:12–16; Habakkuk 1:5"> this was from the LORD, and it is wonderful in our eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 22:22; John 7:44"> And they were seeking to lay hold on Him, and they feared the multitude, for they knew that He spoke the allegory against them, and having left Him, they went away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Luke 11:54; Mark 3:6"> and they send to Him certain of the Pharisees and of the Herodians, that they may ensnare Him in discourse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 20:22; John 7:18"> and they having come, say to Him, “Teacher, we have known that You are true, and You are not caring for anyone, for You do not look to the face of men, but in truth teach the way of God; is it lawful to give tribute to Caesar or not? May we give, or may we not give?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:2; Revelation 2:23; Mark 10:2"> And He, knowing their hypocrisy, said to them, “Why do you tempt Me? Bring Me a denarius, that I may see”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 20:24–26; Revelation 3:12; Matthew 22:19–22"> and they brought, and He says to them, “Whose &#91;is&#93; this image, and the inscription?” And they said to Him, “Caesar’s”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:7; 1 Peter 2:17; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> and Jesus answering said to them, “Give back the things of Caesar to Caesar, and the things of God to God”; and they wondered at Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 22:23–33; Acts 4:1–2; Acts 23:6–9"> And the Sadducees come to Him, who say there is not a resurrection, and they questioned Him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Ruth 4:5; Ruth 1:11–13"> “Teacher, Moses wrote to us that if anyone’s brother may die, and may leave a wife, and may leave no children, that his brother may take his wife, and raise up seed to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 22:25–28; Luke 20:29–33"> There were then seven brothers, and the first took a wife, and dying, he left no seed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and the second took her, and died, not having left seed, and the third in like manner,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and the seven took her, and left no seed, last of all the woman also died;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> in the resurrection, then, whenever they may rise, of which of them will she be wife—for the seven had her as wife?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 20:9; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Matthew 22:29"> And Jesus answering said to them, “Do you not go astray because of this, not knowing the Writings, nor the power of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:35–36; Matthew 22:30; 1 John 3:2"> For when they may rise out of the dead, they neither marry nor are they given in marriage, but are as messengers who are in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Luke 20:37; Genesis 17:7–8"> And concerning the dead, that they rise: have you not read in the Scroll of Moses (at the bush), how God spoke to him, saying, I &#91;am&#93; the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 14:9; Romans 4:17; Mark 12:24"> He is not the God of dead men, but a God of living men; you then go greatly astray.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–40; Luke 10:25–28; Luke 20:39"> And one of the scribes having come near, having heard them disputing, knowing that He answered them well, questioned Him, “Which is the first command of all?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:4; Luke 10:27; Galatians 3:20"> And Jesus answered him, “The first of all the commands—Hear, O Israel: The LORD is our God, the LORD is one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5"> and you will love the LORD your God out of all your heart, and out of all your soul, and out of all your understanding, and out of all your strength—this &#91;is&#93; the first command;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 13:8–9; Matthew 22:39; Leviticus 19:18"> and the second &#91;is&#93; like &#91;it&#93;, this, You will love your neighbor as yourself—there is no other command greater than these.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Deuteronomy 4:35; Isaiah 46:9"> And the scribe said to Him, “Well, Teacher, in truth You have spoken that there is one God, and there is none other but He;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Micah 6:6–8; 1 Samuel 15:22"> and to love Him out of all the heart, and out of all the understanding, and out of all the soul, and out of all the strength, and to love one’s neighbor as one’s self, is more than all the whole burnt-offerings and the sacrifices.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 20:40; Matthew 22:46"> And Jesus, having seen him that he answered with understanding, said to him, “You are not far from the Kingdom of God”; and no one dared question Him anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Matthew 22:41–45; Matthew 26:55"> And Jesus answering said, teaching in the temple, “How do the scribes say that the Christ is son of David?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Matthew 22:43–45"> For David himself said in the Holy Spirit, The LORD said to my Lord, &#124;&#124; Sit on My right hand, &#124;&#124; Until I place Your enemies—Your footstool;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Matthew 11:25; Matthew 1:23"> therefore David himself calls Him Lord, and from where is He his son?” And the great multitude were hearing Him gladly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–7; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 11:43"> and He was saying to them in His teaching, “Beware of the scribes who will to walk in long robes, and love salutations in the marketplaces,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="James 2:2–3"> and first seats in the synagogues, and first couches at the banquets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Luke 20:47; Matthew 23:33"> who are devouring the widows’ houses, and for a pretense are making long prayers; these will receive more abundant judgment.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 21:1–4; 2 Kings 12:9; John 8:20"> And Jesus having sat down opposite the treasury, was beholding how the multitude puts brass into the treasury, and many rich were putting in much,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> and having come, a poor widow put in two mites, which are a penny.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Acts 11:29"> And having called near His disciples, He says to them, “Truly I say to you that this poor widow has put in more than all those putting into the treasury;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 1 John 3:17; Ezra 2:68–69"> for all, out of their abundance, put in, but she, out of her want, put in all that she had—all her living.”</span>
