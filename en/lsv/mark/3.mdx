<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:9–14; Luke 6:6–11; Mark 1:21"> And He entered again into the synagogue, and there was there a man having the hand withered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:32; Luke 14:1; Luke 6:7"> and they were watching Him, whether on the Sabbaths He will heal him, that they might accuse Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 1:28–30; Isaiah 42:4; Luke 6:8"> And He says to the man having the hand withered, “Rise up in the midst.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Matthew 12:10–12; Hosea 6:6"> And He says to them, “Is it lawful on the Sabbaths to do good, or to do evil? To save life, or to kill?” But they were silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:10; Ephesians 4:18; Romans 11:25"> And having looked around on them with anger, being grieved for the hardness of their heart, He says to the man, “Stretch forth your hand”; and he stretched forth, and his hand was restored whole as the other;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:16; Mark 12:13; Matthew 12:14"> and the Pharisees having gone forth, immediately, with the Herodians, were taking counsel against Him how they might destroy Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 4:25; Luke 6:17; Matthew 12:15"> And Jesus withdrew with His disciples to the sea, and a great multitude from Galilee followed Him, and from Judea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Isaiah 34:5; Matthew 11:21"> and from Jerusalem, and from Idumea and beyond the Jordan; and they around Tyre and Sidon—a great multitude—having heard how He was doing great things, came to Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 5:30; John 6:15"> And He said to His disciples that a little boat may wait on Him, because of the multitude, that they may not press on Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56; Matthew 4:23"> for He healed many, so that they threw themselves on Him, in order to touch Him—as many as had plagues;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 1:23–24; Luke 8:28"> and the unclean spirits, when they were seeing Him, were falling down before Him, and were crying, saying, “You are the Son of God”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:16; Mark 1:25; Mark 1:34"> and many times He was charging them that they might not make Him apparent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Luke 6:12–16; Matthew 10:1–4"> And He goes up to the mountain, and calls near whom He willed, and they went away to Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Luke 9:1–6; John 15:16"> and He appointed twelve, that they may be with Him, and that He may send them forth to preach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and to have power to heal the sicknesses, and to cast out the demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:42; Acts 1:13; Matthew 16:16–18"> And He put on Simon the name Peter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Mark 14:33; Jeremiah 23:29"> and James of Zebedee, and John the brother of James, and He put on them names—Boanerges, that is, “Sons of thunder”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14; Matthew 9:9"> and Andrew, and Philip, and Bartholomew, and Matthew, and Thomas, and James of Alpheus, and Thaddeus, and Simon the Zealot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 6:71; John 13:2; Acts 1:16–25"> and Judas Iscariot, who also delivered Him up; and they come into a house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 6:31; John 4:31–34; Mark 9:28"> And a multitude comes together again, so that they are not even able to eat bread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 26:24; John 10:20; Mark 3:31"> and those alongside Him having heard, went forth to lay hold on Him, for they said that He was beside Himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:1; Matthew 10:25; Matthew 9:34"> and the scribes who &#91;are&#93; from Jerusalem having come down, said, “He has Beelzebul,” and, “By the ruler of the demons He casts out the demons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:17–23; Matthew 12:25–30; Matthew 13:34"> And having called them near, He said to them in allegories, “How is Satan able to cast out Satan?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Isaiah 19:2–3; 2 Samuel 20:6"> And if a kingdom is divided against itself, that kingdom cannot be made to stand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 3:16; Psalms 133:1; Galatians 5:15"> and if a house is divided against itself, that house cannot be made to stand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and if Satan rose against himself, and has been divided, he cannot be made to stand, but has an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–26; Matthew 12:29; Luke 11:21–23"> No one is able to spoil the vessels of the strong man, having entered into his house, if first he may not bind the strong man, and then he will spoil his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; 1 John 5:16"> Truly I say to you that all sins will be forgiven to the sons of men, and slanders with which they might have slandered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 12:31–32; Luke 12:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> but whoever may slander in regard to the Holy Spirit has no forgiveness—throughout the age, but is in danger of continuous judgment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:20; Mark 3:22"> because they said, He has an unclean spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:19–21; Matthew 12:46–50"> Then His brothers and mother come, and standing outside, they sent to Him, calling Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> and a multitude was sitting around Him, and they said to Him, “Behold, Your mother and Your brothers seek You outside.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 3:21; Deuteronomy 33:9; Mark 6:3"> And He answered them, saying, “Who is My mother, or My brothers?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 12:49–50; Luke 11:27–28; Romans 8:29"> And having looked around in a circle to those sitting around Him, He says, “Behold, My mother and My brothers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:17; Matthew 7:21; 1 John 3:22–23"> For whoever may do the will of God, he is My brother, and My sister, and mother.”</span>
