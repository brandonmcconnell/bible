<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 10:40; Mark 4:2; John 11:7"> And having risen from there, He comes to the coasts of Judea, through the other side of the Jordan, and again multitudes come together to Him, and as He had been accustomed, again He was teaching them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Luke 11:53–54; John 8:6"> And the Pharisees, having come near, questioned Him if it is lawful for a husband to put away a wife, tempting Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:20; Galatians 4:21; Luke 10:25"> and He answering said to them, “What did Moses command you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1–4; Matthew 5:31–32; Isaiah 50:1"> And they said, “Moses permitted to write a bill of divorce, and to put away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 19:8; Nehemiah 9:26; Acts 7:51"> And Jesus answering said to them, “For the stiffness of your heart he wrote you this command,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 1:27; Genesis 5:2; Genesis 1:1"> but from the beginning of the creation God made them a male and a female;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:5–6; Ephesians 5:31"> on this account will a man leave his father and mother, and will cleave to his wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:28; Genesis 2:24; 1 Corinthians 6:16"> and they will be—the two—for one flesh, so that they are no longer two, but one flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 7:1–3; 1 Chronicles 7:10–13"> what God therefore joined together, do not let man separate.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 9:33; Mark 9:28; Mark 4:10"> And in the house His disciples again questioned Him of the same thing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:31–32; Matthew 19:9; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11"> and He says to them, “Whoever may put away his wife, and may marry another, commits adultery against her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:11; 1 Corinthians 7:13"> and if a woman may put away her husband, and is married to another, she commits adultery.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 19:13–15; Luke 18:15–17; Exodus 10:9–11"> And they were bringing to Him children that He might touch them, and the disciples were rebuking those bringing them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 19:14; Matthew 18:10; Matthew 18:4"> and Jesus having seen, was much displeased, and He said to them, “Permit the children to come to Me, and do not forbid them, for of such is the Kingdom of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:3; Luke 18:17; John 3:3–6"> truly I say to you, whoever may not receive the Kingdom of God as a child—he may not enter into it”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 9:36; Isaiah 40:11; John 21:15–17"> and having taken them in His arms, having put &#91;His&#93; hands on them, He was blessing them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 19:16–30; Luke 18:18–30; Romans 2:7"> And as He is going forth into the way, one having run and having kneeled to Him, was questioning Him, “Good Teacher, what may I do that I may inherit continuous life?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 18:19; Matthew 19:17; Romans 3:12"> And Jesus said to him, “Why do you call Me good? No one &#91;is&#93; good except one—God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Romans 13:9; James 2:11"> you have known the commands: You may not commit adultery, You may not murder, You may not steal, You may not bear false witness, You may not defraud, Honor your father and mother.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31–32; 2 Timothy 3:5; Philippians 3:6"> And he answering said to Him, “Teacher, all these I have kept from my youth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–21; Revelation 2:4; Luke 18:22"> And Jesus having looked on him, loved him, and said to him, “One thing you lack: go away, whatever you have—sell, and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in Heaven, and come, follow Me, having taken up the cross.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 12:15"> And he—gloomy at the word—went away sorrowing, for he was having many possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:26; Luke 18:24; Mark 3:5"> And Jesus having looked around, says to His disciples, “How hardly will they who have riches enter into the Kingdom of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 5:1–3; Proverbs 11:28; Psalms 52:7"> And the disciples were astonished at His words, and Jesus again answering says to them, “Children, how hard it is to those trusting on riches to enter into the Kingdom of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 19:24–25; Luke 18:25; Matthew 23:24"> It is easier for a camel to enter through the eye of the needle, than for a rich man to enter into the Kingdom of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 16:31; 2 Corinthians 11:23; Romans 10:9–13"> And they were astonished beyond measure, saying to themselves, “And who is able to be saved?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Jeremiah 32:27; Job 42:2"> And Jesus, having looked on them, says, “With men it is impossible, but not with God; for all things are possible with God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 19:27–30; Luke 18:28–30; Luke 14:33"> And Peter began to say to Him, “Behold, we left all, and we followed You.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:23; Matthew 5:10–11; Mark 8:35"> And Jesus answering said, “Truly I say to you, there is no one who left house, or brothers, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or fields, for My sake, and for the good news’,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 18:30; Philippians 3:8; 2 Corinthians 6:10"> who may not receive a hundredfold now in this time, houses, and brothers, and sisters, and mothers, and children, and fields, with persecutions, and in the age that is coming, continuous life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Luke 13:30; Matthew 20:16"> and many first will be last, and the last first.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 20:17–19; Luke 18:31–34; Luke 9:51"> And they were in the way going up to Jerusalem, and Jesus was going before them, and they were amazed, and following they were afraid. And having again taken the Twelve, He began to tell them the things about to happen to Him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Matthew 20:17–19; Mark 8:31"> “Behold, we go up to Jerusalem, and the Son of Man will be delivered to the chief priests, and to the scribes, and they will condemn Him to death, and will deliver Him to the nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Matthew 16:21; Mark 14:65"> and they will mock Him, and scourge Him, and spit on Him, and kill Him, and the third day He will rise again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:20–28; 2 Samuel 14:4–11; 1 Kings 2:20"> And there come near to Him James and John, the sons of Zebedee, saying, “Teacher, we wish that whatever we may ask for ourselves, You may do for us”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 15:7; 1 Kings 3:5–15; Mark 10:51"> and He said to them, “What do you wish Me to do for you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Mark 16:19; Luke 24:26"> And they said to Him, “Grant to us that, one on Your right hand and one on Your left, we may sit in Your glory”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 12:50; Matthew 20:21–22; Psalms 75:8"> and Jesus said to them, “You have not known what you ask; are you able to drink of the cup that I drink of, and with the immersion that I am immersed with—to be immersed?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 12:2; Revelation 1:9; John 17:14"> And they said to Him, “We are able”; and Jesus said to them, “Of the cup indeed that I drink of, you will drink, and with the immersion that I am immersed with, you will be immersed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 20:23; Matthew 25:34; John 17:24"> but to sit on My right and on My left is not Mine to give, but—to those for whom it has been prepared.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 20:24; Proverbs 13:10; Romans 12:10"> And the ten having heard, began to be much displeased at James and John,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; Luke 22:25; Matthew 20:25"> but Jesus having called them near, says to them, “You have known that they who are considered to rule the nations exercise lordship over them, and their great ones exercise authority on them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 23:8–12; Luke 14:11; Mark 9:35"> but not so will it be among you; but whoever may will to become great among you, he will be your minister,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and whoever of you may will to become first, he will be servant of all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Philippians 2:5–8; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> for even the Son of Man did not come to be ministered to, but to minister, and to give His life &#91;as&#93; a ransom for many.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 18:35–1; Matthew 20:29–34; John 9:8"> And they come to Jericho, and as He is going forth from Jericho with His disciples and a great multitude, a son of Timaeus—Bartimaeus the blind—was sitting beside the way begging,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 1:24; Matthew 9:27; Revelation 22:16"> and having heard that it is Jesus the Nazarene, he began to cry out, and to say, “The Son of David—Jesus! Deal kindly with me”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:13; Mark 7:26–29; Hebrews 5:7"> and many were rebuking him that he might keep silent, but the more abundantly he cried out, “Son of David, deal kindly with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 18:40; Matthew 20:32–34; John 11:28"> And Jesus having stood, He commanded him to be called, and they call the blind man, saying to him, “Take courage, rise, He calls you”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–9; Hebrews 12:1"> and he, having cast away his garment, having risen, came to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 18:41–43; Mark 10:36; Matthew 7:7"> And answering, Jesus says to him, “What do you will I may do to you?” And the blind man said to Him, “Rabboni, that I may see again”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 9:22; Acts 26:18; Mark 8:25"> and Jesus said to him, “Go, your faith has saved you”: and immediately he saw again, and was following Jesus in the way.</span>
