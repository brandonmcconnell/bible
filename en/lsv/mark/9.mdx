<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 9:28–36; Isaiah 33:17; Matthew 17:1–8"> And after six days Jesus takes Peter, and James, and John, and brings them up to a high mountain by themselves, alone, and He was transfigured before them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 28:3; Daniel 7:9; Revelation 7:14"> and His garments became glittering, exceedingly white, as snow, so as a launderer on the earth is not able to whiten &#91;them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 9:30–31; Matthew 17:3–4; Acts 3:21–24"> And there appeared to them Elijah with Moses, and they were talking with Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 1:23; John 14:8–9; Revelation 22:3–4"> And Peter answering says to Jesus, “Rabbi, it is good to us to be here; and we may make three shelters, for You one, and for Moses one, and for Elijah one”:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Daniel 10:15–19; Mark 16:5–8"> for he was not knowing what he might say, for they were greatly afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Peter 1:17; Matthew 3:17; Mark 1:11"> And there came a cloud overshadowing them, and there came a voice out of the cloud, saying, “This is My Son, the Beloved, hear Him”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 10:16; Acts 8:39–40; Luke 24:31"> and suddenly, having looked around, they saw no one anymore, but Jesus only with themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 12:40; Matthew 17:9–13; Mark 5:43"> And as they are coming down from the mountain, He charged them that they may declare to no one the things that they saw, except when the Son of Man may rise out of the dead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 17:18; Luke 24:7–8; Luke 18:33–34"> and the thing they kept to themselves, questioning together what the rising out of the dead is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Malachi 3:1; Matthew 11:14"> And they were questioning Him, saying that the scribes say that Elijah must come first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 52:14"> And He answering said to them, “Elijah indeed, having come first, restores all things; and how has it been written concerning the Son of Man, that He may suffer many things, and be set at nothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 14:3–11; Matthew 17:12–13; Matthew 11:14"> But I say to you that also Elijah has come, and they did to him what they willed, as it has been written of him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 9:37–42; Matthew 17:14–20; Luke 11:53–54"> And having come to the disciples, He saw a great multitude around them, and scribes questioning with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:30; Mark 9:2–3"> and immediately, all the multitude having seen Him, were amazed, and running near, were greeting Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 5:30–32; Mark 8:11"> And He questioned the scribes, “What do you dispute with them?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 9:25; Luke 11:14; John 4:47"> And one out of the multitude answering said, “Teacher, I brought my son to You, having a mute spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Mark 11:23; Matthew 17:16"> and wherever it seizes him, it tears him, and he foams, and gnashes his teeth, and pines away; and I spoke to Your disciples that they may cast it out, and they were not able.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; John 20:27; Numbers 14:27"> And He answering him, said, “O generation unbelieving, until when will I be with you? Until when will I suffer you? Bring him to Me”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 1:26; Luke 4:35; John 8:44"> and they brought him to Him, and he having seen Him, immediately the spirit convulsed him, and he, having fallen on the earth, was wallowing—foaming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 9:33; Acts 4:22; Mark 5:25"> And He questioned his father, “How much time is it since this came to him?” And he said, “From childhood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:22–28; Mark 5:19; Matthew 20:34"> and many times it also cast him into fire, and into water, that it might destroy him; but if You are able to do anything, help us, having compassion on us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:40; Hebrews 11:6; Mark 11:23"> And Jesus said to him, “If you are able to believe! All things are possible to the one that is believing”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 17:5; Hebrews 12:2; Ephesians 2:8"> and immediately the father of the child, having cried out with tears, said, “I believe, Lord; be helping my unbelief.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:18; Luke 9:42; Mark 9:15"> Jesus having seen that a multitude runs together, rebuked the unclean spirit, saying to it, “Spirit—mute and deaf—I charge you, come forth out of him, and you may no longer enter into him”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 9:20; Mark 1:26; Mark 9:18"> and having cried, and convulsed him much, it came forth, and he became as dead, so that many said that he was dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Acts 3:7; Mark 1:41"> but Jesus, having taken him by the hand, lifted him up, and he arose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Mark 7:17; Matthew 15:15"> And He having come into the house, His disciples were questioning Him by Himself, “Why were we not able to cast it forth?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 5:15; Daniel 9:3; Matthew 17:20"> And He said to them, “This kind is able to come forth with nothing except with prayer and fasting.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 17:22–23; Luke 9:43–45; Mark 6:31–32"> And having gone forth there, they were passing through Galilee, and He did not wish that any may know,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Mark 9:12; Luke 24:44–46"> for He was teaching His disciples, and He said to them, “The Son of Man is being delivered into the hands of men, and they will kill Him, and having been killed, the third day He will rise,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 2:50; Luke 9:45; Luke 18:34"> but they were not understanding the saying, and they were afraid to question Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 18:1–5; Hebrews 4:13; Luke 9:46–48"> And He came to Capernaum, and being in the house, He was questioning them, “What were you reasoning in the way among yourselves?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 9:50; Matthew 20:21–24; Matthew 18:1–5"> And they were silent, for they reasoned with one another in the way who is greater;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:25–28; Mark 10:42–45; Luke 22:26"> and having sat down He called the Twelve, and He says to them, “If any wills to be first, he will be last of all, and minister of all.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 10:16; Matthew 19:14–15; Matthew 18:2"> And having taken a child, He set him in the midst of them, and having taken him in His arms, said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 18:3–5; Luke 10:16; Matthew 10:40–42"> “Whoever may receive one of such children in My Name, receives Me, and whoever may receive Me, does not receive Me, but Him who sent Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 9:49–50; Numbers 11:26–29; Luke 11:19"> And John answered Him, saying, “Teacher, we saw a certain one casting out demons in Your Name, who does not follow us, and we forbade him, because he does not follow us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:3; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Matthew 13:28–29"> And Jesus said, “Do not forbid him, for there is no one who will do a mighty work in My Name, and will be readily able to speak evil of Me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 12:30; Luke 11:23"> for he who is not against us is for us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; Matthew 25:40; 1 Corinthians 15:23"> for whoever may give you to drink a cup of water in My Name, because you are Christ’s, truly I say to you, he may not lose his reward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 18:6; Luke 17:1–2; Matthew 18:10"> and whoever may cause to stumble one of the little ones believing in Me, better is it for him if a millstone is hanged around his neck, and he has been cast into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 5:29–30; Matthew 18:8–9; Matthew 25:41"> And if your hand may cause you to stumble, cut it off; it is better for you to enter into life maimed, than having the two hands, to go away into Gehenna, into the fire—the unquenchable—</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> &#91;&#91;where their worm is not dying, and the fire is not being quenched.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 18:8; Matthew 5:22; Mark 9:43"> And if your foot may cause you to stumble, cut it off; it is better for you to enter into life lame, than having the two feet to be cast into Gehenna, into the fire—the unquenchable—</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> &#91;&#91;where their worm is not dying, and the fire is not being quenched.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 18:9; Mark 9:43; Matthew 5:22"> And if your eye may cause you to stumble, cast it out; it is better for you to enter into the Kingdom of God one-eyed, than having two eyes, to be cast into the Gehenna of fire—</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Isaiah 66:24; Mark 9:43–45; Matthew 25:41"> where their worm is not dying, and the fire is not being quenched;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Leviticus 2:13; Ezekiel 43:24"> for everyone will be salted with fire, and every sacrifice will be salted with salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Romans 12:18; Matthew 5:13"> The salt &#91;is&#93; good, but if the salt may become saltless, in what will you season &#91;it&#93;? Have in yourselves salt, and have peace in one another.”</span>
