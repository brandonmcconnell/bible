<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 1:14; Ephesians 4:22–24; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> and do not be conformed to this age, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, for your proving what &#91;is&#93; the will of God—the good, and acceptable, and perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 6:3; Philippians 2:3–8; Proverbs 26:12"> For I say, through the grace that was given to me, to everyone who is among you, not to think above what it ought to think; but to think so as to think wisely, as to each God dealt a measure of faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; 1 Corinthians 12:12–14; Ephesians 4:15–16"> for as in one body we have many members, and all the members do not have the same office,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:20; 1 Corinthians 10:17; 1 Corinthians 12:27–28"> so we, the many, are one body in Christ, and members of one another—each one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10–11; 1 Corinthians 7:7; 1 Corinthians 12:28–31"> And having gifts, different according to the grace that was given to us: whether prophecy, according to the proportion of faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11; 2 Timothy 4:2; Acts 13:1"> or ministry, in the ministry; or he who is teaching, in the teaching;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Timothy 5:17; 1 Corinthians 12:28"> or he who is exhorting, in the exhortation; he who is sharing, in simplicity; he who is leading, in diligence; he who is doing kindness, in cheerfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 34:14; 1 Peter 4:8; 1 Timothy 1:5"> The love unhypocritical: abhorring the evil; cleaving to the good;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; John 13:34–35; Hebrews 13:1"> in the love of brothers, to one another kindly affectioned: in the honor going before one another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10–11; Acts 18:25; Revelation 3:15–16"> in the diligence not slothful; in the spirit fervent; serving the LORD;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Hebrews 10:36; Psalms 62:8"> in the hope rejoicing; in the tribulation enduring; in the prayer persevering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 2 Corinthians 9:12; 1 John 3:17"> to the necessities of the holy ones communicating; the hospitality pursuing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Matthew 5:44; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Bless those persecuting you; bless, and do not curse;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Hebrews 13:3; Psalms 35:13–14"> to rejoice with the rejoicing, and to weep with the weeping,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:8; Proverbs 3:7; James 3:13–17"> of the same mind toward one another, not minding the high things, but with the lowly going along; do not become wise in your own conceit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; 1 Thessalonians 5:15; Matthew 5:39"> giving back to no one evil for evil; providing right things before all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; Matthew 5:9; Romans 14:19"> If possible—so far as in you—with all men being in peace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35; Hebrews 10:30; Proverbs 24:29"> not avenging yourselves, beloved, but give place to the wrath, for it has been written: “Vengeance &#91;is&#93; Mine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 25:21–22; Matthew 5:44; Luke 6:27"> I will repay again, says the LORD”; if, then, your enemy hungers, feed him; if he thirsts, give him drink; for doing this, you will heap coals of fire on his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Luke 6:27–30; Proverbs 16:32"> Do not be overcome by the evil, but overcome, in the good, the evil.</span>
