<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:22; 1 Samuel 12:22; Philippians 3:5"> I say then, did God cast away His people? Let it not be! For I am also an Israelite, of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:14; Romans 8:29–30; Exodus 32:1"> God did not cast away His people whom He knew before; have you not known—in Elijah—what the Writing says? How he pleads with God concerning Israel, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10–18; 1 Kings 18:13; Nehemiah 9:26"> “LORD, they killed Your prophets, and they dug down Your altars, and I was left alone, and they seek my life”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 19:18; Numbers 25:3; Zephaniah 1:4"> but what does the divine answer say to him? “I left to Myself seven thousand men who did not bow a knee to Ba‘al.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 9:27; Ephesians 1:5–6; Romans 9:11"> So then also in the present time there has been a remnant according to the &#91;divine&#93; selection of grace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Romans 4:4–5; 2 Timothy 1:9"> and if by grace, no longer of works, otherwise grace becomes no longer grace; and if of works, it is no longer grace, otherwise work is no longer work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Romans 11:5; 2 Corinthians 4:4"> What then? What Israel seeks after, this it did not obtain, and the chosen obtained, and the rest were hardened,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:4; Isaiah 29:10; Ezekiel 12:2"> according as it has been written: “God gave to them a spirit of deep sleep, eyes not to see, and ears not to hear, to this very day,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 69:22–23; Luke 12:20; Psalms 28:4"> and David says, “Let their table become for a snare, and for a trap, and for a stumbling-block, and for a repayment to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 69:23; Deuteronomy 28:64–68; 2 Peter 2:4"> let their eyes be darkened—not to behold, and You always bow down their back.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 13:46–48; Romans 11:31; Romans 10:19"> I say then, did they stumble that they might fall? Let it not be! But by their fall the salvation &#91;is&#93; to the nations, to arouse them to jealousy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:25; Colossians 1:27; Romans 11:15"> and if their fall &#91;is&#93; the riches of &#91;the&#93; world, and their diminishment the riches of nations, how much more their fullness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 2 Timothy 1:11–12; Ephesians 3:8"> For to you I speak—to the nations—inasmuch as I am indeed an apostle of nations, I glorify my ministry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:16; 1 Timothy 4:16; Romans 9:3"> if I will arouse my own flesh to jealousy by any means, and will save some of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 1:10; Romans 5:10–11; Colossians 1:20–21"> for if the casting away of them &#91;is&#93; a reconciliation of the world, what the reception—if not life out of the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:30; James 1:18; Proverbs 3:9"> And if the first-fruit &#91;is&#93; holy, the lump also; and if the root &#91;is&#93; holy, the branches also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 2:11–13; Ephesians 3:6; Matthew 21:43"> And if certain of the branches were broken off, and you, being a wild olive tree, were grafted in among them, and became a fellow-partaker of the root and of the fatness of the olive tree—</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:12; Romans 11:20; Ephesians 2:19–20"> do not boast against the branches; and if you boast, you do not bear the root, but the root you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 11:17; Romans 11:11–12; Romans 11:23–24"> You will say, then, “The branches were broken off, that I might be grafted in”; right!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:16; 2 Corinthians 1:24; 1 Corinthians 10:12"> By unbelief they were broken off, and you have stood by faith; do not be high-minded, but be fearing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4–9; Jeremiah 25:29; Jeremiah 49:12"> for if God did not spare the natural branches—lest perhaps He also will not spare you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 3:6; 1 Corinthians 15:2"> Behold, then, goodness and severity of God—on those indeed who fell, severity; and on you, goodness, if you may remain in the goodness, otherwise, you also will be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:16; Zechariah 12:10; Matthew 23:39"> And those also, if they may not remain in unbelief, will be grafted in, for God is able to graft them in again;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:17–18; Romans 11:30"> for if you, out of the olive tree, wild by nature, were cut out, and contrary to nature, were grafted into a good olive tree, how much rather will they, who &#91;are&#93; according to nature, be grafted into their own olive tree?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 21:24; 2 Corinthians 3:14–16; Romans 16:25"> For I do not wish you to be ignorant, brothers, of this secret—that you may not be wise in your own conceits—that hardness in part to Israel has happened until the fullness of the nations may come in;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 59:20; Jeremiah 31:31–37; Psalms 14:7"> and so all Israel will be saved, according as it has been written: “There will come forth out of Zion He who is delivering, and He will turn away impiety from Jacob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Isaiah 59:21; Ezekiel 36:25–29"> and this to them &#91;is&#93; the covenant from Me when I may take away their sins.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:15; Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Matthew 21:43"> As regards, indeed, the good tidings, &#91;they are&#93; enemies on your account; and as regards the &#91;divine&#93; selection—beloved on account of the fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 7:21; Numbers 23:19; Malachi 3:6"> for the gifts and the calling of God are irrevocable;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 3:7; Ephesians 2:1–2; 1 Corinthians 7:25"> for as you also once did not believe in God, and now found kindness by the unbelief of these,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 11:15; Romans 10:16; Romans 11:25"> so also these now did not believe, that in your kindness they also may find kindness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 3:9; 1 Timothy 2:4–6"> for God shut up together the whole to unbelief, that to the whole He might do kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 5:9; Job 11:7–9; Psalms 92:5"> O depth of riches, and wisdom and knowledge of God! How unsearchable His judgments, and untraceable His ways!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Job 15:8"> For who knew the mind of the LORD? Or who became His counselor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 41:11; Job 35:7; 1 Corinthians 4:7"> Or who first gave to Him, and it will be given back to him again?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 2:10"> Because of Him, and through Him, and to Him &#91;are&#93; all things; to Him &#91;is&#93; the glory—for all ages. Amen.</span>
