<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 32:17; Romans 15:13; John 16:33"> Having been declared righteous, then, by faith, we have peace toward God through our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Ephesians 3:12; Romans 12:12"> through whom also we have the access by faith into this grace in which we have stood, and we boast on the hope of the glory of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 1:2–3; James 1:12; 1 Peter 3:14"> And not only &#91;so&#93;, but we also boast in the tribulations, knowing that the tribulation works endurance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; James 1:12; 2 Corinthians 4:8–12"> and the endurance, experience; and the experience, hope;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 4:6; Ephesians 3:16–19; Philippians 1:20"> and the hope does not make ashamed, because the love of God has been poured forth in our hearts through the Holy Spirit that has been given to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–5; Romans 5:8; Colossians 2:13"> For in our being still ailing, Christ in due time died for the impious;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:13; 1 John 3:16; Romans 16:4"> for scarcely for a righteous man will anyone die, for the good man perhaps someone also dares to die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 15:13; John 3:16; Romans 5:6"> and God commends His own love to us, that, in our being still sinners, Christ died for us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:10; Romans 8:1; Ephesians 2:13"> much more, then, having been declared righteous now in His blood, we will be saved through Him from the wrath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:18–19; 2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:32"> for if, being enemies, we have been reconciled to God through the death of His Son, much more, having been reconciled, we will be saved in His life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:18–19; Romans 5:10; Isaiah 61:10"> And not only &#91;so&#93;, but we are also boasting in God, through our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom we now received the reconciliation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:21–22; Romans 5:19; Romans 6:23"> because of this, even as through one man sin entered into the world, and through sin—death; and thus to all men death passed through, for that all sinned;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 4:15; 1 Corinthians 15:56; 1 John 3:4"> for until law sin was in the world: and sin is not reckoned when there is not law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:45; Romans 5:17; Hebrews 9:27"> but death reigned from Adam until Moses, even on those having not sinned in the likeness of Adam’s transgression, who is a type of Him who is coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 6:23; Ephesians 2:8; Romans 5:12"> But not as the offense so also &#91;is&#93; the free gift; for if by the offense of the one the many died, much more the grace of God, and the free gift in grace of the one man Jesus Christ, abound to the many;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 13:38–39; Isaiah 1:18; Isaiah 44:22"> and not as through one who sinned &#91;is&#93; the free gift, for the judgment indeed &#91;is&#93; of one to condemnation, but the gift &#91;is&#93; of many offenses to a declaration of “Righteous,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:21–22; 1 Corinthians 15:49; Romans 5:12"> for if by the offense of the one death reigned through the one, much more those who are receiving the abundance of grace and of the free gift of righteousness, in life will reign through the one—Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:22; Romans 5:12; Romans 5:15"> So, then, as through one offense to all men &#91;it is&#93; to condemnation, so also through one declaration of “Righteous” &#91;it is&#93; to all men to justification of life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 5:18; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Philippians 2:8"> for as through the disobedience of the one man, the many were constituted sinners: so also through the obedience of the One, will the many be constituted righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 6:1; Titus 3:3–7; Romans 6:14"> And law came in, that the offense might abound, and where sin abounded, grace hyper-abounded,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Titus 2:11; Romans 5:14; Romans 8:10"> that even as sin reigned in death, so also grace may reign, through righteousness, to continuous life, through Jesus Christ our Lord.</span>
