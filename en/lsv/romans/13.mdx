<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 3:1; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Daniel 2:21"> Let every soul be subject to the higher authorities, for there is no authority except from God, and the authorities existing are appointed by God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:13; Titus 3:1; Romans 13:5"> so that he who is setting himself against the authority, has resisted against God’s ordinance; and those resisting will receive judgment to themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:4–6; 1 Peter 2:13–14; Proverbs 20:2"> For those ruling are not a terror to the good works, but to the evil; and do you wish to not be afraid of the authority? Be doing that which is good, and you will have praise from it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 12:19; 2 Chronicles 19:6; Proverbs 16:14"> for it is a servant of God to you for good; and if you may do that which is evil, be fearing, for it does not bear the sword in vain; for it is a servant of God, an avenger for wrath to him who is doing that which is evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:2; Acts 24:16; 1 Peter 2:19"> For this reason it is necessary to be subject, not only because of the wrath, but also because of the conscience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:17–21; Mark 12:14–17; Matthew 17:24–27"> for because of this you also pay tribute; for they are servants of God, on this very thing attending continually;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; 1 Peter 2:17–18; Luke 20:25"> render, therefore, to all &#91;their&#93; dues; to whom tribute, the tribute; to whom custom, the custom; to whom fear, the fear; to whom honor, the honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:10; Matthew 22:39–40; Galatians 5:14"> To no one owe anything, except to love one another; for he who is loving the other—he has fulfilled law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Matthew 22:39; Luke 10:27"> for, “You will not commit adultery, You will not murder, You will not steal, You will not bear false testimony, You will not covet”; and if there is any other command, in this word it is summed up, in this: “You will love your neighbor as yourself”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:39–40; Romans 13:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> the love to the neighbor works no ill; the love, therefore, &#91;is&#93; the fullness of law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Peter 4:7; Ephesians 5:14"> And this, knowing the time, that for us, the hour already &#91;is&#93; to be aroused out of sleep, for now our salvation &#91;is&#93; nearer than when we believed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:31–32; Ephesians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 6:7"> the night advanced, and the day came near; let us lay aside, therefore, the works of the darkness, and let us put on the armor of the light;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:21; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–5"> as in daytime, let us walk properly; not in reveling and drunkenness, not in promiscuity and licentiousness, not in strife and jealousy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Galatians 5:16–17; Galatians 3:27"> but put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and take no forethought for the flesh—for desires.</span>
