<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 18:18; 1 Peter 1:22–23; 1 Timothy 5:9–10"> And I commend you to Phoebe our sister—being a servant of the assembly that &#91;is&#93; in Cenchrea—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:29; Philippians 1:27; Matthew 10:40–42"> that you may receive her in the LORD, worthily of the holy ones, and may assist her in whatever matter she may have need of you, for she also became a leader of many, and of myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:19; 2 Timothy 4:19; Acts 18:26"> Greet Priscilla and Aquilas, my fellow-workmen in Christ Jesus—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 2:30; 1 John 3:16; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> who laid down their own neck for my life, to whom not only I give thanks, but also all the assemblies of the nations—</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 4:15; Philemon 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:19"> and the assembly at their house; greet Epaenetus, my beloved, who is first-fruit of Achaia to Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 16:12; Matthew 27:55; 1 Timothy 5:10"> Greet Mary, who labored much for us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 16:11; Romans 16:21; Philemon 1:23"> greet Andronicus and Junias, my relatives, and my fellow-captives, who are of note among the apostles, who also have been in Christ before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:1; 1 John 3:14; Romans 16:5"> Greet Amplias, my beloved in the LORD;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 16:2–3; Romans 16:21"> greet Arbanus, our fellow-workman in Christ, and Stachys, my beloved;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:18; 1 Peter 1:7; Deuteronomy 8:2"> greet Apelles, the approved in Christ; greet those of the &#91;household&#93; of Aristobulus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 16:21; Romans 16:7"> greet Herodion, my relative; greet those of the &#91;household&#93; of Narcissus, who are in the LORD;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Hebrews 6:10–11; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> greet Tryphaena, and Tryphosa, who are laboring in the LORD; greet Persis, the beloved, who labored much in the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:21; 1 Timothy 5:2; 2 John 1:1"> Greet Rufus, the chosen one in the LORD, and his mother and mine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; Romans 8:29; Hebrews 3:1"> greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermas, Patrobas, Hermes, and the brothers with them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:2; Romans 1:7; Romans 16:14"> greet Philologus, and Julias, Nereus, and his sister, and Olympas, and all the holy ones with them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:26; 1 Corinthians 16:20; 2 Corinthians 13:12"> greet one another in a holy kiss; the assemblies of Christ greet you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jude 1:19; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Timothy 6:3–5"> And I call on you, brothers, to mark those who are causing the divisions and the stumbling-blocks, contrary to the teaching that you learned, and turn away from them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 2:4; Matthew 7:15; 1 Timothy 6:5"> for such do not serve our Lord Jesus Christ, but their own belly; and through the good word and fair speech they deceive the hearts of the harmless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; 1 Corinthians 14:20; Philippians 2:15"> For your obedience reached to all; I rejoice, therefore, as regards you, and I wish you to be wise, indeed, as to the good, and pure as to the evil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:19; 1 John 3:8; Genesis 3:15"> and the God of peace will bruise Satan under your feet quickly; the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ &#91;be&#93; with you. Amen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Acts 17:5; Acts 13:1"> Timotheus greets you, my fellow-workman, and Lucius, and Jason, and Sosipater, my relatives;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Galatians 6:11; 1 Corinthians 16:21; Romans 16:8"> I, Tertius, greet you (who wrote the letter) in the LORD;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:14; Acts 19:22; 2 Timothy 4:20"> Gaius greets you, my host, and of the whole Assembly; Erastus greets you, the steward of the city, and Quartus the brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> &#91;&#91;The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ &#91;be&#93; with you all! Amen.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:7; Romans 2:16; Colossians 1:26–27"> And to Him who is able to establish you, according to my good news, and the preaching of Jesus Christ, according to the revelation of the secret, having been kept secret in the times of the ages,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 1:5; Galatians 3:8; Romans 15:18"> and now having been revealed, also, through prophetic writings, according to a command of the perpetual God, having been made known to all the nations for obedience of faith—</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Romans 11:36; Revelation 7:10–12"> to the only wise God, through Jesus Christ, to Him &#91;be&#93; glory for all ages. Amen.</span>
