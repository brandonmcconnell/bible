<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; Romans 14:1; Galatians 6:1–2"> And we ought—we who are strong—to bear the weaknesses of the powerless, and not to please ourselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 14:19; 1 Corinthians 10:24; Philippians 2:4–5"> for let each one of us please the neighbor for good, for edification,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:9; Philippians 2:8; John 4:34"> for even the Christ did not please Himself, but according as it has been written: “The reproaches of those reproaching You fell on Me”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Romans 4:23–24"> for as many things as were written before, for our instruction were written before, that through the endurance, and the exhortation of the Writings, we might have the hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 2:4–5; 1 Peter 3:8; Romans 12:16"> And may the God of the endurance, and of the exhortation, give to you to have the same mind toward one another, according to Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 10:29–30; Revelation 1:6; Ephesians 1:3"> that with one accord—with one mouth—you may glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:34; Romans 5:2"> for this reason receive one another, according as also the Christ received us, to the glory of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Matthew 15:24; John 1:11"> And I say Jesus Christ to have become a servant of circumcision for the truth of God, to confirm the promises to the fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:50; Psalms 18:49; Romans 3:29"> and the nations for kindness to glorify God, according as it has been written: “Because of this I will confess to You among nations, and to Your Name I will sing praise”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 67:3–4; Deuteronomy 32:43; Isaiah 42:10–12"> and again it says, “Rejoice you nations, with His people”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 117:1"> and again, “Praise the LORD, all you nations; and laud Him, all you peoples”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 11:10; Isaiah 11:1; Matthew 12:21"> and again, Isaiah says, “There will be the root of Jesse, and He who is rising to rule nations—on Him will nations hope”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:16–17; Romans 12:12; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> and the God of the hope will fill you with all joy and peace in the believing, for your abounding in the hope in power of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 2 Peter 1:12; 1 John 2:21"> And I am persuaded, my brothers—I myself also—concerning you, that you yourselves also are full of goodness, having been filled with all knowledge, also able to admonish one another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 15:10; Romans 1:5"> and the more boldly I wrote to you, brothers, in part, as putting you in mind, because of the grace that is given to me by God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:13; Romans 1:1; Acts 9:15"> for my being a servant of Jesus Christ to the nations, acting as priest in the good news of God, that the offering up of the nations may become acceptable, sanctified by the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14–16; 2 Corinthians 12:11–21; Hebrews 5:1"> I have, then, a boasting in Christ Jesus, in the things pertaining to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 15:12; Romans 1:5; Acts 21:19"> for I will not dare to speak anything of the things that Christ did not work through me, to obedience of nations, by word and deed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:12; Hebrews 2:4; Acts 19:11–12"> in power of signs and wonders, in power of the Spirit of God; so that I, from Jerusalem, and in a circle as far as Illyricum, have fully preached the good news of the Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:13–16; 1 Corinthians 3:9–15; Ephesians 2:20–22"> and so counting it honor to proclaim good news, not where Christ was named—that on another’s foundation I might not build—</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 52:15; Isaiah 65:1"> but according as it has been written: “To whom it was not told concerning Him, they will see; and they who have not heard, will understand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 1:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:17–18"> For this reason, also, I was hindered many times from coming to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 15:32; Romans 1:10–12; Romans 15:29"> and now, no longer having place in these parts, and having a longing to come to you for many years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:28; Romans 1:12; Acts 15:3"> when I may go on to Spain I will come to you, for I hope in going through, to see you, and by you to be set forward there, if of you first, in part, I will be filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Acts 19:21; Acts 20:22"> And now, I go on to Jerusalem, ministering to the holy ones;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:1–9; Acts 11:27–30; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> for it pleased Macedonia and Achaia well to make a certain contribution for the poor of the holy ones who &#91;are&#93; in Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:11; Galatians 6:6; Philemon 1:19"> for it pleased well, and their debtors they are, for if the nations participated in their spiritual things, they ought also, in the fleshly things, to minister to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 15:24; Philippians 4:17; Colossians 1:6"> This, then, having finished, and having sealed to them this fruit, I will return through you, to Spain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 1:10–12; Psalms 16:11"> and I have known that coming to you—in the fullness of the blessing of the good news of Christ I will come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 1:11; 2 Corinthians 4:11"> And I call on you, brothers, through our Lord Jesus Christ, and through the love of the Spirit, to strive together with me in the prayers for me to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:2; 2 Corinthians 8:4; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> that I may be delivered from those not believing in Judea, and that my ministry, that &#91;is&#93; for Jerusalem, may become acceptable to the holy ones;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:18; Philemon 1:20; Philemon 1:7"> that in joy I may come to you, through the will of God, and may be refreshed with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; Philippians 4:9; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> and the God of peace &#91;be&#93; with you all. Amen.</span>
