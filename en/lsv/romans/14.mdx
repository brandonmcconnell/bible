<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:1; 1 Corinthians 8:7–13; 1 Corinthians 9:22"> And receive him who is weak in the faith—not to determinations of reasonings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; Romans 14:22–23; Romans 14:14"> one believes that he may eat all things—and he who is weak eats herbs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:10; Colossians 2:16–17; Luke 18:9"> do not let him who is eating despise him who is not eating: and do not let him who is not eating judge him who is eating, for God received him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 4:11–12; Romans 9:20; Romans 8:31–39"> You—who are you that are judging another’s domestic &#91;affairs&#93;? To his own master he stands or falls; and he will be made to stand, for God is able to make him stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:23; Galatians 4:9–10"> One judges one day above another, and another judges every day &#91;alike&#93;; let each be fully assured in his own mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:30–31; 1 Timothy 4:3–5; Matthew 14:19"> He who is regarding the day, he regards &#91;it&#93; to the LORD, and he who is not regarding the day, he does not regard &#91;it&#93; to the LORD. He who is eating, he eats to the LORD, for he gives thanks to God; and he who is not eating, he does not eat to the LORD, and gives thanks to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; 2 Corinthians 5:15; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20"> For none of us lives to himself, and none dies to himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:20; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 15:23"> for both, if we may live, we live to the LORD; if also we may die, we die to the LORD; both then if we may live, also if we may die, we are the LORD’s;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; Luke 24:26; Hebrews 12:2"> for because of this Christ both died and rose again, and lived again, that He may be Lord both of dead and of living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> And you, why do you judge your brother? Or again, you, why do you set at nothing your brother? For we will all stand at the judgment seat of the Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:22–25; Philippians 2:10–11; Psalms 72:11"> for it has been written: “I live! Says the LORD—Every knee will bow to Me, and every tongue will confess to God”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:5; Matthew 12:36; Matthew 16:27"> so, then, each of us will give reckoning to God concerning himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; James 4:11; 1 Corinthians 8:9–13"> therefore, may we judge one another no longer, but rather judge this, not to put a stumbling-stone before the brother, or an offense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Romans 14:2; 1 Timothy 4:4"> I have known, and am persuaded, in the Lord Jesus, that nothing &#91;is&#93; unclean of itself, except to him who is reckoning anything to be unclean—to that one &#91;it is&#93; unclean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:11–12; Philippians 2:2–4; Galatians 5:13"> and if your brother is grieved through food, you no longer walk according to love; do not destroy with your food that one for whom Christ died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:22; 1 Corinthians 10:29–30"> Do not let, then, evil be spoken of your good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Romans 15:13; 1 Corinthians 4:20"> for the Kingdom of God is not eating and drinking, but righteousness, and peace, and joy in the Holy Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Corinthians 5:11; 1 Peter 2:20"> for he who in these things is serving the Christ, &#91;is&#93; acceptable to God and approved of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Romans 12:18; Psalms 34:14"> So, then, may we pursue the things of peace, and the things of building up one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 14:14–15; Matthew 18:6; Titus 1:15"> Do not cast down the work of God for the sake of food; all things, indeed, &#91;are&#93; pure, but evil &#91;is&#93; to the man who is eating through stumbling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:13; Romans 14:13; Romans 15:1–2"> &#91;It is&#93; not right to eat flesh, nor to drink wine, nor to &#91;do anything&#93; in which your brother stumbles, or is made to fall, or is weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 3:21; Romans 14:5; James 3:13"> You have faith! Have &#91;it&#93; to yourself before God; blessed is he who is not judging himself in what he approves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:6; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 11:29–31"> and he who is making a difference, if he may eat, has been condemned, because &#91;it is&#93; not of faith; and all that &#91;is&#93; not of faith is sin.</span>
