<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:20–22; Romans 9:1–3; John 5:34"> Brothers, the pleasure indeed of my heart, and my supplication that &#91;is&#93; to God for Israel, is—for salvation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 21:20; Romans 9:31–32; Proverbs 19:2"> for I bear them testimony that they have a zeal of God, but not according to knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Luke 16:15; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> for not knowing the righteousness of God, and seeking to establish their own righteousness, they did not submit to the righteousness of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 3:24; Romans 8:3–4; Matthew 5:17–18"> For Christ is an end of law for righteousness to everyone who is believing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Nehemiah 9:29"> for Moses describes the righteousness that &#91;is&#93; of the Law, that, “The man who did them will live in them,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:11–14; Proverbs 30:4; Ephesians 4:8–10"> and the righteousness of faith thus speaks: “You may not say in your heart, Who will go up to Heaven?” (that is, to bring Christ down)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Revelation 1:18; Romans 4:25"> or, “Who will go down to the abyss?” (that is, to bring up Christ out of the dead).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:14; Romans 1:16–17; 1 Timothy 4:6"> But what does it say? “The saying is near you—in your mouth, and in your heart”: that is, the saying of the faith that we preach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 12:8; Acts 16:31; Philippians 2:11"> that if you may confess with your mouth that Jesus &#91;is&#93; LORD, and may believe in your heart that God raised Him out of the dead, you will be saved,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Romans 10:9; Galatians 2:16"> for with the heart &#91;one&#93; believes to righteousness, and with the mouth is confession made to salvation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Romans 9:33; 1 Peter 2:6"> for the Writing says, “Everyone who is believing on Him will not be ashamed,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; Revelation 17:14; Galatians 3:28"> for there is no difference between Jew and Greek, for the same Lord of all &#91;is&#93; rich to all those calling on Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:21; Joel 2:32"> for “Everyone who, if he may have called on the Name of the LORD, will be saved.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 26:17–18; Titus 1:3"> How then will they call on &#91;Him&#93; in whom they did not believe? And how will they believe &#91;on Him&#93; of whom they did not hear? And how will they hear apart from one preaching?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Nahum 1:15; Ephesians 2:17"> And how will they preach, if they may not be sent? According as it has been written: “How beautiful the feet of those proclaiming good tidings of peace, of those proclaiming good tidings of the good things!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 3:3; John 12:38–40"> But they were not all obedient to the good tidings, for Isaiah says, “LORD, who gave credence to our report?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 3:2; Galatians 3:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:13"> So then faith &#91;is&#93; by a report, and the report through a saying of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 19:4; Romans 1:8; Matthew 24:14"> but I say, did they not hear? Yes, indeed, “their voice went forth to all the earth, and their sayings to the ends of the habitable world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Romans 11:11; Romans 11:14"> But I say, did Israel not know? First Moses says, “I will provoke you to jealousy by &#91;that which is&#93; not a nation, &#124;&#124; By an unintelligent nation I will anger you,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:1–2; Romans 9:30; Isaiah 55:4–5"> and Isaiah is very bold and says, “I was found by those not seeking Me; I became visible to those not inquiring after Me”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 65:2–5; 1 Peter 2:8; Jeremiah 35:15"> and to Israel He says, “All the day I stretched out My hands to a people unbelieving and contradicting.”</span>
