<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:1; Zechariah 7:1; Esther 1:2"> Words of Nehemiah son of Hachaliah. And it comes to pass, in the month of Chisleu, the twentieth year, and I have been in Shushan the palace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:2; Ezekiel 6:9; Jeremiah 44:14"> and Hanani, one of my brothers, he and men of Judah come in, and I ask them concerning the Jews, the escaped part that have been left of the captivity, and concerning Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:17; 2 Kings 25:10; Nehemiah 7:6"> and they say to me, “Those left, who have been left of the captivity there in the province, &#91;are&#93; in great evil, and in reproach, and the wall of Jerusalem is broken down, and its gates have been burned with fire.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 9:3; Psalms 137:1; Ezra 10:1"> And it comes to pass, at my hearing these words, I have sat down, and I weep and mourn &#91;for&#93; days, and I am fasting and praying before the God of the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:6; Daniel 9:4–19; Deuteronomy 7:21"> And I say, “Ah, now, O YHWH, God of the heavens, God, the great and the fearful, keeping the covenant and kindness for those loving Him, and for those keeping His commands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 9:20; Daniel 9:4; Psalms 34:15"> please let Your ear be attentive and Your eyes open, to listen to the prayer of Your servant, that I am praying before You today, by day and by night, concerning the sons of Israel, Your servants, and confessing concerning the sins of the sons of Israel, that we have sinned against You; indeed, I and the house of my father have sinned;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 9:5–6; Daniel 9:11; Daniel 9:13"> we have acted very corruptly against You, and have not kept the commands, and the statutes, and the judgments, that You commanded Your servant Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Deuteronomy 4:25–27; Leviticus 26:33–46"> Please remember the word that You commanded Your servant Moses, saying, You trespass—I scatter you among peoples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:11–14; Deuteronomy 30:2–5; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> and you have turned back to Me, and kept My commands, and done them—if your outcast is in the end of the heavens, there I gather them, and have brought them to the place that I have chosen to cause My Name to dwell there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:29; Daniel 9:15–27; Exodus 32:11"> And they &#91;are&#93; Your servants, and Your people, whom You have ransomed by Your great power, and by Your strong hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:6; Nehemiah 2:8; Proverbs 21:1"> Ah, now, O Lord, please let Your ear be attentive to the prayer of Your servant, and to the prayer of Your servants, those delighting to fear Your Name; and please give prosperity to Your servant today, and give him for mercies before this man”; and I have been butler to the king.</span>
