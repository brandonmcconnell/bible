<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 13:2–3; Ezra 8:23; Nehemiah 8:2"> And in the twenty-fourth day of this month the sons of Israel have been gathered with fasting, and with sackcloth, and earth on them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 9:3–10; Nehemiah 13:3; Ezra 10:11"> and the seed of Israel are separated from all sons of a stranger, and stand and confess concerning their sins, and the iniquities of their fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:7–8; Nehemiah 8:3–4"> and rise up on their station, and read in the Scroll of the Law of their God YHWH a fourth of the day, and a fourth they are confessing and bowing themselves to their God YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:7; Nehemiah 12:8; Nehemiah 9:5"> And there stand up on the ascent, of the Levites, Jeshua, and Bani, Kadmiel, Shebaniah, Bunni, Sherebiah, Bani, Chenani, and they cry with a loud voice to their God YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 103:1–2; 1 Peter 1:3; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> And the Levites say, &#91;even&#93; Jeshua, and Kadmiel, Bani, Hashabniah, Sherebiah, Hodijah, Shebaniah, Pethahiah, “Rise, bless your God YHWH, &#124;&#124; From age to age, &#124;&#124; And they bless the Name of Your glory &#124;&#124; That &#91;is&#93; exalted above all blessing and praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:16; Genesis 1:1; Revelation 4:11"> You &#91;are&#93; He, O YHWH, Yourself—You have made the heavens, &#124;&#124; The heavens of the heavens, and all their host, &#124;&#124; The earth and all that &#91;are&#93; on it, &#124;&#124; The seas and all that &#91;are&#93; in them, &#124;&#124; And You are keeping all of them alive, &#124;&#124; And the host of the heavens are bowing themselves to You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 11:31; Genesis 17:5; Isaiah 51:2"> You &#91;are&#93; He, O YHWH God, &#124;&#124; Who fixed on Abraham, &#124;&#124; And brought him out from Ur of the Chaldeans, &#124;&#124; And made his name Abraham,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Genesis 15:18–21; Hebrews 6:18"> And found his heart steadfast before You, &#124;&#124; So as to make with him the covenant, &#124;&#124; To give the land of the Canaanite, &#124;&#124; The Hittite, the Amorite, and the Perizzite, and the Jebusite, and the Girgashite, &#124;&#124; To give &#91;it&#93; to his seed. And You establish Your words, &#124;&#124; For You &#91;are&#93; righteous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 3:7–9; Acts 7:34; Exodus 3:16"> And see the affliction of our fathers in Egypt, &#124;&#124; And their cry have heard by the Sea of Suph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:20; Daniel 9:15; Exodus 18:11"> And give signs and wonders on Pharaoh, &#124;&#124; And on all his servants, &#124;&#124; And on all the people of his land, &#124;&#124; For You have known that they have acted proudly against them, &#124;&#124; And You make to Yourself a name as &#91;at&#93; this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 78:13; Exodus 14:21–22; Exodus 14:27–28"> And You have cleaved the sea before them, &#124;&#124; And they pass over into the midst of the sea on the dry land, &#124;&#124; And their pursuers You have cast into the depths, &#124;&#124; As a stone, into the strong waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 13:21–22; Nehemiah 9:19; Psalms 107:7"> And by a pillar of cloud You have led them by day, &#124;&#124; And by a pillar of fire by night, &#124;&#124; To lighten to them the way in which they go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 119:160; Exodus 20:1; Psalms 119:127–128"> And on Mount Sinai You have come down, &#124;&#124; Even to speak with them from the heavens, &#124;&#124; And You give to them right judgments, &#124;&#124; And true laws, good statutes and commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 20:8–11; Genesis 2:3; Ezekiel 20:12"> And Your holy Sabbath You have made known to them, &#124;&#124; And commands, and statutes, and law, &#124;&#124; You have commanded for them, &#124;&#124; By the hand of Your servant Moses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3–4; Deuteronomy 1:8; Exodus 17:6"> And bread from the heavens You have given to them for their hunger, &#124;&#124; And water from a rock have brought out to them for their thirst, &#124;&#124; And say to them to go in to possess the land &#124;&#124; That You have lifted up Your hand to give to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:29; Psalms 81:11–14; Hebrews 3:15"> And they and our fathers have acted proudly, &#124;&#124; And harden their neck, &#124;&#124; And have not listened to Your commands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 86:5; Joel 2:13; Psalms 145:8–9"> Indeed, they refuse to listen, &#124;&#124; And have not remembered Your wonders that You have done with them, &#124;&#124; And harden their neck and appoint a head, &#124;&#124; To turn back to their service, in their rebellion; And You &#91;are&#93; a God of pardons, &#124;&#124; Gracious, and merciful, long-suffering, and abundant in kindness, &#124;&#124; And have not forsaken them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 106:19–23; Exodus 32:4–8; Ezekiel 20:7–44"> Also, when they have made to themselves a molten calf, &#124;&#124; And say, This &#91;is&#93; your god &#124;&#124; That brought you up out of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And do great despisings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Nehemiah 9:27; Malachi 3:6"> and You, in Your abundant mercies, &#124;&#124; Have not forsaken them in the wilderness—The pillar of the cloud has not turned aside from off them by day, &#124;&#124; To lead them in the way, &#124;&#124; And the pillar of the fire by night, &#124;&#124; To give light to them and the way in which they go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 143:10; Numbers 11:17; Isaiah 63:11–14"> And Your good Spirit You have given, &#124;&#124; To cause them to act wisely; And Your manna You have not withheld from their mouth, &#124;&#124; And water You have given to them for their thirst,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:7; Deuteronomy 8:4; Psalms 34:10"> And forty years You have nourished them in a wilderness; They have not lacked; Their garments have not worn out, &#124;&#124; And their feet have not swelled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Deuteronomy 3:1–17; Deuteronomy 2:26–36"> And You give to them kingdoms, and peoples, &#124;&#124; And apportion them to the corner, &#124;&#124; And they possess the land of Sihon, &#124;&#124; And the land of the king of Heshbon, &#124;&#124; And the land of Og king of Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 15:5; 1 Chronicles 27:23"> And You have multiplied their sons as the stars of the heavens, &#124;&#124; And bring them into the land &#124;&#124; That You have said to their fathers to go in to possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 21:43; Joshua 11:23; Psalms 44:2–3"> And the sons come in, and possess the land, &#124;&#124; And You humble the inhabitants of the land—the Canaanites—before them, &#124;&#124; And give them into their hand, and their kings, &#124;&#124; And the peoples of the land, &#124;&#124; To do with them according to their pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Nehemiah 9:35; Deuteronomy 3:5"> And they capture fortified cities, and fat ground, &#124;&#124; And possess houses full of all good, &#124;&#124; Dug-wells, vineyards, and olive-yards, &#124;&#124; And fruit-trees in abundance, &#124;&#124; And they eat, and are satisfied, &#124;&#124; And become fat, &#124;&#124; And delight themselves in Your great goodness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 14:9; Judges 2:11–12; Nehemiah 9:18"> And they are disobedient, &#124;&#124; And rebel against You, &#124;&#124; And cast Your law behind their back, &#124;&#124; And Your prophets they have slain, &#124;&#124; Who testified against them, &#124;&#124; To bring them back to You, &#124;&#124; And they do great despisings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 106:41–45; 2 Kings 14:27; 2 Kings 13:5"> And You give them into the hand of their adversaries, &#124;&#124; And they distress them, &#124;&#124; And in the time of their distress they cry to You, &#124;&#124; And You, from the heavens, hear, &#124;&#124; And according to Your abundant mercies, &#124;&#124; Give saviors to them, &#124;&#124; And they save them out of the hand of their adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 5:31–1; Judges 4:1; 1 Kings 8:39"> And when they have rest, &#124;&#124; They turn back to do evil before You, &#124;&#124; And You leave them in the hand of their enemies, &#124;&#124; And they rule over them; And they return, and call You, &#124;&#124; And You hear from the heavens, &#124;&#124; And deliver them many times, &#124;&#124; According to Your mercies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:16; Leviticus 18:5; Nehemiah 9:26"> And testify against them, &#124;&#124; To bring them back to Your law; And they have acted proudly, &#124;&#124; And have not listened to Your commands, &#124;&#124; And against Your judgments have sinned—Which man does and has lived in them—And they give a stubborn shoulder, &#124;&#124; And have hardened their neck, &#124;&#124; And have not listened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 2:4; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Isaiah 42:24"> And You draw over them many years, &#124;&#124; And testify against them by Your Spirit, &#124;&#124; By the hand of Your prophets, &#124;&#124; And they have not given ear, &#124;&#124; And You give them into the hand of peoples of the lands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 3:22; Jeremiah 4:27; Nehemiah 9:17"> and in Your abundant mercies &#124;&#124; You have not made them a consumption, &#124;&#124; Nor have forsaken them; For a God, gracious and merciful, &#91;are&#93; You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Nehemiah 1:5; Daniel 9:4"> And now, O our God—God, the great, the mighty, &#124;&#124; And the fearful, &#124;&#124; Keeping the covenant and the kindness—Do not let all the travail that has found us be little before You, &#124;&#124; For our kings, for our heads, and for our priests, &#124;&#124; And for our prophets, and for our fathers, &#124;&#124; And for all Your people, &#124;&#124; From the days of the kings of Asshur to this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 18:25; Psalms 106:6; Job 34:23"> And You &#91;are&#93; righteous concerning all that has come on us, &#124;&#124; For You have done truth, &#124;&#124; And we have done wickedly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:19; 2 Kings 17:13; Deuteronomy 31:21"> And our kings, our heads, &#124;&#124; Our priests, and our fathers, &#124;&#124; Have not done Your law, &#124;&#124; Nor attended to Your commands, &#124;&#124; And to Your testimonies, &#124;&#124; That You have testified against them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:47; Deuteronomy 32:12–15; Jeremiah 5:19"> And they, in their kingdom, &#124;&#124; And in Your abundant goodness, &#124;&#124; That You have given to them, &#124;&#124; And in the land, the large and the fat, &#124;&#124; That You have set before them, &#124;&#124; Have not served You, &#124;&#124; Nor turned back from their evil doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezra 9:9; Deuteronomy 28:48; 2 Chronicles 12:8"> Behold, we—today—&#91;are&#93; servants, &#124;&#124; And the land that You have given to our fathers, &#124;&#124; To eat its fruit and its good, &#124;&#124; Behold, we &#91;are&#93; servants on it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Deuteronomy 28:48; Ezra 7:24"> And its increase it is multiplying to the kings &#124;&#124; Whom You have set over us &#124;&#124; In our sins; And over our bodies they are ruling, &#124;&#124; And over our livestock, &#124;&#124; According to their pleasure, &#124;&#124; And we &#91;are&#93; in great distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:1; 2 Chronicles 34:31; Ezra 10:3"> And for all this we are making a steadfast covenant, &#124;&#124; And are writing, &#124;&#124; And over him who is sealed &#124;&#124; &#91;Are&#93; our heads, our Levites, &#91;and&#93; our priests.”</span>
