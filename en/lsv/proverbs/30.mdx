<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 31:1; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> Words of Agur, son of Jakeh, the burden, a declaration of the man to Ithiel—to Ithiel and Ucal: I have wearied myself &#91;for&#93; God, &#124;&#124; I have wearied myself &#91;for&#93; God, and am consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; 1 Corinthians 3:18; Isaiah 6:5"> For I am more brutish than anyone, &#124;&#124; And do not have the understanding of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:7; Ephesians 3:18–19; Romans 11:33"> Nor have I learned wisdom, &#124;&#124; Yet I know the knowledge of the Holy Ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 3:13; Ephesians 4:8–10; Job 26:8"> Who went up to the heavens, and comes down? Who has gathered the wind in His fists? Who has bound waters in a garment? Who established all ends of the earth? What &#91;is&#93; His Name? And what &#91;is&#93; His Son’s Name? Surely you know!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 18:30; James 3:17; Psalms 12:6"> Every saying of God &#91;is&#93; tried, &#124;&#124; He &#91;is&#93; a shield to those trusting in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:2; Revelation 22:18–19"> Do not add to His words, lest He reason with you, &#124;&#124; And you have been found false.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:42; 2 Kings 2:9; Psalms 27:4"> Two things I have asked from You, &#124;&#124; Do not withhold from me before I die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–8; Psalms 62:9–10; Psalms 119:37"> Put vanity and a lying word far from me, &#124;&#124; Do not give poverty or wealth to me, &#124;&#124; Cause me to eat the bread of my portion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 31:20; Ezekiel 16:49–50"> Lest I become satiated, and have denied, &#124;&#124; And have said, “Who &#91;is&#93; YHWH?” And lest I am poor, and have stolen, &#124;&#124; And have laid hold of the Name of my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:4; Ecclesiastes 7:21; Deuteronomy 23:15"> Do not accuse a servant to his lord, &#124;&#124; Lest he disapprove of you, and you are found guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:20; Proverbs 30:17; Exodus 21:17"> A generation lightly esteems their father, &#124;&#124; And does not bless their mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 18:11; Proverbs 16:2; 2 Timothy 3:5"> A generation—pure in their own eyes, &#124;&#124; But not washed from their own filth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 6:17; Isaiah 2:11; Psalms 101:5"> A generation—how high are their eyes, &#124;&#124; Indeed, their eyelids are lifted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 3:7; Psalms 14:4"> A generation—their teeth &#91;are&#93; swords, &#124;&#124; And their jaw-teeth &#91;are&#93; knives, &#124;&#124; To consume the poor from earth, &#124;&#124; And the needy from &#91;among&#93; men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 30:21; Proverbs 30:29; Proverbs 6:16"> To the leech &#91;are&#93; two daughters—Give! Give! Behold, three things are not satisfied, &#124;&#124; Four have not said “Sufficiency”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:20; Habakkuk 2:5; Genesis 30:1"> Sheol, and a restrained womb, &#124;&#124; Earth—it &#91;is&#93; not satisfied &#91;with&#93; water, &#124;&#124; And fire—it has not said, “Sufficiency,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:22; Deuteronomy 21:18–21; Proverbs 30:11"> An eye that mocks at a father, &#124;&#124; And despises to obey a mother, &#124;&#124; Ravens of the valley dig it out, &#124;&#124; And young eagles eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 42:3; Psalms 139:6"> Three things have been too wonderful for me, &#124;&#124; Indeed, four that I have not known:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Job 39:27; Exodus 22:16"> The way of the eagle in the heavens, &#124;&#124; The way of a serpent on a rock, &#124;&#124; The way of a ship in the heart of the sea, &#124;&#124; And the way of a man in youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 5:6; Proverbs 7:13–23; Numbers 5:11–30"> So—the way of an adulterous woman, &#124;&#124; She has eaten and has wiped her mouth, &#124;&#124; And has said, “I have not done iniquity.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For three things has earth been troubled, &#124;&#124; And for four—it is not able to bear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Ecclesiastes 10:7; 1 Samuel 25:10–11"> For a servant when he reigns, &#124;&#124; And a fool when he is satisfied with bread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 27:15; Proverbs 29:21"> For a hated one when she rules, &#124;&#124; And a maidservant when she succeeds her mistress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 12:7"> Four &#91;are&#93; little ones of earth, &#124;&#124; And they are made wiser than the wise:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–8"> The ants &#91;are&#93; a people not strong, &#124;&#124; And they prepare their food in summer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 104:18; Leviticus 11:5"> hyraxes &#91;are&#93; a people not strong, &#124;&#124; And they place their house in a rock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Joel 1:6–7; Joel 2:7–11"> There is no king to the locust, &#124;&#124; And it goes out—each one shouting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> A spider with two hands takes hold, &#124;&#124; And is in the palaces of a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> There are three going well, &#124;&#124; Indeed, four are good in going:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 23:24; Judges 14:18"> An old lion—mighty among beasts, &#124;&#124; That does not turn back from the face of any,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 3:15–18; Proverbs 20:2; Proverbs 16:14"> A girt one of the loins, &#124;&#124; Also a male goat, &#124;&#124; And a king—troops with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 26:12"> If you have been foolish in lifting yourself up, &#124;&#124; And if you have devised evil—hand to mouth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 29:22; Proverbs 28:25"> For the churning of milk brings out butter, &#124;&#124; And the wringing of the nose brings out blood, &#124;&#124; And the forcing of anger brings out strife!</span>
