<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 37:3; Matthew 6:30–33; Psalms 37:25"> YHWH does not cause the soul of the righteous to hunger, &#124;&#124; And He thrusts away the desire of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 21:5; Proverbs 20:4; Proverbs 19:15"> Poor &#91;is&#93; he who is working &#91;with&#93; a slothful hand, &#124;&#124; And the hand of the diligent makes rich.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 18:11; Proverbs 19:7; Psalms 52:7"> The wealth of the rich &#91;is&#93; his strong city, &#124;&#124; The ruin of the poor &#91;is&#93; their poverty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 6:23; Isaiah 3:10–11; Hebrews 6:10"> The wage of the righteous &#91;is&#93; for life, &#124;&#124; The increase of the wicked for sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–11; Luke 11:28; Proverbs 6:23"> A traveler to life &#91;is&#93; he who is keeping instruction, &#124;&#124; And whoever is forsaking rebuke is erring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–26; Psalms 55:21; 2 Samuel 3:27"> Whoever is covering hatred with lying lips, &#124;&#124; And whoever is bringing out an evil report is a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 1:19; James 3:2; Proverbs 17:27–28"> In the abundance of words transgression does not cease, &#124;&#124; And whoever is restraining his lips &#91;is&#93; wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Proverbs 12:18; Genesis 6:5"> The tongue of the righteous &#91;is&#93; chosen silver, &#124;&#124; The heart of the wicked—as a little thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; Proverbs 17:16; Proverbs 1:29"> The lips of the righteous delight many, &#124;&#124; And fools die for lack of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 107:38; Genesis 26:12; 1 Samuel 2:7–8"> The blessing of YHWH—it makes rich, &#124;&#124; And He adds no grief with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:21; Proverbs 2:14; Proverbs 14:9"> To execute inventions &#91;is&#93; as play to a fool, &#124;&#124; And wisdom to a man of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; Isaiah 66:4; Matthew 5:6"> The feared thing of the wicked meets him, &#124;&#124; And the desire of the righteous is given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 15:5; Psalms 58:9; Matthew 7:24–27"> As the passing by of a windstorm, &#124;&#124; So the wicked is not, &#124;&#124; And the righteous is a perpetual foundation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 25:13; Proverbs 26:6; Matthew 25:26"> As vinegar to the teeth, &#124;&#124; And as smoke to the eyes, &#124;&#124; So &#91;is&#93; the slothful to those sending him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 9:11; Proverbs 3:2; Psalms 91:16"> The fear of YHWH adds days, &#124;&#124; And the years of the wicked are shortened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 8:13; Romans 15:13; Psalms 73:24–26"> The hope of the righteous &#91;is&#93; joyful, &#124;&#124; And the expectation of the wicked perishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 21:15; Romans 2:8–9; Philippians 4:13"> The way of YHWH &#91;is&#93; strength to the perfect, &#124;&#124; And ruin to workers of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 37:22; Psalms 125:1; Psalms 37:9–10"> The righteous is not moved for all time, &#124;&#124; And the wicked do not inhabit the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 37:30; Proverbs 10:13; Proverbs 10:20–21"> The mouth of the righteous utters wisdom, &#124;&#124; And the tongue of contrariness is cut out.</span>
