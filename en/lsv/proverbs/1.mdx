<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; 1 Chronicles 22:9; 1 Chronicles 28:5"> Proverbs of Solomon, son of David, king of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 7:4; Deuteronomy 4:5–6; 2 Timothy 3:15–17"> For knowing wisdom and instruction, &#124;&#124; For understanding sayings of intelligence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:1–9; Job 22:22; Proverbs 8:10–11"> For receiving the instruction of wisdom, &#124;&#124; Righteousness, judgment, and uprightness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:5; Proverbs 8:17; Psalms 119:9"> For giving to simple ones—prudence, &#124;&#124; To a youth—knowledge and discretion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 9:9; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> (The wise hear and increase learning, &#124;&#124; And the intelligent obtain counsels.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Mark 4:11; Psalms 49:4"> For understanding a proverb and its sweetness, &#124;&#124; Words of the wise and their acute sayings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 28:28; Psalms 111:10–1; Ecclesiastes 12:13"> Fear of YHWH &#91;is the&#93; beginning of knowledge, &#124;&#124; Fools have despised wisdom and instruction!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:20; Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 5:1–2"> Hear, my son, the instruction of your father, &#124;&#124; And do not leave the law of your mother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Proverbs 4:9; Genesis 41:42"> For they &#91;are&#93; a graceful wreath to your head, &#124;&#124; And chains to your neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Proverbs 16:29; Psalms 1:1"> My son, if sinners entice you, do not be willing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26; Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 12:6"> If they say, “Come with us, we lay wait for blood, &#124;&#124; We watch secretly for the innocent without cause,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 124:3; Psalms 28:1; Romans 3:13"> We swallow them as Sheol—alive, &#124;&#124; And whole—as those going down &#91;to&#93; the pit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 12:15; Job 24:2–3"> We find every precious substance, &#124;&#124; We fill our houses &#91;with&#93; spoil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> You cast your lot among us, &#124;&#124; One purse is—to all of us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:101; Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 4:14–15"> My son! Do not go in the way with them, &#124;&#124; Withhold your foot from their path,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 6:18; Proverbs 4:16"> For their feet run to evil, &#124;&#124; And they hurry to shed blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Proverbs 7:23; Jeremiah 8:7"> Surely in vain is the net spread out before the eyes of any bird.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 9:16; Esther 7:10; Proverbs 5:22–23"> And they lay wait for their own blood, &#124;&#124; They watch secretly for their own lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 15:27; 2 Peter 2:3; James 5:1–4"> So &#91;are&#93; the paths of every gainer of dishonest gain, &#124;&#124; It takes the life of its owners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 9:3; Colossians 2:3; Luke 11:49"> Wisdom cries aloud in an out-place, &#124;&#124; She gives forth her voice in broad places,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 5:20; Proverbs 9:3; Matthew 13:2"> She calls at the head of the multitudes, &#124;&#124; In the openings of the gates, &#124;&#124; In the city she says her sayings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 1:29; Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 5:12"> “Until when, you simple, do you love simplicity? And have scorners desired their scorning? And do fools hate knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Ezekiel 33:11; Ezekiel 18:27–30"> Turn back at my reproof, behold, &#124;&#124; I pour forth my spirit to you, &#124;&#124; I make known my words with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; Romans 10:21; Jeremiah 7:13"> Because I have called, and you refuse, &#124;&#124; I stretched out my hand, and none is attending,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 81:11; Luke 7:30; Proverbs 1:30"> And you slight all my counsel, &#124;&#124; And you have not desired my reproof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Proverbs 10:24; Psalms 37:13"> I also laugh in your calamity, &#124;&#124; I deride when your fear comes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 3:25–26; Luke 21:34–35; Psalms 58:9"> When your fear comes as destruction, &#124;&#124; And your calamity comes as a windstorm, &#124;&#124; When adversity and distress come on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Micah 3:4; James 4:3; Jeremiah 11:11"> Then they call me, and I do not answer, &#124;&#124; They seek me earnestly, and do not find me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Job 21:14–15; Proverbs 6:23"> Because that they have hated knowledge, &#124;&#124; And have not chosen the fear of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 81:11; Proverbs 1:25; Luke 14:18–20"> They have not consented to my counsel, &#124;&#124; They have despised all my reproof,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 14:14; Jeremiah 6:19; Proverbs 22:8"> And they eat of the fruit of their way, &#124;&#124; And they are filled from their own counsels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 8:36; Jeremiah 2:19; John 3:36"> For the turning of the simple slays them, &#124;&#124; And the security of the foolish destroys them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 25:12–13; Psalms 112:7–8; Isaiah 26:3"> And whoever is listening to me dwells confidently, &#124;&#124; And &#91;is&#93; quiet from fear of evil!”</span>
