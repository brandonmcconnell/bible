<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Ephesians 4:25; Psalms 119:163"> The righteous hates a false word, &#124;&#124; And the wicked causes abhorrence, and is confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:3; Proverbs 11:5–6; Psalms 140:11"> Righteousness keeps him who is perfect in the way, &#124;&#124; And wickedness overthrows a sin offering.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:20; Proverbs 12:15–16; Proverbs 17:14"> A vain man causes debate through pride, &#124;&#124; And wisdom &#91;is&#93; with the counseled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:21; Proverbs 10:2; James 5:1–5"> Wealth from vanity becomes little, &#124;&#124; And whoever is gathering by the hand becomes great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 16:22; Proverbs 13:19; Psalms 119:81–83"> Hope prolonged is making the heart sick, &#124;&#124; And a tree of life &#91;is&#93; the coming desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 15:31; 2 Chronicles 36:16; 2 John 1:8"> Whoever is despising the word is destroyed for it, &#124;&#124; And whoever is fearing the command is repaid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 18:5; Proverbs 10:11; Proverbs 14:27"> The law of the wise &#91;is&#93; a fountain of life, &#124;&#124; To turn aside from snares of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:19; Proverbs 4:19; Acts 7:10"> Good understanding gives grace, &#124;&#124; And the way of the treacherous &#91;is&#93; hard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 15:2; Proverbs 12:22–23; Ephesians 5:17"> Every prudent one deals with knowledge, &#124;&#124; And a fool spreads out folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 25:13; 1 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> A wicked messenger falls into evil, &#124;&#124; And a faithful ambassador is healing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:5; Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 15:31–32"> Whoever is refusing instruction—poverty and shame, &#124;&#124; And whoever is observing reproof is honored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 7:14–17; Proverbs 13:12; Proverbs 29:27"> A desire accomplished is sweet to the soul, &#124;&#124; And an abomination to fools &#124;&#124; &#91;Is&#93; to turn from evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33–34; Proverbs 15:31; Proverbs 9:6"> Whoever is walking with wise men is wise, &#124;&#124; And a companion of fools suffers evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 32:10; Isaiah 3:10–11; Romans 2:7–10"> Evil pursues sinners, &#124;&#124; And good repays the righteous.</span>
