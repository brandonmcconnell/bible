<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 6:20; Proverbs 1:8; 2 Timothy 1:5"> Words of Lemuel, a king, a declaration that his mother taught him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; 1 Samuel 1:11; 1 Samuel 1:28"> “What, my son? And what, son of my womb? And what, son of my vows?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:17; Nehemiah 13:26; Proverbs 7:26–27"> Do not give your strength to women, &#124;&#124; And your ways to wiping away of kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Ecclesiastes 10:17; Isaiah 5:22"> &#91;It is&#93; not for kings, O Lemuel, &#124;&#124; Not for kings, to drink wine, &#124;&#124; And for princes—a desire of strong drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:5"> Lest he drink, and forget the decree, &#124;&#124; And change the judgment of any of the sons of affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:23; Psalms 104:15; 1 Samuel 1:10"> Give strong drink to the perishing, &#124;&#124; And wine to the bitter in soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18"> He drinks, and forgets his poverty, &#124;&#124; And he does not remember his misery again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 82:3–4; Proverbs 24:11–12; Esther 4:13–16"> Open your mouth for the mute, &#124;&#124; For the right of all sons of change.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Leviticus 19:15; Amos 5:11–12"> Open your mouth, judge righteously, &#124;&#124; Both the cause of the poor and needy!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:14; Proverbs 12:4; Proverbs 18:22"> &#91;ALEPH-BET&#93; A woman of worth who finds? Indeed, her price &#91;is&#93; far above rubies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 3:1–7; 2 Kings 4:22–23; 2 Kings 4:9–10"> The heart of her husband has trusted in her, &#124;&#124; And he does not lack spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18–22; 1 Samuel 25:26–27"> She has done him good, and not evil, &#124;&#124; All &#91;the&#93; days of her life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Timothy 5:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:10–12"> She has sought wool and flax, &#124;&#124; And with delight she works &#91;with&#93; her hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 31:24; 1 Kings 9:26–28; Ezekiel 27:3–36"> She has been as ships of the merchant, &#124;&#124; She brings in her bread from afar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:11; Mark 1:35; Luke 12:42"> Indeed, she rises while yet night, &#124;&#124; And gives food to her household, &#124;&#124; And a portion to her girls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Song of Songs 8:12; Joshua 15:18"> She has considered a field, and takes it, &#124;&#124; She has planted a vineyard from the fruit of her hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 38:3; 1 Peter 1:13; Ephesians 6:10"> She has girded her loins with might, &#124;&#124; And strengthens her arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; Matthew 25:3–10; Psalms 127:2"> She has perceived when her merchandise &#91;is&#93; good, &#124;&#124; Her lamp is not extinguished in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:25–26"> She has sent forth her hands on a spindle, &#124;&#124; And her hands have held a distaff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 4:28; Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 22:9"> She has spread forth her hand to the poor, &#124;&#124; Indeed, she sent forth her hands to the needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:22; 2 Samuel 1:24; Proverbs 25:20"> She is not afraid of her household from snow, &#124;&#124; For all her household are clothed &#91;with&#93; scarlet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Proverbs 7:16; Genesis 41:42"> She has made ornamental coverings for herself, &#124;&#124; Silk and purple &#91;are&#93; her clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 12:4; Ruth 4:1; Deuteronomy 16:18"> Her husband is known in the gates, &#124;&#124; In his sitting with &#91;the&#93; elderly of &#91;the&#93; land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:16; Luke 16:19; Proverbs 31:19"> She has made linen garments, and sells, &#124;&#124; And she has given a girdle to the merchant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 40:10; Romans 13:14; Job 29:14"> Strength and honor &#91;are&#93; her clothing, &#124;&#124; And she rejoices at a latter day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 10:31"> She has opened her mouth in wisdom, &#124;&#124; And the law of kindness &#91;is&#93; on her tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; Titus 2:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> She &#91;is&#93; watching the ways of her household, &#124;&#124; And she does not eat bread of sloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:15–17; 2 Timothy 1:5; 1 Kings 2:19"> Her sons have risen up, and pronounce her blessed, &#124;&#124; Her husband, and he praises her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Song of Songs 6:8–9; Ephesians 5:27"> “The daughters who have done worthily &#91;are&#93; many, &#124;&#124; You have gone up above them all.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 1:7; 1 Peter 3:4–5; Proverbs 11:22"> Favor &#91;is&#93; deceitful, and beauty &#91;is&#93; vain, &#124;&#124; A woman fearing YHWH, she may boast herself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Hebrews 6:10; Matthew 7:16"> Give to her of the fruit of her hands, &#124;&#124; And her works praise her in the gates!</span>
