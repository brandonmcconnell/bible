<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 9:14; Proverbs 8:1–3; Matthew 22:3–4"> She has sent forth her girls, &#124;&#124; She cries on the tops of the high places of the city:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 6:32; Proverbs 9:16; Proverbs 8:5"> “Who &#91;is&#93; simple? Let him turn aside here.” Whoever lacks heart: she has said to him,</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Proverbs 4:11; Luke 13:24"> Forsake the simple and live, &#124;&#124; And be blessed in the way of understanding.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 15:12; Proverbs 23:9; 2 Chronicles 36:16"> The instructor of a scorner &#124;&#124; Is receiving for it—shame, &#124;&#124; And a reprover of the wicked—his blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 141:5; Proverbs 13:18; Proverbs 23:9"> Do not reprove a scorner, lest he hate you, &#124;&#124; Give reproof to the wise, and he loves you.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 111:10; Job 28:28; Proverbs 1:7"> The commencement of wisdom &#91;is&#93; the fear of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And a knowledge of the Holy Ones &#91;is&#93; understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 10:27; Proverbs 3:16; Proverbs 3:2"> For by me your days multiply, &#124;&#124; And years of life are added to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 22:2–3; 2 Peter 3:3–4; Ezekiel 18:20"> If you have been wise, you have been wise for yourself, &#124;&#124; And you have scorned—you bear &#91;it&#93; alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 7:11; Proverbs 5:6; 1 Timothy 6:4"> A foolish woman &#91;is&#93; noisy, &#124;&#124; Simple, and has not known what.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 9:3; Proverbs 7:10–12"> And she has sat at the opening of her house, &#124;&#124; On a throne—the high places of the city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 7:25–27; Proverbs 23:27–28; Proverbs 7:13–15"> To call to those passing by the way, &#124;&#124; Who are going straight &#91;on&#93; their paths.</span>
