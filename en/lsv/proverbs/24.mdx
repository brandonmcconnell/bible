<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 3:31"> Do not be envious of evil men, &#124;&#124; And do not desire to be with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 7:14; Psalms 28:3"> For their heart meditates &#91;on&#93; destruction, &#124;&#124; And their lips speak perverseness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; 1 Corinthians 3:9; Proverbs 9:1"> A house is built by wisdom, &#124;&#124; And it establishes itself by understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 15:6; Proverbs 21:20; Proverbs 20:15"> And the inner parts are filled by knowledge, &#124;&#124; &#91;With&#93; all precious and pleasant wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Colossians 1:11; Proverbs 21:22"> The wise &#91;is&#93; mighty in strength, &#124;&#124; And a man of knowledge is strengthening power,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 15:22"> For you make war for yourself by plans, &#124;&#124; And deliverance &#91;is&#93; in a multitude of counselors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; Psalms 10:5; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> Wisdom &#91;is&#93; high for a fool, he does not open his mouth in the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 21:11; Ezekiel 38:10–11; Proverbs 14:22"> Whoever is devising to do evil, &#124;&#124; They call him a master of wicked thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; 2 Corinthians 10:5; Isaiah 55:7"> The thought of folly &#91;is&#93; sin, &#124;&#124; And a scorner &#91;is&#93; an abomination to man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:28–31; Ephesians 3:13; Jeremiah 51:46"> You have showed yourself weak in a day of adversity, &#124;&#124; Your power is restricted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 82:4; Isaiah 58:6–7; 1 John 3:16–17"> If &#91;from&#93; delivering those taken to death, &#124;&#124; And you take back those slipping to the slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 34:11; Revelation 2:23; Jeremiah 17:10"> When you say, “Behold, we did not know this.” Is the Ponderer of hearts not He who understands? And the Keeper of your soul He who knows? And He has rendered to man according to his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15; Song of Songs 5:1; Proverbs 25:16"> My son, eat honey that &#91;is&#93; good, &#124;&#124; And the honeycomb &#91;is&#93; sweet to your palate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:25; Jeremiah 15:16; Proverbs 2:10"> So &#91;is&#93; the knowledge of wisdom to your soul, &#124;&#124; If you have found that there is a posterity &#124;&#124; And your hope is not cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:24; Isaiah 32:18; 1 Samuel 23:20–23"> Do not lay wait, O wicked one, &#124;&#124; At the habitation of the righteous. Do not spoil his resting place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Psalms 37:24; Micah 7:8–10"> For the righteous fall and rise seven &#91;times&#93;, &#124;&#124; And the wicked stumble in evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Obadiah 1:12; Job 31:29; Proverbs 17:5"> Do not rejoice in the falling of your enemy, &#124;&#124; And do not let your heart be joyful in his stumbling,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 1:15–16; Lamentations 4:21–22"> Lest YHWH see, and &#91;it be&#93; evil in His eyes, &#124;&#124; And He has turned His anger from off him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 119:115; Psalms 37:1; Proverbs 23:17"> Do not fret yourself at evildoers, do not be envious at the wicked,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Job 18:5–6; Psalms 11:6"> For there is not a posterity to the evil, &#124;&#124; The lamp of the wicked is extinguished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:1–7; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Titus 3:1"> Fear YHWH, my son, and the king, &#124;&#124; Do not mix yourself up with changers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 90:11; Hosea 5:11; Numbers 16:31–35"> For their calamity rises suddenly, &#124;&#124; And the ruin of them both—who knows!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 28:21; Proverbs 18:5; John 7:24"> These are also for the wise: &#91;It&#93; is not good to discern faces in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; Isaiah 5:23; Proverbs 11:26"> Whoever is saying to the wicked, “You &#91;are&#93; righteous,” &#124;&#124; Peoples execrate him—nations abhor him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:20; 1 Samuel 3:13; Proverbs 28:23"> And it is pleasant to those reproving, &#124;&#124; And a good blessing comes on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Genesis 41:38–57; Proverbs 25:11–12"> He who is returning straightforward words kisses lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 14:28–30; 1 Kings 5:17–18; 1 Kings 6:7"> Prepare your work in an out-place, &#124;&#124; And make it ready in the field—go afterward, &#124;&#124; Then you have built your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Matthew 26:59–60; Proverbs 25:18"> Do not be a witness against your neighbor for nothing, &#124;&#124; Or you have enticed with your lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; Romans 12:17–21; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Do not say, “As he did to me, so I do to him, &#124;&#124; I render to each according to his work.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Proverbs 6:6–19; Job 4:8"> I passed by near the field of a slothful man, &#124;&#124; And near the vineyard of a man lacking heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:18; Isaiah 5:5; Jeremiah 4:3"> And behold, it has gone up—all of it—thorns! Nettles have covered its face, &#124;&#124; And its stone wall has been broken down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; Deuteronomy 13:11; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> And I see—I set my heart, &#124;&#124; I have seen—I have received instruction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6–8; Proverbs 6:4–11; Romans 13:11"> A little sleep—a little slumber—A little folding of the hands to lie down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4"> And your poverty has come &#91;as&#93; a traveler, &#124;&#124; And your want as an armed man!</span>
