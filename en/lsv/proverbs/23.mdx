<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; Psalms 141:4; Ephesians 4:22"> Have no desire to his delicacies, seeing it &#91;is&#93; lying food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; Proverbs 28:20; Matthew 6:19"> Do not labor to make wealth, &#124;&#124; Cease from your own understanding, &#124;&#124; Do you cause your eyes to fly on it? Then it is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 27:24; 1 John 2:16; James 5:1–2"> For wealth makes wings to itself, &#124;&#124; It flies to the heavens as an eagle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 141:4; Proverbs 23:3; Daniel 1:8–10"> Do not eat the bread of an evil eye, &#124;&#124; And have no desire to his delicacies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; Psalms 55:21; Judges 16:15"> For as he has thought in his soul, so he &#91;is&#93;. “Eat and drink,” he says to you, &#124;&#124; And his heart &#91;is&#93; not with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> You vomit up your morsel you have eaten, &#124;&#124; And have marred your words that &#91;are&#93; sweet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 9:7–8; Matthew 7:6; Proverbs 26:4–5"> Do not speak in the ears of a fool, &#124;&#124; For he treads on the wisdom of your words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 22:28; Zechariah 7:10; Deuteronomy 27:17"> Do not remove a border of ancient times, &#124;&#124; And do not enter into fields of the fatherless,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 22:23; Job 19:25; Jeremiah 51:36"> For their Redeemer &#91;is&#93; strong, &#124;&#124; He pleads their cause with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 5:1–2; Proverbs 2:2–6; Proverbs 23:19"> Bring your heart to instruction, &#124;&#124; And your ear to sayings of knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 19:18; Proverbs 29:15"> Do not withhold discipline from a youth, &#124;&#124; When you strike him with a rod he does not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:5; Proverbs 22:15; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> You strike him with a rod, &#124;&#124; And you deliver his soul from Sheol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:24–25; 2 John 1:4; Proverbs 29:3"> My son, if your heart has been wise, &#124;&#124; My heart rejoices, even mine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6; Ephesians 5:4; James 3:2"> And my reins exult when your lips speak uprightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Peter 1:17"> Do not let your heart be envious at sinners, &#124;&#124; But—in the fear of YHWH all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Jeremiah 29:11; Psalms 9:18"> For is there a posterity? Then your hope is not cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 23:26; Proverbs 23:12; Proverbs 4:10–23"> Hear, my son, and be wise, &#124;&#124; And make your heart blessed in the way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 21:34; Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:22"> Do not become drunk with wine, &#124;&#124; Among gluttonous ones of flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 21:17; Proverbs 19:15; Deuteronomy 21:20"> For the drunkard and glutton become poor, &#124;&#124; And drowsiness clothes with rags.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 1:8; John 19:26–27"> Listen to your father, who begot you, &#124;&#124; And do not despise your mother when she has become old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 18:15; Matthew 13:44; Psalms 119:72"> Buy truth, and do not sell, &#124;&#124; Wisdom, and instruction, and understanding,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 15:20; Proverbs 23:15–16"> The father of the righteous rejoices greatly, &#124;&#124; The begetter of the wise rejoices in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 17:25; Luke 11:27–28; Luke 1:58"> Your father and your mother rejoice, &#124;&#124; Indeed, she bearing you is joyful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:2; Psalms 119:9–11; Deuteronomy 6:5"> Give, my son, your heart to me, &#124;&#124; And let your eyes watch my ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14"> For a harlot &#91;is&#93; a deep ditch, &#124;&#124; And a strange woman &#91;is&#93; a narrow pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 7:12; Proverbs 22:14"> She also, as catching prey, lies in wait, &#124;&#124; And she increases the treacherous among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:11; Proverbs 23:21"> Who has woe? Who has sorrow? Who has contentions? Who has complaint? Who has wounds without cause? Who has redness of eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:11; Proverbs 20:1"> Those lingering by the wine, &#124;&#124; Those going in to search out mixed wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 2:16; 2 Samuel 11:2; Psalms 119:37"> Do not see wine when it shows itself red, &#124;&#124; When it gives its color in the cup, &#124;&#124; It goes up and down through the upright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:8; Isaiah 28:7–8; Amos 9:3"> Its latter end—it bites as a serpent, &#124;&#124; And it stings as a viper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jude 1:12–13; Psalms 69:12; Daniel 5:4"> Your eyes see strange women, &#124;&#124; And your heart speaks perverse things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–7; 1 Kings 20:16–22; 1 Samuel 30:16–17"> And you have been as one lying down in the heart of the sea, &#124;&#124; And as one lying down on the top of a mast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 56:12; Jeremiah 5:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> “They struck me, I have not been sick, &#124;&#124; They beat me, I have not known. When I awake—I seek it yet again!”</span>
