<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 132:4; Proverbs 6:10–11; Mark 13:35–36"> Do not give sleep to your eyes, &#124;&#124; And slumber to your eyelids,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 91:3; Psalms 124:7; Proverbs 1:17"> Be delivered as a roe from the hand, &#124;&#124; And as a bird from the hand of a fowler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 20:4; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 6:9"> Go to the ant, O slothful one, &#124;&#124; See her ways and be wise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:39–12; Proverbs 30:27; Job 39:26–30"> Which has no captain, overseer, and ruler,</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; Proverbs 6:6; Proverbs 23:33–34"> A little sleep, a little slumber, &#124;&#124; A little clasping of the hands to rest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 24:34"> And your poverty has come as a traveler, &#124;&#124; And your want as an armed man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; 1 Timothy 5:13; Proverbs 16:27"> A man of worthlessness, a man of iniquity, &#124;&#124; Walking &#91;with&#93; perverseness of mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Proverbs 10:10; Job 15:12"> Winking with his eyes, speaking with his feet, &#124;&#124; Directing with his fingers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 16:17; Micah 2:1; Hosea 8:7"> Contrariness &#91;is&#93; in his heart, devising evil at all times, &#124;&#124; He sends forth contentions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Jeremiah 19:11; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> Therefore his calamity comes suddenly, &#124;&#124; He is broken instantly—and no healing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 11:5; Proverbs 8:13; Proverbs 11:20"> These six has YHWH hated, &#124;&#124; Indeed, seven &#91;are&#93; abominations to His soul:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 120:2–3; Proverbs 12:22; Psalms 101:5"> High eyes, &#124;&#124; False tongues, &#124;&#124; And hands shedding innocent blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 6:5; Proverbs 1:16; Romans 3:15"> A heart devising thoughts of vanity, &#124;&#124; Feet hastening to run to evil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Proverbs 6:14; James 3:18"> A false witness &#91;who&#93; breathes out lies, &#124;&#124; And one sending forth contentions between brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1; Deuteronomy 27:16; Proverbs 7:1–4"> Keep, my son, the command of your father, &#124;&#124; And do not leave the law of your mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 3:3; Proverbs 7:3–4; 2 Corinthians 3:3"> Bind them on your heart continually, &#124;&#124; Tie them on your neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Proverbs 3:23–24; Psalms 119:24"> In your going up and down, it leads you, &#124;&#124; In your lying down, it watches over you, &#124;&#124; And you have awoken—it talks &#91;with&#93; you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:105; 2 Peter 1:19; Psalms 19:8"> For the command &#91;is&#93; a lamp, &#124;&#124; And the Law a light, &#124;&#124; And a way of life &#91;are&#93; reproofs of instruction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 2:16; Proverbs 7:5; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> To preserve you from an evil woman, &#124;&#124; From the flattery of the tongue of a strange woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 1:14–15; Matthew 5:28; 2 Kings 9:30"> Do not desire her beauty in your heart, &#124;&#124; And do not let her take you with her eyelids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 15:30; Proverbs 29:3; 1 Samuel 2:36"> For a harlot consumes to a cake of bread, &#124;&#124; And an adulteress hunts the precious soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 31:9–12; Hosea 7:4–7; James 3:5"> Does a man take fire into his bosom, &#124;&#124; And are his garments not burned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Does a man walk on the hot coals, &#124;&#124; And are his feet not scorched?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1; Malachi 3:5; 2 Samuel 12:9–10"> So &#91;is&#93; he who has gone in to the wife of his neighbor, &#124;&#124; None who touches her is innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 38:39"> They do not despise the thief, &#124;&#124; When he steals to fill his soul when he is hungry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 19:8; Job 20:18; 2 Samuel 12:6"> And being found he repays sevenfold, &#124;&#124; He gives all the substance of his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 7:7; Hebrews 13:4; Romans 1:22–24"> He who commits adultery &#91;with&#93; a woman lacks heart, &#124;&#124; He who does it is destroying his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 5:9–11; Psalms 51:8; Judges 16:19–21"> He finds a stroke and shame, &#124;&#124; And his reproach is not wiped away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Numbers 5:14; Song of Songs 8:6"> For jealousy &#91;is&#93; the fury of a man, &#124;&#124; And he does not spare in a day of vengeance.</span>
