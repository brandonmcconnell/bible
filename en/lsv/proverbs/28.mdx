<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:7; Psalms 53:5; Isaiah 26:3–4"> The wicked have fled and there is no pursuer, &#124;&#124; And the righteous is confident as a young lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:28; 1 Kings 16:8–29; 2 Kings 15:8–31"> By the transgression of a land its heads are many, &#124;&#124; And by an understanding man, &#124;&#124; Who knows right—it is prolonged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 18:28–30"> A man—poor and oppressing the weak, &#124;&#124; &#91;Is&#93; a sweeping rain, and there is no bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Ephesians 5:11; Matthew 14:4"> Those forsaking the Law praise the wicked, &#124;&#124; Those keeping the Law plead against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 92:6; James 1:5; 1 John 2:27"> Evil men do not understand judgment, &#124;&#124; And those seeking YHWH understand all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 19:1; Proverbs 28:18; Luke 16:19–23"> Better &#91;is&#93; the poor walking in his integrity, &#124;&#124; Than the perverse of ways who is rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:3–4; Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 3:1–35"> Whoever is keeping the Law is an intelligent son, &#124;&#124; And a friend of gluttons &#124;&#124; Causes his father to blush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 13:22; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> Whoever is multiplying his wealth by biting and usury, &#124;&#124; Gathers it for one favoring the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 66:18; 2 Timothy 4:3–4; Zechariah 7:11–13"> Whoever is turning his ear from hearing the Law, &#124;&#124; Even his prayer &#91;is&#93; an abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 26:27; Psalms 37:25–26; Galatians 2:4"> Whoever is causing the upright to err in an evil way, &#124;&#124; He falls into his own pit, &#124;&#124; And the perfect inherits good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 18:17; Ecclesiastes 9:15–17; Proverbs 26:16"> A rich man is wise in his own eyes, &#124;&#124; And the intelligent poor searches him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 28:28"> In the exulting of the righteous the glory &#91;is&#93; abundant, &#124;&#124; And in the rising of the wicked man is apprehensive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 1:8–10; Psalms 32:3–5; Job 31:33"> Whoever is covering his transgressions does not prosper, &#124;&#124; And he who is confessing and forsaking has mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 23:17; Psalms 2:11; Isaiah 66:2"> O the blessedness of a man fearing continually, &#124;&#124; And whoever is hardening his heart falls into evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 2:16; Proverbs 19:12"> A growling lion, and a ranging bear, &#124;&#124; &#91;Is&#93; the wicked ruler over a poor people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Jeremiah 22:15–17; Ecclesiastes 4:1"> A leader lacking understanding multiplies oppressions, &#124;&#124; Whoever is hating dishonest gain prolongs days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; 1 Kings 21:19; Exodus 21:14"> A man oppressed with the blood of a soul, &#124;&#124; Flees to the pit, &#91;and&#93; none takes hold on him &#91;to help&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Proverbs 28:6; Revelation 3:3"> Whoever is walking uprightly is saved, &#124;&#124; And the perverted of ways falls at once.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Judges 9:4; Proverbs 27:23–27"> Whoever is tilling his ground is satisfied &#91;with&#93; bread, &#124;&#124; And whoever is pursuing vanity, &#124;&#124; Is filled &#91;with&#93; poverty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 16:10–12; Proverbs 13:11; Revelation 2:10"> A steadfast man has multiplied blessings, &#124;&#124; And whoever is hastening to be rich is not acquitted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; Proverbs 18:5; Ezekiel 13:19"> &#91;It&#93; is not good to discern faces, &#124;&#124; And a man transgresses for a piece of bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; 1 Timothy 6:9; Proverbs 28:20"> The man &#91;with&#93; an evil eye &#91;is&#93; troubled for wealth, &#124;&#124; And he does not know that want meets him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5–6; Matthew 18:15; Psalms 141:5"> Whoever is reproving a man finds grace afterward, &#124;&#124; More than a flatterer with the tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:4–6; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 19:26"> Whoever is robbing his father or his mother, &#124;&#124; And is saying, “It is not transgression,” &#124;&#124; He is a companion to a destroyer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 11:25; 1 Timothy 6:6; Proverbs 13:10"> Whoever is proud in soul stirs up contention, &#124;&#124; And whoever is trusting on YHWH is made fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Jeremiah 17:9; Job 28:28"> Whoever is trusting in his heart is a fool, &#124;&#124; And whoever is walking in wisdom is delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Deuteronomy 15:7; Psalms 41:1–3"> Whoever is giving to the poor has no lack, &#124;&#124; And whoever is hiding his eyes multiplied curses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Job 24:4; Acts 12:23–24"> A man is hidden in the rising of the wicked, &#124;&#124; And the righteous multiply in their destruction!</span>
