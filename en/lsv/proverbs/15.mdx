<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Jeremiah 16:17; Jeremiah 23:24"> The eyes of YHWH are in every place, &#124;&#124; Watching the evil and the good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 18:8"> A healed tongue &#91;is&#93; a tree of life, &#124;&#124; And perverseness in it—a breach in the spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:31–32; Proverbs 13:1; Proverbs 13:18"> A fool despises the instruction of his father, &#124;&#124; And whoever is regarding reproof is prudent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 8:21; Proverbs 21:20; Proverbs 10:22"> Abundant strength &#91;is in&#93; the house of the righteous, &#124;&#124; And in the increase of the wicked—trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; James 3:6; Ephesians 4:29"> The lips of the wise scatter knowledge, &#124;&#124; And the heart of fools &#91;is&#93; not right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Proverbs 21:27; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> The sacrifice of the wicked &#91;is&#93; an abomination to YHWH, &#124;&#124; And the prayer of the upright &#91;is&#93; His delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:11; Proverbs 21:21; Matthew 7:13"> The way of the wicked &#91;is&#93; an abomination to YHWH, &#124;&#124; And He loves whoever is pursuing righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; John 3:20; Proverbs 13:1"> Discipline &#91;is&#93; grievous to him who is forsaking the path, &#124;&#124; Whoever is hating reproof dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:21; Hebrews 4:13; Psalms 139:8"> Sheol and destruction &#91;are&#93; before YHWH, &#124;&#124; Surely also the hearts of the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Job 21:14; Proverbs 9:7–8"> A scorner does not love his reprover, &#124;&#124; He does not go to the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 17:22; John 14:1; Proverbs 18:14"> A joyful heart makes the face glad, &#124;&#124; And the spirit is struck by grief of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 9:9; Isaiah 30:10; Proverbs 18:15"> The heart of the intelligent seeks knowledge, &#124;&#124; And the mouth of fools enjoys folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:10; Romans 12:12; Proverbs 15:13"> All the days of the afflicted &#91;are&#93; evil, &#124;&#124; And gladness of heart &#91;is&#93; a perpetual banquet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Psalms 37:16; Ecclesiastes 5:10–12"> Better &#91;is&#93; a little with the fear of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Than much treasure, and tumult with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 17:1; Proverbs 21:19; 1 John 4:16"> Better &#91;is&#93; an allowance of green herbs and love there, &#124;&#124; Than a fatted ox, and hatred with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Proverbs 26:21; Proverbs 29:22"> A man of fury stirs up contention, &#124;&#124; And the slow to anger appeases strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 35:8; Proverbs 22:5; Isaiah 30:21"> The way of the slothful &#91;is&#93; as a hedge of briers, &#124;&#124; And the path of the upright is raised up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 23:15–16"> A wise son makes a father glad. And a foolish man is despising his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 5:15; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 14:16"> Folly is joy to one lacking heart, &#124;&#124; And a man of intelligence directs &#91;his&#93; going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Proverbs 20:18; Ecclesiastes 8:6"> The making void of purposes &#91;is&#93; without counsel, &#124;&#124; And in a multitude of counselors it is established.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 50:4; Proverbs 25:11–12; Ephesians 4:29"> Joy &#91;is&#93; to a man in the answer of his mouth, &#124;&#124; And a word in its season—how good!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–2; Psalms 16:11; Matthew 7:14"> A path of life &#91;is&#93; on high for the wise, &#124;&#124; To turn aside from Sheol beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 146:9; Psalms 68:5–6; Proverbs 14:11"> YHWH pulls down the house of the proud, &#124;&#124; And He sets up the border of the widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Jeremiah 4:14; Proverbs 15:23"> Thoughts of wickedness &#91;are&#93; an abomination to YHWH, &#124;&#124; And sayings of pleasantness &#91;are&#93; pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 23:8; Proverbs 1:19; Jeremiah 17:11"> A dishonest gainer is troubling his house, &#124;&#124; And whoever is hating gifts lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15; Proverbs 29:11; Ecclesiastes 5:2"> The heart of the righteous meditates to answer, &#124;&#124; And the mouth of the wicked utters evil things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 9:31; Psalms 145:18–19; Proverbs 15:8"> YHWH &#91;is&#93; far from the wicked, &#124;&#124; And He hears the prayer of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 21:23"> The light of the eyes makes the heart glad, &#124;&#124; A good report makes the bone fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:5; Proverbs 25:12; John 15:3–4"> An ear that is hearing the reproof of life &#124;&#124; Lodges among the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–33; Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 1:7"> Whoever is refusing instruction is despising his soul, &#124;&#124; And whoever is hearing reproof &#124;&#124; Is getting understanding.</span>
