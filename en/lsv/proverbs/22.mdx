<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 19:18"> Folly is bound up in the heart of a youth, &#124;&#124; The rod of discipline puts it far from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 22:22–23; Psalms 12:5; James 2:13"> He &#91;who&#93; is oppressing the poor to multiply his &#91;riches&#93;, &#124;&#124; Is giving to the rich—only to want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:12; Psalms 90:12; Proverbs 3:1"> Incline your ear, and hear words of the wise, &#124;&#124; And set your heart to my knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Proverbs 2:10; Psalms 119:103"> For they are pleasant when you keep them in your heart, &#124;&#124; They are prepared together for your lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Jeremiah 17:7; 1 Peter 1:21"> That your trust may be in YHWH, &#124;&#124; I caused you to know today, even you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> Have I not written to you three times &#124;&#124; With counsels and knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:3–4; 1 Peter 3:15; John 20:31"> To cause you to know the certainty of sayings of truth, &#124;&#124; To return sayings of truth to those sending you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 7:10; Malachi 3:5; Exodus 23:6"> Do not rob the poor because he &#91;is&#93; poor, &#124;&#124; And do not bruise the afflicted in the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 140:12; Proverbs 23:11"> For YHWH pleads their cause, &#124;&#124; And has spoiled the soul of their spoilers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 29:22; 2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Proverbs 21:24"> Do not show yourself friendly with an angry man, &#124;&#124; And do not go in with a man of fury,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 13:20; Psalms 106:35"> Lest you learn his paths, &#124;&#124; And have received a snare to your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 11:15; Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 6:1–5"> Do not be among those striking hands, &#124;&#124; Among sureties &#91;for&#93; burdens.</span>
