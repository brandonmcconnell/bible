<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Luke 1:76; Mark 1:2–3"> “Behold, I am sending My messenger, &#124;&#124; And he has prepared a way before Me, &#124;&#124; And suddenly the Lord whom you are seeking comes into His temple, &#124;&#124; Even the Messenger of the Covenant, &#124;&#124; Whom you are desiring, &#124;&#124; Behold, He is coming,” said YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Isaiah 4:4; 1 Corinthians 3:13–15"> “And who is bearing the day of His coming? And who is standing in His appearing? For He &#91;is&#93; as fire of a refiner, &#124;&#124; And as soap of a fuller.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:25; Proverbs 17:3; Zechariah 13:9"> And He has sat, a refiner and purifier of silver, &#124;&#124; And He has purified the sons of Levi, &#124;&#124; And has refined them as gold and as silver, &#124;&#124; And they have been bringing a present near to YHWH in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:1–3; Isaiah 56:7; Zechariah 8:3"> And the present of Judah and Jerusalem has been sweet to YHWH, &#124;&#124; As in days of old, and as in former years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:8–9; Hebrews 13:4; Deuteronomy 5:11"> And I have drawn near to you for judgment, &#124;&#124; And I have been a witness, &#124;&#124; Making haste against sorcerers, and against adulterers, &#124;&#124; And against swearers to a falsehood, &#124;&#124; And against oppressors of the hire of a hired worker, &#124;&#124; Of a widow, and of a fatherless one, &#124;&#124; And those turning aside a sojourner, &#124;&#124; And who do not fear Me,” said YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Hebrews 13:8; James 1:17"> “For I, YHWH, have not changed, &#124;&#124; And you, the sons of Jacob, &#124;&#124; You have not been consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 1:3; Romans 10:21; Romans 10:3"> Even from the days of your fathers You have turned aside from My statutes, &#124;&#124; And you have not taken heed. Return to Me, and I return to you,” said YHWH of Hosts. “And you have said, &#124;&#124; In what do we turn back?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Matthew 22:21; Mark 12:17"> Does man deceive God? But you are deceiving Me, &#124;&#124; And you have said, &#124;&#124; In what have we deceived You? The tithe and the raised-offering!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–19; Haggai 1:6–11; Haggai 2:14–17"> You are cursed with a curse! And you are deceiving Me—this nation—all of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; Matthew 6:33; Proverbs 3:9–10"> Bring in all the tithe to the treasure-house, &#124;&#124; And there is food in My house; When you have tried Me, now, with this,” said YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; “Do I not open the windows of the heavens to you? Indeed, I have emptied on you a blessing until there is no space.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:14; Joel 2:22; Zechariah 8:12"> And I have pushed against the consumer for you, &#124;&#124; And He does not destroy the fruit of your ground, &#124;&#124; Nor does the vine in the field miscarry to you,” said YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 61:9; Isaiah 62:4; Zechariah 8:23"> “And all the nations have declared you blessed, &#124;&#124; For you are a delightful land,” said YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 2:17; Isaiah 37:23; Romans 9:20"> “Your words have been hard against Me,” said YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And you have said, What have we spoken against You?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 58:3; Zephaniah 1:12; Psalms 73:8–13"> You have said, A vain thing to serve God! And what gain when we kept His charge? And when we have gone in black, &#124;&#124; Because of YHWH of Hosts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Jeremiah 12:1–2; Malachi 2:17"> And now, we are declaring the proud blessed, &#124;&#124; Indeed, those doing wickedness have been built up, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they have tempted God, and escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 20:12; Psalms 56:8; Malachi 4:2"> Then have those fearing YHWH spoken to one another, &#124;&#124; And YHWH attends and hears, &#124;&#124; And a scroll of memorial is written before Him &#124;&#124; Of those fearing YHWH, &#124;&#124; And of those esteeming His Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Deuteronomy 7:6; 2 Corinthians 6:18"> And they have been to Me,” said YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; “In the day that I am appointing—a peculiar treasure, &#124;&#124; And I have had pity on them, &#124;&#124; As one has pity on his son who is serving him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 25:46; Psalms 58:10–11; Genesis 18:25"> And you have turned back and considered, &#124;&#124; Between the righteous and the wicked, &#124;&#124; Between the servant of God and him who is not His servant.”</span>
