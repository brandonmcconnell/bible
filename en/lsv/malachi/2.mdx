<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 5:1; Malachi 1:6; Jeremiah 13:13"> “And now, to you &#91;is&#93; this charge, O priests,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:18; Revelation 14:7; Psalms 69:22"> If you do not listen, and if you do not lay &#91;it&#93; to heart, &#124;&#124; To give glory to My Name,” said YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; “I have sent the curse against you, &#124;&#124; And I have cursed your blessings, &#124;&#124; Indeed, I have also cursed it, &#124;&#124; Because you are not laying &#91;it&#93; to heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nahum 3:6; Malachi 2:9; Job 20:7"> Behold, I am pushing away the seed before you, &#124;&#124; And have scattered dung before your faces, &#124;&#124; Dung of your festivals, &#124;&#124; And it has taken you away with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Ezekiel 38:23; Matthew 3:12"> And you have known that I have sent this charge to you, &#124;&#124; For My covenant being with Levi,” said YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 25:12–13; Ezekiel 34:25; Ezekiel 37:26"> “My covenant has been of life and of peace with him, &#124;&#124; And I make them a fear to him, and he fears Me, &#124;&#124; And because of My Name he has been frightened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; 2 Timothy 2:15–16; Revelation 14:5"> The law of truth has been in his mouth, &#124;&#124; And perverseness has not been found in his lips, &#124;&#124; In peace and in uprightness he walked with Me, &#124;&#124; And he brought back many from iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24–25; Leviticus 10:11; Deuteronomy 21:5"> For the lips of a priest preserve knowledge, &#124;&#124; And they seek law from his mouth, &#124;&#124; For he &#91;is&#93; a messenger of YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:29; Jeremiah 18:15; Isaiah 30:11"> And you, you have turned from the way, &#124;&#124; You have caused many to stumble in the Law, &#124;&#124; You have corrupted the covenant of Levi,” said YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:30; Deuteronomy 1:17; Matthew 19:17–18"> “And I also, I have made you despised and low before all the people, &#124;&#124; Because you are not keeping My ways, &#124;&#124; And are accepting by superficial things in the Law.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; Malachi 2:11; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> Have we not all one Father? Has not our God created us? Why do we deal treacherously, &#124;&#124; Each against his brother, &#124;&#124; To defile the covenant of our fathers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–18; Ezra 9:1–2; Nehemiah 13:23–29"> Judah has dealt treacherously, &#124;&#124; And abomination has been done in Israel and in Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; For Judah has defiled the holy thing of YHWH that He has loved, &#124;&#124; And has married the daughter of a strange god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:29; Leviticus 20:3; Ezekiel 24:21"> YHWH cuts off the man who does it, &#124;&#124; Tempter and tempted—from the tents of Jacob, &#124;&#124; Even he who is bringing a present near to YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–15; Jeremiah 14:12; Psalms 78:34–37"> And you do this a second time, &#124;&#124; Covering the altar of YHWH with tears, &#124;&#124; With weeping and groaning, &#124;&#124; Because there is no more turning to the present, &#124;&#124; Or receiving of a pleasing thing from your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 2:15; Ecclesiastes 9:9; Isaiah 54:6"> And you have said, “Why?” Because YHWH has testified between you &#124;&#124; And the wife of your youth, &#124;&#124; That you have dealt treacherously against her, &#124;&#124; And she &#91;is&#93; your companion, and your covenant-wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 19:4–6; Malachi 2:14; Mark 10:6–8"> And He did not make one &#91;only&#93;, &#124;&#124; And a remnant of the Spirit &#91;is&#93; for him. And what &#91;is&#93; the one &#91;alone&#93;! He is seeking a godly seed. And you have been watchful over your spirit, &#124;&#124; And with the wife of your youth, &#124;&#124; None deal treacherously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 19:3–9; Matthew 5:31–32; Mark 10:2–12"> “For He hates sending away,” said YHWH, God of Israel, &#124;&#124; “And he &#91;who&#93; has covered violence with his clothing,” said YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; “And you have been watchful over your spirit, &#124;&#124; And you do not deal treacherously.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:24; 2 Peter 3:3–4; Ecclesiastes 8:11"> You have wearied YHWH with your words, &#124;&#124; And you have said, “In what have we wearied Him?” In your saying, “Every evildoer &#91;is&#93; good in the eyes of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And He is delighting in them,” &#124;&#124; Or, “Where &#91;is&#93; the God of judgment?”</span>
