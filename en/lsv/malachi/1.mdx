<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:1; Isaiah 13:1; Haggai 1:1"> The burden of a word of YHWH to Israel by the hand of Malachi:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 41:8–9; Jeremiah 31:3; Deuteronomy 10:15"> “I have loved you, said YHWH, &#124;&#124; And you have said, &#124;&#124; In what have You loved us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Obadiah 1:10; Ezekiel 35:3–4; Jeremiah 49:16–18"> Is not Esau Jacob’s brother?” A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And I love Jacob, and Esau I have hated, &#124;&#124; And I make his mountains a desolation, &#124;&#124; And his inheritance for dragons of a wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:14; Ezekiel 35:9; Ezekiel 11:10"> Because Edom says, We have been made poor, &#124;&#124; And we return and we build the ruins, &#124;&#124; Thus said YHWH of Hosts: They build, and I destroy, &#124;&#124; And &#91;men&#93; have called to them, &#124;&#124; O region of wickedness, &#124;&#124; O people whom YHWH defied for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:7; Psalms 58:10–11; Ezekiel 38:16"> And your eyes see, and you say, &#124;&#124; YHWH is magnified beyond the border of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:46; Isaiah 1:2; John 13:13–17"> A son honors a father, and a servant his master. And if I &#91;am&#93; a father, where &#91;is&#93; My glory? And if I &#91;am&#93; a master, where &#91;is&#93; My fear? Said YHWH of Hosts to you, &#124;&#124; O priests, despising My Name! And you have said, In what have we despised Your Name?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Malachi 1:12; 1 Corinthians 11:27–32; Malachi 1:8"> You are bringing defiled bread near on My altar, &#124;&#124; And you have said, In what have we defiled You? In your saying, The table of YHWH—it &#91;is&#93; despicable,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Leviticus 22:19–25; Hosea 8:13"> And when you bring the blind near for sacrifice, &#91;saying&#93;, &#124;&#124; There is no evil, &#124;&#124; And when you bring the lame and sick near, &#91;saying&#93;, &#124;&#124; There is no evil; Now bring it near to your governor—Does he accept you? Or does he lift up your face?” said YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 32:11; Jeremiah 27:18; 1 Peter 1:17"> “And now, please appease the face of God, &#124;&#124; And He favors us; This has been from your own hand, &#124;&#124; Does He accept your faces?” said YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Amos 5:21–24; Jeremiah 6:20"> “Who &#91;is&#93; even among you, &#124;&#124; And he shuts the double doors? Indeed, you do not kindle My altar for nothing, &#124;&#124; I have no pleasure in you,” said YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; “And I do not accept a present of your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:6; Isaiah 54:5; Isaiah 66:19–20"> For from the rising of the sun to its going in, &#124;&#124; Great &#91;is&#93; My Name among nations, &#124;&#124; And in every place incense is brought near for My Name, and a pure present, &#124;&#124; For great &#91;is&#93; My Name among nations,” said YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 1:6–8; Malachi 2:8; Amos 2:7"> “And you are defiling it in your saying, &#124;&#124; The table of YHWH—it is defiled, &#124;&#124; As for its fruit—its food is despicable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 6:3; Leviticus 22:19–23; Mark 14:37–38"> And you have said, Behold, what a weariness, &#124;&#124; And you have puffed at it,” said YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; “And you have brought in plunder, &#124;&#124; And the lame and the sick, &#124;&#124; And you have brought in the present! Do I accept it from your hand?” said YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 5:1–10; Ecclesiastes 5:4–5"> “And a deceiver &#91;is&#93; cursed, who has in his drove a male, &#124;&#124; And is vowing, and is sacrificing a marred thing to the Lord, &#124;&#124; For I &#91;am&#93; a great King,” said YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; “And My Name &#91;is&#93; revered among nations!”</span>
