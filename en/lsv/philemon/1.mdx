<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:1; Ephesians 3:1; Philemon 1:9"> Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timotheus the brother, to Philemon our beloved and fellow-worker,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Colossians 4:17; Romans 16:5"> and Apphia the beloved, and Archippus our fellow-soldier, and the assembly in your house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Ephesians 1:2; 2 Corinthians 13:14"> Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:8; Ephesians 1:16; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> I give thanks to my God, always making mention of you in my prayers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 1:4; Ephesians 1:15; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> hearing of your love and faith that you have to the Lord Jesus and toward all the holy ones,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 1:9–11; 2 Peter 1:8; Colossians 1:9"> that the fellowship of your faith may become working in the full knowledge of every good thing that &#91;is&#93; in you toward Christ Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:9; 2 Corinthians 7:4"> for we have much joy and comfort in your love, because the yearnings of the holy ones have been refreshed through you, brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Corinthians 10:8; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> For this reason, having in Christ much boldness to command you that which is fit—</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; Ephesians 4:1; Romans 12:1"> because of the love I rather plead, being such a one as Paul the aged, and now also a prisoner of Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 4:9; 1 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Timothy 1:2"> I beg you concerning my child—whom I begot in my bonds—Onesimus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:11; Job 30:1–2; 1 Peter 2:10"> who once was to you unprofitable, and now is profitable to me and to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; Jeremiah 31:20; Mark 11:25"> whom I sent again to you—he who is my own heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Ephesians 3:1; 1 Corinthians 16:17"> whom I intended to retain to myself, that in your behalf he might minister to me in the bonds of the good news,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Chronicles 29:17; 1 Peter 5:2–3"> but apart from your mind I willed to do nothing, so that your good deed may not be as of necessity, but of willingness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Genesis 45:5–8; Isaiah 20:6"> for perhaps because of this he departed for an hour, that you may have him continuously,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Matthew 23:8; 1 Corinthians 7:22"> no longer as a servant, but above a servant—a beloved brother, especially to me, and how much more to you, both in the flesh and in the LORD!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:23; Matthew 10:40; Philemon 1:12"> If, then, you have fellowship with me, receive him as me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–7"> and if he did hurt to you, or owes anything, charge this to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:11; Galatians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 4:15"> I, Paul, wrote with my hand, I will repay; besides, that I may not say that you also owe to me yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="3 John 1:4; Philemon 1:7; Philemon 1:12"> Yes, brother, may I have profit of you in the LORD; refresh my yearnings in the LORD;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:4; 2 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 7:16"> I wrote to you having been confident in your obedience, having known that you will also do above what I may say;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:11; Philippians 2:24; 2 John 1:12"> and at the same time also prepare for me a lodging, for I hope that through your prayers I will be granted to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:7; Romans 16:7; Colossians 4:12"> Epaphras greets you (my fellow-captive in Christ Jesus),</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10–11; Colossians 4:14; Colossians 4:10"> Marcus, Aristarchus, Demas, Lucas, my fellow-workmen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:22; Romans 16:23; Romans 16:20"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ &#91;is&#93; with your spirit! Amen.</span>
