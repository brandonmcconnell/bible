<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:5; Revelation 13:8; Luke 10:20"> and I also ask you, genuine yoke-fellow, be assisting those women who strove along with me in the good news, with Clement also, and the others, my fellow-workers, whose names &#91;are&#93; in &#91;the&#93; Scroll of Life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:16–18; Romans 12:12; James 1:2–4"> Rejoice in the LORD always; again I will say, rejoice!</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 14:27; Isaiah 26:3; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> and the peace of God, that is surpassing all understanding, will guard your hearts and your thoughts in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 3:17; Titus 2:7; Romans 12:9–21"> As to the rest, brothers, as many things as are true, as many as &#91;are&#93; revered, as many as &#91;are&#93; righteous, as many as &#91;are&#93; pure, as many as &#91;are&#93; lovely, as many as &#91;are&#93; of good report, if any worthiness, and if any praise, think on these things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 1:22; Philippians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–8"> the things that you also learned, and receive, and hear, and saw in me, do those, and the God of peace will be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; Galatians 6:6; 2 Corinthians 7:6–7"> And I rejoiced in the LORD greatly, that now at length you flourished again in caring for me, for which also you were caring, and lacked opportunity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–9; Hebrews 13:5–6; Matthew 6:31–34"> I do not say that in respect of want, for I learned in the things in which I am—to be content;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; 2 Corinthians 12:7–10; 2 Corinthians 11:27"> I have known both to be abased, and I have known to abound; in everything and in all things I have been initiated, both to be full and to be hungry, both to abound and to be in want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Colossians 1:11"> I have strength for all things, in Christ’s strengthening me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Philippians 1:7; Hebrews 10:34"> but you did well, having shared in my tribulation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 11:8–12; Acts 16:40–5"> and you have known, even you Philippians, that in the beginning of the good news when I went forth from Macedonia, no assembly communicated with me in regard to giving and receiving except you only;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; Acts 17:1; 1 Thessalonians 2:18"> because in Thessalonica also, both once and again you sent to my need;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Titus 3:14; Hebrews 6:10; 2 Corinthians 9:9–13"> not that I seek after the gift, but I seek after the fruit that is overflowing to your account;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Philippians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 9:12"> and I have all things, and abound; I am filled, having received from Epaphroditus the things from you—an odor of a refreshing fragrance—a sacrifice acceptable, well-pleasing to God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8–11; Psalms 84:11; 1 Peter 5:10"> and my God will supply all your need, according to His riches in glory in Christ Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Psalms 115:1; Revelation 14:7"> and to God, even our Father, &#91;is&#93; the glory through the ages of the ages. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 1:2; Philemon 1:23–24; Colossians 4:10–14"> Every holy one in Christ Jesus greets you; the brothers with me greet you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 1:13; 2 Corinthians 13:13; Romans 16:16"> all the holy ones greet you, and especially those of Caesar’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:14; Romans 16:20; Romans 16:23"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ &#91;is&#93; with you all! Amen.</span>
