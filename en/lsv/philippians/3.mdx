<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 5:11; Philippians 4:4; James 1:2"> As to the rest, my brothers, rejoice in the LORD; indeed, &#91;it&#93; is not tiresome to me to write to you the same things, and for you &#91;is&#93; sure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:13; Titus 1:16; Revelation 22:15"> Look out for the dogs! Look out for the evil-workers! Look out for the mutilation!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:11; Deuteronomy 30:6; Romans 7:6"> For we are the circumcision, who are serving God by the Spirit, and glorying in Christ Jesus, and having no trust in flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:18–22"> though I also have &#91;cause of&#93; trust in flesh. If any other one thinks to have trust in flesh, I more:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:22; Romans 11:1; Acts 23:6"> circumcision on the eighth day! Of the race of Israel! Of the tribe of Benjamin! A Hebrew of Hebrews! According to law—a Pharisee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 1:13–14; Acts 8:3; Acts 26:9–10"> According to zeal—persecuting the Assembly! According to righteousness that is in law—becoming blameless!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 14:33; Matthew 16:26; Matthew 13:44–46"> But what things were gains to me, these I have counted loss, because of the Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; Romans 8:18; Philippians 3:7"> yes, indeed, and I count all things to be loss, because of the excellence of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord, because of whom I suffered loss of all things, and count them to be refuse, that I may gain Christ, and be found in Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Galatians 2:16"> not having my righteousness, which &#91;is&#93; of law, but that which &#91;is&#93; through faith from Christ—the righteousness that is of God by faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; Romans 8:17; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> to know Him, and the power of His resurrection, and the fellowship of His sufferings, being conformed to His death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 20:35–36; Revelation 20:5; Acts 23:6"> if anyhow I may attain to the resurrection of the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:11–12; 2 Peter 1:5–8; Psalms 138:8"> Not that I already obtained, or have already been perfected, but I pursue, if I also may lay hold of that for which I was also laid hold of by Christ Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:62; Hebrews 12:1–2; Philippians 3:12"> brothers, I do not reckon myself to have laid hold &#91;of it&#93;, but one thing &#91;I do&#93;—indeed forgetting the things behind, and stretching forth to the things before—</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24; 1 Peter 5:10; Revelation 3:21"> I pursue to the mark for the prize of the high calling of God in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 7:17; James 1:4–5; Proverbs 2:3–6"> As many, therefore, as &#91;are&#93; perfect—let us think this, and if &#91;in&#93; anything you think otherwise, this also will God reveal to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 2:6; Revelation 3:3; Revelation 2:4–5"> but to what we have attained—walk by the same rule, think the same thing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Philippians 4:9; 1 Corinthians 4:16; 1 Peter 5:3"> together become my followers, brothers, and observe those thus walking, according as you have us—a pattern;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 4:17; Isaiah 8:11; Galatians 6:12"> for many walk of whom I told you &#91;about&#93; many times—and now also weeping tell—&#91;they are&#93; the enemies of the Cross of the Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 16:18; Romans 8:5–7; 2 Peter 2:3"> whose end &#91;is&#93; destruction, whose god &#91;is&#93; the belly, and whose glory &#91;is&#93; in their shame, who are minding the things on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 2:19; Colossians 3:1–3; 2 Corinthians 4:18–1"> For our citizenship is in the heavens, from where we also await a Savior—the Lord Jesus Christ—</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 3:2; Colossians 3:4; 1 Corinthians 15:42–56"> who will transform the body of our humiliation to its becoming conformed to the body of His glory, according to the working of His power, even to subject all things to Himself.</span>
