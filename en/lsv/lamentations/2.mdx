<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2; Psalms 132:7; Matthew 11:23"> &#91;ALEPH-BET&#93; How the Lord clouds in His anger the daughter of Zion, &#124;&#124; He has cast from the heavens &#91;to&#93; earth the beauty of Israel, &#124;&#124; And has not remembered His footstool in the day of His anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Lamentations 2:17; Psalms 89:39–40; Lamentations 3:43"> The Lord has swallowed up, &#124;&#124; He has not pitied any of the pleasant places of Jacob, &#124;&#124; He has broken down in His wrath &#124;&#124; The fortresses of the daughter of Judah, &#124;&#124; He has caused to come to the earth, &#124;&#124; He defiled the kingdom and its princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 74:11; Psalms 75:10; Psalms 75:5"> He has cut off in the heat of anger every horn of Israel, &#124;&#124; He has turned backward His right hand &#124;&#124; From the face of the enemy, &#124;&#124; And He burns against Jacob as a flaming fire, &#124;&#124; It has devoured all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 21:5; Lamentations 3:12–13"> He has bent His bow as an enemy, &#124;&#124; His right hand has stood as an adversary, &#124;&#124; And He slays all the desirable ones of the eye, &#124;&#124; In the tent of the daughter of Zion, &#124;&#124; He has poured out as fire His fury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 2:2; Jeremiah 30:14; Jeremiah 52:13"> The Lord has been as an enemy, &#124;&#124; He has swallowed up Israel, &#124;&#124; He has swallowed up all her palaces, &#124;&#124; He has destroyed His fortresses, &#124;&#124; And He multiplies in the daughter of Judah &#124;&#124; Mourning and moaning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Lamentations 1:4; Zephaniah 3:18; Lamentations 4:16"> And He shakes as a garden His dwelling place, &#124;&#124; He has destroyed His appointed place, &#124;&#124; YHWH has forgotten in Zion the appointed time and Sabbath, &#124;&#124; And despises, in the indignation of His anger, king and priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:21; Amos 2:5; Psalms 74:3–8"> The Lord has cast off His altar, &#124;&#124; He has rejected His sanctuary, &#124;&#124; He has shut up into the hand of the enemy &#124;&#124; The walls of her palaces, &#124;&#124; A noise they have made in the house of YHWH &#124;&#124; Like a day of appointment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Isaiah 34:11; Isaiah 3:26"> YHWH has devised to destroy the wall of the daughter of Zion, &#124;&#124; He has stretched out a line, &#124;&#124; He has not turned His hand from destroying, &#124;&#124; And He causes bulwark and wall to mourn, &#124;&#124; Together—they have been weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:26; Hosea 3:4; 2 Chronicles 15:3"> Sunk into the earth have her gates, &#124;&#124; He has destroyed and broken her bars, &#124;&#124; Her king and her princes &#91;are&#93; among the nations, &#124;&#124; There is no law, also her prophets &#124;&#124; Have not found vision from YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 3:26; Isaiah 15:3; Job 2:12–13"> Sit on the earth—&#91;the&#93; elderly of Zion’s daughter keep silent, &#124;&#124; They have caused dust to go up on their head, &#124;&#124; They have girded on sackcloth, &#124;&#124; The virgins of Jerusalem have &#124;&#124; Put their head down to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 1:20; Job 16:13; Lamentations 1:16"> My eyes have been consumed by tears, &#124;&#124; My bowels have been troubled, &#124;&#124; My liver has been poured out to the earth, &#124;&#124; For the breach of the daughter of my people; In infant and suckling being feeble, &#124;&#124; In the broad places of the city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Ezekiel 30:24"> To their mothers they say, &#124;&#124; “Where &#91;are&#93; grain and wine?” In their becoming feeble as a pierced one &#124;&#124; In the broad places of the city, &#124;&#124; In their soul pouring itself out into the bosom of their mothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Lamentations 1:12; Jeremiah 8:22; 2 Samuel 5:20"> What do I testify &#91;to&#93; you, what do I liken to you, &#124;&#124; O daughter of Jerusalem? What do I equal to you, and I comfort you, &#124;&#124; O virgin daughter of Zion? For great as a sea &#91;is&#93; your breach, &#124;&#124; Who gives healing to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:28; Isaiah 58:1; Jeremiah 5:31"> Your prophets have seen for you a false and insipid thing, &#124;&#124; And have not revealed concerning your iniquity, &#124;&#124; To return your captivity, &#124;&#124; And they see for you false burdens and causes of expulsion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:16; Psalms 48:2; Psalms 50:2"> Everyone passing by the way clapped hands at you, &#124;&#124; They have hissed—and they shake the head &#124;&#124; At the daughter of Jerusalem: “Is this the city of which they said: The perfection of beauty, a joy to all the land?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 35:21; Lamentations 3:46; Job 16:9–10"> Opened against you their mouth have all your enemies, &#124;&#124; They have hissed, indeed, they gnash the teeth, &#124;&#124; They have said: “We have swallowed &#91;her&#93; up, &#124;&#124; Surely this &#91;is&#93; the day that we looked for, &#124;&#124; We have found—we have seen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 89:42; Ezekiel 5:11; Deuteronomy 31:16–17"> YHWH has done that which He devised, &#124;&#124; He has fulfilled His saying &#124;&#124; That He commanded from the days of old, &#124;&#124; He has broken down and has not pitied, &#124;&#124; And causes an enemy to rejoice over you, &#124;&#124; He lifted up the horn of your adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:145; Jeremiah 9:1; Lamentations 2:8"> Their heart has cried to the Lord; O wall of the daughter of Zion, &#124;&#124; Cause to go down tears as a stream daily and nightly, do not give rest to yourself, &#124;&#124; Do not let the daughter of your eye stand still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Psalms 119:147–148; Isaiah 26:9"> Arise, cry aloud in the night, &#124;&#124; At the beginning of the watches. Pour out your heart as water, &#124;&#124; Before the face of the Lord, &#124;&#124; Lift up to Him your hands, for the soul of your infants, &#124;&#124; Who are feeble with hunger at the head of all out-places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:9; Lamentations 4:10; Psalms 78:64"> See, O YHWH, and look attentively, &#124;&#124; To whom You have acted thus, &#124;&#124; Do women eat their fruit, infants of a handbreadth? Slain in the sanctuary of the Lord are priest and prophet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:17; Lamentations 3:43; Zechariah 11:6"> Lain on the earth &#91;in&#93; out-places have young and old, &#124;&#124; My virgins and my young men have fallen by the sword, &#124;&#124; You have slain in a day of Your anger, &#124;&#124; You have slaughtered—You have not pitied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:25; Psalms 31:13; Jeremiah 16:2–4"> You call as &#91;at&#93; a day of appointment, &#124;&#124; My fears from all around, &#124;&#124; And there has not been in the day of the anger of YHWH, &#124;&#124; An escaped and remaining one, &#124;&#124; They whom I stretched out and nourished, &#124;&#124; My enemy has consumed!</span>
