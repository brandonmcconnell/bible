<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:19–22; Lamentations 2:19; Isaiah 1:21"> &#91;ALEPH-BET&#93; How the gold has become dim, &#124;&#124; Changed the best—the pure gold! Stones of the sanctuary are poured out &#124;&#124; At the head of all out-places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:14; Jeremiah 19:11; Romans 9:21–23"> The precious sons of Zion, &#124;&#124; Who are comparable with fine gold, &#124;&#124; How they have been reckoned earthen bottles, &#124;&#124; Work of the hands of a potter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:10; Luke 23:28–29; Lamentations 2:20"> Even dragons have drawn out the breast, &#124;&#124; They have suckled their young ones, &#124;&#124; The daughter of my people has become cruel, &#124;&#124; Like the ostriches in a wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 22:15; Lamentations 2:11–12; Matthew 7:9–11"> The tongue of a suckling has cleaved to his palate with thirst, &#124;&#124; Infants asked for bread, they have no dealer &#91;of it&#93; out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 6:3–7; Luke 16:19; Luke 15:16"> Those eating of delicacies have been desolate in out-places, &#124;&#124; Those supported on scarlet have embraced dunghills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 19:25; Luke 12:47; Luke 10:12"> And greater is the iniquity of the daughter of my people, &#124;&#124; Than the sin of Sodom, &#124;&#124; That was overturned as &#91;in&#93; a moment, &#124;&#124; And no hands were stayed on her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 51:7; Song of Songs 5:10; Psalms 144:12"> Purer were her Nazarites than snow, &#124;&#124; Whiter than milk, ruddier of body than rubies, &#124;&#124; Of sapphire their form.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 5:10; Psalms 102:3–5; Job 30:30"> Their face has been darker than blackness, &#124;&#124; They have not been known in out-places, &#124;&#124; Their skin has cleaved to their bone, &#124;&#124; It has withered—it has been as wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:23; Leviticus 26:39; Jeremiah 16:4"> Better have been the pierced of a sword &#124;&#124; Than the pierced of famine, &#124;&#124; For these flow away, pierced through, &#124;&#124; Without the increase of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 2:20; Isaiah 49:15; 2 Kings 6:26–29"> The hands of merciful women have boiled their own children, &#124;&#124; They have been for food to them, &#124;&#124; In the destruction of the daughter of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:20; Ezekiel 22:31; Jeremiah 17:27"> YHWH has completed His fury, &#124;&#124; He has poured out the fierceness of His anger, &#124;&#124; And He kindles a fire in Zion, &#124;&#124; And it devours her foundations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Deuteronomy 29:24–28; Psalms 48:4–6"> The kings of earth did not believe, &#124;&#124; And any of the inhabitants of the world, &#124;&#124; That an adversary and enemy would come &#124;&#124; Into the gates of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Jeremiah 6:13; Jeremiah 26:8–9"> Because of the sins of her prophets, &#124;&#124; The iniquities of her priests, &#124;&#124; Who are shedding in her midst the blood of the righteous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:34; Isaiah 56:10; Isaiah 1:15"> They have wandered naked in out-places, &#124;&#124; They have been defiled with blood, &#124;&#124; Without &#91;any&#93; being able to touch their clothing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46"> “Turn aside—unclean,” they called to them, &#124;&#124; “Turn aside, turn aside, do not touch,” &#124;&#124; For they fled—indeed, they have wandered, &#124;&#124; They have said among nations: “They do not add to sojourn.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 5:12; Isaiah 9:14–16; Deuteronomy 28:64–65"> The face of YHWH has divided them, &#124;&#124; He does not add to behold them, &#124;&#124; They have not lifted up the face of priests, &#124;&#124; They have not favored &#91;the&#93; old and elderly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:16; Isaiah 20:5; 2 Kings 24:7"> While we exist—consumed are our eyes for our vain help, &#124;&#124; In our watchtower we have watched for a nation &#91;that&#93; does not save.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 8:2; Ezekiel 7:2–12; Lamentations 3:52"> They have hunted our steps from going in our broad-places, &#124;&#124; Near has been our end, fulfilled our days, &#124;&#124; For our end has come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Jeremiah 4:13; Isaiah 5:26–28"> Swifter have been our pursuers, &#124;&#124; Than the eagles of the heavens, &#124;&#124; On the mountains they have burned &#91;after&#93; us, &#124;&#124; In the wilderness they have laid wait for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:21; Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 39:5"> The breath of our nostrils—the anointed of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Has been captured in their pits, of whom we said: “We live among nations in his shadow.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Amos 1:11; Revelation 16:15; Job 1:1"> Rejoice and be glad, O daughter of Edom, &#124;&#124; Dwelling in the land of Uz, &#124;&#124; Even to you a cup passes over, &#124;&#124; You are drunk, and make yourself naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 40:2; Psalms 137:7; Jeremiah 50:20"> Completed &#91;is&#93; your iniquity, daughter of Zion, &#124;&#124; He does not add to remove you, &#124;&#124; He has inspected your iniquity, O daughter of Edom, &#124;&#124; He has removed &#91;you&#93; because of your sins!</span>
