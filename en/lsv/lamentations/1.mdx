<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 3:26; Ezra 4:20; 1 Kings 4:21"> &#91;ALEPH-BET&#93; How she has sat alone, &#124;&#124; The city abounding with people! She has been as a widow, &#124;&#124; The mighty among nations! Princes among provinces, &#124;&#124; She has become tributary!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Micah 7:5; Psalms 77:2–6"> She weeps severely in the night, &#124;&#124; And her tear &#91;is&#93; on her cheeks, &#124;&#124; There is no comforter for her out of all her lovers, &#124;&#124; All her friends dealt treacherously by her, &#124;&#124; They have been to her for enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64–67; Lamentations 2:9; Amos 9:1–4"> Removed has Judah because of affliction, &#124;&#124; And because of the abundance of her service; She has dwelt among nations, &#124;&#124; She has not found rest, &#124;&#124; All her pursuers have overtaken her between the straits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Joel 1:8–13; Jeremiah 10:22"> The ways of Zion are mourning, &#124;&#124; Without any coming at the appointed time, &#124;&#124; All her gates are desolate, her priests sigh, &#124;&#124; Her virgins are afflicted—and she has bitterness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15–27; Jeremiah 30:14–15; Ezekiel 9:9"> Her adversaries have become chief, &#124;&#124; Her enemies have been at ease, &#124;&#124; For YHWH has afflicted her, &#124;&#124; For the abundance of her transgressions, &#124;&#124; Her infants have gone captive before the adversary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:25; Jeremiah 13:18; Deuteronomy 28:25"> And all her honor goes out from the daughter of Zion, &#124;&#124; Her princes have been as harts—They have not found pasture, &#124;&#124; And they go powerless before a pursuer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Deuteronomy 4:34–37; Psalms 42:4"> Jerusalem has remembered &#124;&#124; &#91;In&#93; the days of her affliction and her mournings, all her desirable things that were from the days of old, &#124;&#124; In the falling of her people into the hand of an adversary, &#124;&#124; And she has no helper; Adversaries have seen her, &#124;&#124; They have laughed at her cessation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 59:2–13; Lamentations 1:20–22; Jeremiah 13:26"> A sin has Jerusalem sinned, &#124;&#124; Therefore she has become impure, &#124;&#124; All who honored her have esteemed her lightly, &#124;&#124; For they have seen her nakedness, &#124;&#124; Indeed, she herself has sighed and turns backward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Isaiah 47:7; Psalms 25:18"> Her uncleanness &#91;is&#93; in her skirts, &#124;&#124; She has not remembered her latter end, &#124;&#124; And she comes down wonderfully, &#124;&#124; There is no comforter for her. See, O YHWH, my affliction, &#124;&#124; For an enemy has exerted himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:3; Jeremiah 51:51; Psalms 74:4–8"> His hand has spread out an adversary &#124;&#124; On all her desirable things, &#124;&#124; For she has seen—Nations have entered her sanctuary, &#124;&#124; Concerning which You commanded, &#124;&#124; “They do not come into the assembly to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:6; Jeremiah 38:9; Lamentations 2:12"> All her people are sighing—seeking bread, &#124;&#124; They have given their desirable things &#124;&#124; For food to refresh the body; See, O YHWH, and behold attentively, &#124;&#124; For I have been lightly esteemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 23:28–31; Daniel 9:12; Jeremiah 30:24"> &#91;Is it&#93; nothing to you, all you passing by the way? Look attentively, and see, &#124;&#124; If there is any pain like my pain, &#124;&#124; That He is rolling to me? Whom YHWH has afflicted &#124;&#124; In the day of the fierceness of His anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 30:30; Habakkuk 3:16; Ezekiel 17:20"> From above He has sent fire into my bone, &#124;&#124; And it subdues it, He has spread a net for my feet, &#124;&#124; He has turned me backward, &#124;&#124; He has made me desolate—all the day sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:48; Isaiah 47:6; Proverbs 5:22"> Bound has been the yoke of my transgressions by His hand, &#124;&#124; They are wrapped together, &#124;&#124; They have gone up on my neck, &#124;&#124; He has caused my power to stumble, &#124;&#124; The Lord has given me into hands, I am not able to rise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Isaiah 28:18; Isaiah 63:3"> The Lord has trodden down all my mighty ones in my midst, &#124;&#124; He proclaimed an appointed time against me, &#124;&#124; To destroy my young men, &#124;&#124; The Lord has trodden a winepress, &#124;&#124; To the virgin daughter of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 1:2; Jeremiah 13:17; Psalms 119:136"> For these I am weeping, &#124;&#124; My eye, my eye, is running down with waters, &#124;&#124; For a comforter has been far from me, &#124;&#124; Refreshing my soul, &#124;&#124; My sons have been desolate, &#124;&#124; For mighty has been an enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; Jeremiah 4:31; Lamentations 1:9"> Zion has spread forth her hands, &#124;&#124; There is no comforter for her, &#124;&#124; YHWH has charged concerning Jacob, &#124;&#124; His neighbors &#91;are&#93; his adversaries, &#124;&#124; Jerusalem has become impure among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:14–15; Zephaniah 3:5; Psalms 119:75"> YHWH is righteous, &#124;&#124; For I have provoked His mouth. Now hear, all you peoples, and see my pain, &#124;&#124; My virgins and my young men have gone into captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Lamentations 1:2; Lamentations 1:11; Lamentations 2:20"> I called for my lovers, they have deceived me, &#124;&#124; My priests and my elderly have expired in the city; When they have sought food for themselves, &#124;&#124; Then they give back their soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Deuteronomy 32:25; Isaiah 16:11"> See, O YHWH, for distress &#91;is&#93; to me, &#124;&#124; My bowels have been troubled, &#124;&#124; My heart has been turned in my midst, &#124;&#124; For I have greatly provoked, &#124;&#124; From outside the sword has bereaved, &#124;&#124; In the house &#91;it is&#93; as death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Lamentations 1:8; Lamentations 2:15; Lamentations 1:16"> They have heard that I have sighed, &#124;&#124; There is no comforter for me, &#124;&#124; All my enemies have heard of my calamity, &#124;&#124; They have rejoiced that You have done &#91;it&#93;, &#124;&#124; You have brought in the day You have called, &#124;&#124; And they are like to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:18; Psalms 109:14–15; Revelation 6:10"> All their evil comes in before You, &#124;&#124; And one is doing to them as You have done to me, &#124;&#124; For all my transgressions, &#124;&#124; For many &#91;are&#93; my sighs, and my heart &#91;is&#93; sick!</span>
