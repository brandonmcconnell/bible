<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 88:7; Job 19:21; Psalms 88:15–16"> &#91;ALEPH-BET&#93; I &#91;am&#93; the man &#91;who&#93; has seen affliction &#124;&#124; By the rod of His wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 30:26; Isaiah 59:9; Amos 5:18–20"> He has led me, and causes to go &#91;in&#93; darkness, and without light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Job 31:21; Isaiah 1:25"> Surely against me He turns back, &#124;&#124; He turns His hand all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 38:13; Psalms 51:8; Psalms 31:9–10"> He has worn out my flesh and my skin. He has broken my bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 3:19; Jeremiah 23:15; Job 19:8"> He has built up against me, &#124;&#124; And sets around poverty and weariness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 88:5–6; Psalms 143:3; Psalms 143:7"> In dark places He has caused me to dwell, &#124;&#124; As the dead of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 19:8; Job 3:23; Jeremiah 40:4"> He has hedged me in, and I do not go out, &#124;&#124; He has made heavy my chain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 30:20; Psalms 22:2; Job 19:7"> Also when I call and cry out, &#124;&#124; He has shut out my prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 63:17; Lamentations 3:11; Isaiah 30:28"> He has hedged my ways with hewn work, &#124;&#124; My paths He has made crooked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 10:9; Amos 5:18–20; Hosea 13:7–8"> A bear lying in wait He &#91;is&#93; to me, &#124;&#124; A lion in secret hiding places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 6:1; Job 16:12–13; Lamentations 1:13"> My ways He is turning aside, and He pulls me in pieces, &#124;&#124; He has made me a desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 38:2; Job 7:20; Job 6:4"> He has bent His bow, &#124;&#124; And sets me up as a mark for an arrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 6:4; Job 41:28; Deuteronomy 32:23"> He has caused to enter into my reins &#124;&#124; The sons of His quiver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:7; Lamentations 3:63; Psalms 22:6–7"> I have been a derision to all my people, &#124;&#124; Their song all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:15; Jeremiah 23:15; Ruth 1:20"> He has filled me with bitter things, &#124;&#124; He has filled me &#91;with&#93; wormwood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 20:17; Jeremiah 6:26; Psalms 3:7"> And He breaks with gravel my teeth, &#124;&#124; He has covered me with ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 59:11; Zechariah 8:10; Jeremiah 8:15"> And You cast off my soul from peace, &#124;&#124; I have forgotten prosperity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 17:15; 1 Samuel 27:1; Psalms 116:11"> And I say, My strength and my hope have perished from YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Lamentations 3:15; Lamentations 3:5; Psalms 89:50"> Remember my affliction and my mourning, &#124;&#124; Wormwood and gall!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 42:11; Psalms 42:5–6; Job 21:6"> Remember well, and my soul bows down in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; Psalms 130:7; Psalms 77:7–11"> This I turn to my heart—therefore I hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Luke 1:50; Nehemiah 9:31"> The kindnesses of YHWH! For we have not been consumed, &#124;&#124; For His mercies have not ended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 10:23; Psalms 30:5; Isaiah 33:2"> New every morning, abundant &#91;is&#93; Your faithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Psalms 16:5; Lamentations 3:21"> My portion &#91;is&#93; YHWH, my soul has said, &#124;&#124; Therefore I hope for Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Micah 7:7–8; Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 130:5–6"> YHWH &#91;is&#93; good to those waiting for Him, &#124;&#124; To the soul &#91;that&#93; seeks Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 37:34; Psalms 130:5"> &#91;It is&#93; good when one stays and stands still &#124;&#124; For the salvation of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:1; Matthew 11:29–30; Psalms 119:71"> &#91;It is&#93; good for a man that he bears a yoke in his youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:17; Lamentations 2:10; Psalms 102:7"> He sits alone, and is silent, &#124;&#124; For He has laid &#91;it&#93; on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 40:4; Luke 15:18–19; Romans 3:19"> He puts his mouth in the dust, if so be, there is hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 16:10; Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 26:67"> He gives to his striker the cheek, &#124;&#124; He is filled with reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 94:14; Psalms 103:8–10; Psalms 77:7"> For the Lord does not cast off for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 78:38; Psalms 30:5; Psalms 103:11"> For though He afflicted, yet He has pitied, &#124;&#124; According to the abundance of His kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:11; Ezekiel 18:32; Hebrews 12:9–10"> For He has not afflicted with His heart, &#124;&#124; Nor does He grieve the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11–12; Isaiah 51:22–23; Psalms 102:20"> To bruise under one’s feet any bound ones of earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> To turn aside the judgment of a man, &#124;&#124; Before the face of the Most High,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 59:15; Habakkuk 1:13; Jeremiah 22:3"> To subvert a man in his cause, the Lord has not approved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Psalms 33:9–11; Isaiah 46:10; Proverbs 16:9"> Who &#91;is&#93; this—he has spoken, and it is, &#124;&#124; &#91;And&#93; the Lord has not commanded &#91;it&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Amos 3:6; Jeremiah 32:42"> From the mouth of the Most High does not go forth the evils and the good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 19:3; Micah 7:9; Genesis 4:5–7"> Why does a living man sigh habitually, &#124;&#124; A man for his sin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:5; Psalms 139:23–24; Joel 2:12–13"> We search our ways, and investigate, &#124;&#124; And turn back to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 25:1; Psalms 28:2; Psalms 86:4"> We lift up our heart on the hands to God in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 15:18–19; Nehemiah 9:26; 2 Kings 24:4"> We have transgressed and been rebellious, &#124;&#124; You have not forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Lamentations 2:17; Lamentations 2:21; Lamentations 3:66"> You have covered Yourself with anger, &#124;&#124; And pursue us; You have slain—You have not pitied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 3:8; Zechariah 7:13; Psalms 97:2"> You have covered Yourself with a cloud, &#124;&#124; So that prayer does not pass through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:13; Lamentations 2:15; Lamentations 3:14"> Outcast and refuse You make us &#124;&#124; In the midst of the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 22:6–8; Lamentations 2:16; Psalms 44:13–14"> Opened against us their mouth have all our enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Isaiah 24:17–18; Isaiah 51:19; Jeremiah 48:43–44"> Fear and a snare has been for us, &#124;&#124; Desolation and destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Jeremiah 9:18; Jeremiah 9:1"> Streams of water go down my eye, &#124;&#124; For the destruction of the daughter of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:17; Psalms 77:2; Lamentations 1:16"> My eye is poured out, &#124;&#124; And does not cease without intermission,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Isaiah 63:15; Lamentations 5:1; Daniel 9:16–19"> Until YHWH looks and sees from the heavens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:9; Luke 19:41–44; Lamentations 1:18"> My eye affects my soul, &#124;&#124; Because of all the daughters of my city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Psalms 35:7; Jeremiah 37:15–16"> Hunting—my enemies have hunted me without cause like the bird.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Daniel 6:17; Jeremiah 38:9; Jeremiah 38:6"> They have cut off my life in a pit, &#124;&#124; And they cast a stone against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Jonah 2:3–5; Ezekiel 37:11; Lamentations 3:18"> Waters have flowed over my head, I have said, I have been cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Acts 16:24–28; Psalms 142:3–7; Jonah 2:2–4"> I called Your Name, O YHWH, from the lower pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Isaiah 38:5; Job 34:28; Psalms 6:8–9"> You have heard my voice, do not hide Your ear at my breathing—at my cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 41:10; Isaiah 58:9"> You have drawn near in the day I call You, You have said, Do not fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Psalms 34:22–1; Jeremiah 51:36; Psalms 103:4"> You have pleaded, O Lord, the pleadings of my soul, &#124;&#124; You have redeemed my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 9:4; Genesis 31:42"> You have seen, O YHWH, my overthrow, &#124;&#124; Judge my cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:19–20; Psalms 10:14; Lamentations 3:59"> You have seen all their vengeance, &#124;&#124; All their thoughts of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Lamentations 5:1; Psalms 89:50; Zephaniah 2:8"> You have heard their reproach, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; All their thoughts against me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:3; Psalms 59:7; Psalms 140:3"> The lips of my withstanders, &#124;&#124; Even their meditation against me all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Psalms 139:2; Lamentations 3:14; Job 30:9"> Their sitting down, and their rising up, &#124;&#124; Behold attentively, I &#91;am&#93; their song.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Psalms 28:4; Jeremiah 11:20; 2 Timothy 4:14"> You return to them the deed, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; According to the work of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; 1 Corinthians 16:22; Deuteronomy 2:30"> You give to them a covered heart, &#124;&#124; Your curse to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Psalms 8:3; Lamentations 3:43; Psalms 115:16"> You pursue in anger, and destroy them, &#124;&#124; From under the heavens of YHWH!</span>
