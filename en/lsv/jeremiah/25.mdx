<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:1; 2 Kings 24:1–2; Daniel 1:1"> The word that has been to Jeremiah concerning all the people of Judah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah—it &#91;is&#93; the first year of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:11; Jeremiah 19:14–15; Mark 7:14–15"> Which Jeremiah the prophet has spoken concerning all the people of Judah, even to all the inhabitants of Jerusalem, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:7; Jeremiah 26:5; Jeremiah 1:2"> “From the thirteenth year of Josiah son of Amon king of Judah, and to this day—this twenty-third year—the word of YHWH has been to me, and I speak to you, rising early and speaking, and you have not listened;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:3; Jeremiah 26:5; Jeremiah 13:10–11"> and YHWH has sent all His servants to you, the prophets, rising early and sending, and you have not listened, nor inclined your ear to hear, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:7; Genesis 17:8; Isaiah 55:6–7"> Please turn back, each from his evil way, and from the evil of your doings, and dwell on the ground that YHWH has given to you and to your fathers from age to age,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:19; Jeremiah 7:6; Jeremiah 35:15"> and you do not go after other gods to serve them, and to bow yourselves to them, nor do you provoke Me to anger with the work of your hands, and I do no evil to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 21:15; Proverbs 8:36; Deuteronomy 32:21"> and you have not listened to Me,” a declaration of YHWH, “so as to provoke Me to anger with the work of your hands for evil to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Therefore, thus said YHWH of Hosts: Because that you have not obeyed My words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 13:3; Jeremiah 18:16; Jeremiah 1:15"> behold, I am sending, and have taken all the families of the north—a declaration of YHWH—even to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, My servant, and have brought them in against this land, and against its inhabitants, and against all these surrounding nations, and have devoted them, and appointed them for an astonishment, and for a hissing, and for continuous ruins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Revelation 18:22–23; Ezekiel 26:13"> And I have destroyed from them the voice of rejoicing, and the voice of joy, voice of bridegroom and voice of bride, noise of millstones, and the light of lamps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 9:2; Zechariah 1:12; 2 Chronicles 36:21–22"> And all this land has been for a desolation, for an astonishment, and these nations have served the king of Babylon seventy years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 9:2; Isaiah 13:19; Jeremiah 29:10"> And it has come to pass, at the fullness of seventy years, I charge against the king of Babylon, and against that nation,” a declaration of YHWH, “their iniquity, and against the land of the Chaldeans, and have appointed it for continuous desolations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 1:5; Daniel 5:31"> And I have brought in on that land all My words that I have spoken against it, all that is written in this scroll, that Jeremiah has prophesied concerning all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:6; Jeremiah 27:7; Jeremiah 50:9"> For laid service on them—also them—have many nations and great kings, and I have given repayment to them according to their doing, and according to the work of their hands.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 75:8; Isaiah 51:17; Revelation 14:10"> For thus said YHWH, God of Israel, to me: “Take the wine cup of this fury out of My hand, and you have caused all the nations to drink it to whom I am sending you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nahum 3:11; Jeremiah 51:7; Jeremiah 51:39"> and they have drunk, and shaken themselves and shown themselves &#91;to be&#93; foolish, because of the sword that I am sending among them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 25:28; Ezekiel 43:3"> And I take the cup out of the hand of YHWH, and cause all the nations to drink to whom YHWH sent me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; Psalms 60:3; Jeremiah 44:22"> Jerusalem, and the cities of Judah, &#124;&#124; And its kings, its heads, &#124;&#124; To give them to ruin, to astonishment, &#124;&#124; To hissing, and to reviling, as &#91;at&#93; this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:13–26; Nahum 3:8–10; Jeremiah 46:2"> Pharaoh king of Egypt, and his servants, &#124;&#124; And his heads, and all his people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 1:1; Ezekiel 30:5; Isaiah 20:1"> And all the mixed people, &#124;&#124; And all the kings of the land of Uz, &#124;&#124; And all the kings of the land of the Philistines, &#124;&#124; And Ashkelon, and Gaza, and Ekron, &#124;&#124; And the remnant of Ashdod,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:1–22; Psalms 137:7; Jeremiah 9:26"> Edom, and Moab, and the sons of Ammon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:4; Amos 1:9–10; Ezekiel 28:22–23"> And all the kings of Tyre, &#124;&#124; And all the kings of Sidon, &#124;&#124; And the kings of the island that &#91;is&#93; beyond the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:26; Jeremiah 49:32; Jeremiah 49:8"> Dedan, and Tema, and Buz, &#124;&#124; And all cutting the corners &#91;of the beard&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:14; Jeremiah 25:20; Ezekiel 30:5"> And all the kings of Arabia, &#124;&#124; And all the kings of the mixed people, &#124;&#124; Who are dwelling in the wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 11:11; Genesis 10:22; Isaiah 13:17"> And all the kings of Zimri, &#124;&#124; And all the kings of Elam, &#124;&#124; And all the kings of Media,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:41; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 50:1–46"> And all the kings of the north, &#124;&#124; The near and the far off to one another, &#124;&#124; And all the kingdoms of the earth, &#124;&#124; That &#91;are&#93; on the face of the ground, &#124;&#124; And King Sheshach drinks after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:16; Habakkuk 2:16; Ezekiel 21:4–5"> “And you have said to them, Thus said YHWH of Hosts, God of Israel: Drink, indeed drink abundantly, &#124;&#124; And vomit, and fall, and do not rise, &#124;&#124; Because of the sword that I am sending among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 34:33; Jeremiah 51:29; Isaiah 14:24–27"> And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; When they refuse to receive the cup out of your hand to drink, &#124;&#124; That you have said to them, Thus said YHWH of Hosts: You certainly drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:21; Proverbs 11:31; 1 Peter 4:17"> For behold, in the city over which My Name is called, &#124;&#124; I am beginning to bring ruin, &#124;&#124; And are you entirely acquitted? You are not acquitted, for I am proclaiming a sword, &#124;&#124; For all inhabitants of the land,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Amos 1:2; Joel 3:16; Isaiah 42:13"> “And you, you prophesy all these words to them, and have said to them: YHWH roars from the high place, &#124;&#124; And gives forth His voice from His holy habitation, &#124;&#124; He surely roars for His habitation, &#124;&#124; A shout as those treading &#91;the grapes&#93;, &#124;&#124; God answers all the inhabitants of the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Joel 3:2; Isaiah 66:16"> A rumbling has come to the end of the earth, &#124;&#124; For YHWH has a controversy with nations, &#124;&#124; He has executed judgment for all flesh, &#124;&#124; He has given the wicked to the sword,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:19; Isaiah 34:2; 2 Chronicles 15:6"> Thus said YHWH of Hosts: “Behold, evil is going out from nation to nation, &#124;&#124; And a great whirlwind is stirred up from the sides of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 66:16; Jeremiah 8:2; Psalms 79:3"> And the pierced of YHWH have been in that day, &#124;&#124; From the end of the earth even to the end of the earth, &#124;&#124; They are not lamented, nor gathered, nor buried, &#124;&#124; For they are dung on the face of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Jeremiah 50:27; Psalms 2:9"> Howl, you shepherds, and cry, &#124;&#124; And roll yourselves, you majestic of the flock, &#124;&#124; For your days have been full &#124;&#124; For slaughtering, and &#91;for&#93; your scatterings, &#124;&#124; And you have fallen as a desirable vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 11:20; Amos 2:14; Jeremiah 52:8–11"> And refuge has perished from the shepherds, &#124;&#124; And escape from the majestic of the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:8; Jeremiah 25:34"> A voice of the cry of the shepherds, &#124;&#124; And a howling of the majestic of the flock, &#124;&#124; For YHWH is spoiling their pasture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 27:10–11; Isaiah 32:14"> And the peaceable habitations have been cut down, &#124;&#124; Because of the fierceness of the anger of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 5:6; Hosea 13:7–8"> He has forsaken His covert as a young lion, &#124;&#124; Surely their land has become a desolation, &#124;&#124; Because of the oppressing fierceness, &#124;&#124; And because of the fierceness of His anger!”</span>
