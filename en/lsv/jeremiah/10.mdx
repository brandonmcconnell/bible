<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Jeremiah 42:15; Amos 7:16"> Hear the word, O house of Israel, &#124;&#124; That YHWH has spoken for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Leviticus 18:3; Luke 21:25–28"> Thus said YHWH: “Do not accustom yourselves to the way of the nations, &#124;&#124; And do not be frightened by the signs of the heavens, &#124;&#124; For the nations are frightened by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 44:9–20; Leviticus 18:30; 1 Peter 1:18"> For the statutes of the peoples are vanity, &#124;&#124; For one has cut a tree from a forest, &#124;&#124; Work of the hands of a craftsman, with an axe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 46:7; Isaiah 40:19–20; Psalms 115:4"> They beautify it with silver and with gold, &#124;&#124; They fix it with nails and with hammers, &#124;&#124; And it does not stumble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 46:7; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Isaiah 41:23–24"> They &#91;are&#93; stiff as a palm, and they do not speak, &#124;&#124; They are surely carried, for they do not step, &#124;&#124; Do not be afraid of them, for they do no evil, &#124;&#124; Indeed, to do good is also not in them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 96:4; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 48:1"> Because there is none like You, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; You &#91;are&#93; great, and Your Name &#91;is&#93; great in might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 15:4; Psalms 22:28; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> Who does not fear You, King of the nations? For it is befitting to You, &#124;&#124; For among all the wise of the nations, &#124;&#124; And in all their kingdom, there is none like You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 10:2; Jeremiah 10:14; Psalms 115:8"> And as one they are brutish and foolish, &#124;&#124; An instruction of vanities &#91;is&#93; the tree itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 115:4; Daniel 10:5; 1 Kings 10:22"> Spread-out silver is brought from Tarshish, &#124;&#124; And gold from Uphaz, &#124;&#124; Work of an artisan, and of the hands of a refiner, &#124;&#124; Their clothing &#91;is&#93; blue and purple, &#124;&#124; Work of the skillful—all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Isaiah 57:15; Nahum 1:6"> And YHWH &#91;is&#93; a God of truth, &#124;&#124; He &#91;is&#93; a living God, and a perpetual King, &#124;&#124; The earth shakes from His wrath, &#124;&#124; And nations do not endure His indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 96:5; Isaiah 2:18; Lamentations 3:66"> Thus you say to them, “The gods who have not made the heavens and earth, &#124;&#124; They perish from the earth, &#124;&#124; And from under these heavens.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; Job 9:8; Proverbs 3:19"> The Maker of the earth by His power, &#124;&#124; The Establisher of the world by His wisdom, &#124;&#124; Who, by His understanding, stretched forth the heavens—</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Job 38:22; Job 38:34–35"> When He gives forth His voice, &#124;&#124; A multitude of waters &#91;is&#93; in the heavens, &#124;&#124; And He causes vapors to come up from the end of the earth, &#124;&#124; He has made lightnings for rain, &#124;&#124; And brings out wind from His treasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 135:16–18; Psalms 115:4–8; Isaiah 46:7–8"> Every man is brutish by knowledge, &#124;&#124; Every refiner is put to shame by a carved image, &#124;&#124; For his molten image &#91;is&#93; false. And there is no breath in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:22; Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 51:18"> They &#91;are&#93; vanity, work of erring ones, &#124;&#124; They perish in the time of their inspection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:9; Jeremiah 32:18; Jeremiah 31:35"> The Portion of Jacob &#91;is&#93; not like these, &#124;&#124; For He &#91;is&#93; the Framer of all things, &#124;&#124; And Israel &#91;is&#93; the rod of His inheritance, &#124;&#124; YHWH of Hosts &#91;is&#93; His Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:3–12; Jeremiah 6:1; Matthew 24:15"> Gather your merchandise from the land, &#124;&#124; O dweller in the bulwark,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:29; Zechariah 1:6; Deuteronomy 28:63–64"> For thus said YHWH: “Behold, I am slinging out the inhabitants of the land at this time, &#124;&#124; And have been an adversary to them, &#124;&#124; So that they are found out.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 7:9; Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 14:17"> Woe to me for my breaking, &#124;&#124; My striking has been grievous, &#124;&#124; And I said, “Surely this &#91;is&#93; my sickness, and I bear it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; Jeremiah 31:15; Isaiah 54:2"> My tent has been spoiled, &#124;&#124; And all my cords have been broken, &#124;&#124; My sons have gone out from me, and they are not, &#124;&#124; There is none stretching out my tent anymore, &#124;&#124; And raising up my curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2–10; Jeremiah 12:10; Jeremiah 23:1–2"> For the shepherds have become brutish, &#124;&#124; And they have not sought YHWH, &#124;&#124; Therefore they have not acted wisely, &#124;&#124; And all their flock is scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Habakkuk 1:6–9; Jeremiah 6:22"> A voice of a report, behold, it has come, &#124;&#124; Even a great shaking from the north country, &#124;&#124; To make the cities of Judah a desolation, &#124;&#124; A habitation of dragons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 20:24; Proverbs 16:1; Psalms 37:23"> I have known, O YHWH, that not of man &#91;is&#93; his way, &#124;&#124; Not of man &#91;is&#93; the going and establishing of his step.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 38:1; Psalms 6:1; Jeremiah 30:11"> Discipline me, O YHWH, only in judgment, &#124;&#124; Not in Your anger, lest You make me small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 79:6–7; Jeremiah 8:16; Job 18:21"> Pour out Your fury on the nations that have not known You, &#124;&#124; And on the families that have not called on Your Name, &#124;&#124; For they have eaten up Jacob, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they have eaten him up, indeed, they consume him, &#124;&#124; And they have made his habitation desolate!</span>
