<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 2:12; Jeremiah 3:22; Jeremiah 3:12"> “If you return, O Israel,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “Return to Me, &#124;&#124; And if you turn aside Your abominations from My face, &#124;&#124; Then you do not bemoan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 65:16; Jeremiah 9:24; Genesis 22:18"> And you have sworn—YHWH lives, &#124;&#124; In truth, in judgment, and in righteousness, &#124;&#124; And nations have blessed themselves in Him, &#124;&#124; And they boast themselves in Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Luke 8:7; Luke 8:14"> For thus said YHWH, &#124;&#124; To the man of Judah, and to Jerusalem: “Till for yourselves tillage, &#124;&#124; And do not sow to the thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:16; Deuteronomy 30:6; Romans 2:28–29"> Be circumcised to YHWH, &#124;&#124; And turn aside the foreskins of your heart, &#124;&#124; O man of Judah, and you inhabitants of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; Lest My fury goes out as fire, and has burned, &#124;&#124; And there is none quenching, &#124;&#124; Because of the evil of your doings.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:14; Jeremiah 6:1; Joshua 10:20"> Declare in Judah, and sound in Jerusalem, and say: “Blow a horn in the land, &#124;&#124; Call, Fill up together, &#124;&#124; And say, Assemble yourselves and let us go into the fortified cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:2; Jeremiah 1:13–15; Jeremiah 51:27"> Lift up an ensign toward Zion, &#124;&#124; Strengthen yourselves, do not stand still, &#124;&#124; For I am bringing evil in from the north, &#124;&#124; And a great destruction.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; Jeremiah 5:6; Isaiah 6:11"> A lion has gone up from his thicket, &#124;&#124; And a destroyer of nations has journeyed, &#124;&#124; He has come forth from his place &#124;&#124; To make your land become a desolation, &#124;&#124; Your cities are laid waste, without inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 22:12; Jeremiah 6:26; Isaiah 10:4"> For this, gird on sackcloth, lament and howl, &#124;&#124; For the fierce anger of YHWH has not turned back from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:9–16; Isaiah 29:9–10; Isaiah 22:3–5"> “And it has come to pass in that day,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “The heart of the king perishes, &#124;&#124; And the heart of the princes, &#124;&#124; And the priests have been astonished, &#124;&#124; And the prophets wonder.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–12; Isaiah 37:35"> And I say, “Aah! Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; Surely you have entirely forgotten this people and Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; Saying, Peace is for you, &#124;&#124; And a sword has struck to the soul!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 13:15; Ezekiel 17:10; Jeremiah 23:19"> At that time it is said of this people, &#124;&#124; And of Jerusalem: “A dry wind of high places in the wilderness, &#124;&#124; The way of the daughter of My people &#124;&#124; (Not for winnowing, nor for cleansing),</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:16; Ezekiel 5:8; Ezekiel 6:11–13"> A full wind from these comes for Me, &#124;&#124; Now also, I speak judgments with them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 19:1; Isaiah 66:15; Habakkuk 1:8"> “Behold, he comes up as clouds, &#124;&#124; And his chariots as a windstorm, &#124;&#124; His horses have been lighter than eagles, &#124;&#124; Woe to us, for we have been spoiled.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 55:7; Acts 8:22"> Wash evil from your heart, O Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; That you may be saved, &#124;&#124; Until when do you lodge in your heart &#124;&#124; Thoughts of your strength?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:16; Joshua 17:15; Judges 18:29"> For a voice is declaring from Dan, &#124;&#124; And sounding sorrow from Mount Ephraim:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:15; Ezekiel 21:22; Isaiah 39:3"> “Make mention to the nations, &#124;&#124; Behold, sound to Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; Besiegers are coming from the far-off land, &#124;&#124; And they give forth their voice against cities of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:23; 2 Kings 25:1–4; Nehemiah 9:30"> As the keepers of a field &#124;&#124; They have been against her all around, &#124;&#124; For she has been rebellious with Me,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:19; Psalms 107:17; Proverbs 1:31"> “Your way and your doings have done these to you, &#124;&#124; This &#91;is&#93; your distress, for &#91;it is&#93; bitter, &#124;&#124; For it has struck to your heart.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Isaiah 16:11; Isaiah 22:4"> My bowels, my bowels! I am pained &#91;in&#93; the walls of my heart, &#124;&#124; My heart makes a noise for me, I am not silent, &#124;&#124; For I have heard the voice of a horn, &#124;&#124; O my soul—a shout of battle!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Matthew 10:28; Joel 1:15"> Destruction on destruction is proclaimed, &#124;&#124; For all the land has been spoiled, &#124;&#124; My tents have suddenly been spoiled, &#124;&#124; In a moment—my curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:10; 2 Chronicles 36:3; 2 Chronicles 36:6–7"> Until when do I see an ensign? Do I hear the voice of a horn?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; Romans 16:19; Jeremiah 5:21"> “For My people &#91;are&#93; foolish, &#124;&#124; They have not known Me, &#124;&#124; They &#91;are&#93; foolish sons, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they &#91;are&#93; not intelligent, &#124;&#124; They &#91;are&#93; wise to do evil, &#124;&#124; And they have not known to do good.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Mark 13:24–25; Isaiah 13:10"> I looked &#91;to&#93; the earth, and behold—formless and void, &#124;&#124; And to the heavens, and they &#91;had&#93; no light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:20; Isaiah 5:25; Nahum 1:5–6"> I have looked &#91;to&#93; the mountains, &#124;&#124; And behold, they are trembling. And all the hills moved themselves lightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:2–3; Jeremiah 12:4; Jeremiah 9:10"> I have looked, and behold, man is not, &#124;&#124; And all birds of the heavens have fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Jeremiah 12:4; Jeremiah 14:2–6"> I have looked, and behold, &#124;&#124; The fruitful place &#91;is&#93; a wilderness, &#124;&#124; And all its cities have been broken down, &#124;&#124; Because of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Because of the fierceness of His anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:28; Jeremiah 5:10; Jeremiah 5:18"> For thus said YHWH: “All the land is a desolation, but I do not make a completion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 50:3; Hosea 4:3"> For this the earth mourns, &#124;&#124; And the heavens above have been black, &#124;&#124; Because I have spoken—I have purposed, &#124;&#124; And I have not relented, &#124;&#124; Nor do I turn back from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19–21; Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 52:7"> From the voice of the horseman, &#124;&#124; And of him shooting with the bow, &#124;&#124; All the city is fleeing, &#124;&#124; They have come into thickets, &#124;&#124; And they have gone up on cliffs, &#124;&#124; All the city is forsaken, &#124;&#124; And there is no one dwelling in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 9:30; Lamentations 1:2; Lamentations 1:19"> And you, O spoiled one, what do you do? For you put on scarlet, &#124;&#124; For you adorn yourself &#91;with&#93; ornaments of gold. For you tear your eyes with pain, &#124;&#124; In vain you make yourself beautiful, &#124;&#124; Unhealthy ones have kicked against you, &#124;&#124; They seek your life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 1:17; Isaiah 1:15; Jeremiah 13:21"> For I have heard a voice as of a travailing woman, &#124;&#124; Distress, as of one bringing forth a firstborn, &#124;&#124; The voice of the daughter of Zion, &#124;&#124; She laments herself, &#124;&#124; She spreads out her hands, &#124;&#124; Woe to me now, my soul is weary of slayers!”</span>
