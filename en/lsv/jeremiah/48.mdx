<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 32:3; Numbers 32:37–38; Jeremiah 48:22–23"> Concerning Moab: “Thus said YHWH of Hosts, God of Israel: Woe to Nebo, for it is spoiled, &#124;&#124; Kiriathaim has been captured &#91;and&#93; put to shame, &#124;&#124; The high tower has been put to shame, &#124;&#124; Indeed, it has been broken down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 32:37; Jeremiah 31:36; Isaiah 16:14"> There is no longer praise of Moab, &#124;&#124; In Heshbon they devised evil against her: Come, and we cut it off from &#91;being&#93; a nation, &#124;&#124; Also, O Madmen, you are cut off, &#124;&#124; A sword goes after you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 15:5; Jeremiah 48:34; Jeremiah 48:5"> A voice of a cry &#91;is&#93; from Horonaim, &#124;&#124; Spoiling and great destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 137:9; Numbers 21:27–30; Esther 8:11"> Moab has been destroyed, &#124;&#124; Her little ones have caused a cry to be heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 15:5"> For the ascent of Luhith with weeping, &#124;&#124; Weeping goes up, &#124;&#124; For in the descent of Horonaim &#124;&#124; Adversaries have heard a cry of desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:6; Jeremiah 51:6; Job 30:3–7"> Flee, deliver yourselves, &#124;&#124; You are as a naked thing in a wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 21:29; Jeremiah 9:23; Psalms 52:7"> For because of your trusting in your works, &#124;&#124; And in your treasures, even you are captured, &#124;&#124; And Chemosh has gone out in a removal, &#124;&#124; His priests and his heads together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Jeremiah 51:56; Jeremiah 25:9"> And a spoiler comes to every city, &#124;&#124; And no city escapes, &#124;&#124; And the valley has perished, &#124;&#124; And the plain has been destroyed, as YHWH said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 11:1; Jeremiah 48:28; Isaiah 16:2"> Give wings to Moab, that she may go forth and flee away, &#124;&#124; And her cities are for a desolation, &#124;&#124; Without an inhabitant in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; 1 Kings 20:42; Judges 5:23"> Cursed &#91;is&#93; he who is doing the work of YHWH slothfully, &#124;&#124; And cursed &#91;is&#93; he &#124;&#124; Who is withholding his sword from blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:12; Zechariah 1:15; Jeremiah 48:29"> Moab is secure from his youth, &#124;&#124; And he &#91;is&#93; at rest for his preserved things, &#124;&#124; And he has not been emptied out from vessel to vessel, &#124;&#124; And he has not gone into captivity, &#124;&#124; Therefore his taste has remained in him, &#124;&#124; And his fragrance has not been changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nahum 2:2; Isaiah 30:14; Ezekiel 25:9–10"> Therefore, behold, days are coming, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And I have sent wanderers to him, &#124;&#124; And they have caused him to wander, &#124;&#124; And they empty out his vessels, &#124;&#124; And they dash his bottles in pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 45:16; Judges 11:24; Jeremiah 48:39"> And Moab has been ashamed because of Chemosh, &#124;&#124; As the house of Israel has been ashamed &#124;&#124; Because of Beth-El, their confidence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 33:16; Isaiah 10:13; Isaiah 10:16"> How do you say, We &#91;are&#93; mighty, &#124;&#124; And men of strength for battle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:27; Jeremiah 46:18; Jeremiah 51:57"> Moab is spoiled, and has gone up &#91;from&#93; her cities, &#124;&#124; And the choice of its young men &#124;&#124; Have gone down to slaughter, &#124;&#124; An affirmation of the King, &#124;&#124; YHWH of Hosts &#91;is&#93; His Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 13:22; Jeremiah 1:12; Ezekiel 12:28"> The destruction of Moab is near to come, &#124;&#124; And his calamity has hurried exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Isaiah 14:4–5; Ezekiel 19:11–14"> Bemoan for him, all you around him, &#124;&#124; And all knowing his name, say, &#124;&#124; How it has been broken, the staff of strength, &#124;&#124; The rod of beauty!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 21:30; Isaiah 47:1; Isaiah 15:2"> Come down from glory, sit in thirst, &#124;&#124; O inhabitant, daughter of Dibon, &#124;&#124; For a spoiler of Moab has come up to you, &#124;&#124; He has destroyed your fortifications.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36; 1 Samuel 4:16; 1 Samuel 4:13–14"> Stand on the way, and watch, O inhabitant of Aroer, &#124;&#124; Ask the fugitive and escaped, &#124;&#124; Say, What has happened?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 16:2; Numbers 21:26–28; Deuteronomy 2:36"> Moab has been put to shame, &#124;&#124; For it has been broken down, &#124;&#124; Howl and cry, declare in Arnon, &#124;&#124; For Moab is spoiled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 13:18; Isaiah 15:4; Jeremiah 48:8"> And judgment has come to the land of the plain—to Holon, &#124;&#124; And to Jahazah, and on Mephaath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:18; Numbers 33:46; Numbers 32:34"> And on Dibon, and on Nebo, &#124;&#124; And on Beth-Diblathaim, and on Kirathaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 13:17; Jeremiah 48:1; Numbers 32:38"> And on Beth-Gamul, and on Beth-Meon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 2:2; Jeremiah 48:41; Joshua 21:36"> And on Kerioth, and on Bozrah, &#124;&#124; And on all cities of the land of Moab, &#124;&#124; The far off and the near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 75:10; Psalms 10:15; Zechariah 1:19–21"> The horn of Moab has been cut down, &#124;&#124; And his arm has been broken, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:42; Isaiah 29:9; Jeremiah 51:39"> Declare him drunk, &#124;&#124; For he made himself great against YHWH &#124;&#124; And Moab has struck in his vomit, &#124;&#124; And he has been for a derision—even he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:26; Micah 7:8–10; Zephaniah 2:8"> And was Israel not the derision to you? Was he found among thieves? For since your words concerning him, &#124;&#124; You bemoan yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 55:6–7; Judges 6:2; Song of Songs 2:14"> Forsake cities, and dwell in a rock, &#124;&#124; You inhabitants of Moab, &#124;&#124; And be as a dove making a nest in the passages of a pit’s mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 16:6; Psalms 138:6; Isaiah 2:11–12"> We have heard of the arrogance of Moab, &#124;&#124; Exceedingly proud! His haughtiness, and his arrogance, &#124;&#124; And his pride, and the height of his heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:36; Isaiah 16:6; Isaiah 37:28–29"> I have known, a declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; His wrath, and &#91;it is&#93; not right, &#124;&#124; His boastings—they have done nothing right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 15:5; 2 Kings 3:25; Jeremiah 48:36"> Therefore I howl for Moab, even for Moab—all of it, &#124;&#124; I cry for men of Kir-Heres, it mourns,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 16:8–9; Numbers 21:32; Joshua 13:19"> With the weeping of Jazer, I weep for you, O vine of Sibmah, &#124;&#124; Your branches have passed over a sea, &#124;&#124; They have come to the Sea of Jazer, &#124;&#124; On your summer fruits, and on your harvest, &#124;&#124; A spoiler has fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 5:10; Haggai 2:16; Jeremiah 25:9–10"> And joy and gladness have been removed from the fruitful field, &#124;&#124; Even from the land of Moab, &#124;&#124; And I have caused wine to cease from winepresses, &#124;&#124; Shouting does not proceed, &#124;&#124; The shouting &#91;is&#93; not shouting!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 15:4–6; Numbers 32:3; Deuteronomy 34:3"> Because of the cry of Heshbon to Elealeh, &#124;&#124; They have given their voice to Jahaz, &#124;&#124; From Zoar to Horonaim, &#124;&#124; A heifer of the third &#91;year&#93;, &#124;&#124; For even &#91;the&#93; waters of Nimrim become desolations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 15:2; Isaiah 16:12; Numbers 28:14"> And I have caused to cease in Moab, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Him who is offering in a high place, &#124;&#124; And him who is making incense to his god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 15:7; Isaiah 16:11; Isaiah 15:5"> Therefore My heart sounds as pipes for Moab, &#124;&#124; And My heart sounds as pipes for men of Kir-Heres, &#124;&#124; Therefore the abundance he made perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:5; Genesis 37:34; Jeremiah 47:5"> For every head &#91;is&#93; bald, and every beard diminished, &#124;&#124; On all hands—cuttings, and on the loins—sackcloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:28; Jeremiah 25:34; Isaiah 22:1"> On all roofs of Moab, and in her broad-places, &#124;&#124; All of it—lamentation, &#124;&#124; For I have broken Moab as a vessel in which there is no pleasure,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4–6; Lamentations 4:1; Lamentations 2:1"> “How it has been broken down! They have howled, &#124;&#124; How Moab has turned the neck ashamed! And Moab has been for a derision &#124;&#124; And for a terror to all around her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 8:8; Deuteronomy 28:49; Jeremiah 49:22"> For thus said YHWH: “Behold, he flees as an eagle, &#124;&#124; And has spread his wings to Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 21:3; Isaiah 13:8; Micah 4:9–10"> The cities have been captured, &#124;&#124; And the strongholds are caught, &#124;&#124; And the heart of the mighty of Moab &#124;&#124; Has been in that day as the heart of a distressed woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:2; Isaiah 7:8; Isaiah 37:23"> And Moab has been destroyed from &#91;being&#93; a people, &#124;&#124; For he exerted himself against YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Lamentations 3:47; Isaiah 24:17–18; Deuteronomy 32:23–25"> Fear, and a snare, and a trap, &#91;are&#93; for you, &#124;&#124; O inhabitant of Moab—a declaration of YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:23; 1 Kings 19:17; Jeremiah 46:21"> Whoever is fleeing because of the fear falls into the snare, &#124;&#124; And whoever is coming up from the snare is captured by the trap, &#124;&#124; For I bring to her—to Moab—The year of their inspection, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; Numbers 21:28; Zechariah 10:4"> In the shadow of Heshbon fugitives have stood powerless, &#124;&#124; For fire has gone forth from Heshbon, &#124;&#124; And a flame from within Sihon, &#124;&#124; And it consumes the corner of Moab, &#124;&#124; And the crown of the sons of Shaon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 21:29; Jeremiah 48:7; Judges 11:24"> Woe to you, O Moab, &#124;&#124; The people of Chemosh have perished, &#124;&#124; For your sons were taken with the captives, &#124;&#124; And your daughters with the captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:39; Jeremiah 49:6; Jeremiah 23:20"> And I have turned back &#91;to&#93; the captivity of Moab, &#124;&#124; In the latter end of the days,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH! “Until now &#91;is&#93; the judgment of Moab.”</span>
