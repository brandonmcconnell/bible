<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:25; Jeremiah 37:3; Jeremiah 52:24"> The word that has been to Jeremiah from YHWH, in King Zedekiah’s sending to him Pashhur son of Malchiah and Zephaniah son of Maaseiah the priest, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:3; Jeremiah 42:4–6; Jeremiah 37:7"> “Please inquire of YHWH for us, for Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has fought against us; perhaps YHWH deals with us according to all His wonders, and causes him to go up from off us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And Jeremiah says to them, “Thus you say to Zedekiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:5; Jeremiah 37:8–10; Isaiah 5:5"> Thus said YHWH, God of Israel: Behold, I am turning around the weapons of battle &#124;&#124; That &#91;are&#93; in your hand, &#124;&#124; With which you fight the king of Babylon, &#124;&#124; And the Chaldeans, who are laying siege against you, &#124;&#124; At the outside of the wall, &#124;&#124; And I have gathered them into the midst of this city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; Exodus 6:6; Isaiah 5:25"> And I have fought against you, &#124;&#124; With an outstretched hand, and with a strong arm, &#124;&#124; And in anger, and in fury, and in great wrath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Ezekiel 14:13; Jeremiah 12:3–4"> And I have struck the inhabitants of this city, &#124;&#124; Both man and beast, &#124;&#124; They die by a great pestilence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:17; 2 Kings 25:5–7; Habakkuk 1:6–10"> And after this,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “I give Zedekiah king of Judah, &#124;&#124; And his servants, and the people, &#124;&#124; And those left in this city, &#124;&#124; From the pestilence, from the sword, and from the famine, &#124;&#124; Into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, &#124;&#124; And into the hand of their enemies, &#124;&#124; And into the hand of those seeking their life, &#124;&#124; And he has struck them by the mouth of the sword, &#124;&#124; He has no pity on them, &#124;&#124; Nor does he spare, nor does he have mercy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:19; Deuteronomy 30:15; Deuteronomy 11:26"> “And you say to this people, Thus said YHWH: Behold, I am setting before you the way of life, &#124;&#124; And the way of death!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:2; Jeremiah 45:5; Jeremiah 39:18"> Whoever is abiding in this city—dies, &#124;&#124; By sword, and by famine, and by pestilence, &#124;&#124; And whoever is going forth, &#124;&#124; And has fallen to the Chaldeans, &#124;&#124; Who are laying siege against you—lives, &#124;&#124; And his life has been for a spoil to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:11; Amos 9:4; Jeremiah 44:27"> For I have set My face against this city for calamity, &#124;&#124; And not for good,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH. “It is given into the hand of the king of Babylon, &#124;&#124; And he has burned it with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:20; Micah 3:1; Jeremiah 13:18"> And as for the house of the king of Judah,” &#124;&#124; Hear a word of YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Proverbs 31:8–9; Jeremiah 4:4"> O house of David, thus said YHWH: “Decide judgment in the morning, &#124;&#124; And deliver the plundered from the hand of the oppressor, &#124;&#124; Lest My fury go forth as fire, &#124;&#124; And has burned, and none is quenching, &#124;&#124; Because of the evil of your doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:8; Lamentations 4:12; Obadiah 1:3–4"> Behold, I &#91;am&#93; against you,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “O inhabitant of the valley, rock of the plain, &#124;&#124; Who are saying, Who comes down against us? And who comes into our habitations?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10–11; Jeremiah 32:19; Proverbs 1:31"> And I have laid a charge against you, &#124;&#124; According to the fruit of your doings,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And I have kindled a fire in its forest, &#124;&#124; And it has consumed all its outskirts!”</span>
