<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:17; Isaiah 22:4; Lamentations 2:11"> Who makes my head waters, &#124;&#124; And my eye a fountain of tears? And I weep by day and by night, &#124;&#124; For the wounded of the daughter of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:10; Jeremiah 5:7–8; Hosea 4:2"> Who gives me in a wilderness &#124;&#124; A lodging-place for travelers? And I leave my people, and go from them, &#124;&#124; For all of them &#91;are&#93; adulterers, &#124;&#124; An assembly of treacherous ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:8; Jeremiah 4:22; Judges 2:10"> “And they bend their tongue, &#124;&#124; Their bow &#91;is&#93; a lie, &#124;&#124; And they have not been mighty for steadfastness in the land, &#124;&#124; For they have gone forth from evil to evil, &#124;&#124; And they have not known Me,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Proverbs 26:24–25; Psalms 12:2–3"> “You each beware of his friend, &#124;&#124; And do not trust any brother, &#124;&#124; For every brother utterly supplants, &#124;&#124; For every friend slanderously walks,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 6:12; Habakkuk 2:13; Job 15:5"> And they each mock at his friend, &#124;&#124; And they do not speak truth, &#124;&#124; They taught their tongue to speak falsehood, &#124;&#124; They have labored to commit iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; Jeremiah 13:10; Jeremiah 8:5"> Your dwelling &#91;is&#93; in the midst of deceit, &#124;&#124; Through deceit they refused to know Me,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:25; Jeremiah 6:27; Malachi 3:3"> Therefore, thus said YHWH of Hosts: “Behold, I am refining them, and have tried them, &#124;&#124; For how do I deal with the daughter of My people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 28:3; Proverbs 26:24–26; Jeremiah 9:3"> Their tongue &#91;is&#93; a slaughtering arrow, &#124;&#124; It has spoken deceit in its mouth, &#124;&#124; It speaks peace with its neighbor, &#124;&#124; And in its heart it lays its ambush,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:9; Jeremiah 5:29; Isaiah 1:24"> Do I not see after them for these things?” A declaration of YHWH, “Does My soul not avenge itself against a nation such as this?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:4; Hosea 4:3; Jeremiah 12:10"> I lift up weeping and wailing for the mountains, &#124;&#124; And a lamentation for the habitations of the wilderness, &#124;&#124; For they have been burned up without any passing over, &#124;&#124; Nor have they heard the voice of livestock, &#124;&#124; From the bird of the heavens and to the beast—they have fled, &#124;&#124; They have gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 25:2; Isaiah 13:22; Jeremiah 10:22"> “And I make Jerusalem become heaps, &#124;&#124; A habitation of dragons, &#124;&#124; And I make the cities of Judah a desolation, &#124;&#124; Without inhabitant.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 14:9; Psalms 107:43; Psalms 107:34"> Who &#91;is&#93; the wise man? And he understands this, &#124;&#124; And he to whom the mouth of YHWH spoke? And he declares it, &#124;&#124; For why has the land perished? It has been burned up as a wilderness, &#124;&#124; Without any passing through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:19; Jeremiah 22:9; Ezra 9:10"> And YHWH says: “Because of their forsaking My law that I set before them, &#124;&#124; And they have not listened to My voice nor walked in it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:17; Jeremiah 7:24; 1 Peter 1:18"> And they walk after the stubbornness of their heart, &#124;&#124; And after the Ba‘alim, &#124;&#124; That their fathers taught them,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:14; Lamentations 3:15; Psalms 80:5"> Therefore, thus said YHWH of Hosts, God of Israel: “Behold, I am causing them—this people—to eat wormwood, &#124;&#124; And I have caused them to drink water of gall,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Ezekiel 5:2; Leviticus 26:33"> And I have scattered them among nations &#124;&#124; Which they did not know, they and their fathers, &#124;&#124; And have sent the sword after them, &#124;&#124; Until I have consumed them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Amos 5:16–17; Ecclesiastes 12:5; 2 Chronicles 35:25"> Thus said YHWH of Hosts: “Consider, and call for mourning women, &#124;&#124; And they come, &#124;&#124; And send to the wise women, &#124;&#124; And they come,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:1; Isaiah 22:4; Jeremiah 14:17"> And they hurry, and lift up a wailing for us. And tears run down our eyes, &#124;&#124; And waters flow from our eyelids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Ezekiel 7:16–18; Deuteronomy 28:29"> For a voice of wailing is heard from Zion: How we have been spoiled! We have been greatly ashamed, &#124;&#124; Because we have forsaken the land, &#124;&#124; Because they have cast down our dwelling places.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 23:27–30; Isaiah 32:9–13; Job 22:22"> But hear, you women, a word of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And your ear receives a word of His mouth, &#124;&#124; And teach your daughters wailing, &#124;&#124; And each her neighbor lamentation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:11; Ezekiel 9:5–6; Jeremiah 15:7"> For death has come up into our windows, &#124;&#124; It has come into our palaces, &#124;&#124; To cut off the suckling from outside, &#124;&#124; Young men from the broad places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:2; Jeremiah 25:33; Psalms 83:10"> Speak thus, “A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And the carcass of man has fallen, &#124;&#124; As dung on the face of the field, &#124;&#124; And as a handful after the reaper, &#124;&#124; And there is none gathering.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27–29; Isaiah 5:21; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> Thus said YHWH: “Do not let the wise boast himself in his wisdom, &#124;&#124; Nor let the mighty boast himself in his might, &#124;&#124; Do not let the rich boast himself in his riches,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:17; Galatians 6:14; 1 Corinthians 1:31"> But let the boaster boast himself in this, &#124;&#124; In understanding and knowing Me, &#124;&#124; For I &#91;am&#93; YHWH, doing kindness, &#124;&#124; Judgment, and righteousness, in the earth, &#124;&#124; For I have delighted in these,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 2:8–9; Galatians 5:2–6; Romans 2:25–26"> “Behold, days are coming,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And I have laid a charge on all circumcised in the foreskin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Leviticus 26:41; Ezekiel 44:7"> On Egypt, and on Judah, and on Edom, &#124;&#124; And on the sons of Ammon, and on Moab, &#124;&#124; And on all cutting the corner &#91;of the beard&#93;, &#124;&#124; Who are dwelling in the wilderness, &#124;&#124; For all the nations &#91;are&#93; uncircumcised, &#124;&#124; And all the house of Israel &#91;are&#93; uncircumcised in heart!”</span>
