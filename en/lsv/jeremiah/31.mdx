<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:22; Jeremiah 30:24; Zechariah 13:9"> “At that time,” a declaration of YHWH, “I am the God of all families of Israel, &#124;&#124; And they are My people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 10:33; Psalms 95:11; Matthew 11:28"> Thus said YHWH: “A people remaining from the sword &#124;&#124; Have found grace in the wilderness &#124;&#124; When Israel went to find rest.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 103:17; Isaiah 54:8–9; Hosea 11:4"> YHWH has appeared to me from afar, “I have loved you with perpetual love, &#124;&#124; Therefore I have drawn you &#91;with&#93; kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:7; Jeremiah 31:13; Amos 5:2"> I build you again, &#124;&#124; And you have been built, &#124;&#124; O virgin of Israel, &#124;&#124; You put on your tambourines again, &#124;&#124; And have gone out in the chorus of the playful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 9:14; Leviticus 19:23–25; Deuteronomy 28:30"> You plant vineyards in mountains of Samaria again, &#124;&#124; Planters have planted, and made common.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:4–5; Micah 4:1–3; Jeremiah 50:19"> For there is a day, &#124;&#124; Watchmen have cried on Mount Ephraim, &#124;&#124; Rise, and we go up to Zion, to our God YHWH”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 14:7; Psalms 28:9; Isaiah 37:31"> For thus said YHWH: “Sing &#91;with&#93; joy for Jacob, &#124;&#124; And cry aloud at the head of the nations, &#124;&#124; Sound, praise, and say, &#124;&#124; Save, O YHWH, Your people, the remnant of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Isaiah 42:16; Jeremiah 23:8"> Behold, I am bringing them in from the north country, &#124;&#124; And have gathered them from the sides of the earth, &#124;&#124; Blind and lame &#91;are&#93; among them, &#124;&#124; Conceiving and travailing one—together, &#124;&#124; A great assembly—they return here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:4; Jeremiah 3:4; Exodus 4:22"> They come in with weeping, &#124;&#124; And I bring them with supplications, &#124;&#124; I cause them to go to streams of waters, &#124;&#124; In a right way—they do not stumble in it, &#124;&#124; For I have been a Father to Israel, &#124;&#124; And Ephraim—he &#91;is&#93; My firstborn.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; Ezekiel 34:12; Deuteronomy 30:4"> Hear a word of YHWH, O nations, &#124;&#124; And declare among the far off in the islands, and say: He who is scattering Israel gathers him, &#124;&#124; And has kept His flock as a shepherd,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Titus 2:14; Isaiah 44:23"> For YHWH has ransomed Jacob, &#124;&#124; And redeemed him from a hand stronger than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 35:10; Isaiah 58:11; Joel 3:18"> And they have come in, &#124;&#124; And have sung in the high place of Zion, &#124;&#124; And flowed to the goodness of YHWH, &#124;&#124; For wheat, and for new wine, and for oil, &#124;&#124; And for the young of the flock and herd, &#124;&#124; And their soul has been as a watered garden, &#124;&#124; And they do not add to grieve anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 30:11; Isaiah 61:3; Isaiah 51:11"> “Then a virgin rejoices in a chorus, &#124;&#124; Both young men and old men—together, &#124;&#124; And I have turned their mourning to joy, &#124;&#124; And have comforted them, &#124;&#124; And gladdened them above their sorrow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:25; 1 Peter 2:9; Jeremiah 33:9"> And satisfied the soul of the priests &#91;with&#93; fatness, &#124;&#124; And My people are satisfied with My goodness,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 2:16–18; Jeremiah 40:1; Psalms 77:2"> Thus said YHWH: “A voice is heard in Ramah, &#124;&#124; Wailing &#91;and&#93; the weeping of bitterness, &#124;&#124; Rachel is weeping for her sons, &#124;&#124; She has refused to be comforted for her sons, because they are not.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ruth 2:12; Jeremiah 30:3; Hebrews 6:10"> Thus said YHWH: “Withhold your voice from weeping, and your eyes from tears, &#124;&#124; For there is a reward for your work,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And they have turned back from the land of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 3:21; Jeremiah 29:11–16; Lamentations 3:18"> And there is hope for your latter end,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And the sons have turned back &#91;to&#93; their border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Psalms 80:3; Job 5:17"> I have surely heard Ephraim bemoaning himself, &#124;&#124; You have disciplined me, &#124;&#124; And I am disciplined, as a heifer &#91;that is&#93; not taught, &#124;&#124; Turn me back, and I turn back, &#124;&#124; For You &#91;are&#93; my God YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:31; Jeremiah 3:25; Ezekiel 21:12"> For after my turning back I regretted, &#124;&#124; And after my being instructed I struck on the thigh, &#124;&#124; I have been ashamed, I have also blushed, &#124;&#124; For I have borne the reproach of my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 55:7; Micah 7:18–19; Isaiah 63:15"> Is Ephraim a precious son to Me? A child of delights? For since My speaking against him, &#124;&#124; I still thoroughly remember him, &#124;&#124; Therefore My bowels have been moved for him, &#124;&#124; I love him greatly,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; Isaiah 62:10; Psalms 84:5"> “Set up signs for yourself, &#124;&#124; Make heaps for yourself, &#124;&#124; Set your heart to the highway, the way you went, &#124;&#124; Turn back, O virgin of Israel, &#124;&#124; Turn back to these cities of yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:4; Jeremiah 7:24; Matthew 1:21"> Until when do you withdraw yourself, O backsliding daughter? For YHWH has prepared a new thing in the land, &#124;&#124; Woman surrounds man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3; Isaiah 1:26; Jeremiah 50:7"> Thus said YHWH of Hosts, God of Israel: “Still they say this word in the land of Judah, &#124;&#124; And in its cities, &#124;&#124; In My turning back &#91;to&#93; their captivity, &#124;&#124; YHWH blesses you, habitation of righteousness, &#124;&#124; Mountain of holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 8:4–8; Ezekiel 36:10; Zechariah 2:4"> And farmers have dwelt in Judah, &#124;&#124; And in all its cities together, &#124;&#124; And they have journeyed in order.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:6; Matthew 11:28; John 4:14"> For I have satiated the weary soul, &#124;&#124; And I have filled every grieved soul.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 127:2; Zechariah 4:1–2"> On this I have awoken, and I behold, and my sleep has been sweet to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; Zechariah 10:9; Ezekiel 36:9"> “Behold, days are coming,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And I have sown the house of Israel, &#124;&#124; And the house of Judah, &#124;&#124; With seed of man and seed of beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 18:7–9"> And it has been, as I watched over them to pluck up, &#124;&#124; And to break down, and to throw down, &#124;&#124; And to destroy, and to afflict; So I watch over them to build, and to plant,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Lamentations 5:7; Ezekiel 18:2–3; Jeremiah 31:30"> “In those days they no longer say: Fathers have eaten unripe fruit, &#124;&#124; And the sons’ teeth are blunted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:20; Isaiah 3:11; Deuteronomy 24:16"> But—each dies for his own iniquity, &#124;&#124; Every man who is eating the unripe fruit, &#124;&#124; His teeth are blunted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 10:16–17; Ezekiel 37:26; Jeremiah 31:31–34"> Behold, days are coming,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And I have made a new covenant &#124;&#124; With the house of Israel &#124;&#124; And with the house of Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 24:6–8; Deuteronomy 5:3; Exodus 19:5"> Not like the covenant that I made with their fathers, &#124;&#124; In the day of My laying hold on their hand, &#124;&#124; To bring them out of the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; In that they made My covenant void, &#124;&#124; And I ruled over them,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:3; Hebrews 10:16; Hebrews 8:10"> “For this &#91;is&#93; the covenant that I make, &#124;&#124; With the house of Israel, after those days,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “I have given My law in their inward part, &#124;&#124; And I write it on their heart, &#124;&#124; And I have been their God, &#124;&#124; And they are My people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Hebrews 8:12; 1 John 2:27"> And they do not teach anymore &#124;&#124; Each his neighbor, and each his brother, &#124;&#124; Saying, Know YHWH, &#124;&#124; For they all know Me, from their least to their greatest,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH; “For I pardon their iniquity, &#124;&#124; And I make no more mention of their sin.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Isaiah 51:15; Psalms 136:7–9"> Thus said YHWH, &#124;&#124; Who is giving the sun for a light by day, &#124;&#124; The statutes of moon and stars for a light by night, &#124;&#124; Quieting the sea when its billows roar, &#124;&#124; YHWH of Hosts &#91;is&#93; His Name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 54:9–10; Jeremiah 33:20–26; Psalms 89:36–37"> “If these statutes depart from before Me,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “Even the seed of Israel ceases &#124;&#124; From being a nation before Me &#91;for&#93; all the days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:24–26; Isaiah 40:12; Jeremiah 33:22"> Thus said YHWH: “If the heavens above can be measured, &#124;&#124; And the foundations of earth searched below, &#124;&#124; Even I kick against all the seed of Israel, &#124;&#124; For all that they have done,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 14:10; 2 Kings 14:13; Jeremiah 30:18"> “Behold, days &#91;are coming&#93;,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And the city has been built for YHWH, &#124;&#124; From the Tower of Hananeel to the Corner Gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Zechariah 2:1–2; Ezekiel 40:8"> And the measuring line has gone out again before it, over the height of Gareb, &#124;&#124; And it has gone around to Goah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:23; Joel 3:17; 2 Kings 11:16"> And all the valley of the carcasses and of the ashes, &#124;&#124; And all the fields to the Brook of Kidron, &#124;&#124; To the corner of the Horse Gate eastward, &#124;&#124; &#91;Are&#93; holy to YHWH, &#124;&#124; It is not plucked up, &#124;&#124; Nor is it thrown down anymore for all time!”</span>
