<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word that has been to Jeremiah from YHWH, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:2; Revelation 3:22; Amos 7:16"> “Stand in the gate of the house of YHWH, and you have proclaimed this word there, and have said, Hear a word of YHWH, all you of Judah, who are coming in at these gates, to bow before YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:11; Jeremiah 26:13; Isaiah 1:16–19"> Thus said YHWH of Hosts, God of Israel: “Amend your ways, and your doings, &#124;&#124; And I cause you to dwell in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Matthew 3:9–10; Zephaniah 3:11"> Do not trust for yourselves &#124;&#124; To the words of falsehood, saying, &#124;&#124; The temple of YHWH! The temple of YHWH! These &#91;are&#93; the temple of YHWH!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:12–13; Isaiah 1:19; Jeremiah 7:3"> For if you thoroughly amend your ways and your doings, &#124;&#124; If you thoroughly do judgment &#124;&#124; Between a man and his neighbor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:21–24; Zechariah 7:9–12; Jeremiah 13:10"> You do not oppress sojourner, fatherless, and widow, &#124;&#124; And innocent blood is not shed in this place, &#124;&#124; And &#91;you&#93; do not walk after other gods, for evil to yourselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Jeremiah 25:5; Jeremiah 3:18"> Then I have caused you to dwell in this place, &#124;&#124; In the land that I gave to your fathers, &#124;&#124; From age even to age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 28:15; Ezekiel 13:6–16; Jeremiah 23:14–16"> Behold, you are trusting for yourselves &#124;&#124; On the words of falsehood, so as not to profit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:6; Exodus 20:3; James 4:1–4"> Stealing, murdering, and committing adultery, &#124;&#124; And swearing to falsehood, and giving incense to Ba‘al, &#124;&#124; And going after other gods whom you did not know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:34; Ezekiel 23:37; Ezekiel 23:39"> And you have come in and stood before Me, &#124;&#124; In this house on which My Name is called, &#124;&#124; And have said, We have been delivered, &#124;&#124; In order to do all these abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:45–46; Isaiah 56:7; Matthew 21:13"> Has this house, &#124;&#124; On which My Name is called, &#124;&#124; Been a den of burglars in your eyes? Even I, behold, have seen,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Jeremiah 26:6; 1 Samuel 4:10–11"> “But go now to My place that &#91;is&#93; in Shiloh, &#124;&#124; Where I caused My Name to dwell at first, &#124;&#124; And see that which I have done to it, &#124;&#124; For the wickedness of My people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Jeremiah 7:25"> And now, because of your doing all these works,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And I speak to you, rising early and speaking, &#124;&#124; And you have not listened, &#124;&#124; And I call you, and you have not answered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 78:60; 2 Kings 25:9; Jeremiah 7:4"> I also have done to the house on which My Name is called, &#124;&#124; In which you are trusting, &#124;&#124; And to the place that I gave to you, and to your fathers, &#124;&#124; As I have done to Shiloh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:1; 2 Kings 17:23; Jeremiah 52:3"> And I have cast you from before My face, &#124;&#124; As I have cast out all your brothers, &#124;&#124; The whole seed of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:14; Jeremiah 14:11–12; Jeremiah 15:1"> And you do not pray for this people, &#124;&#124; Nor lift up crying and prayer for them, &#124;&#124; Nor intercede with Me, for I do not hear you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:6–18; Ezekiel 14:23; Jeremiah 6:27"> Are you not seeing what they are doing &#124;&#124; In cities of Judah, and in streets of Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:13; Jeremiah 44:17–19; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> The sons are gathering wood, &#124;&#124; And the fathers are causing the fire to burn, &#124;&#124; And the women are kneading dough, &#124;&#124; To make cakes to the queen of the heavens, &#124;&#124; And to pour out drink-offerings to other gods, &#124;&#124; So as to provoke Me to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:11; Jeremiah 2:19; Daniel 9:7–8"> Are they provoking Me to anger?” A declaration of YHWH, “Is it not themselves, &#124;&#124; For the shame of their own faces?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:18; Lamentations 2:3–5; Isaiah 42:25"> Therefore, thus said Lord YHWH: “Behold, My anger and My fury is poured out on this place, &#124;&#124; On man, and beast, and on tree of the field, &#124;&#124; And on fruit of the ground, &#124;&#124; And it has burned, and it is not quenched.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Jeremiah 6:20; Amos 5:21–23"> Thus said YHWH of Hosts, God of Israel: “Add your burnt-offerings to your sacrifices and eat flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; 1 Samuel 15:22; Psalms 51:16–17"> For I did not speak with your fathers, &#124;&#124; Nor did I command them in the day of My bringing them out of the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; Concerning the matters of burnt-offering and sacrifice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Deuteronomy 5:33; Exodus 19:5–6"> But this thing I commanded them, saying, &#124;&#124; Listen to My voice, &#124;&#124; And I have been to you for God, &#124;&#124; And you are to Me for a people, &#124;&#124; And have walked in all the way that I command you, &#124;&#124; So that it is well for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 81:11–12; Jeremiah 8:5; Jeremiah 7:26"> And they have not listened, nor inclined their ear, &#124;&#124; And they walk in the counsels, &#124;&#124; &#91;And&#93; in the stubbornness, of their evil hearts, &#124;&#124; And are backward and not forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15; Jeremiah 25:4; Nehemiah 9:26"> Even from the day when your fathers &#124;&#124; Went out of the land of Egypt until this day, &#124;&#124; I send all My servants the prophets to you, &#124;&#124; Daily rising early and sending,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Jeremiah 7:24"> And they have not listened to Me, &#124;&#124; Nor inclined their ear—and they harden their neck, &#124;&#124; They have done evil above their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:2; Jeremiah 1:7; Isaiah 65:12"> And you have spoken all these words to them, &#124;&#124; And they do not listen to you, &#124;&#124; And you have called to them, &#124;&#124; And they do not answer you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 59:14–15; Proverbs 1:7; Isaiah 1:4–5"> And you have said to them: This &#91;is&#93; the nation that has not listened, &#124;&#124; To the voice of its God YHWH, &#124;&#124; Nor have they accepted instruction, &#124;&#124; Steadfastness has perished, &#124;&#124; Indeed, it has been cut off from their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 1:20; Micah 1:16; Jeremiah 6:30"> Cut off your crown, and cast &#91;it&#93; away, &#124;&#124; And lift up lamentation on high places, &#124;&#124; For YHWH has rejected, &#124;&#124; And He leaves the generation of His wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:34; Ezekiel 7:20; Daniel 9:27"> For the sons of Judah &#124;&#124; Have done evil in My eyes,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “They have set their abominations in the house &#124;&#124; On which My Name is called—to defile it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 23:10; Jeremiah 32:35; Leviticus 18:21"> And have built the high places of Tophet, &#124;&#124; That &#91;are&#93; in the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, &#124;&#124; To burn their sons and their daughters with fire, &#124;&#124; Which I did not command, &#124;&#124; Nor did it come up on My heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:11; Jeremiah 19:6; 2 Kings 23:10"> Therefore, behold, days are coming,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And it is no longer said, The Tophet, &#124;&#124; And, Valley of the Son of Hinnom, &#124;&#124; But, Valley of the Slaughter, &#124;&#124; And they have buried in Tophet—without place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:9; Deuteronomy 28:26; Revelation 19:17–18"> And the carcass of this people has been for food &#124;&#124; To a bird of the heavens, and to a beast of the earth, &#124;&#124; And there is none troubling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Hosea 2:11; Ezekiel 26:13"> And I have caused to cease from cities of Judah, &#124;&#124; And from streets of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; The voice of joy, and the voice of gladness, &#124;&#124; Voice of bridegroom, and voice of bride, &#124;&#124; For the land becomes a desolation!”</span>
