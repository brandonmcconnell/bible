<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:15; Jeremiah 1:1–2"> The word that has been to Jeremiah from YHWH, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:32; Isaiah 30:8; Habakkuk 2:2–3"> “Thus spoke YHWH, God of Israel, saying, Write for yourself all the words that I have spoken to you on a scroll.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14; Jeremiah 16:15; Jeremiah 30:18"> For behold, days are coming—a declaration of YHWH—and I have turned back &#91;to&#93; the captivity of My people Israel and Judah, said YHWH, and I have caused them to return to the land that I gave to their fathers, and they possess it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And these &#91;are&#93; the words that YHWH has spoken concerning Israel and concerning Judah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:10–11; Amos 8:10; Isaiah 5:30"> Surely thus said YHWH: “We have heard a voice of trembling, &#124;&#124; Fear—and there is no peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nahum 2:10; Joel 2:6; Jeremiah 4:31"> Now ask and see, is a male bringing forth? Why have I seen every man &#124;&#124; &#91;With&#93; his hands on his loins, as a travailing woman, &#124;&#124; And all faces have been turned to paleness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Zechariah 14:1–2; Malachi 4:1"> Woe! For that day &#91;is&#93; great, without any like it, &#124;&#124; Indeed, it &#91;is&#93; the time of Jacob’s tribulation, &#124;&#124; Yet he is saved out of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nahum 1:13; Ezekiel 34:27; Isaiah 9:4"> And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; I break his yoke from off your neck, &#124;&#124; And I draw away your bands, &#124;&#124; And strangers lay no more service on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 3:5; Isaiah 55:3–5; Luke 1:69"> And they have served their God YHWH, &#124;&#124; And David their king whom I raise up to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:27–28; Isaiah 43:5; Jeremiah 29:14"> And you, do not be afraid, My servant Jacob, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Nor be frightened, O Israel, &#124;&#124; For behold, I am saving you from afar, &#124;&#124; And your seed from the land of their captivity, &#124;&#124; And Jacob has turned back and rested, &#124;&#124; And is quiet, and there is none troubling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:24; Jeremiah 1:19; Isaiah 8:10"> For I &#91;am&#93; with you,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “To save you, &#124;&#124; For I make an end of all the nations &#124;&#124; To where I have scattered you, &#124;&#124; Only, I do not make an end of you, &#124;&#124; And I have disciplined you in judgment, &#124;&#124; And do not entirely acquit you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:18; Jeremiah 30:15; Jeremiah 14:17"> For thus said YHWH: “Your breach is incurable, your stroke grievous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:11; Jeremiah 8:22; Jeremiah 30:17"> There is none judging your cause to bind up, &#124;&#124; There are no healing medicines for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:6; Job 30:21; Lamentations 1:2"> All loving you have forgotten you, &#124;&#124; They do not seek you, &#124;&#124; For I struck you with the stroke of an enemy, &#124;&#124; The discipline of a fierce one, &#124;&#124; Because of the abundance of your iniquity, your sins have been mighty!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:14; Jeremiah 30:12; Jeremiah 6:6–7"> Why do you cry concerning your breach? Your pain &#91;is&#93; incurable, &#124;&#124; Because of the abundance of your iniquity, &#124;&#124; Your sins have been mighty! I have done these to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:25; Isaiah 33:1; Exodus 23:22"> Therefore all consuming you are consumed, &#124;&#124; And all your adversaries—all of them—Go into captivity, &#124;&#124; And your spoilers have been for a spoil, &#124;&#124; And I give up all your plunderers to plunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Jeremiah 33:6; 1 Peter 2:24"> For I increase health to you, &#124;&#124; And I heal you from your strokes,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “For they have called you an outcast, &#91;saying&#93;, &#124;&#124; It &#91;is&#93; Zion, &#124;&#124; There is none seeking for her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Haggai 2:7–9; Psalms 102:13; Jeremiah 30:3"> Thus said YHWH: “Behold, I turn back &#91;to&#93; the captivity of the tents of Jacob, &#124;&#124; And I pity his dwelling places, &#124;&#124; And the city has been built on its heap, &#124;&#124; And the palace remains according to its ordinance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:22; Isaiah 51:11; Isaiah 60:22"> And thanksgiving has gone forth from them, &#124;&#124; And the voice of playful ones, &#124;&#124; And I have multiplied them and they are not few, &#124;&#124; And made them honorable, and they are not small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Jeremiah 2:3; Jeremiah 50:33–34"> And his sons have been as before, &#124;&#124; And his congregation is established before Me, &#124;&#124; And I have seen after all his oppressors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 16:5; Ezekiel 34:23–24; Acts 5:31"> And his majestic one has been of himself, &#124;&#124; And his ruler goes forth from his midst, &#124;&#124; And I have caused him to draw near, &#124;&#124; And he has drawn near to Me, &#124;&#124; For who &#91;is&#93; he who has pledged his heart &#124;&#124; To draw near to Me?” A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 21:3; Ezekiel 36:28; Jeremiah 31:33"> “And you have been to Me for a people, &#124;&#124; And I am to you for God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:19–20; Zechariah 9:14; Jeremiah 25:32"> Behold, a whirlwind of YHWH—Fury has gone forth—a cutting whirlwind, &#124;&#124; It stays on the head of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:20; Hosea 3:5; Micah 4:1"> The fierceness of the anger of YHWH &#124;&#124; Does not turn back until He has done &#91;it&#93;, &#124;&#124; Indeed, until His establishing the purposes of His heart. In the latter end of the days you consider it!</span>
