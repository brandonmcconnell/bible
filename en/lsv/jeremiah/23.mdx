<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:21; Zechariah 11:15–17; Ezekiel 34:2–10"> “Woe to shepherds destroying, &#124;&#124; And scattering the flock of My pasture,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 32:34; Jeremiah 21:12; Micah 7:4"> Therefore, thus said YHWH, God of Israel, &#124;&#124; Against the shepherds who feed My people: “You have scattered My flock, and drive them away, &#124;&#124; And have not inspected them, &#124;&#124; Behold, I am charging on you the evil of your doings,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 9:14–15; Jeremiah 32:37; Isaiah 43:5–6"> “And I gather the remnant of My flock &#124;&#124; Out of all the lands to where I drove them, &#124;&#124; And have brought them back to their fold, &#124;&#124; And they have been fruitful, and multiplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:14–15; 1 Peter 1:5; Ezekiel 34:23–31"> And I have raised shepherds for them, &#124;&#124; And they have fed them, &#124;&#124; And they no longer fear, nor are frightened, &#124;&#124; Nor are they lacking,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Isaiah 4:2; Isaiah 9:7"> “Behold, days are coming,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And I have raised a righteous shoot to David, &#124;&#124; And a king has reigned and acted wisely, &#124;&#124; And done judgment and righteousness in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Jeremiah 33:16; Philippians 3:9"> Judah is saved in His days, and Israel dwells confidently, &#124;&#124; And this &#91;is&#93; His Name that YHWH proclaims Him: Our Righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:14–15; Jeremiah 23:3; Isaiah 43:18–19"> Therefore, behold, days are coming,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And they no longer say, YHWH lives who brought up &#124;&#124; The sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5–6; Amos 9:14–15; Ezekiel 34:13"> But—YHWH lives, who brought up, &#124;&#124; And who brought in, the seed of the house of Israel, &#124;&#124; From the land of the north, &#124;&#124; And from all the lands to where I drove them, &#124;&#124; And they have dwelt on their own ground!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Lamentations 3:15; Psalms 60:3"> In reference to the prophets: My heart has been broken in my midst, &#124;&#124; All my bones have fluttered, &#124;&#124; I have been as a man—a drunkard, &#124;&#124; And as a man—wine has passed over him, &#124;&#124; Because of YHWH, and of His holy words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 4:2–3; Psalms 107:34; Jeremiah 9:2"> For the land has been full of adulterers, &#124;&#124; For the land has mourned because of these, &#124;&#124; The pleasant places of the wilderness have dried up, &#124;&#124; And their course is evil, and their might—not right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:4; Jeremiah 6:13; Matthew 21:12–13"> “For both prophet and priest have been profane, &#124;&#124; Indeed, I found their wickedness in My house,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:16; Jeremiah 11:23; Psalms 35:6"> “Therefore their way is as slippery places to them, &#124;&#124; They are driven into thick darkness, &#124;&#124; And they have fallen in it, &#124;&#124; For I bring in calamity against them, &#124;&#124; The year of their inspection,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:8; Isaiah 9:16; 1 Kings 18:18–21"> “And I have seen folly in prophets of Samaria, &#124;&#124; They have prophesied by Ba‘al, &#124;&#124; And cause My people—Israel—to err.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9–10; Jeremiah 29:23; Ezekiel 22:25"> And I have seen a horrible thing in prophets of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; Committing adultery, and walking falsely, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they strengthened the hands of evildoers, &#124;&#124; So that they have not turned back, &#124;&#124; Each from his wickedness, &#124;&#124; All of them have been as Sodom to me, &#124;&#124; And its inhabitants as Gomorrah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:14; Jeremiah 9:15; Lamentations 3:5"> Therefore, thus said YHWH of Hosts, concerning the prophets: “Behold, I am causing them to eat wormwood, &#124;&#124; And have caused them to drink water of gall, &#124;&#124; For from prophets of Jerusalem &#124;&#124; Profanity has gone forth to all the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14; Ezekiel 13:6; Galatians 1:8–9"> Thus said YHWH of Hosts: “You do not listen to the words &#124;&#124; Of the prophets who are prophesying to you, &#124;&#124; They are making you vain things, &#124;&#124; They speak a vision of their own heart, &#124;&#124; Not from the mouth of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Jeremiah 13:10; Amos 9:10"> Diligently saying to those despising, &#124;&#124; The word of YHWH: Peace is for you, &#124;&#124; And &#91;to&#93; everyone walking in the stubbornness of his heart, they have said: Evil does not come to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:22; Isaiah 40:13–14; Amos 3:7"> For who has stood in the counsel of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And sees and hears His word? Who has regarded My word, and listens?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:23; Jeremiah 25:32; Amos 1:14"> Behold, a whirlwind of YHWH—Fury has gone out, even a piercing whirlwind, &#124;&#124; It stays on the head of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:24; Isaiah 55:11; Zechariah 1:6"> The anger of YHWH does not turn back &#124;&#124; Until His doing, and until His establishing, &#124;&#124; The thoughts of His heart, &#124;&#124; In the latter end of the days &#124;&#124; You attend to it with understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14; Jeremiah 27:15; Jeremiah 23:32"> “I have not sent the prophets, and they have run, &#124;&#124; I have not spoken to them, and they have prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:18; Zechariah 1:4; Jeremiah 25:5"> But if they stood in My counsel, &#124;&#124; Then they cause My people to hear My words, &#124;&#124; And they turn them back from their evil way, &#124;&#124; And from the evil of their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 139:1–10; 1 Kings 20:23; 1 Kings 20:28"> &#91;Am&#93; I a God near,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And not a God far off?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Psalms 139:7; Psalms 90:8"> Is anyone hidden in secret places, &#124;&#124; And I do not see him?” A declaration of YHWH, “Do I not fill the heavens and the earth?” A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:32; Jeremiah 23:28; Jeremiah 29:8"> “I have heard that which the prophets said, &#124;&#124; Who prophesy falsehood in My Name, saying, &#124;&#124; I have dreamed, I have dreamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:1–2; Jeremiah 14:14; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> Until when is it in the heart of the prophets? The prophets of falsehood, &#124;&#124; Indeed, prophets of the deceit of their heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 8:33–34; Judges 3:7; Acts 13:8"> Who are devising to cause My people &#124;&#124; To forget My Name by their dreams, &#124;&#124; That they each recount to his neighbor, &#124;&#124; As their fathers forgot My Name for Ba‘al.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 1 Timothy 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:12–13"> The prophet with whom &#91;is&#93; a dream, &#124;&#124; Let him recount the dream, &#124;&#124; And he with whom &#91;is&#93; My word, &#124;&#124; Let him truly speak My word. What does the straw &#91;have to do&#93; with the grain?” A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Jeremiah 5:14; Luke 24:32"> “Is it not thus? My word &#91;is&#93; as fire,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH. “And as a hammer—it breaks a rock in pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; Ezekiel 13:8; Jeremiah 14:14–15"> Therefore, behold, I &#91;am&#93; against the prophets,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “Each stealing My words from his neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:17; 2 Chronicles 18:10–12; 2 Chronicles 18:5"> Behold, I &#91;am&#93; against the prophets,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “Who are making their tongue smooth, &#124;&#124; And they affirm—an affirmation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:7–18; Zephaniah 3:4; Lamentations 2:14"> Behold, I &#91;am&#93; against the prophets of false dreams,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And they recount them, and cause My people to err, &#124;&#124; By their falsehoods, and by their instability, &#124;&#124; And I have not sent them, &#124;&#124; Nor have I commanded them, &#124;&#124; And they are not profitable to this people at all,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Malachi 1:1; Jeremiah 12:7; Habakkuk 1:1"> “And when this people, or the prophet, &#124;&#124; Or a priest, asks you, saying, What &#91;is&#93; the burden of YHWH? Then you have said to them: You &#91;are&#93; the burden, and I have left you,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 13:3; Lamentations 2:14"> “And the prophet, and the priest, and the people, &#124;&#124; That says, The burden of YHWH, &#124;&#124; I have seen after that man, and after his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Jeremiah 42:4; Jeremiah 33:3"> Thus you each say to his neighbor, &#124;&#124; And each to his brother: What has YHWH answered? And what has YHWH spoken?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:10; 2 Peter 3:16; Isaiah 3:8"> And you do not mention the burden of YHWH anymore, &#124;&#124; For the burden to each is His word, &#124;&#124; And you have overturned the words of the living God, YHWH of Hosts, our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Thus you say to the prophet, &#124;&#124; What has YHWH answered you? And what has YHWH spoken?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:13–14"> And if you say, The burden of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Therefore, thus said YHWH: Because of your saying this word, &#124;&#124; The burden of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And I send to you, saying, &#124;&#124; You do not say, The burden of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:33; Ezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 13:13"> Therefore, behold, I have utterly taken you away, &#124;&#124; And I have sent you out, &#124;&#124; And the city that I gave to you, &#124;&#124; And to your fathers, from before My face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:11; Ezekiel 5:14–15; Jeremiah 42:18"> And I have put continuous reproach on you, &#124;&#124; And continuous shame that is not forgotten!”</span>
