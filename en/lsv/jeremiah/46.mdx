<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Zechariah 2:8; Jeremiah 4:7"> That which has been the word of YHWH to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 23:29; Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 25:19"> for Egypt, concerning the force of Pharaoh-Necho king of Egypt, that has been by the Euphrates River, in Carchemish, that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has struck, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 21:5; Joel 3:9; Nahum 2:1"> “Set shield and buckler in array, &#124;&#124; And draw near to battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:9–11; Jeremiah 51:3; 1 Samuel 17:5"> Gird the horses, and go up, you horsemen, &#124;&#124; And station yourselves with helmets, &#124;&#124; Polish the javelins, put on the coats of mail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:29; Jeremiah 6:25; Jeremiah 46:21"> Why have I seen them dismayed &#91;and&#93; turned backward? And their mighty ones are beaten down, &#124;&#124; And they have fled &#91;to&#93; a refuge, &#124;&#124; And did not turn their face, &#124;&#124; Fear &#91;is&#93; all around—a declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 11:19; Jeremiah 46:12; Psalms 27:2"> The swift do not flee, nor do the mighty escape, &#124;&#124; Northward, by the side of the Euphrates River, &#124;&#124; They have stumbled and fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:2; Isaiah 8:7–8; Daniel 11:22"> Who is this? He comes up as a flood, &#124;&#124; His waters shake themselves as rivers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 10:13–16; Ezekiel 32:2; Isaiah 37:24–26"> Egypt comes up as a flood, &#124;&#124; And the waters shake themselves as rivers. And he says, I go up; I cover the land, I destroy the city and the inhabitants in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 66:19; Ezekiel 27:10; Nahum 3:9"> Go up, you horses; and boast yourselves, you chariots, &#124;&#124; And go forth, you mighty, &#124;&#124; Cush and Phut handling the shield, &#124;&#124; And Lud handling—treading the bow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:42; Joel 1:15; Jeremiah 46:2"> And that day &#91;is&#93; to Lord YHWH of Hosts &#124;&#124; A day of vengeance, &#124;&#124; To be avenged of His adversaries, &#124;&#124; And the sword has devoured, and been satisfied, &#124;&#124; And it has been watered from their blood, &#124;&#124; For a sacrifice &#91;is&#93; to Lord YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; In the land of the north, by the Euphrates River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:22; Micah 1:9; Nahum 3:19"> Go up to Gilead, and take balm, &#124;&#124; O virgin daughter of Egypt, &#124;&#124; You have multiplied medicines in vain, &#124;&#124; There is no healing for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nahum 3:8–10; Isaiah 19:2; Jeremiah 46:6"> Nations have heard of your shame, &#124;&#124; And your cry has filled the land, &#124;&#124; For the mighty stumbled on the mighty, &#124;&#124; Together they have fallen—both of them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:30; Jeremiah 43:10–13; Isaiah 19:1–25"> The word that YHWH has spoken to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the coming in of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, to strike the land of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:10; Jeremiah 44:1; Jeremiah 46:3–4"> “Declare in Egypt, and sound in Migdol, &#124;&#124; And sound in Noph, and in Tahpanhes, say, &#124;&#124; Stand firm and prepare yourself, &#124;&#124; For a sword has devoured around you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 66:15–16; Psalms 68:2; Psalms 18:39"> Why has your bull been swept away? He has not stood, because YHWH thrust him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 26:36–37; Jeremiah 51:9; Jeremiah 46:6"> He has multiplied the stumbling, &#124;&#124; Indeed, one has fallen on his neighbor, &#124;&#124; And they say: Rise, and we return to our people, &#124;&#124; And to the land of our birth, &#124;&#124; Because of the oppressing sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11–16; Exodus 15:9; 1 Kings 20:10"> They have cried there: Pharaoh king of Egypt &#91;is&#93; a desolation, &#124;&#124; The appointed time has passed by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:15; Judges 4:6; Joshua 19:22"> &#91;As&#93; I live—an affirmation of the King, &#124;&#124; YHWH of Hosts &#91;is&#93; His Name, &#124;&#124; Surely as Tabor &#91;is&#93; among mountains, &#124;&#124; And as Carmel by the sea—he comes in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 48:18; Ezekiel 30:13"> Make goods for yourself for removal, &#124;&#124; O inhabitant, daughter of Egypt, &#124;&#124; For Noph becomes a desolation, &#124;&#124; And has been burned up, without inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hosea 10:11; Jeremiah 47:2; Jeremiah 1:14"> Egypt &#91;is&#93; a very beautiful heifer, &#124;&#124; Destruction comes into her from the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:5; Obadiah 1:13; 2 Kings 7:6"> Even her hired ones in her midst &#91;are&#93; as calves of the stall, &#124;&#124; For even they have turned, &#124;&#124; They have fled together, they have not stood, &#124;&#124; For the day of their calamity has come on them, &#124;&#124; The time of their inspection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 14:8; Isaiah 29:4; Micah 1:8"> Its voice goes on as a serpent, &#124;&#124; For they go with a force, &#124;&#124; And they have come in to her with axes, &#124;&#124; As hewers of trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 6:5; Joel 2:25"> They have cut down her forest, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; For it is not searched, &#124;&#124; For they have been more than the grasshopper, &#124;&#124; And they have no numbering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:15; Ezekiel 29:1–21; Jeremiah 46:11"> The daughter of Egypt has been ashamed, &#124;&#124; She has been given into the hand of the people of the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:11; Jeremiah 43:12–13; Isaiah 20:5–6"> YHWH of Hosts, God of Israel, has said: Behold, I am seeing after Amon of No, &#124;&#124; And after Pharaoh, and after Egypt, &#124;&#124; And after her gods, and after her kings, &#124;&#124; And after Pharaoh, and after those trusting in him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:11; Jeremiah 44:30; Ezekiel 29:8–14"> And I have given them into the hand of those seeking their life, &#124;&#124; And into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, &#124;&#124; And into the hand of his servants, &#124;&#124; And afterward it is inhabited, &#124;&#124; As &#91;in&#93; days of old—a declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5; Jeremiah 30:10–11; Isaiah 43:1"> And you, you do not fear, My servant Jacob, &#124;&#124; Nor &#91;are&#93; you dismayed, O Israel, &#124;&#124; For behold, I am saving you from afar—And your seed from the land of their captivity, &#124;&#124; And Jacob has turned back, &#124;&#124; And has been at rest, and been at ease, &#124;&#124; And there is none disturbing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:32; Revelation 3:19; Jeremiah 10:24"> You do not fear, My servant Jacob, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; For I &#91;am&#93; with you, &#124;&#124; For I make an end of all the nations &#124;&#124; To where I have driven you, &#124;&#124; And I do not make an end of you, &#124;&#124; And I have reproved you in judgment, &#124;&#124; And do not entirely acquit you!”</span>
