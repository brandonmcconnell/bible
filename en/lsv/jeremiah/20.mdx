<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 2:37–38; 1 Chronicles 24:14; 2 Kings 25:18"> And Pashhur son of Immer the priest—who &#91;is&#93; also overseer, leader in the house of YHWH—hears Jeremiah prophesying these things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:13; Zechariah 14:10; Jeremiah 1:19"> and Pashhur strikes Jeremiah the prophet, and puts him in the stocks that &#91;are&#93; by the Upper Gate of Benjamin, that &#91;is&#93; by the house of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; Jeremiah 46:5; Lamentations 2:22"> And it comes to pass on the next day, that Pashhur brings Jeremiah out from the stocks, and Jeremiah says to him, “YHWH has not called your name Pashhur, but Terror on Every Side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:21; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 21:4–10"> For thus said YHWH: Behold, I am making you for a fear to yourself, &#124;&#124; And to all loving you, &#124;&#124; And they have fallen by the sword of their enemies, and your eyes are beholding, &#124;&#124; And all Judah I give into the hand of the king of Babylon, &#124;&#124; And he has removed them to Babylon, &#124;&#124; And he has struck them with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:13; Ezekiel 22:25; Lamentations 1:10"> And I have given all the strength of this city, &#124;&#124; And all its labor, and all its precious things, &#124;&#124; Indeed, I give all the treasures of the kings of Judah into the hand of their enemies, &#124;&#124; And they have spoiled them, and taken them, &#124;&#124; And have brought them into Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14–15; Lamentations 2:14; Ezekiel 22:28"> And you, Pashhur, and all dwelling in your house, &#124;&#124; Go into captivity. And you enter Babylon, &#124;&#124; And there you die, and there you are buried, &#124;&#124; You and all loving you, &#124;&#124; To whom you have prophesied falsely.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 9:7; Jeremiah 20:9; Lamentations 3:14"> You have persuaded me, O YHWH, and I am persuaded; You have hardened me, and prevail, &#124;&#124; I have been for a laughter all the day, &#124;&#124; Everyone is mocking at me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Jeremiah 6:10; Jeremiah 5:15–17"> Because from the time I speak I cry out, I shout, “Violence and destruction!” &#124;&#124; For the word of YHWH has been to me &#124;&#124; For reproach and for derision all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 39:3; Job 32:18–20; Acts 4:20"> And I said, “I do not mention Him, &#124;&#124; Nor do I speak anymore in His Name,” &#124;&#124; And it has been in my heart &#124;&#124; As a burning fire shut up in my bones, &#124;&#124; And I have been weary of containing, &#124;&#124; And I am not able.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Psalms 31:13; Isaiah 29:21"> For I have heard the evil report of many, “Fear &#91;is&#93; all around!” “Declare, and we declare it!” All my allies are watching &#91;for&#93; my halting, “Perhaps he is enticed, and we prevail over him, &#124;&#124; And we take our vengeance on him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:19; Jeremiah 1:8; Jeremiah 15:20"> And YHWH &#91;is&#93; with me, as a mighty, awesome One, &#124;&#124; Therefore my persecutors stumble and do not prevail, &#124;&#124; They have been exceedingly ashamed, &#124;&#124; For they have not acted wisely, &#124;&#124; Continuous confusion is not forgotten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Jeremiah 11:20; Psalms 54:7"> And, O YHWH of Hosts, trier of the righteous, &#124;&#124; Beholder of reins and heart, &#124;&#124; I see Your vengeance on them, &#124;&#124; For I have revealed my cause to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; Psalms 35:9–11; Psalms 69:33"> Sing to YHWH, praise YHWH, &#124;&#124; For He has delivered the soul of the needy &#124;&#124; From the hand of evildoers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:10; Job 3:3–16"> Cursed &#91;is&#93; the day in which I was born, &#124;&#124; The day that my mother bore me, &#124;&#124; Let it not be blessed!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 21:5–6; Luke 1:14; Jeremiah 1:5"> Cursed &#91;is&#93; the man who bore tidings &#91;to&#93; my father, &#124;&#124; Saying, “A male child has been born to you,” &#124;&#124; Making him very glad!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:22; Jeremiah 48:3–4; Deuteronomy 29:23"> Then that man has been as the cities, &#124;&#124; That YHWH overthrew, and did not relent, &#124;&#124; And he has heard a cry in the morning, &#124;&#124; And a shout at noontime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 10:18–19; Job 3:10–11; Ecclesiastes 6:3"> Because he has not put me to death from the womb, &#124;&#124; And my mother is to me—my grave, &#124;&#124; And her womb &#91;is&#93; a perpetual pregnancy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 3:20; Lamentations 3:1; 1 Peter 4:14–16"> Why &#91;is&#93; this? I have come out from the womb &#124;&#124; To see labor and sorrow, &#124;&#124; Indeed, my days are consumed in shame!</span>
