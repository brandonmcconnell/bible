<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:28; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 7:1"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:60; Ezekiel 16:8; Jeremiah 11:6"> “Go, and you have called in the ears of Jerusalem, saying, Thus said YHWH: I have remembered for you &#124;&#124; The kindness of your youth, the love of your espousals, &#124;&#124; Your going after Me in a wilderness, in a land not sown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 14:4; James 1:18; Isaiah 41:11"> Israel &#91;is&#93; holy to YHWH, &#124;&#124; The first-fruit of His increase, &#124;&#124; All consuming him are guilty, &#124;&#124; Calamity comes to them,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Micah 6:1; Isaiah 51:1–4"> Hear a word of YHWH, O house of Jacob, &#124;&#124; And all you families of the house of Israel—</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 17:15; Romans 1:21; Jeremiah 10:14–15"> Thus said YHWH: “What perversity have your fathers found in Me, &#124;&#124; That they have gone far off from Me, &#124;&#124; And go after vanity, and become vain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:10; Deuteronomy 8:14–16; Hosea 13:4"> And have not said, Where &#91;is&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; Who brings us up out of the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; Who leads us in a wilderness, &#124;&#124; In a land of deserts and pits, &#124;&#124; In a dry land, and of death-shade, &#124;&#124; In a land that none has passed through, &#124;&#124; Nor has man dwelt there?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Jeremiah 16:18; Numbers 13:27"> Indeed, I bring you into a land of fruitful fields, &#124;&#124; To eat its fruit and its goodness, &#124;&#124; And you come in and defile My land, &#124;&#124; And have made My inheritance an abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18; Jeremiah 5:31; Jeremiah 10:21"> The priests have not said, Where &#91;is&#93; YHWH? And those handling the Law have not known Me. And the shepherds transgressed against Me, &#124;&#124; And the prophets have prophesied by Ba‘al, &#124;&#124; And have gone after those who do not profit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:35–36; Jeremiah 2:35; Exodus 20:5"> Therefore, I yet plead with you,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And I plead with your sons’ sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 120:5; 1 Chronicles 23:1; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> For pass to the islands of Chittim and see, &#124;&#124; And send to Kedar and consider well, &#124;&#124; And see if there has been &#91;anything&#93; like this:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 1:23; Isaiah 37:19; Jeremiah 16:20"> Has a nation changed gods? (And they &#91;are&#93; no gods!) And My people has changed its glory &#124;&#124; For that which does not profit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Jeremiah 22:29; Jeremiah 6:19"> Be astonished, you heavens, at this, &#124;&#124; Indeed, be frightened, be greatly desolated,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 4:14; John 7:37; Jeremiah 17:13"> “For My people have done two evils, &#124;&#124; They have forsaken Me, a fountain of living waters, &#124;&#124; To hew out for themselves wells—broken wells, &#124;&#124; That do not contain the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 4:22; Isaiah 50:1; Genesis 15:3"> &#91;Is&#93; Israel a servant? Is he a child of the house? Why has he been for a prey?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 50:17; Jeremiah 9:11"> Young lions roar against him, &#124;&#124; They have given forth their voice, &#124;&#124; And make his land become a desolation, &#124;&#124; His cities have been burned without inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 43:7–9; Jeremiah 44:1"> Also sons of Noph and Tahapanes &#124;&#124; Consume you—the crown of the head!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:18; Jeremiah 2:19; Jeremiah 2:13"> Do you not do this to yourself? &#91;By&#93; your forsaking your God YHWH, &#124;&#124; At the time He is leading you in the way?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 13:3; Isaiah 31:1; Jeremiah 2:36"> And now, why do you &#91;go&#93; in the way of Egypt, &#124;&#124; To drink the waters of Sihor? And why do you &#91;go&#93; in the way of Asshur, &#124;&#124; To drink the waters of the River?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 3:9; Jeremiah 4:18; Jeremiah 2:17"> Your wickedness instructs you, &#124;&#124; And your backslidings reprove you, &#124;&#124; Know and see that an evil and a bitter thing &#91;is&#93; your forsaking your God YHWH, &#124;&#124; And My fear not being on you,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:2; Leviticus 26:13; Jeremiah 17:2"> “For from of old you have broken your yoke, &#124;&#124; Drawn away your bands, and say, I do not serve, &#124;&#124; For on every high height, and under every green tree, &#124;&#124; You are wandering—a harlot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:4; Exodus 15:17; Psalms 44:2"> And I planted you &#91;as&#93; a choice vine, wholly of true seed, &#124;&#124; And how have you been turned &#124;&#124; Into the degenerate shoots of a strange vine to Me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:1; Job 14:17; Hosea 13:12"> But though you wash with natron, &#124;&#124; And multiply soap to yourself, &#124;&#124; Your iniquity is marked before Me,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:31; Proverbs 30:12; Proverbs 30:20"> “How can you say, I have not been defiled, &#124;&#124; I have not gone after the Ba‘alim? See your way in a valley, know what you have done, &#124;&#124; A swift dromedary winding her ways,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:6; Jeremiah 2:27; Hosea 5:15"> A wild donkey accustomed to a wilderness, &#124;&#124; She has swallowed up wind in the desire of her soul, &#124;&#124; Her meeting—who turns her back? None seeking her weary themselves, &#124;&#124; In her month they find her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16; Jeremiah 18:12; Jeremiah 3:13"> Withhold your foot from being unshod, &#124;&#124; And your throat from thirst, &#124;&#124; And you say, It is incurable, &#124;&#124; No, for I have loved strangers, and I go after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezra 9:7; Nehemiah 9:32–34; Jeremiah 32:32"> As the shame of a thief when he is found, &#124;&#124; So has the house of Israel been put to shame, &#124;&#124; They, their kings, their heads, &#124;&#124; And their priests, and their prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:16; Hosea 7:14; Psalms 78:34–37"> Saying to wood, You &#91;are&#93; my father! And to a stone, You have brought me forth, &#124;&#124; For they turned to me the back and not the face, &#124;&#124; And in the time of their distress, &#124;&#124; They say, Arise, and save us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:13; Deuteronomy 32:37; Judges 10:14"> And where &#91;are&#93; your gods, that you have made to yourself? Let them arise, if they may save you, &#124;&#124; In the time of your distress, &#124;&#124; For—the number of your cities have been your gods, O Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:13; Daniel 9:11; Jeremiah 5:1"> Why do you strive with Me? All of you have transgressed against Me,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26; 1 Thessalonians 2:15; Acts 7:52"> “I have struck your sons in vain, &#124;&#124; They have not accepted instruction, &#124;&#124; Your sword has devoured your prophets, &#124;&#124; As a destroying lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:7–9; Deuteronomy 32:15; Micah 6:9"> O generation, see the word of YHWH: Have I been a wilderness to Israel? A land of thick darkness? Why have My people said, &#124;&#124; We wandered freely, &#124;&#124; We do not come to You again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 106:21; Jeremiah 13:25; Jeremiah 3:21"> Does a virgin forget her ornaments? A bride her bands? And My people have forgotten Me &#91;for&#93; days without number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 57:7–10; Jeremiah 2:36; Ezekiel 16:27"> Why do you make your ways pleasing to seek love? Therefore you have even taught the wicked your ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Kings 21:16; Jeremiah 19:4; 2 Kings 24:4"> Also the blood of innocent needy souls &#124;&#124; Has been found on your skirts, &#124;&#124; I have not found them by digging, &#124;&#124; But on all these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; 1 John 1:8–10; Job 33:9"> And you say, Because I have been innocent, &#124;&#124; Surely His anger has turned back from me? Behold, I have been judged with you, &#124;&#124; Because of your saying, I have not sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:22; Hosea 5:13; 2 Chronicles 28:16"> What? You are very vile to repeat your way, &#124;&#124; You are even ashamed of Egypt, &#124;&#124; As you have been ashamed of Asshur,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:19; Jeremiah 37:7–10; Jeremiah 17:5"> Also you go out from this, &#124;&#124; And your hands on your head, &#124;&#124; For YHWH has kicked at your confidences, &#124;&#124; And you do not give prosperity to them!”</span>
