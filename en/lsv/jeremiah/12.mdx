<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:18; 2 Kings 20:3; Psalms 17:3"> And You, O YHWH, You have known me, &#124;&#124; You see me, and have tried my heart with You, &#124;&#124; Draw them away as sheep to slaughter, &#124;&#124; And separate them for a day of slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:10; Psalms 107:34; Jeremiah 4:25"> Until when does the earth mourn, &#124;&#124; And the herb of the whole field wither? For the wickedness of those dwelling in it, &#124;&#124; Beast and bird have been consumed, &#124;&#124; Because they said, “He does not see our latter end.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12; Proverbs 24:10; Hebrews 12:3–4"> “For you have run with footmen, &#124;&#124; And they weary you, &#124;&#124; And how you fret yourself with horses! Even in the land of peace, &#124;&#124; &#91;In which&#93; you are confident—And how will you do in the rising of Jordan?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 26:25; Jeremiah 9:4; Micah 7:5–6"> For even your brothers and the house of your father, &#124;&#124; Even they dealt treacherously against you, &#124;&#124; Even they called after you fully, &#124;&#124; Do not trust in them when they speak good things to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:6; Jeremiah 11:15; Psalms 78:59–60"> I have forsaken My house, &#124;&#124; I have left My inheritance, &#124;&#124; I have given the beloved of My soul &#124;&#124; Into the hand of her enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Hosea 9:15; Jeremiah 2:15"> My inheritance has been to Me as a lion in a forest, &#124;&#124; She gave forth against Me with her voice, &#124;&#124; Therefore I have hated her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 56:9; Jeremiah 7:33; 2 Kings 24:2"> &#91;Is&#93; My inheritance a speckled bird to Me? Is the bird around against her? Come, assemble, every beast of the field, &#124;&#124; Come for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–7; Jeremiah 23:1; Isaiah 63:18"> Many shepherds destroyed My vineyard, &#124;&#124; They have trodden down My portion, &#124;&#124; They have made My desirable portion &#124;&#124; Become a wilderness—a desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:10; Isaiah 42:25; Jeremiah 14:2"> He has made it become a desolation, &#124;&#124; The desolation has mourned to Me, &#124;&#124; All the land has been desolated, &#124;&#124; But there is no one laying it to heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:6; Amos 9:4; Ezekiel 14:17"> Spoilers have come in on all high places in the plain, &#124;&#124; For the sword of YHWH is consuming, &#124;&#124; From the end of the land even to the end of the land, &#124;&#124; There is no peace to any flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:38; Haggai 1:6"> They sowed wheat, and have reaped thorns, &#124;&#124; They have become sick—they do not profit, &#124;&#124; And they have been ashamed of your increases, &#124;&#124; Because of the fierceness of the anger of YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:18; Zechariah 2:8; Zechariah 10:6–12"> Thus said YHWH: “Concerning all My evil neighbors, who are striking against the inheritance that I caused My people—Israel—to inherit: Behold, I am plucking them from off their ground, &#124;&#124; And I pluck the house of Judah out of their midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:6; Jeremiah 48:47; Jeremiah 49:39"> And it has been, after My plucking them out, &#124;&#124; I turn back, and have pitied them, &#124;&#124; And I have brought them back, &#124;&#124; Each to his inheritance, and each to his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:2; Joshua 23:7; Zephaniah 1:5"> And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; If they learn the ways of My people well, &#124;&#124; To swear by My Name, YHWH lives, &#124;&#124; As they taught My people to swear by Ba‘al, &#124;&#124; Then they have been built up in the midst of My people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 60:12; Psalms 2:8–12; Daniel 11:4"> And if they do not listen, &#124;&#124; Then I have plucked up that nation, &#124;&#124; Plucking up and destroying,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH!</span>
