<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:26–27; Jeremiah 51:1–14; Isaiah 13:1–3"> The word that YHWH has spoken concerning Babylon, concerning the land of the Chaldeans, by the hand of Jeremiah the prophet:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 46:1; Isaiah 21:9; Jeremiah 51:44"> “Declare among nations, and sound, &#124;&#124; And lift up an ensign, sound, do not hide, &#124;&#124; Say, Babylon has been captured, &#124;&#124; Bel has been put to shame, &#124;&#124; Merodach has been broken, &#124;&#124; Her grievous things have been put to shame, &#124;&#124; Her idols have been broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 51:62"> For a nation from the north has come up against her, &#124;&#124; It makes her land become a desolation, &#124;&#124; And there is not an inhabitant in it. From man even to beast, &#124;&#124; They have moved, they have gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31; Hosea 1:11; Hosea 3:5"> In those days, and at that time,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “Sons of Israel come in, &#124;&#124; They and sons of Judah together, &#124;&#124; Going on and weeping they go, &#124;&#124; And they seek their God YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:40; Hebrews 8:6–10; Isaiah 55:3"> They ask the way &#91;to&#93; Zion, &#124;&#124; Their faces &#91;are&#93; toward that place: Come in, and we are joined to YHWH, &#124;&#124; A perpetual covenant—never forgotten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6; Matthew 9:36; Ezekiel 34:14"> My people have been a perishing flock, &#124;&#124; Their shepherds have caused them to err, &#124;&#124; Causing them to go back &#91;on&#93; the mountains, &#124;&#124; They have gone from mountain to hill, &#124;&#124; They have forgotten their crouching-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:8; Jeremiah 2:3; Zechariah 11:5"> All finding them have devoured them, &#124;&#124; And their adversaries have said: We are not guilty, &#124;&#124; Because that they sinned against YHWH, &#124;&#124; The habitation of righteousness, &#124;&#124; And the hope of their fathers—YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Jeremiah 51:6; Revelation 18:4"> Move from the midst of Babylon, &#124;&#124; And go out from the land of the Chaldeans; And be as male goats before a flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 15:14; 2 Samuel 1:22"> For behold, I am stirring up, &#124;&#124; And am causing to come up against Babylon, &#124;&#124; An assembly of great nations from &#91;the&#93; land of the north, &#124;&#124; And they have set in array against her, &#124;&#124; From there she is captured, &#124;&#124; Its arrow—as a skillful hero—does not return empty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:12; Isaiah 33:23; Isaiah 33:4"> And Chaldea has been for a spoil, &#124;&#124; All her spoilers are satisfied,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 47:6; Ezekiel 25:15–17; Ezekiel 26:2–3"> “Because you rejoice, because you exult, &#124;&#124; O spoilers of My inheritance, &#124;&#124; Because you increase as a heifer &#91;at&#93; the tender grass, &#124;&#124; And cry aloud as bulls,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:43; Jeremiah 49:2; Galatians 4:26"> Your mother has been greatly ashamed, &#124;&#124; She who bore you has been confounded, &#124;&#124; Behold, the last of nations &#91;is&#93; a wilderness, &#124;&#124; A dry land, and a desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:16; Jeremiah 49:17; Lamentations 2:15–16"> Because of the wrath of YHWH it is not inhabited, &#124;&#124; And it has been a desolation—all of it. Everyone passing by at Babylon is astonished, &#124;&#124; And hisses because of all her plagues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:29; Habakkuk 2:17; 1 Samuel 17:20"> Set yourselves in array against Babylon all around, &#124;&#124; All you treading a bow, &#124;&#124; Shoot at her, have no pity on the arrow, &#124;&#124; For she has sinned against YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Revelation 18:6; Jeremiah 46:10"> Shout against her all around, &#124;&#124; She has given forth her hand, &#124;&#124; Her foundations have fallen, &#124;&#124; Her walls have been thrown down, &#124;&#124; For it &#91;is&#93; the vengeance of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Be avenged of her, as she did—do to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:9; Jeremiah 46:16; Isaiah 13:14"> Cut off the sower from Babylon, &#124;&#124; And him handling the sickle in the time of harvest, &#124;&#124; Because of the oppressing sword, &#124;&#124; Each to his people—they turn, &#124;&#124; And each to his land—they flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:15; John 10:10–12; 2 Kings 18:9–13"> Israel &#91;is as&#93; a scattered sheep, &#124;&#124; Lions have driven &#91;him&#93; away, &#124;&#124; At first, the king of Asshur devoured him, &#124;&#124; And now, at last, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has broken his bone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 10:12; Zephaniah 2:13–15; Nahum 1:1–3"> Therefore, thus said YHWH of Hosts, God of Israel: Behold, I am seeing after the king of Babylon, &#124;&#124; And after his land, &#124;&#124; As I have seen after the king of Asshur;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:18; Micah 7:14; Jeremiah 30:18"> And I have brought Israel back to his habitation, &#124;&#124; And he has fed on Carmel and on Bashan; And his soul is satisfied on Mount Ephraim and on Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 7:19; Jeremiah 31:34; Isaiah 1:9"> In those days, and at that time,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “The iniquity of Israel is sought, and it is not, &#124;&#124; And the sin of Judah, and it is not found, &#124;&#124; For I am propitious to those whom I leave!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:23; 1 Samuel 15:3; Jeremiah 50:3"> Against the land of Merathaim, go up against it, &#124;&#124; And to the inhabitants of Pekod, &#124;&#124; Dry up and devote their posterity,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And do according to all that I have commanded you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:19–21; Jeremiah 51:54–56; Isaiah 21:2–4"> A noise of battle &#91;is&#93; in the land, &#124;&#124; And of great destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:20–24; Isaiah 14:4–6; Revelation 18:16"> How it has been cut and broken, &#124;&#124; The hammer of the whole earth! How Babylon has been for a desolation among nations!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 9:4; Daniel 5:30–31; Job 40:2"> I have laid a snare for you, &#124;&#124; And you are also captured, O Babylon, &#124;&#124; And you have known, &#124;&#124; You have been found, and are also caught, &#124;&#124; For you have stirred yourself up against YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:55; Jeremiah 51:25; Amos 3:6"> YHWH has opened His treasury, &#124;&#124; And He brings out the weapons of His indignation, &#124;&#124; For a work &#91;is&#93; to Lord YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; In the land of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 14:23; Jeremiah 50:10; Isaiah 45:3"> Come in to her from the extremity, &#124;&#124; Open her storehouses, &#124;&#124; Raise her up as heaps, and devote her, &#124;&#124; Let her have no remnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:21; Isaiah 34:7; Jeremiah 48:44"> Slay all her cows, they go down to slaughter, &#124;&#124; Woe &#91;is&#93; on them, for their day has come, &#124;&#124; The time of their inspection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Jeremiah 50:15; Lamentations 1:10"> A voice of fugitives and escaped ones &#124;&#124; &#91;Is&#93; from the land of Babylon, &#124;&#124; To declare in Zion the vengeance of our God YHWH, &#124;&#124; The vengeance of His temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:56; Isaiah 47:10; Daniel 4:37"> Summon archers to Babylon, all treading the bow, &#124;&#124; Encamp against her all around, &#124;&#124; Let &#91;her&#93; have no escape; Repay to her according to her work, &#124;&#124; According to all that she did—do to her, &#124;&#124; For she has been proud against YHWH, &#124;&#124; Against the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:26; Jeremiah 18:21; Jeremiah 9:21"> Therefore her young men fall in her broad places, &#124;&#124; And all her men of war are cut off in that day,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Jeremiah 50:29; Ezekiel 39:1"> “Behold, I &#91;am&#93; against you, O pride,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; “For your day has come, the time of your inspection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:14; Jeremiah 49:27; Isaiah 10:12–15"> And pride has stumbled, &#124;&#124; And he has fallen, and has none raising up, &#124;&#124; And I have kindled a fire in his cities, &#124;&#124; And it has devoured all around him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 14:17; Exodus 9:2–3; Isaiah 58:6"> Thus said YHWH of Hosts: The sons of Israel are oppressed, &#124;&#124; And the sons of Judah together, &#124;&#124; And all their captors have kept hold on them, &#124;&#124; They have refused to send them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 54:5; Jeremiah 15:21; Isaiah 41:14"> Their Redeemer &#91;is&#93; strong, &#124;&#124; YHWH of Hosts &#91;is&#93; His Name, &#124;&#124; He thoroughly pleads their cause, &#124;&#124; So as to cause the land to rest, &#124;&#124; And He has given trouble to the inhabitants of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:6; Daniel 5:30; Daniel 5:7–8"> A sword &#91;is&#93; for the Chaldeans,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And it &#91;is&#93; on the inhabitants of Babylon, &#124;&#124; And on her heads, and on her wise men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:22; Nahum 3:13; Isaiah 44:25"> A sword &#91;is&#93; on the princes, &#124;&#124; And they have become foolish; A sword &#91;is&#93; on her mighty ones, &#124;&#124; And they have been broken down;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Nahum 3:13; Jeremiah 51:21"> A sword &#91;is&#93; on his horses and on his chariot, &#124;&#124; And on all the rabble who &#91;are&#93; in her midst, &#124;&#124; And they have become women; A sword &#91;is&#93; on her treasuries, &#124;&#124; And they have been spoiled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Revelation 16:12; Isaiah 44:27; Jeremiah 50:2"> A sword &#91;is&#93; on her waters, and they have been dried up, &#124;&#124; For it &#91;is&#93; a land of carved images, &#124;&#124; And they boast themselves in idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 13:20–22; Revelation 18:2; Jeremiah 25:12"> Therefore desert-dwellers dwell with howlers, &#124;&#124; Indeed, daughters of the ostrich have dwelt in her, &#124;&#124; And it is not inhabited anymore forever, &#124;&#124; Nor dwelt in from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 19:24–25; Jude 1:7; Isaiah 13:19–20"> As the overthrow by God of Sodom, &#124;&#124; And of Gomorrah, and of its neighbors,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “None dwell there, &#124;&#124; Nor does a son of man sojourn in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:27–28; Jeremiah 6:22–23; Isaiah 13:2–5"> Behold, a people has come from the north, &#124;&#124; Even a great nation, &#124;&#124; And many kings are stirred up from the sides of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 5:30; Isaiah 13:17–18; Isaiah 47:6"> They seize bow and javelin, &#124;&#124; They &#91;are&#93; cruel, and they have no mercy, &#124;&#124; Their voice sounds as a sea, and they ride on horses, &#124;&#124; Set in array as a man for battle, &#124;&#124; Against you, O daughter of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:31; Jeremiah 49:24; Isaiah 21:3–4"> The king of Babylon has heard their report, &#124;&#124; And his hands have been feeble, &#124;&#124; Distress has seized him; pain as a travailing woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:19–21; Isaiah 46:11; Psalms 89:6"> Behold, he comes up as a lion, &#124;&#124; Because of the rising of the Jordan, &#124;&#124; To the enduring habitation, &#124;&#124; But I cause to rest, I cause them to run from off her. And who is chosen? I lay a charge on her, &#124;&#124; For who &#91;is&#93; like Me? And who convenes Me? And who &#91;is&#93; this shepherd who stands before Me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:20; Jeremiah 51:10–11; Revelation 17:16–17"> Therefore, hear the counsel of YHWH, &#124;&#124; That He counseled concerning Babylon, &#124;&#124; And His plans that He has devised &#124;&#124; Concerning the land of the Chaldeans; Do little ones of the flock not drag them out, &#124;&#124; Does He not make the habitation desolate over them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:21; Revelation 18:9–19; Ezekiel 31:16"> From the sound of Babylon having been captured, &#124;&#124; The earth has been shaken, &#124;&#124; And a cry has been heard among nations!”</span>
