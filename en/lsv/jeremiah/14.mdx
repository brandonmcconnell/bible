<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:8"> That which has been the word of YHWH to Jeremiah concerning the matters of the scarcities:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 3:26; Jeremiah 8:21; 1 Samuel 5:12"> “Judah has mourned, and her gates have languished, &#124;&#124; They have mourned to the earth, &#124;&#124; And the cry of Jerusalem has gone up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:30; Psalms 40:14; 2 Kings 18:31"> And their majestic ones have sent their little ones to the water, &#124;&#124; They have come to ditches, &#124;&#124; They have not found water, &#124;&#124; They have turned back—their vessels empty! They have been ashamed, &#124;&#124; And have blushed and covered their head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joel 1:19–20; Joel 1:11; Jeremiah 3:3"> Because the ground has been broken, &#124;&#124; For there has been no rain in the land, &#124;&#124; Farmers have been ashamed, &#124;&#124; They have covered their head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 29:9; Job 39:1–4; Isaiah 15:6"> For even the doe in the field has brought forth—to forsake &#91;it!&#93; &#124;&#124; For there has been no grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:24; Job 39:5–6; Joel 1:18"> And wild donkeys have stood on high places, &#124;&#124; They have swallowed up wind like dragons, &#124;&#124; Their eyes have been consumed, for there is no herb.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 25:11; Hosea 5:5; Jeremiah 14:20–21"> Surely our iniquities have testified against us, &#124;&#124; O YHWH, work for Your Name’s sake, &#124;&#124; For our backslidings have been many, &#124;&#124; We have sinned against You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:13; Jeremiah 50:7; Isaiah 43:3"> O Hope of Israel—its Savior in time of trouble, &#124;&#124; Why are You as a sojourner in the land? And as a traveler turned aside to lodge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 63:19; Isaiah 59:1; Jeremiah 15:16"> Why are You as one mute? As a mighty one not able to save? And You &#91;are&#93; in our midst, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; And Your Name is called over us, &#124;&#124; Do not leave us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Hosea 8:13; Psalms 119:101"> Thus said YHWH concerning this people: “They have loved to wander well, &#124;&#124; They have not restrained their feet, &#124;&#124; And YHWH has not accepted them, &#124;&#124; Now He remembers their iniquity, &#124;&#124; And inspects their sin.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:16; Jeremiah 11:14; Exodus 32:32–34"> And YHWH says to me: “You do not pray for this people for good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:11; Ezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 1:28"> When they fast, I do not listen to their cry, and when they cause burnt-offering and present to ascend, I do not accept them; for I am consuming them by sword, and by famine, and by pestilence.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 4:10; 2 Peter 2:1"> And I say, “Aah! Lord YHWH, behold, the prophets are saying to them: You do not see a sword, indeed, famine is not &#91;on&#93; you, for I give true peace to you in this place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6–7; Ezekiel 12:24; Jeremiah 29:8–9"> And YHWH says to me: “The prophets are prophesying falsehood in My Name. I did not send them, nor command them, nor have I spoken to them. A false vision, and divination, and vanity—indeed, they are prophesying the deceit of their own heart to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12–13; Ezekiel 14:10; Jeremiah 23:14–15"> Therefore, thus said YHWH concerning the prophets who are prophesying in My Name and I have not sent them, and they are saying, Sword and famine is not in this land: By sword and by famine are these prophets consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Proverbs 1:31; Jeremiah 15:2–3"> And the people to whom they are prophesying are cast into out-places of Jerusalem, because of the famine and the sword, and they have none burying them—them, their wives, and their sons, and their daughters—and I have poured out their evil on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:21; Jeremiah 9:1; Jeremiah 13:17"> And you have said this word to them: Tears come down my eyes night and day, &#124;&#124; And they do not cease, &#124;&#124; For &#91;with&#93; a great breach, &#124;&#124; The virgin daughter of my people has been broken, &#124;&#124; A very grievous stroke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:15; Lamentations 1:20; Jeremiah 8:10"> If I have gone forth to the field, &#124;&#124; Then, behold, the pierced of the sword! And if I have entered the city, &#124;&#124; Then, behold, the diseased of famine! For both prophet and priest have gone up and down &#124;&#124; To a land that they did not know.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Lamentations 5:22; Jeremiah 8:15; Jeremiah 6:30"> Have You utterly rejected Judah? Has Your soul loathed Zion? Why have You struck us, &#124;&#124; And there is no healing to us? Looking for peace, and there is no good, &#124;&#124; And for a time of healing, and behold, terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:25; Psalms 32:5; 1 John 1:7–9"> We have known, O YHWH, our wickedness, &#124;&#124; The iniquity of our fathers, &#124;&#124; For we have sinned against You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:7; Psalms 106:45; Jeremiah 3:17"> Do not despise, for Your Name’s sake, &#124;&#124; Do not dishonor the throne of Your glory, &#124;&#124; Remember, do not break Your covenant with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joel 2:23; Jeremiah 5:24; Psalms 135:7"> Are there any among the vanities of the nations causing rain? And do the heavens give showers? Are You not He, O our God YHWH? And we wait for you, for You have done all these!</span>
</div>
