<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:5; 2 Kings 23:16; Jeremiah 7:32–34"> “At that time,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “They bring the bones of the kings of Judah, &#124;&#124; And the bones of its princes, &#124;&#124; And the bones of the priests, &#124;&#124; And the bones of the prophets, &#124;&#124; And the bones of inhabitants of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; Out of their graves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 23:5; Jeremiah 9:22; Deuteronomy 4:19"> And have spread them to sun, and to moon, &#124;&#124; And to all the host of the heavens, that they have loved, &#124;&#124; And that they have served, &#124;&#124; And that they have walked after, &#124;&#124; And that they have sought, &#124;&#124; And to which they have bowed themselves, &#124;&#124; They are not gathered, nor buried, &#124;&#124; They are for dung on the face of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 9:6; Jeremiah 29:14; Jeremiah 23:3"> And death is chosen rather than life &#124;&#124; By all the remnant who are left of this evil family, &#124;&#124; In all the remaining places, to where I have driven them,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 24:16; Micah 7:8; Hosea 6:1"> “And you have said to them, Thus said YHWH: Do they fall, and not rise? Does he turn back, and not return?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:6; Jeremiah 5:3; Zechariah 7:11"> Why has this people of Jerusalem &#124;&#124; Turned back—a continuous backsliding? They have kept hold on deceit, &#124;&#124; They have refused to turn back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 14:2; Ezekiel 18:28; Luke 15:17–19"> I have given attention, indeed, I listen, &#124;&#124; They do not speak right, &#124;&#124; No man has sighed over his wickedness, &#124;&#124; Saying, What have I done? Everyone has turned to his courses, &#124;&#124; As a horse is rushing into battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:3; Jeremiah 5:4–5; Proverbs 6:6–8"> Even a stork in the heavens has known her seasons, &#124;&#124; And turtle, and swallow, and crane, &#124;&#124; Have watched the time of their coming, &#124;&#124; And—My people have not known the judgment of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 9:41; Job 5:12–13; Isaiah 10:1–2"> How do you say, We &#91;are&#93; wise, &#124;&#124; And the Law of YHWH &#91;is&#93; with us? Surely, behold, it has worked falsely, &#124;&#124; The false pen of scribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:15; Psalms 119:98–100; Job 5:12"> The wise have been ashamed, &#124;&#124; They have been frightened, and are captured, &#124;&#124; Behold, they kicked against a word of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And what wisdom do they have?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:12–13; Ezekiel 33:31; Isaiah 56:10–12"> Therefore, I give their wives to others, &#124;&#124; Their fields to dispossessors, &#124;&#124; For from the least even to the greatest, &#124;&#124; Everyone is gaining dishonest gain, &#124;&#124; From prophet even to priest, everyone is dealing falsely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Ezekiel 13:10–16; Lamentations 2:14"> And they heal the breach of the daughter of My people slightly, &#124;&#124; Saying, Peace, peace! And there is no peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:3; Isaiah 3:9; Jeremiah 6:15"> They were ashamed when they did abomination! Indeed, they are not ashamed at all, &#124;&#124; And they have not known blushing, &#124;&#124; Therefore, they fall among falling ones, &#124;&#124; They stumble in the time of their inspection, said YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Joel 1:7; Jeremiah 17:8"> I utterly consume them,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “There are no grapes in the vine, &#124;&#124; Indeed, there are no figs in the fig tree, &#124;&#124; And the leaf has faded, &#124;&#124; And the strength they have passes from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:15; Jeremiah 9:15; Lamentations 3:19"> Why are we sitting still? Be gathered, and we go into the fortified cities, &#124;&#124; And we are silent there, &#124;&#124; For our God YHWH has made us silent, &#124;&#124; Indeed, He causes us to drink water of gall, &#124;&#124; For we have sinned against YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:19; Jeremiah 4:10; Jeremiah 8:11"> Looking for peace—and there is no good, &#124;&#124; For a time of healing, and behold—terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 18:29; Judges 5:22; Jeremiah 4:15–16"> The snorting of his horses has been heard from Dan, &#124;&#124; From the voice of the neighings of his mighty ones, &#124;&#124; All the land has trembled, &#124;&#124; And they come in and consume the land and its fullness, &#124;&#124; The city and the inhabitants in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:11; Psalms 58:4–5; Deuteronomy 32:24"> For behold, I am sending serpents among you, &#124;&#124; Vipers that have no charmer, &#124;&#124; And they have bitten you,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 1:16–17; Jeremiah 6:24; Isaiah 22:4"> My sorrow &#91;is&#93; beyond comfort, &#124;&#124; My heart &#91;is&#93; sick in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 39:3; Isaiah 13:5; Psalms 135:21"> Behold, the voice of a cry of the daughter of my people from a far-off land, &#124;&#124; Is YHWH not in Zion? Is her King not in her? “Why have they provoked Me with their carved images, &#124;&#124; With the vanities of a foreigner?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; Matthew 25:1–12; Luke 13:25"> Harvest has passed, summer has ended, &#124;&#124; And we have not been saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:17; Joel 2:6; Nahum 2:10"> For a breach of the daughter of my people I have been broken, &#124;&#124; I have been dark, &#124;&#124; Astonishment has seized me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:11; Genesis 37:25; Jeremiah 51:8"> Is there no balm in Gilead? Is there no physician there? Why has the health of the daughter of my people not gone up?</span>
