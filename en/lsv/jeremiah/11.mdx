<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word that has been to Jeremiah from YHWH, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:6; Exodus 19:5; 2 Chronicles 29:10"> “Hear the words of this covenant, and you have spoken to the men of Judah, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:26; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 29:19–20"> and you have said to them, Thus said YHWH God of Israel: Cursed &#91;is&#93; the man who does not obey the words of this covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:12; Jeremiah 7:22–23; Deuteronomy 4:20"> That I commanded your fathers, &#124;&#124; In the day of My bringing them out from the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; Out of the iron furnace, saying, Listen to My voice, and you have done them, &#124;&#124; According to all that I command you, &#124;&#124; And you have been to Me for a people, &#124;&#124; And I am to you for God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:12–13; Psalms 105:9–11; Jeremiah 28:6"> In order to establish the oath that I have sworn to your fathers, &#124;&#124; To give to them a land flowing with milk and honey, as this day.” And I answer and say, “Amen, O YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:22; Romans 2:13; Jeremiah 7:2"> And YHWH says to me, “Proclaim all these words in the cities of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem, saying, &#124;&#124; Hear the words of this covenant, &#124;&#124; And you have done them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15; Ephesians 4:17; 1 Samuel 8:9"> For I certainly testified against your fathers, &#124;&#124; In the day of My bringing them up out of the land of Egypt—until this day, &#124;&#124; Rising early and testifying, saying, Listen to My voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 7:26; Jeremiah 35:15"> And they have not listened nor inclined their ear, &#124;&#124; And they each walk in the stubbornness of their evil heart, &#124;&#124; And I bring on them all the words of this covenant, &#124;&#124; That I commanded to do, and they did not.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 6:9; Matthew 26:3–4; Micah 3:11"> And YHWH says to me: “A conspiracy is found in the men of Judah, &#124;&#124; And in the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:16; Ezekiel 16:59; Psalms 78:8–10"> They have turned back to the iniquities of their first fathers, &#124;&#124; Who refused to hear My words, &#124;&#124; And they have gone after other gods to serve them, &#124;&#124; The house of Israel and the house of Judah &#124;&#124; Have made void My covenant that I made with their fathers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 1:28; Isaiah 1:15"> Therefore, thus said YHWH: “Behold, I am bringing calamity on them, &#124;&#124; That they are not able to go out from, &#124;&#124; And they have cried to Me, &#124;&#124; And I do not listen to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Deuteronomy 32:37; 2 Chronicles 28:22"> And the cities of Judah, and inhabitants of Jerusalem have gone, &#124;&#124; And they have cried to the gods, &#124;&#124; To whom they are making incense, &#124;&#124; And they give no deliverance at all to them, &#124;&#124; In the time of their distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; 2 Kings 23:13; Isaiah 2:8"> For the number of your cities have been your gods, O Judah, &#124;&#124; And &#91;for&#93; the number of the streets of Jerusalem You have placed altars to a shameful thing, &#124;&#124; Altars to make incense to Ba‘al.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:16; Jeremiah 11:11; 1 John 5:16"> And you, you do not pray for this people, &#124;&#124; Nor do you lift up cry and prayer for them, &#124;&#124; For I do not listen in the time of their calling to Me for their distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 2:14; Titus 1:15; Jeremiah 3:1–2"> What has My beloved to do in My house, &#124;&#124; Her doing wickedness with many, &#124;&#124; And does the holy flesh pass over from you? When you do evil, then you exult.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:17–24; Isaiah 27:11; Psalms 52:8"> An olive, green, beautiful, of good fruit, &#124;&#124; Has YHWH called your name, &#124;&#124; At the noise of a great tumult He has kindled fire against it, &#124;&#124; And its thin branches have been broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 5:2; Jeremiah 2:21; Jeremiah 12:2"> And YHWH of Hosts, who is planting you, &#124;&#124; Has spoken calamity concerning you, &#124;&#124; For the evil of the house of Israel, and of the house of Judah, &#124;&#124; That they have done to themselves, &#124;&#124; To provoke Me to anger, to make incense to Ba‘al.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:11–12; 2 Kings 6:9–10; Ezekiel 8:6–18"> And, O YHWH, cause me to know, and I know, &#124;&#124; Then You have showed me their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7–8; Jeremiah 20:10; Psalms 83:4"> And I &#91;am&#93; as a trained lamb brought to slaughter, &#124;&#124; And I have not known &#124;&#124; That they have devised schemes against me: “We destroy the tree with its food, &#124;&#124; And cut him off from the land of the living, &#124;&#124; And his name is not remembered again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:12; Jeremiah 17:10; 1 Samuel 16:7"> And O YHWH of Hosts, judging righteousness, &#124;&#124; Trying reins and heart, &#124;&#124; I see Your vengeance against them, &#124;&#124; For I have revealed my cause to You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; Amos 2:12; Luke 13:33–34"> Therefore, thus said YHWH concerning the men of Anathoth, who are seeking your life, saying, “Do not prophesy in the Name of YHWH, and you do not die by our hands.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; 2 Chronicles 36:17; Jeremiah 9:21"> Therefore, thus said YHWH of Hosts: “Behold, I am seeing after them, &#124;&#124; The chosen ones die by sword, &#124;&#124; Their sons and their daughters die by famine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:12; Micah 7:4; Hosea 9:7"> And they have no remnant, &#124;&#124; For I bring calamity to the men of Anathoth, &#124;&#124; The year of their inspection!”</span>
