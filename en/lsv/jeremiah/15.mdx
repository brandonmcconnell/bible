<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:14; Ezekiel 14:20–21; Jeremiah 14:11"> And YHWH says to me: “Though Moses and Samuel should stand before Me, &#124;&#124; My soul is not toward this people, &#124;&#124; Send from before My face, and they go out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:11; Zechariah 11:9; Revelation 13:10"> And it has come to pass, when they say to you, &#124;&#124; To where do we go out? That you have said to them, &#124;&#124; Thus said YHWH: Those who &#91;are&#93; for death—to death, &#124;&#124; And those who are for the sword—to the sword, &#124;&#124; And those who are for famine—to famine, &#124;&#124; And those who are for captivity—to captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:26; Leviticus 26:22; Leviticus 26:16"> And I have appointed four kinds over them,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “The sword to slay, and the dogs to drag, &#124;&#124; And the bird of the heavens, &#124;&#124; And the beast of the earth, to consume and to devour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; 2 Kings 23:26–27; 2 Kings 24:3–4"> And I have given them for a trembling &#124;&#124; To all kingdoms of the earth, &#124;&#124; Because of Manasseh son of Hezekiah king of Judah, &#124;&#124; For that which he did in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 51:19; Nahum 3:7; Jeremiah 21:7"> For who has pity on you, O Jerusalem? And who bemoans for you? And who turns aside to ask of your welfare?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Zephaniah 1:4; Isaiah 1:4"> You have left Me,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “You go backward, &#124;&#124; And I stretch out My hand against you, &#124;&#124; And I destroy you, &#124;&#124; I have been weary of relenting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 3:12; Jeremiah 51:2; Isaiah 41:16"> And I scatter them with a fan, &#124;&#124; In the gates of the land, &#124;&#124; I have bereaved &#91;them&#93;, &#124;&#124; I have destroyed My people, &#124;&#124; They did not turn back from their ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 3:25–1; Jeremiah 6:4–5; Luke 21:35"> Its widows have been more to Me than the sand of the seas, &#124;&#124; I brought on them—against the mother—A young man—a spoiler—at noon. I caused wrath and trouble to fall on her suddenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:5; Isaiah 47:9; Lamentations 1:1"> The bearer of seven has languished, &#124;&#124; She has breathed out her spirit, &#124;&#124; Her sun has gone in while yet day, &#124;&#124; It has been ashamed and confounded, &#124;&#124; And I give up their remnant to the sword before their enemies,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 6:22; Exodus 22:25; Proverbs 26:2"> Woe to me, my mother, &#124;&#124; For you have borne me, &#124;&#124; A man of strife and a man of contention to all the land, &#124;&#124; I have not lent on usury, &#124;&#124; Nor have they lent on usury to me—All of them are reviling me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Jeremiah 29:11–14; Psalms 106:46"> YHWH said, “Did I not direct you for good? Did I not intercede for you in a time of evil, &#124;&#124; And in a time of adversity, with the enemy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 40:9; Jeremiah 21:4–5; Isaiah 45:9"> Does one break iron, &#124;&#124; Northern iron and bronze?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:3; Psalms 44:12; Isaiah 52:3"> I give your strength and your treasures for a prey—not for price, &#124;&#124; Even for all your sins, and in all your borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:13; Deuteronomy 32:22; Jeremiah 17:4"> And I have caused your enemies &#124;&#124; To pass over into the land &#91;that&#93; You have not known, &#124;&#124; For a fire has been kindled in My anger, &#124;&#124; It burns against you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:3; Psalms 69:7–9; Luke 21:17"> You, You have known, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; Remember me, and inspect me, &#124;&#124; And take vengeance for me of my pursuers, &#124;&#124; In Your long-suffering do not take me away, &#124;&#124; Know &#91;that&#93; I have borne reproach for You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 10:9–10; Ezekiel 3:1–3; Psalms 119:101–103"> Your words have been found, and I eat them, &#124;&#124; And Your word is to me for a joy, &#124;&#124; And for the rejoicing of my heart, &#124;&#124; For Your Name is called on me, O YHWH, God of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; Lamentations 3:28; Psalms 26:4–5"> I have not sat in an assembly of deriders, &#124;&#124; Nor do I exult, because of your hand—I have sat alone, &#124;&#124; For You have filled me &#91;with&#93; indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:15; Job 34:6; Micah 1:9"> Why has my pain been continuous? And my wound incurable? It has refused to be healed, &#124;&#124; You are surely as a failing stream to me, &#124;&#124; Waters &#91;that are&#93; not steadfast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 3:7; Luke 21:15; Leviticus 10:10"> Therefore, thus said YHWH: “If you turn back, then I bring you back, &#124;&#124; You stand before Me, &#124;&#124; And if you bring out the precious from the vile, &#124;&#124; You are as My mouth! They return to you, &#124;&#124; And you do not return to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18–19; Acts 18:9–10; Ezekiel 3:9"> And I have made you to this people &#124;&#124; For a wall—bronze—fortified, &#124;&#124; And they have fought against you, &#124;&#124; And they do not prevail against you, &#124;&#124; For I &#91;am&#93; with you to save you, &#124;&#124; And to deliver you,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Isaiah 49:24–25; Jeremiah 50:34"> “And I have delivered you from the hand of evildoers, &#124;&#124; And I have ransomed you &#124;&#124; From the hand of the terrible!”</span>
