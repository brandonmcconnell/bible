<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:2; Luke 3:19–20; 2 Samuel 24:11–12"> Thus said YHWH: “Go down &#91;to&#93; the house of the king of Judah, and you have spoken this word there, and have said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 1:32; Isaiah 9:7; Jeremiah 22:4"> Hear a word of YHWH, O king of Judah, who are sitting on the throne of David, you, and your servants, and your people, who are coming in at these gates,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:12; Micah 6:8; Jeremiah 22:17"> Thus said YHWH: “Do judgment and righteousness, &#124;&#124; And deliver the plundered from the hand of the oppressor, &#124;&#124; And sojourner, orphan, and widow, you do not oppress nor wrong, &#124;&#124; And innocent blood you do not shed in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:25"> For if you certainly do this thing, &#124;&#124; Then kings sitting for David on his throne &#124;&#124; Have come in by the gates of this house, &#124;&#124; Riding on chariot, and on horses, &#124;&#124; He, and his servants, and his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 22:16"> And if you do not hear these words, &#124;&#124; I have sworn by Myself,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “That this house is for a desolation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Isaiah 6:11; Isaiah 27:10"> For thus said YHWH concerning the house of the king of Judah: “You &#91;are&#93; Gilead to Me—head of Lebanon, &#124;&#124; If not—I make you a wilderness, &#124;&#124; Cities &#91;that&#93; are not inhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 37:24; Isaiah 10:33–34; Jeremiah 4:6–7"> And I have separated destroyers for you, &#124;&#124; Each with his weapons, &#124;&#124; And they have cut down the choice of your cedars, &#124;&#124; And have cast them on the fire.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 9:8–9; 2 Chronicles 7:20–22; Deuteronomy 29:23–26"> And many nations have passed by this city, &#124;&#124; And they have each said to his neighbor, “Why has YHWH done thus to this great city?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 22:17; 2 Chronicles 34:25; Jeremiah 50:7"> And they have said, “Because that they have forsaken &#124;&#124; The covenant of their God YHWH, &#124;&#124; And bow themselves to other gods, and serve them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:2; Jeremiah 22:11; Isaiah 57:1"> You do not weep for the dead, nor bemoan for him, &#124;&#124; Weep severely for the traveler, &#124;&#124; For he does not return again, &#124;&#124; Nor has he seen the land of his birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:15; 2 Chronicles 36:1–4; 2 Kings 23:34"> For thus said YHWH concerning Shallum son of Josiah king of Judah, who is reigning instead of his father Josiah, who has gone forth from this place: “He does not return here again;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 23:34; Jeremiah 22:18"> For he dies in the place to where they have removed him, &#124;&#124; And he does not see this land again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 5:4; Micah 3:10; Leviticus 19:13"> Woe to him who is building his house by unrighteousness, &#124;&#124; And his upper chambers by injustice, &#124;&#124; He lays service on his neighbor for nothing, &#124;&#124; And he does not give his wage to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Isaiah 5:8–9; Haggai 1:4"> Who is saying, &#124;&#124; I build a large house for myself, &#124;&#124; And airy upper chambers, &#124;&#124; And he has cut out its windows for himself, &#124;&#124; Covered with cedar, and painted with vermillion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 23:25; Isaiah 3:10; Ecclesiastes 10:17"> Do you reign, because you are fretting yourself in cedar? Your father—did he not eat and drink? Indeed, he did judgment and righteousness, &#124;&#124; Then &#91;it is&#93; well with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:24; Isaiah 1:17; 1 John 2:3–4"> He decided the cause of the poor and needy, &#124;&#124; Then &#91;it is&#93; well—is it not to know Me?” A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 2:14; 2 Kings 24:4; Jeremiah 22:3"> “But your eyes and your heart are not, &#124;&#124; Except on your dishonest gain, &#124;&#124; And on shedding of innocent blood, &#124;&#124; And on oppression, and on doing of violence.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 13:30; Jeremiah 16:6; Jeremiah 16:4"> Therefore, thus said YHWH concerning Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah: “They do not lament for him, &#124;&#124; Oh, my brother! And, Oh, my sister! They do not lament for him, &#124;&#124; Oh, lord! And, Oh, his splendor!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:30; 1 Kings 21:23–24; 2 Chronicles 36:6"> He is buried &#91;with&#93; the burial of a donkey, &#124;&#124; Dragged and cast out there to the gates of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 27:12; Ezekiel 23:22; Lamentations 1:2"> Go up to Lebanon, and cry, &#124;&#124; And give forth your voice in Bashan, &#124;&#124; And cry from Abarim, &#124;&#124; For all loving you have been destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:30; Jeremiah 3:25; Jeremiah 7:22–28"> I have spoken to you in your ease, &#124;&#124; You have said, I do not listen, &#124;&#124; This &#91;is&#93; your way from your youth, &#124;&#124; For you have not listened to My voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:30–31; Jeremiah 12:10; Jeremiah 23:1–2"> Wind consumes all your friends, &#124;&#124; And your lovers go into captivity, &#124;&#124; Surely then you are ashamed, &#124;&#124; And have blushed for all your wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:24; Jeremiah 4:30–31; Jeremiah 22:6"> O dweller in Lebanon, making a nest among cedars, &#124;&#124; How gracious have you been when pangs come to you, &#124;&#124; Pain—as of a travailing woman.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:1; Song of Songs 8:6; 1 Chronicles 3:16"> “&#91;As&#93; I live,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “Though Coniah son of Jehoiakim king of Judah was a seal on My right hand, surely there I draw you away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:20–21; Jeremiah 21:7; 2 Kings 24:15–16"> And I have given you into the hand of those seeking your life, &#124;&#124; And into hands of which you are afraid, &#124;&#124; Into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, &#124;&#124; And into the hand of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 24:15; Jeremiah 15:2–4; Isaiah 22:17"> And I have cast you, &#124;&#124; And your mother who bore you, to another country, &#124;&#124; Where you were not born, and you die there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:14; 2 Kings 25:27–30; Psalms 86:4"> And to the land to where they are lifting up their soul to return, &#124;&#124; They do not return there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 31:12; Hosea 8:8; Jeremiah 48:38"> A grief—a despised broken thing—is this man Coniah? A vessel in which there is no pleasure? Why have they been cast up and down, &#124;&#124; He and his seed, &#124;&#124; Indeed, were they cast on to a land that they did not know?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Micah 1:2; Jeremiah 6:19"> Earth, earth, earth, hear a word of YHWH!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:30; Psalms 94:20; Matthew 1:11–16"> Thus said YHWH: “Write this man down &#91;as&#93; childless, &#124;&#124; A man—he does not prosper in his days, &#124;&#124; For none of his seed prospers, &#124;&#124; Sitting on the throne of David, &#124;&#124; And ruling in Judah again!”</span>
