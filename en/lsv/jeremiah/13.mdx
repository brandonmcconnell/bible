<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:11; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 27:2"> Thus said YHWH to me: “Go, and you have acquired a girdle of linen for yourself, and have placed it on your loins, and you do not cause it to enter into water.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:8; John 15:14; Proverbs 3:5"> And I get the girdle, according to the word of YHWH, and I place &#91;it&#93; on my loins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:8"> And there is a word of YHWH to me a second time, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 4:10; Psalms 137:1; Jeremiah 51:63–64"> “Take the girdle that you have acquired, that &#91;is&#93; on your loins, and rise, go to the Euphrates, and hide it there in a hole of the rock”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; Exodus 39:42–43; 2 Timothy 2:3"> and I go and hide it by the Euphrates, as YHWH commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And it comes to pass, at the end of many days, that YHWH says to me, “Rise, go to the Euphrates, and take the girdle there, that I commanded you to hide there”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:12; Isaiah 64:6; Jeremiah 13:10"> and I go to the Euphrates, and dig, and take the girdle from the place where I had hid it; and behold, the girdle has been marred, it is not profitable for anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying, “Thus said YHWH:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremiah 13:15–17; Zephaniah 3:11"> Thus I mar the excellence of Judah, &#124;&#124; And the great excellence of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; Jeremiah 9:14; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16"> This evil people, who refuse to hear My words, &#124;&#124; Who walk in the stubbornness of their heart, &#124;&#124; And go after other gods to serve them, &#124;&#124; And to bow themselves to them, &#124;&#124; Indeed it is—as this girdle, that is not profitable for anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Jeremiah 33:9; Psalms 81:11"> For as the girdle cleaves to the loins of a man, &#124;&#124; So I caused to cleave to Me &#124;&#124; The whole house of Israel, &#124;&#124; And the whole house of Judah,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “To be to Me for a people, and for a name, &#124;&#124; And for praise, and for beauty, &#124;&#124; And they have not listened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19"> And you have said this word to them, &#124;&#124; Thus said YHWH, God of Israel: Every bottle is full of wine, &#124;&#124; And they have said to you: Do we not certainly know that every bottle is full of wine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 63:6; Psalms 75:8; Psalms 60:3"> And you have said to them, &#124;&#124; Thus said YHWH: Behold, I am filling all the inhabitants of this land, &#124;&#124; And the kings who sit for David on his throne, &#124;&#124; And the priests, and the prophets, &#124;&#124; And all the inhabitants of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; &#91;With&#93; drunkenness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Psalms 2:9; Jeremiah 19:9–11"> And have dashed them against one another, &#124;&#124; And the fathers and the sons together,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “I do not pity, nor spare, nor do I have mercy, &#124;&#124; So as not to destroy them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 28:14–22; Revelation 2:29; Acts 4:19–20"> Hear, and give ear—do not be haughty, &#124;&#124; For YHWH has spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:9; Isaiah 8:22; Isaiah 5:30"> Give glory to your God YHWH, &#124;&#124; Before He causes darkness, &#124;&#124; And before your feet stumble on dark mountains, &#124;&#124; And you have waited for light, &#124;&#124; And He has made it for death-shade, &#124;&#124; And has appointed &#91;it&#93; for thick darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 2:2; Jeremiah 9:1; Jeremiah 14:17"> And if you do not hear it, &#124;&#124; My soul weeps in secret places, because of pride, &#124;&#124; Indeed, it weeps and wails, &#124;&#124; And the tear comes down my eyes, &#124;&#124; For the flock of YHWH has been taken captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:26; 2 Kings 24:12; 2 Kings 24:15"> Say to the king and to the mistress: Make yourselves low—sit still, &#124;&#124; For your principalities have come down, &#124;&#124; The crown of your beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:27; Jeremiah 39:9; Deuteronomy 28:52"> The cities of the south have been shut up, &#124;&#124; And there is none opening, &#124;&#124; Judah has been removed—all of her, &#124;&#124; She has been completely removed—</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:22; Jeremiah 1:14; Habakkuk 1:6"> Lift up your eyes, and see those coming in from the north, &#124;&#124; Where &#91;is&#93; the drove given to you, your beautiful flock?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 13:8; Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 5:31"> What do you say when He looks after you? And you have taught them &#91;to be&#93; over you—leaders for head? Do pangs not seize you as a travailing woman?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nahum 3:5; Jeremiah 16:10–11; Jeremiah 5:19"> And when you say in your heart, “Why have these met me?” For the abundance of your iniquity &#124;&#124; Have your skirts been uncovered, &#124;&#124; Have your heels suffered violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; Jeremiah 9:5; Matthew 19:24–28"> “Does a Cushite change his skin? And a leopard his spots? Can you also do good, who are accustomed to do evil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 1:4; Leviticus 26:33; Ezekiel 5:12"> And I scatter them as stubble, &#124;&#124; Passing away, by a wind of the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 20:29; Jeremiah 2:32; Psalms 9:17"> This &#91;is&#93; your lot, the portion of your measures from Me,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “Because you have forgotten Me, &#124;&#124; And trust in falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Lamentations 1:8; Hosea 2:10; Ezekiel 23:29"> I have also made your skirts bare before your face, &#124;&#124; And your shame has been seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hosea 8:5; Ezekiel 6:13; Ezekiel 24:13"> Your adulteries, and your neighings, &#124;&#124; The wickedness of your whoredom, on heights in a field, &#124;&#124; I have seen your abominations. Woe to you, O Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; You are not &#91;yet&#93; made clean—after when &#91;will you be&#93; again?”</span>
