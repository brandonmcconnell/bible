<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 18:23–32; 2 Chronicles 16:9; Ezekiel 22:30"> “Go to and fro in streets of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And see now and know, &#124;&#124; And seek in her broad places, &#124;&#124; If you find a man, &#124;&#124; If there is one doing judgment, &#124;&#124; Seeking steadfastness—Then I am propitious to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Jeremiah 4:2; Hosea 4:1–2"> And if they say, YHWH lives, &#124;&#124; Surely they swear to a falsehood.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:30; Jeremiah 7:28; Isaiah 9:13"> YHWH, Your eyes, are they not on steadfastness? You have struck them, and they have not grieved, &#124;&#124; You have consumed them, &#124;&#124; They have refused to receive instruction, &#124;&#124; They made their faces harder than a rock, &#124;&#124; They have refused to turn back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Hosea 4:6; Isaiah 28:9–13"> And I said, “Surely these &#91;are&#93; poor, &#124;&#124; They have been foolish, &#124;&#124; For they have not known the way of YHWH, &#124;&#124; The judgment of their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:20; James 2:5–7; Matthew 19:23–26"> I go to the great, and I speak with them, &#124;&#124; For they have known the way of YHWH, &#124;&#124; The judgment of their God.” Surely they have broken the yoke together, &#124;&#124; They have drawn away the bands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Habakkuk 1:8; Zephaniah 3:3"> Therefore a lion out of the forest has struck them, &#124;&#124; A wolf of the deserts spoils them, &#124;&#124; A leopard is watching over their cities, &#124;&#124; Everyone who is going out of them is torn, &#124;&#124; For their transgressions have been many, &#124;&#124; Their backslidings have been mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Joshua 23:7; Jeremiah 2:11"> “I am not propitious to you for this, &#124;&#124; Your sons have forsaken Me, &#124;&#124; And are satisfied by &#91;those who are&#93; not gods, &#124;&#124; I satisfy them, and they commit adultery, &#124;&#124; And at the house of a harlot &#124;&#124; They gather themselves together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:27; Deuteronomy 5:18; 2 Samuel 11:2–4"> Fed horses—they have been early risers, &#124;&#124; They each neigh to the wife of his neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:9; Jeremiah 5:29; Isaiah 1:24"> Do I not lay a charge for these?” A declaration of YHWH, “And on a nation such as this, &#124;&#124; Does My soul not avenge itself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:27; Matthew 22:7; Amos 9:8"> Go up on her walls, and destroy, &#124;&#124; And I do not make a completion, &#124;&#124; Turn her branches aside, &#124;&#124; For they &#91;are&#93; not YHWH’s,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:20; Isaiah 48:8; Hosea 6:7"> For the house of Israel has dealt treacherously against Me, &#124;&#124; And the house of Judah,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Ezekiel 12:22–28; Jeremiah 23:14–17"> They have lied against YHWH, &#124;&#124; And they say, “&#91;It is&#93; not He, &#124;&#124; Nor does evil come in against us, &#124;&#124; Indeed, we do not see sword and famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:15; Job 8:2; Jeremiah 14:13"> And the prophets become wind, &#124;&#124; And the word is not in them, &#124;&#124; Thus it is done to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:29; Jeremiah 1:9; Hosea 6:5"> Therefore, thus said YHWH, God of Hosts: “Because of your speaking this word, &#124;&#124; Behold, I am making My words become fire in your mouth, &#124;&#124; And this people wood, &#124;&#124; And it has devoured them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:49; Jeremiah 4:16; Isaiah 33:19"> Behold, I am bringing against you a nation from afar, &#124;&#124; O house of Israel,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “It &#91;is&#93; a strong nation, &#124;&#124; It &#91;is&#93; an ancient nation, &#124;&#124; A nation whose tongue you do not know, &#124;&#124; Nor understand what it speaks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 5:28; Psalms 5:9; Romans 3:13"> Its quiver &#91;is&#93; as an open tomb, &#124;&#124; All of them—mighty ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 26:16; Deuteronomy 28:33; Hosea 8:14"> And it has consumed your harvest and your bread, &#124;&#124; They consume your sons, and your daughters, &#124;&#124; It consumes your flock, and your herd, &#124;&#124; It consumes your vine and your fig tree, &#124;&#124; It makes your fortified cities poor, &#124;&#124; In which you are trusting—by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:10; Romans 11:1–5; Jeremiah 4:27"> And even in those days,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “I do not make you a completion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 9:8–9; Deuteronomy 28:47–48; Jeremiah 22:8–9"> And it has come to pass, when you say, Why has our God YHWH done all these &#91;things&#93; to us? That you have said to them, As you have forsaken Me, &#124;&#124; And serve the gods of a foreigner in your land, &#124;&#124; So you serve strangers in a land &#91;that is&#93; not yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Declare this in the house of Jacob, &#124;&#124; And sound it in Judah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 28:26; Ezekiel 12:2; Isaiah 6:9–10"> Now hear this, &#124;&#124; O people, foolish and without heart, &#124;&#124; They have eyes, and they do not see, &#124;&#124; They have ears, and they do not hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Psalms 104:9; Job 26:10"> Do you not fear Me?” A declaration of YHWH; “Are you not pained from My presence? Who has made sand the border of the sea, &#124;&#124; A perpetual limit, and it does not pass over it, &#124;&#124; They shake themselves, and they are not able, &#124;&#124; Indeed, its billows have sounded, and they do not pass over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:28; Deuteronomy 21:18; Psalms 95:10"> And this people has an apostatizing and rebelling heart, &#124;&#124; They have turned aside, and they go on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Joel 2:23; Genesis 8:22"> And they have not said in their heart, &#124;&#124; Now let us fear our God YHWH, who is giving rain, &#124;&#124; The autumn rain and the spring rain, in its season, &#124;&#124; He keeps the appointed weeks of harvest for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 59:2; Psalms 107:17; Psalms 107:34"> Your iniquities have turned these away, &#124;&#124; And your sins have kept the good from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 1:11; Psalms 64:5; Ezekiel 22:2–12"> For the wicked have been found among My people. He watches like one who sets snares, &#124;&#124; They have set up a trap—they capture men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 1:12; Jeremiah 9:6; Hosea 12:7–8"> As a cage full of birds, &#124;&#124; So their houses are full of deceit, &#124;&#124; Therefore they have been great, and are rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 1:23; Deuteronomy 32:15; Jeremiah 7:6"> They have been fat, they have shone, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they have surpassed the acts of the evil, &#124;&#124; They have not judged judgment, &#124;&#124; The judgment of the fatherless—and they prosper, &#124;&#124; And they have not judged the judgment of the needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:9; Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 9:9"> Do I not inspect for these,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “On a nation such as this, &#124;&#124; Does My soul not avenge itself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hosea 6:10; Jeremiah 23:14; Isaiah 1:2"> An astonishing and horrible thing has been in the land:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6; Jeremiah 14:14; Micah 2:11"> The prophets have prophesied falsely, &#124;&#124; And the priests bear rule by their means, &#124;&#124; And My people have loved &#91;it&#93; so, &#124;&#124; And what do they do at its latter end?”</span>
