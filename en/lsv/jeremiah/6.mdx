<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:14; Judges 1:21; Joshua 18:21–28"> “Strengthen yourselves, sons of Benjamin, &#124;&#124; From the midst of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And blow a horn in Tekoa, &#124;&#124; And lift up a flame over Beth-Haccerem, &#124;&#124; For evil has been seen from the north, &#124;&#124; And great destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:31; Isaiah 1:8; Isaiah 3:16–17"> I have cut off the lovely and delicate one, &#124;&#124; The daughter of Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 19:43; Jeremiah 39:1–3; 2 Kings 24:10–12"> Shepherds and their droves come to her, &#124;&#124; They have struck tents by her all around, &#124;&#124; They have each fed &#91;in&#93; his own station.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:8; Zephaniah 2:4; Joel 3:9"> “Sanctify the battle against her, &#124;&#124; Rise, and we go up at noon. Woe to us, for the day has turned, &#124;&#124; For the shades of evening are stretched out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:13; Isaiah 32:14; 2 Chronicles 36:19"> Rise, and we go up by night, &#124;&#124; And we destroy her palaces.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:19–20; Jeremiah 32:24"> For thus said YHWH of Hosts: “Cut down her wood, &#124;&#124; And pour out a mound against Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; She &#91;is&#93; the city to be inspected, &#124;&#124; She &#91;is&#93; full of oppression in her midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:8; Ezekiel 7:23; Ezekiel 7:11"> As the digging of a well, is &#91;for&#93; its waters, &#124;&#124; So she has dug &#91;for&#93; her wickedness, &#124;&#124; Violence and spoil is heard in her, &#124;&#124; Sickness and striking &#91;are&#93; continually before My face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:18; Hosea 9:12; Jeremiah 17:23"> Be instructed, O Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; Lest My soul be alienated from you, &#124;&#124; Lest I make you a desolation, &#124;&#124; A land &#91;that is&#93; not inhabited.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:9; Jeremiah 16:16; Obadiah 1:5–6"> Thus said YHWH of Hosts: “They surely glean, as a vine, the remnant of Israel, &#124;&#124; Put your hand back, as a gatherer to the baskets.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Jeremiah 7:26; John 7:7"> To whom do I speak, and testify, and they hear? Behold, their ear &#91;is&#93; uncircumcised, &#124;&#124; And they are not able to attend. Behold, a word of YHWH has been to them for a reproach, &#124;&#124; They do not delight in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:21; Jeremiah 20:9; Micah 3:8"> And I have been filled with the fury of YHWH &#124;&#124; (I have been weary of containing), “To pour &#91;it&#93; on the suckling in the street, &#124;&#124; And on the assembly of youths together, &#124;&#124; For even husband with wife are captured, &#124;&#124; &#91;The&#93; elderly with one full of days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:10; Isaiah 5:25; Deuteronomy 28:39–43"> And their houses have been turned to others, &#124;&#124; Fields and wives together, &#124;&#124; For I stretch out My hand against the inhabitants of the land,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:11; Jeremiah 8:10; Micah 3:11"> “For from their least to their greatest, &#124;&#124; Everyone is gaining dishonest gain, &#124;&#124; And from prophet even to priest, &#124;&#124; Everyone is dealing falsely,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:10; Jeremiah 23:17; Jeremiah 4:10"> And they heal the breach of the daughter of My people slightly, &#124;&#124; Saying, Peace, peace! And there is no peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:12; Matthew 15:14; Jeremiah 3:3"> They were ashamed when they did abomination! Indeed, they are not at all ashamed, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they have not known blushing, &#124;&#124; Therefore they fall among those falling, &#124;&#124; In the time I have inspected them, &#124;&#124; They stumble,” said YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 30:21; Deuteronomy 32:7; Jeremiah 18:15"> Thus said YHWH: “Stand by the ways and see, and ask for paths of old, &#124;&#124; Where &#91;is&#93; this—the good way? And go in it, &#124;&#124; And find rest for yourselves. And they say, We do not go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:4; Isaiah 58:1; Zechariah 7:11"> And I have raised up watchmen for you, &#124;&#124; Attend to the voice of the horn. And they say, We do not attend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:10; Jeremiah 31:10; Micah 6:5"> Therefore hear, O nations, and know, O congregation, &#124;&#124; That which &#91;is&#93; on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Jeremiah 8:9; Jeremiah 17:10"> Hear, O earth, behold, I am bringing evil on this people, &#124;&#124; The fruit of their plans, &#124;&#124; For they gave no attention to My words, &#124;&#124; And My law—they kick against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11; Amos 5:21–22; Isaiah 60:6"> Why &#91;is&#93; this to Me? Frankincense comes from Sheba, &#124;&#124; And the sweet cane from a far-off land, &#124;&#124; Your burnt-offerings &#91;are&#93; not for acceptance, &#124;&#124; And your sacrifices have not been sweet to Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; Ezekiel 3:20; Jeremiah 13:16"> Therefore, thus said YHWH: “Behold, I give stumbling blocks to this people, &#124;&#124; And fathers and sons have stumbled against them together, &#124;&#124; The neighbor and his friend perish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:41–43; Jeremiah 10:22; Jeremiah 6:1"> Thus said YHWH: “Behold, a people has come from a north country, &#124;&#124; And a great nation is stirred up from the sides of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 13:18; Jeremiah 50:42; Luke 21:25–26"> They take hold of bow and javelin, &#124;&#124; It &#91;is&#93; fierce, and they have no mercy, &#124;&#124; Their voice sounds as a sea, &#124;&#124; And they ride on horses, &#124;&#124; Set in array as a man of war, &#124;&#124; Against you, O daughter of Zion.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 50:43; Jeremiah 49:24"> We have heard its sound, our hands have been feeble, &#124;&#124; Distress has seized us, pain as of a travailing woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:29; Jeremiah 20:10; Psalms 31:13"> Do not go forth to the field, &#124;&#124; And do not walk in the way, &#124;&#124; For the enemy has a sword, &#124;&#124; Fear &#91;is&#93; all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:8; Zechariah 12:10; Isaiah 32:11"> O daughter of my people, &#124;&#124; Gird on sackcloth, &#124;&#124; And roll yourself in ashes, &#124;&#124; Make mourning &#91;as for&#93; an only one, &#124;&#124; A most bitter lamentation, &#124;&#124; For the spoiler suddenly comes against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18; Jeremiah 15:20; Ezekiel 3:8–10"> “I have given you a watchtower, &#124;&#124; A fortress among My people, &#124;&#124; And you know, and have tried their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:4; Jeremiah 5:23; Jeremiah 18:18"> All of them are turned aside by apostates, &#124;&#124; Walking slanderously—bronze and iron, &#124;&#124; All of them are corrupters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:13; Jeremiah 9:7; 1 Peter 4:12"> The bellows have been burned, &#124;&#124; The lead has been consumed by fire, &#124;&#124; A refiner has refined in vain, &#124;&#124; And the wicked have not been drawn away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hosea 9:17; Psalms 119:119; Isaiah 1:22"> They have called them rejected silver, &#124;&#124; For YHWH has kicked against them!”</span>
