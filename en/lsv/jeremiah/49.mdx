<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 1:13–15; Zephaniah 2:8–11; Ezekiel 25:2–10"> Concerning the sons of Ammon: “Thus said YHWH: Has Israel no sons? Has he no heir? Why has Malcam possessed Gad, &#124;&#124; And his people have dwelt in its cities?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:19; Ezekiel 21:20; Deuteronomy 3:11"> Therefore, behold, days are coming, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And I have sounded a shout of battle in Rabbah of the sons of Ammon, &#124;&#124; And it has been for a heap—a desolation, &#124;&#124; And her daughters are burned with fire, &#124;&#124; And Israel has succeeded its heirs, said YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:7; Jeremiah 48:37; Isaiah 15:2"> Howl, Heshbon, for Ai is spoiled, &#124;&#124; Cry, daughters of Rabbah, gird on sackcloth, &#124;&#124; Lament, and go to and fro by the hedges, &#124;&#124; For Malcam goes into captivity, &#124;&#124; His priests and his princes together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Jeremiah 9:23; Jeremiah 21:13"> Why do you boast yourself in valleys? Your valley is flowing, &#124;&#124; O backsliding daughter, &#124;&#124; Who is trusting in her treasures: Who comes to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:5; Jeremiah 49:29; Jeremiah 15:8"> Behold, I am bringing fear in on you, &#124;&#124; A declaration of Lord YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; From all around you, &#124;&#124; And you have each been driven out before it, &#124;&#124; And there is no gatherer of the wandering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:39; Jeremiah 48:47; Ezekiel 16:53"> And after this I return the captivity of the sons of Ammon, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 1:11–12; Jeremiah 25:21; Genesis 36:15"> Concerning Edom: “Thus said YHWH of Hosts: Is wisdom no longer in Teman? Has counsel perished from the intelligent? Has their wisdom vanished?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:23; Jeremiah 46:21; Jeremiah 49:30"> Flee, turn, go deep to dwell, you inhabitants of Dedan, &#124;&#124; For I brought the calamity of Esau on him, &#124;&#124; The time I inspected him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Obadiah 1:5–6; Isaiah 17:6"> If gatherers have come to you, &#124;&#124; They do not leave gleanings, &#124;&#124; If thieves in the night, &#124;&#124; They have destroyed their sufficiency!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:14; Malachi 1:3–4; Isaiah 45:3"> For I have made Esau bare, &#124;&#124; I have uncovered his secret places, &#124;&#124; And he is not able to be hidden, &#124;&#124; Spoiled &#91;is&#93; his seed, and his brothers, &#124;&#124; And his neighbors, and he is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 68:5; Hosea 14:3; James 1:27"> Leave your orphans—I keep alive, &#124;&#124; And your widows—trust on Me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:28–29; Obadiah 1:16; Jeremiah 25:15"> For thus said YHWH: They whose judgment is not to drink of the cup, &#124;&#124; Do certainly drink, &#124;&#124; And you &#91;are&#93; he that is entirely acquitted! You are not acquitted, for you certainly drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 22:16; Isaiah 34:6; Genesis 36:33"> For I have sworn by Myself, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; That for a desolation, for a reproach, &#124;&#124; For a dry place, and for a reviling—is Bozrah, &#124;&#124; And all her cities are for continuous ruins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 30:4; Isaiah 37:7; Ezekiel 7:25–26"> I have heard a report from YHWH, &#124;&#124; And an ambassador is sent among nations, &#124;&#124; Gather yourselves and come in against her, &#124;&#124; And rise for battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 1:51; Obadiah 1:2; Micah 7:10"> For behold, I have made you little among nations, &#124;&#124; Despised among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:25; Amos 9:2; Job 39:27"> Your terribleness has lifted you up, &#124;&#124; The pride of your heart, &#124;&#124; O dweller in clefts of the rock, &#124;&#124; Holding the high place of the height, &#124;&#124; For you make your nest high as an eagle, &#124;&#124; From there I bring you down, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:7; Jeremiah 50:13; Jeremiah 49:13"> And Edom has been for a desolation, &#124;&#124; Everyone passing by her is astonished, &#124;&#124; And hisses because of all her plagues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:33; Deuteronomy 29:23; Jeremiah 50:40"> As the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah, &#124;&#124; And its neighbors, said YHWH, &#124;&#124; No one dwells there, &#124;&#124; Nor does a son of man sojourn in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:5; Jeremiah 30:21; Jeremiah 50:44–46"> Behold, he comes up as a lion, &#124;&#124; Because of the rising of the Jordan, &#124;&#124; To the enduring habitation, &#124;&#124; But I cause to rest, &#124;&#124; I cause him to run from off her, &#124;&#124; And who is chosen? I lay a charge concerning her, &#124;&#124; For who is like Me? And who convenes Me? And who &#91;is&#93; this shepherd who stands before Me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:45; Malachi 1:3–4; Jeremiah 49:7"> Therefore, hear the counsel of YHWH, &#124;&#124; That He has counseled concerning Edom, &#124;&#124; And His plans that He has devised &#124;&#124; Concerning the inhabitants of Teman: Do little ones of the flock not drag them out, &#124;&#124; Does He not make their habitation desolate?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:46; Ezekiel 32:10; Isaiah 14:4–15"> The earth has shaken from the noise of their fall, &#124;&#124; The cry—its voice is heard at the Sea of Suph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:40–41; Hosea 8:1; Isaiah 13:8"> Behold, He comes up as an eagle, and flies, &#124;&#124; And He spreads His wings over Bozrah, &#124;&#124; And the heart of the mighty of Edom has been in that day, &#124;&#124; As the heart of a travailing woman.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 10:9; 2 Kings 18:34; Genesis 15:2"> Concerning Damascus: “Hamath and Arpad have been ashamed, &#124;&#124; For they have heard an evil report, &#124;&#124; They have been melted, sorrow &#91;is&#93; in the sea, &#124;&#124; It is not able to be quiet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Damascus has been feeble, &#124;&#124; She turned to flee, and fear strengthened her, &#124;&#124; Distress and pangs have seized her, as a travailing woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:9; Jeremiah 51:41; Isaiah 1:26"> How it is not left—the city of praise, &#124;&#124; The city of My joy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:30; Amos 4:10; Jeremiah 11:22"> Therefore her young men fall in her broad places, &#124;&#124; And all the men of war are cut off in that day, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Amos 1:3–5; 1 Kings 15:18–20; Jeremiah 43:12"> And I have kindled a fire against the wall of Damascus, &#124;&#124; And it consumed palaces of Ben-Hadad!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 6:3; Genesis 25:13; Isaiah 11:14"> Concerning Kedar, and concerning the kingdoms of Hazor that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has struck: “Thus said YHWH: Arise, go up to Kedar, &#124;&#124; And spoil the sons of the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:5; Jeremiah 6:25; Habakkuk 3:7"> They take their tents and their flock, &#124;&#124; Their curtains, and all their vessels, &#124;&#124; And they carry away their camels for themselves, &#124;&#124; And they called concerning them, &#124;&#124; Fear &#91;is&#93; all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 27:6; Isaiah 10:7"> Flee, bemoan mightily, go deep to dwell, &#124;&#124; You inhabitants of Hazor—a declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; For Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has given counsel against you, &#124;&#124; Indeed, he devises a scheme against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:11; Numbers 23:9; Deuteronomy 33:28"> Rise, go up to a nation at rest, &#124;&#124; Dwelling confidently, a declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; It has no double gates nor bar, &#124;&#124; They dwell alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:26; Jeremiah 25:23; Jeremiah 49:36"> And their camels have been for a prey, &#124;&#124; And the multitude of their livestock for a spoil, &#124;&#124; And I have scattered them to every wind, &#124;&#124; Who cut off the corner &#91;of the beard&#93;, &#124;&#124; And from all its passages I bring in their calamity, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:22; Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 51:37"> And Hazor has been for a habitation of dragons, &#124;&#124; A desolation for all time, &#124;&#124; No one dwells there, nor does a son of man sojourn in it!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 10:22; Daniel 8:2; Jeremiah 25:25"> That which has been the word of YHWH to Jeremiah the prophet concerning Elam, in the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 22:6; Jeremiah 51:56; Psalms 46:9"> “Thus said YHWH of Hosts: Behold, I am breaking the bow of Elam, &#124;&#124; The beginning of their might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:32; Revelation 7:1; Ezekiel 5:10"> And I have brought four winds to Elam, &#124;&#124; From the four ends of the heavens, &#124;&#124; And have scattered them to all these winds, &#124;&#124; And there is no nation to where outcasts of Elam do not come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:16; Jeremiah 48:2; Jeremiah 49:22"> And I have frightened Elam before their enemies, &#124;&#124; And before those seeking their life, &#124;&#124; And I have brought calamity on them, &#124;&#124; The heat of My anger, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And I have sent the sword after them, &#124;&#124; Until I have consumed them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:10; Daniel 7:9–14"> And I have set My throne in Elam, &#124;&#124; And I have destroyed king and princes from there, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:47; Hosea 3:5; Micah 4:1"> And it has come to pass, in the latter end of the days, I return the captivity of Elam, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH!”</span>
