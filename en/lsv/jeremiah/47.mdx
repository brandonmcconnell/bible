<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:20; Genesis 10:19; 1 Kings 4:24"> That which has been the word of YHWH to Jeremiah concerning the Philistines, before Pharaoh strikes Gaza:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:20; Jeremiah 8:16; Isaiah 8:7–8"> “Thus said YHWH: Behold, waters are going up from the north, &#124;&#124; And have been for an overflowing stream, &#124;&#124; And they overflow the land and its fullness, &#124;&#124; The city, and those dwelling in it, &#124;&#124; And men have cried out, &#124;&#124; And every inhabitant of the land has howled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:16; Judges 5:22; Nahum 3:2–3"> From the sound of the stamping of the hooves of his mighty ones, &#124;&#124; From the rushing of his chariot, &#124;&#124; The noise of his wheels, &#124;&#124; Fathers have not turned to sons, &#124;&#124; From feebleness of hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 9:7; Deuteronomy 2:23; Isaiah 20:6"> Because of the day that has come to spoil all the Philistines, &#124;&#124; To cut off every helping remnant from Tyre and from Sidon. For YHWH is spoiling the Philistines, &#124;&#124; The remnant of the island of Caphtor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:37; Jeremiah 25:20; Jeremiah 41:5"> Baldness has come to Gaza, &#124;&#124; Ashkelon has been cut off, &#124;&#124; O remnant of their valley, &#124;&#124; Until when do you cut yourself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:3–5; Jeremiah 12:12; John 18:11"> Behold, sword of YHWH, until when are you not quiet? Be removed to your sheath, rest and cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:17; Micah 6:9; Isaiah 10:6"> How will it be quiet, &#124;&#124; And YHWH has given a charge to it, &#124;&#124; Against Ashkelon and against the seashore? He has appointed it there!”</span>
