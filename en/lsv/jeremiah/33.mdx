<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:28; Jeremiah 37:21; Jeremiah 32:2–3"> And there is a word of YHWH to Jeremiah a second time—and he &#91;is&#93; yet detained in the court of the prison—saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:3; Exodus 15:3; Isaiah 37:26"> “Thus said YHWH who has made it, YHWH who has formed it, to establish it, YHWH &#91;is&#93; His Name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12; Isaiah 65:24; Ephesians 3:20"> Call to Me, and I answer you, indeed, I declare to you great and inaccessible things—you have not known them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:24; Habakkuk 1:10; Ezekiel 4:2"> For thus said YHWH, God of Israel, concerning the houses of this city, and concerning the houses of the kings of Judah that are broken down for the &#91;siege&#93; mounds, and for the tool;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17; Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 21:4–7"> they are coming to fight with the Chaldeans, and to fill them with the carcasses of men, whom I have struck in My anger, and in My fury, and &#91;for&#93; whom I have hidden My face from this city, because of all their evil:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 10:10; Isaiah 30:26; Titus 3:5–6"> Behold, I am increasing health and cure to it, &#124;&#124; And have healed them, and revealed to them &#124;&#124; The abundance of peace and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:26; Jeremiah 30:3; Amos 9:14–15"> And I have turned back the captivity of Judah, &#124;&#124; And the captivity of Israel, &#124;&#124; And I have built them as at the first,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; Micah 7:18–19; Psalms 51:2"> And cleansed them from all their iniquity &#124;&#124; That they have sinned against Me, &#124;&#124; And I have pardoned all their iniquities &#124;&#124; That they have sinned against Me, &#124;&#124; And that they transgressed against Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 126:2–3; Isaiah 60:5; Zephaniah 3:17–20"> And it has been for a name of joy to Me, &#124;&#124; For praise, and for beauty, to all nations of the earth, &#124;&#124; Who hear of all the good that I am doing them, &#124;&#124; And they have feared, &#124;&#124; And they have trembled for all the good, &#124;&#124; And for all the peace, that I am doing to it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:43; Jeremiah 32:36; Ezekiel 37:11"> Thus said YHWH: “Again is heard in this place—of which you are saying, It &#91;is&#93; dry, without man and without beast, &#124;&#124; In cities of Judah, and in streets of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; That are desolated, without man, &#124;&#124; And without inhabitant, and without beast—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:34; Psalms 106:1; Psalms 107:22"> A voice of joy and a voice of gladness, &#124;&#124; Voice of bridegroom and voice of bride, &#124;&#124; The voice of those saying, &#124;&#124; Thank YHWH of Hosts, for YHWH &#91;is&#93; good, &#124;&#124; For His kindness &#91;is&#93; for all time, &#124;&#124; Who are bringing in thanksgiving to the house of YHWH, &#124;&#124; For I return the captivity of the land, &#124;&#124; As at the first,” said YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 65:10; Ezekiel 34:12–15; Jeremiah 36:29"> Thus said YHWH of Hosts: “Again there is in this place—that is dry, &#124;&#124; Without man and beast, &#124;&#124; And in all its cities—a habitation of shepherds, &#124;&#124; Causing the flock to lie down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:32; Luke 15:4; Jeremiah 17:26"> In the cities of the hill-country, &#124;&#124; In the cities of the low country, &#124;&#124; And in the cities of the south, &#124;&#124; And in the land of Benjamin, &#124;&#124; And in the outskirts of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And in the cities of Judah, &#124;&#124; Again the flock passes by under the hands of the numberer,” said YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Haggai 2:6–9; Jeremiah 23:5; 1 Chronicles 17:13–14"> “Behold, days are coming,” a declaration of YHWH, “And I have established the good word &#124;&#124; That I spoke to the house of Israel, &#124;&#124; And concerning the house of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 11:1–5; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Isaiah 4:2"> In those days, and at that time, &#124;&#124; I cause a Shoot of righteousness to shoot up to David, &#124;&#124; And He has done judgment and righteousness in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:6; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Isaiah 45:17"> In those days Judah is saved, &#124;&#124; And Jerusalem dwells confidently, &#124;&#124; And this &#91;is&#93; what she is called: YHWH &#91;is&#93; Our Righteousness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 2:4; Psalms 89:29–37; Luke 1:32–33"> For thus said YHWH: “A man sitting on the throne of the house of Israel &#91;is&#93; never cut off from David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Revelation 1:6; Deuteronomy 18:1"> And to the priests—the Levites, &#124;&#124; A man is not cut off from before My face, &#124;&#124; Causing a burnt-offering to ascend, &#124;&#124; And perfuming a present, and making sacrifice—all the days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And there is a word of YHWH to Jeremiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 8:22; Psalms 89:37; Isaiah 54:9–10"> “Thus said YHWH: If you can break My covenant with the day, &#124;&#124; And My covenant with the night, &#124;&#124; So that they are not daily and nightly in their season,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 89:34; 2 Chronicles 7:18; 2 Samuel 23:5"> My covenant is also broken with My servant David, &#124;&#124; So that he does not have a son reigning on his throne, &#124;&#124; And with the Levites the priests, My ministers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; Genesis 22:17; Hebrews 11:12"> As the host of the heavens is not numbered, &#124;&#124; Nor the sand of the sea measured, &#124;&#124; So I multiply the seed of My servant David, &#124;&#124; And My ministers the Levites.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And there is a word of YHWH to Jeremiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:2–4; Psalms 83:4; Psalms 44:13–14"> “Have you not considered what this people have spoken, saying, &#124;&#124; The two families on which YHWH fixed, &#124;&#124; He rejects them? And they despise My people, so that they are no longer a people before them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 74:16–17; Jeremiah 31:35–36; Psalms 104:19"> Thus said YHWH: “If My covenant &#91;is&#93; not with day and night, &#124;&#124; &#91;And if&#93; I have not appointed the statutes of the heavens and earth—</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:37; Isaiah 14:1; Hosea 2:23"> I also reject the seed of Jacob, and My servant David, &#124;&#124; From taking rulers from his seed &#124;&#124; For the seed of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, &#124;&#124; For I return their captivity, and have pitied them.”</span>
