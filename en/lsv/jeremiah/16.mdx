<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:2; Jeremiah 2:1; Jeremiah 1:4"> And there is a word of YHWH to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:26–27; Luke 23:29; Genesis 19:14"> “You do not take a wife to yourself, &#124;&#124; Nor do you have sons and daughters in this place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:9; Jeremiah 16:5; Jeremiah 6:21"> For thus said YHWH, &#124;&#124; Of the sons and of the daughters who are born in this place, &#124;&#124; And of their mothers—those bearing them, &#124;&#124; And of their fathers—those begetting them in this land:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:33; Psalms 83:10; Jeremiah 9:22"> “They die painful deaths, &#124;&#124; They are not lamented, nor are they buried, &#124;&#124; For they are dung on the face of the ground, &#124;&#124; And are consumed by sword and by famine, &#124;&#124; And their carcass has been for food &#124;&#124; To the bird of the heavens, &#124;&#124; And to the beast of the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:16–23; Isaiah 27:11; Jeremiah 15:1–4"> For thus said YHWH: “Do not enter the house of a mourning-feast, &#124;&#124; Nor go to lament nor bemoan for them, &#124;&#124; For I have removed My peace from this people,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “The kindness and the mercies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Jeremiah 41:5; Jeremiah 47:5"> And great and small have died in this land, &#124;&#124; They are not buried, and none lament for them, &#124;&#124; Nor does any cut himself, nor become bald for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 9:4; Ezekiel 24:17; Deuteronomy 26:14"> Nor do they deal out to them for mourning, &#124;&#124; To comfort him concerning the dead, &#124;&#124; Nor cause them to drink a cup of consolations &#124;&#124; For his father and for his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:17; Ecclesiastes 7:2–4; Isaiah 22:12–14"> You do not enter a house of banqueting, &#124;&#124; To sit with them, to eat and to drink,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:10; Jeremiah 7:34; Ezekiel 26:13"> For thus said YHWH of Hosts, God of Israel: “Behold, I am causing to cease from this place, &#124;&#124; Before your eyes, and in your days, &#124;&#124; The voice of joy, and the voice of rejoicing, &#124;&#124; The voice of bridegroom and voice of bride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:19; 1 Kings 9:8–9; Deuteronomy 29:24–25"> And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; When you declare all these words to this people, &#124;&#124; And they have said to you, &#124;&#124; Why has YHWH spoken all this great evil against us? Indeed, what &#91;is&#93; our iniquity, and what &#91;is&#93; our sin, &#124;&#124; That we have sinned against our God YHWH?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 106:35–41; Deuteronomy 29:25–26; Jeremiah 22:9"> Then you have said to them: Because that your fathers have forsaken Me, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And go after other gods, and serve them, &#124;&#124; And they bow themselves to them, &#124;&#124; And have forsaken Me, and not kept My law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:10; Jeremiah 7:26; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> You also have done evil above your fathers, &#124;&#124; And behold, you are each walking after the stubbornness of his evil heart, &#124;&#124; So as not to listen to Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:36; Deuteronomy 4:26–28; Jeremiah 15:14"> And I have cast you from off this land, &#124;&#124; On to a land that you have not known, &#124;&#124; You and your fathers, &#124;&#124; And you have served other gods there by day and by night, &#124;&#124; Where I do not give grace to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:7–8; Deuteronomy 15:15; Exodus 20:2"> Therefore, behold, days are coming,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And it is not said anymore, &#124;&#124; YHWH lives, who brought up the sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:18; Jeremiah 24:6; Jeremiah 32:37"> But, YHWH lives, who brought up the sons of Israel out of the land of the north, &#124;&#124; And out of all the lands to where He drove them, &#124;&#124; And I have brought them back to their land, &#124;&#124; That I gave to their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:14–15; Amos 4:2; Micah 7:2"> Behold, I am sending for many fishers,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “And they have fished them, &#124;&#124; And after this I send for many hunters, &#124;&#124; And they have hunted them from off every mountain, &#124;&#124; And from off every hill, and from holes of the rocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Jeremiah 23:24; Proverbs 5:21"> For My eyes &#91;are&#93; on all their ways, &#124;&#124; They have not been hidden from My face, &#124;&#124; Nor has their iniquity been concealed from before My eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 35:33–34; Isaiah 40:2; Revelation 18:6"> And I have repaid a first—A second time—their iniquity and their sin, &#124;&#124; Because of their defiling My land, &#124;&#124; With the carcass of their detestable things, &#124;&#124; Indeed, their abominations have filled My inheritance.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; Jeremiah 17:17; Nahum 1:7"> O YHWH, my strength, and my fortress, &#124;&#124; And my refuge in a day of adversity, &#124;&#124; Nations come to You from the ends of the earth, &#124;&#124; And say, “Our fathers only inherited falsehood, &#124;&#124; Vanity, and none among them is profitable.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 37:19; Psalms 115:4–8; Galatians 4:8"> Does man make gods for himself, &#124;&#124; And they—no gods?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 9:16; Psalms 83:18; Jeremiah 33:2"> “Therefore, behold, I am causing them to know at this time, &#124;&#124; I cause them to know My hand and My might, &#124;&#124; And they have known that My Name &#91;is&#93; YHWH!”</span>
