<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:11; Jeremiah 40:13; Jeremiah 6:13"> And they come near—all the heads of the forces, and Johanan son of Kareah, and Jezaniah son of Hoshaiah, and all the people from the least even to the greatest—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:7; Leviticus 26:22; Acts 8:24"> and they say to Jeremiah the prophet, “Please let our supplication fall before you, and pray for us to your God YHWH for all this remnant; for we have been left a few out of many, as your eyes see us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 86:11; Proverbs 3:6; Psalms 25:4–5"> and your God YHWH declares to us the way in which we walk, and the thing that we do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:23; 1 Samuel 3:17–18; Exodus 8:29"> And Jeremiah the prophet says to them, “I have heard: behold, I am praying to your God YHWH according to your words, and it has come to pass, the whole word that YHWH answers you, I declare to you—I do not withhold a word from you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 31:50; Judges 11:10; Malachi 3:5"> And they have said to Jeremiah, “YHWH is against us for a true and faithful witness, if—according to all the word with which your God YHWH sends you to us—we do not do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:23; Deuteronomy 5:29; Romans 8:7"> Whether &#91;it is&#93; good or evil, to the voice of our God YHWH, to whom we are sending you, we listen; because it is good for us when we listen to the voice of our God YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Isaiah 28:16; Habakkuk 2:3"> And it comes to pass, at the end of ten days, that there is a word of YHWH to Jeremiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:1; Jeremiah 41:11–16; Jeremiah 43:2–5"> and he calls to Johanan son of Kareah, and to all the heads of the forces that &#91;are&#93; with him, and to all the people, from the least even to the greatest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 19:4; Jeremiah 42:2; 2 Kings 19:6"> and he says to them, “Thus said YHWH, God of Israel, to whom you sent me, to cause your supplication to fall before Him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Ezekiel 36:36; Jeremiah 24:6"> If you certainly dwell in this land, then I have built you up, and I do not throw down; and I have planted you, and I do not pluck up; for I have relented concerning the calamity that I have done to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Jeremiah 1:19; Jeremiah 1:8"> Do not be afraid of the king of Babylon, whom you are afraid of; do not be afraid of him—a declaration of YHWH—for I &#91;am&#93; with you, to save you, and to deliver you from his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11; Psalms 106:45–46; Proverbs 16:7"> And I give mercies to you, and he has pitied you, and caused you to return to your own ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16; Exodus 5:2; Jeremiah 42:10"> And if you are saying, We do not dwell in this land—not to listen to the voice of your God YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:17; Jeremiah 4:19; Isaiah 31:1"> saying, No; but we enter the land of Egypt, that we see no war, and do not hear the sound of a horn, and are not hungry for bread; and we dwell there—</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16; Jeremiah 44:12–14; Jeremiah 42:17"> And now, therefore, hear a word of YHWH, O remnant of Judah! Thus said YHWH of Hosts, God of Israel: If you really set your faces to enter Egypt, and have gone to sojourn there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:27; Ezekiel 11:8; Amos 9:1–4"> then it has come to pass, the sword that you are afraid of, overtakes you there, in the land of Egypt; and the hunger, because of which you are sorrowful, cleaves after you there in Egypt, and there you die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:22; Jeremiah 44:28; Jeremiah 44:13–14"> Thus are all the men who have set their faces to enter Egypt to sojourn there; they die—by sword, by hunger, and by pestilence, and there is not a remnant and an escaped one to them, because of the calamity that I am bringing in on them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 18:16; Jeremiah 29:18"> for thus said YHWH of Hosts, God of Israel: As My anger and My fury have been poured out on the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so My fury is poured out on you in your entering Egypt, and you have been for an execration, and for an astonishment, and for a reviling, and for a reproach, and you do not see this place anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16; Isaiah 30:1–7; Nehemiah 9:26"> YHWH has spoken against you, O remnant of Judah, do not enter Egypt: certainly know that I have testified against you today;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:3–4; Ezekiel 33:31; Matthew 22:15–18"> for you have showed yourselves perverse in your souls, for you sent me to your God YHWH, saying, Pray for us to our God YHWH, and according to all that our God YHWH says, so declare to us, and we have done &#91;it&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:7; Zechariah 7:11–12; Acts 20:26–27"> and I declare to you today, and you have not listened to the voice of your God YHWH, and to anything with which He has sent me to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:17; Ezekiel 6:11; Hosea 9:6"> And now, certainly know that by sword, by famine, and by pestilence you die, in the place that you have desired to go to sojourn there.”</span>
