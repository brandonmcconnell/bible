<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 1:11–12; Joel 3:19; Ezekiel 35:3–15"> Thus said Lord YHWH to Edom &#124;&#124; (We have heard a report from YHWH, &#124;&#124; And an ambassador among nations was sent, &#124;&#124; “Rise, indeed, let us rise against her for battle”):</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:18; Isaiah 23:9; Micah 7:10"> “Behold, I have made you little among nations, &#124;&#124; You &#91;are&#93; despised exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–15; Proverbs 29:23; Jeremiah 49:16"> The pride of your heart has lifted you up, &#124;&#124; O dweller in clefts of a rock &#124;&#124; (A high place &#91;is&#93; his habitation, &#124;&#124; He is saying in his heart: Who brings me down &#91;to&#93; earth?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 9:2; Job 20:6–7; Habakkuk 2:9"> If you go up high as an eagle, &#124;&#124; And if you set your nest between stars, &#124;&#124; From there I bring you down,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:9; Deuteronomy 24:21; Isaiah 17:6"> “If thieves have come in to you, &#124;&#124; If spoilers of the night, &#124;&#124; How have you been cut off! Do they not steal their sufficiency? If gatherers have come in to you, &#124;&#124; Do they not leave gleanings?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:10; Psalms 139:1; Isaiah 45:3"> How has Esau been searched out! His hidden things have flowed out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Jeremiah 49:7; Jeremiah 30:14"> All your allies have sent you to the border, &#124;&#124; Forgotten you, prevailed over you, have your friends, &#124;&#124; They make your bread a snare under you, &#124;&#124; There is no understanding in him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; Job 5:12–14; Psalms 33:10"> Is it not in that day,” a declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “That I have destroyed the wise out of Edom, &#124;&#124; And understanding out of the mountain of Esau?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Amos 1:12; Isaiah 63:1–3"> And your mighty ones have been broken down, O Teman, &#124;&#124; So that everyone of the mountain of Esau is cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 1:11; Malachi 1:3–4; Genesis 27:41"> For slaughter, for violence &#91;to&#93; your brother Jacob, &#124;&#124; Shame covers you, &#124;&#124; And you have been cut off for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 137:7; Nahum 3:10; Joel 3:3"> In the day of your standing aloof from the opposite &#91;side&#93;, &#124;&#124; In the day of strangers taking his force captive, &#124;&#124; And foreigners have entered his gates, &#124;&#124; And have cast a lot for Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; Even you &#91;are&#93; as one of them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Micah 4:11; Proverbs 17:5"> And—you do not look on the day of your brother, &#124;&#124; On the day of his alienation, &#124;&#124; Nor do you rejoice over sons of Judah, &#124;&#124; In the day of their destruction, &#124;&#124; Nor make your mouth great in a day of distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 1:15; Ezekiel 35:5; Ezekiel 35:10"> Nor come into a gate of My people in a day of their calamity, &#124;&#124; Nor look, even you, on its misfortune in a day of its calamity, &#124;&#124; Nor send forth against its force in a day of its calamity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 1:6; Psalms 31:8; Obadiah 1:12"> Nor stand by the breach to cut off its escaped, &#124;&#124; Nor deliver up its remnant in a day of distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 2:13; Habakkuk 2:8; Ezekiel 30:3"> For near &#91;is&#93; the Day of YHWH, on all the nations, &#124;&#124; As you have done, it is done to you, &#124;&#124; Your deed turns back on your own head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Jeremiah 49:12; Jeremiah 25:15–16"> For—as you have drunk on My holy mountain, &#124;&#124; All the nations drink continually, &#124;&#124; And they have drunk and have swallowed, &#124;&#124; And they have been as &#91;if&#93; they have not been.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Amos 9:11–15; Isaiah 14:1–2"> And in Mount Zion there is an escape, &#124;&#124; And it has been holy, &#124;&#124; And the house of Jacob has possessed their possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Isaiah 10:17; Obadiah 1:16"> And the house of Jacob has been a fire, &#124;&#124; And the house of Joseph a flame, &#124;&#124; And the house of Esau for stubble, &#124;&#124; And they have burned among them, &#124;&#124; And they have consumed them, &#124;&#124; And there is not a remnant to the house of Esau,” &#124;&#124; For YHWH has spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:44; Zephaniah 2:4–7; Amos 9:12"> And they have possessed the south with the mountain of Esau, &#124;&#124; And the low country with the Philistines, &#124;&#124; And they have possessed the field of Ephraim, &#124;&#124; And the field of Samaria, &#124;&#124; And Benjamin with Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 17:9–10; Luke 4:26; Jeremiah 32:44"> And the expulsion of this force of the sons of Israel, &#124;&#124; That &#91;is with&#93; the Canaanites to Zarephat, &#124;&#124; And the expulsion of Jerusalem that &#91;is&#93; with the Sepharad, &#124;&#124; Possess the cities of the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; James 5:20; Revelation 11:15"> And saviors have gone up on Mount Zion, &#124;&#124; To judge the mountain of Esau, &#124;&#124; And the kingdom has been to YHWH!</span>
