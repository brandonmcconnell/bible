<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; John 1:14; Zephaniah 3:14–15"> Sing, and rejoice, O daughter of Zion, &#124;&#124; For behold, I am coming, and have dwelt in your midst, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.</span>
