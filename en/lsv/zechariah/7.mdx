<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 8:21; Jeremiah 26:19; 1 Samuel 13:12"> And Beth-El sends Sherezer and Regem-Melech, and its men, to appease the face of YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 8:19; James 4:8–10; Malachi 2:7"> speaking to the priests who &#91;are&#93; at the house of YHWH of Hosts, and to the prophets, saying, “Do I weep in the fifth month—being separated—as I have done these so many years?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16"> And there is a word of YHWH of Hosts to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 1:12; Isaiah 58:4–6; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> “Speak to all the people of the land, and to the priests, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:20–21; 1 Corinthians 11:26–29; Hosea 8:13"> When you fasted with mourning in the fifth and in the seventh &#91;months&#93;—even these seventy years—did you keep the fast &#91;to&#93; Me? And when you eat, and when you drink, is it not you who are eating, and you who are drinking?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–20; Jeremiah 17:26; Jeremiah 7:5"> Are &#91;these&#93; not the words that YHWH proclaimed by the hand of the former prophets, in Jerusalem’s being inhabited, and &#91;in&#93; safety, and its cities around it, and the south and the plain—abiding?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And there is a word of YHWH to Zechariah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Psalms 82:2–4; Proverbs 21:3"> “Thus spoke YHWH of Hosts, saying, &#124;&#124; Judge &#91;with&#93; true judgment, &#124;&#124; And do kindness and mercy with one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:28; Isaiah 1:23; James 5:4"> And do not oppress widow, and fatherless, &#124;&#124; Sojourner, and poor, &#124;&#124; And you do not devise the calamity of one another in your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:29; Jeremiah 8:5; Hosea 4:16"> And they refuse to attend, &#124;&#124; And they give a stubborn shoulder, &#124;&#124; And their ears are made heavy against hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Nehemiah 9:29–30; 2 Chronicles 36:16"> And their heart they have made adamant, &#124;&#124; Against hearing the Law, and the words, &#124;&#124; That YHWH of Hosts sent by His Spirit, &#124;&#124; By the hand of the former prophets, &#124;&#124; And there is great wrath from YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–28; Jeremiah 14:12; Isaiah 1:15"> And it comes to pass, as He called, &#124;&#124; And they have not listened, &#124;&#124; So they call, and I do not listen,” said YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Deuteronomy 28:64; Jeremiah 23:19"> “And I toss them on all the nations, &#124;&#124; That they have not known, &#124;&#124; The land has been desolate behind them, &#124;&#124; Of any passing by and turning back, &#124;&#124; And they set a desirable land for a desolation!”</span>
