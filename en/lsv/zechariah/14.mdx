<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 13:9; Isaiah 2:12; Isaiah 13:6"> Behold, a day has come to YHWH, &#124;&#124; And your spoil has been divided in your midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 21:20–24; Isaiah 13:16; Romans 9:27–29"> And I have gathered all the nations to Jerusalem to battle, &#124;&#124; And the city has been captured, &#124;&#124; And the houses have been spoiled, &#124;&#124; And the women are lain with, &#124;&#124; Half the city has gone forth in a removal, &#124;&#124; And the remnant of the people are not cut off from the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 12:9; Zephaniah 3:19; Zechariah 2:8–9"> And YHWH has gone forth, &#124;&#124; And He has fought against those nations, &#124;&#124; As in the day of His fighting in a day of conflict.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 1:3–4; Ezekiel 11:23; Acts 1:11–12"> And His feet have stood, in that day, &#124;&#124; On the Mount of Olives, &#124;&#124; That &#91;is&#93; eastward before Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And the Mount of Olives has been cleft at its midst, &#124;&#124; To the east, and to the west, a very great valley, &#124;&#124; And half of the mountain is removed toward the north, &#124;&#124; And half toward the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jude 1:14–15; Amos 1:1; Matthew 25:31"> And you have fled &#91;to&#93; the valley of My mountains, &#124;&#124; For the valley of the mountains joins to Azal, &#124;&#124; And you have fled as you fled before the shaking, &#124;&#124; In the days of Uzziah king of Judah, &#124;&#124; And my God YHWH has come in, &#124;&#124; All holy ones &#91;are&#93; with You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:10–11; Psalms 112:4; John 12:46"> And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; The precious light is not, it is dense darkness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 22:5; 1 Thessalonians 5:2; Daniel 12:4"> And there has been one day, &#124;&#124; It is known to YHWH, not day nor night, &#124;&#124; And it has been at evening-time—there is light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 22:1–2; John 7:38; John 4:14"> And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; Living waters go forth from Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; Half of them to the Eastern Sea, &#124;&#124; And half of them to the Western Sea, &#124;&#124; It is in summer and in winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5–6; Revelation 11:15; Daniel 7:27"> And YHWH has become King over all the earth, &#124;&#124; In that day YHWH is one, and His Name one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Joshua 15:32; 1 Kings 15:22"> All the land is changed as a plain, &#124;&#124; From Gebo to Rimmon, south of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And she has been high, and has dwelt in her place, &#124;&#124; Even from the Gate of Benjamin &#124;&#124; To the place of the first gate, to the front gate, &#124;&#124; And from the Tower of Hananeel, &#124;&#124; To the wine-vats of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 22:3; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Amos 9:15"> And they have dwelt in her, &#124;&#124; And destruction is no more, &#124;&#124; And Jerusalem has dwelt confidently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:17–20; Isaiah 66:15–16; Micah 4:11–13"> And this is the plague with which YHWH plagues all the peoples who have warred against Jerusalem: He has consumed away its flesh, &#124;&#124; And it is standing on its feet, &#124;&#124; And its eyes are consumed in their holes, &#124;&#124; And its tongue is consumed in their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 12:4; Revelation 17:12–17; Judges 7:22"> And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; A great destruction &#91;from&#93; YHWH is among them, &#124;&#124; And they have each seized the hand of his neighbor, &#124;&#124; And his hand has gone up against the hand of his neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 23:18; Zechariah 12:2; 2 Chronicles 20:25–27"> And also Judah is fought with in Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And the force of all the surrounding nations has been gathered, &#124;&#124; Gold, and silver, and apparel, in great abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 14:12"> And so is the plague of the horse, of the mule, &#124;&#124; Of the camel, and of the donkey, &#124;&#124; And of all the livestock that are in these camps, &#124;&#124; As this plague.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 14:17–19; Isaiah 66:23; Isaiah 66:18–21"> And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; Everyone who has been left of all the nations, &#124;&#124; Who are coming in against Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; They have also gone up from year to year, &#124;&#124; To bow themselves to the King, YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; And to celebrate the Celebration of the Shelters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Amos 4:7–8; Isaiah 60:12; Jeremiah 14:4"> And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; That he who does not go up of the families of the land to Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; To bow himself to the King, YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; Even on them there is no shower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 14:15; Deuteronomy 11:10–11; Zechariah 14:12"> And if the family of Egypt does not go up, nor come in, &#124;&#124; Then on them is the plague &#124;&#124; With which YHWH plagues the nations &#124;&#124; That do not go up to celebrate the Celebration of Shelters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 3:19"> This is the punishment of the sin of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And the punishment of the sin of all the nations, &#124;&#124; That do not go up to celebrate the Celebration of Shelters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16–17; Exodus 39:30; Leviticus 8:9"> In that day “Holy to YHWH” is &#91;engraved&#93; on bells of the horse, &#124;&#124; And the pots in the house of YHWH &#124;&#124; Have been as bowls before the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:9; Romans 14:6–7; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> And every pot in Jerusalem and in Judah, &#124;&#124; Have been holy to YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; And all those sacrificing have come in, &#124;&#124; And have taken of them, and boiled in them, &#124;&#124; And in that day there is no merchant anymore in the house of YHWH of Hosts!</span>
