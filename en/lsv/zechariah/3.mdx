<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 6:11; Exodus 29:6; Job 29:14"> He also said, “Let them set a pure turban on his head.” And they set the pure turban on his head, and clothe him with garments. And the Messenger of YHWH is standing,</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 4:10; Jeremiah 50:20; Isaiah 28:16"> For behold, the stone that I put before Joshua, &#124;&#124; On one stone &#91;are&#93; seven eyes, &#124;&#124; Behold, I am engraving its engraving, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; And I have removed the iniquity of that land in one day.</span>
