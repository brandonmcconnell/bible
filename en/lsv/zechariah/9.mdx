<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 1:3–5; Jeremiah 49:23–27; Amos 3:12"> The burden of a word of YHWH against the land of Hadrach, and Demmeseh—his place of rest: (When to YHWH &#91;is&#93; the eye of man, &#124;&#124; And of all the tribes of Israel).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:23; Ezekiel 28:12; Ezekiel 28:2–5"> And also Hamath borders thereon, &#124;&#124; Tyre and Sidon, for—very wise!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:33; Job 27:16; Ezekiel 28:4–5"> And Tyre builds a bulwark for herself, &#124;&#124; And heaps silver as dust, &#124;&#124; And gold as mire of out-places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:18; Ezekiel 26:17; Isaiah 23:1–7"> Behold, the Lord dispossesses her, &#124;&#124; And He has struck her force in the sea, &#124;&#124; And she is consumed with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:26; Revelation 18:9–17; Jeremiah 47:1"> Ashkelon sees and fears, &#124;&#124; Also Gaza, and she is exceedingly pained, &#124;&#124; Also Ekron—for her expectation dried up, &#124;&#124; And a king has perished from Gaza, &#124;&#124; And Ashkelon does not remain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 1:8; Zephaniah 2:10; 1 Peter 5:5"> “And a foreigner has dwelt in Ashdod, &#124;&#124; And I have cut off the excellence of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 3:12; Ezekiel 16:57–61; Isaiah 60:14–16"> And turned aside his blood from his mouth, &#124;&#124; His abominations from between his teeth, &#124;&#124; And he has remained, even he, to our God, &#124;&#124; And he has been as a leader in Judah, &#124;&#124; And Ekron as a Jebusite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 54:14; Isaiah 60:18; Isaiah 52:1"> And I have pitched a camp for My house, &#124;&#124; Because of the passer through, and of the returner, &#124;&#124; And an exactor does not pass through against them again, &#124;&#124; For now, I have seen with My eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 21:4–7; Zephaniah 3:14–15; John 12:13–16"> Rejoice exceedingly, O daughter of Zion, &#124;&#124; Shout, O daughter of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; Behold, your King comes to you, &#124;&#124; Righteous and having salvation, &#124;&#124; Afflicted—and riding on a donkey, &#124;&#124; And on a colt—a son of female donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 1:7; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Hosea 2:18"> And I have cut off the chariot from Ephraim, &#124;&#124; And the horse from Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; Indeed, the bow of battle has been cut off, &#124;&#124; And He has spoken peace to nations, &#124;&#124; And His rule &#91;is&#93; from sea to sea, &#124;&#124; And from the river to the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:14; Exodus 24:8; Psalms 102:19–21"> Also You—by the blood of Your covenant, &#124;&#124; I have sent Your prisoners out of the pit, &#124;&#124; There is no water in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 42:10; Isaiah 61:7; Lamentations 3:21–22"> Return to a stronghold, &#124;&#124; You prisoners of the hope, &#124;&#124; Even today a second announcer I restore to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; Zechariah 10:3–7; Isaiah 49:2"> For I have trodden for Myself with Judah, &#124;&#124; I have filled a bow &#91;with&#93; Ephraim, &#124;&#124; And I have stirred up your sons, O Zion, &#124;&#124; Against your sons, O Javan, &#124;&#124; And I have set you as the sword of a hero.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; Isaiah 66:15; Psalms 144:5–6"> And YHWH appears for them, &#124;&#124; And His arrow has gone forth as lightning, &#124;&#124; And Lord YHWH blows with a horn, &#124;&#124; And He has gone with whirlwinds of the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Zechariah 10:7; Zechariah 10:5"> YHWH of Hosts covers them over, &#124;&#124; And they consumed, and subdued sling-stones, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they have drunk, &#124;&#124; They have made a noise as wine, &#124;&#124; And they have been full as a bowl, &#124;&#124; As corners of an altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 62:3; Luke 12:32; Haggai 2:23"> And their God YHWH has saved them in that day, &#124;&#124; As a flock of His people, &#124;&#124; For stones of a crown are displaying themselves over His ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 33:17; Isaiah 62:8–9; 1 John 4:8–11"> For what His goodness! And what His beauty! Grain the young men, &#124;&#124; And new wine the virgins—make fruitful!</span>
