<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of YHWH of Hosts, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:18; Zechariah 1:14–16; Isaiah 42:13–14"> “Thus said YHWH of Hosts: I have been zealous for Zion with great zeal, &#124;&#124; With great heat I have been zealous for her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 1:16; Zechariah 2:10–11; John 1:14"> Thus said YHWH: I have turned back to Zion, &#124;&#124; And I have dwelt in the midst of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And Jerusalem has been called The City of Truth, &#124;&#124; And The Mountain of YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; The Holy Mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 65:20–22; Job 5:26; Lamentations 5:11–15"> Thus said YHWH of Hosts: Again old men and old women dwell &#124;&#124; In broad places of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And each his staff in his hand, &#124;&#124; Because of abundance of days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:13; Matthew 11:16–17; Jeremiah 30:19–20"> And broad places of the city are full of boys and girls, &#124;&#124; Playing in its broad places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:27; Romans 4:20–21; Numbers 11:22–23"> Thus said YHWH of Hosts: Surely it is wonderful in the eyes of the remnant of this people in those days, &#124;&#124; Also in My eyes it is wonderful, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:8; Romans 11:25–27; Malachi 1:11"> Thus said YHWH of Hosts: Behold, I am saving My people from the land of the rising, &#124;&#124; And from the land of the going in, of the sun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:28; Ezekiel 11:20; Jeremiah 31:33"> And I have brought them in, &#124;&#124; They have dwelt in the midst of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And they have been to Me for a people, &#124;&#124; And I am to them for God, &#124;&#124; In truth and in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; 1 Chronicles 22:13; Ezra 5:1–2"> Thus said YHWH of Hosts: Let your hands be strong, &#124;&#124; You who are hearing in these days these words from the mouth of the prophets, &#124;&#124; That in the day the house of YHWH of Hosts has been founded, &#124;&#124; The temple &#91;is&#93; to be built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Haggai 1:6–11; Haggai 2:16–18; Amos 3:6"> For before those days there has been no hiring of man, &#124;&#124; Indeed, a hiring of beasts there is none; And to him who is going out, &#124;&#124; And to him who is coming in, &#124;&#124; There is no peace because of the adversary, &#124;&#124; And I send all men—each against his neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 12:1; Haggai 2:19; Zechariah 8:8–9"> And now, not as &#91;in&#93; the former days &#91;am&#93; I to the remnant of this people, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 26:4–5; Deuteronomy 33:13; Genesis 27:28"> Because of the sowing of peace, &#124;&#124; The vine gives her fruit, &#124;&#124; And the earth gives her increase, &#124;&#124; And the heavens give their dew, &#124;&#124; And I have caused the remnant of this people &#124;&#124; To inherit all these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:20; Zechariah 8:9; Psalms 72:17"> And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; As you have been a reviling among nations, &#124;&#124; O house of Judah, and house of Israel, &#124;&#124; So I save you, and you have been a blessing, &#124;&#124; Do not fear, let your hands be strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Ezekiel 24:14; Jeremiah 4:28"> For thus said YHWH of Hosts: As I purposed to do evil to you, &#124;&#124; When your fathers made Me angry—said YHWH of Hosts—And I did not relent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 8:13; Micah 7:18–20; Luke 12:32"> So I have turned back, I have purposed, in these days, &#124;&#124; To do good with Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And with the house of Judah—do not fear!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Zechariah 7:9; Psalms 15:2"> These &#91;are&#93; the things that you do: Each speak truth with his neighbor, &#124;&#124; Judge &#91;with&#93; truth and peaceful judgment in your gates,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Micah 2:1–3; Jeremiah 44:4"> And do not devise the evil of his neighbor in your heart, &#124;&#124; And do not love a false oath, &#124;&#124; For all these &#91;are&#93; things that I have hated, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And there is a word of YHWH of Hosts to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 8:16; Jeremiah 39:2; Zechariah 7:5"> “Thus said YHWH of Hosts: The fast of the fourth, and the fast of the fifth, and the fast of the seventh, and the fast of the tenth &#91;months&#93;, are to the house of Judah for joy and for rejoicing, and for pleasant appointed times, and the truth and the peace they have loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 2:2–3; Micah 4:1–2; Zechariah 2:11"> Thus said YHWH of Hosts: Yet peoples come, and inhabitants of many cities,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 7:2; Hosea 6:3; Psalms 146:1–2"> Indeed, inhabitants of one &#124;&#124; Have gone to another, saying, &#124;&#124; We go diligently, &#124;&#124; To appease the face of YHWH, &#124;&#124; To seek YHWH of Hosts—I go, even I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 8:21; Isaiah 60:3–22; Revelation 21:24"> Indeed, many peoples have come in, and mighty nations, &#124;&#124; To seek YHWH of Hosts in Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And to appease the face of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 55:5; 1 Corinthians 14:25; Isaiah 60:3"> Thus said YHWH of Hosts: In those days ten men of all languages of the nations take hold, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they have taken hold on the skirt of a man, a Jew, saying, &#124;&#124; We go with you, for we heard God &#91;is&#93; with you!”</span>
