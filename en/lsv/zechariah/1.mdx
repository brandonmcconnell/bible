<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:4; Zechariah 7:1; Zechariah 1:7"> In the eighth month, in the second year of Darius, a word of YHWH has been to Zechariah, son of Berechiah, son of Iddo the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:6; Lamentations 5:7; Ezekiel 22:31"> “YHWH was angry against your fathers—wrath!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Malachi 3:7; Jeremiah 3:22; Jeremiah 4:1"> And you have said to them, Thus said YHWH of Hosts: Return to Me, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; And I return to you, said YHWH of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 78:8; Psalms 106:6–7; Jeremiah 35:15"> You will not be as your fathers, &#124;&#124; To whom the former prophets called, &#124;&#124; Saying, Thus said YHWH of Hosts: Please turn back from your evil ways and from your evil doings, &#124;&#124; And they did not listen, &#124;&#124; Nor attend to Me—a declaration of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5; Job 14:10–12; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> Your fathers—where &#91;are&#93; they? And the prophets—do they live for all time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Lamentations 2:17; Deuteronomy 28:15; Ezekiel 12:25–28"> Only, My words and My statutes, &#124;&#124; That I commanded My servants the prophets, &#124;&#124; Have they not overtaken your fathers? And they turn back and say: As YHWH of Hosts designed to do to us, &#124;&#124; According to our ways, and according to our doings, &#124;&#124; So He has done to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 1:1"> On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month (it &#91;is&#93; the month of Sebat), in the second year of Darius, a word of YHWH has been to Zechariah, son of Berechiah, son of Iddo the prophet. &#91;He was&#93; saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 6:4; Isaiah 55:13; Zechariah 13:7"> I have seen by night, and behold, one riding on a red horse, and he is standing between the myrtles that &#91;are&#93; in the shade, and behind him &#91;are&#93; horses, red, bay, and white.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 4:4–5; Zechariah 5:5; Zechariah 2:3"> And I say, “What &#91;are&#93; these, my lord?” And the messenger who is speaking with me says to me, “I show you what these &#91;are&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 1:11; Zechariah 6:5–8; Hebrews 1:14"> And the one who is standing between the myrtles answers and says, “These &#91;are&#93; they whom YHWH has sent to walk up and down in the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 1:10; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 103:20–21"> And they answer the Messenger of YHWH who is standing between the myrtles, and say, “We have walked up and down in the land, and behold, all the land is sitting still, and at rest.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 7:5; Jeremiah 29:10; Psalms 102:13"> And the Messenger of YHWH answers and says, “YHWH of Hosts! Until when do You not pity Jerusalem, and the cities of Judah, that You have abhorred these seventy years?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Isaiah 40:1–2; Jeremiah 30:10–22"> And YHWH answers the messenger, who is speaking with me, good words, comfortable words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 2:18; Isaiah 40:1; Zechariah 1:17"> And the messenger who is speaking with me, says to me, “Call, saying, Thus said YHWH of Hosts: I have been zealous for Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And for Zion &#91;with&#93; great zeal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:12–17; Jeremiah 51:24; Isaiah 54:8"> And &#91;with&#93; great wrath I am angry against the nations who are at ease, &#124;&#124; For I was a little angry, and they assisted—for evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3; Ezra 6:14–15; Zechariah 2:10–11"> Therefore, thus said YHWH: I have turned to Jerusalem with mercies, &#124;&#124; My house is built in it, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; And a line is stretched over Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Zechariah 2:12; Zephaniah 3:15–17"> Again call, saying, &#124;&#124; Thus said YHWH of Hosts: Again My cities overflow from good, &#124;&#124; And YHWH has again comforted Zion, &#124;&#124; And He has fixed again on Jerusalem.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 5:13; 2 Kings 24:1–20; Zechariah 5:9"> And I lift up my eyes, and look, and behold, four horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 1:21; Zechariah 8:14; Zechariah 2:2"> And I say to the messenger who is speaking with me, “What &#91;are&#93; these?” And he says to me, “These &#91;are&#93; the horns that have scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 10:3–5; Zechariah 12:2–6; Zechariah 9:12–16"> And YHWH shows me four artisans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 75:10; Zechariah 1:19; Psalms 75:4–5"> And I say, “What &#91;are&#93; these coming to do?” And He speaks, saying, “These &#91;are&#93; the horns that have scattered Judah, so that no one has lifted up his head, and these come to trouble them, to cast down the horns of the nations who are lifting up a horn against the land of Judah—to scatter it.”</span>
