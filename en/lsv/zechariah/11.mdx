<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:6–7; Jeremiah 22:23; Luke 21:23–24"> Open, O Lebanon, your doors, &#124;&#124; And fire devours among your cedars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 2:12–17; Ezekiel 31:17; Isaiah 10:33–34"> Howl, O fir, for the cedar has fallen, &#124;&#124; For their majestic ones were destroyed, &#124;&#124; Howl, you oaks of Bashan, &#124;&#124; For the enclosed forest has come down,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:44; Jeremiah 25:34–36; Zechariah 11:8"> A voice of the howling of the shepherds! For their robe of honor was destroyed, &#124;&#124; A voice of the roaring of young lions! For the excellence of Jordan was destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 11:7; Matthew 23:37; Romans 15:8"> Thus said my God YHWH: “Feed the flock of the slaughter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:7; Hosea 12:8; 1 Timothy 6:5–10"> Whose buyers slay them, and are not guilty, &#124;&#124; And their sellers say, Blessed &#91;is&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; And I am rich, &#124;&#124; And their shepherds have no pity on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:14; Matthew 22:7; Isaiah 9:19–21"> For I no longer have pity on inhabitants of the land, &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And behold, I am causing man to come forth, &#124;&#124; Each into the hand of his neighbor, &#124;&#124; And into the hand of his king, &#124;&#124; And they have beaten down the land, &#124;&#124; And I do not deliver out of their hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Zechariah 11:4; Isaiah 11:4"> And I feed the flock of slaughter, even you, you afflicted of the flock; and I take two staffs to myself; I have called one Pleasantness, and I have called the other Bands, and I feed the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 49:7; Matthew 23:34–36; John 15:18"> And I cut off the three shepherds in one month, and my soul is grieved with them, and also their soul has abhorred me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:11; Matthew 13:10–11; Matthew 21:19"> And I say, “I do not feed you, the dying, let die; and the cut off, let be cut off; and the remaining ones, let each eat the flesh of its neighbor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 11:7; Psalms 89:39; Hebrews 8:8–13"> And I take my staff Pleasantness, and cut it apart, to make void my covenant that I had made with all the peoples:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:12; Luke 2:38; Deuteronomy 31:21"> and it is broken in that day, and the afflicted of the flock who are observing me know well, that it &#91;is&#93; a word of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Exodus 21:32; Luke 22:3–6"> And I say to them: “If good in your eyes, give my hire, and if not, refrain”; and they weigh out my hire—thirty pieces of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 27:3–10; Acts 1:18–19; Isaiah 53:2–3"> And YHWH says to me, “Cast it to the potter”; the good price that I have been prized at by them, and I take the thirty pieces of silver, and cast them &#91;into&#93; the house of YHWH, to the potter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 9:21; Isaiah 11:13; Acts 23:7–10"> And I cut apart my second staff, Bands, &#124;&#124; To break the unity between Judah and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10–12; Luke 11:40; Jeremiah 2:26–27"> And YHWH says to me, “Again take to yourself the instrument of a foolish shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2–6; Jeremiah 23:2; Matthew 23:2–4"> For behold, I am raising up a shepherd in the land, &#124;&#124; The cut off he does not inspect, &#124;&#124; The shaken off he does not seek, &#124;&#124; And the broken he does not heal, &#124;&#124; The standing he does not sustain, &#124;&#124; And the flesh of the fat he eats, &#124;&#124; And their hooves he breaks off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:1; Ezekiel 13:3; 1 Corinthians 8:4"> Woe &#91;to&#93; the worthless shepherd, forsaking the flock, &#124;&#124; A sword &#91;is&#93; on his arm, and on his right eye, &#124;&#124; His arm is utterly dried up, &#124;&#124; And his right eye is very dim!”</span>
