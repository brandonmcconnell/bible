<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 42:5; Jeremiah 51:15; Isaiah 57:16"> The burden of a word of YHWH on Israel. A declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Stretching out the heavens, and founding earth, &#124;&#124; And forming the spirit of man in his midst:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:14; Psalms 75:8; Isaiah 51:22–23"> “Behold, I am making Jerusalem a cup of reeling &#124;&#124; To all the surrounding peoples, &#124;&#124; And it is also against Judah, &#124;&#124; In the siege against Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 21:44; Zechariah 14:2–4; Daniel 2:34–35"> And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; I make Jerusalem a burdensome stone to all the peoples, &#124;&#124; All loading it are completely pressed down, &#124;&#124; And gathered against it have been all nations of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:28; Zechariah 12:3; Zechariah 12:6"> In that day,” a declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “I strike every horse with astonishment, &#124;&#124; And its rider with madness, &#124;&#124; And on the house of Judah I open My eyes, &#124;&#124; And every horse of the peoples I strike with blindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 10:12; Joel 3:16; Psalms 18:32"> And leaders of Judah have said in their heart, &#124;&#124; The inhabitants of Jerusalem &#91;are&#93; strength to me, &#124;&#124; In YHWH of Hosts their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Obadiah 1:18; Micah 5:5–8; Zechariah 2:4"> In that day I make the leaders of Judah &#124;&#124; As a hearth of fire among trees, &#124;&#124; And as a torch of fire in a sheaf, &#124;&#124; And they have consumed—on the right and on the left—all the surrounding peoples, &#124;&#124; And Jerusalem has again inhabited her place in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:18; Romans 3:27; Luke 1:51–53"> And YHWH has saved the tents of Judah first, &#124;&#124; So that the beauty of the house of David does not become great against Judah, &#124;&#124; And the beauty of the inhabitant of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 5:2–4; Zechariah 9:8; Joel 3:16–17"> In that day YHWH covers over the inhabitant of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And the stumbling among them has been in that day as David, &#124;&#124; And the house of David as God—As the Messenger of YHWH—before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 54:17; Zechariah 14:2–3; Haggai 2:22"> And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; I seek to destroy all the nations &#124;&#124; Who are coming in against Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Ezekiel 39:29; Joel 2:28–29"> And I have poured on the house of David, &#124;&#124; And on the inhabitant of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; The Spirit of grace and supplications, &#124;&#124; And they have looked to Me whom they pierced, &#124;&#124; And they have mourned over Him, &#124;&#124; Like a mourning over the only one, &#124;&#124; And they have been in bitterness for Him, &#124;&#124; Like a bitterness over the firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:24; 2 Kings 23:29"> In that day, great is the mourning of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; As the mourning of Hadadrimmon in the Valley of Megiddon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 7:3; Luke 3:31; 2 Samuel 5:14"> And the land has mourned—every family apart, &#124;&#124; The family of the house of David apart, &#124;&#124; And their women apart; The family of the house of Nathan apart, &#124;&#124; And their women apart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:7; 2 Samuel 16:5; 1 Chronicles 23:10"> The family of the house of Levi apart, &#124;&#124; And their women apart; The family of Shimei apart, &#124;&#124; And their women apart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 9:12"> All the families that are left, &#124;&#124; Every family apart, and their women apart!”</span>
