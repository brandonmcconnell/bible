<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:12; Revelation 16:19; Ezekiel 5:2–4"> And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; In all the land,” a declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “Two parts in it are cut off—they expire, &#124;&#124; And the third is left in it.</span>
