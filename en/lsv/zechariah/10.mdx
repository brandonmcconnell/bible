<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Jeremiah 27:9; Ezekiel 34:5"> Because the teraphim spoke iniquity, &#124;&#124; And the diviners have seen a falsehood, &#124;&#124; And dreams of the vanity they speak, &#124;&#124; &#91;With&#93; vanity they give comfort, &#124;&#124; Therefore they have journeyed as a flock, &#124;&#124; They are afflicted, for there is no shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Ezekiel 34:7–10; Jeremiah 23:1–2"> “My anger burned against the shepherds, &#124;&#124; And I lay a charge against the male goats, &#124;&#124; For YHWH of Hosts has inspected His flock, the house of Judah, &#124;&#124; And set them as His splendid horse in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 9:10; Zechariah 9:8; Numbers 24:17"> From him &#91;is&#93; a cornerstone, &#124;&#124; From him a nail, from him a battle-bow, &#124;&#124; From him goes forth every exactor together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Haggai 2:22; Zechariah 9:13; Psalms 20:7"> And they have been as heroes, &#124;&#124; Treading in mire of out-places in battle, &#124;&#124; And they have fought, for YHWH &#91;is&#93; with them, &#124;&#124; And have put to shame riders of horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Zechariah 10:12; Jeremiah 3:18"> And I have made the house of Judah mighty, &#124;&#124; And I save the house of Joseph, &#124;&#124; And I have caused them to dwell, for I have loved them, &#124;&#124; And they have been as &#91;if&#93; I had not cast them off, &#124;&#124; For I &#91;am&#93; their God YHWH, &#124;&#124; And I answer them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 9:15; Zechariah 9:17; 1 Samuel 2:1"> And Ephraim has been as a hero, &#124;&#124; And their heart has rejoiced as wine, &#124;&#124; And their sons see, and they have rejoiced, &#124;&#124; Their heart rejoices in YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 5:26; Isaiah 7:18; Jeremiah 33:22"> I hiss for them, and I gather them, &#124;&#124; For I have redeemed them, &#124;&#124; And they have multiplied as they multiplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Hosea 2:23; Esther 8:17"> And I sow them among peoples, &#124;&#124; And in far-off places they remember Me, &#124;&#124; And they have lived with their sons, &#124;&#124; And they have turned back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 11:11; Micah 7:14; Zechariah 8:7"> And I have brought them back from the land of Egypt, &#124;&#124; And I gather them from Asshur, &#124;&#124; And I bring them into the land of Gilead and Lebanon, &#124;&#124; And &#91;space&#93; is not found for them there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:13; Micah 5:5–6; Isaiah 43:2"> And He has passed over through the sea, &#124;&#124; And has pressed and struck billows in the sea, &#124;&#124; And all depths of a flood have been dried up, &#124;&#124; And the excellence of Asshur has been brought down, &#124;&#124; And the rod of Egypt turns aside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Isaiah 2:5; Zechariah 10:6"> And I have made them mighty in YHWH, &#124;&#124; And in His Name they walk up and down,” &#124;&#124; A declaration of YHWH!</span>
