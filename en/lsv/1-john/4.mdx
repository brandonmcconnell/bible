<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; 2 Peter 2:1; 1 Timothy 4:1"> Beloved, do not believe every spirit, but prove the spirits, if they are of God, because many false prophets have gone forth into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:14; 1 Corinthians 12:3; 1 Timothy 3:16"> In this you know the Spirit of God: every spirit that confesses Jesus Christ having come in the flesh, it is of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 2:22; 2 John 1:7; 1 John 2:18"> and every spirit that does not confess Jesus Christ having come in the flesh, it is not of God; and this is that of the antichrist, which you heard that it comes, and now it is already in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:31; 1 John 5:4; Romans 8:37"> You are of God, little children, and you have overcome them; because greater is He who &#91;is&#93; in you, than he who is in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:23; 2 Peter 2:2–3; John 17:14"> They are of the world, because they speak of this from the world, and the world hears them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 14:17; John 10:27; 1 Corinthians 2:12–14"> we are of God; he who is knowing God hears us; he who is not of God, does not hear us; from this we know the Spirit of truth and the spirit of error.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 4:20–1; 1 John 4:8; 1 Peter 1:22"> Beloved, may we love one another, because love is of God, and everyone loving has been begotten of God, and knows God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 4:16; Psalms 86:15; 2 Corinthians 13:11"> he who is not loving did not know God, because God is love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:16; 1 John 5:11; Romans 8:32"> In this was revealed the love of God in us, because God has sent His only begotten Son into the world, that we may live through Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–5; Romans 5:8–10; 1 John 4:19"> in this is love, not that we loved God, but that He loved us, and sent His Son &#91;as&#93; the propitiation for our sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 15:12–13; Colossians 3:13; John 13:34"> Beloved, if God so loved us, we also ought to love one another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 1:18; 1 John 2:5; 1 John 4:17–18"> no one has ever seen God; if we may love one another, God remains in us, and His love is having been perfected in us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 John 3:24; 1 Corinthians 3:16–17"> in this we know that we remain in Him, and He in us, because of His Spirit He has given us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:10; John 3:16–17; John 4:42"> And we have seen and testify that the Father has sent the Son &#91;as&#93; Savior of the world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 10:9; 1 John 5:5; Matthew 10:32"> whoever may confess that Jesus is the Son of God, God remains in him, and he in God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 3:24; Psalms 36:7–9; 1 John 3:1"> and we have known and believed the love that God has in us; God is love, and he who is remaining in love, remains in God, and God in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 4:12; 1 John 2:28; Romans 8:29"> In this, love has been perfected with us, that we may have boldness in the day of judgment, because even as He is, we also are in this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7; Romans 8:15; 1 John 4:12"> There is no fear in love, but perfect love casts out fear, because fear involves punishment, and he who is fearing has not been made perfect in love;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 4:10; John 3:16; John 15:16"> we love Him, because He first loved us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 John 3:17; 1 John 2:9; 1 John 4:12"> if anyone may say, “I love God,” and may hate his brother, he is a liar; for he who is not loving his brother whom he has seen, how is he able to love God whom he has not seen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 22:37–39; Leviticus 19:18; 1 John 3:11"> And this &#91;is&#93; the command we have from Him, that he who is loving God, may also love his brother.</span>
