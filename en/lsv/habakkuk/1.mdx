<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nahum 1:1; Isaiah 22:1"> The burden that Habakkuk the prophet has seen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 22:1–2; Jeremiah 14:9; Psalms 13:1–2"> Until when, O YHWH, have I cried, &#124;&#124; And You do not hear? I cry to You, “Violence!” And You do not save.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 55:9–11; Jeremiah 9:2–6; Jeremiah 20:8"> Why do You show me iniquity, &#124;&#124; And cause &#91;me&#93; to behold perversity? And spoiling and violence &#91;are&#93; before me, &#124;&#124; And there is strife, and contention lifts &#91;itself&#93; up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:126; Isaiah 1:21–23; Job 21:7"> Therefore law ceases, &#124;&#124; And judgment does not go forth forever, &#124;&#124; For the wicked is surrounding the righteous, &#124;&#124; Therefore wrong judgment goes forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:40–41; Lamentations 4:12; Isaiah 29:14"> “Look on nations, and behold and marvel greatly. For a work He is working in your days, &#124;&#124; You do not believe though it is declared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 24:2; Jeremiah 5:15; Isaiah 23:13"> For behold, I am raising up the Chaldeans, &#124;&#124; The bitter and hasty nation, &#124;&#124; That is going to the broad places of earth, &#124;&#124; To occupy dwelling places not his own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:5–9; Jeremiah 52:9–11; Deuteronomy 5:27"> He &#91;is&#93; terrible and fearful, &#124;&#124; His judgment and his excellence go forth from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Zephaniah 3:3; Hosea 8:1"> His horses have been swifter than leopards, &#124;&#124; And sharper than evening wolves, &#124;&#124; And his horsemen have increased, &#124;&#124; Even his horsemen from afar come in, &#124;&#124; They fly as an eagle, hastening to consume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Hosea 13:15; Hosea 1:10"> All for violence—he comes in, &#124;&#124; Their faces swallowing up the east wind, &#124;&#124; And he gathers a captivity as the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:6; Jeremiah 32:24; Isaiah 14:16"> And he scoffs at kings, &#124;&#124; And princes &#91;are&#93; a laughter to him, &#124;&#124; He laughs at every fortification, &#124;&#124; And he heaps up dust, and captures it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 5:20; Daniel 4:30–34; Jeremiah 4:11–12"> Then the spirit has passed on, &#124;&#124; Indeed, he transgresses, &#124;&#124; And &#91;ascribes&#93; this—his power—to his god.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Jeremiah 30:11; Jeremiah 46:28"> Are You not of old, O YHWH, my God, my Holy One? We do not die, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; You have appointed him for judgment, &#124;&#124; And, O Rock, You have founded him for reproof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:4–5; Psalms 34:15–16; Jeremiah 12:1–2"> Purer of eyes than to behold evil, &#124;&#124; You are not able to look on perverseness, &#124;&#124; Why do You behold the treacherous? You keep silent when the wicked &#124;&#124; Swallow the more righteous than he,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 6:7"> And You make man as fishes of the sea, &#124;&#124; As a creeping thing &#91;with&#93; none ruling over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 4:2; Jeremiah 16:16; Psalms 10:9"> He has brought up each of them with a hook, &#124;&#124; He catches it in his net, and gathers it in his dragnet, &#124;&#124; Therefore he delights and rejoices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:11; Deuteronomy 8:17; Daniel 4:30"> Therefore he sacrifices to his net, &#124;&#124; And makes incense to his dragnet, &#124;&#124; For by them &#91;is&#93; his portion fertile, and his food fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:5–8; Ezekiel 25:1–17; Jeremiah 25:9–26"> Does he therefore empty his net, &#124;&#124; And continually not spare to slay nations?</span>
