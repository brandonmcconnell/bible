<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 7:1–17; Psalms 86:1–17; Psalms 90:1–17"> A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, &#91;set&#93; on shigionoth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 85:6; Psalms 90:13–17; Hosea 6:2–3"> O YHWH, I heard your report, &#124;&#124; I have been afraid, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; Your work! In midst of years revive it, &#124;&#124; In the midst of years You make known, &#124;&#124; In anger You remember mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Psalms 4:4; Obadiah 1:9"> God comes from Teman, &#124;&#124; The Holy One from Mount Paran. Pause! His splendor has covered the heavens, &#124;&#124; And His praise has filled the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Matthew 17:2; Job 26:14"> And the brightness is as the light, &#124;&#124; He has rays out of His hand, &#124;&#124; And there—the hiding of His strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 12:29–30; Numbers 16:46–49; Psalms 78:50–51"> Before Him goes pestilence, &#124;&#124; And a burning flame goes forth at His feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:26; Deuteronomy 33:15; Nahum 1:5"> He has stood, and He measures earth, &#124;&#124; He has seen, and He shakes off nations, &#124;&#124; And mountains of antiquity scatter themselves, &#124;&#124; The hills of old have bowed, &#124;&#124; The ways of old &#91;are&#93; His.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 15:14–16; Genesis 10:6–7; Genesis 25:1–4"> Under sorrow I have seen tents of Cushan, &#124;&#124; Curtains of the land of Midian tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 68:17; Deuteronomy 33:26–27; Exodus 14:21–22"> Has YHWH been angry against rivers? Against rivers &#91;is&#93; Your anger? &#91;Is&#93; Your wrath against the sea? For You ride on Your horses—Your chariots of salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; Psalms 105:41; Psalms 78:15–16"> You make Your bow utterly naked, &#124;&#124; The tribes have sworn, saying, “Pause!” You cleave the earth &#91;with&#93; rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:24; Joshua 4:23–24; Psalms 93:3"> Seen You—pained are mountains, &#124;&#124; An inundation of waters has passed over, &#124;&#124; The deep has given forth its voice, &#124;&#124; It has lifted up its hands high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 144:5–6; Psalms 18:12–14; Joshua 10:11–13"> Sun—moon—has stood—a habitation, &#124;&#124; Your arrows go on at the light, &#124;&#124; At the brightness, the glittering of Your spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Micah 4:12–13; Numbers 21:23–35"> In indignation You tread earth, &#124;&#124; In anger You thresh nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 110:6; Psalms 105:15; Psalms 68:19–23"> You have gone forth for the salvation of Your people, &#124;&#124; For salvation with Your Anointed, &#124;&#124; You have struck the head of the house of the wicked, &#124;&#124; Laying bare the foundation to the neck. Pause!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 9:14; Daniel 11:40; Psalms 118:10–12"> You have pierced the head of his leaders with his own rods, &#124;&#124; They are tempestuous to scatter me, &#124;&#124; Their exultation &#91;is&#93; as to consume the poor in secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 77:19; Habakkuk 3:8; Exodus 15:8"> You have proceeded through the sea with Your horses—the clay of many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:9; Habakkuk 1:5–11; Habakkuk 3:2"> I have heard, and my belly trembles, &#124;&#124; At the noise have my lips quivered, &#124;&#124; Rottenness comes into my bones, &#124;&#124; And in my place I tremble, &#124;&#124; That I rest for a day of distress, &#124;&#124; At the coming up of the people, he overcomes it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:17; Joel 1:16–18; Deuteronomy 28:15–18"> Though the fig tree does not flourish, &#124;&#124; And there is no produce among vines, &#124;&#124; The work of the olive has failed, &#124;&#124; And fields have not yielded food, &#124;&#124; The flock has been cut off from the fold, &#124;&#124; And there is no herd in the stalls,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 13:15; Philippians 4:4; Micah 7:7"> Yet I, in YHWH I exult, &#124;&#124; I am joyful in the God of my salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:34; Psalms 18:33; Philippians 4:13"> YHWH the Lord &#91;is&#93; my strength, &#124;&#124; And He makes my feet like does, &#124;&#124; And causes me to tread on my high-places. To the overseer with my stringed instruments!</span>
