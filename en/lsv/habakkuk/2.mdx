<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 21:8; Isaiah 62:6; 2 Samuel 18:24"> On my charge I stand, and I station myself on a bulwark, and I watch to see what He speaks against me, and what I reply to my reproof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:8; Revelation 14:13; Revelation 1:18–19"> And YHWH answers me and says: “Write a vision, and explain on the tablets, &#124;&#124; That he may run who is reading it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Hebrews 10:36–37; Ezekiel 12:25"> For yet the vision &#91;is&#93; for a season, &#124;&#124; And it breathes for the end, and does not lie, &#124;&#124; If it lingers, wait for it, &#124;&#124; For surely it comes, it is not late.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:17; Hebrews 10:38; Galatians 3:11–12"> Behold, a presumptuous one! His soul is not upright within him, &#124;&#124; And the righteous lives by his faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:11–12; Proverbs 27:20; Proverbs 20:1"> And also, because the wine &#91;is&#93; treacherous, &#124;&#124; A man is haughty, and does not remain at home, &#124;&#124; Who has enlarged his soul as Sheol, &#124;&#124; And is as death that is not satisfied, &#124;&#124; And gathers to itself all the nations, &#124;&#124; And assembles to itself all the peoples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 23:7; Micah 2:4; Jeremiah 50:13"> Are these not—all of them—an allegory taken up against him, &#124;&#124; And a moral of acute sayings for him, &#124;&#124; And say, Woe &#91;to&#93; him who is multiplying &#91;what is&#93; not his? Until when also is he multiplying to himself heavy pledges?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 47:11; Jeremiah 8:17"> Do your usurers not instantly rise up, &#124;&#124; And those shaking you awake, &#124;&#124; And you have been a spoil to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Habakkuk 2:17; Jeremiah 27:7"> Because you have spoiled many nations, &#124;&#124; All the remnant of the peoples spoil you, &#124;&#124; Because of man’s blood, and of violence &#91;to&#93; the land, &#124;&#124; &#91;To&#93; the city, and &#91;to&#93; all dwelling in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:16; Jeremiah 22:13–19; Isaiah 28:15"> Woe &#91;to&#93; him who is gaining evil gain for his house, &#124;&#124; To set his nest on high, &#124;&#124; To be delivered from the hand of evil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Nahum 1:14; 2 Kings 9:26; Jeremiah 36:31"> You have counseled a shameful thing to your house, &#124;&#124; To cut off many peoples, and your soul &#91;is&#93; sinful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:40; Joshua 24:27; Job 31:38–40"> For a stone cries out from the wall, &#124;&#124; And a beam from the wood answers it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 3:10; Daniel 4:27–31; Nahum 3:1"> Woe &#91;to&#93; him who is building a city by blood, &#124;&#124; And establishing a city by iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Isaiah 50:11; Proverbs 21:30"> Behold, is it not from YHWH of Hosts &#124;&#124; And peoples are fatigued for fire, &#124;&#124; And nations for vanity are weary?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; Psalms 22:27; Psalms 72:19"> For the earth is full of the knowledge of the glory of YHWH, &#124;&#124; As the waters cover over a sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:7; Genesis 9:22; Revelation 18:3"> Woe &#91;to&#93; him who is giving drink to his neighbor, &#124;&#124; Pouring out your bottle, and also making drunk, &#124;&#124; In order to look on their nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 47:3; Nahum 3:5–6; Isaiah 51:21–23"> You have been filled—shame without honor, &#124;&#124; Drink also, and be uncircumcised, &#124;&#124; Turn around to you does the cup of the right hand of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And shameful spewing &#91;is&#93; on your glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:8; Psalms 55:23; Zechariah 11:1"> For violence &#91;done to&#93; Lebanon covers you, &#124;&#124; And spoil of beasts frightens them, &#124;&#124; Because of man’s blood, and of violence &#91;to&#93; the land, &#124;&#124; &#91;To&#93; the city, and &#91;to&#93; all dwelling in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:8; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Isaiah 44:9–10"> What profit has a carved image given &#124;&#124; That its former has hewn it? A molten image and teacher of falsehood, &#124;&#124; That the former has trusted on his own formation—to make mute idols?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 135:17; Jeremiah 10:4; 1 Kings 18:26–29"> Woe &#91;to&#93; him who is saying to wood, Awake, &#124;&#124; To a mute stone, Stir up, &#124;&#124; It &#91;is&#93; a teacher! Behold, it is overlaid—gold and silver, &#124;&#124; And there is no spirit in its midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 2:13; Zephaniah 1:7; Psalms 46:10"> And YHWH &#91;is&#93; in His holy temple, &#124;&#124; Be silent before Him, all the earth!”</span>
