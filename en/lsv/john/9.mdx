<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And passing by, He saw a man blind from birth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 13:2; John 9:34; Acts 28:4"> and His disciples asked Him, saying, “Rabbi, who sinned, this one or his parents, that he should be born blind?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:40; Ecclesiastes 9:1–2"> Jesus answered, “Neither this one sinned nor his parents, but that the works of God may be revealed in him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:35; Ephesians 5:16; John 4:34"> it is necessary for Me to be working the works of Him who sent Me while it is day; night comes when no one is able to work:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; Acts 13:47"> when I am in the world, I AM &#91;the&#93; light of the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 8:23; Mark 7:33; Revelation 3:18"> Saying these things, He spat on the ground, and made clay of the spittle, and rubbed the clay on the eyes of the blind man, and said to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5; John 9:11; John 11:37"> “Go away, wash at the pool of Siloam,” which is, interpreted, Sent. He went away, therefore, and washed, and came seeing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; 1 Samuel 21:11; Luke 18:35"> the neighbors, therefore, and those seeing him before, that he was blind, said, “Is this not he who is sitting and begging?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Others said, “This is he”; and others, “He is like to him”; he himself said, “I am &#91;he&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 9:26; John 9:21; Ecclesiastes 11:5"> They said, therefore, to him, “How were your eyes opened?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 9:27; Jeremiah 36:17"> He answered and said, “A man called Jesus made clay, and rubbed my eyes, and said to me, Go away to the pool of Siloam, and wash; and having gone away and having washed, I received sight”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 5:11–13; Exodus 2:18–20; John 7:11"> they said, therefore, to him, “Where is that One?” He says, “I have not known.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:57; John 11:46–47; John 12:42"> They bring him who once &#91;was&#93; blind to the Pharisees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:9; Luke 13:10–17; Matthew 12:1–14"> and it was a Sabbath when Jesus made the clay, and opened his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:26–27; John 9:10–11"> Again, therefore, the Pharisees also were asking him how he received sight, and he said to them, “He put clay on my eyes, and I washed—and I see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:43; John 10:19; Matthew 12:2"> Certain of the Pharisees therefore said, “This Man is not from God, because He does not keep the Sabbath”; others said, “How is a man—a sinful one—able to do such signs?” And there was a division among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:19; John 6:14; Acts 3:22–26"> They said to the blind man again, “You—what do you say of Him—that He opened your eyes?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 3:15–19; John 5:44; Isaiah 26:11"> And he said, “He is a prophet.” The Jews, therefore, did not believe concerning him that he was blind and received sight, until they called the parents of him who received sight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:8–9; Acts 4:14; Acts 3:10"> and they asked them, saying, “Is &#91;this&#93; your son, of whom you say that he was born blind? How then does he now see?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> His parents answered them and said, “We have known that this is our son, and that he was born blind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and how he now sees, we have not known; or who opened his eyes, we have not known; he is of age, ask him; he himself will speak concerning himself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 7:13; John 16:2; Luke 6:22"> His parents said these things, because they were afraid of the Jews, for the Jews had already agreed together, that if anyone may confess Him—Christ, he may be put out of the synagogue;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 9:21"> because of this his parents said, “He is of age, ask him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 7:19; John 9:16; John 8:49"> They called, therefore, the man who was blind a second time, and they said to him, “Give glory to God, we have known that this Man is a sinner”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 5:10; John 9:30; John 5:11"> he answered, therefore, and said, “If He is a sinner—I have not known, one thing I have known, that, being blind, now I see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And they said to him again, “What did He do to you? How did He open your eyes?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:67; John 9:10–15"> He answered them, “I told you already, and you did not hear; why do you wish to hear &#91;it&#93; again? Do you also wish to become His disciples?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 2:17; John 7:19; Matthew 27:39"> They reviled him, therefore, and said, “You are His disciple, and we are Moses’ disciples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:14; Numbers 12:2–7; Acts 7:35"> we have known that God has spoken to Moses, but this One—we have not known where He is from.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; Psalms 119:18; Isaiah 29:18"> The man answered and said to them, “Why, in this is a wonderful thing, that you have not known where He is from, and He opened my eyes!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 28:9"> And we have known that God does not hear sinners, but if anyone may be a worshiper of God, and may do His will, He hears him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Revelation 16:18; Job 20:4"> from the age it was not heard that anyone opened eyes of one who has been born blind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 9:16; John 3:2; Acts 5:38–39"> if this One were not from God, He were not able to do anything.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 9:22; John 9:2; Isaiah 66:5"> They answered and said to him, “In sins you were born altogether, and you teach us?” And they cast him forth outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 20:31; 1 John 5:13; John 10:36"> Jesus heard that they cast him forth outside, and having found him, He said to him, “Do you believe in the Son of God?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Song of Songs 5:9; John 1:38"> He answered and said, “Who is He, Lord, that I may believe in Him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 4:26; Psalms 25:8–9; John 14:21–23"> And Jesus said to him, “You have both seen Him, and He who is speaking with you is He”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; Matthew 28:9; Luke 24:52"> and he said, “I believe, Lord,” and worshiped Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:19; John 12:46; Acts 26:18"> And Jesus said, “I came to this world for judgment, that those not seeing may see, and those seeing may become blind.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Matthew 15:12–14; John 7:47–52"> And those of the Pharisees who were with Him heard these things, and they said to Him, “Are we also blind?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; John 15:22–24; 1 John 1:8–10"> Jesus said to them, “If you were blind, you were not having had sin, but now you say—We see, therefore your sin remains.”</span>
