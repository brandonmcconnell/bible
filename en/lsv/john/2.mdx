<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 4:46; Proverbs 31:10–12; John 21:2"> And &#91;on&#93; the third day a wedding happened in Cana of Galilee, and the mother of Jesus was there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:40–49; Matthew 10:40–42; 1 Corinthians 7:39"> and also Jesus was called, and His disciples, to the wedding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Philippians 4:6; Isaiah 24:11"> and wine having failed, the mother of Jesus says to Him, “They have no wine”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:6; John 13:1; John 7:30"> Jesus says to her, “What &#91;is that&#93; to Me and to you, woman? My hour is not yet come.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:14; Genesis 6:22; Hebrews 11:8"> His mother says to the servants, “Whatever He may say to you—do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 3:25; Hebrews 9:10; Ephesians 5:26"> And there were six water-jugs of stone there, placed according to the purifying of the Jews, holding each two or three measures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:2–6; John 2:5; John 2:3"> Jesus says to them, “Fill the water-jugs with water”; and they filled them—to the brim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 2:9; Proverbs 3:5–6; Romans 13:7"> and He says to them, “Draw out, now, and carry to the headwaiter”; and they bore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 4:46; John 7:17; Psalms 119:100"> And as the headwaiter tasted the water become wine, and did not know where it is from (but the servants knew, who have drawn the water), the headwaiter calls the bridegroom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 7:16–17; Proverbs 9:1–6; Luke 16:25"> and says to him, “Every man, at first, sets forth the good wine; and when they may have drunk freely, then the inferior; you kept the good wine until now.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:30–31; John 1:14; John 3:2"> This &#91;is the&#93; beginning of the signs Jesus did in Cana of Galilee, and revealed His glory, and His disciples believed in Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; Matthew 13:55–56; Mark 6:3"> after this He went down to Capernaum, He, and His mother, and His brothers, and His disciples; and they did not remain there many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:41; John 11:55; John 2:23"> And the Passover of the Jews was near, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:45–46; Mark 11:15; Mark 11:17"> and He found in the temple those selling oxen, and sheep, and doves, and the money-changers sitting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:6; Zechariah 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> and having made a whip of small cords, He put all forth out of the temple, also the sheep, and the oxen; and He poured out the coins of the money-changers, and He overthrew the tables,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 21:13; Jeremiah 7:11; Mark 11:17"> and He said to those selling the doves, “Take these things from here; do not make the house of My Father a house of merchandise.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 69:9; Psalms 119:139"> And His disciples remembered that it is written: “The zeal of Your house ate Me up”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 8:11; Mark 11:27–28"> the Jews then answered and said to Him, “What sign do You show to us—that You do these things?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Mark 15:29; Matthew 27:40"> Jesus answered and said to them, “Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The Jews, therefore, said, “This temple was built &#91;in&#93; forty-six years, and will You raise it up in three days?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:20–22"> But He spoke concerning the temple of His body;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 12:16; John 16:4; Luke 24:7–8"> when, then, He was raised out of the dead, His disciples remembered that He said this to them, and they believed the Writing, and the word that Jesus said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 7:31; Luke 8:13; John 6:14"> And as He was in Jerusalem, in the Passover, in the celebration, many believed in His Name, beholding His signs that He was doing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:64; Hebrews 4:13; Acts 1:24"> and Jesus Himself was not trusting Himself to them, because of His knowing all &#91;men&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 13:11; Matthew 9:4; John 6:64"> and because He had no need that any should testify concerning man, for He Himself was knowing what was in man.</span>
