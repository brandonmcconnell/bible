<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 16:9; Matthew 27:60; John 19:25"> And on the first &#91;day&#93; of the weeks, Mary the Magdalene comes early (there being yet darkness) to the tomb, and she sees the stone having been taken away out of the tomb;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:23; John 20:13; John 21:24"> she runs, therefore, and comes to Simon Peter, and to the other disciple whom Jesus was cherishing, and says to them, “They took away the Lord out of the tomb, and we have not known where they laid Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:12"> Peter, therefore, went forth, and the other disciple, and they were coming to the tomb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12; 1 Corinthians 9:24"> and the two were running together, and the other disciple ran forward more quickly than Peter, and came first to the tomb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:40; John 20:11; John 11:44"> and having stooped down, sees the linen clothes lying, yet, indeed, he did not enter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:17; John 21:7; Matthew 16:15–16"> Simon Peter, therefore, comes, following him, and he entered into the tomb, and beholds the linen clothes lying &#91;there&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 11:44"> and the napkin that was on His head not lying with the linen clothes, but apart, having been folded up, in one place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 20:25; John 20:4; John 1:50"> then, therefore, the other disciple who came first to the tomb entered also, and he saw and believed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Isaiah 25:8; Isaiah 53:10–12"> for they did not yet know the Writing, that it was necessary for Him to rise again out of the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 7:53; John 16:32"> The disciples therefore went away again to their own friends,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:5"> and Mary was standing near the tomb, weeping outside; as she was weeping, then, she stooped down into the tomb, and beholds two messengers in white, sitting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:3–7; Revelation 3:4; Mark 16:5–6"> one at the head and one at the feet, where the body of Jesus had been laid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:15; John 2:4; 1 Samuel 1:8"> And they say to her, “Woman, why do you weep?” She says to them, “Because they took away my Lord, and I have not known where they laid Him”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 21:4; Matthew 28:9"> and having said these things, she turned backward, and sees Jesus standing, and she had not known that it is Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 20:13; John 18:7; John 1:38"> Jesus says to her, “Woman, why do you weep? Whom do you seek?” She, supposing that He is the gardener, says to Him, “Lord, if You carried Him away, tell me where You laid Him, and I will take Him away”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:3; John 1:38; Acts 10:3"> Jesus says to her, “Mary!” Having turned, she says to Him, “Rabboni!” That is to say, “Teacher.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:28; Ephesians 4:8–10; Galatians 4:6–7"> Jesus says to her, “Do not be touching Me, for I have not yet ascended to My Father; and be going on to My brothers, and say to them, I ascend to My Father and your Father, and &#91;to&#93; My God and your God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 24:10; Matthew 28:10; John 20:1"> Mary the Magdalene comes, reporting to the disciples that she has seen the LORD, and &#91;that&#93; He said these things to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 20:26; John 14:27; John 20:21"> It being, therefore, evening, on that day, the first &#91;day&#93; of the weeks, and the doors having been shut where the disciples were assembled through fear of the Jews, Jesus came and stood in the midst, and says to them, “Peace to you”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 16:22; John 16:20; John 20:27"> and having said this, He showed them His hands and side; the disciples, therefore, rejoiced, having seen the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 17:18–19; John 13:20; John 3:17"> Jesus, therefore, said to them again, “Peace to you; according as the Father has sent Me, I also send you”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 33:4; Genesis 2:7; Acts 8:15"> having said this, He breathed on &#91;them&#93;, and says to them, “Receive the Holy Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 18:18; Matthew 16:19; Mark 2:5–10"> if you may forgive the sins of any, they are forgiven them; if you may retain of any, they have been retained.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 11:16; John 6:66–67; Hebrews 10:25"> And Thomas, one of the Twelve, who is called Didymus, was not with them when Jesus came;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:30; Mark 16:11; Hebrews 3:12"> the other disciples, therefore, said to him, “We have seen the Lord!” And he said to them, “If I may not see the mark of the nails in His hands, and may &#91;not&#93; put my finger into the mark of the nails, and may &#91;not&#93; put my hand into His side, I will not believe.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 20:19; Isaiah 54:10; John 20:21"> And after eight days, again His disciples were within, and Thomas &#91;was&#93; with them; Jesus comes, the doors having been shut, and He stood in the midst and said, “Peace to you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 20:25; 1 John 1:1–2; John 20:20"> Then He says to Thomas, “Bring your finger here, and see My hands, and bring your hand, and put &#91;it&#93; into My side, and do not become unbelieving, but believing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:1; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 7:14"> And Thomas answered and said to Him, “My Lord and my God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Hebrews 11:1"> Jesus says to him, “Because you have seen Me, Thomas, you have believed; blessed &#91;are&#93; those having not seen, and having believed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 21:25; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Romans 15:4"> Many indeed, therefore, other signs Jesus also did before His disciples that are not written in this scroll;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 10:43; John 3:15–16; 1 John 5:1"> and these have been written that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that believing, you may have life in His Name.</span>
