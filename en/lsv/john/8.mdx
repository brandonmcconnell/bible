<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Mark 11:1; Luke 19:37"> And at dawn He came again to the temple &#91;courts&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 26:55; Luke 4:20; Luke 21:37"> and all the people were coming to Him, and having sat down, He was teaching them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and the scribes and the Pharisees bring to Him a woman having been taken in adultery, and having set her in the midst,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> they say to Him, “Teacher, this woman was taken in the very crime &#91;of&#93; committing adultery,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Ezekiel 16:38–40"> and in the Law, Moses commanded us that such be stoned; You, therefore, what do You say?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:13; Luke 10:25; Ecclesiastes 3:7"> And this they said, trying Him, that they might have to accuse Him. And Jesus, having stooped down, with the finger He was writing on the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:1–5; Romans 2:21–25; Romans 2:1–3"> and when they continued asking Him, having bent Himself back, He said to them, “The sinless of you—let him cast the first stone at her”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and again having stooped down, He was writing on the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:22; Romans 2:15; 1 John 3:20"> and they having heard, and being convicted by the conscience, were going forth one by one, having begun from the elders—to the last; and Jesus was left alone, and the woman standing in the midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:11–12"> And Jesus having bent Himself back, and having seen no one but the woman, said to her, “Woman, where are those—your accusers? Did no one pass sentence on you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 3:17; John 5:14; Isaiah 1:16–18"> And she said, “No one, Lord”; and Jesus said to her, “Neither do I pass sentence on you; be going on, and sin no more.”&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 12:46; John 9:5; John 12:35"> Again, therefore, Jesus spoke to them, saying, “I AM the light of the world; he who is following Me will not walk in the darkness, but he will have the light of life.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 5:31–47"> The Pharisees, therefore, said to Him, “You testify of Yourself, Your testimony is not true”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:28; John 13:3; John 8:42"> Jesus answered and said to them, “And if I testify of Myself—My testimony is true, because I have known from where I came, and to where I go, and you have not known from where I come, or to where I go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; John 7:24; John 12:47"> You judge according to the flesh; I do not judge anyone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:31; John 8:29; John 16:32"> and even if I do judge My judgment is true, because I am not alone, but I and the Father who sent Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:6; Hebrews 10:28"> and also in your law it has been written that the testimony of two men is true;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:38; John 8:58; John 8:25"> I am &#91;One&#93; who is testifying of Myself, and the Father who sent Me testifies of Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:3; John 8:54–55; John 14:6–9"> They said, therefore, to Him, “Where is Your father?” Jesus answered, “You have neither known Me nor My Father: if you had known Me, you had also known My Father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:30; John 7:8; Mark 12:41"> Jesus spoke these sayings in the treasury, teaching in the temple, and no one seized Him, because His hour had not yet come;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:34; John 8:24; 1 Corinthians 15:17–18"> therefore Jesus said again to them, “I go away, and you will seek Me, and you will die in your sin; to where I go away, you are not able to come.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 8:52; Psalms 22:6; John 8:48"> The Jews, therefore, said, “Will He kill Himself, because He says, To where I go away, you are not able to come?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 3:31; John 17:14; John 17:16"> And He said to them, “You are from beneath, I am from above; you are of this world, I am not of this world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; Mark 16:16; John 3:18"> I said, therefore, to you, that you will die in your sins, for if you may not believe that I AM, you will die in your sins.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:17–29; Luke 22:67; John 1:22"> They said, therefore, to Him, “You—who are You?” And Jesus said to them, “Even what I spoke of to you at the beginning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 15:15; John 12:47–50; John 8:40"> many things I have to speak concerning you and to judge, but He who sent Me is true, and I—what things I heard from Him—these I say to the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; John 8:43; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4"> They did not know that He spoke to them of the Father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 5:19; John 5:30; John 6:38"> Jesus, therefore, said to them, “When you may lift up the Son of Man then you will know that I AM; and I do nothing of Myself, but according as My Father taught Me, these things I speak;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:38; John 16:32; John 4:34"> and He who sent Me is with Me; the Father did not leave Me alone, because I always do the things pleasing to Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 7:31; John 2:23; John 11:45"> As He is speaking these things, many believed in Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 John 1:9; John 15:4–9; 2 Timothy 3:14"> Jesus, therefore, said to the Jews who believed in Him, “If you may remain in My word, you are truly My disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 8:2; John 8:36; John 17:17"> and you will know the truth, and the truth will make you free.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:9; John 8:39; Genesis 15:13"> They answered Him, “We are seed of Abraham; and we have been servants to no one at any time; how do You say—You will become free?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 6:16; 2 Peter 2:19; Proverbs 5:22"> Jesus answered them, “Truly, truly, I say to you, everyone who is committing sin, is a servant of sin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–7; Luke 15:31; Romans 8:15–17"> and the servant does not remain in the house—throughout the age, the Son remains—throughout the age;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Galatians 5:1; Romans 8:2"> if then the Son may make you free, in reality you will be free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 8:45–47; John 7:1"> I have known that you are seed of Abraham, but you seek to kill Me, because My word has no place in you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 5:19; John 8:44; John 8:41"> I speak that which I have seen with My Father, and you, therefore, do that which you have seen with your father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; John 8:37; Romans 9:7"> They answered and said to Him, “Our father is Abraham”; Jesus says to them, “If you were children of Abraham, the works of Abraham you were doing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 8:26; Galatians 4:29; Galatians 4:16"> and now, you seek to kill Me—a Man who has spoken to you the truth I heard from God; Abraham did not do this;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; John 8:38; Isaiah 63:16"> you do the works of your father.” They said, therefore, to Him, “We have not been born of whoredom; we have one Father—God”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:49; John 14:10; John 17:8"> Jesus then said to them, “If God were your father, you were loving Me, for I came forth from God, and am come; for neither have I come of Myself, but He sent Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 12:39–40; Romans 8:7–8; John 7:17"> why do you not know My speech? Because you are not able to hear My word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 13:10; 1 John 3:12; 1 John 3:8–10"> You are of a father—the Devil, and the desires of your father you will to do; he was a manslayer from the beginning, and he has not stood in the truth, because there is no truth in him; when one may speak the falsehood, he speaks of his own, because he is a liar—also his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Galatians 4:16; John 18:37"> And because I say the truth, you do not believe Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; John 16:8; 1 Peter 2:22"> Who of you convicts Me of sin? And if I speak truth, why do you not believe Me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 10:26–27; 2 John 1:9; John 18:37"> He who is of God, he hears the sayings of God; because of this you do not hear, because you are not of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 7:20; John 8:52; Matthew 10:5"> The Jews, therefore, answered and said to Him, “Do we not say well, that You are a Samaritan, and have a demon?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Peter 2:23; Isaiah 49:3; John 13:31–32"> Jesus answered, “I do not have a demon, but I honor My Father, and you dishonor Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 5:41; John 8:54; John 7:18"> and I do not seek My own glory; there is &#91;One&#93; who is seeking and is judging;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 5:24; John 3:15–16"> truly, truly, I say to you, if anyone may keep My word, he may not see death—throughout the age.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 9:24; John 8:48; Hebrews 2:9"> The Jews, therefore, said to Him, “Now we have known that You have a demon; Abraham died, and the prophets, and You say, If anyone may keep My word, he will not taste of death—throughout the age!</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 4:12; Romans 9:5; Hebrews 7:1–7"> Are You greater than our father Abraham, who died? And the prophets died; whom do You make Yourself?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 8:50; Proverbs 25:27; 2 Peter 1:17"> Jesus answered, “If I glorify Myself, My glory is nothing; it is My Father who is glorifying Me, of whom you say that He is your God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 8:19; John 8:44; John 7:28–29"> and you have not known Him, and I have known Him, and if I say that I have not known Him, I will be like you—speaking falsely; but I have known Him, and I keep His word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13; Genesis 22:18; Galatians 3:7–9"> your father Abraham was glad that he might see My day; and he saw, and rejoiced.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> The Jews, therefore, said to Him, “You are not yet fifty years old, and You have seen Abraham?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Exodus 3:14; Colossians 1:17; John 17:5"> Jesus said to them, “Truly, truly, I say to you, before Abraham’s coming—I AM”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 11:8; John 10:30–33; John 10:39–40"> they took up, therefore, stones that they may cast at Him, but Jesus hid Himself, and went forth out of the temple, going through the midst of them, and so passed by.</span>
