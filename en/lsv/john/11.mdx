<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; John 12:17; 2 Kings 20:1–12"> And there was a certain one ailing, Lazarus, from Bethany, of the village of Mary and Martha her sister—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 12:3; Mark 14:3; Matthew 26:6–7"> and it was Mary who anointed the LORD with ointment, and wiped His feet with her hair, whose brother Lazarus was ailing—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:5; Revelation 3:19; 2 Timothy 4:20"> therefore the sisters sent to Him, saying, “Lord, behold, he whom You cherish is ailing”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 11:40; John 9:3; 1 Peter 4:14"> and Jesus having heard, said, “This ailment is not to death, but for the glory of God, that the Son of God may be glorified through it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:9–13; John 16:27; John 17:26"> And Jesus was loving Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 30:18; Isaiah 55:8–9; Genesis 45:1–5"> when, therefore, He heard that he is ailing, then indeed He remained in the place in which He was two days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:40–42; Luke 9:51; Acts 20:22–24"> then after this, He says to the disciples, “We may go to Judea again”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:31; John 8:59; Acts 21:12–13"> the disciples say to Him, “Rabbi, the Jews were just seeking to stone You, and again You go there?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 9:4; John 12:35; Jeremiah 31:9"> Jesus answered, “Are there not twelve hours in the day? If anyone may walk in the day, he does not stumble, because he sees the light of this world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:10–11; Proverbs 4:18–19; Jeremiah 13:16"> and if anyone may walk in the night, he stumbles, because the light is not in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 11:13; John 11:43–44"> He said these things, and after this He says to them, “Our friend Lazarus has fallen asleep, but I go on that I may awake him”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> therefore His disciples said, “Lord, if he has fallen asleep, he will be saved”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:24"> but Jesus had spoken about his death, but they thought that He speaks about the repose of sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:25; John 16:29; John 10:24"> Then, therefore, Jesus said to them freely, “Lazarus has died;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; John 14:10–11; Isaiah 54:15"> and I rejoice, for your sake (that you may believe), that I was not there; but we may go to him”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 13:37; Matthew 10:3; John 21:2"> therefore Thomas, who is called Didymus, said to the fellow-disciples, “We may go—we also, that we may die with Him,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 11:39; Hosea 6:2; Acts 2:27–31"> Jesus, therefore, having come, found him having already been four days in the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 14:20; Revelation 21:16; John 6:19"> And Bethany was near to Jerusalem, about fifteen stadia off,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 2:11; John 11:31; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> and many of the Jews had come to Martha and Mary, that they might comfort them concerning their brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; 1 Thessalonians 4:17; Matthew 25:1"> Martha, therefore, when she heard that Jesus comes, met Him, and Mary kept sitting in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:32; 1 Kings 17:18; Matthew 9:18"> Martha, therefore, said to Jesus, “Lord, if You had been here, my brother had not died;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:31; John 11:41–42; Matthew 28:18"> but even now, I have known that whatever You may ask of God, God will give to You”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:43–44"> Jesus says to her, “Your brother will rise again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Acts 24:15; John 5:28–29"> Martha says to Him, “I have known that he will rise again in the resurrection in the last day”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14; John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12"> Jesus said to her, “I AM the resurrection, and the life; he who is believing in Me, even if he may die, will live;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 10:28; John 5:24; Romans 8:13"> and everyone who is living and believing in Me will not die—throughout the age;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:16; John 6:69; John 6:14"> do you believe this?” She says to Him, “Yes, Lord, I have believed that You are the Christ, the Son of God, who is coming into the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 11:20; John 13:13; Mark 10:49"> And having said these things, she went away, and called Mary her sister privately, saying, “The Teacher is present, and calls you”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Psalms 27:8; Song of Songs 3:1–4"> she, when she heard, rises up quickly, and comes to Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:20"> and Jesus had not yet come into the village, but was in the place where Martha met Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:19; Genesis 37:35; 2 Samuel 12:16–18"> the Jews, therefore, who were with her in the house, and were comforting her, having seen Mary that she rose up quickly and went forth, followed her, saying, “She goes away to the tomb, that she may weep there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 11:21; Luke 17:16; Luke 5:8"> Mary, therefore, when she came where Jesus was, having seen Him, fell at His feet, saying to Him, “Lord, if You had been here, my brother had not died”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:38; John 12:27; Mark 3:5"> Jesus, therefore, when He saw her weeping, and the Jews who came with her weeping, groaned in the spirit, and troubled Himself, and He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 15:47; Matthew 28:6; John 1:39"> “Where have you laid him?” They say to Him, “Lord, come and see”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Hebrews 4:15; John 11:33"> Jesus wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 11:3; 1 John 3:1; 1 John 4:9–10"> The Jews, therefore, said, “Behold, how He was cherishing him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 11:32; Luke 23:39"> And certain of them said, “Was not this One, who opened the eyes of the blind man, also able to cause that this one might not have died?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:33; Luke 24:2; Matthew 27:60"> Jesus, therefore, again groaning in Himself, comes to the tomb, and it was a cave, and a stone was lying on it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 11:17; Mark 16:3; Psalms 49:7"> Jesus says, “Take away the stone”; the sister of him who has died—Martha—says to Him, “Lord, he already stinks, for he is four days dead”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:23–26; Romans 4:17–25"> Jesus says to her, “Did I not say to you that if you may believe, you will see the glory of God?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; John 17:1; Psalms 123:1"> Therefore they took away the stone where the dead was laid, and Jesus lifted His eyes upwards, and said, “Father, I thank You that You heard Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 3:17; John 17:21; 1 John 4:14"> and I knew that You always hear Me, but because of the multitude that is standing by, I said &#91;it&#93;, that they may believe that You sent Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33–36; Acts 9:40; Acts 9:34"> And saying these things, He cried out with a loud voice, “Lazarus, come forth!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 5:25; John 20:7; John 11:25–26"> And he who died came forth, feet and hands being bound with grave-clothes, and his face was bound around with a napkin; Jesus says to them, “Loose him, and permit to go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 11:19; John 10:41"> Many, therefore, of the Jews who came to Mary, and beheld what Jesus did, believed in Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 9:13; Acts 5:25; John 12:37"> but certain of them went away to the Pharisees, and told them what Jesus did;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 12:19; Acts 4:5–6"> the chief priests, therefore, and the Pharisees, gathered together &#91;the&#93; Sanhedrin and said, “What may we do? Because this Man does many signs?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Matthew 23:35–38; Matthew 27:25"> If we may leave Him alone thus, all will believe in Him; and the Romans will come, and will take away both our place and nation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 11:51; John 18:13–14"> And a certain one of them, Caiaphas, being chief priest of that year, said to them, “You have not known anything,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:14; Luke 24:46; Romans 3:8"> nor reason that it is good for us that one man may die for the people, and not the whole nation perish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Exodus 28:30"> And he did not say this of himself, but being chief priest of that year, he prophesied that Jesus was about to die for the nation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 10:16; Isaiah 49:6; 1 John 2:2"> and not for the nation only, but that also the children of God, who have been scattered abroad, He may gather together into one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Acts 5:33; Acts 9:23; Mark 14:1"> From that day, therefore, they took counsel together that they may kill Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 7:1; John 7:4; 2 Chronicles 13:19"> Jesus, therefore, was no longer freely walking among the Jews, but went away from there into the region near the wilderness, to a city called Ephraim, and there He tarried with His disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:17–20; Hebrews 9:13–14; John 6:4"> And the Passover of the Jews was near, and many went up to Jerusalem out of the country before the Passover, that they might purify themselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 7:11; John 11:7–8"> therefore they were seeking Jesus and said with one another, standing in the temple, “What appears to you—that He may not come to the celebration?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 9:22; John 8:59; John 5:16–18"> And both the chief priests and the Pharisees had given a command, that if anyone may know where He is, he may show &#91;it&#93;, so that they may seize Him.</span>
