<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Isaiah 27:2–3; 1 Corinthians 3:9"> “I AM the true vine, and My Father is the vinedresser;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 3:10; Hebrews 12:10–11; Matthew 15:13"> every branch not bearing fruit in Me, He takes it away, and everyone bearing fruit, He cleanses by pruning it, that it may bear more fruit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 17:17; Ephesians 5:26; 1 Peter 1:22"> you are already clean, because of the word that I have spoken to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Galatians 2:20; John 8:31"> remain in Me, and I in you, as the branch is not able to bear fruit of itself, if it may not remain in the vine, so neither you, if you may not remain in Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Colossians 1:10; John 15:16"> I AM the vine, you the branches; he who is remaining in Me, and I in him, this one bears much fruit, because apart from Me you are not able to do anything;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:19; Hebrews 6:7–8; Matthew 13:41"> if anyone may not remain in Me, he was cast forth outside as the branch, and was withered, and they gather them, and cast into fire, and they are burned;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; 1 John 5:14; John 14:13"> if you may remain in Me, and My sayings may remain in you, whatever you may wish you will ask, and it will be done to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:31; Matthew 5:16; Philippians 1:11"> In this was My Father glorified, that you may bear much fruit, and you will become My disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 17:23–24; John 17:26; 1 John 2:28"> According as the Father loved Me, I also loved you, remain in My love;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 14:15; 1 John 5:3; 1 John 3:21–24"> if you may keep My commands, you will remain in My love, according as I have kept the commands of My Father, and remain in His love;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 17:13; Romans 15:13; John 16:24"> these things I have spoken to you, that My joy in you may remain, and your joy may be full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 4:21; 1 Peter 4:8; John 13:34"> This is My command, that you love one another, according as I loved you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 10:11; Ephesians 5:2; Romans 5:6–8"> greater love has no one than this, that anyone may lay down his life for his friends;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 12:50; 1 John 5:3; James 2:23"> you are My friends if you may do whatever I command you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; 1 Corinthians 2:9–12; Matthew 13:11"> I no longer call you servants, because the servant has not known what his lord does, and I have called you friends, because all things that I heard from My Father, I made known to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 15:19; Jeremiah 1:5–7; Ephesians 2:10"> You did not choose Me, but I chose you, and appointed you, that you might go away, and might bear fruit, and your fruit might remain, that whatever you may ask of the Father in My Name, He may give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:12; 1 John 3:14–17; 1 Peter 2:17"> These things I command you, that you love one another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 3:13; John 7:7; Mark 13:13"> if the world hates you, you know that it has hated Me before you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 4:4–5; John 17:14–16; Ephesians 1:4–11"> if you were of the world, the world would have been cherishing its own, but because you are not of the world, but I chose &#91;you&#93; out of the world—because of this the world hates you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:12; John 13:16; Matthew 10:24"> Remember the word that I said to you: A servant is not greater than his lord; if they persecuted Me, they will also persecute you; if they kept My word, they will also keep yours;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 10:22; John 16:3; Matthew 10:18"> but all these things will they do to you, because of My Name, because they have not known Him who sent Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:41; James 4:17; Acts 17:30"> if I had not come and spoken to them, they were not having sin; but now they have no pretext for their sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 8:40–42; 2 John 1:9; 1 John 2:23"> He who is hating Me, hates My Father also;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 5:36; John 14:9; John 7:31"> if I did not do among them the works that no other has done, they were not having sin, and now they have both seen and hated both Me and My Father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 109:3; Psalms 69:4; Psalms 35:19"> but—that the word may be fulfilled that was written in their law—They hated Me without a cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 14:26; John 14:16–17; John 16:13–15"> And when the Comforter may come, whom I will send to you from the Father—the Spirit of truth, who comes forth from the Father, He will testify of Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 21:24; Acts 10:39–42"> and you also testify, because you are with Me from the beginning.”</span>
