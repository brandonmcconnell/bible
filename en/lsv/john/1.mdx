<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; John 17:5; Revelation 19:13"> In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> this One was in the beginning with God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:16–17; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Revelation 4:11"> all things happened through Him, and without Him not even one thing happened that has happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; John 5:26"> In Him was life, and the life was the light of men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 3:19–20; John 12:36–40; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> and the light shined in the darkness, and the darkness did not perceive it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; John 3:28; Isaiah 40:3–5"> There came a man—having been sent from God—whose name &#91;is&#93; John,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:4; John 1:36; John 5:33–35"> this one came for testimony, that he might testify about the Light, that all might believe through him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:20; John 3:28; Acts 19:4"> that one was not the Light, but—that he might testify about the Light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 12:46; John 1:4; 1 John 2:8"> He was the true Light, which enlightens every man, coming into the world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 17:25; 1 John 3:1; 1 Corinthians 2:8"> He was in the world, and the world was made through Him, and the world did not know Him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Isaiah 53:2–3; John 3:32"> He came to &#91;His&#93; own, and &#91;His&#93; own did not receive Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 3:26; Romans 8:14; 1 John 3:1"> but as many as received Him, to them He gave authority to become sons of God—to those believing in His Name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 3:9; James 1:18; 1 Peter 1:23"> who were begotten, not of blood, nor of will of flesh, nor of will of man, but of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:9; 1 Timothy 3:16; Isaiah 40:5"> And the Word became flesh, and dwelt among us, and we beheld His glory, glory as of &#91;the&#93; only begotten of &#91;the&#93; Father, full of grace and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Colossians 1:17; Luke 3:16"> John testifies concerning Him, and has cried, saying, “This was He of whom I said, He who is coming after me has come before me, for He was before me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:19; Romans 5:2; Ephesians 2:5–10"> And we all received out of His fullness, and grace for grace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:14; John 14:6; Romans 3:19–26"> for the Law was given through Moses, the grace and the truth came through Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; John 6:46; Matthew 11:27"> No one has ever seen God; the only begotten God who is on the bosom of the Father—He has expounded &#91;Him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 3:15–18; John 10:24; Acts 19:4"> And this is the testimony of John when the Jews sent priests and Levites out of Jerusalem, that they might question him, “Who are you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 1:7–8; Matthew 3:11–12; John 3:28–36"> And he confessed and did not deny, and confessed, “I am not the Christ.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Deuteronomy 18:15–18; Matthew 11:14"> And they questioned him, “What then? Are you Elijah?” And he says, “I am not.” “Are you the prophet?” And he answered, “No.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:13"> Then they said to him, “Who are you, that we may give an answer to those sending us? What do you say concerning yourself?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Mark 1:3; Matthew 3:3"> He said, “I &#91;am&#93; a voice of one crying in the wilderness: Make straight the way of the LORD, as Isaiah the prophet said.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 11:53; Matthew 23:13–15; Luke 11:39–44"> And those sent were of the Pharisees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18; Deuteronomy 18:15; John 1:20–22"> and they questioned him and said to him, “Why, then, do you immerse, if you are not the Christ, nor Elijah, nor the prophet?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 1:10–11; Luke 3:16; Acts 1:5"> John answered them, saying, “I immerse in water, but in the midst of you has stood He whom you have not known, it is this One who is coming after me, who has been before me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 1:30; Mark 1:7; Matthew 3:11"> of whom I am not worthy that I may loose the strap of His sandal.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 10:40; John 3:23; Judges 7:24"> These things came to pass in Bethabara, beyond the Jordan, where John was immersing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 John 2:2; 1 John 3:5"> On the next day John sees Jesus coming to him and says, “Behold, the Lamb of God, who is taking away the sin of the world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:27; John 1:15; Luke 3:16"> this is He concerning whom I said, After me comes a Man who has come before me, because He was before me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Luke 1:17; Malachi 4:2–5"> and I did not know Him, but that He might be revealed to Israel, because of this I came immersing in water.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 1:10; Luke 3:22; Matthew 3:16"> And John testified, saying, “I have seen the Spirit coming down out of Heaven as a dove, and &#91;that&#93; One remained on Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:5; Matthew 3:11; Luke 3:16"> and I did not know Him, but He who sent me to immerse in water, He said to me, On whomsoever you may see the Spirit coming down, and remaining on Him, this is He who is immersing in the Holy Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; John 1:49; Psalms 89:26–27"> and I have seen, and have testified, that this is the Son of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 3:25–26; Malachi 3:16"> On the next day, again, John was standing, and two of his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 1:29; 1 Peter 1:19–20; Isaiah 65:1–2"> and having looked on Jesus walking, he says, “Behold, the Lamb of God”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Romans 10:17; John 1:43; Zechariah 8:21"> and the two disciples heard him speaking, and they followed Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 18:7; John 3:2; John 1:49"> And Jesus having turned, and having beheld them following, says to them, “What do you seek?” And they said to Him, “Rabbi” (which is, being interpreted, Teacher), “where do You remain?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:29; Matthew 11:28–30; John 6:37"> He says to them, “Come and see”; they came and saw where He remains, and they remained with Him that day and the hour was about the tenth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 1:16–20; Matthew 4:18–22; John 1:40–42"> Andrew, the brother of Simon Peter, was one of the two who heard from John, and followed Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 4:25; John 1:45; Psalms 89:20"> this one first finds his own brother Simon and says to him, “We have found the Messiah,” (which is, being interpreted, Anointed One),</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 21:15–17; 1 Corinthians 3:22; 1 Corinthians 15:5"> and he brought him to Jesus: and having looked on him, Jesus says, “You are Simon, the son of Jonas, you will be called Cephas,” (which is interpreted, A rock).</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 9:9; John 14:8; Isaiah 65:1"> On the next day He willed to go forth to Galilee, and He finds Philip and says to him, “Follow Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 12:21; Matthew 11:21; Mark 8:22"> And Philip was from Bethsaida, of the city of Andrew and Peter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18–22; Luke 24:27; Micah 5:2"> Philip finds Nathanael and says to him, “Him of whom Moses wrote in the Law, and the Prophets, we have found, Jesus the Son of Joseph, who &#91;is&#93; from Nazareth!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 7:52; John 7:41–42; John 4:29"> And Nathanael said to him, “Is any good thing able to be out of Nazareth?” Philip said to him, “Come and see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Revelation 14:5; Psalms 32:2; Psalms 73:1"> Jesus saw Nathanael coming to Him, and He says concerning him, “Behold, truly an Israelite, in whom is no guile”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; John 2:25; Psalms 139:1–2"> Nathanael says to Him, “From where do You know me?” Jesus answered and said to him, “Before Philip’s calling you, being under the fig tree, I saw you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:15; Zechariah 9:9; Matthew 27:42"> Nathanael answered and says to Him, “Rabbi, You are the Son of God, You are the King of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 11:40; John 20:29; Luke 1:45"> Jesus answered and said to him, “Because I said to you, I saw you under the fig tree, you believe; you will see greater things than these”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Acts 7:56; Luke 22:43"> and He says to him, “Truly, truly, I say to you, from now on you will see Heaven opened, and the messengers of God going up and coming down on the Son of Man.”</span>
</div>
