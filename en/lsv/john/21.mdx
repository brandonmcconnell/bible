<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:23; Matthew 28:16"> After these things Jesus Himself again appeared to the disciples on the Sea of Tiberias, and He revealed Himself thus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:21–22; John 1:45–1; John 4:46"> Simon Peter, and Thomas who is called Didymus, and Nathanael from Cana of Galilee, and the &#91;sons&#93; of Zebedee, and two of His other disciples were together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 5:5; Matthew 4:18–20; 2 Thessalonians 3:7–9"> Simon Peter says to them, “I go away to fish”; they say to him, “We go—we also—with you”; they went forth and immediately entered into the boat, and on that night they caught nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 20:14; Luke 24:15–16; Luke 24:31"> And morning having now come, Jesus stood at the shore, yet indeed the disciples did not know that it is Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; Philippians 4:11–13; 1 John 2:13"> Jesus, therefore, says to them, “Boys, do you have any meat?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:4–7; Acts 2:41; John 2:5"> They answered Him, “No”; and He said to them, “Cast the net at the right side of the boat, and you will find &#91;some&#93;”; they cast, therefore, and no longer were they able to draw it, from the multitude of the fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 13:23; John 20:2; Matthew 14:28–29"> That disciple, therefore, whom Jesus was loving says to Peter, “It is the Lord!” Simon Peter, therefore, having heard that it is the LORD, girded on the outer coat (for he was naked), and cast himself into the sea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11"> and the other disciples came by the little boat, for they were not far from the land, but about two hundred cubits away, dragging the net of the fishes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 21:13; 1 Kings 19:5–6; John 21:10"> when, therefore, they came to the land, they behold a fire of coals lying &#91;there&#93;, and a fish lying on it, and bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Jesus says to them, “Bring from the fishes that you caught now”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 5:6–8; Acts 2:41"> Simon Peter went up, and drew the net up on the land, full of great fishes—one hundred fifty-three; and though they were so many, the net was not split.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:41; Luke 9:45; John 16:19"> Jesus says to them, “Come, dine”; and none of the disciples were venturing to inquire of Him, “Who are You?” Knowing that it is the LORD;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 21:9; Acts 10:41; Luke 24:42–43"> Jesus, therefore, comes and takes the bread and gives &#91;it&#93; to them, and the fish in like manner;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 20:26; John 20:19"> this &#91;is&#93; now a third time Jesus was revealed to His disciples, having been raised from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; 1 John 4:19; Romans 15:1"> When, therefore, they dined, Jesus says to Simon Peter, “Simon, &#91;son&#93; of Jonas, do you love Me more than these?” He says to Him, “Yes, Lord; You have known that I cherish You”; He says to him, “Feed My lambs.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:2; 1 Peter 2:25"> He says to him again, a second time, “Simon, &#91;son&#93; of Jonas, do you love Me?” He says to Him, “Yes, Lord; You have known that I cherish You”; He says to him, “Tend My sheep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 13:38; John 16:30; Mark 14:72"> He says to him the third time, “Simon, &#91;son&#93; of Jonas, do you cherish Me?” Peter was grieved that He said to him the third time, “Do you cherish Me?” And he said to Him, “Lord, You have known all things; You know that I cherish You.” Jesus says to him, “Feed My sheep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 13:36; Acts 12:3–4; Acts 21:11"> truly, truly, I say to you, when you were younger, you were girding yourself and were walking to where you willed, but when you may be old, you will stretch forth your hands, and another will gird you, and will carry &#91;you&#93; to where you do not will”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Peter 1:14; John 21:22; Matthew 10:38"> and this He said, signifying by what death he will glorify God; and having said this, He says to him, “Follow Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 21:7; John 20:2; John 21:24"> And having turned, Peter sees the disciple whom Jesus was loving following (who also reclined in the dinner on His breast and said, “Lord, who is he who is delivering You up?”)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 13:23–24; Acts 1:6–7; Matthew 24:3–4"> Having seen this one, Peter says to Jesus, “Lord, and what of this one?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 2:25; Revelation 3:11; Mark 9:1"> Jesus says to him, “If I will him to remain until I come, what &#91;is that&#93; to you? Follow Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:29; Job 33:13; Daniel 4:35"> This word, therefore, went forth to the brothers that this disciple does not die, yet Jesus did not say to him that he does not die, but, “If I will him to remain until I come, what &#91;is that&#93; to you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 19:35; John 15:27; 1 John 5:6"> This is the disciple who is testifying concerning these things, and he wrote these things, and we have known that his testimony is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; John 20:30–31; Psalms 71:15"> And there are also many other things—as many as Jesus did—which, if they may be written one by one, I think the world itself does not even have place for the scrolls written. Amen.</span>
