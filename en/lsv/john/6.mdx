<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 6:31–44; John 6:23; Luke 5:1"> After these things Jesus went away beyond the Sea of Galilee (of Tiberias),</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:24–25; Matthew 12:15; Matthew 15:30–31"> and there was following Him a great multitude, because they were seeing His signs that He was doing on the ailing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 6:15; Luke 9:28; Luke 6:12–13"> and Jesus went up to the mountain, and He was sitting with His disciples there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 2:13; John 11:55; John 5:1"> and the Passover was near, the celebration of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 15:33; Luke 9:12–13; Matthew 14:14–15"> Jesus then having lifted up &#91;His&#93; eyes and having seen that a great multitude comes to Him, says to Philip, “From where will we buy loaves, that these may eat?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 22:1; Deuteronomy 13:3; 2 Chronicles 32:31"> And this He said, trying him, for He Himself had known what He was about to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 11:21–22; Mark 6:37; John 12:5"> Philip answered Him, “Two hundred denarii worth of loaves are not sufficient to them, that each of them may receive some little”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:40–44; Matthew 4:18"> one of His disciples—Andrew, the brother of Simon Peter—says to Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; Matthew 14:17; Mark 6:38"> “There is one little boy here who has five barley loaves and two fishes, but these—what are they to so many?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 9:14–16; Matthew 15:35–36; Mark 6:39–41"> And Jesus said, “Make the men to sit down”; and there was much grass in the place, the men then sat down, in number, as it were, five thousand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 6:23; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Matthew 15:36"> and Jesus took the loaves, and having given thanks He distributed &#91;them&#93; to the disciples, and the disciples to those reclining, in like manner, also of the little fishes as much as they wished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 1:53; Nehemiah 9:25; Nehemiah 8:10"> And when they were filled, He says to His disciples, “Gather together the broken pieces that are left over, that nothing may be lost”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; 2 Kings 4:2–7; 2 Corinthians 9:8–9"> they gathered together, therefore, and filled twelve hand-baskets with broken pieces, from the five barley loaves that were over to those having eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–18; Matthew 21:11; Acts 7:37"> The men, then, having seen the sign that Jesus did, said, “This is truly the Prophet who is coming into the world”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:36; John 6:15–21; John 7:3–4"> Jesus, therefore, having known that they are about to come, and to seize Him by force that they may make Him king, retired again to the mountain Himself alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And when evening came, His disciples went down to the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 6:45; John 6:24–25; John 2:12"> and having entered into the boat, they were going over the sea to Capernaum, and darkness had already come, and Jesus had not come to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 107:25; Psalms 135:7; Matthew 14:24"> the sea also—a great wind blowing—was being raised,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 6:47–49; Matthew 14:25–26; Job 9:8"> having pushed onward, therefore, about twenty-five or thirty stadia, they behold Jesus walking on the sea, and coming near to the boat, and they were afraid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 14:27–31; Isaiah 43:1–2; Isaiah 44:8"> and He says to them, “I AM; do not be afraid”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 6:51; Matthew 14:32–33; Revelation 3:20"> they were willing then to receive Him into the boat, and immediately the boat came to the land to which they were going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 6:45; Matthew 14:22; John 6:2"> On the next day, the multitude that was standing on the other side of the sea, having seen that there was no other little boat there except one—that into which His disciples entered—and that Jesus did not go in with His disciples into the little boat, but His disciples went away alone</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:11–12"> (and other little boats came from Tiberias, near the place where they ate the bread, the LORD having given thanks),</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:17; Luke 8:40; Mark 6:53"> when therefore the multitude saw that Jesus is not there, nor His disciples, they also entered into the boats themselves, and came to Capernaum seeking Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 1:38–39; Matthew 23:7"> and having found Him on the other side of the sea, they said to Him, “Rabbi, when have You come here?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 4:3–4; Philippians 3:19; Philippians 2:21"> Jesus answered them and said, “Truly, truly, I say to you, you seek Me, not because you saw signs, but because you ate of the loaves, and were satisfied;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 3:2; Isaiah 55:2; John 6:54"> do not work for the food that is perishing, but for the food that is remaining to continuous life, which the Son of Man will give to you, for the Father sealed Him—&#91;even&#93; God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 16:30; Acts 9:6; Matthew 19:16"> Therefore they said to Him, “What may we do that we may work the works of God?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 John 3:23; Acts 16:31; John 3:36"> Jesus answered and said to them, “This is the work of God, that you may believe in Him whom He sent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; John 10:38; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> Therefore they said to Him, “What sign, then, do You do, that we may see and may believe You? What do You work?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:15; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:24–25"> Our fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, according as it is having been written: He gave them bread out of Heaven to eat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 15:1; John 6:58; Exodus 16:4"> Jesus, therefore, said to them, “Truly, truly, I say to you, Moses did not give you the bread out of Heaven, but My Father gives you the true bread out of Heaven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 8:42; John 17:8; John 3:13"> for the bread of God is Him coming down out of Heaven, and giving life to the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 4:15; John 6:26; Psalms 4:6"> Therefore they said to Him, “Lord, always give us this bread.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 6:48–58; Revelation 22:17; John 6:41"> And Jesus said to them, “I AM the bread of life; he who is coming to Me may not hunger, and he who is believing in Me may not thirst—at any time;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8–9; Luke 16:31; John 12:37"> but I said to you that you also have seen Me, and you do not believe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; John 6:39; John 17:24"> all that the Father gives to Me will come to Me; and him who is coming to Me, I will never cast outside,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 5:30; John 4:34; John 3:31"> because I have come down out of Heaven, not that I may do My will, but the will of Him who sent Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 17:12; John 6:40; John 18:9"> And this is the will of the Father who sent Me, that all that He has given to Me, I may lose none of it, but may raise it up in the last day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 6:54; John 5:24; Romans 6:23"> and this is the will of Him who sent Me, that everyone who is beholding the Son, and is believing in Him, may have continuous life, and I will raise him up in the last day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 6:51–52; John 6:48; John 6:33"> The Jews, therefore, were murmuring at Him, because He said, “I AM the bread that came down out of Heaven”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Mark 6:3; Matthew 13:55–56"> and they said, “Is this not Jesus, the Son of Joseph, whose father and mother we have known? How then does this One say, I have come down out of Heaven?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; John 6:64; Mark 9:33"> Jesus answered, therefore, and said to them, “Do not murmur with one another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 6:65; John 6:39–40; Matthew 11:25–27"> no one is able to come to Me if the Father who sent Me may not draw him, and I will raise him up in the last day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Isaiah 54:13; Jeremiah 31:33–34; Hebrews 10:16"> it is having been written in the Prophets: And they will all be taught of God; everyone, therefore, who heard from the Father, and learned, comes to Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 1:18; John 7:29; Matthew 11:27"> not that anyone has seen the Father, except He who is from God, He has seen the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:36; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 5:9–10"> Truly, truly, I say to you, he who is believing in Me has continuous life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 6:51; 1 Corinthians 11:24–25; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> I AM the bread of life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3–5; Jude 1:5; Zechariah 1:5"> your fathers ate the manna in the wilderness and they died;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 6:33; John 6:51; Romans 8:10"> this is the bread that is coming down out of Heaven, that anyone may eat of it, and not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 22:19; John 6:33; Hebrews 10:5–12"> I AM the living bread that came down out of Heaven; if anyone may eat of this bread he will live—throughout the age; and the bread also that I will give is My flesh, that I will give for the life of the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 6:41"> The Jews, therefore, were striving with one another, saying, “How is this One able to give us &#91;His&#93; flesh to eat?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 John 5:12; Matthew 26:26–28; John 6:55"> Jesus, therefore, said to them, “Truly, truly, I say to you, if you may not eat the flesh of the Son of Man, and may not drink His blood, you have no life in yourselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 6:39–40; John 6:27; Philippians 3:7–10"> he who is eating My flesh, and is drinking My blood, has continuous life, and I will raise him up in the last day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 15:1; John 8:31; John 6:32"> for My flesh is truly food, and My blood is truly drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="1 John 3:24; John 15:4–5; John 14:20"> he who is eating My flesh, and is drinking My blood, remains in Me, and I in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; John 5:26; John 17:21"> According as the living Father sent Me, and I live because of the Father, he also who is eating Me, even that one will live because of Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 3:36; John 6:47–51; John 6:41"> this is the bread that came down out of Heaven; not as your fathers ate the manna, and died; he who is eating this bread will live—throughout the age.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 6:24; Proverbs 8:1–3; Luke 4:31"> He said these things in a synagogue, teaching in Capernaum;</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="John 6:66; John 8:43; John 8:31"> many, therefore, of His disciples having heard, said, “This word is hard; who is able to hear it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="John 2:24–25; John 6:64; John 21:17"> And Jesus having known in Himself that His disciples are murmuring about this, said to them, “Does this stumble you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 16:19; John 3:13; John 17:4–5"> If then you may behold the Son of Man going up where He was before?</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Galatians 5:25; Hebrews 4:12; Psalms 119:93"> It is the Spirit that is giving life; the flesh does not profit anything; the sayings that I speak to you are spirit, and they are life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="John 10:26; 2 Timothy 2:19; Hebrews 4:13"> but there are certain of you who do not believe”; for Jesus had known from the beginning who they are who are not believing, and who is he who will deliver Him up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="John 6:44–45; John 6:37; John 3:27"> and He said, “Because of this I have said to you, No one is able to come to Me if it may not have been given him from My Father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Luke 9:62; 1 John 2:19; Hebrews 10:38"> From this &#91;time&#93; many of His disciples went away backward, and were no longer walking with Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Ruth 1:11–18; John 6:70; Luke 14:25–33"> Jesus, therefore, said to the Twelve, “Do you also wish to go away?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Psalms 73:25; John 5:24; 1 John 5:11–13"> Simon Peter, therefore, answered Him, “Lord, to whom will we go? You have sayings of continuous life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="John 11:27; Luke 9:20; Mark 8:29"> and we have believed, and we have known, that You are the Christ, the Son of the living God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="John 13:27; John 13:2; John 17:12"> Jesus answered them, “Did I not choose you—the Twelve? And of you—one is a devil.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Matthew 26:14–16; Jude 1:4; Acts 1:16–20"> And He spoke of Judas, &#91;son&#93; of Simon Iscariot, for he was about to deliver Him up, being one of the Twelve.</span>
