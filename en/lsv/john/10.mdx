<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; John 10:8–10; Romans 16:18"> “Truly, truly, I say to you, he who is not entering through the door to the fold of the sheep, but is going up from another side, that one is a thief and a robber;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 10:14; Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:4"> and he who is entering through the door is shepherd of the sheep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:11–16; 2 Timothy 2:19; John 10:4"> the doorkeeper opens to this one, and the sheep hear his voice, and his own sheep he calls by name, and leads them forth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:30; John 10:16; John 12:26"> and when he may put forth his own sheep, he goes on before them, and the sheep follow him, because they have known his voice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 4:5–6; Colossians 2:6–10; Mark 4:24"> and they will not follow a stranger, but will flee from him, because they have not known the voice of strangers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Psalms 82:5; Proverbs 28:5"> Jesus spoke this allegory to them, and they did not know what the things were that He was speaking to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 14:6; Ephesians 2:18; John 10:1"> Jesus therefore said again to them, “Truly, truly, I say to you, I AM the door of the sheep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Jeremiah 23:1; John 10:1"> all, as many as came before Me, are thieves and robbers, but the sheep did not hear them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 14:6; Ephesians 2:18; John 10:7"> I AM the door, if anyone may come in through Me, he will be saved, and he will come in, and go out, and find pasture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 19:10; John 6:51; John 12:47"> The thief does not come, except that he may steal, and kill, and destroy; I came that they may have life, and may have &#91;it&#93; abundantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:14–15; Psalms 23:1; John 15:13"> I AM the good shepherd; the good shepherd lays His life down for the sheep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 20:29; Zechariah 11:16–17; Ezekiel 34:2–6"> and the hired worker, and not being a shepherd, whose own the sheep are not, beholds the wolf coming, and leaves the sheep, and flees; and the wolf snatches them, and scatters the sheep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:20; John 12:6; Acts 18:17"> and the hired worker flees because he is a hired worker, and is not caring for the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 10:27; John 10:11; 2 Timothy 2:19"> I AM the good shepherd, and I know My &#91;sheep&#93;, and am known by Mine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 1:4; John 10:11; Isaiah 53:10"> according as the Father knows Me, and I know the Father, and My life I lay down for the sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 56:8; John 11:52; 1 Peter 2:25"> and other sheep I have that are not of this fold, these also it is necessary for Me to bring, and My voice they will hear, and there will become one flock—one shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 10:18; John 10:11; Hebrews 2:9"> Because of this the Father loves Me, because I lay down My life, that again I may take it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:9; Titus 2:14; John 2:19–21"> no one takes it from Me, but I lay it down of Myself; authority I have to lay it down, and authority I have again to take it; this command I received from My Father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:16; 1 Corinthians 3:3; John 7:40–43"> Therefore, again, there came a division among the Jews, because of these words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:20; Mark 3:21; John 8:47–48"> and many of them said, “He has a demon, and is mad, why do you hear Him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 4:11; Isaiah 35:5–6; Psalms 94:9"> Others said, “These sayings are not those of a demoniac; is a demon able to open blind men’s eyes?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the Dedication in Jerusalem came, and it was winter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 5:12; Acts 3:11"> and Jesus was walking in the temple, in the porch of Solomon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 22:67–70; John 1:19; John 8:25"> the Jews, therefore, came around Him and said to Him, “Until when do You hold our soul in suspense? If You are the Christ, tell us freely.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 10:38; John 8:58; John 14:11"> Jesus answered them, “I told you, and you do not believe; the works that I do in the Name of My Father, these testify concerning Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:47; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; John 12:37–40"> but you do not believe, for you are not of My sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 10:16; John 10:14; Revelation 3:20"> according as I said to you: My sheep hear My voice, and I know them, and they follow Me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:39–40; John 6:37; John 3:15–16"> and I give continuous life to them, and they will not perish—throughout the age, and no one will snatch them out of My hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:37; John 17:2; John 17:9"> My Father, who has given to Me, is greater than all, and no one is able to snatch out of the hand of My Father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:1–2; John 14:9; John 17:21–23"> I and the Father are one.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 5:18; John 8:59; 1 Samuel 30:6"> Therefore, again, the Jews took up stones that they may stone Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 John 3:12; John 5:36; Psalms 35:12"> Jesus answered them, “I showed you many good works from My Father; because of which work of them do you stone Me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 5:18; Leviticus 24:16; John 10:30"> The Jews answered Him, saying, “We do not stone You for a good work, but for slander, and because You, being a man, make Yourself God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 82:6–7; Exodus 7:1; Psalms 82:1"> Jesus answered them, “Is it not having been written in your law: I said, you are gods?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Romans 13:1; Luke 16:17"> If He called them gods to whom the word of God came (and the Writing is not able to be broken),</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 5:17–18; John 3:17; Jeremiah 1:5"> of Him whom the Father sanctified and sent into the world, do you say—You slander, because I said, I am &#91;the&#93; Son of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 15:24; John 10:25; John 12:37–40"> If I do not do the works of My Father, do not believe Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 14:20; John 10:25; John 10:30"> and if I do, even if you may not believe Me, believe the works, that you may know and may believe that the Father &#91;is&#93; in Me, and I in Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 7:30; John 8:59; John 7:44"> Therefore they were seeking again to seize Him, and He went forth out of their hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 1:28; John 11:54; John 7:1"> and went away again to the other side of the Jordan, to the place where John was at first immersing, and remained there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 1:33–34; John 1:29–30; Mark 1:37"> and many came to Him and said, “John, indeed, did no sign, and all things, as many as John said about this One were true”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 4:41; John 2:23; John 7:31"> and many believed in Him there.</span>
