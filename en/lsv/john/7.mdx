<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 5:16–18; John 4:3; Matthew 21:38"> And Jesus was walking after these things in Galilee, for He did not wish to walk in Judea, because the Jews were seeking to kill Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:16–19; Deuteronomy 16:13–16; Exodus 23:16–17"> and the celebration of the Jews was near—that of Shelters—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; John 7:5; Luke 8:19"> His brothers, therefore, said to Him, “Depart from here, and go away to Judea, that Your disciples may also behold Your works that You do;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:20; Proverbs 18:1–2; Matthew 23:5"> for no one does anything in secret, and himself seeks to be in public; if you do these things—reveal Yourself to the world”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 3:21; John 1:11–13; Micah 7:5–6"> for not even His brothers were believing in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 7:8; John 2:4; John 7:30"> Jesus, therefore, says to them, “My time is not yet present, but your time is always ready;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 3:19; Galatians 4:16"> the world is not able to hate you, but it hates Me, because I testify concerning it that its works are evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:6; John 8:20; John 7:30"> You—go up to this celebration; I do not yet go up to this celebration, because My time has not yet been fulfilled”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and saying these things to them, He remained in Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Matthew 10:16; Isaiah 42:2–3"> And when His brothers went up, then also He Himself went up to the celebration, not openly, but as in secret;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:56"> the Jews, therefore, were seeking Him in the celebration, and said, “Where is that One?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 7:40–43; John 9:16; John 7:32"> And there was much murmuring about Him among the multitudes, some indeed said, “He is good”; and others said, “No, but He leads the multitude astray”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 9:22; John 19:38; John 20:19"> no one, however, was speaking freely about Him, through fear of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:28; Luke 19:47; John 7:37"> And it being now the middle of the celebration, Jesus went up to the temple, and He was teaching,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54"> and the Jews were wondering, saying, “How has this One known letters—having not learned?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:10; John 14:24; John 17:8"> Jesus answered them and said, “My teaching is not Mine, but His who sent Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:31–32; John 8:43; Jeremiah 31:33–34"> if anyone may will to do His will, he will know concerning the teaching, whether it is of God, or—&#91;if&#93; I speak from Myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 5:41; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33; John 13:31–32"> He who is speaking from himself seeks his own glory, but he who is seeking the glory of him who sent him, this one is true, and unrighteousness is not in him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 1:17; John 11:53; Matthew 12:14"> has not Moses given you the Law? And none of you does the Law; why do you seek to kill Me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:20; John 8:48; John 8:52"> The multitude answered and said, “You have a demon, who seeks to kill You?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:23; John 5:2–11"> Jesus answered and said to them, “I did one work, and you all wonder,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Genesis 17:10–14; Romans 4:9–11"> because of this, Moses has given you the circumcision—not that it is of Moses, but of the fathers—and on a Sabbath you circumcise a man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:5; John 5:14–16; Matthew 12:2"> if a man receives circumcision on a Sabbath that the Law of Moses may not be broken, are you angry with Me that I made a man all whole on a Sabbath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–4; James 2:9; John 8:15"> Do not judge according to appearance, but the righteous judgment judge.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 7:10–11"> Certain, therefore, of the Jerusalemites said, “Is this not He whom they are seeking to kill?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7–8; John 7:48; John 12:42"> And behold, He speaks freely, and they say nothing to Him; did the rulers truly know that this is the Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:41–42; John 6:42"> But this One—we have known where He is from; and the Christ, when He comes, no one knows where He is from.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:42; John 8:19; John 8:26"> Jesus cried, therefore, in the temple, teaching and saying, “You have both known Me, and you have known from where I am; and I have not come of Myself, but He who sent Me is true, whom you have not known;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:55; Matthew 11:27; John 17:18"> and I have known Him, because I am from Him, and He sent Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 8:20; John 10:39; John 7:6"> They were seeking, therefore, to seize Him, and no one laid the hand on Him, because His hour had not yet come,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:42; Matthew 12:23; John 10:41–42"> and many out of the multitude believed in Him and said, “The Christ—when He may come—will He do more signs than these that this One did?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 18:3; Luke 22:52–53; John 11:47–48"> The Pharisees heard the multitude murmuring these things concerning Him, and the Pharisees and the chief priests sent officers that they may take Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 16:5; John 13:33; Mark 16:19"> Jesus, therefore, said to them, “Yet a short time I am with you, and I go away to Him who sent Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; John 13:33–36; John 14:6"> you will seek Me, and you will not find; and where I am, you are not able to come.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="James 1:1; Isaiah 11:12; Zephaniah 3:10"> The Jews, therefore, said among themselves, “To where is this One about to go that we will not find Him? Is He about to go to the dispersion of the Greeks, and to teach the Greeks?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 3:9; John 7:34; John 12:34"> What is this word that He said, You will seek Me, and you will not find? And, Where I am, you are not able to come?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 55:1; John 4:14; John 6:35"> And in the last, the great day of the celebration, Jesus stood and cried, saying, “If anyone thirsts, let him come to Me and drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 44:3; Isaiah 58:11"> he who is believing in Me, according as the Writing said, Rivers of living water will flow out of his belly”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 2:17; Joel 2:28; John 14:16–17"> and this He said of the Spirit, which those believing in Him were about to receive; for not yet was the Holy Spirit, because Jesus was not yet glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; John 1:21; John 6:14"> Many, therefore, out of the multitude, having heard the word, said, “This is truly the Prophet”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:52; John 1:46; John 1:49"> others said, “This is the Christ”; and others said, “Why, does the Christ come out of Galilee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:5; Luke 2:4"> Did the Writing not say that out of the seed of David, and from Beth-Lehem—the village where David was—the Christ comes?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 7:12"> A division, therefore, arose among the multitude because of Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 7:30; Acts 23:11; John 8:20"> And certain of them were willing to seize Him, but no one laid hands on Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 7:32; Acts 5:21–27"> the officers came, therefore, to the chief priests and Pharisees, and they said to them, “Why did you not bring Him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 7:28–29; Luke 4:22; John 7:26"> The officers answered, “Never so spoke man—as this Man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 7:12; 2 Corinthians 6:8; 2 Kings 18:32"> The Pharisees, therefore, answered them, “Have you also been led astray?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 12:42; 1 Corinthians 1:20; 1 Corinthians 2:8"> Did anyone out of the rulers believe in Him? Or out of the Pharisees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20–21; John 9:40; John 9:34"> But this multitude, that is not knowing the Law, is accursed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:39; John 3:1–2"> Nicodemus says to them—he who came by night to Him—being one of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Deuteronomy 1:17; Deuteronomy 19:15–19"> “Does our law judge the Man, if it may not hear from Him first, and know what He does?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 7:41; John 1:46; Isaiah 9:1–2"> They answered and said to him, “Are you also out of Galilee? Search and see that a prophet has not risen out of Galilee”; &#91;&#91;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Job 5:12–13; Psalms 76:10"> and each one went on to his house, but Jesus went on to the Mount of Olives.</span>
