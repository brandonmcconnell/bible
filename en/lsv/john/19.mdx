<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 20:19"> Then, therefore, Pilate took Jesus and scourged &#91;Him&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Luke 23:11; Matthew 27:27–31"> and the soldiers having plaited a garland of thorns, placed &#91;it&#93; on His head, and they cast a purple garment around Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 27:29; Matthew 26:49; Luke 1:28"> and said, “Hail! The King of the Jews”; and they were giving Him slaps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; John 19:6; Luke 23:4"> Pilate, therefore, again went forth outside and says to them, “Behold, I bring Him to you outside, that you may know that I find no fault in Him”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:2; Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 7:14"> Jesus, therefore, came forth outside, bearing the thorny garland and the purple garment; and he says to them, “Behold, the Man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:31; John 19:4; Acts 2:23"> When, therefore, the chief priests and the officers saw Him, they cried out, saying, “Crucify! Crucify!” Pilate says to them, “Take Him yourselves and crucify, for I find no fault in Him”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 24:16; John 5:18; Matthew 26:63–66"> the Jews answered him, “We have a law, and according to our law He ought to die, for He made Himself Son of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 19:13; Acts 14:11–19"> When, therefore, Pilate heard this word, he was more afraid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 18:33; Psalms 38:13–15; Philippians 1:28"> and entered again into the Praetorium and says to Jesus, “Where are You from?” And Jesus gave him no answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 5:19; John 18:39; Daniel 3:14–15"> Pilate, therefore, says to Him, “Do You not speak to me? Have You not known that I have authority to crucify You, and I have authority to release You?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 13:1; John 3:27; Acts 2:23"> Jesus answered, “You would have no authority against Me if it were not having been given you from above; because of this, he who is delivering Me up to you has greater sin.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 18:33–36; Mark 6:16–26"> From this &#91;time&#93; Pilate was seeking to release Him, and the Jews were crying out, saying, “If you may release this One, you are not a friend of Caesar; everyone making himself a king speaks against Caesar.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 82:5–7; Matthew 27:19; Amos 4:7"> Pilate, therefore, having heard this word, brought Jesus outside—and he sat down on the judgment seat—to a place called, “Pavement,” and in Hebrew, Gabbatha;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:5; Mark 15:25"> and it was the Preparation of the Passover, and as it were the sixth hour, and he says to the Jews, “Behold, your King!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 23:18; Acts 21:36; John 19:6"> And they cried out, “Take away! Take away! Crucify Him!” Pilate says to them, “Will I crucify your King?” The chief priests answered, “We have no king except Caesar.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 23:24–25; Matthew 27:26–31; Mark 15:15–20"> Then, therefore, he delivered Him up to them, that He may be crucified, and they took Jesus and led &#91;Him&#93; away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:33; Luke 14:27; Luke 23:26"> and carrying His cross, He went forth to the &#91;place&#93; called “Place of &#91;the&#93; Skull,” which is called in Hebrew, Golgotha—</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Mark 15:24–27; Galatians 3:13"> where they crucified Him, and with Him two others, on this side and on that side, but Jesus in the middle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 27:37; Luke 23:38; Mark 15:26"> And Pilate also wrote a title, and put &#91;it&#93; on the cross, and it was written: “JESUS THE NAZARENE, THE KING OF THE JEWS”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 26:14; Revelation 16:16; Acts 22:2"> therefore many of the Jews read this title, because the place was near to the city where Jesus was crucified, and it was having been written in Hebrew, in Greek, in Latin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:19"> The chief priests of the Jews therefore said to Pilate, “Do not write, The King of the Jews, but that this One said, I am King of the Jews”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 76:10; Psalms 65:7; Proverbs 8:29"> Pilate answered, “What I have written, I have written.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 23:34; Mark 15:24; Matthew 27:35"> The soldiers, therefore, when they crucified Jesus, took His garments, and made four parts, to each soldier a part, also the coat, and the coat was seamless, from the top woven throughout;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 22:18; John 19:28; John 19:36–37"> they said, therefore, to one another, “We may not tear it, but cast a lot for it, whose it will be”; that the Writing might be fulfilled, that is saying, “They divided My garments to themselves, and they cast a lot for My clothing”; the soldiers, therefore, indeed, did these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:55–56; John 20:1"> And there stood by the cross of Jesus His mother, and His mother’s sister, Mary of Cleopas, and Mary the Magdalene;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 21:24; John 20:2; John 2:4"> Jesus, therefore, having seen &#91;His&#93; mother, and the disciple standing by, whom He was loving, He says to His mother, “Woman, behold, your son”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 12:48–50; John 16:32; 1 Timothy 5:2–4"> afterward He says to the disciple, “Behold, your mother”; and from that hour the disciple took her to his own &#91;home&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Psalms 22:15; John 19:30"> After this, Jesus knowing that all things have now been accomplished, that the Writing may be fulfilled, says, “I thirst”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 19:18; Luke 23:36; Mark 15:36"> a vessel, therefore, was placed full of vinegar, and having filled a sponge with vinegar, and having put &#91;it&#93; around a hyssop stalk, they put &#91;it&#93; to His mouth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 17:4; Philippians 2:8; Colossians 2:14–17"> when, therefore, Jesus received the vinegar, He said, “It has been accomplished.” And having bowed the head, gave up the spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:42; Mark 15:42"> The Jews, therefore, that the bodies might not remain on the cross on the Sabbath, since it was the Preparation (for that Sabbath day was a great one), asked of Pilate that their legs may be broken, and they &#91;are&#93; taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43"> The soldiers, therefore, came, and they indeed broke the legs of the first and of the other who was crucified with Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and having come to Jesus, when they saw Him already having been dead, they did not break His legs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 John 5:6; Zechariah 12:10; Zechariah 13:1"> but one of the soldiers pierced His side with a spear, and immediately there came forth blood and water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 21:24; Hebrews 2:3–4; 1 John 1:1–3"> and he who has seen has testified, and his testimony is true, and that one has known that he speaks true things, that you also may believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 34:20; Numbers 9:12; Exodus 12:46"> For these things came to pass, that the Writing may be fulfilled, “A bone of Him will not be broken”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Revelation 1:7; Psalms 22:16–17"> and again another Writing says, “They will look to Him whom they pierced.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:42–47; Matthew 27:57–61; Proverbs 29:25"> And after these things, Joseph of Arimathea—being a disciple of Jesus, but concealed, through the fear of the Jews—asked of Pilate, that he may take away the body of Jesus, and Pilate gave leave; he came, therefore, and took away the body of Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 7:50–52; Song of Songs 4:14; John 3:1–21"> and Nicodemus also came—who came to Jesus by night at the first—carrying a mixture of myrrh and aloes, as it were, one hundred pounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:44; John 20:5–7; Acts 5:6"> Therefore they took the body of Jesus, and bound it with linen clothes with the spices, according as it was the custom of the Jews to prepare for burial;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 23:53; Matthew 27:60; John 20:15"> and there was a garden in the place where He was crucified, and a new tomb in the garden, in which no one was yet laid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 19:31; John 19:14; Isaiah 53:9"> therefore, because the tomb was near, there they laid Jesus because of the Preparation of the Jews.</span>
