<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 4:2; Romans 12:12; 1 Thessalonians 5:17"> And He also spoke an allegory to them, that it is always necessary to pray and not to faint,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; Psalms 8:1–4; Luke 18:4"> saying, “A certain judge was in a certain city—he is not fearing God, and he is not regarding man—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Jeremiah 5:28; Deuteronomy 27:19"> and a widow was in that city, and she was coming to him, saying, Do me justice on my opponent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12–13; Luke 16:3; Luke 12:17"> and he would not for a time, but after these things he said in himself, Even if I do not fear God, and do not regard man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 11:8; Luke 18:39; Judges 16:16"> yet because this widow gives me trouble, I will do her justice, lest, continuously coming, she may bruise me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 7:13"> And the LORD said, “Hear what the unrighteous judge says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35–37; 2 Peter 3:9; Luke 11:13"> and will God not execute justice to His chosen ones, who are crying to Him day and night—bearing long in regard to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:23–26; 2 Peter 3:8–9; Matthew 24:9–13"> I say to you that He will execute justice to them quickly; but the Son of Man having come, will He find faith on the earth?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Proverbs 30:12; Isaiah 65:5"> And He also spoke to some who have been trusting in themselves that they were righteous, and have been despising the rest, this allegory:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 21:31–32; 1 Kings 8:30; 2 Kings 20:5"> “Two men went up to the temple to pray, one a Pharisee, and the other a tax collector;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 6:5; Isaiah 65:5; Revelation 3:17"> the Pharisee having stood by himself, thus prayed: God, I thank You that I am not as the rest of men, rapacious, unrighteous, adulterers, or even as this tax collector;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Matthew 23:23–24; Matthew 9:14"> I fast twice in the week, I give tithes of all things—as many as I possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; 1 John 1:8–10; 1 Timothy 1:15"> And the tax collector, having stood far off, would not even lift up the eyes to the sky, but was striking on his breast, saying, God be propitious to me—the sinner!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 14:11; Luke 16:15; James 4:10"> I say to you, this one went down declared righteous, to his house, rather than that one: for everyone who is exalting himself will be humbled, and he who is humbling himself will be exalted.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 10:13–16; Matthew 19:13–15; 1 Samuel 1:24"> And they were also bringing the babies near, that He may touch them, and the disciples having seen, rebuked them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 18:3–4; 1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 7:14"> and Jesus having called them near, said, “Permit the little children to come to Me, and do not forbid them, for of such is the Kingdom of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 18:3; Mark 10:15; Psalms 131:1–2"> truly I say to you, whoever may not receive the Kingdom of God as a little child, may not enter into it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 10:17–31; Acts 16:30; Matthew 19:16–30"> And a certain ruler questioned Him, saying, “Good Teacher, what having done—will I inherit continuous life?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 15:14–16; James 1:17; Hebrews 7:26"> And Jesus said to him, “Why do you call Me good? No one &#91;is&#93; good, except one—God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Deuteronomy 5:16–21; Romans 13:9"> you have known the commands: You may not commit adultery, You may not murder, You may not steal, You may not bear false witness, Honor your father and your mother.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 3:6; Romans 10:2–3; Mark 10:20–21"> And he said, “All these I kept from my youth”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 12:33; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; Matthew 19:21"> and having heard these things, Jesus said to him, “Yet one thing to you is lacking: all things—as many as you have—sell, and distribute to the poor, and you will have treasure in Heaven, and come, follow Me”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; 1 John 2:15; Job 31:24–25"> and he, having heard these things, became very sorrowful, for he was exceedingly rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; James 2:5–7; Mark 10:23–27"> And Jesus having seen him become very sorrowful, said, “How hardly will those having riches enter into the Kingdom of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 23:24"> For it is easier for a camel to enter through the eye of a needle, than for a rich man to enter into the Kingdom of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 13:23"> And those who heard, said, “And who is able to be saved?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Matthew 19:26; Luke 1:37"> And He said, “The things impossible with men are possible with God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 10:28; Philippians 3:7; Matthew 19:27"> And Peter said, “Behold, we left all, and followed You”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 10:29–31; Matthew 10:37–39; Luke 14:26–28"> and He said to them, “Truly I say to you that there is not one who left house, or parents, or brothers, or wife, or children, for the sake of the Kingdom of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5–6; Job 42:10; Revelation 2:17"> who may not receive back manifold more in this time, and in the coming age, continuous life.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 20:17–19; Isaiah 53:1–12; Psalms 22:1–31"> And having taken the Twelve aside, He said to them, “Behold, we go up to Jerusalem, and all things will be accomplished that have been written through the prophets to the Son of Man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 23:11; Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3"> for He will be delivered up to the nations, and will be mocked, and insulted, and spit on,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:3–4; Luke 24:7; Matthew 27:63"> and having scourged they will put Him to death, and on the third day He will rise again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32; Luke 24:25"> And they understood none of these things, and this saying was hid from them, and they were not knowing the things said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:29–34; Mark 10:46–52; Luke 16:20–21"> And it came to pass, in His coming near to Jericho, a certain blind man was sitting beside the way begging,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 21:10–11; Luke 15:26"> and having heard a multitude going by, he was inquiring what this may be,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; John 19:19; John 12:35–36"> and they brought him word that Jesus the Nazarene passes by,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Luke 18:39; Matthew 15:22"> and he cried out, saying, “Jesus, Son of David, deal kindly with me”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 32:26–28; Psalms 141:1; Luke 18:15"> and those going before were rebuking him, that he might be silent, but he was crying out much more, “Son of David, deal kindly with me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 20:31–34; Mark 10:48–52"> And Jesus having stood, commanded him to be brought to Him, and he having come near, He questioned him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Kings 3:5–15; Matthew 20:21–22; Philippians 4:6"> saying, “What do you will I will do to you?” And he said, “Lord, that I may receive sight.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 8:3; Luke 8:48; Luke 17:19"> And Jesus said to him, “Receive your sight; your faith has saved you”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 146:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:10–12; Isaiah 43:21"> and instantly he received sight, and was following Him, glorifying God; and all the people, having seen, gave praise to God.</span>
