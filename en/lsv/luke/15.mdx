<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:15; Matthew 9:10–13; Luke 5:29–32"> And all the tax collectors and sinners were coming near to Him, to hear Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:11; Luke 19:7; Luke 7:39"> and the Pharisees and the scribes were murmuring, saying, “This One receives sinners, and eats with them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And He spoke to them this allegory, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Ezekiel 34:11–12; Matthew 18:12–14"> “What man of you having one hundred sheep, and having lost one out of them, does not leave behind the ninety-nine in the wilderness, and go on after the lost one, until he may find it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Luke 19:9; Isaiah 46:3–4"> And having found, he lays &#91;it&#93; on his shoulders rejoicing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 15:10; Luke 15:24; 1 Peter 2:25"> and having come into the house, he calls together the friends and the neighbors, saying to them, Rejoice with me, because I found my sheep—the lost one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 5:32; Luke 15:10; Luke 15:32"> I say to you that &#91;more&#93; joy will be in Heaven over one sinner converting, rather than over ninety-nine righteous men who have no need of conversion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Ezekiel 34:12; John 10:16"> Or what woman having ten drachmas, if she may lose one drachma, does not light a lamp, and sweep the house, and seek carefully until she may find?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 15:6–7"> And having found, she calls together the female friends and the neighbors, saying, Rejoice with me, for I found the drachma that I lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 15:7; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Ezekiel 33:11"> So I say to you, joy comes before the messengers of God over one sinner converting.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 21:23–31"> And He said, “A certain man had two sons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:16–17; Mark 12:44; Luke 15:30"> and the younger of them said to the father, Father, give me the portion of the substance falling to &#91;me&#93;, and he divided to them the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Jeremiah 2:5; Proverbs 21:17"> And not many days after, having gathered all together, the younger son went abroad to a far country, and there he scattered his substance, living riotously;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11; Amos 8:9–12; Hosea 2:9–14"> and he having spent all, there came a mighty famine on that country, and himself began to be in want;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:3; 2 Timothy 2:25–26; Isaiah 1:5"> and having gone on, he joined himself to one of the citizens of that country, and he sent him into the fields to feed pigs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 6:19–21; Lamentations 4:5; Hosea 12:1"> and he was desirous to fill his belly from the husks that the pigs were eating, and no one was giving to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:28; Ephesians 2:4–5; Luke 15:18–19"> And having come to himself, he said, How many hired workers of my father have a superabundance of bread, and I am perishing here with hunger!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 John 1:8–10; Luke 15:21"> Having risen, I will go on to my father, and will say to him, Father, I sinned—to Heaven, and before you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 42:6; Luke 5:8; Psalms 84:10"> and I am no longer worthy to be called your son; make me as one of your hired workers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 103:10–13; Psalms 86:5; Psalms 86:15"> And having risen, he went to his own father, and he being yet far distant, his father saw him, and was moved with compassion, and having ran he fell on his neck and kissed him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Luke 15:18–19; Jeremiah 3:13"> and the son said to him, Father, I sinned—to Heaven, and before you, and I am no longer worthy to be called your son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 41:42; Esther 8:2; Revelation 6:11"> And the father said to his servants, Bring forth the foremost robe, and clothe him, and give a ring for his hand, and sandals for the feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; Matthew 22:2–14; Psalms 63:5"> and having brought the fatted calf, kill &#91;it&#93;, and having eaten, we may be merry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 2:5; Ezekiel 34:16; Luke 19:10"> because this son of mine was dead, and lived again, and he was lost, and was found; and they began to be merry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 150:4; Jeremiah 31:4; Luke 15:11–12"> And his elder son was in a field, and as, coming, he drew near to the house, he heard music and dancing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and having called near one of the young men, he was inquiring what these things might be,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 9:17; Philemon 1:16; Acts 22:13"> and he said to him, Your brother has arrived, and your father killed the fatted calf, because he received him back in health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jonah 4:9; 1 Samuel 18:8; Jonah 4:1–4"> And he was angry, and would not go in, therefore his father, having come forth, was pleading him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 18:11–12; Zechariah 7:3; Isaiah 65:5"> and he answering said to the father, Behold, so many years I serve you, and never did I transgress your command, and you never gave to me a kid that I might make merry with my friends;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Luke 15:12–13; Luke 15:32"> but when your son—this one who devoured your living with prostitutes—came, you killed to him the fatted calf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Romans 9:4; Romans 11:1"> And he said to him, Child, you are always with me, and all my things are yours;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–10; Luke 15:24; Isaiah 35:10"> but to be merry, and to be glad, it was necessary, because this your brother was dead, and lived again, he was lost, and was found.”</span>
