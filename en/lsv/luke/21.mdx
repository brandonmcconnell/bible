<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 12:41–44; John 8:20; Matthew 27:6"> And having looked up, He saw those who cast their gifts into the treasury—rich men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 12:42"> and He also saw a certain poor widow casting two mites there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 10:34; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3"> and He said, “Truly I say to you that this poor widow cast in more than all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 2:44–45; Luke 15:12; Acts 4:34"> for all these out of their superabundance cast into the gifts to God, but this one out of her want, all the living that she had, cast in.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 2:20; Matthew 24:1–51; Mark 13:1–37"> And &#91;as&#93; some were speaking about the temple, that it has been adorned with good stones and devoted things, He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:2; Mark 13:2; Lamentations 5:18"> “These things that you behold—days will come in which there will not be left a stone on a stone that will not be thrown down.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 1:6–7; Mark 13:14; Mark 13:3–4"> And they questioned Him, saying, “Teacher, when, then, will these things be? And what &#91;is&#93; the sign when these things may be about to happen?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Mark 13:5–6; Ephesians 5:6"> And He said, “See—you may not be led astray, for many will come in My Name, saying, I am &#91;He&#93;, and the time has come near; do not then go on after them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 21:28; Matthew 24:6–8; Mark 13:7–8"> and when you may hear of wars and uprisings, do not be terrified, for it is necessary for these things to happen first, but the end &#91;is&#93; not immediately.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:19–20; Mark 13:8; 2 Chronicles 15:5–6"> Then He said to them, “Nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:29–30; Luke 21:25–27"> also great shakings, and there will be famines and pestilences in every place; also there will be fearful things and great signs from the sky;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12–14; Acts 5:40; Acts 22:30"> and before all these, they will lay on you their hands, and persecute, delivering up to synagogues and prisons, being brought before kings and governors for My Name’s sake;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:12; Philippians 1:28; 2 Thessalonians 1:5"> and it will become to you for a testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 12:11–12; Mark 13:11; Matthew 10:19–20"> Settle, then, to your hearts, not to meditate beforehand to reply,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; Ephesians 6:19; Luke 12:12"> for I will give to you a mouth and wisdom that all your opposers will not be able to refute or resist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 12:53; Revelation 6:9; Micah 7:5–6"> And you will be delivered up also by parents, and brothers, and relatives, and friends, and they will put &#91;some&#93; of you to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:19; Luke 6:22; John 15:21"> and you will be hated by all because of My Name—</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 10:30; 1 Samuel 14:45; Luke 12:7"> and a hair out of your head will not perish;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Matthew 10:22; James 1:3"> in your patience possess your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 19:43; Daniel 9:27; Mark 13:14"> And when you may see Jerusalem surrounded by encampments, then know that her desolation has come near;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:1; Genesis 19:17; Luke 17:31–33"> then those in Judea, let them flee to the mountains; and those in her midst, let them depart out; and those in the countries, do not let them come in to her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 9:7; Isaiah 65:12–16; Isaiah 63:4"> because these are days of vengeance, to fulfill all things that have been written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 13:17; Matthew 24:19; Luke 23:29"> And woe to those with child, and to those giving suck, in those days; for there will be great distress on the land, and wrath on this people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:25; Revelation 11:2; Isaiah 63:18"> and they will fall by the mouth of the sword, and will be led captive to all the nations, and Jerusalem will be trodden down by nations, until the times of nations be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Joel 2:30–31; Revelation 6:12–14"> And there will be signs in sun, and moon, and stars, and on the earth distress of nations with perplexity, sea and wave roaring;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Deuteronomy 28:65–67; 2 Peter 3:10–12"> men fainting at heart from fear, and expectation of the things coming on the world, for the powers of the heavens will be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 24:30; Revelation 1:7; Mark 13:26"> And then they will see the Son of Man coming in a cloud, with power and much glory;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 8:23; Isaiah 60:1–2; Ephesians 4:30"> and these things beginning to happen, bend yourselves back, and lift up your heads, because your redemption draws near.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:32–35; Mark 13:28–30"> And He spoke an allegory to them: “See the fig tree, and all the trees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 12:57"> when they may now cast forth, having seen, of yourselves you know that now the summer is near;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 5:9; Hebrews 10:37; 1 Peter 4:7"> so also you, when you may see these things happening, you know that the Kingdom of God is near;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 13:30; Matthew 24:34; Matthew 23:36"> truly I say to you, this generation may not pass away until all may have come to pass;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Mark 13:31; Matthew 24:35"> the sky and the earth will pass away, but My words may not pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 4:19; Luke 12:45–46; Luke 12:40"> And take heed to yourselves lest your hearts may be weighed down with carousing, and drunkenness, and anxieties of life, and suddenly that day may come on you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; Psalms 11:6; Jeremiah 48:43–44"> for it will come as a snare on all those dwelling on the face of all the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 John 2:28; Matthew 25:13; 1 Peter 5:8"> watch, then, in every season, praying that you may be accounted worthy to escape all these things that are about to come to pass, and to stand before the Son of Man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 22:39; Mark 11:19; Matthew 21:17"> And He was teaching in the temple during the days, and during the nights, going forth, He was lodging at &#91;that&#93; called the Mount of Olives;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 8:1–2"> and all the people were coming early to Him in the temple to hear Him.</span>
