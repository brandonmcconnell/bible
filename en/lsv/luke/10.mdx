<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 6:7–13; Luke 9:52; Numbers 11:24–26"> And after these things, the LORD also appointed seventy others, and sent them by twos before His face, to every city and place to where He Himself was about to come,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:36–38; John 4:35–38; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9"> then He said to them, “The harvest &#91;is&#93; indeed abundant, but the workmen few; implore then the Lord of the harvest, that He may put forth workmen to His harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; Acts 20:29; Ezekiel 2:3–6"> Go away; behold, I send you forth as lambs in the midst of wolves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 22:35; 2 Kings 4:29; Luke 10:4–12"> carry no bag, no leather pouch, nor sandals; and greet no one on the way;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:6; Matthew 10:12–13; Isaiah 57:19"> and into whatever house you enter, first say, Peace to this house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:16; 2 Corinthians 2:15–16; Ephesians 5:6"> and if indeed there may be there the son of peace, your peace will rest on it; and if not so, it will turn back on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:17–18; Matthew 10:10–11; Galatians 6:6"> And remain in that house, eating and drinking the things they have, for worthy &#91;is&#93; the workman of his hire; do not go from house to house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:27; Matthew 10:40; Luke 10:10"> and into whatever city you enter, and they may receive you, eat the things set before you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; John 3:5; Luke 10:11"> and heal the ailing in it, and say to them, The Kingdom of God has come near to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Matthew 10:14; Acts 13:51"> And into whatever city you enter, and they may not receive you, having gone forth to its broad places, say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 10:9; Acts 13:51; Acts 13:26"> And the dust that has cleaved to us from your city, we wipe off against you, but know this, that the Kingdom of God has come near to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 10:15; Matthew 11:24; Lamentations 4:6"> and I say to you that it will be more tolerable for Sodom in that day than for that city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 23:1–18; Joel 3:4–8; Ezekiel 26:1–26"> Woe to you, Chorazin; woe to you, Bethsaida; for if the mighty works that were done in you had been done in Tyre and Sidon, they had converted long ago, sitting in sackcloth and ashes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 15:22–25; Romans 2:1; Luke 12:47–48"> but it will be more tolerable for Tyre and Sidon in the judgment than for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–15; Matthew 4:13; Luke 13:28"> And you, Capernaum, which were exalted to Heaven, you will be brought down to Hades.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 13:20; Matthew 10:40; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> He who is hearing you, hears Me; and he who is putting you away, puts Me away; and he who is putting Me away, puts away Him who sent Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Mark 16:17; Luke 9:1"> And the seventy turned back with joy, saying, “Lord, and the demons are being subjected to us in Your Name”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 12:7–9; Isaiah 14:12; Revelation 9:1"> and He said to them, “I was beholding Satan having fallen as lightning from Heaven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 91:13; Mark 16:18; Romans 16:20"> behold, I give to you the authority to tread on serpents and scorpions, and on all the power of the enemy, and nothing by any means will hurt you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 20:15; Revelation 20:12; Hebrews 12:23"> but do not rejoice in this, that the spirits are subjected to you, but rejoice rather that your names were written in the heavens.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3; Matthew 13:11–16; Matthew 11:25–27"> In that hour Jesus was glad in the Spirit and said, “I confess to You, Father, Lord of Heaven and of earth, that You hid these things from wise men and understanding, and revealed them to babies; yes, Father, because so it became good pleasure before You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 11:27; John 1:18; John 6:44–46"> All things were delivered up to Me by My Father, and no one knows who the Son is, except the Father, and who the Father is, except the Son, and he to whom the Son may resolve to reveal &#91;Him&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:16–17"> And having turned to the disciples, He said, by themselves, “Blessed the eyes that are perceiving what you perceive;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 8:56; Hebrews 11:13; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> for I say to you that many prophets and kings wished to see what you perceive, and did not see, and to hear what you hear, and did not hear.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–39; Luke 18:18; Matthew 19:16–19"> And behold, a certain lawyer stood up, trying Him, and saying, “Teacher, what having done, will I inherit continuous life?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Isaiah 8:20; Galatians 3:21–22"> And He said to him, “In the Law what has been written? How do you read &#91;it&#93;?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 12:30–31; Matthew 22:37–40; Deuteronomy 6:5"> And he answering said, “You will love the LORD your God out of all your heart, and out of all your soul, and out of all your strength, and out of all your understanding, and your neighbor as yourself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:11; Leviticus 18:5; Matthew 19:17"> And He said to him, “You answered correctly; do this, and you will live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Romans 4:2; Leviticus 19:34"> And he, willing to declare himself righteous, said to Jesus, “And who is my neighbor?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 88:4; Luke 18:31; Lamentations 2:12"> And Jesus having taken up &#91;the word&#93;, said, “A certain man was going down from Jerusalem to Jericho, and fell among robbers, and having stripped him and inflicted blows, they went away, leaving &#91;him&#93; half dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 3:16–18; Psalms 69:20; Jeremiah 5:31"> And by a coincidence a certain priest was going down in that way, and having seen him, he passed over on the opposite side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 27:10; Acts 18:17; Psalms 109:25"> and in like manner also, a Levite, having been around the place, having come and seen, passed over on the opposite side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 8:50; Jeremiah 38:7–13; Luke 7:13"> But a certain Samaritan, journeying, came along him, and having seen him, he was moved with compassion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 12:20; Psalms 147:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> and having come near, he bound up his wounds, pouring on oil and wine, and having lifted him up on his own beast, he brought him to an inn, and was careful of him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Luke 14:13; Romans 16:23"> and on the next day, going forth, taking out two denarii, he gave to the innkeeper and said to him, Be careful of him, and whatever you may spend more, I, in my coming again, will give back to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 7:42; Matthew 21:28–31; Matthew 17:25"> Who, then, of these three, seems to you to have become neighbor of him who fell among the robbers?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 3:16–18; Micah 6:8; 1 Peter 2:21"> And he said, “He who did the kindness with him,” then Jesus said to him, “Be going on, and you be doing in like manner.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:19–20; John 11:1–5; John 12:1–3"> And it came to pass, in their going on, that He entered into a certain village, and a certain woman, by name Martha, received Him into her house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 8:34; Luke 8:35; John 12:3"> and she also had a sister, called Mary, who also, having seated herself beside the feet of Jesus, was hearing the word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 6:27; Luke 12:29; Matthew 14:15"> and Martha was distracted about much serving, and having stood by Him, she said, “Lord, do You not care that my sister left me alone to serve? Say then to her that she may partake along with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; Luke 21:34; Matthew 6:25–34"> And Jesus answering said to her, “Martha, Martha, you are anxious and disquieted about many things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Psalms 16:5–6; John 10:27–28"> but of one thing there is need, and Mary chose the good part; that will not be taken away from her.”</span>
