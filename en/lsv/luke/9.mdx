<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 14:12; Luke 10:19; Matthew 16:19"> And having called together His twelve disciples, He gave them power and authority over all the demons, and to cure diseases,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 10:7–8; Luke 10:9; Matthew 3:2"> and He sent them to proclaim the Kingdom of God, and to heal the ailing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Psalms 37:3; Luke 10:4–12"> And He said to them, “Take nothing for the way, neither staff, nor leather pouch, nor bread, nor money; neither have two coats each;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:11; Mark 6:10; Luke 10:5–8"> and into whatever house you may enter, remain there, and depart from there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:51; Acts 18:6; Mark 6:11"> and as many as may not receive you, going forth from that city, even the dust from your feet shake off, for a testimony against them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 4:30; Mark 16:20; Acts 5:15"> And going forth they were going through the several villages, proclaiming good news, and healing everywhere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 6:14–28; Matthew 14:1–12; Luke 3:1"> And Herod the tetrarch heard of all the things being done by Him, and was perplexed, because it was said by some that John has been raised out of the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:21; Luke 9:19; Mark 6:15"> and by some that Elijah appeared, and by others, that a prophet, one of the ancients, was risen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 23:8; Luke 13:31–32"> and Herod said, “I beheaded John, but who is this concerning whom I hear such things?” And he was seeking to see Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 11:21; Hebrews 13:17; Matthew 14:13–21"> And the apostles having turned back, declared to Him how they did great things, and having taken them, He withdrew by Himself into a city called Bethsaida,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 9:2; Luke 5:31; Hebrews 4:16"> and the multitudes having known followed Him, and having received them, He was speaking to them concerning the Kingdom of God, and He cured those having need of service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:5–15; Hosea 13:5"> And the day began to decline, and the Twelve having come near, said to Him, “Let away the multitude, that having gone into the surrounding villages and the fields, they may lodge and may find provision, because here we are in a desolate place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; John 6:5–9; 2 Kings 4:42–43"> And He said to them, “You give them to eat”; and they said, “We have no more than five loaves and two fishes: except, having gone, we may buy food for all this people”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 8:6; 1 Corinthians 14:40; Mark 6:39–40"> for they were about five thousand men. And He said to His disciples, “Cause them to recline in companies, in each fifty”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and they did so, and made all to recline;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; 1 Timothy 4:4–5; Matthew 15:36"> and having taken the five loaves and the two fishes, having looked up to the sky, He blessed them, and broke, and was giving to the disciples to set before the multitude;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 4:44; Matthew 15:37–38; John 6:11–13"> and they ate, and were all filled, and there was taken up what was over to them of broken pieces, twelve baskets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 16:13–16; Luke 11:1; Matthew 26:36"> And it came to pass, as He is praying alone, the disciples were with Him, and He questioned them, saying, “Who do the multitudes say I am?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Luke 9:7–8; Matthew 14:2"> And they answering said, “John the Immerser; and others, Elijah; and others, that a prophet, one of the ancients, was risen”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 16:15–17; John 4:29; John 7:41"> and He said to them, “And you—who do you say I am?” And Peter answering said, “The Christ of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 16:20; Matthew 17:9; Mark 8:30–31"> And having charged them, He commanded &#91;them&#93; to say this to no one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Luke 24:7; Luke 18:31–34"> saying, “It is necessary for the Son of Man to suffer many things, and to be rejected by the elders, and chief priests, and scribes, and to be killed, and to be raised the third day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 10:38–39; Luke 14:26–27; Romans 8:13"> And He said to all, “If anyone wills to come after Me, let him disown himself, and take up his cross daily, and follow Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 17:33; John 12:25; Acts 20:23–24"> for whoever may will to save his life will lose it, and whoever may lose his life for My sake, he will save it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 8:36; Matthew 16:26; Psalms 49:6–8"> for what is a man profited, having gained the whole world, and having lost or having forfeited himself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:32–33; 2 Timothy 2:12; Luke 12:8–9"> For whoever may be ashamed of Me and of My words, of this one will the Son of Man be ashamed when He may come in His glory, and the Father’s, and the holy messengers’;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:28; Mark 9:1; John 21:22–23"> and I say to you, truly, there are certain of those standing here who will not taste of death until they may see the Kingdom of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 17:1–13; Matthew 26:37–39; Mark 14:33–36"> And it came to pass, after these words, as it were eight days, that having taken Peter, and John, and James, He went up to the mountain to pray,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 17:2; Mark 9:2–3; Exodus 34:29–35"> and it came to pass, in His praying, the appearance of His face became altered, and His clothing became flashing white.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 3:3–6; 2 Corinthians 3:7–11; Luke 1:17"> And behold, two men were speaking together with Him, who were Moses and Elijah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Philippians 3:21; 1 Peter 1:11–12; 1 Peter 5:10"> who having appeared in glory, spoke of His outgoing that He was about to fulfill in Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 8:18; John 1:14; Daniel 10:9"> but Peter and those with him were heavy with sleep, and having awoken, they saw His glory, and the two men standing with Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 9:5–6; Mark 10:38; Psalms 4:6–7"> And it came to pass, in their parting from Him, Peter said to Jesus, “Master, it is good to us to be here; and we may make three shelters: one for You, and one for Moses, and one for Elijah,” not knowing what he says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 40:34–38; Matthew 17:5–7; Judges 13:22"> and as he was speaking these things, there came a cloud, and overshadowed them, and they feared in their entering into the cloud,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 3:22; Acts 3:22–23; Matthew 3:17"> and a voice came out of the cloud, saying, “This is My Son, the Beloved; hear Him”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 17:9; Mark 9:9–10; Mark 9:6"> and when the voice was past, Jesus was found alone; and they were silent, and declared to no one in those days anything of what they have seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 17:14–20; Mark 9:14–29"> And it came to pass on the next day, they having come down from the mountain, a great multitude met Him there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; John 4:47; Luke 7:12"> and behold, a man from the multitude cried out, saying, “Teacher, I implore You, look on my son, because he is my only begotten;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 9:20; Luke 8:29; Luke 4:35"> and behold, a spirit takes him, and suddenly he cries out, and it convulses him, with foaming, and it hardly departs from him, bruising him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Acts 19:13–16; Luke 10:17–19"> and I implored Your disciples that they might cast it out, and they were not able.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:5; Romans 2:4; John 20:27"> And Jesus answering said, “O generation, unsteadfast and perverse, until when will I be with you, and endure you? Bring your son near here”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Mark 9:26–27; Luke 7:15; Revelation 12:12"> and as he is yet coming near, the demon threw him down, and convulsed &#91;him&#93;, and Jesus rebuked the unclean spirit, and healed the youth, and gave him back to his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:43–45; Luke 5:26; Matthew 17:22"> And they were all amazed at the greatness of God, and while all are wondering at all things that Jesus did, He said to His disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 9:22; Acts 2:23; Luke 2:19"> “Lay to your ears these words, for the Son of Man is about to be delivered up into the hands of men.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 18:34; Luke 2:50; John 12:16"> And they were not knowing this saying, and it was veiled from them, that they might not perceive it, and they were afraid to ask Him about this saying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 18:1–5; Mark 9:33–37; Matthew 20:20–22"> And there entered a reasoning among them, this—who may be greater of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 139:23; John 16:30; Luke 5:22"> And Jesus having seen the reasoning of their heart, having taken hold of a child, set him beside Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Peter 5:6; Luke 14:11; Mark 9:37"> and said to them, “Whoever may receive this child in My Name, receives Me, and whoever may receive Me, receives Him who sent Me, for he who is least among you all—he will be great.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 9:38–40; Numbers 11:27–29; Mark 10:13–14"> And John answering said, “Master, we saw a certain one casting forth the demons in Your Name, and we forbade him, because he does not follow with us”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 12:30; Luke 11:23; 1 Corinthians 12:3"> and Jesus said to him, “Do not forbid, for he who is not against us, is for us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Isaiah 50:5–9; Mark 16:19; Luke 13:22"> And it came to pass, in the completing of the days of His being taken up, that He fixed His face to go on to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 10:5; Luke 17:16; Luke 10:1"> and He sent messengers before His face, and having gone on, they went into a village of Samaritans, to make ready for Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 4:9; John 4:40–42; Luke 9:48"> and they did not receive Him, because His face was going on to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="2 Kings 1:10–14; James 3:14–18; James 1:19–20"> And His disciples James and John having seen, said, “Lord, will You &#91;that&#93; we may command fire to come down from Heaven, and to consume them, as Elijah also did?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; 1 Peter 3:9; Jeremiah 17:9"> And having turned, He rebuked them and said, “You have not known of what spirit you are,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Romans 12:21; John 3:17"> for the Son of Man did not come to destroy men’s lives, but to save”; and they went on to another village.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Matthew 8:19–22; John 13:37; Luke 9:57–60"> And it came to pass, as they are going on in the way, a certain one said to Him, “I will follow You wherever You may go, Lord”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Matthew 8:20; Luke 14:26–33; James 2:5"> and Jesus said to him, “The foxes have holes, and the birds of the sky places of rest, but the Son of Man has nowhere He may recline the head.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; Matthew 6:33; 1 Kings 19:20"> And He said to another, “Follow Me”; and he said, “Lord, permit me, having gone away, to first bury my father”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:3–4; 1 Timothy 5:6; John 21:15–17"> and Jesus said to him, “Permit the dead to bury their own dead, and you, having gone away, publish the Kingdom of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Luke 14:26; Luke 14:18–20; 1 Kings 19:20"> And another also said, “I will follow You, Lord, but first permit me to take leave of those in my house”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Philippians 3:13; Hebrews 10:38; 2 Peter 2:20–22"> and Jesus said to him, “No one having put his hand on a plow, and looking back, is fit for the Kingdom of God.”</span>
