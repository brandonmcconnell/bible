<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 1:16–20; Matthew 4:18–22; Mark 3:9"> And it came to pass, in the multitude pressing on Him to hear the word of God, that He was standing beside the Lake of Gennesaret,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 1:19; Matthew 4:21"> and He saw two boats standing beside the lake, and the fishers, having gone away from them, were washing the nets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 4:18; Matthew 13:1–2; John 1:41–42"> and having entered into one of the boats, that was Simon’s, He asked him to put back a little from the land, and having sat down, was teaching the multitudes out of the boat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 21:6; Matthew 17:27"> And when He left off speaking, He said to Simon, “Put back into the deep, and let down your nets for a catch”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 2:5; John 15:14; Luke 6:46–48"> and Simon answering said to Him, “Master, through the whole night, having labored, we have taken nothing, but at Your saying I will let down the net.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; John 21:6–11; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> And having done this, they enclosed a great multitude of fishes, and their net was breaking,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 6:2; Philippians 4:3; Acts 11:25"> and they beckoned to the partners who &#91;are&#93; in the other boat, having come, to help them; and they came, and filled both the boats, so that they were sinking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:5; Job 42:5–6; Revelation 1:17"> And Simon Peter having seen, fell down at the knees of Jesus, saying, “Depart from me, because I am a sinful man, O Lord”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 4:32; Psalms 8:8; Mark 9:6"> for astonishment seized him, and all those with him, at the catch of the fishes that they took,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 4:19; Matthew 13:47; Ezekiel 47:9–10"> and in like manner also James and John, sons of Zebedee, who were partners with Simon; and Jesus said to Simon, “Do not fear, from now on you will be catching men”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 4:20; Luke 5:28; Matthew 19:27"> and they, having brought the boats on the land, having left all, followed Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Luke 17:16; Matthew 8:2–4"> And it came to pass, in His being in one of the cities, that behold, a man full of leprosy, and having seen Jesus, having fallen on &#91;his&#93; face, he implored Him, saying, “Lord, if You may will, You are able to cleanse me”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:29–30; John 4:50–53; Hosea 14:4"> and having stretched forth &#91;His&#93; hand, He touched him, having said, “I will, be cleansed”; and immediately the leprosy went away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 14:2–32; Luke 17:14; Mark 1:44"> And He charged him to tell no one, “But having gone away, show yourself to the priest, and bring near for your cleansing according as Moses directed, for a testimony to them”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 15:30–31; Luke 12:1; Mark 1:45–2"> but the more was the report going abroad concerning Him, and great multitudes were coming together to hear, and to be healed by Him of their sicknesses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 1:35–36; Matthew 14:23"> and He was withdrawing Himself in the desolate places and was praying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 8:46; Matthew 11:5"> And it came to pass, on one of the days, that He was teaching, and there were sitting by Pharisees and teachers of the Law, who were come out of every village of Galilee, and Judea, and Jerusalem, and the power of the LORD was—to heal them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 2:3–12; Matthew 9:2–8; John 5:5–6"> And behold, men carrying a man on a bed, who has been struck with palsy, and they were seeking to bring him in, and to place before Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 2:4; Matthew 10:27; Matthew 24:17"> and having not found by what way they may bring him in because of the multitude, having gone up on the housetop, through the tiles they let him down with the little bed, into the midst before Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 14:9; Matthew 9:2; Mark 2:5"> and He having seen their faith, said to him, “Man, your sins have been forgiven you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Mark 2:6–7; Luke 7:49"> And the scribes and the Pharisees began to reason, saying, “Who is this that speaks evil words? Who is able to forgive sins, except God only?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 24:38; Isaiah 66:18; Psalms 139:2"> And Jesus having known their reasonings, answering, said to them, “What reason you in your hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 2:9; Matthew 9:5"> Which is easier—to say, Your sins have been forgiven you? Or to say, Arise, and walk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Isaiah 53:11; Luke 8:54"> And that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on the earth to forgive sins—(He said to the one struck with palsy)—I say to you, arise, and having taken up your little bed, be going on to your house.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 9:24; Luke 5:13; Psalms 107:20–22"> And immediately having risen before them, having taken up &#91;that&#93; on which he was lying, he went away to his house, glorifying God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Matthew 12:23; Luke 7:16"> and astonishment took all, and they were glorifying God, and were filled with fear, saying, “We saw strange things today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 2:13–22; Matthew 9:9–17; John 1:43"> And after these things He went forth, and beheld a tax collector, by name Levi, sitting at the tax office, and said to him, “Follow Me”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 5:11; Matthew 19:22–27; 1 Kings 19:19–21"> and he, having left all, having arisen, followed Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 2:15; Luke 15:1; 1 Corinthians 10:27"> And Levi made a great entertainment to Him in his house, and there was a great multitude of tax collectors and others who were with them reclining,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 21:28–32; Luke 5:17; Mark 7:3"> and the scribes and the Pharisees among them were murmuring at His disciples, saying, “Why do You eat and drink with tax collectors and sinners?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Matthew 9:12–13; Jeremiah 8:22"> And Jesus answering said to them, “They who are well have no need of a physician, but they that are ill:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Luke 15:10; 1 Timothy 1:15–16"> I did not come to call righteous men, but sinners, to conversion.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 9:14–17; Mark 2:18–22; John 3:25"> And they said to Him, “Why do the disciples of John fast often, and make supplications—in like manner also those of the Pharisees—but Yours eat and drink?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 3:29; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 54:5"> And He said to them, “Are you able to make the sons of the bride-chamber—in the Bridegroom being with them—to fast?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 17:22; Acts 14:23; Acts 3:21"> But days will come, and when the Bridegroom may be taken away from them, then they will fast in those days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 9:16–17; Mark 2:21–22; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> And He spoke also an allegory to them: “No one puts a patch of new clothing on old clothing, and if otherwise, the new also makes a split, and with the old the patch does not agree, that &#91;is&#93; from the new.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 9:13; Psalms 119:83; Joshua 9:4"> And no one puts new wine into old skins, and if otherwise, the new wine will burst the skins, and itself will be poured out, and the skins will be destroyed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 8:8–13; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ezekiel 36:26"> but new wine is to be put into new skins, and both are preserved together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39; Mark 7:7–13; Jeremiah 6:16"> and no one having drunk old, immediately wishes new, for he says, The old is better.”</span>
