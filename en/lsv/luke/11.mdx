<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 19:14; Luke 22:39–45; Romans 8:26–27"> And it came to pass, in His being in a certain place praying, as He ceased, a certain one of His disciples said to Him, “Lord, teach us to pray, as also John taught his disciples.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:6–15; Ecclesiastes 5:2; Psalms 72:18–19"> And He said to them, “When you may pray, say: Our Father who is in the heavens, hallowed be Your Name; Your kingdom come, Your will come to pass, as in Heaven also on earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 6:11; Matthew 6:34; Proverbs 30:8"> be giving us daily our appointed bread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 6:12–15; Matthew 26:41; Isaiah 43:25–26"> and forgive us our sins, for we also ourselves forgive everyone indebted to us; and may You not bring us into temptation, but deliver us from the evil &#91;one&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:1–8"> And He said to them, “Who of you will have a friend, and will go on to him at midnight, and may say to him, Friend, lend me three loaves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> seeing a friend of mine came out of the way to me, and I have not what I will set before him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 13:25; Galatians 6:17; Luke 7:6"> and he from within answering may say, Do not give me trouble, the door has already been shut, and my children are with me in the bed, I am not able, having risen, to give to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:26; Colossians 4:12; Romans 15:30"> I say to you, even if he will not give to him, having risen, because of his being his friend, yet because of his persistence, having risen, he will give him as many as he needs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 11:24; John 16:23–24; Matthew 21:22"> and I say to you, ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:3; Lamentations 3:54–58; Psalms 31:22"> for everyone who is asking receives; and he who is seeking finds; and to him who is knocking it will be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 7:9; Isaiah 49:15"> And of which of you—the father—&#91;if&#93; the son will ask &#91;for&#93; a loaf, will present to him a stone? And &#91;if&#93; a fish, instead of a fish, will present to him a serpent?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 9:10; Luke 10:19; Ezekiel 2:6"> And &#91;if&#93; he may ask &#91;for&#93; an egg, will present to him a scorpion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 7:11; Isaiah 44:3–4; Romans 8:32"> If, then, you, being evil, have known to be giving good gifts to your children, how much more will the Father who is from Heaven give the Holy Spirit to those asking Him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 12:22–24; Matthew 9:32–34; Mark 7:32–37"> And He was casting forth a demon, and it was mute, and it came to pass, the demon having gone forth, the mute man spoke, and the multitudes wondered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 9:34; Matthew 12:24–30; John 8:48"> and certain of them said, “By Beelzebul, ruler of the demons, He casts forth the demons”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 6:30; 1 Corinthians 1:22; Mark 8:11–12"> and others, tempting, were asking &#91;for&#93; a sign out of Heaven from Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 3:23–27; Matthew 12:25–29; Isaiah 19:2–3"> And He, knowing their thoughts, said to them, “Every kingdom having been divided against itself is desolated; and house against house falls;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 12:26; Matthew 12:31–34; Luke 11:15"> and if Satan was also divided against himself, how will his kingdom be made to stand? For you say by Beelzebul is My casting forth the demons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 9:49; Job 15:6; Romans 3:19"> but if I, by Beelzebul, cast forth the demons—your sons, by whom do they cast forth? Because of this they will be your judges;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 8:19; Matthew 12:28; Luke 10:9"> but if by the finger of God I cast forth the demons, then the Kingdom of God came unaware on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 3:27; Matthew 12:29"> When the strong man may keep his hall armed, his goods are in peace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–25; Isaiah 53:12; 1 John 3:8"> but when the stronger than he, having come on &#91;him&#93;, may overcome him, he takes away his whole armor in which he had trusted, and he distributes his spoils;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:30; Luke 9:50; Mark 9:40"> he who is not with Me is against Me, and he who is not gathering with Me scatters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 12:43–45; 1 Peter 5:8; Job 2:2"> When the unclean spirit may go forth from the man, it walks through waterless places seeking rest, and not finding, it says, I will return to my house from where I came forth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–12; 2 Peter 2:10–19; Jude 1:8–13"> and having come, it finds &#91;it&#93; swept and adorned;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 5:14; Matthew 12:45; 2 Peter 2:20–22"> then it goes, and takes to it seven other spirits more evil than itself, and having entered, they dwell there, and the last of that man becomes worse than the first.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 1:42; Luke 1:48; Luke 23:29"> And it came to pass, in His saying these things, a certain woman having lifted up the voice out of the multitude, said to Him, “Blessed the womb that carried You, and the breasts that You sucked!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 6:47–48; Luke 8:21; Psalms 112:1"> And He said, “Indeed, rather, blessed those hearing the word of God, and keeping &#91;it&#93;!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 11:16; Mark 8:11–12; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> And the multitudes crowding together on Him, He began to say, “This generation is evil, it seeks after a sign, and a sign will not be given to it, except the sign of Jonah the prophet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 12:40–42; Jonah 1:17; Jonah 3:2–10"> for as Jonah became a sign to the Ninevites, so also will the Son of Man be to this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:1; 1 Kings 10:1–13; Romans 2:27"> A queen of the south will rise up in the judgment with the men of this generation, and will condemn them, because she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon; and behold, &#91;One&#93; greater than Solomon &#91;is&#93; here!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 7:26; Luke 11:31; Jonah 3:5–4"> Men of Nineveh will stand up in the judgment with this generation, and will condemn it, because they converted at the proclamation of Jonah; and behold, &#91;One&#93; greater than Jonah &#91;is&#93; here!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:16–17; Mark 4:21–22; Matthew 5:15–16"> And no one having lighted a lamp, puts &#91;it&#93; in a secret place, nor under the measure, but on the lampstand, that those coming in may behold the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 6:22–23; Acts 26:18; Jeremiah 5:21"> The lamp of the body is the eye, when then your eye may be simple, your whole body is also lightened; and when it may be evil, your body is also darkened;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 16:25; 2 Peter 1:9; James 3:13–17"> take heed, then, lest the light that &#91;is&#93; in you is darkness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 20:27; Proverbs 6:23; Proverbs 4:18–19"> if then your whole body is lightened, not having any part darkened, the whole will be lightened, as when the lamp by the brightness may give you light.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 7:36; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23; Luke 14:1"> And in &#91;His&#93; speaking, a certain Pharisee was asking Him that He might dine with him, and having gone in, He reclined,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 15:2–3; Mark 7:2–5; John 3:25"> and the Pharisee having seen, wondered that He did not first immerse Himself before the early meal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Proverbs 30:12; Genesis 6:5"> And the LORD said to him, “Now you, the Pharisees, make the outside of the cup and of the plate clean, but your inward part is full of robbery and wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 12:20; 1 Corinthians 15:36; Matthew 23:26"> Unthinking &#91;ones&#93;! Did He who made the outside not also make the inside?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 12:33; James 1:27; Titus 1:15"> But what you have given &#91;as&#93; alms, and behold, all things are clean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 23:23; Luke 18:12; Proverbs 21:3"> But woe to you, the Pharisees, because you tithe the mint, and the rue, and every herb, and you pass by the judgment and the love of God; these things &#91;you&#93; should do, and those not to be neglecting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 20:46; Mark 12:38–39; Luke 14:7–11"> Woe to you, the Pharisees, because you love the first seats in the synagogues and the salutations in the marketplaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 23:27–28; Psalms 5:9; Acts 23:3"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you are as the unseen tombs, and the men walking above have not known.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 9:40; Matthew 22:35; Jeremiah 6:10"> And one of the lawyers answering, says to Him, “Teacher, saying these things, You also insult us”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Isaiah 10:1; Matthew 23:2–4"> and He said, “And to you, the lawyers, woe! Because you burden men with burdens &#91;too&#93; grievous to bear, and you yourselves do not touch the burdens with one of your fingers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15; Acts 7:51; Matthew 23:29–33"> Woe to you, because you build the tombs of the prophets, and your fathers killed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="James 5:10; Matthew 23:31; 2 Chronicles 36:16"> Then you testify, and are well pleased with the works of your fathers, because they indeed killed them, and you build their tombs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Colossians 2:3; Ephesians 4:11"> because of this also the wisdom of God said: I will send to them prophets, and apostles, and some of them they will kill and persecute,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 9:5–6; Revelation 18:20–24; 2 Kings 24:4"> that the blood of all the prophets, that is being poured forth from the foundation of the world, may be required from this generation—</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20–22; Matthew 23:35; Hebrews 11:4"> from the blood of Abel to the blood of Zacharias, who perished between the altar and the house; yes, I say to you, it will be required from this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Malachi 2:7; Luke 11:45–46"> Woe to you, the lawyers, because you took away the key of the knowledge; you yourselves did not enter; and you hindered those coming in.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:5; Jeremiah 20:10; Luke 20:20"> And in His speaking these things to them, the scribes and the Pharisees began fearfully to urge and to press Him to speak about many things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Mark 12:13; Matthew 22:15; Matthew 22:35"> laying wait for Him, and seeking to catch something out of His mouth, that they might accuse Him.</span>
