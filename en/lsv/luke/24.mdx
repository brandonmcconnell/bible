<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 16:5–6; Matthew 28:3–5; Revelation 1:18"> and on their having become afraid, and having inclined the face to the earth, they said to them, “Why do you seek the living with the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 17:22–23; Luke 9:22; Mark 8:31"> He is not here, but was raised; remember how He spoke to you, being yet in Galilee,</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 12:16; John 14:26; John 2:19–22"> And they remembered His sayings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 24:22–24; Mark 16:7–8; Matthew 28:7–8"> and having turned back from the tomb, told all these things to the Eleven, and to all the rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 27:56; John 20:11–18; Luke 8:2–3"> And it was Mary the Magdalene, and Joanna, and Mary of James, and the other women with them, who told these things to the apostles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 9:16; Mark 16:11; Genesis 19:14"> and their sayings appeared before them as idle talk, and they were not believing them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 20:3–10"> And Peter having risen, ran to the tomb, and having stooped down he sees the linen clothes lying alone, and he went away to his own home, wondering at that having come to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 24:18; Mark 16:12–13"> And behold, two of them were going on during that day to a village, being sixty stadia distant from Jerusalem, the name of which &#91;is&#93; Emmaus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:45; Deuteronomy 6:7; Malachi 3:6"> and they were conversing with one another about all these things that have happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:20; John 14:18–19; Luke 24:36"> And it came to pass in their conversing and reasoning together, that Jesus Himself, having come near, was going on with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 20:14; John 21:4; Luke 24:31"> and their eyes were restrained so as not to know Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:20–22; John 16:6; Ezekiel 9:4–6"> and He said to them, “What &#91;are&#93; these words that you exchange with one another, walking, and you are sad?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 19:25"> And one, whose name was Cleopas, answering, said to Him, “Are You alone visiting Jerusalem and have not known the things having come to pass in it in these days?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; Acts 7:22; John 4:19"> And He said to them, “What things?” And they said to Him, “The things about Jesus of Nazareth, who became a man—a prophet—powerful in deed and word, before God and all the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:13–15; Acts 4:8–10; Luke 23:13"> how also the chief priests and our rulers delivered Him up to a judgment of death, and crucified Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:68; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Luke 2:38"> and we were hoping that it is He who is about to redeem Israel, and also with all these things, this third day is passing today since these things happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 16:9–10; Matthew 28:7–8; John 20:1–2"> And certain of our women also astonished us, coming early to the tomb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and having not found His body, they came, saying to have also seen an apparition of messengers who say He is alive,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 24:12; John 20:1–10"> and certain of those with us went away to the tomb, and found &#91;it&#93; so, even as the women said, and they did not see Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 7:18; Mark 9:19; Hebrews 5:11–12"> And He said to them, “O inconsiderate and slow in heart to believe on all that the prophets spoke!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:11; Isaiah 53:1–12; Zechariah 13:7"> Was it not necessary &#91;for&#93; the Christ to suffer these things, and to enter into His glory?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Luke 24:44; Deuteronomy 18:15"> And having begun from Moses, and from all the Prophets, He was expounding to them in all the Writings the things about Himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 6:48; Genesis 19:2; Genesis 32:26"> And they came near to the village to where they were going, and He made an appearance of going on further,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 4:8; Luke 14:23; Genesis 19:3"> and they constrained Him, saying, “Remain with us, for it is toward evening,” and the day declined, and He went in to remain with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; Luke 24:35; Matthew 15:36"> And it came to pass, in His reclining with them, having taken the bread, He blessed, and having broken, He was giving to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 8:59; Luke 4:30"> and their eyes were opened, and they recognized Him, and He became unseen by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 24:45; Jeremiah 23:29; Hebrews 4:12"> And they said to one another, “Was our heart not burning within us as He was speaking to us in the way, and as He was opening the Writings up to us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 16:13; John 20:19–26; Acts 1:14"> And they, having risen up the same hour, turned back to Jerusalem, and found the Eleven gathered together, and those with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5; Mark 16:7; Luke 22:54–62"> saying, “The Lord was indeed raised, and was seen by Simon”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 16:12–13; Acts 2:42; Luke 24:30–31"> and they were expounding the things in the way, and how He was made known to them in the breaking of the bread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:16; Isaiah 57:18; Mark 16:14"> and as they are speaking these things, Jesus Himself stood in the midst of them, and says to them, “Peace to you”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:13; Matthew 14:26–27; Job 4:14–16"> and being amazed, and becoming frightened, they were thinking themselves to see a spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:14; Hebrews 4:13; Daniel 4:19"> And He said to them, “Why are you troubled? And why do reasonings come up in your hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 20:27; 1 John 1:1; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> See My hands and My feet, that I am He; handle Me and see, because a spirit does not have flesh and bones as you see Me having.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And having said this, He showed the hands and the feet to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 21:5; Psalms 126:1–2; Genesis 45:26–28"> and while they are not believing from the joy, and wondering, He said to them, “Do you have anything here to eat?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And they gave to Him part of a broiled fish, and of a honeycomb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 10:41"> and having taken, He ate before them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Luke 24:26–27; Zechariah 9:9"> and He said to them, “These &#91;are&#93; the words that I spoke to you, being yet with you, that it is necessary to be fulfilled all the things that are written in the Law of Moses, and the Prophets, and the Psalms, about Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Acts 16:14; 1 John 5:20; Psalms 119:18"> Then He opened up their understanding to understand the Writings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–12; Isaiah 50:6; Luke 24:44"> and He said to them, “Thus it has been written, and thus it was necessary &#91;for&#93; the Christ to suffer, and to rise out of the dead &#91;on&#93; the third day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 5:31; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 2:38"> and conversion and forgiveness of sins is to be proclaimed in His Name to all the nations, beginning from Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 2:32; Acts 13:31"> and you are witnesses of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 1:4; Joel 2:28–32"> And behold, I send the promise of My Father on you, but you—abide in the city of Jerusalem until you are clothed with power from on high.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Mark 11:1"> And He led them forth outside—to Bethany, and having lifted up His hands He blessed them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Acts 1:9; Mark 16:19; 2 Kings 2:11"> and it came to pass, in His blessing them, He was parted from them, and was carried up into Heaven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 28:9; John 16:22; Psalms 30:11"> and they, having worshiped Him, turned back to Jerusalem with great joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Mark 16:20; Acts 2:46–47; Acts 5:41–42"> and were continually in the temple, praising and blessing God. Amen.</span>
