<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:1–3; 2 Peter 1:16–19; John 20:31"> Seeing that many took in hand to set in order a narration of the matters that have been fully assured among us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:27; Acts 26:16; Hebrews 2:3"> as they delivered to us, who from the beginning became eyewitnesses, and officers of the word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 1:1; Acts 26:25; Acts 11:4"> it seemed good also to me, having followed from the first after all things exactly, to write to you in order, most noble Theophilus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 20:31; Acts 18:25; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> that you may know the certainty of the things wherein you were instructed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:10; Matthew 2:1; Nehemiah 12:4"> There was in the days of Herod, the king of Judea, a certain priest, by name Zacharias, of the division of Abijah, and his wife of the daughters of Aaron, and her name Elizabeth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:1; Genesis 17:1; Philippians 2:15"> and they were both righteous before God, going on in all the commands and righteousnesses of the LORD blameless,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Genesis 18:11; 1 Samuel 1:2"> and they had no child, because that Elizabeth was barren, and both were advanced in their days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 24:19; 2 Chronicles 31:2"> And it came to pass, in his acting as priest, in the order of his division before God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:11; Exodus 30:7–8; 1 Chronicles 23:13"> according to the custom of the priesthood, his lot was to make incense, having gone into the temple of the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:17; Revelation 8:3; Hebrews 4:14"> and all the multitude of the people were praying outside, at the hour of the incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:26–27; Acts 10:3–4; Judges 13:3"> And there appeared to him a messenger of the LORD standing on the right side of the altar of the incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:22; Judges 6:22; Luke 2:9–10"> and Zacharias, having seen, was troubled, and fear fell on him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:21; Acts 10:31; Genesis 25:21"> and the messenger said to him, “Do not fear, Zacharias, for your supplication was heard, and your wife Elizabeth will bear a son to you, and you will call his name John,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:58; Proverbs 23:24; Genesis 21:6"> and there will be joy to you, and gladness, and many will rejoice at his birth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Numbers 6:2–4; Luke 7:33"> for he will be great before the LORD, and wine and strong drink he may not drink, and he will be full of the Holy Spirit, even from his mother’s womb;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Matthew 21:32; Malachi 3:1"> and he will turn many of the sons of Israel to the LORD their God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 4:5–6; Matthew 11:14; Luke 1:76"> and he will go before Him, in the spirit and power of Elijah, to turn hearts of fathers to children, and disobedient ones to the wisdom of righteous ones, to make ready for the LORD, a people prepared.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 38:22; Genesis 17:17; Luke 1:7"> And Zacharias said to the messenger, “Whereby will I know this? For I am aged, and my wife is advanced in her days?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 1:26; Matthew 18:10; Daniel 9:21–23"> And the messenger answering said to him, “I am Gabriel, who has been standing near before God, and I was sent to speak to you, and to proclaim this good news to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:26; Ezekiel 24:27; Luke 1:22"> and behold, you will be silent, and not able to speak, until the day that these things will come to pass, because you did not believe my words that will be fulfilled in their season.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27"> And the people were waiting for Zacharias, and wondering at his lingering in the temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 1:62; John 13:24; Acts 21:40"> and having come out, he was not able to speak to them, and they perceived that he had seen a vision in the temple, and he was beckoning to them, and remained mute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; 2 Kings 11:5–7"> And it came to pass, when the days of his service were fulfilled, he went away to his house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and after those days, his wife Elizabeth conceived, and hid herself five months, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 30:22–23; Isaiah 54:1–4; Genesis 21:1–2"> “Thus the LORD has done to me, in days in which He looked on &#91;me&#93;, to take away my reproach among men.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; Luke 1:19; John 7:41"> And in the sixth month the messenger Gabriel was sent by God, to a city of Galilee, the name of which &#91;is&#93; Nazareth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:16"> to a virgin, betrothed to a man, whose name &#91;is&#93; Joseph, of the house of David, and the name of the virgin &#91;is&#93; Mary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 6:12; Daniel 10:19; Luke 1:30"> And the messenger having come in to her, said, “Greetings, favored one, the LORD &#91;is&#93; with you; blessed &#91;are&#93; you among women”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:17; Luke 1:12; Acts 10:4"> and she, having seen, was troubled at his word, and was reasoning of what kind this salutation may be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Romans 8:31; Luke 12:32"> And the messenger said to her, “Do not fear, Mary, for you have found favor with God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:21; Matthew 1:25"> and behold, you will conceive in the womb, and will bring forth a Son, and call His Name Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; Isaiah 16:5; 2 Samuel 7:11–13"> He will be great, and He will be called Son of the Highest, and the LORD God will give Him the throne of His father David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Daniel 2:44; Hebrews 1:8; Daniel 7:13–14"> and He will reign over the house of Jacob for all ages; and of His kingdom there will be no end.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Judges 13:8–12; Acts 9:6"> And Mary said to the messenger, “How will this be, seeing I do not know a husband?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; John 1:49; Matthew 1:20"> And the messenger answering said to her, “The Holy Spirit will come on you, and the power of the Highest will overshadow you, therefore also the holy-begotten thing will be called Son of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> and behold, Elizabeth, your relative, she also has conceived a son in her old age, and this is the sixth month to her who was called barren;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Jeremiah 32:27; Mark 10:27"> because nothing will be impossible with God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Romans 4:20–21; Psalms 119:38; Psalms 116:16"> And Mary said, “Behold, the maidservant of the LORD; let it be to me according to your saying,” and the messenger went away from her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 1:65; Joshua 15:48–59; Joshua 10:40"> And Mary having arisen in those days, went into the hill-country, with haste, to a city of Judea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and entered into the house of Zacharias, and greeted Elizabeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 1:67; Acts 4:8; Luke 1:44"> And it came to pass, when Elizabeth heard the salutation of Mary, the baby leapt in her womb; and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 1:28; Judges 5:24; Genesis 22:18"> and spoke out with a loud voice and said, “Blessed &#91;are&#93; you among women, and blessed &#91;is&#93; the Fruit of your womb;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 20:28; John 13:13; Luke 2:11"> and from where &#91;is&#93; this to me, that the mother of my Lord might come to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> For behold, when the voice of your salutation came to my ears, the baby in my womb leapt in gladness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 20:29; John 11:40; Luke 11:27–28"> and blessed &#91;is&#93; she who believed, for there will be a completion to the things spoken to her from the LORD.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:1–10; Psalms 34:2–3; Psalms 35:9"> And Mary said, “My soul magnifies the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 35:9; 1 Timothy 2:3; Habakkuk 3:18"> And my spirit was glad on God my Savior,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 138:6; Malachi 3:12; 1 Corinthians 1:26–28"> Because He looked on the lowliness of His maidservant, &#124;&#124; For behold, from now on all the generations will call me blessed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 126:2–3; Psalms 111:9; Ephesians 3:20"> For He who is mighty did great things to me, &#124;&#124; And holy &#91;is&#93; His Name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 103:17–18; Exodus 20:6; Genesis 17:7"> And His kindness &#91;is&#93; to generations of generations, &#124;&#124; To those fearing Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 98:1; Isaiah 52:10; 1 Samuel 2:3–4"> He did powerfully with His arm, &#124;&#124; He scattered abroad the proud in the thought of their heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; James 4:10; Psalms 107:40–41"> He brought down the mighty from thrones, &#124;&#124; And He exalted the lowly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 107:8–9; Luke 6:21; Psalms 34:10"> He filled the hungry with good, &#124;&#124; And the rich He sent away empty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Psalms 98:3; Micah 7:20; Isaiah 44:21"> He has taken hold of His servant Israel, &#124;&#124; To remember kindness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Psalms 105:6–10; Genesis 17:19; Psalms 132:11–17"> As He spoke to our fathers, &#124;&#124; To Abraham and to his seed—throughout the age.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And Mary remained with her about three months, and turned back to her house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Genesis 21:2–3; Luke 2:6–7"> And to Elizabeth was the time fulfilled for her bringing forth, and she bore a son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Luke 1:14; Romans 12:15"> and the neighbors and her relatives heard that the LORD was making His kindness great with her, and they were rejoicing with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Genesis 17:12; Leviticus 12:3; Philippians 3:5"> And it came to pass, on the eighth day, they came to circumcise the child, and they were calling him by the name of his father, Zacharias,</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Luke 1:13; 2 Samuel 12:25; Luke 1:63"> and his mother answering said, “No, but he will be called John.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> And they said to her, “There is none among your relatives who is called by this name,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Luke 1:22"> and they were making signs to his father, what he would wish him to be called,</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 1:60; Isaiah 30:8; Luke 1:13"> and having asked for a tablet, he wrote, saying, “John is his name”; and they all wondered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Luke 1:20; Exodus 4:15–16; Ezekiel 33:22"> and his mouth was opened immediately, and his tongue, and he was speaking, praising God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Luke 1:39; Acts 5:11; Luke 7:16"> And fear came on all those dwelling around them, and in all the hill-country of Judea were all these sayings spoken of,</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Acts 11:21; Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51"> and all who heard laid them up in their hearts, saying, “What then will this child be?” And the hand of the LORD was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Luke 1:41; Luke 1:15"> And his father Zacharias was filled with the Holy Spirit, and prophesied, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Psalms 106:48; Luke 7:16; Psalms 41:13"> “Blessed &#91;is&#93; the LORD, the God of Israel, &#124;&#124; Because He looked on, &#124;&#124; And worked redemption for His people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Ezekiel 29:21; 2 Samuel 22:3"> And raised a horn of salvation to us, &#124;&#124; In the house of His servant David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Acts 3:21–24; Romans 1:2; 2 Peter 1:21"> As He spoke by the mouth of His holy prophets, &#124;&#124; Which have been from the age;</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Psalms 106:10; Jeremiah 23:6; Zephaniah 3:15–20"> Salvation from our enemies, &#124;&#124; And out of the hand of all hating us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13–18; Micah 7:20; Psalms 106:45"> To do kindness with our fathers, &#124;&#124; And to be mindful of His holy covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73" data-xref="Genesis 22:16–18; Psalms 105:9; Deuteronomy 7:8"> An oath that He swore to Abraham our father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7; Zephaniah 3:15–17; Isaiah 54:13–14"> To give to us, without fear, &#124;&#124; Having been delivered out of the hand of our enemies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v75" data-v="75" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Ephesians 2:10; Titus 2:11–14"> To serve Him, in holiness and righteousness &#124;&#124; Before Him, all the days of our life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v76" data-v="76" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 40:3–5; Mark 1:2–3"> And you, child, &#124;&#124; Prophet of the Highest will you be called; For you will go before the face of the LORD, &#124;&#124; To prepare His ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v77" data-v="77" data-xref="Acts 5:31; Acts 2:38; Acts 10:43"> To give knowledge of salvation to His people &#124;&#124; In forgiveness of their sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v78" data-v="78" data-xref="Malachi 4:2; 2 Peter 1:19; Revelation 22:16"> Through the yearnings of our God, &#124;&#124; In which the rising from on high looked on us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v79" data-v="79" data-xref="Isaiah 9:2; Matthew 4:16; Acts 26:18"> To give light to those sitting in darkness and death-shade, &#124;&#124; To guide our feet to a way of peace.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v80" data-v="80" data-xref="Luke 2:40; Mark 1:3–4; Luke 2:52"> And the child grew, and was strengthened in spirit, and he was in the deserts until the day of his showing to Israel.</span>
