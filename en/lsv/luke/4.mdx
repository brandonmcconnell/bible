<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:1–11; Acts 10:38; Mark 1:12–13"> And Jesus, full of the Holy Spirit, turned back from the Jordan, and was brought in the Spirit into the wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Hebrews 4:15; Deuteronomy 9:9"> being tempted by the Devil forty days, and He did not eat anything in those days, and they having been ended, He afterward hungered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 4:3; Luke 3:22"> and the Devil said to Him, “If You are &#91;the&#93; Son of God, speak to this stone that it may become bread.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Matthew 4:4; Matthew 6:31"> And Jesus answered him, saying, “It has been written, that, Not on bread only will man live, but on every saying of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; 1 Corinthians 15:52; Matthew 4:8"> And the Devil having brought Him up to a high mountain, showed to Him all the kingdoms of the world in a moment of time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 12:31; 1 John 5:19; John 14:30"> and the Devil said to Him, “To You I will give all this authority, and their glory, because to me it has been delivered, and to whomsoever I will, I give it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 8:28; Psalms 72:11; Isaiah 45:14"> You, then, if You may worship me—all will be Yours.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Deuteronomy 10:20; Matthew 4:10"> And Jesus answering him said, “&#91;&#91;Get behind Me, Satan, for&#93;&#93; it has been written: You will worship the LORD your God, and Him only you will serve.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 4:5–6; Luke 4:3; Matthew 8:29"> And he brought Him to Jerusalem, and set Him on the pinnacle of the temple, and said to Him, “If You are the Son of God, cast Yourself down from here,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 91:11–12; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Corinthians 11:14"> for it has been written: To His messengers He will give charge concerning you, to guard over you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 91:12"> and: On hands they will bear you up, lest at any time you may dash your foot against a stone.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; Hebrews 3:8–9; 1 Corinthians 10:9"> And Jesus answering said to him, “It has been said, You will not tempt the LORD your God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; James 4:7; Matthew 4:11"> And having ended all temptation, the Devil departed from Him until a convenient season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 4:23–25; Acts 10:37; Matthew 4:12"> And Jesus turned back in the power of the Spirit to Galilee, and a fame went forth through all the surrounding region concerning Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Matthew 9:8; Matthew 9:35"> and He was teaching in their synagogues, being glorified by all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:2; Acts 13:14–16; Matthew 2:23"> And He came to Nazareth, where He has been brought up, and He went in, according to His custom, on the day of the Sabbaths, into the synagogue, and stood up to read;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1–3; Acts 13:15; Acts 7:42"> and there was given over to Him a scroll of Isaiah the prophet, and having unfolded the scroll, He found the place where it has been written:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1; Matthew 11:5; Psalms 146:7–8"> “The Spirit of the LORD &#91;is&#93; on Me, &#124;&#124; Because He anointed Me &#124;&#124; To proclaim good news to the poor, &#124;&#124; Sent Me to heal the broken of heart, &#124;&#124; To proclaim to captives deliverance, &#124;&#124; And to blind receiving of sight, &#124;&#124; To send the bruised away with deliverance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 61:2; Isaiah 63:4; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> To proclaim the acceptable year of the LORD.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 4:17; John 8:2; Luke 19:48"> And having folded the scroll, having given &#91;it&#93; back to the officer, He sat down, and the eyes of all in the synagogue were gazing on Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 4:25–26; Acts 3:18; Acts 2:16–18"> And He began to say to them, “Today this writing has been fulfilled in your ears”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 13:54–56; Psalms 45:2; Acts 6:10"> and all were bearing testimony to Him, and were wondering at the gracious words that are coming forth out of His mouth, and they said, “Is this not the Son of Joseph?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 4:46–53; Matthew 11:23–24; Mark 2:1–12"> And He said to them, “Certainly you will say to Me this allegory, Physician, heal yourself; as great things as we heard done in Capernaum, do also here in Your country”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 13:57; John 4:44; Mark 6:4–5"> and He said, “Truly I say to you, no prophet is accepted in his own country;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 5:17; 1 Kings 17:1; Matthew 20:15"> and of a truth I say to you, many widows were in the days of Elijah, in Israel, when the sky was shut for three years and six months, when great famine came on all the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 17:9–24; Matthew 11:21; Obadiah 1:20"> and to none of them was Elijah sent, but—to Sarepta of Sidon, to a woman, a widow;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 5:1–27; John 17:12; Matthew 12:4"> and many lepers were in the time of Elisha the prophet, in Israel, and none of them was cleansed, but—Naaman the Syrian.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 5:33; 2 Chronicles 24:20–21; Jeremiah 38:6"> And all in the synagogue were filled with wrath, hearing these things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:59; Acts 21:28–32; Numbers 15:35"> and having risen, they put Him forth outside the city, and brought Him to the brow of the hill on which their city had been built—to cast Him down headlong,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:39; John 8:59; John 18:6–7"> and He, having gone through the midst of them, went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 1:21–28; Matthew 4:13; Acts 13:50–2"> And He came down to Capernaum, a city of Galilee, and was teaching them on the Sabbaths,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 4:36; Matthew 7:28–29; John 6:63"> and they were astonished at His teaching, because His word was with authority.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 1:23"> And in the synagogue was a man having a spirit of an unclean demon, and he cried out with a great voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 1:24; James 2:19; Matthew 8:29"> “Aah! What &#91;regards&#93; us and You, Jesus, O Nazarene? You came to destroy us; I have known who You are—the Holy One of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Luke 4:39; Matthew 8:26"> And Jesus rebuked him, saying, “Be silenced, and come forth out of him”; and the demon having cast him into the midst, came forth from him, having hurt him nothing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 4:32; 1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 12:22–23"> and amazement came on all, and they were speaking together with one another, saying, “What &#91;is&#93; this word, that with authority and power He commands the unclean spirits, and they come forth?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 1:45; Luke 4:14; Isaiah 52:13"> And there was going forth a fame concerning Him to every place of the surrounding region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 8:14–15; Mark 1:29–31; John 11:22"> And having risen out of the synagogue, He entered into the house of Simon, and the mother-in-law of Simon was pressed with a great fever, and they asked Him about her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Luke 4:35; Luke 8:24"> and having stood over her, He rebuked the fever, and it left her, and immediately, having risen, she was ministering to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 1:32–34; Matthew 8:16–17; Mark 5:23"> And at the setting of the sun, all, as many as had any ailing with manifold diseases, brought them to Him, and He, having put hands on each one of them, healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:34; James 2:19; Luke 4:34–35"> And demons were also coming forth from many, crying out and saying, “You are the Christ, the Son of God”; and rebuking, He did not permit them to speak, because they knew Him to be the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 1:35–38; John 4:34"> And day having come, having gone forth, He went on to a desolate place, and the multitudes were seeking Him, and they came to Him, and were restraining Him—not to go on from them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 1:38–39; 2 Timothy 4:2; John 9:4"> and He said to them, “Also to the other cities it is necessary for Me to proclaim good news of the Kingdom of God, because for this I have been sent”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Mark 1:39; Luke 4:15"> and He was preaching in the synagogues of Galilee.</span>
