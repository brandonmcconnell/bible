<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 27:1–2; Matthew 27:11–14; Luke 22:66"> And having risen, the whole multitude of them led Him to Pilate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 19:12; Luke 23:14"> and began to accuse Him, saying, “We found this One perverting the nation, and forbidding to give tribute to Caesar, saying Himself to be Christ, a king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 1:49; 1 Timothy 6:13; Luke 22:70"> And Pilate questioned Him, saying, “You are the King of the Jews?” And He answering him, said, “You say &#91;it&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:22"> And Pilate said to the chief priests and the multitude, “I find no fault in this Man”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 1:14; John 1:43; John 2:11"> and they were the more urgent, saying, “He stirs up the people, teaching throughout the whole of Judea—having begun from Galilee—to this place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 5:37; Luke 13:1"> And Pilate having heard of Galilee, questioned if the Man is a Galilean,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Luke 13:31; Matthew 14:1"> and having known that He is from the jurisdiction of Herod, he sent Him back to Herod, he also being in Jerusalem in those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:7–9; Matthew 14:1; Mark 6:14"> And Herod rejoiced exceedingly having seen Jesus, for he was wishing to see Him for a long &#91;time&#93;, because of hearing many things about Him, and he was hoping to see some sign done by Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 27:14; Matthew 7:6; 1 Peter 2:23"> and was questioning Him in many words, and He answered him nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 11:53; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:5"> And the chief priests and the scribes stood vehemently accusing Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Matthew 27:27–30; Psalms 69:19–20"> and Herod with his soldiers having set Him at nothing, and having mocked, having cast radiant apparel around Him, sent Him back to Pilate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 4:27; Revelation 17:13–14; Psalms 83:4–6"> and both Pilate and Herod became friends with one another on that day, for they were previously at enmity between themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:14; Matthew 27:21–23; John 18:38"> And Pilate having called together the chief priests, and the rulers, and the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:4; Hebrews 7:26; Luke 23:1–2"> said to them, “You brought this Man to me as perverting the people, and behold, I having examined &#91;Him&#93; before you, found no fault in this Man in those things you bring forward against Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> no, neither Herod, for he sent Him back to us, and behold, nothing worthy of death is having been done by Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Luke 23:22; Mark 15:15"> having corrected, therefore, I will release Him,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> &#91;&#91;for it was necessary for him to release to them one at every celebration,&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:36; Acts 3:14; Acts 22:22"> and they cried out—the whole multitude—saying, “Away with this One, and release Barabbas to us,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Luke 23:5; Luke 23:2"> who had been cast into prison, because of a certain sedition made in the city, and murder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 15:15; Matthew 14:8–9; Matthew 27:19"> Pilate again then—wishing to release Jesus—called to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:22–25; Mark 15:13; John 19:15"> but they were calling out, saying, “Crucify! Crucify Him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 23:14; Luke 23:20; 1 Peter 3:18"> And he said to them a third time, “Why, what evil did He do? I found no cause of death in Him; having corrected Him, then, I will release &#91;Him&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 11:8; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 22:12–13"> And they were pressing with loud voices asking Him to be crucified, and their voices, and those of the chief priests, were prevailing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 17:15"> and Pilate gave judgment for their request being done,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Mark 15:6–7; Mark 15:15"> and he released him who because of sedition and murder has been cast into the prison, whom they were asking for, and he gave up Jesus to their will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:32–44; Mark 15:21–32; Acts 6:9"> And as they led Him away, having taken hold on Simon, a certain Cyrenian, coming from the field, they put the cross on him to carry &#91;it&#93; behind Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 8:52; Luke 8:2; Mark 15:40"> And a great multitude of the people were following Him, and of women, who also were beating themselves and lamenting Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Song of Songs 8:4; Song of Songs 5:8; Song of Songs 1:5"> and Jesus having turned to them, said, “Daughters of Jerusalem, do not weep for Me, but weep for yourselves and for your children;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:19; Luke 21:23–24; Mark 13:17–19"> for behold, days come in which they will say, Blessed the barren, and wombs that did not bear, and breasts that did not give suck;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 6:16; Hosea 10:8; Isaiah 2:19"> then they will begin to say to the mountains, Fall on us, and to the hills, Cover us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; John 15:6; Proverbs 11:31"> for if they do these things in the green tree, what may happen in the dry?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:27; Isaiah 53:12; Matthew 27:38"> And there were also others—two evildoers—with Him, to be put to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 15:22–23; John 19:17–18; John 18:32"> and when they came to the place that is called “&#91;Place&#93; of &#91;the&#93; Skull,” there they crucified Him and the evildoers, one on the right hand and one on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Acts 7:60; 1 Peter 3:9"> And Jesus said, “Father, forgive them, for they have not known what they do”; and parting His garments they cast a lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 22:17; Isaiah 42:1; 1 Peter 2:4"> And the people were standing, looking on, and the rulers were also sneering with them, saying, “He saved others, let Him save Himself, if this be the Christ, the Chosen One of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Matthew 27:48; Matthew 27:34"> And the soldiers were also mocking Him, coming near and offering vinegar to Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and saying, “If You are the King of the Jews, save Yourself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:26; Matthew 27:37; John 19:19–22"> And there was also an inscription written over Him &#91;&#91;in letters of Greek, and Latin, and Hebrew&#93;&#93;: “THIS IS THE KING OF THE JEWS.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:35"> And one of the evildoers who were hanged was slandering Him, saying, “If You are the Christ, save Yourself and us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Luke 12:5; Revelation 15:4"> And the other answering, was rebuking him, saying, “Do you not even fear God, that you are in the same judgment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–41; Ezra 9:13; 1 Peter 1:19"> And we indeed justly, for we are receiving back &#91;things&#93; worthy of what we did, but this One did nothing out of place”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Romans 10:9–14; Acts 16:31; 1 John 5:11–13"> and he said to Jesus, “Remember me, Lord, when You may come in Your kingdom”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Psalms 50:15; 1 Timothy 1:15–16"> and Jesus said to him, “Truly I say to you, today you will be with Me in Paradise.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Mark 15:33–34; Amos 8:9"> And it was, as it were, the sixth hour, and darkness came over all the land until the ninth hour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 9:3–8"> and the sun was darkened, and the veil of the temple was torn in the middle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 31:5; Acts 7:59; John 19:30"> and having cried with a loud voice, Jesus said, “Father, into Your hands I commit My spirit”; now having said this, He breathed His last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:39; Luke 23:41"> And the centurion having seen what was done, glorified God, saying, “Truly this Man was righteous”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 18:13; Acts 2:37; Jeremiah 31:19"> and all the multitudes having come together to this sight, beholding the things that came to pass, turned back striking their breasts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 8:2; Psalms 38:11; Luke 23:55"> and all His acquaintances stood far off, and women who followed Him from Galilee, beholding these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Acts 11:24; Acts 10:22"> And behold, a man, by name Joseph, being a counselor, a man good and righteous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Luke 2:38; Isaiah 8:12"> from Arimathea, a city of the Jews, who also himself was expecting the Kingdom of God, he was not consenting to their counsel and deed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 19:38–42"> he having gone near to Pilate, asked for the body of Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 53:9; Mark 15:46"> and having taken it down, he wrapped it in fine linen, and placed it in a hewn out tomb, where no one was yet laid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:31; Mark 15:42"> And it was &#91;the&#93; Day of Preparation, and Sabbath was approaching,</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:61; Mark 15:47"> and the women who also have come with Him out of Galilee having followed after, beheld the tomb, and how His body was placed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Luke 24:1; Mark 16:1; Deuteronomy 5:14"> and having turned back, they made ready spices and ointments, and on the Sabbath, indeed, they rested, according to the command.</span>
