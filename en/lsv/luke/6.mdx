<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:25; Mark 2:23–28; Matthew 12:1–8"> And it came to pass, on a Sabbath, as He is going through the grainfields, that His disciples were plucking the ears, and were eating, rubbing with the hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:2; John 5:16; John 5:9–11"> and certain of the Pharisees said to them, “Why do you do that which is not lawful to do on the Sabbaths?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:3–6; Mark 2:25–26; Matthew 12:3–5"> And Jesus answering said to them, “Did you not read even this that David did when he hungered, himself and those who are with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–9"> how he went into the house of God, and took the Bread of the Presentation, and ate, and gave also to those with him, which it is not lawful to eat, except only to the priests?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 2:27; Revelation 1:10; Mark 9:7"> And He said to them, “The Son of Man is Lord also of the Sabbath.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 3:1–6; Matthew 12:9–14; Luke 4:31"> And it came to pass also, on another Sabbath, that He goes into the synagogue, and teaches, and there was there a man, and his right hand was withered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 20:20; Psalms 38:12; Luke 13:14"> and the scribes and the Pharisees were watching Him, if on the Sabbath He will heal, that they might find an accusation against Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 42:4; Matthew 9:4; Luke 5:22"> And He Himself had known their reasonings and said to the man having the withered hand, “Rise, and stand in the midst”; and he having risen, stood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 3:4; Matthew 12:12–13; Luke 14:3"> Then Jesus said to them, “I will question you something: is it lawful on the Sabbaths to do good, or to do evil? To save life or to kill?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 4:6–7; Mark 3:5; John 5:8"> And having looked around on them all, He said to the man, “Stretch forth your hand”; and he did so, and his hand was restored whole as the other;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–2; Acts 5:33; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> and they were filled with madness, and were speaking with one another what they might do to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Mark 6:46; Mark 1:35"> And it came to pass in those days, He went forth to the mountain to pray, and was passing the night in the prayer of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 3:13–19; Matthew 9:36–4; Revelation 21:14"> and when it became day, He called near His disciples, also having chosen twelve from them, whom He also named apostles:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Matthew 4:21; Matthew 4:18"> Simon, whom He also named Peter, and his brother Andrew, James and John, Philip and Bartholomew,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 9:9; Mark 3:18; Acts 1:13"> Matthew and Thomas, James of Alphaeus, and Simon called Zealot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:22; John 6:70–71; Mark 3:18"> Judas of James, and Judas Iscariot, who also became betrayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:21; Matthew 4:23–25; Psalms 107:17–20"> And having come down with them, He stood on a level spot; and a crowd of His disciples, and a great multitude of the people from all Judea, and Jerusalem, and the seacoast of Tyre and Sidon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 17:15; Matthew 15:22; Acts 5:16"> who came to hear Him and to be healed of their diseases, &#91;gathered&#93;. And those harassed by unclean spirits &#91;also gathered&#93; and were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Mark 3:10; Acts 5:15–16"> And all the multitude were seeking to touch Him, because power was going forth from Him, and He was healing all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 2:5; Luke 6:20–24; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> And He, having lifted up His eyes to His disciples, said: “Blessed the poor—because yours is the Kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 6:35; Matthew 5:6; James 1:12"> Blessed those hungering now—because you will be filled. Blessed those weeping now—because you will laugh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 5:10–12; Matthew 10:22; 1 Peter 4:12–16"> Blessed are you when men will hate you, and when they will separate you, and will reproach, and will cast forth your name as evil, for the Son of Man’s sake—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 5:41; Revelation 21:7; Revelation 3:12"> rejoice in that day, and leap, for behold, your reward &#91;is&#93; great in Heaven, for according to these things were their fathers doing to the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 6:2; James 5:1–6; Psalms 49:6–7"> But woe to you—the rich, because you have gotten your comfort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 65:13; James 4:9; Proverbs 14:13"> Woe to you who have been filled—because you will hunger. Woe to you who are laughing now—because you will mourn and weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 15:19; James 4:4; Matthew 7:15"> Woe to you when all men will speak well of you—for according to these things were their fathers doing to false prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:15; Luke 6:35; Romans 12:17–21"> But I say to you who are hearing, love your enemies, do good to those hating you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; 1 Corinthians 4:12; Romans 12:14"> bless those cursing you, pray for those maligning you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 10:34; Matthew 5:39–42; 2 Corinthians 11:20"> and to him striking you on the cheek, give also the other, and from him taking away from you the mantle, also the coat you may not keep back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:7–10; 2 Corinthians 9:6–14; Matthew 6:12"> And to everyone who is asking of you, be giving; and from him who is taking away your goods, do not be asking again;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Galatians 5:14; Matthew 22:39"> and as you wish that men may do to you, do also to them in like manner;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 5:46–47; 1 Peter 2:19–20"> and—if you love those loving you, what grace is it to you? For also the sinful love those loving them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and if you do good to those doing good to you, what grace is it to you? For also the sinful do the same;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:42; Luke 14:12–14; Luke 6:35"> and if you lend &#91;to those&#93; of whom you hope to receive back, what grace is it to you? For also the sinful lend to sinners—that they may receive again as much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 5:44–45; John 13:35; Proverbs 19:17"> But love your enemies, and do good, and lend, hoping for nothing again, and your reward will be great, and you will be sons of the Highest, because He is kind to the ungracious and evil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 5:1–2; Matthew 5:48; 1 Peter 1:15–16"> be therefore merciful, as also your Father is merciful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; James 5:9; Matthew 7:1–5"> And do not judge, and you may not be judged; do not condemn, and you may not be condemned; release, and you will be released.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; Deuteronomy 15:10; Mark 4:24"> Give, and it will be given to you; good measure, pressed, and shaken, and running over, they will give into your bosom; for with that measure with which you measure, it will be measured to you again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 15:14; 2 Timothy 3:13; 1 Timothy 6:3–5"> And He spoke an allegory to them, “Is blind able to lead blind? Will they not both fall into a pit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 13:16; Matthew 10:24–25; John 15:20"> A disciple is not above his teacher, but everyone perfected will be as his teacher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 8:7; James 1:24; Romans 2:1"> And why do you behold the speck that is in your brother’s eye, and do not consider the beam that &#91;is&#93; in your own eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Romans 2:21–29; 1 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Psalms 51:9–13"> Or how are you able to say to your brother, Brother, permit, I may take out the speck that &#91;is&#93; in your eye—yourself not beholding the beam in your own eye? Hypocrite, first take the beam out of your own eye, and then you will see clearly to take out the speck that &#91;is&#93; in your brother’s eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Matthew 12:33; Matthew 7:16–20"> For there is not a good tree making bad fruit, nor a bad tree making good fruit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 12:33; James 3:12; Galatians 5:19–23"> for each tree is known from its own fruit, for they do not gather figs from thorns, nor do they crop a grape from a bramble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 4:23; Matthew 12:34–37; Ephesians 4:29"> The good man out of the good treasure of his heart brings forth that which &#91;is&#93; good; and the evil man out of the evil treasure of his heart brings forth that which &#91;is&#93; evil; for out of the abounding of the heart his mouth speaks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Galatians 6:7; Malachi 1:6; Matthew 7:21–23"> And why do you call Me, Lord, Lord, and do not do what I say?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="James 1:22–25; Matthew 7:24–27; Luke 11:28"> Everyone who is coming to Me, and is hearing My words, and is doing them, I will show you to whom he is like:</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:10–15; Psalms 62:2; Matthew 7:25–26"> he is like to a man building a house, who dug and deepened, and laid a foundation on the rock, and a flood having come, the stream broke forth on that house, and was not able to shake it, for it had been founded on the rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; James 1:22–26; Matthew 23:3"> And he who heard and did not, is like to a man having built a house on the earth, without a foundation, against which the stream broke forth, and immediately it fell, and the ruin of that house became great.”</span>
