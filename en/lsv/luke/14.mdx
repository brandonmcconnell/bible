<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:2; Luke 20:20; Psalms 62:4"> And it came to pass, on His going into the house of a certain one of the chiefs of the Pharisees, on a Sabbath, to eat bread, that they were watching Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and behold, there was a certain dropsical man before Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:10; Mark 3:4; Luke 13:14–16"> and Jesus answering spoke to the lawyers and Pharisees, saying, “Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Matthew 21:25–27"> And they were silent, and having taken hold of &#91;him&#93;, He healed him, and let &#91;him&#93; go;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 13:15; Exodus 23:4–5; Matthew 12:11–12"> and answering them He said, “Of which of you will a donkey or ox fall into a pit, and he will not immediately draw it up on the Sabbath day?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 20:40; Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10"> And they were not able to answer Him again to these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Luke 11:43; Matthew 23:6"> And He spoke an allegory to those called, marking how they were choosing out the first couches, saying to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7"> “When you may be called by anyone to wedding feasts, you may not recline on the first couch, lest &#91;one&#93; more honorable than you may have been called by him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 11:2; Proverbs 3:35"> and he who called you and him having come will say to you, Give to this one &#91;your&#93; place, and then you may begin to occupy the last place with shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7; Proverbs 15:33; 1 Samuel 15:17"> But when you may be called, having gone on, recline in the last place, that when he who called you may come, he may say to you, Friend, come up higher; then you will have glory before those dining with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 18:14; Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 29:23"> because everyone who is exalting himself will be humbled, and he who is humbling himself will be exalted.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:46; James 2:1–6; Luke 6:32–36"> And He also said to him who called Him, “When you may make an early meal or a dinner, do not be calling your friends, nor your brothers, nor your relatives, nor rich neighbors, lest they may also call you again, and a repayment may come to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 14:21; Isaiah 58:10; Proverbs 14:31"> but when you may make a feast, be calling poor, maimed, lame, blind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Acts 24:15; John 5:29"> and you will be blessed, because they have nothing to repay you, for it will be repaid to you in the resurrection of the righteous.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; Luke 22:30; Luke 13:29"> And one of those dining with Him, having heard these things, said to Him, “Blessed &#91;is&#93; he who will eat bread in the Kingdom of God”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 22:2–14; Revelation 22:17; Isaiah 55:1–7"> and He said to him, “A certain man made a great dinner, and called many,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:27–29; Matthew 10:1–4; Matthew 22:3–4"> and he sent his servant at the hour of the dinner to say to those having been called, Be coming, because now all things are ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 17:26–31; Luke 18:24"> And all began with one &#91;voice&#93; to excuse themselves. The first said to him, I bought a field, and I have need to go forth and see it; I beg of you, have me excused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And another said, I bought five yoke of oxen, and I go on to prove them; I beg of you, have me excused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:5; 1 Corinthians 7:33; Luke 14:26–28"> And another said, I married a wife, and because of this I am not able to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 14:13; Matthew 11:5; Luke 14:24"> And that servant having come, told these things to his lord, then the master of the house, having been angry, said to his servant, Go forth quickly into the broad places and lanes of the city, and the poor, and maimed, and lame, and blind, bring in here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 14:2; Ephesians 3:8; 1 Timothy 2:5–6"> And the servant said, Lord, it has been done as you commanded, and still there is room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:9–10; Colossians 1:28; Acts 13:47–48"> And the lord said to the servant, Go forth into the ways and hedges, and constrain to come in, that my house may be filled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Acts 13:46; John 8:24"> for I say to you that none of those men who have been called will taste of my dinner.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:24–27; Luke 12:1"> And there were going on with Him great multitudes, and having turned, He said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; John 12:25; Philippians 3:8"> “If anyone comes to Me, and does not hate his own father, and mother, and wife, and children, and brothers, and sisters, and yet even his own life, he is not able to be My disciple;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 9:23–25; Mark 8:34–37"> and whoever does not carry his cross, and come after Me, is not able to be My disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 24:27; Matthew 10:22; Joshua 24:19–24"> For who of you, willing to build a tower, does not first, having sat down, count the expense, whether he has the things for completing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Lest that he having laid a foundation, and not being able to finish, all who are beholding may begin to mock him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 6:11; 2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:11–14"> saying, This man began to build, and was not able to finish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 25:8; 2 Kings 18:20–22"> Or what king going on to engage with another king in war, does not, having sat down, first consult if he with ten thousand is able to meet him who is coming against him with twenty thousand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 12:20; James 4:6–10; Luke 12:58"> And if not so—he being yet a long way off—having sent a delegation, he asks the things for peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; 1 John 2:15–16; Luke 5:28"> So, then, everyone of you who does not take leave of all that he himself has, is not able to be My disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:13; Colossians 4:6; Mark 9:49–50"> The salt &#91;is&#93; good, but if the salt becomes tasteless, with what will it be seasoned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Luke 9:44; Revelation 2:11"> It is neither fit for land nor for manure—they cast it outside. He who is having ears to hear—let him hear.”</span>
