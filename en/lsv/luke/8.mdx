<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Romans 10:15; Matthew 9:35"> And it came to pass thereafter, that He was going through every city and village, preaching and proclaiming good news of the Kingdom of God, and the Twelve &#91;are&#93; with Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:9; Matthew 27:55–56; Mark 15:40–41"> and certain women who were healed of evil spirits and sicknesses, Mary who is called Magdalene, from whom seven demons had gone forth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 4:46–53; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Acts 9:36–39"> and Joanna wife of Chuza, steward of Herod, and Susanna, and many others, who were ministering to Him from their substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 4:1–9; Matthew 13:2–9"> And a great multitude having gathered, and those who from city and city were coming to Him, He spoke by an allegory:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; James 1:23–24; Mark 4:15"> “The sower went forth to sow his seed, and in his sowing some indeed fell beside the way, and it was trodden down, and the birds of the sky devoured it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7–8; Luke 8:13; Mark 4:5–6"> And other fell on the rock, and having sprung up, it withered, through having no moisture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Luke 8:14; Luke 21:34"> And other fell amidst the thorns, and the thorns having sprung up with it, choked it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 2:7"> And other fell on the good ground, and having sprung up, it made fruit a hundredfold.” Saying these things, He was calling, “He having ears to hear—let him hear.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 4:10; Matthew 13:10; Matthew 13:18"> And His disciples were questioning Him, saying, “What may this allegory be?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; Mark 4:11; Matthew 11:25"> And He said, “To you it has been given to know the secrets of the Kingdom of God, but to the rest in allegories, that seeing they may not see, and hearing they may not understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Mark 4:14–20; James 1:21"> And this is the allegory: the seed is the word of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–14; Revelation 12:9; James 1:23–24"> and those beside the way are those hearing, then comes the Devil, and takes up the word from their heart, lest having believed, they may be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 6:4; John 5:35; Proverbs 12:12"> And those on the rock: they who, when they may hear, receive the word with joy, and these have no root, who for a time believe, and in time of temptation fall away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; 1 Timothy 6:17"> And that which fell to the thorns: these are they who have heard, and going forth, through anxieties, and riches, and pleasures of life, are choked, and do not bear to completion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Galatians 5:22–26; Colossians 1:10"> And that in the good ground: these are they who in an upright and good heart, having heard the word, retain &#91;it&#93;, and bear fruit in continuance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 11:33; Matthew 5:15–16; Mark 4:21–22"> And no one having lighted a lamp covers it with a vessel, or puts &#91;it&#93; under a bed; but he puts &#91;it&#93; on a lampstand, that those coming in may see the light,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 12:2–3; Matthew 10:26; Mark 4:22"> for nothing is secret, that will not become visible, nor hid, that will not be known and become visible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:12; Luke 19:26; Mark 4:23–25"> See, therefore, how you hear, for whoever may have, there will be given to him, and whoever may not have, also what he seems to have will be taken from him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 12:46–50; Mark 3:31–35; Mark 3:21"> And there came to Him His mother and brothers, and they were not able to get to Him because of the multitude,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 1:14; Mark 6:3; Matthew 13:55–56"> and it was told Him, saying, “Your mother and Your brothers stand outside, wishing to see You”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 11:27–28; James 1:22; John 13:17"> and He answering said to them, “My mother and My brothers! They are those who are hearing the word of God, and doing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 8:23–27; Mark 4:35–41; Mark 6:45"> And it came to pass, on one of the days, that He Himself went into a boat with His disciples, and He said to them, “We may go over to the other side of the lake”; and they set forth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 93:3–4; Psalms 124:2–4; Psalms 148:8"> and as they are sailing He fell deeply asleep, and there came down a storm of wind to the lake, and they were filling, and were in peril.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 107:25–29; Luke 4:39; Psalms 104:6–9"> And having come near, they awoke Him, saying, “Master, Master, we perish!” And He, having arisen, rebuked the wind and the raging of the water, and they ceased, and there came a calm,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 8:26; Proverbs 30:4; Mark 4:40–41"> and He said to them, “Where is your faith?” And they being afraid wondered, saying to one another, “Who, then, is this, that He even commands the winds and the water, and they obey Him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 5:1–20; Matthew 8:28–34"> And they sailed down to the region of the Gadarenes that is opposite Galilee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 5:2–5; 1 Samuel 19:24; Numbers 19:16"> and He having gone forth on the land, there met Him a certain man, out of the city, who had demons for a long time, and was not clothed with a garment, and was not abiding in a house, but in the tombs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; 2 Peter 2:4; Mark 5:6–8"> and having seen Jesus, and having cried out, he fell before Him, and with a loud voice, said, “What &#91;regards&#93; me and You, Jesus, Son of God Most High? I implore You, may You not afflict me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 5:3–5; Luke 9:39; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> For He commanded the unclean spirit to come forth from the man, for many times it had caught him, and he was being bound with chains and shackles—guarded, and breaking apart the bonds he was driven by the demons into the deserts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 26:53; Luke 8:2; Matthew 8:29"> And Jesus questioned him, saying, “What is your name?” And he said, “Legion,” because many demons were entered into him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Philippians 2:10–11; Job 2:5"> and he was calling on Him that He may not command them to go away into the abyss,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3; Revelation 20:7"> and there was there a herd of many pigs feeding on the mountain, and they were calling on Him that He might permit them to enter into these, and He permitted them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; John 8:44; Luke 8:22–23"> and the demons having gone forth from the man, entered into the pigs, and the herd rushed down the steep into the lake, and were drowned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 5:14; Matthew 8:33; Matthew 28:11"> And those feeding &#91;them&#93;, having seen what was come to pass, fled, and having gone, told &#91;it&#93; to the city, and to the fields;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 10:39; Acts 22:3; 1 John 3:8"> and they came forth to see what was come to pass, and they came to Jesus, and found the man sitting, out of whom the demons had gone forth, clothed, and right-minded, at the feet of Jesus, and they were afraid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 4:24"> and those also having seen &#91;it&#93;, told them how the demoniac was saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 16:39; Luke 8:28; Luke 10:10–11"> And the whole multitude of the region of the Gadarenes asked Him to go away from them, because they were pressed with great fear, and He having entered into the boat, turned back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 9:13–16; Mark 5:18–20; Psalms 145:3–12"> And the man from whom the demons had gone forth was imploring of Him to be with Him, and Jesus sent him away, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 17:15–18; 1 Timothy 5:8; Deuteronomy 10:21"> “Return to your house, and tell how God did great things to you”; and he went away through all the city proclaiming how Jesus did great things to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 9:1; Mark 5:21; Mark 6:20"> And it came to pass, in the turning back of Jesus, the multitude received Him, for they were all looking for Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 5:22–43; Luke 13:14; Acts 18:8"> and behold, there came a man whose name &#91;is&#93; Jairus, and he was a chief of the synagogue, and having fallen at the feet of Jesus, was calling on Him to come to his house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 7:12; Luke 8:45; Job 1:18–19"> because he had an only daughter about twelve years &#91;old&#93;, and she was dying. And in His going away, the multitudes were thronging Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 5:25–26; Matthew 9:20–22; Leviticus 15:25–33"> and a woman, being with a flow of blood for twelve years, who, having spent all her living on physicians, was not able to be healed by any,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Mark 5:27–28; Deuteronomy 22:12; Acts 19:12"> having come near behind, touched the fringe of His garment, and immediately the flow of her blood stood still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 5:30–32; Luke 9:13; Luke 5:5"> And Jesus said, “Who &#91;is&#93; it that touched Me?” And all denying, Peter and those with him said, “Master, the multitudes press You, and throng &#91;You&#93;, and You say, Who &#91;is&#93; it that touched Me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 5:17; 1 Peter 2:9"> And Jesus said, “Someone touched Me, for I knew power having gone forth from Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 5:33; Luke 17:15–16; 1 Corinthians 2:3"> And the woman, having seen that she was not hid, trembling, came, and having fallen before Him, for what cause she touched Him declared to Him before all the people, and how she was healed instantly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Matthew 9:22; Hebrews 4:2"> and He said to her, “Take courage, daughter, your faith has saved you, be going on to peace.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 5:35–43; Isaiah 7:12; Luke 7:6"> While He is yet speaking, there comes a certain one from the chief of the synagogue’s &#91;house&#93;, saying to him, “Your daughter has died, do not harass the Teacher”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 11:40; John 11:25; Mark 5:36"> and Jesus having heard, answered him, saying, “Do not be afraid, only believe, and she will be saved.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Mark 14:33; Matthew 6:5–6; 1 Kings 17:19–23"> And having come into the house, He permitted no one to go in, except Peter, and James, and John, and the father of the child, and the mother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 23:27; John 11:4; Zechariah 12:10"> and they were all weeping, and beating themselves for her, and He said, “Do not weep, she did not die, but sleeps”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Luke 16:14; Isaiah 53:3; John 11:39"> and they were deriding Him, knowing that she died;</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; John 11:43; Mark 1:31"> and He having put all forth outside, and having taken hold of her hand, called, saying, “Child, arise”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 11:44; 1 Kings 17:21–23; Mark 5:43"> and her spirit came back, and she arose immediately, and He directed that there be given to her to eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Luke 5:14"> and her parents were amazed, but He charged them to say to no one what had come to pass.</span>
