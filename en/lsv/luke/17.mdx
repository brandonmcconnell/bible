<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:7; Romans 14:13; 1 Corinthians 11:19"> And He said to the disciples, “It is impossible for the stumbling blocks not to come, but woe &#91;to him&#93; through whom they come;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 9:42; Matthew 18:14; Matthew 18:3–6"> it is more profitable to him if a weighty millstone is put around his neck, and he has been cast into the sea, than that he may cause one of these little ones to stumble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 19:17; Matthew 18:21; Matthew 18:15–17"> Take heed to yourselves, and if your brother may sin in regard to you, rebuke him, and if he may change his mind, forgive him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; Matthew 18:21–22; Romans 12:20"> and if seven times in the day he may sin against you, and seven times in the day may return to you, saying, I change my mind, you will forgive him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Philippians 4:13"> And the apostles said to the LORD, “Add to us faith”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 17:20; Matthew 21:21; Mark 9:23"> and the LORD said, “If you had faith as a grain of mustard, you would have said to this sycamine, Be uprooted, and be planted in the sea, and it would have obeyed you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:11; Luke 13:15; Luke 14:5"> But who is he of you—having a servant plowing or feeding—who, to him having come in out of the field, will say, Having come, recline at once?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 12:37; Genesis 43:16; 2 Samuel 12:20"> But will not &#91;rather&#93; say to him, Prepare what I may dine, and having girded yourself around, minister to me, until I eat and drink, and after these things you will eat and drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Does he have favor to that servant because he did the things directed? I think not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:16–17; Job 22:2–3; 1 Peter 5:5–6"> So also you, when you may have done all the things directed you, say, We are unprofitable servants, because that which we owed to do we have done.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 9:51–52; John 4:3–4"> And it came to pass, in His going on to Jerusalem, that He passed through the midst of Samaria and Galilee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46; Luke 5:12; 2 Kings 7:3"> and He entering into a certain village, there ten leprous men met Him, who stood far off,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Matthew 20:30–31; Matthew 9:27"> and they lifted up the voice, saying, “Jesus, Master, deal kindly with us”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:14; Leviticus 13:1–46; 2 Kings 5:14"> and having seen &#91;them&#93;, He said to them, “Having gone on, show yourselves to the priests”; and it came to pass, in their going, they were cleansed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 30:11–12; Psalms 103:1–4; Psalms 118:18–19"> and one of them having seen that he was healed turned back, glorifying God with a loud voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 8:5–25; Matthew 10:5; Revelation 19:10"> and he fell on &#91;his&#93; face at His feet, giving thanks to Him, and he was a Samaritan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:21; Psalms 106:13; Genesis 3:9"> And Jesus answering said, “Were not the ten cleansed, and the nine—where?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 50:23; Matthew 15:24–28; Psalms 106:13"> There were none found who turned back to give glory to God, except this foreigner”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Luke 8:48; Mark 10:52"> and He said to him, “Having risen, be going on, your faith has saved you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 18:36; Luke 19:11; Daniel 2:44"> And having been questioned by the Pharisees when the Kingdom of God comes, He answered them and said, “The Kingdom of God does not come with observation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 12:28; Romans 14:17; Mark 13:21"> nor will they say, Behold, here; or, Behold, there; for behold, the Kingdom of God is within you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 5:35; Matthew 9:15; John 8:21–24"> And He said to His disciples, “Days will come when you will desire to see one of the days of the Son of Man and you will not behold &#91;it&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Matthew 24:23–26; Luke 17:21"> and they will say to you, Behold, here; or, Behold, there; you may not go away, nor follow;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 24:27; Matthew 24:30; Malachi 4:1–2"> for as the lightning is flashing out of one &#91;part&#93; under the sky &#91;and&#93; shines to the other &#91;part&#93; under the sky, so will the Son of Man also be in His day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Luke 9:22; Mark 8:31"> and first it is necessary for Him to suffer many things, and to be rejected by this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 24:37–39; Luke 17:24; Genesis 7:7–23"> And as it came to pass in the days of Noah, so will it also be in the days of the Son of Man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 12:19–20; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–3; Luke 16:19–23"> they were eating, they were drinking, they were marrying, they were given in marriage, until the day that Noah entered into the Ark, and the flood came, and destroyed all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:49–50; Genesis 18:20–21; James 5:1–5"> in like manner also, as it came to pass in the days of Lot; they were eating, they were drinking, they were buying, they were selling, they were planting, they were building;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6; Zephaniah 2:9; Jude 1:7"> and on the day Lot went forth from Sodom, He rained fire and brimstone from the sky, and destroyed all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:7; Revelation 1:7"> According to these things it will be, in the day the Son of Man is revealed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 21:21; Mark 13:14–16; Jeremiah 45:5"> in that day, he who will be on the housetop, and his vessels in the house, do not let him come down to take them away; and he in the field, in like manner, do not let him turn backward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 19:26; Hebrews 10:38–39; 2 Peter 2:18–22"> remember the wife of Lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:35–37; Matthew 10:39; John 12:25"> Whoever may seek to save his life, will lose it; and whoever may lose it, will preserve it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 24:40–41; 2 Peter 2:9; Malachi 3:16–18"> I say to you, in that night there will be two &#91;men&#93; on one bed: one will be taken, and the other will be left;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:41; Exodus 11:5; Judges 16:21"> two &#91;women&#93; will be grinding at the same place together: one will be taken, and the other will be left;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> &#91;&#91;two &#91;men&#93; will be in the field: one will be taken, and the other left.”&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 24:28; Job 39:29–30; Revelation 19:17–18"> And they answering say to Him, “Where, Lord?” And He said to them, “Where the body &#91;is&#93;, there the eagles will be gathered together.”</span>
