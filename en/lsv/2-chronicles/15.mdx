<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20; 2 Chronicles 20:14; Numbers 24:2"> And the Spirit of God has been on Azariah son of Oded,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 4:8; 2 Chronicles 24:20; 2 Chronicles 15:4"> and he goes out before Asa and says to him, “Hear me, Asa, and all Judah and Benjamin; YHWH &#91;is&#93; with you—in your being with Him, and if you seek Him, He is found by you, and if you forsake Him, He forsakes you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:9; Leviticus 10:11; 2 Chronicles 17:8–9"> and many days &#91;are&#93; to Israel without &#91;the&#93; true God, and without a teaching priest, and without law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6; Deuteronomy 4:29–30; 2 Chronicles 15:15"> and it turns back in its distress to YHWH, God of Israel, and they seek Him, and He is found by them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 5:6; Psalms 121:8; 1 Samuel 13:6"> and in those times there is no peace for him who is going out, and for him who is coming in, for many troubles &#91;are&#93; on all the inhabitants of the lands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 21:22–24; Matthew 24:7; 2 Chronicles 33:11"> and they have been beaten down, nation by nation, and city by city, for God has troubled them with every adversity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Joshua 1:7; Joshua 1:9"> and you, be strong, and do not let your hands be feeble, for there is a reward for your work.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:12; 2 Chronicles 13:19; 2 Chronicles 4:1"> And at Asa’s hearing these words, and the prophecy of Oded the prophet, he has strengthened himself, and causes the abominations to pass away out of all the land of Judah and Benjamin, and out of the cities that he has captured from the hill-country of Ephraim, and renews the altar of YHWH that &#91;is&#93; before the porch of YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:16; Acts 9:31; Zechariah 8:21–23"> and gathers all Judah and Benjamin, and the sojourners with them out of Ephraim, and Manasseh, and out of Simeon—for they have defected to him from Israel in abundance, in their seeing that his God YHWH &#91;is&#93; with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esther 8:9"> And they are gathered to Jerusalem in the third month of the fifteenth year of the reign of Asa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:13–15; Numbers 31:28–29; Numbers 31:50"> and sacrifice to YHWH on that day from the spoil they have brought in—seven hundred oxen and seven thousand sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 24:14; 2 Chronicles 23:16; Deuteronomy 10:12"> and they enter into a covenant to seek YHWH, God of their fathers, with all their heart and with all their soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:20; Psalms 115:13; 1 Kings 18:40"> and everyone who does not seek for YHWH, God of Israel, is put to death, from small to great, from man to woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:13; Psalms 81:1–4; Nehemiah 10:29"> And they swear to YHWH with a loud voice, and with shouting, and with trumpets, and with horns,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:7; 2 Chronicles 15:12; Isaiah 26:8"> and all Judah rejoices concerning the oath, for they have sworn with all their heart, and they have sought Him with all their goodwill, and He is found by them, and YHWH gives rest to them all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 15:13–24; 1 Kings 15:10; 2 Kings 23:15"> And also Maachah, mother of Asa the king—he has removed her from &#91;being&#93; mistress, in that she has made for an Asherah a horrible thing, and Asa cuts down her horrible thing, and beats &#91;it&#93; small, and burns &#91;it&#93; by the Brook of Kidron:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 14:4; 2 Kings 12:3; 1 Kings 11:4"> yet the high places have not been removed from Israel; only, the heart of Asa has been perfect all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:51; 1 Chronicles 26:20–26; 1 Kings 15:14–15"> And he brings in the sanctified things of his father, and his own sanctified things, to the house of God: silver, and gold, and vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 15:31; 1 Kings 15:33; 1 Kings 15:16–17"> And war has not been until the thirty-fifth year of the reign of Asa.</span>
