<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 8:64; Ezekiel 43:13–17; 1 Kings 9:25"> And he makes an altar of bronze: twenty cubits its length, and twenty cubits its breadth, and ten cubits its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 3:5; Zechariah 13:1; Exodus 30:18–21"> And he makes the molten sea; ten by the cubit, from its edge to its edge, round in encompassment, and its height &#91;is&#93; five by the cubit, and a line of thirty by the cubit surrounds it around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:10; Ezekiel 10:14; 1 Corinthians 9:9–10"> And the likeness of oxen &#91;is&#93; under it, encompassing it all around, ten to the cubit, surrounding the sea around; two rows of oxen are cast in its being cast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 28:19–20; Revelation 21:14; Mark 16:15"> It is standing on twelve oxen, three facing the north, and three facing the west, and three facing the south, and three facing the east, and the sea &#91;is&#93; on them above, and all their back parts &#91;are&#93; within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 7:26"> And its thickness &#91;is&#93; a handbreadth, and its lip as the work of the lip of a cup flowered with lilies; holding &#91;within&#93;—it contains three thousand baths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 7:38; Exodus 30:18–21; 1 Kings 7:40"> And he makes ten lavers, and puts five on the right, and five on the left, to wash with them; they purge the work of the burnt-offering with them; and the sea &#91;is&#93; for priests to wash with.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–40; 1 Kings 7:49; John 8:12"> And he makes the ten lampstands of gold, according to their ordinance, and places &#91;them&#93; in the temple, five on the right, and five on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 7:48; Malachi 1:12; Exodus 25:23–30"> And he makes ten tables, and places &#91;them&#93; in the temple, five on the right, and five on the left; and he makes one hundred bowls of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 6:36; 2 Kings 21:5; 1 Kings 7:12"> And he makes the court of the priests, and the great court, and doors for the court, and he has overlaid their doors with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 7:39"> And he has placed the sea on the right shoulder eastward, from toward the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 7:40–51; 1 Kings 7:14"> And Huram makes the pots, and the shovels, and the bowls, and Huram finishes to make the work that he made for King Solomon in the house of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 7:41; 2 Chronicles 3:15–17"> two pillars, and the bowls, and the crowns on the heads of the two pillars, and the two networks to cover the two bowls of the crowns that &#91;are&#93; on the heads of the pillars;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 7:20; 1 Kings 7:42; Jeremiah 52:23"> and the four hundred pomegranates for the two networks, two rows of pomegranates for the one network, to cover the two bowls of the crowns that &#91;are&#93; on the front of the pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 7:27–43"> And he has made the bases; and he has made the lavers on the bases;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> the one sea, and the twelve oxen under it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:13; 1 Kings 7:13–14; 2 Chronicles 4:11"> and the pots, and the shovels, and the forks, and all their vessels, his father Huram has made for King Solomon, for the house of YHWH, of bronze purified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:46"> The king has cast them in the circuit of the Jordan, in the thick soil of the ground, between Succoth and Zeredathah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:47; Jeremiah 52:20; 1 Chronicles 22:14"> And Solomon makes all these vessels in great abundance, that the weight of the bronze has not been searched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 9:13; Daniel 5:2–3; Exodus 25:23–30"> And Solomon makes all the vessels that &#91;are for&#93; the house of God, and the altar of gold, and the tables, and on them &#91;is&#93; Bread of the Presentation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:7; Exodus 25:31–37; 1 Kings 6:5"> and the lampstands, and their lamps, for their burning according to the ordinance, before the oracle, of gold refined;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 6:29; Exodus 37:20; 1 Kings 6:35"> and the flowers, and the lamps, and the tongs of gold—it &#91;is&#93; the perfection of gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 12:13; 2 Kings 25:14; Jeremiah 52:18"> and the snuffers, and the bowls, and the spoons, and the censers, of gold refined, and the opening of the house, its innermost doors to the Holy of Holies, and the doors of the house to the temple, of gold.</span>
