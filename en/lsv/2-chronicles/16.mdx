<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:9; 2 Chronicles 15:5; 1 Kings 12:27"> In the thirty-sixth year of the reign of Asa, Baasha king of Israel has come up against Judah, and builds Ramah, so as not to permit any going out and coming in to Asa king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 12:18; 2 Chronicles 28:21; 2 Kings 18:15"> And Asa brings out silver and gold from the treasures of the house of YHWH and of the house of the king, and sends &#91;them&#93; to Ben-Hadad king of Aram, who is dwelling in Damascus, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 15:4; 2 Chronicles 18:3; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> “A covenant &#91;is&#93; between me and you, and between my father and your father, behold, I have sent silver and gold to you; go, break your covenant with Baasha king of Israel, and he goes up from off me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 2 Chronicles 17:12; 1 Kings 15:20"> And Ben-Hadad listens to King Asa, and sends the heads of the forces that he has to cities of Israel, and they strike Ijon, and Dan, and Abel-Maim, and all the stores, cities of Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And it comes to pass, at Baasha’s hearing, that he ceases from building Ramah, and lets his work rest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:17; Joshua 15:38; Isaiah 10:29"> and Asa the king has taken all Judah, and they carry away the stones of Ramah and its wood that Baasha has built &#91;with&#93;, and he builds Geba and Mizpah with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:2; 1 Kings 16:1; Psalms 146:3–6"> And at that time Hanani the seer has come to Asa king of Judah and says to him, “Because of your leaning on the king of Aram, and you have not leaned on your God YHWH, therefore the force of the king of Aram has escaped from your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:3; 2 Chronicles 13:16; 2 Chronicles 16:7"> Did the Cushim and the Lubim not become a very great force for multitude, for chariot, and for horsemen? And in your leaning on YHWH He gave them into your hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Proverbs 15:3; Jeremiah 16:17"> for YHWH—His eyes go to and fro in all the earth, to show Himself strong &#91;for&#93; a people whose heart &#91;is&#93; perfect toward Him; you have been foolish concerning this, because from now on there are wars with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:26; Acts 16:23–24; 2 Samuel 12:13"> And Asa is angry at the seer, and puts him &#91;in&#93; the house of stocks, for &#91;he is&#93; in a rage with him for this; and Asa oppresses &#91;some&#93; of the people at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:15; 2 Chronicles 9:29; 2 Chronicles 35:27"> And behold, the matters of Asa, the first and the last, behold, they are written on the scroll of the kings of Judah and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; 2 Chronicles 28:22; 1 Chronicles 10:14"> And Asa is diseased—in the thirty-ninth year of his reign—in his feet, until his disease is excessive; and also in his disease he has not sought YHWH, but among physicians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 15:24"> And Asa lies with his fathers, and dies in the forty-first year of his reign,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 50:2; 2 Chronicles 21:19; Jeremiah 34:5"> and they bury him in &#91;one of&#93; his graves that he had prepared for himself in the City of David, and they cause him to lie on a bed that &#91;one&#93; has filled &#91;with&#93; spices, and various kinds of mixtures, with perfumed work; and they burn a very great burning for him.</span>
