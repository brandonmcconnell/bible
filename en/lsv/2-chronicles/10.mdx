<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:6; Joshua 20:7; 1 Chronicles 12:38"> And Rehoboam goes to Shechem, for all of Israel has come &#91;to&#93; Shechem to cause him to reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 11:40; 1 Kings 12:2; 1 Kings 11:28"> And it comes to pass, at Jeroboam son of Nebat—who &#91;is&#93; in Egypt because he has fled from the face of Solomon the king—hearing, that Jeroboam turns back out of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 12:3"> and they send and call for him, and Jeroboam comes in, and all of Israel, and they speak to Rehoboam, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 12:4; Exodus 2:23; Matthew 11:29–30"> “Your father made our yoke sharp, and now, make light &#91;some&#93; of the sharp service of your father, and &#91;some&#93; of his heavy yoke that he put on us, and we serve you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 12:5; Proverbs 3:28"> And he says to them, “Yet three days—then return to me”; and the people go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 32:7; Proverbs 19:20; Proverbs 27:10"> And King Rehoboam consults with the aged men who have been standing before his father Solomon in his being alive, saying, “How are you counseling to answer this people?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Genesis 49:21; 1 Kings 12:7"> And they speak to him, saying, “If you become good to this people, and have been pleased with them, and spoken to them good words, then they have been servants to you &#91;for&#93; all the days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:14; Proverbs 1:25; Ecclesiastes 10:16"> And he forsakes the counsel of the aged men that they counseled him, and consults with the boys who have grown up with him, those standing before him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 10:6; 2 Samuel 17:5–6; 1 Kings 22:6–8"> and he says to them, “What are you counseling, and we answer this people that have spoken to me, saying, Make light &#91;some&#93; of the yoke that your father put on us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 29:23; Proverbs 13:16; 2 Samuel 17:7–13"> And the boys who have grown up with him speak with him, saying, “Thus you say to the people who have spoken to you, saying, Your father made our yoke heavy, and you, make light &#91;some&#93; of our yoke; thus you say to them, My little finger is thicker than the loins of my father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; Isaiah 47:6; Revelation 9:10"> and now, my father laid a heavy yoke on you, and I add to your yoke; my father disciplined you with whips, and I—with scorpions.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 12:12–15; 2 Chronicles 10:5"> And Jeroboam comes in, and all the people, to Rehoboam on the third day, as the king spoke, saying, “Return to me on the third day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 10:8; Proverbs 19:27; 1 Samuel 25:10–11"> And the king answers them sharply, and King Rehoboam forsakes the counsel of the aged men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 12:5; Ecclesiastes 10:16"> and speaks to them according to the counsel of the boys, saying, “My father made your yoke heavy, and I add to it; my father disciplined you with whips, and I—with scorpions.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 11:29–39; 1 Samuel 2:25; 2 Chronicles 25:16–20"> And the king has not listened to the people, for the revolution has been from God, for the sake of YHWH’s establishing His word that He spoke by the hand of Abijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam son of Nebat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:1; 2 Chronicles 10:19; Psalms 76:10"> And all Israel have seen that the king has not listened to them, and the people send back &#91;word to&#93; the king, saying, “What portion do we have in David? Indeed, there is no inheritance in a son of Jesse; &#91;go&#93;, each to your tents, O Israel! Now see your house, O David!” And all &#91;in&#93; Israel go to their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:1; 1 Kings 12:17; 1 Kings 11:36"> As for the sons of Israel who are dwelling in the cities of Judah—Rehoboam reigns over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 5:14; 1 Kings 4:6; Acts 7:57–58"> And King Rehoboam sends Hadoram, who &#91;is&#93; over the tribute, and the sons of Israel cast stones at him, and he dies; and King Rehoboam has strengthened himself to go up into a chariot to flee to Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 12:19–20; Ezra 9:7; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> and Israel transgresses against the house of David to this day.</span>
