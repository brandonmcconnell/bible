<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:31; 1 Kings 2:10; 1 Kings 14:31"> And Abijah lies with his fathers, and they bury him in the City of David, and his son Asa reigns in his stead. In his days the land was quiet &#91;for&#93; ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:11; 2 Chronicles 31:20; 1 Kings 15:14"> And Asa does that which is good and that which is right in the eyes of his God YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:5; Exodus 34:13; 2 Kings 23:14"> and turns aside the altars of the stranger, and the high places, and breaks the standing-pillars, and cuts down the Asherim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:19; Psalms 119:10; Psalms 101:2–8"> and commands to Judah to seek YHWH, God of their fathers, and to do the Law and the command;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:4; 2 Chronicles 34:7"> and he turns aside the high places and the images out of all cities of Judah, and the kingdom is quiet before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:15; 1 Chronicles 22:9; Job 34:29"> And he builds cities of bulwarks in Judah, for the land has quiet; and there is no war with him in these years, because YHWH has given rest to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; 2 Chronicles 14:6; Jeremiah 29:12–14"> And he says to Judah, “Let us build these cities, and surround &#91;them&#93; with wall, and towers, double gates, and bars, while the land &#91;is&#93; before us, because we have sought our God YHWH, we have sought, and He gives rest to us all around”; and they build and prosper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:3; 2 Chronicles 17:14–19; 2 Chronicles 11:1"> And Asa has a force of three hundred thousand from Judah carrying buckler and spear, and two hundred and eighty thousand from Benjamin carrying shield and treading bow; all these &#91;are&#93; mighty men of valor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:8; 2 Chronicles 12:2–3; 2 Chronicles 11:8"> And Zerah the Cushite comes out to them with a force of one million &#91;men&#93; and three hundred chariots, and he comes to Mareshah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 1:17; Joshua 19:4"> and Asa goes out before him, and they set in array &#91;for&#93; battle in the Valley of Zephathah at Mareshah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:19; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Psalms 22:5"> And Asa calls to his God YHWH and says, “YHWH, it is nothing for You to help, between the mighty and those who have no power; help us, O YHWH, our God, for we have leaned on You, and in Your Name we have come against this multitude; O YHWH, You &#91;are&#93; our God; do not let mortal man prevail against You!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:15; Psalms 136:17–18; Deuteronomy 28:7"> And YHWH strikes the Cushim before Asa and before Judah, and the Cushim flee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:19; Genesis 26:1; Genesis 20:1"> and Asa and the people who &#91;are&#93; with him pursue them even to Gerar, and there falls &#91;many&#93; from the Cushim, for they have no preserving, because they have been broken before YHWH and before His camp; and &#91;those of Judah&#93; carry away very much spoil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:10; Genesis 35:5; 2 Chronicles 20:29"> and strike all the cities around Gerar, for a fear of YHWH has been on them, and they spoil all the cities, for abundant spoil has been in them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:30–47; Numbers 31:9; 1 Samuel 30:20"> and they have also struck tents of livestock, and they capture sheep in abundance, and camels, and return to Jerusalem.</span>
