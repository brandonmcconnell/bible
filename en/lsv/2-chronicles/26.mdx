<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:1; Matthew 1:8–9; 2 Chronicles 33:25"> And all the people of Judah take Uzziah (and he &#91;is&#93; a son of sixteen years), and cause him to reign instead of his father Amaziah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 16:6; 2 Chronicles 25:28; 2 Chronicles 25:23"> He has built Eloth, and restores it to Judah after the king’s lying with his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 15:2–3; Amos 1:1; Hosea 1:1"> Uzziah &#91;is&#93; a son of sixteen years in his reigning, and he has reigned fifty-two years in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother &#91;is&#93; Jecholiah of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:2"> And he does that which is right in the eyes of YHWH, according to all that his father Amaziah did,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; 2 Chronicles 24:2; 1 Chronicles 22:13"> and he is as one seeking God in the days of Zechariah who has understanding in visions of God: and in the days of his seeking YHWH, God has caused him to prosper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:29; 1 Samuel 5:6; 1 Samuel 5:1"> And he goes forth and fights with the Philistines, and breaks down the wall of Gath, and the wall of Jabneh, and the wall of Ashdod, and builds cities &#91;around&#93; Ashdod, and among the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:16; 1 Chronicles 5:20; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> And God helps him against the Philistines, and against the Arabians who are dwelling in Gur-Ba‘al and the Mehunim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:2; 2 Chronicles 17:11; Genesis 19:38"> And the Ammonites give a present to Uzziah, and his name goes to the entering in of Egypt, for he strengthened himself greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:13; 2 Chronicles 25:23; 2 Kings 14:13"> And Uzziah builds towers in Jerusalem, by the Corner Gate, and by the Valley Gate, and by the angle, and strengthens them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 26:18–21; Isaiah 29:17; 2 Kings 3:4"> and he builds towers in the wilderness, and digs many wells, for he had much livestock, both in the low country and in the plain, farmers and vinedressers in the mountains, and in Carmel; for he was a lover of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 5:2"> And Uzziah has a force making war, going forth to the host, by troops, in the number of their reckoning by the hand of Jeiel the scribe and Masseiah the officer, by the hand of Hananiah &#91;one&#93; of the heads of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The whole number of heads of the fathers of the mighty men of valor &#91;is&#93; two thousand and six hundred;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:5; 2 Chronicles 14:8; 2 Chronicles 11:1"> and by their hand &#91;is&#93; the force of the host—three hundred thousand and seven thousand and five hundred warriors, with mighty power to give help to the king against the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 20:16; 1 Samuel 17:49"> And Uzziah prepares for them, for all the host, shields, and spears, and helmets, and coats of mail, and bows, even to stones of the slings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 31:4; Matthew 4:24; 2 Chronicles 2:7"> And he makes inventions in Jerusalem—a device of an inventor—to be on the towers, and on the corners, to shoot with arrows and with great stones, and his name goes out to a distance, for he has been wonderfully helped until he has been strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:25; 2 Chronicles 25:19; Proverbs 16:18"> And at his being strong his heart has been high to destruction, and he trespasses against his God YHWH, and goes into the temple of YHWH to make incense on the altar of incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:10; 1 Chronicles 26:6; 1 Chronicles 12:28"> And Azariah the priest goes in after him, and with him eighty priests of YHWH, sons of valor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 30:7–8; Numbers 18:7; Jeremiah 13:18"> and they stand up against Uzziah the king, and say to him, “&#91;It is&#93; not for you, O Uzziah, to make incense to YHWH, but for priests, sons of Aaron, who are sanctified to make incense; go forth from the sanctuary, for you have trespassed, indeed, for you &#91;will&#93; not &#91;have&#93; honor from YHWH God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 12:10; 2 Chronicles 25:16; 2 Kings 5:25–27"> And Uzziah is angry, and in his hand &#91;is&#93; a censer to make incense, and in his being angry with the priests—the leprosy has risen in his forehead, before the priests, in the house of YHWH, from beside the altar of incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:22; Esther 6:12; Leviticus 14:34"> And Azariah the head priest looks toward him, and all the priests, and behold, he &#91;is&#93; leprous in his forehead, and they hurry him there, and also he himself has hurried to go out, for YHWH has plagued him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 13:46; Numbers 12:15; 2 Kings 7:3"> And Uzziah the king is a leper to the day of his death, and inhabits a separate house—a leper, for he has been cut off from the house of YHWH, and his son Jotham &#91;is&#93; over the house of the king, judging the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; Isaiah 6:1; 2 Chronicles 9:29"> And the rest of the matters of Uzziah, the first and the last, Isaiah son of Amoz the prophet has written;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:27; 2 Chronicles 21:20; 2 Chronicles 26:18"> and Uzziah lies with his fathers, and they bury him with his fathers in the field of the burying-place that the kings have, for they said, “He &#91;is&#93; a leper”; and his son Jotham reigns in his stead.</span>
