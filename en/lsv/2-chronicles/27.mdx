<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:12; Isaiah 1:1; Matthew 1:9"> Jotham &#91;is&#93; a son of twenty-five years in his reigning, and he has reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother &#91;is&#93; Jerushah daughter of Zadok.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 15:34–35; Acts 5:13; 2 Chronicles 26:4"> And he does that which is right in the eyes of YHWH, according to all that his father Uzziah did; only, he has not come into the temple of YHWH; and again the people are doing corruptly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:14; Nehemiah 3:26–27; Jeremiah 20:2"> He has built the Upper Gate of the house of YHWH, and in the wall of Ophel he has built abundantly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:5–10; 2 Chronicles 26:9–10; Joshua 14:12–13"> and he has built cities in the hill-country of Judah, and he has built palaces and towers in the forests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 11:4–33; 2 Samuel 10:1–14; Jeremiah 49:1–6"> And he has fought with the king of the sons of Ammon, and prevails over them, and the sons of Ammon give to him in that year one hundred talents of silver, and ten thousand cors of wheat, and ten thousand of barley; the sons of Ammon have returned this to him both in the second year and in the third.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:5; 2 Chronicles 19:3"> And Jotham strengthens himself, for he has prepared his ways before his God YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:22–23; 2 Kings 15:36; 2 Chronicles 32:32–33"> And the rest of the matters of Jotham, and all his battles, and his ways, behold, they are written on the scroll of the kings of Israel and Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 27:1"> He was a son of twenty-five years in his reigning, and he has reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 15:38"> and Jotham lies with his fathers, and they bury him in the City of David, and his son Ahaz reigns in his stead.</span>
