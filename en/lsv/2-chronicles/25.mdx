<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 14:1–6"> Amaziah &#91;was&#93; a son of twenty-five years &#91;when&#93; he has reigned, and he has reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother &#91;is&#93; Jehoaddan of Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:14; James 4:8; Acts 8:21"> and he does that which is right in the eyes of YHWH—only, not with a perfect heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:25–26; 2 Kings 14:5–22; Genesis 9:5–6"> And it comes to pass, when the kingdom has been strong on him, that he slays his servants, those striking his father the king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:16; Ezekiel 18:20; 2 Kings 14:5–6"> and he has not put their sons to death, but &#91;did&#93; as is written in the Law, in the Scroll of Moses, whom YHWH commanded, saying, “Fathers do not die for sons, and sons do not die for fathers, but they each die for his own sin.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 1:3; 2 Chronicles 17:14–18; 2 Chronicles 11:1"> And Amaziah gathers Judah and appoints them according to the house of the fathers, for heads of the thousands and for heads of the hundreds, for all Judah and Benjamin; and he inspects them from a son of twenty years and upward, and finds them &#91;to be&#93; three hundred thousand chosen ones, going forth to the host, holding spear and buckler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he hires out of Israel one hundred thousand mighty men of valor, with one hundred talents of silver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:2; 1 Kings 12:28; Hosea 9:13"> and a man of God has come to him, saying, “O king, the host of Israel does not go with you; for YHWH is not with Israel—all the sons of Ephraim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:11; 2 Chronicles 20:6; Philippians 4:19"> but if you are going, do &#91;it&#93;, be strong for battle, God causes you to stumble before an enemy, for there is power in God to help, and to cause to stumble.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 10:22; Deuteronomy 8:18"> And Amaziah says to the man of God, “But what to do about the hundred talents that I have given to the troop of Israel?” And the man of God says, “YHWH has more to give to you than this.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 12:24; 2 Samuel 19:43; Proverbs 29:22"> And Amaziah separates them, of the troop that has come to him from Ephraim, to go to their own place; and their anger burns mightily against Judah, and they return to their place in the heat of anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 14:7; Psalms 60:1; 2 Samuel 8:13"> And Amaziah has strengthened himself, and leads his people, and goes to the Valley of Salt, and strikes ten thousand of the sons of Seir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:10; 1 Chronicles 20:3; 2 Samuel 12:31"> And the sons of Judah have taken captive ten thousand alive, and they bring them to the top of the rock, and cast them from the top of the rock, and all of them have been broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:5; 1 Kings 16:29; 1 Kings 9:17"> And the sons of the troop that Amaziah has sent back from going with him to battle—they rush against cities of Judah, from Samaria even to Beth-Horon, and strike three thousand of them, and seize much prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:23; Deuteronomy 7:25; Deuteronomy 7:5"> And it comes to pass after the coming in of Amaziah from striking the Edomites, that he brings in the gods of the sons of Seir, and establishes them for gods for himself, and bows himself before them, and he makes incense to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 96:5; 2 Chronicles 25:11–12; 2 Chronicles 25:7"> And the anger of YHWH burns against Amaziah, and He sends a prophet to him, and he says to him, “Why have you sought the gods of the people that have not delivered their people out of your hand?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:20–21; Jeremiah 29:26; 2 Chronicles 18:25"> And it comes to pass, in his speaking to him, that he says to him, “Have we appointed you for a counselor to the king? For you &#91;must&#93; cease; why do they strike you?” And the prophet ceases and says, “I have known that God has counseled to destroy you, because you have done this and have not listened to my counsel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 14:8–14; 2 Chronicles 25:13; Proverbs 20:3"> And Amaziah king of Judah takes counsel, and sends to Joash son of Jehoahaz, son of Jehu, king of Israel, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; 1 Kings 4:33; Psalms 80:13"> “Come, we look one another in the face.” And Joash king of Israel sends to Amaziah king of Judah, saying, “The thorn that &#91;is&#93; in Lebanon has sent to the cedar that &#91;is&#93; in Lebanon, saying, Give your daughter to my son for a wife; and a beast of the field that &#91;is&#93; in Lebanon passes by and treads down the thorn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:16; 2 Chronicles 32:25; Proverbs 18:6"> You have said, Behold, I have struck Edom; and your heart has lifted you up to boast; now, abide in your house, why do you stir yourself up in evil, that you have fallen, you and Judah with you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:7; 1 Kings 12:15; 1 Peter 2:8"> And Amaziah has not listened, for it &#91;is&#93; from God in order to give them into &#91;their&#93; hand, because they have sought the gods of Edom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19–20; 1 Samuel 6:9; Joshua 21:16"> and Joash king of Israel goes up, and they look one another in the face, he and Amaziah king of Judah, in Beth-Shemesh, that &#91;is&#93; Judah’s,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:10; 2 Chronicles 28:5–6; 1 Kings 22:36"> and Judah is struck before Israel, and they flee—each to his tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:17; Nehemiah 8:16; 2 Chronicles 26:9"> And Joash king of Israel has caught Amaziah king of Judah, son of Joash, son of Jehoahaz, in Beth-Shemesh, and brings him to Jerusalem, and breaks down in the wall of Jerusalem from the Gate of Ephraim to the Corner Gate—four hundred cubits,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:15; 2 Chronicles 12:9; 2 Kings 14:14"> and &#91;takes&#93; all the gold, and the silver, and all the vessels that are found in the house of God with Obed-Edom, and the treasures of the house of the king, and the sons of the pledges, and turns back to Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 14:17–22"> And Amaziah son of Joash, king of Judah, lives after the death of Joash son of Jehoahaz, king of Israel, fifteen years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:34; 2 Kings 14:15"> and the rest of the matters of Amaziah, the first and the last, behold, are they not written on the scrolls of the kings of Judah and Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:25; Joshua 10:31; 2 Kings 14:19"> And from the time that Amaziah has turned aside from after YHWH—they make a conspiracy against him in Jerusalem, and he flees to Lachish, and they send after him to Lachish, and put him to death there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 14:20"> and lift him up on the horses, and bury him with his fathers in the city of Judah.</span>
