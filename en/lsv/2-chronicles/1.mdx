<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:25; 1 Kings 2:12; Genesis 39:2"> And Solomon son of David strengthens himself over his kingdom, and his God YHWH &#91;is&#93; with him, and makes him exceedingly great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:1; 1 Chronicles 27:1; 2 Chronicles 30:2"> And Solomon says to all Israel, to heads of the thousands, and of the hundreds, and to the ones judging, and to every honorable one of all Israel, heads of the fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:29; 1 Chronicles 16:39; Leviticus 1:1"> and they go—Solomon, and all the assembly with him—to the high place that &#91;is&#93; in Gibeon, for God’s Tent of Meeting has been there, that Moses, servant of YHWH, made in the wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:17; 1 Chronicles 15:25–28; 2 Samuel 6:2"> but David had the Ark of God brought up from Kirjath-Jearim to the &#91;place&#93; David had prepared for it, for he stretched out a tent for it in Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 31:2; Exodus 38:1–7; 1 Chronicles 2:19–20"> and the altar of bronze that Bezaleel son of Uri, son of Hur made, he put before the Dwelling Place of YHWH; and Solomon and the assembly seek Him &#91;there&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 3:4; 1 Kings 8:63; 1 Chronicles 29:21"> And Solomon goes up there, on the altar of bronze, before YHWH, that &#91;is&#93; at the Tent of Meeting, and causes one thousand burnt-offerings to ascend on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; 1 John 5:14–15; John 16:23"> In that night God has appeared to Solomon and says to him, “Ask—what do I give to you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:5; Isaiah 55:3; 2 Samuel 22:51–1"> And Solomon says to God, “You have done great kindness with my father David, and have caused me to reign in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12–16; Genesis 13:16; Psalms 132:11–12"> Now, O YHWH God, Your word with my father David is steadfast, for You have caused me to reign over a people &#91;as&#93; numerous as the dust of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 3:9; Proverbs 4:7; Proverbs 2:2–6"> now, give wisdom and knowledge to me, and I go out before this people, and I come in, for who judges this—Your great people?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 3:17; Proverbs 23:7; 1 Samuel 16:7"> And God says to Solomon, “Because that this has been with your heart, and you have not asked for riches, wealth, and honor, and the life of those hating you, and also have not asked for many days, and ask for wisdom and knowledge for yourself, so that you judge My people over which I have caused you to reign—</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 1:5; 2 Chronicles 9:22; Matthew 6:33"> the wisdom and the knowledge is given to you, and riches and wealth and honor I &#91;also&#93; give to you, that there has not been so to the kings who &#91;are&#93; before you, and after you it is not so.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:3; 1 Kings 4:24–25"> And Solomon comes in to Jerusalem &#91;from&#93; the high place that &#91;is&#93; in Gibeon, from before the Tent of Meeting, and reigns over Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 4:26; 1 Kings 10:26–29; 2 Chronicles 9:25"> and Solomon gathers chariots and horsemen, and he has one thousand and four hundred chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen, and he places them in the cities of the chariots, and with the king in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:27; Isaiah 9:10; 1 Kings 10:27–29"> And the king makes the silver and the gold in Jerusalem as stones, and the cedars he made as sycamores that &#91;are&#93; in the low country, for abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:28; 1 Kings 10:28–29"> And the source of the horses that &#91;are&#93; for Solomon &#91;is&#93; from Egypt and from Keva; merchants of the king take &#91;them&#93; from Keva at a price,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 10:29"> and they come up, and bring out a chariot from Egypt for six hundred pieces of silver, and a horse for one hundred and fifty, and so for all the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Aram—they bring &#91;them&#93; out by their hand.</span>
