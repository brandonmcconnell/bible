<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 27:13–14; Psalms 37:7; James 5:7–11"> TO THE OVERSEER. A PSALM OF DAVID. I have diligently expected YHWH, &#124;&#124; And He inclines to me, and hears my cry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:23; Psalms 119:133; Psalms 27:5"> And He causes me to come up &#124;&#124; From a pit of desolation—from mire of mud, &#124;&#124; And He raises up my feet on a rock, &#124;&#124; He is establishing my steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 33:3; Psalms 103:1–5; Psalms 34:1–6"> And He puts a new song in my mouth, “Praise to our God.” Many see and fear, and trust in YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; Psalms 84:11–12; Psalms 34:8"> O the blessedness of the man &#124;&#124; Who has made YHWH his trust, &#124;&#124; And has not turned to the proud, &#124;&#124; And those turning aside to lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:11; Psalms 139:17–18; Exodus 15:11"> You have done much, my God YHWH; Your wonders and Your thoughts toward us, &#124;&#124; There is none to arrange to You, &#124;&#124; I declare and speak: They have been more than to be numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5–12; Isaiah 1:11; 1 Samuel 15:22"> Sacrifice and present You have not desired, &#124;&#124; But a body You have prepared for me, &#124;&#124; Burnt and sin-offering You have not asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:7–9; Luke 24:44; John 5:39"> Then I said, “Behold, I have come, &#124;&#124; In the roll of the scroll it is written of me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:22; John 4:34; Jeremiah 15:16"> I have delighted to do Your pleasure, my God, &#124;&#124; And Your law &#91;is&#93; within my heart.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 22:25; Mark 16:15–16; Psalms 22:22"> I have proclaimed tidings of righteousness &#124;&#124; In the great assembly, &#124;&#124; Behold, I do not restrain my lips, &#124;&#124; O YHWH, You have known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 1:16–17; Acts 20:20–21; Romans 10:9–10"> I have not concealed Your righteousness &#124;&#124; In the midst of my heart, &#124;&#124; I have told of Your faithfulness and Your salvation, &#124;&#124; I have not hidden Your kindness and Your truth, &#124;&#124; To the great assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 23:6; Psalms 43:3; Psalms 57:3"> You, O YHWH, do not restrain Your mercies from me, &#124;&#124; Your kindness and Your truth continually keep me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 38:4; Psalms 73:26; Psalms 69:4"> For innumerable evils have surrounded me, &#124;&#124; My iniquities have overtaken me, &#124;&#124; And I have not been able to see; They have been more than the hairs of my head, &#124;&#124; And my heart has forsaken me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 25:17–18; Psalms 70:1–5; Psalms 38:22"> Be pleased, O YHWH, to deliver me, &#124;&#124; O YHWH, make haste for my help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Psalms 35:4; Psalms 71:13"> They are ashamed and confounded together, &#124;&#124; Who are seeking my soul to destroy it, &#124;&#124; They are turned backward, &#124;&#124; And are ashamed, who are desiring my evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 35:21; Psalms 69:24–25; Psalms 35:25"> They are desolate because of their shame, &#124;&#124; Who are saying to me, “Aha, aha.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 35:27; Psalms 68:3; Acts 19:17"> All seeking You rejoice and are glad in You, &#124;&#124; Those loving Your salvation continually say, “YHWH is magnified.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 70:5; Hebrews 13:6; Isaiah 41:17"> And I &#91;am&#93; poor and needy, &#124;&#124; The Lord devises for me. You &#91;are&#93; my help and my deliverer, O my God, do not linger.</span>
