<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 70:5; Psalms 71:12; Psalms 40:13"> A PSALM OF DAVID. O YHWH, I have called You, hurry to me, &#124;&#124; Give ear &#91;to&#93; my voice when I call to You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 8:3–4; Revelation 5:8; 1 Timothy 2:8"> My prayer is prepared—incense before You, &#124;&#124; The lifting up of my hands—the evening present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 1:26; Psalms 39:1; Micah 7:5"> Set, O YHWH, a watch for my mouth, &#124;&#124; Watch over the door of my lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Psalms 119:36; Isaiah 63:17"> Do not incline my heart to an evil thing, &#124;&#124; To habitually do actions in wickedness, &#124;&#124; Working iniquity with men, &#124;&#124; Indeed, I do not eat of their pleasant things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:5; Revelation 3:19; Galatians 6:1"> The righteous beat me &#91;in&#93; kindness. And reprove me, &#124;&#124; My head does not disallow oil of the head, &#124;&#124; For my prayer &#91;is&#93; still about their distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:12; 1 Chronicles 12:38; 1 Chronicles 11:1–3"> Their judges have been released by the sides of a rock, &#124;&#124; And they have heard my sayings, &#124;&#124; For they have been pleasant.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 140:5; Psalms 119:110; Luke 20:20"> Keep me from the trap they laid for me, &#124;&#124; Even snares of workers of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:8; Psalms 140:9; Psalms 64:7–8"> The wicked fall in their dragnets together, until I pass over!</span>
