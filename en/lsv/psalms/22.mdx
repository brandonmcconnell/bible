<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 15:34; Matthew 27:46; Hebrews 5:7"> TO THE OVERSEER. &#91;SET&#93; ON “DOE OF THE MORNING.” A PSALM OF DAVID. My God, My God, why have You forsaken Me? Far from My salvation, &#124;&#124; The words of My roaring?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Luke 18:7; Luke 22:41–46"> My God, I call by day, and You do not answer, &#124;&#124; And by night, and am not silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 50:23; Deuteronomy 10:21; Isaiah 6:3"> And You &#91;are&#93; holy, &#124;&#124; Sitting—the Praise of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 14:31; Exodus 14:13–14; Hebrews 11:8–32"> In You our fathers trusted; They trusted, and You deliver them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Isaiah 49:23; Romans 9:33"> To You they cried, and were delivered, &#124;&#124; In You they trusted, and were not disappointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Job 25:6; Isaiah 49:7"> And I &#91;am&#93; a worm, and no man, &#124;&#124; A reproach of man, and despised of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 15:29; Psalms 109:25; Matthew 27:39–40"> All beholding Me mock at Me, &#124;&#124; They make free with the lip—shake the head,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 27:42–43; Mark 15:30–32; Psalms 91:14"> “Roll to YHWH, He delivers Him, &#124;&#124; He delivers Him, for He delighted in Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 71:6; Isaiah 49:1–2; Psalms 71:17"> For You &#91;are&#93; He bringing Me forth from the womb, &#124;&#124; Causing Me to trust, &#124;&#124; On the breasts of My mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 1:15; Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 49:1"> On You I have been cast from the womb, &#124;&#124; From the belly of My mother You &#91;are&#93; My God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 71:12; Psalms 72:12; Psalms 10:1"> Do not be far from Me, &#124;&#124; For adversity is near, for there is no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 4:1–3; Psalms 68:30; Acts 4:27"> Many bulls have surrounded Me, &#124;&#124; Mighty ones of Bashan have surrounded Me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Psalms 35:21; Lamentations 3:46"> They have opened their mouth against Me, &#124;&#124; A lion tearing and roaring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 31:10; Daniel 5:6; Job 30:16"> I have been poured out as waters, &#124;&#124; And all my bones have separated themselves, &#124;&#124; My heart has been like wax, &#124;&#124; It is melted in the midst of My bowels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 19:28; Psalms 69:3; Psalms 69:21"> My power is dried up as an earthen vessel, &#124;&#124; And My tongue is cleaving to My jaws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 19:37; Zechariah 12:10; Matthew 27:35"> And You appoint Me to the dust of death, &#124;&#124; For dogs have surrounded Me, &#124;&#124; A company of evildoers has surrounded Me, &#124;&#124; Piercing My hands and My feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:35; Job 33:21; Matthew 27:39–41"> I count all My bones—they look expectingly, &#124;&#124; They look on Me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:35; Luke 23:34; Mark 15:24"> They apportion My garments to themselves, &#124;&#124; And they cause a lot to fall for My clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 22:11; Psalms 40:13; Psalms 40:17"> And You, O YHWH, do not be far off, &#124;&#124; O My strength, hurry to help Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:17; Zechariah 13:7; Psalms 17:13"> Deliver My soul from the sword, &#124;&#124; My only one from the paw of a dog.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17; Numbers 23:22; 1 Peter 5:8"> Save Me from the mouth of a lion &#124;&#124; And You have answered Me from the horns of the high places!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 2:11–12; Romans 8:29; Psalms 40:9–10"> I declare Your Name to My brothers, &#124;&#124; In the midst of the assembly I praise You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 135:19–20; Revelation 15:4; Psalms 107:1–2"> You who fear YHWH, praise Him, &#124;&#124; All the seed of Jacob, honor Him, &#124;&#124; And be afraid of Him, all you seed of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 50:6–9; Psalms 116:3–6"> For He has not despised, nor detested, &#124;&#124; The affliction of the afflicted, &#124;&#124; Nor has He hidden His face from Him, &#124;&#124; And in His crying to Him He hears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 40:9–10; Psalms 35:18; Psalms 111:1"> Of You My praise &#91;is&#93; in the great assembly. I complete My vows before His fearers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 69:32; Psalms 40:16; Isaiah 65:13"> The humble eat and are satisfied, &#124;&#124; Those seeking Him praise YHWH, &#124;&#124; Your heart lives forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Isaiah 45:22; Psalms 2:8"> Remember and return to YHWH, &#124;&#124; Do all the ends of the earth, &#124;&#124; And bow themselves before You, &#124;&#124; Do all families of the nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; Psalms 47:7–8; Revelation 11:15"> For to YHWH &#91;is&#93; the kingdom, &#124;&#124; And He is ruling among nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; Isaiah 26:19; Psalms 45:12"> And the fat ones of earth have eaten, &#124;&#124; And they bow themselves, &#124;&#124; All going down to dust bow before Him, &#124;&#124; And he &#91;who&#93; has not revived his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 2:13; Psalms 14:5; 1 Peter 2:9"> A seed serves Him, &#124;&#124; It is declared of the Lord to the generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 78:6; Psalms 86:9; Romans 1:17"> They come and declare His righteousness, &#124;&#124; To a people that is born, that He has made!</span>
