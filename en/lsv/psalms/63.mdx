<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 143:6; Psalms 84:2; Psalms 42:1–2"> A PSALM OF DAVID, IN HIS BEING IN THE WILDERNESS OF JUDAH. O God, You &#91;are&#93; my God, earnestly I seek You, &#124;&#124; My soul has thirsted for You, &#124;&#124; My flesh has longed for You, &#124;&#124; In a dry and weary land, without waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Psalms 105:4; Psalms 84:2–11"> So I have seen You in the sanctuary, &#124;&#124; To behold Your strength and Your glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:16; Psalms 51:15; Philippians 1:23"> Because better &#91;is&#93; Your kindness than life, &#124;&#124; My lips praise You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 104:33; Psalms 134:2; Psalms 28:2"> So I bless You in my life, &#124;&#124; I lift up my hands in Your Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 36:7–9; Isaiah 25:6; Ezra 3:11–13"> As &#91;with&#93; milk and fatness is my soul satisfied, &#124;&#124; And &#91;with&#93; singing lips my mouth praises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Psalms 119:147–148; Psalms 149:5"> If I have remembered You on my bed, &#124;&#124; I meditate on You in the watches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Psalms 61:4; Psalms 27:9"> For You have been a help to me, &#124;&#124; And I sing in the shadow of Your wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 18:35; Psalms 73:23"> My soul has cleaved after You, &#124;&#124; Your right hand has taken hold on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 55:15; Psalms 9:17; Psalms 40:14"> And they who seek my soul for desolation, &#124;&#124; Go into the lower parts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; Ezekiel 35:5; Ezekiel 39:4"> They cause him to run on the edge of the sword, &#124;&#124; They are a portion for foxes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Psalms 21:1; Isaiah 65:16"> And the king rejoices in God, &#124;&#124; Everyone swearing by Him boasts, &#124;&#124; But the mouth of those speaking lies is stopped!</span>
