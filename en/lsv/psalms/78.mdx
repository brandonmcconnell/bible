<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:4; Isaiah 55:3; Psalms 74:1"> AN INSTRUCTION OF ASAPH. Give ear, O my people, to my law, &#124;&#124; Incline your ear to sayings of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; Mark 4:34; Matthew 13:11–13"> I open my mouth with an allegory, &#124;&#124; I bring forth hidden things of old,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 44:1; Exodus 13:14–15; Exodus 12:26–27"> That we have heard and know, &#124;&#124; And our fathers have recounted to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:19; Psalms 145:4–6; Deuteronomy 4:9"> We do not hide from their sons, &#124;&#124; Recounting praises of YHWH to a later generation, &#124;&#124; And His strength, and His wonders that He has done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Ephesians 6:4; Deuteronomy 4:45"> And He raises up a testimony in Jacob, &#124;&#124; And has placed a law in Israel, &#124;&#124; That He commanded our fathers, &#124;&#124; To make them known to their sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 102:18; Joel 1:3; Esther 9:28"> So that a later generation knows, &#124;&#124; Sons who are born, rise and recount to their sons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:29; Revelation 14:12; 1 John 5:3"> And place their confidence in God, &#124;&#124; And do not forget the doings of God, &#124;&#124; But keep His commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 78:37; Exodus 32:9; 2 Kings 17:14"> And they are not like their fathers, &#124;&#124; A generation apostatizing and being rebellious, &#124;&#124; A generation—it has not prepared its heart, &#124;&#124; Nor &#91;is&#93; its spirit steadfast with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:41–44; 1 Samuel 31:1; Psalms 78:57"> Sons of Ephraim—armed bearers of bow, &#124;&#124; Have turned in a day of conflict.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:20; Deuteronomy 31:16; Nehemiah 9:26–29"> They have not kept the covenant of God, &#124;&#124; And they have refused to walk in His law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 106:13; Deuteronomy 32:18; Psalms 106:21–22"> And they forget His doings, &#124;&#124; And His wonders that He showed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Ezekiel 30:14; Isaiah 19:11"> He has done wonders before their fathers, &#124;&#124; In the land of Egypt—the field of Zoan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Exodus 15:8; Psalms 66:6"> He cleft a sea, and causes them to pass over, &#124;&#124; Indeed, He causes waters to stand as a heap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 105:39; Exodus 14:24; Exodus 13:21–22"> And leads them with a cloud by day, &#124;&#124; And with a light of fire all the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 105:41; Numbers 20:11; Exodus 17:6"> He cleaves rocks in a wilderness, &#124;&#124; And gives drink—as the great deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 20:8; Numbers 20:10–11; Deuteronomy 8:15"> And brings out streams from a rock, &#124;&#124; And causes waters to come down as rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; Hebrews 3:16–19; Deuteronomy 9:12–22"> And they still add to sin against Him, &#124;&#124; To provoke the Most High in the dry place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; Numbers 11:4; 1 Corinthians 10:9"> And they try God in their heart, &#124;&#124; To ask food for their lust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 21:5; Numbers 11:4; Exodus 16:3"> And they speak against God—they said: “Is God able to array a table in a wilderness?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; Numbers 11:21–23; Psalms 78:41"> Behold, He has struck a rock, &#124;&#124; And waters flow, indeed, streams overflow. “Also, &#91;is&#93; He able to give bread? Does He prepare flesh for His people?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jude 1:5; 1 Corinthians 10:11; Numbers 11:1–3"> Therefore YHWH has heard, &#124;&#124; And He shows Himself angry, &#124;&#124; And fire has been kindled against Jacob, &#124;&#124; And anger has also gone up against Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:18–19; Hebrews 3:12; Jude 1:5"> For they have not believed in God, &#124;&#124; Nor have they trusted in His salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Malachi 3:10; Psalms 33:9"> And He commands clouds from above, &#124;&#124; Indeed, He has opened doors of the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:4; Psalms 105:40; John 6:31–71"> And He rains manna on them to eat, &#124;&#124; Indeed, He has given grain of the heavens to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 14:20; Matthew 15:37; Psalms 103:20"> Each has eaten food of the mighty, &#124;&#124; He sent provision to them to satiety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 11:31; Psalms 135:7"> He causes an east wind to journey in the heavens, &#124;&#124; And leads a south wind by His strength,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 11:18–19; Exodus 16:12–13; Numbers 11:32"> And He rains on them flesh as dust, &#124;&#124; And as sand of the seas—winged bird,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And causes &#91;it&#93; to fall in the midst of His camp, &#124;&#124; Around His dwelling places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 106:15; Numbers 11:19–20"> And they eat, and are greatly satisfied, &#124;&#124; And He brings their desire to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 11:33–34; Luke 16:19–23; Numbers 22:20–22"> They have not been estranged from their desire, &#124;&#124; Their food &#91;is&#93; yet in their mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16"> And the anger of God has gone up against them, &#124;&#124; And He slays among their fat ones, &#124;&#124; And He caused youths of Israel to bend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 12:37; Psalms 78:22; Psalms 78:11"> With all this they have sinned again, &#124;&#124; And have not believed in His wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 14:35; Numbers 14:29; Numbers 26:64–65"> And He consumes their days in vanity, &#124;&#124; And their years in trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Numbers 21:7; Judges 3:12–15"> If He slew them, then they sought Him, &#124;&#124; And turned back, and earnestly sought God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Isaiah 41:14; Exodus 15:13"> And they remember that God &#91;is&#93; their rock, &#124;&#124; And God Most High their redeemer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; Isaiah 29:13; Hosea 11:12"> And they deceive Him with their mouth, &#124;&#124; And lie to Him with their tongue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 8:21; Psalms 78:8; Psalms 51:10"> And their heart has not been right with Him, &#124;&#124; And they have not been steadfast in His covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 48:9; Numbers 14:18–20; Ezekiel 20:17"> And He, the Merciful One, pardons iniquity, and does not destroy, &#124;&#124; And has often turned back His anger, &#124;&#124; And does not awaken all His fury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 6:3; James 4:14; Job 7:7"> And He remembers that they &#91;are&#93; flesh, &#124;&#124; A wind going on—and it does not return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; Psalms 95:8–10; Hebrews 3:15–17"> How often do they provoke Him in the wilderness, &#124;&#124; Grieve Him in the desolate place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Hebrews 3:8–11; Numbers 14:22; Acts 7:39"> Indeed, they turn back, and try God, &#124;&#124; And have limited the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Judges 8:34; Psalms 78:11; Jeremiah 32:21"> They have not remembered His hand &#124;&#124; The day He ransomed them from the adversary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:22; Exodus 4:21; Exodus 3:19–20"> When He set His signs in Egypt, &#124;&#124; And His wonders in the field of Zoan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 105:29; Revelation 16:3–6; Exodus 7:17–21"> And He turns their streams to blood, &#124;&#124; And they do not drink their floods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Exodus 8:21–24; Psalms 105:30–31; Exodus 8:2–15"> He sends among them the beetle, and it consumes them, &#124;&#124; And the frog, and it destroys them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 10:12–15; Psalms 105:34–35; Amos 7:1–2"> And gives their increase to the caterpillar, &#124;&#124; And their labor to the locust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 105:32–33; Exodus 9:18–34"> He destroys their vine with hail, &#124;&#124; And their sycamores with frost,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Exodus 9:19; Exodus 9:28"> And delivers their beasts up to the hail, &#124;&#124; And their livestock to the burning flames.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Exodus 12:13; 1 Kings 22:21–22; Lamentations 4:11"> He sends on them the fury of His anger, &#124;&#124; Wrath, and indignation, and distress—A discharge of evil messengers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:4; 2 Peter 2:4–5; Romans 8:32"> He ponders a path for His anger, &#124;&#124; He did not keep back their soul from death, &#124;&#124; Indeed, He delivered up their life to the pestilence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 105:23; Psalms 135:8; Psalms 106:22"> And He strikes every firstborn in Egypt, &#124;&#124; The first-fruit of the strong in tents of Ham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Psalms 77:20; John 10:11–42"> And causes His people to journey as a flock, &#124;&#124; And guides them as a drove in a wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Exodus 14:19–20; Exodus 15:10; Exodus 14:27–28"> And He leads them confidently, &#124;&#124; And they have not been afraid, &#124;&#124; And the sea has covered their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Exodus 15:17; Isaiah 11:9"> And He brings them to the border of His sanctuary, &#124;&#124; This mountain His right hand had acquired,</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Joshua 13:7; Psalms 44:2; Nehemiah 9:22–25"> And casts out nations from before them, &#124;&#124; And causes them to fall in the line of inheritance, &#124;&#124; And causes the tribes of Israel to dwell in their tents,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Judges 2:11–12; Deuteronomy 32:15–21; Psalms 78:40–41"> And they tempt and provoke God Most High, &#124;&#124; And have not kept His testimonies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Hosea 7:16; Ezekiel 20:27–28; Psalms 78:8"> And they turn back, &#124;&#124; And deal treacherously like their fathers, &#124;&#124; They have been turned like a deceitful bow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Leviticus 26:30; 1 Kings 12:31"> And make Him angry with their high places, &#124;&#124; And make Him zealous with their carved images,</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Psalms 106:40; Lamentations 2:7; Deuteronomy 32:19"> God has heard, and shows Himself angry. And kicks exceedingly against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; 1 Samuel 4:4–11; Jeremiah 26:6–9"> And He leaves the Dwelling Place of Shiloh, &#124;&#124; The tent He had placed among men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Psalms 132:8; 1 Samuel 4:21–2; 1 Samuel 4:17"> And He gives His strength to captivity, &#124;&#124; And His beauty into the hand of an adversary,</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Psalms 89:38; Isaiah 64:9; 1 Samuel 4:10–11"> And delivers His people up to the sword, &#124;&#124; And showed Himself angry with His inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Jeremiah 16:9; Deuteronomy 32:22"> Fire has consumed His young men, &#124;&#124; And His virgins have not been praised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:17; Job 27:15; Ezekiel 24:23"> His priests have fallen by the sword, &#124;&#124; And their widows do not weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6; Isaiah 51:9"> And the Lord wakes as a sleeper, &#124;&#124; As a mighty one crying aloud from wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:6; Jeremiah 23:40; Job 40:12"> And He strikes His adversaries backward, &#124;&#124; He has put a continuous reproach on them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:21–1; 2 Samuel 6:2; 2 Samuel 6:17"> And He kicks against the tent of Joseph, &#124;&#124; And has not fixed on the tribe of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Psalms 87:2; Psalms 132:12–14; Ruth 4:17–22"> And He chooses the tribe of Judah, &#124;&#124; With Mount Zion that He loved,</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Job 26:7; 1 Chronicles 29:19; 1 Samuel 2:8"> And builds His sanctuary as a high place, &#124;&#124; Like the earth, He founded it for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:11–12; Acts 13:22; Exodus 3:1"> And He fixes on His servant David, &#124;&#124; And takes him from the folds of a flock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:2; 1 Chronicles 11:2; Ezekiel 34:23–24"> He has brought him in from behind suckling ones, &#124;&#124; To rule over Jacob His people, &#124;&#124; And over Israel His inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="1 Kings 9:4; 2 Timothy 2:15; Isaiah 11:2–4"> And he rules them according to the integrity of his heart, &#124;&#124; And leads them by the skillfulness of his hands!</span>
