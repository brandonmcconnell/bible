<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 86:12; 1 Chronicles 16:24; Psalms 145:1–3"> TO THE OVERSEER. &#91;SET&#93; ON “DEATH OF THE SON.” A PSALM OF DAVID. I confess, O YHWH, with all my heart, &#124;&#124; I recount all Your wonders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; Psalms 5:11; Psalms 7:17"> I rejoice and exult in You, &#124;&#124; I praise Your Name, O Most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:9; Revelation 6:12–17; Psalms 80:16"> In my enemies turning backward, &#124;&#124; They stumble and perish from Your face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 140:12; 1 Peter 2:23; Psalms 98:9"> For You have done my judgment and my right. You have sat on a throne, &#124;&#124; Judging &#91;with&#93; righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Deuteronomy 9:14; Proverbs 13:9"> You have rebuked nations, &#124;&#124; You have destroyed the wicked, &#124;&#124; You have blotted out their name for all time and forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:22–23; Exodus 14:13; Micah 7:8"> The enemy—&#91;your&#93; destructions have been completed forever, &#124;&#124; As for cities you have plucked up, &#124;&#124; Their memorial has perished with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 103:19; Hebrews 13:8; Psalms 102:12"> And YHWH abides for all time, &#124;&#124; He is preparing His throne for judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 96:13; Psalms 98:9; Revelation 20:12–13"> And He judges the world in righteousness, &#124;&#124; He judges the peoples in uprightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 91:1–2; Nahum 1:7"> And YHWH is a tower for the bruised, &#124;&#124; A tower for times of adversity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 91:14; Proverbs 18:10; Isaiah 26:3–4"> They trust in You who know Your Name, &#124;&#124; For You have not forsaken those seeking You, O YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 107:22; Psalms 96:1–2; Psalms 148:1–5"> Sing praise to YHWH, inhabiting Zion, &#124;&#124; Declare His acts among the peoples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 34:6; Exodus 3:7; Revelation 16:6"> For He who is seeking for blood &#124;&#124; Has remembered them, &#124;&#124; He has not forgotten the cry of the afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 30:3; Psalms 107:18"> Favor me, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; See my affliction by those hating me, &#124;&#124; You who lift me up from the gates of death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 13:5; Psalms 51:12; Psalms 106:2"> So that I recount all Your praise, &#124;&#124; In the gates of the daughter of Zion. I rejoice on Your salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 7:15–16; Psalms 57:6; Psalms 35:8"> Nations have sunk in a pit they made, &#124;&#124; Their foot has been captured in a net that they hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 7:5; Judges 1:7; Proverbs 12:13"> YHWH has been known, &#124;&#124; He has done judgment; By a work of his hands &#124;&#124; The wicked has been snared. Meditation. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 50:22; Job 8:13; Romans 2:8–9"> The wicked turn back to Sheol, &#124;&#124; All nations forgetting God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Proverbs 23:18; Proverbs 24:14"> For the needy is not forgotten forever, &#124;&#124; &#91;Nor&#93; the hope of the humble lost for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6"> Rise, O YHWH, do not let man be strong, &#124;&#124; Let nations be judged before Your face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 62:9; Psalms 82:6–7; Isaiah 31:3"> Appoint them to fear, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; Let nations know they &#91;are&#93; men! Selah.</span>
