<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; Deuteronomy 10:21; Psalms 83:1"> TO THE OVERSEER. A PSALM OF DAVID. O God of my praise, do not be silent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 52:4; Proverbs 6:17; Acts 6:13"> For the mouth of wickedness, and the mouth of deceit, &#124;&#124; They have opened against me, &#124;&#124; They have spoken with me—A tongue of falsehood, and words of hatred!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:4; Psalms 35:7; Psalms 35:20"> They have surrounded me about, &#124;&#124; And they fight me without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 38:20; Psalms 69:12–13; Psalms 35:7"> For my love they oppose me, and I—prayer!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 17:13; Genesis 44:4; Psalms 38:20"> And they set against me evil for good, &#124;&#124; And hatred for my love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 3:1; Matthew 5:25; Matthew 27:4"> Appoint the wicked over him, &#124;&#124; And an adversary stands at his right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 28:9; Proverbs 21:27; Proverbs 15:8"> In his being judged, he goes forth wicked, &#124;&#124; And his prayer is for sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 55:23; Acts 1:16–26; Matthew 27:5"> His days are few, another takes his oversight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 22:24; Jeremiah 18:21; Lamentations 5:3"> His sons are fatherless, and his wife a widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:25; Genesis 4:12–14; Isaiah 16:2"> And his sons wander continually, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they have begged, &#124;&#124; And have sought out of their dry places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 5:5; Job 20:18; Job 18:9–19"> An exactor lays a snare for all that he has, &#124;&#124; And strangers spoil his labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Psalms 137:8–9; James 2:13"> He has none to extend kindness, &#124;&#124; Nor is there one showing favor to his orphans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Psalms 37:28; Job 18:19"> His posterity is for cutting off, &#124;&#124; Their name is blotted out in another generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Nehemiah 4:5; Jeremiah 18:23"> The iniquity of his fathers &#124;&#124; Is remembered to YHWH, &#124;&#124; And the sin of his mother is not blotted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 34:16; Psalms 90:8; Job 18:17"> They are continually before YHWH, &#124;&#124; And He cuts off their memorial from earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:18; James 2:13; Psalms 37:14"> Because that he has not remembered to do kindness, &#124;&#124; And pursues the poor man and needy, &#124;&#124; And the struck of heart—to slay,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Proverbs 14:14; Ezekiel 35:6"> And he loves reviling, and it meets him, &#124;&#124; And he has not delighted in blessing, &#124;&#124; And it is far from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 73:6; Numbers 5:22; Matthew 27:3–5"> And he puts on reviling as his robe, &#124;&#124; And it comes in as water into his midst, &#124;&#124; And as oil into his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 109:29; Psalms 132:18; Psalms 109:18"> It is to him as apparel—he covers himself, &#124;&#124; And he girds it on for a continual girdle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:14; 1 Kings 2:44; Psalms 94:23"> This &#91;is&#93; the wage of my accusers from YHWH, &#124;&#124; And of those speaking evil against my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 31:3; Psalms 69:16; Psalms 79:9–10"> And You, O Lord YHWH, &#124;&#124; Deal with me for Your Name’s sake, &#124;&#124; Because Your kindness &#91;is&#93; good, deliver me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:1; Psalms 40:17; Psalms 102:4"> For I &#91;am&#93; poor and needy, &#124;&#124; And my heart has been pierced in my midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 10:19; 1 Chronicles 29:15; Psalms 102:10–11"> I have gone as a shadow when it is stretched out, &#124;&#124; I have been driven away as a locust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 32:3–4; Hebrews 12:12; Matthew 4:2"> My knees have been feeble from fasting, &#124;&#124; And my flesh has failed of fatness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 22:6–7; Psalms 69:19–20; Romans 15:3"> And I have been a reproach to them, &#124;&#124; They see me, they shake their head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:86; Psalms 57:1; Psalms 69:13"> Help me, O YHWH my God, &#124;&#124; Save me, according to Your kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 37:7; 1 Kings 18:36–37; Exodus 8:19"> And they know that this &#91;is&#93; Your hand, &#124;&#124; You, O YHWH, You have done it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:20; John 16:22; Psalms 109:17"> They revile, and You bless, &#124;&#124; They have risen, and are ashamed, &#124;&#124; And Your servant rejoices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 132:18; Psalms 35:26; Job 8:22"> My accusers put on blushing, and are covered, &#124;&#124; Their shame &#91;is&#93; as an upper robe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 111:1; Psalms 35:18; Psalms 22:22–25"> I thank YHWH greatly with my mouth, &#124;&#124; And I praise Him in the midst of many,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 121:5; Psalms 16:8; Psalms 140:12"> For He stands at the right hand of the needy, &#124;&#124; To save from those judging his soul.</span>
