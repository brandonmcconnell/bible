<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Jeremiah 12:3; Jeremiah 17:9–10"> TO THE OVERSEER. A PSALM OF DAVID. YHWH, You have searched me, and know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:4; 2 Kings 19:27; Psalms 94:11"> You have known my sitting down, &#124;&#124; And my rising up, &#124;&#124; You have attended to my thoughts from afar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:24; Job 31:4; Genesis 28:10–17"> You have fanned my path and my lying down, &#124;&#124; And have been acquainted &#91;with&#93; all my ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 19:14; Hebrews 4:12–13; Psalms 50:19–21"> For there is not a word in my tongue, &#124;&#124; Behold, O YHWH, You have known it all!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Psalms 34:7; Revelation 1:17"> You have besieged me behind and before, &#124;&#124; And You place Your hand on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Job 42:3; Job 26:14"> Knowledge too wonderful for me, &#124;&#124; It has been set on high, &#124;&#124; I am not able for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:23–24; Jonah 1:3; Jonah 1:10"> To where do I go from Your Spirit? And to where do I flee from Your face?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 9:2–4; Jonah 2:2; Job 34:21–22"> If I ascend the heavens—You &#91;are&#93; there, &#124;&#124; And spread out a bed in Sheol, behold, You!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 19:6; Psalms 74:16–17; Malachi 4:2"> I take the wings of morning, &#124;&#124; I dwell in the uttermost part of the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 23:3; Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 73:23"> Also there Your hand leads me, &#124;&#124; And Your right hand holds me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:22; Jeremiah 23:24; Psalms 94:7"> And I say, “Surely darkness bruises me,” &#124;&#124; Then night &#91;is&#93; light to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; Job 34:22; Hebrews 4:13"> Also darkness does not hide from You, &#124;&#124; And night shines as day, &#124;&#124; As &#91;is&#93; darkness so &#91;is&#93; light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Psalms 119:73; Isaiah 44:2"> For You have possessed my reins, &#124;&#124; You cover me in my mother’s belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 1:26–27; Psalms 111:2; Psalms 104:24"> I confess You, because I have been fearfully distinguished. Your works &#91;are&#93; wonderful, &#124;&#124; And my soul is knowing &#91;it&#93; well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:5; Psalms 139:13; Job 10:8–11"> My substance was not hid from You, &#124;&#124; When I was made in secret, &#124;&#124; Curiously worked in the lower part of earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 56:8; Malachi 3:16; Revelation 20:12"> Your eyes saw my unformed substance, &#124;&#124; And all of them are written on Your scroll, &#124;&#124; The days they were formed—And not one among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; Jeremiah 29:11; Isaiah 55:8–9"> And how precious Your thoughts have been to me, &#124;&#124; O God, how great has been their sum!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 3:5; Psalms 139:3; Psalms 40:5"> I recount them! They are more than the sand, &#124;&#124; I have awoken, and I am still with You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 5:6; Psalms 119:115; Isaiah 11:4"> Do You slay, O God, the wicked? Then, men of blood, turn aside from me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jude 1:15; Exodus 20:7; Psalms 74:18"> Who exchange You for wickedness, &#124;&#124; Your enemies &#91;are&#93; lifted up to vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 119:158; Psalms 31:6; Psalms 26:5"> Do I not hate, YHWH, those hating You? And grieve myself with Your withstanders?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 101:3–8; Luke 14:26"> I have hated them &#91;with&#93; perfect hatred, &#124;&#124; They have become enemies to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 26:2; Job 31:6; 1 Peter 1:7"> Search me, O God, and know my heart, &#124;&#124; Try me, and know my thoughts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 143:10; Jeremiah 17:9–10; Psalms 25:8–9"> And see if a grievous way be in me, &#124;&#124; And lead me in a perpetual way!</span>
