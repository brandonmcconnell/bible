<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 78:1; Psalms 33:8; Matthew 11:15"> TO THE OVERSEER. A PSALM OF THE SONS OF KORAH. Hear this, all you peoples, &#124;&#124; Give ear, all you inhabitants of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 62:9; Proverbs 22:2; Jeremiah 5:4–5"> Both low and high, together rich and needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 19:14; Proverbs 22:17; Matthew 12:35"> My mouth speaks wise things, &#124;&#124; And the meditations of my heart &#91;are&#93; things of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Matthew 13:35; Numbers 12:8"> I incline my ear to an allegory, &#124;&#124; I open my riddle with a harp:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 27:24; Romans 8:33–34; Amos 5:13"> Why do I fear in days of evil? The iniquity of my supplanters surrounds me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Psalms 52:7; Jeremiah 9:23"> Those trusting on their wealth, &#124;&#124; And in the multitude of their riches, &#124;&#124; Show themselves foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Timothy 2:6; 1 Peter 1:18"> A brother ransoms no one at all, &#124;&#124; He does not give to God his atonement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Job 36:18–19"> And precious &#91;is&#93; the redemption of their soul, &#124;&#124; And it has ceased for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:48; Psalms 16:10; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> And still he lives forever, &#124;&#124; He does not see the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Psalms 39:6; Hebrews 9:27"> For he sees wise men die, &#124;&#124; Together the foolish and brutish perish, &#124;&#124; And have left their wealth to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 4:17; Psalms 5:9; Psalms 64:6"> Their heart &#91;is that&#93; their houses &#91;are&#93; for all time, &#124;&#124; Their dwelling places from generation to generation. They proclaimed their names over the lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18–21; Psalms 49:20; Psalms 82:7"> And man does not remain in honor, &#124;&#124; He has been like the beasts, they have been cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:20; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Luke 16:27–28"> This their way &#91;is&#93; folly for them, &#124;&#124; And their posterity are pleased with their sayings. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; 1 Corinthians 6:2; Daniel 7:22"> They have set themselves as sheep for Sheol, &#124;&#124; Death afflicts them, &#124;&#124; And the upright rule over them in the morning, &#124;&#124; And their form &#91;is&#93; for consumption. Sheol &#91;is&#93; a dwelling for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 73:24; Psalms 56:13; Psalms 86:13"> Only, God ransoms my soul from the hand of Sheol, &#124;&#124; For He receives me. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 37:1; Revelation 21:26"> Do not fear when one makes wealth, &#124;&#124; When the glory of his house is abundant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:7; Job 27:19; Luke 16:24"> For at his death he receives nothing, &#124;&#124; His glory does not go down after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 12:19; Deuteronomy 29:19; Psalms 10:3"> For he blesses his soul in his life &#124;&#124; (And they praise you when you do well for yourself).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 33:30; Genesis 15:15; Psalms 56:13"> It comes to the generation of his fathers, &#124;&#124; They do not see the light forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 49:12; Ecclesiastes 3:18–19; Job 4:21"> Man in honor, who does not understand, &#124;&#124; Has been like the beasts, they have been cut off!</span>
