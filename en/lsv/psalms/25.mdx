<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 143:8; Psalms 86:4; Psalms 24:4"> BY DAVID. &#91;ALEPH-BET&#93; To You, O YHWH, I lift up my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalms 41:11; Psalms 71:1"> My God, in You I have trusted, &#124;&#124; Do not let me be ashamed, &#124;&#124; Do not let my enemies exult over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25; Isaiah 40:31; Isaiah 49:23"> Also let none waiting on You be ashamed, &#124;&#124; Let the treacherous dealers without cause be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 86:11; Psalms 27:11; Psalms 143:8"> Your ways, O YHWH, cause me to know, &#124;&#124; You teach me Your paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 8:34; Isaiah 30:18; Psalms 25:10"> Cause me to tread in Your truth, and teach me, &#124;&#124; For You &#91;are&#93; the God of my salvation, &#124;&#124; Near You I have waited all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 103:17; Colossians 3:12; Luke 1:50"> Remember Your mercies, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; And Your kindnesses, for they &#91;are&#93; from the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 38:17; Job 13:26"> Sins of my youth, and my transgressions, &#124;&#124; Do not remember. According to Your kindness be mindful of me, &#124;&#124; For Your goodness’ sake, O YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 1:5; Psalms 32:8; Ephesians 1:17–18"> Good and upright &#91;is&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; Therefore He directs sinners in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Psalms 23:3; Matthew 5:5"> He causes the humble to tread in judgment, &#124;&#124; And teaches the humble His way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 5:11; Romans 8:28; Hebrews 8:8–12"> All the paths of YHWH &#91;are&#93; kindness and truth, &#124;&#124; To those keeping His covenant, &#124;&#124; And His testimonies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 79:9; 1 John 2:12; Isaiah 43:25"> For Your Name’s sake, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; You have pardoned my iniquity, for it &#91;is&#93; great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:23; Psalms 32:8; John 7:17"> Who &#91;is&#93; this—the man fearing YHWH? He directs him in the way He chooses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 37:11; Proverbs 1:33; Proverbs 19:23"> His soul remains in good, &#124;&#124; And his seed possesses the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 15:15; John 14:21–23; John 7:17"> The secret of YHWH &#91;is&#93; for those fearing Him, &#124;&#124; And His covenant—to cause them to know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 141:8; Psalms 124:7–8; Psalms 31:4"> My eyes &#91;are&#93; continually to YHWH, &#124;&#124; For He brings my feet out from a net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 86:16; Daniel 9:17; Micah 7:19"> Turn to me, and favor me, &#124;&#124; For I &#91;am&#93; lonely and afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 107:6; Psalms 34:19; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> The distresses of my heart have enlarged themselves, &#124;&#124; Bring me out from my distresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Psalms 119:132; Psalms 51:8–9"> See my affliction and my misery, &#124;&#124; And bear with all my sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 56:2; Psalms 52:2; Psalms 38:19"> See my enemies, for they have been many, &#124;&#124; And they have hated me with violent hatred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 71:1–2; Psalms 121:7; Joel 2:26–27"> Keep my soul, and deliver me, &#124;&#124; Do not let me be ashamed, for I trusted in You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 11:3; Psalms 41:12; Proverbs 20:7"> Integrity and uprightness keep me, &#124;&#124; For I have waited &#91;on&#93; You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 130:8; Psalms 51:18–19; Psalms 14:7"> Redeem Israel, O God, from all his distresses!</span>
