<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 62:1; Psalms 76:11; Psalms 116:17–18"> TO THE OVERSEER. A PSALM OF DAVID. A SONG. To You, silence &#91;and&#93; praise, O God, in Zion, &#124;&#124; And to You a vow is completed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; Jeremiah 29:12–13; Psalms 86:9"> Hearer of prayer, all flesh comes to You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 38:4; Psalms 79:9; Hebrews 9:14"> Matters of iniquities were mightier than I, &#124;&#124; Our transgressions—You cover them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; Psalms 84:4; Psalms 23:6"> O the blessedness of &#91;him whom&#93; You choose, &#124;&#124; And draw near, he inhabits Your courts, &#124;&#124; We are satisfied with the goodness of Your house, &#124;&#124; Your holy temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:21; Psalms 66:3; Revelation 15:3–4"> By fearful things in righteousness You answer us, &#124;&#124; O God of our salvation, &#124;&#124; The confidence of all far off &#124;&#124; The ends of the earth and sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 93:1; Micah 6:2; Habakkuk 3:6"> Establishing mountains by His power, &#124;&#124; He has been girded with might,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 89:9; Isaiah 17:12–13; Psalms 93:3–4"> Restraining the noise of seas, the noise of their billows, &#124;&#124; And the multitude of the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 148:3; Psalms 136:8; Psalms 104:20–23"> And the inhabitants of the uttermost parts &#124;&#124; Are afraid from Your signs, &#124;&#124; You cause the outgoings of morning and evening to sing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 46:4; Psalms 104:13–15; Psalms 68:9–10"> You have inspected the earth, and water it, &#124;&#124; You make it very rich, the stream of God &#91;is&#93; full of water, &#124;&#124; You prepare their grain, &#124;&#124; When thus You prepare it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; 1 Corinthians 3:6–7; Deuteronomy 32:2"> Its ridges have been filled, &#124;&#124; Its furrow has been deepened, &#124;&#124; You soften it with showers, &#124;&#124; Its springing up You bless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joel 2:21–26; Psalms 103:4; Psalms 5:12"> You have crowned the year of Your goodness, &#124;&#124; And Your paths drop fatness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 38:26–27; Psalms 104:10–13; Joel 2:22"> The pastures of a wilderness drop, &#124;&#124; And You gird the hills with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 55:12; Acts 14:17; Isaiah 35:1–2"> The meadows are clothed with the flock, &#124;&#124; And valleys are covered with grain, &#124;&#124; They shout—indeed, they sing!</span>
