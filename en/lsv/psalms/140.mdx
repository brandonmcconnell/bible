<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:48; Psalms 140:4; Psalms 71:4"> TO THE OVERSEER. A PSALM OF DAVID. Deliver me, O YHWH, from an evil man, &#124;&#124; Keep me from one of violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 56:6; Psalms 36:4; Psalms 64:5–6"> Who have devised evils in the heart, &#124;&#124; All the day they assemble &#91;for&#93; wars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 58:4; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Romans 3:13–14"> They sharpened their tongue as a serpent, &#124;&#124; Poison of a viper &#91;is&#93; under their lips. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 36:11; Psalms 71:4; Psalms 17:5"> Preserve me, YHWH, from the hands of the wicked, &#124;&#124; Keep me from one of violence, &#124;&#124; Who have devised to overthrow my steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 142:3; Psalms 35:7; Jeremiah 18:22"> The proud hid a snare for me—and cords, &#124;&#124; They spread a net by the side of the path, &#124;&#124; They have set snares for me. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 16:2; Psalms 31:14; Psalms 119:57"> I have said to YHWH, “You &#91;are&#93; my God, &#124;&#124; Hear, YHWH, the voice of my supplications.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 62:7; Psalms 89:26; Psalms 18:1–2"> O YHWH, my Lord, strength of my salvation, &#124;&#124; You have covered my head in the day of armor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 10:2–3; Deuteronomy 32:27; Psalms 27:12"> Do not grant, O YHWH, the desires of the wicked, &#124;&#124; Do not bring forth his wicked scheme, &#124;&#124; They are high. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 7:16; Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 12:13"> The chief of my surrounders, &#124;&#124; The perverseness of their lips covers them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Psalms 21:9; Revelation 21:8"> They cause burning coals to fall on themselves, &#124;&#124; He casts them into fire, &#124;&#124; Into deep pits—they do not arise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:21; Proverbs 18:21; Isaiah 3:11"> A talkative man is not established in the earth, &#124;&#124; One of violence—evil hunts to overflowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 9:4; 1 Kings 8:49; Proverbs 23:10–11"> I have known that YHWH executes &#124;&#124; The judgment of the afflicted, &#124;&#124; The judgment of the needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 11:7; Isaiah 3:10"> Surely the righteous give thanks to Your Name, &#124;&#124; The upright dwell with Your presence!</span>
