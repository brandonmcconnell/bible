<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:23–24; Psalms 100:1; Psalms 98:4"> TO THE OVERSEER. A SONG. A PSALM. Shout to God, all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 105:2–3; Revelation 5:13; Isaiah 49:13"> Praise the glory of His Name, &#124;&#124; Make honorable His praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:44; Psalms 65:5; Psalms 81:15"> Say to God, “How fearful Your works—By the abundance of Your strength, &#124;&#124; Your enemies feign obedience to You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 22:27; Psalms 117:1; Psalms 96:1–2"> All the earth bows to You, &#124;&#124; They sing praise to You, they praise Your Name.” Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 126:1–3; Numbers 23:23; Psalms 66:16"> Come, and see the works of God, &#124;&#124; Fearful acts toward the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; Exodus 15:1–21; Joshua 3:14"> He has turned a sea to dry land, &#124;&#124; Through a river they pass over on foot, &#124;&#124; There we rejoice in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 11:4; Matthew 28:18; Psalms 140:8"> Ruling by His might for all time, &#124;&#124; His eyes watch among the nations, &#124;&#124; The stubborn do not exalt themselves. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 98:4; Revelation 19:1; Jeremiah 33:11"> Bless our God you peoples, &#124;&#124; And sound the voice of His praise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 121:3; Psalms 112:6; Psalms 94:18"> Who has placed our soul in life, &#124;&#124; And has not permitted our feet to be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 48:10; Psalms 17:3; 1 Peter 1:6–7"> For You have tried us, O God, &#124;&#124; You have refined us as the refining of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 1:13; Ezekiel 12:13; Lamentations 3:2–66"> You have brought us into a net, &#124;&#124; You have placed pressure on our loins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:1–2; Isaiah 51:23; Psalms 40:2–3"> You have caused man to ride at our head. We have entered into fire and into water, &#124;&#124; And You bring us out to a watered place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 100:4; Psalms 56:12; Ecclesiastes 5:4"> I enter Your house with burnt-offerings, &#124;&#124; I complete my vows to You,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:11; Judges 11:35–36; Psalms 18:6"> For my lips were opened, &#124;&#124; And my mouth spoke in my distress:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 51:19; 2 Samuel 6:17–19; 1 Chronicles 16:1–3"> “I offer to You burnt-offerings of fatlings, &#124;&#124; With incense of rams, I prepare a bullock with male goats.” Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 71:15; Psalms 34:11; Psalms 71:18"> Come, hear, all you who fear God, &#124;&#124; And I recount what He did for my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:6; Psalms 145:1; Psalms 116:12"> I have called to Him &#91;with&#93; my mouth, &#124;&#124; And exaltation &#91;is&#93; under my tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 9:31; Proverbs 28:9; Proverbs 15:29"> Iniquity, if I have seen in my heart, &#124;&#124; The Lord does not hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 116:1–2; Hebrews 5:7; Lamentations 3:55–56"> But God has heard, &#124;&#124; He has attended to the voice of my prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 86:12–13; Psalms 68:35; Psalms 51:11"> Blessed &#91;is&#93; God, &#124;&#124; Who has not turned aside my prayer, &#124;&#124; And His loving-kindness, from me!</span>
