<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Psalms 130:1–2; Psalms 142:2–3"> A PRAYER OF THE AFFLICTED WHEN HE IS FEEBLE, AND POURS OUT HIS COMPLAINT BEFORE YHWH. O YHWH, hear my prayer, indeed, my cry comes to You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 27:9; Isaiah 8:17"> Do not hide Your face from me, &#124;&#124; In a day of my adversity, &#124;&#124; Incline Your ear to me, &#124;&#124; In the day I call, hurry, answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalms 37:20; Job 30:30"> For my days have been consumed in smoke, &#124;&#124; And my bones have burned as a firebrand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7; Psalms 37:2; Psalms 77:3"> Struck as the herb, and withered, is my heart, &#124;&#124; For I have forgotten to eat my bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 19:20; Lamentations 4:8; Psalms 6:8"> From the voice of my sighing &#124;&#124; My bone has cleaved to my flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:14; Isaiah 38:14; Isaiah 34:11–15"> I have been like to a pelican of the wilderness, &#124;&#124; I have been as an owl of the dry places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 77:4; Psalms 38:11; Deuteronomy 28:66–67"> I have watched, and I am &#124;&#124; As a bird alone on the roof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 26:11; Psalms 69:9–10; Isaiah 65:15"> All the day my enemies reproached me, &#124;&#124; Those mad at me have sworn against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Psalms 80:5; Micah 1:10"> Because I have eaten ashes as bread, &#124;&#124; And have mingled my drink with weeping,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 38:3; Psalms 90:7–9; Psalms 147:6"> From Your indignation and Your wrath, &#124;&#124; For You have lifted me up, &#124;&#124; And cast me down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 14:2; Psalms 109:23; Psalms 144:4"> My days &#91;are&#93; stretched out as a shadow, &#124;&#124; And I am withered as the herb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 135:13; Lamentations 5:19; Exodus 3:15"> And You, O YHWH, abide for all time, &#124;&#124; And Your memorial from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; Galatians 4:4; Isaiah 40:2"> You rise—You pity Zion, &#124;&#124; For the time to favor her, &#124;&#124; For the appointed time has come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:2; Ezra 7:27; Ezra 3:1–3"> For Your servants have been pleased with her stones, &#124;&#124; And they favor her dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:43; Psalms 138:4; Isaiah 60:3–22"> And nations fear the Name of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And all kings of the earth Your glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–2; Psalms 147:2; Jeremiah 33:7"> For YHWH has built Zion, &#124;&#124; He has been seen in His glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:6; Psalms 22:24; Jeremiah 29:11–14"> He turned to the prayer of the destitute, &#124;&#124; And He has not despised their prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 22:30–31; Romans 15:4; Isaiah 43:21"> This is written for a later generation, &#124;&#124; And the people created praise YAH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalms 14:2; 1 Kings 8:43"> For He has looked &#124;&#124; From the high place of His sanctuary. YHWH looked attentively from the heavens to earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 79:11; Psalms 146:7; Exodus 3:7"> To hear the groan of the prisoner, &#124;&#124; To loose sons of death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:22; Isaiah 51:11; Psalms 79:13"> To declare in Zion the Name of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And His praise in Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 8:20–23; Matthew 24:14; Psalms 72:8–11"> In the peoples being gathered together, &#124;&#124; And the kingdoms—to serve YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 89:38–47; 1 Timothy 4:1–3; 2 Thessalonians 2:3–12"> He has humbled my power in the way, &#124;&#124; He has shortened my days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 39:13; Habakkuk 1:12; Psalms 102:12"> I say, “My God, do not take me up in the midst of my days,” &#124;&#124; Your years &#91;are&#93; through all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; Exodus 20:11; Hebrews 1:10–12"> You founded the earth before time, &#124;&#124; And the heavens &#91;are&#93; the work of Your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Matthew 24:35; Isaiah 34:4"> They perish, and You remain, &#124;&#124; And all of them become old as a garment, &#124;&#124; You change them as clothing, &#124;&#124; And they are changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 13:8; James 1:17; Malachi 3:6"> And You &#91;are&#93; the same, and Your years are not finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 69:35–36; Psalms 45:16–17; Psalms 22:30–31"> The sons of Your servants continue, &#124;&#124; And their seed is established before You!</span>
