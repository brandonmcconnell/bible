<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 10:35; Isaiah 51:9–10; Isaiah 42:13–14"> TO THE OVERSEER. A PSALM. A SONG OF DAVID. God rises &#91;and&#93; His enemies are scattered! And those hating Him flee from His face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 1:4; Psalms 37:20; Isaiah 9:18"> You drive them away as the driving away of smoke, &#124;&#124; As the melting of wax before fire, &#124;&#124; The wicked perish at the presence of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 64:10; Psalms 100:1–2; 1 Thessalonians 5:16"> And the righteous rejoice, they exult before God, &#124;&#124; And they rejoice with gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:26; Isaiah 12:4–6; Psalms 66:4"> Sing to God—praise His Name, &#124;&#124; Raise up a highway for Him who is riding in deserts, &#124;&#124; In YAH &#91;is&#93; His Name, and exult before Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 10:14; Jeremiah 49:11; Psalms 82:3–4"> Father of the fatherless, and judge of the widows, &#124;&#124; &#91;Is&#93; God in His holy habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:33; Psalms 113:9; Psalms 107:10"> God—causing the lonely to dwell at home, &#124;&#124; Bringing out bound ones into prosperity, &#124;&#124; Only—the stubborn have inhabited a dry place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Judges 5:4; Judges 4:14"> O God, in Your going forth before Your people, &#124;&#124; In Your stepping through the wilderness, Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 19:18; Exodus 19:16; Deuteronomy 5:23–25"> The earth has shaken, &#124;&#124; Indeed, the heavens have dropped before God, &#124;&#124; This Sinai—before God, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:14; Psalms 65:9–13; Psalms 78:24–27"> You shake out a shower of free-will gifts, O God. Your inheritance, when it has been weary, &#124;&#124; You have established it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:9–10; Psalms 74:19; Matthew 11:5"> Your creature has dwelt in it, &#124;&#124; You prepare for the poor in Your goodness, O God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Psalms 40:3; Exodus 17:9–16"> The Lord gives the saying, &#124;&#124; The female proclaimers &#91;are&#93; a numerous host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 10:16; Judges 5:19; 1 Samuel 30:24"> Kings of hosts utterly flee away, &#124;&#124; And a female inhabitant of the house apportions spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 105:37; Genesis 49:14; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11"> Though you lie between two boundaries, &#124;&#124; Wings of a dove covered with silver, &#124;&#124; And her pinions with yellow gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 9:48; Psalms 51:7; Isaiah 1:18"> When the Mighty spreads kings in it, it snows in Salmon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 87:1–2; Psalms 78:68–69; Deuteronomy 3:10"> A hill of God &#91;is&#93; the hill of Bashan, &#124;&#124; A hill of heights &#91;is&#93; the hill of Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Psalms 132:13–14; Revelation 21:2–3"> Why do you envy, O high hills, &#124;&#124; The hill God has desired for His seat? YHWH also dwells forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Daniel 7:10; Revelation 5:11"> The chariots of God &#91;are&#93; myriads, thousands of changes, &#124;&#124; The Lord &#91;is&#93; among them, in Sinai, in the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 4:8–10; 2 Corinthians 6:16; Revelation 21:3"> You have ascended on high, &#124;&#124; You have taken captivity captive, &#124;&#124; You have taken gifts for men, &#124;&#124; That even the stubborn may rest, O YAH God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; Lamentations 3:23; Psalms 55:22"> Blessed &#91;is&#93; the Lord, day by day He lays on us. God Himself &#91;is&#93; our salvation. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Revelation 1:18; 1 Samuel 2:6"> God Himself &#91;is&#93; to us a God for deliverances, &#124;&#124; And YHWH Lord has the outgoings of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:13; Psalms 7:12; Psalms 110:6"> Only—God strikes &#124;&#124; The head of His enemies, &#124;&#124; The hairy crown of a habitual walker in his guilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 21:33; Ezekiel 36:24; Amos 9:2–3"> The Lord said: “From Bashan I bring back, &#124;&#124; I bring back from the depths of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; Psalms 58:10; 1 Kings 22:38"> So that you dash your foot &#124;&#124; In the blood of enemies—the tongue of Your dogs.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 63:2; 2 Samuel 6:12–17; 1 Chronicles 15:16–24"> They have seen Your goings, O God, &#124;&#124; Goings of my God, my king, in the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; Judges 11:34; Exodus 15:20"> Singers have been before, &#124;&#124; Behind &#91;are&#93; players on instruments, &#124;&#124; Virgins playing with timbrels in the midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 48:1; Deuteronomy 33:28; Psalms 26:12"> In assemblies bless God, &#124;&#124; The Lord—from the fountain of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:21; Judges 20:35; Judges 21:6–25"> There &#91;is&#93; little Benjamin, their ruler, &#124;&#124; Heads of Judah their defense, &#124;&#124; Heads of Zebulun—heads of Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 138:8; Psalms 71:3; Psalms 42:8"> Your God has commanded your strength, &#124;&#124; Be strong, O God, You have worked this for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 10:10; Psalms 76:11; Isaiah 60:6–11"> Because of Your temple at Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; Kings bring a present to You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 89:10; Psalms 22:12–13; Jeremiah 50:11"> Rebuke a beast of the reeds, a herd of bulls, &#124;&#124; With calves of the peoples, &#124;&#124; Each humbling himself with pieces of silver, &#124;&#124; You scatter peoples delighting in conflicts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 45:14; Zephaniah 3:10; Psalms 44:20"> Fat ones come out of Egypt, &#124;&#124; Cush causes her hands to run to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 67:2–5; Romans 15:10–11; Psalms 117:1–2"> Kingdoms of the earth, sing to God, &#124;&#124; Praise the Lord! Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; Psalms 18:10; Psalms 68:4"> To Him who is riding on the heavens of the heavens of old, &#124;&#124; Behold, He gives with His voice a strong voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 19:6; 2 Peter 1:17; 1 Chronicles 16:28–29"> Ascribe strength to God, &#124;&#124; His excellence &#91;is&#93; over Israel, and His strength in the clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 29:11; Colossians 1:11; Philippians 4:13"> Fearful, O God, out of Your sanctuaries, &#124;&#124; The God of Israel Himself, &#124;&#124; Giving strength and might to the people. Blessed &#91;is&#93; God!</span>
