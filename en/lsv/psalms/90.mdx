<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Psalms 71:3; 1 John 4:16"> A PRAYER OF MOSES, THE MAN OF GOD. Lord, You have been a habitation, &#124;&#124; To us—in generation and generation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 13:8; Genesis 1:1; Psalms 102:24–27"> Before mountains were brought forth, &#124;&#124; And You form the earth and the world, &#124;&#124; Even from age to age You &#91;are&#93; God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Psalms 146:4"> You turn man to a bruised thing, &#124;&#124; And say, Return, you sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 3:8; Psalms 39:5; Matthew 24:43"> For one thousand years in Your eyes &#91;are&#93; as yesterday, &#124;&#124; For it passes on, indeed, &#91;as&#93; a watch by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6; Psalms 73:20; Job 22:16"> You have inundated them, they are asleep, &#124;&#124; In the morning he changes as grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 14:2; Psalms 92:7; James 1:11"> In the morning it flourishes, and has changed, &#124;&#124; At evening it is cut down, and has withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 39:11; Psalms 59:13; Exodus 14:24"> For we were consumed in Your anger, &#124;&#124; And we have been troubled in Your fury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:17; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Hebrews 4:12–13"> You have set our iniquities before You, &#124;&#124; Our hidden things at the light of Your face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 78:33; Psalms 39:5; Psalms 90:4"> For all our days pined away in Your wrath, &#124;&#124; We consumed our years as a meditation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalms 78:39; 2 Samuel 19:35"> The days of our years, in them &#91;are&#93; seventy years, &#124;&#124; And if, by reason of might, eighty years, &#124;&#124; Yet their enlargement &#91;is&#93; labor and vanity, &#124;&#124; For it has been cut off quickly, and we fly away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 26:24; Isaiah 33:14; Nahum 1:6"> Who knows the power of Your anger? And according to Your fear—Your wrath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:16–17; Psalms 39:4; John 9:4"> Let &#91;us&#93; know to number our days correctly, &#124;&#124; And we bring the heart to wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Psalms 135:14; Deuteronomy 32:36"> Turn back, O YHWH, until when? And regret concerning Your servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 23:6; Psalms 86:4; Psalms 31:7"> Satisfy us at morning &#91;with&#93; Your kindness, &#124;&#124; And we sing and rejoice all our days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 126:5–6; Psalms 30:5; John 16:20"> Cause us to rejoice according to the days &#124;&#124; Wherein You have afflicted us, &#124;&#124; The years we have seen evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:2; Psalms 44:1; Deuteronomy 32:4"> Let Your work appear to Your servants, &#124;&#124; And Your honor on their sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 16:3; 1 Corinthians 3:7; Job 22:28"> And let the pleasantness of our God YHWH be on us, &#124;&#124; And establish on us the work of our hands, &#124;&#124; Indeed, establish the work of our hands!</span>
