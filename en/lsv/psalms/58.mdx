<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:20; Isaiah 10:1; Ezekiel 22:27"> Even in heart you work iniquities, &#124;&#124; In the land you ponder the violence of your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 51:5; Isaiah 48:8; Proverbs 22:15"> The wicked have been estranged from the womb, &#124;&#124; They have erred from the belly, speaking lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 140:3; James 3:8; Ecclesiastes 10:11"> Their poison &#91;is&#93; as poison of a serpent, &#124;&#124; As a deaf cobra shutting its ear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:17; Isaiah 19:3; Deuteronomy 18:11"> Which does not listen to the voice of whisperers, &#124;&#124; A charmer of most skillful charms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Job 4:10–11; Hosea 5:14"> O God, break their teeth in their mouth, &#124;&#124; Break down the jaw-teeth of young lions, O YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 112:10; Joshua 7:5; Psalms 64:3"> They are melted as waters, &#124;&#124; They go up and down for themselves, &#124;&#124; His arrow proceeds as they cut themselves off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 3:16; Ecclesiastes 6:3; Psalms 37:35–36"> He goes on as a snail that melts, &#124;&#124; &#91;As&#93; an untimely birth of a woman, &#124;&#124; They have not seen the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 118:12; Proverbs 10:25; Ecclesiastes 7:6"> Before your pots discern the bramble, &#124;&#124; As living, He whirls away in His burning anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 68:23; Psalms 91:8; Psalms 64:10"> The righteous rejoices that he has seen vengeance, &#124;&#124; He washes his steps in the blood of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 67:4; Isaiah 3:10; Psalms 64:9"> And man says: “Surely fruit &#91;is&#93; for the righteous: Surely there is a God judging in the earth!”</span>
