<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 3:4; Psalms 142:1–3; Psalms 50:1"> TO THE OVERSEER. FOR JEDUTHUN. A PSALM OF ASAPH. My voice &#91;is&#93; to God, and I cry, &#124;&#124; My voice &#91;is&#93; to God, &#124;&#124; And He has given ear to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Genesis 37:35; Isaiah 26:9"> I sought the Lord in a day of my distress, &#124;&#124; My hand has been spread out by night, &#124;&#124; And it does not cease, &#124;&#124; My soul has refused to be comforted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 61:2; Psalms 142:2–3; Job 31:23"> I remember God, and make a noise, &#124;&#124; I meditate, and my spirit is feeble. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 7:13–15; Psalms 6:6; Job 2:13"> You have taken hold of the watches of my eyes, &#124;&#124; I have been moved, and I do not speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7; Isaiah 51:9; Psalms 143:5"> I have reckoned the days of old, &#124;&#124; The years of the ages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Psalms 4:4; Psalms 139:23–24"> I remember my music in the night, &#124;&#124; I meditate with my heart, and my spirit searches diligently:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 85:1; Lamentations 3:31–32; Psalms 85:5"> Does the Lord cast off for all ages? Does He add to be pleased no longer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 9:6; 2 Peter 3:9"> Has His kindness ceased forever? The saying failed from generation to generation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 40:11; Isaiah 49:14–15; Isaiah 63:15"> Has God forgotten &#91;His&#93; favors? Has He shut up His mercies in anger? Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 42:3; Mark 9:24; Exodus 15:6"> And I say: “My weakness is, &#124;&#124; The changes of the right hand of the Most High.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 143:5; Psalms 105:5; 1 Chronicles 16:12"> I mention the doings of YAH, &#124;&#124; For I remember Your wonders of old,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 71:24; Psalms 145:11; Deuteronomy 6:7"> And I have meditated on all Your working, &#124;&#124; And I talk concerning Your doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 73:17; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 89:6–8"> O God, Your way &#91;is&#93; in holiness, &#124;&#124; Who &#91;is&#93; a great god like God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:27; Psalms 136:4; Exodus 15:11"> You &#91;are&#93; the God doing wonders. You have made Your strength known among the peoples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 9:29; Deuteronomy 9:26"> You have redeemed Your people with strength, &#124;&#124; The sons of Jacob and Joseph. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Habakkuk 3:15; Joshua 3:15–16"> The waters have seen You, O God, &#124;&#124; The waters have seen You, &#124;&#124; They are afraid—also depths are troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; 2 Samuel 22:15; Habakkuk 3:11"> Thick clouds have poured out waters, &#124;&#124; The skies have given forth a noise, &#124;&#124; Also—Your arrows go up and down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 97:4; 2 Samuel 22:14; Revelation 11:19"> The voice of Your thunder &#91;is&#93; in the spheres, &#124;&#124; Lightnings have lightened the world, &#124;&#124; The earth has trembled, indeed, it shakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; Psalms 29:10; Nahum 1:3–4"> Your way &#91;is&#93; in the sea, &#124;&#124; And Your paths in many waters, &#124;&#124; And Your tracks have not been known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Psalms 78:52; Exodus 14:19"> You have led Your people as a flock, &#124;&#124; By the hand of Moses and Aaron!</span>
