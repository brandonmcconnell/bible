<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; 1 Chronicles 22:12–13; Isaiah 11:2"> BY SOLOMON. O God, give Your judgments to the king, &#124;&#124; And Your righteousness to the king’s Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 32:1; Psalms 72:12–14; Revelation 19:11"> He judges Your people with righteousness, &#124;&#124; And Your poor with judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Psalms 96:11–13; Psalms 72:16"> The mountains bear peace to the people, &#124;&#124; And the heights by righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 11:4; Ezekiel 34:15–16; Psalms 109:31"> He judges the poor of the people, &#124;&#124; Gives deliverance to the sons of the needy, &#124;&#124; And bruises the oppressor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:36–37; Psalms 72:17; Daniel 7:14"> They fear You with the sun, and before the moon, &#124;&#124; Generation—generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; Deuteronomy 32:2; 2 Samuel 23:4"> He comes down as rain on mown grass, &#124;&#124; As showers—sprinkling the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:4; Malachi 4:2; Psalms 92:12"> The righteous flourish in His days, &#124;&#124; And abundance of peace until the moon is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Zechariah 9:10; Psalms 80:11"> And He rules from sea to sea, &#124;&#124; And from the river to the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Micah 7:17; Luke 19:27"> Desert-dwellers bow before Him, &#124;&#124; And His enemies lick the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6; Psalms 68:29; Isaiah 49:7"> Kings of Tarshish and of the islands send back a present. Kings of Sheba and Seba bring a reward near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Isaiah 49:22–23; Revelation 11:15"> And all kings bow themselves to Him, &#124;&#124; All nations serve Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 29:12; Revelation 3:17–18; Psalms 102:17"> For He delivers the needy who cries, &#124;&#124; And the poor when he has no helper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Psalms 109:31; Matthew 5:3"> He has pity on the poor and needy, &#124;&#124; And He saves the souls of the needy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 116:15; Revelation 19:2; Psalms 69:18"> He redeems their soul from fraud and from violence, &#124;&#124; And their blood is precious in His eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:14; Revelation 5:8–14; Jude 1:25"> And He lives, and the gold of Sheba &#91;is&#93; given to Him, &#124;&#124; And prayer is continually made for Him, &#124;&#124; All day He is continually blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Psalms 92:12–14; Isaiah 30:23"> There is a handful of grain in the earth, &#124;&#124; On the top of mountains, &#124;&#124; Its fruit shakes like Lebanon, &#124;&#124; And they flourish out of the city as the herb of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:23; Psalms 89:36"> His Name is for all time, &#124;&#124; Before the sun is His Name continued, &#124;&#124; And they bless themselves in Him, &#124;&#124; All nations pronounce Him blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:13; Psalms 106:48; 1 Chronicles 29:10"> Blessed is YHWH God, God of Israel, &#124;&#124; He alone is doing wonders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:21; Matthew 6:10; Psalms 41:13"> And blessed &#91;is&#93; the Name of His glory for all time, &#124;&#124; And the whole earth is filled &#91;with&#93; His glory. Amen and amen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:1; Job 31:40; Jeremiah 51:64"> The prayers of David son of Jesse have been ended.</span>
