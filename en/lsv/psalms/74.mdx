<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Psalms 79:13; Psalms 100:3"> AN INSTRUCTION OF ASAPH. Why, O God, have You cast off forever? Your anger smokes against the flock of Your pasture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Exodus 15:16; Exodus 15:13"> Remember Your congregation &#124;&#124; &#91;That&#93; You purchased of old, &#124;&#124; You redeemed the rod of Your inheritance, &#124;&#124; This Mount Zion—You dwelt in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 1:10; Psalms 79:1; Daniel 9:17"> Lift up Your steps to the continuous desolations, &#124;&#124; Everything the enemy did wickedly in the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Lamentations 2:7; Matthew 24:15; Daniel 6:27"> Your adversaries have roared, &#124;&#124; In the midst of Your meeting-places, &#124;&#124; They have set their ensigns as ensigns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 5:6; 2 Chronicles 2:14; Jeremiah 46:22–23"> He is known as one bringing in on high &#124;&#124; Against a thicket of wood—axes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 6:18; 1 Kings 6:29; 1 Kings 6:35"> And now they break down its engravings, &#124;&#124; Together, with axe and hatchet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 25:9; Psalms 89:39; Ezekiel 24:21"> They have sent Your sanctuary into fire, &#124;&#124; They defiled the Dwelling Place of Your Name to the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 83:4; 2 Kings 4:23; Esther 3:8–9"> They said in their hearts, “Let us oppress them together,” &#124;&#124; They burned all the meeting-places of God in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 8:11; Ezekiel 7:26; 1 Samuel 3:1"> We have not seen our ensigns, &#124;&#124; There is no longer a prophet, &#124;&#124; Nor with us is one knowing how long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 89:50–51; Psalms 44:16; Psalms 89:46"> Until when, O God, does an adversary reproach? Does an enemy despise Your Name forever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 2:3; Psalms 78:65–66; Psalms 44:23"> Why do You turn back Your hand, &#124;&#124; Even Your right hand? Remove &#91;it&#93; from the midst of Your bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 44:4; Exodus 19:5–6; Isaiah 33:22"> And God &#91;is&#93; my king of old, &#124;&#124; Working salvation in the midst of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Psalms 78:13; Ezekiel 29:3"> You have divided &#91;the&#93; sea by Your strength, &#124;&#124; You have shattered heads of dragons by the waters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 72:9; Psalms 104:25–26"> You have broken the heads of leviathan, &#124;&#124; You make him food for the people of desert-dwellers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; Isaiah 48:21; Psalms 105:41"> You have cleaved a fountain and a stream, &#124;&#124; You have dried up perennial flowings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 136:7–9; Matthew 5:45; Psalms 104:19"> The day &#91;is&#93; Yours, &#124;&#124; The night &#91;is&#93; also Yours, &#124;&#124; You have prepared a light-giver—the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:8; Acts 17:26; Genesis 8:22"> You have set up all the borders of earth, &#124;&#124; Summer and winter—You have formed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:8; Revelation 16:19; Deuteronomy 32:6"> Remember this—an enemy reproached YHWH, &#124;&#124; And a foolish people have despised Your Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Song of Songs 2:14; Matthew 10:16; Psalms 9:18"> Do not give up to a &#91;wild&#93; creature, &#124;&#124; The soul of Your turtle-dove, &#124;&#124; Do not forget the life of Your poor ones forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Genesis 17:7–8; Hebrews 8:10"> Look attentively to the covenant, &#124;&#124; For the dark places of earth, &#124;&#124; Have been full of habitations of violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 35:10; Psalms 9:18"> Do not let the oppressed turn back ashamed, &#124;&#124; Let the poor and needy praise Your Name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 74:18; Psalms 53:1; Isaiah 52:5"> Arise, O God, plead Your plea, &#124;&#124; Remember Your reproach from a fool all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Psalms 65:7; Jonah 1:2"> Do not forget the voice of Your adversaries, &#124;&#124; The noise of Your withstanders is going up continually!</span>
