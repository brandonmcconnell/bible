<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 24:19"> BY DAVID. &#91;ALEPH-BET&#93; Do not fret because of evildoers, &#124;&#124; Do not be envious against doers of iniquity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 92:7; Psalms 90:5–6; James 1:10–11"> For they are cut off speedily as grass, &#124;&#124; And fade as the greenness of the tender grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; Matthew 6:31–33; Psalms 62:8"> Trust in YHWH, and do good, &#124;&#124; Dwell &#91;in&#93; the land, and enjoy faithfulness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:7; Psalms 145:19; John 15:16"> And delight yourself on YHWH, &#124;&#124; And He gives to you the petitions of your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 16:3; 1 Peter 5:7; Philippians 4:6–7"> Roll your way on YHWH, &#124;&#124; And trust on Him, and He works,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 54:17; Job 11:17; Isaiah 58:8"> And has brought out your righteousness as light, &#124;&#124; And your judgment as noon-day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 5:7–11; Psalms 27:14; Psalms 62:5"> Be silent for YHWH, and stay yourself for Him, &#124;&#124; Do not fret because of him &#124;&#124; Who is making his way prosperous, &#124;&#124; Because of a man doing wicked schemes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Proverbs 16:32; James 1:19–20"> Desist from anger, and forsake fury, &#124;&#124; Do not fret yourself to only do evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:21; Psalms 25:13; Psalms 37:29"> For evildoers are cut off, &#124;&#124; As for those waiting on YHWH, they possess the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 24:24; Psalms 103:16; Psalms 37:35–36"> And yet a little &#91;while&#93;, and the wicked is not, &#124;&#124; And you have considered his place, and it is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:5; Galatians 5:22–23; Isaiah 26:3"> And the humble possess the land, &#124;&#124; And they have delighted themselves &#124;&#124; In the abundance of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Psalms 35:16; Psalms 31:13"> The wicked is devising against the righteous, &#124;&#124; And gnashing his teeth against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Proverbs 1:26; 1 Samuel 26:10"> The Lord laughs at him, &#124;&#124; For He has seen that his day comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 29:27; Psalms 11:2; 1 John 3:12"> The wicked have opened a sword, &#124;&#124; And they have bent their bow, &#124;&#124; To cause the poor and needy to fall, &#124;&#124; To slaughter the upright of the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 7:14–15; Psalms 35:8; Jeremiah 51:56"> Their sword enters into their own heart, &#124;&#124; And their bows are shattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Proverbs 15:16–17; 1 Timothy 6:6"> Better &#91;is&#93; the little of the righteous, &#124;&#124; Than the store of many wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 63:8; Psalms 145:14"> For the arms of the wicked are shattered, &#124;&#124; And YHWH is sustaining the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 1:6; Romans 6:23; Psalms 103:17"> YHWH knows the days of the perfect, &#124;&#124; And their inheritance is for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 33:19; Proverbs 10:3; Job 5:20–22"> They are not ashamed in a time of evil, &#124;&#124; And they are satisfied in days of famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 68:2; Psalms 102:3; Luke 13:3"> But the wicked perish, and the enemies of YHWH, &#124;&#124; Have been consumed as the preciousness of lambs, &#124;&#124; They have been consumed in smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Psalms 112:5; Proverbs 22:7"> The wicked is borrowing and does not repay, &#124;&#124; And the righteous is gracious and giving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 3:33; 1 Corinthians 16:22; Galatians 3:13"> For His blessed ones possess the land, &#124;&#124; And His reviled ones are cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 16:9; Psalms 119:133; Proverbs 4:26"> The steps of a man &#91;are&#93; from YHWH, &#124;&#124; They have been prepared, &#124;&#124; And He desires his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 24:16; Psalms 145:14; Psalms 94:18"> When he falls, he is not cast down, &#124;&#124; For YHWH is sustaining his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 37:28; Psalms 112:2"> I have been young, &#124;&#124; I have also become old, &#124;&#124; And I have not seen the righteous forsaken, &#124;&#124; And his seed seeking bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 112:5; Matthew 5:7; Psalms 37:21"> All the day he is gracious and lending, &#124;&#124; And his seed &#91;is&#93; for a blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 34:14; Proverbs 16:6; 1 John 2:16–17"> Turn aside from evil, and do good, and dwell for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 11:7; Proverbs 2:22; Psalms 21:10"> For YHWH is loving judgment, &#124;&#124; And He does not forsake His saintly ones, &#124;&#124; They have been kept for all time, &#124;&#124; And the seed of the wicked is cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 2:21; Psalms 37:9; 2 Peter 3:13"> The righteous possess the land, &#124;&#124; And they dwell on it forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Proverbs 25:11–13; Ephesians 4:29"> The mouth of the righteous utters wisdom, &#124;&#124; And his tongue speaks judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 40:8; Deuteronomy 6:6; Isaiah 51:7"> The law of his God &#91;is&#93; his heart, &#124;&#124; His steps do not slide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 19:47–48; Psalms 10:8–10; Psalms 37:12"> The wicked is watching for the righteous, &#124;&#124; And is seeking to put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 109:31; 2 Peter 2:9; Romans 8:1"> YHWH does not leave him in his hand, &#124;&#124; Nor condemn him in his being judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Psalms 37:9; Proverbs 20:22"> Look to YHWH, and keep His way, &#124;&#124; And He exalts you to possess the land, &#124;&#124; In the wicked being cut off—you see!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 5:3; Ezekiel 31:18; Isaiah 14:14–19"> I have seen the wicked terrible, &#124;&#124; And spreading as a green native plant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 37:10; Acts 12:22–23; Isaiah 10:16–19"> And he passes away, and behold, he is not, &#124;&#124; And I seek him, and he is not found!</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 32:17; Isaiah 57:1–2; 2 Timothy 4:6–8"> Observe the perfect, and see the upright, &#124;&#124; For the latter end of each &#91;is&#93; peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Proverbs 14:32; Psalms 52:5; Psalms 1:4–6"> And transgressors were destroyed together, &#124;&#124; The latter end of the wicked was cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 91:15; Isaiah 12:2"> And the salvation of the righteous &#91;is&#93; from YHWH, &#124;&#124; Their strong place in a time of adversity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 31:5; 1 Chronicles 5:20; Daniel 6:23"> And YHWH helps them and delivers them, &#124;&#124; He delivers them from the wicked, &#124;&#124; And saves them, &#124;&#124; Because they trusted in Him!</span>
