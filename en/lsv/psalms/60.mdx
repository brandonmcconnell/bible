<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:12–13; 2 Samuel 8:3; Psalms 44:9"> TO THE OVERSEER. &#91;SET&#93; ON “LILY OF TESTIMONY.” A MIKTAM OF DAVID, TO TEACH, IN HIS STRIVING WITH ARAM-NAHARAIM AND WITH ARAM-ZOBAH, WHEN JOAB TURNS BACK AND STRIKES EDOM IN THE VALLEY OF SALT—TWELVE THOUSAND. O God, You had cast us off, &#124;&#124; You had broken us—had been angry! You turn back to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; Hosea 6:1; Isaiah 30:26"> You have caused the land to tremble, &#124;&#124; You have broken it, &#124;&#124; Heal its breaches, for it has moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 71:20; Isaiah 51:22; Isaiah 51:17"> You have shown Your people a hard thing, &#124;&#124; You have caused us to drink wine of trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 20:5; Isaiah 11:12; Exodus 17:15"> You have given an ensign to those fearing You, &#124;&#124; To be lifted up as an ensign &#124;&#124; Because of truth. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 108:6–13; Psalms 17:7; Isaiah 41:10"> That Your beloved ones may be drawn out, &#124;&#124; Save &#91;with&#93; Your right hand, and answer us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 13:27; Psalms 89:35; Genesis 12:6"> God has spoken in His holiness: I exult—I apportion Shechem, &#124;&#124; And I measure the Valley of Succoth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 49:10; Deuteronomy 33:17; Joshua 13:31"> Gilead &#91;is&#93; Mine, and Manasseh &#91;is&#93; Mine, &#124;&#124; And Ephraim &#91;is&#93; the strength of My head, &#124;&#124; Judah &#91;is&#93; My lawgiver,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; 2 Samuel 8:1–2; Psalms 108:9–10"> Moab &#91;is&#93; My pot for washing, &#124;&#124; Over Edom I cast My shoe, &#124;&#124; Shout, concerning Me, O Philistia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:26–31; 2 Samuel 11:1; Judges 1:12"> Who brings me &#91;to&#93; a city of bulwarks? Who has led me to Edom?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 108:11; Joshua 7:12; Psalms 60:1"> Is it not You, O God? Have You cast us off? And do You not go forth, O God, with our hosts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 146:3; Psalms 108:12; Psalms 130:8"> Give to us help from adversity, &#124;&#124; And the deliverance of man &#91;is&#93; vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 44:5; Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 63:3"> We do mightily in God, &#124;&#124; And He treads down our adversaries!</span>
