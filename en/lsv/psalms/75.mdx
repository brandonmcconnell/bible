<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; Psalms 138:2; Jeremiah 10:6"> TO THE OVERSEER. “DO NOT DESTROY.” A PSALM OF ASAPH. A SONG. <span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:17; Acts 17:31; Acts 1:7"> When I receive an appointment, I judge uprightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; Isaiah 24:19; Hebrews 1:3"> The earth and all its inhabitants are melted, &#124;&#124; I have pondered its pillars. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 1:21; Proverbs 1:22; Proverbs 8:5"> I have said to the boastful, “Do not be boastful,” &#124;&#124; And to the wicked, “Do not raise up a horn.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Ezekiel 2:4; Isaiah 48:4"> Do not raise up your horn on high &#124;&#124; (You speak with a stiff neck).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 3:3"> For not from the east, or from the west, &#124;&#124; Nor from the wilderness—&#91;is&#93; elevation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 1:52; Daniel 2:21–22; Psalms 147:6"> But God &#91;is&#93; judging, &#124;&#124; This He makes low—and this He lifts up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 21:20; Jeremiah 25:15; Psalms 11:6"> For a cup &#91;is&#93; in the hand of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And the wine has foamed, &#124;&#124; It is full of mixture, and He pours out of it, &#124;&#124; Surely wring out its dregs, &#124;&#124; And all the wicked of the earth drink,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 9:14; Psalms 145:1–2; Psalms 40:10"> And I declare &#91;it&#93; for all time, &#124;&#124; I sing praise to the God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 92:10; Psalms 89:17; Jeremiah 48:25"> And I cut off all horns of the wicked, &#124;&#124; The horns of the righteous are exalted!</span>
