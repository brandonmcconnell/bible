<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 34:4; Psalms 143:1–3; Lamentations 3:55–56"> TO THE OVERSEER. A PSALM OF DAVID. Hear, O God, my voice, in my meditation, &#124;&#124; You keep my life from the fear of an enemy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 143:9; Psalms 59:2; Psalms 109:2–3"> Hide me from the secret counsel of evildoers, &#124;&#124; From the tumult of workers of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 58:7; James 3:6–8"> Who sharpened their tongue as a sword, &#124;&#124; They directed their arrow—a bitter word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 55:19; Psalms 11:2; 1 Peter 2:22–23"> To shoot the perfect in secret places, &#124;&#124; Suddenly they shoot him, and do not fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 140:5; Psalms 10:11; Ezekiel 8:12"> They strengthen an evil thing for themselves, &#124;&#124; They recount of the hiding of snares, &#124;&#124; They have said, “Who looks at it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9–10; Matthew 26:59; Psalms 5:9"> They search out perverse things, “We perfected a searching search,” &#124;&#124; And the inward part of man, and the heart, &#91;are&#93; deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Proverbs 6:15"> And God shoots them &#91;with&#93; an arrow, &#124;&#124; Their wounds have been sudden,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 12:13; Psalms 140:9"> And they cause him to stumble, &#124;&#124; Their own tongue &#91;is&#93; against them, &#124;&#124; Every looker on them flees away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:10; Jeremiah 50:28; Psalms 40:3"> And all men fear, and declare the work of God, &#124;&#124; And they have wisely considered His deed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 32:11–1; Psalms 40:3; 1 Corinthians 1:30–31"> The righteous rejoice in YHWH, &#124;&#124; And have trusted in Him, &#124;&#124; And all the upright of heart boast!</span>
