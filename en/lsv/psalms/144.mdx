<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:34; 2 Samuel 22:35; Psalms 18:2"> BY DAVID. Blessed &#91;is&#93; YHWH my Rock, &#124;&#124; Who is teaching My hands for war, &#124;&#124; My fingers for battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:2–3; Psalms 18:2; Psalms 91:2"> My kind one, and my bulwark, &#124;&#124; My tower, and my deliverer, &#124;&#124; My shield, and in whom I have trusted, &#124;&#124; Who is subduing my people under me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 8:4; Job 7:17; Hebrews 2:6"> YHWH, what &#91;is&#93; man that You know him? The son of man, that You esteem him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 102:11; Psalms 39:5–6; Psalms 109:23"> Man has been like a breath, &#124;&#124; His days &#91;are&#93; as a shadow passing by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 104:32; Psalms 18:9; Exodus 19:18"> YHWH, incline Your heavens and come down, &#124;&#124; Strike against mountains, and they smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Psalms 18:13–14; Deuteronomy 32:42"> Send forth lightning, and scatter them, &#124;&#124; Send forth Your arrows, and trouble them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Psalms 18:16; Psalms 54:3"> Send forth Your hand from on high, &#124;&#124; Free me, and deliver me from many waters, &#124;&#124; From the hand of sons of a stranger,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; Psalms 41:6; Psalms 62:4"> Because their mouth has spoken vanity, &#124;&#124; And their right hand &#91;is&#93; a right hand of falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 33:2–3; Psalms 40:3; Psalms 149:1"> O God, I sing to You a new song, &#124;&#124; I sing praise to You on a stringed instrument of ten strings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 18:50; Psalms 140:7; Psalms 33:16–18"> Who is giving deliverance to kings, &#124;&#124; Who is freeing His servant David from the sword of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 144:7–8; Isaiah 44:20; Psalms 12:2"> Free me, and deliver me &#124;&#124; From the hand of sons of a stranger, &#124;&#124; Because their mouth has spoken vanity, &#124;&#124; And their right hand &#91;is&#93; a right hand of falsehood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 128:3; 1 Peter 3:3–6; Isaiah 44:3–5"> Because our sons &#91;are&#93; as plants, &#124;&#124; Becoming great in their youth, &#124;&#124; Our daughters as hewn stones, &#124;&#124; Polished—the likeness of a palace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:13–14; Leviticus 26:10; Malachi 3:10"> Our granaries &#91;are&#93; full, bringing out from kind to kind, &#124;&#124; Our flocks are bringing forth thousands, &#124;&#124; Ten thousands in our out-places,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3–5; Deuteronomy 28:7; Isaiah 24:11"> Our oxen are carrying, there is no breach, &#124;&#124; And there is no outgoing, &#124;&#124; And there is no crying in our broad places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 33:12; Ephesians 1:3; Psalms 146:5"> O the blessedness of the people that is thus, &#124;&#124; O the blessedness of the people whose God &#91;is&#93; YHWH!</span>
