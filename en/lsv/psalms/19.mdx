<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 1:19–20; Psalms 8:3; Psalms 50:6"> TO THE OVERSEER. A PSALM OF DAVID. The heavens &#91;are&#93; recounting the glory of God, &#124;&#124; And the expanse &#91;is&#93; declaring the work of His hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 74:16; Psalms 134:1–3; Genesis 8:22"> Day to day utters speech, &#124;&#124; And night to night shows knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:19"> There is no speech, and there are no words. Their voice has not been heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 10:18; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalms 98:3"> Their line has gone forth into all the earth, &#124;&#124; And their sayings to the end of the world, &#124;&#124; In them He placed a tent for the sun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 62:5; Philippians 3:13–14; 1 Corinthians 9:24–26"> And he, as a bridegroom, goes out from his covering, &#124;&#124; He rejoices as a mighty one &#124;&#124; To run the path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:5; Job 22:14; Colossians 1:23"> From the end of the heavens &#91;is&#93; his going out, &#124;&#124; And his revolution &#91;is&#93; to their ends, &#124;&#124; And nothing is hid from his heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 119:130; Psalms 111:7; Colossians 3:16"> The law of YHWH &#91;is&#93; perfect, refreshing the soul, &#124;&#124; The testimonies of YHWH &#91;are&#93; steadfast, &#124;&#124; Making the simple wise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:105; Psalms 12:6; Jeremiah 15:16"> The precepts of YHWH &#91;are&#93; upright, &#124;&#124; Rejoicing the heart, &#124;&#124; The command of YHWH &#91;is&#93; pure, enlightening the eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:24; Psalms 119:142; Psalms 119:137–138"> The fear of YHWH &#91;is&#93; clean, standing for all time, &#124;&#124; The judgments of YHWH &#91;are&#93; true, &#124;&#124; They have been righteous—together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:103; Proverbs 3:13–15; Psalms 119:72"> They are more desirable than gold, &#124;&#124; Indeed, than much fine gold; and sweeter than honey, &#124;&#124; Even liquid honey of the comb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; 2 John 1:8; Hebrews 11:6–7"> Also—Your servant is warned by them, “In keeping them &#91;is&#93; a great reward.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 90:8; Job 6:24; Psalms 139:23–24"> &#91;His&#93; errors—who understands? Declare me innocent from hidden ones,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 119:133; Romans 6:12–14; 1 Samuel 25:39"> Also—keep back Your servant from presumptuous ones, &#124;&#124; Do not let them rule over me, &#124;&#124; Then I am perfect, &#124;&#124; And declared innocent of much transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:34; Psalms 51:15; Psalms 119:108"> Let the sayings of my mouth, &#124;&#124; And the meditation of my heart, &#124;&#124; Be for a pleasing thing before You, &#124;&#124; O YHWH, my rock, and my redeemer!</span>
