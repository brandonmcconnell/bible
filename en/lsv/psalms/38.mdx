<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 6:1; Isaiah 54:8; Hebrews 12:5–11"> A PSALM OF DAVID. “TO CAUSE TO REMEMBER.” YHWH, do not reprove me in Your wrath, &#124;&#124; Nor discipline me in Your fury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:4; Psalms 32:4; Psalms 64:7"> For Your arrows have come down on me, &#124;&#124; And You let down Your hand on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:2; Psalms 51:8; Psalms 31:9–10"> Soundness is not in my flesh, &#124;&#124; Because of Your indignation, &#124;&#124; Peace is not in my bones, &#124;&#124; Because of my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Ezra 9:6; Matthew 11:28"> For my iniquities have passed over my head, &#124;&#124; As a heavy burden—too heavy for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 69:5; Jeremiah 8:22; Isaiah 1:5–6"> Stunk—my wounds have become corrupt, &#124;&#124; Because of my folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 30:28; Psalms 35:14; Psalms 42:9"> I have been bent down, &#124;&#124; I have been bowed down—to excess, &#124;&#124; I have gone mourning all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:18–19; Acts 12:23; Psalms 102:3"> For my flanks have been full of drought, &#124;&#124; And soundness is not in my flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 3:24; Psalms 32:3; Job 30:28"> I have been feeble and struck—to excess, &#124;&#124; I have roared from disquietude of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:17; Psalms 6:6; 2 Corinthians 5:2"> Lord, all my desire &#91;is&#93; before You, &#124;&#124; And my sighing has not been hid from You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:7; Psalms 88:9; Psalms 69:3"> My heart &#91;is&#93; panting, my power has forsaken me, &#124;&#124; And the light of my eyes, &#124;&#124; Even they are not with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Job 19:13–17; Luke 23:49"> My lovers and my friends stand aloof from before my plague. And my neighbors have stood far off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:20; Psalms 140:5; 2 Samuel 17:1–3"> And those seeking my soul lay a snare, &#124;&#124; And those seeking my evil &#124;&#124; Have spoken mischievous things, &#124;&#124; And they meditate &#91;on&#93; deceits all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 39:2; Isaiah 53:7; Psalms 39:9"> And I, as deaf, do not hear. And as a mute one who does not open his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:13; John 8:6; Mark 15:3–5"> Indeed, I am as a man who does not hear, &#124;&#124; And in his mouth are no reproofs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 39:7; Psalms 17:6; Psalms 138:3"> Because for You, O YHWH, I have waited, &#124;&#124; You answer, O Lord my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 94:18; Deuteronomy 32:35; Psalms 35:24–26"> When I said, “Lest they rejoice over me, &#124;&#124; In the slipping of my foot they magnified themselves against me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 38:6; Psalms 6:6; Psalms 35:15"> For I am ready to halt, &#124;&#124; And my pain &#91;is&#93; continually before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 32:5; Proverbs 28:13; Job 33:27"> For I declare my iniquity, &#124;&#124; I am sorry for my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; John 15:18–25; Psalms 69:4"> And my enemies &#91;are&#93; lively, &#124;&#124; They have been strong, and those hating me without cause, &#124;&#124; Have been multiplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:12; 1 John 3:12; Jeremiah 18:20"> And those paying evil for good accuse me, &#124;&#124; Because of my pursuing good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:19; Psalms 22:24; Psalms 22:11"> Do not forsake me, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; My God, do not be far from me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; Psalms 40:13; Isaiah 12:2"> Hurry to help me, O Lord, my salvation!</span>
