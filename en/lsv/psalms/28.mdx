<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 143:7; Psalms 18:2; Proverbs 1:12"> BY DAVID. To You, O YHWH, I call, &#124;&#124; My rock, do not be silent to me! Lest You are silent to me, &#124;&#124; And I have been compared &#124;&#124; With those going down to the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 138:2; Psalms 141:2; Psalms 140:6"> Hear the voice of my supplications, &#124;&#124; In my crying to You, &#124;&#124; In my lifting up my hands toward your holy oracle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 55:21; Psalms 12:2; Jeremiah 9:8–9"> Do not draw me with the wicked, &#124;&#124; And with workers of iniquity, &#124;&#124; Speaking peace with their neighbors, &#124;&#124; And evil in their heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 22:12; 2 Timothy 4:14; Revelation 18:6"> Give to them according to their acting, &#124;&#124; And according to the evil of their doings. Give to them according to the work of their hands. Return their deed to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:12; Romans 1:28; John 12:37"> For they do not attend to the doing of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And to the work of His hands. He throws them down, &#124;&#124; And does not build them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 31:21–22; Psalms 107:19–22; Psalms 118:5"> Blessed &#91;is&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; For He has heard the voice of my supplications.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 30:11–12; Psalms 56:3–4; Isaiah 61:10"> YHWH &#91;is&#93; my strength, and my shield, &#124;&#124; In Him my heart trusted, and I have been helped. And my heart exults, &#124;&#124; And I thank Him with my song.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 20:6; Psalms 140:7; Isaiah 61:1"> YHWH &#91;is&#93; strength to him, &#124;&#124; Indeed, the strength of the salvation of His anointed &#91;is&#93; He.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; Psalms 78:71; Deuteronomy 9:29"> Save Your people, and bless Your inheritance, &#124;&#124; And feed them, and carry them for all time!</span>
