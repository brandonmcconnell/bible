<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 47:6–8; Psalms 45:6; Psalms 34:1"> A PRAISE &#91;SONG&#93; OF DAVID. &#91;ALEPH-BET&#93; I exalt You, my God, O king, &#124;&#124; And bless Your Name for all time and forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 7:15; Psalms 71:6; Psalms 119:164"> Every day I bless You, &#124;&#124; And praise Your Name for all time and forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 147:5; Romans 11:33; Job 5:9"> YHWH &#91;is&#93; great, and greatly praised, &#124;&#124; And there is no searching of His greatness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 78:3–7; Psalms 71:18; Deuteronomy 6:7"> Generation to generation praises Your works, &#124;&#124; And they declare Your mighty acts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 12:4; Psalms 119:27; Psalms 72:18"> The majesty, the glory of Your splendor, &#124;&#124; And the matters of Your wonders, I declare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Daniel 3:28–29; Psalms 107:31–32"> And they tell of the strength of Your fearful acts, &#124;&#124; And I recount Your greatness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 63:7; Psalms 36:10; 2 Corinthians 9:11–12"> They send forth the memorial of the abundance of Your goodness. And they sing of Your righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 14:18; Psalms 103:8; Psalms 86:5"> YHWH &#91;is&#93; gracious and merciful, &#124;&#124; Slow to anger, and great in kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nahum 1:7; Psalms 100:5; Matthew 5:45"> YHWH &#91;is&#93; good to all, &#124;&#124; And His mercies &#91;are&#93; over all His works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 19:1; Psalms 103:22; Revelation 7:9–12"> O YHWH, all Your works confess You, &#124;&#124; And Your saints bless You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; 1 Chronicles 29:11–12; Psalms 93:1–2"> They tell of the glory of Your kingdom, &#124;&#124; And they speak of Your might,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 12:10; Psalms 105:1; Matthew 28:18"> To make His mighty acts known to sons of men, &#124;&#124; The glory of the majesty of His kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Daniel 7:14; Psalms 10:16"> Your kingdom &#91;is&#93; a kingdom of all ages, &#124;&#124; And Your dominion &#91;is&#93; in all generations. &#91;&#91;YHWH &#91;is&#93; faithful in all His words, &#124;&#124; And kind in all His works.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 146:8; Psalms 37:24; Psalms 94:18"> YHWH is supporting all who are falling, &#124;&#124; And raising up all who are bowed down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Psalms 104:27; Acts 17:25"> The eyes of all look to You, &#124;&#124; And You are giving their food to them in its season,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 104:28; Psalms 107:9; Psalms 132:15"> Opening Your hand, and satisfying &#124;&#124; The desire of every living thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Zephaniah 3:5; Isaiah 45:21"> YHWH &#91;is&#93; righteous in all His ways, &#124;&#124; And kind in all His works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12–13; Psalms 34:18; Deuteronomy 4:7"> YHWH &#91;is&#93; near to all those calling Him, &#124;&#124; To all who call Him in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Ephesians 3:16–20; John 15:7"> He does the desire of those fearing Him, &#124;&#124; And He hears their cry, and saves them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:6; John 10:27–29; Romans 8:28–30"> YHWH preserves all those loving Him, &#124;&#124; And He destroys all the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 71:8; Psalms 51:15; Psalms 150:6"> My mouth speaks the praise of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And all flesh blesses His Holy Name, &#124;&#124; For all time and forever!</span>
