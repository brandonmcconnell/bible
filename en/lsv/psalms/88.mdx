<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 22:2; Luke 18:7"> A SONG. A PSALM OF THE SONS OF KORAH. TO THE OVERSEER. &#91;SET&#93; ON “SICKNESS TO AFFLICT.” AN INSTRUCTION OF HEMAN THE EZRAHITE. O YHWH, God of my salvation, &#124;&#124; Daily I have cried, nightly before You,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 31:2; Psalms 141:1–2; Lamentations 3:8"> My prayer comes in before You, &#124;&#124; Incline Your ear to my loud cry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 107:18; Lamentations 3:15–19; Mark 14:33–34"> For my soul has been full of evils, &#124;&#124; And my life has come to Sheol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; 2 Corinthians 1:9; Psalms 143:7"> I have been reckoned with those going down &#91;to&#93; the pit, &#124;&#124; I have been as a man without strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:8; Psalms 31:22; Isaiah 14:9–12"> Among the dead—free, &#124;&#124; As pierced ones lying in the grave, &#124;&#124; Whom You have not remembered anymore, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they have been cut off by Your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:15; Psalms 86:13; Psalms 143:3"> You have put me in the lowest pit, &#124;&#124; In dark places, in depths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Job 6:4; Romans 2:5–9"> Your fury has lain on me, &#124;&#124; And You have afflicted &#91;with&#93; all Your breakers. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Isaiah 49:7; Jeremiah 32:2"> You have put my acquaintance far from me, &#124;&#124; You have made me an abomination to them, &#124;&#124; Shut up—I do not go forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 11:13; Psalms 38:10; Psalms 86:3"> My eye has grieved because of affliction, &#124;&#124; I called You, O YHWH, all the day, &#124;&#124; I have spread out my hands to You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:5; Isaiah 26:19; Psalms 30:9"> Do You do wonders to the dead? Does Rephaim rise? Do they thank You? Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Proverbs 15:11; Psalms 73:18"> Is Your kindness recounted in the grave? Your faithfulness in destruction?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Isaiah 8:22"> Are Your wonders known in the darkness? And Your righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:3; Psalms 30:2; Mark 1:35"> And I, to You, O YHWH, I have cried, &#124;&#124; And in the morning my prayer comes before You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 13:24; Psalms 13:1; Psalms 44:24"> Why, O YHWH, do You cast off my soul? You hide Your face from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:4; Luke 22:44; Job 7:11–16"> I &#91;am&#93; afflicted, and expiring from youth, &#124;&#124; I have borne Your terrors—I pine away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 90:11; Isaiah 53:4–6; Romans 8:32"> Your wrath has passed over me, &#124;&#124; Your terrors have cut me off,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 22:16; Psalms 116:3; Psalms 69:1–2"> They have surrounded me as waters all the day, &#124;&#124; They have gone around against me together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 88:8; Psalms 38:11; Job 19:12–15"> You have put lover and friend far from me, &#124;&#124; My acquaintance &#91;is&#93; the place of darkness!</span>
