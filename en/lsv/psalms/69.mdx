<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 69:14–15; Isaiah 43:2; Lamentations 3:54"> TO THE OVERSEER. &#91;SET&#93; ON “LILIES.” BY DAVID. Save me, O God, for waters have come to the soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 40:2; Jeremiah 38:6; Matthew 7:25"> I have sunk in deep mire, &#124;&#124; And there is no standing, &#124;&#124; I have come into the depths of the waters, &#124;&#124; And a flood has overflown me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Psalms 119:82; Isaiah 38:14"> I have been wearied with my calling, &#124;&#124; My throat has been burned, &#124;&#124; My eyes have been consumed, waiting for my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:25; Psalms 35:19; Psalms 109:3–5"> Those hating me without cause &#124;&#124; Have been more than the hairs of my head, &#124;&#124; Mighty have been my destroyers, &#124;&#124; My lying enemies, &#124;&#124; That which I did not take away—I bring back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 38:9; Psalms 17:3; Jeremiah 16:17"> O God, You have known &#124;&#124; Concerning my overturn, &#124;&#124; And my desolations have not been hid from You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 25:3; Psalms 35:26; Luke 24:19–21"> Do not let those waiting on You be ashamed because of me, &#124;&#124; O Lord, YHWH of Hosts, &#124;&#124; Do not let those seeking You &#124;&#124; Blush because of me, &#124;&#124; O God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 44:22; Jeremiah 15:15; Isaiah 50:6"> For because of You I have borne reproach, &#124;&#124; Shame has covered my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; John 1:11; Matthew 26:70–74"> I have been a stranger to my brother, &#124;&#124; And a foreigner to sons of my mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 15:3; John 2:14–17; Psalms 119:139"> For &#91;my&#93; zeal for Your house has consumed me, &#124;&#124; And the reproaches of Your reproachers &#124;&#124; Have fallen on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:13; Psalms 102:8–9; Luke 7:33–34"> And I weep in the fasting of my soul, &#124;&#124; And it is for a reproach to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 9:7; Jeremiah 24:9; Job 17:6"> And I make my clothing sackcloth, &#124;&#124; And I am for an allegory to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:15–16; Mark 15:17–19; Daniel 5:23"> Those sitting at the gate meditate concerning me, &#124;&#124; And those drinking strong drink, &#124;&#124; Play on instruments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:2; Isaiah 49:8; Psalms 55:16–17"> And my prayer &#91;is&#93; to You, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; A time of good pleasure, O God, &#124;&#124; In the abundance of Your kindness, &#124;&#124; Answer me in the truth of Your salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 144:7; Psalms 109:21; Jeremiah 38:6–13"> Deliver me from the mire, and do not let me sink, &#124;&#124; Let me be delivered from those hating me, &#124;&#124; And from deep places of waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 12:15–16; Acts 2:24; Jonah 2:2–7"> Do not let a flood of waters overflow me, &#124;&#124; Nor let the deep swallow me up, &#124;&#124; Nor let the pit shut her mouth on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 109:21; Psalms 25:16; Psalms 63:3"> Answer me, O YHWH, for Your kindness &#91;is&#93; good, &#124;&#124; Turn to me according to the abundance of Your mercies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 27:9; Psalms 102:2; Psalms 70:1"> And do not hide Your face from Your servant, &#124;&#124; For I am in distress—hurry, answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 22:19; Psalms 22:1; Psalms 111:9"> Be near to my soul—redeem it, &#124;&#124; Ransom me because of my enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 53:3; 1 Peter 2:23"> You have known my reproach, &#124;&#124; And my shame, and my blushing, &#124;&#124; All my adversaries &#91;are&#93; before You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 63:5; Psalms 142:4; Mark 14:50"> Reproach has broken my heart, and I am sick, &#124;&#124; And I look for a bemoaner, and there is none, &#124;&#124; And for comforters, and I have found none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36; Mark 15:23"> And they give gall for my food, &#124;&#124; And cause me to drink vinegar for my thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Romans 11:8–10; 1 Peter 2:8"> Their table before them is for a snare, &#124;&#124; And for a repayment—for a trap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 5:6; Romans 11:10; Isaiah 6:9–10"> Their eyes are darkened from seeing, &#124;&#124; And their loins continually shake You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 79:6; Hosea 5:10; Exodus 15:15"> Pour Your indignation on them, &#124;&#124; And the fierceness of Your anger seizes them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 1:20; Matthew 23:38; Jeremiah 7:12–14"> Their tower is desolated, &#124;&#124; There is no dweller in their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4; Zechariah 1:15; 2 Chronicles 28:9"> For they have pursued him &#91;whom&#93; You have struck, &#124;&#124; And recount of the pain of Your pierced ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; Nehemiah 4:5; 2 Timothy 4:14"> Give punishment for their iniquity, &#124;&#124; And they do not enter into Your righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Philippians 4:3; Exodus 32:32–33; Luke 10:20"> They are blotted out of the scroll of life, &#124;&#124; And are not written with the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 91:14–16; Psalms 40:17; Psalms 20:1"> And I &#91;am&#93; afflicted and pained, &#124;&#124; Your salvation, O God, sets me on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 28:7; Psalms 34:3; Psalms 118:28–29"> I praise the Name of God with a song, &#124;&#124; And I magnify Him with thanksgiving,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 50:13–14; Psalms 50:23"> And it is better to YHWH than an ox, &#124;&#124; A bullock—horned &#91;and&#93; hoofed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 22:26; Psalms 34:2; John 16:22"> The humble have seen—they rejoice, &#124;&#124; You who seek God—and your heart lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 68:6; Acts 5:18–19; Zechariah 9:11–12"> For YHWH listens to the needy, &#124;&#124; And He has not despised His bound ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 96:11; Isaiah 49:13; Psalms 150:6"> The heavens and earth praise Him, &#124;&#124; Seas, and every moving thing in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 44:26; Psalms 51:18; Isaiah 46:13"> For God saves Zion, &#124;&#124; And builds the cities of Judah, &#124;&#124; And they have dwelt there, and possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 102:28; Psalms 37:29; Acts 2:39"> And the seed of His servants inherit it, &#124;&#124; And those loving His Name dwell in it!</span>
