<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 17:23; Daniel 4:1–2; 1 Chronicles 29:10–12"> TO THE OVERSEER. WITH STRINGED INSTRUMENTS. A PSALM OF ASAPH. A SONG. God &#91;is&#93; known in Judah, &#124;&#124; His Name &#91;is&#93; great in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 14:18; Isaiah 12:6; Psalms 132:13–14"> And His dwelling place is in Salem, &#124;&#124; And His habitation in Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 46:9; Ezekiel 39:3–4; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> There He has shattered arrows of a bow, &#124;&#124; Shield, and sword, and battle. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1–4; Daniel 7:17–28; Ezekiel 19:6"> You &#91;are&#93; bright, majestic above hills of prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nahum 3:18; Psalms 13:3; Isaiah 46:12"> The mighty of heart have spoiled themselves, &#124;&#124; They have slept their sleep, &#124;&#124; And none of the men of might found their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Psalms 80:16; Exodus 15:21"> From Your rebuke, O God of Jacob, &#124;&#124; Both rider and horse have been fast asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Psalms 2:12; Psalms 89:7"> You, fearful &#91;are&#93; You, &#124;&#124; And who stands before You, &#124;&#124; Since You have been angry?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:29–30; Habakkuk 2:20; Judges 5:20"> You have sounded judgment from the heavens, &#124;&#124; Earth has feared, and has been still,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:5; Zephaniah 2:3; Isaiah 11:4"> In the rising of God to judgment, &#124;&#124; To save all the humble of earth. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:17; Matthew 24:22; Daniel 3:19–20"> For the fierceness of man praises You, &#124;&#124; You gird on the remnant of fierceness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:4–6; Psalms 50:14; Psalms 68:29"> Vow and complete to your God YHWH, &#124;&#124; All you surrounding Him, &#124;&#124; They bring presents to the Fearful One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:6; 2 Chronicles 32:21; Psalms 68:12"> He gathers the spirit of leaders, &#124;&#124; Fearful to the kings of earth!</span>
