<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 119:154; Jeremiah 51:36"> BY DAVID. Strive, YHWH, with my strivers, fight with my fighters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 91:4; Exodus 15:3; Isaiah 42:13"> Take hold of shield and buckler, and rise for my help,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 27:2; Psalms 51:12"> And draw out spear and lance, &#124;&#124; To meet my pursuers. Say to my soul, “I &#91;am&#93; your salvation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 70:2–3; Jeremiah 46:5; Psalms 35:26"> They are ashamed and blush, those seeking my soul, &#124;&#124; Turned backward and confounded, &#124;&#124; Those devising my evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 21:18; Isaiah 29:5; Isaiah 37:36"> They are as chaff before wind, &#124;&#124; And a messenger of YHWH driving away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:12; Psalms 73:18; Jeremiah 13:16"> Their way is darkness and slipperiness, &#124;&#124; And a messenger of YHWH—their pursuer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:25; Psalms 140:5; Psalms 9:15"> For without cause they hid their net &#91;in&#93; a pit for me, &#124;&#124; Without cause they dug for my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 141:9–10; Proverbs 5:22; Luke 21:34"> Desolation meets him—he does not know, &#124;&#124; And his net that he hid catches him, &#124;&#124; He falls into it for desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Psalms 58:10–11; Luke 1:46–47"> And my soul is joyful in YHWH, &#124;&#124; It rejoices in His salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 140:12; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 51:8"> All my bones say, “YHWH, who is like You, &#124;&#124; Delivering the poor from the &#91;one&#93; stronger than he, &#124;&#124; And the poor and needy from his plunderer.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:12; Matthew 26:59–60; Acts 24:5–6"> Violent witnesses rise up, &#124;&#124; That which I have not known they ask me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 10:32; Jeremiah 18:20; Psalms 38:20"> They pay me evil for good, bereaving my soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 69:10–11; Job 30:25; Matthew 5:44"> And I—in their sickness my clothing &#91;is&#93; sackcloth, &#124;&#124; I have humbled my soul with fastings, &#124;&#124; And my prayer returns to my bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:41–42; 2 Samuel 1:11–12; Genesis 24:67"> As &#91;if&#93; a friend, as &#91;if&#93; my brother, &#124;&#124; I habitually walked, &#124;&#124; As a mourner for a mother, &#124;&#124; I have bowed down mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Psalms 7:2; Psalms 35:25–26"> And they have rejoiced in my halting, &#124;&#124; And have been gathered together, &#124;&#124; The strikers were gathered against me, &#124;&#124; And I have not known, &#124;&#124; They have torn, and they have not ceased;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Job 16:9; Psalms 37:12"> With profane ones, mockers in feasts, &#124;&#124; Gnashing their teeth against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:13; Psalms 22:20–21; Psalms 57:4"> Lord, how long do You behold? Keep my soul back from their desolations, &#124;&#124; My only one from young lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:12; Psalms 22:22–25; Psalms 116:18"> I thank You in a great assembly, &#124;&#124; I praise You among a mighty people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 69:4; John 15:25; Proverbs 6:13"> Do not let my enemies rejoice over me &#91;with&#93; falsehood, &#124;&#124; Those hating me without cause wink the eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 120:5–7; Matthew 12:19; Acts 23:15"> For they do not speak peace, &#124;&#124; And against the quiet of the land, &#124;&#124; They devise deceitful words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:13; Psalms 40:15; Psalms 70:3"> And they enlarge their mouth against me, &#124;&#124; They said, “Aha, aha, our eye has seen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 3:7; Psalms 10:1; Psalms 28:1"> You have seen, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; Do not be silent, O Lord—do not be far from me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6; Isaiah 51:9"> Stir up, and wake to my judgment, &#124;&#124; My God, and my Lord, to my plea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 26:1; Psalms 7:8"> Judge me according to Your righteousness, O YHWH my God, &#124;&#124; And they do not rejoice over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Psalms 124:3; 2 Samuel 20:19"> They do not say in their heart, “Aha, our desire.” They do not say, “We swallowed him up.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 38:16; Job 19:5; Psalms 132:18"> They are ashamed and confounded together, &#124;&#124; Who are rejoicing at my evil. They put on shame and confusion, &#124;&#124; Who are magnifying themselves against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:40–41; Psalms 149:4; Psalms 40:16"> They sing and rejoice, who are desiring my righteousness, &#124;&#124; And they continually say, “YHWH is magnified, &#124;&#124; Who is desiring the peace of His servant.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 145:21; Psalms 71:24; Psalms 145:5"> And my tongue utters Your righteousness, &#124;&#124; All the day Your praise!</span>
</div>
