<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 3:18; Proverbs 16:6; Psalms 143:12"> TO THE OVERSEER. BY A SERVANT OF YHWH, BY DAVID. The transgression of the wicked &#124;&#124; Is affirming within my heart, “Fear of God is not before his eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:19; Psalms 10:3; Psalms 49:18"> For he made &#91;it&#93; smooth to himself in his eyes, &#124;&#124; To find his iniquity to be hated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 55:21; Psalms 10:7; Jeremiah 4:22"> The words of his mouth &#91;are&#93; iniquity and deceit, &#124;&#124; He ceased to act prudently—to do good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Proverbs 4:16; Romans 12:9"> He devises iniquity on his bed, &#124;&#124; He stations himself on a way not good, &#124;&#124; He does not refuse evil.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 100:5; Psalms 108:4; Psalms 103:11"> O YHWH, Your kindness &#91;is&#93; in the heavens, &#124;&#124; Your faithfulness &#91;is&#93; to the clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 77:19; Romans 11:33; Psalms 71:19"> Your righteousness &#91;is&#93; as mountains of God, &#124;&#124; Your judgments &#91;are&#93; a great deep. You save man and beast, O YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 86:5; Psalms 91:4; Ruth 2:12"> How precious &#91;is&#93; Your kindness, O God, &#124;&#124; And the sons of men trust &#124;&#124; In the shadow of Your wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 65:4; Isaiah 58:11"> They are filled from the fatness of Your house, &#124;&#124; And You cause them to drink the stream of Your delights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 8:12; John 4:14; 1 John 1:7"> For a fountain of life &#91;is&#93; with You, &#124;&#124; In Your light we see light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Hebrews 8:11; Psalms 97:10–11"> Draw out Your kindness to those knowing You, &#124;&#124; And Your righteousness to the upright of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 16:8; Psalms 17:8–14; Psalms 119:51"> Do not let a foot of pride meet me, &#124;&#124; And do not let a hand of the wicked move me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 1:5; Psalms 58:10–11; Psalms 55:23"> Workers of iniquity have fallen there, &#124;&#124; They have been overthrown, &#124;&#124; And have not been able to arise!</span>
