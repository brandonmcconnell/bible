<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Psalms 62:7; Psalms 28:7"> TO THE OVERSEER. A PSALM OF DAVID. YHWH, the king is joyful in Your strength, &#124;&#124; How greatly he rejoices in Your salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Psalms 20:4–5; Psalms 2:8–9"> You gave the desire of his heart to him, &#124;&#124; And You have not withheld the request of his lips. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:30; Ephesians 1:3; Psalms 59:10"> For You put blessings of goodness before him, &#124;&#124; You set a crown of fine gold on his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 91:16; Revelation 1:18; Psalms 119:77"> He has asked for life from You, &#124;&#124; You have given length of days to him, &#124;&#124; For all time and forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; John 17:22; Revelation 5:8–13"> Great &#91;is&#93; his glory in Your salvation, &#124;&#124; You place splendor and majesty on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 2:28; Psalms 45:7; Psalms 16:11"> For You make him blessings forever, &#124;&#124; You cause him to rejoice with joy, &#124;&#124; By Your countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 16:8; Psalms 91:2; Psalms 20:7–8"> For the king is trusting in YHWH, &#124;&#124; And in the kindness of the Most High he is not moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 10:10; Psalms 72:9; Psalms 110:1–2"> Your hand comes to all Your enemies, &#124;&#124; Your right hand finds Your haters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Psalms 50:3; Psalms 18:8"> You make them as a furnace of fire, &#124;&#124; At the time of Your presence. YHWH swallows them in His anger, &#124;&#124; And fire devours them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:28; 1 Kings 13:34; Job 18:16–19"> You destroy their fruit from earth, &#124;&#124; And their seed from the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 10:2; Psalms 2:1; Psalms 31:13"> For they stretched out evil against You, &#124;&#124; They devised a wicked scheme, they do not prevail,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; Psalms 18:40; Psalms 64:7"> For You make them turn their back, &#124;&#124; When You prepare Your strings against their faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 16:5–7; Revelation 19:1–6; Revelation 15:3–4"> Be exalted, O YHWH, in Your strength, &#124;&#124; We sing and we praise Your might!</span>
