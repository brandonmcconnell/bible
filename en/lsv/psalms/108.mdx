<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 49:10; Psalms 122:5; 2 Samuel 2:8"> Gilead &#91;is&#93; Mine, Manasseh &#91;is&#93; Mine, &#124;&#124; And Ephraim &#91;is&#93; the strength of My head, &#124;&#124; Judah &#91;is&#93; My lawgiver,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 14:29–32; Ruth 4:7–8; 2 Samuel 21:15–22"> Moab &#91;is&#93; a pot for My washing, &#124;&#124; On Edom I cast My shoe, &#124;&#124; Over Philistia I habitually shout.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Obadiah 1:3–4; Isaiah 63:1–6; Psalms 20:6–8"> Who brings me &#91;into&#93; the fortified city? Who has led me to Edom?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:9; 2 Chronicles 13:12; 1 Samuel 17:36"> Have You not, O God, cast us off? And You do not go out, O God, with our hosts!</span>
