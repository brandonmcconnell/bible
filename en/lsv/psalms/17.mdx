<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 142:6; Psalms 61:1; 1 John 3:21"> A PRAYER OF DAVID. Hear, O YHWH, righteousness, attend my cry, &#124;&#124; Give ear &#91;to&#93; my prayer, without lips of deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:6; Ezekiel 18:25; 2 Thessalonians 1:6–9"> My judgment goes out from before You; Your eyes see uprightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 11:23; Job 23:10; Psalms 26:2"> You have proved my heart, &#124;&#124; You have inspected by night, &#124;&#124; You have tried me, You find nothing; My thoughts do not pass over my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Psalms 119:9–11; 1 Corinthians 3:3"> As for doings of man, &#124;&#124; Through a word of Your lips I have observed &#124;&#124; The paths of a destroyer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 44:18; Psalms 119:133; 1 Samuel 2:9"> To uphold my goings in Your paths, &#124;&#124; My steps have not slipped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 116:2; Psalms 86:7; Psalms 88:2"> I called You, for You answer me, &#124;&#124; O God, incline Your ear to me, hear my speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 60:5; Psalms 5:11–12; Psalms 31:21"> Separate Your kindness wonderfully, O Savior of the confiding, &#124;&#124; By Your right hand, from withstanders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:10; Psalms 91:4; Psalms 91:1"> Keep me as the apple, the daughter of the eye; Hide me in the shadow of Your wings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 35:7; Psalms 7:5"> From the face of the wicked who spoiled me, &#124;&#124; &#91;From&#93; my enemies in soul who go around against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:3; Psalms 123:4; Psalms 31:18"> They have closed up their fat, &#124;&#124; Their mouths have spoken with pride:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 37:14; Proverbs 6:13–14; Psalms 10:8–10"> “Our steps have now surrounded &#91;him&#93;”; They set their eyes to turn aside in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 7:2; 1 Peter 5:8; Psalms 10:9"> His likeness as a lion desirous to tear, &#124;&#124; As a young lion dwelling in secret places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 44:26; Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6"> Arise, O YHWH, go before his face, &#124;&#124; Cause him to bend. Deliver my soul from the wicked, Your sword,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:18; John 17:14; Luke 16:25"> From men, Your hand, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; From men of the world, their portion &#91;is&#93; in life, &#124;&#124; And &#91;with&#93; Your hidden things You fill their belly, &#124;&#124; They are satisfied &#91;with&#93; sons, &#124;&#124; And have left their abundance to their sucklings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:18; Matthew 5:6; Psalms 11:7"> I—in righteousness, &#124;&#124; I see Your face; I am satisfied, in awaking, &#91;with&#93; Your form!</span>
