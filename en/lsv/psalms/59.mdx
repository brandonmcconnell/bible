<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:11–24; Psalms 18:48; Psalms 57:1"> TO THE OVERSEER. “DO NOT DESTROY.” A MIKTAM OF DAVID, IN SAUL’S SENDING, AND THEY WATCH THE HOUSE TO PUT HIM TO DEATH. Deliver me from my enemies, O my God, &#124;&#124; Set me on high from my withstanders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 139:19; Psalms 26:9; Psalms 55:23"> Deliver me from workers of iniquity, &#124;&#124; And save me from men of blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 56:6; Psalms 69:4; 1 Samuel 24:11"> For behold, they laid wait for my soul, &#124;&#124; Strong ones are assembled against me, &#124;&#124; Not my transgression nor my sin, O YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 35:23; Isaiah 51:9; Proverbs 1:16"> Without punishment they run and prepare themselves, &#124;&#124; Stir up to meet me, and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 84:8; Psalms 55:15; Psalms 9:15"> And You, YHWH, God of Hosts, God of Israel, &#124;&#124; Awake to inspect all the nations. Do not favor any treacherous dealers of iniquity. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 59:14; Psalms 22:16; 1 Samuel 19:11"> They return at evening, &#124;&#124; They make a noise like a dog, &#124;&#124; And go around the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Proverbs 15:2; Psalms 10:11"> Behold, they belch out with their mouths, &#124;&#124; Swords &#91;are&#93; in their lips, for “Who hears?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 1:26; Psalms 37:13; Psalms 2:4"> And You, O YHWH, laugh at them, &#124;&#124; You mock at all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 62:2; Psalms 27:14"> O my Strength, to You I take heed, &#124;&#124; For God &#91;is&#93; my tower—the God of my kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 54:7; Psalms 59:17; 1 Peter 5:10"> God goes before me, &#124;&#124; He causes me to look on my enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 3:3; Revelation 9:6; Job 40:12"> Do not slay them, lest my people forget, &#124;&#124; Shake them by Your strength, &#124;&#124; And bring them down, O Lord our shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 12:13; Proverbs 18:7; Psalms 10:7"> The sin of their mouth &#91;is&#93; a word of their lips, &#124;&#124; And they are captured in their pride, &#124;&#124; And they recount from the curse and lying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; Psalms 7:9; Ezekiel 39:7"> Consume in fury, consume and they are not, &#124;&#124; And they know that God is ruling in Jacob, &#124;&#124; To the ends of the earth. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 22:16; Psalms 59:6"> And they return at evening, &#124;&#124; They make a noise like a dog, &#124;&#124; And they go around the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 15:23; Psalms 109:10; Job 30:1–7"> They wander for food, &#124;&#124; If they are not satisfied—then they murmur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 21:13; Ephesians 3:20; Romans 15:9"> And I sing of Your strength, &#124;&#124; And at morning I sing of Your kindness, &#124;&#124; For You have been a tower to me, &#124;&#124; And a refuge for me in a day of adversity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 59:9–10; Psalms 46:1; Psalms 18:1"> O my Strength, I sing praise to You, &#124;&#124; For God &#91;is&#93; my tower, the God of my kindness!</span>
