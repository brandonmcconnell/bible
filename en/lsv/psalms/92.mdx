<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 147:1; Psalms 107:1; Ephesians 5:19"> A PSALM. A SONG FOR THE SABBATH DAY. &#91;It is&#93; good to give thanks to YHWH, &#124;&#124; And to sing praises to Your Name, O Most High,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Lamentations 3:22–23; Psalms 89:1–2; John 1:17"> To declare Your kindness in the morning, &#124;&#124; And Your faithfulness in the nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 33:2; 1 Chronicles 13:8; Nehemiah 12:27"> On ten strings and on stringed instrument, &#124;&#124; On higgaion, with harp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; Psalms 8:6; Luke 1:47"> For You have caused me to rejoice, O YHWH, in Your work, &#124;&#124; I sing concerning the works of Your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 11:33–34; Isaiah 55:8–9; Psalms 40:5"> How great Your works have been, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; Your thoughts have been very deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Psalms 94:8"> A brutish man does not know, &#124;&#124; And a fool does not understand this—</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 73:18–20; Psalms 37:38; Psalms 37:35–36"> When the wicked flourish as an herb, &#124;&#124; And all workers of iniquity blossom—For their being destroyed forever and ever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; Acts 12:22–24; Acts 12:1"> And You &#91;are&#93; high for all time, O YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 37:20; Psalms 89:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:7–9"> For behold, Your enemies, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; For behold, Your enemies perish, &#124;&#124; All workers of iniquity separate themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Psalms 89:17; Psalms 45:7"> And You exalt my horn as a wild ox, &#124;&#124; I have been anointed with fresh oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 91:8; Psalms 54:7; Psalms 59:10"> And my eye looks on my enemies, &#124;&#124; Of those rising up against me, &#124;&#124; The evildoers, my ears hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:8; Psalms 1:3; Psalms 52:8"> The righteous flourish as a palm-tree, &#124;&#124; He grows as a cedar in Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 3:18; Isaiah 61:3; Ephesians 3:17"> Those planted in the house of YHWH, &#124;&#124; In the courts of our God, flourish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; Jeremiah 17:8; Psalms 71:18"> Still they bring forth in old age, &#124;&#124; They are fat and flourishing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Romans 9:14; Psalms 62:6"> To declare that YHWH my Rock &#91;is&#93; upright, &#124;&#124; And there is no perverseness in Him!</span>
