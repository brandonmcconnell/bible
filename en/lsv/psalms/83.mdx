<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; Psalms 35:22; Psalms 50:3"> A SONG. A PSALM OF ASAPH. O God, let there be no silence to You, &#124;&#124; Do not be silent, nor be quiet, O God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 81:15; Matthew 27:24; Judges 8:28"> For behold, Your enemies roar, &#124;&#124; And those hating You have lifted up the head,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 64:2; Psalms 27:5; Psalms 31:20"> They take crafty counsel against Your people, &#124;&#124; And consult against Your hidden ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:2; Jeremiah 11:19; Psalms 74:8"> They have said, “Come, and we cut them off from &#91;being&#93; a nation, &#124;&#124; And the name of Israel is not remembered anymore.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:2; Revelation 17:13; Isaiah 8:9–10"> For they consulted in heart together, &#124;&#124; They make a covenant against You,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:10; 2 Chronicles 20:1; Psalms 137:7"> Tents of Edom, and Ishmaelites, Moab, and the Hagarenes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 13:5; Ezekiel 27:3; Ezekiel 27:9"> Gebal, and Ammon, and Amalek, Philistia with inhabitants of Tyre,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:9; Genesis 25:3; 2 Kings 15:19"> Asshur is also joined with them, &#124;&#124; They have been an arm to sons of Lot. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 4:15–24; Isaiah 9:4; Judges 5:21"> Do to them as Midian, &#124;&#124; As Sisera, as Jabin, at the Brook of Kishon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:17; Joshua 17:11; 1 Samuel 28:7"> They were destroyed at Endor, &#124;&#124; They were dung for the ground!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 7:25; Judges 8:12–21"> Make their nobles as Oreb and as Zeeb, &#124;&#124; And as Zebah and Zalmunna—all their princes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:11; Psalms 83:4; Psalms 74:7–8"> Who have said, “Let us occupy the pastures of God for ourselves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 21:18; Psalms 35:5; Jeremiah 13:24"> O my God, make them as a rolling thing, &#124;&#124; As stubble before wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:22; Isaiah 9:18; Malachi 4:1"> As a fire burns a forest, &#124;&#124; And as a flame sets hills on fire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 9:17; Psalms 50:3; Psalms 58:9"> So You pursue them with Your whirlwind, &#124;&#124; And trouble them with Your windstorm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 9:19–20; Psalms 6:10; Psalms 109:29"> Fill their faces &#91;with&#93; shame, &#124;&#124; And they seek Your Name, O YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 40:14–15; Psalms 35:26"> They are ashamed and troubled forever, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they are confounded and lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 6:3; Psalms 59:13; Jeremiah 16:21"> And they know that You—Your Name &#91;is&#93; YHWH—By Yourself &#91;are&#93; the Most High over all the earth!</span>
