<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 49:3; 2 Samuel 23:2; Job 33:3"> TO THE OVERSEER. &#91;SET&#93; ON “LILIES.” AN INSTRUCTION OF THE SONS OF KORAH. A SONG OF LOVES. My heart has stirred a good word, &#124;&#124; I am telling my works to the King, &#124;&#124; My tongue &#91;is&#93; the pen of a speedy writer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 4:22; John 7:46; Hebrews 7:26"> You have been beautified above the sons of men, &#124;&#124; Grace has been poured into Your lips, &#124;&#124; Therefore God has blessed You for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 1:16; Isaiah 49:2"> Gird Your sword on the thigh, O Mighty &#91;One&#93;, &#124;&#124; Your splendor and Your majesty!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 65:5; Revelation 6:2; Revelation 19:11"> As for Your majesty—prosper &#91;and&#93; ride! Because of truth, meekness, &#91;and&#93; righteousness, &#124;&#124; And Your right hand shows You fearful things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 21:12; Numbers 24:8; Psalms 38:2"> Your arrows &#91;are&#93; sharp—Peoples fall under You—In the heart of the enemies of the King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 1:8–9; Psalms 93:2; John 1:1"> Your throne, O God, &#91;is for&#93; all time and forever, &#124;&#124; A scepter of uprightness &#124;&#124; &#91;Is&#93; the scepter of Your kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 1:9; Psalms 21:6; Isaiah 61:1–3"> You have loved righteousness and hate wickedness, &#124;&#124; Therefore God, Your God, has anointed You &#124;&#124; With oil of joy above Your companions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Songs 1:3; 2 Corinthians 2:14–16; John 19:39"> Myrrh, and aloes, &#91;and&#93; cassia &#124;&#124; &#91;Cover&#93; all Your garments; Out of palaces of ivory, &#124;&#124; Stringed instruments have made You glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 2:19; Revelation 21:9; Ephesians 5:26–27"> Daughters of kings &#91;are&#93; among Your precious ones, &#124;&#124; A queen has stood at Your right hand, &#124;&#124; In pure gold of Ophir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Song of Songs 2:10–13; Genesis 12:1; Matthew 10:37"> Listen, O daughter, and see, incline your ear, &#124;&#124; And forget your people, and your father’s house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 95:6; Isaiah 54:5; Song of Songs 4:1–5"> And the King desires your beauty, &#124;&#124; Because He &#91;is&#93; your Lord—bow yourself to Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:29; Isaiah 60:3; Isaiah 49:23"> And the daughter of Tyre with a present, &#124;&#124; The rich of the people appease your face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–8; Isaiah 61:10; Romans 3:22"> All glory &#91;is&#93; the daughter of the king within, &#124;&#124; Her clothing &#91;is&#93; with filigrees of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:2; Song of Songs 8:13; Exodus 28:39"> In various colors she is brought to the King; Afterward, virgins, her companions, &#124;&#124; Are brought to You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 7:15–17; Jude 1:24; Isaiah 55:12–13"> They are brought with joy and gladness, &#124;&#124; They come into the palace of the King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; 1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 1:6"> Instead of Your fathers are Your sons, &#124;&#124; You appoint them for princes in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 62:3; Malachi 1:11; Psalms 72:17–19"> I make mention of Your Name in all generations, &#124;&#124; Therefore peoples praise You, &#124;&#124; For all time and forever!</span>
