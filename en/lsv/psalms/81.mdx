<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:7; Psalms 66:1; Psalms 100:1–2"> TO THE OVERSEER. ON THE GITTITH. BY ASAPH. Cry aloud to God our strength, &#124;&#124; Shout to the God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 149:1–3; Psalms 92:3; Colossians 3:16"> Lift up a song, and give out a timbrel, &#124;&#124; A pleasant harp with stringed instrument.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 10:1–10; Numbers 15:3; 2 Chronicles 2:4"> Blow a horn in the month, &#124;&#124; In the new moon, at the day of our festival,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For it &#91;is&#93; a statute to Israel, &#124;&#124; An ordinance of the God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 114:1; Deuteronomy 28:49; 1 Corinthians 14:21–22"> He has placed it—a testimony on Joseph, &#124;&#124; In his going forth over the land of Egypt. A lip, I have not known—I hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Isaiah 10:27; Exodus 6:6"> I turned aside his shoulder from the burden, &#124;&#124; His hands pass over from the basket.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 19:19; Numbers 20:13; Exodus 2:23"> In distress you have called and I deliver you, &#124;&#124; I answer you in the secret place of thunder, &#124;&#124; I try you by the waters of Meribah. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 50:7; John 3:11; Isaiah 1:19"> Hear, O My people, and I testify to you, &#124;&#124; O Israel, if you listen to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 43:12; Deuteronomy 32:12; Deuteronomy 6:14"> There is not in you a strange god, &#124;&#124; And you do not bow yourself to a strange god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:7; Psalms 107:9; Exodus 20:2"> I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH, &#124;&#124; Who brings you up out of the land of Egypt. Enlarge your mouth, and I fill it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:18; Jeremiah 7:23–24; Jeremiah 2:11–13"> But My people did not listen to My voice, &#124;&#124; And Israel has not consented to Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 1:24; Jeremiah 7:24; Isaiah 30:1"> And I send them away in the enmity of their heart, &#124;&#124; They walk in their own counsels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 48:18; Deuteronomy 5:29; Deuteronomy 10:12–13"> O that My people were listening to Me, &#124;&#124; Israel would walk in My ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 1:8; Judges 2:20–23; Numbers 14:9"> As a little thing I cause their enemies to bow, &#124;&#124; And I turn back My hand against their adversaries,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:10; Romans 1:30; Exodus 20:5"> Those hating YHWH should have feigned &#91;obedience&#93; to Him, &#124;&#124; And their time would last for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–14; Psalms 147:14; 1 Samuel 14:25–26"> He causes him to eat of the fat of wheat, &#124;&#124; And I satisfy you &#91;with&#93; honey from a rock!</span>
