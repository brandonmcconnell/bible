<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Psalms 141:3; James 1:26"> TO THE OVERSEER. FOR JEDUTHUN. A PSALM OF DAVID. I have said, “I observe my ways, &#124;&#124; Against sinning with my tongue, &#124;&#124; I keep a curb for my mouth, &#124;&#124; While the wicked &#91;is&#93; before me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:20; Psalms 38:13–14; Job 32:19–20"> I was mute &#91;with&#93; silence, &#124;&#124; I kept silent from good, and my pain is excited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:32; Jeremiah 20:9; Ezekiel 3:14"> My heart &#91;is&#93; hot within me, &#124;&#124; In my meditating the fire burns, &#124;&#124; I have spoken with my tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 90:12; Psalms 103:14; Job 14:13"> “Cause me to know, O YHWH, my end, &#124;&#124; And the measure of my days—what it &#91;is&#93;,” &#124;&#124; I know how frail I &#91;am&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 90:9–10; Psalms 144:4; Psalms 89:47"> Behold, You have made my days handbreadths, &#124;&#124; And my age &#91;is&#93; as nothing before You, &#124;&#124; Only, every man set up &#91;is&#93; all vanity. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:20–21; James 4:14; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> Only, each habitually walks in an image, &#124;&#124; Only, &#91;in&#93; vain, they are disquieted, &#124;&#124; He heaps up and does not know who gathers them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Psalms 38:15; Psalms 119:81"> And now, what have I expected? O Lord, my hope—it &#91;is&#93; of You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 44:13; Psalms 65:3; Joel 2:17"> Deliver me from all my transgressions, &#124;&#124; Do not make me a reproach of the fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 2:10; 2 Samuel 16:10; Leviticus 10:3"> I have been mute, I do not open my mouth, &#124;&#124; Because You have done &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:34; Job 13:21; Psalms 25:16–17"> Turn aside Your stroke from off me, &#124;&#124; From the striving of Your hand I have been consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:28; 1 Corinthians 11:30–32; Isaiah 50:9"> With reproofs against iniquity, &#124;&#124; You have corrected man, &#124;&#124; And dissolve his desirableness as a moth, &#124;&#124; Only, every man &#91;is&#93; vanity. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Hebrews 11:13; 2 Kings 20:5"> Hear my prayer, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; And give ear &#91;to&#93; my cry, &#124;&#124; Do not be silent to my tear, &#124;&#124; For I &#91;am&#93; a sojourner with You, &#124;&#124; A settler like all my fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 10:20–21; Job 14:10–12; Job 14:5–6"> Look from me, and I brighten up before I go and am not!</span>
