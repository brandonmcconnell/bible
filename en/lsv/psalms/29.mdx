<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 93:3–4; Job 37:2–5; Exodus 9:33"> The voice of YHWH &#91;is&#93; on the waters, &#124;&#124; The God of glory has thundered, &#124;&#124; YHWH &#91;is&#93; on many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:5; Psalms 33:9; Jeremiah 51:15–16"> The voice of YHWH &#91;is&#93; with power, &#124;&#124; The voice of YHWH &#91;is&#93; with majesty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 2:13; Judges 9:15; Psalms 104:16"> The voice of YHWH &#91;is&#93; shattering cedars, &#124;&#124; Indeed, YHWH shatters the cedars of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:9; Numbers 23:22; Psalms 114:4–7"> And He causes them to skip as a calf, &#124;&#124; Lebanon and Sirion as a son of Reems,</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 46:2–5; Psalms 48:9; Ezekiel 20:46–48"> The voice of YHWH pains the oaks, &#124;&#124; And makes bare the forests, &#124;&#124; And in His temple everyone says, “Glory!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 6:17; Psalms 10:16; 1 Timothy 1:17"> YHWH has sat on the flood, &#124;&#124; And YHWH sits &#91;as&#93; king for all time,</span>
