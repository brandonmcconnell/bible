<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Psalms 118:6; Exodus 15:2"> BY DAVID. YHWH &#91;is&#93; my light and my salvation, &#124;&#124; Whom do I fear? YHWH &#91;is&#93; the strength of my life, &#124;&#124; Of whom am I afraid?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:12; Psalms 14:4; Psalms 9:3"> When evildoers come near to me to eat my flesh, &#124;&#124; My adversaries and my enemies to me, &#124;&#124; They have stumbled and fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 3:6; 1 Peter 3:14; Romans 8:35–37"> Though a host encamps against me, &#124;&#124; My heart does not fear, &#124;&#124; Though war rises up against me, &#124;&#124; In this I &#91;am&#93; confident.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; Psalms 23:6; Psalms 65:4"> One &#91;thing&#93; I asked of YHWH—it I seek: My dwelling in the house of YHWH, &#124;&#124; All the days of my life, &#124;&#124; To look on the pleasantness of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And to inquire in His temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 31:20; Psalms 138:7"> For He hides me in a dwelling place in the day of evil, &#124;&#124; He hides me in a secret place of His tent, &#124;&#124; He raises me up on a rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 3:3; Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 107:22"> And now my head is lifted up, &#124;&#124; Above my enemies—my surrounders, &#124;&#124; And I sacrifice in His tent sacrifices of shouting, &#124;&#124; I sing, indeed, I sing praise to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 130:2–4; Psalms 13:3; Psalms 5:2"> Hear, O YHWH, my voice—I call, &#124;&#124; And favor me, and answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 105:4; Jeremiah 29:12–13; Isaiah 55:6–7"> My heart said to You, “They sought my face, &#124;&#124; Your face, O YHWH, I seek.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 143:7; Psalms 102:2"> Do not hide Your face from me, &#124;&#124; Do not turn Your servant aside in anger, &#124;&#124; You have been my help. Do not leave me, nor forsake me, &#124;&#124; O God of my salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Isaiah 40:11; John 16:32"> When my father and my mother &#124;&#124; Have forsaken me, then YHWH gathers me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; Psalms 5:8; Psalms 86:11"> Show me, O YHWH, Your way, &#124;&#124; And lead me in a path of uprightness, &#124;&#124; For the sake of my beholders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 31:8; Psalms 41:11; Matthew 26:59–60"> Do not give me to the will of my adversaries, &#124;&#124; For false witnesses have risen against me, &#124;&#124; And they breathe out violence to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 142:5; 2 Corinthians 4:16; 2 Corinthians 4:8–14"> I had not believed to look on the goodness of YHWH &#124;&#124; In the land of the living!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 31:24; Isaiah 30:18"> Look to YHWH—be strong, &#124;&#124; And He strengthens your heart, &#124;&#124; Indeed, look to YHWH!</span>
