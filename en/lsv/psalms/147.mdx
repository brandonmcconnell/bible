<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:3; Isaiah 56:8; Psalms 51:18"> YHWH &#91;is&#93; building Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; He gathers the driven away of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 34:18; Job 5:18; Jeremiah 33:6"> Who is giving healing to the broken of heart, &#124;&#124; And is binding up their griefs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 40:26; Psalms 8:3; Psalms 148:3"> Appointing the number of the stars, &#124;&#124; He gives names to all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:28; Psalms 139:17–18; Psalms 96:4"> Our Lord &#91;is&#93; great, and abundant in power, &#124;&#124; There is no narration of His understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 146:8–9; Psalms 25:9; 1 Peter 5:6"> YHWH is causing the meek to stand, &#124;&#124; Making the wicked low to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:1–2; Psalms 47:6–7; Psalms 68:32"> Answer to YHWH with thanksgiving, &#124;&#124; Sing to our God with a harp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 5:10; Psalms 65:9–13; Genesis 9:14"> Who is covering the heavens with clouds, &#124;&#124; Who is preparing rain for the earth, &#124;&#124; Who is causing grass to spring up &#91;on&#93; mountains,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 104:27–28; Job 38:41; Matthew 6:26"> Giving to the beast its food, &#124;&#124; To the young of the ravens that call.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Isaiah 31:1; Proverbs 21:31"> He does not delight in the might of the horse, &#124;&#124; He is not pleased in the legs of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 33:18; Malachi 3:16–17; Zephaniah 3:17"> YHWH is pleased with those fearing Him, &#124;&#124; With those waiting for His kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 12:6; Isaiah 52:7; Joel 2:23"> Glorify, O Jerusalem, YHWH, &#124;&#124; Praise your God, O Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 128:3–6; Psalms 115:14–15; Isaiah 44:3–5"> For He strengthened the bars of your gates, &#124;&#124; He has blessed your sons in your midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 60:17–18; Psalms 29:11; Psalms 81:16"> Who is making your border peace, &#124;&#124; He satisfies you &#91;with&#93; the fat of wheat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 37:12; 2 Thessalonians 3:1; Psalms 107:20"> Who is sending forth His saying &#91;on&#93; earth, &#124;&#124; His word runs very speedily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 38:29; Job 37:6; Psalms 148:8"> Who is giving snow like wool, &#124;&#124; He scatters hoarfrost as ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 38:29–30; Job 38:22–23; Job 37:9–10"> Casting forth His ice like morsels, &#124;&#124; Who stands before His cold?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 147:15; Psalms 33:9; Job 37:10"> He sends forth His word and melts them, &#124;&#124; He causes His wind to blow—the waters flow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 4:4; Psalms 78:5; Deuteronomy 33:2–4"> Declaring His words to Jacob, &#124;&#124; His statutes and His judgments to Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:32–34; Romans 3:1–2; 1 Peter 2:9–10"> He has not done so to any nation, &#124;&#124; As for judgments, they have not known them. Praise YAH!</span>
