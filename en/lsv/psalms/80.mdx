<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 85:5; Isaiah 58:2–3; Psalms 59:5"> YHWH, God of Hosts, until when? You have burned against the prayer of Your people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Isaiah 30:20; Psalms 102:9"> You have caused them to eat bread of tears, &#124;&#124; And cause them to drink &#124;&#124; With tears a third time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Isaiah 36:8; Isaiah 37:23"> You make us a strife to our neighbors, &#124;&#124; And our enemies mock at it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 51:10; Isaiah 64:5; Jeremiah 4:14"> God of Hosts, turn us back, &#124;&#124; And cause Your face to shine, and we are saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Psalms 44:2; Ezekiel 17:6"> You bring a vine out of Egypt, &#124;&#124; You cast out nations, and plant it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 24:12; Isaiah 27:6; 1 Kings 4:20"> You have looked before it, and root it, &#124;&#124; And it fills the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 104:16"> Hills have been covered &#91;with&#93; its shadow, &#124;&#124; And its boughs &#91;are&#93; cedars of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 72:8; 1 Chronicles 18:3; 1 Kings 4:24"> It sends forth its branches to the sea, &#124;&#124; And its shoots to the river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 5:5; Psalms 89:40–41; Isaiah 18:5–6"> Why have You broken down its hedges, &#124;&#124; And everyone passing by the way has plucked it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:6; Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 39:1–3"> A boar out of the forest tears it, &#124;&#124; And a wild beast of the fields grazes it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 63:15; Psalms 90:13; Daniel 9:16–19"> God of Hosts, turn back, we implore You, &#124;&#124; Look from the heavens, and see, and inspect this vine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 15:1; Psalms 80:8; Jeremiah 2:21"> And the root that Your right hand planted, &#124;&#124; And the branch You made strong for Yourself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 39:11; John 15:6; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> Burned with fire—cut down, &#124;&#124; They perish from the rebuke of Your face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Psalms 89:21; Psalms 110:1"> Let Your hand be on the man of Your right hand &#124;&#124; On the son of man You have strengthened for Yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:107; Psalms 119:25; Psalms 119:40"> And we do not go back from You, &#124;&#124; You revive us, and in Your Name we call.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:22–23; Psalms 80:3; Psalms 27:4"> O YHWH, God of Hosts, turn us back, &#124;&#124; Cause Your face to shine, and we are saved!</span>
