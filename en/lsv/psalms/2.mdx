<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 4:25–26; Revelation 17:14; Psalms 46:6"> Why have nations assembled tumultuously? And peoples meditate vanity?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 26:59; Matthew 27:1; Revelation 17:12–14"> Kings of the earth station themselves, and princes have been united together, against YHWH, and against His Messiah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:5; Luke 19:14; 1 Peter 2:7–8"> “Let us draw off Their cords, &#124;&#124; And cast Their thick bands from us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 37:13; Psalms 59:8; Proverbs 1:26"> He who is sitting in the heavens laughs, &#124;&#124; The Lord mocks at them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 11:4; Revelation 19:15; Psalms 110:5–6"> Then He speaks to them in His anger, and in His wrath He troubles them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Revelation 14:1; Ephesians 1:22"> “And I have anointed My King, &#124;&#124; On Zion—My holy hill.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 5:5; Acts 13:33; Hebrews 1:5–6"> I declare concerning a statute: YHWH said to me, “You &#91;are&#93; My Son, today I have brought You forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 22:27; Psalms 89:27; Psalms 72:8"> Ask of Me and I give nations &#91;as&#93; Your inheritance, &#124;&#124; And the ends of the earth &#91;for&#93; Your possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 2:26–27; Revelation 12:5; Psalms 89:23"> You rule them with a scepter of iron, &#124;&#124; You crush them as a vessel of a potter.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 14:9; Isaiah 60:10–11; Isaiah 60:3"> And now, O kings, act wisely, &#124;&#124; Be instructed, O judges of earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; Hebrews 12:25; Hebrews 12:28–29"> Serve YHWH with fear, &#124;&#124; And rejoice with trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 40:4; John 5:23; Psalms 84:12"> Kiss the Chosen One &#91;&#91;or Son&#93;&#93;, lest He is angry, &#124;&#124; And you perish &#91;from&#93; the way, &#124;&#124; When His anger burns but a little, &#124;&#124; O the blessedness of all trusting in Him!</span>
