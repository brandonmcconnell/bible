<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 26:3–4; Psalms 56:1; Psalms 31:23"> A MIKTAM OF DAVID. Preserve me, O God, for I trusted in You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 73:25; Psalms 31:14; Psalms 89:26"> You have said to YHWH, “You &#91;are&#93; my Lord”; My goodness &#91;is&#93; not above You;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 12:26; Hebrews 6:10; Proverbs 13:20"> For the holy ones who &#91;are&#93; in the land, &#124;&#124; And the honorable, all my delight &#91;is&#93; in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 23:13; Joshua 23:7; Psalms 32:10"> Their griefs are multiplied, &#91;who&#93; have hurried backward; I do not pour out their drink-offerings of blood, &#124;&#124; Nor do I take up their names on my lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Psalms 142:5; Lamentations 3:24"> YHWH &#91;is&#93; the portion of my share, and of my cup, &#124;&#124; You uphold my lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:19; Ephesians 1:18; Psalms 21:1–3"> Lines have fallen to me in pleasant places, &#124;&#124; Indeed, a beautiful inheritance &#91;is&#93; for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 73:24; Isaiah 50:4; Psalms 77:6"> I bless YHWH who has counseled me; Also &#91;in&#93; the nights my reins instruct me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 62:6; Acts 2:25–28; Psalms 121:5"> I placed YHWH before me continually, &#124;&#124; Because &#91;He is&#93; at my right hand I am not moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 2:26; Psalms 30:12; Psalms 57:8"> Therefore my heart has been glad, &#124;&#124; And my glory rejoices, &#124;&#124; Also my flesh dwells confidently:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:27–31; Acts 13:35–38; Psalms 49:15"> For You do not leave my soul to Sheol, &#124;&#124; Nor give your Holy One to see corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 2:28; Jude 1:24; Psalms 36:7–8"> You cause me to know the path of life; In Your presence &#91;is&#93; fullness of joys, &#124;&#124; Pleasant things &#91;are&#93; by Your right hand forever!</span>
