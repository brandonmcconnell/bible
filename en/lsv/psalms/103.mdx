<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 57:7–11; John 4:24"> BY DAVID. Bless, O my soul, YHWH, &#124;&#124; And all my inward parts—His Holy Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 63:7; Psalms 116:12; Psalms 105:5"> Bless, O my soul, YHWH, &#124;&#124; And do not forget all His benefits,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Isaiah 53:5; Jeremiah 17:14"> Who is forgiving all your iniquities, &#124;&#124; Who is healing all your diseases,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 56:13; Psalms 71:23; Psalms 103:12"> Who is redeeming your life from destruction, &#124;&#124; Who is crowning you &#91;with&#93; kindness and mercies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 107:9; 1 Timothy 6:17"> Who is satisfying your desire with good, &#124;&#124; Your youth renews itself as an eagle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 12:5; Psalms 72:12"> YHWH is doing righteousness and judgments &#124;&#124; For all the oppressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 33:13; Psalms 147:19; Psalms 78:11"> He makes His ways known to Moses, &#124;&#124; His acts to the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Joel 2:13; Exodus 34:6–7"> YHWH &#91;is&#93; merciful and gracious, &#124;&#124; Slow to anger, and abundant in mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 57:16; Psalms 30:5; Micah 7:18–19"> He does not strive forever, &#124;&#124; Nor does He watch for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Lamentations 3:22; Nehemiah 9:31"> He has not done to us according to our sins, &#124;&#124; Nor according to our iniquities &#124;&#124; Has He conferred benefits on us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 57:10; Luke 1:50; Psalms 36:5"> For as the height of the heavens &#91;is&#93; above the earth, &#124;&#124; His kindness has been mighty over those fearing Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; 1 John 1:7; Micah 7:18"> He has put our transgressions far from us—as the distance of east from west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 11:11–12; Hebrews 12:5–11; Luke 15:21–22"> As a father has mercy on sons, &#124;&#124; YHWH has mercy on those fearing Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 10:9; Psalms 78:38–39"> For He has known our frame, &#124;&#124; Remembering that we &#91;are&#93; dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; James 1:10–11; Isaiah 51:12"> Mortal man! His days &#91;are&#93; as grass, &#124;&#124; He flourishes as a flower of the field;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7; Job 20:9; Job 14:10"> For a wind has passed over it, and it is not, &#124;&#124; And its place does not discern it anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 100:5; Exodus 20:6; Ephesians 2:4–7"> And the kindness of YHWH &#124;&#124; &#91;Is&#93; from age even to age on those fearing Him, &#124;&#124; And His righteousness to sons’ sons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Psalms 132:12; Luke 1:6"> To those keeping His covenant, &#124;&#124; And to those remembering His precepts to do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 47:2; Philippians 2:9–10; Psalms 9:7"> YHWH has established His throne in the heavens, &#124;&#124; And His kingdom has ruled over all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 1:14; Psalms 148:2; Luke 1:19"> Bless YHWH, you His messengers, &#124;&#124; Mighty in power—doing His word, &#124;&#124; To listen to the voice of His word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 22:19; Nehemiah 9:6; Luke 2:13"> Bless YHWH, all you His hosts, &#124;&#124; His ministers—doing His pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Psalms 145:10; Isaiah 42:10–12"> Bless YHWH, all you His works, &#124;&#124; In all places of His dominion. Bless, O my soul, YHWH!</span>
