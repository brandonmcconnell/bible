<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 2:4–5; Psalms 125:1; Psalms 46:7"> God is known for a tower in her high places.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 14:25; 2 Kings 7:6–7; 2 Kings 19:35–37"> They have seen—so they have marveled, &#124;&#124; They have been troubled, they were hurried away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 13:13; Isaiah 13:6–8; Isaiah 21:3"> Trembling has seized them there, &#124;&#124; Pain, as of a travailing woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:17; Ezekiel 27:25–26; 1 Kings 22:48"> By an east wind You shatter ships of Tarshish.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 26:3; Psalms 40:10; Psalms 77:10–14"> We have thought, O God, of Your kindness, &#124;&#124; In the midst of Your temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 1:11; Deuteronomy 28:58; Psalms 113:3"> As &#91;is&#93; Your Name, O God, so &#91;is&#93; Your praise, &#124;&#124; Over the ends of the earth, &#124;&#124; Righteousness has filled Your right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 97:8; Revelation 16:5–7; Revelation 18:20"> Mount Zion rejoices, &#124;&#124; The daughters of Judah are joyful, &#124;&#124; For the sake of Your judgments.</span>
