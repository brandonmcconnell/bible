<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 120:1; Psalms 129:1; Lamentations 5:1"> A SONG OF THE ASCENTS. Remember, YHWH, for David, all his afflictions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 49:24; Psalms 65:1; Psalms 119:106"> Who has sworn to YHWH, &#124;&#124; He has vowed to the Mighty One of Jacob:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Haggai 1:4; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Matthew 6:33"> “If I enter into the tent of my house, &#124;&#124; If I go up on the couch of my bed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 6:4; Ruth 3:18; Genesis 24:33"> If I give sleep to my eyes, &#124;&#124; To my eyelids—slumber,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:22; Acts 7:46–49; 2 Chronicles 2:6"> Until I find a place for YHWH, dwelling places for the Mighty One of Jacob.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:12; 1 Samuel 7:1; Micah 5:2"> Behold, we have heard it in Ephratah, &#124;&#124; We have found it in the fields of the forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 99:5; Psalms 5:7; Lamentations 2:1"> We come into His dwelling places, &#124;&#124; We bow ourselves at His footstool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:41–42; Numbers 10:35–36; Psalms 68:1"> Arise, O YHWH, to Your rest, &#124;&#124; You, and the Ark of Your strength,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 29:14; Revelation 19:8; Psalms 104:1"> Your priests put on righteousness, &#124;&#124; And Your pious ones cry aloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 84:9; 2 Chronicles 6:42; 1 Kings 11:34"> For the sake of Your servant David, &#124;&#124; Do not turn back the face of Your anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12; Acts 2:30; Psalms 89:3–4"> YHWH has sworn truth to David, &#124;&#124; He does not turn back from it: “Of the fruit of your body, &#124;&#124; I set on the throne for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Acts 2:30; Isaiah 59:21"> If your sons keep My covenant, &#124;&#124; And My testimonies that I teach them, &#124;&#124; Their sons also forever and ever &#124;&#124; Sit on the throne for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 78:68–69; Hebrews 12:22; Psalms 87:2"> For YHWH has fixed on Zion, &#124;&#124; He has desired &#91;it&#93; for a seat to Himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 132:8; 1 Kings 8:13; Psalms 76:2"> “This &#91;is&#93; My rest forever and ever, &#124;&#124; Here I sit, for I have desired it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 147:14; Leviticus 26:4–5; Proverbs 3:9–10"> I greatly bless her provision, &#124;&#124; I satisfy her poor &#91;with&#93; bread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:41; Psalms 132:9; John 16:24"> And I clothe her priests &#91;with&#93; salvation, &#124;&#124; And her pious ones sing aloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 1:69; Ezekiel 29:21; 2 Chronicles 21:7"> There I cause a horn to spring up for David, &#124;&#124; I have arranged a lamp for My Anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Job 8:22; Psalms 21:8–9"> I clothe His enemies &#91;with&#93; shame, &#124;&#124; And His crown flourishes on Him!”</span>
