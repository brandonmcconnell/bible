<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 25:2; Daniel 4:37; Psalms 35:19"> A PSALM. A SONG OF THE DEDICATION OF THE HOUSE OF DAVID. I exalt You, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; For You have drawn me up, &#124;&#124; And have not let my enemies rejoice over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 103:3–4; 2 Kings 20:5; Psalms 147:3"> My God YHWH, I have cried to You, &#124;&#124; And You heal me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 16:10; Jonah 2:4–6"> YHWH, You have brought up my soul from Sheol, &#124;&#124; You have kept me alive, &#124;&#124; From going down &#91;to&#93; the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 97:12; Revelation 4:8; 1 Chronicles 16:4"> Sing praise to YHWH, you His saints, &#124;&#124; And give thanks at the remembrance of His holiness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 54:7–8; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Psalms 126:5–6"> For—a moment &#91;is&#93; in His anger, &#124;&#124; Life &#91;is&#93; in His goodwill, &#124;&#124; At evening remains weeping, and at morning singing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:19; 2 Corinthians 12:7; Psalms 119:117"> And I have said in my ease, “I am not moved for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 104:29; Psalms 143:7; Psalms 30:5"> O YHWH, in Your good pleasure, &#124;&#124; You have caused strength to remain for my mountain,” &#124;&#124; You have hidden Your face—I have been troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:6–7; Psalms 34:6; Psalms 130:1–2"> To You, O YHWH, I call, &#124;&#124; And to YHWH I make supplication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 6:5; Psalms 115:17–18; Isaiah 38:18"> “What gain &#91;is&#93; in my blood? In my going down to corruption? Does dust thank You? Does it declare Your truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 54:4; Psalms 143:1; Psalms 143:7–9"> Hear, O YHWH, and favor me, O YHWH, be a helper to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:4; Jeremiah 31:13–14; Ecclesiastes 3:4"> You have turned my mourning to dancing for me, &#124;&#124; You have loosed my sackcloth, &#124;&#124; And gird me &#91;with&#93; joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 145:2; Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 44:8"> So that glory praises You, and is not silent, &#124;&#124; O YHWH, my God, I thank You for all time!</span>
