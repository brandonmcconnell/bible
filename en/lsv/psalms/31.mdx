<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalms 25:2; Psalms 71:1–3"> TO THE OVERSEER. A PSALM OF DAVID. In You, O YHWH, I have trusted, &#124;&#124; Do not let me be ashamed for all time, &#124;&#124; In Your righteousness deliver me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 71:2–3; Psalms 102:2; Psalms 86:1"> Incline Your ear to me quickly, deliver me, &#124;&#124; Be to me for a strong rock, &#124;&#124; For a house of bulwarks to save me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 143:10–11; Luke 1:79; Psalms 79:9"> For You &#91;are&#93; my rock and my bulwark, &#124;&#124; For Your Name’s sake lead me and tend me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 25:15; Psalms 140:5; Psalms 19:14"> Bring me out from the net that they hid for me, &#124;&#124; For You &#91;are&#93; my strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 23:46; Acts 7:59; Deuteronomy 32:4"> Into Your hand I commit my spirit, &#124;&#124; You have redeemed me, YHWH God of truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jonah 2:8; Psalms 24:4; Jeremiah 10:15"> I have hated the observers of lying vanities, &#124;&#124; And I have been confident toward YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Isaiah 63:9; Psalms 119:153"> I rejoice, and am glad in Your kindness, &#124;&#124; In that You have seen my affliction, &#124;&#124; You have known my soul in adversities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 36:16; Deuteronomy 32:30; Psalms 18:19"> And You have not shut me up, &#124;&#124; Into the hand of an enemy, &#124;&#124; You have caused my feet to stand in a broad place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 6:7; Psalms 88:9; Psalms 73:26"> Favor me, O YHWH, for distress &#91;is&#93; to me, &#124;&#124; My eye, my soul, and my body &#124;&#124; Have become old by provocation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 88:15; Psalms 32:3–4; Psalms 38:3"> For my life has been consumed in sorrow &#124;&#124; And my years in sighing. My strength has been feeble because of my iniquity, &#124;&#124; And my bones have become old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Psalms 88:8; Psalms 88:18"> I have been a reproach among all my adversaries, &#124;&#124; And to my neighbors exceedingly, &#124;&#124; And a fear to my acquaintances, &#124;&#124; Those seeing me without—fled from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 88:4–5; Psalms 2:9; Romans 9:21–22"> I have been forgotten as dead, out of mind, &#124;&#124; I have been as a perishing vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; Matthew 27:1; Lamentations 2:22"> For I have heard an evil account of many, &#124;&#124; Fear &#91;is&#93; all around. In their being united against me, &#124;&#124; They have devised to take my life,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 56:3–4; Psalms 18:2; Psalms 43:5"> And I have trusted on You, &#124;&#124; O YHWH, I have said, “You &#91;are&#93; my God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Ecclesiastes 3:1–8; John 17:1"> In Your hand &#91;are&#93; my times, &#124;&#124; Deliver me from the hand of my enemies, &#124;&#124; And from my pursuers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 4:6; Psalms 6:4; Daniel 9:9"> Cause Your face to shine on Your servant, &#124;&#124; Save me in Your kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 25:2–3; Jeremiah 20:11; Psalms 34:5"> O YHWH, do not let me be ashamed, &#124;&#124; For I have called You, let the wicked be ashamed, &#124;&#124; Let them become silent to Sheol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jude 1:15; Psalms 94:4; 1 Samuel 2:3"> Let lips of falsehood become mute, &#124;&#124; That are speaking against the righteous, &#124;&#124; Ancient sayings, in pride and contempt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9; Isaiah 64:4; Lamentations 3:23–25"> How abundant is Your goodness, &#124;&#124; That You have laid up for those fearing You, &#124;&#124; You have worked for those trusting in You, &#124;&#124; Before sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 27:5; Psalms 32:7; Psalms 91:1–4"> You hide them in the secret place of Your presence, &#124;&#124; From schemes of man, &#124;&#124; You conceal them in a dwelling place, &#124;&#124; From the strife of tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:7–13; Psalms 17:7; 1 Peter 2:9"> Blessed &#91;is&#93; YHWH, &#124;&#124; For He has made His kindness marvelous &#124;&#124; To me in a city of bulwarks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 116:11; Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 88:16"> And I have said in my haste, “I have been cut off from before Your eyes,” &#124;&#124; But You have heard the voice of my supplications, &#124;&#124; In my crying to You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 97:10; Psalms 94:2; Psalms 145:20"> Love YHWH, all you His saints, &#124;&#124; YHWH is keeping the faithful, &#124;&#124; And repaying a proud doer abundantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Psalms 146:5; Colossians 1:11"> Be strong, and He strengthens your heart, &#124;&#124; All you who are waiting for YHWH!</span>
