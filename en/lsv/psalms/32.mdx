<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Psalms 38:8; Psalms 38:3"> When I have kept silence, my bones have become old, &#124;&#124; Through my roaring all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:11; Psalms 38:2–8; Psalms 39:10–11"> When by day and by night Your hand is heavy on me, &#124;&#124; My moisture has been changed &#124;&#124; Into the droughts of summer. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; 2 Samuel 12:13; Psalms 38:18"> I cause You to know my sin, &#124;&#124; And I have not covered my iniquity. I have said, “I confess to YHWH concerning My transgressions,” &#124;&#124; And You have taken away the iniquity of my sin. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6; Isaiah 43:2; 2 Corinthians 6:2"> For every saintly one prays this to You, &#124;&#124; In the time to find &#91;You&#93;. Surely at an overflowing of many waters, &#124;&#124; They do not come to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 91:1; Psalms 119:114; Psalms 9:9"> You &#91;are&#93; a hiding place for me, &#124;&#124; You keep me from distress, &#124;&#124; Surround me &#91;with&#93; songs of deliverance. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Psalms 33:18; Psalms 25:8–10"> I cause you to act wisely, &#124;&#124; And direct you in the way that you go, &#124;&#124; I cause My eye to take counsel concerning you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 26:3; Jeremiah 4:22; James 3:3"> Do not be as a horse—as a mule, &#124;&#124; Without understanding, &#124;&#124; With bridle and bit, its ornaments, to curb, &#124;&#124; Not to come near to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2–3; Proverbs 16:20; Proverbs 13:21"> Many &#91;are&#93; the pains of the wicked; As for him who is trusting in YHWH, &#124;&#124; Kindness surrounds him.</span>
