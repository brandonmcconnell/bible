<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 113:3; Joshua 22:22; Matthew 25:32"> A PSALM OF ASAPH. The God of gods—YHWH—has spoken, &#124;&#124; And He calls to the earth &#124;&#124; From the rising of the sun to its going in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 48:2; Psalms 80:1; Revelation 21:23"> From Zion, the perfection of beauty, God shone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 7:10; Numbers 16:35; Leviticus 10:2"> Our God comes, and is not silent, &#124;&#124; Fire devours before Him, &#124;&#124; And around Him it has been very tempestuous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Isaiah 1:2; Deuteronomy 31:28"> He calls to the heavens from above, &#124;&#124; And to the earth, to judge His people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 26:28; Exodus 24:3–8; Hebrews 13:20"> Gather My saints to Me, &#124;&#124; Making covenant with Me over a sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:6; Psalms 75:7; Psalms 89:5"> And the heavens declare His righteousness, &#124;&#124; For God Himself is judging. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Psalms 81:8; Jeremiah 2:9"> Hear, O My people, and I speak, &#124;&#124; O Israel, and I testify against you, &#124;&#124; God—I &#91;am&#93; your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–31; Hebrews 10:4–10; Hosea 6:6"> I do not reprove you for your sacrifices, &#124;&#124; Indeed, your burnt-offerings &#124;&#124; &#91;Are&#93; continually before Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 6:6–8; Hebrews 10:4–6; Psalms 69:31"> I do not take a bullock from your house, &#124;&#124; &#91;Or&#93; male goats from your folds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5–6; Psalms 8:6–8; Psalms 104:14"> For every beast of the forest &#91;is&#93; Mine, &#124;&#124; The livestock on the hills of oxen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–22; Matthew 6:26; Luke 12:24"> I have known every bird of the mountains, &#124;&#124; And the wild beast of the field &#91;is&#93; with Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Deuteronomy 10:14; Psalms 24:1–2"> If I am hungry I do not tell &#91;it&#93; to you, &#124;&#124; For the world and its fullness &#91;is&#93; Mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Do I eat the flesh of bulls, &#124;&#124; And drink the blood of male goats?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 56:12; Deuteronomy 23:21"> Sacrifice to God confession, &#124;&#124; And complete your vows to the Most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 107:28; Psalms 91:15; Psalms 34:3–4"> And call Me in a day of adversity, &#124;&#124; I deliver you, and you honor Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Psalms 78:36–38; Romans 2:17–24"> And to the wicked God has said: What to you—to recount My statutes? That you lift up My covenant on your mouth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26; Proverbs 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:3–4"> Indeed, you have hated instruction, &#124;&#124; And cast My words behind you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 1:32; 1 Timothy 5:22; Hebrews 13:4"> If you have seen a thief, &#124;&#124; Then you are pleased with him, &#124;&#124; And your portion &#91;is&#93; with adulterers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 52:2–4; Psalms 55:21"> You have sent forth your mouth with evil, &#124;&#124; And your tongue joins deceit together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Proverbs 10:18; Leviticus 19:16"> You sit, you speak against your brother, &#124;&#124; You give slander against a son of your mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:4–5; Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 57:11"> These you did, and I kept silent, &#124;&#124; You have thought that I am like you, &#124;&#124; I reprove you, and set in array before your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 8:13; Psalms 9:17; Hosea 4:6"> Now understand this, &#124;&#124; You who are forgetting God, &#124;&#124; Lest I tear, and there is no deliverer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 91:16; James 3:13; Psalms 24:4–5"> He who is sacrificing praise honors Me, &#124;&#124; As for him who makes a way, &#124;&#124; I cause him to look on the salvation of God!</span>
