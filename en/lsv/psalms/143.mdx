<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 71:2; Psalms 31:1; Psalms 140:6"> A PSALM OF DAVID. O YHWH, hear my prayer, &#124;&#124; Give ear to my supplications, &#124;&#124; Answer me in Your faithfulness—in Your righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; Romans 3:20; Job 25:4"> And do not enter into judgment with Your servant, &#124;&#124; For no one living is justified before You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:6; Psalms 7:1–2; Psalms 7:5"> For an enemy has pursued my soul, &#124;&#124; He has bruised my life to the earth, &#124;&#124; He has caused me to dwell in dark places, &#124;&#124; As the dead of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 142:3; Psalms 77:3; Psalms 102:3–4"> And my spirit has become feeble in me, &#124;&#124; My heart has become desolate within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 77:5–6; Psalms 77:10–12; Micah 6:5"> I have remembered days of old, &#124;&#124; I have meditated on all Your acts, &#124;&#124; I muse on the work of Your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 63:1; Job 11:13; Psalms 84:2"> I have spread forth my hands to You, &#124;&#124; My soul &#91;is&#93; as a weary land for You. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 28:1; Psalms 27:9"> Hurry, answer me, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; My spirit has been consumed, &#124;&#124; Do not hide Your face from me, &#124;&#124; Or I have been compared with those going down &#91;to&#93; the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; Proverbs 3:5–6; Isaiah 48:17"> Cause me to hear Your kindness in the morning, &#124;&#124; For I have trusted in You, &#124;&#124; Cause me to know the way that I go, &#124;&#124; For I have lifted up my soul to You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 61:3–4; Psalms 59:1"> Deliver me from my enemies, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; Near You I am covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; John 14:26; Hebrews 13:21"> Teach me to do Your good pleasure, &#124;&#124; For You &#91;are&#93; my God—Your Spirit &#91;is&#93; good, &#124;&#124; Lead me into a land of uprightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 31:1; Ephesians 2:4–5; Psalms 71:2"> You quicken me for Your Name’s sake, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; In Your righteousness, You bring out my soul from distress,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 54:5; Psalms 116:16; Psalms 119:94"> And in Your kindness cut off my enemies, &#124;&#124; And have destroyed all the adversaries of my soul, &#124;&#124; For I &#91;am&#93; Your servant!</span>
