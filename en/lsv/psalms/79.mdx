<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 3:12; 2 Chronicles 36:19; Psalms 74:1–4"> A PSALM OF ASAPH. O God, nations have come into Your inheritance, &#124;&#124; They have defiled Your holy temple, &#124;&#124; They made Jerusalem become heaps,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Jeremiah 34:20; Jeremiah 16:4"> They gave the dead bodies of Your servants &#124;&#124; &#91;As&#93; food for the birds of the heavens, &#124;&#124; The flesh of Your saints &#124;&#124; For the wild beast of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:4; Jeremiah 14:16; Revelation 11:9"> They have shed their blood &#124;&#124; As water around Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And there is none burying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 80:6; Lamentations 5:1; Psalms 89:41"> We have been a reproach to our neighbors, &#124;&#124; A scorn and a derision to our surrounders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Psalms 74:1; Psalms 85:5"> Until when, O YHWH? Are You angry forever? Your jealousy burns as fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:25; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Psalms 14:4"> Pour Your fury on the nations who have not known You, &#124;&#124; And on kingdoms that have not called on Your Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34–35; Zechariah 1:15; Isaiah 64:10–11"> For &#91;one&#93; has devoured Jacob, &#124;&#124; And they have made his habitation desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 64:9; Psalms 142:6; Psalms 116:6"> Do not remember for us the iniquities of forefathers, &#124;&#124; Hurry, let Your mercies go before us, &#124;&#124; For we have been very weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:7; Psalms 25:11; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> Help us, O God of our salvation, &#124;&#124; Because of the glory of Your Name, &#124;&#124; And deliver us, and cover over our sins, &#124;&#124; For Your Name’s sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:10; Exodus 7:5; Psalms 115:2"> Why do the nations say, “Where &#91;is&#93; their God?” Let &#91;it&#93; be known among the nations before our eyes, &#124;&#124; The vengeance of the blood of Your servants that is shed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 102:20; Psalms 146:6–7; Isaiah 33:2"> Let the groaning of the prisoner come in before You, &#124;&#124; According to the greatness of Your arm, &#124;&#124; Leave the sons of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:18; Genesis 4:15; Leviticus 26:28"> And return to our neighbors, &#124;&#124; Sevenfold to their bosom, their reproach, &#124;&#124; With which they reproached You, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 95:7; Isaiah 43:21; Psalms 74:1"> And we, Your people, and the flock of Your pasture, &#124;&#124; We give thanks to You for all time, &#124;&#124; We recount Your praise from generation to generation!</span>
