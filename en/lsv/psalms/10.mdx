<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Psalms 27:9; Psalms 13:1–3"> Why, YHWH, do You stand at a distance? Do You hide in times of adversity?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 59:12; Psalms 36:11; Psalms 7:16"> Through the pride of the wicked, &#124;&#124; Is the poor inflamed, &#124;&#124; They are caught in schemes that they devised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 94:4; Luke 12:19; Zechariah 11:5–8"> Because the wicked has boasted &#124;&#124; Of the desire of his soul, &#124;&#124; And he has blessed a dishonest gainer, &#124;&#124; He has despised YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 14:1–2; Acts 8:22; Romans 1:28"> The wicked does not inquire according to the height of his face. “There is no God!” &#91;are&#93; all his schemes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 92:5–6; Isaiah 26:11; Psalms 12:5"> His ways writhe at all times, &#124;&#124; Your judgments &#91;are&#93; on high before him, &#124;&#124; All his adversaries—he puffs at them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:11; Matthew 24:48; Isaiah 56:12"> He has said in his heart, “I am not moved, &#124;&#124; &#91;And am&#93; not in calamity to generation and generation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Psalms 140:9; Psalms 140:3"> His mouth is full of oaths, &#124;&#124; And deceits, and fraud: Under his tongue &#91;is&#93; perverseness and iniquity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:14; 1 Samuel 22:18; Jeremiah 22:17"> He sits in an ambush of the villages, &#124;&#124; He slays the innocent in secret places. His eyes secretly watch for the afflicted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 17:12; Psalms 59:3; Micah 7:2"> He lies in wait in a secret place, as a lion in a covert. He lies in wait to catch the poor, &#124;&#124; He catches the poor, drawing him into his net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:21–22; 1 Samuel 18:21–26; 2 Samuel 15:5"> He is bruised—he bows down, &#124;&#124; The afflicted has fallen by his mighty ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Psalms 94:7; Psalms 73:11"> He said in his heart, “God has forgotten, &#124;&#124; He has hid His face, &#124;&#124; He has never seen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 5:9; Psalms 9:12; Psalms 94:2"> Arise, O YHWH! O God, lift up Your hand! Do not forget the humble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:9–10; Psalms 74:10; Genesis 9:5"> Why has the wicked despised God? He has said in his heart, “It is not required.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 68:5; Psalms 146:9; Psalms 55:22"> You have seen, &#124;&#124; For You behold perverseness and anger; By giving into Your hand, &#124;&#124; The afflicted leave &#91;it&#93; on You, &#124;&#124; You have been a helper of the fatherless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:12; Psalms 37:17; Job 38:15"> Break the arm of the wicked and the evil, &#124;&#124; Seek out his wickedness, find none;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 29:10; Jeremiah 10:10; 1 Timothy 1:17"> YHWH &#91;is&#93; King for all time and forever, &#124;&#124; The nations have perished out of His land!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; 2 Chronicles 34:27; Psalms 34:15"> You have heard the desire of the humble, O YHWH. You prepare their heart; You cause Your ear to attend,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 82:3; Isaiah 11:4"> To judge the fatherless and bruised: He adds no more to oppress—man of the earth!</span>
