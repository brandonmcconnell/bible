<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:1–51; Colossians 1:11; 1 John 4:19"> TO THE OVERSEER. BY A SERVANT OF YHWH, BY DAVID, WHO HAS SPOKEN TO YHWH THE WORDS OF THIS SONG IN THE DAY YHWH DELIVERED HIM FROM THE HAND OF ALL HIS ENEMIES, AND FROM THE HAND OF SAUL, AND HE SAYS: I love You, O YHWH, my strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 144:2; Psalms 62:7; Psalms 19:14"> YHWH &#91;is&#93; my rock, and my bulwark, &#124;&#124; And my deliverer, &#124;&#124; My God &#91;is&#93; my rock, &#124;&#124; I trust in Him: My shield, and the horn of my salvation, &#124;&#124; My high tower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 91:15; Psalms 145:3; Psalms 62:8"> I call on YHWH, the Praised One, &#124;&#124; And I am saved from my enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 116:3; Matthew 27:24–25; Mark 14:33–34"> Cords of death have surrounded me, &#124;&#124; And streams of the worthless make me afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 88:3–8; Acts 2:24"> Cords of Sheol have surrounded me, &#124;&#124; Snares of death have been before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; 2 Samuel 22:7; Psalms 34:15"> In my adversity I call YHWH, &#124;&#124; And I cry to my God. He hears my voice from His temple, &#124;&#124; And My cry comes into His ears before Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 4:31; Psalms 68:7–8; Acts 16:25–26"> And the earth shakes and trembles, &#124;&#124; And foundations of hills are troubled, &#124;&#124; And they shake—because He has wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 21:9; Psalms 11:6; Nahum 1:5–6"> Smoke has gone up from His nostrils, &#124;&#124; And fire from His mouth consumes, &#124;&#124; Coals have been kindled by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 144:5–15; Deuteronomy 33:26; 2 Samuel 22:10"> And He inclines the heavens, and comes down, &#124;&#124; And thick darkness &#91;is&#93; under His feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; 2 Samuel 22:11–12; Psalms 99:1"> And He rides on a cherub, and flies, &#124;&#124; And He flies on wings of wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Deuteronomy 4:11; Psalms 91:1"> He makes darkness His secret place, &#124;&#124; Around Him His dwelling place, &#124;&#124; Darkness of waters, thick clouds of the skies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; Revelation 16:21; Matthew 17:2"> From the brightness before Him His thick clouds have passed on, &#124;&#124; Hail and coals of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 104:7; 1 Samuel 7:10; Psalms 140:10"> And YHWH thunders in the heavens, &#124;&#124; And the Most High gives forth His voice, &#124;&#124; Hail and coals of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 144:6; Habakkuk 3:11; Numbers 24:8"> And He sends His arrows and scatters them, &#124;&#124; And much lightning, and crushes them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 106:9; Exodus 15:8; Micah 6:2"> And the streams of waters are seen, &#124;&#124; And foundations of the earth are revealed, &#124;&#124; From Your rebuke, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; From the breath of the wind of Your anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:17; Psalms 144:7; Psalms 57:3"> He sends from above—He takes me, &#124;&#124; He draws me out of many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14–15; Psalms 35:10; Psalms 142:6"> He delivers me from my strong enemy, &#124;&#124; And from those hating me, &#124;&#124; For they have been stronger than I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Obadiah 1:10–14; Deuteronomy 32:35; Psalms 46:11"> They go before me in a day of my calamity &#124;&#124; And YHWH is for a support to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 118:5; Psalms 31:8; Psalms 18:36"> And He brings me forth to a large place, &#124;&#124; He draws me out, because He delighted in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 24:4; Matthew 6:4; Psalms 58:11"> YHWH repays me &#124;&#124; According to my righteousness, &#124;&#124; According to the cleanness of my hands, &#124;&#124; He returns to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 119:102; Psalms 17:4; Psalms 26:1"> For I have kept the ways of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And have not done wickedly against my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:13; Psalms 119:112; Psalms 119:30"> For all His judgments &#91;are&#93; before me, &#124;&#124; And I do not turn His statutes from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 18:8–9; Psalms 11:7; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> And I am perfect with Him, &#124;&#124; And I keep myself from my iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:41–42; Ruth 2:12; 1 Samuel 26:23"> And YHWH returns to me, &#124;&#124; According to my righteousness, &#124;&#124; According to the cleanness of my hands, &#124;&#124; Before His eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Psalms 62:12; Matthew 18:33–35"> With the kind You show Yourself kind, &#124;&#124; With a perfect man You show Yourself perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 3:34; Leviticus 26:23–24; James 2:13"> With the pure You show Yourself pure, &#124;&#124; And with the perverse You show Yourself a wrestler,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; James 2:5; Isaiah 57:15"> For You save a poor people, &#124;&#124; And cause the eyes of the high to fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:29; Job 29:3; Isaiah 42:16"> For You light my lamp, &#124;&#124; My God YHWH enlightens my darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:30; Ephesians 6:10–13; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> For by You I run &#91;against&#93; a troop! And by my God I leap a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Proverbs 30:5; 2 Samuel 22:31"> God—perfect &#91;is&#93; His way, &#124;&#124; The saying of YHWH is tried, &#124;&#124; He &#91;is&#93; a shield to all those trusting in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:31; Psalms 86:8; 2 Samuel 22:32"> For who &#91;is&#93; God besides YHWH? And who &#91;is&#93; a rock except our God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 45:5; Psalms 28:7; 2 Samuel 22:33"> God—who is girding me &#91;with&#93; strength, &#124;&#124; And He makes my way perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:19; Deuteronomy 32:13; Deuteronomy 33:29"> Making my feet like does, &#124;&#124; And on my high places causes me to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 144:1; Psalms 46:9; 2 Samuel 22:36"> Teaching my hands for battle, &#124;&#124; And a bow of bronze was brought down by my arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:36; James 3:17–18; Psalms 28:7"> And You give to me the shield of Your salvation, &#124;&#124; And Your right hand supports me, &#124;&#124; And Your lowliness makes me great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; 2 Samuel 22:37; Job 36:16"> You enlarge my step under me, &#124;&#124; And my ankles have not slipped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 53:10–12; Psalms 44:5; Psalms 35:2"> I pursue my enemies, and overtake them, &#124;&#124; And do not turn back until they are consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:1–25; 2 Samuel 22:39; 1 Samuel 23:5"> I strike them, and they are not able to rise, &#124;&#124; They fall under my feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:40–41; Psalms 18:32; 1 Corinthians 15:25–28"> And You gird me &#91;with&#93; strength for battle, &#124;&#124; You cause my withstanders to bow under me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 21:12; Psalms 94:23"> As for my enemies—You have given to me the neck, &#124;&#124; As for those hating me—I cut them off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; Job 27:9; Job 35:12–13"> They cry, and there is no savior, &#124;&#124; On YHWH, and He does not answer them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 10:6; Zechariah 10:5; 2 Kings 13:7"> And I beat them as dust before wind, &#124;&#124; I empty them out as mire of the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Isaiah 55:5; 2 Samuel 3:1; Isaiah 52:15"> You deliver me &#124;&#124; From the strivings of the people, &#124;&#124; You place me for a head of nations, &#124;&#124; A people I have not known serve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 66:3; Psalms 81:15; Deuteronomy 33:29"> At the hearing of the ear they listen to me, &#124;&#124; Sons of a stranger feign obedience to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Micah 7:17; James 1:11; Isaiah 24:4"> Sons of a stranger fade away, &#124;&#124; And are slain out of their close places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:47; Psalms 57:5; Psalms 18:2"> YHWH lives—and blessed &#91;is&#93; my rock, &#124;&#124; And exalted is the God of my salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 47:3; Romans 12:19; Nahum 1:2"> God—who is giving vengeance to me, &#124;&#124; And He subdues peoples under me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 140:1; Psalms 140:4; Psalms 86:14"> My deliverer from my enemies, &#124;&#124; You raise me above my withstanders, &#124;&#124; Deliver me from a man of violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Romans 15:9; 2 Samuel 22:50–51; Psalms 72:18–19"> Therefore I confess You among nations, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; And I sing praise to Your Name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 144:10; Psalms 132:10; 2 Samuel 7:13"> Magnifying the salvation of His king, &#124;&#124; And doing kindness to His anointed, &#124;&#124; To David, and to his seed—for all time!</span>
