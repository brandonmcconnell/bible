<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 101:1; Psalms 89:8; Psalms 119:90"> AN INSTRUCTION OF ETHAN THE EZRAHITE. Of the kind acts of YHWH, I sing for all time, &#124;&#124; From generation to generation I make known Your faithfulness with my mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 36:5; Nehemiah 9:31; Psalms 89:5"> For I said, “Kindness is built for all time, &#124;&#124; The heavens! You establish Your faithfulness in them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 132:11; 2 Samuel 7:10–16; Psalms 89:34–35"> I have made a covenant for My chosen, &#124;&#124; I have sworn to My servant David:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12–16; Luke 1:32–33; Psalms 132:12"> “Even for all time I establish your seed, &#124;&#124; And have built your throne to generation and generation.” Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 97:6; Psalms 19:1; Isaiah 44:23"> And the heavens confess Your wonders, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; Your faithfulness &#91;is&#93; also in an assembly of holy ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 86:8; Psalms 71:19; Psalms 113:5"> For who in the sky, compares himself to YHWH? &#91;Who&#93; is like to YHWH among sons of the mighty?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 47:2; Psalms 76:7–11; Hebrews 12:28–29"> God is very terrible, &#124;&#124; In the secret counsel of His holy ones, &#124;&#124; And fearful over all surrounding Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 71:19; 1 Samuel 2:2; Jeremiah 32:17"> O YHWH, God of Hosts, &#124;&#124; Who &#91;is&#93; like You—a strong YAH? And Your faithfulness &#91;is&#93; around You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 65:7; Psalms 107:25–29; Mark 4:41"> You &#91;are&#93; ruler over the pride of the sea, &#124;&#124; In the lifting up of its billows You restrain them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 87:4; Psalms 68:30; Psalms 144:6"> You have bruised Rahab, as one wounded. You have scattered Your enemies with the arm of Your strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:11; Genesis 1:1; Psalms 115:16"> The heavens &#91;are&#93; Yours, &#124;&#124; The earth &#91;is&#93; also Yours, &#124;&#124; The habitable world and its fullness, &#124;&#124; You have founded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 12:1; Job 26:7; Joshua 19:22"> North and south You have appointed them, &#124;&#124; Tabor and Hermon sing in Your Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 4:34–35; Psalms 89:10; Psalms 62:11"> You have an arm with might, &#124;&#124; Strong is Your hand—high Your right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Proverbs 16:12; Psalms 45:6–7"> Righteousness and judgment &#124;&#124; &#91;Are&#93; the fixed place of Your throne, &#124;&#124; Kindness and truth go before Your face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Job 29:3; Psalms 4:6"> O the blessedness of the people knowing the shout, &#124;&#124; O YHWH, they habitually walk in the light of Your face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Philippians 4:4; Philippians 3:9; Luke 1:47"> They rejoice in Your Name all the day, &#124;&#124; And they are exalted in Your righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 75:10; Psalms 92:10; Psalms 148:14"> For You &#91;are&#93; the beauty of their strength, &#124;&#124; And in Your good will is our horn exalted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 47:9; Psalms 71:22; Isaiah 33:22"> For our shield &#91;is&#93; of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And our king to the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:18; 1 Kings 11:34; Psalms 89:3"> Then You have spoken in vision, &#124;&#124; To Your saint, indeed, &#124;&#124; You say, I have placed help on a mighty one, &#124;&#124; Exalted a chosen one out of the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12–13; Isaiah 61:1–3; 1 Samuel 16:1"> I have found My servant David, &#124;&#124; With My holy oil I have anointed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Psalms 18:32–39; Psalms 89:13"> With whom My hand is established, &#124;&#124; My arm also strengthens him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:9; 2 Samuel 7:10; Matthew 4:1–10"> An enemy does not exact on him, &#124;&#124; And a son of perverseness does not afflict him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:9; 2 Samuel 3:1; Psalms 18:40"> And I have beaten down his adversaries before him, &#124;&#124; And I plague those hating him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 89:16–17; 2 Corinthians 1:20; Psalms 91:14"> And My faithfulness and kindness &#91;are&#93; with him, &#124;&#124; And in My Name is his horn exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 80:11; Psalms 2:8; Psalms 72:8–11"> And I have set his hand on the sea, &#124;&#124; And his right hand on the rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; 2 Samuel 22:47; Psalms 95:1"> He proclaims to Me: “You &#91;are&#93; my Father, &#124;&#124; My God, and the rock of my salvation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; Colossians 1:18; Psalms 2:7"> I also appoint him firstborn, &#124;&#124; Highest of the kings of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; Jeremiah 33:20–21; Acts 13:32–34"> For all time I keep for him My kindness, &#124;&#124; And My covenant &#91;is&#93; steadfast with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 89:4; Deuteronomy 11:21; Psalms 89:36"> And I have set his seed forever, &#124;&#124; And his throne as the days of the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; 1 Chronicles 28:9; Psalms 119:53"> If his sons forsake My law, &#124;&#124; And do not walk in My judgments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 55:20"> If they defile My statutes, &#124;&#124; And do not keep My commands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; Hebrews 12:6–11; Proverbs 3:11–12"> I have looked after their transgression with a rod, &#124;&#124; And their iniquity with strokes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 54:8–10; 2 Samuel 7:15; Lamentations 3:31–32"> And I do not break My kindness from him, &#124;&#124; Nor do I deal falsely in My faithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Matthew 24:35; James 1:17"> I do not profane My covenant, &#124;&#124; And I do not change that which is going forth from My lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 132:11; Hebrews 6:13; Titus 1:2"> Once I have sworn by My holiness, &#124;&#124; I do not lie to David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 72:5; Psalms 89:29; Isaiah 59:21"> His seed is for all time, &#124;&#124; And his throne &#91;is&#93; as the sun before Me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35–36; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalms 104:19"> It is established as the moon for all time, &#124;&#124; And the witness in the sky is steadfast. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Deuteronomy 32:19; Psalms 106:40"> And You, You have cast off, and reject, &#124;&#124; You have shown Yourself angry with Your anointed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Lamentations 5:16; Psalms 74:7; Psalms 89:34–36"> Have rejected the covenant of Your servant, &#124;&#124; You have defiled his crown to the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 80:12; Lamentations 2:2; Lamentations 2:5"> You have broken down all his hedges, &#124;&#124; You have made his fortifications a ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Lamentations 5:1; Ezekiel 5:14–15"> Everyone passing by the way has spoiled him, &#124;&#124; He has been a reproach to his neighbors,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 80:6; Revelation 11:10; Deuteronomy 28:43"> You have exalted the right hand of his adversaries, &#124;&#124; You have caused all his enemies to rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 7:8–12; Numbers 14:42; Psalms 44:10"> Also—You turn back the sharpness of his sword, &#124;&#124; And have not established him in battle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 4:1–2; 2 Thessalonians 2:3–10; Psalms 89:39"> Have caused &#91;him&#93; to cease from his brightness, &#124;&#124; And have cast down his throne to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 109:29; Psalms 44:15; Micah 7:10"> You have shortened the days of his youth, &#124;&#124; Have covered him over &#91;with&#93; shame. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 79:5; Psalms 78:63; Psalms 88:14"> Until when, O YHWH, are You hidden? Does Your fury burn as fire forever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Job 14:1; Job 10:9; Job 7:7"> Remember how &#91;short&#93; my lifetime &#91;is&#93;. Why have You created in vain &#124;&#124; All the sons of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 49:15; Hebrews 11:5; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> Who &#91;is&#93; the man that lives, and does not see death? He delivers his soul from the hand of Sheol. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:15; Hebrews 7:21; Isaiah 55:3"> Where &#91;are&#93; Your former kindnesses, O Lord, &#124;&#124; &#91;Which&#93; You have sworn to David in Your faithfulness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 74:22; Psalms 74:18; Psalms 69:9"> Remember, O Lord, the reproach of Your servants, &#124;&#124; I have borne in my bosom all the strivings of the peoples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14–16; Psalms 74:10; 2 Samuel 16:7–8"> With which Your enemies reproached, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; With which they have reproached &#124;&#124; The steps of Your anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Psalms 72:18–19; Psalms 41:13; 1 Timothy 1:17"> Blessed &#91;is&#93; YHWH for all time. Amen and amen!</span>
