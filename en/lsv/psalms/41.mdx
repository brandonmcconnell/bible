<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Proverbs 14:21; 2 Corinthians 9:8–14"> TO THE OVERSEER. A PSALM OF DAVID. O the blessedness of him &#124;&#124; Who is acting wisely to the poor, &#124;&#124; In a day of evil YHWH delivers him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 45:4–5; Psalms 27:12; 1 Timothy 4:8"> YHWH preserves him and revives him, &#124;&#124; He is blessed in the land, &#124;&#124; And You do not give him into the will of his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 20:5–6; Psalms 73:26; Philippians 2:26–27"> YHWH supports &#91;him&#93; on a bed of sickness, &#124;&#124; You have turned his bed in his weakness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 103:3; James 5:15–16; 2 Chronicles 30:18–20"> I said, “O YHWH, favor me, &#124;&#124; Heal my soul, for I sinned against You,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Psalms 102:8; Psalms 38:12"> My enemies say evil of me: When he dies—his name has perished!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; Luke 11:53–54; Proverbs 26:24–26"> And if he came to see—he speaks vanity, &#124;&#124; His heart gathers iniquity to itself, &#124;&#124; He goes out—at the street he speaks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:28; Psalms 56:5–6; Psalms 31:13"> All hating me whisper together against me, &#124;&#124; Against me they devise evil to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 71:11; Job 2:7–8; Matthew 27:41–46"> A worthless thing is poured out on him, &#124;&#124; And because he lay down he does not rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 13:18; Psalms 55:12–14; Matthew 26:23"> Even my ally, in whom I trusted, &#124;&#124; One eating my bread, &#124;&#124; Made the heel great against me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 21:8–10; Luke 19:27; Psalms 18:37–42"> And You, YHWH, favor me, &#124;&#124; And cause me to rise, &#124;&#124; And I give repayment to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Colossians 2:15; Psalms 124:6; Psalms 147:11"> By this I have known, &#124;&#124; That You have delighted in me, &#124;&#124; Because my enemy does not shout over me.</span>
