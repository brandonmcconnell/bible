<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 83:3; Psalms 56:6; Jeremiah 20:10"> For my enemies have spoken against me, &#124;&#124; And those watching my soul have taken counsel together,</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 130:7; 1 John 3:3; Psalms 71:6"> And I continually wait with hope, &#124;&#124; And have added to all Your praise.</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 35:10; Psalms 36:5–6; Psalms 57:10"> And Your righteousness, O God, &#91;is&#93; to the heights, &#124;&#124; Because You have done great things, &#124;&#124; O God, who &#91;is&#93; like You?</span>
