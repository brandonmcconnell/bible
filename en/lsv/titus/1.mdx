<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 1:1; 1 Timothy 6:3; 2 Timothy 2:25"> Paul, a servant of God, and an apostle of Jesus Christ, according to the faith of the chosen ones of God, and an acknowledging of truth that &#91;is&#93; according to piety,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; 1 John 2:25; 2 Timothy 1:9"> on hope of continuous life, which God, who does not lie, promised before times of ages</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:11; Titus 3:4–6; 1 Timothy 1:1"> (and He revealed His word in &#91;His&#93; own times), in preaching, which I was entrusted with, according to a charge of God our Savior,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:2; Jude 1:3; 2 Peter 1:1"> to Titus—true child according to a common faith: Grace, &#91;&#91;kindness,&#93;&#93; peace, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ our Savior!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 14:23; 1 Timothy 1:3; Ecclesiastes 12:9"> For this cause I left you in Crete, that you may arrange the things lacking, and may set elders down in every city, as I appointed to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2–7; 1 Timothy 3:12; Titus 1:6–8"> if anyone is blameless, a husband of one wife, having believing children, not under accusation of riotous living or insubordinate—</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; 2 Peter 2:10; 1 Peter 4:10"> for it is required of the overseer to be blameless, as God’s steward, not self-pleased, nor prone to anger, not given to wine, not an abuser, not given to shameful gain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; Titus 2:7; 1 Kings 5:7"> but a lover of strangers, a lover of &#91;the&#93; good, sober-minded, righteous, holy, self-controlled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:15; 2 Timothy 1:13; 1 Timothy 6:3"> holding—according to the teaching—to the steadfast word, that he may also be able to exhort in the sound teaching, and to convict the deniers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:6; 1 John 4:1; Galatians 2:4"> for there are many both insubordinate, vain-talkers, and mind-deceivers—especially those of the circumcision—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:6; 1 Timothy 6:5; Romans 3:19"> whose mouths must be covered, who overturn whole households, teaching what things it should not, for &#91;the&#93; sake of shameful gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 16:18; 1 Timothy 4:2; Acts 17:28"> A certain one of them, a prophet of their own, said, “Cretans! Always liars, evil beasts, lazy bellies!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 2:2; 1 Timothy 5:20; Titus 2:15"> This testimony is true; for which cause convict them sharply, that they may be sound in the faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:4; Colossians 2:22; Galatians 4:9"> not giving heed to Jewish fables and commands of men, turning themselves away from the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Romans 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:23"> All things, indeed, &#91;are&#93; pure to the pure, and nothing &#91;is&#93; pure to the defiled and unsteadfast, but even the mind and the conscience of them &#91;is&#93; defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 2:4; Isaiah 29:13; Ephesians 5:6"> They profess to know God, but they deny &#91;Him&#93; by their works, being abominable, and disobedient, and disapproved to every good work.</span>
