<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 9:28–36; Matthew 26:37; Mark 5:37"> And after six days Jesus takes Peter, and James, and his brother John, and brings them up to a high mountain by themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 104:2; Matthew 28:3; Luke 9:29"> and He was transfigured before them, and His face shone as the sun, and His garments became white as the light,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:44; Mark 9:4; Malachi 4:5"> and behold, Moses and Elijah appeared to them, talking together with Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 9:5–6; Luke 9:33; John 14:8–9"> And Peter answering said to Jesus, “Lord, it is good to us to be here; if You will, we may make three shelters here: one for You, and one for Moses, and one for Elijah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:17; Matthew 12:18; Deuteronomy 18:15"> While he is yet speaking, behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them, and behold, a voice out of the cloud, saying, “This is My Son, the Beloved, in whom I delighted; hear Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 26:14; 2 Peter 1:18; Daniel 8:17"> And the disciples having heard, fell on their face, and were exceedingly afraid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 10:18; Matthew 14:27; Daniel 8:18"> and Jesus having come near, touched them, and said, “Rise, do not be afraid,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:36; Mark 9:8; Acts 12:10–11"> and having lifted up their eyes, they saw no one, except Jesus only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 8:30; Mark 9:9–13; Matthew 17:23"> And as they are coming down from the mountain, Jesus charged them, saying, “Say to no one the vision, until the Son of Man may rise out of the dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 4:5–6; Matthew 11:14; John 1:21"> And His disciples questioned Him, saying, “Why then do the scribes say that Elijah must come first?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 3:21; Luke 1:16–17; Malachi 4:6"> And Jesus answering said to them, “Elijah does indeed come first, and will restore all things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3–12; Matthew 16:21; Acts 13:24–28"> and I say to you, Elijah already came, and they did not know him, but did with him whatever they would, so also the Son of Man is about to suffer by them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 11:14"> Then the disciples understood that He spoke to them concerning John the Immerser.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 9:14–29; Mark 1:40; Acts 10:25–26"> And when they came to the multitude, there came to Him a man, kneeling down to Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Job 1:10–19; Mark 9:20–22"> and saying, “Lord, deal kindly with my son, for he is &#91;a&#93; lunatic, and suffers miserably, for he often falls into the fire, and often into the water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Luke 9:40; Acts 19:15–16"> and I brought him near to Your disciples, and they were not able to heal him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; Matthew 6:30; Mark 16:14"> And Jesus answering said, “O generation, unsteadfast and perverse, until when will I be with you? Until when will I bear you? Bring him to Me here”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 9:22; Mark 1:34; Mark 5:8"> and Jesus rebuked him, and the demon went out of him, and the boy was healed from that hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 9:28; Mark 4:10"> Then the disciples having come to Jesus by Himself, said, “Why were we not able to cast him out?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 17:6; Mark 11:23; Luke 1:37"> And Jesus said to them, “Through your want of faith; for truly I say to you, if you may have faith as a grain of mustard, you will say to this mountain, Move from here to there, and it will move, and nothing will be impossible to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> &#91;&#91;and this kind does not go forth except in prayer and fasting.”&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Luke 18:31–34; Matthew 17:23"> And while they are living in Galilee, Jesus said to them, “The Son of Man is about to be delivered up into the hands of men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Isaiah 53:10–12; Mark 8:31"> and they will kill Him, and the third day He will rise,” and they were exceedingly sorry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 38:26; Exodus 30:13; Mark 9:33"> And they having come to Capernaum, those receiving the didrachmas came near to Peter and said, “Your teacher—does He not pay the didrachmas?” He says, “Yes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 22:21; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 22:17"> And when he came into the house, Jesus anticipated him, saying, “What do you think, Simon? The kings of the earth—from whom do they receive custom or poll-tax? From their sons or from the strangers?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Peter says to Him, “From the strangers.” Jesus said to him, “Then the sons are free;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; 1 Corinthians 8:9; 1 Corinthians 9:19–22"> but that we may not cause them to stumble, having gone to the sea, cast a hook, and the fish that has come up first take up, and having opened its mouth, you will find a stater, that having taken, give to them for Me and you.”</span>
