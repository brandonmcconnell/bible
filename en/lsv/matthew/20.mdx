<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 13:47; Matthew 13:24; Song of Songs 8:11–12"> “For the kingdom of the heavens is like to a man, a householder, who went forth with the morning to hire workmen for his vineyard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:26; Ecclesiastes 12:1; Matthew 22:19"> and having agreed with the workmen for a denarius a day, he sent them into his vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:13; Acts 2:15; Matthew 20:6–7"> And having gone forth about the third hour, he saw others standing in the marketplace idle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; 1 Peter 4:2–3; Luke 19:7–10"> and to these he said, Go—also you—to the vineyard, and whatever may be righteous I will give you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24–26; Acts 10:3; Mark 15:33–34"> and they went away. Again, having gone forth about the sixth and the ninth hour, he did in like manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; John 9:4; Acts 17:21"> And about the eleventh hour, having gone forth, he found others standing idle and says to them, Why have you stood here idle all day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:11–12; Hebrews 6:10; John 9:4"> They say to him, Because no one hired us; he says to them, Go—you also—to the vineyard, and whatever may be righteous you will receive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:15; 1 Peter 4:10; Romans 2:6–10"> And evening having come, the lord of the vineyard says to his steward, Call the workmen, and pay them the reward, having begun from the last—to the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 20:6–7; Luke 23:40–43; Matthew 20:2"> And they of about the eleventh hour having come, each received a denarius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the first having come, supposed that they will receive more, and they received, they also, each a denarius,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:16; Luke 19:7; Acts 22:21–22"> and having received &#91;it&#93;, they were murmuring against the householder, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:5–6; 2 Corinthians 11:23–28; Luke 12:55"> that, These, the last, worked one hour, and you made them equal to us, who were bearing the burden of the day—and the heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:50; Matthew 22:12; Romans 9:14–15"> And he answering said to one of them, Friend, I do no unrighteousness to you; did you not agree with me for a denarius?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:2; Luke 15:31; Matthew 6:6"> Take that which is yours, and go; and I will to give to this, the last, also as to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 9:15–24; Deuteronomy 15:9; Jonah 4:1–4"> is it not lawful to me to do what I will in my own? Is your eye evil because I am good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Mark 10:31; Matthew 22:14"> So the last will be first, and the first last, for many are called, and few chosen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 10:32–34; Luke 18:31–34; Matthew 16:13"> And Jesus going up to Jerusalem, took the twelve disciples by themselves in the way and said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Acts 2:23; Matthew 27:1"> “Behold, we go up to Jerusalem, and the Son of Man will be delivered to the chief priests and scribes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Mark 15:16–20; Matthew 27:27–31"> and they will condemn Him to death, and will deliver Him to the nations to mock, and to scourge, and to crucify, and the third day He will rise again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Mark 10:35–45; Matthew 8:2"> Then came near to Him the mother of the sons of Zebedee, with her sons, prostrating and asking something from Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Mark 10:36–37; Esther 5:3"> and He said to her, “What do you will?” She says to Him, “Say that they may sit—these two sons of mine—one on Your right hand and one on the left, in Your kingdom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 26:42; Luke 22:42; Matthew 26:39"> And Jesus answering said, “You have not known what you ask for yourselves; are you able to drink of the cup that I am about to drink? And with the immersion that I am immersed with, to be immersed?” They say to Him, “We are able.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 1:9; Matthew 25:34; Acts 12:2"> And He says to them, “Of My cup indeed you will drink, and with the immersion that I am immersed with you will be immersed; but to sit on My right hand and on My left is not Mine to give, but—to those for whom it has been prepared by My Father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Philippians 2:3; Mark 10:41"> And the ten having heard, were much displeased with the two brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 22:25–27; Matthew 20:25–28; John 13:12–17"> and Jesus having called them near, said, “You have known that the rulers of the nations exercise lordship over them, and those &#91;who are&#93; great exercise authority over them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 10:43; Mark 9:35; Mark 10:45"> but not so will it be among you, but whoever may will among you to become great, let him be your servant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:26; Mark 9:33–35; Matthew 18:4"> and whoever may will among you to be first, let him be your servant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 22:27; Hebrews 9:28; Titus 2:14"> even as the Son of Man did not come to be ministered to, but to minister, and to give His life &#91;as&#93; a ransom for many.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 10:46–52; Luke 18:35–43; Matthew 9:27–31"> And they going forth from Jericho, there followed Him a great multitude,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 10:46; Isaiah 42:16; Matthew 21:9"> and behold, two blind men sitting by the way, having heard that Jesus passes by, cried, saying, “Deal kindly with us, Lord—Son of David.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 19:13; Colossians 4:2; Matthew 7:7–8"> And the multitude charged them that they might be silent, and they cried out the more, saying, “Deal kindly with us Lord—Son of David.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:37; Philippians 4:6; Matthew 20:21"> And having stood, Jesus called them and said, “What do you will &#91;that&#93; I may do to you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 119:18; Ephesians 1:17–19"> They say to Him, “Lord, that our eyes may be opened”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15–16; Matthew 9:29; Acts 26:18"> and having been moved with compassion, Jesus touched their eyes, and immediately their eyes received sight, and they followed Him.</span>
