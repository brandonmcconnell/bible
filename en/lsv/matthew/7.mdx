<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 6:37; Romans 2:1–2; James 4:11–12"> “Do not judge, that you may not be judged,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 2:13; Mark 4:24; Luke 6:38"> for in what judgment you judge, you will be judged, and in what measure you measure, it will be measured to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 8:7–9; Galatians 6:1; Luke 6:41–42"> And why do you behold the speck that &#91;is&#93; in your brother’s eye, and do not consider the beam that &#91;is&#93; in your own eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Or, how will you say to your brother, Permit &#91;that&#93; I may cast out the speck from your eye, and behold, the beam &#91;is&#93; in your own eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:42; Psalms 51:9–13; Matthew 23:13–28"> Hypocrite, first cast out the beam out of your own eye, and then you will see clearly to cast out the speck out of your brother’s eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 9:7–8; Proverbs 23:9; Matthew 10:14–15"> You may not give that which is &#91;holy&#93; to the dogs, nor cast your pearls before the pigs, that they may not trample them among their feet, and having turned—may tear you apart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; 1 John 3:22; John 15:7"> Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 81:10; Psalms 81:16; Luke 23:42–43"> for everyone who is asking receives, and he who is seeking finds, and to him who is knocking it will be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13"> Or what man is of you, of whom, if his son may ask &#91;for&#93; a loaf—a stone will he present to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And if he may ask &#91;for&#93; a fish—a serpent will he present to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13; James 1:17; Romans 8:32"> If, therefore, you being evil, have known to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father who &#91;is&#93; in the heavens give good things to those asking Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:31; Romans 13:8–10; Galatians 5:13–14"> All things, therefore, whatever you may will that men may be doing to you, so also do to them, for this is the Law and the Prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 13:24–25; Proverbs 16:25; John 14:6"> Go in through the narrow gate, because wide &#91;is&#93; the gate and broad the way that is leading to the destruction, and many are those going in through it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 13:23–30; Matthew 22:14; Matthew 16:24–25"> how narrow &#91;is&#93; the gate and compressed the way that is leading to life, and few are those finding it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 4:1; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Matthew 24:11"> But take heed of the false prophets who come to you in sheep’s clothing, and inwardly are ravenous wolves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:43–45; Matthew 12:33; James 3:12"> From their fruits you will know them; do &#91;men&#93; gather grapes from thorns? Or figs from thistles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 12:33–35; Galatians 5:22–24; Ephesians 5:9"> So every good tree yields good fruits, but the bad tree yields evil fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; 1 John 3:9–10"> A good tree is not able to yield evil fruits, nor a bad tree to yield good fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 3:10; Hebrews 6:8; John 15:2–6"> Every tree not yielding good fruit is cut down and is cast into fire:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 7:16; Acts 5:38"> therefore from their fruits you will know them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:13; Luke 6:46; James 1:22"> Not everyone who is saying to Me, Lord, Lord, will come into the kingdom of the heavens, but he who is doing the will of My Father who is in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 13:25–27; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Malachi 3:17–18"> Many will say to Me in that day, Lord, Lord, have we not prophesied in Your Name? And in Your Name cast out demons? And in Your Name done many mighty things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Psalms 6:8; Luke 13:25"> And then I will acknowledge to them, that—I never knew you, depart from Me you who are working lawlessness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 6:47–49; John 13:17; 1 Corinthians 3:10–11"> Therefore, everyone who hears these words of Mine, and does them, I will liken him to a wise man who built his house on the rock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 1:12; Psalms 125:1–2; 1 Peter 1:7"> and the rain descended, and the streams came, and the winds blew, and they beat on that house, and it did not fall, for it had been founded on the rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:9; Luke 6:49; James 2:20"> And everyone who is hearing these words of Mine, and is not doing them, will be likened to a foolish man who built his house on the sand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 13:19–22; 2 Peter 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> and the rain descended, and the streams came, and the winds blew, and they beat on that house, and it fell, and its fall was great.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 4:32; Mark 1:22; John 7:46"> And it came to pass, when Jesus finished these words, the multitudes were astonished at His teaching,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12–13; Luke 20:8; Deuteronomy 18:18–19"> for He was teaching them as having authority, and not as the scribes.</span>
