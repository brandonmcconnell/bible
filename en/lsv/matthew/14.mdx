<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Mark 8:15; Luke 9:7–9"> At that time Herod the tetrarch heard the fame of Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 16:14; John 10:41; Mark 8:28"> and said to his servants, “This is John the Immerser, he rose from the dead, and because of this the mighty energies are working in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 6:17; Luke 3:19–20; Matthew 4:12"> For Herod having laid hold on John, bound him, and put him in prison, because of Herodias, his brother Philip’s wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:21; Leviticus 18:16; Mark 6:18"> for John was saying to him, “It is not lawful to you to have her,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 11:9; Mark 6:19–20"> and willing to kill him, he feared the multitude, because as a prophet they were holding him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 6:21–23; Genesis 40:20; Hosea 1:5–6"> But the birthday of Herod being kept, the daughter of Herodias danced in the midst, and pleased Herod,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 5:3; Esther 5:6; Esther 7:2"> after which with an oath he professed to give her whatever she might ask.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 6:24; Proverbs 29:10; Proverbs 1:16"> And she, having been instigated by her mother, says, “Give me here on a plate the head of John the Immerser”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:12–16; 1 Samuel 14:28; Mark 6:26"> and the king was grieved, but because of the oaths and of those dining with him, he commanded &#91;it&#93; to be given;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 17:12; Matthew 21:35–36; Luke 9:9"> and having sent, he beheaded John in the prison,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 16:6; Genesis 49:7; Ezekiel 16:3–4"> and his head was brought on a plate, and was given to the girl, and she brought &#91;it&#93; near to her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 8:2; Matthew 27:58–61"> And his disciples having come, took up the body, and buried it, and having come, they told Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:10–17; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 6:30–44"> and Jesus having heard, withdrew from there in a boat to a desolate place by Himself, and the multitudes having heard followed Him on land from the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Matthew 9:36; Hebrews 2:17"> And Jesus having come forth, saw a great multitude, and was moved with compassion on them, and healed their sick;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 15:23; Mark 8:3; Mark 6:35–36"> and evening having come, His disciples came to Him, saying, “The place is desolate, and the hour has now past, let away the multitudes that, having gone into the villages, they may buy food for themselves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; Ecclesiastes 11:2"> And Jesus said to them, “They have no need to go away—you give them to eat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:33–34; Mark 6:37–38; Luke 9:13"> And they say to Him, “We have nothing here except five loaves and two fishes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And He said, “Bring them to Me here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 8:6–7; Luke 24:30; 1 Samuel 9:13"> And having commanded the multitudes to recline on the grass, and having taken the five loaves and the two fishes, having looked up to the sky, He blessed, and having broken, He gave the loaves to the disciples, and the disciples &#91;gave&#93; to the multitudes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:12; Mark 6:42–44; Matthew 16:8–10"> and they all ate, and were filled, and they took up what was over of the broken pieces twelve hand-baskets full;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 9:8–11; Acts 4:4"> and those eating were about five thousand men, apart from women and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 6:45–51; John 6:15–21; Matthew 15:39"> And immediately Jesus constrained His disciples to go into the boat, and to go before Him to the other side, until He might let away the multitudes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 6:46; Matthew 26:36"> and having let away the multitudes, He went up to the mountain by Himself to pray, and evening having come, He was there alone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:18; Mark 6:48; Matthew 8:24"> and the boat was now in the midst of the sea, distressed by the waves, for the wind was contrary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:8; John 6:19; Mark 6:48"> And in the fourth watch of the night Jesus went away to them, walking on the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 6:49–50; Revelation 1:17; Luke 1:11–12"> and the disciples having seen Him walking on the sea, were troubled, saying, “It is an apparition,” and from the fear they cried out;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Revelation 1:17–18; John 16:33"> and immediately Jesus spoke to them, saying, “Take courage! I AM; do not be afraid.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:68; Romans 12:3; Matthew 26:33–35"> And Peter answering Him said, “Lord, if it is You, command me to come to You on the waters”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Mark 9:23; Acts 3:16"> and He said, “Come”; and having gone down from the boat, Peter walked on the waters to come to Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 3:54–57; 2 Timothy 4:16–17; Mark 14:38"> but seeing the vehement wind, he was afraid, and having begun to sink, he cried out, saying, “Lord, save me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:6–8; Psalms 138:7; Genesis 22:14"> And immediately Jesus, having stretched forth the hand, laid hold of him and says to him, “Little faith! For why did you waver?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 107:29–30; Mark 4:41; John 6:21"> And they having gone into the boat, the wind stilled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:27; Psalms 2:7; Romans 1:4"> and those in the boat having come, worshiped Him, saying, “You are truly God’s Son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 6:53–56; Luke 5:1; John 6:24–25"> And having passed over, they came into the land of Gennesaret,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 4:24–25; Mark 6:55; Mark 1:28–34"> and having recognized Him, the men of that place sent forth to all that surrounding region, and they brought to Him all who were ill,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Mark 3:10; Exodus 28:33–43"> and were calling on Him that they might only touch the fringe of His garment, and as many as touched were saved.</span>
