<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:1; Luke 20:45–46; Matthew 15:10–20"> Then Jesus spoke to the multitudes, and to His disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 20:46; Mark 12:38; Malachi 2:7"> saying, “On the seat of Moses sat down the scribes and the Pharisees;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:16; Romans 2:19–24"> all, then, as much as they may say to you to observe, observe and do, but according to their works do not do, for they say, and do not do;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 11:46; Acts 15:10; Galatians 6:13"> for they bind together burdens &#91;too&#93; heavy and grievous to bear, and lay &#91;them&#93; on the shoulders of men, but with their finger they will not move them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 6:1–16; John 12:43; Philippians 2:3"> And all their works they do to be seen by men, and they make broad their phylacteries, and enlarge the fringes of their garments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 John 1:9; Luke 11:43–54; Mark 12:38–39"> they also cherish the first couches at the banquets, and the first seats in the synagogues,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 1:38; John 1:49; John 3:26"> and the salutations in the marketplaces, and to be called by men, Rabbi, Rabbi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 3:1; 1 Corinthians 1:12–13; 1 Peter 5:3"> And you may not be called Rabbi, for one is your teacher—the Christ, and you are all brothers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:1; Hebrews 12:9; Malachi 1:6"> and you may not call &#91;any&#93; your father on the earth, for one is your Father, who is in the heavens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> nor may you be called teachers, for one is your teacher—the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:19; Galatians 5:13; John 13:14–15"> And the greater of you will be your servant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; James 4:6; Proverbs 29:23"> and whoever will exalt himself will be humbled, and whoever will humble himself will be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Matthew 23:29; Matthew 23:23"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because you shut up the kingdom of the heavens before men, for you do not go in, nor do you permit those going in to enter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> &#91;&#91;Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because you eat up the houses of the widows, and for a pretense make long prayers, because of this you will receive more abundant judgment.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 2:3; Acts 13:10; Acts 13:43"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because you go around the sea and the dry land to make one proselyte, and whenever it may happen—you make him a son of Gehenna twofold more than yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:14; Matthew 5:33–35; James 5:12"> Woe to you, blind guides, who are saying, Whoever may swear by the temple, it is nothing, but whoever may swear by the gold of the temple—is debtor!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Numbers 16:38–39; Psalms 94:8"> Fools and blind! For which &#91;is&#93; greater, the gold, or the temple that is sanctifying the gold?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And, Whoever may swear by the altar, it is nothing, but whoever may swear by the gift that is on it—is debtor!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:29"> Fools and blind! For which &#91;is&#93; greater, the gift, or the altar that is sanctifying the gift?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> He therefore who swore by the altar, swears by it, and by all things on it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; 1 Kings 8:13; 2 Chronicles 6:2"> and he who swore by the temple, swears by it, and by Him who is dwelling in it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 5:34; Psalms 11:4; Isaiah 66:1"> and he who swore by Heaven, swears by the throne of God, and by Him who is sitting on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Micah 6:8; Hosea 6:6"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because you give tithe of the mint, and the dill, and the cumin, and neglected the weightier things of the Law—judgment, and kindness, and faith; these it was necessary to do, and those not to neglect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 19:24; Matthew 27:6–8; Matthew 15:2–6"> Blind guides! Who are straining out the gnat, and are swallowing the camel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:39–40; Matthew 15:19–20; Mark 7:4–13"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because you make clean the outside of the cup and the plate, and within they are full of robbery and self-indulgence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; James 4:8; Hebrews 10:22"> Blind Pharisee! First cleanse the inside of the cup and the plate, that the outside of them may also become clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 11:44; Acts 23:3; Numbers 19:16"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because you are like to whitewashed graves, which outwardly indeed appear beautiful, and within are full of bones of dead men, and of all uncleanness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Psalms 51:6; Matthew 23:5"> so also you outwardly indeed appear to men righteous, and within you are full of hypocrisy and lawlessness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 11:47–48; Acts 2:29"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because you build the graves of the prophets, and adorn the tombs of the righteous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:30; Matthew 23:34–35; Matthew 21:35–36"> and say, If we had been in the days of our fathers, we would not have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 7:51–52; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Joshua 24:22"> So that you testify to yourselves that you are sons of them who murdered the prophets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 15:16; Numbers 32:14; 1 Thessalonians 2:16"> and you fill up the measure of your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:7; Matthew 12:34; Matthew 5:22"> Serpents! Brood of vipers! How may you escape from the judgment of Gehenna?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 10:16–17; Matthew 13:52; Colossians 1:28"> Because of this, behold, I send to you prophets, and wise men, and scribes, and of them you will kill and crucify, and of them you will scourge in your synagogues, and will pursue from city to city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20–22; Zechariah 1:1; Hebrews 11:4"> that on you may come all the righteous blood being poured out on the earth from the blood of Abel the righteous, to the blood of Zacharias son of Barachias, whom you murdered between the temple and the altar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Matthew 24:34; Mark 13:30–31"> truly I say to you, all these things will come on this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 13:34–35; Ruth 2:12; Psalms 91:4"> Jerusalem, Jerusalem, that are killing the prophets, and stoning those sent to you, how often I willed to gather your children together, as a hen gathers her own chickens under the wings, and you did not will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:5; Luke 13:35; Luke 21:24"> Behold, your house is left to you desolate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 118:26; Matthew 21:9; Romans 11:25"> for I say to you, you may not see Me from now on, until you may say, Blessed &#91;is&#93; He who is coming in the Name of the LORD.”</span>
