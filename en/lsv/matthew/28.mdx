<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 16:1–8; John 20:1–10; Matthew 27:56"> Now after &#91;the&#93; Sabbaths, it being dawn, toward the first &#91;day&#93; of the weeks, Mary the Magdalene came, and the other Mary, to see the grave,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 16:26; John 20:12–13; Mark 16:3–5"> and behold, there came a great earthquake, for a messenger of the LORD, having come down out of Heaven, having come, rolled away the stone from the door, and was sitting on it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 9:3; Acts 1:10; Mark 16:5"> and his countenance was as lightning, and his clothing white as snow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Daniel 10:7; Matthew 27:65–66"> and from the fear of him the keepers shook, and they became as dead men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; Hebrews 1:14; Isaiah 41:10"> And the messenger answering said to the women, “Do not fear, for I have known that you seek Jesus who has been crucified;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 16:6; Matthew 12:40; Luke 24:6–8"> He is not here, for He rose, as He said; come, see the place where the LORD was lying;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Luke 24:22–24; Matthew 28:10"> and having gone quickly, say to His disciples that He rose from the dead; and behold, He goes before you to Galilee, there you will see Him; behold, I have told you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 2:11; Mark 16:8; Luke 24:36–41"> And having gone forth quickly from the tomb, with fear and great joy, they ran to tell His disciples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 28:17; John 20:14–17; Matthew 14:33"> and as they were going to tell His disciples, then behold, Jesus met them, saying, “Greetings!” And having come near, they laid hold of His feet, and worshiped Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:29; John 20:17; Matthew 28:7"> Then Jesus says to them, “Do not fear, go away, tell My brothers that they may go away to Galilee, and there they will see Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 27:65–66; Matthew 28:4"> And while they are going on, behold, certain of the guard having come into the city, reported to the chief priests all the things that happened,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:3–4; Acts 4:5–22; Matthew 27:1–2"> and having been gathered together with the elders, having also taken counsel, they gave much money to the soldiers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:64"> saying, “Say that His disciples having come by night, stole Him—we being asleep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Acts 12:19"> and if this is heard by the governor, we will persuade him, and you keep free from anxiety.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 27:8; Matthew 26:15"> And they, having received the money, did as they were taught, and this account was spread abroad among Jews until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Matthew 28:10; Matthew 28:7"> And the eleven disciples went to Galilee, to the mountain where Jesus appointed them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 45:11; Psalms 2:12; John 5:23"> and having seen Him, they worshiped Him, but some wavered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:35; 1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 11:27"> And having come near, Jesus spoke to them, saying, “All authority in Heaven and on earth was given to Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 2:38–39; Acts 1:8"> having gone, then, disciple all the nations, immersing them into the Name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Joshua 1:5; Acts 18:9–10"> teaching them to observe all, whatever I commanded you, and behold, I am with you all the days—until the full end of the age.”</span>
