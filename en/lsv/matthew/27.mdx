<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 15:1; John 18:28; Micah 2:1"> And morning having come, all the chief priests and the elders of the people took counsel against Jesus, so as to put Him to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 20:19; Acts 3:13; 1 Timothy 6:13"> and having bound Him, they led &#91;Him&#93; away, and delivered Him up to Pontius Pilate, the governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; Luke 22:47–48; Job 20:15–29"> Then Judas—he who delivered Him up—having seen that He was condemned, having regretted, brought back the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and to the elders, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:30; Titus 1:16; Acts 18:15–17"> “I sinned, having delivered up innocent blood”; and they said, “What &#91;is that&#93; to us? You will see!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 1:18–19; 2 Samuel 17:23; 1 Kings 16:18"> And having cast down the pieces of silver in the temple, he departed, and having gone away, he strangled himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:7–9; Deuteronomy 23:18; Isaiah 61:8"> And the chief priests having taken the pieces of silver, said, “It is not lawful to put them into the treasury, seeing it is the price of blood”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and having taken counsel, they bought the potter’s field with them, for the burial of strangers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:19; Matthew 28:15; Joshua 4:9"> therefore that field was called, “Field of Blood,” to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 1:22; Leviticus 27:2–7"> Then was fulfilled that spoken through Jeremiah the prophet, saying, “And I took the thirty pieces of silver, the price of Him who has been priced, whom they of the sons of Israel priced,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 11:13"> and gave them for the potter’s field, as the LORD appointed to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 23:2–3; Mark 15:2–5; Matthew 26:25"> And Jesus stood before the governor, and the governor questioned Him, saying, “Are You the King of the Jews?” And Jesus said to him, “You say &#91;it&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:63; Isaiah 53:7; Matthew 27:14"> And in His being accused by the chief priests and the elders, He did not answer anything;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:62; John 18:35; Acts 22:24"> then Pilate says to Him, “Do You not hear how many things they witness against You?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; Psalms 71:7; 1 Corinthians 4:9"> And He did not answer him, not even to one word, so that the governor wondered greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:38–40; Luke 23:16; John 19:16"> And at the celebration the governor had been accustomed to release one to the multitude, a prisoner, whom they willed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 15:7; Acts 3:14; Luke 23:25"> and they had a noted prisoner then, called Barabbas,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 19:15; 1 Kings 18:21; Mark 15:9–12"> therefore they having been gathered together, Pilate said to them, “Whom do you will &#91;that&#93; I may release to you? Barabbas or Jesus who is called Christ?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Acts 5:17; Mark 15:10"> For he had known that they had delivered Him up because of envy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Proverbs 29:1; Matthew 27:24"> And as he is sitting on the judgment seat, his wife sent to him, saying, “Nothing—to you and to that Righteous One, for I suffered many things today in a dream because of Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:14–15; John 19:15–16; John 18:40"> And the chief priests and the elders persuaded the multitudes that they might ask for themselves Barabbas, and might destroy Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and the governor answering said to them, “Which of the two will you &#91;that&#93; I may release to you?” And they said, “Barabbas.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–3; Isaiah 49:7; Acts 13:38"> Pilate says to them, “What then will I do with Jesus who is called Christ?” They all say to him, “Let &#91;Him&#93; be crucified!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:18–19; Acts 7:57; Acts 23:12–15"> And the governor said, “Why, what evil did He do?” And they were crying out the more, saying, “Let &#91;Him&#93; be crucified!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:4; Psalms 26:6; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> And Pilate having seen that it profits nothing, but rather a tumult is made, having taken water, he washed the hands before the multitude, saying, “I am innocent from the blood of this Righteous One; you will see &#91;to it&#93; yourselves”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 2:19; Acts 5:28; Acts 7:52"> and all the people answering said, “His blood &#91;is&#93; on us, and on our children!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; John 19:1; Mark 15:15"> Then he released Barabbas to them, and having scourged Jesus, he delivered &#91;Him&#93; up that He may be crucified;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 18:28; John 18:33; Acts 10:1"> then the soldiers of the governor having taken Jesus to the Praetorium, gathered to Him all the band;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 15:17; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> and having unclothed Him, they put a crimson cloak around Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 20:19; Psalms 69:19–20"> and having plaited Him a garland out of thorns they put &#91;it&#93; on His head, and &#91;put&#93; a reed in His right hand, and having kneeled before Him, they were mocking Him, saying, “Hail, the King of the Jews!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 53:7"> And having spit on Him, they took the reed, and were striking on His head;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 Kings 21:13; 1 Kings 21:10"> and when they had mocked Him, they took off the cloak from Him, and put His own garments on Him, and led Him away to crucify &#91;Him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:21; Matthew 16:24; Luke 23:26"> And coming forth, they found a man, a Cyrenian, by name Simon: they impressed him that he might carry His cross;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 19:17–19; Mark 15:22–32; Mark 15:34"> and having come to a place called Golgotha, which is called “Place of &#91;the&#93; Skull,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Mark 15:23; Matthew 27:48"> they gave Him vinegar mixed with gall to drink, and having tasted, He would not drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 22:18; Mark 15:24–32; Luke 23:34"> And having crucified Him, they divided His garments, casting a lot, &#91;&#91;that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet: “They divided My garments to themselves, and they cast a lot over My clothing”;&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:44; Mark 15:39"> and sitting down, they were watching Him there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 23:38; John 19:19–22; Mark 15:26"> and they put up over His head, His accusation written: “THIS IS JESUS, THE KING OF THE JEWS.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43; Isaiah 53:12"> Then two robbers are crucified with Him, one on the right hand and one on the left,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 109:25; Lamentations 1:12; Psalms 69:7–12"> and those passing by kept slandering Him, wagging their heads,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 26:61; Matthew 4:6; Matthew 4:3"> and saying, “You that are throwing down the temple, and in three days building &#91;it&#93;, save Yourself; if You are the Son of God, come down from the cross.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Isaiah 28:22; Psalms 22:12–13"> And in like manner also the chief priests mocking, with the scribes and elders, said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 1:49; Matthew 27:37; Acts 4:14"> “He saved others; He is not able to save Himself! If He is King of Israel, let Him come down now from the cross, and we will believe Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 22:8; John 10:36; Isaiah 37:10"> He has trusted on God, let Him now deliver Him if He wants Him, because He said, I am &#91;the&#93; Son of God”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:38; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:39–43"> with the same also the robbers, who were crucified with Him, were reproaching Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Amos 8:9; Mark 15:33–34; Luke 23:44–45"> And from the sixth hour darkness came over all the land to the ninth hour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 53:10"> and about the ninth hour Jesus cried out with a great voice, saying, “Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani?” That is, “My God, My God, why did You forsake Me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Mark 15:35–36; Matthew 11:14"> And certain of those standing there having heard, said, “He calls Elijah”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36"> and immediately, one of them having run, and having taken a sponge, having filled &#91;it&#93; with vinegar, and having put &#91;it&#93; on a reed, was giving Him to drink,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> but the rest said, “Let alone, let us see if Elijah comes—about to save Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:30; Isaiah 53:9–12; Psalms 22:14–15"> And Jesus having again cried with a great voice, yielded the spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Mark 15:38; Hebrews 6:19"> and behold, the veil of the temple was torn in two from top to bottom, and the earth quaked, and the rocks were split,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; 1 Thessalonians 4:14; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> and the tombs were opened, and many bodies of the holy ones who have fallen asleep, arose,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Isaiah 48:2; Revelation 21:2"> and having come forth out of the tombs after His rising, they went into the holy city, and appeared to many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:36; Mark 15:39; Matthew 27:43"> And the centurion, and those with him watching Jesus, having seen the earthquake, and the things that were done, were exceedingly afraid, saying, “Truly this was God’s Son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Luke 23:48–49; John 19:25–27"> And there were there many women beholding from afar, who followed Jesus from Galilee, ministering to Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 19:25; Mark 15:47–1; Matthew 28:1"> among whom was Mary the Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James and of Joses, and the mother of the sons of Zebedee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Mark 15:42–47; Luke 23:50–56"> And evening having come, there came a rich man from Arimathea named Joseph, who also himself was discipled to Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 23:52–53; Mark 15:44–46"> he having gone near to Pilate, asked for himself the body of Jesus; then Pilate commanded the body to be given back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> And having taken the body, Joseph wrapped it in clean linen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 28:2; Matthew 27:66"> and laid it in his new tomb that he hewed in the rock, and having rolled a great stone to the door of the tomb, he went away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Matthew 28:1; Matthew 27:56"> now Mary the Magdalene was there, and the other Mary, sitting opposite the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 15:42; John 19:14; John 19:42"> And on the next day that is after the Preparation, the chief priests and the Pharisees were gathered together to Pilate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 9:22; Matthew 16:21; John 2:19"> saying, “Lord, we have remembered that this deceiver said while yet living, After three days I rise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Matthew 28:13; Matthew 12:45"> command, then, the grave to be made secure until the third day, lest His disciples, having come by night, may steal Him away, and may say to the people, He rose from the dead, and the last deceit will be worse than the first.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Matthew 27:66; Matthew 28:11–15; Proverbs 21:30"> And Pilate said to them, “You have a guard, go away, make &#91;it&#93; secure—as you have known”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Daniel 6:17; Matthew 27:60; 2 Timothy 2:19"> and they, having gone, made the grave secure, having sealed the stone, together with the guard.</span>
