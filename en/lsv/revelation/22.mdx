<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Colossians 2:18–19; Revelation 14:7"> and he says to me, “Behold—No! For I am your fellow servant, and of your brothers the prophets, and of those keeping the words of this scroll; worship God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 8:26; Revelation 1:3; 1 Peter 4:7"> And He says to me, “You may not seal the words of the prophecy of this scroll, because the time is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:10; Proverbs 4:18; Ezekiel 3:27"> The &#91;one&#93; being unrighteous—let him be unrighteous still; and the filthy—let him be filthy still; and the righteous—let him do righteousness still; and the holy—let him be holy still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Isaiah 62:11; Isaiah 40:10"> Behold, I come quickly, and My reward &#91;is&#93; with Me, to render to each as his work will be;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 21:6; Isaiah 44:6"> I am the Alpha and the Omega—the Beginning and End—the First and the Last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; 1 John 5:3; Revelation 22:7"> Blessed are those washing their robes that the authority will be theirs to the Tree of Life, and they may enter into the city by the gates;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; Galatians 5:19–21; Philippians 3:2"> and outside &#91;are&#93; the dogs, and the sorcerers, and the whoremongers, and the murderers, and the idolaters, and everyone cherishing and doing falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; 2 Peter 1:19; Isaiah 11:1"> I, Jesus, sent My messenger to testify to you these things concerning the assemblies; I am the root and the offspring of David, the radiant morning star!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:14; Revelation 21:6; Isaiah 55:1–3"> And the Spirit and the bride say, Come! And he who is hearing—let him say, Come! And he who is thirsting—let him come; and he who is willing—let him take the water of life freely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:2; Proverbs 30:6"> For I testify to everyone hearing the words of the prophecy of this scroll, if anyone may add to these, God will add to him the plagues that have been written in this scroll,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:2; Luke 11:52; Revelation 22:2"> and if anyone may take away from the words of the scroll of this prophecy, God will take away his part from the Scroll of Life, and out of the holy city, and the things that have been written in this scroll.”</span>
