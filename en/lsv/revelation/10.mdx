<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Matthew 17:2; Revelation 1:15–16"> And I saw another strong messenger coming down out of Heaven, clothed with a cloud, and a rainbow on the head, and his face as the sun, and his feet as pillars of fire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:9–10; Revelation 10:5; Revelation 5:1–5"> and he had in his hand a little scroll opened, and he placed his right foot on the sea, and the left on the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 31:4; Revelation 4:5; Joel 3:16"> and he cried with a great voice, as a lion roars, and when he cried, the seven thunders spoke out their voices;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 12:4; Daniel 8:26; Daniel 12:9"> and when the seven thunders spoke their voices, I was about to write, and I heard a voice out of Heaven saying to me, “Seal the things that the seven thunders spoke,” and, “You may not write these things.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Deuteronomy 32:40; Revelation 16:17"> And the messenger whom I saw standing on the sea, and on the land, lifted up his hand to the sky,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 4:11; Revelation 4:9; Revelation 16:17"> and swore by Him who lives through the ages of the ages, who created the sky and the things in it, and the land and the things in it, and the sea and the things in it—that time will not be yet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 3:3–9; Romans 16:25; Luke 24:44–47"> but in the days of the voice of the seventh messenger, when he may be about to sound the trumpet, and the secret of God may be accomplished, as He declared to His own servants, to the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 10:2; Revelation 10:4–5; Isaiah 30:21"> And the voice that I heard out of Heaven is again speaking with me, and saying, “Go, take the little scroll that is open in the hand of the messenger who has been standing on the sea, and on the land”:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:1–3; Jeremiah 15:16; Ezekiel 2:8"> and I went away to the messenger, saying to him, “Give me the little scroll”; and he says to me, “Take, and eat it up, and it will make your belly bitter, but in your mouth it will be sweet as honey.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:3; Proverbs 16:24; Psalms 119:103"> And I took the little scroll out of the hand of the messenger, and ate it up, and it was in my mouth sweet as honey, and when I ate it my belly was made bitter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:4; Jeremiah 1:9–10; Revelation 17:15"> and he says to me, “You must again prophesy about many peoples, and nations, and tongues, and kings.”</span>
