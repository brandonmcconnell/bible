<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:6; James 2:26; Revelation 1:4"> “And to the messenger of the assembly in Sardis write: These things says He who is having the Seven Spirits of God, and the seven stars: I have known your works, and that you have the name that you live, and you are dead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; 2 Timothy 4:1–4; Revelation 16:15"> be watching, and strengthen the rest of the things that are about to die, for I have not found your works fulfilled before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 2:5; 2 Peter 3:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:2"> Remember, then, what you have received and heard, and keep &#91;it&#93; and convert: if, then, you may not watch, I will come on you as a thief, and you may not know what hour I will come on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 3:5; Revelation 7:9; Ecclesiastes 9:8"> You have a few names even in Sardis who did not defile their garments, and they will walk with Me in white, because they are worthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 10:32; Luke 12:8; Revelation 21:27"> The &#91;one&#93; thus overcoming will be clothed in white garments, and I will not blot out his name from the Scroll of Life, and I will confess his name before My Father, and before His messengers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 2:7"> He who is having an ear—let him hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 22:22; Matthew 16:19; Revelation 1:18"> And to the messenger of the assembly in Philadelphia write: These things says the Holy &#91;One&#93;, the True &#91;One&#93;, having the key of David, the &#91;One&#93; opening and no one will shut, and shutting and no one opens:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:9; Colossians 4:3; Revelation 3:7"> I have known your works; behold, I have set before you a door having been opened, which no one is able to shut it, because you have little power, and yet have kept My word, and have not denied My Name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:14; Revelation 2:9; Isaiah 49:23"> behold, I give from the synagogue of Satan—those saying themselves to be Jews, and are not, but lie—behold, I will make them that they may come and prostrate before your feet, and may know that I loved you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 2:9; Revelation 14:12; Zechariah 13:9"> Because you kept the word of My endurance, I also will keep you from the hour of the trial that is about to come on all the world, to try those dwelling on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 1:12; Revelation 22:12; Revelation 2:10"> Behold, I come quickly, be holding fast that which you have, that no one may receive your garland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 22:4; Revelation 21:2; Revelation 14:1"> He who is overcoming—I will make him a pillar in the temple of My God, and he may not go outside anymore, and I will write on him the Name of My God, and the name of the city of My God, the New Jerusalem, that comes down out of Heaven from My God—also My new Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 2:7"> He who is having an ear—let him hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; Revelation 1:5; 2 Corinthians 1:20"> And to the messenger of the assembly of the Laodiceans write: These things says the Amen, the Witness—the Faithful and True—the Chief of the creation of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:11; Matthew 6:24; 1 Kings 18:21"> I have known your works, that you are neither cold nor hot; I wish you were cold or hot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 2:5; Zechariah 11:8–9; Jeremiah 14:19"> So—because you are lukewarm, and neither cold nor hot, I am about to vomit you out of My mouth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 13:7; Hosea 12:8; Romans 7:24"> because you say—I am rich, and have grown rich, and have need of nothing, and have not known that you are the wretched, and miserable, and poor, and blind, and naked;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; 1 Peter 1:7; Isaiah 55:1"> I counsel you to buy from Me gold fired by fire, that you may be rich, and white garments that you may be clothed, and the shame of your nakedness may not be revealed, and with eye-salve anoint your eyes, that you may see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 3:11–12; 1 Corinthians 11:32; Hebrews 12:5–11"> As many as I cherish, I convict and discipline; be zealous, then, and convert;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 12:36–37; Revelation 19:9; John 14:21–23"> behold, I have stood at the door, and I knock; if anyone may hear My voice, and may open the door, I will come in to him, and will dine with him, and he with Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:12; Matthew 19:28; Revelation 2:26–27"> He who is overcoming—I will give to him to sit with Me in My throne, as I also overcame and sat down with My Father in His throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 2:17"> He who is having an ear—let him hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies.”</span>
