<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 5:12–14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Hebrews 9:14"> For this reason, having left the word of the beginning of the Christ, we may advance to perfection, not laying again a foundation of conversion from dead works, and of faith on God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:13–57; Acts 17:31–32; Romans 6:3–5"> of the teaching of immersions, also of laying on of hands, also of &#91;the&#93; resurrection of the dead, and of continuous judgment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:21; 1 Corinthians 16:7; 1 Corinthians 4:19"> and this we will do, if God may permit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–29; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Matthew 5:13"> for &#91;it is&#93; impossible for those once enlightened, having also tasted of the heavenly gift, and having become partakers of the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:3; Luke 8:13; Matthew 13:20–21"> and tasted the good saying of God, also the powers of the coming age,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 2:21; Hebrews 10:29; Hebrews 6:4"> and having fallen away, to renew &#91;them&#93; again to conversion, having crucified to themselves the Son of God again, and exposed to public shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Isaiah 44:3; Psalms 65:9–13"> For the earth, having drunk in the rain coming on it many times, and is bringing forth herbs fit for those because of whom it is also dressed, partakes of blessing from God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; John 15:6; Luke 13:7–9"> but that which is bearing thorns and briers &#91;is&#93; disapproved of, and near to cursing, whose end &#91;is&#93; for burning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:39; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Hebrews 6:4–6"> but we are persuaded, concerning you, beloved, the things that are better, and accompanying salvation, though even thus we speak,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; Hebrews 13:16; Galatians 6:10"> for God is not unrighteous to forget your work, and the labor of love that you showed to His Name, having ministered to the holy ones and ministering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–8; Hebrews 3:6; Romans 5:2–5"> and we desire each one of you to show the same diligence, to the full assurance of the hope to the end,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 10:36; 1 John 2:25"> that you may not become slothful, but followers of those who through faith and patient endurance are inheriting the promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 22:15–18; Luke 1:73; Jeremiah 22:5"> For God, having made promise to Abraham, seeing He was not able to swear by &#91;any&#93; greater, swore by Himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Deuteronomy 1:10; Nehemiah 9:23"> saying, “Blessing I will indeed bless you, and multiplying I will multiply you”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 12:2–3; Romans 4:17–25; Exodus 1:7"> and so, having patiently endured, he obtained the promise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:11; Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 31:53"> for men swear by the greater, and the oath &#91;is&#93; for confirmation of the end of all their controversy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 6:18; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 55:11"> in which God, more abundantly willing to show to the heirs of the promise the immutability of His counsel, interposed by an oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Titus 1:2; 1 Samuel 15:29"> that through two immutable things, in which &#91;it is&#93; impossible for God to lie, we may have a strong comfort, having fled for refuge, to lay hold on the hope being set before &#91;us&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 43:5; Romans 5:5–10; Hebrews 9:7"> which we have, as an anchor of the soul, both sure and steadfast, and entering into that within the veil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14; Hebrews 3:1; Hebrews 5:6"> to where a forerunner entered for us—Jesus, having become Chief Priest throughout the age after the order of Melchizedek.</span>
