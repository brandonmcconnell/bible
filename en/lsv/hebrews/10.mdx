<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Hebrews 9:11; Colossians 2:17"> For the Law having a shadow of the good things coming—not the very image of the matters, every year, by the same sacrifices that they offer continually, is never able to make perfect those coming near,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Micah 7:19; Psalms 103:12"> since, would they not have ceased to be offered, because of those serving having no more conscience of sins, having been purified once?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 9:7; Exodus 30:10; Leviticus 23:27–28"> But in those &#91;sacrifices&#93; is a remembrance of sins every year,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:11; Isaiah 1:11–15; Micah 6:6–8"> for it is impossible for blood of bulls and goats to take away sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:6–8; Isaiah 1:11; Isaiah 7:14"> For this reason, coming into the world, He says, “Sacrifice and offering You did not will, and a body You prepared for Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 1:10; Matthew 3:17; Psalms 147:11"> in burnt-offerings, and concerning sin-offerings, You did not delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 40:7–8; John 4:34; John 6:38"> Then I said, Behold, I come (in a volume of the scroll it has been written concerning Me), to do, O God, Your will”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5; Mark 12:33"> saying above, “Sacrifice, and offering, and burnt-offerings, and concerning sin-offering You did not will, nor delight in” (which are offered according to the Law),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 7:18–19; Hebrews 12:27–28; Hebrews 10:7"> then He said, “Behold, I come to do, O God, Your will”; He takes away the first that He may establish the second;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:14; Hebrews 10:12; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> in which will, we have been sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 5:1; Numbers 28:3; Hebrews 10:4"> and every priest, indeed, has daily stood serving, and offering the same sacrifices many times, that are never able to take away sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Colossians 3:1; Romans 8:34"> But He, having offered one sacrifice for sin—to the end, sat down at the right hand of God—</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:13; Psalms 110:1; Luke 20:43"> as to the rest, expecting until He may place His enemies &#91;as&#93; His footstool,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:1; Hebrews 9:14"> for by one offering He has perfected to the end those being sanctified;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 3:6"> and the Holy Spirit also testifies to us, for after that He has said before,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33–34; Hebrews 8:8–12; Romans 11:27"> “This &#91;is&#93; the covenant that I will make with them after those days, says the LORD, giving My laws on their hearts, and I will write them on their minds,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Hebrews 8:12"> and, “I will remember their sins and their lawlessness no more”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 10:2; Hebrews 10:14"> and where &#91;there is&#93; forgiveness of these, there is no longer offering for sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 4:16"> Having, therefore, brothers, boldness for the entrance into the holy places, by the blood of Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 14:6; John 10:9; Hebrews 9:3"> which &#91;is&#93; the way He initiated for us—new and living, through the veil, that is, His flesh—</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 4:14–16; Hebrews 8:1"> and a Great Priest over the house of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25; John 3:5; Ephesians 5:26"> may we draw near with a true heart, in full assurance of faith, having the hearts sprinkled from an evil conscience, and having the body bathed with pure water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:24; 1 Corinthians 1:9; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> may we hold fast the unwavering profession of the hope (for He who promised &#91;is&#93; faithful),</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; 1 John 3:18; Hebrews 13:1"> and may we consider to provoke one another to love and to good works,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 2:42; 1 Thessalonians 5:11; Matthew 18:20"> not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as &#91;is&#93; a custom of some, but exhorting, and so much the more as you see the Day coming near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 2:20–22; Hebrews 6:4–6; John 9:41"> For &#91;if&#93; we are sinning willingly after receiving the full knowledge of the truth—there remains no more sacrifice for sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Hebrews 2:3; Zephaniah 1:18"> but a certain fearful looking for of judgment, and fiery zeal, about to devour the opposers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:2; Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:2–13"> anyone having set aside a law of Moses dies without mercies on the basis of two or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 6:6; Ephesians 4:30; Isaiah 63:10"> Of how much worse punishment will he be counted worthy who trampled on the Son of God, and counted the blood of the covenant a common thing, by which he was sanctified, and having insulted the Spirit of grace?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35–36; Romans 12:19; Nahum 1:2"> For we have known Him who is saying, “Vengeance &#91;is&#93; Mine, I will repay, says the LORD”; and again, “The LORD will judge His people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; Luke 12:5; Hebrews 12:29"> &#91;It is&#93; fearful to fall into &#91;the&#93; hands of &#91;the&#93; living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 6:4; Hebrews 12:4; 2 Timothy 2:3–13"> But call to your remembrance the former days, in which, having been enlightened, you endured much conflict of sufferings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:9; Philippians 4:14; Hebrews 11:36"> this indeed, being made spectacles with both insults and afflictions, now this, having become partners of those so living,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:16; Matthew 5:11–12"> for you also sympathized with my bonds, and the robbery of your goods you received with joy, knowing that you have in yourselves a better substance in the heavens, and an enduring one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Galatians 6:8–10; Hebrews 3:6"> You may not cast away, then, your boldness, which has great repayment of reward,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 12:1; Psalms 37:7; James 5:7–11"> for you have need of patience, that having done the will of God, you may receive the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="James 5:7–9; Habakkuk 2:3–4; Luke 18:8"> “For yet &#91;in&#93; a very, very little &#91;while&#93;, He who is coming will come, and will not linger,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Galatians 3:11; Habakkuk 2:4; Romans 1:17"> but, “The righteous will live by faith; and if he may draw back, My soul has no pleasure in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 John 5:5; 1 Peter 1:5; Mark 16:16"> But we are not of those drawing back to destruction, but of those believing to a preserving of soul.</span>
