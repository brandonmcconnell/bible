<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Philippians 3:14; Hebrews 4:14–15"> For this reason, holy brothers, partakers of a heavenly calling, consider the Apostle and Chief Priest of our profession, Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 12:7; 1 Timothy 3:15; Ephesians 2:22"> being faithful to Him who appointed Him, as also Moses &#91;was&#93; in all His house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9; 1 Peter 2:5–7; Zechariah 6:12–13"> For this One has been counted worthy of more glory than Moses, inasmuch as He who builds it has more honor than the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 3:3; Hebrews 1:2; Esther 2:10"> For every house is built by someone, and He who built all things &#91;is&#93; God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 12:7; Hebrews 3:2; Exodus 14:31"> and Moses &#91;was&#93; indeed steadfast in all His house, as an attendant, for a testimony of those things that were to be spoken—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Hebrews 10:35"> but Christ, as a Son over His house, whose house we are, if we hold fast the boldness and the rejoicing of the hope to the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:7–11; Hebrews 3:15; Isaiah 55:3"> For this reason, as the Holy Spirit says, “Today, if you may hear His voice—</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; Exodus 17:7; Zechariah 7:11–12"> you may not harden your hearts, as in the provocation, in the day of the temptation in the wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Deuteronomy 4:9; Exodus 19:4"> in which your fathers tempted Me; they proved Me, and saw My works &#91;for&#93; forty years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 95:10; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Psalms 67:2"> for this reason I was grieved with that generation and said, They always go astray in &#91;their&#93; heart, and these have not known My ways;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:34–35; Hebrews 4:3; Numbers 32:10–13"> so I swore in My anger, They will &#91;not&#93; enter into My rest.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 17:9"> Watch out, brothers, lest there will be in any of you an evil heart of unbelief in the falling away from the living God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Hebrews 10:24–25; 2 Timothy 4:2"> but exhort one another every day, while &#91;it&#93; is called “Today,” that none of you may be hardened by the deceitfulness of sin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 3:6; Hebrews 6:11; Hebrews 12:10"> for we have become partakers of the Christ, if we may hold fast the confidence &#91;we had&#93; at the beginning to the end,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7–8; Psalms 95:7; Hebrews 10:38"> as it is said, “Today, if you may hear His voice, you may not harden your hearts, as in the provocation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 14:30; Numbers 26:65"> For who &#91;were those&#93;, having heard, &#91;that&#93; provoked, but not all those having come out of Egypt through Moses?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:29; Numbers 26:64–65; Numbers 14:32–33"> But with whom was He grieved forty years? Was it not with those who sinned, whose carcasses fell in the wilderness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Hebrews 4:6; Deuteronomy 1:34–35"> And to whom did He swear that they will not enter into His rest, except to those who did not believe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jude 1:5; John 3:36; Mark 16:16"> And we see that they were not able to enter in because of unbelief.</span>
