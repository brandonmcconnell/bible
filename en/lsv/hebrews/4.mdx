<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 12:15; Hebrews 4:9; Proverbs 28:14"> We may fear, then, lest a promise being left of entering into His rest, anyone of you may seem to have come short,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; Romans 10:16–17; Hebrews 11:6"> for we also are having good news proclaimed, even as they, but the word heard did not profit them, not being mixed with faith in those who heard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Matthew 11:28–29; 1 Peter 1:20"> for we enter into the rest—we who believed, as He said, “So I swore in My anger, They will &#91;not&#93; enter into My rest”; and yet the works were done from the foundation of the world,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Exodus 31:17; Genesis 2:1–2"> for He spoke in a certain place concerning the seventh &#91;day&#93; thus: “And God rested in the seventh day from all His works”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Hebrews 4:3; Hebrews 3:11"> and in this &#91;place&#93; again, “They will &#91;not&#93; enter into My rest”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Hebrews 3:18–19; Acts 13:46–47"> since then, it remains for some to enter into it, and those who first heard good news did not enter in because of unbelief—</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:7; Hebrews 3:7–8; Hebrews 3:15"> again He limits a certain day, “Today,” in David saying, after so long a time, as it has been said, “Today, if you may hear His voice, you may not harden your hearts,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:15; Joshua 22:4; Deuteronomy 12:9"> for if Joshua had given them rest, He would not have spoken after these things concerning another day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10; Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:14–17"> there remains, then, a Sabbath rest to the people of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Hebrews 4:3–4; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> for he who entered into His rest, he also rested from his works, as God from His own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:28–30; Luke 13:24; Hebrews 3:18–1"> May we be diligent, then, to enter into that rest, that no one may fall in the same example of the unbelief,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:29; Isaiah 55:11; Ephesians 6:17"> for the Word of God is living, and working, and sharper—beyond every two-edged sword—and piercing as far as &#91;the&#93; division of soul and spirit, of joints and also marrows, and a discerner of thoughts and intents of the heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:5; Psalms 33:13–15; Proverbs 15:3"> and there is not a created thing hidden before Him, but all things &#91;are&#93; naked and open to His eyes—with whom is our reckoning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 9:12"> Having, then, a great Chief Priest having passed through the heavens—Jesus the Son of God—may we hold fast the profession,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17–18; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Isaiah 53:4–5"> for we do not have a Chief Priest unable to sympathize with our weaknesses, but &#91;One&#93; tempted in all things in like manner, &#91;yet&#93; without sin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:19–23"> we may come near, then, with freedom, to the throne of grace, that we may receive kindness, and find grace—for seasonable help.</span>
