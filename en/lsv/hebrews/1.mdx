<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 1:20–21; Joel 2:28; Numbers 12:6–8"> In many parts and many ways, God, having spoken long ago to the fathers by the prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Colossians 1:16–18"> in these last days speaks to us in &#91;His&#93; Son, whom He appointed heir of all things, through whom He also made the ages;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; Revelation 4:11; John 14:9–10"> who being the brightness of the glory, and the impress of His subsistence, bearing up also all things by the saying of His might—having made a cleansing of our sins through Himself, sat down at the right hand of the Greatness in the highest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:21; Philippians 2:9–11; 1 Peter 3:22"> having become so much better than the messengers, as He inherited a more excellent name than them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; Acts 13:33; Hebrews 5:5"> For to which of the messengers did He ever say, “You are My Son—today I have begotten You?” And again, “I will be to Him for a Father, and He will be to Me for a Son?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:7; Colossians 1:18; Deuteronomy 32:43"> And when again He may bring the firstborn into the world, He says, “And let them worship Him—all messengers of God”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 104:4; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 6:17"> and to the messengers, indeed, He says, “The &#91;One&#93; who is making His messengers spirits, and His ministers a flame of fire”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 45:6–7; Isaiah 9:6–7; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> but to the Son: “Your throne, O God, &#91;is&#93; throughout the age of the age; The scepter of righteousness &#91;is the&#93; scepter of Your kingdom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 45:7; Psalms 23:5; Isaiah 61:3"> You loved righteousness, and hated lawlessness; Because of this He anointed You—God, Your God—With oil of gladness above Your partners”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 102:25–27; Genesis 1:1; John 1:1–3"> and, “You, LORD, founded the earth at the beginning, &#124;&#124; And the heavens are a work of Your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Mark 13:31; Luke 21:33"> These will perish, but You remain, &#124;&#124; And all will become old as a garment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 102:26–27; Hebrews 13:8; John 8:58"> And You will roll them together as a mantle, and they will be changed, &#124;&#124; But You are the same, and Your years will not fail.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Mark 12:36; Matthew 22:44"> And to which of the messengers did He ever say, “Sit at My right hand, &#124;&#124; Until I may make Your enemies Your footstool?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 34:7; Psalms 103:20–21; Psalms 91:11–12"> Are they not all spirits of service—being sent forth for ministry because of those about to inherit salvation?</span>
