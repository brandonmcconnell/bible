<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 8:3; Hebrews 10:11; Hebrews 7:27"> For every chief priest taken out of men is set in things &#91;pertaining&#93; to God in behalf of men, that he may offer both gifts and sacrifices for sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 2:18; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> being able to be gentle to those being ignorant and going astray, since he is also surrounded with weakness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 9:7; Hebrews 9:7; Leviticus 16:6"> and because of this &#91;weakness&#93; he ought, just as for the people, so also for himself, to bring forward &#91;sacrifices&#93; for sins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 28:1; Numbers 16:40; Numbers 16:5"> and no one takes the honor to himself, but he who is called by God, as also Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; John 8:54; Acts 13:33"> So also the Christ did not glorify Himself to become Chief Priest, but He who spoke to Him: “You are My Son, today I have begotten You”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 7:21"> just as He also says in another &#91;place&#93;, “You &#91;are&#93; a priest throughout the age, according to the order of Melchizedek”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:32–39; Luke 22:41–44; John 17:1"> who in the days of His flesh having offered up both prayers and supplications with strong crying and tears to Him who was able to save Him from death, and having been heard in respect to that which He feared,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 2:8; Hebrews 10:5–9; John 4:34"> though being a Son, &#91;He&#93; learned obedience by the things which He suffered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:15"> and having been made perfect, He became the cause of continuous salvation to all those obeying Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 5:5–6; Hebrews 6:20"> having been called by God a Chief Priest according to the order of Melchizedek,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Acts 28:27; 2 Peter 3:16"> concerning the Word, of whom we have much &#91;to speak&#93;, and of hard explanation to say, since you have become dull of hearing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 6:1; 1 Corinthians 3:1–3; 1 Peter 2:2"> for even owing to be teachers, because of the time, again you have need that one teach you what &#91;are&#93; the elements of the beginning of the oracles of God, and you have become having need of milk, and not of strong food,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Peter 2:2; 2 Timothy 3:16"> for everyone who is partaking of milk &#91;is&#93; unskilled in the word of righteousness—for he is an infant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14–15; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; Ephesians 1:18"> and the strong food is of perfect men, who because of the use are having the senses exercised, to both the discernment of good and of evil.</span>
