<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 4:20–1; 1 Peter 3:8; 1 Peter 1:22"> Let brotherly love remain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 25:35; Genesis 18:1–3; 1 Peter 4:9"> Do not be forgetful of hospitality, for through this some entertained messengers unaware.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 25:36; Hebrews 10:34; Romans 12:15"> Be mindful of those in bonds, as having been bound with them, of those maltreated, as yourselves also being in the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Proverbs 5:15–23; Galatians 5:19"> The marriage &#91;is to be&#93; honored by all, and the bed undefiled, for God will judge whoremongers and adulterers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:8; Joshua 1:5; Deuteronomy 31:6"> &#91;Be&#93; without covetous behavior, being content with the things present, for He has said, “No, I will not leave, no, nor forsake you,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 118:6–9; Isaiah 41:10; Romans 8:31"> so that we boldly say, “The LORD &#91;is&#93; to me a helper, and I will not fear what man will do to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Hebrews 13:17; Philippians 3:17"> Be mindful of those leading you, who spoke to you the word of God, who, considering the outcome of &#91;their&#93; behavior, imitate &#91;their&#93; faith:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Malachi 3:6; James 1:17; Revelation 1:8"> Jesus Christ—the same yesterday and today and for all ages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:14; 1 Timothy 6:3–5; Ephesians 5:6"> Do not be carried away with strange and manifold teachings, for &#91;it is&#93; good that by grace the heart is confirmed, not with meats, in which they who were occupied were not profited;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:7–8; 1 Corinthians 10:20; 1 Corinthians 9:13"> we have an altar from which they who are serving the Dwelling Place have no authority to eat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 16:27; Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 9:11"> for of those beasts whose blood is brought for sin into the holy places through the chief priest—of these the bodies are burned outside the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12–14; John 19:17–18; Acts 7:58"> For this reason, also Jesus—that He might sanctify the people through &#91;His&#93; own blood—suffered outside the gate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 11:26; 1 Peter 4:14–16; Acts 5:41"> now then, may we go forth to Him outside the camp, bearing His reproach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; Colossians 3:1–3; Hebrews 11:12–16"> for we have no abiding city here, but we seek the coming one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 107:21–22; Psalms 50:14; Romans 12:1"> Through Him, then, we may always offer up a sacrifice of praise to God, that is, the fruit of lips, giving thanks to His Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:13; Galatians 6:10; Romans 12:13"> And do not be forgetful of doing good and of fellowship, for God is well-pleased with such sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; 1 Timothy 5:17; Hebrews 13:7"> Be obedient to those leading you, and be subject, for these watch for your souls, as about to give account, that they may do this with joy, and not sighing, for this &#91;is&#93; unprofitable to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:25; 2 Thessalonians 3:1; Colossians 4:3"> Pray for us, for we trust that we have a good conscience, willing to behave well in all things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philemon 1:22; Romans 1:10–12; Romans 15:31–32"> and I call on &#91;you&#93; to do this more abundantly, that I may be restored to you more quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:11; Hebrews 10:22; Psalms 23:1"> And the God of peace, who brought up the Great Shepherd of the sheep out of the dead—by the blood of a perpetual covenant—our Lord Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; 2 Thessalonians 2:17; Philippians 4:13"> make you perfect in every good work to do His will, doing in you that which is well-pleasing before Him, through Jesus Christ, to whom &#91;is&#93; the glory through the ages of the ages! Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:1; 1 Peter 5:12; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> And I beg you, brothers, endure the word of the exhortation, for I have also written to you through few words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philemon 1:1; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; 2 Timothy 1:8"> Know that the brother Timotheus is released, with whom I will see you, if he may come more shortly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 13:17; Acts 18:2"> Greet all those leading you, and all the holy ones. Those from Italy greet you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Colossians 4:18; 2 Timothy 4:22; Titus 3:15"> The grace &#91;is&#93; with you all! Amen.</span>
