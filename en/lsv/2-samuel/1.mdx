<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:12; 2 Samuel 4:10; Joshua 7:6"> and it comes to pass, on the third day, that behold, a man has come in out of the camp from Saul, and his garments &#91;are&#93; torn, and earth &#91;is&#93; on his head; and it comes to pass in his coming to David, that he falls to the earth and pays respect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 1:15–19; 2 Kings 5:25"> And David says to him, “Where do you come from?” And he says to him, “I have escaped out of the camp of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:16; 1 Samuel 31:1–6; 1 Chronicles 10:1–6"> And David says to him, “What has been the matter? Please declare &#91;it&#93; to me.” And he says, “That the people have fled from the battle, and also a multitude of the people have fallen, and they die; and also Saul and his son Jonathan have died.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 25:2; Proverbs 14:15"> And David says to the youth who is declaring &#91;it&#93; to him, “How have you known that Saul and his son Jonathan &#91;are&#93; dead?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:4; 2 Samuel 1:21; 1 Chronicles 10:4–6"> And the youth who is declaring &#91;it&#93; to him says, “I happened to meet in Mount Gilboa, and behold, Saul is leaning on his spear; and behold, the chariots and those possessing horses have followed him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:12; 2 Samuel 9:6; Judges 9:54"> and he turns behind him, and sees me, and calls to me, and I say, Here I &#91;am&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:17; 1 Samuel 30:13; 1 Samuel 30:1"> And he says to me, Who &#91;are&#93; you? And I say to him, I &#91;am&#93; an Amalekite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And he says to me, Please stand over me and put me to death, for the arrow has seized me, for all my soul &#91;is&#93; still in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Judges 9:54; 2 Kings 11:12"> And I stand over him, and put him to death, for I knew that he does not live after his falling, and I take the crown which &#91;is&#93; on his head, and the bracelet which &#91;is&#93; on his arm, and bring them to my lord here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:31; Genesis 37:29; 2 Samuel 3:31"> And David takes hold on his garments, and tears them, and also all the men who &#91;are&#93; with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17; Psalms 35:13–14; Matthew 5:44"> and they mourn, and weep, and fast until the evening, for Saul, and for his son Jonathan, and for the people of YHWH, and for the house of Israel, because they have fallen by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:8"> And David says to the youth who is declaring &#91;it&#93; to him, “Where &#91;are&#93; you from?” And he says, “I &#91;am&#93; the son of a sojourner, an Amalekite.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:9; 1 Samuel 31:4; 2 Peter 2:10"> And David says to him, “How were you not afraid to put forth your hand to destroy the anointed of YHWH?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 2:34; Judges 8:20; 2 Samuel 4:10–12"> And David calls to one of the youths and says, “Draw near—fall on him”; and he strikes him, and he dies;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Luke 19:22; Leviticus 20:9"> and David says to him, “Your blood &#91;is&#93; on your own head, for your mouth has testified against you, saying, I put to death the anointed of YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:25; 2 Samuel 1:19; Genesis 50:11"> And David laments with this lamentation over Saul, and over his son Jonathan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 10:13; Genesis 49:8; Deuteronomy 4:10"> and he says to teach the sons of Judah “The Bow”; behold, it is written on the Scroll of the Upright:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:27; 2 Samuel 1:25; Deuteronomy 4:7–8"> “The beauty of Israel &#124;&#124; &#91;Is&#93; wounded on your high places; How the mighty have fallen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 1:10; 1 Samuel 18:6; Judges 11:34"> Do not declare &#91;it&#93; in Gath, &#124;&#124; Do not proclaim the tidings in the streets of Ashkelon, &#124;&#124; Lest they rejoice—The daughters of the Philistines, &#124;&#124; Lest they exult—The daughters of the uncircumcised!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:1; Isaiah 21:5; 1 Samuel 10:1"> Mountains of Gilboa! No dew nor rain be on you, &#124;&#124; And fields of raised-offerings! For there has become loathsome &#124;&#124; The shield of the mighty, &#124;&#124; The shield of Saul—without the anointed with oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:4; Isaiah 34:6–7; 1 Samuel 14:6–14"> From the blood of the wounded, &#124;&#124; From the fat of the mighty, &#124;&#124; The bow of Jonathan &#124;&#124; Has not turned backward; And the sword of Saul does not return empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Judges 14:18; Lamentations 4:19"> Saul and Jonathan! They are loved and pleasant in their lives, &#124;&#124; And in their death they have not been parted. They have been lighter than eagles, &#124;&#124; They have been mightier than lions!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Jeremiah 2:32; Judges 5:30"> Daughters of Israel! Weep for Saul, &#124;&#124; Who is clothing you &#91;in&#93; scarlet with delights. Who is lifting up ornaments of gold on your clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:19; 2 Samuel 1:27; Judges 5:18"> How the mighty have fallen &#124;&#124; In the midst of the battle! Jonathan &#91;was&#93; wounded on your high places!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:17; 1 Samuel 23:16; 1 Samuel 20:41"> I am in distress for you, my brother Jonathan, &#124;&#124; You were very pleasant to me; Your love was wonderful to me, &#124;&#124; Above the love of women!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:25; 2 Samuel 1:19; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> How the mighty have fallen, &#124;&#124; Indeed, the weapons of war perish!”</span>
