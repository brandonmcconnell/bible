<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:2; 1 Chronicles 11:11–47; 1 Chronicles 27:32"> These &#91;are&#93; the names of the mighty ones whom David has: sitting in the seat &#91;is&#93; the Tachmonite, head of the captains—he &#91;is&#93; Adino, who hardened himself against eight hundred—wounded at one time.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 21:31; 2 Samuel 23:10; Psalms 3:8"> and he stations himself in the midst of the portion, and delivers it, and strikes the Philistines, and YHWH works a great salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1; 2 Samuel 5:18; Joshua 12:15"> And three of the thirty heads go down and come to the harvest, to David, to the cave of Adullam, and the company of the Philistines are encamping in the Valley of Rephaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:4–5; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Samuel 10:5"> and David &#91;is&#93; then in a fortress, and the station of the Philistines &#91;is&#93; then in Beth-Lehem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:14; Psalms 42:1–2; Isaiah 41:17"> and David longs and says, “Who gives me a drink of the water of the well of Beth-Lehem, which &#91;is&#93; by the gate?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 35:14; 2 Corinthians 5:14; Philippians 2:17"> And the three mighty ones cleave through the camp of the Philistines, and draw water out of the well of Beth-Lehem, which &#91;is&#93; by the gate, and take &#91;it&#93; up, and bring &#91;it&#93; to David; and he was not willing to drink it, and pours it out to YHWH,</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:51; 1 Chronicles 11:23; Colossians 2:15"> And he has struck the Egyptian man, a man of appearance, and a spear &#91;is&#93; in the hand of the Egyptian, and he goes down to him with a rod, and takes the spear violently away out of the hand of the Egyptian, and slays him with his own spear.</span>
