<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 6:66–68; Acts 11:23; 2 Samuel 19:15"> And every man of Israel goes up from after David, after Sheba son of Bichri, and the men of Judah have cleaved to their king, from the Jordan even to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:21–22; 2 Samuel 15:16; Genesis 40:3"> And David comes to his house at Jerusalem. And the king takes the ten women-concubines whom he had left to keep the house, and puts them in a house of ward and sustains them, and he has not gone in to them, and they are shut up to the day of their death, living in widowhood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:13; 2 Samuel 17:25; 1 Chronicles 2:17"> And the king says to Amasa, “Call the men of Judah for me &#91;within&#93; three days, and you, stand here,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:13; 1 Samuel 13:8"> and Amasa goes to call Judah, and tarries beyond the appointed time that he had appointed him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:17; 2 Samuel 11:11; 1 Kings 1:33"> and David says to Abishai, “Now Sheba son of Bichri does evil to us more than Absalom; you, take the servants of your lord, and pursue after him, lest he has found fortified cities for himself, and delivered himself &#91;from&#93; our eye.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 1 Kings 1:38; 2 Samuel 20:23"> And the men of Joab go out after him, and the Cherethite, and the Pelethite, and all the mighty men, and they go out from Jerusalem to pursue after Sheba son of Bichri;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:30; 2 Samuel 2:13; 2 Samuel 20:4–5"> they &#91;are&#93; near the great stone that &#91;is&#93; in Gibeon, and Amasa has gone before them, and Joab &#91;is&#93; girded; he has put his long robe on him, and on it a girdle—a sword &#91;is&#93; fastened on his loins in its sheath; and he has gone out, and it falls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:47–48; Matthew 26:48–49; Micah 7:2"> And Joab says to Amasa, “Are you &#91;in&#93; peace, my brother?” And the right hand of Joab lays hold on the beard of Amasa to give a kiss to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:23; 2 Samuel 3:27; Judges 3:21"> and Amasa has not been watchful of the sword that &#91;is&#93; in the hand of Joab, and he strikes him with it to the fifth &#91;rib&#93;, and sheds out his bowels to the earth, and he has not repeated &#91;it&#93; to him, and he dies; and Joab and his brother Abishai have pursued after Sheba son of Bichri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:13; 2 Samuel 20:21; 2 Kings 9:32"> And a man has stood by him, of the young men of Joab, and says, “He who has delight in Joab, and he who &#91;is&#93; for David—after Joab!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21–22; Psalms 9:16; 2 Samuel 17:25"> And Amasa is rolling himself in blood, in the midst of the highway, and the man sees that all the people have stood still, and he brings around Amasa out of the highway to the field, and casts a garment over him when he has seen that everyone who has come by him has stood still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:12; Isaiah 7:3; 2 Kings 18:17"> When he has been removed out of the highway, every man has passed on after Joab, to pursue after Sheba son of Bichri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 15:29; 1 Kings 15:20; 2 Chronicles 16:4"> And he passes over through all the tribes of Israel to Abel, and to Beth-Maachah, and to all the Berites, and they are assembled, and also go in after him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 19:32; Jeremiah 32:24; 1 Kings 15:20"> and they go in and lay siege against him, in Abel of Beth-Maachah, and cast up a mound against the city, and it stands in a trench, and all the people who are &#91;are&#93; with Joab are destroying, to cause the wall to fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:2; Ecclesiastes 9:14–18; 1 Samuel 25:32–33"> And a wise woman calls out of the city, “Hear! Hear! Please say to Joab, Come near here, and I speak to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:12; 1 Samuel 25:24"> And he comes near to her, and the woman says, “Are you Joab?” And he says, “I &#91;am&#93;.” And she says to him, “Hear the words of your handmaid”; and he says, “I am hearing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:10–11"> And she speaks, saying, “They spoke often in former times, saying, Let them diligently inquire at Abel, and so they finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:19; 2 Samuel 21:3; 2 Samuel 17:16"> I &#91;am&#93; of the peaceable, faithful ones of Israel; you are seeking to destroy a city and mother in Israel; why do you swallow up the inheritance of YHWH?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:29; Proverbs 28:13; Jeremiah 17:9"> And Joab answers and says, “Far be it—far be it from me; I do not swallow up nor destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:9; Judges 2:9; 2 Samuel 17:2–3"> The matter &#91;is&#93; not so; for a man of the hill-country of Ephraim—Sheba son of Bichri &#91;is&#93; his name—has lifted up his hand against the king, against David; give him up by himself, and I go away from the city.” And the woman says to Joab, “Behold, his head is cast to you over the wall.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:1; Ecclesiastes 7:19; 2 Samuel 11:6–21"> And the woman comes to all the people in her wisdom, and they cut off the head of Sheba son of Bichri, and cast &#91;it&#93; to Joab, and he blows with a horn, and they are scattered from the city, each &#91;goes&#93; to his tents, and Joab has turned back to Jerusalem to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16–18; 1 Chronicles 18:15–17; 1 Kings 4:3–6"> And Joab &#91;is&#93; over all the host of Israel, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada &#91;is&#93; over the Cherethite, and over the Pelethite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 4:3; 1 Kings 12:18; 1 Kings 4:6"> and Adoram &#91;is&#93; over the tribute, and Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud &#91;is&#93; the remembrancer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 4:4; 2 Samuel 8:17; 1 Chronicles 18:16"> and Sheva &#91;is&#93; scribe, and Zadok and Abiathar &#91;are&#93; priests,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:38; Exodus 2:14; Genesis 41:45"> and also, Ira the Jairite has been minister to David.</span>
