<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 8:10; 1 Samuel 8:12; Exodus 17:9"> And David inspects the people who &#91;are&#93; with him, and sets over them heads of thousands and heads of hundreds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 7:16; 2 Samuel 15:19–22; 2 Samuel 10:7–10"> and David sends the third of the people by the hand of Joab, and the third by the hand of Abishai, son of Zeruiah, brother of Joab, and the third by the hand of Ittai the Gittite, and the king says to the people, “I certainly go out—I also—with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:17; Exodus 17:10–12; 1 Kings 22:31"> And the people say, “You do not go out, for if we utterly flee, they do not set &#91;their&#93; heart on us; and if half of us die, they do not set &#91;their&#93; heart to us—for &#91;you are&#93; now like ten thousand of us; and now, &#91;it is&#93; better that you are for a helper to us from the city.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:24; 2 Samuel 18:1; 1 Samuel 29:2"> And the king says to them, “That which is good in your eyes I do”; and the king stands at the side of the gate, and all the people have gone out by hundreds and by thousands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:18–21; 2 Samuel 16:11; Psalms 103:13"> and the king charges Joab, and Abishai, and Ittai, saying, “&#91;Deal&#93; gently—for me, for the youth, for Absalom”; and all the people heard in the king’s charging all the heads concerning Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 17:18; Joshua 17:15; Judges 12:4–6"> And the people go out into the field to meet Israel, and the battle is in a forest of Ephraim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; 2 Samuel 2:31; 2 Samuel 2:26"> and the people of Israel are struck there before the servants of David, and the striking there is great on that day—twenty thousand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 15:10; Joshua 10:11; Psalms 43:1"> and the battle there is scattered over the face of all the land, and the forest multiplies to devour among the people more than those whom the sword has devoured in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:26; Proverbs 30:17; Job 31:3"> And Absalom meets before the servants of David, and Absalom is riding on the mule, and the mule comes in under an entangled bough of the great oak, and his head takes hold on the oak, and he is placed between the heavens and the earth, and the mule that &#91;is&#93; under him has passed on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And one man sees, and declares &#91;it&#93; to Joab, and says, “Behold, I saw Absalom hanging in an oak.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Joab says to the man who is declaring &#91;it&#93; to him, “And behold, you have seen—and why did you not strike him there to the earth—and &#91;it would be&#93; on me to give to you ten pieces of silver and one girdle?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:5"> And the man says to Joab, “Indeed, though I am weighing on my hand one thousand pieces of silver, I do not put forth my hand to the son of the king; for in our ears the king has charged you, and Abishai, and Ittai, saying, Observe who &#91;is&#93; against the youth—against Absalom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:19–20; Hebrews 4:13; 2 Samuel 4:10–12"> or I had done a vain thing against my soul, and no matter is hid from the king, and you would station yourself opposite from &#91;me&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 45:5; Matthew 12:40; 2 Samuel 14:30"> And Joab says, “&#91;It is&#93; not right &#91;that&#93; I linger before you”; and he takes three darts in his hand, and strikes them into the heart of Absalom, while he &#91;is&#93; alive, in the midst of the oak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And they go around—ten youths carrying weapons of Joab—and strike Absalom, and put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:28; 2 Samuel 20:22; 1 Corinthians 14:8"> And Joab blows with a horn, and the people turn back from pursuing after Israel, for Joab has kept back the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 7:26; Joshua 8:29; 2 Samuel 19:8"> and they take Absalom and cast him into the great pit in the forest, and set up a very great heap of stones over him, and all Israel has fled—each to his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 14:17; 2 Samuel 14:27; 1 Samuel 15:12"> And Absalom has taken, and sets up for himself in his life, the standing-pillar that &#91;is&#93; in the king’s valley, for he said, “I have no son to cause my name to be remembered”; and he calls the standing-pillar by his own name, and it is called “The Monument of Absalom” to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:36; 2 Samuel 18:31; Psalms 9:4"> And Ahimaaz son of Zadok said, “Please let me run, and I bear the king tidings, for YHWH has delivered him out of the hand of his enemies”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:5; 2 Samuel 18:33; 2 Samuel 17:16–21"> and Joab says to him, “You are not a man of tidings this day, but you have borne tidings on another day, and this day you do not bear tidings, because the king’s son &#91;is&#93; dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Joab says to Cushi, “Go, declare to the king that which you have seen”; and Cushi bows himself to Joab, and runs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 1:28; Ephesians 5:4"> And Ahimaaz son of Zadok adds again and says to Joab, “And whatever it is, please let me run, I also, after the Cushite.” And Joab says, “Why &#91;is&#93; this—you are running, my son, and &#91;there are&#93; no tidings found from you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 20:4"> “And whatever happens,” he said, “let me run.” And he says to him, “Run”; and Ahimaaz runs the way of the circuit, and passes by the Cushite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:8; 2 Samuel 13:34; Ezekiel 33:2–7"> And David is sitting between the two gates, and the watchman goes to the roof of the gate, to the wall, and lifts up his eyes, and looks, and behold, a man running by himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And the watchman calls, and declares &#91;it&#93; to the king, and the king says, “If &#91;he is&#93; by himself, tidings &#91;are&#93; in his mouth”; and he comes, coming on and drawing near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the watchman sees another man running, and the watchman calls to the gatekeeper, and says, “Behold, a man running by himself”; and the king says, “This one is also bearing tidings.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 9:20; 1 Kings 1:42; Romans 10:15"> And the watchman says, “I see the running of the first as the running of Ahimaaz son of Zadok.” And the king says, “This &#91;is&#93; a good man, and he comes with good tidings.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:4; Genesis 14:20; Genesis 24:27"> And Ahimaaz calls and says to the king, “Peace”; and he bows himself to the king, on his face, to the earth, and says, “Blessed &#91;is&#93; your God YHWH who has shut up the men who lifted up their hand against my lord the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:22; 2 Samuel 20:9; 2 Samuel 18:19–20"> And the king says, “Peace to the youth—for Absalom?” And Ahimaaz says, “I saw the great multitude, at the sending away of the servant of the king, even your servant &#91;by&#93; Joab, and I have not known what &#91;it is&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the king says, “Turn around, station yourself here”; and he turns around and stands still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:19; 2 Samuel 18:28; Luke 18:7–8"> And behold, the Cushite has come, and the Cushite says, “Let tidings be proclaimed, my lord, O king; for today YHWH has delivered you out of the hand of all those rising up against you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 5:31; 1 Samuel 25:26; Daniel 4:19"> And the king says to the Cushite, “Peace to the youth—for Absalom?” And the Cushite says, “Let them be—as the youth—the enemies of my lord the king, and all who have risen up against you for evil.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:4; 2 Samuel 12:10–23; Proverbs 17:25"> And the king trembles, and goes up on the upper chamber of the gate, and weeps, and thus he has said in his going, “My son Absalom! My son! My son Absalom! Oh that I had died for you, Absalom, my son, my son!”</span>
