<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Samuel 15:32; 2 Samuel 9:2–13"> And David has passed on a little from the top, and behold, Ziba, servant of Mephibosheth, &#91;is there&#93; to meet him, and a couple of donkeys &#91;are&#93; saddled, and on them &#91;are&#93; two hundred loaves, and one hundred bunches of raisins, and one hundred of summer-fruit, and a bottle of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:29; Judges 10:4; Judges 5:10"> And the king says to Ziba, “What &#91;are&#93; these to you?” And Ziba says, “The donkeys for the household of the king to ride on, and the bread and the summer-fruit for the young men to eat, and the wine for the wearied to drink in the wilderness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:9–10; Psalms 15:3; 2 Samuel 19:24–30"> And the king says, “And where &#91;is&#93; the son of your lord?” And Ziba says to the king, “Behold, he is abiding in Jerusalem, for he said, Today the house of Israel gives back the kingdom of my father to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Proverbs 18:17; Proverbs 19:2"> And the king says to Ziba, “Behold, all that Mephibosheth has &#91;is&#93; for you”; and Ziba says, “I have bowed myself—I find grace in your eyes, my lord, O king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:16; 1 Samuel 17:43; Exodus 22:28"> And King David has come to Bahurim, and behold, a man there is coming out, of the family of the house of Saul, and his name &#91;is&#93; Shimei, son of Gera, he comes out, coming out and reviling;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and he stones David with stones, and all the servants of King David, and all the people, and all the mighty men on his right and on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:13; 1 Samuel 25:17; 1 Kings 21:10"> And thus said Shimei in his reviling: “Go out, go out, O man of blood, and man of worthlessness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:16; 2 Samuel 3:28–29; 1 Kings 2:32–33"> YHWH has turned back on you all the blood of the house of Saul, in whose stead you have reigned, and YHWH gives the kingdom into the hand of your son Absalom; and behold, you &#91;are&#93; in your evil, for you &#91;are&#93; a man of blood.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; 2 Samuel 9:8; 1 Samuel 24:14"> And Abishai son of Zeruiah says to the king, “Why does this dead dog revile my lord the king? Please let me pass over and I turn aside his head.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:22; Romans 9:20; John 18:11"> And the king says, “And what do I &#91;have to do&#93; with you, O sons of Zeruiah? For let him revile; even because YHWH has said to him, Revile David; and who says, Why have You done so?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:1–4; Genesis 45:5; 2 Samuel 12:11–12"> And David says to Abishai, and to all his servants, “Behold, my son who came out of my bowels is seeking my life, and also surely now the Benjamite; leave him alone, and let him revile, for YHWH has commanded &#91;so&#93; to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Hebrews 12:10; Psalms 25:18"> it may be YHWH looks on my affliction, and YHWH has turned back good to me for his reviling this day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 23:23; 2 Samuel 16:5–6"> And David goes with his men in the way, and Shimei is going at the side of the hill opposite him, going on, and he reviles, and stones with stones close by him, and has dusted with dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the king comes in, and all the people who &#91;are&#93; with him, wearied, and they are refreshed there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:37; 2 Samuel 15:12"> And Absalom and all the people, the men of Israel, have come to Jerusalem, and Ahithophel with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 11:12; 1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Kings 1:25"> and it comes to pass, when Hushai the Archite, David’s friend, has come to Absalom, that Hushai says to Absalom, “Let the king live! Let the king live!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:25; 2 Samuel 15:32–37; Deuteronomy 32:6"> And Absalom says to Hushai, “&#91;Is&#93; this your kindness with your friend? Why have you not gone with your friend?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:13; 2 Samuel 5:1–3"> And Hushai says to Absalom, “No, for he whom YHWH has chosen, and this people, even all the men of Israel, I am his, and I abide with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:34; Psalms 55:21; 1 Samuel 28:2"> and secondly, for whom do I labor? Is it not before his son? As I served before your father, so I am before you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 8:10; Psalms 37:12–13; Isaiah 29:15"> And Absalom says to Ahithophel, “Give counsel for yourself &#91;for&#93; what we do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:3; 2 Samuel 15:16; Zechariah 8:13"> And Ahithophel says to Absalom, “Go in to the concubines of your father, whom he left to keep the house, and all Israel has heard that you have been abhorred by your father, and the hands of all who &#91;are&#93; with you have been strong.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:11–12; 2 Samuel 15:16; 2 Samuel 20:3"> And they spread out the tent for Absalom on the roof, and Absalom goes in to the concubines of his father before the eyes of all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:12; 2 Samuel 17:14; 2 Samuel 17:23"> And the counsel of Ahithophel which he counseled in those days &#91;is&#93; as &#91;when&#93; one inquires at the word of God; so &#91;is&#93; all the counsel of Ahithophel both to David and to Absalom.</span>
