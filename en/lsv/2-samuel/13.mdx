<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:9; 2 Samuel 3:2–3; 2 Samuel 13:15"> And it comes to pass afterward that Absalom son of David has a beautiful sister, and her name &#91;is&#93; Tamar, and Amnon son of David loves her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; 1 Kings 21:4; Song of Songs 5:8"> And Amnon has distress—even to become sick, because of his sister Tamar, for she &#91;is&#93; a virgin, and it is hard in the eyes of Amnon to do anything to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:9; 1 Corinthians 3:19; James 3:15"> And Amnon has a friend, and his name &#91;is&#93; Jonadab, son of Shimeah, David’s brother, and Jonadab &#91;is&#93; a very wise man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Leviticus 18:9; Esther 5:13–14"> and says to him, “Why &#91;are&#93; you thus lean, O king’s son, morning by morning? Do you not declare &#91;it&#93; to me?” And Amnon says to him, “Tamar—sister of my brother Absalom—I am loving.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 6:24–25; Proverbs 19:27; Acts 23:15"> And Jonadab says to him, “Lie down on your bed, and feign yourself sick, and your father has come in to see you, and you have said to him: Please let my sister Tamar come in and give me bread to eat; and she has made the food before my eyes so that I see &#91;it&#93;, and have eaten from her hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:6; Matthew 13:33"> And Amnon lies down, and feigns himself sick, and the king comes in to see him, and Amnon says to the king, “Please let my sister Tamar come, and she makes two cakes before my eyes, and I eat from her hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And David sends to Tamar, to the house, saying, “Now go to the house of your brother Amnon and make food for him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Tamar goes to the house of her brother Amnon, and he is lying down, and she takes the dough, and kneads, and makes cakes before his eyes, and cooks the cakes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 45:1; John 3:20; Judges 3:19"> and takes the frying-pan, and pours out before him, and he refuses to eat, and Amnon says, “Have everyone go out from me”; and everyone goes out from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Amnon says to Tamar, “Bring the food into the inner chamber, and I eat from your hand”; and Tamar takes the cakes that she has made and brings &#91;them&#93; to her brother Amnon &#91;in&#93; the inner chamber,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 39:11–12"> and she brings &#91;them&#93; near to him to eat, and he lays hold on her, and says to her, “Come, lie with me, my sister.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 19:23; Leviticus 20:17; Judges 20:6"> And she says to him, “No, my brother, do not humble me, for it is not done so in Israel; do not do this folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 19:24; Genesis 20:12; Genesis 19:8"> And I—to where do I cause my reproach to go? And you are as one of the fools in Israel; and now, please speak to the king; for he does not withhold me from you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:11; Esther 7:8; Judges 20:5"> And he has not been willing to listen to her voice, and is stronger than she, and humbles her, and lies with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:17"> And Amnon hates her—a very great hatred—that greater &#91;is&#93; the hatred with which he has hated her than the love with which he loved her, and Amnon says to her, “Rise, go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And she says to him, “Because of the circumstances this evil is greater than the other that you have done with me—to send me away”; and he has not been willing to listen to her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and calls his young man, his servant, and says, “Now send this one away from me outside, and bolt the door after her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Genesis 37:3; Psalms 45:13–14"> And a long coat &#91;is&#93; on her, for such upper robes daughters of the king who &#91;are&#93; virgins put on—and his servant takes her outside, and has bolted the door after her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 7:6; Jeremiah 2:37; 2 Samuel 1:11"> And Tamar takes ashes for her head, and has torn the long coat that &#91;is&#93; on her, and puts her hand on her head, and goes, going on and crying;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Genesis 46:15; Genesis 34:2"> and her brother Absalom says to her, “Has your brother Amnon been with you? And now, my sister, keep silent, he &#91;is&#93; your brother; do not set your heart to this thing”; and Tamar dwells—but desolate—in the house of her brother Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:7; 2 Samuel 3:28–29; 1 Samuel 2:29"> And King David has heard all these things, and it is very displeasing to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 19:17–18; Genesis 31:24; Genesis 24:50"> and Absalom has not spoken with Amnon either evil or good, for Absalom is hating Amnon, because that he humbled his sister Tamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 38:12–13; 1 Samuel 25:4; 2 Chronicles 26:10"> And it comes to pass, after two years of days, that Absalom has shearers in Ba‘al-Hazor, which &#91;is&#93; near Ephraim, and Absalom calls for all the sons of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:8–15; Jeremiah 41:6–7; Psalms 55:21"> And Absalom comes to the king and says, “Now behold, your servant has shearers, please let the king go—and his servants—with your servant.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 14:23; Judges 19:7–10; 2 Samuel 14:22"> And the king says to Absalom, “No, my son, please let us not all go, and we are not too heavy on you”; and he presses on him, and he has not been willing to go, and he blesses him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:9; 2 Samuel 3:27; 2 Samuel 11:13–15"> And Absalom says, “If not, please let my brother Amnon go with us”; and the king says to him, “Why does he go with you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–26"> And Absalom urges for him, and he sends Amnon and all the sons of the king with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 19:6; Judges 19:22; Judges 19:9"> And Absalom commands his young men, saying, “Now see, when the heart of Amnon &#91;is&#93; glad with wine, and I have said to you, Strike Amnon, that you have put him to death; do not fear; is it not because I have commanded you? Be strong, indeed, become sons of valor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:9; 1 Kings 1:33; 2 Kings 1:9–12"> And the young men of Absalom do to Amnon as Absalom commanded, and all the sons of the king rise, and they ride, each on his mule, and flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And it comes to pass—they &#91;are&#93; in the way—and the report has come to David, saying, “Absalom has struck all the sons of the king, and there is not left &#91;even&#93; one of them”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:11; 2 Samuel 12:16; Job 1:20"> and the king rises, and tears his garments, and lies on the earth, and all his servants are standing by &#91;with&#93; torn garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:9; 2 Samuel 13:3; Genesis 27:41"> And Jonadab son of Shimeah, David’s brother, answers and says, “Do not let my lord say, The whole of the young men, the sons of the king, they have put to death; for Amnon alone &#91;is&#93; dead, for it has been appointed by the command of Absalom from the day of his humbling his sister Tamar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:19"> and now, do not let my lord the king lay the word to his heart, saying, All the sons of the king have died, for Amnon alone &#91;is&#93; dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 4:8–14; Proverbs 28:17; Amos 5:19"> And Absalom flees, and the young man who is watching lifts up his eyes and looks, and behold, many people are coming by the way behind him, on the side of the hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And Jonadab says to the king, “Behold, the sons of the king have come; as the word of your servant, so it has been.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:33; 2 Samuel 12:21; 2 Samuel 13:15"> And it comes to pass at his finishing to speak, that behold, the sons of the king have come, and they lift up their voice, and weep, and also the king and all his servants have wept—a very great weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:3; 2 Samuel 14:32; 2 Samuel 13:34"> And Absalom has fled, and goes to Talmai, son of Ammihud, king of Geshur, and &#91;David&#93; mourns for his son all the days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:8; 2 Samuel 14:23; 2 Samuel 14:32"> And Absalom has fled, and goes to Geshur, and is there &#91;for&#93; three years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 24:67; Genesis 38:12; Genesis 37:35"> and King David determines to go out to Absalom, for he has been comforted for Amnon, for &#91;he is&#93; dead.</span>
