<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 7:11–12; Genesis 26:1; Genesis 12:10"> And there is a famine in the days of David &#91;for&#93; three years, year after year, and David seeks the face of YHWH, and YHWH says, “&#91;This is&#93; for Saul and for the bloody house, because that he put the Gibeonites to death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 9:3–21; 2 Kings 10:31; 1 Samuel 14:44"> And the king calls for the Gibeonites and says to them—as for the Gibeonites, they &#91;are&#93; not of the sons of Israel, but of the remnant of the Amorite, and the sons of Israel had sworn to them, and Saul seeks to strike them in his zeal for the sons of Israel and Judah—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:19; Hebrews 9:22; 1 Samuel 26:19"> indeed, David says to the Gibeonites, “What do I do for you? And with what do I make atonement? And bless the inheritance of YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 49:6–8; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> And the Gibeonites say to him, “We have no silver and gold by Saul and by his house, and we have no man to put to death in Israel”; and he says, “What you are saying I do to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:1; Daniel 9:26; Matthew 7:2"> And they say to the king, “The man who consumed us, and who devised against us—we have been destroyed from stationing ourselves in all the border of Israel—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:24; Numbers 25:4–5; 1 Samuel 11:4"> let there be given to us seven men of his sons, and we have hanged them before YHWH, in the height of Saul, the chosen of YHWH.” And the king says, “I give”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:15; 1 Samuel 18:3; 1 Samuel 23:18"> and the king has pity on Mephibosheth son of Jonathan, son of Saul, because of the oath of YHWH that &#91;is&#93; between them, between David and Jonathan son of Saul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:19; 2 Samuel 3:7"> and the king takes the two sons of Rizpah daughter of Aiah, whom she bore to Saul, Armoni and Mephibosheth, and the five sons of Michal daughter of Saul whom she bore to Adriel son of Barzillai the Meholathite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ruth 1:22; Deuteronomy 21:1–9; 2 Samuel 6:17"> and gives them into the hand of the Gibeonites, and they hang them in the hill before YHWH; and the seven fall together, and they have been put to death in the days of harvest, in the first &#91;days&#93;, the commencement of barley-harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:23; 1 Kings 21:27; Joel 2:23"> And Rizpah daughter of Aiah takes the sackcloth, and stretches it out for herself on the rock, from the commencement of harvest until water has been poured out on them from the heavens, and has not permitted a bird of the heavens to rest on them by day, or the beast of the field by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:4; Ruth 2:11–12"> And it is declared to David that which Rizpah daughter of Aiah, concubine of Saul, has done,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 17:11; 1 Samuel 31:10–13; 2 Samuel 2:5–7"> and David goes and takes the bones of Saul, and the bones of his son Jonathan, from the possessors of Jabesh-Gilead, who had stolen them from the broad place of Beth-Shan, where the Philistines hanged them, in the day of the Philistines striking Saul in Gilboa;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and he brings up there the bones of Saul, and the bones of his son Jonathan, and they gather the bones of those hanged,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 18:28; 2 Samuel 24:25; Joshua 7:26"> and bury the bones of Saul and of his son Jonathan in the land of Benjamin, in Zelah, in the burying-place of his father Kish, and do all that the king commanded, and God accepts the plea for the land afterward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 14:10–11; 2 Samuel 5:22; Ecclesiastes 12:3"> And again the Philistines have war with Israel, and David goes down, and his servants with him, and they fight with the Philistines; and David is weary,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:18; 2 Samuel 21:20; 1 Samuel 17:4–5"> and Ishbi-Benob, who &#91;is&#93; among the children of the giant—the weight of his spear &#91;is&#93; three hundred &#91;shekels in&#93; weight of bronze, and he is girded with a new one—speaks of striking David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 11:36; 2 Samuel 18:3; Psalms 132:17"> and Abishai son of Zeruiah gives help to him, and strikes the Philistine, and puts him to death; then the men of David swear to him, saying, “You do not go out with us to battle again, nor quench the lamp of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:29; 2 Samuel 21:20; 2 Samuel 21:16"> And it comes to pass afterward that the battle is again in Gob with the Philistines. Then Sibbechai the Hushathite has struck Saph, who &#91;is&#93; among the children of the giant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 20:5; 1 Samuel 17:4–11; 1 Chronicles 11:26"> And the battle is again in Gob with the Philistines, and Elhanan son of Jaare-Oregim, the Beth-Lehemite, strikes &#91;a brother of&#93; Goliath the Gittite, and the wood of his spear &#91;is&#93; like a weavers’ beam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:18; 2 Samuel 21:16; 1 Chronicles 20:6"> And the battle is again in Gath, and there is a man of &#91;great&#93; stature, and the fingers of his hands &#91;are&#93; six, and the toes of his feet &#91;are&#93; six—twenty-four in number, and he has also been born to the giant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:10; 1 Samuel 17:36; 1 Samuel 16:9"> and he reproaches Israel, and Jonathan son of Shimeah, David’s brother, strikes him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 20:8; Romans 8:31; Jeremiah 9:23"> these four have been born to the giant in Gath, and they fall by the hand of David, and by the hand of his servants.</span>
