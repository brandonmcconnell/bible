<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:1; 1 Chronicles 27:23–24; James 1:13–14"> And the anger of YHWH adds to burn against Israel, and &#91;an adversary&#93; moves David about them, saying, “Go, number Israel and Judah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:10; Judges 20:1; 2 Samuel 20:23"> And the king says to Joab, head of the host that &#91;is&#93; with him, “Now go to and fro through all the tribes of Israel, from Dan even to Beer-Sheba, and inspect the people; then I have known the number of the people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:11; Psalms 115:14; Proverbs 14:28"> And Joab says to the king, “Indeed, your God YHWH adds to the people, as they are, one hundred times, and the eyes of my lord the king are seeing; and my lord the king, why is he desirous of this thing?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Ecclesiastes 8:4; 1 Chronicles 21:4"> And the word of the king is severe toward Joab, and against the heads of the force, and Joab goes out, and the heads of the force, &#91;from&#93; before the king to inspect the people, even Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 13:9; Deuteronomy 2:36; Joshua 13:16"> and they pass over the Jordan, and encamp in Aroer, on the right of the city that &#91;is&#93; in the midst of the Brook of Gad, and to Jazer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 19:28; Genesis 31:47–48; Judges 18:28–29"> and they come to Gilead, and to the land of Tahtim-Hodshi, and they come to Dan-Jaan, and around to Sidon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:29; Joshua 11:3; Genesis 21:22–33"> and they come to the fortress of Tyre, and all the cities of the Hivite, and of the Canaanite, and go out to the south of Judah, to Beer-Sheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And they go to and fro through all the land, and come in to Jerusalem at the end of nine months and twenty days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:23–24; 1 Chronicles 21:5–6; Numbers 1:44–46"> and Joab gives the account of the inspection of the people to the king, and Israel is eight hundred thousand men of valor, drawing sword, and the men of Judah five hundred thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13; 1 Samuel 13:13; Proverbs 28:13"> And the heart of David strikes him, after that he has numbered the people, and David says to YHWH, “I have greatly sinned in that which I have done, and now, O YHWH, please cause the iniquity of Your servant to pass away, for I have acted very foolishly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:5; 1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Chronicles 29:29"> And David rises in the morning, and the word of YHWH has been to Gad the prophet, seer of David, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 12:6–10; Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 3:12"> “Go, and you have spoken to David, Thus said YHWH: I am lifting up three &#91;choices&#93; for you, choose one of them, and I do &#91;it&#93; to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:12; Leviticus 26:25; 2 Samuel 21:1"> And Gad comes to David, and declares &#91;it&#93; to him, and says to him, “Does seven years of famine come to you in your land? Or are you fleeing before your adversary &#91;for&#93; three months—and he is pursuing you? Or is there pestilence &#91;for&#93; three days in your land? Now, know and see what word I take back to Him sending me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 51:1; Psalms 119:156; 1 Chronicles 21:13"> And David says to Gad, “I have great distress; please let us fall into the hand of YHWH, for His mercies &#91;are&#93; many, but do not let me fall into the hand of man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:14; 1 Chronicles 27:24; Numbers 25:9"> And YHWH gives a pestilence on Israel from the morning even to the time appointed, and there dies of the people, from Dan even to Beer-Sheba, seventy thousand men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 2:13–14; Genesis 6:6; Exodus 12:23"> and the messenger puts forth his hand to Jerusalem to destroy it, and YHWH sighs concerning the calamity, and says to the messenger who is destroying among the people, “Enough, now, cease your hand”; and the messenger of YHWH was near the threshing-floor of Araunah the Jebusite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; 1 Chronicles 21:16–17; 2 Samuel 24:10"> And David speaks to YHWH when he sees the messenger who is striking among the people, and says, “Behold, I have sinned, indeed, I have done perversely; and these—the flock—what have they done? Please let Your hand be on me and on the house of my father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:18–30; 2 Samuel 24:11"> And Gad comes to David on that day and says to him, “Go up, raise an altar to YHWH in the threshing-floor of Araunah the Jebusite”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Nehemiah 9:26; 2 Chronicles 36:16"> and David goes up, according to the word of Gad, as YHWH commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 18:2; Ruth 2:10; 1 Chronicles 21:20–21"> And Araunah looks, and sees the king and his servants passing over to him, and Araunah goes out and bows himself to the king &#91;with&#93; his face to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:22; Numbers 25:8; 2 Samuel 21:3–14"> And Araunah says, “Why has my lord the king come to his servant?” And David says, “To buy the threshing-floor from you, to build an altar to YHWH, and the plague is restrained from the people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 19:21; 1 Samuel 6:14; 1 Chronicles 21:22"> And Araunah says to David, “Let my lord the king take &#91;it&#93; and cause that which is good in his eyes to ascend; see, &#91;here are&#93; the oxen for a burnt-offering, and the threshing instruments, and the instruments of the oxen, for wood”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:40–41; Isaiah 32:8; Psalms 20:3–4"> Araunah has given the whole &#91;as&#93; a king to a king; and Araunah says to the king, “Your God YHWH accepts you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:24–25; Malachi 1:12–14; Genesis 23:13"> And the king says to Araunah, “No, for I surely buy from you for a price, and I do not cause burnt-offerings to ascend to my God YHWH for nothing”; and David buys the threshing-floor and the oxen for fifty shekels of silver,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:14; Lamentations 3:32–33; Genesis 8:20"> and David builds an altar to YHWH there, and causes burnt-offerings and peace-offerings to ascend, and YHWH accepts the plea for the land, and the plague is restrained from Israel.</span>
