<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 20:35–41; Psalms 51:1–19; 2 Samuel 11:10–17"> And YHWH sends Nathan to David, and he comes to him, and says to him: “Two men have been in one city, one rich and one poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:16; Job 1:3; 2 Samuel 5:13–16"> The rich has very many flocks and herds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Micah 7:5; 2 Samuel 11:3; Proverbs 5:18–19"> but the poor one has nothing, except one little ewe-lamb which he has bought and keeps alive. And it grows up together with him and with his sons. It eats of his morsel, and it drinks from his cup, and it lies in his bosom, and it is as a daughter to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 1:14; 2 Samuel 11:3–4; Genesis 18:2–7"> And a traveler comes to the rich man, and he spares to take from his own flock, and from his own herd, to prepare for the traveling &#91;man&#93; who has come to him; and he takes the ewe-lamb of the poor man and prepares it for the man who has come to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 38:24; Romans 2:1; 1 Samuel 26:16"> And the anger of David burns against the man exceedingly, and he says to Nathan, “YHWH lives, surely the man who is doing this &#91;is&#93; a son of death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:1; Luke 19:8; Proverbs 6:31"> and &#91;for&#93; the ewe-lamb he repays fourfold, because that he has done this thing, and because that he had no pity.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 14:14; 1 Samuel 16:13; 2 Samuel 22:1"> And Nathan says to David, “You &#91;are&#93; the man! Thus said YHWH, God of Israel: I anointed you for king over Israel, and I delivered you out of the hand of Saul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 84:11; Romans 8:32; 2 Samuel 9:7"> and I give to you the house of your lord, and the wives of your lord, into your bosom, and I give the house of Israel and Judah to you; and if &#91;that is&#93; too little, then I add such and such &#91;things&#93; to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:19; Psalms 139:1–2; 1 Samuel 15:23"> Why have you despised the word of YHWH, to do evil in His eyes? You have struck Uriah the Hittite by the sword, and you have taken his wife for a wife for yourself, and you have slain him by the sword of the sons of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:14–15; Matthew 26:52; Romans 2:4"> And now, the sword does not turn aside from your house for all time, because you have despised Me, and take the wife of Uriah the Hittite to be for a wife for yourself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:21–22; Deuteronomy 28:30; 2 Samuel 15:10"> thus said YHWH: Behold, I am raising up calamity against you, out of your &#91;own&#93; house, and have taken your wives before your eyes, and given &#91;them&#93; to your neighbor, and he has lain with your wives before the eyes of this sun;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:22; 2 Samuel 11:4–15; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> for you have done &#91;it&#93; in secret, and I do this thing before all Israel, and before the sun.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; 2 Samuel 24:10; Proverbs 28:13"> And David says to Nathan, “I have sinned against YHWH.” And Nathan says to David, “Also—YHWH has caused your sin to pass away; you do not die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Isaiah 52:5; Psalms 89:31–33"> only, because you have caused the enemies of YHWH to greatly despise by this thing, also—the son who is born to you surely dies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:38; Psalms 104:29; 2 Chronicles 13:20"> And Nathan goes to his house, and YHWH strikes the boy, whom the wife of Uriah has borne to David, and it is incurable;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:31; Acts 9:9; Psalms 69:10"> and David seeks God for the youth, and David keeps a fast, and has gone in and lodged, and lain on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:35; 1 Samuel 28:23"> And &#91;the&#93; elderly of his house rise against him, to raise him up from the earth, and he has not been willing, nor has he eaten bread with them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 20:15"> and it comes to pass on the seventh day, that the boy dies, and the servants of David fear to declare to him that the boy is dead, for they said, “Behold, in the boy being alive we spoke to him, and he did not listen to our voice; and how do we say to him, The boy is dead? Then he has done evil.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And David sees that his servants are whispering, and David understands that the boy is dead, and David says to his servants, “Is the boy dead?” And they say, “Dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 1:20; Ruth 3:3; Lamentations 3:39–41"> And David rises from the earth, and bathes and anoints &#91;himself&#93;, and changes his raiment, and comes into the house of YHWH, and bows himself, and comes to his house, and asks and they place bread for him, and he eats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:15"> And his servants say to him, “What &#91;is&#93; this thing you have done? Because of the living boy you have fasted and you weep, and when the boy is dead you have risen and eat bread.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 38:1–3; Isaiah 38:5; Jonah 3:9–10"> And he says, “While the boy is alive I have fasted and I weep, for I said, Who knows &#91;if&#93; YHWH pities me and the boy has lived?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:35; Job 30:23; Luke 23:43"> And now, he has died, why &#91;is&#93; this—I fast? Am I able to bring him back again? I am going to him, and he does not return to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 1:6; 1 Chronicles 28:5–6; 1 Chronicles 22:9–10"> And David comforts his wife Bathsheba, and goes in to her, and lies with her, and she bears a son, and he calls his name Solomon; and YHWH has loved him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:4; Nehemiah 13:26; 1 Kings 1:11"> and sends by the hand of Nathan the prophet, and calls his name Jedidiah, because of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 20:1–3; Deuteronomy 3:11; 2 Samuel 11:25"> And Joab fights against Rabbah of the sons of Ammon, and captures the royal city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; 2 Samuel 11:1; Deuteronomy 3:11"> and Joab sends messengers to David and says, “I have fought against Rabbah—I have also captured the city of waters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 7:18"> and now, gather the rest of the people, and encamp against the city, and capture it, lest I capture the city, and my name has been called on it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And David gathers all the people, and goes to Rabbah, and fights against it, and captures it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 20:2"> and he takes the crown of their king from off his head, and its weight &#91;is&#93; a talent of gold, and precious stones, and it is on the head of David; and he has brought out the spoil of the city—very much;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 20:3; Psalms 21:8–9; Amos 1:3"> and he has brought out the people who &#91;are&#93; in it, and sets &#91;them&#93; to the saw, and to cutting instruments of iron, and to axes of iron, and has caused them to pass over into the brick-kiln; and so he does to all the cities of the sons of Ammon; and David turns back, and all the people, to Jerusalem.</span>
