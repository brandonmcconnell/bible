<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:5; 2 Samuel 18:14; Proverbs 17:25"> And it is declared to Joab, “Behold, the king is weeping and mourning for Absalom”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 19:12; Proverbs 16:15"> and the salvation on that day becomes mourning to all the people, for the people have heard on that day, saying, “The king has been grieved for his son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 31:27; 2 Samuel 19:32; 2 Samuel 17:24"> And the people steals away, on that day, to go into the city, as the people steal away, who are ashamed, in their fleeing in battle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:33; 2 Samuel 15:30"> and the king has covered his face, indeed, the king cries &#91;with&#93; a loud voice, “My son Absalom! Absalom, my son, my son!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 18:47–48; Psalms 3:8; Nehemiah 9:27"> And Joab comes into the house to the king and says, “Today you have put to shame the faces of all your servants, those delivering your life today, and the life of your sons, and of your daughters, and the life of your wives, and the life of your concubines,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 23:5; 2 Samuel 3:24–25; Proverbs 19:9–10"> to love your enemies, and to hate those loving you, for today you have declared that you have no princes and servants, for today I have known that if Absalom &#91;were&#93; alive, and all of us dead today, that then it were right in your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 14:28; Genesis 34:3; Proverbs 19:15"> And now, rise, go out and speak to the heart of your servants, for I have sworn by YHWH, that &#91;if&#93; you are not going out—there does not lodge a man with you tonight; and this &#91;is&#93; worse for you than all the evil that has come on you from your youth until now.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:24; 2 Samuel 18:4; 2 Samuel 19:3"> And the king rises, and sits in the gate, and they have declared to all the people, saying, “Behold, the king is sitting in the gate”; and all the people come in before the king, and Israel has fled, each to his tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:14; Genesis 3:12–13; 2 Samuel 8:1–14"> And it comes to pass, all the people are contending through all the tribes of Israel, saying, “The king delivered us out of the hand of our enemies, indeed, he himself delivered us out of the hand of the Philistines, and now he has fled out of the land because of Absalom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:14; Judges 18:9; Hosea 8:4"> and Absalom whom we anointed over us &#91;is&#93; dead in battle, and now, why are you silent—to bring back the king?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:29; 1 Kings 2:35; 2 Samuel 15:35–36"> And King David sent to Zadok and to Abiathar the priests, saying, “Speak to &#91;the&#93; elderly of Judah, saying, Why are you last to bring back the king to his house, since the word of all Israel has come to the king, to his house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:1; Genesis 2:23; Ephesians 5:30"> you &#91;are&#93; my brothers, you &#91;are&#93; my bone and my flesh, and why are you last to bring back the king?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:25; 1 Kings 19:2; Ruth 1:17"> And say to Amasa, Are you not my bone and my flesh? Thus God does to me, and thus He adds, if you are not head of the host before me instead of Joab &#91;for&#93; all the days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Acts 4:32; Psalms 110:2–3"> And he inclines the heart of all the men of Judah as one man, and they send to the king, “Return, you and all your servants.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 5:9; 1 Samuel 11:14–15"> And the king turns back, and comes to the Jordan, and Judah has come to Gilgal, to go to meet the king, to bring the king over the Jordan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 2:8; 2 Samuel 16:5–13; 1 Kings 2:36–46"> and Shimei son of Gera, the Benjamite, who &#91;is&#93; from Bahurim, hurries, and comes down with the men of Judah, to meet King David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:1–4; 2 Samuel 9:2; 2 Samuel 9:10"> and one thousand men &#91;are&#93; with him from Benjamin, and Ziba servant of the house of Saul, and his fifteen sons and his twenty servants with him, and they have gone prosperously over the Jordan before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 81:15; Revelation 3:9; Psalms 66:3"> And the ferry-boat has passed over to carry over the household of the king, and to do that which &#91;is&#93; good in his eyes, and Shimei son of Gera has fallen before the king in his passing over into the Jordan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:15; 2 Samuel 13:20; 2 Samuel 13:33"> and he says to the king, “Do not let my lord impute iniquity to me; neither remember that which your servant did perversely in the day that my lord the king went out from Jerusalem—for the king to set &#91;it&#93; to his heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 12:25; Psalms 78:34–37; Genesis 48:14"> for your servant has known that I have sinned; and behold, I have come today, first of all the house of Joseph, to go down to meet my lord the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; 1 Samuel 26:9; 1 Kings 21:10–11"> And Abishai son of Zeruiah answers and says, “Is Shimei not put to death for this—because he reviled the anointed of YHWH?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:13; 2 Samuel 16:10; 2 Samuel 3:39"> And David says, “And what do I &#91;have to do&#93; with you, O sons of Zeruiah, that today you are for an adversary to me? Is any man put to death in Israel today? For have I not known that today I &#91;am&#93; king over Israel?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 2:46; 1 Kings 2:8–9; 1 Kings 2:37"> And the king says to Shimei, “You do not die”; and the king swears to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:5; Matthew 6:16; 2 Samuel 15:30"> And Mephibosheth son of Saul has come down to meet the king—and he did not prepare his feet, nor did he prepare his upper lip, indeed, he did not wash his garments, even from the day of the going away of the king, until the day that he came in peace—</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:17"> and it comes to pass, when he has come to Jerusalem to meet the king, that the king says to him, “Why did you not go with me, Mephibosheth?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:2–3; 2 Samuel 4:4; 2 Samuel 9:3"> And he says, “My lord, O king, my servant deceived me, for your servant said, I saddle the donkey for myself, and ride on it, and go with the king, for your servant &#91;is&#93; lame;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:17; 2 Samuel 14:20; 1 Samuel 29:9"> and he utters slander against your servant to my lord the king, and my lord the king &#91;is&#93; as a messenger of God; and do that which is good in your eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:13; 2 Samuel 9:10; 2 Samuel 21:6–9"> for all the house of my father have been nothing except men of death before my lord the king, and you set your servant among those eating at your table, and what right do I have anymore—even to cry anymore to the king?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 18:15; Psalms 101:5; Psalms 82:2"> And the king says to him, “Why do you speak anymore of your matters? I have said, You and Ziba—share the field.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 1:20; Acts 20:24; 2 Samuel 1:26"> And Mephibosheth says to the king, “Indeed, let him take the whole, after that my lord the king has come in peace to his house.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 2:7; Nehemiah 7:63; Ezra 2:61"> And Barzillai the Gileadite has gone down from Rogelim, and passes over the Jordan with the king, to send him away over the Jordan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:2; Job 1:3; Proverbs 16:31"> and Barzillai &#91;is&#93; very aged, a son of eighty years, and he has sustained the king in his abiding in Mahanaim, for he &#91;is&#93; a very great man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 25:34–40; Luke 22:28–30; 2 Samuel 9:11"> and the king says to Barzillai, “Pass over with me, and I have sustained you in Jerusalem with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="James 4:14; 1 Corinthians 7:29; Genesis 47:8–9"> And Barzillai says to the king, “How many &#91;are&#93; the days of the years of my life, that I go up with the king to Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:33; Ezra 2:65; Psalms 90:10"> I &#91;am&#93; a son of eighty years today; do I know between good and evil? Does your servant taste that which I am eating, and that which I drink? Do I listen anymore to the voice of male and female singers? And why is your servant for a burden to my lord the king anymore?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 6:38"> As a little thing, your servant passes over the Jordan with the king, and why does the king repay me this repayment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Kings 2:7; Jeremiah 41:17; 2 Samuel 19:40"> Please let your servant turn back again, and I die in my own city, near the burying-place of my father and of my mother—and behold, your servant Chimham, let him pass over with my lord the king, and do to him that which &#91;is&#93; good in your eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And the king says, “Chimham goes over with me, and I do to him that which &#91;is&#93; good in your eyes, indeed, all that you fix on me I do to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 31:55; Ruth 1:14; Genesis 47:7"> And all the people pass over the Jordan, and the king has passed over, and the king gives a kiss to Barzillai, and blesses him, and he turns back to his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 21:9; Genesis 49:10; 2 Samuel 19:11–15"> And the king passes over to Gilgal, and Chimham has passed over with him, and all the people of Judah, and they bring over the king, and also the half of the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Judges 8:1; Judges 12:1; John 7:5–6"> And behold, all the men of Israel are coming to the king, and they say to the king, “Why have our brothers, the men of Judah, stolen you—and they bring the king and his household over the Jordan, and all the men of David with him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:12; 2 Samuel 5:1; 1 Chronicles 2:3–17"> And all the men of Judah answer against the men of Israel, “Because the king &#91;is&#93; near to us, and why &#91;is&#93; this &#91;that&#93; you are displeased about this matter? Have we eaten of the king’s &#91;substance&#93; at all? Has he lifted up a gift to us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Galatians 5:20; 2 Samuel 5:1; 1 Kings 11:30–31"> And the men of Israel answer the men of Judah, and say, “We have ten parts in the king, and also more than you in David; and why have you lightly esteemed us, that our word has not been first to bring back our king?” And the word of the men of Judah is sharper than the word of the men of Israel.</span>
