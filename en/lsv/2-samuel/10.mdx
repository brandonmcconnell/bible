<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1–3; Judges 11:12–28; Judges 10:7–9"> And it comes to pass afterward, that the king of the sons of Ammon dies, and his son Hanun reigns in his stead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1; Deuteronomy 23:3–6; Nehemiah 13:1–3"> and David says, “I do kindness with Hanun son of Nahash, as his father did kindness with me”; and David sends to comfort him by the hand of his servants concerning his father, and the servants of David come into the land of the sons of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 42:16; Genesis 42:9; 1 Corinthians 13:5"> And the heads of the sons of Ammon say to their lord Hanun, “Is David honoring your father in your eyes because he has sent comforters to you? For has David not sent his servants to you to search the city, and to spy it, and to overthrow it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 41:5; 1 Chronicles 19:3–4"> And Hanun takes the servants of David, and shaves off the half of their beard, and cuts off their long robes in the midst—to their buttocks, and sends them away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:5; 1 Kings 16:34; Joshua 6:24–26"> and they declare &#91;it&#93; to David, and he sends to meet them, for the men have been greatly ashamed, and the king says, “Abide in Jericho until your beard springs up—then you have returned.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:3; 2 Samuel 8:5; Genesis 34:30"> And the sons of Ammon see that they have been abhorred by David, and the sons of Ammon send and hire Aram of Beth-Rehob, and Aram of Zoba, twenty thousand footmen, and the king of Maacah &#91;with&#93; one thousand men, and Ish-Tob &#91;with&#93; twelve thousand men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:8–39; 1 Chronicles 19:8–19"> and David hears, and sends Joab, and all the host—the mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:6; Joshua 19:28; Numbers 13:21"> And the sons of Ammon come out, and set in array &#91;for&#93; battle, at the opening of the gate, and Aram of Zoba, and Rehob, and Ish-Tob, and Maacah, &#91;are&#93; by themselves in the field;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 20:42–43; Joshua 8:21–22"> and Joab sees that the front of the battle has been to him before and behind, and he chooses &#91;out&#93; of all the chosen in Israel, and sets in array to meet Aram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and he has given the rest of the people into the hand of his brother Abishai, and sets in array to meet the sons of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 6:2; Nehemiah 4:20; Philippians 1:27–28"> And he says, “If Aram is stronger than I, then you have been for salvation to me, and if the sons of Ammon are stronger than you, then I have come to give salvation to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:13; Deuteronomy 31:6; 1 Samuel 3:18"> be strong and strengthen yourself for our people, and for the cities of our God, and YHWH does that which is good in His eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 20:13–21; 1 Kings 20:28–30; 1 Chronicles 19:14–15"> And Joab draws near, and the people who &#91;are&#93; with him, to battle against Aram, and they flee from his presence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and the sons of Ammon have seen that Aram has fled, and they flee from the presence of Abishai, and go into the city; and Joab turns back from the sons of Ammon, and comes to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 14:2–3; Micah 4:11–12; Isaiah 8:9–10"> And Aram sees that it is struck before Israel, and they are gathered together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:16; 2 Samuel 8:3–8; 1 Chronicles 18:3"> and Hadadezer sends, and brings out Aram which &#91;is&#93; beyond the River, and they come to Helam, and Shobach, head of the host of Hadadezer, &#91;is&#93; before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:17"> And it is declared to David, and he gathers all Israel, and passes over the Jordan, and comes to Helam, and Aram sets itself in array to meet David, and they fight with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:18; 2 Samuel 8:4; Psalms 46:11"> and Aram flees from the presence of Israel, and David slays seven hundred charioteers and forty thousand horsemen of Aram, and he has struck Shobach, &#91;the&#93; head of its host, and he dies there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:6; Revelation 18:10; Psalms 48:4–5"> And all the kings—servants of Hadadezer—see that they have been struck before Israel, and make peace with Israel, and serve them; and Aram is afraid to help the sons of Ammon anymore.</span>
