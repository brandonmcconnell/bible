<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 4:4; Titus 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 20:14–17"> And the king says, “Is there not yet a man of the house of Saul, and I do with him the kindness of God?” And Ziba says to the king, “Jonathan has yet a son—lame.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:1; 1 Kings 2:7; 2 Samuel 19:28"> And David says to him, “Do not be afraid; for I certainly do with you kindness because of your father Jonathan, and have given back to you all the field of your father Saul, and you continually eat bread at my table.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:29; 2 Samuel 16:4; Isaiah 32:8"> And the king calls to Ziba servant of Saul and says to him, “All that was of Saul and of all his house, I have given to the son of your lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:7; 2 Samuel 19:28; 2 Kings 25:29"> and you have served the land for him, you and your sons, and your servants, and have brought in, and there has been bread for the son of your lord, and he has eaten it; and Mephibosheth, son of your lord, continually eats bread at my table”; and Ziba has fifteen sons and twenty servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:1–4; 2 Samuel 19:26; 2 Samuel 19:17"> And Ziba says to the king, “According to all that my lord the king commands his servant, so your servant does.” “As for Mephibosheth,” &#91;says the king,&#93; “he is eating at my table as one of the sons of the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:40–44; 1 Chronicles 8:8; 1 Chronicles 8:34–40"> And Mephibosheth has a young son, and his name &#91;is&#93; Micha, and everyone dwelling in the house of Ziba &#91;are&#93; servants to Mephibosheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:7; 2 Samuel 9:3; 2 Samuel 9:10–11"> And Mephibosheth is dwelling in Jerusalem, for he is continually eating at the table of the king, and he &#91;is&#93; lame &#91;in&#93; his two feet.</span>
