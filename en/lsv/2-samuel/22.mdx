<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jonah 2:3; Psalms 69:14–15; Revelation 17:15"> When the breakers of death surrounded me, &#124;&#124; The streams of the worthless terrify me,</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Psalms 116:4; Exodus 3:7"> In my adversity I call on YHWH, &#124;&#124; And I call to my God, &#124;&#124; And He hears my voice from His temple, &#124;&#124; And my cry &#91;is&#93; in His ears,</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Psalms 18:15; Psalms 18:8"> Smoke has gone up by His nostrils, &#124;&#124; And fire devours from His mouth; Brands have been kindled by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 8:12; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 144:5"> And He inclines the heavens and comes down, &#124;&#124; And thick darkness &#91;is&#93; under His feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; Psalms 18:10; 1 Samuel 4:4"> And He rides on a cherub and flies, &#124;&#124; And is seen on the wings of the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; 2 Samuel 22:10; Job 36:29"> And He sets darkness around Him &#91;for His&#93; dwelling places, &#124;&#124; Darkness of waters &#91;and&#93; thick clouds of the skies.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 29:3–9; 1 Samuel 2:10; Job 37:2–5"> YHWH thunders from the heavens, &#124;&#124; And the Most High gives forth His voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Joshua 10:10; Habakkuk 3:11"> And He sends forth arrows, and scatters them; Lightning, and troubles them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nahum 1:4; Habakkuk 3:8–10; Psalms 114:3–7"> And the streams of the sea are seen, &#124;&#124; &#91;The&#93; foundations of the world are revealed, &#124;&#124; By the rebuke of YHWH, &#124;&#124; From the breath of the spirit of His anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Psalms 144:7; Psalms 18:16"> He sends from above—He takes me, &#124;&#124; He draws me out of many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; 2 Timothy 4:17; Psalms 56:9"> He delivers me from my strong enemy, &#124;&#124; From those hating me, &#124;&#124; For they were stronger than me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 71:20–21; 1 Samuel 23:26–27; Psalms 118:10–13"> They are before me in a day of my calamity, &#124;&#124; And YHWH is my support,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 31:8; Psalms 118:5; 2 Samuel 15:26"> And He brings me out to a large place, &#124;&#124; He draws me out for He delighted in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 24:4; 1 Samuel 26:23; Psalms 7:8"> YHWH repays me, &#124;&#124; According to my righteousness, &#124;&#124; According to the cleanness of my hands, He returns to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32; Genesis 18:19; Psalms 128:1"> For I have kept the ways of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And have not done wickedly against my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:30; Psalms 119:102; John 15:14"> For all His judgments &#91;are&#93; before me, &#124;&#124; As for His statutes, I do not turn from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 6:9; Job 1:1; Genesis 17:1"> And I am perfect before Him, &#124;&#124; And I keep myself from my iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:21; 2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:7–8"> And YHWH returns to me, &#124;&#124; According to my righteousness, &#124;&#124; According to my cleanness before His eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; James 2:13"> With the kind You show Yourself kind, &#124;&#124; With the perfect man You show Yourself perfect,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Psalms 18:26; Leviticus 26:23–28"> With the pure You show Yourself pure, &#124;&#124; And with the perverse You show Yourself a wrestler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 72:12–13; Isaiah 5:15; James 4:6–7"> And You save the poor people, &#124;&#124; But Your eyes on the high cause &#91;them&#93; to fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; John 8:12; Job 29:3"> For You &#91;are&#93; my lamp, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; And YHWH lightens my darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 18:29; Philippians 4:13; Psalms 118:10–12"> For by You I run &#91;against&#93; a troop, &#124;&#124; By my God I leap a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Proverbs 30:5; Psalms 18:30"> God—His way &#91;is&#93; perfect, &#124;&#124; The saying of YHWH is tried, &#124;&#124; He &#91;is&#93; a shield to all those trusting in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:2; Deuteronomy 32:31; Jeremiah 10:6–7"> For who is God except YHWH? And who &#91;is the&#93; Rock except our God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Isaiah 41:10; Philippians 4:13"> God—my bulwark, &#91;my&#93; strength, &#124;&#124; And He makes my way perfect;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Isaiah 58:14; Habakkuk 3:19"> Making my feet like does, &#124;&#124; And causes me to stand on my high places,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 144:1; Psalms 18:33–34; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> Teaching my hands for battle, &#124;&#124; And a bow of bronze was brought down by my arms,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 6:16; Psalms 115:14; Psalms 84:11"> And You give the shield of Your salvation to me, &#124;&#124; And Your lowliness makes me great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Psalms 17:5; 1 Samuel 2:9"> You enlarge my step under me, &#124;&#124; And my ankles have not slipped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 21:8–9; 2 Samuel 5:18–25; 2 Samuel 10:14"> I pursue my enemies and destroy them, &#124;&#124; And I do not turn until they are consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Malachi 4:1; Psalms 110:1"> And I consume them, and strike them, &#124;&#124; And they do not rise, and fall under my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 44:5; Isaiah 60:14; Colossians 1:11"> And You gird me &#91;with&#93; strength for battle, &#124;&#124; You cause my withstanders to bow under me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; Joshua 10:24; 2 Thessalonians 1:8–9"> And my enemies—You give to me the neck, &#124;&#124; Those hating me—and I cut them off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; 1 Samuel 28:6; Micah 3:4"> They look, and there is no savior; To YHWH, and He has not answered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Micah 7:10; Isaiah 10:6; 2 Kings 13:7"> And I beat them as dust of the earth, &#124;&#124; As mire of the streets I beat them small—I spread them out!</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:1–14; Isaiah 55:5; 2 Samuel 3:1"> And You deliver me &#124;&#124; From the strivings of my people, &#124;&#124; You place me for a head of nations; A people I have not known serve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 81:15; Psalms 66:3; Isaiah 56:3"> Sons of a stranger feign obedience to me, &#124;&#124; At the hearing of the ear they listen to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Micah 7:17; James 1:11; Isaiah 64:6"> Sons of a stranger fade away, &#124;&#124; And gird themselves by their close places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 89:26; 2 Samuel 22:3; Job 19:25"> YHWH lives, and blessed &#91;is&#93; my Rock, &#124;&#124; And exalted is my God—The Rock of my salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 94:1; Psalms 144:2; 2 Samuel 18:31"> God—who is giving vengeance to me, &#124;&#124; And bringing down peoples under me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 140:1; 2 Samuel 7:8–9; Numbers 24:7"> And bringing me forth from my enemies, &#124;&#124; Indeed, You raise me up above my withstanders. You deliver me from a man of violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Romans 15:9; Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 145:1–2"> Therefore I confess You, O YHWH, among nations, &#124;&#124; And I sing praise to Your Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 89:29; Psalms 18:50; Psalms 89:20"> Magnifying the salvations of His king, &#124;&#124; And doing loving-kindness to His anointed, &#124;&#124; To David, and to his seed—for all time!”</span>
