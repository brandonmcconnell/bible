<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12–13; 1 Samuel 16:1; 2 Samuel 5:3"> And all &#91;the&#93; elderly of Israel come to the king at Hebron, and David makes a covenant with them in Hebron before YHWH, and they anoint David for king over Israel, according to the word of YHWH by the hand of Samuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 1:21; Genesis 15:21; Joshua 15:8"> And David goes, and all Israel, to Jerusalem—it &#91;is&#93; Jebus—and the Jebusite &#91;is&#93; there, the inhabitants of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:2; 1 Kings 8:1; Psalms 87:2"> And the inhabitants of Jebus say to David, “You do not come in here”; and David captures the fortress of Zion—it &#91;is&#93; the City of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16; 2 Samuel 20:23; 2 Samuel 3:27"> And David says, “Whoever strikes the Jebusite first becomes head and prince”; and Joab son of Zeruiah goes up first and becomes head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:5; 2 Samuel 5:7; Psalms 2:6"> And David dwells in the fortress, therefore they have called it the City of David;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 9:6; 1 Kings 11:27; 2 Kings 12:20"> and he builds the city all around, from Millo, and to the circumference, and Joab restores the rest of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1; Isaiah 41:10; 1 Chronicles 9:20"> And David goes, going on and becoming great, and YHWH of Hosts &#91;is&#93; with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:3; 1 Samuel 16:1; 1 Chronicles 12:38"> And these &#91;are&#93; heads of the mighty ones whom David has, who are strengthening themselves with him in his kingdom, with all Israel, to cause him to reign, according to the word of YHWH, over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:8; 1 Chronicles 27:2; 1 Chronicles 12:18"> And this &#91;is&#93; an account of the mighty ones whom David has: Jashobeam son of a Hachmonite &#91;is&#93; head of the thirty; he is lifting up his spear against three hundred—wounded, at one time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:4; 2 Samuel 23:17–19; 2 Samuel 23:23"> And after him &#91;is&#93; Eleazar son of Dodo the Ahohite, he &#91;is&#93; among the three mighty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:11–12; 1 Samuel 17:1"> he has been with David in Pas-Dammim, and the Philistines have been gathered there to battle, and a portion of the field is full of barley, and the people have fled from the face of the Philistines,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 144:10; 2 Kings 5:1; 1 Samuel 14:23"> and they station themselves in the midst of the portion, and deliver it, and strike the Philistines, and YHWH saves &#91;with&#93; a great salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:9; 2 Samuel 5:18; Isaiah 17:5"> And three of the thirty heads go down on the rock to David, to the cave of Adullam, and the host of the Philistines is encamping in the Valley of Rephaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Samuel 13:23"> and David &#91;is&#93; then in the fortress, and the station of the Philistines &#91;is&#93; then in Beth-Lehem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 12:3; 2 Samuel 23:15–16"> and David longs and says, “Who gives me water to drink from the well of Beth-Lehem that &#91;is&#93; at the gate!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:13; 1 Samuel 19:5; Acts 20:24"> And the three break through the camp of the Philistines, and draw water from the well of Beth-Lehem that &#91;is&#93; at the gate, and carry and bring &#91;it&#93; to David, and David has not been willing to drink it, and pours it out to YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:17; Psalms 72:14; Judges 9:17"> and says, “Far be it from me, by my God, to do this; do I drink the blood of these men with their lives? For with their lives they have brought it”; and he was not willing to drink it; these &#91;things&#93; the three mighty ones did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:6; 1 Chronicles 2:16; 2 Samuel 20:6"> And Abishai brother of Joab, he has been head of the three: and he is lifting up his spear against three hundred—wounded, and has a name among three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:41; Matthew 13:8"> Of the three he is more honored than the &#91;other&#93; two, and becomes their head; but he has not come to the &#91;first&#93; three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:20–23; 2 Samuel 8:18; Joshua 15:21"> Benaiah son of Jehoiada, son of a man of valor, of great deeds, from Kabzeel: he has struck the two lion-like Moabites, and he has gone down and struck the lion in the midst of the pit, in the day of snow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:7; 1 Samuel 17:4; Deuteronomy 3:11"> And he has struck the man, the Egyptian, a man of &#91;great&#93; measure—five by the cubit—and in the hand of the Egyptian &#91;is&#93; a spear like a weavers’ beam, and he goes down to him with a rod, and violently takes away the spear out of the hand of the Egyptian, and slays him with his own spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> These &#91;things&#93; Benaiah son of Jehoiada has done, and has a name among the three mighty ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:23"> Of the thirty, behold, he &#91;is&#93; honored, but he has not come to the &#91;first&#93; three, and David sets him over his guard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:7; 2 Samuel 21:19; 2 Samuel 2:18–23"> And the mighty ones of the forces &#91;are&#93; Asahel brother of Joab, Elhanan son of Dodo of Beth-Lehem,</span>
