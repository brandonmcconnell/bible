<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:3; 2 Samuel 5:11–16; Ezra 3:7"> And Huram king of Tyre sends messengers to David, and cedar-wood, and craftsmen of walls, and craftsmen of wood, to build a house for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:7; Psalms 89:20–37; 2 Samuel 7:16"> And David knows that YHWH has established him for king over Israel, because of the lifting up on high of his kingdom, for the sake of His people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 5:18–19; 2 Samuel 5:13; Ecclesiastes 7:26–29"> And David takes wives again in Jerusalem, and David begets sons and daughters again;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:5–9; 2 Samuel 5:14; Luke 3:31"> and these &#91;are&#93; the names of the children whom he has in Jerusalem: Shammua, and Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:15; 1 Chronicles 3:6"> and Ibhar, and Elishua, and Elpalet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and Nogah, and Nepheg, and Japhia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:8; 2 Samuel 5:16"> and Elishama, and Beeliada, and Eliphalet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:11; Revelation 11:15–18; Psalms 2:1–6"> And the Philistines hear that David has been anointed for king over all Israel, and all the Philistines go up to seek David, and David hears, and goes out before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:15; 1 Chronicles 14:13; 2 Samuel 23:13"> And the Philistines have come, and rush into the Valley of Rephaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 3:6; 1 Chronicles 14:14; 1 Chronicles 13:3"> and David asks of God, saying, “Do I go up against the Philistines—and have You given them into my hand?” And YHWH says to him, “Go up, and I have given them into your hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:21; Exodus 14:28; 2 Samuel 5:20"> And they go up into Ba‘al-Perazim, and David strikes them there, and David says, “God has broken up my enemies by my hand, like the breaking up of waters”; therefore they have called the name of that place Ba‘al-Perazim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 32:20; 1 Samuel 5:2–6; 2 Kings 19:18"> And they leave their gods there, and David commands, and they are burned with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:9; 1 Kings 20:22; 2 Samuel 5:22–25"> And the Philistines add again, and rush into the valley,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 9:6–7; 1 Chronicles 14:10; Psalms 27:4"> and David asks again of God, and God says to him, “Do not go up after them, turn around from them, and you have come to them from the front &#91;&#91;or in front&#93;&#93; of the mulberries;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 13:4; Acts 2:2; Philippians 2:12–13"> and it comes to pass, when you hear the sound of the stepping at the heads of the mulberries, then you go out into battle, for God has gone out before you to strike the camp of the Philistines.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 15:14; John 13:17; Genesis 6:22"> And David does as God commanded him, and they strike the camp of the Philistines from Gibeon even to Gazer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Joshua 6:27; Exodus 15:14–16"> and the name of David goes out into all the lands, and YHWH has put his fear on all the nations.</span>
