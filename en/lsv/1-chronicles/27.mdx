<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:11; 2 Samuel 23:8"> Over the first division for the first month &#91;is&#93; Jashobeam son of Zabdiel, and twenty-four thousand &#91;are&#93; on his division;</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:9; 1 Chronicles 11:12"> And over the division of the second month &#91;is&#93; Dodai the Ahohite, and Mikloth &#91;is&#93; also president of his division, and twenty-four thousand &#91;are&#93; on his division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:17; 1 Kings 4:4–5"> Head of the third host, for the third month, &#91;is&#93; Benaiah son of Jehoiada, the head priest, and twenty-four thousand &#91;are&#93; on his division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:20–23; 1 Chronicles 11:22–25; 2 Samuel 22:20–23"> This Benaiah &#91;is&#93; a mighty one of the thirty, and over the thirty, and his son Ammizabad &#91;is in&#93; his division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:26; 2 Samuel 23:24; 2 Samuel 2:18–23"> The fourth, for the fourth month, &#91;is&#93; Asahel brother of Joab, and his son Zebadiah after him, and twenty-four thousand &#91;are&#93; on his division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:25; 1 Chronicles 26:29"> The fifth, for the fifth month, &#91;is&#93; the prince Shamhuth the Izrahite, and twenty-four thousand &#91;are&#93; on his division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:28; 2 Samuel 23:26"> The sixth, for the sixth month, &#91;is&#93; Ira son of Ikkesh the Tekoite, and twenty-four thousand &#91;are&#93; on his division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:26"> The seventh, for the seventh month, &#91;is&#93; Helez the Pelonite, of the sons of Ephraim, and twenty-four thousand &#91;are&#93; on his division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:18; 1 Chronicles 11:29; Numbers 26:20"> The eighth, for the eighth month, &#91;is&#93; Sibbecai the Hushathite, of the Zerahite, and twenty-four thousand &#91;are&#93; on his division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:28; 2 Samuel 23:27"> The ninth, for the ninth month, &#91;is&#93; Abiezer the Antothite, of the Benjamite, and twenty-four thousand &#91;are&#93; on his division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:30; 2 Samuel 23:28"> The tenth, for the tenth month, &#91;is&#93; Maharai the Netophathite, of the Zerahite, and twenty-four thousand &#91;are&#93; on his division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:31; 2 Samuel 23:30"> Eleventh, for the eleventh month, &#91;is&#93; Benaiah the Pirathonite, of the sons of Ephraim, and twenty-four thousand &#91;are&#93; on his division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:29; 1 Chronicles 4:13; Judges 3:9"> The twelfth, for the twelfth month, &#91;is&#93; Heldai the Netophathite, of Othniel, and twenty-four thousand &#91;are&#93; on his division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And over the tribes of Israel: the leader of the Reubenite &#91;is&#93; Eliezer son of Zichri; of the Simeonite, Shephatiah son of Maachah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:30; 1 Chronicles 24:31; 1 Chronicles 12:27–28"> of the Levite, Hashabiah son of Kemuel; of the Aaronite, Zadok;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:6; 1 Samuel 17:29; 1 Samuel 17:13"> of Judah, Elihu, of the brothers of David; of Issachar, Omri son of Michael;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> of Zebulun, Ishmaiah son of Obadiah; of Naphtali, Jerimoth son of Azriel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> of the sons of Ephraim, Hoshea son of Azaziah; of the half of the tribe of Manasseh, Joel son of Pedaiah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:27; 1 Samuel 14:50–51; 2 Samuel 3:37"> of the half of Manasseh in Gilead, Iddo son of Zechariah; of Benjamin, Jaasiel son of Abner; of Dan, Azareel son of Jeroham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:1"> These &#91;are the&#93; heads of the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; 1 Chronicles 21:2–5; Hebrews 11:12"> But David has not taken up their number from a son of twenty years and under, for YHWH had said &#91;He would&#93; multiply Israel as the stars of the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:1–17; 2 Samuel 24:1–15"> Joab son of Zeruiah has begun to number, but has not finished; and there is wrath against Israel for this, and the number has not gone up in the account of the Chronicles of King David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:2; Exodus 1:11; 2 Chronicles 26:10"> And Azmaveth son of Adiel &#91;is&#93; over the treasures of the king; and Jonathan son of Uzziah &#91;is&#93; over the treasures in the field, in the cities, and in the villages, and in the towers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and Ezri son of Chelub &#91;is&#93; over workmen of the field for the service of the ground;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and Shimei the Ramathite &#91;is&#93; over the vineyards; and Zabdi the Shiphmite &#91;is&#93; over what &#91;is&#93; in the vineyards for the treasures of wine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 10:27; 2 Chronicles 1:15; 1 Kings 4:7"> and Ba‘al-Hanan the Gederite &#91;is&#93; over the olives and the sycamores that &#91;are&#93; in the low country; and Joash &#91;is&#93; over the treasures of oil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:16; Isaiah 65:10"> and Shitrai the Sharonite &#91;is&#93; over the herds that are feeding in Sharon; and Shaphat son of Adlai &#91;is&#93; over the herds in the valleys;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 47:6; Job 1:3"> and Obil the Ishmaelite &#91;is&#93; over the camels; and Jehdeiah the Meronothite &#91;is&#93; over the donkeys;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:10"> and Jaziz the Hagerite &#91;is&#93; over the flock; all these &#91;are&#93; heads of the substance that King David has.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:21; 2 Samuel 13:3; 1 Chronicles 11:11"> And Jonathan, David’s uncle, &#91;is&#93; counselor, a man of understanding, &#91;and&#93; he is also a scribe; and Jehiel son of Hachmoni &#91;is&#93; with the sons of the king;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:12; 2 Samuel 15:37; 2 Samuel 15:32"> and Ahithophel &#91;is&#93; counselor to the king; and Hushai the Archite &#91;is&#93; the friend of the king;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:6; 1 Kings 1:7; 1 Chronicles 27:5"> and after Ahithophel &#91;is&#93; Jehoiada son of Benaiah, and Abiathar; and the head of the host of the king &#91;is&#93; Joab.</span>
