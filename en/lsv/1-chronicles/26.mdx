<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:19; Numbers 26:9–11; Psalms 44:1"> For the divisions of the gatekeepers, of the Korahites: Meshelemiah son of Kore, of the sons of Asaph;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:21"> and sons of Meshelemiah: Zechariah the firstborn, Jediael the second, Zebadiah the third, Jathniel the fourth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Elam the fifth, Jehohanan the sixth, Elioenai the seventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 15:24; 1 Chronicles 15:18"> And moreover, sons of Obed-Edom: Shemaiah the firstborn, Jehozabad the second, Joah the third, and Sacar the fourth, and Nethaneel the fifth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 128:1"> Ammiel the sixth, Issachar the seventh, Peullethai the eighth, for God has blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:28; 1 Timothy 6:12; Judges 6:12"> And sons have been born to his son Shemaiah, who are ruling throughout the house of their father, for they &#91;are&#93; mighty men of valor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Sons of Shemaiah: Othni, and Rephael, and Obed, &#91;and&#93; Elzabad; his brothers Elihu and Semachiah &#91;are&#93; sons of valor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:6; 1 Peter 4:11; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11"> All these &#91;are&#93; of the sons of Obed-Edom; they, and their sons, and their brothers, men of valor with might for service—sixty-two of Obed-Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:14; 1 Chronicles 26:1"> And sons and brothers of Meshelemiah, sons of valor—eighteen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2"> and of Hosah, of the sons of Merari, &#91;are these&#93; sons: Shimri the head (though he was not firstborn, yet his father sets him for head),</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Hilkiah the second, Tebaliah the third, Zechariah the fourth; all the sons and brothers of Hosah—thirteen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8"> For these &#91;are&#93; the divisions of the gatekeepers, of the heads of the mighty ones, &#91;with&#93; charges just as their brothers, to minister in the house of YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8; 1 Chronicles 24:31; 1 Chronicles 24:5"> and they cause lots to fall, the small as well as the great, according to the house of their fathers, for every gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the lot falls eastward to Shelemiah; and &#91;for&#93; his son Zechariah, a counselor with understanding, they cause lots to fall, and his lot goes out northward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:24; 1 Chronicles 26:17"> to Obed-Edom southward, and to his sons, the house of the gatherings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:24; 1 Chronicles 26:10–12; 1 Kings 10:5"> to Shuppim and to Hosah to the west, with Shallecheth Gate, in the highway, the ascent, watch next to watch;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 9:24"> to the east &#91;are&#93; six Levites, to the north &#91;are&#93; four each day, to the south &#91;are&#93; four each day, and for the gatherings, two by two;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 23:11"> at Parbar, to the west, &#91;are&#93; four at the highway, two at Parbar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 16:11"> These are the divisions of the gatekeepers, of the sons of the Korahite, and of the sons of Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:22; 1 Chronicles 28:12–19; 1 Chronicles 22:14–16"> And of the Levites, Ahijah &#91;is&#93; over the treasures of the house of God, even for the treasures of the holy things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:7; 1 Chronicles 6:17; 1 Chronicles 29:8"> Sons of Laadan, sons of the Gershonite, of Laadan, heads of the fathers of Laadan the Gershonite: Jehieli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:8; Nehemiah 10:38; 1 Chronicles 26:20"> Sons of Jehieli: Zetham and his brother Joel, over the treasures of the house of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 3:27; 1 Chronicles 23:12; Numbers 3:19"> Of the Amramite, of the Izharite, of the Hebronite, of &#91;the&#93; Uzzielite—</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:20; 1 Chronicles 23:15–16"> and Shebuel son of Gershom, son of Moses, &#91;is&#93; president over the treasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:17–18; 1 Chronicles 23:15; Exodus 18:4"> And his brothers, of Eliezer: Rehabiah his son, and Jeshaiah his son, and Joram his son, and Zichri his son, and Shelomith his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:11; 1 Chronicles 29:2–9; 1 Chronicles 22:14"> This Shelomith and his brothers &#91;are&#93; over all the treasures of the holy things that David the king, and heads of the fathers, even heads of thousands, and of hundreds, and heads of the host, sanctified;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 12:14; Joshua 6:19; Nehemiah 10:32–34"> from the battles and from the spoil, they sanctified to strengthen the house of YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Samuel 17:55; 2 Samuel 10:9–14"> and all that Samuel the seer, and Saul son of Kish, and Abner son of Ner, and Joab son of Zeruiah sanctified, everyone sanctifying &#91;anything—it is&#93; by the side of Shelomith and his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:4; Nehemiah 11:16; 2 Chronicles 34:13"> Of the Izharite, Chenaniah and his sons &#91;are&#93; for the outward work over Israel, for officers and for judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:17; 1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> Of the Hebronite, Hashabiah and his brothers, one thousand and seven hundred sons of valor, &#91;are&#93; over the inspection of Israel, beyond the Jordan westward, for all the work of YHWH, and for the service of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:19; Joshua 21:39; 1 Chronicles 29:27"> Of the Hebronite, Jerijah &#91;is&#93; the head of the Hebronite, according to the generations of his fathers. In the fortieth year of the reign of David they have been sought out, and there are found among them mighty men of valor, in Jazer of Gilead—</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:11; 1 Chronicles 12:37; 1 Chronicles 23:24"> and his brothers, two thousand and seven hundred sons of valor, &#91;are&#93; heads of the fathers, and King David appoints them over the Reubenite, and the Gadite, and the half of the tribe of the Manassite, for every matter of God and matter of the king.</span>
