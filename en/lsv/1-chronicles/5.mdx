<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; Genesis 29:32; Genesis 48:15–22"> As for sons of Reuben, firstborn of Israel—for he &#91;is&#93; the firstborn, and on account of his profaning the bed of his father his birthright has been given to the sons of Joseph son of Israel, and &#91;he is&#93; not to be reckoned by genealogy for the birthright,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:6; Micah 5:2; Hebrews 7:14"> for Judah has been mighty over his brother, and for leader above him, and the birthright &#91;is&#93; to Joseph—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:9; Exodus 6:14; Numbers 26:5–9"> sons of Reuben, firstborn of Israel: Enoch, and Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Sons of Joel: Shemaiah his son, Gog his son, Shimei his son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Micah his son, Reaiah his son, Ba‘al his son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; 2 Kings 15:29; 2 Chronicles 28:20"> Beerah his son, whom Tilgath-Pilneser king of Asshur removed; he &#91;is&#93; prince of the Reubenite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:17"> And his brothers, by their families, in the genealogy of their generations, &#91;are&#93; heads: Jeiel, and Zechariah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:4; Numbers 32:34; Numbers 32:38"> and Bela son of Azaz, son of Shema, son of Joel—he is dwelling in Aroer, even to Nebo and Ba‘al-Meon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 22:8–9"> and he dwelt at the east even to the entering in of the wilderness, even from the Euphrates River, for their livestock were multiplied in the land of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 21:9; 1 Chronicles 5:18–21; Psalms 83:6"> And they have made war with the Hagarites in the days of Saul, who fall by their hand, and they dwell in their tents over all the face of the east of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 13:11; Joshua 13:24–28; Deuteronomy 3:10–17"> And the sons of Gad have dwelt opposite them in the land of Bashan to Salcah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Joel the head, and Shapham the second, and Jaanai and Shaphat in Bashan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and their brothers of the house of their fathers &#91;are&#93; Michael, and Meshullam, and Sheba, and Jorai, and Jachan, and Zia, and Heber—seven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> These &#91;are&#93; sons of Abihail son of Huri, son of Jaroah, son of Gilead, son of Michael, son of Jeshishai, son of Jahdo, son of Buz;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Ahi son of Abdiel, son of Guni, &#91;is&#93; head of the house of their fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:29; Song of Songs 2:1; Isaiah 35:2"> and they dwell in Gilead in Bashan, and in her small towns, and in all outskirts of Sharon, on their outskirts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 14:16; 2 Kings 14:28; 2 Kings 15:32"> all of them reckoned themselves by genealogy in the days of Jotham king of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:3; Joshua 4:12–13"> Sons of Reuben, and the Gadite, and the half of the tribe of Manasseh, of sons of valor, men carrying shield and sword, and treading bow, and taught in battle, &#91;are&#93; forty-four thousand and seven hundred and sixty, going out to the host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 25:15; 1 Chronicles 1:31; 1 Chronicles 5:10"> And they make war with the Hagarites, and Jetur, and Naphish, and Nodab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 22:4–5; Psalms 9:10; 2 Chronicles 18:31"> and they are helped against them, and the Hagarites are given into their hand, and all who &#91;are&#93; with them, for they cried to God in battle, and He received their plea, because they trusted in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 18:13; Numbers 31:35"> And they take their livestock captive—fifty thousand of their camels, and two hundred and fifty thousand sheep, and two thousand donkeys, and one hundred thousand of mankind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:8; Romans 8:31; Joshua 23:10"> for many have fallen pierced, for the battle &#91;is&#93; of God; and they dwell in their stead until the expulsion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:8–9; Song of Songs 4:8; Psalms 133:3"> And the sons of the half of the tribe of Manasseh dwelt in the land, from Bashan to Ba‘al-Hermon, and Senir, and Mount Hermon; they have multiplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:38"> And these &#91;are&#93; heads of the house of their fathers: even Epher, and Ishi, and Eliel, and Azriel, and Jeremiah, and Hodaviah, and Jahdiel, men mighty in valor, men of renown, heads of the house of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 17:5; Psalms 106:34–39; Exodus 34:15"> And they trespass against the God of their fathers, and go whoring after the gods of the peoples of the land whom God destroyed from their presence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 15:19; 2 Kings 17:6; 2 Kings 15:29"> and the God of Israel stirs up the spirit of Pul king of Asshur, and the spirit of Tilgath-Pilneser king of Asshur, and he removes them—even the Reubenite, and the Gadite, and the half of the tribe of Manasseh—and brings them to Halah, and Habor, and Hara, and the River of Gozan to this day.</span>
