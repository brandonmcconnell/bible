<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:1; 1 Chronicles 17:1–5; Psalms 132:5"> And he makes houses for himself in the City of David, and prepares a place for the Ark of God, and stretches out a tent for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Deuteronomy 31:9; Numbers 18:1–8"> Then David said, “None &#91;are&#93; to carry the Ark of God, except the Levites, for YHWH has fixed on them to carry the Ark of God and to serve Him for all time.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 8:1; 1 Chronicles 13:5; 1 Chronicles 15:1"> And David assembles all Israel to Jerusalem, to bring up the Ark of YHWH to its place that he had prepared for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 6:16–22; 1 Chronicles 12:26–28; 1 Chronicles 6:16–30"> And David gathers the sons of Aaron and the Levites—</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:22–24"> of sons of Kohath: Uriel the chief and one hundred and twenty of his brothers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:29–30"> of sons of Merari: Asaiah the chief and two hundred and twenty of his brothers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:11; 1 Chronicles 23:8"> of sons of Gershom: Joel the chief and one hundred and thirty of his brothers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 6:22"> of sons of Elizaphan: Shemaiah the chief and two hundred of his brothers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 26:30–31"> of sons of Hebron: Eliel the chief and eighty of his brothers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> of sons of Uzziel: Amminadab the chief and one hundred and twelve of his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:28; 1 Kings 2:35; 1 Samuel 22:20–23"> And David calls to Zadok and to Abiathar the priests, and to the Levites, to Uriel, Asaiah, and Joel, Shemaiah, and Eliel, and Amminadab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:6; Exodus 19:14–15; 1 Chronicles 15:14"> and says to them, “You &#91;are&#93; heads of the fathers of the Levites; sanctify yourselves, you and your brothers, and you have brought up the Ark of YHWH, God of Israel, to &#91;the place&#93; I have prepared for it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:3; 1 Corinthians 14:40; 1 John 1:8–10"> because you &#91;did&#93; not &#91;do it&#93; at the first, our God YHWH made a breach on us, because we did not seek Him according to the ordinance.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:15; Joel 2:16–17; 2 Chronicles 29:34"> And the priests and the Levites sanctify themselves to bring up the Ark of YHWH, God of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Numbers 7:9; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> and sons of the Levites carry the Ark of God, as Moses commanded, according to the word of YHWH, above them with poles on their shoulder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; 2 Chronicles 5:13; 1 Chronicles 16:42"> And David says to the heads of the Levites to appoint their brothers the singers, with instruments of song, psalteries, and harps, and cymbals, sounding, to lift up with the voice for joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:39; 1 Chronicles 6:44; 1 Chronicles 6:33"> And the Levites appoint Heman son of Joel, and of his brothers, Asaph son of Berechiah, and of the sons of Merari their brothers, Ethan son of Kushaiah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4; 1 Chronicles 26:8; 1 Chronicles 13:14"> and with them their brothers, the seconds &#91;in rank&#93;: Zechariah, Ben, and Jaaziel, and Shemiramoth, and Jehiel, and Unni, Eliab, and Benaiah, and Maaseiah, and Mattithiah, and Elipheleh, and Mikneiah; and Obed-Edom and Jeiel the gatekeepers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:6; 1 Chronicles 16:5; 1 Chronicles 13:8"> and the singers, Heman, Asaph, and Ethan, &#91;are&#93; to sound with cymbals of bronze;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 46:1–11"> and Zechariah, and Aziel, and Shemiramoth, and Jeheil, and Unni, and Eliab, and Maaseiah, and Benaiah, with psalteries over the girls’ &#91;voices&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5; 1 Chronicles 25:6–7; Psalms 81:1–2"> and Mattithiah, and Elipheleh, and Mikneiah, and Obed-Edom, and Jeiel, and Azaziah, to oversee with harps on the eighth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:27; 1 Chronicles 15:16; 1 Chronicles 25:7–8"> And Chenaniah, head of the Levites, &#91;is&#93; over the burden; he instructs about the burden, for he &#91;is&#93; intelligent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 84:10; 2 Kings 22:4; 1 Chronicles 9:21–23"> And Berechiah and Elkanah &#91;are&#93; gatekeepers for the Ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:6; Numbers 10:8; 1 Chronicles 15:28"> And Shebaniah, and Joshaphat, and Nethaneel, and Amasai, and Zechariah, and Benaiah, and Eliezer the priests, are blowing with trumpets before the Ark of God; and Obed-Edom and Jehiah &#91;are&#93; gatekeepers for the Ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:12–23; Micah 5:2; Philippians 3:3"> And it is David, and &#91;the&#93; elderly of Israel, and the heads of the thousands, who are going to bring up the Ark of the Covenant of YHWH from the house of Obed-Edom with joy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 23:29; 1 Chronicles 29:14; Psalms 66:13–15"> and it comes to pass, in God’s helping the Levites carrying the Ark of the Covenant of YHWH, that they sacrifice seven bullocks and seven rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:18; 2 Samuel 6:14"> And David is wrapped in an upper robe of fine linen, and all the Levites who are carrying the Ark, and the singers, and Chenaniah head of the burden of the singers; and an Ephod of linen &#91;is&#93; on David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; 1 Chronicles 15:16; 2 Chronicles 5:12–13"> And all of Israel is bringing up the Ark of the Covenant of YHWH with shouting, and with the sound of a horn, and with trumpets, and with cymbals, sounding with psalteries and harps,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:20–23; 2 Samuel 6:16; Deuteronomy 31:26"> and it comes to pass, the Ark of the Covenant of YHWH is entering into the City of David, and Michal daughter of Saul is looking through the window, and sees King David dancing and playing, and despises him in her heart.</span>
