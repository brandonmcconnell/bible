<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:1; Ezra 6:16–18; 2 Chronicles 5:6–7"> And they bring in the Ark of God, and set it up in the midst of the tent that David has stretched out for it, and they bring burnt-offerings and peace-offerings near before God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 47:10; Genesis 14:19; Leviticus 1:3"> and David ceases from offering the burnt-offering and the peace-offerings, and blesses the people in the Name of YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:10; Ezekiel 45:17; 2 Chronicles 35:7–8"> and gives a portion to every man of Israel, both man and woman: to each a cake of bread, and a measure of wine, and a grape-cake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:27–32; Psalms 72:18; Psalms 103:2"> And he puts before the Ark of YHWH, those ministering from the Levites, even to make mention of, and to thank, and to give praise to YHWH, God of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:39; 2 Chronicles 29:25; 1 Chronicles 15:16–24"> Asaph the head, and his second Zechariah; Jeiel, and Shemiramoth, and Jehiel, and Mattithiah, and Eliab, and Benaiah, and Obed-Edom, and Jeiel, with instruments of psalteries, and with harps; and Asaph is sounding with cymbals;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:12; 2 Chronicles 29:26–28; 2 Chronicles 5:12–13"> and Benaiah and Jahaziel the priests &#91;are&#93; continually &#91;sounding&#93; with trumpets before the Ark of the Covenant of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:1; Psalms 18:1; 2 Samuel 23:1–2"> On that day, at that time, David has given at the beginning to give thanks to YHWH by the hand of Asaph and his brothers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 105:1–15; Isaiah 12:4; 2 Kings 19:19"> “Give thanks to YHWH, call on His Name, &#124;&#124; Make His doings known among the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Psalms 96:1–3; Psalms 98:1–4"> Sing to Him, sing psalms to Him, &#124;&#124; Meditate on all His wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; Jeremiah 29:13; Psalms 34:2"> Boast yourselves in His Holy Name, &#124;&#124; The heart of those seeking YHWH rejoices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:8–9; Zephaniah 2:2–3; Psalms 68:35"> Seek YHWH and His strength, &#124;&#124; Seek His face continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 111:4; Psalms 119:20; Psalms 103:2"> Remember His wonders that He did, &#124;&#124; His signs, and the judgments of His mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Exodus 19:5–6; Psalms 135:4"> O seed of Israel, His servant, &#124;&#124; O sons of Jacob, His chosen ones!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 48:10–11; Isaiah 26:9; Exodus 15:2"> He &#91;is&#93; our God YHWH, &#124;&#124; His judgments &#91;are&#93; in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 105:8; Malachi 4:4; Deuteronomy 7:9"> Remember His covenant for all time, &#124;&#124; The word He commanded—To one thousand generations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 26:3; Genesis 17:2; Genesis 35:11"> Which He has made with Abraham, &#124;&#124; And His oath—to Isaac,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Genesis 35:11–12; Joshua 24:11–13"> And He establishes it to Jacob for a statute, &#124;&#124; To Israel &#91;for&#93; a perpetual covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Micah 2:5; Genesis 17:8"> Saying, To you I give the land of Canaan, &#124;&#124; The portion of your inheritance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Deuteronomy 7:7; Acts 7:5"> When you were few in number, &#124;&#124; As a little thing, and sojourners in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 46:6; Genesis 20:1; Genesis 46:3"> And they go up and down, &#124;&#124; From nation to nation, &#124;&#124; And from a kingdom to another people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 12:17; Genesis 20:3; Exodus 7:15–18"> He has not permitted any to oppress them, &#124;&#124; And reproves kings on their account:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 20:7; Psalms 105:15; Genesis 49:8–10"> Do not come against My anointed ones, &#124;&#124; And do no evil against My prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 96:1–13; Psalms 71:15; Isaiah 51:6–8"> Sing to YHWH, all the earth, &#124;&#124; Proclaim His salvation from day to day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2–6; Daniel 4:1–3; Psalms 22:27"> Recount His glory among nations, &#124;&#124; His wonders among all the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 89:7; Psalms 76:7; Psalms 66:3–5"> For great &#91;is&#93; YHWH, and greatly praised, &#124;&#124; And He &#91;is&#93; fearful above all gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 102:25; Leviticus 19:4; Isaiah 42:5"> For all gods of the peoples &#91;are&#93; nothing, &#124;&#124; And YHWH has made the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 96:6; Psalms 28:7–8"> Splendor and majesty &#91;are&#93; before Him, &#124;&#124; Strength and joy &#91;are&#93; in His place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:10; Philippians 4:13; Psalms 100:1–2"> Ascribe to YHWH, you families of peoples, &#124;&#124; Ascribe to YHWH glory and strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 100:4; Psalms 96:9; Isaiah 6:3"> Ascribe to YHWH the glory of His Name, &#124;&#124; Lift up a present, and come before Him. Bow yourselves to YHWH, &#124;&#124; In the beauty of holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 33:9; Psalms 148:5–6; Hebrews 1:3"> Be pained before Him, all the earth; Also, the world is established, &#124;&#124; It is not moved!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 96:10; Revelation 19:6; Psalms 97:1"> The heavens rejoice, and the earth is glad, &#124;&#124; And they say among nations, YHWH has reigned!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 148:9–10; Isaiah 44:23; Psalms 98:7–8"> The sea roars, and its fullness, &#124;&#124; The field exults, and all that &#91;is&#93; in it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 98:9; Psalms 96:12–13; Revelation 11:17–18"> Then trees of the forest sing, &#124;&#124; From the presence of YHWH, &#124;&#124; For He has come to judge the earth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 106:1; 2 Chronicles 7:3; Psalms 107:1"> Give thanks to YHWH, for &#91;He is&#93; good, &#124;&#124; For His kindness &#91;is&#93; for all time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 106:47–48; 1 Peter 2:9; Psalms 14:7"> And say, Save us, O God of our salvation, &#124;&#124; And gather us, and deliver us from the nations, &#124;&#124; To give thanks to Your Holy Name, &#124;&#124; To triumph in Your praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 8:15; Psalms 72:18–19; 1 Kings 8:56"> Blessed &#91;is&#93; YHWH, God of Israel, &#124;&#124; From age to age”; And all the people say, “Amen,” and have given praise to YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; Ezra 3:4; 1 Chronicles 16:4–6"> And he leaves there &#91;those&#93; of Asaph and of his brothers, before the Ark of the Covenant of YHWH, to minister before the Ark continually, according to the matter of a day in its day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:10; 1 Chronicles 13:14; 1 Chronicles 25:3"> both Obed-Edom and their sixty-eight brothers, and Obed-Edom son of Jeduthun, and Hosah, for gatekeepers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 3:4; 1 Chronicles 15:11; 2 Chronicles 1:3–4"> and Zadok the priest, and his brothers the priests, before the Dwelling Place of YHWH, in a high place that &#91;is&#93; in Gibeon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 28:3–8; Exodus 29:38–42; 2 Chronicles 31:3"> to cause burnt-offerings to ascend continually to YHWH on the altar of burnt-offering, morning and evening, and for all that is written in the Law of YHWH that He charged on Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:13; 1 Chronicles 25:1–6; 2 Chronicles 20:21"> And with them &#91;are&#93; Heman and Jeduthun, and the rest of those chosen, who were defined by name, to give thanks to YHWH, for His kindness &#91;is&#93; for all time;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 150:3–6; 1 Chronicles 25:6–7; 2 Chronicles 29:25–28"> and with them—Heman and Jeduthun—&#91;are&#93; trumpets and cymbals for those sounding, and instruments of the song of God, and the sons of Jeduthun &#91;are&#93; at the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:19–20; Genesis 18:19; Psalms 101:2"> And all the people go, each to his house, and David turns around to bless his house.</span>
