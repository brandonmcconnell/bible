<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1; 1 Chronicles 21:18–28; Genesis 28:17"> And David says, “This is the house of YHWH God, and this &#91;is&#93; the altar for burnt-offering for Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:17; 1 Kings 9:20–21; 1 Kings 5:17–18"> And David says to gather the sojourners who &#91;are&#93; in the land of Israel, and appoints hewers to hew hewn-stones to build a house of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:14; 1 Chronicles 29:2; 1 Kings 7:47"> And David has prepared iron in abundance for nails for leaves of the gates and for couplings, and bronze in abundance—there is no weighing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 5:6–10; Ezra 3:7; 2 Chronicles 2:3"> and cedar-trees without number, for the Zidonians and the Tyrians brought in cedar-trees in abundance to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 3:7; 1 Chronicles 29:1; John 3:30"> And David says, “My son Solomon &#91;is&#93; a youth and tender, and the house to be built for YHWH &#91;is&#93; to be made exceedingly great, for renown and for beauty to all the lands; now let me prepare for it”; and David prepares in abundance before his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 27:23; Acts 20:25–31; Acts 1:2"> And he calls for his son Solomon, and charges him to build a house for YHWH, God of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 132:5; 1 Kings 8:16–20; 2 Chronicles 6:7–9"> and David says to his son Solomon, “As for me, it has been with my heart to build a house for the Name of my God YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 5:3; 1 Chronicles 28:3; 1 Chronicles 17:4–10"> and the word of YHWH &#91;is&#93; against me, saying, You have shed blood in abundance, and you have made great wars; you do not build a house for My Name, for you have shed much blood to the earth before Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 4:25; 1 Kings 5:4; 1 Kings 4:20"> Behold, a son is born to you; he is a man of rest, and I have given rest to him from all his surrounding enemies, for Solomon is his name, and I give peace and quietness to Israel in his days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 1:5; 2 Samuel 7:13–14; 1 Chronicles 17:12–14"> he builds a house for My Name, and he is to Me for a son, and I &#91;am&#93; to him for a father, and I have established the throne of his kingdom over Israel for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:16; 1 Chronicles 28:20; 2 Timothy 4:22"> Now my son, YHWH is with you, and you have prospered, and have built the house of your God YHWH, as He spoke concerning you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 1:5; Proverbs 14:8; 1 Kings 3:9–12"> Only, may YHWH give wisdom and understanding to you, and charge you concerning Israel, even to keep the Law of your God YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 1:6–9; 1 Chronicles 28:20; 1 Chronicles 28:7"> then you prosper, if you observe to do the statutes and the judgments that YHWH charged Moses with concerning Israel; be strong and courageous; do not fear, nor be cast down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:3; 2 Kings 25:16; 1 Chronicles 29:4–7"> And behold, in my affliction I have prepared for the house of YHWH one hundred thousand talents of gold, and one million talents of silver; and of bronze and of iron there is no weighing, for it has been in abundance; and I have prepared wood and stones, and you add to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 28:6; Exodus 35:32; 1 Kings 7:14"> And with you in abundance &#91;are&#93; workmen, hewers and craftsmen of stone and of wood, and every skillful man for every work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:11; Philippians 4:13; Ephesians 5:14"> Of the gold, of the silver, and of the bronze, and of the iron, there is no number; arise and do, and YHWH is with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:1–6; 3 John 1:8; 1 Chronicles 29:6"> And David gives charge to all heads of Israel to give help to his son Solomon, &#91;saying&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9; 1 Chronicles 23:25; 2 Samuel 7:1"> “Is your God YHWH not with you? Indeed, He has given rest to you all around, for He has given the inhabitants of the land into my hand, and the land has been subdued before His people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Deuteronomy 4:29; 1 Chronicles 16:11"> Now give your heart and your soul to seek for your God YHWH, and rise and build the sanctuary of YHWH God, to bring in the Ark of the Covenant of YHWH, and the holy vessels of God, to the house that is built for the Name of YHWH.”</span>
