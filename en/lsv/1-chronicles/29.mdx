<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:5; 1 Kings 3:7; 2 Chronicles 13:7"> And David the king says to all the assembly, “My son Solomon—the one on whom God has fixed—&#91;is&#93; young and tender, and the work &#91;is&#93; great, for the palace is not for man, but for YHWH God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:3–5; 2 Corinthians 8:3; Isaiah 54:11–12"> and with all my power I have prepared for the house of my God, the gold for &#91;things of&#93; gold, and the silver for &#91;things of&#93; silver, and the bronze for &#91;things of&#93; bronze, the iron for &#91;things of&#93; iron, and the wood for &#91;things of&#93; wood, shoham stones, and settings, and stones of painting and of diverse colors, and all &#91;kinds of&#93; precious stone, and stones of white marble, in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:24; Psalms 27:4; Psalms 26:8"> And again, because of my delighting in the house of my God, the substance I have—a peculiar treasure of gold and silver—I have given for the house of my God, even over and above all I have prepared for the holy house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 9:28; 1 Chronicles 22:14; Job 28:16"> three thousand talents of gold, of the gold of Ophir, and seven thousand talents of refined silver, to overlay the walls of the houses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 7:2–3; Exodus 35:5–9; Ezra 7:15–16"> even gold for &#91;things of&#93; gold, and silver for &#91;things of&#93; silver, and for all the work by the hand of craftsmen; and who &#91;is&#93; he that is offering to willingly consecrate his hand to YHWH today?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Chronicles 27:25–1; Isaiah 60:3–10"> And the heads of the fathers, and the heads of the tribes of Israel, and the heads of the thousands, and of the hundreds, and of the heads of the work of the king, offer willingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:70–71; Ezra 2:69"> And they give for the service of the house of God five thousand talents and ten thousand drams of gold, and ten thousand talents of silver, and eighteen thousand talents of bronze, and one hundred thousand talents of iron;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:8; 1 Chronicles 26:21–22"> and he with whom stones are found has given &#91;them&#93; to the treasury of the house of YHWH, by the hand of Jehiel the Gershonite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7–8; 2 Corinthians 8:3; 1 Kings 8:61"> And the people rejoice because of their offering willingly, for with a perfect heart they have offered willingly to YHWH; and David the king has also rejoiced &#91;with&#93; great joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 1:3; 1 Peter 1:3; Romans 1:7"> And David blesses YHWH before the eyes of all the assembly, and David says, “Blessed &#91;are&#93; You, YHWH, God of Israel, our Father, from age even to age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Daniel 4:34–35; 1 Timothy 1:17"> To You, O YHWH, &#91;is&#93; the greatness, and the might, and the beauty, and the victory, and the splendor, because of all in the heavens and in the earth; to You, O YHWH, &#91;is&#93; the kingdom, and He who is lifting up Himself over all for head;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 3:16; 2 Chronicles 20:6; Deuteronomy 8:18"> and the riches, and the honor &#91;are&#93; from before You, and You are ruling over all, and in Your hand &#91;is&#93; power and might, and &#91;it is&#93; in Your hand to make great and to give strength to all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 106:1; 2 Corinthians 9:15; 2 Corinthians 8:16"> And now, our God, we are giving thanks to You, and giving praise to Your beautiful Name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:17; 2 Corinthians 3:5; Romans 11:36"> indeed, because who &#91;am&#93; I, and who &#91;are&#93; my people, that we retain power to offer thus willingly? But the whole &#91;is&#93; from You, and we have given to You out of Your &#91;own&#93; hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 144:4; Leviticus 25:23; Psalms 102:11"> for we &#91;are&#93; sojourners and settlers before You, like all our fathers; our days on the land &#91;are&#93; as a shadow, and there is none abiding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:14; Psalms 24:1; 2 Chronicles 31:10"> O our God YHWH, all this store &#91;with&#93; which we have prepared to build a house for You, for Your Holy Name, &#91;is&#93; out of Your &#91;own&#93; hand, and the whole &#91;is&#93; of You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Proverbs 11:20; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> And I have known, my God, that You are trying the heart, and desire uprightness; I, in the uprightness of my heart, have willingly offered all these; and now, I have seen Your people who are found here with joy to offer willingly to You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Jeremiah 10:23; Hebrews 13:21"> O YHWH, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, our fathers, keep this for all time for the imagination of the thoughts of the heart of Your people, and prepare their heart for You;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 72:1; 1 Chronicles 28:9; James 1:17"> and give a perfect heart to my son Solomon, to keep Your commands, Your testimonies, and Your statutes, and to do the whole, even to build the palace &#91;for&#93; which I have prepared.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 95:6; Exodus 4:31; Psalms 135:19–21"> And David says to all the assembly, “Now bless your God YHWH”; and all the assembly blesses YHWH, God of their fathers, and bows and pays respect to YHWH and to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:4–9; Numbers 15:7; Ezra 6:17"> And they sacrifice sacrifices to YHWH, and cause burnt-offerings to ascend to YHWH on that next day: one thousand bullocks, one thousand rams, one thousand lambs, and their oblations, and sacrifices in abundance for all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 2:35; 1 Chronicles 23:1; Exodus 24:11"> And they eat and drink before YHWH on that day with great joy, and cause Solomon son of David to reign a second time, and anoint &#91;him&#93; before YHWH for leader, and Zadok for priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 2:12; 1 Chronicles 28:5; Romans 13:1"> And Solomon sits on the throne of YHWH for king instead of his father David, and prospers, and all of Israel listens to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3–9; Ezekiel 17:18; Genesis 24:2"> and all the heads, and the mighty men, and also all the sons of King David have given a hand under Solomon the king;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:12; 2 Chronicles 1:1; Ecclesiastes 2:9"> and YHWH makes Solomon exceedingly great before the eyes of all Israel, and puts on him the splendor of the kingdom that has not been on any king over Israel before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:14; Psalms 78:71–72"> And David son of Jesse has reigned over all Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 2:11; 2 Samuel 5:4–5; 1 Chronicles 3:4"> and the days that he has reigned over Israel &#91;are&#93; forty years; he reigned in Hebron seven years, and he reigned in Jerusalem thirty-three;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Acts 13:36; 1 Chronicles 23:1"> and he dies in a good old age, satisfied with days, riches, and honor, and his son Solomon reigns in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 2 Samuel 12:1–7; 1 Chronicles 21:9–11"> And the matters of David the king, the first and the last, behold, they are written beside the matters of Samuel the seer, and beside the matters of Nathan the prophet, and beside the matters of Gad the seer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Daniel 4:23; Daniel 2:21"> with all his reign, and his might, and the times that went over him, and over Israel, and over all kingdoms of the lands.</span>
