<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:1; 2 Samuel 12:26–31; Amos 1:14"> And it comes to pass, at the time of the turn of the year—at the time of the going out of the messengers—that Joab leads out the force of the host, and destroys the land of the sons of Ammon, and comes in and besieges Rabbah—David is abiding in Jerusalem—and Joab strikes Rabbah, and breaks it down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:30–31; 2 Samuel 8:11–12; 1 Chronicles 18:11"> And David takes the crown of their king from off his head, and finds it &#91;to be&#93; a talent of gold &#91;in&#93; weight, and &#91;with&#93; precious stone in it, and it is on the head of David; and he has brought out very much spoil of the city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:2–5; 1 Kings 9:21; Judges 8:6–7"> and he has brought out the people who &#91;are&#93; in it, and sets &#91;them&#93; to the saw, and to cutting instruments of iron, and to axes; and thus David does to all cities of the sons of Ammon, and David turns back—and all the people—to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:18–22; 2 Samuel 21:15; Joshua 12:12"> And it comes to pass after this, that war remains in Gezer with the Philistines; then Sibbechai the Hushathite has struck Sippai, of the children of the giant, and they are humbled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:19; 1 Samuel 21:9; 1 Samuel 17:7"> And there is war with the Philistines again, and Elhanan son of Jair strikes Lahmi, brother of Goliath the Gittite, the wood of whose spear &#91;is&#93; like a weavers’ beam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:20"> And there is war in Gath again, and there is a man of &#91;great&#93; measure, and his fingers and his toes &#91;are&#93; six and six—twenty-four &#91;total&#93;, and he has also been born to the giant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13; 1 Samuel 16:9; Isaiah 37:23"> And he reproaches Israel, and Jonathan son of Shimea, brother of David, strikes him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Joshua 14:12; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> These were born to the giant in Gath, and they fall by the hand of David, and by the hand of his servants.</span>
