<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 5:2; Genesis 29:31; Genesis 37:11"> And Rachel sees that she has not borne to Jacob, and Rachel is envious of her sister, and says to Jacob, “Give me sons, and if there is none—I die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 16:2; Psalms 127:3; 2 Kings 5:7"> And Jacob’s anger burns against Rachel, and he says, “Am I in stead of God who has withheld from you the fruit of the womb?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 50:23; Job 3:12; Ruth 4:11"> And she says, “Behold, my handmaid Bilhah, go in to her, and she bears on my knees, and I am built up, even I, from her”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; Genesis 21:10; 2 Samuel 12:11"> and she gives Bilhah her maidservant to him for a wife, and Jacob goes in to her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and Bilhah conceives, and bears a son to Jacob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Lamentations 3:59; Psalms 35:24; Psalms 43:1"> and Rachel says, “God has decided for me, and has also listened to my voice, and gives a son to me”; therefore she has called his name Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Bilhah, Rachel’s maidservant, conceives again, and bears a second son to Jacob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:24–25; Genesis 23:6; Genesis 49:21"> and Rachel says, “With wrestlings of God I have wrestled with my sister, indeed, I have prevailed”; and she calls his name Napthali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 30:4; Genesis 30:17; Genesis 29:35"> And Leah sees that she has ceased from bearing, and she takes Zilpah her maidservant, and gives her to Jacob for a wife;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and Zilpah, Leah’s maidservant, bears a son to Jacob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Isaiah 65:11; Genesis 35:26"> and Leah says, “A troop is coming”; and she calls his name Gad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And Zilpah, Leah’s maidservant, bears a second son to Jacob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:48; Proverbs 31:28; Song of Songs 6:9"> and Leah says, “Because of my happiness, for daughters have pronounced me blessed”; and she calls his name Asher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Song of Songs 7:13; Genesis 25:30"> And Reuben goes in the days of wheat-harvest, and finds love-apples in the field, and brings them to his mother Leah, and Rachel says to Leah, “Please give to me of the love-apples of your son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 16:13; 1 Corinthians 4:3; Numbers 16:9–10"> And she says to her, “Is your taking my husband a little thing, that you have also taken the love-apples of my son?” And Rachel says, “He therefore lies with you tonight, for your son’s love-apples.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Jacob comes in from the field at evening; and Leah goes to meet him and says, “You come in to me, for &#91;in&#93; hiring I have hired you with my son’s love-apples”; and he lies with her during that night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 30:6; Genesis 30:22; Exodus 3:7"> And God listens to Leah, and she conceives, and bears a son to Jacob, a fifth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:14–15; Deuteronomy 33:18; Genesis 46:13"> and Leah says, “God has given my hire, because I have given my maidservant to my husband”; and she calls his name Issachar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Leah conceives again, and she bears a sixth son to Jacob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:23; Matthew 4:13; Genesis 49:13"> and Leah says, “God has endowed me—a good dowry; this time my husband dwells with me, for I have borne six sons to him”; and she calls his name Zebulun;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:1–3; Genesis 34:26; Genesis 46:15"> and afterward she has borne a daughter, and calls her name Dinah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 29:31; Psalms 113:9; Psalms 127:3"> And God remembers Rachel, and God listens to her, and opens her womb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Genesis 29:31; Luke 1:27"> and she conceives and bears a son, and says, “God has gathered up my reproach”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:22–26; Genesis 35:24; Genesis 37:2"> and she calls his name Joseph, saying, “YHWH is adding to me another son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 24:54; Genesis 24:56; Genesis 28:15"> And it comes to pass, when Rachel has borne Joseph, that Jacob says to Laban, “Send me away, and I go to my place, and to my land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 29:30; Hosea 12:12; Genesis 31:26"> give up my wives and my children, for whom I have served you, and I go; for you have known my service which I have served you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Acts 7:10; Isaiah 61:9"> And Laban says to him, “Now if I have found grace in your eyes—I have observed diligently that YHWH blesses me for your sake.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 29:15; Genesis 29:19"> He also says, “Define your hire to me, and I give.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 31:6; Genesis 31:38–40; 1 Peter 2:18"> And he says to him, “You have known that which I have served you &#91;in&#93;, and that which your substance was with me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Deuteronomy 11:10"> for &#91;it is&#93; little which you have had at my appearance, and it breaks forth into a multitude, and YHWH blesses you at my coming; and now, when do I make, I also, for my own house?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:4–6; Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 118:8"> And he says, “What do I give to you?” And Jacob says, “You do not give me anything; if you do this thing for me, I turn back; I have delight; I watch your flock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 31:8; Genesis 31:10; Genesis 30:35"> I pass through all your flock today to turn aside every speckled and spotted sheep from there, and every brown sheep among the lambs, and speckled and spotted among the goats—and it has been my hire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; 2 Samuel 22:21; 1 Samuel 26:23"> and my righteousness has answered for me in the day to come, when it comes in for my hire before your face—everyone which is not speckled and spotted among &#91;my&#93; goats, and brown among &#91;my&#93; lambs—it is stolen with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:7; Numbers 22:29; 1 Corinthians 14:5"> And Laban says, “Behold, O that it were according to your word”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 31:9"> and he turns aside during that day the striped and the spotted male goats, and all the speckled and the spotted female goats, everyone that &#91;has&#93; white in it, and every brown one among the lambs, and he gives into the hand of his sons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> and sets a journey of three days between himself and Jacob; and Jacob is feeding the rest of the flock of Laban.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 31:9–13; Ezekiel 31:8"> And Jacob takes to himself a rod of fresh poplar and almond and plane-tree, and peels in them white peelings, making bare the white that &#91;is&#93; on the rods,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and sets up the rods which he has peeled in the gutters in the watering troughs (where the flock comes to drink), in front of the flock, that they may conceive in their coming to drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 12:35–36; Genesis 31:9–12; Genesis 31:40"> and the flocks conceive at the rods, and the flock bears striped, speckled, and spotted ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And Jacob has parted the lambs, and he puts the face of the flock toward the striped, also all the brown in the flock of Laban, and he sets his own droves by themselves, and has not set them near Laban’s flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And it has come to pass, whenever the strong ones of the flock conceive, that Jacob sets the rods before the eyes of the flock in the gutters, to cause them to conceive by the rods,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> and when the flock is feeble, he does not set &#91;them&#93;; and the feeble ones have been Laban’s, and the strong ones Jacob’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Genesis 30:30; Genesis 26:13–14"> And the man increases very exceedingly, and has many flocks, and maidservants, and menservants, and camels, and donkeys.</span>
