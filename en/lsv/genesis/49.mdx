<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 24:14; Jeremiah 23:20; Daniel 10:14"> And Jacob calls to his sons and says, “Be gathered together, and I declare to you that which happens with you in the latter end of the days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 34:11; Proverbs 7:1; Proverbs 23:22"> Be assembled, and hear, sons of Jacob, &#124;&#124; And listen to your father Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:17; Psalms 78:51; Psalms 105:36"> Reuben! You &#91;are&#93; my firstborn, &#124;&#124; My power, and beginning of my strength, &#124;&#124; The abundance of exaltation, &#124;&#124; And the abundance of strength;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; 1 Chronicles 5:1; Deuteronomy 27:20"> Unstable as water, you are not abundant; For you have gone up your father’s bed; Then you have defiled &#91;it&#93;: He went up my couch!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 34:25–31; Genesis 46:10–11; Genesis 29:33–34"> Simeon and Levi &#91;are&#93; brothers! Instruments of violence—their espousals!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Psalms 26:9; Psalms 16:9"> Into their secret, do not come, O my soul! Do not be united to their assembly, O my glory; For in their anger they slew a man, &#124;&#124; And in their self-will eradicated a prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:1–9; 1 Chronicles 4:24–31; 1 Chronicles 6:65"> Cursed &#91;is&#93; their anger, for &#91;it is&#93; fierce, &#124;&#124; And their wrath, for &#91;it is&#93; sharp; I divide them in Jacob, &#124;&#124; And I scatter them in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 7:14; Genesis 27:29; 1 Chronicles 5:2"> Judah! Your brothers praise you! Your hand &#91;is&#93; on the neck of your enemies, &#124;&#124; Sons of your father bow themselves to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Numbers 23:24; Revelation 5:5"> A lion’s whelp &#91;is&#93; Judah, &#124;&#124; For prey, my son, you have gone up; He has bent, he has crouched as a lion, &#124;&#124; And as a lioness; who causes him to arise?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Numbers 24:17; Isaiah 9:6"> The scepter does not turn aside from Judah, &#124;&#124; And a lawgiver from between his feet, &#124;&#124; Until his Seed comes; And His &#91;is&#93; the obedience of peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 63:1–3; Joel 3:18; Revelation 7:14"> Binding to the vine his donkey, &#124;&#124; And to the choice vine the colt of his donkey, &#124;&#124; He has washed in wine his clothing, &#124;&#124; And in the blood of grapes his covering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29"> Red &#91;are&#93; eyes with wine, &#124;&#124; And white &#91;are&#93; teeth with milk!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:18–19; Genesis 30:20; Joshua 19:10–16"> Zebulun dwells at a haven of the seas, &#124;&#124; And he &#91;is&#93; for a haven of ships; And his side &#91;is&#93; to Sidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:32; Genesis 30:18; Deuteronomy 33:18"> Issachar &#91;is&#93; a strong donkey, &#124;&#124; Crouching between the two folds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 81:6; Matthew 23:4; Joshua 14:15"> And he sees rest, that &#91;it is&#93; good, &#124;&#124; And the land, that &#91;it is&#93; pleasant, &#124;&#124; And he inclines his shoulder to bear, &#124;&#124; And is a servant for tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 30:6; Deuteronomy 33:22; Judges 18:26–27"> Dan judges his people, &#124;&#124; As one of the tribes of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:35; Judges 18:22–31; Judges 14:1–15"> Dan is a serpent by the way, a viper by the path, &#124;&#124; Which is biting the horse’s heels, &#124;&#124; And its rider falls backward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:174; Psalms 119:166; Micah 7:7"> For Your salvation I have waited, YHWH!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 30:11; 1 Chronicles 5:11–22; Deuteronomy 33:20–21"> Gad! A troop assaults him, &#124;&#124; But he assaults last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:24–25; Genesis 30:13; Genesis 46:17"> Out of Asher his bread &#91;is&#93; fat; And he gives delicacies of a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:23; Genesis 30:8; Joshua 19:32–39"> Naphtali &#91;is&#93; a doe sent away, &#124;&#124; Who is giving beautiful young ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 41:52; Psalms 128:3; Psalms 1:1–3"> Joseph &#91;is&#93; a fruitful son; A fruitful son by a fountain, &#124;&#124; Daughters step over the wall;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:24; Psalms 64:3; Psalms 118:13"> And embitter him indeed, they have striven, &#124;&#124; Indeed, archers hate him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 132:2; Isaiah 28:16; Psalms 132:5"> And his bow abides in strength, &#124;&#124; And strengthened are the arms of his hands &#124;&#124; By the hands of the Mighty One of Jacob, &#124;&#124; From where is a shepherd, a son of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 28:13; Genesis 35:11; Philippians 4:19"> By the God of your father who helps you, &#124;&#124; And the Mighty One who blesses you, &#124;&#124; Blessings of the heavens from above, &#124;&#124; Blessings of the deep lying under, &#124;&#124; Blessings of breasts and womb—</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:15–16; Habakkuk 3:6; Isaiah 66:5"> Your father’s blessings have been mighty &#124;&#124; Above the blessings of my progenitors, &#124;&#124; To the limit of the perpetual heights &#124;&#124; They are for the head of Joseph, &#124;&#124; And for the crown of the one &#124;&#124; Separate &#91;from&#93; his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 20:25; Genesis 35:18; Ezekiel 22:27"> Benjamin! A wolf tears; In the morning he eats prey, &#124;&#124; And at evening he apportions spoil.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Esther 8:11; 1 Kings 18:31"> All these &#91;are&#93; the twelve tribes of Israel, and this &#91;is&#93; that which their father has spoken to them, and he blesses them; each according to his blessing he has blessed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 47:30; Genesis 50:13; Genesis 15:15"> And he commands them and says to them, “I am being gathered to my people; bury me by my fathers, at the cave which &#91;is&#93; in the field of Ephron the Hittite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 23:20; Genesis 23:16–18; Genesis 23:8–9"> in the cave which &#91;is&#93; in the field of Machpelah, which &#91;is&#93; on the front of Mamre, in the land of Canaan, which Abraham bought with the field from Ephron the Hittite for a possession of a burying-place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; Genesis 35:29; Genesis 50:13"> (there they buried Abraham and his wife Sarah; there they buried Isaac and his wife Rebekah; and there I buried Leah);</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 23:17–20"> the purchase of the field and of the cave which &#91;is&#93; in it, &#91;is&#93; from sons of Heth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 49:29; Genesis 25:8; Genesis 35:29"> And Jacob finishes commanding his sons, and gathers up his feet to the bed, and expires, and is gathered to his people.</span>
