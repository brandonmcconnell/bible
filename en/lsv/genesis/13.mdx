<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:9–20; Joshua 10:40; 2 Samuel 24:7"> And Abram goes up from Egypt (he and his wife, and all that he has, and Lot with him) toward the south;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Psalms 112:1–3; Genesis 26:12–13"> and Abram &#91;is&#93; exceedingly wealthy in livestock, in silver, and in gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 12:8–9; Genesis 12:6"> And he goes on his journeys from the south, even to Bethel, to the place where his tent had been at the commencement, between Bethel and Hai—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12; Genesis 12:7–8; Isaiah 58:9"> to the place of the altar which he made there at the first, and there Abram preaches in the Name of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:29; Genesis 25:27; Genesis 4:20"> And also to Lot, who is going with Abram, there has been sheep and oxen and tents;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 36:6–7; Ecclesiastes 5:10–11; 1 Timothy 6:9"> and the land has not permitted them to dwell together, for their substance has been much, and they have not been able to dwell together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 26:20; Genesis 12:6; James 3:16"> and there is a strife between those feeding Abram’s livestock and those feeding Lot’s livestock; and the Canaanite and the Perizzite &#91;are&#93; then dwelling in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:6–7; Acts 7:26; Psalms 133:1"> And Abram says to Lot, “Please let there not be strife between me and you, and between my shepherds and your shepherds, for we &#91;are&#93; men—brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 34:10; 1 Peter 3:8–12"> Is not all the land before you? Please be parted from me; if to the left, then I to the right; and if to the right, then I to the left.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:3; Isaiah 51:3; Genesis 14:8"> And Lot lifts up his eyes, and sees the whole circuit of the Jordan that it &#91;is&#93; all a watered country (before YHWH’s destroying Sodom and Gomorrah, as YHWH’s garden, as the land of Egypt), in your coming toward Zoar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 13:9; 1 Peter 2:17; Hebrews 10:25"> and Lot chooses for himself the whole circuit of the Jordan; and Lot journeys from the east, and they are parted—a man from his companion;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 19:29; Genesis 14:12; Genesis 19:1"> Abram has dwelt in the land of Canaan, and Lot has dwelt in the cities of the circuit, and moves &#91;his&#93; tent to Sodom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 2:10; 2 Peter 2:6–8; Jude 1:7"> and the men of Sodom &#91;are&#93; evil, and sinners before YHWH exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 49:18; Deuteronomy 3:27; Genesis 28:14"> And YHWH said to Abram, after Lot’s being parted from him, “Now lift up your eyes and look from the place where you &#91;are&#93;, northward, and southward, and eastward, and westward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:7; Genesis 35:12; Genesis 15:18"> for the whole of the land which you are seeing, I give it to you and to your Seed for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 28:14; Numbers 23:10; Genesis 32:12"> And I have set your seed as dust of the earth, so that, if one is able to number the dust of the earth, even your seed is numbered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Numbers 13:17–24"> rise, go up and down through the land, to its length, and to its breadth, for to you I give it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 18:1; Genesis 8:20; Genesis 35:27"> And Abram moves &#91;his&#93; tent, and comes, and dwells among the oaks of Mamre, which &#91;are&#93; in Hebron, and builds there an altar to YHWH.</span>
