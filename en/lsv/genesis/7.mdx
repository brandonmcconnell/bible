<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 24:37–39; 2 Peter 2:5–9; Genesis 6:9"> And YHWH says to Noah, “Come, you and all your house, into the Ark, for I have seen you &#91;are&#93; righteous before Me in this generation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 8:20; Leviticus 10:10; Leviticus 11:1–47"> you take seven pairs from all the clean beasts to yourself, a male and its female; and of the beasts which are not clean—two, a male and its female;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> also, seven pairs from &#91;each&#93; bird of the heavens, a male and a female, to keep alive seed on the face of all the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 6:13; Genesis 7:12; Genesis 7:17"> for after seven more days I am sending rain on the earth &#91;for&#93; forty days and forty nights, and have wiped away all the substance that I have made from off the face of the ground.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 6:22; Luke 8:21; Exodus 39:32"> And Noah does according to all that YHWH has commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 5:32; Genesis 8:13"> And Noah &#91;is&#93; a son of six hundred years, and the flood of waters has been on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 17:27; Genesis 6:18; Matthew 24:38"> And Noah goes—and his sons, and his wife, and his sons’ wives with him—into the Ark, out of the presence of the waters of the flood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> from the clean beast, and from the beast that &#91;is&#93; not clean, and from the bird, and of everything that is creeping on the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 7:16; Genesis 2:19; Jeremiah 8:7"> they have come to Noah two by two into the Ark, a male and a female, as God has commanded Noah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 6:17; Genesis 7:4; Luke 17:27"> And it comes to pass, after seven days, that waters of the flood have been on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 8:2; Malachi 3:10; Proverbs 8:28–29"> In the six hundredth year of the life of Noah, in the second month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on this day all &#91;the&#93; fountains of the great deep have been broken up, and the network of the heavens has been opened,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Matthew 4:2; Genesis 7:17"> and the shower is on the earth &#91;for&#93; forty days and forty nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 6:18; Genesis 7:1; Genesis 6:10"> On this very same day Noah, and Shem, and Ham, and Japheth, sons of Noah, and Noah’s wife, and the three wives of his sons with them, went into the Ark—</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:8–9; Genesis 7:2–3"> they, and every living creature after its kind, and every beast after its kind, and every creeping thing that is creeping on the earth after its kind, and every bird after its kind (every bird, every wing).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 6:19–20; Isaiah 11:6"> And they come to Noah, into the Ark, two by two of all the flesh in which &#91;is&#93; a living spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 25:10; Luke 13:25; Psalms 91:1–10"> and they that are coming in, male and female of all flesh, have come in as God has commanded him, and YHWH closes &#91;it&#93; for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:12"> And the flood is on the earth &#91;for&#93; forty days, and the waters multiply and lift up the Ark, and it is raised up from off the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; Psalms 69:15; Job 22:16"> and the waters are mighty and multiply exceedingly on the earth; and the Ark goes on the face of the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 104:6–9; Psalms 46:2–3; 2 Peter 3:6"> And the waters have been very, very mighty on the earth, and all the high mountains are covered which &#91;are&#93; under the whole heavens;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:23; Psalms 104:6"> the waters prevailed from above the mountains, and cover &#91;them&#93; fifteen cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 6:13; Genesis 7:4; Genesis 6:17"> and all flesh expires that is moving on the earth, among bird, and among livestock, and among beast, and among every teeming thing which is teeming on the earth, and all mankind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Genesis 6:17"> all in whose nostrils &#91;is&#93; breath of a living spirit—of all that &#91;is&#93; in the dry land—have died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 2:5; 1 Peter 3:20; Hebrews 11:7"> And all the substance that is on the face of the ground is wiped away—from man to beast, to creeping thing, and to bird of the heavens; indeed, they are wiped away from the earth, and only Noah is left, and those who &#91;are&#93; with him in the Ark;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 8:3–4"> and the waters are mighty on the earth &#91;for&#93; one hundred and fifty days.</span>
