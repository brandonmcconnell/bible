<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 4:25; Job 25:4; Psalms 51:5"> And Adam lives one hundred and thirty years &#91;&#91;or two hundred and thirty years&#93;&#93;, and begets &#91;a son&#93; in his likeness, according to his image, and calls his name Seth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:1–3; Luke 3:36–38; Genesis 5:26"> And the days of Adam after his begetting Seth are eight hundred years &#91;&#91;or seven hundred years&#93;&#93;, and he begets sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 5:8; Genesis 5:11; Genesis 3:19"> And all the days of Adam which he lived are nine hundred and thirty years, and he dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 4:26"> And Seth lives one hundred and five years &#91;&#91;or two hundred and five years&#93;&#93;, and begets Enos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Seth lives after his begetting Enos eight hundred and seven years &#91;&#91;or seven hundred and seven years&#93;&#93;, and begets sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And all the days of Seth are nine hundred and twelve years, and he dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 3:37; 1 Chronicles 1:2"> And Enos lives ninety years &#91;&#91;or one hundred and ninety years&#93;&#93;, and begets Cainan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Enos lives after his begetting Cainan eight hundred and fifteen years &#91;&#91;or seven hundred and fifteen years&#93;&#93;, and begets sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And all the days of Enos are nine hundred and five years, and he dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 3:37"> And Cainan lives seventy years &#91;&#91;or one hundred and seventy years&#93;&#93;, and begets Mahalaleel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Cainan lives after his begetting Mahalaleel eight hundred and forty years &#91;&#91;or seven hundred and forty years&#93;&#93;, and begets sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And all the days of Cainan are nine hundred and ten years, and he dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:2"> And Mahalaleel lives sixty-five years &#91;&#91;or one hundred and sixty-five years&#93;&#93;, and begets Jared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Mahalaleel lives after his begetting Jared eight hundred and thirty years &#91;&#91;or seven hundred and thirty years&#93;&#93;, and begets sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And all the days of Mahalaleel are eight hundred and ninety-five years, and he dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 4:17; 1 Chronicles 1:3; Jude 1:14–15"> And Jared lives one hundred and sixty-two years, and begets Enoch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Jared lives after his begetting Enoch eight hundred years, and begets sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And all the days of Jared are nine hundred and sixty-two years, and he dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 3:37"> And Enoch lives sixty-five years &#91;&#91;or one hundred and sixty-five years&#93;&#93;, and begets Methuselah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 6:9"> And Enoch habitually walks with God after his begetting Methuselah three hundred years &#91;&#91;or two hundred years&#93;&#93;, and begets sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And all the days of Enoch are three hundred and sixty-five years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 11:5–6; 1 John 1:7; Genesis 5:22"> And Enoch habitually walks with God, and he is not, for God has taken him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 4:18"> And Methuselah lives one hundred and eighty-seven years, and begets Lamech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Methuselah lives after his begetting Lamech seven hundred and eighty-two years, and begets sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And all the days of Methuselah are nine hundred and sixty-nine years, and he dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Lamech lives one hundred and eighty-two years &#91;&#91;or one hundred and eighty-eight years&#93;&#93;, and begets a son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 2:5; Hebrews 11:7; Matthew 24:37"> and calls his name Noah, saying, “This &#91;one&#93; comforts us concerning our work, and concerning the labor of our hands, because of the ground which YHWH has cursed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Lamech lives after his begetting Noah five hundred and ninety-five years &#91;&#91;or five hundred and sixty-five years&#93;&#93;, and begets sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And all the days of Lamech are seven hundred and seventy-seven years &#91;&#91;or seven hundred and fifty-three years&#93;&#93;, and he dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 6:10; Genesis 10:21; Genesis 9:18–19"> And Noah is a son of five hundred years, and Noah begets Shem, Ham, and Japheth.</span>
