<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:8; Genesis 42:20; Deuteronomy 8:16"> and my cup, the silver cup, you put in the mouth of the bag of the young one, and his grain-money”; and he does according to the word of Joseph which he has spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The morning is bright, and the men have been sent away, they and their donkeys—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 10:32; Psalms 35:12; Proverbs 17:13"> they have gone out of the city—they have not gone far off—and Joseph has said to him who &#91;is&#93; over his house, “Rise, pursue after the men; and you have overtaken them, and you have said to them: Why have you repaid evil for good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; Leviticus 19:26; 2 Kings 21:6"> Is this not that with which my lord drinks? And he observes diligently with it; you have done evil &#91;in&#93; that which you have done.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he overtakes them, and speaks to them these words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 38:16–18; Genesis 37:18–32; 2 Kings 8:13"> and they say to him, “Why does my lord speak according to these words? Far be it from your servants to do according to this word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 43:12; Genesis 42:35; Romans 13:9"> behold, the money which we found in the mouth of our bags we brought back to you from the land of Canaan, and how do we steal from the house of your lord silver or gold?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:32; Psalms 7:3–5; Job 31:38–40"> With whomsoever of your servants it is found, he has died, and we also are to my lord for servants.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 22:3; Genesis 44:17; Genesis 44:33"> And he says, “Now also, according to your words, so it &#91;is&#93;; he with whom it is found becomes my servant, and you are acquitted”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and they hurry and take down each his bag to the earth, and each opens his bag;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 44:26–32; Genesis 42:36–38; Genesis 43:14"> and he searches—at the eldest he has begun, and at the youngest he has completed—and the cup is found in the bag of Benjamin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:11; Genesis 37:29–34; 2 Samuel 1:2"> and they tear their garments, and each loads his donkey, and they return to the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 50:18; Genesis 43:25; Genesis 43:16"> And Judah and his brothers come to the house of Joseph, and he is yet there, and they fall to the earth before him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 4:10; Genesis 21:26; Genesis 3:13"> and Joseph says to them, “What &#91;is&#93; this deed that you have done? Have you not known that a man like me diligently observes?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 44:9; Daniel 9:7; Numbers 32:23"> And Judah says, “What do we say to my lord? What do we speak? And how do we justify ourselves? God has found out the iniquity of your servants; behold, we &#91;are&#93; servants to my lord, both we, and he in whose hand the cup has been found”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; 2 Samuel 23:3; Genesis 18:25"> and he says, “Far be it from me to do this; the man in whose hand the cup has been found, he becomes my servant; and you, go up in peace to your father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 32:22; Genesis 41:40; Genesis 18:30"> And Judah comes near to him and says, “O my lord, please let your servant speak a word in the ears of my lord, and do not let your anger burn against your servant—for you are as Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 43:7; Genesis 43:29; Genesis 42:7–10"> My lord has asked his servants, saying, Do you have a father or brother?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 37:3; Genesis 42:38; Luke 7:12"> And we say to my lord, We have a father, an aged one, and a child of old age, a little one; and his brother died, and he is left alone of his mother, and his father has loved him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 42:15; Genesis 42:20; Amos 9:4"> And you say to your servants, Bring him down to me, and I set my eye on him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 44:30; Genesis 42:38"> and we say to my lord, The youth is not able to leave his father, when he has left his father, then he has died;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 43:5; Genesis 43:3; Genesis 42:15–20"> and you say to your servants, If your young brother does not come down with you, you do not add to see my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 42:29–34"> And it comes to pass, that we have come up to your servant my father, that we declare to him the words of my lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 43:2; Genesis 43:5"> and our father says, Return, buy for us a little food,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 11:7; Genesis 43:4–5"> and we say, We are not able to go down; if our young brother is with us, then we have gone down; for we are not able to see the man’s face, and our young brother not with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 46:19; Genesis 35:16–18; Genesis 30:22–25"> And your servant my father says to us, You have known that my wife bore two to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 37:33; Genesis 37:13–14; Genesis 42:36"> and one goes out from me, and I say, Surely he is torn—torn! And I have not seen him since;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 42:38; Genesis 43:14; Deuteronomy 31:17"> when you have taken also this from my presence, and harm has met him, then you have brought down my grey hairs with evil to Sheol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:1; 2 Samuel 18:33; Genesis 44:31"> And now, at my coming to your servant my father, and the youth not with us (and his soul is bound up in his soul),</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 44:29; Genesis 37:35; 2 Corinthians 7:10"> then it has come to pass, when he sees that the youth is not, that he has died, and your servants have brought down the grey hairs of your servant our father with sorrow to Sheol;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 43:8–9; Genesis 43:16"> for your servant obtained the youth by guarantee to my father, saying, If I do not bring him to you—then I have sinned against my father all the days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 John 3:16; Exodus 32:32; Hebrews 7:22"> And now, please let your servant abide instead of the youth &#91;as&#93; a servant to my lord, and the youth goes up with his brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Esther 8:6; Psalms 116:3; Psalms 119:143"> for how do I go up to my father, and the youth not with me? Lest I look on the evil which finds my father.”</span>
