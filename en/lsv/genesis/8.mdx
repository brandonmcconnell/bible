<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Matthew 8:26–27; Proverbs 8:28"> and the fountains of the deep and the network of the heavens are closed, and the shower is restrained from the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 7:24; Genesis 7:11"> And the waters return from off the earth, going on and returning; and the waters are lacking at the end of one hundred and fifty days.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 6:16; Daniel 6:10"> And it comes to pass, at the end of forty days, that Noah opens the window of the Ark which he made,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 147:9; 1 Kings 17:6; 1 Kings 17:4"> and he sends forth the raven, and it goes out, going out and turning back until the drying of the waters from off the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Songs 2:14; Song of Songs 1:15; Matthew 10:16"> And he sends forth the dove from him to see whether the waters have been lightened from off the face of the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; Psalms 116:7; Deuteronomy 28:65"> and the dove has not found rest for the sole of her foot, and she turns back to him, to the Ark, for waters &#91;are&#93; on the face of all the earth, and he puts out his hand, and takes her, and brings her in to him, into the Ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:10; Genesis 8:12"> And he stays yet seven more days, and adds to send forth the dove from the Ark;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:15; Zechariah 4:12–14; Romans 10:15"> and the dove comes to him at evening, and behold, an olive leaf &#91;is&#93; torn off in her mouth; and Noah knows that the waters have been lightened from off the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Isaiah 30:18; Genesis 8:10"> And he stays yet seven more days, and sends forth the dove, and it did not add to return to him anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 7:11"> And it comes to pass in the six hundredth and first year, in the first &#91;month&#93;, on the first of the month, the waters have been dried from off the earth; and Noah turns aside the covering of the Ark, and looks, and behold, the face of the ground has been dried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:13–14; Genesis 7:11"> And in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the earth has become dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And God speaks to Noah, saying, “Go out from the Ark, you, and your wife, and your sons, and your sons’ wives with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 7:13; Psalms 121:8; Psalms 91:11"> every living thing that &#91;is&#93; with you, of all flesh, among bird, and among livestock, and among every creeping thing which is creeping on the earth, bring out with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:22; Genesis 9:1; Jeremiah 31:27–28"> and they have teemed in the earth, and been fruitful, and have multiplied on the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 121:8"> And Noah goes out, and his sons, and his wife, and his sons’ wives with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> every beast, every creeping thing, and every bird; every creeping thing on the earth, after their families, have gone out from the Ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15–16; Romans 12:1; Genesis 22:9"> And Noah builds an altar to YHWH, and takes from every clean beast and from every clean bird, and causes burnt-offerings to ascend on the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:15; Ephesians 5:2; Isaiah 54:9–10"> and YHWH smells the refreshing fragrance, and YHWH says to His heart, “I do not continue to disfavor the ground because of man anymore, though the imagination of the heart of man &#91;is&#93; evil from his youth; and I do not continue to strike all living anymore, as I have done;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 5:7; Psalms 74:16–17; Jeremiah 5:24"> during all &#91;the&#93; days of the earth, seed-time and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night never cease.”</span>
