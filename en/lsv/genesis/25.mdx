<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:32–33; Genesis 23:1–2; Genesis 28:1"> And Abraham adds and takes a wife, and her name &#91;is&#93; Keturah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:32–33; Numbers 25:17–18; Job 2:11"> and she bears to him Zimran, and Jokshan, and Medan, and Midian, and Ishbak, and Shuah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Jeremiah 49:8; Ezekiel 27:20"> And Jokshan has begotten Sheba and Dedan; and the sons of Dedan were Asshurim, and Letushim, and Leummim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6"> and the sons of Midian &#91;are&#93; Ephah, and Epher, and Enoch, and Abidah, and Eldaah: all these &#91;are&#93; sons of Keturah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 24:36; Hebrews 1:2; Galatians 3:29"> And Abraham gives all that he has to Isaac;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 21:14; Judges 6:3; Acts 14:17"> and to the sons of the concubines whom Abraham has, Abraham has given gifts, and sends them away from his son Isaac (in his being yet alive) eastward, to the east country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 12:4"> And these &#91;are&#93; the days of the years of the life of Abraham, which he lived, one hundred and seventy-five years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 35:28–29; Genesis 15:15; 1 Chronicles 29:28"> and Abraham expires, and dies in a good old age, aged and satisfied, and is gathered to his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 50:13; Genesis 35:29; Genesis 49:29–30"> And his sons Isaac and Ishmael bury him at the cave of Machpelah, at the field of Ephron, son of Zoar the Hittite, which &#91;is&#93; before Mamre—</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 23:16; Genesis 49:31"> the field which Abraham bought from the sons of Heth—there Abraham has been buried, and his wife Sarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 16:14; Genesis 24:62"> And it comes to pass after the death of Abraham, that God blesses his son Isaac; and Isaac dwells by the Well of the Living One, my Beholder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 83:6; Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:13"> And these &#91;are&#93; the generations of Ishmael, Abraham’s son, whom Hagar the Egyptian, Sarah’s handmaid, has borne to Abraham;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:29–31; Isaiah 60:7; Song of Songs 1:5"> and these &#91;are&#93; the names of the sons of Ishmael, by their names, according to their births: firstborn of Ishmael, Nebajoth; and Kedar, and Adbeel, and Mibsam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 21:11; Isaiah 21:16"> and Mishma, and Dumah, and Massa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:19; Job 2:11"> Hadar, and Tema, Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 17:20; Genesis 17:23"> these are sons of Ishmael, and these &#91;are&#93; their names, by their villages, and by their towers; twelve princes according to their peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Genesis 25:7–8"> And these &#91;are&#93; the years of the life of Ishmael, one hundred and thirty-seven years; and he expires, and dies, and is gathered to his people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 16:12; Genesis 20:1; 2 Kings 23:29"> and they dwell from Havilah to Shur, which &#91;is&#93; before Egypt, in &#91;your&#93; going toward Asshur; in the presence of all his brothers has he fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 1:2; Luke 3:34; Acts 7:8"> And these &#91;are&#93; the generations of Isaac, Abraham’s son: Abraham has begotten Isaac;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 24:29; Genesis 24:67; Genesis 22:23"> and Isaac is a son of forty years in his taking Rebekah, daughter of Bethuel the Aramean, from Padan-Aram, sister of Laban the Aramean, to him for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Isaiah 58:9; Ezra 8:23"> And Isaac makes plea to YHWH before his wife, for she &#91;is&#93; barren: and YHWH accepts his plea, and his wife Rebekah conceives,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Samuel 28:6; 1 Samuel 30:8"> and the children struggle together within her, and she says, “If &#91;it is&#93; right—why &#91;am&#93; I thus?” And she goes to seek YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 9:10–13; Genesis 27:40; Genesis 27:29"> And YHWH says to her, “Two nations &#91;are&#93; in your womb, and two peoples from your bowels are parted; and the &#91;one&#93; people is stronger than the &#91;other&#93; people; and the older serves the younger.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And her days to bear are fulfilled, and behold, twins &#91;are&#93; in her womb;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 27:11; Genesis 27:16; Genesis 27:23"> and the first comes out all red as a hairy robe, and they call his name Esau;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 27:36; Hosea 12:3; Genesis 38:28–30"> and afterward his brother has come out, and his hand is taking hold on Esau’s heel, and one calls his name Jacob; and Isaac &#91;is&#93; a son of sixty years in her bearing them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 11:9; Genesis 21:20; Genesis 10:9"> And the youths grew, and Esau is a man acquainted &#91;with&#93; hunting, a man of the field; and Jacob &#91;is&#93; a plain man, inhabiting tents;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 27:19; Genesis 27:31; Genesis 27:4"> and Isaac loves Esau, for &#91;his&#93; game &#91;is&#93; in his mouth; and Rebekah is loving Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 13:25; Isaiah 40:30–31; 1 Samuel 14:28"> And Jacob boils stew, and Esau comes in from the field, and he &#91;is&#93; weary;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 36:1; Exodus 15:15; Genesis 36:9"> and Esau says to Jacob, “Please let me eat some of this red-red thing, for I &#91;am&#93; weary”; therefore &#91;one&#93; has called his name Edom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> and Jacob says, “Sell your birthright to me today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachi 3:14; Job 21:15; Job 22:17"> And Esau says, “Behold, I am going to die, and what is this to me—a birthright?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 12:16; Genesis 27:36; Hebrews 6:16"> And Jacob says, “Swear to me today”: and he swears to him, and sells his birthright to Jacob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Philippians 3:18–19; Hebrews 12:16–17; 1 Corinthians 15:32"> and Jacob has given bread and stew of lentils to Esau, and he eats, and drinks, and rises, and goes; and Esau despises the birthright.</span>
