<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 1:1–3; Hebrews 11:3; Isaiah 45:18"> In &#91;the&#93; beginning God created the heavens and the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:23; Psalms 104:30; Isaiah 45:18"> and the earth was formless and void, and darkness &#91;was&#93; on the face of the deep, and the Spirit of God &#91;was&#93; fluttering on the face of the waters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:6; John 1:5; Isaiah 45:7"> and God says, “Let light be”; and light is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:13; Genesis 1:18; Ecclesiastes 11:7"> And God sees that the light &#91;is&#93; good, and God separates between the light and the darkness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 74:16; Isaiah 45:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:5"> and God calls the light “Day,” and the darkness He has called “Night”; and there is an evening, and there is a morning—day one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:15; Psalms 33:6; Job 37:18"> And God says, “Let an expanse be in the midst of the waters, and let it be separating between waters and waters.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 8:28–29; Psalms 148:4; Matthew 8:27"> And God makes the expanse, and it separates between the waters which &#91;are&#93; under the expanse, and the waters which &#91;are&#93; above the expanse: and it is so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:5; Genesis 1:19; Genesis 1:10"> And God calls the expanse “Heavens”; and there is an evening, and there is a morning—&#91;the&#93; second day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Peter 3:5; Jonah 1:9; Jeremiah 5:22"> And God says, “Let the waters under the heavens be collected to one place, and let the dry land be seen”: and it is so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Psalms 104:31; Genesis 1:4"> And God calls the dry land “Earth,” and the collection of the waters He has called “Seas”; and God sees that &#91;it is&#93; good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 104:14–17; Hebrews 6:7; Psalms 147:8"> And God says, “Let the earth yield tender grass, herb sowing seed, fruit-tree (whose seed &#91;is&#93; in itself) making fruit after its kind, on the earth”: and it is so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:28; Isaiah 55:10–11; Matthew 13:24–26"> And the earth brings forth tender grass, herb sowing seed after its kind, and tree making fruit (whose seed &#91;is&#93; in itself) after its kind; and God sees that &#91;it is&#93; good;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and there is an evening, and there is a morning—&#91;the&#93; third day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:19–20; Deuteronomy 4:19; Jeremiah 31:35"> And God says, “Let luminaries be in the expanse of the heavens, to make a separation between the day and the night, then they have been for signs, and for appointed times, and for days and years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and they have been for luminaries in the expanse of the heavens to give light on the earth”: and it is so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 136:7–9; Deuteronomy 4:19; Psalms 8:3"> And God makes the two great luminaries, the great luminary for the rule of the day, and the small luminary—and the stars—for the rule of the night;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 8:3; Psalms 8:1; Job 38:12"> and God gives them in the expanse of the heavens to give light on the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35; Psalms 19:6"> and to rule over day and over night, and to make a separation between the light and the darkness; and God sees that &#91;it is&#93; good;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and there is an evening, and there is a morning—&#91;the&#93; fourth day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:19; Psalms 104:24–25; 1 Kings 4:33"> And God says, “Let the waters teem with the teeming living creature, and let &#91;the&#93; bird fly above the earth on the face of the expanse of the heavens.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 104:24–26; Genesis 1:25; Genesis 8:19"> And God creates the great dragons, and every living creature that is creeping, which the waters have teemed with, after their kind, and every bird with wing, after its kind, and God sees that &#91;it is&#93; good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 40:15; Genesis 8:17; Psalms 107:38"> And God blesses them, saying, “Be fruitful, and multiply, and fill the waters in the seas, and let the bird multiply in the earth”:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and there is an evening, and there is a morning—&#91;the&#93; fifth day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 50:9–10; Genesis 6:20; Genesis 8:19"> And God says, “Let the earth bring forth the living creature after its kind, livestock and creeping thing, and beast of the earth after its kind”: and it is so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5; Job 12:8–10; Genesis 2:19–20"> And God makes the beast of the earth after its kind, and the livestock after their kind, and every creeping thing of the ground after its kind, and God sees that &#91;it is&#93; good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 3:10; Ephesians 4:24; Psalms 8:4–8"> And God says, “Let Us make man in Our image, according to Our likeness, and let them rule over &#91;the&#93; fish of the sea, and over &#91;the&#93; bird of the heavens, and over livestock, and over all the earth, and over every creeping thing that is creeping on the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 19:4; Ephesians 2:10; Genesis 5:1–2"> And God creates the man in His image; in the image of God He created him, a male and a female He created them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 9:1; Genesis 9:7; Leviticus 26:9"> And God blesses them, and God says to them, “Be fruitful, and multiply, and fill the earth, and subdue it, and rule over &#91;the&#93; fish of the sea, and over &#91;the&#93; bird of the heavens, and over every living thing that is creeping on the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 9:3; Psalms 104:14–15; Psalms 145:15–16"> And God says, “Behold, I have given to you every herb sowing seed, which &#91;is&#93; on the face of all the earth, and every tree in which &#91;is&#93; the fruit of a tree sowing seed, to you it is for food;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 104:14; Psalms 145:15–16; Psalms 147:9"> and to every beast of the earth, and to every bird of the heavens, and to every creeping thing on the earth, in which &#91;is&#93; breath of life, every green herb &#91;is&#93; for food”: and it is so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; Psalms 104:24; Psalms 19:1–2"> And God sees all that He has done, and behold, &#91;it is&#93; very good; and there is an evening, and there is a morning—the sixth day.</span>
