<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Genesis 25:11; Genesis 21:22–32"> And there is a famine in the land, besides the first famine which was in the days of Abraham, and Isaac goes to Abimelech king of the Philistines, to Gerar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Psalms 37:3; Genesis 17:1"> And YHWH appears to him and says, “Do not go down toward Egypt, dwell in the land concerning which I speak to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Psalms 105:9; Genesis 17:8"> sojourn in this land, and I am with you, and bless you, for to you and to your seed I give all these lands, and I have established the oath which I have sworn to your father Abraham;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 3:25; Genesis 15:5; Genesis 22:17–18"> and I have multiplied your seed as stars of the heavens, and I have given to your seed all these lands; and all nations of the earth have blessed themselves in your Seed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 7:24; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 22:16"> because that Abraham has listened to My voice, and keeps My charge, My commands, My statutes, and My laws.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 20:1"> And Isaac dwells in Gerar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Genesis 12:13; Genesis 20:2"> and men of the place ask him of his wife, and he says, “She &#91;is&#93; my sister”: for he has been afraid to say, “My wife—lest the men of the place kill me for Rebekah, for she &#91;is&#93; of good appearance.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 62:5; Proverbs 5:18–19; Proverbs 7:6"> And it comes to pass, when the days have been prolonged to him there, that Abimelech king of the Philistines looks through the window, and sees, and behold, Isaac is playing with his wife Rebekah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Abimelech calls for Isaac and says, “Behold, she &#91;is&#93; surely your wife; and how could you have said, She &#91;is&#93; my sister?” And Isaac says to him, “Because I said, Lest I die for her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 12:18–19"> And Abimelech says, “What &#91;is&#93; this you have done to us? As a little thing one of the people had lain with your wife, and you had brought on us guilt”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 105:15; Genesis 20:6; Proverbs 6:29"> and Abimelech commands all the people, saying, “He who comes against this man or against his wife, dying does die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:8; Genesis 24:1; Genesis 26:3"> And Isaac sows in that land, and finds in that year a hundredfold, and YHWH blesses him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 112:3; Proverbs 10:22; Genesis 24:35"> and the man is great, and goes on, going on and becoming great, until he has been very great,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 37:11; Genesis 13:2; Proverbs 27:4"> and he has possession of a flock, and possession of a herd, and an abundant service; and the Philistines envy him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 21:30; Genesis 21:25"> and all the wells which his father’s servants dug in the days of his father Abraham, the Philistines have stopped them, and fill them with dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 1:9"> And Abimelech says to Isaac, “Go from us; for you have become much mightier than we”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and Isaac goes from there, and encamps in the Valley of Gerar, and dwells there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 13:2; Hosea 2:17; Numbers 32:38"> and Isaac turns back, and digs the wells of water which they dug in the days of his father Abraham, which the Philistines have stopped after the death of Abraham, and he calls to them names according to the names which his father called them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 4:10–11; John 7:38; Song of Songs 4:15"> And Isaac’s servants dig in the valley, and find there a well of living water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:25"> and shepherds of Gerar strive with shepherds of Isaac, saying, “The water &#91;is&#93; ours”; and he calls the name of the well “Strife,” because they have striven habitually with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 4:6"> and they dig another well, and they strive also for it, and he calls its name “Hatred.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 17:6; Exodus 1:7; Psalms 118:5"> And he removes from there, and digs another well, and they have not striven for it, and he calls its name “Enlargements,” and says, “For—now has YHWH given enlargement to us, and we have been fruitful in the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Genesis 21:31; Genesis 46:1"> And he goes up from there &#91;to&#93; Beer-Sheba,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 15:1; Exodus 3:6; Isaiah 43:1–2"> and YHWH appears to him during that night and says, “I &#91;am&#93; the God of your father Abraham, do not fear, for I &#91;am&#93; with you, and have blessed you, and have multiplied your seed, because of My servant Abraham”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Psalms 116:17; Genesis 12:7–8"> and he builds there an altar, and preaches in the Name of YHWH, and stretches out there his tent, and there Isaac’s servants dig a well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–32; Genesis 20:3"> And Abimelech has gone to him from Gerar, and Ahuzzath his friend, and Phichol head of his host;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 26:16; Judges 11:7; Acts 7:14"> and Isaac says to them, “Why have you come to me, and you have hated me, and you send me away from you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–23; Isaiah 45:14; Isaiah 61:9"> And they say, “We have certainly seen that YHWH has been with you, and we say, Now let there be an oath between us, between us and you, and let us make a covenant with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 24:31; Genesis 21:22; Genesis 22:17"> do no evil with us, as we have not touched you, and as we have only done good with you, and send you away in peace; you &#91;are&#93; now blessed of YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:3; Romans 12:18; Hebrews 12:14"> And he makes a banquet for them, and they eat and drink,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:16–17; Hebrews 6:16; Genesis 25:33"> and rise early in the morning, and swear to one another, and Isaac sends them away, and they go from him in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 2:4–5"> And it comes to pass during that day that Isaac’s servants come and declare to him concerning the circumstances of the well which they have dug, and say to him, “We have found water”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Genesis 26:28"> and he calls it Shebah, &#91;oath,&#93; therefore the name of the city &#91;is&#93; Beer-Sheba, &#91;Well of the Oath,&#93; to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 36:13; Exodus 34:16"> And Esau is a son of forty years, and he takes a wife, Judith, daughter of Beeri the Hittite, and Bashemath, daughter of Elon the Hittite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 27:46–2; Genesis 28:8; Genesis 6:2"> and they are a bitterness of spirit to Isaac and to Rebekah.</span>
</div>
