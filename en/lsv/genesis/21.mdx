<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 12:6; Genesis 18:10; Genesis 18:14"> And YHWH has looked after Sarah as He has said, and YHWH does to Sarah as He has spoken;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 4:22; Hebrews 11:11; Genesis 18:10"> and Sarah conceives, and bears a son to Abraham, to his old age, at the appointed time that God has spoken of with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 17:19; Romans 9:7; Genesis 21:6"> and Abraham calls the name of his son who is born to him, whom Sarah has borne to him—Isaac;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:8; Deuteronomy 12:32; John 7:22–23"> and Abraham circumcises his son Isaac, &#91;being&#93; a son of eight days, as God has commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; Romans 4:19; Genesis 17:1"> And Abraham &#91;is&#93; a son of one hundred years in his son Isaac being born to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 126:2; Isaiah 54:1; Genesis 17:17"> and Sarah says, “God has made laughter for me; everyone who is hearing laughs for me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 18:11–12; Isaiah 66:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:10"> She also says, “Who has said to Abraham, Sarah has suckled sons, that I have born a son for his old age?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:22; Hosea 1:8; Genesis 40:20"> And the boy grows, and is weaned, and Abraham makes a great banquet in the day of Isaac’s being weaned;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 4:29; Genesis 16:15; Galatians 4:22"> and Sarah sees the son of Hagar the Egyptian, whom she has borne to Abraham, mocking,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:35; 1 John 2:19; Galatians 4:22–31"> and she says to Abraham, “Cast out this handmaid and her son; for the son of this handmaid has no possession with my son—with Isaac.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 12:11; Genesis 17:18; Matthew 10:37"> And the thing is very wrong in the eyes of Abraham, for his son’s sake;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:18; Romans 9:7–8; Genesis 17:19"> and God says to Abraham, “Let it not be wrong in your eyes because of the youth, and because of your handmaid: all that Sarah says to you—listen to her voice, for in Isaac is a seed called to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 16:10; Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:18"> As for the son of the handmaid also, for a nation I set him, because he &#91;is&#93; your seed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Psalms 119:60; Genesis 16:7"> And Abraham rises early in the morning, and takes bread, and a bottle of water, and gives to Hagar (placing &#91;it&#93; on her shoulder), also the boy, and sends her out; and she goes on, and goes astray in the wilderness of Beer-Sheba;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 3:9; Psalms 63:1; Jeremiah 14:3"> and the water is consumed from the bottle, and she places the boy under one of the shrubs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Genesis 29:11; 1 Samuel 24:16"> And she goes and sits by herself opposite &#91;him&#93;, far off, about a bow-shot, for she said, “Do not let me look on the death of the boy”; and she sits opposite &#91;him&#93;, and lifts up her voice, and weeps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 16:11; Psalms 50:15; Exodus 3:7"> And God hears the voice of the youth; and the messenger of God calls to Hagar from the heavens and says to her, “What to you, Hagar? Do not fear; for God has listened to the voice of the youth where he &#91;is&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 21:13; Genesis 16:10; Genesis 17:20"> rise, lift up the youth, and lay hold on him with your hand, for I set him for a great nation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 22:31; 2 Kings 6:17–20; Isaiah 35:5–6"> And God opens her eyes, and she sees a well of water, and she goes and fills the bottle &#91;with&#93; water, and causes the youth to drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Genesis 39:21; Genesis 16:12"> and God is with the youth, and he grows, and dwells in the wilderness, and is an archer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 24:3–4; Numbers 12:16"> and he dwells in the wilderness of Paran, and his mother takes for him a wife from the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 3:7; Genesis 26:26; Genesis 26:28"> And it comes to pass at that time that Abimelech speaks—Phichol also, head of his host—to Abraham, saying, “God &#91;is&#93; with you in all that you are doing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Genesis 20:14; Hebrews 6:16"> and now, swear to me by God here: you do not lie to me, or to my continuator, or to my successor; according to the kindness which I have done with you—do with me, and with the land in which you have sojourned.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 6:16; Genesis 14:13; Romans 12:18"> And Abraham says, “I swear.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 26:15–22; Genesis 29:8; Proverbs 25:9"> And Abraham reasoned with Abimelech concerning the matter of a well of water which Abimelech’s servants have violently taken away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 13:7; 2 Kings 5:20–24"> and Abimelech says, “I have not known who has done this thing, and even you did not declare to me, and I also, I have not heard except today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 14:22–23; Isaiah 32:8; Romans 1:31"> And Abraham takes sheep and oxen, and gives to Abimelech, and they make, both of them, a covenant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and Abraham sets seven lambs of the flock by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:14; Genesis 33:8; Exodus 12:26"> And Abimelech says to Abraham, “What &#91;are&#93; they—these seven lambs which you have set by themselves?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 31:52; Joshua 24:27; Joshua 22:27–28"> And he says, “For—the seven lambs you accept from my hand, so that it becomes a witness for me that I have dug this well”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 26:33; Genesis 21:14; 1 Kings 4:25"> therefore he has called that place “Beer-Sheba,” for both of them have sworn there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 13:17; Genesis 21:27; Judges 13:1"> And they make a covenant in Beer-Sheba, and Abimelech rises—Phichol also, head of his host—and they return to the land of the Philistines;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Isaiah 40:28; Psalms 90:2"> and &#91;Abraham&#93; plants a tamarisk in Beer-Sheba, and preaches there in the Name of YHWH, the perpetual God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; 1 Peter 2:11; Hebrews 11:9"> and Abraham sojourns in the land of the Philistines many days.</span>
