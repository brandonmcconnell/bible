<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 9:7; Genesis 1:28; Genesis 8:17"> And God blesses Noah and his sons, and says to them, “Be fruitful, and multiply, and fill the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Genesis 1:28; James 3:7"> and your fear and your dread is on every beast of the earth, and on every bird of the heavens, on all that creeps on the ground, and on all fishes of the sea—into your hand they have been given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Colossians 2:21–22; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> Every creeping thing that is alive, to you it is for food; as the green herb I have given to you the whole;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:23; Deuteronomy 15:23; Deuteronomy 12:16"> only flesh in its life—its blood—you do not eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 21:28–29; Exodus 21:12; Genesis 4:9–10"> And only your blood for your lives do I require; from the hand of every living thing I require it, and from the hand of man, from the hand of every man’s brother I require the life of man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17; Matthew 26:52; Revelation 13:10"> whoever sheds man’s blood, by man is his blood shed: for in the image of God has He made man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 1:28; Genesis 9:1; Genesis 9:19"> And you, be fruitful and multiply, teem in the earth, and multiply in it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And God speaks to Noah, and to his sons with him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 6:18; Isaiah 54:9–10; Romans 1:3"> “And I, behold, I am establishing My covenant with you, and with your seed after you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 8:1; Genesis 9:15–16; Psalms 36:5–6"> and with every living creature which &#91;is&#93; with you, among bird, among livestock, and among every beast of the earth with you, from all who are going out of the Ark—to every beast of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 54:9; Genesis 8:21–22; Genesis 7:21–23"> And I have established My covenant with you, and all flesh is not cut off anymore by waters of a flood, and there is not a flood to destroy the earth anymore.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 17:11; Matthew 26:26–28; Exodus 12:13"> And God says, “This is a token of the covenant which I am giving between Me and you, and every living creature that &#91;is&#93; with you, to continuous generations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Revelation 4:3; Revelation 10:1"> My bow I have given in the cloud, and it has been for a token of a covenant between Me and the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and it has come to pass (in My sending a cloud over the earth) that the bow has been seen in the cloud,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Ezekiel 16:60; Isaiah 54:8–10"> and I have remembered My covenant which is between Me and you, and every living creature among all flesh, and the waters no longer become a flood to destroy all flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 54:8–10; Jeremiah 32:40; 2 Samuel 23:5"> and the bow has been in the cloud, and I have seen it—to remember the perpetual covenant between God and every living creature among all flesh which &#91;is&#93; on the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And God says to Noah, “This &#91;is&#93; a token of the covenant which I have established between Me and all flesh that &#91;is&#93; on the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 10:1; Genesis 10:6; Genesis 9:25–27"> And the sons of Noah who are going out of the Ark are Shem, and Ham, and Japheth; and Ham is father of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:4–28; Genesis 8:17; Genesis 5:32"> These three &#91;are&#93; sons of Noah, and from these has all the earth been overspread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:6; Proverbs 10:11; Isaiah 28:24–26"> And Noah remains a man of the ground, and plants a vineyard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Habakkuk 2:15–16; Proverbs 23:31–32"> and drinks of the wine, and is drunken, and uncovers himself in the midst of the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 30:17; Galatians 6:1; Matthew 18:15"> And Ham, father of Canaan, sees the nakedness of his father, and declares to his two brothers outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 6:1; Exodus 20:12; 1 Timothy 5:17"> And Shem takes—Japheth also—the garment, and they place on the shoulder of them both, and go backward, and cover the nakedness of their father; and their faces &#91;are&#93; backward, and their father’s nakedness they have not seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And Noah awakens from his wine, and knows that which his young son has done to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 9:23; Deuteronomy 27:16; Judges 1:28–30"> and says: “Cursed &#91;is&#93; Canaan, &#124;&#124; Servant of servants he is to his brothers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 144:15; Genesis 12:1–3; Luke 3:23–36"> And he says: “Blessed of my God YHWH &#91;is&#93; Shem, &#124;&#124; And Canaan is servant to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 11:12; Romans 15:12; Hebrews 11:9–10"> God gives beauty to Japheth, &#124;&#124; And he dwells in tents of Shem, &#124;&#124; And Canaan is servant to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Noah lives after the flood three hundred and fifty years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 5:27; Psalms 90:10; Genesis 5:32"> and all the days of Noah are nine hundred and fifty years, and he dies.</span>
