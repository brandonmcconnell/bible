<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 26:1; Genesis 26:6; Genesis 18:1"> And Abraham journeys from there toward the land of the south, and dwells between Kadesh and Shur, and sojourns in Gerar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:15; Genesis 26:7; Genesis 12:11–13"> and Abraham says concerning his wife Sarah, “She is my sister”; and Abimelech king of Gerar sends and takes Sarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 20:7; Matthew 27:19; Psalms 105:14"> And God comes to Abimelech in a dream of the night and says to him, “Behold, you &#91;are&#93; a dead man, because of the woman whom you have taken—and she married to a husband.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 18:23–25; 1 Chronicles 21:17; 2 Samuel 4:11"> And Abimelech has not drawn near to her, and he says, “Lord, do you also slay a righteous nation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 7:8; 1 Kings 9:4; Daniel 6:22"> Has he not himself said to me, She &#91;is&#93; my sister! And she, even she herself, said, He &#91;is&#93; my brother; in the integrity of my heart, and in the innocence of my hands, I have done this.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:34; Proverbs 21:1; 1 Samuel 25:26"> And God says to him in the dream, “Indeed, I have known that in the integrity of your heart you have done this, and I withhold you, even I, from sinning against Me, therefore I have not permitted you to come against her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 42:8; 1 Samuel 7:5; 2 Samuel 24:17"> and now send back the man’s wife, for he &#91;is&#93; inspired, and he prays for you, and you live; and if you do not send back, know that dying you die, you and all that you have.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Abimelech rises early in the morning, and calls for all his servants, and speaks all these words in their ears; and the men fear exceedingly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 12:18; Genesis 26:10; Genesis 39:9"> and Abimelech calls for Abraham and says to him, “What have you done to us? And what have I sinned against you, that you have brought on me, and on my kingdom, a great sin? Works which are not done you have done with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Abimelech also says to Abraham, “What have you seen that you have done this thing?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 12:12; Job 28:28"> And Abraham says, “Because I said, Surely the fear of God is not in this place, and they have slain me for the sake of my wife;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 11:29; Genesis 12:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> and also, she is truly my sister, daughter of my father, only not daughter of my mother, and she becomes my wife;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 12:1; Genesis 12:9; Acts 7:3–5"> and it comes to pass, when God has caused me to wander from my father’s house, that I say to her, This &#91;is&#93; your kindness which you do with me: at every place to where we come, say of me, He &#91;is&#93; my brother.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 12:16; Genesis 20:2; Genesis 20:7"> And Abimelech takes sheep and oxen, and servants and handmaids, and gives to Abraham, and sends back his wife Sarah to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 34:10; Genesis 13:9; Genesis 47:6"> and Abimelech says, “Behold, my land &#91;is&#93; before you, where it is good in your eyes, dwell”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5; Genesis 20:5; Genesis 24:65"> and to Sarah he has said, “Behold, I have given one thousand pieces of silver to your brother; behold, it is to you a covering of eyes, to all who are with you”; and by all this she is reasoned with.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 5:16; Matthew 21:22; Job 42:9–10"> And Abraham prays to God, and God heals Abimelech and his wife, and his handmaids, and they bear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 12:17; Genesis 30:2; Genesis 16:2"> for YHWH restraining had restrained every womb of the house of Abimelech, because of Sarah, Abraham’s wife.</span>
