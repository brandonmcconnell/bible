<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 1:28"> And it comes to pass that mankind has begun to multiply on the face of the ground, and daughters have been born to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 82:6–7; 1 John 2:16; 2 Peter 2:14"> and the sons of God see that they, the daughters of men, &#91;are&#93; beautiful, and they take women for themselves of all whom they have chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:30; Isaiah 63:10; Jeremiah 11:11"> And YHWH says, “My Spirit does not strive in man for all time, for indeed, he &#91;is&#93; flesh, but his days have been one hundred and twenty years.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 13:33; 2 Samuel 21:15–22; 1 Samuel 17:4"> The giants were in the earth in those days, and even afterward, when sons of God come in to the daughters of men, and they have borne to them those who &#91;were&#93; the mighty from of old, the men of renown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; Romans 1:28–31; Mark 7:21–23"> And YHWH sees that the wickedness of man &#91;is&#93; abundant in the earth, and every imagination of the thoughts of his heart &#91;is&#93; only evil every day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; 1 Samuel 15:11; Ephesians 4:30"> and YHWH regrets that He has made man in the earth, and He grieves Himself—to His heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Psalms 37:20; Jeremiah 12:3–4"> And YHWH says, “I wipe away man whom I have created from off the face of the ground, from man to beast, to creeping thing, and to bird of the heavens, for I have regretted that I have made them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:19; Hebrews 4:16; Luke 1:30"> And Noah found grace in the eyes of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:6; Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5"> These &#91;are&#93; the generations of Noah: Noah &#91;is&#93; a righteous man; he has been perfect among his generations; Noah has habitually walked with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 5:32"> And Noah begets three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:17; Hosea 4:1–2; Habakkuk 2:8"> And the earth is corrupt before God, and the earth is filled &#91;with&#93; violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 53:2–3; Proverbs 15:3; Psalms 14:1–3"> And God sees the earth, and behold, it has been corrupted, for all flesh has corrupted its way on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:1–2; 1 Peter 4:7; Ezekiel 7:2–6"> And God said to Noah, “An end of all flesh has come before Me, for the earth has been full of violence from their presence; and behold, I am destroying them with the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:20; Luke 17:27; Matthew 24:38"> Make an ark of gopher-wood for yourself; you make rooms within the Ark, and you have covered it from inside and from outside with pitch;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11; Genesis 7:20"> and this &#91;is&#93; how you do it: three hundred cubits &#91;is&#93; the length of the Ark, fifty cubits its breadth, and thirty cubits its height;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 8:6; Genesis 7:16; Ezekiel 41:16"> you make a window for the Ark, and you finish it to a cubit from above; and you put the opening of the Ark in its side; you make it &#91;with&#93; lower, second, and third &#91;stories&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; 2 Peter 2:5; Genesis 7:21–23"> And I, behold, I am bringing in the flood of waters on the earth, to destroy from under the heavens all flesh in which &#91;is&#93; a living spirit; all that &#91;is&#93; in the earth expires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; Genesis 7:7; Genesis 17:7"> And I have established My covenant with you, and you have come into the Ark—you, and your sons, and your wife, and your son’s wives with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 7:15–16; Genesis 7:8–9; Genesis 8:17"> and of all that lives, of all flesh, you bring two from every &#91;kind&#93; into the Ark, to keep alive with you; they are male and female.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 10:11–12; Genesis 2:19; Genesis 7:8–16"> From the bird after its kind, and from the livestock after its kind, &#91;and&#93; from every creeping thing of the ground after its kind, two of every &#91;kind&#93; they come to you, to keep alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 1:29–30; Psalms 145:16; Psalms 136:25"> And you, take for yourself from all food that is eaten; and you have gathered &#91;it&#93; to yourself, and it has been for you and for them for food.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; John 15:14; Matthew 7:24–27"> And Noah does according to all that God has commanded him; so he has done.</span>
