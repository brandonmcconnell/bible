<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 17:17"> And the life of Sarah is one hundred and twenty-seven years—years of the life of Sarah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 23:19; Genesis 13:18; 1 Chronicles 6:57"> and Sarah dies in Kirjath-Arba, which &#91;is&#93; Hebron, in the land of Canaan, and Abraham goes to mourn for Sarah, and to lament her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 10:15; Genesis 23:7; 1 Samuel 26:6"> And Abraham rises up from the presence of his dead, and speaks to the sons of Heth, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; Hebrews 11:9; Genesis 17:8"> “A sojourner and a settler I &#91;am&#93; with you; give to me a possession of a burying-place with you, and I bury my dead from before me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the sons of Heth answer Abraham, saying to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Genesis 14:14; Exodus 32:22"> “Hear us, my lord; a prince of God &#91;are&#93; you in our midst; in the choice of our burying-places bury your dead: none of our burying-places do we withhold from you, from burying your dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; Genesis 19:1; Proverbs 18:24"> And Abraham rises and bows himself to the people of the land, to the sons of Heth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; 1 John 2:1–2; 1 Kings 2:17"> and he speaks with them, saying, “If it is your desire to bury my dead from before me, hear me, and meet for me with Ephron, son of Zoar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 13:8; Romans 12:17"> and he gives to me the cave of Machpelah, which he has, which &#91;is&#93; in the extremity of his field; for full money does he give it to me, in your midst, for a possession of a burying-place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 34:20; Genesis 34:24; Genesis 23:18"> And Ephron is sitting in the midst of the sons of Heth, and Ephron the Hittite answers Abraham in the ears of the sons of Heth, of all those entering the gate of his city, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:24; Isaiah 32:8; Deuteronomy 19:15"> “No, my lord, hear me: the field I have given to you, and the cave that &#91;is&#93; in it, to you I have given it; before the eyes of the sons of my people I have given it to you—bury your dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 23:7; Genesis 18:2; Genesis 19:1"> And Abraham bows himself before the people of the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 13:8; 2 Samuel 24:24; Genesis 14:22–23"> and speaks to Ephron in the ears of the people of the land, saying, “Only—if you would hear me—I have given the money of the field—accept from me, and I bury my dead there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Ephron answers Abraham, saying to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:12; Exodus 30:13; Exodus 30:15"> “My lord, hear me: the land—four hundred shekels of silver; between me and you, what &#91;is&#93; it? Bury your dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12; Jeremiah 32:9; Exodus 30:13"> And Abraham listens to Ephron, and Abraham weighs to Ephron the silver which he has spoken of in the ears of the sons of Heth, four hundred silver shekels, passing with the merchant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; Genesis 50:13; Genesis 23:20"> And established are the field of Ephron, which &#91;is&#93; in Machpelah, which &#91;is&#93; before Mamre, the field and the cave which &#91;is&#93; in it, and all the trees which &#91;are&#93; in the field, which &#91;are&#93; around all its border,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; Jeremiah 32:12; Genesis 34:20"> to Abraham by purchase, before the eyes of the sons of Heth, among all entering the gate of his city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:13; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Ecclesiastes 12:5"> And after this Abraham has buried his wife Sarah at the cave of the field of Machpelah before Mamre (which &#91;is&#93; Hebron), in the land of Canaan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:10–14; Ruth 4:7–10; Genesis 49:31–32"> and established are the field, and the cave which &#91;is&#93; in it, to Abraham for a possession of a burying-place, from the sons of Heth.</span>
