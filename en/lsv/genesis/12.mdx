<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–6"> And YHWH says to Abram, “Go for yourself, from your land, and from your family, and from the house of your father, to the land which I show you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:9; Genesis 18:18; Galatians 3:14"> And I make you become a great nation, and bless you, and make your name great; and be a blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 27:29"> And I bless those blessing you, and I curse him who is cursing you, and all families of the ground have been blessed in you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Genesis 11:31; Genesis 11:27"> And Abram goes on, as YHWH has spoken to him, and Lot goes with him, and Abram &#91;is&#93; a son of seventy-five years in his going out from Haran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 14:14; Genesis 11:31; Hebrews 11:8–9"> And Abram takes his wife Sarai, and his brother’s son Lot, and all their substance that they have gained, and the persons that they have obtained in Haran; and they go out to go toward the land of Canaan; and they come to the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 11:9; Deuteronomy 11:30; Genesis 35:4"> And Abram passes over into the land, to the place of Shechem, to the oak of Moreh; and the Canaanite &#91;is&#93; then in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 18:1; Genesis 13:15"> And YHWH appears to Abram and says, “To your seed I give this land”; and there he builds an altar to YHWH, who has appeared to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:31; Genesis 4:26; Genesis 21:33"> And he removes from there toward a mountain at the east of Beth-El, and stretches out the tent (Beth-El at the west, and Hai at the east), and he builds an altar to YHWH there, and preaches in the Name of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 13:3; Genesis 24:62; Genesis 13:1"> And Abram journeys, going on and journeying toward the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 43:1; Genesis 26:1–3; 1 Kings 17:1–18"> And there is a famine in the land, and Abram goes down toward Egypt to sojourn there, for the famine &#91;is&#93; grievous in the land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 29:17; Genesis 12:14"> and it comes to pass as he has drawn near to enter Egypt, that he says to his wife Sarai, “Now behold, I have known that you &#91;are&#93; a woman of beautiful appearance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 20:11; Genesis 26:7; 1 John 1:8–10"> and it has come to pass that the Egyptians see you, and they have said, This &#91;is&#93; his wife; and they have slain me, and you they keep alive:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 20:12–13; Genesis 20:2"> please say you &#91;are&#93; my sister, so that it is well with me because of you, and my soul has lived for your sake.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 6:2; Matthew 5:28; Genesis 39:7"> And it comes to pass, at the entering of Abram into Egypt, that the Egyptians see the woman that she &#91;is&#93; exceedingly beautiful;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 20:2; Genesis 41:1; 2 Kings 18:21"> and princes of Pharaoh see her, and praise her to Pharaoh, and the woman is taken &#91;to&#93; Pharaoh’s house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 20:14; Genesis 13:2; Genesis 24:35"> and to Abram he has done good because of her, and he has sheep and oxen, and male donkeys, and menservants, and handmaids, and female donkeys, and camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:21; Genesis 20:18; Psalms 105:14–15"> And YHWH plagues Pharaoh and his house—great plagues—for the matter of Sarai, Abram’s wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 26:9–11; 1 Samuel 14:43"> And Pharaoh calls for Abram and says, “What &#91;is&#93; this you have done to me? Why have you not declared to me that she &#91;is&#93; your wife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Why have you said, She &#91;is&#93; my sister, and I take her to myself for a wife? And now, behold, your wife, take and go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 105:14–15; Proverbs 21:1; Exodus 18:27"> And Pharaoh charges men concerning him, and they send him away, and his wife, and all that he has.</span>
