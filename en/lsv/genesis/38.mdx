<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1; 2 Kings 4:8; Proverbs 13:20"> And it comes to pass, at that time, that Judah goes down from his brothers, and turns aside to a man, an Adullamite, whose name &#91;is&#93; Hirah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3; Genesis 24:3; Genesis 6:2"> and Judah sees there the daughter of a man, a Canaanite, whose name &#91;is&#93; Shuah, and takes her, and goes in to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> And she conceives, and bears a son, and he calls his name Er;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> and she conceives again, and bears a son, and calls his name Onan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; Genesis 38:26; Genesis 46:12"> and she adds again, and bears a son, and calls his name Shelah; and he was in Chezib in her bearing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:3; Genesis 21:21; Matthew 1:3"> And Judah takes a wife for Er, his firstborn, and her name &#91;is&#93; Tamar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3; Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> and Er, Judah’s firstborn, is evil in the eyes of YHWH, and YHWH puts him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Matthew 22:23–27; Ruth 4:5–11"> And Judah says to Onan, “Go in to the wife of your brother, and marry her, and raise up seed to your brother”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Deuteronomy 25:6; James 3:16"> and Onan knows that the seed is not &#91;reckoned&#93; his; and it has come to pass, if he has gone in to his brother’s wife, that he has destroyed &#91;it&#93; to the earth, so as not to give seed to his brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 11:1; 1 Chronicles 21:7"> and that which he has done is evil in the eyes of YHWH, and He puts him also to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 22:13; Ruth 1:11–13"> And Judah says to his daughter-in-law Tamar, “Abide &#91;as&#93; a widow at your father’s house, until my son Shelah grows up”; for he said, “Lest he die—even he—like his brothers”; and Tamar goes and dwells at her father’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; 2 Samuel 13:39; Judges 14:1"> And the days are multiplied, and the daughter of Shuah, Judah’s wife, dies; and Judah is comforted, and goes up to his sheep-shearers, he and Hirah his friend the Adullamite, to Timnath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And it is declared to Tamar, saying, “Behold, your husband’s father is going up to Timnath to shear his flock”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 38:26; Genesis 38:11–13; Jeremiah 3:2"> and she turns aside the garments of her widowhood from off her, and covers herself with a veil, and wraps herself up, and sits in the opening of Enayim, which &#91;is&#93; by the way to Timnath, for she has seen that Shelah has grown up, and she has not been given to him for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Judah sees her, and reckons her for a harlot, for she has covered her face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 26:15; 2 Samuel 13:11"> and he turns aside to her by the way and says, “Please come, let me come in to you,” for he has not known that she &#91;is&#93; his daughter-in-law; and she says, “What do you give to me, that you may come in to me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:33; Genesis 38:20; Proverbs 20:16"> And he says, “I send a kid of the goats from the flock.” And she says, “Do you give a pledge until you send &#91;it&#93;?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 38:25–26; Hosea 4:11; Jeremiah 22:24"> And he says, “What &#91;is&#93; the pledge that I give to you?” And she says, “Your seal, and your ribbon, and your staff which &#91;is&#93; in your hand”; and he gives to her, and goes in to her, and she conceives to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 38:14; 2 Samuel 14:5; 2 Samuel 14:2"> and she rises, and goes, and turns aside her veil from off her, and puts on the garments of her widowhood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 20:9; Leviticus 19:17; 2 Samuel 13:3"> And Judah sends the kid of the goats by the hand of his friend the Adullamite, to receive the pledge from the hand of the woman, and he has not found her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And he asks the men of her place, saying, “Where &#91;is&#93; the separated one—she in Enayim, by the way?” And they say, “There has not been in this &#91;place&#93; a separated one.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And he turns back to Judah and says, “I have not found her; and the men of the place also have said, There has not been in this &#91;place&#93; a separated one,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; Proverbs 6:33; Revelation 16:15"> and Judah says, “Let her take to herself, lest we become despised; behold, I sent this kid, and you have not found her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 21:9; Ezekiel 23:19; Judges 19:2"> And it comes to pass about three months &#91;after&#93;, that it is declared to Judah, saying, “Your daughter-in-law Tamar has committed fornication; and also, behold, she has conceived by fornication”: and Judah says, “Bring her out—and she is burned.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 37:32; Genesis 38:18; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> She is brought out, and she has sent to her husband’s father, saying, “To a man whose these &#91;are&#93;, I &#91;am&#93; pregnant”; and she says, “Please discern whose these &#91;are&#93;—the seal, and the ribbons, and the staff.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:17; John 8:9; Titus 2:11–12"> And Judah discerns and says, “She has been more righteous than I, because that I did not give her to my son Shelah”; and he has not added to know her again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 25:24"> And it comes to pass in the time of her bearing, that behold, twins &#91;are&#93; in her womb;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and it comes to pass in her bearing, that &#91;one&#93; gives out a hand, and the midwife takes and binds on his hand a scarlet thread, saying, “This has come out first.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:4; Matthew 1:3; Genesis 46:12"> And it comes to pass as he draws back his hand, that behold, his brother has come out, and she says, “What! You have broken forth—the breach &#91;is&#93; on you”; and he calls his name Perez;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:6; 1 Chronicles 2:4; Matthew 1:3"> and afterward his brother has come out, on whose hand &#91;is&#93; the scarlet thread, and he calls his name Zerah.</span>
