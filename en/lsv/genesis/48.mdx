<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; 1 Samuel 23:16; Deuteronomy 3:28"> And &#91;one&#93; declares &#91;it&#93; to Jacob and says, “Behold, your son Joseph is coming to you”; and Israel strengthens himself, and sits on the bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 35:9–12; Exodus 6:3; Genesis 28:3"> And Jacob says to Joseph, “God Almighty has appeared to me, in Luz, in the land of Canaan, and blesses me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 35:11; Genesis 26:4"> and says to me, Behold, I am making you fruitful, and have multiplied you, and given you for an assembly of peoples, and given this land to your seed after you, a continuous possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 41:50–52; Joshua 14:4; Genesis 46:20"> And now, your two sons, who are born to you in the land of Egypt, before my coming to you to Egypt, they &#91;are&#93; mine; Ephraim and Manasseh, as Reuben and Simeon, they are mine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 14:4"> and your family which you have begotten after them are yours; by the name of their brothers they are called in their inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:16–19; Matthew 2:18; Genesis 35:9"> And I—in my coming in from Padan-&#91;Aram&#93; Rachel has died by me in the land of Canaan, in the way, while yet a distance of land to enter Ephrata, and I bury her there in the way of Ephrata, which &#91;is&#93; Beth-Lehem.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Israel sees the sons of Joseph and says, “Who &#91;are&#93; these?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 27:4; Genesis 33:5; Hebrews 11:21"> And Joseph says to his father, “They &#91;are&#93; my sons, whom God has given to me in this &#91;place&#93;”; and he says, “Please bring them to me, and I bless them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 27:1; Genesis 27:27; 1 Samuel 3:2"> And the eyes of Israel have been heavy from age—he is unable to see; and he brings them near to him, and he kisses them and cleaves to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20; Genesis 45:26; Genesis 42:36"> and Israel says to Joseph, “I had not thought &#91;possible&#93; to see your face, and behold, God has also showed me your seed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Leviticus 19:32; Genesis 19:1"> And Joseph brings them out from between his knees, and bows himself on his face to the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and Joseph takes them both, Ephraim in his right hand toward Israel’s left, and Manasseh in his left toward Israel’s right, and brings &#91;them&#93; near to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 41:51; Psalms 118:16; Matthew 19:15"> And Israel puts out his right hand and places &#91;it&#93; on the head of Ephraim, who &#91;is&#93; the younger, and his left hand on the head of Manasseh; he has guided his hands wisely, for Manasseh &#91;is&#93; the firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Colossians 2:6; Isaiah 33:16"> And he blesses Joseph and says, “God, before whom my fathers Abraham and Isaac habitually walked: God who is feeding me from my being to this day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:22; Genesis 49:22; Genesis 48:5"> the Messenger who is redeeming me from all evil blesses the youths, and my name is called on them, and the name of my fathers Abraham and Isaac; and they increase into a multitude in the midst of the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 48:14; Romans 9:7–8; 1 Chronicles 21:7"> And Joseph sees that his father sets his right hand on the head of Ephraim, and it is wrong in his eyes, and he supports the hand of his father to turn it aside from off the head of Ephraim to the head of Manasseh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 19:18; Genesis 29:26; Genesis 43:33"> and Joseph says to his father, “Not so, my father, for this &#91;is&#93; the firstborn; set your right hand on his head.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:17; Numbers 1:33–35; Genesis 25:28"> And his father refuses and says, “I have known, my son, I have known; he also becomes a people, and he also is great, and yet, his young brother is greater than he, and his seed is the fullness of the nations”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 24:60; Ruth 4:11–12; Genesis 28:3"> and he blesses them in that day, saying, “By you does Israel bless, saying, God set you as Ephraim and as Manasseh”; and he sets Ephraim before Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 50:24; Genesis 46:4; Genesis 28:15"> And Israel says to Joseph, “Behold, I am dying, and God has been with you, and has brought you back to the land of your fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:5; Joshua 24:32; 1 Chronicles 5:2"> and I have given to you one portion above your brothers, which I have taken out of the hand of the Amorite by my sword and by my bow.”</span>
