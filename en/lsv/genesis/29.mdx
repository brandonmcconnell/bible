<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 6:3; Judges 6:33; Hosea 12:12"> And Jacob lifts up his feet, and goes toward the land of the sons of the east;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 49:10; John 4:6"> and he looks, and behold, a well in the field, and behold, there &#91;are&#93; three droves of a flock crouching by it, for they water the droves from that well, and the great stone &#91;is&#93; on the mouth of the well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> (When all the droves have been gathered there, and they have rolled the stone from off the mouth of the well, and have watered the flock, then they have turned back the stone on the mouth of the well to its place.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:10; Genesis 27:43; Acts 7:2"> And Jacob says to them, “My brothers, where &#91;are&#93; you from?” And they say, “We &#91;are&#93; from Haran.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 24:24; Genesis 24:29; Genesis 31:53"> And he says to them, “Have you known Laban, son of Nahor?” And they say, “We have known.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:9; Genesis 37:14; Exodus 18:7"> And he says to them, “Does he have peace?” And they say, “Peace; and behold, his daughter Rachel is coming with the flock.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:16; Galatians 6:9–10"> And he says, “Behold, the day &#91;is&#93; still great, &#91;it is&#93; not time for the livestock to be gathered; water the flock, and go, delight yourselves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 16:3; Luke 24:2; Genesis 34:14"> And they say, “We are not able, until all the droves be gathered together, and they have rolled away the stone from the mouth of the well, and we have watered the flock.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:21; Song of Songs 1:7–8; Exodus 2:15–16"> He is yet speaking with them, and Rachel has come with the flock which her father has, for she &#91;is&#93; shepherdess;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 2:17"> and it comes to pass, when Jacob has seen Rachel, daughter of his mother’s brother Laban, and the flock of his mother’s brother Laban, that Jacob comes near and rolls the stone from off the mouth of the well, and waters the flock of his mother’s brother Laban.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 33:4; Genesis 45:14–15; Genesis 45:2"> And Jacob kisses Rachel, and lifts up his voice, and weeps,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 24:28; Genesis 13:8; Genesis 14:14–16"> and Jacob declares to Rachel that he &#91;is&#93; her father’s brother, and that he &#91;is&#93; Rebekah’s son, and she runs and declares &#91;it&#93; to her father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 24:29; Acts 20:37; Romans 16:16"> And it comes to pass, when Laban hears the report of his sister’s son Jacob, that he runs to meet him, and embraces him, and kisses him, and brings him into his house; and he recounts to Laban all these things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:12–13; Genesis 2:23; Judges 9:2"> and Laban says to him, “You &#91;are&#93; surely my bone and my flesh”; and he dwells with him a month of days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 30:28; Genesis 31:7"> And Laban says to Jacob, “Is it because you &#91;are&#93; my brother that you have served me for nothing? Declare to me what your hire &#91;is&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ruth 4:11; Genesis 49:31; Genesis 31:4"> And Laban has two daughters, the name of the older &#91;is&#93; Leah, and the name of the younger Rachel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 12:11; Genesis 46:19–22; Genesis 24:16"> and the eyes of Leah &#91;are&#93; tender, and Rachel has been beautiful of form and beautiful of appearance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 12:12; Exodus 22:16–17; Genesis 34:12"> And Jacob loves Rachel and says, “I serve you seven years for Rachel your younger daughter”:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 6:11; Isaiah 6:5; Psalms 12:2"> and Laban says, “It is better for me to give her to you than to give her to another man; dwell with me”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:7; Ephesians 5:2; Hosea 12:12"> and Jacob serves for Rachel seven years; and they are in his eyes as some days, because of his loving her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 15:1; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 4:1"> And Jacob says to Laban, “Give up my wife, for my days have been fulfilled, and I go in to her”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; Ruth 4:10–13; Judges 14:10–18"> and Laban gathers all the men of the place, and makes a banquet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Micah 7:5; Genesis 38:14–15; Genesis 24:65"> And it comes to pass in the evening, that he takes his daughter Leah, and brings her to him, and he goes in to her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 16:1; Genesis 30:9–12; Genesis 46:18"> and Laban gives his maidservant Zilpah to her, to his daughter Leah, &#91;for&#93; a maidservant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Proverbs 11:31; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> And it comes to pass in the morning, that behold, it &#91;is&#93; Leah; and he says to Laban, “What &#91;is&#93; this you have done to me? Have I not served with you for Rachel? And why have you deceived me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Laban says, “It is not done so in our place, to give the younger before the firstborn;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 18:18; Judges 14:12; Genesis 2:2–3"> fulfill the period of seven &#91;for&#93; this one, and we also give to you this one, for the service which you serve with me yet seven other years.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Jacob does so, and fulfills the period of seven &#91;for&#93; this one, and he gives his daughter Rachel to him for a wife for him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; Genesis 29:24; Genesis 30:3–8"> and Laban gives his maidservant Bilhah to his daughter Rachel for a maidservant for her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 31:41; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 31:15"> And he also goes in to Rachel, and he also loves Rachel more than Leah; and he serves with him yet seven other years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:21; Matthew 10:37; Deuteronomy 21:15"> And YHWH sees that Leah &#91;is&#93; the hated one, and He opens her womb, and Rachel &#91;is&#93; barren;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:7; Exodus 4:31; Exodus 3:7"> and Leah conceives, and bears a son, and calls his name Reuben, for she said, “Because YHWH has looked on my affliction; because now does my husband love me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 34:25; Genesis 49:5–6; Genesis 34:30"> And she conceives again, and bears a son, and says, “Because YHWH has heard that I &#91;am&#93; the hated one, He also gives to me even this &#91;one&#93;”; and she calls his name Simeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 32:26–29; Genesis 49:5–7; Genesis 46:11"> And she conceives again and bears a son, and says, “Now &#91;is&#93; the time, my husband is joined to me, because I have born to him three sons,” therefore has &#91;one&#93; called his name Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 1:2; 1 Chronicles 5:2; Genesis 49:8–12"> And she conceives again and bears a son, and this time says, “I praise YHWH”; therefore has she called his name Judah; and she ceases from bearing.</span>
