<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 3:12; Genesis 3:15; Genesis 4:25"> And the man knew his wife Eve, and she conceives and bears Cain, and says, “I have acquired a man by YHWH”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 3:15; 1 John 3:12; Luke 11:51"> and she adds to bear his brother, even Abel. And Abel is feeding a flock, and Cain has been servant of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 18:12; Leviticus 2:1–11; 1 Kings 17:7"> And it comes to pass at the end of days that Cain brings from the fruit of the ground a present to YHWH;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Proverbs 3:9; Exodus 13:12"> and Abel, he has brought, he also, from the female firstlings of his flock, and from their fat ones; and YHWH looks to Abel and to his present,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Job 5:2; Luke 15:28–30"> and to Cain and to his present He has not looked; and it is very displeasing to Cain, and his countenance is fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Job 5:2; Luke 15:31–32"> And YHWH says to Cain, “Why do you have displeasure? And why has your countenance fallen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:15; Romans 2:6–10; Isaiah 3:10–11"> Is there not, if you do well, acceptance? And if you do not do well, sin &#91;&#91;or a sin-offering&#93;&#93; is lying at the opening, and its &#91;&#91;or His&#93;&#93; desire &#91;is&#93; for you, and you rule over it &#91;&#91;or by Him&#93;&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 23:35; 1 John 3:12–15; Jude 1:11"> And Cain says to his brother Abel, &#91;&#91;“Let us go into the field”;&#93;&#93; and it comes to pass in their being in the field, that Cain rises up against his brother Abel, and slays him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; John 8:44; Psalms 9:12"> And YHWH says to Cain, “Where &#91;is&#93; your brother Abel?” And he says, “I have not known; am I my brother’s keeper?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 12:24; Psalms 72:14; Hebrews 11:4"> And He says, “What have you done? The voice of your brother’s blood is crying to Me from the ground;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Job 16:18; Genesis 3:14"> and now, cursed &#91;are&#93; you from the ground, which has opened her mouth to receive the blood of your brother from your hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 9:17; Leviticus 26:36; Leviticus 26:20"> when you till the ground, it will not add to give its strength to you—a wanderer, even a trembling one, you are in the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:22; Revelation 16:9; Revelation 16:11"> And Cain says to YHWH, “My punishment is too great than to bear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 35:19; Psalms 143:7; Job 15:20–24"> behold, You have driven me today from off the face of the ground, and from Your face I am hid; and I have been a wanderer, even a trembling one, in the earth, and it has been—everyone finding me will slay me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:4; Psalms 79:12; Genesis 4:24"> And YHWH says to him, “Therefore, of any slayer of Cain it is required sevenfold”; and YHWH sets to Cain a token that none finding him will slay him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:39; Job 2:7; Genesis 4:14"> And Cain goes out from before YHWH, and dwells in the land, moving about east of Eden;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 49:11; Ecclesiastes 2:4–11; Daniel 4:30"> and Cain knows his wife, and she conceives, and bears Enoch; and he is building a city, and he calls the name of the city, according to the name of his son—Enoch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 5:21"> And born to Enoch is Irad; and Irad has begotten Mehujael; and Mehujael has begotten Methusael; and Methusael has begotten Lamech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:8; Genesis 2:18"> And Lamech takes to himself two wives, the name of the first Adah, and the name of the second Zillah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 4:2; Jeremiah 35:9–10; John 8:44"> And Adah bears Jabal, he has been father of those inhabiting tents and &#91;having&#93; purchased livestock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 21:12; Isaiah 5:12; Genesis 31:27"> and the name of his brother &#91;is&#93; Jubal, he has been father of everyone handling harp and pipe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:25; Deuteronomy 8:9; 2 Chronicles 2:7"> And Zillah, she also bears Tubal-Cain, an instructor of every craftsman in bronze and iron; and a sister of Tubal-Cain &#91;is&#93; Naamah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 23:18; Exodus 20:13; Leviticus 19:18"> And Lamech says to his wives: “Adah and Zillah, hear my voice; Wives of Lamech, give ear &#91;to&#93; my saying: For I have slain a man for my wound, &#124;&#124; Even a young man for my hurt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 18:22; Genesis 4:15"> For sevenfold is required for Cain, &#124;&#124; And for Lamech seventy-sevenfold.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 4:8; Genesis 4:10–11; 1 Chronicles 1:1"> And Adam again knows his wife, and she bears a son, and calls his name Seth, “for God has appointed for me another seed instead of Abel”: for Cain had slain him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 116:17; 1 Kings 18:24; Joel 2:32"> And to Seth, to him also a son has been born, and he calls his name Enos; then a beginning was made of preaching in the Name of YHWH.</span>
