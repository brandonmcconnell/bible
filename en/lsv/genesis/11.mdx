<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 2:6; Zephaniah 3:9; Isaiah 19:18"> And the whole earth is of one language, and of the same words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 10:10; Daniel 1:2; Genesis 14:1"> and it comes to pass, in their journeying from the east, that they find a valley in the land of Shinar and dwell there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:10; Exodus 1:14; Exodus 2:3"> and they each say to his neighbor, “Give help, let us make bricks, and burn &#91;them&#93; thoroughly”: and the brick is to them for stone, and the bitumen has been to them for mortar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; John 5:44; Luke 1:51"> And they say, “Give help, let us build for ourselves a city and tower with its head in the heavens, and make for ourselves a name, lest we be scattered over the face of all the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 18:21; Psalms 33:13–14; John 3:13"> And YHWH comes down to see the city and the tower which the sons of men have built;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–4; Genesis 3:22; Genesis 11:1"> and YHWH says, “Behold, the people &#91;is&#93; one, and one language &#91;is&#93; to them all, and this it has dreamed of doing; and now, nothing is restrained from them of that which they have purposed to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Psalms 55:9; Job 5:12–13"> Give help, let us go down there and confuse their language, so that a man will not understand the language of his companion.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 1:51; Deuteronomy 32:8; Genesis 10:25"> And YHWH scatters them from there over the face of all the earth, and they cease to build the city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 10:10; Genesis 10:5; Genesis 10:20"> therefore &#91;one&#93; has called its name Babel, for there YHWH has confused the language of all the earth, and from there YHWH has scattered them over the face of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:17–27; Genesis 10:21–22; Luke 3:34–36"> These &#91;are&#93; the generations of Shem: Shem &#91;is&#93; a son of one hundred years, and begets Arphaxad two years after the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 9:7; Psalms 144:12; Genesis 1:28"> And Shem lives after his begetting Arphaxad five hundred years, and begets sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 3:36"> And Arphaxad has lived thirty-five years &#91;&#91;or one hundred and thirty-five years&#93;&#93;, and begets Salah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Arphaxad lives after his begetting Salah four hundred and three years &#91;&#91;or four hundred and thirty years&#93;&#93;, and begets sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Salah has lived thirty years &#91;&#91;or one hundred and thirty years&#93;&#93;, and begets Eber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Salah lives after his begetting Eber four hundred and three years &#91;&#91;or three hundred and thirty years&#93;&#93;, and begets sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 10:21; 1 Chronicles 1:19; Numbers 24:24"> And Eber lives thirty-four years &#91;&#91;or one hundred and thirty-four years&#93;&#93;, and begets Peleg.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Eber lives after his begetting Peleg four hundred and thirty years &#91;&#91;or three hundred and seventy years&#93;&#93;, and begets sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 3:35"> And Peleg lives thirty years &#91;&#91;or one hundred and thirty years&#93;&#93;, and begets Reu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Peleg lives after his begetting Reu two hundred and nine years, and begets sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 3:35"> And Reu lives thirty-two years &#91;&#91;or one hundred and thirty-two years&#93;&#93;, and begets Serug.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Reu lives after his begetting Serug two hundred and seven years, and begets sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 24:2"> And Serug lives thirty years &#91;&#91;or one hundred and thirty years&#93;&#93;, and begets Nahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Serug lives after his begetting Nahor two hundred years, and begets sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 3:34; Joshua 24:2"> And Nahor lives twenty-nine years &#91;&#91;or seventy-nine years&#93;&#93;, and begets Terah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Nahor lives after his begetting Terah one hundred and nineteen years &#91;&#91;or one hundred and twenty-nine years&#93;&#93;, and begets sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 24:2; 1 Chronicles 1:26–27; Genesis 22:20–24"> And Terah lives seventy years, and begets Abram, Nahor, and Haran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 12:4; Genesis 14:12; Genesis 11:31"> And these &#91;are&#93; the generations of Terah: Terah has begotten Abram, Nahor, and Haran; and Haran has begotten Lot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 15:7; Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–4"> and Haran dies in the presence of Terah his father, in the land of his birth, in Ur of the Chaldees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 17:15; Genesis 22:20; Genesis 24:15"> And Abram and Nahor take to themselves wives; the name of Abram’s wife &#91;is&#93; Sarai, and the name of Nahor’s wife &#91;is&#93; Milcah, daughter of Haran, father of Milcah, and father of Iscah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 29:31; Genesis 21:1–2; Luke 1:7"> And Sarai is barren—she has no child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–4; Genesis 24:10"> And Terah takes his son Abram, and Lot, son of Haran, his son’s son, and his daughter-in-law Sarai, wife of his son Abram, and they go out with them from Ur of the Chaldees, to go toward the land of Canaan; and they come to Haran, and dwell there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the days of Terah are two hundred and five years, and Terah dies in Haran.</span>
