<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 32:6–7; Genesis 27:41–42; Genesis 32:16"> And Jacob lifts up his eyes, and looks, and behold, Esau is coming, and with him four hundred men; and he divides the children to Leah, and to Rachel, and to the two maidservants;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 29:30; Genesis 30:22–24; Genesis 37:3"> and he sets the maidservants and their children first, and Leah and her children behind, and Rachel and Joseph last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Luke 14:11; Genesis 18:2"> And he himself passed over before them, and bows himself to the earth seven times, until his drawing near to his brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 45:14–15; Psalms 34:4; Luke 15:20"> and Esau runs to meet him, and embraces him, and falls on his neck, and kisses him, and they weep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 127:3; Isaiah 8:18; Genesis 48:9"> and he lifts up his eyes, and sees the women and the children, and says, “What &#91;are&#93; these to you?” And he says, “The children with whom God has favored your servant.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And the maidservants draw near, they and their children, and bow themselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and Leah also draws near, and her children, and they bow themselves; and afterward Joseph has drawn near with Rachel, and they bow themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:5; Genesis 39:5; Genesis 32:13–20"> And he says, “What to you &#91;is&#93; all this camp which I have met?” And he says, “To find grace in the eyes of my lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 27:39; Philemon 1:7; Genesis 27:41"> And Esau says, “I have abundance, my brother, that which you have, let it be for yourself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 33:26; Revelation 22:4; Genesis 32:30"> And Jacob says, “No, please, now if I have found grace in your eyes, then you have received my present from my hand, because that I have seen your face, as the seeing of the face of God, and you are pleased with me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:27; Philippians 4:18; Genesis 30:43"> please receive my blessing which is brought to you, because God has favored me, and because I have all &#91;things&#93;”; and he presses on him, and he receives &#91;it&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and says, “Let us journey and go on, and I go on before you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; Ezekiel 34:15–16; Ezekiel 34:23–25"> And he says to him, “My lord knows that the children &#91;are&#93; tender, and the suckling flock and the herd &#91;are&#93; with me; when they have beaten them one day, then all the flock has died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 32:3; Isaiah 40:11; Romans 15:1"> Please let my lord pass over before his servant, and I lead on gently, according to the foot of the work which &#91;is&#93; before me, and to the foot of the children, until I come to my lord, to Seir.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Genesis 47:25; Genesis 34:11"> And Esau says, “Please let me place with you some of the people who &#91;are&#93; with me”; and he said, “Why &#91;is&#93; this? I find grace in the eyes of my lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Esau turns back on that day on his way to Seir;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 60:6; Joshua 13:27; Judges 8:5"> and Jacob has journeyed to Succoth, and builds a house for himself, and has made shelters for his livestock, therefore he has called the name of the place Succoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 9:1; Acts 7:16; Joshua 24:1"> And Jacob comes safe &#91;to the&#93; city of Shechem, which &#91;is&#93; in the land of Canaan, in his coming from Padan-Aram, and encamps before the city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:16; John 4:5; Joshua 24:32"> and he buys the portion of the field where he has stretched out his tent, from the hand of the sons of Hamor, father of Shechem, for one hundred kesitah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:7; Genesis 21:33; Genesis 32:28"> and he sets up there an altar, and proclaims at it God—the God of Israel.</span>
