<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 91:11; Psalms 34:7; Hebrews 1:4"> And Jacob has gone on his way, and messengers of God come on him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8; Joshua 5:14; Joshua 21:38"> and Jacob says, when he has seen them, “This &#91;is&#93; the camp of God”; and he calls the name of that place “Two Camps.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:30; Joshua 24:4; Deuteronomy 2:5"> And Jacob sends messengers before him to his brother Esau, toward the land of Seir, the field of Edom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:6; Luke 14:11; Genesis 33:8"> and commands them, saying, “Thus you say to my lord, to Esau, Thus said your servant Jacob: I have sojourned with Laban, and I linger until now;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:8; Genesis 33:15; Genesis 33:11"> and I have ox, and donkey, flock, and manservant, and maidservant, and I send to declare to my lord, to find grace in his eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 33:1; Genesis 32:8; Genesis 32:11"> And the messengers return to Jacob, saying, “We came to your brother, to Esau, and he is also coming to meet you, and four hundred men with him”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:3; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10; Exodus 14:10"> and Jacob fears exceedingly, and is distressed, and he divides the people who &#91;are&#93; with him, and the flock, and the herd, and the camels, into two camps,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; Genesis 33:1–3"> and says, “If Esau comes to one camp, and has struck it—then the camp which is left has been for an escape.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:42; Psalms 34:4–6; Genesis 31:3"> And Jacob says, “God of my father Abraham and God of my father Isaac, YHWH who says to me, Return to your land and to your family, and I do good with you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 18:35; Genesis 24:27; 2 Samuel 7:18"> I have been unworthy of all the kind acts and of all the truth which You have done with your servant—for with my staff I passed over this Jordan, and now I have become two camps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 59:1–2; Proverbs 18:19; Matthew 6:13"> Please deliver me from the hand of my brother, from the hand of Esau: for I am fearing him, lest he come and has struck me—mother beside sons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–15; Hebrews 6:17; Titus 1:2"> and You have said, I certainly do good with you, and have set your seed as the sand of the sea, which is not numbered because of the multitude.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 18:16; Genesis 43:11; Proverbs 19:6"> And he lodges there during that night, and takes from that which is coming into his hand, a present for his brother Esau:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 1:3; Deuteronomy 8:18; Job 42:12"> female goats two hundred, and male goats twenty, ewes two hundred, and rams twenty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> suckling camels and their young ones thirty, cows forty, and bullocks ten, female donkeys twenty, and foals ten;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 33:8–9; Genesis 32:20; Proverbs 2:11"> and he gives into the hand of his servants every drove by itself, and says to his servants, “Pass over before me, and a space you put between drove and drove.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 33:3"> And he commands the first, saying, “When my brother Esau meets you, and has asked you, saying, Whose &#91;are&#93; you? And to where do you go? And whose &#91;are&#93; these before you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 32:4–5"> Then you have said, Your servant Jacob’s: it &#91;is&#93; a present sent to my lord, to Esau; and behold, he also &#91;is&#93; behind us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And he commands also the second, also the third, also all who are going after the droves, saying, “According to this manner do you speak to Esau in your finding him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 42:8–9; Proverbs 21:14; Genesis 43:11"> and you have also said, Behold, your servant Jacob &#91;is&#93; behind us”; for he said, “I pacify his face with the present which is going before me, and afterward I see his face; it may be he lifts up my face”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and the present passes over before his face, and he has lodged during that night in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:16; Deuteronomy 2:37; 1 Timothy 5:8"> And he rises in that night, and takes his two wives, and his two maidservants, and his eleven children, and passes over the passage of Jabbok;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and he takes them, and causes them to pass over the brook, and he causes that which he has to pass over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 12:3–5; Ephesians 6:12; Luke 13:24"> And Jacob is left alone, and One wrestles with him until the ascending of the dawn;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7–9; Hosea 12:3–4; Matthew 26:41"> and He sees that He is not able for him, and He comes against the hollow of his thigh, and the hollow of Jacob’s thigh is disjointed in his wrestling with Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 12:4; Psalms 115:12–13; 2 Corinthians 12:8–9"> and He says, “Send Me away, for the dawn has ascended”: and he says, “I do not send You away, except You have blessed me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And He says to him, “What &#91;is&#93; your name?” And he says, “Jacob.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 2:17; Genesis 35:10; Isaiah 62:2–4"> And He says, “Your name is no longer called Jacob, but Israel; for you have reigned with God and with men, and prevail.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Judges 13:16–18; Job 11:7"> And Jacob asks and says, “Please declare Your Name”; and He says, “Why &#91;is&#93; this, you ask for My Name?” And He blesses him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 24:10–11; Numbers 12:8; Isaiah 6:5"> And Jacob calls the name of the place Peniel: “For I have seen God face to face, and my life is delivered”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7; Genesis 19:15; Judges 8:8"> and the sun rises on him when he has passed over Penuel, and he is halting on his thigh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:5"> therefore the sons of Israel do not eat the sinew which shrank, which &#91;is&#93; on the hollow of the thigh, to this day, because He came against the hollow of Jacob’s thigh, against the sinew which shrank.</span>
