<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Acts 7:11–13; Lamentations 5:10"> And the famine &#91;is&#93; severe in the land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–8; Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 15:16"> and it comes to pass, when they have finished eating the grain which they brought from Egypt, that their father says to them, “Return, buy for us a little food.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 44:23; Genesis 43:5; 2 Samuel 3:13"> And Judah speaks to him, saying, “The man protesting protested to us, saying, You do not see my face without your brother &#91;being&#93; with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> if you are sending our brother with us, we go down, and buy for you food,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Genesis 44:26; Genesis 42:38"> and if you are not sending—we do not go down, for the man said to us, You do not see my face without your brother &#91;being&#93; with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Israel says, “Why did you do evil to me, by declaring to the man that you had yet a brother?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 43:3; Genesis 42:13; Genesis 43:27"> And they say, “The man asked diligently concerning us, and concerning our family, saying, Is your father yet alive? Have you a brother? And we declare to him according to the tenor of these things; do we certainly know that he will say, Bring down your brother?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 42:2; Deuteronomy 33:6; Genesis 45:19"> And Judah says to his father Israel, “Send the youth with me, and we arise, and go, and live, and do not die, both we, and you, and our infants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 42:37; Philemon 1:18–19; Hebrews 7:22"> I am guarantor &#91;for&#93; him, from my hand you require him; if I have not brought him to you, and set him before you—then I have sinned against you all the days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 19:16"> for if we had not lingered, surely now we had returned these two times.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 37:25; Jeremiah 8:22; Ezekiel 27:17"> And their father Israel says to them, “If so, now, do this: take of the praised thing of the land in your vessels, and take down to the man a present, a little balm, and a little honey, spices and myrrh, nuts and almonds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:25; Genesis 42:35; 1 Thessalonians 4:6"> and double money take in your hand, even the money which is brought back in the mouth of your bags, you take back in your hand, it may be it &#91;is&#93; an oversight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 42:38"> And take your brother, and rise, return to the man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 4:16; Genesis 17:1; Acts 21:14"> and God Almighty give to you mercies before the man, so that he has sent to you your other brother and Benjamin; and I, when I am bereaved—I am bereaved.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the men take this present, double money also they have taken in their hand, and Benjamin; and they rise, and go down to Egypt, and stand before Joseph;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 44:1; Genesis 43:19; Genesis 24:2–10"> and Joseph sees Benjamin with them and says to him who &#91;is&#93; over his house, “Bring the men into the house, and slaughter an animal, and make ready, for the men eat with me at noon.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the man does as Joseph has said, and the man brings in the men into the house of Joseph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 42:35; Romans 7:8; Genesis 42:21"> and the men are afraid because they have been brought into the house of Joseph, and they say, “For the matter of the money which was put back in our bags at the commencement are we brought in—to roll himself on us, and to throw himself on us, and to take us for servants—our donkeys also.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And they come near to the man who &#91;is&#93; over the house of Joseph, and speak to him at the opening of the house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 42:3; Genesis 42:10; Genesis 42:27"> and say, “O my lord, we really come down at the commencement to buy food;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 43:12; Genesis 42:27–35; Romans 13:8"> and it comes to pass, when we have come to the lodging-place, and open our bags, that behold, each one’s money &#91;is&#93; in the mouth of his bag, our money in its weight, and we bring it back in our hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and other money have we brought down in our hand to buy food; we have not known who put our money in our bags.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 20:26; Genesis 42:24; John 14:27"> And he says, “Peace to you, do not fear: your God and the God of your father has given to you hidden treasure in your bags, your money came to me”; and he brings out Simeon to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 18:4; Genesis 24:32; Luke 7:44"> And the man brings in the men into Joseph’s house, and gives water, and they wash their feet; and he gives provender for their donkeys,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 43:11; Genesis 43:16"> and they prepare the present until the coming of Joseph at noon, for they have heard that there they eat bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Philippians 2:10–11; Genesis 27:29; Genesis 42:6"> And Joseph comes into the house, and they bring to him the present which &#91;is&#93; in their hand, into the house, and bow themselves to him, to the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 42:13; 1 Samuel 25:5; 1 Samuel 17:22"> and he asks of them of peace and says, “Is your father well? The aged man of whom you have spoken, is he yet alive?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 37:7; Genesis 43:26; 2 Samuel 14:4"> And they say, “Your servant our father &#91;is&#93; well, he is yet alive”; and they bow, and pay respect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 42:13; Numbers 6:25; Genesis 30:22–24"> And he lifts up his eyes and sees his brother Benjamin, his mother’s son, and says, “Is this your young brother, of whom you have spoken to me?” And he says, “God favor you, my son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 42:24; Jeremiah 31:20; 1 Kings 3:26"> And Joseph hurries, for his bowels have been moved for his brother, and he seeks to weep, and enters the inner chamber, and weeps there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 45:1; 1 Peter 3:10; Isaiah 42:14"> and he washes his face, and goes out, and refrains himself, and says, “Place bread.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 46:34; Exodus 8:26; Genesis 43:16"> And they place for him by himself, and for them by themselves, and for the Egyptians who are eating with him by themselves: for the Egyptians are unable to eat bread with the Hebrews, for it &#91;is&#93; an abomination to the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And they sit before him, the firstborn according to his birthright, and the young one according to his youth, and the men wonder one at another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 45:22; 2 Samuel 11:8; 1 Samuel 1:5"> and he lifts up gifts from before him to them, and the gift of Benjamin is five hands more than the gifts of all of them; and they drink, indeed, they drink abundantly with him.</span>
