<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 1:1; Acts 2:5–11; Hebrews 11:13"> Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the chosen sojourners of the dispersion of Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:13; Ephesians 1:4–5; Hebrews 12:24"> according to &#91;the&#93; foreknowledge of God the Father, by &#91;the&#93; sanctification of the Spirit, to &#91;the&#93; obedience and sprinkling of the blood of Jesus Christ: Grace and peace be multiplied to you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:4; Romans 15:13"> Blessed &#91;is&#93; the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who, according to the abundance of His kindness begot us again to a living hope, through the resurrection of Jesus Christ out of the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Hebrews 9:15; 1 Peter 5:4"> to an incorruptible inheritance, and undefiled, and unfading, reserved for you in the heavens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Ephesians 2:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:13–14"> who, in the power of God are being guarded, through faith, to salvation, ready to be revealed in the last time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 5:10; Romans 12:12"> in which you are glad, a little now, if it be necessary, being made to sorrow in various trials,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 23:10; Isaiah 48:10; 1 Peter 4:12"> so that the proof of your faith—much more precious than gold that is perishing, and being approved through fire—may be found to &#91;result in&#93; praise, and honor, and glory, at the revelation of Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 20:29; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Romans 15:13"> whom, having not seen, you love, in whom, now believing &#91;although&#93; not seeing, you are glad with unspeakable joy and have been filled with glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 6:22; James 1:21; Hebrews 11:13"> receiving the outcome of the faith—salvation of your souls;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:17; Luke 10:24; Luke 24:25–27"> concerning which salvation &#91;the&#93; prophets sought out and searched out, who prophesied concerning the grace toward you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 52:13–14; 2 Peter 1:21; Luke 24:25–27"> searching in regard to what or what manner of time the Spirit of Christ that was in them was signifying, testifying beforehand of the sufferings of Christ and the glory after these,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 15:26; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Ephesians 3:10"> to whom it was revealed, that not to themselves, but to us they were ministering these, which now were told to you (through those who proclaimed good news to you), by the Holy Spirit sent from Heaven, to which things messengers desire to look into intently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Luke 12:35; 1 Peter 4:7"> For this reason, having girded up the loins of your mind, being sober, hope perfectly on the grace that is being brought to you in the revelation of Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Titus 3:3–5; 1 Peter 4:2–3"> as obedient children, not fashioning yourselves to the former desires in your ignorance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:48; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Hebrews 12:14"> but according as He who called you &#91;is&#93; holy, you also, become holy in all behavior,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Leviticus 19:2; Leviticus 20:7"> because it has been written: “Become holy, because I am holy”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:34–35; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Matthew 6:9"> and if you call on the Father, who is judging without favoritism according to the work of each, pass the time of your sojourn in fear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:20; Titus 2:14; Psalms 49:7–8"> having known that, not with corruptible things—silver or gold—were you redeemed from your foolish behavior inherited from our forefathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 1:7; 1 Peter 2:22–24; John 1:29"> but with precious blood, as of a lamb unblemished and unspotted—Christ’s—</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:9–10; Hebrews 9:26"> foreknown, indeed, before the foundation of the world, and revealed in the last times because of you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Hebrews 2:9; John 12:44"> who through Him believe in God who raised &#91;Him&#93; out of the dead, and gave glory to Him, so that your faith and hope may be in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Hebrews 13:1; Romans 12:9–10"> Having purified your souls in the obedience of the truth through the Spirit to unhypocritical brotherly love, love one another earnestly out of a pure heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; Hebrews 4:12; John 1:13"> being begotten again, not out of corruptible seed, but incorruptible, through a word of God—living and remaining—throughout the age;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; Psalms 103:15; James 4:14"> because all flesh &#91;is&#93; as grass, and all glory of man as flower of grass; the grass withered, and the flower of it fell away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Matthew 5:18; Psalms 119:89"> but the saying of the LORD remains—throughout the age; and this is the saying of good news that was proclaimed to you.</span>
