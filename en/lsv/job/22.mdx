<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Eliphaz the Temanite answers and says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:10; Galatians 6:7–8; Job 35:6–8"> “Is a man profitable to God, &#124;&#124; Because a wise man is profitable to himself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Psalms 147:10–11"> Is it a delight to the Mighty One &#124;&#124; That you are righteous? Is it gain, &#124;&#124; That you make your ways perfect?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 14:3; Psalms 143:2; Revelation 3:19"> Because of your reverence &#124;&#124; Does He reason &#91;with&#93; you? He enters with you into judgment:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Job 15:5–6; Job 15:31–34"> Is your wickedness not abundant? And there is no end to your iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:26; Ezekiel 18:12; Ezekiel 18:16"> For you take a pledge of your brother for nothing, &#124;&#124; And you strip off the garments of the naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 31:17; Matthew 25:42; Isaiah 58:7"> You do not cause the weary to drink water, &#124;&#124; And you withhold bread from the hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 9:15; Job 29:7–17; Job 13:8"> As for the man of arm—he has the earth, &#124;&#124; And the accepted of face—he dwells in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 24:21; Job 24:3; Ezekiel 22:7"> You have sent widows away empty, &#124;&#124; And the arms of the fatherless are bruised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 13:21; Job 6:4"> Therefore snares &#91;are&#93; all around you, &#124;&#124; And sudden fear troubles you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Lamentations 3:54; Jonah 2:3"> Or darkness—you do not see, &#124;&#124; And abundance of waters covers you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 11:8; Isaiah 66:1; Psalms 8:3–4"> Is God not high &#91;in&#93; the heavens? And see the summit of the stars, &#124;&#124; That they are high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Psalms 10:11; Ezekiel 9:9"> And you have said, How has God known? Does He judge through thickness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Psalms 139:11–12; Psalms 33:14"> Thick clouds &#91;are&#93; a secret place to Him, &#124;&#124; And He does not see, &#124;&#124; And He habitually walks &#91;above&#93; the circle of the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:5; Genesis 6:11–13"> Do you observe the path of the age, &#124;&#124; That men of iniquity have trodden,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 15:32; Genesis 7:17–24; Psalms 55:23"> Who have been cut down unexpectedly? A flood is poured out on their foundation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 21:14–15; Romans 1:28; Psalms 4:6"> Those saying to God, Turn aside from us, &#124;&#124; And what does the Mighty One do to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 21:16; Job 12:6; Acts 14:17"> And He has filled their houses &#91;with&#93; good (And the counsel of the wicked &#124;&#124; Has been far from me).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 58:10; Psalms 107:42; Psalms 52:6"> The righteous see and they rejoice, &#124;&#124; And the innocent mocks at them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6–7; Job 1:16; Job 20:26"> Surely our substance has not been cut off, &#124;&#124; And fire has consumed their excellence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Isaiah 27:5; Philippians 4:7"> Now acquaint yourself with Him, and be at peace, &#124;&#124; Thereby your increase &#91;is&#93; good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Job 23:12; Deuteronomy 4:1–2"> Please receive a law from His mouth, &#124;&#124; And set His sayings in your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 11:13–14; Zechariah 1:3; Job 8:5–6"> If you return to the Mighty you are built up, &#124;&#124; You put iniquity far from your tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:12; 1 Kings 9:28; Genesis 10:29"> So as to set a defense on the dust, &#124;&#124; And a covering on a rock of the valleys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 33:6; Psalms 16:5–6; Genesis 15:1"> And the Mighty has been your defense, &#124;&#124; And silver &#91;is&#93; strength to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Isaiah 58:14; Job 27:10"> For then you delight yourself on the Mighty, &#124;&#124; And lift up your face to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Psalms 116:1; 1 John 5:14–15"> You make supplication to Him, &#124;&#124; And He hears you, &#124;&#124; And you complete your vows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Psalms 90:17; Psalms 20:4"> And you decree a saying, &#124;&#124; And it is established to you, &#124;&#124; And light has shone on your ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 4:6; Job 5:19–27; Luke 1:52"> For they have made low, &#124;&#124; And you say, Lift up. And He saves the bowed down of eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 42:7–8; James 5:15–16; Genesis 18:26–32"> He delivers the one &#91;who is&#93; not innocent, &#124;&#124; Indeed, he has been delivered &#124;&#124; By the cleanness of your hands.”</span>
