<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 41:21; Job 31:35; Job 13:22"> Yet I speak for the Mighty One, &#124;&#124; And I delight to argue for God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:69; Job 16:2; Jeremiah 23:32"> And yet, you &#91;are&#93; forgers of falsehood, &#124;&#124; Physicians of nothing—all of you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 17:28; Job 13:13; Ecclesiastes 5:3"> O that you would keep perfectly silent, &#124;&#124; And it would be to you for wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 34:2; Job 21:2–3; Proverbs 8:6–7"> Please hear my argument, &#124;&#124; And attend to the pleadings of my lips,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 36:4; Job 27:4; Job 32:21–22"> Do you speak perverseness for God? And do you speak deceit for Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; Exodus 23:2–3; Malachi 2:9"> Do you accept His face, if you strive for God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 6:7–8; Jeremiah 17:10; Psalms 44:21"> Is &#91;it&#93; good that He searches you, &#124;&#124; If, as one mocks at a man, you mock at Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 2:9; Psalms 82:2; Psalms 50:21–22"> He surely reproves you, if you accept faces in secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 31:23; Psalms 119:120; Exodus 15:16"> Does His excellence not terrify you? And His dread fall on you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 18:17; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Isaiah 26:14"> Your remembrances &#91;are&#93; allegories of ashes, &#124;&#124; For high places of clay &#91;are&#93; your heights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 7:11; Job 21:3; Job 13:5"> Keep silent from me, and I speak, &#124;&#124; And pass over me what will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 12:3; Psalms 119:109; Ecclesiastes 4:5"> Why do I take my flesh in my teeth? And my soul put in my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 23:4; Job 23:10; Job 19:25–28"> Behold, He slays me—I do not wait! Only, I argue my ways to His face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 12:1–2; Psalms 118:21; Isaiah 33:14"> Also—He &#91;is&#93; to me for salvation, &#124;&#124; For the profane do not come before Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 21:2; Job 33:1; Job 13:6"> Hear my word diligently, &#124;&#124; And my declaration with your ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 23:4; Job 9:2–3; Job 16:21"> Now behold, I have set the cause in order, &#124;&#124; I have known that I am righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 50:7–8; Job 7:11; Job 33:32"> Who &#91;is&#93; he that strives with me? For now I keep silent and gasp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 139:12; Job 9:34–35; Revelation 6:15–16"> Only two things, O God, do with me, &#124;&#124; Then I am not hidden from Your face:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 9:34; Job 33:7; Psalms 39:10"> Put Your hand far off from me, &#124;&#124; And do not let Your terror terrify me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 14:15; Job 9:32; Job 42:3–6"> And You call, and I answer, &#124;&#124; Or—I speak, and You answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 44:20–21; Psalms 139:23; Job 36:8–9"> How many iniquities and sins do I have? Let me know my transgression and my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 19:11; Psalms 13:1; Lamentations 2:5"> Why do You hide Your face? And reckon me for an enemy to You?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 26:36; Job 21:18; 1 Samuel 24:14"> Do You terrify a leaf driven away? And do You pursue the dry stubble?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 25:7; John 5:14; Proverbs 5:11–13"> For You write bitter things against me, &#124;&#124; And cause me to possess iniquities of my youth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 33:11; Job 10:6; Acts 16:24"> And you put my feet in the stocks, &#124;&#124; And observe all my paths—You set a print on the roots of my feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 39:11; Job 4:19; Job 30:17–19"> And he, as a rotten thing, wears away, &#124;&#124; A moth has consumed him as a garment.”</span>
