<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 13:6; Mark 4:9; Psalms 49:1–3"> “And yet, please, O Job, &#124;&#124; Hear my speech and give ear &#91;to&#93; all my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 3:1; Matthew 5:2; Psalms 78:2"> Now behold, I have opened my mouth, &#124;&#124; My tongue has spoken in the palate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 27:4; Proverbs 20:15; 1 Thessalonians 2:3–4"> Of the uprightness of my heart &#91;are&#93; my sayings, &#124;&#124; And my lips have clearly spoken knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 32:8; Genesis 2:7; Job 27:3"> The Spirit of God has made me, &#124;&#124; And the breath of the Mighty quickens me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 33:32–33; Job 32:1; Psalms 50:21"> If you are able—answer me, &#124;&#124; Set in array before me—station yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 4:19; Job 10:9; Job 13:12"> Behold, I &#91;am&#93;, according to your word, for God, &#124;&#124; I have also been formed from the clay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 13:21; Job 9:34; Psalms 32:4"> Behold, my terror does not frighten you, &#124;&#124; And my burden on you is not heavy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:14; Jeremiah 29:23"> Surely you have spoken in my ears, &#124;&#124; And the sounds of words I hear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 16:17; Job 9:21"> I &#91;am&#93; pure, without transgression, &#124;&#124; I &#91;am&#93; innocent, and I have no iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 19:11; Job 13:24–25; Job 9:30–31"> Behold, He develops hindrances against me, &#124;&#124; He reckons me for an enemy to Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:27; Job 31:4; Daniel 4:35"> He puts my feet in the stocks, &#124;&#124; He watches all my paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:25; Job 36:5; Job 1:22"> Behold, you have not been righteous &#91;in&#93; this, &#124;&#124; I answer you, that God is greater than man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 40:2; Isaiah 45:9; Romans 11:34"> Why have you striven against Him, &#124;&#124; When &#91;for&#93; all His matters He does not answer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24; 2 Chronicles 33:10; Job 33:29"> For once God speaks, and twice (he does not behold it),</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 12:6; Job 4:13; Genesis 20:3"> In a dream—a vision of night, &#124;&#124; In the falling of deep sleep on men, &#124;&#124; In slumberings on a bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 36:10; Job 36:15; Psalms 40:6"> Then He uncovers the ear of men, &#124;&#124; And seals for their instruction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 2:11; James 4:10; Genesis 20:6"> To turn aside man &#91;from&#93; doing, &#124;&#124; And He conceals pride from man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 33:30; Job 33:28; 2 Peter 3:9"> He keeps back his soul from corruption, &#124;&#124; And his life from passing away by a dart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 30:17; Job 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> And he has been reproved &#124;&#124; With pain on his bed, &#124;&#124; And the strife of his bones &#91;is&#93; enduring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 107:17–18; Job 3:24; Genesis 3:6"> And his life has nauseated bread, &#124;&#124; And his soul desirable food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 19:20; Job 13:28; Proverbs 5:11"> His flesh is consumed from being seen, &#124;&#124; And his bones are high, they were not seen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 33:28; 2 Samuel 24:16; Job 17:13–16"> And his soul draws near to the pit, &#124;&#124; And his life to those causing death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Ecclesiastes 7:28; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> If there is a messenger by him, &#124;&#124; An interpreter—one of a thousand, &#124;&#124; To declare for man his uprightness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–8; Matthew 20:28; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> Then He favors him and says, &#124;&#124; Ransom him from going down to the pit, &#124;&#124; I have found an atonement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 103:5; 2 Kings 5:14; Joshua 14:10–11"> Fresher &#91;is&#93; his flesh than a child’s, &#124;&#124; He returns to the days of his youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 30:5; Acts 9:11"> He makes supplication to God, &#124;&#124; And He accepts him. And he sees His face with shouting, &#124;&#124; And He returns to man His righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 6:21; 2 Samuel 12:13; Psalms 14:2"> &#91;Then&#93; he looks on men and says, &#124;&#124; I sinned, and I have perverted uprightness, &#124;&#124; And it has not been profitable to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 33:24; Job 3:16; Job 33:22"> He has ransomed my soul &#124;&#124; From going over into the pit, &#124;&#124; And my life looks on the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 1:11; 1 Corinthians 12:6; Philippians 2:13"> Behold, God works all these, &#124;&#124; Twice, &#91;even&#93; three times with man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 56:13; Isaiah 38:17; Job 33:28"> To bring back his soul from the pit, &#124;&#124; To be enlightened with the light of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Job 18:2; Job 13:6; Job 21:2"> Attend, O Job, listen to me, &#124;&#124; Keep silent, and I speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 21:27; Job 15:4–5; Job 22:5–9"> If there are words—answer me, &#124;&#124; Speak, for I have a desire to justify you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 34:11; Proverbs 5:1–2; Psalms 49:3"> If there are not—listen to me, &#124;&#124; Keep silent, and I teach you wisdom.”</span>
