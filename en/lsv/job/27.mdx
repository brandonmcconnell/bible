<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 29:1; Numbers 24:15; Numbers 23:7"> And Job adds to lift up his allegory and says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 34:5; Numbers 14:21; 2 Kings 4:27"> “God lives! He turned aside my judgment, &#124;&#124; And the Mighty—He made my soul bitter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Job 33:4; Job 32:8"> For all the while my breath &#91;is&#93; in me, &#124;&#124; And the wind of God in my nostrils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 13:7; 2 Corinthians 11:10; John 8:55"> My lips do not speak perverseness, &#124;&#124; And my tongue does not utter deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 2:9; Job 13:15; Job 42:7"> Defilement to me—if I justify you, &#124;&#124; Until I expire I do not turn aside my integrity from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 2:3; 1 John 3:20–21; Acts 24:16"> On my righteousness I have laid hold, &#124;&#124; And I do not let it go, &#124;&#124; My heart does not reproach me while I live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 4:19; 1 Samuel 25:26; 2 Samuel 18:32"> My enemy is as the wicked, &#124;&#124; And my withstander as the perverse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Job 8:13; Job 11:20"> For what &#91;is&#93; the hope of the profane, &#124;&#124; When He cuts off? When God casts off his soul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 1:28; Jeremiah 14:12"> &#91;Does&#93; God hear his cry, &#124;&#124; When distress comes on him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 22:26–27; Psalms 37:4; Habakkuk 3:18"> Does he delight himself on the Mighty? Call God at all times?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 71:17; Isaiah 8:11; Job 4:3–4"> I show you by the hand of God, &#124;&#124; That which &#91;is&#93; with the Mighty I do not hide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 21:3; Job 16:3; Job 19:2–3"> Behold, you—all of you—have seen, &#124;&#124; And why &#91;is&#93; this—you are altogether vain?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 20:19–29; Job 15:20–35; Isaiah 3:11"> This &#91;is&#93; the portion of wicked man with God, &#124;&#124; And the inheritance of terrible ones &#124;&#124; They receive from the Mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:41; Job 20:10; Luke 23:29"> If his sons multiply—a sword &#91;is&#93; for them. And his offspring &#91;are&#93; not satisfied &#91;with&#93; bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 78:64; Jeremiah 22:18; 1 Kings 14:10–11"> His remnant are buried in death, &#124;&#124; And his widows do not weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 9:3; Matthew 6:19; Habakkuk 2:6"> If he heaps up silver as dust, &#124;&#124; And prepares clothing as clay,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 13:22; Ecclesiastes 2:26; Proverbs 28:8"> He prepares—and the righteous puts &#91;it&#93; on, &#124;&#124; And the innocent apportions the silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 1:8; Job 8:14–15; Isaiah 51:8"> He has built his house as a moth, &#124;&#124; And as a shelter a watchman has made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 24:24; Psalms 58:9; Psalms 73:19–20"> He lies down rich, and he is not gathered, &#124;&#124; He has opened his eyes, and he is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:21; Job 18:11; Job 21:18"> Terrors overtake him as waters, &#124;&#124; By night a whirlwind has stolen him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Psalms 83:15; Psalms 58:9"> An east wind takes him up, and he goes, &#124;&#124; And it frightens him from his place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4–5; Ezekiel 24:14; Job 11:20"> And it casts at him, and does not spare, &#124;&#124; He diligently flees from its hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Lamentations 2:15; 1 Kings 9:8; Zephaniah 2:15"> It claps its hands at him, &#124;&#124; And it hisses at him from his place.”</span>
