<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 15:15; Job 15:8–10; Job 4:18"> “Pray, call, is there any to answer you? And to which of the holy ones do you turn?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:9; Hosea 7:11; Genesis 30:1"> For provocation slays the perverse, &#124;&#124; And envy puts to death the simple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 73:18–20; Psalms 37:35–36; Psalms 92:7"> I have seen the perverse taking root, &#124;&#124; And I mark his habitation straight away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 127:5; Job 18:16–19; Psalms 119:155"> His sons are far from safety, &#124;&#124; And they are bruised in the gate, &#124;&#124; And there is no deliverer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Hosea 8:7; Jeremiah 51:44"> Whose harvest the hungry eat, &#124;&#124; And even take it from the thorns, &#124;&#124; And the designing swallowed their wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Lamentations 3:38; 1 Samuel 6:9"> For sorrow does not come forth from the dust, &#124;&#124; Nor does misery spring up from the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 14:1; Genesis 3:17–19; Ecclesiastes 5:15–17"> For man is born to misery, &#124;&#124; And the sparks go high to fly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Job 22:21; Psalms 37:5"> Yet I inquire for God, &#124;&#124; And for God I give my word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; Job 9:10; Psalms 86:10"> Doing great things, and there is no searching. Wonderful, until there is no numbering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:24; Acts 14:17; Psalms 147:8"> Who is giving rain on the face of the land, &#124;&#124; And is sending waters on the out-places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 113:7; 1 Samuel 2:7–8; Luke 6:21"> To set the low on a high place, &#124;&#124; And the mourners have been high &#91;in&#93; safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 33:10–11; Psalms 21:11; Isaiah 8:10"> Making void thoughts of the cunning, &#124;&#124; And their hands do not execute wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:19; Psalms 9:15–16; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> Capturing the wise in their subtlety, &#124;&#124; And the counsel of wrestling ones was hurried,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 12:25; Isaiah 59:10; Deuteronomy 28:29"> By day they meet darkness, &#124;&#124; And as night—they grope at noon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 35:10; Psalms 140:12; Psalms 109:31"> He saves the needy from the sword in their mouth, &#124;&#124; And from a strong hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 107:42; 1 Samuel 2:8–9; Psalms 63:11"> And there is hope for the poor, &#124;&#124; And perverseness has shut her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 1:12; Psalms 94:12; Proverbs 3:11–12"> Behold, the blessedness of mortal man, &#124;&#124; God reproves him: And do not despise the discipline of the Mighty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 6:1; Deuteronomy 32:39; Isaiah 30:26"> For He pains, and He binds up, &#124;&#124; He strikes, and His hands heal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; 1 Corinthians 10:13; Proverbs 24:16"> In six distresses He delivers you, &#124;&#124; And in seven evil does not strike on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 33:19; Hosea 13:14; Psalms 37:19"> In famine He has redeemed you from death, &#124;&#124; And in battle from the hands of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 31:20; Psalms 91:5–7; Jeremiah 18:18"> When the tongue scourges you are hid, &#124;&#124; And you are not afraid of destruction, &#124;&#124; When it comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:25; Isaiah 35:9; Psalms 91:13"> At destruction and at hunger you mock, &#124;&#124; And of the beast of the earth, &#124;&#124; You are not afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 26:6; Hosea 2:18; Daniel 6:22"> (For with sons of the field &#91;is&#93; your covenant, &#124;&#124; And the beast of the field &#124;&#124; Has been at peace with you.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 121:7–8; Psalms 91:10; Isaiah 4:5–6"> And you have known that your tent &#91;is&#93; peace, &#124;&#124; And inspected your habitation, and do not err,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 112:2; Psalms 72:16; Deuteronomy 28:4"> And have known that your seed &#91;is&#93; numerous, &#124;&#124; And your offspring as the herb of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 42:16–17; Proverbs 9:11; Psalms 91:16"> You come in full age to the grave, &#124;&#124; As the going up of a stalk in its season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Job 32:11–12; Proverbs 9:12"> Behold, this—we searched it out—it &#91;is&#93; right, listen; And you, know for yourself!”</span>
