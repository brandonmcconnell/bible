<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job answers and says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 4:17; Romans 3:20; Job 25:4"> “Truly I have known that &#91;it is&#93; so, &#124;&#124; But how is man righteous with God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 10:2; Job 40:2; Isaiah 57:15–16"> If he delights to strive with Him—He does not answer him one of a thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 36:5; Romans 11:33; Ephesians 3:20"> Wise in heart and strong in power—Who has hardened toward Him and is at peace?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Luke 21:11; Habakkuk 3:10"> Who is removing mountains, &#124;&#124; And they have not known, &#124;&#124; Who has overturned them in His anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 2:6; Hebrews 12:26; Psalms 75:3"> Who is shaking earth from its place, &#124;&#124; And its pillars move themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Amos 8:9; Luke 21:25–26"> Who is commanding to the sun, and it does not rise, &#124;&#124; And the stars He seals up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Isaiah 40:22; Psalms 104:2–3"> Stretching out the heavens by Himself, &#124;&#124; And treading on the heights of the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Genesis 1:16; Job 38:31–41"> Making the Great Bear, Orion, and the Pleiades, &#124;&#124; And the inner chambers of the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 5:9; Psalms 72:18; Ephesians 3:20"> Doing great things until there is no searching, &#124;&#124; And wonderful, until there is no numbering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 23:8–9; Job 35:14; Psalms 77:19"> Behold, He goes over by me, and I do not see, &#124;&#124; And He passes on, and I do not attend to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 45:9; Job 11:10; Job 23:13"> Behold, He snatches away, who brings it back? Who says to Him, What &#91;are&#93; You doing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 89:10; Job 26:12; Isaiah 30:7"> God does not turn back His anger, &#124;&#124; Proud helpers have bowed under Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:27; Job 9:3; Job 4:19"> How much less do I answer Him? Choose out my words with Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 10:15; Job 8:5; Job 10:2"> Whom, though I were righteous, I do not answer, &#124;&#124; For my judgment I make supplication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 29:24; Exodus 6:9; Psalms 66:18–20"> Though I had called and He answers me, I do not believe that He gives ear &#91;to&#93; my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 16:14; Job 2:3; Job 34:6"> Because He bruises me with a storm, &#124;&#124; And has multiplied my wounds for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:19; Lamentations 3:15; Psalms 88:15–18"> He does not permit me to refresh my spirit, &#124;&#124; But fills me with bitter things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:32–33; Job 9:4; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> If of power, behold, the Strong One; And if of judgment—who convenes me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:5–6; Philippians 3:12–15; Psalms 143:2"> If I am righteous, my mouth declares me wicked; &#91;If&#93; I am perfect, it declares me perverse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; Psalms 139:23–24; Proverbs 28:26"> I am perfect; I do not know my soul, I despise my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:1–3; Luke 13:2–4; Job 10:8"> It is the same thing, therefore I said, &#124;&#124; He is consuming the perfect and the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 24:12; Job 1:13–19; Job 4:7"> If a scourge puts to death suddenly, He laughs at the trial of the innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 10:3; Jeremiah 12:1–2; Psalms 73:3–7"> Earth has been given &#124;&#124; Into the hand of the wicked. He covers the faces of her judges, &#124;&#124; If not—where, who &#91;is&#93; he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 7:6–7; James 4:14; Psalms 90:9–10"> My days have been swifter than a runner, &#124;&#124; They have fled, they have not seen good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Isaiah 18:2; Job 39:27–30"> They have passed on with ships of reed, &#124;&#124; As an eagle darts on food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 7:13; Jeremiah 8:18; Job 7:11"> Though I say, I forget my talking, &#124;&#124; I forsake my corner, and I brighten up!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 88:15–16; Job 21:6"> I have been afraid of all my griefs, &#124;&#124; I have known that You do not acquit me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 21:27; Job 10:14–17"> I become wicked; why &#91;is&#93; this? I labor &#91;in&#93; vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; Psalms 26:6; 1 John 1:8"> If I have washed myself with snow-water, &#124;&#124; And purified my hands with soap,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; Isaiah 59:6; Job 15:6"> Then You dip me in corruption, &#124;&#124; And my garments have detested me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 9:20; Ecclesiastes 6:10; Psalms 143:2"> But if a man like myself—I answer Him, &#124;&#124; We come together into judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:25; 1 John 2:1–2; Job 9:19"> If there were a mediator between us, &#124;&#124; He places his hand on us both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 39:10; Job 13:11; Job 33:7"> He turns aside His rod from off me, &#124;&#124; And His terror does not make me afraid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> I speak, and do not fear Him, but I am not right with myself.”</span>
