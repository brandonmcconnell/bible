<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 29:9; 1 Samuel 24:2; Psalms 104:18"> “Have you known the time of &#124;&#124; The bearing of the wild goats of the rock? Do you mark the bringing forth of does?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:24"> Do you number the months they fulfill? And have you known the time of their bringing forth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> They bow down, &#124;&#124; They bring forth their young ones safely, &#124;&#124; They cast forth their pangs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Their young ones are safe, &#124;&#124; They grow up in the field, they have gone out, &#124;&#124; And have not returned to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 11:12; Job 24:5; Job 6:5"> Who has sent forth the wild donkey free? Indeed, who opened the bands of the wild donkey?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Job 24:5; Deuteronomy 29:23"> Whose house I have made the wilderness, &#124;&#124; And his dwellings the barren land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 5:18; Job 3:18; Exodus 5:13–16"> He laughs at the multitude of a city, &#124;&#124; He does not hear the cries of an exactor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 104:27–28; Psalms 145:15–16; Job 40:15"> The range of mountains &#91;is&#93; his pasture, &#124;&#124; And he seeks after every green thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 23:22; Deuteronomy 33:17; Psalms 92:10"> Is a wild ox willing to serve you? Does he lodge by your crib?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 10:10–11; Psalms 129:3; Job 39:7"> Do you bind a wild ox in a furrow &#91;with&#93; his thick band? Does he harrow valleys after you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:26; Psalms 144:14; Genesis 42:26"> Do you trust in him because his power &#91;is&#93; great? And do you leave your labor to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15; Proverbs 3:16; Amos 2:13"> Do you trust in him &#124;&#124; That he brings back your seed, &#124;&#124; And gathers &#91;it to&#93; your threshing-floor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Zechariah 5:9; 2 Chronicles 9:21"> &#91;The&#93; wing of the crying ostriches exults, but as a pinion and feather of a stork?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For she leaves her eggs on the earth, &#124;&#124; And she warms them on the dust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And she forgets that a foot may press it, &#124;&#124; And a beast of the field treads it down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 4:3; Deuteronomy 28:56–57; 1 Kings 3:26–27"> It has hardened her young ones without her, &#124;&#124; Her labor &#91;is&#93; in vain, without fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Job 17:4; Isaiah 57:17"> For God has caused her to forget wisdom, &#124;&#124; And He has not given a portion &#124;&#124; To her in understanding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 41:29; Job 39:22; Job 5:22"> At the time she lifts herself up on high, &#124;&#124; She laughs at the horse and his rider.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 147:10; Psalms 93:1; Psalms 104:1"> Do you give might to the horse? Do you clothe his neck &#91;with&#93; a mane?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:16; Job 41:20–21; Joel 2:5"> Do you cause him to rush as a locust? The splendor of his snorting &#91;is&#93; terrible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:6; Psalms 19:5; 1 Samuel 17:4–10"> They dig in a valley, and he rejoices in power, &#124;&#124; He goes forth to meet the armor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 39:16; Job 39:18"> He laughs at fear, and is not frightened, &#124;&#124; And he does not turn back from the face of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 41:26–29"> Quiver rattles against him, &#124;&#124; The flame of a spear, and a javelin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 3:6; Luke 24:41; Job 37:20"> He swallows the ground with trembling and rage, &#124;&#124; And does not remain steadfast &#124;&#124; Because of the sound of a horn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 70:3; Ezekiel 26:2; Ezekiel 36:2"> Among the horns he says, Aha, &#124;&#124; And from afar he smells battle, &#124;&#124; Roaring of princes and shouting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:15; Song of Songs 2:12; Leviticus 16:11"> By your understanding does a hawk fly? Does he spread his wings to the south?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Obadiah 1:4; Jeremiah 49:16; Exodus 19:4"> At your command does an eagle go up high? Or lift up his nest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:4"> He inhabits a rock, &#124;&#124; Indeed, he lodges on the tooth of a rock, and fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 9:26"> From there he has sought food, &#124;&#124; His eyes look attentively to a far-off place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:28; Luke 17:37; Ezekiel 39:17–19"> And his brood sucks up blood, &#124;&#124; And where the pierced &#91;are&#93;—there &#91;is&#93; he!”</span>
