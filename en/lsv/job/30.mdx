<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 12:4; Psalms 35:15–16; Psalms 69:12"> “And now, laughed at me, &#124;&#124; Have the younger in days than I, &#124;&#124; Whose fathers I have loathed to set &#124;&#124; With the dogs of my flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Also—the power of their hands, why &#91;is it&#93; to me? On them old age has perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 24:5; Hebrews 11:38; Job 24:13–16"> With want and with harsh famine, &#124;&#124; They are gnawing a dry place &#91;in&#93; the recent night, &#124;&#124; &#91;In&#93; desolation and ruin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:16; 2 Kings 4:38–39; Amos 7:14"> Those cropping mallows near a shrub, &#124;&#124; And their food &#91;is&#93; root of broom trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Daniel 4:32–33; Psalms 109:10"> They are cast out from the midst &#124;&#124; (They shout against them as a thief),</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1–2; Judges 6:2; Isaiah 2:19"> To dwell in a frightful place of valleys, &#124;&#124; Holes of earth and clefts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 6:5; Job 11:12; Genesis 16:12"> They groan among shrubs, &#124;&#124; They are gathered together under nettles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 16:22; Job 40:4; Isaiah 32:6"> Sons of folly—even sons without name, &#124;&#124; They have been struck from the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Lamentations 3:63; Job 17:6; Lamentations 3:14"> And now, I have been their song, &#124;&#124; And I am to them for a byword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 50:6; Numbers 12:14"> They have detested me, &#124;&#124; They have kept far from me, &#124;&#124; And from before me have not spared to spit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:21; Ruth 1:21; Job 12:18"> Because He loosed His cord and afflicts me, &#124;&#124; And the bridle from before me, &#124;&#124; They have cast away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 19:12; Isaiah 3:5; Job 19:18"> A brood arises on the right hand, &#124;&#124; They have cast away my feet, &#124;&#124; And they raise up against me, &#124;&#124; Their paths of calamity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:12; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 69:26"> They have broken down my path, &#124;&#124; They profit by my calamity: He has no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Psalms 18:4; Psalms 69:14–15"> They come as a wide breach, &#124;&#124; Under the desolation have rolled themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 13:3; Job 6:4; Isaiah 44:22"> He has turned terrors against me, &#124;&#124; It pursues my abundance as the wind, &#124;&#124; And as a thick cloud, &#124;&#124; My safety has passed away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Psalms 22:14; Isaiah 53:12"> And now, in me my soul pours itself out, &#124;&#124; Days of affliction seize me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 7:4; Job 33:19–21; Isaiah 38:13"> &#91;At&#93; night my bone has been pierced in me, &#124;&#124; And my gnawing &#91;pain&#93; does not lie down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:5; Job 19:20; Job 2:7"> By the abundance of power, &#124;&#124; Is my clothing changed, &#124;&#124; As the mouth of my coat it girds me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:31; Job 42:6; Genesis 18:27"> Casting me into mire, &#124;&#124; And I have become like dust and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 19:7; Psalms 80:4–5; Psalms 22:2"> I cry to You, &#124;&#124; And You do not answer me, I have stood, and You consider me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 10:14–17; Job 16:9–14; Job 19:6–9"> You are turned to be fierce to me, &#124;&#124; With the strength of Your hand, &#124;&#124; You oppress me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 27:21; Psalms 1:4; Hosea 4:19"> You lift me up, &#124;&#124; You cause me to ride on the wind, &#124;&#124; And You melt—You level me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5–7; Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> For I have known You bring me back &#91;to&#93; death, &#124;&#124; And &#91;to&#93; the house appointed for all living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Matthew 27:39–44; Job 19:7"> Surely not against the heap &#124;&#124; Does He send forth the hand, &#124;&#124; Though they have safety in its ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalms 35:13–14; Jeremiah 13:17"> Did I not weep for him whose day is hard? My soul has grieved for the needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:15; Job 3:25–26; Jeremiah 14:19"> When I expected good, then comes evil, &#124;&#124; And I wait for light, and darkness comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Jeremiah 31:20; Jeremiah 4:19"> My bowels have boiled, and have not ceased, &#124;&#124; Days of affliction have gone before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 42:9; Psalms 38:6; Psalms 43:2"> I have gone mourning without the sun, &#124;&#124; I have risen, I cry in an assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Micah 1:8; Psalms 102:6; Psalms 44:19"> I have been a brother to dragons, &#124;&#124; And a companion to daughters of the ostrich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 4:8; Psalms 102:3; Psalms 119:83"> My skin has been black on me, &#124;&#124; And my bone has burned from heat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 5:15; Isaiah 21:4; Isaiah 22:12"> And my harp becomes mourning, &#124;&#124; And my pipe the sound of weeping.”</span>
