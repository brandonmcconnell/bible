<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job answers and says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 13:4–5; Psalms 69:26; Job 11:2–3"> “I have heard many such things, &#124;&#124; Miserable comforters &#91;are&#93; you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 15:2; Titus 2:8"> Is there an end to words of wind? Or what emboldens you that you answer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 109:25; Psalms 22:7; Lamentations 2:15"> I also, like you, might speak, &#124;&#124; If your soul were in my soul’s stead. I might join against you with words, &#124;&#124; And nod at you with my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 27:9; Galatians 6:1; Isaiah 35:3–4"> I might harden you with my mouth, &#124;&#124; And the moving of my lips might be sparing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 88:15–18; Psalms 77:1–9"> If I speak, my pain is not restrained, &#124;&#124; And I cease—what goes from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:3; Job 1:15–19; Psalms 6:6–7"> Only, now, it has wearied me; You have desolated all my company,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 10:17; Job 19:20; Ruth 1:21"> And You loathe me, &#124;&#124; For it has been a witness, &#124;&#124; And my failure rises up against me, &#124;&#124; It testifies in my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 35:16; Job 13:24; Lamentations 2:16"> His anger has torn, and He hates me, &#124;&#124; He has gnashed at me with His teeth, &#124;&#124; My adversary sharpens His eyes for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:15; Psalms 22:13; Isaiah 50:6"> They have gaped on me with their mouth, &#124;&#124; In reproach they have struck my cheeks, &#124;&#124; Together they set themselves against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:12; Psalms 7:14; Psalms 31:8"> God shuts me up to the perverse, &#124;&#124; And turns me over to the hands of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 7:20; Lamentations 3:11–12; Lamentations 3:4"> I have been at ease, and He breaks me, &#124;&#124; And He has laid hold on my neck, &#124;&#124; And He breaks me in pieces, &#124;&#124; And He raises me to Him for a mark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 20:25; Job 6:4; Lamentations 2:11"> His archers go around against me. He split my reins, and does not spare, &#124;&#124; He pours out my gall to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 15:8; Lamentations 3:3–5; Joel 2:7"> He breaks me—breach on breach, &#124;&#124; He runs on me as a mighty one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 37:34; Psalms 7:5; Psalms 75:5"> I have sewed sackcloth on my skin, &#124;&#124; And have rolled my horn in the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:34; Psalms 102:3–5; Lamentations 1:16"> My face is foul with weeping, &#124;&#124; And on my eyelids &#91;is&#93; death-shade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; Psalms 66:18–19; Job 27:6–7"> Not for violence in my hands, &#124;&#124; And my prayer &#91;is&#93; pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Ezekiel 24:7; Genesis 4:11"> O earth, do not cover my blood! And let there not be a place for my cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 11:31"> Also, now, behold, my witness &#91;is&#93; in the heavens, &#124;&#124; And my testifier in the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 142:2; Job 16:4"> My interpreter &#91;is&#93; my friend, &#124;&#124; My eye has dropped to God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:10; Isaiah 45:9; Romans 9:20"> And He reasons for a man with God, &#124;&#124; As a son of man for his friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 14:10; Job 14:14; Job 14:5"> When a few years come, &#124;&#124; Then I go &#91;on&#93; the path of no return.”</span>
