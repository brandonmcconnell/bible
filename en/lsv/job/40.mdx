<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:32; Job 29:9; Job 21:5"> “Behold, I have been vile, &#124;&#124; What do I return to You? I have placed my hand on my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 33:14; Job 9:3; Job 9:15"> I have spoken once, and I do not answer, &#124;&#124; And twice, and I do not add.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 38:1; 2 Peter 3:10–12; Psalms 50:3–4"> And YHWH answers Job out of the whirlwind and says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:3; Job 42:4; Job 23:3–4"> “Now gird your loins as a man, &#124;&#124; I ask you, and you cause Me to know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:4; Isaiah 28:18; Job 32:2"> Do you also make My judgment void? Do you condemn Me, &#124;&#124; That you may be righteous?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:13; Job 37:4–5; Isaiah 45:9"> And do you have an arm like God? And do you thunder with a voice like His?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 93:1; Psalms 149:4; Isaiah 59:17"> Now put on excellence and loftiness, &#124;&#124; Indeed, put on splendor and beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 4:37; Isaiah 2:11–12; Isaiah 2:17"> Scatter abroad the wrath of your anger, &#124;&#124; And see every proud one, and make him low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 63:3; Malachi 4:3; Psalms 60:12"> See every proud one—humble him, &#124;&#124; And tread down the wicked in their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 2:10; Esther 7:8; Job 14:13"> Hide them in the dust together, &#124;&#124; Bind their faces in secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–9; Romans 5:6; Psalms 44:6"> And even I praise you, &#124;&#124; For your right hand gives salvation to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 40:20; Psalms 104:14; Genesis 1:24–26"> Now behold, behemoth, &#124;&#124; That I made with you: He eats grass as an ox.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Now behold, his power &#91;is&#93; in his loins, &#124;&#124; And his strength in the muscles of his belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 41:23"> He bends his tail as a cedar, &#124;&#124; The sinews of his thighs are wrapped together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 48:4; Job 7:12"> His bones &#91;are&#93; tubes of bronze, &#124;&#124; His bones &#91;are&#93; as a bar of iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 26:13; Isaiah 27:1"> He &#91;is&#93; a beginning of the ways of God, &#124;&#124; His Maker &#91;alone&#93; brings His sword near;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 104:26; Psalms 104:14; Job 40:15"> For mountains bear food for him, &#124;&#124; And all the beasts of the field play there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 35:7; Isaiah 19:6–7"> He lies down under shades, &#124;&#124; In a secret place of reed and marsh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:5; Leviticus 23:40; Isaiah 15:7"> Shades cover him, &#91;with&#93; their shadow, &#124;&#124; Willows of the brook cover him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Isaiah 37:25; Isaiah 28:16"> Behold, a flood oppresses—he does not hurry, &#124;&#124; He is confident though Jordan &#124;&#124; Comes forth to his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 41:1–2"> Does &#91;one&#93; take him by his eyes? &#124;&#124; Does &#91;one&#93; pierce the nose with snares?”</span>
