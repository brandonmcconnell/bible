<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Genesis 18:25"> Does God pervert judgment? And does the Mighty One pervert justice?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 1:5; Job 1:18–19; Job 5:4"> If your sons have sinned before Him, &#124;&#124; And He sends them away, &#124;&#124; By the hand of their transgression,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 4:7–10; Hebrews 3:7–8; Job 11:13"> If you seek for God early, &#124;&#124; And make supplication to the Mighty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 51:9; Isaiah 3:10; Proverbs 15:8"> If you &#91;are&#93; pure and upright, &#124;&#124; Surely now He wakes for you, &#124;&#124; And has completed &#124;&#124; The habitation of your righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 42:12–13; Matthew 13:31–32; Proverbs 19:20"> And your beginning has been small, &#124;&#124; And your latter end is very great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7; Deuteronomy 4:32; Job 15:18"> For inquire, please, of a former generation, &#124;&#124; And prepare for a search of their fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; Psalms 144:4; Job 14:2"> For we &#91;are&#93; of yesterday, and we do not know, &#124;&#124; For our days &#91;are&#93; a shadow on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 18:15; Matthew 12:35; Job 12:7–8"> Do they not show you—speak to you, &#124;&#124; And from their heart bring forth words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 2:3; Isaiah 19:5–7"> Does a rush rise without a marsh? A reed increase without water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:6; Psalms 129:6–7; Matthew 13:20"> While it &#91;is&#93; in its budding—uncropped, &#124;&#124; Even before any herb it withers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 9:17; Job 11:20; Job 13:16"> So &#91;are&#93; the paths of all forgetting God, &#124;&#124; And the hope of the profane perishes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:5–6"> Whose confidence is loathsome, &#124;&#124; And the house of a spider his trust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 27:18; Luke 6:47–49; Matthew 7:24–27"> He leans on his house—and it does not stand, &#124;&#124; He takes hold on it—and it does not abide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 80:11; Job 21:7–15; Job 5:3"> He &#91;is&#93; green before the sun, &#124;&#124; And over his garden his branch goes out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Mark 11:20; Jude 1:12"> His roots are wrapped by a heap, &#124;&#124; He looks for a house of stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:10; Psalms 37:36; Job 7:8"> If &#91;one&#93; destroys him from his place, &#124;&#124; Then it has feigned concerning him, &#124;&#124; I have not seen you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 20:5; Psalms 113:7; 1 Samuel 2:8"> Behold, this &#91;is&#93; the joy of His way, &#124;&#124; And from the dust others spring up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 4:7; Psalms 37:37; Psalms 37:24"> Behold, God does not reject the perfect, &#124;&#124; Nor takes hold on the hand of evildoers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 126:2; Genesis 21:6; Psalms 126:6"> While He fills your mouth with laughter, &#124;&#124; And your lips with shouting,</span>
