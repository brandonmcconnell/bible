<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:28; John 8:12; Job 18:6"> In His causing His lamp to shine on my head, &#124;&#124; By His light I walk &#91;through&#93; darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; Proverbs 3:32; Psalms 27:5"> As I have been in days of my maturity, &#124;&#124; And the counsel of God on my tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 128:3; Psalms 127:3–5; Deuteronomy 33:27–29"> When yet the Mighty One &#91;is&#93; with me. Around me—my young ones,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 81:16; Job 20:17; Deuteronomy 32:13–14"> When washing my goings with butter, &#124;&#124; And the firm rock &#91;is&#93; with me—streams of oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 8:16; Ruth 4:1–2; Deuteronomy 16:18"> When I go out to the gate by the city, &#124;&#124; In a broad place I prepare my seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; Proverbs 16:31; Romans 13:7"> Youths have seen me, and they have been hidden, &#124;&#124; And the aged have risen—they stood up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 10:19"> Princes have kept in words, &#124;&#124; And they place a hand on their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 137:6; Ezekiel 3:26"> The voice of leaders has been hidden, &#124;&#124; And their tongue has cleaved to the palate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Job 31:20; Proverbs 29:2"> For the ear heard, and declares me blessed, &#124;&#124; And the eye has seen, and testifies &#91;to&#93; me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 72:12; Proverbs 21:13; James 1:27"> For I deliver the afflicted who is crying, &#124;&#124; And the fatherless who has no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philemon 1:7; Job 22:9; Job 31:19–20"> The blessing of the perishing comes on me, &#124;&#124; And I cause the heart of the widow to sing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Isaiah 59:17; Psalms 132:9"> I have put on righteousness, and it clothes me, &#124;&#124; My justice as a robe and a crown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 10:31; Matthew 11:5; 1 Corinthians 12:12–31"> I have been eyes to the blind, &#124;&#124; And I &#91;am&#93; feet to the lame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; Psalms 68:5; Job 31:18"> I &#91;am&#93; a father to the needy, &#124;&#124; And the cause I have not known I search out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Proverbs 30:14; 1 Samuel 17:35"> And I break the jaw-teeth of the perverse, &#124;&#124; And from his teeth I cast away prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 30:6–7; Genesis 32:12; Job 42:16–17"> And I say, I expire with my nest, &#124;&#124; And I multiply days as the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8; Hosea 14:5–7"> My root is open to the waters, &#124;&#124; And dew lodges on my branch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:24; Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 18:34"> My glory &#91;is&#93; fresh with me, &#124;&#124; And my bow is renewed in my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Job 29:9–10"> They have listened to me, &#124;&#124; Indeed, they wait, and are silent for my counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:2; Song of Songs 4:11; Matthew 22:46"> After my word they do not change, &#124;&#124; And my speech drops on them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; Psalms 72:6; Zechariah 10:1"> And they wait for me as &#91;for&#93; rain, &#124;&#124; And they have opened wide their mouth &#124;&#124; &#91;As&#93; for the spring rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 89:15; Psalms 4:6; Psalms 126:1"> I laugh at them—they give no credence, &#124;&#124; And do not cause the light of my face to fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 3:2–3; Genesis 14:14–17"> I choose their way, and sit &#91;as&#93; head, &#124;&#124; And I dwell as a king in a troop, &#124;&#124; When he comforts mourners.”</span>
