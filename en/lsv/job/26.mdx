<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job answers and says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3–4; Job 4:3–4; Isaiah 41:5–7"> “How you have helped the powerless, &#124;&#124; Saved an arm not strong!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 71:15–18; Job 32:11–13; Job 33:3"> How you have given counsel to the unwise, &#124;&#124; And made known wise plans in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 22:23–24; Job 32:18; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> With whom have you declared words? And whose breath came forth from you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 88:10; Genesis 6:4; Job 41:1–34"> The Rephaim are formed, &#124;&#124; Also their inhabitants beneath the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 139:8; Proverbs 15:11; Hebrews 4:13"> Sheol &#91;is&#93; naked before Him, &#124;&#124; And there is no covering to destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 9:8; Psalms 104:2–5; Isaiah 40:22"> Stretching out the north over desolation, &#124;&#124; Hanging the earth on nothing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Jeremiah 10:13; Isaiah 5:6"> Binding up the waters in His thick clouds, &#124;&#124; And the cloud is not burst under them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Job 22:14; Habakkuk 3:3–5"> Taking hold of the face of the throne, &#124;&#124; Spreading His cloud over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 8:29; Job 38:8–11; Jeremiah 5:22"> He has placed a limit on the waters, &#124;&#124; To the boundary of light with darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; Psalms 18:7; Job 15:15"> Pillars of the heavens tremble, &#124;&#124; And they wonder because of His rebuke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 51:15; Jeremiah 31:35; Psalms 89:9–10"> By His power He has quieted the sea, &#124;&#124; And by His understanding struck the proud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 104:30; Psalms 74:13–14"> He beautified the heavens by His Spirit, &#124;&#124; His hand has formed the fleeing serpent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:26–29; Psalms 145:3; Romans 11:33"> Behold, these &#91;are&#93; the borders of His way, and how little a matter is heard of Him, and who understands the thunder of His might?”</span>
