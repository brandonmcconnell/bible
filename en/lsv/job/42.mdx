<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job answers YHWH and says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Mark 10:27; Genesis 18:14"> “You have known that &#91;for&#93; all things You are able, &#124;&#124; And no purpose is withheld from You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 131:1; Psalms 139:6; Job 38:2"> &#91;You said&#93;, Who &#91;is&#93; this hiding counsel without knowledge? Therefore, I have declared, and do not understand, &#124;&#124; Too wonderful for me, and I do not know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 40:7; Job 38:3; Genesis 18:30–32"> Please hear, and I speak; &#91;You said&#93;, I ask you, and you cause Me to know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; Romans 10:17; John 12:45"> By the hearing of the ear I heard You, &#124;&#124; And now my eye has seen You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; 1 Timothy 1:13–16; James 4:7–10"> Therefore I loathe &#91;it&#93;, &#124;&#124; And I have sighed on dust and ashes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 11:5–6; Job 32:2–3; Job 32:5"> And it comes to pass after YHWH’s speaking these words to Job, that YHWH says to Eliphaz the Temanite, “My anger has burned against you and against your two friends, because you have not spoken correctly concerning Me, like My servant Job.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:1; James 5:16; Psalms 103:10"> And now, take seven bullocks and seven rams for yourselves, and go to My servant Job, and you have caused a burnt-offering to ascend for yourselves; and Job My servant prays for you, for surely I accept his face, so as not to do folly with you, because you have not spoken correctly concerning Me, like My servant Job.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Acts 9:6; Acts 10:33"> And they go—Eliphaz the Temanite, and Bildad the Shuhite, Zophar the Naamathite—and do as YHWH has spoken to them; and YHWH accepts the face of Job.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:3; Job 8:6–7; Proverbs 22:4"> And YHWH has turned &#91;to&#93; the captivity of Job in his praying for his friends, and YHWH adds &#91;to&#93; all that Job has—to double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 19:13–14; Proverbs 16:7"> And all his brothers come to him, and all his sisters, and all his former acquaintances, and they eat bread with him in his house, and bemoan him, and comfort him concerning all the calamity that YHWH had brought on him, and they each gave to him one kesitah, and each one ring of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 8:7; Ecclesiastes 7:8; Proverbs 10:22"> And YHWH has blessed the latter end of Job more than his beginning, and he has fourteen thousand of a flock, and six thousand camels, and one thousand pairs of oxen, and one thousand female donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 1:2; Psalms 107:41; Psalms 127:3"> And he has seven sons and three daughters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and he calls the name of the first Jemima, and the name of the second Kezia, and the name of the third Keren-Happuch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 27:7; Psalms 144:12; Acts 7:20"> And there have not been found women &#91;as&#93; beautiful as the daughters of Job in all the land, and their father gives to them an inheritance in the midst of their brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 17:6; Psalms 128:6; Genesis 50:23"> And Job lives after this one hundred and forty years, and sees his sons, and his sons’ sons, four generations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Genesis 15:15; Job 5:26"> and Job dies, aged and satisfied &#91;with&#93; days.</span>
