<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Deuteronomy 24:15; James 5:4"> As a servant desires the shadow, &#124;&#124; And as a hired worker expects his wage,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Psalms 39:5"> So I have been caused to inherit months of vanity, &#124;&#124; And they numbered nights of misery to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:67; Job 7:13–14; Job 17:12"> If I lay down, then I have said, When do I rise, &#124;&#124; And evening has been measured? And I have been full of tossings until dawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 17:14; Isaiah 14:11; Job 2:7–8"> My flesh has been clothed &#91;with&#93; worms, &#124;&#124; And a clod of dust, &#124;&#124; My skin has been shriveled and is loathsome,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 9:25; Job 17:15; Job 17:11"> My days swifter than a loom, &#124;&#124; And they are consumed without hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalms 78:39; Jeremiah 15:15"> Remember that my life &#91;is&#93; a breath, &#124;&#124; My eye does not turn back to see good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 20:9; Job 8:18; Psalms 37:36"> The eye of my beholder does not behold me. Your eyes &#91;are&#93; on me—and I am not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 30:15; Job 10:21; 2 Samuel 14:14"> A cloud has been consumed, and it goes, &#124;&#124; So he who is going down to Sheol does not come up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 8:18; Job 20:9; Psalms 103:16"> He does not turn to his house again, &#124;&#124; Nor does his place discern him again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 40:9; 1 Samuel 1:10; Job 10:1"> Also I do not withhold my mouth—I speak in the distress of my spirit, I talk in the bitterness of my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:2–3; Lamentations 3:7; Job 38:6–11"> Am I a sea &#91;monster&#93;, or a dragon, &#124;&#124; That You set a watch over me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Psalms 77:4; Job 9:27–28"> When I said, My bed comforts me, &#124;&#124; In my talking He takes away my couch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 2:1; Genesis 41:8; Genesis 40:5–7"> And You have frightened me with dreams, &#124;&#124; And You terrify me from visions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 27:5; 2 Samuel 17:23"> And my soul chooses strangling, &#124;&#124; Death rather than my bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Job 10:1; Job 9:21"> I have wasted away—I do not live for all time. Cease from me, for my days &#91;are&#93; vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 2:6; Psalms 8:4; Psalms 144:3"> What &#91;is&#93; man that You magnify him? And that You set Your heart to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 38:12–13; Jeremiah 9:7; 1 Peter 1:7"> And inspect him in the mornings, &#124;&#124; &#91;And&#93; in the evenings try him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:18; Job 14:6; Psalms 13:1–3"> How long do You not look from me? You do not desist until I swallow my spittle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 3:12; Job 7:11–12; Job 3:24"> I have sinned, what do I do to You, &#124;&#124; O watcher of man? Why have You set me for a mark to You, &#124;&#124; And I am for a burden to myself—and what?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Job 10:14; 1 John 1:9"> You do not take away my transgression, &#124;&#124; And &#91;do not&#93; cause my iniquity to pass away, &#124;&#124; Because now, I lie down in dust, &#124;&#124; And You have sought me—and I am not!”</span>
