<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job answers and says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 32:4; Job 7:11"> “Also—today my complaint &#91;is&#93; bitter, &#124;&#124; My hand has been heavy because of my sighing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 16:21; Jeremiah 14:7; Job 13:3"> O that I had known—and I find Him, &#124;&#124; I come to His seat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 13:18; Daniel 9:18–19; Exodus 32:12–13"> I arrange the cause before Him, &#124;&#124; And fill my mouth &#91;with&#93; arguments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 13:22–23; Job 10:2; 1 Corinthians 4:3–4"> I know the words He answers me, &#124;&#124; And understand what He says to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 13:21; Job 9:33–34; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Does He strive with me in the abundance of power? No! Surely He puts &#91;it&#93; in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 13:3; Romans 8:1; Isaiah 1:18"> There the upright reason with Him, &#124;&#124; And I escape from mine who is judging—forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 10:1; Job 9:11; Job 35:14"> Behold, I go forward—and He is not, &#124;&#124; And backward—and I do not perceive Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Isaiah 8:17"> &#91;To&#93; the left in His working—and I do not see, &#124;&#124; He is covered &#91;on&#93; the right, and I do not behold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; James 1:2–4; 1 Peter 1:7"> For He has known the way with me, &#124;&#124; He has tried me—I go forth as gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:18; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Job 17:9"> My foot has laid hold on His step, &#124;&#124; I have kept His way, and do not turn aside,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Psalms 119:103; Psalms 119:11"> The command of His lips, and I do not depart. I have laid up above my allotted portion &#124;&#124; The sayings of His mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 115:3; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 46:10"> And He &#91;is&#93; in one &#91;mind&#93;, &#124;&#124; And who turns Him back? And His soul has desired—and He does &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:9; Romans 11:33"> For He completes my portion, &#124;&#124; And many such things &#91;are&#93; with Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 77:3; Job 31:23"> Therefore, I am troubled at His presence, I consider, and am afraid of Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 22:14; Job 27:2; Psalms 88:16"> And God has made my heart soft, &#124;&#124; And the Mighty has troubled me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 19:8; Isaiah 57:1; 2 Kings 22:20"> For I have not been cut off before darkness, &#124;&#124; And before me He covered thick darkness.”</span>
