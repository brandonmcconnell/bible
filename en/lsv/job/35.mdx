<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Elihu answers and says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:36–37; Job 19:6–7; Job 16:17"> “Have you reckoned this for judgment &#91;when&#93; you have said, &#124;&#124; My righteousness &#91;is&#93; more than God’s?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 34:9; Malachi 3:14; Job 9:30–31"> For you say, What does it profit You? What do I profit from my sin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Job 34:8"> I return words, and your friends with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 22:12; Isaiah 55:9; Nahum 1:3"> Behold attentively the heavens—and see, &#124;&#124; And behold the clouds, &#124;&#124; They have been higher than you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:19; Proverbs 8:36; Proverbs 9:12"> If you have sinned, what do you do against Him? And your transgressions have been multiplied, &#124;&#124; What do you do to Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 22:2–3; Psalms 16:2; Luke 17:10"> If you have been righteous, &#124;&#124; What do you give to Him? Or what does He receive from your hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Job 42:8; Acts 27:24"> For a man like yourself &#91;is&#93; your wickedness, &#124;&#124; And for a son of man your righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:23; Job 34:28; Exodus 3:7"> Because of the multitude of oppressions &#124;&#124; They cause to cry out, &#124;&#124; They cry because of the arm of the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Acts 16:25; Psalms 149:5"> And none said, Where &#91;is&#93; God my Maker? Giving songs in the night,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 94:12; Job 36:22; Job 32:8"> Teaching us more than the beasts of the earth, &#124;&#124; Indeed, He makes us wiser than the bird of the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; John 9:31; Psalms 123:3–4"> There they cry, and He does not answer, &#124;&#124; Because of the pride of evildoers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; James 4:3"> Surely God does not hear vanity, &#124;&#124; And the Mighty does not behold it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 9:11; Micah 7:7–9; Psalms 37:5–6"> Indeed, though you say you do not behold Him, &#124;&#124; Judgment &#91;is&#93; before Him, and stays for Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 89:32; Job 9:14; Job 13:15"> And now, because there is not, &#124;&#124; He has appointed His anger, &#124;&#124; And He has not known in great extremity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 38:2; Job 33:8–12; Job 34:35–37"> And Job opens his mouth &#91;with&#93; vanity, &#124;&#124; He multiplies words without knowledge.”</span>
