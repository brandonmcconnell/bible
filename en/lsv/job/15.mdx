<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 42:7; Job 2:11; Job 22:1"> And Eliphaz the Temanite answers and says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 8:2; Hosea 12:1"> “Does a wise man answer &#91;with&#93; vain knowledge? And fill his belly &#91;with&#93; an east wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Matthew 12:36–37; Job 13:4–5"> To reason with a word not useful? And speeches—no profit in them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 3:31; Luke 18:1; Amos 6:10"> Indeed, you make reverence void, and diminish meditation before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:3–5; James 3:5–8; Luke 6:45"> For your mouth teaches your iniquity, &#124;&#124; And you choose the tongue of the cunning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 19:22; Matthew 12:37; Job 9:20"> Your mouth declares you wicked, and not I, &#124;&#124; And your lips testify against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Job 12:12; Job 38:4–41"> Are you the first man born? And were you formed before the heights?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 11:34; Jeremiah 23:18; Job 13:5–6"> Do you hear of the secret counsel of God? And withdraw wisdom to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 11:5; Job 12:3"> What have you known, and we do not know? &#91;What&#93; do you understand, and it is not with us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 32:6–7; Job 8:8–10; Job 12:12"> Both the gray-headed &#124;&#124; And the very aged &#91;are&#93; among us—Greater than your father &#91;in&#93; days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 1:3–5; 1 Kings 22:24"> Are the comforts of God too few for you? And a gentle word &#91;is&#93; with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Mark 7:21–22"> Why does your heart take you away? And why are your eyes high?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:25–27; James 3:2–6; Job 9:4"> Do you turn your spirit against God? And have brought out words from your mouth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; Job 14:4; Proverbs 20:9"> What &#91;is&#93; man that he is pure, &#124;&#124; And that he is righteous, one born of woman?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 25:5; Job 4:18; Job 5:1"> Behold, He puts no credence in His holy ones, &#124;&#124; And the heavens have not been pure in His eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 34:7; Proverbs 19:28; Romans 3:9–19"> Also—surely abominable and filthy &#124;&#124; Is man drinking perverseness as water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 36:2; Job 34:2; Job 13:5–6"> I show you—listen to me—And this I have seen and declare,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 8:8; Isaiah 38:19; Psalms 71:18"> Which the wise declare—And have not hid—from their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Genesis 10:32; Genesis 10:25"> To them alone was the land given, &#124;&#124; And a stranger did not pass over into their midst:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 27:13; James 5:1–6; Luke 12:19–21"> All &#91;the&#93; days of the wicked he is paining himself, &#124;&#124; And few years have been laid up for the terrible one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 18:11; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Leviticus 26:36"> A fearful voice &#91;is&#93; in his ears, &#124;&#124; In peace a destroyer comes to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 20:24–25; Matthew 27:5; Job 9:16"> He does not believe to return from darkness, &#124;&#124; And he watches for the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 109:10; Psalms 59:15; Job 18:12"> He is wandering for bread: Where &#91;is&#93; it? He has known that ready at his hand &#124;&#124; Is a day of darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 24:34; Proverbs 6:11; Job 6:2–4"> Adversity and distress terrify him, &#124;&#124; They prevail over him as a king ready for a boaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Psalms 52:7; Psalms 73:9"> For he stretched out his hand against God, &#124;&#124; And against the Mighty he makes himself mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 18:40; Job 16:12; 2 Chronicles 32:13–17"> He runs to Him with a neck, &#124;&#124; With thick bosses of his shields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 17:10; Jeremiah 5:28; Psalms 73:7"> For he has covered his face with his fat, &#124;&#124; And makes vigor over &#91;his&#93; confidence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 3:14; Jeremiah 51:37; Micah 7:18"> And he inhabits cities cut off, houses not dwelt in, &#124;&#124; That have been ready to become heaps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Psalms 49:16–17; James 1:11"> He is not rich, nor does his wealth rise, &#124;&#124; Nor does he stretch out their continuance on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 4:9; Job 15:22; Job 18:5–6"> He does not turn aside from darkness, &#124;&#124; A flame dries up his tender branch, &#124;&#124; And he turns aside at the breath of His mouth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 59:4; Galatians 6:3; Psalms 62:10"> Do not let him put credence in vanity, &#124;&#124; He has been deceived, &#124;&#124; For vanity is his exchange.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:17; Psalms 55:23; Hosea 9:16"> It is not completed in his day, &#124;&#124; And his bending branch is not green.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:39–40; Isaiah 33:9; Revelation 6:13"> He shakes off his unripe fruit as a vine, &#124;&#124; And casts off his blossom as an olive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 8:13; Job 27:8; Job 8:22"> For the company of the profane &#91;is&#93; barren, &#124;&#124; And fire has consumed tents of bribery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Hosea 10:13; Isaiah 59:4–5"> To conceive misery, and to bear iniquity, &#124;&#124; Even their heart prepares deceit.”</span>
