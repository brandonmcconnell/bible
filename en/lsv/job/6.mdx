<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 4:1"> And Job answers and says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 31:6; Job 23:2; Job 4:5"> “O that my provocation were thoroughly weighed, &#124;&#124; And my calamity in balances &#124;&#124; They would lift up together!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:3; Job 23:2; Psalms 40:5"> For now it is heavier than the sands of the sea, &#124;&#124; Therefore my words have been rash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 38:2; Job 30:15; Psalms 88:15–16"> For arrows of the Mighty &#91;are&#93; with me, &#124;&#124; Whose poison is drinking up my spirit. Terrors of God array themselves &#91;for&#93; me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joel 1:18–20; Jeremiah 14:6; Psalms 42:1"> Does a wild donkey bray over tender grass? Does an ox low over his provender?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 34:3; Luke 14:34; Colossians 4:6"> Is an insipid thing eaten without salt? Is there sense in the drivel of dreams?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 3:24; Ezekiel 4:16; Daniel 10:3"> My soul is refusing to touch! They &#91;are&#93; as my sickening food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:81; Job 17:14–16; Job 6:11–13"> O that my request may come, &#124;&#124; That God may grant my hope!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Jonah 4:3; Jonah 4:8"> That God would please—and bruise me, &#124;&#124; Loose His hand and cut me off!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 23:12; Leviticus 19:2; Isaiah 57:15"> And yet it is my comfort &#124;&#124; (And I exult in pain—He does not spare), &#124;&#124; That I have not hidden &#124;&#124; The sayings of the Holy One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 103:14–16; Psalms 102:23; Psalms 39:5"> What &#91;is&#93; my power that I should hope? And what &#91;is&#93; my end that I should prolong my life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 41:24; Job 40:18"> Is my strength the strength of stones? Is my flesh bronze?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 26:2; 2 Corinthians 1:12; Job 19:28"> Is my help not with me, &#124;&#124; And substance driven from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 17:17; 1 Corinthians 12:26; Galatians 6:2"> To a despiser of his friends &#91;is&#93; shame, &#124;&#124; And the fear of the Mighty he forsakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Jeremiah 15:18; Psalms 41:9"> My brothers have deceived as a brook, &#124;&#124; As a stream of brooks they pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> That are black because of ice, &#124;&#124; By them snow hides itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Job 24:19"> By the time they are warm they have been cut off, &#124;&#124; By its being hot they have been &#124;&#124; Extinguished from their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The paths turn aside of their way, &#124;&#124; They ascend into emptiness, and are lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 21:14; Genesis 25:15; Jeremiah 25:23"> Passengers of Tema looked expectingly, &#124;&#124; Travelers of Sheba hoped for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3–4; Romans 5:5; Romans 9:33"> They were ashamed that one has trusted, &#124;&#124; They have come to it and are confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; 2 Timothy 4:16; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> Surely now you have become the same! You see a downfall, and are afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 20:33; Job 42:11; 1 Samuel 12:3"> Is it because I said, Give to me? And, By your power bribe for me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 49:15; Psalms 49:7–8; Psalms 107:2"> And, Deliver me from the hand of an adversary? And, Ransom me from the hand of terrible ones?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 3:2; Proverbs 9:9; Psalms 39:1–2"> Show me, and I keep silent, &#124;&#124; And what I have erred, let me understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 32:3; Job 13:5; Proverbs 25:11"> How powerful have been upright sayings, &#124;&#124; And what reproof from you reproves?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 8:2; Ephesians 4:14; Job 42:3"> For reproof—do you reckon words? And for wind—sayings of the desperate?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 24:9; Job 24:3; 2 Peter 2:3"> You cause anger to fall on the fatherless, &#124;&#124; And are strange to your friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 13:4; Job 33:3; Job 27:4"> And now, please, look on me, &#124;&#124; Even to your face do I lie?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 17:10; Job 34:5; Job 27:4–6"> Please turn back, let it not be perverseness, &#124;&#124; Indeed, turn back again—my righteousness &#91;is&#93; in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 12:11; Job 6:6; Job 34:3"> Is there perverseness in my tongue? Does my palate not discern calamity?”</span>
