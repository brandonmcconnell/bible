<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 20:1"> And Zophar the Naamathite answers and says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 10:19; James 1:19; Job 18:2"> “Is a multitude of words not answered? And is a man of lips justified?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 24:25; Titus 2:8"> Your boastings make men keep silent, &#124;&#124; You scorn, and none is causing blushing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 6:10; Job 35:2"> And you say, My discourse &#91;is&#93; pure, &#124;&#124; And I have been clean in Your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 38:1–2; Job 31:35; Job 33:6–18"> And yet, O that God had spoken! And opens His lips with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Lamentations 3:22; Daniel 2:28"> And declares to you secrets of wisdom, for counsel has foldings. And know that God forgets of your iniquity for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Ecclesiastes 3:11; Psalms 145:3"> Do you find out God by searching? To perfection find out the Mighty One?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 22:12; Psalms 139:6–8; Isaiah 55:9"> Heights of the heavens—what &#91;can&#93; you do? Deeper than Sheol—what &#91;can&#93; you know?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 139:9–10; Job 28:24–25; Psalms 65:5–8"> Its measure &#91;is&#93; longer than earth, and broader than the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:12–13; Job 12:14; Revelation 3:7"> If He passes on, and shuts up, and assembles, &#124;&#124; Who then reverses it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 10:14; Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23"> For He has known men of vanity, &#124;&#124; And He sees iniquity, &#124;&#124; And one does not consider &#91;it&#93;!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 2:20; Ecclesiastes 3:18; James 3:13–17"> And empty man is bold, &#124;&#124; And man is born &#91;as&#93; the colt of a wild donkey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 143:6; Psalms 88:9; 1 Samuel 7:3"> If you have prepared your heart, &#124;&#124; And have spread out your hands to Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:23; James 4:8; Job 4:7"> If iniquity &#91;is&#93; in your hand, put it far off, &#124;&#124; And do not let perverseness dwell in your tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 22:26; 1 John 2:28; 1 John 3:19–22"> For then you lift up your face from blemish, &#124;&#124; And you have been firm, and do not fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 65:16; Ecclesiastes 5:20; John 16:21"> For you forget misery, &#124;&#124; As waters passed away you remember.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 58:8–10; Psalms 37:6; Proverbs 4:18"> And age rises above the noon, &#124;&#124; You fly—you are as the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:24–26; Psalms 43:5; Psalms 3:5"> And you have trusted because there is hope, &#124;&#124; And searched—in confidence you lie down,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 45:12; Isaiah 60:14; Job 42:8–9"> And you have rested, &#124;&#124; And none is causing trembling, &#124;&#124; And many have begged &#91;at&#93; your face;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:65; Job 31:16; Job 17:5"> And the eyes of the wicked are consumed, &#124;&#124; And refuge has perished from them, &#124;&#124; And their hope &#91;is&#93; a breathing out of soul!”</span>
