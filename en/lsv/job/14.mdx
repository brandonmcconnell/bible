<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 5:7; Ecclesiastes 2:23; Job 25:4"> “Man, born of woman! Of few days, and full of trouble!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; 1 Peter 1:24; Job 8:9"> As a flower he has gone forth, and is cut off, &#124;&#124; And he flees as a shadow and does not stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 144:3; Psalms 143:2; Psalms 8:4"> Also—on this You have opened Your eyes, and bring me into judgment with You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 3:6; Job 15:14; Ephesians 2:3"> Who gives a clean thing out of an unclean? Not one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 39:4; Acts 17:26; Hebrews 9:27"> If his days are determined, &#124;&#124; The number of his months &#91;are&#93; with You, &#124;&#124; You have made his limit, &#124;&#124; And he does not pass over;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 7:19; Psalms 39:13; Job 7:1–2"> Look away from off him that he may cease, &#124;&#124; Until he enjoy as a hired worker his day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; Isaiah 11:1; Job 14:14"> For there is hope for a tree, if it is cut down, &#124;&#124; That it changes again, &#124;&#124; That its tender branch does not cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36; Isaiah 26:19; John 12:24"> If its root becomes old in the earth, &#124;&#124; And its stem dies in the dust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:17–24; Ezekiel 17:3–10; Ezekiel 17:22–24"> From the fragrance of water it flourishes, &#124;&#124; And has made a crop as a plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 7:7–10; Job 14:12"> And a man dies, and becomes weak, &#124;&#124; And man expires, and where &#91;is&#93; he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 19:5; Jeremiah 15:18; Job 6:15–18"> Waters have gone away from a sea, &#124;&#124; And a river becomes waste and dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Job 3:13; Ecclesiastes 3:19–21"> And man has lain down, and does not rise, &#124;&#124; Until the wearing out of the heavens they do not awaken, &#124;&#124; Nor are roused from their sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Isaiah 57:1–2; Isaiah 26:20–21"> O that You would conceal me in Sheol, &#124;&#124; Hide me until the turning of Your anger, &#124;&#124; Set a limit for me, and remember me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Job 7:1; Acts 26:8"> If a man dies—does he revive? All &#91;the&#93; days of my warfare I wait, until my change comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 13:22; Psalms 138:8; Job 10:8"> You call, and I answer You; To the work of Your hands You have desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 10:6; Job 31:4; Psalms 139:1–4"> But now, You number my steps, &#124;&#124; You do not watch over my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:34; Hosea 13:12; Job 21:19"> My transgression &#91;is&#93; sealed up in a bag, and You sew up my iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 6:14; Job 18:4; Jeremiah 4:24"> And yet, a falling mountain wastes away, and a rock is removed from its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 7:6; Job 27:8; Job 19:10"> Waters have worn away stones, &#124;&#124; Their outpourings wash away the dust of earth, &#124;&#124; And You have destroyed the hope of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:14; Job 2:12; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> You prevail &#91;over&#93; him forever, and he goes, &#124;&#124; He is changing his countenance, &#124;&#124; And You send him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Isaiah 63:16"> His sons are honored, and he does not know; And they are little, and he does not attend to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 19:20; Luke 16:23–24"> Only—his flesh is pained for him, &#124;&#124; And his soul mourns for him.”</span>
