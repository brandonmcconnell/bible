<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 40:6; Job 37:1–2; 2 Kings 2:11"> And YHWH answers Job out of the whirlwind and says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 42:3; 1 Timothy 1:7; Job 35:16"> “Who &#91;is&#93; this—darkening counsel, &#124;&#124; By words without knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 40:7; Exodus 12:11; Jeremiah 1:17"> Now gird your loins as a man, &#124;&#124; And I ask you, and you cause Me to know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 104:5; Hebrews 1:10; Proverbs 30:4"> Where were you when I founded the earth? Declare, if you have known understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:12; Zechariah 2:1–2; Job 11:9"> Who placed its measures—if you know? Or who has stretched out a line on it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 26:7; Psalms 104:5; Psalms 93:1"> On what have its sockets been sunk? Or who has cast its cornerstone—</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 5:11; Job 1:6; Ezra 3:11–12"> In the singing together of &#91;the&#93; stars of morning, &#124;&#124; When all &#91;the&#93; sons of God shout for joy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:9; Proverbs 8:29; Jeremiah 5:22"> And He shuts up the sea with doors, &#124;&#124; In its coming forth, it goes out from the womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 1:2"> In My making a cloud its clothing, &#124;&#124; And thick darkness its swaddling band,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:22; Psalms 104:9; Psalms 33:7"> And I measure My statute over it, &#124;&#124; And place bar and doors,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 89:9; Proverbs 8:29; Psalms 65:6–7"> And say, To here you come, and no more, &#124;&#124; And a command is placed &#124;&#124; On the pride of your billows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 74:16; Psalms 148:3–5; Genesis 1:5"> Have you commanded morning since your days? Do you cause the dawn to know its place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 104:35; Job 37:3; Exodus 14:27"> To take hold on the skirts of the earth, &#124;&#124; And the wicked are shaken out of it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:6; Psalms 104:2"> It turns itself as clay of a seal &#124;&#124; And they station themselves as clothed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 10:15; Psalms 37:17; Job 18:5"> And their light is withheld from the wicked, &#124;&#124; And the arm lifted up is broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:24; Job 26:5–6; Psalms 77:19"> Have you come to springs of the sea? And in searching the deep &#124;&#124; Have you walked up and down?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 9:13; Psalms 107:18; Job 3:5"> Were the gates of death revealed to you? And do you see the gates of death-shade?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 89:11–12; Revelation 20:9; Jeremiah 31:37"> You have understanding, &#124;&#124; Even to the broad places of earth! Declare—if you have known it all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:8; Genesis 1:14–18; Genesis 1:3–4"> Where &#91;is&#93; this—the way light dwells? And darkness, where &#91;is&#93; this—its place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 10:19; Job 26:10; Genesis 23:17"> That you take it to its boundary, &#124;&#124; And that you understand the paths of its house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 15:7; Job 38:12; Job 38:4"> You have known—for then you are born, &#124;&#124; And the number of your days &#91;are&#93; many!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 37:6; Psalms 135:7; Psalms 33:7"> Have you come to the treasure of snow? Indeed, do you see the treasures of hail,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; Isaiah 30:30; Exodus 9:18"> That I have kept back for a time of distress, &#124;&#124; For a day of conflict and battle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 38:12–13; Jonah 4:8; Matthew 24:27"> Where &#91;is&#93; this, the way light is apportioned? It scatters an east wind over the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 28:26; Psalms 29:3–10; Job 37:3–6"> Who has divided a conduit for the flood? And a way for the lightning of the voices?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:22; Hebrews 6:7–8; Psalms 107:35"> To cause &#91;it&#93; to rain on a land &#91;with&#93; no man, &#124;&#124; A wilderness &#91;with&#93; no man in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 107:35; Psalms 104:14"> To satisfy a desolate and ruined place, &#124;&#124; And to cause to shoot up &#124;&#124; The produce of the tender grass?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:22; Psalms 147:8; Jeremiah 10:13"> Does the rain have a father? Or who has begotten the drops of dew?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 147:16–17; Job 37:10; Job 38:8"> From whose belly came forth the ice? And the hoarfrost of the heavens, &#124;&#124; Who has begotten it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 37:10"> Waters are hidden as a stone, &#124;&#124; And the face of the deep is captured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Job 9:9"> Do you bind the chains of the Pleiades? Or do you open the cords of Orion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 38:31; Job 9:9; 2 Kings 23:5"> Do you bring out the twelve signs in &#91;their&#93; season? And do you comfort the Great Bear over her sons?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35–36; Genesis 8:22; Job 38:12–13"> Have you known the statutes of the heavens? Or do you appoint &#124;&#124; Its dominion in the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 22:11; Job 36:27–28; Amos 5:8"> Do you lift up your voice to the cloud, &#124;&#124; And abundance of water covers you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 36:32; Job 37:3; Isaiah 65:1"> Do you send out lightnings, and they go &#124;&#124; And say to you, Behold us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Job 32:8; Psalms 51:6; Proverbs 2:6"> Who has put wisdom in the inward parts? Or who has given understanding to the covered part?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Psalms 147:4; Genesis 15:5; Genesis 8:1"> Who numbers the clouds by wisdom? And the bottles of the heavens, &#124;&#124; Who causes to lie down,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> In the hardening of dust into hardness, &#124;&#124; And clods cleave together?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 104:21; Job 4:10–11; Psalms 34:10"> Do you hunt prey for a lion? And fulfill the desire of young lions?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Job 37:8; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 24:9"> When they bow down in dens—Abide in a thicket for a covert?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 12:24; Matthew 6:26; Psalms 147:9"> Who prepares for a raven his provision, &#124;&#124; When his young ones cry to God? They wander without food.”</span>
