<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 42:9; Job 11:1"> And Zophar the Naamathite answers and says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Psalms 31:22; Job 20:3"> “Therefore my thoughts cause me to answer, &#124;&#124; And because of my sensations in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 33:3; Job 20:2; Psalms 78:2–5"> I hear the discipline of my shame, &#124;&#124; And the spirit of my understanding causes me to answer:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 9:1–3; Job 15:10; Job 32:7"> Have you known this from antiquity? Since the placing of man on earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 37:35–36; Job 18:5–6; Acts 12:22–23"> That the singing of the wicked &#91;is&#93; short, &#124;&#124; And the joy of the profane for a moment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–14; Obadiah 1:3–4; Genesis 11:4"> Though his excellence goes up to the heavens, &#124;&#124; He strikes his head against a cloud—</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:10; 1 Kings 14:10; Job 4:20"> He perishes as his own dung forever, &#124;&#124; His beholders say, Where &#91;is&#93; he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 90:5; Psalms 73:20; Isaiah 29:7–8"> He flees as a dream, and they do not find him, &#124;&#124; And he is driven away as a vision of the night,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 7:8; Job 7:10; Job 8:18"> The eye has not seen him, and does not add. And his place does not behold him again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 20:18; Luke 19:8; Job 27:16–17"> His sons oppress the poor, &#124;&#124; And his hands give back his wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:26; Psalms 25:7; Job 21:26"> His bones have been full of his youth, and it lies down with him on the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 15:16; Psalms 10:7; Ecclesiastes 11:9"> Though he sweetens evil in his mouth, hides it under his tongue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 9:43–49; Romans 8:13; Matthew 5:29–30"> has pity on it, and does not forsake it, and keeps it back in the midst of his palate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalms 51:8–9; Psalms 32:3–4"> his food is turned in his bowels, the bitterness of cobras &#91;is&#93; in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:8; Matthew 27:3–4"> He has swallowed wealth, and vomits it. God drives it out from his belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 28:3–6; Romans 3:13; Deuteronomy 32:24"> He sucks &#91;the&#93; gall of cobras, the tongue of a viper slays him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–14; Job 29:6; Luke 16:24"> He does not look on streams, &#124;&#124; Flowing of brooks of honey and butter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 20:10; Job 20:15; Jeremiah 51:44"> He is giving back &#91;what&#93; he labored for, and does not consume &#91;it&#93;; As a bulwark &#91;is&#93; his exchange, and he does not exult.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 35:9; Proverbs 14:31; James 5:4"> For he oppressed—he forsook the poor, &#124;&#124; He has taken a house away violently, &#124;&#124; And he does not build it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13–14; Isaiah 57:20–21"> For he has not known ease in his belly. With his desirable thing he does not deliver himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:11; Job 15:29; Luke 16:24–25"> There is not a remnant to his food, &#124;&#124; Therefore his good does not stay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 15:29; Revelation 18:7; 2 Kings 24:2"> In the fullness of his sufficiency he is constricted. Every perverse hand meets him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 78:30–31; Numbers 11:33; Psalms 11:6"> It comes to pass, at the filling of his belly, &#124;&#124; He sends forth against him &#124;&#124; The fierceness of His anger, &#124;&#124; Indeed, He rains on him in his eating.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 24:18; Amos 5:19; 2 Samuel 22:35"> He flees from an iron weapon, &#124;&#124; A bow of bronze passes through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 16:13; Job 18:11; Deuteronomy 32:41"> One has drawn, &#124;&#124; And it comes out from the body, &#124;&#124; And a glittering weapon proceeds from his gall. Terrors &#91;are&#93; on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 21:9; Psalms 120:4; Isaiah 30:33"> All darkness is hid for his treasures, &#124;&#124; A fire not blown consumes him, &#124;&#124; The remnant is broken in his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Luke 12:2–3; Job 16:18"> The heavens reveal his iniquity, &#124;&#124; And earth is raising itself against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 27:14–19; Revelation 18:17; James 5:1–3"> The increase of his house is removed, &#124;&#124; Poured forth in a day of His anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:13; Job 31:2–3; Psalms 11:5–6"> This &#91;is&#93; the portion of a wicked man from God. And an inheritance appointed him by God.”</span>
