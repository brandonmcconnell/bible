<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 8:1; Job 25:1"> And Bildad the Shuhite answers and says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 13:5–6; Proverbs 18:13; Job 3:17"> “When do you set an end to words? Consider, and afterward we speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; Job 17:10; Job 17:4"> Why have we been reckoned as livestock? We have been defiled in your eyes!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 16:9; Job 14:18; Job 13:14"> He is tearing himself in his anger. Is earth forsaken for your sake? And is a rock removed from its place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Proverbs 24:20; Proverbs 20:20"> Also, the light of the wicked is extinguished. And there does not shine a spark of his fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Job 21:17; Psalms 18:28"> The light has been dark in his tent, &#124;&#124; And his lamp over him is extinguished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Psalms 33:10; Psalms 18:36"> The steps of his strength are restricted, &#124;&#124; And his own counsel casts him down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 9:15; Job 22:10; Psalms 35:8"> For he is sent into a net by his own feet, &#124;&#124; And he habitually walks on a snare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; Job 1:15; Job 1:17"> A trap seizes on the heel, &#124;&#124; The designing prevails over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Romans 11:9; Psalms 11:6"> His cord is hidden in the earth, &#124;&#124; And his trap on the path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 15:21; Jeremiah 6:25; Leviticus 26:36"> Terrors have terrified him all around, &#124;&#124; And they have scattered him—at his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Psalms 34:10"> His sorrow is hungry, &#124;&#124; And calamity is ready at his side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 2:6; Zechariah 14:12; Revelation 6:8"> It consumes the parts of his skin, &#124;&#124; Death’s firstborn consumes his parts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 10:28; Psalms 55:4; Job 11:20"> His confidence is drawn from his tent, &#124;&#124; And it causes him to step to the king of terrors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Deuteronomy 29:23; Isaiah 34:9–10"> It dwells in his tent—out of his provender, &#124;&#124; Sulfur is scattered over his habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 9:16; Isaiah 5:24; Job 15:30"> From beneath his roots are dried up, &#124;&#124; And from above his crop is cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:16; Proverbs 10:7; Psalms 109:13"> His memorial has perished from the land, &#124;&#124; And he has no name on the street.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 20:8; Job 10:22; Job 3:20"> They thrust him from light to darkness, &#124;&#124; And cast him out from the habitable earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:30; Isaiah 14:21–22; Job 20:26–28"> He has no continuator, &#124;&#124; Nor successor among his people, &#124;&#124; And none is remaining in his dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 37:13; Jeremiah 50:27; Ezekiel 21:25"> At this day, those &#91;in the&#93; west have been astonished, &#124;&#124; And those &#91;in the&#93; east have taken fright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Jeremiah 9:3; Jeremiah 10:25"> Only these &#91;are&#93; dwelling places of the perverse, &#124;&#124; And this &#91;is&#93; the place God has not known.”</span>
