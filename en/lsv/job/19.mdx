<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job answers and says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 64:3; Job 18:2; Job 27:2"> “Until when do you afflict my soul, &#124;&#124; And bruise me with words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 31:7; Nehemiah 4:12; Job 18:4–21"> These ten times you put me to shame, you do not blush. You make yourselves strange to me—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Proverbs 9:12; Ezekiel 18:4"> And also—truly, I have erred, &#124;&#124; My error remains with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Psalms 38:16; Psalms 55:12"> If, truly, you magnify yourselves over me, &#124;&#124; And decide my reproach against me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 27:2; Job 16:11–14"> Know now, that God turned me upside down, &#124;&#124; And has set around His net against me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:2–3; Lamentations 3:8; Job 31:35–36"> Behold, I cry out—violence, and am not answered, I cry aloud, and there is no judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 3:7; Job 3:23; Proverbs 4:19"> He hedged up my way, and I do not pass over, &#124;&#124; And He places darkness on my paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:44; Job 29:7–14; Psalms 89:39"> He has stripped my honor from off me, &#124;&#124; And He turns the crown from my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 24:20; Psalms 37:35–36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> He breaks me down all around, and I go, &#124;&#124; And removes my hope like a tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:24; Job 16:9; Job 33:10"> And He kindles His anger against me, &#124;&#124; And reckons me to Him as His adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 30:12; Job 16:13; Isaiah 51:23"> His troops come in together, &#124;&#124; And they raise up their way against me, &#124;&#124; And encamp around my tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 88:8; Psalms 88:18; Psalms 69:8"> He has put my brothers far off from me, &#124;&#124; And my acquaintances have surely been estranged from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Jeremiah 20:10; 2 Samuel 16:23"> My neighbors have ceased &#124;&#124; And my familiar friends have forgotten me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 31:31–32; Psalms 123:3"> Sojourners of my house and my maids, &#124;&#124; Reckon me for a stranger; I have been an alien in their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 1:15–17; Job 1:19"> I have called to my servant, &#124;&#124; And he does not answer, &#124;&#124; With my mouth I make supplication to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 2:9–10; Job 17:1"> My spirit is strange to my wife, &#124;&#124; And my favors to the sons of my &#91;mother’s&#93; womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 2:23; Job 30:12; Job 30:1"> Also sucklings have despised me, I rise, and they speak against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Psalms 109:4–5; Psalms 55:12–14"> All the men of my counsel detest me, &#124;&#124; And those I have loved, &#124;&#124; Have been turned against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 102:5; Lamentations 4:8; Job 33:19–22"> To my skin and to my flesh &#124;&#124; My bone has cleaved, &#124;&#124; And I deliver myself with the skin of my teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:11; Psalms 38:2; Job 2:5"> Pity me, pity me, you my friends, &#124;&#124; For the hand of God has struck against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 69:26; Job 31:31; Job 16:13–14"> Why do you pursue me as God? And are not satisfied with my flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 30:8; Job 31:35; Isaiah 8:1"> Who grants now, that my words may be written? Who grants that they may be inscribed in a scroll?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:1; Exodus 28:21; Exodus 32:16"> With a pen of iron and lead—They may be hewn in a rock forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Isaiah 54:5; Psalms 78:35"> That—I have known my Redeemer, &#124;&#124; The Living and the Last, &#124;&#124; For He raises the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; 1 John 3:2; Psalms 17:15"> And after my skin has surrounded this &#91;body&#93;, &#124;&#124; Then from my flesh I see God—</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Isaiah 26:19; Numbers 24:17"> Whom I see on my side, &#124;&#124; And my eyes have beheld, and not a stranger, &#124;&#124; My reins have been consumed in my bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 19:22; Psalms 69:26; 1 Kings 14:13"> But you say, Why do we pursue after him? And the root of the matter has been found in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Psalms 58:10–11"> Be afraid because of the sword, &#124;&#124; For the punishments of the sword &#91;are&#93; furious, &#124;&#124; That you may know that &#91;there is&#93; a judgment.”</span>
