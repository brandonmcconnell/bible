<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 8:1; Job 22:1; Job 15:1"> And Eliphaz the Temanite answers and says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 32:18–20; Acts 4:20; Jeremiah 6:11"> “Has one tried a word with you? You are weary! And who is able to keep in words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; Hebrews 12:12; Proverbs 10:21"> Behold, you have instructed many, &#124;&#124; And feeble hands you make strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Psalms 145:14; Hebrews 12:12"> Your words raise up the stumbling one, &#124;&#124; And you strengthen bowing knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 19:21; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Job 2:5"> But now, it comes to you, &#124;&#124; And you are weary; It strikes to you, and you are troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 3:26; Proverbs 14:26; Job 1:1"> Is your reverence not your confidence? Your hope—the perfection of your ways?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:25; 2 Peter 2:9; Job 36:7"> Now remember, &#124;&#124; Who, being innocent, has perished? And where have the upright been cut off?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:7–8; Proverbs 22:8; Hosea 8:7"> As I have seen—plowers of iniquity, &#124;&#124; And sowers of misery, reap it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 15:30; Isaiah 11:4; Isaiah 30:33"> From the breath of God they perish, &#124;&#124; And from the spirit of His anger &#91;are&#93; consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 58:6; Proverbs 30:14; Psalms 3:7"> The roaring of a lion, &#124;&#124; And the voice of a fierce lion, &#124;&#124; And teeth of young lions have been broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:10; Job 8:3–4; Job 1:19"> An old lion is perishing without prey, &#124;&#124; And the whelps of the lioness separate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 26:14; Psalms 62:11; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> And a thing is secretly brought to me, &#124;&#124; And my ear receives a little of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 33:14–16; Genesis 2:21; Genesis 31:24"> In thoughts from visions of the night, &#124;&#124; In the falling of deep sleep on men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Job 33:19; Luke 1:12"> Fear has met me, and trembling, &#124;&#124; And the multitude of my bones caused to fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; Daniel 5:6; Psalms 104:4"> And a spirit passes before my face, &#124;&#124; The hair of my flesh stands up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:12"> It stands, and I do not discern its aspect, &#124;&#124; A likeness &#91;is&#93; before my eyes, &#124;&#124; Silence! And I hear a voice:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 9:2; Job 25:4; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> Is mortal man more righteous than God? Is a man cleaner than his Maker?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 25:5–6; 2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6"> Behold, He puts no credence in His servants, &#124;&#124; Nor sets praise in His messengers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 10:9; Genesis 2:7; Genesis 3:19"> Also—the inhabitants of houses of clay &#124;&#124; (Whose foundation &#91;is&#93; in the dust, &#124;&#124; They bruise them before a moth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 20:7; Job 14:14; Job 16:22"> From morning to evening are beaten down, &#124;&#124; Without any regarding, they perish forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 36:12; Psalms 39:11; Job 8:22"> Has their excellence not been removed with them? They die, and not in wisdom!”</span>
