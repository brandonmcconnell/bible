<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Ezekiel 29:4–5; 2 Kings 19:28"> Do you put a reed in his nose? And pierce his jaw with a thorn?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 30:10; Proverbs 25:15; Psalms 55:21"> Does he multiply supplications to you? Does he speak tender things to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 21:6; Deuteronomy 15:17; Psalms 8:5–6"> Does he make a covenant with you? Do you take him for a perpetual servant?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 16:25–30; Job 28:11"> Do you play with him as a bird? And do you bind him for your girls?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 14:11"> (Companions feast on him, &#124;&#124; They divide him among the merchants!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Do you fill his skin with barbed irons? And his head with fish-spears?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 20:11; Luke 14:31–32; 2 Kings 10:4"> Place your hand on him, &#124;&#124; Remember the battle—do not add!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 28:19; 1 Samuel 3:11; Luke 21:11"> Behold, the hope of him is found a liar, &#124;&#124; Also, is one not cast down at his appearance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:4; Psalms 2:11–12; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> None so fierce that he awakes him, &#124;&#124; And who &#91;is&#93; he &#91;who&#93; stations himself before Me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 11:35; Psalms 24:1; Job 35:7"> Who has brought before Me and I repay? Under the whole heavens it &#91;is&#93; Mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 1:25"> I do not keep silent concerning his parts, &#124;&#124; And the matter of might, &#124;&#124; And the grace of his arrangement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; Psalms 32:9; James 3:3"> Who has uncovered the face of his clothing? Who enters within his double bridle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:4; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 58:6"> Who has opened the doors of his face? Around his teeth &#91;are&#93; terrible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; Revelation 5:2–3; Jeremiah 9:23"> A pride—strong ones of shields, &#124;&#124; Shut up—a close seal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> They draw near to one another, &#124;&#124; And air does not enter between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> They adhere to one another, &#124;&#124; They stick together and are not separated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 3:9; Revelation 1:14"> His sneezings cause light to shine, &#124;&#124; And his eyes &#91;are&#93; as the eyelids of the dawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 18:8"> Flames go out of his mouth, sparks of fire escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:13–14"> Smoke goes forth out of his nostrils, &#124;&#124; As a blown pot and reeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 18:8; Isaiah 30:33; Habakkuk 3:5"> His breath sets coals on fire, &#124;&#124; And a flame goes forth from his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; Job 40:16; 1 Corinthians 15:55–57"> Strength lodges in his neck, &#124;&#124; And grief exults before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The flakes of his flesh have adhered—Firm on him—it is not moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Isaiah 48:4; Zechariah 7:12"> His heart &#91;is&#93; firm as a stone, &#124;&#124; Indeed, firm as the lower piece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jonah 1:4–6; Psalms 107:28"> The mighty are afraid at his rising, &#124;&#124; From his breakings they keep themselves free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 39:21–24"> The sword of his overtaker does not stand, &#124;&#124; Spear, dart, and breastplate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> He reckons iron as straw, bronze as rotten wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:10; Job 39:7"> The son of the bow does not cause him to flee, &#124;&#124; Stones of the sling are turned into stubble by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:14"> Darts have been reckoned as stubble, &#124;&#124; And he laughs at the shaking of a javelin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15"> Sharp points of clay &#91;are&#93; under him, &#124;&#124; He spreads gold on the mire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> He causes the deep to boil as a pot, &#124;&#124; He makes the sea as a pot of ointment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 38:30; Genesis 1:2; Genesis 15:15"> He causes a path to shine after him, &#124;&#124; One thinks the deep to be hoary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 40:19"> There is not on the earth his like, &#124;&#124; That is made without terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Psalms 73:6; Job 28:8"> He sees every high thing, &#124;&#124; He &#91;is&#93; king over all sons of pride.”</span>
