<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Elihu answers and says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 1:5; 1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> “Hear, O wise men, my words, &#124;&#124; And, O knowing ones, give ear to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 12:11; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Job 33:2"> For the ear tries words, &#124;&#124; And the palate tastes to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Judges 19:30; Romans 12:2"> Let us choose judgment for ourselves, &#124;&#124; Let us know among ourselves what &#91;is&#93; good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 27:2; Job 33:9; Job 16:17"> For Job has said, I have been righteous, &#124;&#124; And God has turned aside my right,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 6:4; Job 27:4–6; Job 16:13"> Against my right do I lie? My arrow &#91;is&#93; mortal—without transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 15:16; Deuteronomy 29:19; Proverbs 1:22"> Who &#91;is&#93; a man like Job? He drinks scoffing like water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; 1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 13:20"> And he has traveled for company &#124;&#124; With workers of iniquity, &#124;&#124; So as to go with men of wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:14–16; Job 35:3; Malachi 3:14"> For he has said, It does not profit a man &#124;&#124; When he delights himself with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:14; Deuteronomy 32:4; Job 8:3"> Therefore, O men of heart, listen to me; Far be it from God to do wickedness, &#124;&#124; And &#91;from&#93; the Mighty to do perverseness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:6; Revelation 22:12"> For He repays the work of man to him, &#124;&#124; And according to the path of each He causes him to find.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 8:3; Psalms 11:7; Psalms 145:17"> Indeed, truly, God does not do wickedly, &#124;&#124; And the Mighty does not pervert judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:11; Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34–36"> Who has inspected for Himself the earth? And who has placed all the habitable world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:29; Job 7:17; Job 9:4"> If He sets His heart on him, &#124;&#124; &#91;If&#93; He gathers His Spirit and His breath to Himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Isaiah 57:16"> All flesh expires together, &#124;&#124; And man returns to dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:2–6; Job 12:3"> And if &#91;there is&#93; understanding, hear this, &#124;&#124; Give ear to the voice of my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 40:8; Genesis 18:25; 2 Samuel 23:3"> Indeed, does one hating justice govern? Or do you condemn the Most Just?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Proverbs 17:26; Romans 13:7"> Who has said to a king, Worthless, &#124;&#124; To princes, Wicked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Acts 10:34; James 2:5"> That has not accepted the person of princes, &#124;&#124; Nor has known the rich before the poor, &#124;&#124; For all of them &#91;are&#93; a work of His hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 73:19; 1 Samuel 25:37–39; 1 Samuel 26:10"> &#91;In&#93; a moment they die, &#124;&#124; And at midnight people shake, &#124;&#124; And they pass away, &#124;&#124; And they remove the mighty without hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Job 31:4; Jeremiah 16:17"> For His eyes &#91;are&#93; on the ways of each, &#124;&#124; And He sees all his steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Amos 9:2–3; Proverbs 10:29"> There is no darkness nor death-shade, &#124;&#124; For workers of iniquity to be hidden there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Psalms 119:137; Romans 9:20"> For He does not permit man anymore, &#124;&#124; To go to God in judgment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Psalms 94:5; Daniel 2:21"> He breaks the mighty—no searching! And He appoints others in their stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 7:2; Job 34:20; Amos 8:7"> Therefore He knows their works, &#124;&#124; And He has overturned by night, &#124;&#124; And they are bruised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 58:10–11; Deuteronomy 21:21; Isaiah 66:24"> As wicked He has struck them, &#124;&#124; In the place of beholders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 28:5; 1 Samuel 15:11; Isaiah 5:12"> Because that against right &#124;&#124; They have turned aside from after Him, &#124;&#124; And have considered none of His ways,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="James 5:4; Exodus 3:7; Job 35:9"> To cause to come to Him &#124;&#124; The cry of the poor, &#124;&#124; And He hears the cry of the afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 23:13; John 14:27; Psalms 27:9"> And He gives rest, and who makes wrong? And hides the face, and who beholds it? And in reference to a nation and to a man, &#124;&#124; &#91;It is&#93; the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2–12; Job 34:21; 2 Kings 21:9"> From the reigning of a profane man, &#124;&#124; From the snares of a people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Micah 7:9; Job 33:27; Job 42:6"> For has any said to God: I have taken away, &#124;&#124; I do not do corruptly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Psalms 25:4–5; Psalms 139:23–24"> Besides &#91;that which&#93; I see, You show me, &#124;&#124; If I have done iniquity—I do not add?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 33:32; Isaiah 45:9; Psalms 135:6"> Does He repay by you, that you have refused—That you choose, and not I? And what you have known, speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:15; Job 34:4; Job 34:16"> Let men of heart say to me, &#124;&#124; And a wise man is listening to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 35:16; Job 38:2; Job 15:2"> Job—he does not speak with knowledge, &#124;&#124; And his words &#91;are&#93; not with wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Job 21:7; Job 23:16; Job 12:6"> My Father! Let Job be tried—to victory, &#124;&#124; Because of answers for men of iniquity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Job 27:23; Isaiah 1:19–20; 1 Samuel 15:23"> For he adds to his sin, &#124;&#124; He vomits transgression among us, &#124;&#124; And multiplies his sayings to God.”</span>
