<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 139:23–24; Job 9:29; Psalms 143:2"> I say to God, Do not condemn me, &#124;&#124; Let me know why You strive &#91;with&#93; me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Psalms 138:8; Job 14:15"> Is it good for You that You oppress? That You despise the labor of Your hands, &#124;&#124; And shine on the counsel of the wicked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Luke 16:15; Job 9:32"> Do you have eyes of flesh? Do You see as man sees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Peter 3:8; Psalms 90:2–4; Psalms 102:24–27"> &#91;Are&#93; Your days as the days of man? Your years as the days of a man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 14:16; Psalms 10:15; Zephaniah 1:12"> That You inquire for my iniquity, &#124;&#124; And seek for my sin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Daniel 3:15; Psalms 139:1–2"> For You know that I am not wicked, &#124;&#124; And there is no deliverer from Your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:73; Isaiah 43:7; Job 10:3"> Your hands have taken pains about me, &#124;&#124; And they make me together all around, &#124;&#124; And You swallow me up!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Isaiah 64:8; Genesis 3:19"> Please remember &#124;&#124; That You have made me as clay, &#124;&#124; And You bring me back to dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 139:14–16"> Do You not pour me out as milk? And curdle me as cheese?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:4–8; Ephesians 4:16; Job 40:17–18"> Skin and flesh You put on me, &#124;&#124; And fence me with bones and sinews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:28; Job 33:4; Acts 17:25"> Life and kindness You have done with me. And Your inspection has preserved my spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 23:13; Deuteronomy 32:39; Isaiah 45:15"> And these You have laid up in Your heart, I have known that this &#91;is&#93; with You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 7:21; Psalms 130:3; Exodus 34:7"> If I sinned, then You have observed me, &#124;&#124; And do not acquit me from my iniquity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 3:11; Job 9:15; Job 9:20–21"> If I have done wickedly—woe to me, &#124;&#124; And righteously—I do not lift up my head, &#124;&#124; Full of shame—then see my affliction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 38:13; Lamentations 3:10; Job 5:9"> And it rises—as a lion You hunt me. And You turn back—You show Yourself wonderful in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ruth 1:21; Job 16:8; Job 16:11–16"> You renew Your witnesses against me, and multiply Your anger with me, &#124;&#124; Changes and warfare &#91;are&#93; with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–18; Jeremiah 15:10; Job 11:20"> And why from the womb &#124;&#124; Have You brought me forth? I expire, and the eye does not see me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 58:8"> I am as &#91;if&#93; I had not been, &#124;&#124; I am brought from the belly to the grave,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:1; Psalms 39:13; Psalms 103:15–16"> Are my days not few? Cease then, and put from me, &#124;&#124; And I brighten up a little,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 3:5; 2 Samuel 12:23; Job 16:22"> Before I go, and do not return, &#124;&#124; To a land of darkness and death-shade,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 88:12; Jeremiah 2:6; Jeremiah 13:16"> A land of obscurity as thick darkness, &#124;&#124; Death-shade—and no order, &#124;&#124; And the shining &#91;is&#93; as thick darkness.”</span>
