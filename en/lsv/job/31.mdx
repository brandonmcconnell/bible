<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 1:14–15; 1 John 2:16; Matthew 5:28–29"> “I made a covenant for my eyes, &#124;&#124; And how do I attend to a virgin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 20:29; Job 27:13; Hebrews 13:4"> And what &#91;is&#93; the portion of God from above? And the inheritance of the Mighty from the heights?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 21:30; Job 34:22; Matthew 7:13"> Is not calamity to the perverse? And strangeness to workers of iniquity?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 34:21; Proverbs 5:21; 2 Chronicles 16:9"> Does He not see my ways, &#124;&#124; And number all my steps?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Psalms 44:20–21; Psalms 12:2"> If I have walked with vanity, &#124;&#124; And my foot hurries to deceit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 7:8–9; Daniel 5:27; Isaiah 26:7"> He weighs me in righteous balances, &#124;&#124; And God knows my integrity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 15:39; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Matthew 5:29"> If my step turns aside from the way, &#124;&#124; And my heart has gone after my eyes, &#124;&#124; And blemish has cleaved to my hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Leviticus 26:16; Deuteronomy 28:38"> Let me sow—and another eat, &#124;&#124; And let my products be rooted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 11:4; Ecclesiastes 7:26; Judges 16:5"> If my heart has been enticed by a woman, &#124;&#124; And I laid wait by the opening of my neighbor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:10; 2 Samuel 12:11; Isaiah 47:2"> Let my wife grind to another, &#124;&#124; And let others bend over her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Deuteronomy 22:22–24; Job 31:28"> For it &#91;is&#93; a wicked thing, and a judicial iniquity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 6:27; Jeremiah 5:7–9; Job 15:30"> For it &#91;is&#93; a fire, it consumes to destruction, &#124;&#124; And takes root among all my increase,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:14–15; Exodus 21:26–27; Leviticus 25:46"> If I despise the cause of my manservant, &#124;&#124; And of my handmaid, &#124;&#124; In their contending with me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 2:13; Psalms 9:12; Zechariah 2:13"> Then what do I do when God arises? And when He inspects, &#124;&#124; What do I answer Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 14:31; Malachi 2:10; Job 34:19"> Did He that made me in the womb not make him? Indeed, One prepares us in the womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Deuteronomy 15:7–10; Deuteronomy 28:32"> If I withhold the poor from pleasure, &#124;&#124; And consume the eyes of the widow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 29:12–16; John 13:29; Job 22:7"> And I eat my morsel by myself, &#124;&#124; And the orphan has nothing &#91;to&#93; eat of it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> (But from my youth &#124;&#124; He grew up with me as &#91;with&#93; a father, &#124;&#124; And from the belly of my mother I am led),</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 22:6; Luke 3:11; Job 24:4"> If I see &#91;any&#93; perishing without clothing, &#124;&#124; And there is no covering for the needy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:13; Job 29:11"> If his loins have not blessed me, &#124;&#124; And from the fleece of my sheep &#124;&#124; He does not warm himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 22:9; Job 29:12; Micah 7:3"> If I have waved my hand at the fatherless, &#124;&#124; When I see &#91;him&#93; in the gate of my court,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 137:6; Job 38:15; Job 31:40"> Let my shoulder fall from its blade, &#124;&#124; And the bone from my arm be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 13:11; Psalms 119:120; 2 Corinthians 5:11"> For calamity &#91;from&#93; God &#91;is&#93; a dread to me, &#124;&#124; And because of His excellence I am not able.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 49:6–7; Deuteronomy 8:12–14; Proverbs 11:28"> If I have made gold my confidence, &#124;&#124; And to the pure gold have said, My trust;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 62:10; Habakkuk 1:16; Isaiah 10:13–14"> If I rejoice because my wealth &#91;is&#93; great, &#124;&#124; And because my hand has found abundance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Deuteronomy 4:19; Deuteronomy 17:3"> If I see the light when it shines, &#124;&#124; And the precious moon walking,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:6; Deuteronomy 11:16; Psalms 2:12"> And my heart is enticed in secret, &#124;&#124; And my hand kisses my mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–7; Job 31:11; Psalms 50:6"> It also &#91;is&#93; a judicial iniquity, &#124;&#124; For I had lied to God above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17–18; Proverbs 17:5; Psalms 35:13–14"> If I rejoice at the ruin of my hater, &#124;&#124; And stirred up myself when evil found him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:14; Exodus 23:4–5"> Indeed, I have not permitted my mouth to sin, &#124;&#124; To ask with an oath his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 1:11–12; Micah 3:2–3"> If not, say, O men of my tent: O that we had of his flesh, we are not satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Genesis 19:2–3; Judges 19:20–21"> A stranger does not lodge in the street, &#124;&#124; I open my doors to the traveler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Hosea 6:7; Acts 5:8"> If I have covered my transgressions as Adam, &#124;&#124; To hide my iniquity in my bosom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 29:25; Esther 4:11"> Because I fear a great multitude, &#124;&#124; And the contempt of families frightens me, &#124;&#124; Then I am silent, I do not go out of the opening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 13:21–22; Job 40:4–5; Job 17:3"> Who gives to me a hearing? Behold, my mark. The Mighty One answers me, &#124;&#124; And my adversary has written a bill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 22:22; Job 29:14; Isaiah 62:3"> If not—on my shoulder I take it up, &#124;&#124; I bind it &#91;as&#93; a crown on myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Job 13:15; Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 4:15–16"> The number of my steps I tell Him, &#124;&#124; As a leader I approach Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:11; James 5:4; Psalms 65:13"> If my land cries out against me, &#124;&#124; And together its furrows weep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; James 5:4; Isaiah 26:21"> If I consumed its strength without money, &#124;&#124; And the life of its possessors, I have caused to breathe out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; Malachi 1:3; Psalms 72:20"> Instead of wheat let a thorn go forth, &#124;&#124; And instead of barley a useless weed!” The words of Job are finished.</span>
