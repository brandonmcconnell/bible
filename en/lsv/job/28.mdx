<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:9; Numbers 31:22; Genesis 4:22"> Iron is taken from the dust, &#124;&#124; And bronze &#91;from&#93; the firm stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:13; Habakkuk 2:13; Matthew 6:33"> He has set an end to darkness, &#124;&#124; And he is searching to all perfection, &#124;&#124; A stone of darkness and death-shade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> A stream has broken out from a sojourner, &#124;&#124; Those forgotten of the foot, &#124;&#124; They were low, they wandered from man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 28:25–29; Psalms 104:14–15; Genesis 1:11–12"> The earth! Bread comes forth from it, &#124;&#124; And its under-part is turned like fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 28:16; Exodus 24:10; Isaiah 54:11"> A place of the sapphire &#91;are&#93; its stones, &#124;&#124; And it has dust of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 11:6; Job 28:21–23; Job 38:24"> A path—a ravenous bird has not known it, &#124;&#124; Nor has an eye of the falcon scorched it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Nor have the sons of pride trodden it, &#124;&#124; The fierce lion has not passed over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nahum 1:4–6"> He sent forth his hand against the flint, &#124;&#124; He overturned mountains from the root.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:9; Proverbs 14:23; Proverbs 24:4"> Among rocks, he has cleaved brooks, &#124;&#124; And his eye has seen every precious thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:5; Isaiah 45:2–3; Isaiah 44:27"> He has bound overflowing rivers, &#124;&#124; And the hidden thing brings out &#91;to&#93; light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 2:4–6; Job 28:20; James 1:5"> And the wisdom—from where is it found? And where &#91;is&#93; this, the place of understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 8:11; Proverbs 8:18–19; Proverbs 3:14–15"> Man has not known its arrangement, &#124;&#124; Nor is it found in the land of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 11:33–34"> The deep has said, It &#91;is&#93; not in me, &#124;&#124; And the sea has said, It is not with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 16:16; Proverbs 8:10–11; Proverbs 8:19"> Gold is not given for it, &#124;&#124; Nor is silver weighed—its price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 13:12; Ezekiel 28:13; Psalms 45:9"> It is not valued with pure gold of Ophir, &#124;&#124; With precious onyx and sapphire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Revelation 4:6; Revelation 21:11"> Gold and crystal do not equal it, &#124;&#124; Nor &#91;is&#93; its exchange a vessel of fine gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:15; Proverbs 31:10; Lamentations 4:7"> Corals and pearl are not remembered, &#124;&#124; The acquisition of wisdom &#91;is&#93; above rubies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 8:19; Exodus 39:10; Ezekiel 28:13"> The topaz of Cush does not equal it, &#124;&#124; It is not valued with pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 28:12; James 1:5; James 1:17"> And the wisdom—from where does it come? And where &#91;is&#93; this, the place of understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 49:3–4; 1 Corinthians 2:7–10; Matthew 11:25"> It has been hid from the eyes of all living. And from the bird of the heavens &#124;&#124; It has been hidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 83:10–12; Job 26:6; Job 28:14"> Destruction and death have said: With our ears we have heard its fame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 8:22–31; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Jude 1:25"> God has understood its way, &#124;&#124; And He has known its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Zechariah 4:10; 2 Chronicles 16:9"> For He looks to the ends of the earth, &#124;&#124; He sees under the whole heavens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Isaiah 40:12; Job 12:15"> To make a weight for the wind, &#124;&#124; And He meted out the waters in measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 38:25; Job 37:3; Zechariah 10:1"> In His making for the rain a limit, &#124;&#124; And a way for the brightness of the voices,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 19:1; Proverbs 8:22–29"> Then He has seen and declares it, &#124;&#124; He has prepared it, and also searched it out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 111:10; Proverbs 1:7; Proverbs 9:10"> And He says to man: Behold, fear of the Lord, that &#91;is&#93; wisdom, &#124;&#124; And to turn from evil &#91;is&#93; understanding.”</span>
