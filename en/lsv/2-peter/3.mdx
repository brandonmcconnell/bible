<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; 2 Peter 1:12–15; 1 Peter 1:22"> This &#91;is&#93; now, beloved, a second letter I write to you, in both which I stir up your pure mind in reminding &#91;you&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jude 1:17; 2 Peter 2:21; Luke 1:70"> to be mindful of the sayings said before by the holy prophets, and of the command of us the apostles of the LORD and Savior,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jude 1:18; 1 Timothy 4:1–2; 2 Peter 2:10"> knowing this first, that there will come scoffers in the last days, going on according to their own desires,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:15; Malachi 2:17; Jeremiah 5:12–13"> and saying, “Where is the promise of His coming? For since the fathers fell asleep, all things so remain from the beginning of the creation”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:3; Genesis 1:9; Psalms 24:2"> for this they willingly conceal, that the heavens existed long ago, and the earth having been established by the word of God out of water and through water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:38–39; 2 Peter 2:5; Luke 17:27"> through which the world then, having been flooded by water, was destroyed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Isaiah 51:6; 2 Peter 3:12"> and by the same word, the present heavens and earth, having been stored up for fire, are being preserved until &#91;the&#93; day of judgment and destruction of impious men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 90:4; Romans 11:25; 1 Corinthians 12:1"> And do not let this one thing be concealed from you, beloved, that one day with the LORD &#91;is&#93; as one thousand years and one thousand years as one day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; Romans 2:4; Habakkuk 2:3"> the LORD is not slow in regard to the promise, as some count slowness, but is long-suffering to us, not intending any to be lost, but all to come to conversion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:1; Matthew 24:35; 2 Peter 3:7"> and it will come—the Day of the LORD—as a thief in the night, in which the heavens will pass away with a rushing noise, and the elements will be dissolved with burning heat, and &#91;the&#93; earth and the works in it will not be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 1:15; Philippians 1:27; 1 Peter 2:12"> All these, then, being dissolved, what kind of persons ought you to be in holy behaviors and pious acts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Isaiah 34:4; Micah 1:4"> waiting for and hurrying the coming of the Day of God, by which the heavens, being on fire, will be dissolved, and the elements will melt with burning heat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; Revelation 21:1; Isaiah 66:22"> And we wait for new heavens and a new earth according to His promise, in which righteousness dwells;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; Philippians 1:10"> for this reason, beloved, waiting for these things, be diligent, spotless and unblameable, to be found by Him in peace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 2:4; 2 Peter 3:9; 1 Timothy 1:16"> and count the long-suffering of our Lord &#91;as&#93; salvation, according as also our beloved brother Paul—according to the wisdom given to him—wrote to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:6; Hebrews 5:11; James 1:8"> as also in all the letters, speaking in them concerning these things, among which are some things &#91;that are&#93; hard to be understood, which the untaught and unstable twist, as also the other Writings, to their own destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Colossians 2:8; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> You, then, beloved, knowing before, take heed, lest, together with the error of the impious being led away, you may fall from your own steadfastness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Ephesians 4:15; 1 Peter 2:2"> and increase in grace, and in the knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ; to Him &#91;is&#93; the glory both now, and to the day of the age! Amen.</span>
