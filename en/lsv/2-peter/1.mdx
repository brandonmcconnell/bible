<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 2:13; Romans 3:21–26; Titus 1:4"> Simeon Peter, a servant and an apostle of Jesus Christ, to those who obtained an equally precious faith with us in the righteousness of our God and Savior Jesus Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 3:18; John 17:3; Philippians 3:8"> Grace and peace be multiplied to you in the acknowledgment of God and of Jesus our Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 8:28–30"> As His divine power has given to us all things pertaining to life and piety, through the acknowledgment of Him who called us through glory and virtue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 4:23–24; Ezekiel 36:25–27; 2 Corinthians 3:18"> through which the most great and precious promises have been given to us, that through these you may become partakers of a divine nature, having escaped from the corruption &#91;and&#93; lust in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:8; Proverbs 4:23; 2 Peter 3:18"> And &#91;for&#93; this same &#91;reason&#93; also, having brought in all diligence, supplement your faith with virtue, and with virtue—knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Romans 5:3–4; Luke 21:19"> and with knowledge—self-control, and with self-control—endurance, and with endurance—piety,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:1; Romans 12:10; 1 Thessalonians 3:12"> and with piety—brotherly kindness, and with brotherly kindness—love;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Titus 3:14; John 15:2; Hebrews 6:12"> for these things being in you and abounding, make &#91;you&#93; neither inert nor unfruitful in regard to the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Peter 2:18–20; Ephesians 5:26; Titus 2:14"> for he with whom these things are not present is blind, dim-sighted, having become forgetful of the cleansing of his old sins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 3:17; Luke 6:47–49; Hebrews 6:11"> therefore, rather, brothers, be diligent to make steadfast your calling and &#91;divine&#93; selection, for doing these things you may never stumble;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Matthew 25:34; Revelation 3:21"> in this way the entrance into the continuous kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ will be richly supplemented to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 2:21; Romans 15:14–15; Philippians 3:1"> Therefore, I will always be ready to remind you about these things, although you have known &#91;them&#93; and have been established in the present truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 3:1; 2 Peter 1:14; Hebrews 13:3"> and I think right, so long as I am in this tent, to stir you up in reminding &#91;you&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6; John 21:18–19; Deuteronomy 31:14"> having known that the laying aside of my tent is soon, even as our Lord Jesus Christ also showed to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19–29; Psalms 71:18; Joshua 24:24–29"> And I will also be diligent &#91;to ensure&#93; that you always have a remembrance to make of these things after my departure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 1:14; 1 Timothy 1:4"> For having not followed out skillfully devised fables, we made known to you the power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, but having become eyewitnesses of His majesty—</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 9:7; Matthew 17:5; Matthew 3:17"> for having received honor and glory from God the Father, such a voice being borne to Him by the Excellent Glory: “This is My Son, the Beloved, in whom I delighted”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 17:6; Joshua 5:15; Exodus 3:5"> and we heard this voice borne out of Heaven, being with Him at the holy mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Isaiah 9:2; Psalms 119:105"> And we have a more firm prophetic word, to which we do well giving heed, as to a lamp shining in a dark place, until day may dawn, and &#91;the&#93; morning star may arise in your hearts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 3:3; Romans 12:6; Romans 6:6"> knowing this first, that no prophecy of the Writing comes of private exposition,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:15–17; 1 Peter 1:11; 2 Samuel 23:2"> for prophecy never came by &#91;the&#93; will of man, but men spoke from God, being brought by the Holy Spirit.</span>
