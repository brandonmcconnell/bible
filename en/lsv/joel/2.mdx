<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 2:15; Joel 1:15; 1 Peter 4:7"> Blow a horn in Zion, &#124;&#124; And shout in My holy hill, &#124;&#124; All inhabitants of the earth tremble, &#124;&#124; For the Day of YHWH is coming, for &#91;it is&#93; near!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 1:6; Zephaniah 1:14–15; Joel 2:10–11"> A day of darkness and thick darkness, &#124;&#124; A day of cloud and thick darkness, &#124;&#124; As darkness spread on the mountains, &#124;&#124; A people numerous and mighty, &#124;&#124; Like there has not been from of old, &#124;&#124; And after it there is not again—Until the years of generation and generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Joel 1:19–20; Genesis 2:8"> Before it fire has consumed, &#124;&#124; And after it a flame burns, &#124;&#124; As the Garden of Eden &#91;is&#93; the land before it, &#124;&#124; And after it a wilderness—a desolation! And also there has not been to it an escape,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 9:7"> As the appearance of horses &#91;is&#93; its appearance, &#124;&#124; And as horsemen, so they run.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 9:9; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 30:30"> As the noise of chariots, they skip on the tops of the mountains, &#124;&#124; As the noise of a flame of fire devouring stubble, &#124;&#124; As a mighty people set in array for battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nahum 2:10; Isaiah 13:8; Jeremiah 30:6"> Pained are peoples from its face, &#124;&#124; All faces have gathered paleness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 30:27; 2 Samuel 1:23; 2 Samuel 2:18–19"> They run as mighty ones, &#124;&#124; As men of war they go up a wall, &#124;&#124; And they each go in his own ways, &#124;&#124; And they do not change their paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:10; Job 33:18; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> And each does not press his brother, &#124;&#124; They each go on in his way, &#124;&#124; If they fall by the missile, they are not cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:21; John 10:1; Exodus 10:6"> In the city they run to and fro, &#124;&#124; On the wall they run, &#124;&#124; Into houses they go up by the windows, &#124;&#124; They go in as a thief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Isaiah 13:10; Joel 3:15–16"> At their face the earth has trembled, &#124;&#124; The heavens have shaken, &#124;&#124; Sun and moon have been black, &#124;&#124; And stars have gathered up their shining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Malachi 3:2; Nahum 1:6; Jeremiah 30:7"> And YHWH has given forth His voice before His force, &#124;&#124; For His camp &#91;is&#93; very great, &#124;&#124; For mighty &#91;is&#93; the doer of His word, &#124;&#124; For great &#91;is&#93; the Day of YHWH—very fearful, &#124;&#124; And who bears it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 4:8–9; Isaiah 55:6–7; 2 Chronicles 7:13–14"> “And also now,” a declaration of YHWH, &#124;&#124; “Turn back to Me with all your heart, &#124;&#124; And with fasting, and with weeping, &#124;&#124; And with lamentation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Psalms 34:18; Exodus 34:6–7"> And tear your heart, and not your garments, &#124;&#124; And turn back to your God YHWH, &#124;&#124; For He &#91;is&#93; gracious and merciful, &#124;&#124; Slow to anger, and abundant in kindness, &#124;&#124; And relenting from evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jonah 3:9; Haggai 2:19; 2 Kings 19:4"> Who knows—He turns back, &#124;&#124; Indeed—He has relented, &#124;&#124; And He has left behind Him a blessing, &#124;&#124; A present and drink-offering of your God YHWH?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 1:14; Joel 2:1; 1 Kings 21:12"> Blow a horn in Zion, &#124;&#124; Sanctify a fast—proclaim a restraint.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:5; 2 Chronicles 20:13; 1 Corinthians 7:5"> Gather the people, sanctify an assembly, &#124;&#124; Assemble the aged, &#124;&#124; Gather infants and sucklings of the breasts, &#124;&#124; Let a bridegroom go out from his inner chamber, &#124;&#124; And a bride out of her closet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 115:2; Ezekiel 8:16; Psalms 79:10"> Let the priests weep between the porch and the altar, servants of YHWH, &#124;&#124; And let them say: “Have pity, O YHWH, on Your people, &#124;&#124; And do not give Your inheritance to reproach, &#124;&#124; To the ruling over them of nations, &#124;&#124; Why do they say among peoples, Where &#91;is&#93; their God?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 8:2; Psalms 103:13; Zechariah 1:14"> And let YHWH be zealous for His land, &#124;&#124; And have pity on His people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:29; Ezekiel 36:15; Isaiah 62:8–9"> Let YHWH answer and say to His people, &#124;&#124; “Behold, I am sending to you the grain, &#124;&#124; And the new wine, and the oil, &#124;&#124; And you have been satisfied with it, &#124;&#124; And I make you no longer a reproach among nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 14:8; Amos 4:10; Isaiah 34:3"> And the northern I put far off from you, &#124;&#124; And have driven him to a land dry and desolate, &#124;&#124; With his face toward the Eastern Sea, &#124;&#124; And his rear to the Western Sea, &#124;&#124; And his stink has come up, &#124;&#124; And his stench comes up, &#124;&#124; For he has exerted himself to work.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:16–17; Isaiah 54:4; Isaiah 41:10"> Do not fear, O land! Be glad and rejoice, &#124;&#124; For YHWH has exerted Himself to work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 8:12; Psalms 65:12; Psalms 67:6"> Do not fear, O livestock of the field! For pastures of a wilderness have sprung up, &#124;&#124; For the tree has borne its fruit, &#124;&#124; Fig tree and vine have given their strength!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 5:7–8; Philippians 4:4; Leviticus 26:4"> And you sons of Zion, &#124;&#124; Be glad and rejoice in your God YHWH, &#124;&#124; For He has given to you the Teacher for righteousness, &#124;&#124; And causes a shower to come down to you, &#124;&#124; Early rain and spring rain &#91;as&#93; in the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Malachi 3:10; Amos 9:13; Joel 3:18"> And the floors have been full &#91;with&#93; pure grain, &#124;&#124; And the presses have overflown &#91;with&#93; new wine and oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zechariah 10:6; Joel 1:4–7; Joel 2:2–11"> “And I have repaid to you the years that the locust consumed, &#124;&#124; The cankerworm, and the caterpillar, and the palmer-worm, &#124;&#124; My great force that I sent against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalms 22:26; Romans 5:5"> And you have eaten, eating and being satisfied, &#124;&#124; And have praised the Name of your God YHWH, &#124;&#124; Who has dealt with you wonderfully, &#124;&#124; And My people are not ashamed for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:26–28; Leviticus 26:11–12; Zephaniah 3:17"> And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; in the midst of Israel, &#124;&#124; And I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH, and there is none else, &#124;&#124; And My people are not ashamed for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:16–21; Ezekiel 39:29; Isaiah 32:15"> And it has come to pass afterward, &#124;&#124; I pour out My Spirit on all flesh, &#124;&#124; And your sons and your daughters have prophesied, &#124;&#124; Your old men dream dreams, &#124;&#124; Your young men see visions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:13; Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11"> And also on the menservants and on the maidservants, &#124;&#124; In those days I pour out My Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 2:19–20; Luke 21:11; Matthew 24:29"> And I have given wonders in the heavens and in the earth: Blood, and fire, and columns of smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 6:12–13; Matthew 24:29; Malachi 4:1"> The sun is turned to darkness, and the moon to blood, &#124;&#124; Before the coming of the Day of YHWH, &#124;&#124; The great and the fearful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 10:11–14; Acts 2:20–21; Isaiah 46:13"> And it has come to pass, &#124;&#124; Everyone who calls on the Name of YHWH is delivered, &#124;&#124; For in Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there is an escape, &#124;&#124; As YHWH has said, &#124;&#124; And among the remnants whom YHWH is calling!”</span>
