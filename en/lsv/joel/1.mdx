<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 2:16; Jeremiah 1:2; Ezekiel 1:3"> A word of YHWH that has been to Joel, son of Pethuel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:2; Hosea 5:1; Job 8:8"> Hear this, you aged ones, &#124;&#124; And give ear, all you inhabitants of the land, &#124;&#124; Has this been in your days? Or in the days of your fathers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Exodus 13:14; Psalms 145:4"> Concerning it talk to your sons, &#124;&#124; And your sons to their sons, &#124;&#124; And their sons to another generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Amos 4:9; Isaiah 33:4"> What is left of the palmer-worm, the locust has eaten, &#124;&#124; And what is left of the locust, &#124;&#124; The cankerworm has eaten, &#124;&#124; And what is left of the cankerworm, &#124;&#124; The caterpillar has eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:1; Isaiah 32:10–12; Luke 21:34–36"> Awake, you drunkards, and weep, &#124;&#124; And howl all drinking wine, because of the juice, &#124;&#124; For it has been cut off from your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Revelation 9:7–10; Proverbs 30:14"> For a nation has come up on My land, &#124;&#124; Strong, and there is no number, &#124;&#124; Its teeth &#91;are&#93; the teeth of a lion, &#124;&#124; And it has the jaw-teeth of a lioness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 5:6; Amos 4:9; Joel 1:12"> It has made My vine become a desolation, &#124;&#124; And My fig tree become a splinter, &#124;&#124; It has made it thoroughly bare, and has cast down, &#124;&#124; Its branches have been made white.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 22:12; Malachi 2:15; Jeremiah 3:4"> Wail, as a virgin girds with sackcloth, &#124;&#124; For the husband of her youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Joel 2:14; Hosea 9:4"> Present and drink-offering have been cut off from the house of YHWH, &#124;&#124; The priests have mourned, servants of YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Hosea 9:2; Jeremiah 12:11"> The field is spoiled, &#124;&#124; The ground has mourned, &#124;&#124; For the grain is spoiled, &#124;&#124; New wine has been dried up, oil languishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3–4; Isaiah 17:11; Jeremiah 9:12"> Be ashamed, you farmers, &#124;&#124; Howl, vinedressers, for wheat and for barley, &#124;&#124; For the harvest of the field has perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 16:10; Isaiah 24:11; Song of Songs 2:3"> The vine has been dried up, &#124;&#124; And the fig tree languishes, &#124;&#124; Pomegranate, also palm, and apple-tree, &#124;&#124; All trees of the field have withered, &#124;&#124; For joy has been dried up from the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joel 1:8–9; Jeremiah 4:8; Joel 2:17"> Gird, and lament, you priests, &#124;&#124; Howl, you servants of the altar, &#124;&#124; Come in, lodge in sackcloth, servants of my God, &#124;&#124; For present and drink-offering have been withheld from the house of your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 2:15–16; Deuteronomy 29:10–11; 2 Chronicles 20:13"> Sanctify a fast, proclaim a restraint, &#124;&#124; Gather &#91;the&#93; elderly &#91;and&#93; all those inhabiting the land, &#124;&#124; &#91;Into the&#93; house of your God YHWH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 13:6–9; Zephaniah 1:14–18; Jeremiah 30:7"> And cry to YHWH, “Aah! For the day! For &#91;the&#93; Day of YHWH &#91;is&#93; near, &#124;&#124; And it comes as destruction from &#91;the&#93; Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:6–7; Psalms 43:4; Isaiah 62:8–9"> Is food not cut off before our eyes? Joy and rejoicing from the house of our God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 17:10–11; Genesis 23:16"> Scattered things have rotted under their clods, &#124;&#124; Storehouses have been desolated, &#124;&#124; Granaries have been broken down, &#124;&#124; For the grain has withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:4; Hosea 4:3; Jeremiah 14:5–6"> How livestock have sighed! Perplexed have been droves of oxen, &#124;&#124; For there is no pasture for them, &#124;&#124; Also droves of sheep have been desolated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Jeremiah 9:10; Amos 7:4"> To You, O YHWH, I call, &#124;&#124; For fire has consumed lovely places of a wilderness, &#124;&#124; And a flame has set on fire all trees of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 104:21; 1 Kings 17:7; Job 38:41"> Also the livestock of the field long for You, &#124;&#124; For dried up have been streams of water, &#124;&#124; And fire has consumed lovely places of a wilderness!”</span>
