<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:2–4; Isaiah 66:16; Zephaniah 3:8"> Then I have gathered all the nations, &#124;&#124; And caused them to go down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat, &#124;&#124; And I have been in judgment with them there, &#124;&#124; Concerning My people and My inheritance—Israel, &#124;&#124; Whom they scattered among nations, &#124;&#124; And My land they have apportioned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nahum 3:10; Obadiah 1:11; Amos 2:6"> And for My people they cast a lot, &#124;&#124; And they give the young man for a harlot, &#124;&#124; And have sold the young woman for wine, &#124;&#124; That they may drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 1:6–10; Jeremiah 47:4; Isaiah 59:18"> And also, what &#91;are&#93; you to Me, O Tyre and Sidon, &#124;&#124; And all circuits of Philistia? Are you rendering repayment to Me? And if you are giving repayment to Me, &#124;&#124; Swiftly, quickly, I return your repayment on your head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:16–17; 2 Kings 18:15–16; 2 Kings 12:18"> In that My silver and My gold you took, &#124;&#124; And My desirable things that are good, &#124;&#124; You have brought into your temples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:13; Joel 3:3; Deuteronomy 28:68"> And sons of Judah, and sons of Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; You have sold to the sons of Javan, &#124;&#124; To put them far off from their border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5–6; Jeremiah 23:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:6"> Behold, I am stirring them up out of the place &#124;&#124; To where you have sold them, &#124;&#124; And I have turned back your repayment on your head,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 60:14; Job 1:15; Isaiah 14:1–2"> And have sold your sons and your daughters &#124;&#124; Into the hand of the sons of Judah, &#124;&#124; And they have sold them to Sabeans, &#124;&#124; To a nation far off, for YHWH has spoken.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:3–4; Isaiah 8:9–10; Ezekiel 38:7"> Proclaim this among nations, &#124;&#124; Sanctify a war, stir up the mighty ones, &#124;&#124; Come near, come up, let all the men of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 12:8; Micah 4:3; 2 Chronicles 25:8"> Beat your plowshares to swords, &#124;&#124; And your pruning-hooks to javelins, &#124;&#124; Let the weak say, “I &#91;am&#93; mighty.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 13:3; Zephaniah 3:8; Ezekiel 38:9–18"> Hurry, and come in, all you nations around, &#124;&#124; And be gathered together, &#124;&#124; There cause to come down, O YHWH, &#124;&#124; Your mighty ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Isaiah 2:4; Psalms 96:13"> “Let the nations wake and come up to the Valley of Jehoshaphat, &#124;&#124; For there I sit to judge all the nations around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:15–20; Mark 4:29; Hosea 6:11"> Send forth a sickle, &#124;&#124; For harvest has ripened, &#124;&#124; Come in, come down, for the press has been filled, &#124;&#124; Winepresses have overflowed, &#124;&#124; For great &#91;is&#93; their wickedness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Philippians 3:2; Isaiah 34:2–8"> Multitudes, multitudes &#91;are&#93; in the Valley of Decision, &#124;&#124; For near &#91;is&#93; the Day of YHWH in the Valley of Decision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 2:10; Matthew 24:29; Joel 2:31"> Sun and moon have been black, &#124;&#124; And stars have gathered up their shining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 1:2; Joel 2:10–11; Haggai 2:6"> And YHWH roars from Zion, &#124;&#124; And gives forth His voice from Jerusalem, &#124;&#124; And the heavens and earth have shaken, &#124;&#124; And YHWH &#91;is&#93; a refuge to His people, &#124;&#124; And a stronghold to sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:27; Nahum 1:15; Isaiah 52:1"> “And you have known that I &#91;am&#93; your God YHWH, &#124;&#124; Dwelling in Zion, My holy mountain, &#124;&#124; And Jerusalem has been holy, &#124;&#124; And strangers do not pass over into it again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:1–12; Zechariah 14:8; Revelation 22:1–2"> And it has come to pass in that day, &#124;&#124; The mountains drop down juice, &#124;&#124; And the hills flow &#91;with&#93; milk, &#124;&#124; And all streams of Judah go &#91;with&#93; water, &#124;&#124; And a fountain from the house of YHWH goes forth, &#124;&#124; And has watered the Valley of Shittim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 19:1–15; Jeremiah 51:35; Zechariah 14:18–19"> “Egypt becomes a desolation, &#124;&#124; And Edom becomes a desolation, a wilderness, &#124;&#124; For violence &#91;to&#93; sons of Judah, &#124;&#124; Whose innocent blood they shed in their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:25; Amos 9:15; Isaiah 33:20"> And Judah dwells for all time, &#124;&#124; And Jerusalem to generation and generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 4:4; Revelation 21:3; Ezekiel 36:25"> And I have declared their blood innocent, &#124;&#124; &#91;That&#93; I did not declare innocent, &#124;&#124; And YHWH is dwelling in Zion!”</span>
