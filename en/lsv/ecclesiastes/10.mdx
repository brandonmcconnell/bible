<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:18–20; Ecclesiastes 10:14; Ecclesiastes 10:10"> The heart of the wise &#91;is&#93; at his right hand, &#124;&#124; And the heart of a fool at his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 18:2; Proverbs 13:16; Proverbs 18:6"> And also, when he that is a fool &#124;&#124; Is walking in the way, his heart is lacking, &#124;&#124; And he has said to everyone, “He &#91;is&#93; a fool.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 25:15; Ecclesiastes 8:3; 1 Samuel 25:24–44"> If the spirit of the ruler goes up against you, do not leave your place, &#124;&#124; For yielding quiets great sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13; Ecclesiastes 4:7; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> There is an evil I have seen under the sun, &#124;&#124; As ignorance that goes out from the ruler,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 28:12; Proverbs 29:2; Esther 3:1"> He has set the fool in many high places, &#124;&#124; And the rich sits in a low place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Esther 6:8; Proverbs 30:22"> I have seen servants on horses, &#124;&#124; And princes walking as servants on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:27; Psalms 7:15–16; Esther 7:10"> Whoever is digging a pit falls into it, &#124;&#124; And whoever is breaking a hedge, a serpent bites him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Whoever is removing stones is grieved by them, &#124;&#124; Whoever is cleaving trees endangered by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:5; Colossians 4:5; Ecclesiastes 10:15"> If the iron has been blunt, &#124;&#124; And he has not sharpened the face, &#124;&#124; Then he increases strength, &#124;&#124; And wisdom &#91;is&#93; advantageous to make right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:17; Psalms 58:4–5; Psalms 64:3"> If the serpent bites without enchantment, &#124;&#124; Then there is no advantage to a master of the tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 4:22; Proverbs 22:11"> Words of the mouth of the wise &#91;are&#93; gracious, &#124;&#124; And the lips of a fool swallow him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:10–11; Proverbs 29:9; Acts 6:9–11"> The beginning of the words of his mouth &#91;is&#93; folly, &#124;&#124; And the latter end of his mouth &#124;&#124; &#91;Is&#93; mischievous madness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 15:2; Ecclesiastes 6:12; Ecclesiastes 5:3"> And the fool multiplies words: “Man does not know that which is—And that which is after him, who declares to him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:4–5; Isaiah 57:1; Isaiah 35:8–10"> The labor of the foolish wearies him, &#124;&#124; In that he has not known to go to the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 3:12; Isaiah 5:11–12; Isaiah 3:4–5"> Woe to you, O land, when your king &#91;is&#93; a youth, &#124;&#124; And your princes eat in the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 31:4–5; Proverbs 28:2–3; Isaiah 5:11"> Blessed are you, O land, &#124;&#124; When your king &#91;is&#93; a son of nobles, &#124;&#124; And your princes eat in due season, &#124;&#124; For might, and not for drunkenness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; Proverbs 24:30–34; Proverbs 20:4"> By slothfulness is the wall brought low, &#124;&#124; And by idleness of the hands the house drops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Ecclesiastes 2:1–2; 1 Peter 4:3"> For mirth they are making a feast, &#124;&#124; And wine makes life joyful, &#124;&#124; And the silver answers with all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 12:2–3; Acts 23:5; Ecclesiastes 7:21–22"> Even in your mind do not revile a king, &#124;&#124; And in the inner parts of your bed-chamber do not revile the rich: For a bird of the heavens causes the voice to go, &#124;&#124; And a possessor of wings declares the word.</span>
