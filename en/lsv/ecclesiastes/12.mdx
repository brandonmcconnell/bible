<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:30; 1 Samuel 4:18; 1 Samuel 4:15"> While that the sun is not darkened, and the light, &#124;&#124; And the moon, and the stars, &#124;&#124; And the thick clouds returned after the rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 90:9–10; Psalms 102:23; 2 Samuel 21:15–17"> In the day that keepers of the house tremble, &#124;&#124; And men of strength have bowed themselves, &#124;&#124; And grinders have ceased, because they have become few. And those looking out at the windows have become dim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:10; 2 Samuel 19:35; Revelation 18:22"> And doors have been shut in the street. When the noise of the grinding is low, &#124;&#124; And &#91;one&#93; rises at the voice of the bird, &#124;&#124; And all daughters of song are bowed down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 17:13; Jeremiah 9:17–20; Job 30:23"> Also of that which is high they are afraid, &#124;&#124; And of the low places in the way, &#124;&#124; And the almond-tree is despised, &#124;&#124; And the grasshopper has become a burden, &#124;&#124; And want is increased, &#124;&#124; For man is going to his perpetual home, &#124;&#124; And the mourners have gone around through the street.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 4:2–3"> While that the silver cord is not removed, &#124;&#124; And the golden bowl broken, &#124;&#124; And the pitcher broken by the fountain, &#124;&#124; And the wheel broken at the well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Ecclesiastes 3:20–21; Psalms 146:4"> And the dust returns to the earth as it was, &#124;&#124; And the spirit returns to God who gave it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:2; Ecclesiastes 8:8; Ecclesiastes 1:14"> Vanity of vanities, said the preacher, the whole &#91;is&#93; vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 4:32; Proverbs 1:1; 1 Kings 10:8"> And further, because the preacher was wise, he still taught the people knowledge, and gave ear, and sought out—he made right many allegories.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 1:5; 1 Timothy 1:15; Proverbs 16:21–24"> The preacher sought to find out pleasing words, and &#91;that&#93; written &#91;by&#93; the upright—words of truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 2:37; Hebrews 4:12; Ezekiel 34:23"> Words of the wise &#91;are&#93; as the goads, and as nails planted &#91;by&#93; the masters of collections, they have been given by one Shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:18; John 21:25; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> And further, from these, my son, be warned; the making of many scrolls has no end, and much study &#91;is&#93; a weariness of the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:12; Micah 6:8; Deuteronomy 6:2"> The end of the whole matter let us hear: “Fear God, and keep His commands, for this &#91;is&#93; the whole of man.</span>
