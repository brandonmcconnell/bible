<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:8–10; Ecclesiastes 1:12; Ecclesiastes 7:27"> Words of a preacher, son of David, king in Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 144:4; Romans 8:20; Ecclesiastes 5:10"> Vanity of vanities, said the Preacher, vanity of vanities: the whole &#91;is&#93; vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:9; Ecclesiastes 5:16; Ecclesiastes 2:22"> What advantage &#91;is&#93; to man by all his labor that he labors at under the sun?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 104:5; Zechariah 1:5; Ecclesiastes 6:12"> A generation is going, and a generation is coming, and the earth is standing for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 19:4–6; Habakkuk 3:11; Psalms 42:1"> Also, the sun has risen, and the sun has gone in, and to its place panting it is rising there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 3:8; Ecclesiastes 11:5; Psalms 107:25"> Going to the south, and turning around to the north, turning around, turning around, the wind is going, and by its circuits the wind has returned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Psalms 104:6–9"> All the streams are going to the sea, and the sea is not full; to a place to where the streams are going, there they are turning back to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 27:20; Matthew 11:28; Ecclesiastes 4:8"> All these things are wearying; a man is not able to speak, the eye is not satisfied by seeing, nor is the ear filled from hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:15; Ecclesiastes 6:10; Ecclesiastes 2:12"> What &#91;is&#93; that which has been? It &#91;is&#93; that which is, and what &#91;is&#93; that which has been done? It &#91;is&#93; that which is done, and there is not an entirely new thing under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Luke 17:26–30; Matthew 23:30–32"> There is a thing of which &#91;one&#93; says: “See this, it &#91;is&#93; new!” Already it has been in the ages that were before us!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:16; Psalms 9:6; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> There is not a remembrance of former &#91;generations&#93;; and also of the latter that are, there is no remembrance of them with those that are at the last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:1; 1 Kings 4:1–19"> I, a preacher, have been king over Israel in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:10; Genesis 3:19; 1 Timothy 4:15"> And I have given my heart to seek and to search out by wisdom concerning all that has been done under the heavens. It &#91;is&#93; a sad travail God has given to the sons of man to be humbled by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:11; Ecclesiastes 2:17; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> I have seen all the works that have been done under the sun, and behold, the whole &#91;is&#93; vanity and distress of spirit!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:12–13; Isaiah 40:4; Ecclesiastes 3:14"> A crooked thing &#91;one&#93; is not able to make straight, and a lacking thing is not able to be numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:9; 1 Kings 4:30; 1 Kings 10:7"> I spoke with my heart, saying, “I, behold, have magnified and added wisdom above everyone who has been before me at Jerusalem, and my heart has seen wisdom and knowledge abundantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; Ecclesiastes 1:13–14"> And I give my heart to know wisdom, and to know madness and folly: I have known that even this &#91;is&#93; distress of spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 28:28; Ecclesiastes 12:12–13; 1 Corinthians 3:18–20"> for in abundance of wisdom &#91;is&#93; abundance of sadness, and he who adds knowledge adds pain.”</span>
