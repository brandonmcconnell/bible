<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:16; Ecclesiastes 5:8; Isaiah 5:7"> And I have turned, and I see all the oppressions that are done under the sun, and behold, the tear of the oppressed, and they have no comforter; and at the hand of their oppressors &#91;is&#93; power, and they have no comforter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 3:11–26; Ecclesiastes 2:17; Ecclesiastes 9:4–6"> And I am praising the dead who have already died above the living who are yet alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 23:29; Ecclesiastes 6:3–5; Matthew 24:19"> And better than both of them &#91;is&#93; he who has not yet been, in that he has not seen the evil work that has been done under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 3:12; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> And I have seen all the labor, and all the benefit of the work, because for it a man is the envy of his neighbor. Even this &#91;is&#93; vanity and distress of spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 6:10–11"> The fool is clasping his hands, and eating his own flesh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Proverbs 15:16–17; Psalms 37:16"> “Better &#91;is&#93; a handful &#91;with&#93; quietness, than two handfuls &#91;with&#93; labor and distress of spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Psalms 78:33; Zechariah 1:6"> And I have turned, and I see a vain thing under the sun:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 2:16; Proverbs 27:20; Luke 12:20"> There is one, and there is not a second; even son or brother he has not, and there is no end to all his labor! His eye also is not satisfied with riches, and &#91;he does not say&#93;, “For whom am I laboring and bereaving my soul of good?” This also is vanity, it is a sad travail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 27:17; Genesis 2:18; 1 Corinthians 12:18–21"> The two &#91;are&#93; better than the one, in that they have a good reward by their labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:18; Isaiah 35:3–4"> For if they fall, the one raises up his companion, but woe to the one who falls and there is not a second to raise him up!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 1:1–4"> Also, if two lie down, then they have heat, but how has one heat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 3:16–17; Ephesians 4:3; 2 Samuel 23:9"> And if the one strengthens himself, the two stand against him; and the threefold cord is not quickly broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:16; 2 Chronicles 16:9–10; Proverbs 19:1"> Better is a poor and wise youth than an old and foolish king, who has not known to be warned anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Genesis 41:14; Job 5:11"> For from a house of prisoners he has come out to reign, for even in his own kingdom he has been poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:6"> I have seen all the living, who are walking under the sun, with the second youth who stands in his place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; 1 Kings 1:40; Ecclesiastes 2:17"> there is no end to all the people, to all who were before them; also, the latter do not rejoice in him. Surely this also is vanity and distress of spirit.</span>
