<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:19; Ecclesiastes 8:15; James 5:5"> I said in my heart, “Pray, come, I try you with mirth, and look on gladness”; and behold, even it &#91;is&#93; vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 14:13; Ecclesiastes 7:2–6; 1 Peter 4:2–4"> Of laughter I said, “Foolish!” And of mirth, “What &#91;is&#93; this it is doing?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Ecclesiastes 1:17; Proverbs 31:4–5"> I have sought in my heart to draw out with wine my appetite (and my heart leading in wisdom), and to take hold on folly until I see where this &#91;is&#93;—the good to the sons of man of that which they do under the heavens, the number of the days of their lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 7:1–12; 2 Chronicles 8:11; 2 Chronicles 26:10"> I made great my works, I built for myself houses, I planted for myself vineyards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 17:27–29; Song of Songs 4:12–1; Jeremiah 39:4"> I made for myself gardens and paradises, and I planted in them trees of every fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:14; Psalms 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8"> I made for myself pools of water, to water from them a forest shooting forth trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 3:4; Genesis 15:3; 1 Kings 4:23"> I acquired menservants, and maidservants, and sons of the house were to me; also, I had much substance—herd and flock—above all who had been before me in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 10:10; 1 Kings 9:28; 2 Samuel 19:35"> I also gathered for myself silver and gold, and the peculiar treasure of kings and of the provinces. I prepared for myself men-singers and women-singers, and the luxuries of the sons of man—a wife and wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:25; Ecclesiastes 1:16; 1 Kings 10:23"> And I became great, and increased above everyone who had been before me in Jerusalem; also, my wisdom stood with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:9; Ecclesiastes 5:18; Ecclesiastes 3:22"> And all that my eyes asked I did not keep back from them; I did not withhold my heart from any joy, for my heart rejoiced because of all my labor, and this has been my portion, from all my labor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; 1 John 2:16–17; Ecclesiastes 1:3"> and I have looked on all my works that my hands have done, and on the labor that I have labored to do, and behold, the whole &#91;is&#93; vanity and distress of spirit, and there is no advantage under the sun!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:17; Ecclesiastes 7:25; Ecclesiastes 1:9–10"> And I turned to see wisdom, and madness, and folly, but what &#91;is&#93; the man who comes after the king? That which &#91;is&#93; already—they have done it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Ecclesiastes 7:11–12; Matthew 6:23"> And I saw that there is an advantage to wisdom above folly, like the advantage of the light above the darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 49:10; Proverbs 17:24; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> The wise—his eyes &#91;are&#93; in his head, and the fool is walking in darkness, and I also knew that one event happens with them all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:8; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Ecclesiastes 1:2"> and I said in my heart, “As it happens with the fool, it happens also with me, and why am I then more wise?” And I spoke in my heart, that also this &#91;is&#93; vanity:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:11; Ecclesiastes 9:5; Psalms 88:12"> That there is no remembrance to the wise—with the fool—for all time, for that which &#91;is&#93; already, &#91;in&#93; the days that are coming is all forgotten, and how dies the wise? With the fool!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:11; Jonah 4:8; Ecclesiastes 4:2"> And I have hated life, for sad to me &#91;is&#93; the work that has been done under the sun, for the whole &#91;is&#93; vanity and distress of spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:6; Psalms 49:10; Luke 16:27–28"> And I have hated all my labor that I labor at under the sun, because I leave it to a man who is after me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 3:17; Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 9:13"> And who knows whether he is wise or foolish? Yet he rules over all my labor that I have labored at, and that I have done wisely under the sun! This &#91;is&#93; also vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 43:14; Job 17:11–15; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> And I turned around to cause my heart to despair concerning all the labor that I labored at under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:17–18; 2 Chronicles 31:20–21; 2 Chronicles 34:2"> For there is a man whose labor &#91;is&#93; in wisdom, and in knowledge, and in equity, and to a man who has not labored therein he gives it—his portion! Even this &#91;is&#93; vanity and a great evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 127:2; Ecclesiastes 1:3; Philippians 4:6"> For what has been to a man by all his labor, and by the thought of his heart that he labored at under the sun?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 14:1; Job 5:7; Psalms 6:6–7"> For all his days are sorrows, and his travail sadness; even at night his heart has not lain down; this &#91;is&#93; also vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 8:15; 1 Timothy 6:17"> There is nothing good in a man who eats, and has drunk, and has shown his soul good in his labor. This also I have seen that it &#91;is&#93; from the hand of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:1–12; 1 Kings 4:21–24"> For who eats and who hurries out more than I?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 13:22; Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 28:8"> For to a man who &#91;is&#93; good before Him, He has given wisdom, and knowledge, and joy; and to a sinner He has given travail, to gather and to heap up, to give to the good before God. Even this &#91;is&#93; vanity and distress of spirit.</span>
