<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 John 1:4; 2 John 1:2; Psalms 119:11"> for I rejoiced exceedingly, brothers coming and testifying of the truth in you, even as you walk in truth;</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; Luke 12:42; Luke 16:10–12"> Beloved, you act faithfully in whatever you may do toward the brothers and the one &#91;among&#93; strangers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; Titus 3:13; Acts 15:3"> who testified of your love before an assembly, whom you will do well, having sent forward worthily of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:5; Revelation 2:3; 2 Kings 5:15–16"> because they went forth for &#91;His&#93; Name, receiving nothing from the nations;</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 1:7–16; Luke 22:24–27; Matthew 23:4–8"> I wrote to the assembly, but he who is loving to be first among them—Diotrephes—does not receive us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 66:5; 2 Corinthians 13:2; Luke 6:22"> because of this, if I may come, I will cause him to remember his works that he does, talking nonsense against us with evil words; and not content with these, neither does he himself receive the brothers, and he forbids those intending &#91;to receive them&#93;, and he casts &#91;them&#93; out of the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–17; Psalms 37:27; Ephesians 5:1"> Beloved, do not be following that which is evil, but that which is good; he who is doing good, he is of God, &#91;but&#93; he who is doing evil has not seen God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; John 21:24; Acts 22:12"> testimony has been given to Demetrius by all, and by the truth itself, and we also—we testify, and you have known that our testimony is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 John 1:12"> Many things I had to write, but I do not wish to write to you through ink and pen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 43:23; Ephesians 6:23; Romans 16:1–16"> and I hope to see you soon, and we will speak mouth to mouth. Peace to you! The friends greet you. Be greeting the friends by name.</span>
