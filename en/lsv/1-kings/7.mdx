<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 9:10; 2 Chronicles 8:1; Ecclesiastes 2:4–5"> And Solomon has built his own house &#91;in&#93; thirteen years, and he finishes all his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 10:17; 2 Chronicles 9:16; 1 Kings 10:21"> And he builds the House of the Forest of Lebanon; its length &#91;is&#93; one hundred cubits, and its breadth fifty cubits, and its height thirty cubits, on four rows of cedar pillars, and cedar-beams on the pillars;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 6:5"> and &#91;it is&#93; covered with cedar above, on the sides that &#91;are&#93; on the forty-five pillars, fifteen in the row.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:22; Ezekiel 40:16; 1 Kings 6:4"> And frames &#91;are&#93; in three rows, and window &#91;is&#93; toward window three times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And all the openings and the doorposts &#91;are of&#93; square frame; and window &#91;is&#93; toward window three times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 7:12; Ezekiel 41:25–26"> And he has made the porch of the pillars; its length fifty cubits, and its breadth thirty cubits, and the porch &#91;is&#93; before them, and pillars and a thick place &#91;are&#93; before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 20:8; Psalms 122:5; Isaiah 9:7"> And the porch of the throne where he judges—the porch of judgment—he has made, and &#91;it is&#93; covered with cedar from the floor to the floor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:11; 1 Kings 3:1; 1 Kings 9:24"> As for his house where he dwells, the other court &#91;is&#93; within the porch—it has been as this work; and he makes a house for the daughter of Pharaoh—whom Solomon has taken—like this porch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 7:10–11; 1 Kings 5:17"> All these &#91;are&#93; of precious stones, according to the measures of hewn work, sawn with a saw, inside and outside, even from the foundation to the coping, and at the outside, to the great court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:19–20; 1 Corinthians 3:10–11; Isaiah 54:11"> And the foundation &#91;is&#93; of precious stones, great stones, stones of ten cubits, and stones of eight cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Peter 2:5"> and precious stones &#91;are&#93; above, according to the measures of hewn work, and cedar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 6:36; 1 Kings 7:6; John 10:23"> and the great court around &#91;is&#93; three rows of hewn work, and a row of cedar-beams, even for the inner court of the house of YHWH, and for the porch of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:11; 2 Chronicles 2:13–14; 1 Kings 7:40"> And King Solomon sends and takes Hiram out of Tyre—</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:14; 2 Chronicles 4:16; Exodus 31:2–6"> he &#91;is the&#93; son of a woman, a widow, of the tribe of Naphtali, and his father &#91;is&#93; a man of Tyre, a worker in bronze, and he is filled with the wisdom and the understanding, and the knowledge to do all work in bronze—and he comes to King Solomon, and does all his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:21; 2 Chronicles 3:15–17; 1 Kings 7:21"> And he forms the two pillars of bronze; eighteen cubits &#91;is&#93; the height of the first pillar, and a cord of twelve cubits goes around the second pillar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 38:19; 2 Chronicles 4:12–13; Exodus 38:17"> And he has made two chapiters to put on the tops of the pillars, cast in bronze; five cubits the height of the first capital, and five cubits the height of the second capital.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 28:14; Exodus 28:24–25; Exodus 28:22"> Nets of network, wreaths of chain-work &#91;are&#93; for the chapiters that &#91;are&#93; on the top of the pillars, seven for the first capital, and seven for the second capital.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 28:14; Exodus 39:15–18; Exodus 28:22"> And he makes the pillars, and two rows around on the one network, to cover the chapiters that &#91;are&#93; on the top, with the pomegranates, and so he has made for the second capital.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 7:22; 1 Kings 6:32–35; 1 Kings 6:18"> And the chapiters that &#91;are&#93; on the top of the pillars &#91;are&#93; of lily-work in the porch, four cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:16; 2 Chronicles 4:13; Jeremiah 52:22–23"> and the chapiters on the two pillars also &#91;have pomegranates&#93; above, close by the protuberance that &#91;is&#93; beside the net; and the pomegranates &#91;are&#93; two hundred, in rows around on the second capital.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:17; 1 Kings 6:3; 1 Kings 7:12"> And he raises up the pillars for the porch of the temple, and he raises up the right pillar, and calls its name Jachin, and he raises up the left pillar, and calls its name Boaz;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and on the top of the pillars &#91;is&#93; lily-work; and the work of the pillars &#91;is&#93; completed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:2; 2 Kings 25:13; Jeremiah 52:17"> And he makes the molten sea, ten by the cubit from its edge to its edge; &#91;it is&#93; round all about, and its height &#91;is&#93; five by the cubit, and a line of thirty by the cubit surrounds it around;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 6:18; 2 Chronicles 4:3; Exodus 37:17–22"> and knobs beneath its brim around are going around it, ten by the cubit, going around the sea; the knobs &#91;are&#93; in two rows, cast in its being cast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:20; 2 Chronicles 4:4–5; Matthew 28:19"> It is standing on twelve oxen, three facing the north, and three facing the west, and three facing the south, and three facing the east, and the sea &#91;is&#93; on them above, and all their back parts &#91;are&#93; inward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:5; 1 Kings 7:38; 1 Kings 6:18"> And its thickness &#91;is&#93; a handbreadth, and its edge &#91;is&#93; as the work of the edge of a cup, &#91;as&#93; flowers of lilies; it contains two thousand baths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:14; 2 Kings 25:13; Jeremiah 52:17"> And he makes the ten bases of bronze; the length of the one base &#91;is&#93; four by the cubit, and its breadth &#91;is&#93; four by the cubit, and its height &#91;is&#93; three by the cubit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And this &#91;is&#93; the work of the base: they have borders, and the borders &#91;are&#93; between the joinings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hosea 5:14; Ezekiel 10:14; Exodus 37:7"> and on the borders that &#91;are&#93; between the joinings &#91;are&#93; lions, oxen, and cherubim, and on the joinings a base above, and beneath the lions and the oxen &#91;are&#93; additions—sloping work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:10–13; Ezekiel 3:13; Ezekiel 1:15–21"> And four wheels of bronze &#91;are&#93; for the one base, and axles of bronze; and its four corners have shoulders—the molten shoulders &#91;are&#93; under the laver, beside each addition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And its mouth within the capital and above &#91;is&#93; by the cubit, and its mouth &#91;is&#93; round, the work of the base, a cubit and half a cubit; and also on its mouth &#91;are&#93; carvings and their borders, square, not round.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the four wheels &#91;are&#93; under the borders, and the spokes of the wheels &#91;are&#93; in the base, and the height of the one wheel &#91;is&#93; a cubit and half a cubit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:18; Ezekiel 1:16"> And the work of the wheels &#91;is&#93; as the work of the wheel of a chariot, their spokes, and their axles, and their felloes, and their naves; the whole &#91;is&#93; molten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And four shoulders &#91;are&#93; for the four corners of the one base; its shoulders &#91;are&#93; out of the base.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And in the top of the base &#91;is&#93; the half of a cubit in the height all around; and on the top of the base its spokes and its borders &#91;are&#93; of the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 7:29; 1 Kings 6:29; Ezekiel 41:18–20"> And he engraves on the tablets of its spokes, and on its borders, cherubim, lions, and palm-trees, according to the void space of each, and additions all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Thus he has made the ten bases; they all have one casting, one measure, one form.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 40:11–12; Zechariah 13:1; 1 John 1:7"> And he makes ten lavers of bronze; the one laver contains forty baths &#91;and&#93; the one laver &#91;is&#93; four by the cubit; one laver on the one base &#91;is&#93; for &#91;each of&#93; the ten bases;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:10; 2 Chronicles 4:6"> and he puts the five bases on the right side of the house, and five on the left side of the house, and he has put the sea on the right side of the house eastward—from toward the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:11–16; Exodus 24:6; Exodus 39:32–43"> And Hiram makes the lavers, and the shovels, and the bowls; and Hiram completes to do all the work that he made for King Solomon, &#91;for&#93; the house of YHWH:</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Kings 7:15–22; 2 Chronicles 4:12"> two pillars, and bowls of the chapiters that &#91;are&#93; on the top of the two pillars, and the two nets to cover the two bowls of the chapiters that &#91;are&#93; on the top of the pillars;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Kings 7:20"> and the four hundred pomegranates for the two nets (two rows of pomegranates for the one net to cover the two bowls of the chapiters that &#91;are&#93; on the front of the pillars);</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> and the ten bases, and the ten lavers on the bases;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Kings 7:23; 1 Kings 7:25"> and the one sea, the twelve oxen under the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Exodus 27:3; Exodus 38:3; 2 Chronicles 4:16"> and the pots, and the shovels, and the bowls; and all these vessels, that Hiram has made for King Solomon &#91;for&#93; the house of YHWH, &#91;are&#93; of polished bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; 2 Chronicles 4:17; Genesis 33:17"> The king has cast them in the circuit of the Jordan, in the thick soil of the ground, between Succoth and Zarthan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:14; 1 Chronicles 22:3; 2 Chronicles 4:18"> And Solomon places the whole of the vessels; because of the very great abundance, the weight of the bronze has not been searched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Exodus 37:10–16; 2 Chronicles 4:8; Exodus 39:36"> And Solomon makes all the vessels that &#91;are&#93; in the house of YHWH: the altar of gold, and the table of gold (on which &#91;is&#93; the Bread of the Presentation),</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:7; 2 Chronicles 4:20; Zechariah 4:11–14"> and the lampstands, five on the right, and five on the left, before the oracle, of refined gold, and the flowers, and the lamps, and the tongs, of gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 25:29; 2 Chronicles 4:21–22; Exodus 27:3"> and the basins, and the snuffers, and the bowls, and the spoons, and the censers, of refined gold, and the hinges for the doors of the inner-house, for the Holy of Holies, for the doors of the house of the temple, of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:1; 2 Samuel 8:7–11; 1 Chronicles 28:11–18"> And it is complete—all the work that King Solomon has made &#91;for&#93; the house of YHWH, and Solomon brings in the sanctified things of his father David; he has put the silver, and the gold, and the vessels in the treasuries of the house of YHWH.</span>
