<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; Luke 11:31; 2 Chronicles 9:1–12"> And the queen of Sheba is hearing of the fame of Solomon concerning the Name of YHWH, and comes to try him with enigmas,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 25:23; Psalms 4:4; Job 4:2"> and she comes to Jerusalem, with a very great company, camels carrying spices, and very much gold, and precious stone, and she comes to Solomon, and speaks to him all that has been with her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 1:5–6; Matthew 13:11; 1 Kings 10:1"> And Solomon declares to her all her matters—there has not been a thing hid from the king that he has not declared to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; 1 Kings 3:28; 2 Chronicles 9:3–4"> And the queen of Sheba sees all the wisdom of Solomon, and the house that he built,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:16; 1 Kings 4:22–23; 2 Kings 16:18"> and the food of his table, and the sitting of his servants, and the standing of his ministers, and their clothing, and his butlers, and his burnt-offering that he causes to ascend in the house of YHWH, and there has not been anymore spirit in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:5–6"> And she says to the king, “The word that I heard in my land has been true concerning your matters and your wisdom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9; John 20:25–29; 1 John 3:2"> and I gave no credence to the words until I have come, and my eyes see, and behold, not &#91;even&#93; half was declared to me; you have added wisdom and goodness to the report that I heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 8:34; Proverbs 10:21; Luke 11:31"> O the blessedness of your men, O the blessedness of your servants—these—who are standing before you continually, who are hearing your wisdom!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 72:2; 1 Kings 5:7; 2 Samuel 8:15"> Your God YHWH is blessed, who delighted in you, to put you on the throne of Israel; in YHWH’s loving Israel for all time He sets you for king, to do judgment and righteousness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 10:2; Matthew 2:11; 1 Kings 9:14"> And she gives to the king one hundred and twenty talents of gold, and very many spices, and precious stone; there never again came in abundance like that spice that the queen of Sheba gave to King Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 9:27–28; 2 Chronicles 8:18; Psalms 45:9"> And also, the navy of Hiram that bore gold from Ophir, brought in very much almug-trees and precious stone from Ophir;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:1–31; Psalms 150:3–5; Revelation 14:2–3"> and the king makes the almug-trees a support for the house of YHWH, and for the house of the king, and harps and psalteries for singers; there have not come such almug-trees, nor have there been seen &#91;such&#93; to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Matthew 15:28; Psalms 20:4"> And King Solomon gave to the queen of Sheba all her desire that she asked, apart from that which he gave to her as a memorial of King Solomon, and she turns and goes to her land, she and her servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:13–28; 1 Kings 9:28"> And the weight of the gold that has come to Solomon in one year is six hundred sixty-six talents of gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 72:10; 1 Chronicles 9:24; Isaiah 21:13"> apart from &#91;that of&#93; the tourists, and of the traffic of the merchants, and of all the kings of Arabia, and of the governors of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 14:26–28; 2 Chronicles 12:9–10; 2 Chronicles 9:15–16"> And King Solomon makes two hundred bucklers of alloyed gold—six hundred &#91;shekels&#93; of gold go up on the one buckler;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2; 1 Kings 14:26"> and three hundred shields of alloyed gold—three pounds of gold go up on the one shield; and the king puts them &#91;in&#93; the House of the Forest of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 45:8; 1 Kings 10:22; Revelation 20:11"> And the king makes a great throne of ivory, and overlays it with refined gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> the throne has six steps, and a round top &#91;is&#93; to the throne behind it, and hands &#91;are&#93; on this &#91;side&#93; and on that, to the place of the sitting, and two lions are standing near the hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 23:24"> and twelve lions are standing there on the six steps, on this &#91;side&#93; and on that; it has not been made so for any kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 10:17; 1 Kings 7:2; 2 Chronicles 9:20–22"> And all the drinking vessels of King Solomon &#91;are&#93; of gold, and all the vessels of the House of the Forest of Lebanon &#91;are&#93; of refined gold—there are none of silver; it was not reckoned in the days of Solomon for anything,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 22:48; Genesis 10:4; 2 Chronicles 20:36–37"> for the king has a navy of Tarshish at sea with a navy of Hiram; once in three years the navy of Tarshish comes, carrying gold, and silver, ivory, and apes, and peacocks &#91;&#91;or monkeys&#93;&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12–13; Colossians 2:2–3; 1 Kings 4:29–34"> And King Solomon is greater than any of the kings of the earth for riches and for wisdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12; James 1:5; 1 Kings 3:28"> and all the earth is seeking the presence of Solomon, to hear his wisdom that God has put into his heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 42:11; Psalms 72:10; Psalms 72:15"> and they are each bringing his present, vessels of silver, and vessels of gold, and garments, and armor, and spices, horses, and mules, the matter of a year in a year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:25; 1 Kings 4:26; Deuteronomy 17:16"> And Solomon gathers chariots, and horsemen, and he has one thousand and four hundred chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen, and he places them in the cities of the chariot, and with the king in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:15–17; Deuteronomy 17:17; 2 Chronicles 9:27"> And the king makes the silver in Jerusalem as stones, and he has made the cedars as the sycamores that &#91;are&#93; in the low country, for abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16; 2 Chronicles 9:28; Isaiah 31:1–3"> And the outgoing of the horses that King Solomon has &#91;is&#93; from Egypt, and from Keveh; merchants of the king take from Keveh at a price;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 1:4; 2 Kings 7:6–7; Malachi 1:1"> and a chariot comes up and comes out of Egypt for six hundred pieces of silver, and a horse for one hundred and fifty, and so for all the kings of the Hittites, and for the kings of Aram; they bring out by their hand.</span>
